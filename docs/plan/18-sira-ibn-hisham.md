# 18 · سيرة ابن هشام (الجزء الرابع) — mining the classical sira into the endgame of the Prophetic era

**The book.** *السيرة النبوية* — the recension of عبد الملك بن هشام (d. 218 AH) of the sira of محمد بن إسحاق (d. 150 AH), the oldest surviving narrative of the Prophet's life and the primary source behind every later sira, including الرحيق المختوم (document 17). The copy is الجزء الرابع of the standard printed edition — تحقيق مصطفى السقا، إبراهيم الأبياري وعبد الحفيظ شلبي، مطبعة مصطفى البابي الحلبي وأولاده، مصر، 1355هـ/1936م — which covers the last four years, roughly 7–11 AH: Ja'far's return from Abyssinia, umrat al-qada, Mu'ta, the conquest of Mecca, Banu Jadhima, Hunayn and Ta'if, the spoils and the mu'allafa, Tabuk, the year of delegations, the Farewell Pilgrimage, the retrospective sariya accounts, the final illness, the Saqifa and the burial. Where الرحيق is a modern retelling, this is the T1 primary text itself, with Ibn Ishaq's isnads, the poetry, and Ibn Hisham's own annotations («قال ابن هشام»، «عن غير ابن إسحاق»، «يُشك فيه») that register variants the museum records as debated.

**The copy.** `~/islam-museum-harvest/raw/books/sira-ibn-hisham/`: a 376-page image-only PDF (Noor-Book scan of an AUB library copy, 184 MB), fully OCR'd with Apple Vision on the Mac (539 seconds, about 578,000 characters). **Printed page = PDF page − 4** (verified at 13 points). `toc.txt`/`toc.json` hold the فهرس الموضوعات (printed 358–367) — a complete topic index in narrative order with page numbers; `bib.txt` holds فهرس أسماء الكتب (printed 352, ~14 works the editors cite); `index-people.txt`, `index-tribes.txt`, `index-places.txt` the name indexes; `book.txt` and `pages.json` the whole text with `===== [pN] =====` PDF markers; `pages-png/` the page images; `README.md` the OCR error list.

## 1. Rights and how the book may be used

Two layers live in the same pages:

- **The sira text** (Ibn Ishaq / Ibn Hisham) is a classical work — **public domain** (`pd` layer). It may be quoted; quotations still follow the house rule: at most 40 words, verbatim, in quotation marks, with the page (`{cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/N}`).
- **The editors' apparatus** — the taḥqīq footnotes `(١)…` (غريب، مواضع، مقابلات), the margin rubrics, the indexes and the استدراك, dated 1936 — is treated as `copyrighted-reference` (15 §3): facts and citations only; the apparatus is never republished or stored in the repo.

In practice the museum takes, with citations: **facts, chronology, names with full nasab, places, numbers, isnads, dates as given**; quotations of the classical text ≤ 40 words; **never** the editors' notes verbatim, never the OCR text in the repo, never a paraphrase of pages into museum prose. Cite the book page **and** record the isnad head the book itself names («قال ابن إسحاق: حدثني الزهري…») in the claim's note/provenance; a second source for a dated fact comes from the works the editors name in `bib.txt` (البخاري، الاستيعاب، الإصابة، الروض الأنف للسهيلي، معجم البلدان، شرح السيرة لأبي ذر…) or a parallel classical text via OpenITI.

**Status of claims:** `tradition` by default — this is the tradition's own earliest narrative. Where Ibn Hisham marks a variant or a doubt («ويقال»، «عن غير ابن إسحاق»، «يُشك فيه»، «يُروى»), the claim is `debated` or carries `confidence: low` with a note quoting the marker. Contested sections (Saqifa, the executions at the conquest, Banu Jadhima, Asma' bint Marwan and Abu Afak, Umm Qirfa, the prayer over Ibn Ubayy, Zaynab's marriage) carry `perspectives[]` — Sunni first, then academic, then any other view — and Tier A review.

A `source` entity `source/ibn-hisham-saqqa-1936` records this edition; a `work` entity `work/sira-ibn-ishaq-ibn-hisham` records the work itself; each work in `bib.txt` (about 14 titles) becomes a `source` entity so citations resolve.

## 4. The pipeline for this book

1. **OCR** (done): `ocr_apple_vision.py` in the workspace; re-run only if a better scan appears.
2. **Clean**: strip running footers (`- ٣٢١ -`) and the printed line-numbers (`١٠ ١٥ ٢٠ ٢٥` at line ends); split margin rubrics (short interleaved lines — the editors' section markers, matched to `toc.json` entries) out of the text flow and keep them as segment boundaries; attach each `(١)…` footnote block to its page; repair the systematic errors listed in the workspace `README.md` — above all **جمفر → جعفر**, also الحشة/الجبشة → الحبشة، الحديلية → الحديبية، هانم → هاشم، مكلة → مكة.
3. **Segment** by `toc.json` into ~85 sections `{title, page_start, page_end, text, rubrics[], footnotes[]}` → `sections.json` (local).
4. **Extract**, section by section, with the drilling prompt (§6): claims with printed page and ≤40-word quote, dates as the book gives them («جمادى الأولى سنة ثمان»), places, participants with roles and **full nasab**, sides, numbers, the isnad head (`قال ابن إسحاق: حدثني …`), every «قال ابن هشام» variant or doubt marker as a `debated`/note flag, and every Qur'an ayah the text engages.
5. **Judge** (09 §4): second model checks entailment against the quote and the page image; flags single-source dates for a second classical source (البخاري، الطبري، السهيلي، الاستيعاب via `bib.txt` or OpenITI).
6. **Draft** Arabic entities and prose (`content/ar/`) in the docent voice, never the classical voice; `[[…]]` links, `map` blocks where positions are described, `perspectives` where §1 requires.
7. **Validate, PR, review**: batches of 25 entities; Tier A on the contested list in §3.

## 5. Order of work

Fath Mecca first (the richest chapter, ~40 headings, ends with the Kaaba's cleansing), then Hunayn–Ta'if–the spoils, then Mu'ta, then the Abyssinian roster (the person backbone, batched by clan), then Tabuk + Dirar + the three, then the delegation year in three batches, then the retrospective sariyas in two batches, then illness–death–Saqifa–burial (Tier A), then the wives and the small sections (qada, Jadhima, Uzza, Ji'rana, Thaqif wafd, Abu Bakr's hajj, Ka'b ibn Zuhayr, the envoys and the ghazwa lists), then `bib.txt` → `source` entities. Each batch is one pull request.
