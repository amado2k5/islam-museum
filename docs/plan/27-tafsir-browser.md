## 2. Intent

A reader who opens the tafsir wing sees the commentaries of the Qur'an as works in their own right: each commentary is a `work`, each printed volume is a `work` of kind `tafsir-volume` that is part of its commentary and is about the verses it comments on, and each verse page (`passage/quran-S-A`) already names the volume and page it cites. The wing's index lists the commentaries; a commentary page lists its volumes and the verse range each covers; a volume page lists its verses in order.

Nothing in the wing is a second graph. The volume records and their `about` edges are in `data/work/` and are rendered like every other record.

## 3. Model

- **Work of the commentary**: the existing `work/jami-al-bayan` (kind `book`, unchanged) is the commentary.
- **Volume**: a new `work` kind `tafsir-volume` (added to `schema/enums/kinds.yaml`). Each volume is one record, `work/jami-al-bayan-shakir-vNN`, with `part_of` pointing at the commentary and `sources` naming its `source/tafsir-tabari-shakir-vNN` record.
- **Verse coverage**: the volume's `about` relations point at the `passage/quran-S-A` records whose cite lines name that volume. `about` already accepts a `work` as its domain and any target as its range (`schema/predicates.yaml`); no new predicate is needed.
- **Commentary kind**: a commentary that is not split into volumes keeps its existing kind. Other tafsirs in the source records (Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi, Tabari, Manar, Sharawi and the rest) join the wing in later phases, one commentary at a time, each with its own proposal line.

Phase 1 may need a second enum value (`tafsir`) if a commentary record is given kind `tafsir`; that choice is proposed in the Phase 1 pull request description, not made here.
