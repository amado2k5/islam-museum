# Verification ledger: the Grand Tour mission

This is the board, its oracles and its final state. It is written to be re-checked by someone who trusts none of it.

## How to read it

Three states are kept apart everywhere:

| State | Commit | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Production | `8d1b0ef9d7556cc570e9e452e8cd3257e346ab1f` | What visitors see: build run 42832259141, site v1.10.0, deployed 2026-11-10T18:33:45Z. |
| Baseline | `e1d721298ab4b2494a4c8f04d3defcde1808b939` | Main at the moment this branch started. The "before" of every repository measurement. |
| This branch | named in the board below | What the run built. **None of it is live.** Merged is not live, and this is not merged. |

A claim about visitors is a claim about production. Only one oracle measures production (the search test), and it says so in its own output.

An **oracle** is a committed file under `scripts/fable/`. Running it prints what it measured and ends with one line, `RESULT: GREEN` or `RESULT: NOT-GREEN (...)`. Every oracle prints the commit it ran at, the commit the graph artifact was built from, and how many measured files were uncommitted at the time. Its captured output is under `docs/fable-run/oracle-output/`.

Every number in this ledger was produced by one of those runs. The before/after table is generated from the captured outputs by `scripts/fable/numbers.py` into `docs/fable-run/NUMBERS.md`.

## Definitions the numbers rest on

Each of these is a choice. A different choice gives a different number, so each is stated here and in the oracle that uses it.

- **Canon, cornerstone.** A record with `importance` 1 or 2; a cornerstone has 1. The field and the selection already existed on main (PR #5975). This run audited them and added two repairs: the century floor, and wings for cornerstones that had none.
- **Fourteen centuries.** The Hijri centuries 1 to 14 (1 to 1400 AH). Before the Hijra is a prelude; the 15th century is the present.
- **Where a record stands in time (the anchor rule).** Its own date (a person's death, an event's date, a building's `built`, a period's start); for a work with none, its author's death; otherwise it floats within the periods it lists, or anywhere if it lists none. Only stops with a date of their own count toward century coverage. The Hijri year is the one the record states; where it states none it is `floor((CE year - 622) * 33 / 32) + 1`, which is exact to within one year. The coverage oracle prints the stops whose century rests on that arithmetic within a year of a boundary.
- **Wing.** The record's own `wings` field, which is what the wing pages read.
- **The Tour's narrative at a stop.** The lead section (first `## ` section) of the record's own prose. Length is counted as a reader sees it: citation markers and link syntax are not words.
- **Already cited.** A source in the record's `sources:` list, or cited in its prose, at the baseline commit.
- **Dead end.** A cornerstone with fewer than two distinct neighbours in the graph: none (isolated) or one (a leaf, where the only way on is the way back). Stubs are not neighbours. The predicate `related_to` is never counted and never traversed.
- **Hop distance.** The shortest path, in either direction, from a record to its nearest cornerstone, over relations between real records, without `related_to`. A citation (`sources:`) is not a relation and is not a hop.
- **Published.** `status.review` equal to `published`.
- **Contradiction.** Two records, or two fields, that cannot both be right as written, as raised by the four detectors in `scripts/fable/contradictions.ts`. Fixed means corrected from a source the edited record already cited, and no longer raised. Registered means it stands, with its evidence and the reason. A registered entry can be hidden from the detector by a sound fix elsewhere; the register says so.
- **Canon-first.** The first result of the site's own search page for each query in `scripts/fable/search-queries.json` is the canon record named there.
- **Absent.** No real record of the subject's class matches any published spelling, exactly or word for word, other than the near misses that were opened and rejected.
- **Records that hang off the path.** Distinct real records that share at least one relation with at least one Tour stop, not counting the stops.
