# Vision review S

Screenshots looked at: 30 of 30. Defects: 46.

Scope: the homepage and the Time, Place, People and Ideas wings, Arabic and English, at phone (390), tablet (820) and
desktop (1280). Every file was opened whole and then in enlarged crops. Positions are pixel rows and columns in the
screenshot (y from the top, x from the left). Where a row says "measured", the figure comes from counting pixels in
the JPEG, not from reading the image by eye. Arrow and bracket directions were checked the same way, because at this
size they are easy to misread.

Three things I could not settle from a still image, flagged in their rows: whether the bar in S21 is a fixed bottom
bar caught by the full-page capture; whether the map in S42 lets the page scroll; and the screen heights behind S5
(844, 1180 and 800 px, inferred from where the bar and the map end).
