## Checked and found correct

So that these are not taken for things not looked at:

- Arabic mirroring of the page frame is right at all three widths: logo on the right, menu flowing right to left,
  controls at the left end, banner text on the right, grids filling from the right, the coloured edge of the "on this
  day" cards on the right, the breadcrumb chevron pointing left (checked on the pixels) and the bottom bar mirrored.
- Arabic "..." at the end of a shortened summary sits at the left of the last word, as it should (W6 is the one
  exception).
- Bracketed phrases that hold Arabic words and stay on one line are drawn correctly, for example the dated captions
  on Objects and the one-line award titles on Today.
- Text contrast, estimated from the pixels, is 5.7:1 or better for every text style sampled: card labels, card
  summaries, tag lines, the footnote, holder names, and the breadcrumb, lede and lead-in line on the dark banner.
- No text is clipped by its box, no card overflows its column, and no page scrolls sideways.

## Not counted as defects (content, for information)

The brief excludes the content of the text, so these are not in the table, but a visitor would notice them on the
same screens:

- The banner ledes do not describe what the lists below them show. "Living Islam: practices, the Islamic year,
  festivals, food, dress and music by region" opens onto cards about the ant, the apes, the bat, the bear and the bee;
  "Today: nations, institutions, data and the people of the present" opens, after the "on this day" block, onto music
  awards and album charts; "Made: what Muslims make and own today: bookshops, publishers, apps…" opens onto medieval
  ceramics and scripts.
- On the English Words page the spelling changes between cards: "Qur'an" and "Quran", "surah" and "sura".
- Every card in a list repeats the same kind label: "CONCEPT", "PASSAGE", "AWARD"; "مفهوم", "مقطع", "جائزة".
