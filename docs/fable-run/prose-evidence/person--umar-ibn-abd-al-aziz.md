Dropped or changed from the old lead: nothing dropped. Its three facts (allegiance after Sulayman's death; governors and Kharijites; dispute with Yazid ibn al-Muhallab) are sentences 3 and 6 with their original Tabari citations.

Could not be supported and left out:
- The day of the pledge: al-Mas'udi (scan p.172) has Friday, ten nights remaining of Safar 99; al-Sallabi has Friday, ten nights gone of Safar 99 (p.187) and, for Sulayman's death on the same day, ten nights remaining (p.157). Only the month and year are given.
- His age at death: 39 in al-Mas'udi (scan p.172, with 40 and 41 as other reports), about 40 in al-Sallabi (p.161); left out.
- Al-Mas'udi's report that he ended the cursing of Ali from the pulpits (data summary.ar, scan p.173): on the page, but left out of a short lead; it stays in the record's summary.
- "Eighth Umayyad caliph" and the CE dates 681–720 in the uncited «للدارسين» section: not read in any cited source page here. "The first renewer" is in the data summary with al-Sallabi p.223-234 and p.236-249; I did not read those pages, so it is not used.
