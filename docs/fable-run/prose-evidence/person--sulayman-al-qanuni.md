Dropped or changed from the old lead: nothing dropped. The old lead's citation p.200-202 is kept and joined by p.198 (son of Selim) and p.276 (974 AH).

Could not be supported and left out:
- "The revolt of Baba Dhu al-Nun in 926 AH" ⁦(record, section «الفتن الأولى» and data summary)⁩: p. 200 reads «عام ١٥٢٦م», a Gregorian year. The lead does not name this revolt or give it a date.
- The reign's end in the cited pages: p. 202, checked on the page image, prints «٩٢٦ – ٩٧٢ه»; p. 196 prints «٩٧٤ - ٩٢٧ ه»; p. 276 dates his death to 974 AH / 1566 CE. The record's 926 to 974 is kept, with p. 276 cited for 974.
- A birth year: p. 200 gives the place of birth but no year. The year in the data file (about 900 AH, cited to al-Salabi with no page) is found in the record only in al-Suwaidan's telling. The lead gives no birth year.
- The sectarian labels the page attaches to the third and fourth revolts are not carried into the lead.

Revised after verification: sentence 5 opened with «ثم» / "He then", straight after the Egyptian revolt of 930 AH, which p. 200 dates 930/1524; the war on Rhodes began in mid-1522, before it. The connective is removed in both languages and the sentence now names Suleiman («وشنّ سليمان» / "Suleiman waged"), as the page does. No fact was changed. In the English lead the author's name, spelled "Al-Sallabi" twice, now reads "Al-Salabi", the spelling of the source record's label and of the `person/mahmud-ii-uthmani` lead.
