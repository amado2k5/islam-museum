Dropped or changed from the old lead:
- Nothing dropped. The old lead's four short paragraphs are now the first five sentences.
- The old English lead ended "was succeeded by Mustafa II". The Arabic («وخلف مصطفى الثاني»), the data file (`successor_of: person/mustafa-ii-uthmani`) and p.310 all have it the other way: Ahmed III came after Mustafa II. The English now says "succeeding Mustafa II".
- English wording only: "holy war" is now "jihad" (the Arabic word, attributed to al-Salabi as before), "Azak" is now "Azov", "Ahmad III" is now "Ahmed III" (the record's English label).
- Damad Ibrahim Pasha is named without a link. The museum has two records for him: `person/damat-ibrahim-pasha` carries this very passage in its summary but a death year of 1601 and the Wikidata id of the earlier vizier of the same name; `person/nevshehirli-damat-ibrahim-pasha` is a draft card. I did not choose between them.

Could not be supported and left out:
- The terms of the treaty of Falksen. p.310 says the treaty led to «إخلاء مدينة آزاق للصليبيين الروس», which sits against the same paragraph's opening («أن تعيد المورة وآزاق»). Left out.
- The motive al-Salabi gives for lifting the siege («الخيانة تحت فتنة المال والنساء») and the figure of 200,000: his judgement and his number; the old lead did not carry them and the new one does not either.
- The CE year of the treaty of Edirne. The page prints «١١٢٥هـ/١٧١٦م»; the record gives the Hijri year only, and so does the lead.

Revised after verification: sentence 4, "Russia gave up the Black Sea coasts" is now "Russia gave up all it had seized of the Black Sea coasts" ⁦(«عن كل ما استولت عليه من سواحل البحر الأسود»)⁩, as p.311 has it. Nothing else changed.
