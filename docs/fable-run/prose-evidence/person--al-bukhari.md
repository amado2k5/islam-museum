Dropped or changed from the old lead:
- DROPPED: «يوصف في المصدر بأنه أمير المؤمنين في الحديث». The old lead cited it to Fihrist p.321-322. The Fihrist entry reads only «أبو عبد الله محمد بن إسماعيل بن المغيرة البخاري. من علماء المحدثين الثقات وله من الكتب ...» in all three local scans that hold it. The phrase is not on al-Subki's printed pp.2–15 nor in the EI2 article either. I could not find its source, so it is out of the lead.
- CHANGED: the full name (Ibrahim, Bardizba, al-Ju'fi) was cited to the Fihrist, which has only "ibn Isma'il ibn al-Mughira". It is kept and now cited to al-Subki p.2 and EI2 p.1360, where it is.

Could not be supported and left out:
- "Born at Bukhara" (data file summary and `born_in`): al-Subki p.3 says he heard much «ببلده» before travelling and p.8 says he left Bukhara aged fifteen; the EI2 article does not name the birthplace. The lead does not state a birthplace.
- «وقيل سنة ٢٤٧هـ: أعلم من دخل نيسابور» (data file summary): al-Subki printed p.7 reads «أعلم من دخل العراق» (checked on the page image). Not used.
- The doctrinal charge that the EI2 gives as the reason he left Nishapur is left out of the lead; the lead says "opposition".
