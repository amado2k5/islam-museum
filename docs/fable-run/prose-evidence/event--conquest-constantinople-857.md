Could not be supported and left out: nothing. Two points are stated as the sources give them, without a verdict:
- The date. Shadharat has Wednesday 20 Jumada al-Akhira 857; al-Salabi has Tuesday 20 Jumada al-Ula 857, 29 May 1453. The lead gives both, each under its source.
- The length of the siege. Shadharat has fifty days, with the city taken on the fifty-first; al-Salabi p. 113 says the fighting lasted 54 days; the record's uncited section «للدارسين» says about fifty-three. The lead gives only the Shadharat figure, under its source.
