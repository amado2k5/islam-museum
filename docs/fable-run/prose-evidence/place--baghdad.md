Revised after verification:
- Sentence 2 said in the museum's own voice that Baghdad "remained the seat of the caliphate from its foundation until 1258", which restates the article's opening sentence but is stronger than the article taken whole. The sentence now gives it as the Turkish encyclopaedia's statement and adds what the same article says further down: the caliphs resided at Samarra from 836 to 892 (years as the article prints them). A link to `place/samarra` (exists) was added; no source was added.

- «ويعرّفها مدخلها في الموسوعة» / "its entry in the Turkish encyclopaedia of Islam calls it" became «وتعرّفها الموسوعة» / "The Turkish encyclopaedia of Islam calls it", in both languages, for length.
