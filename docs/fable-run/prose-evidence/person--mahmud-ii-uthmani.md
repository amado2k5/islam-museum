Dropped or changed from the old lead: nothing dropped. The old lead was one sentence (name, father, accession in 1223 AH); it is sentence 1 with its citation.

The fez and the turban: the page writes «فاستبدل الطربوش الرومى بالعمامة» and, four lines on, «استبدال العمامة بالطربوش». Both mean that the fez took the turban's place. The lead says so in plain words («اتخذ الطربوش بدل العمامة» / "adopted the fez in place of the turban").

Could not be supported and left out:
- The Hijri date of the treaty of Bucharest. p.339 prints «١٢٣٧هـ / ١٨١٢م»; the two years do not agree with each other. The lead names the treaty without a date.
- The days of the month (8 and 9 Dhu al-Qa'da) and the crowd of 60,000 on p.340-341. The lead keeps to the month and year.
- The order he founded («وسام الافتخار») and the claim that he was the first Ottoman sultan to found one (p.341): left out for length.
- Al-Salabi's long account of why the Janissaries had to go (p.340) is his judgement; not carried.
