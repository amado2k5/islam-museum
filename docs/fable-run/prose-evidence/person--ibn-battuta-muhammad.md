Dropped or changed from the old lead: nothing dropped. "ألّف رحلته المشهورة" is now "صاحب الرحلة المشهورة", because the Rihla itself says he dictated it and Ibn Juzayy wrote it (sentence 7). "على سبيل الرواية" is kept as "على رواية".

Could not be supported and left out:
- The death date 779 AH / 1377 CE in the record's section «للدارسين» (uncited) disagrees with the lead and the data file (776 / 1374). The Mawsu'a records both years (p. 577) and the Durar has him alive until 770 (3/481). The lead keeps 776 as one report and says other dates are given.
- "Reached Fez on the eve of Eid al-Adha" ⁦(record, section «في طبعة المطبعة الخيرية من الرحلة»)⁩: scan p. 566 says he reached Dar al-Tama' on the eve of the feast, stayed there for the day, and then went on to Fez. The lead does not give a day for the arrival.
- The distances and the list of regions in «للدارسين» (117,000 km; 73,000 miles in English) carry no citation and were not found in the pages read. Not used.

Revised after verification: sentence 5, the statement of his school and of the school of the people of Delhi removed (not in the old lead; the lead now says only that the sultan made him judge and named two deputies to act for him). Sentence 7, English only: the date of completion is no longer given to Ibn Juzayy's editing. Sentence 8, the pronoun mended in both languages: the vizier's warning was to Ibn Khaldun, not to Ibn Battuta.
