Seen in the record (not changed, outside the lead):
- Arabic section «ما تغطيه هذه الصفحة», Golius paragraph: «سمّوها القاهرة، وأرادوا بذلك النسبة إلى فاطمة». On PDF pp.397–398 Golius says the dynasty wished to be called Fatimid after Fatima, and separately that the city was named Alcahira, "Victrix", as a name of good omen. The record runs the two together.
- Same paragraph: «شمالي الفسطاط». On PDF p.397 the town added "a septentrione" is an earlier one; of Cairo Golius writes «ab Oriente accrevit civitas ampla», set apart from Misr or Fustat by an interval. This needs a re-read against the page image.
- Golius dates Jawhar's start to 360 AH (PDF p.397); al-Maqrizi gives Sha'ban 358 or Jumada II 359. The record states both without noting the difference.
- The English file has no Golius paragraphs; the Arabic has two. The English body gives "17 Sha'ban 358 AH" where the Arabic says «في شعبان» (the day is on the page, scan p.1030: «يوم الثلاثاء لسبع عشرة خلت من شعبان»). The lead follows the Arabic.
