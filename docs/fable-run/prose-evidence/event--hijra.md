Dropped or changed from the old lead:
- "Many of his companions" is now sentence 2.
- "Later" (the Muslims later chose the Hijra as the start of their calendar): the word is dropped. No source this record cites and that is on this machine says when the choice was made; the hadith states only what the count starts from.

Could not be supported and left out:
- Section «الأهمية»: that the caliph Umar fixed the epoch about fifteen years later at 1 Muharram, 16 July 622 CE, and that Medina became the first Muslim polity. The section has no citation and I did not find the statements in a cited source on this machine.
- Section «ما جرى»: arrival at Quba on 12 Rabi' al-Awwal, cited to `watt-1956-medina` with no page (not on this machine). The data file's note gives Monday 8 Rabi' al-Awwal for Quba from the Raheeq; al-Mas'udi gives Monday the twelfth. The lead states both, each attributed.
