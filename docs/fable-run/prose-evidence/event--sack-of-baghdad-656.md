Dropped or changed from the old lead: nothing dropped. The old second sentence ("the caliphate of Baghdad breaks off for about three years and a half in al-Suyuti") is now sentence 9, worded as the page has it: the caliphate was interrupted for three and a half years, until it was set up in Egypt in Rajab 659. The old English lead said the caliphate ended "after about three years and a half", which is not what the Arabic or the source says; the new English follows the Arabic.

Could not be supported and left out:

- The count of the dead in Ibn Khaldun (p. 901, «ويقال ... ألف ألف وستمائة ألف») is on the page but left out for length; al-Suyuti's figure and the lecturer's caution are given.
- The day-by-day dates of the lecture (22 and 28 Muharram, 4 and 10 Safar) do not agree with al-Suyuti's "entered on the day of Ashura"; only the lecture's month and year in the Common Era are used, attributed to the lecturer.
