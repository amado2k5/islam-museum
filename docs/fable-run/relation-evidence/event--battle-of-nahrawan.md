How the targets were identified (each by its own label and summary):

- place/al-nahrawan: «النهروان», the small town on the Nahrawan canal east of Baghdad in the Sawad; person/abdallah-ibn-wahb-al-rasibi already writes `traveled_to` to it for this same event.
- group/khawarij: «الخوارج».
- person/abdallah-ibn-wahb-al-rasibi: its summary says the Kharijites gave him their allegiance and that he brought them to Nahrawan in 38.
- person/dhu-al-thudiyya: its summary says Ali searched for him among the dead after Nahrawan.
- person/abu-ayyub-al-ansari, person/hujr-ibn-adi ⁦(«حجر بن عدي الكندي»)⁩, person/maqil-ibn-qays ⁦(«معقل بن قيس الرياحي», "one of Ali's commanders")⁩, person/shabath-ibn-ribi ⁦(«شبث بن ربعي»)⁩, person/qays-ibn-sad ⁦(«قيس بن سعد بن عبادة»; the only Qays ibn Sa'd in `data/person/`)⁩: the names in the prose match the labels.

Looked for and not found as records: nothing that the prose names is missing.

Could have been added but was left out:
- person/abu-qatada-al-ansari. The prose says only «وعلى الرجالة أبا قتادة», without the nisba; identifying him is a step the record does not take.
- `caused_by -> event/arbitration-of-siffin`. The record says the Kharijites rebelled «بعد التحكيم» ("after the arbitration"): a sequence, not a stated cause.
- person/abdullah-ibn-abbas and person/abdallah-ibn-khabbab. The first debated the Kharijites at Harura before the battle and the second was killed before it; the record does not place either at the battle.
- person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat is linked in the prose as the historian who reports the battle, not as a participant; `written_about_by` would fit, but he is a narrator of one line and was left out.
