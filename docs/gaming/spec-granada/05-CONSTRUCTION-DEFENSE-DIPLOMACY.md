# Construction, defense, welfare and diplomacy
## Building loop
Choose category → select module → preview snap, terrain grade, network access, footprint, cost and estimated completion → commit queued project → reserve materials/labor → transport/build → activate only when required stages complete. Placement rules use collision volumes, foundation support, slope, road access, water access, budget and era/region eligibility. Provide red invalid/green valid preview plus text/icon (not color alone), accessible list selection and cancellation. In creative sandbox, distinguish free-build from campaign economy; never import a free-build asset as a funded completed canonical structure. Undo before commit, cancel after commitment refunds unused stock only and returns reserved workers. Actual demolition has explicit salvage policy; protect historically authored landmarks in historical mode unless permitted fictional sandbox.

Proposed catalogue groups: houses and upgrades, fields/orchards, canals/reservoirs, granary and warehouse, watermill, bakery, oil press, weaving and ceramics workshops, quarry, market, roads/bridges, wall segments, gates, towers, keeps, courtyards, palace wing, garden and civic facilities. Construction pipeline has layout, foundation, structural shell, finish, operational inspection (simplified game rules), damage and maintenance. Visual phases correspond to state; show scaffolds, materials carts and workers when active. Prevent duplicated materials, floating unattached components, unreachable buildings and fake progress. Modular fortress system should connect wall/tower/gate segments through stable anchors, parameterized length, rotation and elevation. Use simplified structural constraints (not real engineering advice). Preserve original player-created design as versioned Blueprint with module IDs, transforms, cost estimate and provenance. Import preview recalculates rules, prices, collision and era; reject incompatible or unsafe structures. Walkable surfaces and navmesh regenerate locally; no full world recalculation on each placement.

## Population and civic simulation
Households require shelter, nutrition, water, services and work. Employment, wages, migration, capacity and welfare are modeled at bounded aggregate level. Representative citizens have routes and activity mapped to actual jobs or declared decorative population sample. Unemployment and shortages affect public support, revenue and output with explainable lag; do not penalize identity. Welfare metric comprises transparent components, with accessible breakdown. Housing growth without jobs or water is not a free population bonus. Provide civic projects, gardens and scholarship/cultural commissions that require actual resources, deliver meaningful benefits and produce visually rewarding outcomes. Refugee arrival events are fictional scenario instances, emphasize safety and housing needs without dehumanizing language. Offer relief, temporary shelters, allocation and negotiated aid.

## Real-time defensive simulation
Defense is fictionalized medieval strategy, not a historical attack reconstruction. Support fortification health, preparedness, upkeep, supplies, repair, morale, civilian protection, abstract opponent units and AI pressure; resource scarcity and political decisions affect outcomes. Use readable threat tiers, bounded forecasts, pausable orders, response alternatives and a clear after-action report. Do not implement graphic violence, real-world tactical guidance, or rewards for harming civilians. Opposing forces represent historical political entities in period, never modern nations, religions or ethnic groups. No dehumanization or religious superiority. Historical scenario facts and fictional battle mechanics separate.

Each defensive scenario has setup, warning, escalation, opportunity for diplomatic/relief action, resolution and recovery. Opponent AI responds to game-defined objectives and difficulty, not arbitrary hidden resource creation; document exceptions. Model pressure through abstract readiness, supplies, health, contested control and morale. Permit negotiation, relief, withdrawal, temporary losses and recovery rather than requiring annihilation. Noncombat wins: preserve population, reach truce, maintain food and water, safeguard cultural assets or negotiate safe evacuation. Avoid a mandatory wall-building optimal strategy across all scenarios. Do not allow a single decorative castle to solve all crises. Test wall connectivity, construction costs, health invariants, civilian safe routes (abstract game simulation), AI path validity, scenario fairness, pause/save during crisis and post-crisis recovery.

## Diplomacy and royal council
Fictional advisers present two or three meaningful options with known consequences, uncertainty and longer-term commitments. Track relations by political actor, trade access, tribute obligations, reputation, treaty duration, trust and internal factions. No random personality stereotyping. Council examples: negotiate a temporary tariff concession vs invest in domestic resilience; accept a refugee relief commitment vs request mutual aid; trade agreement with delivery obligations vs retain reserves; reserve money for maintenance vs expand agriculture. Preview cash cost, time, public impact and risk; apply decisions at deterministic tick. Log explicit agreement state and expiry. No infinite negotiation reward via cancellation or repeated acceptance. AI should respond to past fulfilled/broken obligations. Treaty terms and historical context clearly labeled as fictional simulation. Distinct victory conditions tied to diplomacy, civilian welfare and preservation.

## Interface and integration
Construction mode opens relevant module palette and placement ghost; Economy/Agriculture mode hides ghost, preserving uncommitted plan only if user confirms. Defense mode displays current threat, fortress condition and preparedness; Overview has one prioritized alert. Royal council scene may show fictional advisers in historically informed architecture with accessible dialogue and decision cards. All effects must be inspectable from state and ledger. Expose versioned data-only construction, diplomacy event and scenario definitions through SDK; do not expose raw mutable state or unsafe behavior execution. Add blueprint cross-game test with Architects after compatibility verified.
