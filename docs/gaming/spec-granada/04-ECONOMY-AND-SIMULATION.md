# Economy, agriculture, logistics and simulation design
## Authoritative state and units
Run a deterministic fixed-step simulation (e.g. one game hour, with day/season aggregates; choose actual interval through profiling and ADR). Rendering interpolates snapshots; no rules tied to FPS or wall-clock speed. Model household groups, employment and representative visible agents rather than individual full-fidelity simulation for tens of thousands of citizens. Entity IDs, tick index, PRNG seed/state, scenario version, content digest and mod lock determine replay. Order commands by tick and sequence; use stable iteration and integer or fixed-point state for quantities/money, overflow guards and explicit rounding. Avoid `Math.random()` or wall time in authoritative rules. Different cameras must not alter simulation. Pausing stops authoritative time; app suspension does not accrue unattended gameplay losses. Use deterministic tests for identical commands across frame rates, save/load and replay.

Ledger accounts include treasury available/reserved, income, expenses, liabilities if enabled, inventory by commodity/location/lot, reservations, in-transit goods, storage capacity, production WIP, losses and consumption. Specify commodity units and currency precision; prices in dinars per gameplay unit, minor units in code. Food reserves can be normalized nutrition-equivalent units, but distinguish them from wheat stocks to prevent double counting. Water measured as modeled volume/flow or explicitly labeled supply ratio, not unexplained percent. Report rate period (day/month/season) next to rates. Make numerical assumptions inspectable in tooltip and journal. No negative inventory, no cash creation via refunds or duplicate delivery, no stock counted simultaneously in warehouse and caravan. Transaction changes are atomic: all validated mutations commit or none do.

## Agriculture and water
Plots carry area, soil class/fertility, crop, growth stage, planted tick, harvest window, irrigation access, workers, weather stress and yield estimate. Crop definitions have suitable region/season, days-to-mature range, water/labor demand, storage rules and source classification. Candidate wheat, barley, olives, grapes, figs, almonds, citrus, vegetables and mulberry require historical/seasonal verification; avoid visually treating all crops as ripe in autumn. Show olive harvest and stored grain or stubble if validated calendar requires. Use a seasonal cycle with distinct spring planting, summer water stress, crop-specific harvest windows and winter storage/maintenance. No literal universal four-season calendar for every crop.

Water network nodes: sources, reservoir, canal, junction, farm demand, town demand, mills. Edges have capacity, condition, loss and directional flow if applicable. Simplify hydraulic behavior to bounded flow network; no professional engineering claims. Water delivered cannot exceed source+released stored water after losses, no cyclic free flow; reservoir never negative. Water priority rules configurable with preview of impact on households and farms. Weather seeded with bounded forecasts; prevent invisible irreversible surprise losses. Labor allocation consumes work hours and wages, prevents simultaneous full employment. Households consume food and water; unmet needs lower welfare, productivity and migration gradually with warnings. Soil yield uses documented fictional formula: potentialYield × soilFactor × growthCompletion × waterFactor × laborFactor × weatherFactor, clamped to defined ranges; actual harvested output limited by maturity, work and destination storage. Explicitly account for surplus stored, immediate sale/processing, field backlog or loss; never silently destroy or duplicate.

## Production and transport
Proposed versioned recipe examples, entirely fictional gameplay tuning pending balance: 10 wheat units → 8 flour + accounted waste/byproduct; 8 flour + water/fuel/labor → 8 bread; 5 olive units → 1 oil unit + accounted byproduct/waste; silk chain includes mulberry leaves, silkworm/cocoon stages, reeling and textile workshop (never instant leaves-to-cloth); clay, fuel and labor → ceramics; quarry, hauling and labor → stone construction stock. Recipes specify input/output units, processing time, worker slots, power/water/fuel, capital/upkeep, byproducts and operating cost. Do not equate different units by mass without conversion definitions. No free processing by repeated start/cancel. Stop when input or output capacity unavailable; show bottleneck and queued jobs. Warehouse lots retain owner, location, quality/expiry, reservation and cost basis. Granary food cannot be double-counted as warehouse stock.

Transport graph edges have source/destination, distance, travel time, condition, allowed mode, capacity and tariff. Shipments reserve goods then load, depart, travel, unload; physical caravan agents represent actual shipment IDs or documented aggregation. Market arrival—not animation end—commits delivery. Support delay, loss, alternative route, partial delivery, cancellation and safe retry. Display delivery estimate and reason for changes. Warehouse space at destination reserved where needed. Ports and trade links extend regional network; real sailing/walking distances not shortened to match local art. Ship travel and external markets can initially be modeled strategically while local scenes remain detailed.

## Markets and transaction semantics
For each market and good, maintain finite supply, demand budget, inventory and replenishment, price history, bid/ask with nonnegative spread. Player buys at ask, sells at bid; tooltip explicitly states viewpoint. Price formula may use bounded supply/demand pressure with smoothing and positive floor; clamp volatility, reserve funds and quote TTL in ticks. Requote if size materially changes price; show quantity, total cost, fees, available stock, post-trade reserves and confirmation. An NPC market cannot pay unlimited money; model liquidity. If market and player are same owner, internal transfer never creates external revenue. Transaction IDs make retries idempotent; cancellation releases reserved cash and goods exactly once. Simulated market network rewards transport and specialization, not instant teleportation. Sale quantity reduces inventory; buying debits treasury and increases stock only once goods settle.

### V3 selected olive estate correction
6,800 expected raw olives × raw bid 28 = 190,400 gross if all accepted at that bid, not 435,200 (that would multiply by oil bid). Available storage 5,200 → 1,600 units overflow. If only 5,200 can be stored/sold at the stated price, conditional gross 145,600. An actual forecast must account for finite demand, allocation, quality, labor, transport, processing and market slippage. Offer split harvest, immediate hauling/processing, sell existing stock to free capacity, build capacity or accept spoilage. Preview costs and time. For fictional recipe 5:1, 6,800 raw olives yield at most 1,360 oil units before additional losses/capacity constraints; at oil bid 64 gross 87,040. This particular V3 mock price combination makes processing less attractive than raw sale at equal units and unlimited demand; **do not force these illustrative prices into final tuning**. Calibrate unit definitions and regional demand, seasonal storage, prices and yield such that processing sometimes pays and raw export is viable too. Check margin against opportunity cost of raw inputs, not just accounting cost. Keep test oracle for UI arithmetic separate from balanced economy.

### V3 Málaga export contract
200 oil units; 12-day deadline; revenue 16,000, allocated production cost 8,000, transportation 480, handling 320, estimated accounting profit 7,200. Local sale at 64 = 12,800 gross, 4,800 accounting margin if same cost and no extra local fees; incremental contract advantage 2,400 before risk and alternative constraints. If sourcing all oil at current ask 82, acquisition cost is 16,400, and profit after listed costs is −1,200: show unattractive option, don't pretend production cost applies to purchased goods. Explain that historical production cost may be sunk; actual cash movement is 16,000 less new expenses, while profit report includes cost basis. If cargo is already reserved, no duplicate reservation. If deadline missed, enforce disclosed penalties, allow partial settlement per terms; never issue reward twice. Display expected downside and confidence, not guaranteed profit.

### Winter reserves
V3 food 36,400 against quest goal 40,000: 91% of target, short 3,600. “Adequate” may only mean current short-term sufficiency; show projected days of supply and explicit winter target separately. Population 72,360 and those illustrative food units are not a historical census or daily individual ration. Define consumption conversion. Reserve policy blocks or confirms sales that breach selected safety floor; allow expert override with explanation. Winter countdown from scenario calendar, not real date. Show forecast variance. No background deterioration while closed.

## Balance lab and instrumentation
Run headless deterministic simulations with strategy bots: subsistence-first, export-specialist, infrastructure-first, mixed, greedy, random legal, deliberate exploit, defensive prioritization. At least 1,000 seeded campaigns over diverse seeds/difficulties (target, report actual count). Use parameter sweeps and sensitivity; log bankruptcy, hunger, wealth growth, choice distributions, completion, storage/transport bottlenecks, AI difficulty. Check conserved quantities and cash ledger every tick, no overflow/NaN, deterministic hashes, market round trips, cancel/retry, concurrent order, partial deliveries, price spikes, repeated recipe rollback, modded extreme data. No single crop or policy should dominate all scenarios. Verify at least three genuinely distinct viable strategies with outcome evidence. Human playtests judge whether meaningful tension feels enjoyable; successful bot does not prove fun. Version balance changes and maintain regression fixtures.
