## What the count comes to

The founder's note said about 50 subjects: 40 in English, and 40 in Arabic of which 30 repeat an English subject and 10 are new. Checked file by file, that is exact: **50 subjects; 30 in both languages, 10 in English only, 10 in Arabic only.** The ten Arabic-only subjects are the ten he named. The ten English-only ones are al-Tusi, al-Shatranj, al-Karaji, al-Masudi, Ibn al-Baytar, al-Qazwini, the Monkey and the Tortoise, Nana Asma'u, Usama ibn Munqidh and Samarqand.

Two things the count hides. The English picture `en-02` is titled for the elephant clock and the Arabic `ar-24` for al-Jazari: one subject. The English story of al-Khwarizmi is "The Star Counters" and the Arabic one "Father of Numbers": two tellings of one subject.

**The thumbnails are pictures and titles for a video series. They are not historical sources, and nothing below is a historical claim.** A museum record is existing source material that has itself not been reviewed for this game; every subject stands as "historical review pending" ([the ledger](../historical-review/ledger.md)).
