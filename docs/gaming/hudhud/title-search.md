# Hudhud title and name search

_Research note, not legal advice and not clearance. I am not a lawyer. This is a first public search made on 26 November 2026; a trademark lawyer or qualified reviewer must search the exact jurisdictions, classes, transliterations and design marks before the founder chooses or uses a title. No decision is made here._

## Scope and method

Names searched: `Hudhud`; `Hudhud: The Atlas of Wonders`; `Atlas of Wonders`; `Hudhud and the Scattered Atlas`; `Hudhud: Keeper of Stories`; `Hudhud's Book of Wonders`; Arabic «هدهد»; and Arabic «أطلس العجائب».

Places searched: Steam, itch.io, Apple App Store, Google Play, Nintendo.com/eShop search, WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO Trademark Search, EUIPO eSearch, CIPO Canadian Trademarks Database, and general web search. No vendor, registry owner, artist, reviewer or lawyer was contacted; nothing was bought or downloaded as an asset.

Important measurement cautions:

- Store counts are broad relevance counts, not exact-title counts. Steam, Google Play and Apple all tokenize or fuzz multiword searches.

- USPTO basic wordmark search produced very large raw counts for multiword queries because it matches terms broadly. I therefore scanned the first 100 returned records for a normalized exact wordmark rather than treating the raw total as meaningful.

- WIPO was run in “Match exact expression” mode for exact counts. The colon in two English composite names caused the WIPO page not to complete; for those, the punctuationless equivalent was also run and is stated explicitly. WIPO “embedded/contains” counts seen during the first pass were much larger and are not treated as exact conflicts.

- Nintendo multiword searches displayed 40 broad catalogue results, but the extraction captured only the header/filter area. I therefore do **not** claim there were 40 relevant Nintendo matches; I only claim that the bare `Hudhud` and the two Arabic names explicitly showed zero results.

- CIPO’s browser page was blocked in automation, but the public JSON endpoint used by that page returned real results. Those endpoint results are what is recorded.

## Bottom line, without deciding

A bare **Hudhud** title is crowded in exactly the children’s education/story market this game enters. **Atlas of Wonders** is already publicly used and also collides with the museum’s own game Atlas. The longer composite English titles had no exact matches in the limited register/store searches run here, but they inherit either the crowded Hudhud element, the Atlas collision, or both. This page is evidence for the founder’s choice, not a recommendation or clearance.
