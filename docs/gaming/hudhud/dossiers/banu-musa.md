## What the records say they did

1. Muhammad, Ahmad and al-Hasan, the sons of Musa ibn Shakir. Ibn al-Nadim describes them as among those who pursued the ancient sciences to the utmost. — source location `content/en/group/banu-musa.md — Overview`; citation `{cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 378-379}`; flag: **stated**.
2. They spent generously on it and exhausted themselves in it. They sent to the land of the Romans for someone to bring them books, and brought translators from distant regions with generous pay. The sciences that dominated them were geometry, mechanical devices, motion, music and astronomy. — source location `content/en/group/banu-musa.md — Pursuit of learning`; citation `{cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 378-379}`; flag: **stated**.
3. Muhammad ibn Musa died in Rabi' al-Awwal of the year 259 AH. Ahmad had a son called Mutahhar, who joined the boon companions of al-Mu'tadid. — source location `content/en/group/banu-musa.md — Their history`; citation `{cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 378-379}`; flag: **stated**.
4. Among their books are "The Book of the Sons of Musa on the Pneumatics", and "Mechanical Devices" by Ahmad, "The Elongated Round Figure" by al-Hasan, "The Motion of the First Sphere" by Muhammad, and "Conic Sections". — source location `content/en/group/banu-musa.md — Their books`; citation `{cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 378-379}`; flag: **stated**.
