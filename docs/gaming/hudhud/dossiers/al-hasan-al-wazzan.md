## Who, where, when

- **Who:** Arabic: الحسن بن محمد الوزان. English record label: Leo Africanus. Record labels: `person/leo-africanus` en='Leo Africanus', ar='الحسن بن محمد الوزان ⁦(ليون الأفريقي)⁩'; `place/fez` en='Fez', ar='فاس'; `place/granada` en='Granada', ar='غرناطة'; `place/tunis` en='Tunis', ar='تونس'
- **Where:** The museum record is filed as Leo Africanus; the dossier keeps both names and does not treat either as a game simplification.
- **When:** `person/leo-africanus` `born`: 1494~; The year is an estimate from his self-reported ages, as the English article sets out following Masonen: he reports that he was twelve when the Portuguese took Safi in 1507 and sixteen when he visited Timbuktu in 1509-10. The Arabic article's infobox gives 1488, while its text reports historians placing his birth at 901 AH/1495 or at 906 AH/1500, and Wikidata carries six competing values (1483, 1490, 1494, 1496, 1500). No Hijri year is recorded: the followed estimate, about 1494, spans 899-900 AH, and the only explicit pairing in the sources read (901 AH with 1495) belongs to a reading that is not followed. He was born at Granada, and his family moved to Fez soon after his birth. `person/leo-africanus` `died`: 1554~; Both articles close his life about 1554 and the English infobox names Tunis as the place of death, but the end is not documented and the English article sets out three theories: that he stayed in Rome and died there about 1550; that he left before the Sack of Rome in 1527, returned to North Africa and lived in Tunis until some time after 1550; and that after Charles V's capture of Tunis in 1535 he moved on to Morocco, his second home. Wikidata's death values (1548, 1550, and even 1600) restate the same uncertainty. No Hijri year is recorded.
