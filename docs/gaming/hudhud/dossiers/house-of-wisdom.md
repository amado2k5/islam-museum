5. Al-Ma'mun, after making peace with the ruler of Cyprus, received a library of Greek books, which Sahl ibn Harun managed. — source location `content/en/group/bayt-al-hikma.md — Ahmad Amin's account`; citation `{cite:duha-islam p.426-427}`; flag: **stated**.
