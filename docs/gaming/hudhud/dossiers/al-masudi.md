## Thumbnail elements without record support

The atlas title is The Man Who Wrote the Meadows of Gold. No record read supports a literal meadow of gold, modern documentary narration, or every report being eyewitness testimony. Treat these as design fiction unless a reviewer supplies support.

## Three candidate quest premises

These are proposals for Claude and the founder, not content.

1. A journey problem asks the player to apply Route, Journal and Network to a documented leg or account, with uncertainty visible rather than hidden.

2. Hudhud helps compare what a traveller says, what a later record says and what the thumbnail invents, using Talk plus Route, Journal and Network.

3. The quest returns a page to the Atlas, with optional context, a hidden collectible and replay goals; this structure is game design only, not historical content.

## Sensitivities

Travel accounts may touch war, slavery, illness, death, sect and living disputes. Do not turn real trauma into amusement; label literary or uncertain reportage; invented dialogue must be dramatized paraphrase.
