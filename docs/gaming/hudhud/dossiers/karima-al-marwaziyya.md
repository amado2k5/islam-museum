4. Ibn al-Jawzi writes that Karima was a respected hadith teacher, with scholars like al-Khatib and Ibn al-Muttalib among her students, and she died in Mecca in 463 AH. — source location `content/en/person/karima-al-marwaziyya.md — Students and death`; citation `{cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 16 p. 135} {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi vol. 16 p. 135}`; flag: **stated**.
