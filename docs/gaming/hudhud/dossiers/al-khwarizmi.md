## Who, where, when

- **Who:** Arabic: الخوارزمي. English: al-Khwarizmi; the prose gives Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi and also records the different kunya Abu Ja‘far in EI2.
- **Where:** Baghdad (`place/baghdad`, [33.3406, 44.4009]) and Sinjār for the meridian event.
- **When:** The data record gives birth 0780/0164 and death 0850/0235, both marked tradition. The prose warns that the Cairo editors do not consider the birth and death dates certain; EI2 instead places him about 184–232 AH / 800–847 CE. Event titles used here date the meridian measurement to 827 and Surat al-ard to 833.
