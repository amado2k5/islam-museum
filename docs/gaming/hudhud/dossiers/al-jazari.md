## Who, where, when

- **Who:** Arabic: الجزري، إسماعيل بن الرزاز. English: Al-Jazari, Ismail ibn al-Razzaz.
- **Where:** Amid (`place/amid`, [40.23, 37.91]); the elephant-clock book belongs to the Artuqid court there, while the surviving folio is later and not assigned to Amid.
- **When:** Born 1136 and died 1206 in the person record, both derived year-only Gregorian dates. The device book is documented as completed in 1205; the illustrated elephant-clock folio is dated 715 AH/1315 CE and is later.
