# Publishing, discoverability and honest review strategy
Canonical Ultra remains uncompromised on islam.museum. Separate compatible builds for desktop portals or lightweight puzzle editions. A curated portal may require mobile, smaller assets, proprietary SDK, ads, network constraints or exclusivity; verify at submission, do not sign or publish without approval. No fake reviews, votes, ranking manipulation, unsolicited mass messaging or guarantee of acceptance. No forced ads canonical.

Create eligibility matrix per game/profile with source/date, requirements, test, pass/fail/not applicable, blocker and cost. Portal SDK adapters separate from simulation, no network on canonical by default. Validate iframe permissions, no top-navigation escape, resizing, focus pause, audio mute, fullscreen, storage restrictions, cross-origin isolation, relative asset paths and offline behavior if required. No unapproved external links or ad integration. Portable ZIP root index, license manifest, version, tested browser support, controls, thumbnail/animated thumbnail, actual gameplay and disclosure. If canonical cannot meet requirements, say not eligible, do not cripple it.

## Press strategy
Localized press page per game: concise hook, gameplay trailer (actual 30–60 sec), 3–6 real images with captions, clean logo, fact sheet, hardware, controls/accessibility, developer story, AI-assisted process accurately attributed, release date/status, credits, contact per museum policy. Capture 16:9 plus vertical clips; no concept art presented as actual. Pitch Golden Age as richly sourced browser city builder, House as real engineering puzzles, Architects as shareable creations, Atlas as global daily game, Knowledge Web as graph-driven discovery. Reviewer demo is a polished full loop, not misleading trailer. Offer honest test build, ask for feedback and respond to issues. Do not request positive scores. Review platforms like OpenCritic/Metacritic have editorial eligibility; investigate current criteria, never assume submission or score. Track legitimate referral and player feedback with privacy consent.
