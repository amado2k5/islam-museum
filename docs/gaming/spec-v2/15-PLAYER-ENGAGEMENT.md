# Player engagement, retention and sharing
Design for intrinsic motivation: mastery, autonomy, discovery, creative expression, narrative stakes and competition by consent. Daily is a format, not the whole experience. Golden Age persistent cities; Silk Road trade networks and character arcs; House inventions; Lost Manuscripts episodic cases; ChronoQuest narrative secrets; Atlas daily geography; Dynasty replayable alternate scenarios; Relics exhibitions; Knowledge Web path mastery; Architects creative portfolio. No artificial energy, loot boxes, forced ads, streak penalties, loss due to absence, dark patterns or mandatory registration. Pause/exit anytime. Archive limited-time content where practical. Difficulty transparent, hint cost disclosed, practice unlimited, no paid advantages.

First 30s: immediate meaningful verb, clear goal and surprising reveal. Five minutes: complete a decision loop and see consequence. Hour: unlock a genuinely new system or completed campaign section. Week: optional harder challenge or creation worth sharing. Month: emergent mastery, new authored content or community designs, not escalating chores. Each GDD includes failure and recovery, alternate strategy and share moment. Introduce Living History themes (astronomy, trade, architecture), with each game interpreting a shared theme independently. Archive after event; no real-time server required for seeded themes.

## Sharing toolkit
Photo mode with safe camera, time of day, depth of field and hide HUD; export actual image with optional attribution. Replay theater uses pinned version/input/state; local screen/video recording where browser APIs support it, explicit user action and consent, no capturing unrelated tabs or audio; no forced watermark. Share seed/score card spoiler-free, deep link to same challenge, invitation without contact scraping. Export world/invention/exhibit bundle for remix if license permits; show creator credit. Public galleries, voting and competitions later only with moderation, spam/abuse controls, privacy and approval. No fabricated social counters. Ranked scores require authority; local comparisons labeled unverified.

## Measurement
Optional privacy-respecting aggregate events: start, first meaningful action, complete loop, return, voluntary share, related exhibit visit, crash, frame quality bucket; avoid precise fingerprinting/PII. Document consent, retention and opt-out. Proposed targets, not forecasts: 70% first-loop completion, D1 35%, D7 15%, D30 8%, share 5%, exhibit click 15%. Define cohort (new consenting players), UTC windows, denominator and missing data, separate by game; small samples report counts, not misleading percentages. Qualitative playtest asks what was fun, confusing, tedious and what they would share; observe voluntary replay and time to first action. No metric substitutes for fun. Public claims require real evidence and uncertainty.
