# Art production and audio direction
Use [visual atlas](20-VISUAL-REFERENCE-ATLAS.md) and GDD references as a brief, not literal historical reconstruction or final textures. Each title has a palette, camera language, silhouettes, material vocabulary and soundscape. Before building all assets, produce a style frame in the actual renderer, a ten-second movement capture, material sheet and one interactive environment; independent reviewers compare readability and atmosphere, not just fidelity to concept art. Avoid common AI prototype failures: identical cube buildings, repetitive patterns, swimming textures, flickering shadows, ungrounded props, fake Arabic script, scale inconsistencies, ugly HUDs and overexposed bloom.

## Pipeline
1. Reference registry: real museum record, documented period/location, rights/credit, confidence, allowed use and visual reconstruction note. Curator reviews landmarks, ornament and clothing; invented props explicitly fictional. Avoid sacred/forbidden depictions. No scraping copyrighted artwork into shipped assets. Respect individual image rights and source license.
2. Asset authoring: evaluate headless Blender/Python/Geometry Nodes for modular walls, arches, domes, windows, roads, terrain, vegetation, props and instruments; author hero silhouettes separately. Players never need Blender. Use reproducible seeds, unit scale meters, right-handed coordinate conventions documented with import conversion, pivots, collision meshes and LOD. Produce PBR textures with calibrated roughness/normal/metallic; texel density consistency; glTF validation and compressed asset variants. Characters use fictional silhouettes, region-appropriate clothing, walk/work/idle sets, animation blend and IK where justified; no generated caricature.
3. Art QA: check silhouette at gameplay camera, material response in day/night, animation foot sliding, intersections, seams, repetitive texture, collisions and readable signage. Render previews at 1440p and UI zoom. Label actual uncertainty; use readable vetted Arabic/Urdu typesetting rather than generated pseudo-text. Original or licensed music/sound, adjustable ambience/music/effects/voice volume, spatial mix where helpful, captions for cues. No audio autoplay before player gesture. Capture sound on/off and hearing-accessibility tests.
4. Asset catalog: immutable ID, source URL, license SPDX or precise terms, author, attribution, historical confidence, game, geometry stats, texture size, bytes, LOD, collision, animation, generated-by metadata and checksums. Reject absent license; keep large originals out of routine catalog build; use approved asset storage and incremental compilation.

## Visual production deliverables
Per title produce actual 16:9 gameplay capture, HUD layout, scene detail, menu, settings, result/failure, creator preview, motion clip and seven-language/RTL examples. For 3D add lighting comparison, materials sheet and asset silhouettes. V2 supplies concept and layout references to bootstrap; create actual engine screenshots as M1/M4–M7 deliverables, not more synthetic mockups in lieu of implementation. Art approval requires visual consistency, depth cues, contrast, intentional composition and fluid motion, not an arbitrary asset count. The in-engine look must be impressive with UI hidden *and* readable with HUD visible.
