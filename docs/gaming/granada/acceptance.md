# Granada: acceptance gates, real status

_Updated 25 November 2026. The criteria are the founder's ([`../spec-granada/contracts/acceptance.json`](../spec-granada/contracts/acceptance.json)) and are not edited here; only the status and the last column change, and only with something that was actually run. They extend the platform's R01 to R18 ([`../acceptance.md`](../acceptance.md)) and replace none of them. The machine-readable copy is [`acceptance.json`](acceptance.json)._

Of 26 gates: 1 passed (the discovery gate, on the author's own evidence), 15 have partial evidence, 10 are untouched. **Granada can be played from a drawn plan and in a 3D valley generated from code, on a development build and on the games' UAT address; the valley is far from the concept image, and no person has played it.** Every gate is a hard gate.
