# The Lantern: state of the game

_28 November 2026. Release status: **released**, on the founder's decision of that day. Code: `gaming/games/lantern/` and `gaming/packages/daily/`. Evidence: [../evidence/lantern/release-2026-11-28.md](../evidence/lantern/release-2026-11-28.md)._

One of the four small daily games. What it is, where its content comes from and what is checked: [daily-games.md](daily-games.md).

- **Released without a person's playtest, a native reader of five of its seven languages, or a real phone.** The evidence page says so, and lists these as the next checks.
- **Loads only its own phrases' names:** 30 to 40 KB compressed a language, where it loaded 140 to 204 KB.
- **Played whole with the keyboard** in all seven languages, on a phone-sized and a desktop screen, in light and dark, by `gaming/tools/lantern-play.ts` on the local host.
