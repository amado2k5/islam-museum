# Owner decision for Claude: Candidate C palace, artist path, vegetation and review gates

Claude,

Record and execute the following numbered instructions as the owner's decision. They supersede any ambiguity about whether to integrate the Candidate C palace, continue scripted hero art, or engage outside art help. They do not authorize a purchase, production deployment, removal of prototype labels, historical approval, or mass asset production.

1. **Use a controlled hybrid path, not option 1 or option 2 alone.** First integrate the existing Candidate C palace into the running game as a reversible evaluation of this one asset under the real strategic camera, terrain, lighting, shadows, atmosphere and UI. In parallel, prepare a professional environment-artist brief, a small paid-art-test specification, a public-domain vegetation survey and a request for non-binding quotes. Do not hire, buy or download production assets until I approve a specific proposal.

2. **Do not replace Set A by default.** Put the palace on a Candidate C branch from current `main` and expose it on UAT through a clearly labelled candidate release, query flag or equivalent reversible selector. Set A remains the default known baseline. Do not change saves. Record the prior and candidate release identifiers and a rollback procedure.

3. **The UAT integration is an evaluation, not proof-frame acceptance.** It may use the present 214,166-triangle palace initially so that its appearance can be judged honestly. It must not be described as accepted, optimized, production-ready, historically accurate, or as the Alhambra. Keep `historical review pending` and the existing fictional/reconstruction wording.

4. **Before any further merge or UAT deployment, resolve the five hidden automated-review flags on PRs #8316 and #8317.** The public GitHub review records reveal only that there are three flags on #8316 and two on #8317; their contents are not posted. Retrieve the actual reports from Devin Review, or perform and record an equivalent independent review of the exact merged diffs if the reports cannot be obtained. For each flag record: source, affected file/line, severity, validity, correction or reason for dismissal, test, reviewer and date. Fix valid defects in follow-up PRs. Local branch work and documentation may continue while this happens, but do not merge another Candidate C code change or deploy its UAT preview with five unknown findings outstanding.

5. **Visual decision on the renders:** Claude's diagnosis is correct. Close up, the palace still reads as assembled procedural geometry: broad flat wall planes, uniformly sharp edges, repeated merlons/corbels/windows, regular hole patterns, limited material breakup and simplified box/lump vegetation. The revision improved colour, wear visibility, the gate and the overall composition, but did not solve that underlying look. At distance the silhouette, tower hierarchy, roofs, courts and fortified mass read clearly, so it is credible as a *provisional strategic-view background landmark*. That judgement is conditional on the real target camera. It is not sufficient for Photo Mode, first-person or a close target comparison without artist rework.

6. **Claude was right to stop scripted final-art production after the palace.** Do not spend another full scripted-art cycle on the estate and street cluster. The palace is the hero piece most naturally suited to scripted walls and towers; if it still fails close, the more organic, inhabited and vegetation-heavy estate/street work is unlikely to prove the method. This is the intended stop/go economy, even though the earlier sentence named three pieces. Claude may make or retain simple footprint/blockout geometry for the estate, street cluster, gate/wall and construction site so an artist can preserve gameplay placement, selection and collision. Those blockouts are not Candidate C final art.

7. **Evaluate the palace in the game at its real visual sizes.** Capture the fixed proof camera at 1920×1080 and 1280×800 on Low, Medium and High, plus the nearest allowed strategic zoom and the farthest Photo Mode view currently in scope. Record the palace's pixel height/width in each view. Capture the identical state with Set A and Candidate C. Include daylight, the approved autumn/harvest state, shadow casting and the atmospheric settings used by the proof frame. Check terrain intersections, hill fit, footprint expansion from 76×50 m to 105×70 m, wall/road/water conflicts, selection, collision proxy, shadow acne, z-fighting and loading failure fallback.

8. **Measure, do not infer, performance.** For each view/preset record median, p95 and p99 frame time; frames above 16.7 and 33.3 ms; draw calls; rendered triangles including shadow passes; geometries; materials; textures; GPU texture estimate where available; JS heap; load/decode time and transfer bytes. Run a ten-minute camera/selection/reload cycle and confirm resource counts return to a plateau. These are developer-machine measurements only until the named M1 Air and Windows i5 operators run the suite.

9. **Keep the palace only if the in-game result passes both conditions:** (a) at the binding strategic camera it materially improves the target comparison and reads as a coherent landmark without exposing its close-up defects; and (b) it fits the whole-scene performance and memory budget. If either fails, do not invest in scripted LOD polishing; include the palace itself in the artist brief. If both pass, keep its scripted layout/base as an artist-editable background asset, while requiring a later near-view treatment for Photo Mode and first-person.

10. **Use an art-directed LOD and streaming plan.** The current 214,166 triangles are not automatically unacceptable for one static landmark, but triangle count alone is not a pass. Nineteen materials, shadow passes, texture decoding, draw calls and the rest of the scene matter. Use these provisional budgets, subject to the minimum-machine measurements:
   - `LOD0`, near Photo Mode/authoring source: up to about 180,000–220,000 triangles, loaded only when a close view needs it.
   - `LOD1`, normal strategic proof camera: target 45,000–70,000 triangles.
   - `LOD2`, distant strategic view: target 10,000–20,000 triangles.
   - Optional very-far impostor/silhouette: about 2,000–5,000 triangles or a baked impostor.
   - Reduce the runtime palace to roughly 4–8 material groups/atlases where the appearance permits; preserve source materials in the `.blend` file.
   - Keep LODs in separate loadable files or ensure the loader loads exactly one representation. Do not place all LOD meshes in one scene and render or retain all of them unnecessarily.
   - LODs must be artist-reviewed for silhouette, arches, roof lines and material seams; automatic decimation may be used for a temporary benchmark, not accepted final art.

11. **The outside role is a senior real-time environment artist with technical-art capability**, not a generic architectural visualizer. Require a portfolio showing shipped or demonstrable game/web real-time environments, modular historic architecture, believable weathering and lived-in detail, Blender, PBR baking/texturing, clean UVs, retopology, art-directed LODs, texture atlases, collision proxies, glTF/GLB export and optimization for integrated/mobile-class GPUs. Three.js/WebGL/WebGPU experience is strongly preferred; otherwise require willingness to work to measured browser budgets. Historical approval remains a separate qualified-review role.

12. **Run a small paid art test before awarding the full package.** After I separately approve spending, commission one representative near-camera slice—preferably the estate entrance/courtyard or the gate plus one wall bay—covering final modelling, weathering, PBR textures, one construction/damaged or maintenance state if relevant, LOD0/1/2, collision, source files and browser-ready export. Estimate: 24–40 artist hours, about USD 1,500–4,000, and roughly 4–8 working days. These are planning estimates, not quotes. Use the test to judge visual quality, communication, source-file cleanliness, optimization and adherence to the museum's provenance rules before contracting the larger set.

13. **The artist brief must contain:** the binding target and fixed proof camera; the current game captures and palace renders; metric scale, footprints, pivots, axes and naming; gameplay selection/collision/construction requirements; exact near/normal/far screen sizes; triangle, material, texture, draw-call and memory budgets; LOD switching rules; lighting and season states; required silhouette and architectural variation; close-up defects to avoid; target material/weathering language; historical-review-pending wording; prohibited unsupported claims; no use of the name Alhambra; allowed source/licence rules; accessibility/readability needs; delivery milestones; one review round at blockout, material test and final; and acceptance tests in the actual browser game.

14. **Require these artist deliverables for every asset/package:**
   - Original editable `.blend` sources with non-destructive/high-resolution sources where applicable.
   - Valid glTF 2.0 `.glb`/`.gltf` runtime exports with metric scale, named nodes, stable origins/pivots and no missing external files.
   - Separate `LOD0`, `LOD1` and `LOD2` outputs or the project's documented LOD metadata, plus simple collision/selection proxies.
   - UVs and master PBR maps: base colour, normal, roughness, metallic where needed and AO; no baked scene lighting in base colour.
   - PNG/TIFF master textures and optimized KTX2/BasisU runtime textures with mipmaps; texture atlas/source files included.
   - Triangle, vertex, primitive/draw-group, material, texture-dimension, decoded-memory and file-size report per LOD.
   - Construction-stage meshes/props where the game visibly builds the asset; the street cluster must preserve logical selectable buildings or documented selection anchors.
   - Turntables and fixed-camera captures, plus an in-game validation build.
   - A provenance/licence manifest for every texture, scan, brush, model fragment and reference that enters the deliverable; disclose any generative-AI use before using it.
   - Written rights allowing the museum to use, modify, redistribute with the game, preserve and migrate the delivered assets worldwide and perpetually, plus delivery of all source files. No undisclosed marketplace assets.

15. **Planning estimate for the full outside-art package:** budget approximately 320–560 artist hours for the estate, modular street cluster of about eight houses, gate/wall segment, construction site, shared material kit, LODs, exports, QA and ordinary revisions. For one strong specialist this is about 8–14 full-time working weeks, often 10–16 calendar weeks with feedback. At an expert freelance rate of roughly USD 50–100/hour, the planning range is approximately USD 16,000–56,000 before taxes, platform fees, historical review and substantial scope changes. A practical target bid for a capable independent artist using modular reuse is roughly USD 20,000–35,000. These are my estimates, not supplier quotes or approved spending. Obtain at least three comparable fixed-milestone quotes after the paid test brief is ready.

16. **Estimate allocation, for quote comparison only:** estate 50–90 hours; eight-house modular street cluster 110–190; gate/wall 40–75; construction site and props 50–90; shared material kit, LODs, exports, integration support and revisions 70–115. Quotes far below this should be checked for missing LODs, source files, unique texturing, browser optimization, revisions or rights. Quotes above it may be justified by exceptional bespoke ornament, research or senior technical-art work.

17. **Use public-domain scanned vegetation only as source material, not unchanged runtime vegetation.** Poly Haven's assets are CC0, but that does not make an arbitrary scan historically suitable or browser-ready. Survey exact species and licence records first. For any chosen scan: retopologize, simplify, atlas textures, create mipmaps/KTX2, make at least three LODs, use alpha-tested foliage carefully, add wind weights, vary colour/scale/rotation, and instance repeated vegetation. Avoid shipping raw photogrammetry or many unique 4K/8K textures.

18. **Use a mixed vegetation system suited to the two 8 GB minimum machines:**
   - A small number of artist-cleaned hero olive/cypress/citrus trees near the estate, about 8,000–20,000 triangles at near LOD, 1,500–4,000 at strategic LOD and 200–800 or an impostor at distance.
   - Instanced low-cost orchard variants for the middle distance, sharing one or two atlases.
   - Crops and ground cover as instanced clumps/cards, not individual blades; aggressively distance-cull them and reduce shadow casting.
   - Hedges as irregular modular foliage/cards with silhouette variation, not solid boxes and not raw dense scans.
   - Prefer opaque or alpha-clipped foliage where possible; control transparency overdraw and disable distant per-plant shadows.
   - Use `THREE.InstancedMesh`/equivalent batching and explicit disposal/lifecycle tests. The survey must record visible quality, triangles, materials, textures, decoded GPU memory, licence and species confidence for each candidate.

19. **The unmet “hand-authored or artist-reviewed” condition blocks acceptance and scaling, not evaluation.** It means the present palace cannot satisfy the art-direction gate, cannot authorize mass production and cannot be called production art. It does not block putting this one asset into a reversible UAT evaluation, making LOD experiments, measuring it, preparing blockouts or writing the artist brief. A professional environment artist's authorship/rework or a documented review by a named qualified artist is required before the direction can be accepted. Historical review by qualified named reviewers remains a separate gate.

20. **Return one decision package, then stop for my ruling.** Include: five-flag disposition table; exact branch/PR/base/head SHAs; reversible UAT URL/release and rollback record; Set A/C fixed-camera comparisons; close, normal and far captures; all performance/resource measurements; terrain/placement defects; provisional LOD results; the vegetation survey; artist role/brief; paid-test scope; three non-binding quotes if obtainable without commitment; and a recommendation of `keep palace as strategic base`, `send palace to artist`, or `discard palace`. Do not engage a vendor, buy assets, mass-produce art, deploy to production, remove prototype labels or claim any proof-frame line passed without the already-required evidence and explicit owner acceptance.
