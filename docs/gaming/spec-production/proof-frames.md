# The three proof frames: briefs and the checklist each must pass

_Generated by `npm --prefix gaming run traceability` from [`proof-frames.json`](proof-frames.json); edit that file, not this page. **None is started.**_

**The rule.** Founder, 26 November 2026: one real-time, interactive proof frame for each game before any asset is produced in number, taken from the running browser game. Acceptance is of the direction of quality, never of the whole game. Granada's first, then The Golden Age's, then Atlas's.

**What is handed over.** For each frame: captures at the three presets and a recording of the interaction, kept as media outside git with their checksums in git; the measurements as a JSON file; the time each asset took; and one page setting the capture beside the target.

## 1. Granada (not-started)

The target: [`granada-v3.jpeg`](../spec-granada/assets/granada-v3.jpeg).

- **The camera.** High over the plain, looking north-east: the estate and the fields fill the lower third, the town climbs through the middle, the palace hill, the far hills and the snow range close the top.
- **Foreground.** An olive estate at harvest with its house, press and store; the wheat beside it; the mill on its channel with the wheel turning; a cart on the road.
- **Midground.** The town: tiled houses in streets, a market square, a wall with a tower and a gate, one part of it under scaffolding.
- **Background.** A palace on its hill above the town; terraced slopes; a far port on the sea to the south-west; the snow range.
- **Architecture and materials.** Lime-washed walls with wear at the foot, clay tile with broken courses, dressed and rubble stone, timber, scaffolding. Every building in the view authored in Blender; none left as a generated box.
- **Land, water, planting, air and light.** The plain's patchwork of plots with rows that read at this height; channels with moving water and wet banks; olives, cypresses, poplars and orchard trees modelled for this view; late-afternoon autumn light, haze toward the range, cloud shadow crossing the plain.
- **People at an activity.** The harvest in the olive estate: pickers on ladders, carriers with baskets, a cart being loaded. They are there because the estate is being harvested, and go when it is done.
- **Motion of the world.** Wind in the trees and the wheat, water in the channels and over the wheel, smoke from two chimneys, cloud shadow.
- **Panels that work.** The top bar with the game's stocks; the objectives on the left; the estate's panel in the centre when it is selected; the market's panel on the right with the market's real prices; the season's panel. No rail entry, catalogue or map that does not yet work.
- **Can be selected.** The olive estate and the mill.
- **A change of state that is seen.** Order the harvest: the pickers come, the trees are stripped, the store's figure rises, the estate's panel changes. Or mend the branch: water reaches the wheel and it turns.
- **What is said of history.** The valley is fiction and says so. The palace and the town are shown as a reconstruction, labelled 'historical review pending', and are not named as an accurate picture of any building until a reviewer has seen them.
- **Built on.** The rules of 'Water for the Valley', the valley's land and water, the authored mill, the teal and gold panels.
- **Waits on.** F3 for the measurements; A1, A2 and A3 only as far as this one view needs

## 2. The Golden Age (not-started)

The target: [`golden-age-target.webp`](../spec-production/assets/golden-age-target.webp).

- **Midground.** A library being built: scaffolding, a crane, its ground marked out; the river with a bridge of arches and two boats.
- **Background.** Roofs and domes to a wall, hills beyond, a sky toward evening.

- **Land, water, planting, air and light.** The river with moving water and reflections of the bank; palms and garden planting; evening light with long shadows and haze over the far city.

- **Panels that work.** The top bar with the district's stocks; the research panel with the project in hand; the objectives; the welfare panel with its causes; the building's own panel. Only what works.
- **Can be selected.** The library under construction.
- **A change of state that is seen.** Dig the last link of a canal: water is seen to arrive and the gardens change. Or the building advances a stage as the season ends.
- **What is said of history.** A fictional district inspired by records of Abbasid Baghdad, and labelled so. Its buildings are a reconstruction, 'historical review pending'.
- **Built on.** The rules of the three chapters, the canal network, the museum links.
- **Waits on.** Granada's proof accepted or its lessons written down; F3

## 3. Atlas (not-started)

The target: [`atlas-target.webp`](../spec-production/assets/atlas-target.webp).

- **The camera.** The globe fills the right two thirds, turned to the Mediterranean and the lands east of it, with the edge of night across one side; the round's panel stands on the left.
- **Foreground.** The round's panel: a picture where one qualifies, the clues, the pin and its marker on the globe.
- **Midground.** The lit hemisphere in relief: mountains, plains and rivers that can be read.
- **Background.** The air at the globe's edge, cloud over it, the night side, stars.
- **Architecture and materials.** Not buildings but the globe's surface: relief shaded by the sun's angle, snow and desert told apart, water with a different sheen from land.
- **Land, water, planting, air and light.** Relief, rivers, cloud and air, lit by one sun; the night side dark, and without modern city lights while a round is unanswered.
- **People at an activity.** The image shows no people. Proposed in their place, for the founder to accept or refuse: the reveal itself, in which the answer's marker rises, the arc from the guess is drawn and the record's place opens.
- **Motion of the world.** Cloud drifting over the globe; the edge of night moving.
- **Panels that work.** The round's panel with its clues and progress; the score and the rounds before; the hints with their costs; the century with a timeline; the layers for terrain, mountains and rivers. No overlay or border layer until its data is reviewed.
- **Can be selected.** The answer's place after the reveal, which opens its museum record.
- **A change of state that is seen.** The reveal: the pin, the arc, the points. And a layer switched on or off changes the globe.
- **What is said of history.** Every round is a museum record; nothing is authored. Relief is of the land today. Night lights are of today and are not history: off during a round, labelled where shown.
- **Built on.** The rules, the pack, the lit sphere, the pin and reveal.
- **Waits on.** The founder's approval of the dataset table (O6): no data is brought in before it; F2, F3, F6
