# Granada vegetation survey — public-domain candidates first

Date: 2026-11-26  
Status: survey only; **historical review pending**. Nothing was downloaded, bought, imported, contacted or approved for acquisition. Sizes and counts below are copied from the source pages or their public metadata; where the source does not state a figure and measuring it would require downloading the asset, this survey says **not measured**.

## Scope and method

The owner asked for public-domain candidates first for olive, cypress, citrus, poplar, pomegranate, fig, vine, wheat, vegetables, reeds and hedging, with exact species and licence records where available. I checked Poly Haven's public model index first, then other public-domain catalogues. I did not use licence-incompatible sources as candidates.

Poly Haven result on 2026-11-26: the public model index had no olive, cypress, poplar, fig, vine/grape, wheat, vegetable-bed, reed or hedge model. It did have fruit props named `lemon` and `food_pomegranate_01`, but those are fruit props, not trees or growing vegetation, so they are not candidates for the vegetation system. Poly Haven's trees are CC0, but the available species list is dominated by trees such as pine, fir, jacaranda, quiver and island trees; they do not answer the named Granada species. No Poly Haven asset was downloaded.

The strongest candidates found are mostly authored or procedural CC0 game assets, not raw photogrammetry. That is useful for budget, but it means they still require artist review for species, silhouette and period fit. They are source material or evaluation candidates only, not approved runtime vegetation.

## Cross-source observations

1. **Public-domain does not mean suitable.** Every candidate above still needs species review, period review, artist review and browser measurement before acquisition or import. “Historical review pending” remains the correct status.
2. **Tevy is the strongest system fit found.** Its tree/crop entries already advertise three LODs and wind data, and the metadata gives exact bytes, triangles and checksums. However, its pages/metadata do not state material counts or texture dimensions, and the files use meshopt-compressed geometry plus KTX2 textures. Nothing was downloaded, so those figures remain unmeasured.
3. **3DAssets.dev is cheap and explicit but static.** Its pages state meshes, materials, texture count and file size exactly, and the files need no meshopt/KTX2 loader. The assets are contributor-reported AI-generated and static, so artist review and added wind weights are essential.
4. **Grab3D gives the clearest texture resolutions.** Its pages state 1K/2K PBR texture sets and light variants, but not material counts. The style is soft/stylised and must be tested against the game's target look.
5. **Raw scans are not the immediate answer.** Poly Haven's large tree scans/photogrammetry-style assets can be hundreds of megabytes or more and millions of triangles. Even where CC0, they would need retopology, atlasing, LODs, compression and species review. No such raw scan should be placed unchanged in the browser game.
6. **Pomegranate is a plain gap.** I found public-domain pomegranate fruit and reference imagery, but no usable public-domain pomegranate tree model with enough technical information to survey. This should be authored/commissioned or revisited in a later search; a generic fruit tree must not be renamed as pomegranate without artist/botanical review.

## Fit with the owner's mixed vegetation budget

- **Hero trees:** tevy olive LOD1, tevy Italian cypress LOD1 and possibly the 3DAssets fig are the most plausible starting points, subject to artist review. They sit in or near the owner's 8,000–20,000 near-LOD and 1,500–4,000 strategic-LOD bands, but exact decoded GPU memory is not measured.
- **Middle-distance orchard/crop instances:** Pixor orange, 3DAssets fig/vine row, tevy wheat LOD1/2 and tevy potato are cheap enough to consider for instanced variants if their style survives review.
- **Ground cover/crops:** wheat and reeds should be tiled/instanced clumps exactly as the owner directed, never individual stalks. Distant shadows should be reduced or disabled.
- **Hedges:** tevy's modular clipped hedge is the only candidate found that is already close to the desired modular cost. Species confidence is low, so it should be treated as generic hedging unless reviewed.
- **Not approved:** no candidate in this document is approved for acquisition, import or runtime use. The owner approves each acquisition separately.
