**The five reports themselves were not obtained.** They sit behind the founder's sign-in to the reviewer's application. **What was done instead**, as instruction 4 allows: an independent review of the two merged diffs exactly as merged (`git diff 649871bc14d^1 649871bc14d` and `git diff 486afb1985b^1 486afb1985b`).
