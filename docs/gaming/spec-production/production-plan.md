# The production plan: phases, streams, dependencies, estimates and gates

_Generated by `npm --prefix gaming run traceability` from [`production-plan.json`](production-plan.json); edit that file, not this page. Written 2026-11-26 on the founder's instruction of that day ([`owner-decisions-2026-11-26.md`](owner-decisions-2026-11-26.md)). **It is a plan. Nothing in it is done unless [`../LEDGER.md`](../LEDGER.md) says so, and all three games are prototypes.**_

**What an estimate here is.** One working session is one reviewable pull request with its checks: the unit the work of 25 and 26 November 2026 was done in. The only measured points are these: one authored building (Granada's watermill) with the pipeline that made it took two sessions; a playable prototype scenario with its rules, page, seven languages and scripted players took about three; a defect found by a reviewer was reproduced, fixed and released in two. Everything else is the author's estimate from those, and is uncertain by a factor of two or more. They are estimates of an agent's effort, never of calendar time: calendar time is set by reviewers, testers, the reference machines, the founder's answers, and runs that take four hours by the clock.

## The order of work

**The rule.** Founder, 26 November 2026: production one game at a time, Granada, then The Golden Age, then Atlas, so that what Granada teaches about rendering, memory, simulation, interface and the art pipeline is applied to the other two. Full production of two games at once only with a separately staffed team for each.

## The critical path to Granada's first production release

**F1** → **F3** → **A0** → _the founder accepts Granada's proof frame_ → **A1** → **A3** → **G6** → **F7** → _operators run the hardware suite on both machines (P3, P4)_ → _testers play (P5)_ → _reviewers decide the ledger's entries (P1, P2)_ → **Q5** → **Q6** → _the founder approves deployment_ → _the founder approves removing the label_

By the estimates, the engineering and art on this chain come to 41 to 76 sessions for Granada. The five steps that are people have no estimate at all, and nothing here can shorten them: reviewers, operators and testers are not yet named. They, not the engineering, set the date of Granada's first production release. The largest single uncertainty in the engineering is A1, the buildings, and it is unknown until the proof frame is judged.
