# The historical-review ledger: every statement of history the games make

_Generated by `npm --prefix gaming run traceability` from [`ledger.json`](ledger.json); edit that file, not this page. How it is used: [`README.md`](README.md)._

**The rule.** Founder, 26 November 2026: an AI may research, organize sources and draft, and never approves its own historical claims. A published museum record is existing source material that itself needs review; it is not human-approved. A claim is approved by a named human reviewer with the right expertise, then by the museum's editor for wording, uncertainty and tone. The founder approves the product and its scope and is never recorded as the historical authority. Until reviewers are named, no new historical claim is published as verified and every entry stands as 'historical review pending'.

**Today:** 22 entries; 22 historical review pending. By kind: 10 sourced, 2 uncertain, 2 reconstruction, 7 fiction, 1 convention. Reviewers named: 0 of 6.

**Kinds:** `sourced`: text or a fact taken from, or linked to, a museum record: existing source material, not yet reviewed by a person; `uncertain`: a claim the source gives as approximate or disputed, or one the game derives from a source; `reconstruction`: something shown as it might have looked, with no reviewed source behind it; `fiction`: invented for play, and said to be on the page; `convention`: a way of counting or naming, not a claim.
