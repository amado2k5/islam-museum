## Notes

- The Fetret Gregorian equivalent printed in the book (`٤٠٣! - ١٤١٣م`) is malformed in OCR/print; recorded as derived 1403–1413 consistent with Mehmed's 1413 reunification (p. 70, 72).
- `event/battle-of-maritsa-763` date is `tradition` + derived and needs a second source.
- Devşirme/child-levy claims and the Badr al-Din polemic are the author's position — see `perspectives` on `group/janissaries` and `person/badr-al-din-simawi`.
- Wikidata draft cards still unverified against the book (~38): later sultans (murad-iv, uthman-ii/iii, mustafa-i, ahmad-i), Köprülü and later viziers (fazil-mustafa-koprulu, piyale, kilic-ali, gedik-ahmed, rustam, ayas-mehmed, lutfi, sinan, kara-ahmed, lala-mustafa, ibrahim-pasha-franji, mahmud-pasha-angelovic, zagan), the valide sultans (gulnus, turhan, kosem, safiye, nurbanu, khurram/hurrem, mahfiruz, dilasub, halime, hafsa, mihrimah, gulbahar, devlet-hatun, nilufer-hatun — the last two are **not in the book**), princes (jem, shahzade-mustafa, shahzade-jihangir, shahzade-bayezid, mustafa-i), corsairs (oruc-reis, iskandar-beg).
