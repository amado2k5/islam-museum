## Entities written or enriched (batch 1)
- `work/tawhid-ibn-khuzayma`: the draft card was rewritten with full ar/en/ur prose covering the title, why it was written, the author's condition and method, its structure, the vision and intercession, its standing, the criticisms (perspectives: the editor; al-Razi and al-Kawthari as the editor quotes them), and manuscripts and editions.
- `person/ibn-khuzayma` (worker A on main had meanwhile folded `person/ibn-khuzayma-muhammad` into it as an alias): enriched with appended sections from the study's biography (lineage, birth and youth, teachers and students, character, standing, works, creed, death). Names and the laqab were added, plus born_in, student_of (10 relations) and traveled_to (9 relations), and the Shafi'i tradition.
- `question/did-the-prophet-see-his-lord`: new, with four Sunni perspectives: affirmation, vision of the heart, denial, and suspension.
- `question/the-hadith-adam-created-in-his-form`: new, with three Sunni perspectives: Ibn Khuzayma's view (the one struck); affirmation (Ahmad, Ishaq and Ibn Qutayba via the editor); and the pronoun referring to Adam (via Ibn Hajar).
