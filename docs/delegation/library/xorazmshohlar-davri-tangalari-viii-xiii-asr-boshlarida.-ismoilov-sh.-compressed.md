## Doubts
- The author's CE dates are sometimes inconsistent (Iraq ibn Mansur's fals of 280 AH dated 892 on p. 47 and 896 on p. 10; Muhammad's reign printed "hij. 567-589 / 1200-1220" on p. 107). Hijri legends on the coins are preferred; inconsistencies are stated in the prose.
- The author's claim that the Ma'munids began c. 967 (Muhammad ibn Ali's fals) is presented as his view; the polity start date is marked `debated`.
- The statement that books were burned and the old language and religion forgotten after the Arab conquest (p. 9) is the author's characterisation, given attributed.
