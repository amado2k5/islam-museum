**Framing:** the civil-war chapters (the Camel, Siffin, the arbitration, al-Hasan's peace, Karbala) are contested; record al-Dinawari's claims as his reports with his attribution words, Sunni position first where a perspectives block is needed, and leave out claims that disparage Companions unless framed. The Sasanian and Abbasid-da'wa material is his most distinctive.

## Done

- **Batch 1** (intro): `source/akhbar-tiwal-amir`, `work/al-akhbar-al-tiwal`, enriched `person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari` (birth, teachers, Isfahan 235, works, death 26 Jumada I 282).

- **Batch 2** (pp. 43–110): an "In al-Dinawari" section on 12 Sasanian and Lakhmid rulers (Ardashir I, Shapur I, Hormizd I, Shapur II, Yazdagird I, Bahram Gur, Peroz, Kavad, Anushirvan, Hormizd IV, Khusraw Parviz, al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir) and on `place/al-hira` (its founding story, as the book's report).

- **Batch 3** (pp. 113–140, 333–363): "In al-Dinawari" sections on al-Qadisiyya, the Bridge, al-Buwayb ("Day of Mihran"), al-Mada'in (28 months), Jalula' (his divergent commander), Nihawand (21 AH, the chain of command), Tustar, the killing of Yazdegerd III (30 AH), the founding of Basra, and the Abbasid da'wa figures Bukayr ibn Mahan, Abu Muslim, Nasr ibn Sayyar and Juday' al-Kirmani.

- **Batch 4** (pp. 332–382): new `event/abbasid-dawa-in-khurasan` (al-Humayma 101 to the oath of Rajab 132) and `person/abu-salama-al-khallal` (kept apart from the Companion in `person/abu-salama`); enriched `person/qahtaba-ibn-shabib`, `event/killing-of-ibrahim-ibn-muhammad-al-imam` (Abu Ubayda's "I supposed they were strangled", attributed), `event/bayat-abu-al-abbas-al-saffah`, `event/murder-of-abu-muslim` (the astrologers' line is cut in the OCR and was left out), `event/battle-of-al-zab` (al-Dinawari names Shahrazur).

- **Batch 5** (pp. 267–400): new `event/battle-of-sillabra` (Nafi' ibn al-Azraq killed), `event/flight-of-ubaydallah-ibn-ziyad-from-basra` (64; Basra nine days without a governor), `event/battle-of-hamadhan-195`; enriched `event/mukhtar-revolt-kufa` (incl. the siege and the 6,000 executed), `event/battle-of-khazir`, `event/battle-of-dayr-al-jathaliq` (Thursday 15 Jumada I 72), `event/siege-of-ibn-al-zubayr-64`, `person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra`, `event/killing-of-amr-al-ashdaq`, `event/qutayba-samarqand`, `event/founding-of-baghdad` (139 against 145), `event/battle-of-rayy-195`, `event/killing-of-amin` (night of Sunday 5 Muharram 198). Left out as sectarian or doubtful: the "angels" story, al-Mukhtar's "worldly gain" confession, the 100,000,000-dirham reading.

- **Duplicates (31 Oct):** `khosrow-ii-parviz` merged into `khosrow-ii`; `siege-of-tustar` and `fall-of-tustar` merged into `conquest-of-tustar` (`al-falalij-and-tustar-jaban` is a different event, kept). Not duplicates, kept apart: `shirawayh` (Shirawayh al-Aswari) and `shiruyeh-ibn-parviz` (Kavadh II); `conquest-of-madain` (Bahurasir, 15) and `conquest-of-al-madain` (entering al-Mada'in, 16). `person/abu-salama` cleaned: the al-Mas'udi material on Abu Salama al-Khallal moved to `person/abu-salama-al-khallal`.
