# Library lane: Lum'at al-I'tiqad (aqeedah, Hanbali)

**Edition and item used:** `books/aqeedah/lumat-itiqad/shamse_20170630` (al-Maktab al-Islami, Beirut, 4th printing 1395 AH; 50 PDF pages; **printed page = PDF page − 1**). The text layer has OCR noise (the glyph for the Prophet's blessing is read as stray letters after «النبي» and «رسول الله», and a few words are misread, for example «أجد» for «أحد»); quotations were compared with the text with that noise in mind, and the honorific was dropped from quotations because the museum does not type honorifics in prose. The editor's footnotes include the editor's own judgements (for example p. 45 on the schools' differences) and are not used. Other copies: `20260226_20260226_2148` (Riyadh 2022, 127 pages, with a study and a longer introduction; gives the author's dates 541 to 620 AH, not yet used because a second source is wanted), `lm3t2` and `Lom3tEli3tiqad`.

**Done (12 Oct 2026):** a second opinion (`sunni-hanbali`, id `lumat-al-itiqad`) with headline, holders and evidence added next to al-Tahawi's (`sunni-hanafi`) on 14 concepts (tawhid, qadar, kasb, kalam Allah, ru'yat Allah, shafa'a, akhira, ahl al-qibla, obedience to rulers, order of the caliphs, love of the Companions, the ten promised Paradise, signs of the Hour, finality of prophethood) and a third opinion on `concept/iman`; each gained an `issue`, a prose section «في لمعة الاعتقاد» and a refreshed «ما لم يُذكر هنا». New: `source/lumat-al-itiqad`, `work/lumat-al-itiqad`, `person/ibn-qudama-al-maqdisi` (undated).

**Where the two creeds differ (recorded, not decided):** the definition of iman (al-Tahawi: affirmation by tongue and assent of heart; Ibn Qudama: speech, action and conviction, increasing and decreasing); describing God as above the throne and «في السماء» (Ibn Qudama) against al-Tahawi's declaring God above the six directions; the vision of God «بأبصارهم» (Ibn Qudama); obedience to whoever takes the caliphate by force (Ibn Qudama adds it); naming Mu'awiya among the caliphs (Ibn Qudama); the detailed description of God's speech as letters and words (Ibn Qudama).

**Flags for a Tier A reviewer:** all of the above are contested; every entry says it records two books' positions only and that Ash'ari, Maturidi, other Sunni schools and non-Sunni views are still to be added from their own books.

**Next:** al-Wasitiyya (Ibn Taymiyya; items under `books/aqeedah/aqida-wasitiyya`; the 48-page item is the shortest) as a third Sunni source, then Ash'ari and Maturidi texts from the library (not yet downloaded for aqeedah beyond these three).
