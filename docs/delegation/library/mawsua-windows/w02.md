## Window notes
- The window starts in the "ملحق تراجم الفقهاء" appendix at p.3130 (headings جابر بن زيد ... الحكم بن عمرو). Most lines in the appendix are cross-references ("تقدمت ترجمته في ج ...", "ر: ...") and are skipped with that reason.
- Full biographical entries in this appendix are mined as persons (class `person`), per the class decision in the brief.
- Other appendix blocks in the window: pp.3473 to 3496, pp.3823 to 3839, pp.4115 to 4124, and later blocks. Concept headwords (for example أمر, أيمان, بغاة, بيض, أيمان) are interleaved with them.
