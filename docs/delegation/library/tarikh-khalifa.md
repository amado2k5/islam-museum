## Done

- **Batch 1** (intro): `source/tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985`, `work/tarikh-khalifa-ibn-khayyat`, enriched `person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat` (lineage, teachers, students, al-Bukhari's 18 citations, the Ahmad ibn Riyah episode, death 230/240/246 with the editor's preference for 240).

- **Batch 2** (officials tables and two rolls): a section "His officials in Khalifa ibn Khayyat" added to 24 caliphs (Abu Bakr to al-Ma'mun, plus Ibn al-Zubayr): 1,097 office-holders by province and office, 331 linked to existing people, editor-restored lines marked; `event/waqat-al-harra` (97 Quraysh, 173 Ansar, 306 total, al-Mada'ini's date 27 Dhu al-Hijja 63, the executed); `event/revolt-of-ibn-al-ashath` (the reciters' roll, Maskin 5,000/4,000).

- **Batch 3** (second dates): an "In Khalifa ibn Khayyat" section with his dated report and named authority on 16 events: Ajnadayn, death of Abu Bakr (editor-restored line), death of Umar, murder of Uthman, the Camel, Siffin, Nahrawan, death of Ali, Yamama, Ullays, death of Mu'awiya (59 vs 60), Marj Rahit, death of Ibn al-Zubayr, death of Abd al-Malik, Karbala, murder of Sa'id ibn Jubayr (95).

- **Batch 4**: second dates on 9 events (the Zab 11 Jumada II 132, Qudayd 9 Safar 130 with its casualty roll, Zayd ibn Ali 122, deaths of Hisham, al-Walid II, Yazid III, al-Mu'tasim, al-Rida's designation 201, the ransom of 231); new `event/hajj-of-199-without-an-imam` (Ibn al-Aftas; the name dispute noted) and `event/battle-of-al-madhar` (67).

- **Batch 6** (pp. 130–148): Khalifa sections added to `event/battle-of-yarmuk` (15 AH; Ibn al-Kalbi's Monday 5 Rajab 15; the fallen), `event/battle-of-al-qadisiyya` (the numbers, the march on al-Mada'in), `event/plague-of-amwas` (18 AH; the dead) and `event/battle-of-nihawand` (21 AH, given a date; Ali's counsel; the three days). Yarmuk and Amwas published. Duplicates still open: `event/amwas-plague` → `plague-of-amwas`; `battle-of-nahavand` and `battle-of-nahavand-21` → `battle-of-nihawand` (edges to repoint first).
