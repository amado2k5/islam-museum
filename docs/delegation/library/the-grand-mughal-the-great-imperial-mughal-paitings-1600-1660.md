## Care
- No painter's faith is recorded as a field; where Beach infers a painter's religion from dress (Bichitr, Balchand) the inference is attributed to him in prose.
- Beach's judgements (decline vs culmination under Shah Jahan, Dara Shikoh as patron, dating of the Windsor pictures) are given as his.
- No images; the catalogue's figurative paintings are described only.
