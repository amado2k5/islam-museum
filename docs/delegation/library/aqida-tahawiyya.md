# Library lane: al-Aqida al-Tahawiyya (aqeedah)

**Edition and item used:** `books/aqeedah/aqidat-tahawiyya/1995-alaqidah_altahawiya-altahawi` (Dar Ibn Hazm, Beirut, 1995; 32 pages; printed page = PDF page). The text layer of this item is OCR with some misreadings (for example the printed «ولا نكفّر» in article 78 was read as «تكفّر»; checked on the page image p. 21), so every quotation was compared with the text and doubtful ones with the images. Other copies: items `20201028_20201028_2009`, `20241226_20241226_1829` (same short text) and `20221202_20221202_1716` (640 pages, a commentary, not read).

**Done (12 Oct 2026, one batch, 15 concepts plus `source`, `work`, `person/al-tahawi`):** tawhid, qadar, khatm al-nubuwwa, kalam Allah (the Qur'an), ru'yat Allah, shafa'a, akhira (grave, resurrection, reckoning), ahl al-qibla and takfir, kasb and istita'a, obedience to rulers, the order of the rightly-guided caliphs, love of the Companions, the ten promised Paradise, karamat al-awliya, signs of the Hour; `concept/iman` extended with the book's definition. All 134 articles are covered except the practical ones (wiping over the socks, article 99; prayer behind any Muslim is included under ahl al-qibla; the throne and chair, 68 to 70; the covenant, 52; the pool, 50; the Mi'raj, 49; the angels, 101 to 102), which wait for a fiqh or hadith batch.

**Flags for a Tier A reviewer (contested; the entries record the book's position only and say so):** kalam Allah, ru'yat Allah, qadar and kasb, takfir, obedience to rulers, the order of the caliphs, love of the Companions. None carries the other schools' views yet; each says it is to be added from their own books, not described through their critics.
