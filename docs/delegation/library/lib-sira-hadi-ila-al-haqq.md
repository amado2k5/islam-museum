## Judgments recorded

- **Narrators corrected.** `work/sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq` listed `person/yala-ibn-muhammad-al-yafrani` (a Zanata amir of the Maghreb, d. 347 AH, from Ibn Khaldun) and al-Hadi himself as authors, and its summary named "Ya'la ibn Muhammad". The book's own opening leaf (printed p. 15) and the editor (pp. 7-8) name Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi and Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah al-Abbasi al-Alawi; the earlier reading is a misreading of the title-page OCR. The work now lists the two real narrators. The Yafrani record is untouched and no longer linked from this book. Held for the founder: whether that unrelated record needs any further change (none made).
- **Genealogy of Ali ibn Muhammad.** The printed chain on p. 15 is garbled in the OCR (one name reads as a fragment). Only the two ends are recorded (Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, and al-Abbas ibn Ali). To check against the page image if the PNGs are ever generated (`pages-png/` is empty on the drive).
- **Reading of "hujja" (p. 8):** "a boy on whom no obligation before God had yet fallen" (not yet of age), not pilgrimage.
- **Added pages (p. 20 footnote):** the editor says pp. 17-20 appear to have been added to the original; al-Kufi's own account begins at p. 21 (the complete Sana'a manuscript begins there). Noted in the work record and the entry event. The records from these pages carry that caveat.
- **Names held back for the page image:** the first name of Abu al-Atahiya (p. 17 OCR "Abdullah ibn Yashir", p. 19 "ibn Bishr"); Abu al-Ashira ibn al-Ruwayya (p. 18, OCR rough); the son called al-Murtada whom al-Hadi appointed over a district whose name is lost in the OCR (p. 18; the OCR gives his first name as al-Qasim, the usual name being Muhammad); the "Zahr" of p. 20; the distance of "al-Rahba of Sana'a" (p. 20 footnote). None is entered as fact. Ibn Abbad of p. 19 is taken to be Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Abi Abbad of p. 17 from the context, marked as inferred.
- **Place identification:** the Shibam of these pages is not linked to `place/shibam` (which is Shibam of Hadramawt) nor to `place/shibam-kawkaban`; the editor's footnote gives only a day-and-night distance from Sana'a. To fix with a gazetteer in a later batch.
- **Contested topic (batch 3):** the imamate record carries a perspectives block (Sunni position first, reusing the cited `source/duha-islam` statement already in `concept/imama`, then the Zaydi position of this book). The record on the reports of the man of Yemen has no perspectives block: the validator requires a Sunni entry first and no cited Sunni or academic source on those reports has been found yet. Held for Tier A review: add a cited Sunni and an academic perspective on the al-Yamani reports. The editor's date notes (Khumarawayh killed in 282 AH, Yahya ibn Umar's rising in 250 AH per al-Tabari) are in the prose.
- **Qur'an:** verses are linked as existing `passage/quran-*` records only (those that exist: 61:10, 4:95, 3:104, 3:110, 9:41, 9:111, 4:59, 35:32, 3:33, 16:43, 15:9, 22:41); no verse is typed. No passage files were created.
- **Reports:** the hadith and athar the preface relays (attributed to Ibn Mas'ud, Ma'qil ibn Yasar, Ali, Zayd ibn Ali, al-Baqir) are described, not copied, and no grade is given.
- **Dates not entered:** the meeting at al-Fur' (Dhu al-Hijja, year not stated in the passage). The poem lines naming "the year of two" are not read as a date.
- **Identity note (batch 4):** the narrator calls al-Hadi himself "Yahya ibn al-Husayn" in places (pp. 40-42); that is not the "Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn Yahya" of the line of Umar ibn Ali who travelled with Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah (p. 36-38). Handled in the prose. The son who travelled with al-Hadi is "Muhammad ibn Yahya" (p. 38), matching `person/al-murtada-muhammad`; this also makes the p. 18 reading "al-Qasim al-Murtada" a likely OCR slip, still held for the page image.
- **Names held back (batch 4):** the servant "Salmim/Sulaym" (p. 38), the Sa'da informants (p. 42) and "Zubayd" (place or tribe, p. 40) are read in doubtful forms and are not entered as entities. The "first man killed by al-Hadi's own hand" is a marginal note in the manuscript, recorded as the annotator's wording.
- **Coordinates:** none given for Bayt Dhukhar, Raydah or al-Rahba (the book gives none and none were guessed); two battle warnings remain until a gazetteer fixes them.
- **Omitted on purpose:** the narrator's contemptuous descriptions of opponents (not repeated in the museum's voice); the Qur'anic passages quoted in the doctrinal preface are left to batch 3, referenced by passage links only.
- **Not recorded:** the second-appendix and body dates; the death date of the father and son (the book gives no year in the pages read).

- **Batch 5 notes:** (1) p. 51 "ya Abu Jafar" shows the narrator Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah bore the kunya Abu Jafar (consistent with the existing person record). (2) One line on p. 50, in which al-Hadi speaks harshly of opponents, is not repeated (same rule as earlier batches). (3) The "father and uncles" of the illness story are read as al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim and Muhammad and al-Hasan from the wider book, marked as inferred in the prose. (4) The Yemeni term "al-athari", the sense of "ahl al-matraba" and the damaged "amarani bi-qabd" are read from context. (5) The settlement terms promised "at the end of our book" for land bought from Muslims are left for when mining reaches them. (6) The charge and orders are not entered as concept records because the tax rulings are a juristic topic that would need a perspectives block with cited Sunni and academic sources; held for Tier A review. (7) The call is dated to Safar 284 AH at month precision (derived); the end of the stay (Jumada) is not entered.

- **Batch 6 notes:** (1) The kunya Abu Jafar is also given to Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi (p. 54, p. 57), so the batch 5 inference that the narrator of the sayings bore it is softened in the batch 5 records; the identity of the "I" of pp. 49-52 stays Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah by the chain at p. 48. (2) Names held back for the page image: Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Malik "al-Yarsami" and Ali ibn Abi Anbasa "al-Samidi" (p. 59, nisba doubtful, not entered as persons); "al-Hasan" quoted p. 58 not identified. (3) The scribe's marginal notes (pp. 54, 56, 57, 58) are marked as the copyist's. (4) Legal points (jizya belonging to the household, slaves bought from the zakat share) are stated as the book's Zaydi position; no concept records, as above, pending a cited Sunni and academic source actually read. (5) Not repeated: the line on p. 50 about opponents.
