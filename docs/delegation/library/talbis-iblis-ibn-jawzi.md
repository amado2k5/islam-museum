## Doubts and exclusions
- Talbis names the Tarsus "labbayk" ascetic only as Abu Hamza; linking him to `person/muhammad-ibn-ibrahim-abu-hamza-al-sufi` is an inference, stated as such in the prose.
- The claimant answered on PDF p. 346 (fear of the Fire) is not named on the pages read; not attributed to Abu Yazid.
- Not yet harvested: chapter 5 (sects and religions, pp. 43-115), chapter 6 (scholars), chapters 7-9, the sama' and tawakkul sections in detail, chapter 11 (karamat). The Malamatiyya passage was not located in the pages read (group/malamatiyya still cites Ibn al-Jawzi through the Kashf translator).
