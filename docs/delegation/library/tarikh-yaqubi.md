**Edition and item used:** archive.org `yaqubi` (1.pdf 473 pp., 2.pdf 631 pp.), ed. M. Th. Houtsma, Leiden: Brill, 1883; Apple Vision OCR. **Volume 2: printed page = scan page + 2.** Cite as `{cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. N}`; preface and title pages by scan page.
**Source id:** `source/tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883` · **work:** `work/tarikh-al-yaqubi` · **author:** `person/al-yaqubi`.

**Framing:** al-Ya'qubi's chapters on the succession after the Prophet, the civil wars and the Alid imams carry his own perspective; under the museum's Sunni frame, record such material only as his attributed report, label it, and put the Sunni position first in a `perspectives` block. The author's faith is not recorded (the book does not state it). **The OCR misreads Arabic numerals** (237 for 227, 318 for 218): take years only where they agree with the context or the record; days, months, places, ages and reign lengths are read from the text.

## Done

- **Batch 1** (vol. 2 pp. 486–625): new `source/tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883`, `work/tarikh-al-yaqubi`, `person/al-yaqubi` (no birth or death date: the book gives none). "In al-Ya'qubi" sections on `event/death-of-al-mahdi` (al-Radhdh, the hunting accident, 22 Muharram 169), `event/death-of-al-hadi` (14 Rabi' I 170), `event/killing-of-amin` (reign lengths, age), `event/death-of-mamun` (al-Badhandun, Thursday 17 Rajab 218, buried at Tarsus), `event/death-of-mutasim` (al-Jawsaq), `event/death-of-wathiq`, `person/al-muntasir`, `event/killing-of-muhtadi` (Ahmad ibn Jamila's house; Rajab 256). Where his day differs from the record's date the difference is stated in the prose; no date field was changed.

- **Batch 2** (vol. 2 pp. 435–624): "In al-Ya'qubi" sections on `event/death-of-abu-al-abbas-al-saffah` (the letter of succession, al-Anbar), `event/death-of-al-mansur` (the banner dream, Bi'r Maymun, 3 Dhu al-Hijja 158), `event/death-of-harun-al-rashid` (Sanabadh near Tus, Jumada I 193), `event/death-of-mutawakkil` (his account of the Turks' attack; al-Muntasir's part marked as disputed), `event/bayat-al-mutazz` (al-Musta'in's abdication terms, Wasit), `event/killing-of-mutazz` (Salih ibn Wasif, Rajab 255), `person/ahmad-ibn-tulun` (the treasury sent to al-Mu'tamid, Alexandria 257, the oath to himself in 258). Left out: the year of Ibn al-Mudabbir's release (the OCR reads 259 against the narrative order) and the hajj-leader lists (years garbled in the OCR; a dataset needs the printed figures checked against another copy).

- **Batch 3** (vol. 2 pp. 619–624, the end of the surviving text): "In al-Ya'qubi" sections on `person/al-mutamid` (accession, the provincial oaths: Muhammad ibn Tahir in Khurasan, Ibn Tulun in Egypt; Isa ibn al-Shaykh's refusal in Palestine and his move to Armenia in 257), `event/zanj-capture-of-ubulla`, `event/zanj-sack-of-basra-257` (the mosque burned; Muhammad al-Muwallad's entry in 258), `person/sahib-al-zanj` (al-Ya'qubi's naming of him as a claimant to Talibid descent, attributed), `person/abu-ahmad-al-muwaffaq` (the boats burned in 258), `person/ahmad-ibn-al-mudabbir` (release after 9 months 25 days; Syria's land tax 257; left al-Fustat Muharram 258), `person/bayakbak` and `person/yarjukh-al-turki` (Egypt's assignment passing between them; Yarjukh killed at Samarra). Left out: the year of Ibn al-Mudabbir's release (as in batch 2), the hajj-leader names, the eclipse, plague and Nile notices (no matching records; candidates for new event records once checked against another copy). The governors of Khurasan under the Abbasids are scattered through earlier reigns and were not reached in this batch.
