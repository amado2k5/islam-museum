**Framing:** al-Ya'qubi's chapters on the succession after the Prophet, the civil wars and the Alid imams carry his own perspective; under the museum's Sunni frame, record such material only as his attributed report, label it, and put the Sunni position first in a `perspectives` block. The author's faith is not recorded (the book does not state it). **The OCR misreads Arabic numerals** (237 for 227, 318 for 218): take years only where they agree with the context or the record; days, months, places, ages and reign lengths are read from the text.
