İstanbul Fetih Cemiyeti 2020), cultural heritage series no. 10, YTB publication 38. Bilingual Turkish/English.

Source record: source/yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-2-2023. Work record (all three volumes): work/kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri.

Page offset: vol. 1 printed = scan − 2; vol. 2 printed = scan + 398; vol. 3 printed = scan + 845 (checked at several page heads). Citations give printed pages.

Method: the 225 entry boxes of the three volumes were listed (location, builder, dates, present name) and checked against existing buildings; entries the museum lacked were harvested from the box and the Turkish/English text.

The book's framing (Pax Ottomanica, "converted by force", national labels) is recorded as the author's; contested origins (e.g. Heraklion's Defterdar Ahmed Pasha mosque said to be a Fatimid foundation, after Evliya) were not harvested.
