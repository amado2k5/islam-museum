# Library lane: Maqalat al-Islamiyyin (al-Ash'ari), the closing creed statement

**Edition and item used:** `books/aqeedah/maqalat-islamiyyin/MaghalatAlIslamyeen` (ed. Muhammad Muhyi al-Din Abd al-Hamid, vol. 1, al-Maktaba al-Asriyya, Sayda and Beirut, 1411 AH / 1990; 366 pages, scanned and OCR'd with Apple Vision; printed page = PDF page). Only printed pages 345 to 352 were read: «حكاية جملة قول أصحاب الحديث وأهل السنة» and the short reports after it. Other copies: `Maghalat_AlIslamyin.pdf`, `MaghalatlIslamyin.pdf`, `24092023153659.pdf` (not extracted).

**What the passage is:** al-Ash'ari reports the whole position of the hadith scholars and Ahl al-Sunna and closes with «وبكل ما ذكرنا من قولهم نقول، وإليه نذهب» (p. 350), so each entry records it as "the position al-Ash'ari reports and says he holds". The editor's footnotes (for example p. 349) are not used.

**Done (13 Oct 2026):** a `sunni-ashari` opinion (id `maqalat-ashari`) added on 13 creed concepts (tawhid, qadar, kasb, kalam Allah, ru'yat Allah, iman, ahl al-qibla, shafa'a, akhira, love of the Companions, the order of the caliphs, obedience to rulers, karamat, signs of the Hour); new `source/maqalat-islamiyyin`, `work/maqalat-islamiyyin`, `person/al-ashari` (undated); new traditions `sunni-ashari` and `sunni-maturidi` in `schema/enums/traditions.yaml`.

**Doubts:** the OCR misreads some letters («مخاوق» for «مخلوق», «تقول» for «نقول», «بُقِرون» for «يقرون»); a machine comparison flagged 25 of 84 quotations for this reason and each was read by eye; the iman and caliph-order wording should be checked on the page images (`pages-png/p0347.png`, `p0348.png`) before the entries are promoted. Notable result: in this passage al-Ash'ari does not use the word kasb; he says God creates the servants' acts.
