## Done

- **Batch 1** (intro pp. ج–ص): `source/futuh-misr-amir-1999`, `work/futuh-misr`, `person/ibn-abd-al-hakam` (enriched: birth c. 187, death 257 at al-Fustat, family, Mihna, method).
- **Batch 2** (pp. 52–109, the conquest): `event/siege-of-babylon-fortress-640` (rewritten from a Wikidata stub: Persian/Roman origin, 4×1000 reinforcement, 7 months vs 1 month, al-Zubayr's ladder, al-Muqawqis to the Island), new `event/treaty-of-babylon` (2 dinars, 3 days' hospitality, 6 million counted / 12 million dinars, variant 8 million), `event/ubada-embassy-to-al-muqawqis`, `event/battle-of-kom-sharik`, `event/battle-of-al-kiryawn`, `place/umm-dunayn`, `person/benjamin-i-of-alexandria`, `person/al-uayrij`, `person/sharik-ibn-sumayy`; enriched `event/conquest-of-egypt` (year 18 at al-Jabiya, army size, the recall letter, route) and `person/al-muqawqis`.

- **Batch 3** (pp. 110–169, Alexandria and al-Fustat): enriched `event/conquest-of-alexandria` (Hulwa and al-Maqs, nine+five months, Friday new moon of Muharram 20, Ubada given command, 22 Muslim dead, the "second conquest" via Ibn Bassama, Mu'awiya ibn Hudayj as messenger, Amr's letter on the city), `place/al-fustat` (Umar's no-water rule, the doves in Amr's tent), `building/masjid-amr-fustat` (qibla, Umar's order to break the minbar, Abu Muslim al-Ghafiqi), `person/kharija-ibn-hudhafa-al-adawi`, `person/maslama-ibn-mukhallad-al-ansari`, `event/conquest-of-egypt` (the treaty-or-force chapters, Ibn Shihab's synthesis); new `place/khitat-al-fustat`, `building/dar-al-baida-fustat`, `building/dar-al-raml-fustat`, `building/dar-al-fulful-fustat`, `person/kab-ibn-dinna-al-absi`, `event/kharijite-plot-against-muawiya-and-amr` (al-Zuhri's version without Ali; kept apart from `event/death-of-ali-ibn-abi-talib`).

- **Batch 4** (pp. 170–231, Umar and Amr): new `event/umar-letter-to-the-nile` (the Nile-bride report; chain names an unnamed informant, recorded as such), `event/muqasama-of-amr-ibn-al-as` (Ibn Maslama's halving; the harsh exchange summarised, no verdict), `event/digging-of-khalij-amir-al-muminin` (three versions), `event/building-of-giza-fortress` (21–22 AH), `event/conquest-of-al-fayyum`, `event/umar-retaliation-for-the-egyptian` ("son of the noblest"), `person/sandar-mawla-zinba` (Munyat al-Asbagh); enriched `event/umar-food-transport-egypt`, `event/kharaj-milking-egypt` (12M / 20M / 14M figures, attributed), `place/al-muqattam`, `building/masjid-amr-fustat` (53, 77, 90 rebuilds), `person/hakim-ibn-hizam` (al-Jar vouchers). Left out on purpose: Umar's harsh words to Abu Hurayra (p. 201), not needed and easily misread without context.

- **Batch 5** (pp. 246–296, Maghrib and al-Andalus): new `event/battle-of-tahudha` (63 AH; Abu al-Muhajir in irons), `person/jurjir-patrician-of-ifriqiya` (seat at Qartajanna; killer "as they claim" Ibn al-Zubayr); enriched `event/conquest-of-al-andalus` (Tangier 92, Julian, the crossing, Shadhuna/Wadi Umm Hakim, the locked house as the book's report, Musa in Rajab 93, "not conquests but the Gathering"), `object/table-of-solomon` (Firas fortress, 200,000 dinars, the missing leg), `person/tariq-ibn-ziyad` (name variant ibn Amr), `person/musa-ibn-nusayr` (79; al-Fustat 96; d. al-Marr 97), `person/uqba-ibn-nafi` (Waddan, Jarma, Kawar, Ma' Faras), `event/founding-of-qayrawan`, `person/kusayla-ibn-lamzam` (d. 64), `person/hassan-ibn-al-numan` (73, 76, 78), `person/al-kahina`, `event/battle-of-sufetula-647` (27), `event/battle-of-the-nobles-740` (123), `event/killing-of-kulthum-ibn-iyad` (123).
