## CATEGORY: aqeedah, sunnah (creed), fiqh, usul, adab/zuhd, lugha

The museum describes doctrine, schools and law; it does not issue rulings and does not argue. Everything here is `describe, attribute, label`.
What to extract, by class:
- `source`, `work` (the book: author, genre `creed`/`fiqh`/`usul`/`zuhd`/`lexicon`, school, date, occasion of writing if stated, structure, its place in the school's curriculum, commentaries/abridgements as `work` stubs).
- `person`: the author and the figures the book's introduction names (teachers, rivals, patrons), with the full biography fields of people.md.
- `group`: schools and movements as the book describes them (ahl al-hadith/athari, ash'ariyya, maturidiyya, the four madhhabs, mu'tazila as described and refuted by the book). Another group's view is labelled before it is stated and is described in that group's own words when available, not only through its critics.
- `concept`: each doctrinal or legal term the book defines (tawhid and its divisions, the attributes, qadar, iman, kufr/takfir as a term, taqlid, ijtihad, qiyas, maqasid, sadd al-dhara'i', istihsan…): one entity per concept, `summary.ar` built from the book's own definition, attributed ("عرّفه … بأنه"), with every school's definition in `perspectives` (Sunni schools each labelled and cited; academic overview; other views labelled).
- `practice`: acts of worship and transactions described in the fiqh books (salah, zakat, siyam, hajj, nikah, buyu'): `summary.ar` descriptive (what Muslims do, in which form, per school), each school's variation as a labelled position, evidence listed as citations to `passage` entities (Qur'an by sura:aya reference, hadith by collection ref), never as a ruling for the reader.
- Tension cards: genuinely hard questions that the book treats (e.g. reason versus transmission, the attributes, intercession, the status of the sinner, jihad rules, apostasy, women's testimony) are written as `tension` cards in the museum's form with at least two cited voices, no verdict, no "the correct view".
- Polemic lists («الرد على الجهمية», refutations of sects): the museum records the book's position as the book's, attributed and labelled; it never states a sect's beliefs only in its opponents' words and never uses derogatory labels in its own voice.
Batching: author and introduction first, then chapter by chapter; at most 25 entities a batch. Fiqh compendia (al-Mughni, al-Majmu') are processed by kitab, one madhhab comparison per concept/practice, not line by line.
Quality checks: every `perspectives` block has the Sunni position(s) first and labelled; no sentence in the museum's voice says what a Muslim must do; no sectarian slur; each school's position is cited to its own authoritative book where possible.
