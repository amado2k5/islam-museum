# Content the old source rules held back: the backlog

Compiled 15 October 2026 from a sweep of `origin/main` (tip `2a8f09e2`), the harvest drive (`/Volumes/exmicroSD/islam-museum-harvest`, mounted) and the git history, the day the founder replaced the "facts only, never paraphrase, licence wall" rules with `docs/sources-policy.md` (read anything, rewrite in the museum's own words, cite). Each numbered item below is one lane for one session. Work them in order unless a lane document says otherwise; open a lane per item in `docs/agents/lanes.md`, keep a progress file under `docs/delegation/`, and push small validated batches to main.

What the sweep established first: no entity on main carries a barred layer (the counts are 8,992 `own-research`, 6,351 `by`, 41 `cc0`), so the wall held. The held-back content shows up as **absence and thinness**: books mined for names and dates only, entities with a one-line summary and no Arabic prose, library books never opened, texts summarised to a sentence. Figures are from the sweep and were not re-counted by hand; treat them as the order of magnitude.

What the new rule changes for every item: the book may now be read in full and what it says written on the entity pages in the museum's own words, with the page cited. What does not change: quotations stay at most 40 words, the two-source rule and doubt markers stay, Wikipedia stays a pointer, the source files stay out of git, nothing is marked reviewed by its author.

## What the sweep did not cover

Only `origin/main` was counted; more than 100 unmerged branches and worktrees (the Nuzhat page batches, `content/tabari-tarikh-trm`, `content/umayyad-books`, `content/volume1-remaining` and others) may already hold some of this. "Thin" was measured as the absence of `content/ar` prose and `completeness: 1`, not by reading each entity. The Wikipedia match used exact normalised labels, so aliases would lower the unmatched count. No book was opened to judge its yield. The second-ring Wikipedia dumps and the OpenITI zip were not parsed.
