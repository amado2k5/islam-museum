- **Kishk's faith.** Only the Arabic Wikipedia infobox names a school (Ahl al-Sunna wa-l-Jama'a); Wikidata says only Islam and the English article names none. Recorded as `faith: unknown` with that note, following the `person/ragheb-al-sergani` precedent, until a second independent source exists.
- **Wikipedia ledger.** The two Kishk articles are recorded as page entries in `docs/authenticity-ledger/pages/` (revision ids in the URLs) and as `source/wikipedia-abdul-hamid-kishk-{en,ar}` and `source/wikidata-abdul-hamid-kishk`.
- **Noisy archive.** The Kishk recordings are old cassette sermons; the Qwen transcripts are mostly garbled. Only windows that read clearly were used (65 of 1,231); stories he tells without a chain are marked `needs_source`; names that are garbled (the Companion of the fox story, the son in the apple story) are left unnamed.
- **Hadith he quotes** (the hadith qudsi of the five conditions, the five heavy in the scale, bayt al-hamd, the expiation hadith, prayer at seven) are described in the museum's words; no matn typed. Qur'an verses he recites without naming the sura are not linked.
- **Sergani (sergani-1).** The 15 archived episodes are a television programme (host and guest), not lessons; the host's own questions are not cited as the speaker's claims. The archive does not hold the whole programme (ep15 refers to an earlier episode on war and peace that is not in it). Faith stays `unknown` as in his record. Garbled names are left unnamed (the Companion at Badr in ep06, the asker in the ep07 hadith); his numbers (casualty count, bullfighting, Egypt's population, jizya rate) are written as his, with `needs_source` where he gives no source. His jizya rate is set beside al-Baladhuri's account in a `perspectives` block. Abandoned claims notes from an earlier attempt (`notes/ep02-ep12.md` in the harvest workspace) were used as a guide only after sampled windows were re-read in the transcripts; ep01 and ep13-ep15 were read directly. Three extended records lacked provenance fields and failed cite-check before this batch (`ilm-al-tarikh`, `asabiyya`, `islam-and-the-rise-of-civilisation`); a truthful provenance line naming their existing sources was added.
- **Recording map.** `site/src/data/recordings-abdul-hamid-kishk.json` (from the manifest, `recordings_map.py`) and one line each in the three maps of `site/src/lib/recordings.ts`, as for Sergani and El-Naggar.

- **Qaradawi archive (run qaradawi-1).** The recordings are studio television lessons with clean audio, so most windows read clearly. Series 1 has a presenter's introduction and closing summary in each episode; only al-Qaradawi's own words were used, and the summaries were not attributed to him. Series 2 has no presenter. Episode 5 of series 1 is titled "al-Nisa' 3" by the archive but its recording repeats episode 4's lesson; it is written once, with both anchors. Contested matter (apostasy, slavery, the sacrificed son) goes in `perspectives` blocks with the Sunni entry first; where no independent Sunni or academic source was read in this run, the entry says "not yet sourced". Series 2 was read through four parallel read-only extraction passes whose claims were then spot-checked against the transcript windows before writing; every anchor in the claims tables matches a transcript line.
