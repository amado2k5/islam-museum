Dear LRM-CS team,

I run islam.museum, an open, free, static museum of Islamic civilisation — a trilingual (Arabic-first) knowledge graph published at islam.museum, built entirely from git-versioned, openly licensed data (CC0) and prose (CC BY-SA 4.0).

We have registered your Diamond-ILS catalogue (stewarded with IDEO, Cairo, on the IFLA-LRM/RDA model) as a Tier-1 bibliographic source. It is exactly the authority layer our collection needs: typed work-to-work relations — commentary, refutation, abridgement, sequel — and sourced author authorities across the classical Islamicate heritage.

Two questions:

1. **Licence.** Is there a licence, terms of use, or data-sharing agreement covering structured reuse of Diamond records (agent and work data)? We currently cite bibliographic facts only, in our own words, with attribution — but a written licence would let us do considerably more, correctly attributed.

2. **Exchange.** Would LRM-CS be open to a reciprocal data exchange? We hold a large, curated, openly licensed knowledge graph of Islamic persons, works, places and events (tens of thousands of records with typed relations and citations). If useful to Diamond's coverage of the Islamicate heritage, we would be glad to share it under CC0.

Either way, even a pointer to the right contact or documentation would help. Thank you for the work you do making this heritage navigable.
