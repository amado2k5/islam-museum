# Content to write: the Shia side and records that lack faith or opinions

_Listed 12 October 2026 after the Aisha traditions audit (founder's request). The scope rule is `docs/scope.md`: the main reference is Sunni Islam and its wasatiyya; every other view is covered, named before it is stated, and tagged secondary or other. Sources for the Shia side come from Shia sources and neutral scholarship (Momen 1985 and Madelung 1997 are already in `data/source/`), never only from critics._

## A. People claimed by more than one tradition who have no opinions section (item 2)

The Prophet, Ali ibn Abi Talib, Khadija, Hamza and Zaynab bint Ali list both Sunni and Twelver Shia in `traditions[]` but have no `perspectives` (no question, no Shia or academic view). For each: an `issue`, a lead Sunni opinion, an academic view, the Twelver Shia view (and Ismaili or Zaydi where they differ), each cited and tagged. Ali first: the Sunni and Shia accounts of his standing and succession (see `event/ghadir-khumm`, `event/saqifa`, `concept/khilafa-rashida`).

Also: their `faith[]` is Sunni only while `traditions[]` lists both; either record the basis for both or reduce `traditions[]` (decide per person with the founder). `person/suraqa-ibn-malik`, `person/zuhayr-ibn-abi-umayya` and `person/umm-mabad` are traditionally reported to have become Muslim, but their faith records stop at polytheism and their Sunni tag was removed on 12 Oct; add a Muslim phase with a source and restore the tag. 30 persons tagged Sunni have no `faith[]` at all (Abu Bakr, Ali, Ibn Sina, al-Nawawi, Malik ibn Anas and others; the list is produced by the audit in the 12 Oct session).

## B. The Shia Imams and the Shia record (item 5)

No records exist for the twelve Imams, which leaves the Twelver tradition thin although every view is in scope.

- **Persons:** the twelve Imams (Ali, Hasan and Husayn exist; Ali Zayn al-Abidin, Muhammad al-Baqir, Ja'far al-Sadiq, Musa al-Kazim, Ali al-Rida, Muhammad al-Jawad, Ali al-Hadi, Hasan al-Askari and the twelfth Imam, as the Twelver tradition describes him); Zayd ibn Ali (Zaydi); Ismaili Imams as the Ismaili tradition names them; each labelled by its tradition with `faith[]` and cited from its own sources.
- **Groups:** Twelver, Ismaili, Zaydi, Ibadi as `denomination` groups with self-designations and history.
- **Works:** Nahj al-Balagha, al-Kafi, Man la Yahduruh al-Faqih, al-Irshad, and the Ismaili and Zaydi counterparts; hadith and creed works with their status in their own tradition.
- **Events and places:** Karbala (Husayn's rising and the mourning on Ashura as practised), the Battle of the Camel and Siffin as the Sunni, academic and Shia accounts, the shrines of Najaf, Karbala, Mashhad, Qom and Samarra; Buyid and Safavid polities exist or are in progress, with the Imamate of Oman and Zaydi Yemen to add.
- **Rules:** each page states whose view it records before stating it; Sunni and other views on the same topic sit beside it in the opinions section; no polemic, no slurs, no description of a view only through its opponents.
