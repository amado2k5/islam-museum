# Browsers and explorers: what each one is, what it has, what it will have

_Living register, opened 6 November 2026 on the founder's instruction. Update it in the same commit as any change to a browser or explorer. Counts are from `data/` and the build caches on the day of the last update; the coverage pages regenerate them._

The museum is one knowledge graph (`data/<class>/<slug>.yaml`, `content/<lang>/<class>/<slug>.md`, `schema/predicates.yaml`) rendered as one static site. A **browser** or **explorer** is a front door over that graph: a set of pages that filters, sorts, groups or walks the same records in a particular way. None of them has data of its own: a job, an invention, a fatwa, a sermon, a poem, a film or a modern album is an ordinary museum record with sources, provenance and trilingual prose, reachable from every other page, exported in the API and indexed by search.

## 1. The rule (AGENTS.md, "Rules that never bend")

Every browser, explorer, class or feature, present and future, is **graph, API and search**:

1. **Graph.** Its records are classes and kinds in the one graph, with relations from `schema/predicates.yaml` (inverses computed, derived edges marked `derived: true`), never a side table, a page-only dataset or a second graph. A feature's own numbers (counts, indices, scores) are computed at build from those records and printed on its pages.
2. **API.** Its records are exported like every class (`/api/v1/<class>/index.json`, `/api/v1/<class>/<slug>.json`, the graph dumps and neighbourhoods), and the feature adds its own static exports under `/api/v1/` for what its pages compute (a section index, a chain, a reach index, a subject list), documented in `docs/data/README.md`, `site/public/openapi/v1.json` and `llms.txt`.
3. **Search.** Its records are entity pages and are indexed by Pagefind; its landing page exposes its title, lede and section headings to Pagefind (`data-pagefind-body` on the header block, section 4) so the feature itself is findable by name; only the long lists are kept out.
4. **Documented.** It has a row here, a row in the plan index (`docs/plan/00-README.md`), a line in the roadmap's "what comes next" while unfinished, a lane row in `docs/agents/lanes.md` while work is open, a status row in `docs/status.md`, and a canary entry for every change.

## 4. Search per feature

Pagefind indexes what sits inside `data-pagefind-body`. Entity pages carry it on their main content (`EntityLayout.astro`), so every record of every browser is searchable; passage (Qur'an verse) pages are left out by design (plan 03 §7). A browser's or explorer's landing page carries `data-pagefind-body` on its header block (title, lede, status line) so the feature itself is findable by its name in every language, and `data-pagefind-ignore` on its long lists so the index is not flooded with names that each have their own page. The time-browser QA list and the old kind pages stay `noindex`.

## 5. Adding a browser or explorer

The checklist is `docs/engineering/10-extensibility.md` §2.7. In short: a plan document and a row in the plan index; records as classes and kinds in the graph (schema through `npm run schema`, predicates registered, validator rules, tests); the pages under `site/src/pages/[lang]/<feature>/` reading the compiled graph, readable without JavaScript, trilingual in the same commit, with the Pagefind header block; the feature's API export and its three documentation lines; the Explore menu entry and the landing card when the home is ready; the lane row, the status row, this register and the canary entry; the founder deploys.
