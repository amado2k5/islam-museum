# Phase 07: Africa sweep

Part of the coverage expansion programme. Master plan: [`README.md`](README.md). Progress: [`progress.md`](progress.md).

## Scope

Timbuktu's scholarly families and libraries, Harar, Sokoto, the Swahili coast. The Tarikh al-Sudan and Tarikh al-Fattash traditions and the Timbuktu chronicles as named sources.

## Source corpora and access

- Tarikh al-Sudan (al-Sa'di) and Tarikh al-Fattash: named sources; **canon status and access not checked in this session.**
- Timbuktu manuscript catalogues: **not checked.**

## Size estimate and derivation

Unsized. Presence measure: Timbuktu 37 files, Harar 46, Sokoto 33, Swahili 11 (text-term matches). Yield per source not measured. Pilot: one chronicle or one city, 30-entry sample.

## Entry criteria

- Phase 1 for chronicles in canon.
- Founder decision 5 (manuscript access).

## Exit criteria (definition of done)

- Sample-based yield and overlap recorded.
- Swahili coast counted separately (11 files).
- Validate 0 errors; cite-check 0 fail.

## Lane mechanics

Waves by city or tradition. Waves of 10 (5+5 across two writers); at most 25 entities per batch; schema-shape checklist in every brief; cite-check gated on the literal "0 fail"; validate gated on the literal "0 errors" as a separate asserted step; queue file with `[ ]` / `[x]` / `[~]`; STOPs logged, not guessed; founder flags one line each; no-skip rule within scope.

## Dependencies on other phases

- Contends with 6 and 8 for shared `place/` entries (Timbuktu institutions belong to Phase 8).

## Known risks

- Chronicles' attributions are disputed in places: each claim labelled with its source.

## Founder decisions needed before this phase starts

- Decision 5 (access).
