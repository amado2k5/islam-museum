# Phase 06: Southeast Asia sweep

Part of the coverage expansion programme. Master plan: [`README.md`](README.md). Progress: [`progress.md`](progress.md).

## Scope

Kingdoms, scholars, pondok and pesantren traditions, poets and patrons of the Malay-Indonesian world, Patani and Brunei included. Malay, Indonesian and Dutch sources admitted on the same terms as any other.

## Source corpora and access

- Sources to be listed by the founder: Malay and Indonesian scholarly works; Dutch colonial sources. **Not checked in this session.**
- Open-access encyclopaedic sources: to be listed; no access claim is made here.

## Size estimate and derivation

Unsized. Presence measure (section 2.5 of the README): Indonesia 242 files, Malaysia 117, Brunei 38, Patani 4 (text-term matches, not curated). Yield per source not measured. First deliverable: a 30-entity sample from a named source, with overlap recorded.

## Entry criteria

## Exit criteria (definition of done)

- Sample-based yield and overlap recorded.

## Lane mechanics

Waves by sub-region: Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei, Patani. Waves of 10 (5+5 across two writers); at most 25 entities per batch; schema-shape checklist in every brief; cite-check gated on the literal "0 fail"; validate gated on the literal "0 errors" as a separate asserted step; queue file with `[ ]` / `[x]` / `[~]`; STOPs logged, not guessed; founder flags one line each; no-skip rule within scope. Place pre-check against existing `place/` and `polity/`.
