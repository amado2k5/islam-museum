## Method

- Research in Chrome on the founder's Mac (claude-in-chrome tools), reading only: Wikipedia articles in every language that has one, page text read in the browser; revisions, Wikidata sitelinks and Q-numbers read through the Wikipedia and Wikidata APIs from the same browser tab (`fetch(…&origin=*)`). No signed-in site was used; nothing was downloaded, so nothing went to the microSD.
- Enrich the existing thin listings first (seeds §16 named leads: the Islamic banks), in place: every Devin listings-lane draft of 16 October 2026 that cited only Wikidata is rewritten from a recorded Wikipedia revision, with its unsourced superlatives dropped and listed below.
- `made.by_muslims: true` with `basis: documented` rests on the cited article's statement that the bank operates under Sharia (a Sharia board, an Islamic-banks law, an Amiri decree, or the bank's own description), named in the record's prose; no founder's or owner's faith is inferred. State-owned banks name the state as owner in prose.
- Rankings only from a cited list with its year, as `honours` on `award/forbes-middle-east-lists` with `result: ranked` and `position`. Industry awards without an award record stay in prose (excluded table).
- Disputes (lawsuits, detentions, loan scandals) are told with each side attributed, the court or body named, no verdict.
