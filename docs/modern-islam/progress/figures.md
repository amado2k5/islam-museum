# Modern Islam Browser — figures: progress

Section file for the figures waves (seeds §0.1 order: figures first; within figures the field "Reciters, muezzins and munshids" first, D-004). Newest first. Lane-wide state and decisions stay in `../progress.md`.

## Status

- **6 November 2026** — Wave 1, batch 03 (`modern-w1-b03`, Claude Code, claude-opus-5-5): the first muezzins of the Masjid al-Haram — `person/ali-ahmad-mulla` (head of the muezzins; birth 1945/1947 and appointment 1975/1984 kept as the sources differ), `person/farouk-hadrawi` (Haram since Muharram 1415 AH, Namira since 1410 AH), `person/ibrahim-al-madani` (Haram 2018 until his death on 21 September 2026) — and three reciters: `person/omar-al-qazabri` (imam of the Hassan II Mosque since Ramadan 2005), `person/al-zain-muhammad-ahmad` (Sudan; Sayyida Sanhouri Mosque since 2001), `person/abdelaziz-al-karaouri` (Casablanca, d. 2025). Thirteen sources, including the Arabic list of the Haramain muezzins (revision 76685271).
- **6 November 2026** — Wave 1, batch 02 (`modern-w1-b02`, Claude Code, claude-opus-5-5), reciters beyond Egypt, same method: `person/fares-abbad` (Yemen, b. 1980; imam in Dhahran since 2006), `person/ibrahim-al-akhdar` (Medina, b. 1364 AH/1944; imam of the Haram and of the Prophet's Mosque), `person/kani-karaca` (Istanbul, 1930–2004; hafiz and mevlid singer, `munshid`), `person/aziz-alili` (b. 1968; muezzin of the Emperor's Mosque, Sarajevo, 1984–1986, then imam of the Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque; `muezzin`), `person/maria-ulfah` (Indonesia, b. 1955; first woman to win an international recitation award in Malaysia, 1980). Ten sources (ar, tr, de and en Wikipedia; Wikidata), each at its recorded revision.
- **6 November 2026** — Wave 1, batch 01 (`modern-w1-b01`, Claude Code, claude-opus-5-5), Egyptian reciters, researched in Chrome on the founder's Mac (Wikipedia and Wikidata, public pages, read only). Six new person records with ar/en/ur prose: `person/mohamed-mahmoud-al-tablawi` (1934–2020; head of the reciters' syndicate 2011–2020), `person/mahmud-ali-al-banna` (1926–1985; deputy head of the syndicate from 1984), `person/ahmad-nuaina` (b. 1954; Chief of Egyptian Quran Recitations since October 2025), `person/mahmoud-al-shahat-anwar` (b. 1984), `person/raghib-mustafa-ghalwash` (1938–2016; also gave the adhan at al-Hussein Mosque, so `muezzin`), `person/muhammad-jebril` (b. 1964; the 2015 dispute told with both sides attributed). Twelve sources, each at its recorded revision. Footprint from Wikidata sitelinks read the same day.
