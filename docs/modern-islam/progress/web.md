## Method notes

- Many famous Muslim apps have no Wikipedia article in any language (Muslim Pro's English article was deleted on 5 August 2022; IslamicFinder, Athan, Tarteel, Quran Majeed, Pillars and Qalbox have none found by title or search in en, ar, id, ms, fr, tr, de or ur). For those the defined sources are the maker's own pages (cited for what the maker says about itself, attributed), press, and the store pages.
- Store figures: the Google Play install badge is read on the page, dated, recorded as `reach` with `platform: play-store`, `metric: installs` and the lower bound of the band; the rating and review count go in the note. Ministry or company totals across platforms stay in prose, attributed, because the reach enum has no all-platform value.
- Two Wikidata items describe Nusuk (Q139822647 and Q114516279); the footprint is their union and the record uses the first.

- **Schema gap, for the founder:** `listing` requires `made` with a boolean `by_muslims`, so a listing cannot say "not established". This batch writes `made: { by_muslims: false, basis: unverified }` where no source attests the makers' faith (Nusuk, Muzz, SalamWeb, Muslim Pro); read it as "not established", not as "made by non-Muslims". Minhaj carries `true, documented` (a platform of the Muslim World League, launched by the head of its scholars' body). A schema change (an optional or three-valued `by_muslims`) would need its own approved proposal.

## Held for the founder's decision (web)
