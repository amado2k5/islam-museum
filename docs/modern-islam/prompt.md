## 3. The window and the inclusion rule

- A record belongs in the browser when at least one dated fact (launch, release, first broadcast, first edition, founding, award, match, public work) falls on or after 3 January 1976, or it carries a `horizon` (upcoming) block. The section is derived by `scripts/modern/sections.ts` from class, kind, dates and fields; you never type a section on a record.
- **Subject:** about Islam or Muslims, or made by Muslims. Non-Muslim makers of works about Islam are in the sections about works, with `made.by_muslims: false` and the basis. Critics and opponents are in, labelled and attributed, with time-scoped `story_roles` (plan 13).
- **Muslims by default.** A Muslim maker or subject is included by default. An explicitly sourced non-Sunni affiliation is **labelled, not excluded**: `traditions` on the record, and in prose the label before the view ("In Twelver Shia tradition, …"). Sunni positions come first wherever views are stated (`docs/scope.md`).
- **Faith only with basis.** `faith.value` is set only when **two independent sources name the specific school or order**; an infobox, a Wikidata value or a category never settles it; "Sunni" or "Shia" alone settles nothing; headship of an order or office never counts; doubt stays `unknown` with the attestations in the note. A nasheed career, a Muslim-majority country, an Islamic subject or a Muslim name is not an attestation. (`docs/agents/lanes/modern-figures.md`, rulings 1 to 6.)
- **"By Muslims" with basis.** `made.by_muslims: true` only with `basis: self-identified` (the maker's own public statement, linked as a source) or `documented` (a reliable source). `unverified` is recorded and never shown as "by Muslims".
- **Out:** incidental appearances; subjects whose only signal is one social-media post; violent extremist media; private individuals; personal data beyond public roles; lyrics; article text; embeds.

## 5. What a complete record looks like

Every record you write ships with: `label` and `summary` in ar, en and ur; its class fields; `relations` written once (inverses are computed); `external_ids` (at least `wikidata` where an item exists); `footprint` (Wikipedia languages with the date checked, Wikidata, catalogues, press); `honours[]` where any award, certification, chart or ranking is sourced; `reach[]` where you read a dated figure; `horizon` where the thing is upcoming; `sources` (defined records, one is enough for a fact, none invented); `provenance` (`layer`, `source` naming what you read and when, `generated_by`, `model`, `run: modern-<section>-b<nn>`); `status: { review: published, completeness: 3 }` once it cites defined sources. Dates are date objects: EDTF, Hijri string, calendar, precision, conversions marked `derived`. No typed honorific. No typed Qur'an text. No quotation over 40 words.

**Rich first pass.** Write the full record and the full prose the first time, with every well-sourced detail the sources give: who, what, when, where, why it matters, what came before and after, who said what about it, where it stands today. Do not leave enrichment for later; later work is revision only (`docs/agents/content-standards.md`).

## 6. Prose: Arabic first, three languages, the museum's voice

- `content/ar/<class>/<slug>.md` first, then `content/en/` and `content/ur/` in the same commit, frontmatter `id`, `lang`, `source_lang: ar`, `translators: []`, with a `provenance` block and `status.review: published`. English is a full mirror of the Arabic; Urdu may be shorter (lead and key sections) where the `amr-khaled` model does so, and full where it can.
- Voice: a warm, precise, neutral docent. Describe, never prescribe; no rulings, no sermon voice, no superlatives the sources do not give. "The museum" never says "enemy of Islam", "the best", "the most important", "a true Muslim", or any equivalent; it says who said what, when, with a citation.
- Every fact-stating sentence carries `{cite:<source-id>}`. A dated platform figure in prose reads "the channel showed about 12 million subscribers in November 2026 {cite:…}", never "has 12 million followers".
- Sections for a work (website, album, film, series, book): what it is · who made it · when and where · what it is about (link historical subjects with `[[class/slug|label]]`) · how it was received, attributed · honours · where it stands today · trivia (cited) · references. For an event (conference, festival, protest, competition): what it is · how it began · how it runs (editions, venues, attendance with status) · what it produced (declarations, recordings, outcomes) · what was said about it, attributed · today · references. For an organisation, outlet, company, institution: what it is · founding · what it does · leaders and structure · reach and footprint (dated) · positions and criticism attributed · today · references. For a person: the `amr-khaled` model.
- Contested matter: the `perspectives` block with Sunni positions first, then the academic view, then any other, each cited and labelled, no verdict; hard questions as tension cards with at least two cited voices.
- Sensitive content (plan 05): no graphic description of violence, no perpetrator media, no naming of private victims; living persons by their public roles only.

## 11. Git, gates and reporting (every role)

- One worktree per session; explicit paths only (never `git add -A`); never force-push; never `git checkout -- <file>`, `git reset --hard`, `git clean`, `git stash drop` or revert what you did not create; never replace a shared file wholesale (`data/_stubs.yaml`, `data/_stubs.d/*`, `docs/status.md`, enums, i18n: append-only or key-level merges).
- Before every push: `git fetch origin`, read `git log HEAD..origin/main --stat` and the diff of every file you also touch, three-way merge keeping both sides' intent; on an unresolvable conflict stop and write it down for the founder.
- Chain the gates and the push with `&&`, never `;`. Fix what fails; never lower a rule, skip a test, widen a timeout or add a crash guard to hide bad data. A `main` already broken by someone else is fixed first in its own small commit that says so.
- Every commit ends with `AI-Agent:`, `AI-Model:`, `AI-Editor:` (truthful; `unknown` if you cannot tell) and the co-author trailer the harness gives you; the same facts go in each record's `provenance`.
- Every change appends its `CANARY.md` entry under the current version block, grouped by kind, written for readers.
- Every entity commit regenerates `docs/coverage.md` (`npm run coverage`) and `docs/modern-islam/coverage.md` (`npm run modern:census`).
- Push small validated commits often; open the PR; merge it yourself once the gates pass; no review is required. Finished work is never left uncommitted, unpushed or in an open PR.
- **Never deploy.** Merged is not live. Each report says what the founder must deploy and whether a Worker or workflow file is among the files.
- Report accurately: what merged, what was excluded and why, what failed and the output, what was skipped. Nothing is invented: not a date, a quote, a page, an ISBN, a figure, a citation, an award.
