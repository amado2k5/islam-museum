Scope note: ten themes and nothing wider — the obligations (`fard`), the individual obligation (`fard-ayn`), the communal obligation (`fard-kifaya`), the acts of obedience (`taat`), the obligatory (`wajib`), the recommended (`mustahabb`), the forbidden (`haram`), the disliked (`makruh`), the minor sins (`saghair`), and the founder's «وخلافه», read as what remains of the scale of the five rulings: the permitted and the concession (`mubah`). The major sins belong to the Hudud and Kaba'ir Browser, the lawful and unlawful by subject to the Halal and Haram Browser, each act of worship to the Ibadat Browser. No new book was downloaded for this build: the books of usul and fiqh the themes need were already OCR'd on the harvest drive (section 2). The museum's voice rule stands: the browser describes what the texts and the jurists say and issues no ruling.

## 6. Known limits (say them plainly)

- The translations (English, Urdu, French, Spanish) of the six new records, the interface strings and the labels and summaries of the harvested passages are machine-made and not read by a person.
- OCR excerpts are machine-read and not corrected; a window the reader garbled is skipped by rule, but short misreadings remain.
- The detector reads words, not meanings: a passage that discusses an obligation without naming it is not found, and a report whose only wording is «نهى عن» is deliberately not classed.
- The `fard-ayn` theme has no Qur'an verses of its own, because the Qur'an does not use the term; its verses are listed under the obligations.
- Page numbers cited from the scans (al-Risala, al-Mustasfa, al-Burhan, Irshad al-Fuhul) are the scan's page numbers as the source records describe them, not always the printed ones.
- The numbering of the hadith-api editions is the edition's own; for Sahih Muslim it differs from the common numbering.

## 7. What comes next

A person's read of the translations and the OCR excerpts; al-Juwayni's al-Waraqat, Ibn Qudama's Rawdat al-Nazir and al-Amidi's al-Ihkam when their scans are acquired and read; Musnad Ahmad for «إياكم ومحقرات الذنوب» and «إن الله يحب أن تؤتى رخصه»; a `perspectives` block on the Hanafi and majority readings of fard and wajib when the Hanafi usul books are harvested; a record per well-known communal obligation where sources allow.
