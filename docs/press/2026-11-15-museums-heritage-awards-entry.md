# islam.museum Entered for the Museums + Heritage Awards 2027

islam.museum has been entered for the **Museums + Heritage Awards 2027**, in the
**Best Digital Experience – Global** category (entry 2636-Digital). The entry
was submitted and paid on **15 November 2026** at the early-bird rate of £100.

The category is the awards' global digital category, open to websites, apps and
online engagement from anywhere in the world. The entry presents the museum as
it honestly is: a one-person, non-profit project — free for everyone and
personally funded by its founder — whose published scale stood at 58,128
entries across 116,000 pages when last verified live (12 November 2026). It
states plainly how the museum is made: entries are researched, drafted,
translated and checked with AI assistance against the sources recorded for each
one, most records are labelled openly as drafts, and every record shows its
sources so that any reader can verify it.

## What happens next

- Entries remain editable until **29 January 2027**, and the museum's entry
  will be strengthened within that window as its content continues to grow.
- The shortlist is announced in the **week of 15 March 2027**.
- The ceremony is on **12 May 2027** at the Hilton Park Lane, London.

islam.museum is a non-profit project, not a business: it is free for everyone —
free to visit, free to download and run locally, free to use as a template and
free to query through its public API — and it is personally funded by its
founder.
