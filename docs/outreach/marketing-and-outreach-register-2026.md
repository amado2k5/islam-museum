# Marketing & outreach register — October to 13 November 2026

_Compiled 13 November 2026. This is the museum's own log of every marketing and
outreach attempt from the project's public start in October 2026 to 13 November
2026: what was sent, submitted, posted or pitched, to whom, and what came back.
It exists so that no target is ever pitched twice, no reply is lost, and any
future helper can see the whole effort at a glance._

**How it was compiled.** From the master send checklist, the project's daily
logs and pack files, and a full sweep of the founder's Gmail Sent folder on
13 November 2026. Sent is treated as ground truth: a send whose tool reported a
timeout is almost always in Sent, and several attempts the working notes had
marked "drafted, not sent" or left unconfirmed were in fact sent — the register
records them as sent and the record-keeping notes at the end say which ones.

**Standing rules the outreach runs under.**

- **Non-profit, not a business.** islam.museum is a non-profit project — free
  for everyone, personally funded by its founder. Every outward text says so.
  Fundraising framing, everywhere: "Free for everyone — but not free for the
  founder." There is no budget for paid partnerships; help is sought from
  volunteers and free partners, who are named and thanked on the site.
- **The founder sends most email himself.** Agents draft, research and, only
  within bounded batches he expressly directs, send. Facebook messages are
  drafted by agents and sent by the founder himself.
- **No-duplicate rule.** Every target is checked against this register and the
  master checklist before any send; different desks of the same organisation
  count as the same target.
- **Search Sent before every send.** Whatever any summary says, the recipient
  is searched in the Sent folder first. (A duplicate proposal sent on
  11 November, after the 10 November send dropped out of a working summary, is
  why this rule is written down — see the Bitsmedia row.)
- **An address that never answered is not emailed again.** Find a direct
  contact for the person instead (applied 13 November: see the NCCM row).
- **Claim discipline.** For the UNESCO–Sharjah Prize the museum is *seeking a
  nomination* through eligible partner organisations; it has not been
  "submitted to UNESCO" or "nominated". The DPGA application is *submitted,
  under review*. Nothing in outreach claims more.
- **Privacy.** This register names organisations, outlets, institutions and
  public offices only. Private individuals — the friends wave, family-adjacent
  contacts, community members — appear in aggregate only. No personal email
  addresses, phone numbers or home addresses appear anywhere in it.

**How to read the outcome column.** *Delivered* = in Sent with no bounce in the
window checked. *No reply* = none recorded as of 13 November 2026. Pack rows
count the letters actually sent, not the letters drafted; items a pack held
back for the founder's decision are named as held, never as sent.
