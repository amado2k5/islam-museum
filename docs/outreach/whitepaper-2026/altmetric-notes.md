# Altmetric: a factual note

Written 15 November 2026.

- Altmetric tracks mentions of research outputs in the sources it follows, and matches a mention to an output by an
  identifier such as a DOI. Its own explanation is at https://www.altmetric.com/about-us/our-data/how-does-it-work/
  (read 15 November 2026).
- No Altmetric score is claimed for the dataset (10.5281/zenodo.23692511) or for the paper
  (10.5281/zenodo.24273663). Neither was looked up.
- A mention can be matched only if it carries the DOI or a link that resolves to the DOI record. A mention of
  "islam.museum" by name, or a link to the site alone, is not matched to either record.

## Legitimate routes by which a mention may arise

| Route | Condition |
|---|---|
| News coverage | A journalist or outlet chooses to cover the project and links the DOI |
| Blogs and scholarly newsletters | An independent author writes about the resource and links the DOI |
| Policy and institutional documents | A body cites the dataset or the paper in a published document |
| Wikipedia | An editor with no conflict of interest cites the paper or dataset where it is a suitable source under Wikipedia's rules. The author should not add it himself |
| Wikidata | Only if and when an item legitimately exists. The earlier item Q141801006 is deleted; no live Wikidata item is claimed |
| The author's own announcements | Permitted and ordinary: one announcement per channel, with the DOI link, stating what the paper is |

## Not to be done

No requests for reposts or mentions in exchange for anything, no repeated posting of the DOI, no automated or
duplicate accounts, and no wording designed to provoke engagement. A score produced that way describes nothing.
