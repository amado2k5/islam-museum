## The ICT in Education Prize, reassessed

The founder's first letter of 17 October named this prize. The Education Explorer has launched since: 65 courses of the Ontario curriculum and 361 lessons (counted in the repository), with course pages in Arabic, English and Urdu. The Urdu pages are live and carry Urdu text and the draft label in Urdu (one course page read on 15 November; the repository holds 645 course prose files in each language). The evidence, set against the 2026 rules (agent-read from the guidelines PDF; the prize page itself was read first-hand):

| Rule (2026 edition) | The museum on 15 November 2026 | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| "The project should have been implemented for at least 1 year with evidence of concrete activities" | Public since 11 October 2026. The Education Explorer opened in November 2026 | **Not met for a 2027 deadline in May** (seven months), unless UNESCO counts the prototype year, which had no learners |
| No self-nomination; a National Commission or an NGO in official partnership nominates | The Canadian Commission abstains from all prize applications. ICDE nominates only from its member organisations | **The same gate as the Sharjah Prize, and narrower:** this prize's rules, as read, do not admit former laureates, eminent personalities or civil society organisations |
| Relevant to the theme of the year | 2025 and 2026 themes were both about artificial intelligence in education. No 2027 theme is published | Unknown |
| "Evidence of learning": how learners' capabilities developed | 361 lessons labelled, in all three languages, as drafts written with AI help and not yet read by a human editor. No measured use | **Not met** |
| Free to its users | Met | |
| Not affiliated with or funded by UNESCO | Met | |

**Verdict.** The museum is not ready for this prize in 2027, and saying otherwise to a nominator would cost the goodwill needed in 2028. Three things would make a 2028 file real:

1. **A year of use with evidence.** The pilot being planned with the International Islamic Youth League (first meeting 24 November 2026) is exactly this: educators reading the lessons, marking them, and the corrections published.
2. **Lessons read by people.** A prize for learning will not go to lessons whose own label says no human has read them.
3. **A nominator found early.** ICDE's reply of 21 October means membership, and membership is for institutions and organisations, which ties this route to the planned Ontario incorporation. Other partner NGOs in the field, on UNESCO's August 2025 list (agent-read): EUROCLIO (history educators), EIFL, Creative Commons, the International Association of Universities.
