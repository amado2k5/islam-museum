## If no letter arrives

Nothing is filed, and nothing is said in public beyond "seeking a nomination". The 22nd edition then leaves a dossier that was true on the day it was written, letter models that meet Article 6.3, a reading of the Statutes checked against the text, and a few scholars and one youth organisation who know the museum. That is not a nomination. It is a prepared file carried into the next call, with a year of public record behind it and an endorser asked months ahead instead of days.

## The founder's decisions, and an honest assessment

The cold letters: send or hold. The Youth League: ask at the meeting or keep the pilot clean of it (my advice: the second). One licence wording everywhere: "data CC0, prose CC BY-SA 4.0, code MIT" is what the repository's files say. The gift-shop and Trustees wording, before an endorser opens the site ([`claims-corrections.md`](claims-corrections.md), S1 and S2).

A nomination can still be filed if one of two professors, or one of four bodies that have never heard the request, signs a page within ten days. Winning is another matter: a jury reading Article 3 as written will look for years the museum does not have, among more than a hundred candidates.
