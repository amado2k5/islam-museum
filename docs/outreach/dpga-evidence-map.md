## What the application cannot yet say

- A legal entity (incorporation in progress; see `GOVERNANCE.md`). The registry accepts individual-owned projects; the form asks for ownership documents, not a company.
- Gatekeeping, not record review: the museum does not check whether the statements inside each record are true. It does gatekeep the material it admits: each book, article, image or artefact is admitted, labelled or refused as a whole, on the basis of its author's school and the authenticity of its edition or institutional copy. The gate is written down (`scripts/harvest/library/authenticity.yaml`), checked by a script (`scripts/check-authenticity.ts`, run by `npm run validate`), applied before any book is fetched (`scripts/harvest/library/authenticity_gate.py`), and published on the site at `/{lang}/authenticity/`. Each harvested book also has a metadata record with SHA-256 fixity hashes in the repository (`docs/authenticity-ledger/books/`); no book text is kept in git. Since 24 Oct 2026 records are published under a citation standard (`docs/agents/publication-standard.md`): every record cites defined sources with recorded provenance, one source is enough for any fact, and readers judge from the citations shown. Records written under the earlier rules still carry `draft` status values. Describe the publication standard and the provenance rule; do not claim reviewed or verified content.
- A third-party accessibility audit; the Lighthouse accessibility budget is self-assessed and not yet run in CI.
- A citable release with a DOI (planned).
