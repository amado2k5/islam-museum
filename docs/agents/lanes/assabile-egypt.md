# Lane `assabile-egypt`

_Opened 1 November 2026 on the founder's instruction. Method: [`../audio-video-harvest.md`](../audio-video-harvest.md) (§4b for the queues). Progress: [`../../delegation/assabile-egypt-progress.md`](../../delegation/assabile-egypt-progress.md)._

## Scope

The recorded lessons of the speakers on the Assabile Egypt list (https://www.assabile.com/lessons/egypt) other than Omar Abdelkafy, who has his own lane (`omar-abdelkafy`): Amr Khaled, Muhammad Metwally al-Shaarawy, Mahmoud al-Masri, Abla al-Kahlawi, Mustafa Hosny, Muhammad Hussein Yacoub, Abu Ishaq al-Heweny, Yusuf al-Qaradawi, Zaghloul El-Naggar, Wagdy Ghoneim, Mosaad Anwar, Mustafa al-Adawi, Ragheb El-Sergani, Abd al-Rahman Mansour, Ahmed Abdussalam, Ahmad Farid, Sayed Elbshbeshy, Abdul Hamid Kishk, Ateya Muhammad Salem.

## What each series yields

1. One `work` `kind: lecture-series` per series, `authors`/`delivered` → the speaker's `person`, cited to `source/assabile-<speaker>`; it appears in the Khutab and Durus Explorer.
2. Topic entities (concepts, practices, events, persons, works) and enrichment of existing ones, every factual sentence cited `{cite:assabile-<speaker> <series>/epNN hh:mm:ss}`.
3. The speaker's `person` record created (from Wikipedia/Wikidata facts per the broad rule, with the faith rule of `publication-standard.md`) or enriched.

## Rules

As `audio-video-harvest.md` §2 and the `omar-abdelkafy` lane's standing founder decisions (exclusions, opinions and rants, contested matter in `perspectives`, polemical flagged). Speakers whose views are contested are described and attributed, never judged in the museum's voice.
