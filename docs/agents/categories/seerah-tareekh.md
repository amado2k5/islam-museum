## CATEGORY: seerah, shamail and tareekh (chronicles, histories, geography, travel)

Follow the book-mining procedure of docs/plan/17-raheeq-makhtum.md §3 to §6 (claims table, entity extraction per class, batch order) and docs/plan/18-sira-ibn-hisham.md (rules on isnads, doubt markers, dates). Those documents already mined two sira books; this book is a further witness. What this changes:
- Do not create parallel events. Search `data/event/` first; add this book's page as another source (and its isnad/variant) to the existing event. Create new events/sub-events only for what is genuinely absent; a different date or number given by this book becomes a second dated claim with its own source and `status: debated`, never an overwrite.
- Classes: `event` (with sides, commanders, casualties, sizes as sourced fields), `person` (full nasab, dates), `place` (kind, coordinates only from docs/plan/16 §6 or Wikidata), `polity` and `period` for chronicles beyond the Prophetic era (caliphates, dynasties: rulers as `person` with reign dates, capitals as `place`, `part_of` chains), `journey` (legs with from/to/date/mode/page), `work` (letters, treaties, sermons, poems the book reports), `dataset` rows (CSV with a source column) for lists of battles, rulers, governors, delegations.
- Chronicles (al-Tabari, Ibn al-Athir, Ibn Kathir's Bidaya): batch by hijri year (annals) or by reign; capture the annalist's own isnad/attribution and «قال الواقدي / قال أبو مخنف» markers. Contested episodes (the fitna, Karbala, Siffin, the Saqifa, the Abbasid revolution) use `perspectives`: Sunni historiographical position(s) first, the academic view, then other labelled views, no verdict, Tier A review flag in the progress file.
- Geography and travel (Mu'jam al-Buldan, al-Muqaddasi, Ibn Battuta): `place` entities with historical names, kind, region, `part_of`; distances and descriptions as attributed claims; coordinates only from the allowed sources.
- Shamail/dalail (al-Tirmidhi's Shamail, al-Shifa, Dala'il): descriptive entries as attributed reports; `practice`/`concept` for terms; no depictions, no visual descriptions rendered as images.
Batching: sections of about 25 entities; start with the introduction and biography of the author, then the Prophetic-era sections, then later periods in chronological order.
