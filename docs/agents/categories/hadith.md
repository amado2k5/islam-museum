## CATEGORY: hadith collections (and commentaries, hadith sciences, rijal when noted)

What to extract, by class:
- `source/<id>`: the collection or commentary (edition, volumes). `work/<id>`: the work itself: author (person), genre `hadith-collection`, structure (number of books/chapters as printed), recension/riwaya, transmission to the museum's main time periods. One `work` per collection; chapters are not entities unless the museum cites them.
- `person`: the compiler (full name with nasab, kunya, laqab, birth and death in Hijri as the book or its introduction gives, teachers and students named, travels, school/madhhab, creed tradition `sunni`), plus the narrators the museum needs: the Companions at the top of isnads the book lists for the cited reports (reuse existing Companion entities), key tabi'un. Rijal books (tahdhib, mizan, jarh wa ta'dil): record each named scholar's verdicts as attributed claims ("قال أحمد: ثقة"), never one museum-voice verdict; where critics disagree, use a `perspectives` block with the critics named.
- `passage` (hadith) only for reports that an existing event, practice, concept or person page cites: `unit: hadith`, `work`, `ref` such as `bukhari:3`, matn excerpt ≤ 40 words, isnad head as printed, grade with grader and book/page, and a `mentions`/`evidence_for`-type relation (check predicates) to the entity it documents. Report the collection's own numbering and say which numbering (Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi, Muhammad Zuhayr al-Nasir, Dar al-Salam…).
- `concept` and `practice`: when a chapter heading defines an act or term (wudu', the six pillars of iman, qiyam), create it with `summary.ar` from the collection's chapter plus 2 to 3 anchoring reports, each cited.
- `event`: reports that narrate datable events (the first revelation, the Hijra, the Farewell Pilgrimage): add the report as a source with its isnad head on the existing event rather than creating a parallel event.
- Edges to the Qur'an: `revealed_about` only when the report itself names the occasion; quote the report's words (≤ 40), never the ayah.
Batching: by book/kitab of the collection (كتاب الإيمان, كتاب الوضوء…) or by 40 to 60 printed pages of a commentary. Do NOT try to enter whole collections: target the reports and chapters that connect to entities already in `data/` (run `grep -rl` on names), then the introductions and bio sections; breadth comes from linking, not from copying.
Quality checks: compiler dates agree with a second source (the introduction plus a rijal book); every grade names its grader; no hadith is called sahih/da'if in the museum's own voice.
