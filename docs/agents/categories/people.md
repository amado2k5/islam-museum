## CATEGORY: sahaba, sahabiyat, tabaqat and biographical dictionaries

What to extract:
- `person` for every entry the museum needs, in this priority: (1) people already referenced by existing entities (grep `data/_stubs*.yaml` and relations for unresolved person targets: resolve them first), (2) the ten promised paradise, the four caliphs, the Badr veterans, the wives and children of the Prophet, the Muhajirat and Ansar women, the Companions who narrated most, (3) the rest by tabaqa order of the book.
- Fields: `names[]` with full Arabic name and nasab, kunya, laqab, nisba; `gender`; birth and death as the book gives them (Hijri string, with place; many give relative dates: record as a note anchored to an event or ruler); tribe/clan `member_of`; family edges (`child_of`, `spouse_of`, `sibling_of` per predicates); conversion (when/where/at whose hand, as reported, with the book's doubt markers); hijra(s); battles and expeditions with role; offices; narration count only if the book states it; manner of death and burial place (`died_in`, `buried_at` if available). Faith status: `traditions: [sunni]` for Companions with faith basis "tradition"; a person's faith is recorded only with its basis, never inferred.
- Sahabiyat: women appear as full persons, not as footnotes to men: their own roles (narrators, fighters, nurses at battles, poets, scholars, traders), `story_roles`, and their sources. Do not generalise from one report to "women in Islam"; keep to attributed, cited particulars.
- Disputes among Companions (Jamal, Siffin, the killing of Uthman): describe actions and attribute judgements; `perspectives` with the Sunni position first (the Ahl al-Sunna stance of restraint is itself a position to cite, with who stated it).
- Rijal-style verdicts on Companions: the Sunni creed holds the Companions' 'adala; record that as the position of named scholars with a source, and do not grade individuals.
- `group` for tribes/clans and for categories (السابقون الأولون, أهل بدر, أهل الصفة, العشرة المبشرة) with `members`/`member_of` edges; `dataset` rows (CSV with a source column) for lists such as Badr veterans or Uhud martyrs.
Batching: by tabaqa or by letter in the dictionaries, at most 25 persons a batch. For giant dictionaries (al-Isaba, Usd al-Ghaba, Tahdhib al-Kamal, Siyar) do NOT process every entry in order: work from the museum's priority list (above) and look the entries up through the book's alphabetical order or index.
Quality checks: no duplicate persons (search label.ar and the ASCII slug; check `names[]` of existing people and the Prophetic-era people of the `people` lane); every dated fact has two sources or a flag; kunya/nasab agree between this book and the second source.
