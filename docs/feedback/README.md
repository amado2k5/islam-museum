# Feedback on islam.museum — collected 2026-11-07

What people have sent about islam.museum itself — impressions, praise, criticism, feature requests and usability observations — gathered from Facebook/Messenger, Instagram, email and institutional replies received up to 7 November 2026.

**Privacy note:** names are included at the founder's direction. Quotations are limited strictly to the parts of each message that concern the website/museum; personal content (greetings, family news, condolences, personal history) has been removed. Entries marked *(summary)* are faithful summaries from the records where no verbatim text survives; entries marked *(verbatim)* quote the person's own words, website-related parts only.

## Recurring themes

- **Discoverability — where do I start, and good sections feel hidden.** Raised by Wira Perdana (visitors need a starting point by interest — period, place, person, theme), Abdalla Gamal (many nice sections feel hidden and should be surfaced more easily) and Abdullahi an-Na'im (asked simply how he can access and explore it).
- **Sources need to be more prominent and clearer.** Raised by Wira Perdana (make sources easy to find, especially where historical accounts differ) and Abdalla Gamal (sources appear as comma-separated links with unclear numbers/counts).
- **Mobile experience.** Abdalla Gamal found the timeline a bit hard to browse on mobile; Islam Ali likewise said he would review the site properly on a larger screen.
- **More visuals and interactivity.** Mahmoud Nasr asked for more pictures and videos, AI-generated visuals and interactive VR; Wira Perdana asked for more visual storytelling (illustrations, interactive maps, timelines, simple simulations); Abdalla Gamal wants more interactive sections and more detailed adult sections.
- **Audio.** Wira Perdana suggested audio versions of articles, Audible-style.
- **Education and children — beyond the West.** Mahmoud Nasr argued the museum is as useful for teenagers and children in Egypt and the Arab countries as in the West, and proposed a competitions section (Kahoot-style) that schools and Islamic lesson centres could use.
- **Contactability.** Abdalla Gamal suggested making contact details (WhatsApp, email) easier to find so people can reach the founder with more detail.
