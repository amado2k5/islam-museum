## Differences between language records, and how the connection texts handled them

- concept/saa, Urdu record: it gives the Medinese sa' as "five and a half" Iraqi ratls and the Medinese mudd as "a ratl and a half", where the English and Arabic records say five and a third and a ratl and a third. The connection gives no ratl figures in any language and says only that the Medinese sa' is smaller and that a sa' is four mudds.
- concept/saa, English summary mentions a "drinking vessel" gloss; not restated.
- concept/dhira-fiqh: the Urdu record's opening omits the sense of the measuring cubit that the Arabic and English records give; the record was therefore not linked (it would need a connection written from the Arabic and English alone).
- Dates: the connection to event/egypt-census-837 gives "837 AH" and "ھ" / "هـ" as the records do. "The end of the eighth century" prints without an era in the records; the card now says "eighth century AH" ("الهجري", "ہجری"), as the fleet review asked, because the census is dated in Hijri years.
