# Sweep: social studies and geography (Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8)

Reviewer: fresh context. Rules applied: PIPELINE.md "Fit the grade", "Adjacent is not a link", "Writing a connection text". Read per lesson: the record, the English Brief, and each linked record through `entity_brief.py --full`. Arabic and Urdu cards were checked only for digits (the same numbers appear in all three languages in every card; no mismatch found); nouns were not compared word by word. Nothing was edited except this file.

Verdicts: K = KEEP, F = FIX, R = REMOVE (a unsuitable for grade, b adjacent, c too hard, d ruling or advice in the museum's voice, e debate or talk offered as the subject; "h" = record carries hateful content, which PIPELINE says is never linked). Lessons of `ca-on-g4-ss-a-*` are listed under Grade 4. `ca-on-g5-social-studies-b-1` is in fleet-flagged.md; no Grade 5 lesson belongs to this sweep.

## Patterns

1. The same record is used where it fits and where it does not. `invention/tabula-rogeriana` appears in four lessons (one each in Grades 1, 2, 3 and 4); only the Grade 2 "ways of showing the world" use is honest. A reference to a map is not a map element, a scale or a location.
2. Rulings in the museum's voice survive in cards: `concept/hima` ("would have gained unlawfully", "forfeit"), `concept/agriculture-in-islam` (dead land, three years idle, "the ruler's leave") in G3, G4, G8, and `concept/jiwar` (forty houses, shared walls, pre-emption) in Grade 1. These need either attribution or a cut.
3. "The record says" is used as an attribution (G1 hima, G2 Eid, G2 Ramadan, G3 hima, G4 Medina treaty). It names nobody.
4. Word-history sentences in `concept/sabil` ("siqaya ... fifth century AH") sit in every lesson that uses it, from Grade 1 to Grade 7, and teach nothing the expectation asks for.
5. Hard material is offered to young children: Grade 2 moon-sighting, Grade 2 "seven climes ... twenty-four degrees", an etymological point on "fitr" in Grade 2, aqiqa slaughter in Grade 1.
6. A record can carry hateful or racialised content the card avoids but the page shows: `concept/medina-society-at-hijra` (usury and incitement said of the Jews, Grade 4) and `concept/athar-al-iqlim` (climate and the character, colour and religion of peoples; the Ham story, Grade 7). The card's wording does not protect the student who opens the record.
7. Records that are one sentence (`event/nile-record-low-278`, `place/harrat-al-rajla`) cannot carry an expectation; the Brief admits it ("the record says no more than that").
8. Duplicate use of one record inside a lesson (Fustat twice in Grade 8 a-1, hisba and muhtasib in Grade 4 a-2) adds a card without adding what the expectation asks for.
9. Digits were checked across English, Arabic and Urdu for all 69 cards and agree; the nouns were not compared and should be spot-checked by an Arabic reader before the FIX edits are made.

## Records for the founder

- `concept/medina-society-at-hijra`: prose reporting al-Raheeq al-Makhtum's description of the Jews of Medina ("considered their property lawful, earned through usury and pledges, and incited the tribes against one another"). Hateful content as a reported opinion; should carry attribution and a perspectives block, or be reviewed.
- `concept/athar-al-iqlim`: climate determinism about peoples' character and religion, skin colour and the Ham story; should be reviewed before any use in an educational context.
