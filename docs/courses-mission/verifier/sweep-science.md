Two cross-cutting remarks used below:
- "The record says / the record adds / as the record presents it" appears in many cards. Where it introduces a description it is tolerable; where it introduces a judgement of worth or a view, name the holder instead (flagged per row).
- Cards for ages 6 to 8 reuse the record's scholarly register (Artuqids, Tulunid, qibla-arcade, gnomon, slotted rod, double-acting). Each is flagged FIX where it applies.

## Patterns

1. The earlier grades (1 to 3) were built from records far above the age: Alhazen's reflection problem for age 9, a theory of vision with obsolete anatomy for age 6, castle clocks and slotted-rod pumps for ages 6 to 8. Fit-the-grade was applied to subject matter (weapons, sacrifice) but not to difficulty.
2. "Adjacent" links dominate the removals: hima for "healthy environment" and "ecosystem"; zellij for "factors of suitability"; girih and geometric patterns for "symmetry"; natron and plant-ash glass for "environmental impact"; the double-piston pump for "energy"; Marib dam for "effects of a stable structure"; anatomy and al-Razi for "organs". In each the card's closing question supplies the noun the record lacks.
