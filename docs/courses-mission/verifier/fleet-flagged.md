## Patterns the coordinator should fix in the runbook

1. **Rulings stated as "the museum's record says X".** Wherever a card reports a ruling (abortion, riba, saving, fasting, slaughter), the actor must be the author or body ("the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia records that classical jurists hold..."), never "the record". Add a gate: any card containing forbidden / permitted / obligatory / recommended / must / unlawful needs a named human or institutional subject in the same sentence, in all three languages.
2. **Health and body cards need a not-advice line.** Any card on food, illness, pregnancy, fasting, medicine or contagion must carry, in the card or in the Brief, "this is a historical report or a legal ruling, not medical or dietary advice". Add a trigger list (cupping, honey, hamiyya, diabetes, pregnant, plague, ablution with treated water).
3. **Religious purity is not safety.** For anything about water or food, say that a purity ruling (tahara, halal) is not a hygiene or safety standard.
4. **Do not copy a record's own unattributed sentences into a card as if they were attributed.** "Diet must follow...", "a divinely-wise quarantine before the science of contagion", and "The most honoured of the Islamic arts" are the record's voice, not a source's. A card should quote the source's claim and its holder, or drop the sentence.
5. **A link needs the expectation's own words, and "adjacent" is not enough.** Concrete failures: plant-cell cloning beside genomics (sbi3u-d-1), a charity ruling beside waste-collection technology (svn3m-e-2), consultation of advisers beside "solicit input from the public" (g5 social studies), a levitating-idol legend beside forces (sph3u-c-1), one-liner pattern records beside symmetry (g6 math e-1), a daytime eclipse beside "night sky" (ses4u-b-2). Add a gate: the Brief's first sentence must name which noun in the expectation the record actually contains; if none, no connection.
6. **Wikidata is not a source for evaluative prose.** The "noblest art" superlative has only `source/wikidata`. Add to the card gate: a card whose only source is Wikidata may state entity facts only, no superlatives, causes or rankings. Re-scan the eleven lessons that repeat it and any card citing `source/wikidata` alone.
7. **Check the record before offering a lesson built on it.** `concept/islamufubiya` contains a "Jewish fingers" poll result. Add a gate that blocks a connection when the record contains a poll, a vote, or a percentage from a broadcast, or any line the card would invite the class to read; require a human read of the whole record, not just its first lines. The same gate catches `needs_source` markers in the record's prose (the Suwaidan records).
8. **Sources that are lecturers, preachers or TV programmes need a source-quality note.** Beside "major psychological theories" (hsp3u-c-1) or "Canadian equity issues" (hse4m-c-1), say what the source is and refuse it if the expectation requires scholarship.
9. **Grade-level check.** Add a vocabulary check for Grades 3 to 6: terms such as nisab, riba, idkhar, hadd, fidya, and operations the grade has not taught (multiplying a negative number in Grade 6) are rejected or glossed.
10. **Translation fidelity.** "Soup" for silq (chard) shows English and Arabic/Urdu diverged. Add a check that the cards' key nouns agree across the three languages (a script that tokenises the Arabic and Urdu for the entity's own names and common nouns would catch it), and that units match the record ("leagues", not "farsakhs").
11. **Briefs must say what the record does not contain, in the expectation's own terms.** The good Briefs (sph4u-e-1: "Neither uses a wave model"; hzb3m-d-1: "does not set out the present public debates") do this; the weak ones (sph4u-b-3, hfa4u-c-1, svn3m-e-1) name the topic but not the missing concept (inertial frames, nutritional needs, remediation processes).
12. **Titles must match the content.** "Protecting the public from plague" covers a record on leprosy. Add a check of the lesson title against each linked record's subject.
13. **Prose pages the student opens must contain what the card says.** The al-Khidr Husayn lines are only in `data/concept/shura-in-islam.yaml` `summary.en`, not in the English page; a card should cite text visible on the linked page.
