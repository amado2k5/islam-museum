## Does the page tell the student where they are and what to do next?

Mostly yes: the hero's "Lesson 1 of 4" with dots and "1 of 16 in the course", the numbered sections, the "You are here" box, and the Previous/Next cards make place and next step clear. The weak spots are the step rail (no current step, clipped on phones), the contradictory resume banner versus "Next up" on the map (EN-33), and the classroom view on a phone (EN-1, EN-2).
