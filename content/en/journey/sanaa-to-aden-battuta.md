## Overview
A stage of [[person/ibn-battuta-muhammad|Ibn Battuta]]'s journey in Yemen. He stayed in [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]] and visited its great mosque, then travelled from it to [[place/aden|Aden]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.159}.

## Sanaa
He describes its rains as heavy, flowing downpours, and its mosque as one of the finest mosques, containing a relic of one of the prophets {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.159}.

## Aden
A harbour of the land of Yemen on the coast of the Great Sea, surrounded by mountains, with only one approach to it. It has no crops, no trees and no water, but it has cisterns in which rainwater gathers {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.159}.

The text does not give the duration of the road or its details.
