## Overview
A stage of [[person/ibn-jubayr-muhammad|Ibn Jubayr's]] journey to the pilgrimage. He left [[place/qus|Qus]] on camels with the camel-drivers from al-Mubarraz on the southern side of the town, and then reached [[place/aydhab|Aydhab]] {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.

## In the desert
They set out on Monday, the thirteenth of Safar, and at the beginning of the night they halted at a water known as al-Hajir. After it the book mentions the water points: Umma, Mujallaj, al-'Ashira, two stages from Aydhab, al-Khubayb, Shaghib, Astan, al-'Abdayn, Anqash and Mahatt al-Luqayta. The order of these points is not settled, because their mention in the text runs across two routes {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.

The book states that this road was deadly to pilgrims through thirst {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.

## Aydhab
They arrived on Saturday. It is a city on the coast of the Jeddah sea, without walls, and from it the jalab sail to Jeddah {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.
