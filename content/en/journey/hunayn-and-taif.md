## Overview
The route of the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] after the conquest of Mecca, in Shawwal of the 8th year AH: from Mecca to [[place/wadi-hunayn|the Wadi Hunayn]], where the battle took place; then the pursuit of the remnants of Hawazin and Thaqif to [[place/awtas|Awtas]] and [[place/taif|al-Taif]]; then the siege of al-Taif; and then the return by way of [[place/al-jirana|al-Ji'rana]], with the 'umra, to Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.413-423}.

## The route
The Prophet set out from Mecca with twelve thousand men on the sixth of Shawwal, and reached Hunayn on the night of Wednesday, the tenth of the month. [[person/malik-ibn-awf|Malik ibn Awf]] had gone ahead and had set his ambushes in the valley {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.414-415}. The book does not describe the stages between Mecca and Hunayn, though it mentions the Sidra of Dhat Anwat on the road {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.415}.

After the defeat and the rally, the remnants of the enemy scattered toward al-Taif, Nakhla and Awtas. A group led by Abu Amir al-Ash'ari was sent to Awtas {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.417}.

After the spoils were gathered at al-Ji'rana, the Prophet marched to al-Taif. He passed the Yamani Nakhla, then Qarn al-Manazil, then Liyya, and he ordered the fortress of Malik ibn Awf there to be demolished. Then he camped near the fortress of al-Taif and besieged it {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.418}. The scholars of the sira differ on the length of the siege {cite:fath-al-bari 8/45}.

When the siege dragged on and the fortress resisted, the Prophet decided to leave. He called out the order to depart and said: "We return, repentant, worshipping our Lord and praising Him" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.419}. He then stayed at al-Ji'rana for some fifteen nights, dividing the spoils {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.419-420}.

When the division was complete, he entered ihram for the 'umra from al-Ji'rana and performed it, then turned back to Medina, six nights before the end of Dhu al-Qa'da of the 8th year AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.422}. The text does not describe the stages of the road from Mecca to Medina.

## On the expedition
At al-Ji'rana the Prophet divided the spoils first among those whose hearts were to be reconciled. Some of the Ansar then reproached him, and he gathered them and addressed them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.419-421}. The delegation of Hawazin arrived after the spoils had been distributed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.421-422}.
