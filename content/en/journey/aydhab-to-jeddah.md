## Overview
A stage of [[person/ibn-jubayr-muhammad|Ibn Jubayr]]'s pilgrimage: from [[place/aydhab|Aydhab]] to [[place/jeddah|Jeddah]] across the Red Sea, after a long stay at Aydhab and a hard desert road, which he describes before arriving {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 pp.362–368}.

## The route
The pilgrims boarded the jalba for the crossing to Jeddah on Monday in Rabi' al-Awwal. They stayed at the anchorage because the wind had dropped, then set sail on Tuesday morning. The stay at Aydhab lasted about twenty-three days, during which he complained of hardship, bad water and bad air {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 p.362}.

## On the road
The book describes the desert road that pilgrims coming from the Nile take on camels, with guides from the Beja, and the many who died of thirst on it {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 pp.364–368}.
