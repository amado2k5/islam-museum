## Overview
The earthly route of the Night Journey: from [[place/mecca|the Sacred Mosque in Mecca]] to [[place/jerusalem|the Furthest Mosque in Jerusalem]] on the Buraq, in the company of Gabriel, as part of the [[event/isra-and-miraj|event of the Night Journey and the Ascension]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.137-138}.

## The route
Al-Rahiq states that the Prophet was taken by night from the Sacred Mosque to the Furthest Mosque, riding the Buraq. He then alighted in Jerusalem and led the prophets in prayer {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.138}. The text gives no stations between the two cities and no distance, so only the two endpoints are named here.

## The ascent
After Jerusalem the text describes the ascent to the heavens, the meeting with the prophets there, and the prescription of prayer. This is a matter of faith rather than a geographic route, so it is not included in this route {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.138-140}.

## The return
On these pages the text does not describe the Prophet's return to Mecca by a particular road. It says only that he told his people in the morning what he had seen {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.140}.

## Note on the order
Al-Rahiq gives the chronology in different accounts and does not weigh between them, so no specific date is attributed to this route {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.137}.
