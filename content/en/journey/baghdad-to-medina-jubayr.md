## Overview
A passage from [[person/ibn-jubayr-muhammad|Ibn Jubayr's journey]] on the Iraqi pilgrim road from [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] to [[place/kufa|Kufa]] and then to [[place/medina|Medina]], through the desert {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 pp.205–219}.

## The route
From Baghdad to al-Hilla by way of al-Qantara, al-Qurash and Zarairan, then on to Kufa. From Kufa the pilgrims travelled through al-Aqaba, Waqisa, al-Gharra', al-Shuqra, Zubala, al-Haytham, Birkat al-Marjum, al-Bayda, Zurud, Sutayra, al-Naqranat and al-Hajir, and then to Medina, where they stayed five days {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 pp.205–219}.
