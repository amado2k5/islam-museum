## Overview
The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] set out from [[place/medina|Medina]] with two hundred riders toward the encampments of Banu Sulaym at [[place/al-kudr|al-Kudr]]. He found that Banu Sulaym had fled and left behind five hundred camels. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.234}

He stayed in their dwelling-places for three days, then returned to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.234}
