## Overview
A stage of [[person/ibn-jubayr-muhammad|Ibn Jubayr's]] journey to the pilgrimage. He travelled upstream on the Nile from [[place/cairo|Cairo]] to [[place/qus|Qus]], passing Jabal al-Muqla, Manfalut, Asyut, Akhmim and Qina {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.

## Stops
- Jabal al-Muqla: Ibn Jubayr mentions it halfway between Egypt and Qus.
- Manfalut: a place near the western bank, with markets. Its wheat is brought to Egypt because it is good.
- Asyut: a city on the western bank, about three miles from the Nile, with date-palm gardens around it.
- Akhmim: a city on the eastern bank, with two mosques and remains of Coptic buildings.
- Qina: a city of Upper Egypt, on the eastern side of the Nile.

Between Akhmim and Qina the book mentions other places, among them Tayja'a, Minshat al-Sudan, al-Bulayna, Tishna, Tandara and Qift. Their modern locations are not known here {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.

## Arrival
He reached Qus on Thursday, the twenty-fourth of Muharram, and lodged at the inn of Ibn al-Ajami at al-Munya, a suburb outside the city {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907}.
