## Summary
The Hijra path: from the Prophet's house at Mecca to the [[place/ghar-thawr|cave of Thawr]], then by the unaccustomed coast road to [[place/quba|Quba]] and Medina, under the hired guide [[person/abdullah-ibn-urayqit|Abdullah ibn Urayqit]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 163-167}.

## The route
The Prophet and [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] left by a wicket in Abu Bakr's house at night and reached the cave of Thawr toward Yemen — opposite the Medina road to which eyes first turn {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 163-164}. After three nights the guide came to them with the two mounts on Monday night, the first of Rabi' al-Awwal 1 AH; he took them deep south, then turned west toward the coast and north near the Red Sea shore, on a road almost no one used {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 166-167}.

Ibn Ishaq relates the spots in their order: lower Mecca, then the coast to below Asfan, then below Amaj, then past Qudayd, then al-Kharrar, then Thaniyyat al-Murra, then Liqf, then Madlajat Liqf, then Madlajat Majah, then Marjih Mahaj, then Marjih Dhu al-Ghadwayn, then Batn Dhi Kashr, then al-Jadajid, then al-Ajrad, then Dhi Salam, then al-'Ababid, then al-Faja, then al-'Arj, then Thaniyyat al-'A'ir right of Rakuba, then Batn Ri'm, then Quba {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 167} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## On the way
[[person/suraqa-ibn-malik|Suraqa ibn Malik]] caught up with them; his mare's forelegs sank, so he asked their protection and turned back, diverting the pursuit {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 168-169}. They passed the tent of [[person/umm-mabad|Umm Ma'bad]], and the Prophet milked her barren ewe {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 169}. [[person/abu-burayda-al-aslami|Abu Burayda]] met them and became Muslim with seventy of his people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 170}. [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|Al-Zubayr]] met them in a caravan of traders returning from Syria and clothed them in white garments {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 170}.
