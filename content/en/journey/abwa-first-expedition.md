## Overview
In Safar of the 2nd year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] set out with seventy men of the emigrants to intercept a Quraysh caravan. He reached [[place/waddan|Waddan]] and met no opposition. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

## The route
He marched from [[place/medina|Medina]] to Waddan. Waddan lies twenty-nine miles from Rabigh on the Medina side. He was away for fifteen nights. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

## On the expedition
He appointed [[person/sad-ibn-ubada|Sa'd ibn Ubada]] over Medina, and concluded a treaty with Banu Damra. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

The same event is described as [[event/expedition-of-al-abwa-waddan|the expedition of al-Abwa']].
