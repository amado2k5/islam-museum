## Overview
A sea stage of [[person/ibn-battuta-muhammad|Ibn Battuta]]'s journey. He stayed in [[place/aden|Aden]], became acquainted with its judges and scholars, then sailed from it for four days until he reached [[place/zaila|Zeila]]. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.159}

## Zeila
He describes it as a city of the Barbara from Sudan, who follow the Shafi'i school. {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.159}
