## Overview
In Shawwal of the 1st year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] sent [[person/ubayda-ibn-al-harith|Ubayda ibn al-Harith]] with sixty riders from the emigrants. He met Abu Sufyan with two hundred men in the valley of [[place/rabigh|Rabigh]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.

The two sides shot arrows at each other, and no battle took place {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.
