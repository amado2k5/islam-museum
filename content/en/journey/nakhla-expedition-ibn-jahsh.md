## Overview
The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] sent [[person/abdullah-ibn-jahsh|Abdullah ibn Jahsh]] in Rajab of the 2nd year AH with twelve men of the emigrants {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.200}.

## The letter
He wrote a letter for him and ordered him not to look into it until he had travelled for two days. When he looked, it said that he should continue until he reached [[place/nakhla-al-yamaniya|Nakhla]], between Mecca and al-Ta'if, to watch for a Quraysh caravan. They were riding one camel, which two of them took turns riding {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.200}.

The text does not give the distance from Medina to Nakhla here.
