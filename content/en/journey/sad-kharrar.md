## Overview
The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] sent Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in Dhu al-Qa'da of the 1st year AH with twenty riders, to intercept a Quraysh caravan. He instructed him not to go beyond [[place/al-kharrar|al-Kharrar]], which is near al-Juhfa {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.

They travelled on foot by night and hid by day, and found that the caravan had passed the day before {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.

The same event is described as [[event/expedition-of-al-kharrar|the expedition of al-Kharrar]].
