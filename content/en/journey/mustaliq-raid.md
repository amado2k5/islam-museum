## Overview
The route of the Banu Mustaliq raid in Sha'ban of the 6th year AH: the Prophet set out from Medina to the water of [[place/qudayd|al-Muraysi' in the area of Qudayd]], then returned to Medina after he gave the order to depart {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.325-331}.

## The route
The Prophet set out two nights into Sha'ban, and appointed [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] over Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.325-326}. Al-Rahiq does not describe the stations between Medina and al-Muraysi', so none are listed here.

He reached al-Muraysi', a water belonging to Banu Mustaliq in the area of Qudayd, toward the coast, and the raid took place there {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.326}. After the affair was over, the Prophet remained at al-Muraysi'. A quarrel broke out there over water between a hired man of Umar and another man from Juhayna. [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abdullah ibn Ubayy]] spoke words that angered people, and then the Prophet gave the order to depart at an hour when he did not usually set out {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.327-330}.

The company travelled through the day and the night until he stopped with the people for a rest, and then they entered Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.330-331}. Al-Rahiq says the army halted at some stations on the road without naming them, so no place is given here for them.

## On the road
On the road, [[person/aisha|Aisha]] fell behind the company while looking for a necklace she had lost. She then returned with Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal, after the army had halted at midday. The people of the slander spread the story when they reached Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.331-332}. During the raid the Prophet married [[person/juwayriya-bint-al-harith|Juwayriya bint al-Harith]] from among the captives, and the Muslims freed a hundred households of her people {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.326}.
