## Overview
In Rabi' al-Akhir of the 3rd year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] led a patrol of three hundred fighters to [[place/buhran|Buhran]], a mine in the Fur' area of the Hijaz {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.245}.

He stayed there through the month of Rabi' al-Akhir and then Jumada al-Ula, then returned to [[place/medina|Medina]] without meeting battle {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.245}.
