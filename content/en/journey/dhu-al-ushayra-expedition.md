## Overview
In Jumada al-Ula of the 2nd year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] set out with one hundred and fifty of the emigrants, or it is said two hundred, to intercept the Quraysh caravan returning from Syria. He reached Dhu al-Ushayra and found that the caravan had passed it by several days {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.199-200}.

## The route
They were on thirty camels, which they rode in turns. Dhu al-Ushayra lies in the area of Yanbu' {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.199-200}.

## Outcome
In this expedition he concluded a treaty of non-aggression with Banu Mudlij and Damra. This caravan is the one that became the reason for the Battle of Badr the Great {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.199-200}.

The same event is described as [[event/expedition-of-dhu-al-ushayra|the expedition of Dhu al-Ushayra]].
