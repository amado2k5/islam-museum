## Overview
In Rajab of the fifth year of the mission, the first group of the Prophet's companions set out for [[place/abyssinia|Abyssinia]]: twelve men and four women, led by [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]], with him [[person/ruqayya-bint-muhammad|Ruqayya, the Prophet's daughter]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.92-93}.

## The route
They set out by night, secretly, so that Quraysh would not notice them. They made for the sea and the port of Shu'ayba. Providence arranged for them two merchant ships, which sailed them to Abyssinia. When Quraysh noticed, they went out in pursuit, but when they reached the shore they found that the emigrants had already set off, safe {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.92-93} {cite:rahma-lil-alamin}. The Muslims stayed in Abyssinia in the best of neighbourhood {cite:zad-al-maad}.

## After arrival
A false report reached the people of Mecca that Quraysh had become Muslim, so some of the emigrants returned to Mecca in Shawwal of the same year. When they were an hour of daylight away from it, they learned the truth, and those who had returned went back to Abyssinia. None of the rest entered Mecca except in hiding or under the protection of a man of Quraysh {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.94} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

Quraysh then sent [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-abi-rabia|Abdullah ibn Abi Rabi'a]] with gifts, first to the patriarchs of the [[person/negus-of-aksum|Negus]] and then to him, asking that the emigrants be returned. The Negus returned the gifts and kept the Muslims with him. [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]] was their spokesman {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.94-96} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.

## In Abla al-Kahlawi's programme 'In the Love of al-Mustafa'
[[person/abla-al-kahlawi|Abla al-Kahlawi]] calls the migration to Abyssinia 'the first hijra' in her programme [[work/fi-hub-al-mustapha|'In the Love of al-Mustafa']], and says the Prophet ordered his Companions to make it because he could not bear for blood to be shed between brothers, despite what Quraysh had done, and wanted it to heal the rift and spare lives. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:04:00} He chose Abyssinia, she says, because it had a noble king of the religion of Christ under whom no right was lost, who protected the wronged and kept his promises; in her view the follower of any religion who holds to its principles will meet others on common ground, and the Negus protected them and received them well. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:04:30} She describes the hardship of the journey, saying they took a boat from Jidda to an African coast, and that among them were the Prophet's daughter and her husband Uthman ibn Affan. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep14 00:05:00} According to The Sealed Nectar they sailed from the port of Shu'ayba. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.92-93}
