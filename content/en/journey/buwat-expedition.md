## Overview
In Rabi' al-Awwal of the 2nd year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] set out with two hundred of his companions to intercept a Quraysh caravan, in which were Umayya ibn Khalaf and a hundred men of Quraysh. He reached [[place/buwat|Buwat]] and met no opposition {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.

## The route
Buwat is a mountain of the Juhayna range on the Syria road, about four barids (a unit of distance) from [[place/medina|Medina]]. He appointed [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]] over Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.

The same event is described as [[event/expedition-of-buwat|the expedition of Buwat]].
