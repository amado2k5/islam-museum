## Overview
Ibn Battuta's route from [[place/medina|Medina]] to [[place/mecca|Mecca]] on his first pilgrimage, with the Syrian caravan, as he recounts it in his journey {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.78–79}.

## The route
He left Medina for Mecca, and near Dhu al-Hulayfa he entered ihram for the Hajj alone, then alighted at al-Rawha {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.78}.

From al-Rawha to [[place/badr|Badr]], passing al-Safra, a valley with water, palm trees and a fortress {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.78}.

From Badr to al-Buzwa, then the valley of Rabigh. From there it was three stages to Khulays, then the ascent of al-Suwayq, 'Asfan and Batn Marr, and he reached Mecca at dawn {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.78–79}.
