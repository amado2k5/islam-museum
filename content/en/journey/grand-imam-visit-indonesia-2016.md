## Summary
Grand Imam Prof. Dr. Ahmad al-Tayyib, Grand Shaykh of al-Azhar and Chairman of the Muslim Council of Elders, visited the Republic of Indonesia from 20 to 27 February 2016, his first visit there since assuming the leadership of al-Azhar {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 34}. The book Rihlat al-Imam al-Akbar ila Indunisiya records the visit day by day, alongside a country profile and an overview of Indonesia's relations with Egypt and al-Azhar {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 170}.

## Departure and Arrival
The trip began on Saturday morning, 20 February 2016, from Cairo to Abu Dhabi, where the delegation spent the night, and the private plane took off on Sunday morning for Jakarta {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 51}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 53}. It landed at Halim Perdanakusuma Airport at 10:20 p.m. Jakarta time, where he was received by the Indonesian Minister of Religious Affairs and a number of leaders and ambassadors {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 54}.

## The Four Days
- **Monday, 22 February:** He met President Joko Widodo at the Presidential Palace; the president said al-Azhar is a fortress for the community against extremism, and the Grand Imam announced an increase in scholarships for Indonesian students {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 58}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 62}. He then visited the Indonesian Council of Ulama, chaired the seventh session of the Muslim Council of Elders, and met the ambassadors of Arab and Islamic countries {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 63}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 69}.
- **Tuesday, 23 February:** At Syarif Hidayatullah State Islamic University in Jakarta, he delivered a global address to the Muslim community, addressing the unfair linking of Islam with terrorism, attacks on Muslims in Myanmar, and the situation of al-Aqsa Mosque, and calling for holding fast to unity and rejecting discord {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 79}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 89}. Afterward, he visited the Center for Qur'anic Studies—named the "al-Azhar al-Sharif Center for Qur'anic Studies"—and al-Azhar Mosque in Jakarta, and was then received by the Vice President {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 104}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 108}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 111}.
- **Wednesday, 24 February:** He traveled to Malang in East Java, where Maulana Malik Ibrahim State Islamic University awarded him an honorary doctorate, and he delivered an address on knowledge and on al-Azhar's methodology in creed and jurisprudence {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 112}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 117}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 126}.
- **Thursday, 25 February:** He visited the Darussalam Gontor Institute and University via the city of Madiun, announced fifty additional scholarships for male and female students to study at al-Azhar, and inaugurated the university's Graduate Studies building {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 132}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 140}.

## Return
The delegation returned on Friday, 26 February, to Abu Dhabi, and arrived in Cairo on Saturday, 27 February 2016 {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 144}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 145}.

## What the Book Notes on the Outcomes of the Visit
The book lists twenty-one outcomes of the visit, including deepening cooperation in education and religious outreach, increasing scholarships, agreeing to support the Center for Qur'anic Studies, and establishing a council of scholars in Indonesia as a model it hopes to see replicated, though all of these are the assessments of the book's authors rather than facts verified by another source {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 146}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 149}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 150}.

## Controversial Positions
The book states that the Grand Imam said Shi'is are Muslims and may not be declared outside the fold of Islam, while warning at the same time against spreading Shi'ism among Sunnis in Indonesia, and attributes to him a statement about A'isha, Mother of the Believers, that touches on points of dispute between Sunnis and Shi'is {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 65}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 104}{cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 149}. These are the Grand Imam's statements as transmitted by the book; they must be presented alongside the diverse Sunni, academic, and opposing viewpoints before any judgment is made, and they have not yet been checked against a second source.

## Caution
The entire visit is drawn from a single book published by the Islamic Research Academy, and many of its figures (the number of scholarships and envoys, trade exchange, and population) require a second source; some figures conflict within the book itself, such as the number of dispatched teachers (26 on p. 36, and 31 on p. 43).
