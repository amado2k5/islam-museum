## Overview
A passage from [[person/ibn-jubayr-muhammad|Ibn Jubayr's journey]] from [[place/badr|Badr]] to [[place/medina|Medina]], passing al-Safra and [[place/al-rawha|al-Rawha]]. The text says they reached Medina on Monday, the third of Muharram of the year 580 {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 pp.233–234}.

## The route
From Badr to al-Safra, from there to Lidda, then to the well of Dhat al-Ilm, also known as al-Rawha. Then they took the Shi'b Ali road, then Thurban and al-Bayda, then the valley of al-Aqiq, where the minaret of Quba came into view. They entered Medina after noon {cite:rihla-ibn-jubayr-1907 pp.233–234}.

## Notes on pagination
The page numbers follow the scanned file of the 1907 edition. The text does not describe the road from Mecca to Badr in this passage, and does not clearly order the stations between al-Rawha and Medina, so no continuous sequence from Mecca is listed. No Gregorian date has been calculated.
