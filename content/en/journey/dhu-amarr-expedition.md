## Overview
In Muharram of the 3rd year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] set out with about four hundred and fifty fighters. He reached the water called [[place/dhu-amarr|Dhu Amarr]], where Banu Tha'laba and Muharib had gathered and then dispersed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.241}.

He stayed there for about the month of Safar, then returned to [[place/medina|Medina]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.241}.
