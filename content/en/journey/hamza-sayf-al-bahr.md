## Overview
The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] sent Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib in Ramadan of the 1st year AH, with thirty men of the emigrants, to intercept a Quraysh caravan that had come from Syria. They reached the seashore at Sayf, on the side of [[place/al-is|al-Is]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197}.

## The encounter
Hamza met the caravan, which had Abu Jahl with it, and three hundred men. Majdi ibn Amr came between the two groups, and so they did not fight {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197}.
