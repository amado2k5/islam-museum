## Overview
A trading journey with the wealth of [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]] to Syria, on which the [[person/muhammad|Prophet Muhammad]] went out, with her servant [[person/maysara|Maysara]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60}.

## The route
- **From Mecca to Syria:** Khadija offered that he should go out with her wealth as a merchant, and she would give him the best of what she gave other merchants. He accepted and went out, with Maysara, until they reached Syria {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60}.
- **From Syria to Mecca:** When he returned to Mecca, Khadija saw in her wealth a trustworthiness and blessing that she had not seen before. Maysara told her what he had seen of his fine qualities and noble character {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.60-61}.

## On the road
The text does not mention stations between Mecca and Syria, and does not identify a particular city in Syria, so no stations are listed.

## Notes
The text gives no date for this journey other than the Prophet's age at the time (twenty-five years), so no date has been set for it. The journey was the beginning of the story of his marriage to Khadija {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.60}.
