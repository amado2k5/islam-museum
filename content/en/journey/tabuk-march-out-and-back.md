## Summary
Ibn Ishaq sketches [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]'s march to [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]] through scattered spots, not ordering them all in an explicit sequence. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162-172}

## The stations mentioned
- [[place/thaniyyat-al-wada|Thaniyyat al-Wada']]: where the Prophet pitched his camp on setting out. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}
- [[place/al-jurf|Al-Jurf]]: where the Prophet camped and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] caught up with him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/163}
- [[place/al-hijr|Al-Hijr]]: where the Muslims camped and drew from its well, then were forbidden to drink from it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/164-165}
- [[place/al-akhdar|Al-Akhdar]]: where Abu Ruhm travelled by night near the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/172}
- [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]]: where the Prophet stayed some thirteen to nineteen nights. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}
- [[place/wadi-al-mushaqqaq|Wadi al-Mushaqqaq]]: which the Prophet passed on his return. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/171}

## Notes
The order of stations between al-Jurf, al-Hijr and Tabuk is taken from the narrative order; Ibn Ishaq gives neither explicit distances nor precise dates. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162-172}

The narrative then continues to the halt at Dhu Awan and Masjid al-Dirar — the subject of the next batch.
