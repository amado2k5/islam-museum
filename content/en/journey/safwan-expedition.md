## Overview
In Rabi' al-Awwal of the 2nd year AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] set out with seventy men to pursue Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri, who had raided the pastures of Medina. He reached the valley of [[place/safwan|Safwan]] on the Badr side, but did not overtake him, and returned without fighting {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.

He appointed [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] as his deputy {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.

The same event is described as [[event/expedition-of-safwan|the expedition of Safwan]].
