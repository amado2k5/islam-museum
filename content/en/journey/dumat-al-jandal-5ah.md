## Overview
The Prophet's march to [[place/dumat-al-jandal|Dumat al-Jandal]] in Rabi' al-Awwal of the 5th year AH: he set out from [[place/medina|Medina]] with a thousand Muslims, guided by a man of Banu Udhra called Madhkur {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp.299-300}.

## The route
The Prophet set out five nights before the end of Rabi' al-Awwal of the 5th year AH, and appointed [[person/siba-ibn-urfuta|Siba' ibn Urfuta]] over Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.300}. He travelled by night and hid by day, until he drew near Dumat and took some of their livestock and herders {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.300}. The text says Dumat is fifteen nights from Medina, a distance that does not fix the day of arrival {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.300}.

## In Dumat
The people of Dumat fled in every direction, so when the Muslims reached their open ground they found no one. The Prophet stayed for some days and sent out raiding parties and divided the army into detachments, but they found no one {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.300}.

## The return
The Prophet then returned to Medina, and in that expedition made a truce with [[person/uyayna-ibn-hisn|Uyayna ibn Hisn]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.300}. The expedition is placed in the context after the Minor Badr: the Prophet remained in Medina for six months, and then news reached him of the tribes around Dumat {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.300}.
