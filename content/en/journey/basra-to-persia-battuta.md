## Overview
Ibn Battuta's route from [[place/basra|Basra]] to Fars and Iraq al-Ajam, and then his return to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]], as he recounts it in his journey {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.117–147}. The road passes through al-Ubulla and Abadan in the Gulf, then by land through Tustar and Ayadhaj to [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]] and [[place/shiraz|Shiraz]], and then returns to Iraq by way of [[place/kufa|Kufa]].

## The route
From Basra he boarded a sunbuq to [[place/ubulla|al-Ubulla]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.117}, then a small boat to [[place/abbadan|Abadan]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.117–118}.

From Abadan he sailed for four days to the town of Majul, then travelled by land through Ramiz as far as [[place/tustar|Tustar]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.118–120}. From Tustar, three days through lofty mountains brought him to [[place/ayadhaj|Ayadhaj]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.121–122}. He then went on to [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]], passing the towns of Ishtakhran and Firuzan {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.124}.

From Isfahan he headed for Shiraz, passing Kulayl, Isawma', Dasht al-Rum and Mayin {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.126–127}, and he stayed there a long time {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.127–136}. He then went out to [[place/kazarun|Kazarun]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.136}, then to al-Zaydin and al-Huwayza, and from there he set out for Kufa across the desert {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.137}.

From Kufa he passed the well of Mulaha, then al-Hilla and Karbala, and from there went on to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.138–139}. From Baghdad he travelled for ten days with the sultan Abu Sa'id's camp as far as [[place/tabriz|Tabriz]] {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 pp.146–147}, and then returned to Baghdad with a letter to its governor {cite:rihla-ibn-battuta-1958 p.147}.

## Notes on pagination
The page numbers are the printed numbers of the 1958 edition. The scanned copy used in preparing this page adds one to the printed page number in the file.

The book does not give daily dates for each halt in this passage. The periods mentioned (days and stages) are given as they appear.
