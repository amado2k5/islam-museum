## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that when [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] had finished with Damascus, he passed by Ba'labakk and made peace with its people, and then came to [[place/homs|Homs]], whose people made peace with him for a hundred and seventy thousand dinars. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Ba'labakk
Abu Ubayda sent ahead [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] and Malhan ibn Ziyar al-Ta'i. They made peace with the people of Ba'labakk for safety for their lives, their property, their churches, their houses and their mills, and the [[concept/jizya|jizya]] and the kharaj for those who remained. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Homs
al-Baladhuri, quoting al-Waqidi, relates that a host of the enemy fled toward Homs, and its people surrendered and asked for safe-conduct. Abu Ubayda halted at the Gate of al-Rastan, and its people made peace with him for safety for their lives, their property and their churches, with an exception of a quarter of the church of John for the mosque. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abu Ubayda appointed [[person/ubada-ibn-al-samit|Ubada ibn al-Samit]] over Homs. After that Homs and Qinnasrin became one thing. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
