## Overview
In 624 AH the sultan Jalal al-Din, who was at Tabriz, learned that the Tatars had marched on Isfahan, where his family was. He set out for it and prepared to meet them. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 113}

## The battle
Ibn al-Imad relates that the brother of the sultan Ghiyath al-Din abandoned him and turned away, and Jahan Pahlawan followed him. Then the right wing of Jalal al-Din broke the left wing of the Tatars, and his left wing charged their right. People rejoiced at the victory. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 113}

Then the Tatars charged once more with their ambush. Night had come, the emirs were killed and the fighting grew fierce. Jalal al-Din held firm with a small group, then was routed, was wounded by a spear, and his army was scattered. The source describes it as an epic battle unheard of in the rout of both sides, and it took place in Ramadan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 113}
