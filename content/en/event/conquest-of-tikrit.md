## Overview
al-Tabari reports, in the account of Sayf, that the conquest of Tikrit was in Jumada of 16 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.35}

The data for this page rests on a single source and a single report, and the details of the conquest need an additional source.
