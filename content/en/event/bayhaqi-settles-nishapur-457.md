## Summary
Toward the end of his life al-Bayhaqi moved to Nishapur and settled there as a traditionist and compiler; he was a voluminous writer whose works were said to reach a thousand fascicules — most famous the Sunan al-Kubra, of which al-Subki declared nothing comparable in accuracy, arrangement and quality, while Imam al-Haramayn al-Juwayni praised his writings in support of the Shafi'i school {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1190}.
