## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that after Yarmuk [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] marched to Homs and then to [[place/qinnasrin|Qinnasrin]], with [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] at the head of his vanguard. Its people fought him, then took refuge in their fort and asked for peace, and he made peace with them on terms like those of Homs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.197}

## The breach of the treaty
The chief of Qinnasrin was of the Tanukh. Abu Ubayda invited them to Islam, and some of them embraced it, while the Banu Salih ibn Hulwan of Qudaa remained Christian. Then news reached him that the people of Qinnasrin had broken the treaty, and he sent against them [[person/al-simt-ibn-al-aswad|al-Simt ibn al-Aswad al-Kindi]], who besieged them and took it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.197}

Abu Ubayda made peace with the people of Hadir of Aleppo for the [[concept/jizya|jizya]], and they later became Muslims. The conquest is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. The date is approximate and derived.
