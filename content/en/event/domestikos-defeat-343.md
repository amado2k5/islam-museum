## Overview
News came in 343 AH of a great battle between the Domestikos, the Byzantine commander, and [[person/sayf-al-dawla|Sayf al-Dawla]]. A great number of the Domestikos's men and the chiefs of his patricians were killed in it. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.668}
