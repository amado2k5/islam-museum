## Overview
Hasan ibn Ahmad and Abdallah ibn Munir, cut off from their companions, turned on Banu al-Harith and drove them back. At the Yemeni gate of the village, Hasan broke the bolted gate with his sword, and they rode out to Ali ibn Muhammad at al-Buqayra and told him that his father had fallen as a martyr, in the book's word.

## Detail
Hasan ibn Ahmad al-Bada'ani and Abdallah ibn Munir al-Mazwi were separated from their companions when Banu al-Harith came between them. The pursuers followed them, and they turned on them and drove them off, though there was no room for their horses. They left by the Yemeni gate of the village, and three horsemen of their companions caught up with them. They found the gate of the Darb bolted, and Hasan struck it with his sword and broke it. All of them rode out toward the fort, where Ali ibn Muhammad was, and met him at a place called al-Buqayra. They told him that his father had fallen as a martyr and those with him.
