## Summary

Talha ibn al-Mutamid (al-Muwaffaq, as the source names him) returned from the Zanj war in the year 259 AH, worn down by illness. Al-Muʿtamid ʿala Allah ordered Musa ibn Bugha to set out from Samarra for the Zanj war in Dhu al-Qaʿda. The caliph saw him off and bestowed robes on him on the road. There were wars between him and the Zanj over about a dozen months, the account of which is long, and then he was relieved of the war. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 105}.

The Zanj entered Ahwaz in this year and killed about fifty thousand people. It is among the fiercest engagements Ibn al-Jawzi records in the Zanj advance outside their stronghold in Basra. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 105}.
