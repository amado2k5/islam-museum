## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the Muslims gathered at the monastery of Hind in 14 AH, and the Persians sent against them Mihran ibn Mihrbandadh al-Hamadhani with twelve thousand men. The Muslims gave him time until he had crossed the bridge, and then the two hosts met at al-Buwayb. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-356} The Persian commander was [[person/mihran-ibn-mihrbandadh|Mihran]]. The report says that Queen Buran, daughter of Khosrow, was the one who sent him.

## Command and fighting
The accounts differ. According to the Bajila, the commander was [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah]]; according to Rabi'a, it was [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]]; and it is also said that each group had its own leader. [[person/shurahbil-ibn-al-simt|Shurahbil ibn al-Simt]] distinguished himself, and [[person/masud-ibn-haritha|Mas'ud ibn Haritha]], brother of al-Muthanna, was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-356}

al-Baladhuri relates that al-Muthanna told the Muslims not to be frightened by the fall of his brother. They charged as one man until Mihran was killed and the Persians were routed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-356}

## Who killed Mihran
Jarir and [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-hassan-al-dabbi|al-Mundhir ibn Hassan al-Dabbi]] disputed who had killed him. al-Mundhir took his belt, and Jarir took the rest of his spoils. It is also reported that others were among his killers. [[person/qurt-ibn-jammakh-al-abdi|Qurt ibn Jammakh al-Abdi]] is named among those who fought until his sword was bent. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.354-356} al-Buwayb lies in [[place/al-buwayb|al-Buwayb]].

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] calls this battle “the Day of Mihran” and reports that Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali led the reinforcements, that Buran ordered twelve thousand picked cavalry under Mihran ibn Mihruya al-Hamadhani, and that al-Muthanna cried “I am al-Muthanna” as his brother Mas'ud ibn Haritha was killed. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 114} “They mention” that al-Muthanna killed Mihran. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 115}
