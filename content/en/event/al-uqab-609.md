## Overview
The battle of al-Uqab was fought in al-Andalus in 609 AH. [[person/muhammad-al-nasir-almohad|Muhammad al-Nasir al-Muwahhidi]] was the commander of the Muslims. He left Marrakesh for Seville and prepared for a campaign, but he was met by the people of the 'Udwa from among the Jalaliqa, and the Muslims were routed at the end of Safar of that year. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1682}

## The account in the sources
Ibn Khaldun reports that al-Nasir returned to Marrakesh after the battle and died in Sha'ban 610 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1682}

"Shadharat al-Dhahab" describes it as the great battle in al-Andalus between al-Nasir and the Franks, in which a great number were martyred. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.36-37}

al-Maqqari in "Nafh al-Tib", quoting the author of "al-Dhakhira al-Sunniyya", says that al-Nasir gathered six hundred thousand fighters. al-Maqqari holds that the cause of the disaster was poor management, because al-Nasir and his vizier made light of the men of al-Andalus who knew how to fight the Franks. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.383}

## Note on the date
