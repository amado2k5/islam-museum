## Overview
In 169 AH, eight nights before the end of Muharram, [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] died. He rode in pursuit of game, the game ran into a ruin and he followed it, and his back struck the doorway, and he died instantly. It is also said that he died poisoned. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 443}

## Note
The second account, the poison account, is related by al-Suyuti with the phrase "it is said". The report comes from a single source and needs an additional source.

## In al-Ya'qubi
[[person/al-yaqubi|Al-Ya'qubi]] relates that al-Mahdi left Baghdad in Muharram for the Jibal and camped at a village called al-Radhdh in the district of Masabadhan. Out hunting, he chased a gazelle that dashed through the doorway of a ruin; his horse charged in after it and the doorway struck him. He was carried to his tents and died with eight nights of Muharram 169 AH remaining, aged forty-eight. Al-Ya'qubi also tells of a dream ten days earlier, in which an old man recited verses on the passing of the palace and its people. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 486} {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 487}
He gives the caliphate as ten years, one month and twenty-two days; al-Mahdi's son Ali, son of Rayta, led the funeral prayer; he was buried at al-Radhdh and left eight sons: Musa, Harun, Ali, Ubayd Allah, Ishaq, Ya'qub, Ibrahim and Mansur. His day of death (eight nights before the end of Muharram) differs from the report of 8 Muharram. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 487}
