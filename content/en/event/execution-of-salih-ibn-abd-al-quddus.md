## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that Salih ibn Abd al-Quddus of Basra was brought before the caliph [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] on a charge of heresy, and then al-Mahdi ordered him killed. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 444}

## The account
Al-Mahdi wanted to kill him, and Salih said he repented, and recited verses about the old man who does not abandon his character. Al-Mahdi dismissed him, then brought him back as he was about to leave, and reminded him of his words. He told him that he would not give up his character until he died, and then ordered him killed. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 444}

The account is linked with al-Mahdi's campaign against the heretics, and that link needs an additional source.
