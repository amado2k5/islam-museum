## Overview
The news came in Rabi al-Akhir 311 AH that [[person/abu-tahir-al-qarmati|Abu Tahir al-Janabi al-Qarmati]] had entered [[place/basra|Basra]] before dawn with one thousand seven hundred men. He remained there for seventeen days, loading onto his camels as much as he could. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 466}

## The entry
Ibn al-Jawzi says that the entry was before dawn on Monday, five days before the end of Rabi al-Akhir. Abu Tahir set ladders against the wall at night and climbed, then went down into the town, killed the gatekeepers and opened the gates. He placed gravel and sand between the leaves of each gate so that they could not be closed. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 466}

## What happened afterwards
He put the people of Basra to the sword, burned the Mirbad, and demolished the mosque and the mosque of the tomb of Talha. People fled and threw themselves into the water, and most of them drowned. Abu Tahir loaded the goods, the women and the children on his camels. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 466}
