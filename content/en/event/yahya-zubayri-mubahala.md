## Overview
In 175 AH al-Suyuti relates that Abd Allah ibn Mus'ab al-Zubayri claimed against [[person/yahya-ibn-abdallah-al-alawi|Yahya ibn Abd Allah al-Alawi]] that he had invited him to rise against [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]]. Yahya made a mubahala with him in the presence of al-Rashid. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 460-461}

## The mubahala
Yahya interlaced his hand with al-Zubayri's, and asked him to say a prayer asking God to leave him to his own power and strength and to punish him if Yahya had invited him to dissent against the commander of the faithful. Al-Zubayri faltered and said it, then Yahya said the same. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 460-461}

## The last report
Al-Suyuti says that al-Zubayri died on the same day. This is al-Suyuti's report, and needs an additional source to verify it.



## The exchange — what each side said
- **Al-Zubayri (the accusation)**: Abdallah ibn Mus'ab al-Zubayri claimed before al-Rashid that Yahya ibn Abdallah al-Alawi had called on him to rise against the Commander of the Faithful — an accusation whose price was blood.
- **Yahya (the mubahala challenge)**: linked his hand into al-Zubayri's hand in al-Rashid's presence, and asked him to invoke a curse upon himself: to ask God to leave him to his own power and strength and punish him if Yahya had truly called him to dissent — putting the claim itself on trial: let him belie me with the curse if he can.
- **Al-Zubayri (the invocation)**: faltered, then said the prayer as asked — his hesitation in the wording is recorded in the report.
- **Yahya (the counter-invocation)**: said the same prayer upon himself — the curse falling equally on accuser and accused.
- **The outcome (al-Suyuti's account)**: al-Zubayri died that same day — the report reads the death as the mubahala's verdict; al-Suyuti's report alone wants another witness. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.460-461}
