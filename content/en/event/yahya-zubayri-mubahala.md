## Overview
In 175 AH al-Suyuti relates that Abd Allah ibn Mus'ab al-Zubayri claimed against [[person/yahya-ibn-abdallah-al-alawi|Yahya ibn Abd Allah al-Alawi]] that he had invited him to rise against [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]]. Yahya made a mubahala with him in the presence of al-Rashid. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 460-461}

## The mubahala
Yahya interlaced his hand with al-Zubayri's, and asked him to say a prayer asking God to leave him to his own power and strength and to punish him if Yahya had invited him to dissent against the commander of the faithful. Al-Zubayri faltered and said it, then Yahya said the same. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 460-461}

## The last report
Al-Suyuti says that al-Zubayri died on the same day. This is al-Suyuti's report, and needs an additional source to verify it.
