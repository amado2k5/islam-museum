## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/yuhanna-ibn-rubah|Yuhanna ibn Ruba]], lord of [[place/ayla|Ayla]], came to the Prophet while he was at Tabuk, and made peace with him for a poll tax. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
He imposed one dinar a year on each free adult of his land, which came to three hundred dinars. He stipulated that they provide hospitality for any Muslims who passed by them. The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] wrote them a letter that they should be protected and defended. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
It is related from Talha al-Aili that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz did not add anything to the people of Ayla above the three hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The peace is part of [[event/expedition-to-tabuk|the expedition of Tabuk]], and it connects with the concept of [[concept/jizya|the poll tax]]. The date is derived from that of the expedition.
