## The story
Malik relates from Zayd ibn Aslam that Ata ibn Yasar told him the Messenger of God was in the mosque, and a man with disheveled hair and beard entered; the Messenger gestured him out, meaning to fix his hair and beard, and when the man returned he said: is this not better than one of you coming with disheveled hair and beard like a shaytan {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2732|Muwatta 2732]].

## The meaning
In this report the disheveled appearance is likened to the shaytan in refinement and dignity, and the Prophet s guidance to fix one s appearance before entering the mosque {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
