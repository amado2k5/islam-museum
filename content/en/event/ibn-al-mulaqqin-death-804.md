## Summary
Ibn al-Mulaqqin's state changed at the end of his life; his son Nur al-Din 'Ali confined him until he died on 6 Rabi' I 804 at Cairo, aged eighty-one, and was buried beside his father in the hawsh of the Sufis outside Bab al-Nasr {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/45} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 dhayl p. 123}.
