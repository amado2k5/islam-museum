## Summary
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Habib al-Mawardi al-Basri al-Shafi'i was born at [[place/basra|Basra]] in 364/974; his father was a maker and seller of rose-water, from which his nisba derives {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 896}.

## His upbringing
He completed his studies at Basra and [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] and became a teacher; he died at Baghdad in Rabi' I 450, aged eighty-six {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 896} {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.199-200}.
