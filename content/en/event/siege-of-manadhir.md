## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] marched to Manadhir and besieged its people, then [[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi ibn Ziyad]] took it by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege
The fighting grew severe. In the army was [[person/al-muhajir-ibn-ziyad|al-Muhajir ibn Ziyad]], al-Rabi's brother, who was fasting. He wanted to fight, and Abu Musa ordered every fasting man either to break his fast or not go out to fight. He drank a draught of water and said he had not drunk it from thirst, then fought until he was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The people of Manadhir took his head and set it up on their fortress between two battlements. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
Abu Musa left al-Rabi as his deputy over Manadhir and marched to al-Sus. Al-Rabi took Manadhir by force, killed the fighting men and enslaved the families. Greater and lesser Manadhir came into Muslim hands, and Abu Musa appointed Asim ibn Qays al-Sulami over them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Another account in al-Baladhuri says that Umar wrote that Manadhir was like a village of the outskirts of Sawad, and ordered the goods taken from it returned. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The place is [[place/manadhir|Manadhir]]. The date needs an additional source.
