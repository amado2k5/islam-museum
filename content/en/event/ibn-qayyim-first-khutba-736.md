## Overview
On 2 Rajab 736/15 February 1336 Ibn Qayyim delivered his first khutba in the mosque that Nadim al-Din ibn Khalikhan had recently founded in the gardens of the Ghuta, outside Bab Sharqi and Bab Tuma {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 854}.
