## Overview
Al-Subki heard hadith in his youth in Egypt {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 756 p. 264}, and was among those who trained in disputation under Sayf al-Din Isa ibn Dawud al-Baghdadi al-Hanafi, who lived in Egypt until his death in Jumada I 705 {cite:siyar-alam-nubala p. 2986}.
