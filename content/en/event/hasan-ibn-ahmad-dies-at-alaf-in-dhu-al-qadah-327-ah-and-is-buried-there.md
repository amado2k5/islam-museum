## Overview
Al-Hasan ibn Ahmad falls ill at Alaf and dies on Thursday, eleven nights into Dhu al-Qa'da 327 AH. He is buried at Alaf.

## Detail
The book says al-Hasan ibn Ahmad fell ill at Alaf and died on Thursday, eleven nights into Dhu al-Qa'da of the same year, and was buried at Alaf. His brother al-Qasim remained at al-Ghayl near Saada after him. The book does not give the cause of the illness.
