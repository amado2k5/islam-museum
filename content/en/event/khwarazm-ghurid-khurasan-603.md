## Summary
In 603 AH al-Husayn ibn Khurmil, governor of Herat, inclined toward Khwarazmshah against the Ghurids; the Khwarazmian army approached the country and then the arrangement faltered between the two sides. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 312}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Ibn Khurmil had been corresponding with Khwarazmshah about defecting to him and leaving Ghurid allegiance, while deceiving Ghiyath al-Din by keeping the khutba in his name pending the Khwarazmian army's arrival; when the army drew near he refused to pronounce the khutba for Ghiyath al-Din, lodged the troops at the city gate and attended them daily with generous provisions. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 312} Then news came that Khwarazmshah had besieged Balkh without pressing close, so Ibn Khurmil regretted the alliance, told his intimates they had erred in joining a man he saw as incapable, sent the army back and dispatched rich gifts. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 312} Ghiyath al-Din seized Ibn Khurmil's iqta' and sent to Karziyan to take his wealth, sons and animals and arrest his companions; the people of Herat resolved to seize him and write to Ghiyath al-Din, so Ibn Khurmil went to the qadi Sa'id, gathered the notables, softened his tone and displayed obedience to Ghiyath al-Din. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 312}
