## Overview
In 249 AH al-Tabari mentions the killing of Utamish and his secretary, and the disorder of the Turks in Samarra with Wasif, Bugha and the Turkish soldiers in general. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.263}

What is not covered here: the details of the killing and its causes. This needs an additional source.
