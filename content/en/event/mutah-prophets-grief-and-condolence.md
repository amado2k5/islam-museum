## Overview
When Ja'far was struck, the Prophet came to the house of [[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma bint Umays]] and asked for Ja'far's children. When he saw them he sniffed them, and his eyes shed tears. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

He told Asma that Ja'far and his companions had been struck that day. He ordered the people not to neglect feeding the family of Ja'far. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

[[person/aisha|Aisha]] related that grief appeared on the Prophet's face when the news of Ja'far's death came. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
