## Overview
Shams al-Din Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya died at Damascus on 13 Rajab 751, and his funeral was very crowded {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/403}. The Encyclopaedia of Islam gives the variant 23 Rajab 751/26 September 1350 and adds that he was buried beside his mother in the Bab al-Saghir cemetery; his son Jamal al-Din Abd Allah (d. 756) succeeded him in teaching at the Sadriyya {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 855}.
