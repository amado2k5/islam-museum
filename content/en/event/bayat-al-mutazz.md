## Overview
In Muharram 252 AH al-Musta'in was deposed and al-Mu'tazz was given the oath, and the oath was taken for him in Baghdad. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.348}

## What al-Tabari reports
al-Tabari mentions the killing of Shurayh al-Habashi. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.354} He also mentions al-Mu'tazz's letter ordering the names of Bugha and Wasif struck from the registers. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.354}

## What al-Suyuti reports
According to al-Suyuti, al-Mu'tazz was given the oath when al-Musta'in was deposed, when he was nineteen years old, and no one younger than him had held the caliphate before him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.557}
