## Overview
As the Mongols closed on Harran in 667/1269 its people were driven to flee; Abd al-Halim ibn Taymiyya took refuge in Damascus with his four sons — among them Ahmad, aged six. There his father directed the Sukkariyya madrasa, and the boy was educated under Damascene masters including Shams al-Din Abd al-Rahman al-Maqdisi, first Hanbali qadi al-qudat of Syria after Baybars' reform of the judiciary {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 983}.
