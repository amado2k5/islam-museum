## Overview
In the morning the two sons of Ya'fur went out early making for "Zahr" (the unpointed word is read here as printed), the narrator and his companions going with them, with al-Yafi'i left behind in the fort; when they came to a place printed "'Aqb", horse came out of Sana'a, and they thought [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibn Khalaf]] had come out to fight and stood a while, but it was horse asking safe-conduct, and Ibn Khalaf had gone out of Sana'a, fleeing to [[place/tihama|Tihama]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.272}. The two men went on and the party with them until they came to [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]], and they went to the house of Abu Ja'far al-Alawi and lodged there, the people sheltering them; after a while Jaftam came in with a party and some of the sons of Ya'fur met him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.272} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.273}.

## Detail
The narrator and his companions hoped to leave Sana'a and it did not come to pass, so Abu al-Qasim sent to the two sons of Ya'fur to consult them on going home, since he did not feel safe that day; they said: to your family you cannot go today, but we shall send Ahmad ibn Abi al-Khayr with you until you reach Shibam, and you will stay there some days until we join you, then go from there to your family in the best of conditions, God willing; and they spent that night at Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.273}. The narrator describes Sana'a that day as in distress: the price on the day they entered and were shut in was five makkuk to the dinar and kept falling until they left, when it was one makkuk, and people died of emaciation {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.273}; the note adds the reading of manuscript S, "most of them died of emaciation", and the statement of Ghayat al-Amani (1/190) that in this year the drought grew severe in Yemen until people ate one another, many died and a number of villages were ruined, which is the editor's statement, not the book's {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.273}. The word read "Zahr" at the start is left unlinked as it is not clear whether it is the district of that name, and Jaftam is not linked to a person record. "The house of Abu Ja'far al-Alawi" is as printed. The report is from a Zaydi book in the narrator's voice; it follows [[event/abu-al-qasim-is-led-out-of-the-fort-and-his-irons-are-struck-off-by-asad-ibn-abi-yafur-291|his leaving the fort and the striking off of his irons]].
