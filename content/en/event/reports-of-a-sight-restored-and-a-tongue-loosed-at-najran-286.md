## Overview
After the felling of the wrongdoer's palms at [[place/rajla-najran|Rajla]] the narrator says that [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] worked "a great sign at Najran", that it is not much for such a man, and that a believer's prayer is answered, so how much more an imam, one for whom "reason and eyewitness bear witness to purity and standing before God". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130} All of this is told by Zaydi narrators and attributed to them; the museum neither confirms nor denies it. See [[concept/karama|karama]] and [[concept/karamat-al-awliya|the karamat of the friends of God]].

## The boy's sight
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] says that a boy was brought to al-Hadi whose sight had gone with smallpox; he passed his hand over the boy's eyes and prayed for him and the boy saw, and "I saw the boy seeing after we reached Najran". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} The manuscript margin has "the karama of restoring the boy's sight". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131}

## The child's tongue
[[person/musa-ibn-ali-ibn-abd-al-jabbar-al-sarimi|Musa ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Jabbar al-Sarimi]], when told of the cloud at Madhab in [[event/al-hadi-reconciles-khawlan-and-banu-salman-at-madhab-and-the-cloud-286]], said he would add another report: he had a small son who had not spoken, he tried every remedy until he resolved to take him to Mecca, and when a letter from al-Hadi came they took his seal ring, put it in water and gave it to the boy to drink; the boy spoke clearly, and people saw him speaking and some had seen him not speaking. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} The margin has "the karama of loosing the boy's tongue". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} This is al-Sarimi's own account; the book does not say which letter it was or when.

## Hearsay about two detractors
The narrator adds that someone he trusts at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] told him he heard a man disparage al-Hadi and belittle him at root, and that within days an affliction took him, his leg gave way before he died and then he died; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} and that he also heard a woman spoke ill, and rising at dawn the fire took her and she burned. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} The narrator comments that this is not to be denied, because God helps His friends by causes, gives them what they want at a time to establish proof against creation and withholds at other times to try them; the margin has "karama". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} Both are hearsay about unnamed people, and the museum keeps the two reports short and takes no position on them.

## Notes
The book gives no date for the ring report. Al-Sarimi is in the book's index at p. 131 only. The restored sight is the narrator's own eyewitness claim, and the others are reports he received.
