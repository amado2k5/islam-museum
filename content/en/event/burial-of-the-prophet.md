## Overview
After the oath to Abu Bakr, the people turned to preparing the Messenger of God for burial. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 671} Ali ibn Abi Talib, al-Abbas and his two sons al-Fadl and Qutham, Usama ibn Zayd and Shuqran, the Prophet's freedman, took charge of his washing. He was washed in his shirt, and wrapped in three white garments, and was buried from the middle of the night of Wednesday. No one led the people in the prayer over him. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 671}{cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672} {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672}

## What happened
Ibn Ishaq says that the people turned to preparing the Prophet on Tuesday after the oath to Abu Bakr. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 671} The Prophet was not stripped of his clothes for washing; water was poured over the shirt and the body was rubbed from beneath it. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 671}{cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672} Aws ibn Khawli asked Ali to let the Ansar take part in the washing, and he agreed. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 671} He was wrapped in three white garments and a striped Yemeni cloak. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672}

Ibn Ishaq reports that the people disagreed about the place of burial. Abu Bakr said that he had heard the Prophet say: "No prophet is taken up but he is buried where he dies." The bed on which he died was lifted, and the grave was dug beneath it. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672} Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah and Abu Talha were called to dig the grave. Abu Ubayda would dig the trench as it is done for the people of Mecca, and Abu Talha would dig the niche as it is done for the people of Medina. Abu Talha's man came first and dug the niche for the Messenger of God. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672}

The people entered to him in groups to pray for him, and then he was buried from the middle of the night of Wednesday. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672} Aisha said that the people did not know of his burial until they heard the sound of spades in the depths of the night. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672}

Ali, al-Fadl, Qutham, Shuqran and Usama went down into the grave. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 672} It is mentioned that al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba claimed that he was the last of the people to have seen the Prophet at the grave, because he had dropped his ring into the grave on purpose, and Ibn Ishaq includes this in his report. {cite:ibn-hisham-dar-ibn-hazm 673}

## Ibn Ishaq's detailed account
[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas]], [[person/al-fadl-ibn-al-abbas|al-Fadl]], [[person/qutham-ibn-al-abbas|Qutham]], [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama]] and [[person/shuqran-mawla-rasul|Shuqran]], the Prophet's freedman, took charge of his washing. Usama and Shuqran poured the water, and Ali washed him, holding him against his chest, with his shirt on, rubbing the body from behind the shirt. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/312} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/313} [[person/aws-ibn-khawli|Aws ibn Khawli]] came in to Ali asking for his share of the Prophet, and Ali let him in. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/312}

Ibn Ishaq relates from Aisha that the people disagreed: should they strip the Prophet of his clothes as they strip their dead, or wash him in his clothes? God cast sleep upon them until no man among them was without his chin on his chest. Then someone spoke to them from the side of the house, whom they did not know: "Wash the Prophet in his clothes." So they washed him with the shirt on him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/313}

He was wrapped in three garments: two cotton sheets of Sahar, and a striped cloak. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/313} [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] used to dig for the people of Mecca, and [[person/abu-talha-al-ansari|Abu Talha]] used to dig for the people of Medina and cut a niche. al-Abbas called two men and said: "O God, choose for Your Messenger"; Abu Talha's man found Abu Talha and brought him, and he cut a niche for the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/313} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314}

The Muslims disagreed about the place of burial: some said he should be buried in his mosque, and others with his companions. Abu Bakr said: "I heard the Prophet say: No prophet is taken up but he is buried where he dies." So his bed was lifted and the grave was dug beneath it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314} The people entered upon him in groups, men, then women, then children, and no one led the people in prayer over him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314} He was buried in the middle of the night of Wednesday. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314} Aisha did not know of his burial until she heard the sound of spades in the depths of the night. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314} Ali, al-Fadl, Qutham and Shuqran went down into the grave, and Aws ibn Khawli went down with them after Ali had ordered him to. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/315}

Shuqran took a velvet cloth that the Prophet used to spread, put it in the grave and said: "By God, no one shall wear it after you, ever." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/315} Ibn Hisham relates from al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba that he used to claim he was the last to have seen the Prophet at the grave, saying that he had dropped his ring into it on purpose. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/315} A group from Iraq asked Ali in Kufa about this, and he said: "Al-Mughira lied; the last to have seen the Prophet in the grave was Qutham ibn al-Abbas." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/315}

## Notes on the reports
- The story of the washing and shrouding from Ibn Ishaq is given on several chains: Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr, al-Husayn ibn Abd Allah, and Yahya ibn Abbad from his father from Aisha. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/312}
- Disagreement among the people over the shrouding and the wrapping is transmitted from Ja'far ibn Muhammad from his father, and from Zayn al-Abidin. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/313}
- Ibn Hisham notes that one copy has "Asad" in place of "As'ad" in one line of Hassan's verse. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/314}
- The report about al-Mughira with Ali and Qutham comes in Ibn Ishaq's narration on the chain "from Muqsim from Abd Allah ibn al-Harith". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/315}

## For Sprouts
After the pledge to Abu Bakr, Ali ibn Abi Talib, al-Abbas and his sons al-Fadl and Qutham, Usama and Shuqran the Prophet's freedman washed the Prophet; he was washed with his shirt on and shrouded in three white cloths. The people entered in relays to pray over him with no imam, and he was buried on Wednesday night beneath the bed on which he died.

## For Explorers
Ibn Ishaq records that the people turned to preparing the Prophet on Tuesday after the pledge to Abu Bakr: Ali, al-Abbas, al-Fadl, Qutham, Usama and Shuqran washed him — washed with his shirt on, water poured over it and the body rubbed beneath — and shrouded him in three white cloths and a striped Yemeni wrap. They differed over the burial place until Abu Bakr said he heard the Prophet say no prophet is taken but he is buried where he is taken; the bed was lifted and the grave dug beneath — Abu Talha dug the lahd as the Medinans do. The people entered in relays — men, then women, then boys — praying over him with no imam; he was buried in the middle of Wednesday night, and A'isha says they did not know until they heard the picks in the night.

## For Scholars
Ali, al-Abbas, al-Fadl, Qutham, Usama and Shuqran washed the Prophet — Usama and Shuqran pouring water while Ali washed him against his chest with the shirt on — and Ibn Ishaq reports from A'isha that the people slept until a speaker called: wash him in his clothes. He was shrouded in three cloths: two Sahari pieces and a striped Yemeni wrap. The dispute over the burial place was settled by the Prophet's word through Abu Bakr — no prophet is taken but he is buried where he is taken; the bed was lifted and dug beneath, Abu Talha digging the lahd as the Medinans do (Abu Ubayda dug the shaqq of the Meccans). The people entered in relays praying over him with no imam — men then women then boys — and he was buried mid-Wednesday night; A'isha did not know until she heard the picks. Ali, al-Fadl, Qutham, Shuqran and Aws ibn Khawli descended into the grave, and Shuqran buried with him the velvet the Prophet used to lie on. Ibn Hisham records al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba's claim to be the last to see him in the grave, and Ali's reply that the last was Qutham ibn al-Abbas.
