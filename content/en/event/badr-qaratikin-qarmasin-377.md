## Overview
This battle of 377 AH is recorded in "Tarikh Ibn Khaldun" (al-Ibar). When Musharrif al-Dawla was settled in Baghdad, his commander Qaratikin al-Jahshiyari controlled his state, and this weighed on him, so he equipped him with armies to fight Badr ibn Hasanawayh the Kurd. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The course of the battle
The two armies met in the valley of Qarmisin, Badr was routed and went into hiding, and the troops of Qaratikin settled into their tents. Then Badr charged again, catching them before they could mount, killed many of them and seized what they had. Qaratikin escaped to the bridge of al-Nahrawan. Badr afterwards took control of the districts of the Jibal. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
