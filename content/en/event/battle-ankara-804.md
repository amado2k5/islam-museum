## Overview
In 804 AH (1402 CE) the armies of [[person/bayazid-i-yildirim|Bayazid I]] and Timur met near Ankara. al-Sallabi's book on the Ottoman state estimates Bayazid's forces at about a hundred and twenty thousand. Timur advanced at the head of large forces on 20 July 1402 CE. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 69-70}

## The battle
It was a summer of intense heat, and many of Bayazid's soldiers died of thirst. The Tatar soldiers and the soldiers of the Asian principalities he had recently conquered fled and joined Timur. The Mongols won, and Bayazid was taken captive and died the following year. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 69-70}

## Afterwards
The Christian states of the West rejoiced at Timur's victory and congratulated him. After the battle he took Iznik and Bursa, and freed Izmir from the knights of Rhodes. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 69-70}
