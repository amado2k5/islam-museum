## Overview
Some of the enemy fled to Juzjan, and al-Ahnaf sent [[person/al-aqra-ibn-habis|al-Aqra' ibn Habis al-Tamimi]] against them with cavalry. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The sender here is [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]].

## The counsel of al-Aqra'
al-Aqra' addressed Banu Tamim, urging them to love one another and to be generous to one another. He told them to begin by fighting their bellies and their private parts, so that their religion would be set right, and not to embezzle, so that their spoils and their struggle would be preserved for them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
al-Aqra' met the enemy at [[place/juzjan|Juzjan]]. The Muslims were first pushed back, then they charged again, routed the enemy and took the town by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} al-Baladhuri quotes verses by Ibn al-Gharizah al-Nahshali lamenting young men who fell at Juzjan.
