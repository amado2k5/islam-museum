## Overview
The day of Dhu Qar: [[group/banu-shayban|Banu Shayban]]'s victory over the Persian host and the retinue of Iyas ibn Qabisa — the first day the Arabs prevailed over the Persians.

## The story
When Chosroes imprisoned [[person/al-numan-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir]], king of al-Hira, he put [[person/iyas-ibn-qabisa|Iyas ibn Qabisa al-Ta'i]] in his place and ordered him to demand of [[person/hani-ibn-masud|Hani ibn Mas'ud]] the surrender of al-Nu'man's family and property in his keeping; Hani refused out of pride and honour, and declared war on the king {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Soon Chosroes' marzbans and battalions came in Iyas's train, and a mighty battle was fought between the two sides at Dhu Qar in which Banu Shayban prevailed and the Persians were routed utterly {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## Its standing
This was the first day the Arabs prevailed over the Persians, and it came shortly after the Prophet's birth: the Prophet was born eight months into Iyas ibn Qabisa's governorship of al-Hira {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
A man named Hani kept his friend the king's deposit and refused to hand it to the Persians, so they fought him — and his tribe defeated the Persian army near Kufa.
