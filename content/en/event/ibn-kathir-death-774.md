## Summary
Ibn Kathir died on Thursday 16 Sha'ban 774 at Damascus, aged seventy-four {cite:suluk-maqrizi j.3 q.1 p. 208}; al-Durar agrees on Sha'ban 774 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
## Al-Maqrizi's obituary
Al-Maqrizi names him among the year's dead: the shaykh 'Imad al-Din Abu l-Fida' Isma'il, son of Shihab al-Din 'Umar ibn Kathir, al-Qurashi al-Shafi'i — imam, mufassir, muhaddith, preacher and jurist {cite:suluk-maqrizi j.3 q.1 p. 208}.
