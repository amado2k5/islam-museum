## The story
Malik relates from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala from Sa id ibn al-Musayyib who used to say: the Messenger of God said: the shaytan intends one or two; when they are three he does not intend them {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2802|Muwatta 2802]]. The muhaqqiq notes the report is mursal by agreement of the Muwatta transmitters.

## The meaning
In this hadith the shaytan does not intend a gathering of three; it belongs to the reports of mutual aid and company against whispers {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}. Its chain is mursal by the muhaqqiq agreement.
