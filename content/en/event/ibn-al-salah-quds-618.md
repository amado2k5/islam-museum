## Summary
On his second tour of al-Sham, Ibn al-Salah taught in Jerusalem at the Salahiyya madrasa, the school named after al-Malik al-Nasir Salah al-Din Yusuf b. Ayyub. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 229-230}

## Students gathered round him
He stayed a while and the people studied under him and benefited; Ibn al-'Imad calls it 'the Nizamiyya school in the noble city of Jerusalem'. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 229} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}
