## Overview
Then [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] called those who were in the prison from [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]], such as [[person/asad-ibn-abi-yafur|As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur]] and Ibrahim ibn Khalaf {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.222}. He told them of their ill treatment of him and the good he had shown them, and said: I have given you your lives, so fear God in your secret and in your open conduct; he showed them grace and freed them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.222}. The heading in the text reads: al-Hadi's freeing of As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur and Ibrahim ibn Khalaf from the prison of Shibam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.222}.

## Detail
The other manuscript's footnote has your affairs where the text has your lives in al-Hadi's words; both readings stand. Ibrahim ibn Khalaf is linked to the record of [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif]] on the name and the line of Tarif; [[event/battle-of-al-rahba-288|the account of the battle of al-Rahba]] says he later met al-Hadi in battle, and the pardon here comes before that, as the book tells it. The pardon and his words are the narrator's account, attributed to him, with no judgment. It is followed by [[event/al-hadi-leaves-shibam-fights-on-the-road-and-goes-by-najr-and-al-bawn-to-raydah-and-bayt-dhud-288]].
