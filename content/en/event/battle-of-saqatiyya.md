## Overview
At al-Saqatiyya below Kaskar: the fugitives joined Narsi, the king's nephew, with the men of Barusma and al-Zawabi and Bustam's sons Banduwayh and Tiruwayh; Abu 'Ubayd fell on them from the Namarq, the Persians broke, Narsi fled, and the Muslims seized his camp and lands. They took the narsiyan — a date-palm only Chosroes or his honored ate, planted by none else — fed it to the peasants, sent its fifth to 'Umar, and wrote: "God has fed us foods the Khusraws used to guard; we wished you to see them and thank His favor." Earlier Buran had been enthroned for ten years after Rustam put out Azarmidukht's eye {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 275}.
