## Overview
In 131 AH al-Tabari mentions the death of Nasr ibn Sayyar. Among what he reports in that year is that Qahtaba sent his son al-Hasan against Nasr, who was in Qumis. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.403}

The page is short because the available data is limited to this report. The details of the death need an additional source.
