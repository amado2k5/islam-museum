## Overview
In 298 AH the army of the amir Ahmad ibn Isma'il al-Samani marched to [[place/sijistan|Sistan]] under [[person/husayn-ibn-ali-al-marwazi|al-Husayn ibn Ali al-Marwazi]]. The army besieged [[person/al-muaddal-ibn-ali-ibn-al-layth|al-Mu'addal ibn Ali ibn al-Layth]], the last of the Saffarid amirs there, and he made peace with them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 46-47}

## What happened
The commander Ahmad ibn Sahl, Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar and Simajur al-Dawati were with him. Ahmad had marched to Abu Ali Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Layth at Bust and taken him prisoner. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 46-47}

## After the conquest
The Samanids took Sistan and appointed Abu Salih Mansur ibn Ishaq over it. Al-Mu'addal and Subkari were carried to Baghdad, displayed on two elephants. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 46-47}

The source gives the month of Rajab 298 AH; the Gregorian conversion is derived and needs an additional source.
