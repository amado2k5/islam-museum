## Overview
The author of "Tarikh al-Khulafa" says that the activity of the Batiniyya increased in Iraq in 494 AH, and they killed people. The danger grew so great that the amirs wore armour under their clothes. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 658,660}

## Some of those killed
They killed many people, among them [[person/al-ruwayni|al-Ruwayani]], author of "al-Bahr" and shaykh of the Shafi'is, whom the Batiniyya killed in Baghdad. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 658,660}

The perpetrators are described here in the source's own terms. The data does not include the account of the Batiniyya themselves. It needs an additional source.
