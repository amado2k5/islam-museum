## Summary
At the end of 1832 the Ottoman army of Grand Vizier Reshid Pasha was crushed under the walls of [[place/konya|Konya]] by the Egyptian army of Ibrahim Pasha, son of Muhammad Ali, in the first Turko-Egyptian war. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 288}

## A city that could still fight
The battle proves that Konya, though long since reduced to a provincial centre of the vast Ottoman Empire, could still occasionally play a military role. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 288}
