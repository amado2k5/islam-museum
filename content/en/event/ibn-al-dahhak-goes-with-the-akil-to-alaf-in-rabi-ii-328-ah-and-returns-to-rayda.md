## Overview
The Akil call Ibn al-Dahhak out in Rabi II. He goes with them to Alaf on Saturday, eight nights into the month. The Yirsamis fight them, and one of them is killed. Ibn al-Dahhak returns to Rayda, and Yahya ibn Ahmad stays at al-Ghayl.

## Detail
The book says al-Qasim held on until Rabi II came, when the Akil called Ibn al-Dahhak out. He went with them on Saturday, eight nights into the month, reached Alaf and wrote to Banu Sa'd. He then set out, and the Yirsamis fought him and one of them was killed; he entered the Yirsam quarter, the verb at that point being unclear in the scan. Banu Sa'd spread rumours against al-Qasim and he left them, and his brother Yahya ibn Ahmad went to al-Ghayl and stayed with him. Ibn al-Dahhak returned to Rayda on Saturday, two days before the end of the month. The book does not state the year of these events; the year 328 is placed here by narrative order only and is not stated by the book.
