## Overview
Ibn Taymiyya's first incursion into political life came in 693/1293 with the affair of Assaf al-Nasrani, a Christian of Suwayda accused of having insulted the Prophet. His intransigence in the affair led to his first imprisonment, at the Adhrawiyya, where he wrote his first major work, al-Sarim al-maslul 'ala shatim al-Rasul {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 983}.
