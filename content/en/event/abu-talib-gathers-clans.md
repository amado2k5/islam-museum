[[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]] kept watch on the polytheists, fearful for his nephew, weighing incident upon incident: their threat to confront him, their attempt to trade him for Umara ibn al-Walid, Abu Jahl's stone, Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt's strangling of the Prophet with his cloak, and Ibn al-Khattab going out with his sword before his conversion — from these he caught the scent of harm. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

So he gathered Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib to stand as one hand with him in protecting his nephew; they all answered him except [[person/abu-lahab|Abu Lahab]], who broke with his father's clan for their enemy — and this gathering was the prelude to [[event/plot-of-dar-al-nadwa|Quraysh's plot]] and [[event/boycott-of-banu-hashim|the boycott]] that followed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
