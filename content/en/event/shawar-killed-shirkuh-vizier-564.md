## Summary
In Rabi al-Awwal 564 AH, Asad al-Din Shirkuh took control of the land of Egypt with Nur al-Din's troops, after he had defeated the Franks at Bilbays. The Franks had besieged Cairo, and Shawar had set fire to the city of Misr; the fire burned there for fifty-four days. Al-Adid had appealed to Nur al-Din by sending locks of his women's hair. Shawar then made peace with the Franks for a million dinars, and they withdrew. When Shirkuh arrived, Shawar was killed and the vizierate was given in his place. The new vizier ruled justly among the people for some days, then died. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 11-15}
