## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad says: I asked my father [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] how [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] took the people's pledge. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117} The manuscript margin carries the heading "the form of the pledge". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum describes what it says without a verdict, and the pledge in Islamic practice generally has its own record at [[concept/bayah]].

## The pledger first repents
He said that Yahya ibn al-Husayn used to take the pledge of the people: he took the man by the hand and had him repent before he pledged, telling him to say: O God, I turn to You from every sin, fault and misdeed, so accept my repentance, forgive my sin, ease my affair, help me against myself and make Paradise sure to me by Your mercy. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117}

## The covenant
He then told him to swear by God, than whom there is no god, knower of the unseen and the seen, the Merciful, the Compassionate, failing which God's covenant and compact and the strictest that God took from the prophets would be upon him: that he would support him; stand for the right with him; command good and forbid wrong; take the right from whoever owed it, near or far, noble or humble, with no fear of any blame in God's cause; and obey him so long as he obeyed God, so that if he disobeyed God no obedience would be owed him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117}

## After the covenant
The narrator says he used to hear him, once he had taken the covenant, say "O God, bear witness", and then recite a verse of the sura of al-Fath that those who pledge to the Prophet pledge to God, given as [[passage/quran-48-10|48:10]] in the editor's footnote. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117} See [[event/al-hadi-bayat-al-sabr-and-the-eclipse-prayer-286]].
