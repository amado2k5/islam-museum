## Overview
"He stood with only seven horsemen", and the page then names them: al-Qasim and Abdullah, sons of Muhammad ibn al-Qasim; al-Husayn ibn Musa ibn Sulayman; Ali ibn Muhammad; Yahya al-Sulaymi; Ibrahim al-Hajuri; Ali ibn Sa'id al-Yarsami; and Husayn al-Aqadi {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}.

## Detail
The footnote on p. 297 reads "it is to be noted that the number of these horsemen is eight, so perhaps the right number is nine" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}. This is the editor's note; the museum does not decide it and adds or drops no name. It keeps "Ali ibn Muhammad" apart from Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah al-Abbasi, the book's narrator, because the text does not equate them, so no record was made for "Ali ibn Muhammad" here. The other names have records: [[person/al-qasim-ibn-muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-horseman-292|al-Qasim]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-horseman-292|Abdullah]], sons of Muhammad ibn al-Qasim; [[person/al-husayn-ibn-musa-ibn-sulayman-horseman-292|al-Husayn ibn Musa ibn Sulayman]]; [[person/yahya-al-sulaymi-horseman-292|Yahya al-Sulaymi]]; [[person/ibrahim-al-hujuri-horseman-292|Ibrahim al-Hajuri]]; [[person/ali-ibn-said-al-yarsami-horseman-292|Ali ibn Sa'id al-Yarsami]]; and [[person/husayn-al-aqadi-horseman-292|Husayn al-Aqadi]]. Al-Hadi then stood "facing the Hakami's camp, and they had fallen on the camp and plundered it" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.297}. The event follows [[event/the-fight-in-tihama-and-the-breaking-of-al-hadis-left-wing-292|the breaking of the left wing]]. The account is in a Zaydi book in the voice of the imam's followers; its descriptions and its judgments on opponents are the narrator's or the poet's, and the museum adopts none of them and gives no verdict.
