## Summary
In 369/980 an embassy from Constantinople reached Baghdad seeking the surrender of the rebel commander Bardas Skleros, who had taken refuge in Diyar Bakr. Adud al-Dawla sent his reply to the Byzantine court 'by the hand of the qadi Abu Bakr al-Bakillani' {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 233}; al-Baqillani's own biographical entry likewise records a visit to the Byzantine court {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1018}.
