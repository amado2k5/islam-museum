## Summary
Ibn Khallikan kept company with Ibn al-Salah at Damascus in 632/1235 and gathered his fatwas into a volume; he had reached the city in Shawwal 632/June–July 1235. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}

## A pupil's testimony
Ibn Khallikan wrote: he was one of the most eminent men of his age in tafsir, hadith and fiqh, with a share in several disciplines; his fatwas were sound, and he was one of the shaykhs from whom I benefited. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186}
