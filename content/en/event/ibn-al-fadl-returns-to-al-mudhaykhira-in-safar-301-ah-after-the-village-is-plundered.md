## Overview
Ibn al-Fadl returns to al-Mudhaykhira in Safar 301 AH, after the village is plundered and only five men are left with him.

## Detail
Ibn al-Fadl leaves al-Mudhaykhira on Thursday, six days before the end of Shawwal 300 AH, goes to Jaysh'an, which he uses as a pretext to fight Madhhij, and then to al-Sirw and a fortress called Sana'. Ramzam al-Madhhaji fights him, and a truce follows on the condition that he not enter their land. At the end of Safar 301 AH he learns that Mulahiz has sent a force against al-Mudhaykhira, leaves al-Sirw secretly at night, and finds the village plundered and the people scattered; he finds only five men, and stays at al-Mudhaykhira, which the author says is in his unbelief and apostasy.
