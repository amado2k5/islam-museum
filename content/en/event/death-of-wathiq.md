## Overview
The caliph [[person/al-wathiq|al-Wathiq]] died in [[place/samarra|Samarra]] on Wednesday, six nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 232 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.534}

## A story after his death
al-Suyuti relates that he was left alone when he died, and while people were busy with the oath to al-Mutawakkil, a large rat came and pulled out his eye and ate it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.534}

## Dating
The corresponding Gregorian date (around August 847 CE) is derived from the Hijri date and is not stated in the source. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.534}
