## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stayed at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] and sent governors to the districts, charged them, reminded them of the days of God and told them to command the good and forbid the wrong; he wrote them the letter that the narrator has given at the head of the book on the zakat of grain and livestock, set out in [[event/al-hadi-charge-to-his-governors-284|his charge to his governors of 284 AH]]; a few days later he wrote another letter and ordered it read at Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.211}. The narrator does not give the text of the second letter. When Safar began al-Hadi went out to Shibam with [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]], prayed the Friday prayer in the mosque and gave an eloquent sermon in the narrator's words, admonishing the people and reminding them of God; he told them that what used to be taken from them in grain, fines and levies was not due from them and that he had lifted it all, taking from them only what God had made obligatory and not going beyond God's rule, and the people, the narrator says, rejoiced {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.211}.

## Detail
Then he went up to [[place/bayt-dhukhar|Bayt Dhukhar]], went round it and gathered its people and spoke to them as he had spoken to the people of Shibam, telling them to fear God and to command the good and forbid the wrong, and appointed over the mountain a man of the line of al-Hasan ibn Ali called [[person/ali-ibn-al-abbas-governor-of-the-mountain-288|Ali ibn al-Abbas]], one of those living in Tabaristan; he came down to Shibam, stayed some days and sent governors to its districts, charging them to fear God and command the good and forbid the wrong {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.211}. He left his son Abu al-Qasim at Shibam with troops and went back to Sana'a, where he stayed some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.211}. The narrator does not say which mountain he gave, and from the context it is understood to be Bayt Dhukhar, which is probable and not certain. The lifting of fines and levies is the narrator's report of al-Hadi's sermon; the museum does not discuss its juristic status or compare it. This Shibam is not linked to a place record because the editor does not identify it.
