## Overview
Ibn Khaldun records the battle of Qayrawan in 748 AH, and counts it among the greatest events of the Marinid state. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1761}

## The account
The Arabs of Ifriqiya from the sons of Muhalhil, the Ka'ub and the Banu Sulaym rose against the armies of the sultan [[person/abu-al-hasan-al-marini|Abu al-Hasan al-Marini]], and set Ahmad ibn Hamza over them. The sultan advanced against them from Tunis at the time of the pilgrimage, met them at al-Thaniyya before Qayrawan, and defeated them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1761}

## The reversal
Then they broke their word again and surrounded him in Qayrawan. His battle line collapsed and his camp was plundered. He reached Sousse and boarded his fleets to Tunis. The Arabs besieged Tunis until the sultan withdrew to the Maghrib, when his son [[person/abu-inan-faris|Abu Inan]] rose up to seize the kingdom. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1762}
