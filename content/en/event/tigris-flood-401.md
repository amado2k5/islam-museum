## Summary
In Rajab of year 401 the Tigris rose, and the rise stretched into Ramadan until it reached twenty-one cubits; water entered most riverside houses and the quarters of Qati'at al-Daqiq, Bab al-Tibn, Bab al-Sha'ir and Bab al-Taq {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.251}.

## What the flood did
The water overflowed onto the Mosque of al-Kaff at Qati'at al-Daqiq and wrecked it, carrying away its piers and roofs; the banks burst and villages and fortresses drowned {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.252}.

## For young visitors
In year 401 the Tigris flooded over Baghdad, filling whole riverside districts and drowning a mosque, forts and villages — only one year after the river had run too low for boats.
