

## The exchange — what each side said
- **Shah Abdul Aziz (the author)**: the Tuhfa is an ordered reply to Twelver roots — the divinely designated imamate, the imams' infallibility, taqiyya, the standing of the dissenting Companions — presenting in each chapter the opposing case in its own words first, then dismantling it from inside Sunni principles (text, consensus, reason).
- **The Shia argumentative material (as the book presents it)**: the Ghadir and Thaqalayn texts, Ali's designated authority, and the recorded historical stances in the Sahihs and chronicles.
- **The author's answer (chapter by chapter)**: builds on distinguishing the sound-chained from the fabricated: what is proven of merit is consensually accepted, while the explicit designation on which the doctrine rests is exactly what is in question; Companion conduct is governed by the rule of ijtihad.
- **Al-Alusi (the abridger)**: corrected and refined the Tuhfa in Arabic — pruning and strengthening the argument — making it the Sunni schoolbook of reply.
- **The value**: a written disputation between two texts — the Tuhfa reads the doctrine from inside and answers it by its own method, and remains the Sunni school's root in its field. {cite:al-islam-al-murajaat} {cite:mahajjah-al-murajaat-critique}
