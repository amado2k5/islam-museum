## Summary
Tuhfa Ithna Ashariyya by Shah Abdul Aziz Dehlawi: the methodical Persian reply to Shi'ism, whose Arabic abridgement by al-Alusi is the Sunni debaters' reference. {cite:al-islam-murajaat}

## Standing
Not a debate transcript but a composed written disputation: it orders the opponent's claims and answers them one by one — the Sunni constitution of refutation.
