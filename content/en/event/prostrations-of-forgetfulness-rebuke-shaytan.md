## The story
Malik relates from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar mursal: if one of you doubts his prayer and does not know whether he prayed three or four, let him pray one rak a and prostrate twice sitting before the salam; if the rak a was the fifth the two prostrations complete it, and if it was the fourth the two prostrations are a rebuke to the shaytan {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-252|Muwatta 252]].

## The meaning
In this report the two prostrations of forgetfulness, when the prayer is complete, are a rebuke to the shaytan — a rebuff to him for what he whispered into the worshipper s prayer {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
