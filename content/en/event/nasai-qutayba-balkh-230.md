## Summary
Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb al-Nasa'i journeyed to Qutayba ibn Sa'id al-Thaqafi — shaykh of al-Bukhari and Muslim and the muhaddith of Khurasan resident at Balkh — when he was fifteen, and said: 'I stayed with him a year and two months' {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 617} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 106}.

## In his biographies
He kept the hadith of Ibn Lahi'a in his notebooks as received through Qutayba yet would not teach them, until Abu Talib Ahmad ibn Nasr the hafiz said: 'Who could endure what al-Nasa'i endures?' {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 108}.
