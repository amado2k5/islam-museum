## Overview
In 260 famine gripped all the lands of Islam: Mecca's sojourners were driven out by the dearth to Medina and elsewhere, its governor departed, and a kurr of wheat at Baghdad reached one hundred and fifty dinars for months; that year the Zanj commander 'Ali b. Zayd al-'Alawi of Kufa was killed {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 110}.
