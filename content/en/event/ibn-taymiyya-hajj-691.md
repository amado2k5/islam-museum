## Overview
Ibn Taymiyya performed the pilgrimage to Mecca toward the end of 691/November 1292 and was back in Damascus in 692/February 1293, bringing from the journey the substance of his treatise Manasik al-hajj, in which he denounced a number of innovations that had entered the rites of the pilgrimage {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 983}.
