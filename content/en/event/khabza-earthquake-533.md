## Summary
In 533 AH an earthquake struck Khabza. About 200,000 people, or 30,000, were killed. It was felt within a radius of ten leagues {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 582}.

## The sinking that followed
In 534 AH Khabza sank into the earth, and where the town had been there was black water. Merchants who had come from the town stayed at the cemeteries, weeping for their families {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 584}.
