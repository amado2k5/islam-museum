## Overview
al-Baladhuri describes how [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] came to hold the land of [[place/al-sibayn|al-Sibayn]] after he had borne the cost of damming the breaches, and how it then passed to the caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 413}

## Damming the breaches
Breaches in the dams burst out in the days of [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]], and he wrote to [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] that he estimated the cost of damming them at three million dirhams. al-Walid thought the sum excessive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 413}

Maslama offered to pay for the work if he were granted the low-lying lands where the water collects, on condition that al-Hajjaj took charge of the money. al-Walid agreed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 413}

## Development
Maslama obtained adjoining lands in several districts (tasasij), dug al-Sibayn, brought in workers and cultivators, and developed the lands. He added many estates to them, and the source says that this was to strengthen his position. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 413}

## After the Abbasid state
When the Abbasid state came, and the wealth of the Umayyads was seized, [[person/dawud-ibn-ali|Dawud ibn Ali]] ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas was granted all of al-Sibayn. It was later bought from his heirs with its rights and boundaries, and it became one of the estates of the caliphate. The subject is connected to [[concept/iqta|iqta']]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 413}
