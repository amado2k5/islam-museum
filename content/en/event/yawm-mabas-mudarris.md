## Summary
The Day of Mabas and Mudarris was one of the last days of Aws and Khazraj before Bu'ath. They met at the two walls: Khazraj stood behind Mudarris and Aws behind Mabas. They fought fiercely for days, and then Aws were routed until they entered their houses and fortified towers, a shameful defeat such as they had never suffered. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 507}
