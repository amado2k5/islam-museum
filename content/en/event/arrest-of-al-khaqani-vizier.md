## Overview
In 313 AH the vizier Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Khaqani was arrested and placed under guard in his house. On the same day, Ahmad ibn Ubayd Allah al-Khasibi was invested with the vizierate. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.109}
