## Overview
The Day of Rahrahan is one of the days of the Arabs before Islam, between the Banu Amir ibn Sa'sa'a and the Banu Darim ibn Malik of Tamim. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40-41}

## The account
al-Baladhuri, in "Ansab al-Ashraf", relates that Khalid ibn Ja'far al-Kilabi fought the Banu Abs, and with his companions killed their lord Zuhayr ibn Judhayma, and then sought protection with al-Nu'man. al-Harith ibn Zalim guaranteed Ghatafan that he would finish with Khalid, and he killed him at the court of al-Nu'man, and Ma'bad ibn Zurara gave him refuge. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40-41}

[[person/al-ahwas-ibn-jafar|al-Ahwas ibn Ja'far]] went out with the Banu Amir against the Banu Darim, and they met at Rahrahan and fought. [[person/mabad-ibn-zurara|Ma'bad ibn Zurara]] was struck with a spear, and was taken captive by Udrut of the Amir and by al-Tufayl, the two sons of Malik of Ghani, who gave them twenty camels as reward. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40-41}

## Dating
There is no verified date for the day. It belongs to the days of the Arabs before Islam, about a generation before it. It needs an additional source. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 40}
