## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha al-Shaybani]] used to raid the Sawad with men of his tribe, and his news reached [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], who asked about him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Introducing al-Muthanna
[[person/qays-ibn-asim|Qays ibn Asim al-Minqari]] told Abu Bakr that he was a man of no obscure fame and no unknown lineage. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The commission
Al-Muthanna came to Abu Bakr and asked to be put in charge of those among his people who had accepted Islam, to fight the Persians. Abu Bakr wrote him a commission to that effect, and he marched until he camped at Khafan and called his people to Islam, and they accepted it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Afterwards Abu Bakr wrote to Khalid ibn al-Walid ordering him to march to Iraq, and wrote to al-Muthanna ordering him to hear and obey Khalid. Al-Baladhuri says that Abu Bakr sent Khalid from [[place/medina|Medina]], and that the other account on this differs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date is approximate, before Khalid's march to Iraq in 12 AH according to al-Baladhuri (p. 337), and the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived.
