In Sha'ban of the sixth year of the Hijra, intelligence reached the Prophet that al-Harith ibn Abi Dirar, chief of Banu al-Mustaliq of Khuza'a, was gathering his people and mustering subtribes of the Arabs to raid Medina; the Prophet sent Burayda ibn al-Husayb al-Aslami as a scout to confirm the report, and when he returned confirming it, the Prophet called up the Muslims and went out with about seven hundred fighters, a large number of hypocrites among them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Muslims surprised them at the water of al-Muraysi' near Qudayd; the two sides exchanged arrows for an hour, then the Muslims charged as one man and routed Banu al-Mustaliq, taking their herds, wealth and captives. Among the captives was Barra bint al-Harith, the people's chief's daughter; she contracted to ransom herself and sought the Prophet's help — he paid her ransom and married her, naming her [[person/juwayriya-bint-al-harith|Juwayriya]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When the Muslims learned of the Prophet's marriage to her they released the captives in their hands, saying "the in-laws of the Messenger of God"; by the blessing of her marriage a hundred households of Banu al-Mustaliq were freed, and she was the most blessed of women upon her people. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

This expedition also witnessed the hypocrites' attempts to kindle tribal strife: a man of the Muhajirun and a man of the Ansar jostled at the well of al-Muraysi', the Muhajir crying "to me, Muhajirun!" and the Ansari "to me, Ansar!"; the Prophet came out angered and said: "the call of the Jahiliyya while I am among you? Leave it — it is rotten." [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abdullah ibn Ubayy]] said: "if we return to Medina, the mightier will drive out the meaner" — and God sent down his refutation and exposure in [[passage/quran-63-8|Sura al-Munafiqun, verse 8]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## For Sprouts
In Sha'ban 6 AH the Prophet marched with some seven hundred to Banu al-Mustaliq and overcame them at the water of al-Muraysi'; he married Juwayriya and a hundred households of her people were freed by her marriage's blessing.

## For Explorers
In Sha'ban of the Hijra's sixth year the Prophet, told that al-Harith ibn Abi Dirar of Banu al-Mustaliq was gathering Arabs to raid Medina, verified it with a scout and marched with some seven hundred fighters. The Muslims surprised them at the water of al-Muraysi' near Qudayd, routed them and took captives. Among them was Barra bint al-Harith, the chief's daughter; she contracted to free herself, the Prophet paid it and married her — Juwayriya. The Muslims freed the captives they held as 'the Messenger of God's in-laws', and a hundred households were freed. The campaign also saw the hypocrites' bid to kindle strife: an Emigrant and an Ansari quarreled at the well and each called his party; the Prophet said 'a call of Jahiliyya while I am among you? leave it, it is rotten'.

## For Scholars
In Sha'ban of the Hijra's sixth year, intelligence reached the Prophet that al-Harith ibn Abi Dirar, chief of Banu al-Mustaliq of Khuza'a, was gathering his people and Arab clans to raid Medina; he sent Burayda ibn al-Husayb al-Aslami to verify, and when he came back confirming, the Prophet rallied the Muslims and marched with some seven hundred fighters, many hypocrites among them. The Muslims surprised them at the water of al-Muraysi' near Qudayd; the two sides shot for an hour, then the Muslims charged as one man and routed Banu al-Mustaliq, taking their cattle, goods and captives. Among the captives was Barra bint al-Harith, the people's chief's daughter; she wrote a contract to free herself and sought the Prophet's help — he paid her contract and married her, naming her Juwayriya. Learning of the marriage, the Muslims released the captives they held, saying 'the Messenger of God's in-laws', and a hundred households of Banu al-Mustaliq were freed by her marriage's blessing — she became the most blessed of women to her people. The campaign also saw the hypocrites' bid to kindle tribal strife: an Emigrant and an Ansari quarreled at al-Muraysi' well, the Emigrant crying 'Muhajirun!' and the Ansari 'Ansar!'; the Prophet came out angered saying 'a call of Jahiliyya while I am among you? leave it, it is rotten'; Abdullah ibn Ubayy said 'when we return to Medina the mightier will drive out the humbler', and God belied and exposed him in [[passage/quran-63-8|Surat al-Munafiqun, verse 8]].
