## Overview
In 158 AH [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] ordered his deputy in Mecca to imprison [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]] and Abbad ibn Kathir, and they were imprisoned. People feared that he would kill them when the pilgrimage arrived. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 426}

## The death
al-Suyuti says that al-Mansur came back sick, and died of a bowel complaint in Dhu al-Hijja, and was buried between al-Hajun and the Well of Maymun, near [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 426}

## Note
The Gregorian date (around October 775 CE) is derived by conversion, and the account comes from a single source. It needs an additional source.
