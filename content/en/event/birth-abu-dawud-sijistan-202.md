## Overview
Abu Dawud, Sulayman ibn al-Ash'ath ibn Ishaq ibn Bashir ibn Shaddad ibn 'Amr ibn 'Imran al-Azdi al-Sijistani, was born in the year two hundred and two {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 358}, around 817 CE {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 133}, his nisba pointing to the province of Sijistan bordering the lands of Sind {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 361}.

## A nisba clarified
Some wrongly claimed Sijistan was a village near Basra; al-Dhahabi answers that Sijistan is a distinct province reached from the side of Herat, and that Abu Dawud had entered Baghdad before ever coming to Basra {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 361}.
