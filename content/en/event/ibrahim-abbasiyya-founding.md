## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/ibrahim-ibn-al-aghab|Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab]] founded the city of al-Abbasiyya near [[place/qayrawan|Qayrawan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}

## The account
The frontier troops besieged Ibrahim in Qayrawan, and the Muradi and the Mamtun came to him with money from the tax of Egypt. When they were given it, they dispersed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}

## The building
Ibrahim built the White Palace south of Qayrawan, two miles from it, and laid out plots around it for the people, who built. He built a congregational mosque of lime, brick and marble columns, roofed it with cedar, and its area was about two hundred cubits by two hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}

He bought slaves and freed them, until they reached five thousand, and settled them around it. He named the city al-Abbasiyya, and it was flourishing until the time of al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}

The date in the data is approximate (around 800 CE) and derived.
