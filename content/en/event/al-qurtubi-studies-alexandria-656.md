## Overview
Al-Qurtubi heard part of al-Mufhim, the commentary on Sahih Muslim, from its author Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Umar al-Qurtubi (Ibn al-Muzayyin), the famous hadith scholar resident at Alexandria {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari 2/211}; the master taught in Egypt and died at the thaghr on 14 Dhu al-Qa'da 656 {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 17/98}. Al-Qurtubi also heard from Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Yahsubi and the hafiz Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Muhammad al-Bakri {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari 2/211}.
