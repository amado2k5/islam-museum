## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] sent for the people and called them, and when they came he asked what had moved them to plunder [[place/athafit|Athafit]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; its sayings are attributed to their narrators and the museum gives no verdict on them.

## The exchange and the repentance
They said the people of Athafit had killed Abu Umar and plundered him and what he carried, and al-Hadi said they had no right to do this without his opinion, until he commanded them what was right. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} He said he had resolved to withdraw and speak to none of them until a people came who knew God and knew the lawful from the unlawful, and told them there would be no dealing between them until they returned everything they had taken from Athafit, and if not he was not right for them nor they for him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} They said they repented and would return what they held; this was on the second of Dhu al-Hijja, and some brought what they had while others did not yet. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109}

## Recovery of the goods
He sent word to the people of Athafit that anyone with something missing should come and take it; they came and recognised their goods, each taking what was his, and some said something of theirs was still missing. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109}

## The day of Arafa
On the day of Tarwiya he sent word for people to come to him on the day of Arafa; a great crowd gathered, and he went out with them, sat in a wide place and preached, reminding them of God. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} He ordered firewood gathered in one place and a fire set to it, and when it blazed said: O people, who among you will stand and enter this fire, and I call God to witness that I give him everything I have of clothing, equipment, goods and money. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} They said: who would enter this fire, and what use are goods to one who enters the fire? He said to them: then what moves you to the deeds that bring you into the fire; fear God and return what you hold of the goods of Muslims, the weak, the poor, widows and the oppressed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} The words are his as the narrator kept them, who adds that he promised them Paradise if they obeyed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} They said with one voice that they would obey him, return what they held and die before him, and then dispersed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109}

## After the Adha
A day or two after the Adha they began returning what they held while the people of Athafit took back their goods; al-Hadi stayed at the [[place/al-darb-banu-rabia-yemen|al-Darb of Banu Rabi'a]] while al-Da'am was at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.109} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} The book adds that al-Hadi kept to al-Darb because it was a fortified place. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} The sequel is in [[event/abu-al-atahiya-promises-horse-and-sends-his-brother-jarrah-285]].
