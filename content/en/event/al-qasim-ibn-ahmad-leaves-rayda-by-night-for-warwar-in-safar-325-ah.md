## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Ahmad learns of a plot against him in Rayda and leaves by night on Saturday, eight days before the end of Safar 325 AH, for Warwar. His brother al-Hasan had dealt with Ibn al-Dahhak against him and funded him.

## Detail
The book says a dispute and estrangement arose between al-Qasim and Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Dahhak until al-Qasim went out to fight him. His tribe did not support him, their ranks broke and they fled from him, and he returned to Rayda. Ibn al-Dahhak secretly corresponded with the tribe, and al-Qasim learned what was planned against him. He left by night on Saturday, eight days before the end of Safar 325 AH, for Warwar. His brother al-Hasan, who had dealt with Ibn al-Dahhak against him and funded him, also went to Warwar, and Ibn al-Dahhak stayed at Rayda in correspondence with Muzaffar ibn Alyan. The weekday and date are as printed.
