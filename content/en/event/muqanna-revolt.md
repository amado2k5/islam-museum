## Overview
The rising of al-Muqanna' the Khurasani took place under al-Mahdi between 159 and 161 AH in the Merv region. As Taqqush presents it, it was an extension of the risings of Sunbadh and the Rawandiyya, and a continuation of the memory of Abu Muslim. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.79}

## Spread
A great multitude followed him in Bukhara and Soghd. His followers, called the Mubayyida, spread through the districts of Kush and Nasaf and raided the safe areas. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.79}

## The end
[[person/said-al-harashi|Sa'id al-Harashi]] defeated them and destroyed them. The source says that [[person/al-muqanna|al-Muqanna']] burned himself before he was taken. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.79}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date, and the event needs an additional source.
