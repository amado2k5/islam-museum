## Summary
Ghiyath al-Din Mahmud ibn Ghiyath al-Din, lord of Ghur, was killed. After him the realm of the Ghurids fell into disorder, until the Khwarazmians took most of their lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 337}
