## Summary
In 605 AH Ghiyath al-Din Mahmud ibn Ghiyath al-Din Muhammad ibn Sam al-Ghuri, the last Ghurid king, was killed together with Ali Shah, Khwarazmshah's brother, on a single day, and all Khurasan submitted to Khwarazmshah. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 337}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that after Khwarazmshah handed Herat to his uncle Amir Malik, he ordered him to march on Ghiyath al-Din Mahmud, lord of Ghur and Firuzkuh, seize him and his brother Ali Shah, and take Firuzkuh; Mahmud offered obedience and asked for safe-conduct, which was granted, but when he came down Amir Malik seized both. They asked to be sent to Khwarazmshah; informed of it, he ordered their execution, and both were killed the same day, whereupon all Khurasan submitted to Khwarazmshah. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 337} Ibn al-Athir adds that this Ghiyath al-Din was the last of the Ghurid kings, that their state was among the best-conducted, most just and most jihad-active of dynasties, and that Mahmud was just, forbearing, generous and among the noblest of kings in character. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 337}
