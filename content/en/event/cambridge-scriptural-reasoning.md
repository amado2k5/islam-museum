

## The exchange — what each side said
- **The Jewish interlocutors (Ochs and colleagues)**: read the Hebrew Bible in its many voices — halakha, midrash, the community's destiny — insisting on the covenant's eternity and the depth of layered interpretation.
- **The Muslim interlocutor (Abdal Hakim Murad and colleagues)**: presented the Qur'an and Sunnah in the frame of sharia and hadith — God's last word preserving the Mosaic root and universalising the message — comparing the legal and ethical orders of the two traditions.
- **The Christian interlocutors**: presented the church's reading of both Testaments — text and doctrine — in the triadic sessions.
- **The exchange**: the 'scriptural reasoning' format: reading the texts passage by passage in the presence of the three, objection living inside interpretation rather than outside the text — not a debate of winner and loser but a shared reading method.
- **The value**: the published session texts are the method's document — a live comparison of three rules of interpretation on the same text. {cite:cambridge-interfaith-programme}
