## Overview
After his stay at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] set out "for Batnat Hujur", because its people had come to Khiwan and pledged to him, with a large army of Hamdan and some of Khawlan; he camped by a pond called "al-Zahriyya" and wrote to them calling them to God, and they wrote asking him to come on. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} He entered the town before the noon prayer on Saturday and its people met him with hearing and obedience. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} The account is Zaydi and the museum gives what it says without a ruling.

## The refused fodder
The townspeople offered him fodder and he disliked it; they urged and pressed him, saying they held the army blameless for what it did, but he refused and accepted neither fodder nor anything else from any of them, stayed Saturday and Monday, and on Tuesday left, putting a governor of the line of Umar ibn Ali over it and telling them to hear and obey. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} This matches his care about the fodder of the people of the lands on pp. 84-85.

## Al-Hisn and Banu Rabi'a
He went on the same day to a town called "al-Hisn", belonging to a Hamdani people called "Banu Rabi'a", who had come to al-Hadi at Sa'da and pledged to him, a group of whom had raided some people of Batnat Hujur, taking their cattle and killing a boy. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} He camped at a pond at al-Hisn and sent for all Banu Rabi'a, saying "the offenders must be given up"; they brought him the offenders and he took them with him, and moved on on Friday. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} The text does not say how he dealt with them.

## Athafit
He went to [[place/athafit|Athafit]], led the Friday prayer there and called the people to pledge, and many did; he stayed Friday, Saturday, Sunday and Monday while people kept coming to him and pledging. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.94} The editor's footnote says Athafit is a village with many vineyards, called "Darna" in the pre-Islamic period. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.93} See [[event/al-hadi-at-athafit-and-the-people-of-bayt-dhud-285]].
