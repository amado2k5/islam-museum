## Taking the Fort of the Kurds and the Fort of Akkar
In Sha'ban 669 AH, Sultan al-Malik al-Zahir Baybars took by the sword the Fort of the Kurds (Hisn al-Akrad), the greatest and strongest of the castles of the Frankish Hospitallers (al-Husbaniyyin). He then besieged the fortress of Acre (Akka) and took it on terms of safe-conduct {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 572}.

The lord of Tripoli submitted to him and offered what he asked, and he made a truce with him for ten years {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 572}.
