## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], when he received Abu Bakr's letter while in al-Hira, left [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] over the region of Kufa, and set out in Rabi al-Akhir 13 AH with eight hundred men, or it is said five hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-155} The event is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]].

## From Iraq to Syria
Khalid took Ayn al-Tamr by force, then fought the people of Sandawda and defeated them. He learned that a group of the Banu Taghlib who had apostatised, led by [[person/rabi-a-ibn-bujayr|Rabi'a ibn Bujayr]], were at al-Madih and al-Musayd; he defeated them and took captives, and sent the captives to Abu Bakr, among them [[person/umm-habib-al-sahba|Umm Habib al-Sahba]]. Then he raided Qaraqir, and at Suwa killed [[person/hurqus-ibn-al-numan|Hurqus ibn al-Nu'man]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-155}

## Crossing the desert
Al-Baladhuri describes how he gave the mounts water, then cut their lips so that they would not chew the cud and grow thirsty. When the water ran out he slaughtered one mount after another, and he and his companions drank from their stomachs. His guide was [[person/rafi-ibn-umayra|Rafi ibn Umayra al-Ta'i]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-155}

## The stages that followed
Then he passed Qarqisiya and Ark, and made peace with the people of Ark. He came to Dumat al-Jandal, Qasm, Tadmur, al-Qaryatayn, Huwwarin and the meadow of Rahit. He sent [[person/busr-ibn-abi-artat|Busr ibn Abi Artat]] and [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] to the Ghuta of Damascus. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-155}

He halted at the pass called the Pass of the Eagle at Damascus, raising a black banner that had been the Prophet's. He then lodged at the Eastern Gate, and the bishop of Damascus brought him lodging and service. He went on until he reached the Muslims at the canal of Busra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.152-155}
