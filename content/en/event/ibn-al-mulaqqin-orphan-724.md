## Summary
Ibn al-Mulaqqin's father died when he was one, entrusting him to Sharaf al-Din 'Isa al-Maghribi — 'al-Mulaqqin', the Qur'an repetition-teacher of the Tulunid mosque — who married his mother, raised him, and gave him the name {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/44} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 dhayl p. 121}.
