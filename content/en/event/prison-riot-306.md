## Overview
In 306 the New Prison's inmates rioted and scaled the wall; Nazad the police-chief rode out, fought them, killed one and flung his head at them — and they subsided; in 307 the mob broke the prisons at al-Mansur's city and those inside escaped, but the city's iron gates were still there — they were lowered, the police hunted down the fugitives, and not one got away {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 439, 446}.
