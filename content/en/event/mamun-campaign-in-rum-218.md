## Overview
Al-Mas'udi relates that [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] raided the land of the Byzantines in 218 AH, having begun building the fortress of al-Tuwana at the mouth of the pass on the side toward Tarsus. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.434-435}

## The Byzantine king's envoy
The Byzantine king sent an envoy with a letter giving him the choice between repaying his expenses, releasing every Muslim captive, or rebuilding what Christianity had destroyed. Al-Ma'mun prayed two rak'as and asked God for guidance, then sent the three terms back and said that there was nothing between him and the king but the sword. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.434-435}

## Conquests and death
He took fifteen fortresses, then camped at the spring of al-Badidun, where he died. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.434-435}
