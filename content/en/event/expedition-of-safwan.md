## Summary
In Rabi' al-Awwal 2 AH (September 623 CE) [[person/kurz-ibn-jabir-al-fihri|Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri]] raided the grazing herds of [[place/medina|Medina]] and plundered the Muslims' livestock; the Prophet went out after him with seventy of his Companions — this expedition is also called the first Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199}

## The pursuit and the banner
The Prophet left his client [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] in charge of Medina; the banner was white, carried by [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}

The Prophet pursued Kurz as far as Wadi [[place/safwan|Safwan]] on the side of [[place/badr|Badr]], but Kurz escaped them and got away; the Prophet and his companions returned to Medina without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/599}
