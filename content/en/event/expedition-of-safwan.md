## Summary
In Rabi' al-Awwal 2 AH (September 623 CE) [[person/kurz-ibn-jabir-al-fihri|Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri]] raided the pasture-herds of [[place/medina|Medina]] and plundered the Muslims' livestock. The Prophet went out in pursuit of him with seventy of his Companions. This expedition is also called the first Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}

## The pursuit and the banner
The Prophet left his freedman [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] in charge of Medina. The banner was white and was carried by [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}

The Prophet pursued Kurz as far as the valley of [[place/safwan|Safwan]] on the side of [[place/badr|Badr]], but Kurz had got away from them. The Prophet and his companions returned to Medina without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/599}


## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.197–200)
In Rabi' al-Awwal of the second year Kurz ibn Jabir raided the pastures of Medina. The Prophet went out with seventy men in pursuit, as far as Safwan on the side of Badr, but did not overtake him. He left Zayd ibn Haritha in charge of Medina, and the banner was with Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.
