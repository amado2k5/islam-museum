## Overview
Ibn al-Imad, in "Shadharat al-Dhahab", reports that in 914 AH a great fire broke out in the city of Aden. About thirty people perished in it, and property and houses were lost beyond counting. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 478}

The date rests on a single report; its conversion to CE is derived. An additional source is needed.
