## Overview
In Rabi' I 309 fires swept Bab al-Sham, Nasr's small market and the shoemakers' quarter of Karkh between the New Bridge and the Harranian arch — many died; and a man of the zanadiqa was killed and thrown into a fire-pit at Bab al-Makhram in which many perished {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 449}.
