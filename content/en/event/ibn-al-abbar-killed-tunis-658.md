## Overview
When Abu Zakariyya' died, his successor al-Mustansir kept Ibn al-Abbar close to him, but his attitude so irritated the sovereign and his courtiers that he was ordered to be tortured; his writings were confiscated and a satire against the amir was found among them, enraging him further — he ordered Ibn al-Abbar killed by lance-thrusts on the morning of 20 Muharram 658 (6 January 1260), and the next day his corpse was burnt together with his books, poems and diplomas {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 705}. Ibn al-Imad records that the lord of Tunis had him killed unjustly on 20 Muharram at the age of sixty-three {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 303}.
