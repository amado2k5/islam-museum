## Overview
Snow fell at Baghdad on Wednesday the eleventh of Rabi' I 398 — a cubit deep in places and a cubit and a half; it stayed a week unmelted, and people shovelled it from their roofs into the streets and lanes, traces remaining some twenty days; it reached Tikrit, and letters came from Wasit of its fall there and across the Batihah, Basra, Kufa, 'Ukbara and Mahruban {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7}.
