## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/waki-ibn-hassan|Waki ibn Hassan]] ruled Khurasan for nine months until [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] came as governor. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Waki's rule
Sulayman wished to appoint him, but he was told that strife raised him up and the community would cast him down, and that there was coarseness and bedouin rudeness in him. The report says that he used to call for a basin and urinate while people looked on. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Yazid's arrival
Sulayman wrote to Yazid ibn al-Muhallab to come to Khurasan, and sent him his commission. His son [[person/mukhallad-ibn-yazid|Mukhallad]] came first, held Waki to account and imprisoned him. He said to him: "Pay God's money." Waki answered: "Was I a treasurer for God?" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Butam
Mukhallad raided [[place/al-buttam|al-Buttam]] and conquered it, then its people broke the covenant after him; Mukhallad left and returned until he entered it, and [[person/jahm-ibn-zahr|Jahm ibn Zahr]] entered it and took money and idols of gold. The people of Butam are attributed to his clientage. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
