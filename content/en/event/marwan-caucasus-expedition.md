## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates the campaign of [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad]], governor of the frontier on behalf of [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]], into the lands of the Khazars and the mountains, and his agreement with their kings on yearly payments. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Entry into Khazar land
Marwan camped at Kasal, then entered the land of the [[group/khazars|Khazars]] by the Gate of the Lan, having been brought in by Asid ibn Zafir al-Sulami with the kings of the mountains. He raided the Saqaliba and took ten thousand members of their households captive. Then he called the great man of the Khazars to Islam or war; he showed Islam, and Marwan made a truce with him to let him keep his kingdom. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The truces
Marwan entered the land of al-Sarir, Tuman, Zarikaran, Hamzin and Sadan, and made peace with their kings for heads of tribute and supplies of food brought every year to the granary at the Gate. He took the fortress of Hamzin after a month's siege. He required the people of Tabarsaran, Shirwan and al-Lakz to pay fixed tribute, and gave the lord of Shirwan and the Tabarsaranshah places at the front and rear of the raid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The end
Marwan marched to al-Dawdaniyya and attacked them, then news came to him of the killing of al-Walid ibn Yazid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The detailed amounts of the tribute and the order of events need an additional source. The date is approximate and derived.
