## Summary
King al-Adil Abu Bakr ibn Ayyub died, having reached a great old age. His son al-Kamil took Egypt, al-Muazzam took Damascus and al-Ashraf took al-Jazira. Most of them obeyed al-Kamil, and the authority of the Ayyubids was gathered in him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.393}.
