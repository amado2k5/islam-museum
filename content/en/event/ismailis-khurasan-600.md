## Summary
A letter was delivered to Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri from the chief of the Ismailis, which moved him to order the killing of a group of them in Khurasan. It is said that this was a trap laid for him, so that the common people would win him over. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 288}
