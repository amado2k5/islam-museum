## Overview
[[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] expanded the Sacred Mosque in 26 AH, as al-Suyuti says, and then the Prophet's Mosque in [[place/medina|Medina]] in 29 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.268-269}

## The Sacred Mosque
He added to it and enlarged it, and bought places for the extension. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.268-269}

## The Prophet's Mosque
He built it with carved stones, made its pillars of stone and its roof of teak, and made its length a hundred and sixty cubits and its width a hundred and fifty. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.268-269}

The recorded date of the event is 26 AH, and the Gregorian conversion is derived.
