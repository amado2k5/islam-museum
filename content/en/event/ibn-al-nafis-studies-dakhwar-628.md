## Summary
Ibn al-Nafis studied medicine under shaykh Muhadhdhib al-Din al-Dakhwar, Damascus's shaykh of medicine, and excelled in the craft and in treatment {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi s.a. 687 p. 312}; al-Dakhwar died in 628/1230, so the apprenticeship predates that year {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 897} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 127-128}.

## The al-Mubdi'un account
Tariq al-Suwaydan relates that a fever mishandled by physicians struck him in 629 AH at the age of twenty-two; it turned him against the doctors and set him on medicine. His father objected, then enrolled him at the Nuri hospital in Damascus, where he studied under Muhadhdhib al-Din Abd al-Rahim and Imran al-Isra'ili {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep17 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep17 00:07:30}.
