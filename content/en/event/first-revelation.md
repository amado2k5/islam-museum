Three years before the commission, solitude was made beloved to the Prophet; he would keep retreat in the [[place/ghar-hira|cave of Hira]] on the Mountain of Light during the month of Ramadan. The first revelation he was opened with was the true dream in sleep: for six months he saw no dream but it came true like the break of dawn — one part in forty-six of prophethood, reckoning its duration at twenty-three years. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

In the Ramadan of the third year of his seclusion the angel came to him and said: "read". He said: "I cannot read." The angel seized him and pressed him until he reached the limit of strain, three times; then God sent down upon him the opening of Sura al-Alaq, through "taught man what he did not know" — as [[person/aisha|Aisha]] related in the Sahih. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

The book inclines to this having been the night of Monday 21 Ramadan, corresponding to 10 August 610 CE, his age then being forty lunar years, six months and twelve days — though historians differ over the month (Rabi' al-Awwal, Ramadan or Rajab) and the day (the 7th, 17th or 18th). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He went back with his heart pounding to [[person/khadija|Khadija]] and said: "cover me, cover me." She reassured him by what she knew in him of keeping kinship ties and aiding the truth, then took him to her cousin [[person/waraqa-ibn-nawfal|Waraqa ibn Nawfal]] — who had become Christian in the Jahiliyya and used to write from the Gospel in Hebrew — who said to him: "this is the Namus that God sent down upon Moses." Waraqa died soon after. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
