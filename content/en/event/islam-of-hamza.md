In the last part of the sixth year of the prophethood, [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]] passed the Prophet at al-Safa, harmed him and reviled him while the Prophet kept silent; a freedwoman of Abdullah ibn Jud'an saw it from her dwelling on al-Safa. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

[[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] came back from hunting girt with his bow — the most honoured young man of Quraysh and the sternest of temper — and the freedwoman told him. Enraged, he went out to Abu Jahl and struck him with the bow, dealing him an ugly head-wound, and said: "you curse my nephew while I am on his religion?" Men of Banu Makhzum stirred and Banu Hashim stirred, and Abu Jahl said: "leave Abu Umara — I did curse his nephew with an ugly curse." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Hamza's conversion began as a proud man's refusal to see his charge insulted; then God opened his breast and he held fast to the firmest handhold, and the Muslims were strengthened by him most mightily. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
