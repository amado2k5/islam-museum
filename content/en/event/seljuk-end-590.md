## Overview
In 590 AH the sultan [[person/tughril-shah-iii|Tughril Shah ibn Arslan]] died, and al-Suyuti names him the last of the Seljuk kings. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693-694}

## Number of kings and length of the state
Al-Suyuti quotes al-Dhahabi that their number was just over twenty kings. The first was Tughrulbeg, who restored al-Qa'im to Baghdad. He estimates the length of their state at a hundred and sixty years. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693-694}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri year, and the report comes from a single source and needs an additional source.
