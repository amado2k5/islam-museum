## Overview
Al-Tirmidhi lost his sight in the last part of his life {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 450}; in later life he became blind, allegedly from excessive weeping {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 579}.
