## Overview
Abu al-Qasim, the son of al-Hadi, reached Sanaa in Jumada I. Then al-Hadi went out with a delegation of the people of Sanaa who had called him to rise, and entered Sanaa on the Wednesday four nights into Jumada II 293 AH, with the Al Ya'fur, the Da'am and the notables of Yemen obeying him.

## Detail
Al-Hadi sent his son Abu al-Qasim, who reached Sanaa in Jumada I. A group of people of Sanaa went out to al-Hadi to call him to rise, and he went out with them and entered Sanaa on the Wednesday four nights into Jumada II 293 AH. With him entered the Al Ya'fur, the Da'am and his son, the two sons of al-Ruwayya, the sons of Ja'far ibn Ibrahim, and the notables of Yemen obeying him. Muhammad ibn al-Husayn and Ibn Kayyala had fought the Qarmatians at the fort of Zahr and entered it, and at Shiban, and had taken what was there. The printed Wednesday for 4 Jumada II 293 AH agrees with the civil-epoch tabular calendar (about 2 April 906 CE, Julian); the astronomical-epoch tabulation gives a Tuesday. The record keeps the printed day.
