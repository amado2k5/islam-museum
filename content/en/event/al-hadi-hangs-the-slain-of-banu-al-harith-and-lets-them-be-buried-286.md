## Overview
Then [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went back to the village at the end of the day and ordered the dead gathered, then ordered them hung in the trees; they were hung head down, a group in every tree, wrapped in rags and loincloths. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.173} He stayed in the village three or four days, then the village stank so badly that no one could eat in it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.173}

## The request to bury
[[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] came to al-Hadi, kissed his head, feet and hands and asked him to give them their brothers' bodies to bury in the wells and pits; he refused them, until they kept on at him and he agreed, reminding them of what he had told them, and the bodies were thrown into ruined wells and pits that lay outside the village. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.173}
