## Overview
[[person/farwa-ibn-musayk|Farwa ibn Musayk al-Muradi]] came to the Prophet, separating himself from and turning away from the kings of Kinda, and he became Muslim. The Prophet appointed him over all of [[group/murad|Murad]], [[group/banu-zubayd|Zubayd]] and [[group/madhhij|Madhhij]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/228} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}

## The report
Before the account, Ibn Ishaq mentions [[event/battle-of-al-radm|the day of al-Radm]] between Murad and Hamdan, and Farwa's poetry about it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/228} When Farwa came to the Prophet he said two verses: when the kings of Kinda turned away, like a man whose sinew has been torn by the pulling of its women, he drew his mount toward Muhammad, hoping for his bounties and the goodness of his hospitality. Ibn Hisham recites from Abu Ubayda the last half-line, "hoping for his bounties and the goodness of his praise." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}

When he reached the Prophet, the Prophet said to him, as it reached Ibn Ishaq: "O Farwa, did it grieve you what befell your people on the day of al-Radm?" He said: "O Messenger of God, who would be struck as my people were struck on the day of al-Radm and not be grieved!" The Prophet said: "As for that, it has only added to your people's good in Islam." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229} He appointed him over Murad, Zubayd and all of Madhhij, and sent with him [[person/khalid-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As]] to collect the alms. He remained with them in their lands until the Prophet died. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}

## Markers of the report
- The Prophet's question to Farwa, "as it reached me", in Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}
- The last half-line of the two verses of Farwa is from Abu Ubayda's narration. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/229}
