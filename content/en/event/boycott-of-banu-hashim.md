When Quraysh saw the conversion of Hamza and Umar, the failure of its bargaining, and the pact of Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib to shield and protect the Prophet, its chiefs gathered in the quarter of Banu Kinana in Wadi al-Muhassab and covenanted to boycott Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib completely: no intermarriage, no trade, no sitting with them, no mixing with them, no entering their houses and no speaking to them — until they handed the Messenger of God over to be killed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

A document was written to that effect and hung inside the Ka'ba — some say Mansur ibn Ikrima wrote it, some say al-Nadr ibn al-Harith — and Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib, believer and unbeliever alike except [[person/abu-lahab|Abu Lahab]], withdrew into Abu Talib's quarter on the night of the new moon of Muharram in the seventh year of the prophethood. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The siege tightened for three years until provisions and supplies were cut off from them: the polytheists would buy up every load of food entering Mecca to keep it from them, until hardship reached them and they ate leaves and hides; the weeping of their children from hunger could be heard beyond the quarter, and nothing reached them except secretly by night — [[person/hisham-ibn-amr|Hisham ibn Amr]] would bring a camel laden with food and loose it into the quarter. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Then Hisham ibn Amr worked to annul the document, going to four of Quraysh's chiefs: [[person/zuhayr-ibn-abi-umayya|Zuhayr ibn Abi Umayya]], [[person/muthim-ibn-adi|al-Mut'im ibn Adi]], [[person/abu-al-bakhtari-ibn-hisham|Abu al-Bakhtari ibn Hisham]] and [[person/zamaa-ibn-al-aswad|Zam'a ibn al-Aswad]]; they covenanted at al-Hujun to annul it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

In the morning Zuhayr circled the House, then called out to Quraysh denouncing the starvation of Banu Hashim; the four men backed him in turn, and al-Mut'im went up to the document to tear it — and found the woodworm had eaten all of it but God's name, after the Prophet had informed his uncle [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]] of that. The document was annulled and the besieged came out of the quarter in Muharram of the tenth year of the prophethood. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
