## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]] raided the [[place/madinat-al-fil|City of the Elephant]] and conquered it. Umayya ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid ibn Usayd had conquered it before him, and then they broke their covenant. [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] tried to enter it and could not. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Kab al-Ashari said of this that the Elephant had given itself into Qutayba's hands, meaning Yazid ibn al-Muhallab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

What is not covered here: the year, which needs an additional source.
