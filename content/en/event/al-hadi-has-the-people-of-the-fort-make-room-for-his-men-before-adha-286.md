## Overview
Afterwards the men and companions with [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] came and complained to him of how much they had ridden and the strain it put on them and their animals; it was the day before Adha. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176} He ordered the people of the fort to make room for them, so they emptied their houses and put their women in hair tents, and made room for those with al-Hadi, and he rested his animals for some days. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176}

## The marginal note
The note in the manuscript says he ordered the houses emptied for the army and their people to go out to hair tents. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176} The book does not name the fort here; the context puts it in the [[place/najran|Najran]] district. Two poems follow: [[event/al-hadis-verses-of-the-hard-ride-and-the-call-to-banu-al-harith-286]].
