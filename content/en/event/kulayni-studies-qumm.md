## Summary
Since the chief teachers of [[person/al-kulayni|Muhammad ibn Ya'qub al-Kulayni]] were scholars of [[place/qumm|Qumm]], the EI2 concludes he must have studied there for a prolonged time, most likely in the last decade of the 3rd century AH (903-913) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 400}.

## Qumm, school of the Imamiyya
Qumm was then the centre of the Imami traditionist school later represented by Ibn Babawayh al-Saduq; it was from this milieu that al-Kulayni emerged before moving to Baghdad {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 400-401}.
