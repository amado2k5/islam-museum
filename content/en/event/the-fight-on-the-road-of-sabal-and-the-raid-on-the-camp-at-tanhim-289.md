## Overview
Then [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] ordered an army out to [[place/sabal-sanaa-yemen|Sabal]]; it went out and stayed there some days, then an army from [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]] came out to them and they fought hard on the road of Sabal, and then, in the narrator's words, God gave them the victory and the men broke back to a place called Tanhim {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239}. When the news reached al-Hadi he sent horse and foot who fell on them in their camp, drove them from it, killed a number, took horses and what spoil there was, and followed them to Bayt Bus; the people of Sabal stayed there and the reinforcement went back to Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239}.

## Detail
The narrator adds that between these encounters men kept going out to the edges of their own and the men's lands, fighting there and plundering, then returning to Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239}. Tanhim is as printed and does not recur in the book. Related: [[event/the-fights-at-dal-and-zahr-and-the-ambush-for-the-horse-of-al-rahba-289]]. The victory is in the narrator's words, not a verdict of the museum.
