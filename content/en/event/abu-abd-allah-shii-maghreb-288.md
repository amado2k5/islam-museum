## Overview
al-Suyuti reports that in 288 AH [[person/abu-abd-allah-al-shii|Abu Abd Allah al-Shi'i]] appeared in the Maghrib and called the tribe of Kutama to the Imam al-Mahdi, and they answered him. According to al-Suyuti, this is counted as the first appearance of the Ubaydiyya, who became the rulers of Egypt. The Mahdi here is linked to [[person/ubayd-allah-al-mahdi|Ubayd Allah al-Mahdi]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 576}

The date rests on a single report; its conversion to CE is derived. An additional source is needed to confirm the year.
