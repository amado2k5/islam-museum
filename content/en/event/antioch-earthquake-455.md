## Summary
In 455 AH there was an earthquake at Antioch, Latakia and part of the lands of Rum (Byzantine territory); and in Dhu al-Hijja of that year there was an earthquake in the land of Wasit that lasted a long time. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.566}

## The scene
In that same year a plague broke out in Mudar, from which about a thousand funerals went out in a single day. It was also the year in which Sultan Tughrilbek died at al-Rayy, and after him his nephew Alp Arslan took over the kingdom. In Muharram 456 Amid al-Mulk al-Kunduri was arrested, and Nizam al-Mulk was given a robe of honour. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.566,574-576}
