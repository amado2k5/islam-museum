## Overview
Shaykha Khokar, appointed governor of Lahore by Mahmud Shah Tughluq, made a timely submission to Timur in 801/1398; but on showing signs of disaffection he was suppressed by an army under the conqueror's grandson Pir Muhammad b. Jahangir {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 38}.
