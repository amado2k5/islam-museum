## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] stayed in [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] until the second Friday, when he went out to the mosque and preached to the people, praising God, admonishing them and telling them what is due to God from them, and speaking at length {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}. He then said: O people, you resent in me only what God relates in His book of the people of Lot when they said, drive out the family of Lot from your town, for they are people who keep themselves pure ([[passage/quran-27-56|Qur'an 27:56]]), but I say to you as my uncle Yusuf said, no blame is on you today, God forgives you ([[passage/quran-12-92|Qur'an 12:92]]) {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}. He went back to his house, and the people were glad of the kindness shown them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}.

## Detail
The verses are not typed here; the museum links only to where they stand. The margin of the manuscript heads the sermon as his sermon in the mosque of Sana'a, which is the copyist's note. The comparisons with the people of Lot and with Yusuf are al-Hadi's words in the sermon as the narrator reports them, and the museum draws no conclusion from them.
