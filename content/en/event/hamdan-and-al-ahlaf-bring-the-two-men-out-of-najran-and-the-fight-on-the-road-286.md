## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad says that when Banu al-Harith fell on the house the news reached Hamdan, and a large army of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], [[group/al-ahlaf-najran|al-Ahlaf]] and Thaqif gathered, came down near the village and sent to Abu Muhammad and Abu al-Husayn asking them to rise with them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} They told them they did not trust Banu Abd al-Madan toward them and that it was established that Banu Abd al-Madan had agreed to Banu al-Harith's demands about the two. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.146} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147}

## Leaving
The two left with them, with their companions and a party of Najran's weaker people from al-Habba. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} When Banu al-Harith heard of their going they intercepted them on the road meaning to seize them; Abu Muhammad stopped until those of the weak who were with him had gone through, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} then charged Banu al-Harith with the Hamdan men who were with him, routed them, went on to the fort and stayed there two days. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147}

## Notes
The book names Thaqif and al-Ahlaf with Hamdan here and does not say where Thaqif lived, so the name is not tied to the museum's Hijazi record of Thaqif. The account follows [[event/banu-al-harith-attack-abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-and-ahmad-ibn-muhammad-at-najran-286]] and leads to [[event/pledge-at-najran-and-the-fight-below-minas-286]].
