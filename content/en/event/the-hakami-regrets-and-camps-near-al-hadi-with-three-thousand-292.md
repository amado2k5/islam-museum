## Overview
"The Hakami regretted what he had done, so he gathered many armies and went out to fight al-Hadi, until he halted near him with three thousand men and more than two hundred horse, while al-Hadi was with eight hundred foot and a hundred horse" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}.

## Detail
A note in the margin of the original, the manuscript the edition follows, reads "the Hakami's breach and his failing of the pledges he had made" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}. The figures are the Zaydi narrator's and the museum sets no other source against them, and it does not judge the note's charge of breach. The page does not give the reasons for the Hakami's regret nor the time between al-Hadi's arrival at Tartar and the Hakami's going out. It follows [[event/al-hadi-at-tartar-returns-what-the-hakami-had-taken-from-the-people-292|the return of what was taken from the villagers]] and precedes [[event/the-fight-in-tihama-and-the-breaking-of-al-hadis-left-wing-292|the fight and the breaking of al-Hadi's left wing]]. The account is in a Zaydi book in the voice of the imam's followers; its descriptions and its judgments on opponents are the narrator's or the poet's, and the museum adopts none of them and gives no verdict.
