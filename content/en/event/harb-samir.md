## Overview
Ibn al-Athir describes it as the first dispute and war to break out among the Helpers, that is among [[group/al-aws|the Aws]] and [[group/al-khazraj|the Khazraj]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488}

## The cause
Ka'b ibn al-Ajlan al-Tha'labi had settled with Malik ibn al-Ajlan al-Salimi and made an alliance with him. A dispute arose in the market of the Banu Qaynuqa' over a horse, and Ka'b boasted, so Samir, a man of the Aws, grew angry and insulted him. Then Samir killed him in the market at Quba. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488}

## The blood-money and arbitration
The Banu Amr ibn Awf offered the blood-money, but Malik would accept only the full blood-money, and among them the blood-money of an ally was half that of a kinsman. They fought, and the clans of the Helpers joined in. Then they made [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-haram|al-Mundhir ibn Haram al-Khazraji]] their arbiter, and he judged the blood-money of a free kinsman and a return to their old custom. They accepted this, but the enmity remained between them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488}
