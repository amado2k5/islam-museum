## Campaign against Cilicia
In Ramadan 673 AH Sultan al-Malik al-Zahir raided the land of Cilicia {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 594}.

He took al-Massisa, Adhana and Ayas, and the army returned with a great number of captives and countless spoils {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 594}.
