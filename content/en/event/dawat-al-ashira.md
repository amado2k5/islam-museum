When "and warn your nearest kin" came down, the Prophet invited Banu Hashim; they came, and with them a party of Banu al-Muttalib — about forty-five men. [[person/abu-lahab|Abu Lahab]] got in first, saying "these are your uncles and cousins — speak, and leave off the sabian talk"; he spoiled the gathering and the Prophet did not speak. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He invited them a second time and informed them that he was God's messenger to them in particular and to all people in general, warning them of death, resurrection and reckoning. [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]] said: "nothing is dearer to us than aiding you and nothing more welcome than your counsel — go on with what you were commanded, for by God I will not cease shielding and protecting you" — though his own soul would not let him part with the religion of Abd al-Muttalib. Abu Lahab said: "this, by God, is the calamity — take him by the hands before someone other than you does"; Abu Talib answered: "by God, we will protect him as long as we remain." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
