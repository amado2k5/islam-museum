## Summary
In 511 AH Baghdad was shaken by an earthquake on the day of 'Arafa, and the walls were seen to move back and forth {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 510}.

## Aftermath
The caliph al-Mustazhir bi-llah died soon after {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 510}.
