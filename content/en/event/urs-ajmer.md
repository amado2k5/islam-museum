## Overview
The annual commemoration of the death of Mu'in al-Din Chishti in Ajmer, India: six days of qawwali, visitation and food attended by hundreds of thousands of Muslims and non-Muslims. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
One of the largest saints' festivals in South Asia; the first popular gate of qawwali on the subcontinent. {cite:wikidata}
