## Summary
After completing his studies at Basra and Baghdad, [[person/al-mawardi|al-Mawardi]] became a teacher; the breadth of his learning drew the attention of the authorities, and he was appointed qadi in several towns — among them Ustuwa near [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]] — before holding the office at Baghdad itself {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 896}.

## Judging and service
Ibn al-Jawzi says of him in his tarjama that he 'held the qada in many lands'; he used to say 'I spread fiqh over four thousand sheets and compressed it into forty', meaning by the former the Hawi and by the latter the Iqna' {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.199}.
