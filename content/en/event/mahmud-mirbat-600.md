## Summary
In 600 AH, Mahmud ibn Muhammad al-Himyari seized the city of Mirbat, Dhofar and the adjoining lands of Hadramawt, having previously served as vizier to its ruler. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 293}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir relates that Mahmud began as the owner of a boat he rented out to sea merchants, then became vizier to the lord of Mirbat; a man of generosity, courage and good conduct, he took over the city when its lord died and the people obeyed him out of love for his generosity. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 293} He adds that in 619 AH Mahmud demolished Mirbat and Dhofar and built a new city on the nearby seashore, channelled a large freshwater spring into it, surrounded it with a wall and moat, fortified it, and named it al-Ahmadiyya; he loved poetry and rewarded poets handsomely. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 293}
