## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] sent his son [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] on a campaign in 163 AH, and he besieged the people of Samalu. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.234}

## Safe-conduct
The people of Samalu, whom the common folk call Samalu, asked for safe-conduct for ten households, among them the Qumis. al-Rashid agreed, on condition that they not be separated. They were settled in Baghdad at the Gate of al-Shammasiyya, and their place was named Samalu. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.234}

In another report that al-Baladhuri gives, they came down under the judgement of al-Mahdi, who spared their lives and gathered them in that place. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.234}

## Those left in the fort
al-Baladhuri says that al-Rashid ordered a call made for those who remained in the fort, and they were sold. An Abyssinian who used to revile al-Rashid and the Muslims was taken and crucified on one of its towers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.234}
