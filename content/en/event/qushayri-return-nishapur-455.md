## Summary
When the Arslanian era dawned in 455/1063 and the vizier Nizam al-Mulk restored the balance between Hanafis and Shafi'is, al-Qushayri returned from Tus to Nishapur, where he lived out ten honoured, obeyed and venerated years until his death; Nizam al-Mulk brought back the exiled imams of Khurasan and honoured them {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 175} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 564} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}.
