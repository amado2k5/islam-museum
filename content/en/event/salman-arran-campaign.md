## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/salman-ibn-rabi-a|Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili]] marched when [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] ordered him toward [[place/arran|Arran]], and took the city of al-Baylaqan by agreement. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.285-287}

## The conquests
He granted its people safety for their lives, property and the walls of their city, and stipulated the poll tax and land tax on them. Then he went to [[place/bardhaa|Bardha'a]] and camped on the river al-Thartur, and its people closed their gates. He fought them for days and raided their villages, and they made peace with him on terms like those of al-Baylaqan, and he entered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.285-287}

He sent his horsemen, who took Shafshin, al-Masfuwan, Awdh, al-Masriyan, al-Harhalîyan and Tabar. He called the Kurds of al-Lasjan to Islam, and they fought him, and he defeated them. Then he crossed the Kur, and the lord of Shaki, the people of Khaytan and the king of Shirwan and others made peace with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.285-287}

The data ties this raid to Salman's death, which is in [[event/death-of-salman-al-khayl|the death of Salman al-Khayl]].
