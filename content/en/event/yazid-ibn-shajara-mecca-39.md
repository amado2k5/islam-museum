## Summary
In the year 39 AH Muʿawiya summoned Yazid ibn Shajara al-Rahawi and ordered him to go to Mecca to lead the people in the Hajj, to take the oath of allegiance there on Muʿawiya's behalf, and to expel the agent of ʿAli from the city. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 726}.
