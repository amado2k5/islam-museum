## Summary
In 434 AH the great earthquake struck Tabriz {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 254}.

## The destruction
Its walls were destroyed. Those who died under the rubble were counted and found to be more than 40,000 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 254}.
