## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan appointed [[person/sinan-ibn-salama|Sinan ibn Salama al-Hudhali]] in the days of Mu'awiya, and he conquered [[place/makran|Makran]] by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
Al-Baladhuri describes Sinan as a virtuous, pious man, and the first to make the troops swear by divorce. He came to the frontier and took Makran by force, built a city there, settled in it and controlled the country. A poet composed a verse about his oath. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
Al-Baladhuri relates from Ibn al-Kalbi that the man who conquered Makran was [[person/hakim-ibn-jabala|Hakim ibn Jabala al-Abdi]]. Then Ziyad appointed [[person/rashid-ibn-amr|Rashid ibn Amr]] over the frontier, who raided al-Qiqan and was victorious, then raided al-Mid and was killed. Sinan took over the command, and Ziyad appointed him over the frontier, and he remained there for two years. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
