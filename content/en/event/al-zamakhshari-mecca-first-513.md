## Overview
On recovering, al-Zamakhshari visited Baghdad and debated with the Hanafi jurist Ahmad ibn Ali al-Damaghani and the grammarian al-Sharif Hibat Allah ibn al-Shajari. He then made the pilgrimage and was welcomed by the amir of Mecca, the Mu'tazili Zaydi man of letters Sharif Ali ibn Hamza ibn Wahhas, staying with him two years as a greatly honoured guest and visiting parts of Arabia and Yemen, before returning to Jurjaniyya, where the Khwarazmshahs Muhammad ibn Anushtakin (d. 521/1127) and his son Atsiz honoured him {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 917-918}.
