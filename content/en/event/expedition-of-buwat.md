## Summary
The Prophet went out in Rabi' al-Awwal 2 AH (September 623 CE) with two hundred of his Companions to intercept a Quraysh caravan in which was [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf al-Jumahi]] with a hundred men of Quraysh and twenty-five hundred camels. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199}

## The march and the banner
The Prophet left [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]] in charge of [[place/medina|Medina]]; the banner was white, carried by [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}

The army reached the mountain of [[place/buwat|Buwat]] on the side of Radwa toward the al-Sham road, found the caravan had already passed them, and the Muslims returned to Medina without an encounter. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/598}
