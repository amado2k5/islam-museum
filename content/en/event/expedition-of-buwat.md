## Summary
The Prophet went out in Rabi' al-Awwal 2 AH (September 623 CE) with two hundred of his Companions to intercept a Quraysh caravan in which was [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf al-Jumahi]], with a hundred men of Quraysh and two thousand five hundred camels {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.

## The march and the banner
The Prophet left [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]] in charge of [[place/medina|Medina]]. The banner was white, carried by [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}.

The army reached the mountain of [[place/buwat|Buwat]], on the side of Radwa facing the road to al-Sham. They found that the caravan had already slipped past them, and the Muslims returned to Medina without meeting the enemy {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/598}.

## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.197-200)
In Rabi' al-Awwal of the second year, the Prophet went out with two hundred of his Companions to intercept a Quraysh caravan in which was Umayya ibn Khalaf. He reached Buwat without meeting any opposition, and he left Sa'd ibn Mu'adh in charge of Medina {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}.
