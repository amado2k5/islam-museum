## Overview
[[person/surad-ibn-abdullah-al-azdi|Surad ibn Abd Allah al-Azdi]] came with a delegation of [[group/al-azd|Azd]] to the Prophet, and he became Muslim and his Islam was good. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/233}

## The account
The Prophet appointed him over those of his people who had become Muslim, and ordered him to fight with those who had become Muslim against the polytheists of the tribes of Yemen who lived near them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/233} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234} He set out, as the Prophet commanded, until he halted at Jarash; see [[event/surad-campaign-against-jurash|the campaign of Surad against Jarash]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/234}
