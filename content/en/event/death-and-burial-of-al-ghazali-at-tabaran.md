## Summary
Abu Hamid al-Ghazali died on Monday, 14 Jumada II 505 AH, aged fifty-five, and was buried in the cemetery of al-Tabaran, a town of the land of Tus, as al-Dhahabi relays from Abd al-Ghafir al-Farisi. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3681} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 38 p. 126} This card treats his death and burial and what surrounds them in the books that could be read; I did not find in them the report attributed to Ibn al-Jawzi, from al-Ghazali's brother Ahmad, about the ablution, the shroud and his last words, so I do not give it here. The general entry on his death is [[event/al-ghazali-death-tus-1111|Death of al-Ghazali at Tus]].

## Context
Al-Dhahabi relays from Ibn al-Najjar that al-Ghazali's father was a craftsman who spun wool and sold it in his shop at Tus; when he was dying he entrusted his two sons, Muhammad and Ahmad, to a righteous Sufi friend, who taught them to write; when what the father left was spent and food became hard to find, the friend advised them to take refuge in the madrasa as though they were students of law, so that they might get their keep. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 38 p. 126} This is among the earliest 'testaments' the books connect with al-Ghazali, and it is his father's.
Al-Dhahabi also says that al-Ghazali had a brother, a preacher and teacher fully accepted in admonition, Abu al-Futuh Ahmad, who taught at the Nizamiyya in Baghdad as his deputy when he gave up teaching and lived until about 520 AH. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 38 p. 126} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3681} See [[person/ahmad-al-ghazali|Ahmad al-Ghazali]].

## The death
Abd al-Ghafir al-Farisi says, as the Siyar quotes him: he died on Monday, 14 Jumada II 505, aged fifty-five. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3681} Ibn Khallikan in Wafayat al-A'yan says he died on Monday, 14 Jumada II 505 at al-Tabaran, that he was born in 450 or, it is said, 451, and that a man of letters, Abu al-Muzaffar, lamented him in verse. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 480}
In the pages I read I found nothing that describes his washing and shroud or who prayed over him, nor a bequest of money or books.

## The grave
Al-Dhahabi says he was buried in the cemetery of al-Tabaran, a town of the land of Tus. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 38 p. 126} Ibn Khallikan says he was buried outside al-Tabaran, a town of Tus. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 480} The record the museum labels [[place/grave-of-al-ghazali|Grave of al-Ghazali]] comes from a draft Wikidata card not yet checked against a page, so I do not tie it here to a text of these books. It lies in [[place/tus|Tus]].

## Where the reports differ
These books agree on the day and year of death and differ on the year of birth: 450 in al-Dhahabi and Ibn Khallikan, with 451 also said. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 38 p. 126} {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 480} Al-Dhahabi notes that his family name, al-Ghazzali or al-Ghazali, refers to a trade in Persian usage. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi vol. 38 p. 126}
The reference list of his entry in the Siyar names Ibn al-Jawzi's al-Muntazam among the sources for the entry. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3681} The volume of al-Muntazam covering this death is not among the scans I read, so the report of the ablution and shroud from his brother Ahmad stays undocumented here and needs reading from the original.
