## Overview
Murad II founded Edirne's Muradiye in 1435, first as a hospice for Mevlevi dervishes, then converted it into a mosque late in his reign {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 68}.

## From dervish house to mosque
The mosque is famed for the tile revetments of its mihrab, among the finest surviving early fifteenth-century Iznik tiles, with a smaller mevlevihane beside it for the dervishes {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 68} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.
