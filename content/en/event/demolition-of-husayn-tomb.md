## Overview
In 236 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] ordered the tomb of al-Husayn and the houses around it demolished, the place to be made into farmland, and people were forbidden to visit it. The site was ploughed and remained a desert. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.539}

## The reactions
al-Suyuti says that al-Mutawakkil was known for hostility to the Prophet's family, and that the Muslims were pained by it. The people of Baghdad wrote curses against him on the walls and the mosques, and poets satirised him. Among the verses transmitted is one saying that if the Umayyads had killed the son of the daughter of their Prophet unjustly, his own paternal kin had brought upon his tomb the same thing, demolished. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.539}
