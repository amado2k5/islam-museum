## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] used to record the property of his governors when he appointed them, and then share with them whatever had increased. He sent [[person/muhammad-ibn-maslama|Muhammad ibn Maslama]] to share out [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]]'s property in Egypt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.307-308}

## The letter and the sharing
Umar wrote to Amr that he had a fortune of goods, slaves, vessels and animals that had not been there when he took over Egypt. Amr replied that their land was land of crops and trade, where they gained more than their expenses. Umar wrote that he had tried enough bad governors, that he had sent Muhammad to share with him, and asked him to show his property and spare him harshness. Amr shared his property with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.307-308}

## The dialogue
Al-Baladhuri relates that Amr complained of the times of "the son of Hantama", meaning Umar, and Muhammad answered him by reminding him of his state before that. Amr asked him not to tell Umar what he had said, and Muhammad promised not to mention anything of what had passed as long as Umar was alive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.307-308}

The date is about the year 20 AH, in Amr's governorship of Egypt; it is derived and needs an additional source.
