## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the people of [[place/qubrus|Cyprus]] helped the Byzantines against the raiders at sea in 32 AH, so [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] raided them in 33 AH with five hundred ships. This came after [[event/first-raid-on-cyprus|the first raid]]. Some transmitters place the raid in 35 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Conquest and confirmation
Mu'awiya took Cyprus by force, killed and took captives, then confirmed them in their peace. He sent to it twelve thousand, all of them on the register, who built mosques there, and a group was moved there from Baalbek, and a city was built there. They were given stipends. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After Mu'awiya
[[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Mu'awiya]] recalled that expedition and ordered the city demolished. When Muhammad ibn Musaffa learned that Yazid had been bribed with a great sum to recall the troops, the people of Cyprus demolished their city and their mosques. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Tribute and peace
[[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] added a thousand dinars to their tribute. [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] lifted it, and then Hisham restored it. When [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] became caliph he said, according to al-Baladhuri: "We are most entitled to be fair to them, and we will not multiply by their oppression," and he returned them to the peace of Mu'awiya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The account also mentions later events, among them the exile of a group of them to Syria by al-Walid ibn Yazid, and Yazid ibn al-Walid's return of them, and the raid of Hayy ibn Mu'ayyif in the caliphate of al-Rashid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
