## Summary
In 675-677/1277-78 the Karamanids of the western Taurus temporarily occupied Konya and installed there a bogus Seljuk known as Jimri. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 pp. 287-288}

## First sign of decline
It was the first episode in the shift of political centres away from Konya as the Mongol protectorate settled over Seljuk Anatolia and the peripheral Turcoman principalities grew at the capital's expense; the city nevertheless kept a cultural and religious vitality attested by the waqfs and buildings of Karamanid princes and dignitaries. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 288}
