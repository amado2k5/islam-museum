## Summary
In the tradition of the cloak, the Prophet wrapped Fatima, Ali, al-Hasan and al-Husayn under a black cloak and recited the verse of purification. Sunni and Shia readers take the word "household" in different ways.

## Encyclopaedia of Islam account
The Encyclopaedia of Islam (2nd ed.) gives the tradition as follows. The Prophet went out one morning, at the time of the visit of the Najran delegation in 10/631, wearing a figured black cloak. Fatima came, then Ali, then al-Hasan and al-Husayn, and he took them under his cloak, embracing them, and recited the verse of purification. Sunnis explain "filthiness" as unbelief, while the Shia explain it as contact with the impure world. In another version the Prophet threw his cloak over his uncle al-'Abbas, saying that he hid them from the fire of hell as he hid them with his cloak. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 286}
