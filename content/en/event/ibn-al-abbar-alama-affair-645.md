## Overview
As chief of the chancellery at Tunis, Ibn al-Abbar was ordered to leave a blank in official documents where the sahib al-'alama filled in the authentication; he did not carry out the instruction, was dismissed and put under arrest in his own house — then pardoned and reinstated in office {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 705}.
