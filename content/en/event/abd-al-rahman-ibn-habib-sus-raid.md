## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that Abd Allah ibn al-Habhab, a client of the Banu Salul, when he was appointed over Ifriqiya, sent [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-al-fihri|Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib al-Fihri]] on a raid to [[place/sus-al-aqsa|al-Sus]] and the land of the Sudan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 324}

## The victory
al-Baladhuri says he gained a victory such as no one had seen. He took two women of that land, whom they called "Tarajan". In a marginal note in one copy, they are said to be of a people called "Barbarwajan". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 324}
