## Overview
The hajib al-Mansur ibn Abi Amir added the great eastern enlargement to the [[building/great-mosque-cordoba|mosque]] about 987, completing the plan the mosque has kept. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}

## What happened
Al-Mansur's was the last and widest of the mosque's great enlargements, stretching east towards the river and completing the ground plan of the Umayyad mosque. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}
