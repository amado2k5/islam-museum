## Overview
After the encounter, Ibn Humayd and his companions entered the village of Maynas and told Banu Rabi'a what had happened. They agreed to stand with him, and told him to camp at the village of al-Mukrab, opposite Najran, saying that Banu al-Harith would gather to him before evening.

## Detail
After the encounter, Ibn Humayd and his companions entered the village of Maynas and told Banu Rabi'a what they had done in the disturbance, and asked them to stand with them. They agreed, and told Ibn Humayd to camp at the village called al-Mukrab, which lies opposite Najran and is the nearest place to it. They said Banu al-Harith would gather to him before evening, and he did as they advised. The news reached Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, who sent to men of Banu Abd al-Madan to tell them what the others had done.
