## The story
Abdullah ibn Umar saw in a dream as though a piece of silk brocade was in his hand; wherever he wished in Paradise it flew to him. He told it to Hafsa, who told the Prophet, who said "Abdullah is a righteous man; would that he prayed at night" {cite:sahih-muslim} [[passage/muslim-2479|Muslim 2479]].

## Its meaning
In this hadith the good dream is glad tidings, and the Prophet interpreted it as a sign of Ibn Umar's goodness, advising him to keep up the night prayer {cite:sahih-muslim}. It is among the texts of the good dream in the Sahih {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Notes
This episode is among the clearest models of the good dream in the Sahih; this browser shows it in the dreams section with the rest of the dream texts {cite:sahih-muslim}.
