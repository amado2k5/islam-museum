## Summary
Al-Hakim began compiling in 337/948-9; his writings came to approach a thousand parts {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 128}.

## The genres
Takhridj of the two Sahihs, 'ilal, biographies, chapters and shaykh-collections; then the large works: Ma'rifat 'ulum al-hadith, the Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn, Tarikh al-Naysaburiyyin, Muzakki al-akhbar, al-Madkhal ila 'ilm al-sahih, al-Iklil and Fada'il al-Shafi'i {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 128}.
