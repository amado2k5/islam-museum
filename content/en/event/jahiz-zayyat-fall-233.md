## Overview
In Baghdad al-Jahiz attached himself to the vizier Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik al-Zayyat; when al-Zayyat fell from favour in 233/847 the fall almost proved fatal to both men {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 385}.

## His patrons after al-Zayyat
After al-Zayyat's fall al-Jahiz attached himself successively to the chief qadi Ahmad ibn Abi Du'ad (d. 240/854) and his son Muhammad (d. 239/853), and finally to al-Fath ibn Khakan (d. 247/861), while retaining ample independence — he was never a courtier {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 385}.
