After the polytheists exhausted weeks and months of mockery, slander and bargaining to no effect, they formed a committee of twenty-five men of Quraysh's chiefs headed by [[person/abu-lahab|Abu Lahab]], which resolved to war on Islam, harm its Messenger and torture those who entered it with every kind of injury and pain. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The worst affliction fell on the powerless: [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal ibn Rabah]], the client of [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf]], was bound with rope and handed to boys who dragged him about Mecca's hills, and rocks were set on his chest in the scorching midday heat while he said "One, One"; and the family of Yasir — [[person/yasir-ibn-amir|Yasir]], [[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya]] and [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar]] — were tortured among Banu Makhzum while the Prophet told them "patience, family of Yasir, your rendezvous is Paradise" — Yasir died under the torture and [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]] speared Sumayya, making her the first martyr in Islam. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

[[person/abu-fakiha|Abu Fukayha]], client of Banu Abd al-Dar, and [[person/khabbab-ibn-al-aratt|Khabbab ibn al-Aratt]] were tortured; Zinnira, al-Nahdiyya and her daughter, Umm Ubayd and the slave-girl of Banu Mu'ammal were handed over — she was a woman whom Umar used to beat while still a polytheist until he tired — and [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] bought these slave-women and freed them, as he freed Bilal and Amir ibn Fuhayra. Mus'ab ibn Umayr's mother starved [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|her son]] and cast him out of her house, and Uthman ibn Affan was wrapped in a mat of palm leaves and smoked from beneath. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
