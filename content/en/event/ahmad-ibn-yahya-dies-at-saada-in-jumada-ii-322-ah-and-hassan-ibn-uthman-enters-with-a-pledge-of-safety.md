## Overview
Ahmad ibn Yahya dies at Sa'da in Jumada II 322 AH, and Hassan ibn Uthman enters the town and grants its people safety.

## Detail
Ahmad returns to Sa'da on Sunday, eight nights into Jumada II 322 AH, stays nine days, and dies on Wednesday forenoon, eighteen nights into the month. The Alawis leave Sa'da and scatter among the Khawlan sub-tribes, who honour them. Hassan ibn Uthman enters Sa'da on Thursday, four nights before the end of Jumada II, grants safety to its people, and does not harm any Alawi or his womenfolk, as the book reports.
