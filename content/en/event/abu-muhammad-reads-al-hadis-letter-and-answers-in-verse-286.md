## Overview
At the foot of p. 152 the book says that when al-Hadi's letter reached [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abu Muhammad Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] he read it to the people and answered it in verse. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.152} The poem, the narrator says, passed between the two men before al-Hadi reached [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], and Ali ibn Muhammad gives it on the word of Abu Jafar ibn Sulayman. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155} The account is Zaydi, told in the voice of the imam's followers, and continues [[event/al-hadi-and-abu-muhammad-exchange-letters-and-verses-from-najran-286]].

## What the poem describes
The poet says he is glad of al-Hadi's news, that sleep has left him for about sixty nights and that he passes them awake and thinking of what surrounds him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.152} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.153} He says his work is to seek God's good pleasure, that he hopes and fears, that obedience to the imam is owed by everyone, and that he must put the quarters right and will not abandon the fight. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.153} He recalls an attack on him at night while his companions slept, through which he kept the enemy off all night, which is the attack told in [[event/banu-al-harith-attack-abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-and-ahmad-ibn-muhammad-at-najran-286]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.154} He threatens his opponents with continuous war and says he asks no reward for what he calls to but affection for kin; the editor notes in a footnote that this line contains the verse [[passage/quran-42-23|42:23]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.154} The poem ends by asking God to pardon all that has passed and to grant reward and right guidance. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155}

## Notes
The editor lists variant readings of the second manuscript in the footnotes. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.153} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.154} The line order of pp. 153-154 is disturbed in the printed text, so only the sense is given here, and the abusive words the poem uses of his opponents are not repeated.
