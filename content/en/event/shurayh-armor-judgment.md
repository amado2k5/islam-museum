## Overview
After [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] returned from Siffin he lost a coat of mail, and found it with a Jew in Kufa. Ali said the mail was his, and that he had neither sold nor given it away. They went to the judge [[person/shurayh-al-qadi|Shurayh]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 306-307}

## In the judge's session
Ali sat beside Shurayh and said that were it not that his opponent was a Jew, he would be seated level with him. Shurayh asked him for proof, and he named Qanbar and al-Hasan as witnesses. Shurayh said that the testimony of a son is not valid for his father, and judged for the Jew. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 306-307}

## After the ruling
Al-Suyuti relates that the Jew said: the commander of the faithful brought me before his judge and he ruled against him. Then he bore witness to the truth, acknowledged that the mail was Ali's mail, and became a Muslim. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 306-307}
