## Overview
Ibn Khaldun relates in "al-Ibar" that in 674 AH the remnants of the Banu Abd al-Mu'min took refuge on the mountain of [[place/tinmal|Tinmal]], the stronghold of the Almohad call. Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Mahalla, agent of the sultan Abu Yusuf over the lands of Marrakesh, stormed it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## What happened
In the battle their caliph, the weak one, was killed, and their vizier Ibn Atush. Those seized in Marrakesh had their necks struck. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun says that the tombs of the Banu Abd al-Mu'min's caliphs were scattered, and the heads of Yusuf and his son Ya'qub al-Mansur were cut off after their bodies had been dug up. He attributes this to Abu Ali al-Miliyani. He says the sultan disapproved of the act, then overlooked it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun says that the line of the Banu Abd al-Mu'min was cut off by this. That is the narrator's judgement, not the museum's; the report comes from a single source and needs an additional source.
