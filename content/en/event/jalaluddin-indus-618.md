## Overview
Ibn al-Imad relates the affairs of Jalal al-Din with the Tatars in 618 AH, among them his meeting with Chinggis Khan at the Indus in Shawwal. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.79}

## The battles
Jalal al-Din gathered his armies and met Tuli Khan, son of Chinggis Khan; Tuli Khan was defeated and a group of Tatars was taken captive. Chinggis Khan then gathered his army and marched toward the Indus, and Jalal al-Din met him in Shawwal, and his army was also defeated. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.79}

## The ambush
Ibn al-Imad says that an ambush of ten thousand came out against the Muslims, crushed the right wing and captured the sultan's son. His order fell apart and he retreated to the edge of the Indus. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.79}
