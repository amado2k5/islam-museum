## Overview
The weight of the fighting at Uhud centred on the banner of the idolaters, which was in the hands of the Banu Abd al-Dar. [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|Al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam]] killed its first bearer, Talha ibn Abi Talha. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.259}

## The succession of bearers
The Banu Abd al-Dar took turns carrying the banner until they were exterminated; those named include Uthman, Abu Sa'd, Artat, Shurayh, Abu Zayd and Ibn Sharahbil. Finally a black slave called Sawab carried it; he fought until both his hands were cut off, then lay upon it with his chest until he was killed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.259}

The source states that ten of them were all exterminated. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.260} This is part of [[event/battle-of-uhud|the Battle of Uhud]] at [[place/uhud|Uhud]].
