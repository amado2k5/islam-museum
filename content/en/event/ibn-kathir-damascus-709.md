## Summary
Ibn Kathir moved with his father to Damascus in 709, as al-Zirikli reports {cite:al-aalam 1/112}; al-Durar al-Kamina records instead that his father died in 703 and that the boy grew up in Damascus {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
## His first teachers
In his Damascus upbringing he heard from Ibn al-Shihna, Ibn al-Zarrad, Ishaq al-Amidi, Ibn 'Asakir, al-Mizzi and Ibn al-Raddi among others, and received ijazas from Egyptian scholars such as al-Dabusi, al-Lawani and al-Khatni {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
