## Overview
After the account of [[event/al-hadi-applies-the-wine-penalty-at-saada-286|al-Hadi applying the wine penalty]], the page {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120} continues with more reports that [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] gives from three narrators: [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]], Muhammad ibn Abi Hisham, and his father [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]]. The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the followers of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Yahya ibn al-Husayn]]; the rulings and numbers are its narrators' report, and the museum neither compares nor judges; the general subject has records at [[concept/khamr]] and [[concept/hudud-penalties]].

## Al-Kufi's report
Al-Kufi said: a man who had drunk wine was brought to Yahya ibn al-Husayn and testimony was given against him; he was frail in body, so three whips were gathered for him and he was struck with all of them together until the full eighty was reached. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120}

## The woman who begged for mercy
Muhammad ibn Abi Hisham told that a woman was taken who had drunk wine, with witnesses testifying to it, and he ordered her flogged the penalty; she said, "Pardon me by the right of Ali ibn Abi Talib", and Yahya ibn al-Husayn answered, "By the right of Ali ibn Abi Talib, were the matter mine I would not strike you, but it is God's"; he then said, "By God, if the penalty fell due on my father I would take it from him." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120}

## Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah's reports
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah said that one day he saw a man brought who had drunk wine, with testimony given, and he was struck; being frail, two whips were gathered for him and he was struck with both together until the eighty were complete. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120} He also heard him one day, speaking of taking what is due and with a group of people about him, say: "By God, if it were my grandfather al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim and the penalty of the sword fell due on him, I would not pray noon prayer until I had struck his neck." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120} The narrator gives it as a statement that what is due is taken from the near as from the far. He saw too two men brought in whose house wine had been found, with a group testifying before Yahya, and he ordered each struck sixty, and they were. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120}

## Notes
The text gives no place or date for these reports. The editor's footnotes mark small manuscript differences (the added words "qad" and "four" taken from the second manuscript) that do not change the sense. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.120}
