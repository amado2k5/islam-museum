## Overview
In Rabi' II 342 word came of a raid by Sayf al-Dawla — plunder, killing, captives, and Constantine the Domesticos taken prisoner; and in 343 word came of a great battle between the Domesticos and Sayf al-Dawla in which many of the Domesticos' men and the chiefs of his patricians were killed {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 664, 666}.
