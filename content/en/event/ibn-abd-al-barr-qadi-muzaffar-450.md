## Judgeship under the Aftasids
Several authorities say Abu 'Amr Ibn 'Abd al-Barr held the judgeship of Ashbula for a time in the state of al-Muzaffar ibn al-Aftas {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 141}; Charles Pellat in the Encyclopaedia of Islam places his qadiship at Lisbon and Santarém under the same ruler {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 706}.
## Bahjat al-Majalis
In those days Ibn 'Abd al-Barr composed Bahjat al-Majalis wa-Uns al-Majalis in verse for al-Muzaffar, a book of adab later abridged by Ibn Luyun {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 706}.
