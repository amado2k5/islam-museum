## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Musa conquered Surraq on terms like those of Ramhurmuz, and then its people broke faith. He sent against them [[person/haritha-ibn-badr|Haritha ibn Badr al-Ghudani]] with a large army, and he did not take it. When [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]] came, he took it by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the conquest
Haritha was afterwards governor of Surraq. al-Baladhuri quotes verses by [[person/abu-al-aswad-al-duali|Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali]] reproaching him for the governorship, and a reply from Haritha declaring his obedience. The place of the event is [[place/surraq|Surraq]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
