## Summary
Ali entered Kufa on Monday, twelve nights into Rajab in the year 36 AH, after escorting A'ishah on her return to Mecca; he declined to stay at the White Palace, stayed instead at al-Rahbah, and prayed two rak'ahs in the Great Mosque {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 155}.

## Reason for Declining the White Palace
Ali was told, "Stay in the White Palace," to which he replied: "No, Umar ibn al-Khattab disliked staying in it, so I dislike it for that reason" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 155}. al-Sallabi cites this stance to illustrate Ali's eagerness to follow the example of the second caliph even in details of lodging, which he considers evidence of Ali's esteem for Umar {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 155}.

## Context of the Entry
The entry into Kufa took place after the Battle of the Camel and escorting A'ishah from Basra, as narrated by the book {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 155}. Immediately afterward in the same section, the book discusses the affection of the Prophet's household for Umar and their naming their children after him, a topic al-Sallabi presents as evidence of the bond between the two sides {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 155}.
