## Overview
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]], under the year 67, writes: in it was the battle of al-Madhar; in it Umar ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib and Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath ibn Qays were killed; and [[person/al-mukhtar-ibn-abi-ubayd|al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd]] was killed when two brothers of Banu Hanifa, Tarif and Tarraf, entered his palace and killed him, then brought his head to [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Mus'ab]], who gave them thirty thousand. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 264} At this point Khalifa gives no more than the battle's name and year.
