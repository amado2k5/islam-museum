## Summary
The Day of Khazaz: a king of Yemen held back some of a delegation from Mudar as hostages and demanded pledges from their chiefs. Kulayb Wa'il gathered Rabi'a, and all of Mudar rallied around him; he was one of three men around whom Mudar rallied. He placed al-Saffah al-Taghlibi at the head of his vanguard and ordered him to light a fire on the mountain of Khazaz at Takhfa; if the enemy reached him, he was to light two fires. Madhhij came by night with the armies of Yemen, and he lit two fires. Kulayb came up with the troops, and they met at Khazaz. Madhhij was defeated and its forces scattered. Amr ibn Kulthum, Kulayb's grandson through his daughter, referred to the day in verse, and he kept his claim to leadership in it despite the pride he took in the day. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 392-393}
