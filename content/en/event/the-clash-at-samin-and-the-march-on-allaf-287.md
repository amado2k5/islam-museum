## Overview
As [[group/banu-hayy-sad-khawlan|the Hayyiyun]] were riding to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] they saw a party of Akilis on the flank of a mountain called Sam'in, fell on them and closed with them in battle; neither al-Hadi nor his companions knew, as he stood waiting for them, until someone cried out that the men were fighting, and al-Hadi and the men with him went back to them to find them fighting, with a number killed among them and many wounded on both sides. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.190} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} Al-Hadi's horse charged them in the valley bottom and thrust at them, night drew near, and al-Hadi withdrew and stayed at Sa'da some days. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191}

## Detail
He then went out to a place called Furwa, which had vines of the Akilis, ordered them cut, and advanced the army to [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]]; when the enemy saw him they climbed the mountain, he halted close to them a while, then went back to [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and stayed some days. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.191} The book does not number or name the dead at Sam'in. The name is as Apple Vision reads it on the page and it has no coordinates.
