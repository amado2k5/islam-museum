## Overview
The Umayyad caliph [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik]] died in late Sha'ban 105 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.399-400}

## Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz's letter
al-Suyuti relates that [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] wrote to Yazid when he was dying: "You are leaving the world to one who will not praise you, and going to one who will not excuse you." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.399-400}

## Dating
The Gregorian year (around 724 CE) is derived from the Hijri year and is not stated in the source. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.400}
