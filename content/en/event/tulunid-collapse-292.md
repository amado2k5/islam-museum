## Overview
Ibn al-Jawzi relates that [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] went out to Egypt, and [[person/harun-ibn-khumarawayh|Harun ibn Khumarawayh]] marched against him. Muhammad entered Fustat in 292 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.342}

## The outcome
He seized the family of Tulun, who were a dozen men, killed and imprisoned them, took over their houses and collected the land tax. This was the end of their affair, and Egypt returned to the Abbasid sultan. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.342}
