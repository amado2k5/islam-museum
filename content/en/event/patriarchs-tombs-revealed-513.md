## Summary
In 513 AH the tombs of Ibrahim, the friend of God, Ishaq and Ya'qub were revealed {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 515}.

## The account
A group of people saw them. Their bodies had not decayed, and in that cave there were lamps of gold and silver. Hamza ibn al-Qalanisi reports this in his history {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 515}.
