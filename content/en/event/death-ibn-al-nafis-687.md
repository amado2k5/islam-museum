## Summary
Ibn al-Nafis died at Cairo on 21 Dhu al-Qa'da 687 (December 1288) aged about eighty, and endowed his house, fortune and books to the Mansuri maristan; none like him came after {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 401-402} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi s.a. 687 p. 312} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 897}.

## On his deathbed
Al-Suwaydan relates that he refused the wine prescribed him as medicine, saying he would not meet God with wine in his body, and died at dawn on Friday {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep17 00:22:30}.
