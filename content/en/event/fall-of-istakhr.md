## Overview
[[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]] went to [[place/istakhr|Istakhr]] in 28 AH, and [[person/mahak|Mahak]] made peace with him on behalf of its people. Then they broke the covenant, and he returned to them and took it by force after hard fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Peace, then breach
Mahak made peace on behalf of the people of Istakhr, and Ibn Amir set out for Jur. When he had left, its people killed his governor over them. When he took Jur, he came back to them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
Ibn Amir's vanguard was [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-matar|Ubayd Allah ibn Ma'tar al-Taymi]]. The people of Istakhr met him at Barajird and fought him and killed him, and he was buried in the orchard of Barajird. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Ibn Amir hurried, and set Abu Barza al-Aslami on his right wing and Ma'qil ibn Yasar on his left. Over the horse was Imran ibn al-Husayn, and over the foot Khalid ibn al-Mu'ammar. He routed them until he drove them into Istakhr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He set up catapults against it. The report says that he killed forty thousand of the non-Arabs, and it is also said about a hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
The sources also relate that the people of Istakhr broke faith during the governorship of Abd Allah ibn Abbas over Iraq for Ali, and it was taken again. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
