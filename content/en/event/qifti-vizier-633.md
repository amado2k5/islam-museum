## Summary
Al-Malik al-Aziz Muhammad appointed al-Qifti vizier in 633/1236 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}; al-Aziz died in Rabi' I 634, leaving his child al-Nasir Yusuf as heir {cite:wafayat-ayan}.
