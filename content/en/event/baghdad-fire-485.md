## Summary
In Jumada al-Akhira 485 AH a great fire broke out in Baghdad, and many people died in it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 376}.

## Among its victims
Among those who burned in this fire was the reciter Abu Abd Allah al-Baniyasi, Malik ibn Ahmad ibn Ali ibn al-Farra al-Baghdadi, aged eighty-seven. He was the last to transmit from Abu al-Hasan ibn al-Salt al-Mujbir {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 376}.
