Muslim relates from Anas ibn Malik that Gabriel came to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] while he was a boy playing with the boys in the [[place/bilad-banu-sad|land of Banu Sa'd]], took him, split his chest, and drew from his heart a clot saying "this is Satan's share of you", then washed it in a basin of gold with Zamzam water and returned him to his people. {cite:sahih-muslim}

In Ibn Ishaq's account at [[person/halima-al-sadiyya|Halima al-Sa'diyya]]'s, two angels came, bore him away, opened his belly, drew out the heart, split it and removed a black clot; when she returned him to her lap she resolved to give him back to his mother out of fear for him, and called out "Muhammad, go back to your mother". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

It is an event among the portents, recounted among the reports of the unseen in the sira literature; the book presents it in the mode of report, not verification. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
