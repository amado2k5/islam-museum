## Overview
After Bayezid I's defeat at Ankara in 804/1402, Timur seized Nicaea (İznik) and Bursa among other towns and fortresses. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 69-70}

## End of the capital era
The seizure ended the period in which Bursa had been the main capital of the Ottoman state, since its conquest in 726/1326. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1397}
