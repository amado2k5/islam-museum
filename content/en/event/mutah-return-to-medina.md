## Overview
When the army of [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'tah]] drew near Medina, the Prophet and the Muslims went out to receive it, and the children came running. He asked for the son of Ja'far, and Abd Allah was brought, and he carried him before him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## The Prophet's words
Some people were throwing dust on the army and shouting "O runners". The Prophet replied: "They are not runners, but they are those who return to the attack, God willing." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

[[person/salama-ibn-hisham|Salama ibn Hisham]] stayed in his house, fearing disgrace. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
