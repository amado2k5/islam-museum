## Overview
In 50 AH al-Tabari mentions the death of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba and the appointment of Ziyad over Kufa. He mentions the flight of al-Farazdaq from Ziyad. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.630}
