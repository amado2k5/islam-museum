## Summary
In 247 it was said: Muhammad ibn Isma'il is the most learned man to enter Nishapur {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 7}; his stays there were celebrated — when he arrived, four thousand men on horses received him, not counting those on mules and donkeys or on foot {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 10}, and Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj came to him, kissed him between the eyes and said: let me kiss your feet, master of masters, chief of the traditionists and physician of hadith in its defects {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 9}.
