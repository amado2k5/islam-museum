## Overview
On the night of Wednesday 16 Safar 373 a tailed star appeared from Aquarius in the west toward Capricorn, climbing to the ecliptic; the people of Baghdad would whistle at it saying "look at it" — and it remained past the rising of dawn {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.131}.
