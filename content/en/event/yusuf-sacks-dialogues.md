## The exchange — what each side said
- **Sacks**: Jewish monotheism is common ground — divine law and the covenanted community — calling for an ethics of monotheism against secularism; the Mosaic covenant stands for its people.
- **Yusuf**: Islamic monotheism shares that root — God's word is one since Abraham, the difference lying in abrogation and extension, not in origin; and the Prophet Muhammad is a messenger to every nation.
- **Sacks**: put the question of plurality — how monotheism preserves religious diversity — answering with an ethics of 'dignity in difference'.
- **Yusuf**: answered from the Islamic tradition on the People of the Book and coexistence — difference being part of the test.
- **The verdict**: documented conversations, not a debate — the scholars' exchange on monotheism, secularism and coexistence; its value is showing the shared monotheistic ground of the two traditions. {cite:youtube-sunni-jewish-debate
