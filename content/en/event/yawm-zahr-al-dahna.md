## Summary
The Day of Zahr al-Dahna was fought between Judayla of Tayy and Banu Asad. Its cause was a satire by Bishr ibn Abi Khazim against Aws ibn Haritha al-Ta'i and his mother Sa'da, after his own people envied him for a robe that al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir had put on him. Al-Hutay'a refused to satirise Aws and said: how can I satirise a man whom I see no wealth in my house except from him? Aws seized Bishr's camels and went after him. Banu Asad protected him, and Aws defeated them at Zahr al-Dahna with a terrible slaughter. Bishr could find no one to protect him until he was handed over to Aws. His mother advised him to seek pardon and return the property, for only praise could wipe away his satire. Aws forgave him and gave him a hundred camels, and Bishr swore that he would not praise anyone else until he died, and he composed his ode. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 471-472}
