## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] moved from [[place/karawi-banu-rabia-yemen|Karawi]] to a place of Banu Rabi'a called [[place/al-darb-banu-rabia-yemen|al-Darb]], which the book calls a strong and mighty place, and stayed there days until his horses were rested and he had put his affairs in order for the war. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} The account is Zaydi and in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum gives it as the narrators tell it, without a verdict.

## The call in Hashid
He then sent criers into Hashid for a set day, and the people gathered on Thursday, the thirteenth of Shawwal. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} His son Abu al-Qasim arrived with a group of Khawlan of Banu Sa'd whom he had met on the way, and they all went to al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} Abu al-Qasim is most likely [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|al-Murtada Muhammad]].

## Why Abu al-Qasim left Khiwan
A narrator who was with Abu al-Qasim ("we were with him") says that Abu al-Qasim had been at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]; when al-Hadi fought al-Da'am "treachery, deceit and the pursuit of corruption" showed among the people, in the narrator's words, and Abu al-Qasim gathered the people of Khiwan, spoke to them at length and asked whether any among them had help or a stand for God and the truth; none then had the desire. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} Elders who did have desire and love gathered to him, among them [[person/ismail-ibn-al-muslim-al-hamdani|Ismail ibn al-Muslim]], Yusuf ibn Mu'adh, Ibrahim and Ghutayf; they advised him and asked him to leave Khiwan because of the people's malice and wrongdoing; seeing how little desire there was he left and they left with him for a place of Banu Ma'mar, reaching it on the night of the Fitr, and he stayed some days. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} He then set out to al-Hadi with a group of Khawlan who had come from [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and a group of Hamdan, and met al-Hadi on the road; on the way they were met by some people, whose place the book names in a reading not certain enough to give. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101}

## The march on Athafit
The forces joined al-Hadi, who marched with them on Thursday until evening at a pond of Banu Sarim, "of the notables of Hamdan and its men"; he spent the night of Friday at the pond and the morning of Friday there, the people watered their animals and tightened their saddles, and he set out for [[place/athafit|Athafit]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} See [[event/second-battle-of-athafit-al-kitd-and-the-ambush-285]].

## The date
Thursday 13 Shawwal 285 AH is Thursday 2 November 898 in the Julian calendar by the tabular calendar, derived, and the weekday agrees with the book.
