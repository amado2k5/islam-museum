## Overview
In al-Baladhuri's account, [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] wrote in 21 AH to [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]], informing him of the hardship of the people of Medina, and ordering him to carry the food of the land tax by sea to Medina. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.303}

## The transport
The food was carried with oil. When it reached [[place/al-jar|al-Jar]], Sa'd al-Jar took charge of receiving it; then it was placed in a house in Medina and divided among the people by measure. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.303}

## After Umar
The transport was cut off in the first strife, then carried in the days of Mu'awiya and Yazid, then ceased until the time of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan. It continued to be carried until the caliphate of Abu Ja'far and what followed it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.303}

## The poll tax
The people of Egypt who paid the poll tax were made a peace in place of the wheat, oil, honey and vinegar at two dinars each, so that each man paid four dinars; the book says that they accepted this. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.303}
