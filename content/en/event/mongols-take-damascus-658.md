## Summary
News of the taking of Aleppo reached [[place/damascus|Damascus]] on Saturday, and [[person/al-nasir-yusuf|al-Malik al-Nasir]] fled. Hulagu's envoys entered the city that day, and the decree granting safety to Damascus was read out. The keys of [[place/hama|Hama]] were handed to Hulagu, and its ruler and al-Nasir went toward [[place/gaza|Gaza]]. The citadel of Damascus resisted, and the Tatars besieged it and kept up the bombardment with twenty catapults against the Tarama Tower until it cracked. Its people asked for safety and were granted it, and the deputy [[person/kitbugha|Kitbugha]] took up residence there. They also took [[place/baalbek|Baalbek]] and its citadel, and they took [[place/nablus|Nablus]] and its districts by the sword. Then they captured al-Malik al-Nasir and took him under safe conduct, and he remained in Hulagu's service for some months. Then Hulagu crossed the Euphrates on his return and left a Tatar contingent in Syria. The Christians of Damascus rose up, raised their heads, lifted the cross and paraded with it, and forced people to stand for it from their shops on the twenty-second of Ramadan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.298-299}.
