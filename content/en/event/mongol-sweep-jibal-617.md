## Overview
In 617 AH the Mongols reached [[place/rayy|al-Rayy]] in pursuit of the Khwarazm Shah, and took it and plundered it while its people were off their guard. Then they went on to [[place/hamadan|Hamadhan]], Zanjan and Qazvin, then Azerbaijan and the lands of the Georgians. Ibn al-Athir relates this in "al-Kamil". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 409-410}

## From al-Rayy to Qazvin
They plundered every town and village on their way. The head of Hamadhan went out with money, clothes and animals asking for safe conduct; they granted it and then left them. They went to Zanjan, then to Qazvin, whose people held out and fought them, and they took it by force. Ibn al-Athir counts more than forty thousand killed from the people of Qazvin. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 409-410}

## Azerbaijan and the Georgians
When winter came they moved to Azerbaijan and reached Tabriz, and its lord Uzbek ibn al-Bahlawan made peace with them with money, clothes and animals. Ibn al-Athir says that he was busy with heavy drinking. Then they turned to the lands of the Georgians, where about ten thousand of them fought them and were routed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 409-410}

A Turkish slave of Uzbek's slaves, named Aqush, joined the Mongols, gathering Turkmen and Kurds. He marched at their head to Georgia. The Georgians gathered near [[place/tiflis|Tiflis]], and were routed in Dhu al-Qa'da. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 409-410}

## Ibn al-Athir's remark
Ibn al-Athir says that he has no doubt that whoever comes after them, when time has passed, will find this event hard to believe and will deny it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 409-410}

## Date
617 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around 1220 CE) that is derived. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 409}
