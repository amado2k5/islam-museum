## Summary
The last pilgrimage [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] led for the people was in the year 23 AH, during which he led the Mothers of the Believers and their guardians before whom they did not veil, and he appointed [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] as his deputy over [[place/medina|Medina]] {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.146}.

## Deputizing Him During Campaigns
The book notes that Ali was Umar's foremost advisor and that Umar appointed him as his deputy over Medina several times, including when he encamped at al-Jabiya after the Roman commander Artabun wrote to him that the conqueror was a man whose name had three letters; Amr ibn al-As realized it was Umar, so he summoned the people, and Umar appointed Ali as his deputy {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.146}.

## Notes for Researchers
- The book mentions the year but does not mention the month of the pilgrimage.
- The details of Umar's appointment of a deputy at al-Jabiya are transmitted from al-Muntazam by Ibn al-Jawzi, as the book indicates.
