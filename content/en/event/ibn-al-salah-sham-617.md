## Summary
Ibn al-Salah entered al-Sham in 617/1220 or earlier, hearing from al-Muwaffaq, the shaykh of the Hanbalis Ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi, from Zayn al-Umana' and from al-Muwaffaq's brother the mufti Fakhr al-Din. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186}

## Sham hearings
He had already come to Damascus before, hearing from the qadi Jamal al-Din ibn al-Harastani; at Aleppo he heard Abu Muhammad ibn al-Ustadh, and at Harran the hafiz 'Abd al-Qadir. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186}
