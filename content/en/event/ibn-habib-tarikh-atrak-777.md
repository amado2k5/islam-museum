## Summary
Ibn Habib brought his Tarikh Dawlat al-Atrak — the Durrat al-Aslak — down to the year 777, and his son Abu al-'Izz Tahir added a continuation {cite:nujum-zahira 11/189}; al-Zirikli gives its coverage as 648 to 778 {cite:al-aalam 2/218-219}.
