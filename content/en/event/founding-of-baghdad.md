## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that Baghdad was an old place, and that [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] made it a garrison city and built in it a city he began in 145 AH, and named it the City of Peace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414}

## The building
When news reached him of the rising of Muhammad and Ibrahim, the sons of Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan, he returned to Kufa, then moved the treasuries, the storehouses and the registers from Kufa to Baghdad in 146 AH. He completed the building of the wall of his city and the old wall of Baghdad in 147 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414}

He bought the land of the city from the owners of villages in Badurya, Qatarbal, the Buq River and the Bayn River, and granted it to his family, his commanders, his troops and his scribes. He made the market-place at al-Karkh, built two mosques and the new bridge over the Sarat. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414}

## Al-Rusafa
He built al-Rusafa for al-Mahdi on the eastern side, which was called the camp of al-Mahdi. Al-Mahdi lodged in it in 151 AH when he returned from Rayy. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.414}

## Al-Suyuti's account
According to al-Suyuti, al-Mansur began building Baghdad in the year 140 AH and finished it in the year 149 AH, which differs from what was said before. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.424-425}
