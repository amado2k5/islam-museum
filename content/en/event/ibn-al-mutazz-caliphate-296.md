## Overview
In Rabi al-Awwal 296 AH [[person/al-muqtadir-billah|al-Muqtadir]] was deposed and [[person/ibn-al-mutazz|Ibn al-Mu'tazz]] was given the oath of the caliphate for a single day. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 585-586}

## Al-Suyuti's account
Al-Suyuti relates that the deposers rode out on the twentieth of Rabi al-Awwal while al-Muqtadir was playing polo; he fled and the gates were shut. The vizier al-Abbas ibn al-Hasan and a group were killed. The commanders, judges and notables gave the oath to Ibn al-Mu'tazz, titled al-Ghalib bi-llah, and he appointed Muhammad ibn Dawud ibn al-Jarrah as vizier. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 585-586}

Then God cast terror into their hearts, as al-Suyuti puts it, and they dispersed without fighting. Ibn al-Mu'tazz fled, was imprisoned, and was then brought out dead. Those jurists and amirs who had deposed him were killed, except four. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 585-586}

## Ibn al-Athir's account
According to Ibn al-Athir, Ibn al-Mu'tazz was titled al-Murtada bi-llah, not al-Ghalib. He took charge of killing the vizier al-Husayn ibn Hamdan, Badr al-Mu'tadidi and Wasif on the way to his garden. Then al-Husayn rode to al-Shaṭiba to kill al-Muqtadir but did not find him. Ibn al-Mu'tazz's men fled when Munis al-Khadim and those with him came by water. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 11-13}
