## Summary
[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and [[person/fatima|Fatima]] complained of the hardship of grinding grain until Fatima's hands blistered, and asked for a servant from among the captives, but the Prophet refused to give them one and taught them glorification of God at every prayer and upon sleeping {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 82} {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 83}.

## The Request
Ali narrates that Fatima ground grain until her hands blistered, and that he said he drew water until his chest ached, so they went to the Prophet, who said to them, "By God, I will not give to you and leave the people of al-Suffa..." and then said he had nothing to spend on them and that he would sell the captives and spend their proceeds on them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 82} {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 83}.

## The Glorification
The Prophet said to them, "Shall I not tell you of something better than what you asked of me?" Then he taught them to say Subhan Allah ten times, Al-hamdu lillah ten times, and Allahu akbar ten times after every prayer, and to say Subhan Allah thirty-three times, Al-hamdu lillah thirty-three times, and Allahu akbar thirty-four times upon going to sleep {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 83}. The book attributes this to what Gabriel taught him, and cites al-Bukhari and Muslim {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 83}.

## Priorities
Al-Sallabi considers that the Prophet managed the economic crisis in Medina by setting priorities, so he met the hunger of the people of al-Suffa out of necessity and gave them precedence over Ali and Fatima's need for a servant {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 83}. He cites as evidence that Ali preserved this counsel, to the point that he said he never neglected it since he was taught it, not even on the night of Siffin {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 83}.
