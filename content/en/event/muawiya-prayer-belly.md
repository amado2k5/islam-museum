## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] wrote for [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] in the year of the conquest. One day the Prophet invited him while he was eating, and he was slow to come, so the Prophet said: "God not satisfy his belly." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The effect of the prayer
Mu'awiya used to say: the prayer of the Messenger of God has overtaken me. According to this account he used to eat seven meals in a day, sometimes more and sometimes less. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
