## Overview
Al-Maqqari in "Nafh al-Tib" says that [[person/sulayman-al-mustain-andalusi|Sulayman al-Musta'in]] and the Berbers with him entered [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] by force in 403 AH after a siege. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}

## What happened
Al-Maqqari describes the siege as having destroyed the villages and open country around Cordoba, and that all the services were lost. Hisham al-Mu'ayyad was killed in secret, and the houses of Cordoba suffered what al-Maqqari considers a disgrace to their women and children. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}

Then the Berbers and the slaves took control of the administration, and held large cities, among them Badis ibn Habus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464-465}

The date rests on a single account and needs an additional source.
