## Overview
Al-Tirmidhi apprenticed himself to al-Bukhari, who trained him and from whom he took the principles of hadith criticism and fiqh until he stood out among his peers and distinguished himself in learning; al-Bukhari himself testified to this when he told him: 'I benefited from you more than you benefited from me' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 451}.

## Al-Bukhari's successor in Khurasan
Al-Tirmidhi was counted al-Bukhari's successor in Khurasan for knowledge, memory and scrupulosity {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 451}, numbering among al-Bukhari's pupils {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 579}.
