## Summary
On the day of [[event/conquest-of-mecca|the Conquest of Mecca]], two men from Banu Makhzum took refuge in the house of [[person/umm-hani-bint-abi-talib|Umm Hani' bint Abi Talib]]; [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] entered intending to kill them, so she locked the door on them and went to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.108}.

## The Incident
Umm Hani' found the Prophet in upper Mecca performing ablution while his daughter Fatima screened him with his garment; after he washed and prayed eight cycles of the forenoon prayer, he said to her, "Welcome, Umm Hani', what brings you?" and she told him what had happened {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.108}. The book cites the Prophet's words, "We have granted protection to whomever you have granted protection," in a form containing a defect in the scanned text, so its exact wording is not reproduced here {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.108}.

## What the Book Affirms
The book affirms that a Muslim's grant of safe-conduct to a non-Muslim from a hostile people places him under protection, so he may not be harmed, and that the conditions for valid safe-conduct among jurists are that it be free of suspicion or that the matter be referred to the ruler to decide {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.108}.

## Notes for Researchers
- The book places the incident at the Conquest without mentioning a specific day; the date is derived.
- The printed text of the Prophet's statement is garbled in the scanned copy, so the original should be consulted before quoting the phrase.
