## Overview
The narrator says that when Ibn Abi Abbad was killed the people fled in all directions to [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]], to [[place/al-bawn-yemen|al-Bawn]] and to [[place/zahr-yemen|Zahr]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. When the men whom [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-muhammad-al-hasani|Ubaydallah ibn Muhammad al-Hasani]] had sent to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] as reinforcement from Zahr drew near it, some said to others that Ibn Abi Abbad was dead and the clans had turned, and asked whether they should not alarm the man at Zahr, who might then owe them a favour with Al Ya'fur; they agreed on it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. Nearing Zahr they shouted for arms, shot at the gate of Zahr and raised alarm with the news of Ibn Abi Abbad's killing; Ubaydallah ibn Muhammad fled to Sana'a and left Zahr empty, and the men entered and freed [[person/abu-al-ghasham-ibn-tarif|Abu al-Ghasham ibn Tarif]] and the men of [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] with him, and stayed at Zahr {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. When al-Hadi heard, he wrote to [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]]: beware, and beware again, of leaving Sana'a, for had the man at Zahr been a man he would have thrown heads to them when they shouted for arms, and this would not have happened {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. Some of the troops who were at Shibam went back to Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}.

## Detail
The page gives no day, so the date is given to the month, derived. The narrator's reading of the reinforcements' intent, that they hoped for a favour with Al Ya'fur, is his reading and is not checked by the museum. Al-Hadi's remark on the governor of Zahr is an opinion in the narrator's report, attributed to the speaker, with no verdict. The man freed is written Abu al-Ghasham ibn Tarif here and Abu al-Ghasham on p. 216, read as one man. It follows [[event/abu-al-atahiya-and-ibn-al-daam-rout-the-qudumis-and-al-hadi-buries-ibn-abi-abbad-288]].
