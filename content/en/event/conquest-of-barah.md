## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[place/barah|Barah]] lies in a land of the Maghrib known as the Great Land, about fifteen days' march from Barqa. Jabala, client of al-Aghlab, raided it and could not take it. It was then conquered by [[person/khalafun-al-barbari|Khalafun al-Barbari]] at the start of the caliphate of [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}

## Details
It was a city on the seashore, and its people were Christians, but not Byzantines. It is said that Khalafun was a client of Rabi'a. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.328}
