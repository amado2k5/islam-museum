## Overview
The report describes the settling of the Asawira in [[place/basra|Basra]] after the siege of Tustar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Alliance and settlement
The [[group/asawira|Asawira]] took part with Abu Musa in the siege of Tustar. Then in Basra they asked which of the quarters was closest in lineage to the Messenger of God, and they were told Banu Tamim. They allied themselves with them and left the Azd. Plots were laid out for them, they settled and dug a canal known as the Canal of the Asawira. [[person/siyah-al-uswari|Siyah al-Uswari]] remained of the view that they should settle with Banu Tamim. The Sayaiija and the Zutt joined them. The Asawira settled among Banu Sa'd, and the Zutt and the Sayaiija among Banu Hanzala. Then they went out with Ibn Amir to Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
