## Overview
On the night of Friday [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent out an army to [[place/zabwa-sanaa-yemen|Zabwa]], where the men had a camp; it fell on them, killed some of them there and took property {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236}. When Friday morning came, the seventeenth of Safar, he went out to meet his army, as the horse and foot of [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]] had confronted those of his army who had entered Zabwa; they fought hard and [[person/abu-al-qasim-al-jafari|Abu al-Qasim al-Ja'fari]], of the line of Ja'far ibn Abi Talib, was killed {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236}.

## Detail
Al-Hadi then advanced with his companions and drove the men back until they took refuge in their fort, and the rest of his army went on to [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] with the spoil from Zabwa while he halted with those who stayed behind {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236}. The rest of the day is in [[event/al-hadi-is-struck-down-in-the-narrow-pass-and-abu-al-qasim-defends-him-289]]. The book names Abu al-Qasim al-Jafari only by descent from Ja'far ibn Abi Talib and does not give his father's name; the narrator asks mercy for him, and the museum only reports that.
