## Overview
The first session whose headship al-Dhahabi assumed was a reading circle in the Great Umayyad Mosque of Damascus, in the first gallery of Zakariyya, in place of the blind reciter Shams al-Din al-Marraqi, in Muharram 700, shortly after his return from his Egyptian journey {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/338}.
