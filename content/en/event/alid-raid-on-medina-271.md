## Overview
On the first of Safar 271 AH, Muhammad and Ali, sons of al-Hasan ibn Ja'far al-Alawi, entered Medina, killed a group of its people, and demanded money from the people. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.101} The commander named in the museum's data is [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-hasan-al-alawi|Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi]].

## Effect
Ibn al-Jawzi says that the people of Medina did not pray in the Mosque of the Messenger of God for four gatherings: neither Friday prayer nor congregational prayer. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.101}

The Gregorian conversion is derived, and the report rests on a single source. It needs an additional source.
