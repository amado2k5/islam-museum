## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates, on the authority of Abu Mikhnaf, that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] wrote to [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]], his governor over Kufa, two months after the battle of Nihawand. He ordered him to send [[person/urwa-ibn-zayd-al-khayl|Urwa ibn Zayd al-Khayl al-Ta'i]] to [[place/rayy|al-Rayy]] and Dastaba with eight thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.443}

## The fighting
The Daylam gathered for him, and the people of al-Rayy reinforced them and fought him. God gave him the upper hand over them and he killed them. He then left his brother [[person/hanzala-ibn-zayd|Hanzala ibn Zayd]] in charge and came to Ammar, asking him to send him to Umar to bring him the good news. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.443}

When Umar saw him he said: "Surely to God we belong and to Him we return." Urwa said: "Rather, praise God, for He has given us victory." Umar named him "the bringer of good news". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.443}
