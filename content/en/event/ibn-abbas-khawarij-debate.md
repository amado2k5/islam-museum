## Summary
The most famous intra-Muslim debate of the first generation: [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] walks into the Khawarij camp at Harura, argues them down with the Qur'an and the Prophet's precedent, and two thousand of them return. {cite:musnad-ahmad 1/342-343} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya 7/283-284}

## The sending
The Khawarij had seceded from Ali after the arbitration at [[place/siffin|Siffin]] and settled at Harura. Ali spoke to them and they would not return, so he told Ibn Abbas to go. Ibn Abbas said: I came to them in the finest dress the people of the House wore — a red turban. They said: welcome, Ibn Abbas, what is this turban? I said: what divides you and me is good; I come from the cousin and son-in-law of the Messenger of God, on whom revelation descended, and they know its interpretation better than you — I put my case to you and you argue back. Some said: do not argue with Quraysh, for God says 'they are a quarrelsome people'; but they let him speak {cite:musnad-ahmad 1/342-343}.

## The three objections and the answers
He asked: what do you hold against the cousin of the Messenger of God? Three things, they said. First, he let men arbitrate in God's religion though God says 'judgement belongs to God alone.' He answered: God made men judge in the case of 'whoever kills game while consecrated shall pay the like of what he killed, judged by two just men among you' — a judgement by men over a rabbit or game; is the judgement of men over the blood of Muslims and the mending of relations not worthier than a judgement over a rabbit? And for the wife estranged from her husband, 'if you fear a breach between them, send an arbitrator from his family and an arbitrator from hers.' Have I brought you out of this or not? They said: no {cite:musnad-ahmad 1/343}.

Second: he fought without taking captives or spoils; if those he fought were God's friends they were wrongly fought, and if enemies, captivity and plunder were lawful. He answered: whom do you fight? Your mother Aisha, or others? Would you make your mother's captivity lawful as the captivity of foreigners? If you say she is your mother then she is, as God said, of the Mothers of the Believers; if you say she is not, you have disbelieved in her. Have I brought you out of this or not? No {cite:musnad-ahmad 1/343}.

Third: he erased from himself the title Commander of the Faithful — if not the believers' commander he is the unbelievers' commander. He answered: of this I have what will please you. You heard that on the day of al-Hudaybiyya Ali wrote 'this is what Muhammad the Messenger of God agreed,' and the polytheists said: had we known you were God's Messenger we would not have kept you from the House; so the Messenger of God said 'erase it, Ali — God, you know I am Your Messenger — erase it and write son of Abdallah.' By God, the Messenger of God was better than Ali, and he erased his own name and did not erase himself from prophethood. Have I brought you out of this or not? No {cite:musnad-ahmad 1/343} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya 7/283}.

## The return
Two thousand of them returned and entered Ali's ranks; six thousand held out, rose against the Uthmaniyya, and the affair of al-Nahrawan took its course {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya 7/284}. The debate thus stands as the model of arguing with the Qur'an itself against those who raised its slogan.
