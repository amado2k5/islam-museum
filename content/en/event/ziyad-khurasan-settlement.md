## Overview
Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan appointed over Basra in 45 AH divided the districts of Khurasan among his agents. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The division
He appointed [[person/umair-ibn-al-ahmar|Umayr ibn al-Ahmar]] over Merv, [[person/khulayd-ibn-abdallah-al-hanafi|Khulayd ibn Abd Allah al-Hanafi]] over Abarshahr, and [[person/qays-ibn-al-haytham|Qays ibn al-Haytham]] over Marw al-Rudh, Talaqan and Fariyab. Nafi' ibn Khalid al-Tahi of the Azd was appointed over Herat, Badhghis, Bushanj and Qadis. Al-Baladhuri says that an amir was the first to settle the Arabs in Merv. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
