## Summary
In 624 AH Jalal al-Din marched into the Ismaili lands, fought them and took vengeance for their killing of his amir, to whom he had granted Ganja as iqta'. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 472}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Ismailis had killed a great amir of Jalal al-Din's whom he had granted the city of Ganja and its districts; the amir was excellent, abundant in good and well-conducted, and used to denounce to Jalal al-Din the plundering and other evil his troops did; his killing weighed heavily on Jalal al-Din and enraged him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 472} So he marched with his armies into the Ismaili lands from the borders of Mut to Kurdakuh in Khurasan, fought them all, killed their people, plundered wealth, enslaved women and children and slaughtered the men, wreaking terrible deeds on them and taking vengeance; their harm and mischief had grown and they had become emboldened ever since the Tatars came into the Islamic lands, so he checked their violence and crushed them, as Ibn al-Athir narrates. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 473}
