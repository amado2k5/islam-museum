## Overview
After the people were driven back to their fortress {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.144} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went to the fortress and, seeing him with his army gathered, they shouted to him and asked for safe-conduct; he went back to his camp that evening and spent the night there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.144}

## The surrender
Next morning he went to them again and they all came out and asked for safe-conduct, which he gave; they heard him and obeyed and went with him to the houses of the "troublemakers". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.144}

## The demolition and the condition
He ordered the houses pulled down and they were; he was firm with them over the matter and told them that if they came back to their country he would call them to account for it, and they undertook that and he turned back to [[place/athafit|Athafit]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.144} The account is Zaydi, told by the imam's followers; it follows [[event/al-hadi-and-the-wadi-fight-against-the-muhdithin-286]] and leads to [[event/al-hadi-at-hawth-and-al-ghubayb-and-the-return-to-khiwan-on-1-dhu-al-qada-286]].
