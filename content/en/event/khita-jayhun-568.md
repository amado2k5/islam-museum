## Summary
The Khita (Qara Khitai) crossed the Jayhun, and war broke out between them and Il Arslan Khwarazmshah. It was the first extension of their power into Transoxiana after the end of the Seljuks {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 37}.
