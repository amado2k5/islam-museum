## Overview
Israeli forces entered Jerusalem on 5 June 1967; the book treats this as an occupation following the June war, and counts the subsequent annexation and changes to the city's landmarks and institutions — the demolition of quarters, mosques and churches — as aggression upon the status quo {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 52, 107-108}.
