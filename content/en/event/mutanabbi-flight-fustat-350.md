## Overview
On the feast of sacrifice of 350 — 20 January 962 — after writing a satire on Kafur, al-Mutanabbi left Fustat secretly and, crossing Arabia after great trials, reached Iraq, where he spent some time in Kufa before settling in Baghdad {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 770}.
