## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] appointed [[person/bugha-al-kabir|Bugha al-Kabir]] over Armenia, and that he set that frontier right and came to Samarra in 241 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.297}

## The campaign
When Bugha came to Badhlis, he seized Musa ibn Zurara, who had been among those who desired the killing of Yusuf and helped against him. He fought the Khuwaythiyya, killing a great slaughter among them and taking many captives. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.297}

He then besieged Ashut ibn Hamza ibn Jajaq, a patrician of Basfarrajan, who was in Baqi, and brought him down from his fortress and carried him to Samarra. He marched to [[place/jurzan|Jurzan]], defeated Ishaq ibn Isma'il and killed him in cold blood, and took Jurzan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.297}

He brought out those who were in [[place/arran|Arran]], and the dissidents and rebels of [[place/armenia|Armenia]] from among the Christians and others, until the frontier was set right in a way that had not been seen before, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.297}
