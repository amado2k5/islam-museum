## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/mughallis-al-abdi|Mughallis al-Abdi]] went to [[person/mansur-ibn-jawhur|Mansur ibn Jawhur al-Kalbi]] in Sind; Mansur met him, killed him and defeated his army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Musa ibn Kab
Abu Muslim appointed [[person/musa-ibn-kab-al-tamimi|Musa ibn Kab al-Tamimi]] and sent him to Sind. He met Mansur, defeated him and his army, and killed his brother Manzur. Mansur fled until he reached the desert, where he died of thirst. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Musa then took charge of Sind, repaired [[place/al-mansura|al-Mansura]] and enlarged its mosque, and campaigned and conquered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
