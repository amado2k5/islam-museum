## Overview
In 81 AH people gathered to Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath to depose al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, and he was the first to declare it. One of them said that if al-Hajjaj was deposed, then Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, who had appointed him, was deposed too. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.338} The heading of the report in the index of the sixth volume is "The dissent of Ibn al-Ash'ath against al-Hajjaj". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.628}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Hajjaj appointed Ibn al-Ash'ath over Sistan, Bust and al-Rukhkh, and he fought the Turks there and their neighbours among the kings of India, such as Rutbil. Then he threw off al-Hajjaj's obedience. Abd al-Malik wrote to him in harsh words denying this. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 122-123}

## The army of the peacocks
A book included among the sources says that the army of the peacocks was prepared in 80 AH at the direction of al-Hajjaj, and reached about forty thousand: twenty thousand from Kufa and as many from Basra. The expenditure on it reached two million dirhams apart from the stipends, and Ibn al-Ash'ath was chosen as its commander. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.88}

## The reciters who rose with Ibn al-Ash'ath
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] gives a "roll of the reciters who rose with Ibn al-Ash'ath". From Basra: [[person/muslim-ibn-yasar|Muslim ibn Yasar]]; Uqba ibn Abd al-Ghafir and Uqba ibn Wassaj, both killed in battle; al-Nadr ibn Anas ibn Malik; Imran ibn Isam al-Dub'i, executed after capture; and Malik ibn Dinar. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 286} [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|Al-Hasan ibn Abi al-Hasan]] was brought out against his will and was not killed. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 287} From Kufa: [[person/said-ibn-jubayr|Sa'id ibn Jubayr]], [[person/amir-al-shabi|Amir al-Sha'bi]], Abd Allah ibn Shaddad ibn al-Had and [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-abi-layla|Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla]], both lost on the night of Dujayl, and Abu al-Bakhtari al-Ta'i, killed in battle. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 287}

## Maskin
He reports from Abu al-Hasan, from Awana, that al-Hajjaj killed five thousand prisoners at Maskin, or four thousand. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 287}
