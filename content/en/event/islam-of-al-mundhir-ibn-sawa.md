## Overview
Before the conquest of Mecca the Prophet sent [[person/al-ala-ibn-al-hadrami|al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami]] to [[person/al-mundhir-ibn-sawa|al-Mundhir ibn Sawa al-Abdi]], lord of [[place/bahrayn|Bahrain]]. He became a Muslim and his Islam was good. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222}

## The report
Ibn Ishaq relates the account at the end of the [[event/delegation-of-abd-al-qays|delegation of Abd al-Qays]]: the Prophet had sent al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami to al-Mundhir ibn Sawa before the conquest of Mecca; he became a Muslim and his Islam was good. He died after the Messenger of God, before the apostasy of the people of Bahrain, while al-Ala was with him as the Prophet's governor over Bahrain. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222} Later sources place this dispatch among [[event/letters-to-rulers-and-kings|the letters to the rulers and kings]] at the end of the sixth year and the beginning of the seventh. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.358}

## Notes on the report
- Ibn Ishaq gives the year of the dispatch only as "before the conquest of Mecca". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/222}
