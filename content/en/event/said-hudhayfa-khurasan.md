## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that in the days of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik, [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] appointed his in-law [[person/said-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz]] over Khurasan. Sa'id was nicknamed "Hudhayfa" because one of the landowners saw him dyed with safflower and his hair combed, and said so. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The war with the Turks
Sa'id sent [[person/sawra-ibn-al-hurr|Sawra ibn al-Hurr]] beyond the river. He camped at [[place/ishtikhan|Ishtikhan]], fought the Turks and defeated them. Then he met the Turks a second time and they defeated him and caused much killing among his companions. Sa'id appointed [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] in his place. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri says that some of the notables of Khurasan complained of Sa'id to Maslama, who removed him, and he quotes a poem a poet wrote satirising him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The text then gives a report about al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah and the cutting of silver and gold under his carpet; its connection with the event is unclear in the data and needs an additional source.
