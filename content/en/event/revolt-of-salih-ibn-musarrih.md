## Overview
In 76 AH al-Tabari mentions the rising of Salih ibn Musarrih al-Tamimi. He describes him as an ascetic, humble and devout man, who had followers in Dara and the land of Mosul and the Jazira, to whom he taught the Qur'an. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.216}
