## Overview
On 4 Sha'ban 294 AH al-Hadi marched out with his army, razed the village of Lubayban, and ordered the palms of Banu Amr of the Ya'am and of a man of the Sinan cut, as the narrator reports.

## Detail
On Sunday, 4 Sha'ban, al-Hadi left with all his troops for the open ground outside the town. He placed Wada'a on the right wing, Shakir and Thaqif on the left, and marched in the centre with the emigrants of Tabaristan and others, with his son Ahmad ibn Yahya and cousins; he left Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah at al-Hijr. At the village of Lubayban he camped, ordered it demolished and burned, and ordered the palms of a group of Ya'am called Banu Amr cut, because they had killed Salim ibn al-Misri. He then ordered the palms of a man of the Sinan cut and his house razed, and seized a slave of his, a sinful man, together with his master, whom the book says made wine and gathered Bedouins to disorder. The punishments and the charges are the narrator's account and are not endorsed.
