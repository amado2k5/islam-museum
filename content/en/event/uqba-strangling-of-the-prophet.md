## Overview
[[person/uqba-ibn-abi-muayt|Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt]] strangled the Prophet with his garment while he was praying in the Hijr of the Ka'ba. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] pushed him away and said: "Will you kill a man because he says: my Lord is God?" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.100} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/544}

## The report
The book relates from Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-As that the Quraysh gathered in the Hijr and spoke of the Prophet. When he had circled them three times and they had taunted him with words, he said: "By Him in whose hand my soul is, I have come to you with the slaughter." Their words took hold of the people; then they rushed at him, and one of them seized the collar of his cloak, while Abu Bakr wept and said: "Will you kill a man because he says: my Lord is God?" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.100} In the report of al-Bukhari from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, Uqba put his garment around the Prophet's neck and choked him violently, until Abu Bakr seized his shoulders and pushed him away. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.100} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/544}

## Notes for researchers
- The reports say that this act was the most severe that Quraysh inflicted on the Prophet, as Ibn Amr ibn al-As himself says. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.100}
