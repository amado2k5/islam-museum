## Summary
Abu al-Husayn Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj ibn Muslim al-Qushayri al-Naysaburi, author of the Jami' al-Sahih and one of the imams of memorisation among the traditionists, was born at Nishapur in Khurasan after the year 200: one report gives 202/817, and Ibn al-Salah is thought to have said 204 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 690-691}. Ibn Khallikan favours 206/821, since Muslim died in 261/875 aged fifty-five lunar years {cite:wafayat-ayan v p. 195}.
