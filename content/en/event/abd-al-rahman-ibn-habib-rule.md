## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib al-Fihri took control of the frontier of [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]] when al-Walid ibn Yazid rose. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 325}

## The account
[[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-habib-al-fihri|Abd al-Rahman ibn Habib]] broke with al-Walid. He cherished that frontier because of what his grandfather Uqba ibn Nafi had left there. [[person/hanzala-ibn-safwan-al-kalbi|Hanzala]] withdrew from it, and Abd al-Rahman stayed. When Yazid ibn al-Walid became caliph, he did not send a governor to the Maghrib. When Marwan ibn Muhammad rose, Abd al-Rahman wrote to him, professed obedience and sent him gifts, and Marwan confirmed him over the frontier. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 325}

## After him
He was succeeded by Ilyas ibn Habib, then Habib ibn Abd al-Rahman, and then the Berbers and the Ibadi Kharijites prevailed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 325}
