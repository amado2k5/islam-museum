## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] came to [[place/daybul|Daybul]] on a Friday, dug a trench, set up a catapult called al-Arusa, and then took it by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege
- Ships came to him carrying men, weapons and tools.
- The letters of al-Hajjaj came back to him, and his letters came back to al-Hajjaj with news of the same state, every three days.
- In Daybul there was a great idol with a tall mast and a red banner. al-Hajjaj ordered him to set up the Arusa and throw at the mast, and it was broken. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
Muhammad advanced against them when they came out to him, and routed them. Then ladders were set up and men climbed, and the first of them was a man of Murad from the people of Kufa. Muhammad remained killing those inside it for three days, and the agent of [[person/dahir|Dahir]] fled from it. He laid out the Muslims' quarters in it, built a mosque and settled four thousand there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the conquest
al-Baladhuri relates on the authority of Mansur ibn Hatim al-Nahwi that he saw the mast broken. And that [[person/anbasa-ibn-ishaq-al-dabbi|Anbasa ibn Ishaq al-Dabbi]], governor of Sind in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim, demolished the top of the minaret and made a prison in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Note
The report gives no date for the conquest, and it needs an additional source.
