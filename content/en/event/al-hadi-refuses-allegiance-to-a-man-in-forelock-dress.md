## Overview
The narrator says he saw [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] one day while people were pledging allegiance to him; a man with a forelock and long hair came and said, "I want to pledge to you on the Messenger of God". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.53} Al-Hadi told him to go and shave that hair and come back, because "we do not take allegiance from people of this fashion"; the man went, shaved and returned, and pledged. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.53} The text does not give the reason for the condition or name the man, and the footnotes show only small manuscript variants. It is a report from a Zaydi book attributed to the imam. See [[concept/bayah]].
