## Overview
The Russian occupation of Edirne in 1878-9 brought further devastation to the city {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## The year the tiles were looted
During the occupation the Russians stripped the Yıldırım mosque of its tiles and the two linked marble rings that had earned it the name Küpeli Cami, and took the tiles of the Selimiye's royal gallery {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.
