## Summary
In Sha'ban 2 AH (February 624 CE), sixteen or seventeen months after the Hijra to [[place/medina|Medina]], the divine command came down to change the qibla of prayer from Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem) to the Ka'ba in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.203}.

## The Prophet's expectation and the revelation
The Prophet loved to be turned toward the qibla of Abraham. He kept turning his face toward the horizon of the sky, awaiting revelation, until the verse on the change of the qibla came down: [[passage/quran-2-144|البقرة 144]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.203} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/65}.

## Religious and social effect
The change of the qibla was a decisive test. It distinguished sincere believers from the hesitant, and it exposed the hypocrites among those who doubted. It declared the independence of the Islamic community and its doctrinal and spiritual distinctiveness, and it announced a new phase in which the Muslims looked forward to cleansing the Sacred Mosque of idols {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.203}.

## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.202-203)
In Sha'ban of the second year, the qibla was changed from Bayt al-Maqdis to the Sacred Mosque. This exposed the weak Muslims and the Jewish hypocrites who had entered their ranks to stir up confusion, and the ranks of the Muslims were cleansed of people of treachery and betrayal {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.203}.

## Sha'rawi on the change of qibla
Sha'rawi holds that the change was a ruling fixed in God's knowledge for a set term, not a response to circumstances, and that the Ka'ba became the qibla because God chose it as His house, not because the place has sanctity in itself. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.509}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.510} He treats the change as an example of abrogation by an equal ruling, since the effort of facing either direction is the same, and the test lies in the command coming from God. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.513} He also says the night journey to Jerusalem was to establish its sanctity in Islam, which is why prayer was first faced there. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.511}

## Sha'rawi on the change of the qibla
Sha'rawi holds that the change of the qibla was the first abrogation in the Qur'an, that the verses foretold the objections of the foolish before they came, that God's commands are not tied to a cause, that the Ka'ba was sacred before the idols the idolaters placed in it, and that the faith of those who died facing Jerusalem is not lost. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.623} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.624} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.625} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.629}
