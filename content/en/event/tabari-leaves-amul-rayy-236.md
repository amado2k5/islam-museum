## Overview
[[person/al-tabari|Al-Tabari]] left his father's house at Amul in search of knowledge aged twelve, about 236/850, and settled at Rayy, the metropolis of northern Persia, where he studied for some five years; his most important teacher there was Abd Allah ibn Humayd al-Razi, through whom he received Ibn Ishaq's Maghazi material transmitted by Salama ibn al-Fadl {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 21}.

## An early start
Al-Tabari says of himself that he had memorised the Qur'an aged seven, led the prayer aged eight and studied hadith aged nine; his father, a moderately prosperous landowner, sent him his allowance with the annual pilgrimage caravan from Khurasan {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 21}.
