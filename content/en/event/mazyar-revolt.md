## Overview
Al-Ma'mun appointed [[person/mazyar-ibn-qarin|Mazyar ibn Qarin]] over the affairs of Tabaristan, and al-Mu'tasim confirmed him. He then rebelled more than six years into al-Mu'tasim's caliphate, and was taken to Samarra in 225 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The war
Al-Mu'tasim wrote to [[person/abd-allah-ibn-tahir|Abd Allah ibn Tahir]] to fight him, and he sent his paternal uncle [[person/al-hasan-ibn-al-husayn-al-tahiri|al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn]]. Mazyar's brother [[person/quhyar-ibn-qarin|Quhyar]] was in correspondence with al-Hasan and Muhammad ibn Ibrahim. By a stratagem of an ambush, Mazyar was taken without a pledge. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The end
He was taken to Samarra and beaten with whips before al-Mu'tasim until he died, and he was crucified together with Babak al-Khurrami. Some of his brother Quhyar's close men were killed in Tabaristan. [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]] was conquered and taken over by Abd Allah ibn Tahir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Ma'mun raised him, and he rebelled in the days of al-Mu'tasim, and al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn captured him and brought him to Samarra. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 450-451}
