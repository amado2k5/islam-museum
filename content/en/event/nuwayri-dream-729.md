## Summary
Al-Nuwayri seems to have remained resident at the Nasiriyya madrasa to the end: he mentions a dream he had in one of its iwans, called al-Iwan al-Bahri, on the eve of Friday 13 Dhu al-Qa'da 729/8 September 1329; exactly when he left administration for full-time composition is unknown {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.
