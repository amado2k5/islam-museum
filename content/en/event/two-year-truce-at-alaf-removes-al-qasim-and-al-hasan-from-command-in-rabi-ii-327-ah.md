## Overview
Ibn al-Dahhak reaches Alaf on Saturday in mid Rabi II 327 AH. Banu Sa'd and others agree a two-year truce on condition that al-Hasan and al-Qasim are removed from command, and each returns to his home.

## Detail
The book says Ibn al-Dahhak left Rayda on Saturday in mid Rabi II 327 AH, reached Alaf, met the Akil, and wrote to all of Banu Sa'd, asking them to make peace and give the command to al-Hasan ibn Ahmad. They disliked this, and then they agreed a two-year truce on condition that al-Hasan and al-Qasim were removed from command. Al-Hasan went to his home at al-Ghayl and al-Qasim went to the clan of Bani Hayy and stayed with them. No fighting took place between them at this time.
