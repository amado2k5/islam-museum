## Overview
Al-Hadi had a slave taken out of prison, gave his sword to al-Huraini to kill him, and the body was crucified at the gate of al-Darb. According to the book the town grew calm and was in awe of the order.

## Detail
The slave, called Ibn Bilal and a client of Abu Quhayna, was taken out of prison. Al-Hadi called al-Huraini, gave him his sword, and ordered him to strike the man's neck; this was done. The body was then crucified at the gate of al-Darb. The book says the town became calm and its people were in awe. The narrator gives the act and the book gives the reason as the imam's order; this record does not add a judgement.
