## Overview
Historical books relate that [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] seized a heretic (zindiq) and ordered his neck struck. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.467}

## The dialogue
The heretic asked him why he was being killed, and al-Rashid answered that he would relieve the servants of God from him. The heretic said that he had forged a thousand hadiths on the Messenger of God, not one letter of which he had uttered. Al-Rashid replied that Abu Ishaq al-Fazari and Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak sift them, bringing them out letter by letter. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.467}

The report is linked to the subject of [[concept/ittisal-al-isnad|the continuity of the chain]] and the protection of hadith from forgery. This is al-Suyuti's report, and the authenticity of its chain needs an additional source.
