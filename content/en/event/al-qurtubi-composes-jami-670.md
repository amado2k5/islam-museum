## Overview
Al-Qurtubi assembled his Qur'an commentary in fifteen volumes, explained the Most Beautiful Names of God in two (al-Asna), and wrote al-Tadhkira on the matters of the hereafter, a commentary on al-Taqassi and other useful works {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari 2/210-211}. Al-Dhahabi said caravans carry his great commentary {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari 2/211}. The tafsir stands out for its wealth of hadiths, its attention to deriving rulings and clarifying the Law's meaning, and its sparing use of isra'iliyyat {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 551}.
