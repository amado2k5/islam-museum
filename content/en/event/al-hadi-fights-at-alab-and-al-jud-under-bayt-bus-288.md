## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] went out at once, on Saturday with six days of Sha'ban gone, until he came to a place called [[place/alab-sanaa-yemen|Alab]] and met the men there, and war broke out between them without his having drawn up his army {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. The narrator says God gave al-Hadi the victory; he routed them and killed a number of their commanders and horsemen, and they turned in flight, so he pursued them to [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]], to a place called al-Jud beneath Bayt Bus, where the fight closed and was hard for an hour {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. He then drew up his army to go back to [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]]; on the road the men all followed him, and he turned on them with a few of his army, killed a number and they withdrew routed, and he came back safe to Sana'a with his armies {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. The men afterwards sent an army to [[place/nuqm-sanaa-yemen|Nuqm]], where they came down and camped {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}.

## Detail
The Saturday the book names does not fall on 6 Sha'ban in the tabular calendar, so the museum dates it to the month only. The narrator's words of triumph are his own and the museum draws no conclusion from them. It follows [[event/abu-al-atahiya-fights-at-ayman-and-warraqatayn-and-withdraws-to-nuqm-288]] and leads to [[event/battle-of-nuqm-shaban-288]].
