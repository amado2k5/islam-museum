## Summary
In 488 AH the imam al-Ghazali came to Damascus as an ascetic, wrote his book Ihya Ulum al-Din there, and taught it in Damascus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 384}.

## His stay
Al-Ghazali stayed in Damascus for two years in his asceticism and seclusion. He then performed the pilgrimage and returned to his homeland {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 384}.
