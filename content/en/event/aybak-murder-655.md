## Overview
In 655 AH al-Mu'izz Aybak, sultan of Egypt, died, and the sources report that his wife Shajar al-Durr killed him. After him they made his son al-Mansur Ali sultan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 710}

## Ibn al-Imad's account
Ibn al-Imad reports that news of [[person/izz-al-din-aybak|al-Mu'izz]]'s marriage to the daughter of the lord of Mosul spread, and this weighed heavily on [[person/shajar-al-durr|Shajar al-Durr]]. She agreed with a group of servants and promised them money. When he entered the bath, they threw him to the ground and strangled him in the night. In the morning the emirs found him dead. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 275}
