## Overview
In Shawwal 799 (July 1397) Ibn Hajar left for the Hijaz and Yemen on a study journey that ran into 801/1398, residing near the Haram at Mecca; the following year he studied in Palestine and Syria, hearing shaykhs at Gaza, Jerusalem, Damascus and Aleppo {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 809} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 271}. His student travels ended with his return from Syria in 803/1400, though he later went on pilgrimage again, revisited Yemen in 806/1403 and toured Syria with Sultan Barsbay's entourage in 836-7/1432-3 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 809}.
