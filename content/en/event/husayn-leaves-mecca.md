## Overview
Al-Husayn wrote to the people of Kufa that he was setting out toward them from Mecca on Tuesday, eight days into Dhu al-Hijja, the day of Tarwiya, in 60 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.395}

## The letter
In his letter he told them to make haste when his messenger came to them. The text of the letter is on the same page of al-Tabari's history. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.395}
