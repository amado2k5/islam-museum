## Summary
In the reign of the sultan Jalal al-Dawla Malikshah the Seljuq, the vizier Nizam al-Mulk gathered the astrologers {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 328}.

## The start of the year
They made Nawruz the first point of the year. That became the basis of the Jalali calendar, named after Jalal al-Dawla Malikshah, which was one of the most accurate calendars known to scholars {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 328}.
