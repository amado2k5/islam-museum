## Overview
[[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] gathered and went on until they spent the night beside [[place/najran|Najran]]; [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] spent it among [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan|Banu Rabi'a]] and Shakir in the upper part of Najran, while the camps of Banu al-Harith were in the lower part; when dawn broke they went to the village. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.168} Al-Hadi was in it with the people of Najran of [[group/yam-tribe|Yam]], [[group/al-ahlaf-najran|al-Ahlaf]] and [[group/wadia-tribe-yemen|Wadi'a]], the people of the fort, about two hundred men of al-Mahra (so read, as in earlier batches) and a clan of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] living in Tihama, nineteen of [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] with shields and spears, a few of his servants and some of Khawlan; Khawlan had asked leave and gone back, and the armies had scattered once al-Hadi pardoned Banu al-Harith. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.168} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.169} When Banu al-Harith reached the edge of the village their companions from within met them in the half-light of dawn. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.169}

## The entry and the prayer
They broke a side of the fort for them and went in until they stood beside the door of al-Hadi's house as he was praying; he did not move until he had finished, then put on some of his arms and hurried without others and went out, and found the men of Najran from Hamdan who had been with him gathered at the lane gate; he shouted to them, went after them and strove to bring them back, but none came, and they said they had been let in on them and on him and told him to draw off with them to the open ground and fight those who followed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.169} He said God forbid he should leave the village or empty it for them; having given up on them he went back with his companions to face the men and fought hard, pressing on, and the Tabaris mixed with them in the street until they were locked together, and men were killed beside the mosque. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.169} The next record is [[event/al-hadi-routs-banu-al-harith-and-the-forty-horsemen-286]]. The counts and charges here are the narrator's.
