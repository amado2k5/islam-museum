## Overview
In 421 the emperor of Rum marched on Aleppo with a host the account puts at three hundred thousand; he was routed near 'Azaz by the Aleppines and the frontier Muslims, fled disguised by night until he saved himself, and his camp and wealth were taken — Ibn al-Jawzi's telling, numbers and all {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.392-393}.
