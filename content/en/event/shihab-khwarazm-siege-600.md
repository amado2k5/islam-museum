## Summary
Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri turned back from his march toward India and besieged Khwarazm, pressing its people hard. The Khwarazm-shah sought help from the Khitai. When they arrived, Shihab al-Din fled to Khurasan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 285}
