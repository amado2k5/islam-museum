## Overview
In 329 the Euphrates rose eleven cubits and burst out by Anbar, sweeping villages under — people, beasts and wild animals drowned; the water poured through al-Sarat toward Baghdad into the western quarter's streets until Bab al-Anbar street was submerged and not a house of it left standing. Buildings collapsed onto al-Sarat, the iron bridge of al-Sarat fell, part of the Daylij canal broke, and the Tigris rose eighteen cubits in May and June {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 608}.
