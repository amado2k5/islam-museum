In Shawwal of the tenth year of the prophethood, about a month after [[person/khadija|Khadija]]'s death, the Prophet married the lady [[person/sawda-bint-zama|Sawda bint Zam'a al-Amiriyya]] at Mecca — the first woman he married after Khadija's death. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Sawda had been among the emigrants to Abyssinia on the second hijra with her husband al-Sakran ibn Amr ibn Abd Shams; when the two returned to Mecca her husband died. The Prophet contracted marriage with her and paid her a dower of four hundred dirhams, and she took charge of his household and daughters after their mother's death. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
