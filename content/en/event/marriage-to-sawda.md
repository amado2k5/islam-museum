In Shawwal of the tenth year of the prophethood, about a month after the death of [[person/khadija|Khadija]], the Prophet married Sawda bint Zam'a al-Amiriyya in Mecca. She was the first woman he married after Khadija. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Sawda had been among the emigrants to Abyssinia in the second migration, with her husband al-Sakran ibn Amr ibn Abd Shams. When the two returned to Mecca, her husband died. The Prophet contracted marriage with her and gave her a dower of four hundred dirhams, and she took charge of his household and his daughters after their mother's death. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## In Shawwal of the tenth year (The Sealed Nectar p. 117)
In this Shawwal the Prophet married [[person/sawda-bint-zama|Sawda bint Zam'a]]. She had embraced Islam long before, and had emigrated in the second migration to Abyssinia with her husband [[person/al-sakran-ibn-amr|al-Sakran ibn Amr]]. Al-Sakran died in the land of Abyssinia or after returning to Mecca. When she had completed her waiting period, the Prophet married her; she was the first woman he married after Khadija. Some years later she gave her turn to Aisha. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.117}
