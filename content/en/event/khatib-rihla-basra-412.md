## Summary
At twenty, about 412, he travelled to Basra and heard Abu 'Umar al-Qasimi ibn Ja'far al-Hashimi, 'Ali ibn al-Qasim al-Shahid, al-Hasan ibn 'Ali al-Saburi and a circle of others {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 87}.
