## Overview
On Saturday, twelve nights gone of Jumada, [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] heard that the men meant to climb the mountain; he ordered the army to mount, and he and [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] rode up, leaving [[person/muhammad-ibn-abbad-al-tamimi|Ibn Abi Abbad]] at Shibam with the men of Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.217} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}. When the Qudumis heard that al-Hadi and Abu al-Atahiya had gone up and that only Ibn Abbad and the Sana'is were at Shibam, the [[group/qudumis|the Qudumis]] went to the village of Shibam on the night of Sunday and by morning stood at the gate of the passage (al-darb); the narrator says there were fools among the villagers who did not like al-Hadi's rule because it had kept them from drink and vice {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}. The men broke the gate and entered and broke the prison, in which were Abu Ziyad and a body of the [[group/al-jafatim-sanaa|the Jafatim]]; when these came out and joined them, the Sana'is fled {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}. Ibn Abi Abbad came from al-Hadi's house and fought them for a while, but they were many and shot arrows and stones at him; he fought on until, in the narrator's word, he died a martyr {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}.

## Detail
This is the event that [[event/revolt-at-shibam-ibn-abbad-killed-288|the account of the added first pages]] tells briefly; both place it in Jumada II, and the present pages give the detail that those did not. The word martyr is the narrator's and is attributed to him, and the formula of mercy after Ibn Abi Abbad's name is not reproduced. Abu Ziyad is named but not entered as a person, and the Jafatim here are linked only to their earlier record. The account continues in [[event/abu-al-atahiya-and-ibn-al-daam-rout-the-qudumis-and-al-hadi-buries-ibn-abi-abbad-288]].
