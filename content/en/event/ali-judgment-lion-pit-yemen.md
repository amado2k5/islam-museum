## Summary
While [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] was in [[place/yemen|Yemen]], he adjudicated the case of the lion's pit: four men fell into a pit dug by some people to trap a lion; they clung to one another, the lion mauled them, and they died, whereupon their relatives took up arms intending to fight {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.114-115}.

## The Ruling
Ali ruled that the blood money be collected from the tribes of those who dug the pit: one-fourth of the blood money for the first because he perished through his own people, one-third for the second, one-half for the third, and the full blood money for the fourth {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}. He said to them: "Do you wish to fight one another while the Messenger of God is alive? I will pass a judgment among you: if you are satisfied with it, that is the judgment; otherwise, keep apart from one another until you go to the Prophet" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}.

## What Is Unclear
The book notes that the people refused to accept it and went to the Prophet, but the page is garbled in the copy read here, so the final ruling is not clear from it {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}.

## The Chain of Transmission
The book narrates the report from Hanash from Ali from the book Fada'il al-Sahabah, describing its chain of transmission as good {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}.
