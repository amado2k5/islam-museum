## Overview
In Sha'ban 798 (May 1396) his guardian-teacher Ibn al-Qattan arranged Ibn Hajar's marriage to Uns, daughter of the army inspector Abd al-Karim ibn Ahmad — on her mother's side a great-granddaughter of the amir Mankutimur, founder of the Mankutimuriyya college. Ibn Hajar moved into his wife's family mansion and lived there until his death; Uns survived him by almost fourteen years {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 809}.
