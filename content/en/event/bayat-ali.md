## Overview
[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] was given the oath of allegiance to the caliphate in Medina on the day after Uthman was killed, around 35 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.293-294}

## The oath
al-Suyuti relates that those Companions who were in Medina gave him their allegiance. He reports that it is said that Talha and al-Zubayr gave their allegiance unwillingly, then went out to Mecca where Aisha was, and set out for Basra seeking vengeance for the blood of Uthman. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.293-294}

## Ali's initial refusal
al-Suyuti relates that Ali first refused and said that the matter was not in their hands, but was for the people of Badr; whoever the people of Badr agreed upon would be the caliph. No one from the people of Badr remained who did not come to him and give him allegiance. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.275}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
