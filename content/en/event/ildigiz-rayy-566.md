## Summary
Shams al-Din Ildiguz, atabeg of Azerbaijan, took the city of Rayy through the agency of Ibn Arabshah al-Ibrahimi, vizier of Sultan Arslan ibn Tughril. He then made himself independent in Azerbaijan and al-Jibal. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 20}
