## Overview
The Javanese celebration of the Prophet's birthday in Yogyakarta: a week of the beating of the large drums, a popular market in front of the great mosque, and processions from the palace. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
One of the oldest celebrations of the birthday held continuously in Southeast Asia; a unique Javanese-Islamic blend. {cite:wikidata}
