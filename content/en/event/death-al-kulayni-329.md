## Summary
al-Kulayni died at Baghdad in 328/939-40 or 329/940-41; the EI2 prefers the latter because [[person/shaykh-al-tusi|al-Tusi]] — having given 328 in his earlier Fihrist — specifies Sha'ban 329/May 941 in his later Rijal {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 401}.

## Funeral and tomb
The funeral prayer was led by the Imami Hasanid [[person/abu-qirat-muhammad-ibn-jafar|Abu Qirat Muhammad ibn Ja'far]], and he was buried near the Bab al-Kufa; Tarikh al-Islam lists him under 329: 'he was at Baghdad and died there, and his tomb is visible with a tablet over it' {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 401} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 250}.
