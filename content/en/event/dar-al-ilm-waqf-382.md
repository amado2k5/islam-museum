## Overview
In 382 AH the vizier Abu Nasr [[person/sabur-ibn-ardashir|Sabur ibn Ardashir]] bought a house in al-Karkh in Baghdad, built it up and named it the House of Knowledge. He then endowed it for the scholars and placed many books in it as an endowment. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 635}

## Note
This account comes from al-Suyuti's "Tarikh al-Khulafa" alone. The Gregorian year (around 992 CE) is derived by conversion. The date needs an additional source.
