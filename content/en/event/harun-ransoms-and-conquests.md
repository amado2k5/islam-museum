## Overview
Al-Suyuti reports that [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]] ransomed the Romans in 189 AH, until no Muslim captive remained in their lands. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.463}

## Conquests of 190 AH
In 190 AH he took Heraclea and sent his armies into the land of the Romans. Sharahil ibn Ma'n ibn Za'ida took the fortress of the Slavs, and Yazid ibn Mukhallad took Malqunniya. [[person/humayd-ibn-mayuq|Humayd ibn Mayuq]] marched to Cyprus, destroyed and burned it, and took sixteen thousand of its people captive. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.463}

An additional source is needed: the account rests on one source, and the Gregorian conversion is derived.
