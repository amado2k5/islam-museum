## Overview
Al-Mas'udi, in "The Meadows of Gold", relates the raid of Hassan, king of Himyar, on Jadis in al-Yamama. It is an account from the reports of the Arabs before Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.455-456}

## The ruse and the raid
Hassan set out with the Himyar, and when they were three nights' march from al-Yamama, Rabah ibn Murra ordered them to uproot a tree each and carry it before them, so that [[person/zarqa-al-yamama|Zarqa al-Yamama]] would not warn the people about them. She looked out and told Jadis that the trees were moving toward them. They called her a liar and were heedless of preparing for war. Hassan attacked them at dawn from above, and plundered their people by killing, and took the women and children captive. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.455-456}

## After the raid
Their king [[person/al-aswad-ibn-ghaffar|al-Aswad ibn Ghaffar]] fled to Tayy, and they gave him protection. The two eyes of al-Yamama were torn out and crucified at the gate of Jaw, and the town was named after it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.455-456}
