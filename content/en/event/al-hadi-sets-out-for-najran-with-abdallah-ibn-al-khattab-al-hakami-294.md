## Overview
Al-Hadi set out for Najran in Rajab 294 AH with his troops and supporters from Hamdan and Khawlan, and with Abdallah ibn al-Khattab al-Hakami, who had come to him.

## Detail
When al-Hadi called out to Khawlan and ordered preparations for the journey, he set out with all his troops and his supporters from Hamdan and Khawlan. Abdallah ibn al-Khattab al-Hakami had come to him with a band of horsemen and men, fleeing for fear of his cousin al-Futayf ibn Muhammad al-Ashajj. The narrator calls al-Futayf a corrupt tyrant who hated the truth and its people; that description is the narrator's and is not repeated here. The departure was on the ten days that remained of Rajab 294 AH.
