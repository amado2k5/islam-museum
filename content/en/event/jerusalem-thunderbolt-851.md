## Lightning strike in Jerusalem
During Shawwal 851 AH a huge bolt of lightning struck Jerusalem {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 268-269}.

Ibn al-ʿImad recorded it among the calamities of the year, which passed over the cities in turn.
