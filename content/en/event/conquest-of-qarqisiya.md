## Summary
[[place/qarqisiya|Qarqisiya]] on the Euphrates was conquered in 16 AH, in the wake of Masabadhan's fall, when Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas sent 'Umar ibn Malik ibn 'Utba with an army that took it by surprise and by force; its people accepted the jizya. It was part of the conquest movement in [[place/jazira|al-Jazira]] and Iraq under [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|'Umar ibn al-Khattab]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 350}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir relates that the troops of al-Jazira had gathered to reinforce Heraclius against Homs and sent a force to the people of Hit, whereupon Sa'd dispatched 'Umar ibn Malik ibn 'Utba ibn Nawfal ibn 'Abd Manaf with an army, placing al-Harith ibn Yazid al-'Amiri over its vanguard {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 350}. Marching on [[place/hit|Hit]], 'Umar ibn Malik found its people entrenched; he left his tents standing, left al-Harith to besiege them, and set out with half the men, coming upon Qarqisiya unawares and taking it by force, so that its people agreed to the jizya {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 350}. He wrote to al-Harith: if they submit, let them depart; otherwise dig a trench facing theirs. Al-Harith's opponents chose to return to their own country, so he let them go and rejoined 'Umar ibn Malik {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 350}. Earlier that same year Dirar ibn al-Khattab had taken [[place/masabadhan|Masabadhan]] by force and killed Adhin ibn al-Hurmuzan; Sa'd left Ibn al-Hudhayl al-Asadi in charge of it, though some said its conquest came after [[event/battle-of-nahawand|Nahavand]] {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 350}.
