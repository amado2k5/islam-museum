## Overview
After the episode of [[event/abu-al-qasims-verse-to-hamdan-and-the-hamstringing-of-ibn-al-dahhaks-mare-286|the hamstringing of Ibn al-Dahhak's mare]], [[person/ibn-al-dahhak-rebel-at-khiwan-286|Ibn al-Dahhak]] saw that his people had given him up, left him and turned cold, throwing words at him and answering him harshly, and he called on [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] for help. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.185} He went to him and asked him to write to Abu al-Qasim to give him safety and overlook his slip; Abu al-Atahiya did well by him, wrote to Abu al-Qasim, and sent men of his companions with him in a party of horse and foot as reinforcement for Abu al-Qasim, asking him to give Ibn al-Dahhak safety and forgive his fault. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.185} When he reached Abu al-Qasim he was given safety, kept to peace and stayed with him at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.185} The book says only Abu al-Qasim, without lineage; in the book's setting this is probably [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|al-Murtada Muhammad]], and Abu al-Atahiya is likewise only probable.

## Detail
Abu al-Atahiya's army had had its pay held back, so he asked Abu al-Qasim to write him a letter, and he wrote and sent it with a man called Ja'far ibn Muhammad al-Zabidi, with verse. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.185} In the verse he congratulates him on what his Lord gave him in helping the chosen ones of the house of Hashim, speaks, in the writer's wording, of his loyalty and help to the family of the Chosen One, prays for God's help for him, calls him a brother sharing in the cause, and closes with a greeting. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.185} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.186} The editor notes that manuscript S omits a verse and what follows it. This is the writer's own verse and praise, not the museum's judgement, and no line of it is quoted. When the letter arrived Abu al-Atahiya sent the troops' pay, Ibn al-Dahhak stayed at Khiwan with Abu al-Qasim, the land grew calm and the strife ceased, the [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Ma'mari]] men sought safety and he gave it, and he wrote to al-Hadi to tell him their news. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.186} Next: [[event/al-hadi-has-the-spoils-gathered-and-divides-them-after-banu-al-harith-286]].
