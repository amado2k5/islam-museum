## Summary
Al-Mizzi took the shaykhhood of the Dar al-Hadith al-Ashrafiyya in Damascus in 718/1318 after Ibn al-Shirishi {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 491}, and it was Ibn Taymiyya who had worked for his appointment {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 492}.

## The founder's condition
The Ashrafiyya is a waqf whose founder stipulated that its shaykh be Ash'ari in creed; al-Mizzi wrote in his own hand that he was an Ash'ari, yet many adversaries doubted his sincerity and suspected him of being corrupted by Ibn Taymiyya {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 240} {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.10 p. 398}. When harm touched the school Ibn Taymiyya said: 'No one has presided over it since it was built worthier of the founder's condition than he' {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/458}; al-Mizzi taught there until his death {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 240}.
