## Overview
Ibn Bustam comes and asks to meet the Madanis and tells them the matter is grave, that he fears destruction for Abu Ja'far and for them from those of Banu al-Harith who had united against them, and advises that they put him out from among them, which is safer for them now and later {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.282}. They agree, come to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and ask him to leave the village, since they fear for him and for themselves {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.282}.

## Detail
They pressed him with a group of the people of Najran, and he agreed to what they asked and went out from them with Ibn Bustam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.282}. When they drew near Minas Ibn Bustam told him that Minas was stronger than al-Hisn and that, if he saw fit, he might go to it; he wished Abu Ja'far to be with him because of what had passed between him and Banu al-Harith, and he feared Yam, and pressed for Abu Ja'far to come; he agreed and went to [[place/minas-najran|Minas]] and sent for his children and womenfolk and settled them there {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.282}. The book does not here explain why Ibn Bustam advised this, and gives only his fear and what lay between him and Banu al-Harith; this is the narrator's account, attributed to him, with no verdict for or against anyone. The paragraph ends with a printed row of dots that is not read. Minas was named earlier in [[event/al-hadi-demolishes-minas-after-moving-out-the-strangers-and-warns-the-harithis-286]].
