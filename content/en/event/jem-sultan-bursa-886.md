## Overview
In the civil war after Mehmed II's death in 886/1481 the people of Bursa sided with Prince Jem, who held the town as sultan for eighteen days and struck coins in his name. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1398}

## Eighteen days
Al-Salabi writes that Jem was resident at Bursa when news of his father's death reached him, and took the townspeople's allegiance; he proposed dividing the empire — Europe for Bayezid, Asia for himself — but Bayezid refused the very principle, marched an army on Bursa and attacked, and Jem fled to the Mamluk sultan Qaytbay in Egypt. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 163} Jem had planned to rule at least Anatolia with Bursa as his capital. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1398}
