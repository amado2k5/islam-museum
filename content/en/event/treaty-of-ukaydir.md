## Summary
Khalid brought Ukaydir to the Prophet, who spared his blood, made peace with him on jizya, then released him — and he returned to his village. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## The account
Then Khalid brought Ukaydir to the Prophet, who spared his blood, made peace with him on jizya, then released him; and he returned to his village. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

A man of Tayy called Bujayr ibn Bajara spoke verses mentioning the Prophet's words to Khalid and what the wild cattle did that night. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- The text does not give the amount of the jizya.
