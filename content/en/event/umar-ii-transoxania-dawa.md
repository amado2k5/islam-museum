## Overview
When [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] became caliph, he wrote to the kings of Transoxiana calling them to Islam, and some of them accepted it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governor of Khurasan
His governor over Khurasan was [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah al-Hakami]]. He imprisoned [[person/mukhallad-ibn-yazid|Mukhallad ibn Yazid]] and the agents of Yazid. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The raid of Ibn Ma'mar
Al-Jarrah sent [[person/abdallah-ibn-mamar|Abd Allah ibn Ma'mar al-Yashkuri]] beyond the river. He penetrated deep into the lands of the enemy and considered entering China. The Turks surrounded him until he ransomed himself from them, and he went to al-Shash. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Umar lifted the land tax from those in Khurasan who converted. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
