## Overview
In one of his gardens al-Radi asked his companions whether they had seen anything finer. They praised the garden, and he replied that al-Suli's chess-playing was finer than those flowers and everything they had described. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 677}

The event is tied to the caliph [[person/al-radi-billah|al-Radi]] and to [[person/al-suli|al-Suli]]. The text does not give the year; the context is al-Radi's reign (322–329 AH) {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 676}.
