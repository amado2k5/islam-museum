## The story
Malik relates from Yazid ibn Abdullah ibn al-Had from Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Taymi from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman from Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the hour in Friday: Abu Hurayra went out to Mount Tur and met Ka b al-Ahbar; Ka b told him of the Torah and he told him of the Prophet, among what he told him that the best day on which the sun rose is Friday — Adam was created on it, sent down from Paradise, repented and died on it, and the Hour rises on it; every creature prostrates on Friday in awe of the Hour save the jinn and humans; and it holds an hour in which a Muslim servant praying asks God for anything and is given it. Ka b said: that is one day every year; Abu Hurayra said: rather every Friday — and Ka b read the Torah and confirmed it. Then Abu Hurayra met Abdullah ibn Salam and told him, and ibn Salam said Ka b erred first then was right, and told him that hour is the last hour of Friday {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-291|Muwatta 291]].

## The meaning
In this report the jinn and humans alone do not prostrate on Friday in awe of the Hour — they are the two exempted from the prostration of creatures on the Day of Resurrection; and the promised hour of Friday is its last hour {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
