## Summary
In Dhu al-Qaʿda 449 AH Sultan Tughrilbak met the caliph al-Qaʾim bi-Amr Allah in a formal audience, arranged after consultations. He entered to find the caliph seated on a high silk throne, wearing the Prophet's mantle (burda) and holding a staff, and the sultan kissed the ground several times. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.523-525}.

## The scene
The Commander of the Faithful said to him, through the chief of chiefs: «O ʿAli, the Commander of the Faithful thanks you for your effort and acknowledges your merit. He has entrusted you with all that God has entrusted to him of His lands, and has returned to you the care of His servants. So fear God in what He has entrusted to you, strive to build up the land and set right its people, spread justice and restrain wrongdoing.» Amid al-Mulk explained the words. The sultan rose, kissed the ground and said: «I am the servant and slave of the Commander of the Faithful, and I carry out his command and prohibition.» He was dressed in seven robes of one design. The caliph girded him with a sword with his own hand, addressed him as «King of the East and the West», tied on him the banner of praise with his own hand from among three banners, and had the investiture document read to him. He asked to shake the noble hand; the caliph gave him his hand twice, and he kissed it and placed it on his eyes. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.523-525}
