## Summary
In 201 AH the caliph al-Ma'mun made Ali ibn Musa his heir apparent, and ordered the soldiers to abandon black and wear green. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.554}

## In the same year
al-Tabari reports that in the same year volunteer enforcers of morality went out to rebuke people who lived in sin. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.551}
