## Overview
In 205 AH al-Ma'mun appointed Tahir ibn al-Husayn over the furthest reaches of the eastern administration. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.577}

## Before that
Earlier, he had given him al-Jazira, the police (shurta) and the two sides of Baghdad. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.577}
