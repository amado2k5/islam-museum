## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Abbasi, a descendant of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, held the stairway until he was killed. Ahmad ibn al-Muntashir al-Hamdani took his place and fought until he was killed. Banu al-Harith climbed the roofs, broke down the sides of the rooms, and drove Abu Ja'far and his men back to a portico in front of the room where the women of the household were.

## Detail
The attackers got into the house after a servant of Ibrahim al-Jumahi opened the door to them, and Abu Ja'far and his men took refuge on its upper floor. Banu al-Harith mounted the stairway, and Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Abbasi, a descendant of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib, met them there and fought on the stairs until he was killed. A Hamdani man named Ahmad ibn al-Muntashir took his place and fought until he was killed too. Banu al-Harith rose at every side of the house, brought ladders, climbed the roofs, and broke the sides of the rooms, until Abu Ja'far and his men reached a portico in front of the room where the women were. Banu al-Harith threw stones and arrows at them. The fighting was very heavy, and many were wounded, and no wall gave them cover from the arrows. The attackers then entered the room where the women were, out of fear that they might come in from behind. When they saw the women of the household and what had come upon them from their enemy, Abu Ja'far told his men to die before any of the attackers reached one of them, saying this would be their glory in this world and their reward in the next. They agreed.
