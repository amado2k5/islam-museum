## Overview
In 348 seventeen boats of pilgrims coming from Wasit sank with six hundred men, women and children aboard; in Jumada I the fitnas between Shi'is and Sunnis worsened — many killed and much burning at Bab al-Taq {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 686}.
