## Summary
Sultan Tughril Beg ordered the seizure and exile of Abu al-Qasim al-Qushayri, Imam al-Haramayn and Abu Sahl ibn al-Muwaqqiq; al-Juwayni hid and left by the Kirman road for the Hijaz, where he settled by the sanctuaries and received the title 'Imam of the two sanctuaries', while the memorist Abu Bakr al-Bayhaqi and al-Qushayri and a host of others made the Pilgrimage — it is said that year brought together some four hundred qadis of the Shafi'is and Hanafis driven from their homelands by the affair {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki pp. 271-272}.
