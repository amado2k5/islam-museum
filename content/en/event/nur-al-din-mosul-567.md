## Summary
Nur al-Din entered and took Mosul, and confirmed Sayf al-Din Ghazi ibn Qutb al-Din Mawdud over it. He continued to annex the lands of Syria and al-Jazira, until no king was left with anything but the name. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 31}
