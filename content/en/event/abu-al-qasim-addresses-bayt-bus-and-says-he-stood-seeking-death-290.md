## Overview
On p. 258 a piece begins "O Bayt Bus, we alighted in your enclosure", in which Abu al-Qasim addresses [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]], the place of his imprisonment, saying that the forsaking of their community after a covenant had come on them, that his imprisonment is light for him, that time has its turns so they should not feel secure, that he sees them diminishing each day while their enemy rises, and that he will not forget their jailing him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.258}. On p. 259 he says that he stood only seeking to be killed, content in God, that prison and fetter did not terrify him, that had he met them with a sword in his hand they would not have escaped, that but for the weakness of the mount under him he would not have been disappointed on the day of the meeting, that his ancestors were always men of understanding and that his father al-Hadi raised him by the judgement of the Book of God since he was suckled {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.259}.

## Detail
The poet points to the weakness of his horse as the reason for what happened and the book does not name the day here. The word "our community" in the line on the forsaking is not explained by the book. Before this are [[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-composes-verses-in-prison-290|the earlier pieces of the same section]]. The museum does not reproduce the lines nor the words of insult in them and gives only their sense as the book has it; the book ascribes the verse to Abu al-Qasim and the museum has not checked the attribution. The praise and the epithets in the lines are the poet's, given as the book gives them, with no verdict in the museum's voice.
