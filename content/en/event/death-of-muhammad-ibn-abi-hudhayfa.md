## Summary
Muhammad ibn Abi Hudhayfa ibn Utba ibn Rabi'a: his father was killed at the battle of al-Yamama and left him an orphan, and Uthman took him in and raised him well. He was once punished with the hadd for drinking, after which he turned to asceticism and devotion. He took part with Abd Allah ibn Sa'd in the raid of al-Sawari, then began to find fault with Uthman and to rouse the Egyptians against him, until he returned a gift of thirty thousand dirhams and said in the mosque: "Do you not see how Uthman deceives me in my religion and bribes me to go along with him?" When the Egyptians marched on Medina, he himself took control of Egypt and expelled from it Ibn Sa'd, Uthman's governor. It is reported that Mu'awiya and Amr ibn al-As later deceived him; he went out to al-Arish with a thousand men, and they set up a catapult against him until he came down with thirty of his companions and was killed. Ibn al-Athir, however, weakened this report, since the seizure of Egypt by Mu'awiya and Amr came after Siffin, not before Qays ibn Sa'd arrived there as governor. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 620-621}
