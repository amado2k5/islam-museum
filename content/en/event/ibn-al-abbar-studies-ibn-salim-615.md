## Overview
Ibn al-Abbar grew up at Valencia in study circles, reading under a body of teachers he names in his Mu'jam; for more than twenty years he was the disciple of the most learned traditionist in Spain, Abu al-Rabi' ibn Salim, who persuaded him to complete the Sila of Ibn Bashkuwal — the seed of the Takmila {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 705}.
