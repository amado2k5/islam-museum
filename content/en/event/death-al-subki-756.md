## Overview
Chief qadi Taqi al-Din al-Subki died in Egypt on the night of Monday in Jumada II 756/June 1355 and was buried that same morning. He had completed ninety-three years and entered his ninety-fourth, having held the bench at Damascus for about seventeen years before ceding it to his son Taj al-Din; on reaching Egypt he survived less than a month. A funeral prayer was held for him at the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus on Friday 6 Jumada II {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 756 pp. 263-264}. He authored some hundred and fifty works in law, fatwas, responsa and poetry {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 pp. 744-745}.
