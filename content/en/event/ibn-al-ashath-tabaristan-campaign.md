## Overview
Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad appointed [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-ashath-al-kindi|Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath ibn Qays al-Kindi]] over [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]], according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
Muhammad made peace with its people and drew up a covenant with them. Then they gave him time until he entered, and they seized the passes against him, and killed his son Abu Bakr. Muhammad escaped. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

After that the Muslims continued to raid that frontier, wary of pushing deep into enemy land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The governor who appointed him was [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]]. The date is not given in the data: an additional source is needed.
