## Summary
In 603 AH the caliph had ʿAbd al-Salam ibn al-Shaykh ʿAbd al-Qadir al-Jili arrested, his books were burned, and he was declared a transgressor. He was the man who had informed on Ibn al-Jawzi, which brought Ibn al-Jawzi to disgrace. The vizier Ibn Yunus had removed from his house books of the philosophers, the Epistles of the Brethren of Purity, and books of magic and star worship, among them an address to Saturn in his own hand. A great fire was lit under the mosque, the books were read out in public, and those who wrote them and those who believed in them were cursed. The curse reached Shaykh ʿAbd al-Qadir and even Imam Ahmad. His grandfather's teaching college was taken out of his father's hands and handed to Ibn al-Jawzi {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 16, 53-54}.
