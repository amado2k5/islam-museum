## Overview
al-Baladhuri gives several reports of a raid led by [[person/al-nusayr|al-Nusayr]] (in one copy al-Tusayr) ibn Daysam, with [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-mihsan|Hudhayfa ibn Mihsan]], toward [[place/tikrit|Tikrit]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 348-349}

## Al-Baladhuri's account
al-Nusayr went from Ayn al-Tamr to a water belonging to Banu Taghlib. He attacked them at night, killing and taking captives. A captive whom he had released guided him to a clan of Rabi'a, and he raided them at night, taking booty and captives. He went by land toward the region of Tikrit, and the Muslims took booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 348-349}

## The account of Abu Mas'ud al-Kufi
He came to Akbara, and its people granted him safe-conduct and brought out food and fodder for his men. He then passed by al-Bardan, whose people were also granted safe-conduct, and then came to al-Mukharram. The source relates al-Kalbi's account of why it was so named. They then crossed a bridge near the palace of Sabur. [[person/khurzad-ibn-mahibandadh|Khurzad ibn Mahibandadh]], who had been assigned to watch over them, came out against them. They fought and defeated him, and then came to Ayn al-Tamr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 348-349}

## The account of al-Waqidi
al-Waqidi said that al-Muthanna ibn Haritha sent al-Nusayr and Hudhayfa after the day of the Bridge and his withdrawal to Khafan, in Umar's caliphate. They fell upon a people of Banu Taghlib, crossed to Tikrit, and obtained cattle and sheep. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 348-349}

## The safe-conduct of Tikrit
'Ataab ibn Ibrahim quotes Abu Mas'ud that al-Nusayr and Hudhayfa granted safe-conduct to the people of Tikrit and wrote them a document. [[person/utba-ibn-farqad|Utba ibn Farqad al-Sulami]] carried it out for them when he conquered al-Tayrahan and Mosul. The report also gives another view: that al-Nusayr was sent by Khalid ibn al-Walid and raided villages at Maskan and Qatrabbul. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 348-349}

## Notes
The reports differ over who sent the raid, and they are not weighed against one another here. The date is derived and needs an additional source.
