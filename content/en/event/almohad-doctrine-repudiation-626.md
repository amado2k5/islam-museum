## Overview
In 626 AH the caliph [[person/al-mamun-almohad|al-Ma'mun ibn al-Mansur]] entered Marrakesh and announced that he was abandoning the mission (da'wa) of the [[group/muwahhidun|Almohads]]. Ibn Khaldun reports that he summoned the elders of the Almohads, enumerated their deeds to them, and killed a hundred of their notables. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1684-1685}

## What he ordered
- Erasing the name of the Mahdi Ibn Tumart from the coinage and the sermon, and denouncing him in the call to prayer in the Berber language.
- Adding the phrase "asbaha wa-l-hamdu lillah" ("morning has come, and praise be to God") to the dawn call to prayer.
- Permission for the Christians who had come with him to build a church in Marrakesh.
- Expelling the Almohads from al-Andalus. Ibn Khaldun says that the common people killed them in every region.

{cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1684-1685}

## After him
The source says that the forms of the mission were restored only after him, through his son al-Rashid, on condition of the elders of Tinmallal. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1684-1685}
