## Overview
Around 1365 [[person/ibn-khaldun|Ibn Khaldun]] was sent from [[place/granada|Granada]] to [[place/seville|Seville]] to conclude a peace treaty with Pedro I of Castile. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}

## In Seville
He wrote that he saw there "the monuments of my ancestors." Pedro received him generously and offered him a post in his service and the return of his ancestral estates. Ibn Khaldun politely declined. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}

## Why it matters
He later returned to Granada, but his enemies turned the chief minister against him, and he left again for North Africa. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}
