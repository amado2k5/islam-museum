## Summary
Tughril Beg survived his permission of the cursing and the imprisonment of al-Qushayri by only a short while before dying in 455/1063; his son Alp Arslan succeeded, and al-Kunduri was soon killed — at Marw al-Rudh at the end of 456/1064, his head carried to Nishapur; then the age dawned with the vizier Nizam al-Mulk, who ordered the mention of cursing dropped and its perpetrators disciplined {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 272} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 309}.
