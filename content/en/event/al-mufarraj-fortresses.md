## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-mufarraj-ibn-sallam|al-Mufarraj ibn Sallam]] conquered twenty-four forts after Khalfun, and then his companions killed him. [[person/sawran|Sawran]] rose after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 328-329}

## The conquest
After Khalfun a man called al-Mufarraj rose, in one copy al-Mufarrakh, son of Sallam. He conquered twenty-four forts and took control of them. He wrote to the postal official in Egypt to inform him of his success, and asked that the Imam appoint him over his region and confirm him in it, so that he might leave the ranks of the usurpers. He built a congregational mosque. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 328-329}

## His killing and his successor
His companions rioted against him and killed him. Sawran, in one copy Sudan, rose after him. He sent his messenger to [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] asking for an appointment and a letter of governorship, but the caliph died before the messenger returned. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 328-329}

al-Muntasir then died after a caliphate of six months, and al-Musta'in rose. He ordered his governor of the Maghrib, Utamish, to confirm Sawran over his region. Utamish was killed before Sawran's messenger set out from Samarra, and Wasif was appointed over the region, and he confirmed and dispatched Sawran. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 328-329}

## Notes
The approximate period, from the time of al-Mutawakkil to Wasif's governorship, is derived and needs an additional source. Some names in the report differ between manuscripts, as noted.
