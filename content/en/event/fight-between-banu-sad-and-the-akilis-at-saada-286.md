## Overview
When [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] saw what Ibn Abbad had resolved he sent a crier to [[group/banu-sad-khawlan|Banu Sa'd of Khawlan]], a great crowd gathered to him and he ordered them to camp in the yard of the Yarsamis at the side of the town. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} Near sunset a party of Banu Hamza came toward al-Hadi's house and Ibn Abbad's men met them; they shot at one another and fighting joined, and the news reached Banu Sa'd, who all came to al-Hadi's house; he looked down on them and said that whoever left was acting without his leave, yet some of the hotheads went off to their own side and did not return. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156}

## Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and Ali are sent
Al-Hadi grew angry and sent [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]], with the narrator [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] and a troop of horse, to turn the people back; they found the fight joined and three men dead, two of Banu Hamza and one Kulaybi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} When Banu Sa'd saw the party their backs stiffened, they charged [[group/al-akiliyyun-saada|the Akilis]], drove them back and took some of their houses, which they burned or pulled down (the second manuscript reads differently). {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} The people were sent away at nightfall with many wounded on all sides. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.156} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157}

## Al-Hadi's rebuke
Ali and his party took Banu Sa'd back to al-Hadi, who blamed them for fighting without his leave and told them to stay in their places. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} The account is Zaydi and the toll and the houses are its own report.
