## Summary
The qadi Abu Bakr al-Baqillani died at the end of a Saturday, seven nights before the close of Dhu'l-Qa'da 403 — 23 Dhu'l-Qa'da 403, corresponding to 5 June 1013 — in Baghdad. His son al-Hasan led the funeral prayer; he was buried in his house in Darb al-Majus and later transferred to the Bab Harb cemetery, and poets of the age mourned him {cite:wafayat-ayan} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1018}.
