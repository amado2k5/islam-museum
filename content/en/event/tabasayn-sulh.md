## Overview
Abu Musa al-Ash'ari sent [[person/abdallah-ibn-budayl|Abd Allah ibn Budayl al-Khuza'i]] as a raider. He came to Kirman and went on until he reached [[place/al-tabasayn|al-Tabasayn]]. These are two fortresses, one called Tabas and the other Qarin, which have palms, and they are the gate of Khurasan. Ibn Budayl took booty there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
Some people of al-Tabasayn came to Umar ibn al-Khattab and made peace with him for sixty thousand, or it is said seventy-five thousand, and he wrote them a letter. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
Al-Baladhuri says that Ibn Budayl may have set out from Isfahan on his own initiative. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
