## Overview
The people of the place gathered to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at [[place/raydah-yemen|Raydah]], a town in Yemen a day's march from Sana'a with springs and vines, in the words of a note the editor takes from the Mu'jam al-Buldan, and were glad of his coming because of what they had heard of his justice and what had befallen them from unjust rulers. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} When he reached it he lifted from its people the levies that were taken from them and are not lawful or permitted to be taken, and told the people to prepare to go out with him to a place called Kharfa. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204}

## Detail
The narrator says that al-Hadi announced that [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] had handed over to him al-Bawn and al-Mashriq and what had been in the hand of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] that Abu al-Atahiya had taken from him, and so he ordered the people to rise with him to go round the districts, enter them and look in on their people, announcing that he would return after some days when he had gone round all that Abu al-Atahiya had given up. Abu al-Atahiya had resolved to hand the whole matter over from his hand to al-Hadi's, and there was a matter between them that no one was told of. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} The names al-Bawn, al-Mashriq and Kharfa are read as printed and are not tied to sites. This is the narrator's account of a Zaydi imam's march and what he announced, and the museum adds no verdict.
