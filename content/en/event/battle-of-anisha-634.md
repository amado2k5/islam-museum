## Overview
al-Maqqari describes the battle of Anisha, in which the hafiz [[person/abu-al-rabi-al-kilai|Abu al-Rabi' al-Kilai]] was killed on Thursday, ten days before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 634 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.477}

## The death of al-Kilai
He kept always at the front of the lines, facing the enemy, calling to those who had fled: "Do you flee from Paradise?" He used to say that the end of his life would be seventy years, on account of a dream he had seen as a child. His student Ibn al-Abbar mourned him in an elegy rhyming in mim. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.477}
