## Summary
In Muharram, Saad al-Din Kumushtakin moved al-Malik al-Salih ibn Nur al-Din from Damascus to Aleppo. He arrested Ibn al-Dayya and his brothers and the chief Ibn al-Khashshab, and took sole control of the young prince's upbringing. The princes of Damascus grew alarmed and wrote to Sayf al-Din Ghazi asking him to cross the Euphrates, but he refused on the advice of Zalfandar. The matter ended with Aleppo being handed over to Saladin. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 60-61}.
