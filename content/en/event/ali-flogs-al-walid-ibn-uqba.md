## Summary
Two men testified before Uthman against al-Walid ibn Uqbah: one that he had drunk wine, and the other that he had vomited it up. Uthman said he would not have vomited it unless he had drunk it, and ordered Ali to carry out the fixed penalty. Ali ordered al-Hasan to flog him, but he did not do so, so he ordered Abd Allah ibn Ja'far, who flogged him while Ali counted until it reached forty, whereupon he said: "Stop; the Prophet flogged forty, Abu Bakr forty, and Umar eighty—each is a Sunnah, and this is dearer to me" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 161}.

## The Events
al-Sallabi quotes the report from Husayn ibn al-Mundhir, in which Uthman ordered the fixed penalty carried out, Ali ordered the flogging, and al-Hasan refrained from flogging before Abd Allah ibn Ja'far carried it out {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 161}. al-Sallabi reads the incident as proof of Ali's closeness to Uthman and his assistance to him in enforcing God's fixed penalties, and that he held the penalty for drinking wine to be forty lashes {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 161}.

## Ali's Stance on the Governors
The book notes in the same passage that Ali defended Uthman regarding what his governors did, saying to the effect that Uthman is not to be blamed for what his governor did by his order, though the scanned text of this sentence is garbled and cannot be quoted verbatim {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 161}.
