## Overview
Next morning [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] moved on and reached a Hamdani town called [[place/al-haira-hamdan-yemen|al-Ha'ira]]; the narrator says some foolish men of the place had molested pilgrims. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} On arriving he sent to the people of the place and ordered them to bring the offenders; they went to look for them at once. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203}

## Detail
He stayed two days in the town without water or fodder beyond a little brought for the army from far off, the people there drinking from a small supply that did not suffice; the narrator says that when al-Hadi came and stayed, God put blessing in that water. That is a marvel reported by a follower of the imam, attributed to him and not affirmed by the museum. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} Al-Hadi remained until the offenders were brought, ordered them bound in iron and took them with him to [[place/khiwan|Khaywan]], where he arrived on a Wednesday, and stayed until the Sunday nine nights gone of Muharram of 288 when he left for [[place/athafit|Athafit]]; the other manuscript reads seven, and the weekday differs by a day from the tabular calendar, so the date stays at month precision. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} Al-Hadi had sent his son Abu al-Qasim to Athafit a day after reaching the place; he stayed one day at Athafit and set out the next morning. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} It follows [[event/al-hadi-leaves-saada-for-khiwan-and-meets-al-daam-at-al-amshiyya-288]] and leads into [[event/al-hadi-at-raydah-lifts-unlawful-levies-and-calls-the-people-to-go-out-with-him-288]].
