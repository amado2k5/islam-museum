## Overview
Hisam ibn Abbad al-Akili goes to Ibn al-Dahhak at Rayda to seek his help against al-Qasim ibn Ahmad and Banu Sa'd. A Hamdan force goes with him on Wednesday, eight days before the end of Shawwal, and they reach al-Hasan ibn Ahmad and the Akil.

## Detail
The book says Hisam ibn Abbad al-Akili went to Ibn al-Dahhak at Rayda to ask his help against al-Qasim ibn Ahmad and against the Banu Sa'd. He set out with a Hamdan force on Wednesday, eight days before the end of Shawwal, and reached al-Hasan ibn Ahmad and the Akil, and they made it appear that he had come to make peace.
