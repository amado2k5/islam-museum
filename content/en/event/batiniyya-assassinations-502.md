## Summary
In 502 AH the Batiniyya killed the chief judge of Isfahan, Ubayd Allah ibn Ali al-Khatibi, in Hamadhan. In Isfahan on the Eid al-Fitr they killed the Hanafi mufti Abu al-Ala Sa'id ibn Muhammad al-Bukhari. In the Friday mosque of Amul, on 11 Muharram, they killed the Shafi'i shaykh Abu al-Mahasin Abd al-Wahid ibn Isma'il al-Ruyani while he was giving dictation {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 484}.

## Its effect
The harm they did alarmed every amir and scholar, because of how boldly they attacked people {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 484}.
