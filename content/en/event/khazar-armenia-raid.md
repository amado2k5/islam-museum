## Overview
In 183 AH the Khazars rose against Armenia, attacked the Muslims, shed blood and took more than a hundred thousand captive. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.461}

Al-Suyuti describes what happened as a great matter of which no like had been heard before. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.461}

The Gregorian date (around 798 CE) is derived.
