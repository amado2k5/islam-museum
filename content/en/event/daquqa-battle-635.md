## Overview
In 635 AH the Tatars reached Daquqa, plundering and corrupting, according to Ibn al-Imad. The emir Baktak al-Khalifati met them with seven thousand men, and the Tatars with ten thousand. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.170}

## The battle
The Muslims were routed after they had killed a multitude and nearly won. Baktak was killed, with a group of notable emirs. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.170}
