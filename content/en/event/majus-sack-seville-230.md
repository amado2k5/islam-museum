## Overview
Al-Maqqari says that in the days of [[person/abd-al-rahman-ii-andalusi|Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Hakam]] the Majus appeared and entered [[place/seville|Seville]] in 230 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari pp.381-382}

## The fighting
The amir sent forces with commanders from Cordoba. The Majus came down from their ships and fought the Muslims, and were defeated after a difficult stand; then reinforcements came. The Muslims took some of their ships and burned them. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari pp.381-382}

## Their departure
The Majus moved on to Shadhuna and stayed there two days, then to Latila, where they raided and took captives, then to Beja and Ashbuna. Their news was cut off when they sailed from Ashbuna. Abd al-Rahman ordered what they had damaged repaired and left a garrison there. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari pp.381-382}
