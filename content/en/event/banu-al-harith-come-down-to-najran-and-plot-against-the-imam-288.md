## Overview
The original has the heading: the account of Banu al-Harith's opposition at Najran. [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] said: in the month of Ramadan, when what happened of the war of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] with [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] happened, [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] came down to [[place/najran|Najran]], when the dates were ripe, in the autumn {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. They called one another to disorder against the imam, and [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] urged them on so that he would have standing and obedience among Banu al-Harith and al-Hadi would need him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}.

## Detail
The narrator says they agreed on disorder with no cause, no wrong seen and no injustice done; that is the narrator's testimony for al-Hadi, which the museum gives attributed and neither affirms nor denies. He adds that al-Hadi was not coming back to them, so [[person/ibn-humayd-of-banu-al-harith-286|Ibn Humayd]] and Ibn Bustam gathered Banu al-Harith and the Bedouin of [[group/shakir-hamdan-tribe|Shakir]] and Tiyam (as printed, probably Yam), giving them some of the property of the poor {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. When [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] saw this, he sent his son Ali ibn Muhammad to the fort with his brother al-Qasim ibn Muhammad and told them to call to Shakir and Thaqif and to face Minas and those in it so that the gathering of Banu al-Harith might divide; they did so, and Shakir, Thaqif and Wadi'a stood with them and went in with them to the village of al-Hajar to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}. The events go on in [[event/banu-al-harith-besiege-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-at-al-hajar-288]]. The text dates this Ramadan only by the year 288 given in the next account.
