## Overview
After his deposition, [[person/al-rashid-billah|al-Rashid]] went to Azerbaijan, and caused corruption in Maragha, Hamadhan and Isfahan. Then he fell seriously ill outside Isfahan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 670}

## His killing
A group of non-Arabs who had been his attendants came in to him and killed him with knives on 16 Ramadan 532 AH, and then all of them were killed. The mantle and the staff were not taken from him until he was killed, and they were brought afterwards to [[person/al-muqtafi-billah|al-Muqtafi]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 670}

Al-Suyuti quotes the writer Imad, who said of him: "al-Rashid had the generosity of al-Hasan al-Yusufi and the liberality of Hatim." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 670}
