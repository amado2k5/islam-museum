## Overview
In 302 AH a group of the [[group/qaramita|Qarmatis]] intercepted a pilgrim caravan at al-Aqaba on the road to Mecca. Ibn al-Jawzi says that they killed the pilgrims, took the women they wanted, and seized what was in the caravan. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 351-352}

## What the source says
Ibn al-Jawzi's account is short. The attackers were the Qarmatis, the place was al-Aqaba on the pilgrims' road to Mecca, and the year was 302 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 351-352}

The Gregorian date in the museum's data is derived from the Hijri year, and should not be relied on beyond that.
