## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Junada ibn Abi Umayya conquered the island of [[place/arwad|Arwad]] in 54 AH, and that [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] settled Muslims there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.330}

## The conquerors
Among those who conquered it were [[person/mujahid-ibn-jabr|Mujahid]] and [[person/tubay-ibn-amirat|Tubay']], son of the wife of Ka'b al-Ahbar, and Mujahid taught Tubay' the Qur'an there. It is said that he taught him in Rhodes. al-Baladhuri describes Arwad as an island near Constantinople. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.330} The commander of the conquest was [[person/janada-ibn-abi-umayya|Junada ibn Abi Umayya]].
