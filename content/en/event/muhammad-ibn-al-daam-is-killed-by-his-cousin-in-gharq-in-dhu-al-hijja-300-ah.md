## Overview
His cousin Ibrahim ibn Ibrahim kills Muhammad ibn al-Da'am at Gharq in Dhu al-Hijja 300 AH, during a drinking bout.

## Detail
Muhammad ibn al-Da'am is killed at Gharq by his cousin Ibrahim ibn Ibrahim, during a drinking bout, on the night of Saturday thirteen nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 300 AH, as the book has it.
