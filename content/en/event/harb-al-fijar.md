The war of al-Fijar was what was fought between [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] and [[group/kinana|Kinana]] on one side and [[group/qays-aylan|Qays Aylan]] on the other; it was called al-Fijar ("the sacrilege") because it proceeded by violating the sanctities of the sacred territory and the sacred months that the Arabs held in awe. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Victory belonged to Qays over Kinana in the day's first part; when midday came, victory belonged to Kinana over Qays. The commander of Quraysh and all Kinana was [[person/harb-ibn-umayya|Harb ibn Umayya]], for his standing among them in age and honour. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Prophet attended it at fifteen years of age, and would hand arrows to his uncles — readying arrows for them to shoot; in its aftermath came the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|pact of al-Fudul]] in Dhu al-Qa'da. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
