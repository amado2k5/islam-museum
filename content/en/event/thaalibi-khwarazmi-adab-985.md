## Summary
In his youth at Nishapur al-Tha'alibi studied adab under the celebrated stylist Abu Bakr al-Khwarazmi — al-Tabari's nephew and author of a famous diwan of rasa'il — before the master's death in 383 AH {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 450}.
