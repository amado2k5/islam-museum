## Overview
[[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] made the matter a council among six of the Companions of the Prophet. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] spoke, Umar named Talha by name, and he assigned the matter to [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]]. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.236}

The index of the fourth volume of al-Tabari names "the story of the council" in the reports of the year 23 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.579}

This summary does not give the whole account; the details of the choice and its outcome need an additional source.
