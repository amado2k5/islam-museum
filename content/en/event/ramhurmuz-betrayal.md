## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Musa made a truce with the people of [[place/ramhurmuz|Ramhurmuz]]. When the truce expired, he sent [[person/abu-maryam-al-hanafi|Abu Maryam al-Hanafi]] against them, and he made peace with them. Then they betrayed it, and Abu Musa took the city by force at the end of his days. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What the source says
The peace was for eight hundred thousand dirhams, or nine hundred thousand. Then the people of Ramhurmuz broke the peace, so the last conquest was by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
