## Overview
Qutayba ibn Sa'id ibn Jamil, Abu Raja' al-Baghlani — the shaykh of the two Shaykhs — was one of the eight masters from whom al-Tirmidhi narrated most heavily in his Sunan, his intake from the eight amounting to three quarters of the book {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 451}.

## On his Khurasan journey
His hearing from Qutayba falls before the shaykh's death in 240/854, a stop on al-Tirmidhi's scholarly journey through the lands of Khurasan {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 450-451}.
