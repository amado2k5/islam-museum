## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] raided [[place/maysan|Maysan]] and took it by force after a hard fight, and gained control of its land. Then the people of Abar Qubadh broke faith, and he took it by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The captives of Maysan
He relates from al-Mada'ini that people used to call Maysan, Dastmaysan, the Euphrates and Abar Qubadh "Maysan". Among its captives were Abu al-Hasan, who was [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], and his brother Sa'id ibn Yasar. Their father's name was Qayruz. They were given to a woman of the Ansar, who freed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
