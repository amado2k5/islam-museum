## Summary
Abu Abd Allah al-Hakim Ibn al-Bayyi' sought knowledge from childhood under the care of his father and maternal uncle; his first hearing of hadith came in 330/941-2, when he was about nine {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 122-127}.

## A house of learning
Abd al-Ghafir al-Farisi described the family as a house of uprightness, scrupulousness and prayer in Islam; al-Hakim recorded his father in his Tarikh al-Naysaburiyyin {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 127}.
