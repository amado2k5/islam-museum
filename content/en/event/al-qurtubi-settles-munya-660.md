## Overview
Al-Qurtubi settled at Munyat Bani Khusayb in Upper Egypt, became its resident and laboured there on his great Qur'an commentary, filling it with every rarity {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 17/97}. The Encyclopaedia of Islam confirms he settled at Munyat Abi l-Khusayb, where he died and was buried {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 550}; the village stood prosperous on the Nile's east bank {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p. 124}.
