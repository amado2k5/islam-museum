## Overview
At al-Buqayra, Yazid ibn Ali and Muhammad ibn Ayyub gave Ali ibn Muhammad a letter from Banu Rabi'a of Hamdan asking him to march with them. He refused until he saw them fighting the enemy, and went back to the fort. The narrator says Banu Rabi'a meant treachery, and the Banu Rabi'a who stayed at Minas raided toward the village to join their companions.

## Detail
Yazid ibn Ali al-Ja'di and Muhammad ibn Ayyub met him at al-Buqayra and gave him the letter, asking him to march with them. They told him that Ibrahim al-Ja'di had gone to the village to join Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah in support. The narrator says Banu Rabi'a meant treachery, so Ali refused what they asked and said: when I see you fighting our enemy, I will come to you. Meanwhile Hasan ibn Ahmad, Ibn Munir and those who had joined them arrived and told him of his father's death and of the killing of the men in the house. He sent Yazid and Muhammad back to their own people, saying he would not begin a war with them unless they began it. Then he returned to the fort, and the Banu Rabi'a who remained at Minas raided the village.
