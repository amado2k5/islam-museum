## Overview
This completes the sentence cut at the foot of p. 245: [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] halted with [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] and stayed some days, and Ibn al-Dahhak was at that time leaning toward [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] and fled the town {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.245} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.246}. People came to al-Hadi and spoke to him about cutting off the man's property and razing his house; he disliked it, and went on until he reached [[place/bayt-dhud-yemen|Bayt Dhu'd]], where he stayed some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.246}.

## Detail
[[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] wrote to the two sons of [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] to meet him at [[place/al-bawn-yemen|al-Bawn]]; they did so, and he went out to meet them until they met at [[place/al-ghayl-al-bawn-yemen|al-Ghayl]]. Al-Hadi had sent [[person/muhammad-ibn-said-ibn-yusuf|Muhammad ibn Sa'id]] with al-Da'am to take the oaths of the two sons of Ya'fur, and he took safety, covenants and pledges from them; they agreed that the two would leave their course (the other copy reads that they would leave it), that al-Hadi and al-Da'am would go toward the district of Sana'a, and each went back to his place {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.246}. What precedes is in [[event/the-strife-between-al-yafur-and-their-slaves-and-al-daams-appeal-to-al-hadi-290|the strife of Al Ya'fur and their letter to al-Da'am]]; what follows is in [[event/al-hadi-waits-at-bayt-dhud-and-falj-and-moves-by-raydah-and-madar-to-sabal-290|al-Hadi's wait at Bayt Dhu'd and Falj and his move to Sabal]]. The book gives Ibn al-Dahhak only by that name, so the museum links him to the Khiwan rebel of 286 AH as probable only, and likewise Muhammad ibn Sa'id; the narrator does not say who the people were who asked for the cutting off of his property.
