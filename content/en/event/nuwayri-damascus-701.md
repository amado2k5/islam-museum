## Summary
In Jumada II 701 (January 1302) al-Nuwayri travelled to Syria at the sultan's request to manage state property, the diwan al-khass, in a country unsettled since Ghazan Khan's great invasion of 699; he stayed at Damascus two years and four months {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 157}.

## His travels in the country
He travelled round the country — he mentions a journey in the Ghawr and describes the Ghuta of Damascus with the same attention to detail he gave Upper Egypt. He seems to have amassed a small fortune: in 703 he had no fewer than ten horses, but an equine epidemic destroyed them, leaving him without even a mount of his own. He befriended Sayf al-Din Balaban al-Jukandar al-Mansuri, appointed shadd of crown property in 702 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 157}.
