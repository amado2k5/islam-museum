## Summary
In 523 AH about 6,000 people accused of Isma'ili belief were killed in Damascus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 546}.

## Background
Bahram al-Astarabadi had entered Syria and concealed a great following. Tughtakin appointed him to Banyas, which was one of Tughtakin's bad decisions, and set up a Isma'ili missionary for Bahram in Damascus. His followers grew there, and they took several Syrian fortresses, including al-Qadmus. The safe conduct of Banyas was handed to the Franks {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 546}.
