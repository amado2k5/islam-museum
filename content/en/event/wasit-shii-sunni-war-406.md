## Overview
In the reports of 406 and 407 AH Ibn al-Jawzi says that the strife between the Shi'a and the Sunnis at [[place/wasit|Wasit]] continued, and the quarters of the Shi'a and the Zaydis in it were plundered and burned. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.283}

## The result
The notables of the Shi'a and the Alids fled the city and made for the Banu Mazyad and asked them for help. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.283}

What is not covered here: the causes of the strife, its parties in detail, and its outcome. This needs an additional source.
