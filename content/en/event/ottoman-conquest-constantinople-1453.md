## Summary
In his commentary on *Hadir al-Alam al-Islami* (The Present State of the Islamic World), Shakib Arslan describes the Ottoman siege of the city from the time of Bayezid I, the siege under Murad II, and its conquest at the hands of [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed the Conqueror]]. {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}

## After the conquest
Arslan mentions the plunder of [[place/istanbul|the city]] for three days, and Friday prayer in Hagia Sophia on the fourth day. He counts the conquest among the most important turning points in the region. {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}

## A note
The source is a single one. Arslan's dates (the start of the siege in 1396 CE, and the abjad count of 853 AH) and what he relates about the artillery of Mehmed II need an additional source before they are relied on. The book does not specify the day of the conquest; this needs an additional source.
