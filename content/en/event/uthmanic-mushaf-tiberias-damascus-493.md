## Overview
In 493 AH the Uthmanic codex was moved from Tiberias to [[place/damascus|Damascus]], out of fear for it. The people went out to receive it, and kept it in a treasury in the enclosure of the mosque. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.658}
