## Overview
The Treaty of Adrianople between the Ottomans and Russia was signed at Edirne in 1125/1713, after Dutch and English mediation {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 306}.

## Edirne the diplomatic capital
Russia ceded Azov and undertook not to interfere in Cossack affairs; Sultan Ahmed III had dismissed his grand vezir Baltacı Mehmed Pasha for continuing the campaign against the Russians {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 306}.
