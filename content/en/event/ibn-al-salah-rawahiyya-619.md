## Summary
When al-Malik al-Mu'azzam had Jerusalem's walls demolished, Ibn al-Salah came to Damascus and was given the teaching post of the Rawahiyya. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186}

## The wall comes down, the people flee
Work on the wall began on the first day of Muharram 616; the people fled, leaving their property — some to Egypt, some to al-Karak, some to Damascus. Ibn al-Salah settled in Damascus for the rest of his life, teaching at the Rawahiyya, then the Ashrafiyya and the Shamiyya. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 65-66} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}
