## Overview
Ibn Bistam feared for the lives of Banu al-Harith, sent them to Maynas, and told Ibn Humayd to keep out of sight; Ibn Humayd settled at a Banu al-Harith stronghold called al-Nakhl. Ibn Bistam sent men to rouse Madhhij, but no one answered.

## Detail
The book says that Ibn Bistam, seeing what had happened, feared destruction for Banu al-Harith and judged that al-Hadi would not drop the pursuit either on the plain or in the mountains. He sent to the Banu al-Harith near him, and they went to Maynas. He sent word to Ibn Humayd to keep hidden, and Ibn Humayd settled at a Banu al-Harith stronghold called al-Nakhl. He sent to al-Jawf Mansur ibn Hisham al-Dahhi and Yazid ibn al-Aswad al-Kamabi to call out to Madhhij, but no one among the people answered them.
