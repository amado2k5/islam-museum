## Overview
A film settled over al-Dhahabi's eyes: he would recognise it, and the sight weakened little by little until it was wholly gone, and he grew angry when told 'if your eye were needled you would see', for it was not a needlable cataract {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/338}. The Encyclopaedia of Islam, after Abu al-Fida and Umar ibn al-Wardi, dates the blindness to 743, or by others to 741 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 215}; even so he kept at his studies night and day and kept composing.
