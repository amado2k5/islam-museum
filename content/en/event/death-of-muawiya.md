## Overview
[[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] died in Rajab 60 AH and was buried between the Gate of al-Jabiya and the Small Gate in [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. It is also said that he lived seventy-seven years. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 329}

## His bequest
al-Suyuti says that he had with him some of the Messenger of God's hair and a paring of his nails, and he bequeathed that they be placed in his mouth and on his eyes. He said: "Do that, and leave me between myself and the Most Merciful of the merciful." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 329}

## The length of his rule
According to al-Suyuti he was governor of Syria for twenty years and caliph for twenty years. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 325}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
