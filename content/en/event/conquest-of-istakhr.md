## Overview
al-Tabari reports that Abu Ma'shar and al-Waqidi said the first conquest of Istakhr was in 23 AH. Sayf said it was after the second Tawwaj. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.174}

The date is disputed among the transmitters. It needs an additional source for the details of the conquest.
