## Overview
In 128 AH al-Harith ibn Surayj was killed in Khurasan. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.330} Al-Tabari says that al-Kirmani was the one who killed him. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.368}
