## Overview
[[person/amr-ibn-madikarib|Amr ibn Ma'dikarib]] came to the Prophet among people of [[group/banu-zubayd|Banu Zubayd]], and became Muslim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230}

## Before the coming
When news of the Prophet's affair reached them, Amr said to [[person/qays-ibn-makshuh|Qays ibn Makshuh al-Muradi]]: "You are the chief of your people, and it has been mentioned to us that a man of Quraysh called Muhammad has come out in the Hijaz saying that he is a prophet. Come with us to him, so that we know his teaching. If he is a prophet, you will not be left in the dark about it, and we will follow him; if he is other than that, we will know his knowledge." Qays refused and made light of his opinion. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230} Amr rode until he came to the Prophet, and became Muslim, confirmed him and believed in him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230}

## His satire of Qays
When that reached Qays ibn Makshuh, he threatened Amr and grew hard on him and said: "You opposed me and abandoned my opinion." Amr said about this [[work/amr-ibn-madikarib-poem-to-qays-ibn-makshuh|a poem]] in which he reminded him that on the day of Dhu Sana' he had given him a command whose rightness was clear, and of fear of God. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/231}

## What followed
Amr remained among his people, Banu Zubayd, whose chief was [[person/farwa-ibn-musayk|Farwa ibn Musayk]]. When the Prophet died he apostatised; see [[event/apostasy-of-amr-ibn-madikarib|the apostasy of Amr ibn Ma'dikarib]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/231}

## Markers of the report
- Ibn Hajar mentions a view that he did not meet the Prophet, but came after his death. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/230} {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1130}
