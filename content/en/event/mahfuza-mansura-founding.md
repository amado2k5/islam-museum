## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-hakam-ibn-awana|al-Hakam ibn Awana al-Kalbi]] built a city called "al-Mahfuza" beyond the lake on the side toward India, to be a refuge for the Muslims. Then [[person/amr-ibn-muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Amr ibn Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] built "al-Mansura" after it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Mahfuza
Al-Hakam took office when the people of India had become unbelievers, except the people of Qasa, so the Muslims had no place of refuge. He built al-Mahfuza and made it a shelter and a refuge, and the elders of the Banu Kalb asked him its name, and he chose this name. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Al-Mansura
Amr ibn Muhammad was with al-Hakam, who entrusted him with weighty affairs, and he sent him on raids from al-Mahfuza. He built a city beyond the lake and called it [[place/al-mansura|al-Mansura]], the one where the agents lodged in the time of the narrator. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
Al-Hakam was killed there. Then Abu Muslim, at the start of the Abbasid dynasty, appointed [[person/mughallis-al-abdi|Mughallis al-Abdi]] over the frontier of Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
