## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/shahrak|Shahrak]], marzban of Fars, gathered a great host and marched until he came to Rayshahr ([[place/rayshahr|Rayshahr]]). [[person/al-hakam-ibn-abi-al-as|al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As]] came out against him, with [[person/sawwar-ibn-hammam|Sawwar ibn Hammam]] at the head of his vanguard. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The battle
They fought very fiercely. Shahrak had posted a man in a valley to kill every one of his companions who fled. al-Baladhuri relates a report of a Persian of the Asawira who told those who wanted to kill him that they did not fight a people who were victorious. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Sawwar killed Shahrak with a spear-thrust and then with the sword. Then Ibn Shahrak charged at Sawwar and killed him. Rayshahr was taken by force. The source compares that day to the day of al-Qadisiyya. The good news was carried to Umar by [[person/amr-ibn-al-ahtam|Amr ibn al-Ahtam]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
