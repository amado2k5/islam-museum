## Summary
In Jumada I 666 AH al-Zahir [[person/baybars|Baybars]] took Jaffa by the sword, took its citadel by surrender terms, and then demolished it. He besieged [[place/shaqif-arnun|al-Shaqif]] for ten days and took it on terms. He then raided the districts of Tripoli, cut down its trees and diverted its rivers, and camped below the fortress of the Kurds, whose people submitted to him. He then moved on to Hama and then Famiya, and advanced on Antioch, taking it in four days. Those killed there were more than forty thousand {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.560}.
