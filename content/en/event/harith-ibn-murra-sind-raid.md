## Summary
Al-Harith ibn Murra al-Abdi set out for the land of Sind as a volunteer raider by Ali's command. He gained spoils and took many captives, and in a single day he distributed a thousand heads. He remained on campaign until he was killed, together with those who were with him except a few, in the land of al-Qiqan, in the year 42, in the days of Mu'awiya. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 730}.
