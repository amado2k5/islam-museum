## Overview
In 602 AH, when news spread that [[person/shihab-al-din-ghuri|Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri]] had been lost in the battle of the Khita, those whom Ibn al-Athir calls "the corrupters" rose up on the borders of the land. Daniyal, lord of the mountain of Judi, apostatised from Islam and followed the Banu Kukar. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 300-301}

## The Banu Kukar
Their dwellings were in fortified mountains between Lahore and Multan. They had obeyed Shihab al-Din and paid him tribute, then they rose, cut the road from Lahore and elsewhere to Ghazna, and seized a large caravan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 300-301}

## The envoy
Shihab al-Din sent his mamluk Aybak, commander of the Indian armies, calling them to obedience. Ibn Kukar asked the envoy: why has the sultan not sent a messenger to us? He answered: and what is your standing that he should send to you? Then what happened, happened. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 300-301}

Ibn al-Athir's account ends here, and what follows needs an additional source. See [[event/killing-of-shihab-al-din-ghuri-602|the killing of Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri]].
