## Overview
Al-Hadi came close to a Banu al-Harith fort called Thala and ordered it pulled down and burned. He spent that night nearby.

## Detail
The book says that after the pursuit al-Hadi's forces moved on to a Banu al-Harith fort called Thala. He camped close to it, ordered it pulled down and burned, and spent that night there. Late in the night, men of Ahlaf came to Yam with news that a party of Wada'a had raided a secret herd of theirs. That raid is the event that follows.
