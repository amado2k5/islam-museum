## Summary
Among the events of 897 AH, Ibn al-Imad records in *Shadharat al-Dhahab* a general plague, which he describes as strange, the like of which had not been heard. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}

## What the source reports
The author relates that it was said that a quarter of the people of the earth died in that plague. This is an estimate attributed to those who said it; the source introduces it with the word "it was said". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}
