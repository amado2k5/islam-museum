## Overview
Osman Gazi died at Söğüt about two years before the conquest; his son Orhan had him carried to Bursa and reburied there. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 36-37}

## The first tomb
Orhan reburied his father in what had been the baptistry of the church of St. Elias the Prophet, converted into a mosque after the conquest; when Orhan himself died he too was buried beside his father rather than in his own külliye. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 36-37}
