## Summary
Ibn Mardanish, lord of eastern al-Andalus, died, and Yusuf ibn Abd al-Mu'min of the Almohads took over his lands. Eastern al-Andalus was secured for him, and Gafsa and its districts were joined to the Maghrib lands he already held {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.37}.
