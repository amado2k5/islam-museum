## Overview
Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr, Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti, was born in Cairo on 1 Rajab 849 (3 October 1445). His father taught Shafiʿi law and acted as substitute qadi; through him the family was of Persian origin and had settled in Asyut in the Mamluk period, while his mother was a Circassian slave. An anecdote prefigures his fertile career: his mother is said to have borne him in the family library, which earned him the nickname "son of books" (ibn al-kutub) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 913-914}.
