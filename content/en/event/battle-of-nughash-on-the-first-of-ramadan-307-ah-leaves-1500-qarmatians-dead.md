## Overview
The battle of Nughash on the first of Ramadan 307 AH leaves 1,500 Qarmatians dead.

## Detail
The two sides meet in the open at a place called Nughash on Tuesday, the first of Ramadan. The book says 1,500 Qarmatians were killed, they were routed with a great defeat, and what they had with them was taken. The lacuna in the printed text (at the foot of p. 404, where the opening of the campaign is missing) is noted by the editor, who quotes Ghayat al-Amani for the year 307 AH, the battle, and its cause: the Qarmatians had grown strong around Musur and harmed their neighbours. The name of the Qarmatian commander given there is not repeated in this record.
