## Overview
Al-Baladhuri lists the governors of Armenia for the Abbasids in succession, among them Rawh ibn Hatim al-Muhallabi, then [[person/khuzayma-ibn-khazim|Khuzayma ibn Khazim]], then Yazid ibn Mazid al-Shaybani. He says that Khuzayma was the most severe of them in his governance, and that he was the one who introduced the survey at Dabil and al-Nashwa, which had not been made before. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.296} The events took place in [[place/armenia|Armenia]] and [[place/dabil|Dabil]].

## The patriarchs
The patriarchs of Armenia lived in their own lands, each protecting his district. When a governor's agent came, if they saw in him chastity, firmness and strength, they paid him the tax and obeyed him; otherwise they made light of his authority. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.296}

## The Mamun period
Khalid ibn Yazid ibn Mazid appointed them in the caliphate of al-Mamun, accepted their gifts and mixed with them himself. Al-Baladhuri says that this corrupted them and made them bold against the governors who came after him under al-Mamun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.296}

The date is approximate, between about 775 and 833 CE, and needs an additional source.
