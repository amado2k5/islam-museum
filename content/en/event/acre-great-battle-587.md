## Summary
There was a great battle at Acre between the Muslims and the Franks who were besieging it. The two sides fought fiercely, in a clash unlike any heard of in those days, with charges and retreats and killing beyond counting on both sides. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 186}.
