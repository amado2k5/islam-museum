## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] stayed in [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] through Ramadan until the day of the Fitr, when he went out to the prayer place with the people; while he was preaching from the pulpit the men raided the gate of Sana'a with their horse, and he ordered [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] who rode out with the horse and drove them off {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}. On the Friday that was the second of the Fitr, al-Hadi went out with all his camp, drew up an army that he sent up [[place/nuqm-sanaa-yemen|Nuqm]] from two sides, and rode with his horse and the rest of his foot to [[place/alab-sanaa-yemen|Alab]] to face the people of [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]]; the two armies climbed upon those at Nuqm, killed many of them, drove them from the mountain, took prisoners and plundered what lay in the men's camp, and al-Hadi returned to Sana'a safe and victorious, as the narrator puts it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}.

## Detail
The narrator is Ali ibn Muhammad, who gives the account from Muhammad ibn Sa'id, as the heading of the section shows: the account returns to the fighting between al-Hadi and Banu Tarif, Ali ibn Muhammad said, Muhammad ibn Sa'id told me {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.231}. The book does not say that the Friday after the Fitr is the second of Shawwal, only the second of the Fitr, and the museum gives Shawwal alone. The account comes after the report of Banu al-Harith at Najran in [[event/banu-al-harith-come-down-to-najran-and-plot-against-the-imam-288]] and the narrator returns to the fighting at Sana'a after it.
