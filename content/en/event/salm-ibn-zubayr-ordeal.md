## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that when Yazid ibn Mu'awiya died, the people dispersed from [[person/sallam-ibn-ziyad|Sallam ibn Ziyad]], and he left Khurasan for [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The ordeal
Ibn al-Zubayr fined him four million dirhams and imprisoned him. Sallam wished he had gone to Syria and stayed in the service of his brother Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He remained in Mecca until [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] besieged Ibn al-Zubayr; he broke through the prison and went to al-Hajjaj, then to Abd al-Malik. Abd al-Malik made him governor of Khurasan, and when he came to Basra he died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
