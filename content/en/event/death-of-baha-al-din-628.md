## Summary
In 628/1231 Baha al-Din Walad died at Konya, and his son Jalal al-Din, then about twenty-four, succeeded him in his teaching post. {cite:britannica-rumi}

## Burhan al-Din Muhaqqiq
The following year Burhan al-Din Muhaqqiq, one of Baha al-Din's former disciples, arrived at Konya and acquainted Jalal al-Din more deeply with the mystical theories that had developed in Iran; he shaped Rumi's spiritual formation until he left Konya about 638/1240. {cite:britannica-rumi}
