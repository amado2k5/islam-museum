## Overview
Thinking themselves surrounded, the men of Banu al-Harith surrendered. Ali ibn Muhammad ordered them freed and their property returned, and forty dinars that one of his men had taken from a man of the clan were given back.

## Detail
The book says the horsemen surrounded the Banu al-Harith, who gave up their weapons and asked for quarter. When Ali saw this, he ordered his men to release them and come back to him, and told the clan he had been sent only to seek al-Dahhaf; he had the spoil returned. A man of the clan then reported that one of Ali's companions had taken forty dinars from him; Ali ordered the servant summoned and the money returned, and then went back to the mosque, where he stayed some days. The OCR of the mosque's name is not certain and the record says only the mosque.
