## Overview
The Alawis return to Sa'da in Ramadan 322 AH, and Zayd ibn Abi al-Abbas is killed after Hassan has held about seventy men.

## Detail
The Alawis go to Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur, who receives and honours them and writes to the Khawlan and Hamdan sub-tribes to rise with them. Hassan leaves Sa'da on Thursday, seven nights into Ramadan, for Barat with a group of Khawlan, and seizes about seventy men of several groups. The book says he imprisoned them. The Alawis return to Sa'da on Thursday in mid-Ramadan and fight on Friday with the other factions of the town, and all of Khawlan join them except two groups. Hassan's men are routed, Zayd ibn Abi al-Abbas al-Mabsi is killed, Hassan goes to Hajar, and the Alawis hold the town. Some of the prisoners are freed by a relative of one of Hassan's men, as the book says. The factions are not named in this record.
