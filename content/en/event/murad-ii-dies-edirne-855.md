## Overview
Murad II died in his palace at Edirne in 855/1451, aged 47, and was buried beside the Muradiye mosque in Bursa {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 83}.

## Death of the sultan
He was succeeded by his son Mehmed II, who did not return to Edirne until he decided to besiege Constantinople {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.
