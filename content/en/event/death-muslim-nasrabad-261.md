## Summary
Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj died on the eve of Sunday and was buried at Nasrabad outside Nishapur on Monday, five nights before the end of Rajab, in 261, aged fifty-five lunar years {cite:wafayat-ayan v p. 195} — which fixes his birth in 206 {cite:wafayat-ayan v p. 195}. He had filled the world with learning, so that the memoriser Abu Ali al-Naysaburi said: "there is no book under the vault of the sky sounder than Muslim's book in the science of hadith" {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 527} {cite:wafayat-ayan v p. 195}.
