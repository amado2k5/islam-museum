## Overview
The narrators differ over who first crossed the Pass of Baghras. Al-Baladhuri gives three opinions. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The opinions
Some said: [[person/maysara-ibn-masruq|Maysara ibn Masruq al-Abbasi]] was sent by [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]]. He met a host of the Romans with Arabs of Ghassan, Tanukh and Iyad among them, and overcame them. Then [[person/malik-al-ashtar|Malik al-Ashtar al-Nakha'i]] joined him as reinforcement. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Others said: the first to cross it was [[person/umayr-ibn-sad|Umayr ibn Sa'd al-Ansari]], when he set out on the matter of Jabala ibn al-Ayham. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abu al-Khattab al-Azdi said: it reached him that Abu Ubayda himself raided in summer, passing Massisa and Tarsus, and reached Zunda. Another said: he sent Maysara, and he reached Zunda. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The event is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]].
