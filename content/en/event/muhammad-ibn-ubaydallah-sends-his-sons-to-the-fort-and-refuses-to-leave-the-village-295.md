## Overview
Banu Abd al-Madan proposed that Muhammad send his sons to Hamdan. He ordered Ali, al-Qasim and Ja'far to the fort instead. They disliked it and asked him to come out with them, since they did not trust Banu Abd al-Madan. He refused and swore not to leave the village, and Ali and his brothers went to the fort.

## Detail
Banu Abd al-Madan said: if you think fit, send your sons to Hamdan, so that they may stay there. He ordered his sons Ali, al-Qasim and Ja'far to go to the fort. They disliked this, and asked him to come out with them, because they did not trust Banu Abd al-Madan. He said: my sons, I have taken pledges from these people and I hope they will not betray me, but go where I order you, for I will not leave the village, and he swore an oath that he would not leave the village or be killed in it. They asked how that could be, and he said: if they fight me, and Banu Abd al-Madan fight with me, I hope to hold the town; and if al-Hadi comes, he will kill them for my sake; or if they kill me, al-Hadi will come in anger and kill them for my sake, and my death will be the cause of the killing with which al-Hadi had threatened them. They said: we will not leave you and we will share your lot. He said: the best course for us is that you be in the fort, and you can bring relief to us from there, since the enemy has surrounded us in the village. Ali ibn Muhammad and his brothers then went to the fort. The oath's wording is partly garbled in the scanned text; the record gives only the sense that he would not leave.
