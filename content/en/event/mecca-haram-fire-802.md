## Fire in the Sacred Mosque in 802 AH
At the end of Shawwal 802 AH a great fire broke out in the Sacred Mosque at Mecca and consumed about a third of it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 21}.

One hundred and thirty marble columns were burned to lime. What burned extended from the Gate of ʿUmra to the Gate of al-Hazura {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 21}.
