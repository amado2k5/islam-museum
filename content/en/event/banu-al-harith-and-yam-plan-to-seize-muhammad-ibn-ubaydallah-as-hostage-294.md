## Overview
Banu al-Harith raised their heads, wrote to al-Musawwid asking him to come to their land and fight al-Hadi, and then joined with Ya'am to seize Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and hold him to ransom for Ali ibn al-Rabi'.

## Detail
The book says Banu al-Harith raised their heads and wrote to al-Musawwid, sending men to assure him of their loyalty and to ask him to come to their land and fight al-Hadi. His reply told them to rise if they were sincere, and that when they had seized the governor he would come to them. Banu al-Harith and Ya'am then agreed to seize Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and hold him to exchange for Ali ibn al-Rabi'. The book says the plan also included harm to his sons and kin; the record gives the plan in outline only.
