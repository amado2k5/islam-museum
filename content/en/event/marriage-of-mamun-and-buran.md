## Overview
In 210 AH [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] married [[person/buran-bint-al-hasan-ibn-sahl|Buran]], daughter of [[person/al-hasan-ibn-sahl|al-Hasan ibn Sahl]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 487-488}

## Dowry and celebration
Her dowry reached forty jewelled bracelets and ten chests of pearls and rubies. Her father gave robes of honour to the commanders and spent on them for seventeen days. He wrote slips with the names of his estates and scattered them over the commanders and the Abbasids; whoever got a slip was given that estate. At the wedding he scattered a tray full of jewels before al-Ma'mun. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 487-488}

## Tribute
Al-Ma'mun ordered the land tax of Fars and the districts of Ahwaz paid to al-Hasan ibn Sahl in the year of the wedding. Al-Mas'udi quotes a verse by Muhammad ibn Hazim al-Bahili on the occasion. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 424}
