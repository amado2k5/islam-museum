## Overview
The Beyazidiye külliye of Bayezid II was built on the west bank of the Tunca in 1484-8 by the architect Hayrettin {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 70}.

## The healing complex
The complex comprised a mosque, a hospital, an insane asylum, a medical school, a hospice and an imaret; the sultan's vakfiye records a staff of 167, of whom 21 served the hospital, and Evliya Çelebi says the patients were regularly made to listen to music {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 73} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.
