## Summary
On the death of al-Shaykh al-Mufid in 413/1022 his pupil al-Sharif al-Murtada took over the leadership of the capital's Imamis, and al-Tusi subsequently became his principal disciple {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 744}.
