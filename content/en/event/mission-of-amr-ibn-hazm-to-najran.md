## Overview
The Prophet sent [[person/amr-ibn-hazm|Amr ibn Hazm]] to the [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b]], after their delegation had been appointed, to teach them the religion and the Sunna and the features of Islam, and to take their alms. He wrote him [[work/charge-to-amr-ibn-hazm|a letter setting out his charge]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/241}

## The account
Ibn Ishaq records the mission in an appendix to [[event/mission-of-khalid-to-banu-al-harith|the account of Khalid and the delegation of the Banu al-Harith]], and gives the full text of the charge. It opens with a statement from God and His Messenger and the verse on fulfilling covenants. It then orders Amr to fear God in everything, to hold to the truth, to bring good news to people, to teach them the Qur'an and give them understanding of it, to be gentle with people in the truth and firm with them against wrong. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/241} It then details the rites of the pilgrimage, the prayer, alms and the poll tax; these are on the charter page. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/242} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243}

## In Ibn Hajar
His son Muhammad related from him a letter written to him by the Prophet containing the obligations, the zakat and the blood-money; it was transmitted by Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and others. Amr was appointed by the Prophet over Najran. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1095}
