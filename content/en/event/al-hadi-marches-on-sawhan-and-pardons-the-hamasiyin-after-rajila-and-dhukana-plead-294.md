## Overview
Al-Hadi marched on Sawhan to raze it; Rajila and Dhukana pleaded with him, and he later pardoned the Hamasiyin, whom Ibn Bistam brought to him seeking safety.

## Detail
On the following day al-Hadi ordered a march on Sawhan, the village of the Hamasiyin, to raze it and cut its palms. Rajila and Dhukana came and asked him to pardon the wrong they had done, and promised to bring the Hamasiyin to him so that he could carry out his judgement; he agreed. He then turned his army back to al-Hijr of Najran and stayed there for some days. Then Ibn Bistam came to him with the Hamasiyin seeking safety; al-Hadi granted it, forgave their fault, and ordered them sent back to their place.
