## Summary
In 373 AH a severe famine struck Baghdad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 80}.

## The price of wheat
A kurr of grain reached 400 dirhams, a price that put bread beyond the reach of ordinary people {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 80}.
