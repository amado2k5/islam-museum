## Overview
Ibn Kayyala returned to Sanaa, climbed Ghumdan and asked Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Hasani to join him in rising against the Qarmatians, with the call made for al-Hadi. They fought the Qarmatian preachers, killed some of them, and then wrote to al-Hadi asking him to come.

## Detail
Ibn Kayyala fell behind Ibn al-Fadl's march toward Tihama at Naqil al-Sawd, returned to Sanaa, climbed Ghumdan, and asked Muhammad ibn al-Husayn al-Hasani to join him in rising against the Qarmatians on the condition that the call be made for al-Hadi. Muhammad agreed. They fought the Qarmatian preachers who were in Sanaa, killed some, and seized their goods on a Tuesday two nights before the end of Rabi' II, and they restrained the people's hands. They wrote to al-Da'am, who sent his son al-Husayn to them with his army. They also wrote to al-Hadi to tell him what had happened and to ask him for help, and he answered them.
