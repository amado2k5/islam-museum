## Overview
The Day of al-Sarara was a war between the Banu Amr ibn Awf and the Banu Akhrath of Yathrib before Islam. Ibn al-Athir presents its reports on pages 491 to 496. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491-496}

## The poetry
The day is linked to the poetry of [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]], among it a verse in which he boasts of the Banu al-Najjar. It contains a poetic exchange between him and [[person/qays-ibn-al-khatim|Qays ibn al-Khatim]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491-496}

The war took place in [[place/medina|Medina]], which was called Yathrib. The date is approximate and belongs to the days of the Aws and the Khazraj.

What is not covered here: the course of the fighting and its outcome. This needs an additional source.
