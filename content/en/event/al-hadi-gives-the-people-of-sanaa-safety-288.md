## Overview
Before [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] entered, the people of [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] feared that he would punish them for what they had done in driving [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] out of the city, fighting him and what they had meant to do to him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.226} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}. When he entered he exposed none of them for what had come from him, spread safety over them and wrote them a letter, read in the markets, granting them safety, and the people were reassured by this from him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}.
