## Overview
Among the events of 337 that Ibn al-Jawzi records in the *Muntazam*: the appearance of the night "phantom" and the floods of waters over Baghdad and its districts — part of that year's catalogue of calamities and nightly terrors {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}.
