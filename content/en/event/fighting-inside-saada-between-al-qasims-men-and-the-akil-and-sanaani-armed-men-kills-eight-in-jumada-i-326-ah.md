## Overview
One of al-Qasim's men is killed inside the quarter, and war breaks out between him and the armed men of Akil and Sanaa on Thursday, six nights into Jumada I 326 AH. Eight are killed between them.

## Detail
The book says that after al-Qasim entered Saada and reached al-Ghayl, Sanaani and Akil men who had no strength against him joined him, and then the Akil fell on one of his men and killed him. War broke out between him and the armed men of Akil and Sanaa on Thursday, six nights into Jumada I of 326 AH. Men fought from the noon prayer until the first part of the night, and eight were killed between them. The editor's notes give variant readings of the verb for the attack, which are not used to settle who began it.
