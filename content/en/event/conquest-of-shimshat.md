## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that when [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] became caliph, he gathered for [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] Syria, al-Jazira and their frontiers, and ordered him to raid Shimshat, which is the fourth Armenia. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.259}

## The conquest
He sent against it [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] and [[person/safwan-ibn-al-muattal|Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal]], and they took it after some days on terms like those of Edessa. Safwan remained there and died there at the end of Mu'awiya's caliphate. It is said that Mu'awiya himself raided it, with them in his company. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.259}

al-Baladhuri mentions that Constantine encamped against it in 133 AH and could not take it. It remained a kharaj land until al-Mutawakkil made it tithed land. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-jazira|conquest of al-Jazira]], and its place is [[place/shimshat|Shimshat]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.259}
