## Summary
The Adnan Rashid–Jay Smith debates of 2014–2015 at Speakers' Corner and universities, on 'Is the Qur'an the Word of God?' and 'Who is the Real Jesus?'. {cite:answering-islam-debates-index}

## The line of the debates
Historical and textual argument in a public space: the sources of the Sira and the Gospels, the soundness of transmission, and rejoinder for rejoinder on the platform.

## The exchange — what each side said
- **Rashid (Qur'an as word of God)**: the Qur'an is a text preserved by memorisation and mass transmission, its codex standardised under Uthman — he defended the consonantal unity and put the prophecy/fulfilment and Qur'anic-pointer arguments; he attacked New Testament transmission.
- **Smith**: built on the historical-critical counter — multiple readings and early manuscripts (Sana'a among them), arguing the Qur'an too has a compositional history open to the same criticism as the Bible; and defended the Christian historical Jesus.
- **Rashid ('the real Jesus')**: presented the Qur'anic Jesus — God's servant and messenger — as the historically superior portrait, with the alternative reading of the crucifixion's end.
- **Smith (rebuttals)**: pressed the pivot: if criticism of the Qur'anic text stands, the challenger's position falls with it — so he defended the warrant of his early sources.
- **The verdict**: staged at universities and Speakers' Corner; the recordings serve as the methodological text for the historical-critical school on both sides. {cite:answering-islam-debates-index}
