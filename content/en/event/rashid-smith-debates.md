## Summary
The Adnan Rashid–Jay Smith debates of 2014–2015 at Speakers' Corner and universities, on 'Is the Qur'an the Word of God?' and 'Who is the Real Jesus?'. {cite:answering-islam-debates-index}

## The line of the debates
Historical and textual argument in a public space: the sources of the Sira and the Gospels, the soundness of transmission, and rejoinder for rejoinder on the platform.
