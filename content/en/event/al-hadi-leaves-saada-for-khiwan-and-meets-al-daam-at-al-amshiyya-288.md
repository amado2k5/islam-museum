## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] stayed at [[place/saada|Sa'da]], and when the new moon of Muharram of the year 288 appeared he sent a messenger to [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] ordering him to send out a force of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] and [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] living at Najran. His son [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]], the narrator of this book, was sent to him with a dense force of horse and foot. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202}

## Detail
When they reached al-Hadi at Sa'da he gathered a great army from [[group/khawlan-tribe|Khawlan]] and set out for [[place/khiwan|Khaywan]], leaving [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-alawi-governor-of-saada-288|Ahmad ibn Muhammad]], of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali, at Sa'da as governor. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202} He camped at al-Amshiyya with Ali ibn Muhammad al-Alawi in his vanguard; the name of the camp is read as printed and not tied to a known site. There [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am ibn Ibrahim]] met him with all of [[group/bakil|Bakil]], and al-Hadi and al-Da'am spent the night at al-Amshiyya. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.203} The narrator calls the army of Khawlan great and praises the imam throughout; the museum records the counts and movements only. It follows [[event/al-hadi-writes-to-banu-al-harith-in-verse-and-they-pledge-to-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-287]] and leads into [[event/al-hadi-at-al-haira-seizes-the-offenders-and-goes-by-khiwan-to-athafit-288]].
