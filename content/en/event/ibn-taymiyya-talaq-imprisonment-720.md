## Overview
In 718/1318 a letter from the sultan forbade Ibn Taymiyya to issue fatwas on repudiation contrary to prevailing Hanbali doctrine: he had denied that three repudiations uttered at once took effect and counted an oath of repudiation a single oath where no repudiation was intended. Two councils presided over by Tankiz in 718 and 719 were followed by a third on 20 Rajab 720/26 August 1320, which found him in breach of the prohibition and condemned him to prison; he spent just over five months in the Damascus citadel, released on 10 Muharram 721/9 February 1321 by decree of al-Malik al-Nasir {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 985}.
