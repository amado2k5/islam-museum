## Summary
Al-Qifti used his influential office to further scholarship: he sheltered at Aleppo Yaqut al-Hamawi, who had fled the Mongols, and gave him much help in the compilation of his great geographical dictionary {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}; this fell within his years over the diwan of finances before his dismissal in 628 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}.
