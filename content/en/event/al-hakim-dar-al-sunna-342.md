## Summary
Al-Hakim kept the special company of the imam Abu Bakr al-Sibghi, who consulted him on questions, rijal criticism and 'ilal, and bequeathed to him the management of his school Dar al-Sunna with charge of its endowments {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 128}.

## Al-Sibghi's death
Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Ishaq al-Sibghi died in Sha'ban 342/953; he had given fatwas for more than fifty years without a single error being recorded against him {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 256}.
