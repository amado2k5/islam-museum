## Overview
In al-Baladhuri's account, Zibatra was an old Byzantine fortress taken by [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]], and it went through cycles of ruin and rebuilding. In the caliphate of [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] the Byzantines came out against it, killed, took captives and destroyed it, and he raided them as far as [[event/conquest-of-amorium|Amorium]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.270}

## The history of the fortress
Al-Baladhuri says that the Byzantines destroyed it in the days of al-Walid ibn Yazid, and then demolished it in the strife of Marwan ibn Muhammad. Al-Mansur built it, then al-Rashid built it and garrisoned it. In the caliphate of [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] they raided its people, and he ordered it repaired and fortified. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.270}

## Al-Mu'tasim's campaign
Al-Baladhuri relates that what the Byzantines had done angered al-Mu'tasim, so he raided them until he conquered Amorium, then ordered Zibatra rebuilt and fortified. The Byzantines were never able to take it after that. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.270}

Al-Mas'udi says that Theophilus, king of the Byzantines, came down upon it with other kings, and took it by the sword, killed and took captives, and raided the lands of Malatya. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.449}
