## Summary
Al-Tha'alibi died at Nishapur in 429 AH — Ibn al-Imad allows the year before — at eighty years of age, having filled East and West with diwans and compilations that al-Safadi's list counts at eighty-six titles {cite:wafayat-ayan} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 246-247} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 450-451}.
