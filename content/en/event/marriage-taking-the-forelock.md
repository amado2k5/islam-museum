## The story
Yahya relates from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam that the Messenger of God said: when one of you marries a woman or buys a slave-girl let him take her forelock and invoke blessing, and when he buys a camel let him take the top of its hump, and let him seek refuge in God from the shaytan {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-14-1575|Muwatta 1575]]. The muhaqqiq notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr called it mursal with all the Muwatta transmitters, its meaning resting on the hadith of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As and Abu Las al-Khuza i.

## The meaning
In this report the Prophet taught the etiquette of entering marriage and buying a slave-girl: taking the forelock, invoking blessing, and seeking refuge from the shaytan {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
