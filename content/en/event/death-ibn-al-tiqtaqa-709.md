## Summary
Safi al-Din Muhammad ibn Ali, known as Ibn al-Tiqtaqa, author of al-Fakhri, died in 709 AH as al-Dhahabi's source list to the Tarikh al-Islam records {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi s.a. 601-610 p. 738}; al-Zirikli in al-A'lam gives only 1302 CE, the year the book was composed {cite:al-aalam j.3 p. 149}.
