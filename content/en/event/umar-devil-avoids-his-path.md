## The story
The Prophet says to Umar ibn al-Khattab: 'by Him in Whose hand is my soul, Satan has never met you walking a path but he took another path than yours' [[passage/muslim-2396|Muslim 2396]].

## The meaning
The hadith praises Umar's uprightness and steadfastness on truth; Satan flees from the path of one whom God has established in right, as Umar was among the people of justice and piety {cite:sahih-muslim}. It is read in the books of asceticism and etiquette as among the clearest texts of the shaytan fleeing from the believer who stands firm {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Notes
The report is from Sahih Muslim, and has parallels in the Sunan books on the shaytan fleeing the believer's path; this browser shows it with the rest of the reports of jinn and devils dealing with human beings {cite:sahih-muslim}.
