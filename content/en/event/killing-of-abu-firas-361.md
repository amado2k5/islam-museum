## Overview
In Rabi al-Akhir 361 AH [[person/abu-firas-al-hamdani|Abu Firas al-Hamdani]] was killed at Badi, a village at the edge of the desert near Homs, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.433}

## Cause
A rift arose between him and his nephew Abu al-Ma'ali ibn Sayf al-Dawla, and Abu al-Ma'ali gathered Bedouins of the Banu Kilab. Qarghuwayh caught up with him, and Abu Firas's companions gave themselves up and mixed with the enemy. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.433}

## The killing
Qarghuwayh ordered a slave of his to kill him. He took his head, and his body was left until Bedouins buried it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.433}
