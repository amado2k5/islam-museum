## Overview
[[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] succeeded his father al-Muhallab in Khurasan and undertook many campaigns. After him al-Hajjaj appointed his brother [[person/al-mufaddal-ibn-al-muhallab|al-Mufaddal ibn al-Muhallab]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The campaigns of Yazid
Al-Batm was taken by [[person/mukhallad-ibn-yazid|Mukhallad ibn Yazid]]. [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-al-abbas|Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Abbas]] went to Herat after the defeat of Ibn al-Ash'ath, killed [[person/al-ruqad-al-mutakki|al-Ruqad al-Mutakki]] and collected the land tax. Yazid marched against him, they fought and he routed them, and ordered that they not be pursued; the Hashimi went to Sind. Yazid raided Khwarazm and took captives, and the troops put on the clothes of the captives and died of the cold. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governorship of al-Mufaddal
He conquered Badhghis after it had rebelled, and took spoils which he divided among the people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
