## Overview
Ibn al-Athir relates that the Franks built a strong fort at the House of Jacob, in a place known as the Ford of Sorrows (makhadat al-ahzan), near Banias. [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] stayed at Banias and raided their lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 95-96}

## The battle
In 575 AH the Franks came out with their king against a group of the Muslim army, and Salah al-Din marched with the troops. The Franks fought very fiercely, then they were routed, many of them were killed, and their king escaped. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 95-96}

## The captives
Many were taken captive, among them [[person/ibn-barzan|Ibn Barzan]], lord of Ramla and Nablus, who ransomed himself for a hundred and fifty thousand Tyrian dinars and the release of a thousand Muslim captives. Most of the work on that day was done by [[person/izz-al-din-farrukhshah|Izz al-Din Farrukhshah]], the nephew of Salah al-Din. Then Salah al-Din destroyed the fort. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 95-96}
