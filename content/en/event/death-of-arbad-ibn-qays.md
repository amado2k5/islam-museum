## Overview
[[person/arbad-ibn-qays|Arbad ibn Qays]] died by a thunderbolt that burned him and his camel, one or two days after he returned to the land of [[group/banu-amir-ibn-sasaa|Banu Amir]] from [[event/assassination-attempt-of-amir-and-arbad|the delegation]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215}

## The account
Ibn Ishaq relates that Arbad's companions, after they had buried [[event/death-of-amir-ibn-al-tufayl|Amir]], came to the land of Banu Amir in mourning, and their people came to them and asked Arbad: "What is behind you?" He said: "Nothing. He invited us to worship something I wish were with me now, so that I could shoot it with arrows until I killed it." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215} He went out a day or two after his words, with a camel following him, and God sent a thunderbolt upon him and upon his camel, and it burned them both. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215} Arbad was the half-brother, on his mother's side, of [[person/labid-ibn-rabia|Labid ibn Rabi'a]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215}

## What was revealed about him and Amir
Ibn Hisham, not Ibn Ishaq, says that Zayd ibn Aslam related from [[person/ata-ibn-yasar|Ata ibn Yasar]], from [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]], that God sent down about Amir and Arbad verses of Sura al-Ra'd, from [[passage/quran-13-8|Ra'd 8]] to [[passage/quran-13-11|Ra'd 11]]. He says that "the successive ones" in the verse are angels by God's command who guard Muhammad, and then mentions Arbad and what God destroyed him with in [[passage/quran-13-13|Ra'd 13]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215}

## The elegies of Labid
Labid elegized him in several poems that Ibn Ishaq quotes, and in one he gives him the kunya Abu Hariz. See [[work/labid-elegies-for-arbad|the elegies of Labid for Arbad]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/217}

## Marks of the report
- The chain to Ibn Abbas is an addition by Ibn Hisham. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215}
- The editors confirm that in one copy "yabī'uhu" stands in place of "yatba'uhu". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/215}
