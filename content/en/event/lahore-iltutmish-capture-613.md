## Overview
Hasan Nizami describes at length in the Taj al-ma'athir the capture of Lahore by Shams al-Din Iltutmish's forces in 613/1217, after the city had been disputed among the former Ghurid officers Qubacha, Yildiz and Iltutmish since the murder of Mu'izz al-Din Muhammad b. Sam in 602/1206 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 37}.
