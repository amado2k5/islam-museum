## Summary
News reached on the third of Shaʿban in the year 285 AH that Raghib the servant, mawla of al-Muwaffaq, had raided by sea. God granted him victory, with many ships and everyone aboard them from the Byzantines (al-Rum). Three thousand of them were beheaded, the ships were burned, and many Byzantine forts were taken. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 295}

## The scene
A brief report that shows sea raids on the Byzantine frontier continuing for years after al-Muwaffaq's death, and that the mawla of the man who led the war against the Zanj now commanded the Muslims' ships at sea. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 295}
