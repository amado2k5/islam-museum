## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-muslim|Yazid ibn Abi Muslim]], client of al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, was appointed by Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik over [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]] and the Maghrib. He reached Ifriqiya in 102 AH, and his guard was made up of [[group/berbers|Berbers]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
Al-Baladhuri says that he branded each of them on the hand with the word "my guard". His conduct became hateful to them, they spoke to one another and agreed to kill him. He went out one evening for the sunset prayer, and they killed him at his prayer place. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik appointed [[person/bishr-ibn-safwan-al-kalbi|Bishr ibn Safwan al-Kalbi]], who struck the neck of [[person/abdallah-ibn-musa-ibn-nusayr|Abd Allah ibn Musa ibn Nusayr]], because he was accused of having killed him and of stirring up the people against him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
[[person/ibn-abd-al-hakam|Ibn Abd al-Hakam]] says Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik appointed Yazid ibn Abi Muslim, al-Hajjaj's secretary, over Ifriqiya in the year 101. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 288} He reports that his guards killed him in the year 102, in al-Layth's account. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 289}
