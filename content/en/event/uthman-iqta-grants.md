## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates from Musa ibn Talha that [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] was the first to grant lands in Iraq as fiefs, from the properties of Khosrow and the land of the exiles. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.381-382}

## Those granted
- [[person/talha-ibn-ubaydullah|Talha]]: al-Nashastaj.
- [[person/khabbab-ibn-al-aratt|Khabbab ibn al-Aratt]]: Isbina, and Sa'bana was also mentioned to him.
- [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]]: land on the bank of the Euphrates.
- [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud]]: land in al-Nahrayn.
- [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]]: Isbina.
- [[person/khalid-ibn-urfuta|Khalid ibn Urfuta]]: land by the bath of A'yan.
- [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]]: land which he sold.
- The report also names Wa'il ibn Hujr, Adi ibn Hatim, al-Ash'ath ibn Qays and Sa'd. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.381-382}

The report names Isbina for both Ammar and Khabbab, and al-Nashastaj repeats for Talha. This is a difference in the report and it is not settled here.

Ibn Mas'ud and Sa'd used to give their lands to be worked for a third and a quarter. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.381-382}

The date falls in the caliphate of Uthman (24–35 AH), and the conversion is derived.
