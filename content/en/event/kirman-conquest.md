## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Uthman ibn Abi al-As met the marzban of [[place/kirman|Kirman]] on the island of Abr Kawan and killed him, and the strength of the people of Kirman was weakened. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest of Bayminda
When [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Ibn Amir]] came to Fars, he sent [[person/mujashi-ibn-masud-al-sulami|Mujashi ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami]] to Kirman in pursuit of Yazdegerd. He reached Bayminda and his army perished there. Then Ibn Amir appointed him over Kirman when he set out for Khurasan. Bayminda took it by force, spared its people and gave them safe conduct. There is a palace there known as the Palace of Mujashi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Shiraj and the surrounding area
Mujashi took Barukhruwa, then came to Shiraj, the city of Kirman, and stayed over it for some days. He fought them, took it by force and left a man there, and many of its people went into exile. Abu Musa had sent [[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi ibn Ziyad]], who took the area around Shiraj and made peace with the people of Bam and al-Andaghar. Then its people became unbelievers and broke their covenant, and Mujashi took it, conquered Jiruft by force and marched through Kirman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
