## The birth
Abu 'Amr Yusuf ibn 'Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Barr al-Namari al-Qurtubi was born at Cordoba on a Friday while the imam was delivering the sermon, five days before the end of Rabi' II in the year 368 — the twenty-ninth of November, as his companion Tahir ibn Mufawwiz showed it written in his father's hand {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 137}.
## A variant
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr said he was born in the same year as Abu 'Imran al-Ghafjumi {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 137}; al-Judhwat al-Muqtabis and Bughyat al-Multamis give his birth in Rajab 362 instead {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 137}.
