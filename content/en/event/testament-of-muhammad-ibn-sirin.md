## Summary
[[person/muhammad-ibn-sirin|Muhammad ibn Sirin]] died at Basra on a Friday in 110 AH, a hundred days after [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]], and Ayyub and Ibn Awn washed him. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 205} Ibn Sa'd transmits his will to his sons and household and records his debt, his instruction about his shroud, and what his son paid for him; what happened at al-Hasan's death is gathered in [[event/deathbed-of-al-hasan-al-basri|the record of al-Hasan's deathbed]].

## The text of the will and its content
Ibn Sa'd relates from Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ata' from Ibn Awn: Ibn Sirin's will was headed "what Muhammad ibn Abi Amra bequeathed to his sons and household." It told them to fear God, mend what lay between them, and obey God and His Messenger if they were believers, and he enjoined on them what Abraham enjoined on his sons and Jacob, the counsel mentioned at [[passage/quran-2-132|Qur'an 2:132]]. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 204} He told them never to cease being brothers of the Ansar and their clients in religion, for chastity and truthfulness are better, more lasting and more honourable than adultery and lying. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 204} Concerning what he left, he provided for the event that something happened to him before he changed his will. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 204} Al-Dhahabi gives the report in abridged form and says only that the will was mentioned. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3452}

## His debt
Ibn Sa'd asked Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Ansari about the debt that burdened Ibn Sirin until he was jailed. He answered that Ibn Sirin had bought food for forty thousand dirhams, was told something about its origin that he disliked, and left it or gave it away as charity while the money remained owed; he was jailed for it, a woman having had him imprisoned, and the man who jailed him was Malik ibn al-Mundhir. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 197} In another report he had sold a slave girl to Umm Muhammad, daughter of Abd Allah ibn Uthman ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi, and she returned complaining she was ill-treated; Muhammad took her back though he had spent the price, and she was the one who had him jailed. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 198} Qatada says he visited him in prison and found him writing poetry for a man. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 198}

## Paying the debt and what he left
His son Abd Allah says that when he took on his father's debt, his father asked, "With full payment?" and he answered, "With full payment," and his father prayed for him. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 204} Bakkar ibn Muhammad says Abd Allah paid thirty thousand dirhams for his father and that before he died his own wealth was valued at about three hundred thousand. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 204} Ibn Awn relates that when Ibn Sirin came by a counterfeit or plated dirham he would not buy with it, and on the day he died he had five hundred such coins. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 200} Al-Dhahabi gives the same report. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3452}

## Shroud and washing
Humayd relates that Ibn Sirin told Suwayd Abu Mahfuz to prepare a striped Yemeni garment in which he would be shrouded. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 204} Ayyub says he used to instruct that the shirt of the dead be given buttons and a hem, and Ayyub says: "I buttoned Muhammad's." {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 205} Hammad ibn Zayd says Muhammad died on Friday, that Ayyub and Ibn Awn washed him, and that he does not know who else was present. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 205}

## His death
Ibn Sa'd reports from Hushaym from Mansur that Ibn Sirin perished a hundred days after al-Hasan, in 110 AH, and from Bakkar ibn Muhammad that he had passed eighty. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 205} Al-Dhahabi quotes Khalid ibn Khidash from Hammad ibn Zayd that he died nine days into Shawwal 110. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3453}

## The grave
The sources read for this record do not identify Ibn Sirin's grave or burial place, so it is not assigned to any particular spot.

## Where the reports differ
Al-Ansari gives the food as the origin of the debt, while Bakkar gives the slave-girl story; Ibn Sa'd does not reconcile them, so the cause of the imprisonment remains disputed. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 197} {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 198} Both accounts agree that he died in 110 AH, after al-Hasan. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 9 p. 205}
