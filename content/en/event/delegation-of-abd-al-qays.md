## Summary
[[group/abd-al-qays|Abd al-Qays]] came in delegation to the Prophet twice: about fourteen men in 5 AH, then forty men in the Year of Delegations, among them al-Jarud ibn al-Ala {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445}.

## The delegation's account
A man of Abd al-Qays called Munqidh ibn Hayyan used to come to Medina for trade; he became Muslim, carried a letter from the Prophet to his people, and they became Muslim and came in delegation in a sacred month, thirteen or fourteen men {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445}. The delegation asked about faith and about drinks; their chief was al-Ashajj al-Asri, of whom the Prophet said: "in you are two qualities God loves — forbearance and deliberateness" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445; sahih-muslim}. The second delegation came in the Year of Delegations, forty men among them al-Jarud ibn al-Ala al-Abdi, who had been a Christian, became Muslim and proved a good Muslim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 445}.

## For young visitors
The tribe of Abd al-Qays came to the Prophet twice from the direction of al-Bahrayn; among them was a man who had been Christian and became Muslim, and a chief the Prophet praised for his forbearance and composure.
