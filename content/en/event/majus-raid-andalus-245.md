## Overview
Al-Maqqari says that Majus ships appeared in al-Andalus in 245 AH. They were met by the ships of the amir Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman and fought them; the Muslims took two of their ships, and a number of them were martyred. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.350-351}

Al-Maqqari describes the Majus as a people of the northern seafarers, and makes this the first known landing of their ships on the coasts of al-Andalus in the caliphate of the Umayyads. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.350-351}

The place in the event data is the coasts of al-Andalus, with a reference to [[place/seville|Seville]].
