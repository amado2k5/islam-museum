## Overview
Mulahiz kills Jarrah ibn Bishr and his brother Muhammad ibn Bishr in Rabi I 300 AH.

## Detail
Mulahiz catches Jarrah ibn Bishr and kills him, with his brother Muhammad ibn Bishr and a group of his companions, on Tuesday two days before the end of Rabi I 300 AH. Ibn al-Fadl withdraws to al-Mudhaykhira on the last day of the month, and Mulahiz returns to Zabid, leaving men in al-Mahjam and al-Kudara.
