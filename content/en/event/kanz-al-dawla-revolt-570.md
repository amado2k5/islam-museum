## Overview
At the beginning of 570 AH [[person/kanz-al-dawla|al-Kanz]] rebelled in Upper Egypt, and a great many of the common people, the Sudanese and the Arabs gathered to him, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.64-65}

## Cause of the campaign
Al-Kanz killed a Salahi amir in his fief, the brother of the amir [[person/abu-al-hayja-al-samin|Abu al-Hayja al-Samin]]. Abu al-Hayja marched to fight him, and sent with him [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] with a group of amirs and many troops. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.64-65}

## The fighting
The army reached the town of Tud, whose people sheltered against them; they fought its people and prevailed. Then the troops marched to al-Kanz and fought him, and he was killed with those with him of the Arabs and others. Ibn al-Athir says that the land was secure after him and its people were at peace. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.64-65}
