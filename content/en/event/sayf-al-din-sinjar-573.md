## Summary
Sayf al-Din Ghazi, lord of Mosul, besieged his brother Imad al-Din Zangi in Sinjar. When Salah al-Din marched toward the Jazira, Sayf al-Din fled before him and returned to Mosul. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 70}
