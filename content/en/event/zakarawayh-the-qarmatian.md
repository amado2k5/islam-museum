## Overview
In 294 AH al-Tabari mentions the report of Zakarawayh ibn Mihrawayh the Qarmati. The news came in Muharram that he had set out from the river al-Muthanna, intending the pilgrims. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.130}

Al-Tabari mentions in the same year that Ibn Kaykhalagh entered Tarsus on a raid at the start of Muharram. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.130}

The details of the report and its outcome are not recorded here, and need an additional source.
