## Overview
The enemy pressed on Hasan ibn Ahmad and Ibn Munir and came between them and their companions. Abu Ja'far then went into the house with the men still with him, and they shut the door on themselves. The narrator's last sight of him before the door closed was of Abu Ja'far with the skirts of his mail gathered up, sword and shield in hand.

## Detail
Hasan ibn Ahmad relates that after they had driven the enemy back they returned to the door of the house, but the enemy pressed them and came between them and their companions. Abu Ja'far then went into the house with the men of his companions who were left, and they shut the door on themselves. Their last sight of him before the door was shut was of him with the skirts of his mail gathered in his belt, sword and shield in hand. Banu al-Harith then surrounded the house. A servant of Ibrahim al-Jumahi had gone in with Abu Ja'far, and when he saw Banu al-Harith he opened the door to them, and they entered the house. Abu Ja'far and his men took refuge on the upper floor.
