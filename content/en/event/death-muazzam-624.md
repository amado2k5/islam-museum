## Summary
In 624 AH al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Isa ibn al-Malik al-Adil Abu Bakr ibn Ayyub, lord of Damascus, died on Friday at the end of Dhu al-Qa'da, and his son al-Nasir Dawud succeeded him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 473}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir, in his notice of the death of the Mu'azzam, lord of Damascus, and the accession of his son, records that he died on Friday at the close of Dhu al-Qa'da of this year, of a dysentery illness; his kingdom of Damascus and its districts had been his established seat within the Ayyubid house since his father al-Adil had arranged his sons' wilayas across the lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 473} On his death his son al-Nasir Dawud became lord of Damascus; what followed between him and his uncle al-Kamil, lord of Egypt, and his uncle al-Ashraf — the dealings of the Ayyubids over Damascus, al-Karak and the rest — Ibn al-Athir narrates in the years that follow. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482}
