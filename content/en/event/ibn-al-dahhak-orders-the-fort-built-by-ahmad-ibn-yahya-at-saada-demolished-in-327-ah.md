## Overview
Ibn al-Dahhak stays in Saada and orders the fort built by Ahmad ibn Yahya demolished, and the Akil give him their obedience. He takes the maks tax from those in the Akil quarter. The Sanaa merchants scatter, and Banu Sa'd write to al-Qasim, who gathers his men again.

## Detail
The book says Ibn al-Dahhak stayed in Saada and ordered the fort that Ahmad ibn Yahya had built there demolished, and attributed this to al-Hasan. The Akil gave him their obedience and asked him to collect the taxes of the town, and he took the maks from those who had entered the Akil quarter, so the people left the town. Banu Sa'd then wrote to him that he had gathered men and caused troubles in the town. They assembled with al-Qasim, who rose with them and with those who answered him from Shakir and the people of Najran. Ibn al-Dahhak, with al-Hasan and his brothers, left for Alaf, and al-Qasim went to al-Ghayl. The book attributes the demolition to al-Hasan in one place and does not state it as its own judgement.
