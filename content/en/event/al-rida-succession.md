## Overview
In 201 AH [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] removed his brother al-Mu'tamin from the succession and made [[person/ali-al-ridha|Ali al-Rida]] ibn Musa al-Kazim heir apparent. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 486-487}

## What al-Ma'mun did
al-Suyuti says that al-Ma'mun wrote about this to all regions, gave Ali the title al-Rida, struck dirhams in his name, and married him to his daughter. He ordered that black be abandoned and green worn. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 486-487}

al-Suyuti attributes this to al-Ma'mun's excessive Shi'ism, to the point that it is said he intended to depose himself and hand over the affair to Ali. This is al-Suyuti's explanation, not the museum's verdict. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 486-487}

## The response of the Abbasids
al-Suyuti says that this weighed very heavily on the Banu al-Abbas. They went out and gave their allegiance to [[person/ibrahim-ibn-al-mahdi|Ibrahim ibn al-Mahdi]], known as al-Mubarak. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 486-487}

## Notes
This is al-Suyuti's account alone. What is not set out here is the view of academic studies and of other sources. The date is derived and needs an additional source.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]], under the year 201, says: in it al-Ma'mun took the oath for Ali ibn Musa ibn Ja'far as caliph after him, deposed al-Qasim ibn Harun, and ordered black to be discarded and green worn. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 470}
