## Summary
Reports say that [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]] ordered Abd al-Rahman ibn Tabi al-Himyari, who was in charge of his estates, to grant [[person/nafi-ibn-al-harith|Nafi ibn al-Harith al-Thaqafi]] land as far as he could walk. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
Nafi walked until his sandal strap broke, and he sat down. Abd al-Rahman said to him: "That is enough." Nafi said: "Had I known, I would have walked on to al-Ubulla." He then asked to be allowed to throw his sandal, and he threw it until it reached the wash-tub. The place is in [[place/basra|Basra]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
