## Overview
Sultan Mehmed I moved the capital of the state from Edirne, 'the city of the ghazis', back to Bursa, 'the city of the jurists'. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 78}

## The city of the jurists
Al-Salabi describes Mehmed I as a lover of peace, learning and the jurists, of noble character and firm resolve, who preferred Bursa over Edirne as his seat; schools and institutes spread through both cities. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 78}
