## Summary
In 601 AH successive quarters of Baghdad erupted in civil strife: many were killed and wounded and houses were looted, until the Diwan sent troops and the city calmed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that on 17 Sha'ban fighting broke out between the people of Bab al-Azaj and al-Ma'muniyya after the former killed a man and tried to parade his body; battle raged at the Great Orchard, and the next day swords and arrows were used and nearby houses were looted, until al-Rukn ibn Abd al-Qadir and Yusuf worked to pacify the crowds and the Turks camped under the watchtower. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297} On the twentieth another fitna broke out between the people of Qatfata and al-Qarya on the west side for the same reason, and troops from the Diwan ended it; on 9 Ramadan a fitna between the people of Suq al-Sultan and the Ja'fariyya began with a quarrel between two men and a battle at the Ja'fariyya cemetery. When the outbreaks multiplied, a senior mamluk amir of the caliph patrolled with a large force, killed suspected men, and the people quieted. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297}
