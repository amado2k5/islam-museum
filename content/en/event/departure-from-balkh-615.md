## Summary
About 615/1218 the jurist and mystic Baha al-Din Walad left Balkh with his family, including his son Jalal al-Din; two reasons are given — a quarrel with the ruler, or fear of the approaching Mongols. {cite:britannica-rumi}

## The road
The family passed through Nishapur, where the famous account has the Sufi poet Farid al-Din Attar meet the boy Jalal al-Din and bless him; they then made the pilgrimage to Mecca and travelled through Syria and Iraq until they reached Anatolia, the land of the Seljuqs of Rum, which enjoyed peace and prosperity. {cite:britannica-rumi}
