## Overview
After the meeting at al-Ghayl, Hamdan asked al-Hadi to give them some days and he waited on them, staying at [[place/bayt-dhud-yemen|Bayt Dhu'd]]; the water ran short there and he moved to a place called Falj in al-Zahir, where he stayed until the new moon of Jumada II {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.246}. He then went to [[place/raydah-yemen|Raydah]] and stayed some days, then to [[place/madar-yemen|Madar]] and stayed some days, and went on to the east of [[group/khawlan-tribe|Khawlan]], where some Khawlanis met him, and halted at a village called Sabal, put an army there and stayed two days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.246}.

## Detail
News then came that [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibn Khalaf]] had halted at a village called Bayt Aqb, and al-Hadi, afraid for his army at Sabal, left by night until he halted at Bayt Hadir, where he stayed some time waiting for aid from al-Ja'fari and the two sons of al-Ruwayya. They delayed and were slow, supplies grew short in the place where the camp was, and many of the people dropped away. An army came out of Sana'a and confronted the people of Sabal; they fought for an hour, wounds were given and no one died {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.246}. Before this is [[event/al-hadi-halts-at-khiwan-and-meets-the-sons-of-yafur-at-al-ghayl-290|the meeting at al-Ghayl]]; after it, [[event/the-fights-near-bayt-hadir-and-the-withdrawal-by-matara-and-madar-to-atwa-290|the fights near Bayt Hadir and the withdrawal to Atwa]]. In a footnote the editor says the original and Ghayat al-Amani 1/188 give "Salil", a tribe in Yemen after which the land is called; the place may be the [[place/sabal-sanaa-yemen|Sabal]] where Abdullah ibn al-Husayn camped in 289 AH, but the museum does not say so. For Falj in al-Zahir the editor refers to the Mu'jam al-Buldan. The narrator does not name al-Ja'fari or the two sons of al-Ruwayya, who may be Abu al-Ushayra and Rabi'.
