## Overview
The Qarmatian who lived at Jabal Musur dies in Jumada II 302 AH, and his son keeps his place.

## Detail
The Qarmatian who lived at Jabal Musur dies on Saturday, eleven nights into Jumada II 302 AH. His son Abu al-Hasan stays in his place with his brothers, and no one disputes what they hold. The editor's note quotes al-Khazraji that after al-Mansur the affairs passed to one of his companions.
