## Summary
Yaqut travelled to Egypt with a cargo of raw cloth and returned with another cargo that brought him profit — his second Aleppo–Egypt–Damascus journey, in 624/1227 {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}

## Merchant and scholar
Yaqut always joined trade and learning in a single journey: he sold and bought books and goods, met scholars and discovered libraries; his travels were all for trade and for knowledge at once {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.
