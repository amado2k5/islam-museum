## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that al-Jarrah, governor of Armenia, fought the Khazars for three days near [[place/ardabil|Ardabil]], and he and his companions were killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
[[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah]] made peace with the people of Hamzin, on condition that they be moved to Khayzan, and he attacked the people of Ghumik, took captives from them, and then dispersed his army at Barda'a and al-Baylaqan. The [[group/khazars|Khazars]] surged up, crossed the Ras, and then withdrew toward Ardabil. He met them four parasangs away on the side toward Armenia, and they fought for three days. The river was named the River of al-Jarrah, and a bridge was named after it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Dating
The event took place in al-Jarrah's governorship, perhaps around 112 AH, and the Gregorian conversion is derived. The date needs an additional source.
