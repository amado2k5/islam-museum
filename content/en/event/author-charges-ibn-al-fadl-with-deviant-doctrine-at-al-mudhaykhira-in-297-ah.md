## Overview
Back at al-Mudhaykhira, the author charges Ibn al-Fadl with professing Magianism, ordering acts that Islam forbids, and killing those who refused. The museum records these as the author's charges, not as established facts.

## Detail
The book says that at al-Mudhaykhira Ibn al-Fadl professed Magianism, ordered marriage within forbidden degrees and the drinking of wine, declared the lawful forbidden and the forbidden lawful, and had those who refused killed. It adds that his orders covered acts against women which the author calls forbidden, and that he rejected what Islam teaches and claimed a title that belongs to God alone. These are the author's own charges, written from al-Hadi's side. The detailed account of acts against women is not reproduced here.
