## Overview
After the fighting with [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]] in [[event/banu-mamar-come-down-on-khiwan-on-15-shaban-and-are-routed-286]] and the coming of a party of Banu Rabi'a on the Tuesday, "when Friday came" [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] told his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]] "to lead the people in prayer". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} The account is Zaydi, in the narrator's voice; the link between this Abu al-Qasim and the Muhammad ibn al-Hadi known as al-Murtada is probable and not certain, since the book says only "Abu al-Qasim".

## The address
Abu al-Qasim went to the mosque, "praised God Most High and extolled Him", mentioned the Prophet, and in his address "spoke of jihad in the way of God, urged the people to it and encouraged them, spoke of the merit of him who undertakes it and what God promised him who stands with him, with much other talk", and called them to the way of the hereafter "until their hearts were humbled by it and they benefited from his words". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} This is the narrator's wording of the effect of the address and the museum gives it as attributed. Then he prayed and went home. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139}

## Where it sits
It is a Friday address in a town the imam held and in the middle of a war with a group of a Hamdani clan. The book gives no text of it, so only what the narrator describes is related here. The mosque is not named on this page.
