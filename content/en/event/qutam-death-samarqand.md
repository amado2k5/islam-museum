## Overview
[[person/qutam-ibn-al-abbas|Qutham ibn al-Abbas]] was with Sa'id ibn Uthman in Khurasan, and died in Samarqand. Al-Baladhuri says it is said that he was martyred there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What the source says
Qutham came to Sa'id ibn Uthman in Khurasan, and Sa'id offered him five shares of the booty. He said: no, but give me one share for myself and one for my horse. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

When news of his death reached Abd Allah ibn Abbas, he said: "How far apart are his birth and his grave." Then he began to pray, and recited the verse about seeking help through patience and prayer. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
