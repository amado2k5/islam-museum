## Overview
In Rajab 283 al-Mu'tadid ordered the Dujayl canal dredged and cleared of a rock blocking its mouth that kept the water back; four thousand dinars were gathered for it from the holders of iqta's and estates and spent on it {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 163}.
