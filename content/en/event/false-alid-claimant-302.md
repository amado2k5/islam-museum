## Overview
In Dhu'l-Qa'da 302 a man was brought before al-Muqtadir bi'llah claiming to be a son of al-Rida the Alid; his case was examined and he proved to be a son of al-Dab'i — so he was paraded through both sides of the city and imprisoned {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 420}.
