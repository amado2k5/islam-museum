## Overview
The narrator says: the report returns to the imprisonment of Abu al-Qasim. When they brought him into the house they stood him in the porch a little, then put him in a room where they kept him until near evening, then brought a heavy fetter and fettered him; he stayed alone two days, his companions having been jailed in the prison, then they brought [[person/muhammad-ibn-said-ibn-yusuf|Muhammad ibn Sa'id]], who had been taken with him, and put him with him on the third day, and the two were together and stayed at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.252}. A sickness came on Abu al-Qasim's leg, which swelled badly; they told the jailers and the fetter was loosened for some days until the leg healed, and then the fetter was put back; a messenger from al-Hadi kept coming and they let him reach Abu al-Qasim {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.252}.

## Detail
Abu al-Qasim asked [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibn Khalaf]] to let Ibn Sa'id speak with him, and he ordered him brought out to him, and a long talk passed between him and Ibn Sa'id; Ibn Khalaf said, tell Abu al-Qasim to write to his father, perhaps matters will draw near, and the letter was written and sent, but one of his companions disliked it, so the letter was stopped and not sent on {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.252}. Ibn Khalaf had jailed a man of Yafi', one of the best of them, called [[person/isa-ibn-maan-al-yafii|Isa ibn Ma'an al-Yafi'i]]; when he left Bayt Bus he kept working at the prison until he got out of it, which weighed hard on [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]], so they moved Abu al-Qasim and his companion to another house, and the two stayed at Sana'a Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan and ten days of Shawwal, and then they took them out by night, put them in a litter and went with them until they reached Bayt Bus, where they stayed some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.252}. Before this is [[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-is-taken-and-led-through-sanaa-290|his capture and journey to Sana'a]]; after it, [[event/jaftam-is-seized-at-arthal-and-jailed-at-bayt-bus-290|the seizing of Jaftam and his jailing at Bayt Bus]]. The book's formula against Ibn Khalaf is the narrator's and the museum does not repeat it. The book does not say who the companion was who stopped the letter, and the sentence on al-Yafi'i leaving Bayt Bus is too unclear for the museum to explain, so it is given as printed.
