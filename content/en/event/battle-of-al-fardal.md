## Overview
al-Tabari, in the account for 251 AH, reports that Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Muzaffar ibn Sisil ordered the army to camp at al-Yasiriyya, then moved to al-Kunasa until al-Fardal ibn Izankajik al-Ashrusani met him there. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.332}
