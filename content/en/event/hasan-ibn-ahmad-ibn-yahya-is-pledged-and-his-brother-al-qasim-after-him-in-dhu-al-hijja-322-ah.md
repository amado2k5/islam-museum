## Overview
Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Yahya is pledged, and his brother al-Qasim after him, in Dhu al-Hijja 322 AH.

## Detail
Among the Alawis, al-Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Yahya rises, the people pledge to him, and they pledge his brother al-Qasim ibn Ahmad on Monday, nine days before the end of Dhu al-Hijja, the last month of that year.
