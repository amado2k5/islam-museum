## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that the sultan [[person/masud-ibn-muhammad-saljuqi|Mas'ud]] took the riding animals, furniture, gold and curtains of the caliph's residence, and left in its stables only four horses and eight mules. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.671-672}

## The demand and the caliph's reply
He also says that they gave their allegiance to [[person/al-muqtafi-billah|al-Muqtafi]] on condition that he should not have horses or travel equipment; this is a report introduced by "it is said". Then in 531 AH the sultan seized the caliph's estates, and sent his vizier to demand a hundred thousand dinars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.671-672}

Al-Muqtafi replied that al-Mustarshid had gone to him with his wealth, that al-Rashid had done what he did and taken what was left, and that the sultan had taken all the furniture and disposed of the mint, the estates and the poll-tax funds. He said that he had sworn to God that he would not take a single grain from the Muslims by injustice. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.671-672}

## The end
The sultan stopped taking from the caliph. In Jumada al-Ula his lands, his dealings and the estates were returned to him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.671-672}
