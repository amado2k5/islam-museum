## Overview
Marand was a small village where Halbas, father of al-Ba'ith, settled. Al-Ba'ith and his son [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-buayth|Muhammad ibn al-Buayth]] fortified it and built palaces there. In the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil Muhammad rebelled, and [[person/buna-al-saghir|Buna al-Saghir]], client of the commander of the faithful, fought him until he defeated him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The result
Buna took Muhammad to Samarra, and demolished the wall of [[place/marand|Marand]] and that palace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Lineage
The source says that al-Ba'ith was of the descendants of Utayb ibn Amr ibn Wahb ibn Afsa ibn Di'mi ibn Judayla ibn Asad ibn Rabi'a, and gives another view: Utayb ibn Awf ibn Sinan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
