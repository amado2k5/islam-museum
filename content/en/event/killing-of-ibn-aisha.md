## Overview
In 210 AH the caliph al-Ma'mun captured Ibn Aisha and his companions, and then Ibn Aisha was killed and crucified. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.602} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.603}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Ma'mun rode to the prison at night until Ibn Aisha was killed, and that he was the first Abbasid to be crucified in Islam. Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Ifriqi was killed with him. He quotes a verse that al-Ma'mun recited when he killed him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.428}

What is not covered here: the reason for his arrest and the details of the charge. This needs an additional source.
