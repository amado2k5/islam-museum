## Summary
In Rabi' al-Awwal 375 AH the Qaramita army attacked [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. They defeated its governor, al-Ghassawi ibn al-Sawda, who fled to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]], and they entered the city. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.145}

## The Alid banner
On Friday the Alid banner was raised there, and a proclamation was made calling people to ride out in obedience. Three days later its people were ordered to make for the wool market of Rabi'a (Suf Rabi'a), and a fierce clash broke out between the Alids and the Qaramita, shedding much blood between them. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.145}
