## Overview
The annual gathering of the Tablighi Jamaat on the banks of the Turag river near [[place/dhaka|Dhaka]]: millions of worshippers gather for days of invitation, remembrance and preaching, and it is the second largest annual Muslim gathering after the Hajj. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
It began in 1954 and became a worldwide Bangladeshi institution; it divides the days into stages so that people come from all the continents. {cite:wikidata}
