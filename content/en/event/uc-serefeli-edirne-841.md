## Overview
Murad II built the Üç Şerefeli Mosque between 841/1437-8 and 851/1447-8, the largest Ottoman edifice before the conquest of Constantinople {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.

## Largest Ottoman building before the conquest
The mosque takes its name from the tallest of its four minarets, which has three balconies; its marble-paved courtyard is counted the first Ottoman mosque courtyard. Evliya Çelebi says it cost 7,000 purses from the booty of the conquest of Izmir {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.
