## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that when [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] became governor of Kufa, he appointed [[person/al-bara-ibn-azib|al-Bara' ibn Azib]] over Qazwin, and ordered him, if God granted its conquest at his hand, to raid the Daylam from it. He set out with [[person/hanzala-ibn-zayd|Hanzala ibn Zayd al-Khayl]] until he came to Abhar. They fought him, then asked for safe-conduct and he granted it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fort of Qazwin
al-Bara' raided [[place/qazwin|Qazwin]]. Its people sought help from the Daylam, but the Daylam stood on the mountain and did not stretch out a hand. They asked for peace, disdained the jizya, and showed Islam. There are two accounts of their affair: they settled in Kufa and allied themselves with Zuhra ibn Hawiya, or they became Muslims and stayed in place, and their land became tithed land. al-Bara' posted five hundred Muslim men among them, among them [[person/tulayha-ibn-khuwaylid|Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then he raided the Daylam until they paid the tribute, and he took Zanjan by force.
