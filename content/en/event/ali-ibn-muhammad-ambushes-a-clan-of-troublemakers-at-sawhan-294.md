## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah was told that a group of troublemakers from a clan spent the night at Sawhan. Ali ibn Muhammad lay in ambush on a mountain above the village and took two of them. His father then demolished the houses of those who had not been caught, and the army returned to al-Hijr.

## Detail
The book says that a group who had been spreading corruption and showing wrongdoing were hunted and scattered. Muhammad was told they sheltered at Sawhan at night. He sent his son Ali with a detachment and ordered him to lie in ambush on a mountain above Sawhan. Ali attacked a place where some of the wanted men were and took two of them, then brought them to his father at Sawhan, who was demolishing the houses of those not caught. The army went back to al-Hijr, and the captured men of Banu al-Harith who had caused trouble were sent to al-Hadi at Sa'da. The name of the clan is not repeated in this record.
