## Overview
In 960 the Mizab al-Rahma of the Sacred House was rebuilt; Abu Bakr al-Yaqim al-Makki coined its chronogram: "O exalted Lord, to whom belongs the Mosque — behold the standing water-spout — God's house-spout is renewed, so we drew — mercy, and the date, from Thy Lord" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 325}.
