## Overview
The battle took place on the plains of Kosovo on 17 October 852 AH / 1448 CE. It lasted three days and ended in a great victory for the Ottomans under [[person/murad-ii-uthmani|Murad II]]. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.82}

## Its effect
al-Sallabi says that the battle removed Hungary, for at least ten years, from the states able to launch offensive operations against the Ottomans. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.82}
