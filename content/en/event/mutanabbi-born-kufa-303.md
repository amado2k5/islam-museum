## Overview
Abu al-Tayyib Ahmad ibn al-Husayn was born in the Kinda quarter of Kufa in 303/915. His family, though in very humble circumstances, claimed descent from the Yamani clan of al-Ju'fi, and he grew up convinced of the superiority of the southern Arabs; schooled in his native town, he distinguished himself by his intelligence, prodigious memory and precocity as a poet {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 769}.
