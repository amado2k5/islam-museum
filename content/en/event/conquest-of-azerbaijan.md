## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba came to Kufa as governor on Umar's behalf, with a letter for [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] appointing him over [[place/azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]]. Hudhayfa marched to Ardabil, and its people fought him for days, and then he made peace with the marzban for eight hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Terms of the peace
The conditions were that none of them be killed or enslaved, that no fire-temple be demolished, that the Kurds of al-Blasjan, Sabalan and Satrudan not be molested, and that the people of al-Shiz not be prevented from dancing at their festivals. Hudhayfa then raided Muqan and Jilan and made peace with them for a tribute. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Governors after him
Umar dismissed Hudhayfa and appointed [[person/utba-ibn-farqad|Utba ibn Farqad]]. He found the people of Ardabil keeping to the covenant, and he raided the districts that had broken it. al-Waqidi relates that al-Mughira conquered it by force in 22 AH, and Ibn al-Kalbi says that he conquered it in 20 AH. When [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn Uqba]] dismissed Utba, they broke the covenant, and he raided them in 25 AH and made peace with them on the terms of Hudhayfa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Afterwards they broke it again under [[person/al-ashath-ibn-qays|al-Ash'ath ibn Qays]], and he pursued them district by district and settled Arabs from the stipend-holders there. Then [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Sa'id ibn al-As]] raided them and struck Muqan and Jilan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
