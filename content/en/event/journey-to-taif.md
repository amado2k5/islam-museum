In Shawwal of the tenth year of the prophethood — after the deaths of Abu Talib and Khadija and with Quraysh grown bold against him — the Prophet set out on foot for al-Ta'if seeking Thaqif's support, his client [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] with him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He sought out three brothers of their chiefs — Abd Yalil, Mas'ud and Habib, the sons of Amr ibn Umayr — offered them Islam and asked for their support; they returned him a vile answer and incited their rabble and slaves against him, who lined up in two rows pelting him with stones until his feet bled. Zayd ibn Haritha shielded him with his own body until he was cut on the head with wounds, and they drove him into a walled garden of Utba and Shayba, the sons of Rabi'a. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When he had settled under the shade of a grapevine he made his famous supplication: "O God, to You I complain of the weakness of my strength, the scarcity of my means and my lowliness before the people." The sons of Rabi'a took pity on him and sent with their Christian servant [[person/addas|Addas]] a cluster of grapes; when the Prophet named God before eating and answered Addas's question about his town, assigning him to the people of Yunus ibn Matta, Addas fell forward kissing his head, hands and feet, declaring his Islam. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Then he turned back toward Mecca, halting at Wadi Nakhla for days; God sent him a band of jinn to hear the Qur'an while he prayed the dawn prayer — when they attended him they said "be silent", and when it ended they turned back to their people as warners. Then he entered Mecca under the protection of [[person/muthim-ibn-adi|al-Mut'im ibn Adi]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
