## The story
Abu Dawud relates that the dead person hears the stamping of the two angels' sandals as they turn away, and is asked 'this one — who is your Lord? What is your religion? Who is your prophet?' If he answers they say 'we have known you' and he is furnished from Paradise; if he refuses he is struck with an iron hammer [[passage/abudawud-4753|Abu Dawud 4753]].

## In Muslim, Tirmidhi and Nasa'i
Muslim, Tirmidhi and Nasa'i carry other versions of the grave questioning, in which the two angels ask the dead three times each; the believer answers 'I testify that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is His messenger' {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:sunan-al-nasai}.

## Notes
These reports belong to the matters of the unseen reported by permission of God; the books of hadith place them in the chapters of asceticism and heart-softening, and this browser gathers them with the rest of the angels' meetings with human beings, linking them to the 'honourable scribes' and the 'angel of death' records [[passage/abudawud-4753|Abu Dawud 4753]] {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.
