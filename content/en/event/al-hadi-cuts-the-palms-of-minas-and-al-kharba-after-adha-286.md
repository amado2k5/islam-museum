## Overview
Some days after the feast [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went to [[place/minas-najran|Minas]] to pull it down, and none of its people came out to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.177} When the news of al-Hadi reached [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]], his threats to them in verse and what happened at Minas when he fell from his horse, it distressed them greatly, their hearts filled with fear and they shut themselves in their forts. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.177} He kept going out to their palms and cutting them, and none of them came out to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.177} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178}

## Al-Kharba
One day he went out to a place called [[place/al-kharba-najran|al-Kharba]] and ordered the palms there cut, then went back to his camp. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} The book does not locate al-Kharba and the editor gives no note on it, so it has no coordinates here.
