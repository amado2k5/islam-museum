## Overview
The battle of al-Yamama is part of the [[event/ridda-wars|wars of the Ridda]]. Abu Bakr sent [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] to [[place/al-yamama|al-Yamama]] to fight [[person/musaylima|Musaylima]], and that was in 12 AH according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.120-127}

## The battle
al-Baladhuri relates that al-Rajjal ibn Unfuwa was the first to meet the Muslims, and he was killed. Notable men and reciters of the Qur'an were martyred, then the Muslims rallied and God sent down His help. The Muslims drove their enemies into a garden called that day [[place/hadiqat-al-mawt|the Garden of Death]], and Musaylima was killed there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.120-127}

Reports differ over who killed Musaylima, and those named include Khidash ibn Bashir, Abd Allah ibn Zayd ibn Tha'laba, Abu Dujana, and [[person/wahshi-ibn-harb|Wahshi ibn Harb]]. Some say they all took part in killing him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.120-127}

## Among the martyrs
al-Baladhuri names among the martyrs [[person/zayd-ibn-al-khattab|Zayd ibn al-Khattab]], [[person/thabit-ibn-qays|Thabit ibn Qays]], Abu Hudhayfa ibn Utba, and Salim, client of Abu Hudhayfa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.120-127}

## The settlement
[[person/mujjaa-ibn-murara|Mujja'a ibn Murara]] told Khalid that most of the people of al-Yamama had not come out to fight him, and he made peace with him. al-Baladhuri's account ends with Khalid appointing a deputy over al-Yamama. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.120-127}

The Gregorian date is derived. The details of the settlement need an additional source.
