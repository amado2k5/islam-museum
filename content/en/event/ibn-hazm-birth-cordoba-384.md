## Overview
[[person/ibn-hazm|Abu Muhammad Ibn Hazm]] was born in the Munyat al-Mughira quarter of [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] on the last night of a Wednesday, the last day of Ramadan, in 384 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 80}

## Context
He came from a family of officials, and he combined the study of hadith, jurisprudence and literature, and became one of the most learned men of al-Andalus. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 80-81}
