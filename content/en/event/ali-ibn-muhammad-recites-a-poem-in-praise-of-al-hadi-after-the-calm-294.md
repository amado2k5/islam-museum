## Overview
Once Najran was settled, Ali ibn Muhammad al-Alawi recited a long poem in praise of al-Hadi, speaking of his knowledge, his restoring of the truth, his descent from Ali and Fatima as the poet frames it, and the deeds of his supporters.

## Detail
The book says the poem was spoken after the town grew calm, the truth appeared and falsehood faded. The poem speaks of al-Hadi as one favoured with knowledge, who enjoined good and called people to righteousness; of his descent, as the poet presents it, from Ali and Fatima; of his fighting against hypocrites and opponents; and of his supporters, whom the poet calls the sons of the one slain at Karbala. The scanned text is partly broken, so this record gives the themes and not the verses.
