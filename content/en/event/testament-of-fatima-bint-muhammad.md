## Summary
[[person/fatima|Fatima]], the Prophet's daughter, died in Medina about six months after her father on the soundest account in Ibn Kathir, on the night of Tuesday when three nights of Ramadan had passed, in the year 11, in al-Waqidi's report. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942} This record deals with what the books relate of her instructions about her washing and bier, her burial by night, the accounts of who prayed over her, and the disagreement about where her grave lies. The general account of her death is in the record [[event/death-of-fatima-bint-muhammad|The death and burial of Fatima bint Muhammad]].

## Her instructions about washing and the bier
Al-Dhahabi relates, from Umm Jafar, that Fatima told [[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma bint Umays]] that she found distasteful what was done to women, when a cloth is thrown over a woman and outlines her. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98} Asma said to her: shall I show you something I saw in Abyssinia? She called for fresh palm fronds, bent them, and threw a cloth over them. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98} Fatima said: how fine and how beautiful this is; when I die, wash me, you and Ali, and let no one come in to me. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98} Al-Dhahabi cites Ibn Abd al-Barr that she was the first in Islam whose bier was covered in that fashion. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98} Ibn Kathir says she charged Asma to wash her. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717}

Al-Dhahabi adds that when she died [[person/aisha|Aisha]] came to go in; Asma said: do not come in. She complained to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], who came, stood at the door and spoke to Asma; she said: she ordered me; he said: then do as she ordered you, and went away. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98}

## Who washed her
Ibn Kathir says Asma washed her, with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] and Salma Umm Rafi, and some say al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib too. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} Sifat al-Safwa says Ali washed her and prayed over her. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 4 p. 331} Ibn Hajar relates from Ibn Sad, through Muhammad ibn Musa, that Ali washed Fatima. {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942}

Ibn Kathir also mentions another report, that she bathed herself before her death and charged that she not be washed after that, and he judges it weak and not to be relied on. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} It appears in al-Isaba from the report of Salma Umm Rafi, in which she bathed, said that no one should uncover a shoulder of hers, and Ali buried her in that washing. {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942} Al-Dhahabi comments on this report: this is munkar. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98}

## The prayer over her
The reports differ on who prayed over her. Ibn Kathir says it was her husband Ali, and it is also said her uncle al-Abbas, and it is said Abu Bakr. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} Al-Isaba relays from Ibn Sad, through Amra, that al-Abbas prayed over Fatima and went down into her grave with Ali and al-Fadl ibn al-Abbas, and, through Ali ibn al-Husayn, that Ali prayed over her and buried her by night after people had settled. {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942} Ibn Hajar relays from al-Waqidi, through al-Shabi, that Abu Bakr prayed over her, and says: this is weak and broken. {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942} He adds that some abandoned transmitters narrated something similar from Malik from Jafar ibn Muhammad from his father, and that al-Daraqutni and Ibn Adi declared it weak. {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942} In Siyar Alam al-Nubala al-Waqidi is quoted: al-Abbas went in and went down into her grave with Ali and al-Fadl. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 97}

## Burial by night
Al-Dhahabi relates from al-Zuhri, from Urwa, from Aisha, that Fatima lived six months after the Prophet and was buried by night, and says al-Waqidi held this the best attested of the accounts. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 97} Ibn Kathir says the sound view is what is established in the Sahih from Aisha, that she lived six months after him, and lists other views from two to eight months. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} Sifat al-Safwa gives views of three months and two months and says the first is sounder. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 4 p. 331}

## Between Abu Bakr and Fatima
Ibn Kathir mentions that she felt aggrieved with Abu Bakr over matters connected with her inheritance and kept her displeasure for the rest of her life; when she fell ill he came and sought to please her, and she was content. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} Al-Dhahabi gives a close report: Abu Bakr came to Fatima when she was ill and asked leave, she admitted him, he made his excuses and spoke with her, and she was content with him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 3 p. 98} For the inheritance question see [[event/fatima-inheritance-claim-fadak|Fatima's inheritance claim and Fadak]].

## Grave and resting place
Ibn Kathir reports that she was buried in al-Baqi. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} Ibn Hajar, however, relays that al-Waqidi asked Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Mawali whether it is true that people say Fatima's grave is in al-Baqi; he answered that she was buried only in a corner of the house of Aqil, with seven cubits between her grave and the road. {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942} Elsewhere Ibn Kathir relays from al-Khatib al-Baghdadi a report from Abu Nuaym that al-Hasan and al-Husayn moved Ali to Medina and buried him in al-Baqi beside Fatima's grave. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 321} The sources I read therefore do not settle where the grave is.

## Where the reports differ
The sources agree on burial by night. They differ on the prayer leader between Ali, al-Abbas and Abu Bakr, on who went down into the grave, on the place of the grave between al-Baqi and a corner of Aqil's house, and on how long she lived after the Prophet. They agree that her washing followed her instruction to Asma and was done with Ali's help, and differ on whether al-Abbas took part. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 717} {cite:isaba scan vol. 2 p. 1942}
