## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] made peace with the people of [[place/al-hira|al-Hira]] in 12 AH, after he had taken Zandurd, Darna and Hurmuzjird and made peace with the people of Alis. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The road to al-Hira
Khalid passed by Zandurd from Kaskar and took it, and came to Alis, where Jaban came out to him with a great host. [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] advanced to meet him and met him at the River of Blood. Khalid made peace with the people of Alis on condition that they be spies for the Muslims against the Persians and guides and helpers. Then Azadhbih, lord of the outposts of Khosrow, met him; the Muslims fought him and routed him. Khalid halted at Khafan and marched to al-Hira. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
[[person/abd-al-masih-ibn-amr|Abd al-Masih ibn Amr]], [[person/hani-ibn-qabisa-al-shaybani|Hani ibn Qabisa al-Shaybani]] and [[person/iyas-ibn-qabisa|Iyas ibn Qabisa]] came out to Khalid. They made peace with him for a hundred thousand dirhams each year, and it is also said eighty thousand, on condition that they be spies for the Muslims against the Persians, and that no church or palace of theirs be demolished. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

al-Baladhuri relates from Abu Mikhnaf a dialogue between Khalid and Abd al-Masih, who was a very old man, which ended in agreement on a hundred thousand to be paid each year. This was the first money brought from Iraq to Medina. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The daughter of Buqayla
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/khuraym-ibn-aws|Khuraym ibn Aws]] said that the Prophet had granted him the daughter of Buqayla if al-Hira were conquered. Khalid excluded her from the treaty and handed her over to him, and her people bought her from him for a thousand dirhams. He then relates that the questioner was a man of Rabi'a, and says: "The first account is more firmly established." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
