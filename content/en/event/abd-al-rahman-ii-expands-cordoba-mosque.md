## Overview
[[person/abd-al-rahman-ii-andalusi|Abd al-Rahman II]] greatly enlarged the [[building/great-mosque-cordoba|mosque's]] court and aisles in the first half of the ninth century to take the growing city's congregation. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}

## What happened
When Cordoba's people multiplied under Abd al-Rahman al-Awsat and the mosque grew too small for them, he extended its building until it held them — the first great enlargement after his grandfather al-Dakhil's foundation. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}
