## Summary
Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Isma'il ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Mughira ibn Bardizba al-Ju'fi al-Bukhari was born at Bukhara in Shawwal 194 and grew up an orphan {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 3, 14}; his nisba al-Ju'fi comes from his great-grandfather al-Mughira, a mawla of Yaman al-Ju'fi the governor of Bukhara at whose hands he accepted Islam {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1360}, and his father Isma'il was one of the scrupulous scholars {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 3}.
