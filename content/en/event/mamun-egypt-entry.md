## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] set out in 215 AH to raid the Byzantines, took the fortress of Qurra by force and the fortress of Majida, then marched to Damascus. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.488}

## Egypt
He returned in 216 AH to the Byzantines and took several fortresses, then went to Egypt and entered it. Al-Suyuti holds that he was the first Abbasid caliph to enter it. He then returned to Damascus and the Byzantine frontier in 217 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.488}
