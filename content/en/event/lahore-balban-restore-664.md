## Overview
The sultan Ghiyath al-Din Balban restored Lahore soon after his accession to the throne of Delhi in 664/1266, following the ruin left by the Mongol sack of 639/1241; it nevertheless remained for decades a frontier region exposed to regular Mongol attacks {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 38}.
