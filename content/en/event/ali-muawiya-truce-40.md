## Summary
In 40 AH, after a long exchange of letters, a truce took place between Ali and Mu'awiya: war was to be set aside, Ali would hold Iraq and Mu'awiya would hold Syria, and neither would enter the other's territory by raid. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 735}.
