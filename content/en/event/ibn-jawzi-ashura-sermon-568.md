## Overview
On Ashura, 10 Muharram 568 (1 September 1172), Ibn al-Jawzi preached to a very large crowd; the same year al-Mustadi' authorised sermons in the caliph's presence at the Badr gate {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 783}.
