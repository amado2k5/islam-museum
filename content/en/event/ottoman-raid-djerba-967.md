## Summary
Piyale Pasha's attack on Djerba, Ramadan 967/May 1560 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249}.

## Details
The fleet achieved its aims against Spain — which did not scruple to call in France — then, at [[person/turgut-reis|Dragut]]'s persuasion, turned to Tripoli to crush the rising near Tajura, entering like a conqueror with enemy flags drooping {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 249}.
