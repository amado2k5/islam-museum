## Summary
The First Fijar of the Ansar is named for the treachery of the Aws against three hostage boys. The Khazraj gathered under Abd Allah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul and met the Aws, led by Abu Qays ibn al-Aslat, at al-Hada'iq. They fought until some of them almost destroyed one another. Qays ibn al-Khatim was in his orchard. He turned back and found that his people had already gone out, and he could get no weapon but a sword. He went out with it, fought bravely, was badly wounded, and his standing grew. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 506}
