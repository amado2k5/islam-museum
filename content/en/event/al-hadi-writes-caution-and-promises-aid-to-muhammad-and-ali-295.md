## Overview
Al-Hadi wrote to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and his son Ali ordering them to be vigilant, to hold off the rebels, to be patient in hardship, and promising them support. Their minds were eased.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi wrote to Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and to his son Ali ibn Muhammad, ordering them to take care, hold off the rebels and wait to see what came of al-Musawwid. He promised them support and told them to be patient in hardship. Their minds were eased on receiving the letter. The book then says that Banu al-Harith and Ya'am kept up their rising for four months, openly and in secret, and that only their fortifying of their forts, against the expected coming of al-Musawwid, held them back from open revolt. When al-Musawwid's march reached al-Kadar, it became clear that he was occupied elsewhere, and the rebels wrote to him asking for reinforcement.
