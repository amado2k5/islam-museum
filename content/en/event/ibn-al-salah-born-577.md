## Summary
Taqi al-Din Abu 'Amr 'Uthman b. 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Uthman b. Musa al-Kurdi al-Shahrazuri — Ibn al-Salah — was born in 577/1181 at Sharakhan, a village in the Irbil district near Shahrazur. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}

## A house of learning
His father Abu al-Qasim Salah al-Din al-Nasri was the shaykh of that district, so the boy grew up in a house of learning and Shafi'i fiqh. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 229}
