## Summary
The ten-year truce between [[person/charles-v|Charles V]] and France, Muharram 945/June 1538 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 224}.

## Consequence
The relative calm it spread over Europe freed Charles for his great 1541 expedition against Algiers. The book's printed text reads '1358', plainly a misprint for 1538 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 224}.
