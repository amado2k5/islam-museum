## Summary
In 552 AH Nur al-Din defeated the Franks at Safed in a great battle {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}.

## Conquests that followed
Nur al-Din took Gaza and Banyas from the Franks and took Shayzar from the Banu Munqidh {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}.
