## Overview
Al-Mutanabbi left Kufa towards the end of 316/928, probably after the town was again sacked by the Karmatians. He went to Baghdad and became panegyrist to his compatriot Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah al-Alawi, then moved to Syria where for two years he led the life of a wandering troubadour, addressing Bedouin chiefs around Manbij and the educated circles of Tripoli and Latakia; the poems of this apprenticeship are hurried and mediocre, though his real genius already shows through {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 769-770}.
