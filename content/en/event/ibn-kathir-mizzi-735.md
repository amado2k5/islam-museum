## Summary
Ibn Kathir kept close to the hafiz of the age, Jamal al-Din al-Mizzi, at Damascus, read the Tahdhib al-Kamal to him and married his daughter {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
## The marriage tie
His wife was Zaynab, daughter of the hafiz al-Mizzi, as the editor of al-Subki's Tabaqat notes {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 394}; the companionship lasted until al-Mizzi's death in 742.
