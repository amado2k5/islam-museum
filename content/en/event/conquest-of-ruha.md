## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates on the authority of Sulayman ibn Ata that when [[person/iyyad-ibn-ghanm|Iyad ibn Ghanm]] conquered Edessa, which Abu Ubayda had sent him to, he stood at its gate. They made peace with him: that they keep their sanctuary and what was around it, that they not build any church beyond what they already had, and that they help the Muslims against their enemy. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.236}

If they left any of the conditions imposed on them, they would have no protection (dhimma). The people of al-Jazira entered into what the people of Edessa had entered into. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.236}

See [[concept/dhimma|dhimma]]. The conquest is part of the [[event/conquest-of-jazira|conquest of al-Jazira]]. The date is approximate and derived.
