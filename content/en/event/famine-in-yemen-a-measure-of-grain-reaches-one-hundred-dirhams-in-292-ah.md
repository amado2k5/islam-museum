## Overview
A severe famine struck Yemen. A mukuk rose to one hundred and forty-eight dirhams, villages were ruined, and people ate one another.

## Detail
A severe famine struck the people of Yemen. The price of a mukuk reached one hundred and forty-eight dirhams, the last word being a fraction that the scan leaves unclear, and the exchange that day was one hundred and twenty dirhams to a struck dinar. Barley and sorghum were priced at less than a mukuk. The villages were ruined, and people ate one another, and the author says no greater famine is recorded. The date is derived from the sequence of the entries: it follows the events of Safar 292 AH on this page.
