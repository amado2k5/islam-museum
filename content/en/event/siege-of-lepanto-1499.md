## Summary
Al-Salabi records [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]]'s naval victory over the Venetians in the Gulf of Lepanto in 905/1499, and the city's capture the following year {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 173}.

## The papal league
With the Ottoman seizure of Venetian positions in Greece, Pope [[person/alexander-vi|Alexander VI]] — at Venice's plea — raised a league of France and Spain against them; the Ottomans weathered the three fleets, French, Spanish and papal, until a peace was made with Venice {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 173}.
