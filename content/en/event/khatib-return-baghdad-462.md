## Summary
In 462 he travelled by Tripoli and Aleppo to Baghdad along the Euphrates road by al-Rahba, dictating on the way, and entered the city in Dhu al-Hijja; he had wished that his Tarikh be heard complete at Baghdad and that he die there beside Bishr — and the Baghdadis heard the book from him at the Nizamiyya {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 93, 107}.
