## Summary
In 254/868 the caliph al-Mu'tazz granted Egypt as an apanage to the Turkish general Bayakbak, who had married the widow of Tulun, Ahmad's father. Ahmad was appointed his stepfather's lieutenant and entered Fustat on 23 Ramadan 254/15 September 868 — the first Muslim governor of Egypt who would go on to annex Syria {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 300} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 666}.
