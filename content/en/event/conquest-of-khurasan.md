## Overview
Khurasan was conquered under 'Abd Allah b. 'Amir: when its people repudiated the treaty after 'Umar's death, he marched with al-Ahnaf b. Qays in the vanguard. The two Tabas — gates of Khurasan and Quhistan — surrendered for six hundred thousand dirhams; Zam, Bakharz and Juwayn fell. Nishapur held out months until one quarter's holder sought terms and let them in by night; its marzban capitulated for a million dirhams and Qays b. al-Haytham took the governorship. Herat's marzban settled for a million, Marw's for two million two hundred thousand. Al-Ahnaf pushed to Tukharistan and besieged Rustaq Suwanjar until they surrendered on condition a Muslim caller give the adhan in the palace and stay until the army withdrew {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir}.
