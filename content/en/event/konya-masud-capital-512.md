## Summary
[[place/konya|Konya]]'s real development dates from the reign of Sultan Mas'ud I (512-550/1118-1155), who resolved to make it his capital and built a mosque and other monuments that his successors completed. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 287}

## Why Konya?
The well-irrigated site, in contrast to the neighbouring desert, commanded the southern route at a time when the Danishmendids denied the Seljuks the northern route to the Dardanelles; the new masters found in it a secure political and military base for their still semi-nomadic peoples. The Seljuk period marked the zenith of the city's history. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 287}
