## Overview
The Langahs were defeated in 1526 by the Arghuns, who collaborated with the Mughals and made Multan a dependency of their empire {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## A Mughal suba
After the Sur interlude in Indo-Muslim history, Multan became the capital of an important suba of the Mughal empire, as the A'in-i Akbari details {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## The long stability
The city enjoyed a long period of stability under the Mughals, until 1752 when Ahmad Shah Durrani forced the emperor to cede the subas of Lahore and Multan {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
