## Overview
Al-Baladhuri mentions a land called [[place/al-usayfan|al-Usayfan]], between Qashmir, Multan and Kabul, whose king became Muslim in the caliphate of [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report
The people of the land worshipped an idol, over which a house had been built. The son of the king fell ill, and he asked the guardians of the house to call on the idol to heal him. They came back and said that it had answered them, but the boy died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The king tore down the house, broke the idol and killed the guardians. Then he called Muslim merchants, who put forward the Oneness of God, and he professed it and became Muslim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
