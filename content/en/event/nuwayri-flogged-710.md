## Summary
Ibn Abbada, wakil al-khass, had made al-Nuwayri his deputy at the Nasiriyya, the Mansuriyya and elsewhere, so that he entered upon the sultan and read affairs with him; puffed up, he spoke loosely of Ibn Abbada. The sultan did not restrain the denunciation but handed him over and empowered Ibn Abbada, who had him flogged severely and confiscated his property; soon after, he was sent away to Syria {cite:suluk-maqrizi p. 91} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.

## The silent page
Al-Nuwayri never hints at this misfortune in his work, mentioning only his transfer to Tripoli — and to Ibn Abbada, who died that same year, he devoted a dry, brief obituary with more to say about the successor than the deceased {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.
