## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that Qaliqala remained unconquered until 133 AH, when Kosan the Armenian besieged it and it fell, and al-Mansur rebuilt it in 139 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.280}

## The fall
Al-Baladhuri says that the king of the Romans went out in 133 AH and besieged the people of Malatya, demolished its wall, and expelled the Muslims to al-Jazira. Then he sent Kosan the Armenian against Qaliqala, whose people were few and whose governor was Abu Karima. Two Armenian brothers dug through a breach in the wall, and let Kosan into the city, and he killed, took captives and destroyed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.280}

## The rebuilding
Al-Baladhuri, quoting al-Waqidi, says that [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] ransomed in 139 AH those of the captives who were still alive, built the city, and returned them to it. He adds that [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] spent five hundred thousand dirhams on it when its wall was almost falling, until it was fortified. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.280}
