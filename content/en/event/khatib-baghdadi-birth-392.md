## Summary
The hafiz Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn 'Ali ibn Thabit — al-Khatib al-Baghdadi — was born in 392; his father Abu al-Hasan had heard al-Kattani and was the preacher of Darzijan, a village below Baghdad on the west bank of the Tigris {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 86}.
