## Overview
Before Uhud Quraysh sold a thousand camels to raise fifty thousand dinars, with which to finance their war against the Muslims. The author of "al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" includes the report in [[event/preparation-for-uhud|the preparation for Uhud]], and dates it to Shawwal 3 AH. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.248}

## What the source says
Quraysh raised the money by selling a thousand camels, and what they collected reached fifty thousand dinars. The sum was set aside to finance the war. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.248}

The Gregorian date in the museum's data (March 625 CE) is derived from the Hijri date.

## Note
The date is recorded by a single source, and a second source has not yet been added.
