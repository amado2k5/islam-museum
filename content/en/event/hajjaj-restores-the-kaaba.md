## Overview
In 74 AH [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf]] demolished the structure of the Ka'ba that Ibn al-Zubayr had built. Ibn al-Zubayr had included the Hijr and made two doors for it. Al-Hajjaj restored it to its first building. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.195}
