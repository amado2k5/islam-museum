## Summary
Shams al-Din Aytaqmish, formerly lord of Hamadhan and the Jibal lands, was killed on his way back from Baghdad toward Hamadhan; Mankli's men took him, killed him and carried his head to Mankli. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 360}

## Details
Under the events of year 610, Ibn al-Athir reports that Aytaqmish — who had come to Baghdad, resided there and been honored by the caliph with robes of honor and all he needed — left Baghdad in Jumada al-Akhira heading for Hamadhan, reached the lands of Ibn Tarjam, met him and waited for the Baghdad troops to join him under an agreed arrangement. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 360} The caliph had dismissed Sulayman ibn Tarjam from the emirate over his tribe of the Turcomans and appointed his younger brother, so Sulayman sent word to Mankli informing him of Aytaqmish's movements; Aytaqmish travelled on, was seized and killed, and his head was carried to Mankli while his companions scattered through the country. The news of his death reached Baghdad and grieved the caliph, who wrote to Mankli denouncing the deed; Mankli answered defiantly, consolidated his hold on the country, and his forces multiplied. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 360}
