## Overview
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] goes on, from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]], with several reports of blessing which the speakers ascribed to the coming and the prayer of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. The passage is all attributed reports, and belongs to the accounts of marvels in this Zaydi book; the museum gives them in the narrators' voice, neither confirming nor denying. It follows [[event/reports-of-al-hadis-seal-and-two-cures-told-at-khiwan-286]].

## Muhammad ibn al-Da'am's harvest
Muhammad ibn Sulayman said: "I heard [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-daam|Muhammad ibn al-Da'am]] say: I had land which I sowed and it yielded two hundred to three hundred faraq; I came to al-Hadi and he gave me dinars; I put one of them into the price of the seed for the estate and sowed it, and it yielded a thousand faraq and more." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} The faraq is a measure whose size the book does not give here, and the museum does not convert it.

## A man of the house of Dud
Muhammad ibn Sulayman said he heard a man of Yemen "of the house of Dud" say to Yahya ibn al-Husayn: "You passed by us on your first journey and we gave you our pledge, and you prayed to God to spare us trouble; since you we have seen no trouble, and trouble was around us yet we saw only good, by the blessing of your prayer for us." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.141} The first journey is al-Hadi's first journey to Yemen in 280 AH, see [[event/al-hadi-first-journey-to-yemen-280]]; it is not asserted that "Dud" here is the "Dhud" of [[place/bayt-dhud-yemen]], so the name stands as printed.

## The rain
The narrator says: "One day I was sitting with Yahya ibn al-Husayn and a party of Hamdan came to him and said: Son of the Messenger of God, our land was dry and barren, and when you came to us and we pledged to you God watered our land and we were rained on as we had never seen." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.141}

## The sadaqa and the crop
He also heard a man say to him: "Son of the Messenger of God, I had a little land of small yield, and when I paid out its sadaqa it bore manyfold what it used to bear." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.141} And he heard al-Hadi urging people to pay the sadaqa on their crops and reminding them of the good in it; the editor notes that the other manuscript reads a different plural of the word for "crops". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.141} The museum gives no juristic ruling on the sadaqa here and relates only what the narrator describes.

## The grain of Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Malik
Then al-Hadi said, the narrator listening: "Abd al-Malik ibn Abd al-Malik told me, having come to me and prayed for me", his family name being read here by optical character recognition as "al-Barsami" and elsewhere in the book as "al-Yarsami", so the reading is not settled; "then he said to me: Son of the Messenger of God, I tell you, by God, the blessing we are in: I used to bring in grain like this year's or more before you came to us, and by the end of the year nothing was left with us so that I bought good grain until our own came." Then he said: "Lately I said to my family: is there any food left with you? They said: our food is as it was and our corn as it was, and that is by your blessing, son of the Messenger of God." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.141}

## Notes
No person record is made for this name, because the family name cannot be read with confidence, as in earlier batches. The date is at month precision because the narrator relays what he heard at different times since al-Hadi came.
