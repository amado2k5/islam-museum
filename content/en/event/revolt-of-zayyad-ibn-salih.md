## Overview
In 135 AH Ziyad ibn Salih went out beyond the river of Balkh. Abu Muslim set out from Merv prepared to meet him, and Abu Dawud sent Khalid ibn Ibrahim. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.466}
