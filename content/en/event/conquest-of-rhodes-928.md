## Overview
al-Sallabi says that Sultan [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Suleiman the Magnificent]] waged a great war on [[place/rudis|Rhodes]] from the middle of 1522 CE, and took it. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.201}

The knights were granted the right to leave it, and went to Malta, where Charles gave them the right to rule that island. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.201}

This is not the conquest of Rhodes in the Umayyad period. The date is 928 AH (1522 CE).
