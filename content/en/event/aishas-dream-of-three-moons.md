## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Yahya ibn Sa'id that Aisha said: I saw three moons fall into my chamber, and I related my dream to Abu Bakr al-Siddiq. When the Prophet died and was buried in her house, Abu Bakr said to her: this is one of your moons, and the best of them. The muhaqqiq notes a variant reads in my chamber {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-2-623|Muwatta 623]].

## The meaning
In this report the dream can match reality years later; Aisha's dream came true with the Prophet's death and burial in her chamber, and Abu Bakr's interpretation that he was the best of her moons {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
