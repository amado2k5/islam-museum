## Overview
Ibn Imad says that in 652 AH the Tatars began conquering the Islamic lands. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.263}

## The state of Baghdad
He reports that the caliph was heedless in his seclusion and pleasures, and that the vizier Mu'ayyad al-Din and the caliph's followers corresponded with Hulagu, and messengers went between them. This is Ibn Imad's description. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.263}
