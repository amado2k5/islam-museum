## Overview
al-Sallabi says that the Ottomans landed on [[place/qubrus|Cyprus]] with an overwhelming force and advanced into the island without difficulty. The fortified Famagusta resisted them until it surrendered in Rabi' al-Akhir 979 AH (August 1571 CE). {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 279-280}

## The siege
The Ottoman force reached a hundred thousand fighters, and they used the known means of siege, from sapping to laying mines, and these had no effect on the garrison. al-Sallabi holds that hunger was what settled the matter. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 279-280}

## After the conquest
The Ottoman state moved a large number of the people of Anatolia to the island, and their descendants are still there. al-Sallabi says that the Orthodox Cypriots welcomed the Ottoman rule, which, in his description, saved them from the Catholic persecution practised by Venice. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 279-280}
