## Summary
In 633/1235 Ibn al-Nafis moved to Cairo and joined the Nasiri hospital, becoming its head and a professor in its medical school {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep17 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-mubdioune/ep17 00:10:00}.

## His pupils
The Encyclopaedia confirms the move to Cairo at an unrecorded date, that he presumably worked at the Nasiri hospital, and that he trained pupils — the best known being Ibn al-Kuff, author of a work on surgery {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 897}.
