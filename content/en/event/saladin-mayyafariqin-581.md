## Summary
After the peace with Mosul, Salah al-Din took Mayyafariqin and its districts in Diyar Bakr, and his realm grew in al-Jazira, Diyar Bakr and Diyar Rabi'a. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 134}
