## Overview
A peace concluded in 1130 AH / 1718 CE with the mediation of Britain and the Netherlands, after the victory of Austria and the fall of Belgrade in 1129 AH / 1717 CE, in the reign of Sultan [[person/ahmed-iii-uthmani|Ahmed III]]. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.311}

## Results
The Austrians took Belgrade and most of Serbia and part of Wallachia. The coasts of Dalmatia remained with Venice, and the Morea returned to the Ottomans. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.311}

Al-Salabi says that the peace allowed the Catholic clergy to recover their old privileges, which became an entry for intervention in the name of protection. A separate agreement on freedom of trade gave Austria the right to protect foreign merchants within the state. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.311}
