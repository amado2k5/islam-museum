## Overview
[[person/al-hadi|al-Hadi]] died in Rabi' al-Akhir 170 AH and left seven sons. The accounts differ on the cause of his death. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 451-452}

## The accounts
al-Suyuti gives several sayings:
- He pushed a boon companion from a bank of cut reeds, and the man clung to him, and they both fell and died together.
- An abscess in his bowels.
- His mother [[person/al-khayzuran|al-Khayzuran]] poisoned him, when he resolved to kill al-Rashid so that he might name his own son as successor.
- His mother was a ruler who had absolute control of great affairs. He rebuked her, then sent her poisoned food, and she fed some of it to a dog, which died. She then worked to have him killed when he fell ill, by covering his face with a carpet, while people sat on its sides. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 451-452}

The source transmits these sayings and does not weigh them against one another.
