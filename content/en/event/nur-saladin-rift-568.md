## Summary
A secret estrangement arose between Nur al-Din and Salah al-Din, which they kept hidden from the people. It came from Salah al-Din's haste to return to Egypt after taking Ayla without permission, and his refusal to help with the wealth of Egypt, to the point that Nur al-Din resolved to march on Egypt and take it out of his hand. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 35}
