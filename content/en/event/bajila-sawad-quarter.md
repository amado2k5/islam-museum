## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the tribe of Bajila made up a quarter of the people on the day of al-Qadisiyya, and that Umar gave them a quarter of al-Sawad. When [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir]] came to him, Umar returned the matter to the people after they had grown numerous. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.373-374}

## The grant and Umm Kurzan
Umar granted Jarir eighty dinars, and it is also said four hundred. A woman of Bajila named [[person/umm-kurzan|Umm Kurzan]] refused to give up her father's share in al-Sawad, and Umar gave her what she asked. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.373-374}

## Other accounts
al-Baladhuri quotes Jarir that the Bajila held the quarter for three years. In al-Waqidi's account, Jarir asked for his quarter after the spoils of Jalula. [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd]] wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] about this, and Umar answered by offering the choice between fighting them for a fee or for the sake of God. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.373-374}

The event took place around 17 AH, after Jalula. This is an estimate recorded in the data. It needs an additional source.
