## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that Khalid came to al-Falalij on his way back from Banaqiya, and then sent an army to Jaban at [[place/tustar|Tustar]], and Jaban fled from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 343-344}

## Al-Falalij
Khalid came to al-Falalij on his way back from Banaqiya. A gathering of Persians had been there, but they dispersed, and he met no resistance. He then returned to al-Hira. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 343-344}

## Jaban at Tustar
News reached Khalid that [[person/jaban|Jaban]] was at Tustar with a great host. He sent [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] al-Shaybani and [[person/hanzala-al-katib|Hanzala ibn al-Rabi']] al-Asadi of Banu Tamim, who is called Hanzala the Scribe. When they reached him, he fled. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 343-344}

## Notes
The data places the event in Khalid's march, around 12 AH. The date is derived and needs an additional source.
