## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Umar sent Abd Allah ibn Budayl al-Khuza'i to [[place/isbahan|Isfahan]] in 23 AH. It is also said that he wrote to Abu Musa al-Ash'ari ordering him to send him with an army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.436}

## Jayy and the Jewish quarter
Ibn Budayl took [[place/jayy|Jayy]] by treaty after a fight, on condition that its people pay the [[concept/kharaj|kharaj]] and the [[concept/jizya|jizya]], and that they be granted safety for their lives and property, except for weapons. He sent [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] to the Jewish quarter, and its people made peace with him on the same terms. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.436}

## The account of the marzban
On the authority of Muhammad ibn Ishaq, the marzban of Isfahan was an aged elder named Fadusfan. He fled toward Kirman, and Ibn Budayl followed him and challenged him, then offered him peace on the jizya. He came back with him and handed over the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.436}

## Other accounts
al-Baladhuri gives a report that Umar sent Ibn Budayl with an army, and he met Abu Musa, who had conquered Qum and Qashan, and they raided Isfahan together. He then says that the most sound account is that Abu Musa conquered Qum and Qashan, and that Ibn Budayl conquered Jayy and the Jewish quarter. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.436}

The conquest of Isfahan was in one of the years 23 and 24 AH. The nobles of Isfahan had strongholds, and when Jayy was taken they entered obedience on payment of the kharaj, and they disdained the jizya and so became Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.436}
