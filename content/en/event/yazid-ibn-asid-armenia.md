## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the caliph [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] appointed Yazid ibn Asid al-Sulami over Armenia. Yazid opened the Gate of the Lan and set up a garrison there of men from the register, and subdued the Sanariyya until they submitted by giving the land tax. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The marriage alliance
Al-Mansur wrote to him ordering him to marry into the family of the king of the Khazars, and he did. The king's daughter bore him a son who died, and she herself died in childbirth. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Administration and building
Yazid sent to the salt-workers and the saltpans of the land of Shirwan and collected from them and appointed an agent over them. He built the two cities of Arjil the Lesser and Arjil the Greater, and settled in them people of Palestine. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The Shimakhiyya
It is related from a number of the elders of Bardha'a that the Shimakhiyya in the district of Shirwan were named after al-Shimakh ibn Shuja', and that the king of Shirwan was in the governorship of Sa'id ibn Sam al-Bahili over Armenia. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date in the caliphate of al-Mansur (around 754 to 775 CE) is derived and approximate. All the information on this page rests on the single report, and needs an additional source.
