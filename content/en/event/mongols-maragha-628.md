## Summary
In 628 AH the Tatars besieged Maragha in Azerbaijan; its people surrendered on a requested aman, the Tatars occupied the town and set a garrison in it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Tatars besieged Maragha of Azerbaijan this year; its people resisted, then yielded to surrender on an aman they asked for and were granted; the Tatars took over the town, killed within it — though not in great numbers — and posted a garrison there. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491} The Tatars' power then grew great and fear of them intensified in Azerbaijan; on which Ibn al-Athir remarks that he sees no king of Islam eager for jihad or for succouring the faith, every one of them devoted to his sport and pleasure and to wronging his subjects — and that, in his view, is more to be feared than the enemy, as he puts it in his account. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}
