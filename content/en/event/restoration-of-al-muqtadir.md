## Overview
In 317 AH, after Nazuk was killed and al-Qahir had left the palace, Mu'nis brought out al-Muqtadir and restored him to the palace. He sat and addressed the people, and then the people gave him their allegiance again. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.124}
