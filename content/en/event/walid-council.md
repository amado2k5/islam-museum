As the pilgrimage season drew near and Quraysh knew the Arabs' delegations would come to them, their chiefs gathered to [[person/al-walid-ibn-al-mughira|al-Walid ibn al-Mughira]] to deliberate the single word they would speak to the Arabs concerning the Prophet. Al-Walid said: "agree on one opinion about him and do not differ, lest some of you belie others." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

They offered him: a soothsayer — he said: he is no soothsayer, we have seen the soothsayers and this is neither their mumbling nor their rhyming; possessed — he said: no choking, no convulsions, no whispering; a poet — he said: he is no poet, we know all poetry; a sorcerer — he said: none of their blowing and knotting. Then he said: "by God, there is a sweetness to his words; the nearest thing to say of him is: a sorcerer who divides a man from his father, a man from his brother, a man from his wife and a man from his clan." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

So they sat on the people's paths when the season came, warning every passer-by of him; and [[person/abu-lahab|Abu Lahab]] would follow the Prophet about their dwellings, at Ukaz, Majanna and Dhu al-Majaz, telling the people "do not obey him — he is a renegade liar". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
