## Overview
Muhyi al-Din Abu Zakariyya Yahya ibn Sharaf ibn Murri al-Hizami al-Dimashqi, known as al-Nawawi, was born in the town of Nawa in the Hawran, in the Jawlan south of Damascus, in Muharram 631 (October 1233) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1096} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya p. 293-294}. He read the Qur'an in his home town while still a boy, whose early promise was already noticed {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1096} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 354-355}."
