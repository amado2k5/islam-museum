## Overview
[[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ibn Abbad al-Akili]] came from Al Tarif's side until he reached al-Ma'ruf, and [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent to [[group/banu-kulayb-saada|Banu Kulayb]] and said: you know that I gave you safety on condition that this man not enter among you; if he enters, the safety is broken and I will fight you all {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.251}. Banu Kulayb came to him in a body and asked him for safety for Ibn Abbad and he agreed; they brought him to him, he sent away the army that had been with him, and al-Hadi told him to go back to the place where he had been, and his companions gathered to him from every place {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.251}.

## Detail
The report comes after the march on Amlah and before the narrator turns back to the imprisonment of Abu al-Qasim; the book gives neither its year nor its month, so 290 AH is an estimate from the order. The book does not say where al-Ma'ruf is. The museum links Ibn Abbad to Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili as probable only. For al-Hadi's earlier march into the land of Ibn Abbad see [[event/al-hadi-marches-on-ibn-abbad-and-razes-the-houses-and-vines-of-allaf-290|the march on Ibn Abbad and the razing at Allaf]]. Before this is [[event/al-hadi-marches-on-amlah-after-the-eid-and-wail-seeks-safety-290|the march on Amlah]].
