## Overview
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] says: [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] told me: [[person/ahmad-ibn-al-dahhak-qadi-of-hamdan|Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak]], "qadi of Hamdan, its jurist and its scholar, to whom they go in that and in all that they need of the lawful and the unlawful", told me: "He and Murawwi ibn Abdullah al-Sa'idi said to me: We pledged to Yahya ibn al-Husayn knowing that there is none on the face of the earth more upright in the right of God than he, and nothing of Muhammad is missing but his person." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} This is the judgement of the two narrators as reported in the book and is among the praises of the imam in this Zaydi narrative; the museum gives it as attributed, neither confirming nor denying. See [[person/murawwi-ibn-abdullah-al-saidi|Murawwi ibn Abdullah al-Sa'idi]].

## The sign they name
They said: "We saw a sign of that in him in what came of his prayer against Abu Mihjan, of the slaves of Al Ya'fur, and his prayer against Jaftam, and what he prayed against befell him." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140}

## Abu Mihjan
Al-Hadi had sent to Abu Mihjan, "in the year in which he was in Yemen on his first going out", a man with a letter calling him to God, "and Abu Mihjan seized the messenger, shaved his head and beard and struck him"; al-Hadi "prayed against him, and he was afflicted in his hands with a great affliction until he died of it in the worst of states". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} This is as the narrators tell it. See [[person/abu-mihjan-abd-al-yafur|Abu Mihjan, of the slaves of Al Ya'fur]]. The first going out of al-Hadi is the journey of 280 AH, [[event/al-hadi-first-journey-to-yemen-280]].

## The son called Abu al-Atahiya
The two narrators said: "And we heard his son Abu al-Atahiya say: My father Abu Mihjan used to say in that affliction: This is a punishment for what I did to the messenger of the Alawite, meaning the messenger of Yahya ibn al-Husayn." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} By the context "his son" refers to Abu Mihjan, making the speaker his son, but the book's index puts this page together with [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya ibn Bishr]] and says "Abdullah ibn Bishr = Abu al-Atahiya" at p. 450, and it is not asserted here whether the two are one man or two.

## Jaftam
"As for Jaftam, he said: O God, drive him out of Yemen in the worst of states; and the year had not turned before he left Yemen, driven out, wandering and afraid." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140} See [[person/jaftam|Jaftam]]. The book's index lists Jaftam at pp. 140, 250, 253, 272 and 273, pages not yet read.

## Notes
The report describes a prayer and what followed it, relayed by the narrator from two witnesses of Hamdan, and the museum describes it without judgement and does not repeat the formulas of cursing. The striking of the imam's messenger and the shaving of his head and beard are stated as the text has them and no more. The date is at month precision because the report is told after the Friday mentioned and the two events it tells took place earlier.
