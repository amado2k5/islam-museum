## Overview
Al-Hadi visits the prison, orders it cleaned, has the readers among the prisoners teach those who cannot read, and then examines the debts of debtors and the offences of the guilty.

## Detail
He stood at the prison, then went in and ordered it cleaned, and ordered the readers among the prisoners to teach those who could not read. He asked about their offences and their debts. For a man in debt he looked into his means and his poverty, and for a man in an offence he looked into his state and his case. Then he went back, having given orders and prohibitions throughout the town. The passage continues at the top of printed page 387.
