## Overview
A feud in Sa'da turns people away from Ahmad ibn Yahya, and Hassan ibn Uthman is invited from Gharq in 322 AH.

## Detail
Early in 322 AH a feud breaks out in Sa'da between several factions; the people of Sanaa side with one of them. Ahmad ibn Yahya keeps to his house, and others turn against him. They write to Hassan ibn Uthman ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur, who was living at Gharq, and invite him. He goes to Mount Barat, gives away the money he had with him, and reaches the Bani al-Harith at Najran in Rabi II of that year. The original has a gap in the text at this point, noted by the editor. The factions are named in the Arabic as printed; they are not repeated in the English and Urdu.
