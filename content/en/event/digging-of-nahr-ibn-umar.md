## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, the governor of Yazid ibn al-Walid, was complained to by the people of [[place/basra|Basra]] about the brackishness of their water, so the canal known as the Canal of Ibn Umar was dug. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The complaint and the permission
The people of Basra brought him two flasks, in one of which was Basra's water and in the other the water of al-Bataiha, and he saw a difference between them. They said that if he dug them a canal they would drink fresh water. He wrote to Yazid, who replied that if the cost of the canal reached the land tax of Iraq he should spend it on it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The cost
A man said in the assembly of Ibn Umar that he reckoned the cost would reach three hundred thousand or more. Ibn Umar said that even if it reached the land tax of Iraq, he would spend it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
