## Execution of Nasimuddin al-Tabrizi, shaykh of the Hurufis, in Aleppo in 820 AH
In 820 AH Shaykh Nasim al-Din al-Tabrizi, a resident of Aleppo and shaykh of the Hurufis there, was killed. His followers had grown in number and his doctrine had spread, until the sultan ordered his execution. He was beheaded, his skin was flayed, and his body was crucified {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 153}.
