## Summary
[[person/charles-v|Charles V]]'s recapture of Tunis from the Ottomans in 942/1535 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 220}.

## The campaign
Circumstances favoured Spain: the Ottomans were busy at war with Safavid Persia and Francis I had promised neutrality. Answering the appeal of the Hafsid [[person/al-hasan-ibn-muhammad-hafsi|al-Hasan b. Muhammad]] and wishing to isolate Istanbul, Charles led 30,000 fighters — Spaniards, Germans, Neapolitans, Sicilians — on five hundred ships out of Barcelona {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 220}.

## Outcome
Khayr al-Din's force could not answer — seven thousand Ottoman soldiers and about five thousand Tunisians, with the Arab tribes failing the muster — so Charles took La Goulette, the stronghold of Tunis's harbour. The Spanish reinstalled al-Hasan b. Muhammad bound to surrender Bône and Mahdia, to aid the Knights of St John at Tripoli, to oppose the Ottomans, and to fund a 2,000-man Spanish garrison at La Goulette castle; Mahdia he could not deliver, for it remained in Ottoman hands {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 220}.
