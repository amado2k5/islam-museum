## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports in "Futuh al-Buldan" that [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] appointed his client Wardan over Alexandria and returned to [[place/al-fustat|al-Fustat]]. Dismissal came to him soon after. [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] then appointed [[person/abdullah-ibn-sad-ibn-abi-sarh|Abd Allah ibn Sa'd]] in his place, in 25 AH; Abd Allah was his foster brother. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.313}

## Accounts of the cause
The first account: Abd Allah had previously been in charge of the kharaj of Egypt. A quarrel took place between him and Amr, and he wrote complaining of him. Uthman dismissed Amr, combined the two posts under Abd Allah, and ordered him to stay in Alexandria as a garrison, because it had been conquered by force and had revolted twice. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.313}

The third account: the people of Egypt asked Uthman to keep Amr in office until he had finished fighting the Byzantines who had landed at Alexandria, because of his knowledge of war. When Amr defeated them, Uthman wanted to make him commander of the war and Abd Allah in charge of the kharaj. Amr refused and said: "I am like one holding the two horns of a cow while the amir milks her." So Uthman appointed Ibn Sa'd over Egypt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.313}

## Abyssinia
al-Baladhuri adds that the Habasha of al-Bayma, that is [[group/habasha-of-al-bayma|the Abyssinians]], kept fighting for seven years after the conquest of Egypt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.313}
