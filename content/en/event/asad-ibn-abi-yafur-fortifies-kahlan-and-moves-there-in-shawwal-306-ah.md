## Overview
Asad has Kahlan fortified and moves there in Shawwal 306 AH, leaving his brother in charge at Sanaa.

## Detail
When Asad reached Kahlan he had it rebuilt and fortified. He then went to live there in Shawwal 306 AH, leaving his brother Abd Allah ibn Abi Ya'fur in charge at Sanaa.
