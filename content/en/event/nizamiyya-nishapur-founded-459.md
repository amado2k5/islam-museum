## Summary
The vizier Nizam al-Mulk founded the Nishapur Nizamiyya about 459/1067 within his network of colleges where the Shafi'i and hadith imams taught {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}; the Encyclopaedia of Islam adds that he founded it in this town specially for the returned exile Imam al-Haramayn al-Juwayni, who taught there to the end of his days with the prayer niche, minbar, sermon and teaching all conceded to him {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 605} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 231}.
