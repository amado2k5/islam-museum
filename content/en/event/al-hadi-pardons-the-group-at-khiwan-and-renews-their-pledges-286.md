## Overview
After the pardon granted at the foot of the last page {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} the whole group reached [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] on the Friday with two days of Sha'ban left in 286 AH, apologised for their bad conduct and admitted their fault; he pardoned them and excused what they confessed, they renewed their pledges and returned to their places. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.143} The account is Zaydi, told by the imam's followers, and continues [[event/al-hadi-grants-banu-mamar-safe-conduct-on-21-shaban-286]].

## What followed the pardon
Al-Hadi sent [[person/husayn-ibn-al-hasan-al-alawi|al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi]] back to [[place/athafit|Athafit]] and sent everyone gathered to him back to their families. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.143} The narrative says matters calmed, that God averted every danger from him, and that he stayed at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] prevailing over his enemy wherever he was; this is the narrator's judgement and the museum neither confirms nor denies it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.143} The editor notes that a whole folio in the manuscript and in the second manuscript was left blank after this point {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.143}, so the next account begins without an introduction: [[event/al-hadi-and-the-wadi-fight-against-the-muhdithin-286]].

## A note on the name
The commander is called al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi on the previous page and "al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn al-Alawi" on this one; the context points to one man and he is meant here. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.143}
