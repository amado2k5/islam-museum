## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates the breach of the people of [[place/kabul|Kabul]], and the killing of [[person/yazid-ibn-ziyad-ibn-abihi|Yazid ibn Ziyad]] in fighting them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The breach and the battle
The people of Kabul broke their word and took [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-ziyad|Abu Ubayda ibn Ziyad]] captive. Yazid ibn Ziyad marched against them and fought them at Junza; he was killed, with many of those with him, and the rest of the people were routed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Ransom and governorship
Salm ibn Ziyad sent [[person/talha-ibn-abdallah-al-khuzai|Talha al-Talhat]], who ransomed Abu Ubayda for five hundred thousand dirhams. Talha went to Sistan as governor on behalf of Salm, and died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
After he appointed a man from the Banu Yashkur as his successor, factionalism arose, each people took its own city, and Ratbil became ambitious for them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
