## Summary
Jamal al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Yusuf ibn Ibrahim ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Shaybani al-Qifti was born in 568/1172 at Qift in Upper Egypt {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}; al-Idfawi's al-Tali' al-Sa'id gives the earlier year 563 {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 325}.
