## Overview
Al-Hadi closed in on the mountain. His brother Abdallah ibn al-Husayn drove a clan from its camp, and the army took the dwellings and herds. The Khawlani division then raided Banu al-Harith's herds, and Banu al-Harith fled to the mountain peaks, leaving their homes and property.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi closed in on the mountain. His brother Abdallah ibn al-Husayn fell upon the camp of a clan whose name is withheld in this record, drove the clan out, and the army took the dwellings and the livestock kept for slaughter. Two men of that clan were killed and one man of Khawlan. Abdallah then found a woman who had been stripped of her clothes, put his own garment over her, set her on his mount, and sent her with Kulayb ibn Najjad al-Hisi, one of those to whom al-Hadi had given safe-conduct, to reach al-Hadi's household. The Khawlani division that al-Hadi had placed at the centre then raided the herds of Banu al-Harith and took many of them. Banu al-Harith were driven up into the mountain peaks and left their dwellings and property.
