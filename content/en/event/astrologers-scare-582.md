## Summary
Al-Imad al-Katib, who was then in the service of Sultan Salah al-Din, reports that astrologers across the lands agreed that the world would be destroyed in Sha'ban that year, when six planets met in the sign of Libra, with a gale of wind. They frightened the Persians and the Byzantines, who dug caves and stocked them with water and food {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 753}.

## A windless night
When the night the astrologers had named came, nothing happened. Al-Imad said: "We were sitting with the sultan, and the candles were burning without moving. We had never seen a night so still" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 753}.
