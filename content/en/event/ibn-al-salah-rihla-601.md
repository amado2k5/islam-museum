## Summary
When he was a little over twenty, Ibn al-Salah set out for Baghdad, where he heard hadith from Abu Ahmad 'Abd al-Wahhab b. Sukayna and 'Umar b. Tabarzad. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185}

## On the road
At Dubays he heard Isma'il b. Ibrahim al-Khazzaz, and at Hamadan Abu al-Fadl b. al-Mu'azzim and a group — the first of his eastern journeys in the pursuit of hadith. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185}
