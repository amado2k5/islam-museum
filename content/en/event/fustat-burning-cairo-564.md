## Overview
Al-Suyuti, in "History of the Caliphs", says that the Franks came toward the land of Egypt in 564 AH with a great army, took Bilbays and besieged Cairo. Its governor burned [[place/al-fustat|Fustat]] out of fear of them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.680}

## The vizierate
The secretary of the lord of Egypt appealed for help to the sultan Nur al-Din, and [[person/asad-al-din-shirkuh|Asad al-Din Shirkuh]] came with his armies, and the Franks left Cairo when they heard of his arrival. [[person/al-adid-fatimid|Al-Adid]] appointed him vizier, and he died sixty-five days later. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.680}

Then al-Adid appointed in his place his nephew [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub]], and gave him the title "al-Malik al-Nasir". Al-Suyuti describes his exercise of the sultanate as complete in every way. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.680}
