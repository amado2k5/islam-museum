## Overview
Ali ibn al-Fadl sends his army against Abu al-Faraat. The book says God gave Abu al-Faraat the victory over them, and many of the army were killed in Safar 300 AH.

## Detail
The book says Ibn al-Fadl sent his army against Abu al-Faraat, who fought them, routed them, and was given the victory over them by God; many of the army were killed in Safar 300 AH. The editor's note says the original and the other copy read the verb in the plural, which the editor takes to be an error for the singular. This completes the sentence begun at the foot of p. 400.
