## Overview
In 296 AH, after al-Muqtadir was deposed, Abd Allah ibn al-Mu'tazz was given the oath of allegiance to the caliphate. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.31}

## al-Tabari's account
The oath was taken in the house of al-Muktafi behind al-Mukharram, and the judges gave him their allegiance. He took the title al-Muntasir bi-Llah, a title that al-Tabari says he chose for himself. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.31}
