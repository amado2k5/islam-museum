## Overview
Ibn Amir sent [[person/abdallah-ibn-khazim-al-sulami|Abd Allah ibn Khazim]] to [[place/sarakhs|Sarakhs]], and its people fought him. Then its lord [[person/zadhuya|Zadhuya]] asked for peace, and Ibn Khazim prevailed over the land of Sarakhs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The first account
Zadhuya asked for peace on condition of safety for a hundred men, and that the women be handed to Ibn Khazim. His daughter fell to Ibn Khazim's share, and he took her and named her Maysha'. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
Al-Baladhuri gives another account in the form "it is said". In it the lord agreed to peace on safety for a hundred persons, named the hundred but not himself, and Ibn Khazim killed him and entered Sarakhs by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
From Sarakhs Ibn Khazim sent Yazid ibn Salim, client of Sharik ibn al-A'war, to Kayf and Wayna, and he took them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The two accounts come from a single source, and the data gives no specific date; a second source is needed.
