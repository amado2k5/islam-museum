## Summary
In 536 AH a great battle took place between the sultan Sanjar and the non-Muslim Turks beyond the Oxus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 591}.

## The defeat and its consequences
The Muslims were struck, and Sanjar escaped with a small group, so few that he reached Balkh with only six men. His wife and daughter were taken captive, and 100,000 or more of his army were killed. The Turks numbered 300,000 horsemen, led by Kuhan the Qara Khitai, who, despite the greatness of his state, was just and did not grant any man an iqta' in his realm {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 591, 594}.
