## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] gave nothing of the tribute to the agents of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What he was told
Rutbil was asked about a people who used to come to him with empty bellies, dark faces from prayer, and sandals of palm fibre. He was told they had died out, and he said they had been more faithful in covenant and more fierce in might than you. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He was asked why he gave tribute to al-Hajjaj and not to them. He answered, according to the account, that al-Hajjaj does not consider what he spends once he has gained his aim, and they spend no dirham except in hope of ten. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri says that he gave none of that tribute to any of the agents of the Umayyads or the agents of Ibn al-Zubayr in Sistan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

What the narrator understood from the story, and the year in which it happened, need an additional source.
