## Summary
About 450/1058 al-Shirazi composed his manual of usul al-fiqh, al-Luma'; between 452/1060 and 453/1061 he wrote the fiqh epitome al-Tanbih, which drew more than seventy commentaries; and about 452/1060 he composed Tabaqat al-fuqaha, one of the oldest surviving works of its genre, recording the mujtahid jurists of all schools before tabaqat literature narrowed to a single madhhab {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 495}.
