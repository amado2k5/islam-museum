## Overview
In 654 AH [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] was flooded by a flood unheard of, according to "Shadharat al-Dhahab". The Tigris rose to a height never seen, and a great many people drowned. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 272}

## The impact
Many houses collapsed on their people, and the people came close to ruin. Boats kept passing through the lanes of Baghdad. The caliph rode in a boat, and people prayed to God with supplication. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 272}
