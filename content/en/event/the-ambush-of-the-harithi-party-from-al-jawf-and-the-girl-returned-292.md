## Overview
"Ali ibn Muhammad said: then it reached al-Hadi that a party of Banu al-Harith was coming from al-Jawf"; he sent a force that lay in wait for them, and when they came the horse and foot charged them, dust rose and the people were mixed so that no one knew another; two of them were struck and many animals with their loads taken {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.292} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}.

## Detail
On one load a girl was riding, and they brought her through to the camp; she had said that she was a slave, but when she reached Qarqar she said she belonged to Ibn Humayd {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. Al-Hadi sent for her, threw a garment over her, mounted her on a Wadi'i slave woman's animal and sent with her a man of Banu al-Harith who was in his camp; he went with her until near the people, called to them, told them the girl's lineage and turned back, and they took her {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. The narrator tells that al-Hadi returned the girl to her people once her lineage was known, giving neither her age nor her name. The notes of manuscript S give two readings, for "girl" and for "camp" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. The account is from the imam's supporters in a Zaydi book and is given here to show that, by the text, the girl was returned without any price; the museum adds nothing to that. The event follows [[event/the-camp-at-qarqar-the-cutting-of-palms-and-the-razing-of-markets-292|the camp at Qarqar]] and precedes [[event/the-fight-at-the-lane-gate-and-the-cutting-of-the-palms-of-banu-khuthaym-292|the fight at the lane gate]]; it is dated to the year because the book gives no day or month after the end of Rajab. Al-Jawf is a place these pages do not locate, and no record was made for it.
