## Overview
When [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] came to [[place/najran|Najran]], the narrator says, people feared his standing after what they had seen of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]; he ruled among them in the best manner, was firm with the dissolute and drew near the upright, and after testing him and learning his conduct they all came to want him and spread word of it in their towns and praised him. That is the narrator's praise of his own father, and the museum records it as such. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.200}

## Detail
Because the truth stayed with them so long the people of falsehood feared it would take hold; some troublemakers of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] went out, after the news of what had happened to the Akilis, to the Harithi desert, gathered a body of horse and raided [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], without getting what they hoped against the imam. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.200} The news reached Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, who rode out at once and followed them down the lower valley of Najran without catching any; while he was following them a Harithi man of loyalty and affection called al-Jahir ibn Ziyad overtook him with another Harithi who then showed affection and asked him to go back to his place since he had no army, fearing that Banu al-Harith might fall on him. They swore he should return; some went on after the raiders and guaranteed to bring them, and he went back to the village and wrote to al-Hadi of the raid. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.200} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.201} See [[person/al-jahir-ibn-ziyad-of-banu-al-harith]].
