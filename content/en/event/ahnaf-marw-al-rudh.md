## Overview
Ibn Amir sent [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] toward Tukharistan. He made peace with the people of Qasr al-Ahnaf, then with the people of [[place/marw-al-rudh|Marw al-Rudh]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Qasr al-Ahnaf
al-Ahnaf came to a place called Qasr al-Ahnaf, a fort in Marw al-Rudh with a large district known as the district of al-Ahnaf. He besieged its people, and they made peace with him for three hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

al-Ahnaf stipulated that a man of the Muslims should enter the fort, give the call to prayer there and stay among them until he left. They agreed, and the treaty covered the whole district. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Marw al-Rudh
al-Ahnaf went to Marw al-Rudh and besieged its people. They fought him hard, but the Muslims routed them and drove them back into their fort. The marzban, who was of the line of Badhan, lord of Yemen, or a kinsman of his, wrote to al-Ahnaf that Badhan's conversion to Islam had moved him to make peace. He made peace with him for sixty thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source quotes al-Mada'ini that some people said the sum was six hundred thousand. al-Ahnaf's horsemen had taken a district called Baghh and driven off its livestock, and the peace came after that. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
