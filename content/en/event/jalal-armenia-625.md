## Summary
In 625 AH Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah reached the land of Akhlat and passed on into Armenia, plundering the country and enslaving women, then turned back because of the heavy snowfall. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 479}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Jalal al-Din came to the land of Akhlat and passed beyond it to the plain of Mush and the mountain of Jur, plundering everyone, taking women captive, enslaving children, killing the men and devastating the villages, then returned to his own land. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 479} When word reached the Jaziran towns — Harran, Saruj and others — that he had crossed Akhlat into Jur and drawn near them, the people feared he would come to them, for it was winter and they supposed he meant to winter in the Jazira where the cold is less severe; they resolved to move to Syria, and some people of Saruj had already reached Manbij in the Syrian lands when news came that he had plundered and withdrawn, so they stayed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 480} The cause of his return was that snow had fallen in the land of Akhlat in unheard-of quantity, so he hastened back. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 480}
