## Brief
In 622 CE the Prophet Muhammad and many of his followers moved from [[place/mecca]] to [[place/medina]]. Muslims later chose this migration, the *hijra*, as the starting point of their calendar.

## What happened
After years of hardship in Mecca, a group from Medina (then called Yathrib) pledged to protect the Prophet, and his followers began to leave Mecca for the oasis in small groups. The Prophet left with [[person/abu-bakr]] and reached the outskirts of Medina in the autumn of 622 CE; [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib]] followed shortly after {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}.

The traditional date of arrival at Quba is 12 Rabi' I, year 1 AH. Historians discuss the exact chronology, so the dates here are marked as tradition {cite:watt-1956-medina}.

## Why it matters
The community that formed in Medina became the first Muslim polity. About fifteen years later the caliph Umar had the Hijri calendar begin with 1 Muharram of this year, which corresponds to 16 July 622 CE in the Julian calendar.

## For young visitors
The Hijra is a journey that started a new calendar. When someone says "year 1447 AH", they are counting years from it.
