[[person/amina-bint-wahb|Amina bint Wahb]] took her orphaned son, then six years old, to Yathrib to visit his father's grave among her maternal kin, her servant [[person/umm-ayman|Umm Ayman]] with them; she stayed in Yathrib a month. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

On the way back to Mecca a sudden illness struck her at the village of al-Abwa between the two sanctuaries; she died there and was buried there, and of his household only his grandfather [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] remained to her son — he brought him back to Mecca and gathered him into his arms until he died two years later. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
