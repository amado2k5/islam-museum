## Summary
The Prophet stayed at Tabuk a "bid'" of nights — fewer than twenty — without exceeding them, then turned back homeward to Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## The account
The Prophet stayed at Tabuk a "bid'" of nights — fewer than twenty — without exceeding them, then turned back homeward to Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/170}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- "A bid' of nights" is an indefinite count between thirteen and nineteen; it has not been converted to a figure.
