## Overview
In 72 AH [[person/musab-ibn-al-zubayr|Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr]] marched out with the people of Iraq against [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] in Syria. Abd al-Malik halted at the Monastery of the Catholicos in the land of al-Sawad, and banners of Rabi'a joined him. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p. 216}

## Mus'ab's death
Those with Mus'ab from Egypt and Yemen abandoned him, so he was left with seven men. He fought until he was killed, and his head was brought to Abd al-Malik. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p. 218} {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p. 220}

The book gives the year in the Hijri calendar, and the date has not been converted to the Gregorian calendar here. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p. 216}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Abd al-Malik wrote to Ibrahim ibn al-Ashtar offering him the Euphrates and all it watered; Ibrahim handed the letter to Mus'ab unopened and refused, warning him that the other commanders had been written to, but Mus'ab would not let him imprison them. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 312} In the morning those commanders went over to Abd al-Malik, Ibrahim was killed, and Mus'ab fought on foot until he was killed, on Thursday the fifteenth of Jumada al-Ula, 72. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 313}
