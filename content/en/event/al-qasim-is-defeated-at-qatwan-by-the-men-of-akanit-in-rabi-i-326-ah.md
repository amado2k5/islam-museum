## Overview
A battle takes place in eastern Hamdan, near Akanit, at a place called Qatwan. The people of Akanit camp against al-Qasim ibn Ahmad, his men are routed and many are killed, and he returns to Warwar.

## Detail
The book says a battle took place in eastern Hamdan, near Akanit, at a place called Qatwan. The people of Akanit camped against al-Qasim ibn Ahmad, his men were routed, many of them were killed, and he returned to Warwar and stayed there. This was in Rabi I 326 AH. The book gives no number for the dead beyond saying many.
