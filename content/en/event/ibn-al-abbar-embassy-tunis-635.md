## Overview
When James I of Aragon besieged Valencia in Ramadan 635 (April–May 1238), Ibn al-Abbar was sent with a deputation to the Hafsid sovereign Abu Zakariyya' of Tunis to beg assistance; he recited before him his qasida rhyming in sin on 4 Muharram, painting a moving picture of his besieged native city {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 705}. The sultan hastened to aid its people, loading fleets with money, provisions and clothing {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 464}.
