## Overview
Ibn Khaldun in "al-Ibar" relates the affair of Utrar around 615 AH: the governor of the city killed merchants who had come from the lands of Genghis Khan, and this was a prelude to the Tatar invasion. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## What happened
Merchants from the lands of Genghis Khan came to Utrar, where Inal Khan, a cousin of the Khwarazm Shah, was. He found their property, and reported to the sultan that they were spies on the lands. The sultan ordered him to seize them, so he took their property and killed them in secret. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## After the affair
Genghis Khan sent to deny this and to demand Inal Khan. The sultan was agitated and killed the envoys. Genghis Khan marched with his armies to his lands. Ibn Khaldun regards the affair as the cause of the Tatars' conquest of the lands of Islam. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
