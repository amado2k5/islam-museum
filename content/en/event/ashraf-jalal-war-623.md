## Summary
In 623 AH al-Malik al-Ashraf's deputy at Akhlat struck troops of Jalal al-Din ibn Khwarazmshah returning from a raid on the Erzurum districts, and recovered their spoils. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 463}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that when Jalal al-Din marched to Kirman he left troops at Tiflis with his vizier Sharaf al-Mulk; their provisions ran short so they raided the Erzurum districts, plundered them, took women captive and gathered countless spoils, and their route home passed along the borders of the wilaya of Akhlat. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 463} Al-Ashraf's deputy at Akhlat, al-Hajib Husam al-Din Ali al-Mawsili, heard of them, gathered the army, marched on them and struck them, recovered the spoils they carried, took much booty from them and returned with his armies safe. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 464} Jalal al-Din's vizier then feared them and wrote to his master at Kirman reporting the situation, urging him to come and warning him of the consequence of delay and neglect, so he returned. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 464}
