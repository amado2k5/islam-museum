## Overview
Around 92 AH [[person/tariq-ibn-ziyad|Tariq ibn Ziyad]] sent Mughith al-Rumi with seven hundred horsemen to [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]]. A shepherd showed them a gap in the wall, and they climbed to it from a fig tree and took the city by force. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 297-299}

## The king of the city
Its king had taken refuge in a church on the west side of the city until the Muslims cut off its water channel. He fled, and Mughith caught up with him near Tatlira. al-Maqqari relates that a drizzle from the sky hid the sound of the horses' hoofs. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 297-299}
