## Overview
The Russians occupied Edirne in the Russo-Ottoman war of 1828-9, an occupation that deeply affected the city's Muslim population {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## The occupation and the Muslims
Muslims began emigrating while Christians moved in from the surrounding villages; Mahmud II visited Edirne for some ten days to raise Muslim morale, ordered a large bridge built over the Meriç and had commemorative coins struck {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.
