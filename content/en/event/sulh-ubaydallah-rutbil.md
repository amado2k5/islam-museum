## Overview
Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan removed al-Rabi ibn Ziyad al-Harithi and appointed [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-abi-bakra|Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra]] over Sistan. He raided as far as Zaran, and [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] sent to him asking for peace over his lands and those of Kabul for a million and two hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Completing the peace
Ubayd Allah accepted, and Rutbil asked him to remit two hundred thousand, which he did, so the peace was concluded at a million dirhams. Ubayd Allah then went to Ziyad and informed him, and Ziyad confirmed the peace. Ubayd Allah returned to Sistan and stayed there until Ziyad died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
