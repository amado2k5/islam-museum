## Overview
Muslim ibn Abbad al-Akili arrives with Banu Nahr, Banu Kulayb and Banu Jama'a on Friday, nineteen nights into Ramadan 326 AH. Ibn al-Dahhak and al-Hasan ibn Ahmad had written to him to plunder Saada, and fighting follows in the town.

## Detail
The book says Muslim ibn Abbad al-Akili arrived with Banu Nahr, Banu Kulayb and Banu Jama'a on Friday, nineteen nights into Ramadan of the same year. Ibn al-Dahhak and al-Hasan ibn Ahmad had written to him to plunder Saada. He was helped by the men of Saada who were of his kin and clients, and by the foolish men who inclined to them, and he entered the Akil quarter. The people fought from Friday to Monday, and the war wore them out.
