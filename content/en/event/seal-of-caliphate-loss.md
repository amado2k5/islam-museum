## Overview
The Messenger of God had a silver ring made, engraved "Muhammad, Messenger of God". The caliphs used the seal in turn: [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], then [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], then [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]]. It fell from Uthman's hand into the well, in the middle of his caliphate according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The search
The well was drained and it could not be found. Uthman then made a new ring engraved "Muhammad, Messenger of God" in three lines. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Accounts of the ring
Al-Baladhuri relates from al-Hasan that the ring was of silver, and its bezel was Abyssinian. He relates from Anas that the Prophet said he had made a ring, and forbade anyone to engrave on its design. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
