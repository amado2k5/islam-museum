## Overview
The earthquake of 1855 CE struck Bursa's monuments hard, hitting the Orhan Gazi, Yıldırım Bayezid and Emir Sultan mosques. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 38, 41, 49}

## The damaged monuments
The Orhan Gazi Mosque suffered serious damage later repaired {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 38}; the Yıldırım Bayezid Mosque lost one of its minarets, never replaced {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 41}; and the Emir Sultan Mosque was damaged and rebuilt {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 49}.
