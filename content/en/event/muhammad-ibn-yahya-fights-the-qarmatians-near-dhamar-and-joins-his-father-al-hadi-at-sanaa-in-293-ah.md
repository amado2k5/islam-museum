## Overview
Muhammad ibn Yahya fought the Qarmatians in the Dhamar district. When they advanced on Dhamar he left and joined his father al-Hadi at Sanaa.

## Detail
Muhammad ibn Yahya fought the Qarmatians in that district. When the Qarmatians grew stronger, and Isa al-Yafi'i helped them, and they marched on Dhamar, Muhammad ibn Yahya went out and joined his father al-Hadi at Sanaa.
