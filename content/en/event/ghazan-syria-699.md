## The Ghazan disturbance in Syria in 699 AH

In Syria there was the disturbance of Ghazan, king of the Tatars. Among the elders of hadith in Damascus and the Mountain, meaning al-Salihiyya, more than one hundred people died. About four hundred were killed in the mountain or died of cold and hunger {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 775}.

About four thousand of them were taken captive, among them seventy from the descendants of Shaykh Abu ʿUmar {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 775}.
