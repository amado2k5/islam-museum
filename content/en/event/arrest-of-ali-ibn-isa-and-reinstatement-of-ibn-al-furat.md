## Overview
In 304 AH the vizier Ali ibn Isa was arrested on Monday, eight nights into Dhu al-Hijja, and was confined in the house of al-Muqtadir. On the same day Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Musa ibn al-Furat was invested with the vizierate. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.59}

## Note
The date is taken from al-Tabari's account of the year, and the report is his alone. The Gregorian conversion is derived.
