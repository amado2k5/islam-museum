## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad says [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] left for [[place/najran|Najran]] on the second of Dhu al-Hijja 286 AH, leaving [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] as governor at Sa'da and leaving the narrator with him, with no troops. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.159} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} He went on until he halted at [[place/al-batna-banu-sad-yemen|al-Batna]], a land of [[group/banu-sad-khawlan|Banu Sa'd of Khawlan]], and sent to them, and a great army gathered to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160}

## The road
Next morning he set out for Najran and spent the night at a place called [[place/al-rakb-yemen|al-Rakb]], for which the editor refers to the "Mu'jam al-Buldan", entry al-Rakb. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} He set out again, and as he drew near Najran the [[group/wadia-tribe-yemen|the Wadi'is]] met him, then, not far on, Abu Muhammad and Abu al-Husayn met him with the people of the fortress from Shakir and Thaqif. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} The museum reads Abu Muhammad as Abdullah ibn al-Husayn, as before, and does not name Abu al-Husayn further.

## Halting at the fortress
He went on until he stopped at the fortress, had his tent pitched in ploughland belonging to the fortress, his companions camped, and he spent that night there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} The day of departure is 2 Dhu al-Hijja 286, which is 9 December 899 Julian by the tabular calendar; the book's own count may run a day ahead.
