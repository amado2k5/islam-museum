## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad's poem opens at a ruined encampment and then calls the imam to go out to Najran.

## Detail
A poem in the kamil metre opens with the poet standing before an encampment that has fallen into ruin and lost its people, and recalls the pleasures of his youth there. The book prints a long description of the women of those days; the museum records only the form of the opening, not that description. By p. 332 the poem turns from the remembered place to a call to set it aside and to a call to action, and then to praise of the imam. The poem is in a Zaydi book, in the voice of the imam's followers; its praise and pride belong to the poet.
