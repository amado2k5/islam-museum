## Summary
Sultan Shah, lord of Merv and the lands around it in Khurasan, died in Ramadan, and his brother Malikshah took over. Sultan Shah had long been at odds with Khwarazm-shah Tekish, until he brought the Qara Khitai against him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 228}
