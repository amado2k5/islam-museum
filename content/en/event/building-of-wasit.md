## Overview
al-Tabari mentions that al-Hajjaj laid out the city of Wasit in 83 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.384}

## al-Tabari's account
He reports that a monk told al-Hajjaj that a mosque would be built at that place. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.384}

## The Salabi book
The book says that al-Hajjaj laid out Wasit in the land of Kaskar. Building began in 83 AH and was finished in 86 AH, after Abd al-Malik gave his approval. The revenue of the whole of Iraq was spent on it for five years. It contains the congregational mosque, the governor's house and the markets. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.20}
