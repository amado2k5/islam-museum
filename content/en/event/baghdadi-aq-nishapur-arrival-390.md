## Summary
Abd al-Qahir ibn Tahir ibn Muhammad al-Tamimi, Abu Mansur, was born and raised in Baghdad, then travelled with his father Abu Abdallah Tahir to Khurasan and settled at Nishapur; the father was a man of wealth and honour, and the son spent it on the people of learning and hadith without earning money by his own scholarship {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 634} {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 238}; the date is approximate, before his studies under Abu Ishaq al-Isfarayini who died in 418 {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba pp. 634-635}.
