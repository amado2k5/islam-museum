## Overview
Abdallah ibn Bistam and Wada'a ordered a raid on Raha, the sides fought, and al-Hadi sent his son Ahmad ibn Yahya, who turned Banu Rabi'a and their companions back to his father.

## Detail
When Abdallah ibn Bistam and Wada'a saw that the Ya'am had sought safety and gone over to al-Hadi, they ordered a group to raid Raha and fight its people and stir up strife, hoping that al-Hadi would move his army to Raha and that the whole camp would be caught in the raid, so that Banu Rabi'a could take vengeance on the Ya'am. When Shakir saw that people had gathered against the people of Raha, they joined the fighting. Al-Hadi sent his son Ahmad ibn Yahya with a group of cousins. When they reached Raha they found the two sides already in combat: three men of Wada'a and one of the Ya'am fell, and there were many wounds. Al-Hadi feared that the whole army would follow him and fall on the people of Raha, so he sent his son to turn them back. Ahmad ibn Yahya turned Banu Rabi'a and their companions away and brought them to al-Hadi, who took sureties from them and from a clan of the Ya'am. The names of the dead are uncertain on the page image and are not recorded here.
