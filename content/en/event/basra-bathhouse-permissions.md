## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/muslim-ibn-abi-bakra|Muslim ibn Abi Bakra]] used to take a thousand dirhams a day from his bathhouse, and a great deal of food. When he fell ill, he entrusted to his brother [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-abi-bakra|Abd al-Rahman]] the bathhouse and its revenue. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Disclosing the revenue
Abd al-Rahman spread the news and asked the sultan's permission to build a bathhouse, and he was granted it. Bathhouses could not be built in [[place/basra|Basra]] except with the permission of the governors. Other notables then asked, among them Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra and al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As, and they were granted it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Muslim recovered from his illness to find that the revenue of his bathhouse had been spoiled, and he began to blame his brother. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
