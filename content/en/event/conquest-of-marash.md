## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Ubayda, while he was at Manbij, sent [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] to the district of [[place/marash|Marash]]. He took its fort on condition that its people leave, and then destroyed it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.265}

## Building and ruin
[[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] built the city of Marash and settled troops in it. When Yazid ibn Mu'awiya died, the Byzantine raids increased and they moved away from it. In 75 AH the Muslims and the Byzantines met in the depths of Marash and fought very fiercely, and the Byzantines were routed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.265}

Then al-Abbas ibn al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik resettled it, fortified it and built a congregational mosque for it. In the days of Marwan ibn Muhammad the Byzantines besieged it until its people made peace with them on condition that they leave, and then they destroyed it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.266}

## The last building
[[person/salih-ibn-ali|Salih ibn Ali]] built it in the caliphate of Abu Ja'far al-Mansur and fortified it, and [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] increased its garrison after he became caliph. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.266}

The date is approximate and derived, around 18 AH for the first conquest.
