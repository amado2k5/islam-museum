## Summary
In Shawwal 1 AH (April 623 CE) the Prophet sent a second detachment under his cousin [[person/ubayda-ibn-al-harith|Ubayda ibn al-Harith ibn al-Muttalib]], with sixty riders of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]]. At Batn [[place/rabigh|Rabigh]] they met a company of Quraysh. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

## The encounter and the shooting
The detachment's banner was white and was carried by [[person/mistah-ibn-uthatha|Mistah ibn Uthatha]]. Over Quraysh was [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan Sakhr ibn Harb]] (or, it is said, Ikrima ibn Abi Jahl), with two hundred riders. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}

The two sides exchanged arrows and did not draw their swords. [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] shot an arrow at the polytheists, and it was the first arrow shot in the cause of God in Islam. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}

## The two Companions' defection
Two Companions who had come out with the Meccan army as a way of escape joined the detachment: [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad ibn Amr al-Bahrani]] and [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan al-Mazini]]. They crossed over to the Muslims and returned with them to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/597}


## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.197–200)
In Shawwal of the first year of the Hijra the Prophet sent Ubayda ibn al-Harith with sixty riders. He met Abu Sufyan with two hundred men at Batn Rabigh. The two sides exchanged arrows without fighting. Miqdad ibn Amr and Utba ibn Ghazwan, who were Muslims who had gone out with the disbelievers, joined the Muslims. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.
