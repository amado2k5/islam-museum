## Summary
In Shawwal 1 AH (April 623 CE) the Prophet sent a second detachment under his cousin [[person/ubayda-ibn-al-harith|Ubayda ibn al-Harith ibn al-Muttalib]] with sixty riders of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]]; they met a company of Quraysh at Batn [[place/rabigh|Rabigh]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 198}

## The encounter and the shooting
The detachment's banner was white, carried by [[person/mistah-ibn-uthatha|Mistah ibn Uthatha]]; over Quraysh was [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan Sakhr ibn Harb]] (or, it is said, Ikrima ibn Abi Jahl) with two hundred riders. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 198} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}

The two sides exchanged arrows without drawing swords; [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] shot the polytheists with an arrow — the first arrow shot in God's cause in Islam. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 198}

## The two Companions' defection
The detachment witnessed the joining of two Companions who had come out with Mecca's army as a means of escape: [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad ibn Amr al-Bahrani]] and [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan al-Mazini]] crossed over to the Muslims and returned with them to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 198} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/597}
