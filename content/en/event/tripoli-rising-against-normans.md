## Overview
When [[person/roger-of-sicily|Roger of Sicily]] took [[place/tarabulus-gharb|Tripoli]] in Ifriqiya, he left the Muslims in it under his rule. Ibn Khaldun relates that [[person/abu-yahya-ibn-matruh|Abu Yahya ibn Matruh]], one of the notables of the city, called the leading men to attack the Christians, and they rose against them and burned them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1638}

## After the rising
Ibn Matruh remained governor of Tripoli until Abd al-Mu'min appointed him over it in 555 AH, sending him there and returning its people to it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1638}

The date is approximate, around 548 AH, and the conversion is derived. A second source is needed for the date.
