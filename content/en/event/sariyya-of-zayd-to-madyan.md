## Overview
A report that Ibn Hisham relates, not Ibn Ishaq, from Abd Allah ibn Hasan ibn Hasan from his mother Fatima bint al-Husayn ibn Ali: the Prophet sent [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] toward [[place/madyan|Madyan]], with [[person/dumayra-mawla-ali|Dumayra]], a client of Ali ibn Abi Talib, and a brother of his. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284}

## The captives
Fatima said: he took captives from the people of Mina'a, that is the coasts, where there were mixed people; they were sold and separated from one another. The Prophet went out to them while they were weeping, and said: what is the matter with them? He was told: they have been separated. He said: do not sell them except all together. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284} Ibn Hisham explains that he meant the mothers and the children. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284}

## In the museum
The report belongs to the origins of the chapter on forbidding the separation of a mother from her child in the sale of slaves; it is among the few places in the raids where the weeping of captives is heard. It is read with [[event/capture-and-release-of-hatims-daughter|the report of Safana, daughter of Hatim]]. Its chain is of the People of the House: Fatima bint al-Husayn from an unnamed person. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284}

## Marks of the report
- "Abd Allah ibn Hasan ibn Hasan" in the printed text, and "Abd Allah ibn Husayn ibn Hasan" in one copy, which the editors consider a distortion. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284}
- The words "and the expedition of Zayd ibn Haritha to Madyan" are missing from one copy. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/284}
- "Mina'" is explained by the report as the coasts; its location is not fixed (needs_source).
