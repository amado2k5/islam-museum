## Overview
[[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]] says he saw [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at night when the boy brought a tray with three loaves and sesame oil, and the narrator ate with him; al-Hadi said: "Praise be to God, Abu Jafar; with enjoining good and forbidding wrong this is plenty." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.57} The report is in a Zaydi book and the words are attributed to the imam.

## The orphan
One day a small orphan boy was brought to al-Hadi; he kept drawing him closer until he sat before him, stroked his head, spoke some words over him and wept, then ordered him a shirt and trousers. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.57}

## The cousin and the small supper
One night he invited a man of his cousins to supper; the servant brought a little food and the cousin laughed. Asked why, he said that al-Hadi would not accept the like for anyone who came to him in his own country, where he used to serve many kinds of food. Al-Hadi said: "This is food with jihad." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.57} The cousin is not named.

## Two orphans at the mosque
Ali ibn Muhammad says he saw from al-Hadi more than he could count: he would go out to the mosque to pray or on some need, and a small child, an old woman or a man would speak to him and he would stand with them a long time while people stood by until they had asked their needs, which he met with clothing, food or other help; he once saw him leave the mosque when two small boys stood and said "O son of the Messenger of God, we are orphans", and he stayed with them a long time stroking their heads and praying for them, and ordered them clothing and provision. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.58} See [[concept/sadaqa]].
