## Summary
al-Suyuti, in *Tarikh al-Khulafa*, reports that in 321 AH the caliph [[person/al-qahir-billah|al-Qahir Billah]] ordered singing-girls and wine to be prohibited. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 597-598}

## The measures
Singers were arrested, effeminate men were exiled, instruments of entertainment were broken, and slave-girls who sang were ordered to be sold as "simple" women. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 597-598}

## The author's remark
al-Suyuti adds that al-Qahir, despite all this, did not sober up from drink and never tired of listening to singing. This is the author's own remark. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 597-598}
