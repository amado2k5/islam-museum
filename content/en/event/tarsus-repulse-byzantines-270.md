## Overview
In Rabi al-Awwal 270 AH the news reached Baghdad that the Byzantines had camped at the district of the Bab al-Tamma, six miles from [[place/tarsus|Tarsus]], some hundred thousand strong, led by the patrician Andreas. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p.74-75}

## The battle
The servant Zaman went out to them by night and attacked them by surprise, and killed their chief and a great many of his companions, said to have reached seventy thousand. The call to arms against Andreas was on Tuesday, the seventh of Rabi al-Awwal. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p.74-75}

## The booty
Ibn al-Jawzi lists what was taken: seven crosses of gold and silver, fifteen thousand beasts and mules, saddles, ornamented swords and belts, four golden chairs, two hundred golden torques, and about ten thousand banners. The numbers are as Ibn al-Jawzi gives them. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p.74-75}
