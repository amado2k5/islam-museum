## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad said: "Al-Hadi entered the village of al-Hajar on Monday, the day of the seventeenth of Sha'ban, stayed there, gave the people safety and they settled in their homes" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}.

## Detail
He stayed on that way, and Ibn Humayd had not yet come to him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}. The date is derived from the tabular calendar: 17 Sha'ban 292 AH is 24 June 905 in the Julian calendar and falls on a Monday as the text says, so the two calendars agree on this day, as on the Sunday in Rajab. Two weekdays that agree with the tabular count support 292 as the year of these events; the next page nevertheless says "the year 291" for the move to Sa'da in Dhu al-Qa'da, a conflict within the text itself that the museum records in [[event/al-hadi-leaves-najran-for-saada-in-dhu-al-qada-292|the event on the departure]]. It follows [[event/banu-khuthaym-and-ibn-bustam-ask-safety-and-it-is-given-292|the granting of safety]]; that the people settled is the report of the imam's supporter in a Zaydi book, which the museum neither confirms nor denies. Al-Hajar is a town of Najran met earlier at [[event/al-hadi-breaches-the-wall-of-al-hajar-286|the breaching of the wall of al-Hajar in 286]].
