## Overview
Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur leaves Sanaa for Shiban on Thursday, eight nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 298 AH. He goes up the mountain, drives the Qarmatians out and takes the fortress of Sharib by force.

## Detail
The book says Asad entered Shiban, went up the mountain and drove the Qarmatians out, then took the fortress of Sharib by force and stayed some days. He then heard that Ibn al-Fadl had set out from al-Mudhaykhira for Sanaa, and Ibn Kayyala withdrew from Dhamar. The next event continues this round of the war.
