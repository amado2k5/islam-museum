## Overview
In Rabi al-Akhir 867 AH a great flood struck [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and entered the Sacred Mosque until the water rose to about the height of the Ka'ba's door-lock, according to the "Shadharat al-Dhahab". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 314}

## What the source says
The source gives the month and the year and describes the height of the water in the Sacred Mosque. The data gives no details of losses or the length of the flood, which needs an additional source.

## Date
Rabi al-Akhir 867 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around January 1463 CE) that is derived. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 314}
