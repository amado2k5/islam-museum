## Overview
When the news reached [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] he called those of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] who were with him at [[place/najran|Najran]] under safe-conduct and the people of settled homes among them, told them that he had heard so and so and that the men were marching on them, and that they should meet them, or some of them, counsel them, restrain them from this folly and remind them of his pardon and his favours first and last, give excuse and warning and call them to God, which was better, more useful and safer for them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.168}

## What they thought and his answer
Banu al-Harith rejoiced at his words and thought it was awe of them and of their companions, and rose from him nudging one another; when he saw this he sent after them and brought them back and told them not to imagine his words came from awe, but were proof against them, an excuse and a warning, and that if the men agreed and fought, God would give al-Hadi a mighty victory over them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.168}

## What he foretold
As the narrator reports, al-Hadi said he could see them hanging by their feet in the trees around the village, a group in every tree, until the village stank with the smell of their corpses, and that they would then come to him and ask him to give them the bodies to bury, and he would not give them until after much trouble, many words and a request for peace; he closed by committing his affair to God and with two verses, [[passage/quran-40-44|Ghafir 40:44]] and [[passage/quran-38-88|Sad 38:88]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.168} This is the imam's speech as the narrator gives it, and what followed is in [[event/al-hadi-routs-banu-al-harith-and-the-forty-horsemen-286]] and [[event/al-hadi-hangs-the-slain-of-banu-al-harith-and-lets-them-be-buried-286]].
