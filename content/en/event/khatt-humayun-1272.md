## Overview
The Imperial Edict (Khatt-i Humayun) was issued in 1856 CE (1272 AH) in the name of the sultan [[person/abd-al-majid-i-uthmani|Abdulmejid I]], confirming the principles of the Gülhane edict and adding privileges and immunities for the non-Muslim subjects of the state. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.376} It was read out in [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]].

## What it contained
Al-Salabi describes it as bolder than the first and more eager to borrow from the West. Among its contents: the abolition of the tax-farming system, the end of bribery and corruption, equality in conscription between Muslims and others, and equal treatment of all subjects whatever their religions and schools. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.376}
