## Summary
Al-Daraqutni died at the end of Tuesday, 7 Dhu al-Qa'da 385, and was buried at Maqbarat Ma'ruf on the Wednesday, aged seventy-nine years and two days {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.184}; al-Dhahabi gives the eighth of Dhu al-Qa'da {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.105}.

## His grave and Ibn Shahin's news
The Shadharat has him buried near Ma'ruf al-Karkhi, aged eighty, and Abu Hafs Ibn Shahin dying a month after him {cite:shadharat-dhahab pp.116-117}.
