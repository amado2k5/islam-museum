## Summary
[[person/amr-ibn-salim|Amr ibn Salim al-Khuza'i]] set out after the [[event/khuzaa-bakr-clash-al-watir|al-Watir]] attack until he reached Medina, where he stood before the Prophet in the mosque adjuring him by the covenant of their fathers and begging his aid for Banu Ka'b {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36}. Ibn Ishaq said: "this was part of what stirred the conquest of Mecca" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36}.

## What happened
Amr ibn Salim stood in the mosque of [[place/medina|Medina]] and recited his famous verses opening "O Lord, I adjure Muhammad / by the ancient covenant of my father and his", asking aid for Banu Ka'b and describing how they were surprised at al-Watir and killed kneeling and prostrate {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36}.

The Prophet said to him: "you are granted aid, Amr ibn Salim" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37}. In al-Isti'ab's version: "may I not be aided if I do not aid Banu Ka'b ibn Abd Manaf and Abd al-Wudud with the aid I gave them" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37}. It is told that when Banu Ka'b entered the mosque afterwards they saw a cloud moving in the sky, and it was said: it is for Banu Ka'b {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37}.

Then [[person/budayl-ibn-warqa|Budayl ibn Warqa']] came with a party of Khuza'a and told the Prophet of Quraysh's backing of Banu Bakr and their breaking of the treaty {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37}. The Prophet ordered preparations — the arming of an army — and said: "it is as if I see Abu Sufyan coming to tighten the pact and lengthen its term" — and so it was {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37}.

## Significance
The appeal turned a local breach into a matter of covenant: the Prophet's ally had cried for help after Quraysh broke the treaty by supplying the aggressors, and the answer became a state's decision, not a tribe's intercession {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/36-37}. Directly after came [[event/abu-sufyan-medina-mission|Abu Sufyan's failed mission]], then [[event/hatib-letter-to-quraysh|Hatib's letter]], then the march on Mecca {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/37-42}.

## For young visitors
A man of Khuza'a stood in the mosque reciting poetry asking help for his people after they were attacked at night. Their Prophet answered him in a single word: "help is granted." It was the start of the road to Mecca.
