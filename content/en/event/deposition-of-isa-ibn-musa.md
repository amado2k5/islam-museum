## Overview
In 147 AH the caliph [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] removed his uncle [[person/isa-ibn-musa|Isa ibn Musa]] from the heir apparentship, and named his son [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] instead. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.425}

## Background
Al-Saffah had named Isa as the successor after al-Mansur. Isa was the one who fought for al-Mansur against the two brothers Muhammad and Ibrahim, and defeated them. Al-Suyuti says that al-Mansur rewarded him by removing him from the succession against his will. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.425}
