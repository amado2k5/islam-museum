## Overview
Asad sends his commanders to Radda. They take the claimant of prophecy captive, without a pledge of safety, and bring him to Asad on Eid al-Fitr 324 AH, and Asad has him imprisoned.

## Detail
The book says Asad sent his commanders and captains to Radda and wrote to its people, and many of them gave him their pledge. The men then went after the claimant of prophecy until they took him captive, not under any pledge of safety, and brought him to Asad humbled on Eid al-Fitr 324 AH. Asad had him kept in prison. The author adds that God defeated the claimant's hope and undid his schemes; that is the author's judgement, not part of the narrative.
