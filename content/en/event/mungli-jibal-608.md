## Summary
In 608 AH a mamluk named Mankli seized the Jibal lands, Isfahan and what lies between, and their lord Shams al-Din Aytaqmish fled him to Baghdad, where the caliph showed him favor. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 356}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that in Sha'ban of this year Aytaqmish, lord of Hamadhan, Isfahan, Rayy and the lands between, came to Baghdad fleeing Mankli; the cause was that Aytaqmish had grown so powerful — his armies so numerous — that he had besieged his own master Abu Bakr ibn al-Bahlawan, lord of Azerbaijan, whereupon a mamluk named Mankli rose against him, disputed the country with him, won the obedience of the Bahlawan mamluks and seized it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 356} When Aytaqmish reached Baghdad the caliph ordered a ceremonial reception: all the people went out and the day of his arrival was famous. His wife arrived in Ramadan in a mahmil, was honored and lodged with her husband, and he resided in Baghdad until the year 610. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 356}
