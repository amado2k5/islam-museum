## Summary
Rayy was taken through the cunning of al-Zaynabi, Abu al-Furqan. Its ruler, Siyawakhsh ibn Mihran, broke ranks and met Nu'aym ibn Muqarrin seeking terms, and said to him: "The people are many and you are few. Send horsemen with me, and I will lead them into their city by an entrance they will not notice, while you attack them." Nu'aym sent the horsemen of his nephew al-Mundhir ibn Amr in at night without the people noticing. Nu'aym then fell upon them, keeping them occupied away from their city, until they heard the takbir behind them; they fled and were slaughtered in a massacre so great it was tallied with reed stalks. God granted the Muslims spoils at Rayy in an amount similar to what there was at al-Mada'in. Nu'aym ruined the old city and ordered al-Zaynabi to build the new Rayy, and the honour of Rayy remained with its people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 407-408}
