## Overview
The Qarmatians took the city of Ja'far ibn Ibrahim al-Manahi in mid-Rabi' I 292 AH. He fled to al-Qurtab near Zabid and was later killed with his cousin Abu al-Futuh ibn Abi Salama at Wadi Nakhla.

## Detail
The Qarmatians had appeared in Yemen and taken control of the east, of Taman and of Mount Musur. They fought Ja'far ibn Ibrahim al-Manahi and drove him out of his city, and took it in mid-Rabi' I 292 AH. He fled with his sons and household to a place called al-Qurtab in the region of Zabid. He asked Ibrahim ibn Muhammad for help and was refused, and he went back to the edge of his lands for fear of being sheltered by anyone. He came to a place called Wadi Nakhla and fought them there; some of those with him turned against him, the fortress he was in was entered, his army was routed, and he and his cousin Abu al-Futuh ibn Abi Salama were killed. The Qarmatians held his city.
