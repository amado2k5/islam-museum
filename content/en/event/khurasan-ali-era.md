## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/mahwiya|Mahwiya]], the marzban of Merv, came to Ali in Kufa, and Ali wrote to him for the landowners, the cavalrymen and the dihqans to pay him the poll tax. Then Khurasan rose against them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The envoy
Ali sent [[person/jada-ibn-hubayra|Ja'da ibn Hubayra al-Makhzumi]] and his mother Umm Hani daughter of Abu Talib. He did not conquer it, and Khurasan was not seen at peace until Ali was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governors
Abu Ubayda mentions that the first of Ali's governors over Khurasan was [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-abra|Abd al-Rahman ibn Abra]], a client of Khuza'a, followed by Ja'da. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
