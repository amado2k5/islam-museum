## Overview
al-Suyuti reports that in 195 AH [[person/muhammad-al-amin|al-Amin]] appointed [[person/ali-ibn-isa-ibn-mahan|Ali ibn Isa ibn Mahan]] over the mountain lands: Hamadhan, Nihawand, Qum and Isfahan. He left Baghdad in mid Jumada al-Akhira with forty thousand men to fight [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 475}

## The battle
al-Ma'mun sent [[person/tahir-ibn-al-husayn|Tahir ibn al-Husayn]] against him with fewer than four thousand men, and the victory went to Tahir. Ali was killed and his army was routed. His head was carried to al-Ma'mun, who had it paraded through Khurasan, and he was greeted with the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 475}

al-Suyuti relates that the news came to al-Amin while he was fishing, and he told the messenger to leave it, because Kawthar had caught two fish and he had caught nothing. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 475}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Ali ibn Isa ibn Mahan marched with sixty thousand men, that Tahir ibn al-Husayn had about ten thousand, and that Ali was struck by an arrow that pierced his armour and died. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 396} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 397} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 398}
