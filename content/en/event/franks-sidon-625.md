## Summary
In 625 AH large numbers of Franks set out from their countries in the west to their holdings in Syria, and they began rebuilding the city of Sidon. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 477}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that many Franks left their western countries that year — Sicily and the lands beyond it — bound for their Syrian possessions of Acre, Tyre and the other places on the Syrian coast, so their numbers grew. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 477} Another group had set out before these, he notes, though circumstances had prevented them from acting; as he presents it, this shows the continued arrival of Frankish reinforcements on the Syrian coast and their growing strength there in those years. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 477}
