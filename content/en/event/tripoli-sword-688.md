## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that the sultan [[person/qalawun|Qalawun]] took Tripoli by the sword in 688 AH; it had been in the hands of the Franks since 503 AH, and its first conquest was in the time of Mu'awiya. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.729-730}

## The report
He relates that Taj al-Din ibn al-Athir composed a letter bearing the good news to the lord of Yemen. Some said that the meaning of "Tripoli" in the Roman tongue is three fortresses gathered together. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.729-730}

A second source is needed for the date.
