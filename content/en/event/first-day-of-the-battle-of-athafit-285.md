## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] marched with all who had answered him, and as they neared [[place/athafit|Athafit]] he ordered the people to draw up for war. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} The account is Zaydi and in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum gives it as the narrators tell it, without a verdict.

## The order of the ranks
He put Banu Rabi'a and Banu Ma'mar of Hamdan and a group of Banu Sa'd of Khawlan in the centre, Banu Sarim on the right and Banu Ubayd of Banu Rabi'a on the left. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} See [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan]], [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan]], [[group/banu-sarim-hamdan]] and [[group/khawlan-tribe]]. [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|Al-Da'am]] and his companions came out of Athafit and also drew up; they came close, battle was joined and they fought until the afternoon prayer, and everyone had many wounds. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100}

## The garments
The narrator says that a day before the war al-Da'am had had his baggage taken out of Athafit, being about to leave it for fear of al-Hadi; when the war began he ordered "dastari" garments spread out and had some of his men call among the bedouins in al-Hadi's camp that whoever wanted clothes and covering should come to them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} The editor's footnote says he found this word in no genealogical work or dictionary and suggests it may come from the Persian "dastar", a turban cloth, or "dastaran", earnest money. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} A group of the bedouins who were with al-Hadi went over and al-Da'am clothed them; al-Hadi's army was shaken by this and al-Da'am's grew stronger, with many horse and men. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100}

## Al-Hadi comes down and the armies part
Seeing his army shaken, al-Hadi came down from the mountain, shouted to the people and urged them; his companions charged al-Da'am's army and drove them back, then both armies stood firm and went on fighting until night fell and they parted, each going to its camp. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} Al-Hadi's camp was at a place of Banu Rabi'a called [[place/karawi-banu-rabia-yemen|Karawi]], where water was scarce; he stayed there and when the water fell he moved on. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.100} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.101} See [[event/al-hadi-at-al-darb-and-abu-al-qasim-joins-285]].

## The date
The book does not date this fighting; it falls between 3 Shawwal 285 AH and Thursday the thirteenth, so only the month is recorded.
