## Summary
The Day of al-Buqay was one of the days of the Ansar. Al-Aws and al-Khazraj met at Buqay al-Gharqad and fought a fierce battle. The victory went to the uninvited intruder who enters a gathering while its people are drinking. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 505}
