## Overview
A war between the Banu Jahjaba of the Aws and the Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar of the Khazraj, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488-489}

## The cause
Ka'b ibn Amr al-Mazini had married a woman of the Banu Salim, and used to visit her. [[person/uhayha-ibn-al-julah|Uhayha ibn al-Julah]], lord of the Banu Jahjaba, sent a group who lay in wait for him and killed him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488-489}

## The fighting
Asim ibn Amr, the brother of the slain man, prepared with his people and told the Banu Jahjaba of the war. They met at al-Rahaba and fought fiercely; the Jahjaba were routed, and Uhayha fled with them. Asim caught up with Uhayha as he entered his fortress, shot him with an arrow, and killed a brother of his. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.488-489}

The two sides were [[group/al-aws|the Aws]] and [[group/al-khazraj|the Khazraj]].
