## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] wrote to his governors over Khurasan about those of Transoxiana who were not under obedience or Islam. He sent his envoys, who enrolled in the register those among them who wished, and their princes' sons. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Winning them over
He won them over with favour: when they came to his door he honoured them and gave them generous stipends and provisions. Then [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]], who succeeded him, followed the same course, until most of his army came from the people of Transoxiana: the Soghdians, the Farghanians, the Ushrusanians and the people of al-Shash. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The result
Al-Baladhuri says that Islam prevailed among them, and they began to raid the Turks beyond them. [[person/abd-allah-ibn-tahir|Abd Allah ibn Tahir]] sent his son [[person/tahir-ibn-abdallah|Tahir]] to raid the lands of Fawzia, and he took places that no one had reached before him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
