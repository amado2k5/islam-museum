## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet reported the shaytan schemes at the adhan so that people do not hear it; when it ends he returns, when the iqama is set he withdraws, and when it ends he returns to whisper between a man and himself, reminding him of what he had forgotten, until the man no longer knows how much he prayed {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-177|Muwatta 177]].

## The meaning
This hadith shows the shaytan's deception around the adhan and iqama; his aim is to waste the prayer or corrupt its count, which is why speech between adhan and iqama was forbidden {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
