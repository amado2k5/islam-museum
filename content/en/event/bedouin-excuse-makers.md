## Summary
The excuse-makers among the Bedouin came and made their excuses to the Prophet, but God did not accept them, in Ibn Ishaq's words; it was mentioned to him that they were a party of Banu Ghifar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## The account
The excuse-makers among the Bedouin came and made their excuses to him, and God did not accept them — in Ibn Ishaq's phrasing. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

Ibn Ishaq said: it was mentioned to me that they were a party of Banu Ghifar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- Ibn Ishaq said: "it was mentioned to me that they were a party of Banu Ghifar" — a marker of weak transmission (p. 162).
