## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Uzra ibn Qays, who was governor of Hulwan in Umar's caliphate, tried to conquer Shahrazur but could not. [[person/utba-ibn-farqad|Utba ibn Farqad]] then raided it and took it after a battle, on terms like those of Hulwan. Scorpions used to strike the Muslims there, and they would die. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The treaty and after
Utba made peace with the people of al-Samaghan and Dara-badhan for the jizya and the kharaj, on condition that they not be killed, enslaved, or prevented from any road they wished to take. He fought the Kurds, and wrote to Umar that his conquests had reached Azerbaijan, and Umar appointed him over it. Shahrazur remained joined to Mosul until it was separated at the end of al-Rashid's caliphate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
