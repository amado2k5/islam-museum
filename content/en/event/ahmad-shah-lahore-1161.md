## Overview
After the death of the Punjab governor Zakariyya Khan in 1158/1745 and the ensuing power struggle between his sons, the Afghan Ahmad Shah Durrani launched his first invasion of the Punjab and occupied Lahore in Muharram 1161/January 1748; following a treaty in the winter of 1165/1751-2 the city passed into the Afghan sphere of influence {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 38}.
