## Overview
A rift arose between the caliph [[person/al-mustarshid-billah|al-Mustarshid]] and the sultan [[person/masud-ibn-muhammad-saljuqi|Mas'ud ibn Muhammad]], and the caliph went out to fight him. Al-Suyuti says that most of his army betrayed him, and Mas'ud captured him and imprisoned him in a fortress near Hamadhan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}

## Baghdad mourns
The people of Baghdad threw dust on their heads, women came out lamenting the caliph, and prayers and the sermon were suspended. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}

## The killing
The sultan [[person/sanjar|Sanjar]] sent to his nephew Mas'ud ordering him to restore the caliph, and Mas'ud kissed the ground before him. Then seventeen of the Batiniyya came in Sanjar's army, attacked the caliph in his camp and killed him with a number of his companions. It is reported that Mas'ud did not know of them; it is also said that he was the one who sent them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}

This happened at [[place/maragha|Maragha]] on Thursday, the sixteenth of Dhu al-Qa'da 529 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.664-665}
