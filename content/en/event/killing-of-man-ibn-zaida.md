## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/man-ibn-zaida|Ma'n ibn Za'ida]] went to [[place/bust|Bust]] out of fear of winter, and a group of Kharijites who disapproved of his conduct killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
The Kharijites slipped in among builders working in his house, and hid their swords in bundles of reeds. They then entered upon him in his tent while he was being cupped, and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
[[person/yazid-ibn-mazyad|Yazid ibn Mazyad]] killed all the killers and then took over Sistan. Al-Baladhuri says that a forged letter was written in his name to al-Mansur, which led to his removal and imprisonment; he was then released and returned to his revenues. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
