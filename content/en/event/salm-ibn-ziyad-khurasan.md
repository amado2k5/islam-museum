## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Yazid ibn Mu'awiya appointed [[person/sallam-ibn-ziyad|Sallam ibn Ziyad]] over Khurasan, and the people of Khwarazm made peace with him for four hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governorship
Sallam crossed the river with his wife [[person/umm-muhammad-bint-abdallah|Umm Muhammad bint Abd Allah]], who was the first Arab woman to cross it with him. He came to Samarqand and its people gave him a thousand blood-money. A son was born to him whom he named al-Sughdi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Sallam sent an army to Khujanda, in which was [[person/asha-hamdan|al-A'sha of Hamdan]]; they were defeated. Then he raided from Merv, crossed the river, and killed [[person/bandun|Bandun]] the Soghdian, who had gathered the Soghd against him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
