## Overview
On the night of Friday Banu al-Harith gathered in their camp and took counsel. They agreed to send twenty men from each of their branches to form an ambush at one side of the village, and they did so.

## Detail
On the night of Friday Banu al-Harith gathered in their camp and took counsel, and their judgement settled on sending twenty men from each of their branches to form an ambush at one side of the village; they did so. The record ends here at the foot of page 369, where the narration continues on page 370 with Aqil ibn Abdallah's warning to Abu Ja'far, which belongs to the next packet.
