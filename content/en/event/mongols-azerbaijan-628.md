## Summary
At the opening of 628 AH the Tatars arrived from Transoxiana into Azerbaijan, devastating, plundering and killing, and seized Rayy, Hamadan and the lands between. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 490}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Tatars came from Transoxiana into Azerbaijan at the start of this year; their kingdom there had settled and its land was being rebuilt while the cities of Khurasan remained ruined, no Muslim daring to inhabit them, and parties of them kept raiding whatever lay in their path, until a company appeared in 625 and had the encounter with Jalal al-Din he related. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 490} When Jalal al-Din was defeated by Ala al-Din Kayqubad and by al-Ashraf in 627, the leader of the Ismailis wrote to the Tatars reporting Jalal al-Din's weakness after that defeat, urging them to march on him and guaranteeing victory; Jalal al-Din's rule and management were bad — he had made an enemy of every neighboring king — so when the Ismaili chief's letters reached them a Tatar party moved first into his lands, took Rayy, Hamadan and the lands between, then turned to Azerbaijan, ruining, plundering and killing whomever they caught of its people, while Jalal al-Din neither dared face them nor could bar them from the country. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 491}
