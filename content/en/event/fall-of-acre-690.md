## Overview
The "Fragments of Gold" records that in 690 AH whatever the Christians held in the land of Syria was taken, so that no fortress or stronghold remained to them there. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 420}

## What the source says
This is the text of the report in the available source, without detail of how the conquest went or who led it. An additional source is needed.
