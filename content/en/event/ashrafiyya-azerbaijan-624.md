## Summary
In Sha'ban of 624 AH al-Hajib Husam al-Din Ali, al-Malik al-Ashraf's deputy at Akhlat, marched into Azerbaijan and took Khoi, Marand, Nakhchivan and the places adjoining them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 473}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir explains the cause: Jalal al-Din's rule was unjust and his soldiers preyed on the subjects, and his wife, the daughter of the Seljuk sultan Tughril — formerly the wife of Uzbak ibn al-Bahlawan, lord of Azerbaijan, through whom she had governed all those lands — was neglected and ignored by Jalal al-Din; fearing him after being cut off from rule and command, she and the people of Khoi invited the Hajib Husam al-Din to come and take over the lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 473} He marched into Azerbaijan and took the city of Khoi and the neighbouring fortresses held by Jalal al-Din's wife, took Marand whose people surrendered through their scribe, then went on to Nakhchivan where they surrendered it too; their strength grew with those lands, and they returned to Akhlat taking Jalal al-Din's wife, the daughter of sultan Tughril, with them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 473}
