## Overview
[[person/louis-ix|Louis]], king of the French, set sail with his fleets at the end of Dhu al-Qa'da 668 AH and landed on the coast of Carthage near [[place/tunis|Tunis]], and the two forces met in the middle of Muharram 669 AH, as Ibn Khaldun relates. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The arrival
Ibn Khaldun, quoting his father, who quoted his own father, says that Louis gathered at Sardinia and then landed with about six thousand horsemen and thirty thousand foot soldiers, on three hundred ships, with seven kings with him. He says that he refused to let the sultan's envoy sit in his presence, and he took eighty thousand gold pieces that had been sent to turn him from his purpose. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The meeting and the truce
The two forces met in the middle of Muharram 669 AH, and about five hundred were killed on both sides. Then the king of the Franks was found dead, so the truce was concluded, and the judge Ibn Zaytun wrote it out in fifteen days. Carthage was ordered destroyed, and its building was carried to Tunis. The sultan [[person/al-mustansir-hafsi|al-Mustansir al-Hafsi]] was a party to these events. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Ibn Khaldun's words about the enemy's destruction express his own view; this page reports them and does not adjudicate them.
