## Overview
[[person/said-ibn-amr-al-jarashi|Sa'id ibn Amr al-Jarashi]] became governor of Khurasan, then marched to [[place/sughd|Sughd]] and conquered most of its forts. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The appointment
When Sa'id came to Khurasan, he ordered his secretary to read out his letter of appointment. The secretary made errors in his reading, and Sa'id told the people that the governor was innocent of that mistake. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sughd
Sa'id sent to Sughd calling its people to obedience and to a change of position. He held back from fighting them until his messengers came back saying that they persisted in opposing him. He then advanced on them, and about twelve thousand men, inclined to obedience, broke away from their great chief. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

al-Jarashi conquered most of the forts of Sughd and gained a full measure of wealth. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
