## Summary
Commentaries and histories report that [[person/yusuf|Yusuf]] asked his Lord to take him in submission, and that at his death he charged that his body be carried with them when the Israelites left Egypt; he was embalmed, placed in a coffin and left in Egypt until [[person/musa|Musa]] took him out with him. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 331} {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} Ibn Kathir relays the report of the old woman of the Israelites who showed Musa the grave, in a hadith he calls very strange and most likely a statement of a Companion rather than of the Prophet. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3388} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3389}

## His prayer for death
Ibn Kathir says that when Yusuf saw his favour complete and his family gathered, he knew this world is no place of permanence; he praised his Lord and asked to be taken, that is when his time came, in submission and to be joined with the righteous; the verses are at [[passage/quran-12-101|Qur'an 12:101]]. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 331} He thinks it possible that he asked this at his death, as the Prophet at his death asked for the highest companionship, or that he asked for death in submission while in good health. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 331} He quotes Ibn Abbas that no prophet before Yusuf ever wished for death, and explains that this was permitted in their law while in ours praying for death is forbidden except in times of trial. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 332}

## His charge and death
Ibn Kathir, relaying the People of the Book, says Yusuf charged at his death that he be carried with them when they left Egypt and buried with his fathers; they embalmed him and put him in a coffin, and he stayed in Egypt until Musa took him out and buried him with his fathers. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} The People of the Book say he died aged one hundred and ten; al-Hasan, in the report of Mubarak ibn Fadala, says he was thrown into the well at seventeen, was away from his father eighty years, lived twenty-three more and died at one hundred and twenty. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} Ibn Kathir adds that some say he charged his brother Judah. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} Al-Tabari, in his report from Ibn Ishaq, says he was buried, as he was told, in a marble chest at a side of the Nile in the depth of the water. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 419} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 450}

## Musa carries his remains
In the Stories of the Prophets Ibn Kathir says that when they left Egypt they took Yusuf's coffin with them. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 448} In his commentary he says Musa asked about Yusuf's grave and an old woman of the Israelites showed him, so he lifted the coffin and took it with them, and it is said that he carried it himself. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3388}

Al-Tabari has a detailed report about the Israelites losing their way: wandering struck them, Musa asked the elders what was wrong with them, and they said that when Yusuf died he took a pledge from his brothers not to leave Egypt until they took him out with them. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 488} Musa asked where his grave was and they did not know; he called out that whoever knew should tell him, until an old woman heard and said: if I show you his grave, will you give me what I ask? He asked his Lord and was permitted; she asked that she should enter no room in Paradise without entering it with him, and then that, being very old and unable to walk, he should carry her, and he did. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 488} Near the Nile she said it was in the depth of the water; he prayed that the water be drawn back and it was; she said dig; he did, and carried the bones, and the way opened for them. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 488} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 489}

Ibn Abi Hatim relays, and Ibn Kathir quotes, a hadith from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in which the Prophet says to a bedouin: could you not be like the old woman of the Israelites? He then tells that Musa set out with the Israelites and lost the road, their scholars recalled Yusuf's pledge, and only an old woman knew the place; she stipulated her price, that she be with him in Paradise, then led them to a pond, told them to drain it and dig, and they brought out the grave; when they lifted it the road was bright as day. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3388} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3389} Ibn Kathir comments: this is a very strange hadith, and it is closer to being a Companion's statement. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3389}

Ibn al-Athir says God ordered Musa to carry the coffin of Yusuf ibn Ya'qub and bury it in the Holy Land; only an old woman knew where, and she showed him the place in the Nile; he took it out, in a marble chest, and took it with him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir scan vol. 3 p. 151}

## How the reports differ
The reports agree that Yusuf took a pledge, that an old woman showed the grave and that it lay in the Nile. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3388} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 488} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir scan vol. 3 p. 151} They differ in detail: in al-Tabari's version she is carried by Musa and the water is drawn back at his prayer; in the hadith Musa sends for her and gives her what she asks; in Ibn al-Athir's the grave is in a marble chest. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 488} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3388} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir scan vol. 3 p. 151} His final burial place is not given in detail in these sources beyond the phrases 'with his fathers' and 'in the Holy Land'. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir scan vol. 3 p. 151} For a grave ascribed to Yusuf near the Ibrahimi Sanctuary, see [[building/al-haram-al-ibrahimi|the Ibrahimi Sanctuary]].

## A note on the nature of the reports
Some of these reports come from the People of the Book and one from a hadith Ibn Kathir calls strange, so their details are not asserted as certain. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir scan vol. 1 p. 3389} What they agree on is that Yusuf asked that his body be moved to the land of his fathers and that Musa carried it out. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 489}
