## Overview
[[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] was killed in Khurasan after [[person/waki-ibn-hassan|Waki ibn Hassan]] rebelled against him. Al-Baladhuri relates the account, and says that Qutayba was fifty-five years old when he was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The rising against him
Al-Baladhuri says that Waki feigned illness and stayed at home, and Qutayba kept sending to him and he kept excusing himself. Then he put on his armour and said that he would come to Qutayba only with his head. He set out for Qutayba's tent, where Qutayba was with his household and people who had remained loyal to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
The accounts differ on who killed him. In one, the tent's ropes were cut so that the tent's pole fell on his head and killed him, and [[person/abdallah-ibn-alwan|Abd Allah ibn Alwan]] cut off his head. Hisham ibn al-Kalbi says that they came into his tent and [[person/jahm-ibn-zahr|Jahm ibn Zahr al-Jumahi]] killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

A group of his brothers and household were killed with him, and his slave-mother al-Sammaa; [[person/dirar-ibn-muslim|Dirar ibn Muslim]] escaped. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After his death
Waki was brought the head of Qutayba, and sent it to Sulayman with [[person/salit-ibn-atta|Sulayt ibn Ata]]. Waki wrote to Lahiq ibn Humayd with his commission over Merv, and the people accepted it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
