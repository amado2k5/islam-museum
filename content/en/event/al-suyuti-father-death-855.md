## Overview
Al-Suyuti’s father died prematurely in 855/1451, when the boy was five years and seven months old and had already reached Surat al-Tahrim in the Qur’an; his father had entrusted his guardianship to a group of scholars, among them al-Kamal ibn al-Humam {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 468-470} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
