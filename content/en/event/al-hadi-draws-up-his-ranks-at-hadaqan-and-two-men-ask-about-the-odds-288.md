## Overview
When [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] was at [[place/hadaqan-sanaa-yemen|Hadaqan]] he drew up his army in a right wing, a left wing and a centre. His men were, by the narrator's count, about a hundred and fifty horse and about six hundred foot; the editor's footnote quotes Ghayat al-Amani, volume 1 page 188, for about seven hundred and fifty horse and foot in all. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205} Then there came to him [[person/yazid-ibn-ali-ibn-jamil-al-shakiri|Yazid ibn Ali ibn Jamil al-Shakiri]] and a man of Khath'am called Abu Rifa'a, and they said to him, in the words of the page, O son of, and the account breaks off at the foot of printed p. 205 and continues on p. 206. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.205}

## Detail
The Khath'ami's name is printed as Abu Rifa'a and is not entered as a person. The two counts differ because the narrator's figures are rounded and the footnote's is a later total; the museum gives both. It follows [[event/abu-al-atahiya-writes-to-al-hadi-and-sends-the-jafatim-and-al-tarif-to-an-ambush-at-al-sirr-288]].
