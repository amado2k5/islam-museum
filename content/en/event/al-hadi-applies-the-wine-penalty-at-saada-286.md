## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad says: my father Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah told me: I saw Yahya ibn al-Husayn when a man was brought to him and he was told this is a drunkard. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119} The manuscript margin carries the heading "his applying the penalties". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the rulings and numbers are Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah's report, and the museum describes what is said without comparison or verdict; the general legal subject has its own records at [[concept/khamr]] and [[concept/hudud-penalties]].

## The drunken man
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] told two of his trusted men to smell the man's breath, and they testified that he was a wine drinker; he held him until the next day when he had recovered from his drunkenness, then had him stretched between two posts and struck with the whip, eighty strokes. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119} He chose the whip himself, of middle thickness, neither thin nor thick, had its tip beaten with a stone, had the man struck stripped of his clothes, told the flogger to spread his blows over the shoulders and not go past them, and told a man to count for the flogger. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119}

## Four men and two women
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah also saw Yahya ibn al-Husayn when four men and two women were brought; some people testified that two of the four were drunk, and when they were examined by smell the odour of wine was found on them, so each was given eighty. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119} Witnesses testified against the other two, who were of his soldiers and companions, that they had found them talking with the two women; he ordered them struck, seventy each, less than the set penalty, as correction. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119}

## The two women
He sent the two women to women to see whether either was pregnant; one was found with child, and he ordered her kept alone in a house until she delivered, unless two trusted men could be found to stand surety for her until she delivered, which two men then did. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119} The other he ordered to sit in a basket of straw and be struck sixty strokes; she protested that she had not been found in any suspicion, and one of his servants was sent to tell her that if she had been found in anything of the kind, and the deed had been proved by testimony, he would have ordered a pit and stoned her, and then asked whether she was free or a slave; she said free, and was struck sixty as correction. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.119} The text gives neither day, year nor place.
