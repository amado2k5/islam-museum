## Overview
Asad orders the son of Ali ibn al-Fadl, his brother and about twenty captives beheaded in Dhu al-Qadah 304 AH.

## Detail
In Dhu al-Qadah 304 AH Asad orders the son of Ali ibn al-Fadl, his brother and the other captives the book calls unbelievers to be beheaded. Their heads are sent to Iraq. The book says they were more than twenty men.
