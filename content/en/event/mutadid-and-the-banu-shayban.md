## Overview
In 280 AH al-Tabari mentions that the caliph [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] marched on the Banu Shayban in the Jazira. When news of his march reached them, they gathered their property and families together. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.32}

Al-Mu'tadid returned to Baghdad, and the Banu Shayban met him asking for pardon and offering hostages. He took five hundred men from them, according to what was said. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.33}
