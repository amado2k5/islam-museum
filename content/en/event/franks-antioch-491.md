## Summary
In 491 AH the Franks set out with a great army, besieged Antioch for seven months, and took it by storm {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 396}.

## After Antioch
The Muslims came out against them and were defeated. The Franks pursued them to al-Ma'arra, where they killed and ravaged, and they stayed there and killed 100,000 Muslims. Forty days later they marched on Homs, whose people made peace with them, and then they turned towards Jerusalem {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 396}.

## Context
This battle was the first link in the great Frankish movement, which in the following year led to the capture of Jerusalem {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 396}.
