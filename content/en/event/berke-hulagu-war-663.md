## Overview
Ibn al-Imad mentions a battle between Berke and his cousin Hulagu, in which Hulagu was defeated, around 663 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.305}

## The report
Berke corresponded with Sultan al-Zahir Baybars, and then there was a battle between him and Hulagu. The author says that Hulagu was routed, many of his men were killed, and many drowned. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.305}

The date is approximate and derived, and it needs an additional source.
