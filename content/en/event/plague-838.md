## Summary
In 838 AH there was a general plague in the lands of the Muslims and elsewhere, in which an uncountable number died, according to Ibn al-Imad in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

The text does not specify a place or a number; this needs an additional source.
