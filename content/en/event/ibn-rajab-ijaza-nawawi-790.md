## Overview
The Hanbali traditionist Sa'd al-Din al-Nawawi (729-805) gave Ibn Rajab an ijaza, which must have been before al-Nawawi's death in 805; it is one of the ijazas named in his EI2 entry. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 933}
