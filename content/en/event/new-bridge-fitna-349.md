## Summary
On Thursday, the third of Sha'ban 349 AH, a strife between Sunnis and Shi'is broke out at the New Bridge in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.687}

## What happened
Friday prayers were disrupted the next day in the main mosques on both sides, except the mosque of Barathā, where the prayer was held. A group of Banu Hashim was seized and detained in the vizier's house, on the grounds that they had been the cause of the strife; they were released the following day. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.687}
