## Summary
Prince Arnat, lord of al-Karak and al-Shawbak, betrayed a caravan of Muslims passing from Egypt to Syria. He held those in it and seized what they carried, although a truce was in force. King Guy denounced him, but he refused to return what he had taken, and this was among the greatest causes of the war. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 142}
