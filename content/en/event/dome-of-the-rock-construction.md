## Overview
The Dome of the Rock built over the sacred rock in the Haram at Jerusalem, by order of 'Abd al-Malik b. Marwan: begun in 66 AH (685 CE) and finished in 72 AH (691 CE); the book relates its cost was drawn from Egypt's kharaj for seven years {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 17, 43}.
