## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the diwan of Syria remained in Greek until [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] became caliph, and that he ordered it transferred in 81 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.271}

## The account
al-Baladhuri says that one of the Byzantine secretaries could not find water to write with, so he urinated into the inkwell. When this reached Abd al-Malik, he ordered [[person/sulayman-ibn-sad-al-khushani|Sulayman ibn Sa'd]] to transfer the diwan. Sulayman asked for the kharaj of Jordan for a year of relief (ma'una). He was given it and became governor of Jordan, and he finished the transfer before the year was out. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.271}

Abd al-Malik put the matter to his secretary [[person/sarjun-ibn-mansur|Sarjun]], who went out downcast and told the Byzantine secretaries to seek their livelihood in some trade other than this one. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.271}

## The revenues
al-Baladhuri mentions that the assessed revenue of Jordan was a hundred and eighty thousand dinars, Palestine three hundred and fifty thousand, Damascus four hundred thousand, and Homs, Qinnasrin and the 'Awasim eight hundred thousand, though some say seven hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.271}

The people of the cut-off craft are the Byzantine secretaries.

The Gregorian date is derived. It needs an additional source.
