## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went on until he halted at a place in the districts of [[place/jayshan-yemen|Jayshan]], then crossed on from it and halted at a place called [[place/thath-yemen|Thath]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}. He gathered its people and told them what was due to God from them, and stayed at Thath some days until everyone in those districts had come to him; he put [[person/muhammad-ibn-abi-al-zubayr-al-yarsami|Muhammad ibn Abi al-Zubayr al-Yarsami]] over Thath, and [[person/abu-al-ushayra-ibn-al-ruwayya|Abu al-Ushayra ibn al-Ruwayya]] asked leave to stay at Thath to mend an estate of his there, and he gave it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}. Al-Hadi went on to a place in the land of Ans called Bashar and spent the night there, left a man of the Yarsamis in it the next morning, and went on until he halted at Yakla for the night, and the next day entered [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] at the end of Rabi I and stayed there {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}.

## Detail
He sent his brother Abu Muhammad to the Hijaz to his elders and his household, to bring them to Yemen; Abu Muhammad set out, and al-Hadi stayed at Sana'a until, at the end of Rabi II, he went out to Shibam, leaving his cousin [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] at Sana'a. At Shibam he stayed through Jumada I and some days of Jumada II, then sent his son Abu al-Qasim with an army to the land of Hamdan {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.215}. The editor's note says Thath is now known as Thah, near Rada', and quotes Ghayat al-Amani 1/179 for "the land of Hamdan"; the narrator's sentence about two "Mukarraman" who came to al-Hadi at Thath is unclear in sense, whether a name, a description or a place, and Apple Vision gives it as printed, so the museum leaves it out. The Abu Muhammad of this passage is al-Hadi's brother Abdullah ibn al-Husayn by the kunya as used on earlier pages. The account continues on p. 216, which is outside this batch.
