## Overview
The accounts of the campaigns of [[person/hassan-ibn-al-numan|Hassan ibn al-Nu'man]] against [[person/al-kahina|al-Kahina]], queen of the Berbers, differ. The battles took place in [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]] and Barqa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Baladhuri
Hassan was appointed and campaigned against al-Kahina, and she defeated him. He came to some palaces in the district of [[place/barqa|Barqa]] and stayed there, so they were called the Palaces of Hassan. Then he campaigned against her a second time and killed her, and he took captives of the Berbers and sent the captives to [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Aziz]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In Ibn Khaldun
Hassan advanced in 79 AH, entered Qayrawan and raided Carthage, which he took by force. Then the Muslims were routed in the valley of Maskiana, and Khalid ibn Yazid al-Qaysi was taken captive. Al-Kahina held the rule of Ifriqiya for five years until Hassan returned, and she was killed on the Aures mountain. The Berbers obtained terms that twelve thousand of them would fight with him as holy warriors. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1607-1608}

## In Salabi's book
He reports that Hassan entered Carthage by force and then returned to Qayrawan; that al-Kahina defeated him near Gabes; that the Byzantines retook Carthage in his absence; and that he reconquered it after al-Kahina was killed. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.58-60}

An additional source is needed: the accounts differ in dates and order, and the museum does not weigh one above the others.
