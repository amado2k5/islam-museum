## Overview
In Ramadan 784 AH al-Salih Hajji was deposed and [[person/barquq|Barquq]] took the sultanate with the title al-Zahir. He was the first of the Circassians to become sultan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 760-761}

## The caliphs in his time
In Rajab 785 AH Barquq seized the caliph al-Mutawakkil, deposed him and imprisoned him in the Citadel of the Mountain. Umar ibn Ibrahim was given the oath and took the title al-Wathiq bi-Llah. Then Barquq repented and restored al-Mutawakkil to the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 760-761}

## Afterwards
In Safar 792 AH Barquq was released from prison and returned to his kingdom, and he continued until he died in Shawwal 801 AH. His son Faraj was put in his place with the title al-Nasir. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 760-761}
