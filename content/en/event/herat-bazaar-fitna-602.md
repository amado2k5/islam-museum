## Overview
In Muharram 602 AH the common people rose up in Herat. A strife broke out between the people of the two markets, the blacksmiths and the coppersmiths, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.300}

## What happened
A group was killed, property was plundered, and houses were ruined. The governor of the city went out to restrain them, and one of the common people struck him with a stone. The mob gathered against him, and he was taken up to the Firuzi palace, where he hid for days until the strife calmed down, and then came out. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.300}
