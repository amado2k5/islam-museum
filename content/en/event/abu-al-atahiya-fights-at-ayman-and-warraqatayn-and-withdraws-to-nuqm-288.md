## Overview
On the evening of Friday night [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] with a company of horse and foot to a place called Ayman, where the army of [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] lay {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227}. When he drew near, the men came out against him together and fighting broke out; they sent a messenger to [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]], where their army was with [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibrahim ibn Khalaf]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.227} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. They all came out for Abu al-Atahiya and his men and the two sides met at a place called Warraqatayn and fought hard until a number of both armies were killed; when the men pressed upon Abu al-Atahiya, he and those with him drew off to [[place/nuqm-sanaa-yemen|Nuqm]], a mountain overlooking Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. He sent word to al-Hadi after Saturday morning came {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}.

## Detail
The museum reads the names Ayman and Warraqatayn as printed, does not fix their sites and makes no record for them. For Bayt Bus the editor cites the Mu'jam al-buldan: a village near Sana'a. The account leads on to [[event/al-hadi-fights-at-alab-and-al-jud-under-bayt-bus-288|al-Hadi's march to Alab]]. The narrator's invocation against Ibrahim ibn Khalaf is not repeated.
