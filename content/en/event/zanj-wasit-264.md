## Summary
In the year 264 AH the Zanj entered Wasit, and the people vacated the town and left it barefoot, each fleeing in whatever direction he went. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 46}

## The scene
Ibn al-Jawzi describes the departure. The Zanj entered houses and found them furnished. People went out, and each one would leave what he owned, bind his feet with his cloak, and walk on. Wasit was set on fire. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 46}
