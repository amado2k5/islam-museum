## Summary
Abu Mansur Abd al-Malik ibn Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Tha'alibi was born in 350 AH at Nishapur {cite:wafayat-ayan}, the city that in his generation became a cultural rival to Baghdad {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 450}; Ibn al-Imad notes he lived eighty years {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 246-247}.
