## Overview
[[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]] was killed in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] after [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] had besieged him for months. He was crucified on Tuesday, seventeen nights into Jumada al-Ula, or as some say al-Akhira, in 73 AH, according to al-Suyuti. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 349}

## The siege
[[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] equipped al-Hajjaj with forty thousand men. He besieged Ibn al-Zubayr and bombarded him with catapults, and his companions abandoned him and slipped away to al-Hajjaj. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 349}

al-Suyuti relates from Muhammad ibn Zayd ibn Abd Allah ibn Umar that a thunderbolt struck the crews of the catapults and burned about fifty men. He relates that Ibn al-Zubayr used to pray in the Hijr while the catapult stone struck the edge of his garment, and he did not turn. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 350}

## His last days
al-Mas'udi says that Urwa ibn al-Zubayr offered him the safe-conduct of Abd al-Malik, and he refused. His mother Asma' bint Abi Bakr said to him: "Die honourably, and beware of being taken captive." When they came in to him in the mosque, one of them called out to him: "O son of the two girdles." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 105}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says Ibn al-Zubayr was killed on Tuesday, thirteen nights before the end of Jumada al-Akhira, 73, aged seventy-three, having been born in the year of the Hijra; al-Layth's report gives Jumada al-Ula. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 269} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 270}
