## Overview
In 260 the Turk Muflih ordered added to al-Mansur's congregational mosque the hall called Dar al-Qattan — long a Christians' diwan; Muflih directed his associate al-Qattan to build it and placed it in the mosque for prayer, whence its name {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 110}.
