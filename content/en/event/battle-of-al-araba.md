## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-sufyan|Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan]] heard that a Byzantine force had gathered at al-Araba in the land of Palestine. He sent against them [[person/abu-umama-al-sudayy|Abu Umama al-Sudayy ibn Ajlan al-Bahili]]. He inflicted a defeat on them, killed their leader, and withdrew. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151-152}

## Abu Mikhnaf's account
al-Baladhuri, quoting Abu Mikhnaf, relates that six Byzantine commanders camped at al-Araba with three thousand men. Abu Umama defeated them and killed one of the commanders. He then pursued them to al-Dabiya, defeated them there, and the Muslims took fine booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151-152}

He adds that elders of the people of Syria counted it the first battle of the Muslims. It belongs to the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]], and its approximate date of 13 AH is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151-152}
