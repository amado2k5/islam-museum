## Overview
The bursting of the [[building/marib-dam|Marib dam]] around 450–451 CE, which the Qur'an mentions as the flood of al-Arim — an event that, in the sira's telling, ruined the settlement of Yemen and scattered the subtribes of Qahtan.

## The story
After Yemen won its independence from the first Abyssinian occupation, breaches began appearing in the Marib dam, until the great flood the Qur'an calls the flood of al-Arim occurred in 450 or 451 CE — a major catastrophe that ruined the civilisation and scattered the peoples {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

The Qur'an mentions it in Sura Saba ([[passage/quran-34-16|Saba 16]] and what follows) {cite:tafhim-al-quran}. Some writers say of fixing those years that the disagreement over it is great among the historical sources {cite:tarikh-ard-al-quran}.

## For young visitors
Yemen had a great dam that held back water; when it broke, a huge flood ran and ruined the land, and many tribes emigrated from Yemen to the rest of the peninsula.
