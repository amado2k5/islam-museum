## Summary
Ibn Hajar, in the biography of Kurz ibn Alqama al-Khuza'i, transmits from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr that Kurz followed the tracks of the Prophet and Abu Bakr to the cave of Thawr, saw a spider's web across its door, and said: the trail ends here; I do not know whether they took the right hand, the left, or climbed the mountain {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.304}.

## Context
This is a report transmitted through later narrators; the early account of the three nights in the cave is in event/ghar-thawr.
