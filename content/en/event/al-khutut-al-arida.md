## Summary
Al-Khutut al-Arida by Muhibb al-Din al-Khatib: the most famous concise Sunni refutation of the core differences — taqiyya, the Companions and Qur'anic preservation. {cite:al-islam-murajaat}

## Standing
Written in reply to contemporary ecumenical literature, it became the translated popular reference of the Sunni school in setting out the points of difference.
