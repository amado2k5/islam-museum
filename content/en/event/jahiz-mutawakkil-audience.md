## Overview
Al-Jahiz was mentioned to al-Mutawakkil as a tutor for one of his sons; when the caliph saw him he was repelled by his looks, granted him ten thousand dirhams and dismissed him {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 486-487} {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 404}.

## Al-Jahiz's own account
Al-Jahiz himself records an interview he claims to have had with al-Mutawakkil, who was anxious to entrust him with the education of his children but finally dismissed him because of his ugliness. The story falls within al-Mutawakkil's caliphate (232-247/847-861), when al-Jahiz was dividing his residence between Baghdad and Samarra {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 385}.
