## Summary
Shihab al-Din petitioned for leave to travel to al-Sham; the sultan instead had the dawadar summon him, issued a rescript against him, confiscated his property and imprisoned him in Sha'ban 739 (February-March 1339) {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/332}.

## Details
His father had died soon after and his brother Ala al-Din was confirmed in the chancery; when Shihab al-Din petitioned to leave for Syria the sultan's buried rancour woke: the dawadar was sent for him, a rescript issued, and his estate seized {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/332}.
