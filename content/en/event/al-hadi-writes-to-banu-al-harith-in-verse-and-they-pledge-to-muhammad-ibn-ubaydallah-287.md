## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] had earlier stood surety for the people of [[place/najran|Najran]] of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] over the youths of their desert. When the letter of [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] reached him he wrote back telling him to be firm with Banu al-Harith, and wrote to Banu al-Harith a stern letter, adding at its foot verses. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.201}

## Detail
By sense, the verses say: I am the son of Muhammad and my father is Ali, my uncle and my maternal uncle are the best who wear sandals, and I follow them as pattern follows pattern; I am the death that cannot be avoided for whoever tries to deceive me, a rain to my friend, and I go through every terror to my enemy; I am patient at the shock of the day of battle; you were long in corruption and thought it hidden from us, but if you keep the terms of my covenant you are safe from the buckets of my war and you have tasted its evil already; otherwise stand firm, for I will fight you by the power of the Lord of Majesty; God has given me victory, aid and money, and my host is strong and numerous, and God's party is victorious and the party of aggression sure to pass; what I say is true and my word may be borne out by deeds. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.201} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202} The claims of descent and the warnings are the imam's own words as the narrator gives them, and the museum adds no verdict. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202}

When the letter reached Banu al-Harith they were afraid of al-Hadi and took care, came to Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, swore hearing and obedience to him and guaranteed the youths of their deserts, and he wrote to al-Hadi to tell him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.202} It follows [[event/banu-al-harith-raid-hamdan-and-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-rides-out-of-najran-287]] and leads into [[event/al-hadi-leaves-saada-for-khiwan-and-meets-al-daam-at-al-amshiyya-288]].
