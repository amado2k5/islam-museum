## Overview
In 440 masses of Ghuzz came from beyond the Oxus to Ibrahim Inal, Tughril Beg's brother; he told them the land was too narrow for them and the right course was to raid the Byzantines. They marched, he after them, until fifteen days from Constantinople — taking over a hundred thousand captives, four thousand coats of mail, and booty hauled on ten thousand wagons — then returned {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.479}.
