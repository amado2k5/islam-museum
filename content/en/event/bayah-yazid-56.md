## Overview
In 56 AH [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] called for the oath of allegiance to his son [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid]] as heir apparent, on the advice of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.146}

## Ziyad's objection
The book says that Ziyad ibn Abihi disliked this, because of what he knew of Yazid's frivolity and his devotion to hunting, and he sent to Mu'awiya to dissuade him. The matter was settled after Ziyad's death. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.146}

## Those who refused
On his way from Mecca to Syria, Mu'awiya summoned those who refused the oath: Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr, Abd Allah ibn Umar, Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr, al-Husayn ibn Ali and Ibn Abbas. Abd al-Rahman was the most vehement in refusing. Mu'awiya gave a sermon in Medina while they were under the pulpit, and the people gave their allegiance to Yazid without showing any dissent. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p.147}

The Gregorian date has not been converted. There is another account of the event, [[event/baya-wilayat-ahd-yazid|the oath to Yazid as heir apparent]], which differs in the year.
