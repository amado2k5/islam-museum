## Overview

The first race between the Prophet and Aisha: on one of his journeys, with Aisha still a young girl who had not yet put on flesh — lean and swift — he said to the people: go ahead; they went ahead, then he said to her: «come, let me race you» — and she raced him and beat him {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## In the hadith

Reported by Abu Dawud, Ahmad and others from Aisha: I went out with the Prophet on one of his journeys while I was a girl who had not put on flesh, and he said to the people: go ahead; they went ahead, then he said to me: «come, let me race you», so I raced him and beat him on foot. She said: and I forgot it {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:musnad-ahmad}. The first race: she was young and lean and she beat him. No field, no prize — it happened on the road of their journey, sport in its natural place between husband and wife.

## Its meaning

A famous report under the heading of good companionship and play: the scholars cite it as proof of the permissibility of spouses racing and refreshing one another, and of the Prophet's tenderness toward his family on his travels. It is the first of two famous races; the second came years later when she had put on flesh and he beat her, laughing: «this one is for that one» {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.
