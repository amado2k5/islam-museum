## Summary
When al-Musta'in deposed himself at the opening of 252/866 he was sent down to Wasit and held for nine months {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 555-556}. Asked whom he wanted in his company he chose Ahmad ibn Tulun, who served him well; al-Mutawakkil's ghilman wrote offering him Wasit itself if he would kill the ex-caliph, and he refused — 'by God, I will never let it be said of me that I killed a caliph I had sworn allegiance to' — and when al-Musta'in was murdered he washed, shrouded and buried the body {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p. 76} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 300}.
