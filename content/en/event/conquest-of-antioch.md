## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] marched from Aleppo to [[place/antakiya|Antioch]], defeated a host that met him at Bahuraha, and besieged its people from all its gates, until they made peace with him for the jizya and for leaving. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege and the treaty
At Bahuraha, about two parasangs from the city, a host of the enemy met him, and he dispersed it and drove it back into the city. Most of the army was at the Persian Gate and the Sea Gate. Some of its people left and some stayed, and a dinar and a jarib (of land) were imposed on each adult male as [[concept/jizya|jizya]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Breach of the covenant
Its people broke the covenant, and Abu Ubayda sent [[person/iyyad-ibn-ghanm|Iyad ibn Ghanm]] and [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]], who conquered it on the first treaty. It is also said that they broke it after his return to Palestine, and that Amr ibn al-As came from Iliya and conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the conquest
Umar wrote to Abu Ubayda to have a group of men of good intention stationed there on garrison duty. [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] moved to it in 42 AH a group of Persians and the people of Ba'labakk and Homs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
