## Overview
According to the account of Abd al-Rahman ibn Ka'b ibn Malik, the first Friday prayer in Islam was led by [[person/asad-ibn-zurara|As'ad ibn Zurara]] in the Harra of the Banu Bayada, at the Naqi' al-Khadamat of [[place/medina|Medina]]; they were forty men. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.236-237}

## The report
His father Ka'b, whenever he heard the call to the Friday prayer, would ask forgiveness for As'ad, because he was the first to gather them for it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.236-237}

## The report of al-Zuhri
It is related from al-Zuhri that [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Musab ibn Umayr]] led the Friday prayer with twelve men. Al-Samhudi reconciles the two reports: As'ad held the Friday prayer with the help of the twelve, and Musab was the imam. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.236-237}

This was before the Prophet's Hijra to Medina. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.236-237}
