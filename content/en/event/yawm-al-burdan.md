## Overview
A day of the Arabs before Islam, related by Ibn al-Athir. On it [[person/ziyad-ibn-al-habula|Ziyad ibn al-Habula]], king of Syria of the Sulayh, raided the people of Hujr ibn Amr, known as [[person/akil-al-murar|Akil al-Murar]], king of Kinda and Rabi'a. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 377-378}

## The raid
The men of Hujr were on a raid to Bahrain, and their families were left behind. Ziyad took the women and the property, and took captive Hind bint Zalim ibn Wahb. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 377-378}

## The pursuit
Hujr, Kinda and Rabi'a returned in pursuit of him, and overtook him at al-Burdan, short of the Ayn Abagh. Hujr camped at the foot of a mountain, and the [[group/bakr-ibn-wail|Bakr]], the [[group/banu-taghlib|Taghlib]] and Kinda camped with him at a water called Hufayr. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 377-378}

Ibn al-Athir relates the story of the spying of Sadus ibn Shayban ibn Dhuhl and Sali' ibn Abd Ghanm on Ziyad's camp, when they came with firewood and sat listening to his speech. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 377-378}

What is not covered here: the outcome of the encounter. The date is approximate and belongs to the days of the Arabs before Islam.
