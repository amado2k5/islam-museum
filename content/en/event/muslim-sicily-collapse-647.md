## Overview
News of the death of the sultan [[person/abu-zakariya-al-hafsi|Abu Zakariya]] reached Sicily in 647 AH. An agreement had been made with the lord of the island that the Muslims of Palermo would share the city and its suburb. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## What happened
The Christians moved quickly against them, and the Muslims took refuge in the fortresses and rough ground. The lord of Sicily besieged them until they came down, then ferried them across the sea to the opposite coast and settled them in the town of Wajjara. After that he moved to Malta and drove the Muslims out of it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The date is derived from the Hijri calendar and tied to the death of Abu Zakariya; it needs a second source.
