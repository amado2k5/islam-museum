## Summary
The Prophet circled the Ka'ba seven times on his mount, touching the Corner with his staff, and pointed with a stick in his hand at the three hundred and sixty idols bound with lead around the House; they fell one after another as he recited [[passage/quran-17-81|"say: truth has come and falsehood has passed away — falsehood is ever bound to pass"]], then he entered the Ka'ba and effaced the images and statues inside it {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/54, 59}.

## The cleansing of the House
The Prophet took the key from Uthman ibn Talha, opened the House and entered with Bilal, Usama ibn Zayd and Uthman ibn Talha {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/54}. He saw inside images, among them a picture of Abraham casting lots with arrows, and ordered them all effaced while reciting: "Abraham was neither a Jew nor a Christian, but a hanif, a muslim" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/55}. He found in it a dove made of sticks and broke it with his noble hand and cast it down {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/54}.

Then he prayed two prostrations inside the House between the two pillars, and ordered [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal ibn Rabah]] to climb onto the roof of the Ka'ba and give the call to the noon prayer — the first call raised above the Sacred House {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/55-56}.

## The conversion of Fadala ibn Umayr
During the circumambulation [[person/fadala-ibn-umayr|Fadala ibn Umayr al-Laythi]] resolved to kill the Prophet and drew near him; the Prophet said: "Fadala?" He said: yes. He said: "what were you saying to yourself?" He said: nothing — I was remembering God. The Prophet smiled and laid his hand on his chest and his heart grew calm; Fadala said: by God, he did not lift his hand from my chest before nothing of God's creation was dearer to me than he {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/59-60}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet entered the Ka'ba and removed its idols and images so the House returned to being a place of worship of God alone, without statues. Bilal climbed onto the Ka'ba's roof and raised the call to prayer in his beautiful voice for all Mecca to hear.
