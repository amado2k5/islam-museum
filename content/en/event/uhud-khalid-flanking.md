## Overview
When the backs of the Muslims were left without archers, [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] wheeled quickly until he reached the rear of the Muslim army at [[place/uhud|Uhud]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264}

## The outflanking
Khalid destroyed [[person/abd-allah-ibn-jubayr|Abd Allah ibn Jubayr]] and his nine companions on the mountain, then swooped down on the Muslims from behind. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264}

## The return of the idolaters
The idolaters heard the shout of Khalid's horsemen and turned upon the Muslims. A woman called Amra bint Alqama al-Harithiyya raised the fallen banner of the idolaters, and the idolaters gathered around it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264}

The Muslims were between Khalid's horsemen from behind and the Quraysh army turned back from the front. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264}

The event is part of [[event/battle-of-uhud|the Battle of Uhud]]. It took place directly after the archers left their position, on the seventh of Shawwal 3 AH according to al-Mubarakfuri. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.264}
