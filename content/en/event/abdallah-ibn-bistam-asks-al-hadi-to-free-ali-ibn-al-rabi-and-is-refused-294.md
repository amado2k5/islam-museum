## Overview
After the prisoners reached Najran, Ibn Bistam hoped to free Ali ibn al-Rabi' and asked al-Hadi to give him up. Al-Hadi refused, calling him a troublemaker. The narrator says Ibn Bistam did not ask out of affection for him.

## Detail
The book says that when the prisoners reached Najran, Ibn Bistam hoped to free Ali ibn al-Rabi'. He went to al-Hadi and asked him to give Ali to him and release him from prison. Al-Hadi disliked this and told him Ali was a troublemaker and one of those called war on religion and the ruin of the Muslims. The narrator says Ibn Bistam's request was not from love for Ali nor from pity, but from a wish to gain Ali as a client to some extent.
