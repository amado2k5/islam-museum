## Overview
The War of al-Basus was between Bakr and Taghlib, the two sons of Wa'il. Ibn al-Athir describes it as the longest and most famous of the days of the Arabs. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.390-397}

## The cause
[[person/kulayb-ibn-rabia|Kulayb ibn Rabi'a al-Taghlibi]] had protected a land that none approached except a warrior. The she-camel of Sa'd ibn Shamis al-Jurmi, a client of [[person/jasas-ibn-murra|Jasas ibn Murra]], entered it. Kulayb shot her udder with an arrow, and [[person/al-basus-bint-munqidh|al-Basus bint Munqidh]], the aunt of Jasas, cried out: "Alas for our humiliation!" {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.390-397}

## The war
Jasas watched for Kulayb's moment of carelessness until he stabbed him and he died. His brother [[person/al-muhalhil|al-Muhalhil ibn Rabi'a]] rose seeking vengeance. The war between [[group/bakr-ibn-wail|Bakr]] and [[group/banu-taghlib|Taghlib]] went on for forty years, according to the source. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.390-397}
