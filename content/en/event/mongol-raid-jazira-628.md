## Overview
In 628 AH, after the defeat of Jalal al-Din ibn Khwarazm Shah before the Mongols at Amid, the Mongols plundered the districts of Diyar Bakr and the Jazira. Ibn al-Athir relates this in "al-Kamil". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 492-494}

## The towns named
They plundered the countryside of Amid, Arzan and Mayafariqin. They made for As'ard, whose people fought them until they granted them safe conduct; then its people surrendered, and the Mongols put them to the sword. Ibn al-Athir relates from some merchants that they estimated the dead at more than fifteen thousand. The Mongols went to Tanzah and did the same. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 492-494}

Then they made for the Wadi al-Qurayshiyya, but the Kurds blocked them and held out against them, and they returned. They plundered the countryside of Mardin, and its people took refuge in the citadel; then Nisibin, Sinjar and al-Khabur. In the village of al-Mu'nisa its people took refuge in a khan, and the Mongols killed everyone in it. They burned Dulays after its people had fortified themselves in the citadel, took Bakri by force, and did the same to Arjish in Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 492-494}

## Mosul and Irbil
A group of them came from Azerbaijan to the lands of Irbil. Its lord, Muzaffar al-Din, came out with his armies, asking for reinforcements from the troops of Mosul. When he heard they had returned to Azerbaijan, he stayed and did not pursue them. [[person/badr-al-din-lulu|Badr al-Din Lu'lu]], lord of Mosul, is mentioned as the one whose troops were requested. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 492-494}

## Accounts of survivors
Ibn al-Athir transmits two accounts from survivors: a man hid in a house full of straw, and saw them killing people; another was with a group and they escaped after one of them had killed a Mongol horseman. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 492-494}

## Date
628 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around 1231 CE) that is derived. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 493}
