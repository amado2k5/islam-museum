## Overview
Sultan [[person/alp-arslan|Alp Arslan]] sent his commander [[person/atsiz-ibn-uwaq|Atsiz ibn Uwaq]] on a campaign to southern Syria, according to al-Sallabi. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 30}

Atsiz took [[place/ramla|Ramla]] and [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] from the Fatimids, but he could not take Ascalon, the gate of Egypt. Before that, Alp Arslan had besieged the Mirdasid state in Aleppo, and forced its amir Mahmud ibn Salih to proclaim the caliphate of the Abbasid caliph in 462 AH (1070 CE). {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 30}

The source does not date the campaign itself; its date in the data is approximate and needs an additional source.
