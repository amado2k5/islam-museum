## Overview
Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik died in 125 AH, on the sixth night of Rabi' al-Akhir, according to al-Tabari. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.200}

## What the sources say
al-Tabari mentions the illness of which he died, and gives different accounts of his age. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.200}

al-Suyuti says that he died in Rabi' al-Akhir, and that he would not bring money into his treasury until forty oaths had been sworn that it had been taken from its owner and that each person with a right had been given his right. He mentions that he built al-Rusafa at Qinnasrin. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 401-402}

The details of the illness and the age need an additional source on this page.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says Hisham died in Rabi' al-Akhir 125, aged fifty-three, or sixty-one according to Hatim. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 356} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 357}
