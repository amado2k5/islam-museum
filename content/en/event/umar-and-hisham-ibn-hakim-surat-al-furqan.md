## Summary
This is the best-known story of its kind and the root of the hadith on which the discussions of the [[concept/al-ahruf-al-sabaa|seven ahruf]] rest. [[passage/muwatta-1-540|Malik gives it in the Muwatta]] from Ibn Shihab from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd al-Qari; it is in [[passage/bukhari-2419|al-Bukhari]] by al-Tinnisi's route from Malik, and in Muslim and Abu Dawud. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.277} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.278}

## What happened
[[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] says: I heard [[person/hisham-ibn-hakim-ibn-hizam|Hisham ibn Hakim ibn Hizam]] reciting Surat [[passage/quran-25-1|al-Furqan]] in a way other than the Messenger of God had taught it to me, and the Messenger of God had taught it to me; I nearly rushed on him, then I held back until he finished, then I seized him by his cloak and brought him to the Messenger of God and told him. The Prophet asked Hisham to recite and he recited as I had heard him; he said: so it was revealed. Then he asked Umar to recite, and he recited, and he said: so it was revealed. Then he said: this Qur'an was revealed in seven ahruf, so read of it what is easy. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.277}

## How scholars read the incident
Ibn al-Jazari points out that Umar and Hisham were both Qurashi, of one idiom and one tribe, and yet differed in reciting the sura, as the sound reports establish; so the seven ahruf cannot be seven scattered dialects as some said. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 34} Khalid al-Sabt says what was recited in the report was one text, and the Prophet endorsed the recitation of each of the two disputants, both Qurashi, so explaining the ahruf as tribal dialects does not stand. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 193} Ibn al-Jazari adds that the Companions who brought such disputes to the Prophet, Umar and Hisham among them, did not differ over the meaning of the Qur'an or its rulings, only over how its letters were read. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 34}

## Questions left open
The report I read does not name which verse differed or the wording at issue, nor the time or place of the incident, so I state none of that; nor do the reports I consulted say why the Prophet taught each in a different form, other than that he said 'so it was revealed'. These are matters taken up in [[question/what-is-meant-by-the-seven-ahruf|What is meant by the seven ahruf?]]

## Consequence
The hadith books report similar incidents with Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Ibn Mas'ud and Amr ibn al-As; see [[concept/qiraat-al-sahaba-fi-al-hadith|the Companions' readings in hadith]]. In some of these reports the Prophet says that those before you differed and were destroyed. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 158} The same concern shows later with Hudhayfa when he saw the disagreement between the people of Syria and Iraq: see [[event/uthman-standardizes-mushaf|the standardization of the codex]].
