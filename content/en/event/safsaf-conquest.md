## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that in 181 AH the fortress of al-Safsaf was taken by force, and that [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]] was its conqueror. In 176 AH the city of Dabsa was taken by the amir Abd al-Rahman ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Salih al-Abbasi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.461}

The report is brief and from a single source, and the location of the fortress needs an additional source.
