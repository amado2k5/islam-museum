## Overview
In Shawwal 469 AH a strife broke out in Baghdad between the Hanbalis and the Ash'aris, related by Ibn al-Jawzi. [[person/abu-nasr-ibn-al-qushayri|Abu Nasr ibn al-Qushayri]] had sat in the Nizamiyya and denounced the Hanbalis, and the two sides quarrelled, and a tailor was killed. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

## The cause as Ibn al-Jawzi tells it
Ibn al-Jawzi says that Abu Nasr ibn al-Qushayri came to Baghdad and sat in the Nizamiyya denouncing the Hanbalis and ascribing anthropomorphism to them. [[person/abu-ishaq-al-shirazi|Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi]] inclined to support him, and wrote to Nizam al-Mulk complaining of the Hanbalis and asking for his help. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

## The strife
He says the common people agreed to attack [[person/sharif-abu-jafar|the Sharif Abu Ja'far]] in his mosque. A group was arranged to turn back the dispute, and the others threw bricks at them. A tailor from the Tuesday market was killed, and his mother cried at the Nubi gate: "al-Mustansir billah, O Mansur!" Abu Ishaq was angry and went to the Bab al-Taq to prepare for travel, and the caliph sent to turn him back. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

## The attempt at reconciliation
The vizier [[person/fakhr-al-dawla-ibn-jahir|Fakhr al-Dawla ibn Jahir]] called on the Sharif Abu Ja'far to reconcile, and he attended with Abu Ishaq, Abu Sa'd the Sufi, and Abu Nasr ibn al-Qushayri. Ibn al-Jawzi relates a discussion among them, in which the Sharif said that his two grandfathers, the caliphs al-Qa'im and al-Qadir, had made their creed public, and that he held to their creed. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

He says that the Sharif fell ill afterwards, and his legs swelled, and it was said that one of the jurists had put poison in his shoe; then he says "God knows best". {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.305-307}

## Note
This is Ibn al-Jawzi's account, and he was a Hanbali. The Ash'ari account and the academic view need an additional source.
