## Overview
al-Suyuti mentions that in the days of al-Ma'mun the descendants of al-Abbas were counted. They numbered thirty-three thousand, men and women, and this was in the year 200 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 518}

The event is tied to the caliph [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]]. The date needs an additional source; the conversion to CE is derived.
