## Overview
In 396 Ahmad b. Muhammad b. 'Umar al-'Alawi led the hajj and preached at Mecca and Medina for al-Hakim, lord of Egypt, as was the custom — and ordered the people of the two sanctuaries to stand at his mention; the like was done in Egypt: at his name they stood and prostrated in the markets and gathering-places {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.231}.
