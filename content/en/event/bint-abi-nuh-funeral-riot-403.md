## Overview
In Shawwal 403 the funeral of the vizier al-Munasih's letter-writer Abu'l-Hayja's wife went out by day with wailing-women, drums, pipes, monks, crosses and candles; a Hashimi stoned the bier, one of the vizier's ghilman struck him with a mace. The funeral fled to the church of the Romans' house; the Muslims followed, looted the church and neighboring houses, and a great fitna flared — mushafs raised in the markets, mosque doors shut, the Caliph's palace approached, Friday prayer abstained — until the caliph's courtiers feigned readiness to quit the city in anger, and the vizier's letter-writer was produced {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}.
