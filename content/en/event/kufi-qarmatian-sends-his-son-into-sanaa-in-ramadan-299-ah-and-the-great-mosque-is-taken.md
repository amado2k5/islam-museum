## Overview
Ibn al-Fadl makes peace with the Kufi and sends the Kufi's son with him. The party enters Sanaa on Monday, nine nights into Ramadan 299 AH, and takes the Great Mosque. The author reports acts there that he condemns, given here only in general terms.

## Detail
Ibn al-Fadl pulled back from his companion, the Kufi, who could not overpower him in his fort. They wrote to each other, came to terms, and the Kufi sent his son with him. The party entered Sanaa on Monday, nine nights into Ramadan 299 AH, while al-Aqra'i and a few men of Sanaa were in the city. They offered no resistance and took the Great Mosque. The printed edition's note identifies the Kufi as al-Mansur Abu al-Qasim al-Hasan ibn Faraj ibn Hawshab ibn Zadan al-Kufi, who came to Yemen with Ali ibn al-Fadl, and cites al-Qadi al-Nu'man's Iftitah al-Da'wa. This packet has not checked that citation. The sentence about the fort is terse, so the entry gives only what the page says plainly.
