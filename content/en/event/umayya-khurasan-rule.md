## Overview
After the strife, Khurasan was divided between two parties, and its notables wrote to Abd al-Malik that the matter would not be right except with a man of Quraysh. He appointed [[person/umayya-ibn-abdallah-ibn-khalid|Umayya ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid]] over Khurasan, and the dispute ended with the killing of [[person/bukayr-ibn-wishah|Bukayr ibn Wishah]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governorship
One group was with Bukayr and another with Bujayr. Umayya appointed Bukayr over Tukharistan, then gave him the raiding of Transoxiana. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The dispute
Umayya resolved to raid Bukhara and then go to Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim at Tirmidh. Bukayr returned to Merv, and seized Ibn Umayya and imprisoned him, and called the people to depose Umayya, and they responded. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Umayya made peace with the people of Bukhara for a small ransom, and built ships after Bukayr had burned them, then returned and fought Bukayr, then made peace with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing of Bukayr
Umayya learned that Bukayr was still striving to depose him, and ordered him seized if he entered his house. He was seized and ordered imprisoned, and [[person/bujayr-ibn-waraqa|Bujayr ibn Waraqa]] sprang upon him and killed him. Then Umayya raided al-Khutal, whose people had broken the peace, and took it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
