## Overview
After his studies in Cairo, al-Subki travelled to Damascus and performed the Pilgrimage, then returned to Egypt to teach at the Mansuriyya madrasa beside the mosque of Ibn Tulun {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 pp. 744-745}; Ibn Kathir records that he journeyed to Syria, studied on his own, wrote and taught {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 756 p. 264}.
