## Summary
The caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah Ahmad ibn al-Mustadi died. He left the caliphate after forty-seven years, the longest Abbasid caliphate, and in that time he gathered more authority, power and sway over kings than any Abbasid before him {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.451}.
