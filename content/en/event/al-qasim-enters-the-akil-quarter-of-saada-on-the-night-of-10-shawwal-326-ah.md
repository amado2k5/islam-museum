## Overview
The Akil leave their quarter in the night, and their leaving is not known until the last part of the night. Al-Qasim enters the village and finds people's goods there in great quantity; their houses are wrecked, and the Akil go to Alaf.

## Detail
The book says al-Qasim marched with a large army against the Akil and Sanaani men on Monday, eight nights into Shawwal, and they fought, and a number of Akil were killed. The fighting became fierce on Tuesday. The Akil and those with them then left by night, and their going was not known until the last part of the night of Wednesday, 10 Shawwal, when the village was entered. The people's goods found there are described as beyond counting; their houses were wrecked, and they went to Alaf, where, the book says, they cursed one another.
