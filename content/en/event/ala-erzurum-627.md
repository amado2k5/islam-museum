## Summary
In 627 AH Ala al-Din Kayqubad, lord of the land of Rum, took the city of Erzurum with the fortresses and treasuries attached to it; its lord had been taken captive in the Akhlat campaign. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 487}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the lord of Erzurum had stayed with Jalal al-Din at Akhlat and witnessed the battle with him; when Jalal al-Din was routed the lord of Erzurum was taken captive and brought before his cousin Ala al-Din Kayqubad, who took him and marched on Erzurum, whose lord surrendered it together with the fortresses, treasuries and everything belonging to it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 487} Ibn al-Athir adds that it was as the proverb says: this unfortunate had come to Jalal al-Din seeking an increase and had been promised some of Ala al-Din's lands — instead he lost his own country, what was in his hands, and remained a prisoner. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 487}
