## The final ruin of Harran
In Ramadan 670 AH the Tatars moved those remaining in Harran to the east, and they destroyed the city and wiped it out completely {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 577}.

Harran had been emptied three years earlier, in 667 AH, when its preacher Ibn Taymiyya and others arrived in Damascus from it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 564}. This ended that ancient city, whose name the Hanbali school of Harran had carried. The family of Ibn Taymiyya came from there, and their learning moved to Syria.
