## Summary
In this year Qutb al-Din Ilghazi ibn Najm al-Din ibn Alpi ibn Timurtash ibn Ilghazi ibn Artuq, lord of Mardin, died, and his son Husam al-Din Yuluq Arslan, a child, succeeded him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 129}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the boy inherited while still a child; his father's mamluk Nizam al-Din al-Baqish took charge of his upbringing and the administration of his kingdom, and Shah-Arman, lord of Akhlat, who was Qutb al-Din's maternal uncle, had influence in the state and had appointed al-Baqish over the son. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 129} Al-Baqish was religious, upright, just and well-conducted: he raised the boy well and married his mother, but when the boy grew up he could not govern his kingdom for the disorder in him, and a mamluk of Nizam al-Din's named Lu'lu' had taken control of the state and pushed the regent into what he did with the boy; so matters remained until the son died. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 129}
