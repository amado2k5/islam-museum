## Overview
Ibn Rajab's father travelled with him in his youth to the Hijaz and [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] for the sake of hearing hadith, and he also heard hadith in Egypt; the editor lists Egypt, Nablus, the Hijaz, Jerusalem, [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and [[place/medina|Medina]] among his journeys for knowledge. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 933} {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 9}

## Purpose of the journey
His travels began in early childhood, when his father carried him from Baghdad, the town of his birth, to Damascus; from there his quest for knowledge and direct audition from the shaykhs unfolded, until he came to count a great many shaykhs. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 9}
