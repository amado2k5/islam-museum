## Overview
After reaching [[place/saada|Sa'da]] "on Wednesday, thirteen days past of Jumada II", with the notables of Hamdan, Muhammad ibn al-Da'am and his horse, [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stayed there some days, then gathered Khawlan and marched with it, with the Hamdan troops who were with him, to [[place/najran|Najran]]: "he set out on Saturday, seventeen days past of the month, and reached Najran on Tuesday." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the arrival at Sa'da is in [[event/ibn-bustam-seeks-safe-conduct-through-al-daam-and-al-hadi-reaches-saada-286]]. The Saturday agrees with the tabular calendar for 17 Jumada II 286 AH, which falls on 30 June 899 Julian.

## The village of Lubayban
Al-Hadi did not dismount in the village but "made for the village of the troublemakers of the Yam, a village called Lubayban". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} The word the book uses, al-muhdithun, means those who had made disorder, and the same people are those put out of the country at the end of his stay; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} they are here of [[group/yam-tribe|Yam]]. The editor notes that al-Hamdani's Sifat Jazirat al-Arab, p. 169, writes the name "Lubaynan". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} See [[place/lubayban-najran|Lubayban]].

## Surety and binding
He sent for the troublemakers; they feared him and climbed into the hills; he meant to cut their property and pull down their houses, but their clans came and asked for agreement and stood surety that they would bring them in. They went, brought them down from the hills and delivered them to him, and he had them made fast on the spot; he then camped at "the village of al-Hajar". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130}

## Abdullah ibn al-Husayn at Najran
Al-Hadi sent his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]], who had come from the Hijaz before al-Hadi left Khiwan for Najran; on arriving and seeing the disorder he took a number of the offenders, jailed them and set the town right until al-Hadi came, and al-Hadi sent him after the troublemakers still at large, and he brought some, whom he jailed and put in irons. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130} Al-Hadi himself took a group of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] and of Yam, and some townsmen of Najran who had been causing disorder, and put them in irons and prison. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130} The editor records that the other manuscript reads "at that moment, the Hijaz" where the text has "had come from the Hijaz". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.130}

## Notes
The book names none of them at this point; those expelled at the end of the stay are named in [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]]. "Banu al-Harith" is taken here as the Najran tribe of that name; the book gives only the name.
