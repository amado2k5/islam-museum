## Overview

[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]], under the year 122, says: in it Zayd ibn Ali ibn Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib was killed at Kufa. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 353}
