## Summary
In the last year of his life al-Nasa'i left Egypt for Damascus in Dhu al-Qa'da 302/915, and al-Daraqutni says he set out on hajj {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 109} {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 620}.
