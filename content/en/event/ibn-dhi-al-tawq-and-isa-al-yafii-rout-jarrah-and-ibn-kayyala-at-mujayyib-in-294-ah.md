## Overview
Ibn Dhi al-Tawq and Isa al-Yafi'i go to al-Maghrib and camp at Mujayyib and Musayyib. Jarrah and Ibn Kayyala march out against them with the men of Sanaa and are routed, with about four hundred killed among the men of Sanaa and others.

## Detail
The book has Ibn Dhi al-Tawq and Isa al-Yafi'i move to al-Maghrib and camp at Mujayyib and Musayyib. Jarrah and Ibn Kayyala march out against them with the men of Sanaa and their forces. The text says they fought and were routed, and that about four hundred were killed from Sanaa and elsewhere. The routed side is Jarrah's, since the dead are named as men of Sanaa and others.
