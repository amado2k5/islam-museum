## Overview
After about ten years in Damascus, al-Maqrizi returned to Egypt around 820 and withdrew to his house, keeping to seclusion, worship and writing; he rarely called on anyone except from necessity, and his fame as a historian became proverbial in his lifetime. He also spent a number of years in Mecca, where he heard traditions from Ibn Sakr and others {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 213} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 254-255}.
