## Summary
Al-Salabi dates [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]]'s cession of the sultanate to his son [[person/selim-i-ottoman|Selim I]] at 18 Safar 918 / 25 April 1512 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 174-175}.

## In Ibn al-Imad
Ibn al-Imad adds that when Bayezid's illness worsened, his son Selim contested him for the throne; he yielded it, then died near Guria {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 485}.
