## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that after the battle of Jalula, Hashim ibn Utba added dense horsemen to [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]] to stand between the Muslims and their enemy. Then Sa'd ordered him to advance on Hulwan with about three thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.423}

## The conquest
When Jarir came near, Yazdegerd fled toward Isfahan. Jarir took [[place/hulwan|Hulwan]] by treaty, and granted its people safety for their lives and property, and that whoever wished to flee should not be molested. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.423}

## After the conquest
Jarir left [[person/uzra-ibn-qays-al-bajali|Uzra ibn Qays al-Bajali]] at Hulwan and went toward Dinawar, but did not conquer it. He took Qarmasin on terms like those of Hulwan. Then, on Umar's order, he marched to Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in 19 AH, as al-Baladhuri relates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.423}
