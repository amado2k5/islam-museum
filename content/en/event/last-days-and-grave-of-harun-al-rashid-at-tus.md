## Summary
The caliph [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] died at [[place/tus|Tus]] in the year 193, ill on his way to Tus, and the histories relate that he ordered his grave dug while he was alive, was carried in a litter until he saw it, and had the Qur'an recited through in it. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 16 p. 14} His son Salih prayed over him and he was buried in a village of Tus called Sanabad. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} The museum keeps a draft card for the [[building/harun-al-rashid-mausoleum-tus|Harun al-Rashid mausoleum at Tus]] whose sources are not yet checked. This record completes the [[event/death-of-harun-al-rashid|record of his death]].

## The illness
Ibn Kathir reports that his illness was a bloody flux, or by another account consumption, and that the physician [[person/jibril-ibn-bukhtishu|Jibril ibn Bukhtishu]] was concealing how things stood with him. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} Al-Rashid told a man to take his urine in a flask to Jibril without saying whose it was, and when Jibril saw it he said it was like that man's water; the man understood whom he meant and asked, and Jibril said he would live only days. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} When this was told to al-Rashid he sent for Jibril, who hid until al-Rashid died. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} In another report al-Dhahabi gives, Jibril erred in his treatment and that was the cause of his death; al-Rashid meant to have him cut up, but Jibril said: wait until tomorrow, you will wake well; and he died that day. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 16 p. 14}

## The grave he ordered dug
Al-Dhahabi relates that al-Rashid dreamt that he was heading for Tus, wept and said: dig me a grave; they dug it, he was carried in a canopy on a camel until he saw the grave, and said: son of Adam, you come to this; and he had men go down and read the Qur'an through there, while he was in a litter at the edge of the grave. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 16 p. 14} Ibn Kathir says he ordered his grave dug in his lifetime and the whole Qur'an read in it, and when carried to see it he kept saying: to this you come, son of Adam, and weeping, and ordered it widened at the chest. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} Ibn Kathir also mentions a dream of a hand and red earth and a voice saying this is the earth of the Commander of the Faithful, and says that Ibn Asakir relates that al-Rashid dreamt of a voice saying something of the kind, while it was said earlier that his brother Musa al-Hadi and their father Muhammad al-Mahdi had seen it, and God knows best. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613}

## His last words
Ibn Kathir relates that when he was dying he said: O God, let us benefit from Your kindness and forgive us our wrongdoing; O You who do not die, have mercy on one who dies. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} He recited two verses in which the person handed his record says that his wealth availed him nothing and his authority has perished, [[passage/quran-69-28|Qur'an 69:28]] [[passage/quran-69-29|Qur'an 69:29]], and spoke lines saying that he was staying at Tus where he had no kinsman, hoped for his Lord's mercy, and bore it with patience and submission. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613}

## Death, prayer and burial
He died at Tus on Saturday, three days into Jumada II of the year 193, or by other reports in Jumada I or Rabi' I. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} He was forty-five, or by other reports forty-six, forty-seven or forty-eight, and his caliphate lasted twenty-three years, one month and eighteen days. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} His son Salih prayed over him and he was buried in a village of Tus called Sanabad. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} Ibn Kathir adds that someone read lines of lament written on al-Rashid's tents at Sanabad as people left Tus after his death. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613}

## What was said of his death
Ibn Kathir relates that al-Fudayl ibn Iyad used to say that no one's death was harder for him than al-Rashid's, for fear of what would follow, and that he prayed God to add to al-Rashid's life from his own; after he died, strife and disagreement appeared, and the doctrine of the Qur'an's createdness, and they knew what he had feared. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 612} Al-Dhahabi says al-Rashid followed al-Mansur's character except in spending money. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 16 p. 15}

## Where the reports differ
The reports differ on the month of his death, on his age and on the cause of his illness: consumption, bloody flux or a mistake in treatment. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 613} Al-Dhahabi gives the digging of the grave as following a dream, and Ibn Kathir says that others also saw such a dream. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 16 p. 14}

## Sources and documentation
The record rests on Ibn Kathir and al-Dhahabi. The volumes of al-Tabari for the year 193 were not among the pages read. No written testament of al-Rashid to his sons at his death was found in these pages.
