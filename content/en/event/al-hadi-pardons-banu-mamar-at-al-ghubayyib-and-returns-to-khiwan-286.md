## Overview
The previous page ends with the people at prayer. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.136} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] told them to keep their ranks "by their units", and they did; the party that had been envoys to the group prayed with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} The account is Zaydi, told by the followers of the imam, and the group are [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]], as before; it follows [[event/al-hadi-at-hawth-and-the-array-at-al-damriyya-286]]. The place al-Ghubayyib, which the editor sends the reader to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab p. 139 for, is on p. 136, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.136} see [[place/al-ghubayyib-yemen|al-Ghubayyib]].

## The envoys' request
The envoys said: "Son of the Messenger of God, leave the people where they are, go out with a party and come forth from the camp, for the group fear you and will pray with you", and al-Hadi came out from his camp. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} When the group saw that he had, they all came to him and said: "Son of the Messenger of God, we have erred and you are the most entitled to pardon us, so grant our wrongdoer to our doer of good." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137}

## Al-Hadi's answer and Hashid's intercession
According to the book he said: "Were it not that Hashid was the first to support me and stand with me of Hamdan, I would have left these two plains running with your blood, but I do not like to let an enemy gloat over Hashid or to cut a hand with a hand." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} The words are attributed to the imam in a war setting and are given as they stand, with no judgement. The editor notes that the original reads "this" and was corrected from the other manuscript to "these". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} A party of the [[group/hashid|Hashid]] men present stood, prayed for him and said: "Son of the Messenger of God, grant them to us and pardon them; they will not go back to anything you dislike." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137}

## The pardon and the return
He granted them to the Hashid men, pardoned them, told them to go to their homes, and they went; al-Hadi returned to [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] "on Thursday, two days remaining of Rajab, in the year 286". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} The Thursday agrees with 29 Rajab in the tabular calendar, so the date is given to the month here.

## What follows
The account runs on into [[event/banu-mamar-come-down-on-khiwan-on-15-shaban-and-are-routed-286]], where the narrator says some people reported that certain men at Khiwan had written to Banu Ma'mar and corrupted them; the narrator gives this on unnamed authority, and the museum neither confirms nor denies it.
