## Summary
Yaqut's stay in Khwarazm was short: the Mongols attacked and he fled leaving all he had, escaping 'like one raised from his grave on the Day of Resurrection', suffering hardships on the road that tire the tongue to describe {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}.

## The Mosul letter
He reached Irbil in 617 but could not settle; he went on to Mosul with his means exhausted and from there wrote al-Qifti a letter telling his story and asking leave to come to him — in it he describes his distress at the Mongol advance — then travelled to Sinjar and on to Aleppo {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}.
