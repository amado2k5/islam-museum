## Summary
The caliph al-Mustanjid bi-Allah died and was succeeded by his son al-Mustadi bi-Amr Allah al-Hasan ibn Yusuf. Al-Mustanjid was among the best of the Abbasid caliphs in intellect, justice and piety, so much so that he became a proverbial example {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.28}.
