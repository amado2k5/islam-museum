## Summary
In 258 AH a plague appeared in al-Ahwaz and Iraq and spread as far as the borders of Fayd, as Ibn al-Jawzi relates in *al-Muntazam*. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 100}

## Its effect
The source reports that those who died in Baghdad each day numbered between five hundred and six hundred. In Basra there was a great "hadda", a collapse of buildings, by which most of the city fell, and more than twenty thousand people died in it. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 100}

The figures come from Ibn al-Jawzi's account alone, and need an additional source.
