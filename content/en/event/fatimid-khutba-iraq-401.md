## Summary
In Muharram of the year 401, the amir Qarwash ibn al-Muqallad al-Uqayli declared his obedience to [[person/al-hakim-bi-amr-allah|al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah]], the Fatimid ruler of Egypt, and had the Friday sermon delivered in his name at Mosul, Kufa and al-Mada'in, before returning to obedience to [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir Billah]]. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.249-251}

## The sermon in Mosul and along the Euphrates
Qarwash gathered the people of Mosul on the fourth Friday of Muharram. He clothed the preacher in a robe of honour and gave him a text of what to preach, which included prayers for the Fatimid imams, up to al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah. He then went down to Kufa, al-Mada'in and al-Qasr, where he had the Fatimid call proclaimed and interfered in their dealings. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.249,251}

## The caliphate rallies, and Baha al-Dawla
News reached the caliph al-Qadir Billah and alarmed him. He sent to Amid al-Juyush and wrote to [[person/baha-al-dawla|Baha al-Dawla]], and dispatched the judge [[person/al-baqillani|Abu Bakr al-Baqillani]] as an envoy to him. Baha al-Dawla released a hundred thousand dinars for the army's expenses and threatened Qarwash that he would march against him if he did not return. Qarwash apologised, removed the Fatimid sermon and restored the Abbasid call. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.251}
