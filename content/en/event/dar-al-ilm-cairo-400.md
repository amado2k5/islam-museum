## Overview
Ibn al-Imad, in "Shadharat al-Dhahab", says that [[person/al-hakim-bi-amr-allah|al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah]] turned to devotion and religion around 400 AH, and ordered the founding of the House of Knowledge (Dar al-Ilm) in Egypt. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 165-166}

## What the source reports
- He brought jurists and traditionists into the house, built up the Hakimi mosque in Cairo, and prayers for him became frequent. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 165-166}
- Things stayed that way for three years. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 165-166}
- Then he began to kill people of learning, closed the house, and prevented many good works. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 165-166}

## Dating
The date of the event is 400 AH as in the source, and the Gregorian year (around 1009 CE) is derived by conversion. No second source is given for the date here, so it needs an additional source.
