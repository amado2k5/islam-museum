## Overview
Al-Maqqari relates from Ibn al-Yas'a that the enemy took the city of Tudela and its sister Tarazona in 524 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.455}

The data does not name the commander or give details of the taking, and needs an additional source. The Gregorian date is derived and approximate.
