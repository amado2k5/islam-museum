## Summary
In 490 AH Arslan Arghun, son of the sultan Alp Arslan and ruler of Merv, Balkh, Nishapur and Termez, was killed by one of his slaves at Damghan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 394}.

## Consequences
Barkiyaruq had sent an army under his brother Sanjar to fight their uncle. When news of the killing reached them, Barkiyaruq took Nishapur and Balkh without a fight, and the khutba was read for him in Samarkand. Sanjar was left in charge of Khurasan. Muhammad ibn Anushtakin, a freedman of the amir Mikail the Seljuq, was placed over Khwarazm and given the title Khwarazmshah {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 394-395}.
