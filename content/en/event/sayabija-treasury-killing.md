## Overview
A group of the [[group/sayabija|Sayabija]] was entrusted with the treasury of [[place/basra|Basra]]. Al-Baladhuri says that their number was forty, and it is said four hundred. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What happened
Talha ibn Ubayd Allah and al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam came to Basra, where [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] had appointed Uthman ibn Hunayf al-Ansari as governor. The Sayabija refused to hand over the treasury until Ali came. The others came upon them in the early hours and killed them, and Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr was in charge of the affair with a group with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Their leader
The leader of the Sayabija at the time was Abu Salama al-Zatti. Al-Baladhuri describes him as a righteous man. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
