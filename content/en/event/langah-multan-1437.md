## Overview
As the Sayyids' grip on the area weakened, Multan became the centre of the Langah dynasty, which ruled between 1437 and 1526 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## Against the Lodis
The Langah state successfully defied Lodi attempts to incorporate Multan into their dominion, keeping the region outside Delhi's sway for nearly a century {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## Its end
The Langahs were defeated in 1526 by the Arghuns, who collaborated with the Mughals; Multan then passed into their dependency and into the Mughal empire's subas {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
