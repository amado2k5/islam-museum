## Overview
[[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili]] wrote to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] asking for safety, and al-Hadi refused to give it. The narrator's reason, set under the heading that Ahmad left for Iraq after asking safety and was not given it, is that al-Hadi had come to know there was no good in him, that he would not counsel him and meant only to disturb Islam and to seek evil turns of fortune against it; that is the narrator's reading of the imam's motive, not the museum's. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.197} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.198} When he despaired of safety he went out to [[place/tihama|Tihama]], then to [[place/mecca|Mecca]], then to [[place/iraq|Iraq]], crying out against al-Hadi to the black-clad force, the Musawwada, and stayed a year in Iraq, in the narrator's words humbled and ignored, his need not looked into; seeing this from the people of Iraq he came back to Mecca and then to Yemen. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.198}

## Detail
The editor's footnote on this page quotes two later works. The first, al-Hur al-Ayn, says Ahmad ibn Abdallah ibn Muhammad ibn Abbad al-Akili went from Yemen to Iraq as a delegate to the caliph [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] at the end of his days, asking help against Yahya ibn al-Husayn, found that [[person/al-muktafi-billah|al-Muktafi]] had already been given the pledge, and was received by him, who sent great armies with him until a letter came from the governor of the two sanctuaries saying that Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Alawi had left Sana'a, so the ruler gave up the plan. The second, Ghayat al-Amani, says Ibn Abbad went to Iraq to seek help from the Musawwada, was not answered after staying in the worst state, and came back humbled. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.198} The two reports differ on how far the caliph's help went; both are the editor's quotations and the museum gives no verdict between them. The name of the governor in the footnote is garbled in the scan and is not entered. It follows [[event/the-kulaybis-swear-obedience-and-the-akilis-are-sent-to-banu-yajr-287]] and the verse on the war follows in [[event/al-hadis-verse-on-his-first-war-with-the-kulaybis-and-akilis-287]].
