## Overview
To the citation of [[passage/quran-35-43|Fatir 35:43]] the letter adds the report of nine men in the city who did mischief and swore among themselves to attack a household by night, and the end of their scheme, as in [[passage/quran-27-48|an-Naml 27:48]] and the verses to [[passage/quran-27-53|an-Naml 27:53]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.277}. The museum does not copy those verses; it links them.

## Detail
Al-Hadi then says he is the readier to take God's discipline, recalls what God commanded His Prophet regarding people of that sort in wrongdoing, and cites the command to give notice on equal terms, as in [[passage/quran-8-58|al-Anfal 8:58]]. He says he is not one who allows treachery, deceit or scheming, and does not say what he does not do, and that he has given them notice of war on equal terms {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.277}. The page notes record that manuscript S reads a different phrase after "all his saying" and has "book" for another word on the page. The book is Zaydi; the descriptions of his opponents are al-Hadi's own words as copied, attributed to him and not repeated.
