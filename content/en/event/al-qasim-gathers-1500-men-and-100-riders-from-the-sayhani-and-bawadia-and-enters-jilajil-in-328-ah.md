## Overview
Al-Qasim goes to the Jilajil ghayl and seeks help from Ibn Amr al-Sayhani and Bawada'a. A thousand five hundred men and a hundred riders come out with him, all of Banu Sa'd meet him, and he enters the town. His brother Yahya returns to Alaf on Friday, twenty-one days before the end of Jumada II.

## Detail
The book says al-Qasim went to the Jilajil ghayl and sought help from Ibn Amr al-Sayhani and Bawada'a. A thousand five hundred men came out with him from them, with a hundred riders, and all of Banu Sa'd met him, and he entered the town. His brother Yahya went back to Alaf on Friday, twenty-one days before the end of Jumada II of the same year. The text then gives the colophon, after which come verses by al-Hadi in praise of Hamdan, which are not part of this packet. Two cautions: the book does not state the year, and 328 AH is placed by narrative order only (after the death of al-Hasan in Dhu al-Qa'da 327 and the Rabi II passage that follows it); and the editor's note records that the manuscript reads "al-Shaykhani" where the text has "al-Sayhani", so the name of Ibn Amr is not settled and the person is not identified.
