## Overview
On Wednesday, twelve days of Dhu al-Qa'da, the men advanced with their armies until they came to [[place/nuqm-sanaa-yemen|Nuqm]], and a body of them charged until it entered the village {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}. [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] sent a party of his army out by the Darb al-Jabbana and another by the Darb Ibn Zamarid and went out himself by the Darb al-Qutay', routing them until they left the village; the fighting closed on the left and the people fought hard until the evening prayer {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}. Then, the narrator says, God gave the victory over them, they were routed and a number were killed, among them the commander of al-Hakami's men, and each side went back to its camp {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}.

## Detail
The narrator writes twelve days of Dhu al-Qa'da; the Wednesday agrees with the tabular count, yet the museum fixes no day, since the phrase may mean days remaining. Al-Hakami is the name given to those from whom the reinforcement came in [[event/al-yafur-and-al-tarif-camp-at-adudan-and-fight-at-the-maydan-of-sanaa-288]], and the text does not name his commander. It comes before [[event/the-army-of-eid-al-adha-is-deceived-and-the-battle-at-the-maydan-288]].
