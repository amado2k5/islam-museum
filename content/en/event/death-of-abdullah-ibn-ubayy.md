## Summary
The head of the hypocrites, [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abdullah ibn Ubayy]], died after the Prophet's return from [[event/expedition-to-tabuk|Tabuk]] in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## The prayer over him and Umar's stand
The Prophet asked forgiveness for him and prayed over him, though [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] tried to stop him from praying over him; the Qur'an came down afterwards agreeing with Umar, forbidding prayer over one who was thus and standing at his grave {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## For young visitors
A man died who said he was a Muslim but harmed the Muslims in secret. The Prophet prayed over him although Umar advised him not to; then God's ruling came agreeing with what Umar had said.
