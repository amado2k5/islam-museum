## Overview
The battle of [[place/tabariyya|Tiberias]] in 429 AH. The caliph al-Zahir sent armies from Egypt to Syria under Anushtakin al-Dizbari to fight Salih ibn Mirdas, lord of Aleppo, and Hassan ibn Mufarrij, lord of Ramla. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1075}

## The outcome
They met them in Jordan at Tiberias, and they were routed. Salih ibn Mirdas and his youngest son were killed, and his eldest son Nasr (Shibl al-Dawla) escaped to Aleppo. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1075}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri year.
