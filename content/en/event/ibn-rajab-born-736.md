## Overview
The Hanbali hafiz Zayn al-Din Abu al-Faraj Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Hasan al-Salami al-Baghdadi, known as Ibn Rajab after the laqab of his grandfather Abd al-Rahman, was born in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] in Rabi' al-Awwal 736 AH, by agreement of the sources consulted by the editor of Jami' al-Ulum wal-Hikam; Ibn Hajar dated the birth to 706, which the editor considers a copyist's slip. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 7} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 933}

## The family
He came from a house of learning: his grandfather Rajab ibn al-Hasan was heard reading in Baghdad — Ibn Rajab says of him, 'hadith was read to my grandfather Abu Ahmad Rajab ibn al-Hasan several times in Baghdad while I attended, aged three, four and five' — and his father, the shaykh Shihab al-Din Ahmad, had heard from the masters of Baghdad. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 8}
