## Summary
Al-Hasan ibn 'Umar ibn Habib, Badr al-Din, was born at Damascus in Sha'ban 710 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/29} {cite:al-aalam 2/218-219}; his father was appointed muhtasib of Aleppo and the boy moved with him, grew up there and took his nisba from the city {cite:al-aalam 2/218-219}.
