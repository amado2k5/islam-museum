## Overview
Under the heading "the report of Abu al-Qasim's going out of the prison with his companion": we went out of the door of the house with nothing on us, and some Bedouin we knew met us and threw clothes over us, and we covered ourselves with them; a man called Ibn Abi al-A'azz took the turban off his head and threw it over Abu al-Qasim, and we went toward the gate of the fort {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.271} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.272}. We met [[person/asad-ibn-abi-yafur|As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur]], and one of his servants said to him "this is Abu al-Qasim"; he got down from his mule, greeted him and told him to ride it, and went on walking before him until a man of his servants gave up his horse and he rode that; we went on until we entered the council room of [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibrahim ibn Khalaf]] in the fort {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.272}.

## Detail
They began to open the irons on Abu al-Qasim and they hardly opened, only after trouble; Muhammad ibn Sa'id said: I saw As'ad put his hand between the iron and Abu al-Qasim's leg for fear it might hurt him as they struck it, and one of his servants said "leave us, we will do it for you", and he said "no, I am seeking blessing by touching the son of the Messenger of God"; so it went until they had taken the irons off him, and some of the clothes they asked for were given back, and they spent the night at Bayt Bus {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.272}. The editor's note on p. 272 says that Ghayat al-Amani (1/189) reports that in this year al-Mu'tadid died and was succeeded by al-Muktafi, who put Najah ibn Najah over Yemen, so that his letters came to Al Ya'fur for their acting for him over Yemen, that Sana'a belonged to Al Ya'fur in particular and for that reason their relation with al-Hadi was uneasy in spite of their love for him, and it also refers to Bulugh al-Maram p. 34; this is the editor's statement, not the book's. As'ad is linked as printed and only as probable; the blessing sought by touching Abu al-Qasim is Muhammad ibn Sa'id's report in a Zaydi book, with no verdict in the museum's voice. The report follows [[event/the-fort-of-bayt-bus-falls-to-the-sons-of-yafur-and-abu-al-qasim-bends-the-sword-291|the fall of the fort]]; see [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]].
