## Overview
"Shadharat al-Dhahab" reports that in 934 AH Imam Ahmad, known as al-Jarad (the Locust), took the city of Harar in the land of Abyssinia. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 617}

## The conquest
The power of Harar weakened and could not resist him, and Imam Ahmad's standing kept growing. He conquered many lands of Abyssinia and persisted in jihad and raiding. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 617}

The author adds that it was said his conquests resembled only those of the Companions. This description is attributed to the "Shadharat", not to the museum. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 617}

## Notes
The Gregorian date is derived from 934 AH, and it needs an additional source.
