## Overview
Al-Muqaddasi visited Khurasan in 374/984, the furthest east of his recorded journeys {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 544}.

## His family's link to Khurasan
Al-Muqaddasi's mother came from Biyar, a small town of Khurasan; her father, Abu Tayyib al-Shawwa, was reasonably well off and an expert in the art of construction, and emigrated to Jerusalem {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 544}.
