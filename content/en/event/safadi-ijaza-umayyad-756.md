## Summary
In Dhu al-Qa'da 756 al-Safadi composed the reply of al-Malik al-Salih Isma'il ibn al-Nasir Muhammad to the letter of the Marinid sultan Abu al-Hasan, and granted ijaza for both risalas in his own hand after Izz al-Din Abu Ya'la Hamza read them back in a single session at the Umayyad Mosque of Damascus — a reading that, the record says, 'graced the ear with eloquence and swayed hearts with its weight' {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 403}.
