## The story
Muslim relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet said: when Ramadan comes the gates of Paradise are opened, the gates of the Fire are closed, and the shayatin are chained {cite:sahih-muslim} [[passage/muslim-1079|Muslim 1079]]. In the Muwatta Malik relates from Abu Suhayl ibn Malik from his father from Abu Hurayra mauquf his saying: when Ramadan enters the gates of Paradise are opened, the gates of the Fire are closed, and the shayatin are chained — the editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr narrated it marfu' from Abu Suhayl {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-4-862|Muwatta 862]].

## The meaning
In this hadith Ramadan is the season in which the shayatin are chained, the gates of the Fire are closed and the gates of Paradise opened — a cosmic picture of fasting's virtue {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Notes
Muslim's report is marfu' while the Muwatta's is mauquf with marfu' routes noted; the Jinn & Devils Browser shows both in the devils section {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
