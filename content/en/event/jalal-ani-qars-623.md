## Summary
In Ramadan of 623 AH Jalal al-Din besieged the Georgian cities of Ani and Qars, then left troops over them and marched on Abkhaz lands, striking them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Jalal al-Din returned that Ramadan from Kirman to Tiflis and marched to the city of Ani, a Georgian town held by Iwani, commander of the Georgian armies, with the nobles who remained with him; he besieged Iwani there and sent a detachment to the city of Qars — also Georgian, both among the strongest and most defensible towns — invested both, fought their garrisons, set up mangonels and pressed the assault. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466} The Georgians defended them vigorously, fearing he would do to them what he had earlier done to their fellows at Tiflis; he stayed until part of Shawwal had passed, then left the army besieging them, returned to Tiflis, and from there sped into the land of Abkhaz and the remnants of the Georgians, striking those in it, plundering, killing, taking captives, devastating and burning the country while his troops took its spoil, and returned to Tiflis. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466}
