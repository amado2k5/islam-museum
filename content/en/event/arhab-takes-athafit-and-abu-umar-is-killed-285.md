## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] had put a man with the kunya Abu Umar over [[place/athafit|Athafit]]; the book calls him [[person/abu-umar-abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan-al-bahrani|Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan]] of Bahrain. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.95} Here it tells that [[person/arhab-ibn-al-daam|Arhab ibn al-Da'am]] and [[group/banu-salman-hamdan|Banu Salman]] reached Athafit after turning away from [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The account is Zaydi and in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum gives it as the narrators tell it, without a verdict.

## The temporisers and the death of Abu Umar
The narrator says some people at Athafit were "temporisers" who had dealt with the attackers over entering the village. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} When Arhab and Banu Salman came to Athafit, Abu Umar called the people out; a small group came to him and the rest let him down and did not fight with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The army grew against him and his companions, entered the village on them and killed him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The praise of him and the description of his killers' motive as corruption are the narrator's words.

## The stay and al-Da'am's arrival
Arhab and Banu Salman entered Athafit "seeking corruption against the imam and the violation of religion and Islam", in the narrator's words, which are his judgment of his opponents and are attributed to him; they stayed there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} When al-Da'am heard what had happened it is said that he was grieved; then he came by a road the book names, whose reading is uncertain, and entered Athafit on the night of Friday and stayed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The news reached al-Hadi and he set out from where he was. See [[event/al-hadi-sets-out-against-al-daam-and-holds-council-285]]. The book gives neither the day nor the month of the killing; it falls before 3 Shawwal 285 AH.
