## Overview
The "Shadharat al-Dhahab" relates that [[place/mecca|Mecca]] was struck by a great flood in Safar 865 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 313}

## What the source says
The available text gives nothing beyond this report: the month, the year, and the description of the flood as great. The extent of the damage or the number of victims is not given in the data for this page, and needs an additional source.

## Date
The Hijri date is Safar 865 AH. Its Gregorian equivalent (around November 1460 CE) is derived, not stated in the source. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 313}
