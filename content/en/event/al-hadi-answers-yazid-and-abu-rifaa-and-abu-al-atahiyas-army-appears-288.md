## Overview
Printed p. 206 completes the question that Yazid ibn Ali ibn Jamil al-Shakiri and Abu Rifa'a, a man of Khath'am, put to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at [[place/hadaqan-sanaa-yemen|Hadaqan]], begun in [[event/al-hadi-draws-up-his-ranks-at-hadaqan-and-two-men-ask-about-the-odds-288|al-Hadi draws up his ranks at Hadaqan]]: they say they see he has resolved on war against this man with no army with him, and they are told the other has a great army {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}. In the narrator's report al-Hadi answers them: be glad, for he will meet me, and if he hands this matter over to me, well, and if not I will strike his neck; and you will enter Sana'a tomorrow, God willing, and pray the Friday prayer in it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}. He then orders [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] to stand in front of the army {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}.

## Detail
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] had the banner that day; al-Hadi had given it to him at [[place/al-haira-hamdan-yemen|al-Ha'ira]], and he carried it before him until he entered Shibam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}. Then Abu al-Atahiya's army came into view, by the narrator's count four hundred horse and ten thousand foot, and when [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] saw al-Hadi's army he drew his own up in right, left and centre {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}. The words al-Hadi speaks are a war-time statement in the narrator's report and the museum passes no judgment on them. The banner held back from the last batch is thus settled; Ali ibn Muhammad is taken here to be the narrator himself, which is probable and not asserted.
