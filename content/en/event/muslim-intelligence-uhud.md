## Summary
While [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]] completed its preparations in Mecca, [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] was watching its movements closely {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 250}. As soon as the Meccan army moved toward [[place/medina|Medina]], al-Abbas sent an urgent letter to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] containing every precise detail of the army's size and equipment.

## Details
Al-Abbas's messenger covered the road between Mecca and Medina — some five hundred kilometres — in only three days {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 250}, and delivered the letter to the Prophet while he was at Quba's prayer ground.

After the letter was read — Ubayy ibn Ka'b read it — the Prophet ordered the news kept secret {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 250}. At once Medina entered a state of general alert: the men did not part from their weapons even during prayer, ready for any emergency.

To secure the Prophet's protection, a detachment of the Ansar — including Sa'd ibn Mu'adh, Usayd ibn Hudayr and Sa'd ibn Ubada — guarded his door and spent the night with him armed {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 250}. Guard detachments were also posted on all Medina's entrances and mountain-passes, and reconnaissance patrols circled the routes the polytheists might take, to avert any surprise {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 250}.
