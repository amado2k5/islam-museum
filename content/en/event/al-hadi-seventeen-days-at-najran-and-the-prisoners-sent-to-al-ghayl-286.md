## Overview
"Al-Hadi stayed at Najran seventeen days and left on the eighteenth of Jumada II." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.132} The date sits badly with what came before: he arrived on Tuesday after the Saturday of the seventeenth, about the twentieth of the month, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} and seventeen days after that reaches early Rajab; the museum keeps what the printed text says and does not correct it, so the date is given to the month. On p. 133 [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] is back at [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and stays some days of Rajab. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133}

## Those put out
"He put out the troublemakers who had made disorder in his land." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} Of [[group/yam-tribe|the Yam]]: Ibn Abi al-Jarrah, Bishr ibn Bakkar, Abu Mihjan, Khabbab ibn al-Muhtamil and a Muhammad ibn "?"; of "Banu al-Himas", one man; and of the Khushaymis, Marzuq ibn Muhammad, Nukhayl ibn Muhajir and Abu al-Wajih ibn Musa. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} The name Bishr ibn Bakkar was checked by re-reading the page image with optical character recognition, and the book leaves the fifth man's name with a question mark. See [[person/marzuq-ibn-muhammad-al-khushaymi|Marzuq ibn Muhammad]], [[person/nukhayl-ibn-muhajir-al-khushaymi|Nukhayl ibn Muhajir]], [[person/abu-al-wajih-ibn-musa-al-khushaymi|Abu al-Wajih ibn Musa]] and [[group/al-khushaymiyyun|the Khushaymis]]. The book's index lists Marzuq also at p. 330 and Abu al-Wajih at pp. 166 and 378, pages not yet read.

## Carried and jailed at al-Ghayl
"He had them fettered, carried them in litters to Sa'da and jailed them in a village near it called [[place/al-ghayl-saada|al-Ghayl]], about half a mile from Sa'da, a village of Banu Hamza and Banu Sa'd, in which are the Futaymis, who are among his trusted and loving men." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} The reason for jailing them at al-Ghayl and not at Sa'da is that [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] had taken a man of the Duhmis of Hamdan called [[person/husayn-ibn-hubaysh-al-duhmi|Husayn ibn Hubaysh]], a troublemaker, and jailed him in the Sa'da prison, and some of al-Akilis worked at it until they got him out; so these were not put in the Sa'da jail. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133}

## Back at Sa'da
"He returned to Sa'da and stayed some days of Rajab, then left for Khiwan on Sunday, sixteen days into Rajab." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} This is told in [[event/al-hadi-leaves-saada-for-khiwan-and-the-dry-journey-286]].

## Notes
The sentence "he left on the eighteenth of Jumada II" is as printed. The book does not say, in these pages, what became of the men after they were jailed. "Nukhayl" is the index reading, p. 458; the body text reads "Nahil" in the OCR and in a Vision re-reading. "Banu al-Himas" is as it stands. The Akilis who helped the Duhmi escape are named in the text without a note.
