## Summary
In 476 AH the people of Harran and their judge, Ibn Jalaba al-Hanbali, decided to hand the city to Junq, the Turkmen commander, because he was a Sunni. They rebelled against Muslim ibn Quraysh, the ruler of Mosul, because he was a Rafidi and was busy besieging Damascus with the Egyptians {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 350}.

## Taking the city with siege engines
Muslim hurried to Harran, bombarded it with siege engines, broke down its walls and took it. Then he slaughtered the judge Abu al-Fath and his two sons {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 350}.
