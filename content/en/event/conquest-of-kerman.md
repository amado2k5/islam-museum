## Overview
Kerman was conquered in 24; then its people broke their covenant, and Ibn 'Amir appointed over them Mujashi' b. Mas'ud al-Sulami — a Companion — and ordered him to fight them {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 428}.
