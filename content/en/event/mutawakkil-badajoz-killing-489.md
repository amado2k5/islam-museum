## Overview
Al-Mutawakkil Abu Hafs Umar ibn Muhammad, known as Sajja, sultan of Badajoz, was killed by [[person/yusuf-ibn-tashfin|Yusuf ibn Tashfin]], amir of the Almoravids, in 489 AH, and his sons were killed with him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1001}

## Ibn Khaldun's account
Ibn Khaldun says that Ibn Abbad, that is [[person/al-mutamid|al-Mu'tamid]], incited Yusuf against him. When suspicion took hold and al-Mutawakkil sensed it, he wrote to Yusuf and urged him to deal with him before he could approach the tyrant. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1001}
