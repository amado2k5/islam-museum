## Overview
After the death of Mu'awiya ibn Yazid, [[person/marwan-ibn-al-hakam|Marwan ibn al-Hakam]] prevailed over Syria and then Egypt, and he remained until his death in 65 AH. He designated his son [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik]] as his successor. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 349}

## Al-Dhahabi's judgement
Al-Suyuti quotes al-Dhahabi as saying that the sounder view is that Marwan is not counted among the commanders of the faithful, that he rebelled against Ibn al-Zubayr, and that his designation of his son is not valid. He says that Abd al-Malik's caliphate was valid from the time Ibn al-Zubayr was killed. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 349}

The opposing view needs an additional source.
