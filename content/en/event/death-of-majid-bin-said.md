## Summary
[[person/majid-bin-said|Majid bin Said]], the first Sultan of [[place/zanzibar|Zanzibar]], died at Zanzibar on 7 October 1870, aged 35 or 36. {cite:wikipedia-majid-bin-said-en}

## Burial and succession
He was buried in the Makusurani Cemetery. He left no son, and his brother [[person/barghash-ibn-said-al-busaidi|Barghash]] succeeded him. {cite:wikipedia-majid-bin-said-en} His reign had begun on 19 October 1856, and during it the succession dispute with Thuwaini ended in the Canning Award of 2 April 1861, a British arbitration that split the dominions into two sultanates. This record does not place that award in Zanzibar, because the sources do not say it happened there. {cite:wikipedia-majid-bin-said-en}
