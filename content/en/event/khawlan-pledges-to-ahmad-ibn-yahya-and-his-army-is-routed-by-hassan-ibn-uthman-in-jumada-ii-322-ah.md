## Overview
Khawlan pledges to Ahmad ibn Yahya, and his army is routed by Hassan ibn Uthman in Jumada II 322 AH.

## Detail
A group of Khawlan who had not come to him come to Ahmad ibn Yahya, ask him to rise, and pledge to him and swear. Letters from Hamdan and from the Ahlaf at Najran call him, and on Wednesday, thirteen nights into Jumada I 322 AH, he sets out, reaches the Ahlaf, camps at Raha, and falls seriously ill. On Thursday, five nights into Jumada II, a battle takes place between him and Hassan. His army has no commander, the men scatter and are routed, and al-Hasan ibn al-Hadi ila al-Haqq is killed with a number of others, as the book states.
