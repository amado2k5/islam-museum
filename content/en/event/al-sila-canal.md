## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] ordered the digging of [[place/nahr-al-sila|the Nahr al-Sila]] and assigned its revenue to the stipends of the people of the two holy sanctuaries. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 409}

## The digging
The Commander of the Faithful al-Mahdi ordered the canal dug. They dug it and revived the lands along it. Its revenue was assigned to the stipends of the people of the two sanctuaries and to expenses there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 409}

## The condition of the cultivators
Those cultivators who gathered there were given a term of fifty years, after which they would share the produce in half. The source says that they kept to this condition in its time. The subject is connected to [[concept/kharaj|kharaj]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 409}
