## Overview
[[person/yazid-ibn-al-walid|Yazid ibn al-Walid]], known as al-Naqis, died on the seventh of Dhu al-Hijja 126 AH, in the same year in which he had taken the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.408-409}

## The length of his caliphate and his age
His caliphate was less than six months. al-Suyuti gives his age as thirty-five years, and it is also said forty-six. It is said that he died of plague. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.408-409}

## His title
He was called al-Naqis (the Deficient) because he reduced the troops' stipends. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.408-409}

## Dating
The Gregorian date (around October 744 CE) is derived from the Hijri date. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.409}

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says Yazid ibn al-Walid died at Damascus ten nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja, 126. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 369}
