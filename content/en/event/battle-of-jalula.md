## Overview
After Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas came down at al-Mada'in, the Muslims completed the conquest of Jalula, Tikrit and Mosul, and then the people moved to Kufa. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.21}

## A disputed date
Abu Ma'shar and al-Waqidi report that the conquest of Jalula was in 19 AH under Sa'd. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.102} al-Baladhuri places the battle at the end of 16 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 368-370} The date is therefore disputed.

## The battle in al-Baladhuri
The Muslims were told that Yazdegerd had gathered a great host at Jalula. Sa'd sent Hashim ibn Utba with twelve thousand men. Hashim found the Persians had fortified themselves and dug a trench, and had placed their families and baggage at Khanaqin. Then the Muslims charged in one attack and routed them, and rode on their backs until darkness fell. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 368-370}

After that Hashim put Jarir ibn Abd Allah in charge at Jalula with a dense force of horse, and Yazdegerd moved out of Hulwan. The Muslims made peace with the people of Mahrudh and al-Bandanijin, and Jarir came to Khanaqin and killed those non-Arabs who remained there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 368-370}

al-Baladhuri, quoting Hisham ibn al-Kalbi, says that the amir of the people at Jalula, on Sa'd's appointment, was Amr ibn Utba ibn Nawfal. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 368-370}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] names Amr ibn Malik ibn Najaba al-Zuhri as the Muslim commander at Jalula', unlike other accounts, with Hujr ibn Adi on the right, Amr ibn Ma'dikarib over the horse and Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid over the foot. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 127} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 128} He says they fought all day until the sun turned yellow, and that Kharija ibn al-Salt found a she-camel of gold set with pearls and rubies and handed it to the officer of the spoils. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 128} Jarir was left at Jalula' with four thousand horse. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 129}
