## Summary
When Amr ibn al-As had taken Egypt, Mu'awiya sent Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-Hadrami to Basra to win over its people. He instructed him to settle among Mudar, win the goodwill of the Azd, and beware of Rabi'a. He settled among the Banu Tamim and preached that Uthman, the "Imam of guidance," had been killed unjustly. He read Mu'awiya's letter reminding them of Uthman's favours to them, and promised them two stipends a year. Then al-Dahhak ibn Qays al-Hilali, chief of police for Ibn Abbas, confronted him and said: "You have brought us what Talha and al-Zubayr brought us. Do you order us to draw our swords against one another so that Mu'awiya may be amir?" Abd Allah ibn Khazim al-Sulami answered him: "We are your supporters and your hand." Al-Ahnaf stood up and said: "Neither my she-camel nor my male camel is in this," and withdrew from the people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 711}.
