## Overview
Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-hubayra|Umar ibn Hubayra]] that the commander of the faithful had no right of "kharasa" in the land of the Arabs, and ordered him to pass over the estates and take their surplus. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The survey
Umar went to the estates, asked about them, and then surveyed them. When the owner of a land asked him where he got the right, he answered that they had inherited it from true fathers, and their children would inherit it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then the people complained of it, and he stopped. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
