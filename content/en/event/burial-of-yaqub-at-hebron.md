## Summary
Ibn Kathir reports, from Ibn Ishaq and the People of the Book, that [[person/yaqub|Ya'qub]] lived in Egypt with his son [[person/yusuf|Yusuf]] for seventeen years and then died; that he had charged Yusuf to bury him with his fathers Ibrahim and Ishaq; and that Yusuf took him to the Levant and buried him in the cave at Habrun. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} Al-Tabari and al-Qurtubi agree on the charge and the burial itself. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 419} {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 scan vol. 3 p. 616}

## Ya'qub's charge to Yusuf
In Ibn Ishaq's report from the People of the Book, as Ibn Kathir gives it, Ya'qub stayed in Egypt with Yusuf for seventeen years and then died, having charged Yusuf to bury him beside his fathers Ibrahim and Ishaq. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} Al-Tabari, in a report he sets out from some of the People of the Book, says that when death came to Ya'qub he made Yusuf his executor, that he had entered Egypt with seventy of his family, and that at his death he told Yusuf to carry his body and bury it by the side of his father Ishaq. {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 419} Al-Qurtubi adds that Ya'qub lived one hundred and forty-seven years, died in Egypt and charged that he be carried to the Holy Land and buried by his father Ishaq, which Yusuf did. {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 scan vol. 3 p. 616}

His charge to all his sons at death is the subject of [[event/testament-of-ibrahim-and-yaqub-to-their-sons|Testament of Ibrahim and Ya'qub to their sons]].

## His age
Ibn Kathir says the People of the Book give Ya'qub's age at entering Egypt as one hundred and thirty, say he stayed seventeen years, and yet total his life at one hundred and forty. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} He calls this a mistake, either in the copy or by them, since they do not usually drop a fraction in a matter like this. {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 333} Al-Qurtubi gives one hundred and forty-seven, which is the sum of the two figures. {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 scan vol. 3 p. 616}

## Preparation and carrying to Habrun
Ibn Kathir gives the following on the authority of the People of the Book and only relays it: when Ya'qub died the people of Egypt wept for him seventy days, and Yusuf ordered the physicians to embalm him with perfumes, which took forty days. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} Yusuf then asked the king of Egypt's leave to go out with his father and bury him among his kin; the king agreed and the notables and elders of Egypt went with him. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} When they reached Habrun they buried him in the cave Ibrahim al-Khalil had bought from Ephron ibn Sakhr the Hittite, and held a seven-day mourning. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} They then went home, and Yusuf's brothers consoled him over their father and appealed to him gently; he honoured them and treated them well, and they stayed in Egypt. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224}

Al-Suddi, as Ibn Kathir relays him, says that Yusuf prepared his father and travelled with him to the Levant and buried him in the cave beside his father Ishaq and grandfather al-Khalil. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-iqra-ebook scan p. 332}

## The grave
The cave where he lies is the one Ibrahim had bought, at al-Khalil, where the [[building/al-haram-al-ibrahimi|Ibrahimi Sanctuary]] stands; for the question of where in the building the graves are placed see that record, [[event/burial-of-ibrahim-at-hebron|the burial of Ibrahim]] and [[event/patriarchs-tombs-revealed-513|the revealing of the tombs in 513]].

## A note on the reports
Most of the detail above on preparation and the days of mourning comes from the books of the People of the Book as Ibn Kathir presents them, with no prophetic statement behind it. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} Ibn Ishaq, al-Suddi, al-Tabari and al-Qurtubi agree on the charge to be buried with his fathers; the remaining details and the figure for his age differ. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 2 p. 224} {cite:tarikh-al-tabari scan vol. 2 p. 419} {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-resala-v1 scan vol. 3 p. 616}
