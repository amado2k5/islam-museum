## Overview
The sultan [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] died in 589 AH, according to al-Suyuti. An envoy reached Baghdad carrying his war-garment, his horse, one dinar and thirty-six dirhams, which was all the money he left. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693}

## After him
Egypt passed to his son Imad al-Din Uthman, al-Malik al-Aziz; Damascus to his son Nur al-Din Ali, al-Malik al-Afdal; and Aleppo to his son Ghiyath al-Din Ghazi, al-Malik al-Zahir. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 693}

See also [[event/death-of-salah-al-din-589|the death of Salah al-Din in Ibn al-Athir's "al-Kamil"]]. The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
