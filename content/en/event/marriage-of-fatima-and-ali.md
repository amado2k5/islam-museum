## Overview
[[person/fatima|Fatima]], the daughter of the Prophet, married [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], the Prophet's cousin, in [[place/medina|Medina]], and the Prophet's descendants continued through the two of them. {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}
