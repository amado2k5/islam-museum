## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] sent [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir al-Bajali]] to [[place/hamadhan|Hamadhan]] in 23 AH. Its people fought him, and his eye was struck by an arrow. Then he took it on terms like those of Nihawand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.433}

## The date
al-Baladhuri says that this was at the end of 23 AH. al-Waqidi says that Jarir conquered Hamadhan in 24 AH, six months after the death of Umar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.433} Some relate that al-Mughira himself marched to it, with Jarir at his vanguard.

## The breach and the treaty
When Sa'd became governor of Kufa for Uthman, he appointed [[person/al-ala-ibn-wahb|al-Ala' ibn Wahb]] over Mah and Hamadhan. The people of Hamadhan broke the covenant, and then they came down under his judgement. He made peace with them for the kharaj of their land, the [[concept/jizya|jizya]] per head, and a hundred thousand dirhams, and then no one was to be molested in their property, their sanctity or their children. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.433}
