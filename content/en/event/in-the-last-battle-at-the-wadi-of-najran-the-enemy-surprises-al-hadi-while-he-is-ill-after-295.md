## Overview
Al-Hadi's companions tell that his last fighting was in the wadi of Najran, while he was ill with the illness of which he died. The enemy surprised his camp at the fort at night, and when the two lines met, his companions broke. Yusuf ibn Abi Harb al-Absi was killed, the last man to fall with al-Hadi.

## Detail
Al-Hadi's companions relate that his last fighting was in the wadi of Najran, and that he was ill with the illness of which he died. The enemy surprised al-Hadi at the fort. His horsemen went out, but he did not go out because he was ill. When the two lines came in sight of each other, the attack fell on his companions and they turned and fled. A man of them named Yusuf ibn Abi Harb al-Absi was killed by the sword, the last martyr to fall with al-Hadi. He fought no more battles until he died.
