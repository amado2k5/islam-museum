## Overview
al-Suyuti mentions that in 138 AH Abd al-Rahman ibn Mu'awiya ibn Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik of the Umayyads entered [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]] and seized it. It remained in the hands of his descendants until after the year 400. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 423-425}

## In al-Suyuti's account
He is described as a man of learning and justice, whose mother was Berber. al-Suyuti quotes the saying: "The world was ruled by two sons of Berber women: al-Mansur and Abd al-Rahman ibn Mu'awiya." He is said to have taken the title of Amir rather than Amir al-Mu'minin, and so did his sons. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 423-425} The contemporary caliph was [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]].
