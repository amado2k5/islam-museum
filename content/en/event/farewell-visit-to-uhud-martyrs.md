## Summary
In early Safar 11 AH the Prophet visited the martyrs of Uhud and prayed over them like one taking leave of the living and the dead, and said from the pulpit that he would go before them and could see his basin {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 464}.

## The account
In early Safar 11 AH the Prophet went out to [[place/uhud|Uhud]] and prayed over the martyrs like one taking leave of the living and the dead {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 464; sahih-al-bukhari}. Then he returned to the pulpit and said: "I go before you and am a witness over you; and by God, I am looking at my basin even now; I have been given the keys of the treasuries of the earth; and by God, I do not fear that you will associate others with God after me, but I fear for you that you will vie with one another for it" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 464; sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
Before his death the Prophet visited the graves of the martyrs of Uhud and prayed over them as though bidding them farewell, then reassured his Companions that he would go before them to the Basin.
