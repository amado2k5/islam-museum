## Overview
al-Tabari relates that Suhayl ibn Adi marched to Kirman, and fought its people in its lowest lands, and God routed them. Its marzban, al-Nasir ibn Amr, was killed. Suhayl entered by the road of the villages to Jaytarfat. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.180}

By al-Tabari's dating the event was in 23 AH. It needs an additional source.
