## Overview
After some thirty years of rule by a triumvirate of Sikh chieftains, Ranjit Singh wrested Lahore from its three lords in Safar 1214/July 1799; proclaimed maharaja of the Punjab in 1802, he restored much of the city's prestige, repairing its walls and embarking on a programme of construction {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 39}.
