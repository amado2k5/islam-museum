## Overview
In 258 Sa'id b. Ahmad b. Sa'id b. Salm al-Bahili was arrested — headman of the Bahilis who had set their sights on the Bata'ih after the Zanj left it and worked corruption there; sent to Baghdad, al-Mu'tamid 'ala Allah ordered him lashed seven hundred strokes, and he was scourged then crucified in Rabi' II — whereupon the rest of their chiefs joined the Zanj {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 100}.
