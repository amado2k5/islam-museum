## Overview
Ibn Hajar opened his hadith lectures at the Shaykhuniyya college in Shawwal 808 (March 1406); he later taught at the renovated Jamaliyya from its opening in Rajab 811 (November 1408) and at the Mankutimuriyya from Jumada II 812 (October 1409). He also held the mufti's seat in the Dar al-Adl from 811 until his death and served as associate preacher and imam at al-Azhar and the Mosque of Amr {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 809} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 810}.
