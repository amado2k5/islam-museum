## Overview
Ibrahim ibn Khalaf sets out from al-Kudra' in Muharram 292 AH for Mount Bayt Dhakhar. The servants of Adnan, the lord of the place, kill him at Tuhrani and his head is sent to his mawali.

## Detail
Ibrahim ibn Khalaf set out at the start of Muharram 292 AH from al-Kudra' toward Mount Bayt Dhakhar. When he reached the edge of the mountain at a place called Tuhrani, the servants ('ubayd) of Adnan, the lord of that place, met him and killed him. His companions fled, and the head was sent to his mawali. Derived conversion: 1 Muharram 292 AH falls in about November 904 CE (tabular calendar). The identity of Ibrahim ibn Khalaf is not settled in any source on the drive, so no person record is made.
