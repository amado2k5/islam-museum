## Overview
In Jumada I 400 work began on a stout wall around the shrine at al-Ha'ir; Abu Muhammad al-Hasan b. al-Fadl b. Sahlan had visited the shrine and vowed it a protective wall against the Bedouin who pressed upon the place — so he fulfilled his vow, built it strong, raised and widened it, set firm gates on it, some of iron, and the shrine was fortified and made fair {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.246}. Another notice dates the building to 403.
