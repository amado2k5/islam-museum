## Overview
Before the account the copyist gives a heading: the coming of al-Rabi' ibn al-Ruwayya to al-Hadi with aid from Ja'far ibn Ibrahim al-Ja'fari {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}; nothing in the three pages after the heading tells it, so the museum records it only as a heading. The narrator then says: [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and [[person/abu-al-ghasham-ibn-tarif|Abu al-Ghasham ibn Tarif]] set out from Shibam with their armies and camped at Adudan, where they stayed some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}. On Tuesday, fourteen days of Shawwal gone, the men came down with their armies to the parade ground of [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]]; [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went out with his armies and stood in front of them until the prayer was due, dismounted and prayed, and went back to his post; the men charged his left with their horse and a number of his companions were struck {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}. Al-Hadi then ordered horse who charged the men, scattered them and drove them to their camp; he returned to Sana'a, and reinforcements of al-Hakami with horse and foot reached Banu Ya'fur and Al Tarif {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.233}.

## Detail
The museum reads the place name Adudan as printed on the page; the reading wavered between ayn and kaf in nearby places, and the museum does not fix the site. A Tuesday with fourteen days of Shawwal gone does not agree with the tabular calendar, so it gives the month only. The word al-Hakami is a name or title that the text does not explain. The account is followed by [[event/fight-at-nuqm-in-dhu-al-qada-and-the-hakamis-commander-is-killed-288]].
