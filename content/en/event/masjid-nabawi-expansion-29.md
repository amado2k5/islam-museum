## Summary
In year 29, 'Uthman enlarged the Prophet's Mosque in Medina in Rabi' I, during his caliphate {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.475}.

## Ibn al-Athir's description
Ibn al-Athir says: "He carried the gypsum from Batn Nakhl, built it of engraved stone, made its columns of stone with lead in them, made its length a hundred and sixty cubits and its width a hundred and fifty cubits, and kept its doors as they had been in 'Umar's days — six doors" {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.475}.

## For young visitors
The caliph 'Uthman made the Prophet's Mosque in Medina bigger, building it with stone and gypsum and six doors.
