## Overview
Al-Mas'udi relates that Shapur camped with his troops at the fortress of [[place/hadra|al-Hadr]], the fortress of al-Dayzan al-Tanukhi, and spent a month there without finding a way to take it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 552-554}

## The stratagem and the entry
Al-Nudayra, daughter of al-Dayzan, who had fallen in love with him, as al-Mas'udi relates, sent to him to scatter straw in the river al-Tharthar above the fortress and then follow it to see where it entered. He did so, and brought men in by that place. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 552-554}

The people of the fortress were not aware until Shapur's men were among them. Al-Dayzan and those with him were killed, and he ordered the fortress demolished. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 552-554}

## Background
Al-Mas'udi says that al-Dayzan had many soldiers, was at peace with the Byzantines, and his men raided Iraq and the countryside. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 552-554}

The event involves [[person/sabur-ibn-ardashir|Shapur ibn Ardashir]] and [[person/al-dayzan-ibn-muawiya|al-Dayzan ibn Mu'awiya]], and takes place at [[place/hadra|al-Hadr]]. The date needs an additional source.
