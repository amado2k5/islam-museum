## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]] sent his brother [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-muslim|Abd al-Rahman ibn Muslim]] against Khurrazad. He fought him and killed him, and took four thousand captives and killed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The event took place in [[place/khwarizm|Khwarizm]].

## Afterwards
The first king of Khwarizm became king on the terms agreed with him. His people told him he was weak, rose against him and killed him. Qutayba then appointed his brother [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-muslim|Ubayd Allah ibn Muslim]] over Khwarizm. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
