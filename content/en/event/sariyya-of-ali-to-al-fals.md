## Summary
After [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] purified the Sacred House of idols, he sent out expeditions one after another to cleanse the Peninsula of the remaining traces of pre-Islamic paganism; among them was [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali's]] expedition to al-Fuls, the idol of Tayyi', in Rabi' al-Akhir {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.110}.

## The Incident
Ali set out with one hundred and fifty men of the Ansar, on one hundred camels and fifty horses, carrying a black banner and a white standard; they raided the settlement of the clan of Hatim al-Ta'i at dawn, demolished and destroyed al-Fuls, and captured prisoners, camels, and sheep—among the captives was the sister of Adi ibn Hatim, while Adi fled to Syria {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.110}.

## Notes for Researchers
- The book mentions neither the Hijri year nor the day; the recorded date is derived from the sequence of the conquest and remains disputed in exact dating, so it is treated as an event whose year is uncertain.
- The number of men and the count of camels and horses are transmitted as stated in the book without verification from a second source.
