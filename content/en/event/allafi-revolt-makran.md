## Overview
al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf appointed Sa'id ibn Aslam al-Kilabi over the frontier of [[place/makran|Makran]]. Then Mu'awiya and Muhammad, sons of al-Harith al-Allafi, rose against him. Sa'id was killed, and they took control of the frontier. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governors after that
al-Hajjaj appointed [[person/mujjaa-ibn-sir|Mujja'a ibn Sir al-Tamimi]] over the frontier. He raided, took booty, and conquered groups of Qandabil. [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] completed its conquest after him. Mujja'a died in Makran a year later. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then al-Hajjaj appointed [[person/muhammad-ibn-harun|Muhammad ibn Harun ibn Dhar' al-Namari]]. During his governorship, the king of the Island of Yaqut sent al-Hajjaj a gift of women who had been born Muslim in his lands and whose fathers had died; the fathers had been merchants. al-Baladhuri mentions that the king of the island meant by this to draw close to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The report also mentions [[person/said-ibn-aslam|Sa'id ibn Aslam]] and [[person/imran-ibn-musa-al-barmaki|Imran ibn Musa al-Barmaki]], the builder of al-Bayda. The data gives no detail of his role, which needs an additional source.

## Note
The report comes from "Futuh al-Buldan" alone, and the data holds no date for it.
