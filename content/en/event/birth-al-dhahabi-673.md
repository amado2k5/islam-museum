## Overview
Abu Abd Allah Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Uthman ibn Qaymaz, al-Dhahabi, of Turkmen origin, al-Fariqi, of Damascus, a Shafi'i, was born on 3 Rabi' II 673 at Damascus {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336}; the Encyclopaedia of Islam gives his birth at Damascus or Mayyafariqin on 1 or 3 Rabi' II, corresponding to 5 or 7 October 1274, and records that his milk-brother al-Ala ibn al-Munajja Ahmad ibn Abi al-Khayr issued him an ijaza in his birth year {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 214} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336}.
