## Overview
In 2016 Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad, representing al-Azhar, addressed the UN Security Council in New York on al-Azhar's strategy against extremist thought; the session was chaired by Egypt's foreign minister while Egypt headed the Council's counter-terrorism committee {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 95}. The writer holds that extremism stays within the sphere of thought until joined to physical violence, takfir, or threats — then it becomes terrorism {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 99}; among al-Azhar's tasks: discussing extremist ideas and answering them.
