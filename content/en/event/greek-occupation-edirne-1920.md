## Overview
The Greeks occupied Edirne between 1920 and 1922, after the city had fallen briefly to the Bulgarians in 1913 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 710}.

## A frontier city
Since the Balkan Wars Edirne has been a Turkish frontier city; its population fell from over 100,000 in the mid-nineteenth century to 34,528 at the 1927 census {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 710}.
