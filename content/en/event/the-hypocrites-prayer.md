## The story
Malik relates from al-Ala ibn Abd al-Rahman who said: we entered on Anas ibn Malik after noon and he rose to pray the afternoon prayer; when he finished we mentioned hastening the prayer or he mentioned it, then he said: I heard the Prophet say three times: that is the prayer of the hypocrites — one of them sits until the sun pales and is between the shaytan's horns or upon his horn, then rises and performs four barely remembering God {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-586|Muwatta 586]].

## The meaning
In this hadith prayer at the shaytan's horn-times is the prayer of the hypocrites; it is a prayer of show without sincerity {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
