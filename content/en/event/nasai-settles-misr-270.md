## Summary
After his journeys al-Nasa'i settled in Egypt, lodging at Zuqaq al-Qanadil, where people transmitted from him and his works spread {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 106} {cite:wafayat-ayan i p. 26}.

## In Egypt
Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar describes his devotion to worship night and day, his going out on campaign with the amir of Egypt, his observance of the transmitted practices in ransoming Muslim captives, and his shunning of the sultan's gatherings {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 108-109}; al-Daraqutni called him the most learned of Egypt's shaykhs of his age in law, hadith and the science of narrators {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 109}.
