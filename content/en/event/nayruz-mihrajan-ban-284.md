## Overview
In Jumada I 284 it was proclaimed in Baghdad's quarters and markets: no lighting fires on Nawruz night, no pouring water on its day — and the like on Mihrajan; then on Thursday eve it was proclaimed that the Commander of the Faithful had released the people to light fires and pour water, and the commons exceeded all bounds until they poured water on the police themselves — among the greatest of fitnas {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 172}.
