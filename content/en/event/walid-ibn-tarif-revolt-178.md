## Overview
In 178 AH [[person/al-walid-ibn-tarif-al-shari|al-Walid ibn Tarif al-Shari]] rose in al-Jazira, and his affair grew great; Harun al-Rashid sent against him Yazid ibn Mazyad al-Shaybani. {cite:duha-islam p.951-952}

## The confrontation
It was said to al-Rashid that Yazid was reluctant against al-Walid because of the kinship between them. Yazid told his companions that they were the Kharijites and had a charge, so they should hold firm, and when their charge had spent itself they should charge against them, for when they are defeated they do not return. {cite:duha-islam p.951-952}

Al-Walid was killed and his revolt ended. His sister al-Farida lamented him in poems, among them her famous ode. {cite:duha-islam p.951-952, 957}
