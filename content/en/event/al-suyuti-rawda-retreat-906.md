## Overview
Al-Suyuti’s complete retreat to his house on Rawda island took place in 906/1501, following the conflict with the residents of the Baybarsiyya khankah, whom he regarded as pseudo-Sufis. Forced to reduce their salaries, he had to go into hiding to escape the persecution of Sultan Tuman Bay, who supported his adversaries, and was dismissed from his functions; in his house he concentrated on composing and revising his works {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
