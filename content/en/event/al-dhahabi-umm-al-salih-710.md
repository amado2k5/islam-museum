## Overview
Al-Dhahabi held the professorship of hadith at the turba Umm al-Salih in Damascus and at the al-Nafisiyya madrasa {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/337}, having received his licence to teach from Abu Zakariyya ibn al-Sayrafi, Ibn Abi al-Khayr, al-Qasim al-Irbili and others. He was unable to succeed his teacher al-Mizzi at the al-Ashrafiyya madrasa in Damascus because its founder had stipulated a particular law school for the professor of tradition {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 215}.
