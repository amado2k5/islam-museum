## Overview
The raid of [[person/al-harith-ibn-abi-shamir|al-Harith ibn Abi Shamir al-Ghassani]], called "the Lame", against the [[group/banu-taghlib|Taghlib]]. The king of [[group/ghassan|Ghassan]] in Syria passed by some Taghlibi groups who did not receive him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}

## The exchange
Al-Harith threatened them with a raid that would leave them awake for his coming. [[person/amr-ibn-kulthum|Amr ibn Kulthum]] answered that the people had only woken to the sharpness of their judgement and the strength of their band. Al-Harith threatened to uproot their stock. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}

## The fighting
They fought fiercely, and al-Harith and the Banu Ghassan were routed, and a brother of al-Harith was killed among a great number. Amr ibn Kulthum composed his famous ode about it, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.403}
