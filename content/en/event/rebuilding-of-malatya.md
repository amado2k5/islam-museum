## Overview
In al-Baladhuri's account, [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] wrote in 139 AH to [[person/salih-ibn-ali|Salih ibn Ali]], ordering him to build [[place/malatya|Malatya]] and fortify it. He then decided to send [[person/abd-al-wahhab-ibn-ibrahim|Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ibrahim al-Imam]] as governor of the Jazira and its frontiers; he set out in 140 AH with [[person/hasan-ibn-qahtaba|al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba]] with the troops of Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Building
Seventy thousand were gathered after the summons had been imposed on the people of Syria and the Jazira. They camped at Malatya, and the workers were gathered from every land. The city and its mosque were completed in six months. Al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba sometimes carried stones to hand them to the builders, and he fed people from his own money. Abd al-Wahhab wrote to al-Mansur about this, and al-Mansur replied with stern reproach, and ordered al-Hasan to feed people without having a herald. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Housing and fortification
Two lower rooms and two upper rooms were built for each company of the troops, with a stable above. A garrison was built thirty miles from it, and another on the river Qabaqib. Al-Mansur settled four thousand fighters from the Jazira there, increased the pay of each man by ten dinars, and gave them a subsidy of a hundred dinars. He stored weapons there, gave the troops farms as fiefs, and built the fortress of Qaludhiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Constantine came with more than a hundred thousand and camped at Jayhan; when he heard how many Arabs there were, he drew back from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
