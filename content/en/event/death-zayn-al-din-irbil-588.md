## Summary
Zayn al-Din Yusuf ibn Ali Begtegin, lord of Irbil, died. His brother Muzaffar al-Din Kukburi set out from Harran and took possession of it. He was among the most generous of kings and the closest of them to the scholars. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 199}
