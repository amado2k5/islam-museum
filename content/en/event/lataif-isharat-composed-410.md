## Summary
Before 410/1019 al-Qushayri composed his 'great tafsir' — the celebrated mystical commentary Lata'if al-isharat, which Ibn Khallikan counts among the finest Qur'an commentaries — and he held his ordered assemblies; among his other books are Nahj al-qulub, Ahkam al-sama', Adab al-sufiyya and Uyun al-ajwiba fi funun al-as'ila {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 172-174} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 564}.
