## نظرة عامة

Ibn Ishaq narrates that the Prophet called Rukana ibn Abd Yazid al-Muttalibi to Islam and asked «If I throw you, will you know that it is true?»; Rukana agreed and the Prophet threw him twice {cite:sira-ibn-ishaq-zakkar p. 276}; Ibn Hajar carries the report in al-Isaba with discussion of its chain {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba}.
