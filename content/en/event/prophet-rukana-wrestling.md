## Overview

The Prophet's wrestling of Rukana ibn Abd Yazid — the strongest wrestler of Quraysh — is among the most famous reports on bodily sport in the seerah: he wrestled him and threw him {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## In the hadith

In the report of Abu Dawud and others: Rukana ibn Abd Yazid wrestled the Prophet and the Prophet threw him {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}. In one narration: time after time — throwing him more than once, though Rukana was the strongest man of Quraysh. In the historical reports it happened at the outset of the da'wa, and its astonishment reached Rukana; it is told among the causes of his reconsideration {cite:isaba}. Rukana accepted Islam after the conquest of Mecca and holds a place among the Companions.

## Its meaning

Wrestling is an ancient sport, and here is the Prophet against the champion of Mecca. In another report he corrected the concept of strength: «the strong one is not he who throws people down; the strong is he who holds himself in anger» {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. Sport is skill and composure, not mere overpowering.
