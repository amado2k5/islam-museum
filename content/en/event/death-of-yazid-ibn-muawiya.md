## Overview
[[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Mu'awiya]] died in the middle of Rabi' al-Awwal 64 AH, while his army was besieging Ibn al-Zubayr in Mecca. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.345}

## The effect on the siege
al-Suyuti says that the news came while the fighting was still going on. Ibn al-Zubayr called out to the people of Syria that their "tyrant" had perished, so the army dispersed, humiliated. The description in the call is Ibn al-Zubayr's, not the museum's. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.345}

## al-Dhahabi's judgement
al-Suyuti quotes al-Dhahabi that when Yazid did to the people of Medina what he did, along with his drinking of wine and his committing of abominations, the people became hostile to him, and more than one rose against him, and God did not bless his life. This is al-Dhahabi's judgement as quoted by al-Suyuti. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.345}

## Dating
The Gregorian date (around November 683 CE) is derived from the Hijri date. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.345}
