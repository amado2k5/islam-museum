## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] set out for [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] until he was near it; [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] came out from Sana'a, from Shibam and from Zahr to meet him in a great army of horse and foot {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. The narrator puts al-Hadi's force at about five or six hundred foot and under a hundred horse, and the men's at about five hundred horse and two thousand foot {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. When they met, al-Hadi drew up and ordered his army: he put Abu al-Atahiya on the left with most of the horse, took the right himself with thirty Mudar horsemen, and put the Tabaris in the centre, having taken the Tabaris' horses to mount the Mudaris on them and ordered the Tabaris to dismount {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. When the men's armies came near, their horse charged the horse of Abu al-Atahiya on the left, which held its ground; seeing them make for Abu al-Atahiya, al-Hadi charged until he mingled with them and struck down men among them, their horse failed and turned in flight, and Abu al-Atahiya charged after them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. Al-Hadi pressed the charge, mingled with them with his companions and kept at them, driving and spearing; he killed their standard-bearer and other horsemen, and they fled, with Abu al-Atahiya's horse following them to a place called al-Marwa; they scattered in the valleys and ravines, and a number of the horsemen asked for safety {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. Al-Hadi's foot followed the men's foot and killed many of them, taking much weaponry, clothes and spoil, and al-Hadi rode back until he met his army, drew it up and entered Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}.

## Detail
The page names no field beyond the nearness of Sana'a. [[event/battle-of-al-rahba-288|The account of the battle of al-Rahba]] in the added first pages says Ibrahim ibn Khalaf ibn Tarif met al-Hadi at al-Rahba, that al-Hadi prevailed and that he entered Sana'a on a Friday in Rajab; the museum takes it as probably the same meeting told from another telling and does not assert it, since neither place nor month is given here. The two forces' numbers are the narrator's estimates. The narrator's way of explaining the few overcoming the many and his words of abuse for the opponents are not repeated and are attributed to him. It comes after [[event/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-comes-from-the-hijaz-and-al-daam-declines-to-march-288]] and leads to [[event/al-hadi-enters-sanaa-a-second-time-after-leaving-shibam-288]].
