## Summary
In the version that Ibn al-Athir finds most likely, the Second Fijar of the Ansar began when Amr ibn al-Nu'man al-Bayadi swore that he would not let water touch his head until his people had camped at the dwellings of Qurayza and al-Nadir, or had killed their hostages. The Jews refused to leave, on the advice of Ka'b ibn Asad, and the hostages were attacked and killed. Abd Allah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul opposed this. He and his companions freed those who were with them, among them Sulaym ibn Asad, grandfather of Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi. Qurayza and al-Nadir then allied with the Aws against the Khazraj. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 509}
