## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
In its entry on the Rif, The Oxford Dictionary of Islam calls the Rif Rebellion (1921 to 1926) an important anticolonial movement fighting the protectorate forces of Spain and France. It names as its leader Muhammad ibn Abd el-Krim al-Khattabi (died 1963), whom it calls an Islamic reformer. In 1921, it says, Rifian forces caused up to nineteen thousand casualties among the Spanish, the greatest single defeat of any European power in North African colonial history. The Rifian Republic of 1923 to 1926, it adds, put weight on modern self-determination and on applying Islamic law. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e2016}
