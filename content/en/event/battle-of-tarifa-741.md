## Overview
The battle of [[place/tarifa|Tarifa]], which Ibn Khaldun calls "the battle of Tarifa and the testing of the Muslims". In it the sultan of the Maghrib, [[person/abu-al-hasan-al-marini|Abu al-Hasan al-Marini]], and the sultan of al-Andalus, [[person/yusuf-i-nasrid|Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf]], met the army of the king of Castile, [[person/alfonso-xi-castile|Alfonso XI]], and it ended in the defeat of the Muslims. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1965-1966}

## Before the battle
Abu al-Hasan crossed with his fleet at the end of 740 AH and camped at the square of Tarifa. Abu al-Hajjaj came to him with the army of al-Andalus, and they surrounded the town and set up the siege engines. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1965-1966}

The Christian side sent out a fleet that blocked the strait and cut off the supplies to the camp, so the Muslims' stay lengthened and their provisions ran out. The lord of Lisbon of Portugal joined the king of Castile. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1965-1966}

## The battle
Ibn Khaldun relates that an ambush force entered Tarifa by night. When the fighting broke out the next day, it appeared on the backs of the Muslims, their line was broken, and they retreated. Women who defended themselves were killed, and the Christians reached the concubines of the sultan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1965-1966}

Ibn Khaldun describes the defeat as a "testing" of the Muslims, and mentions that the king of Castile grew strong by it over the frontiers of al-Andalus. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1965-1966}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri year.
