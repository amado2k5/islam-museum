## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that Abd al-Aziz ibn Abd Allah ibn Amir came as governor of [[place/sijistan|Sistan]] on behalf of al-Qabba', that is al-Harith ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Rabi'a al-Makhzumi, in the days of Ibn al-Zubayr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Fighting Rutbil
The people of the region admitted him to Zaranj, and they fought [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]]. [[person/abu-afraa-umayr|Abu Afra' Umayr al-Mazini]] killed him, and the polytheists were routed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Ibn Nashira
[[person/abdallah-ibn-nashira|Abd Allah ibn Nashira al-Tamimi]] sent to Abd al-Aziz asking him to take what was in the treasury and leave, and he did. Then Ibn Nashira entered Zaranj, and Waki' ibn Abi Sud al-Tamimi went over to Abd al-Aziz. Ibn Nashira gathered a force, and Abd al-Aziz fought him with Waki' beside him. Ibn Nashira's horse stumbled and he was killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
