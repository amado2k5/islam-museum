## Overview
In 161 AH the Byzantine commander Michael went out of the pass of al-Hadath with eighty thousand men, according to al-Baladhuri in "Futuh al-Buldan", and raided the depth of [[place/marash|Marash]]. [[person/isa-ibn-ali|Isa ibn Ali]] was there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 267}

## The raid
Al-Baladhuri relates from Ibn Sa'd, from al-Waqidi, that Michael killed, burned and took captive a multitude of Muslims, then reached the gate of the city of Marash. Isa's clients, and the people of the city and its fighters, came out to him shooting arrows; he withdrew before them until he drew them away from the city, then turned on them and killed eight of Isa's clients. The rest took refuge in the city and shut the gates; he besieged them and then withdrew until he camped at Jayhan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The response
News reached Thumama ibn al-Walid al-Absi, who was at Dabiq and had been put in charge of the summer raids in 161 AH. He sent a large body of horsemen against Michael, and they were destroyed except for those who escaped. This alarmed the caliph [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]], who prepared the army sent in the following year, 162 AH, under [[person/hasan-ibn-qahtaba|al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Date
161 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around 777 CE) that is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 267}
