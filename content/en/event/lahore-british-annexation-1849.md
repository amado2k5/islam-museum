## Overview
As a result of the second war with the British and the deposition of Maharaja Dalip Singh, Lahore was formally incorporated into the British empire in March 1849; under the two treaties of Lahore (March and December 1846) it had already accepted a temporary British garrison and a permanent British Resident {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 39}.
