## Overview
Ibn al-Jawzi taught as assistant to Abu Hakim al-Nahrawani at Bab al-Azad; when his shaykh died in 556/1161 he succeeded him as master of the Bab al-Azad and Ma'muniyya madrasas {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 783}.
