## Overview
Toledo was one of the first great Andalusian cities the Franks recovered, in 478 AH, from [[person/al-mamun-ibn-dhi-al-nun|al-Qadir bi-llah, son of Dhu al-Nun]]. Alfonso besieged it very hard, said to be for seven years. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.352-354}

## The date
The accounts differ on the day. In Ibn Khallikan it was Tuesday, the first of Safar; in Ibn Alqama, Wednesday the tenth of Muharram. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.353-354}

## The reports
Al-Maqqari reports from Ibn Bassam that wheat used to stay stored in the people of the city for fifty years. In the year of the taking it spoiled quickly. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.352-354}

The enemy made its people submit to his judgement, and Ibn Dhi al-Nun came out holding an astrolabe. The king began converting the mosque into a church in Rabi al-Awwal 496 AH. At its taking Ibn al-Assal composed verses urging the people of al-Andalus to leave. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.352-354, 447-448}

The battle of al-Zallaqa took place the following year. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.352-354}
