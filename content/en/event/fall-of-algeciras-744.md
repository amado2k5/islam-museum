## Overview
Al-Maqqari reports that the sultan [[person/abu-al-hasan-al-marini|Abu al-Hasan al-Marini]] equipped many fleets for the holy war in al-Andalus, and the Franks seized al-Jazira al-Khadra and many of those ships. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 389-390}

## The account
Al-Maqqari says that the Franks gathered large hosts to seize what remained of the Muslims' land. The people of al-Andalus called on Sultan Abu al-Hasan, who came himself to Ceuta. The Franks had come with uncountable ships, and they stopped him from crossing and from helping the people of al-Andalus, until they took al-Jazira al-Khadra and broke him in his ships. A letter he sent to the sultans of Egypt, Syria and the Hijaz makes this plain. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 389-390}
