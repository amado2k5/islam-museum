## Overview
After his return to [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stayed "some days", then word reached him that the group, that is [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]], "had gathered and taken counsel about marching on Khiwan to fight al-Hadi"; "al-Hadi did not deny it and did not believe it, because of the kindness, pardon and forgiveness he had shown them". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} The account is Zaydi; it follows [[event/al-hadi-pardons-banu-mamar-at-al-ghubayyib-and-returns-to-khiwan-286]].

## What was said about incitement
The narrator says "some people said that certain men in Khiwan had sent to them about it", offering them something of the tithe, sending them food and urging them to act against the imam, so that about three hundred men gathered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} This is said of unnamed people, and the narrator's description of their motives is his own judgement; the museum gives it as attributed and neither confirms nor denies it.

## The fifteenth of Sha'ban
"On Sunday, the fifteenth of Sha'ban, after the dawn prayer, the group appeared on a hill near Khiwan." The people took their arms and went to al-Hadi's house, and he looked from the hall in the domed room where he lived and saw the group shouting on the hill. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.137} Men kept breaking away to go at them and he ordered them back; they refused, so he told his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]] to ride out and turn the people back and leave the group where they were until the people came out to them, using a harsh word for the group that the museum does not repeat. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138} Abu al-Qasim rode "in a shirt and a cloak" until he had turned the people, who had already drawn near the group, and they returned to al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138}

## The exchange at the hill
When the group saw Abu al-Qasim had turned the people they came near him, and he called to the people of Khiwan; a party of them spoke with him and said: "Our clan, there is no war between us and you and we want only good for you, so take this man out from among you." A man of the town answered: "As for us, we shall not take him out, but if you want him out, come forward and take him out"; the next phrase reads something like "and that is mockery of him", and its sense is not clear. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138} From the context the speakers of the first words are the group on the hill and the answer comes from the townspeople.

## Al-Hadi's array and the fight
Al-Hadi heard of it, ordered the horses saddled, put on his arms as did Abu al-Qasim, had the people mount and rode out of the house, dividing the horse of [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] and their companions into right and left; with the right he put men of Khiwan, with the left men of Sana'a and others, while he took the Hamdani horse of Khiwan, some Khiwani horsemen and notables, the foot of Khiwan and of the Tabaris and a crowd of ordinary people, and went toward the group. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138} The group came down from the hill shooting arrows and throwing stones and the fight began; al-Hadi ordered the foot to close with them, then he and Abu al-Qasim charged until they were in their midst. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138} The group fled "in the ugliest rout, none turning back for another". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138}

## On the edge of the hill
Al-Hadi called the foot, who followed, and he dismounted on the edge of the hill "because horses had no room to work there" and fought on foot, the folio mark 27 verso falling here. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138} The fighting grew hard; a number of Tabaris and men of Khiwan were lightly wounded, a slave of one of the men of Khiwan was killed by an arrow, and "many wounds from al-Hadi's men reached them as they routed them". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.138} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} Some were caught and brought to him, and he forgave them and sent them back to their companions, and "they all left, routed and beaten". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139}

## Afterwards
Al-Hadi went back to his house and sent a crier among the people; on Tuesday a party of [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan|Banu Rabi'a]] came and stayed with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} The narrator then turns to the Friday address in [[event/abu-al-qasim-leads-the-friday-prayer-and-preaches-jihad-at-khiwan-286]].

## Notes
Sunday the fifteenth of Sha'ban is a Saturday in the tabular calendar, so the book's calendar runs a day ahead of the tabular as in earlier batches; the date is derived. On p. 138 the optical reading of the line on the fight at the edge of the hill is damaged, so the phrase "between them and al-Hadi" is left unglossed. No names are given of the dead or wounded beyond those described.
