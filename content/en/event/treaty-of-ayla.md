## Summary
When the Prophet reached Tabuk, Yuhanna ibn Ru'ba, lord of Ayla, came to him, made peace and paid the jizya; the people of Jarba' and Adhruh came and paid the jizya, and he wrote them a letter still in their hands — and to Yuhanna and the people of Ayla he wrote a letter of security whose text Ibn Ishaq preserves. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

## The account
When the Prophet reached Tabuk, Yuhanna ibn Ru'ba, lord of Ayla, came to him, made peace with the Prophet and paid him the jizya. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

The people of Jarba' and Adhruh came and paid him the jizya; he wrote them a letter, which is with them per Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

To Yuhanna ibn Ru'ba and the people of Ayla he wrote a letter of security — see [[work/covenant-of-ayla|the Messenger's letter to Yuhanna]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/169}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- The text gives neither the amount of the jizya nor its basis of reckoning; the museum has added no figure.
