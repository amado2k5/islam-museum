## Summary
In early Shawwal of 626 AH Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah besieged Akhlat until he took it by storm on Sunday 28 Jumada al-Ula of 627. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 484}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Jalal al-Din besieged the city of Akhlat — belonging to al-Malik al-Ashraf and held by his troops — from early Shawwal; the garrison held out aided by the townspeople, who feared Jalal al-Din for his ill conduct and gave themselves over to defiance and abuse, so stubbornness gripped him; he sat before them the whole winter, dispersing much of his army among nearby villages because of the intense cold and snow — Akhlat being one of the coldest and snowiest towns — while he showed a resolve and patience that astounded reason, setting several mangonels on the city whose stones breached part of the wall, which the townspeople rebuilt. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 484} He kept pressing them without let-up until late Jumada al-Ula of 627, then stormed it in successive assaults and took it by force on Sunday the twenty-eighth of Jumada al-Ula, as Ibn al-Athir narrates. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 484}
