## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Ziyad appointed [[person/mundhir-ibn-al-jarud|al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud al-Abdi]], whose kunya was Abu al-Ash'ath, over the frontier of India. He raided al-Buqan and al-Qiqan, and the Muslims were victorious and took booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Qusdar
Al-Mundhir sent raiding parties into the enemy's lands, and conquered [[place/qusdar|Qusdar]] and took captives there. [[person/sinan-ibn-salama|Sinan ibn Salama]] had conquered it before him, but its people broke their covenant, and Sinan died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source also gives a report that Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad appointed Sinan ibn Salama, and that Ibn Hurri was in charge of his raiding parties. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Afterwards
Al-Baladhuri says that the people of al-Buqan were Muslims in his time, and that Imran ibn Musa al-Barmaki built a city there called al-Bayda in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
