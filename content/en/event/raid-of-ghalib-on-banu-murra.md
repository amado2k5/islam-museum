## Overview
In the list of raids, Ibn Ishaq mentions the raid of [[person/ghalib-ibn-abdullah-al-laythi|Ghalib ibn Abd Allah al-Kalbi, of the Kalb of Layth]], on the land of the [[group/banu-murra|Banu Murra]], in which the Muslims took Mirdas ibn Nahik, an ally of the Banu Murra from the Huraqa of [[group/juhayna|Juhayna]], and he was killed by [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]] and a man of the Ansar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271} Ibn Ishaq gives no date for it, and no count or detail other than the account of Mirdas. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}

## Usama's account
Ibn Ishaq relates from [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]] that he and a man of the Ansar caught up with Mirdas, and when they raised their weapons against him he said: I testify that there is no god but God; they did not desist until they had killed him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271} When they came to the Prophet and told him, he said to Usama: who will stand for you against "there is no god but God"? Usama excused himself, saying he had said it only to save himself from being killed; the Prophet repeated: then who will stand for you with it, Usama? {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271} Usama says that the Prophet kept repeating it to him until he wished that his past Islam had never been, and that he had become a Muslim that day and had not killed him. Then he said: I covenant with God never to kill a man who says "there is no god but God." The Prophet said: will you say it after me, Usama? He said: after you. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}

## In the museum
This report is one of those most cited for the subject of [[concept/shahada|the testimony]] and the protection of blood by it. The Prophet did not accept Usama's reading of what the speaker inwardly meant, and made the word alone a barrier against killing. The collections of the Sahihs relate the incident through other chains, with the place and the killed man named differently (needs_source); Ibn Ishaq abbreviates it and places it in the context of Ghalib's raid. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}

## Marks of the report
- Ibn Hisham says that Mirdas's ancestry to "the Huraqa" of Juhayna is what Abu Ubayda told him; the editors of the Saqqa edition note that the wording is disordered in some copies. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}
- The report from Usama is given with the wording "and this was from his account about Usama ibn Zayd", without a continuous chain in Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/271}

## For young visitors
In one of the expeditions a man said "there is no god but God" before he was killed, but Usama did not believe him and killed him. The Prophet was grieved and asked him repeatedly: who will defend you on the Day of Resurrection when this man comes with his word? So Usama learned never to kill anyone who says this word.
