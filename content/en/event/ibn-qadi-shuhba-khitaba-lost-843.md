## Overview
On Thursday the tenth of Dhu al-Qa'da 843, Burhan al-Din Ibrahim ibn Taba'uf was installed in the preachership of the Umayyad Mosque of Damascus in place of Ibn Qadi Shuhba. {cite:suluk-maqrizi p. 1188-1189}

## Combined offices
Ibn Qadi Shuhba had until then combined the chief judgeship, the mosque's preachership and its muftiship. {cite:nujum-zahira 15/523}
