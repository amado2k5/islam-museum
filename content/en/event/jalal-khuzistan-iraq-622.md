## Summary
At the opening of 622 AH Jalal al-Din ibn Khwarazmshah reached Khuzistan and Iraq from India; he besieged Tustar while his troops scattered to plunder. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 443}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Jalal al-Din came to Khuzistan and Iraq at the start of this year from India, whither he had gone when the Tatars marched on Ghazna; finding no room to remain there he moved via Kirman to Isfahan — held by his brother Ghiyath al-Din — which his agents had already prepared for him and which he took; he then went on to Fars, where his brother had seized part of the land, restored what his brother had taken to its atabeg Sa'd, and made peace with him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 443} From there he moved into Khuzistan and besieged the city of Tustar in Muharram; its keeper and amir was Muzaffar al-Din, known as Wajh al-Sab', a mamluk of the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah, who guarded and defended it resolutely, while the Khwarazmian troops scattered plundering as far as Badaraya and Bakisaya and down toward Basra, where its police amir Miltikin struck them and killed a party; the siege lasted about two months before he raised it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 443}
