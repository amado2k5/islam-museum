## Overview
The men marched on Tuesday, the first of Rajab, to [[place/atwa-yemen|Atwa]], where [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] was; he brought out his army and drew it up, putting the Hamdanis who were with him on the right, the [[group/khawlan-tribe|Khawlan]] men on the left, and himself in the centre among the [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|Tabaris]] and the Alawites {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.247}. The narrator names the Alawites who were with him that day: his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]], his son Abu al-Qasim and his son Ahmad, Muhammad and Ali the sons of al-Hasan ibn al-Qasim, Ibrahim and Abdullah the sons of Muhammad ibn al-Qasim, Ali and al-Qasim the sons of Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan, Muhammad ibn al-Qasim and al-Hasan ibn Tahir {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.247} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248}. The book then says that these, all together, were with him that day of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib; the museum cannot tell which part of the list the description covers.

## Detail
The narrator goes on: when the men came near the left wing, it charged their right and drove it back and killed some of them, and their left advanced on al-Hadi's right, in which was a man of Khiwan called Muhammad Bahar; as they came close he signalled to them to advance, and they advanced and he gave way without fighting and left the place where he stood {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248}. This is the narrator's report of the man, so the museum does not judge his intention, and does not repeat the narrator's words against him. The men then climbed until they were behind al-Hadi and the companions who stood with him, while these drove back and killed those before them, until the men grew many against them, got behind them and cut them off from the road {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248}. What follows is in [[event/al-hadi-is-hemmed-in-and-the-tabaris-ask-him-to-leave-290|the encirclement of al-Hadi and the Tabaris' plea that he leave]], and what precedes in [[event/the-fights-near-bayt-hadir-and-the-withdrawal-by-matara-and-madar-to-atwa-290|the fights near Bayt Hadir and the withdrawal to Atwa]]. The Alawites named are not linked to records, since the book does not give their lines; the date is month precision because the book's Tuesday is a day later than the tabular count.
