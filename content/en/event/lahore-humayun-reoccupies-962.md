## Overview
After some fifteen years of Afghan rule under Shir Shah Suri and his successors, Humayun reoccupied Lahore in Rabi' II 962/February 1555, returning the city to Mughal rule and ushering in the era of its greatest prosperity {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 38}.
