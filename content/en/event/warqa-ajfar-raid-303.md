## Overview
In 303 Warqa' b. Nawfal fell on the Arabs in the Ajfar region, killing a band and capturing another, and brought them to Baghdad — where the mob fell on the prisoners and killed them, one man flogged at Bab al-'Amma. It was said he was lord of the fortress of al-Hajir and that the pilgrims had sought his protection {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 418}.
