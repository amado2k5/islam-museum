## Overview
According to al-Baladhuri, [[person/thabit-ibn-nuaym-al-judhami|Thabit ibn Nu'aym al-Judhami]] rebelled against Marwan ibn Muhammad, and went to [[person/musafir-al-qassab|Musafir al-Qassab]], who appointed him over Armenia and Azerbaijan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}

## The rising
Musafir came secretly to [[place/ardabil|Ardabil]], and some of the Shurat came out with him, and others who shared their view joined them in Bajarwan, Warthan and al-Baylaqan. Then they camped at Yunan. Marwan appointed Ishaq ibn Muslim over Armenia, and he fought Musafir without stop. Musafir was in the fortress of the dogs in Sisjan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}

## The end
In the caliphate of al-Saffah, Abu Ja'far [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] appointed the Jazira and Armenia and sent a commander from the people of Khurasan, who fought them until he prevailed and killed Musafir. He brought down the people of al-Baylaqan under safe conduct, and their leader was Dudd ibn Asfar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.294}

The date runs from about 126 AH to the caliphate of al-Saffah; it is a derived estimate and needs an additional source.
