## Summary
Al-Safadi came to Damascus as a young man of twenty and had his first meeting with Ibn Taymiyya in 717/1317 or 718/1318, displaying a store of knowledge he proudly and repeatedly recalls in his own works; he read with al-Shihab Mahmud, Ibn Sayyid al-Nas, Ibn Nubata and Abu Hayyan, and heard hadith at Damascus from al-Mizzi and Ibn Jama'a {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 vol. 2 p. 88}.
