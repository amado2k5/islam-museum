## Summary
In middle age al-Daraqutni journeyed to al-Sham and Egypt, hearing the qadi Abu al-Tahir al-Dhuhli and that generation {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.101}.

## His stay with Ibn Hinzaba in Egypt
The vizier Ja'far ibn al-Furat Ibn Hinzaba was dictating hadith in Egypt; al-Daraqutni went out to him, stayed with him a while and composed for him the Musnad, gained much wealth from his side, and transmitted reports from him in Kitab al-Madih {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.216}.
