## Overview
A battle at [[place/al-ubulla|al-Ubulla]] took place, according to al-Baladhuri, while [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] was marching to al-Hira in 12 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.337-338}

## The ruse
Khalid came to Basra, where [[person/suwayd-ibn-qutba|Suwayd ibn Qutba]] al-Dhuhli was, with a group of his people. Suwayd said that the people of al-Ubulla had gathered against him. Khalid proposed that he go out by day and return by night, and enter Suwayd's camp with his companions. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.337-338}

He did so, headed toward al-Hira, then turned back at night and entered the camp. The people of al-Ubulla came in the morning, having been told that Khalid had withdrawn, and when they saw the great number in the camp, they were broken. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.337-338}

## The outcome
When Khalid saw that terror had struck their hearts, he ordered an assault on them. They were routed and many were killed, and a group of them drowned in the Tigris of Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.337-338}

al-Baladhuri mentions a disagreement over the name of the governor of Basra: Suwayd ibn Qutba, or Qutba ibn Qatada. The Gregorian date is derived.
