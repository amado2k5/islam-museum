## Overview
When morning came [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] marched on [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], entered it, and the people put on well-being and were at ease; on the second day he went out to the mosque. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the sermon is summarised here as the narrator relays it, and the museum gives no verdict on it.

## The sermon
He climbed the minbar and preached a fluent sermon, praised God, invoked blessing on the Prophet, then spoke of time and its reverses on its people and that the outcome is for the God-fearing and victory from God for the believers. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114} Turning to the people of Khiwan he reproached them sharply: they had pledged and then broken it, covenanted and then violated it, sworn and then lied, promised and then failed to keep their word; and he listed what he charged them with, a leaning to the unjust, a preference for diversion, wine and instruments, and turning away from piety. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114} This is the imam's description of them as the narrator gives it; the museum neither confirms nor denies it.

He said that God's promise to His friends was fulfilled and recited a verse on the truth coming and falsehood vanishing, [[passage/quran-17-81|17:81]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114} Were it not for the faith in him, the habit of kindness, grace, forbearance, knowledge, noble stock, the strength of the community and the completeness of his proof, he said, he would have let punishment loose on them and freed the believers' hands against them until they learned the consequence of their treachery. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114}

## Pardon and warning
He then told them to praise God for His pardon and for overlooking their evil deed, and said as his uncle Yusuf said, "no blame on you today, God will forgive you, He is the most merciful of the merciful"; the editor's footnote gives the reference [[passage/quran-12-92|12:92]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114} He told them to look to their affairs and make up for their slip, since he would not excuse them again for any error or stumble. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114} He closed by referring to God's pardon of what is past and His vengeance on whoever returns, with footnote [[passage/quran-5-95|5:95]], and said he asked God's forgiveness for himself and for them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.114} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115}

## The oath and his return
He then had them swear by God to hear and obey, they swore, and he went back to his lodging. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115} See [[event/al-daam-gives-up-the-fight-and-leaves-khiwan-for-ghuraq-286]] and [[event/al-daam-swears-to-al-hadi-and-a-governor-is-placed-in-his-land-286]].
