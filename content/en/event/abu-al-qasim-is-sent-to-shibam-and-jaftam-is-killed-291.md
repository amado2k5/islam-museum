## Overview
When morning came they set out and the man set out with them until they reached Shibam and lodged there and stayed some days while letters passed between Abu al-Qasim and the two sons of Ya'fur, who promised him leave to go, until Jaftam was killed, after which they felt secure for some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.273}.

## Detail
The man is Ahmad ibn Abi al-Khayr, whom the two sons of Ya'fur said they would send with him in [[event/ibn-khalaf-flees-to-tihama-and-abu-al-qasim-enters-sanaa-with-the-sons-of-yafur-291|the report of the entry into Sana'a]]; the narrator does not name him here. The book does not say who killed Jaftam, nor where or how, and does not link it to the earlier seizing of him in [[event/jaftam-is-seized-at-arthal-and-jailed-at-bayt-bus-290|Arthal and his jailing at Bayt Bus]] and move to Zahr; that link is the museum's, probable only, because of the name. A note says the original has "we felt secure" where manuscript S has "we stayed", so the sense differs a little between the copies. The date is to the year. Shibam was left unidentified by the editor in earlier reports and is not linked to a place record. The report is in a Zaydi book in the voice of Muhammad ibn Sa'id.
