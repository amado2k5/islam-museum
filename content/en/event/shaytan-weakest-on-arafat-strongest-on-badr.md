## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Talha ibn Ubaydallah ibn Kariz that the Messenger of God said: the shaytan was never seen on a day smaller, more repelled, more despised and more enraged than on the Day of Arafat — all because of the descent of mercy and God's pardoning of great sins — save what he saw on the Day of Badr; asked what he saw, he said: he saw Jibril directing the angels {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-6-1269|Muwatta 6:1269]]. The muhaqqiq notes its isnad is mursal.

## The meaning
This report joins two sides: the shaytan's rout on Arafat through the descent of mercy and pardon of sins, and the terror of what he saw on Badr when Jibril directed the angels in the Muslims' aid; it is transmitted with a mursal isnad in the Muwatta {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
