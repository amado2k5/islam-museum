## Overview
Probably in 888/1483 al-Suyuti declared that he had reached the rank of mujtahid mutlaq muntasib, exercising ijtihad within the method of the Shafiʿi imam, and affirmed that his ijtihad extended beyond the revealed law to hadith and the Arabic language. The claim aroused a real fitna among his contemporaries, chief among them the traditionist Muhammad al-Sakhawi {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
