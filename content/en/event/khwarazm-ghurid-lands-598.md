## Summary
In this year Ghiyath al-Din Abu al-Fath Muhammad ibn Sam al-Ghuri, lord of Ghazna, died; the Ghurid state passed to his brother Shihab al-Din and their kingdom began to recede before Khwarazmshah. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 281}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir records that Ghiyath al-Din died in Jumada al-Ula of this year and his death was concealed; his brother Shihab al-Din was at Tus preparing to march on Khwarazmshah when word of his brother's death reached him, so he went to Herat and only on arrival there, in Rajab, held mourning and let the death be made public. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 281} Ghiyath al-Din had been lord of Ghazna and parts of Khurasan and other lands; he left children as Ibn al-Athir notes, and his brother Shihab al-Din took over his kingdom. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 281} In Ibn al-Athir's account this event marks the start of the shift of power in the east, for the war that then ran between Shihab al-Din and Khwarazmshah ate away the Ghurid lands until their end. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 281}
