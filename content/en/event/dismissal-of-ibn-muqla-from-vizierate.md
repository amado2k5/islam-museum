## Overview
In 318 AH Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Muqla was removed from the vizierate, and he was entrusted to be kept in the house and imprisoned in it. The caliph appointed Abu al-Qasim Sulayman ibn al-Hasan ibn Makhlad as vizier. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.130}
