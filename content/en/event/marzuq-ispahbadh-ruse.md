## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the people of [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]] broke their covenant in the caliphate of al-Mansur, and [[person/marzuq-abu-al-khasib|Marzuq Abu al-Khasib]] used a stratagem until the Muslims took the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The story
Al-Mansur sent [[person/khazim-ibn-khuzayma|Khazim ibn Khuzayma]] and [[person/rawh-ibn-hatim|Rawh ibn Hatim]], with Marzuq, his client, among them. When the affair dragged on, Marzuq asked them to beat him and shave his head and beard, and they did. He then fled to the ispahbadh and said that they had mistreated him, and he promised to show him the weaknesses of the Arabs. The ispahbadh clothed him, gave him gifts and trusted him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Marzuq wrote to the two commanders with what they needed, and contrived the opening of a gate until it was opened. The Muslims entered the city and spread through the land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
