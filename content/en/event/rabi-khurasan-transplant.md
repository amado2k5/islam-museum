## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan appointed [[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi ibn Ziyad al-Harithi]] over Khurasan in 51 AH, and transferred with him about fifty thousand people from the two garrison cities with their families. He settled them beyond the river. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Those transferred
Among them were [[person/burayda-ibn-al-husayb|Buraydah ibn al-Husayb al-Aslami]], who died in Merv in the days of Yazid ibn Mu'awiya. Also among them was [[person/abu-barza-al-aslami|Abu Barza al-Aslami]], who died in Merv. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source says that al-Rabi was the first to order the troops to ride in pairs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The death of al-Rabi
Al-Baladhuri says that when news reached him of the killing of Hujr ibn Adi al-Kindi, it grieved him and he called for death; he collapsed that day and died in 53 AH. He appointed his son [[person/abdallah-ibn-al-rabi|Abd Allah]] as his successor. Abd Allah fought the people of Amul, defeated them, then made peace with them, and returned to Merv, where he died two months later. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
