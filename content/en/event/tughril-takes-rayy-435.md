## Summary
In 435 AH Tughril Beg the Seljuq took Rayy. His army lost so many men there, through killing and plunder, that only about 3,000 people were left in the city {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 254}.

## The letter of al-Mawardi
Envoys of Tughril Beg came to Baghdad. The caliph al-Qadir bi-llah sent the judge al-Mawardi to him to censure what he had done in the lands and to order him to treat the subjects well. Tughril Beg received him and honoured him out of respect for the caliph's message {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 254}.
