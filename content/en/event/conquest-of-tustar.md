## Overview
The conquest of Tustar and capture of Hormuzan: Yazdegerd, safe at Marw, kept stinging the Persians over their lost kingdom until they and the people of Ahwaz bound themselves to mutual aid. 'Umar wrote Sa'd to send a dense force under al-Nu'man b. Muqarrin to sit opposite Hormuzan and learn his situation, and wrote Abu Musa to send another under Sahl b. 'Adi with al-Bara' b. Malik, Mujazzi'a b. Thawr and 'Arfaja b. Harthama — so fell Ramhurmuz, then Tustar, and Hormuzan was taken {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 367-368}.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Ammar marched with half the men of Kufa, and that al-Bara' ibn Malik on the right and Majza'a ibn Thawr on the left were both killed. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 130} He says a man of the city betrayed his people and showed the Muslims a way in through the channel of the Dujayl, after which two hundred men opened the gate. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 131} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 132} Al-Hurmuzan surrendered on the judgement of the commander of the faithful and was sent to Umar. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 132}
