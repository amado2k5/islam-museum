## Overview
The conquest of Tustar and capture of Hormuzan: Yazdegerd, safe at Marw, kept stinging the Persians over their lost kingdom until they and the people of Ahwaz bound themselves to mutual aid. 'Umar wrote Sa'd to send a dense force under al-Nu'man b. Muqarrin to sit opposite Hormuzan and learn his situation, and wrote Abu Musa to send another under Sahl b. 'Adi with al-Bara' b. Malik, Mujazzi'a b. Thawr and 'Arfaja b. Harthama — so fell Ramhurmuz, then Tustar, and Hormuzan was taken {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 367-368}.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Ammar marched with half the men of Kufa, and that al-Bara' ibn Malik on the right and Majza'a ibn Thawr on the left were both killed. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 130} He says a man of the city betrayed his people and showed the Muslims a way in through the channel of the Dujayl, after which two hundred men opened the gate. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 131} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 132} Al-Hurmuzan surrendered on the judgement of the commander of the faithful and was sent to Umar. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 132}

## In al-Baladhuri: the siege
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] marched to [[place/tustar|Tustar]] and asked Umar for reinforcement, and Umar sent [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]] with the people of Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Preparation
Abu Musa wrote to Umar asking for help, and Umar ordered Ammar to march with the people of Kufa. Ammar put [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]] in front. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The account names the commanders of the right wing, the left, the cavalry and the infantry of both armies, among them al-Bara' ibn Malik, Anas ibn Malik, al-Bara' ibn Azib, Hudhayfa and al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
The people of Tustar fought them fiercely. The people of Basra and Kufa charged until they reached the city's gate. [[person/al-bara-ibn-malik|Al-Bara' ibn Malik]] fought at the gate until he was killed, and [[person/hormuzan|Hormuzan]] entered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## In al-Baladhuri: the fall
The Muslims took [[place/tustar|Tustar]] under the command of [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]], and [[person/hormuzan|Hormuzan]] was taken captive and submitted to the judgement of Umar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The battle
Nine hundred of the people of Tustar were killed and six hundred were taken captive, and their necks were struck afterwards. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The entry
A man of the non-Arabs asked for safe conduct on condition that he show the Muslims the point of weakness. He became a Muslim and stipulated that a share be set for him and his sons. Abu Musa sent with him [[person/ashras-ibn-awf|Ashras ibn Awf]] of the Shayban, who waded with him across the Dujayl over a ridge of stones and showed him the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abu Musa chose forty men with Mujza'a ibn Thawr, and two hundred followed them by night. The guide brought them into the city, where they killed the guards and cried out "God is great" on the wall. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Hormuzan
Hormuzan fled to his fortress. Abu Musa crossed in the morning and took the city. Hormuzan asked for safe conduct, but Abu Musa would only give it on the judgement of Umar, and he accepted that. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
