## Overview
At Marj al-Rum west of Damascus: Abu 'Ubayda and Khalid marched from Fihl toward Hims and camped at Dhu'l-Kila'; Heraclius sent the patrician Theodorus to Marj al-Rum while the Roman Shans faced Abu 'Ubayda as reinforcement. When Theodorus struck for Damascus, Khalid shadowed him in a flying column; Yazid b. Abi Sufyan met him head-on and Khalid fell on his rear — none escaped but the fleetest, Theodorus was killed, and the Muslims took the spoils. Abu 'Ubayda then fought Shans at Marj al-Rum in a great slaughter that killed Shans too, pursued the survivors to Hims, and Heraclius withdrew to Edessa {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 321}.
