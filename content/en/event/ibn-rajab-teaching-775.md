## Overview
Ibn Rajab taught in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in the madrasas al-Hanbaliyya, al-Sharifiyya and al-'Umariyya al-Shaykhiyya, and in al-Turba al-'Izziyya, and he lived at al-Madrasa al-Sukkariyya at al-Qusa'in. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 934} {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 14}

## The preaching sessions
Ibn al-Imad writes: 'his sessions of counsel broke hearts and were blessed and beneficial to the public; every faction gathered around him and hearts inclined to him in love', and 'he knew nothing of people's affairs and never frequented the holders of office'. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 14}
