## Overview
The Qarmatians camped at Dhubaya, and As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur went out and fought them on a Tuesday in Muharram. He killed four hundred of them and then returned to Sanaa.

## Detail
Al-Yafi'i was living at Dhamar, and his men went out against the Qarmatians, and al-Yafi'i's men were routed, and his son gave safe conduct to Ibn al-Fadl. They then went toward al-Yafi'i, who fled with those with him to Sanaa, and Isa ibn al-Musman gave safe conduct to the Qarmatian and they marched toward Sanaa and camped at Dhubaya. As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur went out against them. The author says they numbered more than forty thousand. The battle was on a Tuesday, six nights gone from Muharram, and the author says the fighting was stubborn and four hundred men were killed among them. As'ad returned to Sanaa at the end of that day. The printed Tuesday for 6 Muharram 293 AH does not match the tabular calendar, which gives a Thursday (civil epoch) or a Wednesday (astronomical epoch); the record keeps the printed weekday and flags it. The figure of forty thousand is the author's own.
