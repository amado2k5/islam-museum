## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah al-Amiri, one of Banu Dhib, fought advancing and retreating until he was killed. He is a different man from Abu Ja'far, who has the same father's name. Then Jafar ibn Ahmad al-Hamdani charged the enemy, was pelted with arrows and stones, was cut off from his companions, and was killed.

## Detail
Then Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah al-Amiri, one of Banu Dhib, went out and kept fighting, advancing and retreating, until he was killed. The book keeps him apart from Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, the owner of the house. After him Jafar ibn Ahmad went out and charged the enemy. They pelted him with arrows and stones, cut him off from his companions, and killed him. The nisba of Jafar is printed in more than one form in the scan, which the record reads as Hamdani from the context, and this is flagged for the verifier.
