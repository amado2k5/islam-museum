## Overview
When [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] fled to the Mountain of [[place/al-ukhdud|al-Ukhdud]] they clung to it and threw away arms and clothes, and the people took them from every side. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} Then [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] pardoned those who turned their backs, the routed and the wounded, ordered the army to draw off and not follow them, turned the people back and stopped them climbing the mountain against them, and ordered the heads taken, so those by the road were taken and many more besides; then he ordered the village lanes shut and kept people from following. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} In a footnote the editor says Sifat Jazirat al-Arab (p. 67) puts al-Ukhdud in Khawlan, while Muntakhabat fi akhbar al-Yaman (p. 31) means the trench of Najran that Dhu Nuwas dug; the museum does not decide between them.

## To Rajla and Madhud
After the noon prayer he drew up his army and went out in order until he reached [[place/rajla-najran|Rajla]], at the foot of the wadi, the last village of Najran, and found the routed of Banu al-Harith lying under the palms with their women and mounts; they had brought the pack-bags and mounts to carry off the plunder of Najran, and when they saw him and he saw them they leapt to their camels and horses, mounted and fled. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} Al-Hadi picked a body from every thirty or forty riders, ordered the army to halt, and went on alongside them, drove by drive, until he had put them out of lower Najran and reached one of their waters called [[place/madhud-najran|Madhud]], then turned back, not wishing to kill them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} Their baggage, women and camels, driven by the women, met him; he let their road pass and ordered that no one should molest them, and no one did. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172}

## Thila
He went on to a fort of theirs called [[place/thila-najran|Thila]], around which were food stores, and ordered the army to plunder it and pull the fort down and burn it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.172} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.173} The figures and what was done are as the narrator tells them.
