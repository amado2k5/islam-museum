## Overview
After the sunset prayer at Baghdad the amir Abu Ahmad al-Muwaffaq came to Abu Dawud and said: three wishes — move to Basra and make it your home so that seekers of knowledge travel to you and it flourishes through you, for it lies ruined and deserted after the ordeal of the Zanj; transmit the Sunan to my sons; and hold them a separate session {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 361-362}.

## People are equal in knowledge
Abu Dawud answered: as for that, there is no way — people are equal in knowledge. So al-Muwaffaq's sons attended, sitting behind a curtain drawn over a sleeve, hearing with the common folk {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 362}, and Abu Dawud made Basra his home {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 133}.
