## Overview
[[person/al-wathiq-abu-dabus|Al-Wathiq Abu Dabus]], the last of the Almohad caliphs of the Banu Abd al-Mu'min, was killed in Muharram 668 AH in his war with the Banu Marin. {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 16}

## What Ibn Khallikan reports
The killing took place in a place three days' journey from Marrakesh. After it the Banu Abd al-Mu'min dynasty came to an end, and the Banu Marin, kings of Tlemcen, seized their kingdom. {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 16}
