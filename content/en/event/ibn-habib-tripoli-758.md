## Summary
His Durar al-Kamina entry places Ibn Habib at Tripoli in 758, in the course of his audition tours in search of hadith {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/30}; he had already heard from Ibrahim ibn Muslim, his father 'Umar and al-Fakhr ibn Khatib Birin, and heard at al-Hilla, Egypt and Alexandria {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/29}.
