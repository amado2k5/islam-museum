## Overview
Ibn Ishaq relates that two liars spoke in the time of the Prophet: [[person/musaylima|Musaylima ibn Habib]] in al-Yamama among the Banu Hanifa, and [[person/al-aswad-al-ansi|al-Aswad ibn Ka'b al-Ansi]] in Sana'a, and he relates the Prophet's dream about them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246}

## The dream
Ibn Ishaq relates from Yazid ibn Abd Allah ibn Qusayt, from [[person/ata-ibn-yasar|Ata ibn Yasar]] or his brother Sulayman ibn Yasar, from [[person/abu-said-al-khudri|Abu Sa'id al-Khudri]] that he heard the Prophet preaching to the people from his pulpit, saying: "O people, I saw the Night of Power and then it was made to be forgotten by me; and I saw two golden bracelets on my arm and I disliked them, and I blew on them and they flew off; I interpreted them as these two liars: the lord of Yemen and the lord of al-Yamama." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246}

## The saying about the impostors
Ibn Ishaq relates from one he does not doubt, from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]], that he heard the Prophet say: the Hour will not come until thirty impostors come out, all claiming to be prophets. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246}

## Marks of the report
- Doubt in the chain: "Ata ibn Yasar or his brother Sulayman ibn Yasar". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246}
- The dream is in the Sahihs in other wordings, as al-Mubarakfuri relates. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.452}
- "The two liars" is the wording of Ibn Ishaq and the tradition, not the museum's description. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246}
