In Safar of the fourth year of the Hijra, a party of Adal and al-Qara came to the Prophet claiming there was Islam among them, and asked him to send with them some of his Companions to teach them the religion and the Qur'an; he sent ten of the Companions with them, appointing [[person/asim-ibn-thabit|Asim ibn Thabit]] over them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When they reached the water of al-Raji' between Rabigh and Jidda, they betrayed them and called upon them a subtribe of Hudhayl called Banu Lihyan, who followed them with nearly a hundred archers until they surrounded them at Fadfad, offering them safety if they surrendered. Asim refused to come down into the protection of a polytheist and fought on with his companions until seven of them were killed and Asim was martyred. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Quraysh wanted to take something of Asim's body to identify him — he had killed great men of theirs at Badr — but God sent over him a cloud-like swarm of hornets that shielded him from their emissaries, so they could not cut off any of his flesh. Khubayb ibn Adi, Zayd ibn al-Dathina and Abdullah ibn Tariq were captured; Abdullah was killed on the road, and [[person/khubayb-ibn-adi|Khubayb]] and Zayd were sold in Mecca — Safwan ibn Umayya killed Zayd for his father, and Khubayb was crucified at al-Tan'im after establishing the practice of two prostrations before being killed in steadfastness. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
