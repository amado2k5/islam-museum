## Overview
Ibn Humayd asked for safe-conduct through Abdallah ibn Munir al-Muzawi and the two advisers. Muhammad granted it on condition that he stay in his house. Ibn Humayd then sought safety for his companions; Muhammad granted it to all except three men. Ali and al-Qasim had entered the village without their father's knowledge, and he sent them back to their post.

## Detail
When Abdallah ibn Munir al-Muzawi reached the Sirr of Banu Mazin, Ibn Humayd sent for him and asked him to seek safe-conduct for him from Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah. Muhammad ibn al-Haytham and Ahmad ibn al-Arbad went with him to Muhammad, and they asked on Ibn Humayd's behalf, and Muhammad granted him safety on condition that he remain in his house. The book says Ibn Humayd meant by this to divide Abu Ja'far from those gathered around him. Ibn Humayd then asked safety for his companions. Muhammad granted it to them all except Mansur ibn Hisham, Yazid ibn al-Aswad and Abu al-Nadr ibn al-Rabi' al-Madani, to whom, he said, no safe-conduct would ever be given; Ibn Humayd accepted this. Ali and al-Qasim then heard of the safe-conduct and of Ibn Humayd's return to his house, and thinking that matters had settled, went into the village without their father's knowledge. He rebuked them for going there without his leave and ordered them back to their post, and they went. Muhammad had written to al-Hadi asking for reinforcements when Ibn Humayd came to Najran, and he told his sons that reinforcements would surely come and be with them in the fort until they saw how matters turned.
