## Overview
Bayezid I built Bursa's Grand Mosque (Ulu Cami) in 1396-1399 CE, financed with the spoil of his victory over the crusader army at Nicopolis on 24 September 1396. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 42}

## The grandest mosque of Anatolia
Freely calls it the grandest of all the great mosques erected in Anatolia in the two centuries before the conquest of Constantinople in 1453; twelve great piers divide its vast interior into twenty domed bays, and its two minarets rise from the north corners, the first carrying an inscription naming Bayezid I. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 42}
