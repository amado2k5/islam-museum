## Overview
Chief qadi Taj al-Din Abd al-Wahhab ibn Taqi al-Din al-Subki died in Damascus on the night of Tuesday 7 Dhu al-Hijja 771 (c. 2 July 1370), having been stricken on the preceding Saturday night after the Friday sermon; the Encyclopaedia of Islam records that he died of plague soon after his release {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/426-427} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 745}.
