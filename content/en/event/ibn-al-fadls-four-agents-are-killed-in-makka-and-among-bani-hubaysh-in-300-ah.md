## Overview
Ibn al-Fadl sends two men to Makka and two to the land of Bani Hubaysh. All four are killed; two of them are flogged to death and crucified at Makka, in 300 AH.

## Detail
Ibn al-Fadl sends Muhammad ibn Dirham al-Jayshani and Hasan ibn Abi al-Mutalhif al-Sanamani to Makka. A man named Ajj ibn Hajj catches them, flogs them to death and crucifies them, and the author gives thanks for it. He also sends Abd al-Rahman ibn Dirham and Ibn Harun to the land of Bani Hubaysh, to stir corruption among its people; both are killed. The place is printed as Makka, and the passage does not make clear whether the holy city is meant.
