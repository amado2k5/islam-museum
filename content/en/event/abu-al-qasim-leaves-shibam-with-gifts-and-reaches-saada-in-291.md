## Overview
Abu al-Qasim wrote to the two sons of Ya'fur complaining of the length of his stay and his weariness of the place, and they sent him mounts, robes and a sword and dispatched letters excusing their delay, and he accepted their excuse and shared what they had sent among his servants and those others who were present; they wrote to Ali ibn al-Hasan al-Aqra'i to go out with him until he brought him where he wished {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.274}. They left Shibam until they reached [[place/al-ghayl-al-bawn-yemen|al-Ghayl]], where they met the agents of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] who were at al-Bawn; they sent al-Aqra'i back and went on with them until they came to [[place/raydah-yemen|Raydah]] and spent the night, then went on until they spent the night at [[place/warwar-yemen|Warwar]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.274}.

## Detail
Abu al-Qasim said: how did you see the dream I told you at Bayt Bus? Then they went on, with al-Da'am (the note has "Ibn al-Da'am") until they reached a place printed "Gharaq" and halted there, then went on to Sa'da, Ibn al-Da'am going with them until they reached [[place/saada|Sa'da]] in the first days of the year 291 AH, al-Hadi being at that time in Sa'da {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.274}. His words recall [[event/abu-al-qasim-tells-his-dream-of-the-sons-of-yafur-and-the-rising-of-the-prisons-291|the dream]] that had come true, as the narrator reports it, with no verdict in the museum's voice on the dream. Ali ibn al-Hasan al-Aqra'i, Ibn al-Da'am and the place "Gharaq" are left without records; "al-Ghayl" is read as printed and linked to the nearest record as probable only. It follows [[event/abu-al-qasim-lets-himself-down-from-shibam-and-is-brought-back-291|the attempt to leave Shibam]]. The report is in a Zaydi book in the voice of Muhammad ibn Sa'id.
