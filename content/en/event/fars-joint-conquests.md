## Overview
At the end of Umar's caliphate, [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] and [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]] came together and conquered Arrajan, Shiraz and Sinis in [[place/fars|the land of Fars]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Arrajan and Shiraz
They took Arrajan by treaty, on the poll tax and the land tax. They took Shiraz, which is of the land of Ardashir Khurra, on condition that its people be protected subjects who pay the tax, except those who wished to leave, and that they not be killed or enslaved. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sinis and others
They conquered Sinis and left its people to cultivate the land. Uthman took the fortress of Janaba by safe conduct, and also Jarra, Kazarun and al-Nubandajan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

[[person/harim-ibn-hayyan|Harim ibn Hayyan al-Abdi]] took the fortress of Shubayr by force, after a siege and fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
