## Overview
Jahangir made Lahore his capital from 1031/1623, and it continued as such for most of the reign of Shah Jahan, who was particularly attached to it as his birthplace, establishing a carpet manufactory there and renovating the dawlat-khana {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 38}.
