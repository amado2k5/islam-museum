## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] appointed [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi]] over Bahrain and Oman in 15 AH, and he sent an army to [[place/tana|Tana]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The raids
Uthman sent his brother [[person/al-hakam-ibn-abi-al-as|al-Hakam]] to Bahrain and went on himself to Oman. Then he sent an army to Tana, and wrote to Umar about it after his return. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He sent al-Hakam to Barus, and his other brother [[person/al-mughira-ibn-abi-al-as|al-Mughira]] to the creek of Daybul, where he met the enemy and was victorious. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Umar's reply
Umar wrote: "O brother of Thaqif, you have carried a bundle of sticks on a stick," and swore that if the army had been struck, he would have taken from his tribe the like of them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
