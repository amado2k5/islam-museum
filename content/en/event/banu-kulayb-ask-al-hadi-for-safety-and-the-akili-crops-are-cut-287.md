## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] went back to [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and stayed there the Monday; on the Tuesday he went out early to [[place/al-buqa-saada-yemen|al-Buqa]] and ordered the Akilis' sorghum uprooted, which a marginal note of the manuscript records as his order to pull up the crop, while the editor notes that the other manuscript reads the verb differently. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196} He then went on to [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196}

## Detail
Near it he found a party of Kulaybis with a chief called al-Zubayr ibn Muhammad; when they saw him they cried out to God, God, son of the Messenger of God, we hear and obey. Al-Hadi answered that if they were as they said they should come down to him and they would have safety. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196} Under a heading of the book, Banu Kulayb ask al-Hadi for safety, he granted it on condition that they remove the Akilis, who were on a mountain called al-Adana; the Kulaybis came down, he gave them safety, they asked it for their cousins and he gave it to them as well, and he went back to Sa'da. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.196} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.197} The man called al-Zubayr here is linked to [[person/al-zubayr-al-kulaybi]] on the name and the tribe alone. The report is the imam's follower's, with no verdict from the museum.
