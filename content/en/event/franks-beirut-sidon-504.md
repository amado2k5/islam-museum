## Summary
In 504 AH the Franks took Beirut by the sword, and then took Sidon on terms of safe conduct {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 487}.

## Context
These two conquests came after the Franks took Tripoli in 503 AH. The ports of the Syrian coast fell one city after another {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 486-487}.
