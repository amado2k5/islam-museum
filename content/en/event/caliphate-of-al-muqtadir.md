## Overview
In Dhu al-Qa'da 295 AH Ja'far ibn Ahmad al-Muqtadir was given the oath of the caliphate after the death of al-Muktafi bi-Llah. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.27}

al-Tabari reports that the treasury then held fifteen million dinars. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.28}
