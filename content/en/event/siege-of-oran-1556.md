## Summary
The Ottoman campaign against Oran in 963/1556, which did not take the city {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 243-244}.

## Preparation
After Ottoman intelligence uncovered the Sa'di-Spanish talks, Süleyman ordered Oran and Mers el-Kebir attacked and sent forty ships; Salih Reis thus had about seventy ships and forty thousand men {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 243}.

## The sailing and the siege
[[person/salih-rais|Salih Reis]] died of plague in Rajab 963, and the commander Yahya carried out his plan, sailing on Oran; en route came the sultanic order naming [[person/hasan-corso|Hasan Corso]] beylerbey. Oran was closely besieged but not taken, for Spain's continuous relief {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 243-244}.
