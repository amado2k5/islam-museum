When [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]] was twenty-five years old, [[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]] — a noble Qurayshi merchant — hired him for a trading journey to Syria with her wealth; her servant [[person/maysara|Maysara]] went with him and saw in him gentleness, noble character, sound judgement, truthful speech and trustworthy conduct, and reported what he saw to her. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Khadija confided what was in her heart to her friend [[person/nafisa-bint-munya|Nafisa bint Munya]], who went and sounded him out; when he answered with acceptance she spoke to his uncles, who asked for her hand from her uncle, and he paid her a dower of twenty young she-camels — she was then forty years old. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

She was the first woman the Messenger of God married, and he took no other wife while she lived; she was a home of safety and contentment for the whole of his life with her. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
