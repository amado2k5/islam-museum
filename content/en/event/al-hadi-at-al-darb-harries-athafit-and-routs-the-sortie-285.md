## Overview
After the story of the caravan {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.106} [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] kept to the [[place/al-darb-banu-rabia-yemen|al-Darb of Banu Rabi'a]] and sent a party at intervals of a few nights to [[place/athafit|Athafit]] to shoot at [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]]'s lodging, alarm him and keep him awake at night. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.106} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; its sayings are attributed to their narrators and the museum gives no verdict on them.

## The sending in mid Dhu al-Qa'da
In the middle of Dhu al-Qa'da al-Hadi ordered a group of Banu Sarim and Banu Rabi'a to go to Athafit to see who of his army was left with al-Da'am, since, says the narrator, that army had grown weary and tired. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.106} The narrator says it reached him that some of them told one another not to fight the son of the Messenger of God, and that the trial then carried them from words to fighting; some were hit by arrows and some of their horses died. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.106} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.107} He also heard someone report that al-Da'am used to call down evil on those who had pushed him into war with al-Hadi, meaning some companions with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.107} These are reports from unnamed people and are given as the narrator's.

## The sortie and the rout
When al-Hadi's party came near Athafit, al-Da'am's army saw them, thought them few, about a hundred men, and came out against them; al-Hadi's men withdrew to a mountain near Athafit, al-Da'am's followed, and al-Hadi's men set their backs to the mountain while the criers cried the news to al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.107} He sent a group of Banu Sa'd of [[group/khawlan-tribe|Khawlan]], who joined them in the fighting; when al-Da'am's men saw the reinforcement, says the narrator, God cast terror in their hearts after hard fighting, and al-Hadi's men routed them, to the point that the narrator heard of men throwing away their arms and their horses' armour. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.107} They drove them into the village until night came between them; al-Da'am's men entered the village wounded and al-Hadi's men went back safe and glad, in the account of a narrator who is on al-Hadi's side. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.107}

## Al-Da'am goes to Khiwan
In the morning al-Hadi sent criers among the people, promised a set day and resolved to camp against al-Da'am at Athafit; when this reached al-Da'am and he was sure of it, he left Athafit for [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] on the morning of a Wednesday with days of Dhu al-Qa'da remaining. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.107} See [[event/al-daam-leaves-athafit-for-khiwan-and-the-plunder-of-athafit-285]].
