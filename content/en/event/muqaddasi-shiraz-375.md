## Overview
At Shiraz in 375/985 al-Muqaddasi took the decision to compose [[work/ahsan-al-taqasim|Ahsan al-Taqasim]], at a time when, by his own account, he had passed — by how much? — the age of forty {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 544}.

## The project
Al-Muqaddasi intended to found a new science of geography describing the whole domain of Islam, surpassing his predecessors, useful to travellers, scholars and chiefs {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 544}.
