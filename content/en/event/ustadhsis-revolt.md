## Overview
In 150 AH Khurasani armies rose against obedience under the amir Ustadhsis, and he took most of Khurasan. [[person/al-mansur|Al-Mansur]] sent [[person/khazim-ibn-khuzayma|Khazim ibn Khuzayma]] to fight them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.425-426}

## The events
Al-Suyuti says that the number of the Khurasani army reached three hundred thousand fighters. Ajtham al-Marwazi faced them, was killed, and his army was plundered. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.425-426}

Khazim prepared a large army, and the two sides met in a famous battle, in which it is said seventy thousand were killed. Ustadhsis fled to a mountain. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.425-426}

## The end
In the following year Khazim ordered the captives, who numbered fourteen thousand, to be beheaded. Then they besieged Ustadhsis for a time, until he gave himself up; they shackled him and released his soldiers, who were thirty thousand. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.425-426}

All the numbers come from al-Suyuti's account, and the phrase "it is said" signals reserve about the number of the dead. The report needs an additional source.
