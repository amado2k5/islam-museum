## Summary
On 21 Muharram 625/1 January 1228 Yaqut began the final fair copy of the Mujam al-Buldan, destined for his patron Ibn al-Kifti's library {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}.

## He never reached Rusafa
He kept revising the work to the end of his life; in this last revision he never reached the article 'Rusafat Baghdad'. He had also composed al-Mushtarik wad'an wa-l-muftariq suq'an — a register of identically spelled toponyms naming different places — in 623/1226, reworking it until his death {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265-266}. In these years he lived in the khan outside Aleppo under al-Qifti's wing {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}.
