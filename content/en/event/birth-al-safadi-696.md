## Summary
Salah al-Din Khalil ibn Aybak al-Safadi was born at Safad, his family's home town, about 696/1297; his father, the amir Izz al-Din Aybak, was of Turkic origin and showed no concern for the boy's religious-legal studies until he turned twenty, whereupon the youth sought learning on his own {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 vol. 2 p. 88}.
