## Summary
At Nishapur al-Baghdadi studied under the master Abu Ishaq Ibrahim ibn Muhammad al-Isfarayini — the Ash'ari jurist and theologian whose school was famous — reading the principles of religion under him and mastering many fields, above all arithmetic {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 634}; he also heard Abu Amr ibn Nujayd, Abu Amr Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn Matar, Abu Bakr al-Isma'ili and Abu Ahmad ibn Adi {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 265} {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 238}; the date is approximate within his teacher's lifetime (d. 418).
