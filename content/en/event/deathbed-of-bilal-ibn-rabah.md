## Summary
[[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal ibn Rabah]], the Prophet's muezzin, went out to Syria as a fighter after the Prophet's death and remained there until he died. The books preserve his words as he was dying, and differ widely on the year of his death and the place of his grave: Damascus, Darayya or Aleppo. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 345}

## His departure for Syria
Ibn al-Athir reports that after the Prophet's death Bilal came to Abu Bakr and said he had heard the Prophet say that the best deed of a believer is striving in God's cause and that he wished to keep watch on the frontier until he died; Abu Bakr adjured him not to leave him, and he stayed until Abu Bakr died. He then said the same to Umar, who answered as Abu Bakr had, and he refused. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 130} In another report he told Umar that he had called the adhan for the Prophet until he died and then for Abu Bakr until he died, since Abu Bakr was his benefactor, and so left for Syria as a fighter; he called the adhan once for Umar when Umar entered Syria, and no day saw more weeping than that one. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 130} Ibn al-Athir adds that he settled at Darayya among Khawlan and, with his brother, sought a marriage into one of their households. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 130}

## His words at death
Ibn Asakir, in the abridgement of his history, reports from Sa'id ibn Abd al-Aziz that Bilal said as death came: tomorrow we meet the beloved ones, Muhammad and his party; his wife would cry out, alas, and he would say, rather, what joy. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-ibn-asakir-fikr-1995 scan vol. 1 p. 318} Al-Dhahabi gives the same words, reporting from al-Taymi, Ibn Ishaq and others before stating that he died in the year 20. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 345}

## What he left
I found no report in the books I read of any property or belongings left by Bilal. Ibn al-Athir quotes Abu Umar that he had a brother named Khalid and a sister named Ghufayra, and that Bilal left no descendants. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 131} Ibn al-Athir also names his wife as Hind al-Khawlaniyya. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 1592}

## His death
There are three views on the year: 20 for Ibn Ishaq and others and for Ibn Sa'd, Ibn Bukayr's 17 or 18 as the year of the plague of Amwas, and 21 in another report. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 104} His age was a little over sixty according to al-Waqidi, {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 131} and sixty-three according to al-Mada'ini. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-ibn-asakir-fikr-1995 scan vol. 1 p. 319} Al-Bukhari says he died in Syria in Umar's time. {cite:isaba scan vol. 1 p. 457}

## Grave and the differing reports
The books give four reports on his grave, each attributed to its narrator:
- Buried at Bab al-Saghir in Damascus: al-Waqidi and Ibn Sa'd, and Yahya ibn Bukayr. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 131} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-ibn-asakir-fikr-1995 scan vol. 1 p. 318}
- Buried at Bab Kaysan in Damascus: Ali ibn Abdallah al-Tamimi; Ibn Zayd says he was carried from Darayya and buried at Bab Kaysan. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 345}
- Died and buried at Darayya in the cemetery of Khawlan, as Abd al-Jabbar ibn Muhammad relates in his history of Darayya from a number of the people of Khawlan. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 345}
- Died at Aleppo and buried by the Bab al-Arba'in: Ali ibn Abd al-Rahman. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 131}
Marwan ibn Muhammad al-Tatari says he died at Darayya and was carried to be buried at Bab al-Saghir. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 345} In his entry on Bilal, Ibn Kathir calls the first view, burial at Bab al-Saghir, the sounder, {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 104} but elsewhere judges that the tomb at Bab al-Saghir called Bilal's is apparently that of Bilal ibn Abi al-Darda' and that the muezzin Bilal was buried at Darayya. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 5 p. 105} The abridger of Ibn Asakir's history likewise says that, after listing the views, the Darayya report appears the sounder. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-ibn-asakir-fikr-1995 scan vol. 1 p. 319} See also [[place/bab-al-saghir-cemetery|Bab al-Saghir Cemetery]], [[place/darayya|Darayya]] and [[place/aleppo|Aleppo]], and the account of [[event/deathbed-of-abu-al-darda|Abu al-Darda''s death]], which touches the confusion over his son Bilal's grave.
