## Summary
Rukn al-Din Sulayman ibn Qilij Arslan, lord of the lands of Rum, died on the sixth of Dhu al-Qa'da. His son Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw had ruled before him, and then the kingship returned to Rukn al-Din. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 292}
