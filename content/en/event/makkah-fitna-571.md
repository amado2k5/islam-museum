## Overview
Ibn al-Athir relates that in Dhu al-Hijja 571 AH a fierce war broke out in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] between the amir of the pilgrims, Tashtakin, and the amir of Mecca, [[person/mukaththir-ibn-isa|Mukaththir ibn Isa]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.77-78}

## The cause
The caliph had ordered the amir of the pilgrims to remove Mukaththir and appoint his brother [[person/dawud-ibn-isa|Dawud]] in his place, because Mukaththir had built a fortress on the mountain of Abu Qubays. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.77-78}

## The events
The pilgrims did not spend the night at Muzdalifa but passed through it, then camped at al-Abtah. Some people of Mecca came out and fought them, and a group on both sides were killed; then the raiders attacked Mecca. Mukaththir fled to his fortress and was besieged there, then he left it and departed from Mecca, and Dawud took command. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.77-78}

Many of the pilgrims were plundered, a great deal was taken from the property of resident merchants, and many houses were burned. Ibn al-Athir relates the story of a man who burned the house of orphans with a flask of naphtha; he was burned by his own naphtha and died three days later. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.77-78}
