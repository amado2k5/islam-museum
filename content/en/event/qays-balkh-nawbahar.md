## Overview
Ibn Amir appointed [[person/qays-ibn-al-haytham|Qays ibn al-Haytham al-Sulami]] over Khurasan, when the people of Badhghis, Herat, Bushanj and Balkh were on the verge of breaking their covenant. Qays marched to [[place/balkh|Balkh]] and had its Nawbahar ruined by [[person/ata-ibn-al-saib|Ata ibn al-Sa'ib]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The campaign
Al-Baladhuri says that Ata ibn al-Sa'ib, client of the Banu al-Layth, known as al-Khashl, was the one put in charge of ruining the Nawbahar. He made bridges over three rivers from Balkh at a distance of a league, and they were called the bridges of Ata. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then the people of Balkh asked for peace and a return to obedience, and Qays made peace with them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the truce
The source says that Qays came to Ibn Amir, who had him beaten a hundred times and imprisoned him. Ibn Amir put Abd Allah ibn Khazim in his place, and the people of Herat, Bushanj and Badhghis sent to him asking for safe conduct and peace; he made peace with them and carried money to Ibn Amir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
