## Overview
On Saturday, the first of Rajab 294 AH, the Qarmatians enter Sanaa and sack it, killing those in the houses of the Alawis and others. They hold the city for three years less eleven days.

## Detail
The book describes a sack in which the Qarmatians killed everyone found in the houses of the Alawis and others, and took large spoils. It says they held Sanaa and its districts for three years less eleven days, destroying the city, and that a sickness then killed a great number of them. Asad and Ibn Kayyala went to Qadam and Jarrah to Ghathr. The book's own Hijri dates are given as printed.
