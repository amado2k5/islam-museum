## Summary
The first military detachment the Prophet commissioned in [[place/medina|Medina]] was in Ramadan 1 AH (March 623 CE): he set his uncle [[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] over thirty men of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]] to intercept a Quraysh caravan coming from al-Sham. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 197}

## The march and the encounter
The Prophet bound for Hamza the first white banner in Islam, carried by [[person/kannaz-ibn-husayn|Kannaz ibn Husayn al-Ghanawi]]; they marched until they reached the seashore on the side of [[place/al-is|al-Is]], where they met Mecca's caravan — three hundred riders under [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl Amr ibn Hisham]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 197} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/83}

## The mediation and the outcome
The two sides drew up for battle, but [[person/majdi-ibn-amr-al-juhani|Majdi ibn Amr al-Juhani]] — an ally at peace with both parties — went between them, moving back and forth until he held them apart, made peace, and both sides departed without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 197} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/595}
