## Summary
The first military expedition the Prophet appointed from [[place/medina|Medina]] took place in Ramadan 1 AH (March 623 CE). He put his uncle [[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] in command of thirty men of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]] to intercept a Quraysh caravan coming from al-Sham. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197}

## The march and the encounter
The Prophet gave Hamza the first white banner in Islam, and it was carried by [[person/kannaz-ibn-husayn|Kannaz ibn Husayn al-Ghanawi]]. They marched until they reached the seashore on the side of [[place/al-is|al-Is]], where they met the Meccan caravan, with three hundred riders under [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl Amr ibn Hisham]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/83}

## The mediation and the outcome
The two sides lined up for battle. [[person/majdi-ibn-amr-al-juhani|Majdi ibn Amr al-Juhani]], an ally who was at peace with both sides, walked between them, going back and forth until he held them apart. Both sides withdrew without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/595}


## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.197–200)
In Ramadan of the second year the Prophet sent Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib with thirty men of the Muhajirun to intercept a Quraysh caravan in which Abu Jahl ibn Hisham was travelling with three hundred men. When the two parties faced each other, Majdi ibn Amr al-Juhani held them apart, and they did not fight. Hamza's banner was the first banner the Prophet tied. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.197}.
