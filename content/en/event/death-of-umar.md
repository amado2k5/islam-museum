## Overview
In 23 AH Abu Lu'lu'a, a slave of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba, stabbed the caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab in the market of Medina. Abu Lu'lu'a was a Christian, according to al-Tabari. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.190}

## After the stabbing
When Umar was struck, he asked his son Abd Allah who had killed him. He told him that it was Abu Lu'lu'a, a slave of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba. Umar praised God that his death had not come at the hands of a man who had prostrated to God in one prostration. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.192}

## al-Suyuti's account
al-Suyuti relates that Umar told his companions that the slave had threatened him before that, when Abu Lu'lu'a said that he would make him a mill that people would talk about. He relates that Umar left Mina, halted at al-Abtah, and prayed to God to take him without losing or neglecting, and that he was killed before the end of Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## Dating
al-Tabari's index places the account in the fourth volume, in 23 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

The speaker recounts that Abu Lu'lu'a, a Persian man, complained to Umar about a daily tax his master took from him, which he said was seven dirhams (needs_source). Umar asked him his trade; he said carpenter, smith and stonemason, and Umar judged that the tax was not much {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:43:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:43:30}.

He recounts that Umar heard Abu Lu'lu'a was making a wind-powered mill, and then heard that Abu Lu'lu'a had threatened to make him a mill that the whole world would talk about. Umar took the threat seriously {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:44:30}. He recalls that Abu Lu'lu'a used to pass Persian captives in the days of Qadisiyya (partly unclear) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:44:30}.

He recounts Umar's dream, in which someone pecked him three times, and then a foreign boy stabbed him three times at the dawn prayer while he was straightening the rows {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:45:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:45:30}.

He says the assailant killed nine worshippers and wounded thirteen before he was seized (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:46:00}. He recounts that Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf threw a cloak over the assailant, who then stabbed himself and died {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:46:30}.

He remarks that three of the four caliphs were killed, yet the creed and its ideas remained {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:47:00}. He says that as soon as Umar fell, he named the six men for the council {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep10 00:47:00}.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says Umar was stabbed in the year 23, three nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja, and lived three days, or seven by another account. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 152} Ma'dan ibn Talha's report has him killed on Wednesday, four nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 152} His age is given variously as sixty-three or fifty-odd; Suhayb led the prayer over him. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 152} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 153}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy relates that Abu Lu'lu'a complained to Umar about the master he worked for and Umar did not rule in his favour, and that Umar asked him about a windmill he was said to be able to make; then he stabbed Umar at the dawn prayer and wounded some twelve Companions, and when Umar was given milk it came out of his wound mixed with blood. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:04:00} He relates that Umar sent his son Abdullah to Aisha to ask leave to be buried beside his two companions, telling him to say 'Umar ibn al-Khattab' and not 'the Commander of the Faithful'; he found her weeping and she agreed, and after his burial she began wearing her head-covering in the chamber out of modesty before him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:07:30}
