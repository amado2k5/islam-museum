## Overview
The sources transmit a correspondence between [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah]] and [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] about the people of Khurasan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.393}

## The letter
Al-Jarrah wrote that the people of Khurasan had become badly governed, and that nothing would set them right but the sword and the whip, and asked for leave to do so. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.393}

## The reply
Umar replied that justice and right are what would set them right, and ordered him to spread them among them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.393}
