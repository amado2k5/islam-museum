## Summary
Three years after his resignation, al-Malik al-Aziz Muhammad ibn al-Zahir appointed al-Qifti to the same post over the diwan of finances, which he then held without interruption until 628/1230 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}.
