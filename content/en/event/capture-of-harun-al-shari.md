## Overview
In 283 AH al-Tabari mentions the account of Harun al-Shari and his capture. al-Mu'tadid marched out to Mosul in Muharram, and sent al-Husayn ibn Hamdan in pursuit of him. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.43}

The letter announcing his capture reached Baghdad in Rabi' al-Awwal. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.43}
