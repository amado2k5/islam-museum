## Overview
In 669/1270-71 al-Nawawi completed two works: the Minhaj al-Talibin, an abridgement of al-Rafiʿi's Muharrar, and the Rawda, an abridged commentary on al-Ghazali's Wajiz. With its two great commentaries, Ibn Hajar's Tuhfa and al-Ramli's Nihaya, the Minhaj came to be regarded from the tenth Islamic century as the supreme authority of the Shafiʿi school {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1096}."
