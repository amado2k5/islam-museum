## Summary
In the year five and ten and five hundred (515) the Dar al-Sultana in Baghdad burned, and property worth a million dinars was destroyed {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.527}.

## The year in Shadharat al-Dhahab
Ibn al-'Imad gives the fire first among the notices of year 515, before that year's obituaries {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.527}.

## For young visitors
The sultan's palace in Baghdad (Dar al-Saltana) burned down in 515 and the loss was reckoned at a million dinars.
