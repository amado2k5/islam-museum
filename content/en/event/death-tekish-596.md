## Summary
Ala al-Din Tekish ibn Il Arslan, Khwarazm-shah, died. He had ruled most of Khurasan, Isfahan, al-Jibal and the lands beyond them, until the lands of Persia (al-Ajam) submitted to him. After him, his son Ala al-Din Muhammad ruled. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 266}
