## Overview
On the first Friday after the death of the Prophet [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] stood on the pulpit and mentioned the verse of stoning, and said it was from the Book of God: "we recited it, understood it, and the Messenger of God stoned, and we stoned after him"; he feared that someone would say, "we do not find stoning in the Book of God", and they would go astray by abandoning an obligation God had sent down. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/308}

## The pledge
He then mentioned the allegiance to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and said that it had been a slip which God guarded against its evil, and that whoever gives allegiance to a man without the consultation of the Muslims, neither he nor the one who gave it has any allegiance; and that there is none among them to whom necks are turned like Abu Bakr. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/309} See [[event/saqifa|the portico]].

## Marks of the report
- The sermon is from Ibn Ishaq from al-Zuhri from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib from Abu Hurayra; in it "Do not turn away from your fathers, for it is disbelief in you" is given as among what was recited. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/308}
- The text of the verse is not in the report itself and is not given here; the museum reads the report as Ibn Ishaq relates it and sets out the disagreement over the ruling on stoning at its entrance (Tier A).
