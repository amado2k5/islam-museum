The Messenger of God was carried by night in his body — on the sound account — with Gabriel from the Sacred Mosque at Mecca to al-Aqsa Mosque at Jerusalem, riding al-Buraq; he alighted there and led the prophets in prayer, then was taken up through the highest heavens. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

In each heaven he saw of the prophets whom he saw, until he reached the Lote-Tree of the Boundary; God ordained on him and his community fifty prayers, and he kept returning to his Lord asking relief on Moses' counsel until they were made five prayers in performance and fifty in reward. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

When morning found him in Mecca he told Quraysh what he had seen; their denial and mockery grew fierce, and they asked him to describe Jerusalem — God unveiled it for him and he described it to them door by door and place by place, and told them of their caravan and the times of its arrival. Word flew to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], who said: if he said it, he has spoken true — and he was named that day "al-Siddiq". {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
