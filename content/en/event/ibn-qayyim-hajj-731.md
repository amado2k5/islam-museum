## Overview
Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya made the pilgrimage to Mecca in 731/1331-2; the Syrian caravan that left Damascus under the leadership of the amir Izz al-Din Aybak is said to have included a considerable number of jurisconsults and traditionists {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 854}.
