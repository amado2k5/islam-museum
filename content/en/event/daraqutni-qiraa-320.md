## Summary
Al-Daraqutni took the recitations from Abu Bakr Ibn Mujahid by sama', and read upon Abu Bakr al-Naqqash, 'Ali ibn Sa'id al-Qazzaz, Ahmad ibn Bawiyan and Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Dibaji, excelling in the qira'at {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.104}.

## His method in the treatise on qira'at
He wrote on the qira'at a short treatise gathering the fundamentals in chapters laid out at the head of the book, a method in which he was never preceded and which the readers after him adopted {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.102}.
