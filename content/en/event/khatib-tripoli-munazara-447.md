## Summary
On his way back from the hajj, about 446–447, he entered Tripoli and held a disputation in the art of preaching with the qadi al-Husayn ibn Bishr, holder of its Dar al-'Ilm, recorded by al-Karajaki in his rihla {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 93}.
