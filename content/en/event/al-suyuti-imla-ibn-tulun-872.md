## Overview
In 872/1467 al-Suyuti took up again the tradition of dictating hadith (imlaʿ) in the mosque of Ibn Tulun, where his father had been a preacher; the practice had lapsed some twenty years earlier at the death of Ibn Hajar al-ʿAsqalani {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
