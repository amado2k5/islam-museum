## Overview
Succeeding his father Abd al-Halim (d. 682/1284) in his post at the Sukkariyya madrasa, Ibn Taymiyya gave his first lesson there on 2 Muharram 683/21 March 1284; a year later, on 1 Safar 684/17 April 1285, he began teaching Qur'anic exegesis {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 983}.
