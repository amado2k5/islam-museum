## Overview
The battle of the Bridge, also called "the day of Qass al-Natif", was fought between the Muslims under [[person/abu-ubayd-ibn-masud|Abu Ubayd]] and the Persians under [[person/dhu-al-hajib-mardanshah|Dhu al-Hajib Mardanshah]], near [[place/baniqya|Baniqya]]. al-Baladhuri says that it was the last day of Ramadan 13 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.351-352}

## The course of the battle
Abu Ubayd ordered the bridge built, and the people of Baniqya helped him. Then the Muslims crossed and met Dhu al-Hajib with four thousand armoured men, with an elephant or more. They fought very fiercely and the Muslims received many wounds. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.351-352}

[[person/salit-ibn-qays|Sulayt ibn Qays]] reminded Abu Ubayd that he had forbidden him to cut the bridge, and he fought until he was killed. Then Abu Ubayd struck the elephant's trunk, and [[person/abu-muhjan-al-thaqafi|Abu Mihjan al-Thaqafi]] struck its leg, and Abu Ubayd was killed. It is said that the elephant knelt upon him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.351-352}

## After his death
His brother al-Hakam took the banner and was killed, then his son Jabr was killed. Then [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] took it for a while, and withdrew with the people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.351-352}

The details come from al-Baladhuri's account, and some of them need an additional source.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Umar gave Abu Ubayd ibn Mas'ud al-Thaqafi command of five thousand, that al-Muthanna advised him not to cross, and that the dead were Abu Ubayd, then his brother al-Hakam, then Salit ibn Qays with a group of the Ansar, while al-Muthanna held the rear. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 113} He dates it to a Saturday in Ramadan, 13, and says Umar wept at the news. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 114}
