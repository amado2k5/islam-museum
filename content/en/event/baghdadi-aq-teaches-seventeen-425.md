## Summary
Abu Mansur al-Baghdadi taught in seventeen fields, a man of dignity and means; Abu Uthman al-Sabuni said of him: he was among the imams of the principles and the leading men of Islam by the consensus of the people of merit {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 265} {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 238}; Abu Bakr al-Bayhaqi, Abd al-Ghaffar ibn Shiruya and Abu al-Qasim al-Qushayri transmitted from him {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 265}, al-Bayhaqi read jurisprudence under him and Nasir al-Marwazi read to him {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba pp. 634-635}; the date is approximate within his mastership after 418 and before his departure in 429.
