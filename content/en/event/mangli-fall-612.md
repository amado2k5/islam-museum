## Overview
Ibn al-Athir relates that in Jumada al-Ula 612 AH [[person/mangli|Mangli]], lord of Hamadhan, Isfahan and al-Rayy, was defeated and killed while fleeing. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.364-365}

## Background
Mangli had taken the lands and killed Ayatghamish, and the caliph's court found fault with him for it. The caliph urged Uzbek ibn al-Bahlawan, lord of Azerbaijan, to fight him, and sent an army whose commander was Muzaffar al-Din Sunqur. He ordered [[person/muzaffar-al-din-kokburi|Muzaffar al-Din Kokburi]], lord of Irbil, to be commander of all the armies. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.364-365}

## The battle
Mangli took refuge in the mountains, and the caliph's army ran short of supplies. Then he camped with his army, attacked Uzbek and defeated him. On the next day he came down with all his army, and they fought very fiercely; Mangli was routed, fled and was killed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.364-365}
