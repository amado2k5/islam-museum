## Overview
In this year occurred what is recorded of Abu Dharr: Mu'awiya's dispatching him from Syria to Medina without a saddle-seat, and 'Uthman's banishing him from Medina to al-Rabdha in the manner related {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 483}.
