## Overview
On 6 Safar 743/11 July 1342 Ibn Qayyim gave his inaugural lecture at the Sadriyya madrasa, where he was to teach until his death {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 854}. He taught at the Sadriyya and led prayer at the Jawziyya; he had read fiqh under al-Majd al-Harrani and Ibn Taymiyya and usul under al-Safi al-Hindi and Ibn Taymiyya {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/401}.
