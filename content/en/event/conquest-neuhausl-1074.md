## Overview
The grand vizier [[person/fazil-ahmed-koprulu|Ahmed Köprülü]] took the fortress of Neuhäusel, east of Vienna, on 25 Safar 1074 AH / 28 September 1663 CE. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.308}

## As al-Sallabi relates it
al-Sallabi says that he refused a truce with Austria and Venice and set out at the head of an army to fight Austria. He describes the fortress as the greatest fortress in Austria. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.308}
