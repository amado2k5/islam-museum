## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that when Abd Allah ibn Khazim al-Sulami was killed, his son [[person/musa-ibn-abdallah-ibn-khazim|Musa]], king of [[place/tirmidh|Tirmidh]], gave him protection and took him in with his followers. Then Musa drove the king out of it and took control of it, while in opposition. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After Musa
When Musa was killed, Tirmidh came into the hands of the governors. Then its people rebelled, and Qutayba ibn Muslim conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
