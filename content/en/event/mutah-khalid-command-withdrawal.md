## Overview
After the three commanders were killed at [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'tah]], [[person/thabit-ibn-aqram|Thabit ibn Aqram]] raised the banner and refused to take command. The Muslims then gathered around [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Khalid defended the army and then withdrew with them safely. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Two accounts
Ibn Hisham relates from al-Zuhri a different account, in which God gave them victory. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Al-Bukhari relates from Khalid his saying: "Nine swords broke in my hand on the day of Mu'tah, and there remained in my hand only a Yemeni blade." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4265}
