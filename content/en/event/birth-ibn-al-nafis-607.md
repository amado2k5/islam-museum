## Summary
The scholar 'Ala' al-Din Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Abi al-Hazm al-Qurashi al-Dimashqi, known as Ibn al-Nafis, was born at Damascus about 607/1210; al-A'lam records: "his origin was the village of Qurash (in Transoxiana) and he was born in Damascus" {cite:al-aalam vol. 5 p. 78}.

## The year of birth
No birth date survives, but he died in 687 AH aged about eighty — the Shadharat margin notes "he was eighty-four" — placing the birth about 603–607 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 401-402} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi s.a. 687 p. 312} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 897}.
