## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]] appointed [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] over Armenia, and he raided the [[group/khazars|Khazars]] and took the city of al-Bab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The vanguard
Maslama put [[person/said-al-harashi|Sa'id al-Harashi]] in command of his vanguard, with [[person/isha-ibn-muslim-al-uqayli|Ishaq ibn Muslim al-Uqayli]] and others. The Khazars uncovered Warthan and were defeated. Then a letter from Maslama reached him blaming him for fighting before his arrival; he was taken to Barda'a and imprisoned. Hisham later ordered his release. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Taking al-Bab
Maslama made peace with the people of Khayzan, and the lords of the mountains made terms with him. He went to the city of al-Bab, where in its fortress were a thousand Khazar households. He threw dung into the spring that fed their cistern, the water was spoiled, and they fled by night. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the conquest
Maslama settled twenty-four thousand people from Syria on regular pay in al-Bab and al-Abwab. He built granaries for food and barley and a store for weapons. [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad]] was with him and fought the Khazars. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date is approximate and derived.
