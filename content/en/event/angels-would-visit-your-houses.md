## The story
Al-Tirmidhi relates marfu' from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet told that God said: the prayer of the wronged is answered, and by My might I will surely aid you even after a while; and had you remained in that state of yours when you left Me, the angels would have visited you in your houses; and had you not sinned Allah would have brought a new creation to sin so that He could forgive them {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} [[passage/tirmidhi-2526|Tirmidhi 2526]].

## The meaning
In this hadith qudsi the angels visit houses whose people are upon obedience, and the servant's sins turn them away from descending {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
