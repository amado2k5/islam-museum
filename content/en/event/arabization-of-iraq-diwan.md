## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates from al-Mada'ini that the diwan of the kharaj of al-Sawad and the rest of Iraq remained in Persian until [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] became governor of Iraq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.421}

## The two secretaries
al-Hajjaj's secretary was Zadhan Farrukh ibn Biri. With him was [[person/salih-ibn-abd-al-rahman|Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman]], a client of Banu Tamim, who wrote in Arabic and Persian. Salih offered to convert the accounts into Arabic, and he converted part of them. Then Zadhan Farrukh was killed in the days of Ibn al-Ash'ath, and al-Hajjaj appointed Salih as secretary and charged him with transferring the diwan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.421}

## The transfer
Mardanshah ibn Zadhan Farrukh objected by asking how some Persian terms should be written, and Salih answered as best he could. al-Baladhuri says that a hundred thousand dirhams were offered to him to feign incapacity and stop the transfer, but he refused. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.421}

Abd al-Hamid ibn Yahya, secretary of Marwan ibn Muhammad, used to say of Salih that he had great merit over the secretaries. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.421}

The event took place in [[place/kufa|Kufa]], according to the data. Zadhan Farrukh is named in the data as [[person/zadhanfarrukh-ibn-biri|Zadhan Farrukh ibn Biri]].
