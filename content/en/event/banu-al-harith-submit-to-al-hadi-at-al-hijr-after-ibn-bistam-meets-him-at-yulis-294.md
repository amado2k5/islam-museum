## Overview
Al-Hadi met Abdallah ibn Bistam at Yulis; then Banu al-Harith came to him in submission, except al-Harith ibn Humayd and the Hamasiyin.

## Detail
At a place called Yulis, al-Hadi met Abdallah ibn Bistam al-Harithi of Banu Rabi'a. Then Banu al-Harith came to him in submission and obedience, except al-Harith ibn Humayd and the Hamasiyin, whose dislike of the imam had come from his earlier mediation between them and the Ya'am. Al-Hadi ordered Wada'a to muster for the war against the Ya'am and to call their Bedouin kin to them. The nisba al-Harithi is read as printed; the Yulis place is not identified on a map.
