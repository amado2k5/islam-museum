## Overview
Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya attached himself to his teacher Ibn Taymiyya from the latter's return to Damascus from Egypt in 712 until Ibn Taymiyya's death, so devoted to him that he never departed from any of his positions and defended them all; it was he who refined his master's books and spread his learning {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/401}. F. de la Granja gives 713/1313 as the start of a discipleship in which Ibn Qayyim absorbed all of Ibn Taymiyya's ideas and helped popularize his work {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 854}.
