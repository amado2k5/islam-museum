## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] came to [[place/rayy|Rayy]] in the caliphate of his father al-Mansur, and built the city in which people live today, and named it al-Muhammadiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The building
He put a ditch around it, and built a congregational mosque, carried out by the hand of [[person/ammar-ibn-abi-al-khasib|Ammar ibn Abi al-Khasib]], and his name was written on its wall. He dated the building to 158 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He surrounded it with a wall of fired brick. The people of Rayy call the inner city and the outer wall the outer city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fortress of al-Zinbadi
The fortress of al-Zinbadi was within al-Muhammadiyya, and al-Mahdi ordered it repaired and lodged in it; it overlooks the congregational mosque and the house of government. At his first arrival al-Mahdi had lodged in a village called al-Siruwan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
