## Summary
In 664 AH al-Malik al-Zahir [[person/baybars|Baybars]] raided, sending his armies along the coasts. They raided the lands of Acre, Tyre, Tripoli and the fortress of the Kurds. He then camped before Safed on the eighth of Ramadan. It was taken after forty days by trickery, and two hundred of its horsemen were beheaded. Many Muslims were martyred in the taking of it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.546}.
