## Overview
In his early thirties Ibn Hajar completed in 804/1401-2 the draft of Ta'liq al-Ta'liq, a work on the isnads of al-Bukhari's Sahih, publishing it three years later; it securely established his scholarly reputation and was the first of his works on al-Bukhari to win fame. His earliest recorded publications — a paper on prosody and a laudatory notice for al-Damamini's book — had appeared in 795 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 810}.
