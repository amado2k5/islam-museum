## Overview
A fire broke out in al-Aqsa Mosque on the morning of Thursday 21 August 1969, destroying its library and eastern side — an act the book calls a crime attributed to Israel. The Islamic Research Academy held an emergency session on 23 August 1969, issuing a statement and resolutions calling for Muslim action and for the United Nations to implement its resolutions on Jerusalem {cite:bayt-al-maqdis-fi-al-islam-azhar p. 79, 102-104}.
