## Summary
In 541 AH the Franks took Tripoli of the Maghrib by the sword, and then settled it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 608}.

## Growing Norman influence
In 543 AH men of the ruler of Sicily took advantage of a severe famine in Ifriqiya. They came with 250 ships. The ruler of Mahdiya fled, and they took the city without a blow or a thrust. From Tripoli of the Maghrib to near Tunis, the lands passed to the Franks {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 615}.
