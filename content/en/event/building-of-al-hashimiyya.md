## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates the story of the city of al-Hashimiyya at [[place/kufa|Kufa]], and how its building passed between [[person/yazid-ibn-umar-ibn-hubayra|Yazid ibn Umar ibn Hubayra]] and the two caliphs al-Saffah and al-Mansur. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.403}

## From Ibn Hubayra to al-Saffah
Yazid ibn Umar ibn Hubayra built a city on the Euphrates and settled in it, but part of it was left unfinished. Then a letter came to him from Marwan ordering him to avoid living next to the people of Kufa, and he left it. When [[person/abu-al-abbas-al-saffah|Abu al-Abbas]] came to power, he settled there, completed its palaces and named it al-Hashimiyya. People used to attribute it to Ibn Hubayra, so he abandoned it and built a new city opposite it, which he called al-Hashimiyya, and then chose al-Anbar and built his city there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.403}

## al-Mansur
When [[person/al-mansur|Abu Ja'far al-Mansur]] became caliph, he settled in al-Hashimiyya at Kufa, completed what remained of it and added to it. Then he moved to Baghdad, built his city and named it the City of Peace. He imprisoned [[person/abdallah-ibn-al-hasan|Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan]] in al-Hashimiyya, because of his two sons Muhammad and Ibrahim, and his grave is there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.403}
