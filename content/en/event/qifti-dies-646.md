## Summary
The vizier al-Qadi al-Akram Jamal al-Din Ali ibn Yusuf al-Shaybani al-Qifti died in Ramadan 646/1248-9 {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 326} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 236}; he had assembled a library no one else had matched, kept neither house nor wife, and bequeathed his books — worth fifty thousand dinars — to al-Malik al-Nasir of Aleppo {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 325}.
