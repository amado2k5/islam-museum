## Overview
In Shawwal 247 AH Muhammad ibn Ja'far al-Muntasir was given the oath of the caliphate, and then moved from al-Ja'fariyya to Samarra. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.234} al-Tabari gives his death in 248 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.251}

## al-Suyuti's account
He was given the oath after his father was killed, and he deposed his two brothers al-Mu'tazz and al-Mu'ayyad from the succession. al-Suyuti says that he showed justice, removed fear from the family of Abu Talib, and restored Fadak to the family of al-Husayn. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.553-554}

He used to revile the Turks and say that they were the killers of caliphs, so they plotted against him. They sent his physician thirty thousand dinars, and he bled him with a poisoned feather. He died on the fifth of Rabi' al-Akhir 248 AH, aged twenty-six or less. He enjoyed the caliphate for less than six months. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.553-554}

al-Suyuti quotes al-Tha'alibi that al-Muntasir and his five forefathers had been caliphs. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.553-554}
