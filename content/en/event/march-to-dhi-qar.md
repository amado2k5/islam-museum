## Summary
After [[person/talha-ibn-ubaydullah|Talha]], [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr]], and [[person/aisha|A'isha]] set out for Basra in the year 36 AH, [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] marched out of Medina toward Kufa and encamped at [[place/dhi-qar|Dhu Qar]] to mobilize the people. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 45} {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 47}

## Reluctance of the People of Medina
Al-Sallabi mentions that many of the people of Medina held back from marching out because they saw that the turmoil had not ended and because the unruly elements in Ali's army were well known to them. Ali delivered a speech on this, complaining of their reluctance. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 45}

## The Army on the March
Al-Tabari narrates, as cited by al-Sallabi, that Ali set out with those who were eager among the Kufans and Basrans numbering seven hundred men, and the author considers this figure closer to the reality of the initial stage. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 45} Then the gathering at Dhu Qar reached larger numbers in the reports, and the author's closest estimate is around twelve thousand at the time of the battle. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 48}

## The Advice of Ibn Salam and al-Hasan
[[person/abdullah-ibn-salam|Abd Allah ibn Salam]] advised Ali not to go to Iraq, and al-Hasan told him the same at al-Rabadha. Ali said that the Prophet had informed him of this beforehand, and he forbade those of his companions who wanted to kill Ibn Salam, saying that he was a righteous man. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 46} {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 46} {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 47}

## Sources and Limitations
All figures are from al-Sallabi's estimates and his citations from al-Tabari and others, and the number of troops has not been independently calculated in this entry. {cite:sallabi-ali-vol2 p. 48}
