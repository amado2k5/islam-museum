## Summary
In 483 AH a great sectarian strife broke out in Baghdad between Sunnis and Rafida (Shi'a). Many were killed, and the city's governor could not control it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}.

## How it ended
The strife ended with the Sunnis gaining the upper hand through their greater numbers and the caliph's helpers. The Shi'a were subdued and practised taqiyya. They accepted that the mosques of al-Karkh bore the inscription: "The best of people after the Messenger of God is Abu Bakr" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}.

## Context
The strife belongs to a long series of sectarian events that Baghdad saw under the Buyid and Seljuq states, including the event of 422 recorded in this museum {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}.
