## Overview
The fighting with the Zanj continued from the accession of al-Mu'tamid in 256 AH until 270 AH, and in that year their leader, the [[person/sahib-al-zanj|Sahib al-Zanj]], was killed. Al-Suyuti says that he claimed he had been sent to mankind and that he knew the unseen. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 564-565}

## The head enters Baghdad
Al-Suyuti says that his head entered Baghdad on a spear, decorative domes were made, the people cried out prayers for [[person/abu-ahmad-al-muwaffaq|al-Muwaffaq]], and poets praised him. The people were reassured and returned to the cities the Zanj had taken, among them Wasit and Ramhurmuz. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 564-565}

## Date
Al-Mas'udi dates his killing to the night of Saturday, two nights into Safar 270 AH in the caliphate of al-Mu'tamid. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 568}

## Ibn al-Jawzi's account
Ibn al-Jawzi says that al-Muwaffaq urged the war against him and encouraged people to jihad, and a group of volunteers joined him. The army crossed on Monday, three days before the end of Muharram, and the Zanj were routed; their city was taken and those held captive in it were freed. Then the messenger came with his head, and the people prostrated in thanks. Ibn al-Muwaffaq, Abu al-Abbas, reached Baghdad with the head on a lance, and rode with a great army to the palace of the caliphate. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.5 p. 74-75}
