## Summary
In Rabi' I of year 400 the Tigris fell to a low never before known; islands appeared in it that had not existed, and boats could not sail it from Anah and al-Rashidiyya on the upper reaches {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.245}.

## The dredging and its strangeness
Ibn al-Jawzi says men were sent to dredge that spot, "and the dredging of the Tigris was found a marvel and wondered at, for the Tigris had never been dredged before this year" {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.245}.

## For young visitors
In year 400 the Tigris ran so low that new islands showed and ships stopped moving, so workers were sent to dig out the riverbed — the first time anyone remembered the Tigris being dredged.
