## Overview
Mulahiz dies at Zabid in early Rabi I 303 AH, and the city passes to Ibn Abi al-Faraat and then to al-Harmali.

## Detail
Mulahiz dies at Zabid in early Rabi I 303 AH. Abd Allah ibn Abi al-Faraat holds the city for fifty days. Then Ibrahim ibn Muhammad al-Harmali, one of the Sultan's commanders who served with Mulahiz, takes command and stays at Zabid, and in late Rabi II of the same year he withdraws to Ibn Abi al-Faraat in the latter's own town. The sentence is kept as printed.
