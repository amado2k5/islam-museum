## Overview
[[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] died at Dayr Simʿan, in the lands of Homs, in Rajab 101 AH, aged thirty-nine and a half years. The day is disputed between the tenth and the fifth from the end of the month. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.397}

## Death by poison
al-Suyuti says that his death was by poison. The reason, in his account, is that the Umayyads grew hostile to him when he pressed them hard and took from their hands much of what they had seized, and that he neglected to take precautions. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.397-398}

In a report that he relates, he asked Mujahid what people were saying of him, and Mujahid said that they said he was bewitched. Umar answered that he was not bewitched, and that he knew the hour in which he had been given the drink. Then he called his servant, who confessed that he had taken a thousand dinars on condition that he give him the drink. Umar put the money into the treasury and let the servant go where no one would see him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.397-398}

## Dating
The corresponding Gregorian date (around February 720 CE) is derived from the Hijri date and is not stated in the source. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.397}
