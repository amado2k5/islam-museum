## Overview
About 200/815-6 comes the first proof of al-Jahiz's literary activity: several works of his on the imamate — a very characteristic subject of the age — won the compliments of al-Ma'mun and opened the door of the caliphal capital to him {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 385}.

## From Basra to Baghdad and Samarra
From then on, without abandoning Basra, al-Jahiz stayed for long periods in Baghdad and later Samarra, devoting himself to literary work of which an appreciable part has survived. In Baghdad he discharged the functions of scribe for three days and was very briefly assistant to Ibrahim ibn al-Abbas al-Suli at the Chancellery {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 385}.
