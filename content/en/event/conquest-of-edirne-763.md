## Overview
Edirne surrendered to the Ottomans in Ramadan 763/July 1362 on condition of free residence, after Lala Shahin Pasha defeated its tekfür at Sazlı-Dere {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 710}.

## From conquest to capital
The city was the first important staging point on the main road from Asia Minor to the Balkans, and it became at once the forward base of Ottoman expansion in Europe {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 710}. Al-Salabi describes it as the second city of the Byzantine Empire after Constantinople {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 57}.
