## Overview
Al-Baladhuri, in "Futuh al-Buldan", reports that Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik appointed [[person/habib-ibn-al-muhallab|Habib ibn al-Muhallab]] over the war in Sind. The kings of India had returned to their kingdoms, among them [[person/habsha-ibn-dahir|Habsha ibn Dahir]] in Brahmanabad. Habib came down on the shore of [[place/mihran|Mihran]], and its people gave him obedience. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Calling the kings
In the caliphate of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] he wrote to the kings inviting them to Islam and obedience, on the terms that they remain kings with what the Muslims have and bearing what they bear. His conduct had reached them, so Habsha and the kings became Muslims and took Arab names. [[person/amr-ibn-muslim|Amr ibn Muslim al-Bahili]] was his governor over the frontier, and he raided part of India and was victorious. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The Muhallabids
In the days of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik the Banu al-Muhallab fled to Sind, and he sent [[person/hilal-ibn-ahwaz|Hilal ibn Ahwaz al-Tamimi]] after them. Mudrik ibn al-Muhallab was killed at Tandabil, and al-Mufaddal, Abd al-Malik, Ziyad, Marwan and Mu'awiya, the sons of al-Muhallab, were killed, and Mu'awiya ibn Yazid among others. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
