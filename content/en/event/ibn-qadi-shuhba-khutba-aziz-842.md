## Overview
When Amir Inal al-Hakimi, governor of Damascus, broke into rebellion and openly restored the name of the deposed sultan al-Malik al-Aziz Yusuf ibn Barsbay, the chief judge Taqi al-Din Ibn Qadi Shuhba invoked al-Aziz from the minbar of the Umayyad Mosque on a Friday — while the khutba in the citadel remained for Sultan al-Zahir Jaqmaq. {cite:suluk-maqrizi p. 1112}

## Jaqmaq's reckoning
Jaqmaq held that sermon against him after the Jakami affair, and it was a cause of his later dismissal. {cite:nujum-zahira 15/523}
