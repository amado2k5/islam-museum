## Summary
The army of the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah was defeated by Sultan Tughril ibn Arslan. Al-Nasir had favoured Tughril and strengthened his position by granting him the atabegate, until Tughril came to rule Rayy, Hamadan, Isfahan and Azerbaijan {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.178}.
