## Overview
Fighting breaks out between the people of Musur and the Qudmiyyin, and the Qarmatians return to Jabal Musur in Rabi I 304 AH after Ahmad ibn Yahya's commander pushes them back.

## Detail
A fierce fight breaks out between the people of Musur and the Qudmiyyin; the Kalabih quarter is entered, burned and plundered. The people of Musur call on Ahmad ibn Yahya, who sends them a commander with an army. They press the enemy at Bari, the Qarmatians draw off and are defeated, and they return to Jabal Musur in Rabi I of the same year (304 AH).
