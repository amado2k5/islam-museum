## Summary
In 425 AH a black wind blew over Nisibin, uprooting many of its orchards and knocking down a palace built of baked brick, stone and lime. Hail fell in the shapes of hands, pillars and fingers {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 228}.

## The earthquake
Ramla was shaken, and about half of it was destroyed. Villages sank into the ground, part of the wall of Jerusalem fell, and the minaret of the mosque of Ascalon collapsed {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 228}.

## The sea withdraws
The sea receded about three leagues, and people went out to gather fish and shells. Then the water came back and carried off some of them {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 228}.
