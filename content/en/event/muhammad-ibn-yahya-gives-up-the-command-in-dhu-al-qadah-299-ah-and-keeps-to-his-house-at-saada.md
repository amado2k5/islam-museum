## Overview
On Thursday, twenty-one nights into Dhu al-Qa'dah 299 AH, Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya gathers the notables of the tribes. He then gives up the command, dismisses his governors in Najran, Hamdan and elsewhere, and keeps to his house at Sa'da.

## Detail
The book says Muhammad ibn Yahya gathered the notables of the tribes on Thursday, twenty-one nights into Dhu al-Qa'dah 299 AH, and sent to them over matters he disliked in them. He then gave up the command, removed his governors from Najran, Hamdan and elsewhere, and kept to his house at Sa'da. In Khawlan, the book says, the position stayed as it was, and no one showed opposition or dislike of his order. One of his cousins stayed at Sa'da to settle quarrels among the people.
