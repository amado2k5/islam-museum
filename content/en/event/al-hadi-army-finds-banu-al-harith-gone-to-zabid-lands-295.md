## Overview
The army searched the mountains for Banu al-Harith and found that they had moved on and fled to the lands of Zabid. Al-Hadi returned with his army, unhurt and with booty, to the village of al-Hajr, where he stayed several days.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi ordered the army, with his brother Abdallah ibn al-Husayn, to search for Banu al-Harith where they had been. They climbed another mountain called Far' al-Dadam and found that the clans had left those heights and fled to the lands of Zabid. Al-Hadi then returned with his whole army, safe and with booty, to the village of al-Hajr, where he stayed for several days.
