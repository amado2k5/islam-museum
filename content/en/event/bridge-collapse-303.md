## Overview
On Wednesday the ninth of Ramadan 303 the pontoon seating on Baghdad's bridge gave way under the crowds and many drowned; on Friday night the eighth of Ramadan a great star plunged, its light lingering an hour like a torch {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 418}.
