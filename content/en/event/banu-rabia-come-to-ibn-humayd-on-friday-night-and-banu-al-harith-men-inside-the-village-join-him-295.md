## Overview
On the night of Friday Ibn Humayd sent to Banu Rabi'a, who came to him. Some men of Banu al-Harith, of Banu Bishr and Banu Amr, who were in the village with Muhammad, agreed to his request. Banu Abd al-Madan also met them in the night, and they agreed on their position on one side of the village.

## Detail
On the night of Friday Banu Rabi'a came to Ibn Humayd. Some men of Banu al-Harith, of Banu Bishr and Banu Amr, were in the village with Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah; Ibn Humayd sent to them and told them what their clans had agreed, and they agreed to his request. Banu Abd al-Madan also met them in the night, and agreed to take their position on one side of the village. Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah had meanwhile written to his son Ali ibn Muhammad about what Banu al-Harith had done on Wednesday, ordering a raid on Sawhan; Ali wrote back that Hamdan had deserted him, that Banu Rabi'a had agreed with the other side, and that the enemy stood ready against him and his companions, and he advised leaving by the road of the lands of Shakir, since everyone was waiting on him. The record gives this exchange as the book describes it; the advice is recorded as the son's own counsel.
