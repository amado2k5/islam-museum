## The father's death
Abu 'Amr Ibn 'Abd al-Barr recorded that his father Abu Muhammad 'Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Barr died in the month of Rabi' II in the year 380, having been born in 330 {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 71}.
## The boy
Ibn 'Abd al-Barr was then twelve; he had grown up in a house of learning, writing under the jurist Abu 'Amr Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Malik al-Ishbili and attaching himself to Ibn al-Faradi {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 141}.
