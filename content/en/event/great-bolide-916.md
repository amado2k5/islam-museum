## Overview
Ibn al-Imad, in "Fragments of Gold", in the events of 916 AH, quotes from "The Clear Light" that a great star fell from the middle of the night heading toward Syria, and the world was lit up with a great light. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.490}

The account is brief, and needs an additional source.
