## Overview
In Sha'ban 759 Taj al-Din al-Subki was dismissed from the Shafi'i judgeship of Damascus, and his brother Baha' al-Din Abu al-Baqa' al-Subki was appointed in his place {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425}.
