## Summary
On the night of Thursday in Ramadan 440 AH, [[person/abu-kalijar|al-Malik Abu Kalijar]] the Buyid died at the age of forty years and several months. His guards looted his treasuries and his weapons, and his son [[person/al-malik-al-rahim|Abu Nasr]], titled al-Malik al-Rahim, succeeded him, the last of the Buyid kings in Iraq. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.478-479,481}

## The investiture
Al-Malik al-Rahim rode from his camp to the House of the Caliphate and came into the presence of the caliph. He kissed the ground and was seated on a chair. He was dressed in the full robe of honour, a black Rusafi turban, a torque and two swords. A jewelled crown was placed on his head, and two bannered flags were displayed for him. The letter of investiture and titulature was read out, and he was enjoined to fear God and to rule his subjects with justice. He was presented with a dark bay horse with a gold saddle. He dismounted, then went up to his tent and sat on his seat while people came to congratulate him; it was a day long remembered. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.478-479}
