## Taking Karak from al-Malik al-Masʿud
In 685 AH Karak was taken from al-Malik al-Masʿud Khidr, son of al-Malik al-Zahir Baybars. Khidr came down from it and marched to Egypt {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 661}.
