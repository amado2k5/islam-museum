## The story
Muslim relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet said: the strong faithful believer is better and dearer to God than the weak believer, and in all things good; be eager for what benefits you, seek Allah s help and do not be helpless; if something befalls you do not say "if only I had done such and such" but say "Allah decreed and did what He willed" — for "if only" opens the shaytan s work {cite:sahih-muslim} [[passage/muslim-2664|Muslim 2664]].

## The meaning
In this hadith, regret over what has passed expressed with "if only" is of the kind of whispering the shaytan opens; patience and contentment with Allah s decree are the recourse {cite:sahih-muslim}.
