## Summary
In 623 AH Jalal al-Din besieged Akhlat and stormed it twice; its people and al-Ashraf's garrison held out until he left in the bitter cold and over the Aywai Turcomans' ravaging of his lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 467}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Jalal al-Din invested Malazgird on Saturday 13 Dhu al-Qa'da, then Akhlat on Monday the 15th; scarcely had he halted before he stormed the city in hard fighting, his troops reached the wall and entered its suburb, stretching hands to plunder and take women, whereupon the people of Akhlat rallied and urged one another on, returned to the troops, drove them out of the town killing many and capturing amirs, while al-Hajib Ali fought on foot in the enemy's throat with great valor. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 467} After resting a few days Jalal al-Din stormed again as on the first day; they fought him until his army was driven back — the people of Akhlat striving hard to defend themselves because of the evil conduct and plundering they had seen from the Khwarazmians — and he remained until the cold grew severe and snow fell, departing on Tuesday seven days before the end of Dhu al-Hijja, his withdrawal owed also to reports of the Aywai Turcomans' ruin of his lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 467}
