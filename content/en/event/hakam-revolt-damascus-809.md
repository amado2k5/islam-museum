## Overview
In 809 AH the revolts of the emirs al-Hakam, Shaykh and Nawruz broke out in Syria, according to the "Fragments of Gold". {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.88}

Al-Hakam was given allegiance to the sultanate in Syria and titled al-Adil. Then his horse stumbled under him in the course of this, and he died. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.88}

What is not covered here: the account rests on a single source, and the Gregorian conversion is derived. An additional source is needed.
