## Summary
[[person/majid-bin-said|Majid bin Said]] became sultan of the Omani empire, based at its capital [[place/zanzibar|Zanzibar]], when his father [[person/said-bin-sultan|Said bin Sultan]] died on 19 October 1856. {cite:wikipedia-majid-bin-said-en}

## The disputed succession
Majid had already been his father's deputy in Zanzibar. Said died at sea that day, and the English article records the burial in the Makusurani Cemetery at Zanzibar. {cite:wikipedia-said-bin-sultan-en} {cite:britannica-said-ibn-sultan} The succession was contested: the elder brother Thuwaini took Muscat and Oman and claimed the whole inheritance. {cite:wikipedia-majid-bin-said-en} Britannica says Said's will divided the dominions between Majid in Zanzibar and Thuwaini in Muscat and Oman. {cite:britannica-said-ibn-sultan}
