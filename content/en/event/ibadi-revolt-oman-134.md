## Overview
In 134 AH the Ibadis of [[place/oman|Oman]] under [[person/al-julanda-ibn-masud|al-Julanda ibn Mas'ud]] rose against the new Abbasid caliphate, as Ahmad Amin relates in "The Dawn of Islam". {cite:duha-islam p. 950}

## The account
The caliph sent against them [[person/khazim-ibn-khuzayma|Khazim ibn Khuzayma]] with an army that sailed across the sea and anchored on the coast of Oman. They fought each other fiercely, with victory going back and forth. {cite:duha-islam p. 950}

## The end
The Abbasid army set fire to the wooden houses of the rebels with torches dipped in naphtha, and the rebels turned to saving their families. Khazim attacked them, and al-Julanda was killed among those killed. The dead reached about ten thousand, and their heads were sent to Basra. {cite:duha-islam p. 950}

The description of the rebels as Kharijites is the source's own term. The Ibadi view of the event is not recorded here; an additional source is needed.
