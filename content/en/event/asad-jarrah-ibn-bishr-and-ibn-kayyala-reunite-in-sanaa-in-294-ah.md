## Overview
The Kufi Qarmatian drives Jarrah ibn Bishr out of Shiban. Jarrah and Ibn Kayyala write to Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur, who comes, and the three keep Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Haddaqi as judge in Sanaa.

## Detail
The book reports that the Kufi Qarmatian drove Jarrah ibn Bishr out of Shiban and that Jarrah fled to Sanaa. Jarrah and Ibn Kayyala wrote to Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur to advance, and he did. The three stayed together in Sanaa and kept Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Haddaqi as judge. A marginal note of the printed edition says Jarrah and Ibn Kayyala were clients of the Banu Ya'fur and that Kayyala's name was al-Hasan.
