## Overview
The janissaries mutinied at Edirne under Murad II on the pretext of the fire in the city, rioting until the rising was pacified by an increase in their pay {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711} {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 82}.

## The janissary rising
Murad II had retired from the throne in Asia Minor leaving his young son Mehmed; the statesmen feared the mutiny would grow out of control {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 82}.
