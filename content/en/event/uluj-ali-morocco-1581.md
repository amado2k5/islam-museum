## Summary
An Ottoman bid to annex Morocco, thwarted by al-Mansur's readiness and diplomacy {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 270-271}.

## Background
With the Ottoman state settled in Tunis, kapudan [[person/kilic-ali-pasha|Uluj Ali]] turned toward Morocco; orders went out for him to annex it {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 270-271}.

## The thwarted confrontation
He reached Algiers in Jumada II 989/June 1581 while [[person/ahmad-al-mansur-al-sadi|al-Mansur]] stood camped at the Tensift — troops ready, kingdom's passes sealed, shores fortified, an embassy bound for Istanbul, and a near-agreement with Spain struck in exchange for Larache and other concessions {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 271}.

## The retreat
Facing events, the Ottoman sultan accepted the fait accompli and abandoned the invasion, ordering Uluj Ali and his deputy Jafar Pasha to leave Morocco and head east, where the Hejaz had grown troubled {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 271}.
