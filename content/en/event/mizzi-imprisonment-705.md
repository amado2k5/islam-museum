## Summary
In 705/1305 al-Mizzi was harmed on account of Ibn Taymiyya: he began reading al-Bukhari's Khalq Af'al al-Ibad, which contains a chapter refuting the Jahmiyya; some held themselves meant by it and the Shafi'i qadi of the day ordered him jailed {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/458}.

## What happened
Ibn Taymiyya went and had him released; the deputy al-Afram had him re-jailed then freed, and ordered proclamation that whoever discusses creed is killed {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/458}. Al-Dhahabi records that he was imprisoned for reading part of the Af'al al-Ibad book seized on by rival scholars, and bore it patiently {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 490-491}. A Shafi'i by legal preference, al-Mizzi had wholeheartedly espoused Ibn Taymiyya's new ideas and creed, which landed him for a short time in jail {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 240}.
