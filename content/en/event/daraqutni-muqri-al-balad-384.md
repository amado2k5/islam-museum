## Summary
Al-Daraqutni excelled in the qira'at and in his last days took the lead in the public reading of the Qur'an {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.104}. He said: al-Kattani and I used to hear hadith together, and people would say 'al-Kattani will come out the muhaddith of the town, al-Daraqutni its muqri' — and I came out the muhaddith and al-Kattani the muqri'' {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.184}.

## Commander of the Faithful in hadith
The qadi Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari called him Commander of the Faithful in hadith; al-Khatib said he was the singular one of his age and the imam of his time, to whom the science of transmitted reports, the defects of hadith and the names of its men came to an end, with truthfulness, reliability and sound belief {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp.102-103}.
