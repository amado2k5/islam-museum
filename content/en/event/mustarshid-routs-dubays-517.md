## Summary
In early 517 AH the caliph al-Mustarshid bi-llah met Dubays al-Asadi. Dubays had grown arrogant and rebellious and had promised his army that he would plunder Baghdad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 533}.

## The battle
Al-Mustarshid drew his sword that day and stood on a hill. Dubays's force was routed, and many of them were killed. About twenty of the caliph's men were killed. The caliph returned victorious and supported by God. Dubays went off and raided, plundered and killed around Basra {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 533}.
