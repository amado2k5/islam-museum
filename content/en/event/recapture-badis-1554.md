## Summary
The recovery of the stronghold Badis — the rock called Vélez — from Spain by a detachment of Salih Reis's army {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 239}.

## During the Fez stay
[[person/salih-rais|Salih Reis]] sent the detachment into the Moroccan Rif while he was in Fez; it retook the great Spanish stronghold {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 239}.
