## Overview
In Safar 756 the decree arrived appointing qadi Taj al-Din Abd al-Wahhab ibn Taqi al-Din al-Subki to the Shafi'i judgeship of Damascus and its districts, on his father's footing and in his father's lifetime, and the people went to congratulate him {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 756 p. 263}.
