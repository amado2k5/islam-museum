## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] made peace with the people of Jundaysabur on terms of safe conduct. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
Abu Musa went to Jundaysabur, and its people were dismayed and asked for safe conduct. He made peace with them on condition that he would kill no one, take no one captive, and not touch their property, except for weapons. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Kalbaniyya
A group of them went to Kalbaniyya, and Abu Musa sent [[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi ibn Ziyad]] against them; he killed them and conquered it. The cavalry sought safe conduct, he granted it, and they became Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
