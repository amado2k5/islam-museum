## Summary
Al-Nuwayri died at Cairo on 21 Ramadan 733/5 June 1333, aged fifty-six, having composed the Nihayat al-Arab fi Funun al-Adab — 9,000 pages in thirty-one volumes written between 714 and 731 — bequeathing his experience and culture to posterity in the form of a summa {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 159} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/197}.

## Closing
Ibn Hajar describes him as good-looking, charming and companionable, and notes that a copy of his ample history in his own hand sold for two thousand dirhams, in thirty volumes; the historical fann of his work closes at 731, two years before his death, and its last annals read like notes set down for later revision which death forestalled {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/197} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 159}.
