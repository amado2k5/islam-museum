## Summary
The disturbances that followed Salah al-Din's death forced al-Qifti to leave in 598/1201 for Aleppo, where the protection and encouragement of a friend of his father let him resume his scholarly pursuits for several years {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}.
