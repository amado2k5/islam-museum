## The story
Malik relates from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki that Abu Muizz al-Aslami told him he was sitting with Abdullah ibn Umar when a woman came to consult him, saying: I came wanting to circle the House, but when I was at the mosque door I spilled blood, so I went back until it left me, then I came again and spilled blood three times. Abdullah ibn Umar said: that is only a strike from the shaytan — wash, seek refuge with a garment, then circle {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-6-1085|Muwatta 1085]].

## The meaning
In this report Ibn Umar counted the sudden bleeding that befalls a woman at the Kaaba door as a strike from the shaytan, and ordered her to wash, seek refuge with a garment, then circle {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
