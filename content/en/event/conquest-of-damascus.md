## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the Muslims returned to [[place/damascus|Damascus]] fourteen nights before the end of Muharram 14 AH. Its people fortified themselves, and it was taken: by the treaty of Khalid through the Eastern Gate, and by the entry of Abu Ubayda by force through the Gate of al-Jabiya. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Distribution of the gates
[[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] halted at the Eastern Gate with about five thousand men, whom Abu Ubayda joined to him. [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] halted at the Gate of Tuma, [[person/shurahbil-ibn-hasana|Shurahbil]] at the Gate of al-Faradis, [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] at the Gate of al-Jabiya, and [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-sufyan|Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan]] at the small gate up to the Gate of Kaysan. [[person/abu-al-darda|Abu al-Darda']] was put in charge of the garrison at Barza. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Khalid's letter
al-Baladhuri relates that the bishop asked for peace, and Khalid wrote him a letter giving them safety for their lives, their property, their churches and the walls of their city, "if they give the jizya". Then he learned from the bishop's companions that it was the night of a festival, so the Muslims climbed the wall with two ladders and opened the gate at sunrise. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abu Ubayda took the Gate of al-Jabiya by force and entered, and met Khalid in al-Maqsilat. Maytham ibn Adi claimed that the people of Damascus had been granted peace on half of their dwellings and their churches, but al-Waqidi said that he had read Khalid's letter and did not find that in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Dating and disagreement
al-Waqidi said that the conquest was in Rajab 14 AH, and that Khalid's letter was dated in Rabi' al-Akhir 15 AH, because it was written without a date. al-Baladhuri gives the view of the editor of the edition that Khalid entered forcibly by the Eastern Gate and Abu Ubayda peacefully by the Gate of al-Jabiya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
