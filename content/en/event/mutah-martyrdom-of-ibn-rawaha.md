## Overview
When Ja'far was killed at [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'tah]], [[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abd Allah ibn Rawaha]] took the banner, and hesitated to charge. He reproached himself in a rajaz poem and then dismounted. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

His cousin brought him a piece of meat, and he took a bite from it. Then he heard the clamour among the people, threw it down and went forward to fight until he was killed. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## The vision
Ibn Hisham says that the Prophet saw his couch in Paradise inclined away from the couches of Zayd and Ja'far, because he hesitated a little and then went on. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
