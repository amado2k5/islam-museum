## Overview
The Day of al-Jufar, also called al-Saylam because of the great number slain in it, related by Ibn al-Athir among the days of the Arabs before Islam. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466}

## What happened
On the anniversary of the Day of al-Nisar the Arabs who witnessed it gathered with their chiefs, and they met at al-Jufar. [[group/banu-tamim|Tamim]] held firm, and the killing grew among the [[group/banu-amir-ibn-sasaa|Banu Amir]], especially among the Banu Amr ibn Tamim. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466}

Ibn al-Athir says that Malik ibn Ka'b, known as Jawwab Qanab al-Ir, fled from his two co-wives. He quotes a poem of Bishr ibn Abi Khazim about the band of Tamim. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466}

What is not covered here: the number of the slain. The date is approximate.
