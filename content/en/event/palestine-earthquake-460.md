## Summary
In 460 AH an earthquake struck Palestine and other lands. It killed 15,000 people of Ramla {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 315}.

## Its effect on the holy sites
Two minarets of the Prophet's Mosque fell. The earth split open and revealed treasures of money. A rock in Jerusalem split and then closed again {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 315}.

## The sea withdraws
The sea receded from the shore for a day's journey. It spread over the cold ground, and people went out to gather what was left. Then the water came back and killed a great number of them. The earthquake reached al-Rahba and Kufa {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 315}.
