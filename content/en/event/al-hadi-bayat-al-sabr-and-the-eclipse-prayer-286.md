## Overview
These two reports give the "pledge of endurance", told by [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] from his father, and [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s prayer at a solar eclipse, told by [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]], both at Sa'da. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum describes it and attributes it to its narrators without a verdict, and the manner of the prayer is the imam's own reasoned practice as reported.

## The pledge of endurance
Ali ibn Muhammad says: I was sitting with him one night and two men came and said, O son of the Messenger of God, we want to pledge you the pledge of endurance; he asked, did you not pledge me with the people? They said yes, but we want the pledge of endurance; he said, sit down, God bless you, and began an eloquent exhortation. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.117} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} He told them that he had examined himself and wished to set apart believing brothers who would endure with him what he said to them; the matter was great and hard, the people had pledged him and they had pledged him, but this was something by which he wished to single out brothers who would endure hunger, hardship, nakedness and harm with him until every four shared one garment and each took a rag to cover himself for prayer. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} If they could endure that, they should come forward; they said, we will endure with you, came forward, and he took from them the covenant described in this book, {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} adding that they would endure with him distress, hardship, severity and ease, hunger and nakedness, until God judged between him and his enemy. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} See [[concept/sabr]] and the covenant's first form in [[event/al-hadi-form-of-the-bayah-286]].

## The eclipse prayer
Ali ibn Muhammad says Muhammad ibn Sulayman told him: I saw al-Hadi when the sun was eclipsed at Sa'da; he ordered a crier up the minaret to call "prayer in congregation", and the people gathered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} Yahya ibn al-Husayn stood, said the takbir and did not recite the Qur'an aloud; when he had finished Muhammad asked what he had read, and he said the opening sura, the two sura of refuge, the sura of sincerity five times in each unit, and the suras of the Cave, Maryam, Ta-Ha, the Prophets and the Ant, spread over the units. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} Muhammad saw him bow, raise his head and recite again until he had done so five times, then prostrate once and sit long after it, then prostrate a second time; asked what he said between the prostrations he answered that he recited and prayed; then he rose and did the same, so that he prayed ten bowings in four prostrations. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.118} The text gives neither the day nor the year. See [[concept/eclipses]].
