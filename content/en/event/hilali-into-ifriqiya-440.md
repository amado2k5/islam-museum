## Summary
In 440 AH al-Mu'izz ibn Badis proclaimed in the Maghrib the call of the Abbasid caliph al-Qa'im bi-Amr Allah, and he renounced obedience to the Fatimid caliph al-Mustansir {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 264}.

## The Arab tribes
Al-Mustansir sent an army of Arabs against him. That was the first entry of the Arabs into Ifriqiya: the Banu Riyah and the Banu Zughba {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 264}.

## What followed
Their affairs went on for a long time. Ibn Khaldun reports that the Hilali Arabs spoiled the roads and villages in the days of al-Mu'izz until they ruined Ifriqiya {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 264; tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p. 1634}.
