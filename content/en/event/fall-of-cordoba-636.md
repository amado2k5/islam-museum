## Overview
Al-Maqqari says that the enemy took [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] on Sunday the twenty-third of Shawwal 636 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 476}

## In context
In the same passage the source records the taking of Zaragoza in 512 AH, the island of Shuqr by treaty in 639 AH, Murcia by treaty in 643 AH, and the siege of Seville in 645 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 476}
