## Overview
The battle of al-Tawahin was fought between Abu al-Abbas ibn al-Muwaffaq and Abu al-Jaysh Khumarawayh ibn Ahmad ibn Tulun at al-Tawahin, in the districts of Palestine, in Shawwal 271 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.579-580}

## al-Mas'udi's account
al-Mas'udi says that it was on Tuesday, fourteen nights before the end of Shawwal. Khumarawayh was routed, and Abu al-Abbas seized his camp. Khumarawayh escaped with a group of his commanders as far as Fustat. Then his servant Sa'd al-A'sar fought Abu al-Abbas, defeated him, plundered his camp and killed his leading commanders. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.579-580}

## Ibn al-Jawzi's account
Ibn al-Jawzi says that it was on the sixteenth of Shawwal. Abu al-Abbas routed Khumarawayh, who fled on a donkey, and the companions of Abu al-Abbas were busy plundering. Then an ambush of Khumarawayh's men came out and routed them, and what was in both camps was lost to plunder. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.101}

The two accounts differ on the day, and agree on the month and the year.
