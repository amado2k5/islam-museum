## Overview
On the morning of Sunday, 8 Shawwal 3 AH, the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] called the people to follow Quraysh after [[event/battle-of-uhud|Uhud]], and he said: "None goes out with us except those who took part in the fighting." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.284}

## The march out
The Muslims responded despite their wounds. They reached [[place/hamra-al-asad|Hamra al-Asad]], eight miles from [[place/medina|Medina]], and camped there. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.284}

## The psychological war
The Prophet sent Ma'bad ibn Abi Ma'bad al-Khuza'i to [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]]. He reported to him that Muhammad had come out with a host the like of which he had not seen, burning with rage against them. The resolve of the Meccan army collapsed and they continued their withdrawal to Mecca. Abu Sufyan sent a party of riders from Abd al-Qays to carry a threat to the Muslims, but it only increased them in faith. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.284}

## The return
The Prophet stayed three days and then returned on Wednesday. He had put to death [[person/abu-azza-al-jumahi|Abu Azza al-Jumahi]], who had broken his covenant. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.286}

The Sahih has it that the verse about those who answered the call was revealed about seventy men who set out in pursuit of the polytheists, among them Abu Bakr and al-Zubayr. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
