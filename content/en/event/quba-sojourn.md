## Summary
The Prophet lodged at [[place/quba|Quba]] on Monday 8 Rabi' al-Awwal 1 AH (23 September 622 CE) and stayed until Friday {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 170-172}.

## The welcome
Medina's Muslims would go out each morning to the harra watching for him until the midday heat drove them back, until a Jew sighted them from atop a fort and cried at the top of his voice: people of Arabia — here is your awaited fortune! The Muslims leapt to arms and went out to meet him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 171} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. They received him and greeted him with the greeting of prophethood; Abu Bakr rose before the people while the Prophet sat silently, until Abu Bakr shaded him with his cloak and the people knew him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 171}.

## The stay and the mosque
The Prophet lodged with [[person/kulthum-ibn-al-hidm|Kulthum ibn al-Hidm]] — the likelier view — or, it is said, Sa'd ibn Khaythama; he stayed at Quba four days on Ibn Ishaq's account, which the author chose (Bukhari has twenty-four, fourteen-odd and fourteen nights), and he founded Quba's mosque and prayed in it — the first mosque founded on God-consciousness after the prophethood {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172}.

[[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] caught up with them at Quba after staying three days in Mecca to return on the Prophet's behalf the deposits people had left with him, then emigrating on foot {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 171-172} {cite:zad-al-maad}.
