## Overview
[[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] says that near [[place/najran|Najran]] there was a town called "Da'a" in the land of Shakir, a people of Hamdan, who had come to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] at [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and Najran and pledged to uphold the truth; he sent men with them to collect their alms, and they took the alms and put them in a house they had. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} A man of Shakir called "Nu'aym", in one manuscript "Rahim", rose against them, spoke to al-Hadi's agents roughly and took the key of the house where the grain was, and the news reached al-Hadi on the night of Friday the fourteenth of Ramadan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} The report is Zaydi, attributing the acts and rulings to the imam.

## The march to Da'a
He set out at once and told the people to go to Da'a; a great crowd of Khawlan, Hamdan and Banu al-Harith went with him; he came to Da'a at dawn and made for the man's house and found he had fled; he ordered the house pulled down and stood by it himself, for it held the owner's grain and dates, standing there for fear any be taken, "so the army took not a date nor a grain of food", and a wall of the house's parapets was demolished. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} When the owner, hiding nearby, saw that, he came forward to al-Hadi, who took him and a man who had helped him in his mischief to Najran and held and shackled them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80}

## The return and the governors
He told the people to prepare to leave for Sa'da, thousands of Hamdan, Banu al-Harith and Khawlan gathered to him, and he set out on Tuesday the eighteenth of Ramadan, putting Ahmad ibn Muhammad, of the line of al-Abbas ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, over Najran, whom the narrator calls scrupulous, learned, chaste and just in his rulings, and charging him to fear God, enjoin good and forbid wrong, apply the penalties and take the right from whoever owed it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} He says the governor flogged a man of Yam for drinking wine with eighty lashes and the man died of the penalty; word reached al-Hadi, who was heard to say something that reads "may God not have mercy", though to whom it refers is unclear in the OCR text and is not stated here; and he cut off the hand of a thief against whom the theft was proved by witnesses. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} With him he appointed a man of Tamim, Muhammad ibn Isa, from Iraq, and the two worked together in God's matter. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} Al-Hadi reached Sa'da on Thursday with nine nights left of Ramadan and stayed there for the rest of the month and some days of Shawwal. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.80} See [[group/banu-hamdan]], [[group/shakir-hamdan-tribe]], [[group/khawlan-tribe]], [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab]] and [[group/yam-tribe]].
