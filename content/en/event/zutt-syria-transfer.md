## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that Mu'awiya transferred from the [[group/zutt|Zutt]] and the ancient Sayabija a number of people to the coasts of Syria and Antioch. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik transferred a group of the Zutt to Antioch and its district. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
