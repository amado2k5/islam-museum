## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad recited an elegy in the khafif metre on his return to the fort. It mourns his father and the killing, praises the Thaqif horsemen who answered his call, reproaches those who did not help him, calls on Hamdan to defend Islam, and demands vengeance on the killers.

## Detail
On the way back to the fort Ali ibn Muhammad recited an elegy in the khafif metre. It mourns his father and the killing, reproaches the Wada'a who did not answer his call, praises the Thaqif horsemen who answered and helped him, calls on Hamdan to defend Islam, and demands vengeance on the men who killed his father. It describes his father as faithful to his pledge and a helper of the religion. The lines are not quoted in this record: the verse order is uncertain on the scan, and only the subject is recorded.
