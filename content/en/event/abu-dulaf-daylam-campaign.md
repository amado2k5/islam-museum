## Overview
[[person/abu-dulaf-al-qasim|Abu Dulaf al-Qasim ibn Isa]] raided [[place/daylam|Daylam]] in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. He was later governor in the caliphate of al-Mu'tasim, in the days when [[person/afshin|al-Afshin]] was governor of the mountains. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The forts
He conquered forts, among them Iqlasim, and made peace with its people in return for tribute. Among them was Bumaj, which he took by force and then made peace with its people in return for tribute. Others were al-Iblam and Andaq, among further forts. al-Afshin sent others besides Abu Dulaf, and he too conquered forts in Daylam. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
