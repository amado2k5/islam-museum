## The exodus
Abu 'Amr Ibn 'Abd al-Barr quit his birthplace Cordoba; the year 403 had devastated the city when Sulayman al-Musta'in and the Berbers stormed it after a siege that starved its villages and gardens {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 464-465}.
## Eastward
He stayed in the west of al-Andalus for a time, then moved to its east, settling at Dania, Balansiya and Shatiba — the town where he would die {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 141}.
