## Overview
al-Maqqari, in "Nafh al-Tib", reports on the authority of Ibn Hayyan that the enemy overcame [[place/barbastro|Barbastro]], the capital of a Britannian district near Saragossa, in 456 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 449}

## The siege
The army of the Ardamaniyyun (Ardamanīyūn) besieged it for forty days. Yusuf ibn Sulayman ibn Hud fell short in protecting it. The enemy entered the first city, and the people took refuge in the inner city. Then the channel that brought the water collapsed, the water was cut off, and people despaired of life. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 449}

## The surrender
The people asked for safe-conduct for themselves, without their property and families, and the enemy granted it. Then he broke faith with them and killed more than six thousand of them, as Ibn Hayyan relates. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 449}
