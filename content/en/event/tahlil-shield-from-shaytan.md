## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet reported: whoever says a hundred times in a day "there is no god but God alone, without partner, His is the kingdom and His is the praise, and He is able over all things" — it becomes a fortress for him against the shaytan that day until evening; no one brought anything better except one who did more {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-560|Muwatta 560]].

## The meaning
In this hadith remembrance fortifies its owner against the shaytan; the repeated tahlil is a daily fortress that blocks his whisperings {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
