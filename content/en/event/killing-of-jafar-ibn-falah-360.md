## Summary
In 360 AH Ja'far ibn Falah al-Kutami, the Ubaydid governor of Syria, was killed by al-Hasan ibn Ahmad al-Qarmati, known as al-A'sam {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 29}.

## The battle at the river Yazid
Ja'far camped on the mound above the river Yazid, outside Damascus. Al-A'sam came against him. Ja'far went out to meet him while he was ill, and the Qarmati captured and killed him, and killed many of his companions. This was on Thursday, 6 Dhu al-Qa'da {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 29}.

## The first Fatimid governor
Ja'far was the first governor the Ubaydids appointed over Damascus. He took Ramla in Dhu al-Hijja 358 AH and then Damascus in Muharram 359 AH, after fighting its people {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 29}.
