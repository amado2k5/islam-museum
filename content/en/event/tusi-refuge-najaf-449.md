## Summary
In Safar 449 al-Tusi's Karkh house was stormed — his books, teaching chair and the pilgrims' mangonels burned in the market — and he took refuge in Najaf, teaching a limited circle including his son Abu Ali al-Hasan for the rest of his life {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.521} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 745}.
