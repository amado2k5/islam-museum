## Overview
[[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub]] died in [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in Safar 589 AH, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 224-225}

## His illness
Ibn al-Athir relates that he went out to meet the pilgrims, then returned and fell ill from that day with a severe illness that lasted eight days. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 224-225}

## Counsel before the illness
Before his illness he consulted his son [[person/al-malik-al-afdal-ali|al-Afdal Ali]] and his brother [[person/al-malik-al-adil-ayyub|al-Malik al-Adil]] about the direction they would head after finishing with the Franks. al-Adil advised Khilat, and al-Afdal advised the lands of Rum. Salah al-Din told both of them that they were mistaken, and resolved on the lands of Rum. He gave al-Adil leave to go to al-Karak, and he fell ill and died before his return. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 224-225}

## His character in Ibn al-Athir
Ibn al-Athir gives two reports of his forbearance: a mamluk threw a spear-shaft at one of them, and it fell near him, and he turned to the other side to feign inattention. He asked for water five times and none came, and he said that thirst had killed him, and he did not blame the delay. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 224-225}
