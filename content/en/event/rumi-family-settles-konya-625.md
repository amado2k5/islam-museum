## Summary
In 625/1228 Baha al-Din Walad and his family were called to Konya, capital of the Seljuqs of Rum, where he taught at one of its many madrasas. {cite:britannica-rumi}

## Laranda before Konya
Before that the family stayed briefly at Laranda (Karaman), where Jalal al-Din's mother died and his eldest son was born; the family then moved to the capital, where Jalal al-Din lived for the rest of his life. {cite:britannica-rumi}
