## Summary
In 715/1315 al-Nuwayri attended the shaykha Wazira bint al-Munadhdha's (d. 716) transmission sessions of Sahih al-Bukhari — part of a lifelong interest in the teaching of hadith {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 156}.

## His masters and auditions
Among his shaykhs: Abd al-Mu'min al-Dimyati (d. 705), whose Kitab Fadl al-Khayl he quotes often; the hadith specialist Ibn Daqiq al-Id (d. 702); chief qadi Ibn Jama'a (d. 733), shaykh of the Nasiriyya khankah; and the shaykha Zaynab bint Yahya ibn Abd al-Salam (d. 735). He also attended al-Salihi al-Hajjar's (d. 730) sessions at the Mansuriyya, heard the Sunan of Abu Dawud from Ibn al-Sabuni (d. 720), the Kitab al-Shifa from Abd al-Haqq ibn Fityan in 708 at the Nasiriyya, and Malik's Muwatta' and Sahih Muslim from the Sharif Izz al-Din al-Dimashqi (d. 715); Izz al-Din al-Faruthi also gave him an ijaza {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 156}.
