## Summary
Al-Dhahabi says of his teacher al-Mizzi: 'I heard from him in the year 694 and took from him the Sahih of al-Bukhari and other works', al-Dhahabi then being about twenty-one {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 491}.

## His students
Among al-Mizzi's students were al-Dhahabi, Abd al-Wahhab al-Subki author of the Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra, and his son-in-law Isma'il ibn Umar Ibn Kathir author of al-Bidaya wa-l-Nihaya {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 240}; a circle took their training from him, among them al-Barzali, Ibn al-Fakhr, al-Ala'i, Ibn Kathir and Ibn al-Attar {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 491}.
