## Overview
Ibn Imad in "Shadharat al-Dhahab" relates that in 910 AH a great earthquake took place in the cities of Zabid and Zayla. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.461}

There was a collapse in the earthquake, and the people of Zayla went out into the desert in fear. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.461}

The data does not give the number of victims or the extent of the destruction, and it needs an additional source.
