## Summary
Al-Bayhaqi travelled through many lands in pursuit of hadith and is credited with transmitting from about a hundred shaykhs; in creed he was an Ash'arite, and by character frugal, pious and scholarly {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1190}.
