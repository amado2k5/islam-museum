## Overview
The stars scatter on the night of Wednesday, eight days before the end of Jumada II of that year, and it recurs so often that people grow afraid.

## Detail
The book records a fall of stars in a note headed 'the falling of the stars', on the Wednesday night eight days before the end of Jumada II of the same year, and says it recurred until people were alarmed. The year is read from the preceding events of 300 AH; the sentence itself does not state it.
