## Overview

The second race between the Prophet and Aisha: years later, when she had «put on flesh» — grown heavier — on another journey he again told the people to go ahead, raced her and beat her, laughing: «this one is for that one» {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## In the hadith

In the continuation of Aisha's report: «until I had put on flesh and returned with the Prophet on one of his journeys; he said to the people: go ahead, they went ahead, then he said to me: come, let me race you — so I raced him and he beat me, and he laughed and said: this one is for that one» {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:musnad-ahmad}. A return for her first win after she grew and gained weight: two races spread across the years of the marriage, remembered by the Prophet in a spirit of play.

## Its meaning

The scholars cite the two reports together under permitted play and good companionship: the race is recreation, and «this one for that one» tells of sporting fairness between them. They are among the most famous reports on marital sport in the whole Sunnah.
