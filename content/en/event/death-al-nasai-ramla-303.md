## Summary
Al-Nasa'i died on Monday, thirteen nights before the end of Safar, in 303/915 in Palestine — at al-Ramla on the preferred report, which al-Dhahabi judges correct — from the effects of what he suffered at Damascus {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 109} {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 620}.

## Another report
Al-Daraqutni reports he had set out on hajj, was attacked at Damascus, and said 'carry me to Mecca', where he died and is buried between al-Safa and al-Marwa, in Sha'ban 303 {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 109} {cite:wafayat-ayan i p. 26}; scholars have counted him a martyr for dying of ill-treatment {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1023}.
