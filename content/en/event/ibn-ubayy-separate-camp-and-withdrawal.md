## Summary
Abdullah ibn Ubayy pitched his own camp separately below the Prophet's camp toward Dhubab — by their claim, no smaller of the two camps; when the Prophet marched, Ibn Ubayy stayed behind him among "the hypocrites and the people of doubt" who stayed behind. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## The account
Abdullah ibn Ubayy pitched his camp separately below the Prophet's, toward Dhubab — by their claim no smaller of the two camps. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

When the Prophet marched, Abdullah ibn Ubayy stayed behind him among the hypocrites and the people of doubt who stayed behind, in Ibn Ishaq's description. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- Ibn Ishaq says "by their claim" about the size of Ibn Ubayy's camp — a marker of weak transmission (p. 162).
- Describing those who stayed behind as "the hypocrites and the people of doubt" is Ibn Ishaq's wording; it is attributed to him.
