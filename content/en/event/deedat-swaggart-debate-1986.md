## Summary
The 1986 Baton Rouge debate between Ahmed Deedat and Jimmy Swaggart on 'Is the Bible the Word of God?' — the most printed and transcribed debate of the modern era. {cite:answering-islam-debates-index}

## Subject and arguments
Deedat challenged that the Bible in circulation is God's own revelation, arguing from variant versions and textual history; Swaggart defended the inspiration of the two Testaments and the doctrine of Christ. The full transcript and video spread so widely that the debate became the model method on which the next generation of Muslim debaters measured itself.
