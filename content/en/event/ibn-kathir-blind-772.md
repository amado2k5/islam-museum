## Summary
Ibn Kathir lost his sight in the last years of his life, before his death in Sha'ban 774, as Ibn Hajar transmits from al-Dhahabi's Mu'jam al-mukhtass {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
## Al-Dhahabi's appraisal
Al-Dhahabi calls him 'the imam, mufti, accomplished traditionist — a many-sided jurist, an exact muhaddith, a critical mufassir, author of useful works' {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
