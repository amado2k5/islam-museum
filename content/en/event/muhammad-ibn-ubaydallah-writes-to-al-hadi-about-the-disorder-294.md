## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah wrote to al-Hadi about the disorder at Najran and urged him to go out and set it right; the book gives the purpose of the letter, not its text.

## Detail
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah wrote to al-Hadi to inform him of the disorder that had broken out and to urge him to go out to the country to set it right and to put its people right. The book gives the purpose of the letter, not its wording. The narrator then reports that Ali ibn Muhammad composed verses urging al-Hadi to go out to Najran. The heading above the report reads "the disorder and the corruption", and it falls under the year 294 given on p. 330. The account is the narrator's; the museum adopts none of its judgements.
