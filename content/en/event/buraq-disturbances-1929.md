## Overview
Disturbances in Palestine in August 1929 over the Buraq Wall, known in Arab usage as the Buraq revolt; the Mandate government set up an inquiry commission, then the British government asked the League of Nations to form an international commission {cite:al-quds-bayn-al-yahudiyya-wal-islam-azhar p. 65}{cite:al-quds-bayn-al-yahudiyya-wal-islam-azhar p. 67}.
