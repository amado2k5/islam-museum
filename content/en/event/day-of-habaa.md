## Overview
The Day of al-Habaa is one of the days of the Arabs before Islam. In it the Banu Abs killed [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-badr|Hudhayfa ibn Badr al-Fazari]], lord of Ghatafan. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 15-16}

## The account
The Banu Dhubyan and the Banu Abs were allies. al-Baladhuri, in "Ansab al-Ashraf", relates that the killing of Hudhayfa was among the causes of the long war of Dahis and al-Ghabra', between Fazara and Abd Allah ibn Ghatafan on one side, and Abs and Dhubyan on the other. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 15-16}

## Dating
There is no verified date for the day. It is estimated as shortly before Islam, when the war of Dahis and al-Ghabra' began. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 15} It needs an additional source.
