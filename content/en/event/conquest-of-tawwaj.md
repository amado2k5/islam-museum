## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that when Umar appointed [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]] over Bahrain and Oman, he sent his brother [[person/al-hakam-ibn-abi-al-as|al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As]] by sea to Fars with an army from Abd al-Qays, al-Azd, Tamim and Banu Najiya. He took the island of Abr Kawan, then went to [[place/tawwaj|Tawwaj]] in the land of Ardashir Khurra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
In the report of Abu Mikhnaf, Uthman ibn Abi al-As himself crossed the sea to Fars, took Tawwaj, built mosques in it and settled Abd al-Qays and others there, and used to raid Arjan from it. Others besides Abu Mikhnaf said that al-Hakam was the one who conquered it and settled the Muslims in it, in 19 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
