## Overview
Muhammad ibn al-Haytham and Ahmad ibn al-Arbad al-Masrabani, who had shown loyalty to al-Hadi, pleaded for Abdallah ibn Musa al-Dahhaf al-Amri, his companions and the men of the Jasiyin. Al-Hadi freed them. The men of trouble then gathered in Wadi Najran and grew more resentful.

## Detail
The book says that Muhammad ibn al-Haytham and Ahmad ibn al-Arbad al-Masrabani, who showed al-Hadi goodwill and sincere counsel, spoke for Abdallah ibn Musa al-Dahhaf al-Amri, his companions and the men of the Jasiyin. Al-Hadi granted their plea and released them. The narrator notes that the men of trouble did not thank the ruler for this favour; they all gathered in Wadi Najran, and their resentment of the ruler grew.
