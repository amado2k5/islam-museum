## Overview
Emir Süleyman, son of Bayezid I, began Edirne's Eski Cami in 804/1402, and his brother Mehmed I completed it in 816/1413 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.

## From the interregnum
Mehmed I named the mosque Süleymaniye after the brother who began it; it later became known as the Ulu Cami and finally as the Eski Cami, the Old Mosque. A stone from a corner of the Ka'ba was set in the window to the right of its mihrab {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.
