## Summary
In 594 AH Ala al-Din Tekish, the Khwarazmshah, took Bukhara, which had belonged to the ruler of the Khitai. There were wars and calamities between them. Tekish won and killed many of the Khitai. This Tekish is the one who ended the Seljuq state {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 796}.
