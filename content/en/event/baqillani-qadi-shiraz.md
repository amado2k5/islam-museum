## Summary
For a time al-Baqillani exercised the office of qadi outside the capital, Baghdad, and the sources mention visits to Shiraz and to the Byzantine court — years that fell within the amirate of Adud al-Dawla ibn Buwayh, whose power was then at its zenith in Iraq and Fars {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1018}.
