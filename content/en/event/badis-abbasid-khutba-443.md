## Overview
In 443 AH [[person/al-muizz-ibn-badis|al-Mu'izz ibn Badis]] ended the sermon for the Ubaydids in the Maghrib and preached for the Abbasids. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 644}

## What the source says
The author of "Tarikh al-Khulafa" gives the report briefly. The Gregorian date (around 1051 CE) is derived by conversion. The source does not give al-Mu'izz's motives or what followed. It needs an additional source.
