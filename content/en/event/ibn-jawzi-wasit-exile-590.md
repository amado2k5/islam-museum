## Overview
In 590/1194 the vizier Ibn Yunus was dismissed and Ibn al-Jawzi was arrested, without good reason it is said, and sent to house arrest at Wasit, where he remained in exile for five years {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 784}.
