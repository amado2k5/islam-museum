## Overview
[[person/rifaa-ibn-zayd-al-judhami|Rifa'a ibn Zayd al-Judhami]] came to the Prophet during the truce of al-Hudaybiyya before Khaybar, and became a Muslim, and the Prophet wrote him a letter to his people, and they became Muslims. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243}

## The report
Rifa'a ibn Zayd al-Judhami, then al-Dubaybi, came to the Prophet during the truce of al-Hudaybiyya before Khaybar. He gave him a slave as a gift, and he became a Muslim and his Islam was good. The Prophet wrote him a letter to his people. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243} In the letter it says that Muhammad, Messenger of God, sent Rifa'a ibn Zayd to his people, all of them and whoever joins them, calling them to God and His Messenger: whoever comes is in the party of God and His Messenger, and whoever turns away has two months' safe conduct. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243} When Rifa'a came to his people, they answered and became Muslims, then they went to [[place/harrat-al-rajla|the lava field of al-Rajla]] and settled there. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243}

## Notes on the report
- Ibn Ishaq places the account among the delegations, although it is before Khaybar; the date is derived from the mention of the truce. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/243}
