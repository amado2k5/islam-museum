## Summary
Ali narrates that the Prophet instructed him to command the people to give charity before speaking privately with him; he gave a dinar, then half a dinar, then a barleycorn's weight of gold, so the Prophet said, "You are indeed frugal," whereupon the alleviation was revealed, and Ali said, "Through me, God lightened the burden on this community" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}.

## The Report
Al-Sallabi mentions this report in the section on the Prophet's interpretation of certain Qur'anic verses. Ali says the Prophet ordered him to command the people to give charity before consulting him privately, so he asked, "How much?" The Prophet said, "A dinar." He said, "They cannot afford it." He said, "Half a dinar." He said, "They cannot afford it." Then he asked him, and he said, "A barleycorn's weight" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}.

## The Alleviation
Al-Sallabi says the Prophet told Ali, "You are indeed frugal," and then the verse of private consultation was revealed in an alleviated form. The book cites Ali's saying: "Through me, God lightened the burden on this community" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}.

## Differing Gradings
The book presents the report from Sunan al-Tirmidhi, noting in the footnote that al-Albani graded it weak, while al-Tirmidhi described it as "sound but isolated" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}. The book does not decide between the two gradings, so it is a report whose status is disputed.

## Note
The book does not date the incident, so no date is established for it. Placing it in Medina remains derived from the context of the gathering.
