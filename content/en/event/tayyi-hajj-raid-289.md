## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that the Tayy rose against the Iraqi pilgrim caravan in 287 AH to seize it. The amir of the caravan, Abu al-Aghar Khalifa ibn al-Mubarak, fought them for a day and a night; [[person/salih-ibn-madrak|Salih, the amir of the Arabs]], was killed, and a great number of his people were taken captive. The pilgrims brought in the captives and the heads on lances. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.575-576}

## The rising of 289
In 289 AH the Tayy rose under their amir [[person/salih-ibn-madrak|Salih ibn Madrak]] and plundered the Iraqi caravan. Goods worth a million mithqals were lost to the pilgrims. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.575-576}

[[person/abu-al-agharr|Abu al-Aghar]] was the caravan's amir in the first affair. Al-Suyuti's text says "the year seven", and fixing the decade needs an additional source.
