## Overview
After mentioning Abu al-Qasim's stay at Bayt Bus, the book says: in the month of Shawwal he stayed at a place called [[place/arthal-yemen|Arthal]] six days, held there, they not allowing him to go to Sana'a; on the seventh day they went out to him in battle order until they met, his companions took safety with them, and they seized him, his son and his nephew and took them up to [[place/bayt-bus-sanaa-yemen|Bayt Bus]] and jailed them, and they stayed so for a time; then they seized Ibn Abi al-Khayr from the fort of Najr and jailed him too in the fort of Bayt Bus {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.253}. The book's heading shows the report is about Jaftam; the text gives no name for the subject of "stayed" apart from the heading.

## Detail
Ali ibn Muhammad then relays what the prisoners said: we never knew a night after we had slept but someone knocking at the house; we opened to him, he came in, examined the irons and looked at them at midnight and left; at dawn they brought a ring narrower than the one on Abu al-Qasim's leg and put it in its place; we stayed some days after that until al-Yafi'i reached Haraz and they feared for Bayt Bus, so they tightened Jaftam's confinement and he stayed days in irons, then they moved him and Ibn Abi al-Khayr to Zahr, and we stayed where we were {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.253}. This is what the prisoners reported, according to the narrator. The Jaftam here is the Abbasid commander whose arrival at Mecca is reported on [[event/al-hadi-at-warwar-treats-with-ibn-khalaf-and-returns-to-saada-on-news-of-jaftam-290|p. 250]], or another man of the same name; the text says neither, so the museum links him to neither the existing Jaftam record nor the Jafatim group, and logs the question in the work ledger. Before this is [[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-is-chained-at-sanaa-and-moved-to-bayt-bus-290|the chaining of Abu al-Qasim and his move to Bayt Bus]]; after it, [[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-composes-verses-in-prison-290|the verses of Abu al-Qasim in prison]]. Ibn Abi al-Khayr of the fort of Najr appears in an earlier report as one of the two sons of Abu al-Khayr; the text gives no name here, so he has no record. The year 290 AH is an estimate from the order.
