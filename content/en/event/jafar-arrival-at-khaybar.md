## Summary
At the opening of the chapter on Ja'far's arrival, Ibn Hisham relates a report about [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]] when he came to the Prophet at Khaybar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}

## The report
The report's chain in Ibn Hisham: Sufyan ibn Uyayna from al-Ajlah from al-Sha'bi; he prefaces it with "Ibn Hisham said", so it is his addition to Ibn Ishaq's account. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}

The report says Ja'far came to the Prophet on the day [[place/khaybar|Khaybar]] fell; the Prophet kissed him between the eyes and embraced him, saying: "I know not which makes me gladder — the conquest of Khaybar or the coming of Ja'far." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}

## Its wider context
The report joins a broader context: Ja'far arrived with the emigrants who came in two ships after al-Hudaybiyya, while the Prophet was at Khaybar, as Ibn Ishaq relates directly after this report. See [[event/emigrants-carried-in-two-ships|the Negus carrying the emigrants in two ships]] and [[event/return-of-abyssinian-emigrants|the coming of the Abyssinian emigrants]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/3}
