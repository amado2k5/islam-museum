## Overview
In 971 floods — a great one among them — entered the Sacred Haram of Mecca and rose a cubit over the Yemeni Corner; the adib Salah al-Din al-Qurashi coined its chronogram: "O asker after the date of the swallowing flood — it rose a cubit over the Yemeni Corner" {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 358-359}.
