## Overview
In 276 word came that a mound on Nahr al-Sarat called Tall Bani Shaqiq had split open, revealing a stone slab carved with unreadable writing and seven graves holding seven intact bodies in fresh shrouds breathing the scent of musk — one bearing a wound's trace in his side, his lip moist as though he had just drunk water {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 100}.
