## Overview
In 121 AH al-Tabari mentions the rising of Zayd ibn Ali. Before that he had disputed with the sons of al-Hasan before Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik in al-Rusafa. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.161}
