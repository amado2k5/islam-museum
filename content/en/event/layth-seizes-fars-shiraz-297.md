## Summary
In 297 [[person/al-layth-ibn-ali-al-saffar|al-Layth ibn Ali ibn al-Layth]] the Saffarid marched from Sistan into [[place/fars|Fars]] and seized it; its governor Subkari fled to Arrajan, and the caliph [[person/al-muqtadir-billah|al-Muqtadir]] sent an army under [[person/muanis-al-muzaffar|Mu'nis al-Khadim]], which recovered the province and took al-Layth prisoner. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 44-45}

## Al-Layth takes Fars
Al-Layth ibn Ali ibn al-Layth marched from Sistan to Fars, took it and gained possession of it, while Subkari fled from it to Arrajan. When the news reached al-Muqtadir he equipped Mu'nis al-Khadim and sent him to Fars in support of Subkari, and the two joined forces at Arrajan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 44}

## The Shiraz force
Al-Layth sent his brother with part of the army to [[place/shiraz|Shiraz]] to hold it, then took the rest of his troops by a shortcut to meet al-Husayn ibn Hamdan. The force sent to Shiraz caught sight of Mu'nis's army; Mu'nis and Subkari fell on them with their troops, and after hard fighting al-Layth's army broke and he was taken prisoner. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 44-45}

## To Baghdad
Mu'nis's companions urged him to seize Subkari and take Fars for himself; instead Mu'nis secretly warned Subkari and ordered him to set out that very night for Shiraz, which he did. Mu'nis then returned to Baghdad with al-Layth in his custody. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 45}
