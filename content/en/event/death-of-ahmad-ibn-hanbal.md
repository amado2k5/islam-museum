## Summary
[[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]] died in Baghdad on a Friday in the year 241, aged seventy-seven and some days according to Abbas al-Duri. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 327} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 329} In his entry in the [[work/tarikh-dimashq|History of Damascus]], Ibn Asakir gathers many reports on the day of his death and on his funeral. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 326} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 335}

## The disagreement over the month
- Twelve nights into Rabi' al-Awwal: the report of [[person/hanbal-ibn-ishaq|Hanbal ibn Ishaq]], Abbas al-Duri and Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Hadrami. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 327}
- Twelve nights into Rabi' al-Akhir: the statement of his son Abd Allah, and the reports of al-Fadl ibn Ziyad, Ya'qub ibn Sufyan and Abd al-Baqi ibn Qani'. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 328} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 329} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 330}
- Thirteen nights before the end of Rabi' al-Akhir: the report of Nasr ibn al-Qasim al-Fara'idi. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 328}
- Abu Umayya Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Tarsusi said 242; Ibn Asakir notes that no one followed him in this. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 329}

On his age there are two reports: seventy-seven years according to most, and seventy-eight in Abd Allah's report. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 328} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 329}

## The funeral
Abd Allah ibn Ahmad said: my father died on Friday in the forenoon and we buried him after the afternoon prayer; [[person/muhammad-ibn-abd-allah-ibn-tahir|Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Tahir]] led the prayer over him, overruling us, though we and the Hashimites had already prayed over him inside the house. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 328} Bayan ibn Ahmad al-Qasabani reported that the bier was set down in the open ground of Abu Qirat, and that Ibn Tahir had those praying behind him counted: they were 800,000 men and 60,000 women, while those who prayed the afternoon prayer in the Rusafa mosque were some twenty thousand. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 331} In another report from him the rows stretched from the Maydan to the bridge of Rab' al-Qati'a. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 334} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 335}

**Other estimates.** Fath ibn al-Hajjaj reported that the governor sent twenty men who estimated the crowd at 1,080,000, not counting those in boats; another report says the Commander of the Faithful sent estimators who reached 1,300,000; and Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami reported 1,700,000, not counting those in boats. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 332} {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 333} Al-Sulami reported from al-Daraqutni, through a chain to Ahmad, that Ahmad used to say: tell the people of innovation, between us and you is the day of the funerals. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 332} Abd al-Wahhab al-Warraq said: we never heard of a larger gathering of Muslims than at Ahmad's funeral. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 332}

**The report of twenty thousand converts.** Ibn Abi Hatim relates through his chain from al-Warkani, Ahmad's neighbour, that on the day Ahmad died twenty thousand Jews, Christians and Magians embraced Islam, and that mourning broke out among four kinds of people. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 333} The edition's editor notes that al-Dhahabi, in *Siyar A'lam al-Nubala'*, called it an objectionable story and showed that al-Warkani died thirteen years before Ahmad. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 333}

**Dreams.** Ibn Asakir relates dreams reported after his death, among them that the brother of a man killed at Qazwin saw him saying God had ordered the martyrs to attend Ahmad's funeral; they noted the night, and it was the night of his death. {cite:tarikh-dimashq-amrawi-v5 p. 331} Such reports are transmitted in the literature of virtues with their chains, and the museum gives them attributed to their narrators.
