## Overview
Abu al-Ashira Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ruwaya went to Tha'at and Rada'. Ibn Dhi al-Tawq and Isa al-Yafi'i fought him at Tha'at, Abu al-Ashira was killed, the town was plundered, and the people withdrew into the mosque. The sentence breaks off at the foot of the page.

## Detail
Abu al-Ashira Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ruwaya went to Tha'at and Rada'. A group of his kin rallied to him and a large force of the people of the town joined him. Ibn Dhi al-Tawq and Isa al-Yafi'i marched against him and fought him at Tha'at, and they took Tha'at. Abu al-Ashira ibn al-Ruwaya was killed, the town was plundered, and the people withdrew into the mosque. The sentence that tells what was done to those inside the mosque breaks off at the foot of printed page 392 and continues on page 393, which is the start of the next packet. The record does not complete it here.
