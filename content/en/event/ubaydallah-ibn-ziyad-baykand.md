## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that after the death of Ziyad, Mu'awiya appointed [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]] over Khurasan at the age of twenty-five. He crossed the river with twenty-four thousand men and came to [[place/baykand|Baykand]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The campaign
The [[person/khatun-of-bukhara|Khatun]] was in Bukhara; she called the Turks for help, and the Muslims defeated them. Then she asked for peace and safety, and he made peace with her for a million, entered the city, and took Ramadin and Baykand. It is said that he also took al-Saghaniyan, and came to Basra with a number of the people of Bukhara and assigned them stipends. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
