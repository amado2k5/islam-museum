## Overview
The narrator says [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stayed at [[place/saada|Sa'da]] until the day of Sunday, two nights gone of Ramadan, and sent criers to Sa'd Khawlan, who gathered to him in the square of Sa'da. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.193} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} He went out to them and ordered a party of Futaymi horse, a party of troops and foot of the Hayyiyun and the Hamzis, put [[person/abu-turab-muhammad-ibn-al-abbas-al-alawi|Abu Turab Muhammad ibn al-Abbas al-Alawi]] over them, and ordered him to go by a road he named until he entered [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]] from its top; al-Hadi went back to Sa'da and spent the night there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} In the morning he ordered the people to go out and make ready for war, a great army gathered to him, and he went on to al-Buq'a, drew up his army as right wing, left wing and centre, then went to a place called al-Ghurayb and halted the army, and went forward with four horsemen until he was near [[place/al-sabra-allaf-yemen|al-Sabra]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194}

## Detail
There he found the enemy had drawn up their army to one side of the mountain above them; he studied them and their dispositions, went back to his companions and said there was no way round them, and one who knew the country said they were in rough ground that could not be mastered, and he said there was no way round them, by God's power and strength. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} He called out to [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]], "Give me yourselves for an hour," and they said they would, son of the Messenger of God; then he drew up his army with the [[group/al-hamziyyun-saada|Hamzis]] on the right, the [[group/banu-hayy-sad-khawlan|Hayyiyun]] and the [[group/al-yarsamiyyun-saada|Yarsamis]] on the left, and himself with the Tabaris and the Hamdanis, drew near the enemy and told the Tabaris to stand with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} [[person/muhammad-ibn-musbih-al-yarsami|Muhammad ibn Musbih al-Yarsami]] came to him and said he was leading his men into peril while the enemy had the upper hand and he feared they would be struck, so he should look to their case and not lead them into harm; al-Hadi said no, they would soon gladden him, God Most High willing. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.194} All these speeches are the narrator's, reported as he gives them. A gloss notes that the original has al-Abshiyyin where the text reads al-Hayyin and that it was corrected from manuscript S. The Sunday of 2 Ramadan is the book's wording and agrees with the tabular calendar. It follows [[event/raid-on-afqayn-and-the-return-of-the-fifth-to-the-mahadhir-287]] and is followed by [[event/the-ascent-of-allaf-and-the-fall-of-al-numays-287]].
