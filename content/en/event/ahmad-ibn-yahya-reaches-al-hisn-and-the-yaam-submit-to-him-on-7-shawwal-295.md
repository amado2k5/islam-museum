## Overview
On 7 Shawwal 295 al-Hadi sent his son Ahmad ibn Yahya ahead with three hundred foot and twenty horse, and ordered him to take position at al-Hisn. The Ya'am came to him in submission and he granted them safety, settling the affairs of Hamdan before al-Hadi arrived.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi at once ordered the prince Ahmad ibn Yahya, with three hundred foot and twenty horsemen, to go ahead of him and take position at al-Hisn. This was on 7 Shawwal 295 AH. Ahmad and Ali ibn Muhammad went out and reached the village of al-Hisn. When the Ya'am saw this, they came with their obedience to Abu al-Hasan Ahmad ibn al-Hadi, who granted them safety and settled the affairs of Hamdan for al-Hadi's coming.
