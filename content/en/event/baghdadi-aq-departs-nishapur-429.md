## Summary
Abu Mansur al-Baghdadi left Nishapur because of the Turkmen disturbance that struck it — it was said that among the calamities of Nishapur was that a man like him was forced to leave — departing for Isfarayin in the days of the Turkmen ascendancy in 429/1038, the year the Seljuks entered the city {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 238} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 635} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 265}.
