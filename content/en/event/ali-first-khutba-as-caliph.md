## Summary
Ali delivered his first sermon of his caliphate after receiving the pledge of allegiance. In it, he called for acting upon the guiding Book of God and fulfilling religious obligations, forbade harming a Muslim except by right, warned against heedlessness of death, and said: "When you see good, take hold of it, and when you see evil, leave it" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 192}.

## Content of the Sermon
al-Sallabi notes that the sermon addressed creed, worship, and ethics, and focused on certain objectives of Islamic law, including that the inviolability of a Muslim stands above all other sanctities {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 192}. Part of its text reads: "God has sent down a guiding Book in which He made clear good and evil; so take the good and leave the evil" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 192}. Ali urged the people to piety and swift action before death, saying that people are ahead of them and the Hour is behind them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 192}.

## What al-Sallabi Concludes
al-Sallabi summarizes the sermon as a call to return to the era in which the people lived during the time of the Prophet and the Rightly Guided Caliphs before him, and notes that Ali indicated the path by which they should enter the new era of the caliphate {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 193}. The book views the sermon as part of demonstrating Ali's commitment to sound management of the transitional phase following the civil strife {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 193}.
