## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the king of Khwarazm was weak, and that his brother [[person/khurrazad|Khurrazad]] opposed him and grew strong against him. He sent to [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]] that he would give him money and hand over the keys, on condition that he make him king of his lands rather than his brother. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} The event took place in [[place/khwarizm|Khwarizm]].

## The cities
He says that Khwarizm has three cities surrounded by walls, the strongest of which is the City of the Elephant. Ali ibn Mujahid said that the City of the Elephant is Samarqand. The king went to the strongest of the cities and sent the money to Qutayba. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
