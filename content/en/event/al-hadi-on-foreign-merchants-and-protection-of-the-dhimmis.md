## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad relates from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]] that he collected merchants' zakat for [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]; visiting merchants who traded and stayed some months in the town led him to ask whether to take it from them, and al-Hadi said: "If we take the zakat of their property we are bound to protect them wherever they are, in our land and elsewhere", so he took nothing from them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.62} This is the imam's view as his narrator gives it and is shown without comparison. See [[concept/zakat]].

## Jews and Christians
He relates that he saw a Jew come to al-Hadi to complain against a man; al-Hadi told the narrator to deal justly with him and look into what lay between them, and then told the Jews and Christians: if anyone harms you, return to me so that I may do you justice against him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.62} See [[concept/ahl-al-dhimma]]. The museum attributes the saying to its narrator and does not state it as a general policy.
