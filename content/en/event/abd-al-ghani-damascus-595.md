## Summary
In 595 AH there was a dispute in Damascus involving the hadith master Abd al-Ghani al-Maqdisi, a known man of good works who called people to the Sunna. The Ash'aris opposed him and issued rulings for his killing, so he was driven out of Damascus as a fugitive {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 799}.
