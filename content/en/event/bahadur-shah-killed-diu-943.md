## Overview
On the third of Ramadan 943 Sultan Bahadur b. Sultan Muzaffar, lord of Gujarat in India, was killed at the port of Diu; the chronogram of his death ran "qutila sultanu-na Bahadur" — our Sultan Bahadur was killed {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 668}.
