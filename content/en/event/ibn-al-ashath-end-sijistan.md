## Overview
[[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-muhammad-ibn-al-ashath|Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath]] broke away from the allegiance of Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan and al-Hajjaj, went out to [[place/sijistan|Sistan]], and his affair ended there, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
Ibn al-Ash'ath made a truce with [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] and went to him. Then Rutbil handed him over, for fear of al-Hajjaj, who had written to him threatening him. Ibn al-Ash'ath threw himself from a mountain, or it is said from a roof, and those who had been guarding him, having chained themselves to him, fell with him, and he died. His head was brought to al-Hajjaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
Al-Hajjaj made peace with Rutbil on condition that he would not raid him for seven years, or it is said nine, after which he would pay every year nine hundred thousand dirhams in goods. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
