## Overview
Shams al-Din Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Ayyub ibn Sa'd ibn Hurayz al-Zur'i al-Dimashqi, known as Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya, was born at Damascus on 7 Safar 691/29 January 1292; his father was the superintendent (qayyim) of the Jawziyya madrasa, which served as a court of law for the Hanbali qadi al-qudat of Damascus {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 853}; Ibn Hajar gives his birth year as 691 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/400}.
