## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] upheld the holy war in his days, and great conquests were made in his caliphate. He describes him as a tyrannical oppressor; this is al-Suyuti's judgement, not the museum's. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.367}

## What he imposed for the weak
- He had orphans circumcised and appointed teachers for them.
- He appointed attendants for the disabled, and guides for the blind.
- He gave stipends to the jurists, the poor and the weak, forbade them to beg from people, and imposed for them what would suffice. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.367}

The date is in his caliphate (86–96 AH), and the conversion is derived.
