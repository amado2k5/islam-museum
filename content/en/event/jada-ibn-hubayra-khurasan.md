## Summary
After Ali returned from Siffin in 37 AH, he sent Ju'da ibn Hubayra al-Makhzumi to Khurasan. He reached Nishapur, where the people had rebelled and refused to submit, so he turned back. Ali then sent Khulayd ibn Qurra al-Yarbu'i, who besieged its people until they made terms with him, and the people of Merv also made terms with him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 678}.
