## Summary
The long 2017 debate between Muhammad Hijab and Ali al-Shobayri: Qur'anic preservation, the Companions and the Imamate. {cite:youtube-hijab-shobayri}

## The line of the debate
Three disciplined axes, with moderated exchange and a circulating digital transcript for the English-speaking audience.

## The exchange — what each side said
- **Hijab**: took the three questions in order — Qur'anic preservation (practical mass transmission and the rasm), Companion uprightness (a rule of transmission, not personal praise), and imamate (merit-texts do not designate office) — demanding the equivalent designation text.
- **Al-Shobayri**: defended the imamate with the Ahl al-Bayt texts, and the Shia stance on the Companions through the rules of accreditation and discreditation, and put his objections to textual preservation via the readings.
- **Hijab (rebuttal)**: rebuilt the common ground: what is established of the Ahl al-Bayt's merit is accepted in Sunni Islam — the dispute is the doctrinal grade of imamate; and he separated the corruption question from the canonical readings.
- **Al-Shobayri (rebuttal)**: held that recorded history removes absolute uprightness, and that imamate is a designated root.
- **The verdict**: the most-watched English-language encounter on the file — its transcripts each side's reference on the three sections. {cite:youtube-hijab-shobayri-2017}
