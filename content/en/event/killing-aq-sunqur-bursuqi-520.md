## Overview
In 520 AH the Batiniyya killed [[person/aq-sunqur-bursuqi|Aq Sunqur al-Bursuqi]], the amir of [[place/mosul|Mosul]], while he was praying in its Great Mosque. He had been occupied with fighting the Crusaders. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.35}

## Ibn al-Athir's description
Al-Salabi, quoting Ibn al-Athir, says that he was a Turkish mamluk who loved the people of knowledge and the righteous, saw justice and practised it, and kept the prayers at their times. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.35}
