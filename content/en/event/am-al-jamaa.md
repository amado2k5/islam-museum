## Overview
The Year of Unity (Am al-Jama'a) is 41 AH, when [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|al-Hasan]] stepped down from the caliphate in favour of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]]. Mu'awiya took up the caliphate in Rabi' al-Akhir or Jumada al-Ula. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.325}

## The name
The year was so called because the community gathered in it under one caliph. In it, Mu'awiya appointed Marwan ibn al-Hakam over Medina. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.325}

## Note
al-Suyuti does not affirm the month. The Gregorian date is derived and rests on a single source. It needs an additional source.
