## Overview
al-Suyuti reports that in Rabi' al-Akhir 359 AH the people of Egypt were permitted to call "come to the best of works" (hayya 'ala khayr al-'amal) and began building the Al-Azhar Mosque, which was completed in Ramadan 361 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 621}
