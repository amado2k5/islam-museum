## Overview
In 625/1228 Sultan Shams al-Din Iltutmish of Delhi defeated Nasir al-Din Qabacha, and Multan became an integral part of the Delhi Sultanate {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## The end of Qabacha's state
Qabacha had ruled an independent state in the region since his master's assassination, but he came out of his 621 encounter with Jalal al-Din Khwarazm-Shah weakened; Iltutmish then defeated him outright {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## What annexation meant
Multan's entry into the Delhi Sultanate closed the contest between the Ghurid mamluks over Sind and the Panjab; the city became a province of Delhi, its governors rotating as raids and rebellions swept its marches {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
