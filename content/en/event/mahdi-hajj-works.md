## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that in 161 AH [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] ordered the road to Mecca to be built up, and he built palaces on it and made cisterns. He ordered that screens be removed from the mosques of Islam and that the pulpits be shortened to the size of the Prophet's pulpit. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.442}

## His pilgrimage in 160 AH
Al-Mahdi performed the pilgrimage in 160 AH, and the keepers of the Ka'ba told him that they feared its collapse from the many curtains on it. He ordered them stripped, and kept only his own covering. Snow was carried to Mecca for him, and al-Suyuti quotes al-Dhahabi saying that this had never been arranged for any king. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.442}
