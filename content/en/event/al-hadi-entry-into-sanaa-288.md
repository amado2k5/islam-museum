## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] entered [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] on the night of Friday, 21 Muharram 288 AH, after [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] had invited him and sworn allegiance to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.17} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18}

## What he did after entering
He called to the Book and the sunna, the address from the pulpits was given in his name as imam, he ordered his name written on the coinage and the tiraz, appointed governors of the districts and collection of the tithes, zakat and jizya, and appointed as judge [[person/muhammad-ibn-ahmad-ibn-zurayq-al-asam|Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Zurayq al-A'sam]], a client of the Banu al-Abbas, who judged according to his school. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18}

## What followed
Al-Hadi then went out to Yahsub, Ra'in and those districts to set them in order, leaving his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] as deputy at Sana'a, and reached Jayshan, where Abu al-Ushayra ibn al-Ruwayya met him in hearing and obedience. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} He returned to Sana'a, stayed some days and went to Shibam, leaving his cousin [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] as deputy at Sana'a. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18}

## A note on these pages
In a footnote the editor says all the pages before Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi's account, that is pp. 17 to 20, appear to have been added to the original; in his view they are not from the first narrator's book. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20}
