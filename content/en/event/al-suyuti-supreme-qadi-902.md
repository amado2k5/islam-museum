## Overview
In 902/1496 al-Suyuti succeeded in having himself nominated "supreme qadi" by the Abbasid caliph al-Mutawakkil in Cairo, but the caliph had to retract when faced with the hostility of the ʿulamaʾ. For al-Suyuti, the Abbasid caliphs were the incarnation of legitimacy, the best guarantors of the revealed Law and the prophetic Sunna {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
