## Overview
During the Prophet's last illness, some of his wives and women of the Muslims, among them [[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma bint Umays]], were with him, and [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas]] was present. They agreed to give him a purgative, and al-Abbas said: I will give it to him. So they gave it to him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/300}

## The Prophet's words on recovering
When he recovered he said: who did this to me? They said: your uncle. He said: this is a medicine that women brought from the direction of this land, and he pointed toward the land of Abyssinia. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/301} Al-Abbas said: we feared, Messenger of God, that you had pleurisy. He said: that is a disease God would never cast upon me. No one in the house is to be given the purgative except my uncle; and [[person/maymuna-bint-al-harith|Maymuna]] was given it while she was fasting, as a punishment for what they had done to him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/301}

## Marks of the report
- The report is from Ibn Ishaq with a chain to Aisha in her transmission; the word "it is said" does not appear in Ibn Hisham's text. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/300}
- The phrase "that they should purge him" means the medicine is poured into the side of the mouth, as the editors of the edition explain. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/300}
