## Overview
The capitulation of Jundishapur: the Muslims marched from Sus and camped at Jundishapur where Zirr b. 'Abd Allah was besieging its people; al-Muqtarib b. Rabi'a joined him after Abu Musa's march to Basra, and they made terms {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 372}.
