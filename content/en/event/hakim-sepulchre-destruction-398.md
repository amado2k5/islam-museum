## Overview
In 398 AH [[person/al-hakim-bi-amr-allah|al-Hakim]] demolished the Church of the Holy Sepulchre in Jerusalem and all the churches in Egypt, according to al-Suyuti. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.637-638}

## Measures against the People of the Book
He ordered the Christians to hang crosses around their necks, each a cubit long and weighing five Egyptian pounds. He ordered the Jews to carry wooden pieces of the same weight, and to wear black turbans. A group of them became Muslims. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.637-638}

## Afterwards
Al-Hakam then permitted the rebuilding of the churches, and allowed those who had become Muslim under compulsion to return to their religion. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.637-638}
