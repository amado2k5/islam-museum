## Overview
Uthman ibn Affan was killed in 35 AH; the index of al-Tabari has the heading "The account of the killing of Uthman". {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.582} After his killing the people came to Ali, who refused at first, then the common people gave him their allegiance. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.433}

## The siege in al-Suyuti
The people of Egypt complained of their governor Ibn Abi Sarh, and they advised Uthman to appoint Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr, and he wrote his commission. Then a slave appeared leading a camel with a letter under Uthman's seal to the governor of Egypt. They turned back and besieged him, and prevented him from water until Ali sent him three waterskins. Al-Suyuti says that Uthman swore that he had not written the letter, and that they recognised the handwriting as Marwan's. Al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba advised him in three matters, but he refused to be the first to succeed the Messenger of God by shedding blood. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.271-276}

He says that the killing took place in the middle of the days of Tashriq in 35 AH, and it is said on Friday, the eighteenth of Dhu al-Hijja. He was buried on the night of Saturday in Hash Kawkab in al-Baqi'. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.274-277}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi describes delegations from Kufa, Basra and Egypt camping at Dhu Khashab. They then seized a slave of Uthman's who had a letter in Marwan's hand, and returned and besieged him for forty-nine days. He says that the sons of the Companions defended him at the door, then two men entered and killed him while the Qur'an was before him; it is said they were Kinana ibn Bishr and Sa'd ibn Hamran. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.637-639}

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says Uthman was killed in the year 35 on a Friday a few days before the end of Dhu al-Hijja; Abu Uthman al-Nahdi says in the middle of the days of Tashriq, and Abu Ma'shar, from Nafi', on Friday the seventeenth or eighteenth of Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 176} That year Ibn Abbas led the pilgrimage at Uthman's order. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 176}
