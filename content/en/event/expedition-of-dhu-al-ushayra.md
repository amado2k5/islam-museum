## Summary
In late Jumada al-Ula 2 AH (November 623 CE) the Prophet went out with a hundred and fifty (some say two hundred) of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]] to intercept a Quraysh caravan bound for al-Sham, which carried great wealth belonging to the people of Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}

## The march and the banner
The Prophet left [[person/abu-salama|Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi]] in charge of [[place/medina|Medina]]. The banner was white and was carried by [[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.200} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/85}

The Prophet reached the place of [[place/dhu-al-ushayra|Dhu al-Ushayra]] in the district of [[place/yanbu|Yanbu]] and found that the caravan had passed him by days. It was the same caravan he had gone out after on its return from al-Sham, and it was the direct cause of the great Battle of Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199}

## The treaty with Banu Mudlij
On this expedition the Prophet concluded a treaty of non-aggression and truce with [[group/banu-mudlij|Banu Mudlij]] and their allies from [[group/banu-damra|Banu Damra]], and returned to Medina in early Jumada al-Akhira without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/599}


## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.197–200)
In Jumada al-Ula and Jumada al-Akhira of the second year, the Prophet went out with some hundred and fifty of the Muhajirun to intercept a Quraysh caravan bound for al-Sham. He reached Dhu al-Ushayra and found that the caravan had got away by days. There he made a pact of non-aggression with Banu Mudlij and their allies from Banu Damra, and he left Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad in charge. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.199} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.200}.
