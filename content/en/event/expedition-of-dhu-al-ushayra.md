## Summary
In late Jumada al-Ula 2 AH (November 623 CE) the Prophet went out with a hundred and fifty (or two hundred, it is said) of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]] to intercept a Quraysh caravan bound for al-Sham carrying great wealth of the people of Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199}

## The march and the banner
The Prophet left [[person/abu-salama|Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi]] in charge of [[place/medina|Medina]]; the banner was white, carried by [[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/85}

The Prophet reached the place of [[place/dhu-al-ushayra|Dhu al-Ushayra]] in the district of [[place/yanbu|Yanbu]] and found the caravan had passed him by days; it was the same caravan he would later go out after on its return from al-Sham — the direct cause of the great battle of Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199}

## The treaty with Banu Mudlij
On this expedition the Prophet concluded a treaty of non-aggression and truce with [[group/banu-mudlij|Banu Mudlij]] and their allies of [[group/banu-damra|Banu Damra]], and returned to Medina in early Jumada al-Akhira without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 199} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/599}
