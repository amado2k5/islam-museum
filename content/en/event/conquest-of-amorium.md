## Overview
al-Mu'tasim set out on a campaign to the land of the Byzantines in 223 AH, after he had killed Babak. It is also said 224 AH or 222 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.57} The following accounts agree that he took Amorium.

## al-Suyuti's account
al-Mu'tasim took it by the sword, killed thirty thousand in it and took the same number captive. al-Suyuti says that the astrologers had judged the omen to be unlucky, so the victory came contrary to what they had said, and that Abu Tammam said on this: "The sword is more truthful than books." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 522-523}

## al-Mas'udi's account
al-Mas'udi says that al-Mu'tasim encamped on the west bank of the Tigris on Monday, two nights into Jumada al-Ula 223 AH, with Ashnas at the vanguard, Itakh on the right wing and Ja'far ibn Dinar the tailor on the left. He entered by the Road of Safety, and al-Afshin by the Road of al-Hadath, and he defeated the king. The patrician Lawi came out to him and handed Amorium over to him, and al-Mu'tasim stayed four days, demolishing and burning. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 449-450}
