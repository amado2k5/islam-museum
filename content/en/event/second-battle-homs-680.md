## The second battle of Homs
In 680 AH the great battle of Homs against the Tatars took place, and a group of amirs and scholars were martyred in it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 643-644}.

Among them was the amir ʿIzz al-Din Uzdamar al-Jamdar, deputy of the sultanate in Damascus for Sunqur al-Ashqar. He had knowledge and virtue and many noble qualities. He was martyred at Homs, advancing and not retreating, at about fifty years of age {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 643}.

Also among them was Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Maktum al-Baʿli, a learned and gifted man of letters, who was martyred in the battle of Homs {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 644}.
