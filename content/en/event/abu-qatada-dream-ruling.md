## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Abu Qatada ibn Rib'i that the Prophet said "the good dream is from God, and the bad dream is from the shaytan" {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2750|Muwatta 31:2750]].

## The instruction
The Prophet instructed whoever sees a dream to seek refuge in God, spit on his left three times, and avoid talking about it {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}. This instruction is among the proofs that the shaytan has a share in false dreams {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.

## Notes
This hadith belongs to the chapters of dreams in the Sunan and the Muwatta; this browser shows it in both the dreams and devils sections because it joins them {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
