## Overview
In 877/1472 al-Suyuti obtained the post of teacher of hadith at the Shaykhuniyya; though nominated by his teacher al-Kafiyaji, he seems to have owed the appointment also to the support of a Mamluk amir {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
