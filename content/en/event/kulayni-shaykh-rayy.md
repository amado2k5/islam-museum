## Summary
al-Kulayni transmitted from several scholars of [[place/rayy|Rayy]], among them his maternal uncle [[person/muhammad-ibn-ali-alan|Muhammad ibn Ali al-Kulayni]], known as 'Alan, and al-Najashi describes him as the shaykh of the Imamiyya in Rayy in his time {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 400-401}.

## Hearing al-Naysaburi
He heard [[person/muhammad-ibn-ismail-al-naysaburi|Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Naysaburi]], transmitter of the traditions and views of [[person/al-fadl-ibn-shadhan|al-Fadl ibn Shadhan]], whom he evidently held in high esteem — whether at Nishapur or elsewhere is unknown {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 400-401}.
