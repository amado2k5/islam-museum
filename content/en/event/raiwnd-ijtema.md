## Overview
The annual gathering of the Tablighi Jamaat near [[place/lahore|Lahore]] at its world centre: hundreds of thousands spend days sleeping in tents, training in the call. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
The largest Tablighi gathering in Pakistan; copies branch out from it in Tajikistan and Africa. {cite:wikidata}
