## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Tasm settled between Yemen and al-Yamama, and Jadis settled at [[place/al-yamama|al-Yamama]], and was known as Jaww. There were wars between them in which Jadis destroyed most of Tasm, so that it was said: "O Tasm, what you met at the hands of Jadis!" {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.8}

## Their end
The remainder of Tasm joined Jadis in al-Yamama. Then Tuba' came from Yemen, and Abd Kulal ibn Mathub came, and killed Tasm and Jadis at al-Yamama. He crucified a woman of Jadis called al-Yamama, daughter of Marr, at the gate of Jaww, and Jaww was named after her. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.8}

## Another account
It is said that Jadis refused a tribute that was owed by them, so Tasm was seized for their wrongdoing. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.8}

There is no verified date for the event, which is among the reports of the time of the Tubba'. {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p.8}
