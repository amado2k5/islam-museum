## Overview
"Then he left for Khiwan on Sunday, sixteen days into Rajab." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} The book's calendar makes Sunday the sixteenth of Rajab 286 AH, while the tabular calendar puts the sixteenth on a Saturday, a one-day difference like those in earlier batches; the date is given to the month. The account is Zaydi.

## The charge
"When he wished to leave for Khiwan he charged [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] to hold Sa'da, to look after the affairs of his flock, to keep those jailed at al-Ghayl of the Harithis and to inspect their irons every two days; they were held in a spacious house, not confined, and as it had an upper storey they asked to be put in it and he put them in it." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} The Harithis are those jailed at [[place/al-ghayl-saada|al-Ghayl]] in [[event/al-hadi-seventeen-days-at-najran-and-the-prisoners-sent-to-al-ghayl-286]]; the book gives their tribe here only as the nisba.

## The road to Khiwan
"Al-Hadi left, and the people were anxious for themselves about the rain on the road, for they left in the season of rain; and he left and no rain or harm touched the people, by God's gift, generosity and kindness, until he entered Khiwan." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} "He arrived at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] on the eighteenth of Rajab." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.134} The narrator says that when he arrived and the people were in their quarters the rain fell on them; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.133} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.134} the margin of the manuscript reads "a karama". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.134} It is a report the narrator ascribes to the imam; the museum neither confirms nor denies it.

## Notes
The book calls the prisoners "the Harithis"; the name al-Harith ibn Ka'b does not occur in these pages. Al-Hadi left on the sixteenth and arrived on the eighteenth by the book's reckoning.
