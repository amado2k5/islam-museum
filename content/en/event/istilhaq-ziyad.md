## Overview
[[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] claimed [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]] as his kinsman and joined him to the lineage of Abu Sufyan, in 43 AH, or it is said 44 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.325}

## What al-Suyuti reports
Al-Suyuti, quoting al-Tha'alibi and others, says that this was the first case in Islam in which the Prophet's ruling was changed. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.325}

The judgement on the event here is attributed to those named. The details of the disagreement over it need an additional source.
