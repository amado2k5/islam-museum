## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates from Ibn al-Kalbi that [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]] appointed [[person/kulthum-ibn-iyad-al-qushayri|Kulthum ibn Iyad al-Qushayri]] over [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]]. Its people rose against him, and he was killed there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The event belongs to Hisham's reign, and the Gregorian date is derived. What is not covered here: the details of the revolt and the killing. This needs an additional source.

## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports that Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik sent Kulthum ibn Iyad al-Qaysi to Ifriqiya in Jumada al-Akhira 123, with Balj ibn Bishr ahead of him. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 294} When he met Habib ibn Abi Ubayda he slighted him, and when Habib advised him on fighting the Berbers he said, "How little we need your opinion, son of Umm Habib." {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 295} He sent Balj with the cavalry, who rode through the night and struck the Berbers at dawn; then the Berber foot advanced until they were among Kulthum and his men, and Kulthum, Habib and those with them were killed, while Balj ibn Bishr, Tha'laba al-Judhami and the Syrians with them fled. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 295} {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 296} Ibn Abd al-Hakam dates Kulthum's death to the year 123. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 296}
