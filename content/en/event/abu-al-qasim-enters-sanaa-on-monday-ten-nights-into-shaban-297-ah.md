## Overview
Al-Hadi sends his son Abu al-Qasim with men of Khawlan and Hamdan. He enters Sanaa on Monday, ten nights into Sha'ban 297 AH, stays there, and several districts come under him. The book says a number of Qarmatian preachers were killed.

## Detail
The book says Abu al-Qasim, son of al-Hadi, came to Sanaa with men of Khawlan and Hamdan on Monday, ten nights into Sha'ban 297 AH. He stayed in the city and sent to the districts, which submitted. A number of Qarmatian preachers were killed, the tribes were secured and the population won over. Ibn Kayyala, then in Tihama with Muzaffar ibn Hajjaj, heard of this and came as far as Alhan, which led to the events that follow.
