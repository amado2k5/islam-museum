## Overview
The battle of al-Walaja took place in Safar 12 AH near Kaskar, on the land side. Ardashir al-Andarzghar, a Persian born in al-Sawad, was sent, and Bahman Jadhawayh was sent after him in pursuit. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.353}
