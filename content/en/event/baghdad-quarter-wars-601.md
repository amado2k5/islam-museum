## Overview
Ibn al-Athir, in "al-Kamil", relates that strife broke out in the quarters of [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] in 601 AH, in Sha'ban and Ramadan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297-298}

## The strife
On the 17th of Sha'ban fighting broke out between the people of Bab al-Azaj and the people of al-Ma'mūniyya. The people of Bab al-Azaj had killed a man and wanted to carry his body around, and the people of al-Ma'mūniyya prevented them. Many were wounded and a number killed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297-298}

On the 20th of Sha'ban strife broke out between the people of Qatufta and of al-Qarya for a similar reason. On the 9th of Ramadan the people of the Sultan's market and the people of al-Ja'fariyya quarrelled and fought in the cemetery of al-Ja'fariyya. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297-298}

## Keeping order
As the strife multiplied, the diwan appointed a senior amir from among the caliph's mamluks with a company. He went round the city and killed those who were suspected, and the people calmed down. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297-298}
