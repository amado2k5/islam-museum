## Overview
Around 1352, at about twenty years of age, [[person/ibn-khaldun|Ibn Khaldun]] was given a post at the court of [[place/tunis|Tunis]]. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}

## The path
Three years later he became secretary to the Marinid sultan in Fez. About two years after that he was imprisoned on suspicion of joining a rebellion, and he was released after nearly two years. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}

## Why it matters
These years began his service at courts that moved quickly between Tunis, Fez, Granada, Tlemcen and Bejaïa, in a period when dynasties rose and fell. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}
