## Summary
In 622 AH the Georgians marched en masse against the city of Ganja in the land of Arran to besiege it; its people held out and fought them until they departed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 443}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Georgians came in their multitudes to Ganja in Arran intending to besiege it, and exerted all their strength, for the people of Ganja were numerous, powerful and possessed great courage from long practice of war against the Georgians. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 443} When they drew near they fought the inhabitants for several days from across the wall without anyone coming out; then one day the people of Ganja came out with the troops the city had and fought the Georgians outside it in the hardest and greatest of battles, and when the Georgians saw that, they knew they had no power over the town, so they departed after the people of Ganja had inflicted heavy losses on them, as Ibn al-Athir narrates. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 443}
