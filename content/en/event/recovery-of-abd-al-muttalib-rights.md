## Summary
After the death of al-Muttalib, [[person/nawfal-ibn-abd-manaf|Nawfal ibn Abd Manaf]] seized the estate of his nephew [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]]. Abd al-Muttalib sought help from his maternal uncles, [[group/banu-al-najjar|Banu al-Najjar]]. His uncle [[person/abu-sad-ibn-adi|Abu Sa'd ibn Adi]] came with eighty riders until his right was restored. Two alliances followed: Nawfal with [[group/banu-abd-shams|Banu Abd Shams]] against [[group/banu-hashim|Banu Hashim]], and [[group/khuzaa|Khuza'a]] with Banu Hashim. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.41-42}

## The seizure
When [[person/al-muttalib-ibn-abd-manaf|al-Muttalib]] died, Nawfal leapt upon Abd al-Muttalib's estate and seized it from him. Abd al-Muttalib asked men of Quraysh to support him against his uncle, but they said: "We will not come between you and your uncle." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50}

## Support from Banu al-Najjar
Abd al-Muttalib wrote verses to his maternal uncles of Banu al-Najjar asking for help. His uncle Abu Sa'd ibn Adi set out with eighty riders and halted at al-Abtah in Mecca. Abd al-Muttalib met him and said: "Stop here, uncle!" He replied: "No, by God, not until I meet Nawfal." He then came and stood over Nawfal, who was sitting in the Hijr with the elders of Quraysh. He drew his sword and said: "By the Lord of this House, if you do not return my sister's son's estate to him, I will make this sword reach you." Nawfal said: "I have returned it to him." The elders of Quraysh were called to witness. Abu Sa'd then went down to Abd al-Muttalib, stayed with him three days, performed the umra, and returned to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50}

## The alliances
When this had happened, Nawfal allied with Banu Abd Shams ibn Abd Manaf against Banu Hashim. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} When Khuza'a saw Banu al-Najjar's support of Abd al-Muttalib, they said: "We gave birth to him as you did, so we are more entitled to support him," because the mother of Abd Manaf was one of them. They entered [[building/dar-al-nadwa|Dar al-Nadwa]] and allied with Banu Hashim against Banu Abd Shams and Nawfal. This is the alliance which the author says became a cause of the conquest of Mecca, as will come. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.50} {cite:mukhtasar-sirat-al-rasul-al-najdi p.41-42}

## Notes for researchers
The book gives no date for the event. It took place after the death of al-Muttalib and before the digging of Zamzam. The source the author refers to is Mukhtasar Sirat al-Rasul by Shaykh Abdullah al-Najdi, pp. 41-42.

## For young visitors
Abd al-Muttalib was wronged and his own people did not help him. His maternal uncles came from Medina with weapons until his right was restored, and Khuza'a and Banu Hashim allied with him.
