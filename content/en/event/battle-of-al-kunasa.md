## Overview
In 197 AH Tahir himself took part in the battle of al-Kunasa, and many of al-Amin's companions were killed in it. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.461} In the same year al-Tabari mentions the battle of Darb al-Hijara. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.463}
