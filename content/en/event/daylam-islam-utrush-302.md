## Overview
In 302 AH the Daylam, who had been Magians, became Muslims at the hand of al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Alawi al-Utrush, as al-Suyuti relates. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 588} For the missionary's own page, see [[person/hasan-al-utrush|al-Hasan al-Utrush]].

## Note
The account comes from a single source, and the Gregorian year (around 914 CE) is derived by conversion. It needs an additional source.
