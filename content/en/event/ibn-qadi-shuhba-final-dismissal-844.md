## Overview
On Monday the twelfth of Muharram 844, Siraj al-Din Umar al-Himsi was robed and restored to the chief judgeship of Damascus in place of Ibn Qadi Shuhba; al-Himsi had come to Cairo, was taken up by men of the state, restored to the post and travelled back to Damascus. {cite:suluk-maqrizi p. 1201-1202}

## After the dismissal
Ibn Qadi Shuhba remained unemployed thereafter; he devoted himself to fatwa, teaching and useful writing for many years, and most of Damascus's students profited from him until he died. {cite:nujum-zahira 15/523} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 269}
