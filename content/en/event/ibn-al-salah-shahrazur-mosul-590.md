## Summary
Ibn al-Salah read fiqh under his father Salah at Shahrazur, whose father was the district's shaykh, then was taken to Mosul where he studied a while and excelled in the madhhab. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185}

## Repeating the 'Muhadhdhab' before manhood
Ibn Khallikan reports that he repeated the whole of the 'Muhadhdhab' before his beard had grown, then served as repeater (mu'id) under the scholar al-Imad Ibn Yunus; at Mosul he heard 'Ubayd Allah b. al-Samin, Nasr Allah b. Salama al-Hayti, 'Abd al-Muhsin the khatib of Mosul and 'Abd Allah b. Abi al-Sinan. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}
