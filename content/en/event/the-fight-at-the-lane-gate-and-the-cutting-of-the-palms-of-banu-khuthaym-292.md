## Overview
Then one day al-Hadi drew near the lane with his army and the people came out to the lane gate; they fought an hour and he struck them a heavy blow, eight men dying that day, "and one who counted the stricken told us: a hundred and seventy", and they pushed on until they entered the lane gate and were sorely tired {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}.

## Detail
Al-Hadi then withdrew and spent the night in his camp; in the morning he went out to them, drew up his army and came near where the fighting had been, and the people shut the lane gate and none came out, so he stood an hour with his army and went back to his camp {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. In the morning his troops went to the side of [[group/banu-khuthaym-najran|Banu Khuthaym]] to cut their palms, which had not been cut before that day; they were sorely tired by it and sent elders of the people of Najran, among them [[person/aqil-ibn-abdallah-of-najran|Aqil ibn Abdallah]], to ask safety for them; al-Hadi promised them well and cut nothing, they asked him to hold off the cutting for two days, he did so, and then went out to the place where he had cut {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}. The word before "until they entered" is glossed by the editor in a note as "they pushed", and the museum follows that gloss {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.293}. The figure of a hundred and seventy is the narrator's, from a counter he does not name, and the museum cannot check it. The story is from the imam's supporters in a Zaydi book; it is not clear from the context whether "they pushed" refers to al-Hadi's army or to the other side. It follows [[event/the-ambush-of-the-harithi-party-from-al-jawf-and-the-girl-returned-292|the previous event]] and leads to [[event/banu-khuthaym-and-ibn-bustam-ask-safety-and-it-is-given-292|the request for safety]].
