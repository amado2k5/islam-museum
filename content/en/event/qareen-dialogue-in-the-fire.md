## The story
God describes the state of the people of the Fire: fetters are cast upon their necks and they are veiled from before them and behind them so they do not see, and Hell is a lure for the shayatin; then a dialogue runs between the sinner and his qareen — the qareen says «this is what I have prepared», that is, this is the punishment I readied for you, and the qareen pleads «our Lord, I did not send him astray but he was in far error» — meaning I did not drive him to disobedience, he was himself in far error {cite:tanzil-quran} [[passage/quran-50-23|Qaf 23]] [[passage/quran-50-27|Qaf 27]].

## The meaning
In these ayahs is a dialogue between the sinner and his qareen on the Last Day: he is shown what was prepared for him of punishment, and the qareen pleads that he did not drive him astray but he was in far error {cite:tanzil-quran}.
