## Overview
The house of al-Aghlab ibn Salim al-Ifriqi opened more than twenty cities of [[place/siqilliya|Sicily]], which remained in Muslim hands. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329} [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-aghab|Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Aghlab]] took from the island, in the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil ala Allah, the palace of Yana and the fort of Ghalyana. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}

## What al-Baladhuri records
Al-Baladhuri gives this account under the heading "the conquest of islands in the sea", directly after the first raid on the island. He calls the conquerors "the house of al-Aghlab ibn Salim al-Ifriqi". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}

## Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Aghlab
Al-Baladhuri attributes to Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Aghlab the taking of the palace of Yana and the fort of Ghalyana "from it", that is from Sicily, in the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil ala Allah. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}

## The date
The text dates these conquests only by the caliph's name, al-Mutawakkil ala Allah, and gives no year. This record therefore gives no year for them.
