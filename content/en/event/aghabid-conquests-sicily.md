## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports that the family of al-Aghlab ibn Salim al-Ifriqi conquered some twenty-odd cities of [[place/siqilliya|Sicily]], and that they remained in Muslim hands. [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-aghab|Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Aghlab]] conquered from it, in the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil, the palace of Yana and the fort of Ghalyana. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
