## Summary
Ibn al-Mulaqqin was born at Cairo on Saturday 14 Rabi' I 723 {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/44}; his father Nur al-Din had left al-Andalus for the land of the Turks, taught its people the Qur'an and earned good money before settling at Cairo {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/44}.
