## Summary
In Sha'ban of 622 AH the pitched battle took place between Jalal al-Din ibn Khwarazmshah and the Georgians, after he had sent them a warning to which they answered defiantly. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir recalls that the Georgians had been raiding the Islamic lands — Akhlat and its districts, Azerbaijan, Arran, Erzurum and Darband of Shirvan — shedding Muslim blood, plundering wealth and seizing lands, while the Muslims lived in daily humiliation, paying them whatever tribute they wished; and Uzbak, lord of Azerbaijan, was absorbed in his appetites, not angered for himself though his country was being taken and his soldiers were greedy. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449} Jalal al-Din, from the day he had turned toward those regions, kept saying he meant to march on the Georgian lands, fight them and take their country; when he took Azerbaijan he sent them a warning, and they answered that they had gone after the Tatars who had done to Aybak — a greater king with more troops and a stronger spirit — and taken their lands without caring about them, the best they could hope for being mere safety, and they began gathering their armies; the battle between the two sides came in Sha'ban of this year. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}
