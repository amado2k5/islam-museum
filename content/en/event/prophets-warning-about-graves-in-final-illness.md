## Summary
Among the words the books report from [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] in his last illness is a warning against taking the graves of prophets as places of prayer, and a prayer that God not make his own grave an idol that is worshipped. Some narrators, foremost [[person/aisha|Aisha]], connect the warning with the place where he was buried. This page sets out the reports as Ibn Kathir and Malik give them, attributes each to its narrator, and rules on no point of law. See also [[event/last-minbar-sermon|The last sermon from the minbar]], where a nearby warning appears, and [[event/prophets-last-counsels-in-final-illness|His counsels in his final illness]]. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 263}

## The report of Aisha and Ibn Abbas
Ibn Kathir quotes al-Bukhari and Muslim, through al-Zuhri from Ubaydallah ibn Abdallah ibn Utba, that Aisha and Ibn Abbas said that when death came upon the Prophet he began to throw a cloak of his over his face, and when he was distressed he uncovered it, and said while in that state: "God's curse on the Jews and Christians, who took the graves of their prophets as places of prayer", warning against what they did. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 263} Ibn Kathir repeats the report elsewhere, in his discussion of what the Prophet wore, and gives it again as al-Bukhari's report from [[person/aisha|Aisha]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]]. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 398} In another report from Aisha in al-Bukhari, through Hilal al-Warraq from Urwa, she heard him say in the illness in which he died: "God cursed the Jews and Christians, who took the graves of their prophets as places of prayer." {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295}

## Malik's mursal reports
In the Muwatta Malik gives, from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar as a mursal report, the Prophet's prayer: "O God, do not make my grave an idol that is worshipped", and his saying that God's anger is fierce against a people who took the graves of their prophets as places of prayer; it stands in the chapter on the collected matters of prayer. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.243} See [[passage/muwatta-1-475|Muwatta 475]]. Elsewhere Malik reports from Isma'il ibn Abi Hakim that he heard [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] say that among the last things the Prophet said were: "God fight the Jews and Christians, who took the graves of their prophets as places of prayer; two religions shall not remain in the land of the Arabs", and Ibn Kathir notes that Malik reported it as a mursal report from Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 263} See [[passage/muwatta-31-2606|Muwatta 2606]]. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.470}

## What Aisha said about the place of the grave
After giving her report of the curse, Ibn Kathir quotes al-Bukhari for Aisha's words: "Were it not for that, his grave would have been brought out, but he feared it would be taken as a place of prayer." {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295} Ibn Kathir gives the wording without comment. From its context, Aisha seems to connect the warning with his burial in her room and not in an open place; this is a reading of the context and not an explicit statement in the report. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295}

## The disagreement over where to bury him
Al-Waqidi reports from Abd al-Rahman ibn Sa'id ibn Yarbu' that when the Prophet died the Companions disagreed about the place of his grave: one said al-Baqi', because he used to ask forgiveness often for its people; one said by his minbar; one said in his place of prayer. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] then came and said he had knowledge of this, having heard the Prophet say: "No prophet is taken but is buried where he died." {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295} Al-Bayhaqi, as Ibn Kathir quotes him, says this is in the hadith of Yahya ibn Sa'id from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad and in that of Ibn Jurayj from his father, both from Abu Bakr from the Prophet as a mursal report. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295} In another report from Salim ibn Ubayd, the Companions asked Abu Bakr where he should be buried, and he said where God took his soul, since God takes it only in a good place. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295} Ibn Kathir also quotes al-Bayhaqi that Aisha told her father a dream of three moons falling into her lap; he said that if her dream was true, three of the best people on earth would be buried in her house, and when the Prophet died he told her that this was the best of her moons; he notes that Malik reported it from Aisha with a break in the chain. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295}

Ibn Kathir reports from Ibn Majah, from Anas, that the Muslims disagreed between the niche-grave and the shaft-grave; Medina had one man who dug niches and another who dug shafts, so they sent for both and the one who dug niches arrived first. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295} In another report from Aisha, Umar told them not to raise their voices near the Messenger of God, alive or dead. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 296} For the washing and burial see [[event/burial-of-the-prophet|The burial of the Prophet]].

## Where the reports differ
- The Prophet's warning about graves comes in at least two forms: the curse on those who took them as places of prayer, in the report of Aisha and Ibn Abbas, and the prayer that his grave not be made an idol, in Malik's mursal report. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 263}
- Ibn Kathir gives some of the reports on the place of burial from al-Waqidi and others from al-Bayhaqi and Ibn Majah, so they come from scattered sources. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 295}
- This page does not treat the legal rulings built on the report; for the idea see [[concept/grave-veneration|Veneration of graves]].
