## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went back until he reached [[place/warwar-yemen|Warwar]], where news kept reaching him, and stayed; he sent to Hamdan and urged them to march and kept turning them to it, but they were slow, and he wrote to al-Ja'fari asking him to send a money aid to help him against the men, and he held back {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.250}. He stayed at Warwar while [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] wrote to [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibn Khalaf]] about peace and the freeing of those in his hands, knowing reasons for it that the narrator does not give {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.250}.

## Detail
The narrator then mentions Jaftam's coming from Iraq, and al-Hadi feared for the members of his household behind him; words passed between him and Ibn Khalaf on the terms that al-Hadi would leave Warwar and, when he reached Sa'da, Ibn Khalaf would free his son and companions, taking his covenant and pledge for it, and he did not agree to that. Al-Hadi stayed at Warwar some time calling the people to muster against Ibn Khalaf and asking their help, and they did not answer; meanwhile a letter reached him that a commander of the Banu al-Abbas called Jaftam had reached Mecca, and he feared for those behind him and went to [[place/saada|Sa'da]], where he stayed a few days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.250}. The text does not name the subject of "did not agree to that" and the museum reads it as Ibn Khalaf from the context. The book says no more of Jaftam here; the museum does not link the existing record of Jaftam, since the book does not say he is the same man.
