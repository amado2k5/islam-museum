After Hamza's and Umar's conversions, the polytheists tried bargaining with the Prophet by pouring out every desire; [[person/utba-ibn-rabia|Utba ibn Rabi'a]] said in Quraysh's assembly while the Prophet sat alone in the mosque: shall I not go to Muhammad, speak with him and offer him matters he might accept some of, so he leaves us be? They said: yes. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}

He sat before him and said: if what you have brought seeks wealth, we will gather for you until you are the richest of us; if you seek honour, we will make you our lord; if you seek kingship, we will crown you king over us; and if what comes to you is a vision you cannot repel, we will seek you medicine and spend our wealth on it. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}

When he finished the Prophet said: "are you done, Abu al-Walid? Then hear me" — and recited the opening of Sura Fussilat until he reached its prostration and prostrated, then said: "you have heard what you heard, Abu al-Walid; it is for you now." Utba went back to his companions saying: by God I have heard words the like of which I never heard — company of Quraysh, obey me and leave this man to what he is upon. They said: by God, Abu al-Walid, he has bewitched you with his tongue. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}
