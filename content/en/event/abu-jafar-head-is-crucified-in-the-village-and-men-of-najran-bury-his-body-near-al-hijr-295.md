## Overview
Banu al-Harith carried Abu Ja'far's head around the villages of Najran, then crucified it on a post in the village and stoned it. A woman of Banu Abd al-Madan named Furat took the head, and her sister took it from her and buried it where only she and her husband knew. Men of Najran asked Ibn Humayd for the body and were given it. They washed and shrouded it, prayed over it, and buried it near the village of al-Hijr, at a place called al-Balat.

## Detail
The narrator says Banu al-Harith carried Abu Ja'far's head through the villages of Najran, then brought it back to the village and crucified it on a post, and pelted it with stones and arrows. A woman of Banu Abd al-Madan named Furat then took the head from the crowd and did to it an act that dishonours the dead, which the record omits. Her sister took the head from her and carried it to her house, where she buried it in a place known only to her and her husband. As for the body, several men of Najran, among them Aqil ibn Abdallah and Abdallah ibn Isa, asked Ibn Humayd for it and he gave it to them. They washed and shrouded it, prayed over it and buried it near the village of al-Hijr, at a place called al-Balat.
