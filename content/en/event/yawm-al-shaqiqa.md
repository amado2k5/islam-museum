## Overview
The Day of al-Shaqiqa was a day for the Banu [[group/banu-dabba|Dabba]] against [[group/banu-shayban|Shayban]], on which [[person/bustam-ibn-qays|Bustam ibn Qays]], lord of Shayban, was killed. Ibn al-Athir relates it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 461-462}

## The raid
Bustam raided the Banu Dabba, with his brother al-Sulayl and Naqid the bird-augur of the Asad. Bustam saw in a dream someone coming and saying something to him about the bucket; he related it to Naqid, who took an omen from it. Bustam disobeyed, and Naqid parted from him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 461-462}

He raided the camels of Malik ibn al-Muntafiq al-Dabbi, which were a thousand she-camels. When the horsemen of Dabba caught up, Malik said: strike the water-skins of the people; they began to slit them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 461-462}

## The killing of Bustam
Asim ibn Khalifa al-Subahi, whom Ibn al-Athir describes as weak-minded, came carrying a spear and said that he would kill Bustam with it. Bustam was killed that day; he was at the rear of the people on his dark-red horse, protecting his companions. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 461-462}

The date is approximate.
