## Overview
In 776 AH the Marinids had two kingdoms: the kingdom of [[place/fez|Fez]] and the kingdom of Marrakesh. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Alliance and siege
Sultan Abu al-Abbas Ahmad and the amir Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi al-Fadl met at Wadi al-Naja and swore to unite against their common enemy. They marched with their armies to Kudyat al-Arai'is in Dhu al-Qa'da 775 AH, and the vizier was defeated. Then they besieged the new city. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Division
Abu al-Abbas entered the new city on the seventh of Muharram 776 AH. Abd al-Rahman moved to Marrakesh and became independent there. They made peace and set Azemmour as the boundary between them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
