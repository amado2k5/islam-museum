## Overview
Ibn al-Fadl fought Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ali at Mount Wafir for about two months. Ibn Ali was routed, and in Jumada II 293 AH Ibn al-Fadl entered al-Kudra' and al-Muhajjam and plundered them.

## Detail
Ibn al-Fadl went to Mount Wafir and fought Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Ali for about two months. Ibn Ali was then routed, and Ibn al-Fadl went to a place called Hakam, and in Jumada II 293 AH he entered al-Kudra' and al-Muhajjam and plundered them. He then went out against Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ali toward Zabid and drove him out, and the Qarmatians entered it. Ibn Ali went to al-Kudra' against him and killed the men there who were his followers, and the Qarmatians killed those of Ahmad's men they caught in Zabid. Ibn al-Fadl withdrew to al-Mudhaykhara, Ibn Ali returned to Zabid, and his brother returned to al-Kudra'.
