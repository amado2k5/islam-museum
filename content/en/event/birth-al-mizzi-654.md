## Summary
Jamal al-Din Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf ibn al-Zaki Abd al-Rahman ibn Yusuf ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Abi al-Zahr al-Quda'i al-Kalbi al-Mizzi was born on 10 Rabi' II 654 outside Aleppo {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 486}; Ibn Hajar gives the spot as al-Ma'qiliyya {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/457}, and the Encyclopaedia of Islam records him born near Aleppo of Arab stock {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 240}.

## Upbringing
As a child he moved with his parents to al-Mizza, a prosperous village just outside Damascus, where he received a traditional education in Qur'an and some fiqh {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 240}; he memorised the Qur'an and studied Shafi'i fiqh for a time {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 488}.
