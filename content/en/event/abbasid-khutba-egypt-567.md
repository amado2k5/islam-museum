## Overview
Sources report that Saladin established the Friday sermon for the Abbasids in Egypt after the sermon for al-Adid, the last Fatimid caliph, was discontinued. The account places it in Muharram 567 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 681-683}

## The account
al-Suyuti quotes al-Imad al-Katib that [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Saladin]] established the sermon for the Banu al-Abbas on the first Friday in Egypt, and then on the second Friday in Cairo. [[person/al-adid-fatimid|al-Adid]] died on the day of Ashura, and Saladin took over the palace and what was in it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 681-683}

## Why the sermon was changed
al-Suyuti quotes Ibn al-Athir that [[person/nur-al-din-mahmud-ibn-zangi|Nur al-Din]] wrote to Saladin ordering him to do so, and Saladin excused himself out of fear that the Egyptians would rise up. Then a non-Arab known as al-Amir al-Alim invoked al-Mustadi from the pulpit before the preacher spoke, and no one objected. Saladin therefore ordered the preachers, the following Friday, to stop the sermon for al-Adid. The sermon was made for the caliph [[person/al-mustadi-billah|al-Mustadi]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 681-683}

## After the death of al-Adid
The "Shadharat al-Dhahab" reports that al-Adid died soon after, and it was said that he died of grief. Saladin showed sorrow for him and sat to receive condolences. He then took over the palace, moved al-Adid's children and household to another place, and provided for their needs. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 702}
