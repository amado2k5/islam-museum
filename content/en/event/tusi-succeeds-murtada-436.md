## Summary
On al-Murtada's death in 436/1044 al-Tusi succeeded him at the head of the capital's Imamis; the caliph al-Qa'im gave him the most prestigious chair of theology, he inherited the Dar al-Ilm and al-Murtada libraries, and his Karkh house was the Imami intellectual centre for over a decade {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 pp. 744-745}.
