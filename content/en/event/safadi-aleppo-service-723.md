## Summary
Al-Safadi's gifts as a stylist and calligrapher opened government service to him: he first held kitabat al-darj at Safad, then at Cairo; he conducted kitabat al-sirr at Aleppo for a time between 723/1323 and 726/1326, and briefly at al-Rahba toward the end of the decade, while shuttling between the capitals Damascus and Cairo {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 vol. 2 p. 88}.
