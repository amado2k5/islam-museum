## Overview
[[person/umar-ibn-hafs-hazarmard|Umar ibn Hafs ibn Uthman ibn Qabisa ibn Abi Safra al-Ataki]], al-Mansur's governor over [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]], was killed by the Ibadi Abu Hatim, and the city of al-Abbasiyya that he had built was destroyed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Appointment
Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mansur appointed Umar ibn Hafs, who had taken Hazarmard captive, and al-Mansur admired him. He entered Ifriqiya and campaigned as far as the furthest land of the Berbers, and built a city that he called al-Abbasiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
He was fought by [[person/abu-khatim-al-saddrati|Abu Hatim al-Saddrati]], an Ibadi, client of Kinda. Umar was killed with a group of his household, the frontier revolted, and the city was destroyed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date is approximately between 759 and 767 CE, and is derived.
