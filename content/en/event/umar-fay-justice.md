## Overview
"Futuh al-Buldan" relates reports of the position of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] on the spoils, that they are public property, belonging neither to Umar nor to his family. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The letter to Hudhayfa
Umar wrote to [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa]] to give people their stipends. Hudhayfa replied that he had done so and much remained. Umar wrote: "Among them is that which God gave them as spoils; it is not for Umar nor for the family of Umar, so divide it among them." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The money of Bahrain
[[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] came from Bahrain with five hundred thousand. A man told Umar that he had seen the foreigners recording a register, so Umar recorded the register. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He imposed five thousand on the first emigrants, four thousand on the Ansar and twelve thousand on the wives of the Prophet. He added every child born, male or female, to a hundred and two jaribs a month. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The book quotes his words: "It is only their right." It also relates the Prophet's saying about one who dies deceiving his subjects. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
