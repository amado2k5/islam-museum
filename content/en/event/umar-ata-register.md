## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] drew up the register in 20 AH and imposed stipends by rank. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The order
He began with the Banu Hashim, then the nearest to the Messenger of God, nearer and nearer; when they were equal in kinship he gave precedence to those of earlier standing. Among the Ansar he began with the clan of [[person/sad-ibn-muadh|Sa'd ibn Mu'adh]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

[[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] had made the division equal among people. The book quotes Umar's words: "I will not make him who fought the Messenger of God like him who fought with him." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The amounts
- For each man of Badr, five thousand dirhams a year, with his ally and his client receiving the same.
- Four thousand for those whose Islam was like the Islam of the people of Badr, and for the emigrants to Abyssinia who witnessed Uhud.
- Two thousand for the sons of the Badr men; al-Hasan and al-Husayn were added to their father's share because of their kinship to the Prophet.
- Twelve thousand for the wives of the Prophet, and Juwayriyya and Safiyya were added to them.
- For [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas]], five thousand, or it is said seven thousand.

{cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Objections and answers
He imposed four thousand on [[person/usama-ibn-zayd|Usama ibn Zayd]] and three thousand on [[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|Abd Allah ibn Umar]]. His son asked him, and he answered, according to the book: "Because he was dearer to the Messenger of God than you, and his father was dearer to the Messenger of God than your father." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Afterwards stipends were imposed by their positions, their reading of the Qur'an and their striving. For a child born alive, a hundred dirhams, increased to two hundred when he grew up; for a foundling, a hundred and a monthly ration. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
