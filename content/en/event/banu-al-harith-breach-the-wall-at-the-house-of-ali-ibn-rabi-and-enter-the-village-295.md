## Overview
A sentry reported to Abu Ja'far that Banu al-Harith had breached the wall near the house of Ali ibn Rabi' and entered the village, and that the Banu Abd al-Madan and Banu al-Harith men who had been with him had met them and let them in. Abu Ja'far sent a servant to call on Banu Abd al-Madan and their clients to come to his support, and not one of them came.

## Detail
The narrator says that a sentry then came and reported that Banu al-Harith had breached the wall near the house of Ali ibn Rabi' and entered the village, and that the Banu Abd al-Madan and Banu al-Harith men who were with Abu Ja'far had met them and let them in. Abu Ja'far ordered one of his servants to call out to Banu Abd al-Madan and their clients to come to him. Not one man came, and they went over to the enemy. Abu Ja'far's companions, horsemen and foot soldiers, gathered around Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah. When none of the Hamdani men or their clients answered him, he knew they had betrayed him and let his enemy in. This is a second breach of the wall, distinct from the breach made on the Wednesday at the Darb gate in the earlier record.
