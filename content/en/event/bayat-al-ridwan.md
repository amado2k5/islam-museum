While the Muslims were halted at al-Hudaybiyya, the Prophet sent Uthman ibn Affan as envoy to Quraysh at Mecca to assure them that the Muslims had come only as pilgrims honouring the Sacred House, wanting no fight. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

Quraysh detained him in Mecca and his return was delayed, until word spread through the Muslim camp that Uthman had been killed. When the news reached the Messenger of God he said: "we shall not leave until we fight the people to the end," and called the Companions to the pledge. They raced to him as he sat under a samura tree and all swore the pledge — that they would not flee and would fight to the death — the first to pledge being Sinan ibn Abi Sinan al-Asadi. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

When all had pledged, the Prophet struck one of his hands upon the other and said: "this is for Uthman" — so the Prophet's hand for Uthman was better than Uthman's hand for himself. Only one man of the hypocrites held back from the pledge, hiding behind his camel. And God sent down His lasting good-pleasure upon those pledgers in Sura al-Fath, as in [[passage/quran-48-18|Sura al-Fath, verse 18]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
