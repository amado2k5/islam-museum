## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the Byzantines gathered in a great host, and [[person/heraclius|Heraclius]] sent them reinforcements. The Muslims met them at [[place/marj-al-suffar|Marj al-Suffar]], as they were heading toward [[place/damascus|Damascus]], at the new moon of Muharram 14 AH. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 162}

## The fighting
They fought very fiercely, and about four thousand of the Muslims were wounded. Then the Byzantines were routed until they reached Damascus and Jerusalem. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 162}

## Those who were martyred
[[person/khalid-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Khalid ibn Said ibn al-As]] was martyred that day; he had consummated his marriage the night before with [[person/umm-hakim-bint-al-harith|Umm Hakim bint al-Harith]]. It is said that she fought with a tent pole and killed seven men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 162-165}

## Differing reports
In the report of Abu Mikhnaf, the battle was twenty nights after Ajnadayn. al-Baladhuri says that al-Waqidi's report is the more firmly established. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 162-165}

He also relates from Hisham ibn al-Kalbi the account of the sword of Samsama that was on Khalid's neck, and that the Umayyads inherited it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 162-165}
