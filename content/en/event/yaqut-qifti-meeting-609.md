## Summary
In 609/1212 the book agent Abu Ali al-Qilawi brought Yaqut before the Qadi al-Akram Ibn al-Kifti (al-Qifti), vizier of Aleppo, carrying the books he had collected {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}. It was a cool first meeting, yet al-Qifti later became Yaqut's greatest patron.

## Al-Qifti's testimony
In the Inbah al-Ruwat al-Qifti writes of this meeting: 'There were but two books in them that I bought from him; I studied his looks and his talk, divined things in him that my intuition did not belie, and knew he was not fit for company' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}. Yaqut was then on his great circuit Baghdad–Syria–Tabriz–Baghdad, 607-10/1210-13 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}.
