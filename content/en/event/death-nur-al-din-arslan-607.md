## Summary
Nur al-Din Arslan Shah ibn Izz al-Din Mas'ud, lord of Mosul, died. He was among the bravest and the most miserly of kings, and so much was said of him. After him his young son Izz al-Din Mas'ud succeeded, and his mamluks alone took charge of affairs {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.353}.
