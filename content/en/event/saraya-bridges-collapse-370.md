## Overview
In 370 the Tigris rose beyond measure, and the Euphrates; a breach burst and the Saraya bridges fell — the New one in mid-Dhu'l-Qa'da and the Old after it on a Wednesday; then work began on the two bridges, vast sums spent on them, and they were built firm {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.110-111}.
