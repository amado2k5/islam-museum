## Summary
[[person/farwa-ibn-amr-al-judhami|Farwa ibn Amr al-Judhami]], the Romans' governor over Ma'an, became Muslim after Mu'ta. The Romans seized him and gave him the choice between apostasy and death; he chose death, and was crucified at Afra' in Palestine. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.445-446}

## The account
Farwa ibn Amr al-Judhami was an Arab commander among the Roman officers, and their governor over the Arabs living around them. His seat was Ma'an and the land of Syria around it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.445; zad-al-maad} He became Muslim after seeing the steadfastness and sincerity of the Muslims at the battle of Mu'ta in the year 8 AH, and he sent a messenger to the Prophet announcing his Islam and gifting him a white mule. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.445-446} When the Romans learned of his Islam, they seized and imprisoned him, then gave him the choice between apostasy and death. He chose death, so they crucified him by a water called Afra' in Palestine and struck off his head. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.446; zad-al-maad}

## For young visitors
Farwa was a commander serving the Romans. When he heard of the Muslims' steadfastness at Mu'ta, he believed and sent a gift to the Prophet. The Romans punished him because he would not turn back from his faith.

## Ibn Ishaq's account
Ibn Ishaq names him Farwa ibn Amr ibn al-Nafira al-Judhami, then al-Nafathi. He reports that Farwa sent a messenger to the Prophet announcing his Islam, and sent him a white mule. Farwa was the Romans' governor over the Arabs near them, and his seat was [[place/maan|Ma'an]] and the land around it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/237} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238} When the news reached the Romans they hunted him down, seized him and imprisoned him. In his prison he composed [[work/farwa-al-judhami-prison-verses|verses]] addressing Sulayma and Abu Kabisha. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238} When they resolved to crucify him at their water called [[place/afra|Afra']] in Palestine, he recited two verses. Al-Zuhri claimed that when they brought him forward to be killed, he said one verse conveying to the leaders of the Muslims that he had submitted to his Lord; then they struck his neck and crucified him by that water. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}

## Notes on the transmission
- Ibn Ishaq does not give the year of the event. Later sources place it after Mu'ta, in the year 8 AH. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.445}
- "Al-Zuhri claimed" introduces the final verse. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}
- The name of the water is given as "Afra'" or "Afra", depending on al-Zurqani and the older sources. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/238}
