## Overview
Al-Hadi sends his son Abu al-Qasim to Dhamar and appoints Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Haddaqi as judge. Muhammad ibn Yahya fights the Qarmatians in that district and then, as they advance on Dhamar, joins his father in Sanaa.

## Detail
Al-Hadi sent his son Abu al-Qasim to Dhamar and appointed Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Haddaqi as judge there. Muhammad ibn Yahya was fighting the Qarmatians in that district. The Qarmatians grew stronger, and Isa al-Yafi'i helped them, and they marched toward Dhamar. Muhammad ibn Yahya went out and joined his father al-Hadi at Sanaa.
