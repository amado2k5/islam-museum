## Overview
On Wednesday 14 Rajab 999 the Mahdawi regime at Ahmadnagar in the Deccan fell; the vizier Jamal Khan was killed, his head brought to Ahmadnagar, paraded through it, then hung up for days — and Burhan Shah took the throne {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 440}.
