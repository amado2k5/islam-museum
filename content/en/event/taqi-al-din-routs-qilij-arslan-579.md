## Summary
Qilij Arslan coveted Syria, since it lay between al-Malik al-Salih and Salah al-Din, and about twenty thousand men gathered to him. Salah al-Din sent his brother Taqi al-Din Umar with a thousand horsemen. Taqi al-Din met them in battle, routed them and ruined the fortress of al-Ahzan. He used to say that he had defeated twenty thousand with a thousand fighters. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 97}
