## Overview
In Jumada al-Akhira 430 AH the Seljuqs took Khurasan and the Jibal, as the text has it, according to Ibn al-Jawzi, and [[person/masud-ibn-mahmud|Mas'ud ibn Mahmud Subuktakin]] fled. [[person/tughrul-beg|Tughrulbeg]], his brother Dawud and others of the sons of Mikail took the lands and divided up their outlying parts. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.400, 413}

## Afterwards
In 432 AH Mas'ud returned to Ghazna, and Tughrulbeg returned to Nishapur. The Ghuzz took all of Khurasan. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.400, 413}

Ibn al-Jawzi describes what appeared of the Ghuzz as going beyond all limits, and mentions their killing of people and their attacks on many scribes and others. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.400, 413}

A question mark stands beside one of the names in the quoted text, so it is not asserted; the point needs an additional source.
