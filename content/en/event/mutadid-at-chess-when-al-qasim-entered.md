## Overview
Al-Mu'tadid was playing chess when his vizier al-Qasim ibn Ubaydallah came in. The caliph quoted a line by Ali ibn Bassam; on seeing al-Qasim he was embarrassed and told him to silence Ibn Bassam. Al-Qasim hurried to do it until the caliph told him to stop. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 660}

The event is tied to the caliph [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] and the vizier [[person/al-qasim-ibn-ubaydallah|al-Qasim ibn Ubaydallah]]. The text does not date the scene; it falls within al-Mu'tadid's reign (279–289 AH) {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 598} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 634}.
