## Overview
In the period of turmoil that "The Sealed Nectar" begins from 300 CE, the Romans entered [[place/aden|Aden]], and with their help the Abyssinians occupied [[place/yemen|Yemen]] for the first time in 340 CE, taking advantage of the rivalry between the tribes of [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] and [[group/himyar|Himyar]]. Their occupation lasted until 378 CE. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24} {cite:tafhim-al-quran 4/195-198}

## The religious effect
The Christian faith came to the lands of the Arabs by way of the occupation of Abyssinia and the Romans. In that time Christian mission entered the regions of Yemen, and near it [[person/faymiyun|Faymiyun]] entered Najran, and its people became Christians. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.40} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.41}

## What followed
Yemen regained its independence after 378 CE, but breaches began to appear in the Ma'rib dam until the [[event/flood-of-al-aram|Flood of al-Arim]] befell it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.24} The second occupation, in 525 CE, is at [[event/abyssinian-occupation-of-yemen|its own entry]].

## Notes for researchers
The dates are the author's estimate from "Tafhim al-Quran", with his note on the great disagreement among the sources about the years. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.25}

## For young visitors
The Abyssinians first occupied Yemen about three centuries before Islam, because the tribes of Yemen were quarrelling, and they stayed for about forty years.
