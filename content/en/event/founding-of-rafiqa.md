## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that al-Rafiqa had no old remains, and that [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] built it in 155 AH on the pattern of his city at Baghdad. He stationed troops from Khurasan in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.247}

## Completion
Building went on under [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] while he was heir apparent. Then [[person/harun-al-rashid|al-Rashid]] built its palaces. Between al-Raqqa and al-Rafiqa there was an open space of farms, and Ali ibn Sulayman moved the markets of al-Raqqa to it, and al-Rashid increased the markets. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.247}

## The Rusafa of Hisham
As for the Rusafa of [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]], he had earlier stopped at al-Zaytuna. After the state was established its estate became Zubayda bint Ja'far's, and she built the enclosure attributed to her in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
