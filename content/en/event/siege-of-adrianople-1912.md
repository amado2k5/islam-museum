## Overview
The Bulgarians besieged Edirne in the First Balkan War of 1912-13 and the city fell under Bulgarian occupation in 1913 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 710}.

## A city under siege
The siege destroyed the minaret of the Dar al-Hadith mosque, later rebuilt; since the Balkan Wars Edirne has been a Turkish frontier city {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 pp. 710, 712}.
