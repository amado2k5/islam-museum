## Overview
At al-Namarq near Kufa: al-Muthanna b. Haritha had rallied the pardoned ridda converts when Abu 'Ubayd b. Mas'ud came up; Jaban came down from Kaskar to the Namarq, they met in a hard fight, and God routed the Persians. Jaban was taken by a trick — he asked his captor Matar b. Fidda al-Tamimi "will you grant me safety?", who did and freed him; when they brought him to Abu 'Ubayd and told him it was Jaban, urging execution, he said: "I fear God to kill a man a Muslim has granted safety — the Muslims are one body; what binds one binds all." Mardanshah was captured by Aktal b. Shammakh al-'Ukayli and killed {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 274-275}.
