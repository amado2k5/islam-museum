## Overview
[[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] marched to [[place/al-sus|al-Sus]] and fought its people, then besieged them until their food ran out, and they asked for safe conduct. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Safe conduct
Their marzban asked that eighty of them be granted safe conduct, on condition that he open the city's gate and hand it over. He named the eighty and took himself out of them. Abu Musa ordered his neck struck, and did not touch the eighty. He killed the rest of the fighting men, took the property and enslaved the families. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The house of Daniel
Abu Musa saw in the fortress a house with a curtain over it. It was said that it held the body of the prophet Daniel. Al-Baladhuri relates that the people of al-Sus were struck by drought and asked the people of Babylon to bury him with them so that they might pray for rain through him. Abu Musa wrote to Umar, who wrote to him to shroud the body and bury it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
[[person/khalid-ibn-zayd-al-muzani|Khalid ibn Zayd al-Muzani]], whose eye had been struck by a worm, relates that the landowner made peace with Abu Musa on terms of safety for a hundred persons. He separated out the hundred and remained himself, and Abu Musa ordered him killed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
