## Summary
In 448 AH the caliph al-Qa'im bi-Amr Allah married the sister of Tughril Beg. The caliph's position was strengthened and the caliphate grew in standing through Tughril Beg's sultanate {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 278}.

## What the marriage meant
This marriage was a bond between the Abbasid caliphate in Baghdad and the rising Seljuq sultanate. It came after years in which the caliphs had been weak before the Buyid amirs. Later the reverse happened: Tughril Beg married the caliph's daughter in 454 AH {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 278; tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 645}.
