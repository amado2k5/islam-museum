## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that the Franks took Damietta in 647 AH while the sultan al-Malik al-Salih was ill, and he died in mid-Sha'ban. His slave-girl [[person/shajar-al-durr|Shajar al-Durr]] concealed his death and sent to his son [[person/turanshah|Turanshah]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709}

## The killing of Turanshah
Turanshah was killed in Muharram 648 AH; his father's mamluks rose against him. They installed Shajar al-Durr, and the Turks swore allegiance to her and to her deputy [[person/izz-al-din-aybak|Izz al-Din Aybak]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709}

## Afterwards
Izz al-Din established himself as sultan in Rabi al-Akhir and took the title al-Malik al-Mu'izz. Then the army swore allegiance to al-Malik al-Ashraf, who was eight years old, and Izz al-Din remained his atabeg. In 652 AH al-Mu'izz abolished the name of al-Ashraf and became independent. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.709}
