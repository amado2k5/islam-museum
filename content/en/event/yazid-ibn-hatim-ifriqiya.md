## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/yazid-ibn-hatim|Yazid ibn Hatim ibn Qabisa ibn al-Muhallab]] was appointed over Ifriqiya after Hazarmard, and set out with fifty thousand men. Abu Ja'far [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] escorted him as far as Jerusalem and spent a great sum on him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The march
Yazid marched until he met [[person/abu-khatim-al-saddrati|Abu Hatim]] at Batr Iblis and killed him, and entered [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]], and matters were set right for him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After him
After him [[person/rawh-ibn-hatim|Rawh ibn Hatim]] was appointed, then [[person/al-fadl-ibn-rawh|al-Fadl ibn Rawh]]; the troops rose against him and slaughtered him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date is derived from the succession of governorships and needs an additional source.
