## Overview
Al-Hadi reached Najran and alighted at al-Hisn; Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah met him at al-Kathib, and none of Banu al-Harith remained with him.

## Detail
Al-Hadi reached Najran seven days before the end of Rajab and alighted at a place called al-Hisn. On the second day after his arrival his supporters from Shakir, Thaqif, Wada'a and the Ahlaf gathered to him, and he marched with them and his troops to a place called al-Kathib. There Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah met him, with his sons, companions and men of Banu Abd al-Madan. None of Banu al-Harith remained with him: they had been afraid on arrival, had hidden themselves, and disliked meeting al-Hadi. The arrival is dated to 23 Rajab; the meeting on the second day after it, about 25 Rajab, is derived from the text.
