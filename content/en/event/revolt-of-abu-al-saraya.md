## Overview
Abu al-Saraya was in charge of organising the rising of Ibn Tabataba in Kufa in 199 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.528} He fled from Kufa in Muharram 200 AH, and was killed in Rabi al-Awwal of the same year. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.534} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.535}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi says that Abu al-Saraya rose in 199 AH with Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Isma'il ibn Tabataba. Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn Dawud rose in Medina, and Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ja'far and Zayd ibn Musa ibn Ja'far in Basra. When Ibn Tabataba died, Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Zayd ibn Ali was put in his place. Ibrahim ibn Musa ibn Ja'far appeared in Yemen, and in Mecca in 200 AH Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad, and the Sabitiyya called to him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 419-420}

## Note
The date is marked as disputed in some of its details, and needs an additional source.
