## Overview
Al-Hadi ordered Ali ibn al-Rabi' al-Madani held in irons for twelve days at al-Hijr, as the book reports.

## Detail
When al-Hadi reached al-Hijr, a village of Najran, he alighted there and ordered his companions to alight. On the second day after his arrival he ordered Ali ibn al-Rabi' al-Madani put in prison and irons. The book calls him one of the people of corruption and sedition against the imam, and he stayed in confinement for twelve days. The word of corruption is the narrator's; the museum records the imprisonment and gives no verdict on the man.
