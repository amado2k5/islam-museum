## Summary
Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj's first hearing of hadith was in 218, when he was about twelve {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 526-527}. He then transmitted copiously from Yahya ibn Yahya, al-Tamimi, al-Qa'nabi, Ahmad ibn Yunus al-Yarbu'i, Isma'il ibn Abi Uways, Sa'id ibn Mansur, Ahmad ibn Hanbal and a great many others {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 526-527}.
