## Overview
Isa ibn Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak dies at Rayda on Sunday, 4 Sha'ban 327 AH. His father had made him his deputy there, and he is buried there. The father leaves Saada on Saturday, 10 of the month, when the news reaches him.

## Detail
The book says Isa ibn Ahmad ibn al-Dahhak fell ill at Rayda, where his father had made him his deputy. He died on Sunday, 4 Sha'ban of the same year and was buried there. The news reached his father, who left Saada on Saturday, 10 of this month. The place name is printed 'bi-Rayda' (the scan text reads as the town of Rayda, which the book spells Rayda elsewhere on the page); the father had been at Saada and left it on hearing the news.
