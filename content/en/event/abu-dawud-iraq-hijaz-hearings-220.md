## Overview
In the year twenty and two hundred, Abu Dawud prayed over 'Affan at Baghdad, then entered Basra as they were saying that 'Uthman ibn al-Haytham the mu'adhdhin had died the day before — he had died in Rajab of that year — and heard Abu 'Amr al-Darir in a single session there {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 358}.

## Hearings at Mecca in the pilgrimage days
In the pilgrimage days of the same year he heard al-Qa'nabi, Sulayman ibn Harb and others at Mecca {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 358}, and at Basra he heard Muslim ibn Ibrahim, 'Ubaydallah ibn Raja', Abu al-Walid, Abu Salama al-Tabudhaki and a circle of teachers {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 358}.
