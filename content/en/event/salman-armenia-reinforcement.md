## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Uthman wrote to [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Sa'id ibn al-As]], his governor over Kufa, ordering him to reinforce [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] with an army commanded by [[person/salman-ibn-rabi-a|Salman ibn Rabi'a al-Bahili]]. Salman marched with six thousand men from Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278-279}

## The booty
The Muslims had surprised the Byzantines and overrun them before Salman arrived. The people of Kufa asked to share in the booty, and their request was refused. Habib and Salman argued sharply. Uthman then wrote that the booty belonged to the people of Syria, and ordered Salman to raid Arran. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278-279}

## Another account
Al-Baladhuri gives another account: Salman set out for Armenia when he was with [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn Uqba]] at Hadithat al-Mawsil in 25 AH, when a letter came with Mu'awiya's reinforcement of eight thousand men. He says that the first account is more firmly established in his view. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278-279}

The place recorded in the data is [[place/qaliqala|Qaliqala]].
