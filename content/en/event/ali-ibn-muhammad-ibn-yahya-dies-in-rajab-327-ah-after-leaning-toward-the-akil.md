## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn al-Husayn dies of an illness on Wednesday, six nights before the end of Rajab 327 AH. He had leaned toward the Akil against his cousin.

## Detail
The book says Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn al-Husayn leaned toward the Akil against his cousin, intending to betray him. An illness struck him, and he died on Wednesday, six nights before the end of Rajab of the same year. The book names the cousin only by the kinship.
