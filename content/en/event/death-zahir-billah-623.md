## Summary
On the fourteenth of Rajab in 623 AH the Imam al-Zahir bi-Amr Allah, commander of the faithful Abu Nasr Muhammad ibn al-Nasir li-Din Allah, died; his caliphate had lasted nine months and fourteen days. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 464}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir describes al-Zahir as an excellent caliph who combined reverence with submission to his Lord and justice and kindness toward his subjects, increasing daily in good works and charity toward the people; he restored justice that had faded and revived beneficence that had been forgotten. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 464} Shortly before his death he issued a rescript to the vizier in his own hand addressed to the men of state, rebuking them while pardoning their past ravaging of the lands and scattering of subjects, commanding justice and forbidding wrong. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 464} After his death they found in a room of his palace thousands of slips, all sealed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 464}
