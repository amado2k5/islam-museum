## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] founded the city of [[place/wasit|Wasit]] in 83 or 84 AH, and built its mosque, its palace and the Green Dome. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.407}

## The name and the site
It was a land of reeds, so it was called Wasit al-Qasab (Wasit of the reeds). It lies at an equal distance from Ahvaz, Basra and Kufa. Al-Baladhuri quotes Ibn al-Qarya: "He built it in a land not his own, and left it to someone not his heir." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

A shaykh of the people of Wasit relates that al-Hajjaj wrote to [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] that he had made a city in the belly of land between the mountain and the two garrisons, so its people were called the Karshiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Rivers and estates
Before Wasit, al-Hajjaj wanted to bring China's wealth from Kaskar, so he dug the River of China, then changed his mind to another. He dug the Nil and the Zabi and revived the lands on them. He took the estates that [[person/abdallah-ibn-darraj|Abd Allah ibn Darraj]] had brought out, and held them for Abd al-Malik. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He carried doors from other cities to his palace and the mosque, and its people cried out, but he paid no attention. This is what al-Baladhuri relates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
