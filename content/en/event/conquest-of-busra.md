## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the Muslims gathered at Busra, the city of Hawran, and appointed [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] to command the fighting for it. Then its people made peace with them for safety in exchange for the [[concept/jizya|jizya]]. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The treaty
The Muslims stayed at [[place/busra|Busra]] and fought its patrician until they drove him back. It is also said that [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-sufyan|Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan]] was in charge of the fighting, because the governorship of it had been given to him from Damascus. Its people made peace for safety for their lives, their property and their children, and some narrators say that the jizya was a dinar and a jarib of wheat for each adult male. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Ma'ab
[[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] marched to Ma'ab in the land of al-Balqa' and conquered it by treaty, on terms like those of Busra. Some say that the conquest of Ma'ab came before Busra, and some that Abu Ubayda conquered it while he was commander of Syria in Umar's days. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
