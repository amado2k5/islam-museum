## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that when Yazdegerd fled from Hulwan, the Persians corresponded with the people of al-Rayy, Qumis, Isfahan and Hamadhan, and gathered to him. They put over them Mardanshah Dhu al-Hajib ([[person/dhu-al-hajib-mardanshah|Dhu al-Hajib]]). Their number was sixty thousand, and it is said a hundred thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 424-428} The place: [[place/nihawand|Nihawand]].

## Mobilisation
Ammar ibn Yasir wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] with news of them, and he ordered that two-thirds of the people of Kufa march and one third remain, and he sent a force from Basra. He appointed [[person/al-numan-ibn-muqarrin|al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin]], and said that if he were killed, the commander would be [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]]. He consulted [[person/hormuzan|al-Hormuzan]], who said that Isfahan was the head and Adharbayjan the two wings. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 424-428}

## The battle
al-Baladhuri relates that al-Nu'man shook his banner three times, and then the Muslims charged. al-Nu'man was the first to fall, and Hudhayfa took the banner, and God granted them victory, and it was named "the conquest of conquests". The left wing was under [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 424-428}

## Differing dates
The conquest was on a Wednesday in 19 AH, and it is also said 20 AH. al-Hasan and Muhammad, quoting Abu Ma'shar, said 21 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 424-428}

See also: [[event/battle-of-nahawand|the battle of Nahawand in al-Tabari and al-Mas'udi]].

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] places Nihawand in the year 21. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 133} He relates Umar's council when he meant to march in person, and Ali's advice that he should not go but send a third of the garrison cities' men, which Umar said was his own view. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 134} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 135} Umar gave command to al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin, and after him, if he fell, Hudhayfa, then Jarir, then al-Mughira, then al-Ash'ath. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 135} The fighting lasted from Wednesday to Friday; al-Nu'man was the first killed, and his brother Suwayd put on his clothes so the men would not know. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 136}
