## Summary
Al-Malik al-Ashraf took the city of Damascus after the death of his brother al-Mu'azzam and the weakness of al-Mu'azzam's son al-Nasir Dawud, and then ruled it autocratically. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482}.
