## Summary
On Monday 2 Sha'ban of 626 AH al-Malik al-Ashraf ibn al-Malik al-Adil took the city of Damascus from his nephew Salah al-Din Dawud ibn al-Mu'azzam, after besieging it with al-Malik al-Kamil. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir gives the cause: when the lord of Damascus feared his uncle al-Kamil he summoned his uncle al-Ashraf for help; al-Ashraf came from the Jaziran lands, entered Damascus to the joy of its lord and people, who had been preparing for a siege — he ordered those precautions abandoned and swore to aid and protect his nephew — then corresponded with al-Kamil and they made terms. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482} Al-Ashraf met his brother al-Kamil on the feast day of Dhu al-Hijja 625 while the lord of Damascus waited at Baysan; afterward, as al-Ashraf and Dawud sat in a tent, Izz al-Din Aybak, a mamluk of the Mu'azzam, came in and drew his master out — he had been told al-Ashraf meant to seize him — and marched him back to Damascus; armies then arrived from al-Kamil to al-Ashraf, who invested Damascus until al-Kamil himself came and the siege bore hard on the inhabitants, whereupon its lord went out and surrendered Damascus on condition of keeping al-Karak, al-Shawbak castle, the Ghawr and Nablus; the citadel of Damascus was handed to al-Ashraf and he entered it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482}
