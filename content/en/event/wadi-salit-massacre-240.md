## Overview
The battle of Wadi Salit, around 240 AH, in which the amir [[person/muhammad-i-andalusi|Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman]] met the people of [[place/tulaytula|Toledo]] and the two kings of Galicia and the Vascones whom they had called in to help. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.386}

## What happened
The people of Toledo had rebelled when the amir took command in 238 AH, and they called for the help of the lord of Galicia in 240 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.386 n.3}

The amir met them at Wadi Salit, where he had laid an ambush for them, and inflicted a defeat on them. The number of the dead from the people of Toledo and their allies reached twenty thousand, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.386}
