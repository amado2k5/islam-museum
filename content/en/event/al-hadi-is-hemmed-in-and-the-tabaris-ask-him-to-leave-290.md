## Overview
Only al-Hadi, the Alawites, the Tabaris and a small band of his companions had stayed on, and he sent his son Ahmad to [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] asking him to come to him with his men; al-Da'am disliked that, and his answer was "tell your father to save himself, this is no time for fighting" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248}. When his son Abu al-Qasim saw this he said: man, get clear of this place, I see matters have grown hard on you and your companions, look behind you and before you; al-Hadi turned and saw the men had taken the road on him, and knew the Tabaris had no way out, so he took his foot out of the stirrup meaning to dismount and fight with the Tabaris and share their fate until they were killed as martyrs {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248}.

## Detail
The Tabaris disliked that and asked him by God not to do it, saying, as the book gives it: your loss is the loss of Islam, and if you remain we hope God will strengthen the truth through you and make the faith prevail by you, and we have been given what we sought, so go on, perhaps God will deliver you {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.248} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.249}. He went, the men met him in front, and he and those with him fought on, before and behind, until some of them were killed and some taken {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.249}. The men then caught up with al-Hadi, their spears reaching him as he fended them off with his whip; one of his servants said, master, draw some of your sword, and he said, by God never, I will not draw my sword unless I water it; a man speared him, he turned, struck the spear with his hand and broke it and threw its head in the man's face, and went on through after great wounds until he and those of the Alawites with him got clear {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.249}. A marginal heading of the book at this point names "Muhammad ibn al-Hadi", while the text names only Abu al-Qasim. Before this is [[event/al-tarif-attack-al-hadi-at-atwa-on-1-rajab-and-the-hamdani-wing-gives-way-290|the attack of Al Tarif at Atwa]]; after it, [[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-is-taken-and-led-through-sanaa-290|the capture of Abu al-Qasim]]. The praise of the Tabaris, the word "martyrs" and the exchange with al-Da'am are the narrator's, given as he gives them and without a verdict; the book does not say why al-Da'am answered as he did.
