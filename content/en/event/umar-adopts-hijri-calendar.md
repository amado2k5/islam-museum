## Summary
[[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] gathered the Companions to establish a fixed calendar by which state affairs could be regulated, and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] said: "From the day the Messenger of God migrated and left the land of polytheism," so Umar adopted it {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.145}.

## Context
The book notes that Umar needed a fixed official calendar to organize and regulate the state, that he considered Ali one of the best people to guide others in this matter, and that Umar used to confide in a man of the Ansar, saying that Ali was the best of leaders {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.145}.

## Notes for Researchers
- The book does not mention the year the calendar was adopted; the listed period is derived from Umar's caliphate and requires verification.
- See [[concept/hijri-calendar|the Hijri calendar]] for astronomical and historical details.
