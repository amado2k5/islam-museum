## Overview
[[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] went forward until he came to a people called [[group/shakir-hamdan-tribe|Shakir of Hamdan]], with whom he had an alliance; he alighted among them and called to them, they answered and undertook to go out with him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167} Then he went on until he reached his companions, who were among [[group/madhhij|Madhhij]], and called and exerted himself; he went out, and the people went out in dense hosts, until he left them on the road and turned aside to Shakir to rouse them, and a great multitude rose with him, and he agreed with his companions on a known day. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167}

## The word reaches al-Hadi
The news reached [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.168} It leads into [[event/al-hadi-warns-the-harithis-of-najran-and-foretells-the-hanging-286]]. The book names none of Shakir and gives the day no date.
