## Summary
Ibn al-Imad, in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*, reports that around 920 AH the "Franks, the Portuguese" appeared in the Indian Ocean, and their harm reached the Arabian Peninsula, the ports of Yemen and Jeddah. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531}

## The Mamluk response
The news reached the sultan [[person/qansuh-al-ghawri|Qansuh al-Ghawri]], who equipped fifty galleys under the command of the amir [[person/husayn-al-kurdi|Husayn al-Kurdi]], together with troops of Turks, Maghribis and Lund. He granted him Jeddah as an iqta and ordered him to fortify it. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531}

## The walls of Jeddah
Husayn began building the wall and its towers, and demolished many people's houses. The author describes this as done with harshness and severity. The wall was completed in less than a year. Then he set out for India, where he met the sultan of Gujarat. The source reports that the Franks left the ports when they heard of his arrival. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531}
