## Summary
Ill from mid-Ramadan, he worsened at the opening of Dhu al-Hijja, made Ibn Khayrun his executor, endowed his books and distributed his estate, and died on Monday 7 Dhu al-Hijja 463; the qadi Ibn al-Muhtadi led the funeral and he was buried at Bab Harb beside Bishr al-Hafi, Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi among the bier-carriers {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 107-108}.
