## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] sent [[person/janada-ibn-abi-umayya|Junada ibn Abi Umayya al-Azdi]] to [[place/rudis|Rhodes]], and he took it by force, and settled Muslims there. This was in 53 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.330}

## Settlement and demolition
al-Baladhuri describes the island as one of the most fertile islands, about sixty miles around, with olives, vines and fruit. On the authority of al-Waqidi, the Muslims stayed there for seven years in a fort they had built for themselves. When Mu'awiya died, [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid]] wrote to Junada ordering him to demolish the fort and withdraw. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.330}

[[person/mujahid-ibn-jabr|Mujahid ibn Jabr]] was living there, teaching the Qur'an to the people.
