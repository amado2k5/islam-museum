## Summary
In Sha'ban 441 AH the people of [[place/al-karkh|al-Karkh]] began building a wall along the Anmat market to fortify their quarter. The Sunni people of al-Qalayin began building a wall around their own market, starting with its gate opposite the gate of the Sammakin. The building became an occasion for rivalry and a resource for troublemakers. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.483-484}

## Processions of brick
The Turks helped the people of al-Qalayin with their money and their mules. A boat loaded with bricks was towed along, its awning of silk brocade and its mast of reed. The people of al-Karkh carried their bricks on men's heads, with drums and horns playing in front of them, and rose water was poured into the foundations, to the point that a woman took off her silk headcloth and gave it to the builders. On the day of Eid al-Fitr strife broke out between the two sides, and there were wounds and deaths. They placed a roof, shops, mats and lamps between the two gates and made it into a mosque, which was called the Gate of Good Fortune (al-Bab al-Mas'ud). {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.483-484}
