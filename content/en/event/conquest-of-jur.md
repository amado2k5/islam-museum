## Overview
Jur is the city of Ardashir Khurra. al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/harim-ibn-hayyan|Harim ibn Hayyan]] was stationed there, and that the Muslims kept attacking it and then withdrawing from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
When [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Ibn Amir]] came down before it, they fought him and then fortified themselves. He took it by the sword, by force, in 29 AH. It is related that it had been raided for several years without its being taken, until the Muslims entered the city by a hidden entrance to it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Ibn Amir also conquered al-Karyan and Fashjan of Darabjird, which had not entered the treaty of al-Hurbadh and had broken it.
