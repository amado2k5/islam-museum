## Summary
[[person/al-shafii|Al-Shafi'i]] died in Egypt in 204 AH, on the last day of Rajab according to Ibn Khallikan. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 464} The biographical books preserve words he spoke to the students who visited him in his illness, which this record gathers: what he said to al-Muzani, to Yunus ibn Abd al-A'la and to al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman, and the place of his burial at al-Qarafa. The books read contain no text of a written testament from him, and al-Dhahabi warns that the "will of al-Shafi'i" reported through al-Husayn ibn Hisham al-Baladi is not sound.

## His illness
Yunus ibn Abd al-A'la says he had never seen anyone suffer from illness as al-Shafi'i did; he visited him and al-Shafi'i said: read to me what comes after the hundred and twentieth verse of [[passage/quran-3-120|Al Imran]]; he read, and when he rose al-Shafi'i said: do not forget me, for I am in distress. Yunus takes it that by this reading he meant what the Prophet and his companions underwent, or something like it. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 17 p. 340} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3292}

## His words to al-Muzani
Al-Muzani relates: I visited al-Shafi'i in the illness in which he died and said, "How are you this morning, Abu Abd Allah?" He raised his head and said: I have become a departer from the world, a parter from my brothers, a meeter of my bad deeds, a comer to God; I do not know whether my soul goes to Paradise, so that I congratulate it, or to the Fire, so that I console it. Then he wept and recited verses in which his hope serves as a ladder to God's pardon. {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-kubra-subki-hajr vol. 1 p. 295} Ibn Khuzayma and others transmit it from al-Muzani in Tarikh al-Islam {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 17 p. 340}, and in the Siyar al-Dhahabi says its chain to him is firm. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3293}

## His words to al-Rabi'
Al-Asamm relates from al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman: I visited al-Shafi'i when he was ill and he asked after our companions; I said they were debating, and he said: I never debated anyone to win, and I would love all people to learn this knowledge, meaning his books, on condition that nothing of it be attributed to me. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 17 p. 341} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3293}

## Death and funeral
Al-Rabi' says this was on Sunday, that he died on Thursday, that they returned from his funeral on Friday night and saw the crescent of Sha'ban in 204, and that he was fifty-odd. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 17 p. 341} Ibn Khallikan says he died on the last day of Rajab in 204. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 464} Ibn Abi Hatim reports from al-Rabi' that al-Azizi, a devout man, dreamed on the night al-Shafi'i died that it was said, "The Prophet died tonight," and in the morning was told that al-Shafi'i had died. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 17 p. 341} The books read do not say who washed him or prayed over him.

## The grave
Ibn Khallikan says al-Shafi'i was buried in the Lesser Qarafa and that his grave is visited there. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 464} The museum has a record of the [[building/mausoleum-of-imam-al-shafii|Mausoleum of Imam al-Shafi'i]] in [[place/al-qarafa|al-Qarafa]], Cairo. Ibn Khallikan also quotes al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman al-Muradi, who saw the crescent of Sha'ban and the traces of the crowd from the funeral, and who dreamed of him afterward and asked what God had done with him; he answered that God seated him on a golden chair and scattered fresh pearls over him. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 464}

## The written will
After a run of reports in al-Shafi'i's entry, al-Dhahabi says: likewise the will of al-Shafi'i by the narration of al-Husayn ibn Hisham al-Baladi is not sound. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 3293} This record therefore attributes no particular written text to al-Shafi'i; no written will transmitted by al-Bayhaqi, al-Subki or Ibn Kathir was found in the books read, so the point stays open for later review.

## Where the reports differ
On the time of death: the last day of Rajab in Ibn Khallikan, Thursday with the funeral ending on Friday night in al-Rabi', and the two can be reconciled. {cite:wafayat-ayan scan vol. 4 p. 464} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi scan vol. 17 p. 341}
