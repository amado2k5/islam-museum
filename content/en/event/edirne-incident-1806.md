## Overview
A second "Edirne incident" occurred in 1806 for reasons like those of the first: a rally against the reforms of Selim III {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## The second incident
It had been preceded five years earlier by the mutiny of Albanian troops at Edirne in 1801 against the same sultan's reforms {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.
