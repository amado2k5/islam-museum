## Summary
When Sultan al-Nasir set about eliminating the senior amirs, Aleppo's governor Shams al-Din Karasunkur fled to the Mongols in 712/1312 together with Damascus's governor Jamal al-Din Aqqush al-Afram — having first tried to win over Syrian amirs including the na'ib of Tripoli. Al-Nuwayri sought to dissuade the governor, recounting their conversation and his arguments, then how he persuaded all but one of Tripoli's amirs not to follow and induced them to renew their oath of allegiance to the sultan {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.

## At the centre of events
The Encyclopaedia of Islam notes that at Tripoli al-Nuwayri stood at the centre of great events: the defectors courted him, and though he could not prevent the flight he contained the damage and renewed the amirs' loyalty {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.
