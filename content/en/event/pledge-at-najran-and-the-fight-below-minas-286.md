## Overview
Some of [[group/banu-abd-al-madan-najran|Banu Abd al-Madan]] and a man of [[group/banu-qatan-najran|Banu Qatan]] called [[person/al-mujahir-ibn-ziyad-al-qatani|al-Mujahir ibn Ziyad]], who had come with a party of Banu Bishr among them Ahmad ibn al-Azyad and Muhammad ibn al-Haytham as the text prints the names, sent to Abu Muhammad that he should meet them with Hamdan, Thaqif and al-Ahlaf, so that they could pledge mutual support and loyalty. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147}

## The pledge
He rode to them with those who followed him; they met and pledged to support and stand with him, and the Madanis and the Khushaymis went home. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147}

## Ibn Bustam and the fort of Minas
[[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] learned of Abu Muhammad's meeting with the Madanis, gathered those of Banu al-Harith near him, brought out the people of [[place/minas-najran|Minas]] and drew them all up before his fort, a fort below Minas. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} Seeing this, Abu Muhammad drew up his companions and set an ambush at one spot; the sides drew near and fighting joined, the ambush came out behind them and Abu Muhammad charged, drove them from the fort, killed two men there, razed the fort and his army took what was in it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147}

## The letter to al-Hadi
Abu Muhammad went back to camp and stayed, and wrote to al-Hadi telling him what Banu al-Harith had done, their opposition and their betrayal. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.147} Al-Hadi wrote back that he was coming himself and sent verses with his letter: see [[event/al-hadi-and-abu-muhammad-exchange-letters-and-verses-from-najran-286]].
