## Overview
Shams al-Din Ibn al-Naqib licensed Taj al-Din al-Subki to issue fatwas and teach before he turned twenty; he was eighteen years old when Ibn al-Naqib died {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/426-427}.
