## Overview
In 232 AH Bugha and Salih al-Abbasi gathered Bedouin tribesmen whom they had brought with them. al-Tabari reports that two thousand two hundred men of Banu Numayr were killed in these battles. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.150}
