## Summary
This is the best-known report on the dotting of the codex, and al-Sabt treats it as the most reliable account of the first official, general dotting. He relays it from the books of al-Dani, al-Zarkashi, al-Suyuti and others, and the sources I read give no particular date. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 329}

## The background
Al-Sabt says Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan saw that Islam's domain had widened, Arabs and non-Arabs had mixed, and foreignness threatened the soundness of the language; confusion in reading the codices was pressing on people so that the common reader found it hard to tell apart the letters and words of an undotted codex. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 328} {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 329}

## What happened
He ordered al-Hajjaj to take this weighty matter in hand, and he commissioned [[person/nasr-ibn-asim|Nasr ibn Asim al-Laythi]] and [[person/yahya-ibn-yamur|Yahya ibn Ya'mar al-Adwani]], both capable of the task, combining knowledge with practice, goodness and piety, and expertise in the foundations of the language and the modes of recitation, both students of Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali. They dotted the codex for the first time, dotting all its similar letters, and committed to no more than three dots on any letter. It then spread among people and had a great effect in removing ambiguity and confusion. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 329}

## What is said alongside
**Ibn Sirin.** The reports mention a dotted codex of Ibn Sirin's that Yahya ibn Ya'mar dotted, recorded by Abu Ubayd, Sa'id ibn Mansur, Ibn al-Duryis, Ibn Abi Dawud and al-Dani. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 329} **Ibn Atiyya.** As in [[question/who-first-pointed-the-mushaf|Who first pointed the codex?]], he says Abd al-Malik ordered it and al-Hajjaj undertook it at Wasit with al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Ya'mar. {cite:muharrar-wajiz-dar-ibn-hazm p.27} **Tens and fives.** Al-Suyuti says in the same place that the marking of tens was ascribed to al-Ma'mun or al-Hajjaj and that Qatada said: 'they began by dotting, then fives, then tens'; see [[concept/tacshir-marking-tens-in-mushaf|marking tens]]. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2245}

## What followed
After the dotting a problem appeared: the earlier vowel signs were dots too, so a new vowelling replaced them; see [[concept/shakl-al-khalil|al-Khalil's vowel signs]]. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 331} I do not know from the sources I read the year or place of the project, so I give no date; see [[concept/naqt-al-ijam|dotting of letters]].
