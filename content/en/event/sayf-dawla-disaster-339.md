## Overview
On Monday the eleventh-from-last of Jumada I 339 came word that Sayf al-Dawla had raided deep into Byzantine lands, taking many fortresses and captives; but when he turned to leave, the Romans seized the pass he meant to exit by. Every Muslim with him perished captive or slain; the Byzantines recovered the captives and took his treasury, horses and arms, and he escaped with a handful — he had set out with a thousand {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 661}.
