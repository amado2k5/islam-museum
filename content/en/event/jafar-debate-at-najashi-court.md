## Summary
The first Islamic debate in the court of a Christian king: the Meccan embassy demands the return of the emigrants from Abyssinia, [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far]] rises and sets out Islam's case, recites Surat Maryam, and [[person/al-najashi|the Negus]] rules for the emigrants. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/337-339}

## The Meccan embassy
When word reached Quraysh that the emigrants had found safety and protection in [[place/abyssinia|Abyssinia]], they sent [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] and Abdallah ibn Abi Rabi'a — then the two cleverest men of Quraysh — with gifts to the Negus, telling his bishops: some of our fools have taken refuge in your land; they abandoned their people's religion without entering yours or ours, and brought an invented religion none of us knows — hand them over so we may return them to their people {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/337}.

## Ja'far's address
The Negus gathered the Muslims and asked: what is this religion in which you left your people, entering neither mine nor any nation's? Ja'far stood and — as the Sira tells it — summed up the passage out of the age of ignorance: worshipping idols, eating carrion, committing outrages, severing kinship, ill-treating neighbours, the strong devouring the weak; then the Messenger's call to God's unity, truthful speech, delivered trusts, kinship kept, good neighbourship and abstention from forbidden things and blood. They believed him, their people turned on them and persecuted them for their religion, until they left for his protection {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/338-339}. The Negus asked: do you have with you anything of what he brought from God? Ja'far recited the opening of Surat Maryam; the Negus wept until his beard was wet and his bishops wept until their books were wet, and he said: this and what Jesus brought come from one lamp {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/339}.

## The Qur'an as the argument
The next day Amr returned and said: they say something monstrous about Jesus son of Mary. The Negus sent for them and asked. Ja'far said: we say of him what our Prophet brought — he is God's servant, His spirit and His word, cast into the untouched virgin Mary. The Negus struck the ground with his hand, picked up a twig and said: Jesus son of Mary does not exceed what you said by this twig; he confirmed their asylum and returned the embassy's gifts {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/339}. It was a complete disputation: question and answer, a recited text, and a verdict between two cases.
