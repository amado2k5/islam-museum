## Overview
Muzaffar ibn Alyan corresponds with al-Qasim ibn Ahmad, promises him a share of his land, swears to him, and returns to Gharq on Saturday in mid Jumada II 325 AH.

## Detail
The book says Ibn al-Dahhak did not keep his terms with Muzaffar ibn Alyan. Muzaffar then corresponded with al-Qasim ibn Ahmad, agreed to give him a share in his land, swore to him, and went back to Gharq on Saturday in mid Jumada II of the same year. Al-Qasim returned to Warwar on Thursday, two days before the end of the month.
