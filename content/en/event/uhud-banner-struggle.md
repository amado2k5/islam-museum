## Overview
A fierce fight and single combats around the banner of the Quraysh at the [[event/battle-of-uhud|Battle of Uhud]], which ended with the extermination of ten of the banner-bearers of the Banu Abd al-Dar. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.259}
