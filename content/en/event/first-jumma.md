## Summary
The first Friday prayer the Prophet prayed with the Muslims, on the well-known account: among Banu Salim ibn Awf, in the mosque in the belly of the valley — a hundred men {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172}.

## What happened
On the fifth day after his halting at [[place/quba|Quba]] — a Friday — the Prophet mounted with [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] riding behind him and sent word to Banu al-Najjar, his maternal kin, who came wearing their swords; he set out toward the city {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172}.

Friday prayer-time caught up with him among Banu Salim ibn Awf, so he held the congregational prayer with them in the mosque in the belly of the valley — a hundred men {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. After the Friday prayer he entered the city, and from that day Yathrib was called the City of the Messenger {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 172}.

## For Sprouts
The Prophet led the first Friday prayer with Muslims in the Ranuna valley, gathering people in love, remembrance, and unity.

## For Explorers
On his journey from Quba toward central Medina, Friday prayer arrived while the Prophet was among the Banu Salim ibn Awf in the Ranuna valley. There he led the first Friday congregational prayer, delivering a moving sermon that encouraged God-consciousness, cooperation, compassion, and sharing food, where the historic Friday Mosque was later built.

## For Scholars
The inaugural Friday congregational prayer (Juma) established by the Prophet following his migration to Medina, taking place in the Ranuna valley within the territory of Banu Salim ibn Awf as he traveled from Quba into the city proper. The Prophet delivered the first formal Friday sermon (khutba), emphasizing ethical righteousness, brotherly affection, mutual charity, and wholesome speech. At this sacred location, the historic Friday Mosque (Masjid al-Juma) was constructed, initiating the weekly congregational rite that stands as a pillar of Islamic communal unity and civic life.
