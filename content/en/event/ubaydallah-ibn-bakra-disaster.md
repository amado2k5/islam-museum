## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that when al-Hajjaj became governor of Iraq he sent [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-abi-bakra|Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra]] to [[place/sijistan|Sistan]]. He marched until he camped near Kabul, and the enemy took a valley against him, and [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] came upon them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace and the battle
Ubayd Allah made peace with them for five hundred thousand dirhams and three of his sons as hostages, and a writ that he would not raid them while he was governor. [[person/shurayh-ibn-hani|Shurayh ibn Hani al-Harithi]] advised him to fight them. They fought and Shurayh was killed, and the people escaped exhausted, and many of them died of thirst and hunger in the desert of Bust. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Ubayd Allah died of grief for what had befallen the people; it is said that he complained of an ear and died, and he appointed his son Abu Bardha'a as his successor. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
