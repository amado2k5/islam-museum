## Overview
The plans for the siege of Constantinople were worked out in Edirne, and the great guns were tested in its environs {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## Preparing the siege
The sultan had the road between Edirne and Constantinople levelled for hauling the giant cannon, which moved from Edirne to the vicinity of the city in two months {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 93}.
