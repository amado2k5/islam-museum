## Overview
In Rabi' al-Akhir 351 AH the common Shi'a of Baghdad wrote on the mosques a curse that named Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan, and this was done by order of [[person/muizz-al-dawla|Mu'izz al-Dawla]]. No one else was named in the curse. Ibn al-Athir relates this in "al-Kamil". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 402}

## What the source says
The source gives only the report, and the data does not detail the reaction of the people of the Sunna or what followed. It needs an additional source.
