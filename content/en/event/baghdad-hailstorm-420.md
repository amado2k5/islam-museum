## Overview
In 420 giant hail fell at al-Nu'maniyya in Iraq — each stone a pound and more; one was said to exceed a qintar. It lay half a cubit deep on the ground like a crouching bull — the like unheard of in history — and uprooted the mighty trunks of olives and apples {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 214}.
