## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates how [[person/ibrahim-ibn-al-aghab|Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab]] became governor of the Maghrib in the caliphate of [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]], after having been one of the notables of the army of Egypt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.327}

## From rising to governorship
Ibrahim rose up with twelve men, who took from the treasury only as much as their pay and no more, and they went to the place of al-Zab east of Qayrawan. The governor of the frontier at that time was [[person/harthama-ibn-ayan|Harthama ibn Ayan]]. Ibrahim won his favour with gifts, and wrote that he had not gone out of obedience, and that need had driven him. So Harthama gave him charge of his district. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.327}

## Al-Rashid's decision
When Harthama was removed, Ibn al-Akki came after him, and his conduct grew so bad that the district rose against him. Al-Rashid consulted Harthama, who advised reconciling with Ibrahim and appointing him. Al-Rashid wrote to him that he had pardoned his offence and appointed him over the Maghrib; he stood at the frontier and held it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.327}

The date recorded in the data is approximate, between about 795 and 800 CE, and the conversion is derived.
