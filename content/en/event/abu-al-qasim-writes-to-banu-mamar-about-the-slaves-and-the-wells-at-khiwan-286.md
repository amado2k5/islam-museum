## Overview
[[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]] wrote to his opponents telling them of [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]]'s fight and the help God gave him against [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.183} He wrote to [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]], privately and otherwise, letters telling them of it too, urging them to turn back to God and stop persisting in falsehood, and to drive out of their land the slaves who were with [[person/ibn-al-dahhak-rebel-at-khiwan-286|Ibn al-Dahhak]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.183}

## The slaves and the wells
The narrator says the slaves cut the road on the weak and that Ibn al-Dahhak told them to; he told them to bury a well the people drank from, and they came by night and buried it until Abu al-Qasim ordered it dug again. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.183} The aunt of al-Da'am's wife, on the edition's reading, told one of her servants to throw tar into a well at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] so that things grew narrow for Abu al-Qasim and he left Khiwan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.183} The edition's printed word reads "al-mubid" in one place where the museum reads "al-'abid" (the slaves), since the sentence before it says so.

## The admonition
Foolish men of Banu Ma'mar sided with Ibn al-Dahhak in what he was doing, and Abu al-Qasim wrote to them admonishing them and reminding them of their precedence and of what [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]] did with Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.183} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.184} He sent verse with the letter, described in [[event/abu-al-qasims-verse-to-hamdan-and-the-hamstringing-of-ibn-al-dahhaks-mare-286]].
