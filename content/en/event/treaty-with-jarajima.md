## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates from the elders of Antioch that the Jarajima were the people of the town of al-Jarjuma on the mountain of al-Lukam. [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] made peace with them after the conquest of Antioch, on condition that they be helpers, informants and guards for the Muslims, that no poll tax be taken from them, and that they have the spoils of whoever they kill of the enemy in war alongside the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.217-219}

## Who entered the peace
Whoever was in their town of merchants, hired men and followers of the Nabataeans and villagers entered the peace, and they were called the Rawadif. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.217-219}

## Afterwards
Al-Baladhuri says that they sometimes stood by the governors and sometimes leaned away, and corresponded with the Byzantines. In the days of Ibn al-Zubayr, [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] made peace with them for a thousand dinars every Friday. Then he sent [[person/suhaym-ibn-al-muhajir|Suhaym ibn al-Muhajir]] to the Byzantine commander and killed him, and proclaimed safety; the Jarajima scattered, and most of them later returned to their town. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.217-219}

The date is approximate, around 16 AH, and the conversion is derived. The event ties the matter to the concept of [[concept/dhimma|protection]] as a peace of service without poll tax.
