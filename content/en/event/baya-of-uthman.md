## Overview
[[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] was given the oath of allegiance to the caliphate three nights after Umar was buried, at the end of Dhu al-Hijja 23 AH, according to al-Suyuti. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.266}

## The council
Before his death Umar sent to [[person/abu-talha-al-ansari|Abu Talha al-Ansari]] that he should be with fifty of the Ansar at the door of the men of the council, and let no one enter to them, and not let them pass the third day before they appointed one of them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.266}

## Consulting Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf
[[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]] removed himself from candidacy, and people began to consult him. He asked Uthman in private whom he would advise if he did not pledge allegiance to him, and Uthman said Ali. He asked [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], and Ali said Uthman. Then he consulted the notables and saw that the inclination of most of them was toward Uthman. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.266-267}

He offered Ali to pledge allegiance to him on the Book of God, the Sunna of His Messenger and the conduct of Abu Bakr and Umar. Ali said: "As far as I am able." He offered it to Uthman, who said: "Yes." So Abd al-Rahman gave him his allegiance, and then the Muhajirun and the Ansar pledged to him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.266-267}

al-Suyuti quotes Ibn Mas'ud: "Our amir is the best of those who remain, and we did not fall short." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.267}
