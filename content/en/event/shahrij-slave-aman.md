## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the Muslims besieged Shahrij for a month. They fought its people for a day, then returned to their camp, and one of their slaves stayed behind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The safe conduct
The slave wrote a safe conduct for the people of the fortress and shot it to them on an arrow. When the Muslims returned to fighting, the people of the fortress came out and said: this is your safe conduct. They wrote about it to Umar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Umar's answer
Umar wrote that the slave of the Muslims is one of the Muslims, and that his protection is as their protection, so they should enforce his safe conduct. They enforced it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
In one account the fortress was at Siraf. The Muslims said the safe conduct of a slave was worth nothing, and the people said that they could not tell a free man from a slave. They wrote to Umar, and he replied in the same terms. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
