## Overview
In Rabi' I 285 the postmaster wrote from Kufa: a yellow wind rose, turned black while the people were in supplication, then the sky rained hard with continuous thunder and lightning — and the village of Ahmadabadh rained white and black stones. From Basra: the wind rose after Friday yellow, then green, then black, with rain such as had never been seen; hail fell, single stones weighing a hundred and fifty dirhams; the wind uprooted five hundred palms on the River al-Hasan and a hundred on the River Ma'qil; the Tigris swelled beyond all memory and many buildings around it collapsed {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}.
