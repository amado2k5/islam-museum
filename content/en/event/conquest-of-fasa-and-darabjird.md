## Overview
In the events of 23 AH al-Tabari reports that Sariya ibn Zunaym went against Fasa and Darabjird and besieged them. They then sought reinforcement, and the Kurds of Fars gathered to them, and a great battle took place. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.178}

## al-Tabari's account
al-Tabari relates that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] saw the battle in his dream and called out to the people, and he ordered Sariya to the mountain, and God routed the enemy. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.178}
