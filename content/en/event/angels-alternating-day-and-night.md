## The story
Al-Nasai relates marfu' that the Prophet told that God said: angels alternate among you by night and angels by day, and they gather at the Fajr and Asr prayers; then those who spent the night among you ascend and He asks them — though He knows best — how they left My servants, and they say: we left them praying and came to them praying {cite:sunan-al-nasai} [[passage/nasai-0485|Nasai 485]].

## The meaning
In this hadith qudsi the angels witness the servants' worship in their homes, gather at the prescribed prayers, and are asked about the state of the servants before God {cite:sunan-al-nasai}.
