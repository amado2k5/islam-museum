## Overview
Ibn al-Jawzi began his career under al-Muktafi through the patronage of the Hanbali vizier Ibn Hubayra, holding a waʿẓ session every Friday in Ibn Hubayra's house {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 783}.
