## Overview
A party of Wada'a raided a secret herd of Yam, killed a man of Yam named Maymun ibn Muhammad al-Dhuhli, wounded another, and drove off much livestock. They then raided another nomad camp and killed a man of Banu Ubayd named Shaddad.

## Detail
The book says that a party of Wada'a raided a secret herd of Yam and treacherously killed one of their men, Maymun ibn Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Dhuhli, wounded another, and drove off much livestock. They ordered their companions not to tell al-Hadi until the last part of the night, lest he learn of it and stop them. They then raided another nomad camp at once, killed a man of Banu Ubayd named Shaddad, and took the property he had with him. The book places these raids in the context of the strife among the Hamdan that Ibn Bistam is said to have wanted, as the next event shows.
