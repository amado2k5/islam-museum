## Overview
On Sunday, fourteen days of Sha'ban gone, the men set out with their armies from all their camps and gathered at the foot of [[place/nuqm-sanaa-yemen|Nuqm]] in a large army {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. The narrator says: one I trust told me that their army that day was about twelve thousand, horse and foot {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228}. [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] went out from [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] with about five hundred of the emigrants and others of its people, leaving by the Darb al-Qutay', and the men drew near, the fighting closed and held for an hour; then al-Hadi charged them, the men were routed and the sword fell among them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.228} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. The people of Bayt Bus withdrew to it and the people of Nuqm to Nuqm; five of the [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|Tabaris]] were killed that day, whom the narrator calls martyrs {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. Al-Hadi returned to Sana'a, and the men stayed at Nuqm for the rest of the month while al-Hadi's men raided them by night and day {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}.

## Detail
A Sunday and the fourteenth of Sha'ban do not agree in the tabular calendar, so the museum gives the month only. The count of the men is from an informant the narrator does not name. The copyist's margin says that the Tabaris' graves lie near the road in Sana'a with a visited shrine over them known as the graves of the Tabaris; that is the copyist's statement in his own time, not the museum's. It follows [[event/al-hadi-fights-at-alab-and-al-jud-under-bayt-bus-288]].
