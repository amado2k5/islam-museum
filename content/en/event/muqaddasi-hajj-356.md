## Overview
Al-Muqaddasi made the pilgrimage to Mecca in 356/967 — the first of his dated journeys that can be recovered from his own book, since his life is known only through Ahsan al-Taqasim {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 544}.
