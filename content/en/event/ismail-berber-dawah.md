## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that Isma'il ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi al-Muhajir, governor of the Maghrib in the caliphate of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]], called the Berbers to Islam. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.323}

## The account
Umar appointed over the Maghrib [[person/ismail-ibn-abdallah-ibn-abi-al-muhajir|Isma'il ibn Abd Allah]], a client of the Banu Makhzum, who governed well. He called the [[group/berbers|Berbers]] to Islam. Umar wrote letters to them to this effect, and Isma'il read them to them in the districts. Al-Baladhuri says that Islam afterwards prevailed in the Maghrib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.323}

The date is approximate, between 717 and 720 CE, and the conversion is derived.
