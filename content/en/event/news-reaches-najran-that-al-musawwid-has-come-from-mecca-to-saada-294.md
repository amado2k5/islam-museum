## Overview
In Dhu al-Hijja pilgrims from Mecca brought word of Najah, whom the book calls al-Musawwid, and reported that he had reached Sa'da from Mecca. The troublemakers pinned their hopes on his coming.

## Detail
The book says that in Dhu al-Hijja pilgrims came from Mecca with news of Najah, whom the book calls al-Musawwid, and that he had travelled from Mecca to Sa'da. The editor's footnote says that Najah was the man the Abbasid caliph al-Muktafi had appointed over Yemen, who removed the Al Ya'fur from it; that he first resolved to come in person, and that he later changed his intention. The rising of Banu al-Harith and Ya'am, described below, depends on the hope of his support. The identification of al-Musawwid with Najah rests on the editor's note and is not settled by the text.
