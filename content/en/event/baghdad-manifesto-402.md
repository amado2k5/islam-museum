## Summary
In Rabi' al-Akhir 402 AH the well-known record impugning the lineage of the Fatimids of Egypt was written in the caliphal diwan at Baghdad, and senior Alids, judges, and leading jurists and hadith scholars affixed their signatures to it. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.255-256}

## The testimony
The signatories testified that the rulers of Egypt, descended from Ubayd Allah al-Mahdi, were pretenders and Kharijites with no lineage among the descendants of Ali ibn Abi Talib; that their claim to be of the People of the House was false and baseless; that this denial was widespread in the two Sanctuaries and well known in the Maghrib; and that they had departed from the ways of Islam and made forbidden things lawful. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.255}

## Principal signatories
A great number of the notables of Baghdad signed the record. From among the Alids came [[person/al-sharif-al-radi|al-Sharif al-Radi]], his brother [[person/al-sharif-al-murtada|al-Sharif al-Murtada]], and Ibn al-Azraq al-Musawi. From among the judges came Abu Muhammad ibn al-Akfani and Abu al-Qasim al-Kharzi. From among the leading jurists came [[person/abu-hamid-al-isfarayini|Abu Hamid al-Isfara'ini]], Abu al-Husayn al-Qaduri the Hanafi, Abu Abd Allah al-Saymari, and Abu Ali ibn Hamkan. The record was read out in Basra and in all the other regions. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.255-256}
