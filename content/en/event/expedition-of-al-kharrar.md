## Summary
In Dhu al-Qa'da 1 AH (May 623 CE) the Prophet sent a third detachment under [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] of twenty men of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]] to intercept a Quraysh caravan passing the place of [[place/al-kharrar|al-Kharrar]] near al-Juhfa. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 198}

## The march and the outcome
The Prophet bound a white banner for the detachment, carried by [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad ibn Amr]], and ordered them not to go beyond al-Kharrar. The fighters travelled on foot, lying hidden by day and marching by night, until they reached al-Kharrar on the morning of the fifth day — to find the caravan had passed the day before, and the detachment returned without an encounter. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 198} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}
