## Summary
In Dhu al-Qa'da 1 AH (May 623 CE) the Prophet sent a third detachment (sariyya) under [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]], with twenty men of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]], to intercept a Quraysh caravan passing the place of [[place/al-kharrar|al-Kharrar]] near al-Juhfa {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.

## The march and the outcome
The Prophet gave the detachment a white banner, carried by [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad ibn Amr]], and ordered them not to go beyond al-Kharrar. The fighters went on foot, hiding by day and marching by night, until they reached al-Kharrar on the morning of the fifth day. They found that the caravan had passed the day before, and the detachment returned without any encounter {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/84}.

## In The Sealed Nectar (pp.197-200)
In Dhu al-Qa'da of the first year, the Prophet sent Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas with twenty riders to intercept the Quraysh caravan. They did not go beyond al-Kharrar, because the caravan had passed the day before {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.198}.
