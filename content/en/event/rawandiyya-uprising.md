## Overview
Al-Tabari mentions the rising of the Rawandiyya. He relates that some said their affair and the affair of Abu Ja'far was in 137 AH or 136 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.505}

## Al-Suyuti's account
Al-Suyuti says that the Rawandiyya, who held the doctrine of metempsychosis, appeared in 141 AH, and that [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] killed them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 424}

## Note
The two accounts differ on the year, so the date is marked as disputed. The report needs an additional source.
