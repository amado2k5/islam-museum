## Overview
In 291 AH al-Tabari mentions the battle between the men of the sultan and the men of the Qarmati, lord of al-Shama. In Muharram the sultan's men routed the Qarmatis and took many of them captive. Then the Qarmati fled into the desert. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.108}
