## Summary
A raid the Prophet sent in Rabi' al-Awwal of year 9 AH under [[person/al-dahhak-ibn-sufyan|al-Dahhak ibn Sufyan al-Kilabi]] to his own people [[group/banu-kilab|Banu Kilab]], to call them to Islam {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## The raid
Al-Dahhak called them to Islam; they refused and fought, so the Muslims defeated them and killed one man of them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet sent a man to his own people to call them gently to Islam first; when they fought him the Muslims defended themselves and prevailed.
