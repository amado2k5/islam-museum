## Summary
In 604 AH the Georgians marched en masse into the wilaya of Akhlat in Armenia, plundering, killing, capturing and enslaving, and ranged through the country unopposed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 320}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Georgians came in their multitudes to the Akhlat wilaya of Armenia, plundered, killed and took many captives, and ranged the country in safety; no force came out of Akhlat to stop them, for its lord was a boy and the regent of his state did not command such obedience over the soldiery. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 320} As the affliction grew severe the people urged one another on; all the Muslim troops of the wilaya gathered with many volunteers and marched on the Georgians, though afraid. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 320} Ibn al-Athir relates that one man saw the deceased righteous shaykh Muhammad al-Basti in a dream saying he had come to aid the Muslims against their enemy; the dream was told to the army's commander, whose resolve strengthened, and he led the troops against the Georgians. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 320}
