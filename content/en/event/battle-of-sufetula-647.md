## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports that when Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh governed Egypt he kept sending cavalry raids to the edges of Ifriqiya, then asked Uthman's leave to invade; Uthman called for volunteers and put al-Harith ibn al-Hakam over them until they reached Ibn Sa'd. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 246} The ruler of Ifriqiya, [[person/jurjir-patrician-of-ifriqiya|Jurjir]], sat at Qartajanna; he was killed, his killer being, as they claim, Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr. The leaders of Ifriqiya then offered money for the army to leave; Ibn Sa'd accepted and returned to Egypt, appointing no governor and founding no camp. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 246} {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 247} Al-Layth dates the conquest of Ifriqiya to the year 27. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 252}
