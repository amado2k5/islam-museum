## Summary
[[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas ibn Malik]] reports that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] wished to write to the king of the Romans, he was told that they do not read a letter unless it is sealed. So he made a ring of silver. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Description of the seal
Anas said that it was as if he were looking at the whiteness of the ring on the Prophet's hand. It was engraved with "Muhammad, Messenger of God." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
