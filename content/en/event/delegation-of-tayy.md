## Overview
A delegation of [[group/tayy|Tayy]] came to the Prophet, and among them was their chief [[person/zayd-al-khayl|Zayd al-Khayl]]. Islam was offered to them, and they embraced it and their Islam was good. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## The account
Ibn Ishaq relates, on the authority of men of Tayy whom he does not suspect, that the Prophet said: no man of the Arabs was mentioned to him for merit and then came to him without being found less than what was said of him, except Zayd al-Khayl, who did not reach all that was in him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224} He then named him Zayd al-Khayr (Zayd of Goodness), and granted him [[place/fayd|Fayd]] and lands with it, and wrote this down for him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224} When he went out returning to his people, the Prophet said: "If Zayd survives the fever of Medina." Ibn Ishaq says that the Prophet named the fever by a name other than "the fever" and "Umm Mildamm", and the narrator did not establish it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224} When he had come from the land of Najd to a water of its waters called [[place/farda|Farda]], he was struck by the fever there and died. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224} When he felt death approaching, he said two verses mentioning his people's departure toward the eastern lands and his leaving him in a house at Farda in Najd. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224} After his death his wife took the books that the Prophet had granted him and burned them with fire. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## From the editors' explanation
"Fayd" is the name of a place; "munjid" means in Najd; "yubriyhi" means the journey wears him down and weakens him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## Markers of the report
- The report is from "men of Tayy whom he does not suspect"; in the name of the fever the narrator "did not establish it". {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}
- The order of this delegation is after the delegation of Banu Hanifa in Ibn Ishaq, and before the report of Adi ibn Hatim. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/224}

## For young visitors
Zayd, the chief of Tayy, came with his people and became a Muslim. The Prophet liked him so much that he changed his name from Zayd al-Khayl to Zayd al-Khayr and gave him land, but he fell ill on the way back and died.
