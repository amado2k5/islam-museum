## Summary
Al-Safadi died at Damascus in the night of Saturday to Sunday, 10 Shawwal 764/23 July 1363, during one of the city's periodic plague outbreaks, while holding the post of wakil al-khizana; al-Dhahabi called him 'the accomplished littérateur and writer who shared in all the disciplines', and Ibn Kathir credits him with nearly two hundred volumes {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 vol. 2 p. 88-89}.
