## Summary
[[person/ibrahim-ibn-muhammad|Ibrahim]], the Prophet's son by [[person/maria-al-qibtiyya|Maria the Copt]], died in Medina in infancy on 28 or 29 Shawwal 10 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 475}.

## The account
Maria the Copt bore the Prophet his son Ibrahim, who died in infancy at Medina in his lifetime on 28 or 29 Shawwal 10 AH, corresponding to 27 January 632 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 475}. The Prophet stood at his grave with tears in his eyes and said: the eye weeps and the heart grieves, and we say nothing but what pleases our Lord; and we are grieved for you, Ibrahim {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. Ibrahim was the last of his children to die in his lifetime; no child of his remained {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 473, 475}.

## For young visitors
Ibrahim, the Prophet's son, died while still small. He wept for him and said the eye weeps and the heart grieves, but we say only what pleases God.
