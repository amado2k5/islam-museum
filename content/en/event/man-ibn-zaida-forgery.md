## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/man-ibn-zaida|Ma'n ibn Za'ida]] engraved the caliph's seal in the time of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], and with it obtained money from the land tax of Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Imprisonment and flight
Umar wrote to [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] to carry out his order, and Ma'n was imprisoned. He schemed to get out: he sent to his family for his she-camel and his slave girl, and left by night until he came to Umar, who was waking the people who prayed at night for the dawn prayer. He said he had come to him repentant. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What Umar decided
Umar consulted the people. One said: cut off his hand; another said: crucify him. [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] said that a man who tells a lie is punished in his own joy. Umar beat him and imprisoned him, then after a time he reconsidered, shared the money with him and let him go. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The place in the event data is [[place/kufa|Kufa]].
