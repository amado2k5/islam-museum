## Summary
In 454/1062 Abu al-Qasim al-Qushayri was at the outskirts of Qazvin in Sultan Tughril Beg's company, reciting his own verse — among it the line 'my heart is on the fire like the aloeswood and the oud'; through these years of exile from Hanafi-dominated Nishapur he was moving between refuges with his family and some of his sons, making Tus his seat {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 175-176} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 564}.
