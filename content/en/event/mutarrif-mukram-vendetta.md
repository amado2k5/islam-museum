## Overview
[[person/mutarrif-ibn-saydan|Mutarrif ibn Saydan al-Bahili]] was appointed by Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr as his police chief in Iraq. Two men were brought to him: al-Nabi ibn Ziyad ibn Zubyan and a Tamimi man, who had cut the road. Al-Nabi was killed and the Tamimi was flogged. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
When Mutarrif was removed and given Ahwaz, [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-ziyad-ibn-zubyan|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad ibn Zubyan]] gathered a force for him and went out against him. They met with a river between them; Mutarrif crossed, and Ibn Zubyan stabbed him and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The vengeance
Mus'ab sent [[person/mukram-ibn-mutarrif|Mukram ibn Mutarrif]] in pursuit of the killer. He reached a place known as the camp of Mukram but did not meet him. Ibn Zubyan joined Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and fought with him against Mus'ab and killed him. The source quotes a verse by al-Ba'ith al-Sukri connected with the event. The place is [[place/khuzistan|Khuzistan]]; the date is not given and needs an additional source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
