## Overview
[[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] says he saw [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] pray the Friday prayer and leave by a road other than the one he had come by; he told him this road was wider, and al-Hadi answered that the Messenger of God used to return by a different road from the one he went by. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.56} Passing the prison he dismounted, the people stopped at its door, and he went in, sat and looked it over. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.56}

## What he ordered
He ordered the prison swept ("qamm") and cleaned, the prisoners looked after with water and food, bedding for them, and the shackles of the shackled opened at each prayer time. Their fetters were a ring with a nail and a ring with a lock, and the lock was opened with a key at each prayer time. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.56} A margin note of the original reads "his order to sweep and clean the prison", and al-Qamus is cited for "qamm" meaning to sweep. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.56} The text gives neither the number of prisoners nor the reason for their detention. It is a Zaydi report attributing the act to the imam.
