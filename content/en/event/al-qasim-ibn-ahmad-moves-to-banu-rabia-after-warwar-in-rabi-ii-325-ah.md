## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Ahmad leaves Warwar on the night of Wednesday, three days before the end of Rabi II 325 AH, and reaches the land of Banu Rabi'a. He later returns to Warwar.

## Detail
The book says al-Qasim left Warwar at night, on Wednesday three days before the end of Rabi II of the same year, and reached the land of Banu Rabi'a. He then came back to Warwar on Thursday, two days before the end of the month, and kept going between Warwar and the land of Banu Rabi'a. The book's own description of the manner of his leaving is not repeated here. Weekdays and dates are as printed.
