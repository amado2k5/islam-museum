## Overview
In 282 AH [[person/qatr-al-nada|Qatr al-Nada]], daughter of [[person/khumarawayh|Khumarawayh]] ibn Ahmad ibn Tulun, was conducted to the caliph [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]]. He went in to her in Rabi al-Awwal, and al-Suyuti says that her dowry included four thousand jewelled bracelets and ten chests of gems. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 574}

## What the source says
The source is al-Suyuti's "History of the Caliphs". It names the bride as Qatr al-Nada, the groom as al-Mu'tadid, and her father as Khumarawayh. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 574}

No other details of the celebration or of its political effect are given in the museum's data.
