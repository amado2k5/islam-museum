## Overview
Ali ibn al-Fadl dies at al-Mudhaykhira in Rabi II 303 AH, and his son takes over.

## Detail
Ali ibn al-Fadl falls ill; the book says the illness burst from his lower belly, and that he died in the worst state. He died on Wednesday in the middle of Rabi II 303 AH. His son takes over at al-Mudhaykhira and kills many of his father's companions. The editor's note quotes al-Khazraji's account that he was killed by poison in secret while being bled. The book's curses on the dead man and his son are not reproduced in this record.
