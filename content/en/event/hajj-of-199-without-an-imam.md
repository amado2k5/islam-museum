## Overview
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says that [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] sent Sulayman ibn Dawud ibn Isa ibn Musa to lead the pilgrimage of 199, but Ibn al-Aftas, whom he names Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Hasan ibn Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, rose at [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and raised the white banner; Sulayman withdrew without going to Arafa, and the people stood at Arafa without an imam. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 469}

## A disputed name
A marginal note in the manuscript says that al-Aftas was al-Hasan ibn Ali and that the man in control of Mecca in the days of Abu al-Saraya was Husayn ibn al-Aftas; the editor draws attention to it. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 469}

## The following year
In the year 200 Abu Ishaq, son of the commander of the faithful, led the pilgrimage; Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn Muhammad and Ibn al-Aftas rose at Mecca, and Abu Ishaq overcame them and sent them to al-Ma'mun. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 470}
