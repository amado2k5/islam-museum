## Overview
At the end of Rabi' I 338 a fitna broke out between Sunnis and Shi'is in Karkh and it was looted; and on Thursday with eleven nights remaining of Jumada II the qadi Abu'l-Sa'ib 'Utba b. 'Ubayd Allah al-Hamadhani was removed from the chief judgeship {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 656}.
