## Summary
The Buyid ruler [[person/abu-kalijar|Abu Kalijar]] built the wall of the city of [[place/shiraz|Shiraz]], which Ibn al-Athir records under the events of 425; it ran twelve thousand cubits long and eight cubits wide, with eleven gates, and was finished about 440. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p. 15}

## A wall of eleven gates
Ibn al-Athir records under the events of 425 AH that Abu Kalijar the Buyid restored Mamdhan's estates and built the wall of the city of Shiraz; the wall measured twelve thousand cubits in length and eight cubits in width, had eleven gates, and its construction was completed about 440 AH. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p. 15}
