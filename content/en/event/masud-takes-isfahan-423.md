## Summary
In 423 AH the king Mas'ud ibn Mahmud ibn Subuktakin marched, entered Isfahan by the sword, and killed countless scholars {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 226}.

## The historian's judgement
Ibn al-Imad condemned Mas'ud's act there in harsh terms, describing what was done to the people of the city {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 226}.
