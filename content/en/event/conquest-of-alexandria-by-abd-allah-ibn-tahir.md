## Overview
After Abd Allah ibn Tahir left al-Raqqa for Egypt, al-Tabari mentions his conquest of Alexandria in 210 AH, and it is also said in 211 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.610} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.613}
