## Summary
At the end of Rajab in the year 429, the decree of the caliph [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir Billah]] was issued requiring the protected people (dhimmis) to change the outward dress by which they are recognised on sight. It was issued at the Caliphal Residence in Baghdad, in the presence of the nobles, judges, witnesses, jurists, the Catholicos of the Christians and the Exilarch (Ra's al-Jalut) of the Jews. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.438-439}

## The reading
The reading stated that the rightly guided caliphs had imposed on the protected people fixed limits of subordination, humility and submissiveness, and of distinction from the Muslims in dress. It added that when this group became careless about these limits, they took on the likeness of the Muslims in their dress; so the Commander of the Faithful saw fit to instruct all protected people to display what distinguishes them. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.438-439}
