## Summary
Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj entered Baghdad several times and its people transmitted from him; his last entry was in the year 259 {cite:wafayat-ayan v p. 195}. A great many transmitted from him, among them al-Tirmidhi, who counted him among the trustworthy {cite:wafayat-ayan v p. 195}.
