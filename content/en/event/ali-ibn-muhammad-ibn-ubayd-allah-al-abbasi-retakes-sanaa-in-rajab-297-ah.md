## Overview
On al-Hadi's orders an Abbasi named Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah sets out with a company of his men. They drive the Qarmatian garrison out of Sanaa and enter on Thursday, eleven nights before the end of Rajab 297 AH, and the people of Sanaa are given safety.

## Detail
The book says al-Hadi sent an Abbasi, Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubayd Allah, descended from al-Abbas ibn Ali, with a company of his men. A Qarmatian commander with a force was in the city; they fought him, drove him out, and entered on Thursday, eleven nights before the end of Rajab 297 AH. They stayed some days and the people were given safety. The passage also says he wrote to al-Da'am, whose role here is not clear. No person record is made for Ali ibn Muhammad, whose identity is not checked against a second source.
