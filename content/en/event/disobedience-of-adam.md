## The command to prostrate
God told the angels He would create humankind and knew what they would not, and commanded them to prostrate to Adam; they all prostrated except Iblis, who said 'I am better than him; You created me from fire and created him from clay' [[passage/quran-2-30|al-Baqarah 30]] [[passage/quran-7-11|al-A'raf 11]].

## Iblis's refusal and expulsion
God said to Iblis 'what prevented you from prostrating to what I created with My hands?' and he said 'I am better than him'; he swore by His might to mislead Adam and all his descendants, and was expelled and cursed, having no power over anyone but those He chose; God said to him 'descend from it; it is not for you to be arrogant in it' [[passage/quran-7-12|al-A'raf 12]] [[passage/quran-15-32|al-Hijr 32]].

## The whispering and the disobedience
Then Satan whispered to the two of them and removed them from the tree, and their nakedness appeared to them; he said 'your Lord only forbade you this tree lest you become angels or become immortal', and they called on their Lord 'our Lord, we have wronged ourselves; if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will surely be among the losers' [[passage/quran-7-20|al-A'raf 20]] [[passage/quran-20-121|Ta Ha 121]].

## Notes
In surah Ta Ha Iblis swears by the truth that he will mislead many generations, and God says 'upon you is the curse until the Day of Requital' [[passage/quran-20-115|Ta Ha 115]] [[passage/quran-20-123|Ta Ha 123]]; this incident is the root of every devil story in this browser {cite:tanzil-quran}.
