## Summary
Nu'aym ibn Muqarrin was at Hamadan with twelve thousand men. The Daylam, and the people of Rayy and Azerbaijan, conspired against him, and Isfandiyar, brother of Rustam, came forward with the people of Azerbaijan. Nu'aym went out to meet them, and they clashed at Waj Rud in a great battle comparable to Nahavand; the Persians were routed in a shameful defeat and suffered a slaughter beyond counting. In another account, Jarir ibn Abd Allah conquered it after his eye was struck by an arrow, and he said: "I reckon it with God, who adorned my face with it and let light into what He willed for me, then took it from me in His cause." {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 405-406}.
