## Overview
In 867/1463, hardly eighteen years old, al-Suyuti inherited his father’s position: he taught Shafiʿi law in the mosque of Shaykhu and gave juridical consultations, handling a range of sciences in his fatwas with remarkable brilliance {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
