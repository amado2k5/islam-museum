## Overview
Al-Baladhuri describes the contest of [[person/abdallah-ibn-khazim-al-sulami|Ibn Khazim al-Sulami]] for the governorship of Khurasan after Salm ibn Ziyad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The covenant
Ibn Khazim met Salm ibn Ziyad at Nishapur as he was leaving Khurasan, and wrote him a covenant over it and helped him with a hundred thousand dirhams. A group of Bakr ibn Wa'il and others gathered and raided his baggage, and those with him fought to defend it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sulayman ibn Marthad
[[person/sulayman-ibn-marthad|Sulayman ibn Marthad]] wrote to Ibn Khazim that the covenant he had was worthless. Then they agreed to write to Ibn al-Zubayr so that he might appoint one of them. Ibn al-Zubayr appointed Ibn Khazim, and after six months Urwa ibn Qutba came with his covenant. Sulayman refused, and said that Ibn al-Zubayr was no caliph. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

They met, Ibn Khazim with six thousand men and Sulayman with fifteen thousand. Sulayman was killed by [[person/qays-ibn-asim-al-sulami|Qays ibn Asim al-Sulami]]. Then Ibn Khazim marched against [[person/umar-ibn-marthad|Umar ibn Marthad]] at Taliqan and killed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Aws ibn Thalaba
The Rabi'a gathered to [[person/aws-ibn-thalaba|Aws ibn Thalaba]] at Herat, so Ibn Khazim left his son Musa as deputy and marched to him. In the meantime the Turks raided as far as near Nishapur. Al-Baladhuri says that Ibn Khazim sent someone to poison Aws, and he fell ill. He was also wounded in the fighting and died after some days. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
