## The story
Al-Muwatta relates mursal from Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari that the Prophet wished to adopt two wooden clappers to be struck to gather the people for prayer; Abdullah ibn Zayd al-Ansari of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj saw in a dream two clappers and said they were like what the Messenger of God wanted; he related his dream to the Prophet, who ordered him to call the adhan as he had seen it, and Bilal called the adhan that day {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-172|Muwatta 172]].

## The meaning
This is among the best-known examples of a dream that changed an ordinance of Islam; the true dream can awaken a practical sunnah {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
