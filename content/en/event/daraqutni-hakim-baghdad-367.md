## Summary
In the year 367 al-Hakim Abu 'Abdallah stayed in Baghdad four months, meeting al-Daraqutni much, night and day, and asking him about the 'ilal and the shaykhs {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.102}.

## Al-Hakim's testimony
Al-Hakim said: al-Daraqutni became the unique one of his age in memory, understanding and scrupulousness, an imam among the readers and grammarians; I found him above what had been described to me. His works are too many to list, and I bear witness that he left no like of himself upon the face of the earth {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.102}.
