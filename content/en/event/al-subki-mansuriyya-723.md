## Overview
In the spring of 723 AH the Shafi'i scholar Taqi al-Din al-Subki took over the teaching post at the Mansuriyya madrasa in Cairo from the qadi Jamal al-Din al-Zar'i, who had moved to Damascus {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 723 p. 108}.
