## Overview
In 330 a kurr of wheat reached 210 dinars and a kurr of barley 120 — then wheat sold at 316; the weak ate carrion, the dearth persisted, deaths multiplied, the people were worn by sickness and poverty, the roads were cut, burial fell to the low-born, and folk forsook amusements and games {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 618}.
