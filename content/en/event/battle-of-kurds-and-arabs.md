## Overview
In 281 AH al-Mu'tadid marched out to Mosul in Dhu al-Qa'da. al-Tabari reports that his letter came from the camp, saying that the army had struck the Kurds and the Arabs, and that many of them were killed and drowned at the Zab. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.37}

The entry is short because the source available says no more about this report. It needs an additional source.
