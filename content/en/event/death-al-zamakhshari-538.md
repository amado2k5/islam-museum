## Overview
Abu al-Qasim al-Zamakhshari died at Jurjaniyya in Khwarazm on the night of Arafa in 538, after returning from Mecca, aged seventy-one {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 599-601}. The Encyclopaedia of Islam gives 9 Dhu al-Hijja 538, corresponding to 14 June 1144; he was buried near the town. By his own testimony he had lived at Mecca five years and made the pilgrimage seven times, earning the laqab 'Jar Allah', while at home he was known as the Glory of Khwarazm {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 918}.
