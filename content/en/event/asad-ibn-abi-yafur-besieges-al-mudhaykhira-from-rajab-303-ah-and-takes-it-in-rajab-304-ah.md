## Overview
Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur besieges al-Mudhaykhira from Rajab 303 AH, takes it by force in Rajab 304 AH, and enters Sanaa in Shawwal.

## Detail
Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur leaves Sanaa on Thursday, nine nights into Rajab 303 AH, for Dhamar, then Kahlan, then the district of Ja'far, where people gather to him and swear to him. He fights the men the book calls unbelievers in their fortresses for a long time, enters the fortresses, and the book says he kills many people. He drives them into al-Mudhaykhira and besieges them there. On Thursday, nine nights into Rajab 304 AH, he enters the house by force, takes the captives and seizes all that was there. He enters Sanaa on the festival of the Fast-Breaking at the start of Shawwal 304 AH. The book gives the same day and date, Thursday the ninth of Rajab, in both years.
