## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the caliph [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] killed [[person/mansur-ibn-jamwana|Mansur ibn Jamwana]] at [[place/raqqa|Raqqa]] in 141 AH, and the fortress of Mansur is named after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Mansur ibn Jamwana
He was of the Qays, and was in charge of building and maintaining the fortress, and he stayed in it in the days of Marwan with a large force. He was over the people of Edessa when they refused at the start of the dynasty, and al-Mansur besieged them; he fled, and was then granted safe conduct. When Abd Allah ibn Ali deposed al-Mansur, he appointed him over his police. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
After Abd Allah fled to Basra, Mansur went into hiding; then he was found and brought to al-Mansur, who killed him at Raqqa. In another account letters to the Byzantines were found with him, and al-Mansur struck his neck. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

According to al-Baladhuri, al-Rashid built the fortress of Mansur and garrisoned it in the caliphate of al-Mahdi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
