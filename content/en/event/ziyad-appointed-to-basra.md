Al-Tabari mentions in the year 45 AH the appointment of [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan]] over [[place/basra|Basra]]. It is among the reports of this year in the index of the fifth volume. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.629}
