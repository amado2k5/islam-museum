## Summary
Twelve men among the hypocrites tried to assassinate the Prophet at a mountain pass on the road back from [[event/expedition-to-tabuk|Tabuk]] to Medina, in 9 AH. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.436}

## The account of the pass
The Prophet passed that pass with [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar]] leading his she-camel by its halter and [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] driving it, while the people took the lower part of the valley. They heard the jostling of men behind them who had come upon them, masked. Hudhayfa struck the faces of their mounts with a staff he had. God frightened them, so they fled quickly until they caught up with the company. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.436}

The Prophet told Hudhayfa their names and what they had planned. For this reason Hudhayfa was called the keeper of the Messenger of God's secret. A verse of the Qur'an refers to their scheming (its text is not reproduced here). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.436}

## For young visitors
Some men whom the accounts describe as hypocrites hid in a narrow mountain pass to frighten the Prophet's camel at night. His companion Hudhayfa defended him bravely and drove them away. The Prophet rewarded him by entrusting him with his secret.
