## Summary
Ibn al-Salah travelled to Khurasan, stayed a while and acquired the science of hadith there. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 229} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}

## Nishapur and Marw
At Nishapur he heard Mansur al-Furawi, al-Mu'ayyad al-Tusi, al-Qasim b. al-Saffar, Muhammad b. al-Hasan al-Sirram, Abu al-Ma'ali b. Nasir al-Ansari, Abu al-Najib Isma'il al-Qari' and Zaynab al-Shi'riyya; at Marw he heard Abu al-Muzaffar 'Abd al-Rahim b. al-Sam'ani, Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Mu'sawi, Abu Ja'far al-Sanji, Muhammad b. 'Umar al-Mas'udi and a group. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 185-186}
