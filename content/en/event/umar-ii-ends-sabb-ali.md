## Overview
Al-Suyuti in "History of the Caliphs" says that the Umayyads used to curse [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] in the sermon. When [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] became caliph he abolished it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.394}

## What he did
He wrote to his deputies to abolish it, and in its place he recited the verse of justice and beneficence, [[passage/quran-16-90|al-Nahl 16:90]]. The book says that its recitation in the sermon continued from that time. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.394}

The event occurred in his caliphate (99–101 AH) according to the book, and the Gregorian date is derived.
