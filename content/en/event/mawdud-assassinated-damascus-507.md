## Summary
In 507 AH Mawdud, ruler of Mosul, came with an army to fight the king of the Franks. A great battle took place between them, and then he returned to Damascus {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 500-501}.

## The assassination
He prayed the Friday prayer in the mosque, when a Batini man leapt at him and wounded him, and he died the same day. The king of the Franks wrote to the ruler of Damascus, astonished that a people had killed its leader in the house of its worship on its feast day {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 501}.
