## Overview
Ibn Bistam fortified Maynas and wrote to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah that he was not with Banu al-Harith and Ya'am, and that he had fortified Maynas for al-Hadi, so that al-Hadi could fight Banu al-Harith there if they rose.

## Detail
The book says Ibn Bistam fortified Maynas and wrote to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah to report the gathering of Banu al-Harith and Ya'am, to say that he was not with them, and that he had fortified Maynas for al-Hadi. If the Banu al-Harith rose, al-Hadi would come to the fort and fight them there. The narrator adds that the letter was written so that no one could object to the fortifying of the fort. Ibn Bistam is the same man who had earlier asked for the release of Ali ibn al-Rabi'.
