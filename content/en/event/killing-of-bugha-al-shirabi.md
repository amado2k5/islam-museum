## Overview
In 254 AH Bugha al-Sharabi was killed. Al-Tabari says that the caliph al-Mu'tazz ordered his head brought, and that Walid al-Maghribi was the one who killed him. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.379} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.380}

What is not covered here: the circumstances of the killing and the accounts of other sources. This needs an additional source.
