## Overview
[[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] sent a messenger asking [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] to meet him with ten horsemen; al-Hadi went out with thirty horse and ordered his army to hold its place {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}. Abu al-Atahiya came out with a few of his companions to between the two armies and told them to halt; al-Hadi told his men to halt, except Ali ibn Muhammad, who stayed near him with the banner, and stepped aside. When Abu al-Atahiya saw him he rode toward him, and as he drew near he threw down his spear, bared his head and dismounted, and al-Hadi dismounted too {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.206}. He embraced al-Hadi, kissed his head and hands and knelt before him; al-Hadi sat with him and, in the narrator's words, preached to him and called him to obedience to his Lord, and Abu al-Atahiya hastened to say: put me on oath, Commander of the Faithful. Al-Hadi put him on oath and took his pledge to stand with him for the truth and on the truth, commanding the good and forbidding the wrong {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.207}. The companions of both drew near; Abu al-Atahiya called [[person/muhammad-ibn-abbad-al-tamimi|Muhammad ibn Abi Abbad]], who pledged, then his own companions, who pledged; he remounted and brought one group of his army after another and al-Hadi had them swear to hear and obey until most of the army had sworn; then the two armies mingled, greeted one another and went on together a short way {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.207}.

## Detail
At the time of the afternoon prayer al-Hadi dismounted, made ablution at the spring of [[place/hadaqan-sanaa-yemen|Hadaqan]] with Abu al-Qasim and Abu al-Atahiya, and they prayed. As the sunset prayer drew near Abu al-Atahiya said: go on, with God's blessing, to Sana'a. Al-Hadi asked: shall we spend the night here? He answered: do as you think best, but I do not like you to spend the night here because of what I fear from my cousins at [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]]. Al-Hadi resolved to go on and entered Sana'a on the night of Friday, seven nights remaining of Muharram 288 AH, with Abu al-Atahiya in front of him, and took him to the house where Abu al-Atahiya had been living, and spent the night there {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.207}. The Abu al-Qasim of this passage is al-Hadi's son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]]; the narrator calls him Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya a few pages on (p. 210), which is why the link is made. The date of the entry differs from the one given in the entry record built on pp. 17 to 20, pages the editor thinks were added to the original; the museum gives both and does not reconcile them.
