## Summary
[[person/al-mawardi|Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Mawardi]] died at [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] in Rabi' I 450 — on its 30th, 27 May 1058 per the EI2 — aged eighty-six, and was buried at the Bab Harb cemetery {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.199-200} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 896}.

## His works
He died eleven days after the qadi Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari; none of his works had been brought out in his lifetime — he bequeathed them at his death, and their story came out afterwards {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 294}.
