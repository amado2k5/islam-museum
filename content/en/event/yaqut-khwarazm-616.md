## Summary
In 616/1219 Yaqut was forced to leave Marw for fear of the Mongols and their savage treatment of scholars and libraries {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}. He toured Khurasan — Balkh, Marw al-Rudh, Herat — then went to Nasa and on to Khwarazm {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}.

## The frozen inkwell
He reached Jurjaniyya in the bitterest cold and tried to write and compose, but could not. He says: 'I strove to write something there, but the frozen inkwell would not let me until I brought it near the fire and thawed it; and when I brought the drinking cup near my lips it stuck to them, frozen' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}.
