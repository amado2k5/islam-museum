## Overview
al-Baladhuri reports disagreement over who conquered [[place/qaysariyya|Caesarea]]: it is said [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]], it is said Iyad ibn Ghanm, it is said [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]], and it is said his son Abd Allah. The scholars agree that the first to besiege it was Amr, in Jumada al-Ula 13 AH. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege
Amr remained before it and then marched to every host of the Muslims. He attended Ajnadayn, Fahl, Marj al-Saffar, Damascus and al-Yarmuk. Then Umar appointed [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-sufyan|Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan]] after Abu Ubayda, and he gave the siege of it to his brother Mu'awiya, and he himself went to Damascus and died there at the end of 18 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquest
al-Waqidi relates that Mu'awiya besieged it until he took it. It had been besieged for about seven years, and its conquest was in Shawwal 19 AH, though some say the beginning of 20 AH. According to him the cause of the conquest was that a Jew guided the Muslims by night to a passage in a conduit where there was water, on condition that they grant him and his household safety. The Muslims entered and shouted "Allahu akbar", and opened the gate. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The captives reached four thousand heads, which Mu'awiya sent to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], and he divided them among the orphans of the Ansar. al-Baladhuri quotes a number of figures for its population, which need an additional source. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
