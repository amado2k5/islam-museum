## Overview
[[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]] died on Thursday, eleven nights before the end of Rabi' al-Awwal 227 AH, according to al-Suyuti. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 523-524}

## His last illness
al-Suyuti relates that he said in his illness: "The stratagem is gone, and there is no stratagem left." In another account he began to say: "I am being taken from among this creation." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 523-524}

al-Suyuti says that he had resolved to march to the far west, to rule the lands that had not entered the rule of the Banu al-Abbas. He said to Ahmad ibn al-Khasib that the Banu Umayya had ruled, and that they had an Umayyad amir in al-Andalus. Then his illness grew severe and he died. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 523-524}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says al-Mu'tasim died on Thursday, eleven nights before the end of Rabi' al-Awwal, 227, and al-Wathiq received the oath. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 478}

## In al-Ya'qubi
[[person/al-yaqubi|Al-Ya'qubi]] says al-Mu'tasim died on a Thursday with eleven nights of Rabi' I remaining; his son Harun (al-Wathiq) led the funeral prayer and he was buried in his palace known as al-Jawsaq. His reign lasted eight years, and his sons were Harun al-Wathiq, Ja'far al-Mutawakkil, Muhammad, Ahmad, Ali and al-Abbas. His chamberlains were a group of Turks, among them Wasif. His day (eleven nights before the month's end) differs from those who place the death on the eleventh of the month. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 584}
