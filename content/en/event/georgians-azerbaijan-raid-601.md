## Summary
In 601 AH the Georgians raided the lands of Islam from the direction of Azerbaijan, then the Akhlat region of Armenia as far as Malazgirt, with no Muslim force coming out to stop them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Georgians raided the Islamic lands from Azerbaijan that year, spreading destruction, plunder and captivity, then pushed deep into the Akhlat region of Armenia reaching Malazgirt; no Muslim force came out against them, so they ranged through the country looting and capturing, while the Muslim armies kept falling back ahead of them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297} He adds that they raided the Akhlat lands, came to Arjish and its surroundings, plundered, took captives and laid waste the country, and marched on the fort of al-Tin in the Akhlat district adjoining the land of the Rum. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 297}
