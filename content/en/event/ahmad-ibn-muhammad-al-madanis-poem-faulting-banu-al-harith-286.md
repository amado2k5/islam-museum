## Overview
And [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-madani-poet-286|Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Madani]] said, at al-Hadi's entry into the village at that time, his killing of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] and his taking of Ibn Humayd, a poem faulting Banu al-Harith for what they did. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.179} He says grief drove sleep from him over what came upon his people when their wits strayed and they openly made war on God and on the son of His prophet, that they were told not to perish for their thinking was false, that counsel had been given them and they should accept it, and that they had found an imam descended from lines of purity and godfearing, an imam of guidance for believers and for enemies; he describes him leading the army with a keen blade in hand, standing for God's religion and steadfast for the truth. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.179}

## The poet
The book gives Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Madani no lineage or office. His printed nisba is "al-Madani", while the same edition reads "al-Hamdani" at p. 171, so the museum does not assert that he is [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-al-hamdani-poet-286]] or [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran]]. The lines are not quoted, since the printed order is disturbed. Related: [[event/abdullah-ibn-al-husayns-poem-on-the-killing-of-banu-al-harith-286]].
