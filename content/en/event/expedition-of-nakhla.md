## Summary
In Rajab 2 AH (January 624 CE) the Prophet sent [[person/abdullah-ibn-jahsh|Abdullah ibn Jahsh al-Asadi]] with twelve men of the [[group/muhajirun|Muhajirun]], each pair taking turns on one camel; he wrote him a letter ordering him not to read it until he had travelled two days, then carry out its command and compel none of his companions. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200}

## The mission and the march
Abdullah read the letter after two days and found in it: "when you have read this letter of mine, go on until you halt at Nakhla between Mecca and al-Ta'if; lie in wait there for the caravan of Quraysh and learn their news for us" — he told his companions and they all set off. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200}

On the way [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] and [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan]] lost the camel they had been sharing and stayed behind seeking it, while the rest of the company went on until they halted at [[place/nakhla|Nakhla]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/85}

## The interception of the caravan
A Quraysh caravan passed carrying raisins, hides and trade goods, guarded by [[person/ibn-amir-al-hadrami|Amr ibn al-Hadrami]], [[person/uthman-ibn-abdillah-ibn-al-mughira|Uthman ibn Abdullah ibn al-Mughira]], his brother [[person/nawfal-ibn-abdillah|Nawfal]], and [[person/al-hakam-ibn-kaysan|al-Hakam ibn Kaysan]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200}

The Muslims took counsel — it was the last day of Rajab, the sacred month — and saw that if they left them that night they would enter the sanctuary; so [[person/waqid-ibn-abdillah|Waqid ibn Abdullah al-Tamimi]] shot them with an arrow and killed Amr ibn al-Hadrami. They captured Uthman and al-Hakam, Nawfal escaped, and they brought the caravan and the two captives into [[place/medina|Medina]], setting aside the fifth of the spoil — the first fifth in Islam. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/602}

## The Prophet's stand and the revelation
The Prophet denounced the killing of Ibn al-Hadrami in the sacred month and said: "I did not command you to fight in the sacred month," and withheld action on the caravan and the two captives; the polytheists raised an outcry accusing the Muslims of violating the sanctities. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 200}

Then revelation came down settling it with [[passage/quran-2-217|al-Baqara 217]]; the Prophet took charge of the caravan and the captives, then released the two for ransom and paid the slain man's blood-money. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 200-201}
