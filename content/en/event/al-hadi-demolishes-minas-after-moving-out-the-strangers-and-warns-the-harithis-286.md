## Overview
Next morning [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went out with his army to [[place/minas-najran|Minas]] and ordered the strangers, the Christians and the weak who were there to move their goods out to wherever they liked, and put some of his trusted men with them so that none of their goods was taken. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} When they had taken out their goods he ordered Minas pulled down, and it was pulled down entirely; the manuscript's note says all of it was demolished after its people were taken out. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} He then went back to the village and stayed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178}

## The warning to Banu al-Harith
A party of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] who had not gone out to cry against him came to greet him; he admonished them and said, as the book gives it, that by God they would return another time and he would kill them in a killing greater than this one, and they answered not to say so, O son of the Messenger of God, for they would never return to him in strife; and al-Hadi said he had told them and they would see what he said. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.179} This is as the book relates it, with the words put to al-Hadi and to them, and the museum gives no verdict on them. Compare the book's account of the later events in [[event/al-hadi-hangs-the-slain-of-banu-al-harith-and-lets-them-be-buried-286]].
