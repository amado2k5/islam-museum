## Overview
The Karamanid Turcoman emir besieged Bursa in 1413 CE and destroyed the Orhan Gazi Mosque. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 38}

## The repair
The mosque was repaired four years after its destruction, and its original plan survived later restorations. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 38}
