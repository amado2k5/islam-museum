## Overview
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab]] made it a condition for fighting [[group/azariqa|the Azariqa]] that he choose his men and keep whatever land he won; he raised twenty thousand men, eight thousand of them of the Azd, with his son al-Mughira leading the vanguard of three thousand. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 272} He stayed at the river for forty days, then met Nafi' at a place the editor reads as Sillabra; al-Muhallab was struck in the face and fainted, and the Azariqa's leader [[person/nafi-ibn-al-azraq|Nafi' ibn al-Azraq]] was killed. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 273} Ibn al-Zubayr then dismissed al-Harith from Basra and gave Mus'ab both Iraqs. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 274}
