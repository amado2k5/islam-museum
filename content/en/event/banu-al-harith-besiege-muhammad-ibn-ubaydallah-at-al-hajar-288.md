## Overview
Then [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] gathered and marched until they camped at the gate of the village of [[place/al-hajar-najran|al-Hajar]], their army fifteen hundred foot and a hundred and thirty horse {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}. [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] went out to them with those with him, fifteen horse and fifty foot, and with him were [[group/banu-abd-al-madan-najran|Banu Abd al-Madan]], who had not entered the war with their kinsmen and, the narrator says, held to obedience to the authority {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}. Banu al-Harith stayed three days besieging him at the gate of the village in the month of Ramadan of 288 AH, and [[person/ibn-humayd-of-banu-al-harith-286|Ibn Humayd]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] told those with them to break the fast because of the hardship of the war, hoping that Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah would leave and the village fall into their hands {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}.

## Detail
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah swore that he would not leave the village until Banu Abd al-Madan forsook him and declared themselves apart from him by leaving; Banu Abd al-Madan went on being courteous to him and defending their kinsmen with him, then desire overtook them, and they came to him with al-Mujahir ibn Ziyad the Khushaymi (as printed), Aqil ibn Ubaydallah, Abdullah ibn Isa, Muhammad ibn Aqil and a number of the people of Najran, and asked him to leave their village, saying they feared destruction for him and for themselves; the narrator calls what they said falsehood and lies; he agreed to their request and had the men sign witness against them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}. He ordered his womenfolk to be handed to Rabi' ibn Abi al-Rukud and left by night by the village's Yemeni gate {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.230}. The narrator's explanation of Banu al-Harith's motives as hatred and greed is his testimony, not the museum's. The text does not join this al-Mujahir with [[person/al-mujahir-ibn-ziyad-al-qatani|al-Mujahir ibn Ziyad al-Qatani]] of an earlier record, and the attribution differs between Khushaymi and Qatani, so the museum does not say they are one man.
