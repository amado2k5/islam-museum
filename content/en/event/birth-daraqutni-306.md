## Summary
'Ali ibn 'Umar ibn Ahmad ibn Mahdi ibn Mas'ud ibn al-Nu'man ibn Dinar ibn 'Abdallah, Abu al-Hasan al-Daraqutni, was born in Baghdad on 5 Dhu al-Qa'da 306 — the year 305 is also reported {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.184} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.101}.

## His nisba
Al-Daraqutni refers to Dar al-Qutn, one of the quarters of Baghdad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.116}.
