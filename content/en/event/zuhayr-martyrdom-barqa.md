## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan]] appointed [[person/zuhayr-ibn-qays-al-balawi|Zuhayr ibn Qays al-Balawi]] over Ifriqiya. Zuhayr conquered Tutis, then turned to [[place/barqa|Barqa]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.321}

He received word that a group of the Byzantines had come out of their ships and spread corruption, so he went against them with a small troop of horsemen. He met them, and he and those with him were martyred, and they were buried there. Their graves are called "the graves of the martyrs". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.321}
