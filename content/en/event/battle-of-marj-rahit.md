## Overview
At the end of 64 AH a battle was fought at Marj Rahit, east of [[place/damascus|Damascus]], between Marwan ibn al-Hakam and the supporters of the Umayyads on one side, and the supporters of Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr on the other. Victory went to Marwan. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 30}

The book holds that it was the beginning of the Umayyads' recovery of a state that had been very close to collapse. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 30}

The army of Ibn al-Zubayr in Syria was led by [[person/al-dahhak-ibn-qays-al-fihri|al-Dahhak ibn Qays al-Fihri]]. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715}

The book gives the date in the Hijri calendar, and it has not been converted here.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] reports from Baqi, from al-Layth, that the battle of Rahit was in Dhu al-Hijja, two nights after the Feast of Sacrifice, 64. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 253} He says they fought for twenty days before al-Dahhak ibn Qays was routed. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 259}
