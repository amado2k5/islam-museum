## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah]] stipulated with [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] a quarter of what they conquered, if he went to Iraq. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.353}

## Background
Umar went a year without mentioning Iraq after the disaster of Abu Ubayd. [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|Al-Muthanna]] was staying in the region of Alis calling for jihad. Then Umar called the people to Iraq, and they shunned it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.353}

## The stipulation
Jarir came with the Bajila and asked that he come to Iraq on condition that he and his people be given a quarter of what they conquered, and Umar agreed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.353}

## Differing accounts of the route
Al-Baladhuri says that some people say he passed by Basra and defeated the marzban of al-Madhar; others say he was with Khalid ibn al-Walid; others say he took Fayd and al-Tha'labiyya to al-Mudhayyib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.353}
