## Overview
Al-Baladhuri lists the lands that [[person/iyyad-ibn-ghanm|Iyad ibn Ghanm]] conquered in al-Jazira and the borders of Armenia, in 19 AH and days of Muharram 20 AH. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-jazira|conquest of al-Jazira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.242}

## The conquests
He took Amid and Mayyafariqin without fighting, on the same terms as al-Ruha. He took Nasibin after fighting, then the Tur of Abdin, Mardin and Dara. The patrician of al-Zuzan came to him and made peace with him for a tribute. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.241}

## Toward Khilat
He marched to Arzan and took it, entered the pass and reached Bdlis, crossed to Khilat and made peace with its patrician. Then he returned. The lord of Bdlis guaranteed the land tax of Khilat. He went back to Raqqa and then Homs, and died there in 20 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.242}
