## Overview
Once the news was confirmed, Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah sent his son Ali ibn Muhammad to al-Hadi with a letter asking him to reach the town before the rebels fortified al-Qarya and Maynas. Ali's letter ended with two lines of verse.

## Detail
The book says that once this was confirmed to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, he sent his son Ali ibn Muhammad to al-Hadi. He wrote to tell him of Ibn Humayd's conduct, that all of Banu al-Harith had united in revolt, and asked him to reach the town before they could seize it and fortify themselves at al-Qarya and Maynas. Ali ibn Muhammad added two lines of verse at the end of his letter; the record does not quote them and gives only their subject, that a fire of war is kindled by talk as sparks are kindled by flint.
