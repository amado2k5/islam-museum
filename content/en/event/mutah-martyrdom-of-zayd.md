## Overview
At [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'tah]] [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] carried the banner of the Messenger of God and fought with it until he was killed. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The Prophet announced his death in Medina before the news arrived, and mentioned that he had seen in a dream that God had raised him on a couch of gold. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
