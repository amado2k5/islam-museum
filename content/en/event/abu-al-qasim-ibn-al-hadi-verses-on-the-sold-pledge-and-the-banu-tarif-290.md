## Overview
On p. 260 Abu al-Qasim says that some people were offered the Book and denied it and leaned to instruments and drink, that they sold the pledge to al-Hadi for the dirt of dirhams, and he asks God to hold such people back with manifold punishment, saying that they refuse to be patient without wine and licence and bar people from pleasures, which is their fault in his words, and he counsels the imam not to bow and to make lances his answer {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.260} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.261}. On p. 261 a piece begins with his saying that [[group/banu-tarif|Banu Tarif]] thought him like one terrified among the bonds, in which he says he guards the Prophet's qualities and takes pride in Hashim, Abd Manaf, Qasim and al-Hadi Yahya, says he gives his life in God and that his ancestors stood as callers to God and were met by the tyrants' wrong, and that he contended with his rival for the kingdom of the world and the levy of revenue {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.261}.

## Detail
The charges laid against opponents in the lines are the poet's and the book is Zaydi; the museum adopts none of them. On p. 261 the forebears named are [[person/hashim-ibn-abd-manaf|Hashim]], [[person/abd-manaf-ibn-qusayy|Abd Manaf]], Qasim and al-Hadi Yahya; the book does not say which Qasim, and the probable reading is the imam al-Qasim al-Rassi, al-Hadi's grandfather, which is a probable reading and not linked. The museum does not repeat the words of insult. Before this are [[event/abu-al-qasim-ibn-al-hadi-composes-verses-in-prison-290|the earlier pieces of the same section]]. The museum does not reproduce the lines nor the words of insult in them and gives only their sense as the book has it; the book ascribes the verse to Abu al-Qasim and the museum has not checked the attribution. The praise and the epithets in the lines are the poet's, given as the book gives them, with no verdict in the museum's voice.
