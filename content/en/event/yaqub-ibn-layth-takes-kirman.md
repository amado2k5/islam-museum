## Overview
In 255 AH there was a confrontation between Ya'qub ibn al-Layth and Tawq ibn al-Mughallis outside Kirman, and then Ya'qub took Kirman. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.382}

## After Kirman
Then Ya'qub entered Fars and took captive Ali ibn al-Husayn ibn Quraysh. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.384}

What is not covered here: the details of the confrontation and its direct outcome. This needs an additional source. The date of the event in the museum rests on the index of the ninth volume.
