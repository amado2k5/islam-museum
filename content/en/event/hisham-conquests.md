## Overview
Al-Suyuti mentions three conquests in the time of [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.402}

In his seventh year Caesarea of the Romans was taken by the sword. In his eighth year Khinjara was taken by [[person/abdallah-al-battal|al-Battal]], the famous brave man. In his twelfth year Kharshana in the district of Malatya was taken. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.402}
