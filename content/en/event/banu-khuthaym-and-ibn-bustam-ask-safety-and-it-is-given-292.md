## Overview
"When Banu Khuthaym saw that they said to Ibn Humayd: either you seek safety for us together, or we part and each of us seeks it on his own side", and he agreed to what they asked {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}. They sent to Ibn Bustam, who met them, and they asked him to seek safety for them from al-Hadi; he agreed, went to al-Hadi to ask it, and al-Hadi granted their safety {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}.

## Detail
They asked to meet [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Abu Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] so as to feel at ease with al-Hadi through him; he met them and the notables and best of Banu al-Harith came with him, went in to al-Hadi, asked safety and he gave it {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}. They asked him to hold off from entering the village for two days until they could bring out their womenfolk; he agreed, and they left the village and each went to his place {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.294}. "Abu Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah" is read here, as before, as Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, whose kunya is Abu Ja'far; the rendering from the page image confirmed the print in this form. The text says nothing of who Banu Khuthaym are beyond a group under Ibn Humayd; the museum does not link them to the registered [[group/al-khushaymiyyun|Khushaymis]], since the name is printed here "Khuthaym" and with shin on earlier pages, so a separate record was made, to be merged if the two prove one. The account is from al-Hadi's supporters in a Zaydi book, in which al-Hadi grants safety, and the museum passes no judgement on it. It leads to [[event/al-hadi-enters-al-hajar-on-monday-17-shaban-292|al-Hadi's entry into al-Hajar]].
