## In Ibn Abd al-Hakam
Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports that the Berbers rose against Ubayd Allah ibn al-Habhab at Tangier and killed his agent, led by Maysara al-Faqir al-Madghari, who claimed the caliphate and received allegiance; he then marched to al-Sus and killed Isma'il ibn Ubayd Allah, "the first Berber uprising in the land of Ifriqiya". {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 293} Ibn al-Habhab sent Khalid ibn Abi Habib al-Fihri with the people of Ifriqiya, Quraysh, Ansar and others; Khalid and his men were killed and none of them survived, so the campaign was called the Raid of the Nobles. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 293} {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 294} Al-Layth dates the killing of Isma'il and Khalid to the year 123. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 294}
