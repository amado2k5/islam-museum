## Summary
A detachment the Prophet sent after the fugitives of Hawazin to [[place/awtas|Awtas]] following the battle of Hunayn, under [[person/abu-amir-al-ashari|Abu Amir al-Ash'ari]]; the Muslims defeated Hawazin's remnants, [[person/durayd-ibn-al-simma|Durayd ibn al-Simma]] was killed, Abu Amir was martyred, and Abu Musa al-Ash'ari took the banner {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/97-99}.

## The course of the detachment
When Hawazin broke at Hunayn a company of them took refuge in the valley of Awtas and fortified there, Durayd ibn al-Simma with them {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/97}. The Prophet sent Abu Amir al-Ash'ari with cavalry to pursue and engage them {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/97}.

Abu Amir fought nine brothers of the polytheist leaders, overcame and killed them, then Salama ibn Durayd shot him in the knee and brought him down {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/97}. He handed the banner to his nephew Abu Musa al-Ash'ari saying: "fight them, and ask the Messenger of God to forgive me"; Abu Musa fought until God granted him victory and routed them {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/97-98}.

[[person/rabia-ibn-rufy|Rabi'a ibn Rufay' al-Sulami]] overtook Durayd ibn al-Simma in his litter and killed him with Durayd's own sword {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/93-94}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet sent a part of the army to chase the routed remnants in a valley called Awtas; their brave commander Abu Amir was martyred, and his nephew carried the battle through to victory.
