## Overview
[[person/hujr-ibn-al-harith-al-kindi|Hujr ibn al-Harith al-Kindi]], father of the poet Imru' al-Qays, was killed by the Banu Asad, in an account Ibn al-Athir relates. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 383-384}

## The account
His father had made him king over the Banu Asad, Ghatafan and Kinana, and they owed him a reward and a tribute. When the Banu Asad drove out his tax-collectors and beat them, he marched against them with troops from Rabi'a, Qays and Kinana. He took their chiefs and began killing them with a staff, and imprisoned their nobles, among them Ubayd ibn al-Abras. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 383-384}

## His killing
Ibn al-Athir says that their soothsayer, Awf ibn Rabi'a, foretold the king's death, so they attacked him in his tent. [[person/ilba-ibn-al-harith|Ilba ibn al-Harith al-Kahili]] stabbed him, for Hujr had killed his father. Then they plundered his camels, women and possessions. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 383-384}

His death kindled a war of vengeance by Imru' al-Qays against the [[group/banu-asad-ibn-khuzayma|Banu Asad]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 383-384}
