## The story
Al-Muwatta relates that the Prophet stopped one night on the road to Mecca; Bilal and the company slept until sunrise, so he ordered them to ride until they left the valley, saying there is a shaytan in it, then told Abu Bakr that the shaytan had come to Bilal while he stood praying, laid him down and soothed him like a child until he slept {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-026|Muwatta 26]].

## The meaning
This report shows the shaytan besets the night prayer — he laid Bilal down while he stood until he slept — and that the shaytan dwells in certain places; hence the Prophet ordered them out of that valley {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
