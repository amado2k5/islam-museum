## Summary
After his early education in Cairo, al-Qifti went in 583/1187 to Jerusalem, where his father had been appointed deputy to the Qadi al-Fadil, Salah al-Din's famous chancellor and adviser; he spent many years there as a student, already gathering the material for his later works {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}.
