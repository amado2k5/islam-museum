## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that when [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] had finished with the apostates, he decided to send armies to Syria. He wrote to the people of Mecca, Ta'if, Yemen, and the Arabs of Najd and the Hijaz, calling them to the holy war, and they hurried to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 149}

## Appointing the commanders
Abu Bakr appointed three banners: for [[person/khalid-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Khalid ibn Sa'id ibn al-As]], for [[person/shurahbil-ibn-hasana|Shurahbil ibn Hasana]], and for [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]]. This was on Thursday, at the beginning of Safar 13 AH, while the armies were camped at al-Jurf. [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]] led them in prayer, having declined the banner. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 149}

Al-Baladhuri gives a difference over the lineage of Shurahbil: according to al-Waqidi he was the son of Abd Allah ibn al-Mutta'im al-Kindi, with Hasana as his mother; according to al-Kalbi he was the son of Rabi'a ibn al-Mutta'im. He says that the report that Abu Ubayda was given the banner "is not established". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Removing Khalid ibn Sa'id
Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] disliked appointing Khalid ibn Sa'id, and spoke to Abu Bakr about removing him. He said that he was a proud man who drove his command toward rivalry and partisanship, so Abu Bakr removed him. His banner was given to [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-sufyan|Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan]], and his brother [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] carried it before him. Khalid went with Shurahbil's army seeking reward. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Routes and numbers
Abu Bakr ordered Amr to take the road of Aila toward Palestine, and Yazid and Shurahbil the road of Tabuk. Each commander had three thousand at first; then reinforcements reached them, until each had seven thousand five hundred, and their total reached twenty-four thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri relates from Abu Yakhnaf that Abu Bakr told the commanders: if you gather to fight, your commander is Abu Ubayda, and otherwise Yazid. From al-Waqidi, he appointed Amr over Palestine, Shurahbil over Jordan, and Yazid over Damascus. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The date
The beginning of Safar 13 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around April 634 CE) that is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 149}
