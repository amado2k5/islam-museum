## Overview
The "Shadharat al-Dhahab" relates that the springs of [[place/mecca|Mecca]] were brought in 835 AH until they entered the city. The pools of the Bab al-Mu'alla were filled from them, and the water passed by al-Safa and the Night Market, and its benefit was general. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222}

## What the source says
The data does not name who brought the springs, the source of the water, or the cost of the work. This needs an additional source.

## Date
835 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around 1431–1432 CE) that is derived. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222}
