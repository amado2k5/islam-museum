## Summary
Three men had intercourse with a woman during a single menstrual purity period in [[place/yemen|Yemen]], and they were brought before [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]]. When he asked whether two of them would acknowledge the child and they refused, he said: I cast lots among them, assigned the child to the one upon whom the lot fell, and imposed two-thirds of the blood money upon him {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}.

## The Report
The book narrates it from Zayd ibn Arqam: Three men who had intercourse with a woman in a single purity period were brought to Ali, and he asked each pair of them whether they acknowledged the child, and when they refused, he cast lots among them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}. This was mentioned to the Prophet, who laughed until his molar teeth showed; the book considers that the laughter was out of joy at Ali's being guided to what was right, so the Prophet approved his judgment {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}.

## The Book's Commentary
The book suggests as a possibility that what occurred among these men took place before their conversion to Islam, because their act is forbidden in the religion of God, and the book presents this as a possibility rather than a definitive ruling {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.115}.
