In the pilgrimage season of the eleventh year of the prophethood (July 620 CE), while the Prophet was offering himself to the Arab tribes at their halting-places, he met at al-Aqaba in Mina six young men of the Khazraj from Yathrib: [[person/asad-ibn-zurara|As'ad ibn Zurara]], [[person/awf-ibn-al-harith|Awf ibn al-Harith]], [[person/rafi-ibn-malik|Rafi' ibn Malik]], [[person/qutba-ibn-amir|Qutba ibn Amir]], [[person/uqba-ibn-amir-ibn-nabi|Uqba ibn Amir ibn Nabi]] and [[person/jabir-ibn-abdullah-ibn-riab|Jabir ibn Abdullah ibn Ri'ab]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

He said to them: "who are you?" They said: a band of the Khazraj. He said: "will you not sit while I speak with you?" They said: yes — and sat with him while he set out Islam for them and recited the Qur'an to them. The Jews of Yathrib, whenever they threatened them, used to say: a prophet is being sent now — we will follow him and kill you with him the killing of Ad and Iram. So when they heard his word they said to one another: people, know by God that he is the prophet the Jews threatened you with — do not let them outstrip you to him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

So they answered what he called them to, believed him and became Muslim, saying: we have left our people, and no people are more hostile and wicked than they — perhaps God will unite them through you. They returned to Yathrib carrying the message of Islam, until no house of the Ansar remained in which the Messenger of God was not spoken of. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
