## Summary
Atabeg Izz al-Din Mas'ud ibn Mawdud, lord of Mosul, died after travelling to al-Adil's lands and returning with his illness. He was the last of the Mawdud family to rule Mosul independently {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.228-229}.
