## Overview
The next day [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] left Raydah for a town called Madar, lodged there, and, in the narrator's words, ordered its people and spread his justice through the place; on the Wednesday with twelve days left of Muharram he went on to a place called Kharaqa and spent the night, and the next morning moved to a place near [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] called [[place/hadaqan-sanaa-yemen|Hadaqan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} The weekday of the Wednesday does not match the tabular calendar exactly, as elsewhere in the book, so the date stays at month precision.

## Detail
The narrator turns to [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]]: he had been patient in his affairs and arranged them, by God's guidance in the narrator's telling, and, resolved to hand over to al-Hadi, he feared that his kin of Al Tarif and the non-Arab troops of Jund Jaftam with him would oppose him; of them the narrator says they were sinners and wrongdoers. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} He adds that each man of [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]] had carved out a district of Yemen and lived off it by injustice, which is his charge against them as a follower of the imam, not the museum's finding. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.204} See [[group/al-jafatim-sanaa]].
