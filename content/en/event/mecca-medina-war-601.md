## Overview
In 601 AH a war broke out between the amir of Mecca, [[person/qatada-ibn-idris-al-alawi|Qatada al-Husayni]], and the amir of Medina, [[person/salim-ibn-qasim|Salim ibn Qasim al-Husayni]], each with a large force. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 245}

## The march on Medina
Qatada set out for [[place/medina|Medina]] to besiege it and take it. Salim met him at Dhu al-Hulayfa, after visiting the Prophet's chamber, where he prayed and made supplication. Qatada was defeated, and Salim followed him to Mecca and besieged him there. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 245}

## The reversal
Ibn al-Athir relates that Qatada sent to the commanders with Salim and turned them against him. They sided with him and made an alliance with him. Salim returned to Medina, and Qatada's position became strong again. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 245}
