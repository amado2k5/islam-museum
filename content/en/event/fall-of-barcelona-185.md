## Overview
Al-Maqqari reports that during the strife between al-Hakam ibn Hisham and his uncles, the Franks targeted Barcelona and took it in 185 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 375}

## The account
The armies of the Muslims were late in reaching the area. Al-Hakam sent the armies with the chamberlain Abd al-Karim ibn Mughith to the land of the Galicians, and they inflicted heavy losses there. The enemy got ahead of them to the narrow passes, but he returned in battle order, defeated them and came out victorious. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 375}
