## Overview
In 260 AH Ya'qub ibn al-Layth defeated al-Hasan ibn Zayd al-Talibi, and entered Tabaristan. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.508}

## The rains
al-Tabari says that rains caught Ya'qub in the mountains of Tabaristan for days, and that his army lost most of its animals. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.509}
