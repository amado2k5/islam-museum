## Overview
Ali ibn al-Fadl sets out from al-Mudhaykhira at the end of Jumada for Sanaa, reaches Bahrir, and is fought by Asad and his men, who kill about sixty of his men. Jarrah and his men then press back against him toward Sanaa.

## Detail
The book says Ali ibn al-Fadl set out from al-Mudhaykhira at the end of Jumada and reached Bahrir. Asad and his men went out to him, fought him and killed about sixty of his men. Jarrah and his men then drove him back toward Sanaa. The book does not say which Jumada is meant. The place name is read here as Bahrir, and the verb for the push-back is terse.
