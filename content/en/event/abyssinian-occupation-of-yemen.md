## Overview
Seventy thousand Abyssinian soldiers landed under Aryat in 525 CE in a fleet the Romans had prepared, in revenge for the People of the Trench; then Abraha seized power.

## The story
The Roman emperors incited the Abyssinians and prepared a fleet for them, and seventy thousand Abyssinian soldiers landed and occupied Yemen a second time under Aryat's command in 525 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Aryat remained governor on behalf of the king of Abyssinia until [[person/abraha|Abraha]] — one of his army commanders — assassinated him and ruled in his place after placating the Abyssinian king {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. It is this Abraha who mustered the troops to demolish the Kaaba; he and his soldiers became known as the People of the Elephant {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Abyssinia punished the king of Yemen for burning the Christians and occupied the land; then its commander Abraha turned on its governor and ruled in his place.
