## Overview
[[person/yazdegerd-iii|Yazdegerd]] ibn Shahriyar fled to Khurasan after Abd Allah ibn Amir had conquered Fars. Ibn Amir sent Mujashi' ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami after him as far as Kirman, and Yazdegerd died after that. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.286}

The index of the volume gives the heading "The account of the killing of Yazdegerd, king of Persia" under the year 31 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.4 p.581}

What is not covered here: the details of his death. This needs an additional source.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Mahuya, lord of Merv, called in the Khaqan against Yazdagird, who fled on foot two farsakhs to a mill; the miller asked four dirhams, Yazdagird gave him his sword and belt, and the miller killed him with the mill-pick and threw him into the river. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 139} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 140} He dates it to the sixth year of Uthman's caliphate, the year 30, and says that with it “the kingdom of Persia came to an end”. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 140}
