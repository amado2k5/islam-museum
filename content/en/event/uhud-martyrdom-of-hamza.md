## Overview
[[person/hamza|Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] was killed at the [[event/battle-of-uhud|Battle of Uhud]] by the spear of [[person/wahshi-ibn-harb|Wahshi ibn Harb]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.261}

## In the fighting
The account describes Hamza fighting in the midst of people like a dark camel, levelling the ranks. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.261}

## The ambush
Wahshi was an Abyssinian slave of Jubayr ibn Mut'im, and he lay in wait for Hamza from behind a tree or a rock. Jubayr promised him freedom if he killed Hamza, in revenge for his uncle Tu'ayma ibn Adi, who had been killed at Badr. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.261}

The book quotes Wahshi's words: "I shook my spear until I was satisfied with it, then I threw it at him, and it fell in his belly until it came out between his legs." He then returned to Mecca and was freed. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.261}

## After the battle
[[person/hind-bint-utba|Hind bint Utba]] came and split open Hamza's liver, chewed it, and could not swallow it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.276}

This happened in the first round of the fighting, on the Saturday, the seventh of Shawwal 3 AH according to al-Mubarakfuri. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.261}
