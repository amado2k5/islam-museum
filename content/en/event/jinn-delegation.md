## The story
Sahih Muslim relates that a delegation of jinn came to the Prophet in Medina by night, sat listening to his recitation of the Qur'an and his questions, and asked him what sustains them {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## What passed between them
The Prophet told them their provision is the leaves of sweet-smelling trees and the water of rivers, that their deeds are presented to them, that among them are believers and disbelievers, and ordered them to warn their people; when they asked about his wife Khadija he told them she had died and wept {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In the Qur'an
It matches surah al-Jinn: 'Say: it has been revealed to me that a party of the jinn listened' [[passage/quran-72-1|al-Jinn 1]], which tells of some jinn believing and calling their people to Islam [[passage/quran-72-13|al-Jinn 13]].

## Notes
This meeting is the most famous reported contact between jinn and humans, and the source of later lore about the jinn delegation in the books of seerah and lore {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}.
