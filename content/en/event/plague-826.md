## Summary
In 826 AH there was a severe plague in Syria. It is said that the number who died in it within a few days exceeded fifty thousand. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.186}

A great plague also struck Damietta. All of this is according to Ibn al-Imad in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*; the figures are approximate and need an additional source. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.186}
