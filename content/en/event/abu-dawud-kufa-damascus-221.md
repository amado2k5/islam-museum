## Overview
Abu Dawud entered Kufa in the year twenty-one and went to 'Umar ibn Hafs's house, but a hearing was not granted him {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 358}; at Kufa he heard al-Hasan ibn al-Rabi' al-Burani, Ahmad ibn Yunus al-Yarbu'i and a circle of others {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 359}.

## Then Damascus
He said: I entered Damascus in the year twenty-two {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 362}, where he heard Safwan ibn Salih, Hisham ibn 'Ammar and a circle of others {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 359}.
