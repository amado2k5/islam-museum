## Summary
In 494 AH the Batiniyya grew in number in Iraq and al-Jabal. Their leader was Hasan ibn al-Sabbah {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 400}.

## Their strength
They took control of fortresses and cut the roads. They became a serious concern for people, and their power grew while the sons of Malikshah were busy with their own rivalries {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 400}.
