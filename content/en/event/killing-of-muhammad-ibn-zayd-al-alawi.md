## Overview
The news of the killing of [[person/muhammad-ibn-zayd-al-alawi|Muhammad ibn Zayd al-Alawi]] arrived in Shawwal 287 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.81}

He had set out toward Khurasan, hoping to take it after Isma'il ibn Ahmad had captured Amr ibn al-Layth. When he met the army of Muhammad ibn Harun at the gate of Jurjan, his army was routed. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.81}

What is not covered here: the details of the battle and the manner of his killing, which need an additional source.
