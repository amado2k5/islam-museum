## Overview
The Arbaeen march of Husayn in [[place/karbala|Karbala]]: millions of visitors, most of them Shi'a, walk from Najaf to Karbala in the largest annual gathering on earth. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
Attendance has exceeded twenty million in peak years; processions provide food and lodging free of charge along the road. {cite:wikidata}
