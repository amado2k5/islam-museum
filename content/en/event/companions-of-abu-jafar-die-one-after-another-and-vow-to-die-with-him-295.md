## Overview
Abu Ja'far's companions went out one after another and were killed, among them Maymun ibn Muhammad al-Madani, Ishaq ibn Ibrahim, Ibrahim ibn Muhammad of Khawlan, Yusuf ibn Ya'qub, and Ahmad ibn Abdallah of Khawlan. The rest said there was no life left for them after their companions, went out together in one charge, and were all killed.

## Detail
The trouble around Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and his companions grew heavy, and their enemies multiplied. After Yusuf the Hamdani, Maymun ibn Muhammad al-Madani went out, kept charging forward and striking until he was killed. Then Ishaq ibn Ibrahim went out and fought until he was killed. Then Ibrahim ibn Muhammad of Khawlan went out, his arrows spent, drew his sword and fought until he was killed. Then Yusuf ibn Ya'qub shot a man and killed him, and was himself killed. Then Ahmad ibn Abdallah of Khawlan went out and fought hard until he was killed. The companions then said to one another that they had no life left after their companions, that they had emigrated from their homes and left their property for just such a day, and that their wish had been granted, since their blood was shed for the family of the Prophet. They asked Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah whether they had fulfilled what was due to God and to him. He said yes, and that he had not seen anyone more faithful to his pledge than they. They answered that they would protect him with their own selves and commend him to God, and then they went out in one body, covering themselves with their shields, and kept fighting until all of them were killed. Several names are garbled in the scan and the spelling of the nisbas varies between the scan and the marginal reading; these are flagged for the verifier. Then he charged a man of Banu Bishr named Ahmad ibn al-Ahqad and struck him, cutting off his thumb, so that the man turned and fled. Banu al-Harith then charged him in one body, pelted him with many arrows in the face, and struck at him with swords until his shield was cut to pieces. He went back to the women's room and asked for water. A girl brought him a cup of water, but blood from his face dripped into it, and he pushed it back without drinking.
