## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/musa-ibn-abdallah-ibn-khazim|Musa]] prevailed over [[place/tirmidh|Tirmidh]]. Its landowner and people asked the Turks for help, and they did not help them. Then companions of his father and others joined him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
The Turks and the people of Tirmidh helped their lord and surrounded Musa, attacked his camp at night and plundered it, and sixteen men of the Muslims were hit. [[person/thabit-ibn-qutba|Thabit]] and [[person/hurayth-ibn-qutba|Hurayth]], the two sons of Qutba of the Khuza'a, were with him, and they called on [[person/tarkhun|Tarkhun]] for help, and he came to their aid, and their standing in the army grew. Then Hurayth was killed by an arrow. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Thabit went to Hushura and asked Tarkhun for help, and he prevailed over the outskirts of the city. Musa went back to Tirmidh and fortified himself there; Thabit besieged him with eighty thousand men. Musa made a night attack on Thabit's camp, and the foreigners went back to their lands. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source quotes al-Hajjaj, when the news reached him, as praising God who had given victory to the hypocrites over the polytheists. It adds that the people of Khurasan said they had never seen the like of Musa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
