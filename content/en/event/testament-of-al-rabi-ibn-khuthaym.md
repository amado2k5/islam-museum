## Summary
[[person/al-rabi-ibn-khuthaym|Al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym]] was a Successor of Kufa from the Banu Thawr, who died at Kufa while Ubaydallah ibn Ziyad governed it. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 312} Ibn Sa'd transmits his will by several chains and Abu Nu'aym gives it in the Hilya; the wording differs slightly between them, as set out below.

## His last days
Ibn Sa'd reports that palsy struck al-Rabi' and that he would go out to prayer supported between two men; told that he had been given a dispensation, he would say: when you hear "come to success," answer. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 309} In another report he was led to prayer with the palsy, and when they said "Abu Yazid, you have been given a dispensation," he said he heard "come to prayer, come to success," and that if they could, they should come even crawling. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 309} Dawud al-Attar says Bakr ibn Ma'iz tended him, oiling and delousing his head and washing it, and when al-Rabi's spittle ran one day Bakr wept, and al-Rabi' asked him why. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 309} From al-Hasan: when told, after the palsy, that he might take treatment, he answered that Ad, Thamud, the people of al-Rass and many generations between had passed, among whom there were those who described remedies and those they were described for, and neither the describer nor the one described for remained, but all had perished. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 311} Al-Sha'bi relates that they visited him and asked for a prayer, and he offered a comprehensive prayer of praise, seeking all good and refuge from all evil. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 310}

## The text of the will and its content
Ibn Sa'd relates from Sufyan from his father from Mundhir al-Thawri that at death al-Rabi' made a will saying: this is what al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym acknowledged against himself, calling God to witness, and God is sufficient as witness and as recompenser of His righteous servants; he was content with God as Lord, Muhammad as Prophet and Islam as religion, and content for himself and whoever obeyed him to worship God among the worshippers, praise Him among the praisers and give sincere counsel to the community of Muslims. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 311} He gives it also through Shu'ba from Sa'id ibn Masruq; asked whether he had heard it himself, Sa'id said his elders and the tribe had told him, and the wording is "whoever followed me among the Muslims" in place of "whoever obeyed me." {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 312} Abu Nu'aym gives the will through Jarir from Abu Hayyan al-Taymi from his father and through the Shu'ba route, in which Shu'ba asked Sa'id who had told him and he replied that the tribe had told him from al-Rabi'. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 scan vol. 2 p. 111} {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 scan vol. 2 p. 112} The will is thus a testimony and an expression of contentment with God, the Prophet and Islam, with a pledge to worship, praise and counsel the Muslims; it divides no property.

## That no one be notified
Ibn Sa'd relates that al-Rabi' said: do not notify anyone of me, and draw me out gently to my Lord. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 311} Elsewhere he reports that al-Rabi' charged them to draw him out gently to his Lord, which the narrator explains as meaning that they were not to announce his death to anyone. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 312}

## What he left
The sources read do not mention any money al-Rabi' left. Ibn Sa'd relates from Abd Khayr, who accompanied him on a campaign, that he returned with slaves and animals; when Abd Khayr visited days later he found no trace of them, and when asked al-Rabi' at first did not answer, then recalled the verse at [[passage/quran-3-92|Qur'an 3:92]] about spending from what one loves. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 311} Abu Nu'aym says he wore a long-sleeved shirt worth three or four dirhams. {cite:hilyat-al-awliya-dar-al-kutub-al-ilmiyya-1988 scan vol. 2 p. 113}

## Death and grave
Ibn Sa'd says: al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym died at Kufa while Ubaydallah ibn Ziyad governed it. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 312} The sources read do not name who washed him or where his grave lies, so it is not assigned to any place.

## Where the reports differ
The will's wording differs between "whoever obeyed me" and "whoever followed me," in "and rewarder," and in whether the Prophet is followed by "and the Furqan" or "and the Qur'an as a guide" in the version through Shu'ba and Isra'il from Sa'id ibn Masruq from Mundhir al-Thawri. {cite:tabaqat-ibn-sad-khanji-2001 vol. 8 p. 312} Ibn Sa'd gives no date of death other than the governorship of Ubaydallah ibn Ziyad, so this record estimates none.
