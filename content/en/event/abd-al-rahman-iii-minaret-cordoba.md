## Overview
[[person/abd-al-rahman-iii-al-nasir|Abd al-Rahman al-Nasir]] built the celebrated minaret of the [[building/great-mosque-cordoba|Great Mosque of Cordoba]] about 951 — climbed by twin staircases that meet only at the top and bearing apples of gold and silver. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari} {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.212-214}

## What happened
Travellers described the minaret as a thing without like in Islam: two staircases climbing in alternation to meet at its head, crowned by gilded apples that shone over the town. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.212-214}
