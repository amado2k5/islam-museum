## Overview
The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] came down with seven hundred fighters in the valley of [[place/uhud|Uhud]], facing Medina with the mountain behind him. Al-Mubarakfuri's account describes this place as the best position on the field. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255}

## The company of archers
The Prophet chose fifty skilled archers and put [[person/abd-allah-ibn-jubayr|Abd Allah ibn Jubayr]] in command of them. He ordered them to station themselves on a small hill south-east of the camp known as [[place/jabal-al-rumma|the Hill of the Archers]], and charged them not to leave their position in victory or in defeat. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255}

The book quotes his words to their commander: "Keep the horses away from us with arrows, so that they do not come at us from behind." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255}

## The right and left wings
He put al-Mundhir ibn Amr over the right wing, and [[person/zubayr-ibn-al-awwam|al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam]] over the left, supported by [[person/al-miqdad-ibn-amr|al-Miqdad]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.255}

## The date
It was the morning of Saturday, the seventh of Shawwal 3 AH according to al-Mubarakfuri; the Gregorian date is derived. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.256}

This event is part of [[event/battle-of-uhud|the Battle of Uhud]].
