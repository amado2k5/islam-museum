## Overview
In the morning [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] gathered [[group/banu-hamdan|Hamdan]], the people of Najran, Thaqif and [[group/al-ahlaf-najran|al-Ahlaf]], ordered his army to ride, and went on until he camped at the gate of [[place/minas-najran|Minas]], spread the troops around it on its sides and ordered them to fight the people of the fortress, where [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]], his clan and most of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] were. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160}

## The fighting
The people fought them stubbornly until they broke one side of the fortress, some of [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] entered from the village side and the fighting grew hard there; the people of the fortress climbed on to the roofs and shot at them from behind, in front and from the sides, and threw stones and bricks from above, while the men were in narrow lanes and crowded toward the fight and toward the broken place; it went on until near sunset, when al-Hadi called his army and went back to the fortress. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} The narrator says heavy losses were inflicted on the people of the fortress and nearly as much on his army by arrows, and not a few were killed in all. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160}

## Siege and the split
He stayed at the fortress fighting them at the gates of their fort, one day fighting and one day leaving them, which pressed them hard, cut them from their livelihood and narrowed their ground, and they divided into three camps, some at [[place/sawhan-najran|Sawhan]], some at Minas and some at the village of [[place/al-hajar-najran|al-Hajar]], bound by oath to cry out to one another and saying that wherever he went they would all reinforce. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.160} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.161} See [[event/al-hadis-charge-at-sawhan-286]]. The account is Zaydi, told in the imam's followers' voice, and the losses are its own figures.
