## Overview
The heading in the original's margin reads: al-Hadi's entry into Sana'a the second time after his leaving Shibam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. He entered [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] in the best of states, with none of his companions killed except an Aqili man stabbed in the belly {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. The sentence breaks off at the foot of p. 225 and runs on in p. 226 with the man's death and the people's wonder at the steadfastness of al-Hadi and his men in spite of their few numbers, which lies outside this section {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}.

## Detail
This is the second entry after [[event/al-hadi-entry-into-sanaa-288|the first entry]] and it follows [[event/al-hadi-routs-al-yafur-and-al-tarif-before-sanaa-288]]. [[event/battle-of-al-rahba-288]] says al-Hadi entered Sana'a on a Friday with some days left of Rajab 288 AH, and the text on p. 226 gives only a Friday of the same year; the museum thinks it probably the same entry and does not assert it. The Aqili is not named. The heading is from the margin of the original, that is the copyist's.
