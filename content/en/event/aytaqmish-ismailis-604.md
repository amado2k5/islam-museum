## Summary
In 604 AH Aytaqmish, lord of the Jibal, marched into the Ismaili lands adjoining Qazwin, inflicted a great slaughter, plundered, took captives and captured five of their fortresses. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 319}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Aytaqmish marched that year into the Ismaili lands near Qazwin, killed a great number, plundered and enslaved, besieged their fortresses and took five of them, and resolved to besiege Alamut and exterminate its people. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 319} Then came the rising of the lords of Maragha and Erbil which he had earlier described, and the amir Abu Bakr summoned him, so he left the Ismaili country and marched to Abu Bakr; his plan against their fortresses was never completed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 319}
