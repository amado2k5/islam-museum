## Summary
[[person/ibn-khaldun|Ibn Khaldun]] composed the «Muqaddima» in five months and finished it by the middle of 779 AH. He then revised it and added the histories of the Arabs and Berbers as the «Kitab al-'Ibar». {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p. 652}

## Method
He opens the Muqaddima by saying that history teaches us the conditions of past peoples, and he names his new science 'umran, human social organisation. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p. 22,48}
