## Summary
Al-Safadi's copying activity is already attested in 718/1318 by a manuscript of Ibn Nubata's Khutab in his hand; he went on copying so assiduously that autograph volumes he wrote number nearly five hundred, with twice as much again in his chancery production {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 vol. 2 p. 89}.
