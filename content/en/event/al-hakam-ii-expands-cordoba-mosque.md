## Overview
[[person/al-mustansir-hakam|Al-Hakam al-Mustansir]] made the [[building/great-mosque-cordoba|mosque's]] celebrated enlargement (961–976), perfecting the mihrab and maqsura with interlaced ribbed domes and mosaic sent by the Byzantine king. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}

## What happened
Al-Maqqari counts al-Hakam's enlargement the finest work done on the mosque: a gilded mihrab, interlacing ribbed domes, and mosaic sent by the Byzantine king together with his craftsmen. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}
