## Overview
In 472 AH [[person/muslim-ibn-quraysh|Muslim ibn Quraysh al-Uqayli]] took Aleppo. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.323}

## The agreement
He wrote to the sultan Malikshah a letter acknowledging in his own name that he would pay, in return for it, three hundred thousand dinars a year to the sultan's treasury, and the sultan agreed to this. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.323}
