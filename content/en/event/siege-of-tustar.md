## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] marched to [[place/tustar|Tustar]] and asked Umar for reinforcement, and Umar sent [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]] with the people of Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Preparation
Abu Musa wrote to Umar asking for help, and Umar ordered Ammar to march with the people of Kufa. Ammar put [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]] in front. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The account names the commanders of the right wing, the left, the cavalry and the infantry of both armies, among them al-Bara' ibn Malik, Anas ibn Malik, al-Bara' ibn Azib, Hudhayfa and al-Nu'man ibn Muqarrin. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
The people of Tustar fought them fiercely. The people of Basra and Kufa charged until they reached the city's gate. [[person/al-bara-ibn-malik|Al-Bara' ibn Malik]] fought at the gate until he was killed, and [[person/hormuzan|Hormuzan]] entered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
