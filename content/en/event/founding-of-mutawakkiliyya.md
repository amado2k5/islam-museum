## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] was installed as caliph in Dhu al-Hijja 232 AH, lodged at al-Haruni and built a great deal, then founded a city he named al-Mutawakkiliyya near [[place/samarra|Samarra]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.418}

## The building
He granted people estates in the enclosure that al-Mu'tasim had fenced off. He built a congregational mosque, on which he spent a great deal, and raised its minaret until it could be seen from several farsakhs. Then he founded al-Mutawakkiliyya between al-Karkh, known as Firuz, and al-Qatul, known as Kisra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.418}

## His residence and death
He lodged in it at the beginning of 246 AH, and died in it in Shawwal 247 AH. He was succeeded by [[person/al-muntasir|al-Muntasir]], who moved away from it to Samarra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.418}
