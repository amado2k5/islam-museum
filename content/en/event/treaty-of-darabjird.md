## Overview
A truce made by [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]] with the Hurbudh at [[place/darabjird|Darabjird]], which was the seat of their learning and religion. The Hurbudh made peace with him for money he paid, and on condition that its people be treated like others among the Persians whose lands had been conquered. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the peace
A large gathering formed in the area of Jahrum; Uthman dispersed them and took its land. Then he came to Fasa, and its chief made peace with him on the same terms as Darabjird. It is reported that the Hurbudh made peace over it as well. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
