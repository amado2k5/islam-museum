## Summary
Safi al-Din Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Tabataba, known as Ibn al-Tiqtaqa, was born, it seems, shortly after the Mongol conquest of Baghdad in 656/1258 — he does not mention witnessing it himself — and his line runs back to al-Hasan ibn Ali through Ibrahim al-Tabataba {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.
