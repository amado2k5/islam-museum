## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that [[person/yazid-ibn-abd-al-malik|Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik]] said when he came to power: "Govern by the conduct of [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]]." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399}

## The witnesses
Forty elders were brought to him, and they bore witness for him that there is no reckoning or punishment for the caliphs. This is what al-Suyuti relates, a report transmitted without a judgement on it here. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399}

## The return
Al-Suyuti relates from Ibn al-Majishun that Yazid ruled for forty days by the conduct of Umar and then turned from it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399}

What is not covered here: what replaced that conduct. This needs an additional source.
