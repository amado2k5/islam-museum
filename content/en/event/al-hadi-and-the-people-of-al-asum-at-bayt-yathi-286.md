## Overview
[[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] said: [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi ila al-Haqq]] stayed at [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] until, some days of Jumada I having passed, word reached him of a people in a place called al-A'sum, a journey from Khiwan, whose host would bring a visiting guest what the narrator calls ugly practices that they saw no sin in, but held lawful. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} The account is Zaydi, in the imam's followers' voice; the description of the people is the narrator's, and the museum does not repeat its detail or pass judgment on it; the manuscript margin heads it "the account of al-A'sum". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125}

## Al-Hadi's words
The narrator heard al-Hadi say: "We should fight these people and begin with them before fighting the Romans." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} The sentence is the imam's as the narrator reports it.

## The march to Bayt Yathi'
He said: on a Saturday some days into Jumada I of the year 286 he set out and halted at [[place/athafit|Athafit]], spent the night, and in the morning headed for [[place/bayt-yathi-yemen|Bayt Yathi']]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} This was because the notables of the district had written to him and shown their affection; the town was in the hand of [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]], and when al-Hadi drew near its people came out to him, he entered with them, and they pledged to him, heard him and obeyed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125}

## The admonition at al-A'sum
Al-Hadi sent to [[place/al-asum-yemen|al-A'sum]], and its men and elders came; he admonished them well, reminded them of God and of His days, and said: listen to me, what is said of you is not the way of the people of Islam nor of the laws of the religion of Muhammad, and whoever I learn after today does anything of it, I will march on you with horse and men and spend wealth against you until I wipe you out, you ignorant folk, so fear God for your own sake and guard your women. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} He told them to give women veils with which to cover their faces and not to show their adornment. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126}

## His order to the townspeople
He then told the people of the town as well: it has reached me that the women of the desert enter the markets with faces uncovered, so make the women take veils and not display themselves or show their adornment, as God Almighty has said. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} The editor refers in a footnote to [[passage/quran-24-31|al-Nur 31]] and [[passage/quran-33-33|al-Ahzab 33]]; neither is typed here. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126}

## The pledge and the oath
The people of al-A'sum said: "We are lied about in many things and we repent of all of it, and we pledge to you." They pledged to him, he had them swear and took a covenant, and added to the oath: "your women shall not display themselves, you shall keep them from talking with men who are not their mahram, and you shall hold them to wearing veils." They swore to him on that. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126}

## Return to Athafit
Al-Hadi left Bayt Yathi' on a Friday with days left of Jumada I and reached Athafit the same day, then sent to the people of al-Subay' and Khirfan, with Banu Rabi'a beside him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} What followed is the [[event/al-hadi-peace-between-banu-rabia-and-the-people-of-khirfan-and-al-subay-286|peace he made between Banu Rabi'a and the people of Khirfan and al-Subay']].

## Notes
The name "Bayt Yathi'" is read from the heading, and the body prints it with different dotting; the heading's spelling is taken. The text's figure for the distance of al-A'sum from Khiwan is damaged in the print and is not given. What is said of the people of al-A'sum comes from a single narrator and is not a direct witness; the people themselves say they were falsely accused and promise to repent of whatever was said. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.125} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126}
