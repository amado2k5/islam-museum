## Summary
al-Baladhuri, in *Futuh al-Buldan*, reports that [[person/uqba-ibn-nafi|Uqba ibn Nafi]] sent [[person/busr-ibn-abi-artat|Busr ibn Abi Artat]] to a fortress near Kairouan. He took it, killing and taking captives. It is known today as [[place/qalat-busr|the Fortress of Busr]], near a town called Mujana, by a silver mine. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another report
al-Baladhuri says that he heard from someone who mentioned that it was Musa ibn Nusayr who had sent Busr, and that Busr was eighty-two years old. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## About Busr
The author says that Busr was born a year before the Prophet's death. He adds that someone other than al-Waqidi claims that he narrated from the Prophet, and the author concludes with the words "and God knows best." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date is disputed between the governorship of Uqba and that of Musa ibn Nusayr, and the data give no specific page for it.
