## The story
It reached Malik that men of the Companions saw Laylat al-Qadr in a dream in the last ten nights, and the Messenger of God said: I see your dreams have agreed on the last third; whoever seeks it should seek it in the last third. The muhaqqiq notes Yahya transmitted it as a mursal report {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-5-895|Muwatta 895]].

## The meaning
In this report the dream can be collective evidence for times of worship; the Prophet's saying that the dreams agreed on the last third is a sign it is the promised night {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
