## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that the Franks came to Syria in 492 AH and took Nicaea, the first city they took. Then they took Jerusalem after a siege of a month and a half. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.656-657}

## Their arrival
They came in a great host by way of the sea of Constantinople, and reached Kafr Tab, and the kings and the subjects were alarmed. Al-Suyuti gives a report, "it is said", that the lord of Egypt wrote to them calling them to Syria when he saw the strength of the Seljuks. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.656-657}

## Jerusalem
Al-Suyuti says that they killed there more than seventy thousand, among them scholars and worshippers. They demolished the shrines and gathered the Jews into the church and burned it on them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.656-657}

Then the calls for help reached Baghdad, and the sultans disagreed, so the Franks gained control of Syria. The caliph was [[person/al-mustazhir-billah|al-Mustazhir bi-llah]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.656-657}
