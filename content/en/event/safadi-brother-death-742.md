## Summary
Al-Safadi's younger brother Abu Ishaq Jamal al-Din Ibrahim died on 4 Jumada II 742/15 November 1341; al-Safadi devoted to him an emotional obituary in his Wafi including many of his elegiac verses {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759}.
