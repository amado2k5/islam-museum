## Overview
In 94 AH al-Hajjaj killed Sa'id ibn Jubayr, who had joined the revolt of Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.487} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.488}

The data goes no further than this. The details of the account need an additional source.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] places the killing of Sa'id ibn Jubayr under the year 95: in it al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf killed Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and al-Hajjaj himself died that same year. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 307}
