## Overview
In 125 AH [[person/yahya-ibn-zayd|Yahya ibn Zayd]] was killed in Khurasan. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.228}

Al-Tabari says that Nasr ibn Sayyar wrote to al-Hasan ibn Zayd al-Tamimi, who was at Tus, to look into Yahya's affair, and if Yahya passed by them, not to let him stay. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.229}

What is not covered here: the place of the fighting and its details. This needs an additional source.
