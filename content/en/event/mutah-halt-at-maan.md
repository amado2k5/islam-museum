## Overview
The army of Mu'tah camped at [[place/maan|Ma'an]] in the land of Syria, as part of [[event/battle-of-mutah|the Battle of Mu'tah]]. They learned that Heraclius had come down at Mab in al-Balqa' with a hundred thousand Romans, and that the like of them had joined him from Lakhm, Judham, al-Qayn, Bahra' and Bali. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## The address
The people hesitated for two days over seeking reinforcements. [[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abd Allah ibn Rawaha]] encouraged them and urged them to go on to seek one of the two good things: victory or martyrdom. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
