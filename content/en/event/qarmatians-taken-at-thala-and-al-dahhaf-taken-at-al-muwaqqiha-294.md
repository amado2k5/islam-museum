## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah learned that some Qarmatians were in the fort of Thala with men of Banu al-Harith. He sent his sons Ali and al-Qasim, who took two Qarmatians. Then Ali took al-Dahhaf and his companions at al-Muwaqqiha, a village of Najran, and had them put in prison.

## Detail
The book says Abu Ja'far was told that some Qarmatians were in the fort of Thala, with a group of Banu al-Harith called Dhu Qitn. At dawn he assembled the troops and sent his sons Ali and al-Qasim at sunrise. They took two Qarmatians, one named Muhammad ibn Abdallah, and returned to al-Hijr. Later he was told of other men at al-Muwaqqiha, a village of Najran; Ali went there, took al-Dahhaf and a few men with him, and they were sent to prison.
