## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] set out for Sana'a with large forces from Khawlan and Hamdan, and [[person/ibrahim-ibn-khalaf-ibn-tarif|Ibrahim ibn Khalaf ibn Tarif]] met him at [[place/al-rahba-sanaa|al-Rahba]] with dense troops, while Abd al-Hakim ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur was in Sana'a. They fought, al-Hadi prevailed, routed them and killed a number, and the last of them reached a place the text reads as Zahr. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20}

## The entry into Sana'a
Ibn Abi al-Khayr left Sana'a and al-Hadi entered it on a Friday with some days left of Rajab 288 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} War flared between al-Hadi and Al Ya'fur and Al Tarif; Uthman ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'fur and As'ad ibn Abi Ya'fur took charge, As'ad staying at Shibam, and the man who led the fighting against al-Hadi was Ibrahim ibn Khalaf among the Banu Tarif and other Yemenis who backed him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} See [[group/banu-yafur]] and [[group/banu-tarif]].
