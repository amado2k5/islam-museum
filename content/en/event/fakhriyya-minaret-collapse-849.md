## Overview
The "Fragments of Gold" records that on the night of Friday, the eighth of Muharram 849 AH, the minaret of the Fakhriyya madrasa collapsed in Suwaiqat al-Sahib in Cairo. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 274}

## What the source says
Ibn al-Imad says that the minaret was built a little after the six hundredth year, and that a great many people perished in the rubble. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 274}

The available text gives neither the number of victims nor the cause of the collapse. An additional source is needed.
