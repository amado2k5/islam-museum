## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/said-ibn-uthman|Sa'id ibn Uthman]] took the hostages he had taken from the Soghdians to Medina. He gave their clothes and belts to his clients and dressed them in woollen robes, and ordered them to water and work. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His killing
The hostages came in on him in his session and killed him, then they killed themselves. Al-Baladhuri says that Malik ibn al-Rayb and Khalid ibn Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt composed two odes about him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
