## Overview
[[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] died in Damascus in 42 AH at the age of thirty-five, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.288}

## Before his death
al-Baladhuri relates that Habib wrote to Uthman ibn Affan about what he had conquered in the land of Armenia. Uthman judged that he should be a raider on the frontiers of Syria and al-Jazira, and he appointed [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] over the Armenian frontier. Then he ordered Habib to withdraw and leave Silat ibn Zufar al-Absi there, and Habib returned to Syria to raid the Byzantines. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.288}

## In Damascus
Habib settled in Homs, and then [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] moved him to [[place/damascus|Damascus]], where he died. al-Baladhuri says that Mu'awiya sent him with an army to aid Uthman while Uthman was besieged. When he reached Wadi al-Qura, news came of Uthman's killing, and he turned back. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.288}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
