## The story
Al-Bukhari relates in the chapter of the call to prayer that the rooster saw an angel and crowed, and the Prophet mentioned this in connection with the adhan {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} [[passage/bukhari-3303|Bukhari 3303]].

## The meaning
In this report, among the signs of prayer-time is what the rooster sees of the angels; it is among the proofs that among God's creatures are those who see what man does not see {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
