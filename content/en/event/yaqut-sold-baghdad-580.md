## Summary
Taken captive in Byzantine territory while still very small, Yaqut was carried to Baghdad at five or six and bought by the merchant Askar ibn al-Nasr al-Hamawi, who died in 606 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264}.

## At the kuttab
His master named him Yaqut — a gemstone name of the kind given to mawali — attached to it the name Abd Allah, and put him in the kuttab so that he could keep the business accounts, Askar himself being illiterate and knowing nothing beyond trade {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.
