## Summary
Shortly before his death, fire consumed Ibn al-Mulaqqin's library and most of his near three hundred works — among them the twenty-volume Sharh on al-Bukhari and the five zawa'id commentaries never found again; Ibn Hajar addressed him verses consoling him over the burnt books {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 dhayl p. 122} {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/44-45}.
