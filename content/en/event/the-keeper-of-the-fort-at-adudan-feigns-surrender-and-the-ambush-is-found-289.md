## Overview
Some days later [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] ordered his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]] out with an army in pursuit of the men, and they met on the mountain of [[place/zabwa-sanaa-yemen|Zabwa]] and fought hard, and the men broke and each went back to his camp {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239}. The men had a commander at Adudan, whom they told to ask for safety, offer the fort for sale and call some men into it; this was a trick and deceit on their part, and they hid their armies below the fort, and the keeper of the fort sent to Abdullah ibn al-Husayn that he had secured the fort for him, to hurry and take it and pay him what they had agreed {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.239}.

## Detail
Al-Hadi ordered his brother and his son out with an army and they halted near the fort and sent scouts to search around and beyond it; the scouts came on some of the men's hidden party, who came out of their places in the scouts' faces, they fought hard and the men were routed, some of their leaders were killed and horses taken {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.240}. The narrator credits God's kindness to his friends and quotes a verse ([[passage/quran-33-25|al-Ahzab 33:25]]), linked here without its text {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.240}. The Adudan of the printed text is read other ways in the preceding page of the book, so its reading stays open.
