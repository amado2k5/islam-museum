## Summary
In Ramadan, Abu al-Faraj Abd al-Rahman ibn Ali ibn al-Jawzi died. He was a Hanbali preacher and historian, author of al-Muntazam and many hundreds of other works, and the most famous preacher of his age {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.276}.
