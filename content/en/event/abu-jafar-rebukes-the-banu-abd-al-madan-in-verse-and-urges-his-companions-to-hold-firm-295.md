## Overview
Abu Ja'far spoke verses rebuking Banu Abd al-Madan for their betrayal and calling them the worst of men. He then told his companions not to weaken or despair at their small number and their enemy's great number. They answered that they knew they stood for the truth and their enemies for falsehood, and that their only fear was that the enemy should get the better of him.

## Detail
Abu Ja'far recited verses in the meter al-wafir rebuking Banu Abd al-Madan for their betrayal after they had sworn to support him, saying that betrayal is the mark of a coward and calling them the worst of men. The verses are described here and not quoted. He then turned to his companions and told them not to weaken or despair at their small number and their enemy's great number, and to die with honour in their religion. He referred to a Qur'anic idea, that God praised the few and blamed the many, without the book giving the verse. They answered that they knew they were on the truth and their enemy on falsehood, that nothing troubled them but the enemy's gaining power over him, that they would give their own selves and blood for him, and that they would endure in every condition. He praised them and told them they were better than they had said. This happened at the outer door of his house with his companions gathered around him.
