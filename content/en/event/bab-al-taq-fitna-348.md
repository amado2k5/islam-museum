## Overview
In Jumada al-Ula 348 AH the strife between the Shi'a and the people of the Sunna intensified in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]. Many people were killed between them, and a great fire broke out at Bab al-Taq. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 682}

## What the source says
Ibn al-Jawzi, in "al-Muntazam", reports the event in a few lines. He does not detail its causes or name those who took part. The Gregorian date (around July 959 CE) is derived by conversion, and is not stated in the source. It needs an additional source.
