When illness pressed hard on [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]] and weighed him down, Quraysh feared he would die and they would then seize his nephew, and the Arabs would reproach them saying: they left him alone until his uncle died and then fell on him. So their notables and chiefs went to him — about twenty-five men, among them [[person/utba-ibn-rabia|Utba ibn Rabi'a]], [[person/shayba-ibn-rabia|Shayba]], [[person/abu-jahl|Abu Jahl]], [[person/umayya-ibn-khalaf|Umayya ibn Khalaf]], [[person/abu-sufyan-ibn-harb|Abu Sufyan]] and [[person/al-as-ibn-wail|al-As ibn Wa'il]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

They said to Abu Talib: "Abu Talib, you know your standing among us, and what you see has come upon you and we fear for you. You know what is between us and your nephew — call him and take for him from us and take for us from him: let him leave us and our religion and we will leave him and his religion." Abu Talib called him and put their words to him. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}

The Prophet said: "what if I gave you one word by which you would rule the Arabs and the non-Arabs would submit to you?" — in one version: "I want them for a single word by which the Arabs would submit to them and the non-Arabs would pay them the jizya" — Abu Jahl said: yes, and ten words. He said: "say: there is no god but God, and cast down what you worship besides Him." {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}

They clapped their hands, disdained and said: do you mean to make the gods one god? Then they departed in despair; on that occasion the opening of Sura Sad came down: "and they marvel that a warner has come to them from among themselves, and the disbelievers say 'this is a lying sorcerer — has he made the gods one god? This is a strange thing'." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
