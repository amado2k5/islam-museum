## Overview
In Rajab 296 AH [[person/abu-abd-allah-al-shii|Abu Abd Allah al-Shi'i]] defeated the army of Ibrahim ibn Abi al-Aghlab. [[person/ziyadat-allah-iii|Ziyadat Allah]] fled to Egypt, and al-Shi'i entered Raqqada. With this, the rule of the Aghlabids came to an end. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.803-804}

## Entering Raqqada
He stayed in its palaces and distributed its houses among the Kutama. He proclaimed safety, and gathered the wealth and weapons of Ziyadat Allah and ordered them guarded. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.803-804}

## Before Raqqada
He had besieged Constantine and taken it on terms of safe conduct. He took the country palace by palace: Bulzima by force, then Tabna on terms of safe conduct, then Baghaya, Marmajanna, Tifash and al-Qasrayn. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.803-804}

The date of the event in the Gregorian calendar is converted. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.803}
