## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that in 30 AH Abd Allah ibn Amir sent [[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi ibn Ziyad al-Harithi]] to [[place/sijistan|Sistan]], and he made peace with its people as far as Zaranj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The road to Zaranj
Al-Rabi camped at al-Fahraj, then crossed the desert of seventy-five leagues. He raided the district of Zalaq, and its landowner ransomed himself with gold and silver and made peace. Then the people of Kurkuwiya and Hayson made peace with him without fighting. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The people of Zushat fought him fiercely and some of the Muslims were struck, then he routed them until they were forced into the city. He took Nashrudh and Sharwadh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Zaranj
He besieged Zaranj, and its marzban [[person/aparwiz|Aparwiz]] sent to him asking for safety. Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Rabi sat on a body of the dead and leaned on another and made his companions sit on the dead, and the marzban was awed by the sight. He made peace for a thousand servants, each with a golden cup. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Rabi entered the city; his governorship had lasted two and a half years, and he took forty thousand heads as captives there. His secretary was [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
