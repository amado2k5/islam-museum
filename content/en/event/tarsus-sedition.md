## Overview
In 279 AH al-Tabari mentions the strife at Tarsus, which was a clash between Muhammad ibn Musa al-A'raj and Makhnun, the slave of Raghib. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.28}

## What followed
Then Khumarawayh released Raghib and appointed Ahmad ibn Tughan over the frontiers. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.29}
