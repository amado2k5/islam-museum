## Summary
[[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din Barbarossa]]'s taking of Tunis in 1534 and its annexation to the Ottoman dominions {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 218}.

## Context
Khayr al-Din had long fixed his attention on Tunis — for the Hafsids' weakness and internal strife, for its strategic overlook of the Sicilian strait, and for his ambition to unite the Maghreb under Ottoman rule {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 215}.

## The taking
His fleet touched at Annaba for supplies, advanced to Bizerte and reached La Goulette, taking it without difficulty; the khatibs and ulema came out to receive him while the Hafsid sultan [[person/al-hasan-ibn-muhammad-hafsi|al-Hasan b. Muhammad]] fled to Spain. Khayr al-Din installed his brother [[person/al-rashid-ibn-muhammad-hafsi|al-Amir al-Rashid]] over Tunis and proclaimed its annexation to the Ottoman dominions {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 218}.
