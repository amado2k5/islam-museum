## Overview
In this year the Prophet's seal-ring fell from 'Uthman's hand into Bi'r Aris — a well two miles from Medina; they bailed it out and never found it, and it became one of those things that weighed upon the people {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 483}.
