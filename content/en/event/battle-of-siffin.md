## Overview
The battle of Siffin took place in 37 AH between [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] and [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]]. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.17}

## Its course
al-Tabari reports that Ali addressed his companions before the fighting and urged them to hold the line firm and guard the banners. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.17}

In the course of the fighting, [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] suggested to Mu'awiya that the Qur'ans be raised. The people of Syria raised them on their spears and called for the Book of God, and many people of Iraq answered that call. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.48}

The index of the volume mentions the "night of al-Harir" and the report of Hashim ibn Utba al-Mirqal. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.627}

## The numbers according to al-Mas'udi
al-Mas'udi relates that the dead numbered seventy thousand, of them forty-five thousand from Syria and twenty-five thousand from Iraq. He says that the stay there lasted a hundred and ten days, and that the engagements were seventy. Twenty-five of the Companions were killed with Ali, among them [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.645-646}

The figures come from al-Mas'udi's account, and checking them needs an additional source.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] says the fighting at Siffin was on Wednesday the seventh of Safar, 37, the truce on the night of Saturday the tenth of Safar, and that Ammar ibn Yasir and Hashim ibn Utba were killed. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 191}
