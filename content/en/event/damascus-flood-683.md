## The great flood of Damascus
In Shaʿban 683 AH a huge flood came at night in Damascus. The Egyptian army was camped in the valley, and it suffered losses beyond description {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 652}.

Houses were destroyed and the rivers were blocked. The water broke the locks of the Faradis Gate and entered as far as the Muqaddamiyya college, and it broke the bridge of the Faradis Gate {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 652}.
