## Overview
After [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] accepted the invitation of the [[place/najran|Najran]] delegation he ordered the people gathered on the Monday, the last day of Jumada I of 284 AH by the sentence that begins on p. 66, then led them outside [[place/saada|Sa'da]] and drew them up himself in right wing, left wing and centre. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.66} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} The report is Zaydi, told by [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] and his son, and the praise in it is the narrator's.

## The drawing up
A man of Hamdan said "I never saw the like of this array", and al-Hadi said that this is how the Messenger of God drew up his companions at Badr or Uhud, the doubt being the narrator's. Then he described fighting, how one thrusts with a lance and strikes with a sword, and took a lance and showed them what he had described. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} The narrator heard the Hamdani, named in the text Muhammad ibn Bihar and called renowned for horsemanship and courage, say he had never seen the like of Abu al-Husayn in his horsemanship, and Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili, also said to be a rider, answered that no one could handle the lance as he could. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} The two names are not entered as persons since their reading is doubtful.

## Sermon and march
They returned to Sa'da and prepared what he needed for the march; on Friday the fourth of Jumada II he led the prayer and gave an eloquent sermon reminding them of jihad and urging reform of the people and the land, then went home; on Saturday he had his tent pitched outside the village and spent the night in it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} On Sunday the sixth of Jumada II 284 AH he set out "in the name of God and with His blessing" for Najran with a dense army of Khawlan and others; the journey took two days and he reached upper Najran on the second. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.67} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} See [[group/khawlan-tribe]].
