## Overview
Al-Suyuti, in "History of the Caliphs", reports that in the days of al-Muttaqi the Green Dome in the City of al-Mansur at Baghdad collapsed. It had been built by al-Mansur, and the sources describe it as the crown of Baghdad and a glory of the Abbasids. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.608}

## Its description
It was eighty cubits high, and beneath it was a vestibule twenty cubits by twenty. On its top was a statue of a horseman with a spear in his hand, and if it faced a direction, it was known that someone from outside would appear from there. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.608}

Its top fell on a night of rain and thunder. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.608}
