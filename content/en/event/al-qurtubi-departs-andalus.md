## Overview
Al-Maqqari places Abu Abdallah Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Qurtubi in the chapter "Andalusis who travelled to the lands of the East": he left Cordoba as an emigrant and settled in Egypt {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari 2/210-211}. The Encyclopaedia of Islam says he "made his way to the Orient and settled at Munyat Abi l-Khusayb in Upper Egypt" {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 550}.
