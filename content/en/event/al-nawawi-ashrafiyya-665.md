## Overview
In 665/1267 al-Nawawi was appointed head of the al-Ashrafiyya dar al-hadith in Damascus in succession to Shihab al-Din Abu Shama, who had just died. He took nothing from its stipend, living on the little his father sent him from Nawa {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1096} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 355-356} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya p. 294}."
