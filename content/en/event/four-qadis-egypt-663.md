## Summary
In 663 AH four judges, one for each of the great legal schools, were appointed in the lands of Egypt. Taj al-Din ibn Bint al-Aʿazz had stopped carrying out many cases, so affairs were at a standstill. Jamal al-Din Aydughdi al-ʿAzizi proposed appointing one judge for each school. The idea pleased Sultan [[person/baybars|Baybars]], who put it into effect at the end of the year, and it was then done in Damascus as well {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.542}.
