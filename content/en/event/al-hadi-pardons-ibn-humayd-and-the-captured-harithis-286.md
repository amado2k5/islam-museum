## Overview
When [[person/ibn-humayd-of-banu-al-harith-286|Ibn Humayd]] and the group of his companions were brought before [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], he asked what they said and what they thought when they did what they did, with no foul or ugly act seen from him, when what had come from him to them was kindness and good, while what came from them was boldness against God, enmity and wrong. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.166} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167} They answered that what he did to them they had deserved; he sat silent a long while, then raised his head and said he would say what Joseph said to his brothers, as in [[passage/quran-12-92|Surat Yusuf, verse 92]], and told them to go to their families and homes, pardoned and forgiven; they prayed for him and thanked him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167}

## Ibn Humayd with al-Hadi
Ibn Humayd stayed on an hour until food came, ate of it and stayed into the evening; al-Hadi told him to go back to his children lest they be anxious for him, and he went to his house on the horse of al-Hadi's brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] with its armour and wearing his own mail, and al-Hadi said nothing about it until he took it back partway along the road. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167} This is the narrator's account, and both the rebuke and the pardon are given as his words about his imam.

## After the pardon
The book says that many of those al-Hadi had pardoned swore, as they drew near their homes, that they would enter no house and see no family until they had called out against al-Hadi, gathered for him and fought him; they went at once to their desert tribes and outlying places, called out all of Madhhij, and stayed asking their commanders and chiefs for horses, mail and arms. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.167} The account continues in [[event/al-harithis-gather-madhhij-and-ibn-bustam-brings-shakir-of-hamdan-286]].
