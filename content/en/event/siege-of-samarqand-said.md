## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/said-ibn-uthman|Sa'id ibn Uthman]] raided [[place/samarqand|Samarqand]], and [[person/khatun-of-bukhara|Khatun]] helped him with the people of Bukhara. The matter ended in a peace for seven hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
Sa'id camped at the gate of Samarqand and swore that he would not leave until he took it and threw down its citadel. Its people fought him for three days, and the fiercest fighting was on the third day. His eye and the eye of [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] were struck. Al-Baladhuri gives another account that al-Muhallab's eye was struck at Taliqan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
A man showed him a palace where the sons of their kings and their great men were, and he besieged them. The people of the city feared that the palace would be taken by force, and asked for peace. He made peace with them for seven hundred thousand dirhams and hostages from their great men's sons, and on condition that he enter the city and leave by the other gate. The number of hostages was fifteen, or it is said forty, or eighty. He threw a stone at the citadel; the stone stuck in its window, and then he departed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
