## Overview
Ranjit Singh took the city of Multan in 1818 after long warfare with the Durranis over the region, and Sikh rule settled on it {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## Diwan Sewan Mal
Diwan Sewan Mal governed Multan between 1821 and 1844, extending his influence over a number of adjacent areas {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## The end of Sikh rule
Sikh rule stayed stable until Ranjit Singh's death in 1839; in 1845 the British began their campaign against the disintegrating state and occupied the city in 1848 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
