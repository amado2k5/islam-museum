## Overview
In 261 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutamid|al-Mu'tamid]] gave allegiance to make his son Ja'far, known as al-Mufawwad ila Allah, his heir, and after him his brother [[person/abu-ahmad-al-muwaffaq|al-Muwaffaq]] Talha. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.565}

## Division of provinces
He appointed his son over the Maghrib, Syria, the Jazira and Armenia. He appointed his brother over the East, Iraq, Baghdad, the Hijaz, Yemen, Fars, Isfahan, al-Rayy, Khurasan, Tabaristan, Sistan and Sind. He gave each of them two banners, white and black. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.565}

## The condition
He stipulated that if anything befell him, the matter would go to his brother if his son had not come of age. He wrote the deed and sent it with the chief judge Ibn Abi al-Shawarib to be hung in the Ka'ba. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.565}
