## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] set out from [[place/ayn-al-tamr|Ayn al-Tamr]] for Syria, and charged [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] to return to his authority without falling short or flagging. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.349-350}

## Timing
His departure was in Rabi al-Akhir, or it is said Rabi al-Awwal, of 13 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.349-350}

## Differing accounts
Al-Baladhuri gives one account that Khalid came from Ayn al-Tamr to Duma and took it, then came to al-Hira, and from there went on to Syria. He says that the more correct account is that he set out from Ayn al-Tamr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.349-350}
