## Overview
In 252 AH al-Tabari mentions the rising of Husayn ibn Muhammad al-Talibi. There was an exchange between him and Harb ibn Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Harb over leadership of the people. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.361}
