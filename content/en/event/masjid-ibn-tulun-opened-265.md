## Summary
In 265/879 the mosque of Ahmad ibn Tulun in al-Qata'i' was first opened for prayer. One of the first criticisms was that its sahn had no place for ablution; the builder answered that he had omitted it deliberately, for the uncleanliness it brings, and promised a tank behind the mosque — and people complained that the tank stood outside the mosque proper, contrary to their custom {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 306}.
