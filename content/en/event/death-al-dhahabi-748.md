## Overview
Al-Dhahabi died at Damascus on the night of 3 Dhu al-Qa'da 748 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/338} — the Sunday-Monday night corresponding to 4 February 1348 as al-Subki and al-Suyuti report — and was buried at the Bab al-Saghir; he had been blinded some years before. Among his most famous pupils was Abd al-Wahhab al-Subki, author of the Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra, whose father Taqi al-Din al-Subki was his most intimate friend {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 214-215}.
