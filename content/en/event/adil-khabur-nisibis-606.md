## Summary
In 606 AH al-Malik al-Adil Abu Bakr ibn Ayyub took Khabur and Nisibis and besieged the city of Sinjar, all of them in the Jazira lands held by Qutb al-Din Muhammad ibn Zangi ibn Mawdud. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 348}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir explains that Qutb al-Din had a deep enmity with his cousin Nur al-Din Arslan Shah ibn Mas'ud, lord of Mosul; in 605 a marriage alliance was formed between Nur al-Din and al-Adil, whose son married a daughter of Nur al-Din, and Nur al-Din's viziers encouraged him to correspond with al-Adil and agree to divide Qutb al-Din's lands and the wilaya of Sanjar Shah's son — Jazirat Ibn Umar and its districts — Qutb al-Din's realm going to al-Adil and the Jazira to Nur al-Din. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 348} This suited Nur al-Din, who wrote to al-Adil, and al-Adil accepted gladly, knowing that once he held those lands he could take Mosul and beyond; he also tempted Nur al-Din by suggesting the lands would go to the son married to Nur al-Din's daughter, who would serve him at Mosul. The pact was sealed and sworn; al-Adil marched quickly from Damascus to the Euphrates and took Khabur, and when Nur al-Din heard of his arrival he grew apprehensive and consulted his companions on what to do. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 348}
