## Summary
The Day of al-Rabi was one of the days of al-Aws and al-Khazraj, after the Day of al-Jisr. They met at a wall at al-Sifh and fought until some of them nearly destroyed the others. Al-Aws were routed, and al-Khazraj pursued them all the way to their homes, contrary to the custom between them. Al-Aws asked for peace, but the Banu al-Najjar refused to answer them. They fortified their women and children in the strongholds, and then al-Khazraj held back from them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 504}
