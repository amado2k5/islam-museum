## Summary
Selim I's entry into Egypt in 923/1517 after [[event/battle-ridaniyya-923|Ridaniyya]], ending the Circassian sultanate {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531}.

## Before the entry
The chronicler Salahsur — companion of Selim and author of the manuscript *Fath-nameh Diyar al-Arab* — recounts that Selim wept long in the Dome of the Rock in Jerusalem and prayed the prayer of need that God open Egypt to him {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 188}.

## The settlement
Selim appointed [[person/khayr-bak|Khayr Bak]] over Egypt and [[person/janbardi-al-ghazali|Janbirdi al-Ghazali]] over Damascus {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 188}; per Ibn al-Imad he kept the four judges and the endowments as they were, and Tuman Bay was hanged at Bab Zuwayla and buried at the Nawti cemetery {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 531, 559-561}.
