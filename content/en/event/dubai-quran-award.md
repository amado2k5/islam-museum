## Overview
An annual international prize and competition in the memorisation and recitation of the Qur'an, held by Dubai since 1997; memorisers from more than a hundred countries compete for major prizes. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
One of the largest Qur'an competitions in the world; it has become an institutional Ramadan season in Dubai. {cite:wikidata}
