## Overview
Ibn al-Athir, in "al-Kamil", relates that the Khazars and the Turks defeated the Muslims in 32 AH at Balanjar. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir pp. 503–504 v.2}

## The account
Uthman had written to [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-rabia|Abd al-Rahman ibn Rabi'a]], who was at the gate, forbidding him to storm it, and expressing his fear for the Muslims. But Abd al-Rahman raided toward [[place/balanjar|Balanjar]]. They fought him very fiercely, and he was killed. The people were routed, and a group of them met [[person/salman-ibn-rabi-a|Salman ibn Rabi'a]], whom Sa'id ibn al-As had sent as reinforcement, and they were saved by him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir pp. 503–504 v.2}

Among the army were Salman al-Farisi and Abu Hurayra. The source says that the people of Balanjar placed the body of the commander in a coffin, and used it to pray for rain. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir pp. 503–504 v.2}
