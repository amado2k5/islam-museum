## Summary
al-Suyuti, in *Tarikh al-Khulafa*, reports that [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] came to [[place/medina|Medina]] while Muhammad ibn Imran al-Talhi was its judge. The cameleers complained to al-Mansur about a matter, and the judge wrote to him asking him to attend and to give them justice. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 431-432}

## The session
al-Mansur attended, together with al-Rabi. The judge did not stand up for him; instead he loosened his cloak and sat with his arms around his knees. He then called the litigants, who made their claims, and he ruled for them against the caliph. When he had finished, al-Mansur said to him: "May God reward you for your religion with the best reward," and ordered that ten thousand dinars be given to him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 431-432}

## A similar report from Basra
The author gives a similar report: al-Mansur wrote to Sawwar ibn Abdallah, judge of Basra, asking him to hand over a disputed piece of land to the commander. Sawwar refused until evidence was produced. al-Mansur said that he had filled the land with justice, and that his judges were turning him back to the truth. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 431-432}

The date is approximate: during al-Mansur's caliphate, between 137 and 158 AH.
