## Summary
In 377 AH Sharaf al-Dawla lifted many injustices from Iraq {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 88}.

## Restoring property
Among these, he returned to the nobleman Abu al-Hasan Muhammad ibn Umar all of his property. Its yearly revenue had been 2,500,000 dirhams, and prices in Baghdad were beyond description at the time {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 88}.
