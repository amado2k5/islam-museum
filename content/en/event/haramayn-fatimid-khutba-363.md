## Overview
Al-Suyuti reports that the Friday sermon and the summons were proclaimed in the two sanctuaries for [[person/al-muizz-li-din-allah|al-Mu'izz the Fatimid]] in Dhu al-Hijja of 363 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.626}

An additional source is needed: the account rests on a single source, and the Gregorian conversion is derived.
