## Overview
In Dhu al-Qa'da Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik went to al-Hadi and spoke for the Ya'am prisoners held over the Wada'a. Al-Hadi released them, and seventeen blood-money payments, matching the number of the Wada'a dead, were carried.

## Detail
The book says al-Hadi had imprisoned the Ya'am to cut their ties with the Wada'a. In Dhu al-Qa'da Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik went to him, spoke for the Ya'am prisoners held because of the Wada'a, and asked for their release. Al-Hadi released them, and seventeen blood-money payments were carried, matching the number of the dead. The book does not identify Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik, so no person record is made here.
