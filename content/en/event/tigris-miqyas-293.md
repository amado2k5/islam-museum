## Overview
In 293 a gauge was erected on the Tigris spanning both banks, twenty-five cubits long — a round mark at every cubit and a square mark every five cubits inscribed in iron, by which the measure of the rises was known {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 349}.
