## The earthquakes of Zabid and Mawza, 916 AH

In the year 916 the city of Zabid was struck by a severe earthquake, then shaken again and a third time. On the afternoon of that day a great star fell from the east towards Syria, visible in daylight, and it was followed by a violent trembling and loud thunder {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 490}.

Then the city of Mawza and its surroundings were struck by a great earthquake of a kind no one had heard of. Tremors continued by night and day, small and large, and they harmed the people of the district severely. Houses cracked, hardly a house was left without damage, the land prepared for farming split open, graves were knocked down and the wells were mixed up {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 490}.

In the same year another great star fell at midnight, and the world was lit up by it {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 490}.
