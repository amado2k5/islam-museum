## Summary
Al-Nasir sent the prince Abu al-Hayja to Hamadan, and he took it and the surrounding mountain country. Then Khwarazm-Shah Takash overpowered it, and the caliph's vizier honoured him and sent him back. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 236}.
