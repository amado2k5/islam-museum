## Overview
The narrator says Wadi'a gathered to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, who led them to al-Hisn, met Shakir and Thaqif and bound them and Wadi'a in alliance to help him and stand with him, then went back to his place with the Wadi'is; when Banu al-Harith heard it their hopes broke and what they had thought failed them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.290}. Then "on Sunday, four days gone of Rajab" al-Hadi set out for Najran {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.290}.

## Detail
The passage carries the heading "Al-Hadi leaves for Najran again": on reaching the upper wadi he was met by Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah with a body of Wadi'is, and went on to al-Hisn, where he spent the night {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.290}. In the morning al-Ahlaf and those of Yam living at Najran came to him and excused their gathering against Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah with Banu al-Harith and the Yam bedouin, telling him they had been frightened into it; he excused them and overlooked their slip, stayed that day, and the Shakir bedouin came to him {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.290}. The date is derived from the tabular calendar: 4 Rajab 292 AH is 12 May 905 in the Julian calendar and falls on a Sunday, as the text says, so the two calendars agree here, unlike some earlier batches; the year 292 comes from the narrative's order after the oath of Jumada I. The telling is in the voice of the imam's supporters in a Zaydi book, and the museum describes it without judgement. What came before about al-Hisn, Thaqif, Shakir and Wadi'a is in [[event/the-abd-al-madan-march-on-minas-and-the-fight-at-its-two-gates-292|the previous batch]] and in [[event/ali-and-al-qasim-sons-of-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-ask-shakir-thaqif-and-wadia-for-aid-292|the sons' call to Shakir, Thaqif and Wadi'a]]. These pages do not place al-Hisn on the map; the question is in the ledger.
