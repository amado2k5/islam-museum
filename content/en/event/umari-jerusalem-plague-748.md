## Summary
When the plague struck, Ibn Fadl Allah resolved on the hajj, then took his household to Jerusalem; his wife died there and he buried her and returned {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/333}.

## Details
On the Day of Arafa 749, back from Jerusalem, al-Umari died of a quartan fever — the plague years closing with the wife's death and then the author's own {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/333}.
