## Overview
In Jumada al-Ula 8 AH the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] sent an army of three thousand fighters to Syria. This was one stage of [[event/battle-of-mutah|the Battle of Mu'tah]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## The commanders
He appointed three commanders in order: [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]], then [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]], then [[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abd Allah ibn Rawaha]]. People bade farewell to the commanders as they left [[place/medina|Medina]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Ibn Hisham states that the date is Jumada al-Ula of the eighth year, on the authority of Ibn Ishaq. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
