## Overview
'Umar sent 'Alqama b. Mujazziz al-Mudliji against Abyssinia — which had been raiding the lands of Islam — and the Muslims were struck hard; 'Umar bound himself never again to carry anyone by sea, meaning for raiding {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}.
