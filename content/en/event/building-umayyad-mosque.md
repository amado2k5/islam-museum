## Overview
In 87 AH [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] began to build the great mosque of [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.367}

## al-Suyuti's account
al-Suyuti says that al-Walid also wrote to have the Prophet's mosque expanded and rebuilt. He relates from Ibn Abi Abla that al-Walid conquered India and al-Andalus and built the mosque of Damascus, and that he used to give bowls of silver to be divided among the readers of the mosque of Jerusalem. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.367}

The Gregorian date is derived. The details of the building need an additional source.
