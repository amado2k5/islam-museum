## Overview
Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Isma'il ibn Abi Bishr (Ishaq ibn Salim) al-Ash'ari al-Yamani al-Basri was born at Basra in 260/873-4; it is also said that he was born in 270 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 743} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 15/85}. He was ninth in descent from the Companion Abu Musa al-Ash'ari {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 743}.

## His lineage and early life
His line goes back to the Ash'aris of Yemen, and al-Dhahabi transmits a long pedigree ascending to Bilal ibn Abi Burda, son of the Companion Abu Musa al-Ash'ari {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 15/85}. Little is known of his early years at Basra beyond that he learned kalam in his native city until he excelled in it {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 743}.
