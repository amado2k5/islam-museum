## Overview
In Safar 588 AH the Banu Amir gathered in a great number under the leadership of Umayra and marched on [[place/basra|Basra]]. Its amir was Muhammad ibn Isma'il, deputising for its iqta' holder, the amir Tughril, a mamluk of the caliph al-Nasir. They arrived on Saturday, the sixth of Safar. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 214-215}

## Fighting and plunder
The amir Muhammad went out to them, and the fighting took place at the Square Road until the end of the day. The Arabs breached the wall by night and entered the town, and plundered the inns and some quarters. Then the forces the amir had gathered from the people of Basra and the countryside were routed. The Arabs entered Basra and plundered it, and its people left it. The people later returned to it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 214-215}

Ibn al-Athir says that he saw this very same story in the year 593 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 214-215}
