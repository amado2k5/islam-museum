## Overview
Al-Tabari returned from Egypt to Baghdad about 256/870, ending his student Wanderjahre; he settled there for the remaining fifty years of his life, devoting himself to teaching and authorship {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 21}.

## Fifty years of writing
Ibn al-Jawzi reports that al-Tabari spent forty years writing forty folios a day, and that when he presented his commentary at thirty thousand folios they judged it longer than a lifetime could finish, so he cut it to about three thousand — and did the same for the History {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 171-172}.
