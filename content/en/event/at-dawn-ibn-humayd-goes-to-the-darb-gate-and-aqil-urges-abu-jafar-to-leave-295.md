## Overview
At the dawn prayer after the Friday night, Ibn Humayd went out to the Darb gate with the Banu al-Harith men who had stayed with him. Aqil ibn Ubaydallah and Abdallah ibn Isa, with a group of Najran men, urged Abu Ja'far to leave the village, and he disliked the advice and did not accept it.

## Detail
The book says that Ibn Humayd went out to the Darb gate at the dawn prayer with the Banu al-Harith men who had stayed with him. At the same time Aqil ibn Ubaydallah and Abdallah ibn Isa came to Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah with a group of Najran men and advised him to leave, and he disliked the advice. While they were talking, Ali ibn Ibrahim al-Madani arrived and reported that Ibrahim al-Jumahi and Muhammad ibn al-Lahhaz al-Muhajjal were at the Darb gate, with a few Banu al-Harith men, wanting to reach Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah. The name al-Lahhaz is printed with a garbled letter in the scan and is recorded as printed.
