## Overview
The people of wickedness and injustice plan to kill al-Hadi in the mosque and shoot at him from the minaret. The first arrow misses. He prays with the people and then says he will keep away from them until God judges between them.

## Detail
After the people of wickedness and injustice agreed to kill him by ambush, they sat in the minaret of the mosque to shoot at him when he went in. When he came out they hurried and shot at him before he entered the mosque. The first arrow missed, his foot entered the mosque, he pushed forward into the doorway, and the door was struck by arrows, and two arrows fell on a cloak he was wearing. He stayed silent until he had led the people in prayer and the morning had brightened, then he told them, and they came out and gathered the arrows from the mosque door. He then said that he had hoped to live among them as one of them, to lead their prayers himself and not to hide from them, but they began with a plot and wanted his life. He therefore said he would draw a veil and keep himself apart until God judges between him and them. The record reports the speech as the narrator gives it.
