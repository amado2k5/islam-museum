## Summary
From Khwarazm al-Tha'alibi moved on to Ghazna, capital of the recently established Ghaznavid dynasty, and resided there for some time {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 450-451}; the flowering of New Persian was unfolding around him at Mahmud's court, yet his works stay silent about it, for he remained a strident champion of Arabic and its literature {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 452}.
