## Overview
Abu Muslim al-Khurasani was killed in 137 AH, according to the index of al-Tabari. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.479}

## The accounts
Al-Suyuti says that al-Mansur took over the caliphate at the start of 137 AH, and that the first thing he did was kill Abu Muslim, the man who had called them to power. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.423}

Al-Mas'udi says that al-Mansur sent al-Jarir al-Bajali to him, and he came despite the warning of Malik ibn al-Haytham. He used to find in earlier books that he would be killed by the Byzantines. He entered al-Mansur's tent at Rumiyya on the Tigris in Madain, and al-Mansur had ordered Uthman ibn Nahik, the commander of his guard, with a group to stand behind his couch. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.279-280}

The index of al-Tabari also records the rising of Abd Allah ibn Ali and his defeat before this. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.667}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Abu Muslim went to Abu Ja'far with a thousand horsemen, leaving his army behind, and met him at al-Rumiyya. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 380} Abu Ja'far lodged him in a palace and visited him for three days; on the fourth he posted Uthman ibn Nahik, head of the guard, with men, who came out with swords when Abu Ja'far clapped. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 380} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 381} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 382}
