## Summary
He journeyed to al-Sham as a pilgrim, hearing at Damascus, Tyre and Mecca, where he met al-Quda'i; he read the whole of Sahih al-Bukhari in five days upon Karima al-Marwaziyya, then returned to Baghdad — in 446 {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 92-93}.
