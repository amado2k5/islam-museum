## Overview
The trench of Najran: the burning of Najran's Christians by [[person/dhu-nuwas|Dhu Nuwas]], the Judaized Himyarite king, in October 523 CE — the "People of the Trench" of Sura al-Buruj.

## The story
Judaism entered Yemen through the convert Tubba' As'ad Abu Karib, who had adopted it at Yathrib and brought two rabbis to Yemen; when his son Yusuf Dhu Nuwas ruled he attacked the Christians of [[place/najran|Najran]] and called on them to adopt Judaism {cite:tafhim-al-quran}.

When they refused he dug the trench for them and burned them with fire, making no distinction between man and woman, small child and aged elder; the dead are said to have numbered between twenty and forty thousand, in October of 523 CE {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

The Qur'an recounted part of this story in Sura al-Buruj: "slain were the People of the Trench" ([[passage/quran-85-4|al-Buruj 4]] and what follows) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## Aftermath
This stirred militant Christianity to conquest and expansion under the Roman emperors over the Arabs' lands; they incited the Abyssinians and prepared the fleet for them, who occupied Yemen in 525 CE {cite:tafhim-al-quran}.

## For young visitors
Yemen's king wanted to force the Christians of Najran to leave their religion; when they refused he burned them in a trench of fire — the story the Qur'an mentions.
