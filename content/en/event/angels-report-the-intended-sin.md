## The story
From the hadith of Ibn Abbas in the Forty of al-Nawawi with Ibn Rajab's commentary marfu' from his Lord: Allah wrote down good deeds and bad deeds then made it clear — whoever intends a good deed and does not do it, Allah writes it a complete good deed; if he intends it and does it, Allah writes it ten to seven hundred fold to many multiples; whoever intends an evil deed and does not do it, Allah writes it a complete good deed; if he intends it and does it, Allah writes it an evil deed or crime; and the angels say: My Lord, that is Your servant who intends an evil deed — You know him best {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam} [[passage/arbaun-37|Forty 37]].

## The meaning
In this hadith qudsi the angels witness the servant's intention before he acts, and Allah honours whoever abandons the evil deed at the mere intention by writing a complete good deed {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.
