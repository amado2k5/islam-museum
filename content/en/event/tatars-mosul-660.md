## Summary
In early Ramadan 660 AH the Tatars took [[place/mosul|Mosul]] by trickery after a siege of several months. They reassured the people, broke down the wall, and then gave the city to the sword for nine days. They spared its ruler, [[person/al-salih-ismail-ibn-lulu|al-Malik al-Salih Ismaʿil]], son of [[person/badr-al-din-lulu|Badr al-Din Lulu]], for a few days, then killed him, and killed his son ʿAlaʾ al-Din, the king. With this the house of Lulu ended in Mosul after about forty years {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.308}.
