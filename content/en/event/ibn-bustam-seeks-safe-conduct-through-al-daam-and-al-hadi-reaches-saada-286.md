## Overview
The heading in the text is "the account of Ibn Bustam's going to al-Da'am". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] had fled once he was sure that [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] was leaving [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] for [[place/najran|Najran]], and went to [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] and asked him to seek safe-conduct for him from al-Hadi. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} The account is Zaydi, in the imam's followers' voice; "he overlooked his slip" is its narrator's wording.

## The safe-conduct
Al-Da'am met al-Hadi, took the matter upon himself and asked safe-conduct for Ibn Bustam; al-Hadi ila al-Haqq gave him safe-conduct, overlooked his slip, and Ibn Bustam travelled with him and mixed in his force. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} The [[event/al-hadi-letter-from-najran-and-the-meeting-with-al-daam-at-iyan-286|letter that came from Najran]] gives the mischief ascribed to Ibn Bustam, and the text does not go back to it here.

## Arrival at Sa'da
Al-Hadi reached [[place/saada|Sa'da]] on Wednesday, thirteen days gone of Jumada II, with the notables of Hamdan, Muhammad ibn al-Da'am and his horse. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} He stayed some days at Sa'da, then gathered Khawlan and set out with them to Najran together with the Hamdan forces he had with him; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.129} his leaving on Saturday, seventeen days gone, and reaching Najran on the Tuesday, are where the next pass through the book begins.

## Notes
The book's "Wednesday, thirteen days gone" is a day later than the tabular Tuesday for 13 Jumada II, so the book runs a day ahead of the tabular calendar as elsewhere; the date is taken to the month and derived by conversion. Nothing on this page concerns a penalty for Ibn Bustam, and none is stated here.
