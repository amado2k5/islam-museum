## Overview
When [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] became caliph, a group from [[place/samarqand|Samarqand]] came to him and complained that Qutayba had entered their city and settled the Muslims in it by treachery. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The ruling
Umar wrote to his governor to appoint a judge for them to examine what they had mentioned; if he decided that the Muslims should leave, they would leave. He appointed [[person/jumay-ibn-hadir|Jumay ibn Hadir al-Bahili]], who ruled that the Muslims should leave, on the basis that they would repudiate them on equal terms. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The outcome
The people of Samarqand disliked war and acknowledged the Muslims, and they stayed among them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
