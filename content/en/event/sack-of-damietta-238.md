## Overview
In 238 AH, as al-Suyuti relates, the Byzantines raided Damietta and plundered and burned it, and took six hundred women captive, then left quickly by sea. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.540}

The caliph at the time was [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]]. The date and the numbers rest on a single source and need an additional source.
