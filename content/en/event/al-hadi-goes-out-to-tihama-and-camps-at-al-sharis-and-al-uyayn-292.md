## Overview
"Al-Hadi went out to Tihama with a large force of Banu al-Harith and Khawlan, and halted at a place called al-Sharis, and many men of Hujur came to him" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}. Then he "advanced to a place called al-Uyayn and halted there and stayed some days" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}.

## Detail
While he stayed, a messenger came from the Hakami with letters "asking him to come to Tartar" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}. The editor vocalises the two names al-Sharis and al-Uyayn; the first OCR read them "al-Shurays" and "al-Muyayn" and the reading was corrected on the page image. The book does not locate either place and gives no year or month for this departure, so the museum places it after the Hakami matter of p. 295 ([[event/the-hakami-letters-and-the-oaths-and-abu-al-qasim-sent-to-khaywan-292|the letters, the oaths and Abu al-Qasim sent to Khaywan]]) and dates it to 292 AH for ordering only; it may belong to the end of that year or the next. The Hakami is given here without a name; p. 295 names two Hakamis, [[person/ibrahim-ibn-ali-al-hakami|Ibrahim ibn Ali al-Hakami]] and [[person/al-ghatrif-al-hakami|al-Ghatrif al-Hakami]], and the text does not settle which is meant. It is followed by [[event/al-hadi-at-tartar-returns-what-the-hakami-had-taken-from-the-people-292|the march to Tartar]]. The account is in a Zaydi book in the voice of the imam's followers; its descriptions and its judgments on opponents are the narrator's or the poet's, and the museum adopts none of them and gives no verdict.
