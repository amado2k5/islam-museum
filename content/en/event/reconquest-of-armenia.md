## Overview
In al-Baladhuri's account, during the strife of Ibn al-Zubayr Armenia rebelled, and its free men and their followers allied. When [[person/muhammad-ibn-marwan|Muhammad ibn Marwan]] was appointed over Armenia by his brother Abd al-Malik, he fought them and defeated them, killed and took captives, and took control of the land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The churches
He promised those among them who remained that he would show them honour; they gathered for this in churches in the district of Khilat. He shut them in and set guards at their doors. Umm Yazid, daughter of Usayd, from al-Sisjan, was taken captive; she was the daughter of its patrician. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governors before him
Al-Baladhuri lists the governors of Armenia: Uthman appointed al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba over Azerbaijan and Armenia, then removed him and appointed al-Qasim ibn Rabi'a al-Thaqafi; the report differs on who governed after him. Al-Ash'ath ibn Qays was appointed by Ali ibn Abi Talib. Then Mu'awiya appointed Abd Allah ibn Hatim al-Bahili, who died there, and his brother [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-hatim-al-bahili|Abd al-Aziz ibn Hatim]] took over. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Abd al-Aziz built Dabil and fortified it, built al-Nashwa, repaired Bardha'a, and renewed al-Baylaqan. It is said that the one who renewed Bardha'a was Muhammad ibn Marwan; al-Waqidi says that Abd al-Malik built it through Hatim al-Bahili or his son. Abd al-Malik appointed Uthman ibn al-Walid ibn Uqba over Armenia. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The place is [[place/armenia|Armenia]]. The Gregorian date is derived and the report needs an additional source.
