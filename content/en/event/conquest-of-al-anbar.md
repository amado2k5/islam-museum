## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] marched to [[place/al-anbar|al-Anbar]], and its people fortified themselves, and then made peace with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.344}

## The raid on the market of Baghdadh
A man guided him to the market of Baghdadh, the old market by the horn of the Sirat. [[person/al-muthanna-ibn-haritha|al-Muthanna ibn Haritha]] was sent and raided it, and the Muslims took the gold and silver and light goods. Then they spent the night at al-Silhayn and came to al-Anbar. al-Baladhuri says that some relate that Khalid himself led the raid, and that this is not established. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.344}

## The treaty
They besieged the people of al-Anbar and burned its outskirts, and they made peace with Khalid. al-Anbar was so called because the granaries of the Persians were there. al-Baladhuri relates from al-Sha'bi that its people had a covenant and a pact. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.344}

The elders of al-Anbar told al-Baladhuri that they were made peace with in the caliphate of Umar for four hundred thousand dirhams and a thousand Qatwani cloaks each year, and that [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]] undertook the treaty. It is also said that it was for eighty thousand, and al-Baladhuri concludes: "God knows best." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.344}
