## Summary
Al-Muraja'at: 112 letters attributed to Abd al-Husayn Sharaf al-Din al-Musawi and Salim al-Bishri, Shaykh of al-Azhar, published 1936 on the succession, the Imamate and hadith authority. {cite:al-islam-murajaat}

## The attribution question
The book appeared decades after al-Bishri's death with no external manuscript to secure it; many Sunnis deny its authenticity and hold it a one-sided composition. The museum describes this dispute without deciding it, presenting the work as the most famous modern Shia–Sunni debate text.
