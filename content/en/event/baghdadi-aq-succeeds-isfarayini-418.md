## Summary
The master Abu Ishaq al-Isfarayini died at Nishapur on the day of Ashura in 418, then was carried to his home town Isfarayin and buried at his well-known shrine {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 210}; his pupil Abd al-Qahir al-Baghdadi was seated in his place to dictate in the mosque and dictated for years, and the imams thronged to read under him {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba pp. 634-635} {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 238}.
