## Summary
Late in life at Nishapur al-Tha'alibi composed the Tatimmat al-Yatima, a supplement to his Yatimat al-Dahr following the same geographical arrangement, gathering the writers who had risen to prominence in his own adult lifetime, and giving generous publicity to his students — foremost al-Bakharzi, author of the Dumyat al-Qasr {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 451}.
