## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] gathered the Banu Marwan when he succeeded, and announced the return of Fadak to its state in the time of the Prophet. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.378}

## The report
Umar said, according to the book, that the Prophet had Fadak, from which he spent and supported the young of the Banu Hashim and married off their unmarried women. Fatima asked for it and he refused. It remained so in the lifetime of Abu Bakr and then Umar; then Marwan granted it as a fief, and then it came to Umar. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.378}

He then said: "I have seen a matter that the Messenger of God withheld from Fatima; it is not a right for me," and he called them to witness that he returned it to what it had been. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.378}

## The grievances
He began with his relatives and the people of his house and took what was in their hands, and this was called "grievances". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.378}

The date is approximate, at the start of his caliphate in 99 AH, and derived.
