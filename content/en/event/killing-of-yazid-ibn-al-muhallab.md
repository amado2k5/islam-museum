## Overview
In Safar 102 AH [[person/yazid-ibn-al-muhallab|Yazid ibn al-Muhallab]] was killed, after al-Abbas ibn al-Walid and Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik came to fight him on the orders of Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.590}

## Al-Suyuti's account
Al-Suyuti says that this happened at al-Aqr, a place near Karbala. He quotes al-Kalbi as saying that the Umayyads sacrificed religion on the day of Karbala and generosity on the day of al-Aqr. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 399-400}
