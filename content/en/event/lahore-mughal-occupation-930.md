## Overview
Daulat Khan Lodi, the sultan's kinsman who governed the Punjab, encouraged Babur's designs on Hindustan, leading to the occupation of Lahore by the Mughals in 930/1524 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 38}.
