## Summary
Yaqut went to Damascus in 613/1216 and sat in one of its markets, where he debated a man zealous for a Companion; the exchange ended in his speaking of the Companion in an impermissible way {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.

## The riot and the flight
The people of Damascus rose against him and nearly killed him; the affair reached the governor al-Mu'tamad al-Mawsili, who sought him in vain. Yaqut fled Damascus and reached Aleppo fearful and watchful; after a few months' refuge he continued his flight towards Khurasan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}. He had read Kharijite books earlier, and a strong streak of them had lodged in his mind {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.
