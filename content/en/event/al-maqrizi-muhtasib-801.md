## Overview
In 801 al-Maqrizi took up the office of muhtasib (market inspector) of Cairo for the first time, a term lasting only a few months; he held it again in 802 and 807 — Ibn al-Imad sums it up as 'muhtasib of Cairo more than once'. Alongside it he wrote official tawqi' endorsements, sat as deputy judge, preached in the Mosque of Amr and the Madrasa of al-Hasan, served as imam and administrator of the al-Hakim mosque, and taught hadith at the Mu'ayyadiyya {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 213} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 254-255}.
