## Summary
At the opening of 628 AH the people of all Azerbaijan submitted to the Tatars and carried wealth to them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 494}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that at the start of this year the people of Azerbaijan submitted to the Tatars and brought them riches; this followed the arrival of a Tatar party from Azerbaijan in the Erbil districts in Dhu al-Hijja, where they killed those they met on the road — the Aywai Turcomans, the Kurds, the Jawzaqan and others — entered the land of Erbil, plundered the villages and killed whomever they caught; Muzaffar al-Din, lord of Erbil, came out with his armies reinforced by Mosul's troops and marched at them, but on hearing the Tatars had turned back to Azerbaijan he stayed in his own land, while they reached al-Karkhini, Daquq and other places and returned unharmed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 494} Ibn al-Athir closes the year's account by noting that no confirmed news of Jalal al-Din had reached them — whether he had been killed, or lay hidden for fear of the Tatars, or had left the lands for elsewhere. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 494}
