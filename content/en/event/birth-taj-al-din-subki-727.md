## Overview
Abd al-Wahhab ibn Ali ibn Abd al-Kafi ibn Ali ibn Tamam — Taj al-Din Abu al-Nasr al-Subki al-Ansari al-Khazraji al-Shafi'i — was born in Cairo in 727, 728 or 729 AH (c. 1327–1329 CE); al-Dhahabi's Mu'jam al-Saghir gives 728 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 745}.
