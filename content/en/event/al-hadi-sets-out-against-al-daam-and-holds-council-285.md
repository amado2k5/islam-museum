## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] heard that [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] had entered [[place/athafit|Athafit]] and left his place for a place of Banu Rabi'a, whose name is printed in a form the manuscript reading of which is uncertain (the editor's text reads "Mushawwat"), so it is not entered as a place. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} He spent the night there, the night of Saturday, the night of the Fitr. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The account is Zaydi and in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum gives it as the narrators tell it, without a verdict.

## The gathering and the counsel
In the morning al-Hadi sent criers among the people, and they gathered to him on Monday, three days into Shawwal 285 AH; they took counsel on the war and agreed together on war against al-Da'am. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.99} The Banu Rabi'a here are of Hamdan and have no link to Rabi'a ibn Nizar; they appear again in the line of battle. See [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan]].

## The date
The weekday matches the tabular calendar: 3 Shawwal 285 AH falls on a Monday and corresponds to 23 October 898 in the Julian calendar, derived. In earlier batches the book's weekdays ran one day before the tabular ones; on these pages they agree. The gathering was followed by the march on Athafit. See [[event/first-day-of-the-battle-of-athafit-285]].
