## Overview
Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari died at Baghdad in 324/935-6, and a group of Hanbalis and scholars joined in finding fault with him {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 15/86} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 743} {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim pp. 648-649}.

## His last testament
Al-Bayhaqi reports through Zahir ibn Ahmad al-Sarakhsi: when al-Ash'ari's end drew near at his house in Baghdad he summoned me and said, 'Bear witness that I declare no one of the people of the qibla an unbeliever, for all point to one object of worship; all this is but a difference of wording' {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 15/86}.
