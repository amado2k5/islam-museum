## Summary
A Hebrew manuscript from Meknes documenting a direct debate between Rabbi Berdugo and an unnamed Sunni scholar in the late eighteenth century. {cite:tandfonline-berdugo-meknes}

## The exchanges
Abrogation of the earlier law, the immutability of the Torah, and history as a sign of divine favour — axes that remain those of Muslim–Jewish disputation today.
