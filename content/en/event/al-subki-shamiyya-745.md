## Overview
On Sunday 21 Dhu al-Hijja 745 chief qadi Taqi al-Din al-Subki gave his first lesson at the Shamiyya al-Barraniyya madrasa in Damascus in succession to Shams al-Din ibn al-Naqib, before an assembly of qadis, notables, amirs and scholars {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 745 p. 226}.
