## Overview
In Safar 578 AH the Muslims took al-Shaqif, known as Habs Jaldak, which is in the districts of Tiberias. Ibn al-Athir relates that when the Franks learned of Salah al-Din's march from Egypt to Syria, they gathered at al-Karak, and their lands were emptied on the side of Syria. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.110}

## The conquest
[[person/izz-al-din-farrukhshah|Izz al-Din Farrukhshah]] gathered the armies of Syria and raided the lands of the Franks, plundered Dabburiyya and its surroundings, and took al-Shaqif. The Muslims had suffered great harm from it, so they rejoiced at it. He sent to Salah al-Din with the good news. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.111}
