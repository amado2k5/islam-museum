## Overview
Al-Hadi checked on the protected people, said that the rule applies to them, and warned that anyone who harmed them would answer for it. One or two men and a woman or two embraced Islam because of his gentleness and justice.

## Detail
He checked on the protected people and said that the rule applies to them, and that the Messenger had enjoined concern for them. He told them: whoever harms you, tell me of him, and whoever looks upon your sanctuary or attacks you, I will bring upon him what came upon those who broke the covenant of God and of His messenger. One man or two, and a woman or two, kept coming to Islam because of the gentleness and justice they saw in him.
