## Overview
al-Tabari reports that [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]] removed [[person/tariq-ibn-ziyad|Tariq ibn Ziyad]] from al-Andalus in 93 AH and sent him to Toledo. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.481}

## al-Baladhuri's account
al-Baladhuri relates that Musa wrote a harsh letter to Tariq for having deceived the Muslims, and ordered him not to go beyond Cordoba. Musa then marched to Cordoba, and Tariq placated him and he was content with him, and Tariq conquered [[place/tulaytula|Toledo]]. There he found a great table, which Musa had sent as a gift to [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] in Damascus in 96 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

al-Maqqari reports from some history books that many treasures were found in Toledo, among them the table of Solomon. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.325}
