## Overview
[[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] came to [[place/ayn-al-tamr|Ayn al-Tamr]] and besieged its fortress, which held a large garrison of Persians. Al-Baladhuri relates this and dates it to Khalid's march in 12 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege
The people of the fortress came out and fought, then kept to their fortress. Khalid besieged them until they asked for safe conduct, and he refused. He took the fortress by force, killed and took captives, and found in a church there a group he took captive. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The captives of the church
Al-Baladhuri names among these captives [[person/humran-ibn-aban|Humran ibn Aban]], client of Uthman; [[person/sirin-father-of-ibn-sirin|Sirin]], father of Muhammad ibn Sirin; [[person/yasar-mawla-qays|Yasar]], grandfather of Muhammad ibn Ishaq; and Nusayr, father of Musa ibn Nusayr. These became clients, and this is the origin of the report about the [[concept/mawali|clients]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Differing accounts
Al-Baladhuri relates from Ibn al-Kalbi that Abu Farwa and Nusayr were Arabs of the Bali, taken captive from Syria in the days of Abu Bakr. He gives another account that Khalid made peace with the people of the fortress, and that the captives were found in a church. From al-Sha'bi, Khalid made peace with the people of al-Hira and Ayn al-Tamr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The dead
Khalid was fought by [[person/hilal-ibn-uqqa-al-namiri|Hilal ibn Uqqa al-Namiri]], who was victorious over him, killed him and crucified him. [[person/umayr-ibn-riab|Umayr ibn Ri'ab]] was martyred by an arrow that struck him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## For Sprouts
Khalid ibn al-Walid besieged the fortress of Ayn al-Tamr on his march of 12 AH and took it by storm; in its church was a group who became the mawali remembered in history.

## For Explorers
Khalid ibn al-Walid came to Ayn al-Tamr and besieged its fortress, which held a large Persian garrison; al-Baladhuri tells it, dating it to Khalid's march of 12 AH. The people of the fort came out fighting then held to it; Khalid besieged them until they asked for quarter, he refused, and took the fort by storm, taking captives. In a church there he found a group he took captive, among them Humran ibn Aban, later Uthman's freedman; Sirin, father of Muhammad ibn Sirin; and Yasar, grandfather of Ibn Ishaq — these became mawali, which is the root of the account on the mawali. Ibn al-Kalbi says Abu Furwa and Nusayr were Arabs of Bali taken in Abu Bakr's days from Syria, and another saying has Khalid settling terms with the fort's people with the captives found in a church.
