## Overview
Al-Hadi sent his servant Abu al-Mushira with some of his servants and men of Shakir. They lay in ambush on Mount Dukhna overlooking Suhan and killed three men of Banu al-Harith, among them Abu al-Surram ibn Ali. They brought the heads to al-Hadi, who ordered them crucified at the gate of al-Darb.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi sent a servant of his called Abu al-Mushira, with a group of his servants and men of Shakir. They lay in ambush on a mountain called Dukhna that overlooks Suhan and fell on a group of Banu al-Harith, killing three men, among them Abu al-Surram ibn Ali, whom a man of Shakir named Abu al-Mahir ran down and killed, and two men of a clan whose name is withheld in this record, called Haws and Amir. They brought the heads to al-Hadi, who on their arrival ordered them crucified at the gate of al-Darb. The book reports the order as al-Hadi's act and does not comment on it.
