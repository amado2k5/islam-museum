## Overview
In 736 AH a dispute arose between the caliph [[person/al-mustakfi-billah|al-Mustakfi]] and the sultan [[person/al-nasir-muhammad-ibn-qalawun|al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun]]. The caliph was seized and imprisoned in the tower and forbidden to meet people. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.736,739}

## Exile to Qus
In Dhu al-Hijja 737 AH he was exiled to Qus with his children and family, nearly a hundred people, and provision was made for them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.736,739}

## The cause of the dispute
Al-Suyuti attributes the quarrel to the slander carried by the caliph's nephew Ibrahim. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.736,739}

Al-Mustakfi remained in Qus until he died in Sha'ban 740 AH and was buried there. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.736,739}
