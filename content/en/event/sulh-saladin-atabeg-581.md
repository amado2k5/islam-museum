## Summary
A peace was agreed between Salah al-Din and Atabeg Izz al-Din Mas'ud ibn Mawdud, lord of Mosul. Each ruler would keep his own city, and each would support the other against anyone who attacked the lands of either. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 134}
