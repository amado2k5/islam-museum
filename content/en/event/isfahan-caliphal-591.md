## Overview
In 591 AH the army of the caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah entered Isfahan, after the head of the Shafi'is there offered to surrender the city. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.238}

## The account
The caliph equipped an army under Sayf al-Din Toghril for Isfahan. There were troops of the Khwarazm-shah in it with his son, and the people disliked them. [[person/sadr-al-din-al-khujandi|Sadr al-Din al-Khujandi]], head of the Shafi'is, wrote to the court in Baghdad offering the surrender of the city. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.238}

## Entry
The court's army came to the outskirts of the city, and the Khwarazmians left for Khurasan; some of their rearguard were caught and taken. The caliph's army entered Isfahan and took it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.239}
