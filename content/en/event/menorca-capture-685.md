## Overview
In 685 AH the king of Barcelona landed on the island of Minorca with his fleet and about twenty thousand fighters, and the island fell into his hands. This is what Ibn Khaldun relates in "al-Ibar". {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## How they landed
Ibn Khaldun says that they passed by the island as if they were travelling merchants, and asked to land to draw water. Its ruler, Abu Umar ibn Hakam, allowed them. When they had landed they declared war. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The fighting and surrender
The army pressed for three days, and the Muslims inflicted heavy losses on them in the thousands, while the king of Barcelona stayed back from the advance. On the third day he advanced with the army, the Muslims were routed and they were shut up in the fortress. They granted Ibn Hakam safe conduct for his family and retinue, and they went out to Ceuta, and the rest surrendered to the enemy's judgement. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
