## Overview
The accounts differ on the time when Basra was founded as a garrison city. Al-Tabari says that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] sent [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan]] there in 14 AH according to al-Mada'ini, and that Sayf mentioned it was founded in the spring of 16 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.590}

## Al-Tabari's account
Al-Tabari has a section entitled "The Building of Basra", running from page 590 to 597. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.630}

## Al-Baladhuri's account
Al-Baladhuri says that Basra was founded as a garrison between the day of al-Nukayla and the day of al-Qadisiyya, and that Utba founded it and then asked leave to go on pilgrimage, leaving al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba in charge. [[person/suwayd-ibn-qutba|Suwayd ibn Qutba]] had been first in the raiding, and reinforcements came to him from [[person/harthama-ibn-arfaja|Harthama ibn Arfaja]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Umar appointed Abu Musa governor of Basra and summoned al-Mughira to Medina after he had been accused, and then sent him back. In another account al-Mughira only became governor of Basra after he came from al-Qadisiyya. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The date
This is disputed: 14 AH according to al-Mada'ini, and the spring of 16 AH according to Sayf. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.590}

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that Umar sent Utba ibn Ghazwan with two thousand men, who took al-Ubulla by force, the port for Oman, al-Bahrayn, Fars, India and China, and that Nafi' ibn al-Harith was the first to mark out a plot at Basra. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 116} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 117}
