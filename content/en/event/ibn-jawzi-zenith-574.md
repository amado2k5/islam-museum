## Overview
In 574/1178–9 Ibn al-Jawzi stood at the zenith of his career: five madrasas under his direction, more than 150 works written, and a dais the caliph al-Mustadi' had erected for him in the Palace mosque in 570 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 783-784}.
