## Overview
In 253 AH Muflih, who led the vanguard of Musa ibn Baga, attacked Abd al-Aziz ibn Abi Dulaf outside Hamadhan. He defeated him, killed him and took captives, according to al-Tabari. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.373}
