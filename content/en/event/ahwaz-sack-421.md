## Overview
Jalal al-Dawla entered Ahwaz and let his soldiers loose on it for sixteen days; his letter reached Baghdad on the eleventh of Rabi' II 421 describing what was taken — the letter claimed booty beyond five thousand thousand dinars and two thousand slave-girls captured — along with what al-Malik al-Muntasir's own account says of itself and its numbers {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.388-389}.
