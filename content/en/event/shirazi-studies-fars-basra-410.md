## Summary
From 410/1019 to 415/1024 Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi — who had begun fiqh in his native Firuzabad — pursued his legal education at Shiraz and then at Basra under various Shafi'i masters, each of whom he later gave a biographical entry in his Tabaqat al-fuqaha {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 494}.
