## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-ashath-al-khuzai|Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath al-Khuza'i]] entered Ifriqiya as governor at the end of the caliphate of Abu al-Abbas, with seventy thousand men, or it is said forty thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.325}

## His governorship and the revolt of the troops
He governed Ifriqiya for four years and repaired the city of [[place/qayrawan|al-Qayrawan]]. Then the troops of the province and others rose against him. Al-Baladhuri heard from informants that he fought them for forty days while he was in his palace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.325}

## Victory and his removal
Those who were loyal to him, who had come with him from Khurasan and elsewhere, gathered to him, and he defeated those who had fought him. He reviewed them by name, and killed whoever was called Mu'awiya, Sufyan, Marwan or a name matching the names of the Umayyads, and spared those with other names. Then [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] removed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.325}

The date is derived in the data: between about 754 and 758 CE.
