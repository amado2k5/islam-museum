## Overview
The women of Abu Ja'far's household were taken to the house of Muhammad ibn Sanjab, who looked after them well. He hid a ten-year-old son of Abu Ja'far, Musa, from Ibn Humayd, who had sent for the boy to kill him, and swore that he did not know where the boy was.

## Detail
The narrator says the women of the household were taken to the house of Muhammad ibn Sanjab, who took good care of them and of those with them. Ibn Sanjab gathered the boys of Banu al-Harith, among whom was a son of Abu Ja'far named Musa, ten years old. Ibn Humayd sent for the boy to be brought to him so that he could be killed. Ibn Sanjab hid him and swore that the boy was not with him and that he did not know where he was. The name of Ibn Sanjab varies in the printed text, and the reading here follows the main text.
