## Summary
In 424 AH Baghdad was in turmoil under the rule of the bandits, who took people's money in broad daylight, killed the police chief's men, and took goods worth 10,000 dinars from one merchant {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 227}.

## Al-Barjami
People broke into houses, which they had thought no one could do, out of fear of their chief al-Barjami. He was known never to touch a woman and never to let anyone take anything from one {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 227}.

## Worsening trouble
In 426 AH the bandits' harm grew. They took money openly, showed immorality and openly broke the fast in Ramadan, until they effectively controlled Baghdad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 230}.
