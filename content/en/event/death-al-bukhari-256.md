## Summary
Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Bukhari died at Khartank on the festival night, the first night of Shawwal 256, having reached sixty-two years less thirteen nights; he was in a house alone and they found him dead when morning came, and he was buried at Khartank {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 14}. He had been heard at night, after finishing the night prayer, saying in his supplication: O God, the earth has grown narrow for me despite its breadth, so take me to You — and before the month was out God took him {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 14}.
