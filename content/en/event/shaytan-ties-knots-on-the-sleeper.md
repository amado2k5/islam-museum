## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet reported the shaytan ties three knots on the crown of one who sleeps, striking at each knot "your night is long, sleep"; if he wakes and remembers God a knot is loosed, if he performs ablution a knot is loosed, if he prays a knot is loosed, and he rises lively and good-natured {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-486|Muwatta 486]].

## The meaning
This hadith is among the best-known texts of the shaytan's scheming in sleep; the shaytan besets the sleeper with heedlessness until he rises lazy, and remembrance, ablution and prayer undo his knots {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}. The Sunan rely on it for the fiqh of the pre-dawn meal and night prayer {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
