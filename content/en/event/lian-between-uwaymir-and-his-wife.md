## Summary
The mutual oath of cursing (li'an) occurred between [[person/uwaymir-al-ajlani|Uwaymir al-Ajlani]] and his wife after the Prophet's return from Tabuk in year 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## The account
Uwaymir accused his wife, and the two swore the mutual oaths before the Prophet by the procedure the Qur'an legislated in Sura al-Nur; then he separated them — among the first applications of the li'an ruling {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## For young visitors
A man accused his wife before the Prophet, who conducted between them the li'an procedure the Qur'an legislated, then separated them without wronging either.
