## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates the building of the fortress of [[place/masisa|al-Masisa]] in 84 AH on its old foundation, and its completion in 85 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.225-227}

## The building
On the authority of al-Waqidi and others: [[person/abdullah-ibn-abd-al-malik|Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]] raided in the summer campaign, entered by the route of Antioch, and came to al-Masisa. He built its fortress and placed in it a garrison of soldiers, among them three hundred men whom he had chosen, and the Muslims had not settled it before. He built a mosque on top of the hill of the fortress. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.225-227}

It is said by Abu al-Khattab al-Azdi that the first to build its fortress in Islam was [[person/abd-al-malik-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan]], through the hand of his son Abd Allah. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.225-227}

## Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz
[[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] wanted to demolish al-Masisa and the forts between it and Antioch, and said that he disliked the Byzantines besieging its people. The people informed him that it had been built to be a barrier before Antioch. He refrained from demolishing it, and built a congregational mosque for them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.225-227}

al-Masisa is one of the chief fortresses of the Syrian [[concept/thughur|frontier]]. The rest of the account is cut out of the museum's data.
