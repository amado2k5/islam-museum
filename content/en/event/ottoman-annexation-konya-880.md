## Summary
After being occupied by Bayezid Yildirim, [[place/konya|Konya]] was definitively annexed to the Ottoman state in 880/1475 together with the remainder of the Karamanid state. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 288}

## Continuity of institutions
Ottoman documentary archives show that its waqfs and Seljuk and Karamanid institutions were in general maintained; but within that vast empire, far removed from all the important areas, the city could no longer be more than a provincial centre. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 288}
