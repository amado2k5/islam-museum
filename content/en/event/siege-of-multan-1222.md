## Overview
The narrative histories record a Mongol siege of Multan in 1222, as Mongol columns pushed to the Indus marches in pursuit of Jalal al-Din Khwarazm-Shah; the city held for its master Nasir al-Din Qabacha and was not taken.

## Qabacha's Multan
Nasir al-Din Qabacha, a mamluk of Shihab al-Din al-Ghuri, had settled over Multan, Uch, Lahore and Daybul down to the seacoast; when his master was assassinated in 603/1206 he stood as an independent sultan in the region {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## The Khwarazmian shock
Jalal al-Din reached the region fleeing the advancing Mongols, and his unsuccessful encounter with Qabacha in 621/1224 weakened the sultan's position; Iltutmish then defeated him in 625/1228 and Multan entered the Delhi Sultanate {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
