## Overview
In 63 AH the people of Medina deposed [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Mu'awiya]] and rose against him; he sent a large army against them, and the Battle of al-Harra took place at the gate of Taba. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.344-346}

## The cause of the deposition
Al-Suyuti relates from [[person/abdullah-ibn-hanzala|Abd Allah ibn Hanzala]] that they did not rise against Yazid until they feared stones would fall on them from heaven, because of what they saw of his excess in sins. This is the description of the people of Medina as al-Suyuti relates it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.344-346}

## The battle
Yazid ordered his army to fight them, then to march on Mecca to fight [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Ibn al-Zubayr]]. Al-Suyuti relates from al-Hasan that hardly anyone escaped from them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.344-346}

Al-Suyuti says that a great many of the Companions and others were killed, Medina was plundered, and the number of those killed from Quraysh and the Ansar was three hundred and six men. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.344-346}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
According to al-Mas'udi, a number of the Banu Hashim and the rest of Quraysh and the Ansar were killed. He names among them Abd Allah ibn Ja'far, al-Fadl ibn al-Abbas ibn Rabi'a and Hamza ibn Abd Allah ibn Nawfal. He mentions some ninety of Quraysh and as many of the Ansar, and four thousand of those whom the count reached. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.67-68}

He says that the people swore allegiance that they were slaves of Yazid, except Ali ibn al-Husayn and Ali ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Abbas. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.67-68}

The two numbers differ between the sources, and the museum does not favour between them. The Shi'i report and other views are not added here and need an additional source.
