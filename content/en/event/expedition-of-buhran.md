## Summary
The expedition of Buhran was a large fighting patrol of three hundred fighters the Prophet led in Rabi' al-Akhir 3 AH (October 624 CE) to a land called [[place/buhran|Buhran]] — a mining site in the Hijaz in the district of al-Fur' between Mecca and Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 245}

## The aim and the march
The Prophet left [[person/ibn-umm-maktum|Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum]] in charge of [[place/medina|Medina]] and went out meaning to watch Quraysh and Banu Sulaym, secure the roads and prevent any mustering that threatened Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 245} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/91}

The Prophet stayed at Buhran through the months of Rabi' al-Akhir and Jumada al-Ula of the third year of the Hijra, met neither ambush nor battle, and brought the army back safely to Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 245} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 2/50}
