## Overview
One of Ibn Abi Abbad's companions slipped away as the men entered Shibam, climbed the mountain and told [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] they had come in; al-Hadi ordered [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] and [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-daam|Muhammad ibn al-Da'am]] to go down and fight them, and they went down with a body of the army and split to meet the men by two routes {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218}. When the men saw Abu al-Atahiya and Muhammad ibn al-Da'am they fled out of Shibam and were followed, and many of them were killed, while the rest clung to the mountain tops {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.218} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. The two returned to Shibam and spent the night there, and al-Hadi spent it on the mountain of [[place/bayt-dhukhar|Bayt Dhukhar]]; in the morning he came down and buried Ibn Abi Abbad {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. The narrator says it reached him from Abu al-Atahiya that al-Hadi said as he buried him: I wish I had been with you and died a martyr; may God give me what He gave you {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}. Abu al-Atahiya then went up the mountain and stayed, and al-Hadi stayed at Shibam {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.219}.

## Detail
The sentence on the report reads, with the dashes of the print, as al-Hadi being the speaker and Abu al-Atahiya the source of the report; the museum takes it so from the context. The words are al-Hadi's as the narrator received them, not a judgment by the museum on Ibn Abi Abbad's state. The burial agrees with [[event/revolt-at-shibam-ibn-abbad-killed-288|the account of the added pages]], that al-Hadi came down and buried him, and there is no conflict between the two. No numbers are given for the dead. It follows [[event/the-qudumis-enter-shibam-break-the-prison-and-ibn-abi-abbad-is-killed-288]].
