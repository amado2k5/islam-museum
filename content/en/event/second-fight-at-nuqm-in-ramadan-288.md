## Overview
On Wednesday, for a day of Ramadan, the number before the word day being missing in both the printed text and the page image, they gathered their armies from every side and came to their position of the first fight, and a large force entered the village by the Darb al-Qutay' {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] sent his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]] with a part of the army to meet the men, sent a party that came out on them from the Darb al-Jabbana, and went out himself behind Abu al-Qasim by the Darb al-Qutay', finding him locked in battle with the men {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}. He charged and drove them out of the village until he forced them to the mountain of [[place/nuqm-sanaa-yemen|Nuqm]]; the fighting held between them and him until near night, then, the narrator says, God gave him the victory, he routed them and killed a number, and each side returned to its camp {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.229}.

## Detail
The number of the day of Ramadan is missing at the printed place, so the museum gives no day. Abu al-Qasim here is al-Hadi's son, called on other pages Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya, and is taken to be Muhammad al-Murtada, which the museum does not assert. The account goes with [[event/battle-of-nuqm-shaban-288]].
