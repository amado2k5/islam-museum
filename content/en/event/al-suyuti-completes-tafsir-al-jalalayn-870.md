## Summary
[[person/al-suyuti|Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti]] completed [[person/jalal-al-din-al-mahalli|Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli]]'s commentary from the start of Surat al-Baqara to the end of Surat al-Isra', so that the two parts became one book, [[work/tafsir-al-jalalayn|Tafsir al-Jalalayn]]. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.4} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.369}

## The dates as al-Suyuti gives them
In the closing note of his completion al-Suyuti says he began it on Wednesday 1 Ramadan 870 AH, finished composing it on Sunday 10 Shawwal of the same year, and finished the fair copy on Wednesday 6 Safar 871. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.370} He likens the span to the appointed time of al-Kalim, Moses. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.369} The Gregorian dates in the data are tabular conversions and so approximate.
