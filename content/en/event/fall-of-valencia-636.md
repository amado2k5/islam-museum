## Overview
The enemy overcame Valencia by treaty on Tuesday the seventeenth of Safar 636 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464}

## The appeal
Ibn al-Abbar wrote to the sultan of Ifriqiya appealing with a poem. The sultan hastened to help its people, and loaded fleets with money, food and clothing, until the enemy overcame it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464}

Its people made a covenant with the Christian that he would give them their lives in 637 AH. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464}

The date of the event in the Gregorian calendar is converted. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.464}
