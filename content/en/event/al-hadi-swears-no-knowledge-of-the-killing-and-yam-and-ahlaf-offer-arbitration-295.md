## Overview
Al-Hadi gathered the people and swore that he had no knowledge of Ibn Bistam's killing. He said that had he wanted him dead he would have ordered him to come to him with a hundred men of Banu al-Harith so that he could strike off all their necks. Yam and Ahlaf offered to be judged before the Imam, pointing out that Ibn Bistam had killed six of their men. Banu Rabi'a were reassured by his word.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi gathered the people and swore to them that he had known nothing of Ibn Bistam's killing. He said that had he wanted him dead he would have ordered him to come to him with a hundred men of Banu al-Harith so that he could strike off all their necks, and the people believed him. Yam and Ahlaf offered to be judged before him with Banu Rabi'a, pointing out that Ibn Bistam had killed six of their men. Banu al-Harith had gathered at Maynas and said that the killing was al-Hadi's doing. Al-Hadi then sent to Banu Rabi'a and swore to them, and they were reassured. Sulayman ibn al-Najm and Yazid ibn Ali ibn Jundub came to him, heard his words and accepted his excuse, then went back to their people. The book reports these words as al-Hadi's own.
