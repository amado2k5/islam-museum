## Overview
Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan appointed Umayya ibn Abd Allah ibn Khalid ibn Usayd over Khurasan. Umayya sent his son [[person/abdullah-ibn-umayya|Abd Allah ibn Umayya]] to Sistan and appointed him over it while he was in Kirman. [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] was the king who came after the first Rutbil, who had been killed, and he stood in awe of the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The first truce
Rutbil made peace with Abd Allah when he arrived at Bust, for a million, and he sent him gifts and slaves. Abd Allah refused to accept it and said: "If he fills this colonnade with gold for me, well; otherwise there is no peace between me and him." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The trap and the second truce
The narrator describes him as one who was deluded. Rutbil left the land open to him until he had gone deep into it, and then he seized the mountain passes and narrow defiles. Abd Allah asked that they let him go without taking anything from them, but they refused and said: "Rather, take three hundred thousand dirhams as peace, and write us a document that you will not raid our land while you are governor, nor burn or ruin it." He did so. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

When this reached Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, he dismissed him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
