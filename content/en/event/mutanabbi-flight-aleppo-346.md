## Overview
After nine years with Sayf al-Dawla, al-Mutanabbi's arrogance and fame had won him implacable enemies, led by the famous Abu Firas. When the amir tired of his poet's insolence and protection lapsed, Abu al-Tayyib no longer felt his life safe; he fled secretly from Aleppo with all he owned and took refuge in Damascus (c. 346/958, derived from the nine-year stay) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 770}.
