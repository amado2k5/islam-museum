## Overview
Under the heading "the story of Abu al-Atahiya" the book says he was a man of the kings and sultans of Yemen called Abd Allah ibn Bishr, with the kunya [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]], governor of [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]] and its districts. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} His vizier was a man called [[person/muhammad-ibn-abbad-al-tamimi|Muhammad ibn Abi Abbad]] of Tamim; both felt affection and desire for the truth and had written to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], desiring him and his justice and affirming his imamate and merit. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the museum attributes its sayings to the narrators and gives no verdict.

## The promise of horse
Abu al-Atahiya promised to supply him with horse and men to be with him and a strength against his enemy, and al-Hadi waited for this from him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} Al-Hadi then resolved to move from the al-Darb of Banu Rabi'a to a place of Banu Sarim which is also called al-Darb, in the last days of Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} The return of the loot, told just before, is in [[event/al-hadi-has-the-loot-returned-and-the-gathering-on-the-day-of-arafa-285]].

## Muhammad ibn Sulayman and Ismail ibn al-Muslim are sent
[[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] says al-Hadi ordered him to go to Sanaa to press Abu al-Atahiya for the horse, and he left with some days of Dhu al-Hijja remaining; al-Hadi moved and stayed at a place called "al-Bawn" and others, and the people scattered through the land because the soldiers feared al-Hadi for what they had heard of his enforcing the hadd penalties, taking the right and driving out falsehood and depravity, which was hard on them, so they did not want to come to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} The editor's footnote says those meant are the soldiers from Abu al-Atahiya's side. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} [[person/ismail-ibn-al-muslim-al-hamdani|Ismail ibn al-Muslim]] had gone to Abu al-Atahiya to ask for troops, returned with some of them and stayed at a place called [[place/raydah-yemen|Raydah]] waiting for the rest of the army. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110}

## The arrival of Jarrah ibn Bishr
The narrator left Sanaa with a day or two of Dhu al-Hijja remaining and reached al-Hadi at the al-Darb of Banu Sarim a day or two into Muharram 286 AH. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.110} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} Abu al-Atahiya ordered the soldiers to march to al-Hadi; some came and some turned back, and seeing their dislike of going to him he sent his brother [[person/jarrah-ibn-bishr|Jarrah]] with fifty horsemen, who came to the al-Darb of Banu Sarim in the last days of Muharram. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.111} See [[event/al-daam-weighs-peace-with-al-hadi-and-sets-conditions-286]].
