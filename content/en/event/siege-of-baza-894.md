## Overview
In 894 AH the lord of Castile came before Baza. [[person/al-zaghal|Al-Zaghal]], lord of Guadix, had sent all his troops and commanders and gathered the people of the relief from Guadix, Almeria, al-Munakib and the Alpujarras. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 525}

## Holding out
Great battles took place until the enemy retreated, and they could not prevent entry and exit in Rajab, Sha'ban and Ramadan. Then the siege was tightened and the cannon brought up to the walls, and the situation grew severe in Dhu al-Qa'da and Dhu al-Hijja, and food ran short. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 525}

## The stratagem
The people of the city displayed all kinds of food in the markets to show strength, when they were in the greatest weakness. Al-Maqqari quotes the phrase "war is deception". A great man of the Christians entered as a spy to see the condition of the people. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 525}

## The surrender
The Christians entered the fortress of Baza on Friday, the tenth of Muharram 895 AH, and expelled the Muslims from the city and settled them in the suburb, for fear of revolt. Then the lord of Castile moved to Almeria, and al-Zaghal came out to meet him and gave up the fortresses and castles. Only Granada and its villages remained, according to al-Maqqari. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 526}
