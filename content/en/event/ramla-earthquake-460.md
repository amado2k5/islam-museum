## Summary
In Jumada I of the year 460 AH an earthquake struck the land of Palestine and destroyed the town of Ramla. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.598}

## The scene
Some merchants wrote about this earthquake that the whole of Ramla sank into the ground. During the quake the sea receded so far that its bed was uncovered and people walked on it, and then it returned to its former state. One corner of the congregational mosque in Egypt was also altered. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.598}
