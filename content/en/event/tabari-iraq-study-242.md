## Overview
Al-Tabari went from Rayy to Baghdad before he was seventeen, hoping to study with Ahmad ibn Hanbal — a hope cut short by Ibn Hanbal's death. By 242/856-7 he had moved to southern Iraq, studying with the leading scholars of Wasit, Basra and Kufa — among them Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la al-San'ani, Muhammad ibn Bashshar (Bundar) and Abu Kurayb — then returned to Baghdad within two years for a further eight {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 21}.

## Tutor to a vizier's son
During this period he was made tutor to a son of Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya ibn Khaqan, vizier of al-Mutawakkil, sometime between 244/858-9 and 248 — a post that could have opened the way to office had he wanted it {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 21}.
