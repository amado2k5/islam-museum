## Summary
In 444 AH records were written in the diwan at Baghdad that named the ruler of Egypt and his predecessors and cast doubt on the lineage they claimed, following what had happened in the time of [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir]] in the [[event/baghdad-manifesto-402|Baghdad manifesto of 402 AH]]. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.496-497}

## The records
They denied the link of the rulers to the Messenger of God and to Ali and Fatima, attributed them to the Dayṣāniyya among the Magians and the Qaddāḥiyya among the Jews, and stated that they had left Islam. The signatures of nobles, judges, witnesses and scholars were taken on these records, at a moment meant to revive the Abbasid call, to which the ruler of the Maghrib had moved the khutba. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.496-497}
