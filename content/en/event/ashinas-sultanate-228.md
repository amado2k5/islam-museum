## Overview
In 228 AH the caliph [[person/al-wathiq|al-Wathiq]] appointed [[person/ashinas-al-turki|Ashinas al-Turki]] as his deputy in the sultanate, and clothed him in two jewelled sashes and a jewelled crown. al-Suyuti says he thinks this was the first time a caliph appointed a sultan, because the Turks had grown numerous only in the days of al-Wathiq's father. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 529}
