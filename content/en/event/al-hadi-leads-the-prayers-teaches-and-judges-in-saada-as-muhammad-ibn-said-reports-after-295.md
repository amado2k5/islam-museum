## Overview
Muhammad ibn Sa'id reports that al-Hadi led the congregational prayers in Sa'da, taught the duties of religion and inheritance, and settled disputes gently.

## Detail
Muhammad ibn Sa'id reports that when al-Hadi came down to Sa'da and lived in the government house, he led the people in congregational prayer, never cutting the prayers short by night or day. Between the prayers he sat and taught the people the duties of religion and the duties of inheritance, and they brought their disputes to him, and he explained the matter to them gently.
