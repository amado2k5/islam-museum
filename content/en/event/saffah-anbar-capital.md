## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that in 134 AH [[person/abu-al-abbas-al-saffah|Abu al-Abbas al-Saffah]] moved to [[place/al-anbar|al-Anbar]] and made it the seat of the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.419}
