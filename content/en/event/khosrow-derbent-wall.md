## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates from people learned in Armenian affairs that the commander of [[person/qubad-ibn-firuz|Qubad ibn Firuz]] crossed the land of Arran, then Qubad joined him, built cities there and closed the gap between the land of Shirvan and the Gate of the Lan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273-276}

## Anushirvan's building
His son [[person/khosrow-i-anushirvan|Anushirvan]] ruled after him. He built the city of [[place/bab-al-abwab|the Gate and the Gates]] and other cities, fortresses and castles, and settled the fortresses with the men of valour from the Siyasija. He conquered what the Romans held of Armenia and rebuilt Dabil. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273-276}

## The wall story
The account says that Anushirvan wrote to the king of the Turks asking for a truce and proposing marriage to his daughter. The king sent him a woman whom one of his wives had adopted as her own daughter. They met and drank together, and during the feast a fire broke out in the camp. Anushirvan then proposed building a wall between them with a gate in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273-276}

On the sea side he built it of stone and lead, three hundred cubits wide, and extended it three miles into the sea. He hung iron gates at its entrance and set a hundred horsemen to guard it. The account says that the Khaqan was later told that he had been deceived, and that he had been given someone other than his daughter. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.273-276}

The date is approximate (around 542 CE) and needs an additional source.
