## Overview
Ibn Kayyala enters Sanaa on Saturday, three nights into Muharram 299 AH. Ali ibn al-Fadl comes on Thursday and enters Sanaa on the night of Ashura, staying eleven days before he leaves for Madar.

## Detail
The book says Ibn Kayyala, coming from Dhamar, entered Sanaa on Saturday, three nights into Muharram 299 AH. Ali ibn al-Fadl came on Thursday and people fled before him; he entered Sanaa on the night of Ashura and stayed eleven days. Asad and Ibn Kayyala went to al-Kalabih in the district of Qadam and stayed some days. Ibn al-Fadl left Sanaa for Madar, stayed some days and returned to Shiban. The footnote gives al-Kalabih as the name in the Sifat al-Jazira, page 113.
