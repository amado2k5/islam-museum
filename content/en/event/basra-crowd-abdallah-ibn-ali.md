## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Sulayman ibn Ali built al-Mughitha, and that al-Mansur wanted to extract an estate from the marshes (al-Bitiha) and ordered the making of al-Subaytiyya. [[person/sulayman-ibn-ali|Sulayman]] and the people of [[place/basra|Basra]] disliked this. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The shouting
The people of Basra gathered at the gate of [[person/abdallah-ibn-ali-ibn-abdallah|Abd Allah ibn Ali]], who was with his brother Sulayman, fleeing from al-Mansur. They shouted: "Come down to us, and we will pledge allegiance to you." Sulayman held them back and dispersed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The delegation from Basra
Sulayman sent to al-Mansur a delegation that included Suwayd ibn Abd Allah, Dawud ibn Abi Hind, Ali ibn Bashir and Sa'id ibn Abi Urwuba, carrying a drawing of the marsh. They told him of their fear that their water would turn salty, and he ordered the work to be halted. Then he came to Basra and ordered al-Subaytiyya to be extracted. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
