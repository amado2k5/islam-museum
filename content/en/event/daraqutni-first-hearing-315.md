## Summary
In Baghdad al-Daraqutni heard from Abu al-Qasim al-Baghawi, Ibn Abi Dawud, Ibn Sa'id, al-Husayn ibn al-Mahamili and many others — all of the generation of Baghdad's senior shaykhs who died about 316–318, so his hearing of them came before he was ten or thereabouts {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.101} {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.183}.

## The young man at al-Saffar's circle
It is told that in his youth he attended Isma'il al-Saffar's circle copying a fascicule while al-Saffar dictated; told that a hearer who copies has no valid sama', he answered that his grasp differed from his challenger's, then enumerated the eighteen hadiths dictated, with their isnads and texts in order of the dictation to the last of them, and the people marvelled {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 pp.183-184} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp.102-103}.
