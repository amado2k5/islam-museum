After the first revelation came down in the [[place/ghar-hira|cave of Hira]] and [[person/waraqa-ibn-nawfal|Waraqa ibn Nawfal]] died, the revelation paused for a time. Ibn Sa'd relates from Ibn Abbas that it lasted days — which the book prefers, indeed considers settled after weighing the matter — while the well-known claim that it lasted three years or two and a half he does not consider sound at all. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

In the days of the pause the Prophet remained sorrowful and grieved, visited by perplexity and wonder; whenever the pause stretched long for him, Gabriel would appear to him and reassure him that he was truly God's messenger, and his heart would be stilled — as al-Bukhari relates in the book of dream-interpretation. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

Then Gabriel came to him a second time: al-Bukhari relates from Jabir ibn Abdullah that he heard him tell how he heard a voice from the sky, lifted his gaze, and there was the angel who had come to him at Hira seated on a chair between heaven and earth; he was so struck that he sank to the ground, went in to his family and said: "cover me, cover me" — and God sent down "O you who are covered" through "and defilement, flee", after which the revelation grew warm and came in succession. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
