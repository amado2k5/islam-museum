## Overview
"Then the Hakami moved with his armies until he drew near al-Hadi, and when al-Hadi saw him he drew up his force; the people met, the fighting was joined, then al-Hadi's left wing gave way and the rout fell on his companions" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}.

## Detail
"Al-Hadi turned across the host and took to the flanks of the army" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.296}; the editor notes that the word "al-Hadi" here is added from manuscript S. The book gives neither the place of the battle nor its day, nor the number killed at this stage. It is the report of the imam's supporters and the museum has no account of the other side from this source. The event lies between [[event/the-hakami-regrets-and-camps-near-al-hadi-with-three-thousand-292|the Hakami's camping near him]] and [[event/al-hadi-stands-with-seven-horsemen-and-the-eight-names-292|his standing with seven horsemen]]. The account is in a Zaydi book in the voice of the imam's followers; its descriptions and its judgments on opponents are the narrator's or the poet's, and the museum adopts none of them and gives no verdict.
