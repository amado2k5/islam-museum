## Overview
Ahmad ibn Yahya returns from the Hijaz at the end of Dhu al-Hijja 300 AH. On Sunday, eight nights into Safar 301 AH, the notables of Khawlan gather around him, seek his help against his brother and ask him to take the command as their fathers did. He agrees, and men of Hamdan and Najran pledge allegiance to him.

## Detail
The book says Ahmad ibn al-Hadi came back from the Hijaz at the end of Dhu al-Hijja 300 AH and lived with his brother. On Sunday, eight nights into Safar 301 AH, the notables of Khawlan gathered around him, sought his help against his brother and asked him to take the command as his father had done. He agreed; they gave him covenants of obedience and of joining him against anyone who opposed him, and men of Hamdan and Najran pledged to him. He then sent his commanders and governors to all his districts. The printed edition's marginal note titles this section as the return of al-Nasir li-Din Allah Ahmad ibn Yahya from the Hijaz to Sa'da.
