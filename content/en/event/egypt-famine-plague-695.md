## Famine and plague in Egypt in 695 AH
The year 695 began with the people of Egypt suffering a severe famine and an excessive plague, to the point that they ate carrion. On a single day one thousand five hundred funerals were carried out. They dug great pits and buried many people together in them {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748}.
