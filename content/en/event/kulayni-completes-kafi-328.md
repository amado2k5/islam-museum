## Summary
At Baghdad [[person/al-kulayni|al-Kulayni]] completed his voluminous work [[work/al-kafi|al-Kafi]], on which he is said to have worked for twenty years {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 401}.

## The book's design
Though mostly a collection of traditions of the Imams, it was meant as a guide to authoritative Imami doctrine in theology and fiqh, and so is arranged by subject; only exceptionally are the views of Imami scholars quoted, such as al-Fadl ibn Shadhan's elaborations on inheritance law. It is divided into the Usul, the Furu', and the Kitab al-Rawda {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 401}.
