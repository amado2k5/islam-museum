## Overview
In 396/1006 Mahmud of Ghazna directed a campaign at Multan itself; its amir Abu'l-Futuh Dawud ibn Nasr fled to Sarandib, and Mahmud took the city and laid the jizya on it {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p. 97-98}.

## The first campaign, 396
Mahmud had already defeated Jaypal, then Anandpal ibn Jaypal, who had refused him passage. When the lord of Multan — whose people followed the Qarmati way — heard of Mahmud's coming he fled to Sarandib, and Mahmud broke the city's resistance and took it by force {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p. 97-98}.

## The second campaign, 400-401
When Dawud returned to Qarmati allegiance, Mahmud invaded his land in 400 and 401, seized his capital, captured Dawud and sent him to Ghur where he ended his days; the Encyclopaedia adds that some of his followers were put to the sword {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p. 98} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
