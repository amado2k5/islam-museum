## Summary
Sultan Tughril ibn Arslan grew strong against Qizil Arslan al-Ildakuzi and took from him lands in al-Jibal and Azerbaijan. The wars went back and forth until Tughril gained the upper hand. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 163}
