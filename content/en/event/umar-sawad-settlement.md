## Overview
In al-Baladhuri's account, when the Sawad was conquered the people asked [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] to divide it, and Sa'd wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]. Umar ordered him to divide the goods and the horses after the fifth, and to leave the land and the rivers to their workers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.370}

## Umar's reason
Umar said in the letter: if I divided it among those who were present, nothing would remain for those who came after them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.370}

In the report of Ibrahim al-Taymi, the troops told him they had taken it by force with their swords; he refused and said: "What then is for those of the Muslims who come after you?" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Consultation
Umar wished to divide it and ordered a count of them, and the share of a man came to three peasants. He consulted the Companions of the Prophet, and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] said: "Leave them as a supply for the Muslims." He sent [[person/uthman-ibn-hunayf|Uthman ibn Hunayf]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The status of the people of the Sawad
Sulayman ibn Yasar said that Umar confirmed the Sawad to those in the loins of men and the wombs of women, and made them protected people on whom the poll tax and land tax were due, with no slavery over them. Mujahid said that the land is not sold or bought, because it was taken by force and not divided. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik wanted to make the people of the Sawad spoils, but the account of what Umar had done was mentioned to him, and God turned him away from them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The landowners
[[person/jamil-ibn-busbuhri|Jamil ibn Busbuhri]], [[person/bistam-ibn-tarsi|Bistam ibn Tarsi]] and other landowners converted to Islam, and Umar did not interfere with them or take the land from their hands, and he removed the poll tax from their necks. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The site of the event is [[place/kaskar|Kaskar]], and the date is about 17 AH according to the account. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.370}
