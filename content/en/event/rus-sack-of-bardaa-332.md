## Overview
Ibn al-Athir relates in "al-Kamil" that a group of the Rus came out on the sea in 332 AH and went up the river Kur to [[place/bardhaa|Bardha'a]]. They defeated the deputy of the marzban and his Daylami and volunteer troops, and entered the city. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.308-310}

## The events
The Rus called out safety to the people of the city and behaved well at first, as Ibn al-Athir says. Then their crier called for the people of the city to leave within three days. Most of them stayed, so the Rus put them to the sword and took some ten thousand captives. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.308-310}

They gathered those who remained in the mosque and demanded a ransom of twenty dirhams for each man. When the money was not collected they killed them, and enslaved the captives. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.308-310}

## The end
Ibn al-Athir says that the Rus ate too many fruits, and a plague and death struck them. The [[person/marzuban-ibn-muhammad|marzuban]] set an ambush for them, and a great number of them were killed, among them their commander, and the rest were besieged in the fortress of Shahrastan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.308-310}
