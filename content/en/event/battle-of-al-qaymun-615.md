## Overview
Among the events of 615 AH in [[work/mirat-al-zaman|Mir'at al-zaman]], [[person/sibt-ibn-al-jawzi|Sibt ibn al-Jawzi]] records that [[person/al-malik-al-muazzam|al-Malik al-Mu'azzam Isa]] met the Franks at al-Qaymun in Jumada II and defeated them. The battle took place during [[event/franks-damietta-616|the Frankish siege of Damietta]], a few days before the death of his father [[person/al-malik-al-adil-ayyub|al-Adil]]. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.228} {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.231}

## Background
The Franks landed before [[place/dimyat|Damietta]] in Rabi' I 615. Al-Adil, at Marj al-Suffar, sent the troops he had with him to his son al-Kamil in Egypt, "and al-Mu'azzam stayed on the coast with the army of Syria facing the Franks". {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.227} Al-Adil had summoned al-Mu'azzam and told him to dismantle the fortress of al-Tur that he had built, so that its men and equipment could be used to defend Damietta; al-Mu'azzam hesitated for some days, then agreed, and moved its contents to Jerusalem, Ajlun, al-Karak and Damascus. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.227}

## The battle
Sibt ibn al-Jawzi writes: "In Jumada II al-Mu'azzam met the Franks at al-Qaymun and was given victory over them." He killed a great many of them, captured a hundred Templar knights and brought them into [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] "with their banners reversed". {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.228} Elsewhere he gives the day: "Thursday 5 Jumada II — some say Wednesday." {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.231}

## Afterwards
Two or three days later al-Adil died at Aliqin on Friday 7 or 8 Jumada II; a carrier pigeon was sent to al-Mu'azzam at Nablus, who came and kept the death secret, as told in [[event/death-al-adil-615|the death of al-Adil]]. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.231} {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.232} Among the deaths of the previous year (614), in his notice on Badr al-Din Muhammad ibn Abi al-Qasim al-Hakkari, one of al-Mu'azzam's senior amirs, Sibt ibn al-Jawzi says he "fell as a martyr at al-Tur", that he had always wished for martyrdom, and that he built a madrasa for the Shafi'is in Jerusalem. {cite:mirat-al-zaman-risala-2013-v22 p.226}
