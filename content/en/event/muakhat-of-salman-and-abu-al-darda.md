## Summary
The Prophet paired Salman and Abu al-Darda' as brothers. Visiting Umm al-Darda', Salman found her plain and neglected in dress; she told him "your brother Abu al-Darda' has no need of this world" — absorbed in worship. When Abu al-Darda' came, Salman told him: "your Lord has a right over you, your self has a right over you, and your family has a right over you — so give every right-holder his right" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1968}, and the Prophet affirmed Salman's counsel to his brother.
