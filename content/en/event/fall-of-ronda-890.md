## Overview
In Jumada I 890 AH the king of Castile besieged Ronda. He demolished its walls, and its people came out under safe conduct, and the lands west of Malaga obeyed him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.520}

## Ronda
The people of Ronda had gone out to support Dhakwan and others. After its fall the king sent forces to some of the fortresses to besiege Malaga. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.520}

## The Ramadan campaign
In Ramadan of that year he set out for the fortress of Qanbil, demolished its walls, and its people came out under safe conduct. He took fortresses from it, Mashaqir and the fortress of al-Lawz. Al-Maqqari says that he never turned toward a region without uprooting it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.520}
