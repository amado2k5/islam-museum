## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that in 572 AH [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] ordered the building of the great wall surrounding Egypt and Cairo, and put [[person/bahaa-al-din-qaraqush|Baha' al-Din Qaraqush]] in charge of its building. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.684-685}

## The wall and the citadel
Al-Suyuti quotes Ibn al-Athir that the circuit of the wall was twenty-nine thousand three hundred cubits by the Hashimi measure. In the same year he ordered a citadel built on the Muqattam mountain, which became the seat of the sultanate. It was not completed until the days of al-Malik al-Kamil, the first to live in it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.684-685}

He says that Salah al-Din also built the tomb of Imam al-Shafi'i. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.684-685}
