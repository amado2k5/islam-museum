## Summary
Early in his career al-Tha'alibi journeyed to Bukhara during the death throes of the Samanid dynasty which Mahmud of Ghazna was to supersede {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 450-451}; the Samanids ended in 389/999 when Mahmud and the Ilig Khan partitioned their realm {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 450-451}.
