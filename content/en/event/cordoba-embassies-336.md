## Overview
In 336 AH envoys of the ruler of Constantinople came to al-Nasir, [[person/abd-al-rahman-iii-al-nasir|Abd al-Rahman]], bearing his gift, and al-Nasir celebrated their arrival at [[place/madinat-al-zahra|al-Zahra]]. al-Maqqari describes the decoration of the palace and the troops riding out in arms. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.400}

## The letter
They delivered their king's letter, which was on dyed parchment written in gold in Greek script. al-Nasir ordered the preachers to give the sermon, and they were dumbfounded. Then [[person/mundhir-ibn-said-al-balluti|Mundhir ibn Sa'id al-Balluti]] stood and preached, and recited a poem he improvised. al-Nasir afterwards gave him the judgeship. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.401}

## Later embassies
Then came envoys from the king of the Slavs, from the king of the Germans and from the kings of the Franks, and al-Nasir sent a bishop with the envoy of the Slavs to their king. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.402}
