## Overview
On his father's order Abu al-Qasim leaves Sanaa on Saturday, twelve nights into Shawwal 297 AH, so that he would not be fighting Ibn Kayyala and the Qarmatians at once. The Qarmatians from Shiban re-enter the city and stay fourteen days, finding no one there, the book says.

## Detail
The book says al-Hadi wrote to his son ordering him to withdraw from the city and not to fight Ibn Kayyala, so that he would not face Ibn Kayyala and the Qarmatians together. Abu al-Qasim left Sanaa on Saturday, twelve nights into Shawwal 297 AH, with everyone who had been there that day. At Warwar he went on to Sa'da and joined his father. Some who had come out with him stayed behind, and the Qarmatians from Shiban entered Sanaa, stayed fourteen days, and found no one there.
