## Overview
In Safar 64 AH the army of the people of Syria besieged [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Ibn al-Zubayr]] in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and threw catapults at him, as al-Suyuti relates. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 345}

## The siege
Al-Suyuti says that the amir of the army died on the road, and another amir was appointed over them. They came to Mecca and besieged Ibn al-Zubayr and fought him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 345}

The sparks of their fires burned the curtains of the Ka'ba and its roof, and the two horns of the ram which, al-Suyuti says, was given as a ransom for Isma'il, which were in the roof. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 345}

## The death of Yazid
Al-Suyuti relates that Yazid died in the middle of Rabi al-Awwal that year. News of it came while the fighting continued. Ibn al-Zubayr called out to the people of Syria that their lord had died, and they dispersed and were snatched up by the people. Ibn al-Zubayr called for allegiance to himself and took the title of caliph, and the people of Syria gave their allegiance to Mu'awiya ibn Yazid. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 345}

The date of the event is derived. The data cites two other sources, to which the sentences here are not attributed, so the report needs an additional source.

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that al-Husayn ibn Numayr set up mangonels on Abu Qubays and bombarded the Mosque, and that on hearing of Yazid's death he asked for a truce, the gates were opened for the circumambulation, and he secretly offered to take Ibn al-Zubayr to Syria to receive allegiance there, which Ibn al-Zubayr refused aloud. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 267} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 268}
