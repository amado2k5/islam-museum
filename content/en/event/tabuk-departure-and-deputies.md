## Summary
The Prophet pitched his camp at Thaniyyat al-Wada'; Ibn Hisham says he appointed Muhammad ibn Maslama al-Ansari over Medina, and relates from al-Darawardi from his father that he appointed Siba' ibn Urfuta. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## The account
When the Prophet went out he pitched his camp at Thaniyyat al-Wada', a ridge overlooking Medina, per the editors. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

Ibn Hisham said: he appointed Muhammad ibn Maslama al-Ansari over Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

And Ibn Hisham relates from Abd al-Aziz ibn Muhammad al-Darawardi from his father that on his departure for Tabuk the Prophet appointed Siba' ibn Urfuta over Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- Disagreement over who was left in charge of Medina: Muhammad ibn Maslama (Ibn Hisham's own statement) or Siba' ibn Urfuta (al-Darawardi's account from his father); Ibn Hisham does not adjudicate (p. 162).
- The spelling "al-Darawardi" in the editors' version vs. the account's "al-Andarawardi" — the editors' form is the well-known one (note p. 162).
