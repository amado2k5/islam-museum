## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/muhammad-ibn-abi-bakr|Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr]] was appointed governor of Egypt twice in the caliphate of Ali, and was then killed and burned by [[person/muawiya-ibn-khudayj|Muawiya ibn Hudayj]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Egypt after Uthman's killing
Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/muhammad-ibn-abi-hudhayfa|Muhammad ibn Abi Hudhayfa]] prevailed over Egypt after the killing of Uthman. Then Ali appointed Qays ibn Sa'd over it, and he removed him; he appointed Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr, and he removed him and appointed Malik al-Ashtar, who fell ill at al-Qulzum. Then he returned Muhammad to it a second time. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
Muawiya ibn Hudayj killed him and burned him inside the body of a donkey, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The place of the event in the museum's data is [[place/al-fustat|Fustat]], and the Gregorian date is derived.
