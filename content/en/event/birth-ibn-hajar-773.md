## Overview
Shihab al-Din Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Muhammad, known as Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani, was born in Cairo on 12 Sha'ban 773 {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 270}; the Encyclopaedia of Islam gives 22 Sha'ban 773, corresponding to 28 February 1372. Family tradition traced his nisba al-Asqalani to 587/1191, when Saladin ordered Ascalon destroyed and its Muslim inhabitants resettled; his forebears moved to Alexandria and then to Cairo {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 808}.
