## Summary
His father the imam Abu Muhammad Abdallah ibn Yusuf al-Juwayni died in Dhu al-Qa'da 438/1047 when he was twenty; he was seated in his father's place to teach before completing twenty — teaching while going out to al-Bayhaqi's madrasa and perfecting the usul under Abu al-Qasim al-Isfara'ini al-Iskaf {cite:wafayat-ayan} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 230-231} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala j.18 pp. 2574-2575} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 605}.
