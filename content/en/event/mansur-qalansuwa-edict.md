## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that in 153 AH [[person/al-mansur|al-Mansur]] required his subjects to wear tall caps (qalansuwa). They made them of reed and paper and wore them black. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.426}

Al-Suyuti quotes verses by Abu Dulama on this matter, which contain a mocking comparison of the caps. The text is in the source and is not reproduced here. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.426}
