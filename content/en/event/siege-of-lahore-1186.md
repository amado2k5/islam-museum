## Overview
After the loss of Ghazna itself in 558/1163, Lahore served as the Ghaznavid capital, until its capture by the Ghurids in 583/1187 put an end to the dynasty {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 37}.
