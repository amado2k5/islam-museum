## Summary
The qadi Abu Bakr al-Baqillani learned kalam from Abu Abd Allah ibn Mujahid al-Ta'i, one of al-Ash'ari's own companions, and transmitted hadith from Abu Bakr al-Qati'i; he had a large, well-attended lecture circle {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 169}. McCarthy notes that he studied usul al-din under disciples of al-Ash'ari and drew many to his lectures {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1018}.
