## Summary
In 508 AH an earthquake struck al-Jazira. Thirteen towers of the walls of Edessa (al-Ruha) fell, and parts of the walls of Harran {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 501}.

## Losses
Samosata sank into the earth. About a hundred houses fell in Balis, and half of the citadel was overturned {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 501}.
