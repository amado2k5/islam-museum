## Summary
[[person/mahmud-al-alusi|Mahmud al-Alusi]] began writing [[work/ruh-al-maani|Ruh al-Ma'ani]] on the night of 16 Sha'ban 1252 AH, in the thirty-fourth year of his life. {cite:ruh-al-maani-ilmiyya-1994-v1 p.5}

## The dream
In the preface to his commentary al-Alusi says he kept putting off writing until, on a Friday night in Rajab 1252, he had a dream "that I do not count as a jumble of dreams": God commanded him to fold up the heavens and the earth and join what was split in them; he raised one hand to the sky and lowered the other to the water's resting place. He woke awed by it, found in some books that it pointed to writing a commentary, set aside his excuses and began. {cite:ruh-al-maani-ilmiyya-1994-v1 p.5} He says he wrote it in the reign of [[person/mahmud-ii-uthmani|Sultan Mahmud Khan son of Abd al-Hamid Khan]]. {cite:ruh-al-maani-ilmiyya-1994-v1 p.5} The Gregorian date in the data is a tabular conversion and so approximate.
