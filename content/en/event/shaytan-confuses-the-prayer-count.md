## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet reported the shaytan comes to the praying man and confounds him until he does not know how much he has prayed; whoever finds that should prostrate twice while sitting {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-263|Muwatta 263]].

## The meaning
This hadith shows the shaytan besets prayer with forgetfulness of the count, and that God legislated the two prostrations of forgetfulness for certainty about the prayer; it belongs to the chapters of forgetfulness in the Sunan and the Muwatta {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
