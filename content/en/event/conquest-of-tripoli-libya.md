## Overview
al-Baladhuri, on the authority of Ali ibn Abi Talha, relates that [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] came down at [[place/atrabulus|Tripoli]] in 22 AH, was fought, and then took it by force. He took there many loads of Byzantine cloth from merchants, sold them and divided their price among the Muslims. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.316}

## The letter to Umar
He wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] that they had reached Tripoli, which was nine days' march from Ifriqiya, and asked his permission to raid it. Umar forbade him, and said that it was not Ifriqiya but a place of division and treachery. al-Baladhuri explains this by the fact that its people used to pay something to the king of the Byzantines and then betray him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.316}
