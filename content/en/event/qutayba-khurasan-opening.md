## Overview
Al-Hajjaj appointed [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim al-Bahili]] over Khurasan; he crossed the river and the kings beyond it came to him in obedience. He then left his brother [[person/salih-ibn-muslim|Salih]] over Transoxiana. This is what al-Baladhuri relates. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Crossing the river
Al-Baladhuri says that when Qutayba reached Taliqan, the landowners of Balkh met him. After crossing the river, the king of Saghaniyan came to him with gifts and a golden key, and gave him obedience. Another king, of Wasumashan, had been pressing him, which is why he gave what he gave. The king of Kaphian did the same. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Salih ibn Muslim
Qutayba returned to Merv and left Salih, who conquered Kasan and Awrasht in Farghana, and conquered Sakhr and Akhsikat. [[person/nasr-ibn-sayyar|Nasr ibn Sayyar]] was in his army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source says that [[person/nuh-ibn-asad|Nuh ibn Asad]] was the last to conquer Kasan and Awrasht, and that its people broke their covenant in the caliphate of al-Mansur. The lord of Jozjanan made peace with Qutayba, and then Qutayba returned and died at Taliqan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
