## Overview
Hujr ibn Adi al-Kindi and his companions were killed in 51 AH, as al-Tabari reports. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.253}

## The account
Al-Tabari says that Mu'awiya appointed al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba over Kufa in 41 AH; he summoned him and gave him instructions, and then events followed that led to their killing. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.253}
