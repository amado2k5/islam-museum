## Overview
In 1349 the Black Death struck [[place/tunis|Tunis]] and took both of [[person/ibn-khaldun|Ibn Khaldun]]'s parents. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}

## Background
The family had crossed from Spain after the fall of Seville and Córdoba and settled in Tunis, where its members held high administrative posts. His father had given up administration to devote himself to theology, law and letters. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}

## Why it matters
The loss marked a turning point. Ibn Khaldun then entered the service of rulers in the Maghreb and Spain. {cite:britannica-ibn-khaldun}
