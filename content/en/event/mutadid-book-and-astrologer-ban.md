## Overview
In the first year of his caliphate, [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] forbade the booksellers to sell the books of the philosophers and the like, and forbade the storytellers and astrologers to sit in the street. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.569,573}

Al-Suyuti quotes al-Suli that he ordered that no astrologer or storyteller sit in the street, and made the booksellers swear not to sell the books of philosophy and dialectic. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.569,573}

The year 279 AH is derived, and the date needs a second source.
