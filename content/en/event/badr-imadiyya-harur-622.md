## Summary
In 622 AH Badr al-Din Lu'lu', lord of Mosul, took the citadels of al-Imadiyya and Harur in the districts of Mosul. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 455}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Badr al-Din took the citadel of al-Imadiyya in the Mosul districts this year; he had earlier related how its people rebelled against him in 615 and surrendered it to Imad al-Din Zangi, then returned to Badr al-Din's obedience in defiance of Imad al-Din; once they had come back to him, its affairs settled and he held it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 455} As Ibn al-Athir narrates it in this year's events, the episode shows how the fortresses of the Jazira kept changing hands among the amirs who competed for the wilayas in those days. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 455}
