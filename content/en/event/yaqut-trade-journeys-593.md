## Summary
His master Askar sent him on trading voyages: he shuttled to the island of Kish in the Indian Ocean, to Oman, then to Syria {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}; a documented itinerary ran Baghdad–Amid–Syria–Baghdad in 593-4/1197-8 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}.

## What travel earned him
Yaqut read while travelling and met men of learning until a considerable scholarly temperament was formed in him; his early education he owed to Askar's provision and then to a curiosity he turned to profit on the trade roads {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264}.
