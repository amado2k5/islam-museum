## Overview
In Sha'ban 580 AH [[person/ali-ibn-ghaniya|Ali ibn Ishaq, known as Ibn Ghaniya]], lord of Majorca, took the city of [[place/bejaia|Bejaia]], as Ibn al-Athir relates. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 128-129}

## Entering the city
He heard of the death of Yusuf ibn Abd al-Mu'min, so he equipped a fleet of twenty vessels and anchored on the coast of Bejaia. About two hundred horsemen and four thousand foot of the Mulaththamun went out. He entered the city without fighting, because its governor had gone to Marrakesh and left no army. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 128-129}

## The governor's return
A group of what remained of the Banu Hammad state joined him. The governor returned with three hundred Almohad horsemen and about a thousand Arabs. When they met, those who were with the governor joined al-Mulaththam, and the governor fled to Marrakesh. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 128-129}

## Constantine
Ibn Ghaniya obeyed the districts of Bejaia except Constantine, and he besieged it until an Almohad army came by land and sea in Safar 581 AH. His two brothers Yahya and Abd Allah went out of it fleeing, and he withdrew from it to Ifriqiya. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 128-129}
