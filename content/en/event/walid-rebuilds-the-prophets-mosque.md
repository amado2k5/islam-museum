## Overview
The sources say that [[person/al-walid-ibn-abd-al-malik|al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik]] demolished the Prophet's Mosque and rebuilt and enlarged it, in 87 or 88 AH according to the reports. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.436} {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.367}

## Al-Tabari's account
In Safar 88 AH the demolition of the mosque began. Al-Walid sent to the king of the Byzantines, informing him and asking for his help, and he sent him a hundred thousand mithqals of gold, a hundred workers and forty loads of mosaic. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.436}

## Al-Suyuti's account
According to al-Suyuti, al-Walid began building the mosque of Damascus, and wrote for the expansion and rebuilding of the Prophet's Mosque in 87 AH. The years of the two sources differ. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.367}

## The inclusion of the chambers
Al-Salabi says that al-Walid wished to expand, and included the chambers of the Mothers of the Believers, the chamber of Fatima and the chamber of Aisha in the mosque. When Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz learned of it from Medina, he gathered the ten jurists and the notables of the people, and they denied it and disliked it. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.129-130}

He says that the jurists held that the chambers should be left as they were, and Umar wrote to al-Walid to that effect, and al-Walid ordered him to renew the building as he wished. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.130-131}
