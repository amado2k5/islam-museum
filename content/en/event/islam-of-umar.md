[[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] became Muslim in Dhu al-Hijja of the sixth year, three days after [[event/islam-of-hamza|Hamza's conversion]]; the Prophet had prayed: "O God, strengthen Islam by the dearer to You of the two men — Umar ibn al-Khattab or Abu Jahl ibn Hisham" — and the dearer of them to God was Umar. {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}

The sum of the accounts is that he took refuge one night by the mosque and heard the Prophet reciting the opening of Sura Ta-Ha, and Islam entered his heart; then he went out with his sword meaning to kill the Prophet. [[person/nuaym-ibn-abdullah|Nu'aym ibn Abdullah]] met him and directed him to the house of his sister [[person/fatima-bint-al-khattab|Fatima]] and her husband [[person/said-ibn-zayd|Sa'id ibn Zayd]]; there he heard the opening of Ta-Ha, then went to the house of al-Arqam and proclaimed the testimony before the Prophet. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The Muslims went out in two rows — [[person/hamza|Hamza]] in one and Umar in the other — until they entered the mosque, and a gloom fell on Quraysh such as had never struck them; the Prophet named Umar that day "al-Faruq". [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Ibn Mas'ud]] used to say: "we could not pray at the Ka'ba until Umar became Muslim … we have been mighty since Umar became Muslim." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
