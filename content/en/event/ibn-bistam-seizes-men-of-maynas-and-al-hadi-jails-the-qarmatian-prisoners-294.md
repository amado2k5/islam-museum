## Overview
A senior Qarmatian leader from al-Hijr, Ibn Ghabra, was among those taken. Ibn Bistam seized men of Maynas, his relatives and clients, and brought them to al-Hadi, who ordered them jailed.

## Detail
The book says Ibn Ghabra, of the clan of Ashid, was a senior Qarmatian and a missionary from the village of al-Hijr. Ibn Bistam then seized some men of the village of Maynas, relatives and clients of his own, brought them to al-Hadi, and they were put in prison. The passage then turns to al-Hadi's decision to leave for Sa'da and his orders for the tax and for those left in charge of Najran.
