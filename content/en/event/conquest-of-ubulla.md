## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/utba-ibn-ghazwan|Utba ibn Ghazwan]] raided [[place/ubulla|al-Ubulla]] and took it by force. He wrote to Umar that it was the port of Bahrain, Oman, India and China, and sent the letter with Nafi' ibn al-Harith al-Thaqafi. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The following campaign
They crossed the Euphrates and overcame its people, and [[person/mujashi-ibn-masud-al-sulami|Mujashi ibn Mas'ud al-Sulami]] was sent over the Euphrates and took it by force. Utba marched to al-Madhar, then Dastmaysan and Abar Qubadh. Between al-Fahraj and the Euphrates there was a treaty, and the rest of al-Ubulla was taken by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

[[person/arda-bint-al-harith|Arda bint al-Harith]], the wife of Utba, recited verses urging the fighting. The booty was divided by [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]], who was then a boy. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
