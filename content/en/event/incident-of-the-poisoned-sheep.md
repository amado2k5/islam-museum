Once matters had settled for the Prophet and the Muslims at Khaybar, Zaynab bint al-Harith, wife of Sallam ibn Mishkam, set about gifting the Prophet a roasted sheep; she asked which part was dearest to him and was told the foreleg, so she put abundant steeped poison into it. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

When it was set before the Prophet he took the foreleg and bit a mouthful but could not swallow it; [[person/bishr-ibn-al-bara|Bishr ibn al-Bara]] took a piece and swallowed his morsel. The Prophet spat out his morsel and said: "lift your hands — this bone tells me it is poisoned." Bishr's colour changed and he died that same hour, a martyr. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

The Prophet summoned the woman and asked her: what drove you to this? She said: "you killed my father, my uncle and my husband, so I said — if he is a king we shall be rid of him, and if he is a prophet, God will inform him." The Prophet pardoned her at first; then, when Bishr ibn al-Bara died, she was killed for him in retaliation. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
