## Summary
Al-Mizzi died on Saturday 12 Safar 742 and was buried the next day in the Sufi cemetery; the people grieved him deeply and he left no one like him {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 493}.

## His last days
He was ill for a few days without breaking off his hadith teaching until near the end, and died between the zuhr and asr prayers while reciting the Throne Verse; his son-in-law Ibn Kathir says it was plague. The people prayed over him in the congregational mosque then outside Bab al-Nasr, and he was buried in the Sufi cemetery near Ibn Taymiyya, the funeral gathering being very large {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 4/461}.
