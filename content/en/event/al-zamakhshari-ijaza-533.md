## Overview
In 533/1138 al-Zamakhshari made a further trip to Mecca, passing through Baghdad where he visited the celebrated man of letters Abu Mansur Mawhub ibn al-Jawaliqi and received his ijaza {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 918}. He had heard hadith in Baghdad on his journeys and debated with its scholars {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 599} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 917}.
