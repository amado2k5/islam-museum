## Overview
In 243 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] came to Damascus and liked it. A palace was built for him at Darayya and he resolved to live there. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.541}

## The advice
Yazid ibn Muhammad al-Muhallabi recited verses to him warning him against leaving Iraq and against letting Syria gloat over him. He changed his mind, and returned after two or three months. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.541}
