## Overview
A dispute over a land grant at Marghab, near Merv, arose between the heirs of Hilal ibn Ahwaz al-Mazini and [[person/bashir-ibn-ubaydallah-ibn-abi-bakra|Bashir ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Bakra]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The story
The grant, eight thousand jaribs, had been made to Hilal ibn Ahwaz by Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik. Bashir dug the Marghab and its channels and called it the Marghab of Merv, and said the grant was his. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

[[person/humayri-ibn-hilal|Humayri ibn Hilal]] disputed it with him. Bashir had complained to [[person/khalid-ibn-abdallah-al-qasri|Khalid ibn Abd Allah al-Qasri]], who accepted his claim. Khalid wrote to Malik ibn al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud, who was in charge of the affairs of [[place/basra|Basra]], to allow Bashir access to the Marghab and his land. Amr ibn Yazid al-Usaydi was helping Humayri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
