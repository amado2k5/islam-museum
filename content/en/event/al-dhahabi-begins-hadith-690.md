## Overview
Al-Dhahabi began hearing hadith at Damascus in 690/1291 or 691 under Yusuf al-Mizzi, Umar ibn Kawwas, Ahmad ibn Hibat Allah ibn Asakir and Yusuf ibn Ahmad al-Kamuli, and studied law under Kamal al-Din ibn al-Zamlikani, Burhan al-Din al-Fazari and Kamal al-Din ibn Qadi Shubba; he was a Shafi'i {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 215}. Ibn Hajar records that he pursued the quest for some twenty years, hearing much from Ibn Qudayr, Ibn Asakir and Yusuf al-Mizzi {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 3/336}.
