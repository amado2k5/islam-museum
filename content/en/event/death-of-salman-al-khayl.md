## Overview
[[person/salman-ibn-rabi-a|Salman ibn Rabi'a]] was killed, with four thousand Muslims, after the Khaqan met him with his horsemen beyond the river of Balanjar, according to al-Baladhuri. The takbir could be heard in their predicament. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.287}

## Who he was
al-Baladhuri says that Salman was the first to be appointed judge in Kufa, and that he spent forty days without a litigant coming to him. He related from Umar ibn al-Khattab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.287}

## At Balanjar
With Salman was Qurza ibn Ka'b al-Ansari, the man who brought news of his death to Uthman. Concerning him and Qutayba ibn Muslim, Ibn Jumana al-Bahili said two verses mentioning two graves: a grave at Balanjar and a grave in China. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.287}

The Khaqan's army here is the [[group/khazars|Khazars]] according to the data of the entity. The approximate date is after the conquest of Arran in Uthman's caliphate, and it is derived.
