## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad said: Muhammad ibn Sa'id told me that when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] first entered the village of [[place/al-hajar-najran|al-Hajar]] a party of Banu Rabi'a came to ask safety for [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] and he agreed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} They went to [[place/minas-najran|Minas]] to bring him and found he had fled to the land of [[group/shakir-hamdan-tribe|Shakir]], so al-Hadi stayed in the village of [[place/najran|Najran]] and told a crier to proclaim safety for any of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] who came to him, except [[person/al-abras-al-madani|al-Abras al-Madani]] and his son, the two Madanis, who had taken captive the girl already mentioned. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178}

## The night at Minas
Al-Hadi ordered Abu Muhammad, [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]], to go out with a body of the army to Minas and spend the night there, and he did so. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.178} The girl is the one whose story is at p. 146; the museum does not name her because the book does not.
