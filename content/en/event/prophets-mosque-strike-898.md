## Summary
Ibn al-Imad, in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*, reports that lightning struck the Prophet's Mosque in [[place/medina|Medina]] in 898 AH, shortly before midday on Wednesday, the eighteenth of Safar. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}

## The damage
The lightning hit the main minaret; the crescent at its top was split, and part of its lower structure fell. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}

## In the same year
The author adds that in that year the strange plague struck Persia, and that about half of it also burned. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 368}

The date of the Gregorian day is derived from the Hijri date.
