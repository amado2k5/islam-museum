## Overview
Ibn al-Jawzi was freed in 595/1198–9 through the caliph's mother, won over by his son Muhyi al-Din Yusuf; he made a triumphant return to Baghdad and died soon after, in 597 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 784}.
