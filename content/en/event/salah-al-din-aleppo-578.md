## Summary
In 578 AH Salah al-Din set out and opened Harran, Suruj, Sinjar, Nisibin and Raqqa. He besieged Mosul and was baffled by its strength {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 739}.

## Aleppo
A messenger from the caliph came with an order for him to leave Mosul, and he left. He returned and took Aleppo from Izz al-Din Mas'ud al-Atabeki, and compensated him with Sinjar {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 739}.
