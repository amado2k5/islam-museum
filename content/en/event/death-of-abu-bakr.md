## Overview
[[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] died on the night of Tuesday, eight nights before the end of Jumada al-Akhira 13 AH, aged sixty-three, as [[person/aisha|Aisha]] relates in al-Suyuti's "Tarikh al-Khulafa". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## The onset of illness
Aisha says that his illness began when he bathed on Monday, the seventh of Jumada al-Akhira, which was a cold day. He had fever for fifteen days, during which he did not go out to the prayer. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## Accounts of the cause
- The source relates from Ibn Umar that the cause of his death was his grief for the Prophet's death, so his body kept weakening until he died. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
- It also relates that he and al-Harith ibn Kalada ate a broth that had been given to him as a gift, and [[person/al-harith-ibn-kalada|al-Harith]] said that there was poison in it for a year, and that they would die on the same day, and so it was. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
- It also quotes al-Sha'bi saying that the Prophet and Abu Bakr had been poisoned. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

These are differing accounts, and the source transmits them without weighing them against one another.

## His testament
At his death he told Aisha to look at the milch-camel, the bowl and the blanket that they used to benefit from when he took charge of the affairs of the Muslims, and to return them to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] when he died. When he died, she sent them to him. The account relates Umar's words: "You have tired whoever comes after you." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
In a line restored by the editor, [[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] reports from Ali ibn Muhammad and Abu al-Yaqzan that Abu Bakr died on Tuesday, eight nights before the end of Jumada al-Akhira, 13, aged sixty-three, an age on which there is no disagreement. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 121} Ibn Ishaq puts it at two years, three months and twelve days after the Prophet's death. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 121}
