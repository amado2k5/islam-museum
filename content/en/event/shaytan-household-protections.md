## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Jabir ibn Abdullah that the Messenger of God said: close the door, tie the waterskin, upturn or cover the vessel, and extinguish the lamp; for the shaytan does not open what is closed, untie what is tied or uncover a vessel, though the mouse may set fire to people's houses {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2686|Muwatta 31:2686]].

## The meaning
In this hadith the shaytan has ways into people's houses, and the precautionary sunnahs close them: shutting the door, setting the waterskin, covering the vessel and extinguishing the lamp {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
