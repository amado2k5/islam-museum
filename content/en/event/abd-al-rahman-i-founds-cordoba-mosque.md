## Overview
[[person/abd-al-rahman-al-dakhil|Abd al-Rahman al-Dakhil]] began building the [[building/great-mosque-cordoba|Great Mosque of Cordoba]] in 169/785 on the site of an old church whose Christian share he had purchased, and finished it quickly. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}

## What happened
Al-Maqqari records that Abd al-Rahman ibn Mu'awiya, once his rule in al-Andalus was secure, began the mosque on the site where the church had stood — the kernel of the great mosque that the Umayyad amirs after him enlarged. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}
