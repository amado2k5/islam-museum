## Overview
From 891/1486 al-Suyuti was also placed in charge of the Baybarsiyya khankah. About forty years old, he decided to retire from public life: he progressively resigned his functions and stopped delivering fatwas, giving as reasons the corruption and ignorance in the milieu of the ʿulamaʾ. At the same time his relations with Sultan Qa’it Bay grew acrimonious, for he refused to pay the customary monthly visit to the ruler {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
