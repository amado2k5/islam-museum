## Summary
The Spanish governor of Oran's attack on Mostaganem, Dhu'l-Qa'da 965/August 1558 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 246}.

## The reckoning and the rout
Seeing the Ottoman recovery of Tlemcen as a mortal threat, the [[person/conde-de-alcaudete|Count of Alcaudete]] led twelve thousand men to take Mostaganem — the Ottoman base against Oran — and make it his own against Algiers; the attempt failed with heavy losses and he was among the dead {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 246}.
