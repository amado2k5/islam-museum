## Summary
In 1097 the army of the First Crusade passed through [[place/konya|Konya]] (Iconium) on its march across Anatolia; it found no serviceable fortifications there but drew benefit from the gardens around the town. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 287}

## A Byzantine base
In the centuries of the Arab invasions Konya had been a Byzantine military base which the raiders deliberately circumvented, in favour of Tarsus or of Cappadocia by the northern routes; this is why the town is so seldom mentioned in the military histories. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 287}
