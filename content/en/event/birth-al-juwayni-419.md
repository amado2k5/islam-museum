## Summary
Abu al-Ma'ali Abd al-Malik ibn Abdallah ibn Yusuf al-Juwayni was born in Muharram at the opening of 419 — 18 Muharram 419/17 February 1028 by the Encyclopaedia of Islam — at Bushtanikan, a village on the outskirts of Nishapur in the district of Juwayn {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 605} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 230} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala j.18 p. 2574}; the Muntazam, the Kamil and Tarikh al-Khamis put his birth in 417 {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 230}.
