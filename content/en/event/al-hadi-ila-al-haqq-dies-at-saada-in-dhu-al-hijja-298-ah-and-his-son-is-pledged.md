## Overview
Al-Hadi ila al-Haqq Yahya ibn al-Husayn dies at Sa'da on Sunday, ten nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 298 AH, and is buried on Monday before noon. People pledge allegiance to his son Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya on Thursday, the first of Muharram 299 AH.

## Detail
The book gives the death as Sunday, ten nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 298 AH, at Sa'da, and the burial on Monday before noon. The pledge to Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya was on Thursday, the first of Muharram 299 AH. The text says he stayed at Sa'da with Hamdan, Khawlan and Najran under his authority. The printed edition's marginal note gives the heading 'the death of al-Hadi'.
