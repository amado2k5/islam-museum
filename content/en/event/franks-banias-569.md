## Summary
When Nur al-Din died, the Franks gathered and besieged Banias. Shams al-Din ibn al-Muqaddam went out to them with the army of Damascus. He first spoke to them gently, then grew harsh: otherwise, he said, they would seek help from Sayf al-Din in Mosul and from Saladin in Egypt, and the fear of Nur al-Din no longer weighed on him. The Franks recognized that he was sincere. They made peace with him for money and prisoners, and the truce was settled. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 60}
