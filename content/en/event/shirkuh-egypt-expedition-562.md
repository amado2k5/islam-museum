## Overview
In 562 AH the sultan [[person/nur-al-din-mahmud-ibn-zangi|Nur al-Din]] equipped the amir [[person/asad-al-din-shirkuh|Asad al-Din Shirkuh]] with two thousand horsemen for Egypt. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 679-680}

## The siege and the Franks
Shirkuh camped at Giza and besieged Cairo for about two months. Its ruler appealed to the Franks for help, and they entered by Damietta to relieve him. Shirkuh then withdrew to Upper Egypt. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 679-680}

## The battle and Alexandria
A battle took place between him and the Egyptians, in which al-Suyuti says he was victorious despite the smallness of his army, and killed thousands of the Franks. He then collected the land tax of Upper Egypt. The Franks made for Alexandria, which his nephew [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub]] had taken, and besieged it for four months. Shirkuh marched against them, and they withdrew, and he returned to Syria. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 679-680}

The Gregorian date is derived, and the report rests on a single source and needs an additional source.
