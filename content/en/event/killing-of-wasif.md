## Overview
[[person/wasif-al-turki|Wasif al-Turki]] was killed in Shawwal 253 AH, after groups of soldiers caused unrest demanding their stipends. Al-Mu'tazz then placed the affairs that had been in Wasif's hands under Bugha al-Sharabi. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.374}

## Al-Mas'udi's account
Al-Mas'udi says that the Pharaonic and Turkish people of the Karkh quarter of Samarra attacked him, and Bugha escaped from them. Al-Mu'tazz set Salih ibn Wasif in his father's place. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 552}
