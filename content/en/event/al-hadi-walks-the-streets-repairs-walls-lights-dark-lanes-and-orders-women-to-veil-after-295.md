## Overview
Al-Hadi walks the markets and lanes, orders a leaning wall repaired, a road cleared and a dark alley lit at night, orders women to veil, and is said to have introduced the face-veil for women in Yemen.

## Detail
When al-Hadi rose from his sitting he walked the markets and lanes with his companions. If he saw a leaning wall he ordered its owners to repair it, if he saw a bad road he ordered it cleared, and if he saw a dark alley he ordered its people to light it at night for passers-by and for those going to the mosque and elsewhere. If he saw a woman he ordered her to veil, and if she was past the age of marriage he ordered her to cover herself. The narrator says that he introduced the face-veil for women in Yemen and ordered them to wear it. The record gives this as the narrator's account of the Hadi's rule in Sa'da.
