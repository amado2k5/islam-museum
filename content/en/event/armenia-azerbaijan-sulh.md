## Overview
When 'Uthman dismissed 'Utba b. Farqad, Azerbaijan repudiated the treaty; al-Walid b. 'Uqba campaigned with 'Abd Allah b. Shabil al-Ahmasi in the van, raiding Muqan, al-Yabr and al-Taylasan until the Kurds of Azerbaijan sought peace on Hudhayfa's terms — eight hundred thousand dirhams. Salman b. Rabi'a al-Bahili was sent into Armenia with twelve thousand, killing, capturing and plundering; word came that the Maurians had set out with eighty thousand, and Habib b. Maslama resolved on a night attack. His wife Umm 'Abd Allah bint Yazid al-Kalbiyya asked "where is your appointment?" — "the Maurian's pavilion." He stormed the camp and found she had beaten him to it: the first woman to enter the Maurian's pavilion {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 457-458}.
