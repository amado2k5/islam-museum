## Overview
The end of the night story that began on p. 51: [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] sent a trusted man of [[place/saada|Sa'da]] to the place the sound came from; the man went with two servants, came back and said they were people holding a wedding and that it was not a place where drink was served. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.52} Al-Hadi turned to the narrator, who in the chain of the preceding pages is [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]], and swore that God would be worshipped truly if "the cushion were folded for him and the land made level", and that he would make the religion of Muhammad prevail. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.52} The book is Zaydi and the words are the imam's as his narrator gives them.

## A letter dictated
The narrator says he was with him one day while he dictated a letter to some people, in which he wrote: I call you to God, to rule by His Book and His Prophet's sunna, and to enjoining good and forbidding wrong; then he turned and asked "how many of creation will perish in this call of ours?" and said "O God, I call You to witness that I call them to what I declare and what I keep secret." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.52} The text does not name the recipients.

## On the community's good and why he went out
He heard him say that if he could buy the good of this community with all he owned he would do so. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.52} He also heard him swear by God and by the right of Muhammad that he had not sought this task and had not gone out by choice but under compulsion from the proof set against him; that he wished he had room to sit at home; that when night fell he would examine what he had done that day and argue with himself, and found "only going out or disbelief in what God sent down to Muhammad"; and that were it not for God's honour he would not have looked into this matter. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.52} The imam judges himself in these words; the museum attributes them and neither endorses nor rejects them. The editor records small variants between manuscripts in his notes. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.52}
