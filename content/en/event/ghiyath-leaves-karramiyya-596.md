## Summary
Ghiyath al-Din Muhammad al-Ghuri, lord of Ghazna and of part of Khurasan, left the Karramiyya school, which he had followed for a long time, and turned to the Sunni school. He then began ordering the Karramiyya to be killed and expelled from his lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 264}
