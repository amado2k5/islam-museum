## Overview
The first Day of al-Kullab was a battle in which the sons of al-Harith al-Kindi fought one another after his death, at the water of al-Kullab between Basra and Kufa. Ibn al-Athir relates it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 410-411}

## The cause of the fighting
When al-Harith ibn Amr died, the affair of his sons, the kings, was dispersed among the tribes of Mudar. Each of them gathered those with him and they marched against one another. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 410-411}

## The encounter
[[person/shurahbil-ibn-al-harith|Shurahbil]] came down with those with him of [[group/bakr-ibn-wail|Bakr ibn Wa'il]] and their allies. [[person/salama-ibn-al-harith-al-taghlibi|Salama]] came with those with him of [[group/banu-taghlib|Taghlib]] and the dependants, led by al-Saffah ibn Khalid. They fought a fierce battle, and each of them called out a hundred camels for whoever brought the head of the other. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 410-411}

## The end
Victory at the end of the day went to Taghlib and Salama. Dhu al-Sanina of Taghlib followed Shurahbil, who struck him on the knee and cut off his leg, so he died; then his brother Abu Hanash charged upon him, struck him and threw him from his horse, took his head and sent it to Salama. Ibn al-Athir says that remorse and grief were recognised in Salama's face. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 410-411}

The date is approximate.
