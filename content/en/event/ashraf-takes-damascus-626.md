## Overview
On Monday, 2 Sha'ban 626 AH, [[person/al-malik-al-ashraf|al-Malik al-Ashraf]] took Damascus from his nephew Dawud ibn al-Mu'azzam, according to Ibn al-Athir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482}

## The account
Dawud had sought help from his uncle al-Ashraf against his other uncle [[person/al-malik-al-kamil|al-Kamil]], but then al-Ashraf and al-Kamil made peace. al-Ashraf besieged Damascus until al-Kamil arrived. Dawud was pressed by shortage of money, and sold his wives' jewellery. He then went out to al-Kamil and offered to surrender Damascus, on condition that he keep al-Karak, al-Shawbak, al-Ghawr and Nablus, and that Aybak keep Salkhad. al-Kamil handed the citadel to al-Ashraf, after his brother had taken Harran, al-Ruha, al-Raqqa, Sarudj and Ras al-Ayn. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 482}
