## Overview
In 1752 Ahmad Shah Durrani forced the feeble Mughal emperor to cede to him the subas of Lahore and Multan {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## The Sikh wars
The following decades were dominated by warfare with the Sikhs, which culminated in Ranjit Singh's conquest of the city in 1818 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## From Durrani to British
Sikh rule stayed firm until Ranjit Singh's death in 1839; the British then occupied the city in 1848 and annexed it with the rest of the province {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
