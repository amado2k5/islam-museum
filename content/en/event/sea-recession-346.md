## Overview
In 346 the sea receded eighty cubits, revealing mountains and islands no one knew or had heard of; and in Dhu'l-Hijja word came of a great earthquake at Rayy and its environs in which many people died {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 670}.
