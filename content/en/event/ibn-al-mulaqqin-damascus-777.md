## Summary
In 777 Ibn al-Mulaqqin journeyed to Damascus, heard the last pupils of al-Fakhr Ibn al-Bukhari, gave fatwas and taught; the imams praised him as a hafiz and Taj al-Din al-Subki wrote a commendation on his Sharh al-Minhaj {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/44}.
