## The taking of Qalat al-Rum in 691 AH
In 691 AH Sultan al-Malik al-Ashraf Salah al-Din Khalil besieged Qalat al-Rum, which lies next to the fortress of al-Bira. Its people were Christians under Tatar rule. He set up catapults against it and pressed the siege until it was taken twenty-five days later, in Rajab {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 729}.

Al-Shihab Mahmud wrote a letter announcing the victory in a work he called "al-Fath" (The Conquest). One of his phrases in it says that the Thursday of Islam fell upon the people of Sunday on a Saturday, and God blessed the community in its Saturday and its Thursday {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 729}.
