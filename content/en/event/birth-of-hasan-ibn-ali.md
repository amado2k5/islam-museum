## Summary
[[person/hasan-ibn-ali|Al-Hasan ibn Ali]] was born in Medina in the middle of Ramadan in the third year of the Hijra, according to what the book mentions, and the Prophet named him Hasan after Ali had named him Harb {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 86}.

## Date
Al-Sallabi states that al-Hasan was born in the middle of Ramadan in the third year, while other views place it in Sha'ban, or in the fourth or fifth year, and notes that in his book al-Sira al-Nabawiyya he preferred the view that he was born in the fourth year {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 86}. Thus the date recorded in the museum is favored from al-Sira, while remaining marked as disputed.

## Naming and the Call to Prayer
Ali narrates that he named his son "Harb," and the Prophet came and said, "Show me my son; what have you named him?" He replied, "Harb," and the Prophet said, "Rather, he is Hasan" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 86}. Abu Rafi narrates that he saw the Prophet pronounce the call to prayer in al-Hasan's ear when Fatima gave birth to him {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 86}.

## The Aqiqa
Fatima said, "Shall I not offer two rams as an aqiqa for my son?" The Prophet said, "No, rather shave his head and give the weight of his hair in silver as charity to the needy and the people of al-Awfad," who were poor Companions of the Prophet in the mosque or al-Suffa {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 86}. Abu Rafi is the narrator here, as the book states.
