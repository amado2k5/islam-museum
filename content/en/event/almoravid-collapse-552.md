## Summary
In 552 AH the Mulaththimun (Almoravid) state in al-Andalus was extinguished; nothing remained to them but the island of Majorca {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}.

## Details
Under the events of 552 Ibn al-'Imad records that the state of the Mulaththimun — the Almoravids, so named for their face-veils — was extinguished in al-Andalus, leaving them nothing of their dominion but the island of Majorca, after their rule had flourished over the Maghreb and al-Andalus together {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}. In the same year Nur al-Din Mahmud ibn Zangi routed the Franks at Safad in a great battle, and took Gaza and Banyas from the Franks and ruled Shayzar from Ibn Munqidh {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}. It was a year full of the rise and fall of powers: while the Almoravid state was snuffed out in the west, Nur al-Din's emirate in Syria expanded at the expense of Franks and local princes alike {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 641}.
