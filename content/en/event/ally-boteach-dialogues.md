## Summary
The recorded Shabir Ally–Shmuley Boteach dialogues — the Muslim–Jewish debate in its modern academic form. {cite:youtube-sunni-jewish-debate}

## Themes
Covenant and finality, halakha and sharia, and the standing of prophethood — in a moderated dialogue, not a polemical platform.

## The exchange — what each side said
- **Ally**: the Qur'an completes and universalises revelation — Moses' message is national, Muhammad's universal, and Qur'anic law preserves the Torah's root while widening its reach.
- **Boteach**: the Mosaic covenant is eternal and particular to Israel — the Torah is a charge not repealed, and Halakha a self-standing order no later faith completes.
- **Ally**: compared the two laws — the permitted and forbidden in each system, and the place of the Prophet Muhammad in the Torah's promises.
- **Boteach**: defended the covenant's particularity, placing the Prophet Muhammad in a universal rather than covenantal frame.
- **The verdict**: a documented dialogue rather than a staged debate — the recorded exchange held to the question of scope: covenant particularity versus message universality. {cite:answering-islam-debates-index}
