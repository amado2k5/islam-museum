## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates from al-Dhahabi that Egypt in the days of the Fatimid caliph [[person/al-mustansir-fatimid|al-Mustansir]] was struck by a famine unlike any since the time of Joseph. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.644}

The famine lasted seven years, until people ate one another, according to the report; and it is said that a loaf was sold for fifty dinars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.644}

The date is approximate, around 457 AH, and needs an additional source.
