## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/tamim-ibn-zayd|Tamim ibn Zayd al-Atabi]] governed Sind after al-Junayd, grew weak and feeble, and died near [[place/daybul|Daybul]] by a water called the water of the buffaloes. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His generosity
Tamim was one of the generous Arabs. He found in the treasury of Sind eighteen million Tatariyya dirhams, and hastened to spend them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Leaving India
Al-Baladhuri says that in Tamim's days the Muslims left the lands of India and abandoned their posts, and did not return to them until al-Baladhuri's time. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The story of al-Farazdaq
Al-Baladhuri relates the story of a young man of the Banu Yarbu', called Khunays or Habish, who was in Tamim's army in India. He asked his mother to ask al-Farazdaq to write to Tamim for his return, and al-Farazdaq wrote verses to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
