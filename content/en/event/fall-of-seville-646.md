## Overview
The Castilians besieged [[place/seville|Seville]] in 645 AH. On Monday the fifth of Sha'ban 646 AH the king of Castile took it by treaty, after a siege of about a year and five months. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.476-477}

The date of the event in the Gregorian calendar is converted. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.477}
