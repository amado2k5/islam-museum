## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports from Abu Ubayda the account of [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Ibn Amir]] and the lands beyond the river, and his putting on the state of ihram in thanks to God. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
Ibn Amir conquered the lands on this side of the river. When news of him reached the lands beyond, they asked him to make peace with them, and he did. It is said that he crossed the river until he came to a place; it is also said that they came to him and he made peace. He sent men to collect what had been agreed, and beasts, slave-girls, silk and garments came to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The ihram
Then he put on the state of ihram in thanks to God, performed the umra, and came to Uthman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri notes that apart from Abu Ubayda, none mentioned his crossing of the river or his making peace with the people of the far bank. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
