## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] stayed at Raydah two days, and then [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] came to him with an army of Hamdan; when he came, al-Hadi marched until he halted at [[place/madar-yemen|Madar]] and stayed there some days {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. His brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] then came to him with about eighty men of Mudar and some Alids who had come with him from the Hijaz; he brought his women, settled them at Sa'da and joined al-Hadi {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. When he arrived al-Hadi told him to meet [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am ibn Ibrahim]] and ask his help against [[group/banu-tarif|Al Tarif]], as al-Da'am had promised {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224}. When Abdullah ibn al-Husayn met al-Da'am he turned him from the war on Banu Tarif, saying the men were in a host that they could not face; he excused himself from marching and went back on what he had given al-Hadi {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.224} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}. Abdullah ibn al-Husayn went back to al-Hadi and told him of al-Da'am's letting him down {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.225}.

## Detail
The clause that the scan prints as al-Hadi turning from the war on Banu Tarif does not read straight as printed, and the scan reads it the same; the museum understands it from the context as al-Da'am turning Abdullah from the war, since the next page speaks of al-Da'am's letting him down, so the reading is probable and not certain. The number of the Mudar men is the narrator's, about eighty. Abdullah's women are not named. No month or day is given, and the order of the events is that of p. 224, which follows the missing p. 223. It comes before [[event/al-hadi-routs-al-yafur-and-al-tarif-before-sanaa-288]].
