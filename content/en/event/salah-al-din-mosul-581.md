## Summary
In 581 AH Salah al-Din laid siege to Mosul. The daughter of the king Nur al-Din, who was married to the ruler of the city, came to serve him and submitted to him, but he sent her back empty-handed. He besieged Mosul, and its people gave their lives and fought fiercely, so he withdrew from them because of the city's strength {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748}.

## Mayyafariqin and the peace of Shahrazur
He then came down on Mayyafariqin and took it by safe conduct. He returned to Mosul and besieged it again, until a peace was agreed: its people would have the khutba read for him, its ruler would be under his command, and Salah al-Din would receive Shahrazur and its fortresses {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748}.

## Illness at Harran
He then moved on and fell ill, and his sickness grew severe at Harran. Rumours of his death spread, and his beard and hair fell out {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 748}.
