## Summary
The Prophet went out in the middle of one night to [[place/al-baqia|al-Baqi']], greeted the graveyard's people, asked forgiveness for them and gave them tidings that he would soon join them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 464}.

## The account
The Prophet went out in the middle of the night to al-Baqi', asked forgiveness for its people and said: peace upon you, people of the graves — may you rejoice in what you have come to, given what the people have come to {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 464; sahih-muslim}. He told them of coming trials like segments of dark night, the last of each worse than the first, and gave them tidings, saying: we shall surely join you {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 464}.

## For young visitors
On a quiet night the Prophet went to al-Baqi' cemetery, greeted its people, prayed for them and told them he would join them.
