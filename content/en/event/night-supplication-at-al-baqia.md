## Summary
The Prophet went out one night, in its middle, to [[place/al-baqia|al-Baqi']]. He greeted the people of the graves, asked forgiveness for them, and gave them tidings that he would soon join them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.464}

## The account
The Prophet went out in the middle of the night to al-Baqi', asked forgiveness for its people, and said: "Peace be upon you, people of the graves. May you be at ease in what you have come to, while the people are in what they are in." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.464; sahih-muslim} He warned them of coming trials like pieces of a dark night, where the last follows the first and the last is worse than the first, and gave them tidings, saying: "We shall surely join you." {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.464}

## For young visitors
On a quiet night the Prophet went to the Baqi' cemetery, greeted its people, prayed for them, and told them that he would join them.

## In Ibn Hisham's Sira
Ibn Ishaq reports that the Prophet woke [[person/abu-muwayhiba|Abu Muwayhiba]] in the middle of the night to accompany him to al-Baqi'. He asked forgiveness for its people, and then gave Abu Muwayhiba the choice between the keys of the world and eternity, or meeting God. He chose to meet his Lord. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/291} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/292}
