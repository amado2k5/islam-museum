## Summary
In 511 AH a great flood struck Sinjar. It destroyed its walls and drowned many people {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 510}.

## Traces of the flood
The flood carried the city gate half a day's journey away, and buried the city for years. It also carried a cot with a child in it, which caught on an olive tree. The child survived and grew up {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 510}.
