## Overview
Al-Hadi stands with the sellers of each goods, orders them to stop adulteration, and when asked about price-fixing answers that the prohibition applies to honest and pious traders.

## Detail
He stood before the sellers of every kind of goods and ordered them not to adulterate their wares, to clean them of adulteration, to state clearly what they sell and to keep the measures they promise. They asked him whether price-fixing was not forbidden. He answered that injustice and adulteration are forbidden, and they agreed. He said that the prohibition of price-fixing applies to people of honesty and piety, and that when injustice shows itself in sales, those who are close to God must forbid all corruption and restrain the unjust man in his injustice. The record gives only this answer. The wider juristic debate on price-fixing is not summarised here.
