## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that Mu'awiya appointed [[person/said-ibn-uthman|Sa'id ibn Uthman ibn Affan]] over Khurasan, and he crossed the river, the first to do so with his troops. [[person/rufay-abu-al-aliya|Rufay Abu al-Aliya al-Riyahi]] was with him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The meeting at Bukhara
When [[person/khatun-of-bukhara|Khatun]] learned that he had crossed the river, she sent him a peace offer. Then the Soghdians, the Turks, and the people of Kush and Nasaf came against Sa'id with a hundred and twenty thousand, and they met at [[place/bukhara|Bukhara]]. Khatun had come to regret paying the tribute and broke the covenant. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

A slave of Sa'id's withdrew with some of those in the gatherings, and the rest were broken. Khatun gave the pledge and restored the peace, and Sa'id entered the city of Bukhara. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The figure of one hundred and twenty thousand is as al-Baladhuri relates it, and needs an additional source.
