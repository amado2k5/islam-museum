## Summary
The Mubahala of Najran is the most famous debate between Islam and Christianity in the Prophet's lifetime: an episcopal delegation from [[place/najran|Najran]] argued with the Prophet over the divinity of Jesus, the Qur'an came down with the counter-argument, the two parties were summoned to a mutual imprecation, and the Christians declined and made peace instead. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/573-577}

## Background to the delegation
In 10 AH a delegation of the Christians of Najran arrived, about sixty riders including al-Aqib, al-Sayyid and their bishop Abu Haritha ibn Alqama, master of their school. They lodged in the mosque and prayed toward the east, and the Messenger of God left them to it; then they argued with him about Jesus, saying he was the son of God and that they knew no father for him {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/573-574}. God then sent down some eighty-odd verses from the opening of Surat Al Imran concerning them and those who argue as they did; Ibn Ishaq says they contain their refutation and the exposition of their error {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/574-575}.

## Proof for proof
The heart of the debate was that the Christians argued for the divinity of Jesus from his having no father. The Qur'anic answer turned the argument on its holders: the greatest case of creation without a father and mother is Adam, and no one said that of him — 'the likeness of Jesus before God is as the likeness of Adam; He created him from dust, then said to him "Be," and he was.' If being fatherless proved divinity, Adam would have the better claim {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/575}.

## The imprecation
When they would not desist, the Prophet called them to gather so that both parties might pray earnestly and lay the curse of God on the liars. On the appointed day he came out carrying [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|al-Hasan]], holding [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]]'s hand, with [[person/fatima|Fatima]] walking behind him and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] behind her. When al-Aqib saw them he said to his companions: if we imprecate against them we will be destroyed — had there been other than truth in his heart he would not have brought out these people {cite:sahih-muslim 2404} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya 5/44}. They said: Abu al-Qasim, we have decided not to imprecate you, to leave you on your religion and return on ours, and they asked him for a trustworthy man to send with them to divide their goods — it was [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]] {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/576-577}.

## The settlement and after
He made peace with them on two thousand garments a year and whatever arms they agreed on, and the Christians of Najran remained secure until Umar evacuated those who stayed to the Najraniyya land in Iraq opposite Kufa {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya 5/45}. Sahih Muslim records that when the verse of the mubahala came down the Prophet called Ali, Fatima, Hasan and Husayn and said: 'O God, these are my family' {cite:sahih-muslim 2404}.
