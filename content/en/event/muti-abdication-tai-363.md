## Overview
In 363 AH the caliph [[person/al-muti-billah|al-Muti' li-Allah]] was struck by paralysis and his tongue grew heavy, and the chamberlain of [[person/subuktakin|Subuktakin]], lord of Izz al-Dawla, urged him to depose himself and hand the matter to his son [[person/al-tai-billah|al-Ta'i li-Allah]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.624}

## The deposition
Al-Muti' did so, and the matter was given to al-Ta'i on Wednesday, the thirteenth of Dhu al-Qa'da. The reign of al-Muti' had lasted twenty-nine years and some months. The judge Ibn Umm Shayban confirmed the deposition, and afterwards he was called "the honourable shaykh". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.624}

## The accounts
Ibn Shahin said that, as far as he could establish, he deposed himself without compulsion. He went out to Wasit with his son and died in Muharram 364 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.624}

Al-Dhahabi said that al-Muti' and his son were weakened under the Buyids, and that the affairs of the caliphs remained weak until al-Muqtafi became caliph. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.624}
