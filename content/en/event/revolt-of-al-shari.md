## Overview
Abu al-Amud al-Shari rose in the region of Yemen, al-Bawazij and Mosul in the days of al-Muntasir, with those of the Muhakkima of Rabi'a and the Kurds who joined him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 520-521}

## The end
Al-Muntasir sent him an army under the Turk Sama, who captured him and brought him to the caliph. Al-Muntasir pardoned him, took a pledge from him and released him. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 520-521}

The date recorded in the data is 248 AH.
