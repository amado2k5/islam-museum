## Overview
al-Maqdisi, in "Ahsan al-Taqasim", relates that an earthquake in the Abbasid state brought down the roofed part of the al-Aqsa Mosque, except what lay around the mihrab. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.143}

## The rebuilding
The news reached the caliph, and he was told that the treasury of the Muslims could not cover the cost of rebuilding it. He wrote to the governors of the provinces and to the commanders that each of them should build one portico. They built it more solidly and more substantially than before. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.143}

al-Maqdisi adds that this section remained a visible mark there up to the marble columns, and that [[person/abd-allah-ibn-tahir|Abd Allah ibn Tahir]] added porticos on marble columns to it. {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p.143}

## Note
al-Maqdisi gives neither the year of the earthquake nor the name of the caliph. It needs an additional source.
