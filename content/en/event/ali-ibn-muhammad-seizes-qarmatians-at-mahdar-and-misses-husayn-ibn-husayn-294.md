## Overview
Al-Hadi ordered Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah to seek out the Qarmatians. His sons Ali and al-Qasim rode with a hundred men; Ali took fifteen Qarmatians at Mahdar, and al-Qasim did not find their missionary at his house in Rijla.

## Detail
The book says Ali went to a place called Mahdar, where those ascribed to the Qarmatians were. Their missionary was Husayn ibn Husayn of Hashid, a client of Banu Umayya, who lodged in the village of Rijla in Najran. Al-Qasim surrounded his house but did not find him. Ali took fifteen Qarmatians, and the two brothers returned to al-Hadi, who had already taken some of them. The editor's footnote dates the Qarmatian missions in Yemen to 291 AH and cites sources that were not checked for this record.
