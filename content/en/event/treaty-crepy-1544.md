## Summary
Treaty between [[person/charles-v|Charles V]] and the king of France at Crépy-en-Laonnois, 18 September 1544 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 232}.

## Context
Al-Salabi ties it to Charles's disaster before Algiers and the pressure of the Protestant rising in Germany: he had attacked north-eastern France, been repulsed under Châteaudun's walls, and had to leave for Germany {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 232}.

## Outcome
By it [[person/khayr-al-din-barbarossa|Khayr al-Din]]'s forces evacuated Toulon and returned to Istanbul, carrying Elba and the coastal towns' booty on the way {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 232}.
