When [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] reached twelve years of age, his uncle [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]] set out trading to al-Sham; parting from his nephew was too hard for him, so he took him along, until when they halted at [[place/busra|Busra]] in the land of al-Sham a monk named [[person/bahira|Bahira]] — whose name, in the author, is Jirjis — came out to them, made them food and honoured them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

When he examined them he recognised in the boy a mark found in their books — the seal of prophethood between his shoulders, as they read of it — and ordered that they take him back and not bring him into al-Sham, fearing the Jews for him; so Abu Talib sent him back to Mecca with some of his servants. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

The tradition relates that Bahira asked him about certain matters and found him as written in their books; this story belongs to the reports of the unseen that the sira writers transmit. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
