## Overview
After several journeys in the Peninsula fi talab al-'ilm, Ibn al-Abbar acted as secretary to the Mu'minid (Ibn Mardanish's) governors of Valencia in the last years of local Quda'i rule, before their government fell with the city in 636/1238 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 705}.
