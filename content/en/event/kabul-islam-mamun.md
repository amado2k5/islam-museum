## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Kabul was conquered and its king showed Islam and obedience in the days of al-Ma'mun in Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
The governors of al-Mahdi and al-Rashid had collected tribute from Ratbil of Sistan in proportion to their strength and weakness. When al-Ma'mun was in Khurasan the tribute was paid to him doubled, and Kabul was conquered. His governor entered it and the post reached it, so he sent him fresh myrobalan from it. Then things were set right for a time. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
