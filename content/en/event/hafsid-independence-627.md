## Overview
Ibn Khaldun, in "The Lessons" (al-Ibar), reports that in 627 AH the amir [[person/abu-zakariya-al-hafsi|Abu Zakariya al-Hafsi]] took sole power in Ifriqiya and broke the obedience to the caliph al-Ma'mun. Al-Ma'mun had erased the name of al-Mahdi from the coinage and the sermon. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Thus the Hafsid state was founded in [[place/tunis|Tunis]] by the Hafsids of the Hintata, descendants of the chief of the Almohad shaykhs. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
