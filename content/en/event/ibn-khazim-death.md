## Overview
[[person/abdallah-ibn-khazim-al-sulami|Abd Allah ibn Khazim al-Sulami]] was killed near Merv in Khurasan, according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The dispute with Tamim
Ibn Khazim appointed his son Muhammad over Herat, and the Banu Tamim rose up and killed him. His father seized Uthman ibn Bashir ibn al-Muhtafiz and killed him by confinement, and killed a man of Tamim. Tamim then plotted to depose him, and a group of them went over with [[person/bujayr-ibn-waraqa|Bujayr ibn Waraqa]] to Abrashahr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Abd al-Malik's letter
A letter from Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan came to him appointing him over Khurasan, and he fed the letter to his messenger. He said he would not meet God having broken the pledge to the disciple of the Messenger of God's apostle and pledged to the son of the one he had banished. So Abd al-Malik wrote his appointment to [[person/bukayr-ibn-wishah|Bukayr ibn Wishah]], who had deposed Ibn Khazim and called the people of Merv to the oath. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
Ibn Khazim went toward his son Musa at Tirmidh; Bujayr followed and fought him. [[person/waki-ibn-al-dawraqiyya|Waki ibn al-Dawraqiyya]] came out and they stabbed him, and Waki sat on his chest seeking vengeance for his maternal brother Duwayla. His two sons Anbasa and Yahya were killed with him. Bukayr brought his head, which was sent to Abd al-Malik and displayed in Damascus. His right hand was cut off and sent to the sons of Uthman ibn Bashir. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
