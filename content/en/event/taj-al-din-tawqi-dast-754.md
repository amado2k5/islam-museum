## Overview
In 754 Taj al-Din al-Subki held the tawqi' al-dast in Damascus; he had already been appointed by his father to the Dar al-Hadith al-Ashrafiyya, held the preachership of the mosque, and deputised for his father in judgment {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425}.
