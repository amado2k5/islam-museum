## Summary
In 350 AH Mu'izz al-Dawla al-Buwayhi built the Dar al-Sultana in Baghdad, a palace of great beauty and size {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 2}.

## Cost and grandeur
It cost 13 million dirhams, and its foundations were dug 33 cubits deep {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 2}.

## After centuries
By about 600 AH its traces had been erased. Its site became a river bed where wild animals took shelter, and part of its foundation still survives {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 2}.
