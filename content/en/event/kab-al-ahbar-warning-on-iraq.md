## The story
It reached Malik that Umar ibn al-Khattab intended to go out to Iraq, and Ka'b al-Ahbar said to him: do not go out to it, Commander of the Faithful, for nine-tenths of sorcery is in it, and in it are the wicked of the jinn and the incurable disease. The muhaqqiq holds the report is munqati' and does not stand, though if it does stand, Ka'b transmitted it from the books of the Jews {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2795|Muwatta 31:2795]].

## The meaning
This report has a broken isnad in the Muwatta's muhaqqiq, and Ka'b may have transmitted it from the books of the People of the Book; still, it is among the texts that made Iraq in Muslim consciousness a land of sorcery and wicked jinn {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
