## Summary
After returning from the conquest campaign the Prophet stayed in Medina receiving delegations, sending officials and dispatching callers; no sooner had the new moon of Muharram 9 AH risen than he sent the sadaqa collectors to gather alms from the tribes {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 424}.

## The list of collectors
The Prophet sent Uyayna ibn Hisn to Banu Tamim, Yazid ibn al-Husayn to Aslam and Ghifar, Abbad ibn Bishr al-Ashhali to Sulaym and Muzayna, Rafi' ibn Makith to Juhayna, Amr ibn al-As to Banu Fazara, al-Dahhak ibn Sufyan to Banu Kilab, Bashir ibn Sufyan to Banu Ka'b, Ibn al-Lutbiyya al-Azdi to Banu Dhubyan, al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya to San'a', and Ziyad ibn Labid to Hadramawt {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 424}.

He sent [[person/adi-ibn-hatim|Adi ibn Hatim]] to Tayy and Banu Asad, Malik ibn Nuwayra to Banu Hanzala, al-Zibriqan ibn Badr and Qays ibn Asim to Banu Sa'd, [[person/al-ala-ibn-al-hadrami|al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami]] to al-Bahrayn, and Ali ibn Abi Talib to Najran to collect both sadaqa and jizya {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}.

Not all these officials were sent in Muharram; the dispatch of several waited on their tribes' embracing Islam. This extraordinary attention shows how far the Islamic call had succeeded after the truce of al-Hudaybiyya and then the conquest of Mecca, after which people entered God's religion in throngs {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 425}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet sent men to gather the alms of every tribe to the Muslims' treasury in Medina, to be spent on good works; for each tribe he chose a man who knew it or was one of its own.
