## Summary
After the Prophet returned from the campaign of the Conquest (Fath), he stayed in Medina receiving delegations, sending out officials and dispatching preachers. As soon as the new moon of Muharram 9 AH appeared, he sent out the collectors of alms (musaddiqun) to the tribes {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.424}.

## The list of collectors
The Prophet sent Uyayna ibn Hisn to Banu Tamim; Yazid ibn al-Husayn to Aslam and Ghifar; Abbad ibn Bishr al-Ashhali to Sulaym and Muzayna; Rafi' ibn Makith to Juhayna; Amr ibn al-As to Banu Fazara; al-Dahhak ibn Sufyan to Banu Kilab; Bashir ibn Sufyan to Banu Ka'b; Ibn al-Lutbiyya al-Azdi to Banu Dhubyan; al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya to San'a'; and Ziyad ibn Labid to Hadramawt {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.424}.

He sent [[person/adi-ibn-hatim|Adi ibn Hatim]] to Tayy and Banu Asad, Malik ibn Nuwayra to Banu Hanzala, al-Zibriqan ibn Badr and Qays ibn Asim to Banu Sa'd, [[person/al-ala-ibn-al-hadrami|al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami]] to al-Bahrayn, and Ali ibn Abi Talib to Najran to collect both the alms and the jizya {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.425}.

Not all of these officials were sent in Muharram. The dispatch of several of them was delayed until their tribes embraced Islam. This close attention shows how far the Islamic call had succeeded after the truce of al-Hudaybiyya and then the conquest of Mecca, after which people entered God's religion in throngs {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.425}.

## Ibn Ishaq's account
After the delegations, Ibn Ishaq mentions that the Prophet sent his commanders and officials for the alms to every land that Islam had reached. He sent [[person/al-muhajir-ibn-abi-umayya|al-Muhajir ibn Abi Umayya ibn al-Mughira]] to San'a', where al-Ansi rose against him while he was there. He sent [[person/ziyad-ibn-labid-al-bayadi|Ziyad ibn Labid]], brother of Banu Bayada of the Ansar, to Hadramawt and its alms. He sent [[person/adi-ibn-hatim|Adi ibn Hatim]] over the alms of Tayy and over Banu Asad. He sent [[person/malik-ibn-nuwayra|Malik ibn Nuwayra]], whom Ibn Hisham calls al-Yarbu'i, over the alms of Banu Hanzala. He divided the alms of Banu Sa'd between two men: [[person/al-zibriqan-ibn-badr|al-Zibriqan ibn Badr]] for one district and [[person/qays-ibn-asim|Qays ibn Asim]] for another. He had sent [[person/al-ala-ibn-al-hadrami|al-Ala ibn al-Hadrami]] to al-Bahrayn, and he sent [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] to the people of Najran to collect their alms and bring back their jizya {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/247}. Ibn Ishaq's list is shorter than the list in the Sealed Nectar, and it does not mention a month {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/246}.

## For young visitors
The Prophet sent men to gather the alms of each tribe for the Muslims' treasury in Medina, to be spent on good causes. For each tribe he chose a man who knew it or was one of its own.

## For Sprouts
The Prophet sent men to gather the sadaqa from every tribe to the Muslims' treasury in Medina, choosing for each tribe a man who knew it or was of its people.
