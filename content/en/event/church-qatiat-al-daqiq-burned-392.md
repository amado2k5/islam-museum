## Summary
On Monday, 7 Rabi' II of the year two and ninety and three hundred (392), the common people of Baghdad rose against the Christians, looted the church at Qati'at al-Daqiq and set it on fire; it collapsed on a crowd of Muslims — men, boys and women — who perished {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.219}.

## The year's unrest
The incident comes in al-Muntazam under the events of 392, a year thick with disorder in Baghdad: in Ramadan the strife grew and outrages and harlotry spread; the Khurasani forces halted on the road west for want of safe passage and the Hajj from the east was suspended that year {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.219}.

## For young visitors
In old Baghdad a mob attacked the Christians and burned their church in the quarter called Qati'at al-Daqiq; its roof fell on Muslim bystanders too, and they died — one of the troubles the chroniclers list for that year.
