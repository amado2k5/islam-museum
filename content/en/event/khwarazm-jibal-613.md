## Summary
Ala al-Din Muhammad Khwarazmshah marched into the Jibal lands and took them, captured Atabeg Sa'd ibn Dikla, lord of Fars, then honored and released him on terms dividing the lands between them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 371}

## Details
Under the events of 614 AH, Ibn al-Athir reports that Khwarazmshah marched into the Jibal and took it; the causes of his move were that he had conquered Transoxiana, defeated the Khita and grown mighty, that he longed to be proclaimed in the khutba at Baghdad and titled Sultan — which was refused, and it angered him that whenever his envoy reached Baghdad others were given precedence — and that Aghlamish, who had taken the Jibal lands and had the khutba said for Khwarazmshah, had been killed by the Batinis, so he marched lest the lands slip from obedience; he came swiftly with earth-covering armies and took Rayy. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 371} Meanwhile Atabeg Sa'd ibn Dikla, lord of Fars, hearing of Aghlamish's death, gathered his armies and marched on the Jibal hoping to take it undefended; he reached Isfahan, whose people obeyed him, and advanced on Rayy unaware of Khwarazmshah's arrival; he met the vanguard, took it for local troops, fought and nearly defeated it, then Khwarazmshah's standard appeared — he asked and was told, surrendered as his army broke, and was brought captive to Khwarazmshah, who honored him, promised kindness, secured his life and made him swear obedience; they agreed he would surrender part of the lands and keep part, and he was released with an army to Fars to hand over what was agreed. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 371}
