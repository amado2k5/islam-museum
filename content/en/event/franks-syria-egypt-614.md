## Summary
In 614 AH the seaborne reinforcements of the Franks arrived off Syria — the opening of the Damietta affair, which lasted from beginning to end four years less a month. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 373}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir records that the affair of the Franks' appearance in Syria, their march to the lands of Egypt, their seizure of the city of Damietta and its return to Muslim hands lasted four years less a month from first to last; he places it here because their appearance fell in this year, and narrates it continuously so that each part follows the next. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 373} In this year the Franks' reinforcements arrived by sea from Greater Romania and other Frankish lands in the west and north, and the one in charge was the lord of Romania, who was held in great esteem among the Franks. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 373}
