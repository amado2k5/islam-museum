## Overview
On Wednesday Ibn Humayd led all of Banu al-Harith forward, and the fighting at the Darb gate became general. They broke down part of the village wall, and the fighting moved to the breach. Ahmad ibn Abdallah distinguished himself there. Abu Ja'far then charged with a group of his servants and drove the enemy back from the wall; after they withdrew, the defenders rebuilt what had been broken.

## Detail
On Wednesday Ibn Humayd led all of Banu al-Harith forward until the fighting at the Darb gate became general. They fought for an hour of the day, men were wounded, and they broke down part of the village wall, so the fighting moved to the breached side, where it was fiercest. Ahmad ibn Abdallah distinguished himself there, and the fighting went on until sunset. The book gives an account from al-Husayn ibn Ahmad al-Ba'dani and Abdallah ibn Munir al-Muzawi, who said that they saw the swords clash between their side and the enemy until the wall was broken by the blows, and that the enemy had hoped to enter. Abu Ja'far came with a group of his servants, and when they saw him they charged the enemy and drove them from the broken wall, so that the two sides faced one another at the Darb gate where the fighting had begun at dawn; they exchanged shots for a while, then the enemy withdrew to their camp. They did not leave until they had rebuilt what had been broken. The book also describes Ahmad ibn Abdallah as having taken men of Banu Amir from Quraysh; that phrase is ambiguous in the scanned text and is not recorded here.
