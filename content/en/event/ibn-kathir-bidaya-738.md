## Summary
Ibn Kathir compiled the Tafsir, the history and the Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya; he began a large book of ahkam left unfinished and a commentary on al-Bukhari; he critiqued the hadiths of Adillat al-Tanbih and of Ibn al-Hajib's Mukhtasar; and he abridged Ibn al-Salah's 'ulum al-hadith with additions of his own {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
## Al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya and the rest
Al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya is a history on the model of Ibn al-Athir's al-Kamil which he concluded with the events of 738; his books include the eight-volume Jami' al-Masanid, al-Ijtihad fi talab al-jihad and the five-volume Takmil on rijal — his works travelled the lands in his lifetime {cite:al-aalam 1/112-113} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
