## Overview
The caliph [[person/al-mustansir-billah-egypt|al-Mustansir]] resolved to go to Iraq. Sultan [[person/baybars|Baybars]] went out with him to escort him as far as Damascus, and equipped him and the sons of the lord of Mosul with about a million dinars and sixty thousand dirhams. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.724}

## The route
The caliph set out with the kings of the East: the lord of Mosul, the lord of Sinjar and al-Jazira. The Aleppo caliph [[person/al-hakim-bi-amr-illah|al-Hakim]], whom the amir of Aleppo, Aqush, had set up, met him and submitted to him. The army took al-Hadītha and then Hit. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.724}

## Clash with the Tatars
A Tatar force met them, and some Muslims were killed, and al-Mustansir disappeared on the third of Muharram 660 AH. It is said he was killed, and it is said he escaped and fled. His caliphate lasted less than six months. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.724}

The "Shadharat al-Dhahab" says that he set out to take Baghdad, and that he disappeared in the battle and al-Hakim was routed. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.296}
