## Overview
Abu Dawud died on the sixteenth of Shawwal in the year two hundred and seventy-five {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 362}, February of 889 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 133}, at Basra, the city he had made his home.

## What was said of him
Musa ibn Harun said: Abu Dawud was created in this world for hadith and in the hereafter for Paradise; and Zakariyya al-Saji said: the Book of God is the foundation of Islam, and the book of Abu Dawud is its covenant {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 360}. His Sunan was transmitted by Abu 'Ali al-Lu'lu'i, Abu Bakr ibn Dasa, Abu Sa'id ibn al-A'rabi and others, al-Lu'lu'i's recension becoming the most favoured {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 359} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 133}.
