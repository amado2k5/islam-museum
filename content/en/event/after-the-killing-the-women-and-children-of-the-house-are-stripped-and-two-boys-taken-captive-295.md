## Overview
After the killing, the men of Banu al-Harith stripped the women and children of the house and wounded some of them. Two boys were taken captive to Mansur ibn Hisham al-Dahmi, and a six-year-old grandson of Abu Ja'far was struck when he threw himself over his grandfather's body.

## Detail
The narrator says that Banu al-Harith put their swords into the women and children. They stripped the women of their clothes and goods until they were left without covering, and each woman was struck by a blade or an arrow. They took a six-month-old boy to dash against a wall, but his mother caught up with him after he had been dragged along the ground and took him back. The grandson of Abu Ja'far named al-Hasan, six years old, threw himself over his grandfather and was struck down and fell unconscious. Two boys of the house, aged five and four, were taken to the house of Mansur ibn Hisham al-Dahmi, who gave them dates, food and water. The elder boy told Mansur that he did not doubt God would destroy them through al-Hadi. Mansur struck him, and the boy threw a handful of dust at Mansur. The curses the narrator uses are not repeated here.
