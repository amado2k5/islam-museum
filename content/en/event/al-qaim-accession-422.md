## Summary
On the night of Monday, 11 Dhu al-Hijja 422 AH, the caliph [[person/al-qadir-billah|al-Qadir billah]] died at the age of eighty-six, after a caliphate of forty-one years, a length no caliph before him had reached. His son [[person/al-qaim-billah|al-Qa'im bi-amr Allah]] came out at the time of the afternoon prayer, led the people in the sunset prayer, then prayed the funeral prayer over his father, and sat at Dar al-Shajara, where the people gave him the oath of allegiance. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.399-400}

## The first oath of allegiance
The first to give the oath was [[person/al-sharif-al-murtada|al-Sharif al-Murtada]], naqib of the Talibids (the Alid notables). The next day Abu al-Hasan ibn Isa ibn al-Muqtadir came and gave the oath. The form of the pledge continued as acceptance of his imamate and commitment to the conditions of obeying him. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.400}

## Oath money and the caliph's conduct
The guards rioted over the oath money, until a Hashimi was killed by a Turk. It was agreed that three million dinars would be paid to them. Ibn al-Jawzi relates that al-Qa'im offered a caravanserai, an orchard and a heap of goods for sale to pay it, one of the scenes of asceticism that marked his conduct. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.402-403}
