## Summary
An armed clash on the morning of the conquest day at Mount [[place/al-khandama|al-Khandama]] in Mecca, when a faction of Quraysh and Banu Bakr fighters under Safwan, Ikrima and Suhayl tried to resist the entry of [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]]'s column; the skirmish ended quickly in the resisters' defeat, about twelve of them killed and three Muslims martyred {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/49-50}.

## What happened
The fighters gathered at al-Khandama and prepared their arms; among them was [[person/himas-ibn-qays|Himas ibn Qays al-Dayli]], who had threatened the Prophet's Companions before the conquest {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/49}. When they met Khalid ibn al-Walid's column there was an exchange of arrows and a little fighting {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

Two horsemen strayed from Khalid's army — [[person/kurz-ibn-jabir-al-fihri|Kurz ibn Jabir al-Fihri]] and [[person/khunays-ibn-khalid|Khunays ibn Khalid al-Khuza'i]] — who took another road, met the fighters, and Khunays was killed first while Kurz stood fighting for him, chanting verses until he too was killed; [[person/salama-ibn-al-mayla|Salama ibn al-Mayla' al-Juhani]] was martyred with them {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

The resisters broke quickly: about twelve or thirteen of them were killed and the rest fled; Himas ibn Qays went back to his house in flight {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/50}.

## For young visitors
A small group of Quraysh tried to fight the Muslims' cavalry at Mount al-Khandama, but the fighting ended quickly and they fled to their houses, and the army entered Mecca in general peace.
