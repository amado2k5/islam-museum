## Summary
In the year 39 AH Muʿawiya dispatched ʿAbd Allah ibn Masʿada al-Fazari to Tayma. Al-Dahhak ibn Qays had instructed him to pass below Waqisa and raid whoever passed by from Iraq who was not under obedience. Muʿawiya himself marched until he came near the Tigris, and then turned back. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 725-726}.
