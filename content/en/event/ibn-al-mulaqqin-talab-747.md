## Summary
Ibn al-Mulaqqin sought hadith on his own account, hearing the hafizes of his age such as Ibn 'Abd al-Da'im, and graduated under Zayn al-Din al-Rahbi and Mughaltay, copying much from them and collecting the ajza' and the great books {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 dhayl pp. 121-122}.
