## Overview
Al-Suyuti died in his house on Rawda island on 19 Jumada I 911 (18 October 1505). His scholarly reputation and the aura of godliness around him reached their zenith: people bought his clothes as if they were relics, and he left behind more than five hundred works {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 468-470}.
