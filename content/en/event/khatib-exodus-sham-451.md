## Summary
After the Basasiri fitna, in 451, he left Baghdad for al-Sham as conditions fell into turmoil and stayed until Safar 457 {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 92}; Ibn al-Jawzi says he went into hiding then left for Damascus, Tyre, Tripoli and Aleppo {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 pp. 265-267}.
