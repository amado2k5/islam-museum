## Summary
When Abu Mansur reached Isfarayin its people rejoiced at his coming beyond description, but he stayed only a little while before dying in 429; the people of learning agreed to bury him beside the master Abu Ishaq, and their two graves stand side by side {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki pp. 238-239} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 265}; Ibn al-Najjar's history gives 427, which al-Subki calls a scribal error {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p. 239}, and Ibn Shakir transmits 420 {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 635}.
