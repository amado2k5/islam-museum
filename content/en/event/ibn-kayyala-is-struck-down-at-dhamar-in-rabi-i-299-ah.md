## Overview
Ibn Kayyala is struck down at Dhamar on Tuesday, ten nights into Rabi' I 299 AH. Asad holds the city, sends his governors to the districts and stays at Dhamar.

## Detail
The book uses the passive 'was struck' for Ibn Kayyala at Dhamar on Tuesday, ten nights into Rabi' I 299 AH. The word can mean killed or wounded, and the same sentence carries an invocation against him that this museum does not reproduce. Asad stays in the city, sends his governors to the districts and remains at Dhamar. The same page says Sanaa was empty during this period and that the judge Abd al-Ala ibn Muhammad came to hold the preaching and the prayers.
