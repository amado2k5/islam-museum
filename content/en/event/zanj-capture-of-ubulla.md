## Overview
In 256 AH the Zanj entered Ubulla in Rajab. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.471}

Then the people of Abadan surrendered to the lord of the Zanj. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.472}

## In al-Ya'qubi
[[person/al-yaqubi|Al-Ya'qubi]], early in his account of al-Mu'tamid's caliphate, says the rebel at Basra, whom he calls 'the man who claimed descent from the family of Abu Talib' and names Ali ibn Muhammad, advanced on al-Ubulla, plundered it, laid it waste and burned it, and that Sa'id ibn Salih marched against him and fought him at Nahr Abi al-Khasib. {cite:tarikh-yaqubi-houtsma-1883 vol. 2 p. 620}
