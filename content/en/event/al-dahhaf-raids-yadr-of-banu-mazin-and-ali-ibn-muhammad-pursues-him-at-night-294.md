## Overview
On the night of 15 Ramadan, al-Dahhaf ibn Musa al-Amri of Banu al-Harith raided Yadr of Banu Mazin. Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah wanted him, so Ali ibn Muhammad set out at night with his brother and a cousin and reached al-Sayrim at dawn.

## Detail
The book says that on the night of 15 Ramadan al-Dahhaf ibn Musa al-Amri of Banu al-Harith raided Yadr of Banu Mazin in Najran; Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah was looking for him. He asked the people of Sirr for food and to spend the night, they refused and drove him off, and he fled. Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah heard of it and sent his son Ali with his brother al-Qasim and his cousin Ibrahim ibn Muhsin in pursuit. They found a group of Banu al-Harith at the lower end of Najran, and al-Dahhaf had gone to al-Sayrim, a thick grove. Ali camped at al-Hazura before dawn, prayed, placed his brother on the right wing and Sa'id ibn Abi Sawra on the left, and at morning fell on al-Sayrim, where the men of Banu al-Harith were stripped of their weapons and goods.
