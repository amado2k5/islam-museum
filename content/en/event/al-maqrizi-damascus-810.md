## Overview
Around 810/1408 al-Maqrizi moved to Damascus and spent about ten years there. He taught at the Iqbaliyya and administered the endowments of the Qalanisiyya and the great Nuri hospital — a post legally reserved for the Shafi'i judge of Damascus. That judgeship itself was offered to him by al-Nasir ibn Barquq, but he refused it. In Syria he seems to have resolved to give up an unsatisfying public career and devote himself wholly to historical scholarship {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 6 p. 213} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 255}.
