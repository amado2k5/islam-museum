## Summary
The Ottoman entry into Fez on 3 Safar 961/8 January 1554 (the printed text reads "964") {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 238-239}.

## The road to Fez
After the Sa'di defeat near Badis, the fall of Taza and the battle of Kadiyat al-Makhali before the city, the Ottoman forces with [[person/abu-hasun-al-wattasi|Bu Hasun]] entered Fez; the imam preached in the Ottoman sultan's name and the Porte declared Morocco annexed {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 238-239}.

## Four months
[[person/salih-rais|Salih Reis]] stayed four months securing Ottoman affairs and recovering Badis from Spain, before Fez later returned to the Sa'dis {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 239-241}.
