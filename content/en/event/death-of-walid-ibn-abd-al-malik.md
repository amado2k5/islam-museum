## Overview
The Umayyad caliph al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik died in the middle of Jumada al-Akhira 96 AH, on Saturday, according to al-Sallabi. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.140-141}

## The length of his caliphate and his age
al-Tabari gives different accounts of the length of his caliphate and his age. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.6 p.495} al-Suyuti says that he died aged fifty-one. He quotes al-Dhahabi that jihad lived in his days, and that great conquests were made in them as in the days of Umar ibn al-Khattab. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.369}

## The question of succession
al-Sallabi says that al-Walid wanted to depose his brother Sulayman and appoint his son Abd al-Aziz, but Sulayman refused. Only al-Hajjaj, Qutayba and close associates gave the oath to Abd al-Aziz, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz refused to give it. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.140-141}
