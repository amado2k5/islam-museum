## Summary
When the leprous king of the Franks died, the kingship passed to his mother. She married Guy and placed the crown on his head, with the testimony of the patriarch and the notables. This weighed heavily on Count Raymond, lord of Tripoli, who had been guardian of her sister's son and was eager for the kingship. When he was asked for the revenues of the boy's guardianship, he openly broke away and corresponded with Saladin, promising him the whole kingdom of the Franks, and he released his knights. Their word divided and their unity scattered, and this was among the greatest causes of the conquest of Jerusalem. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 141-142}
