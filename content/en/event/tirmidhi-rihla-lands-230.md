## Overview
Al-Tirmidhi ranged on his scholarly journey through the lands of Khurasan, Iraq and the Hijaz, hearing and gathering copiously {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 450}. He travelled widely in search of traditions through Khurasan, Iraq and the Hijaz {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 579}.

## His teachers
He apprenticed under al-Bukhari, Muslim and Abu Dawud al-Sijistani and their peers, his most important masters being men of the two Shaykhs' layer and elders they had reached {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 450-451}. He shared nine shaykhs with the five imams, among them Muhammad ibn Bashshar Bundar (d. 252), Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna (d. 257), al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Azim al-Anbari (d. 246) and Nasr ibn Ali al-Jahdami (d. 250) {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 451}.
