## Summary
At the Tulunid mosque al-Mulaqqin taught him the Qur'an then the 'Umda, intending Malik's madhhab until a son of Ibn Jama'a advised the Minhaj instead; he was then heard before Ibn Sayyid al-Nas and Qutb al-Din al-Halabi, with ijazas from al-Mizzi and others {cite:shadharat-dhahab 7/44}.
