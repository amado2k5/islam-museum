## Summary
[[group/hamdan|Hamdan]] came in delegation in 9 AH; the Prophet sent Khalid to call them for six months, then Ali — and they all became Muslim. When the news reached him the Prophet fell prostrate and said: peace be upon Hamdan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 449-450}.

## The delegation's account
The Hamdan delegation came in 9 AH after the Prophet's return from Tabuk; the Prophet wrote them a document granting what they asked and set Malik ibn al-Namt over those of his people who had become Muslim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449}. To the rest he sent Khalid ibn al-Walid to call them to Islam; he stayed six months calling them and they did not answer {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449; ibn-hisham-sira}. Then he sent Ali ibn Abi Talib, ordering him to relieve Khalid; Ali came to Hamdan, read the Prophet's letter to them and called them to Islam — and they all became Muslim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 449-450}. Ali wrote to the Prophet with the good news of their conversion; when the Prophet read the letter he fell prostrate, then raised his head and said: peace be upon Hamdan, peace be upon Hamdan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 450}.

## For young visitors
Khalid called the tribe of Hamdan for six months and they did not respond; then Ali went to them, read them the Prophet's letter, and they all became Muslim — and the Prophet prostrated in gratitude and greeted them.
