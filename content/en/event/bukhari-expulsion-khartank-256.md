## Summary
When al-Bukhari returned to Bukhara, its governor Khalid ibn Ahmad al-Dhuhli sent word asking him to bring the books of the Jami' and the Tarikh so that he might hear from him; al-Bukhari refused, saying: I will not abase learning nor carry it to people's doors — if he needs any of it let him come to my mosque or my house. The governor then asked him to hold a special session for his children, and he refused to single anyone out. This estrangement between them ended in his expulsion {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 14} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1360}; he settled at Khartank, a village of Samarkand two parasangs away, where he had relatives with whom he lodged {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 14}.
