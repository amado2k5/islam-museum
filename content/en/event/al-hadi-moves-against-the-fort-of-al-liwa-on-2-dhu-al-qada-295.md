## Overview
After some days at al-Hijr, al-Hadi went out on Sunday, two days into Dhu al-Qa'da, to the place where Banu al-Harith had fortified themselves, a rugged mountain called al-Liwa, which they believed no one could reach.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi stayed some days at al-Hijr, the village of Najran, and then went out on Sunday, two days into Dhu al-Qa'da, to the place where Banu al-Harith had fortified themselves. It was a rugged, steep mountain called al-Liwa, and Banu al-Harith believed that no one could take them there.
