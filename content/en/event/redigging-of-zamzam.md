## Overview
Abd al-Muttalib dug the well of Zamzam guided by a dream; in it he found the Jurhumites' buried treasures — he struck the swords into a door for the Ka'ba, and established Zamzam water for the pilgrims.

## The story
Abd al-Muttalib was commanded in a dream to dig Zamzam, and its place was described to him; he dug and found in it the things the Jurhumites had buried when they fled into exile — swords, coats of mail and two gazelles of gold {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

He struck the swords into a door for the Ka'ba and set the two gazelles in the door, and established the watering of pilgrims from Zamzam {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## Quraysh's dispute
When the well of Zamzam appeared, Quraysh disputed with Abd al-Muttalib saying "share us in it"; he said: that I will not do. They would not leave him until they took him to judgment before a priestess of Banu Sa'd — and on the road God showed them what moved them to assign Zamzam to Abd al-Muttalib alone {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
A dream came to Abd al-Muttalib showing him the place of the buried well of Zamzam; he dug it and restored its water for the pilgrims.
