## Summary
The 1981 Durban debate between Ahmed Deedat and Josh McDowell on 'Was Christ Crucified?'. {cite:answering-islam-debates-index}

## The axis of the debate
Deedat argued from the Qur'anic text of al-Nisa 157 and from the narrative discrepancies in the crucifixion accounts; McDowell argued from the Gospel testimony and historical sources. Its text translation remains one of the most circulated debate texts.
