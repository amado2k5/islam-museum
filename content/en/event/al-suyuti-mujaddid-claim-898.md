## Overview
In consistency with his claim to ijtihad, al-Suyuti, two or three years before 900/1494, announced himself as the renewer (mujaddid) of Islam for the ninth century of the Hijra — the subject of an epistle in his Hawi and of a passage in his autobiography al-Tahadduth bi-niʿmat Allah {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 914}.
