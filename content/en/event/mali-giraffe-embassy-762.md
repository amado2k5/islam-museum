## Overview
Ibn Khaldun relates that the embassy of the king of Mali, Mansa Sulayman, reached [[place/fez|Fez]] in Safar 762 AH with a gift from their sultan, a giraffe. The gift was a return for a gift from the sultan Abu al-Hasan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The day of the embassy
The sultan sat for a public audience in the Golden Tower, and people crowded to see the giraffe in admiration of its form, and poets recited verses of praise. The embassy greeted the sultan by throwing dust on their heads. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

They excused the delay in the gift by the struggles of the kings of Mali for power after the death of Mansa Sulayman. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
