## Overview
[[person/al-harith-ibn-murra|al-Harith ibn Murra al-Abdi]] set out as a volunteer for the frontier of India, with Ali's permission. He gained booty and captives, and then he was killed, together with most of his companions, in the land of [[place/qiqan|al-Qiqan]] in 42 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The raid
al-Harith was victorious, took booty and captives, and divided a thousand heads of captives and livestock in one day. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
al-Harith and those with him were killed in the land of al-Qiqan, save a few, and this was in 42. Al-Qiqan is among the lands of Sind on the side toward Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
