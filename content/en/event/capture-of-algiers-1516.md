## Summary
[[person/uruj-barbarossa|Aruj Barbarossa]]'s entry into Algiers in 922/1516, in the very year the Mamluks fell before the Ottomans at Marj Dabiq {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 210}.

## Before the entry
The brothers Aruj and Khayr al-Din had failed at Bougie and were trapped at Jijel between the Hafsids and [[person/salim-al-tumi|Salim al-Tumi]], the Spanish-backed ruler of Algiers. Al-Salabi holds the more probable view that contact with the Ottomans predated Aruj's death and the conquest of Syria and Egypt — and that it was the arriving Ottoman support which let the brothers reach Algiers before the Spanish could, for the site's excellence {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 210}.

## The entry
Aruj entered Algiers, killed its ruler on proof of his appeals for Spanish troops, took the port of Cherchell, and the city was his {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 210}.
