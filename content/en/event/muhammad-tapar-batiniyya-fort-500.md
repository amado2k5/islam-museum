## Summary
In 500 AH the sultan Muhammad ibn Malikshah campaigned against the Batiniyya and took their fortress in Isfahan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 410}.

## The fortress and its lord
Its lord, Ahmad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Attash, was killed. He had held it for twelve years. It was built by Malikshah on top of a mountain, at a cost of 200,000 dinars {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 410}.
