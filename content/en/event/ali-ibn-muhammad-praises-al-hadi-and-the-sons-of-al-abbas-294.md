## Overview
The poem praises al-Hadi for generosity, courage and piety, and names the sons of al-Abbas as his helpers.

## Detail
The poet praises al-Hadi as the imam who gathered generosity, courage and piety, a lion fierce in battle, and says that the land was made glad by his allegiance. He names the sons of al-Abbas as his helpers, men who kept their pledge and defended him with their lives. The poem says that their father aided al-Husayn at Karbala and that their brother al-Abbas was among the best men. It repeats boasts of Hashimite descent. These are the poet's claims as the book prints them; the museum records them as claims, not as established history.
