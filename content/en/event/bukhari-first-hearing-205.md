## Summary
Al-Bukhari's first hearing was in 205 when he was about ten years old; reared an orphan, he memorised hadith by heart until his companions would correct what they had written down from what he dictated to them from memory {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 3} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1360}; he was so gifted in intelligence and memory that as a boy he could already correct his teachers {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1360}.
