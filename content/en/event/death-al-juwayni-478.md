## Summary
Overcome by fever he was carried to Bushtanqan for its mild air and grew weaker; he died on the night of Wednesday 25 Rabi' II 478 — 20 August 1085 — aged fifty-nine. Carried that same night to the town, he was buried in his house, then transferred years later to the Husayn cemetery beside his father; his minbar was broken and the markets closed, and his nearly four hundred pupils broke their inkwells and pens and kept the mourning for a year {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 238-239} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala j.18 p. 2576} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 605}.
