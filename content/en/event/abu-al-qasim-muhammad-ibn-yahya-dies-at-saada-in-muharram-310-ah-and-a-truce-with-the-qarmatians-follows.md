## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya dies at Sa'da in Muharram 310 AH, and a truce with the Qarmatians follows in Sha'ban.

## Detail
Abu al-Qasim Muhammad ibn Yahya dies at Sa'da on Sunday, seven nights into Muharram at the start of 310 AH, and is buried on Monday forenoon. Ahmad ibn Yahya takes up the command. The Qarmatians ask for a truce and write to a group of Hamdan, and the truce is made between them in Sha'ban 310 AH. The editor's note records that copy P reads nine for seven.
