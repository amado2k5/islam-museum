## Summary
When al-Muwaffaq, absorbed in the Zanj war, judged Ibn Tulun's remittance insufficient, he dispatched in 263/877 a force under Musa ibn Bugha to remove him; the expedition collapsed under the soldiers' demands and the fear Ibn Tulun's forces inspired. Ibn Tulun had written to al-Muwaffaq pointing out that he had no formal jurisdiction over Egypt at all {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 300-301} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 668}.
