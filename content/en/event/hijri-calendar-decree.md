## Summary
In the year 16, Umar ibn al-Khattab set the dating of the calendar on the advice of Ali ibn Abi Talib. The start of the Islamic calendar was made the Prophet's migration from Mecca to Medina, the dating by which the community still works. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 351}.
