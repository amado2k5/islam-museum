## Overview
After Banu al-Harith refused to pay and drove off the servants, Ibn Humayd demanded that Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah free Ali ibn al-Rabi'. Muhammad answered him harshly. Ibn Humayd stayed in the village for fourteen days, then was moved to Sarra, and went back to his kin and broke the covenants between them.

## Detail
The book says that Banu al-Harith would not pay, and that when Muhammad sent his servants, the Harithi men drove them off and wanted to kill them. Ibn Bistam went to Ibn Humayd and told him the matter aimed at humiliating Banu al-Harith. Ibn Humayd then sent men of Najran to Muhammad, saying that the affair did not suit Banu al-Harith and that they would pay nothing. He also told Muhammad that he had imprisoned Ali ibn al-Rabi' and must release him or hand him over. Muhammad answered harshly. Ibn Humayd stayed in the village for several days while Muhammad was wary of him; the Banu Abd al-Madan then intervened, and he was moved to Sarra after fourteen days. Once there he sent to his kin to announce the breach, and they backed him and broke the covenants between the parties.
