## Overview
A collection of reports about the policy and judgements of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]], as al-Suyuti gives them in "History of the Caliphs". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## With his governors
When he appointed a governor he recorded his property, and he shared with transgressing governors their wealth, taking half and giving them half. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## On expenditure
He spent a long time without taking anything from the public money until he was in need, and he consulted the Companions of the Prophet. [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] said: "A lunch and a supper," and he took that. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## In the armies
He wrote that no commander of an army or a detachment should flog anyone with the prescribed punishment until he was on the road. He said that affairs are set right only by firmness without tyranny, and gentleness without weakness. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## The woman's case
A woman came to him complaining that her husband prayed at night and fasted by day. Umar praised him, and [[person/kab-ibn-suwar|Kab ibn Suwar]] understood that she was complaining that he did not come near her bed. Umar ordered him to judge between them, and he made it for her a day and a night out of every four. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## On the pilgrimage
On his pilgrimage he spent sixteen dinars, and he said: "O servant of God, we have been extravagant with this money." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
