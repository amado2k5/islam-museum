## Overview
[[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] says [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] looked to the quarter of what was got from the tithe taken on dates, kept it whole, gathered all the trustworthy of the people of [[place/najran|Najran]] to guard and administer it, and told them to divide it among the poor, the needy and those in need from respectable households who keep out of sight; it was divided as he ordered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} Then the grain collection fell due, and he ordered the due tenth of wheat measured, and the half-tenth for what is watered by draw-camels and enclosures, with a quarter of it divided among those to whom the date quarter went. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} This is from a Zaydi book and continues what [[event/al-hadi-charge-to-his-governors-284]] said about a quarter of the collection for the poor.

## How long he stayed
He stayed at Najran through Jumada II, Rajab, Sha'ban and eighteen days of Ramadan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.79} The text does not give the amount distributed or the number of those helped.
