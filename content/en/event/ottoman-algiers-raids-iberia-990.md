## Summary
The Algiers fleet's raids on Spanish soil and the Canaries, 990/1582 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 271-272}.

## The raids
The Ottoman wali of Algiers readied his fleet to fight Spain on its own soil; the mujahidun landed at Barcelona and dealt destruction, crossed the Strait of Gibraltar and struck the Canary Islands, destroying the military posts and taking their plunder {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 271-272}.
