## Overview
In 477 AH [[person/sulayman-ibn-qutlumush|Sulayman ibn Qutlumush]], the Seljuk ruler of Konya and Aksaray, marched to Syria and took Antioch from the Byzantines. It had been in their hands since 358 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.651-652}

## After the conquest
He sent to Sultan Malikshah to bring him the good news. al-Suyuti quotes al-Dhahabi that the Seljuks were the rulers of the land of Rum, their rule lasted long, and some of them remained until the time of al-Malik al-Zahir Baybars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.651-652}
