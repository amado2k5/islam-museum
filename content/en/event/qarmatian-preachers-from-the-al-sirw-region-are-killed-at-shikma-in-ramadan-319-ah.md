## Overview
Two Qarmatian preachers from the al-Sirw region are killed at the fortress of Shikma' in Ramadan 319 AH.

## Detail
In Asad's time, after Ibn al-Fadl's death, two men from the al-Sirw region appeared as Qarmatian preachers and a crowd followed them. They went to a fortress called Shikma'. Asad sent commanders and forces against them, the book says God gave the victory, the preacher of unbelief was killed, and their heads were taken to Kahlan. The Muslims, the book says, were grieved by what had befallen them. The date is Friday, two days before the end of Ramadan 319 AH.
