## Summary
In 436 AH the sultan Abu Kalijar entered Baghdad {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 256}.

## The al-Ulab
The drums called al-ulab were beaten for him at the five daily prayers, a honour given to no one before him except three times {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 256}.
