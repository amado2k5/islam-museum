## Summary
Between Jumada II and Shawwal 701 (February to early June 1302), during a stay in Mosul on the way to Tabriz, Ibn al-Tiqtaqa wrote his history al-Fakhri for Fakhr al-Din Isa ibn Ibrahim of Mosul {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.

## Shape of the book
The work consists of a brief Furstenspiegel followed by biographies of the caliphs down to al-Musta'sim, each followed by the biographies of that caliph's viziers; its skilfully chosen anecdotes and reflective approach make it enjoyable and instructive to a degree uncommon in medieval scholarly historiography {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.
