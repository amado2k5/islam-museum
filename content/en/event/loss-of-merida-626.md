## Overview
Al-Maqqari says that the enemy entered the district of Merida in 626 AH, through [[person/ibn-hud-al-judhami|Muhammad ibn Hud]], and that this was the beginning of the calamities at his hands. Merida was the capital of the lands of al-Jawf in the time of the Arabs and the non-Arabs, and it is five days' journey from Cordoba. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 469-470}
