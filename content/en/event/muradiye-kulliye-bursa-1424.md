## Overview
Murad II built the Muradiye külliye in 1424-1426 CE, the last imperial mosque complex erected in Bursa. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 49}

## The complex
Besides the mosque it included a medrese, an imaret, the founder's türbe and later tombs; Freely calls the Muradiye medrese the most beautiful to survive from its era. {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 49}
