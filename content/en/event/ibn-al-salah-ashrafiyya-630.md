## Summary
In 630/1233 Ibn al-Salah took the mashyakha of the al-Ashrafiyya dar al-hadith in Damascus, then the Shamiyya al-Sughra. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 186}

## Three schools at once
When al-Malik al-Ashraf built the dar al-hadith he entrusted its teaching to him, and he took the madrasa of Sitt al-Sham opposite the Nur al-Din bimaristan, discharging all three posts — Rawahiyya, Ashrafiyya and Shamiyya — without neglecting a single one. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 230} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 959}
