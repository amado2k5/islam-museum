## Overview
In al-Baladhuri, on the authority of Jabir ibn Samura, the people of [[place/kufa|Kufa]] complained to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] about [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]], saying he did not perform the prayer well. Umar then removed him, and governors succeeded one another over Kufa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The complaint
Sa'd replied that he prayed with them the prayer of the Prophet. Umar sent men to ask about him, and the people of the mosques praised him until they reached the mosque of the Banu Abs. [[person/abu-saada|Abu Sa'da]] said that he did not divide fairly or judge with justice. Sa'd prayed against him, and al-Baladhuri relates that Abd al-Malik ibn Umayr saw him afterwards in a wretched state. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

[[person/amr-ibn-madikarib|Amr ibn Ma'dikarib]] testified to Sa'd's fair division and justice, and Umar answered his questions about war and weapons. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The succession of governors
Umar removed Sa'd and appointed [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]], who was complained of as weak in policy, so he removed him after a year and nine months. Then he appointed [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] until Umar died. Uthman reappointed Sa'd, then removed him and appointed [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn Uqba]], then Sa'id ibn al-As. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The statements of the accused and the witnesses are given here as al-Baladhuri recorded them, and the museum passes no judgement on them.
