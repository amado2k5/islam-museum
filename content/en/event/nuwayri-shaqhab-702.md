## Summary
Al-Nuwayri took part in a battle against the Mongols on 29 Sha'ban 702/18 April 1303 alongside the amir Ala' al-Din Mughultay, and so could describe the war against the Mongols and the victory of Shaqhab as an eyewitness in the historical section of his encyclopaedia {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 157}.

## The victory of Shaqhab
Shaqhab — near Damascus in early Ramadan 702 — was the battle that broke the last great Ilkhanid thrust into Mamluk Syria; Ibn Taymiyya was also present. The diwan official's fighting beside Mughultay let him narrate its details from observation rather than hearsay {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 157}.
