## Summary
Badr al-Din Hasan ibn Zayn al-Din 'Umar ibn Habib al-Halabi al-Shafi'i died at Aleppo in Rabi' II 779, aged about seventy; he had served in royal and chancery correspondence, as deputy qadi and deputy secretary of the privy chancery, and was the master of his age in insha' and shurut {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/30} {cite:nujum-zahira 11/189} {cite:al-aalam 2/218-219}.
