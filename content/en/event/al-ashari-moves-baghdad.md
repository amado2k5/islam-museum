## Overview
Late in life Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari moved to Baghdad, where he lived and taught {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 743}. A group of imams took from him, among them Abu al-Hasan al-Bahili, Abu al-Hasan al-Kirmani, Abu Zayd al-Marwazi, Abu Abd Allah ibn Mujahid al-Basri, Zahir ibn Ahmad al-Sarakhsi and Abu Sahl al-Su'luki; through these and their like his way in kalam spread until his followers came to be known as the Ash'ariyya {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 15/86} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 743}.

## His circle and pupils
Al-Dhahabi gives the names of the imams who took from him, and al-Bayhaqi reports through Zahir ibn Ahmad al-Sarakhsi that al-Ash'ari called him to witness at his house in Baghdad near his death {cite:siyar-alam-nubala 15/86}.
