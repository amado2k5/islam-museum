## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates the governorship of [[person/al-junayd-ibn-abd-al-rahman|al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman]] over the frontier of Sind in the Umayyad period, and his wars there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The governorship and the killing of Sasa
Al-Junayd took the frontier first from Umar ibn Hubayra, then from Hisham. He settled on the bank of the Mihran, and there was an exchange of hostages for tribute between him and the king of India. Then the two sides fought and the king's army was taken captive. [[person/sasa-ibn-dahir|Sasa ibn Dahir]] was killed by treachery after al-Junayd had made him feel safe. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The raids
Al-Junayd raided al-Kirj after they broke their word, and breached its wall with battering rams, and entered by force. He sent his agents to Murmad, al-Mandal, Dahanaj and Biruas, and took al-Bayluman and al-Jurz. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Wealth
Al-Baladhuri says that al-Junayd gathered in his house forty million, apart from what he gave to his visitors. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
