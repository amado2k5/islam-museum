## Overview
Ahmad ibn Yahya lifts the siege of the Qarmatian stronghold and goes home in 308 AH, rather than fight the Sultan's commander.

## Detail
The chief of their town surrenders to Ahmad ibn Yahya, and the men fight the Qarmatians in their fort until they despair of their lives. They then write to al-Harmali and send him money, and he sends a force to their aid. When Ahmad learns this he dislikes fighting al-Harmali, because the Sultan would take it that he was fighting his own commander and the pilgrimage trade of his land would stop. Some townspeople urge him, and he dismisses his soldiers, leaves the town and returns to his land in 308 AH. The editor's note says the copy P reads 'fight his commander' and that the Sultan here means Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur.
