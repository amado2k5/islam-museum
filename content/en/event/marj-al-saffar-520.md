## Overview
The battle of Marj al-Saffar took place at the end of 520 AH, between the Franks and Atabeg Tughtakin of Damascus. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The battle
The kings and commanders of the Franks gathered, marched to Damascus and camped at Marj al-Saffar. Tughtakin asked the Turkmen of Diyar Bakr for help, and left his son Buri in charge of Damascus. Then he was unhorsed, the Muslims were routed, and the Frankish cavalry pursued them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The end
The Turkmen foot soldiers held firm in the battle and fought to the death; they killed the Frankish foot soldiers and plundered their camp. The horsemen returned and found their camp plundered and their infantry dead. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
