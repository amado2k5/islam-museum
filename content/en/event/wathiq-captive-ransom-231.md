## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that in 231 AH the caliph [[person/al-wathiq|al-Wathiq]] ransomed one thousand six hundred Muslim captives from the Byzantines. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 530}

## The condition of the ransom
Al-Suyuti relates that [[person/ahmad-ibn-abi-duad|Ibn Abi Duad]] said: whoever among the captives says the Qur'an is created will be released and given two dinars, and whoever refuses will be left in captivity. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 530}

A second source is needed to confirm the event and its details, since the museum has only this passage of the book.
