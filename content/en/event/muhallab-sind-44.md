## Overview
In 44 AH, in the days of Mu'awiya, [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] raided the frontier of India, and reached Banna and the marshes between Multan and Kabul, and the enemy met him and he fought them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Hamstringing the horses
In the land of [[place/qiqan|al-Qiqan]] al-Muhallab met eighteen Turkish horsemen on hamstrung horses. They fought him and all were killed. Al-Muhallab said something to the effect: these foreigners are not more entitled than we are to gird up. So he hamstrung his horses, and according to the account he was the first of the Muslims to do this. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
