## Summary
In this year al-Bahlawan ibn Ildigiz took the city of Tabriz by treaty — it was part of the lands of Aqsunqur al-Ahmadili — and gave it to his brother Qizil Arslan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 70}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir explains that al-Bahlawan had marched on Maragha and besieged it; the son of Aqsunqur al-Ahmadili had died leaving the kingdom by will to his son Falak al-Din. Al-Bahlawan first besieged the citadel of Rawindiz, left it when it resisted, and besieged Maragha while sending his brother Qizil Arslan with an army to besiege Tabriz as well. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 70} While he fought the people of Maragha they captured a party of his troops, but the qadi of Maragha, Sadr al-Din, released them with robes of honor, which pleased al-Bahlawan; the qadi then negotiated a peace on condition that they surrender Tabriz to al-Bahlawan. The offer was accepted, terms were settled, oaths were sworn, al-Bahlawan received Tabriz and gave it to his brother Qizil Arslan, then departed from Maragha with his army. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 70}
