## Summary
Yaqut finished the draft of the Mujam al-Buldan at Aleppo on 20 Safar 621/13 March 1224 and presented an autograph copy to al-Qifti's library {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}.

## More than geography
The book is not confined to geographical and topographical names; it includes literary and poetic matter and biographies of prominent figures from the places presented, some cities and regions treated at length — Aleppo and its district, and Marw — the places where Yaqut had long resided {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265-266}.
