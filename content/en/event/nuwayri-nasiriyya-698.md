## Summary
Under the events of 698/1298 al-Nuwayri mentions for the first time — after recording his birth — his recruitment to the diwan al-khass at Cairo and his residence at the Nasiriyya madrasa, inaugurated by al-Malik al-Adil Kitbugha and acquired by al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun on his return to power {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 157}.

## The madrasa and its waqf
He describes the Nasiriyya at length and reproduces the text of its waqf, openly criticising its administrator, the tawashi Shuja' al-Din 'Anbar al-Lala, the sultan's tutor, accusing him of embezzlement and demanding he return money owed to the madrasa's staff — boldness the Encyclopaedia of Islam notes among the signs of his self-confidence {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 157-158}.
