## Overview
Next day [[person/al-zubayr-al-kulaybi|al-Zubayr al-Kulaybi]], [[person/al-walid-ibn-hayyan-al-jamai|al-Walid ibn Hayyan al-Jama'i]] and some of the Yarsamis went in to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and asked safe-conduct for the Akilis, and he granted it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} They brought him Abbad al-Akili and the notables of al-Rabi'a, while [[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili]] stayed away and went out to a place of theirs called [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]], a mountain where they fortify themselves, and stayed there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} Al-Hadi then ordered the people to prepare to go out to [[place/najran|Najran]] and wrote to Abu Muhammad, that is [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abu Muhammad Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] at Najran, of his arrival at Sa'da and the Akili affair. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157}

## Al-Hadi's verses
Al-Hadi spoke verses on it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} In them, as their author has it, the blamers pressed on him when they saw him not turn aside in the places of standing, the fire of war was kindled, and to those who said he had paid his due to war he answers that he called all the people to the truth while most turn from piety and follow wickedness, that he told them to leave it, that the Book came to them with its laws, and that they must take the truth or be at war. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} He ends by saying he speaks the word of truth and has more for every fighter. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.158} The editor gives variant readings of the second manuscript in the footnotes. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.157} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.158} The abusive words are not repeated.
