## Overview
An annual festival in [[place/fes|Fez]], begun in 1994, that brings together Sufi chants, qawwali and hymns with the music of other faiths on the stages of the old city. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
It has become one of the best-known festivals of spiritual music in the world; it is held in the courtyards and gardens of the palaces of Fez. {cite:wikidata}
