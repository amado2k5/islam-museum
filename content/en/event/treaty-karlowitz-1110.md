## Overview
The Treaty of Karlowitz was signed south-west of Zagreb on the Danube in 1110 AH / 1699 CE, in the reign of Sultan [[person/mustafa-ii-uthmani|Mustafa II]]. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.310}

## The terms
By it the Ottomans withdrew from the lands of Hungary and the province of Transylvania. Every state that had been paying tribute stopped paying it. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.310}

## Al-Salabi's judgement
Al-Salabi holds that the Ottoman surrender of their lands was the beginning of the Ottoman withdrawal from Europe, and that it marks the move into an age of disintegration and rapid decline. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.310}
