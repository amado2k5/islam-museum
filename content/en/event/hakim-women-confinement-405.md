## Summary
In 405 AH the caliph al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah forbade women in Egypt ever to leave their homes or enter the public baths {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 173}.

## Details of the ban
He also stopped the shoemakers from making the shoes women used to walk outside in. It is one of the best-known decrees that historians recorded among his changeable rulings {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 173}.

## Enforcement
Several women who disobeyed his order were killed, and some old women were drowned {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 173}.
