## Summary
After completing his preliminary studies at Tus, Abu Ja'far al-Tusi left a basically Shafi'i Khurasan, increasingly under Ghaznavid control, for Baghdad where the Shi'i Buyids were dominant, in 408/1017; there he studied under leading Imami masters, above all al-Shaykh al-Mufid {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 744}.
