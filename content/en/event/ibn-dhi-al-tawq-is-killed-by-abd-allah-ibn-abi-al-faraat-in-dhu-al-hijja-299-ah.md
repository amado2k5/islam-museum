## Overview
On Friday, thirteen days into Dhu al-Hijja 299 AH, Abd Allah ibn Abi al-Faraat catches Ibn Dhi al-Tawq al-Jayshani with one companion and kills them both. Their heads are sent to Mulahiz ibn Abd Allah.

## Detail
The book says Abd Allah ibn Abi al-Faraat caught Ibn Dhi al-Tawq al-Jayshani and killed him and one man with him, and sent their heads to Mulahiz ibn Abd Allah. The author calls Ibn Dhi al-Tawq a man of great trouble who showed unbelief and apostasy; that is the author's judgement, recorded as his. The sentence ends at the foot of p. 400 with 'and he sent', and continues on p. 401, where Ibn al-Fadl sends his army against Abu al-Faraat.
