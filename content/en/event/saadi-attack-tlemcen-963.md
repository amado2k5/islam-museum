## Summary
The Sa'di army's surprise entry into Tlemcen in 963 AH {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.

## The opportunity
[[person/muhammad-al-shaykh-al-sadi|Muhammad al-Shaykh]] seized the moment of the Ottoman fleet's sailing for Istanbul — with Tlemcen's men away with the mujahidun at Oran — entered the city, and set over it the commander Ibn Ghannam, chief of Bani Rashid {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.

## The stand
The Ottoman garrison under [[person/mahmud-safa-bak|Mahmud Safa Bak]] held until the attack was contained {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 244}.
