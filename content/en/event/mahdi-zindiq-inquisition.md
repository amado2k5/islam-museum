## Overview
Al-Suyuti says that [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] pursued the heretics (zanadiqa) and destroyed a great many of them. He holds that al-Mahdi was the first to order books of disputation written to refute the heretics and the unbelievers. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.440,443}

## After 166 AH
He adds that al-Mahdi pursued them with great energy in 166 AH and after, searched for them in every region, and killed people on suspicion. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.440,443}

This is al-Suyuti's account alone in the event data. The academic view and other views need an additional source.
