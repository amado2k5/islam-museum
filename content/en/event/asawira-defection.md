## Overview
The report describes the offer of the Asawira to enter Islam on conditions, and Umar's acceptance of them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The offer
[[person/siyah-al-uswari|Siyah al-Uswari]] commanded the vanguard of Yazdegerd. He was then sent to Ahwaz, and [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa al-Ash'ari]] was besieging Sus while al-Kulbaniyya were at their post. When he saw the rising of Islam, he sent to Abu Musa that they wished to enter his religion on conditions: that they would fight their enemies among the Persians alongside him; that they would not fight one another during disputes; that the Muslim Arabs would protect them; and that they might settle wherever they chose and be counted among the holders of stipends. The events took place near [[place/khuzistan|Khuzistan]].

## The reply
Abu Musa said: "You have what we have, and you owe what we owe." They refused, so he wrote to Umar, and Umar wrote to him to give them everything they had asked. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
