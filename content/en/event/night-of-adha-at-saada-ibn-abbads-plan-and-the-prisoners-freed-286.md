## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad says [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] then sent to "al-Mahra" and to Khawlan, as the text prints it and the museum reads al-Mahra, and wrote to [[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] to bring them out to him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} The Banu al-Harith men held in prison at Sa'da, among them, were worked on by [[person/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili|Ahmad ibn Abbad al-Akili]] until he slipped them a file; they filed their irons off and left the prison to join their people. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163}

## Ibn Abbad's plan
Before that Ahmad ibn Abbad had gathered a party of those who obeyed him and meant to fall on Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and ride that same hour to [[place/al-ghayl-saada|al-Ghayl]], strike there, remove those in it and free the prisoners, and this was the night of Adha. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} When the news proved true Muhammad sent to some of the Yarsamis asking them to stay thirty men in his house until his cries reached Banu Sa'd; they disliked it and said they did not wish to put themselves forward in a matter that might destroy them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} Letters had already come to him from [[person/al-hasan-ibn-ali-al-futaymi|al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Futaymi]] telling what Ibn Abbad had agreed and that some of the Yarsamis helped him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163}

## What Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah did
Muhammad went to al-Hasan ibn Ali with the narrator and two slaves; at al-Ghayl they sent criers among [[group/banu-sad-khawlan|Banu Sa'd of Khawlan]], a great army gathered and stayed at al-Ghayl, and the narrator's father told them not to leave until the people came back from the Eid prayer. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} [[person/muhammad-ibn-abi-hisham|Muhammad ibn Abi Hisham]], a man who was fond of him, came and begged him by God not to pray the Eid for fear of his enemy; he disliked it, told him he had gathered his friends that night, and in the morning went out and prayed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.163} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.164}

## The prisoners get out
When Ibn Abbad learned what Muhammad had done he lost hope of what he had wished for and worked on the prisoners' affair until he got them out as told; among those who left were [[person/abu-al-wajih-ibn-musa-al-khushaymi|Abu al-Wajih]] and Yahya, while [[person/marzuq-ibn-muhammad-al-khushaymi|Marzuq]] stayed behind, thinking it a disgrace to him, until al-Hadi later set him free. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.164} The account is Zaydi and its charges are the narrator's. It follows [[event/al-hadis-charge-at-sawhan-286]] in the order of the telling and is followed by [[event/al-hadi-breaches-the-wall-of-al-hajar-286]]. The museum does not assert that this Muhammad ibn Abi Hisham is the one named on p. 120.
