## Summary
The conquest of Thessalonica under Murad II — after crushing the risings the book says emperor Manuel II stood behind, Murad resolved to teach him a practical lesson: he stormed Thessalonica in March 833/1431 and the city became, in al-Sallabi's words, an inseparable part of the Ottoman state {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 79}.
