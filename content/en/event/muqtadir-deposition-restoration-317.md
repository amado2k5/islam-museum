## Overview
In 317 AH the eunuch [[person/muanis-al-muzaffar|Muanis]] rose against the caliph [[person/al-muqtadir-billah|al-Muqtadir]] and gave his allegiance to [[person/al-qahir-billah|al-Qahir billah]], then the troops returned him to the caliphate two days later. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.591-592}

## The deposition
Al-Suyuti says that Muanis came out when he heard that al-Muqtadir intended to appoint Harun ibn Gharib commander of the commanders. He brought the army to the caliph's residence and took al-Muqtadir out on the night of the fourteenth of Muharram, and had al-Muqtadir testify to his own deposition. Muanis and the commanders gave their allegiance to Muhammad ibn al-Mu'tadid and gave him the title al-Qahir, and the vizierate was entrusted to Ibn Muqla. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.591-592}

## The return
The troops came asking for their allegiance payment and their annual pay. They killed the chamberlain and turned to Muanis's house asking for al-Muqtadir. They carried him on their shoulders to the palace of the caliphate. Al-Qahir was taken, weeping, and al-Muqtadir kissed him and reassured him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.591-592}

## Other accounts
In Ibn al-Athir they took al-Muqtadir and his mother and maternal aunt to Muanis's house, and summoned the judge Abu Amr to witness the deposition; he hid it and then handed it to al-Muqtadir when he returned. Ibn al-Athir says that the troops' anger came from an order by Nazuk to remove their tents. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.148-150}

In Ibn al-Jawzi al-Muqtadir was deposed on Saturday in the middle of Muharram, and Ibn Muqla was given the vizierate. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.514}
