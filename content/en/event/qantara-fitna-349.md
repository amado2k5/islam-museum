## Overview
On Thursday the third of Sha'ban 349 a Sunni–Shi'a fitna broke out at the New Bridge; next day's Friday prayer was suspended in every congregational mosque on both banks except Baratha, where prayer was held. A group of the Banu Hashim were seized and detained at the vizier's house as the fitna's cause, and released next day {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 687}.
