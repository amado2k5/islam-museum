## Summary
Isma'il ibn 'Umar ibn Kathir, 'Imad al-Din Abu l-Fida', was born in a village of the district of Busra al-Sham in the year 700 or shortly after {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}, a birth the Dhayl fixes at 701 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 dhayl p. 99}.
## Name and place
He is al-Qurashi al-Busrawi then al-Dimashqi; al-Zirikli writes that he was born in a village of the Busra al-Sham district — that is, in the Hawran — before growing up in Damascus {cite:al-aalam 1/112}.
