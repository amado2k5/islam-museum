## Summary
Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Fadl Allah died at Damascus of a quartan fever on the Day of Arafa, 9 Dhu al-Hijja 749 (1 March 1349), leaving Masalik al-Absar, al-Ta'rif bi'l-Mustalah al-Sharif, essays and verse; al-Dhahabi records his many literary works and long reach in the two crafts and skill in the two rhetorics {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/333} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 790-791}.

## Details
Obstinate and outspoken, quick to make enemies, he served less time in the diwan than his father or brothers — yet outshone them all as a writer, the family's true glory being the encyclopedist rather than the official {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 790-791} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/332}.
