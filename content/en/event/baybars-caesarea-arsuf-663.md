## Summary
Sultan [[person/baybars|Baybars]] came in 663 AH, besieged Caesarea and took it by storm. The citadel resisted for some days and was then taken. He then attacked Arsuf and took it by the sword in Rajab. He then returned and had his son al-Malik al-Saʿid proclaimed sultan in Shawwal, and had him ride in royal splendour at the age of five {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.541}.
