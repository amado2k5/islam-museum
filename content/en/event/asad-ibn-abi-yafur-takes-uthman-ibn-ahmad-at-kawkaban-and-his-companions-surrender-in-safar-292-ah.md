## Overview
Uthman ibn Ahmad went up Mount Bayt Dhakhar and held Kawkaban. As'ad sent troops by a route called Bayt Khiyam, defeated him, imprisoned him, and gave safe conduct to his companions who surrendered.

## Detail
The two sons of Ya'fur, Uthman and As'ad, to whom affairs in Sanaa had passed, appointed Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-A'jam as judge, and a quarrel broke out between them. Uthman ibn Ahmad went up Mount Bayt Dhakhar and held Kawkaban. As'ad went to Sanaa in Safar of this year and stayed a few days, then sent a force that climbed the mountain from a place called Bayt Khiyam. When they reached the height, As'ad followed them with his men, and Uthman and his men fortified themselves in Kawkaban and fought in their place. As'ad won, took Uthman, entered the fortress and imprisoned him, and all his companions surrendered and were given safe conduct. As'ad then stayed at Shiban and Sanaa.
