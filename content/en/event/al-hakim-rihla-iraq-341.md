## Summary
In 341/952-3, a few months after the death of Isma'il al-Saffar, al-Hakim journeyed to Iraq; he made the hajj and travelled through Khurasan and Transoxiana {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 122}.

## Nearly two thousand shaykhs
His teachers at Nishapur alone numbered about a thousand, and about a thousand more in Iraq and the other lands; Abu Ya'la al-Khalili counted two journeys to Iraq and the Hijaz, the second in 368, on which he debated al-Daraqutni and won his approval {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 123-125}.
