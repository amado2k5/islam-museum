## Summary
In 570 AH Salah al-Din came and took Damascus without a blow or a thrust. Al-Salih Isma'il ibn Nur al-Din, the martyr, went to Aleppo with his entourage {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 716}.

## The campaign in Syria
Salah al-Din then marched and besieged Homs with siege engines, and then Manbij. He took Hama in Jumada al-Akhira. He besieged Aleppo and handled the affairs of Nur al-Din's family badly. Then he withdrew, took back Homs, turned to Baalbek and took it. He met Izz al-Din Mas'ud ibn Mawdud, ruler of Mosul and brother of its ruler, and the people of Mosul were routed on the slopes of Hama in a terrible defeat. He left his brother Saif al-Islam in charge of Damascus, while his brother al-Adil was in Egypt {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 716-717}.
