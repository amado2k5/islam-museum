## Summary
On 7 April 1972 four gunmen shot [[person/abeid-karume|Abeid Karume]] dead as he played bao at the Afro-Shirazi Party headquarters in [[place/zanzibar|Zanzibar]] Town. {cite:wikipedia-abeid-karume-en} {cite:wikipedia-abeid-karume-ar}

## What the articles say followed the killing
The English and Arabic articles say that reprisals followed against people suspected of opposing his regime. The day 7 April became a public holiday in Zanzibar. {cite:wikipedia-abeid-karume-en} {cite:wikipedia-abeid-karume-ar} Wikidata agrees with the articles on the date of death and the place, Zanzibar Town. {cite:wikidata-abeid-karume}
