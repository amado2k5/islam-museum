## Overview
After the death of al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik and the accession of Sulayman, [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] was taken in chains with [[person/muawiya-ibn-al-muhallab|Mu'awiya ibn al-Muhallab]], and [[person/salih-ibn-abd-al-rahman|Salih ibn Abd al-Rahman]] imprisoned him at [[place/wasit|Wasit]] and tortured him until he died. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The change of governor
Sulayman appointed Salih over the kharaj of Iraq, and appointed [[person/yazid-ibn-abi-kabsha|Yazid ibn Abi Kabsha]] over Sind. Yazid died eighteen days after arriving in Sind. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Muhammad in prison
Al-Baladhuri says that Muhammad recited verses on his people's failure to protect him. The people of India wept for him and made an image of him in a casket. Salih then tortured him, with men of the family of Abu Aqil, until he killed them. The account adds that al-Hajjaj had killed Adam, Salih's brother, and that Adam held the views of the Kharijites. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source gives verses by Hamza ibn Bid al-Hanafi in his praise, saying that he led armies for seventeen years of pilgrimage. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
