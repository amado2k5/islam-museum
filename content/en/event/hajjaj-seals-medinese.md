## Overview
In 74 AH [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] went to [[place/medina|Medina]]. Al-Suyuti reports that he began to be harsh with its people and to belittle the remaining Companions there. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.354}

## The sealing
He sealed them on their necks and hands to humiliate them. The source names among them Anas, Jabir ibn Abd Allah and Sahl ibn Sa'd al-Sa'idi. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.354}

What is not covered here: the account rests on a single source, and the Gregorian conversion is derived. An additional source is needed.
