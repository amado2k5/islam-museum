## Overview
In 1907 the Turkestan journal *al-Waqt* reported Japan's intent to hold a congress of religions in Tokyo; the newspaper *al-Liwa'* nominated Muhammad Farid Wajdi and Shaykh Tantawi Jawhari as Egypt's delegates, and Wajdi prepared his French treatise *Ambassador of Islam* — only for it to emerge that the congress did not exist {cite:sufara-al-islam-azhar p. 19}{cite:sufara-al-islam-azhar p. 20}. The book notes the idea materialized some thirty years later, when Shaykh Muhammad Mustafa al-Maraghi's letter was read at it.
