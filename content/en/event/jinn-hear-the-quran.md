## Overview
The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] stayed at [[place/wadi-nakhla|Wadi Nakhla]] on his way to Mecca after Ta'if, and a band of the jinn listened to him, without his knowing they were present until God informed him of it through the verses revealed about it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.127} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.128}

## The Qur'an on it
The book says that God mentioned this event in the Surah al-Ahqaf and in the Surah al-Jinn. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.127} In it the jinn believed and returned warning their people, and the sequence of the reports implies that they came as a delegation again later. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.128}

## The good news
The author sees in the verses a promise of the success of the mission, and that no force of the universe can stand between it and its success. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.128} After that the cloud of sorrow and despair cleared, and the Prophet resolved to return to Mecca and resume presenting Islam. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.128}

## Notes for researchers
- The account of the jinn here is based on what is in the Qur'an, al-Bukhari and Muslim, and the book presents it as a report that does not depend on scholarly verification.

## For young visitors
On his way to Mecca he passed a group of jinn who listened to the Qur'an and believed, then returned to their people calling them to Islam.
