## Overview
News came in 280 AH of the raid of [[person/ismail-ibn-ahmad|Isma'il ibn Ahmad]] into the land of the Turks. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.146}

## The account
Ibn al-Jawzi says that Isma'il killed a great many Turks and took the city of their king. He captured the king's wife Khatun and about ten thousand people, and seized many beasts. Each Muslim horseman received a thousand dirhams from the booty. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.146}
