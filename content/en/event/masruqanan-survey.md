## Overview
Al-Masruqanan was a grant held by the family of Abu Bakra, originally one hundred jaribs. Al-Baladhuri says that the surveyors of al-Mansur measured it as a thousand jaribs. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The family were allowed to keep a hundred, and the rest was taken back.
