## Summary
The great Ottoman campaign against Malta, 973/1565 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 252}.

## The objective
Sultan [[person/sulayman-al-qanuni|Süleyman]] resolved to take Malta, the greatest Christian stronghold in the central Mediterranean and seat of the [[group/knights-of-st-john|Knights of St John]]; he sent [[person/piyale-pasha|Piyale Pasha]] with the fleet and ordered [[person/turgut-reis|Dragut]] and [[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan b. Khayr al-Din]] to join — Hasan with twenty-five ships and three thousand men {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 252}.

## Siege and withdrawal
The fleet stood off Malta on 18 May and pressed a tight siege until Christian relief under the Sicilian viceroy arrived with twenty-eight warships; the Ottoman fleet withdrew on 18 Rabi' I 973/8 December 1565 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 252}.
