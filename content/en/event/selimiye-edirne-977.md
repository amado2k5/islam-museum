## Overview
Sinan built the Selimiye at Edirne in 1569-75 for Selim II, considering it the crowning achievement of his career {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p. 73}.

## Sinan's masterwork
Its dome, resting on eight piers, stands six cubits higher than the dome of Ayasofya, and four three-balconied minarets rise at the four corners of the courtyard {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 712}.
