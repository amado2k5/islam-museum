## Overview
Under the heading "The rest of the report of the Ma'mariyyin and the fighting with them", [[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] says: Abu Jafar [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]] told me: "On Saturday, the twenty-first of Sha'ban, at the noon prayer, the group came, about three hundred men, and leaned on a hill near Khiwan." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.141} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} The group are [[group/banu-mamar-hamdan|Banu Ma'mar]], and the account is Zaydi, in the narrator's voice. It follows [[event/banu-mamar-come-down-on-khiwan-on-15-shaban-and-are-routed-286]].

## Al-Hadi rides out
When [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] knew of them he told the people to mount and they did; he put on his arms and Abu al-Qasim his, the two rode their mounts, and he resolved to fight them; he left the village and went down the valley until he was near the group. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} In the valley [[person/husayn-ibn-al-hasan-al-alawi|al-Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi]] met him, "who had come from Athafit, where he was governor, with an army of Banu Rabi'a and Banu Sarim, who came as reinforcement for al-Hadi when word reached them of the going out of the Ma'mariyyin against al-Hadi"; he told the people to array and resolved to fight. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} See [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan]] and [[group/banu-sarim-hamdan]]; it is not asserted that this al-Husayn is the governor of Saqayn.

## The elders step in
While he was arraying the people for the charge a party of the group's elders and notables came and said: "Son of the Messenger of God, we have turned the group away and they will never return to the like of this; they want only good, so go back to your house and dismiss your armies." The elders of Khiwan and notables of Hamdan and Khawlan pressed him and said: "We shall go to them, rebuke their act and remind them of your kindness to them." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} When they had said much he "went back to Khiwan with his companions of the Tabaris and others, like an angry man, until he entered his house". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142}

## The group's return and the plea for safe-conduct
The elders of Khiwan went to the group with their clans; all rebuked their act and moved them from their places, and they asked them to go back to al-Hadi and plead for them; so they went to him and said: "Son of the Messenger of God, the group are sorry for what they did and are your clan, and some of them served with you; return to them with your grace, kindness and mercy; they ask your safe-conduct, and neither we nor they despair of your goodness and kindness." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} They said that "someone who wanted their ruin had made fun of them, their error had become clear and they had come to themselves; before that they were with al-Hadi in the war at Athafit, sincere for him and diligent in the war on his enemy". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} The book does not name whoever "wanted their ruin".

## The safe-conduct
"He recognised that and was gracious to them at the request of the clan for them, and gave them safe-conduct, and by that he bound all Hamdan." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142} The editor notes that the original reads "against them" and was corrected from the other manuscript to "to them" in the phrase "your kindness to them". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.142}

## Notes
Saturday the twenty-first of Sha'ban is a Friday in the tabular calendar, so the calendar runs a day ahead, as in earlier batches; the date is derived. This page and those before name the group only as "al-Ma'mariyyin" and "the group", and the book names no leader of theirs here. Printed p. 142 ends with the safe-conduct; p. 143 begins with what follows.
