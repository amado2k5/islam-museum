## Summary
In 596/1199-1200 a falling-out obliged Askar to emancipate Yaqut and part with him {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121}.

## A copyist for hire
Yaqut earned his living copying: between 596 and 603 he transcribed three hundred mujallads, and wide reading brought him further gains. His former master afterwards relented, gave him goods and made him a mudaraba partner {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.
