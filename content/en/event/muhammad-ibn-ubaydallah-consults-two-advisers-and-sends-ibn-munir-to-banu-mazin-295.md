## Overview
Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah consulted Muhammad ibn al-Haytham and Ahmad ibn al-Arbad about Ibn Humayd. They advised him to send a man of his own to gather Banu Amr and Banu Bishr, and he sent Abdallah ibn Munir al-Muzawi to the Yasr of Banu Mazin.

## Detail
The book says Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah sent for Muhammad ibn al-Haytham and Ahmad ibn al-Arbad, consulted them about Ibn Humayd, and asked what they knew. They said: send with us a man of your own, and we shall gather Banu Amr and Banu Bishr to him and fight this man. He sent Abdallah ibn Munir al-Muzawi with them and ordered him to lodge at the Yasr of Banu Mazin, which he did. The book describes the two advisers as men who showed counsel and goodwill at that time, and the record gives that as the book's own description.
