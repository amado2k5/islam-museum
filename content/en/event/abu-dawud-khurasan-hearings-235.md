## Overview
In Khurasan Abu Dawud heard Qutayba ibn Sa'id, Ishaq ibn Rahwayh and a circle of others {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 359}; since Ibn Rahwayh died in 238 and Qutayba in 240, the hearings fall in the 230s of the hijra.

## Early writing
Abu 'Abdallah al-Hakim said he took down hadith in Khurasan before leaving for Iraq — in his own land and at Herat — and that he had written early at Nishapur, later travelling with his son to Khurasan {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 361}.
