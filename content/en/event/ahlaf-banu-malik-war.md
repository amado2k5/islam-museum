## Overview
A war fought in al-Ta'if between two branches of [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]], the Ahlaf and Banu Malik, shortly before Islam. Ibn al-Athir narrates it in "al-Kamil". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 512-513}

## The cause of the war
The Ahlaf's part in taking al-Ta'if was great, and they boasted of it over Banu Malik. When their horses multiplied, they set aside a protected pasture called Jaladhan in the land of Banu Nasr ibn Mu'awiya of the Yarbu'. Banu Nasr and their chief [[person/afif-ibn-awf|Afif ibn Awf]] were angered and fought them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 512-513}

Banu Malik and their chief [[person/jundub-ibn-awf|Jundub ibn Awf]] seized the opportunity, given old grudges, and allied with Banu Yarbu' against the Ahlaf. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 512-513}

## The fighting
The first battle between them was on the day of al-Ta'if. The Ahlaf won and drove their opponents into a valley called Lahab, and they slaughtered them in great numbers in the gorge of al-Aban. They then fought for days, among them the day of Umar Dhi Kinda and the day of Karuba. Ibn al-Athir says that Afif gave a cry which, they claim, made seventy pregnant women miscarry. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 512-513}

## Seeking allies and the end of the war
Banu Malik went seeking alliance with Daws and Khath'am. The Ahlaf went out to Medina seeking the alliance of the Ansar, and [[person/masud-ibn-mutib|Mas'ud ibn Mu'tib]] came to [[person/uhayha-ibn-al-julah|Uhayha ibn al-Julah]]. Uhayha turned him away and said that his brother, whom he had left behind, had the better claim to reconciliation, even if it cost him much. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 512-513}

Mas'ud left, and Uhayha provided him with a boy skilled in building fortified towers (utum). He built for him a tower, the first fortified tower built in al-Ta'if. Ibn al-Athir says that no notable war was fought between them after that. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 512-513}
