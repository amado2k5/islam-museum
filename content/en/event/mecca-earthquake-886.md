## The great earthquake of Mecca in 886 AH
On the seventeenth of Muharram 886 AH there was a huge earthquake in Mecca, the like of which had not been heard of. It came in a year which also saw a bolt of lightning that set fire to the Prophet's Noble Mosque in its Ramadan {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 344}.
