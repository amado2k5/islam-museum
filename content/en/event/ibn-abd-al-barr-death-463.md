## Death at Shatiba
The hafiz Abu 'Amr Ibn 'Abd al-Barr died at Shatiba in eastern al-Andalus on the eve of Friday, the last day of Rabi' II 463; he was buried on Friday after the 'asr prayer and his companion Abu al-Hasan Tahir ibn Mufawwiz al-Mu'afiri led the funeral prayer {cite:wafayat-ayan p. 71} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 142}.
## Ninety-five years
He had completed ninety-five years and five days; in his eminence he was the highest isnad in al-Andalus of his time, and though he began as a Zahiri for a long stretch, he returned to reasoning by analogy and often inclined to the Shafi'i school {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 142}. Al-Dhahabi judges him no less than al-Khatib, al-Bayhaqi or Ibn Hazm in breadth of knowledge {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 142}.
