## Summary
[[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] asked for the hand of [[person/umm-kulthum-bint-ali|Umm Kulthum]], daughter of [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] and [[person/fatima|Fatima]]; Ali said, "I have done so," and Umar came to the Muhajirun rejoicing and saying, "Congratulate me" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.150}.

## The Reason as Stated by Umar
Umar stated that the reason for his marriage to her was what he had heard from the Prophet regarding every tie of marriage and lineage being severed on the Day of Resurrection except his tie of marriage and his lineage; the text in this passage is garbled in the copy read, so it is not quoted verbatim {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.150}.

## Umar's Love for the Prophet's Household
Al-Sallabi holds that Umar harbored a special love for the Prophet's household that he did not hold for others because of their kinship to the Prophet and the Prophet's injunction to honor them, and for this reason he sought Umm Kulthum in marriage {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.150}. The book mentions Shi'i reports on this matter presented by Shaykh Ihsan Ilahi Zahir, and the book presents the reports without accepting all of them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.150}.
