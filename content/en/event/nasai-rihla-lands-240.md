## Summary
After 240/854 al-Nasa'i heard from a host of shaykhs in Khurasan, Iraq, al-Sham, Egypt, the Hijaz and the Jazira, travelling to Marw, Nishapur and beyond {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 106} {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 617}.

## His teachers there
Among the best known were Qutayba ibn Sa'id, Ishaq ibn Rahwayh, Hisham ibn Ammar, Isa ibn Hammad, al-Husayn ibn Mansur al-Sulami al-Naysaburi, Amr ibn Zurara, Muhammad ibn al-Nadr al-Marwazi, Abu Kurayb and Suwayd ibn Nasr {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 106}; scholars describe the journeys as extensive before he settled in Egypt {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1023}.
