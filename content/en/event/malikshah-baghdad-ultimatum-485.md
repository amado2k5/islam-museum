## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that [[person/malikshah|Malikshah]] returned to Baghdad in 485 AH, and sent to the caliph [[person/al-muqtadi-billah|al-Muqtadi]] that he should leave Baghdad to him and go to whatever land he chose. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.653}

## The respite and the death
The caliph asked for a respite, then ten days through the sultan's vizier. The sultan fell ill and died. This is counted as a mark of honour for the caliph, according to the account, and it is said that the caliph used to fast and curse Malikshah. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.653}

## After his death
His wife Turkan concealed his death and made the amirs swear allegiance to her son Mahmud, who was five years old. Then his brother Barkiyaruq rose against him. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.653}
