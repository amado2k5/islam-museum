## Overview
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that after [[person/ali-ibn-isa-ibn-mahan|Ali ibn Isa ibn Mahan]] was killed at Rayy, al-Amin sent Abd al-Rahman al-Abnawi with thirty thousand men of the Abna'; [[person/tahir-ibn-al-husayn|Tahir ibn al-Husayn]] met and defeated him, and he held out in [[place/hamadhan|Hamadhan]] for a month before taking safe-conduct. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 398} He then made a dawn attack on Tahir's men at Asadabad and was killed. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 399} Afterwards two of al-Amin's commanders, each with five thousand, fell back from Qarmisin to Hulwan without a fight. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 399} See [[event/battle-of-rayy-195|the battle of Rayy]].
