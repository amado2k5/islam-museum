## Summary
Recalled to Cairo in Ramadan 703 (April 1304), al-Nuwayri resumed work as mubashir amlak al-khass al-sharif, administering the diwan al-khass, the bimaristan al-Mansuri and the whole range of Mansuri waqfs, apparently under chief qadi Ibn Makhluf's supervision {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 157}.

## Witness to great affairs
Back in residence at the madrasa he witnessed the opening of the Ibn Taymiyya affair (705-709), which began at the Nasiriyya itself, and was pressed into a minor mediating role between his patron Ibn Makhluf and Damascus's governor Aqqush al-Afram. In 705 Ibn Sasra became chief qadi of Cairo and al-Nuwayri appeared as witness in some matters before him. When the amir al-Turkumani died he was charged with sequestering his assets and erecting him a turba with a maintenance waqf — al-Nasir himself walked to al-Qarafa to draw its plan on the ground {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 157-158}.
