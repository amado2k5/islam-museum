## Summary
This record covers what the books say of the last days of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] after the stabbing, and of his washing, funeral prayer, burial and what he left, complementing [[event/death-of-umar|the death of Umar]] and [[event/testament-of-umar-after-the-stabbing|his testament after the stabbing]]. He was stabbed on Wednesday, four nights before the end of Dhu al-Hijja 23 AH, lived three days and died; he was buried on Sunday, the first of Muharram 24 AH, in Aisha's room beside the Prophet and Abu Bakr. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 138} {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 418}

## His last days
They carried Umar to his house with the blood running; he would come to and then faint, and when they reminded him of the prayer he came to and said, "There is no share in Islam for one who abandons prayer," then made ablution and prayed. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 408} Ibn Kathir says that after the pilgrimage Umar had asked God at al-Abtah to take him and to grant him martyrdom in the city of His Prophet. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 137} In the report of Abu Ishaq the doctor said to him: make your will, for I think you will die today or tomorrow. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 409} Al-Muhibb al-Tabari says the people of historical knowledge agree that he lived three days after the stabbing and then died. {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 418}

Al-Muhibb al-Tabari also reports that as he lay dying with his head in his son Abd Allah's lap he was saying, "I have wronged myself, but I am a Muslim; I pray all my prayers and I fast." {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 418}

## Asking leave to be buried
Umar sent to Aisha to ask permission to be buried with his two companions, and she gave it, as in the report of al-Bukhari. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 403} {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 404} In the report from Urwa, Umar sent to her saying, "Let me be buried with my two companions," and she said, "Yes, by God"; whereas when a man among the Companions sent to her she would say, "No, by God, I will never prefer them." {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 408} Ibn Hajar relays from Ibn Sa'd, from Malik, that Umar feared she had given permission during his life out of shyness and might withdraw it after his death, so he told them to ask her again. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 413} Hafsa came with the women and wept beside him for a while, then went to an inner room set aside for the men. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 404}

## Washing, shroud and prayer
In Usd al-Ghaba Ibn al-Athir says that when Umar died the prayer was said over him in the mosque, he was carried on the Messenger's bier and his son Abd Allah washed him. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 914} From Ja'far ibn Muhammad, from his father: when Umar had been washed, shrouded and laid on his bier, Ali stood by him and said there was no man on earth whose record he would more love to meet God with than this one lying covered, and wept until his beard was wet. {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 419}

Two reports on the prayer fit together. From Urwa ibn al-Zubayr: when Umar was killed Ali and Uthman raced to pray over him, and Suhayb said, "Get away from me; I have been given more than the prayer over Umar in your affairs, and I lead you in the obligatory prayer," and Suhayb prayed over him. {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 418} In al-Bidaya Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf told Ali and Uthman, "This is not for you; it belongs to Suhayb, whom Umar charged to lead the people," and Suhayb stepped forward and prayed. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 145} Ibn Qutayba, quoted by Ibn al-Athir, says Suhayb prayed over him and he was buried with the Messenger of God and Abu Bakr. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 914}

## Burial and the grave
In Usd al-Ghaba those who went down into his grave were his son Abd Allah, Uthman ibn Affan, Sa'id ibn Zayd and Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 914} In al-Bidaya the members of the council went down with his son, except Talha, who was absent. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 145} In al-Bukhari's report they took out his bier and walked; Abd Allah ibn Umar greeted and said, "Umar ibn al-Khattab asks permission," Aisha said, "Bring him in," and he was laid there with his two companions. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 404}

Ibn Hajar relays from Ibn Sa'd that Aisha said she had not taken off her outer clothes since Umar was buried in her house. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 412} He also cites Ibn Zabala's Akhbar al-Madina, by a weak line from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, that the three graves lie in the porch of Aisha's house with a place beside them where Jesus will be buried, and mentions a report attributed to the Prophet that only his grave, those of Abu Bakr and Umar and a place for Jesus son of Mary were there, which he does not consider established. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 412} For the place see [[place/medina|Medina]] and [[building/prophets-mosque|the Prophet's Mosque]].

## What he left
Umar charged that his debt be paid, and Ibn Hajar sets out the disagreement over its size: eighty-six thousand in al-Bukhari's report, twenty-six thousand in Ibn Zabala, and Nafi' denied there was any debt because one heir sold his inheritance for a hundred thousand. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 411} {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 412} Ibn Kathir counts seven wives, whom Umar married in the age of ignorance and in Islam and who were divorced or left widowed, and thirteen children, among them Zayd the elder and Zayd the younger, Asim, Abd Allah, three named Abd al-Rahman, Ubayd Allah, Iyad, Hafsa, Ruqayya and Zaynab. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 139} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 140}

## Where the reports differ
- The day of the stabbing: Ibn Kathir prefers Wednesday with four nights remaining of Dhu al-Hijja and mentions other statements of three or seven nights remaining. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 138}
- The burial: al-Waqidi, from Ibn Sa'd, says he was buried on the morning of the new moon of Muharram 24, and Uthman al-Akhnasi considered that a slip. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 138} {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 914}
- His age: Ibn Kathir counts ten statements, including fifty-five and sixty-three, and says al-Waqidi found sixty the best established. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 138} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 139}
- Who prayed over him: Suhayb in the reports cited. {cite:riyad-nadira-ilmiyya-1984 scan vol. 3 p. 418}
- Who went into the grave: Abd Allah, Uthman, Sa'id and Abd al-Rahman in one report, the members of the council except Talha in another. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 914} {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 145}
