## Summary
In Sha'ban of 620 AH war broke out between the Muslims and the Georgians in Armenia around the fortress of Sarmari; its lord struck the Georgian rearguard and recovered what they had plundered. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 435}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the lord of Sarmari fortress, one of the districts of Armenia, travelled to Akhlat — for he owed obedience to its lord Shihab al-Din Ghazi ibn al-Adil Abu Bakr ibn Ayyub — and attended him, leaving an amir of his as deputy in his land; this amir gathered a force, raided the Georgian lands, plundered several villages and returned. The Georgians heard of it, so the lord of Dwin, named Shalwa, one of their chief amirs, gathered his army, marched to Sarmari, besieged it for days, plundered its town and countryside and withdrew. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 435} The lord of Sarmari heard the news, returned to Sarmari and arrived on the very day the Georgians had left; he took his troops, pursued them, fell on their rear, killed some of them, took booty and recovered what they had seized from his land. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 435}
