## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Bakr put [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] in command of the people and ordered him to march on [[person/tulayha-ibn-khuwaylid|Tulayha ibn Khuwaylid al-Asadi]], who had claimed prophethood while at Buzakha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.133-134} It is part of the [[event/ridda-wars|wars of the Ridda]].

## The battle
Khalid sent ahead [[person/ukasha-ibn-mihsan|Ukasha ibn Mihsan]] and [[person/thabit-ibn-aqram|Thabit ibn Aqram]], and they were killed by Hibal ibn Khuwaylid, then Tulayha and his brother. Then the two sides fought very fiercely. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.133-134}

[[person/uyayna-ibn-hisn|Uyayna ibn Hisn]] was with Tulayha, with seven hundred men of Banu Fazara. When he saw the swords of the Muslims prevailing, he asked Tulayha about Gabriel, then told his people that this man was a liar, and withdrew from him. The people were routed and the Muslims prevailed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.133-134}

## After the defeat
Uyayna was taken captive and brought to Medina, and Abu Bakr spared his blood and released him. Tulayha fled, put on the state of pilgrim for an umra, and then came to Medina. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.133-134}

The approximate date of 11 AH is derived.
