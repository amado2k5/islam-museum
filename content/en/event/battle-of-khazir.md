## Overview
In 66 AH [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]] marched with the armies of Syria toward Iraq. When he reached Mosul ([[place/mosul|Mosul]]), he met [[person/ibrahim-ibn-al-ashtar|Ibrahim ibn al-Ashtar]], who commanded the cavalry of Iraq on behalf of al-Mukhtar, at al-Khazir. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p. 209}

## The outcome
The book describes the battle as a great one. Ibn Ziyad was killed in it, along with al-Husayn ibn Numayr, Shurahbil ibn Dhi al-Kala' and notables of Syria. {cite:bakr-umayyad-state-1134715 p. 209}

al-Khudari reports that the victory went to Ibn al-Ashtar, and that the troops of Syria suffered killing and drowning. {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.300} He holds that their rout came when the vein of partisanship between Qays and Yemen throbbed. {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.365}
