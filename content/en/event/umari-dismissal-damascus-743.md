## Summary
Shihab al-Din was dismissed from the Damascus chancery on 3 Safar 743 (July 1342), his brother Badr al-Din Muhammad taking his place; he was held at the Fulkiyya four months, then summoned to Egypt over the many complaints against him until his brother Ala al-Din interceded and he returned to Damascus out of office — never to hold one again {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/333} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 790-791}.

## Details
The complaints against him multiplied until they brought him down; al-Dhahabi, who heard hadith with him from Sitt al-Qudat bint al-Shirazi, credits him with a long reach in the two crafts and brilliance in the two rhetorics {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/333} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 790-791}.
