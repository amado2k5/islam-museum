## The story
Malik relates from Zayd ibn Aslam from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Said al-Khudri from his father: the Prophet ordered the praying person not to let anyone pass between his hands and to push him away as much as he can; if he refuses, let him fight him — for he is only a shaytan {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-421|Muwatta 421]].

## The meaning
In this report, whoever passes before the praying person commits an act likened to the shaytan s cutting of the prayer, and the Prophet was stern in forbidding it {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
