## Summary
When al-Malik al-Zahir Ghazi died in 613/1216 al-Qifti resigned; three years later al-Zahir's successor al-Malik al-Aziz Muhammad restored him to the same post {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 872}.
