## Summary
Ibn al-Tiqtaqa appears to have travelled widely in Iraq and Adharbaydjan, and like his father he was a naqib of the Alids, though probably with more regionally limited authority {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.
