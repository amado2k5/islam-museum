## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that the island between the two rivers was salt marsh, and [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] granted it to one of his nephews. When the young man came to look at it, [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]] ordered water sent into it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The purchase
The young man said that the commander of the faithful had granted him a marsh he had no need of. Ziyad bought it from him for two hundred thousand dirhams, dug its channels and granted land from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
