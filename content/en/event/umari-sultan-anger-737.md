## Summary
When Tankiz asked al-Nasir to confirm Alam al-Din Ibn al-Qutb as katib al-sirr, Ibn Fadl Allah objected that he was a Copt; ordered to raise Ibn al-Qutb's salary he flared, 'Is it not enough that he is katib al-sirr, that his pay must be raised too?' then rose before the sultan saying, 'Your service is forbidden me.' Al-Nasir's wrath burst: he had Ahmad's brother Ala al-Din installed in his place and confined him to his house {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/332} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 790-791}.

## Details
Shihab al-Din told his father what had passed; the old man rose at once, went in to al-Nasir, apologized, confessed the fault and begged pardon. The sultan ordered that the son Ala al-Din be seated in Shihab al-Din's place and that Shihab al-Din be confined to his house {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/332}.
