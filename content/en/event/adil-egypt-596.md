## Summary
Al-Malik al-Adil Abu Bakr ibn Ayyub took the land of Egypt from his nephew al-Afdal, and so Syria, Egypt, al-Jazira, Yemen and the two sanctuaries came together under his rule. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 265}.
