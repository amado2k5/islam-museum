## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates several reports of the sending of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] of [[person/uthman-ibn-hunayf|Uthman ibn Hunayf]] to survey the Sawad and impose the land tax on it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.375-377}

## The survey
Uthman found the Sawad to be thirty-six million jaribs, and imposed on each jarib a dirham and a qafiz. Yahya ibn Adam says that the qafiz is the Hajjaji measure, sealed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.375-377}

## Distribution in Kufa
From Qatada from Abu Majlaz: Umar appointed [[person/ammar-ibn-yasir|Ammar ibn Yasir]] over prayer and the armies, [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud]] over judgement and the treasury, and Uthman ibn Hunayf over the surveying of the land. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.375-377}

He imposed on the jarib of palms and vines ten dirhams, on sugarcane six, on wheat four, and on barley two dirhams. He wrote this to Umar, who approved it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.375-377}

## Other accounts
- In the report of al-A'mash, Umar appointed [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] over what lay beyond the Tigris and Ibn Hunayf over what lay on this side, and they imposed a qafiz and a dirham on the jarib.
- [[person/al-mughira-ibn-shuba|al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba]] wrote that they had other kinds of grain, and eight of each was imposed.
- In the report of al-Iz'ar ibn Harith there are other details of the measures, among them ten dirhams on vines and sugar-cane and five on cotton.
- In the report of al-Sha'bi, five dirhams on sugar-cane and ten on vines. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.375-377}

The differences among the reports in the amounts are clear, and the museum does not weigh one above the others. The date is around 18 AH by estimate.
