## Summary
Yaqut returned from the island of Kish in 606/1209 to find his former master Askar dead {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121}.

## Books for capital
He satisfied the widow and sons with what fell to them, kept the remainder as his capital and travelled with it, making books part of his merchandise; selling books in Baghdad brought him to rare manuscripts and to the foremost scholars of the age {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 121} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.
