## Overview
[[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] was campaigning with the people of Syria and Iraq in the conquest of Armenia and Azerbaijan, and the disagreement of the reciters over the recitation alarmed him. He alerted [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] to this, and he copied the codices and sent them to the regions. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

## What Uthman did
Uthman sent to [[person/hafsa|Hafsa]] to borrow the pages that [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]] had gathered in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

He charged Zayd ibn Thabit, [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]], [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Sa'id ibn al-As]] and [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-al-harith|Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham]] with copying them into codices. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

He told the three Qurashis that when they disagreed with Zayd about something they should write it in the tongue of Quraysh, since the Qur'an was revealed in their tongue. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

He sent a codex to every region and ordered that every other page or codex be burned. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

## The report of Zayd
In the attached report about Zayd, a verse of al-Ahzab was missing at the copying, and was found with Khuzayma ibn Thabit. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4988}

## The date
The Bukhari report does not give a year. The event is estimated between about 25 and 30 AH, an estimate, and needs an additional source.

## The disagreement of boys and teachers
Ibn Ashta, as al-Suyuti relays, from Abu Qilaba, that Anas ibn Malik said they differed over reading in Uthman's time until the boys and teachers fought; it reached Uthman, who said: with me you deny it and err in it, so whoever is further from me is worse in denial and error; Companions of Muhammad, gather and write for the people an Imam. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 389} In the report, when they differed and pushed against each other over a verse, they would say: the Messenger of God taught this to so-and-so, and he would be sent for, though three days from Medina, and asked how the Messenger of God taught him, and they would write it, leaving a space for it. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 389} Al-Sabt comments that the distant cities differed and quarrelled more than Medina and the Hijaz, and that the seven ahruf were not known to all the people of those cities; each Companion in a region taught them only what he knew, and they had no comprehensive codex to refer to. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 235}

## The committee and its method
Al-Sabt says Uthman began carrying out the decision about the end of 24 and the beginning of 25 AH, entrusting the copying to four, Zayd ibn Thabit, Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr, Sa'id ibn al-As and Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham, the last three of Quraysh, though some reports say twelve men were assigned; they wrote nothing until it was put before the Companions and they confirmed that the Messenger of God had recited so. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 236} Ibn Abi Dawud relayed that twelve men of Quraysh and the Ansar sent for the rab'a in Umar's house, and when they disputed something they postponed it. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 390} Al-Sabt adds that they agreed to write in the codices only what they were sure was Qur'an and settled in the final review. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 236} See [[concept/al-arda-al-akhira|the final review]].

## What scholars took from Uthman's act
Al-Qadi Abu Bakr al-Baqillani, as al-Suyuti relays, says Uthman did not intend what Abu Bakr did, gathering the Qur'an between two boards, but intended to unite them on the readings established and known from the Prophet and to cancel what was not. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 392} Ali says, as Ibn Abi Dawud relays, that Uthman did what he did with the codices only with a group of the Companions. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 391} In another weak report Ali said: had I been in charge I would have done with the codices what Uthman did. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 393} On the number, the sending and the teaching see [[concept/masahif-al-amsar|the Uthmanic codices]]; on Ibn Mas'ud's stand see [[event/ibn-masud-and-the-uthmani-codex|Ibn Mas'ud and the Uthmanic codex]].
