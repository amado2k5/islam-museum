## Overview
[[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] was campaigning with the people of Syria and Iraq in the conquest of Armenia and Azerbaijan, and the disagreement of the reciters over the recitation alarmed him. He alerted [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] to this, and he copied the codices and sent them to the regions. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

## What Uthman did
Uthman sent to [[person/hafsa|Hafsa]] to borrow the pages that [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]] had gathered in the caliphate of Abu Bakr. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

He charged Zayd ibn Thabit, [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]], [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Sa'id ibn al-As]] and [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-al-harith|Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham]] with copying them into codices. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

He told the three Qurashis that when they disagreed with Zayd about something they should write it in the tongue of Quraysh, since the Qur'an was revealed in their tongue. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

He sent a codex to every region and ordered that every other page or codex be burned. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4987}

## The report of Zayd
In the attached report about Zayd, a verse of al-Ahzab was missing at the copying, and was found with Khuzayma ibn Thabit. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4988}

## The date
The Bukhari report does not give a year. The event is estimated between about 25 and 30 AH, an estimate, and needs an additional source.
