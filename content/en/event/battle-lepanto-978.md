## Overview
The battle of Lepanto took place in 978 AH / 1571 CE. The source available here gives no details of its course. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.273}

## What followed in North Africa
al-Sallabi says that the region that had belonged to the Beylerbey of Algiers was afterwards divided into three provinces: Tripoli, Tunis and Algiers. They became ordinary provinces like the rest of the Ottoman provinces. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.273}

An additional source is needed for the course of the battle and its date.
