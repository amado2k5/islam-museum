## Overview
The battle of Lepanto took place in 978 AH / 1571 CE. The source available here gives no details of its course. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.273}

## What followed in North Africa
al-Sallabi says that the region that had belonged to the Beylerbey of Algiers was afterwards divided into three provinces: Tripoli, Tunis and Algiers. They became ordinary provinces like the rest of the Ottoman provinces. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.273}

An additional source is needed for the course of the battle and its date.

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam supplies what this record lacked about the battle. It says Lepanto was fought in 1571, with the Ottoman state facing an alliance of Venice, the Habsburgs and the papacy; the Dictionary labels the Habsburgs Austro-Hungarian. It reports that the Ottoman defeat blocked the Ottoman advance into the Mediterranean and that it was read as a religious triumph for Christian Europe over the Muslim Turks. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1341} The description of the Habsburg partner is the Dictionary's own wording, and naming the exact branch would need another historical source.
