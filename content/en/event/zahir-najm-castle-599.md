## Summary
Al-Zahir Ghazi ibn Salah al-Din took the castle of Najm on the Euphrates from his brother al-Afdal. Al-Adil had assigned the castle to al-Afdal as an iqta' (land grant) in compensation for Egypt. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 283}
