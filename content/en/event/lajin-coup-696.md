## Lajin's coup against al-Malik al-ʿAdil Kitbugha in 696 AH
Al-Malik al-ʿAdil Kitbugha set out for Egypt. When he reached al-Lajjun, Husam al-Din Lajin al-Mansuri fell upon Bayhas and Baktut al-Azraq, the two wings of their master al-ʿAdil, and killed them {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 758}.

Al-ʿAdil was afraid. He rode in secret with four mamluks and made for Damascus, entering the citadel, but this did not help him, and his rule ended. The Egyptians submitted to Husam al-Din, and no two of them disagreed about him. He took the title al-Malik al-Mansur. Al-ʿAdil was placed under guard and settled in the fortress of Salkhad, where he remained content {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 758}.
