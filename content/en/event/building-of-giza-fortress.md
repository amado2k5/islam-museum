## Overview
Ibn Abd al-Hakam reports from Ibn Lahi'a, from Yazid ibn Abi Habib and Ibn Hubayra, that Hamdan and their allies chose to settle at [[place/al-jizah|Giza]]. [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] wrote to [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr]]: how could he accept his companions being scattered from him, with a river between them so that he could not know what might befall them? He told him to gather them to him, and if they refused, to build them a fortress from the Muslims' fay'. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 175}

## The building
Amr put it to them and they refused, liking their position at Giza, so he built them the fortress at Giza in the year 21 and finished it in the year 22. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 176} Other Egyptian elders report that they told Amr: "It is a forward post we have advanced in the path of God; we will not leave it for another." {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 176}

## The people of Giza
Giza was settled by Yafi', among them Mubarrah ibn Shihab, by Hamdan, by Dhu Asbah, among them Abu Shamir ibn Abraha, and by a group of al-Hajr, among them Alqama ibn Junada. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 176}
