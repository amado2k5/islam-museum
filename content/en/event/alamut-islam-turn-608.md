## Overview
In 608 AH an envoy of Jalal al-Din Hasan, lord of Alamut, came to the caliph. He reported that his people had entered Islam and disowned the Batiniyya. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.39-40}

## What the report says
"Shadharat al-Dhahab" reports that they built mosques and congregational mosques and fasted Ramadan, and that the caliph was glad of this. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.39-40}

## Note
The report rests on a single source, and so does its date. It needs an additional source.
