## Overview
al-Tabari reports that in 130 AH the battle of the Kharijite Abu Hamza took place at Qudayd, and then he entered Medina. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.393} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.394}

He had met Abd Allah ibn Yahya, known as Talib al-Haqq, in 128 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.348}

Needs an additional source: the date comes from al-Tabari's index alone, and the data holds no details of the battle.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] places the battle of Qudayd in the year 130: the people of Medina met Abu Hamza at Qudayd on Thursday the ninth of Safar and were routed; he then gives a roll of those killed at Qudayd. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 391} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 392} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 393}
