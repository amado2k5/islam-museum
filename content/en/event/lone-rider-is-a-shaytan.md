## The story
Malik relates from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala from Amr ibn Shuayb from his father from his grandfather that the Prophet said: the lone rider is a shaytan, two riders are two shaytans, and three are a riding party {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2801|Muwatta 2801]].

## The meaning
In this report, travelling alone exposes one to the shaytan, and companionship on the road is a safeguard from his whispers and schemes {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
