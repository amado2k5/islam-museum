## Overview
[[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] says that when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] reached upper [[place/najran|Najran]], Wadi'a, Shakir, Thaqif, Yam and the Ahlaf met him, were glad of his coming and pledged; they told him what had passed between them and Banu al-Harith, men killed, wealth lost, roads cut, what is sacred violated and homes ruined, and put themselves, their blood and their property in his hand, which he thanked them for. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} The editor's footnotes say Wadi'a is a Yemeni tribe, Shakir a tribe of Hamdan, Yam a Yemeni tribe and Banu al-Harith ibn Ka'b a Madhhij tribe. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} See [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab]].

## The reconciliation and the oath
He went on near the village of Najran, where Banu al-Harith met and greeted him and were glad of his nearness after the trial and strife and the loss of men and wealth between themselves and Hamdan; he alighted "under athl", glossed from al-Qamus as trees, before the village, called Hamdan and Banu al-Harith and sat them by him, gave an eloquent sermon reminding them of God and His days, then had a Qur'an brought and made them swear to one another and to hear and obey him, to give up strife and enmity, and to enjoin good and forbid wrong; a great number pledged to him on the spot and mixed together. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68}

## At the village of al-Ajar
He then rode on to a village of Najran read as "al-Ajar" (the reading is not certain) and stayed days, showing his justice, righting wrongs and dividing equally; the country grew calm, people went back to their homes and property and were safe on the roads; on the Friday he led the prayer and in his sermon called to the truth, to allegiance to him and to jihad with him, and people hurried to pledge, and he took pledges until the afternoon prayer. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.68} This is a Zaydi description of his conduct, attributed to its narrator.
