## Summary
Amr ibn al-As marched from Egypt to Barqa and made peace with its people for a tribute of thirteen thousand dinars; they stipulated that they could sell whichever of their children they wished in payment of the tribute. He then besieged Tripoli for a month without success, until a man of the Banu Mudlij went out hunting with seven companions. To escape the heat they took the route along the sea, and found a passage between the sea and the town where the wall did not run continuous. They entered and shouted takbir. The Byzantines thought the town had been taken and fled to their ships, and Amr entered with his army; the Byzantines escaped only with what they could carry lightly. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 408-409}
