## Summary
Al-Nuwayri began a second copy of the Nihaya: its first volume finished 20 Dhu al-Qa'da 721/11 December 1321, the fifth on 22 Jumada I 722/8 June 1322, the seventeenth on 7 Ramadan 722/19 September and the eighteenth on 26 Ramadan/8 October — eighteen volumes in under ten months {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 159}.

## The copyist's capacity
His biographers noted he could fill three manuscript notebooks in a day; besides the copies of his encyclopaedia he made and sold there were eight copies of the Sahih of al-Bukhari — writing the copy, filling it, carrying over its collations and readings and finishing it in a thousand — and he was deemed an excellent calligrapher and bookbinder {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 159} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/197}.
