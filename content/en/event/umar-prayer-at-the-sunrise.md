## The story
Malik relates from Ibn Umar that Umar ibn al-Khattab used to say: "do not seek your prayer at sunrise or sunset, for the shaytan's horns rise with the sunrise and set with the sunset," and he would beat people for praying then {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-589|Muwatta 589]].

## The meaning
In Umar's saying the prohibited prayer-times are tied to the shaytan's horn; at sunrise and sunset the sun is where the shaytan holds sway, which is why prayer was forbidden in those hours {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}. The Muwatta cites parallels of this prohibition in the chapters of prayer-times {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.

## Notes
This is among the reports of the Companions' dealings with the shaytan in the Muwatta; this browser shows it in the devils section {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
