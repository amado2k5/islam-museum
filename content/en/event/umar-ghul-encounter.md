## What the books of reports relate
The books of history and reports mention that Umar ibn al-Khattab met the ghul on one of his journeys before Islam; in some versions he heard its wailing at night or saw it, and the scholars have classed these reports among the weakly transmitted lore of the jinn {cite:muruj-al-dhahab} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl}.

## The ghul in the jinn books
The ghul is one of the creatures of the jinn in the Arabic books of lore: the books of natural history describe it as a being that takes a woman's or man's shape to mislead the traveller in the wilderness; its reports in this browser are gathered under its concept record {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}. This episode joins the rest of what the lore books record of humans meeting jinn before Islam.
