## Overview
Al-Tabari relates that in 256 AH the men of the [[person/sahib-al-zanj|Sahib al-Zanj]] entered Ahwaz, and took captive Ibrahim ibn al-Mudabbir. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.472}

Ibn al-Jawzi, on the other hand, relates the entry of the Zanj into Ahwaz in 259 AH, and says that they killed about fifty thousand. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.101}

The two dates differ between the two sources, and the data does not settle between them.
