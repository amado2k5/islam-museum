## نظرة عامة

The Prophet held a race for the trained horses from al-Hafya' to Thaniyyat al-Wada' — some six or seven miles — and one for the untrained from al-Thaniyya to the mosque of Banu Zurayq, a mile or so; Abdullah ibn Umar was among the winners — his report in the two Sahihs {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
