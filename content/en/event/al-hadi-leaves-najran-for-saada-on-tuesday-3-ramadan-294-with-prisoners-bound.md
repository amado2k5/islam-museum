## Overview
Al-Hadi left Najran on Tuesday, 3 Ramadan. He took the Qarmatian prisoners tied with ropes, the Ya'am prisoners on camels, and Ali ibn al-Rabi' bound. Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah escorted him to al-Qadar al-A'la and then went back to al-Hijr.

## Detail
The book says al-Hadi left on Tuesday, 3 Ramadan. He ordered the Qarmatian prisoners in his custody tied with ropes, the Ya'am prisoners loaded onto camels, and Ali ibn al-Rabi' brought bound and guarded by a group of men. Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah escorted him to a place called al-Qadar al-A'la in Najran and then went back to al-Hijr, where he stayed some days without trouble. The narrator says the town was calm, the truth showed and falsehood faded. The tabular conversion of the day is derived; the book's weekday is recorded as given.
