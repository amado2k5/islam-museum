## Overview
Al-Kufi relates that one night, being with [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], he saw a weak man come to him at the hour before dawn seeking redress against some people; he knocked at the door and al-Hadi said "Who knocks at this hour?"; a man at the door said "A man seeking redress", and he said "Let him in." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.62} The man made his complaint, and al-Hadi sent three men with him then and there to bring his opponents, and told his narrator: "Abu Jafar, praise be to God, who has favoured us with His blessing and made us a mercy to His creatures: this man comes to us for redress at this hour; were he to go to any of these tyrants he would not have come near his door at this hour"; and added: "An imam of ours is not one who hides himself from the weak in a time of pressing need." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.62} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.63} The saying contains the imam's judgement of "the tyrants" in general, which the museum gives as his, naming nobody.
