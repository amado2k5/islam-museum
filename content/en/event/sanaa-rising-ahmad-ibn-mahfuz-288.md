## Overview
After the events at Shibam a crowd from [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]] rose with [[person/ahmad-ibn-mahfuz|Ahmad ibn Mahfuz]], broke the prison and drove [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] out, and [[person/abd-al-qahir-ibn-ahmad-ibn-naim|Abd al-Qahir ibn Ahmad ibn Na'im]] took the city. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} The text does not give the day of the rising, but it follows the Shibam account of Jumada II 288 AH, so it is dated to the year only.

## Where the account stands
It belongs to the pages the editor thinks were added to the original. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20}
