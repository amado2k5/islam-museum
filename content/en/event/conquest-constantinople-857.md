## Overview
Sultan [[person/mehmed-ii|Mehmed the Conqueror]] took Constantinople on Wednesday, 20 Jumada al-Akhira 857 AH, after a siege that lasted fifty days, according to Ibn al-Imad. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.344-345}

## The conquest
Ibn al-Imad says that the sultan brought ships overland and by sea, besieged the city with the utmost severity, and took it on the fifty-first day. He prayed the Friday prayer in Hagia Sophia. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.344-345}

## Inside the war council
al-Sallabi relates that when four ships sent by the Pope reached the city, the emirs and viziers gathered and told the sultan that there was no hope of conquest. He sent his vizier to the tent of [[person/aq-shams-al-din|Shaykh Aq Shams al-Din]], who answered that God would certainly grant the conquest. The war council decided to continue. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.113-114}
