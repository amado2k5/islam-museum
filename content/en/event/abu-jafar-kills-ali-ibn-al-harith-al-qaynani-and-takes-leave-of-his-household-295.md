## Overview
Wounded, Abu Ja'far killed his attacker Ali ibn al-Harith al-Qaynani by thrusting his sword into him, then took leave of his women and children and commended them to God.

## Detail
When Ali ibn al-Harith al-Qaynani came to seize him, Abu Ja'far could not hold his sword in his hand because of the wound, so he set the blade against the man's chest and leaned on it with both hands until it went through him, and the man fell dead. He then charged his women and children, greeted them and bade them farewell, and said that God was their successor. He praised and thanked God and asked that God let him reach the praised station promised to His friends, and said that he had kept his pledge. One sentence in this passage is garbled in the printed text, so the record gives its sense only.
