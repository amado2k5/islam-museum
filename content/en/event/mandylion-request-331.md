## Overview
In 331 a letter came from the king of the Byzantines seeking the kerchief on which — as the account runs — Christ had wiped his face so that his image was fixed in it, that kerchief being in the church of Edessa; and he promised, if it were sent to him, to release the Muslim captives {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 623}.
