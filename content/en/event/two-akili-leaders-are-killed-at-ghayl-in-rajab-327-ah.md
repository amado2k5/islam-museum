## Overview
The Akil leave Alaf with Ibn al-Dahhak and come to al-Ghayl on Wednesday, sixteen nights into Rajab 327 AH. Al-Qasim goes out against them, and the two sons of Abbad al-Akili are killed with a group of their men, and they flee back to Alaf.

## Detail
The book says the Akil left Alaf with Ibn al-Dahhak, gathered a force and came to al-Ghayl on Wednesday, sixteen nights into Rajab of the same year. Al-Qasim went out against them with his men, and the battle broke out. Al-Wajih and Ahmad, the two sons of Abbad ibn Abdallah al-Akili, were killed with a group of their companions, and they fled to Alaf. A number of Ibn al-Dahhak's men were taken captive, and al-Qasim pardoned them and let them go. Ibn al-Dahhak and the Akil stayed at Alaf to await the gathering of their tribes.
