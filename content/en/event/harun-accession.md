## Overview
[[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] succeeded by his father's appointment when his brother al-Hadi died. It was on the night of Saturday, fourteen nights before the end of Rabi al-Awwal 170 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.456}

On the same night a son was born to him, [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|Abd Allah al-Ma'mun]]. Al-Suyuti relates from al-Suli that in no other age was there a night in which a caliph died, another caliph rose, and a caliph's son was born, except this one. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.456}
