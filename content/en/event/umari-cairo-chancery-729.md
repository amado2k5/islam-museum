## Summary
When Muhyi al-Din Yahya ibn Fadl Allah was made head of the chancery and katib al-sirr at Cairo in 729/1329 after Damascus, his son Shihab al-Din Ahmad began his public career as an assistant to his ageing father {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 pp. 790-791}.

## Details
His father Muhyi al-Din Yahya had first headed the chancery at Damascus before being transferred to Egypt, and Shihab al-Din attended him in office and inherited his gift for ornate official prose {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/425-426}.
