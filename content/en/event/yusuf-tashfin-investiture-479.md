## Overview
In 479 AH [[person/yusuf-ibn-tashfin|Yusuf ibn Tashfin]], lord of Ceuta and Marrakesh, sent to the caliph [[person/al-muqtadi-billah|al-Muqtadi]] asking that he invest him with the sultanate and confirm to him the lands in his hand. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.652}

## The investiture
Al-Muqtadi sent him the robes of honour, the banners and the investiture, and gave him the title "Commander of the Muslims." Yusuf rejoiced at it, and the jurists of the Maghrib were pleased by it. Al-Suyuti says that it was he who founded the city of Marrakesh. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.652}
