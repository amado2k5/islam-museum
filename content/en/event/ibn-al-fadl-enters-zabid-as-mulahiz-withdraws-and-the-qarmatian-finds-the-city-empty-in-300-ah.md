## Overview
Ibn al-Fadl enters Zabid after Mulahiz leaves it, and the Qarmatian finds the city empty of money and people in 300 AH.

## Detail
Ibn al-Fadl sets out against Mulahiz at Zabid. Jarrah, who had been in correspondence with Ibn al-Fadl and whom the book says had joined his unbelief, is asked to come out under a pledge of safety and does so as far as al-Mawr in Tihama. Ibn al-Fadl reaches Zabid; Mulahiz leaves with his men and the townspeople, and they scatter across Tihama, Mulahiz going to al-Mahjam. The book then says the Qarmatian entered Zabid and found no money and no one; the sentence does not say whom it means, and the context suggests Ibn al-Fadl. The sentence has no day, so the year alone is given.
