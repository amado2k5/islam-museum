## Overview
At dawn on the Saturday they went early to Arthal and plundered all the property in it; some of their troops came into view and parleyed with the people of Bayt Bus, and Ibn Khalaf sent a contingent from Sana'a, which entered with the people of the fort and stood with the men until midday with no fighting between them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.270} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.271}. They then went back to their camp, and the contingent asked Ibn Khalaf for its pay and he gave it nothing, so they went back to Sana'a, and each stayed where he was on the night of Sunday {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.271}.

## Detail
The Arthal here is the place where Jaftam had been seized before, in [[event/jaftam-is-seized-at-arthal-and-jailed-at-bayt-bus-290|the report of his seizing]], and the name is read as printed. "They" is the troops of the two sons of Ya'fur named on p. 270, and the contingent is one Ibn Khalaf sent from Sana'a, not al-Hadi's men. The book does not give the reason for its leaving unpaid; the narrator reports it. The date is to the month, since the museum could not match the Saturday and Sunday to the tabular calendar. The report follows [[event/abu-al-qasim-tells-his-dream-of-the-sons-of-yafur-and-the-rising-of-the-prisons-291|Abu al-Qasim's dream and the breaking of the prison of Sana'a]]. The narrator belongs to the imam's followers in a Zaydi book, and what he says of the opponents is his own, with no verdict in the museum's voice.
