## Overview
In 739 AH Taqi al-Din al-Subki came from Egypt as judge of Damascus and its districts; the people rejoiced at his learning, piety and honesty. He lodged at the Adiliyya al-Kubra as his predecessors had, taught at the Ghazzaliyya and the Atabakiyya, and succeeded the late chief qadi Jalal al-Din Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Qazwini {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 739 pp. 195-196}. He held the bench about seventeen years {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 756 p. 264}.
