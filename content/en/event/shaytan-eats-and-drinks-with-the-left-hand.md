## The story
Malik relates from Ibn Shihab from Abu Bakr ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Abdullah ibn Umar from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God said: when one of you eats let him eat with his right hand, and let him drink with his right hand, for the shaytan eats with his left and drinks with his left {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2671|Muwatta 2671]].

## The meaning
In this hadith the right hand is the etiquette of eating and drinking, and the shaytan differs from the believer in it, eating and drinking with the left {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
