## Summary
[[person/sad-ibn-al-rabi|Sad ibn al-Rabi]] was a Khazraji of the Ansar, a leader at al-Aqaba, a veteran of Badr, and was killed as a martyr at [[place/uhud|Uhud]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira scan p. 395} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 316} The books keep his last message to the Prophet and to his people, the words he gave to the man who came looking for him among the dead, then what he left in property and two daughters, and where he was buried.

## Before the message
Al-Dhahabi says the Prophet paired Sad with [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]], and Sad offered him half his wealth and to divorce one of his two wives so that Abd al-Rahman might marry her; Abd al-Rahman declined and prayed for him. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 316} Ibn Hisham relates from Abu Bakr al-Zubayri that a man came in to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and found a small girl, a daughter of Sad ibn al-Rabi, on his chest as he kissed her; the man asked who she was and he said: the daughter of a man better than I, Sad ibn al-Rabi, who was one of the leaders on the night of al-Aqaba, fought at Badr and was killed at Uhud. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira scan p. 395}

## The message as Ibn Hisham relates it
In Ibn Ishaq's report, from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Sasaa, the Prophet said: who will look for me to see what became of Sad ibn al-Rabi, whether he is among the living or among the dead? A man of the Ansar said: I will; he went about among the slain until he found him wounded with some breath left. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 316} The man told him that the Messenger of God had ordered him to see whether he was among the living or the dead; he said: I am among the dead, so carry my greeting to the Messenger of God and tell him that Sad ibn al-Rabi says to you: may God reward you for us with the best He ever rewarded a prophet for his people. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira scan p. 395} He sent his greeting to his people too, saying that they would have no excuse before God if harm reached their prophet while an eye of theirs still blinked. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira scan p. 395} The man said: I did not leave until he died, and I went to the Messenger of God and told him. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira scan p. 395}

## Other reports, with the messenger named
Ibn Abd al-Barr says Malik relayed this report without naming the man, and that Rubayh ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Said al-Khudri, from his father, from his grandfather, said the man was [[person/ubayy-ibn-kab|Ubayy ibn Kab]], and that the Prophet said that day that he had seen spearpoints levelled at Sad. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280} In Malik's wording Sad said he had been struck with twelve thrusts and that his vital parts were pierced. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280} In Ubayy's version Sad said: by God, by God, remember what you pledged to the Messenger of God on the night of al-Aqaba; you will have no excuse before God if harm reaches your prophet while an eye of yours still blinks. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280} Ubayy said: I did not leave until he died; I told the Prophet, who said that he had been sincere to God and His messenger, living and dead. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280}

Al-Dhahabi gives a report from Kharija ibn Zayd, from his father [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]], that the Prophet sent him on the day of Uhud to look for Sad, saying: if you see him, give him my greeting and ask him how he finds himself. He found him at his last breath with seventy wounds; Sad answered: peace on the Messenger of God and on you; tell him I find the scent of Paradise, and tell my people the Ansar that they will have no excuse before God if harm reaches the Messenger of God while an eyelash of theirs still moves; then his soul went out. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 317}

## What he left
Al-Dhahabi relays from Jabir that Sad's wife came with their two daughters and said their father had been killed with the Prophet at Uhud as a martyr, that their uncle had taken their property and left them nothing, and that they could not be married without property; the Prophet said God would decide in the matter, the verses of inheritance were revealed, and he sent for their uncle and told him to give the two daughters two thirds, their mother an eighth, and keep the rest. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 317} Al-Isti'ab says he left two daughters and the Prophet gave them two thirds, which was his first explanation of the verse on inheritance. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280}

## Burial
Ibn Abd al-Barr relays from Ibn Ishaq that Sad ibn al-Rabi and Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Abi Zuhayr were buried in one grave at Uhud. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280} In the pages I read I found no report of his washing or of prayer over him, and no description of his grave.

## Where the reports differ
The reports differ on the messenger: unnamed in Ibn Hisham and in Malik's version, Ubayy ibn Kab in Ibn Abd al-Barr on al-Khudri's authority, Zayd ibn Thabit in Kharija ibn Zayd's. They differ in Sad's wording: twelve thrusts in some, seventy wounds in others, the scent of Paradise in one. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 280} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 2 p. 317} They agree on his message to his people, that they have no excuse if harm reaches the Prophet.

## Sources and documentation
This record rests on Ibn Hisham's Sira, al-Dhahabi's Siyar Alam al-Nubala and Ibn Abd al-Barr's al-Isti'ab. The page numbers given are scan pages.
