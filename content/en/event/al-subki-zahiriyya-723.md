## Overview
In Shawwal 723 al-Subki took up the shaykhhood of the Zahiriyya hadith school in Cairo after Zaki al-Din Abd al-Azim ibn Sharaf al-Din al-Dimyati; the post was taken from him in Dhu al-Qa'da for Fath al-Din Ibn Sayyid al-Nas al-Ya'mari {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 723 p. 109}.
