## Overview
Ibn Hajar died in Cairo on the night of Saturday 18 Dhu al-Hijja 852, about an hour after the evening prayer (22 February 1449), and was buried at al-Rumayla; his funeral was famously thronged {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 273} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 810}. His last illness had begun in Dhu al-Qa'da that year, months after the Baybarsiyya khanqah was restored to him and the chief judgeship finally lost; his pupil al-Sakhawi wrote his biography in al-Jawahir wa'l-Durar {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 810}.
