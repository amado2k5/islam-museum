## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that the king of Kabul, Kabul Shah, paid the tribute and submitted to obedience after [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] sent an army against him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
When al-Ma'mun succeeded, he raided Sughd, Ushrusana and Farghana, and pressed them with raids while he was in Khurasan. He wrote to them calling them to Islam and obedience. He sent an army against [[place/kabul|Kabul]] Shah; he paid the tribute and submitted. The post reached it so that fresh myrobalan was carried from it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
