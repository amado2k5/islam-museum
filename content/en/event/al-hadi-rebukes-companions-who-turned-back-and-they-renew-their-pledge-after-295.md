## Overview
After Yusuf's killing, al-Hadi rebukes the companions who fled, and they apologise, renew the pledge and make their repentance sound.

## Detail
When Yusuf's body was brought in, al-Hadi came out although he was sick and saw his companions in flight. He rebuked them. He said that he had been away from them for one hour and the enemy found an entry, and that they had not turned back for their wounded brother who was hit among them, and that this was the fruit of corrupt intentions, a hidden weariness of holy war and weak certainty. They said that the fault had fallen on them for what they did, and that his rebuke and his arguments against them went on until they renewed the pledge and made their repentance sound. Al-Hadi then said that people turn back only because of some disobedience among them, and began to tell them the story the Qur'an tells of the people of Moses and of Bal'am ibn Ba'ura.
