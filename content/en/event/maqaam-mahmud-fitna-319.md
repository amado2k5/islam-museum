## Overview
In 319 AH a strife broke out in Baghdad over the interpretation of the verse "the praised station", which is [[passage/quran-17-79|Al-Isra 79]]. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 593}

## The dispute
Al-Suyuti, in "History of the Caliphs", says that the Hanbalis held that its meaning is that God seats him on His throne, and others said it is intercession. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 593}

## Outcome
The quarrel lasted until they fought, and al-Suyuti says that a great many people were killed. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 593}
