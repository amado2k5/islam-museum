## Overview
Ibn Khaldun reports that when the sultan Abu Inan al-Marini died in 759 AH, the resolve of [[person/abu-hammu-musa-ii|Abu Hammu Musa ibn Yusuf]] grew strong to recover his kingdom. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Recovery
The Dawawida and the Zughba from Tunis carried him to Tlemcen, where there were battalions of the Banu Marin. He besieged it for three days, then stormed it on the morning of the fourth day, Wednesday 8 Rabi al-Awwal 760 AH. He occupied his palace, received the oath of allegiance, and drove the Banu Marin out of the cities of his realm. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
