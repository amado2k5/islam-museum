## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Abu Ubayda appointed [[person/ubada-ibn-al-samit|Ubada ibn al-Samit]] over Homs, and he came to [[place/ladhiqiyya|Latakia]], where its people fought him. There was a great gate there that a multitude of people could not open. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.180}

## The ruse
Ubada encamped far from the city and dug trenches in which a man and his horse could hide. Then the Muslims showed that they were returning to Homs, and came back at night to their trenches. When the people of Latakia opened their gate in the morning, the Muslims surprised them and entered. It was taken by force, and the Muslims built a congregational mosque there on Ubada's order. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.180}

## After the conquest
Some Christians fled and then asked for safe-conduct, and they were assessed a tribute and allowed to keep their church. In 100 AH the Byzantines raided the coast and demolished the city, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ordered it rebuilt. Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik completed it after his death. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.181}

The conquest is part of the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]]. al-Baladhuri also credits Ubada and the Muslims with him with the conquest of Antartus. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.181}
