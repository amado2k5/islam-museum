## Overview
Ifriqiya rose: Heraclius sent his patrician to take from its people what the Muslims had taken; they refused, saying "we pay what was taken from us — he ought to have excused us given what the Muslims took of us." The patrician landed at Carthage and gathered the people of Ifriqiya. Mu'awiya dispatched the exiled patrician's counterpart Mu'awiya b. Hudayj al-Sakuni; he came to an Ifriqiya ablaze, camped at Qammuniyya, met the patrician's thirty thousand, routed the Romans, and Ibn Hudayj mastered Ifriqiya {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 460}.
