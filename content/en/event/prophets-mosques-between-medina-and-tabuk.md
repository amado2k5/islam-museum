## Summary
Ibn Ishaq enumerates seventeen places between Medina and Tabuk where the Prophet had known, named mosques. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174-175}

## The places
A mosque at Tabuk; at Thaniyyat Madaran; at Dhat al-Zurab; at al-Akhdar; at Dhat al-Khitmi; at Ala'; at Taraf al-Batra' from Dhanab Kawakib; at al-Shiqq (Shiqq Tara); at Dhi al-Jifa; at Sadr Hawda; at al-Hijr; at al-Su'ayd; at al-Wadi (today's Wadi al-Qura); at al-Raq'a of al-Shiqqa (Shiqqa of Banu Udhra); at Dhi al-Marwa; at al-Fayfa'; and at Dhi Khashab. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174-175} The table is in [[dataset/prophets-mosques-between-medina-and-tabuk|the dataset]].

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- "Dhat al-Khitmi" is verified on the page image (machine reading confuses it).
- The text does not pinpoint these mosques' locations, nor whether they were buildings or prayer-stations; the heading's word "masajid" is Ibn Ishaq's wording.
- Three of the places have pages from batch 6: [[place/al-akhdar|al-Akhdar]], [[place/al-hijr|al-Hijr]] and [[place/tabuk|Tabuk]].
