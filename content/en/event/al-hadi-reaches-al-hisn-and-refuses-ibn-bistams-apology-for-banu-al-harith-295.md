## Overview
Al-Hadi reached al-Hisn in Shawwal 295. Ibn Bistam met him and apologised for Banu al-Harith, but al-Hadi would not accept the apology without questioning Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and clarifying the whole matter. Ibn Bistam went home downcast. Al-Hadi then entered the village and lodged in a house there.

## Detail
The book says that when al-Hadi reached al-Hisn, Ibn Bistam met him, greeted him and apologised on behalf of Banu al-Harith. Al-Hadi would not accept the apology without questioning Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah and clarifying the matter, and Ibn Bistam went back to his place in low spirits. The next morning al-Hadi rode into the village. The bondsmen of Banu al-Harith came to him seeking protection, and he granted it and treated them well. He sent word to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah not to leave the village until he came to him, and Muhammad met him only at the Gate of al-Darb. Al-Hadi entered the village, lodged in a house there, ordered Muhammad to billet the army, and warned that the troops must not harm the people of the village or enter their women's quarters. A large force of horse and foot was with him.
