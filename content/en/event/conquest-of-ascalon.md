## Summary
In 23 AH [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]] led the summer expedition accompanied by [[person/ubada-ibn-al-samit|'Ubada ibn al-Samit]], [[person/abu-ayyub-al-ansari|Abu Ayyub al-Ansari]], Abu Dharr and Shaddad ibn Aws, and took Ascalon by a peace settlement. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir records under the events of year twenty-three of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|'Umar ibn al-Khattab's]] caliphate that Mu'awiya campaigned in the summer expedition with 'Ubada ibn al-Samit, Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, Abu Dharr and Shaddad ibn Aws in his company {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}. In that year Mu'awiya conquered Ascalon "on sulh" — by a negotiated settlement with its people rather than by storm {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}. Listing the officials of that year, he notes that Shurayh was judge of Kufa and Ka'b ibn Sur judge of Basra, adding the report that Abu Bakr and 'Umar had no judge of their own {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}. Among the year's deaths he names Qatada ibn al-Nu'man al-Ansari {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}. The fall of Ascalon belongs to the Syrian coastal campaigns Mu'awiya conducted during his governorship of Syria, extending Muslim rule over the Palestinian littoral shortly before the end of 'Umar's caliphate {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 449}.
