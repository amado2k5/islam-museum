## Summary
In the days of the caliph al-Muqtadi, [[person/nizam-al-mulk|Nizam al-Mulk]] gathered the astrologers, who fixed Nawruz at the first point of Aries. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.650}

## The change
Before this, Nawruz fell when the sun entered the middle of Pisces. The source says that what Nizam al-Mulk did became the basis of the calendars. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.650}

The source does not specify the year; it is approximately 471 AH, during the caliphate of al-Muqtadi. This needs an additional source.
