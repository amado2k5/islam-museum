## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that when Musa al-Hadi came to Rayy, he went to [[place/qazwin|Qazvin]] and ordered a city built on its land, known as the City of Musa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Endowment and governors
He bought land called Rustambadh and made it an endowment for the city's needs. His client Amr al-Rumi governed it, and after him Muhammad ibn Amr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The City of al-Mubarak
Al-Mubarak al-Turki built a fortress that was called the City of al-Mubarak, and a group of his clients lived in it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
