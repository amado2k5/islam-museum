## Overview
After [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] entered [[place/al-hajar-najran|al-Hajar]] the people fell away from him, and only six men and seven horsemen went in with him; he went on until he stood at the door of the house where he lived in the village, with the camps of [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] facing him in the middle of the street, gathered at the mosque about half a bowshot away. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.165} Seeing how few were with him they shot at them with arrows, and he ordered the six to draw up facing the people and took counsel with his horsemen, who advised him to leave and hurry out, since the people were great armies, horse and foot. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.165}

## Al-Hadi's answer
The book says al-Hadi told them that if they left they would never enter it again and the people would grow bold against them; he called them to patience, asked them to cover his back and leave him and what lay before him, and they undertook to do so. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.165} This is the narrator's report of his words; the museum offers no estimate of the situation.

## The charge in the street and the lane
He drew his companions up behind him and charged the people; the first of them was a man who often abused him, and he speared him in the chest so that the spear came out at his back and he fell dead; he went on cutting through them and they broke to the side of the street and entered a lane beside the mosque. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.165} He followed them into the lane and pressed them until, packed tight, they stood with him in their midst, unable to strike him for the crush, while his companions charged from behind; when the people saw him among them they despaired of him and went off to the end of the street. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.165} Al-Hadi then drove his horse to the end of the lane and turned toward them, not knowing where his companions had gone, with the Harithis between him and the road. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.165} The story continues in [[event/al-hadi-kills-abu-al-wajih-and-takes-al-hajar-286]].
