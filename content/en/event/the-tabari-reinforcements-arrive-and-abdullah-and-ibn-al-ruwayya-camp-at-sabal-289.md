## Overview
After [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] took up behind him on his horse a weary man of the Tabaris and brought him to [[place/sanaa|Sana'a]], a reinforcement of [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] reached him on Thursday, nine nights gone of Safar in the year 289, and they stayed some days at Sana'a {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236}. On Tuesday, fourteen days of Safar gone, he ordered his brother [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] and [[person/rabi-ibn-al-ruwayya|Rabi' ibn al-Ruwayya]], who went to a place called [[place/sabal-sanaa-yemen|Sabal]] and stayed there some days facing the men's camp at a place called [[place/ghayman-sanaa-yemen|Ghayman]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.236}.

## Detail
The report follows [[event/the-sorties-of-17-dhu-al-hijja-and-the-night-fight-at-sanaa-288]]. The weekdays given (Thursday and Tuesday) do not agree with the tabular calendar (two days apart), so the museum gives the month only. The book says only that the reinforcement was of the Tabaris and gives no number. This is a Zaydi book in the voice of the imam's followers and the museum passes no judgement on it.
