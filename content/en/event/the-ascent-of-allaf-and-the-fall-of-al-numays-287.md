## Overview
Then [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] ordered the Hamdanis to head for the enemy from the side of the mountain, and they climbed until they were with them on the summit and closed with them; a man of Hamdan from [[place/khiwan|Khaywan]] was hit and killed, and by the narrator's account the enemy shot heavily into al-Hadi's men and wounded many but killed only the Hamdani. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} When al-Hadi saw how often they were being hit by arrows on rough ground he called out to [[group/tabari-emigrants-with-al-hadi|the Tabaris]] to go on in their array, little by little, to fight their enemy, with good news of God's help and aid; they went forward and he went behind them and put a party of Khawlan and Hamdan archers among the Tabaris, al-Hadi driving them on as they climbed the side of a great, long, towering, rough mountain that a man on foot cannot climb except with effort and fatigue. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195}

## Detail
Al-Hadi and the Tabaris with him kept on until they were level with the enemy on the summit, and al-Hadi charged a man of theirs, thrust him and laid him low, and one of his companions came up and killed him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} When the Tabaris saw al-Hadi had charged and thrust, they and his companions with him charged and drove them to their fort known as [[place/al-numays-allaf-yemen|al-Numays]] in [[place/allaf-yemen|Allaf]], which the Akilis and Rabi'a did not think any man in the world could master or reach for its difficulty and narrow paths. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} When the enemy saw what had befallen them they left Allaf, al-Hadi went down into the valley bottom, and he and his companions entered the village of al-Numays and he ordered it pulled down; it was pulled down and burned, the enemy driven from the land and a number of them killed. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} [[person/abu-turab-muhammad-ibn-al-abbas-al-alawi|Abu Turab Muhammad ibn al-Abbas al-Alawi]] came up with the force he had until he reached al-Hadi at Allaf, and at that point [[person/muhammad-ibn-musbih-al-yarsami|Muhammad ibn Musbih al-Yarsami]] came to al-Hadi, where the account breaks off at the foot of the page. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.195} A gloss says the order was to pull down and burn; the museum gives no judgement on the destruction or the killing, which is the report of the imam's follower.
