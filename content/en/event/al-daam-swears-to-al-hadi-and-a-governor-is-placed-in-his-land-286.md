## Overview
The report then reached al-Hadi that when [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] came to his own land he ordered the call to prayer with "come to the best of work", had wine flogged, made the commanding of good and forbidding of wrong public, and sent to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] asking to meet him in order to swear to him and repent to God of what he had done. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; the description of al-Da'am's repentance and feelings is the narrator's and the museum gives no verdict.

## The meeting and the oath
Al-Hadi agreed, and met him near [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]], where he swore to him, together with his cousins and his son, and went back to his land. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115}

## The governor
Al-Da'am asked al-Hadi to send a man of his own to be governor there; al-Hadi sent a man to his land and sent with him [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Sulayman]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115} When they arrived al-Da'am showed love for al-Hadi, hearing and obedience; the two had the sermon given in al-Hadi's name in al-Da'am's land, called the people to the right and to commanding good and forbidding wrong, took a group of his servants found drunk and applied the penalty, while al-Da'am showed joy and pleasure in it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115} What was due in his land was collected, he heard and obeyed and gave al-Hadi all he asked, and al-Hadi left a governor of his own over the country to command good, forbid wrong and collect the alms; Abu Jafar and his companion were recalled. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115}

The editor's footnotes say the clause "we had the sermon given" stands in the first person plural in both the manuscript and manuscript S and was corrected to the dual, and that the two witnesses differ over the phrase "and the commanding". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.115} See [[event/al-hadi-enters-khiwan-and-preaches-to-its-people-286]].
