## Overview
In Jumada I 743 the post brought a summons calling chief qadi Taqi al-Din al-Subki to Egypt as its judge; he rode out with the courier while the notables saw him off. He returned to his bench in Damascus on Sunday 8 Rajab carrying a decree of the khitaba as well — but the populace rioted in favour of their own khatib Ibn al-Jalal until al-Subki ceded the pulpit to him and kept the judgeship {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya s.a. 743 pp. 215-217}.
