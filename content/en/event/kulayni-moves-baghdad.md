## Summary
At an unknown date, perhaps in the first decade of the 4th century AH (913-923), [[person/al-kulayni|al-Kulayni]] moved to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 401}.

## Darb al-Silsila
He lived and taught in Darb al-Silsila near the Bab al-Kufa on the west bank of the Tigris, and there he completed his great book, al-Kafi {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 401}.
