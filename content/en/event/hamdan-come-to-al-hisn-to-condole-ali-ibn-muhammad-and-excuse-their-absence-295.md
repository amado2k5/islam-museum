## Overview
When Ali ibn Muhammad reached the fort, the men of Hamdan came to him to condole him on his father and apologised for having stayed away from helping him.

## Detail
When Ali ibn Muhammad reached the fort, the men of Hamdan came to him to condole him on his father, and they apologised for the absence that had kept them from helping him. This ends the account of al-Buqayra in this part of the book.
