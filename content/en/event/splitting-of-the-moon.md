## Overview
The sayings in the Sahih of al-Bukhari relate that the people of [[place/mecca|Mecca]] asked [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] for a sign, and he showed them the moon split into two halves, until they saw Hira between them. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3868}

## The reports
In another report they said: "Muhammad has bewitched us." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3868}

[[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Ibn Mas'ud]] relates that the moon split while they were with the Prophet at Mina, and he said: bear witness. A group went off toward the mountain. He once said it split in Mecca. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3869}

[[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] relates that the moon split in the time of the Messenger of God. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3870}

## In the Qur'an
Muslims read this event in the opening of the surah of al-Qamar: [[passage/quran-54-1|al-Qamar 1]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3868}

The hadiths do not give the year of the event, which falls in the Meccan period after the mission began. Fixing the year needs an additional source.
