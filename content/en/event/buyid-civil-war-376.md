## Summary
In 376 AH the Buyid state began to weaken. The army turned from Samsam al-Dawla to his brother Sharaf al-Dawla {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 86}.

## The brother's surrender
Samsam al-Dawla was humbled and went to his brother, accepting whatever he would do. He entered and kissed the ground several times. Sharaf al-Dawla said, "How are you? You have made us lonely," and then had him imprisoned {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 86}.

## The Daylam and the Turks
The Daylam, numbering 19,000, fought the Turks, who numbered 3,000. The Daylam were routed and 3,000 of them were killed. The Turks honoured Sharaf al-Dawla and brought him to Baghdad, where al-Ta'i gave him a welcome. The news of Samsam al-Dawla was kept quiet and his fate was completed {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 86-87}.
