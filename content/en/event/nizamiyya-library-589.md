## Summary
Al-Nasir li-Din Allah ordered the building of a book library at the Nizamiyya madrasa in Baghdad, and it was completed in Rabi al-Awwal from the Ribat al-Harim that he had founded. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 228}
