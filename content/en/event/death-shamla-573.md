## Summary
In this year Shamla al-Turkmani, lord of Khuzistan, died; his dominion had grown wide, his standing great, and he had built several fortresses over more than twenty years. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 71}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the cause of his death was that he marched against a group of Turcomans; forewarned, they sought help from Shams al-Din al-Bahlawan ibn Ildigiz, lord of Persian Iraq, who sent them an army. In the battle Shamla was hit by an arrow, then taken captive together with his son and his brother's son, and he died two days later; he belonged to the Aqshari Turcomans, and his son succeeded him. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 71} Ibn al-Athir notes that Shamla's rule had expanded so that he built several fortresses and remained in power for more than twenty years. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 71}
