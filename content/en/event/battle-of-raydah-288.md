## Overview
[[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|Al-Hadi]] left Shibam with Abu al-Atahiya ibn Bishr, went to Bayt Nud and stayed some days there in Jumada II 288 AH; then [[person/sasaa-ibn-jafar-al-hamdani|Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far]] came to him with people of Qudum and others and they fought him at [[place/raydah-yemen|Raydah]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} He routed them, killed a great many, and razed the palace of Raydah at the end of Jumada II. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20}

## After the battle
Al-Hadi then set out for Sana'a with many troops from Khawlan and Hamdan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.20} See [[event/battle-of-al-rahba-288]].
