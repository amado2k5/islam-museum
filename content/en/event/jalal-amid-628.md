## Summary
Jalal al-Din ibn Khwarazm-shah reached Amid, where he was utterly routed by the Tatars. Ibn al-Athir reports from a merchant that they estimated the Muslim dead there at more than fifteen thousand. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 492}
