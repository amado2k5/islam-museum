## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad asked Ya'am and Wada'a to join him in a raid on Banu al-Harith. They declined, citing feuds among their own clans, so he rode out with about ten horsemen of Shakir and Thaqif to al-Buqayra.

## Detail
Ali ibn Muhammad had sent to Ya'am and Wada'a asking for their help and a joint raid on Banu al-Harith. They did not answer and excused themselves with a feud among their clans. He rode out with horsemen of Shakir and Thaqif, ten in all, and went to al-Buqayra. Earlier he had written to Ibrahim al-Ja'di and to a group of Banu Rabi'a, telling them what al-Hadi had done, how al-Hadi made the sons of Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah swear to support them, and how al-Hadi seized the Ba'amiyin and the allies for killing Ibn Bistam. He told them that al-Hadi had heard of their conduct, and that if they were what was known of them they should act against the enemy at once, and if not, he would deal with them as their conduct showed.
