## Overview
Bursa's silk industry was badly hit in the years of the Turkish war of independence (1337-1341/1919-1922), before a partial recovery under the Turkish Republic's protectionist policy. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1399}
