## Overview
Al-Suyuti relates that in 211 AH [[person/abd-allah-al-mamun|al-Ma'mun]] ordered it proclaimed: "Absolved of responsibility is anyone who speaks well of Mu'awiya." He also ordered it proclaimed that the best of creation after the Messenger of God is Ali ibn Abi Talib. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.488}

This is al-Suyuti's wording in the event data. The position of Sunni scholars and the academic view need an additional source.
