## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] sent to the governor of [[place/azerbaijan|Azerbaijan]], [[person/utba-ibn-farqad|Utba ibn Farqad]], some sweet khabis, and then wrote to him reminding him that he should eat only what the Muslims could be satisfied with. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report
Umar sent Utba a sweet of khabis wrapped in cotton cloth. When it arrived, Utba asked whether it was money, and they said no, so he returned the gift. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then Utba made two baskets of khabis, wrapped them in leather and felt, and sent them to Umar with his client Suhaym. Umar tasted the khabis and asked whether the emigrants had eaten of it until they were satisfied. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The letter
Umar wrote to him that this was not from his labour nor from the labour of his mother or his father, and ordered him to eat only what the Muslims could be satisfied with in their camps. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
