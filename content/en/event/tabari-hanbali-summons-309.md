## Overview
In Dhu al-Qa'da 309/922 al-Tabari was brought to the house of the vizier Ali ibn Isa for a disputation with the Hanbalis; he attended but they did not appear, so he returned home. They had taken offence at several things {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 449-450}.

## The grievance
Among their grievances was his book on the disagreements of the jurists, which did not list Ahmad ibn Hanbal; asked why, he answered that Ibn Hanbal had been a muhaddith, not a faqih, whereupon some Hanbalis turned against him — matters reached the point that a crowd prevented a daytime burial at his death, and he was buried by night in his own house {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 99-100}.
