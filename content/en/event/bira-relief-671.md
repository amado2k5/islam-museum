## Fording the Euphrates and relieving al-Bira

In 671 AH the Tatars reached the bank of the Euphrates and camped before the fortress of al-Bira {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 581}.

The sultan was in Damascus. He hurried his march and ordered the amirs to ford the Euphrates. Sayf al-Din Qalawun and Baysari crossed first with the sultan, and the army followed them {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 581}.

They fell upon the Tatars, killed a great number of them, and took two hundred prisoners, praise be to God {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 581}.
