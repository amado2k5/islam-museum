## Summary
In 11 AH al-Tabari reports that God strengthened the people of Islam in Oman through Ikrima and Hudhayfa. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.316}

## Result
al-Tabari says that they killed ten thousand of their opponents in the battle, and sent the fifth share to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] with Arfaja. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.3 p.316}

The report is short, and the details of the battle need an additional source.
