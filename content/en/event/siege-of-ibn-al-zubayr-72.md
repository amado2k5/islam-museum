## Overview
Al-Mas'udi relates that al-Hajjaj besieged [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Ibn al-Zubayr]] in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] for fifty nights, from the new moon of Dhu al-Qa'da 72 AH. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## The siege
Al-Hajjaj remained at al-Ta'if for months and then advanced on Mecca. When he took Abu Qubays, he wrote to Abd al-Malik, who exulted with his household and those in the mosque of Damascus, and the cry of "Allahu akbar" spread to the markets. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

## The pilgrimage during the siege
Al-Mas'udi says that Ibn al-Zubayr prevented the pilgrims from circling the House. Al-Hajjaj stood with the people at Arafat in the state of ihram, wearing a coat of mail and a helmet, at the age of thirty-one. Ibn al-Zubayr sacrificed at Mecca and did not go out to Arafat. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 104-105}

The date of the siege is not recorded in an organised form in the data, and it needs an additional source.
