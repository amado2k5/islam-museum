## The story
Abu Sa'id al-Khudri told Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Sa'sa'a al-Ansari that he saw him loving sheep and the open country, so when he was among his flock or in the desert and the prayer was announced he should raise his voice: no jinn, no human being and nothing hears the muezzin's call to its extent but it will testify for him on the Day of Resurrection {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-1-176|Muwatta 176]].

## The meaning
In this report jinn, humans and everything else hear the call to prayer to its full extent, and everyone who hears that announcement testifies for the muezzin on the Day of Resurrection; whoever is among his flock or in the open country is encouraged to raise his voice so the call reaches all who hear it, jinn and humans alike {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.

## Notes
