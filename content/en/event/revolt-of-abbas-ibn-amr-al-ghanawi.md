## Overview
In 287 AH al-Tabari mentions the rising of al-Abbas ibn Amr al-Ghanawi from Basra, as it was said, with the troops joined to him and the volunteers of Basra. His aim was Abu Sa'id al-Janabi and those who had joined him of the Qarmatis, and he met Abu Sa'id. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.10 p.77}
