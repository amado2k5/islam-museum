## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that a group of the people of Khurasan agreed to fight [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] and gave their allegiance to [[person/waki-ibn-hassan|Waki ibn Hassan al-Tamimi]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Qutayba's sermon
Qutayba addressed the people, enumerating his kindness to them, and condemned their scant loyalty and their opposition to him, and frightened them with the foreigners on whom he had relied. They agreed to fight him and gave him no answer. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Choosing Waki
They asked [[person/al-hudayn-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Hudayn ibn al-Mundhir]] to take charge of them, but he refused and pointed to Waki. He said, according to the report, that Waki was a bedouin whom his tribe obeyed, of the Banu Tamim, and that Qutayba had killed the Banu al-Ahtham, so they sought him for their blood. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Waki gave them his hand and they gave him allegiance. The envoy between him and them before that had been Hayyan, client of Mushtail. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
