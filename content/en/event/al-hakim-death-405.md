## Summary
Al-Hakim died at Nishapur on Tuesday 3 Safar 405/1014. He entered the bathhouse, washed and came out, said 'ah' — and died while still in his waist-cloth before dressing; he was buried after the 'asr prayer on Wednesday, the qadi Abu Bakr al-Jiri leading the funeral prayer {cite:wafayat-ayan} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 129-130}.

## Dating variants
Tarikh al-Islam gives 8 Safar; al-Khalili's 403 is counted an error. Al-Hasan ibn Ash'ath al-Qurashi related that he saw him in a dream on a horse in fine condition, saying 'deliverance' {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 126-130}.
