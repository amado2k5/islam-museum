## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Uthman appointed [[person/said-ibn-al-as|Sa'id ibn al-As]] over Kufa in 29 AH. The patrician of Tus wrote to him and to [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]], governor of Basra, inviting them to Khurasan on condition that he would make king over it whichever of the two prevailed. Ibn Amir got there first, and Sa'id raided [[place/tabaristan|Tabaristan]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The campaign
It is said that [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|al-Hasan]] and [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn]], sons of Ali, were with him on the campaign. It is also said that he raided it without a letter reaching him. He took Tumaysa and Namana, and made peace with the king of Jurjan for two hundred thousand dirhams. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
