## Overview
Al-Harith ibn al-Harith hid behind the door and struck Abu Ja'far from behind; he fell among Banu al-Harith, who struck him with swords until he died, and then stripped his arms, mount and goods.

## Detail
The narrator says al-Harith ibn al-Harith had hidden behind the door. When Abu Ja'far came out toward them, al-Harith followed him from behind and struck him on the nape of the neck, and he fell among them. They put their swords into him until he died. They then stripped his arms and clothing. Al-Harith ibn al-Harith took his sword. A man of a clan whose name is withheld in this record took his coat of mail and lance. Others took his turban, his seal ring, his shield, his horse and his spear. The book names each of these men, and the names are not recorded here.
