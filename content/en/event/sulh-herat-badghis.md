## Overview
[[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]] sent an army to Herat under [[person/aws-ibn-thalaba|Aws ibn Thalaba]], or it is said Khudayd ibn Abd Allah al-Hanafi. The great man of Herat came to Ibn Amir and made peace with him over [[place/harat|Herat]], Badhghis and Bushanj, without a tyrant or rebels, though these two were taken by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The text of the letter
Al-Baladhuri says that Ibn Amir wrote to the great man of Herat a letter ordering him to fear God, advise the Muslims and improve the land. He made peace with him for a poll tax to be divided over the lands with justice, and whoever withholds what is due has no covenant and no protection. [[person/rabi-ibn-nahshal|Rabi ibn Nahshal]] wrote the letter, and Ibn Amir sealed it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Another account
In a second account Ibn Amir marched to Herat and fought its people, then a marzban made peace with him for a million dirhams over Herat, Bushanj and Badhghis. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
