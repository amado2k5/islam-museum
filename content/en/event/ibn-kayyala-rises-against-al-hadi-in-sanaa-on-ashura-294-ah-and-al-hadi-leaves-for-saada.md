## Overview
On Ashura 294 AH Ibn Kayyala moves against al-Hadi in Sanaa. Al-Hadi does not fight him and leaves the city for Sa'da, and Ibn Kayyala stays in Sanaa.

## Detail
The book has Asad ibn Abi Ya'fur away in the Hamdan country, staying at Warwar, when on Ashura 294 AH Ibn Kayyala moves against al-Hadi in Sanaa. Al-Hadi does not fight him; he leaves the city for Sa'da and Ibn Kayyala remains in Sanaa. The year is 294 AH, as the page reads. Dates are the book's own Hijri dates.
