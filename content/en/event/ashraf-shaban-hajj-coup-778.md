## Overview
In 778 AH Sultan [[person/al-ashraf-shaban|al-Ashraf Sha'ban]] was killed, and his son Ali took the sultanate with the title al-Mansur. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 759}

## The journey and the revolt
al-Ashraf set out for the pilgrimage, with the caliph, the judges and the emirs with him. The emirs turned against him and he fled back to Cairo. They wished to make the caliph [[person/al-mutawakkil-ala-llah|al-Mutawakkil ala Allah]] sultan, but he refused, so they made the son of al-Ashraf sultan. al-Ashraf hid until they found him, and they strangled him in Dhu al-Qa'da. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 759}
