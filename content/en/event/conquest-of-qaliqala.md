## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that when Uthman became caliph he wrote to [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya]] ordering him to send [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama al-Fihri]] to Armenia. It is also said that Uthman wrote to Habib directly, and that is the more firmly established. Habib set out with six or eight thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.277}

## The conquest
Habib came to [[place/qaliqala|Qaliqala]] and fought its people until he drove them back into the city. They asked for safe-conduct on condition of leaving and paying the jizya, and many of them left for the land of the Byzantines. Habib remained there for several months. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.277}

## Reinforcement and battle
When he learned that the patrician of Arminaqis had gathered a great host against the Muslims, he wrote to [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]]. Mu'awiya sent him two thousand men, whom he settled at Qaliqala as a garrison. Then the Muslims made a night attack on the Byzantines at al-Fadhhat and overwhelmed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.278}

al-Baladhuri, quoting al-Waqidi, gives another report about the siege of Dabil, and says that the most firmly held view is that the encounter was at Qaliqala. The date is approximate and derived.
