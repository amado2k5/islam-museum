## Summary
A raid the Prophet sent in Rabi' al-Akhir of year 9 AH under [[person/alqama-ibn-mujazziz|Alqama ibn Mujazziz al-Mudliji]] with three hundred fighters, against men of Abyssinia gathered near Jeddah's coasts to carry out piracy against the people of Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## The raid
Alqama crossed the sea until he reached an island where the pirates were entrenched; when they heard of the Muslims' march on them they fled, and the mission ended without fighting {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 426}.

## For young visitors
A large detachment of three hundred men went out to protect the Red Sea coast from pirates who were attacking Mecca's caravans — and the pirates fled when they heard they were coming.
