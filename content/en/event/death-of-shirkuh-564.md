## Summary
Asad al-Din Shirkuh died a few days after he had taken up the vizierate. By order of al-ʿAdid, the robe of investiture was then conferred on his nephew Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub. He was the youngest of the Nuri amirs in age, and they had put him forward in their letters because they knew his humility, good conduct and kindness to his companions {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.15}.
