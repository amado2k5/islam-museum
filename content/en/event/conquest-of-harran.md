## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/iyyad-ibn-ghanm|Iyad ibn Ghanm]] marched to [[place/harran|Harran]], halted at Ajda, and its people closed their gates. It was then taken on terms like those of Edessa. It is part of the [[event/conquest-of-jazira|conquest of al-Jazira]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Harran and Edessa
Some of its Harranians sent to him asking that he go to Edessa so that they could make peace with him in secret, and the Christians agreed to that. He came to Edessa, where its people had mustered for him, and the Muslims routed them, and they sought peace and safe-conduct. It is related that Iyad found Harran empty, its people having moved to Edessa, so they made peace on terms like those of Edessa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Samosata and after
Iyad marched to Samosata and found that [[person/safwan-ibn-al-muattal|Safwan ibn al-Mu'attal]] and [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] had taken control of villages and forts in it, and its people made peace with him. Then they broke faith, and he besieged it until he took it. He came to Suruj, Ras Kifa and the White Land, and made peace with the people of their forts on terms like those of Edessa. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
