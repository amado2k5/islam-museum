## Summary
[[person/umm-kulthum-bint-muhammad|Umm Kulthum]], the Prophet's daughter and wife of [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]], died in 9 AH {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## Dhu al-Nurayn
The Prophet grieved for her deeply and said to Uthman: "had I a third, I would have given her to you in marriage." Uthman had married her sister Ruqayya before her — which is why he was called Dhu al-Nurayn, "he of the two lights" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.
