## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates the governorship of Imran ibn Musa over the frontier of Sind in the caliphate of [[person/al-mutasim-abi-ishaq|al-Mu'tasim]], and his fighting against the Zutt and the Mayd. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Governorship and campaigns
Al-Mu'tasim wrote to Imran ibn Musa appointing him over the frontier. He went out to al-Qaqan, who are Zutt, fought them, and built a city he called al-Bayda, and settled troops in it. Then he came to al-Mansura and went on to Qandabil, where he fought its usurper Muhammad ibn al-Khalil and took it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The Zutt and the Mayd
Imran raided the Mayd and killed three thousand of them. He took the poll tax from the Zutt, and ordered each man of them to take a dog with him at a review. Then he dug a canal from the sea that he ran into the marsh of the Mayd until its water became salt. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The death
Partisanship arose between the Nizari and the Yamani, and Imran inclined toward the Yamani. Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz al-Hubari went against him and killed him by surprise. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
