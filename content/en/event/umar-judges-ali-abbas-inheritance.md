## Summary
Al-Abbas and Ali brought a dispute before Umar ibn al-Khattab regarding what God had bestowed upon His Messenger from the properties of Banu al-Nadir; Umar asked the group present about the Prophet's saying, "We are not inherited from; what we leave is charity," and they affirmed it, then Umar explained the administration of the charity from the time of the Prophet to the time of Abu Bakr and Ali, and swore that Ali was truthful in it {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 152}.

## Context and the Two Disputants
Al-Sallabi states that the dispute occurred between al-Abbas and Ali, that the group present with Umar consisted of Uthman, Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, al-Zubayr, and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, and that Umar reminded them of the text before passing judgment {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 152}. Al-Abbas had asked for his share of his nephew's property, and Ali had asked for his wife Fatima's share from her father, as Umar recounted in his address to them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 153}.

## Umar's Argument
Umar cited the text affirmed by the group, then noted that the Prophet used to spend on his family their sustenance for the year from this property and place the remainder in God's treasury, that Abu Bakr administered it after the Prophet, and that Ali then administered it for two years {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 152}. Umar told them that the condition for handing it over to them was that they administer it as the Prophet, Abu Bakr, and Umar had administered it, and they accepted; he then said: "And if you are unable to manage it, hand it back to me, for I will suffice you in it" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 153}.

## Al-Sallabi's View
Al-Sallabi cites this report to show that Ali was truthful in administering the charity, that Umar confirmed him in this, and that the disagreement was over the management of the property rather than Ali's integrity {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 153}. In establishing the hadith, the book relies on the Companions who testified to it before Umar {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 152}.
