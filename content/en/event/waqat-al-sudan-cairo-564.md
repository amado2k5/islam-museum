## Overview
In early Dhu al-Qa'da 564 AH the Sudanese troops in [[place/cairo|Cairo]] fought the army of [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din]] between the two palaces, after the killing of [[person/mutamin-al-khilafa|Mu'tamin al-Khilafa]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.18-19}

## The cause
Mu'tamin al-Khilafa was the eunuch of the palace of the Fatimid caliph al-Adid and the one who controlled it. He was killed after his correspondence with the Franks inviting them to Egypt was exposed. Salah al-Din dismissed the palace servants and appointed Baha' al-Din Qaraqush over them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.18-19}

## The fighting
The Sudanese were angered by his killing, because he had favoured them. The report says that their number exceeded fifty thousand; they sought war against the Salahi troops, and fought between the two palaces, and killing grew among both sides. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.18-19}

## The end
Salah al-Din sent men who burned their quarter known as al-Mansura. They were routed, asked for safety and were expelled from Egypt, except those who took refuge in Giza. [[person/shams-al-dawla-turanshah|Shams al-Dawla]], the brother of Salah al-Din, crossed to them with a body of the army, and destroyed most of them with the sword. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.18-19}

The Gregorian date (around August 1169 CE) is derived from the Hijri date.
