## Summary
Ibn al-Imad, in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*, reports that the plague occurred in 841 AH in the middle of winter in the land of al-Sham. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}

## Course of the epidemic
The plague became widespread in Hama, Aleppo and Homs, then moved to Damascus at the end of winter, and then reached the lands of Egypt. {cite:shadharat-dhahab}
