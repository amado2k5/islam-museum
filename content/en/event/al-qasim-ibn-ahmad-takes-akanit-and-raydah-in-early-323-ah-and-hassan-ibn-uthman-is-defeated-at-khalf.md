## Overview
Al-Qasim ibn Ahmad takes Akanit and Raydah in early 323 AH, and Hassan ibn Uthman's men are defeated at Khalf.

## Detail
Al-Qasim ibn Ahmad goes to the land of Hamdan, and people answer him and go with him to Akanit. Muzaffar ibn Alyan ibn al-Da'am, who was then living at Raydah and supporting Hassan ibn Uthman, sends men to fight al-Qasim at Akanit. Muzaffar's men are routed back to Raydah, and when they arrive with him he leaves without a fight and abandons the town, going to Gharq in Muharram 323 AH. Al-Qasim goes to Raydah, stays there, and the townspeople answer him. Hassan ibn Uthman then marches out with the Bani al-Harith and their allies toward Sa'da by the road through Shakir. At a place called Khalf, people of Wa'ila call out to Sa'da, a group of Khawlan and others come out, they fight hard, and Hassan and his men are routed with many killed; he returns to Najran. The Harithis then write to al-Hasan ibn Ahmad, give him their allegiance, make peace with Hamdan, and appoint a governor over themselves.
