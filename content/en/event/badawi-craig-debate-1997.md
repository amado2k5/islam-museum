## Summary
The 1997 debate between Jamal Badawi and William Lane Craig on 'The Concept of God in Islam and Christianity'. {cite:reasonablefaith-concept-of-god-debate}

## The line of the debate
Tawhid against Trinity: Badawi reduces the concept of persons to a violation of rational and scriptural unicity, Craig argues the Trinity's philosophical coherence. Recording and transcript are published and circulating.
