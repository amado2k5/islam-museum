## Overview
Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/muawiya-ibn-khudayj|Mu'awiya ibn Hudayj al-Kindi]] raided [[place/siqilliya|Sicily]] in the days of [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]], that he was the first to raid it, and that it continued to be raided after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}

## The capture by Abd Allah ibn Qays
Al-Baladhuri, from al-Waqidi, reports that [[person/abdallah-ibn-qays-al-dazzaqi|Abd Allah ibn Qays al-Dazzaqi]] took captives in Sicily, and found idols of gold and silver set with jewels. He sent them to Mu'awiya, who sent them on to Basra to be carried to India and sold there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.329}

## The date
The source gives no particular year, saying only "in the days of Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan". The period is therefore set approximately between 660 and 680 CE, and it is derived from Mu'awiya's reign, not from a dated statement in the text.
