## Overview
In 571/1175, on his first Indian expedition, Mu'izz al-Din Muhammad ibn Sam of Ghur drove the Ismaili rulers out of Multan, set an orthodox governor over the province and captured Uch {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 461}.

## "Extracted from the hands of the Karmatis"
Juzjani describes the conquest as wresting Multan from the hands of the Karmatis; the Encyclopaedia notes that the Ismailis had recovered from Mahmud of Ghazna's defeat and held the city for nearly a hundred and seventy years before the Ghurid came {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## What followed
With this conquest Multan entered the Ghurid dominion, and after Mu'izz al-Din's assassination in 603/1206 it fell to his mamluk Nasir al-Din Qabacha, who ruled a short-lived state from it {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 461}.
