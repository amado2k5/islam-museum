## Overview
After the reports in [[event/reports-of-a-sight-restored-and-a-tongue-loosed-at-najran-286]] the narrator supposes someone saying: "If you describe this of Yahya ibn al-Husayn and claim his prayer is answered, we have seen many enemies who fight him and spoil his governors' work, so why did he not call down on them until God relieved him of them?" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.131} What follows is the view of the Zaydi narrators; the museum gives it as a description of their argument, not a judgment on it. See [[concept/imama|the imamate]], where the Sunni position stands first.

## The answer
The narrator says the answer "has been given before": "God gives them and tries them, to multiply their reward by it and turn the pain of punishment from them." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.132} He says the Prophet "was tried by his people more than Yahya ibn al-Husayn was tried", that his uncles and all Quraysh fought him, that he prayed and the moon split for him and he called the trees and they joined together, and that al-Husayn ibn Ali was killed and kept from water, as was Zayd ibn Ali, and that the prophets and messengers before them fought and suffered trials and, had they asked God to turn them aside, He would have. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.132}

## Reading "We made"
The narrator cites the verse [[passage/quran-25-31|al-Furqan 25:31]] and says that "making" here is "naming the enemy": it is not creating them but naming them; God named the enemies of the prophets among the criminals and judged them by their deeds; he cites [[passage/quran-5-103|al-Ma'ida 5:103]] to show that "God did not make a bahira" means He did not name it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.132} He closes with [[passage/quran-50-37|Qaf 50:37]] and says the argument would be long and is clear to "whoever has a heart or gives ear". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.132} This is the narrator's reading as the book gives it; the commentators hold other readings and the museum does not weigh them here.

## A promise of a separate book
He adds that if everything the deniers deny of the signs of Yahya ibn al-Husayn's imamate were written, it would fill a separate book, "and perhaps we shall take it up, compile and write it, God willing". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.132} The book does not say it was written.

## Notes
In this passage the Zaydi narrative calls the imam's opponents "criminals"; the museum gives that as the narrator's wording and does not use it in its own voice. The editor's footnote on p. 131 records "yustajab" in the other manuscript where the text has "mustajab".
