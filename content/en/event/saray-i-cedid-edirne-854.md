## Overview
Murad II built the New Imperial Palace (Saray-ı Cedid-i Amire) in 854/1450 on an island in the Tunca; Mehmed II continued its construction the following year {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## The island palace
Enlarged by successive sultans, the palace held 18 pavilions, 8 mosques, 17 large gates, 14 baths and 5 courts by the late seventeenth century, housing some six to ten thousand people {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.
