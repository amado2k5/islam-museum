## Summary
A man said he would not care to do any deed after embracing Islam other than maintaining the Sacred Mosque, whereupon Ali replied that jihad was better than all of that, and the verse favoring faith and jihad was revealed {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}.

## The Report
Al-Sallabi cites this incident in the section on Ali's method of knowing the occasions of revelation, noting that the man said, "I do not care to do any deed after Islam except to maintain the Sacred Mosque," and Ali ibn Abi Talib said, "Jihad in the way of God is better than all of this" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}.

## Umar's Objection and the Revelation of the Verse
Umar ibn al-Khattab then said, "Do not raise your voices near the pulpit of the Messenger of God," and told him to ask the Prophet after the prayer. He asked him, and the verse in Surat al-Tawba was revealed favoring faith, migration, and jihad over providing water for pilgrims and maintaining the Sacred Mosque {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 47}. Al-Sallabi considered the report evidence that Ali knew the occasions of revelation, which is the means by which rulings are derived.
