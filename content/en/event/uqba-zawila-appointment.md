## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates from Mu'awiya ibn Salih that [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] wrote to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] that he had appointed [[person/uqba-ibn-nafi|Uqba ibn Nafi al-Fihri]] over the Maghrib, and that he reached [[place/zawila|Zawila]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.315}

## What the letter says
Amr mentioned that everything between Zawila and [[place/barqa|Barqa]] had submitted; their Muslims paid the alms, and their covenanters acknowledged the poll tax. He imposed on them what he thought they could bear. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.315}

He ordered his agents to take the alms from the rich and return it to the poor, to take the poll tax from the protected people and carry it to him in Egypt, and to take a tenth and a half-tenth from the lands of the Muslims, and from the people of the peace what they had agreed to. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.315}

## The date
The event is dated around 22 AH, after the peace of Barqa and before Tripoli, and the conversion is derived. A second source is needed to fix the year.
