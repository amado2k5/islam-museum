## Summary
Abu Mansur Ibrahim ibn al-Hasan ibn Hamdan al-Sayrafi, a widely-heard scholar who wished to compile a mu'allal musnad, held a weekly day on which al-Daraqutni attended him; al-Daraqutni would give the defects of the hadiths against his own notebooks while Abu Bakr al-Barqani transferred them and al-Daraqutni dictated the 'ilal to him, until a great deal had come out of it {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.183}.

## How the Kitab al-'Ilal came out
Abu Mansur died before it was finished, so al-Barqani transmitted al-Daraqutni's words — that is the Kitab al-'Ilal which people narrate from al-Daraqutni {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.183}. Al-Barqani said: al-Daraqutni dictated the 'Ilal to me from memory; whoever would know what that means, let him read the Kitab al-'Ilal to learn what the huffaz were {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p.103}.
