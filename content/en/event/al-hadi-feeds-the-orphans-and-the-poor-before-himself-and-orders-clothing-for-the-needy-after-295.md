## Overview
Al-Hadi fed the orphans and the poor before eating himself, ordered the treasurer to feed wayfarers and the disabled, and ordered clothing for people in every season.

## Detail
He broke the food for the orphans and moistened it with butter, then said, bring them in. He looked at which of them was too weak to eat, and said that such a one was cheated of his share, so that he ate with the poor and a portion was set aside for him. He would not eat any food until he had fed the poor from it. He ordered the treasurer to feed the wandering poor evening and morning, and the disabled according to their need and what the treasury held. He ordered clothes to be made in every season, shirts for women, men and boys, and in winter he ordered someone to buy wool, saying that every season has its clothing and every age its dress.
