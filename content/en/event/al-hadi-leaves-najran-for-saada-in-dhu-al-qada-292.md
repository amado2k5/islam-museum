## Overview
Al-Hadi stayed at Najran, people calmed and mixed together and the land settled, and all of Yam and Banu al-Harith sought his safety, until seven days of Dhu al-Qa'da had passed {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.295}. Under the heading "Al-Hadi's going to Sa'da": "In Dhu al-Qa'da of the year two hundred and ninety-one al-Hadi left Najran until he came to Sa'da and stayed there" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.295}.

## Detail
The text says "ninety-one" here, and both the OCR and a reading of the page image give "one and nin(e)ty" with the dot of the ayn lost, so the print is 291 AH; but the narrative before it, ordered after the oath of Jumada I 292, gives Sunday four days into Rajab and Monday the seventeenth of Sha'ban, both of which agree with the tabular calendar in 292 AH and not in 291. The event is therefore dated to Dhu al-Qa'da 292 with a derived date, and the record notes that the printed text says 291; the museum neither corrects the text nor accepts the conflict, and the cause may be a slip of a copyist or editor, or the year meant may be that in which the entry to Najran began. The question is logged in the ledger for an outside source. The text gives no reason for leaving beyond what follows of the Hakami letters, which come in [[event/the-hakami-letters-and-the-oaths-and-abu-al-qasim-sent-to-khaywan-292|the next event]]. This is the account of the imam's supporters in a Zaydi book.
