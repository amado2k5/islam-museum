## Summary
In 334/945-6 al-Hakim became the mustamli of Abu Hatim Ibn Hibban at Nishapur — their first meeting — writing down what the master dictated in his circle {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 122} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 113}.

## The master and his scribe
Ibn Hibban had settled at Nishapur as qadi and polymath; the young al-Hakim served him as dictation-copyist, the start of his link with the greatest hadith scholars of the craft {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 113}.
