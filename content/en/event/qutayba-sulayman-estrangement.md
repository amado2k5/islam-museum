## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba]] was estranged from [[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik]], because he had worked for the allegiance to Abd al-Aziz ibn al-Walid, to keep it away from Sulayman. When Sulayman succeeded al-Walid, Qutayba called the people to depose him, and none answered him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The sermon
The source says that Qutayba addressed the people and cited [[person/yazid-ibn-tazwan|Hubnaqa al-Aishi]] as an example, and called on them to depose Sulayman, but no one answered him. He then spoke harshly about the Banu Tamim, the Banu Bakr ibn Wa'il and the Azd, in words al-Baladhuri records. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Sulayman's letter
Sulayman wrote to Qutayba confirming his governorship, ordering him to release those held in prison, to pay people their stipends, and to allow anyone who wished to return. He ordered his messenger to inform the people of what he had written. Qutayba said this was the work of Ali; he addressed the people, who were angry with him for the insults he had hurled at them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
