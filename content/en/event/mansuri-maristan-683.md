## Summary
In 683/1284 the Mansuri hospital endowed by sultan al-Malik al-Mansur Qalawun was completed in Cairo {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 897}.

## Ibn al-Nafis's endowment
Ibn al-Nafis, to whom the headship of medicine in Egypt had come, died four years later leaving his house, fortune and books as an endowment to this maristan {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi s.a. 687 p. 312} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 402}.
