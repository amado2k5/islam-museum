## Overview
Abu al-Qasim wrote to the two sons of Ya'far complaining of his long stay and his boredom at the place. They sent him mounts, robes and a sword, and two letters apologising for his stay. He accepted their excuse, shared what reached him among his servants and others present, and wrote to Ali ibn al-Hasan al-Aqra' to go with him. They then set out and reached Sa'da in days of the year 291 AH, when al-Hadi ila al-Haqq was living in Sa'da.

## Detail
The narrator says that Abu al-Qasim wrote to the two sons of Ya'far complaining of his long stay and his boredom at the place. They sent him mounts, robes and a sword, and sent letters apologising for his stay. He accepted their excuse, shared what they had sent among his servants and others present, and they wrote to Ali ibn al-Hasan al-Aqra' to go with him as far as he wished. The party left Shibam and reached al-Ghayl, where they met the Da'am's agents who were at al-Bawn. They sent al-Aqra' back and went on with those agents until they reached Ra'ida, spent the night there, and then went on until they spent the night at Warwar. Abu al-Qasim asked how the narrator had found the dream he had told him at a place the scan reads as Bayt Bus. They went on until they reached Gharq in the Da'am's country, halted there, and then went on to Sa'da, with Ibn al-Da'am travelling with them, reaching Sa'da in days of the year 291 AH, when al-Hadi ila al-Haqq was living in Sa'da.
