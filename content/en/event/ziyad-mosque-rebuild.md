## Overview
When Mu'awiya appointed [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]] over Basra, he greatly enlarged the mosque, built it of baked brick and lime, and roofed it with teak. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What he did
Ziyad said that an imam should not step over people, so he moved the governor's house from al-Dahna to the qibla side of the mosque; the imam used to go out through a door in the qibla wall. He made the front of the pulpit five pillars, and built its minaret of stone. Al-Baladhuri says that he was the first to make the screened enclosure (maqsura). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The building and the verses
Ziyad went round the building and asked the notables of Basra about its defects, and they said they knew no building more sound than it. He said of the pillars, which had four arches, that they were thicker than the others. Al-Baladhuri quotes verses describing the building attributed to Haritha ibn Badr, and said to be of al-Bu'ayth al-Mujashi'i. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The building in question is [[building/great-mosque-of-basra|the Great Mosque of Basra]].
