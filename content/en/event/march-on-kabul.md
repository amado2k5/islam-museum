## Overview
When Mu'awiya appointed Ibn Amir over Basra, Ibn Amir appointed [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] over Sistan. He raided the area around it until he reached [[place/kabul|Kabul]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The men
He made [[person/abbas-ibn-al-husayn-al-hibati|Abbad ibn al-Husayn]] head of his police. With him among the notables were [[person/abdallah-ibn-khazim-al-sulami|Abd Allah ibn Khazim]], [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] and others. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The siege of Kabul
He besieged its people for months and bombarded them with catapults until a great breach opened. Abbad spent a night there fighting the polytheists. Al-Baladhuri relates from al-Hasan al-Basri that he did not think a man could stand in place of a thousand until he saw Abbad. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

In the morning the people of Kabul came out to fight. Ibn Khazim struck one of their elephants, which fell against the gate, so they could not close it, and the Muslims entered by force. Abu Mikhnaf attributes the hamstringing of the elephant to al-Muhallab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the conquest
Ibn Samura sent news of the conquest with Umar ibn Ubayd Allah ibn Ma'mar and al-Muhallab. He then took other places, some by force and some by agreement, and returned to Kabul after its people broke their covenant and he took it again. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
