## Overview
Once affairs in Najran were settled, Abdallah ibn al-Khattab al-Hakami asked al-Hadi to march on Tihama and for reinforcements. The imam sent horsemen and foot soldiers, and wrote to Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah to send him the force he had.

## Detail
The book says Abdallah ibn al-Khattab al-Hakami asked al-Hadi to march toward Tihama and for reinforcements. The imam sent many horsemen and foot soldiers, and he wrote to Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah al-Alawi, who sent Ibn al-Khattab the force he had with him.
