## Overview
[[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] took Talaqan by treaty, then marched to [[place/balkh|Balkh]], where its people made peace with him. He then turned toward Khwarazm but could not take it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Talaqan and Fariyab
al-Ahnaf took Talaqan by treaty and also took Fariyab. The account gives another view, that Fariyab was taken by Amir ibn Ahmar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The treaty of Balkh
al-Ahnaf marched to Balkh, which is the city of Tukhara. Its people made peace with him for four hundred thousand. The source gives another figure, seven hundred thousand, and describes it as the more reliable. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He appointed [[person/asid-ibn-al-mutashammis|Asid ibn al-Mutashammis]] over Balkh; he is said to have been al-Ahnaf's nephew. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Khwarazm
al-Ahnaf marched to Khwarazm, could not take it, and returned to Balkh. Asid had already collected its treaty payment. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
