## Summary
In 605 AH the Georgians marched in force into the wilaya of Akhlat, besieged the city of Azjish, took it by storm, plundered and burned it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 345}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the Georgians came in their multitudes into the wilaya of Akhlat and made for Azjish; they besieged it, took it by force, plundered all the wealth and goods in it, took its people captive and as slaves, burned and ruined it utterly so that none of its people remained and it lay desolate on its foundations. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 345} Najm al-Din Ayyub, lord of Armenia, was in the city of Akhlat with many troops but did not go out against the Georgians — among the reasons were their numbers and his fear of the people of Akhlat for the killing and harm he had previously done them, fearing that if he left he could not return; so the Georgians went home unmolested. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 345} Ibn al-Athir adds that grave and grievous as this was for Islam and its people, it was little compared to what the Georgians did from 614 to 617, as he later narrates. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 345}
