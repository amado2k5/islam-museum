## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad relates from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]], from Muhammad ibn al-Hajjaj "al-Yarsami" (the affiliation uncertain) and groups of Yemenis, that they suffered drought and their land went dry in 280 AH; when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] reached them the rain came and followed in succession until they wished there were less. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.65} The narrator heard men of Banu Uqayl say "Abu al-Husayn passed through no place in our land but rain fell", and groups of Yemenis from scattered districts say "the rain came to us by Abu al-Husayn's blessing". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.65}

## The people of Bayt Dhu'd
He heard men from a place called "Bayt Dhu'd" say to al-Hadi: "Son of the Messenger of God, you came upon us on your first journey and we pledged to you and you asked God to spare us strife; we have seen no strife since you; strife was all around us and we saw only good through the blessing of your prayer for us." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.65} The name resembles the place "Bayt Dhukhar" of an earlier batch, but the readings differ and they are not identified as one.

## Note
These are the testimonies of Zaydi narrators to their imam's blessing; the museum neither affirms nor denies them, and where the text ties the rain to his arrival that is the narrators' way of putting it. See [[event/al-hadi-first-journey-to-yemen-280]].
