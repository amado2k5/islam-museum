## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad says Abu Jafar ibn Sulayman told him that when [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] reached [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] from the land of "al-Qusayb", as the text prints it and the editor does not identify, he ordered the people to prepare to go out to [[place/najran|Najran]], in the past days of Dhu al-Qa'da 286 AH, and then went out to [[place/saada|Sa'da]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155}

## Who stayed at Khiwan
He left his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Abu al-Qasim]] at Khiwan with a force of Abu al-Mutahiya's, horse and men. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.155} The museum links Abu al-Qasim here to [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Muhammad al-Murtada]] as a probable reading only. The account follows [[event/al-hadi-at-hawth-and-al-ghubayb-and-the-return-to-khiwan-on-1-dhu-al-qada-286]] and is followed by [[event/ahmad-ibn-abbad-al-akili-leaves-saada-and-writes-to-al-hadi-286]].
