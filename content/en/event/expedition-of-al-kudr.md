## Summary
Only seven days after the Prophet's return from Badr, [[place/medina|Medina]]'s intelligence reported that the tribes of [[group/banu-sulaym|Banu Sulaym]] and [[group/ghatafan|Ghatafan]] were mustering forces to raid; the Prophet went out with two hundred riders in Shawwal 2 AH to surprise them. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 234}

## The march and the banner
The Prophet left [[person/ibn-umm-maktum|Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum]] in charge of Medina; the expedition's banner was with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]. The army pressed on swiftly until it reached a water called [[place/al-kudr|Qarqarat al-Kudr]]. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 234} {cite:zad-al-maad 2/90}

## The spoils and the return
When the polytheists sensed the Muslims' approach they scattered and fled into the mountaintops and valley gorges; the Muslims drove off five hundred camels of their herds. The Prophet set aside the fifth and divided the rest among the fighters — two camels to each man — and returned to Medina after an absence of three days without fighting. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 235} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 2/44}
