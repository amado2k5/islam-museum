## Summary
The shaykh Ibn Abi al-Fath died on the morning of Sunday 4 Dhu al-Hijja 739 at the age of seventy-four and a half, and 'after him our shaykh al-Mizzi took the shaykhhood of the Nuriyya, Ibn Raf'a that of the Qawsiyya, and al-Abd that of the Nafisiyya' {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 459}.
