## Summary
Ibn al-Tiqtaqa's father, Taj al-Din Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ramadan, chief naqib of the Alids, had gained great wealth and influence, but in a game of political intrigue against the brothers Ala' al-Din and Shams al-Din al-Juwayni he lost his life and property; Ibn Inaba's 'Umdat al-Talib tells the story {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.
