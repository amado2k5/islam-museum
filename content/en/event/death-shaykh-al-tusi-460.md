## Summary
Abu Ja'far al-Tusi died at the blessed Mashhad of Najaf in Muharram 460/1067 and was buried there; the Shaykh al-Tusi mosque holds his tomb {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 490} {cite:musawat-atabat}.
