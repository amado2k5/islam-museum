## Overview
Before the fight at Sawhan [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] went out to [[place/minas-najran|Minas]] to cut some of [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]]'s palms, cut through the day, and at the noon prayer ordered his men to turn back. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.175} He was riding a weak horse and not the one he fought on, a chestnut called Abu al-Hamahim, because [[group/banu-al-harith-ibn-kab|Banu al-Harith]] knew his horse: if he rode it they knew he would fight them, and if he rode another they knew he was not fighting that day. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.175}

## The fall
When the people of Minas saw al-Hadi on a horse other than his fighting horse they came out of their fort and got ahead of his column to the gate to cut it off from the fort; near the lane were pits he did not know of, he drove his horse at the gate of the lane and he and the horse fell into one. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.175} At that moment he reached for his sword and drew it, took his shield and sprang up before them shouting at them, and none came near him after his shout; they had hoped for him before and fought him without finding a weakness, and many were wounded that day and a number killed when he fell. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.175} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176}

## The horse's face
The narrator says the face of al-Hadi's horse had fallen into the pit and that he put out his left hand and took it from under the grip of the shield, and that God kept him from the harm of that day, which is the narrator's wording and not the museum's judgement. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176} A companion asked why he took the horse's face and had not left it, and he answered that nothing stopped him leaving it but the thought that they would take it and say they had taken the face of his horse, so he took it in spite of them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176} The printed marginal gloss on the words "face of the horse" is too disturbed to read, so the museum leaves the meaning open. Another horse was brought and he rode it as night drew near. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.176} The next episode is [[event/al-hadi-has-the-people-of-the-fort-make-room-for-his-men-before-adha-286]].
