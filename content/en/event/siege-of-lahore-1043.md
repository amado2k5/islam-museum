## Overview
In 435/1043-4 Lahore underwent a long siege by a confederacy of Hindu princes, but it failed; the city remained firmly in Ghaznavid hands, serving as the administrative centre of their Indian provinces and effectively the second capital of the empire {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 37}.
