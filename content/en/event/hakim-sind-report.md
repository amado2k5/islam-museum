## Overview
When [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] became caliph, he wrote to [[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Abd Allah ibn Amir]], the governor of Iraq, asking him to send someone who knew the frontier of [[place/sind|India]] and would bring him news of it. He sent [[person/hakim-ibn-jabala|Hakim ibn Jabala al-Abdi]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The report
Uthman asked Hakim about the state of the land. He said, according to the source: its water is thin, its fruit is poor, and its thieves are bold; if the army there is small they will be lost, and if it is large they will go hungry. Uthman asked him: "Are you a counsellor or a mere rhymer?" So no one raided it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
