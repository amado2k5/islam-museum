## Overview
After al-Hadi did not grant Ibn Bistam's request to free Ali ibn al-Rabi', Ibn Bistam asked for the Qarmatian prisoners whom al-Hadi had taken. Al-Hadi released them and sent Ibn Bistam's envoy back with them.

## Detail
The book says that when al-Hadi did not grant Ibn Bistam's request to free Ali ibn al-Rabi', Ibn Bistam turned to the Qarmatian prisoners whom al-Hadi had taken. Al-Hadi released them and sent Ibn Bistam's envoy back with them. The book gives no day for this episode; it falls in the year 294 AH, before the Dhu al-Hijja events that follow.
