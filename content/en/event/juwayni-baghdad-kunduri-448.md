## Summary
When affairs fell into disorder in the ordeal, al-Juwayni was forced to leave Nishapur for the army camp and then Baghdad, where he kept company with the vizier Abu Nasr al-Kunduri, travelling with him and meeting and disputing the great scholars in his presence until he grew seasoned, polished and famous {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 230} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala j.18 pp. 2574-2575}.
