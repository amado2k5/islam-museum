## Overview
During the new Mongol invasion of 702/1303 Ibn Taymiyya was present at the victory of Shaqhab on 4 Ramadan 702/22 April 1303, having been commissioned to issue a fatwa permitting the fighters to break the fast. Before that, in 700, he had ridden the barid post to Cairo in Jumada I, rousing the people to jihad and asking Sultan al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun to intervene in Syria {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 983} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 429}.
