## Summary
In the days of the caliph al-Musta'li bi-llah, his brother Nizar, to whom the Nizari movement is traced through the fortress of Alamut, fled and entered Alexandria {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 402}.

## The revolt and its suppression
The city's people pledged allegiance to him, and its judge Ibn Ammar and its governor Aftakin supported him. Al-Afdal ibn Badr al-Jamali attacked them, and Aftakin came out to fight him and defeated him. Al-Afdal then attacked a second time, won, and returned to Cairo with Aftakin and Nizar. Aftakin was put to death, and Nizar was walled up alive and died {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 402}.
