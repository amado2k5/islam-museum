## Overview
Al-Baladhuri lists the governors of Armenia in the Umayyad period, and mentions the work of [[person/al-jarrah-ibn-abdallah-al-hakami|al-Jarrah ibn Abd Allah]] at Bardha'a and his raid against the Khazars. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.289}

## Governors before him
[[person/sulayman-ibn-abd-al-malik|Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik]] appointed Adi ibn Adi al-Kindi, and then [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] appointed him. Al-Baladhuri gives a report that Umar's governor was Hatim ibn al-Nu'man, and says it is not established. Then Yazid ibn Abd al-Malik appointed others, among them al-Harith ibn Amr al-Ta'i. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.289}

## Al-Jarrah's governorship
Al-Jarrah came to Bardha'a, and the difference in measures and weights was brought to him; he fixed them and made a measure named the Jarrahi. Al-Baladhuri says that its people used it in their dealings until his time. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.289}

## The raid on the Khazars
He crossed the Kur, marched against the Khazars and killed a great number of them. He fought the people of the land of Hamzin, then made peace with them and moved them to a district. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.289}
