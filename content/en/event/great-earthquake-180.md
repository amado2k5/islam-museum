## Overview
Al-Suyuti, in "History of the Caliphs", says that the great earthquake was in 180 AH, and that the top of a minaret of [[place/alexandria|Alexandria]] fell in it. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.461}

The account is brief, and needs an additional source.
