## Summary
Shihab al-Din entered Damascus in Muharram 741 (June-July 1340) and took charge of its chancery in place of Shihab Yahya ibn al-Qaysarani, in the reshuffle that followed the arrest of Tankiz, viceroy of Syria, in Dhu al-Hijja 740 {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/333} {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/520}.

## Details
He was given the Damascus chancery after the fall of Tankiz — the same Tankiz whose request to seat Alam al-Din Ibn al-Qutb as katib al-sirr had first provoked al-Umari's fateful quarrel {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/332}.
