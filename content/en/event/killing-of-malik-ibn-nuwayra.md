## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] marched after Buzakha against the Banu Tamim, and [[person/malik-ibn-nuwayra|Malik ibn Nuwayra]], brother of [[person/mutammim-ibn-nuwayra|Mutammim]], was killed. The event is part of the [[event/ridda-wars|Ridda wars]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Malik
Al-Baladhuri says that Malik had been an agent of the Prophet over the alms of the Banu Hanzala. When the Prophet died, Malik left them what was in his hand of the obligatory shares, and said to them: "your affairs are your own property." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Two accounts of the killing
In one account Khalid fought them at al-Bitah and killed Malik. In another, he sent out raiding parties, and [[person/dirar-ibn-al-azwar|Dirar ibn al-Azwar]] captured Malik and a group with him, brought them to Khalid, and Khalid ordered their necks struck; Dirar took charge of killing Malik. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

It is reported that Malik told Khalid that he had not apostatised. Abu Qatada al-Ansari testified that the Banu Hanzala laid down their arms and gave the call to prayer. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Umar's position
Al-Baladhuri says that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] told Abu Bakr that he had sent a man who killed Muslims. This is a statement attributed to Umar in al-Baladhuri, and the museum passes no judgement on it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Mutammim's elegy
Al-Baladhuri relates that Mutammim came to Umar and told him of his grief for his brother, and recited his elegy. Umar said to him that no one had consoled him with a better consolation than the one he had given him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date of the event is after Buzakha in the caliphate of Abu Bakr, and the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived.
