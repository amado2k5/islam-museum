## Overview
Ibn Rajab's father, the shaykh Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Rajab al-Baghdadi, moved with his children from [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] to [[place/damascus|Damascus]] in 744 AH, when Ibn Rajab was about eight years old; the father had already heard from the masters of Baghdad before the move. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 8} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 933}

## First steps in learning
His father saw to his education, above all in hadith, and Ibn Rajab became a master of isnad-craft and the science of hidden defects before rising to the front rank of the Hanbali school, joining hadith and fiqh. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam pp. 8-9} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 933}
