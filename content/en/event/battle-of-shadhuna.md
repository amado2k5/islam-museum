## Overview
At the end of Ramadan 92 AH [[person/tariq-ibn-ziyad|Tariq ibn Ziyad]] met the army of King Roderic near Shadhuna. The Muslims won a decisive victory, the Gothic army was routed, and the road to the conquest opened. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.45}

## Its significance
According to the book, historians hold that this battle decided the fate of al-Andalus in favour of the Muslims. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p.45}

The Hijri date is given as the book gives it; it has not been converted to the Gregorian calendar. The book probably refers to the same engagement that another source calls the battle of Wadi Lakka; linking the two needs an additional source.
