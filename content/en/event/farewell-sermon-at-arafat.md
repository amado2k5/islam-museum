## Summary
On the day of Arafa, 9 Dhu al-Hijja 10 AH, the Prophet addressed more than a hundred thousand in the comprehensive [[work/farewell-sermon|Farewell Sermon]], declaring blood and property inviolable, abolishing the blood-feuds and usury of the Jahiliyya, and taking the people's witness that he had delivered the message {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq pp. 459-460}.

## The account
A hundred and twenty-four — or a hundred and forty-four — thousand gathered around the Prophet in the belly of the valley at Arafa, and he stood among them preaching {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 459}. He opened: "people, hear what I say, for I do not know — perhaps I will not meet you after this year of mine in this standing-place, ever" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 459; ibn-hisham-sira}. He declared their blood and property inviolable like the sanctity of that day, month and land; that the first blood put down of the Jahiliyya's was the blood of Ibn Rabi'a ibn al-Harith, and the first usury put down the usury of [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 459; sahih-muslim}. He charged them to treat women well, told them he had left among them that which, if they held fast to it, they would never go astray — the Book of God — and that there would be no prophet after him and no community after them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 460; sahih-muslim}. Then he asked what they would say of him, and they bore witness that he had conveyed, fulfilled and counselled; he raised his forefinger to the sky and said: "O God, bear witness" — three times {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 460}. The one who cried the Prophet's words out to the people at Arafa was [[person/rabia-ibn-umayya-ibn-khalaf|Rabi'a ibn Umayya ibn Khalaf]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 460; ibn-hisham-sira}. After the sermon the verse [[passage/quran-5-3|al-Ma'ida 3]] — "this day I have perfected your religion for you" — came down, and Umar wept and said: after perfection there is only decline {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 460; sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
On Mount Arafat the Prophet addressed the largest gathering of Muslims ever around him, teaching them in one sermon that people's blood and property are sacred, charging them with women and with God's Book — and they all bore witness that he had delivered the message.
