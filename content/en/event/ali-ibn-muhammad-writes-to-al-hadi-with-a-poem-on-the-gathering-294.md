## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad wrote to al-Hadi about the gathering of Banu al-Harith and Ya'am, and added at the foot of the letter a poem urging al-Hadi to come to Najran before the rebels could consolidate. It names the tribes gathered against them and the tribes that support the family of the Prophet.

## Detail
The book says that Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah wrote to al-Hadi about the gathering. His son Ali ibn Muhammad, whom the editor takes to be the narrator of the book, wrote to him the state of the town and added verses at the end. The poem opens with a lament on grey hair, says the enemies had sworn to kill the men of the house, claims that al-Musawwid had come to help them, and urges al-Hadi to Najran. It names the tribes that had gathered, among them Khuthaym and Madhhij, and the tribes that support the family of the Prophet, among them Banu Shakir and Thaqif. The record gives the subject of the verses and does not quote them.
