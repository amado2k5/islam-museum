## Overview
Al-Hadi had ten men of Yam and Ahlaf jailed in chains. Banu Rabi'a came to him asking that he commend them to Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, saying that after Ibn Bistam they had no one but al-Hadi. He agreed, and had Abu Ja'far swear to support Banu Rabi'a and to look after their affairs.

## Detail
The book says that al-Hadi had ten men of Yam and Ahlaf held in chains and in prison and secured them. When Banu Rabi'a saw this, and knew that al-Hadi would not hold back in the matter of Ibn Bistam, they came to him and asked him to commend them to Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, saying that after Ibn Bistam they had no one but al-Hadi. He agreed, and at once sent for Abu Ja'far, who had Banu Rabi'a with him, and had him swear to them that he would support them against anyone who wronged them, take charge of their affairs and look after them.
