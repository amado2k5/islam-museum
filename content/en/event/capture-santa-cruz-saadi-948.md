## Summary
The Sa'di capture of the fortress of Santa Cruz from the Portuguese in 948/1541 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 227}.

## The Ottoman aid
Al-Salabi quotes [[person/joao-iii-portugal|João III]]'s letter to his Madrid ambassador — 22 Ramadan 948/December 1541 — that the Sa'di ruler now possessed Ottoman artillery, war engines and drilled troops, shown at the siege; that made holding Safi and Azemmour too hard, so he ordered their immediate evacuation and the fortifying of Mazagan {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 227}.
