## Summary
Muhammad ibn Yahya al-Dhuhli grew jealous of him as crowds gathered at his session, and proclaimed that whoever attended al-Bukhari's session should not attend his; al-Bukhari was attacked for saying that his own recitation of the Qur'an was created — though he held the Qur'an itself to be the uncreated word of God — and he left Nishapur for Bukhara {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1360} {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 12-13}.
