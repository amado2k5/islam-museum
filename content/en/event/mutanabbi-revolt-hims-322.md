## Overview
Towards the end of 322/933 al-Mutanabbi led a rising that began at Latakia and spread to the western borders of the Samawa, where the Banu Kalb were ever ready to rebel; without adhering to Karmatism he exploited its principles among the marauding Bedouins. After initial successes he and his Bedouins were defeated; he was captured and imprisoned at Hims, tried, held two years, made to retract his errors and set free — gaining from the adventure only the laqab 'al-Mutanabbi' (he who professes to be a prophet) and the conviction that poetry would realise his ambitions {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 769-770}.
