## Summary
In 20 AH [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|'Umar ibn al-Khattab]] divided [[place/khaybar|Khaybar]] among the Muslims and expelled its Jews, divided Wadi al-Qura, and removed the Jews of [[place/najran|Najran]] to Kufa; in the same year he dismissed Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas from Kufa over its people's complaints. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir records among the events of year twenty that 'Umar dismissed Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas from the governorship of Kufa because its people complained of him, saying "he does not pray well" {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}. In that year 'Umar divided Khaybar among the Muslims and expelled the Jews from it, and he likewise divided Wadi al-Qura {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}. He also removed the Jews of Najran, who were resettled in Kufa {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}. In the same year 'Umar sent 'Alqama ibn Mujazziz al-Mudliji against Abyssinia, which had been raiding the lands of Islam; the Muslims suffered losses, and 'Umar bound himself never again to embark anyone by sea for a campaign — though some dated that expedition to year thirty-one {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}. Among the year's deaths he lists Usayd ibn Hudayr {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 387}. These measures belonged to 'Umar's management of fay' lands and Muslim interests in the Hijaz and Iraq.
