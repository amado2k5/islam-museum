## Overview
al-Suyuti reports that Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad al-Umawi, ruler of [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]], took the title Amir al-Mu'minin, al-Nasir li-Din Allah. This was at a time when the Abbasid state had grown weak. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 605}

## The account
al-Suyuti says that [[person/abd-al-rahman-iii-al-nasir|Abd al-Rahman]] declared that he was the most entitled of people to the caliphate, and he seized most of al-Andalus. He describes him as a man of dignity, of jihad and of praiseworthy conduct, and says that he conquered seventy forts. He adds that those called Amir al-Mu'minin had become three: the Abbasid in Baghdad, this one in al-Andalus, and al-Mahdi in Qayrawan. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 605}

Needs an additional source: al-Suyuti places the account under the year 325 without specifying the year of the title, so the recorded date is approximate and derived.
