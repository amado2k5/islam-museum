## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] expelled the people of [[place/najran|Najran]] to al-Najraniyya near Kufa, and that after him the caliphs successively altered the garments imposed on them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The expulsion
Al-Baladhuri says that when Umar succeeded to the caliphate, he found the people of Najran had taken to usury and had multiplied, and he feared for Islam from them. So he expelled them to [[place/al-najraniyya|al-Najraniyya]], bought their properties and possessions, and wrote to them granting them latitude to cultivate land in Syria and Iraq. The name is attributed to them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

In the report of Salim ibn Abi al-Ja'd, they reached forty thousand and envied one another, so they asked to be expelled, and then regretted it. He reports that they came to [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], and said that Umar was right in his judgement and that he disliked to oppose him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## What followed
Al-Baladhuri lists the changes to the garments imposed on them. [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] remitted two hundred garments by a letter to [[person/al-walid-ibn-uqba|al-Walid ibn Uqba]]. [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|Al-Hajjaj]] brought them back to one thousand eight hundred garments. [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] imposed two hundred garments on them. [[person/abu-al-abbas-al-saffah|Al-Saffah]] confirmed them at two hundred, and [[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] reaffirmed them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The approximate date of about 20 AH is derived; the book does not state it.
