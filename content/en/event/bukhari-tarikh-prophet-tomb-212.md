## Summary
Al-Bukhari said of himself: when I reached eighteen I began to compile the Tarikh at the Prophet's tomb on moonlit nights {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 5}; it is the Tarikh al-Kabir giving the biographies of the men whose names appear in the isnads, which he wrote as a young man by moonlight at the tomb {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1361}.
