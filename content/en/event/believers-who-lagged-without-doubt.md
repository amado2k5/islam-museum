## Summary
Ibn Ishaq mentions that the resolve of a number of Muslims lagged behind the Prophet until they stayed behind him without doubt and without suspicion; he names Ka'b ibn Malik, Marara ibn al-Rabi', Hilal ibn Umayya and Abu Khaythama among them, describing them as truthful men whose Islam could not be questioned. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

## The account
The Prophet's journey settled in and he resolved to march; a number of Muslims' resolve had lagged behind him until they stayed behind him without doubt and without suspicion. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

Among them: Ka'b ibn Malik ibn Abi Ka'b, brother of Banu Salama; Marara ibn al-Rabi', brother of Banu Amr ibn Awf; Hilal ibn Umayya, brother of Banu Waqif; and Abu Khaythama, brother of Banu Salim ibn Awf. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}

Ibn Ishaq describes them as truthful men whose Islam could not be questioned. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}
