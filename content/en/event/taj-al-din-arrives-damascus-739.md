## Overview
Taj al-Din al-Subki came to Damascus in 739 with his father, the chief qadi Taqi al-Din, and heard hadith there from Zaynab bint al-Kamal, Ibn Abi al-Yusr and others {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425}.
