## Overview
Ibn al-Fadl left Sanaa for the place called Qadam, then went to Shiban, then to al-Maghrib, where his men plundered, took women captive and seized property.

## Detail
Ibn al-Fadl held back his men from plunder on Sunday, five days before the end of Muharram, and left Sanaa that day for the place called Qadam. He fought there for about fifty days without winning, and the people there did not come close to him. Ibn al-Yafi'i was killed at Qadam with a group of his men. Ibn al-Fadl then went to Shiban, met his companion there, and stayed with him about a month. Then he went to al-Maghrib and camped at Bayt Khawlan, and his men plundered al-Maghrib, took women captive and seized property. The printed phrase for the day gives five nights before the end of Muharram; the record keeps it as printed.
