## Summary
In 494 AH Godfrey, who had taken Jerusalem in 491 AH, besieged the city of Acre, and an arrow struck and killed him {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 400}.

## Note
This is what the Islamic historians report. Latin accounts say he died of illness in Jerusalem {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 400}.
