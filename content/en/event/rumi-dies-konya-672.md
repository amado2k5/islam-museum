## Summary
[[person/jalal-al-din-rumi|Jalal al-Din Muhammad al-Rumi]] died at Konya on 17 December 1273 (672 AH) at the age of sixty-six, and his disciples afterwards formed the Mawlawiyya order. {cite:britannica-rumi}

## Tomb and tekke
The earliest iwan containing his cenotaph is dated 672/1273-74 and the inscribed wooden cenotaph itself 674/1277, within what became the Mevlevi complex and museum; Kaykubad I, as Jalal al-Din's patron, had created Konya's greatest spiritual memorial in the tekke that originated in 628/1231. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.1 p. 289}
