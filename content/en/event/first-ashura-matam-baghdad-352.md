## Overview
On the day of Ashura in 352 AH, [[person/muizz-al-dawla|Mu'izz al-Dawla]] required the people of Baghdad to wail and hold mourning over al-Husayn. The book quotes al-Dhahabi that this was the first time he was mourned in Baghdad. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.17}

## What happened
He ordered the markets closed and sackcloth hung on them, and forbade the cooks from making food. Women went out with loosened hair, striking themselves. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.17}

## The festival of Ghadir
The same report says that on the eighteenth of Dhu al-Hijja that year the Shi'a observed the festival of Ghadir. They beat the drums and prayed the festival prayer in the desert. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.17}

The date of the event in the Gregorian calendar is converted. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.17}
