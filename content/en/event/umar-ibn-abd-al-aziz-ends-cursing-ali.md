## Overview
[[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]] ended the practice of the Umayyads of cursing [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] from the pulpits, and wrote to the provinces to abandon it. {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.330}

## The replacement
In its place he put a verse of justice and beneficence: [[passage/quran-16-90|al-Nahl 16:90]]. {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.330}

## The accompanying reports
The book says that this followed the warning of Ubayd Allah ibn Utba ibn Mas'ud, and that Kuthayyir Azza praised him for it. {cite:khudari-umayyad-state p.330}

The book does not give a date for the act within his caliphate (99–101 AH).
