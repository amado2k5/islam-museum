## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad relates from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]], from Muhammad ibn Hisham, the narrator adding that he also heard the report from groups of Yemenis, that they went out with [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] on his first journey in 280 AH, making for [[place/sanaa|Sanaa]], and before reaching the town passed trees of "firsak", that is peach; one of his army took a peach and ate it, and when al-Hadi learned of it he turned back from where he was. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.63} The report belongs with [[event/al-hadi-first-journey-to-yemen-280]].

## The lamp
A Yemeni told the narrator that when al-Hadi was urged to remain in Yemen, he said: "I find nothing for myself except to be like this lamp that burns itself and gives light to others." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.63} This is the Zaydi tradition, and the museum attributes the praise to its narrators.

## A request to plunder
Al-Kufi heard al-Hadi say that a Yemeni had said to him: "Son of the Messenger of God, leave us to plunder whatever we come upon"; he replied: "Never, by God, will that be." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.63} The text names neither the man nor the place.
