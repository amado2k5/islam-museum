## Overview
Al-Suyuti, in "History of the Caliphs", says that in 235 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutawakkil|al-Mutawakkil]] required the Christians to wear the "Asali", a garment that distinguishes them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.538}

## What is not covered here
The book gives no detail here on the reasons or the implementation. An additional source is needed.
