## The story
From the hadith of Abdullah ibn Masud in the Forty of al-Nawawi with Ibn Rajab's commentary: each of you is gathered in his mother's womb forty days as a drop, then becomes a clot like that, then a chewed lump like that; then Allah sends the angel who blows the spirit into him and commands four things: that his provision, his term, his deed, and whether he is wretched or blessed be written {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam} [[passage/arbaun-04|Forty 4]].

## The meaning
In this hadith the angel is the one who blows the spirit into the fetus by Allah's command, and the decree — provision, deed, term, and blessedness or wretchedness — is written at the moment of the blowing {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}.
