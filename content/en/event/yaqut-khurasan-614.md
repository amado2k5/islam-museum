## Summary
From Aleppo Yaqut travelled to Mosul and Irbil, then on towards Khurasan, avoiding Baghdad for fear his Damascus opponent might recognise him and spread the news — which would mean death {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}. This was in 613-14/1216-17 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}.

## Shadhiyakh of Nishapur
At Nishapur awaited him a trial of a sentimental kind: he fell in love with a beautiful Turkish slave girl and stayed near two years, then separated from her — to Ray and Sarakhs — full of sorrow {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265} {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 277}.
