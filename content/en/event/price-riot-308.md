## Overview
At the end of 308 prices spiked and the Baghdad mob surged on Hamid b. al-'Abbas's gate; his ghilman came out fighting and killed a number of the commoners. On Friday they barred the imam from prayer, demolished the minbars, smashed the police benches and burned the prisons. The sultan ordered the mob fought — they were seized and beaten; Hamid's surety was voided and bread was sold at five dinars less {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi}.
