## Summary
In the year 600 AH, Kawkja, the mamluk of al-Bahlawan who ruled Rayy, Hamadhan and the Jibal lands, was killed, and another mamluk of al-Bahlawan named Aytaqmish took his place over the same domains. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 292}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that Aytaqmish was a mamluk whom al-Bahlawan had raised, promoted and trusted; Aytaqmish gathered troops from the mamluks and others, marched against Kawkja, and the two sides fought until Kawkja was killed in battle and Aytaqmish seized the country. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 292} He kept Uzbak ibn al-Bahlawan as the nominal holder of royal title, while Aytaqmish himself directed the kingdom's affairs; Ibn al-Athir describes him as gallant, brave and unjust, and describes Kawkja as a just ruler of good conduct. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 292}
