## Overview
In Safar 297 AH the Qarmatian and Ibn Dhi al-Tawq march from al-Mudhaykhira on Zabid, defeat Ibn Hajjaj, sack the city and hold it for seven days before returning to al-Mudhaykhira.

## Detail
The book says the Qarmatians defeated Ibn Hajjaj, who fled to al-Mahjam. They sacked Zabid and killed a great number. The author gives, as reported to him, thirty-five thousand women taken captive. They stayed seven days and went back to al-Mudhaykhira, leaving Ahmad ibn Ali in Zabid, where Ibn Hajjaj drove him out. The captive figure is the author's report and is not checked against a second source.
