## Summary
In 606 AH the amir Nasrat al-Din Abu Bakr ibn al-Bahlawan, lord of Azerbaijan, took the city of Maragha after its lord Ala al-Din Qarasunqur died leaving an infant heir. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 342}

## Details
Ibn al-Athir reports that the cause was that Maragha's lord Ala al-Din Qarasunqur died that year, succeeded by an infant son whose upbringing and the conduct of his state fell to a servant of his father's. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 342} An amir who had served the father rebelled and gathered a large force; the servant sent his troops against him and they were defeated. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 342} In this weakness of the child's state and its regent, the lord of Azerbaijan found his way to the city and took it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 342}
