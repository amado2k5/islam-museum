## Overview
On Monday, five days gone into Shawwal, [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] ordered [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] to go out with an army to the fort of [[place/alab-sanaa-yemen|Alab]], where he spent the night before Tuesday; at the morning prayer a party of the men's horse raided the fort, he came down, drove them off, killed some and went back to his stronghold {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}. The men came out with their army to a place called [[place/hidayn-sanaa-yemen|Hidayn]], Abu al-Atahiya sent to tell al-Hadi, and al-Hadi went out with all his army while Abu al-Atahiya came down from the fort with his men; al-Hadi advanced on them as far as Hidayn and drew up his men in right, left and centre, with Abu al-Atahiya and his men on the left {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}. The men charged him, al-Hadi charged the left and the centre with his men and routed them, they turned back, the sword fell among them and many were killed; they went up Hidayn and [[person/ali-ibn-sulayman-ibn-al-qasim|Ali ibn Sulayman]] followed them {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}.

## Detail
In the footnote the editor adds the words of the author of Ghayat al-amani, Tabr Hidayn, and al-Hamdani's statement that the Tabr is a mountain near Sana'a, and the statement of a modern writer whose name is printed there that the mountains of Hidayn lie south of Sana'a about seven kilometres away {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.232}. A Monday with five days gone into Shawwal does not agree with the tabular calendar, so the museum gives no day. It goes with [[event/the-deaths-of-ali-ibn-sulayman-and-abu-al-atahiya-at-hidayn-288|the deaths of Ali ibn Sulayman and Abu al-Atahiya at Hidayn]].
