## Summary
Al-Salabi records great earthquakes striking Constantinople in the time of [[person/bayazid-ii|Bayezid II]] {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 174}.

## The damage
The quakes ruined 1,070 houses and 109 mosques, a great part of the palaces and city walls; water channels were choked, the sea rose over the walls in waves, and the shocks recurred daily for forty-five days. When they subsided the sultan set 15,000 workers to rebuild what had fallen {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 174}.
