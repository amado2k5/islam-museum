## Summary
[[person/al-ghamidiyya|The Ghamidi woman]] was stoned in year 9 AH after she came and confessed the indecency against herself {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## The account
She confessed before the Prophet, who turned her away until she confessed fully and insisted; then he deferred her until she bore her son and weaned him — and she was stoned after the weaning {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439; sahih-muslim}. The Prophet said of her that she had repented a repentance which, if divided among seventy of Medina's people, would have sufficed them {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## For young visitors
A woman confessed a grave sin and insisted on bearing its punishment after bearing and weaning her child — and the Prophet described her repentance as having been immense.
