## Overview
Then on Sunday, ten days gone of Jumada II, letters reached [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] from [[person/ahmad-ibn-muhammad-governor-of-najran|Abu al-Husayn Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Abbasi]], his governor at [[place/najran|Najran]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} The account is Zaydi, in the imam's followers' voice; in the text it carries the heading "Abu al-Qasim's stay at Khiwan". The date is the book's own and is converted only to Jumada II 286 AH.

## The letter from Najran
The letter said that [[person/abdullah-ibn-bustam|Ibn Bustam]] had set about mischief against the imam, and had offered the Yamis money in it so that strife would return between them and Banu al-Harith; among those who backed him in it were Marzuq ibn Muhammad, Abu al-Wajih ibn Musa and Nahil ibn Muhajir, the Khushaymis, and strife and mischief broke out in the town. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} The name "al-Khushaymiyyun" and the third name are given as printed, with no check by the editor in the footnote; for the earlier history of the tribes see [[event/al-hadi-at-najran-oath-between-hamdan-and-banu-al-harith-284]].

## Writing to al-Da'am
When the letter came al-Hadi wrote to [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] to meet him in the land of [[group/banu-salman-hamdan|Banu Salman]], because Banu Salman had not yet heard him and feared him and were afraid of him, from what they had done earlier at Athafit to Abu Umar. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} Al-Da'am went out from [[place/ghuraq-yemen|Ghuraq]] and al-Hadi from [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127}

## Abu al-Qasim at Khiwan, and the meeting at Iyan
He left his son Abu al-Qasim at Khiwan, and the two met at a place called [[place/iyan-yemen|Iyan]], a land of Banu Salman, where al-Da'am met him with Banu Salman and asked safe-conduct for them from al-Hadi; al-Hadi gave them safe-conduct and they swore to him hearing and obedience. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} The son is most likely [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Muhammad ibn al-Hadi]], going by the kunya and the earlier pages, but the full name is not given here.

## On to al-Qamashiyya and Asal
Al-Hadi went on toward Najran with al-Da'am until they halted at a place called [[place/al-qamashiyya-yemen|al-Qamashiyya]] and spent the night; then they went about the peace between the Salmanis and the Khawlanis, because Banu Salman had killed two men of [[group/khawlan-tribe|Khawlan]], and al-Hadi asked al-Da'am to meet him with the body of Banu Salman. Next morning he left al-Qamashiyya and halted at a place called [[place/asal-khawlan|Asal]], in the land of Khawlan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127} In the footnotes the editor refers to Sifat Jazirat al-Arab for the three places: Iyan on p. 73, al-Qamashiyya on p. 83 and Asal on p. 82. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.127}

## Notes
The day and month come from the book; the Julian conversion is derived from the tabular calendar and carries the usual one-day difference of this book. The text names the imam's son only by the kunya.
