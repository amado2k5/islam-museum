## Overview
In 319 AH the vizier Sulayman ibn al-Hasan was arrested. al-Tabari says that the tightness of money in his days and the continuing unrest of the troops were among the causes, and that poets satirised him. Abu al-Qasim Ubayd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Kalwadhani was then appointed vizier. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.11 p.138}
