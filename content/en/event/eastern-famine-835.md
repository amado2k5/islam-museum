## Overview
The "Fragments of Gold" states that the East, from Baghdad to Tabriz, was ruined in 835 AH by the severity and generality of high prices. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222}

## What the source says
Ibn al-Imad says that people reached such a state that they ate dogs and carrion. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 222}

The available text gives no further detail on the causes or the duration. An additional source is needed.
