## Summary
Al-Bukhari set out in 210 after he had heard much in his own town from Muhammad ibn Salam al-Baykandi, Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Baykandi, Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Musnadi, Ibrahim ibn al-Ash'ath and others {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 3}; when he reached sixteen, having already memorised the works of Ibn al-Mubarak, he made the pilgrimage with his mother and his brother, and when they returned he remained behind seeking hadith {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki j.2 p. 5} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1360}.
