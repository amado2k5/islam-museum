## Overview
In Ramadan 627 AH the enemies of Jalal al-Din al-Khwarazmi broke his army, as Ibn al-Imad relates. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.127}

## The account
The people of Syria and others feared the Khwarazmians, and agreed to make war on Jalal al-Din. They met him in Ramadan, defeated him and plundered his camp. He fled to Khilat with seven men, then to Azerbaijan. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.127}

## The peace
Jalal al-Din sent to the king al-Ashraf asking for peace, and Khilat was given safety. Ibn al-Imad relays from al-Muwaffaq Abd al-Latif that the Khwarazmians were defeated with little provision. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.127}
