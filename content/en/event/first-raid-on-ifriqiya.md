## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]] appointed [[person/abdullah-ibn-sad-ibn-abi-sarh|Abd Allah ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Sarh]] over Egypt and the Maghrib, and he sent the Muslims in cavalry raids that struck the borders of [[place/ifriqiya|Ifriqiya]] and took booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The order to raid
Uthman had held back from raiding Ifriqiya, then resolved on it after consulting, and wrote to Abd Allah ordering the raid, and reinforced him with a great army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri names among the army [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-zubayr-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr]], [[person/busr-ibn-abi-artat|Busr ibn Abi Artat]] and [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-abi-bakr|Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr]]. The poet [[person/abu-dhuayb-al-hudhali|Abu Dhu'ayb al-Hudhali]] died there. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Fighting and peace
Al-Baladhuri reports from Ibn al-Zubayr that the Byzantine commander of Ifriqiya fought them for days until he killed him, that it was he who killed him, and that his army broke up. Then Ibn Abi Sarh sent out raiding parties, who took booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Then the great men of Ifriqiya asked for peace in return for a great sum of money to be spared their land. The reports differ over its amount; al-Baladhuri gives among them three hundred qintars of gold, and two million and five hundred thousand dinars. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The date
Al-Baladhuri dates the letter to Abd Allah to 27 AH, and says it is said to be 28 or 29. The date therefore remains disputed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.317}
