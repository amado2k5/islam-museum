## Overview
Sultan al-Ashraf Barsbay appointed Ibn Hajar chief qadi of the Shafi'is in Egypt in Muharram 827, after dismissing Ala al-Din al-Bulqini: Ibn al-Imad dates it 17 Muharram {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 271}, while the Encyclopaedia of Islam gives 27 Muharram (31 December 1423) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 810}. The office remained his through repeated dismissals and reinstatements for a combined total of about twenty-one years, until he lost it finally on 25 Jumada II 852 (26 August 1448) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 810}.
