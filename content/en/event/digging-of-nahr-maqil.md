## Overview
Al-Baladhuri, in "Futuh al-Buldan", gives two accounts of the origin of the Canal of Maqil in [[place/basra|Basra]]. In both the canal is attributed to the Companion [[person/maqil-ibn-yasar|Maqil ibn Yasar al-Muzani]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The first account
Umar ordered Abu Musa to dig the other canal and to run it by the hand of Maqil ibn Yasar, and so it was named after him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The second account
It is said that al-Mundhir ibn al-Jarud al-Abdi spoke to Mu'awiya about digging the canal of Thar, and Mu'awiya wrote to [[person/ziyad-ibn-abi-sufyan|Ziyad]]. Ziyad dug the canal and ran it by the hand of Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakra or another. When they had finished and wanted to open it, Ziyad sent Maqil, and he opened it for its blessing, since he was one of the Companions. The people then said: "the Canal of Maqil". {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The test
Al-Baladhuri reports that Ziyad gave a man a thousand dirhams, and told him to reach the Tigris and ask about the owner of the canal; if anyone said it was the Canal of Ziyad, he would keep the thousand. The man came back and said he had asked no one without being told that it was the Canal of Maqil. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
