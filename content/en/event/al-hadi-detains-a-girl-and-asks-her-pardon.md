## Overview
Ali ibn Muhammad relates from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]] that he saw [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] with a party disputing over a girl, one claimant being absent, and al-Hadi ordered her held in a place until he could look into her case. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.60} After two or three days he sent to some people of the town and said: know that I had this girl detained when she was not liable to detention, and I did so only hoping the matter would be cut short; go to her, ask her to hold me blameless, and free her. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.60} The text does not say what the dispute was, nor name the girl or the claimants. It is a report in a Zaydi book attributing the act to the imam.
