## Summary
Saad al-Din Kumushtakin, stepson of al-Malik al-Salih, was killed. After him the Franks judged the position of Aleppo to be weak, so they marched on Harim and besieged it, though it was the most secure of the Muslim fortresses in their path {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 89}.
