## Overview
Ibn Imad says that Lank, that is Timur, raided the lands of India in 801 AH and took Delhi, and took many captives from it. When he returned to Samarqand the Indian captives were sold at a very low price because of their number. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.3}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
