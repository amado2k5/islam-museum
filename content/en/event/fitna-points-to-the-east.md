## The story
Malik relates from Abdullah ibn Dinar from Abdullah ibn Umar who said: I saw the Messenger of God pointing to the east and saying: here — the fitna is here, the fitna is here — from where the shaytan horn rises {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2794|Muwatta 2794]].

## The meaning
In this report the Prophet pointed to the east, where the shaytan horn rises, and said the fitna is there; it belongs to the fitna hadiths that tie the direction of trial to the shaytan times {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
