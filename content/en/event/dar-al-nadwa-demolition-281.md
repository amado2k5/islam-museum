## Overview
In 281 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] demolished the Dar al-Nadwa in Mecca and made it a mosque beside the Sacred Mosque, as al-Suyuti mentions in "Tarikh al-Khulafa". {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 574}

## Note
The Gregorian year (around 894 CE) is derived by conversion. The report is from a single source, and it needs an additional source.
