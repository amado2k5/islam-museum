## The story
In Jami' al-Tirmidhi a hadith qudsi in which the Prophet said: tonight my Lord, blessed and exalted, came to me in the fairest form — I think he said in a dream — and said: O Muhammad, do you know what the Highest Assembly disputes about? I said: no. He placed His hand between my shoulders... {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} [[passage/tirmidhi-3233|Tirmidhi 3233]].

## The meaning
This hadith qudsi is among the texts of the vision in which there is a divine address; the Prophet's vision may be in wakefulness or sleep according to the different reports {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
