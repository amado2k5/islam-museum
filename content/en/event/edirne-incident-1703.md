## Overview
Mustafa II was deposed in the "Edirne incident" of 1115/1703 and replaced by his brother Ahmed III, under pressure from malcontents coming from Istanbul {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## A sultan deposed in residence
Mustafa II held his court at Edirne, famous for its royal hunting parties, when the disaffected moved from Istanbul; the incident opened the way to the Tulip Era {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.
