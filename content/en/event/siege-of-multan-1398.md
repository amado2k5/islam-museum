## Overview
When Timur Lang swept into India in 1398-9 his grandson Pir Muhammad ibn Jahangir besieged Multan for six months until it fell {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## After the invasion
In the invasion's wake Multan came under Khidr Khan, founder of the short-lived Sayyid dynasty of Delhi, which cut the city's first long bond with Delhi's earlier rulers {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.

## In the city's memory
Within about a century the city would be the centre of the Langah state, which resisted the Lodis until 1526 — so the Timurid siege opened a new hundred years of its history {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 603}.
