## Overview
[[person/al-raghib-al-isfahani|al-Raghib al-Isfahani]] compiled his alphabetical lexicon [[work/mufradat-gharib-al-quran|Mufradat alfaz al-Qur'an]], the clearest display of his predilection for subtle semantic analysis; its influence can still be traced in tafsir and in the lexicons of Qur'anic vocabulary {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 389-390}.

## Within a Qur'anic series
In the Mufradat's introduction al-Raghib mentions two earlier works of his — the Risala munabbiha 'ala fawa'id al-Qur'an and the Risala fi'l-qawanin al-dalla 'ala tahqiq munasabat al-alfaz, both apparently lost — and promises a book on the obscure distinctions between apparent synonyms in the Qur'an, probably the Durrat al-ta'wil fi mutashabih al-tanzil {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 389-390}.

No source dates the composition precisely; it is placed tentatively within his activity in the early fifth century before his death {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 389-390}.
