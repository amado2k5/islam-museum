## Summary
al-Baladhuri, in *Futuh al-Buldan*, reports that the plague of Amwas was in 18 AH, and that a number of Muslims died in it. It falls within the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of al-Sham]] and in [[place/amwas|Amwas]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Those who died in it
Among them was [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]], who was the commander and was fifty-eight years old. Among them was [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Muadh ibn Jabal]] of Banu Salima of the Khazraj, known as Abu Abd al-Rahman, who died in the district of al-Uqhuwana in Jordan at the age of thirty-eight. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The succession
al-Baladhuri reports that Abu Ubayda appointed a successor when he was dying, and then gives a disagreement. It was said that he appointed [[person/iyyad-ibn-ghanm|Iyad ibn Ghanm]] al-Fihri; it is also said that he appointed Amr ibn al-As, and Amr then appointed his son. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Umar meets the people of Adhri'at
He reports, quoting Abdallah ibn Qays, that he was among those who met [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] with Abu Ubayda when Umar came to al-Sham. The people of Adhri'at, who were the muqallisun, met him with swords and sweet basil. Umar wanted to stop them. Abu Ubayda said that this was their custom, and that stopping them might be understood as a breach of their covenant. Umar said: "Leave them." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

The speaker recounts that Umar, in his last days as he says, intended to spend two months in Syria and two in Egypt to see the conditions for himself rather than rely on reports (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 01:11:00}.

He recounts that when Umar reached Syria, the plague had spread there, and people hesitated over entering. Ibn Umar then reported that he had heard the Prophet say: if plague is in a land and you are outside it, do not enter it; if it is in a land and you are within it, do not leave it. Umar turned back {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 01:11:00}.

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]], following Ibn Ishaq, records the plague of Amwas in Syria among the events of the year 18, the same year in which he places the Year of Ashes (the famine of al-Ramada). Among those who died in it he names [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah]], Mu'adh ibn Jabal, Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan, Shurahbil ibn Hasana and al-Harith ibn Hisham ibn al-Mughira. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 138}

## In Ibn al-Athir
The plague of Amwas in Syria in 18 AH took the flower of the conquest's commanders: Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah, the commander of the people; Mu'adh ibn Jabal; Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan; al-Harith ibn Hisham; and Suhayl ibn Amr. Umar wrote to Abu Ubayda summoning him on an urgent matter, intending to bring him out of the epidemic. Abu Ubayda understood Umar's purpose and wrote back: "I am among the soldiers of the Muslims, and I have no desire to be apart from them, so release me from your order." When Umar read the letter he wept. He was asked, "Has Abu Ubayda died?" He said, "No, but it is as if it had already happened." Abu Musa al-Ash'ari's advice to the people was that they should move out of the villages into the open country and its fresh air until the plague lifted. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 376-377}.
