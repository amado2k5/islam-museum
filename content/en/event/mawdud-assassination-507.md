## Overview
In 507 AH [[person/mawdud|Mawdud]], lord of Mosul, was killed in the great mosque of [[place/damascus|Damascus]] by an Batini assassin. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 661}

## Events
Mawdud had brought an army to fight the king of the Franks at Jerusalem, and a battle took place between them. Then he returned to Damascus, and while he was praying the Friday prayer in the mosque, a Batini sprang at him and wounded him, and he died the same day. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 661}

Al-Suyuti relates that the king of the Franks wrote to the lord of Damascus that a people had killed their chief on their festival day, in the house of their worship, and that God should destroy them. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 661}
