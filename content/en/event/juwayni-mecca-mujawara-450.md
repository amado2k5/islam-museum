## Summary
Al-Juwayni reached the Hijaz in 450/1058 and resided at Mecca and Medina for four years, teaching, giving fatwas and gathering the paths of the school, and so received the title 'Imam of the two sanctuaries' {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 605} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi p. 230} {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 310}.
