## Summary
Abu Abd al-Rahman Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb ibn Ali ibn Sinan ibn Bahr ibn Dinar al-Khurasani al-Nasa'i was born at the city of Nasa in Khurasan in 215/830, with 214 also reported {cite:tazkirat-al-huffaz j.2 p. 617} {cite:wafayat-ayan i p. 26-27} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1023}.

## His legacy
He became one of the imams of hadith and composed the Sunan counted among the six canonical books {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 1023-1024}.
