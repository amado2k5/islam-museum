## Overview
On Wednesday morning al-Qasim went out to Yam and Ahlaf and asked them to raid Sawhan. They refused and offered to raid Banu Rabi'a instead, which he declined. Ali ibn Muhammad then sent to Wadi'a asking for support. Wadi'a did not answer, citing the strife between itself and Yam.

## Detail
The next morning al-Qasim went out to Yam and Ahlaf, and asked them to raid the village of the clan whose name is withheld here. They were unwilling. They said that if he wished they would raid with him against Banu Rabi'a. He told them he had no need of Banu Rabi'a, since al-Hadi had made them swear to give support and to defend the cause; they did not obey him in the raid on Sawhan. Ali ibn Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah then sent to Wadi'a asking for support. Wadi'a did not agree, and pleaded the strife between itself and Yam. The object of the oath in the phrase about defending it is unclear in the scanned text, so the record gives the sense only.
