## Overview
In 132 AH Marwan ibn Muhammad was killed after his defeat at the Zab. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.437} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.432}

## According to al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti says that Marwan fled to Egypt, and Salih, the brother of Abd Allah, followed him. They met at the village of Busir, where Marwan was killed in Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.413}

He gives a report transmitted by al-Suli that his head was sent to Abd Allah ibn Ali, and a cat came and tore out its tongue; Abd Allah then said what he said about the wonders of time. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.413}
