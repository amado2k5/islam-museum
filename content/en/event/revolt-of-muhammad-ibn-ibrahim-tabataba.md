## Overview
In 199 AH Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn Tabataba rose in Kufa, calling to "the contentment from the House of Muhammad". Al-Tabari says that the reports differ on the reason for his rising, among them al-Ma'mun's removal of Tahir ibn al-Husayn from the eastern provinces and his assigning of al-Hasan ibn Sahl to them. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.528}
