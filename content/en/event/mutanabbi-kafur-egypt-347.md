## Overview
From Damascus al-Mutanabbi went to Egypt and to Fustat, where he obtained the patronage of the Ikhshidid regent Kafur, and was forced to sing the praises of a patron for whom in his heart he felt only contempt — his panegyrics barely conceal regret at losing Sayf al-Dawla's favour. He agreed to celebrate Kafur, it is said, only because he had been promised the governorship of Sidon; when the promises went unfulfilled he courted the Ikhshidid general Abu Shuja' Fatik, until relations with Kafur broke down {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 770}.
