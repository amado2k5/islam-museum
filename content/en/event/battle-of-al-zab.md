## Overview
In 132 AH Marwan ibn Muhammad was defeated at al-Zab, and he was killed after the defeat. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.432} {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.437}

## al-Suyuti's account
al-Suyuti says that the Banu al-Abbas rose against Marwan, led by Abd Allah ibn Ali, uncle of al-Saffah. The two hosts met near Mosul, Marwan was broken and returned to Syria. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.413}

## al-Mas'udi's account
al-Mas'udi relates that Marwan camped at the Lesser Zab and set up a bridge over it. Abd Allah ibn Ali came to him with the armies of the people of Khurasan two nights before the end of Jumada al-Akhira 132 AH. Marwan was defeated on Saturday, eleven nights into that month, and three hundred men of the Umayyads drowned, among them Ibrahim ibn al-Walid, the deposed. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.231-232}

## In Khalifa ibn Khayyat
[[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] reports that Marwan camped at the two Zabs below Mosul and that the armies met on the morning of Saturday the eleventh of Jumada al-Akhira, 132, when Marwan was routed. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 403} He gives Marwan's army as 150,000 in Abu al-Dhayyal's report and 100,000 in another. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 p. 403}
