## Summary
In 601 AH the lord of Amid besieged the city of Khartabirt demanding its surrender, then withdrew, keeping a fort he had taken on the nearby lake of Sahsanin. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 296}

## Details
Khartabirt had belonged to Imad al-Din ibn Qara Arslan; on his death his son Nizam al-Din Abu Bakr succeeded and sought protection with Ghiyath al-Din ibn Qilij Arslan against his cousin, while the lord of Amid — a client of al-Malik al-Adil — coveted the city after Rukn al-Din's death. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 296} Al-Malik al-Ashraf marched with him and the armies of the Jazira from Sinjar, Jazirat Ibn Umar and Mosul; they arrived in Sha'ban and took the suburbs in Ramadan. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 296} Its lord appealed to Ghiyath al-Din, who sent six thousand horsemen with al-Malik al-Afdal of Samisat; at Malatya the lord of Amid left the siege, besieged Lake Sahsanin with its two forts and took one of them on 2 Dhu al-Hijja. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 296} Envoys passed between the parties as the Rum army demanded the lake's return and the lord of Amid refused; the matter dragged on, the fort stayed in his hand, and both armies dispersed to their lands. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 296}
