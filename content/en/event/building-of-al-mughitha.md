## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates how Sulayman ibn Ali improved the water of Basra by building al-Mughitha. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
The water that came to the people of [[place/basra|Basra]] was scarce, and most of the water of al-Bitiha went down the Nahr al-Dayr. The people used to draw sweet water from al-Ubulla. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

When [[person/sulayman-ibn-ali|Sulayman ibn Ali]] came, he built al-Mughitha and made its sluices on al-Bitiha. He held back the water from the Nahr al-Dayr and turned it to the Nahr Ibn Umar, and spent a million dirhams on it. The people of Basra complained of the saltiness of the water and of the entry of sea water, so the sandbank was dammed and their water was made sweet again. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
