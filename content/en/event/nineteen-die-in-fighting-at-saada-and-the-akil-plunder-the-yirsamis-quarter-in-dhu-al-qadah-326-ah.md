## Overview
A battle between the two sides kills nineteen men. On Monday, five days into Dhu al-Qa'da 326 AH, the Akil enter the Yirsamis quarter, wreck it and plunder it. Ibn al-Dahhak then withdraws to Rayda.

## Detail
The book says a battle between the two sides killed nineteen men from both. Some of those with him came in upon al-Qasim, and he fled to al-Ushsha in Saada. The Akil entered the Yirsamis quarter on Monday, five days into Dhu al-Qa'da of the same year, wrecked it and plundered it. Al-Hasan went to al-Ghayl, Ibn al-Dahhak and his men withdrew to Rayda, and al-Qasim returned to Saada and stayed there and at al-Ghayl.
