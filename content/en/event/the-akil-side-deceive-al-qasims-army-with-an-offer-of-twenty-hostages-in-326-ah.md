## Overview
The Akil side write to al-Qasim that they will hand over twenty captives and that Muslim will leave the town, and they ask the tribes for help. He agrees, his companions scatter, and they then attack him at dawn.

## Detail
The book says the Akil side and those with them deceived al-Qasim and his men. They wrote to him that they would hand him twenty captives and that Muslim would leave the town, and they sent to the tribes asking for their help and to stop the strife among them. He agreed, and they schemed against him until those with him scattered; then they attacked him at dawn when his army had broken up, and he fled to al-Ghayl. The book's own words about the deception are not repeated in this record.
