## Overview
In the days of the rising of Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath, the Kurds raided and spread corruption. [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|Al-Hajjaj]] sent [[person/amr-ibn-hani-al-absi|Amr ibn Hani al-Absi]] against the people of Damascus, who defeated them and killed a great number. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The expedition against the Daylam
Then al-Hajjaj ordered him to raid [[place/daylam|the Daylam]], and he raided them with twelve thousand men. Among them were eighty of the Banu Ajl and their clients from Kufa, among them [[person/muhammad-ibn-sinan-al-ujli|Muhammad ibn Sinan al-Ujli]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The origin of the quarter of Qazwin
Muhammad ibn Sinan settled in a village of the villages of Dastabi, then went on to Qazwin and built a house in its outer quarter. The people of the frontier blamed him, but he paid no heed. His household built outside the city with him, and then the people moved and built until the outer quarter of the city was complete. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
