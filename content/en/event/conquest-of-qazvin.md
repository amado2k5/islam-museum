## Summary
Al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba sent al-Bara' ibn Azib to Qazvin. He came to Abhar and made peace with its people. Then he attacked Qazvin, and its people sent to the Daylam asking for help; the Daylam came and halted on the mountain, without lifting a hand. When the people of Qazvin saw this, they asked for terms like those of Abhar. Al-Bara' then campaigned against the Daylam until they paid the tribute; he also raided Jilan and al-Tayalisan, and took Zanjan by force. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 406}
