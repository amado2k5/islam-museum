## Overview
[[place/damascus|Damascus]] fell to the commander [[person/atsiz-ibn-uwaq|Atsiz]] in 468 AH / 1075 CE, and the call to the Abbasid caliph was proclaimed there. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.33}

al-Sallabi places this in the context of the expansion of the Seljuk state under Sultan [[person/malikshah|Malikshah]], from Afghanistan in the east to Asia Minor in the west and the lands of Syria in the south. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p.33}
