## Overview
In 245 AH al-Tabari mentions the death of Najah ibn Salama. In the same page he mentions the building of al-Mahuzah and the Byzantine raid on Samosata. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.9 p.676}

The page is short because the available data is limited to this report. The details need an additional source.
