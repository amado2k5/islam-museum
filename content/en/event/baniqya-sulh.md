## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Khalid ibn al-Walid sent [[person/bashir-ibn-sad-al-ansari|Bashir ibn Sa'd al-Ansari]] to [[place/baniqya|Baniqya]], where he met a force of non-Arabs under [[person/farrukhbandadh|Farrukhbandadh]]. He defeated them and killed Farrukhbandadh, and he was wounded by a wound from which he died at 'Ayn al-Tamr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The peace
Khalid sent [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]] to the people of Baniqya. [[person/busbuhra-ibn-saluba|Busbuhra ibn Saluba]] came out to him, offered an apology and proposed peace. Jarir made peace with him for a thousand dirhams and a tayalisan (a shawl). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The shawl
Khalid sent the tayalisan and the thousand dirhams to Abu Bakr, and he gave the tayalisan to [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn ibn Ali]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Differing reports
al-Baladhuri says that some said Ibn Saluba came to Khalid himself. Some people deny that Jarir came to Iraq except in the caliphate of Umar, while Abu Mikhnaf and al-Waqidi said that he came twice. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date is around 12 AH, on Khalid's march, according to the estimate in the data (al-Baladhuri, p. 342).
