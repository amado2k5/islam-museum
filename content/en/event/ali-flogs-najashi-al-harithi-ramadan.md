## Summary
The book reports that Ali flogged the poet al-Najashi al-Harithi for drinking wine during Ramadan {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 306}: eighty lashes, then imprisoned him, and the next day flogged him twenty more {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 306}.

## The Incident
The book narrates from Ata' from his father that Ali flogged al-Najashi al-Harithi, a poet who had drunk wine during Ramadan {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 306}. The ruling appears in the book's discussion of the fixed penalty for drinking, noting that the penalty is obligatory for anyone who drinks wine and that reason is an objective of the law preserved by enforcing the penalty {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 307}.

## Details of the Flogging and Imprisonment
After the first flogging, Ali imprisoned him, then brought him out the next day and flogged him twenty more lashes, saying to him: "I have only flogged you these twenty for your audacity toward God and your breaking the fast in Ramadan" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 306}. The book returns to the incident in the chapter on discretionary punishment, citing imprisonment as an example {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p. 316}.
