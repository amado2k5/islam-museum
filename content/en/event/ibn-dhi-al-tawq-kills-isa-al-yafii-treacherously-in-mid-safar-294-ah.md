## Overview
In mid-Safar 294 AH Ibn Dhi al-Tawq kills Isa al-Yafi'i and some of his companions by treachery. Al-Yafi'i's surviving followers seek safety in Sanaa.

## Detail
The book dates this to the middle of Safar of the same year, 294 AH, and calls the killing treachery. Ibn Dhi al-Tawq, the Qarmatian, is named as the actor. Isa al-Yafi'i had earlier helped the Qarmatians, according to the book. His surviving men then sought safety in Sanaa.
