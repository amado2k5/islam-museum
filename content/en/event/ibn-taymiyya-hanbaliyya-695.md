## Overview
On 17 Sha'ban 695/20 June 1296 Ibn Taymiyya began teaching at the Hanbaliyya, the oldest Hanbali madrasa of Damascus, succeeding his late teacher Zayn al-Din Ibn al-Munajja. Under al-Malik al-Mansur Lajin (696-8/1297-9) the sultan appointed him to exhort the faithful to jihad for the expedition against the kingdom of Little Armenia {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 983}.
