## Overview
[[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] relates that after three days at [[place/al-fur-medina|al-Fur']] [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] told them al-Hadi had turned away from going and meant to send the Yemeni envoys back, which grieved them deeply. About midday a servant of al-Hadi came to tell them to saddle their animals, saying his master had resolved to go to Yemen. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.37} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.38}

## What al-Hadi said of his hesitation
On the road Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah asked him about his intention to stay behind. He said he had turned away from going and meant to send the envoys of Yemen back, because of what he had seen of the people of Yemen's temper and their little desire for the truth, until the night before he left he saw the Prophet in a dream asking why he was sluggish about going, telling him to rise and bid them clear the earth of these filths; he understood that nothing was meant but the disobedience of people on earth, and pledged to rise. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.39} This is a dream the narrator reports from al-Hadi, in the Zaydi narrator's voice.

## Farewell and companions
His uncles and cousins went with them a while and then said farewell; Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah heard [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim-al-rassi|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] tell al-Hadi that if his knees could carry him he would fight beside him, and ask God to make them partners in all he was engaged in. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.38} He also asked al-Hadi to send him something of what he took as spoil, even ten dirhams, to keep for blessing. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.39} The party was small: with al-Hadi only his son [[person/al-murtada-muhammad|Muhammad ibn Yahya]], Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Hasani, Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah, Yahya ibn al-Husayn of the line of Umar ibn Ali, Idris ibn Ahmad of the line of Jafar, and ten of his servants. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.38} See [[person/yusuf-ibn-muhammad-al-hasani]] and [[person/idris-ibn-ahmad-al-jafari]].
