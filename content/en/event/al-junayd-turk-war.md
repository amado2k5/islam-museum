## Overview
In 112 AH [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham]] appointed al-Junayd ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Murri over Khurasan, and he fought the Turks. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The war
[[person/al-junayd-ibn-abd-al-rahman|al-Junayd]] met the Turks and fought them. He sent vanguards who caught the son of the Khaqan while he was drunk and out hunting. They brought him to al-Junayd, who sent him on to Hisham. He kept fighting the Turks until he drove them back. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Reinforcements
al-Junayd wrote to Hisham asking for reinforcement, and Hisham sent him Amr ibn Muslim with ten thousand men from Basra, and Abd al-Rahman ibn Nu'aym with ten thousand men from Kufa. He sent him thirty thousand spears and thirty thousand shields, and gave him a free hand over the stipend roll, so he enrolled fifteen thousand men. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The end of his governorship
The source says that the missionaries of Banu Hashim spread through his province and their cause grew strong. al-Junayd died in Merv, and Hisham appointed [[person/asim-ibn-abdallah-al-hilali|Asim ibn Abd Allah al-Hilali]] over Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source quotes Abu Ubayda that some districts of Tukharistan had broken away under al-Junayd, and that he then restored them to their treaty and their separate arrangement. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Notes
The year 112 is taken from the source text; the date needs an additional source.
