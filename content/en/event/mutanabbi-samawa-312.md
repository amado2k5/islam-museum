## Overview
Towards the end of 312/924, perhaps under pressure from the Karmatians who had just taken and sacked Kufa, Abu al-Tayyib and his family made a first stay of two years in the Samawa, the desert steppe between the Sawad of Kufa and the Palmyrene, among the nomadic Banu Kalb; there the boy gained the profound knowledge of Arabic of which he was later so proud, returning to Kufa at the beginning of 315/927 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 7 p. 769}.
