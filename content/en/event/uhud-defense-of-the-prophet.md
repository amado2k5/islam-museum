## Overview
After the horsemen had outflanked them, only nine men remained with [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] in the rearguard. A stone that Utba ibn Abi Waqqas threw at him broke his lower tooth and cut his lip. Ibn Qami'a struck him on the cheek, and two links of his helmet entered it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.267}

## Those who defended him
[[person/talha-ibn-ubaydullah|Talha ibn Ubayd Allah]] and [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] held firm with him. Sa'd shot until his quiver was exhausted, and Talha was wounded thirty-five or thirty-nine times. [[person/abu-ubayda-ibn-al-jarrah|Abu Ubayda]] pulled out the two links of the helmet with his front teeth. [[person/abu-dujana|Abu Dujana]] shielded him with his back from the arrows. [[person/nusayba-bint-kab|Nusayba bint Ka'b]] defended him until she was struck by twelve wounds. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.267}

The data also includes [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]], Malik ibn Sinan and [[person/musab-ibn-umayr|Musab ibn Umayr]] among the participants; the details are in the source on pages 267–273. This is part of [[event/battle-of-uhud|the Battle of Uhud]].
