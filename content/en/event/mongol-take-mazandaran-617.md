## Overview
When one group of the Mongols despaired of reaching the Khwarazm Shah in his fortress by the sea, they turned back and made for the lands of Mazandaran, and took them very quickly despite their strength and the difficulty of entry. Ibn al-Athir relates this in "al-Kamil". {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 408}

## The land's strength
Ibn al-Athir says that Mazandaran had long stayed unconquered, both in the past and recently. When the Muslims took the lands of the Persian kings from Iraq to the far end of Khurasan, its districts still paid the land tax without the Muslims entering it, until it was conquered in the days of Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik in 90 AH. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 408}

## Conquest and the mother of the Khwarazm Shah
According to the source, the Mongols killed, enslaved, plundered and burned in Mazandaran. Then they made toward al-Rayy and came upon the mother of the Khwarazm Shah, his women, and their property and treasures; she had left Khwarazm for al-Rayy and then Isfahan to take refuge. They sent them all to [[person/genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]] in Samarqand. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 408}

## Date
617 AH, with a Gregorian equivalent (around 1220 CE) that is derived. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 408}
