## Summary
At twenty-three, about 415, he travelled to Nishapur and then Isfahan; al-Barqani had advised Nishapur over Egypt — a whole circle of teachers rather than a single man — and wrote with him a letter of recommendation to Abu Nu'aym {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 87-91}.
