## Overview
On Sunday at sunrise the two sons of Ya'fur and al-Yafi'i advanced with their troops until they came near the fort and the fighting began; stones and arrows fell on the narrator and his companions inside the house, so they went into the room, and the men fought for an hour {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.271}. Then Al Ya'fur spread out clothes, and six men who faced them above Bayt Bus came down to them and they clothed them, one of them staying behind; some of Al Ya'fur's troops looked in, and when they were with them inside the fort the man at the gate of the pass fled; they came to break the door and a man met them and said "I have the key, do not break the door", so they opened the door and entered the fort, took prisoner all who were in it and plundered what they found {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.271}.

## Detail
A group of them entered the house where the narrator and Abu al-Qasim were and took what they had on, and one of them raised his sword at Abu al-Qasim to strike him; Abu al-Qasim seized his wrist and the sword and bent it until it was like a ring, and they let the two go {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.271}. The report is from a Zaydi book in the voice of the imam's followers, and the bending of the sword and the strength it implies are as the narrator saw them, with no verdict in the museum's voice. The book does not name those who spread the clothes nor the six who came down, and does not say whether the clothes were a sign of agreement; the man with the key is not named. The date is to the month: the Sunday did not match the tabular calendar in the museum's check. It follows [[event/the-sons-of-yafur-plunder-arthal-and-the-reinforcement-of-ibn-khalaf-leaves-291|the plunder of Arthal and the leaving of Ibn Khalaf's contingent]]; [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]] and al-Yafi'i appear in the earlier report of Abu al-Qasim's chaining.
