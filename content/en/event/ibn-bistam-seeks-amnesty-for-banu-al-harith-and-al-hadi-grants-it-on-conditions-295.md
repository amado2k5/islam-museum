## Overview
Ibn Bistam asked amnesty for Banu al-Harith, and al-Hadi granted it on conditions: they must pay the zakat due, their runaway slaves who came over would not be returned, they must enter the community as the people had, they must not cause any innovation or side with an innovator, and if one of them rose, they would all be answerable together. Al-Hadi sent men to collect the zakat from the sirr of Banu Mazin.

## Detail
The book says that Ibn Bistam asked amnesty for those of Banu al-Harith who had gone into the sirr, and al-Hadi granted it on conditions: they must pay the sadaqat due to God from their property; the slaves who came to him from them would not be sent back; they must enter what the people had entered; they must neither cause any innovation nor side with an innovator, and if one of them innovated, the hand of all of them would be against him. They accepted and were sent back to their places. Al-Hadi sent Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi and Abdallah ibn Muhammad al-Sa'di with horsemen and foot soldiers to the sirr of Banu Mazin to collect the sadaqat owed by Banu al-Harith. Then Ibn Bistam came back with those of Banu al-Harith who had sought amnesty through him, asking al-Hadi to release Ali ibn al-Rabi' al-Madani for them.
