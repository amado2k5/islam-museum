## Overview
[[person/hormuzan|Hormuzan]] was brought as a captive to [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] in [[place/medina|Medina]], and it was [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas ibn Malik]] who brought him. Al-Baladhuri relates this. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The dialogue
Umar said to Hormuzan: "Speak." He asked: "Is it the speech of a living man or a dead one?" Umar said: "No harm." Hormuzan then said that the Persians used to raid the Arabs as long as God was not with them. Then Umar asked Anas his opinion of him. Anas said he was a thorn among the people; if he were killed they would despair of life and fight harder, and if he were spared they would hope. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## After the dialogue
Al-Baladhuri reports that al-Bara ibn Malik and Mujza'a ibn Thawr told Anas: "You have no way to kill him." Al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam testified for Anas, so Hormuzan was released; he became a Muslim, and Umar granted him a stipend. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
Hormuzan was accused of having sat with Abu Lu'lu'a, the slave of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba, in the killing of Umar. [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-umar|Ubayd Allah ibn Umar]] went with him to a horse of his and struck him with the sword, killing him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
