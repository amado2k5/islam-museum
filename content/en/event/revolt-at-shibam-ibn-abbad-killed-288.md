## Overview
A man of the line of Abu al-Khayr ibn Ya'fur left Shibam, together with a man of Hamdan called [[person/sasaa-ibn-jafar-al-hamdani|Sa'sa'a ibn Ja'far]]; they stopped beside [[place/bayt-dhukhar|Bayt Dhukhar]], allied against [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], with a crowd behind them. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.18} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19}

## The reason, as the book gives it
The narrator says al-Hadi had held their hands back from what was not due and forbade them wine, corruption and wrongs, and they rose to fight him, resenting it. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} This reason is the narrator's own.

## The course of events
Al-Hadi had climbed Jabal Bayt Dhukhar and left [[person/muhammad-ibn-abbad-al-tamimi|Muhammad ibn Abbad]] as deputy at Shibam; the opponents entered Shibam and he was killed, in Jumada II 288 AH. Al-Hadi came down from the mountain and drove them off, they fled, and he entered Shibam and buried Ibn Abbad. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.19} See also [[group/banu-yafur|Al Ya'fur]].
