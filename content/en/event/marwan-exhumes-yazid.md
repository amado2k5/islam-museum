## Overview
The first thing [[person/marwan-ibn-muhammad|Marwan ibn Muhammad]] did when the matter was secured to him, in the middle of Safar 127 AH, was to order that [[person/yazid-ibn-al-walid|Yazid the deficient]] be dug up from his grave and crucified. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 412}

## The reason given by al-Suyuti
Al-Suyuti says that this was because Yazid had killed al-Walid ibn Yazid. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 412}
