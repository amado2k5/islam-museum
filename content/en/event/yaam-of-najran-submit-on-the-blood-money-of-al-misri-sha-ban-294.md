## Overview
After seven days of al-Hadi's stay, the Ya'am sought safety on condition that they bear the blood-money of al-Misri, which he set at one thousand mithqal dinars; they surrendered to his judgement.

## Detail
Al-Hadi stayed at the place for seven days, asking the Ya'am to return to the truth, and they refused. When hardship pressed on them, the punishment they had suffered and their scattering in the mountain heights, they sent notables of Hamdan who were with al-Hadi, among them Abu Ma'mar al-Dalani and Ali ibn al-Hajjaj al-Shakiri, to ask for safety. He granted it on condition that they bear the blood-money of al-Misri, which he set at one thousand mithqal dinars. They accepted and surrendered to his judgement. The reading of the names and the sum is as printed; the names of the mediators are not yet recorded as persons.
