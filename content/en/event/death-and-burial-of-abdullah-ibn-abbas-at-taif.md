## Summary
[[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Abdallah ibn Abbas]] died at Ta'if in the year 68, aged seventy-one in Ibn al-Jawzi's report. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 1 p. 276} His death came after he and [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-hanafiyya|Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya]] had left Mecca for Ta'if. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} This record gathers what the books relate of Ibn al-Hanafiyya praying over him, what he said of him, the report of the white bird, and what is said of his grave.

## The move to Ta'if
Al-Dhahabi relates from Atiyya al-Awfi that after the dispute between Ibn al-Zubayr and Ibn Abbas and Ibn al-Hanafiyya they were taken out until they halted at Ta'if, and that Abu al-Tufayl al-Kinani composed verses when Ibn al-Zubayr stopped Abdallah ibn Abbas meeting people. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} Ibn Abbas had gone blind late in life, and Ibn Abd al-Barr gives two lines of his on this, saying that if God took the light of his eyes, light remained in his tongue and heart. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 426}

## Death and age
Ibn al-Jawzi says he died at Ta'if in 68, aged seventy-one. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 1 p. 276} Al-Dhahabi says Ali ibn al-Madini held that he died in 68 or 67, and al-Waqidi, al-Haytham and Abu Nuaym in 68. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415}

## Ibn al-Hanafiyya's prayer and words
Ibn Abd al-Barr says Ibn al-Hanafiyya prayed over him with four takbirs and said: today the rabbani of this community has died, and he had a tent set up over his grave. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 424} Al-Dhahabi relates from Salim ibn Abi Hafsa, from Abu Kulthum, that Ibn al-Hanafiyya said this when Ibn Abbas was buried, and notes that some reported it from Mundhir al-Thawri in place of Abu Kulthum. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} Sifat al-Safwa relates from Mundhir that Ibn al-Hanafiyya said when Ibn Abbas died: today the rabbani of this community has died. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 1 p. 276}

## Jabir's saying
Ibn al-Jawzi says that when news of his death reached [[person/jabir-ibn-abdullah|Jabir ibn Abdallah]] he clapped one hand on the other and said: the most knowledgeable and most forbearing of men has died; this community has suffered a loss that cannot be mended. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 1 p. 276}

## The report of the bird
Ibn al-Jawzi relates from Maymun ibn Mihran that he attended the funeral of Abdallah ibn Abbas at Ta'if, and when the body was laid down for the prayer a white bird came and entered his shrouds; it was searched for and not found, and when the earth was levelled over him they heard a voice and saw no one, reciting from Surat al-Fajr the verses on the tranquil soul being called back to its Lord to enter among His servants and His garden. {cite:sifat-al-safwa-tartusi-2012 scan vol. 1 p. 276} See [[passage/quran-89-27|al-Fajr 27]] and [[passage/quran-89-30|al-Fajr 30]].

Al-Dhahabi relays from Husayn ibn Waqid, from Abu al-Zubayr, that when Ibn Abbas died a white bird came and entered his shrouds; al-Ajlah adds from Abu al-Zubayr that they took it to be his knowledge, and Ata ibn al-Saib adds from Said ibn Jubayr that it was not seen afterwards. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} He relates from Hammad ibn Salama, from Bujayr ibn Abi Ubayd, that he died at Ta'if and as they carried out his bier a great white bird came from the direction of Wajj and mingled with his shrouds, and they did not see it again; they took it to be his knowledge. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} From Salim al-Aftas, from Said ibn Jubayr, a bird of a kind not seen before entered his bier and was not seen leaving it, and when he was buried this verse was recited at the edge of the grave, no one knowing who recited it. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} He says Bassam al-Sayrafi named the bird a ghurnuq, and al-Dhahabi remarks: this is a widely transmitted matter. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415}

Ibn Abd al-Barr relays from al-Zubayr that Ibn Abbas died at Ta'if and a white bird came and entered his bier as it was carried and was not seen leaving, and notes that it is related that a white bird came out of his grave. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 426}

## Grave and resting place
The sources say he was buried at Ta'if and that Ibn al-Hanafiyya had a tent set up over his grave. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 424} In the pages I read I found no description of where the grave lies within Ta'if nor any report of a structure over it, so I say nothing on that.

## Where the reports differ
The reports differ on the year of death between 67 and 68, on the form of the bird report, whether it entered the shrouds or the bier or came out of the grave, and on who reported Ibn al-Hanafiyya's saying, Abu Kulthum or Mundhir. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2415} {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 3 p. 426}
