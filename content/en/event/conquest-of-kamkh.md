## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that Habib ibn Maslama raided the fort of Kamkh after the conquest of Shimshat and could not take it, and that Safwan raided it and could not manage to take it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.259}

## The conquest by Umayr ibn al-Habab
In 59 AH Safwan raided it with [[person/umayr-ibn-al-habab|Umayr ibn al-Habab al-Sulami]], and Umayr climbed the wall and fought on it alone until he drove back the Byzantines and the Muslims went up. Then the Byzantines prevailed over it again, and [[person/maslama-ibn-abd-al-malik|Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik]] conquered it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}

## Later conquests
In 149 AH al-Mansur sent al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba and Muhammad ibn al-Ash'ath on campaign, and placed over them [[person/al-abbas-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ali|al-Abbas ibn Muhammad]]. They set up catapults, and the Muslims made siege engines until they conquered it. In 177 AH Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Ansari, governor of Shimshat, took it. Then [[person/abd-allah-ibn-tahir|Abd Allah ibn Tahir]] took it in the caliphate of al-Ma'mun. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.260}

al-Baladhuri mentions that some Christians of Shimshat tried to hand it over to the Byzantines because of a property of theirs. The date is derived from the account.
