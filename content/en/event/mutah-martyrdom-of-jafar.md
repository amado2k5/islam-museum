## Overview
After the killing of Zayd, [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]] took the banner at [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'tah]]. He dismounted from his sorrel horse and hamstrung it, and it is reported that he was the first to hamstring a mount in Islam; then he fought until he was killed. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Ibn Hisham's additions
Ibn Hisham adds, on the authority of one he trusts, that he held the banner in his right hand until it was cut off, then in his left until that was cut off, then he embraced it with his upper arms until he was killed, aged thirty-three. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

With the report introduced by "it is said", a Roman struck him and cut him in two. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
