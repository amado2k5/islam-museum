## Overview
After [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] returned from [[event/al-hadi-and-the-people-of-al-asum-at-bayt-yathi-286|Bayt Yathi']] to [[place/athafit|Athafit]] he sent to the [[group/al-subay-arhab|people of al-Subay']] and to [[group/khirfan-arhab|Khirfan]], with [[group/banu-rabia-hamdan|Banu Rabi'a]] beside him. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} The heading in the text is "the account of the peace al-Hadi made between Banu Rabi'a and the people of Khirfan and al-Subay' over the killing between them", and the manuscript margin says "his making peace between Banu Rabi'a and the people of al-Subay' and Khirfan over a killing claim". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} The account is Zaydi, in the imam's followers' voice.

## The claim
Banu Rabi'a had a claim against the people of al-Subay' and Khirfan, a claim over a life they held against them. The people of al-Subay' and Khirfan came to him, he brought them together with Banu Rabi'a, set right what lay between them and fixed their matter at nine hundred dinars, and they mixed freely. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} The text does not say whether the dinars are blood-money or some other settlement, who paid them or to whom, and none of that is assumed.

## Return to Khiwan
Then on the Saturday he left Athafit, put Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi in charge of it, and reached [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] the same day and stayed there. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.126} Husayn ibn al-Hasan al-Alawi is not identified here; it is not said that he is the [[person/husayn-ibn-al-hasan-governor-of-saqayn|governor of Saqayn]] named elsewhere, and the text does not exclude it.

## Notes
The text gives no date finer than Jumada I and what follows. The preceding account has him leave Bayt Yathi' on a Friday with days left of Jumada I, and this account has a Saturday after it, so the peace falls at the end of Jumada I or just after.
