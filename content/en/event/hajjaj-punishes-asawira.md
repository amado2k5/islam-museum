## Overview
The source reports that the Asawira (Persian cavalry in Muslim service) did not fight with the Muslims at the Battle of the Camel or at Siffin, nor in any of their wars until the day of Mas'ud. They did fight after it at al-Rabadha, and took part in the affair of Ibn al-Ash'ath with [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The punishment
Al-Hajjaj demolished the houses of the [[group/asawira|Asawira]], cut their stipends, and expelled some of them. He said to them, according to the source: "It was in your agreement that you would not help one another against us." This took place in [[place/basra|Basra]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
