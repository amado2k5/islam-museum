## Summary
Some men of the "hypocrites", in Ibn Ishaq's account, said to one another: do not march out in the heat — out of scant appetite for jihad, doubt in the truth and agitation against the Prophet; he says two verses of Sura al-Tawba came down about them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

## The account
Men of the hypocrites said to one another: do not march out in the heat. This is Ibn Ishaq's own description of them and of their motives: scant appetite for jihad, doubt in the truth and agitation against the Prophet. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

He says God sent down about them verses 81 and 82 of Sura al-Tawba. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/160}

## What was revealed in the event
- [[passage/quran-9-81|Qur'an 9:81]]: revealed about those who said "do not march out in the heat", in Ibn Ishaq's account (p. 160).
- [[passage/quran-9-82|Qur'an 9:82]]: revealed about those who said "do not march out in the heat", in Ibn Ishaq's account (p. 160).

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- The word "hypocrites" and the description of motives are Ibn Ishaq's phrasing; the museum does not attribute it to itself.
