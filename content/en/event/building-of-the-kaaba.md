## Summary
On the third recorded journey of [[event/ibrahim-visits-ismail-in-mecca|Abraham's journeys to Mecca]], [[person/ibrahim|Abraham]] met his son [[person/ismail|Ishmael]] after a long time. Together they built the [[building/kaaba|Ka'ba]] and raised its foundations, and Abraham proclaimed the pilgrimage to the people, as God had commanded him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.19} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20}.

## The meeting
Abraham came a third time and found Ishmael trimming an arrow under a tree near Zamzam. When he saw him, he rose to him and did what a father does with a son and a son with a father. Their meeting came after a long time {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.19} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/475-476}.

## Building and the call to pilgrimage
This time they built the Ka'ba and raised its foundations, and Abraham proclaimed the pilgrimage to the people, as God had commanded him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.20} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1/475-476}. Before that, the site of the House had been a raised mound of ground to which the floods came {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.18}.

## Not covered here
The building of the Ka'ba by Quraysh in the Prophet's youth, and the dispute over placing the Black Stone, belong to [[event/rebuilding-of-the-kaaba|the rebuilding of the Ka'ba and the arbitration]]. What remained in the Jahiliyya of the veneration of the House, circumambulation and pilgrimage in [[concept/diyanat-al-jahiliyya|the religions of the Arabs]] is also treated elsewhere {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38}.

## For young visitors
The father met his son after a long separation. Together they built God's House in Mecca, and then Abraham called the people to the pilgrimage.
