## Overview
The third recorded journey: Abraham's meeting with his son Ishmael after a long absence, their raising of the foundations of the House together, and the call to pilgrimage.

## The story
On the third journey Abraham found Ishmael trimming an arrow of his under a tree near [[place/mecca|Zamzam]]; they rose to each other as a father does with his son and a son with his father, and on this visit they built the [[building/kaaba|Ka'ba]] and raised its foundations {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Then Abraham proclaimed the pilgrimage to the people as God commanded him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. The site of the House before that had been a rise in the ground like a small hill {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## For young visitors
The father met his son after a long separation, and together they built the House of God in Mecca; then Abraham called the people to the pilgrimage.
