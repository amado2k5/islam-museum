## Summary
[[person/negus-of-aksum|The Negus Ashama]], king of Abyssinia, died in 9 AH, and the Prophet offered the prayer for the absent over him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.439}

## The prayer for the absent
The Negus had sheltered the first emigrants to Abyssinia, treated them well and protected them from the delegations of Quraysh. He became a Muslim at the hands of Ja'far and his companions. When the news of his death reached the Prophet, he went out with the people and prayed over him the prayer for the absent, as an honour to him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.439}

## For young visitors
The king of Abyssinia, whom the accounts describe as just, who protected the early Muslims in his land, died. The Prophet prayed over him from Medina a special prayer for one who dies far away from them.
