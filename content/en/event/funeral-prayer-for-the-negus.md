## Summary
[[person/negus-of-aksum|The Negus Ashama]], king of Abyssinia, died in 9 AH, and the Prophet prayed for him the prayer for the absent {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## The prayer for the absent
The Negus had sheltered the first emigrants to Abyssinia, treated them well and protected them from Quraysh's delegations, and had become Muslim at the hands of Ja'far and his companions; when the Prophet heard the news of his death he went out with the people and prayed for him the prayer for the absent, in honour of him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 439}.

## For young visitors
The just king of Abyssinia who protected the first Muslims in his land died, so the Prophet prayed for him from Medina the special prayer for one who dies far away.
