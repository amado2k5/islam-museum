## Summary
Ala al-Din Muhammad Khwarazm Shah besieged the city of Herat on the first of Rajab in the year 600. It was his first appearance in Khurasan after the death of Takash, and he then withdrew from it {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 285}.
