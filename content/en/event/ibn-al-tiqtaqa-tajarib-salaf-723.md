## Summary
A Persian translation of al-Fakhri by Hindushah ibn Sanjar made in 723-4/1323-4, entitled Tajarib al-salaf, indicates the title of Ibn al-Tiqtaqa's work as Munyat al-fudala' fi tawarikh al-khulafa' wa-l-wuzara' — probably a later edition of the historical section of the Fakhri; a Kitab al-Ghaya is also mentioned by Ibn al-Fuwati in Talkhis Majma' al-adab {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.
