## Summary
In 635 AH [[person/muhammad-i-ibn-al-ahmar|Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn Nasr ibn al-Ahmar]] took control of [[place/granada|Granada]] with the help of its people, and settled there after the death of Ibn Hud. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1009}

## Its significance
Granada became the seat of the Banu al-Ahmar state. In Ibn Khaldun's description, it was the last of the Islamic states in al-Andalus. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1009}
