## Summary
The defeat of the Wattasid king [[person/ahmad-al-wattasi|Ahmad]] before the Sa'dis near Wadi al-'Abid in 943/1536 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 221}.

## The battle
The Khalut tribes who formed the Wattasid army's vanguard deserted, spreading chaos through its ranks {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 221}.

## Consequence
The defeat drove Ahmad al-Wattasi toward Portugal: an eleven-year treaty placing Moroccans settled around Asilah, Tangier and Qasr al-Saghir under the jurisdiction of the king of Fas, and banning purchase of booty taken from Moroccans aboard Ottoman or French ships {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 221-222}.
