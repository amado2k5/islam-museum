## Summary
Al-Safadi made the pilgrimage in 755/1354 together with the poet Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn Abd Allah al-Khayyat, as his own sama' note in A'yan al-'asr attests; one of his works, Hakikat al-mafaz ila al-Hijaz, appears to have centred on this journey {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 759}.
