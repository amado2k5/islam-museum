## Overview
Amr ibn Luhayy's bringing idols into Mecca and setting Hubal at the Ka'ba's core — the first chapter in the history of idolatry in the peninsula after Ishmael's era.

## The story
Ibn Ishaq says: the first to introduce idols was Amr ibn Luhayy; he saw the people of al-Balqa' venerating idols, so he carried Hubal to Mecca and commanded the people to worship and venerate it, until the practice spread and every subtribe had an idol {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

The people of old had been on the religion of Abraham and Ishmael; when they spread out from Mecca and the place grew too small for them, everyone who departed took a stone of the sanctuary's stones with him in veneration — until worship of idols and stones took their place {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## For young visitors
After Ishmael's time the people forgot monotheism little by little; they carried the sanctuary's stones with them on journeys, then worshipped them — until Amr ibn Luhayy brought the great idols.
