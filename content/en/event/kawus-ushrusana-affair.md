## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Kawus, king of Ushrusana, asked [[person/al-fadl-ibn-sahl|al-Fadl ibn Sahl]], the vizier of al-Ma'mun, for peace in return for a tribute, and was granted it. When al-Ma'mun came to Baghdad, Kawus refused to keep his word. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing of the steward
Kawus had a close steward who married his daughter to al-Fadl ibn Kawus; he would bring al-Fadl near and disparage [[person/haydar-ibn-kawus|Haydar ibn Kawus]], known as al-Afshin. Haydar rushed on the steward and killed him at the gate of their city, and fled to Hashim ibn Mahmud al-Khuttali, to write to his father to seek his pardon. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Haydar's Islam
When news of what Kawus had done reached him, Haydar showed Islam and came to Baghdad. He described to al-Ma'mun how easy the matter was in Ushrusana, and pointed him to a shorter road to it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The events took place in [[place/ushrusana|Ushrusana]]. The date of the incident is not given in the data.
