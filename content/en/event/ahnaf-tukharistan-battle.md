## Overview
The people of Tukharistan and the surrounding region gathered to fight the Muslims. [[person/al-ahnaf-ibn-qays|al-Ahnaf ibn Qays]] met them with about five thousand men, and the battle ended in their defeat. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Gathering of the opponents
The people of Jawzjan, Talaqan, Fariyab and those around them gathered, reaching thirty thousand. The people of Saghaniyan came to them from the eastern side of the river. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The plan and the position
al-Ahnaf returned to his fort. He went out at night and heard a man lighting a fire under his cooking pot, or kneading dough, who said that the right plan was to camp between the Murghab and the mountain: the Murghab on his right and the mountain on his left, so that from the enemy he would face only as many as his own men. al-Ahnaf saw this as sound and did it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
He had five thousand Muslims with him: four thousand Arabs and a thousand Muslims of the non-Arab peoples. He raised his banner and charged. The king of Saghaniyan made for al-Ahnaf with a spear; al-Ahnaf snatched it from his hand and fought fiercely. He killed three of the drummers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The opponents were then routed, the Muslims killed a great many of them, and the rest laid down their arms wherever they wished, under safe conduct. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
