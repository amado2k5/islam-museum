## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Nafi' from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God said: I was shown last night at the Kaaba; I saw a ruddy man, the finest of men, circuiting the House. I asked: who is this? He was told: this is the Messiah son of Mary. Then I saw a curly man blind in his right eye, and I asked: who is this? He was told: this is the Messiah the Dajjal {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2666|Muwatta 31:2666]].

## The meaning
In this report the true vision may come to the Prophet in wakefulness as in sleep, and it carries knowledge of the end-times; it is among the texts of vision and the hereafter in the Muwatta {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
