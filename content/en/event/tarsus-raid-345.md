## Overview
The news came in 345 AH that the Byzantines pushed into the people of [[place/tarsus|Tarsus]] on the sea and killed eighteen hundred men of them. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.672}

## What followed the raid
Ibn al-Jawzi says that they burned the villages around Tarsus and took their people captive. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p.672}

The Gregorian date is derived from the Hijri date.
