## Overview
Abu al-Qasim Mahmud ibn Umar ibn Muhammad al-Zamakhshari al-Khwarazmi was born on Wednesday 17 Rajab 467 at Zamakhshar in Khwarazm {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 601}; others give 27 Rajab 467, corresponding to 18 March 1075 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 449-450}. His father, imam of the local mosque, taught him the Qur'an {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 12 p. 917}.
