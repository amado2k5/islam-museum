## Summary
Al-Nuwayri left Tripoli in Jumada I 712 (September 1312) and entered Cairo on 20 Rajab/20 November of the same year after a brief stay at Damascus; the circumstances of his departure are obscure. According to his biographers he was then appointed nazir al-diwan of two Nile Delta provinces, al-Dakhaliyya and al-Murtahiyya {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.

## The end of his administrative service
He seems to have administered the two provinces' revenues until about 716/1316 while residing at Cairo — making roughly eighteen years (698-716) of service across the three most important diwans: al-khass, al-insha' and al-jaysh {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 158}.
