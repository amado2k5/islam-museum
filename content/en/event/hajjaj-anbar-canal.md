## Overview
Al-Baladhuri, in "Futuh al-Buldan", reports that the landowners of Anbar asked [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] to dig them a canal. He wrote to [[person/sad-ibn-amr-ibn-haram|Sa'd ibn Amr ibn Haram]] ordering him to dig it, and they dug until they reached a mountain that could not be cut, and left it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Completion by al-Hajjaj
When [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] became governor of Iraq he gathered the agents from every region and spent on the digging until he finished it. So the mountain was attributed to al-Hajjaj and the canal to Sa'd ibn Amr. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Other canals
[[person/al-khayzuran|Al-Khayzuran]] ordered the digging of the canal known as Mahdud, and called it al-Rayyan. It was called Mahdud because her agent divided it into sections and set a boundary for each. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

On the Shila canal there are two accounts: the Banu Shila ibn Furukhzadan of Merv claim that Sabur dug it for their grandfather, while others say it was named after a man who undertook its digging in the days of al-Mansur, and that the canal had been buried in earlier times. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
