## Summary
In the Medinan brothering the Prophet paired the emigrant Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf with the Ansari Sa'd ibn al-Rabi'. Abd al-Rahman relates that he arrived in Medina with no property, and Sa'd told him: "I am the wealthiest of the Ansar — take half my wealth; and I have two wives — name the one you prefer and I will divorce her so you may marry her." Abd al-Rahman answered: "God bless you in your family and your wealth — show me the way to the market" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3937}. He went to the market of Banu Qaynuqa' and worked until he prospered, and the Prophet saw him days later, already married with a bridal gift of a date-stone's weight of gold {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3937}.
