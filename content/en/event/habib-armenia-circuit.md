## Overview
Al-Baladhuri, in "Futuh al-Buldan", relates the circuit of [[person/habib-ibn-maslama|Habib ibn Maslama]] through [[place/armenia|Armenia]], which is part of the [[event/conquest-of-qaliqala|conquest of Qaliqala]]. He dates it to about 25 AH in the caliphate of Uthman, an estimate that is derived. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The agreements
Habib came to Khilat, and its patrician brought him money and a gift, which he did not accept. He made peace with the lord of the tolls over his lands, and with patricians and people of districts for a tribute or an annual tax. Al-Baladhuri says that the people of Dabil fortified their city; he set catapults against them until they asked for safe conduct. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The text of the treaty for the people of Dabil is given. It secured their persons, property, churches, places of worship and city wall, in return for the poll tax and the land tax. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Dhat al-Lujum
Al-Baladhuri, from elders of Dabil, relates the name "Dhat al-Lujum" (possessor of bridles): the Muslims let their animals loose without bridles, and a group of the non-Arabs attacked them and took the bridles. Then the Muslims turned on them and recovered what had been taken. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

A messenger from the patrician of Jurzan came to him, asking for peace on behalf of its people, and he wrote them a letter of safe conduct on condition. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
