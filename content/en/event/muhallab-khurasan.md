## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates in "Futuh al-Buldan" that al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf was given the governorship of Khurasan along with the two Iraqs, and he appointed [[person/al-muhallab-ibn-abi-sufra|al-Muhallab ibn Abi Sufra]] over it in 99 AH. Al-Muhallab's name was Zalim ibn Sarraq, of the Azd, and his kunya was Abu Sa'id. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His campaigns
He undertook many campaigns, and conquered al-Khutal, which had rebelled, and conquered Khujanda. The Soghdians paid him tribute, and he raided Kush and Nasaf. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## His death
He returned and died at Zaghul in Marw al-Rudh at al-Shawsha. The beginning of his illness was grief for his son [[person/al-mughira-ibn-al-muhallab|al-Mughira ibn al-Muhallab]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
