## Summary
Shamla, one of the Turkish amirs known as the Khazraj, seized the land of Fars and ruled its people. They then united against him, drove him out and recovered their land. At that time Fars was held in turn by the Salghurids, after the Buyids had died out. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 19}
