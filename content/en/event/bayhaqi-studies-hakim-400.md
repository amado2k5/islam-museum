## Summary
Al-Bayhaqi heard hadith from al-Hakim Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Nisaburi, author of the Mustadrak 'ala al-Sahihayn — a work he used extensively in his own compilations — and from other Nishapuri shaykhs, about a hundred in all, before al-Hakim's death in 405/1014 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1190}.
