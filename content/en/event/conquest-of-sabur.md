## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]] came to Sabur in 23 AH, and it is also said 24 AH, and found its people in awe of the Muslims. The brother of Shahrak saw in a dream a man of the Arabs stripping him of his shirt, so he asked for safe-conduct and peace. Uthman made peace with him on condition that no one be killed or taken captive, that he have a dhimma, and that he pay money in advance. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The breach and the conquest
Then the people of Sabur broke the covenant, and [[person/abu-musa-al-ashari|Abu Musa]] took it by force in 26 AH, with Uthman ibn Abi al-As in his vanguard. The city is [[place/sabur|Sabur]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
