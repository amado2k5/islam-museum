## Overview
Al-Maqqari relates that in 586 AH it reached Ya'qub al-Mansur al-Muwahhidi that the Franks had taken [[place/silves|Silves]]. He went to it himself, besieged it and took it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 384}

## The recovery
Al-Mansur at once sent an army of the Almohads and Arabs, which conquered four cities that the Franks had taken from the Muslims forty years before. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 384}

## The truce
The lord of Toledo feared him and asked for a truce, and he made a five-year truce with him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 384}

The commander is [[person/yaqub-al-mansur-al-muwahhidi|Ya'qub al-Mansur]]. The date is converted by derivation.
