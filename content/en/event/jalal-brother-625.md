## Summary
A dispute arose between Jalal al-Din ibn Khwarazm-shah and his brother Ghiyath al-Din, who was staying with the Ismailis. Many of Jalal al-Din's soldiers dispersed and went over to his brother. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 476}
