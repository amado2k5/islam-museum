## Overview
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] says that [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] had gone to Yemen in 280 AH as far as a place called [[place/al-sharfa-yemen|al-Sharfa]], near Sana'a; the people submitted and obeyed him, he stayed among them a short while, and then they abandoned him and went back to what displeases God; he found no supporters among them and went back until he reached his own country in the Hijaz. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36}

## What followed, as the book tells it
The narrator says that tribulation then overtook the people of Yemen, with strife and exile, after their failure and obstinacy toward the truth and its people, and that they wrote to al-Hadi asking him to come to them and telling him of their repentance. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36} The verdict on the people of Yemen is the narrator's own. A marginal note in the manuscript calls this the account of al-Hadi's first journey to Yemen. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.36} See [[event/al-hadi-letters-to-medina-283]].
