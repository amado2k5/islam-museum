## Summary
The Prophet left Ali in charge of his family and ordered him to stay with them. The hypocrites spread the rumour that he had found Ali a burden, so Ali took up his weapons and caught up with the Prophet at al-Jurf. The Prophet told him he had left him behind for what he had left behind him, and asked: are you not content to be to me as Aaron was to Moses, except that there is no prophet after me? Ali returned to Medina. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/163}

## The account
The Prophet left Ali ibn Abi Talib in charge of his family and ordered him to stay with them; the hypocrites spread their rumour about him, saying he had left him behind only because he found him a burden and wanted relief from him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/163}

So Ali took up his weapons and went out until he reached the Prophet, who was halting at al-Jurf, and told him that the hypocrites claimed he had found him a burden and wanted relief from him. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/163}

The Prophet said: they lied. I left you behind only for what I left behind me — go back and be my deputy over my family and yours. "Are you not content, Ali, to be to me in the position of Aaron to Moses, except that there is no prophet after me?" Ali returned to Medina and the Prophet went on his journey. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/163}

Ibn Ishaq relates, from Muhammad ibn Talha ibn Yazid ibn Rukana, from Ibrahim ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, from his father Sa'd, that he heard the Prophet say these words to Ali. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/163}

:::perspectives
- tradition: sunni
  summary: Ibn Ishaq relates it as an answer to the hypocrites' rumour that the Prophet had found Ali a burden; Sunni commentators therefore usually read the saying as deputising Ali over the household for the duration of the campaign, likened to Aaron deputising for Moses in his absence — not a declaration of succession after the Prophet; this summary needs a second witness from hadith commentaries (needs_source).
  sources: [source/ibn-hisham-saqqa]
- tradition: academic
  summary: Researchers note that the report is transmitted in Sunni and Shi'i sources alike and that the disagreement is over its significance, not its occurrence; the summary needs a specific academic reference (needs_source).
  sources: [source/ibn-hisham-saqqa]
- tradition: shia-twelver
  summary: The Imami (Twelver) view sees in the phrase 'in the position of Aaron to Moses' proof that Ali holds Aaron's position to Moses in the succession after the Prophet; the summary needs a specific Shi'i source (needs_source).
  sources: [source/ibn-hisham-saqqa]
:::

## Markers of weakness and doubt
- The "position of Aaron" hadith admits multiple interpretations; the museum records Ibn Ishaq's account with its chain, then the three views in order — Sunni, academic, Twelver Shia — and does not adjudicate.
- Ibn Ishaq's chain: Muhammad ibn Talha ibn Yazid ibn Rukana from Ibrahim ibn Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas from his father Sa'd (p. 163).
