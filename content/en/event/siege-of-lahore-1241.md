## Overview
Under Iltutmish's weak successors the governors of Lahore were frequently in rebellion, and Kabir Khan Ayaz was virtually independent there when the Mongols took and sacked the city in 639/1241; they did not follow up their victory but abandoned it immediately {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5.2 p. 37}.
