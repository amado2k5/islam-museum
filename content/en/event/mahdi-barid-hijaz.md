## Overview
In 166 AH [[person/al-mahdi|al-Mahdi]] moved to the Palace of Peace and ordered the postal service set up from the Prophet's city, from Yemen and from Mecca to the capital, with mules and camels. Al-Suyuti, quoting al-Dhahabi, says that this was the first postal service made from the Hijaz to Iraq. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.442}

The conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived and approximate. The place in the event data is [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]].
