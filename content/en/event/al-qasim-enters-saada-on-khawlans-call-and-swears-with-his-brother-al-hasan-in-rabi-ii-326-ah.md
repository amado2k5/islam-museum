## Overview
Muzaffar ibn Alyan advises al-Qasim to go to Saada. He sets out with a group from Bakil, reaches Asal in Khawlan, enters the town, and makes peace with his brother al-Hasan, each swearing that their hands are one in the right.

## Detail
The book says many in Khawlan invited al-Qasim, and Muzaffar advised him to go to Saada. He set out with a group from Bakil, horsemen and foot soldiers, and reached Asal in Khawlan. His brother al-Hasan did not like his coming into the town, and Khawlan did not answer him on that, so they admitted al-Qasim to the town, and he went on to al-Ghayl, their camp. In Rabi II of the same year the two brothers made peace, and each swore to the other that their hands were one in the right, and that if either broke it, the hands of the group would be against him.
