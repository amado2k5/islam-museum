## Overview
[[person/ibn-hazm|Ibn Hazm]] died in the desert, in his village of Mant Lisham in the district of [[place/labla|Labla]], on the evening of a Sunday two nights before the end of Sha'ban, 456 AH, after rulers had driven him from his homeland. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 80-81}

## Legacy
His works number some four hundred volumes by his son's account, among them Tawq al-Hamama and al-Fisal fi al-Milal wa al-Nihal. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 80-81}
