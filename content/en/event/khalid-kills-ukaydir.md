## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Ukaydir withheld the alms and broke his covenant after the death of the Prophet. He left [[place/dumat-al-jandal|Dumat al-Jandal]] and joined al-Hira. [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] sent [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid]] against him; Khalid killed [[person/ukaydir-ibn-abd-al-malik|Ukaydir]] and conquered Dumat. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.83-84}

## Background
Ukaydir had built in al-Hira a building he called Duma, after Dumat al-Jandal. His brother Hurayth ibn Abd al-Malik embraced Islam on what was in his hands, and it was handed to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.83-84}

## Al-Waqidi's account
In al-Waqidi's account, Khalid passed by Dumat al-Jandal on his way from Iraq to Syria, took it and captured captives, among them Layla bint al-Jawdi al-Ghassani, whom Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr afterwards married. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.83-84}

The date is about 12 AH, derived from the order of events in the account.
