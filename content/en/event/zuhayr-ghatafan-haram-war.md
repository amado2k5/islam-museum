## Overview
This is a war from the days of the Arabs before Islam, related by Ibn al-Athir from [[person/zuhayr-ibn-janab-al-kalbi|Zuhayr ibn Janab al-Kalbi]] and [[group/ghatafan|Ghatafan]]. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.378}

## The cause
After the Banu Bughid ibn Rayth ibn Ghatafan became mighty and grew rich by their killing of Sada', a tribe of Madhhij, they said: "We will take a sanctuary like Mecca, whose game is not killed and whose refugee is not harried." They built a sanctuary, and the Banu Murra ibn Awf were in charge of it. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.378}

## The raid
When this reached Zuhayr, who was called the soothsayer for the soundness of his judgement, he said that he would not leave Ghatafan to take a sanctuary while he was alive. He raided them, fought them very fiercely and defeated them. He took a horseman of theirs in their sanctuary and killed him, and put an end to that sanctuary. Then he granted Ghatafan favour, returned the women and took the property. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.378}
