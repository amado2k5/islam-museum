## Summary
Ibn Kathir took knowledge from Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyya, became filled with love for him and was tried on his account {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 1/374}.
## Context
His teacher's final trials — the divorce proceedings and the citadel imprisonment of 726 — reached his students and lasted until Ibn Taymiyya's death at Damascus in 728.
