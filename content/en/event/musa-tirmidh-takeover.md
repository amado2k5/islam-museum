## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/musa-ibn-abdallah-ibn-khazim|Musa ibn Abd Allah ibn Khazim al-Sulami]] came to Samarqand, where its king [[person/tarkhun|Tarkhun]] honoured him. Then Tarkhun expelled him when one of his companions killed a Soghdian man. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Tirmidh
Musa went to the lord of Kush, then came to [[place/tirmidh|Tirmidh]], which was a fortress. He stayed with its landowner, who prepared food for him; when he had eaten he lay down. The landowner told him to leave, and he answered in words to the effect that he knew no house like this, and he fought its people. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
