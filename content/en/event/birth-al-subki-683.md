## Overview
Ali ibn Abd al-Kafi ibn Ali ibn Tamam — Taqi al-Din Abu al-Hasan al-Subki al-Ansari al-Khazraji al-Shafi'i — was born in Safar 683/April 1284 in Egypt; his family traced its origin to the town of Subk in the Manf district of the western Nile Delta {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 pp. 744-745}.
