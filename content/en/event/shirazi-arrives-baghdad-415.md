## Summary
In 415/1024 Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi was in Baghdad, attending the classes of the jurist al-Qazwini (d. 440/1048), a proponent of the legal thinking developed by al-Baqillani, and — more significantly — becoming the leading disciple and accredited assistant (mu'id) of the qadi Abu al-Tayyib al-Tabari (d. 450/1058) {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 494}.
