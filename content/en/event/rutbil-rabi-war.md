## Overview
In al-Baladhuri's account, Kabul Shah gathered forces against the Muslims and drove out those who were among them in Kabul. [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] came and prevailed over Zabulistan and Rukhkh until he reached Bust. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The fighting
[[person/al-rabi-ibn-ziyad-al-harithi|al-Rabi ibn Ziyad]] went out with the people and fought Rutbil at Bust and defeated him. He followed him as far as Rukhkh and fought him there, then went on and took the lands of al-Dawar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The data names [[person/kabul-shah|Kabul Shah]] as the one who drove the Muslims out of Kabul. The history of this war is not recorded in the data and needs an additional source.
