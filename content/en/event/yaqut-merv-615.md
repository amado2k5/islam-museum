## Summary
Yaqut reached Marw and stayed three years among its famous libraries {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}. There, in 615/1218-19, the idea of compiling the Mujam al-Buldan came to him during a course of his teacher Abu al-Muzaffar Abd al-Rahim ibn al-Sam'ani, who let him into his family's two rich libraries {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 265}.

## Ten waqf repositories
In the Mujam he wrote: 'Had not the Tatars arrived and ruined those lands I would never have left it for all my life, for the kindness, gentleness and good company of its people and the wealth of reliable source books — I left it while it held ten waqf repositories whose like in quantity and quality I never saw in the world' {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 278}. Among them were the libraries of the al-Sam'ani family, Sharaf al-Malik al-Mustawfi, Nizam al-Malik Muhammad b. Ishaq, the vizier Majd al-Malik, and the Amidiyya, Aziziyya, Khatuniyya, Kamaliyya and Damriyya foundations {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 11 p. 264}.
