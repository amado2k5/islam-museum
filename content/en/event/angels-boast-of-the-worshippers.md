## The story
Ibn Majah relates marfu' that the Prophet told that God said: receive glad tidings — your Lord has opened a door of heaven and boasts of you to the angels, saying: look at My servants who have performed an obligation and are awaiting another {cite:sunan-ibn-majah} [[passage/ibnmajah-0801|Ibn Majah 801]].

## The meaning
In this hadith qudsi God boasts of His praying servants before the angels — the prayer reaches the highest angels as testimony for the servant {cite:sunan-ibn-majah}.
