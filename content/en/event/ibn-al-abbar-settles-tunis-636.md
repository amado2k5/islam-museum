## Overview
Ibn al-Abbar returned to Valencia but left it a few days after it was captured by James I on 17 Safar 636 (28 September 1238); he stopped at Bougie before going on to Tunis, where Abu Zakariyya' received him favourably and appointed him chief of his chancellery {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 705}.
