## Summary
During [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|the Treaty of al-Hudaybiya]], some enslaved people came out from Mecca to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], and their masters wrote asking for their return, but the Prophet refused to return them {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.100}.

## The Prophet's Statement
The Prophet said to Quraysh, "You shall surely desist, or God will send against you one who will strike your necks with the sword for the religion, whose heart God has tested for faith" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.100}. The Companions asked who it was, each of them hoping it would be said of him, and he said, "It is the mender of the sandal," having given Ali his sandal to mend {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.100}.
