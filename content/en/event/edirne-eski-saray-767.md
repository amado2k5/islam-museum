## Overview
Murad I built a new palace outside the walls of Edirne's citadel and moved into it in 767/1365-6, having found the tekfür's castle palace inadequate {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## The Old Palace
Evliya Çelebi places the Old Palace near the Selim mosque in the Kavak Meydanı quarter, ringed by high walls some 5,000 paces in circumference, with a gate called the Bab-ı Hümayun {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.
