## Overview
[[person/abd-allah-ibn-amir|Ibn Amir]] appointed [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-samura|Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura]] over Sistan, and he advanced into its land as far as [[place/jabal-al-zur|Mount al-Zur]], according to al-Baladhuri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The conquests
He came to Zaranj and besieged its governor in his palace on one of their feast days, and he made peace with him for two million dirhams and two thousand slaves. He prevailed over the land between Zaranj and Kish on the side of India. He took the road of al-Rakhaj to the land of the Dawar, besieged them on the mountain of al-Zur, and then made peace with them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The idol of al-Zur
The number of the Muslims was eight thousand, and each man received four thousand. He entered upon al-Zur, an idol of gold with two rubies for eyes, cut off its hand and took the two rubies. Then he said to the governor: "Take the gold and the jewels; I only wanted to show you that it neither harms nor benefits." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

He also took Bust and Zabul by treaty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
