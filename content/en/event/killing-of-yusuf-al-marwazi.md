## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Mutawakkil appointed [[person/yusuf-ibn-muhammad-al-marwazi|Yusuf ibn Muhammad al-Marwazi]] over [[place/armenia|Armenia]] for two years of his caliphate. When he reached [[place/khilat|Khilat]] he seized its patrician Baqrat ibn Ashut and took him to Samarra, and the patricians and the free men recoiled from him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Background
Al-Baladhuri says that al-Mu'tasim appointed al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Baghisi, known as al-Ma'muni, over the frontier. He was lenient with the patricians of Armenia and its free men, until they grew more opposed to the authority. Ishaq ibn Isma'il ibn Shu'ayb prevailed over Jarzan, and Sahl ibn Sanbat the patrician attacked the agent of Haydar ibn Kawus al-Afshin and killed his secretary. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The cause of the uprising
An agent of Yusuf named al-Mala' ibn Ahmad went to a monastery in Sisjan known as the Monastery of the Cups, which the Christians of Armenia revered and brought gifts to. He took what was in it and mistreated its people. The patricians were greatly angered, corresponded with one another and urged each other to rebel, and secretly approached the Khuwaythiyya, known as al-Azṭan, to rise against Yusuf for taking their patrician. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The killing
They attacked him at Batrun while he had scattered his companions among the villages, and they killed him and seized what was in his camp. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Note: the date is derived from the phrase "two years of the caliphate of al-Mutawakkil", and it needs an additional source.
