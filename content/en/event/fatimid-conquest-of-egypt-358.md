## Overview
The caliph [[person/al-muizz-li-din-allah|al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah]] sent his commander [[person/jawhar-al-siqilli|Jawhar]] to Egypt, who took it and laid out the city of Cairo, in Sha'ban 358 AH. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.620-622}

## The cause
When [[person/kafur-al-ikhshidi|Kafur al-Ikhshidi]] died, order collapsed and money for the troops grew scarce. A group wrote to al-Mu'izz asking him to send an army to take over Egypt. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.620-622}

## The conquest and the building
Jawhar stopped at the site of Cairo and laid it out, and built the residence of the amir for al-Mu'izz, which is known as the Two Palaces. He cut off the Friday sermon for the Abbasids. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.620-622}

Al-Mu'izz entered Egypt in Ramadan 362 AH with the coffins of his forefathers. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.620-622}

## Ibn al-Athir's account
According to Ibn al-Athir, al-Mu'izz sent Jawhar with a dense army to the lands of Egypt, and he took them. {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.444}
