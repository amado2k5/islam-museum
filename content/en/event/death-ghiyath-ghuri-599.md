## Summary
Ghiyath al-Din Muhammad ibn Sam, ruler of Ghur, Ghazna, Khurasan and India, died. He was among the most just of kings and the soundest in judgement, so much so that it was said no one like him had been seen on a throne. After him his brother Shihab al-Din Muhammad ruled independently {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p.281}.
