## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that al-Mansur appointed [[person/man-ibn-zaida|Ma'n ibn Za'ida]] over Sistan. Ma'n wrote to [[person/rutbil|Rutbil]] ordering him to pay the tribute he had agreed to pay al-Hajjaj. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The campaign
Rutbil sent camels, Turkish tents and slaves, and inflated their value, so Ma'n became angry and marched to al-Rukhkh, with [[person/yazid-ibn-mazyad|Yazid ibn Mazyad]] at the head of his vanguard. He found that Rutbil had gone out to [[place/zabulistan|Zabulistan]], so he took it, took many captives and captured about thirty thousand. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Mawand
[[person/mawand|Mawand]], Rutbil's deputy, asked for safe conduct on condition that he would take him to the commander of the faithful. Ma'n granted it and sent him to Baghdad with five thousand of their fighting men. Al-Mansur honoured him and granted him a stipend. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
