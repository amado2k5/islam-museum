## Summary
Scattered notes in his own work show that before 698/1298 al-Nuwayri spent some time in Upper Egypt and in Egypt generally: in the agriculture chapters he speaks from personal experience of districts visited and practices observed, and in the animal section he mentions the elephant tusks he saw at Qus in 697/1298 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 p. 156}.
