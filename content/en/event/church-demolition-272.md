## Overview
In Rabi' II 272 the Baghdad crowd gathered and demolished what had been rebuilt of the church whose ruin had been mentioned the year before, and carried off great wealth from it — for they objected to their riding of beasts {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi p. 84-85}.
