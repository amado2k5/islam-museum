## Overview
In 60 AH the people of [[place/kufa|Kufa]] sent their messengers to [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|Husayn ibn Ali]] while he was in Mecca, inviting him to come to them. He sent them his cousin [[person/muslim-ibn-aqil|Muslim ibn Aqil]]. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.5 p.347}

What is not covered here: the texts of the letters and the number of messengers, which need an additional source.
