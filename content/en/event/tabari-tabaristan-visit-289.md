## Overview
During his half-century in Baghdad al-Tabari left only twice, both times for his native Tabaristan; the second journey fell in 289-90/902-3 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 21}.

## Al-Shammasiyya
By 290/903 he was installed in the al-Shammasiyya quarter on the eastern side of Baghdad, where he lived until his death — and where his troubles with the Hanbalis played out {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 22}.
