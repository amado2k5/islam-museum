## Overview
[[person/qutayba-ibn-muslim|Qutayba ibn Muslim]] raided Baykand in 87 AH, with [[person/nizak|Nizak]] with him, then took Bukhara, and killed Nizak in Tukharistan. Al-Baladhuri relates this, and al-Salabi adds that the fighting lasted so long that al-Hajjaj wrote to the provinces to pray for the army. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.90-91}

## Baykand
Al-Baladhuri says that Qutayba crossed the river from Zam to [[place/baykand|Baykand]], the nearest of the cities of Bukhara to the river. Its people betrayed him and called on the Soghdians for help; he fought them and besieged them, then they asked for peace, and he took it by force. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Tumshkat, Karminiya and Bukhara
Qutayba raided Tumshkat and Karminiya in 88 AH, and left his brother [[person/bashshar-ibn-muslim|Bashshar ibn Muslim]] as his deputy over Merv. Then he raided Bukhara and took it by peace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Baladhuri quotes Abu Ubayda Ma'mar ibn al-Muthanna that the people of Bukhara were wary of him, so he asked to enter it and pray two rak'as, and they let him in. He had hidden a group who entered and overwhelmed the people at the gate, and he took great booty. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Nizak
The source says that he struck the Soghdians, killed Nizak in Tukharistan and crucified him, and took Kush and Nasaf by peace. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Salabi says that Bukhara was taken three years after the regular raiding began. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.90-91}
