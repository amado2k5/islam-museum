## Overview
In 253 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutazz|al-Mu'tazz billah]] sent his freedman [[person/musa-ibn-bugha|Musa ibn Bugha al-Kabir]] against the Talibiyyun who had appeared among the [[place/daylam|Daylam]] and the region of Tabaristan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The campaign
The Daylam had gathered round a man of theirs known as [[person/al-kawki|al-Kawki]]. Musa ibn Bugha raided the Daylam and went deep into their land; they fought him and he defeated them. The source says that his weight fell heavily on them and his hard blows were strong. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date comes from the source's phrase "and this was the year 253". The data gives no detail of the outcome of the campaign afterwards, and this needs an additional source.
