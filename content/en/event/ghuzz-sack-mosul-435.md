## Summary
In the year 435 the Ghuzz entered [[place/mosul|Mosul]]. They seized the household of [[person/qarwash-ibn-al-muqallad|Qarwash ibn al-Muqallad]], its amir, did damage there, and plundered more than two hundred thousand dinars from his residence. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.459,489}

## The aftermath
Abu al-Barakat, stepson of Abu Ja'far al-Samnani, reached the caliph seeking support against them, and the sharif Abu al-Hasan ibn Ja'far al-Nassaba arrived fleeing. Qarwash and [[person/dubays|Dubays ibn Ali ibn Mazyad]] joined together to strike at the Ghuzz, and killed a great number of them. In Baghdad, the Friday sermon was delivered for the king Abu Kalijar. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.459}
