## Summary
Six men of [[group/thaqif|Thaqif]] under [[person/abd-yalil-ibn-amr|Abd Yalil ibn Amr]] came to Medina in Ramadan 9 AH; they became Muslim on condition that the Prophet undertake the demolition of al-Lat, and he set [[person/uthman-ibn-abi-al-as|Uthman ibn Abi al-As]] over them {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 448}.

## The delegation's account
[[person/urwa-ibn-masud|Urwa ibn Mas'ud]], chief of Thaqif, became Muslim after the Prophet's return from al-Ta'if and went back calling his people; they shot him with arrows until they killed him {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 448}. Thaqif lasted months after his death, then saw they had no strength to fight the Arabs around them and agreed to send a delegation; Abd Yalil refused unless they sent men with him, so they became six, among them Uthman ibn Abi al-As, the youngest {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 448}. A tent was pitched for them at the side of the mosque so they could hear the Qur'an and see the people at prayer {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 448}. They asked the Prophet to write them a peace permitting adultery, wine and usury, keeping al-Lat, exemption from prayer, and that they would not break their idols with their own hands — he refused it all {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 448; ibn-hisham-sira}. Finding no way out, they submitted and became Muslim, stipulating that the Prophet undertake the demolition of al-Lat; he accepted, wrote them a document and set Uthman ibn Abi al-As over them for his eagerness to learn {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 448}. On returning the delegation hid the truth from their people and showed gloom; Thaqif took pride for some days, then God cast terror into their hearts and they asked for the terms that had been agreed — and Thaqif became Muslim {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p. 449}.

## For young visitors
Six men from the people of al-Ta'if came asking the Prophet for hard conditions; he accepted none of them, so they became Muslim upon the truth, and when they went back their tribe became Muslim after them.
