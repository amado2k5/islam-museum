## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that the people of Armenia rebelled in the governorship of [[person/al-hasan-ibn-qahtaba|al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba al-Ta'i]], and their leader was [[person/mushayil-al-armani|Mushayil the Armenian]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.295}

## The event
This came after the removal of Ibn Usayd and Bakkar ibn Muslim al-Uqayli. [[person/al-mansur|Al-Mansur]] sent reinforcements under Amir ibn Isma'il. Al-Hasan met Mushayil in battle, and he was killed and his gatherings scattered, and affairs were set in order for al-Hasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.295}

Al-Baladhuri says that the Nahr al-Hasan at al-Baylaqan, the Bagh al-Hasan at Barda'a, and the estates known as al-Hasaniyya were named after al-Hasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.295}

The date falls in the caliphate of al-Mansur; it is a derived estimate and needs an additional source.
