## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/muhammad-ibn-al-qasim|Muhammad ibn al-Qasim]] devised a way to cross the Mihran by a bridge he built, while Dahir was distracted from him. [[person/dahir|Dahir]] met him on an elephant with elephants around him, and they fought very fiercely. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The death of Dahir
Dahir dismounted and fought, and he was killed toward evening, and his companions were routed. In the report of al-Mada'ini, the one who killed him was a man of Banu Kilab. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The place is [[place/mihran|the Mihran]]. The date of the battle is not given on the page, and it needs an additional source.
