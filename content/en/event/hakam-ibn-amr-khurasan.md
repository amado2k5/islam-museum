## Overview
Ziyad appointed [[person/al-hakam-ibn-amr|al-Hakam ibn Amr al-Ghifari]] over Khurasan. The source describes him as chaste and as having been a Companion. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## How the appointment happened
Ziyad said to his chamberlain Fil: "Bring me al-Hakam", meaning al-Hakam ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi. The chamberlain brought him al-Hakam ibn Amr. When Ziyad saw him he sought his blessing and said: "A righteous man among the Companions of the Messenger of God", and appointed him over Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Conclusion
Al-Hakam died in Khurasan in 50 AH, and was the first to pray beyond the river. The source relates that Abd Allah ibn al-Mubarak asked a man from Saghaniyan: "Do you know who conquered your land?" He said: "No." He said: "It was conquered by al-Hakam ibn Amr al-Ghifari." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
