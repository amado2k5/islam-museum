## Overview
[[person/ali-ibn-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Ali ibn Muhammad]] has it from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman]], who is Abu Jafar, that he stayed in the mosque praying after Abu al-Qasim left, "and there was a party of Banu Rabi'a and of Hajur talking and whispering about those who fight al-Hadi ila al-Haqq". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} The whole passage is the narrator's reports, attributed to people the book does not know by name, and the margin of the original carries the heading "karamat of al-Hadi"; {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} the museum gives them as attributed and neither confirms nor denies them. They follow the address of [[event/abu-al-qasim-leads-the-friday-prayer-and-preaches-jihad-at-khiwan-286]].

## The report of Shu'ayb
Muhammad ibn Sulayman heard one of them say: "Some days ago we were in the district of Athafit; Shu'ayb of the people of al-Subay' told us, when we had spoken of al-Hadi", and this man of al-Subay' was "of Arhab, of the clan of al-Da'am, and one who had supported him in the war against the imam", who had been in doubt about al-Hadi "because they had not met him nor sat with him nor heard his words nor learned from them". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} The man then related from Shu'ayb, [[person/shuayb-al-subayi|Shu'ayb al-Subay'i]], that he fell "very ill in my throat so that I could not swallow any of my spittle, and I was close to death"; when a letter from al-Hadi came to the people of al-Subay' "I took the seal that sealed the letter and said: O God, if the owner of this is in the right, give me health, and I ate the seal; it was not long before my throat opened and I ate and drank". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} See also [[group/al-subay-arhab|the people of al-Subay']].

## The report of Ibrahim ibn Sulayman
A man of [[group/hajur-tribe-yemen|Hajur]], "with no fault in his religion and school", called [[person/ibrahim-ibn-sulayman-al-hajuri|Ibrahim ibn Sulayman]], said: "How many signs have we seen since al-Hadi came! I myself have seen the like of this", and told of a man among them whose bowels "ran night and day"; he sought treatment without benefit, and when al-Hadi's letter came he took the seal that was on it, went to the man and said: "Eat this, I hope it helps you", "and the man took it and ate it, and God gave him health, and what he used to find left him". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.139} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.140}

## Notes
Both reports are of eating the seal of a letter in the hope of cure, and belong to the accounts of marvels in this Zaydi book; the museum offers no judgement on them or on the act. The first speaker is not named. The book says Shu'ayb and his companions had before this been among the imam's opponents and then bore witness to what they saw, which is the narrator's description. The date is at month precision because the account is told after the Friday following the middle of Sha'ban, and the time of the two cures themselves is not known.
