## Overview
A battle between [[person/tariq-ibn-ziyad|Tariq ibn Ziyad]] and Roderic, king of the Goths, on the river Lakka in the district of Shadhuna, in the last nights of Ramadan 92 AH. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.69}

## The battle
The book says that the war lasted eight days, in which three thousand Muslims were martyred. Then the Goths were routed, and it is said that Roderic drowned. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.69}

Tariq chose the field of battle with care: he anchored his right wing to the lake, his left wing to the valley, and placed his rear in the mountains of Ratina. {cite:salabi-umayyad-state-2020 p.69}

The Gregorian date is not given in the book.
