## The story
Al-Muwatta relates from Anas ibn Malik that the Messenger of God said: the good dream of a righteous man is a forty-sixth part of prophethood. From Ata ibn Yasar that the Messenger of God said: nothing remains of prophethood after me but the glad tidings. They asked: what are the glad tidings, Messenger of God? He said: the good dream which a righteous man sees or which is seen for him, a forty-sixth part of prophethood. From Abu Hurayra that the Messenger of God, when he finished the dawn prayer, would say: did any of you see a dream last night? And he would say: nothing remains of prophethood after me but the good dream {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997} [[passage/muwatta-31-2746|Muwatta 2746]] [[passage/muwatta-31-2749|Muwatta 2749]] [[passage/muwatta-31-2748|Muwatta 2748]].

## The meaning
These texts belong to the chapters of dreams in the Muwatta; they hold that the good dream is a remnant of prophethood after revelation ceased, which is why it was counted among the glad tidings {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997}.
