## Overview
[[person/abu-jafar-muhammad-ibn-ubaydallah-al-abbasi|Muhammad ibn Ubaydallah]] says he saw two companions come to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]], whom the text calls "Hasan ibn Ali ibn Qutayma" and "Ubaydallah ibn Hudhayf" (both readings uncertain); they said that if he took a tenth or half-tenth only from thirty-three and a third farq of grain nothing would ever be gathered. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.61} He answered: "Nothing will ever be gathered from this; by God if this and this", meaning heaven and earth, "met upon me until my ribs were wrenched apart, I would take nothing but the due." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.61} Another companion asked: there are people here who freely give more than is due, may we take from them what would set us right? He said: "By God, I will not set you right by the corruption of myself." {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.61} These are the imam's words as his narrator gives them in a Zaydi book. See [[concept/ushr]] and [[concept/nisab]].
