## Summary
Ibn al-Imad, in *Shadharat al-Dhahab*, records that in 913 AH the Franks overcame "the fortress of Hormuz" and took it. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 476}

The account in the source is limited to this sentence. The details of the fighting, its participants and its exact date need an additional source.
