## Summary

On the second day of Shawwal in the year 970 AH there was a great and terrible flood in Hadramawt, the like of which had not been heard of. It destroyed much of that region and ruined many palm trees. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 357}.

## How it is remembered by its people

The people of the region remember it and date events by it; they call it the flood of the crown (sayl al-iklil). The jurist Abd Allah ibn Ahmad ibn Fallah al-Hadrami included its date in a verse, saying: a flood in the valley of Hadramawt harmed ... in the sleep of the crown of stars you slept. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 357}.
