## Overview
The fire of 1158/1745 burnt down some sixty quarters of Edirne and hastened the city's decline {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 p. 711}.

## Fire and earthquake
It was followed by the earthquake of 1164/1751; the Selimiye itself was repaired after the 1752 earthquake and again in 1808 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 2 pp. 711-712}.
