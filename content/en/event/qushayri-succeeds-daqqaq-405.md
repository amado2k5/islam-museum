## Summary
Abu Ali al-Daqqaq married al-Qushayri to his daughter Fatima, and when al-Daqqaq died in 405/1015 his son-in-law succeeded him leading the remembrance sessions in the madrasa he had built in 391/1001 — known from then on as the Kushayriyya; by then al-Qushayri had taken law from Abu Bakr al-Tusi at Tus, kalam and speculation from Ibn Furak, then attended Abu Ishaq al-Isfarayini and studied Ibn al-Baqillani's works, and after al-Daqqaq he kept company with Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 5 p. 564} {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi pp. 171-172}.
