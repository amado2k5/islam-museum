## Summary
The books of hadith and history report that [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] came from al-Sunh after [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] died and went in to his body, then went out to the people, among whom [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] had denied the death, and gave a short address in which he recited two verses of the Qur'an, and the people grew calm. The reports differ in the wording of the address and in some details, as they do on the day and hour of the death. The account of the death from Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Hisham is in [[event/death-of-the-prophet|The death of the Prophet]], Umar's denial in [[event/umar-denial-of-the-prophets-death|Umar's denial of the Prophet's death]], and what followed the address in [[event/saqifa|The Saqifa]]. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 337}

## What is reported of his last moments
[[person/aisha|Aisha]] says in the two Sahihs that she heard the Prophet before he died, leaning on her chest, say: "O God, forgive me, have mercy on me and join me to the Highest Companion." {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 266} In Ahmad she reports that he used to say: "No prophet but his soul is taken, then he sees the reward, then it is returned to him and he is given the choice of its being returned or his joining"; when she looked at him she said: then by God he will not choose us, and he said: "With the Highest Companion in Paradise." {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 266} In the report of Hammad ibn Zayd from Ibn Abi Mulayka she went to recite protective words over him and he raised his eyes to the sky and said: "In the Highest Companion"; Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr came in with a fresh palm stick, he used it as a toothstick better than he ever had, then he went to hand it to her and it fell from his hand. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 264} She also says "he died between my lung and my chest", and that she never found a sweeter scent than his when his soul left. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 266} [[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama]] reports that she put her hand on his chest on the day he died, and weeks passed in which she ate and made ablution and the scent of musk did not leave her hand. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 266} These reports are connected with the toothstick and the choice in [[event/death-of-the-prophet|The death of the Prophet]].

## The report of al-Bukhari from Aisha
Aisha reports in al-Bukhari that the Messenger of God died while Abu Bakr was at al-Sunh, meaning al-Aliya in Isma'il's explanation, and that Umar stood and said: "By God, the Messenger of God has not died, and God will raise him and he will cut off the hands and feet of men." {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 337} Abu Bakr came, uncovered the Messenger of God, kissed him and said: "By my father and mother, you were good living and dead; by Him in whose hand is my soul, God will never make you taste death twice." Then he went out and said: "You who swear, gently." And when Abu Bakr spoke, Umar sat down. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 337} Abu Bakr praised God and said: "Whoever worshipped Muhammad, Muhammad has died; and whoever worshipped God, God is living and does not die", then recited [[passage/quran-39-30|al-Zumar 39:30]] and [[passage/quran-3-144|Al Imran 3:144]]. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 337} The narrator says the people sobbed weeping, and the Ansar gathered around Sa'd ibn Ubada in the hall of Banu Sa'ida. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 337}

## The report of al-Zuhri from Abu Salama and Ibn Abbas
Ibn Kathir quotes al-Bayhaqi, by al-Zuhri from Abu Salama, that Aisha told him Abu Bakr came on a horse from al-Sunh, dismounted and entered the mosque, spoke to no one until he went in to Aisha, and made for the Messenger of God, who was covered in a striped Yemeni cloak; he uncovered his face, bent over and kissed him and wept, and said: "By my father and mother, O Messenger of God, God will never join two deaths on you; the death written for you you have died." {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268} Then, from Abu Salama from Ibn Abbas: Abu Bakr went out while Umar was speaking to the people and said "Sit down, Umar", and Umar refused; he said it again and he refused; then Abu Bakr made the testimony and the people came to him, and he said: "As for what follows: whoever of you worshipped Muhammad, Muhammad has died; and whoever worshipped God, God is living and does not die", and recited the verse [[passage/quran-3-144|Al Imran 3:144]]. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268} The report adds that it was as if the people had not known God had sent down this verse until Abu Bakr recited it, and all took it from him, so that every man one heard was reciting it. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268} Al-Zuhri reports from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib that Umar said that as soon as he heard Abu Bakr recite it he knew it was the truth, his legs gave way, he fell to the ground, and he knew that the Messenger of God had died. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268}

## The report of Ahmad from Aisha
In Ahmad's report from Aisha, which Ibn Kathir gives, Abu Bakr on entering kissed the Prophet's forehead three times and said: "Alas my Prophet, alas my chosen, alas my friend", then went out to the mosque where Umar was speaking, spoke, praised God and recited the verse; Umar said: "Is it in the Book of God? I did not know it was in the Book of God"; then he told them: "This is Abu Bakr, so pledge allegiance to him", and they did. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 267}{cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268}

## Urwa's report: a longer address
Ibn Kathir quotes al-Bayhaqi, through Ibn Lahi'a from Abu al-Aswad from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, that Umar stood addressing the people and threatening whoever said the Prophet had died, while Amr ibn Qays ibn Za'ida, Ibn Umm Maktum, at the back of the mosque was reciting the verse of Al Imran, and the people were weeping and surging, hearing nothing. [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|Al-Abbas]] went out and asked the people whether any of them had a covenant from the Prophet about his death; they said no; he asked Umar, who said no; and al-Abbas said: bear witness that no one can testify to a covenant given to him about his death. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268} Then Abu Bakr came from al-Sunh on his mount and dismounted at the door of the mosque, entered Aisha's house where the women around the Prophet had veiled their faces except Aisha, uncovered him, knelt and kissed him, weeping, and said that what Ibn al-Khattab was saying was nothing, for he had died, by Him in whose hand is my soul. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 268}{cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 269} He went to the minbar and Umar sat; Abu Bakr said that God had announced His Prophet's death to him while he was alive among them and had announced theirs to them, and recited Al Imran 3:144; Umar said: "Is this verse in the Qur'an? By God I did not know it had been sent down before today"; Abu Bakr then recited verses on death: [[passage/quran-39-30|al-Zumar 39:30]], [[passage/quran-28-88|al-Qasas 28:88]], [[passage/quran-55-26|al-Rahman 55:26]], [[passage/quran-55-27|55:27]] and [[passage/quran-3-185|Al Imran 3:185]]. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 269}

He then said, in this report, that God had given Muhammad a long life until he established His religion, made His cause manifest, conveyed His message and strove in His path, then took him in that state; that he had left them on the way, and none perishes except after clear proof and healing; that whoever had God as his Lord, God is living and does not die, and whoever worshipped Muhammad and treated him as a god, his god has perished; that they should fear God, hold fast to their religion; that the Book of God was among them, the light and the healing; and that God's swords were drawn and had not yet been laid down, and they would fight whoever opposed them as they had fought alongside the Messenger of God. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 269} Then the Emigrants went with him to the Prophet, and the report goes on to the washing and shrouding, for which see [[event/burial-of-the-prophet|The burial of the Prophet]]. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 269}

## The day and hour of the death
Ibn Kathir says the Prophet died when the forenoon grew intense that day, and it is also said at noon. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 270} In the report of Ibn Jurayj as he gives it, he died in the forenoon on Monday and was buried the next day in the forenoon. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 298} On the date there are several views. Al-Waqidi reports from Abu Ma'shar from Muhammad ibn Qays that the Prophet fell ill on a Wednesday with eleven nights left of Safar, in the house of Zaynab bint Jahsh, for thirteen days, and died on Monday with two nights of Rabi' al-Awwal gone; and in another report of al-Waqidi, the illness began with two nights left of Safar and he died on Monday with twelve nights of Rabi' al-Awwal gone, which Ibn Sa'd affirms. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 282} Ibn Ishaq says he died with twelve nights of Rabi' al-Awwal gone, on the day he had come to Medina as an emigrant; al-Layth says one night gone, Sa'd ibn Ibrahim two nights, Abu Nu'aym al-Fadl ibn Dukayn the first of Rabi'; and Sayf ibn Umar reports from Ibn Abbas ten nights gone. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 282} Ibn Kathir says the well known view is that of Ibn Ishaq and al-Waqidi. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 282} He gives al-Suhayli's objection that, since the standing at Arafa in the Farewell Pilgrimage was a Friday, the twelfth of Rabi' al-Awwal cannot have been a Monday, and the answer that it is possible with different sightings of the new moon, if Mecca saw the Dhu al-Hijja moon on Thursday night and Medina on Friday night. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 283} On the burial there are two reports: Tuesday in Ibn Sa'd, the night of Wednesday in Ibn Ishaq. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 282}

## Ibn Kathir's reservations about some reports
After the address, Ibn Kathir reports from al-Waqidi that Asma bint Umays put her hand between the Prophet's shoulders and said he had died, the seal having been lifted from between his shoulders; he then says that al-Waqidi is weak, his sources unnamed, the report broken and against what is sound, and that he has passed over most of what al-Waqidi and others report on the death for weak chains and objectionable texts. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 269}

## Where the reports differ
- The wording of Abu Bakr's address varies among the report of al-Bukhari, that of al-Zuhri from Ibn Abbas and that of Urwa, the last being longer and having additions that the first lacks. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 269}
- In al-Zuhri's report Abu Bakr says "Sit down, Umar" and he refuses; in al-Bukhari's it is "You who swear, gently", and Umar sits when Abu Bakr speaks. {cite:fath-al-bari scan vol. 1 p. 337}
- The day and hour of the death have the views given above, and this page decides for none of them.
