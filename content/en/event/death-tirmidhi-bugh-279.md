## Overview
Al-Tirmidhi died on the night of Monday, thirteen nights before the end of Rajab, in 279/892, having left a positive scholarly mark on the history of hadith {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba p. 451}; he died at Tirmidh in 279/892 {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 10 p. 579}.

## His students
Al-Hububi, al-Haytham ibn Kulayb and many others narrated from him {cite:taqrib-al-tahdhib p. 558}.
