## Overview
The first raid on al-Andalus: when Ifriqiya was conquered, 'Uthman ordered 'Abd Allah b. Nafi' b. al-Husayn and 'Abd Allah b. Nafi' b. 'Abd al-Qays to march on al-Andalus — they came by sea — and 'Uthman wrote to those levied with them: "Constantinople is opened only from al-Andalus." They set out, the Berbers with them, and God opened to the Muslims {cite:kamil-ibn-al-athir p. 466}.
