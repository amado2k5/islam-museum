## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates the unrest in Sistan after the killing of Uthman, which included the rising of [[person/hasaka-ibn-utab|Hasaka ibn Utab al-Habati]], and his killing by a messenger of Ali, and then his killing by an army that Ali sent. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Before the Battle of the Camel
Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura stayed at Zaranj until the affair of Uthman was in turmoil; he appointed a deputy and left. The people of Zaranj expelled the deputy and shut the city. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The rising of the Habata
After Ali had finished with the Battle of the Camel, Hasaka ibn Utab and [[person/imran-ibn-al-fasil|Imran ibn al-Fasil]] came out with a group of poor Arabs. They camped at Zalaq and took money from it, then entered Zaranj after making peace with its marzban. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Ali sent [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-jaza|Abd al-Rahman ibn Jaza al-Ta'i]] to Sistan, and Hasaka killed him. Ali said that he would kill four thousand of the Habata; it was said to him that they did not number five hundred. Al-Baladhuri relates from Abu Mikhnaf another account, from a different messenger, Awn ibn Jumda. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The army sent
Ali wrote to Ibn Abbas to appoint a man over Sistan with four thousand men, and [[person/rubayi-ibn-al-kas|Rubayi ibn al-Kas]] was sent. When they arrived, Hasaka fought them and they killed him, and Rubayi brought the country under control. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
