## Summary
In 479 AH the Abbasid khutba was restored in the two holy cities, and the Fatimid (Ubaydid) khutba was ended there {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 366}.

## Context
The khutba of the Two Sanctuaries had once been given to the Ubaydids and then returned to the Abbasids in the events of that year. The Hijaz was one of the places where the two rival calls overlapped {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 366}.
