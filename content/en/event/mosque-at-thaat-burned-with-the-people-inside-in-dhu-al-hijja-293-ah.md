## Overview
The book says the mosque at Tha'at was set on fire with the men, women and children inside, nine nights into Dhu al-Hijja 293 AH, and attributes the burning to the Qarmatian and his followers.

## Detail
This completes the account that begins on the previous page. After Abu al-Ashira Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Ruwaya is killed at Tha'at, the people withdraw into the mosque, and the book says the building was set on fire with those inside, men, women and children. It gives the date as nine nights into Dhu al-Hijja 293 AH and attributes the burning to al-Qarmati and the Qarmatians. The author writes from al-Hadi's side, so this is his account of the other side. Dates are the book's own Hijri dates.
