## Overview
al-Baladhuri, on the authority of al-Waqidi, relates that Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan sent [[person/musa-ibn-nusayr|Musa ibn Nusayr]] as governor of Ifriqiya. It is also said that he appointed him in the time of al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik in 89 AH. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.322}

## The conquest
He conquered [[place/tanja|Tangier]] and settled in it, and he was the first to settle there, and he laid out the Muslims' quarters in it. His horsemen reached [[place/sus-al-adna|al-Sus al-Adna]], where he settled them and took captives from them, and they submitted to him. The date of the conquest is disputed. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.322}

The commander on one account: [[person/abd-al-aziz-ibn-marwan|Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan]].
