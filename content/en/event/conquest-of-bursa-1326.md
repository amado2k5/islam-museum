## Overview
The Ottomans blockaded Bursa for many years until the starved city surrendered on 2 Jumada I 726/6 April 1326 and paid a heavy tribute. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1398}

## The conquest
Al-Salabi writes that Osman crowned his fortress conquests by taking Bursa in 717/1317 after a hard, years-long struggle with the commander of its garrison, who finally yielded the city. {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 47} The Encyclopaedia of Islam records that the Ottomans first laid siege to Bursa after sweeping its plain following their victory over the Byzantine tekfur of Bursa at the pass of Dinboz, and that after the surrender the Byzantine commander left for Constantinople while his chief adviser, responsible for the capitulation, remained with the Ottomans. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1397-1398}

## The first capital
After its fall Bursa became the main capital of the Ottoman state from 726/1326 until 805/1402. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1397}
