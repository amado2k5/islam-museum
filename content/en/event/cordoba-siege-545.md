## Overview
In 545 AH Alfonso, lord of Toledo and the land of the Jalaliqa, marched on [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] with forty thousand horsemen, besieged it, and its people were in severe dearth. Abd al-Mu'min sent an army of twelve thousand horsemen, and Alfonso withdrew when they drew near him. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.382}

## After the siege
The commander Abu al-Ghamr al-Sa'ib handed Cordoba to Yahya ibn Maymun, commander of Abd al-Mu'min's army. Then the Franks went back to their lands. In the year after, the army of Abd al-Mu'min entered al-Andalus with twenty thousand men, and the lord of Granada and others entered the obedience of the Almohads. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.382}
