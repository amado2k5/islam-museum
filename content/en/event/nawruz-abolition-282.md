## Summary
In 282 AH the caliph [[person/al-mutadid|al-Mu'tadid]] abolished what had been done at Nawruz: lighting fires and pouring water over people. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.574}

## The source's view
The source describes this as the removal of a Magian custom. The dating is by the Hijri year alone; the conversion to the Gregorian calendar is derived. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.574}
