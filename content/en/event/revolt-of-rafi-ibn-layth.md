## Overview
In 190 AH Rafi ibn Layth ibn Nasr ibn Sayyar appeared in Samarqand in opposition to Harun, and he deposed him and withdrew his hand from obedience. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.8 p.319}
