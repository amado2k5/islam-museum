## Overview
At the start of Abu Bakr al-Suli's service under al-Muktafi, he played al-Mawardi, a chess player the caliph favoured and watched. Al-Suli beat him decisively, the caliph's favour shifted, and he remarked to al-Mawardi that his rosewater had turned to urine. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 677}

The event is tied to the caliph [[person/al-muktafi-billah|al-Muktafi]] and to [[person/al-suli|al-Suli]]. The text does not date the match; it is placed within al-Muktafi's reign (289–295 AH) {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 634}.
