## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-umar|Ubayd Allah ibn Umar]] killed [[person/jufayna-al-ibadi|Jufayna al-Ibadi]] and his two sons, after accusing him of supporting Abu Lu'lu'a in the killing of his father. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
Jufayna was from al-Hira, a Christian, and a wet nurse of [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]]. Al-Baladhuri attributes the accusation to [[person/ubaydallah-ibn-umar|Ubayd Allah ibn Umar]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The date and place are not given in the data: an additional source is needed.
