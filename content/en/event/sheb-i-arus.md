## Overview
The wedding night in [[place/konya|Konya]]: the annual commemoration of the departure of Jalal al-Din Rumi, with performances of the Mevlevi sema in which the dervishes turn in their white robes. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
The best-known visible Sufi gathering in the world; hundreds of thousands are drawn to it every December for centuries. {cite:wikidata}
