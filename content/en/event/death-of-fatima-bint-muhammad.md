## Summary
[[person/fatima|Fatima]], daughter of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], died about six months after his death, on the night of Tuesday, the third of Ramadan in the year 11 AH, in a report transmitted by the book from Malik, from Ja'far ibn Muhammad, from his father, from Ali ibn al-Husayn {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.138}.

## Illness and Care
The book states that [[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma bint Umays]] nursed and assisted her, and took part in washing and preparing her body, and that Fatima asked her not to have a cloth draped over a woman that would outline her figure; so she called for fresh palm branches, bent them, and draped a cloth over them, whereupon Fatima said, "How fine and seemly this is! By this a woman is distinguished from men" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.137}. The book states that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] nursed her himself {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.137}.

## Condolences
[[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] came to offer condolences to Ali, and said, "O Abu al-Hasan, do not precede us in praying over the daughter of the Messenger of God" {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.138}.

## The Funeral Prayer Over Her
In one report, Abu Bakr prayed over her and pronounced four takbirs, while in Muslim's report Ali prayed over her, and the book states that Muslim's report is the stronger one {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.138}. The prayer over her took place at night, and she was buried at night {cite:sallabi-ali-vol1 p.138}.

## Notes for Researchers
- The Hijri date is taken from a report whose chain the book does not verify at this point; the Gregorian month is an approximation.
- The book mentions that Ali said to Abu Bakr, "Step forward, O Abu Bakr," in one report, and the text on the page is garbled.
