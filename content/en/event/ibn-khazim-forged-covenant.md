## Overview
Al-Baladhuri reports that [[person/abdallah-ibn-khazim-al-sulami|Ibn Khazim]] forged a covenant in the name of Ibn Amir, and took charge of Khurasan. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The account
The hosts of the Turks gathered in Khurasan, and he defeated them. Then he came to Basra before the killing of Uthman. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The source's word for the covenant is "forged". Other details are not recorded in the data; an additional source is needed.
