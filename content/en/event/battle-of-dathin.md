## Overview
al-Baladhuri relates that the first encounter between the Muslims and their enemy was in a village of the villages of Gaza called Dathin, against the patrician of Gaza, and that it was before Khalid ibn al-Walid came to Syria. After hard fighting the Muslims won. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151} It is in the district of Palestine, whose amir was [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]], and it belongs to the [[event/conquest-of-syria|conquest of Syria]].

## Another report
Abu Hafs al-Shami related from elders of the people of Syria that the first battle was the battle of al-Araba, and that the Muslims had not fought before it since they left the Hijaz. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.151}

The approximate date of 13 AH is derived.
