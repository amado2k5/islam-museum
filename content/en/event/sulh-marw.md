## Overview
Kanazbak, marzban of Tus, came to Ibn Amir and made peace with him for six hundred thousand dirhams. The marzban of [[place/marw|Merv]], [[person/al-shahijan|al-Shahijan]], sent asking for peace, and Ibn Amir sent [[person/hatim-ibn-al-numan|Hatim ibn al-Nu'man al-Bahili]] to Merv, who made peace with it. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The amount of the peace
Al-Baladhuri says the peace was for two million two hundred thousand dirhams, and quotes other sayings: a million dirhams and two hundred thousand jaribs of wheat and barley, or a million and a hundred thousand ounces. From Abu Ubayda: it was in slave girls, servants, animals and goods, since the people had no coin at that time. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Its conditions
He stipulated that they make room for the Muslims in their homes, and that they themselves divide the money, and that the Muslims had only to receive it. All of Merv was at peace except the village of al-Sanj, which was taken by force. Then Yazid ibn Mu'awiya turned the land tax into money when he came to power. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
