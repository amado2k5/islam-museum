## Overview
The reports differ on the date of the oath of allegiance to Abu al-Abbas Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Ali, known as al-Saffah, in 132 AH. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.420}

## Disagreement over the date
al-Tabari says that the oath was on the night of Friday, thirteen days into Rabi' al-Akhir. He quotes al-Waqidi that it was in Jumada al-Ula, and Abu Ma'shar that it was in Rabi' al-Awwal. {cite:tabari-tarikh-abu-al-fadl j.7 p.420}

al-Suyuti says that he was given the caliphate in Kufa on the third of Rabi' al-Awwal of the same year. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.418-419}

## The sermon
al-Suyuti relates that he led the people in the Friday prayer and gave a sermon, and at the end of it he said that he had increased the stipends of the people of Kufa by a hundred hundred, and that he was "the shedder of blood, the destroyer, the one who lays waste." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.418-419}

For this reason the date is recorded here by year only, and its status is "disputed".

## In al-Dinawari
[[person/abu-hanifa-al-dinawari|Al-Dinawari]] reports that al-Hasan ibn Qahtaba came to Kufa and brought Abu al-Abbas out to the congregational mosque, where he preached, and he reports from al-Haytham ibn Adi that the oath to Abu al-Abbas as caliph, with Abu Ja'far as heir, was taken in Rajab 132. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 369} {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 370} He says that earlier Muhammad ibn Khalid al-Qasri had entered the congregational mosque of Kufa with the Yamanis dressed in black on the day of Ashura, 132. {cite:akhbar-tiwal-amir p. 367}
