## Overview
After the opinion of the people of [[place/khiwan|Khiwan]] split, some saying fight, others saying do not fight but deceive the man so that he goes out and they go out with him from the village, let him enter it and decide afterwards, and still others saying other things, so that opinions multiplied. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.112} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} When they disagreed [[person/al-daam-ibn-ibrahim|al-Da'am]] said: as for me, I was the first to bring this man and take him out of his land and send for him until he came to this country; I have resolved never to fight him and to hear and obey, so act for yourselves while I leave Khiwan for my own land. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} The account is Zaydi, in the voice of the imam's followers; al-Da'am's words are given as the narrator reports them and the museum gives no verdict.

## His going out to Ghuraq
Al-Da'am sent a party of the elders and notables of Khiwan to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] with this message, and himself left Khiwan, some days of Safar having passed, for [[place/ghuraq-yemen|Ghuraq]], while al-Hadi was then at [[place/hawth-yemen|Hawth]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} The editor's footnote says the manuscript has "khuruj" for the heading and the correction is from manuscript S, and that Yaqut in the Mu'jam al-buldan calls Khiwan "a city in Yemen belonging to Hamdan". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113}

## Safe-conduct for Khiwan
The elders of Khiwan reached Hawth, told al-Hadi what al-Da'am had said and asked safe-conduct for the people of Khiwan; he gave it, and when morning came he marched on Khiwan. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.113} See [[event/al-daam-weighs-peace-with-al-hadi-and-sets-conditions-286]], [[event/al-hadi-at-khirfan-and-al-subay-and-the-march-to-hawth-286]] and [[event/al-hadi-enters-khiwan-and-preaches-to-its-people-286]].
