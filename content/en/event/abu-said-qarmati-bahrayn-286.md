## Overview
al-Suyuti reports that in 286 AH [[person/abu-said-al-qarmati|Abu Sa'id al-Qarmati]] appeared in Bahrain, and his strength grew. He was the father of Abu Tahir Sulayman, whom al-Suyuti later says tore out the Black Stone. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 575}

Fighting broke out between him and the caliph's army. He raided Basra and its surroundings and defeated the caliph's army several times. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p. 575}

The date rests on a single report; its conversion to CE is derived. An additional source is needed.
