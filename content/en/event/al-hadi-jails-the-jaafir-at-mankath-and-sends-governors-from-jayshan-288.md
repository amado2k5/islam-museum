## Overview
In the course of his stay at [[place/mankath-yemen|Mankath]] [[person/abu-al-atahiya-ibn-bishr|Abu al-Atahiya]] came to [[person/al-hadi-yahya-al-rassi|al-Hadi]] and told him that a body of the Ja'afir had resolved to stir sedition in the army and had agreed on something touching al-Hadi and Abu al-Atahiya; al-Hadi ordered them all gathered, sent them to Sana'a and ordered them jailed and bound in irons, and when they reached Abu Muhammad [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-rassi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn]] he carried out what he had been ordered {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}. Then [[person/abu-al-ushayra-ibn-al-ruwayya|Abu al-Ushayra ibn al-Ruwayya]] came to al-Hadi at Mankath with a dense army, and al-Hadi set out with Abu al-Ushayra, put [[person/abdullah-ibn-al-husayn-al-futayimi|Abdullah ibn al-Husayn al-Futayimi]] over Mankath, charging him to fear God and command the good and forbid the wrong, and went on until he halted at [[place/jayshan-yemen|Jayshan]] {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}.

## Detail
Its people met him, he entered and stayed three days, ordered its people to gather to him and told them to fear God, urged them to obey him and to command the good and forbid the wrong, and told them what was due from them and to them. At Jayshan he sent a man of the Tabaris called [[person/ali-ibn-dirr-governor-of-aden-288|Ali ibn Dirr]], who was, in the narrator's description, chaste, Muslim, devout and learned in the lawful and the forbidden, and appointed him over [[place/aden|Aden]] with a charge to fear God. He left Jayshan and left behind [[person/abu-abd-allah-al-razi-of-jayshan|Abu Abd Allah al-Razi]], a virtuous and good man in the narrator's words, charging him to fear God and command the good and forbid the wrong {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.214}. The phrase "on al-sawa'a" in the report of the Ja'afir has no clear sense in the printed text and Apple Vision gives it as printed, so the museum does not interpret it and does not describe the Ja'afir beyond what is written; the copyist's marginal note reads "his imprisoning of those accused of corruption". The entry record places Abu al-Ushayra's meeting with al-Hadi at Jayshan, and this report places it at Mankath; the museum does not reconcile them.
