## Overview
In 127 AH [[person/abdallah-ibn-muawiya-ibn-abdallah-ibn-jafar|Abd Allah ibn Mu'awiya ibn Abd Allah ibn Ja'far]] called the people to himself in Kufa, and fought its governor [[person/abdallah-ibn-umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Abd Allah ibn Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]]. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 p. 607-611}

## The causes
In al-Tabari's account, Ibn Mu'awiya had come to Kufa as a visitor seeking the favour of Ibn Umar, and had no intention of rising. When the partisan strife arose, the people of Kufa said to him: call people to yourself, for the Banu Hashim have a better claim to the matter than the Banu Marwan. One of its causes was that Ibn Umar gave great gifts to Mudar and Rabi'a, and gave two men of Yemen nothing like them, so they became angry. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 p. 607-611}

## Allegiance and expansion
The people rose until they gathered in the mosque, pledged allegiance to Ibn Mu'awiya, and brought him into the palace. The pledge came to him from al-Mada'in. He then prevailed over Hulwan, the Jibal, Hamadhan, Qumis, Isfahan and al-Rayy. {cite:tabari-khayyat-part2 p. 607-611}

The Gregorian date is derived.
