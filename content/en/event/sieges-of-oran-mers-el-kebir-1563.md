## Summary
Hasan b. Khayr al-Din's siege of Oran and Mers el-Kebir, 971/1563 {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 250-251}.

## Army and base
[[person/hasan-ibn-khayr-al-din|Hasan]] marched from Algiers with fifteen thousand musketeers, a thousand Sipahi horse under [[person/ahmad-muqran-al-zawawi|Ahmad Muqran]], and twelve thousand Zawawa and Bani Abbas, the fleet carrying supplies to the Mostaganem base; he invested the cities on 13 April {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 250}.

## The lifting
Spanish and Portuguese relief kept converging at Malta in answer to the governor's call, while the beylerbey lay six stages off; Hasan raised the siege before the rest arrived {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 250-251}.
