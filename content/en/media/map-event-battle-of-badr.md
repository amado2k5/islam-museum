## Description
Depicts: [[event/battle-of-badr|the Battle of Badr]]. Alt text: schematic map of the Battle of Badr. It shows the following places: Medina, Mecca, and Badr (the wells). Dashed lines show the march of each side: the Muslims of Medina and the Quraysh of Mecca. The locations are approximate. Licence: CC0.
