## Summary
Kasım Pasha Camii was built in 1478 by Evliya Kasım Pasha, vezir under both Murat II and Mehmet II {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.99}.

## Form
A single-unit mosque of dressed stone, its eleven-metre dome on squinches set high in the walls and covered outside by triangular projections between the corners and the drum; the entrance is on the left beside the north-east minaret, and the building was partially restored in 1964 {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.99}.

## For young visitors
A vezir built it in 1478 — its door sits on the left, next to its minaret.
