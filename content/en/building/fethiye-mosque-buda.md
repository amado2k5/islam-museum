## Overview
The Fethiye mosque was a church built in the thirteenth century, the oldest structure in the castle, converted with the Matthias Church into a mosque after Buda Castle was taken. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 59} Evliya Çelebi says it resembled the Great Mosque and that its bell tower became a minaret; it was called the Saatli (Clock) Mosque from the clock on the tower. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 59}

## History
Before the Ottomans it had been the castle's main church where Hungarian kings were crowned; it kept its original form until the 1940s, having remained in use under Ottoman rule, until it was badly hit by bombing in the Second World War; only the tower was restored, the walls left at foundation level, and it now serves visitors with a view over the castle. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 59}
