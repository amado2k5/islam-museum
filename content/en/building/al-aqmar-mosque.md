## Overview
The al-Aqmar Mosque in Cairo stands at the end of the market of the candle-makers, near the market of the poultry-sellers. Its site was formerly the market of the wheat-dealers {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1063}.

## The gate and its inscriptions
Al-Maqrizi reports that Amir Yalbugha al-Salimi told him he had read words in the lines inscribed on the threshold of the al-Aqmar mosque's gate {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1132}.

## The Mukhalliq corner
The Mukhalliq corner lies opposite the basin of the al-Aqmar mosque, on the right of anyone entering the mosque known as Mu'bad Musa {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1131}.
