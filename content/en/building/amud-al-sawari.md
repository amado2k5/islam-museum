## Overview
The Column of the Pillars is a tall column standing in the open outside [[place/iskandariyya|Alexandria]], with a base beneath it and a capital above. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 240}

## Al-Umari's description
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari says that among columns it has no equal in its height or its roundness. He adds that there are stories about it, written in books and passed around by word of mouth. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 240}
