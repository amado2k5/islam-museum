## Summary
The al-Hamra Mosque of [[place/kufa|Kufa]]; the Mawsu'at al-Atabat — in a report al-Majlisi transmits from Muhammad ibn Muslim from Abu Ja'far — counts it among Kufa's 'blessed' mosques alongside those of Ghani, Bani Zafar, [[building/al-sahlah-mosque|al-Sahlah]] and Ja'fi {cite:musawat-atabat}.
