## Overview
The White Monastery is a monastery built of white stone, which gave it its name. It lies west of the Nile, at the edge of the cliff overlooking the cultivated land, opposite Akhmim ([[place/akhmim|Akhmim]]), and it is the monastery of Bu Shenouda. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 374-375}

## Al-Umari's description
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari passed by it in the company of the sultan and described it. A long canal flows from the Nile and ends in a wide basin east of the monastery, overlooked by a hill of silt from the canals. He says he had never seen anything as pleasant in winter and spring. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 374-375}
