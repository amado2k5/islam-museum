## Overview
The most famous historic market in [[place/cairo|Cairo]], built by Amir Jahrkas al-Khalili in 1382 as a caravanserai for merchants; today its lanes are full of coppersmiths, goldsmiths and cafés around al-Husayn. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The destination of every visitor to Cairo for six centuries; the spirit of the Mamluk bazaar survives in its noise and perfumes. {cite:wikidata}
