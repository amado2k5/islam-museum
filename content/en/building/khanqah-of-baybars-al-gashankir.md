## Overview
The Khanqah of Rukn al-Din Baybars al-Jashnakir in Cairo, within the Dar al-Wizara, faces the Dirb al-Asfar, the slaughter ground where the caliphs sacrificed animals {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1065} {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1200}.

## Its neighbours
Al-Maqrizi says that the Qaraqanshiyya madrasa and the Khanqah of Rukn al-Din Baybars were both part of the Dar al-Wizara, and that the lands beside the khanqah extended to Bab al-Jawaniyya {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1065}.

He also says that the house of Amir Qazman and the house of the vizier Sanqur al-A'sar stood beside the khanqah {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1065}.
