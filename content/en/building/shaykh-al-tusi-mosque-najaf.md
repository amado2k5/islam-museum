## Summary
The Shaykh al-Tusi Mosque of [[place/najaf|Najaf]]; the Mawsu'at al-Atabat records that the Bab al-Tusi — the north gate of the [[building/imam-ali-shrine|Imam Ali shrine]]'s court — is named for Shaykh al-Ta'ifa Abu Ja'far Muhammad al-Tusi (d. 460 AH), and leads into Tusi street where this mosque holds the Shaykh's tomb {cite:musawat-atabat}.
