## Overview
The khanqah-madrasa of [[person/barsbay|al-Ashraf Barsbay]] in the [[place/eastern-cemetery-cairo|Eastern Cemetery]] (1432) belongs to one of Cairo's most striking ensembles, with the tomb of Ganibak al-Ashrafi (1432) and the tomb of the amir Qurqumas (1511) moved there in 1983. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.210} What strikes the eye is the gathering of domes whose geometric patterns extend from the dome surfaces to the faceted, stepped cubes that carry them. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.210}

## The star domes
Three of the four domes survive: Barsbay's own, the eastern one for his relatives, and the free-standing tomb of Ganibak to the north. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.211} Following Behrens-Abouseif's analysis, Barsbay's dome moves from rows of eight-pointed stars at the base to seven-pointed in the middle and six-pointed towards the apex, like a fine net; the eastern dome runs twelve-, eight-, twelve- and seven-pointed stars; Ganibak's starts with bisected twelve-pointed stars and turns to ten-pointed stars. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.211} For Yeomans the placing of these masses and the balance of solid and void make this one of the most satisfying groupings in Cairo. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.213}

## The khanqah
A double flight of steps leads to the portal, and a small vestibule to a rectangular prayer hall of three aisles parallel to the qibla under a painted ceiling, with reused polychrome marble on floor and dado; the original mihrab is lost, but a wooden minbar (1451) made for the mosque of al-Ghamri survives. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.213} The mausoleum keeps its original mihrab of polychrome mosaic inlaid with mother-of-pearl, with the cenotaph before it. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.213} The ruins to the south held two-storey apartments for Sufis and their families, and across the road stood a zawiya for the Rifa'i order; Behrens-Abouseif notes that khanqahs were generally independent of particular orders while zawiyas served one shaykh's tariqa. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.213}
