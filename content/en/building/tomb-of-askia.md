## Overview
The Tomb of Askia at Gao in Mali is among the chief remains of the Songhay empire's mud-brick architecture of the late fifteenth century, attributed to Sultan Askia Muhammad {cite:prussin-hatumere-1986}.

## In the sources
The tomb stands as a mud-brick pyramid braced with palm beams at the edge of Gao, witness to the Songhay kingdom and its architecture {cite:prussin-hatumere-1986}.
