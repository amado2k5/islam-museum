## Overview
The covered straight souq of [[place/damascus|Damascus]] from Bab al-Nasr to the square of the Umayyad Mosque; it is old, and took this name in the late Ottoman period. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The main market in one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in history; today everything is sold there, from silk to ice cream. {cite:wikidata}
