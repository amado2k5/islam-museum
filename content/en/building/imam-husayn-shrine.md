## Overview
The shrine of Husayn ibn Ali at Karbala over the place of his killing in 61 AH on Ashura; a major pilgrimage centre for the Twelver Shi'a {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya} {cite:momen-1985}.

## Perspectives
- **Sunni:** the site of Husayn's martyrdom is held in esteem as Ibn Kathir records, while rulings on grave visitation differ {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya}.
- **Academic:** the shrine's growth made Karbala a Twelver pilgrimage and learning city {cite:momen-1985}.
- **Twelver:** the noblest of shrines and the destination of Ashura and Arba'in pilgrimage {cite:momen-1985}.
