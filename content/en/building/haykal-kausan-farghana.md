## Overview
A house built by King Kawus in the city of Farghana, to which worshippers of the stars came, those who sought nearness through the sun. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 223}

## Its destruction
According to Abu Ubayd al-Bakri, al-Mu'tasim destroyed it. Al-Umari says that its destruction has an amusing story, which appears in the book "Kitab al-Zaman". {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 223}
