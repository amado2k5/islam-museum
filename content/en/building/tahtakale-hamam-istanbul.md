## Overview
Tahtakale Hamamı stands on Kantarcılar Caddesi, the "Avenue of the Scale-Makers", in Istanbul; an enormous double bath dating to the age of Mehmet the Conqueror, it has recently been restored and converted into a shopping mall {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.132}.

## Architecture
Its most impressive chamber is the almost square camekân, 16.70 by 16.25 metres, covered by a huge dome on a low drum {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.132}.
