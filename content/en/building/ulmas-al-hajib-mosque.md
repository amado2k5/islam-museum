## Overview
The mosque of Almas al-Hajib on the street outside Bab Zuwayla in Cairo, built by Amir Sayf al-Din Almas al-Hajib and completed in 730 AH {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2087}.

## The builder
Al-Maqrizi says that Almas was one of the mamluks of al-Malik al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun, who raised him until he became one of the great amirs {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2087}.
