## Overview
The mosque of Sidi Abd al-Rahman stands in [[place/algiers|Algiers]]. [[person/rexford-newcomb|Newcomb]] gives it the figures 1108 and 1697, the first apparently a Hijri year matching the second. {cite:newcomb-decorative-tiles-north-africa-1929 p.27}

## Its tiles
Newcomb names it among the buildings of the city richest in ceramic ornament, saying it makes large use of tiles for its decoration, even in the adjoining cemetery. {cite:newcomb-decorative-tiles-north-africa-1929 p.27}
