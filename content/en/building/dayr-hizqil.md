## Overview
The Monastery of Hizqil is a monastery in Syria for the mentally ill. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 344}

## The account of al-Mubarrad
Al-Umari reports from al-Mubarrad that he went in and asked its monks about a madman with a pleasant way of speaking, who would make them laugh. They pointed to a high hall, and he spent the evening with the man in charge of their affairs, and he saw their ranks according to the degree of their afflictions. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 344}

Di'bil ibn Ali also mentioned it in his satire of Abu Abbad, the secretary of al-Mamun. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 344}
