## Overview
A mosque and madrasa in [[place/cairo|Cairo]] built by Sultan Hasan al-Nasir in 1363: four madrasas for the four schools of law around an open courtyard, and the tallest Mamluk minaret in the city. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Travellers called it 'the pyramid of Islamic architecture' for its massiveness; it is still the largest surviving Mamluk mosque. {cite:wikidata}
