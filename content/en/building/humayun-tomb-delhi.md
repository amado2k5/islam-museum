## Summary
Humayun's Tomb is among the best-known Mughal tombs. It combines the chahar bagh garden and the noni-partite plan {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.4} {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.13}.

## Origin of the design
Rezavi cites the court historian Khwand Amir's account of a pleasure boat-house built on the orders of Humayun, which the author takes as the model for the tomb. He notes that the design is attributed to Mirza Ghiyas, who came to India with Babur {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.14}.
