## Overview
A small mosque without a minaret built by Shah Abbas in [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]] in 1619; a lovely dome whose colours change with the daylight, and a tunnel entrance that leads the visitor from the square to the dome. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Historians regard it as the most delicate Safavid architecture; it was built as a private royal prayer hall, not a congregational mosque. {cite:wikidata}
