## Overview
The Tabakhane ("tannery") Bath is named after the Ottoman tanners' quarter (Tabán) in which it lay, and is known today as the Rác Bath. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 43} Built in the 1560s, it was bought by Sokollu Mustafa Pasha from the qadi of Pest, Mevlana Hasan Efendi, and endowed; like the other Buda baths it is laid out with iwans round an octagonal pool. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 43}

## Today
Many travellers reported its waters as curing many illnesses. Badly damaged in the Second World War, it was restored and joined to a large thermal complex, and it cannot be seen from outside for the new buildings around it. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 43}
