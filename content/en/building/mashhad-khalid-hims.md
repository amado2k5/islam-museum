## Overview
A shrine outside [[place/homs|Homs]] that the common people attribute to [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]]. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 219}

## Al-Umari's correction
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari firmly denies this attribution. He says that Umar ibn al-Khattab removed Khalid from Homs and summoned him to Medina, where he died, and that Umar was displeased with him after his death. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 219}

Al-Umari holds that the tomb belongs to Khalid ibn Yazid ibn Mu'awiya. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 219}
