## Overview
The mosque of Selim II in [[place/edirne|Edirne]], built by the architect Sinan in 1575; its dome, 31.5 metres in diameter, was for centuries the widest Islamic dome, and it has four minarets. Historians regard it as the peak of Ottoman architecture. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Sinan himself said it was his masterpiece, in which he perfected his craft; today it is still a working mosque and a model of classical Ottoman architecture. {cite:wikidata}
