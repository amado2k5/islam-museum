## Summary
Dar al-Nadwa was the assembly house of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]], founded by [[person/qusayy-ibn-kilab|Qusayy ibn Kilab]] on the north side of the [[building/kaaba|Kaaba]] mosque, with its door opening onto the mosque. It was where the affairs of Quraysh were decided {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.30} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/125}.

## Its merit
The Sealed Nectar notes that this house gave Quraysh a distinction, because it secured the unity of their word and settled disputes by peaceful means {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.30} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.31} {cite:ikhbar-al-kiram p.152} {cite:muhadarat-tarikh-al-umam-al-islamiyya 1/36}. Its presidency was the first public mark of Qusayy's leadership. There they consulted on the grave matters that befell them, and there they gave their daughters in marriage {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.31}.

## After Qusayy
Qusayy bequeathed the presidency of the house to his son Abd al-Dar. After the offices were divided with the Banu Abd Manaf, the Dar al-Nadwa, the banner and the custodianship of the Kaaba remained in the hands of the Banu Abd al-Dar {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.31} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/129-132}. See [[concept/manasib-quraysh|the offices of Quraysh]].

Dar al-Nadwa was Mecca's great council, where the elders of Quraysh gathered to agree on their important affairs.
