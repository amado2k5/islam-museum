## Overview
Dar al-Nadwa: Quraysh's assembly hall which Qusayy founded on the north side of the Sacred Mosque, where their grave affairs were decided.

## The story
Among Qusayy's merits is that he founded Dar al-Nadwa on the north side of the Ka'ba's mosque, opening its door onto the mosque; it was Quraysh's assembly, in which their grave affairs were decided {cite:ibn-hisham-sira}. This house held rank over Quraysh because it guaranteed unity of word and the fair settling of problems {cite:ikhbar-al-kiram}.

In it they consulted on the grave matters that befell them, and married off their daughters {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}. Dar al-Nadwa's presidency remained with Banu Abd al-Dar after the offices were divided {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Dar al-Nadwa was Mecca's great council hall, where Quraysh's elders gathered to agree on their important affairs.
