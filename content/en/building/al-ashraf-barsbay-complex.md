## Overview
[[person/barsbay|Al-Ashraf Barsbay]] built two khanqah-madrasas, one near the amber market on al-Mu'izz street and the other in the Eastern Cemetery; Yeomans's map dates the al-Mu'izz one to 1425. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.126} {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.210} It was built for sixty Sufi students, with a cruciform interior like Barquq's madrasa on the same street. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.210}

## The waqf inscription
Yeomans points to the tiraz inscription round the courtyard, which spells out the conditions of the waqf so that later rulers could not, like al-Mu'ayyad Shaykh, take liberties with such endowments. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.210}

## A note on identity
This record was a general card for the Barsbay complex in Cairo; it is here given to the al-Mu'izz street madrasa, and the Eastern Cemetery complex has its own record, the [[building/khanqah-mausoleum-of-sultan-barsbay|khanqah-mausoleum of Barsbay]]. {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.210}
