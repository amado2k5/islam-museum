## Overview
The Grand Mosque of the Islamic Center of Cleveland stands at Parma, Ohio; it was built between 1989 and 1995 by Gerald M. Rembowski, and the catalogue places it among the "imported designs" and calls it an Islamic architectural landmark. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 12-13} Its gold-plated dome is a replica of the [[building/dome-of-the-rock|Dome of the Rock]] in Jerusalem. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 12-13}
