## Overview
The Western Wall of the Haram al-Sharif in Jerusalem — Muslims call it the Buraq Wall, Jews the Wailing Wall — an inseparable part of the sanctuary's wall, over a hundred meters long and some twenty high; most archaeologists date its lowest courses to Herod's temple, as the 1930 commission reported {cite:al-quds-bayn-al-yahudiyya-wal-islam-azhar p. 77}. The Palestine administration regulated Jewish prayer there under limited arrangements in 1925, 1928 and 1929, amid recurring complaints over benches, screens and lamps; in 1928 the White Paper confirmed the status quo.
