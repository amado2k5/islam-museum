## Overview
The Ottoman buildings of Hungary are the surviving mosques, tombs, baths and fortresses of the Ottoman period, catalogued by Mehmet Emin Yılmaz's bilingual guide *A Guide for Ottoman Architecture in Hungary* (2024) across thirty-six named places from Buda-Pest to Pécs, Szigetvár and Kanije {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 pp.20-21}.

## What the guide records
After the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam's "Budin" article, the guide lays out visiting routes with a card per monument: in Buda-Pest the Tomb and museum of Gül Baba, the Veli Bej, Király, Tabakhâne, Yeşil Direkli and Rác baths, the Toygun Pasha and Sokollu Mustafa Pasha mosques (both now churches), Buda Castle, Abdurrahman Abdi Pasha's tomb and the Grand Mosque of Pest; in Pécs the Gâzi Kasım Pasha Mosque, the castle, and the cathedral that served as Sultan Süleyman Mosque; and in Szigetvár, Kanije and beyond the surviving tombs, mosques and baths {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 pp.20-21,26-27,105-107}.
