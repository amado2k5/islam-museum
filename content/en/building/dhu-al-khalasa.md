## Overview
Dhu al-Khalasa was a house of idols, venerated by Khath'am and Bajila and the people of Tihama close to them, who went on pilgrimage to it. It was called the "Yemeni Ka'ba" and the "Syrian Ka'ba", to distinguish it from the house in Mecca. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3823}

## Its destruction
The Sahih reports that the Prophet said to [[person/jarir-ibn-abd-allah-al-bajali|Jarir ibn Abd Allah al-Bajali]]: "Will you not relieve me of Dhu al-Khalasa?" So he set out with a hundred and fifty horsemen from Ahmas, broke it, and killed whoever was found with it. The Prophet prayed for them and for Ahmas. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3823}
