## Overview
The Saburi House of Learning in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]: an endowed library founded by the vizier [[person/sabur-ibn-ardashir|Abu Nasr Sabur ibn Ardashir]] in 381 AH. Its holdings exceeded ten thousand volumes and it lasted about seventy years until it burned when Tughril Beg entered Baghdad in 450 AH. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.364-365}

## Foundation and endowment
Sabur bought a house between the two walls, filled it with books of every science, and endowed it for students of knowledge — an early model of a public-facing library before the age of the Nizamiyya schools. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.364-365}

## Survival and burning
The house served scholars for about seventy years until Tughril Beg's entry into Baghdad in 450 AH, when it burned with the treasures it held — the loss Ibn al-Jawzi records in the biography of its founder. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.365}
