## Overview
The Monastery of al-Naqi lies south of [[place/busra|Busra]]. The monk [[person/bahira|Bahira]] was there. Al-Umari reports that the meeting between Bahira and the Prophet took place there, according to the well-known account. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 217}
