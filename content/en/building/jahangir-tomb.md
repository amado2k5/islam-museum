## Overview
The tomb of the emperor [[person/jahangir-mughal|Jahangir]] is at Shahdara near [[place/lahore|Lahore]]. [[person/john-brookes|Brookes]] notes that Jahangir made Lahore his real capital, perhaps because it was nearer Kashmir, and that near it [[person/nur-jahan|Nur Jahan]] laid out the huge Dilkusha garden at Shahdara, where Jahangir was buried in a tomb of his wife's design, she and her brother [[person/asaf-khan-iv|Asaf Khan]] being buried close by. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.138}

## Among the garden tombs
[[person/syed-ali-nadeem-rezavi|Rezavi]] lists it with Humayun's tomb, the tombs at Sikandra and the tomb of I'timad al-Dawla as a typical Mughal funerary garden in the [[concept/chaharbagh|chaharbagh]] tradition. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002 p.129}
