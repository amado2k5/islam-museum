## Summary
The Semiz Ali Pasha arasta — known locally as the Kapalı Çarşı — is an old Ottoman market one block west of the bedesten, built by Sinan in 1568 for Semiz Ali Pasha, another of Süleyman's grand vezirs {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.101}.

## Form
A narrow stone-and-brick structure some 300 metres long with 126 vaulted shops flanking a central corridor, its arch voussoirs alternating red and white stone and gateways on all sides; badly damaged by fire in 1992, it has since been repaired {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.101}.

## For young visitors
A tunnel-like shopping street Sinan built in 1568 — it burned in 1992 and came back to life.
