## Overview
A palace between Sinjar and Nasibin, built by al-Abbas ibn Amr al-Ghanawi of the people of Tall Sayyar. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 245-248}

## Its builder
He was the builder of al-Raqqa and Ras al-Ayn, from the fortress of Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, and he governed al-Yamama and Bahrain. Al-Mu'tadid sent him against the Qaramita with ten thousand horsemen; all of them were killed, and he alone was spared. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 245-248}

## What was written on the palace
The palace became a board for sermons. Sayf al-Dawla wrote on it in his own hand in 331 AH, and beneath it al-Ghadanfar ibn al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Hamdan wrote in 362 AH, then al-Muqallid ibn al-Musayyab ibn Rafi' in 388 AH. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 245-248}

Then his son Qarwash wrote in 401 AH. He had resolved to demolish it, saying that it was ill-omened, but he left it standing. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 245-248}
