## Overview
Rumeli Hisarı, the castle Mehmed II built on the European shore of the Bosphorus at its narrowest point, opposite Bayezid's fortress on the Asian side {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 92}.

## Site and purpose
The Byzantine emperor offered money to stop its building and Mehmed pressed on; when complete, the book says, it rose 82 m, and only 660 m separated the two castles — close enough for their cannon to bar any ship's run to Constantinople {cite:dawlat-uthmaniyya-salabi p. 92}.
