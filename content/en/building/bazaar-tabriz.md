## Overview
The covered bazaar of [[place/tabriz|Tabriz]], the largest connected covered trading complex in the world on the UNESCO list; a network of halls and vaults on the old Silk Road. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It has been the trade centre of western Persia since the Middle Ages; it is still a working bazaar, not a tourist relic. {cite:wikidata}
