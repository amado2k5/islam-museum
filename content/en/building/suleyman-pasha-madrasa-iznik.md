## Summary
The Medrese of Süleyman Pasha in İznik was built a few years after the conquest of Nicaea by Süleyman Pasha, Orhan Gazi's eldest son; it was the first Ottoman building designed specifically as a medrese and is thus of particular interest in Ottoman architectural history {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.72}.

## Plan
Its three-sided porticoed courtyard is open on one side today, though it was almost certainly walled in originally {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.72}. The dershane is off-centre, with two cells to its north and three to its south and three cells on each of the other two sides; porticoes front the student cells, and a pool with a fountain stood at the courtyard's centre {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.72-73}.

## For young visitors
The very first 'college' the Ottomans ever built stands in İznik, the city Orhan conquered.
