## Overview
Kumrulu Mescit, the "Little Mosque of the Turtle Dove" on the Fifth Hill, takes its name from a fragment of Byzantine sculpture built into the adjoining çeşme, showing two turtle doves drinking from the Fountain of Life {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.136-137}.

## History
Its interest lies chiefly in its founder: Atik Sinan, Mehmet the Conqueror's chief architect and designer of the original Fatih Camii. His tombstone stands in the mosque garden, its inscription recording that he was executed in 1471 — supposedly, Freely reports, for not making the Mosque of the Conqueror higher than Haghia Sophia {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.137}.
