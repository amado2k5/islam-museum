## Overview
The Ma'rib dam: Yemen's engineering marvel; its building began in the time of Saba''s Mukarribs, and its great breach around 450 CE is the flood of al-Arim.

## The story
Building began on the dam known as the Ma'rib dam in the Mukarribs' time at their capital Sirwah; it has a great place in Yemen's history {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

After Yemen's independence from the first Abyssinian occupation, breaches began to occur in it, until the great flood came — which the Qur'an mentions as the flood of al-Arim — in 450 or 451 CE, bringing ruin to the settled lands and scattering the peoples {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
The Ma'rib dam is a mighty water-barrier in ancient Yemen; one day it broke, a great valley flooded, and many tribes scattered.
