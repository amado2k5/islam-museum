## Overview
A mosque in Casablanca opened in 1993 on a rock extending into the Atlantic Ocean; its minaret is 210 metres, among the tallest minarets in the world, and its hall holds twenty-five thousand worshippers. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
King Hassan II built it with the hands of six thousand Moroccan craftsmen; it is the tallest mosque in Morocco and appears on the two-hundred-dirham banknote. {cite:wikidata}
