## Overview
The Attarine Mosque in Alexandria was built by Badr al-Jamali, Amir al-Juyush, from confiscated funds, and finished in Rabi' I 479 AH {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1078}.

## The taking of Alexandria
Al-Maqrizi says that Badr besieged Alexandria for several days in Muharram 477 AH, after a group there had risen with his son al-Awhad, and took it by force {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1078}.

## Badr
Al-Maqrizi says that Abu al-Najm Badr al-Jamali was an Armenian mamluk of Jamal al-Dawla ibn Ammar, and was named al-Jamali after him {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1077}.
