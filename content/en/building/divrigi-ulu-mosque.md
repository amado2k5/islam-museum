## Overview
The Great Mosque (Ulucami) of Divriği in Anatolia; Aptullah Kuran dates it to 1230 CE / AH 628 {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman p.160}.

## In the sources
Kuran lists it among the later mosques following the pattern of the Umayyad Great Mosque of Damascus and the Kairawan mosque in emphasizing the central aisle, alongside the twelfth-century Kale Mescidi (Fortress Mosque) at Erzurum and the Mosque of Isa Bey at Selçuk dedicated in 1375 {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman p.160}.
