## Overview
The Great Mosque of Divrigi in Anatolia; Kuran dates it to 1230 CE/AH 628 and counts it with the Ulucami of Erzurum among the Seljuk-era mosques preceding classical Ottoman architecture {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman}.

## In the sources
Kuran cites the Divrigi Ulucami among his examples of earlier Islamic mosques in Anatolia {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman}.
