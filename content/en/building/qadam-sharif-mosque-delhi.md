## Overview
The Qadam Sharif shrine lies between Paharganj and the Qutb road in Delhi, in the locality known as Nabi Karim. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 introduction} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 75} Its large square tomb is believed to have been built by [[person/firuz-shah-tughluq|Firuz Shah Tughluq]] for himself, and used instead for his son Prince Fath Khan, who died before him. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 75} Tradition says that his spiritual guide [[person/jahaniyan-jahangasht|Makhdum Jahaniyan Jahangasht]] brought from Mecca at his order a stone bearing the Prophet's footprint (*qadam*), which was placed at Fath Khan's grave; the sultan then built a mosque and a school and enclosed the tomb within high battlemented walls. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 75} The author dates the tomb, mosque, madrasa and tank to 1374. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 introduction}

## Architecture
The mosque is in the Firuzian style, counted among Khan-i Jahan's mosques, and stands some fifteen yards from the shrine inside the enclosure. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 75} It is strongly built of rubble and mortar, three bays wide on the façade and two bays deep, the compartments covered by vaults. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 75} North of the central mihrab is a pulpit, and next to it a small space about a yard square fenced by a wall four feet high; on the centre of the roof stands a red-stone *chhatri* from which the call to prayer was given. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 75}

## Its state
The mosque is now in ruins; the author photographed its gateway, which has no arch but two pillars carrying a straight stone beam, and the good stone of its fabric lying scattered about. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 95} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 111}
