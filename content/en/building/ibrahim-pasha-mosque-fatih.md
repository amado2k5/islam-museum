## Overview
Ibrahim Pasha Camii stands on the slope of the Third Hill leading down to the Golden Horn in Istanbul; according to the Hadikat-ül Cevami, a survey of the city's mosques around 1780, it was founded in AH 883 (1478) by Çandarlı Ibrahim Pasha, grand vezir under Bayezit II {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.136}.

## History
The complex originally included a medrese, a mektep, a hamam and a fountain, none of which have survived; the mosque itself lay in ruins for many years and was restored in the 1960s — a restoration Freely faults for destroying all that was original in the structure {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.136}.
