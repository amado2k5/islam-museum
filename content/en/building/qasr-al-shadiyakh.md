## Overview
A palace at the Nishapur gate of Khurasan, which was the seat of government for some of its rulers. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 233}

Its building was likened to Ghumdan, so much so that it was said in verse: "Drink, and may the crown be yours with pleasure, leaning on al-Shadhiyakh, and leave Ghumdan to Yemen." {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 233}

On its gate, according to al-Umari's report, the poet [[person/ali-ibn-al-jahm|Ali ibn al-Jahm]] was crucified. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 233}

The palace is near [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]].
