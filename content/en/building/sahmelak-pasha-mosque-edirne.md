## Summary
Şahmelek Pasha Camii, directly across the road from Gazi Mihal's bath, is a single-unit mosque built in 1428; its founder Şahmelek Pasha, a blind man, took part in the succession war between Beyazit I's sons and was an important figure under Mehmet I and Murat II {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.94-95}.

## Form
Instead of a porch it has a two-bayed vestibule with doorways at either end; the south-east minaret appears a nineteenth-century replacement, the square prayer room is domed on squinches, and the founder lies in a simple open türbe abutting the north-east corner {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.95}.

## For young visitors
A mosque built in 1428 by a blind vezir — whose simple open tomb still hugs its corner.
