## Summary
The külliye of Sokollu Mehmet Pasha is Lüleburgaz's principal Ottoman monument; Sinan built it in 1569–71 for the grand vezir, and it was the most impressive and extensive complex on the Edirne–Istanbul road {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.107-108}.

## The buildings
The külliye originally comprised a mosque, a medrese, a darülkura, a hamam and a caravansarai; all the buildings still stand except the caravansarai, of which only the entryway, a few adjacent rooms and a tower opposite its north side remain {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.108}.

## For young visitors
Sinan built a whole travellers' station on the great road — mosque, college, bath — and it is still standing.
