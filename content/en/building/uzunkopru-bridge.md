## Summary
Uzunköprü — 'the Long Bridge' — is a town named after the exceptionally long bridge spanning the Ergene River at its entrance from the Havsa road {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.106}.

## Form
The bridge was built for Murat II in 1444 by Hacı Ivaz Pasha: 1,392 metres long, carried on 173 arches {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.106}.

## For young visitors
A bridge more than a kilometre long that people have crossed since 1444 — the whole town is named after it.
