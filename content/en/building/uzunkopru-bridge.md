## Overview
Uzunköprü, "the Long Bridge", is a town on the old highway through Thrace that takes its name from the exceptionally long bridge spanning the Ergene River at the town's entrance from the Havsa road {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.91}.

## The structure
The bridge was built for Murat II in 1444 by Hacı Ivaz Pasha; it is 1,392 metres long and carried on 173 arches {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.91}.
