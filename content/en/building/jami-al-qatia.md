## Overview
The congregational mosque built by Muhdhib al-Dawla 'Ali b. Nasr at the Qati'a in Baghdad in 379 — the same year he also restored the watering-places at Wasit, finished at a cost of six thousand {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.149}.
