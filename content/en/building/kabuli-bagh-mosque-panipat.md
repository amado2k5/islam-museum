## Summary
The Kabuli Bagh Mosque at Panipat was built by Babur around 1527. It has a chahartaq central nave, a high peshtaq (portal) and lower lateral wings with four domed bays {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.9} {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.11}.

## Comparison
Rezavi compares its plan with the Namazgah Mosque at Qarshi near Samarqand, and with the arch-netted transition zones in plaster, to argue for Iranian-Timurid rather than Sharqi (Jaunpur) origins {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.10} {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.11}.
