## Overview

The Kadhimiya shrine of Baghdad, raised over the graves of the imams Musa al-Kazim and Muhammad al-Jawad. Ibn Quluwayh's Kamil al-Ziyarat locates the grave 'in al-Kadhimiya in the noble riwaq' and dedicates a chapter to the visitation of al-Kazim and al-Jawad with its invocations and order {cite:kamil-ziyarat}.

The city of Kadhimiya grew around the shrine into Baghdad's northern district and one of its scholarly centers.
