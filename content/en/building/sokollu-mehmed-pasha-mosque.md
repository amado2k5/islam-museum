## Overview
The Sokollu Mehmed Pasha Mosque stands on the Marmara slope of the First Hill at Kadirga Limani near the Hippodrome; the grand vezir Sokollu Mehmed Pasha and his wife Esma Han Sultan, daughter of Selim II, built it in 979 AH/1571-1572 CE — among the most beautiful of Sinan's smaller mosques {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Sokollu added a medrese and a dervish tekke behind the mosque per his deed of foundation {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
