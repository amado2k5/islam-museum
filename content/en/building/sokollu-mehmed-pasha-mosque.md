## Overview
Sokollu Mehmet Pasha Camii stands on the Marmara slope of the First Hill at Kadirga Liman, just below the Hippodrome; it was built in AH 979 (1571-2) by the grand vezir Sokollu Mehmet Pasha and his wife Esma Han Sultan, daughter of Selim II, and Freely ranks it with Rüstem Pasha among the most beautiful of Sinan's smaller mosques {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.287}.

## The founder
Sokollu Mehmet Pasha was perhaps the greatest grand vezir of the sixteenth century: born in the castle of Sokol in Bosnia, taken in the devşirme and trained in the Palace School, he held office under Süleyman, Selim II and Murat III, serving continuously as grand vezir from 1564 until 1579, when he was murdered in the Divan itself {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.288}. As his deed of foundation explains, he added a medrese and a dervish tekke behind the mosque {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.287-288}.

## Architecture
The courtyard served as a medrese, the scholars living in little domed cells under the portico — each with its own door, window, fireplace and book recess — while teaching went on in the domed dershane over the stairway and in the mosque itself {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.288}. The prayer hall is a hexagon inscribed in an almost square rectangle, covered by a dome counter-balanced by corner semidomes, with a low gallery on slender marble columns around three sides {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.288}.
