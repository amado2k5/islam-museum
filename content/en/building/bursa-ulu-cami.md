## Overview
The Bursa Grand Mosque (Ulu Cami); built by Bayezid I in 1396-1399 CE in the first Ottoman capital {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely devotes a section to the mosque within Bursa's architecture, noting it was built in 1396-9 by Beyazit and that its many-domed prayer hall ranks it among the greatest Anatolian mosques of its two centuries {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
