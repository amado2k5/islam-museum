## Overview
The Süleymaniye Camii of Edirne was built in 1548 by Gazi Süleyman Pasha, a vezir under Süleyman the Magnificent {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.84}.

## Architecture
It is a single-unit mosque with a square prayer room under a hemispherical dome, once preceded by a porch of three domed bays; the minaret that rose at the north-east corner has now vanished, and all that remains of the portico are its columns and arches with red-and-white voussoirs {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.84}.
