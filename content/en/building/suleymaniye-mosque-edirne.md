## Summary
Edirne's Süleymaniye Camii was built in 1548 by Gazi Süleyman Pasha, a vezir of Süleyman the Magnificent {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.99}.

## Form
A single-unit mosque with a square prayer room under a hemispherical dome, preceded by a three-domed porch; the minaret has vanished and of the portico only the columns and arches remain, their voussoirs alternating red and white {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.99}.

## For young visitors
A 1548 mosque that lost its minaret — but kept a porch of red-and-white stone arches.
