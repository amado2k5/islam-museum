## Overview
The Monastery of Hind was built by al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir for his daughter Hind, so that she could worship in it. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 322-323}

## Al-Umari's description
Hind went out to it from her father's palace and stayed a year on the way, camping in leisure and hunting, a distance of about a farsakh. Bishr ibn Farwan dug a canal from the Euphrates, which kept flowing until the monastery fell into ruin. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 322-323}

Al-Nu'man prayed in it and drew near to God there, and he hung five hundred lamps of gold and silver in its sanctuary. It was there that the well-known story took place between al-Nu'man, Adi ibn Zayd and the wife of Hukm ibn Amr al-Lakhmi. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 322-323}
