## Overview
The Bayezid II Mosque of Istanbul, completed in 1506 CE, is Freely's exemplar of the third stage of Ottoman mosque evolution {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely reads the Bayezid mosque (1506) as the completed stage in which the bays surrounding the central space are integrated under half domes on its sides {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
