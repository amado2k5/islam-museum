## Overview
At Masjid al-Khayf in [[place/mina|Mina]], Sultan [[person/qaytbay|al-Ashraf Qaytbay]] in 874 AH / 1469 ordered a [[building/sabil-of-qaytbay-mina|sabil]] adjoining the mosque on the right of the entrance, with a yellow marble façade and four windows, one door to the road and another into the mosque. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.42}

## Water in the mosque
The mosque had an old cistern inside; Qaytbay's works added a new cistern outside feeding the inner one through a well-built lime-lined channel, re-dug a lined well opposite the gate, and whitewashed the whole mosque inside and out, as Najm al-Din Umar ibn Fahd describes in al-Haddad's quotation. {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.42} {cite:haddad-asbila-makka-madina-2004 p.43}
