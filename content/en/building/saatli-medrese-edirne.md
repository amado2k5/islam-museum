## Summary
Saatlı Medrese, the 'Medrese with a Clock', was founded in the reign of Murat II; the eponymous clock has vanished {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.97}.

## Form
A five-bay porch with a huge entryway recalls thirteenth-century Seljuk buildings of Anatolia; the medrese measures 44 by 36 metres around a porticoed courtyard — five cells north, six east, domed-square dershane and mescit south, toilets and bath in the corners {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.97}.

## For young visitors
An old college that lost its clock — but kept a gate as grand as a Seljuk palace's.
