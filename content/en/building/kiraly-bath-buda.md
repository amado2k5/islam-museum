## Overview
Building of this bath began on the order of Arslan Pasha, governor of Buda, in 1565, and was completed by Sokollu Mustafa Pasha. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 35} Under the Ottomans it was called the Rooster Gate Bath or the Gated Bath; it is now known as the King's (Király) Bath because the Király family owned it in the nineteenth century. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 35}

## The building
Its warm room consists of small niches and iwans round an octagonal pool; the large domed cold room has not survived, and like the other Buda baths it was enlarged and kept in use after Ottoman rule ended. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 35}
