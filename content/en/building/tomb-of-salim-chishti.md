## Overview
The tomb of Shaykh [[person/salim-chishti|Salim Chishti]] stands in the courtyard of the great mosque of [[place/fatehpur-sikri|Fatehpur Sikri]], the only marble building in Akbar's red sandstone palace city. [[person/jalal-al-din-akbar|Akbar]] had visited the shaykh at Sikri for his prayers for an heir, and his son Salim was born in 1569. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.133} {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.134}

## Description
[[person/john-brookes|Brookes]] says the tomb was built for the shaykh by Shah Jahan, and calls it a small building of exquisite refinement, much of its design in the Indian tradition, contrasting with its surroundings in the middle of the court. A deserted ablution pool stands in the courtyard, and in the heat mats are laid for walking barefoot, since it is a holy place where shoes are removed: the stone paving of the court is hot while the marble stays cool. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.134} {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.135}
