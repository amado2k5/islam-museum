## Overview
The Isa Bey Mosque stands at Selçuk near Ephesus; it was dedicated in 1375 (AH 777) {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman p.160}.

## In the sources
Kuran lists it among the later mosques following the Damascus-Kairawan pattern of an emphasized central aisle, alongside the Ulucami of Divriği (1230) and the Kale Mescidi of Erzurum {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman p.160}. He also notes that, with the Manisa Ulucami built a year later, it is among the rare great mosques possessing a fountain court before such courts appeared in Ottoman architecture in the mid-fifteenth century — a pair he suggests may have directly influenced their emergence {cite:kuran-mosque-early-ottoman p.176}.
