## Overview
The Hüsameddin Pasha Mosque stands in the centre of Shtip, in the Meydin (Muhyiddin) Baba quarter; its building date is not known exactly, and its founder was Hüsameddin Pasha, known as the conqueror of the Morea. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 370} The archive of the General Directorate of Foundations holds an endowment record under his name dated 1269 (1853). {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 370} Evliya Çelebi, passing through Shtip in 1072 (1661-62), mentions it briefly as an artful mosque on a hilltop, lead-roofed with a masonry minaret. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 370}

## Building and conversion
Its square prayer hall, 12.40 metres a side outside, has a single dome; in front is a three-bay portico of pointed arches on four columns, reached by a double stair of seven steps, and the qibla wall projects outward in five faces with the mihrab — features that suggest the fifteenth century. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 370} {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 371} Only the base of its minaret survives. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 370} On 2 August 1973 the Macedonian Liberation Day service began to be held there, its minaret was pulled down and it became a church; in 2006, after a long lawsuit supported by archive documents, it was returned to the Mufti's office of Eastern Macedonia. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 370} {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-1-2023 p. 371}
