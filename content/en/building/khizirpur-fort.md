## Summary
The Ancient Khizirpur Fort, now called Hajiganj Fort, is a hexagonal water fort on the east bank of the Shitalakshya river near Narayanganj, south of Dhaka.

## Story
A 2023 architectural documentation paper in IJRIAS presents the fort as one of the oldest water forts in Bengal. It places the fort on the opposite bank from Sonakanda Fort {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.96}. The fort has no inscription that dates its construction, so its Mughal attribution rests on its style and on comparison with nearby forts {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.101}.

The authors favour Subadar Mir Jumla as its builder, and note that another account credits Isha Khan {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.102}. The paper itself says the evidence is not conclusive, so the attribution is the authors' view and not a settled finding {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.103}.

## Architecture
The plan is hexagonal, with one arched gateway facing north, a tower at each of the six corners and a central courtyard that is now a playing field {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.106}{cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.108}. The walls are thick and their parapets are decorated, and the large towers held cannon platforms facing the river {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.109}.

An observation tower of ordinary brick stands inside the fort. The authors think it is a later addition, and the Department of Archaeology's 1975 publication calls it a huge circular drum {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.110}.

## Condition and conservation
The paper describes neglect, use of the grounds as a playing field and pasture, and building around the site {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.111}. A joint eviction drive by the city corporation and the Department of Archaeology cleared encroachments, but the narrow road at the entrance still threatens the site's setting {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.112}. The authors' proposals for restoring the setting, managing tourism and coordinating agencies are their own recommendations {cite:khizirpur-fort-ijrias-2023 p.113}.
