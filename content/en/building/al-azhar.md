## Overview
Al-Azhar Mosque in [[place/cairo|Cairo]] was founded by the [[polity/fatimid-caliphate|Fatimids]] as a mosque for their mission. In 378 AH the vizier [[person/yaqub-ibn-killis|Yaqub ibn Killis]] turned it into a university where the sciences and literature were taught. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.403}

## From mission to learning
The mosque was at first limited to hosting the Fatimid mission. It later became one of the most important centres of culture in Egypt, alongside the mosques of Amr, Ibn Tulun and al-Hakim. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.403}
