## Overview
The Older Monastery of Hind is the monastery of Hind the Great, daughter of al-Harith ibn Amr ibn Hujr, king of Kinda, on the edge of Najaf. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 327}

## Harun al-Rashid's visit
[[person/harun-al-rashid|Harun al-Rashid]] visited it and went in. On its wall he saw verses written in lament for the Banu al-Mundhir, and he wept. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 327}
