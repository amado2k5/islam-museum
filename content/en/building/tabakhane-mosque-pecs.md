## Overview
The mosque lies outside the walls in the former tanners' quarter by the Tettye brook, today's Ágoston Square. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 119} According to the restitution by the Hungarian archaeologist G. Győző, it had a single-domed prayer hall and a three-domed portico. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 119}

## History
The present St Augustine's Church was built on its main walls in 1712; its south wall shows the mosque's windows with their frames and arches, and the portico survives with its walls and bases, as does the upper window of the north front. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 119}
