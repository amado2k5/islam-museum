## Overview
The grave of the emperor [[person/aurangzeb-mughal|Aurangzeb]] (d. 1707) is at Khuldabad near his southern capital of Aurangabad in the Deccan, where he died while still campaigning. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.160}

## Its plainness
[[person/john-brookes|Brookes]] describes it as extremely modest beside the tombs of earlier Mughal rulers: a small square enclosure with a plain marble wall. He records that Aurangzeb spent whatever time he could spare from affairs of state copying the Qur'an by hand; the copies were sold, and he directed that only money from those sales be spent on building his tomb. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.160} Aurangabad also holds the [[building/bibi-ka-maqbara|tomb]] he built for his first wife on the model of the Taj Mahal. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.160}
