## Overview
The Babri Masjid of Ayodhya, built in the Mughal era (traditionally dated 1528) and demolished in December 1992; subject of a documented historical and legal dispute {cite:noorani-babri-2003}.

## Perspectives
- **Muslim account:** a functioning waqf mosque built under Babur that stood until its 1992 demolition {cite:noorani-babri-2003}.
- **Academic:** the site's claims are weighed through documents, excavation reports and litigation rather than any narrative taken at face value {cite:noorani-babri-2003}.
- **Hindu claim:** the site is revered as Rama's birthplace (Ramjanmabhoomi), the mosque standing where a temple had been {cite:noorani-babri-2003}.
