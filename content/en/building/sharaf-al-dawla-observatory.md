## Overview
The observation ordered by the Buyid sultan [[person/sharaf-al-dawla|Sharaf al-Dawla]] in [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] in 378 AH, on the pattern of what al-Ma'mun had done in his day. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.145}

## The observation house
Sharaf al-Dawla ordered the tracking of the seven planets in their courses and their movement through the zodiac. A solid house was built in the queen's palace at the end of the garden; observations were taken and a formal record was written, signed by those who knew geometry, affirming the fitness of the site for that house. {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.7 p.145}
