## Overview
The Cimcime Mosque stood within the walls of Timişoara in the quarter named after it; it was built before 1569 by the Timişoara treasurer Mehmed Çelebi, who endowed it with two mills and seven shops for its income, and the foundations archive holds a record of it dated 1587. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-2-2023 p. 662} {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-2-2023 p. 663}

## Its fate
After the Ottomans left Timişoara the mosque was given in 1716 to Jesuit priests and used for a time as a storehouse; it became a church in 1718, was repaired in 1754, and remained a church until 1913, when it was entirely demolished. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-2-2023 p. 662} A view of Timişoara drawn by H. Ottendorf in 1663 shows it in the Ottoman period. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-2-2023 p. 663}
