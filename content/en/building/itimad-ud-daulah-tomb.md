## Overview
The tomb of I'timad al-Dawla at [[place/agra|Agra]] was built by [[person/nur-jahan|Nur Jahan]] for her parents, as [[person/john-brookes|Brookes]] records. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.154}

## Its place in Mughal architecture
For Brookes the white marble inlay that culminated in the [[building/taj-mahal|Taj Mahal]] had already been used here. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.154} [[person/syed-ali-nadeem-rezavi|Rezavi]] counts it among the typical Mughal funerary gardens set in a [[concept/chaharbagh|chaharbagh]], {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002 p.129} and names it among the Agra buildings that show Iranian idioms such as the arch-and-panel. {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002 p.132} {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002 p.133}
