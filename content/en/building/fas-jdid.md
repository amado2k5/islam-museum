## Overview
Fez al-Jadid is the new city founded by Sultan [[person/abu-yusuf-yaqub-marini|Abu Yusuf Yaqub ibn Abd al-Haqq al-Marini]], alongside the old city of [[place/fes|Fez]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Founding
Ibn Khaldun reports that when the sultan had settled in the west, his rule was secure, and his entourage and his visitors had grown, he decided to lay out a city to house his commanders and troops. He founded the new city as the seat of the Marinid dynasty and the residence of its sultan and army. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He then ordered the building of the citadel of the city of Meknes. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
