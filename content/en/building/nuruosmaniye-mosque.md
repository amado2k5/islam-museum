## Overview
The Nuruosmaniye Mosque on the Second Hill east of Istanbul's Covered Bazaar; the first great Ottoman mosque in the baroque style, begun by Mahmut I in 1748 and finished in 1755 CE {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely notes that Mahmut I began the mosque in 1748 and his brother and successor finished it in 1755, and describes its outer courtyard with plane-trees and its two precinct entrances {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
