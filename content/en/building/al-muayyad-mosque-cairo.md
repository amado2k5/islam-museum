## Overview
The mosque of Sultan al-Mu'ayyad Shaykh stands inside Bab Zuwayla in Cairo. Al-Malik al-Mu'ayyad Abu al-Nasr Shaykh al-Mahmudi al-Zahiri built it on the site of Khizanat Shamail, where criminals were imprisoned, and of the qaysariyya of Sanqur al-Ashqar, the Darb al-Saghira, and the qaysariyya of Baha al-Din Arslan {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2143}.

## The qaysariyya
The qaysariyya of Sanqur al-Ashqar was taken into the Mu'ayyadi mosque when it was demolished in Jumada I 818 AH {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1535}.

## The minaret
Al-Maqrizi says that Baha al-Din Muhammad was in charge of the mosque's building and its upkeep, and he gives verses that were said about the tilt of the minaret over Bab Zuwayla {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2147}.
