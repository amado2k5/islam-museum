## Overview
The tomb of Timur in [[place/samarqand|Samarkand]] (1404) with its tall, ribbed blue dome; Timur, his sons, grandsons and his teacher Mir Sayyid Baraka are buried there. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It became the most famous model of Timurid architecture and inspired the tombs of the Mughals in India; its Persian name 'Gur-i Amir' means the tomb of the prince. {cite:wikidata}
