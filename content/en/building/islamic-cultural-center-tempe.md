## Overview
The Islamic Cultural Center at Tempe, Arizona, was built in 1984 by M. Afzal Ibrahim, and Omar Khalidi counts it among the "imported designs" transplanted from Islamic lands. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 4-5} {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 8-9} Its architecture and decoration were inspired by the [[building/dome-of-the-rock|Dome of the Rock]] in Jerusalem: a minaret and a golden dome attached to an eight-sided structure faced with tiles bearing Qur'anic verses in calligraphy. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 8-9}
