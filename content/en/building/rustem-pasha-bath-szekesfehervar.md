## Overview
The endowment deed of this bath records it as built on the order of the grand vizier Rüstem Pasha, while Hungarian publications ascribe it to Güzelce Rüstem Pasha; it lies within the castle of Székesfehérvár (İstolni Belgrad). {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 177}

## History
Out of use after Ottoman rule ended, it fell into ruin, and its remains show a double bath with a men's section larger than the women's; the warm room, private rooms and furnace of the men's side are exposed, and the warm room is hexagonal, rare among the baths of Anatolia and Rumelia; the author urges its protection. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 177}
