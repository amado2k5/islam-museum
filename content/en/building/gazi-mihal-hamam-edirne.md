## Summary
The hamam of Gazi Mihal stands at the eastern approach to the bridge of the same name over the Tunca; it is the oldest hamam in Edirne, now abandoned and in ruins {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.94}.

## Founder and date
An inscription dates the bath to 1406; it was built by Gazi Mihal, a Greek nobleman who converted to Islam and joined the Ottoman service — the same man who built the bridge and the mosque on the far side {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.94}.

## For young visitors
Edirne's oldest bathhouse was built in 1406 by a new Ottoman commander — and it still stands, though ruined.
