## Overview
The Mihrimah Sultan Mosque on Istanbul's Sixth Hill near the Edirne Gate; the second of two complexes Sinan built for Mihrimah, Suleyman the Magnificent's daughter, completed in 1565 CE {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely records that Sinan built two complexes for Mihrimah: the first at Üsküdar on the Asian shore of the Bosphorus, the second on the Sixth Hill of the old city, completed in 1565 {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
