## Overview
The Church of the Blessed Virgin Mary by the Elizabeth Bridge in Pest was known under the Ottomans as the Kebir (Great) Mosque; a minaret was added after it became a mosque in the name of Süleyman the Magnificent following his conquest of the castle. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 71}

## History
When Ottoman rule ended the minaret was pulled down and it became a church again; inside are a mihrab from its time as a mosque and a hand-painted inscription uncovered in the recent restoration reading “Allah, Muhammad, Ali”. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 71}
