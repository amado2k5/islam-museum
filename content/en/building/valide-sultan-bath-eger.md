## Overview
The bath stands in the street at the foot of the town wall by the castle's south gate and was built on the order of Safiye Sultan, wife of Murad III and mother of Mehmed III, the conqueror of Eger. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 165} Its remains show the classical Turkish bath plan of four iwans and four private rooms. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 165}

## History
It stayed in use after Ottoman rule ended, fell into ruin after 1856, and was protected through excavation and restoration between 1958 and 1988: its wall remains were restored under a protective cover and the piping and ventilation beneath the floor uncovered, though its domes are entirely gone. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 165} Its basin and ornamental slab are shown in the castle museum. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 165}
