## Summary
The Quwwat al-Islam Mosque is the first monumental structure of the Delhi Sultanate, built within the Qutb complex. Rezavi reads it as evidence of Iranian origins in Indo-Muslim architecture {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.2}.

## Plan
Its model is the Ghurid mosque, and it follows the Seljuq four-aiwan courtyard plan with modifications. At the mosque, a central ogee-shaped arch replaces the aiwan, flanked by two lower arches {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.3}. The author dates it to about AD 1197 and considers its carved arabesques more naturalistic than their Ghurid models {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.3}.
