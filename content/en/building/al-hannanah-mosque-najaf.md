## Summary
The al-Hannanah Mosque of Najaf, among the city's most-visited mosques of blessing {cite:musawat-atabat}.

It stands north of the city on the left of the road to Kufa, near al-Thawiyya — the burial place of many of Imam Ali's intimates {cite:musawat-atabat}.
