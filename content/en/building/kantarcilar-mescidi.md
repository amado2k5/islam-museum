## Summary
'Kantarcılar Mescidi, the Small Mosque of the Scale-Makers, is the first of the three Fatih-era mosques on the market street along the Golden Horn. Both street and mosque take their name from the scalemakers whose shops have stood here since at least 1636, when Evliya Çelebi writes of them marching in the great guild procession.' {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.148-149}

"The mosque was founded in Fatih's reign by one Sarı Demirci Mevlana Mehmet Muhittin — 'the Yellow Ironworker', a guild of ironworkers that Evliya says had their workshops here. It has been restored several times and is of little interest except for its great age." {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.149}
