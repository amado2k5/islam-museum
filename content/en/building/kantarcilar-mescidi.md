## Overview
Kantarcılar Mescidi, the "Small Mosque of the Scale-Makers", is the first of three Fatih-era mosques along the market street by the Golden Horn; it and its street are named for the scale-makers who have had their shops here since at least 1636, when Evliya Çelebi described them marching in the great procession of the guilds {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.133}.

## History
The mosque was founded during Mehmet the Conqueror's reign by one Sarı Demirci Mevlana Mehmet Muhittin — "the Yellow Ironworker", the name of a guild of ironworkers that Evliya says had their workshops here in his day; it has been restored several times and is, in Freely's judgement, of little interest except for its great age {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.133-134}.
