## Overview
In this mosque, known under the Ottomans as the Hünkar (Sultan's) Mosque and the Great Mosque, the first Friday prayer after the conquest of Buda Castle was held on 2 September 1541. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 61} The author relates that [[person/gul-baba|Gül Baba]] then asked to be washed with the water of the hot spring and buried in Buda, and died during the prayers; Süleyman the Magnificent carried his coffin and Ebussuud Efendi, chief judge of Rumelia, led the funeral prayer. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 61}

## History
Evliya Çelebi tells of an exchange between the sultan and Ebussuud as the church was converted: over the east gate was a white marble relief of a winged dragon struck by a mounted rider; when Ebussuud said that images were forbidden and should be broken, the sultan threw his Kashmir shawl over them and ordered them covered rather than broken. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 61} This is Evliya's account as the author quotes it. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 61}
