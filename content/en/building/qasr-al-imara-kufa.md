## Overview
The Government House in [[place/kufa|Kufa]]. Al-Umari says it was demolished, and not a trace of it remains. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248-249}

## A well-known account
It is related by Abd al-Malik ibn Umayr. He said that he was with Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan in this palace when the head of Mus'ab ibn al-Zubayr was brought. He told him that he had been in it with Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad when the head of al-Husayn was brought. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248-249}

He was then in it with al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd, with the head of Ibn Ziyad before him, and then with Mus'ab, with the head of al-Mukhtar before him. Abd al-Malik rose from his place and ordered that the arch be demolished. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248-249}
