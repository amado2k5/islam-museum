## Overview
The Great Mosque of Djenne in Mali is the grandest example of Sudano-Sahelian mud-brick architecture; the present building stands from the early twentieth century on the site of an older mosque {cite:prussin-hatumere-1986}.

## In the sources
Academic study describes the mosque's mud walls braced by protruding palm beams and its place in the fabric of old Djenne {cite:prussin-hatumere-1986}.
