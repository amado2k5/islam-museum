## Overview
A palace in [[place/basra|Basra]], known as the Palace of Qubadh and the Palace of al-Hajjaj. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248}

## Its history
Before the city was laid out, it was a residence where the Sasanian kings stayed during their hunting, and where the cavalry came out to their pleasure grounds. It later fell into ruin until [[person/al-hajjaj-ibn-yusuf|al-Hajjaj]] rebuilt it, and it took his name. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248}

## The contest of boasting
The famous contest of boasting between Jarir and al-Farazdaq took place in it, when al-Hajjaj told them to come to him dressed as their fathers had dressed in the days of ignorance. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248}

Al-Farazdaq wore brocade and silk and sat in a dome. Jarir wore chain mail, girded on a sword, took up a spear, and came with forty horsemen of the Banu Yarbu'. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 248}
