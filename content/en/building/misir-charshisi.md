## Overview
Mısır Çarşısı, the "Egyptian Market" — known in English as the Spice Bazaar — is the handsome L-shaped vaulted building west of the Yeni Camii in Istanbul, the market of that mosque's külliye; it is called Egyptian because it was originally endowed with the Cairo imposts {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.339}.

## History and structure
Famous for selling spices and medicinal herbs — still sold there, including a preparation known as the "Sultan's Aphrodisiac" — it now deals in many other goods and is one of the city's most popular markets {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.339-340}. It has 88 vaulted rooms in all, chambers above the entryways at the ends of its two halls, and a small mescit near the L's inner corner; above the main entrance, beside the mosque court's north-west corner, is the Pandelis Restaurant, one of the oldest and best eating places in the city {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.340}.
