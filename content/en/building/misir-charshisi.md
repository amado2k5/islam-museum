## Overview
The spice market in [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]] (1664) beside the New Mosque; the scents of cloves, cardamom and saffron have filled it since Ottoman times. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It was called 'Egyptian' because its goods came from Egypt; it remains one of the most famous spice markets in the world. {cite:wikidata}
