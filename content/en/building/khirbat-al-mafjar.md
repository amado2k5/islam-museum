## Overview
A winter palace about three miles north of Jericho, which the book attributes to the caliph [[person/hisham-ibn-abd-al-malik|Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik]]. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 159}

## What the book describes
Its walls are decorated with human and animal figures. The name of the caliph Hisham is inscribed on one of its walls. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 159}

It has panels of plant and animal designs, and images of girls dancing. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006 p. 159}
