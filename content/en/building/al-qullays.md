## Overview
Al-Qullays: the church Abraha built in Yemen and called the Yemeni Ka'ba, to divert the Arabs' pilgrimage to it — the preface to the Elephant campaign.

## The story
Once Abraha held Yemen's government, he began spreading the Christian religion with the fullest energy and widest scope, to the point that he built a church in Yemen called the Yemeni Ka'ba, intending to divert the Arabs' pilgrimage to it and to demolish God's House at Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Al-Qullays is a great church Abraha built to rival the Ka'ba at Mecca; his plan failed as the Qur'an recounted in Surat al-Fil.
