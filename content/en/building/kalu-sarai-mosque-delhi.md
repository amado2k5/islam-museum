## Overview
The Kalu Sarai mosque stands on the Mehrauli road in Delhi, in the neighbourhood of the [[building/begumpur-mosque|Begumpur mosque]]. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 90} It has no inscription, but it has been identified as a Firuzian mosque built by [[person/khan-i-jahan-junan-shah|Khan-i Jahan]], Firuz Shah Tughluq's vizier, and the author dates it to about AH 789/1387 by analogy with his contemporary mosques. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 90}

## Fabric and state
It is built of rubble and mortar on a high plinth and roofed with cupolas, in the same Firuzian manner. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 90} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 95} Its north and south halls have been destroyed and only part of it remains; when the author surveyed it she found no inscription and no entrance gateway, and people living in it rather than praying there. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 90} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 92} At the same point she reproduces Zafar Hasan's and Syed Ahmad Khan's description of an octagonal building on a high plinth, which Syed Ahmad Khan calls Badi Manzil and, on the authority of the *Akhbar al-Akhyar*, a bastion of Jahanpanah where Shaykh Hasan Tahir lived under Sikandar Lodi and was buried nearby; how that building relates to the mosque is not clear in her text. {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 90} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 91} {cite:seema-khan-mosque-architecture-firuzshah-tughlaq-2011 p. 92}
