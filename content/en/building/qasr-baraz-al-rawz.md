## Overview
The palace of Baraz al-Rawz lay near Baghdad; al-Mu'tadid erected fine buildings at Baraz al-Rawz, and in AH 287 he entered Baghdad from his pleasure park there and ordered a palace built at a site he had chosen, the equipment being brought and work begun {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v12 pp.335,410}.

## In the sources
Ibn al-Jawzi relates that al-Mu'tadid found Baghdad's air unwholesome because of the markets' smoke, so he staked out and built al-Thurayya, joined it to the Hasani palace and moved into it, and erected fine buildings at Baraz al-Rawz; when he fell ill in his last days he ordered a palace above al-Shammasiyya for the clean air but died before it was finished {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi v12 p335}.
