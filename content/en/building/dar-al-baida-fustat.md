## Overview
Ibn Abd al-Hakam says that Abd al-Rahman ibn Udays al-Balawi laid out the White House; others say it was an open court in front of the mosque and the house of [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]], where the Muslims' horses stood at the mosque door. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 152}

## Marwan's house
When [[person/marwan-ibn-al-hakam|Marwan ibn al-Hakam]] came to Egypt in the year 65 he built it as his own house, saying a caliph should not be in a land where he had no house, and it was built for him in two months. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 152} An addition in one manuscript says it was built in forty days and that it collapsed all at once in the days of al-Mahdi, a full hundred years later. {cite:futuh-misr-amir-1999 p. 152}
