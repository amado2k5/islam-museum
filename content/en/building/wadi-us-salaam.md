## Overview

The Wadi-us-Salaam cemetery of Najaf, among the widest and most continuously used burial grounds in the Islamic world. Nuzhat al-Ghari relates that the believer's soul is settled in Wadi-us-Salaam, and records the reading 'Wadi al-Salama' — valley of safety for whomever is buried in it {cite:nuzhat-ghari-najaf}.

Its nearness to the shrine of Ali ibn Abi Talib made it the preferred burial ground for Twelver Shi'a, and generations of Najaf's scholars and people lie in it.
