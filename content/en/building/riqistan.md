## Overview
The famous square of [[place/samarqand|Samarkand]], framed by three Timurid madrasas covered in mosaic: Ulugh Beg (1420), Sher-Dor and Tilya-Kari; it was the centre of education and trade in the city. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The most famous architectural showpiece of Central Asia; its image stands for everything that represents Islamic heritage in Uzbekistan. {cite:wikidata}
