## Overview
The covered old souq of [[place/aleppo|Aleppo]], the longest continuous historic market in the world at about 13 kilometres of lanes. Much of it burned in the war of 2012 and has been partly restored. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Among the UNESCO sites of old Aleppo; what was destroyed has been partly restored, and some of its khans have returned to work. {cite:wikidata}
