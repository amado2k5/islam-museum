## Overview
A mosque in a cave on Mount Qasyun in [[place/damascus|Damascus]], called the Cave of Gabriel. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 209}

## Its construction
Abu al-Faraj Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Mu'allim began building it in 370 AH. The report says that he saw Gabriel in a dream, ordering him to build a mosque in which God would be prayed to and remembered. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 209}

## Its description
Al-Umari describes it as a spacious mosque, paved with white marble, with all its columns of marble. He adds that it is never without the presence of the righteous, and is kept full of remembrance. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 209}

## What needs a source
The dream is a report transmitted by al-Umari alone, and it needs an additional source.
