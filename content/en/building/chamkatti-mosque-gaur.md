## Overview
The Chamkatti mosque stands on the west side of the Dinajpur road a little east of the Lukachuri Gate, the eastern entrance of the citadel of Gaur; with the Rajbibi and Lattan mosques it forms a connected group of square domed mosques. {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 115} Its name links it with a group of mendicants who tortured themselves and were called Chamkattis, "skin-cutters", and some say Yusuf Shah built it in honour of a famous faqir who performed before him. {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 115} {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 116} The author notes that this sultan, known as just and virtuous, prohibited wine and built three mosques at Gaur: Chamkatti (AH 883), [[building/darasbari-mosque|Darasbari]] (884) and [[building/tantipara-mosque-gaur|Tantipara]] (885). {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 116}

## The building
It is a cubical building whose inner chamber is 23 feet 8 inches each way, buttressed by octagonal corner towers; its brick walls were faced with stone, and it measures 50 feet 4 inches by 33 feet 8 inches overall. {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 116} {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 117} Brick squinches lead from square to octagon, the octagon carries a sixteen-sided gallery, and the dome rises in three receding stages. {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 117} To the square plan begun by the Eklakhi tomb it adds an eastern verandah 9 feet 11 inches wide with three arches, tunnel-vaulted at the centre and groin-vaulted at the sides; the chain-and-bell motif dominates the facade, and the cornice is curved as in most Bengali mosques. {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 117} Its inscription of AH 883 is in the British Museum and was republished by the author in 1966. {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 117} {cite:hasan-mosque-architecture-pre-mughal-bengal-1979 p. 123}
