## Overview
A palace in the upper northern district of [[place/damascus|Damascus]], attributed to Heraclius. In the time of Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari it was known as the Palace of the Sun of Kings. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 250}

## Its condition in al-Umari's time
Only the castle and the bath remained. The castle had become a hospice for the poor. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 250}

It was still a residence for kings and a pleasure ground for the people of the city, because it overlooks the river Barada and the valley. Sultan Salah al-Din stayed in it. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 250}
