## Summary
The al-Askari shrine at Samarra, holding the graves of Ali al-Hadi and Hasan al-Askari {cite:musawat-atabat}.

## Building history
The Mawsu'at al-Atabat al-Muqaddasa (Samarra section), citing Tarikh Shaykh al-Sarra, records that Nasir al-Dawla the Hamdanid covered the grave with curtains in 333; Mu'izz al-Dawla the Buyid then raised the dome and sirdab and appointed caretakers; Adud al-Dawla fenced the rawda in teak in 368; Amir Arslan restored dome and grave in 445; Barkyaruq the Seljuk renewed the doors in 495; and the Abbasid al-Nasir rebuilt dome and minarets, inscribing the Twelve Imams' names on the arch ring in 606 — followed by the Jalayirid Abu Uways Hasan (748–750), the Safavid Shah Husayn (1106) and Ahmad al-Dunbuli al-Barmaki (1200) {cite:musawat-atabat}.
