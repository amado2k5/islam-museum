## Overview
The Rustem Pasha Mosque is among the most beautiful of Sinan's vezirial mosques, standing in the ancient market quarter by the Golden Horn above the Galata Bridge; its foundation charter dates it to 968 AH/1561 CE, the year its founder died {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Rustem Pasha rose to be Suleyman's grand vezir in 1544-1553 and again 1555-1561; the mosque is famed for its Iznik tilework {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
