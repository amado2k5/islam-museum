## Overview
The Hagia Sophia of Iznik (Nicaea) stands at the intersection of the town's two main streets; the most important surviving monument of Byzantine Nicaea, it was converted into a mosque after the Turkish conquest and is now open as an archaeological site {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.55}.

## Context
Orhan Gazi took Iznik in 1331 after it had been Byzantine, and he and his vezirs then built mosques, medreses, hamams and tombs in a town that in the empire's early years ranked second only to Bursa {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.54-55}.
