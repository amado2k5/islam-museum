## Overview
The Sultan Ahmed Mosque — the Blue Mosque — is among the last imperial mosques of the classical Ottoman age; Ahmed I had the architect Mehmet Agha begin it in 1609 east of the ancient Hippodrome, and it was completed with its pious foundations in 1616 {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely describes it as among the city's most splendid mosques, with its cascade of domes and semidomes and six slender minarets accentuating the corners {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
