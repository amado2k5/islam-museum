## Overview
The Albanian Islamic Center at Harper Woods, Michigan, was founded by Albanian Muslim immigrants and built in 1962 by Frank Beymer. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 12-13} The catalogue places it among the "imported designs": its Ottoman exterior, sleek arches, dome and colour scheme make a clear statement of the mosque's national character. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 12-13}
