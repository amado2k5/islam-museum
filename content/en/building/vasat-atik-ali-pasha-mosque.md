## Overview
The Vasat Atik Ali Pasha Mosque — known to Freely as Zincirli Kuyu Camii — stands on the Fifth Hill of Istanbul; it was founded around 1500 by Hadım Ali Pasha, grand vezir of Bayezit II {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.203}.

## Architecture
It is a small rectangular building of brick and stone covered by six equal domes in two rows of three supported on two rectangular piers; its original three-bay porch had vanished and has been reconstructed, a reconstruction Freely calls hideous, while the minaret rises at the north-west corner {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.204}. Its interest is as a tiny example of the Seljuk-borrowed Ulu Cami type common in the first or Bursa period of Ottoman architecture — a space covered by a multiplicity of equal domes on piers — though Freely finds it heavy and oppressive at this small scale {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.204}.
