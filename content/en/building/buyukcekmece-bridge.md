## Summary
The Bridge of Süleyman the Magnificent at Büyükçekmece is among the most notable monuments on the Edirne–Istanbul road; Sinan erected it in 1566–7 where highway 100 crosses a huge inlet of the Marmara {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.109-110}. It is a splendid structure of four linked spans with a total of 28 arches {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.110}.

## The roadside group
Five Ottoman monuments stand at Büyükçekmece: the oldest, Fatih Camii, built by Mehmet II soon after the conquest of Constantinople; the other four are all by Sinan — a caravansarai and this bridge for Süleyman, and a medrese and mescit for Sokollu Mehmet Pasha {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.109-110}.

## For young visitors
A bridge of twenty-eight arches Sinan built more than 450 years ago still carries the great west road.
