## Overview
Ahmad ibn Tulun built the mosque in al-Askar, beside the upper police headquarters (al-Shurta al-Ulya) {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.881}.

## Site and building
He built his mosque on the hill of Yashkur, and there he carried out a great building programme {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.882}.

## The divan
When al-Askar became the residence of Egypt's emirs, the divan (the government office) moved to Ibn Tulun's mosque. Under al-Aziz billah it moved again, to the house of the vizier Ya'qub ibn Killis {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.279}.
