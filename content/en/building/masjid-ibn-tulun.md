## Overview
The oldest mosque in [[place/cairo|Cairo]] that survives in its original form (879), built by Ahmad ibn Tulun; a wide courtyard and an external spiral minaret in the Abbasid Samarra style. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It has survived almost all the major restorations; it stands today as a living model of early Abbasid architecture in Egypt. {cite:wikidata}
