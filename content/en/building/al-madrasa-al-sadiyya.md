## Overview
The Sadiyya madrasa in [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]] was built by Prince Nasr ibn Sabuktigin, brother of the sultan [[person/mahmud-ghaznawi|Mahmud of Ghazna]]. {cite:duha-islam p.67}

## Cited as evidence
Al-Subki and al-Suyuti cited it, together with the [[building/al-madrasa-al-bayhaqiyya|Bayhaqiyya]], to show that madrasas existed before Nizam al-Mulk. {cite:duha-islam p.67}
