## Overview
The Yeşil Direkli ("Green Column") Bath lies on the bank of the Danube facing Gürz İlyas (Gellért) Hill and was built on the order of Sokollu Mustafa Pasha, governor of Buda, in 1556. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 45} It is the most sumptuous Turkish thermal bath in Hungary, with a dome ten metres across, and its water comes from the Cave of Gül Baba just across the road. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 45}

## Interior and present state
The interior Evliya Çelebi described — eight high-arched great domes round a grand pool — survives intact. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 45} It was enlarged with outbuildings after Ottoman rule ended and still serves as a bath. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 45}
