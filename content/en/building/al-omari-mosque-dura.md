## Summary
The Omari Mosque in [[place/dura|Dura]], Hebron Governorate, is an old mosque in the heart of the old town, named by tradition after the caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab. It stands on the remains of earlier buildings and combines Mamluk and Ottoman features; the sources record no precise construction date. {cite:dura-municipality} {cite:riwaq-register}

## Fabric and role
The two-storey building of dressed stone is roofed by a semi-barrel vault on stone arches and crowned by a dome, with a tall prominent mihrab. In the late Ottoman and Mandate periods it was a centre of scholarly debate and teaching in Dura. It remains fully in use for worship, rated in good structural and physical condition in RIWAQ's register of historic buildings. {cite:riwaq-register} {cite:dura-municipality}
