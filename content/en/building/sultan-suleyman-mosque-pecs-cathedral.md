## Overview
The cathedral stands in the former inner fortress of Pécs; built in the eleventh century, it served under the Ottomans as a mosque in the name of Süleyman the Magnificent. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 105} Evliya Çelebi praised it lavishly, describing walls carved and set with precious stones within and without, and saying its arches, windows and marbles could be matched only by those of al-Aqsa in Jerusalem. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 105}

## History
The cathedral, some 22 by 70 metres, has towers at its four corners, one of which is said to have been built under a wooden minaret in Ottoman times. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 105} The only trace of that period inside is a repair inscription of Ahmed Ağa of Belgrade to the left of the entrance, among the timbers of the bell. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 105}
