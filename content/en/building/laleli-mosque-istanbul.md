## Overview
The Laleli ('Lily') Mosque on Istanbul's Third Hill; founded by Mustafa III and built between 1759 and 1763 CE by Mehmet Tahir Ağa {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely calls it perhaps the best of the baroque mosques and describes the high terrace above a labyrinth of winding passages and vaulted shops {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
