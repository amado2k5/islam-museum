## Overview
The Ahmadiyya (Ahmediye) Mosque stands in Akhaltsikhe (Ahıska), Georgia; it was built about 1749 by Sefer Paşazâde el-Hac Ahmed Pasha, governor of Çıldır {cite:yilmaz-turkish-monuments-churches-2023 pp.252-253}.

## Fate
It was converted into a church in 1828 with the end of Ottoman rule in the region, and is today the Castle Museum, known also as the Ahmediye Mosque {cite:yilmaz-turkish-monuments-churches-2023 pp.252-253}.
