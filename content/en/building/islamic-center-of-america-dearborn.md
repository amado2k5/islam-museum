## Overview
The Islamic Center of America at Dearborn, Michigan, was built in 2005 by Alan Abbas and Luna Construction to designs by Paul Bertin and David Donnellon, one of the exhibition's two authors, and the catalogue calls it the largest mosque in the United States. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue credits} {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 8-9}

## Architecture
The catalogue places it among the "imported designs": its golden domes, Moorish arches and two 110-foot minarets topped with crescents of solid brass represent traditional Islamic architecture, and the central dome rises almost 20 metres. {cite:khalidi-donnellon-mosques-usa-canada-catalogue pp. 8-9}
