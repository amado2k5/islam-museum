## Overview
The Kamiliya madrasa in Cairo, known as Dar al-Hadith, adjoins the new Zahiriyya madrasa {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1062}.

## Where it stood
It lay opposite Bab al-Bahr, the gate known today as Bab Qasr Bashtak {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1032}.

## Its endowment
On the left of the passer-by in the market of the cloth-sellers, there is a rented shop-block that belongs to the endowment of the Kamiliya madrasa {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.1063}.
