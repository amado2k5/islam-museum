## Overview
The Ali Bitchin Mosque stands on Bab El-Oued Street in Algiers; it was built in 1623 {cite:yilmaz-turkish-monuments-churches-2023 p.218}.

## Fate
Under the French occupation its minaret was demolished and it was converted into a church, Notre Dame (1843-1962), losing most of its original features; after Algeria's independence its minaret was rebuilt and it became a mosque again {cite:yilmaz-turkish-monuments-churches-2023 pp.218-219}.
