## Summary
al-Haram al-Sharif is the sanctuary esplanade of the Aqsa mosque in Jerusalem, holding the Dome of the Rock, its domes and arcades; al-Muqaddasi describes it in Ahsan al-Taqasim. The name is taken from an open reference description (Wikidata), and its details are not yet checked on this page {cite:wikidata}.

## In the sources
The Aqsa mosque within this sanctuary is named in the verse of the Night Journey, and the sources of the Sunnah, the seerah and the history books carry reports of Bayt al-Maqdis and its surroundings; this place's page in the Sacred Places Browser gathers those mentions grouped by corpus.
