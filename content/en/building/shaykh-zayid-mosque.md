## Overview
The Grand Mosque of Abu Dhabi, opened in 2007 and named after the founder of the UAE, Sheikh Zayed; it has 82 white domes, four minarets and the largest hand-made prayer carpet in the world. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The largest mosque in the Emirates and one of the largest in the world; it became a symbol of contemporary Islamic architecture and a destination for millions of visitors. {cite:wikidata}
