## Summary
The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi, capital of the United Arab Emirates, is the country's largest mosque and its main place of daily prayer. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} It was built between 1994 and 2007 and inaugurated in December 2007. The project was launched by the late president [[person/zayed-bin-sultan-al-nahyan|Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan]], who wanted a building that would unite the cultural diversity of the Islamic world with historical and modern values of architecture and art, and who was buried in its courtyard after his death in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en}

## Design and construction
The mosque was designed under the management of the Syrian architect Yusef Abdelki, with three other Syrian designers who completed and developed the design: Basem Barghouti, Moataz al-Halabi and Imad Malas. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} Abdelki drew on several sources, among them the Abu al-Abbas al-Mursi Mosque in Alexandria and the [[building/badshahi-mosque|Badshahi Mosque]] in Lahore, from which the dome layout and floor plan were taken, and on Persian, Mughal and Indo-Islamic architecture; its arches are Moorish in character and its minarets Arab. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} A joint venture of the Italian contractors Impregilo and Rizzani de Eccher built it with more than 3,000 workers, and a joint venture of ACC and Six Construct of the Belgian BESIX group completed it between 2004 and 2007. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} Its main axis is turned about 12 degrees south of true west to face the Kaaba in [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en}

## Size and capacity
The complex measures about 290 by 420 metres over more than 12 hectares. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} The mosque centre's site gives a capacity of over 40,100 worshippers, over 7,000 of them in the main prayer hall, with two smaller halls of 1,500 each, one for women; the article's infobox gives more than 41,000. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} It has 82 domes of seven sizes and four corner minarets about 107 metres high by the centre's site, 104 by the infobox. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} Its courtyard of about 17,000 square metres of floral marble mosaic, designed by the British artist Kevin Dean, is considered the largest example of marble mosaic in the world. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en}

## Materials and decoration
The exterior is clad in Sivec marble from Prilep in North Macedonia, the interior in Italian Lasa marble and the annexes in Indian Makrana, and the marble is inlaid with lapis lazuli, carnelian, amethyst and mother of pearl. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} The main prayer hall's carpet, made by the Iran Carpet Company to a design by the artist Ali Khaliqi, measures 5,627 square metres, was knotted by about 1,200 to 1,300 weavers in some two years, and weighs 35 tonnes. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} Seven chandeliers come from the Faustig company of Munich, the largest 10 metres across and 15 high. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} The ninety-nine names of God appear on the qibla wall in Kufic script, and the mosque's Naskh, Thuluth and Kufic inscriptions were drafted by Mohammed Mandi al-Tamimi of the UAE, Farouk Haddad of Syria and Mohammed Allam of Jordan. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} The lighting designers Speirs and Major made its exterior lighting follow the phases of the moon. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en}

## Today
The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Center runs the mosque from offices in the western minarets, with a multilingual library in the north-eastern minaret. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} TripAdvisor ranked it the world's second favourite landmark two years running, as The National reported in 2017. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} In November 2022 a smaller replica opened in Surakarta, Indonesia. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en} It has Wikipedia articles in fifty-four languages. {cite:wikipedia-sheikh-zayed-grand-mosque-en}
