## Overview
This mosque at Feres (Ferecik), on the Turkish-Greek border, was built by Musa Çelebi, son of Sultan Bayezid I, between 1410 and 1413, when he was active in Western Thrace. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-3-2023 p. 1034} Evliya Çelebi, touring the region in 1667, records it as the mosque of Sultan Musa Çelebi son of Yıldırım Bayezid Khan. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-3-2023 p. 1034} The author counts it among the early Ottoman buildings and notes from satellite images that it is aligned to the qibla. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-3-2023 p. 1034}

## Conversion
After the population exchange of 1923 it was made a church; a photograph of the dedication of the church of St Nicholas in 1925 shows its facades of bare cut stone. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-3-2023 p. 1034} Originally it had a minaret and a gabled roof without a dome; the conversion altered its features, especially the facades, renewed the roof, demolished the minaret and added a bell tower. {cite:yilmaz-kiliseye-cevrilen-turk-eserleri-3-2023 p. 1034}
