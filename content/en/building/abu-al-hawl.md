## Overview
The Sphinx is a huge statue near the Great Pyramid at [[place/al-ahram-giza|the Pyramids of Giza]], in a low area to the east. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari describes it as an eyewitness. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.238}

## Al-Umari's description
Only its head and neck show above the ground; it looks like the head of an Abyssinian monk wearing a hood. Its face has a red colouring that has lasted through the ages. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.238}

Al-Umari says that if it stood up, its height would not be less than twenty cubits. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.238}

## What is said about it
It is said to be a talisman that keeps sand from the cultivated land, since it stands at the edge of the sand on the side of the farmland. This is a report attributed to al-Umari with the words "it is said". {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.238}
