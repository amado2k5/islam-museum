## Overview
The old souq of [[place/muscat|Muscat]] on the corniche; frankincense, kandar, daggers and incense for centuries of Omani maritime trade. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
One of the oldest surviving markets in the Gulf; the smell of frankincense still greets visitors before they see the shops. {cite:wikidata}
