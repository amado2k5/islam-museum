## Overview
A heritage market in Doha on the bank of the old valley: spices, falcons, cafés and an open-air theatre, restored by Qatar from 2006 to return the city's heritage centre to life. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It became the heart of heritage life in modern Doha; a living market, not a closed museum. {cite:wikidata}
