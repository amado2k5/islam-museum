## Overview
The bath lies about 500 metres south of Eger castle; built in the first years of the seventeenth century, it was founded according to the author by Abdurrahman Abdi Pasha, the last governor of Buda. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 168}

## History
It was enlarged with buildings added round it after the Ottoman period and is still in use; its main hall is octagonal and domed, with a hot thermal pool. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 168}
