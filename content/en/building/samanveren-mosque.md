## Overview
Samanveren Camii, the "Mosque of the Inspector of Straw", stands uphill from Ağızlıkçı Sokağı in Istanbul; it was erected by a certain Sinan Ağa, who in Mehmet the Conqueror's time was inspector of straw, whence its name {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.130-131}.

## Architecture
It is a quaint building of brick-and-stone construction with curious leaf-like brick decorations on what survives of its original minaret; the present minaret, whose arcaded loggia for the müezzin replaces a şerefe, is part of a modern restoration {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.131}. The mosque itself sits on the first floor over a vaulted ground storey, and is a near-twin of the neighbouring Timurtaş Camii; both belong to the age of Fatih, though the exact date of neither is known {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 pp.131-132}.
