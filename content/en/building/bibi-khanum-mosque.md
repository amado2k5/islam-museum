## Overview
The great mosque of Timur in [[place/samarqand|Samarkand]] (1404), which he intended to be the greatest mosque in the world; its gate and two minarets remain among the most massive monuments of the Timurid age. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The people of Samarkand say its dome was the dome of heaven; parts of it collapsed and it was restored in the twentieth century. {cite:wikidata}
