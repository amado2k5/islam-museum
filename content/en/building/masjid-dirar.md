## Summary
A mosque built by twelve men Ibn Ishaq names; while the Prophet was preparing for Tabuk they asked him to pray in it and he put them off until his return; then, when word of it reached him at Dhu Awan, he ordered its demolition and burning. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173-174}

## The builders and the request
Ibn Ishaq names its builders, [[group/builders-of-masjid-al-dirar|the mosque's men]] — twelve men. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174} Their asking the Prophet to pray in it is in [[event/masjid-al-dirar-builders-ask-the-prophet-to-pray|the request event]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/173}

## The demolition
The Prophet ordered its demolition and burning; Malik ibn al-Dukhshum and Ma'n ibn Adi carried it out — the account is in [[event/demolition-of-masjid-al-dirar|the demolition event]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## The name
Ibn Ishaq mentions that the builders' mosque of "al-shiqaq" (dissension) was removed from Khidham ibn Khalid's house; the two names appear to be of one building in the account's context, and linking them is the museum's reading of the text's wording (needs_source). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/174}

## Source notes and markers of doubt
- The verse: the book contents itself with its opening then "to the end of the story" (p. 174); the museum linked 9:107 explicitly and 9:108 within the wording.
- The mosque's location is not specified in the text.
