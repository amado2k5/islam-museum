## Summary
The Salahiyya Madrasa in [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]] was founded in 588 AH (1192) by Sultan Salah al-Din in the Crusader convent and annexed Church of Saint Anne, after he recovered the city, converting the complex into a college for Shafi'i scholars. {cite:archnet} {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

## History
The Crusaders built the site as a church and convent in the twelfth century; Salah al-Din made it a teaching endowment, appointed masters and students, and endowed it with property. It kept its religious and teaching role for centuries — one of the first Ayyubid endowments in Jerusalem after its recovery. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim} {cite:tei-epigraphie}
