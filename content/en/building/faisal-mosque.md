## Overview
A mosque in Islamabad completed in 1986, designed by the Turkish architect Vedat Dalokay in the form of a Bedouin tent without a dome; it is named after King Faisal, who funded it. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It was the largest mosque in the world when it opened and is still the fourth largest; its shape differs from every mosque tradition the Islamic world has known. {cite:wikidata}
