## Overview
The house of [[person/al-arqam-ibn-abi-al-arqam|al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam]] of Banu Makhzum, on [[place/al-safa|al-Safa]] in Mecca. The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] used it as the centre of his mission and as the place where he gathered the Muslims, from the fifth year of prophethood. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.91} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.92}

## Why the Prophet chose it
Gathering in secret was necessary at the start of the mission. If the polytheists had learned that the Muslims were gathering openly, they would have stood between the Prophet and the training and teaching of his companions, and it would have led to open conflict. The house of al-Arqam was out of the sight of the tyrants and their assemblies, so the Prophet gathered the Muslims there. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.91} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.92}

## What is not covered here
The book does not describe the house or its exact location on al-Safa, nor what happened there after the Hijra. These are dealt with in their own places.

## For young visitors
The house of al-Arqam was a home on the hill of al-Safa, where the Prophet met his companions in secret while people were afraid of Quraysh.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy describes Dar al-Arqam in Mecca as the place where Muslims understood the question of tawhid over thirteen years, and holds that the land of Mecca at the start of the call grew mercy, affection and brotherhood. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:14:30}

The Sheikh describes the land of Mecca at the start of the call as a land in which feelings of mercy, affection and brotherhood grew, and he treats Dar al-Arqam as the place where tawhid was understood in those years. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:14:30}

The Sheikh lists the fruits of this environment: Uthman ibn Affan, who spent generously; Abu Bakr, who gave all his wealth; Umar ibn al-Khattab, who spoke the truth; and Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud, who recited the Qur'an and was true to the Book of God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:15:00}

The Sheikh counts these men as the fruit of that land, which grew mercy and affection, after Muslims had understood tawhid in it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:15:00}
