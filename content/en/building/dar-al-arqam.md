## Overview
The house of [[person/al-arqam-ibn-abi-al-arqam|al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam]] of Banu Makhzum, on [[place/al-safa|al-Safa]] in Mecca. The [[person/muhammad|Prophet]] used it as the centre of his mission and as the place where he gathered the Muslims, from the fifth year of prophethood. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.91} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.92}

## Why the Prophet chose it
Gathering in secret was necessary at the start of the mission. If the polytheists had learned that the Muslims were gathering openly, they would have stood between the Prophet and the training and teaching of his companions, and it would have led to open conflict. The house of al-Arqam was out of the sight of the tyrants and their assemblies, so the Prophet gathered the Muslims there. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.91} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.92}

## What is not covered here
The book does not describe the house or its exact location on al-Safa, nor what happened there after the Hijra. These are dealt with in their own places.

## For young visitors
The house of al-Arqam was a home on the hill of al-Safa, where the Prophet met his companions in secret while people were afraid of Quraysh.
