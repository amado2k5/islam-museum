## Overview
A football stadium in Lusail City, north of Doha, with a seating capacity of 88,966. It is the largest venue by capacity of the 2022 World Cup. {cite:qfa-lusail-stadium}

## Design
Its design is inspired by the interplay of light and shadow in traditional Arab lanterns. {cite:qfa-lusail-stadium}

## Matches
It hosted the 2022 FIFA World Cup final and the 2025 Arab Cup final. {cite:qfa-lusail-stadium}
