## Overview
The mosque took Hamza Bey's name from the palanka (redoubt) he built in Ottoman times; it was built under Süleyman the Magnificent and repaired by İsmail Pasha, governor of Buda, in the 1660s. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 189}

## History
Abandoned after Turkish rule in Hungary ended, it was destroyed by a flood in 1838, leaving only its cut-stone minaret, which K. Ferenczy restored in 1970-72, renewing the damaged parts and building a symbolic mihrab of concrete. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 189} It is one of the three Turkish minarets surviving in Hungary. {cite:yilmaz-ottoman-architecture-hungary-2024 p. 189}
