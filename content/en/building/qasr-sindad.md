## Overview
A palace in Iraq near the Nile, in the land of al-Azir on the river Sindad. It was the home of the Al Muhriq. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 229}

## In poetry
Al-Aswad ibn Ya'fur composed his famous ode in which he mentions the Al Muhriq and Iyad, the people of al-Khawarnaq and al-Sadir and Ma'rib, and the palace of battlements on Sindad. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p. 229}
