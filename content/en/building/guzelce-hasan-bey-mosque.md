## Summary
Güzelce ('Handsome') Hasan Bey Camii is Hayrabolu's principal monument, built in 1406 for a son-in-law of Beyazit I {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.106}.

## Form
The mosque is preceded by an avlu, one of the earliest mosque courtyards in Ottoman architecture; its peripheral arcade stood on rectangular piers — three on the sides, four at the ends — but the collapsed upper structure leaves it uncertain whether it was domed or vaulted {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.106}. The court's north entrance is covered by a domed canopy Kuran considers unique in early Ottoman architecture {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.106}. The probably-later minaret rises at the north-west; the main prayer hall carries a high dome on pendentives over a windowless dodecagonal drum, flanked by four smaller domed chambers on Turkish triangles that served as dervish hospices {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.106-107}.

## For young visitors
A 1406 mosque with one of the oldest mosque courtyards anywhere — and little side rooms where travelling dervishes slept.
