## Overview
The Blue Mosque in [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]] (1616), with six minarets and twenty thousand İznik tiles covering its walls in blue; Sedefkar Mehmed Agha built it for Sultan Ahmed I opposite Hagia Sophia. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
When it was built it was the only Ottoman mosque with six minarets, which caused controversy for rivalling the Haram in Mecca; today it is the most famous Islamic tourist landmark in Turkey. {cite:wikidata}
