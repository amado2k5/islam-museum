## Overview
The Great Bazaar of [[place/isfahan|Isfahan]] around the Naqsh-e Jahan square; its covered lanes stretch for kilometres from the square to the old mosque. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The heart of Safavid trade; it still bustles with coppersmiths, weavers and sellers of miniatures. {cite:wikidata}
