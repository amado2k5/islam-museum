## Overview
Jami al-Qarafa, today called Jami al-Awliya, also known as the Mosque of the Dome {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2437} {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2456}.

## Name
The mosque is named after the Banu Abd Allah ibn Manih ibn Mazrub {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2456}.

## Building and renewal
In his account of the Qarafa palace that Sayyida Tafrid, mother of al-Aziz billah, built in 366 AH, al-Maqrizi says that she also built the Jami al-Qarafa. Al-Amir bi-Ahkam Allah later renewed it and whitewashed it in 520 AH {cite:khitat-maqrizi-1998 p.2463}.
