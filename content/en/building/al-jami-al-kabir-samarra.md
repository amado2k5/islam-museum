## Overview
The Great Mosque of [[place/samarra|Samarra]], built by al-Mu'tasim and al-Mutawakkil in 851. In its time it was the largest mosque in the world with its wide courtyard; its spiral minaret, the famous Malwiya, remains. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
A symbol of Samarra and of Abbasid architecture; the image of the Malwiya was printed on the Iraqi currency. {cite:wikidata}
