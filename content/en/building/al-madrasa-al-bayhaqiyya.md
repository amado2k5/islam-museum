## Overview
The Bayhaqiyya madrasa in [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]] is, according to al-Maqrizi, the first madrasa whose founding is recorded in Islam. {cite:duha-islam p.67-68}

## Al-Maqrizi's statement
Al-Maqrizi says that the people of Nishapur are the first in whom it is recorded that they built a madrasa, and that the Bayhaqiyya was built there. {cite:duha-islam p.67-68}

## The reply to al-Dhahabi
Al-Subki and al-Suyuti used it to answer al-Dhahabi's claim that [[person/nizam-al-mulk|Nizam al-Mulk]] was the first to establish madrasas. Their argument is that it existed before Nizam al-Mulk was born. {cite:duha-islam p.67-68}

## The builder
It was built by the people of the city, or some of them, in honour of Bayhaq ibn Abi Bayhaq, the governor who held standing there. {cite:duha-islam p.67-68}
