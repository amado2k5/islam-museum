## Overview
The Fatih Mosque was the first great sultanic complex of Ottoman Istanbul; Mehmet the Conqueror chose its site on the Fourth Hill and began it about a decade after the conquest, built by the architect Atik Sinan; the original was destroyed in the earthquake of 22 May 1766 and rebuilt {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
Freely notes the Conqueror led the way himself with an enormous complex known as Fatih Camii, and his vezirs followed, erecting mosque complexes across the city {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
