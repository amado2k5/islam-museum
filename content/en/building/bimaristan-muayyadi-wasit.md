## Overview
A bimaristan opened at Wasit on Tuesday with fifteen nights remaining of Dhu'l-Qa'da 413; built by Mu'ayyid al-Mulk Abu 'Ali al-Hasan al-Rakji, minister of Musharraf al-Dawla — drugs and syrups were carried to it, a keeper, physicians and agents installed, and endowments settled on it {cite:muntazam-ibn-al-jawzi j.8 p.350}. At its founder's death in 434 it was recalled that he had founded this bimaristan; many censured him — "he wastes wealth on no purpose" — yet his end was praised.
