## Summary
Kara Ahmet Pasha Camii — one of the most masterful and lovely of Sinan's works per Freely — stands on the Seventh Hill just inside Top Kapı of the Theodosian Walls; it was built for Gazi Kara Ahmet Pasha, who succeeded Rüstem Pasha as grand vezir in 1553 only to be executed by Süleyman two years later {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.297}.

## Construction
Per Aptullah Kuran, construction began in 1558, the founder's small türbe went up the same year, and mosque and medrese were finished in the early 1560s {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011 p.297-298}.

## For young visitors
A sultan executed his grand vezir, yet the architect Sinan completed the vezir's mosque after his death — it remains among Istanbul's loveliest.
