## Overview
A well-known palace in the city of [[place/zafar|Zafar]] in Yemen, the seat of the Yahsib. In "Masalik al-Absar" the city was once called by its name. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.233}

## What the sources say
The text suggests that it may be the palace of Raydan, which al-Idrisi mentions in Zafar. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002 p.152}

The data gives no description of the palace's architecture or its date of construction: an additional source is needed.
