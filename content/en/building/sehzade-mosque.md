## Overview
The Shehzade Mosque is a great complex on Istanbul's Third Hill, ordered by Suleyman the Magnificent in memory of his son Shehzade Mehmet, who died of smallpox in 1543; Sinan designed and built it 1544-1548, calling it his 'apprentice work' {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.

## In the sources
An outer precinct wall encloses the mosque with its medrese, imaret, primary school, tabhane and Prince Mehmet's turbe, around which later tombs rose {cite:freely-ottoman-architecture-2011}.
