## Overview
The Bibi ka Maqbara at Aurangabad in the Deccan is the tomb of the first wife of [[person/aurangzeb-mughal|Aurangzeb]], modelled on the [[building/taj-mahal|Taj Mahal]]. [[person/john-brookes|Brookes]] attributes it to Aurangzeb himself. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.160}

## Brookes's assessment
Brookes calls it a weak copy of the Taj design: its many elements, built largely in plaster for economy, create a restless effect at odds with the purpose of a tomb, and show that the climax of Mughal design had passed; this is his judgement. He links it to the depleted treasury left by Shah Jahan's building programme. {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.160} {cite:brookes-gardens-of-paradise-1987 p.161}
