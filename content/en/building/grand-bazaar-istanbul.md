## Overview
The oldest and largest covered market in the world, in [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]]; Mehmed the Conqueror began it in 1461, and it grew until it had 61 streets and four thousand shops. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Between a quarter and half a million people visit it every day; it is still the heart of traditional trade in Istanbul. {cite:wikidata}
