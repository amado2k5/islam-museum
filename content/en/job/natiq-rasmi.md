## Summary

The natiq rasmi speaks with the tongue of the state, sultan or caliph, declaring its position inward and outward. An old office in a new name: 'the Prophet's spokesman' Thabit ibn Qays, the young state's envoys to kings, the caliph's voice in the provinces — down to today's 'spokesman of' government, presidency, army or movement {cite:tdv-elci}.

## The Prophet's spokesman

Thabit ibn Qays ibn Shammas was the Prophet's orator: he answered arriving delegations, replied to poets and storytellers, and spoke for him on the occasions — hence his titles 'khatib of the Ansar' and 'khatib of the Messenger' {cite:tdv-elci}. The new state had its public tongue from Medina.

## The forty-eight envoys

In 7 AH the Prophet sent six envoys bearing the letter of the call to the kings: Amr ibn Umayya al-Damri to the Negus, Dihya al-Kalbi to Heraclius, Hatib ibn Abi Balta'a to the Muqawqis, Abdullah ibn Hudhafa al-Sahmi to Chosroes, Shuja' ibn Wahb to Jabala ibn Ayham and al-Harith ibn Umayr to Busra — and Ibn Hudayda counts forty-eight of his envoys {cite:tdv-elci}. They were the state's tongue abroad: delivering the letter, calling, and returning the answer, their blood held inviolable by the custom of nations {cite:tdv-elci}.

## In the inherited state

The later states inherited the envoy's tongue: the sahib al-barid carried provincial news to the caliph and wrote his command back, his small office the state's voice below; the sultan's crier proclaimed his decrees; the insha secretary drafted his official letters {cite:tdv-elci}. Then the modern state raised the office of official spokesman speaking for the government, answering the press — the old office wearing its new name {cite:tdv-elci}.

## His qualities

He is chosen as the envoy was: honest statement that adds and drops nothing; restraint that keeps what must be kept; presence of mind before questioners; and knowledge of the language of those addressed {cite:tdv-elci}. Faithfulness is complete only when he reports what he was sent with, not what he wishes.
