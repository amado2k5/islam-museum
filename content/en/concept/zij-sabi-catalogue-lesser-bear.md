## The Lesser Bear

The star catalogue opens with the figure of the Lesser Bear. Its stars are listed one by one, beginning with the star on the tip of its tail and then the star at the root of its tail. Each star's longitude and latitude stand in a table beside it; those figures are not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.245}
