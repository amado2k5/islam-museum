## Definition
Inqiraz is lexically cutting off and passing away, and the jurists use it in that sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3540}

## Passing of the age of consensus
The usulis differ on whether the passing of the age of those who agreed is a condition of the authority of a consensus:
- The majority do not require it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3540}
- Some do require it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3540}
- Others distinguish: a consensus by word, by deed or by both does not need it, while a consensus by silence about an objector does, a view reported from Abu Ali al-Jubba'i. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3540}

## Endowments to a line that may die out
- The Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis do not validate an endowment to a line that may die out, such as an endowment to children, since they require perpetuity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3540}
- The Malikis validate it; who receives the endowment when the line ends is treated under the endowment entry. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3540}
