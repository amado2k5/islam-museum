## Signs
If the coma is from something drunk of the narcotic medicines, the book lists them by name and they are not carried here; it is known by the signs that he gives for each of them in the chapter on poisons, and by the coma coming with other signs of choking, greenness and coldness of the extremities, swelling of the tongue and change of smell {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} The pulse is fallen, ant-like (namli) and weak, not irregular but frequent with the frequency of the worm-like (dudi) and ant-like pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} If it is irregular it has no order and no steadiness, but returns from irregularity to frequency and from frequency to irregularity, and so it is known that he has been given one of these or has drunk it, and each is treated as stated in the chapter on poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}

## Outlook
All kinds of coma from cold in the substance of the brain, or from a medicine drunk, are followed by corruption in memory and thought {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}
