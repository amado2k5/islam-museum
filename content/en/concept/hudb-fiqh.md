## Summary
Hudb is the hair that grows on the eyelids; the hair above the eyes is called hajib. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16449} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16450}

## Injury to an eyelash
If a single eyelash is pulled out and does not regrow, the Hanafis and Hanbalis require a full diya. The Malikis and Shafi'is hold there is neither retaliation nor diya, only an assessed compensation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16450}

## Washing the eyelashes in ablution
If the hair is dense and does not show the skin, the Malikis and Hanbalis hold that washing its outer surface suffices, and the Hanbalis recommend combing it. The Hanafis require passing water over the hair without washing beneath it; the Shafi'is require washing outer and inner surfaces. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16450} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16451}
