## Summary
Rabigh is a valley between the two sanctuaries near the sea, a known place close to al-Juhfa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}

## The miqat
Al-Juhfa was the miqat for the people of Greater Syria, Egypt and the Maghrib. It has long since disappeared, so pilgrims from those lands take ihram from Rabigh out of caution; it lies a little before al-Juhfa for those coming from Medina. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}

## Distance
Rabigh is about 220 kilometres from Mecca. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}

## Where to look further
The rules of ihram and the miqats are given under ihram. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}
