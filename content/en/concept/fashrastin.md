## Nature
This is of the genus of fashra, with a leaf like the large ivy, and its root is black outside and yellow within {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Actions and properties
Like fashra in its actions, but a little weaker {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Joints
It also benefits palsy greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Organs of the head
Its heart, eaten when it first comes up, acts on epilepsy as fashra does {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Organs of the chest
It cleanses the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Excretory organs
Its heart, eaten when it first comes up, promotes urine, and it does what fashra does in all of this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its heart, eaten when it first comes up, brings on the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}
