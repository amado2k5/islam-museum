## Ulcers of the lung and the chest
And it often befalls the chest ulcers arising from putrefied abscesses that they corrupt the bones until the rotten part needs cutting away so that what adjoins it may be saved — and the rot may spread to the membrane next to it (a hand-work on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.354}
