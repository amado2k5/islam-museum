## Nature
It is wood and roots of wood brought from the lands of China, of India and of the Arabs, hard and compact in its hardness, some of it speckled and inclining to black, of good scent and astringent with a slight bitterness, with a bark like skin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Selection
The best of its kinds is the mandali, brought from the middle of the lands of India according to some, then the one called the Indian, which is of the mountain and of the roots, and is preferred to the mandali because it does not breed lice and clings more to cloth; among the best aloeswood is the samandur, from Safala, a country of China at the end of the lands of India, then the qamari, then the sanfi, then the qaqulli, the barri, the qatfi and the Chinese called qashmuri, which is moist and sweet, and below that the jala'i, the mantaqi, the lawami and the ribtani; the mandali is for the most part good; and in general the best aloeswood is the one that sinks most in water, the floating being lifeless and poor. Aloeswood is roots and bases of trees that are pulled up and buried in the earth until they rot, and the pure aloeswood remains, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree, as I think {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Actions and properties
It is subtle, opening obstructions, breaking winds and removing the excess of moisture, and it strengthens the viscera and all the organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Cosmetics
Chewing it perfumes the breath greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Joints
It strengthens the nerves and gives them an oiliness and a gentle viscosity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Organs of the head
It benefits the brain greatly and strengthens the senses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Organs of the chest
It strengthens the heart and gladdens it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.614}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It removes putrid moisture from the stomach and strengthens it and strengthens the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Excretory organs
It has a power restraining the bowels, and benefits dysentery, especially the black-bilious {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}
