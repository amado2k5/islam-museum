## Summary
Tahrif is the alteration of speech from its places in earlier scriptures. In his commentary on al-An'am 115, [[person/rashid-rida|Muhammad Rashid Rida]] holds that the verse saying that God's words cannot be changed concerns their truth, justice and binding force, not the wording of earlier books. {cite:tafsir-manar-v08 p.11} {cite:tafsir-manar-v08 p.12}

## The Qur'an's evidence on alteration
Rida states that the Qur'an established the alteration by the People of the Book before us of their speech, and their forgetting a portion of what they were commanded. He holds that God did not take on the preservation of the text of any one particular book of His. {cite:tafsir-manar-v08 p.12} [[passage/quran-6-115|Al-An'am 115]]

## Alteration of the good tidings
In his commentary on al-Nisa' 153 to 161, Rida asks whether it is surprising that the Children of Israel, after breaking the covenant of the Torah, would alter the good tidings of Jesus and Muhammad and oppose them. {cite:tafsir-manar-v06 p.13} [[passage/quran-4-155|An-Nisa 155]]

## The author's view
This is Rida's reading of the verses of the Qur'an, and it is presented here as his opinion. The views of other exegetes on the difference between altering the wording and altering the meaning have not yet been presented, nor the view of contemporary researchers.
