## Nature and selection
A kind of bird {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

## Temperament and actions
Hotter than all domestic birds. Some say it heats the cold-tempered and brings fever to the hot-tempered {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: its fat is excellent for easing pain and for calming deep burning in the body, the best of bird fats; its meat brings wind and its gizzard is very nourishing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

Adornment: its fat clears the complexion and its meat makes one fat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

Breath and chest: clears the voice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

Digestion: its meat is slow and heavy in the stomach, especially goose; the wings are the lightest, and when digested it is more nourishing than the meat of other birds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

Excretory organs: the book says it increases desire and semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.
