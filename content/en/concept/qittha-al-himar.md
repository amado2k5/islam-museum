## Choice
The best is the yellow, straight one like qittha' with true bitterness, and the best of its juice is the white, smooth, light one that resembles the squill, a year after it was made {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Actions and properties
Subtle and dissolving; its root, leaf and fruit cleanse and dissolve, its rind dries more, and the power of the juice of its root and leaf is one {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Cosmetics
Its juice, and the juice of its root and leaf, benefit jaundice, and the powder of its dried form removes the marks of black scars and cleanses the dirt of the face {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Swellings and pustules
A poultice of its root with barley flour dissolves every old phlegmatic swelling, and it bursts wounds, especially with terebinth gum and especially its juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its dried form sprinkled on itch and ringworm benefits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Joints
It benefits pains of the joints; its decoction as an enema benefits sciatica, and with vinegar it is a poultice for gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Organs of the head
Its juice dissolves thick migraine as a snuff with milk, and smeared with milk on the nostril it empties many superfluities; it benefits the helmet headache and chronic headache; the juice of the leaf is weaker; and dropped into the ear it calms its pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.657}

## Respiratory organs
Purging with its juice is very suitable for one with bad breathing, and the palate is smeared with its juice, with honey and old oil, for phlegmatic quinsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits dropsy by bringing out the watery humour, a wondrous benefit without harm; it causes the vomiting of phlegm and yellow bile; drunk with honey water it benefits clearly and brings both out easily, without hurt or harm to the stomach; the book then gives ways of using it as an emetic and of treating its excess, left out here because they are doses and methods of preparation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Excretory organs
It purges phlegm and blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its juice drives urine and the menses, and spoils the foetus when carried {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}
