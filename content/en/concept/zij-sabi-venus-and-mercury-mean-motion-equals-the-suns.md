## Summary
Al-Battani holds that the mean motion of Venus and Mercury is the same as the Sun's mean motion; their motion is taken from what remains of the Sun's motion after the planet's mean longitude is completed. The apogee of each is slightly above what Ptolemy's book gives. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.

## The amount of the excess
He says Venus's apogee exceeds Ptolemy's setting by about four and a half parts, and Mercury's by about two and a half; he reduced these by the time between Ptolemy and his own observations and added the daily share. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.
