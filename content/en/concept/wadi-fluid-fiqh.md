## Summary
Wadi is the thick white fluid that comes after urine or on lifting a heavy weight; the word also means young palm shoots. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16629}

## Wadi, semen and madhi
Semen comes with desire, madhi is thin and comes with desire, and wadi comes after urine. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16630}

## Purity and removal
Most jurists hold wadi impure; the Hanbalis hold that what is not eaten is impure. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16630} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16631}
It must be removed when needed, by water or stones; its exit requires ablution but not bathing, and breaks the ablution by agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16631} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16632}

## A doubtful wet mark
One who wakes with a wet mark and is unsure whether it is semen or wadi is not bound to bathe, according to most jurists; the Shafi'is let him act on the probable case. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16632} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16633}
