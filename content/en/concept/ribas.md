## Nature
A plant that grows in spring on the mountain, with the power of the sourness of the citron and of the unripe grape {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Temperament
Cold and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Actions and properties
Quenching, cutting blood, calming heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Swellings and pustules
It benefits plague {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Ocular organs
It sharpens sight when used as kohl with its juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Excretory organs
Beneficial for bilious diarrhoea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Fevers
It benefits measles, smallpox and plague {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}
