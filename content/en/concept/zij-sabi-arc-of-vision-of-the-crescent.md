## Summary
Al-Battani sets the arc of vision from the equator at about ten and a half and a third parts when the Sun is at its nearest and the Moon at its farthest, and at about thirteen and a third parts in the opposite case. He says this arc is clear in the climes with the reckoning. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.130}.

## The figure and the ratio
He shows that if the interval between the Sun and the Moon is ten and a half and a third parts of equatorial time, and the Moon's distance from the Sun in parts of the zodiac is thirteen and a third, the Moon is at the place of sighting, unless the state of the air changes it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.133}.
