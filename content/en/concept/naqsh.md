## Summary
Naqsh, in the lexicon, is a trace or mark, or colouring a thing with two or more colours. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15975} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15976}

## Rulings on engraving and decoration
A ruler is recommended to have an engraved ring; most jurists allow engraving the owner's name, the Hanbalis dislike engraving the divine name, and the Hanafis forbid pictures. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15976} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15977}
Most jurists dislike engraving the mosque; the Hanafis, some Malikis and Shafi'is allow it with light ornament. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15977}
The Hanafis and Malikis allow decorating houses with gold and silver under conditions; the Shafi'is and Hanbalis forbid it as wasteful. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15977} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15978}
The Shafi'is forbid henna designs on the hand of a woman in ihram, and most jurists dislike writing on graves. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15978}
