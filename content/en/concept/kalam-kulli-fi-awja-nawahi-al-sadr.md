## A general discourse on the pains of the chest's regions
Pleurisy is a hot tumour in the chest's regions — either in the inner muscles and the membrane lining the chest, or in the partitioning diaphragm, which is the true kind, or in the apparent outer muscles, or the outer membrane with the skin's participation or without; the greatest and most terrible of this is what is in the diaphragm itself, and the hardest (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.341}
