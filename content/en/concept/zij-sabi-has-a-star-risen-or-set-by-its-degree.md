## Summary
The text lays down a short rule: if the part with which the star rises follows the rising part at that time, the star has not yet risen; if it precedes the rising part, the star has risen and is above the earth. Likewise, if the part with which it sets follows the setting part it has not yet set, and if it precedes it the star has set. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.57}.

## The reason given
He explains that a star does not rise until the part that rises with it has risen, and does not set until the part that sets with it has set, so that the state of the part is a measure of the state of the star. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.57}.
