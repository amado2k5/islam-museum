## Overview

Harisa is a superficial head wound that scratches and slightly splits the skin without drawing blood. It is also called the scratching or peeling wound. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6722}

## The ruling

The Shafiis and Hanbalis, and one Hanafi view, hold that there is no retaliation for it, and that a just assessment is due because no fixed sum is set by revelation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6723}

The Malikis, and the apparent Hanafi view, require retaliation measured in length, breadth and depth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6723}

## Related wounds

The daamiya, the baadiha, the mutalahima and the simhaq are wounds that share the same general rule of a just assessment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6722}
