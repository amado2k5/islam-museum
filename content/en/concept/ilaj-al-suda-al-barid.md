## Without matter, and with matter
Warming by what is warm in act helps cold headache; for the chilled, who in the editor's footnote is the cooled or the one struck by severe cold, food is to be reduced, the bowels loosened, bodily, psychic and mental movements kept from him, the cold drink forbidden and exposure to cold denied {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.57} All who have headache from cold benefit, after cleansing if it is needed, from the warming kinds of the local measures mentioned, when there is no fear that humours will be stirred to intensity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.57-58}

## Phlegmatic matter and black-bile matter
As for cold headache with a phlegmatic matter, the body is evacuated if the humour is shared in it, then the food is reduced or made finer, ripening means and bounded evacuations, beginning from the least and then less, then the other treatments described in the Canon, and what quiets its pains is also used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.58} As for cold headache with a black-bile matter, here too one must act as was said in the Canon: draw blood if needed because the blood prevails or is corrupt, evacuate by degrees after the detailed ripenings, then change the temperament and use what produces a fine, praised, moist, thin blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.58}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.57-58}
