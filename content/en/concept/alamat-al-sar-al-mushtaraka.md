## The shared signs and the warning
The signs shared by most kinds of epileptics are the fur on their tongues and the greenness of the veins beneath them; often it is preceded by a change of the body from its temperament, heaviness in the head, dizziness, darkness of the eye, heaviness of the tongue and the senses, disturbance of its motions and yellowness of the face, bad dreams, forgetfulness or fright and fear and cowardice, self-talk and unease — especially if he is angry or a swelling occurs in his belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.122} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.123}

## Severity of the fit and what it shows
The harmful kind is that which is preceded by a strong shaking, a frequent strong disturbance, then followed by a long deep stillness with increase and harm to the breathing — which shows abundance of matter and weakness of power. If that is found with confusion of intellect, lasting forgetfulness, dullness or dementia, which does not diminish on evacuation or even arise when the bowels are loosened by evacuants, judge that the lesion is from the brain alone; then if you find nothing in the nervous organs, the spleen, the liver, or any of the extremities, the lesion is confirmed for you as being in the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.123}

## The easy and the difficult, and the sign of wind
The sign of easy epilepsy is that the accidents are sounder, that its sufferer returns quickly to his senses and is ashamed as he wakes, and that his recovery is hastened by snuffs and smelling things and by what moves vomiting when put into the throat, whether he vomits or not. The sign of the difficult kind is difficulty of breath, long disturbance then long dullness after it, and little recovery by smelling and snuff. The sign of that whose cause is a thick wind generated in him is that he feels no heaviness with it or near it, but feels a ringing and a stretching, and his convulsion is not severe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.123}
