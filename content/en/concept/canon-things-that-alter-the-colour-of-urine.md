## Summary
Ibn Sina lists foods and drinks that dye the urine and states outside the natural course such as fasting, sleeplessness, fatigue, anger, intercourse and vomiting, so that urine is not to be trusted when they are present {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.183}.

## Dyes
Saffron and cassia pulp dye the urine toward yellow and red, vegetables toward red and blue, murri toward black, and intoxicating wine turns the urine to its own colour; nor should the person have come into contact with a dye such as henna, for one who has dyed with it may have the urine stained by it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.183}.

## States outside the natural course
Among states outside the natural course, fasting, sleeplessness, fatigue, hunger and anger change the colour of the water, dyeing it toward yellow and red; intercourse greases it strongly; and vomiting and evacuation likewise change the proper colour and consistency of the urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.183}.
