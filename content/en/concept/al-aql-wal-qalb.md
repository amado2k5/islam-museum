## Overview
Muhammad Farid Wajdi holds man charged with balancing intellect and heart together: the intellect perceives truth and falsehood, the heart gives the delicacy of feeling and nobility of emotion that deter wrong even when one knows its harm. Scientific progress alone, he notes, did not prevent wars and destruction — Islam joined the education of mind and heart and called to reflection on horizons and souls {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 32} {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 33} {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 35}. This is the author's analysis, not the museum's ruling.

## In Abu Ishaq al-Heweny's «Difaf al-Kawthar»
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] opens an episode on the heart by calling it the most precious thing in a person, on which the good of his world and his Hereafter depends, and cites the hadith of [[person/al-numan-ibn-bashir|al-Nu'man ibn Bashir]] narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim, which ends by saying that in the body is a morsel which, if sound, makes the whole body sound and, if corrupt, corrupts the whole body, and it is the heart. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:02:30} He cites a hadith that he says al-Nasa'i and Ahmad narrated with a sound chain, that Satan sat in wait for the son of Adam on his paths: on the path of Islam, then of emigration, then of jihad, and each time he disobeyed him. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:04:00} He says he will treat the heart along several lines: its importance, what gives it life, what moves it, the kinds of hearts, and the ailments that sicken or destroy it. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:18:00}

Al-Heweny holds that the heart matters because the intellect is in it; he says this is the most preferred of the scholars' views, and that without intellect a person understands nothing of God and His Messenger and his worldly life is not set right either. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:18:30} He mentions among the scholars' proofs verses that ascribe reasoning and understanding to hearts, {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:19:30} and the hadith that the pen is lifted from the insane until he recovers, and concludes that revelation benefits only the sane, so losing the heart is losing this world and the next. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny dhifaf-al-kawtar/ep15 00:20:00}
