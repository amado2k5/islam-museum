## Overview
The Gardens of Eden (jannat 'adn) in the Qur'anic text are among paradise's names; Ibn Kathir reports that God created the Garden of Eden with His own hand and let down its fruits {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
The Tahawiyya creed affirms paradise and the Fire are created, never perishing {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §106}; Ibn Kathir details the reports on the Garden of Eden {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
