## Overview

A hail is a barrier; in fiqh, a sutra is the screen between the worshipper and one who passes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6697}

## Barrier and purity

The Hanafis hold that touching the private part breaks ablution even without a barrier; the entry sets out the rules for ablution, bathing and touching the Qur'an. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6698} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6699}

## Barrier between imam and follower

Most jurists hold that the follower cannot follow the imam if a large river on which boats travel, or a road on which a cart can pass, lies between them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6700}
