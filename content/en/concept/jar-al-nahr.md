## Nature and selection
A plant whose flower resembles the water lily. It grows submerged and shows a little above the water, and it is close in strength to batbat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}. The editor's footnote suggests the name may be salq al-ma' (a water plant); this is the editor's identification, not the book's description.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: cold and astringent, as it is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: suits malignant ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
