## Overview
In al-Baladhuri, *fath anwatan* (conquest by force) is the taking of a city or a land by fighting and siege. It is contrasted with conquest by treaty ([[concept/fath-sulh|conquest by treaty]]). {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.39-41}

## The example of Khaybar
Al-Baladhuri relates from Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from al-Zuhri, that the Prophet took Khaybar by force. Its property and land were then divided among the fighters. In the report of Ibn Abbas, Khaybar was divided into one thousand five hundred and eighty shares, according to the number of fighters. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.39-41}

The Prophet had from it the *safaya* (choice portions), from which he fed his wives, his uncle al-Abbas, Abu Bakr, Umar, al-Hasan, al-Husayn, and the clan of al-Muttalib. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.39-41}

## The difference from treaty
What was taken by treaty remained with its people on the terms of the agreement, as with Fadak. What was taken by force became booty to be divided among those who witnessed the conquest. This principle determines the fate of the land in the section on [[concept/fay|fay]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.39-41}
