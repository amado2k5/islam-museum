## Summary
Rayba denotes suspicion and doubt, and the juristic meaning aligns with its lexical usage. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9042} It is recommended to leave matters that induce suspicion and adopt what is clear of doubt out of scrupulous caution, and rulers are advised to overlook private faults and refrain from seeking suspicions among the public so as not to corrupt them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9042} Entertaining groundless suspicion against someone of apparent righteousness is forbidden, while suspicion is not ruled out regarding those who openly display wrongdoing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9043} Suspicion impacts waiting periods and establishing pregnancy, whereby a woman in doubt regarding pregnancy waits out a full gestation period of nine months according to the Malikis until doubt is dispelled. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9044} In judicial proceedings, suspicion among witnesses warrants their separation by the judge for individual examination prior to character witness verification in order to ascertain truth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9045}
