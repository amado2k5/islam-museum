## Summary
In the lexicon ru'ya is perceiving a thing with the eye, and it may also be by the heart; the jurists mostly use the first sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8539}

## General ruling
The ruling on seeking sight varies with its purpose: sighting the new moon of Ramadan is a collective duty for the Hanafis, seeing a woman one intends to marry is recommended, seeing what is awra is unlawful, and seeing ordinary things is permitted. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8540}

Deliberately looking at what is awra is forbidden except in necessity, such as treatment or testimony, and spouses may look at each other's bodies. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8540} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8541}

## Where it is discussed
The effect of sight is treated under the option of sight in sale, under testimony, under the sighting of the crescent for the months, and under betrothal and the rules of tayammum and prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8541} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8542} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8546}
