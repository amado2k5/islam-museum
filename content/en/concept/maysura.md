## Definition
Maysura means ease and wealth, the opposite of hardship; the verse on debt in al-Baqara (280) uses the word. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15378}

## Payment by a solvent debtor
The jurists agree that a debtor who has the means, with a debt that is due and claimed, must pay without delay or evasion, citing the saying: the delay of the wealthy is a wrong. One who refuses while able is a wrongdoer; the jurists differ on the penalty, whether he is imprisoned or held under surveillance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15378} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15379}

## Respite for an insolvent debtor
If a debtor cannot pay a due debt because his insolvency is established before the judge or the creditor, respite until he is at ease is recommended, citing the verse on debt and the hadith: whoever grants respite to a debtor in hardship earns a charity for each day before the debt falls due, and twice that after it falls due. Also the hadith: whoever grants respite or remits the debt will be shaded by God, and the story of the man who ordered his servants to overlook those in hardship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15379}

## Related terms
