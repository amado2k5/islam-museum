## Nature and selection
Like the mouse onion in its power and taste; it is used in its place but is weaker {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

## Temperament and actions
No separate temperament is given in this entry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: eases cold pains of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Poisons: helps poisons and the stings of scorpion and tarantula, drunk and applied, when mixed with fig {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.
