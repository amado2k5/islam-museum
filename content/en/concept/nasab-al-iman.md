## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the lineage of the prophets is a lineage of method and following, not of blood, race or place, and that each soul answers for what it earned. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.621} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.622}

## Lineage does not intercede for the one who does not follow
Sha'rawi holds that a claim of descent from Abraham, Isaac and Jacob does not help someone who departs from their method, because prophetic lineage is a lineage of faith resting on following and creed, and nothing intercedes on the Day of Judgement since each soul has its own deeds. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.622}

## Justice and grace for descendants
Sha'rawi reads the joining of believing descendants to their fathers as an honour to the fathers' deeds, not an addition to the children's deeds, and distinguishes the justice set by [[passage/quran-53-39|53:39]] from the grace God gives to whom He wills. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.602} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.603}

## Limits of this batch
This is Sha'rawi's view in these pages. Legal rulings on lineage, other exegetes' readings of the joining of descendants, the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.603}
