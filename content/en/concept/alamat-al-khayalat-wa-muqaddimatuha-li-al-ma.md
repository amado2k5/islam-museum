## The signs distinguishing the causes

The sign of what comes from keenness of sense is that it is light, not of one course and shape, and accompanies a man throughout healthy sight without defect following. The corneal kind is pointed to by its stated causes and by standing a long while without increasing or leading to harm in the sight. What comes from a cause in the egg-white humour has a long duration without a great lesion, coming either after hot ophthalmia or after a cooling or heating cause, known by intuition, especially when the cornea is found polished and clear with no roughness at all, then it stays fixed, not increasing, not leading to great harm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.208}

## The vaporous floater and the one heralding the water

What is caused by stomachic and bodily vapours is known by their flaring with vapour-raisers, at repletion and digestion, at motions, giddiness and staggering; it does not rest in one state but waxes and wanes, is not confined to one eye but is in both eyes; if swimming of the head is present its indication is confirmed, and if vomiting, evacuation, lightening the food and care for digestion increase or lessen it ⁦(the read text is broken)⁩. If the health and soundness of the eye continue six months with the owner of khayalat he is most probably safe; and the floater that is a forerunner of the water keeps clouding the sight step by step until the water descends or descends after it at once, and rarely passes six months. If you see the khayalat go and return, increase and lessen, know that they are not of the water; and if the second kind lasts long without continuing to weaken the sight, know that it is not of the water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.209}

## Principles of treatment

Of the khayalat, the one most deserving of attention is that which heralds the water. What comes from dryness is benefited by the known moisteners; what is from moisture or otherwise is benefited by all the clearing collyria. The one heralding the water begins with cleansing the body, especially the stomach, then cleansing the head with gargles, snuffs and chews. As for sternutatories: inasmuch as they relax and cleanse, cleansing is hoped from them, yet inasmuch as they move violently there is fear of their stirring the water, especially if it lies under the nerve and near it. Venesection of the artery behind the ear benefits at the beginning of the water, and it has been said that letting flow the vessel on the temples benefits at its outset ⁦(the medicines, recipes and measures are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.209}
