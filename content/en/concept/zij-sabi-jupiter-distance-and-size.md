## Jupiter
The text says Jupiter's size at its nearest distance is about one and a ninth times its size at its farthest. Its farthest distance is given as about twelve thousand, eight hundred and twenty-four, and its mean diameter as one twelfth of the Sun's diameter. Its diameter is about four times the Earth's diameter and a little more, and its bulk about eighty-one times the Earth's. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.188}

## Reservations about the figures
The farthest distance of Jupiter and its size ratio are approximate in the text, and the mean figures are disturbed in places, so they are to be checked against the original. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.188}
