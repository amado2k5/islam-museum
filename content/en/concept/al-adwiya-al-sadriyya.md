## The chest drugs
The chapter's yield is the division by ranks: the weak drugs first, then the stronger and the strongest; among them what cleanses, softens, cuts, ripens, binds, and what strengthens the breath's tools and eases the breath; among them what is used as a draught, what as a poultice, what is made a lohoch and what is made a pill held under the tongue; among the tried items are those the book describes as tried, and the compounds ascribed to the ancient physicians who set them down (all drug and plant names, formulae, measures and dispensing forms are excluded, per the register's rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.305}
