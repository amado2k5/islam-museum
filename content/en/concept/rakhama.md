## Organs of the head
Its gall is dropped with violet oil on the side opposite the migraine and the earache, and is given to boys as snuff or dropped into their ears for what is called the wind of boys {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Ocular organs
The gall is used as kohl with cold water for the white of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Poisons and bites
Ibn al-Batriq said that its gall is dried in a glass vessel in the shade and used as kohl on the side of the sting of the viper, and the author says he does not credit it; some have mentioned that it was tried for the poison of the scorpion, the snake and the hornet and was beneficial, and the author reckons it was smeared on {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It is said that its droppings, used as a fumigation, cause the foetus to be lost {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}
