## Summary
This record gathers what the books read say about the Companions and members of the Prophet's household buried in al-Baqi', attributing each report to its narrator. It is a companion to the record of [[place/al-baqi|al-Baqi']] and does not replace it, and it points to [[place/baqi-al-gharqad|Baqi' al-Gharqad]]. The earliest thing the books record is a disagreement over who was the first buried there: Uthman ibn Maz'un among the Muhajirun, As'ad ibn Zurara among the Ansar. {cite:isaba scan vol. 1 p. 210} {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 2 p. 59}

## The first buried at al-Baqi'
Ibn Hajar relays from al-Waqidi, through Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm, that the first buried at al-Baqi' was As'ad ibn Zurara, which is the Ansar's view, and that the Muhajirun said the first buried there was Uthman ibn Maz'un. {cite:isaba scan vol. 1 p. 210} Ibn Abd al-Barr gives the same, and says Mus'ab al-Zubayri held that the first buried at al-Baqi' was Uthman ibn Maz'un. {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 2 p. 59} {cite:isti-ab-ibn-abd-al-barr scan vol. 2 p. 552} Ibn al-Athir says Uthman was the first of the Muhajirun to die in Medina, in the second year of the Hijra, and the first to be buried at al-Baqi'. {cite:usd-al-ghaba scan vol. 1 p. 833}
The Siyar relays that the Prophet set a stone at the head of [[person/uthman-ibn-mazun|Uthman ibn Maz'un]] and said, 'This is the grave of our forerunner'; it is a mursal report of Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Rafi'. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2667} In another mursal report he told a man, 'Bring that rock and put it by my brother's grave, so that I may know it and bury beside it those of my family I bury'; the man could not lift it, so the Prophet carried it and set it at the grave. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2667} These texts do not give a ruling on the site of the grave but explain why a stone is mentioned as a marker.
As for [[person/asad-ibn-zurara|As'ad ibn Zurara]], Ibn Hajar relays from al-Hakim that the Prophet adorned the mother and the aunt of Zaynab bint Nubayt with jewellery of gold set with pearls, and that their father As'ad ibn Zurara had bequeathed the two of them to the Messenger of God. {cite:isaba scan vol. 1 p. 210}

## The household of the Prophet
**Ibrahim, the Prophet's son.** Al-Waqidi, as Ibn Kathir relays, says he died on Tuesday, ten nights into Rabi' al-Awwal of the year 10 at eighteen months old, among Banu Mazin ibn al-Najjar in the house of Umm Burda bint al-Mundhir, and was buried at al-Baqi'; the sun was eclipsed on the day of his death and people said it was eclipsed for Ibrahim's death. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 3 p. 337} See [[person/ibrahim-ibn-muhammad|Ibrahim ibn Muhammad]].
**Ruqayya, the Prophet's daughter.** In the account of Badr Ibn Kathir reports that Zayd arrived with the good news just as they were levelling the earth over [[person/ruqayya-bint-muhammad|Ruqayya]] at al-Baqi'. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 6 p. 359} In the books read I found no grave location for any other daughter of the Prophet.
**Al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib.** Al-Dhahabi says he lived eighty-eight years, that Uthman prayed over him and that he was buried at al-Baqi', where a great dome built by the Abbasid caliphs stands over his tomb. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 382} {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2126} Al-Dhahabi says he died in 32 AH, adding that Khalifa and others said 34 and al-Mada'ini 33. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2126} Al-Layth relays from Mujahid that he freed seventy slaves at his death. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2126} See [[person/abbas-ibn-abd-al-muttalib|al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib]].
**Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib.** Al-Dhahabi says she died in 20 AH and was buried at al-Baqi' at some seventy years. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 354} See [[person/safiyya-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib]].
**Al-Hasan ibn Ali.** The Siyar relays from Ibn Umar, in al-Waqidi, that he was present at al-Hasan's death and told al-Husayn: fear God, do not stir up a fitna or shed blood; bury your brother beside his mother, for he charged you with this. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 1435} In Abu Hazim's version, when al-Hasan was dying he told al-Husayn: bury me beside my father, meaning the Prophet, unless you fear bloodshed, in which case bury me in the Muslims' graveyards; when he died al-Husayn armed himself and gathered his clients, and [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] adjured him by God and by his brother's testament, and he was buried at al-Baqi'. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 1435} Al-Dhahabi quotes a man that on the day al-Hasan was buried Abu Hurayra said, 'May God fight Marwan; he said he would not let the son of Abu Turab be buried with the Messenger of God when Uthman had been buried at al-Baqi'.' {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 1435} See [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|Hasan ibn Ali]].

## The Prophet's wives and those counted with them
**Aisha.** Al-Dhahabi relays from al-Waqidi that she died on the seventeenth of Ramadan after the witr prayer, ordered that she be buried that night, the Ansar gathered and no night had more people, and she was buried at al-Baqi'. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2090} Isma'il ibn Abi Khalid relays from Qays that Aisha said, 'I introduced something after the Messenger of God; bury me with his wives,' and she was buried at al-Baqi'. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2090} Al-Dhahabi says Abu Hurayra prayed over her while Marwan's deputy governed Medina, and that those who said she is buried west of the Damascus mosque are mistaken. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2090} See [[person/aisha|Aisha]].
**Safiyya bint Huyayy.** Al-Dhahabi says her grave is at al-Baqi' and that she bequeathed a third of her estate to a Jewish brother of hers, the third being thirty thousand. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2034} See [[person/safiyya-bint-huyayy|Safiyya bint Huyayy]].
**Maria the Copt.** Ibn Kathir says she died in Muharram 16 AH as al-Waqidi and Ibn Jarir report; Umar prayed over her and used to gather the people for her funeral; she was buried at al-Baqi'. {cite:al-bidaya-wal-nihaya scan vol. 4 p. 76} See [[person/maria-al-qibtiyya|Maria al-Qibtiyya]] and [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]].

## Where the reports differ
The first burial is disputed between Uthman ibn Maz'un and As'ad ibn Zurara, with the views assigned to the Muhajirun and the Ansar. {cite:isaba scan vol. 1 p. 210} The year of al-Abbas's death is 32 AH for al-Dhahabi, 33 for al-Mada'ini and 34 for Khalifa and others. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2126} The year of Aisha's death is 57 or 58 AH, and the place of her grave al-Baqi' or west of the Damascus mosque, the second of which al-Dhahabi calls mistaken. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2090} The reports on al-Hasan's burial give the grave of his grandfather, the side of his mother, or the Muslims' graveyards. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 1435} I found no description of the present graves of these people in these books.
