## Nature
It is an Armenian drug, also called Persian. One authority the book quotes says it is stronger than euphorbium. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the fourth degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Actions
Burning, and it spoils the taste. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Uses by organ
Organs of digestion: it burns the stomach and brings on vomiting. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
