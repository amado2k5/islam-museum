## Summary
On Al Imran 7, Sha'rawi holds that the clear (muhkam) is what leaves no room for disagreement, and that the ambiguous (mutashabih) is what takes effort to grasp, so the clear is for action and the ambiguous for faith {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1272} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1273}.

## Referring the ambiguous to the clear
He says the believer refers the ambiguous to the clear. In the verse about God's hand, God is declared free of human likeness, and no practical ruling follows from the ambiguous text {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1276} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1277}.

## Deviation and desire
He reports that those with deviation in their hearts follow the ambiguous seeking discord. Deviation begins as an inclination, then thought submits to the heart's tilt, and desire is the disease of opinion {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1278} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1282}.

## The pause
He reports that some scholars pause after the name of God so that "those firm in knowledge" begins a new sentence, while others join it. He holds that the meaning reaches one point either way, and names none of those who said it {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1281}.
