## Summary
Al-Battani says that in the motion of the Sun and the other planets the unequal and mean days carry no amount that gives a perceptible effect, while in the Moon it is very clear for the swiftness of its motion, since the most that gathers between the unequal and mean days is close to half an hour, and in this span the Moon's motion is at some times about twelve minutes {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.

## The doubling
What lies between the days exceeding the mean days and those falling short of them is double that {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}. The spread between the two extremes is thus about an hour, and it is in this setting that [[concept/zij-sabi-day-inequality-table-and-the-apogee-drift|the table of the inequality of days]] comes {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.78-79}.
