## The repeated step
Once the corrected moments are known the steps are repeated. The latitude motion for both moments is taken, the Moon's latitude is subtracted from it, and the difference between the half-radii and the latitude is found. The result is divided by the Moon's motion to give a corrected time. When the second and third differences agree the moments are right; if not, a fraction of an hour is added or taken away and the comparison is repeated until the place is correct. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.153-154}

## What the repetition is for
The text says that the difference between the three parallaxes is corrected by the table and that additions and subtractions are reckoned in the hour, a sixth and a half-sixth. This is all detail on the previous step. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.153-154}
