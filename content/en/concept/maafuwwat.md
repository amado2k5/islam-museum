## Summary
Excused impurities are those whose presence is pardoned, so that prayer is not invalidated by them and they need not be washed. The principle is that what is hard to perform or to avoid falls away as an obligation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14780} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14781}

## The standard of excuse
Hardship has three grades: the highest, where excuse is agreed; the lowest, where none is allowed; and an intermediate grade whose placement jurists dispute. The dispute over excusing impurities follows from this. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14781}

## The schools
The Hanafis divide impurities into heavy and light. A heavy impurity is excused up to the size of a dirham, and a light one up to less than a quarter of the garment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14782} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14783}
The Malikis have four categories; some impurities are excused in small and large amounts, and some are excused only in their traces. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14783} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14784}
The Shafi'is classify by quantity and by whether the impurity falls on water or on cloth; the blood of fleas is excused on cloth and body but not in water. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14784} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14785} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14786}
The Hanbalis start from no excuse and list exceptions: a small amount of blood during prayer, pus and matter on one view, and traces after istijmar once cleaned. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14786} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14787} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14788}

## In prayer and zakat
Covering the awrah is a condition of valid prayer; it is not excused when uncovered on purpose. On zakat the majority excuse nothing in monetary wealth, while Abu Hanifa treats the surplus over the nisab as excused up to a fifth of a nisab. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14788}
