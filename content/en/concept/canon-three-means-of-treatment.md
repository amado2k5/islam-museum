## Summary
Ibn Sina opens the fourth Fann, on the ways of treating diseases taken as wholes, in thirty-two chapters, with a general chapter on treatment: treatment is completed by three things, regimen and feeding, the use of drugs, and the use of the hands {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.267}.

## Regimen and food
By regimen he means attention to the counted necessary causes that run in the course of habit, food being one of them. The rules of regimen, as to quality, correspond to the rules of drugs, but food has rules of its own in the matter of quantity, since food may be withheld, reduced, kept moderate or increased {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.267}.
