## The heart
What comes from cold and dryness without matter has as its cause a black-bile bad temperament in the heart, with matter or without, in which the brain shares, because the psychic pneuma is joined to the vital pneuma and is of its substance, so its corrupt black-bile temperament spoils the temperament of the brain and turns it to blackness; it may also come from other cooling and drying causes, not from the heart alone, though it cannot be without some sharing from the heart, and the greater part of the cause may well be the heart, so the treatment of the heart must go with the treatment of the brain in this illness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.104}

## The blood of the heart
Ibn Sina says that when the blood of the heart is smooth, thin, clear and gladdening it resists the corruption of the brain and sets it right, and it is no wonder that the starting point is, in most cases, the heart, since it is not far that the temperament of the heart spoiled first and the brain followed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.104} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.105}

## At the end of material illnesses
Melancholia may come at the end of material illnesses, especially acute ones, and then it is a sign of death; the person then recalls death and the dead often {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.105}
