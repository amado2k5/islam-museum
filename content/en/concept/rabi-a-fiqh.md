## Summary
A rabi'a is the lookout of a people, posted on a height to watch the enemy so the people are not surprised. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8614} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8615} He is distinguished from the spy, the garrisoned fighter, the guard and the ambusher on the road. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8615} In jihad he is one of the people and has a share of the booty like the fighters. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8615} In retaliation for killing most jurists do not punish him if he did not do the killing, while the Malikis do if he colluded. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8615} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8616} In banditry he is treated as a direct perpetrator by the Malikis, Hanafis and Hanbalis, while the Shafi'is hold no hadd penalty on one who only helped. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8616}
