## Overview
Al-Baladhuri relates that Ali went out to the market and saw that his people had taken possession of their places. He said: "That is not theirs. The market of the Muslims is like their place of prayer; whoever reaches a spot first has it for the day, until he leaves it." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.420}

## After Ali
In the time of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba they used to go out to the market, and whoever sat in a place had the better right to it until nightfall. When Ziyad came, he said: "Whoever sits in a place has the better right to it so long as he is in it." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.421}

The matter is connected with [[concept/fay|fay']] in al-Baladhuri's *Futuh al-buldan*.
