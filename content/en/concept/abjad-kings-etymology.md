## Overview
Al-Masudi relates that the letters of the abjad, twenty-nine in number, on which the reckoning of letter values (hisab al-jummal) is based, are named after the kings of the people of Shu'ayb, of the Banu al-Mahd ibn Jundal ibn Ya'sub ibn Madyan. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.459}

## The names
Abjad was king of Mecca and its surroundings in the Hijaz. Hawwaz and Hutti were two kings in the land of Wajj, which is the land of al-Ta'if and the parts of Najd joined to it. Kalaman, Sa'fas and Qarashat were kings of Midian, and it is said they were in the land of Egypt. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.459}

Al-Masudi notes that there are other accounts of the letters besides this one, which the records here do not set out.
