## Summary
Niyaba in the lexical sense is making another one's deputy in a matter; technically it is one person's standing in for another in an act. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16677}

## Related terms
Niyaba differs from wilaya, qawama, wakala and wasiyya; it is the wider notion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16677} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16678}

## The two kinds
Contractual representation is agency (wakala); legal representation is guardianship over those unable to act through youth or the like, and it is over property or over the person. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16679} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16680}

## Substitution in worship
Substitution is permitted in purely financial worship, such as zakat, expiations and vows, and not in purely bodily worship, such as prayer and fasting. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16681} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16682}
It is permitted in the pilgrimage and the lesser pilgrimage under conditions, among them that the principal is permanently unable and has funds, and that the deputy has first performed his own pilgrimage, according to the Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16683} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16684} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16685} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16686}

## Pilgrimage for the dead and hire
Hiring for the pilgrimage on behalf of the dead is permitted by the Malikis, Shafi'is and in one Hanbali report, and forbidden by the Hanafis. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16696} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16699}
