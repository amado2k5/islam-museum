## Its removal
Its owner is made to sneeze by one of the drugs of the known kind, while his mouth and his sound nostril are held shut; when he sneezes, the thing comes out of it — as though this were among what has been mentioned before ⁦(the drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.252}
