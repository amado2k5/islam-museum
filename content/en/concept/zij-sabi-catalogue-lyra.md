## Lyra (an-nasr al-waqi')

The catalogue gives the Lyre the Arabic name an-nasr al-waqi', the falling eagle, and says that its brightest star, which the catalogue places with the lyre-holder, is itself the an-nasr. The catalogue uses the same word for Aquila, so the two figures should not be confused. Star positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.148}
