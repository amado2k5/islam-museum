## Measuring convulsion by epilepsy
Convulsion is as it were the epilepsy of a member as epilepsy is the convulsion of the whole body; the difference between them is generality and particularity, that most epilepsy is swift and may come in turns, and other differences you know (see the notion of epilepsy in its earlier chapter) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.143}

## The matter's entry into the fibre
The matter making the convulsion convulses only by entering the substance of the fibre in a spreading, benefiting entry, yet crowding in the gap {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.143}
