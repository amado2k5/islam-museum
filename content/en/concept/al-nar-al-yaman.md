## Summary
The last major sign: a fire out of the depth of Aden in Hadramawt driving people to the gathering — halting where they halt, resting where they rest.

## In the texts
Bukhari says it herds people toward al-Sham, their gathering place.

## Note
From the hadith of Hudhayfa in Sahih Muslim and the hadith in al-Bukhari.
