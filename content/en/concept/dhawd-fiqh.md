## Summary
A dhawd is a small herd of camels, from three to ten, and its rules are treated under the zakat of camels. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8499}
The least nisab for zakat on camels is five: nothing is due on fewer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8500}
On five camels one sheep is due, and on ten two sheep. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8500}
The jurists differ over giving a male in place of a sheep and over giving its value. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8500}
