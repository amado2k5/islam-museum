## Temperament
The sweet is cold to the first degree and moist in it, and the sour is cold and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.666}

## Actions and properties
The sour suppresses bile and stops the flowing of superfluities to the viscera, especially its drink (sharab), and in all its kinds, even the sour, there is cleansing together with astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.666}

## Swellings and pustules
The seeds of the pomegranate with honey are a smear for whitlow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Wounds and ulcers
The seeds with honey are a smear for foul rough ulcers, its calyces are for wounds, especially burnt, and jullanar glues wounds; the sweet is softening, and all of it is of little nourishment, though a good one, but its seed is poor, and its most astringent parts are its calyces {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Organs of the head
The seeds with honey benefit ear pain and are a smear for the inside of the nose, and the seed ground and mixed with honey benefits mouth sores as a smear; if the sweet pomegranate is boiled in the drink (sharab), then pounded and the ear poulticed with it, it benefits its swelling; and the drink (sharab) of pomegranate and its rubb benefit hangover, especially the sour rubb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Ocular organs
The juice of the sour with honey benefits pterygium, and the juice of the sweet and the bitter with honey benefits heat of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Organs of the chest
The sour roughens the throat and chest, and the sweet softens them and strengthens the chest; when the seed is given in rainwater it benefits spitting of blood, and all of it benefits palpitation and cleanses the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
All of it gives good chyme, and the best for the stomach is the tart pomegranate, which benefits burning of the stomach; the sweet suits the stomach because of the gentle astringency in it; the sour harms the stomach, and besides, the seed of the pomegranate is poor for the stomach and burning; all of it is of little nourishment, and the tart may be more beneficial to the stomach than the apple and the quince {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Excretory organs
The sour promotes urine more than the sweet, and both promote it; the seed of the pomegranate with honey benefits ulcers of the stomach, and the sour harms the stomach and the intestine; its sawiq benefits bilious diarrhoea and strengthens the stomach; and the bark of the root of the pomegranate with nabidh expels worms {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its sawiq sets right the craving of pregnant women, and likewise its rubb, especially the sour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}

## Fevers
The tart pomegranate benefits fevers and burning, while the sweet has often harmed those with hot fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.667}
