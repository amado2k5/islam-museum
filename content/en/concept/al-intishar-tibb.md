## Its definition and causes

Intishar is the uveal pupil becoming wider than its nature; it may follow a headache, or an outward cause such as a blow or a shock, or causes in the pupil itself, in the egg-white humour or the uvea. If the egg-white humour moistens and increases it crowds the uvea and moves it to widen; the dryness of the egg-white humour does not cause widening by itself but accidentally, as uveal dryness follows it. If the uvea itself dries and stretches to its edges like perforated skins stretched when dry, its hole may widen like theirs, especially if it is crowded by the moistures; it may also happen from a moisture entering its substance, increasing its thickness and stretching it to coarseness so the pupil widens, and it may come from a spreading swelling arising in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.210}

## Its outcome and what is untreatable

The widening may be natural, harming the sight so things are seen smaller than they should be; or accidental, with the same effect; and it may go so far that nothing is seen, for the eye often widens until the breadth reaches the corona and no sight worth mention remains. What comes of a blow or shock has no treatment ⁦(the book transmits from a trustworthy man that he treated a widening produced by a blow by bleeding the patient at once, so he recovered after a few days — transmitted on his authority, not endorsed)⁩. If the widening is from rupture of the retinal layer's continuity it has no treatment at all on any side, and what is from widening of the hollow nerve is hard to cure {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.210} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.211}

## Principles of treatment

The natural kind has no treatment; what is from dryness is benefited by the stated moisteners; what is from moisture is benefited by venesection when there is abundance in the body and by bleeding the neighbouring vessels and drawing them, by evacuations and by pouring salted or brined water on the head, especially mixed with vinegar; laxative evacuation should not be overdone lest it weaken the power without evacuating what is wanted. The other eye is kohled with tutty so it does not spread like the first, and cupping the nape benefits for its drawing backward; the kind that follows a blow is treated on the pattern of the hot swelling ⁦(the details of measures, recipes and named galls are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.211}
