## The signs of pleurisy
The true pleurisy has five signs: a continuous fever for the heart's neighbourhood; the second a pricking pain under the ribs, the organ being membranous; the third narrowness of breath from the tumour's pressure; the fourth a saw-like differing pulse; the fifth the cough (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.343}
