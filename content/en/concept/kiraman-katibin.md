## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an describes them as noble recording scribes [[passage/quran-82-11|al-Infitar 11]], says there are guardians over you [[passage/quran-82-10|al-Infitar 10]], that every soul has a guardian over it [[passage/quran-86-4|al-Tariq 4]], and mentions them in the context of recording [[passage/quran-50-18|Qaf 18]].

## In hadith
Al-Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud and Musnad Ahmad narrate that every son of Adam has two scribes recording his deeds, at his right and his left, who never cease {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:musnad-ahmad}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details that the scribes are the angels charged with writing deeds, noble because of their honour with God {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Scholars differ on their number and the details of their positions, but agree they are angels who write every deed of the servant {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
