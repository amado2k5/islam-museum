## The sign of what is by the brain's sharing
What arises by the brain's sharing is shown by the state of the other senses sharing hearing in it and the motor powers' sharing too; strongest of signs is the tongue's sharing, especially when it follows phrenitis and confusion of mind and other temperamental brain lesions told of in the brain's chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.219}
## The sign of what is special to the nerve
What is special to the nerve is inferred from the soundness of brain and meatus, the soundness of hearing's passages and the former continuance of sound hearing. If the cause is a dubayla or a piercing swelling in the nerve itself, fevers point to it — with ague and rigor, and fever, confusion of mind and delirium attend it, and there is danger unless it opens. If the swelling is not in the nerve itself fever need not be, save as a day-fever, and there is stretching, pain, heaviness and throbbing; winds are shown by ringing and buzzing not parted from heaviness; an ulcer's irritation by itch with the pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.219}
## The sign of the occlusion and the other causes
The occlusion is often without heaviness, or with heaviness; with no heaviness, a lesion and no overwhelming bad temperament, it is the occlusion, and the foregoing management may point to it. An occlusion of a boil or the like is shown by throbbing; of blood, by the foregoing flow of blood. A bad temperament without stretching: if cold, it is hurt by cold things and worsens in the coldest end of day; if hot, the reverse, with inflammation and stinging felt; with a matter present heaviness is felt too, especially at prostration. Dryness is marked by coming after wakefulness and fasting with wasting of face and eye; worms by constant tickling with worms sometimes coming out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.219} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.220}
