## Capricornus (al-Jady)

Capricorn is al-Jady, the kid (haedus). Its stars run along the back under the shoulder, the knees, the hooves, the thigh and the tail, where the list notes the northern stars at the start of the tail. The notes after the list discuss one star's magnitude with reference to al-Sufi. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.163}
