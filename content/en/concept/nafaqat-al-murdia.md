## Definition
The maintenance of the nursing mother is what is owed to the mother who suckles her child — food, clothing and lodging upon the child's father for the duration of the suckling — and it belongs to the child before it belongs to the mother, for the milk is his nourishment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The root of the chapter
God says in the verse of suckling in Sura al-Baqara: upon the father of the child is their provision and their clothing in fairness — placing the nursing mother's food and clothing upon the child's father, neither upon the child nor upon the mother — having just said that the mothers suckle their children two full years for whoever wills the suckling completed. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## With the jurists
If the nursing mother is the father's wife her maintenance is the maintenance of marriage; if she is divorced she has the wage of her suckling and a maintenance like the wage of her peer, unless she is in her waiting period, in which case they are joined for those who hold it. If the mother nurses freely or a stranger nurses for a wage, the suckling belongs to whichever woman the child accepts, with the parents' consent; and no mother is to be harmed on account of her child — she is not starved until she suckles, nor is the child taken from her except for fear over him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
