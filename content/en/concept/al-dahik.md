## Definition
Laughter and smiling are human states that the Qur'an and the Sunnah mention with praise or blame according to their setting: the laughter of joy, good news and wonder, and the laughter of mockery and heedlessness.

## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an attributes making people laugh and weep to God in [[passage/quran-53-43|al-Najm 43]]. Laughter of good news and wonder includes the laughter of Abraham's wife in [[passage/quran-11-71|Hud 71]], Solomon smiling and laughing at the ant's words in [[passage/quran-27-19|al-Naml 19]], and the laughing, rejoicing faces in [[passage/quran-80-39|Abasa 39]]. Blamed laughter includes the criminals laughing at the believers in [[passage/quran-83-29|al-Mutaffifin 29]], Pharaoh's people laughing at the signs in [[passage/quran-43-47|al-Zukhruf 47]], and the rebuke of those who laugh and do not weep in [[passage/quran-53-60|al-Najm 60]]. The situation is then reversed in [[passage/quran-83-34|al-Mutaffifin 34]].

## In the Sunnah
The hadith books report the Prophet laughing until his molars showed in the account of the last person to enter Paradise {cite:sahih-muslim 186}, and laughing at the man who came to ask about his expiation and then found no one needier of the alms than himself {cite:muwatta-malik}. They also warn against lying to make people laugh {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 4990}.
