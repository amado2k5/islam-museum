## Summary
Algebra is a branch of mathematics that solves problems involving the unknown and treats equations by general methods. In this museum it is associated with [[person/al-khwarizmi|al-Khwarizmi]] and his book [[work/al-jabr-wal-muqabala|al-Jabr wa al-Muqabala]] {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.11}.

## Note
The definition of algebra here is general. The statement about the book's standing is taken from the introduction by the editors of the 1939 edition. The book's mathematical examples have not yet been read.
