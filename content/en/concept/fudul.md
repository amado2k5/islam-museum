## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that fudul, following and asking about what does not concern a person in other people's affairs, is a plague among people. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:58:30} He gives the example of someone who keeps asking about a watch on another's wrist until he leads him into lying, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:58:30} and he calls for teaching children from a young age to leave it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:59:00}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)
The Sheikh holds that curiosity about others has "killed us", and that some people keep pressing with questions until they lead the person they talk to into lying; he gives the example of someone who praises a watch on another's wrist and then keeps asking about it, whether it was a gift, who gave it to you, until he puts its owner in an awkward position {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:58:30}. He calls for teaching children from a young age to leave off such curiosity, and mentions a small boy whose mother asked where he had been and who answered that he had been on an errand for the Messenger, without disclosing his secret (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:59:00}.
