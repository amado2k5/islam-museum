## Nature and selection
It is a preparation brought from Armenia, found in sheep's hooves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}. The editor's footnote says that shiyaf is an eye remedy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: used on hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.
Joints: helps hot gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.
