## Summary
Dibaj is a kind of cloth whose warp and weft are of silk, that is natural silk. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8309}
Its general rules are those of silk, and the jurists give no separate rules for it except in some particular cases. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8309}

## Wiping with dibaj
The Hanafis dislike wiping oneself with a piece of dibaj, since it wastes property without need. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8309}
The Shafi'is allow it even for men, because such use is not treated as ordinary use of it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8309}
Details are under the entry on silk. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8309}
