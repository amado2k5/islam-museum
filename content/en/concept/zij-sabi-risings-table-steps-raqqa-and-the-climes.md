## Summary
Al-Battani sets down the risings of the signs for the city of al-Raqqa at a step of a whole degree, and for the other climes at a step of ten degrees, because the difference between the risings is small over a step of that size, and he makes the step in the increase of the day between the tables finer than half an hour so that the use of the risings is exact and sound. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.

## Al-Raqqa at one degree, the climes at ten
He says: we have set down the risings of the signs for the city of al-Raqqa at a full step of one degree and for the rest of the climes at a step of ten degrees, because of the small difference falling between the risings over a step of this size. The tables are not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.

## A step finer than half an hour
He says: we made the step of the increase of the day in the risings drawn in the tables a small fraction of an equal hour, so that it would be sounder for the work with risings that is needed, and more exact than the risings made at a step of half an hour. The number of this step is garbled in the scan and is not given, but the text states that it is finer than the half-hour step used in other tables. The passage points to tables other than his made at half an hour, and does not name their author. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.
