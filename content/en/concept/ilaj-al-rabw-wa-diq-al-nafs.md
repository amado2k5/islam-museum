## The treatment of asthma and the breath's narrowness
The kind arising from smoky vapours overwhelming the heart and from humours in the arteries may profit by venesection (named on the book's wording, the vein not named). The wind-kind's treatment aims at dissolving what resists dissolution, with oil-anointing and poulticing of chest and sides. The catarrh-kind requires working at stopping the catarrhs and breaking up what has gathered. The suspected kind of bad-breath narrowness that is not of the narrowness's chapter had its treatment told in the hard-breath chapter. The kind arising from the breath profits by drinking milks, juices, the cold moistening oils and a thin-tempered drink, and by shunning the fierce heaters, dissolvers and driers — the moistening washes, salves and soft unguents suit them. The kind arising from heat with burning is treated with the cooling salves — in truth a kind of bad breath, not of narrow breath. The cold-kind takes the drunk and smeared warmers (the names of the drugs, oils, poultices, draughts, fumigations, clysters, formulae and measures are all excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.320}
