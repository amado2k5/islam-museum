## Summary
A clear smoke covering the people; counted among the ten. Ibn Mas'ud read it as the Meccan famine of the drought years, while most scholars hold it is still awaited.

## In the texts
[[passage/quran-44-10|the day the sky brings clear smoke]]

## Note
The disagreement is Companion-old: two documented readings, and the text does not settle its time.
