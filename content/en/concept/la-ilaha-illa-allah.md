## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that faith has some sixty branches, the highest of which is the saying "la ilaha illa Allah", and that the nation advances only by understanding what this testimony requires and what it truly is. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 01:11:00}

## The testimony at the root of the religion
The Sheikh holds that the religion rests on the creed of tawhid, and that "there is no god but God, and Muhammad is the Messenger of God" is its foundation, which the Companions understood as it truly is. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep03 00:04:00}
He holds that the religion stands on the Qur'an and the Sunna, on creed and law, and that it includes the tawhid of worship and the tawhid of lordship. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep03 00:08:30}

## The testimony and the ethics of daily life
The Sheikh holds that the Companions counted trustworthiness, truthfulness and refusing false testimony as part of the meaning of "la ilaha illa Allah", so that for them prayer was not separated from trustworthiness and worship was not separated from dealings. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep03 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep03 00:13:00}
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's view (opinion): people today separate worship from dealings, and this separation is a fault in them, since the Companions understood the testimony in full. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep03 00:52:30}

## The tree and the branches of faith
The Sheikh likens "la ilaha illa Allah" to a tree whose leaves and fruit are fresh and whose branches number more than seventy (needs_source: the number is not sourced in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 00:02:00}
He holds that people fixed on minor matters, enlarging them, and left the root of belief, so that they discuss what breaks ablution more than what breaks the testimony. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 00:02:30}

## Abu Jahl and the testimony
The Sheikh says that the first branch of faith is the testimony, and that Abu Jahl understood what it demanded and rejected it; he holds that a Muslim society must return to the branches of faith. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:39:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:39:30}

## The understanding of the Companions
The Sheikh holds that the Companions understood "la ilaha illa Allah" and for that reason were strong. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 00:57:00}
He holds that the prophets, the Companions and the righteous predecessors understood best the ethics of this testimony. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 00:05:30}

## Preaching in Mecca
The Sheikh recounts that the Prophet stayed in Mecca for thirteen years and taught people nothing about prayer, zakat, the pilgrimage or jihad, but only the meaning of "la ilaha illa Allah" (needs_source: this is the Sheikh's account and is not sourced in the recording), and that once they understood it each one of them became a whole community. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 01:06:30}
He says that Muslims in [[building/dar-al-arqam|Dar al-Arqam]] understood the question of tawhid over thirteen years. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:14:30}

## Responsibility before God
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's view (opinion): everyone who says "la ilaha illa Allah", from the ruler to the farmer, is accountable before God for his work. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep09 00:12:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy asks about the person who pronounced the two testimonies and then died without prayer or fasting, and holds that Abu Jahl was cleverer than such a man, because he grasped that 'there is no god but God' is not merely a phrase to be said but calls for belief and action. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:19:00} He says that the sinners among those who affirm God's oneness are punished in the Fire for a time, each according to his measure, and then brought out, and that God is jealous for the word of oneness in their hearts. These are his words of exhortation. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:22:00}
