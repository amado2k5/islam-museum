## Summary
Al-Battani turns to the known places of the earth in longitude and latitude and states that the earth is round, that its centre is the centre of the heavens, that air surrounds it on every side, and that to the zodiac it stands like a point. He says this was shown by reasoning in Ptolemy and agreed to by others of the ancients. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.29}.

## The earth at the middle of the heavens
He says the reasoning laid down by [[person/batlamus|Ptolemy]], and agreed by others of the ancients, shows that the earth is round, that its centre is the centre of the heavens, that air surrounds it from all sides, and that at the zodiac's scale it stands like a point. The text credits the proof to Ptolemy and others and does not rehearse it, using the conclusion as the base for the account of the inhabited earth that follows and for its division into longitudes and latitudes. See [[work/al-majisti|the Almagest]]. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.29}.
