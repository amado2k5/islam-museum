## Summary
The maqasid of the Shari'a are the purposes and wisdoms that the usul scholars take the Lawgiver to intend across all or most of the rulings, not confined to a single kind of ruling. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14891}

## Kinds
Al-Shatibi holds that the duties of the Shari'a return to the preservation of its purposes among people, and that these fall into three kinds: necessities, needs and refinements. The detail is in the usul appendix. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14891}
