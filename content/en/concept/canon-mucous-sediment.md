## Summary
Ibn Sina says mucous sediment shows thick, raw humour and may be a crisis pushed out through the urinary organs, and he warns against being fooled by what looks like praiseworthy sediment when it is not the time of maturation. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## What it shows
Mucous sediment shows thick raw matter, either abundant in the body or driven from the urinary organs as a crisis, as in sciatica and joint pain; lightness after it is the sign. It may become fine and thin and be taken for praiseworthy sediment, so one is not to be misled by its look in illness when the time and signs of maturation are absent; it may also show strong cold in the kidney's temperament. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.
