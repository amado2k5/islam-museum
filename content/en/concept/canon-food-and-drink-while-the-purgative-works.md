## Summary
Ibn Sina advises whoever has drunk a drug not to eat or drink until it has finished, giving bread soaked in a little wine to someone whose stomach cannot bear it, a barley or pomegranate drink beforehand to a hot-tempered person with a weak stomach, and washing the anus with hot water rather than cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.280}.

## Food
Someone of hot temper and weak build and stomach who is to drink a drug does better to have taken beforehand something like barley water or pomegranate juice, so that some light food is in the stomach; anyone else is better off drinking it on an empty stomach. The drinker should neither eat nor drink until the drug has finished its work; if his stomach cannot bear going without, because it is bilious and bile quickly pours into it or because he has been abstaining and hungry for long, he is given bread soaked in a little wine on top of the drug before the purging starts. The anus should not be washed with cold water but with hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.280}.
