## Nature
A kind of animal, which the editor identifies as the chameleon. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.494}

## Uses by organ
Organs of the eye: it is said that its blood prevents plucked eyelashes from growing back. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.494}
Poisons: it is said that its egg is a deadly poison, which the book gives in its fourth book. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.494}
