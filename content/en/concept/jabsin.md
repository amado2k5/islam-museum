## Nature and selection
It is the gypsum stone, white, flaky and translucent; burnt, it becomes finer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}. The footnote calls it jifsin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: cold and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}. Actions: astringent, placed on the areas of bleeding; it has stickiness, and burnt it becomes finer and dries more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}.

## Uses by organ
Head: spread on the forehead, or the head wrapped in it, stops nosebleed, especially with Armenian clay, lentil, myrtle water and a little vinegar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}.
Eyes: mixed with egg white so it does not harden, and placed on bloody ophthalmia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}.
Poisons: among the choking poisons, and extreme in that {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.424}.
