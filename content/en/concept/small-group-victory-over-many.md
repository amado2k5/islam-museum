## Summary
On al-Baqara 249, Sha'rawi holds that a small believing group can overcome a large one by God's leave, and that victory comes from faith, not numbers {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1044}.

## Seeing and feeling
He distinguishes sight, which is shared, from the feeling that follows it. All who crossed the river saw Jalut's army, but some were afraid and said they had no strength, while others said that a small group had overcome a large one {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1055}.

## Patience and help
He holds that help comes in proportion to patience: God adds to the aid of those who exhaust their own means. He cites the Al Imran verses on three and then five thousand angels for those who endure and fear God, without giving his own reading of the number here {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1055} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1056}.
