## Nature
A stone like the crab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Temperament
Cold and moist in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Actions and properties
It dries and cleanses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Ocular organs
It sharpens sight; this is the end of the discourse on the letter ra, and the sum of the drugs we have mentioned is twenty-five in number {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}
