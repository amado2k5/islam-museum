## Temperament and actions
Its temperament is hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}. The editor's footnote says that adhrion is al-hanwa, a kind of plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}.

## Uses by organ
It helps alopecia (da' al-tha'lab) ground with vinegar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}. Its ash with vinegar is applied to sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}. It helps all poisons, and especially stings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}.

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book gives the saying of Dioscorides that the mountain kind, when a woman touches it and uses it as a pessary, makes her miscarry at that hour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation.
