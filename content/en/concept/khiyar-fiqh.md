## Summary
Khiyar is lexically choosing and selecting; to give someone the choice between two things is to leave the choice to him; technically it is a party\'s right to annul or confirm a contract for a legal cause or by a stipulated term. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7887} It differs from non-binding nature (the power to withdraw by one\'s own will, a natural feature of some contracts, while option is an added qualifier to a contract that is binding by origin), and from annulment for invalidity, whose effect does not reach back in some kinds according to al-Kasani. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7887}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7888} Annulment by option has retroactive effect as if the contract were never concluded, unlike annulment in non-binding contracts which does not touch what came before. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7888}
