## In hadith
Al-Bukhari and Muslim narrate in the hadith of the nutfa that God sends an angel to the fetus after forty days who shapes him and creates his hearing, sight, skin, flesh and bones, then says 'wretched or happy' and writes down his deeds {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details that the angel charged with the fetus is the one who writes his deeds before birth, and that 'wretched or happy' are written of him {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Scholars differ on the details of the angel's work and the meaning of 'wretched or happy', but agree the hadith establishes an angel charged with the fetus {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
