## Nature and selection
Nature: the leaf of indigo. Choice: the Khurasani is the best. Temperament: it leans toward heat at the end of the first degree, and toward dryness in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.451}

## Actions
It has astringency and cleansing. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.451}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it dyes hair. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.451}
