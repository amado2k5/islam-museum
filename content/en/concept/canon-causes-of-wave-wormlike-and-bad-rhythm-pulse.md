## Summary
Ibn Sina explains the wave, wormlike and bad-rhythm pulses by weak power, soft instrument and the states of the times of rest and motion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.

## The wave pulse
The wave pulse is mostly caused by weakness of power, which cannot spread things one after another. Softness of the instrument can also cause it even if the power is not very weak, for a moist, soft instrument does not take a shake or a penetrating motion in a free part as a dry, hard one does: dryness prepares for shaking and trembling, and in the hard dry the end moves from the movement of the beginning, whereas in the moist soft one part may move without another being affected {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.

## Bad rhythm
The cause of a bad-rhythm pulse is, where the shortfall lies in the times of rest, an excess of need, and where it lies in the times of motion, an excess of weakness or want of need. The cause of the full, the empty, the hot, the cold, the high and the low is evident. The line on the wormlike pulse is cut off in the print here and is not cited {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.
