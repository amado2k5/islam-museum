## Nature
Most used of this plant is its root. Dioscorides said that it is a plant with a stem like that of mazaryun and a crown like the crown of dill, resembling hair, and a thick root, but it stings the tongue strongly when tasted {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Selection
The best is the hot one that burns the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.612}

## Temperament
Some of no account claimed it is cold and subtle, but it is hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.612}

## Actions and properties
It draws phlegm when chewed, its power is burning, and it promotes sweat when rubbed on with oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.612}

## Joints
Rubbing with it, with its decoction and with its oil benefits chronic relaxation and numbness of the nerve and prevents tetanus from arising in one in whom it is arising {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.612}

## Organs of the head
It is strongly opening to obstructions of the sieve bone and the nose, its decoction is useful against toothache, especially the cold kind, and its root firms loose teeth if cooked in vinegar and held in the mouth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.612}

## Fevers
If the body is rubbed with it with oil before the onset of rigors it benefits rigors that come with fever and without fever, as some people claimed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.612}
