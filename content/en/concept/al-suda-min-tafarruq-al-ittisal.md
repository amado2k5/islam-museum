## Where it occurs and its causes
A break of continuity may occur in the membranes of the brain, in its substance, or in the vessels, which are then torn {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} It may come from the movement of vapours and winds, from the start or because of an obstruction; it may come from an eating humour; or from a blow, a fall or a cut from outside {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} What comes from within may not knit and may remain an ulcer that harms the head and keeps up the headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} A blow or a fall may be light in burden and be treated, or may reach the point where the brain is shaken by it and perishes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.47-48}

## What is attributed to physicians of India about worms
Some physicians of India are reported to have said that the cause of headache may be worms generated in the regions of the head that hurt by their movement, tearing and eating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} Some people thought this unlikely, but Ibn Sina says it need not be thought unlikely, for worms are often generated between the front of the head and the upper part of the nasal fossae, so it is possible that they arise from the membranes, even if that is rare {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48}
