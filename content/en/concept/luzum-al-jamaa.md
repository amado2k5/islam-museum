## Summary
The narration Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy presents concerns good, evil and the community, and he reads it as a description of the time of trials. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:10:00}

## The narration as he gives it
He says it is narrated in Sahih al-Bukhari. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:10:00} In it a Companion asks the Prophet about evil after good, and about good after evil. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:10:30} (needs_source: the Companion's name is not clear in the automatic transcript.)

## The instruction to hold together
He recounts that the Prophet described people who are known and disliked, who call to the gates of hell. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:11:00} The Prophet instructed Muslims to hold to the community of believers and its leader. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:11:30}

## Application to his time
He holds that this description fits what the nation is living through in his own time. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep01 00:11:30}
