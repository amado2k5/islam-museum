## Summary
The five planets are most irregular at three points on their epicycles: when the planet reaches the far point, when it reaches the mean point, and when it reaches the near point. Elsewhere the epicycle's centre lies between the two apsides on the eccentric. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.

## Checked against observation
He says he checked the observed longitudinal motions of the planets, and entered in the tables what he tested, after adding to each planet's longitude the correction he found for it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.
