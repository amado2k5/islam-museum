## Summary
The major signs are ten portents the Prophet listed in the hadith of Hudhayfa ibn Usayd al-Ghifari: the Smoke, the Dajjal, the Beast, the sun rising from the west, the descent of Isa, Ya'juj and Ma'juj, three landslides, and the Fire driving people to the gathering {cite:sahih-muslim}.
