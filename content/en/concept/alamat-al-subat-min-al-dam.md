## Signs
As for that from blood, it is known from swelling of the jugular veins (awdaj), redness of the eyes and cheeks, redness of the tongue, a sense of heat in the head and the like of what you have learnt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} If the blood or phlegm besides that is gathered as swellings gather, you see the signs of qaranitus, lithargus or the sleepless coma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}
