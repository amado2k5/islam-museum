## Summary
Verses such as [[passage/quran-2-168|al-Baqara 168]] tell people to eat of what is lawful and good on the earth and forbid following the footsteps of the devil {cite:tanzil-quran}. [[passage/quran-5-3|al-Ma'ida 3]] lists what is forbidden as food, such as carrion, blood, swine flesh and what was dedicated to other than God {cite:tanzil-quran}. The books of hadith report the prohibition of every intoxicant {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4343}, of domestic donkeys and of every fanged beast of prey {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3806}, and the making lawful of fish, locust, liver and spleen {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3314}. In the report about the dabb lizard, it was eaten at the Prophet's table while he left it out of distaste {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3793}.

## In the Qur'an
[[passage/quran-2-168|al-Baqara 168]] tells people to eat of what is lawful and good on the earth and forbids them to follow the footsteps of the devil {cite:tanzil-quran}; [[passage/quran-2-172|al-Baqara 172]] tells the believers to eat of the good things God has provided and to give thanks {cite:tanzil-quran}; and [[passage/quran-16-114|al-Nahl 114]] tells them to eat of the lawful and good provision of God and to thank His bounty {cite:tanzil-quran}. [[passage/quran-2-173|al-Baqara 173]] says that God forbade carrion, blood, swine flesh and what was dedicated to other than God, and makes an exception for the one driven by necessity, neither craving nor transgressing {cite:tanzil-quran}; [[passage/quran-16-115|al-Nahl 115]] and [[passage/quran-6-145|al-An'am 145]] repeat the same list {cite:tanzil-quran}, and [[passage/quran-6-145|al-An'am 145]] specifies that the forbidden blood is blood that flows out {cite:tanzil-quran}. [[passage/quran-5-3|al-Ma'ida 3]] gives the detail of what is forbidden as food: carrion, blood, swine flesh, what was dedicated to other than God, what died by strangling, a blow, a fall or goring, what a beast of prey devoured except what was slaughtered while still alive, and what was slaughtered at idols; it also mentions seeking decisions by divining arrows {cite:tanzil-quran}. [[passage/quran-5-4|al-Ma'ida 4]] makes the good things lawful and mentions the catch of trained hunting animals with God's name mentioned over it {cite:tanzil-quran}, and [[passage/quran-5-5|al-Ma'ida 5]] makes the good things lawful and says that the food of the People of the Book is lawful for the believers and their food is lawful for them {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the Sunnah
In [[passage/abudawud-api-3679|Sunan Abi Dawud 3679]] Ibn Umar reports from the Prophet that every intoxicant is wine and every intoxicant is unlawful, and that whoever dies while habitually drinking wine will not drink it in the next life {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3679}. In [[passage/abudawud-api-3681|Sunan Abi Dawud 3681]] Jabir reports that whatever intoxicates in a large amount is unlawful even in a small amount {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3681}, and in [[passage/abudawud-api-3687|Sunan Abi Dawud 3687]] Aisha reports that every intoxicant is unlawful and that of what intoxicates by the faraq measure, a handful is unlawful {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3687}. In [[passage/bukhari-api-4343|Sahih al-Bukhari 4343]], [[passage/bukhari-api-4344|Sahih al-Bukhari 4344]] and [[passage/abudawud-api-3684|Sunan Abi Dawud 3684]] Abu Musa al-Ash'ari asks the Prophet about two drinks made in his land, al-bit' from honey and al-mizr from barley, and the Prophet answers that every intoxicant is unlawful {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4343} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4344} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3684}. In [[passage/abudawud-api-3685|Sunan Abi Dawud 3685]] the Prophet forbade wine, gambling, the drum and al-ghubayra, and said that every intoxicant is unlawful; Abu Dawud adds from Abu Ubayd that al-ghubayra is al-sukurka, a drink the Abyssinians make from sorghum {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3685}. The saying that every intoxicant is unlawful appears in [[passage/arbaun-46|Arba'un 46]] {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam 46}, in [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3387|Sunan Ibn Majah 3387]] from Ibn Umar {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3387}, in [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3388|Sunan Ibn Majah 3388]] from Ibn Mas'ud {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3388}, and in [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3389|Sunan Ibn Majah 3389]] from Mu'awiya with the addition that it is unlawful for every believer {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3389}. In [[passage/abudawud-api-3488|Sunan Abi Dawud 3488]] the Prophet says that God forbade fat to the Jews, who sold it and ate its price, and that when God forbids a people to eat something, He forbids them its price {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3488}. In [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3233|Sunan Ibn Majah 3233]] Abu Hurayra reports that eating every fanged beast of prey is unlawful {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3233}, and in [[passage/abudawud-api-3806|Sunan Abi Dawud 3806]] Khalid ibn al-Walid says that at Khaybar the Prophet declared that the property of those under treaty is not lawful except by right, and that domestic donkeys, their horses and mules, every fanged beast of prey and every clawed bird are forbidden {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3806}. In [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3314|Sunan Ibn Majah 3314]] Ibn Umar reports that the Prophet said two kinds of carrion and two kinds of blood were made lawful for us: the carrion is fish and locust, and the blood is liver and spleen {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3314}. In [[passage/abudawud-api-3793|Sunan Abi Dawud 3793]] Ibn Abbas says that his aunt gave the Prophet ghee, dabb lizards and dried cheese; he ate of the ghee and the cheese and left the dabb out of distaste, and the dabb was eaten at his table, and had it been unlawful it would not have been eaten at God's Messenger's table {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3793}. In [[passage/bukhari-api-7267|Sahih al-Bukhari 7267]] some of the Prophet's Companions, among them Sa'd, began to eat meat, and one of the Prophet's wives called out that it was dabb meat, so they held back; the Prophet said to eat, for it is lawful, or he said there is no harm in it, the narrator being unsure, but that it is not of his own food {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 7267}. In [[passage/bukhari-api-5507|Sahih al-Bukhari 5507]] Aisha reports that some people told the Prophet that others bring them meat and they do not know whether God's name was mentioned over it; he said to mention His name over it themselves and eat it, and Aisha adds that they had recently left disbelief {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5507}. [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3367|Sunan Ibn Majah 3367]] gives the report of Salman about ghee, cheese and furs, in which the lawful is what God made lawful in His Book, the unlawful is what God forbade in His Book, and what He was silent about is among what He pardoned {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3367}.
