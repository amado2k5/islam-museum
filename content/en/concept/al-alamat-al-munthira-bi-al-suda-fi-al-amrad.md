## The urine as a warning sign
Urine like the urine of donkeys indicates that there was a headache which has dissolved, or that there is one that is fixed, or that there will be one {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53} So too the whitening and thinness of the urine in fevers and at the times of crisis indicate the movement of matters to the head, and that is a thing that causes headache without fail {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53}
