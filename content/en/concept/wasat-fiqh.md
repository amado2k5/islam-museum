Wasat in the jurists' usage has three senses: the moderate, the choice, and what lies between two ends. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17187}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17188} The general rule is that what is due in each kind is its middling part; as an example, the zakat on livestock is the middling animal, on the report "but from the middle of your property". {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17188}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17189} Flogging for penalties and discretionary punishment uses a middling whip, neither new and cutting nor worn and painless; stones for stoning are the size of a palm; and the feeding in expiation is of the middle quality of food eaten by one's household, a point on which the jurists differ in detail. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17189}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17190} In the sense of choice, wasat is used of the middle prayer, on whose identity the jurists differ at length. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17190} In the sense of what lies between two ends, it is recommended that the imam stand in the middle of the row; standing at its right or left wing is reported as contrary to the sunna. Women's imam stands in the middle of the women's row. Eating from the middle of a dish or bread is disliked, and sitting in the middle of a circle is disliked by most jurists and forbidden by some Shafi'is. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17190}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17191}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17192}

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] explains the straight path of al-Fatiha as the middle way in creed, worship, morals and law. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:27:30} He says that through the hadith of the three men who wanted to overdo worship the Prophet established the middle way. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:04:00} He links the balance of Surat al-Rahman to ecological balance, and says the command neither to exceed in the balance nor to skimp in it ([[passage/quran-55-8|al-Rahman 8]]) is the method of the middle way (wasatiyya), with neither excess nor laxity, and that holding to it is "the ship of salvation" for the Muslim community. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep19 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep19 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep19 00:33:30} In spending he distinguishes squandering (tabdhir), spending on the forbidden even a little, from excess (israf), overspending on the permitted ([[passage/quran-25-67|al-Furqan 67]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep11 00:19:00}
