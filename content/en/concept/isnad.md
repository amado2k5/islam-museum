## Overview
The *isnad* (chain of transmission) is the series of narrators leading to the text of a hadith, in the form "so-and-so related to us from so-and-so," until the report ends at the Prophet or a Companion. {cite:sahih-muslim}

## Its function
By it the scholars of hadith knew the soundness of a report from its weakness. They studied the narrators for their probity and precision, and they looked into whether the chain was connected or broken. {cite:sahih-muslim}

## In the introduction to Sahih Muslim
Muslim opened his *Sahih* by speaking of the greatness of the isnad and of its protection from falsehood. It has been said that it is one of the distinctions of this community. {cite:sahih-muslim}
