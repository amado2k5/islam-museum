## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that divine legislation carries within it what new cases may arise, without amendment, because God knows what will occur. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.719}

## The completeness of the closing law
Sha'rawi says Islam is the sealing way, with no way after it, so it includes every possibility; human legislation is amended because the legislator misses a ruling when it arises. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.719}

## Guidance from God
He argues that guidance can come only from God: the human legislator benefits from what he legislates and leans toward his own desire, while the one who legislates gains nothing from it. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.777}

## Limits of this batch
Sha'rawi's examples of the political and economic systems of his time are his own view; historical sources on them are not entered here, nor the academic view or other views. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.777}
