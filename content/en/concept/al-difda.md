## What it is and its cause
The ranula is a kind of hard gland forming under the tongue, resembling the frog in the colour compounded of the tongue's surface and its vessels; its cause is a thick viscid moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.260}
## Its treatment
The corroding cutting dissolving drugs of the best drying of this kind are tried on it; if they fail, the drugs named in the Aqrabadhin are used, with venesection beneath the tongue and the strong thrush-drugs; if they fail there is no escape from the hand's work. Among the praised drugs for it is rubbing the ranula boy's tongue with a thing of this kind which heals him, and among the tried is what is gathered with egg-white and placed under the tongue (the names and recipes are left out, the venesection site carried as the book names it and the hand-work as a report) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.260}
