## Nature and cause
It is the counterpart of ru'una, except that it is in the back of the brain, because it is a deficiency in one of the acts of the back of the brain, or an abolition in all of it; its first cause according to Galen is cold, either simple, or with dryness so that the likeness is not impressed in it, or with moisture so that what is impressed in it is not kept {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99}

## Signs
If it is with dryness, sleeplessness points to it, and he keeps past things and cannot keep present and passing things {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99} If it is with moisture, coma points to it, and he does not keep past things at all, and perhaps keeps the present and passing for a longer period than the past; if there is simple cold there, it is numbness (khadar) and dizziness (sadar) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99} It may be from dryness with heat, and then confusion of the mind is with it, either in that part of the brain itself, or in a belly of it or in its vessel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99} It may come from a confusion or ill temperament in the temples carried to the brain; some of the earlier writers mentioned this, and it was tried and witnessed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99} The signs of its causes and kinds are to be learnt from the rules already given and are not repeated in each illness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99}

## Outlook
Most forgetfulness and corruption of memory comes from cold and moisture, and it may come from swellings of the brain, especially the cold; know that forgetfulness, when it comes with health, warns of the grave diseases of the brain, such as epilepsy (sar'), apoplexy and lithargus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99}
