## Summary
After the signs of the original temperaments Ibn Sina gives the signs of foreign, incidental ones: hot, cold, moist and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## Hot
A foreign hot temperament shows a harmful burning of the body, distress from fevers, loss of strength on movement as heat surges, excessive thirst, inflammation at the mouth of the stomach, bitterness in the mouth, a pulse tending to weakness with great speed and frequency, harm from heating things taken and relief from cooling ones, and poor state in summer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## Cold
The signs of an unnatural cold temperament are poor digestion, little thirst, slack joints, many phlegmatic fevers, distress from catarrhs and from taking cooling things, relief from taking warming things, and poor state in winter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## Moist
The signs of an unnatural moist temperament match those of cold, with flabbiness, flow of saliva and mucus, loose bowels, poor digestion, harm from taking what is moist, much sleep and swollen eyelids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## Dry
The signs of an unnatural dry temperament are roughness, sleeplessness, incidental wasting, harm from taking what is dry, poor state in autumn, relief from what moistens, and an immediate soaking up of hot water and fine oil with strong receptivity to them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.
