## Summary
In the sixth chapter Ibn Sina advises coating the traveller's face with viscous substances that act as a glue, such as psyllium mucilage, mucilage of the 'arfaj shrub, tragacanth dissolved in water, gum dissolved in water, egg white and fine wheat cake soaked in water, and refers the treatment of cracking by wind, cold or sun to the discussion of cosmetics {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.265}.

## The coatings
The face is coated with viscous things that carry glue, such as psyllium mucilage, mucilage of the 'arfaj, tragacanth dissolved in water, gum dissolved in water, egg white, fine wheat cake soaked in water, and a lozenge after the recipe of Qurayton {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.265}.

## Cracking
If wind, cold or sun has cracked it, its treatment is sought in the discussion of cosmetics {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.265}.
