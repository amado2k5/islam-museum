## Imagination and thought
The power of estimation and intuition indicates the strength of the brain's temperament, and its weakness an affliction in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13} Of this kind is the power of imagination and forming; when it is strong it helps to indicate the health of the front of the brain, and it is strong when a person can hold well the forms of sensed things such as shapes, designs, tastes, sounds and tunes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13} He mentions that an able geometer looks once at a drawn figure and its form is impressed on his soul {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13}

When the act of imagination fails, the form of a sensed thing is not kept once it has gone; its weakness, its becoming hard to do, and the failure of its act mostly indicate an excess of cold, or dryness or moisture, in the front of the brain, cold being the cause in itself and the other two causes by accident because they bring it on, while change and confusion of its act indicate an excess of heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13}

He says this affliction may befall people of sound mind, whose knowledge of the fair and the foul is complete and whose speech is sound, yet who imagine people present who are not there outside, and imagine the sounds of drummers, as Galen reported that it befell the physician Rutlus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13}

Corruption of the power of thought is either failure, called loss of reason, or weakness, called folly, and their origin is cold, dryness or moisture of the front of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13} When thought is confused until one approves what is not right it is called mixing of reason, and it indicates either a swelling, or a hot dry bilious matter, which is the savage madness, or a black-bilious matter, which is melancholia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13} Of these dispositions, the leaning to cowardice points more to cold and the leaning to boldness and anger more to heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.14} When thoughts move in many movements, confused and varied, there is heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.14}

## Memory and dreams
As for corruption of memory, one kind is failure, as Galen reported that a plague arose in the region of Abyssinia from many carcasses left after a fierce battle, and then reached the land of the Greeks, bringing such forgetfulness that a man forgot the name of himself and of his father {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.14}

Most of what befalls as weakness of memory comes from a corruption in the back of the brain from cold, moisture or dryness; when it is confused so that the person recalls what he never knew, it indicates a hot temperament with matter or without matter, a dry matter being the likelier {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.14}

Inference from dreams: seeing many yellow and hot things indicates the prevalence of yellow bile, and likewise seeing many things that suit one temperament or another {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.14} Confused dreams indicate heat and dryness and so warn of hot brain diseases, while frightening dreams and ones that are not remembered mostly indicate cold and moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.14}
