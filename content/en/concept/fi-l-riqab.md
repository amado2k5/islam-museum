## Overview
The fifth zakat category: helping contracted slaves pay their manumission and freeing Muslim captives — among the greatest doors of emancipation in the law; jurists differ over its continuance after slavery ended. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
