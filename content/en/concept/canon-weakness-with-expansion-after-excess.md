## Summary
Ibn Sina notes that excess exercise with little rubbing, excess intercourse and frequent bathing can be followed by weakness with expansion, treated with restorative exercise, dry rubbing with an astringent oil and moistening food in small quantity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.247}.

## Causes and remedy
After excess exercise with little rubbing there may come weakness with expansion, and it may also come from excess intercourse and frequent bathing. They should be treated with restorative exercise and dry rubbing toward firmness with an astringent oil, and take moistening foods small in quantity and balanced between heat and cold, or slightly toward heat. They do the same if weakness, sleeplessness, grief or dryness from anger comes upon them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.247}.
