## Summary
Ibn Sina describes the hot-tempered who are balanced in the passive qualities as nearer to health, quicker to grow teeth and hair, eloquent and swift in walking, with bile often generated in them; their regimen is helped by vomiting with hot water or purging with preserved violet, tamarind, shir khushk and manna, by lighter exercise and up to three baths a day, and by opening remedies if stretching arises in the liver and abdomen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.255-256}.

## Signs of the balanced hot
Those of hot temperament who are balanced in the two passive qualities are nearer to health at the start, their constitution quicker to grow teeth and hair, and they are eloquent, fluent and swift walkers. Then, if heat becomes excessive and dryness grows, a biting temperament arises, and in many of them bile is generated in abundance. Their regimen in the first age is that of the balanced; when they move on, they are moved to the regimen of one whose urine is to be promoted and whose bile is to be evacuated {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.255}.

## Vomiting and purging
When nature does not suffice to turn the humour toward evacuation, it is helped by gentle things. For vomiting, something like drinking much hot water alone or with date wine; for purging, preserved violet, tamarind, shir khushk and manna {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.255}.

## Exercise, bath and opening remedies
Their exercise is lightened and they are fed food of good chyme; at times they should bathe three times in a day, and every warming cause is kept from them. If bathing after a meal does not bring stretching or hardening in the region of the liver and abdomen, they may use it safely; if something of the kind appears, they take opening remedies such as an infusion of wormwood, aloe water, anise, bitter almond and oxymel, and are kept from bathing after food. The opening remedies are given after the first meal is digested and before the second, anointing with oil is kept up, thin white wine is given, and cold water helps them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.255-256}.
