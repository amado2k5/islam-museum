## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the signs of a dominant phlegm from colour, touch, saliva, urine, sleep, pulse and dreams {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.

## The signs
Excess whiteness of colour, puffiness, softness to the touch, coldness, much sticky saliva and little thirst unless the phlegm is salty, especially in old age, weak digestion, sour belching, white urine, much sleep and laziness, slackness of the nerves, dullness, and a soft pulse tending to slowness and irregular spacing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.

## Indications and dreams
Age, habit, earlier regimen, trade and country also count, and dreams in which waters, rivers, snow, rain and cold with shivering are seen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.
