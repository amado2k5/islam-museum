## Overview
The sura in Qur'anic-sciences usage is the named division of the masahif comprising a number of ayat; the masahif count a hundred and fourteen suras {cite:itqan-suyuti}.

## In the sources
Al-Suyuti's Itqan treats the meaning of Qur'an, sura and aya among the first topics of tafsir terminology {cite:itqan-suyuti}; the frozen Tanzil text makes the suras and their names the reference organization {cite:tanzil-quran}.
