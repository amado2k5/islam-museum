## Summary
In the tenth chapter Ibn Sina puts off to the particular books and to the treatise on treatment the discussion of coitus, its moderation and the mending of its harm, and of laxative drugs and the mending of their harm, saying the keeper of health must attend to evacuation, expectoration, easy sweating and urination {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.242}.

## What is put off
The tenth chapter is on what must be put off from this place. Among what would be mentioned here is coitus, its moderation and the mending of its harm, and Ibn Sina leaves it to the particular books. Also spoken of here would be laxative drugs and the mending of their harm, and part of that he leaves to his treatise on treatment and part to his discussion of laxative drugs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.242}.

## The keeper of health
Ibn Sina says only that the one who keeps his health must attend to evacuation, expectoration, easy sweating and the passing of urine. The printed text is garbled in the order of these words, so they are given as they come, with nothing added {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.242}.
