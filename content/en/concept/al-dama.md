## Its definition, divisions and causes
This disease is that the eye is always wet with a watery moisture and a tear may flow; some of it is congenital, some incidental; of the incidental, some is necessary in health and some follows a disease, ceasing when it ceases, as in the fevers. The cause of the incidental is weakness of the holding or the digesting-concocting power, or a decrease of the canthus in nature, or from using a sharp drug, or after cutting the pterygium. The source of those moistures is the brain, flowing from it to the eye in one of the two paths repeatedly mentioned. The congenital kind is for a brain lesion and brain swellings; it may occur in the sleepless fevers among the day's fevers; in the putrid sanguine fevers it is much, and the tear's flowing may be much in distension — all of this is of the quick-passing incidental kind following a disease {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.188}

## The treatment
The canon in treating it is using the moderate astringent drugs; the kind occurring after pterygium cutting or its being eaten by a drug is treated with drugs of the named kind — if it has perished and been extirpated it never regrows; the kind not from pterygium cutting is treated with tutty and the tutty kohls and all the viscid collyria ⁦(the drug, kohl and recipe names are not carried)⁩. Among what has been tried for it is entering the bath fasting and staying in it, and dripping vinegar and water into the eye frequently. As for the congenital kind, it hardly accepts treatment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.188} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.189}
