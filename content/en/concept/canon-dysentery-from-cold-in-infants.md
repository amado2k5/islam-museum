## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions dysentery that befalls infants from a cold that strikes them, treated with a drug pounded, sifted, kneaded with old cow's ghee and given in cold water; the names of its drugs are garbled in the scanned text and are not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.

## Treatment
Children may have zahir, a loosening of the belly with a little blood, from a cold that strikes them, and it helps them to take drugs, three dirhams of each, pounded, sifted, kneaded with old cow's ghee, and a dose given in cold water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}. The names of the drugs are garbled in the scanned text and are left out.
