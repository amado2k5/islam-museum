## Summary
For Ibn Sina dull pain comes from a very cold temperament, from blocked pores in the passages of the sensing spirit running to the organ by a nerve, or from fullness of vessels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## The three causes
The cause of dull pain is one of three: a very cold temperament, blockage of the pores of the passages of the sensing spirit that runs to the organ through a nerve, or fullness of vessels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.
