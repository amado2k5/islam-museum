## The maqala heading
The fifth maqala is headed, as printed, 'on brain illnesses whose afflictions are in the acts of voluntary movement qawiyya' (the last word carried as printed, not interpreted) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.113}

## Nature
Dawar is that the person imagines that things are turning around him and that his brain and body are turning, so that he cannot stand firm but falls, and he often dislikes sounds; there comes to him of itself what comes to one who has turned round on himself fast and long and cannot stand firm, standing or sitting, or open his eyes; that is because of what comes by itself to the pneuma in the cavities of his brain and in its veins and arteries, as it comes to it when it turns continually {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.113}

## How it differs from epilepsy and from sadar
The difference between epilepsy and dawar is that dawar may last a while, while epilepsy is sudden and its sufferer falls still and recovers; sadar is that when the person stands his eye darkens and he is ready to fall, and the severe kind resembles epilepsy except that it is not with convulsion, as epilepsy is {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.113}
