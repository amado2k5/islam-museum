## Nature
It is well known. Some say that the root of the mountain tarkhun is what they call aqir qarha. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}

## Temperament
It appears hot and dry to the second degree, though it has a numbing power. One writer not relied upon says it is hot and dry. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}

## Properties
It dries humours and absorbs them, and it has some useful cooling. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}

## Organs of the head
It helps thrush when chewed and held in the mouth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}

## Organs of the breath
It causes throat pain. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}

## Organs of digestion
It causes difficult digestion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}
