## Principles of cure
As for the coma that is a sign of an illness in some organ, the way of its cure is, as printed, "fasd" of that organ by regimen so that it is cleansed and what is in it goes, and the brain is strengthened so that it does not receive the matter; the word "fasd" is carried as printed and is not interpreted {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93} Then he passes to what dissolves, if something is held in the brain; this is known from the rule given for fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}

## From a drink taken
What comes from a drink of the kind listed under poisons is treated according to what was drunk, as he says in Book V {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}
