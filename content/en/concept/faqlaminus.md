## Nature
It is said to be bakhur maryam, a genus of artanitha {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Actions and properties
Its power purifies by cleansing and cutting, opens and dissolves, and when the root is drunk it causes much sweating and also vertigo {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Cosmetics
Drunk with grape-syrup or honey-water it cures jaundice, and its root purifies the skin and removes freckles; its decoction benefits the chaps that come from cold, and likewise oil warmed in its root {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Swellings and pustules
Its root removes pustules and its juice dissolves hardenings; it dissolves swelling of the spleen, scrofula and wounds, fresh or dry, and removes scurf too {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its root mixed with vinegar and honey, or alone, heals wounds before they grow old, and its decoction poured on the head suits the ulcers there {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Joints
It benefits twisting of the nerve and gout, all as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Organs of the head
Mixed with wine it intoxicates strongly, and its water is sometimes snuffed up to cleanse the head, and it calms cold headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Ocular organs
Its water with honey suits the water that comes in the eye and weakness of sight, and likewise snuffed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Organs of the chest
Some give its root to drink to those with asthma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is applied as a poultice on the spleen with vinegar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Excretory organs
Drunk with badhrumali it loosens phlegm and watery chyme, and its root drunk with honey loosens strongly; it is used as a pessary on wool for loosening the belly, and its water mixed with vinegar and smeared on a prolapsed anus returns it inward, and its juice opens the mouths of the veins in the anus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that drunk or used as a pessary it brings on the menses, that some claimed that its fresh root, tied on the neck or the upper arm, prevents conception, that smeared on the navel, the flank and the side it makes the foetus fall, and that it kills the foetus strongly, its juice being stronger in this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Poisons and bites
It is drunk with wine against deadly drugs and poisons, especially the sea hare {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}
