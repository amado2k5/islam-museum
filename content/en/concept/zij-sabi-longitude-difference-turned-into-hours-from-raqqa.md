## Summary
To get the hours of another place than Raqqa, take the difference between its longitude and Raqqa's from the table of city longitudes and divide it by fifteen; the result is the hours of distance. If the city's longitude is greater than Raqqa's, the hours of distance are subtracted from the equated hours, since the city lies to the east; if it is less, they are added. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.109}.

## The rule of fifteen
Dividing by fifteen means that each fifteen degrees of longitude equal one hour of time; this is what turns a difference in longitude into a difference in hours. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.109}.
