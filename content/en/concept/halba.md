## Temperament and actions
Temperament: dry at the end of the first degree, and never free of a strange moisture. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Actions: its strength ripens and softens, because warmth and stickiness are gathered in it; its stickiness keeps the harm of its heat from prevailing, and its heat works gently. Its juice is poor, though not little. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: its oil with myrtle helps hair and the traces of sores; it helps cold chapping with its saliva, especially with rose oil, and it enters remedies for kalaf and for improving the complexion; it changes the taste and removes the stench of the body and of sweat. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Swellings and pustules: it dissolves phlegmy and hard swellings; its flour helps hot swellings inside and out when they are not inflamed but have turned somewhat hard, and it softens and ripens tubercles. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Wounds: with rose oil it helps burns. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Organs of the head: washed on the head it cleans scurf, especially with bitter stuff. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Organs of the chest: clears the voice, feeds the lung a little, softens the chest and throat, and calms cough and asthma, especially boiled with honey, dates or figs. Best is to gather it with a thick date, take the juice of both, mix with much honey, warm gently over embers, and take it well before a meal. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.487}
Organs of digestion: with natron it helps the spleen as a poultice. Its decoction in vinegar is for a weak stomach, especially the fresh kind; vinegar and bitter stuff prevent the harm of eating it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.487}
Excretory organs: a patient sits in its decoction for swelling and pain of the womb and its contraction; its decoction in vinegar helps ulcers of the bowel, and the fresh kind eaten in mouthfuls with vinegar likewise. Its decoction in water helps tenesmus and diarrhoea, and its oil helps swellings of the anus; it is also given by enema for tenesmus and colic, and it stirs the bowel to clear its contents because of its sharpness. Its decoction with honey loosens thick humours; with duck fat it helps a womb hard in labour. It is good for piles, sweetens the stool, and makes the urine and sweat foul. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.487}
