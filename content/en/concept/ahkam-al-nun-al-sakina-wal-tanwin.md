## The chapter
Al-Jamzuri states in Tuhfat al-Atfal: "for the nun when it is still, and for the tanwin, there are four rules — so take my explanation", and Ibn al-Jazari divides the same ruling in the Jazariyya into izhar, idgham, iqlab and ikhfa {cite:ar-wikisource}.

## The four rules
Izhar before the six throat letters: hamza, ha, ayn, ha, ghayn and kha. Idgham in the six letters gathered in «يرملون»: with ghunna in «ينمو», and without ghunna in the lam and the ra — except when the nun and the letter fall in one word, as in «دنيا» and «صنوان», where there is no idgham. Iqlab at the ba, the nun turned into a mim with ghunna and ikhfa. And ikhfa at the remaining letters, gathered by al-Jamzuri in the line «صف ذا ثنا كم جاد شخص قد سما دم طيبًا زد في تقى ضع ظالما» {cite:ar-wikisource}.

## In the two matns
The chapter comes brief in the Jazariyya, in four bayts, and detailed in the Tuhfa with the letters' examples and mnemonics; it is the first of the rules with which children begin memorising tajwid in both matns {cite:ar-wikisource}.
