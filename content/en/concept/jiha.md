## Overview

Jiha in the language is the place one faces or aims at. In fiqh it names the purpose to which property is assigned. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6667}

## Endowment for a perpetual purpose

An endowment for a purpose that never ends, such as the poor, is allowed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6669}

## Disputes over the purpose

If the giver says the payment was a gift and the receiver says it was part of the dower, the one who denies the contract is believed on oath. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6669}

## How a child follows Islam

A child follows Islam when one of its parents converts, or when it is a foundling in Muslim territory. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6670}
