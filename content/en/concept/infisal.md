## Definition
Infisal is lexically cutting off, the reflexive of fasl and the opposite of joining; it may be visible or hidden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3536}

## Related term
Bayna, separation, which is used mainly of a divorce that is not revocable. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3536}

## Parts that separate
- What is forbidden to look at before separation remains forbidden after it, such as the private parts. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3536}
- A woman who takes in a severed male member incurs no hadd punishment, though the act is forbidden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3536}
- Parts separated from a dead body are washed, prayed over and buried by some jurists, citing the practice of the Companions; Abu Hanifa and Malik hold that the prayer is made only if most of the body is present. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3536}
- The Shafi'is recommend wrapping and burying what separates from a living person, such as a hand, a nail, hair or a clot. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3536}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3537}

## Miscarriage and birth
- A miscarriage born alive and then dying takes the ruling of an adult in naming, inheritance, retaliation, washing, prayer and burial; some Malikis exclude naming before the seventh day. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3537}
- One born dead is not prayed over but is buried; the washing and shrouding are disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3537}
- One born dead does not inherit, by agreement; the Hanafis hold that one separated by a blow from a wrongdoer inherits, by the rule of the ghurra. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3537}

## Separation of spouses
It happens by one of three things: dissolution or its effect, divorce, or death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3537}

## Separation in an exception
If the excepted matter is separated from the rest for a long time in an acknowledgement or oath, the exception is void; some say it is valid while the session continues. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3537}
