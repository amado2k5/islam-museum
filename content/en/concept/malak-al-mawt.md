## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an names him expressly: 'Say: the angel of death, who is entrusted with you, will take you' [[passage/quran-32-11|al-Sajdah 11]], and elsewhere says people are taken by night and day [[passage/quran-6-60|al-An'am 60]].

## In hadith
Muslim and Ibn Majah narrate in the hadith of the Isra' that the angel of death is charged with taking souls, coming to people and taking them as commanded {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:sunan-ibn-majah}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details his work, noting he is charged with taking souls and is among the angels entrusted with death {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Scholars differ on his appearance and how many he takes at once, but agree he is an angel charged with the taking; some mention he comes to the grave with Munkar and Nakir {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
