## Summary
Sha'rawi explains talbis as putting one thing into another so that the appearance changes, as a person dresses in a garment. He reads the verse ([[passage/quran-3-71|Al Imran 71]]) as addressing some People of the Book who mixed truth with falsehood and concealed it knowingly {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1537}.

## Forms of talbis
Sha'rawi counts among these the alteration of the Torah and the Gospel by adding what neither Moses nor Jesus brought. His chief example is the denial of the tidings of the Prophet, which he says were present in their books. They professed faith in Moses and Jesus and then denied the final prophet, though they knew Islam to be the true religion {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1537}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1538}.

## The verse in context
Sha'rawi reports that some of the People of the Book told their companions to believe in what was sent down to the believers at the start of the day and to disbelieve at its end. He reads this as a ruse to spread doubt among the unlettered believers. He treats the following verse as the reply, stating that guidance is God's guidance and that bounty is in his hand {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1538}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1539}.
