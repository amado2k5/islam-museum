## Nature and selection
The stone that draws iron; the best is black tinged with red, pure and unmixed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.562}.

## Actions and properties
Cleansing and purifying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.562}.

## Excretory organs
It is given in the drink of iron filings, and for one in whose belly iron dross is held, the stone draws it and takes it with it on leaving. It is said that given with the water of qaratin it loosens a thick chyme; the quantity named is not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.562}.
