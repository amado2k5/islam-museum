## Overview
The display is the scene of the Day where creation is presented to God for reckoning and recompense: 'stop them; they are to be questioned' [[passage/quran-37-24|the stopping verse]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the texts
The Qur'an pictures them presented in ranks: 'and they are presented to your Lord in ranks — you have come to Us as We created you the first time' [[passage/quran-18-48|the ranked-display verse]]. In the hadith God has the servants stand before Him until the sweat bridles them and reaches their ears {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
