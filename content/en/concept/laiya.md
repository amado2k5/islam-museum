## Nature and selection
A foothill tree with a flower faintly sweet-smelling that bees graze; it may be the tree called by names in the text (the reading is uncertain), though the author says he does not verify it. Its power is akin to that of marrubium, but weaker; it is a yatu' (spurge). The editor's note places it among the spurges {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree; it is also said to be hot and dry to the fourth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Actions and properties
The book says that if some of its milk is thrown into a fish pool it 'puts it out' (the book's word) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It makes one vomit strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Excretory organs
It loosens water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.
