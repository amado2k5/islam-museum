## Nature
Dioscorides said that the people of Jurjan call it bawt; it is a plant with a jointed stem like the stem of razyanaj, large, with a leaf like the leaf of barna'us but thinner, heavy in smell, with branches at its top and a crown bearing a white flower and a seed like anisun but whiter, and it has hollow roots; this drug is one of the deadly drugs and kills by cold; Rufus said its leaf is like the leaf of yabruh but yellower and much more yellow, its root thin and without fruit, its seed the colour of nanakhwah but larger, without taste or smell, and it has a viscid moisture; Masih said it is a kind of bish, and did not do well; the author says that qubiyun occurs in Greek and was translated by shawkaran and also by bish, and the symptoms of bish have been attributed to qubiyun, so people disagree about it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.674-675}

## Temperament
Cold and dry in the third degree to the fourth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Choice
The best is what is baqriti, atbi'i and qaliqla, as the book prints the names {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Actions and properties
It prevents the flow of blood, congeals the blood and numbs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Cosmetics
When smeared on the place of plucking, its coldness prevents the hair growing again, and it is laid as a poultice on the breast so that it does not enlarge {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Swellings and pustules
Its juice soothes the carbuncle and namla {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Joints
A smear on hot gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Organs of the head
Its juice is good for the moistures that befall the ear, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Ocular organs
Its juice is used for pains of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Organs of the chest
It is laid on the breast as a poultice so that it does not enlarge, and it prevents the flow of milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Excretory organs
It stops blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Poisons and bites
It is a deadly poison, and its treatment is the drinking of the pure drink (al-sharab al-sirf) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it benefits pain of the wombs, that the testicle is laid with it as a poultice so that it does not enlarge, and that the organs of semen are anointed with it and so it prevents nocturnal emission {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}
