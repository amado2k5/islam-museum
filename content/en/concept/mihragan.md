## Overview
*Mihragan* is the sixteenth day of Mihr (Mehr) among the Persians. It lies one hundred and seventy-four days from Nawruz. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.507}

Al-Masudi counts it among the festivals of the non-Arabs in which they show gladness. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.507}
