## Nature
An animal like the tick, known in Syria, found in beds, and it seems to be what is known among us as al-anhal. The editor's footnote says that fasafis is the bedbug {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Organs of the chest
It is said that drunk with vinegar or with wine it brings the leech out of the throat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Excretory organs
It is said that ground and put in the opening of the urethra it cures difficulty in urination {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that smelled it benefits suffocation of the womb and revives {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Fevers
It is said that put in a broad bean and swallowed before the quartan fever takes hold it benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Poisons and bites
It is said that swallowed without the bean it benefits the stings of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}
