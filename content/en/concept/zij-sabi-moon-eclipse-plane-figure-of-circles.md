## The geometric figure
The reckoning draws the eclipse as circles on a flat plane: a circle of the sum of half-radii around the centre, circles for the Moon at the beginning, at the end, and a circle for the shadow. The Moon's latitude is laid off on the appropriate side, which fixes the centre of the Moon at the beginning, at mid-eclipse and at the end. The shadow circle touches the two Moon circles, and the line between the Moon's centres at the start and at the middle measures the minutes of fall. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.158-160}

## The line of passage
The text says that the line through the three centres of the Moon circles is the Moon's path from the beginning to the end of the eclipse. The letters of the figure on page 160 are Arabic abjad letters in the OCR and are not reproduced here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.160}
