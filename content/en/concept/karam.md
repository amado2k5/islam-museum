## Summary
For Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy, generosity (karam) is to give without asking to whom one gives or how much {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:13:00}. He holds that this meaning truly applies only to God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:14:00}, who provides for everyone and leaves the sun, the rain and the air for them {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:15:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 08)

The Sheikh defines the truly generous person as one who, when he gives, does not ask to whom he is giving or how much {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:13:00}.

He gives the example of a brother who asks his brother for something the other can afford: delaying it, or giving less than was asked, is in his view not generosity {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:13:30}.

He says that the generous one asks neither whom he gave nor how much, and that this meaning truly applies only to God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:14:00}.

He mentions that the population of the earth is about seven billion (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:14:00}.

He estimates that about one and a half billion of them are monotheists and about five and a half billion are not, yet God provides for all of them (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:14:30}.

He says that God has not withheld the sun, the rain or the air from the people of a country that denies Him, that He left oxygen in the air for everyone, and that floods and diseases come from time to time as a wake-up call to people {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:15:00}.
