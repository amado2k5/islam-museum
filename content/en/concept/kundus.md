## Nature and temperament
Its root is what is mostly used, and it is well known. Some say its nature is hot and dry from the third to the fourth degree. The editor's note calls it the sneezing-wood. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519}

## Actions and properties
It is a cleansing, scouring, blistering, sharp, irritant plant that provokes vomiting, and it cuts phlegm and black bile. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519}

## Cosmetic use and skin
It clears leukoderma and vitiligo, especially the dark kind, and freckles. It helps scabies greatly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519}

## Organs of the head, eye and digestion
A sneezing agent, one of the medicines that clear the ear and wash out its dirt. It disperses wind in the nostrils, helps catarrh, and opens strongly the blockages of the strainer (the nasal passage). It may help the eye preparations made for sight. It is a strong emetic and dissolves hardness of the spleen. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): a purgative that brings on urine; when it is taken, it brings on the menses, expels the foetus, and breaks up stone very well. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519}

## Substitutes
Its substitute for vomiting is the nutmeg of vomiting; the weights are left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519}
