## Overview
The first generation differed over writing hadith: both prohibition and permission were transmitted, and the prevailing reports favor permission — the author presents 'Abd Allah b. 'Amr b. al-'As's sheet called *al-Sadiqa*, written with the Prophet's leave; the Muslims afterwards agreed to broaden the license, without which knowledge would have perished in later ages {cite:manhaj-tawthiq-al-sunna-azhar p. 28}{cite:manhaj-tawthiq-al-sunna-azhar p. 29}{cite:manhaj-tawthiq-al-sunna-azhar p. 31}. Ibn al-Salah reconciles the conflicting reports: permission went to those feared forgetful, prohibition to those trusted for memory — or lest hadith be mixed with sheets of the Qur'an {cite:manhaj-tawthiq-al-sunna-azhar p. 30}. Compilation followed.
