## Its definition and treatment
It is a disease following the itch-scab, and cold applications on the lid may bequeath it. Its treatment: collyriation with polishing drugs, always using the bath and avoiding wine; it may be much rubbed with the probe and the soft red collyrium, while rubbing with sugar may inflame it or scab over ⁦(the named drugs are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.194}
