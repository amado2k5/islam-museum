## Overview
Ahmad 'Umar Hashim holds unity the basis of every good in this world and the next, and division among the gravest afflictions — citing the verse of holding fast to God's rope and Abu Hurayra's hadith on what God first approves for Muslims {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 114} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 115} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 116}. He recounts — in the formula "it is said" — the long enmity of Aws and Khazraj reconciled by Islam {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 115}. The communal worships — congregational and 'Id prayers, hajj, fasting, zakat — are unity put into practice.
