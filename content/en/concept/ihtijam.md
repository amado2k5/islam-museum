## Summary
Ihtijam is asking for cupping. Cupping is a medical act with the consequences of medicine; for instance, the cupper may look at the private parts of the cupped person in necessity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1718} The blood of cupping is impure like other blood, though wiping suffices to clean the wound out of necessity, and the mosque must be kept free of cupping. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1718} Liability for harm caused by the cupper is treated under injuries. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1718}
