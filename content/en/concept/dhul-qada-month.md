## Definition and name
Dhu al-Qa'da is the eleventh month, named for the Arabs sitting out journeys and raids in honour of its sanctity; it is one of the four sacred months. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}

## In the seerah
In Dhu al-Qa'da of year seven the Prophet performed the 'umra of qada', staying in Mecca three days, and in Dhu al-Qa'da of year eight the 'umra of al-Ji'rana after his return from Hawazin. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## Its sanctity and rules
It opens the three consecutive sacred months; fighting is forbidden in it on the majority view except defensively, and in it the pilgrims enter ihram for the hajj. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
