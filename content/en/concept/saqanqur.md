## Nature
A Nile lizard hunted in Egypt, which they claim is the offspring of the crocodile on land {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Selection
The best part of it is the region of the loins {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it can rouse desire so that it is calmed only by sipping broth of lettuce and lentils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.
