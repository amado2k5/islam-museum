## Summary
Al-Battani finds the weekday from the remainder when the day count is divided by seven. The remainder marks the day, counted from Sunday, and the result shows the weekday on which the sought month begins. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.

## A rule to keep to
He recommends repeating the reckoning in the same order each time, and that the zij's dates be computed by this reckoning alone, without adding or subtracting from it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.
