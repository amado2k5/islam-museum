## Summary
"The model for discovering leaders" is the first of three models on which [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] builds the making of a leader in his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]]: discovery, then leadership upbringing, then training. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:03:30} It has three parts: the signs, what to look for, and how to look. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:03:00} Its idea, for him, is that an ordinary person cannot be made directly into a leader, but an effective person can, and someone who is not yet effective must first go through leadership upbringing. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:04:00} The signs are probabilities, not certainties: they show that a person's readiness to lead is higher than others', and the more of them meet, the higher the probability. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:42:30} He set it out in episodes 9 to 13.

## The six signs
**1. Analytical ability.** He says he used to call this sign "intelligence", until, while writing his book *The Making of Intelligence*, he became convinced that IQ is largely inherited and that there is no scientific link between leadership and general intelligence; analysis, by contrast, is a part of intelligence that can be trained. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:10:30} Analysis for him means breaking a complex situation or text into its elements, connecting scattered information, and anticipating the course of events. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:12:00} Its marks include clever questions, clever comments, quick understanding and sound predictions; for quick understanding he cites the Prophet at Badr asking two captured water-boys how many camels the Quraysh army slaughtered each day, and, on hearing nine or ten, estimating the army at nine hundred to a thousand. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:34:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:35:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:36:30}

**2. Taking the initiative.** Here he looks for projects, ideas, managing groups, founding organisations and creativity. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:48:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:11:30} For projects he cites the tent of [[person/rufayda-al-aslamiyya|Rufayda al-Aslamiyya]] in the Prophet's mosque at the time of the Trench, which he calls the first military hospital in Islam and its keeper the first military nurse, citing Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:48:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep09 00:49:30} For creativity, [[person/salman-al-farisi|Salman al-Farisi]]'s proposal to dig the trench. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:23:30} For founding organisations, [[person/abu-basir|Abu Basir]], who after the Treaty of Hudaybiyya gathered a band on the coast that intercepted Quraysh's caravans, until Quraysh asked for the clause to be dropped under which anyone coming from Mecca as a Muslim was handed back. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:22:00}

**3. Courage.** He defines it as "holding firm despite fear", a virtue between recklessness and cowardice. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:28:30} Its marks include standing firm in moments of fear, as the Prophet did at Hunayn, when, in his account, fewer than a hundred of twelve thousand stood with him; boldness of opinion; criticism, which he says must come with achievement and aim at ideas, not persons; and influence on others. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:45:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep10 00:49:30}

**4. Seriousness.** It is not the opposite of humour, which is a helpful trait, but of triviality and marginality; people for him are of three grades: marginal, then effective, then leader. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:05:30} Its marks: activity on public questions of opinion, for which he cites the Prophet's presence in his youth at the [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Hilf al-Fudul]], adding his view that Muslims support international agreements that lift injustice; membership of several clubs; hobbies that build skill; reading and breadth of culture; and care for managing time. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:27:30}

**5. Ambition.** For him it is the aspiration to lofty things, praiseworthy only when it agrees with the Sharia. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:35:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:36:00} Its marks: aspiring to high positions, wanting to leave a lasting mark, and setting goals that are clear, realistic, hard but possible, time-bound and measurable. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep11 00:48:30}

**6. A leadership environment.** The children of leaders, officers and senior academics have a higher chance, provided they actually mix with their parents' world; only about 10% of leaders' children, he says, turn out otherwise, a lower share in his view than in the rest of society. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:05:30} The kinds of such environment, for him: the leadership family, the wealthy family, the activist family, and being an orphan; the orphan, in his view, keeps a mother's tenderness and carries responsibility early. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:25:30} He holds that all these environments met in the Prophet. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:32:00}

## How to look
His means of discovery: intelligence and multiple-intelligence tests; personality tests, for which he cites the hadith of Anas describing several Companions each by his particular strength; talent tests and what is called "talent management"; and personal interviews, in which the candidate is asked about his family, his projects and his part in them, the organisations he founded or joined, and what he reads. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:32:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:43:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:44:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:48:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep12 00:50:30}

## Applied to Amr ibn al-As
He devoted episode 13 to applying the six signs to [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] as a "natural-born leader". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep13 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep13 00:23:00} Natural-born leaders, he says, are about 1% of people; 98% can learn leadership; 1% are unfit for it; and those closest to the natural-born are the easiest to train. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep13 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep13 00:03:30}
