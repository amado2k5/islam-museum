## Actions and properties
It is not drunk in anything, but is used from outside, among the cleansing drugs used from outside and among the softening and dissolving poultices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Cosmetics
It is applied to bahaq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Ulcers
It is applied to scab and qawabi, and eating ulcers are covered with it as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Organs of nourishment
The roots of the white kind are given with wine and one with dropsy benefits from them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Excretory organs
The roots of its white kind kill worms {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Poisons
In its black kind there is something deadly; a report attributed to the book, without any method {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}
