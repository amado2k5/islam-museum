## Summary
Chapter 24 begins with finding the star's distance from the equator and the part that culminates with it from the azimuth of its rising or setting, when that is known by measurement. The chord of the altitude of the first of Aries in that place is multiplied by the chord of the azimuth of the star's rising or setting and divided by the radius, and the arc of the result is the star's distance from the equator in the direction of the azimuth. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.58}.

## The wording as it stands
This formula is reported as it stands in the text without judging it; in part of it the scan's wording seems to alter the forms of the chords, so nothing beyond what was read is claimed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.58}.
