## Temperament
Cold and dry in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Actions and properties
It has strengthening and softening. The book states the proportion of water for cooking it, which is not carried over here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Cosmetic uses
With saffron on freckles it removes them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It heals ulcers and mends them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Eyes
It stops the flow of matters to the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Chest and breathing
It softens the chest, and a gruel made from it keeps catarrh from the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Excretory organs
Nashastaj alone, or with lentils, binds the nature and prevents the passage of bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.
