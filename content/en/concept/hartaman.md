## Nature and selection
Nature: its grain has the strength of barley, sitting between wheat and barley; its gruel and coarse meal are more astringent than barley gruel and meal. Temperament: moderate, leaning to moisture. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}

## Actions
It dries without sting, with resolving and astringency together. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
