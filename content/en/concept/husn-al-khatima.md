## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 9)

The Sheikh tells of a famous female singer who lay dying for months, asked for a drum to be brought and beaten to her rhythm, and whose soul departed to the beat of the drum (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:22:00}. He mentions that Abu Hamid al-Ghazali devoted a chapter to good endings and bad endings {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:22:30}. He recounts a man who worked in auctions and who, while dying, kept repeating the calls of the auction, asking who would bid higher, each time he was told to say the declaration of oneness (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:22:30}.

He recounts a man addicted to playing chess who, while dying, spoke the words of the game each time his sons prompted him to say the testimony of faith, and who died in that state (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:23:00}. The Sheikh says that people die on what they lived by and are raised on what they died on, and he prays that God grant us a good ending {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:23:30}. He then returns to the surah of al-Nazi'at, saying that the "Nazi'at" ([[passage/quran-79-1|Qur'an 79:1]]) relate to the criminal disbelievers, and that the "Nashitat" ([[passage/quran-79-2|Qur'an 79:2]]) are angels who draw out the believer's soul to meet its Lord {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:23:30}.

The Sheikh relates a conversation with a man who said he loves God; asked what keeps him from meeting Him, the man answered that it was the departure of the soul, and the Sheikh reminded him of the hadith that whoever loves to meet God, God loves to meet him, and whoever dislikes meeting God, God dislikes meeting him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:24:00}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim that a sign of success at the end is turning to God at the beginning, and moves from it to a good end: he mentions the hadith that a man may do the deeds of the people of Paradise as far as people can see and then have his life sealed with the deeds of the people of the Fire, and the reverse, and that deeds are judged by their endings; he says he prefers Muslim's version of it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:23:30} He applies the maxim to beginnings in the family, advising the choice of a righteous mother before a child is born, and quoting the answer given to one who asked whom to marry his daughter to: marry her to someone who fears God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:13:30}
