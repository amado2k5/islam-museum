## Nature and selection
The best is the pure and the kind that swells and increases in water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Actions and properties
A gluing and astringent force {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Helps pains of the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Excretory organs
Stronger in restraining the belly than the sealed earth, and kills worms {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.
