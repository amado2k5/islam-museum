## Overview
Al-Suyuti reports from Ibn Ishaq that Quraysh had no king to whom all affairs returned. Each clan had a general authority for its chief. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

## The offices
Among the Banu Hashim were the provision of water and the provision of food, meaning that no one ate or drank except from their food and drink. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

Among the Banu Abd al-Dar were the chamberlainship, the standard, and Dar al-Nadwa. That is, no one entered the House except by their permission; when Quraysh tied a war banner, the Banu Abd al-Dar tied it for them; and when they gathered for a matter, whether to confirm or to annul it, they gathered only in Dar al-Nadwa. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}

Abu Bakr, in the Jahiliyya, had charge of the blood-money and the fines. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434}
