## Summary
Ibn Sina ends chapter 25 with the treatment of hard, raw, windy and ulcerous swellings and then internal ones, and warns against mistaking a swelling for a hernia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.

## Hard swellings
For hard swellings that have gone past the beginning, the rule is to soften them now with what heats little and cleanses, then press on with dissolving; then, if it is feared that what remains will harden as part dissolves, to return to softening, and to keep doing so until all of it is consumed in the periods of softening and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.

## Raw and windy swellings
Raw swellings are treated with what heats with fineness, and windy swellings with what heats with fineness of substance, to dissolve the wind and widen the pores, since the cause of windy swellings is thick wind in blocked pores {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.

## Ulcerous swellings
Some swellings tend to ulcerate, like the ant, and these are cooled as an inflammation is, but not moistened; rather they are dried, because here the symptom has overcome the cause. The symptom is the ulceration expected or present. The treatment of ulceration is drying, and moistening harms it most {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.

## Internal swellings
For internal swellings the matter is reduced by venesection and purging, and the patient avoids the bath, wine and excessive bodily and psychic movements such as anger. At the beginning what repels is used without heavy loading, especially in the stomach or liver, and when the time for dissolving comes the astringent, sweet-smelling drugs are not left out. The relaxants of the bowel that are used as ripeners suit swellings, such as nightshade berries and cassia fistula, nightshade berries having a special property for dissolving hot internal swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.

## Food and outcome
Patients are fed only light food and not at the time of the fit. A person afflicted by a swelling of the viscera together with collapse of strength is on the way to death, because strength revives only by food and food then harms most. If the swelling bursts, he should drink what washes it, such as honey water or sugar water, then take what ripens gently with some drying, and at the end only drying drugs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.

## A warning about hernia
Error is possible in internal swellings below the belly: they may not be swellings but hernias, and incising them is dangerous; or the swelling may be internal and in the bowel itself, not in the peritoneum, and incising it is dangerous {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.305}.
