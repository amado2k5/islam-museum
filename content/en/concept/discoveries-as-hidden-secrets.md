## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that what people call a discovery is the appearance of a secret that already existed, and that God allowed to appear. Faith says God allowed the secret to be born {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1100} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1101}.

## Two kinds of the unseen
What has means in God's cosmos is reached by inquiry and experiment. What has no means God bestows on some of His chosen, and nothing of it is learned by research {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1102}.

## Chance and inquiry
He says many discoveries came by chance during other research, and holds that this is an overflow of bounty rather than of effort {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1101}.
