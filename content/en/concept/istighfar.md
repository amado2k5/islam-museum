## Summary
On the believers' prayer in Al Imran 16, Sha'rawi holds that God legislated repentance and forgiveness because He knows that the self can betray its owner. Seeking forgiveness is a duty that must be carried out {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1330}.

## Fear and love
He says that once a servant knows God has guaranteed forgiveness, he approaches Him with love rather than fear, and the dread leaves him. The prayer "our Lord, we believe; forgive us" shows knowledge of the weight of belief {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1329} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1330}.

## Repentance as mercy
He holds that repentance is a mercy that opens the door for one who sinned and turned back. The sting of a sin when remembered is written as a good deed, and God's legislation meets the reality of human nature {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1330}.
