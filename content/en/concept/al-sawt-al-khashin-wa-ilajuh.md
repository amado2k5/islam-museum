## The harsh voice and its treatment
Those struck by it from the uvula's cutting should have a decoction of this kind cooked for them, to the measure that takes off its foam, then mixed with hot water, gargled with, and its owner given of it to drink; its old is more useful than its fresh, on the book's wording (the decoction's name and measure are excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.326}
