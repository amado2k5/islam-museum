## Summary
Al-Battani takes the Moon's motion in longitude from what remains in Ptolemy's book, adding the correction he made to the Sun's motion. His motion in the inequality he takes exactly as Ptolemy set it, with no addition or subtraction. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.82}.

## Correcting the latitude motion
Al-Battani found that Ptolemy's latitude motion exceeds his own by a few minutes. He spread these minutes over the time between Ptolemy and his own observations, subtracted them from the latitude motion, and used the remainder in the tables. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.82}.
