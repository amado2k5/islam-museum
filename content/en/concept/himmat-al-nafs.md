## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy discusses high aspiration (himma), holding that great souls wear out their bodies in pursuit of their aims and that the person with a goal does not settle for what is base. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:11:00}
He gives a saying of Ibn al-Jawzi on loftiness of aspiration, calls for high aspiration in asking and in giving, and stresses that a clear goal is needed. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:12:30}

## The speaker's view on high aspiration
The Sheikh says that great souls wear out their bodies in pursuit of their aims, and holds that the person with a goal does not settle for what is base (needs_source: garbled-passage). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:11:00}

## Ibn al-Jawzi's saying on aspiration
The Sheikh relays a saying of [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] to the effect that one whose aspiration reaches the sky is not content with the stars; he says he read it when he was young (needs_source: quote-attribution). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:11:30}

## Asking and giving
The Sheikh says that whoever asks God for Paradise should ask for its highest part (needs_source: hadith-unnamed). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:12:00}
He tells of asking a wealthy man to give a small sum to the needy, and by this calls for high aspiration in asking and in giving. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:12:00}

## Defining the goal
The Sheikh says that whoever wants success needs a clear goal, and that the Prophet made clear to us what he loved among matters. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep02 00:12:30}
