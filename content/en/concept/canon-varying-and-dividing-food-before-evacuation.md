## Summary
Ibn Sina advises whoever means to purge or vomit to divide the day's food into several meals and to vary both foods and drinks, so that the stomach comes to long to push out what is in it upward or downward {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.276}.

## The reason
Ibn Sina says whoever wishes to purge or vomit should split his food, taking the day's amount in several portions, and should make his foods and drinks different from one another. The reason he gives is that this makes the stomach long to push out its contents upward or downward, whereas food that is of one kind and is taken after other food is clutched tightly by the stomach, especially if it is small in quantity. Someone whose bowels are already loose should do none of this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.276}.
