## Nature and selection
Asarun is a herb brought from the land of China, with many seeds and large roots with crooked knots, resembling the til (sedge), sweet-smelling and stinging the tongue; it has a purple flower among the leaves near the roots, like the flower of henbane; its roots are the most useful part of it, and its strength is the strength of wajj, which is stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}. The best is the keen-smelling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}.

## Temperament and actions
It is hot and dry in the third degree, and it was said that its dryness is less than its heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}. It opens and soothes all inner pains, especially its infusion that the book mentions in the chapter on dropsy, and it refines, resolves, warms the cold organs and cleanses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}.

## Uses by organ
It helps sciatica and old pain of the hips, especially its infusion mentioned in the chapter on dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}. It helps thickening of the cornea in the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}. It helps obstruction of the liver greatly and its hardness, helps jaundice and dropsy, and its benefit for fever is greater, and it helps hardness of the spleen greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}. It strengthens the bladder and the kidney and purges, and it is like white hellebore in cleansing the abdomen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}. Quantities, the way the infusion is made and its length are not reproduced.

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
In the section on the excretory organs the book says that it "promotes them", in the dual, without the printed line naming what is promoted {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}; and that it increases the semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.350}; reports attributed to the book, without recommendation.
