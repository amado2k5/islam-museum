## Sagittarius (al-Rami)

The figure is named ar-Rami, the archer (jaculator), and its heading also gives al-Qaws, the bow, and al-Sahm, the arrow. The list names stars in the arrow's tip, the hand, the bow, the shoulder, the eye and the leg, and a double nebulous star in the eye. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.162}
