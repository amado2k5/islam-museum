## Summary
"Knowing the maqlub" is the twenty-second type in [[person/ibn-al-salah|Ibn al-Salah]], who defines it by example: a well-known hadith from Salim, say, is assigned instead to Nafi', so that it becomes rare and sought after. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 101}

## Al-Bukhari in Baghdad
Ibn al-Salah relates that when [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] came to [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]], a group of hadith scholars gathered before his session and took a hundred hadiths, inverting their texts and chains — giving this chain's text to another chain and this text's chain to another text — then came to his session and put them to him; when they had finished he turned to them and restored every text to its chain and every chain to its text, and they acknowledged his excellence. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 101} Inversion here was a test of a hadith scholar's memory, not a narration meant to transmit.

## An example that is also a hidden defect
Another example, which also illustrates [[concept/illa|a hidden defect]], is what Ishaq ibn Isa al-Tabba' related from Jarir ibn Hazim from Thabit from [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas]] from the Prophet: "when the prayer is called, do not stand until you see me". {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 102} Ishaq said: I went to [[person/hammad-ibn-zayd|Hammad ibn Zayd]] and asked him about it, and he said: Abu al-Nadr was mistaken; we were all in Thabit al-Bunani's session with Hajjaj ibn Abi Uthman, and Hajjaj al-Sawwaf told us this from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir from Abd Allah ibn Abi Qatada from his father from the Prophet, and Abu al-Nadr thought it was among what Thabit told us from Anas — Abu al-Nadr being Jarir ibn Hazim. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 102}

## Related notions
Inversion is a kind of error in chain or text, close to [[concept/mudtarib|the mudtarib]] and [[concept/mudraj|the mudraj]], and it borders on [[concept/wad-al-hadith|forgery]] when a narrator does it deliberately to produce something rare, as in the Salim–Nafi' example. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 101}
