## Summary
Al-Battani finds the ascendant by adding the part of the sphere that has turned since the star's rising to the rising times against the part that rises with the star in the clime; with the result he finds the ascendant and midheaven by the earlier procedure. Another way takes the returned arc and subtracts it from the rising times of the part culminating with the star in the right sphere when the star is on the east side, and adds it when on the west. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.55-56}.

## What the measurement is for
This completes the aim with which the chapter began, to derive the ascendant and midheaven from the measurement of a single star without a measurement of the Sun. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.56}.
