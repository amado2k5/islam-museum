## Nature and selection
Temperament: hot in the first degree and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}

## Actions
Actions: it has astringency and fineness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}

## Uses by organ
Head organs: it polishes the teeth, and grows hair when smeared with lily oil and when washed with it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Head organs: washed with it, it cleans the al-abriya (a term the book uses) and polishes the teeth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Eye organs: it clears the eye and removes its whiteness; the burnt form is stronger. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Excretory organs: the powdered and the burnt form are very helpful for bladder stone. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
