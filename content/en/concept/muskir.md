## Overview
Intoxicants, in the forty-sixth hadith of Ibn Rajab's additions, are a general category and not specific to wine: "Every intoxicant is forbidden." The Prophet was asked about *bit'* (honey wine) and *mizr* (barley beer), and he made the ruling general. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.881}

In Muslim's wording, "whatever intoxicates to the point of neglecting prayer is forbidden." Ibn Rajab holds that the hadith is the foundation for the prohibition of all intoxicants that cover the mind. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.881}

He notes that the prohibition came gradually: wine was first forbidden at the time of prayer, and then forbidden absolutely. The subject is part of the chapter on [[concept/halal-haram|the lawful and the unlawful]]. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.881}
