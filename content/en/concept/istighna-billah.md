## Summary
The Sheikh distinguishes between being self-sufficient in God and being free of a thing. The first, he says, is true self-sufficiency and true faith, while the second is asceticism regarding that thing. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:23:30}

## When people are self-sufficient in worldly things
The Sheikh says that when people are occupied with the world, with building, rank or social standing, the believer should be occupied with God, and when people are self-sufficient in the world, one should be self-sufficient in God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:23:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:24:00}

## The first sign: fearing God alone
The Sheikh says that the first sign of self-sufficiency in God is that a person fears none but God, and that fear of a creature shows that self-sufficiency in God has not been reached. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:24:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:26:00}

## The second sign: what is in God's hand
The Sheikh says that the second sign is depending on what is in God's hand rather than on what is in one's own hand. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:28:00}
