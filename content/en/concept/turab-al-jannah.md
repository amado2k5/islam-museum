## Overview
The Prophet described the soil and gravel of Paradise as fragrant musk, in one of the scenes of the honour of its people on the day they enter it — if its very soil is musk, then what of what lies above it {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In the Sunnah
In Sahih Muslim, Abu Sa'id al-Khudri said the Prophet said: «You will be led over the gravel of Paradise — al-Baqilli.» We said: Messenger of God, what is al-Baqilli? He said: «its soil is fragrant musk» {cite:sahih-muslim}.
