## Overview
The shahid in Sunni tradition is one killed defending religion, life or property; Bukhari reports: "whoever is killed defending his property is a martyr" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## Who counts as a martyr
Bukhari reports that the martyrs are five: the plague-stricken, the one who dies of abdominal disease, the drowned, the one killed under a collapsed building, and the martyr in God's path {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}; and that "the one who dies of abdominal disease and the stabbed are martyrs" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} — the jurists extended the rank to those who die by epidemic, drowning or falling buildings {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}.
