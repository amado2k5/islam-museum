## Summary
Ibn Sina names two vessels on the belly, one over the liver and the other over the spleen, then divides the times of venesection into two, a time of choice and a time of necessity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.298-299}.

## The two vessels of the belly
Among the vessels opened in the body are two on the belly, one set over the liver and the other over the spleen; the right is opened in dropsy and the left in ailments of the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.298}.

## The time of choice and the time of necessity
Know that venesection has two times, a time of choice and a time of necessity. The time chosen is mid-morning after digestion and evacuation are complete, while the time of compulsion is the time that makes it due, which cannot be put off and in which no heed is paid to a preventing cause {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.298-299}.
