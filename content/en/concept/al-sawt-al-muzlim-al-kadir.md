## The darkened, murky voice
Its cause is a very thick moisture, and what benefits in it is exercise, wrestling, holding the breath, dry rubbing with linen cloths, entering the bath, and using the lightening, cutting foods (the foods' names excluded per the rule; the rubbing and wrestling lines on the book's description) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.326}
