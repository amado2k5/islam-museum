## Nature
The book says this earth is very airy and resembles the earths already described, but is weaker than they are. It cleanses without a sting and weakens the senses. The editor's note identifies it as the earth of the island of Crete. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Eye
The book says it helps the ulcers of the eye and what the text calls its kumna, a condition the text names but does not explain. The kumna is not identified here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}
