## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that concealing what God sent down in clear signs for a small price is a loss, and that its penalty is of the same kind as the act. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.721} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.722}

## The small price
Sha'rawi says the price may be a bribe, or what the concealer takes from his followers so that God's rulings follow people's desires. A thing is priced only by one who knows its worth, and God's way can be priced only by God. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.722}

## Fire in the belly and God's speech
Sha'rawi reads the eating of fire in the belly as a penalty of the same kind as the act. He holds that God's not speaking to them denies the speech of intimacy and mercy, not the speech of punishment, and offers Moses at the staff as an example of the speech of intimacy. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.722} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.723} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.724}

## The three whom God will not address
Sha'rawi recounts the hadith of three whom God will not address on the Day of Judgement and explains each case; the footnote attributes it to Muslim. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.725}

## Concealment takes effort
Sha'rawi holds that concealing the truth takes effort, since speaking it does not. He adds that the concealer misleads others and bears the burden of those he misled. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.725} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.722}

## Limits of this batch
Other exegetes' readings of the kinds of concealment, the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.722}
