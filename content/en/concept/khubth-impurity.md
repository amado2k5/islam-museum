## Summary
Khabath (impurity) lexically is whatever is disliked for baseness, sensible or not; technically it is the very substance of impurity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7535} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7536} It is distinguished from rijs (filth) and dans (dirt). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7536} The jurists agree that removing impurity is commanded and differ on whether it is obligatory; the Hanafis, Shafiis and Hanbalis make its removal a condition of valid prayer except what is excused, while the Malikis have two views, sunna or obligation when one remembers and is able. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7536} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7537} Four substances are agreed to be impurity: carrion of a land animal with blood, pig flesh, flowing blood, and a man's urine and dung. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7537}
