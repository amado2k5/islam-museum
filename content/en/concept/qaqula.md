## Nature
There are large ones and small ones. The large are like a small nut, black, crumbling from a white grain that stings the tongue like cubeb and has fragrance; the small are like the clove in shape and also fragrant. The editor notes in a footnote that it is cardamom {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.642-643}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Actions and properties
With its heating it has astringency, especially the kind that has a calyx, and especially the calyx itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
With the water of mastic and of two pomegranates it benefits vomiting and nausea, and it strengthens the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}
