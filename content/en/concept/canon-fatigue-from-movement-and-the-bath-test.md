## Summary
Ibn Sina says that stopping movement at the first sign of fatigue prevents it, after which restorative exercise is used, and the person's state is known by bathing: if the bath brings on shivering or fever the matter has passed the limit, and he is not bathed but evacuated {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.

## The rule
Every fatigue whose cause is movement is prevented by leaving off movement at the first sign of it. Restorative exercise is then used, so that moderate movement drives the matters to the skin and rubbing, between the movements and in their pauses, dissolves them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.

## The bath as a test
His state is known by bathing. If the bath brings on shivering, the matter exceeds the limit, especially if it brings on fever; then he should not bathe but be evacuated and have his temperament set right. If the bath brings on none of this, he benefits from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.
