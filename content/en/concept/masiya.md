## Summary
Lexically disobedience is leaving obedience and defying a command; technically it is a forbidden act intended in itself. A slip (zalla) differs because it is not intended in itself. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14768}

## Classifications
Most scholars divide sins into minor and grave, a view a group of scholars rejected, holding that every sin is grave and only relatively smaller. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14768} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14769} Al-Halimi used three categories, minor, grave and gross. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14769} Al-Mawardi divided them by the soul's inclination, those it craves and those it shuns. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14770}

## Consequences
Sins carry worldly penalties (fixed hudud for adultery, theft and drinking, and discretionary ta'zir) and spiritual deprivations such as lost knowledge and provision, shortened life, and weakened will to repent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14770} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14773}

## Repentance, persistence and concealment
Repentance is obligatory at once. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14774} Persistence turns a minor sin into a grave one, and the Shafi'is recommend giving alms after each sin. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14775} Concealment is recommended where a hadd is involved; public confession is not sinful in the Hanafi view, while boasting of sin is forbidden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14775} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14776}

## Legal effects
Sin does not create a concession: an offender on a sinful journey does not shorten the prayer or break the fast, while sin that happens alongside a valid cause does not bar the concession. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14777} Zakat is not given to a traveller on a sinful journey, nor to a debtor in sin before repentance, according to the majority. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14777} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14778} Endowments, bequests, vows and hiring for sin are invalid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14778} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14779}
