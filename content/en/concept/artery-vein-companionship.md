## Summary
Ibn Sina shows that some arteries go along with veins; where the artery accompanies the vein on the spine it rides it, in the outer organs it dives beneath it, and there are two benefits in their going together {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.87}.

## The arrangement
Where the artery accompanies the muscles lying on the vein along the spine, the artery rides the vein so that the baser carries the nobler. In the outer organs the artery sinks beneath the vein to be better hidden and sheltered, with the vein as a shield for it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.87}.

## The two benefits
Arteries and veins were made companions for two reasons: one, that the veins be tied to the membranes covering the arteries and be watered from the organs between them; the other, that each draw from the other {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.87}.
