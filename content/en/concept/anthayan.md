## Definition
Anthayan are the two testicles, the term being used in this sense in the juristic books. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3458}

## Ruling
- The testicles belong to the private parts that must be covered. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3459}
- Castration of a man is forbidden, following the report of Ibn Mas'ud, who said that when they campaigned with the Prophet with nothing, they asked whether they might castrate themselves, and he forbade it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3459}
- Injury to them without intent carries a diya, and for one testicle half the diya; if the injury to one ends the offspring, no more than half the diya is due. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3459}
- For deliberate injury, retaliation is due according to the Shafi'is, Hanbalis and Malikis; the Hanafis deny it, since no exact measure of the injury can be set. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3459}
- Castrating an animal is allowed by some jurists and disliked by others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3460}
