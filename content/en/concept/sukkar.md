## Nature
Sugar cane is in the nature of sugar and more softening than it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Temperament
The coldest is the tabarzad, which is subtler; in sum it is hot at the end of the first degree and moist in it, the aged kind turning toward dryness in the first, and the older it grows the drier it becomes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Actions and properties
Softening, cleansing and washing; the Sulaymani is more softening, especially the fanidh, and the older the sugar the subtler it grows {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Organs of the eye
What is taken from the cane like a gum cleanses the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Organs of the chest
It softens the chest and removes its roughness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is good for the stomach except the one in which bile is generated, which it harms by turning to bile; it opens blockages and causes less thirst than honey; the aged kind makes turbid blood and clears phlegm from the stomach, and in sugar cane there is help toward vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Excretory organs
It loosens the bowels, especially the kind found on the cane like salt, and the Sulaymani; the red is more softening, and it may cause wind or may calm wind, and with almond oil it is useful for colic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.
