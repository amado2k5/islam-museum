## Its definition and treatment
It is a moisture that thickens and turns to stone inside the lid, whitish, resembling hail. Treatment: dissolving smears are used on it ⁦(the smear names and recipes are not carried; the chapter ends here and what follows goes to the next packet)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.196}
