## Overview
Al-Masudi mentions that the Prophet forbade going beyond Ma'add ibn Adnan in lineage, so people stopped there. He explains this by the distance of lineages and the multiplicity of opinions over a long period. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.565-566}

## The dispute over what lies above Ma'add
Al-Masudi says that the genealogists disagreed about what lies above Ma'add. He mentions different reports: the version of Ibn Hisham from Ibn Ishaq, another version, the report of Ibn al-A'rabi from Hisham al-Kalbi, and the book of Baruch ibn Naria. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.565-566}

What is not recorded here: the chain of the Prophet's prohibition and its grade. It needs a further source.
