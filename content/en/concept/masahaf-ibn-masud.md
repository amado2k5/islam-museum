## Summary
Ibn Mas'ud's codex is one of the private codices some Companions wrote for themselves before Uthman's unification; it belonged to [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abdallah ibn Mas'ud]], and after the unification was among those Uthman ordered burned. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 239}

## What is reported of his stand
Al-Sabt says it is reported of Ibn Mas'ud that he first objected to Uthman's codices and refused to burn his own, then returned to the community when their merits became clear to him. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 239} See [[event/ibn-masud-and-the-uthmani-codex|Ibn Mas'ud and the Uthmanic codex]].

## Readings attributed to him
Among al-Sabt's examples of the difference of ahruf is the reading of Ibn Mas'ud and Abu al-Darda' at [[passage/quran-92-3|al-Layl 92:3]], dropping the relative particle, which al-Bukhari reports. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 175} Ibn al-Jazari, relaying Makki, puts it in the class soundly transmitted by solitary reports yet against the script, so not to be read. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 24} Al-Sabt relays that Ibn Mas'ud disliked explanation being brought into recitation, and the reports of "keep the Qur'an bare" are ascribed to him: see [[concept/tajrid-al-mushaf|keeping the codex bare]]. {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt p. 333}

## What I did not read
From the sources I read I do not know the order or number of suras of this codex or the text of what it holds, though Ibn al-Nadim is relayed on the order of Ubayy's codex; see [[concept/masahif-al-sahaba|the Companions' codices]]; and see [[event/mushaf-ibn-masud-burned-398|the burning of a codex attributed to him in Baghdad in 398 AH]]. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 1465}
