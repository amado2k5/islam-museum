## Summary
Nawm in the lexical sense is calm and stillness; technically it is the relaxing of the brain's nerves through vapours that rise from the stomach. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16667} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16668}

## Sleep, drowsiness, stupor and fainting
Nu'as is the first stage of sleep, sina a drowsiness that does not remove reason, and fainting the loss of sensation and movement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16668}

## Its rulings
Sleep is permitted in itself; it may be obligatory when a duty depends on it, recommended when one dozes in prayer or recitation, forbidden when one sleeps through the prayer time, and disliked in some cases, such as sleeping after the afternoon prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16668} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16669}

## Recommended conduct before sleep and on waking
Before sleep one is recommended to put out the lamps, close the water-skin and the door, and lay the right hand under the cheek. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16670}
On waking one is recommended to recite the prescribed formulas and to wash the hands three times before putting them into a vessel. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16671}

## Sleep and ablution
Sleep breaks the ablution in general, according to the majority, relying on the saying 'the eyes are the binding of the buttocks'. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16672}

## The sleeper's acts
The sleeper's words in legal acts are void: his consecration, divorce and admission do not take effect, except for what needs no intention, such as standing at Arafat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16673} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16674}

## Injury and destruction of property
An injury by a sleeper is treated as an error: the diya falls on his kin and expiation on him; in destruction of property he is liable like one who is awake. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16675} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16676}

## Sleep in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episodes 1, 2, 9 and 10)
The Sheikh counts sleep among the blessings, says excess in permissible things harms a person, and warns against exaggeration in religion (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:41:00} He says that if a person dozes in prayer he should sleep until the drowsiness passes, since he may curse himself without noticing (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 01:09:00}
He says that sleep before midnight is more beneficial than sleep after it, attributing this to scholars without verification (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 01:10:00} He says sleep is linked to growth hormone, a claim that could not be checked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 01:10:30} He urges early sleep, holding that staying up late wastes health (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:48:30}
