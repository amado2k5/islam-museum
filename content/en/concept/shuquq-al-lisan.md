## Treating the fissures
In the treatment of the fissures in the tongue: a mucilage of this kind held in the mouth and swallowed, eating trotters and soft-cooked eggs; and among the tried is the fresh butter produced by rubbing pieces of this kind (a tried thing the book carries; the names left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.260}
