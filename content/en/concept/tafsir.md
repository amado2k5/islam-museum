## Summary
In Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's account, tafsir is a reading of Juz' 'Amma beginning with Surat An-Naba'. It rests on joining verses, understanding the sense of words, and gathering the scenes of Judgement from several surahs. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:37:00}

## The themes of the Qur'an
He lists the themes of the Qur'an: creed, then worship, then dealings, then stories. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:06:00}

## Surat An-Naba'
He says the lesson is on the first surah of Juz' 'Amma, Surat An-Naba'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:05:00}
He says the surah's subject is the Great News, about which the disbelievers ask after mockery, and that the Qur'an addresses the believer with affirmation and the disbeliever with clarification. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:08:30}

## Reading the scenes together
The Sheikh says the scenes of Judgement are not taken from one surah; they are laid out together and their order is then examined. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:37:00}
He reads Surat Taha, saying that God makes clear the Qur'an was not sent to make the Prophet miserable, and that the disjointed letters are not names (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:37:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:18:30}

## Words and their senses
He explains that the Arabic word for a set time means the end of a term, and distinguishes what was written on believers (fasting and fighting) from prayer, which is set at fixed times (his reading of the wording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:15:30}
He explains that a garden (jannah) is so named because it surrounds those within it, and that the jinn are named from concealment (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:12:30}

## The order of the surahs
He says the Meccan precedes the Madani, so Surat Maryam, which is Meccan, precedes Al 'Imran, which is Madani, and draws the consequence for the sense of night and day (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:11:00}
