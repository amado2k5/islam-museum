## The half-sixth correction
A half-sixth of the fall minutes is added to them and the result subtracted from the latitude motion at the time of opposition, giving the Moon's latitude at the absolute start of the eclipse. The same minutes with a half-sixth added are then placed on the latitude motion to give the latitude at the absolute end. The procedure adjusts the latitude across the two moments. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.152-153}

## A note on the wording
The phrase for the half-sixth recurs on page 153 with some disturbance, and the structure is reported as read from the context. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.153}
