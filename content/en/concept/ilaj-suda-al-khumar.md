## The principle
As for the hangover headache, the first thing needed is to cleanse the stomach, either by a mild or middling vomiting, and if vomiting is not apt, or its use is left, to loosen the bowels, and if some hot disease prevents the use of what is hot, to loosen by what is gentler {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.65} In this headache vapours pour up, so the sufferer is made to sleep until the drink (sharab) in his stomach is digested, which shows in the colouring and dyeing of the urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.65}

## The management after that
The sufferer is fed what cools, moistens and refines, he is given astringent fruit, and the drink is to be water and nothing else, unless the stomach is weak and its slackening is feared, when much cold water is forbidden {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.65-66} The root of its treatment is to make him sleep and calm him; if he does not calm, it is repeated; then, once the nausea, if there was any, is gone and the headache remains, the treatment is changed, for what helped at first to strengthen the head and stop vapour does harm after the vapour has gone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.66} As the hangover lightens little by little, a movement that does not tire him is begun, and then feeding on the lighter meats {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.66}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.65-66}
