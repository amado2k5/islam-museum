## Summary
From the difference between the pulse of the two sides of one person, Ibn Sina argues that expansion and contraction belong to the artery's own substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.177}.

## Two sides that differ
It may happen to one person that the temperament of his two sides differs, one side cold and the other hot, so that the pulses of the two sides differ as heat and cold require, the hot side having the pulse of a hot temperament and the cold side that of a cold one {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.177}.

## What it shows
From this it is known that the pulse in its expansion and contraction is not an ebb and flow from the heart but an expansion and contraction of the artery's own substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.177}.
