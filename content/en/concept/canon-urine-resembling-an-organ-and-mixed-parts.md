## Summary
Ibn Sina ends the third chapter by saying that urine resembling the colour of an organ, if lasting, shows an ailment of that organ, by the cloud or smoke in its lower part, by telling raw matter from pus by stench, and by the state of urine of mixed parts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.192}.

## Organ and cloud
Urine that resembles the colour of some organ, if it lasts, shows an ailment of that organ. Some have said that when something like a cloud or smoke lies in the lower part of the urine the illness will be long, and if it lasts through the whole illness it warns of death. Raw matter is told from pus by the stench {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.192}.

## Urine of mixed parts
The more large parts there are in urine of mixed parts, the more it shows that nature's work in it is more penetrating, nature more capable and the pores more open {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.192}.
