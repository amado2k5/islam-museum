## Summary
The rights of brothers are the duties a Muslim owes a fellow Muslim: "a Muslim is a Muslim's brother — he does not wrong him, forsake him or despise him" {cite:sahih-muslim 2564}. Among the named rights are visiting the sick, blessing the sneezer, answering invitations, releasing an oath and counselling the one who asks {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. Al-Bukhari's al-Adab al-Mufrad contains chapters on attending to needs, sharing and giving gifts between brothers {cite:al-adab-al-mufrad}.
