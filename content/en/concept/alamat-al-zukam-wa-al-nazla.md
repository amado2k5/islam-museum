## The sign of the hot one
The sign of the acute hot nazla, if nasal, is redness of the face and eyes, the burning and thinness of the flow and its heat to the touch; a fever may occur with it and brings no benefit. If it is of the throat, then the severity of what descends to the throat, the intensity of its burning and thinness, together with inflammation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.242}
## The sign of the cold one
The sign of the cold nazla is the coldness of the flow if it is in the nose, tickling in the nose with a stretching of the forehead, a strong occlusion and nasal voice, and the thickness of the matter may betray it; if it is toward the throat, then the coldness of what is hawked up, its whiteness, and the benefit from a fever if one occurs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.242}
