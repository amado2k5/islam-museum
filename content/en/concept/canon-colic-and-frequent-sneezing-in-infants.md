## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions a colic that makes the child twist and cry, treated by fomenting the belly, and repeated sneezing that may come from a swelling about the brain and is then cooled, or else powdered basil is blown into the nostrils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.

## Colic
Colic may befall them so that they twist and cry, and the belly should be fomented with hot water and plenty of hot oil with a little wax {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.

## Repeated sneezing
Repeated sneezing may befall them, and it may come from a swelling about the brain; if so, the swelling is treated by cooling, smearing and anointing with cooling juices and oils. If it is not from a swelling, powdered basil should be blown into their nostrils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.
