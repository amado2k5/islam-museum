## The hard breath
In the book the hard breath is wider than the narrow, for it may come with a wide passage when the power or the diaphragm is marred or the stomach blocked; among the causes named are the laxative drug whose effect is not completed, the sharp clyster falling short, and the venesection that does not reach the need in pleurisy (names and forms excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.314}
