## Nature and selection
It is the plant called kawjashm, meaning eye of the ox. Its flower is yellow, its leaf has a red middle, and it is fuller than chamomile leaf {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}. The editor's footnote says bahar is the eye of the ox and other plants {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the second degree and dry in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.

## Uses by organ
Head: smelling it helps thick wind in the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.
