## Definition
Diyafa is the Muslim custom of honouring the guest and sheltering the weak. The hadith collected by al-Nawawi says: «whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let him honour his neighbour; and whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let him honour his guest». Ibrahim set the example when he hastened a calf to his angelic guests, Lut when he hosted them, and the Prophet's household when they fed the needy and the guest. {cite:arbaun-nawawi} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## The manners of hospitality
Its manners among Muslims: preferring the guest over oneself, the first day's full reception, serving food quickly, good conversation, and keeping harm from the neighbour and the guest. Tribes and households kept the custom until hospitality became one of the noblest social customs by which Muslims are known everywhere. {cite:al-adab-al-mufrad} {cite:sahih-muslim}
