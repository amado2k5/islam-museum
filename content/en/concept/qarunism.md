## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that Qarunism is a doctrine of life: the rich man who hoards his wealth, whom his riches keep from the Giver, so that he forgets his Lord and does not see the right of the poor in his money. He holds that this case recurs through history. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:15:00}

## The trial of Qarun's wealth
The Sheikh speaks of the trial of wealth that afflicted Qarun, who attributed what he had to his own knowledge, while the Sheikh stresses that wealth belongs to God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:09:30}

## Defining Qarunism
The Sheikh says that Qarunism is a doctrine of life, the doctrine of the rich man whom wealth deceived until he forgot his Lord. He names it after Qarun wherever the description fits, even when the person is not Qarun himself. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:08:00}
He distinguishes it from Pharaonism, and describes its bearer as the hoarding miser who keeps his wealth and is preoccupied with the blessing rather than with giving. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:08:30}

## The grateful and the hoarder
The Sheikh compares the miser who keeps a million for himself with the grateful rich man who sees in his wealth the share of the poor. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:08:30}
He holds that the sincere believer sees what the poor are owed, while the one who only claims faith sees only what is taken from him; it is insight, not sight, that sees this. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:09:00}

## The case repeats
The Sheikh holds that people today look at the billionaires of the world and wish for the same, so the case of Qarun repeats, and human conduct has been one from Adam onward. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:15:00}
