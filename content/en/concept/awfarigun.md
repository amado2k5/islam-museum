## Nature and selection
The book explains its name as the Roman dadi {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. The editor's note gives the plant name al-dazi {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}.

## Actions
Excretory organs: the book says it moves urine and, moderately, the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Joints: the book says that drunk over forty consecutive days it cures sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Fevers: the book says its seed, drunk, removes quartan fever {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}.

The menses report is the book's listing, not a recommendation, and is logged for the founder's ruling.
