## Nature
A noble substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Temperament
Subtle and balanced {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Actions and properties
Its filings enter the drugs for melancholy, and the best and quickest-healing cautery is that made with a cautery iron of gold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Cosmetics
Holding it in the mouth removes foul breath, and its filings enter the drugs for da' al-tha'lab and da' al-hayya, as application and in their drinks {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Ocular organs
It strengthens the eye as kohl {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits pains of the heart, palpitation and hadith al-nafs greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}
