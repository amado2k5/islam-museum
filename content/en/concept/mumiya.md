## Nature and selection
In the force and nature of pitch and bitumen mixed, but extreme and wide in benefit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Actions and properties
Fine and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Swellings and pustules
It helps phlegmatic swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.

## Joints
Good for the pains of dislocation, fracture, a fall, a blow, palsy and facial palsy, drunk and as a liniment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It helps migraine, cold headache, epilepsy and vertigo as a snuff with marzanjush water; for aching ear, flow of pus from the ear, heaviness of the tongue, the headache called the egg and old headache the book gives several measures and vehicles, which are not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.

## Chest and breathing
It stops the spitting of blood from the lung, and was tried for quinsy, for sore throat, for cough and for palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
For weakness of the stomach, phlegmatic retching, a fall on the chest and stomach, the liver, hiccup and pain of the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.

## Excretory organs
Good for ulcers of the urethra and bladder, given with milk; a little mixed with flour and inserted helps the one who cannot hold back urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.

## Poisons and bites
Listed for poisons, and for scorpions with pure wine, and put on the sting with cow's butter; the measures named are not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.565}.
