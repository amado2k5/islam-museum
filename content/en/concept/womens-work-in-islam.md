## Overview
Al-Maraghi holds that woman is man's sister in origin and humanity: Islam granted her ownership, sale, purchase, gift and inheritance, and learning and work follow necessarily {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 23}. He points to the Mothers of the Believers and the women Companions in teaching and public service — 'A'isha as a teacher of Islam, Asma' serving the hijra, Umm Sulaym and 'A'isha carrying water to the wounded at Uhud {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 24} {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 25}. The book permits a woman's work so long as it is serious, modest, and harmful neither to morals nor society.
