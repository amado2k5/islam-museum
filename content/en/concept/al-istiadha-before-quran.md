## Summary
Sha'rawi comments on the command to seek refuge in God from the accursed Satan before reciting the Quran, in [[passage/quran-16-98|Q. 16:98]]. He holds that a believer who turns to God becomes stronger than his weakness, and that the refuge clears the reader's receiving faculty. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.27}

## The doors of Satan
Sha'rawi says Satan approaches a believer through the door where he sees weakness: a man strict in prayer is tempted through money, and one strict about money through women, wine or bad company. He also cites the saying that wealth is not diminished by charity. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.27} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.28}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's commentary and is attributed to him. The readings of the exegetes and jurists on the meaning of the refuge are not yet presented.
