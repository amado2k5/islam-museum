## The count of moments
If the Moon is not totally eclipsed, the eclipse has three moments: the beginning, the middle and the end. The same is true of a total eclipse with no stay. If there is a stay the eclipse has five moments: the three already named together with the beginning of totality and the end of totality. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.152}

## What the moments mean in general terms
The text says these moments are stated in general terms and are made exact afterwards by the calculation, because the latitude changes between them. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.152}
