## The canon of treatment
If it must be treated, let the aim be soothing the pain, cleansing the body and the head region of the turbid humour, and feeding good-chyme wheat foods with no heating in them; drinking milk benefits him. There must be used in it egg white with rosemary and a little saffron, and the white collyrium, and every collyrium made like starch, ceruse, gum and opium, and all those in which the softening and narcotic kinds occur ⁦(the named collyria and their recipes are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.182}
