## Temperament
There is heat in it, while its dryness and moisture are according to the amount of its salt and borax, more or less {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.713}

## Actions and properties
It has a cleansing power for salt, borax and wheat matters and a power cooling sourness, draws deep matters to the surface of the body and dissolves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.713}

## Joints
The pain that is in the sole of the foot is poulticed with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.713}
