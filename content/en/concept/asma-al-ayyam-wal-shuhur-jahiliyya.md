## Overview
In the Jahiliyya the Arabs called the days: Sunday *Awwal*, Monday *Ahwan*, Tuesday *Jubar*, Wednesday *Dubar*, Thursday *Mu'nis*, Friday *Arubba*, and Saturday *Shiyar*. Their poet said: "I hope to live, and that my day will be one of *Awwal*, *Ahwan*, *Jubar*, or *Ardi*, *Dubar*; and if I am spared, then *Mu'nis*, *Arubba*, or *Shiyar*." They called the months al-Muharram *Natiq*, Safar *Thaqil*, then *Talig* and *Najir*, *Asalakh*, *Amih*, *Ahlak*, *Kas'*, *Zahir*, *Barak*, *Harf*, and *Ta'as*, which is Dhu al-Hijja. Sunday was named because it was the first day of time that God created, and Saturday because creation ceased on it, and Adam was created at its end, named after a sandal (*Sabtiyya*) whose hair had been cut off. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.510-511}
