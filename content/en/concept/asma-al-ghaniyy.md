## Overview
al-Ghaniyy — The Self-Sufficient. It is name number 88 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-10-68|10:68]]، [[passage/quran-22-64|22:64]]، [[passage/quran-31-26|31:26]]، [[passage/quran-35-15|35:15]]، [[passage/quran-47-38|47:38]]، [[passage/quran-57-24|57:24]]، [[passage/quran-6-133|6:133]]، [[passage/quran-60-6|60:6]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/muslim-2965|2965]].
