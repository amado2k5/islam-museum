## The two eclipse tables
For an approximate reading from the tables the Moon's true latitude at the time of opposition is entered in the two lunar eclipse tables, one for the farthest distance and one for the nearest. If the latitude is found in the nearer table alone, the digits and the minutes of fall are taken from it. If it falls in both, the difference between the two rows is taken and the corrections from the third table are applied to the far row. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.162-163}

## The figures in this part
The figures of the two tables are not transcribed here, because the pages carry numerals disturbed in the OCR; only the step is described. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.162-163}
