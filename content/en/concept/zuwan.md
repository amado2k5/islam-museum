## Nature and selection
Nature: zuwan is a name people give to two things. One is a grain like wheat from which people make bread; the other is a poor intoxicating grain. The discussion here is of the first, not the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.473}
Selection: the best is light in leaf, not worm-eaten or crumbling, sticky when chewed to a reddish colour, with a slight astringency. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.473}

## Temperament and actions
Paulus said its strength is close to that of wheat in heat and cold, and that it dries. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.473}

## Close of the letter
This is the end of the discussion of the letter zay, which is twenty-seven drugs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.473}
