## Nature and selection
Nature: a kind of pine called the Indian pine, its stalks resemble those of the zarnabad; it has slight sharpness. Its milk is hot, pungent and thirst-causing. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Temperament: its dryness, in the third degree, is greater than its heat. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}

## Actions
Its milk has a burning pungency, and its emetic essence has astringency. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}

## Uses by organ
Joints: good for slackness of the nerve, paralysis and facial palsy; the book says nothing is better for these. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Head: good for cold diseases of the brain. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Digestive organs: its milk causes thirst. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Excretory organs: it breaks up stones in the kidney and bladder, binds the bowels, and removes slackness of the anus when one sits in its decoction. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
