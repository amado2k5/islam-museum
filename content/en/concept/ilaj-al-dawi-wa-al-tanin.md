## What to avoid, and strengthening the organ
All these must avoid the sun, the bath, violent movement, shouting and vomiting; the organ acting on it is examined — above all the stomach — and cleansed; brain and ear are strengthened, the brain by such as is known, the ear by such as is known; the first temperament is consulted in this, and aid sought on the two known sayings ⁦(oil names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.226}
## Each cause and its treatment
What is from repletion: body or head is cleansed by what is known and the regimen lightened. The crisis-kind must not be stirred — it passes with the passing of the fever. For what is from acuteness of sense some people order narcotics, the most right of what they order being the known; what is from pus is treated as the swelling and pus ⁦(narcotic and drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.226}
## The convalescents', the post-phrenitis and the viscid
In convalescents and those whose temperament has dried: if the cause is dryness, nourishment and moistening with temperate oils inclining to cold or hot as needed; if weakness, use of the named drops that rectify the occurring temperament. If the cause is a matter driven to it in phrenitis or a thick viscid humour, all the things said in the pain and hardness-of-hearing chapters; what is from a cold viscid humour has a tried tablet for it. Supper must be avoided in all of them. One of the earlier scholars said nothing benefits whistling like the futanj drug described for preserving ⁦(the saying is transmitted as attributed to the ancients; drug and tablet names and measures are not carried)⁩; and what was said of common treatments in the hardness-of-hearing and pain chapters — especially the cold kinds — is to be considered {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.227}
