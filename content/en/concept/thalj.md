## Actions and properties
It is bad for the elderly and for those in whom cold humours arise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}

## Organs of the head
Snow water calms hot toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}

## Joints
Snow harms the sinew by confining the hot vapours running in it and holding them back from dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It harms the stomach, especially one in which cold humours arise, and makes thirsty by gathering heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}
