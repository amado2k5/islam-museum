## Summary
Hamiyya lexically means prohibition, to keep the sick from what harms him, and it is a lawful form of treatment on the hadith that God sent a cure for every disease except old age. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7331} Ibn al-Qayyim takes as its root the Quranic permission to tayammum for the sick, who is kept from water; the narration of Umm al-Mundhir relates the Prophet's order to Ali to avoid food and to take barley and soup; and Umar kept a sick man from food until he sucked a date-stone. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7331} Diet must follow the principles of medicine and sound experience. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7332}
