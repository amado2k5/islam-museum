## The order of size
The text gives the order of the bodies of the world by size: the Sun first, then the fifteen stars named in the first magnitude, then Jupiter, then Saturn, then the other fixed stars of the second magnitude, then Mars, then the Earth, then the Moon, then Venus, and last Mercury, the smallest of the bodies of the world. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.189}

## The six magnitudes
The text divides the fixed stars in the figures into six magnitudes; each class below the fifteen stars decreases in size down to the sixth magnitude, where a star is sixteen times the Earth's size. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.189-190}
