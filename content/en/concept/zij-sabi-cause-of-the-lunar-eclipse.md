## Summary
A lunar eclipse happens because the Earth's conical shadow stretches through the air beyond the sphere of Mercury. When the Moon is at one of the nodes of its sphere at opposition, it enters that shadow and its light is dimmed. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.83}.

## The shadow and the gradual dimming
Al-Battani explains that the eclipse is complete as the Moon lies closer to the centre of the shadow; when it is on the middle line of the shadow the eclipse is at its fullest, and the whole light can fail. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.83}.
