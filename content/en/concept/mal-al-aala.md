## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an mentions 'the people of the higher assembly' in Sad [[passage/quran-38-69|38:69]] in their dialogue with Iblis, and in al-Sajdah says some of them listened to Iblis [[passage/quran-37-8|37:8]].

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir interprets 'the higher assembly' as the nearest angels, noting they were in council with God when Adam was created {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some held 'the higher assembly' are the angels of the highest heaven, others that they are those who listened to Iblis at the council of creation; the primary view takes them as God's nearest angels {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
