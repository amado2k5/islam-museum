## Emerging from under the rays
The first morning appearance of Saturn, Jupiter and Mars in the east is the first time they emerge from the sun's rays after the sun has passed them. For each planet the corrected anomaly is checked: at a stated value the planet has appeared in the morning, and at another value it sets in the evening, when the Sun overtakes it and hides it with its rays. Venus and Mercury are reckoned in the same way for their four cases. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.181}

## The anomaly values in the text
The anomaly values given at this point are garbled in the text, so they are not reproduced; they must be checked in the original. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.181}
