## Overview
Ghallab surveys early European writings on Islam he characterizes as prejudiced and ignorant: the *Song of Roland* claiming Muslims worship a trinity of Muhammad, Apollo and Tervagant; the poem of "Jerusalem" describing a golden statue of the Prophet on an elephant; a tale permitting a woman multiple husbands {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 37} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 38} {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 39}. He notes that later thinkers — Pascal, Montesquieu, Voltaire, Renan, Casanova, Dermenghem — also erred against Islam {cite:al-islam-kama-yarahu-al-urubiyyun-azhar p. 40}, seeing Voltaire's Prophet as a screen for attacking the founders of all religions.
