## Nature
It is said to be a rocky plant growing in a place of much shade; some said it is a kind of squill, and others said otherwise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Actions and properties
Fine and dissolving, without much heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It helps the spleen wonderfully when taken with an oxymel made of vinegar in which its leaf has been cooked, over a course of days, and it helps hiccup and jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Excretory organs
It breaks up stone in the kidney and bladder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It is said that, hung on the body, it prevents conception, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.
