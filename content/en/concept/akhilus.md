## Nature and selection
Also called sandristus. Galen is quoted as saying that it is more astringent than sandristus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. The editor's note identifies it as the milfoil plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}.

## Actions
Excretory organs: the book says it cuts the bursting of blood, intestinal ulcers, and the bleeding that befalls women {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. The women's bleeding is the book's listing and is logged for the founder's ruling.
