## Overview
'Arjun holds that rulers command the power of execution while scholars command the word — and the word in truth outlasts the sword {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 75}. He cites the obligation laid on scholars to expound God's religion and the prohibition of wrong as a pillar of divine victory {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 75}; 'Izz al-Din b. 'Abd al-Salam in the Crusader wars as the imam who stirred dignity in the umma {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 76}; and 'Umar b. 'Abd al-'Aziz's saying that rule is a responsibility shared between governor and governed {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 74}. He proposes that scholars draft a written reform plan to submit to those in charge.
