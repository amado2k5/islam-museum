## Overview
al-Wahhab — The Bestower. It is name number 16 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-3-8|3:8]]، [[passage/quran-38-35|38:35]]، [[passage/quran-38-9|38:9]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
