## Summary
The option of stipulation (khiyar al-shart) is a compound name for the right, set by stipulation, of one party to choose between confirming and annulling; Ibn Arafa defines it as a sale whose conclusion is held back for a confirmation expected. Other names: the stipulated option (distinguished from the legal option, such as defect), the option of deliberation, and the sale of option. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7923}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7924} Its lawfulness: most jurists hold it lawful and not contrary to the contract, on the report of Ibn Umar and of Habban ibn Munqidh ("when you sell, say: no deceit" and "you have the option three nights"); al-Nawawi and Ibn al-Humam report agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7924}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7925} Wording: no fixed words; any word that conveys the option (consent, will) is enough, and custom counts for the Malikis, as when people have long bargained with an option in a certain sale. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7925}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7926} Conditions: it must be stipulated in the contract or its session in the Shafii and Hanbali view; the Hanafis allow it to be added later as if at the contract; it must be limited by a known term, since an unlimited option causes gross ignorance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7926}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7927}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7927}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7928} Term: the least is not fixed and may be a moment; for the greatest, Ahmad, Muhammad al-Shaybani, Abu Yusuf and others allow any term by agreement, while Ibn Abi Layla, Ibn Shubruma and others too; Ahmad says he dislikes a long term, and the author of Ghayat al-Muntaha finds a term far beyond custom (such as a thousand years) invalid; Malik limits it by need and what is usual for the sale. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7928}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7929} Purpose: to let the party consider and test the thing; the term under the Malikis is measured by what the thing needs to be tested and consulted about. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7928}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7929}
