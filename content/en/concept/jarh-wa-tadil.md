## Overview
The Companions, Successors and those after them studied the transmitters' conditions to tell the truthful from the liar — knowing the best-memorized, the most accurate, and the longest-companioned of those above them; they resorted to history as lying grew common, and some set aside days for speaking of the men {cite:manhaj-tawthiq-al-sunna-azhar p. 43}{cite:manhaj-tawthiq-al-sunna-azhar p. 44}. The accepted narrator among them: trustworthy, accurate, comprehending, sane, adult, free of the lapses of honor and the sin — if one condition failed, his narration was rejected.

## In al-Dhahabi's Mizan al-I'tidal
In the introduction to [[work/mizan-al-itidal|Mizan al-I'tidal]], [[person/al-dhahabi|al-Dhahabi]] traces the science through a chain of imams: first, in his account, [[person/yahya-ibn-said-al-qattan|Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Qattan]]; then his students [[person/yahya-ibn-main|Ibn Ma'in]], [[person/ali-ibn-al-madini|Ibn al-Madini]], [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]], al-Fallas and Abu Khaythama; then their students Abu Zur'a, Abu Hatim, [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]], Muslim and al-Jawzajani; then al-Nasa'i, Ibn Khuzayma, al-Tirmidhi, al-Dulabi, al-Uqayli, Ibn Hibban, Ibn Adi and others. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 5} {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 6}

He ranks the terms of vindication from the highest: "firm, a proof", "firm, a hafiz", "trustworthy, exact", "trustworthy, trustworthy", then "trustworthy", then "truthful", "no harm in him" and "there is no harm in him", then "his place is truthfulness", "good in hadith", "sound in hadith", "a middling shaykh", "a shaykh, fair in hadith", "truthful, God willing" and "fairly sound". {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 7} The lowest terms of criticism for him are "imposter" and "liar", then "not strong", "not a proof", "there is talk about him", "soft", "of poor memory", "disputed" and the like, which show that a transmitter is discarded, weak, or held in suspense. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 7} He makes the turn of the year 300 the dividing line between early and late transmitters. {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 7} He reports al-Bukhari saying that it is not permitted to narrate from anyone of whom he said "munkar al-hadith". {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1 PDF p. 8} On how innovation bears on accepting a report, see [[question/riwayat-ahl-al-bida|narration from people of innovation]].
