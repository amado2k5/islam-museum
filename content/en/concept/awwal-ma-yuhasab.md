## Overview
The servant's first reckoning on the Day is over his prayer; if it is sound the rest of his work is sound, and if it is spoiled the rest is spoiled {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.

## In the hadith
Al-Tirmidhi records that the first thing the servant is reckoned for on the Day is his prayer {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}. In Ibn Mas'ud's report at al-Tirmidhi, the feet of the son of Adam do not move on the Day from before his Lord until he is asked about five: his life and how he spent it, his youth and how he wore it, his wealth — where he earned it and where he spent it — and what he did with what he knew {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}. Ibn Majah carries the reckoning of prayer among what the servants are asked {cite:sunan-ibn-majah}.
