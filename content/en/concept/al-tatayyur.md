## Summary
Ill-omen belief is minor shirk the Prophet abolished: the omen is nothing, and its best is the good word; whoever lets an omen turn him back has associated partners — the line between God's glad tiding and Satan's seed of weakness {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
