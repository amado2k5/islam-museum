## Overview
'Arjun makes this one of the clearest social laws: God does not change a people's ease and blessing until they change the obedience and goodness in themselves — a law covering good and ill alike {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 52}. False wishes and crying unaccompanied by earnest work carry no weight in these laws {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 52}. He ties the law to the fitra on which God created mankind: distort it by misconduct and punishment descends to awaken them; return to good and He lifts it.
