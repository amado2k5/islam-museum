## Nature and selection
A seed from India or Sind, in two kinds, small ones without ridges and large ones with ridges. The small is better {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: it draws phlegm out of the joints, and is excellent in this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}. Excretory organs: it purges phlegm from the intestines, worms, and habb al-qar (the book's term), and is very strong in this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.
