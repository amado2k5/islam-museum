## Summary
The sixth and last chapter of the nerves group says the first sacral pair mingles with the lumbar nerves, and that the remaining pairs and the single nerve growing from the tip of the coccyx spread in the muscles of the anus, penis, bladder and uterus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.83}.

## Their course
He says the first sacral pair mingles with the lumbar nerves, as has been said. The remaining pairs, and the single nerve growing from the tip of the coccyx, spread in the muscles of the anus and the penis itself, in the muscle of the bladder and the uterus, in the abdominal membrane, in the inner parts entering from the pubic bone, and in the muscles arising from the sacrum {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.83}.
