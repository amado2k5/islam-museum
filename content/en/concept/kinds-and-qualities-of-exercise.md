## Summary
Ibn Sina opens chapter 2, on the kinds of exercise, by dividing exercise into that which some human occupation calls for and pure exercise sought for its own sake, then names its qualities: little and much, strong and weak, quick and slow, brisk and lax, with a moderate between each pair of extremes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.222}.

## Occupational and pure exercise
Some exercise is called for by taking up one of the works of human life, and some is pure exercise, sought only because it is exercise and aimed at the benefits of exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.222}.

## The qualities
Some exercise is little and some much, some strong and hard and some weak, some quick and some slow, some brisk, meaning a mixture of strength and speed, and some lax; between each two extremes a moderate kind exists {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.222}.
