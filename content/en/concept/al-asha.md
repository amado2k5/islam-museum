## Its definition, cause and those it befalls

Night blindness is the disabling of sight at night while it sees by day and weakens toward the end of day. Its cause is an abundance of moistures. It befalls the kohl-eyed rather than the blue-eyed, the small-pupilled, and those whose eye holds many colours and streaks, for these point to a scant visual spirit in their constitution; and this complaint may come from a disease in the eye itself or by participation of the stomach and brain, known by their signs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.206}

## The treatment

If there is abundance, let him be bled and use the known evacuants repeatedly; and among its tried medicines are the persevered compounds described for it ⁦(the names, recipes, and the drawing of what drips from the pierced goat liver and its compounds are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.207}
