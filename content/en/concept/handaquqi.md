## Nature and temperament
Nature: there is a wild kind, a cultivated kind and an Egyptian kind; bread is made from its seed and eaten. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}
Temperament: hot and dry at the end of the second degree according to Ibn Jurayj; hot in the middle of the second according to Ibn Masawayh; the cultivated kind's heat seems to be at the end of the first. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}

## Actions
The cultivated kind cleanses and dries moderately; the wild has binding with warmth, and its oil helps thick wind. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: the wild kind helps kalaf, and so does the cultivated. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}
Wounds: juice of the cultivated kind with honey cleans ulcers. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}
Joints: its oil is good for joint pains from wind, and against the fear of paralysis; some were cured with it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}
Organs of the head: causes a headache when its juice is snuffed, and helps those who have frequent falling sickness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}
Organs of the eye: juice of the cultivated kind helps whiteness and cloud of the eye, especially with honey. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.485}
Organs of the chest: helps pain of the ribs from phlegm, especially the wild kind; it can cause throat pain and quinsy, and its harm is corrected with coriander, lettuce and endive. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Organs of digestion: helps the pain of the cold windy stomach; its oil is a remedy for dropsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Excretory organs: brings urine and the menses. The wild kind with wine and molokhia seed is good for bladder pain; its oil helps testicle pain and womb pain. The wild kind helps cholera and firms the belly, and both it and its seed excite sexual power. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
Poisons: when its water is sprinkled on the sting of the scorpion, the pain eases at once; its seed is stronger than the plant in treating scorpion stings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.486}
