## Its two causes
The lip's tremor mostly comes by sharing with the mouth of the stomach — especially when nausea is there and a motion towards casting something out, above all in the acute diseases and the times of crises; and it may be by the nerve reaching it from the brain and the spinal cord {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.284}
