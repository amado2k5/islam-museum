## Nature and selection
It is buds, husks and calyces resembling mace, though less red and tending to yellow, fragrant, with a little astringency, close to nardin in power; it is also called naghbusht {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Actions and properties
Fine and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Good for the cold stomach and liver, so that it helps as sunbul helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Substitutes
The book gives it a substitute made of ginger, pistachio and sunbul in proportions by weight; the proportions are not carried over here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.
