## Wakefulness and sleeplessness
Wakefulness is a state of the animal when its animal spirit stands ready toward the instruments of sensation and movement and uses them; sleeplessness is excess in wakefulness and a departure from the natural order {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94}

## Causes
Its temperamental cause is heat and dryness, because of the fieriness of the spirit, which moves continually outward; heat is the stronger and earlier cause; sleeplessness may also come from the nitrous quality (buraqiyya) of the moisture lodged in the brain, or from pain, or from distressing thought {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94} Some sleeplessness is from light and the lighting of the place, when it befalls one disposed to sleeplessness; some from bad digestion and much fullness; some from what inflates and disturbs the humours and the dreams and frightens in sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94} Some occurs in fevers, as dry stinging vapours rise to the brain; the pain that befalls the old from sleeplessness is from the nitrous quality and saltiness of their humours and the dryness of the substance of their brain; some is from a melancholic swelling or a cancer in the region of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94} It is said that whoever is gripped by severe sleeplessness and then falls into a cough dies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94}
