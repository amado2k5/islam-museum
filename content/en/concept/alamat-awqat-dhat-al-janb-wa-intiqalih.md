## The signs of pleurisy's times, kinds and transition
Every pleurisy whose pain is stilled neither by expectoration, venesection, diarrhoea nor otherwise is to be expected to suppurate or to kill before that, by the rest of the indications (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.347}
