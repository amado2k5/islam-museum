## Overview
The mu'min in Sunni usage is one who believes what the Prophet brought and acknowledges all he said — 'we name the people of our qibla Muslims and believers' {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §73}.

## In the sources
Ibn Taymiyya's Kitab al-Iman is devoted to the reality of iman and the theologians' dispute over it — some confining it to assent, others including deeds {cite:kitab-iman-ibn-taymiyya}; the creed affirms grave sinners among the believers do not remain forever in the Fire {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §89}.
