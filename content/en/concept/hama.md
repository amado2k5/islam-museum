## Summary
The hama is one of the beliefs of the Jahiliyya that The Sealed Nectar lists together with [[concept/tiyara|omen-taking (tiyara)]] and contagion. They believed that a slain man would not be at rest until his blood was avenged, and that his soul would become a hama, an owl, which flies over the wilderness crying "Sada, sada" or "Give me to drink, give me to drink". Once he was avenged, it would settle and rest {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 2/851-857}.

## Not covered here
The hadith "There is no contagion, no omen-taking, and no hama" and its commentaries will be presented under their own entries.

## For young visitors
They thought the soul of the slain became a bird that demands revenge. Islam abolished this belief.
