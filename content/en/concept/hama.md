## Overview
Hama: their belief that a slain man's spirit leaves his head as a bird crying "Give me drink" until his vengeance is taken; Islam abolished it along with the tiyara.

## The story
Among the beliefs of the Jahiliyya was that the murdered man, if his vengeance was not taken, a hama issued from his grave — a bird like an owl — crying "Give me drink, give me drink", meaning "give me my killer's blood to drink" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Islam abolished it wholesale, with the rest of the superstitions of bird-omens and contagion {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
They thought a murdered man's spirit became a bird seeking revenge; Islam annulled this belief.
