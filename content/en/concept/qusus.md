## Nature
Its kinds are three, black, white and red, all acrid and astringent; one kind yields something called ladhan, and qusus in origin is ladhan or something else, for the two are close in state {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Temperament
Its nature leans to heat, and some of its kinds may be cold, but ladhan itself is hot at the end of the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Actions and properties
It is harmful to the nerve and astringent, especially in its leaf, and its flower binds the bowel; ladhan is heating, opens the mouths of the vessels and softens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Cosmetics
Its tear kills lice and shaves the hair; ladhan mixed with wine and smeared on the marks of ulcers improves them, and mixed with wine, myrrh and myrtle oil it prevents the falling of hair, but it does not reach the point of benefiting alopecia, because its dissolving is little {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its decoction in wine benefits many ulcers; as a poultice it stops the creeping of a malignant ulcer, and a cerate is made from it for burns of fire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Joints
Harmful to the nerve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Organs of the head
Its juice used as a snuff with iris oil, honey and natron dissolves chronic headaches; the juice of the heads of the black kind, warmed in a pomegranate rind and dropped into the ear on the side opposite the aching tooth, benefits; its water as a snuff is good for cleansing the head, heals the chronic flow from the nose and dries its sores {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
The spleen, poulticed with the fresh plant in vinegar, benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Excretory organs
Its white flower in wine benefits dysentery, and its flower binds the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that, as a poultice of the fresh plant and its heads, it brings on the menses; that fumigation with it after purity prevents conception; that its rod, carried from the head end, brings on the menses and expels the foetus; and that ladhan is used as a fumigation so that the afterbirth falls {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Poisons and bites
Its roots, given with vinegar and wine, benefit the bite of the tarantula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}
