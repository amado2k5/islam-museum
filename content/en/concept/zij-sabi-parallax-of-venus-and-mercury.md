## Summary
Al-Battani says the parallax is perceptible also in Venus and Mercury, but it is greater in Mercury because it is nearer the Moon, especially when Mercury is at its nearest point, where its parallax equals the Moon's parallax at the Moon's farthest point. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.116}.

## Compared with the Moon
He compares the parallax of Venus and Mercury with the Moon's, noting that Venus's is smaller and Mercury's larger, because nearness to the Earth fixes the amount of the parallax. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.116}.
