## Nature
A light stone with the smell of the desert (qafr) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Joints
It benefits gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Organs of the head
When the epileptic is fumigated with it, it benefits him {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It benefits suffocation of the womb; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Poisons
Its smoke drives away vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}
