## Headache from obstruction
As for headache from obstruction, its treatment is by ripening in the ways the reader knows, then evacuation, then dissolving, then ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.62} If the temperament in the head is sharp and the obstruction thick, treatment is hard, and opening is to be used; then if a headache stirs or the head is harmed by the hot treatment, it is made good by coolers that bring relaxing and have no astringency, and when it quiets the treatment is resumed, and this is done again and again until the obstruction is opened {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.62}

## Winds and vapours
The one from thick winds is treated first by avoiding everything that vaporises and puffs, hot or cold; if its beginning is from the stomach, the evacuations mentioned are used in treating it, and after the treatment the head is strengthened by what has warming and astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.62-63} As for the one from vapours, if their generation is in the head itself and the sick person does not find in the stomach flatulence and rumbling, and it does not increase and lessen with fullness and emptiness and with vaporising foods and foods of little vapour, its treatment is the well-known dispersing foods and strengthening the head, and that suffices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.63} If it is from the stomach, the treatment is according to what strengthens the stomach, and the head is strengthened after the treatment; and if you feel that the vaporous matter has an excess of heat you strictly avoid the dissolvers that heat much, and begin first with drawing the matter away and cleansing, and then use what is moderate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.63}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.62-63}
