## The signs
As for that from blood, the first of its signs is that its common symptoms generally come with laughter; drops of nosebleed come upon him, his breath grows large, his eye waters and gathers rheum, and the wakefulness that overtakes him is not excessive {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.79} The roughness of the tongue tends toward a redness leaning to black and then blackens, the tongue is heavy and he may be lazy about speech from the heaviness of the tongue, his fancies are red, the vessels of his face are red, his eye is full, and he sits down and gets up again and again without need {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.79}
