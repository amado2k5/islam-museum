## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions swellings of the gum at teething and swellings with spasm at the tendons of the jaws in infants, and their treatment by gentle pressure with the finger, anointing with the oils named, a honey rinse and a head douche {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.211}.

## What occurs
Among the particular diseases of children are swellings in the gum at teething, and swellings at tendons in the region of the two jawbones, with spasm in them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.211}.

## Treatment
When this happens the finger should press on them gently and they should be anointed with the oils named in the chapter on the coming of teeth. Some claimed that a rinse of honey beaten with chamomile oil may be used, or honey with the gum of the terebinth, and that a douche made with water in which chamomile and dill were boiled may be used on the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.211}.
