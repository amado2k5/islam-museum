## The annual return and the leap year
The year of the epoch in the era of Dhu al-Qarnayn and the wanted year are taken, and the smaller is subtracted from the larger to leave the whole years elapsed. These years are multiplied by eighty-six degrees and thirty-six minutes, which is the excess of the solar year over whole days; the full revolutions are discarded and the rest is divided by the figure the text gives for one hour, garbled in the scan, into equal hours that are added to the calendar hours of the original. If the sum passes twenty-four hours, twenty-four are removed and one day added to the days elapsed in the month. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.196-197}

## The leap year and the month of Shubat
If the year is a leap year and the month of Shubat has passed, one day is subtracted from the days elapsed in the month, and the result is the calendar day. The Sun's mean is then computed on the figure given, and if its true place equals the first place that is the moment of transfer. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.196-197}
