## Summary
Sha'rawi presents the challenge in [[passage/quran-2-23|Al-Baqara 23]], "bring a surah like it", and holds that God graded it from the whole Quran, to ten surahs, to one surah. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.192} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.197}

## A like, not the same text
Sha'rawi holds that God asked for a like and not the Quran's own text, allowed the challenged to call witnesses other than God, and that "if you are truthful" means that a spoken statement must match the fact it describes. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.198} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.199}

## The verdict of inability
Sha'rawi reads "if you do not, and you never will" in [[passage/quran-2-24|Al-Baqara 24]] as a judgement of inability, and holds that the doubt they raise is contrived. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.200} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.201}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's reading and is attributed to him. The scan gives no views of scholars of inimitability or Quranic sciences on this aspect, and none has been checked.
