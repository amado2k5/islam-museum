## Summary
The epicycle's centre sits on the far point twice in a lunar month, once at mean conjunction and once at mean opposition. At those moments the single inequality does not combine with the second one. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.77}.

## Daily motion
On the epicycle the Moon moves about thirteen degrees and eleven minutes a day, and the centre itself shifts slightly along the zodiac. This reckoning drops the single inequality at the two mean syzygies. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.77}.
