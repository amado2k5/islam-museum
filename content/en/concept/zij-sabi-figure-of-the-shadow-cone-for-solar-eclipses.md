## Summary
Al-Battani draws a figure of several circles whose centres lie on one straight line: the Earth's circle, the Moon's, the Sun's and the shadow's. Through the tangent lines and rays he reaches the ratios of the diameters and of the shadow. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.88}.

## The rays
Rays are drawn from the two ends of the Sun's diameter; they touch the Earth's circle and meet on the far side, forming the cone of shadow. Al-Battani uses these lines to show the shadow's radius at a given limit. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.89}.
