## Summary
Latm, in lexicon, is striking the cheek with the open palm, and a single blow is a latmah; the jurists keep the same sense. Safʿ is a blow on the nape with the palm, and wakz is a push or a blow with the fist closed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13615} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13616}.

## Striking the cheeks at a calamity
The jurists agree that striking the cheeks, scratching them, tearing the collar and similar acts at a calamity are forbidden, on the hadith in the Sahih: he is not of us who strikes the cheeks, tears the collar or calls with the calls of ignorance {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13615}.

## Retaliation for a slap
Most jurists hold that there is no retaliation for a slap on the cheek if it causes no wound or loss of use, because exact equality is impossible, and only discretionary punishment is due {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13616}. Ibn al-Qayyim held that retaliation for a slap is due, citing the verses on like for like in punishment, and said Ahmad stated it and that those who disagreed departed from his school's text {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13616} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13617}.
