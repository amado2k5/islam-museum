## Overview
The author equates the economic functions of the state with what is called state intervention in the modern era, thereby distinguishing it from the night-watchman state that only handles security, defense, and the judiciary {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 89}.

## A Limit on Planning
The author states that planning in the Islamic state is less extensive than socialist planning, because total central control disables freedoms and replaces the market mechanism with the planning apparatus {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 91} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 92}.
