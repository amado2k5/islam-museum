## Nature
A known bird {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.714}

## Organs of the head
Dioscorides says, as the book relates, that two small stones are found in the inside of its young, one of a single colour and the other of many colours, and that when they are taken under conditions of time, wrapped in a piece of skin and tied on the upper arm or neck of one whose mind is confused or who has epilepsy, he benefits, and he says that he tried it; an account attributed to Dioscorides which the book does not endorse here, its conditions left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.714}

## Ocular organs
Eating the khattaf sharpens sight, and it may be dried and cleansed, the amount stated for the draught being left out, especially the burnt remains; the book mentions a kohl of the young with honey; it is said that its brain with honey benefits the beginning of cataract, and likewise the brain of the bat; a report attributed to the book, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.714}

## Respiratory organs
Its ash benefits quinsy, and likewise when it is salted and dried and some of it drunk with water it benefits cough and swelling of the uvula and tonsils; the amount is left out; a report attributed to the book, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.714}
