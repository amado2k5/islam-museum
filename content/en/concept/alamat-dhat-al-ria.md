## The signs of peripneumonia
And Hippocrates mentioned that if abscesses occur in them near the two breasts and what adjoins them, and fistulas open, they are delivered — the reason of that is known; and likewise if abscesses occur in the leg it is a praised sign; and if it passes, in the rare case, into pleurisy the breath diminishes and a pricking occurs (a saying attributed to Hippocrates) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.351}
