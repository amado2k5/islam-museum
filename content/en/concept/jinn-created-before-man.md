## In the Qur'an
[[passage/quran-15-27|al-Hijr 27]] says the jinn were created earlier, before the man of the preceding verse [[passage/quran-15-26|al-Hijr 26]], from the fire of the scorching wind. [[passage/quran-55-15|al-Rahman 15]] says the jinn were created from a flame of fire, after mentioning man created from clay [[passage/quran-55-14|al-Rahman 14]]. [[passage/quran-18-50|al-Kahf 50]] describes Iblis as one of the jinn, and [[passage/quran-7-12|al-A'raf 12]] reports his plea that he was of fire and Adam of clay. The Qur'anic text thus affirms that the jinn were created before man and from fire; in these verses it does not say they lived on the earth before Adam.

## In the hadith
Muslim relates from Aisha that the Prophet said the angels were created from light, the jinn from a flame of fire, and Adam from what has been described to you. I read the hadith in a scanned edition as no. 3113; the number commonly given is 2996, in Muhammad Fu'ad Abd al-Baqi's numbering, but I could not check it in that edition because sunnah.com and dorar.net could not be opened. {cite:sahih-muslim-harvest-20260227-2223 no. 3113} Ibn Kathir cites the hadith in his commentary on the verse of al-Kahf to show that Iblis was made of fire and the angels of light. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir on 18:50}

## In the reports of Companions and Successors
Ibn Kathir, on [[passage/quran-2-30|al-Baqara 30]], has the jinn on earth before Adam, two thousand years earlier in Mujahid's report from Abd Allah ibn Amr, and God sending a force of angels who pushed them to the islands of the seas; he also has from al-Hasan al-Basri that the jinn were on earth corrupting and shedding blood. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 pp. 70-71} Al-Tabari has from Ibn Abbas that the jinn of the Qur'an were created from a flame of fire and that the first to dwell on earth were the jinn. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF p. 459} From al-Hasan al-Basri, by a chain Ibn Kathir calls sound to him, Iblis was not an angel for the blink of an eye and is the origin of the jinn as Adam is the origin of humans. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 p. 77} Ibn Kathir also relays from Shahr ibn Hawshab that Iblis was one of the jinn whom the angels drove out, and one of them took him captive to heaven, and from Sa'd ibn Mas'ud that the angels fought the jinn and Iblis, then young, was captured. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 p. 77}

:::perspectives
- **Sunni view: the jinn were an earlier creation and lived on earth.** From al-Dahhak on Ibn Abbas and Mujahid on Abd Allah ibn Amr; al-Tabari held it possible but did not adopt it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 473-475}
- **Another Sunni view: Iblis is the origin of the jinn and was not an angel.** Al-Hasan al-Basri. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 p. 77}
- **Transmitted Sunni view: Iblis belonged to a tribe of angels called al-jinn or al-hinn.** From Ibn Abbas by chains in al-Tabari; the spelling and the sense are disputed, set out in [[concept/al-hinn|Al-Hinn]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 459, 462}
:::

## What the sources do not say
Neither the verses nor the sound hadith I read fix the interval between the creation of the jinn and of Adam, or say that the jinn lived on earth before him; that rests on Companion and Successor reports, weighed in [[concept/earth-before-adam|who held the earth before Adam]]. I do not repeat here what is in [[concept/jinn|The jinn]] and [[concept/jinn-anwa|Kinds of jinn]]. See also [[question/who-lived-on-earth-before-adam|the related question]].
