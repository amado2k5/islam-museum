## Nature
It is al-khilaf, and the book defers the discussion to the chapter of the letter kha. With this the discussion of the letter sad ends, the book counting eleven drugs in all that it named under it. The editor refers in a footnote to al-Mu'tamad fi al-adwiya al-mufrada {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}
