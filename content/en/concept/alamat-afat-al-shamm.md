## The signs of occlusion, weakness and putrefaction
If a man comes not to perceive odours and the flow of wastes there runs as usual, there is no occlusion in the sieve; if the breath's passage through the nose is stopped with a nasal twang in open speech, there is a deep-seated occlusion; if the flow runs as usual and there is no occlusion, it is a weakness and lessening of the smell. But if he finds a smell of putrefaction and inhales foulness, the cause is a humour turned putrid in one of these places, discerned by what you have learnt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.235} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.236}
## Unfamiliar odours in the acute diseases
If in the acute diseases he smells odours unfamiliar and unaccustomed, not from any odorous thing at hand — while he perceives a smell like fish, wet clay, clarified butter or the like — and there are bad signs, then death is to be feared ⁦(the wording as printed)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.236}
