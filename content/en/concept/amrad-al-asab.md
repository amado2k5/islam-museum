## The second fann
The second fann, on diseases of the nerves, comprises one maqala; as for the nerve itself, its origin, distribution, shape, nature and dissection are already known {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.135}

## The classes of disease that befall it
As for its diseases, know that the three classes of disease may befall it — the temperamental, the organic, and the dissolution of continuity — and the lesion appears in its natural, sensory and motor functions {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.135}
