## Nature and selection
Dioscorides said: it is a plant used as fuel for fire, tending to roughness, with one stem and round leaves; at the bases of the leaves is a shield-like fruit of two layers that grows broad; it grows in mountain places and rugged spots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.574}.

## Cosmetic uses
Pounded, mixed with honey and smeared on freckles and vitiligo, it cleanses them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.574}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its decoction, drunk, calms hiccup when there is no fever, and so, the book says, do holding it in the hand or looking at it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.574}.

## Poisons and bites
It is thought that pounded into food and eaten it helps the bite of a dog; it is said that hung in a house it keeps the health of the people and livestock within, and hung on the necks of livestock it wards off ills and afflictions. A phrase before this is unclear in the copy and is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.574}.
