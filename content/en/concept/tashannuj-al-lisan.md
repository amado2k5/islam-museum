## Its causes
The tongue's convulsion may come from a viscid moisture stretching its muscle sideways, from a constricting melancholy, or in the acute diseases when they bring convulsion to the tongue's muscle by drying and deforming; the convulsion may also show harm in speech {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.257}
## Its treatment
The tongue's convulsion is treated in the Canon not far from the treatment of convulsion; specifically, among it: compresses at the neck's root of this kind, singly and compounded, gargling with their oils and holding a mouthful lukewarm for a while, and lozenge-forms made of hot oils, dissolving sweets and seeds of this kind; in fevers the oils used are tepid and the named places wetted with tepid water and moist juices (the names are left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.257}
