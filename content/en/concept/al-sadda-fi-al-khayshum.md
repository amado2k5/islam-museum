## Its definition and sign
The occlusion in the nasal fossa is the thing held inside it until it bars what passes from the throat to the nose or from the nose to the throat; it may be a viscid sticky humour, protruding flesh, or a scabby crust. This occlusion produces the nasal twang, in that it keeps the excess of the breath from seeping through the fossa {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.248}
## Its treatment
There is taken of the known kind, made into a pill, and snuffed with a water of the known kind ⁦(the parts and names are not carried)⁩. Often the case drives to hand-work: scraping the nose with the nose's own probe by which the scraping can be done, going on scraping till it is clean; and sometimes so much comes out by the scraping that a man marvels at its amount — it nearly reaches half a ratl ⁦(the hand-work is carried as the book's description)⁩. If that does not avail, what we said in the chapter of the piles is done {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.248}
