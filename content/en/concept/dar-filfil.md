## Nature and selection
Small things like fingertips, shaped like the spread flower of the ḥilāf (willow), but smaller; firm and compact; its sharpness is close to pepper's; it is the first fruit of the pepper {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}. The best is not manufactured, and does not dissolve in warm water even if left all day; it tastes like pepper {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}. The footnote gives a Latin name, which is not used.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot in the third degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}. Actions: it dissolves and removes cold disorders {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}.

## Uses by organ
Eyes: with grilled goat liver it helps the film over the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}.
Food: it digests, stirs and strengthens the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}.
Excretory organs: it increases desire, and imitates ginger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}; a labelled book listing.
