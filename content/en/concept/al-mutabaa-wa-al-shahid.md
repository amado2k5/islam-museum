## Overview
In [[work/nuzhat-al-nazar|*Nuzhat al-Nazar*]], [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] deals with what strengthens a report thought to be [[concept/al-hadith-al-gharib|solitary]]: if, after it was thought relatively solitary, someone is found who agrees with its narrator, that is corroboration (*mutaba'a*); if a text from another Companion resembling it is found, that is a supporting report (*shahid*); and the search for them is *i'tibar*. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 68-71}

## Corroboration and its degrees
Corroboration has degrees: if it is for the narrator himself it is complete, and if it is for his teacher or someone higher it is partial; it serves to strengthen the report. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 69}

An example is what [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] transmits in *al-Umm* from Malik, from 'Abd Allah ibn Dinar, from [[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|Ibn 'Umar]], that the Prophet said: "The month is twenty-nine days; do not fast until you see the new moon and do not break the fast until you see it, and if it is clouded over, complete the count as thirty." Some thought al-Shafi'i alone transmitted this wording from Malik, since Malik's other students gave it as "then estimate for it"; but a corroborator was found, 'Abd Allah ibn Maslama al-Qa'nabi, through whom [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] gives it from Malik: this is complete corroboration. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 69}

Partial corroboration was also found: in the *Sahih* of Ibn Khuzayma through 'Asim ibn Muhammad, from his father, from his grandfather Ibn 'Umar, worded "complete thirty", and in the *Sahih* of Muslim through 'Ubayd Allah ibn 'Umar, from Nafi', from Ibn 'Umar, worded "estimate thirty". {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 69} Corroboration is not limited to the same wording: agreement in meaning suffices, but it must come through that same Companion. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 70}

## The supporting report
If a text is found transmitted from another Companion that resembles it in wording and meaning, or in meaning alone, that is a supporting report. For the same hadith, an example in wording is al-Nasa'i's report through Muhammad ibn Hunayn from Ibn 'Abbas, exactly like Ibn 'Umar's; an example in meaning is al-Bukhari's report through Muhammad ibn Ziyad from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]], "complete the count of Sha'ban as thirty". {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 70}

Ibn Hajar mentions that some restricted corroboration to agreement in wording, whether through the same Companion or not, and the supporting report to agreement in meaning; and that each term is sometimes used for the other, "and the matter is easy". {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 70}

## I'tibar
Tracing the routes of a report thought to be solitary through the collections, musnads and booklets, to learn whether it has a corroborator, is *i'tibar*. Ibn Hajar observes that [[person/ibn-al-salah|Ibn al-Salah]]'s heading "knowing i'tibar, corroborations and supporting reports" may suggest that i'tibar is a third category beside them; it is not, but the way of arriving at them. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 70-71}
