## Summary
The Sheikh says that a person's limbs work for his heart, the heart being the king or manager that directs their movement. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:01:30} He relates that the Great Wall of China was breached only a few times in history, and that each time it was through a bribe paid to some corrupt guards, and by analogy Satan enters the heart only through a 'bribed' limb (needs_source: the historical report as the Sheikh tells it). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:04:00}

## Satan's doors through the limbs
He asks: does Satan enter through the eye that does not lower itself from the forbidden or from people's faults; through the hand that takes what is doubtful, noting that whoever eats the doubtful for forty days finds his limbs disobey him; through the hand of a writer or journalist who writes what sets people, groups and schools against each other; through the tongue; or through feet that do not walk to mosques, to reconciliation and to funerals? {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:06:00}

## The daily programme
He proposes that at dawn prayer one set conditions for one's limbs: eye, do not look at the forbidden; hand, do not reach for the doubtful; foot, do not go where God is displeased; then watch them through the day; then sit for a few minutes before sleep and take account of the good and bad one did, increasing the good and seeking forgiveness for the bad; then reproach the limbs. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:07:30} He holds that in this way a person is freed from 'servitude to clay' and lights the 'garment of light' within him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:08:00} See also: [[concept/muraqaba|watchfulness]].
