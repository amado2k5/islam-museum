## The ethnic appellation
Al-Azhari, in general, is an ethnic appellation that denotes a person who has studied at the al-Azhar University at Cairo. It is a nisba, like the other names of scholars that record a place of study or residence, and it is applied to many different scholars of the Islamic world. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 870}
