## Summary
In the first episode of his Practical Administration course, Tareq al-Suwaidan presents management as a human science as old as humanity, and holds that the community's second problem after identity is management — the problems of health, education and armies are, to him, management problems {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:07:30}.

## His division of the science
Al-Suwaidan divides management into four parts: administrative operations, administrative functions, administrative skills, and a fourth part he added himself, the modern trends in management {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:44:30}.
The agreed operations are four: planning, organising, directing and controlling; the disputed ones he judges too small to count as primary — coordination falls under planning, staff selection under organising, and implementation under planning or controlling {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:49:00}.
The administrative functions are six: personnel, purchasing, financing (including accounting), production or service, and marketing {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:53:30}.
The five chief skills: decision-making, presentation, negotiation, running meetings by the nominal-group method, and active listening {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:56:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:58:00}.
Among the modern trends he names: teamwork and organisational behaviour, change (of individuals and of organisations), the learning organisation (on which he has a book and an album), ethics or religion in management, and the skill of delegation {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:59:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 01:01:30}.

## Who needs what
Everyone who manages people needs the operations, whatever their position; the functions are needed by the organisation, not by every manager; and the more skills a manager gains, the better his performance {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:45:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:47:30}.
