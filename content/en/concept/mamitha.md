## Nature and selection
Lumps yellow in colour to black, easily broken, with bitterness and a watery and an earthy substance; the coolness of its wateriness is not severe but like pond water; its origin is a herb at Manbij with a strong scent, a bitter taste and a saffron-coloured juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Temperament
Cold and dry in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Actions and properties
Astringent to a sound degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Swellings and pustules
Helpful for thick hot swellings, and it cures erysipelas that is not strong or great in hard bodies, but not the small ones nor in soft bodies, because it over-dries them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Eyes
It helps in remedies for ophthalmia at its onset {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.
