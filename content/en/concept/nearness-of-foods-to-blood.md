## Summary
Ibn Sina notes that some medicinal foods lie nearer to being drugs and some nearer to being foods, and that foods themselves differ in how close they stand to the substance of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.133-134}.

## Examples
Some foods are close in nature to the substance of blood, such as wine, egg yolk and meat broth; others are a little farther, such as bread and meat; and others are very far, such as the medicinal foods {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.134}.
