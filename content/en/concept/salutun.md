## Nature
Dioscorides said that some people claimed salutun is a plant the people of al-Sham call the spider, with a leaf like the white of khamalawn, which is eaten when fresh with salt and fat after being boiled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
If its milk or its tear drawn from its root is drunk with honey water it causes excessive vomiting in the day {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}
