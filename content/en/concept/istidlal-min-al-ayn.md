## What the eye indicates
Inference from the eye in general is from the state of its vessels, its heaviness and lightness, its colour, whether yellow, dull, leaden or red, and the state of its feel, and all this comes very close in indication to what is in the brain itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20}

Dryness of the eye may indicate dryness of the brain, and flowing of rheum and tears, when there is no complaint in the eye itself, indicates moisture of the front of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20} Large vessels of the eye in hot diseases indicate a burning of the brain and its swellings, especially when the flow is from one of the two eyes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20} When a rheum like a spider's web begins to cover the pupil and then gathers, the time of death is near {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20}

The eye that stays open and does not blink, as it may in qaranitus and sometimes in lethargus, indicates, when the power dissolves, a great affliction in the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20} One that blinks much indicates burning, heat and madness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20} One that keeps its look fixed on one place, the eye of the one with birsam, indicates obsession and melancholia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20} From its movements the imaginings of the brain are inferred, from the beliefs of anger, grief, fear and love {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20}

Protrusion indicates swellings or fullness of the vessels of the brain, and smallness and sunkenness indicate much dissolving of the substance of the brain, as occurs in sleeplessness, qutrub and love {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20} It may also indicate erysipelas of the brain and an eruption in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20}
