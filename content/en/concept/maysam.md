## Nature and selection
A grain like terebinth, triangular in section, yellowish and sweet-smelling, among the things used for fumigation; there is a garden kind with three leaves, a wild kind and an Egyptian kind from which bread is made, which seems to be the harba. The editor's footnote says maysam is habb al-ban {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Temperament
The garden kind is moderate and the wild is in the second degree in heat and dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Actions and properties
The garden kind with three leaves is slightly drying in force, and the wild is stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.
