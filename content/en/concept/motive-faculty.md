## Summary
In the sixth chapter Ibn Sina describes the motive faculty as that which tightens and slackens the tendons and so moves the organs and joints; it is a genus that varies with the origins of movements and follows the judgment of estimation that brings about resolve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.101}.

## Its act
The motive faculty is that which tightens and slackens the tendons and by them moves the organs and joints, extending and bending them and sending its force through the nerve joined to the muscle. It is a genus varying with the variety of the origins of movements, so that in each muscle there is another nature, and it follows the judgment of estimation that brings about the resolve to act {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.101}.
