## Summary
Linguistically, udwan means going beyond a limit. Jurists mostly use it for an unjust attack on a person's life or property, which calls for retaliation (qisas) or compensation (daman) {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11521}.

## Related terms
Zulm is placing a thing where it does not belong and going beyond what is right; ithm is what deserves blame. Ithm is wider than udwan {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11522}.

## General ruling
The ruling depends on the object. A deliberate attack on life or limbs is forbidden and entails retaliation; theft or armed robbery against property entails the fixed penalty; seizing another's right by aggression, as in usurpation or plunder, entails compensation by returning the thing or its equivalent or value; aggression against honour, through unlawful intercourse or slander, entails the fixed penalty, and lesser offences entail discretionary punishment {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11523}.

## Where it is discussed
The details are treated in their own entries: homicide and retaliation, theft and armed robbery, usurpation, plunder and destruction of property, and unlawful intercourse and slander {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11523}.
