## The transmitted treatments
Paulus said: it is treated with the like of honey drink and fenugreek juice when chronic and thickened, and the frankincense collyrium with saffron and al-abar, or it is opened with rosemary and flaxseed mucilage and cooked radish, unless ophthalmia prevents, and it is cleansed with the like of the myrrh and shahtaraj collyrium; if there is no ulcer this collyrium is used ⁦(Paulus's word transmitted as a report attributed to its speaker; the collyrium's recipe and measures are not carried)⁩. Also the magnet drug made for pterygium, and the Samos clay drug mentioned in the watery-bladders chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.181}
