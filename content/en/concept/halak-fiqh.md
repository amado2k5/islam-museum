## Summary
Halak is the loss of a thing's intended benefit, whether or not it survives; the word is also used of death. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16548}

## Destruction of a sold article
If a sold article perishes before delivery, it is at the seller's risk. If it perishes with the buyer and a defect is later found, the buyer recovers compensation for the defect; the Malikis hold that where the destruction followed a defect the seller concealed, the buyer recovers the whole price. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16549} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16550}

## Destruction of property and zakat
If the property perishes before a year is complete, or before the owner can pay, the zakat falls away; if it perishes after the owner could pay, the zakat stands as a debt. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16550}

## Destruction of a sacrifice
If a sheep designated for sacrifice dies before the day of sacrifice without the owner's negligence, he owes nothing; if it perishes through his negligence, he is liable. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16550} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16551}

## Pledge and loan
The Shafi'is and Hanbalis treat the pledgee's possession as a trust and do not hold him liable for loss without fault; the Hanafis treat it as a liability. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16551}
