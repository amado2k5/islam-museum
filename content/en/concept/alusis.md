## Nature
Some claimed that the people of Tabaristan call alusis barbaham; it is a plant like the nettle in everything, except that its leaf is smoother than the nettle leaf and, when rubbed, gives off a very foul smell; it has fine flowers and small purple fruit, and, so it is said, grows in salt marshes, on roads and in ruins {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}

## Actions and properties
Its power dissolves hardness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}

## Wounds and ulcers
Useful for malignant and eating ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}

## Swellings and pustules
Useful as a lukewarm poultice for cancerous swellings, scrofula and other swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}

## Organs of the head
The power of the leaf and the rods is useful for a swelling behind the ear and of the tonsils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}
