## Nature
It was said to be the oil of the castor plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is suited to itch and to ulcers on the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Excretory organs
It is suited to hot swellings of the anus; when drunk it loosens, and it brings out the worms that are in the belly, being very good for this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it is suited to closure of the mouth of the womb, even by smearing with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}
