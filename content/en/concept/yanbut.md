## Nature and temperament
The book identifies it as the Nabataean carob, which it discussed under the letter kha. Its cold and hot qualities are slight, and it is dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.511}

## Properties
Its strength is emetic without a sting. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.511}

## Excretory organs and conception (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it prevents conception. Recorded as a labelled listing for the founder's ruling; not written as advice. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.511}

## Poisons
A decoction of it kills fleas. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.511}
