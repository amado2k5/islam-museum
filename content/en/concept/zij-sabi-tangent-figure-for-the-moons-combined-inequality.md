## Summary
Al-Battani draws a figure with tangent lines to the epicycle and to the eccentric sphere, and right triangles, to reach the combined inequality by geometry. In it the distance between the two centres comes to about ten parts and nineteen minutes. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.

## Eight parts in the radius
At the position the text names, the radius of the inclined epicycle is close to eight parts, and the tangent circle is used together with a bisected chord to fix the angle. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.

## Measuring the sides
By ratios between the sides he shows the sum reaches about seven parts and forty minutes, and he closes by repeating the reckoning on the same figure at other places of the Moon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.82}.
