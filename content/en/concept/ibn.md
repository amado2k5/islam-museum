## Summary
The Kuwaiti encyclopedia gives the literal sense of ibn as the son in direct line by blood, whether from the back or the belly; its use for a son's son is figurative. For the father, the son is every male born to him on a valid bed, under a defective marriage, by an intercourse mistaken in law, or by a bond of ownership; for the mother, every male she bore from a marriage or from an illicit union. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1456} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1457}

## General ruling
The son is an agnate in his own right and the first of the agnates. He takes the whole estate when alone, and the remainder after the fixed shares; he makes his sister an agnate with him; and he is not excluded from inheritance at all. The jurists agree that he inherits the patronage (wala') in place of a daughter. The majority hold that he has the right to contract his mother's marriage. The son of unlawful intercourse is ascribed to his mother alone. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1457}

## Where it is discussed
The rules on the son are treated in their places in the books of fiqh: inheritance, marriage, foster-relations, maintenance, custody, descent, unlawful intercourse, offences and the aqiqa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1458}
