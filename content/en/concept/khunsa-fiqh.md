## Summary
Khuntha is lexically one who is neither purely male nor female, or who has the features of both, from khanth, softness; technically one with both sets of organs, or with neither and only an opening for urine. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7867} It differs from the mukhannath, one who imitates women in softness, speech and movement; one made so by birth is not blamed, while one who imitates by choice is cursed in the sound reports. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7867}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7868} A clear khuntha shows male or female signs and is judged by them; an unclear one has no clear signs or contrary ones, and is of two kinds: both organs with equal signs, or no organ but an opening. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7868} Before puberty the sex is set by urination: from the penis, a boy; from the vagina, a girl; from both, the earlier; the majority rely on the report of Ibn Abbas and hold that the function at birth is urination. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7868}
