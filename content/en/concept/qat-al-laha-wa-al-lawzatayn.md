## Its manner and limits
Its cutting: the tongue is pressed down, the uvula seized with the mould and drawn downward, and it is not cut to the root — something of it is left, for if it is brought near the palate the blood hardly ever stops; yet not too little is cut either lest the lesion remain as it was — what exceeds the natural is cut off. If it is red and swollen, cutting it is dangerous and a blood may burst forth not stanched by any stanching. Of the drugs making it fall by cautery is what he described: the uvula is gripped with the described tool and held an hour without cutting until it works in it, then returned to until it blackens — once blackened it falls within three days at most, the treated man bent over, mouth open, so his saliva runs out and is not held in his mouth. As for the tonsils: they are hung on a hook and drawn outward as far as possible without the membranes being drawn with them, then cut round above the root at the quarter of the length with the cutting tool, one after the other, after watching the stated conditions in their colour and size; once the cut piece falls the blood is left to flow a sound amount with its owner prone on his face lest blood enter his throat, then he rinses with cooled water and vinegar, vomits and coughs to clean his inside, then what cuts the blood is laid on it of what he described (all carried as the book's descriptions, not procedures; the names of this kind left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.299}
