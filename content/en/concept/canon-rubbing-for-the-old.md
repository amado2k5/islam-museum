## Summary
In the fifth chapter Ibn Sina says briefly that rubbing the old must be moderate in kind and amount and must not fall on the weak organs, using rough cloths or bare hands; it benefits them and prevents bouts of illness in their organs, and the bath with rubbing benefits them. Some words in the middle of the chapter are garbled in the printing and are not taken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.253-254}.

## Moderation
The rubbing must be moderate in kind and amount and not fall upon the weak organs. In the middle of this passage of the printing some words are garbled and are not taken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.253}.

## The benefit
The text then says that rubbing with rough cloths or bare hands benefits them and prevents bouts of illness in their organs, and that the bath with rubbing benefits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.253-254}.
