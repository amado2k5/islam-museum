## Equuleus (al-Faras)

The catalogue names the Little Horse by the Greek buruțumis, the horse's head, and by the Arabic al-faras, the horse. Its stars lie in the head and the mouth, and the magnitude column marks four of them as dim. The section follows Delphinus, and its positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.153}
