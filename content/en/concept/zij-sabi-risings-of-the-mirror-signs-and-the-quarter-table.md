## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the risings of Pisces equal those of Aries, Virgo those of Libra, Aquarius those of Taurus, Capricorn those of Gemini, Sagittarius those of Cancer and Leo those of Scorpio, so that it is enough to know the shares between the first of Aries and the first of Cancer, from one degree to ninety. He then explains how to build the risings table degree by degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.

## The mirror signs
He says: know that the risings of Pisces equal those of Aries, the risings of Virgo those of Libra, the risings of Aquarius those of Taurus, the risings of Capricorn those of Gemini, the risings of Sagittarius those of Cancer and the risings of Leo those of Scorpio. So it is enough, in finding the risings, to know the shares between the first of Aries and the first of Cancer, from one degree to ninety. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.

## Building the table degree by degree
He says: if you wish to tabulate the risings for each degree or for more, find the share of one degree in the difference of the day, and the share of two degrees, three, four, up to the completion of the ninety that fill the difference of the whole quadrant. Having done so, take the right-sphere rising of the first degree of Aries and put it in two places; subtract the degree's share from one place and add it to the other. What is reduced is the rising of the first degree of Aries; the one increased is added to one hundred and eighty, and its complement from one hundred and eighty leaves the risings between the first of Aries and twenty-nine degrees of Virgo, while the rising of the degree of Aries taken from three hundred and sixty leaves the risings between the first of Aries and twenty-nine degrees of Pisces. The wording of this passage is disordered in the scan, so this gives its order. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.
