## Summary
Niyya in the lexical sense is intention; technically, for the jurists, the purpose of obeying and drawing near to God. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16711} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16712}
