## Summary
Niyya in the lexical sense is intention. The Hanafis define it as the purpose of obeying and drawing near to God; the Malikis as the heart's purpose in what one does; the Shafi'is as intending a thing together with doing it; the Hanbalis as the heart's resolve to perform an act of worship seeking God. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16711} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16712}

## What needs intention
Acts commanded in their form, such as prayer, purification, fasting and the rites, need intention; those whose purpose is met by performance alone, such as repaying debts, do not. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16713}
Most jurists hold intention a condition of worship; most Shafi'is a pillar of it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16714}

## Specification and purification
Intention must specify the act where it could be mistaken for another of its kind, such as the obligatory prayers and fasting; it is not required in the pilgrimage, the purifications and zakat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16727} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16730}
The majority hold intention a condition of ablution, the Hanafis a sunna; removing filth needs none according to the majority. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16731} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16747} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16750}

## Real and constructive intention
Intention is real or constructive: the real one is a condition at the start of worship, the constructive one suffices for its continuance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16738} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16739}
Intention cannot be delegated; the schools differ on combining intentions and on splitting it over the parts of ablution and worship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16743} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16745} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16746}

## Contracts, oaths and jihad
Intention matters for allusive wording in divorce, zihar, waqf, marriage, emancipation and retaliation; a clear divorce takes effect without it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16755} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16759} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16760}
In oaths the Hanafis follow the intention of the swearer if wronged and of the one demanding the oath if he is the wrongdoer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16757}
Intention affects jihad, slaughter, hunting and lost property. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16761} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16762} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16763}
