## Summary
In chapters 3 and 4 Ibn Sina lists the causes of moistening and of drying, naming in the first rest, sleep, retention of what should be evacuated, abundant food and the bath after eating, and in the second movement, wakefulness, heavy evacuation, intercourse and scarcity of food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.144}.

## Moistening causes
The causes of moistening are many: rest and sleep; retention of what should be evacuated; evacuation of the drying humour; abundant food; moistening food and drug; contact with moistening things, especially the bath and above all after a meal; contact with what cools and so confines the moisture; contact with what warms gently and so makes moisture flow; and moderate joy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.144}.

## Drying causes
The causes of drying are also many: movement, wakefulness and heavy evacuation; intercourse; scarce food and dry food; drying drugs; excessive psychic movements and their frequency; contact with drying things; bathing in astringent waters; freezing cold, which makes the organ hold back and draw food to itself and which constricts, causing blockages that keep food from passing; and contact with what is very hot and over-dissolves, so that even frequent bathing belongs here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.144}.
