## Evacuation
If you see a fullness of humours, blood is taken out; if you see a prevailing of bile in the body by the urine and the other signs, you evacuate the bile; if it is bilious black bile or pure black bile you may need to evacuate by what belongs to it; then turn to the head and evacuate, if it has a fullness of blood or of black bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102}

## After evacuation
After the evacuation attention turns to cooling and moistening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103} Whenever you see the bowel hard, take care lest harmful vapours rise to the head from the heaviness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}

## Regimen
Someone he is ashamed before and holds in awe sits in front of the sick person {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103} Their foods must be moist in every case, yet with their moisture they must not be of those that produce obstructions, and they are not given what drives the urine, for that harms them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103} The rest of their treatment, in what they must guard and beware, is the treatment of melancholia, which he gives in its chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.102-103}
