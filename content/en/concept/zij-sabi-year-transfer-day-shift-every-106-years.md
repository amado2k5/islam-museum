## The advance by one day
Every hundred and six years the day of the month on which the year stood at the start shifts earlier by one day. To shorten the work, the years are divided into groups of four, and the remainder is reckoned as a fraction and added to the calendar hours. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.197}

## The fractional shortcut
The text gives the excess of the year over whole days as three parts and twenty-four minutes, and says the exact reckoning is kept in small parts. Several numbers in the passage are disturbed, so only the form is reported. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.197}
