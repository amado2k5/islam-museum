## Turning the minutes into hours
The minutes of fall are divided by the Moon's hourly motion to give the hours of fall. Subtracting these from the hours of mid-eclipse gives the time of the beginning, and adding them gives the time of the end of the eclipse. Where there is a stay, its hours are added to or taken from the same figures to fix the beginning and end of totality. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.152-153}

## The effect of the time of motion
The text notes that these times are given in general terms, since the Moon's latitude changes between the start and the middle of the eclipse and again between the middle and the end. The times are therefore corrected by the change in latitude, which is done by repeating the calculation. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.152-153}
