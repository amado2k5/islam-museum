## Actions and properties
Its power is cleansing. The editor's note refers to the Tadhkira of Dawud al-Antaki for its identification {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It helps soft, heavy pains in the viscera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Excretory organs
It helps kidney stone, and its decoction breaks it; its root is useful for ulcers of the gut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.
