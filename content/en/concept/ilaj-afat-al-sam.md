## A universal rule, then the bilious and the hot
First: all that is dropped in the ear must be lukewarm, neither cold nor hot — a universal rule — then detail. Of the bilious kind, the bile is evacuated by the laxative, for a bilious diarrhoea often comes naturally and the deafness goes with it, as a bilious flux is often checked and deafness occurs; with heat alone, cooling oils and the like ⁦(drug names and measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.220}
## What is from cold and cold matter
What is from cold and cold matter benefits from all the hot oils — all that after evacuating the cold matter, if congested, by the known general evacuations of the body and those special to the head region, and after the douches known for it. Exercise is of strong benefit, as are loud shouting in the ear and trumpet sounds; a funnel may be set in the ear for the vapour of dissolving decoctions to reach it ⁦(oil, juice, compound and pill names and their measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.220}
## What is from dryness, from occlusion, and after causes
What is from dryness is treated by keeping the bath, moistening food and drink, pouring temperate oil and lukewarm water on the head and snuffing what is known; what is from occlusion is treated as said in the occlusion's chapter; sudden hardness of hearing may benefit from what is known; what follows headache benefits from what is known, and what follows phrenitis must begin with evacuation then the known drops ⁦(drug, compound and pill names and their measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.221}
## The common drugs and the evacuation for hardness of hearing
Returning to the head of the matter: all heaviness of hearing, its pains, winds, ringing and buzzing arising from a cold matter and cold have common drugs known; boys so afflicted benefit from what is known; useful fomentations are applied to the eye and below the ear; the douches named in the head's chapter are set in a tube with the ear opposite so their vapour enters it. For hardness of hearing the fit evacuation is many in number, small in measure each time, to keep the strength and reach ripeness; what is from swellings is treated hot and cold as known, no need to repeat ⁦(drug, fomentation, douche and oil names and their measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.221} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.222}
