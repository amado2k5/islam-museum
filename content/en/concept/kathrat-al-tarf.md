## Its causes and those it befalls

Frequent blinking comes from a light mote in the eye or from pustules; it may be frequent in those given to stretching of the parts and those disposed to it, and is rare in acute diseases, with stretching and convulsion ⁦(a short chapter; its ending in the read copy is at the limit of what is carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.199}
