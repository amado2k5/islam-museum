## Summary
Ruqya in the lexicon is the charm recited over a sufferer from fever or fits, and its plural is ruqa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8942} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8943} The majority allow healing by recitation under three conditions: that it use the words of God or His names and attributes; that it be in Arabic or in words whose meaning is known; and that the one who recites believe that it works only by God's leave, following the saying that there is no harm in charms when they contain no shirk. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8943} Some scholars disliked it as harming trust in God, and some allowed it only for the evil eye and a sting. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8943} The majority allow a fee for ruqya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8944}
