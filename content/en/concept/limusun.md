## Actions and properties
Its fruit is astringent and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.

## Excretory organs
It helps looseness of the bowels and of blood, given in wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it likewise for flooding of the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.
