## Summary
'Amm is the father's brother; the plural is a'mam or 'umumah {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11841}.

## Inheritance
The full-blood paternal uncle takes precedence as an agnate; the father, grandfather, brothers and sons of brothers block him. The maternal uncle is among the distant kin {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11841} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11842}.

## Washing the dead and marriage
The jurists differ on his place in washing and praying over the dead {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11842}. In marriage guardianship he comes after the father, grandfather, brothers and nephews; the majority allow him to compel his ward, while the Hanafis deny compulsion of an adult woman {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11842} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11843}.

## Custody
In custody he is ranked by inheritance, and the full-blood uncle precedes the half-blood one {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11843}.

## Where to look further
Inheritance is treated under «irth», custody under «hadana», and the paternal aunt under «amma» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11843} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11844}.
