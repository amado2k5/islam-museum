## The measures of strength
The text gives the strength of the Sun in aspects as fifteen degrees ahead and behind, that of the Moon and Jupiter as twelve degrees ahead and behind, that of Venus as eight parts, and those of Mars, Mercury and Saturn as seven parts each ahead and behind. The strong measures are those given in the chapter on the sizes of the bodies and their diameters. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.199}

## Reading the number for the Sun
The Sun's strength figure is read here as fifteen degrees from a disturbed phrase and needs confirmation from the Latin text. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.199}
