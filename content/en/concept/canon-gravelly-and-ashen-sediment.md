## Summary
Ibn Sina says gravelly sediment always shows a stone formed, forming or breaking up, and ashen sediment mostly shows phlegm or pus that lingered, changing colour and breaking into pieces. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Gravelly
Gravelly sediment always shows a stone, formed, forming or dissolving; the red kind is from the kidney, the non-red from the bladder. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Ashen
Ashen sediment mostly shows phlegm or pus that was held so long that its colour changed and its parts broke up, and it may be due to an accidental burning of them. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.
