## Summary
Al-Battani lists the Persian months from Farvardin, says Nawruz is the first day of the year, and states that the five added days at the end are not counted in the months. The whole Persian year is three hundred and sixty-five days without fraction. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.

## On the five days
He says the day that follows the completed days of the Persian year is Nawruz, and that the added days are dropped and not counted, which fixes the first day of the Persian month sought. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.104}.
