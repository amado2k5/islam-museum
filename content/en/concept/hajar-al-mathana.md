## Nature
Some say that the stone formed in the bladder, when drunk by a man afflicted with it, breaks up bladder stones {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}. The author says this is one of the treatments he does not hold. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.498}
