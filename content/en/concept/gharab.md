## Choice
Its bark is used and its gum is used, and a good bawraq forms on it, among the best of the kinds of bawariq for eating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Actions and properties
Its flower and its leaf and the juice of both are among the drying drugs without stinging, and there is an astringency in it; its bark is in its strength, but drier {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Cosmetics
The ash of its tree, with vinegar, dries warts and makes them fall, whether inverted or not; the bark of its root enters hair dye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its rind and leaf, when put on cuts and bad fresh wounds, benefit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Joints
Its decoction is a good nutul (pouring) for gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Organs of the head
The juice of its leaf, when dripped into the ear, benefits earache, and likewise its fresh rind; its decoction is a wash for hazaz {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Ocular organs
Its gum and its flower clear dimness of sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Organs of the chest
Its fruit is beneficial for spitting of blood, and its rind is also beneficial {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Organs of nourishment
Its juice expels leeches {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}
