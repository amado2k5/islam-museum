## Choice
The bread should be clean, salted, well kneaded, leavened and well baked in the oven, stale and not eaten hot; hot bread is not accepted by nature, the tannur bread is followed by the furni and the rest is bad; fat bread is better than thin; furni bread is not like the tannur bread in being cooked evenly from both sides, and the bread of the mulla is raw inside; the washed is cooling, little in nourishment, floating on the stomach, fit for the heated, and it generates no obstructions and does not warm; the book gives the way of washing it, left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.715}

## Actions and properties
Samidh is more nourishing than the rest and better in nourishment but slower to pass, hawari following it; khushkar with much bran passes quickly but is less nourishing and the worst; what is well baked is more nourishing, as is that with little leavening, but its nourishment is sticky and obstructing and fits only those who exercise much, the bread of the mulla being of this kind; the washed is little in nourishment, far from obstruction and light in baking and weight; the bread of thin wheat is in the rule of khushkar, the bread of qata'if generates a thick humour, the crumbled is slow to digest and the best is that mixed with almond oil; bread made with milk is much nourishing, slow to descend and obstructing; and a poultice of bread is warmer than a poultice of wheat because of the salt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.715}

## Cosmetics
Bread of new wheat fattens quickly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.715}

## Swellings and pustules
Wheat bread with the water of qaratin and the fitting juices is good for hot swellings, softening and cooling them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.715}

## Wounds and ulcers
Bread mixed with water and salt and rubbed on ringworm benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Organs of nourishment
Hot bread causes thirst by its heat and floats in the stomach by its vaporous moisture and satisfies quickly for that reason; the hot is quicker in digestion and slower in descent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Excretory organs
Khushkar bread softens nature, hawari binds, the leavened loosens, the unleavened binds, the bread of the mulla is of what binds, old dry bread binds even if nothing else is mixed with it, the bread of qata'if binds the belly, and thin bread binds the belly more than fat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}
