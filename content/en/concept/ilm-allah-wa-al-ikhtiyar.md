## Overview
In the section on the acts of servants, Abduh takes up how God’s knowledge, power and will fit with human choice, and says the question belongs to the search for the secret of decree that the believer is told not to pursue{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.64}. He holds that acquisition (kasb) does not in the end escape compulsion{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.65}, and that God’s knowledge of what a person will choose does not remove the choice{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.67}{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.68}. These are the author’s views, presented as his.
