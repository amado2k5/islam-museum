## Summary
'The conditions for the acceptance of prayer' is the title of a sermon series by [[person/abdul-hamid-kishk|Abdul Hamid Kishk]], built on a hadith qudsi he relates, whose sense is that God accepts the prayer of one who humbles himself through it before His greatness, does not lord it over His creation, does not pass the night persisting in sin against Him, spends his day in His remembrance, and shows mercy to the poor, the wayfarer and the widow, and to the afflicted. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:16:00} He counts them as five conditions and gives a sermon to each. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:12:00}

## Conditions of validity and conditions of acceptance
Kishk sets what is usually said about the conditions of a valid prayer, such as covering the body and ritual purity, beside the conditions he speaks of, those of its acceptance (needs_source: some words of the line are garbled). {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01 00:23:30}

## The five conditions as he presents them
1. **Humbling oneself through prayer before God's greatness.** He makes it the first condition and joins it to speaking of the Prophet as teacher and guide. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:14:00}
2. **Not lording it over God's creation.** He explains it as not harming people, and says harm comes in kinds according to the limbs: the eyes harm when they look at what God forbade or wish a blessing away from someone, the ears when they listen to people's secrets and to what God forbade hearing, and so do the tongue, the belly, the hands, the feet and the heart. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:15:00}
3. **Not passing the night persisting in sin.** He opens this sermon with [[concept/tawhid|the oneness of God]] as the ground for leaving sins. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:12:00}
4. **Spending the day in remembrance of God.** He calls it 'the matter of matters' and explains the reality and manners of [[concept/dhikr|remembrance]]. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:15:00}
5. **Mercy to the poor, the wayfarer, the widow and the afflicted.** He makes it the humane and social side of the hadith and gives it two sermons. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:16:30}

## How he groups the conditions
Kishk sorts the five conditions into five aspects, moral, communal, psychological, creedal and humane, and holds that the hadith thereby gathers every side of the Sharia. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:15:30}

## Prayer and action
In the last sermon he says that what is heard in the mosques must settle in the heart, and that when people leave the mosques they must turn it into practice, because speech without action is of no use. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:15:30}
