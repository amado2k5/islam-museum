## The checkers
Among what checks it: snuffing an oil of the known kind and another oil strongly calming to it; it may also be checked by swallowing a hot draught, bathing the head in hot water, pouring a hot oil into the ears, lying on a warm pad placed under the nape, smelling things of the known kind that cut it — and thought and distraction may cut it. As for boys, they benefit from the dripping of a sound kidney set on the fire, roasted and taken before it cooks through — its drippings are inhaled or snuffed ⁦(a report carried as in the book, labelled)⁩. Firm patience with it helps, for it checks it — a sufficient treatment for the weak kind. Among what checks it: binding the eye, the ear, the limbs and the palate, forceful gaping, stuffing, fixing the gaze upward, tossing and turning, anointing the muscles — especially the jaw muscles — with moistening oils, deep sleep, avoiding sudden waking, and keeping clear of dust and smoke ⁦(the oil and smelling names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.251}
## The sternutatories
The sternutatory drugs: they are drugs of the known kind, taken together or singly, applied on a feather in the nose; or things of the known kind taken and smeared in the same way. The light sternutatories include a thing of the known kind when smelt, and things of the known kind — this being among what makes the fevered sneeze. Smearing the inside of the nose with the sternutatory drug is better than a puff into it ⁦(the sternutatory names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.251} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.252}
