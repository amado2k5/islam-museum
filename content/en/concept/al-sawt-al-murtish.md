## The trembling voice
He lessens his talk as much as possible, and his laughter, movement, running, ascending, descending and anger, lays down his hands and rests them as much as he can, then lies down and forces the speech, having weighted his chest with something like lead placed upon it to the measure he can bear; and the best of foods for him is what strengthens his side — the muscles and the trotters and what holds glutinousness and astringency (the weighting and food lines on the book's description; the trotters a labelled listing) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.327}
