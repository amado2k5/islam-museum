## Definition
Hidana is the keeping and raising of the child safe from what harms body and religion; its name is the lap (hidr) because the keeper holds the child to her breast, and in fiqh it is a right of the child first and then of the keeper. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The root of the chapter
A woman came to the Prophet and said: Messenger of God, this son of mine — my belly was his vessel, my breast his drink, my lap his home; his father divorced me and wants to take him from me. The Prophet said to her: you have more right to him so long as you do not marry — and on this report in Sunan Abi Dawud the jurists built the mother's precedence in custody. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}

## The order and the choice
The mother comes first, then her mother however high, then the father's mother, then the maternal aunt, then the paternal aunt in the well-known Hanbali order; custody lapses when the keeper marries a non-relative for most jurists and returns when the marriage ends, the child chooses between his parents at seven in one narration of Ahmad and unqualifiedly for al-Shafi'i, and a girl's custody lasts until she marries or comes of age for most jurists. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
