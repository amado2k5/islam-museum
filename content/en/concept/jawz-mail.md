## Nature and selection
A narcotic poison like the walnut, with short thick thorns; it resembles the vomiting nut, and its seed is like the citron seed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.431}. The editor's footnote describes a plant with a large white flower under a span high {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.431}. The identity is not settled and is queued for outside research.

## Actions
Narcotic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.431}.

## Uses by organ
Head: it stupefies and is bad for the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.431}. The book states a measure that makes a person drunk; the measure is left out.
Poisons: it is an enemy of the heart, and a small amount is deadly for that day {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.431}.
