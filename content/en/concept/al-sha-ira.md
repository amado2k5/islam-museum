## Its definition and matter

The stye is an oblong swelling appearing on the edge of the eyelid, resembling the barley grain in shape, and its matter is in most cases predominating blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.196}

## The treatment

The stye is treated by venesection and evacuation as their management is known, then by compress and by liniments of the medicines prescribed for it ⁦(the names and measures are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.196}
