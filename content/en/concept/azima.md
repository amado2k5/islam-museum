## Summary
In the lexicon, azima is effort and earnestness in a matter, and God's azima is His obligation. As a technical term it is the ruling for the person in a case of excuse, while the forbidding cause still exists {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11598} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11599}.

## Divisions
The Hanafis divide azima into obligation, necessity, sunna and voluntary. Al-Qarafi restricts it to the obligatory and the recommended, while al-Baydawi holds that all five rulings apply to it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11599}.

## Choosing between the original ruling and the concession
Sometimes the law lifts hardship, so that the person may choose between the original ruling and the concession (rukhsa). The jurists differ on which to prefer {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11599} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11600}. The full discussion is in the legal theory appendix {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11600}.
