## Overview
Alfunsho is the transcription of Alfonso adopted by most Arab chroniclers of al-Andalus. Several monarchs of Christian Spain in the Middle Ages bore the name. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 386}

## Variant forms
The forms Idhfunsho and al-Idhfunsho correspond to the old Latin-Gothic form Ildefonso and occur occasionally in the chronicles. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 386}
