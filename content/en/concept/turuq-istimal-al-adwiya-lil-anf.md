## The kinds of routes
Know that the treatments of the nose include some that need not come by way of the nose, like gargles and head embrocations, and some that are its own, like fumigations and smellings; the snuffs, which are wet bodies dripped into the nose; the inhalations, wet bodies drawn into the nose with the drawing of air; and the insufflations, dry prepared things blown into the nose, which must be blown through a tube {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.234} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.235}
## The manner of snuffing
Whoever you give a snuff to should rightly fill his mouth with water, be told to lie down and bend his head back, then the snuff is dripped into his nose; everything placed in the nose must be drawn fully upward so it does its work. The sharp drugs dripped or blown in are often followed by a stinging that then settles. When a sharp pungent snuff is given, it is better that wet cloths soaked in hot water lie on the head, and that sweating has preceded — by milk dripped on him or an oil of the known kind poured on; when the snuff has done its work, follow it by dripping milk into the nose with a cold oil of this kind, which benefits ⁦(the oil and drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.235}
