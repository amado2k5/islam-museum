## Summary
Al-Battani describes another way to find the time of conjunction, by minutes: the equated conjunction hours after noon are multiplied by two and a half minutes. If the product is thirty minutes the conjunction is at midnight; if less it is before midnight, and if more it is after. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.145}.

## Turning the minutes into degrees
The resulting minutes are treated as degrees and the seconds as minutes; the times of the day and night hours are then found. If the degrees are fewer than the day-hour times the conjunction is by day, and the result is divided by a sixth of the day-hour times to give the seasonal hours after noon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.145}.
