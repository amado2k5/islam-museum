## Summary
In chapters 14 and 15 Ibn Sina explains that enlargement of bone and glands comes from abundance of matter and strength of the attracting powers aided by rubbing and warming plasters, and that deficiency arises in original creation, through too little matter or an erring or weak transforming power, or from afflictions from outside or inside {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.146}.

## Enlargement
The causes of enlargement of bones and glands are abundance of matter, strength of the attracting powers in themselves, and strength of the attracting powers helped by rubbing and warming with plasters such as the pitch plaster and the like. This last applies to bone and not to glands {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.146}.

## Deficiency
Deficiency is either present in original creation, through lack of matter or error and weakness of the transforming power, or it comes from afflictions, sometimes from outside, such as cutting, a blow or the spoiling of cold, and sometimes from inside, such as erosion and putrefaction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.146}.
