## Longitude and latitude
If two bodies are close in longitude but not in latitude their conjunction is not true; a full conjunction needs agreement in longitude, latitude and side. They stay conjoined until one lies from the other by half the sum of their discs. If they are on opposite sides no conjunction is counted. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.198}

## The closeness of the discs
The half-sum of the two discs gives the degree of closeness between the bodies, as the earlier chapter on diameters explained. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.198}
