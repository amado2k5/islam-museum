## Their causes and kinds
Ulcers may be bred in the nose, either from sharp or bad vapours or from sharp catarrhs; they are either stinking putrid ones, scabby crusted ones, pustular ulcers, or excoriating ulcers ⁦(the editor's footnote: wounds that lead to the skin's being stripped off them)⁩ — and they are either apparent or hidden {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.246}
