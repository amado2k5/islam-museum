## Overview

In fiqh usage hakim covers the caliph, the governor, the judge and the arbitrator, and used alone it means the judge. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6727}

## Appointment

Appointing a judge is a communal duty, and the appointment of the ruler is an obligation on the imam. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6728}

## Authority

A judge's authority is valid only when the imam or his deputy appoints him with permission. The detail is in the entry on judiciary. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6728}
