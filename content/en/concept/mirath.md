## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy warns against denying an heir his inheritance, and says that whoever keeps an heir from his inheritance will be kept by God from his own inheritance in Paradise on the Day of Resurrection (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:23:30}. He reminds listeners that God is the one who set the limits, laid down the obligatory shares and distributed the estates, so a person has no right to interfere in their distribution {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:23:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)
The Sheikh warns against denying an heir his inheritance, and says that whoever keeps an heir from his inheritance will be kept by God from his own inheritance in Paradise on the Day of Resurrection (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:23:30}. He reminds listeners that God is the one who set the limits, laid down the obligatory shares and distributed the estates, so a person has no right to interfere in their distribution, and he recalls that a believing man or woman has no choice once God and His Messenger have decided a matter {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:23:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh holds that some elderly fathers blessed only with daughters fear for them after their death and sign everything over to them as a barrier against their uncles, which he counts as a whisper of Satan; he holds that the real insurance for one's offspring is fear of God and upright speech, mentions that whoever deprives an heir of his inheritance God will deprive of his inheritance in Paradise, and says that in more than four decades of preaching he never saw a father who disinherited some heirs and gave his daughters everything without the daughters' affairs turning out other than he wished (needs_source: the hadith and his experience as he states them). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:04:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
Omar Abdelkafy warns against tricks in inheritance, such as signing land over to some children and not others to secure them after one's death, saying the man is called to account for it in his grave while his heirs enjoy it, and recalls that the Prophet refused to witness an unequal gift among children, saying he would not bear witness to injustice. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:08:30}
