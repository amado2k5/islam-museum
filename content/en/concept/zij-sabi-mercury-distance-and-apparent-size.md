## Mercury
The text gives Mercury's farthest distance as one hundred and sixty-six times the Earth's radius. The variation of its size between its farthest and nearest positions is given as about two and a third and a quarter to one. Its apparent diameter is about four and a half and a sixth of a minute of arc, and its bulk is about one part in seventeen of the Earth. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.186-187}

## Caution with the figures
Some of Mercury's figures, such as the part in seventeen, are hard to read in the scan and are to be checked against the original before they are quoted. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.186-187}
