## Morning rising and evening setting
When the planet's anomaly runs from zero to qaf, 180 degrees, it is in the morning rising, and when it runs from qaf onward it is in the evening setting. The rising is named for the morning and the setting for the evening. This is chapter 48 and gives the rising and the disappearance of the five wandering planets. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.180}

## The difference in the anomaly
The excess of anomaly is reckoned so that the first rising and the last setting can be fixed as needed, according to the rising point. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.180}
