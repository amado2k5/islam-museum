## Origin
The title was originally granted in the Mamluk kingdom to all those who had seniority in service and in years, so that there was a whole group of amirs each of whom was called al-amir al-kabir. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 466}

## Restriction
In the days of Shaykhun al-'Umari (752/1352) the title was reserved for the commander-in-chief (atabak al-'asakir) of the kingdom, and from that date it became the most common title of the commander-in-chief beside that of his rank. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 466}
