## Summary
The Sheikh lists the effects of disobedience: darkness in the face, darkness in the path behind, emptiness in people's hearts, lack of humility before people, and lack of contentment with God (needs_source: the origin of this list is not given in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:17:00}

## The effects of obedience
The Sheikh sets against these the effects of obedience: light in the face, adornment in the heart, love in people's hearts, blessing in the family, and God's remembrance of the servant. He gives the last in words he attributes to God, which are not reproduced here. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:18:00}

## Sins take away blessings
The Sheikh says that sins take away blessings, and that the Noble Writers complain to their Lord of the abundance of gross sins. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 00:16:30}

## Look at the greatness of the One disobeyed
The Sheikh says that a person should not look at the smallness of a sin, but at the greatness of the One whom he has disobeyed. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:22:30}
