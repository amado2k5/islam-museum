## Nature
It was said in the chapter of the letter qaf at qasab al-dharira, but we mention another part of its actions {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Ulcers
It was said that nothing is better for a burn by fire than dharira with rose oil and vinegar; in the printed page the word dharira is run together with the heading of this section, and it is not counted as part of the sentence {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Organs of nourishment
It benefits swellings of the stomach and intestines, and swellings of the liver, and dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}
