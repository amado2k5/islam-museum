## Summary
A rahib is, in the lexical sense, one who fears God; in general use it is a Christian who withdraws from the world for worship, and the plural is ruhban. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8572}

## Distinctions
The qissis is the Christian scholar and the ahbar are the Jewish scholars; the rahib is the withdrawn devotee. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8572} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8573}

## Killing in jihad
If monks take part in fighting, mix with the people, or help the fighters with their counsel, they may be killed when taken, by agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8572}
If they keep apart in their monasteries without taking part, the majority (Hanafis, Malikis and Hanbalis, and one view of the Shafi'is) say they are not killed; the more apparent Shafi'i view allows it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8572} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8573}

## Jizya
Whether monks owe the jizya is disputed; the detail is under jizya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8573}
