## Summary
Al-Battani describes a calculation for the equation: the chord of the argument and the chord of what remains to complete it to a known limit are taken, each is multiplied by the radius of the planet's epicycle and the product divided by the radius; what comes out for the complement chord is added to sixty and squared, the square of what came out for the first chord is added to it, the root is taken and kept; then the complement figure is multiplied by the radius, divided by the kept root, and its arc taken {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.76-77}.

## The second case and what comes out
If the parts exceed a certain limit, its complement is added to the parts according to that limit and the work is repeated: the chord of the remainder and the chord of its complement are taken and multiplied by half of the kept root and divided by the radius; the complement of what the parts yield is taken from sixty, squared, and the square of what the complement yields is added; the root is taken; the complement figure is multiplied by the radius and divided by the root, and its arc taken; whatever the arc reaches is the share of those parts, that is the equation wanted for the planet {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.77}. The text passes no judgement on the rule, and the numerals of the limits are disturbed, so only its structure is carried over {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.77}.
