## Overview
'Arjun holds it among God's laws in the struggle of truth and falsehood that He repels some people by others — without it, places of worship would be demolished and the earth corrupted {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 68}. The permission to fight in Surat al-Hajj came to repel the aggressors' injustice from believers driven from their homes; the Muslim community does not fight for love of fighting or for transgression {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 65}. Preparing deterrent strength as able is obligatory — strength prepared to deter, not to attack {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 64}.
