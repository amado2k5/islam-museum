## Deeds and motions

Among deeds and motions is all that dries, such as much coitus ⁦(carried as a listing for the founder's ruling)⁩, long gazing at bright things, excessive reading of fine script — moderation in it benefits — and likewise fine handiwork, sleeping on a full stomach, and late supper; one weak of sight must wait until the meal descends, for all repletion harms him, all that dries the nature harms him, all that turbids the blood harms him, and sugar harms him. Vomiting benefits in cleansing the stomach yet harms in moving the matters of the brain and driving them to it; if it cannot be avoided it should come after food and gently. Bathing harms, excessive sleep harms, hard weeping, frequent venesection and especially cupping ⁦(the read text is broken here)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.206}

## Foods

Among the harmful foods are the salty, the acrid, the wind-releasing, what hurts the mouth of the stomach and the thick turbid wine; and it names among vegetables and foods what should be avoided ⁦(the list of names is carried only in sum)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.206}
