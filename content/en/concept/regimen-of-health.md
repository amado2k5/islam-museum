## Overview
Ibn Sina notes that preserving health and removing disease require further subjects — among them regimen in food and drink, choice of air, and the measure of motion and rest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.15}. The third fann of the first book treats the shared regimen of adults in seventeen chapters, beginning with exercise — since most of health-preservation is to exercise — then the regimen of food, then of sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.221}.
