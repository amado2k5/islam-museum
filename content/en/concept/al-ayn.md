## Summary
The evil eye is real, as the hadith states; it strikes the envied by God's leave without touch, repelled by ruqya and remembrance.

## In the texts
[[passage/quran-113-1|seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak]] · [[passage/quran-68-51|they would nearly trip you with their eyes]]

## Note
In the account of Amir ibn Rabi'a and Sahl ibn Hunayf it brought a man near death; protection is dhikr and prayers for blessing.
