## Summary
Ibn Sina notes that a drug may be needed that matches the humour in quality, such as scammony for bile, in which case a drug of opposite quality that does not hinder purging, such as myrobalan, is mixed in and the resulting bad temperament put right afterward; for those with swellings of the viscera he names gentle laxatives {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.277}.

## Matching and correcting
Ibn Sina says need may force the use of a drug that matches the evacuated humour in quality, as scammony does when bile must be evacuated. One then mixes into it something of opposite quality that agrees with purging or at least does not stop it, such as myrobalan, and puts right any bad temperament that arises afterward. People with swellings of the viscera purge and vomit weakly, and if one is forced to evacuate them he names bindweed, safflower, polypody and cassia pulp and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.277}.
