## Summary
Ibn Sina describes three kinds close to the wave pulse, the wormlike, the ant and the saw, and shows how they are alike and how they differ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## The wormlike and the ant
The wormlike resembles the wave pulse but is small and very frequent, its frequency suggesting speed though it is not quick. The ant pulse is much smaller or more frequent still. The unevenness of both in rise, and in earliness and lateness, is plainer to the touch than their unevenness in breadth, which may not show at all {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## The saw pulse
The saw pulse resembles the wave pulse in the unevenness of its parts in rise, breadth, and earliness and lateness, but it is hard, and in its hardness its parts differ. It is a quick, frequent, hard pulse uneven in the size of its expansion and in hardness and softness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.
