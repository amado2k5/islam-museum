## Nature
A plant called halibi because it has the property of curing swellings of the ureter, as a poultice or hung on the body; it is composed of powers, like the rose. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}

## Temperament
It has a cooling power together with some heat. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}

## Actions
It dissolves, and has a cooling, expelling power. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: the book says it cures the swelling arising in the ureter when hung on the body, let alone when applied as a poultice. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}
