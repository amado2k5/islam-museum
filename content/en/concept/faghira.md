## Nature
A grain like the chickpea with a grain like mahlab, and inside it a black grain like hemp seed, brought from Safala {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Actions and properties
It has dissolving and astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It enters the remedies that put right the cold stomach and liver, and benefits cold indigestion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Excretory organs
It benefits cold diarrhoea and restrains the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}
