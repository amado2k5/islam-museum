# The Arab woman and the international conventions

## The episode and the votes
The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode debated the woman's global and Arab status and the international conventions — now binding on signatory states with UN monitoring bodies {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:02:00}. The youth voted: only 8% judged the woman's global situation worse than the man's; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:10:30} and 32% judged the Arab woman worse than the rest of the world {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:32:30}.

## Figures of the gap — as the panelists aired them
From the episode's figures: women do two-thirds of the world's work hours yet receive a tenth of its income; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:16:30} women head about 30% of households in the third world; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:20:00} and women's parliamentary representation is 15% globally and lower in the Arab states — while Rwanda sits at 49%, above America {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:35:30}.

## Did the conventions help?
Panelists split: one saw them making some governments care more, though comparing Beijing 1995 with today shows improvement is not due to them; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:11:00} another warned they pour all cultures into one template — restructuring the family and the concept of gender — 'globalisation contains four core issues' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:13:30}. One replied the Arab woman fares better — the Western family is nearly ended {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 00:43:00}.

## The conclusion — take what lifts injustice
The host closed with the equation: everyone contributes to lifting injustice from women; parts of the conventions excellently serve that end so we support them, and whatever directly collides with our religion we reject, our religion coming first — 'and this is the wasatiyya on the subject' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep15 01:17:00}.
