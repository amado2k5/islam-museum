## The word absolutely and specifically
Falij may be said absolutely and may be said in the specific verified sense: the word falij in the absolute usage may mean what relaxation means, while falij in the specific sense is the relaxation general to one side of the body lengthwise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.136}

## The two kinds of the specific
Of it is the kind in the side beginning at the neck, where the face and head with it remain sound, and the kind that runs through the whole side from head to foot; the Arabic language points to this meaning by falij, for falj in their tongue may signify a half, a halving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.136}

## Its boundary with apoplexy, and its smallness
If falij takes the organs of the head, were it general to them it would be apoplexy; and of it is what is confined to a single finger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.136}
