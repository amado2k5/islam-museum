## Summary
The Sheikh says that the reward of a planted tree continues for as long as any person or bird eats from it, or any creature shelters under it, which is the meaning of ongoing charity. He says this is reported in a hadith and a narration (the text is not reproduced). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:07:30}

## Knowledge, the mosque, the Qur'an and the library
The Sheikh says that teaching people beneficial knowledge, teaching the interpretation of the Qur'an's verses, building a mosque, writing a copy of the Qur'an, founding a charitable library, or taking part in building a school, brings a reward that reaches the person after his death. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:11:30}

## A charitable project for orphans in Giza
The Sheikh says that he and those with him carried out a large project caring for orphans in Giza governorate. As an example he speaks of 500 pounds, of which 100 pounds would go to someone who memorises the Qur'an (needs_source: the sum is given as an illustration and has not been verified). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 01:13:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
## Key to good, key to evil
The Sheikh holds that the best of people with God is one whom He makes a reliever of people's distress, and the best relief is guiding a wrongdoer or taking a sinner by the hand; a servant is either a key to good and a lock on evil or the reverse; he cites the hadith 'whoever establishes a good practice in Islam... and whoever establishes a bad practice', noting that [[person/qabil|Qabil]], the first killer, bears a share of the burden of every killer after him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:05:00} He gives examples of what he calls 'ongoing evil': a channel director who makes a presenter remove her hijab and bears the burden of those she influences, and a dancer who founds a dance institute before her death; this is his view. He mentions the hadith that deeds end except for three, and calls for good that reaches others: a sunshade, a head covering or bottles of water for workers in the heat. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep26 00:10:30}
