## The rule
Its treatment is evacuation of blood and purging by what brings out each humour; then the head is strengthened by what is known from the general rule; if its cause is a cold striking the brain, the book names what suits it; the discussion of it should not be long, for what we gave before suffices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.118}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. The book here names a compound purgative and named drugs, and they are not reproduced. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.118}
