## Nature and selection
Ithmid is described as the essence of dead lead, with a power like that of calcined lead {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}. The good kind has shining fragments, no foreign matter or dirt, and crumbles very easily {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}.

## Temperament and actions
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second; it dries more strongly than Syrian red alum. It astringes and dries without burning, and stops bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: it heals ulcers, removes proud flesh and closes the wound; on a burn, with soft fat, it prevents ulceration, and an ulcer that forms is healed when it is mixed with wax and white lead {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}. Head: it prevents the cerebral nosebleed that comes from the membranes of the brain. Eyes: it preserves their health and clears the filth of their ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}. Excretory organs: when taken, it helps uterine haemorrhage {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}. Its substitute is calcined anak {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}.
