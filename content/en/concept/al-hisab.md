## Summary
The Reckoning on Judgment Day is fixed in Book and Sunnah: every people is summoned by its leader, each takes his record — who receives it in his right hand is reckoned an easy reckoning and returns rejoicing; who receives it behind his back calls for destruction.

## In the texts
In the hadith, God draws the believer close until he admits his sins, then says 'I veiled them for you in the world and I forgive them today' — and who is interrogated in the reckoning is punished.
