## Nature
The wild kind of it is in the strength of black poppy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.707}

## Temperament
Galen said that the coldness of the garden kind is not great, but like the coldness of pond water, and its moisture is thicker than the moisture of chard and subtler than that of mallow; it is said that in moistening and drying it is between cabbage, al-qatf and al-yamaniya; the author says that whoever says it is cold in the third judges it a poor nourishment and little of it, which is not so, and it is likely to be in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Actions and properties
There is no cleansing, astringency or loosening in it, being free of saltiness, harshness and the like; the blood generated from it is better than that generated from the vegetables; the most nourishing is the cooked, it benefits watery flux, the unwashed is better, washing increases its flatulence as with all cold vegetables, and it is quick to digest; used in the middle of drinking it prevents excess of intoxication {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Swellings and pustules
Applied, it benefits hot swellings and erysipelas when they are not large and severe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Joints
It is a poultice of benefit on a sprain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Organs of the head
It brings sleep and removes sleeplessness boiled and raw, and benefits delirium and the burning of the head by the sun, and is a remedy for blockage of the nostrils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Ocular organs
The milk of the wild kind cleanses ulcers of the cornea and that of the garden kind is near it, it is a poultice for hot ophthalmia, the milk of the wild kind benefits al-gharb, and eating it continually darkens the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Organs of nourishment
It benefits thirst and heat and inflammation of the stomach; the garden kind is good for the stomach and quick to digest, taking it with vinegar whets the appetite, and eating it benefits jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Excretory organs
The milk of lettuce loosens watery chyme; the amount and the way of taking it are left out; the milk of the garden kind, when it has grown large, is near the milk of the wild; the lettuce itself neither binds nor loosens, being neither salty nor harsh nor cleansing, but it is a promoter (mudirr), the printed line not saying of what {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Poisons
The milk of the wild kind is given for the sting of the tarantula and the scorpion; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it increases milk, that its seed dries semen, calms the desire for intercourse and benefits frequent nocturnal emission, that the plant itself is less in this than its seed, and that the wild kind promotes menses; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}
