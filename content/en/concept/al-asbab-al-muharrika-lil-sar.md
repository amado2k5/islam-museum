## Air, seasons and winds
Among the causes that provoke epilepsy is moving to a certain air — just as among what removes it is moving to a certain air — and every excessive solar or fiery heat and every cold; frequent intercourse (a labelled line, held for the founder's ruling); it may also be stirred by much rain and the north and south winds together: the north wind and northern lands because it congests the materials and stops them filtering through, the south wind and southern lands because it moves the humours, fills the brain, thins them and stirs them up; it flares much in winter as in the north, and is rarer in northern lands but deadly there, for but for a strong cause it would not occur {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.125} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.126}

## Senses and motions
Smells, pleasant or not, may provoke it, as may motion and watching swift turning motions, looking down from a height, long stays in the bath, bathing before digestion, and pouring hot water on the head; old wine harms him, and what has not clarified of the new and not settled, and raw wine harsh in the brain, and everything thick, flatulent, astringent and cold, and every sharp burning thing; indigestion too provokes epilepsy by stirring and moving the humours, as do overeating, bad digestion and sleeplessness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.126}

## Psychic pains and impressions
Strong psychic pains — grief, anger and fear — and strong sensory impressions: hearing great sounds like thunder, the beating of drums and the lion's roar, jingling sounds like bells, and screeching like the creak of a sharp door; also seeing dazzling lights like blinding lightning and the light of the sun's disc, and touching strong motions like the motions of storm winds. Epilepsy may be stirred by exercise on a full stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.126}

## Drugs that induce the fit
The book has a chapter on the drugs that provoke epilepsy and unmask the epileptic — named fumigations and substances referred to the tables of the Second Book, whose names are not carried here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.126}
