## Nature and selection
Buha is a grass that grows with bish; wherever a bish grows beside it, its tree does not bear fruit. It is the greatest antidote of bish and has all the benefits of bish for leukoderma and leprosy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}. Bish mush is an animal that lives at the root of bish, like a mouse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: helps leukoderma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.
Joints: helps leprosy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.
Poisons: an antidote to every poison and to vipers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.415}.
