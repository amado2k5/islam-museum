## Overview
The author defines the rational person (al-insan al-rashid) as one who manages their resources, their needs, and the needs of those around them well, and holds that the economic problem confronts both rich and poor alike {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 21} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 22} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 23}.

## Hayy ibn Yaqzan
The author invokes the symbolic figure of Hayy ibn Yaqzan, finding in him economic conduct paired with ethics and reflection, and regards him as superior to the abstract "economic man" absorbed solely in material utilities {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 33} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 35}.
