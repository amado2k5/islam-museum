## Summary
Rabd in the lexicon is a sheep pen; the jurists use it for the houses and dwellings around a city, and for the pen. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8611} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8612}

## Shortening the prayer on a journey
The outskirts count as the city for shortening the prayer on a journey, and so do villages joined to them; orchards do not, even when people of the town live in them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8613}

## Friday and Eid prayers in the outskirts
Friday and Eid prayers are valid in outskirts that belong to the city when the conditions are met, and most jurists hold them invalid in outskirts outside it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8613} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8614}

## Reviving outskirts
Reviving applies only to dead land, and outskirts are not dead land, so they may not be revived. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8614}

## Sheep pens
Prayer in sheep pens is allowed by agreement when free of impurity, while prayer in camel resting places is disliked. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8614}
