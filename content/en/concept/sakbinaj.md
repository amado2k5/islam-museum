## Nature
It is a tree with no use in it but in its gum, and some say a kind of qanna changes and becomes sakbinaj. The book reports from Dioscorides that it is the gum of a plant like the cucumber in shape, growing in a watered land; the good kind is clear, red outside and white within, with a scent between that of asafoetida and that of qanna, pungent, and it is sometimes adulterated with another gum {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Selection
The better of its two kinds is the denser and clearer, its inside tending to red and its outside to white, dissolving quickly in water, unlike the kind adulterated with qanna; the best is the Isfahani {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Actions and properties
Dissolving, thinning, dispersing, warming and cleansing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Cosmetics
Whoever uses it in his food, his colour improves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Joints
It helps palsy and the tearing of muscles and their tendons, it purges the matter in the hips, and likewise cold pains of the joints {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Organs of the head
It dissolves cold headache, and the windy kind is useful against epilepsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Organs of the eye
As a collyrium it helps darkness of the eye, thickness of the eyelids and scars in the eye; it is among the best of drugs for the water descending in the eye, and it cleanses ulcers that arise in the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Organs of the chest
It helps pain of the chest and the side and chronic cough, it clears the chest strongly and brings out raw humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It helps dropsy and brings out the yellow water, and a poultice of it with bitter almond, rue or honey helps pain of the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Excretory organs
It helps colic as an enema and a drink and griping, brings out stones from the kidneys and bladder, helps pains of the womb, softens the belly gently and brings out the sticky humour and yellow water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Fevers
It helps periodic fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Poisons and bites
It is given in wine against the stings of vermin and all deadly poisons, its action stronger than that of qanna, and it may help as a salve in all of this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it increases desire, and that drunk with a honey drink it provokes the menses and kills the foetus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.595}.
