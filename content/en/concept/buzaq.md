## Nature and selection
The strongest kind is that of the hungry on an empty stomach, especially of hot temperament {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

## Temperament and actions
Its strength depends on being taken fasting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: helps ringworm (qawba) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Eyes: helps eye spots and white spots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Poisons: kills all vermin, snakes and scorpions {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.
