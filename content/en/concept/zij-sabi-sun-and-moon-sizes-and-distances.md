## The preface and the reference to the Almagest
Al-Battani says the distances, diameters and sizes of the Sun and the Moon were set out in the Almagest and in what was measured in eclipses. He begins with the spheres from the lunar sphere up to Saturn, as the earlier sages before Ptolemy had reported them. He states that the Sun's diameter is five and a half times the Earth's diameter, a result the text says is shown by measurement. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.185-186}

## The diameter of the Sun
Since the Sun's diameter is five and a half times the Earth's diameter, when the Sun's middle diameter is taken as set out, the Earth's diameter on that scale comes to two hundred and one and a half. The text uses this as the base for its scale of sizes. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.189-190}
