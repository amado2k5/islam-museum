## Summary
Ibn Sina says wine benefits the bilious by bringing out bile and the phlegmatic by ripening moisture, the more so the more fragrant and pleasant it is, and that it carries through the body, cuts phlegm, brings out yellow bile in the urine and makes black bile slip away, while one of strong brain does not quickly grow drunk and his mind clears {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## Benefit by temperament
Wine benefits the bilious by driving out bile and the moist by ripening their moisture; the more fragrant it is, and the better its scent and taste, the more fitting it is. Ibn Sina calls it an excellent conveyor through the whole body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## On the humours
It cuts and dissolves phlegm, brings out yellow bile in the urine and elsewhere, makes black bile slip so that it leaves easily and checks its harm by opposition, and loosens whatever is congealed without much foreign heating. One of strong brain does not quickly become drunk, his brain does not take the bad vapours that rise, and his mind clears as other minds do not with the same drink {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.
