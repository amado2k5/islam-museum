## Summary
Ibn Sina says the bath warms, cools, moistens and dries, helps and harms, and sums its benefits as bringing sleep, dissolving what is meant to dissolve, shaking out what is meant to be shaken out in its natural direction, stopping looseness and removing fatigue, and its harms as weakening the heart if overdone, bringing fainting and nausea, stirring still matters and readying them for putrefaction and turning them to the cavities and weak organs, so that swellings arise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.226-227}.

## The benefits
It was learned earlier that the bath warms, cools, moistens, dries, helps and harms. Its benefits are bringing sleep, dissolving what is meant to dissolve, shaking out what is meant to be shaken out in its natural direction, stopping looseness of the bowels and removing fatigue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.226-227}.

## The harms
Its harms are weakening the heart if overdone, bringing fainting and nausea, and stirring still matters, readying them for putrefaction and turning them toward the cavities and weak organs, so that swellings arise from them on the outside of the organs and the inside {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.
