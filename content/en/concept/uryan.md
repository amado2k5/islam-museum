## Summary
In the lexicon, a naked person (uryan) is one stripped of clothing, the opposite of dressing; the technical sense does not differ {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11574}.

## Bathing naked
Bathing naked in front of people is forbidden according to all jurists. When alone it is permitted but disliked, and covering oneself is recommended {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11575}.

## Entering a bathhouse naked
The majority hold that entering a bathhouse is permitted, provided that no part of the body that must be covered is exposed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11575} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11576}.

## Praying naked
According to the majority, covering the private parts is a condition for a valid prayer, so one unable to cover himself prays naked {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11576} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11577}. The manner differs: the Hanafis and Hanbalis have the naked person sit and signal the bowing and prostration {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11576}. Whether to repeat the prayer after finding a covering is disputed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11578}.

## Circumambulating naked
The Hanafis, Shafi'is, Malikis and Hanbalis all require covering for tawaf, so a naked person able to cover himself may not validly circumambulate {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11578}.

## Where to look further
