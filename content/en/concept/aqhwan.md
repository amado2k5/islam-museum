## Nature and selection
Aqhwan has a white kind and a yellow-red kind, the white being stronger; it is fine stalks with a flower of white petals, sharp in smell and taste {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. The book reports Dioscorides as saying that some people call it amariyun, others qurinbun and others arqasmun, and that it has leaves like coriander leaves, a round white flower with a yellow middle, a smell with some heaviness and a bitter taste {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}.

## Temperament and actions
It is hot in the third degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It warms, ripens and opens obstructions, and in its red kind there is astringency and holding back of kinds of flow together with its resolving, though its astringency and drying are the greater; it promotes sweat, and so does its oil when anointed, it opens the mouths of the vessels, and it resolves and refines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}.

## Uses by organ
It induces torpor (musbit), and when its fresh flower is smelled it induces sleep, and its oil helps pains of the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It helps twisting of the nerve when its decoction is wetted on a piece of wool and laid on it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It dissolves the hot swelling in the stomach and the clotted blood in it, and helps cold swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It helps fistulas, strips scabs and malignant ulcers, and helps wounds of the nerve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It helps asthma; the book says it is drunk as aftimun is drunk, and the method is not reproduced {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It is bad for the mouth of the stomach, though it dissolves and dries what is drawn to it and dissolves clotted blood in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It promotes urine strongly, dissolves clotted blood in the bladder with honey water, and crushes stone when drunk with its flower {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It purges black bile and phlegm, helps hot swellings of the anus, opens piles, it and its oil, helps hydrocele after it is incised, and helps colic, pain of the bladder and hardness of the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}.

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that its fuqqah in wine promotes the menses and the urine, and so does using its oil as a pessary, for it promotes strongly, and that using its oil as a pessary also dissolves hardness of the womb and opens the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.355}; reports attributed to the book, without recommendation.
