## Actions and properties
It makes a sticky chyme that is not thick, but is praiseworthy with little waste {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps hot cough, especially with barley kashk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Well digested, good in chyme, sticky and not thick. The sign of its good digestion is how fast it swells and turns to shreds in cooking, though its nourishment is not abundant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.

## Excretory organs
It loosens the bowels by the stickiness in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.
