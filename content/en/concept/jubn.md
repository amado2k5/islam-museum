## Nature and selection
Cheese is made from milk, or from buttermilk, and is called aqit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}. The best is the one between the chewy and the crumbly; the sour-made is best; spices that cut and disperse make it worse; the goat cheese from goats grazing on purgative herbs is better than that from goats on fenugreek and vetch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: fresh cheese is cold and moist in the second degree; the aged salted kind is hot and dry; whey carries a bitter part of the first blood and some heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}. Actions: it cleanses; fresh moist cheese is nourishing and fattening and is best followed by honey; aged cheese is hot, cleansing and bitter; the salted, not aged, kind is between; whey fattens dogs greatly; aqit has a dissolving strength {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: whey with purgatives that clear black bile helps freckles; fresh cheese cooked with a thickened pomegranate-peel decoction prevents facial spasm; aged salted cheese wastes the body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.
Swellings and wounds: fresh unsalted cheese prevents swelling of wounds; the aged kind suits bad ulcers and wounds; the fresh suits light wounds, especially with willow leaves and wild sorrel, and its whey drunk for the itch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.
Joints: aged cheese ground with oil or with the brine of salted cow's feet is applied to a joint stone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.
Eyes: unsalted cheese is a poultice for inflammation and for an ecchymosis in the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.
Chest: boiled in water and given to a nursing mother, it increases her milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.
Food: the salted kind is bad for the stomach; the book cites Dioscorides that fresh cheese is good for the stomach, which the book itself treats as doubtful; aqit is less harmful to the stomach than ordinary cheese {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428}.
Excretory organs: it breeds stones in the kidney and bladder, especially the fresh kind; its whey purges yellow bile, and with honey it is more useful; the medicinal use is a water made from goat or sheep milk; roasted cheese is good for intestinal ulcers and stops diarrhoea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.428} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.429}.
