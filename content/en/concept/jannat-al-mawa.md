## Overview
Jannat al-Ma'wa — the garden of refuge — is the lodging of the people of Paradise, named in two places: «the garden of refuge is your lodging» in al-Sajda, and «near it is the garden of refuge» beside the Lote-Tree of the Boundary in the Mi'raj scene of al-Najm {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the exegesis
Ibn Kathir explains «near it is the garden of refuge» as beside the Lote-Tree of the Boundary — the boundary at which the knowledge of the creatures ends {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
