## Summary
The prophets' dreams are the visions the prophets saw in sleep or waking in the manner of prophethood; among them are those in the Qur'an of Joseph, Abraham and the Prophet {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## Joseph's dream
Joseph saw in his youth eleven stars and the sun and the moon prostrating to him, and told his father, who forbade him to recount it to his brothers {cite:tanzil-quran}. In prison he saw the two young men — one pressing wine and the other carrying bread that birds ate — and interpreted them {cite:tanzil-quran}. And the king saw seven fat cows eaten by seven lean ones and seven green ears and seven dry, and he interpreted them as seven years of plenty and seven of famine {cite:tanzil-quran}. Jacob said to him: this is the interpretation of my dream of old that my Lord has made true {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## Abraham's dream
Abraham said to his son when they walked together: I see in a dream that I am sacrificing you; the son obeyed and said: my father, do what you are commanded {cite:tanzil-quran}. Then God said to him: you have fulfilled the vision; thus do We reward the good {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## The Prophet's dreams
The Prophet saw in his sleep that he was in Paradise where a woman was performing ablution beside a palace for Umar, and he recounted it to his Companions {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. He saw as if he were drinking milk until it came out of his nose, and interpreted it as knowledge {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. And he saw that God showed him the enemy at Badr as few in number {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## Perspectives
- **Ahl al-Sunna:** the prophets' dreams are true and from God; they are not wondered at nor denied.
- **The exegetes:** they differed in interpreting some dreams but agreed they are among the signs of prophethood.
