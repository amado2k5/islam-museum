## Summary
The zodiac (ecliptic) is the circle traced by the sun's own motion from west to east, divided into twelve equal signs of thirty degrees each {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 116}. Al-Farghani calls it "the circle of the middle of the zodiac" and "the second motion" {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 116}.

## The Signs and Their Names
The signs are named: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, and Pisces {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 116}. The entire sphere comprises 360 degrees, and each degree has sixty minutes {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 149}.

## Its Intersection with the Celestial Equator
The circle of the zodiac intersects the celestial equator at two opposite points, so six signs lie north of the celestial equator, from the beginning of Aries to the end of Virgo, and six lie south, from the beginning of Libra to the end of Pisces {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 149}. At the two points of intersection occur the two equinoxes {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 113}.

## The Sun's Path Along It
The sun traverses the sphere in its own motion from west to east, so its path follows this second motion {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 182}, completing the circuit in about 365 and a quarter days {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 182}. The planets move along the same sphere according to what results from their own motion {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 116}.
