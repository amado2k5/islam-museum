## Summary
Falah is success. Sha'rawi holds that the word "those who prosper" in [[passage/quran-2-5|Al-Baqara 5]] comes from ploughing the earth for sowing, and that God used it for the Hereafter to bring the unseen near through the seen. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.132} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.133}

## Upon guidance
Sha'rawi holds that "upon" (ala) indicates elevation: the one guided is raised by guidance and not humbled, and the method lifts its follower and protects him from harm. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.132} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.134}

## In error
Sha'rawi holds that "in" (fi) indicates an enclosing container: error surrounds its holder and rules him, and an enclosing fault leaves no outlet. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.133}
