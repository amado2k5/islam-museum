## The naming
Rabi' al-Awwal and Rabi' al-Akhir are the third and fourth months, named for the spring season in which they fell when the Arab month-names were fixed; they drift out of it as the lunar months advance. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}

## The birth in Rabi' al-Awwal
On the prevalent report the Prophet was born on a Monday in Rabi' al-Awwal in the year of the Elephant; the day is debated — the eighth, the second, and others were said. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## The hijra in it
The Prophet left Mecca on the hijra in Rabi' al-Awwal and reached Medina on Monday the twelfth, and from it the Muslims later opened their Hijri reckoning in the caliphate of 'Umar. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## Rabi' al-Akhir
Rabi' al-Akhir is the fourth month and follows Rabi' al-Awwal; no special worship is prescribed in it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
