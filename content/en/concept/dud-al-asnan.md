## What it is
For the tooth-worms he gives a pellet-making of seeds and a fat, one grain of which is fumed under a covered funnel-head (the names and measures left out; the fumigation carried as the book's description, not a procedure) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.278}
