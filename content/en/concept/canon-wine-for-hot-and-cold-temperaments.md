## Summary
Ibn Sina sets out which kind of wine suits which temperament: the thin white for the hot-tempered, the thick sweet for one who wants fat and strength, with care over its blocking, and the old red for the cold, phlegmatic temperament {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## The thin white
As for wine, the thin white kind suits those of hot temperament better; it does not cause headache and may even moisten, easing the headache that arises from inflammation of the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## The thick sweet and the old red
The thick sweet wine suits one who wants to put on flesh and strength, with watchfulness over its tendency to block, and the old red suits the person of cold, phlegmatic temperament {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.
