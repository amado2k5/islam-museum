## Summary
In the lexicon, asb is the mating of a stallion, that is its covering of a female; it may also mean its semen or the fee for mating {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11600}.

## Sale of a stallion's service
The jurists agree that selling a stallion's service is not permitted, because of the prophetic prohibition, and they explain that the service does not yet exist when the contract is made {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11601}.

## Hiring a stallion for mating
The majority of Hanafis, the sounder view among the Shafi'is and the basic Hanbali position forbid it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11601}. The Malikis allow it for a fixed time or a fixed number of times, but not until the female conceives; the Hanbalis allow it in cases of need when no one provides the service free {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11602}.

## Where to look further
