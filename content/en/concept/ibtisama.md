## Overview
In Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons the smile is a doorway to good dealing: he urges a person to begin a meeting with a smile, a greeting of peace and a kind enquiry {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:33:00}. He relates that the Prophet smiled at a man who came in while he was angry {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:34:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)

The Sheikh urges beginning with a smile, a greeting of peace and a kind enquiry after the other person's state, since souls are disposed by nature to love goodness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:33:00}. He relates an occasion when the Prophet was angry: Abu Bakr was gentle, Umar said the man should be let in, and when the Prophet saw him he smiled despite his state of mind {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:34:00}. The Sheikh holds that if a person meets an enemy and smiles at him, the enemy knows that he is strong {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:50:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh holds that a person loses nothing by smiling, recites humorous lines by a 'not optimistic' poet whose wife is alarmed to see him smile and is told he was exercising his mouth, and says he has travelled and found many Muslims who seem to treat their teeth as something to hide and never smile; he wants the smile to be in the heart before the lips, not a yellow smile over a heart full of rancour, and recites a line about smiling even when the sky is gloomy; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep21 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep21 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep21 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep21 00:10:00}
