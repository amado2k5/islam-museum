## The motion and the tables
The text says the orbit of the fixed stars lies on the poles of the ecliptic and has not moved since its motion became known, that their latitude is constant, and that the distances between them are fixed. They move together round the sphere from west to east, which is known by comparing two observations: they advance one degree in every sixty-six solar years. Al-Battani drew up a table of their motions from which the values for the years are taken. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.191-192}

## The question of motion in the stars
The text raises whether the stars move the sphere or are moved by it, and states that one body cannot move in two different directions at once. This prepares the discussion of the claims of the talisman-makers that follows. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.191-192}
