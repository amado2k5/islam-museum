## Overview
Haya is the disposition that restrains from the disgraceful; Bukhari gives it a chapter 'haya is part of faith' {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## In the sources
Bukhari's chapter collects the hadiths on haya, and the encyclopedia treats the rulings connected to it and what is taken out of modesty {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 7)

The Sheikh distinguishes between a poet who served Islam and one who did not, and says the gap between them is wide {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:23:00}. He recalls Umm Sulaym's words to Abu Talha as an example of modesty, and says he wants women to keep their modesty and the new generation to grow up with it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:23:00}.

The Sheikh says that modesty shows in how people deal with one another, that a woman's voice is not awra, and that when the imam errs in prayer the men say Subhan Allah while the women clap {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:23:30}.

He regards the clapping in this case as a door of modesty rather than a way of erasing the woman, so that women keep their modesty by it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:24:00}. He relates that last year an imam in one mosque made a mistake in his recitation (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:24:00}.

The Sheikh says that modesty is not merely a matter of the face or of lowering the eyes to the ground, which is one of its forms, and that its forms are many {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:26:00}. He gives the example of a wife whose husband's family visit her: she receives them well and honours his mother, who bore him and lived with him for many years, and he counts this as her modesty towards her husband {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:26:00}.

He counts it as modesty when a woman holds her tongue towards her husband's father, brother and sister {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:26:30}. The Sheikh asks whether religion is only hijab, niqab, a beard and a short robe, and says these are the last page in the book of reform {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:26:30}.

He says that every religion has a defining character, and that the character of Islam is modesty {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:27:00}. He holds that the worst thing we have is resisting information, and he criticises those who cling to the view of a single scholar and accept no other, like villagers who follow only their own imam {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:27:00}.
