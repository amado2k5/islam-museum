## Summary
In the lexicon, afal is a fleshy growth in a woman's genital passage that arises after childbirth and not in virgins {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11673}.

## Distinction from ratq
Some jurists distinguish afal, which comes after childbirth, from ratq, a closure present from creation; both are defects that give an option to end a marriage {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11673} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11674}.

## The option of annulment
The Malikis and Hanbalis hold that afal gives the husband an option to annul the marriage; Abu Hanifa and Abu Yusuf hold that neither spouse has such an option {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11673} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11674}. The Shafi'is count ratq and qaran as defects peculiar to women that give the option {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11674}.
