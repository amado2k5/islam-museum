## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy says that the angel and the devil each have an inspiration, a lamma, that comes to the heart. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:12:00} He describes the angel's inspiration as a promise of good that affirms the truth. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:12:00}

## The lamma of the angel and of the devil
The Sheikh says that the angel has an inspiration and the devil has an inspiration, and that each gets a turn. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:12:00}

## The angel's inspiration
The Sheikh says that the angel's inspiration promises good and affirms the truth. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:12:00}

## The devil's inspiration
The Sheikh mentions the devil's inspiration in contrast, but the passage that describes it is garbled in the transcript, so its content is not stated here (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:12:00}

## A sign of right guidance: word and deed
The Sheikh says that whoever's word agrees with his deed has hit the mark (needs_source: the statement is partly garbled in the transcript, and the reverse case is not clear). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:13:00}
