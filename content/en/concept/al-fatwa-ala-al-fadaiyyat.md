# Fatwa on the satellite channels — its benefit and harm

## The episode and the question
The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode debated the satellite-fatwa phenomenon — the fatwa no longer private to its questioner but heard by millions everywhere, so scholars differed and complaints multiplied {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 00:02:00}.

## Why people turn to it
The youth voted on the reasons: ease of access first, 35% because it reaches a more-learned person than those around them, and 20% because it gives less-strict answers {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 00:09:00}.

## Whom to ask
On the reference: 43% chose their country's scholars, 42% the fiqh bodies and academies, and only 15% 'the most-learned on the channels' — a surprise the host noted {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 00:32:30}.

## The solution — regulation or collective fatwa only
On solving the satellite-fatwa problems, 39% voted one option and 31% for restricting channels to transmitting collective fatwas and abandoning individual ijtihad on air {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep13 01:08:30}.
