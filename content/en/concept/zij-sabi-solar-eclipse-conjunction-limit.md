## Conjunction and the eclipse limits
The chapter on the solar eclipse begins by examining the mean latitude motion at conjunction. If it lies between the solar eclipse limits printed at the top of the conjunction and full-moon pages, an eclipse is possible; if it exceeds or falls short of them, no eclipse occurs in any clime. The reckoner then asks whether the conjunction is in daytime or near sunrise or sunset, to know whether all or part is seen. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.161}

## Keeping to the limits
The text says that where the eclipse is uncertain the limits are used to decide, and that doubt lies between two values. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.161}
