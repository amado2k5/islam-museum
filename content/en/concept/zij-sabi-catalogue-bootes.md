## The Guardian of the North

The catalogue calls this figure the Guardian of the North, and the text gives its name as al-Baqqara. Its stars come directly after those of Cepheus, and their positions in longitude and latitude stand in a table that is not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.247}
