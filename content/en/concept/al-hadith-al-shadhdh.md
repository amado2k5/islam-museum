## Overview
When an acceptable narrator is contradicted by someone stronger, through greater precision, greater numbers or some other ground of preference, the stronger report is called the preserved (*mahfuz*) and the weaker one the irregular (*shadhdh*): so [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]] sets it out in [[work/nuzhat-al-nazar|*Nuzhat al-Nazar*]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 66}

## The example
Ibn Hajar's example is the report given by al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Maja through [[person/sufyan-ibn-uyayna|Sufyan ibn 'Uyayna]], from 'Amr ibn Dinar, from 'Awsaja, from [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn 'Abbas]], that a man died in the Prophet's time leaving no heir but a freedman he had set free. Ibn Jurayj and others corroborated Ibn 'Uyayna's connected chain, but Hammad ibn Zayd transmitted it from 'Amr ibn Dinar from 'Awsaja without mentioning Ibn 'Abbas. Abu Hatim said: the preserved version is Ibn 'Uyayna's. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 66-67}

Ibn Hajar comments that Hammad ibn Zayd was upright and precise, yet Abu Hatim preferred the version of those who outnumbered him. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 67}

## The accepted definition
From this Ibn Hajar concludes that the irregular report is what an acceptable narrator transmits in contradiction of someone more worthy, and calls this the accepted technical definition. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 67}

## Irregular and objectionable
Between the irregular and the [[concept/al-hadith-al-munkar|objectionable (*munkar*)]] report Ibn Hajar sees a partial overlap: both require contradiction, but the irregular is the report of a trustworthy or truthful narrator and the objectionable that of a weak one; whoever treats them as the same, he says, has overlooked this. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 68}

## Its link to the conditions of the sound report
Freedom from irregularity is part of the definition of the [[concept/al-hadith-al-sahih|sound]] and the good report, which is why Ibn Hajar rejects the unqualified acceptance of [[concept/ziyadat-al-thiqa|a trustworthy narrator's additions]]. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 64}
