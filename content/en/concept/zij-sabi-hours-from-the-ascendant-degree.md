## Summary
To find the hours from a given ascendant, look at it: if it lies between the Sun's degree and its opposite going in the order of the signs, the time is day; if it lies between the opposite and the Sun's degree, the time is night. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.111}.

## Working out the hours
If it is day, the risings of the Sun's degree are subtracted from the risings of the ascendant's degree in the clime; what remains is the arc turned since sunrise, which is divided by the daytime hour-lengths. If it is night, the risings of the Sun's opposite are subtracted and the result divided by the night hour-lengths. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.111}.
