## Nature and selection
Ibrisam is silk, counted among the heart-exhilarating drugs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. The raw kind is best; the boiled kind may be used if it is not dyed, and the degummed kind is preferred to the burnt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the first degree and dry in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. It thins, dries and exhilarates by a property of its own {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Its dryness is tempered by the balance of its temperament; it is among the medicines that strengthen the spirit and the stomach for digesting food, and no weight is given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.

## Uses by organ
Food organs: the book says it helps hardness of the lung, through its bitterness and tanning, thinning and drying without burning, and its moderate dryness; no particular kind is singled out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Eyes: made into a kohl, it helps, prevents tearing and dries the sores of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.
