## What shields the eye
Whoever cares for the eye's health must shield it from dust, smoke, air beyond the mean in heat and cold, and blowing, cold and pestilential winds; not prolong the fixed stare at one thing; wean off much weeping; lessen looking at fine work save sometimes as exercise; not prolong sleeping on the back; all thick foods and drinks, all things vaporous to the head, all that dries excessively and all that breeds much vapour harm it; both much sleep and much waking hurt the eye greatly, the moderate of each being the fitting; and what clears and sharpens the eye is diving into clear water and opening the eye in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.166}

## What harms sight: acts and foods
The things harming sight are of three kinds — acts and motions, foods, and the handling of foods; among the acts: all that dries, long gazing at bright things, and reading fine print excessively — moderate reading being useful — likewise fine work and sleeping on repletion and on a late meal; one weak in sight should wait until digestion, then sleep; every repletion harms him, all that clouds the blood — salty, acrid and the like — harms him, and drunkenness harms him; vomiting helps by cleansing the stomach but harms by moving the brain's matters toward it — if unavoidable it should be after food and gentle; the bath is harmful, excessive sleep, much weeping, much venesection and especially repeated cupping; among foods: the salty, acrid, vaporous and what hurts the stomach's mouth ⁦(the listed food names are not carried)⁩; and mishandled food is that taken so its digestion spoils and its vapour multiplies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.166} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.167}

## Coitus ⁦(section held for the founder's ruling)⁩
The book counts much coitus among the most harmful things for the eye and among the acts that dry and harm the sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.166} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.166}
