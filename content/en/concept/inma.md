## Definition
Inma is lexically the causing of an increase: the reflexive verb of nama, meaning to grow. Growth is an increase, either real, as by birth or trade, or assumed, as by property remaining in one's hand or that of an agent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3559}

## Related terms
- Tathmir and istithmar: investment, with the same sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3559}
- Trade: exchanging property for gain, and a means of growing it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3559}
- Iktisab: seeking livelihood, which is wider than inma. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3559}
- Ziyada: increase that may come from outside, whereas inma comes from the thing itself. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3560}
- Kanz (hoarding), ta'til (idleness), qinya (keeping for use) and iddikhar (saving) are their opposites. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3560}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3561}

## Its lawfulness
Growing property is lawful. The Qur'an allowed sale and trade (sura al-Baqara, 2:275), and the law allowed partnerships and mudaraba for this purpose; preserving property is one of its aims, and growing it is one of the means of preserving it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3561}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3562}

## Its ruling by intention
- Obligatory: to gain enough for oneself and dependants and to pay a debt. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3562}
- Recommended: beyond need, to help the poor and benefit kin. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3562}
- Permitted: beyond need, for comfort and display of ease. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3563}
- Disliked: beyond need, for boasting and rivalry, even if lawfully gained. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3563}

## For one with control but not ownership
A guardian, executor, supervisor of an endowment, agent, judge or ruler in public funds may grow the property by lawful authority, and the jurists cite hadith and reports of the Companions for this. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3563}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3564}

## For one with ownership but not control
A foolish person, a minor or an insane person is restricted in disposal to protect his property; a discerning minor may act with his guardian's leave. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3564}

## Growth by unlawful means
Growth by usury, gambling, or trade in wine is forbidden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3565}

## Game that goes out of sight
Inma in this sense means the game going out of sight after it is shot. It is attributed to Ibn Abbas, who said to eat what was killed outright and to leave what went out of sight; the jurists differ on it. It is treated in the entry on hunting. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3565}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3566}
