## The signs
The sign of hot swellings: constant fever, heaviness, throbbing, pain that reaches the root of the eye, and perhaps the eyes protrude with it, mixing of reason and quickness of pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.26} If the swelling is in the membranes the pain is stronger and the pulse leans to the sawlike kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.26}

The signs of phlegmatic swellings are forgetfulness, coma and much heaviness, puffiness and swelling, and a wavy pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.26}

The signs of black-bilious swellings are sleeplessness, obsession with a particular heaviness, and hardness of the pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.26}

He says he has left out here the signs of weakness and strength of the brain, the signs of the humour that prevails in it, and the signs of its own diseases and of those by sharing, relying on what he gave of this in the chapter on headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.26}
