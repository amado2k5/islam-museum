## Nature and selection
Mirdasanj is burnt lead, and it can be made from something other than lead; it is refined at length by boiling, burning and washing. The details of the burning and washing are not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Temperament
Galen said it leans to drying but is weak in warming and cooling; others hold that it leans to cold, and the washed kind is certainly cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Actions and properties
Astringent and drying, it cleanses a little with astringency and glueing, and thins the thick; its astringency and cleansing are slight; it is the base of ointments, gathering the drugs and breaking the excess of dissolving, corroding and astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.560}.

## Cosmetic uses
It sweetens the smell of the body and armpit, prevents chafing of the thigh, clears freckles, dark marks and dead blood, especially the washed kind, removes the marks of smallpox and stops sweat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.560}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It makes flesh grow in ulcers only incidentally, but Galen said it neither cleanses, nor dirties, nor grows, nor diminishes, being rather the base of ointments, and it helps chafing of the folds of the body and the thighs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.560}.

## Eyes
The washed white kind goes into kohls and clears the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.560}.

## Excretory organs
If drunk it stops urine; women in the author's country give it to children for diarrhoea and ulcers of the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.560}.

## Poisons and bites
It is deadly: it suppresses urine, bloats the belly and the ureters, whitens the tongue, chokes and tightens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.560}.
