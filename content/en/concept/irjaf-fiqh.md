## Irjaf (spreading alarm)
Irjaf in the lexicon is violent agitation; it is also used for dwelling on bad news and mentioning strife, because it causes agitation among people. In the usage of the jurists it is seeking strife and spreading lies and falsehood to cause distress. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2161}
Takhdhil is discouraging people from fighting and making them averse to going out, while irjaf is spreading agitation among the Muslims, so irjaf is wider than takhdhil. Ishaa'a is making public news that should be kept hidden, in order to shame people. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2161}
Irjaf is forbidden, and abandoning it is obligatory, because it harms the Muslims, and its doer deserves a discretionary penalty. God ordered His Prophet to deal with the spreaders of alarm in Medina, and when the Prophet learned that some hypocrites were discouraging people at the expedition to Tabuk, he sent men of his Companions to burn their houses over them, and they did. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2162} A commander may not take a spreader of alarm with him to fight, and if the commander himself is one of them it is not recommended to go out with him; a spreader who goes out with the army gets no share of the booty, nor a reward from it. The jurists treat these rulings in the book on jihad and in the division of booty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2162}
