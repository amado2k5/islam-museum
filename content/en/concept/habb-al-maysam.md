## Nature
A berry the size and colour of pepper, which breaks easily into a very white, fragrant kernel. The editor's note identifies it as habb al-ban. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Uses by organ
Organs of digestion: it is good for a cold and slack stomach, it is said. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
