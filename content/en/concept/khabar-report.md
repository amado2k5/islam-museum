## Summary
Khabar (report) lexically is what is conveyed and spoken of; among the traditionists it usually means the same as hadith, athar is wider, and naba' is a report of great benefit. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7537} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7538} A report about a particular matter is confession, testimony or claim; a report about a general matter is narration, judgement or legal opinion. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7538} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7539} A trusted man's report that water, cloth or food is impure is accepted when he states the cause; an unbeliever, a sinner or a madman is not believed in impurity, and the discerning child is disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7539} The report of a trusted man about the qibla is accepted by the majority; the unbeliever, the mad and the non-discerning child are rejected, and the discerning child and the sinner are disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7540} On the sighting of the Ramadan crescent, the Shafiis, Hanbalis and a Hanafi report accept one just witness; the Hanafis require several witnesses when the sky is clear. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7540}
