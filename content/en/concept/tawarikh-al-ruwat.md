## Summary
Type sixty of [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]]'s al-Taqrib is "dates" — of narrators' births, of their hearing, of their arrival in a given city — and their "deaths"; he calls it an important art by which one knows whether a hadith is connected or broken. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 502} [[person/al-suyuti|Al-Suyuti]] explains it in [[work/tadrib-al-rawi|Tadrib al-Rawi]], giving examples of its use, its books, and then "branches" with choice dates. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 502} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 505}

## Dates as a weapon against liars
Al-Nawawi says: some people claimed to narrate from others; the dates were examined, and it turned out they claimed to have heard from them years after their death. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 502} One example: [[person/ismail-ibn-ayyash|Isma'il ibn Ayyash]] tested a man by asking in what year he had written from [[person/khalid-ibn-madan|Khalid ibn Ma'dan]]; the man said 113, and Isma'il replied: you claim to have heard from him seven years after he died — for on one view he died in 106, with other years also given. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 502} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 503} [[person/al-hakim-al-nisaburi|Al-Hakim]] asked Muhammad ibn Hatim al-Kashshi, who was narrating from [[person/abd-ibn-humayd-al-kissi|Abd ibn Humayd]], when he was born; he said 260, and al-Hakim said: this man heard from Abd thirteen years after his death. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 503} The editor Muhammad Awwama defends the reading *ikhtibaran* ("as a test") against *ikhtiyaran* found in some printings. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 502}

The judge [[person/hafs-ibn-ghiyath|Hafs ibn Ghiyath]] said: if you suspect a shaykh, reckon with him by the two ages — his age and the age of the one he wrote from. [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]] said: when the narrators made use of lying, we made use of dates against them. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 503} Hassan ibn Yazid said: we found no help against liars like dates; we say to the shaykh, in what year were you born? and once he admits his birth we know whether he is truthful or lying; the editor notes that Tarikh Baghdad and al-Jami' give the name as Hassan ibn Zayd. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 503}

## Books of death-dates
Abu Abd Allah [[person/muhammad-ibn-fattuh-al-humaydi|al-Humaydi]] said: three of the hadith sciences must be given priority — hidden defects, names that look alike but differ, and the death-dates of shaykhs — and there is no book on it. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 504} Al-Suyuti explains that he meant no exhaustive book, for there are books: the Wafayat of Ibn Zabr and of Ibn Qani', with supplements to Ibn Zabr by Abd al-Aziz ibn Ahmad al-Kattani, then Abu Muhammad al-Akfani, then Ibn al-Mufaddal, then [[person/abd-al-azim-al-mundhiri|al-Mundhiri]], then the sharif Izz al-Din al-Husayni, then Ahmad ibn Aybak al-Dimyati, then [[person/abd-al-rahim-al-iraqi|al-Iraqi]]. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 504} The editor supplies their death years and adds books the commentator missed, such as Khalifa ibn Khayyat's al-Tabaqat and Ya'qub ibn Sufyan's al-Ma'rifa wa-l-Tarikh. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 504}

## Choice dates
Al-Nawawi then lists selected branches. The first is the Prophet's age: the sound view is sixty-three, and the same for his two companions [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] and [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]; for the Prophet sixty, sixty-five and sixty-two were also said. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 505} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 506} On [[event/death-of-the-prophet|his death]] al-Nawawi says he was taken in the forenoon of Monday 12 Rabi' I, 11 AH; al-Suyuti says the sira scholars do not differ on this except over the day of the month: most say the twelfth, [[person/musa-ibn-uqba|Musa ibn Uqba]] and [[person/layth-ibn-sad|al-Layth ibn Sa'd]] say the first, and Sulayman al-Taymi the second. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 507} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 508} He reports from al-Iraqi that [[person/al-suhayli|al-Suhayli]] found the majority view hard to square with reckoning, since the Day of Arafa in the Farewell Pilgrimage was a Friday, so that 12 Rabi' I could not have been a Monday whatever the lengths of the months; al-Iraqi concluded that, as a matter of dating, the view of al-Taymi and those who agree with him is stronger. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 508} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 509} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 510}

Another is that dating begins from the Hijra: al-Suyuti cites al-Bukhari's report from Sahl ibn Sa'd that they counted neither from the mission nor from the Prophet's death but from his arrival in Medina, Ibn al-Musayyab's report that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] advised Umar to count from the day of the Hijra, Ibn Sirin's account of Umar's consultation on the starting point, and that [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] advised making Muharram the first month, all in the year 17. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 511} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 512} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 513} See [[event/umar-adopts-hijri-calendar|the adoption of the Hijri calendar]].

Then come the deaths and ages of the Ten promised Paradise, with the caution that some are disputed; then two Companions who lived sixty years in the Jahiliyya and sixty in Islam and died in Medina in 54: [[person/hakim-ibn-hizam|Hakim ibn Hizam]] and [[person/hassan-ibn-thabit|Hassan ibn Thabit]]. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 515} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 520} Then the founders of the followed schools: Sufyan al-Thawri, [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik]], [[person/abu-hanifa|Abu Hanifa]], [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] and [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]]; then the authors of the relied-upon books: [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]], [[person/muslim-ibn-al-hajjaj|Muslim]], Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and [[person/al-nasai|al-Nasa'i]]; and then seven hafizes "who wrote well and whose works brought great benefit": [[person/al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni]], al-Hakim, Abd al-Ghani ibn Sa'id, Abu Nu'aym, [[person/ibn-abd-al-barr|Ibn Abd al-Barr]], al-Bayhaqi and [[person/al-khatib-al-baghdadi-ahmad|al-Khatib al-Baghdadi]]. {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 524} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 526} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 528} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 536} {cite:tadrib-rawi-awwama-v5 p. 540}
