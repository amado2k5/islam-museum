## Nature and selection
This is the wild 'arfaj. It is one of the yatu'at, and its seed is hot like theirs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}. The editor's footnote says the same: wild 'arfaj.

## Temperament and actions
Its seed is hot like the yatu'at {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: loosens the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.
