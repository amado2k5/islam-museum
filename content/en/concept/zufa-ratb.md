## Nature and selection
Nature: grime that gathers on the wool of sheep tails in Armenia; it drips onto a kind of grass and takes its strength and its milky sap, and sometimes it is liquid, boiled and firmed there. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.456}
Temperament: hot in the second degree and moist in the first. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.456}

## Actions
Actions: it ripens and dissolves. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.456}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it dissolves hard swellings and the swelling called dashbad when the part is poulticed with it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.456}
Digestion organs: with fig and borax it is a poultice for the spleen; drunk, it helps the spleen and dropsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.456}
Excretory organs: it dissolves hardenings in the region of the bladder and the womb, and helps their coldness and the coldness of the kidneys. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.456}
