## Summary
The chapter's heading announces that these distances give the true distance of the Moon from the Sun according to the Moon's latitude, and al-Battani says at its end that this chapter is most needed in the work on nativities, that is on matters of birth; a word before that in the original is damaged in the scan. The chapter is theoretical in method and practical for two ends: the visible distance of the Moon from the Sun, and the casting of nativities. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.61-65}.

## The end of the chapter
The chapter closes with this note of purpose, and chapter 27 then begins on the length of the year and the Sun's motion in it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.65}.
