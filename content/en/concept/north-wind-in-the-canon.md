## Summary
In the tenth chapter, on the causes of winds, Ibn Sina returns to the winds in another order and begins with the north wind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.126}.

## What the north wind does
The north wind strengthens and tightens, checks outward discharges, closes the pores, strengthens digestion, binds the belly, promotes urine and corrects putrid epidemic air {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.126}.

## When it follows the south
When the south wind comes first and the north follows, the south causes flowing and the north squeezes inward, and this may lead to an opening outward; then flows from the head become frequent, with chest ailments, the northern diseases and pains of the nerve, of the bladder and womb, difficulty in urinating, cough, pains of the ribs, side and chest, and horripilation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.126}.
