## Nature and temperament
A plant that is the Roman inj'dan, according to the editor's note. Nature: its seed and root warm and dry in the third degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}

## Properties and digestion
It expels wind, opens and resolves. Organs of digestion: it ripens and aids digestion and dissolves wind, especially in the stomach, and strengthens it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): the book says that a dirham's weight of it purges worms (the weight is the book's, not advice), and that gourd seed and its kernel bring on the menses strongly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}

## Poisons
The book says it helps against every sting, as is said. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}
