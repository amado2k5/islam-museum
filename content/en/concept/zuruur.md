## Nature and selection
Nature: a thorny tree with leaves like those of a kind of pear-tree (luqurashi); its small fruit resembles the apple, but is smaller than an apple, with a pleasant red colour and three seeds in each fruit. Its tree resembles the apple tree even in its leaves, but smaller. Its root and fruit are round and edible, astringent in taste, with broad lower parts. The book says elsewhere that the colour of this tree's fruit is yellow. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Temperament: some say it is cold and moist. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.467}

## Actions
Actions: it is astringent, more astringent than ghubayra; it checks yellow bile and flows. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.467}

## Uses by organ
Head organs: it causes headache. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.467}
Digestion organs: it is bad for the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.467}
