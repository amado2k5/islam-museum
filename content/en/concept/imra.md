## Definition
Mar' is a person; its feminine is mar'a, which with the hamza of joining becomes imra'a, the name for an adult woman. In some chapters, such as inheritance, it can apply to a girl. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3385}

## Ruling
- A woman has a right to care in childhood, to upbringing and education, and a father must train his daughter well. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3385}
- Once she has judgement she has an independent financial standing and may express her own will, so she is not married without her consent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3385}
- A woman must keep her femininity, cover herself, and avoid being alone with strangers, so she stands behind the men's rows in prayer; she may not imitate men. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3385}
- She is bound by the religious duties, with some differences in forms of worship; because God gave her menstruation, pregnancy and childbirth, she is eased in some acts of worship for them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3385}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3386}
- Because of the frailty of her make-up, she in general does not hold the offices of amirate or judgeship, and jihad is not required of her; her testimony is half a man's. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3386}
- Custody of children goes first to the mother for her tenderness; her principal work is her home, husband and children, and her maintenance is due from her husband even if she is rich; the man is in charge of her. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3386}
