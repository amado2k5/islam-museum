## Overview
The hur al-'ayn in Islamic conception are among the promised blessings of paradise for the believers, mentioned in the chapters interpreting paradise's description {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
Ibn Kathir mentions the hur al-'ayn in the chapters on paradise's attributes {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; the Sharh al-Tahawiyya details paradise's bliss {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.
