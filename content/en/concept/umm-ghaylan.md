## Nature and selection
A known tree of the desert, among the thorny trees {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.362}. A footnote identifies it as Acacia arabica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.362}.

## Temperament and actions
Dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.

## Uses by organ
Astringent; it checks blood and kinds of flux {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Chest: it stops spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Excretory organs: it stops discharge from the uterus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.
