## Definition
Ramadan is the ninth month, named from al-ramad, burning heat, for it fell in high summer when the months were named. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The fast imposed
God imposed its fast in Sha'ban of the second hijri year in the verse: whoever of you witnesses the month let him fast it; the Prophet fasted nine Ramadans. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The Qur'an and the Night of Power
In Ramadan the Qur'an was sent down, and in it is the Night of Power, better than a thousand months, sought in the last ten nights on the prevalent report. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The gates of good
When Ramadan enters, the gates of paradise are opened, the gates of the fire shut and the devils chained; in it are the tarawih prayer, the retreat of the last ten days, and an 'umra equal to a hajj. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
