## Overview
Muhammad Farid Wajdi holds Islam, in its social and credal roots, no new religion but the religion God revealed to Noah and the prophets after him; the religions' differences in their roots go back to their followers' alteration of what descended {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 24}. He makes God's vicegerency on earth an open commission to every people that commands the right and forbids the wrong, not the preserve of one race {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 49}. This is the author's own view, attributed to him.
