## Nature
Galen said that the state of it is like the state of the crab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Temperament
Masarjawayh said that it is hot and moist in moderation before it is salted {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Actions and properties
When salted and aged it engenders black bile and a bad itch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Swellings and pustules
Galen said that it dissolves hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It nourishes well {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Excretory organs
It softens the belly and drives out the tapeworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it adds to semen and adds to desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}
