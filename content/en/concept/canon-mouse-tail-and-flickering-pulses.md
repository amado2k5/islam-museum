## Summary
Ibn Sina defines the mouse-tail pulse and the flickering pulse, two pulses that grade the unevenness of their parts through decrease and increase {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## The mouse-tail pulse
The mouse-tail pulse grades the unevenness of its parts from decrease to increase and from increase to decrease. It may run over many beats, or within one beat over many parts or in one part. Its most proper unevenness concerns size, though it may also be by slowness and speed, strength and weakness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## The flickering pulse
The flickering pulse starts from a low and rises to a limit in increase, then reverses in order until it reaches the first limit of decrease, so that it is like two mouse tails joined at the larger end {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.
