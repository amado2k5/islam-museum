## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the chord of the arc which is the difference of two arcs of known chord is known, that the chord of half of any arc of known chord is known, and that the chord of the arc composed of two known arcs is known, by rules that rest on multiplying chords by one another and by what remains to the semicircle, on division by the diameter and on roots; and he concludes that all the known chords of the semicircle come out by these ways. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.18}.

## The chord of a difference
For any two arcs of known chord, the chord of the arc between them in difference is also known: multiply the chord of each arc by the chord of what remains to the other up to the semicircle, take the difference of the two products, divide it by the diameter, and the result is the chord of that arc. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.18}.

## The chord of the half
For any arc of known chord, the chord of its half is also known: subtract the chord of what remains of the arc to the semicircle from the whole diameter, take half of what is left, multiply it by the whole diameter, take the root, and the result is the chord of half that arc. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.18}.

## The chord of the sum
If two arcs of known chord are laid on one another to become a single arc, the chord of the composite arc is known: multiply the chord of each by the chord of what remains to the other, take the difference, divide it by the whole diameter, and you have the chord of what remains of the composite arc to the semicircle; squared and subtracted from the square of the diameter, its root is the chord of the composite arc. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.18}.
