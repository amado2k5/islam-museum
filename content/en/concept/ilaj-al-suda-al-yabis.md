## Dry headache without matter
As for the dry headache that comes with a bilious or bloody matter, the discussion of it has passed; what remains is dry headache without matter, whose first treatment is the management of the chyme, especially foods rich in nourishment, and then it is inclined, as to hot and cold, to what suits better {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.61} Moistening means are of use, and if the temperament needs tempering by cooling or warming, it is tempered {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.61-62} Dryness may bring a clear decrease in the substance of the brain and make it ready for pains, and there the treaters must use what nourishes and moistens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.62}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.61-62}
