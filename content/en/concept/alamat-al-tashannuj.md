## Pulse and vessel
The convulsed man's pulse is elongated, varying in place, rising and falling like arrows turned from an archer's bow, the motions of its beats differing in speed and slowness; the vessel is hot, warmer than the other organs, and the vessel's bulk gathered as the vessel gathers in the shivering fit — not as the compressed, nor as in the vessel's hardness from long disease or with pain of the bowels, but like the gathering of the parts of a bowel stretched from its two ends {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.145}

## Signs by the qualities
The convulsion from repletion is signed by coming suddenly and not quickly drinking up the oil put on it, unless heat strikes it; that from dryness comes little by little after evacuative diseases of any kind, or an evacuation by medicines, or weakening and self-evacuation; that from injury is known by the outward cause and the drinks — as when the hurt is from the stomach, the brain shares with it then the nerve, the man feeling before it faintness, distress and wringing of the stomach, and that convulsion may follow a bilious or verdigris vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.145}

## Signs of onset
Among the signs pointing to convulsion's onset are smallness and unevenness of the pulse first, then its passing into what was said; often the face reddens, squint and turning show in the eyes, breath is cut and fails, baseless laughter may occur, nature is bound and dries; the urine is often retained and often not, coming out like blood-water with bubbles; hiccup, sleeplessness, headache and tremor befall them; in the fevers a twist of the eye, redness in the limb, squinting, grinding of teeth and blackness of the tongue warn of it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.146}

## Signs of the moist and of fever
It is told that the convulsion occurring for fever, and in the fevers it is warned of by the eye's twist, redness in the limb, squint, grinding of teeth, black tongue, stretching of the scalp and throbbing of the temples and head vessels; among the signs of the moist convulsion is much wind in the organs, especially if the belly swells with it, especially at its start; hot urine in convulsion and in extension is bad, showing the cause is heat; throbbing shows one of two things: either a swelling in the bowels magnifying the throbbing, or thinness in them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.146}
