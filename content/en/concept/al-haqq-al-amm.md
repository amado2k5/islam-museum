## Overview
Public right, according to Zaydan Qa'dat, is the right that serves the cause of faith, or the divine right realized through the just distribution of production, since what belongs to God reaches people, and God receives from it only obedience and piety {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 102}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 103}.

## The Four Beneficiaries
The author holds that the public right pertains to four beneficiaries: the individual, the community, the homeland, and religion {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 103}. The individual's share is that the right reaches those entitled to it among the poor, the needy, and the wronged, for which a treasury for Muslims, such as the public treasury (bayt al-mal), may be designated {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 103}.

## The Community and Public Interests
The community's share comprises public interests that raise the standard of society, such as building schools, hospitals, utilities, roads, and transport, as well as a social security institution that protects the vulnerable, including the elderly and orphans {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 103}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 104}.

## The Homeland
The author places the homeland's right in production and its preservation, viewing it as the condition for the survival of wealth, for if the homeland weakens, forces of evil will divide up its resources and raw materials {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 104}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 105}. He includes among its rights preparing the military force with which it is defended, citing God's words in [[passage/quran-8-60|al-Anfal 60]] {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 104}.

## Religion
He counts among the rights of religion building mosques that serve as schools of religious thought, establishing cultural centers, and printing books and pamphlets that reveal Islamic truths, including economic ones {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 105}. This is the author's own conception, and the book does not present a comparative analysis of it.
