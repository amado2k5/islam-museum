## Summary
Ibn Sina says the body of one far advanced in years does not take of food what it took in youth, so his food turns into residue and he should eat less than his habit; and one used to a heavy regimen who lightens it lets air into passages the heavy food used to fill, so that when he returns to heavy food blockages arise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## Old age
One far advanced in age does not receive from food what his body received when young, so his food becomes residue; he should not eat the amount of his habit but less {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## A changed regimen
For one used to a coarse regimen who lightens it, air enters the passages that the coarse regimen used to fill and that the light regimen does not fill now, so when he goes back to coarse food blockages arise in him {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.
