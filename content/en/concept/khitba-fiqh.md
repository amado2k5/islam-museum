## Summary
Khitba, with a short vowel on the kha, is the verb khataba, to propose marriage to a woman; the marriage contract gives ownership of enjoyment by intent between a man and a woman free of legal bar; the proposal comes before it and does not carry its effects. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7714} The proposal is usually a means to marriage and not a condition of it; the majority permit it, and the Shafiis prefer it, following the Prophet\'s proposals to Aisha and Hafsa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7714} Proposing to an unmarried woman with no waiting period is lawful, directly or by hint, by agreement; proposing to a married woman is forbidden; proposing to one barred by an impediment, such as a Magian woman until she accepts Islam, is not lawful. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7714}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7715} A proposal to a woman in waiting varies by her state: a direct proposal to another man\'s waiting woman is forbidden by agreement, on the Quranic verse that distinguishes hint from express offer; a hint is what admits of the desire to marry or of something else. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7715}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7716} A hint to a woman in a revocable waiting period is forbidden by agreement; a hint to a widow is permitted; a hint to a woman divorced irrevocably is permitted by the Malikis, the Shafiis in the preferred view and the Hanbalis, while the Hanafis and a Shafii view forbid it if it causes enmity with the former husband; a hint to a woman in the waiting period of an invalid marriage or a dissolution is permitted by most. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7716}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7717}
