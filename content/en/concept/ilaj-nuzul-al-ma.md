## Regimen at its onset
If the water is caught early, regimen benefits it; once established there is only the couching. Its man must abandon repletion, drink and the bath, keep to one midday meal, and avoid fish, fruits and coarse meats above all ⁦(the coitus line is a labelled listing set aside for the founder's ruling)⁩. Vomiting, though it benefits by cleansing the stomach, harms in this disease's particular; the rule of its drug treatment is known from the khayalat chapter. He records tried things and grants that learned men tried viper's bile and it did not act as a poison at all ⁦(the tried remedies, compounds and their measures are not carried; the trial is transmitted as the book's report)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.214}
## The conditions of the couching
For the couching, cleansing of body and head must come first, with venesection if needed; then it must be seen that the couched is not split-headed — lest a swelling arise in the coats — nor cough-ridden, nor easily vexed or quick to anger, for vexation and anger stir relapse; drink and the bath must be abandoned. Even so the couching is only used after the water stands still, lets down what it will, and thickens a little — this is called its completion. Venesection harms it; the patient's food at that time is chickpea-water to hold the place to which it is moved ⁦(the venesection sites are not carried)⁩. When couching is intended, the patient is first fed on fresh fish and moist water-weighing foods and something strengthening the water's harm, then couched {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.214}
## The manner of the couching as he describes it
In sum, water too thin or too thick does not obey the couching. When couching, the patient is fixed looking toward the inner canthus and the nose, held in that posture in a place not facing a window nor in very bright light; then the needle pierces between the two coats until it faces the aperture, finding a kind of space and cavity. Some practitioners withdraw the needle and insert the needle-tail — the aqilid — up to the aperture to clear the way for its sharp end and to habituate the patient to patience; then the needle is brought in to the marked limit, raised against the water and kept pressing it down until the eye clears and the water is pressed behind the cornea from below; the needle is held in place a good while so the water stays there, then lifted and watched for return — if it returns the procedure is repeated; if the water will not follow the line of its inclination it is pushed toward where it tends and dispersed; if it returns on the days the eye is treated, the needle is returned to that very hole. If blood flows into the aperture it must likewise be pressed and not left to clot, for then it has no cure ⁦(all of this is the book's description of what practitioners do, carried as description, not as a procedure)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.215}
## After the couching
After couching, the couched eye is dressed with a cotton dressing, and the sound eye is also bound lest it move and help the sick one; the patient sleeps on his back three days in darkness, and repeated dressings, posture-keeping and a week's lying down may be needed if there is swelling or headache. Best is that the patient keep his posture until the pain goes; the bandage is loosed only every three days, the drug renewed, and fomenting at the loosening with the known waters is permitted ⁦(drug and fomentation names are not carried)⁩. People have their ways in couching, some piercing below the cornea and drawing the water out there — this is dangerous, for if the water is thickened the vitreous humour comes out with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.215}
