## Summary
Tensive pain is caused by wind or a humour that stretches a nerve; pressing pain by matter that narrows the organ's place or wind that surrounds it, so that it seems gripped {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## Tensive
The cause of tensive pain is wind, or a humour that stretches the nerve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## Pressing
The cause of pressing pain is matter that narrows the space around the organ, or wind that encloses it, so that it is as if gripped and so pressed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.
