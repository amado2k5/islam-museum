## Overview
Ahmad 'Umar Hashim holds love of homeland among the signs of faith, and that jihad was legislated as defense of creed and homeland and repelling of wrong {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 121}. Protecting the homeland goes beyond repelling invasion: it includes resisting corrupt thought and malicious rumor, and guarding the homeland's secrets from its enemies {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 122}. Homelands fall in three: the private — one's town and state; the general — the Arab and Islamic worlds; and the broadest — all humanity {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 122}.
