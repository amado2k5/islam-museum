## The slowness of the fixed stars
The wandering planets do not join the fixed stars in aspect because the fixed stars move so slowly, and aspects are not counted from the rays of a star falling on the ecliptic when the star lies at the same distance from it. The forms are looked at instead at the angles and in proximity, especially with the Sun. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.200}

## The reckoning by proximity
The wandering planets require knowledge of their distances from one another and of the measures of their rays on the ecliptic, according to their latitudes at births and their changes. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.200}
