## Summary
For [[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]] in the programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']], love of the Prophet means that the Prophet be dearer to a Muslim than father, mother, child, spouse and self, and its practical sign is following him. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:09:00} He says love has degrees and urges viewers to compete in its measure rather than be content with having it. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:03:30}

## God's love for the Prophet, and the Prophet's love for his Lord
He begins with God's love for His Prophet, citing the oath by his life and by his town, God's blessing on whoever blesses him, and the rule that a convert's Islam is not valid until he testifies to the messengerhood. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:07:30} Then the Prophet's love for his Lord: he found rest in prayer and prayed whenever something troubled him, prolonged his prostration when praying alone, and stood at night until his feet swelled, in thanks. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:02:00}

## The Companions' love
He relates from [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] that the Prophet was dearer to them than fathers, mothers, children, wealth and cold water to the thirsty, and tells how [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] came to say the Prophet was dearer to him than himself. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:04:00} He tells of the Ansari woman who asked after the Prophet before her own family, the answer of [[person/khubayb-ibn-adi|Khubayb]] as a captive, [[person/abu-qatada-al-ansari|Abu Qatada]]'s night spent propping the Prophet on his mount, and the Companions' reverence for him that [[person/urwa-ibn-masud|Urwa ibn Mas'ud]] described at al-Hudaybiyya. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:06:30} Khubayb's answer and Urwa's description are also related in Sahih al-Bukhari, as their records show. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} The highest degree of love, for him, is what he relates of [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]]: his wish that Abu Talib's hand had been in the Prophet's instead of his own father's, so that the Prophet's heart would rejoice. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:09:00}

## The love of animals and inanimate things
He then speaks of 'love of a strange kind': the camel that prostrated to the Prophet, the two trees that came to him, the palm trunk that yearned for him, and the mountain of which the Prophet said it loves us and we love it; the detail is in [[concept/mujiza|miracle]]. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep14 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep15 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep16 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep17 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep17 00:08:30}

## Love in practice
He says love is not enough as words from someone who neglects prayer, wrongs his parents, harms his neighbour and eats his siblings' inheritance; love means putting the Prophet's command, deed and word first. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:09:00} The detail is in [[concept/ittiba|following]]. This is the speaker's presentation as recorded, attributed to him.
