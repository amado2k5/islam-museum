## Nature and selection
A herb whose leaf resembles wheat leaf but is softer; its fruit has two or three husks with a hair-like down; its juice is better than the herb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}. The footnote gives the name only.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}. Actions: drying and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings: it softens swellings that have begun to harden, and prevents the hardness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.
Eyes: it helps the condition the book calls al-gharb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.
