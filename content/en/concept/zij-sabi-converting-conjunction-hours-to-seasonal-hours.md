## Summary
Al-Battani turns the equated conjunction hours into seasonal hours by dividing them by the lengths of the day or night hours; the count of seasonal hours from sunrise or sunset comes out, and the remainder is a fraction of an hour. The result is then moved to another place by the difference in longitude divided by fifteen. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.143}.

## Before or after midnight
If the conjunction falls before midnight in seasonal hours it is before midnight, and if after it is after; the amount it precedes or follows midnight is counted to find the exact time at night. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.144}.
