## Nature
Dioscorides: some people call qamar quraysh fantundas; it is the fruit of tannub, which is in pods, and the pods are sometimes called sanawbar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.661}

## Actions and properties
Its power is astringent and heats mildly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.661}

## Organs of the chest
Used alone or with honey it benefits cough and pain of the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.661}

## Close of the letter
Here ends the speech on the letter qaf, and the total of the drugs mentioned in this chapter is fifty-two by count {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.661}
