## Summary
Of the jinn's harm to man: a touch of possession striking him by God's leave — the Qur'an says of the usurer that he rises only as one whom Satan throws down by the touch; the Prophet warded off the jinn's eye and did ruqya for his Companions {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
