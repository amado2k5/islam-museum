## Summary
Ibn Sina presents the physicians' disagreement over the double-beat pulse, whether it is one beat or two, and sets a rule for counting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## The disagreement
The physicians differ over the double-beat pulse: some make it one beat uneven in earliness and lateness, others say it is two beats following close on each other. In sum the time between them is not wide enough to hold a contraction and then an expansion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## The rule for counting
Not everything in which two knocks are felt must be two beats, otherwise the returning pulse with a broken expansion would be two beats. It must be counted as two beats only if it began and expanded, then returned to the depth contracting, then became expanded once more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.
