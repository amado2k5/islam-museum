## Summary
The ninth genus is the body's expulsion of waste and the quality of what it expels, the tenth the state of the soul's powers in their acts and passions, to which dreams are added; Ibn Sina notes that all or most of this concerns temperaments belonging to the original constitution {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.162-163}.

## Expelling waste
If expulsion continues and what comes out in stool, urine, sweat and the rest is hot, strongly smelling and coloured, it shows heat; the opposite shows cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.162}.

## The soul's powers
Strong anger, impatience, sharpness, understanding, daring, boldness, good opinion of others, good hope, hardness, briskness, manly character, little sloth and little passivity show heat, and their opposites show cold. Steadiness of anger and contentment, of imagination and of memory, shows dryness, and passions that quickly pass show moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.162}.

## Dreams
Dreams are of the same kind. One whose temperament is dominated by heat dreams of warming himself at fires or sitting in the sun; one dominated by cold dreams of snow or of being plunged in cold water; and, it is said, the one of each humour dreams of what matches his humour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.162-163}.

## Original constitution
Ibn Sina says all or most of this belongs to the signs of temperaments in the original constitution {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.
