## Nature
Dioscorides said that it is a plant with long, notched, rough, thorny leaves and a stem without a thorn bearing round heads like large olive stones, with a flower resembling saffron and a white bloom, some of it inclining to red, and its flower is sometimes used in food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Actions and properties
It has a moderate astringency together with ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Cosmetics
It cleanses freckles and vitiligo {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is put with vinegar on ringworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Organs of the head
Wild safflower, if a liniment of it is made with honey, benefits thrush of children {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}
