## Overview
al-Hakam — The Judge. It is name number 28 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-12-40|12:40]]، [[passage/quran-12-67|12:67]]، [[passage/quran-19-12|19:12]]، [[passage/quran-28-70|28:70]]، [[passage/quran-28-88|28:88]]، [[passage/quran-6-57|6:57]]، [[passage/quran-6-62|6:62]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
