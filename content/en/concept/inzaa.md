## Definition
Inzaa' is causing an animal to mount another for breeding; the word is used of sheep, beasts and cows. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3469}
Asb al-fahl (the stallion's fee) is the hire paid for covering, or the covering itself, or its seed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3469}

## Ruling
- Covering that does no harm, such as a horse with a mare or a donkey, is lawful. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3469}
- Covering mares by donkeys is disliked by some jurists, following the hadith of Ali that "only those who do not know do that"; the reason is that it weakens and reduces the horses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3469}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3470}
- The Hanafis allow donkeys to cover mares and the reverse. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3470}
- Covering a pledged animal is refused by the Shafi'is if the animal is expected to give birth before the debt falls due. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3470}
