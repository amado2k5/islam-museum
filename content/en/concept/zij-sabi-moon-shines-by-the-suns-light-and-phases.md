## Summary
Al-Battani explains that the Moon receives light from the Sun, and that the half facing the Sun is the lit half. When the half facing the Earth is the lit one the Moon appears full in the middle of the month; when the lit half faces away from the Earth it is new. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.92}.

## The diagram
He draws a circle for the Moon's sphere with the Sun's circle beside it, places the Moon at each distance from the Sun, and shows how the lit piece grows and shrinks with that distance, first on a flat circle and then on a sphere. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.92}.
