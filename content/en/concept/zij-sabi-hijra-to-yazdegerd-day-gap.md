## Summary
Al-Battani takes the gap between the Hijra and the death of Yazdegerd as 3,624 days, and subtracts this number from the Hijri day count to reach the Persian date from a Hijri one. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.103}.

## Its place in the reckoning
He uses this gap to convert a Hijri date into a Persian one: he takes the Hijra's day count, subtracts this interval, then counts the days and months of the Persian year. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.103}.
