## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the Moon's apparent diameter varies with its distance from the Earth. At its farthest it is about twenty-nine and a half minutes, and its apparent speed per hour changes with it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.87}.

## Diameter and speed together
He ties the apparent diameter to the speed and uses both to reckon how far an eclipse reaches at each place; Ptolemy used a fixed lunar diameter, which al-Battani's observations contradict. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.87}.
