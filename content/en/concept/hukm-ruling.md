## Summary
Hukm means judgement, lexically a prevention. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7232} For the majority of usulis the legal ruling is the address of the lawgiver concerning the acts of the obliged, by demand, choice or stipulation; for the jurists it is the effect of that address, such as obligation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7232} The majority count five kinds of legal ruling (obligatory, recommended, permitted, forbidden, disliked); the Hanafis add two (wajib, and disliked-as-forbidden). The majority equate fard and wajib, the Hanafis separate them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7233} The ruling by stipulation includes cause, condition, impediment, dispensation or strictness, and validity or nullity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7233} The entry refers the reader to the separate headwords and to the usuli appendix for the details. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7233}
