## Definition
Iyas is lexically the cutting off of hope and desire, and the jurists use it in two senses: the end of menstruation through age, and the cutting off of hope in a thing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3692}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3693}

## Related terms
- Qu'ud: the end of a woman's menstruation and childbearing; a woman in this state is called qa'ida, pl. qawa'id. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3693}
- 'Aqr and 'aqm: barrenness; a barren woman may still menstruate, which distinguishes her from a woman past menstruation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3693}

## Age of iyas
- The age is disputed: fifty, or fifty-five for the Hanafis, sixty, the age of the women of her family in one Shafi'i view, and a distinction between Arab and non-Arab women in some reports. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3694}
- The Malikis and Hanbalis have a least and a greatest age: fifty at least, seventy at most for the Malikis and sixty for Ahmad, with what lies between treated as doubtful. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3694}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3695}
- Blood after the age is not menstruation in the prevailing Hanafi view; a cessation caused by illness or suckling does not count as iyas until the age is reached. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3694}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3696}
- The Hanafis require a long cessation, six months in the sounder view, before iyas is declared. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3695}

## Rules for a woman past menstruation
- Her waiting period after divorce is three months, on the Qur'anic text in sura al-Talaq (65:4). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3695}
- The sunna form of divorce for her, for the Hanafis, is one pronouncement at the start of each month. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3695}
- She is licensed to put off her outer garment without display, on sura al-Nur (24:60). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3696}

## Despair of God's mercy and repentance
- Despair of provision, of forgiveness or of God's mercy is forbidden; the scholars count it among the grave sins, on sura Yusuf (12:87) and sura al-Hijr (15:56). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3696}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3697}
- The repentance of one who despairs of life, like a dying person, is refused by the majority, while some Hanafis distinguish his repentance from his faith. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3697}
