## Nature
The book attributes to Stephanus the claim that this creature lives in olive trees and is close to the locust. It sings most of the time with a creaking sound. The people of Syria call it al-zayz, the people of Tabaristan give it a name meaning grape singer, and the people of Khurasan call it jathrad. The editor's note identifies it as the cicada. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}

## Excretory organs
Roasted on a flat pan and eaten, it helps pains of the bladder, according to the book. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}
