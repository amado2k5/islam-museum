## Summary
Obedience (taa) is compliance with the command of God and His Messenger, and its plural is acts of obedience (taat); its counterpart in the texts is [[concept/masiya|disobedience (masiya)]]. Under this section the browser gathers every text in the museum's sources that commands obedience to God and His Messenger or praises it, and every text that names acts of obedience. The museum's founder named them among the sections of the browser, so they are placed on the scale above the two degrees of the act, the obligatory and the recommended; this arrangement is the work of the browser.

## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an table places under this section the verses whose wording carries the command to obey or the promise made for it:
- The command to obey God and the Messenger: [[passage/quran-3-32|Al 'Imran 32]] and [[passage/quran-3-132|132]], [[passage/quran-4-59|al-Nisa' 59]], in which those in authority appear, [[passage/quran-5-92|al-Ma'ida 92]], [[passage/quran-8-1|al-Anfal 1]], [[passage/quran-8-20|20]] and [[passage/quran-8-46|46]], [[passage/quran-24-54|al-Nur 54]] and [[passage/quran-24-56|56]], [[passage/quran-47-33|Muhammad 33]], [[passage/quran-58-13|al-Mujadila 13]], [[passage/quran-64-12|al-Taghabun 12]] and [[passage/quran-64-16|16]].
- The promise to whoever obeys: [[passage/quran-4-13|al-Nisa' 13]] and [[passage/quran-4-69|69]], [[passage/quran-24-52|al-Nur 52]], [[passage/quran-33-71|al-Ahzab 71]], [[passage/quran-48-17|al-Fath 17]], [[passage/quran-49-14|al-Hujurat 14]].
- Obedience to the Messenger is obedience to God: [[passage/quran-4-80|al-Nisa' 80]].
- The saying of the believers, "We hear and we obey": [[passage/quran-2-285|al-Baqara 285]], [[passage/quran-5-7|al-Ma'ida 7]] and [[passage/quran-24-51|al-Nur 51]].
- The description of the believing men and women by obedience: [[passage/quran-9-71|al-Tawba 71]] and [[passage/quran-33-33|al-Ahzab 33]]; and the regret of those who did not obey in [[passage/quran-33-66|al-Ahzab 66]].

The text of each verse is in its own entry, from the Tanzil text, and is not written here.

## In the hadith
- "Whoever obeys me has obeyed God, and whoever disobeys me has disobeyed God, and whoever obeys the commander has obeyed me, and whoever disobeys the commander has disobeyed me" [[passage/bukhari-api-2956|al-Bukhari 2956]] {cite:hadith-api-fawazahmed0 Bukhari 2956}.
- The limit of obedience: the Prophet sent out an army and appointed a man over them. The man lit a fire and said, "Enter it." Some of them wished to enter it, and others said, "We only fled from it." When this was mentioned to the Prophet, he said to those who had wished to enter it, "Had they entered it, they would have remained in it until the Day of Resurrection", and he said to the others, "There is no obedience in disobedience; obedience is only in what is right" [[passage/bukhari-api-7257|al-Bukhari 7257]] {cite:hadith-api-fawazahmed0 Bukhari 7257}.
- Drawing near through the obligations and then through the supererogatory acts, in the sacred hadith [[passage/bukhari-6502|al-Bukhari 6502]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 6502}, by which this section is linked with the sections on [[concept/fard|the obligations]] and the recommended acts.
- "What I have forbidden you, avoid; and what I have commanded you, do of it what you are able" [[passage/muslim-1337|Muslim 1337]] {cite:sahih-muslim 1337}. In its wording, compliance with a command is according to ability.

## Obedience and hearing
The pairing of "hearing and obeying" is frequent in the hadith, in the pledge to the Prophet and in obedience to those in authority, and it has its entry, [[concept/taat-wulat-al-amr|obedience to those in authority]]. The browser includes a text of "hearing and obeying" in this section only when it joins another of its terms.

## Among the scholars
Ibn Rajab states in his commentary on al-Bukhari that the majority of the Sunnis hold that all acts of obedience are part of faith, and his excerpt is included in the browser [[passage/fath-bari-ibn-rajab-arkan-1-14|Fath al-Bari by Ibn Rajab]]. The place of this section among the degrees is in the entry on [[concept/al-ahkam-al-taklifiyya|the taklifi rulings]].
