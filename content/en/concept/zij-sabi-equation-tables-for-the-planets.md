## Summary
Al-Battani arranges the planetary equation tables: the sixth gives the mean equation, the fifth the amounts of decrease, the seventh the amounts of increase, the fourth the minutes taken from the increase or decrease, and the third the equation of the apogee and of the centre between the two centres. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.95}.

## The radius of the epicycle
The radius of each planet's epicycle is set by its mean equation: the equation grows in the lower part of the circle above the mean and falls in the upper part below it, and the sixth table holds that mean equation itself. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.95}.
