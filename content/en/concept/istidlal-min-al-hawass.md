## Sight, hearing and smell
Sight is afflicted when its act fails, weakens or is confused so that the person imagines things that do not exist outside, such as phantoms, gnats, sparks and smoke; when the affliction is not particular to the eye, an affliction in the brain is inferred from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.11-12}

In hearing it may weaken so that only what is near and loud is heard, or be confused so that the person hears what does not exist outside, such as a roar like running water, the striking of hammers, the sound of drums or the rustling of winds; from this one infers either a dry temperament in the middle of the brain or winds and vapours held in it or rising to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12} Weakness and failure are from much cold, and one who hears as if from afar, from moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12}

In smell it may be lost, weakened or confused so that smells are sensed that do not exist outside; this mostly indicates a humour held in the front of the brain, unless it is something particular to the nostril {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12}

## Taste, touch, and the murkiness and clarity of the senses
Taste and touch may go the same way, except that their change from the natural course mostly indicates a particular corruption in the nearby parts and, less often, sharing from the brain, especially when it is general, like numbness of the whole body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12}

The senses may share in a kind of weakness or strength, which indicates a lasting state in the brain, murkiness and clarity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12} Murkiness does not go with every weakness, but murkiness always indicates a matter and clarity a dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12} Sometimes murkiness sets in suddenly and sadar results, which indicates a vaporous matter in the vessels of the brain and the net {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12}

The rule in these afflictions is that what runs like confusion mostly follows a hot dry temperament, and what runs like lessening and weakness mostly follows cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.12} Some of the sensing organs are very near the brain, such as hearing and smell, so most of their afflictions that do not go away with cleansing and tempering come from the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.13}
