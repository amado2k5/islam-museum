## The kinds of hearing's disorder
Disorders of hearing are like the disorders of all functions: the lesion of every function is either that the function is annulled — here, loss of hearing; or lessened — hearing does not exhaust and does not hear from afar; or altered — hearing what is not, like the ringing, buzzing and whistling that occur in the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.218}
## Original and adventitious
A disorder of hearing is either original — congenital deafness, hardness or dullness — or adventitious. Deafness is not the same as hardness: deafness is the meatus created solid inside, lacking the inner cavity described, the one like the uvea holding the still air which hears the sound by its waving; hardness and dullness are that the lesion does not reach loss of sensation — the dullness may approach the general annulment of deafness while a cavity exists but the nerve does not convey the sense's power, the hardness a lessening without annulment, or the terms may be agreed in reverse; hardness often follows flux and is easy of removal ⁦(the clause carried as printed)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.218}
## Which accept treatment, and its causes
Of lost hearing the naturally born kind has no cure, as with all born kinds of dullness and hardness; of the occurring kind, one of long standing is chronic, near despair or hard of treatment, while a recent occurring hardness may accept treatment. Its causes may be an organ's sharing — the brain's sharing, or of organs near it as at the first teething and at toothaches — or a lesion special to hearing itself, either the nerve or the meatus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.218}
## The lesion of nerve and passage
The lesion in the hearing nerve may admit all the causes of homoeomerous diseases in it — organic and continuity-loss kinds. Of the homoeomerous: every kind of single and compound bad temperament, mostly cold, each possibly with a melancholic, bilious, phlegmy matter of raw phlegm, or windy; a bilious diarrhoea is often checked and deafness follows, and it may be the same with other diarrhoeas naturally occurring that are checked in time. Of the organic in the nerve: an occlusion caused by humour, pus, a swelling of dubayla, a hot or hard swelling, a film of dirt, flaccidity or a puffer; of continuity-loss: an ulcer or corrosion ⁦(dubayla: a disease in the cavity, or an abscess and boil appearing in it — the editor's gloss from the Qamus)⁩. Of what arises in the passage, most is an occlusion from a bodily cause or from outside: bodily — a wart, swelling, extra flesh, worms, much dirt, a thick humour, earwax, the clotting of pus from a burst swelling, or worms; external — sand, a pebble or a pip entering, or blood partly flowed from the ear and partly stayed clotting; it may come suddenly or little by little. An adventitious lesion may remain after a fever goes, either passing like what comes at the body's movements, or fixed — the crisis itself pushing the matter to the ear and settling it there; this adventitious kind is often heralded by vomiting, nosebleed or the stopping of a diarrhoea ⁦(simlakh: what comes out of the ear of dirt — the editor's gloss)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.218} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.219}
