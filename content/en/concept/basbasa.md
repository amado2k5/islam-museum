## Nature and selection
Like piled, wrinkled, dry leaves of red and yellow, like shells, and wood and leaf that rasp the tongue like cubeb, brought from China. Ibn Masawayh said it is the shell of jawzbawa. Masih said its power is like the fire of musk and lighter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}. The editor's footnote gives it as nutmeg, as Ibn Masawayh said.

## Temperament and actions
Paulus said moderate; others say hot and dry in the second degree, and there is no doubt of its heat and dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: dissolves wind and has some astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Swellings and pustules: dissolves thick hard swellings when put in the plaster called qayruti {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Adornment: sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Head: with violet oil it is used as a snuff for headache from thick wind in the head and for migraine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Digestion: strengthens the liver and stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Excretory organs: binds the flux of the bowel and helps abrasion; the book says it is good for the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.
