## Summary
Hujna is defect and ugliness in the lexical sense. The hajin among horses is the foal of a non-Arab mare by a stallion; among people it is one born of an Arab father and a non-Arab mother. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16447}

## Speech etiquette
Good speech avoids naming disgusting things outright and uses euphemism that conveys the meaning. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16448}

## The spoils share of a hybrid horse
The Hanafis, Malikis and Shafi'is give a hybrid horse the same share as an Arab horse. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16448}
The Hanbalis distinguish: a rider on an Arab horse (ateeq) gets three shares; on a hajin, a muqraf or a birdhawn he gets two, one for himself and one for the horse. They rely on a report from Khalid ibn Ma'dan, transmitted as mursal in Abu Dawud's Marasil. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16449}
