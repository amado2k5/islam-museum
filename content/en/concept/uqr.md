## Summary
In the lexicon, uqr is the dower, especially the dower given to a woman forced or taken as a slave-woman, and also the dower of a woman had by mistake {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11769}.

## Its amount
For a free woman it is the customary dower; for a slave-woman it is a tenth of her value if she was a virgin and half of that if she was not {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11769} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11770}.

## Ajr and uqr
Ajr is wider and covers the price of benefits generally, while uqr is used only of benefit from the woman's body {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11770}.

## When it is due
The dower is due in a valid marriage on the contract and consummation, and in a void marriage on consummation; it is also due for intercourse under a mistake. The jurists differ on whether the stipulated dower or the customary dower is due {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11770}.

## Where to look further
The rules of dower are treated under «mahr», and mistake under «shubha» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11770}.
