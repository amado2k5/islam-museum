## The kinds of the disorder
Disorder enters smell as it enters every faculty: smell is either abolished, weakened, or changed and corrupted. Its abolition and weakness are of two faces: it may fail toward the sense of both the pleasant and the foul, or toward one of them. Its corruption is likewise of two faces: one, smelling hidden odours that are not present; the other, finding pleasant what is not pleasant — like one who enjoys the smell of dung and hates the pleasant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.235}
## Its causes
The cause of these disorders is either a bad simple temperament or a bad humour — in the front of the brain and its two ventricles, or in the two nipple-like bodies themselves — or a hardness in the sieve bone from a humour, a wind, a swelling or cancer, a growth of superfluous flesh, or an occlusion in the membrane above it. What arises from a simple bad temperament is often produced by drugs used and drops dripped that heated a temperament, or numbed and chilled it, or by over-intense air doing one of those; and it may come from a blow or a fall that brings a lesion upon the bone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.235}
