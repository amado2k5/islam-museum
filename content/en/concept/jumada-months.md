## The naming
Jumada al-Ula and Jumada al-Akhira are the fifth and sixth months, named for the freezing of water in the cold season at the time the names were set; they are also called the two Jumadas. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}

## In the seerah
Campaigns recorded by the seerah scholars fell in Jumada al-Ula, and expeditions and events in Jumada al-Akhira; neither has a special devotional rank. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## Their rule
They are non-sacred months in which fighting and work are permitted, and whoever wishes may fast in them voluntarily like any other month. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
