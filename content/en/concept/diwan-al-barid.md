## Overview
Qudama ibn Ja'far describes the *diwan al-barid* (the postal office) as an independent office to which letters from all regions were brought, and from which they were then dispatched to their destinations. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.192}

## Its responsibilities according to Qudama
- Presenting to the caliph the letters of the postal and news agents in the regions, or preparing summaries of them.
- Overseeing the couriers (*ghuwaraniqiyun*), the timekeepers (*muwaqqitun*), and the stagemen (*mu'thibun*) on the post roads, and disbursing their pay.
- Appointing the mapmakers in the cities.

{cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.192}

The head of this office must be a trustworthy person. {cite:masalik-mamalik-ibn-khurdadhbih p.192}
