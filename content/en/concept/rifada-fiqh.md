## Summary
Rifada in the lexicon is a gift or a support; in the pre-Islamic period Quraysh collected money at the pilgrimage season to feed the pilgrims, the rifada and the water-service belonging to the Banu Hashim, and the keeping of the Ka'ba and the banner to the Banu 'Abd al-Dar. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8797} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8798} Quraysh took pride in these offices, and God rebuked those who made the watering of pilgrims equal to faith in God and the Last Day. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8798} Islam allowed the rifada as a kindness to pilgrims and as alms to the poor among them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8798}
