## Nature and selection
Nature: Dioscorides says in his book that halitit is the gum of anjudan, obtained by cutting its root and stalk; the gum then runs out after the cut. The halitit brought from the land of Cyrenaica, when the tongue tastes it, raises a rash over the body like measles at that spot, and its smell is not unpleasant. Another kind known in Syria is weaker. All kinds are adulterated with sagapenum or with broad-bean flour, and the adulteration is detected by taste, smell and colour. The gum is strongest, then the leaf, then the stalk. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}
The two kinds are the foul-smelling and the fragrant; the foul is hotter, and most of it is from Qayrawan. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}
Selection: the best is clear and inclined to red, called bitter, strong in smell but not like leek, not green in colour, not unpleasant in taste, easy to dissolve; when dissolved it turns white. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot at the start of the fourth degree and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}
Actions: it breaks wind and expels it by dissolving it; it also causes flatulence, cuts, and dissolves blood. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: helps alopecia when applied with vinegar and pepper; used in food it improves the colour; it removes tubercle-like warts. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}
Swellings and abscesses: when a malignant swelling that threatens the organ is cut and halitit is applied, it helps; it is good for internal and external abscesses. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.479}
Ulcers: helps the sores called qawabi. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Joints: drunk with pomegranate water it helps tearing of the muscles; it helps pains of the nerve such as tension and paralysis. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Organs of the head: a decaying molar is packed with it, or it is mixed with frankincense and stuck to the tooth; it acts like paeony in epilepsy; gargling with it removes a leech from the throat. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Organs of the eye: good for the start of cataract as a kohl with honey. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Organs of the chest: dissolved in water and swallowed it clears the voice and helps long-standing roughness of the throat; with egg, licked, it helps chronic cough and cold pleurisy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Organs of digestion: with dried fig it helps jaundice; it is harmful to the stomach and liver. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Excretory organs: helps piles, strengthens sexual power, brings urine and the menses, helps colic and intestinal ulcers. Paulus claims a slight purging with astringency, and it helps long-standing cold diarrhoea. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Fevers: very helpful for the quartan fever. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Poisons: placed on the bite of the mad dog and of vermin, especially scorpion and tarantula; drunk or applied with oil it helps these, and it relieves the harm of poisoned arrows. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
