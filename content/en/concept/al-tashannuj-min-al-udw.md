## From the stomach and the hurt
Of this kind is the convulsion of one who vomits a verdigris humour biting the mouth of the stomach, the convulsion arising from the strength of the stomach mouth's sense when bile is driven into it, and the convulsion arising for a disorder in the stomach, the womb and the nervous organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.144}

## From sting, cut and gnawing
The convulsion arising from the sting of the scorpion, the tarantula and the snake on a nerve, or a cut striking the nerve, or its gnawing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.144}

## Worms
Near this is the convulsion occurring for worms {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.145}

## Sharing of womb and bladder (labelled listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book counts among the causes of convulsion the brain's sharing with the womb in its diseases, and the bladder and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.144}
