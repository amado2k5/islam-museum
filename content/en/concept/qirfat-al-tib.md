## Nature
The bark of the clove is thick husks in the colour of cinnamon bark; it has the taste of the clove, so it is weaker in its actions than the clove {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}
