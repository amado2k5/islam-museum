## Nature, choice and temperament
A light, earthy thing like lichen; in Raqqa it is called pigeon dung, and in Baghdad jawz jandam. The best is the Berber kind; the Raqqa kind is weak. Its nature: hot and moist in the first degree, though some say it is slightly cooling, which is not firmly established. The editor's note gives the name as pigeon dung. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.525}

## Properties and cosmetic use
It dries; it has a seminal quality, and it is claimed that it cuts blood. The book gives a preparation with honey and water, whose method and amounts are left out. Cosmetic use: very fattening. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): the book says it increases semen. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}
