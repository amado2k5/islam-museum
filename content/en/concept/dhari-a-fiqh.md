## Summary
Dhari'a in the lexicon is the means that leads to a thing; as a term it is whatever leads to a further end. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8421}
A means may lead to forbidden harms or to goods: travel is a means to pilgrimage, and deferred sales are a means to usury. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8421}
The ruling on a means has two sides, blocking it and opening it; the detail is under the entry on blocking the means. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8422}
