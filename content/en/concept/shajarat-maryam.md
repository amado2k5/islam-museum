## Nature
It is the bukhur Maryam, and it was said of it in the chapter of the letter mim when we mentioned muqla minus; it is of three kinds, one without fruit and two with fruit; the editor's note says that shajar Maryam is the bukhur Maryam {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.675}

## Organs of the head
It benefits the cold catarrh {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}

## Ocular organs
Beneficial for the descent of water in the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}
