## Equal hours and seasonal hours
The hours of a degree from a cusp are found by taking its distance from the midheaven or the nadir in seasonal hours, then finding the difference between its rising on the straight sphere and its rising in the clime. The difference is divided by six and multiplied by the hours of the distance from the cusp, which gives the interval between the two degrees. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.203-204}

## The rule of six
The text states that the share of one hour is its sixth, which is multiplied by the hours of the distance from the cusp. It applies the rule of six to the seasonal hours between the cusps. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.204}
