## Summary
The emergence of the Dajjal is one of the major signs the Prophet foretold: he comes one-eyed, claiming to be God, bearing what resemble paradise and fire to test people, traveling the earth for decades, unable to enter Mecca or Medina, and is killed by Jesus son of Mary at the gate of Ludd. Sunnis agree he appears before the Resurrection and that Jesus brings his trial to an end.
