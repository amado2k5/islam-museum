## Actions and properties
It has astringency and a gluing power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.582}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Its burnt form helps malignant ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.582}.

## Eyes
It is burnt, quenched and washed, and then takes the place of tutia in eye salves; it beautifies the lashes and with nardin grows them, and it is good for ulcers of the eye and the growing of the lid edges {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.582}.
