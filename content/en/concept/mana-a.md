## Summary
In the lexicon mana'a is power and honour: one has mana'a when he has kinsmen to defend him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15069}

## Rebellion and banditry
The jurists require that rebels and bandits have mana'a before the offence counts as baghy or hiraba. Details are in those entries. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15070}

## Protected visitors aiding rebels
The Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis hold that protected visitors who aid rebels break their pact and become like enemy combatants. If they do so under compulsion, while the rebels have mana'a, the pact is not broken. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15070}
