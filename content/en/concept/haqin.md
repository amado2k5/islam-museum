## Overview

A haqin holds back urine, a haqib holds back faeces and a hazeq holds back wind. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6724}

## Ruling on prayer

Prayer in this state is disliked by the Malikis, Shafiis and Hanbalis, as a matter of discouragement; the Hanafis treat it as forbidden and say the prayer must be broken unless the time is about to pass. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6725}

## Judging and giving opinions

A judge or mufti in this state is disliked to rule or give an opinion. A judgment given correctly still stands. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6726}
