## Overview

Jins in the language means a kind of anything; among the jurists it names a set of different sorts. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6545}

## Its effect on zakat, riba and salam

Animals or crops of one kind are added together toward the threshold, while different kinds are not added, so cattle are not added to camels. In ribawi sales the sameness or difference of kind decides the rule. In salam (advance sale) the kind of the item promised must be named. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6546} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6547} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6548}
