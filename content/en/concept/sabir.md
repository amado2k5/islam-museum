## Nature
A congealed juice between red and fair, of which there is the Socotran, the Arabian and the Samanjani. Some said that its plant is like the plant of rasan, and it is not so {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Choice
The best is the Socotran, its water like saffron water and its smell like myrrh, glossy, crumbling, clean of grit; the Arabian is below it in yellowness, weight and shine, and more viscous and harder; the Samanjani is bad, of a stinking smell, greasy, little yellow, with no shine; and when aloes grows old it becomes black {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Temperament
Hot to the second degree and dry in it; it was said to be hot and dry in the third, and it is not so {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Actions and properties
Its power is astringent, drying to the bodies and sleep-inducing; the Indian has many benefits, drying without stinging, with a little astringency, and its stinging is so little that it does not sting bad wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Cosmetics
With honey on the marks of a blow, and it heals the ulcerated whitlow; with wine on falling hair it prevents its falling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Swellings and pustules
It benefits swellings of the anus and the genitals, and especially the swellings of the muscles at the two sides of the tongue, with wine or honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Wounds and ulcers
Good for ulcers slow to heal, especially in the anus, the genitals, the nose and the mouth, and for fistulas {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Joints
It benefits pains of the joints {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Organs of the head
It cleanses the bilious superfluities in the head, and smeared on the forehead and temple with rose oil it benefits headache and cures it; it benefits ulcers of the nose and mouth and is among the drugs useful for bruising of the ear. In the old medicine aloes loosens black bile and benefits melancholia, and Persian aloes kindles the mind and sharpens the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Ocular organs
It benefits ulcers of the eye, its scab and its pains, and itch of the corners, and dries its moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It cleanses the bilious and phlegmatic superfluities in the stomach, restores a false and corrupt appetite, mends the burning and inflammation in the uvula from the heat of the yellow bile of the stomach, loosens the belly without spoiling the food, and may benefit pains of the stomach in a single day; it opens obstructions of the liver but harms the liver, and removes jaundice by its loosening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Excretory organs
It is the best laxative for the stomach, and when it enters the laxatives it repels their harm to the stomach; the washed is weaker in loosening but more beneficial to the stomach, and mixing it with honey lessens its force until it hardly loosens by drawing but brings out what it meets; the Arabian drunk causes distress and colic and loosens, and its force remains in the membranes of the stomach for a day or two; it is put with sweet wine on swelling haemorrhoids and fissures of the anus and stops the blood that flows from them, and heals swellings of the anus and the penis applied with wine and honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Poisons and bites
A warning only: giving aloes in the days of cold is dangerous, for it may loosen blood, whatever the aloes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}

## Substitutes
Its substitute is huḍuḍ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.641}
