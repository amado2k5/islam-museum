## Nature and selection
Nature: a very astringent Indian bark. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.446}

## Actions
Astringent. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.446}

## Uses by organ
Breathing: good for coughing up blood and for pleurisy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.446}
Excretory organs: helps ulcers of the intestines. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.446}
