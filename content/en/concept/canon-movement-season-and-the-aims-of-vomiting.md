## Summary
Ibn Sina states that movement increases vomiting and rest lessens it, that summer is the best season for those who do not vomit easily, and that vomiting as a first cleansing comes from the stomach alone, as a second cleansing from the head and body, while drawing and uprooting come from below {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## Movement and rest
Movement makes vomiting greater and rest makes it less {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## Season
Summer is the best time for using vomiting, and if someone needs it to whom vomiting does not come naturally, summer is the best time to allow it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## Aims of vomiting
As a first cleansing, vomiting clears the stomach alone and not the gut; as a second cleansing it clears the head and the rest of the body; drawing and uprooting come from below {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.
