## Nature and selection
A well-known Roman drug {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Actions and properties
It burns the skin, which is the book's own warning {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It helps facial palsy when put into the nose with beet water; the amount the book gives is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Eyes
It helps swellings of the eyelids and their irritation, and swellings that occur under the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.
