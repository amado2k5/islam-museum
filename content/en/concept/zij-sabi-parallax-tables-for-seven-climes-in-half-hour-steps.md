## Summary
Al-Battani says the tables of parallax in longitude and latitude were set up by an astronomer of Alexandria, who gave the Moon's parallax for seven climes in steps of half an hour of the longest day, drawn for the Moon at the heads of the signs after the Sun's parallax was subtracted. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.124}.

## Attribution of the tables
The text attributes the tables to their author, but the name is garbled in the scan and is not recorded here. Al-Battani took the method of the book of Ptolemy and varied it according to the obliquity. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.124}.
