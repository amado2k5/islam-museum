## Causes of temper and disease
Cold and moisture may meet together in the causes of sleep, but the prior cause of the two is then cold, aided by moisture, as in wakefulness heat and dryness meet, the true cause being heat, aided by dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.90} Coma has other causes: among them the intensifying of the fits of fever, nature turning with its whole essence to the illness and being pressed under the matter, the psychic pneuma following it, especially if the matter of the fever is a cold phlegmatic fever that has been warmed only by putrefaction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.90} It may be from the badness of humours and of vapours rising to the front of the brain from the stomach and the lung in their illnesses and from the other organs, and it may be from many worms and the gourd seed (habb al-qar') {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.90} Every coma that depends on temper is from cold first and moisture second, and it may pass into coma from the like of dhat al-janb and dhat al-ri'a and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91}

## Pressure, pain and weakness
It may be from pressure on the brain itself under the bone of the skull or its plate or its shell when a blow strikes the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.90} The ventricle that is most apt to bring on coma when it is cut is the one most apt to bring it on when it is pressed, and it may be from severe pain from a blow that strikes the muscles of the temple {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91} It may be from the great weakness and dissolving of the pneuma, so that its expanding is hard {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91}

## Reasoning to the place of the disorder
Since the first senses that fail in sleep and coma are sight and hearing, the disorder in coma must be in the front of the brain, with the sharing of a corrupt dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91} For had the front of the brain been sound and the corruption come only on its back, it would not have to strike sight and hearing and there would not be sleep but the loss of movement or touch alone, as that happens in the diseases of stiffness (jumud) and shukhus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91} The blockage in coma must not be complete or very dense, or it would harm breathing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91}

## Womb (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Among the causes of coma Ibn Sina names a severe pain by sharing of a harm in the mouth of the stomach or in the womb, from which the brain contracts and the paths of the sentient pneuma are blocked so that the movement of the pneuma outward is hard {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91}
