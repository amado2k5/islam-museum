## Their cause
The mouth mostly breaks out in pustules for a heat about the stomach and the head, and vapours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.261}
## Their medicines
The simples useful in pustules at the start, when cooling and drying are needed, are the cold astringents of this kind and the cold juices of this kind; many boys have their mouth-pustules treated with sugar and a cold simple of this kind. The hot ones needed at the end are of this kind, and among them are the unclean drugs of this kind (an unclean drug the book names, kept as a labelled listing for the founder's ruling), and the ulcerated ones may need what cauterises of this kind; compound drugs of this kind have been tried for the thick ones (the recipes and names are left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.261}
## When they begin to suppurate
When the pustules begin to suppurate, the mucilages made of seeds of this kind — those very seeds — barley-flour and she-asses' milk alone or with some of these are brought near them (an animal milk the book carries); and a decoction of this kind may be needed. One of the verifying physicians said nothing is more decisive in treating mouth-pustules than holding a lukewarm oil of this kind in the mouth (a saying ascribed to one of the verifying physicians; the oil's name left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.262}
