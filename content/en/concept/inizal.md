## Definition
Inizal is the passive of 'azl, dismissal, which is separating one thing from another. In fiqh it is the loss by a holder of authority of his right to act. It may come through dismissal or by operation of law, as with the apostate or the insane. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3509}

## Principle
One who takes an office with a fixed competence or specific conditions, and then loses that competence or an essential condition, is dismissed by law without need of a decision. This applies in general and is worked out in the imamate, judgeship, endowments and guardianship over orphans. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3509}

## Ceasing and entitlement to dismissal
- Inizal needs no dismissal, and none of the acts of the one who has ceased to hold office take effect. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3509}
- Entitlement to dismissal (istihqaq al-azl) arises when the holder commits what calls for his removal, such as a judge who is corrupt, rules by whim or takes bribes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3509}
