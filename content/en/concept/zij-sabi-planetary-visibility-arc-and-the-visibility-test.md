## The arc of vision
Each planet has an arc of vision, reckoned on its epicycle, by which appearance and disappearance are calculated. If the distance between the planet and the Sun, measured in rising or setting degrees, equals the arc of vision, the planet is seen or disappears on that day. For a first appearance from under the rays, if the distance is less than the arc it has not yet appeared, and if more it has. For a disappearance, less than the arc means it has disappeared, more means it has not. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.181-182}

## The true motion for the reckoning
The planet is reckoned for the day before or after as needed; the difference between the two positions is its true daily motion, and the Sun's true daily motion is subtracted to leave the corrected motion. This is repeated to count the days elapsed. The text says the arcs of vision are reckoned in full through the table. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.181-182}
