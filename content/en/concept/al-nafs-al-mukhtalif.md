## The uneven breath, the doubled and the halved
Three chapters in a row: the first general on regular and irregular unevenness; the second on the doubled, a kind of it, with the causes named; the third on the halved, a harm in half the lung while the other half is sound {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.313}
