## Overview
Error and forgetting are among the matters for which God has pardoned this community, as in the hadith of Ibn Abbas, the thirty-ninth in Ibn Rajab's commentary. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 796-797}

## The hadith
Its text: "God has overlooked for me, for my community, error and forgetting, and what they were compelled to do." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 796-797}

## The meaning
Error is doing what is forbidden without intending it. Forgetting is leaving what is commanded or doing what is prohibited because the heart was distracted from it. According to the preferred view of the commentators on the hadith, both lift accountability, because the intention is absent. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 796-797}

## The worldly effect
Error may carry liability for property, as in the rulings on accidental killing. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 796-797}
