## Summary
Nafs, in the lexicon, is the soul or spirit, blood, the self or essence of a thing, and the eye. Nafas is a breath of air. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15857}

## Rulings on the self
Blood: creatures with no flowing blood are excused from impurity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15858}
Killing a soul without right is classed as intentional, quasi-intentional or mistaken, each with its own rulings. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15858}
Self-defence against an aggressor is lawful by agreement; the Hanafis and, in the sounder Maliki view, require repelling him; the Shafi'is and Hanbalis give detailed cases. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15858}
Suicide is a grave major sin, with punishment from God, as the hadith and Qur'an state. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15858} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15859}
Repentance of a wilful unlawful killer: most jurists accept it; the Hanafis require the heirs' consent, the Shafi'is and Hanbalis require regret and resolve, and Ibn Abbas and Zayd ibn Thabit do not accept it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15859} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15860}
