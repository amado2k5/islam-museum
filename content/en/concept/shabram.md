## Nature
It grows in gardens, with a fine straight stalk, down, and leaves like the leaves of tarkhun as the author estimates, and a milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.678}

## Choice
The best is the light one that tends to redness, thin of bark, and the one with two rods, light of bark; the thick, little red, hard, fibrous one is bad, and the Persian is bad; the book says nothing of it should be used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Temperament
Hunayn said: hot at the beginning of the second degree, dry at the end of the third; its milk is extreme in both, even in the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Actions and properties
It has astringency, sharpness and a bursting of the mouths of the veins, which is one reason it is abandoned; when corrected it is not of benefit, for what the book says in its place; it is on the whole harmful, especially to hot temperaments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Organs of the head
Its milk helps in pulling teeth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It harms the stomach and the liver and is given in the treatment of dropsy; the book describes a soaking and a tabletting with other drugs to make its benefit strong, left out here as a method of preparation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Excretory organs
It purges black bile, phlegm and water; in ancient medicine it was used among the purgatives and then abandoned for its harm and for its bursting of the veins of the anus; the book says that whoever cannot avoid using it should mix aniseed, fennel and cumin with it; its milk is of no good and the book does not think it should be drunk; if its purging is excessive, sitting in cold water is among what stops it; and the book reports that it is given for colic with other drugs named in the chapter on colic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Fevers
It was abandoned for generating fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Poisons and bites
The book says a stated weight of it kills, and the weight is left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it was given up in ancient medicine for its harm to desire and to semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}
