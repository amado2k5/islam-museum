## Summary
The "1442 method" is the model that [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] teaches in the seventh lesson of [[work/leadership-development-academy-1-program|the Leaders' Academy]] for assessing, or founding, any organisation. He calls it his own invention, drawn from long work with dozens of organisations. He says it has no references and that he had not yet written it in any book. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:13:30} He named it 1442 because it is drawn like a football line-up: one element at the top, two rows of four beneath it, and two at the base. That makes eleven elements in all. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:15:00} By "organisation" he means any organised group: a company, an association, a party, a ministry or a state. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:15:00} The same lesson opens the [[concept/tahlil-disc|DISC]] test, which analyses persons rather than organisations. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:03:00}

## One: planning
Planning stands alone at the top, "number one and without rival". He says that when he asks organisations whether their plan is written, ninety per cent say it is not. A plan that is not written, he says, does not count. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:19:30} He links the strategic horizon to size, and asks for goals that can be measured. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:21:30} See [[concept/al-takhtit-al-istratiji|strategic planning]].

## Four: the pillars of work
The first row of four he calls the pillars of work. He compares them to the pillars, obligations and sunnas of the prayer: a cracked pillar brings the whole down. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:23:30}
- **Leadership.** The organisation should be led, not only managed. "Leadership focuses on relationships and the future," he says, while management focuses on results now. He advises a leadership body without portfolios, kept separate from the executive office. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:28:30}
- **Members and staff.** Never hire after a single interview, and prepare the questions. Always ask why the candidate left the last post and what his faults are. Hire for character, analytical ability and speed of learning. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:33:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:36:00} People are hired for competence, he says, but eighty-five per cent of those dismissed lose their posts through behaviour. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:39:00}
- **System and regulations.** Their aim is to reduce errors, not abolish them; otherwise there would be no need for leadership. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:43:30}
- **The curriculum.** He means the idea the organisation stands on, which must be clear and sound, together with its expected outcomes and its phases. He does not mean a textbook. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:45:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:49:00}

## Four: the pillars of competition
The second row of four keeps an organisation ahead of others. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:50:00}
- **Training.** His minimum is fifteen hours a year for every junior employee and forty-five for middle and senior staff, with three per cent of the expenses budget spent on training. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:52:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:53:00}
- **Technology.** It is open to everyone. As historical examples he gives the mangonel at the siege of al-Ta'if and the trench, a system new to the Arabs. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:54:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:55:00}
- **Quality and service.** He names ISO and Six Sigma. He says al-Risala channel obtained ISO in its first six months and was the only Arab channel to hold it. He sums up all quality systems in one law: "write what you do, and do what you write". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:58:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:00:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:01:30}
- **Creativity.** Ask every year, "What is new this year?", and introduce one major new thing each year. He says his Creativity Academy did this in its first years. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:04:00}

## Two: infrastructure
At the base are the building and the money. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:07:00} He says organisations die for two main reasons: the money runs dry, or everything rests on a single leader. So the funding should be planned together with the idea. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:08:30} He closes the lesson on reliance on God after full effort. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 01:09:00}
