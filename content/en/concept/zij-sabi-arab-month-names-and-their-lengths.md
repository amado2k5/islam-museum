## Summary
Al-Battani lists the Arab months from Muharram to Dhu al-Hijja, and states that a lunar month has thirty or twenty-nine days, with Dhu al-Hijja carrying an extra fraction of a day in the reckoning. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.100}.

## The reckoning through the cycle
He sets out how the months vary within a full year, shows that the lunar year is shorter than the solar, and uses that difference to convert dates between the two calendars. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.100}.
