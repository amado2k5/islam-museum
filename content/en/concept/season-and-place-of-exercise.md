## Summary
Ibn Sina sets the best time for exercise in spring at about midday in a temperate room, brings it earlier in summer, and says that by reasoning winter exercise would be put off to evening but other hindrances forbid it, so the place is warmed to make it temperate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.224}.

## The seasons
In spring the best time is about midday in a temperate room, and it is brought earlier in summer. In winter the reasoned course would be to put it off to evening, but other hindrances prevent this, so the place must be warmed in winter until it is temperate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.224}.

## The right time
Exercise is used at the best time, as set out by the digestion of the food and the reduction of the surplus. The best times are the temperate ones, and exercise sometimes throws a hot, dry person into disease, who is well when he leaves it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.224}.
