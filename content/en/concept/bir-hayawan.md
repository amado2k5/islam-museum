## Nature and selection
Well known {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

## Temperament and actions
No separate temperament is given in this entry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: lizard dung helps white patches and freckles by its cleansing; camel dung helps when drunk for that and removes warts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Head: lizard dung helps lichen by its cleansing; camel dung stops nosebleed, and with the drugs for epilepsy it helps when drunk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Eyes: lizard dung cleanses white spots of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Wounds and ulcers: camel dung dissolves pustules and ulcers; sheep dung likewise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Swellings and pustules: goat dung dissolves scrofula very strongly; camel dung and sheep dung likewise for redness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Joints: camel dung eases joint pains and their swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Excretory organs: dried goat dung with wool stops a flow from the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Poisons: goat dung boiled and applied to a viper bite; burnt sheep dung, especially with vinegar, applied to a mad dog's bite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.
