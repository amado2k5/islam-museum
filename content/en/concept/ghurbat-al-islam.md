## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that Islam began as a strange thing and will return to strangeness as it began, and that the second strangeness is harder than the first. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 01:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep07 00:56:00}

## The two strangenesses
The Sheikh says that the religion, or the nation, has lived two strangenesses: the first when the religion came as a strange thing, and it will pass as it began, strange; the second is greater than the first (needs_source: the saying he attributes to the Prophet about the beginning of strangeness is not sourced in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 01:10:30}
He holds that in the first strangeness the Prophet was present and revelation was coming down, and the elite of the first generation followed him; in the second, the religion finds no such support, and it is harder. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep07 00:56:00}

## Steadfastness in strangeness
The Sheikh holds that religion today suffers strangeness, that steadfastness is necessary, and that there are people who defend the religion as it deserves. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:28:00}
