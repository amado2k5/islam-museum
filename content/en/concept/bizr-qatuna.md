## Nature and selection
It comes in two kinds, winter and summer, The best is the full, plump kind that sinks in water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.392}.

## Temperament and actions
Cold and moist in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.392}. The roasted seed, ground in rose oil, is astringent, and it calms headache as a poultice with vinegar; the book calls it excellent for this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.392}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it is used ground with vinegar on hot swellings, herpes and erysipelas, especially those on the ears, and on phlegmatic swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Joints: it is applied for twisted and spasmodic nerves and for gout and joint pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Head: a poultice on the head helps its hot headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Chest: it softens the chest greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Food organs: its mucilage with rose oil or almond oil helps intense bilious thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Excretory organs: the roasted seed ground in rose oil binds and helps chafing, especially in children; its mucilage with violet oil loosens the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Fevers: drunk, it calms the flame of hot fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}.
