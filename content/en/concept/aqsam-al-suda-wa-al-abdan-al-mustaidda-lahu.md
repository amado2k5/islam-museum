## By cause
Some headache has as its cause one of the apparent causes, such as the headache of the hangover and the headache of eating something hot such as garlic; the editor's footnote glosses the hangover headache as the headache that afflicts the drinker of wine after he wakes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45} Some has a cause that came earlier and has arrived and stays, so the headache stays because of it; it may have been a symptom and then become a disease {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45} If it remains a disease after hot fevers it warns of brain illnesses and shows that nature could not drive the matter out completely by nosebleed or otherwise; the illnesses it warns of are the sleepless coma, silence (sukat), madness, slackening and deafness, according to the substance of the matter and its movements {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45}

## By place and strength
Headache divides by its places. It may be in one side of the head, and what is habitual and constant there is called shaqiqa; it may be in the front of the head or in the back; or it may encircle the whole head, and what is habitual and constant there is called bayda and khuda, likened to the "egg of the weapon" (baydat al-silah), which encloses the whole head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45} Headache differs in being severe, middling and weak. Some is so severe that, meeting the fontanelle of a boy with soft bones, it tears it and splits its suture; some is weak, such as most of what occurs in lethargus; and of the weak some is constant and some is not {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45} A headache whose cause is weak may befall some and not others: it befalls the one whose brain has a strong sense and does not befall the one whose brain has a weak sense {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45} The brain is quick to receive what causes headache either through its weakness, and it is known in the universals that weakness follows ill-temperament, or through the strength of its sense, so that it is hurt by every cause however light {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.45}

## What headache leads to
Some headache has no symptoms. Some leads to symptoms in the regions of the head, as when headache, through the severity of the pain, brings about swellings in those regions. Some leads to symptoms that pass to other organs, as when its harm reaches the roots of the nerves and brings about convulsion, or reaches the stomach and brings about loss of appetite, hiccup, nausea, weak digestion and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.45-46}

## Chronic headache
Know that chronic headache is either from phlegm, from black bile, from weakness of the head, from a hard swelling just beginning, or from a hot swelling that has hardened, which is the frequent and the splitting one {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.46} Diseases differ. Sometimes the disease is one whose management by what is due to it in itself is granted; some is not granted but has a companion; and one may be prevented from managing it as is due, as when there is headache and catarrh, for the catarrh opposes the headache in the way of managing it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.46}

## Bodies prone to it, the seasons and fumigants
Headache divides on yet another view: some befalls the healthy person now and then, who has no ailment, and some befalls one with swellings and ailments, awsab being, in the editor's footnote, the plural of wasab, illness and pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.46} Some bodies are prone to headache: the bodies with weak heads and weak digesting organs, in which vapours are generated and bilious humours pour into their stomachs, so that headache comes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.46} Among what is taken there are headache-causing things listed in the tables of simple drugs. All fumigants cause headache, hot or cold, but when they follow one another they push each other back, that is when what hurt by the heat of its vapour came first and was followed by what gives off a cold vapour, or the reverse; but when the harm is of quantity and not of quality alone, their following one another does not help and does harm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.46} Cold headache is frequent in winter because of congestion, and when the summer is northerly with little rain and the autumn southerly and rainy, headache is frequent in the winter; and headache is often because the artery carries bad vapours to the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.46}
