## Overview
In al-Masudi's account, the *hilba* is the racecourse of horse racing among the Arabs. Horses were sent out in groups of ten, and each place in the ten had a name. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.698-699}

## The names of the places
Al-Masudi mentions that the first is the *sabiq al-mujalli* (the foremost, who wins the race). The second is the *musalli*, because he places his muzzle on the croup of the first. The third is the *musalli* (also called the "muslli"), because he shares in the race. The fourth is the *tali*. The fifth is the *mirtah*. The sixth is the *hazzi*. The seventh is the *'atif*. The eighth is the *mu'ammil*. The ninth is the *lateem*. The tenth is the *sakit*. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.698-699}

## The last place
Al-Masudi mentions that the rider of the tenth horse would be filled with humility and abasement. They would tie a rope to his horse's neck and mount a monkey on it with a whip, so as to shame its rider. He reports that some would plant their horse and pelt it with arrows until it grew thin, and that al-Nu'man did this with his horse al-Nahb. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.698-699}
