## Summary
The second motion is the sun's and planets' own motion along the zodiac from west to east, opposite in direction to the first {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 116}. It runs on poles outside those of the first motion, and the sphere it follows is called the zone of the second motion {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 116}.

## Its poles
The zodiac turns on two poles each distant from the equinoctial's poles by an equal amount — each pole's distance from the equinoctial being the zodiac's inclination from it {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 148}{cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 149}.

## The sun's period in it
Al-Farghani shows that the sun covers the zodiac by its own motion in a solar year, returning to its point in about three hundred and sixty-five days and a quarter {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 182}.

## The second motion in the moon and planets
The moon has its own sphere in the second motion; its movement along the zodiac from west to east, contrary to the first motion, is accomplished together with the motion of the epicycle {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 183}{cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 185}. The five planets' motion therein is detailed in chapter fourteen {cite:farghani-elements-astronomy p. 187}.
