## Nature
It is said to resemble qabsum in strength {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.681}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.681}

## Organs of the head
It benefits epilepsy and cuts off the flowing saliva, especially from the mouths of children {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.681}

## Substitutes
Its substitute in its benefit for epilepsy and other ailments is marzanjush {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.681}
