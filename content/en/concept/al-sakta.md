## Definition
Apoplexy disables the organs from sense and motion by an obstruction occurring in the brain's ventricles and in the passages of the sensing and moving pneuma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.130}

## Its severities
If the instruments of motion and breathing are disabled with it, or weakened so the breath does not come easily, there is foam and intervals like choking or like snoring — which is harder, showing the impotence of the power that moves the soul's organs; its hardest is when neither breath nor foam nor snoring appears. If the lesion in the breathing is not great and what makes him snore passes into his throat without coming out of the nose, then though more hopeful than the other it is not free of great danger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.131}

## Hippocrates' saying
Hippocrates said that when apoplexy is strong its sufferer is not cured, and when it is weak he is not easily cured {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.131}
