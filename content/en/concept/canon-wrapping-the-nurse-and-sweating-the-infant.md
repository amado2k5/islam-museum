## Summary
In treating one disease Ibn Sina says the better course is to wrap the nurse and give the child pomegranate water with sakanjabin or cucumber juice with camphor and sugar, then to sweat it with the juice of fresh cane {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}. The disease's name is garbled in the scanned text and so is not given here.

## The method
The better course in it is that the nurse be wrapped, and the child also be given such as pomegranate water with sakanjabin and cucumber juice with a little camphor and sugar, then they are made to sweat by pressing fresh cane and putting its juice on the crown of the head and the foot and wrapping them, for this makes them sweat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.
