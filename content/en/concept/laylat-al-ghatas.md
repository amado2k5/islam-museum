## Overview
According to al-Masudi, the Night of the Epiphany (*Laylat al-Ghatas*) falls on the eleventh of Tubah, corresponding to the sixth of January. It had a great standing among the people of Egypt, and people did not sleep on it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.302-303}

## The account
Al-Masudi relates that he attended it in the year 330 AH, with al-Ikhshid Muhammad ibn Tughj, in his house on the island on the Nile. A thousand torches were lit on both sides of the island and of Fustat, and hundreds of thousands of Muslims and Christians attended. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.302-303}

He describes it as the finest night to be had in Egypt, when the streets are not closed. He notes that most of them bathe in the Nile, and claim that this is protection from illness. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.302-303}
