## Summary
Chapter 11 takes up the azimuth of the altitude and the shadow on the horizon circle, in any country, for any part of the zodiac and any time of day. Al-Battani takes the declination of the part, its chord and its side; the place's latitude, its chord and the chord of its complement; the altitude of the time, its chord and the chord of its complement; then works out the amplitude of the east and the difference of the horizon and adds the two or subtracts one from the other according to side, multiplies by the radius and divides by the chord of the altitude's complement, and the arc of the result is the azimuth. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.37-38}.

## The working
He says: take the declination of the part, find its chord and side, subtract it from ninety and find the chord of what is left; find the chord of the place's latitude and of its complement; take the altitude for any time you wish and find its chord and the chord of its complement. Then multiply the chord of the part's declination by the radius and divide by the chord of the latitude's complement; the result is the chord of the amplitude of the east, a name the scan renders in garbled form and which is read here from context. Keep it and note its side, the side of the declination. Then multiply the chord of the altitude by the chord of the latitude and divide by the chord of the latitude's complement; the result is the chord of the difference of the horizon, always southern. The wording is disordered in the scan, so this is a reading of the text and not a settled quotation. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.37-38}.

## Adding or subtracting, and the arc of azimuth
He says: if the chord of the amplitude of the east and the chord of the difference of the horizon are on the same side, add them; if they are on opposite sides, subtract the smaller from the larger and note the side of the remainder. Multiply the sum or remainder by the radius and divide by the chord of the altitude's complement; the arc of the result is the azimuth of the altitude and of the shadow at that time in that part. If the part is then between the ascendant and midheaven, the azimuth runs from the rising point of the first of Aries and Libra on the horizon circle to the side you found. He adds that the azimuth can also be found by another route, through the angles used for the parallax, which he will explain later in the book. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.38}.
