## Summary
Al-Battani explains that the figure from the third table is the equation of the anomaly and the figure from the fourth table is the equation of the elongation. If the corrected anomaly is less than a hundred and eighty degrees the composite equation is added to the Moon's mean place, and if it is more it is subtracted. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.112}.

## The fifth table and the addition
The fifth table gives the amount added to the single equation according to the minutes; the result is added to the single equation to give the composite, which is then added to or subtracted from the Moon's mean place according to position. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.112}.
