## Nature and temperament
Dioscorides says it is the gum of a tree called tragacantha, which the book has already described. Its nature is cold tending to dry. The editor's note gives the name as goat's thorn. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Properties and eye powders
Its force is that of gum, with drying close to that of gum; it enters eye powders as gum does. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}
