## The discourse on suppuration
And some people lay on the chest a rag dyed with red clay and watch which spot dries fastest — that is the place of the pus — so it is marked, then cauterised or lanced there; indeed it may not be cauterised but the side lanced with a lancet, the posture being set so that the matter comes out with it — and it is taken from it little by little each day without a great deal being drawn at once (a hand-work and an instrument's work on the book's wording, which the author reports of people) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.365}
