## Summary
Before applying the parallax the reckoner decides whether the Moon is above or below the ground: if the Moon's degree lies between the setting degree and the rising degree and on the side of mid-heaven, it is above the ground; if not, it is below. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.125}.

## The table for the sign
Once the Moon is known to be above the ground, the hours of its degree's distance from mid-heaven are entered in the table for the clime, and sought in the row of hours of the sign's table that contains the Moon, from the horizon; the next sign's table is then used. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.125}.
