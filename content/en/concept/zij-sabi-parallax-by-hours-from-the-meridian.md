## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the parallax in the tables varies with the distance, in equal hours, of the Moon's degree from the mid-heaven line. That distance is found in hours and entered into the parallax tables for the clime nearest the latitude of the place. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.124}.

## The direction of the hours
If the Moon lies to the west of the mid-heaven line the hours are sought after noon; if it lies to the east they are sought before noon, provided the hours are fewer than those in the last rows of the tables. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.124}.
