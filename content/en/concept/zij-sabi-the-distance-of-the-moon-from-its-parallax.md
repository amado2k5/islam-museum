## Summary
In chapter forty al-Battani gives the way to find the Moon's distance from the Earth from its parallax in the altitude circle, once that parallax is known, either by observation or by the tables: the parallax of the Moon in longitude and latitude is added to the Sun's parallax when taken from the tables. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.127}.

## Earth's half-radius as the unit
The half-radius of the Earth is taken as one part, so the distance comes out in those parts; it is first counted from the Earth's centre and then the half-radius is taken away to reach the distance from the Earth's surface. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.128}.
