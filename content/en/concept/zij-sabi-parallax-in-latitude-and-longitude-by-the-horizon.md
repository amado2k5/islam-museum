## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the parallax falls in altitude only, and not in longitude, when the setting part of the zodiac is opposite, that is half a circle away. It lies on the mid-heaven line only at the two solstitial points; elsewhere the parallax in latitude and longitude tilts according to the angle of the zodiac with the horizon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.115}.

## The ratio of the two angles
The ratio of the parallax in latitude to the parallax in longitude is the ratio of the chord of one angle to the chord of its complement to a right angle; the product of each of the two parallaxes with itself equals the product of the parallax between the zenith point and the Moon on the altitude circle with itself. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.115}.
