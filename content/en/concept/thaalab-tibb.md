## Actions and properties
It has a dissolving power, and its fur is the hottest of furs, of use to the moist for its dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}

## Joints
The book reports that when the fox is cooked in water and the painful joints are coated with it there is great benefit, and likewise the oil in which it is cooked alive, which is much stronger; it is better that this come after evacuation and purification, so that it does not draw a humour to the joints; likewise the fox's fat may draw something more than it dissolves; it may be cooked in oil alive or slaughtered, and whichever is used dissolves what is in the joints; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}

## Organs of the head
Its fat calms earache when dropped into the ear; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}

## Organs of the chest
Its dried lung is of great benefit to the asthmatic; the amount of the draught is left out; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.698}
