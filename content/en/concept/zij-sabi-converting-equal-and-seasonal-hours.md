## Summary
Al-Battani ends chapter 13 with the conversions: the day or night arc from the hour times by multiplying one hour's time by six for half the arc and doubling it, or by twelve directly; turning equal hours into seasonal ones by multiplying by fifteen and dividing by the time of the seasonal hour; and turning seasonal hours into equal ones by multiplying by the hour's time and dividing by fifteen. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.47}.

## The day or night arc from the hour times
He says: if you want the day arc from the hour times, or the night arc from them, whichever you wish, multiply the times of whichever you want by six; the result is half the arc of the day or the night, whichever you computed for; then double it and the result is its whole arc. If you multiply the parts of the times by twelve you get the amount of the arc of the day or night for the degree you computed it for. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.47}.

## From equal to seasonal, and back
Then he says: if you want to turn equal hours into seasonal ones, multiply the equal hours by fifteen and divide by the times of the hours of the day or the night, whichever you like; the result is seasonal hours of the night or day according to which those equal hours were. If you want to turn seasonal hours into equal ones, multiply the day's hours by the times of the day's hours and the night's hours by the times of the night's hours, divide what is gathered by fifteen, and the result is equal hours with their fractions if a fraction remains. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.47}.
