## Summary
Sha'rawi distinguishes worship by compulsion, which every created thing renders without choice, from worship of love, which God wants from jinn and humans, who come to Him by choice. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.80}

## The sphere of choice
Sha'rawi notes that the heartbeat and digestion are not under human choice, so the sphere of choice is whether one follows the method or leaves it. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.80}

## Slaves and servants
Sha'rawi separates "slaves" ('abid) from "servants" ('ibad), and holds that God addresses the commands of duty to believers who enter a faith covenant, while in the Hereafter all are compelled to obey. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.81} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.82}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's reading and is attributed to him. The scan gives no academic view on the distinction between compulsion and choice, and none has been checked.
