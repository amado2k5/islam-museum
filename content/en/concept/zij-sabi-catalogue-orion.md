## Orion (al-Jabbar)

The catalogue names the figure al-Jabbar, the giant (gigas). It lists the stars of the head, shoulder, the skin carried in the left hand, the belt, the sword and its hilt, and calls the bright star at the end of the left foot rijl al-Jawza', the foot of al-Jawza'. The editor notes that the name al-Miglad, the flail, for one star is restored by conjecture. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.168}
