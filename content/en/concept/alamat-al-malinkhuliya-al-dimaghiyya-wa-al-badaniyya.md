## What is particular to the brain
The sign of what is particular to the brain is excess of thought, constant obsession and a fixed gaze at one thing and at the ground; the colour of the head, the face and the eye points to it, as do the blackness and thickness of the hair of the head, earlier sleeplessness and thought, exposure to the sun and the like, brain illnesses that came before, that the signs he gives for the other organs sharing with the brain are not particularly present, that no benefit shows when that organ is treated and cleansed, and that the symptoms are very great {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}

## What comes by sharing of the whole body
What comes by sharing of the whole body shows as blackness of the body and its 'hulas' (as printed, not interpreted), the holding back of what used to be emptied from the spleen and the stomach, by the urine or from the anus, abundance of body hair and its intense blackness, and earlier use of bad, black-bile-making foods of the kind known from the second book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}

## What follows it
The illnesses that follow melancholia are such as the chronic and the mixed fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}

## Menses (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Among the signs of what comes by sharing of the whole body Ibn Sina lists the holding back of what used to be emptied by menstruation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}
