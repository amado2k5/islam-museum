## Summary
Al-Battani describes sighting the crescent with an instrument called dhat al-safaih. A pole is set up at a place with an open horizon, at the height of a man; a circle divided into degrees is drawn on it; a ruler or tube is fixed on the circle's centre according to the distance of the crescent's bearing from east or west; the instrument is then hung and the end of the ruler is raised. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.137}.

## The line of sight
He says the observer sees the crescent along the direction of the ruler or the tube, so that his line of sight runs from the place of sighting to the Moon on that bearing; this is a picture of the figure he described. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.138}.
