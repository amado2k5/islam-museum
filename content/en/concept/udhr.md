## Summary
Linguistically, udhr is the reason a person offers in his defence, and it means lifting blame {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11526}. It is related to rukhsa, a concession enacted for an excuse while the forbidding cause remains, and to afw, the pardoning of a fault {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11526} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11527}.

## Kinds of excuse
Excuses are specific or general. A specific excuse stays with a person, such as irregular bleeding and involuntary urinary flow, and its effect on purity and prayer is set out in detail {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11527} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11529} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11530}. A general excuse is passing, such as rain, cold or fear {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11531}.

## General ruling
Among the excuses that affect worship are the concessions of travel (shortening, combining, breaking the fast), wiping over leather socks, and dropping Friday prayer; and illness allows dry ablution, combining prayers, and leaving Friday and congregational prayer {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11532} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11533}. Coercion, ignorance, forgetting, madness, fainting, sleep, necessity, need and minority are further excuses, each treated in its place {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11533} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11535}. In contracts, inability to pay maintenance is not an excuse for the Hanafis but permits separation for the others; the delay in claiming pre-emption or returning a defective sale is allowed by recognised excuses; and a binding contract is dissolved when an excuse arises that makes its performance possible only with harm {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11536} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11537} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11538}. A person without the means is excused from fighting in jihad {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11538}.

## Where it is discussed
Each excuse is treated under its own heading: purity and prayer, Friday and congregational prayer, fasting, pilgrimage, retreat in the mosque, coercion, ignorance and forgetting, pre-emption and jihad {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11538}.
