## Nature
Some said it is al-'uruq, called mamiran, and others said the small is mamiran and the large zardjuq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Actions and properties
There is a small kind of it, hot and ulcerating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Organs of the head
Its root is chewed and calms toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Ocular organs
Its juice sharpens sight, the way of preparing it being left out; and when the young of the swallows go blind the mother brings this plant to it and it regains sight, and for that it is named al-khattafi; an account attributed to the book, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}
