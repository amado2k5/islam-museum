## Upbringing in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episodes 2, 4 and 5)
The Sheikh says families who worked for good in this world keep their children on the path, and that a child is shaped by his father's habits or by religion, according to which (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:05:00} He counts among upbringing teaching a child that lying, cheating and backbiting are forbidden, to love what is lawful and dislike what is forbidden, and to be courteous to people (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:58:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:05:30}
He advises a father not to rebuke his child before hearing him, not to treat him harshly, and says children are raised for their own time rather than their parents' time, and that a task is given to a child in stages (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:46:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:47:30}
He calls for prayer for children's righteousness (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:48:00} He holds that a child's rebellion may come from the parents' own sins (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:21:00}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)

The Sheikh says that some fathers forget their children when they are in the first year of primary school, then discover in the third or fourth year of university that the son or daughter is absent from them though physically present {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:38:30}.

He holds that the parents themselves sowed this enmity, and calls first for seeking God's help, citing a verse that speaks of spouses and children {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:39:00}.

He advises the father to pray two rak'as and ask God to turn his children's hearts to obedience and love of Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:39:30}. He advises the father to leave some space between himself and his son before commanding or forbidding him, when he knows the boy will refuse {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:39:30}.

He advises the father not to come with a command at the moment of the final match that a football-loving boy has waited for all year, but to let him enjoy it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:40:00}.

He tells of a father who went out with his five-year-old son to the zoo, where the boy kept asking about the animals {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:40:30}.

He says the father did not know the names of the animals his son asked about, and told his anxious wife to let the boy learn {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:41:00}. The Sheikh comments that this father is arrogant, and that if the boy is called rebellious it is in truth the father who is the rebel {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:41:00}.

He says that children love tenderness, and holds that pet names suit girls, but that boys should not be pampered with them {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:41:30}.

He holds there is no need to pamper a boy with pet names after puberty, in his twenties and thirties, whereas a woman likes to be pampered at any age {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:43:00}.

He advises that a boy of five or six be allowed to take part in the conversation when his father visits his paternal or maternal uncles {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:46:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 5)

The Sheikh says that raising a child not to cheat in an exam is swimming against the current; he may get lower marks because of it, but the Sheikh counts him as rewarded for it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:04:30}.

He notes that parents tell their children not to lie, cheat, backbite or flatter the manager, while a colleague draws close to the manager with praise and is promoted {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:06:00}.

He holds that a father who raises his son to be righteous has freed him from the restraint of leaning on the lowly ways of the world and raised him to the rank of the righteous {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:06:30}.

The Sheikh holds that a woman who wears the hijab or niqab is not restricted; she has boundaries by which she knows the permanent mahram, the temporary mahram and the stranger, whereas the restricted one is she whom any person may look at {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:07:00}.

He says that children think their parents restrict them with the hijab, when in fact the parents have released them from the restraints of this world, and that families bound together in this way are gathered in Paradise (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:07:30}.

He says that our sons' and daughters' rebellion against us comes from the abundance of our sins {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:21:30}. He says that the righteousness of offspring comes from God, and that the father's part is to feed them what is lawful {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:21:30}.

The Sheikh criticises the father who interrogates his daughter and son at home like a control officer at an airport, asking each of them where they are going and what they are doing {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:40:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 6)

The Sheikh says that in the 1950s, as a child, he would enter the mosque and find only elderly retirees in their seventies and eighties (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:10:30}.

He says that since the 1970s and 1980s the mosques, the two Harams and the study circles have been filled by young men and women, and he hopes the best of the community lies in them (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:11:00}.

He relates that when he looks around a university hall he sees nearly all the girls veiled and only two who are not, and he takes this as evidence that hijab is a matter of free choice with no compulsion in it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:11:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 7)

The Sheikh advises the father to be wise in raising his children, not to demand what he knows his children or wife will not do, and to keep a respectful space between himself and his daughter {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:32:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 10)

The Sheikh asks why failure pursues us in our relations with our wives {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:27:00}.

He holds that the cause of troubles with spouses and relatives is that each person puts the key in the wrong door, and had he put it in its right place it would open {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:27:30}. He adds, on visiting a wife's mother, that one must treat her kindly {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:27:30}.
