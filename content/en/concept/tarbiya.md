## Upbringing in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episodes 2, 4 and 5)
The Sheikh says families who worked for good in this world keep their children on the path, and that a child is shaped by his father's habits or by religion, according to which (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:05:00} He counts among upbringing teaching a child that lying, cheating and backbiting are forbidden, to love what is lawful and dislike what is forbidden, and to be courteous to people (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:58:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:05:30}
He advises a father not to rebuke his child before hearing him, not to treat him harshly, and says children are raised for their own time rather than their parents' time, and that a task is given to a child in stages (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:46:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:47:30}
He calls for prayer for children's righteousness (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:48:00} He holds that a child's rebellion may come from the parents' own sins (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:21:00}
