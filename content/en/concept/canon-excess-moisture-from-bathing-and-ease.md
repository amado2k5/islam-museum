## Summary
Ibn Sina says that too much bathing, too much food and drink and a life of ease can make a person feel a surplus of moisture in his limbs, especially the tongue, that harms their actions, and treats it with strong exercise and rough dry rubbing without oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.247}.

## The state and its remedy
From too much bathing, too much food and drink, and a life of ease, a person may feel in his limbs a surplus of moisture, above all in his tongue, so that it harms the actions of the organs. If it comes from an earlier cause, that belongs to particular medicine; if from something just listed, such as drinking, excess repose or strong moistening from the bath, they must be made to undertake strong exercise and rough dry rubbing without oil, or with a little hot oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.247}.
