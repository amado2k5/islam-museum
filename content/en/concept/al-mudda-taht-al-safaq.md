## Its first definition
This is pus held under the cornea, either deep or near, so that the cornea's spot resembles the pterygium; if a splinter corrodes along with it it is called qalqatan — (the end of what this packet covers of the chapter; the rest in the next packet) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.180}
