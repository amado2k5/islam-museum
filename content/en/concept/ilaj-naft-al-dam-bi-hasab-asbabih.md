## The treatment of the spitting of blood by its causes
The Slavs treat the blood-flow with a decoction of a root of this kind on the book's telling, and Galen treats the blood-flow with drugs of this kind and the sweet-smelling drugs, for they strengthen nature to begrudge the blood and to close the wound (reports on the book's wording; the drug and root names all excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.339}
