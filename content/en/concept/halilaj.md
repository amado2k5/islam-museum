## Nature and selection
Nature: Dioscorides says halilaj is well known and has many kinds: the unripe yellow, the black Indian that is fully ripe and fatter, the Kabuli, the largest of all, and the Chinese, which is fine and light. Choice: the best is the deep yellow tending to green, heavy, full and firm; the best Kabuli is the fatter and heavier kind that sinks in water; the best Chinese has a beak. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.447}
Temperament: it is said the yellow is hotter than the black, and the Indian is less cold than the Kabuli; all are cold in the first degree and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}

## Actions
All its kinds quench bile and help against it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: the black kind yellows the complexion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
Swellings and pustules: all the halilaj kinds help leprosy, as the book reports. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
Head: the Kabuli helps the senses, memory and the mind, and helps headache. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
Eyes: the yellow helps relaxed eyes and drives off the matter that runs into the eye. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
Digestive organs: it helps pain of the spleen and all the organs of digestion; the two black kinds strengthen the stomach, and the Kabuli helps dropsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
Excretory organs: the Kabuli and the Indian, fried in oil, bind the bowels; the yellow purges yellow bile, the black purges black bile and helps haemorrhoids; the Kabuli purges black bile and phlegm. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
Fevers: the Kabuli helps old fevers. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
