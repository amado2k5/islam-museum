## Nature and selection
Something like surinjan, brought from the regions of Ifriqiya, with which surinjan is adulterated. The editor's note calls it foal's hoof, a kind of plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it stirs desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.
