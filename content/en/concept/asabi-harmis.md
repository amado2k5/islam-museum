## Nature and selection
The book names it the fuqqah of surunjan {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}; the term surunjan is the book's own. The editor's note says surunjan is the game called the same in Egypt and the Berber game in the physicians' usage of Iraq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament or special action for this drug; it says its power is that of surunjan {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.
