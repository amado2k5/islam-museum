## Scorpius (al-'Aqrab)

The catalogue names the figure al-'Aqrab, the scorpion, and gives its red star qalb al-'aqrab, the heart of the scorpion, as the middle of three bright stars in the chest. Its stars are counted from the eyes down the vertebrae, and the section continues to the sting on the next page. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.161}
