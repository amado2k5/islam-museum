## Summary
Jews and Christians each told the Muslims to join their creed; the reply set the creed of Abraham, a hanif, as the origin, and said he was not one of the idolaters. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.605}

## The meaning of hanifiyya
Hanf means inclination. Sha'rawi holds that the messengers come only when crookedness has spread, so that to turn against the crookedness is straightness. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.606} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.607}

## Belief in what was revealed to the prophets
Sha'rawi reads the verse [[passage/quran-2-136|2:136]] as making the creed of Abraham belief in God alone, in what was revealed to us, to Abraham, Isma'il, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes, and in what was given to Moses, Jesus and the prophets, the sound message of God being one from Adam onward. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.608}

## Abraham as a Muslim in Sha'rawi's reading
Sha'rawi reads the end of [[passage/quran-2-136|2:136]] as saying that Abraham was a Muslim and that all the prophets were Muslims, and that Islam is the servant's surrender to his Lord in worship and in following His way. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.608} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.609}

## Limits of this batch
This is Sha'rawi's view in these pages. Other exegetes' readings of "hanif", the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.606}
