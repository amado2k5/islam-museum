## Nature
Twisted pieces, red and black, sharp in taste, of a good smell and light in weight, brought from the land of China; Masarjawayh said that it is khusrudaru itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Actions and properties
Subtle and dissolving winds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Cosmetics
It sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Organs of nourishment
Good for the stomach and digesting food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Excretory organs
It benefits colic and kidney pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it helps desire; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Substitutes
The book names as its substitute "qirfa qaranful", the two words printed side by side with no word joining them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}
