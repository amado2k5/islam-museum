## Overview
al-Qawiyy — The Strong. It is name number 53 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-11-66|11:66]]، [[passage/quran-28-26|28:26]]، [[passage/quran-42-19|42:19]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/muslim-2664|2664]].
