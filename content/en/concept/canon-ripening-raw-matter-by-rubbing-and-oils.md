## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that when the vessels are clean and the raw matter lies in the fat of the intestine, rubbing may ripen it, above all when the force of heating remedies is carried to it, and he names oils that serve for this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.244}.

## Rubbing and the oils
He says that when the vessels are clean and the raw matter is in the fat of the intestine, rubbing may ripen it. Among the useful oils he lists oil of gharb, oils of dill and chamomile, a decoction of beet root in oil in a double vessel, oil of marshmallow root, oil of the root of the squirting cucumber and oil of lichen, adding that every oil with lichen in it is good {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.244}.
