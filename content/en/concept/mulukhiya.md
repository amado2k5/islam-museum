## Nature and selection
It is the mallow, whose treatment is given in full in the chapter on kha under the mallow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.572}.

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and moist in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.572}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It opens obstructions of the liver, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.572}.
