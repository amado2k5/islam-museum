## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents the development of motivation theories in management — Theory X, then Y, then Z — and proposes his own 'Theory A' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:37:00}.

## Theory X and Theory Y
He explains that Taylor's philosophy rests on man as a machine fuelled by money — Theory X, which dominated for a time {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:30:00}.
Then Mayo argued that man has needs more important than money, like appreciation, security and belonging, producing the principle of 'man as a social being' — Theory Y {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:31:30}.

## Maslow's pyramid and Theory Z
He recounts that Maslow published the hierarchy of human needs in 1943, ordering man's needs from the physiological to self-actualisation — a development of Theory Y {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:32:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:34:00}.
In the 1970s American universities (among them San Diego, Michigan, Harvard, Stanford and Wisconsin) put forward Theory Z: human needs exist but differ from person to person, there is no single pyramid for all, and the successful leader leads each person according to his own pyramid {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:35:30}.

## His Theory A
Al-Suwaidan proposes a theory he calls 'Theory A': a return to the foundation — to creed rather than to mere ethics, because ethics has a creed and a philosophy behind it; he notes that the West preceded Muslims to books on ethical and religious management such as God is my CEO and Jesus is my boss (needs_source: the titles are as the speaker voiced them) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:36:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:37:00}.
