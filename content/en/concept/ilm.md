## Overview
The pursuit of knowledge, in the hadith of Abu Hurayra, the thirty-sixth of the Forty, is among the greatest paths that lead to Paradise: "Whoever takes a path seeking knowledge in it, God makes easy for him a path to Paradise." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## Gatherings of remembrance
The hadith links the pursuit of knowledge with gatherings of remembrance in the houses of God, where the Book is recited and studied. Tranquillity descends upon those who attend, mercy covers them, the angels surround them, and God mentions them among those with Him. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## The end of the hadith
The hadith ends by saying that it is deeds that save, not lineage: "Whoever is held back by his deeds, his lineage will not hasten him." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## From the lectures: happiness in beneficial knowledge
The speaker says that happiness is not in gathering wealth, but in God giving a person knowledge from which he benefits {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep04 00:30:30}.
He urges respect for people of knowledge and says that whoever humbles himself before God is raised by Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep04 00:32:00}.

## Knowledge in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episodes 1, 2, 3 and 9)
The Sheikh says the seeker of knowledge meets three obstacles: first lying, second envy, which closes the way between people and knowledge, and third the world filling the heart (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:04:30}
He says the heart needs a lesson of knowledge every three days, and that when the heart abandons the lesson the limbs disobey and the world overruns it (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:25:30} He holds that gatherings of knowledge are good, and that obedience in them may bring God's pleasure, so a believer should not despise any of it (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:03:30}
He says whoever talks much about what he does not know is not learned (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:25:30} He says the one who sits in a circle of knowledge is better than the angels, because he struggles against himself (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:42:00}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 3)

The Sheikh criticises delving into knowledge that brings no benefit, and gives as an example the search for the name of the wolf that did not eat Yaqub's son, which he sees as entering a subject with nothing to gain from it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:26:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 7)

The Sheikh says that a person in a circle of knowledge is like a fish in water: his life is secure while he is in his natural setting, and if he is far from it destruction is nearer {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:43:30}. He asks how the heart is nourished in such gatherings {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:43:30}.

He refers to the saying that the companion of such righteous sitters never comes to grief (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:46:30}. He holds that the Muslim who sits in a circle of knowledge ranks above the angels, since the angels' nourishment is glorification, declaring God's oneness and sanctification, which they never tire of {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:46:30}.

He says the believer resembles the angels in prayer, and links the angels' wings, in twos, threes and fours, with the rak'as of the five prayers (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:47:00}. He holds that outside prayer he surpasses the angels by sitting and listening to what is good while mercies descend {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep07 00:47:00}.
