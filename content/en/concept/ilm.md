## Summary
In the lexicon, ilm is perception, cognition, awareness, mastery and certainty. As a term it is the occurrence of the form of a thing in the mind, or an attribute that makes its bearer distinguish between meanings {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11797}.

## Its kinds
The speculative theologians divide it into eternal knowledge, which is God's, and created knowledge, which is human and is either necessary or acquired {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11798} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11799}.

## The duty to learn
Each person must learn what he needs of religion, such as the creed, purification, prayer and fasting, and a trader must learn the rules of sale. The sciences that sustain the community's religion and worldly life, such as the Qur'an, hadith and their principles, language, arithmetic and medicine, are a communal duty {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11799} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11800}.

## Recommended, forbidden and disliked knowledge
Wide study of the religious sciences and their tools is recommended. Sorcery, soothsaying, divination and some astrology are forbidden; excessive poetry is disliked, and poetry without frivolity is allowed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11800} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11801}.

## Where to look further
Music is treated under «listening, singing and instruments» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11800}.
