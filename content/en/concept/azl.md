## Summary
In the lexicon, azl means removing someone or setting him aside from a post {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11579}.

## Dismissal of the imam and the minister
The supreme imamate is established by the allegiance of those who bind and loose, and it lasts while its conditions last {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11579}. Most jurists allow the imam to resign, while one view forbids it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11579}. A minister may not dismiss himself, but the imam may dismiss the minister he appointed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11579}.

## Dismissal of the judge
A judge may resign from his office {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11580}. According to the jurists, his office does not end at the death of the imam who appointed him {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11580}. On whether the imam may dismiss him, the jurists give three views: that he may dismiss him absolutely, that he may do so only for reasons, and that he may not do so at all {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11581}.

## Grounds on which a judge loses office
A judge loses office when a condition of fitness is lost, such as madness, fainting, blindness, deafness, muteness, apostasy or wrongdoing, and the schools differ in the detail {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11582} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11583} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11584}. According to the majority, a dismissal takes no effect until the judge knows of it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11585} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11586}.

## Dismissal of agents, guardians and others
The dismissal of an agent, guardian, partner in a profit-sharing contract, surety and administrator of an endowment is treated under each contract {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11586} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11587} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11588}.

## Withdrawal from a wife or slave-woman
Withdrawal (azl), meaning withdrawing before ejaculation, is permitted by the majority with a slave-woman. For a free wife the jurists differ: some permit it absolutely, others require her consent, and they list excuses that make it lawful {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11588} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11589}.
