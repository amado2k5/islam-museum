## Summary
In his commentary on al-Baqara 2:228-232, Sha'rawi reads divorce in stages, once and then twice, as giving the couple room to mend. He holds that returning after the waiting period needs a new contract and a new dower and the woman's consent. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1003}

## Before Islam
Sha'rawi describes the condition of women in the jahiliyya: a man divorced his wife and took her back again and again without limit, leaving her suspended for months, and a widow was secluded after her husband's death. He says Islam settled this disorder. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1001} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1002}

## Divorce once and twice
Sha'rawi holds that the wisdom in divorce being once or twice is that a couple who failed the first time may succeed the second, so Allah gave time to correct the first mistake. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1003}

## Return after the waiting period
If the waiting period has ended and the spouses wish to return, this happens only through a new contract and a new dower. Sha'rawi notes that relatives may block the return out of fear of repetition, and says that once consent is reached no one should stand in its way. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1003} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1004}

## The woman's consent
Sha'rawi notes that the verse attributes the marriage to the women ('that they marry their husbands'), which implies the woman's consent to returning, so a man cannot divorce her and then give her no say in the return. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1003}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
Omar Abdelkafy holds that the frequent divorces that ruin homes come from patience evaporating between spouses, and that the 'shells' of divorce and of demands for it are launched from tongues once patience is gone; he advises a husband provoked by his wife in a moment of anger to be patient and not divorce; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep09 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep09 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep09 00:06:00}
