## Summary
"Khwarazmshah" was the title of the kings of Khwarazm. Shohruxmirzo Ismoilov reports from al-Biruni that 22 Afrighid rulers bore it, and notes that four houses are known to have held it between the fourth and early thirteenth centuries: the Afrighids, the Ma'munids, the Altuntashids and the Anushtegins. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 9} {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 32}

## The title on coins
- On the old Khwarazmian coins the king's name stands with a phrase read as "ruler king" in Old Khwarezmian script; the word Khwarazmshah does not appear. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 33}
- The first coin to write "Khwarazmshah" in Arabic is the dirham of the Afrighid Ahmad ibn Muhammad of 348/959; on his fulus the titles "shah" and "Khwarazmshah" alternate. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 10} {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 49}
- On Ma'munid coins the title is joined to others such as "the just king" and "the noble king". {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 73} {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 76}
- The Oghuz ruler Shah Malik struck dirhams as Khwarazmshah in 1041–1043, so the author counts five houses that held the title. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 80} {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 224}
- The Altuntashids, installed by Mahmud of Ghazna after 1017, have left no known coins. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 11}

## Decline of the title
The author says the title Khwarazmshah appears on no Anushtegin ruler's coin after Il-Arslan: rulers were styled "sultan" because the state had outgrown the geographical limits of Khwarazm, and the title was now given at a provincial level to governing princes. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 117} The title "sultan" itself first appears on Khwarazmian coins under Atsiz. {cite:ismoilov-xorazmshohlar-tangalari-2025 p. 98}
