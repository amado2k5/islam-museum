## Summary
Al-Battani multiplies the returned chord of half the star's day arc by the chord of its altitude at the time of measurement, divides by the chord of its altitude at midheaven, and takes the result from the returned chord of its day arc; the arc of the remainder is the returned arc. If the star is east of midheaven the arc is taken from its half day arc, and if west it is added, so the result is the part of the sphere that has turned since the star's rising until the time of measurement. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.55}.

## The returned chord
The returned chord is the one explained earlier in the chapter on chords, namely what remains of the radius beyond the chord of the complement of the arc, and for al-Battani it is the instrument for computing the hour angle. The wording is a little disturbed in the scan, so the steps are given in the order in which they read. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.55}.
