## Overview
Muhammad Farid Wajdi relays — without naming them — from Muslim scholars that the imitator's faith is not accepted, since imitation can be of the false as of the true; he holds Islam charged every obligated person to use his reason in inference and deduction, guarding him from the rigidity that men of whims exploit {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 34} {cite:muhimmat-al-islam-azhar p. 35}. This report is attributed to the author.
