## Summary
Naw' in the lexical sense is a kind or class; al-Jurjani defined it as a term for many different things by their individuals. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16665}

## Its effect on zakat
If livestock is of one kind, zakat is taken from that kind; most jurists allow substituting a sheep for a goat and the reverse, taking value into account, while the Hanafis forbid it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16666}
If the kinds differ, the Hanbalis and the sounder Shafi'i view let the owner give what he chooses, proportioned by value, while the Hanafis join the kinds together. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16666} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16667}

## Crops and money
Kinds of fruit and crops are joined to complete the threshold; joining gold and silver is disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16667}
