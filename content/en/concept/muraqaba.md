## Summary
In the Sheikh's account, watchfulness means that the heart watches over its owner: whoever does not have a watchful heart gains nothing from being watched by others. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:19:00}

## The watchful heart
The Sheikh says that whoever does not have a watchful heart gains nothing from others watching him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:19:00}

## The story of the three sons
He tells of a righteous imam with three sons, to each of whom he gave a rooster to slaughter where no one could see. The first two slaughtered theirs in a closed room; the third brought his back unslaughtered and said he knew God was with him when the doors were shut (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:18:00}

## The story of the shepherd
He tells the story of Umar and a shepherd, in which a question is asked of the shepherd: where is God (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:18:30}

## The heart and the limbs
He compares the heart to a chief executive under whom the limbs work as staff: when the heart is full of faith and certainty, all actions are set right. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:02:30}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 5)

The Sheikh invites a person to measure himself against the fault he sees in another before judging him, so that he finds his own faults larger than the other's {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:15:00}.

He gives the example of someone who hears his daughter or sister complain about her husband and leans toward taking her side, and advises him to look at himself before judging: if the husband raises his voice a little, he thinks the world has fallen apart, though his own mother and grandmother were patient and he may himself be falling short at home {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:15:30}.

He relays that wisdom can be learned even from the ignorant, by avoiding any act, word or position of theirs that one does not approve of {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:16:00}.

He calls for the restraint to come from within the person, asks whether the endowments authority or the ministry will send a sheikh to every home to remind people of God, and says a person should make himself his own watcher {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:55:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh makes watchfulness the second stage of a daily programme after setting conditions for the limbs at dawn prayer: one watches one's limbs through the day, whether the eye was lowered and whether the lips closed over the tongue so it spoke no evil; then come taking account and reproach. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep17 00:07:00} See: [[concept/muhasabat-al-nafs|taking account of oneself]].
