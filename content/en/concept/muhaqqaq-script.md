## Summary
Muhaqqaq is one of the [[concept/al-aqlam-al-sitta|six pens]], paired with [[concept/rayhan-script|rayhan]], its smaller variant. Maryam Ekhtiar notes that with [[concept/thuluth-script|thuluth]] it suited large Qur'ans. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 25} {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 26}

## The Met's example
The Met holds the colophon of the [[object/anonymous-baghdad-quran|"Anonymous Baghdad Qur'an"]], written in muhaqqaq in 707 AH/1307–8 CE by [[person/ahmad-al-suhrawardi|Ahmad ibn al-Suhrawardi al-Bakri]], pupil of [[person/yaqut-al-mustasimi|Yaqut al-Musta'simi]]; it is a large thirty-part Qur'an made at Baghdad in the Ilkhanid period. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 37}
