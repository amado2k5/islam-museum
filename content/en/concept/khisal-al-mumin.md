## Summary
The traits of the believer are the qualities with which the Qur'an and the hadith describe the one who believes: what is in his heart, what passes over his tongue, and how he treats people. They are not a single list confined to one text; they are scattered passages: verses that begin by describing the believers and then count their deeds, and hadiths that give the believer a parable or say what he is and what he is not. The Pillars and Branches of Faith Browser (the pillars of Islam, the pillars of faith, the branches of faith and the traits of the believer) gathers these passages, together with what is reported from the Companions and the Followers in the biographical and ascetic books.

## The trait and the branch
In his commentary on the hadith of the branches of faith, al-Nawawi says that the branch is a piece of a thing, and that the meaning of the hadith is "some seventy traits" {cite:sharh-sahih-muslim-al-nawawi p.116}. In this sense the traits of the believer are the [[concept/shuab-al-iman|branches of faith]] themselves, seen from the side of the one who holds them. Ibn Rajab, in his commentary on the hadith "Islam is built on five", says that the five are like the pillars and supports of a building, and that "the remaining traits of Islam are like the completion of the building", so that if any of them is missing the building falls short while still standing {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.122}.

## In the Qur'an
The browser's Qur'an table places under this heading the passages that describe the believers in a continuous series:
- The opening of Surat al-Mu'minun, [[passage/quran-23-1|al-Mu'minun 1]] to [[passage/quran-23-11|al-Mu'minun 11]]: humility in prayer, turning away from idle talk, paying the zakat, guarding chastity, keeping trusts and covenants, and being constant in the prayers.
- [[passage/quran-8-2|al-Anfal 2]] to [[passage/quran-8-4|al-Anfal 4]]: hearts that tremble at the mention of God, faith that grows when His verses are recited, trust in Him, establishing prayer, and spending.
- The qualities of the servants of the Merciful, from [[passage/quran-25-63|al-Furqan 63]] to [[passage/quran-25-76|al-Furqan 76]]: walking gently on the earth, answering the ignorant with forbearance, standing in prayer at night, moderation in spending, avoiding polytheism, killing and fornication, and not bearing false witness.
- [[passage/quran-9-71|al-Tawba 71]] and [[passage/quran-9-112|al-Tawba 112]]: the believing men and women as protectors of one another, enjoining what is right and forbidding what is wrong, repentance, worship and praise.
- [[passage/quran-33-35|al-Ahzab 35]]: ten qualities in which men and women are named together, namely submission, faith, devout obedience, truthfulness, patience, humility, giving in charity, fasting, guarding chastity and remembering God often.
- [[passage/quran-49-10|al-Hujurat 10]] and [[passage/quran-49-15|al-Hujurat 15]]: the brotherhood of the believers, and the certainty in which there is no doubt.
- [[passage/quran-70-22|al-Ma'arij 22]] to [[passage/quran-70-35|al-Ma'arij 35]], [[passage/quran-42-36|al-Shura 36]] to [[passage/quran-42-39|al-Shura 39]], [[passage/quran-13-20|al-Ra'd 20]] to [[passage/quran-13-22|al-Ra'd 22]], and [[passage/quran-3-134|Al 'Imran 134]] and [[passage/quran-3-135|135]].

The text of each verse is in its record, taken from the Tanzil text, and is not written here. The choice of these passages is the browser's own work, and it can grow.

## Parables of the believer in the hadith
The hadith compares the believers in their closeness to a building: "The believer to the believer is like a building, each part strengthening the other" {cite:sahih-muslim 2585}; and to a body: "The parable of the believers in their mutual love, mercy and compassion is that of the body: when one limb complains, the rest of the body responds with sleeplessness and fever" {cite:sahih-muslim 2586}. Al-Bukhari reports it from al-Nu'man ibn Bashir in the wording "You see the believers in their mercy, love and compassion for one another like the body" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 6011}.

## How the hadith describes the believer
- Gratitude and patience: "How wondrous is the affair of the believer, for all of his affair is good": if prosperity touches him he is grateful, and if hardship touches him he is patient {cite:sahih-muslim 2999}. This links the heading to the records on [[concept/shukr|gratitude]] and [[concept/sabr|patience]].
- Strength and eagerness for what benefits: "The strong believer is better and more beloved to God than the weak believer, and in each there is good" {cite:sahih-muslim 2664}.
- Love of God and His Messenger, and a person's love for God: "Three things, whoever has them finds the sweetness of faith" {cite:sahih-muslim 0043}; al-Bukhari has it from Anas {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 16}.
- Wishing good for others: "None of you believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself" {cite:sahih-muslim 0045} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 13}.
- Guarding the tongue and honouring the neighbour and the guest: "Whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let him speak good or keep silent", followed by the honouring of the neighbour and of the guest {cite:sahih-muslim 0047}.

## How the hadith describes the Muslim
The hadith says: "The Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand the Muslims are safe, and the emigrant is the one who abandons what God has forbidden" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 10}. It says: "The Muslim is the brother of the Muslim: he does not wrong him and does not abandon him", and goes on to mention meeting a brother's need, relieving his distress and covering his faults {cite:sahih-muslim 2580}. The browser places these hadiths under this heading because they define the person of faith by his traits, not by his rites.

## Traits of the heart in al-'Ayni
When al-'Ayni counted the branches of faith, he made most of the section on belief into traits of the heart: sincerity, repentance, fear, hope, gratitude, faithfulness, patience, humility, mercy, contentment with the decree, trust in God, and then leaving vanity, envy, rancour, anger, deceit and love of the world {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p.212}. Many of these traits have a record in the museum: [[concept/taqwa|taqwa]], [[concept/tawakkul|trust in God]], [[concept/amana|trustworthiness]], [[concept/haya|modesty]] and [[concept/mumin|the believer]].

## Reports of the Companions and the Followers
The section "In the other books" shows short excerpts from the biographical and ascetic books that have been gathered, such as Hilyat al-Awliya', Sifat al-Safwa, the two Kitab al-Zuhd of Ahmad and Ibn al-Mubarak, the Tabaqat and Hayat al-Sahaba, where these books give a description, a parable or the traits of the believer on the tongues of the Companions and the Followers. These excerpts are machine-read from scanned copies and not corrected, and each excerpt has its own record, source and page.
