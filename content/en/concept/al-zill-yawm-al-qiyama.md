## Overview
The Shade on the Day is God's honour for His servants: He shades them in His shade on the day of no shade but His {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## In the hadith
The two Sahihs record from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet said: seven God shades in His shade on the day of no shade but His — a just ruler; a youth raised in God's worship; a man whose heart hangs on the mosques; two who love each other for God, meeting and parting on it; a man called by a woman of rank and beauty who says I fear God; a man who gives charity so secretly his left hand does not know what his right spends; and a man who remembers God alone and his eyes overflow {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
