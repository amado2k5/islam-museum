## Choice
The freshly picked of every kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree, moist in the first, and it is said that its heat is greater than its moisture; its kinds are not all equal, but the sweeter a kind, the hotter it is {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Actions and properties
The blood engendered from it is quick to putrefy and poor, and almond and jallanjabin set it right, with lettuce taken beforehand and vinegar and sikanjabin at the end {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.664-665}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is beneficial for the cold stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}

## Respiratory organs
It harms the larynx and the voice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}

## Excretory organs
It softens the stool {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it adds to the substance of semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}
