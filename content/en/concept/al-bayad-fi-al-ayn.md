## Its two kinds and their arising
Of the whiteness in the eye, the thin occurring on the outer surface is called ghamam, and the thick is called whiteness absolutely; both arise upon the healing of an ulcer or pustule that burst and healed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.184}

## The treatment
The thin one occurring in soft bodies must be kept steamed with hot waters and bathed with hot water, then licking remedies are always used. The chronic thick one occurring in thick bodies must be softened by the said steamings and baths, and the said collyria applied as kohl are triturated in rose-water or dissolved Andarani salt-water and applied in the bath; if the baths fail, collyriation with stronger ones is used, and dyes dyeing the whiteness may be used ⁦(the juice, kohl, collyrium and dye names and their recipes and measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.184} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.185}
