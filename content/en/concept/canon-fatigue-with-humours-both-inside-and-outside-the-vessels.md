## Summary
When the two are equal Ibn Sina says to aim first at digestion with the pepper preparation, to move on to pure pennyroyal once what is in the vessels is digested, to avoid vomiting and purging before thinning, cutting and ripening, and exercise, and then to return gradually to habit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.249}.

## The order
If the two are equal, you aim first at digestion with the pepper preparation; if you wish you add fatarasaliyun by the weight of aniseed to make it more diuretic, or mix in a little pennyroyal while lessening the cumin or pepper preparation he drinks, until at the end pure pennyroyal remains, when what is in the vessels has been digested and the care of what lies outside them remains. Pennyroyal is useful for this kind and harmful for the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.249}.

## Caution, then return to habit
For those in whom both are present, we should keep from them whatever draws strongly outward or inward; so do not hurry to make them vomit or purge until thinning, cutting and ripening have come first, and do not exercise them. When the fatigue has settled, the colour is good and the urine is ripened, rub them much, exercise them lightly and try; if any of the illness returns, leave off, and if it does not, carry them on toward their habit, gradually, until they reach their due of bathing, anointing, rubbing and exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.249}.
