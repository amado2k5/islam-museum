## Summary
Ibn Sina closes the third chapter by comparing the ways of evacuating: purging draws from above and uproots from below, so it suits both the opposite and the like kind of drawing; vomiting draws and uproots the other way round; the effect of venesection varies with the place from which blood is taken; and those who least need evacuation are those with good food and good digestion and the people of hot countries {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.276}.

## The three ways
Purging draws from above and uproots from below, so it suits both the opposite and the like kind of drawing, and suits also once the matters have settled; when the matters are below, it draws them to the opposite side and also uproots them where they are. Vomiting does the drawing and the uprooting the other way round, and venesection differs according to the place from which blood is taken, as already explained. The people least in need of evacuation are those whose food and digestion are good, and those of hot countries need it little {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.276}.
