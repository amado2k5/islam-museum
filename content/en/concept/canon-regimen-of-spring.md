## Summary
Ibn Sina opens the fifth teaching, on transitions, with a single chapter on the regimen of the seasons and begins with spring: venesection and purging early in the season according to need and habit, vomiting in particular, giving up the meats and drinks that heat and moisten, lightening the diet, and exercise moderate yet above that of summer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.

## What is done in spring
In the early days of spring he hastens to venesection and purging according to what is required and to habit, uses vomiting especially, gives up everything that heats and moistens greatly among meats and drinks, and lightens his food, taking moderate exercise above the exercise of summer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.

## Food and drink
He does not fill himself with food but spreads it out, uses quenching drinks and fruit syrups, and gives up what is hot and everything bitter, pungent and salty {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.
