## Difference from fainting
In sum, the difference between the one in coma and the one who has fainted from weakness of the heart is that the pulse of the one in coma is stronger and more like the pulse of the healthy, and the pulse of the one who has fainted is weaker and harder; the faint comes on little by little with a change of colour to yellowness and to the colour of the dead and coldness of the extremities {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93} In coma the colour of the face does not change except to something better, the patch of the face and the nose does not grow thin, and it does not change from the look of sleepers except by the slightest flush and swelling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}

## How coma extends
Coma may extend, and entry into its depth is gradual, beginning with a heavy sleep, unless its cause is a cold that strikes at once or a medicine drunk, which does that outright (qat'an, as printed) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}

## Womb (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina gives the difference between the one in coma and the woman whose womb is suffocated: the one in coma can understand and speak with effort, while she understands with difficulty and does not speak at all; movement, especially of the neck, head and leg, is easier for the one in coma, and sensation and opening the eyelids are easier for the woman whose womb is suffocated; the suffocation of the womb is a cause that falls at once, spends its power, and passes or kills {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}
