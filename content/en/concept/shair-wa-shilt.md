## Nature
Well known; shilt is a kind without husk, and its action is close to its action {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Temperament
Cold and dry in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Actions and properties
It has a cleansing, its nourishment is less than that of wheat, and barley water is stronger than its sawiq; both break the sharpness of humours, the barley water of shilt is moister, and all barley water is beneficial {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Cosmetics
A hot application of it is used on freckles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Swellings and pustules
A porridge-like cooking of it in water with pitch and resin is made as a poultice on hard swellings, and it is used alone, and with its kashk on hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Ulcers
Smeared with strong vinegar and set as a poultice on ulcerated scab it heals it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Joints
As a poultice with quince and vinegar on gout, it prevents the flow of superfluities to the joints {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Organs of the chest
Its water benefits diseases of the chest, and drunk with fennel seed it makes milk abundant; its flour is used as a poultice; and the book mentions iklil al-malik and poppy husk for pain in the side {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its water is bad for the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Excretory organs
Its sawiq binds the belly, while the decoction of its sawiq and its kashk promotes urine, and the water of the kashk of wheat promotes it more strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Fevers
Its water cools and moistens fevers, plain for the hot ones and with celery and fennel for the cold ones; and that cooked with figs is also given, mixed with qaratin water, for phlegmatic fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}
