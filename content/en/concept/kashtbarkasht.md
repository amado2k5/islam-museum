## Nature
It looks like threads wound around one another, mostly up to five, on a single root, dark to yellowish, with no strong taste. Some say it is badshakan; others say its strength is that of badshakan, and this is held to be the more correct view. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.522}

## Temperament and properties
Hot and dry in the second degree. Its property: very fine. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.522}
