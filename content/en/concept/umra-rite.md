## Summary
In the lexicon, 'umra is a visit; as a term it is circling the House and running between Safa and Marwa in the state of ihram {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11821}.

## Ruling
A confirmed sunna for the Malikis and Hanafis; once-in-a-lifetime obligation for the Shafi'is and Hanbalis in the sounder view {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11821}.

## Forms
It may be performed alone (ifrad), combined with the pilgrimage (tamattu'), or in one ihram with it (qiran); the majority hold that the one who combines needs one circuit and one running {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11821} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11822} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11823}.

## Pillars and obligations
The majority count three pillars: ihram, circumambulation and running; the Shafi'is add shaving; the Hanafis count only circumambulation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11826} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11828} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11829}. Running and shaving or clipping are obligatory according to the majority {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11830}.

## Recommended and disliked
It is recommended in Ramadan and repeating it is encouraged; the Malikis dislike repeating it in the months of pilgrimage {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11831} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11832} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11833}.

## Invalidation
It is invalidated by omitting a pillar or by a voiding act before release; the compensation is disputed, and it may be performed on behalf of another person according to the majority {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11834} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11835}.

## Where to look further
Pilgrimage is treated under «hajj», ihram under «ihram», circumambulation under «tawaf» and running under «sa'y» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11829} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11830}.
