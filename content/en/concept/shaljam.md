## Nature
Dioscorides said: it is wild and garden; the wild is a plant with many branches, about a cubit in height, growing in ruins, smooth at the tip, with smooth leaves as wide as a thumb or a little more, and fruit in pods like the broad bean holding small black seeds, white inside when broken; there is another kind of it that nourishes less than the one mentioned {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Temperament
Both are hot in the second degree and moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Actions and properties
Galen said: eating it cooked well nourishes thickly and much, and constant eating of it generates blockages and winds; that cooked in water and salt nourishes less, and the best is that cooked with fat meat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Poisons and bites
Dioscorides reports that for one who has drunk its seed beforehand, deadly drugs are made void {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.679}

## Cosmetics
If a shaljam is taken and burnt, and wax is melted in its hollow with rose oil over hot ash, it is beneficial for long-standing alopecia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Ulcers
The same operation benefits ulcerated cracks arising from cold, and the cooked shaljam does the same as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Organs of the chest
Cooked with fat meat it softens the throat and chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Cooked with meat likewise it nourishes much and warms the kidneys, and it is slow in the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Joints
Its decoction poured on gout is of great benefit, and that cooked with meat warms the back {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Ocular organs
It is said that taking shaljam, cooked or raw, benefits sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Excretory organs
Its water promotes urine, that cooked with meat promotes urine, and eating the leaf of shaljam promotes urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its substance generates semen, that cooked with meat it stirs desire, that its seed likewise moves the desire for intercourse, and that cooked in water and salt it stirs desire less {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.680}
