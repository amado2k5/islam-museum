## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the highest bliss of paradise is God's good pleasure and the vision of Him, that paradises have degrees, and that each worshipper's reward follows his movement and intention {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1326} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1327}.

## Worship for God's own sake
He says that whoever worships God for blessing receives the blessing, while whoever worships Him because He is worthy of worship is given the joy of gazing on Him. Love of God for His own sake is higher than seeking bliss {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1327} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1328}.

## The saying of Rabi'a
He reports that Rabi'a al-Adawiyya worshipped God because He is worthy of worship, not from fear of the fire or hope of paradise, without reproducing her verses {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1327}.

## The hadith
He cites the hadith of Anas on three signs by which one finds the sweetness of faith, and takes it as proof of faith's perfection {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1328}.
