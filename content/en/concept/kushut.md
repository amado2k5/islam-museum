## Nature and temperament
A thing that twines round thorns and trees, resembling Meccan fibre, without leaves; it has small white flowers with bitterness and astringency, and its dominant substance is bitter. Its nature: slightly hot at the start of the first degree and dry at the end of the second, with opposing powers. The editor's note gives the name as aftimiyun. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.522}

## Properties and digestion
Cleansing; it draws fine humours out of the veins and sits heavily in the stomach because of its astringency; it clears the veins and expels the fine humours in them. Organs of digestion: it strengthens the stomach, especially the fried kind, opens the blockages of the liver and the stomach, and strengthens both. Its water is remarkable for jaundice, and its wild juice, crushed and sprinkled on a drink, strengthens a weak stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.522}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): the book says it keeps filth away from the belly of the foetus by cleansing the veins; that it brings on urine and the menses, helps colic, and when taken it checks bleeding; and that the fried kind binds and clears a discharge from the womb. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.522}

## Fevers
The book says its seed and its water help old fevers greatly, as tested. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.522}
