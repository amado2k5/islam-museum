## Definition
Isti'dhan is the Islamic etiquette of entering homes: no one enters a house without permission and a greeting. God sent down «believers, do not enter houses other than your own until you ask leave and greet their people», and commanded boys and servants to ask permission at the three times of undress: before dawn, at midday and after the evening prayer. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## The wisdom of asking leave
The Prophet taught that the eye is the permission: a caller asks leave three times, then is either admitted or turns back. Its manners include standing aside from the door rather than facing it, and greeting before asking. With these manners Islam guarded the privacy of homes and the dignity of their people, making it one of the finest Muslim social customs. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:al-adab-al-mufrad}
