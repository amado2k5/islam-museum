## Venus
The text gives Venus's apparent diameter as thirty-two minutes and twenty-seven seconds of arc, and its bulk as about one part in thirty-six of the Earth. Its size changes between its distances, and the text refers back to what was said of the diameters of the epicycle. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.187-188}

## Reading the fractions
The seconds and fractions are read from the scan and need checking in the original. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.187-188}
