## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an mentions that angels called out to Zakariya in the prayer niche with tidings of Yahya [[passage/quran-3-39|Al 'Imran 39]], and tells his prayer and God's answer with a child in Maryam [[passage/quran-19-17|19:17]] and al-Anbiya' [[passage/quran-21-90|21:90]].

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir interprets that the angels were those who gave Zakariya tidings of Yahya, noting 'the angels called him' in the niche [[passage/quran-3-40|Al 'Imran 40]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; in Qisas al-Anbiya' he notes Zakariya prayed to his Lord and He answered him with a child {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-farmawi-1997}.

## Notes
Some held 'the angels' in the verse are God's messengers to Zakariya, others that they were men in angelic form; the primary view takes them as angels {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
