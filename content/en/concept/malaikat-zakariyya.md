## Overview
Zakariya's angels are those who called to him in the mihrab with the glad tidings of Yahya, as in [[passage/quran-3-39|Al 'Imran 39]], [[passage/quran-19-2|Maryam 2]], [[passage/quran-21-89|Al-Anbiya' 89]], and [[passage/quran-21-90|Al-Anbiya' 90]]. They are among the clearest of what came of meetings of angels with humans in the Qur'an {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## The story in the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-3-39|Al 'Imran 39]]: "So Zakariya called upon his Lord and said: My Lord, grant me from You good offspring; indeed You are the Hearer of supplication" — and in it Zakariya's calling upon his Lord with supplication {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-3-40|Al 'Imran 40]]: "So the angels called to him while he was standing in prayer in the mihrab" — and in it the angels' calling to him while he was standing in prayer in the mihrab {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-3-41|Al 'Imran 41]]: "He said: My Lord, indeed my bones have weakened" — and in it the reason for his incapacity {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-19-2|Maryam 2]] to [[passage/quran-19-15|Maryam 15]]: the story of Zakariya in surah Maryam, and in it his coming to Maryam with mercy {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-21-89|Al-Anbiya' 89]] and [[passage/quran-21-90|Al-Anbiya' 90]]: "But when he came to him, his Lord called to him: O Zakariya, We give you glad tidings of a son whose name is Yahya" — and in it the glad tidings of Yahya expressly {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In hadith
- In al-Bukhari's narration that Zakariya asked his Lord to grant him a son, and that God gave him glad tidings of Yahya {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- In Muslim's narration that Jibril descended upon Zakariya in the mihrab and gave him glad tidings of Yahya {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- In a hadith that Zakariya said "My Lord, my bones have weakened" and God gave him glad tidings of Yahya {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## What they announced
- "A son named Yahya" in [[passage/quran-21-90|Al-Anbiya' 90]] — that is, Yahya son of Zakariya {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- And in tafsir "the angels" in [[passage/quran-3-40|Al 'Imran 40]] are Jibril and those with him {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- And in a narration they gave him glad tidings of Yahya "and the prophet" after him {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that the angels called to Zakariya in the mihrab with the glad tidings of Yahya, and mentions that "the angels called to him" in [[passage/quran-3-40|Al 'Imran 40]] is the calling of Jibril and those with him {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. And al-Qurtubi held that the angels descended upon Zakariya in human form {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## Were they angels or humans?
- **The most famous view**: they are angels, because the Qur'an called them "the angels" in [[passage/quran-3-40|Al 'Imran 40]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- **Another view**: they are humans carrying a message from God, and in it is a problem {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02}.
- And the principle in tafsir is that they are angels because God sends to the prophets only from the angels {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes for researchers
- The story is in three places: Al 'Imran, Maryam and Al-Anbiya', and in them is the calling of the glad tidings {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- Their companion in the glad tidings is Ibrahim on [[concept/malaikat-ibrahim|the angels of Ibrahim page]].
- What is narrated about the details of their calling is from the books of seerah, and the principle is that their glad tidings is established in the Qur'an {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
