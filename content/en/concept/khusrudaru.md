## Nature
Masarjawayh said that it is khawlanjan, and others said otherwise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.705}

## Temperament
Hot and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.705}

## Actions and properties
Dissolving and melting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.705}

## Excretory organs
It benefits colic and kidney pain, and most of its property is in kidney pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.705}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it increases desire; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.705}
