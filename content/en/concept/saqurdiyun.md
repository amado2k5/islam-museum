## Nature and selection
It is the wild garlic, much smaller than the garden kind, with leaves and a long stem on which is a white flower; its matter has been treated fully in the third chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the third degree, or to the fourth according to other people {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Actions and properties
Subtle, opening and cleansing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It heals great and malignant wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Joints
It is good for strain of the muscle {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.
