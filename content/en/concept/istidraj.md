## Summary
Istidraj, as the Sheikh defines it, is God continuing His favour to a servant who falls short in His service, so that the servant goes on without knowing. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:44:30}

## A verse on respite
The Sheikh quotes a verse about respite and the leading on of the wrongdoer, and does not name it in the recording. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:44:30}
