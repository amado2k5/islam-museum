## Summary
Wujub in the lexical sense is affirmation and binding; for the jurists it is the occupation of the duty-bearer's obligation by the duty, and for the usuli scholars the link of the command to the acts of the responsible person. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16623}

## Command and obligation
Command (iljab) is the demand of an act with a prohibition of leaving it; the obligatory is the act of the person himself. A ruling is called iljab when attributed to the lawgiver and wujub when attributed to the person. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16623} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16624}

## Wujub and wujub al-ada
The Shafi'i Zarkashi holds no difference between wujub and wujub al-ada; most Hanafis distinguish them: wujub is the debt laid on the person, and wujub al-ada is the demand to discharge it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16624}
