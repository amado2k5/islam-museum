## Perseus

The catalogue gives Firsaws, Perseus, after Cassiopeia, describing him as the rider who holds the head of the ghoul. The text says that the star on the tip of the rider's right hand is the one that holds the head of the ghoul. Other stars of the figure follow. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.250}
