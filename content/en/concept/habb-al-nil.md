## Nature
It is Indian qurtum (safflower). {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
Selection: the best is heavy, smooth and fresh. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Temperament
Some say hot and dry in the first degree; the correct view is hot and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it helps leukoderma and white vitiligo. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
Organs of digestion: it is very distressing to the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
Excretory organs: it strongly purges thick humours, black bile and phlegm, and worms and gourd seeds. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
Substitutes: the book gives the substitutes by weight; the weights are left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
