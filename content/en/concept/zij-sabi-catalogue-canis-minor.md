## Canis Minor (al-Muqaddam al-Kalb)

The Lesser Dog is named al-Muqaddam al-Kalb, the one placed before the dog, and its heading gives the Greek prokyon. Two bright stars lie at the end of the figure, and the bright star behind them is called ash-Shi'ra ash-Shamiyya. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.172}
