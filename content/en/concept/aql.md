## Summary
In the lexicon, aql is restraint and reason, the opposite of folly; it is also used for blood-money. As a term it is the power prepared to receive knowledge {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11771}.

## Reason as the basis of obligation
The jurists agree that reason is the condition of legal obligation: acts of worship are not owed by the insane or anyone without reason, on the prophetic instruction that the pen is lifted from three {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11771} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11772}.

## Effects of lost reason
Those without reason cannot validly make transactions in property, contracts or dissolutions, and their statements do not bind them or others; they lose their guardianship, though some acts with them are taken into account, such as destruction of property and nursing a child {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11772}. Loss of reason by madness or fainting breaks ablution {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11772}.

## Injury to reason
No retaliation is due for a blow that removes reason, but blood-money is due for its loss through injury {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11772} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11773}.

## Where to look further
Blood-money is treated under «diyat», obligation under «taklif», and capacity under «ahliyya» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11772}.
