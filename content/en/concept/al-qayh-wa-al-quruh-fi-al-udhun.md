## What comes first
First to be put forward: lightening the diet, using what breeds good sweet laudable humour of vegetables and meats, inclining the regimen to the due moderate quality — taking barley-water and the like if the temperament requires — easing exercise, and turning the matter to nose and mouth by sneezes and gargles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.227}
## The sensible and the deep
The ulcers are either sensible to perception or deep, unreached by sense. The sensible are washed with the known, then drying things are blown in the ear — best is not to check or stop it unless excessive, rather to wash and cleanse with the known. Of the deep some are recent, some chronic; the recent are treated with the known, while the chronic deep ones are very bad, sometimes laying the bone bare — shown by the widening of the passage and much stinking pus — and need the known strong drugs ⁦(drug, wash, tent and compound names and measures are not carried; the milk line is a labelled listing for the founder's ruling)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.227} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.228}
## Pus must not be held back; a practitioners' trick
In sum the pus must not be held back — its breeding is prevented and the ulcers dried. Many cunning practitioners stuff the suppurating ear with rags stopping the pus's flow and keep the patient from sleeping on that side so the pus finds no way and is driven toward the soft flesh at the ear's root, where a swelling forms which they lance after ripening and treat until the matter's flow from the ear ceases ⁦(a trick transmitted as the book's report)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.228}
