## Nature
A known pot-herb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Actions and properties
It cuts phlegm, its oil is hotter than radish oil, vermin flee its smoke, the wild kind generates a bad humour, it cleanses and dissolves, and people eat its leaf and roots cooked {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Cosmetics
It clears the face and removes bruise marks and the trace of dead blood, the wild is a good poultice for bahaq, it dries the tongue and benefits alopecia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves hot swellings and every chronic swelling, and is put with sulphur on scrofula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Wounds and ulcers
It benefits mange and ringworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Joints
It benefits pain of the joints and sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Organs of the head
It clears the moistures of the head, the head of one with lethargy is poulticed with it, its water is dropped for earache and toothache, and likewise its oil, especially when asafoetida has been cooked in it, and it is among the drugs that open obstructions of the sieve; some said that drunk fasting it sharpens understanding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Ocular organs
It is used in kohls for film and roughness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Organs of the chest
Pounded and drunk with honey water it removes chronic roughness in the windpipe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It reduces the spleen and makes thirsty {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Fevers
It benefits periodic and old fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it benefits suffocation of the womb and that it stirs desire; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}
