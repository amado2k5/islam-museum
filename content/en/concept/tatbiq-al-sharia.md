# Implementing the sharia — totality versus stages

## The episode and the vote
The fourth 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode examined the slogan 'implementing the sharia' carried on electoral platforms: is it defined for its own advocates, and is it applied wholesale or gradually? {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:02:00} The audience voted: clear in general and detail to 28%, clear in general without details to 69%, unclear to 3% — a result the scholars called faithful to reality {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:07:30}.

## Divine foundation, human detailing
Ahmad Nawfal compressed the rule in a phrase the host dubbed 'the poet of the episode': 'the foundation is divine, the detailing human' — the generalities are revealed and clear (shura is a pillar of Islam), but who represents whom, the term of service, the shape of constituencies are human details within the text's framework, tested in the field where what works endures like an architect's building between conception and execution {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:10:00}. Rulings detailed by text or consensus bind; the rest stays open to ijtihad — like the inheritance of grandchildren predeceased by their father, settled in Jordan by the 'obligatory bequest' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:12:00}.

## The laws are absent — not Islam
Muhammad al-Khatib pressed the distinction: the details in question are 'how the laws are implemented', and they are absent inside the Islamic movements — nobody wrote them except Mawdudi's attempt at an Islamic constitution — while the educational, social, economic programmes await formulation in the sharia's spirit in a modern mould {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:14:30}. Consensus rulings therefore bind while jumhur rulings do not always — his example the triple talaq, on which many codes took Ibn Taymiyya's view {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:16:30}. On non-Muslims, rulings specific to Muslims do not apply — a Christian may drink wine at home and the state may permit its supply to them — but general state law binds all {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:17:30}.

## Gradualism as divine sunna
Hamza Mansur, Hayil Abd al-Hafiz and Fakhri Abu Sufiya converged on gradualism: ''Islam is the solution' is not in doubt, but laws are not a party's making — they follow a long constitutional mechanism' — so the priority is education, media, economy to build the human base {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:21:30}. And 'gradualism is a divine sunna' by which the Qur'an came down in stages; the story of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz's son: 'do not hurry me — God censured wine twice and forbade it the third; had we burdened people with the whole truth at once they would abandon it whole' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:26:30}. The sincerity test: temporary coexistence with usurious banks is acceptable on condition of earnestly seeking the Islamic alternative, not stalling {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:22:00}.

## The sharia is a whole — the path is upbringing
Fakhri Abu Sufiya insisted the sharia is a whole — take some and leave some and the verse rebukes you — and the Islamic constitution exists, applied for 1200 years; the lack is in modern legal drafting only {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:07:30}. But his path to it is upbringing, not coercion: 'the audience is in one valley and I am in another' — we want it to arrive in the Prophetic image: tarbiyya that produces a state that rules {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:04:30}. And a Jordanian survey (1500 questionnaires) found 98.4% want the hudud applied as revealed — the eager faction's index of popular thirst {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 00:44:30}.

## Where to begin
The panel converged on a combination: build the base through tarbiyya and institutions (Islamic executive, legislative, judicial), achieve the self-sufficiency that resists external pressure — then accomplishment follows; the first step, the awakening's spread, is achieved; the second not yet {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:10:30}. The host closed: 'a weak nation cannot philosophise — we need re-upbringing, legal sovereignty and training in applying rulings, not imposing them on people' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep04 01:15:00}.
