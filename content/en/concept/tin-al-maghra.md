## Nature and choice
A known earth. The book says the best is the clean Baghdadi kind, bright red and free of grey admixture. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Properties
Paulus claimed that in astringent and drying actions it is better than the sealed earth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Wounds and ulcers
It closes wounds with new flesh. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Excretory organs
It kills worms. Taken with a drug the book names nimbarsht, it binds the bowels. The nimbarsht is not identified here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.506}
