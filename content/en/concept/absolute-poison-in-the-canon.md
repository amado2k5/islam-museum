## Summary
Absolute poison, for Ibn Sina, is what is not changed in its natural form by the body but keeps acting with its power and form unchanged until the body is corrupted. He does not mean that it is not warmed in the body by the innate heat, since most poisons have no effect until so warmed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.133}.

## Hot and cold poisons
A poison's nature may be hot, so that its nature assists its specific property of dissolving the spirit, as with viper venom and aconite; or cold, so that its nature assists its property of quenching and weakening the spirit, as with scorpion venom and hemlock and everything that chills {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.133}.
