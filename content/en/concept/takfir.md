## Overview
Takfir is the judgement that a Muslim is an unbeliever; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia devotes a section to the rulings on takfir, including takfir of a Muslim, treating it among the most serious and most cautiously guarded rulings {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia treats the related terms of ta'dil and takfir, then the rulings connected to takfir, foremost takfir of a Muslim {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode — kufr versus takfir
The seventh 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode debated the difference between kufr and takfir, when judgment is obligatory and who may issue it — the host described its misunderstanding as having 'caused bloodshed and great strife' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:01:00}. The youth voted: 29% held that whoever does or says what indicates kufr must be declared kafir, 71% held he must not; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:07:00} and 76% held a Muslim has no right to change his religion {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:44:30}.

Panelists drew the rule: 'takfir of the named person' — judging an individual — versus 'takfir of the act': 'this act is kufr' without judging its doer; the example being al-Shafi'i's and Ahmad's exchange on abandoning prayer {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:17:00}. Muhammad al-Banna and Jamil Allam confined the nullifiers to denying 'what is known of religion by necessity' — the categorical fundamentals, not disputed details — and required explaining the doubt, calling to repentance and respite before ruling {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:12:30}. Salah Abd al-Salam stressed excusing ignorance: Arab illiteracy exceeds 70 million — what then of religious illiteracy {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 00:15:00}.

The panel divided on the freedom to change religion: one argued making it available is the channel into Islam, another replied the texts concern the warring hypocrites {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 01:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 01:20:30}. The host closed: the exceptions do not cancel the rule — Islam does not collide with religious freedom, and the established text is revered and applied with the etiquette of capability {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 01:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 01:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep07 01:22:30}.
