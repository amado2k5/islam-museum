## Nature
Ḥunayn says that this is the Diri earth. It comes in two kinds, one white and one grey-purple. It is strong in nature and cold to the touch, and is brought from the sea coasts, especially from a place the book calls al-Sirāf. Its temperament is cold in the second degree and warm in the first. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Properties
The pure kind has many benefits: it cools and dissolves. If it is washed, its dissolving power is lost. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Swellings and pustules
Mixed with vinegar, it is used on the swellings below the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Wounds and ulcers
Both kinds, kneaded with vinegar, help burns and other wounds at once, before they blister or swell. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Head
Kneaded with vinegar, it helps the swellings that arise at the roots of the ears and at the tonsils. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Joints
It helps swellings of the whole body. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Excretory organs
Both kinds soften the hardness of the testicles, according to the book. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}
