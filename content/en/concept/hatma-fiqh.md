## Summary
Hatma', in the lexical sense, is an animal whose front teeth have fallen out from the root. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16808}

## Its fitness for sacrifice
The Hanafis in the sounder view allow it if it grazes and feeds; the Malikis hold that losing one tooth does not harm; the Shafi'is mostly forbid it; the Hanbalis forbid one whose front teeth are gone, and allow it if some remain. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16808} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16809}
