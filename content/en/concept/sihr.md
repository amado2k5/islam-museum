## Summary
Sorcery is real and effective by God's leave; learning it is disbelief, warned of in Harut and Marut at Babylon — the Prophet sought refuge through the mu'awwidhatayn.

## In the texts
[[passage/quran-2-102|we are only a trial, so do not disbelieve]]

## Note
Its effect is illusion and reality together: it deceives sight and harms bodies; lawful ruqya is its remedy, not amulets.
