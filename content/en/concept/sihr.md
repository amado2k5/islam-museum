## Summary
Sorcery is real and established by Book and Sunnah, effective only by God's leave; learning it is disbelief, and God warned of it in the account of Harut and Marut at Babylon — the Prophet sought refuge through the mu'awwidhatayn {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
