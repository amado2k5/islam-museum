## Definition
Ihana is lexically the act of abasing, from hana, to be humbled; it is one of the forms of mockery and contempt. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3595}

## The two senses
- As a wrong, it invites a penalty. When it touches faith or the law, such as bowing to an idol, throwing a copy of the Qur'an into filth, or insulting the prophets and angels, it is unbelief. When it touches people unjustly, such as insult or beating, it is sin. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3595}
- As a fixed penalty: such as the humiliation of protected non-Muslims in paying the poll tax, or a light penalty for one who insults another, in proportion to the rank of the one insulted. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3595}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3596}

## Exceptions
An act that looks like humiliation may be excused by intention or necessity, such as spitting on a board to erase the writing on it, or throwing copies of the Qur'an from a ship in danger of sinking to save lives. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3595}

## Where it is treated
Chiefly under apostasy, slander and discretionary punishment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3596}
