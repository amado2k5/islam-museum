## Summary
When the hours are counted from noon to the given hour, they are multiplied as before, and the risings of the Sun's degree in the right sphere are added. The ascendant and the mid-heaven for that place are thus known. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.110}.

## The risings of the Sun's degree
The risings of the Sun's degree are the times it takes that degree to rise in the given clime; they are tabulated in the rising tables for each clime. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.110}.
