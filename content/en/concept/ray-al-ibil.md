## Temperament
Hot, subtle and drying in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Actions and properties
It is said that the camels are not harmed by the venom of snakes and vermin only because of the theriac quality that comes to them from this pasture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Poisons and bites
It is given to drink for the bites of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}
