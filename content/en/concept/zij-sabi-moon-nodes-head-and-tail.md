## Summary
Al-Battani calls the two points where the inclined sphere cuts the zodiac's sphere the head and the tail. The Moon's latitude runs north from the head and south from the tail, and these are the points where the two circles meet. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.

## Motion of the nodes
The inclined sphere moves against the order of the signs by about three minutes a day, so the two nodes shift by that amount; the tables reckon this together with the Moon's own motion. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.
