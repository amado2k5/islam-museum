## Overview
al-Khabir — The All-Aware. It is name number 31 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-34-1|34:1]]، [[passage/quran-6-103|6:103]]، [[passage/quran-6-18|6:18]]، [[passage/quran-6-73|6:73]]، [[passage/quran-66-3|66:3]]، [[passage/quran-67-14|67:14]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
