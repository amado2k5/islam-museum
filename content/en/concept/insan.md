## Nature and selection
This entry gathers several substances taken from the human body. The book gives them in the form "it is said" and "they say", so they are reported sayings, not the author's recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no single temperament for these substances; the actions are listed below for each substance, by organ.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: human semen is said to clear vitiligo; the salt of children's urine prepared in a copper vessel clears freckles, and their dung helps white patches {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Swellings and pustules: the sediment of human urine is said to calm erysipelas; the dung, used hot, does likewise; the ash of hair cures pustules, and mixed with ghee it prevents flowing swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Wounds and ulcers: urine clears ulcerated scabies and helps ringworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Joints: it is said that menstrual blood calms the pain of gout, and likewise human semen with wax and oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Head: burnt hair, dripped with rose oil into an ear or a painful tooth, is said to calm the pain; the saliva of a fasting person draws worms out of the ear; burnt human bone is given for epilepsy; ear wax helps hemicrania {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Eyes: urine boiled with honey in a copper vessel clears the whiteness of the eye and helps a twitching of the eyelid; burnt hair mixed with another drug helps mange and itching of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Chest: the urine of children, drunk, is said to help laboured breathing; a woman's milk is very useful in consumption {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Food organs: human milk calms stomach pain; it is said that a dose of urine with oxymel, taken without the drinker's knowledge, helps jaundice, especially with honey water and chickpea water; the dung does likewise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Excretory organs: human milk brings on urine. It is said that taking menstrual blood pure prevents conception; this is the book's listing, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. A woman's milk helps sores of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Human urine, it is said, stops diarrhoea and cleanses the womb when boiled with leek; this is the book's listing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Fevers: the dried dung taken with honey or wine helps recurring fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}. Poisons: a woman's milk is an antidote to the marine rabbit; human teeth, crushed and sprinkled on a viper's bite, help it; the dung sprinkled on a human bite helps; saliva kills scorpions and snakes; and if one person bites another with saliva, the bitten limb ulcerates {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.374}.
