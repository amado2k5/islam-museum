## Nature
The fruit of a well-known tree, most of it at Jurjan, and what is found in other lands is smaller than the Jurjani {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Selection
The best is the largest, the finest and the reddest in colour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Temperament
Cold toward the first degree and moderate in dryness and moisture, tending to a little moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Actions and properties
Galen said: I see no benefit in it either in keeping present health or in recovering lost health. Another said that it benefits the sharpness of hot blood, which the author thinks is by its thickening the blood, and the thought that it purifies and washes the blood is one he does not incline to; its nourishment is slight and its digestion hard. The good saying on it is that of Galen: I found no effect of it either in health or in illness, but I found it hard to digest and of little nourishment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Organs of the chest
It is good for the chest and the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is bad for the stomach and hard to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Excretory organs
Some people claimed that it is useful for pain of the kidney and the bladder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}
