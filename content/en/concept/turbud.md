## Nature
Cut pieces of wood, thick and thin, brought from India {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.692}

## Choice
The best is the white that is not worm-eaten, rolled like tubes of thin reed, smooth and quick to crumble, not thick; it may be eaten away so that its power weakens, and the very light and the holed is weak; the book describes a way of improving it, left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.692}

## Actions and properties
Its use brings dryness and desiccation to the body because it draws out the thin moistures, and for that it is used with almond oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.692}

## Excretory organs
It purges much phlegm and a little of the burnt humours when taken powdered, and when boiled the reverse; Masarjawayh said that it purges thick sticky humours and some said that it purges the raw from the hips; the sounder view in the book is that it purges thin phlegm, and when strengthened with ginger and sharp things it purges the thick and raw, but alone it does not purge the thick unless it meets it already loosened in the stomach and intestines; the amount of the draught is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.692}
