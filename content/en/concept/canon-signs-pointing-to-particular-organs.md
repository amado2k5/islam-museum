## Summary
Ibn Sina shows that particular signs point to particular conditions: lasting headache with the eye imagining specks like gnats before the face foretells water descending in the eye; heaviness and pain on the right side, if long, points to a disorder of the liver; heaviness and stretching in the lower back and flank with a change in the urine foretells a kidney disorder; stool devoid of its colour foretells jaundice; and long burning of urine foretells sores in the bladder and penis {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.

## Eye, liver and kidneys
Lasting headache with the eye imagining gnats and the like before the face, if fixed and established, foretells water descending in the eye; heaviness and pain in the right side, if prolonged, points to a disorder of the liver; heaviness and stretching in the lower back and flank, with the urine changed from its habit, foretells a disorder of the kidneys {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.

## Jaundice and bladder
Stool lacking its colour beyond the usual foretells jaundice; and if the burning of the urine goes on long it foretells sores arising in the bladder and the penis {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.
