## What it is
The tooth that grows long is taken with the two fingers or the gripping instrument, then brought down with the file, then rubbed with the astringent tooth-stick he described (the names left out; the gripping and the file carried as the book's description, not a procedure) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.278}
