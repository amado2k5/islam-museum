## Summary
Lexically the wrist is the place where a bracelet sits on the forearm, the joint between forearm and palm. The technical sense does not differ from the lexical one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14766} The wrist is a joint, but not every joint is a wrist. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14766}

## Rulings
The wrist must be washed in ablution. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14766} For theft and banditry the majority hold that the hand is cut at the wrist, citing a report from Abu Bakr and Umar. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14766} Where a hand is cut at the forearm, the majority do not cut the aggressor's hand at the forearm; the Shafi'is take retaliation at the wrist and claim the remainder as arbitration. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14767} The full blood-money is due for both hands cut at the wrist, and half for one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14767}

## Where to look further
Ablution, theft, retaliation and blood-money are treated under their own headings. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14767}
