## The diseases that befall the lung
There befall the lung the diseases proper to the like-parts and the instrumental diseases — above all the blockages in its vessels and the parts of its windpipe, especially the rough vessels, and in the sponginess of its body, and these may arise for every cause of blockage up to apposition — and the shared diseases. The lung's diseases may multiply in winter and autumn for the many catarrhs, above all in a rainy autumn after a dry northerly summer; cold air harms the lung unless it is already afflicted by fierce heat; and the lung's diseases often lead to the liver's diseases, as the fierceness of its cold and its heat lead to dropsy — and so does the diaphragm's {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.305}
