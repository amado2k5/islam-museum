## The matters sticking in the lung
The rule: the sticking matters are made hard to remove by three causes — thickness and stickiness, excessive thinness, or great quantity — and the thick humours are not dried at first but softened, cut, and gently managed into dissolution, the greater care going to the cutting (the means of the honey-water kind named there, and its way, are left out per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.305}
