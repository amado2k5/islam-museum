## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry up to the fourth degree. Thinning, drawing and very resolving. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Cosmetic use
One of the medicines for leukoderma, especially the kind that fire has not touched. Mixed with terebinth gum it removes the marks on the nails, and with vinegar it is used on vitiligo. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Wounds, scabies and itch
Put on ulcerated scabies, wounds and sores; for itching the body is washed with it together with natron. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Joints and the head
It is a plaster for gout with natron and water. Its fumes stop catarrh, and with vinegar and honey it is used on a cracked ear. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}
