## Summary
After the general signs Ibn Sina opens the first part, on the pulse, of nineteen chapters, defining the pulse and giving the four parts of a beat and the place where it is felt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.168}.

## Definition
The pulse is a movement of the vessels of the spirit made of expansion and contraction, to cool the spirit with the breeze. It can be studied in general or for each disease; he treats here the general laws of pulse knowledge and puts the particular ones off to the particular diseases {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.168}.

## Why it matters
The pulse and the urine are general signs of the states of the body, which is why Ibn Sina turns to them after the signs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.168}.
