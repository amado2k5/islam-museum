## Nature and selection
The white is the well known kind, and the black is the pure of Babylonian pitch and others {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Actions and properties
Fine, especially the white, dissolving, melting and opening obstructions {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Joints
It helps pains of the hips and joints, especially the white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Head, ears and teeth
The blue naphtha helps cold pains of the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Eyes
It helps whiteness of the eye and descending water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Chest and breathing
The book lists it for asthma and old cough; the method of use it gives is not carried over here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Excretory organs
It calms colic and winds, and made into a wick it kills worms, the black especially; all of it provokes urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It provokes menses and breaks the winds of the bladder and the cold of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.

## Poisons and bites
It helps stings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.581}.
