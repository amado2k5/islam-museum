## Summary
Naft is the oil that camels are smeared with against mange in the lexicon, and a fixed substance of the earth as a term. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15861}

## Rulings on naft
Zakat: most jurists hold that a petroleum mine owes none; the Hanbalis and an odd Shafi'i view require a quarter of a tenth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15861} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15862}
Ownership: the Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis do not own apparent mines by revival or grant them; the Malikis leave the matter to the ruler. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15862} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15863}
