## Summary
The Sheikh says that the ignorant complain about God to people, and whoever knows his Lord does not complain of Him to creatures. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:17:30}

## One person complaining to another
The Sheikh relates that one of the early generations saw a man complaining to another man, and told him that he was complaining from one who shows him mercy to one who shows him none, and that one who complains to a human complains from the merciful to the one who has no mercy. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:18:00}

## A verse on complaining to God
The Sheikh quotes a verse saying that the one who complains complains only to God, and does not name the verse in the recording. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:18:30}

## Do not complain to every small or great person
The Sheikh says that we should not complain to every small or great person, nor to everyone who has wronged us (needs_source: the Sheikh's wording at this point is garbled in the transcript), and that complaint should be made to the Lord of the worlds. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 00:56:00}
