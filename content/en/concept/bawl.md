## Nature and selection
The most useful urine is that of the Bedouin camel, the noble kind. Human urine is the weakest of urines, and weaker still is the urine of castrated domestic pigs. The strongest is aged urine. The urine of a eunuch is weaker in everything; the clearest is human urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry, it is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: all of it cleanses. Human urine kept with vine ash on a site of bleeding stops it. Camel urine helps the rash (hizaz) when washed with it, and so does bull urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}.

Adornment: cleanses white patches very much {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}.

Wounds and ulcers: ass urine for spreading and wet ulcers, and human urine too, especially aged; it helps peeling, itching and white patches, especially with borax and sour water; its dregs on redness help; a wash for mange, scalp ringworm and worm-eating sores; a wound on the foot that is urinated on and left until healed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Joints: helps nervous pains, especially goat urine, domestic and mountain, especially for cramp and stiffness, and by sniffing it for stiffness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Head: bull urine with bitterness dissolved in it, dropped thin in the ear, eases its pain; goat urine alone does too; with bitterness and aged human urine, it stops pus from the ear. Camel urine is very useful for nasal problems and opens blockages of the filter strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Eyes: left to set in a copper vessel, it helps white spots and mange, especially boys' urine; also boiled with leek {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Breath: the book says nursing boys' urine helps difficult breathing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Digestion: human and camel urine help dropsy and hard spleen, especially with mare's milk; goat urine for fever, especially the mountain kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Excretory organs: pig urine breaks stones in the kidney and bladder and moves them; ass urine helps kidney pain; human urine boiled with leek is used for womb pains when a woman sits in it five days, once daily {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.

Poisons: human urine helps a snakebite when drunk and when poured on the bite, especially from rock snakes; with natron on dog bites and all bites and stings; the aged kind helps all poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.413}.
