## The signs of the spitting of blood
If the patient is laid on the side holding the lesion, what he spits increases; and one must first look so that what is spat be not a nosebleed, told by the nosebleed's matter, its course, and a lightness befalling the head after heaviness (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.334}
