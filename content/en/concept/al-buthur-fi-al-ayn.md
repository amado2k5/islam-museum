## Its colour follows its seat
Of the pustules, what is on the cornea tends to whiteness and what is on the conjunctiva to redness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.180}

## Its treatment
Its treatment is venesection and dripping blood into the eye as told in the tarfa chapter, poulticing the eye with wool soaked in white of egg beaten with what softens, dripping milk with a softener in it, and the collyria ⁦(their names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.180}
