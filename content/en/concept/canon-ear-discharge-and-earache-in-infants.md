## Summary
Ibn Sina explains the flowing of moisture from infants' ears by the moistness of their bodies and brains, names a wool plug dipped in honey and wine and the like, then earache from wind or moisture and its treatment with drugs boiled in oil and dripped in {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.215}.

## Flowing moisture
Moisture may flow from their ears, for their bodies, and especially their brains, are very moist. A tuft of wool is dipped for them in honey and wine mixed with a little alum, or saffron, or a whiff of natron, and put in their ears; sometimes it suffices to dip wool in gall-nut wine, use it with some saffron, and put the saffron in that wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.215}.

## Earache
Earache may befall children from wind or moisture, and is treated with hudud, thyme, tabarzad salt, lentil, myrrh, colocynth seed and juniper, any of which is boiled in oil and dripped in {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.215}.
