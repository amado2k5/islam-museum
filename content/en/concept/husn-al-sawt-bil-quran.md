## The texts
Al-Bukhari has from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet said: "he is not of us who does not yataghanna the Qur'an" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}; and also that he heard Abu Musa al-Ash'ari recite and said: "this one has been given a mizmar of the mizamir of the house of David". The people of the Sunan report "adorn the Qur'an with your voices, for the beautiful voice increases the Qur'an in beauty" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## The explanations
The scholars read yataghanna two ways: some carry it on beautifying and softening the voice in recitation; others — among them Ibn 'Abbas in what is reported — on being content with the Qur'an against everything else, and a group explained it as sufficiency in the Qur'an against falsehood and amusement; the jurists record both {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## The limit of beautifying
The people of performance agree that beautifying the voice is sunnah inside tajwid, and that taghani is not drawing the voice out until it adds to or takes from the letters and the mudud — what takes a letter beyond its measure leaves tajwid for lahn. This is the door adjoining the disagreement over reciting with melodies and maqamat {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
