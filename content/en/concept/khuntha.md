## Nature
Its leaf is like the Syrian leek, it has a smooth stalk with a flower on its head and a long round fruit like the acorn, and it is pungent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Temperament
It is hot and dry, and some said that it is cold and moist, and went far from the mark {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Actions and properties
Cleansing and dissolving, especially its root; burnt it becomes warming, drying and dissolving, and more so its root; its strength is like the strength of lufa ja'd {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Cosmetics
It benefits da' al-tha'lab (alopecia) and da' al-hayya (the serpent's disease), especially the ash of its root; when white bahaq is rubbed with its ash and one sits in the sun, it benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.708}

## Swellings and pustules
Its root with lees of the drink is put on all swellings of the glands and on boils, and a poultice with barley flour benefits at the beginning of hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Wounds and ulcers
When its root with lees of the drink is put on malignant and dirty ulcers it benefits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Joints
It benefits weakness of the muscle and sprain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Organs of the head
When its juice is dropped alone or with frankincense, honey, a drink and myrrh it benefits ear pus, and toothache when dropped into the ear on the side opposite the aching tooth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Ocular organs
In the juice of its root there is benefit for the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Respiratory organs
It benefits pain of the two sides and cough the amount and the way of taking it are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Organs of nourishment
It benefits jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Excretory organs
It promotes urine, and its fruit and flower given with a drink loosen the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it promotes menses, that its root with lees of the drink is good for swellings of the breast, and that with lees of the drink it is a good poultice for swellings of the testicles; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Poisons
The book says that it benefits the bites of vermin and the sting of the scorpion and of dhu al-arba'a wa al-arba'in; the way of giving it is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}
