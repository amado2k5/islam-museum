## The aim and the warning
Your whole aim in treating one with headache from a blow or a fall must be to quiet the pain as far as possible and keep the matter away from the place of pain, either by evacuation or by drawing it away lest it swell, and to treat the wound, if one arose, that it may knit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.67} It cannot knit while the ill-temperament stays; rather, in healing it the temperament of its region must be tempered {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.67} Know that when fever appears in the one with this affliction and the mind is confused, swelling has begun; the first thing to be done in his treatment is to draw blood to prevent the swelling, and if there is fullness, what evacuates by loosening is used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.67} The bath, the drink (sharab), anger, fumigants and heating foods are to be avoided, and if the place begins to swell, strong astringents with cooling are then needed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.67} Know that when the pain reaches the membranes of the brain there is danger in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.67}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.67}
