## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the Messenger's humanity is necessary for the completeness of the message: he must be of his people's kind to be a model who applies the method before them. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.196} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.197}

## The disbelievers' objection
Sha'rawi sets out the disbelievers' objection to the Messenger's humanity, and notes that the Meccans knew him as truthful and trustworthy before his mission, so their doubts were contrived. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.196} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.197}
