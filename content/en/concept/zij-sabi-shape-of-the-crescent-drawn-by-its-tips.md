## Summary
Al-Battani describes how to draw the crescent as it is seen: the evenness of its two tips, their tilt from the zodiac belt, and the amount of light. One draws the Moon's circle and the lit part within it according to the distance from the Sun in parts, and finds the tilt of the two tips against the line of equinox. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.135}.

## The guide lines
He divides the circle by two diameters and draws perpendicular lines from the horizon; the point the crescent reaches is fixed where these lines meet the circumference, and the amount of light is read at each count of parts. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.136}.
