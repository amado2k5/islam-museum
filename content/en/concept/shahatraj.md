## Choice
The best of it is the green, fresh and bitter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Actions and properties
It purifies the blood and opens blockages, having coldness from the astringent taste in it and heat from the bitter taste in it, its coldness being the stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Ulcers
It is drunk for itching and scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Organs of the head
It firms the gums {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach and opens blockages of the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Excretory organs
It softens the nature and brings on urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Substitutes
Its substitute in scab and old fevers is sanamakki {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}
