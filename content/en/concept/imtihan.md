## Definition
Imtihan is, from the root of "work" or "service", the use of a thing or the wearing of it out. The jurists use it in two senses: trade (ihtiraf) and wear (ibtidhal), the latter being not keeping a thing from use. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3380}
It differs from contempt, which treats a thing as worthless. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3380}

## Ruling
On Friday, the feasts and congregational occasions one is asked to wear clothes other than work clothes, following the hadith that one should buy two garments for Friday besides his working clothes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3380}
