## Summary
In chapter 23 Ibn Sina lists the ways of stopping evacuations, then explains those done by drawing without evacuation, by drawing with evacuation, and by helping the evacuation itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.302-303}.

## The ways
Evacuations are stopped either by turning the matter aside without another evacuation, or by an evacuation together with the turning, or by helping the evacuation itself, or by cooling, sealing, astringent or caustic drugs, or by binding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.302}.

## Drawing without evacuation
Stopping an evacuation by drawing without evacuating is like setting cupping glasses on the breast to prevent bleeding from the womb; the best drawing is the one that also soothes the pain of the part drawn from {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.302}.

## Drawing with evacuation
Drawing with evacuation is like opening the basilic vein for this, or stopping vomiting by purging and purging by vomiting, and stopping both by sweating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.302}.

## Helping the evacuation
Stopping by helping the evacuation is like cleansing the stomach and the bowel of viscous humours with the compound purgative aiyaraj, and striving to cleanse the mouth of the stomach by vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.303}.
