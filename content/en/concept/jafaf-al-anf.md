## Its causes and treatment
It may be from heat, or from a severe dryness, or from a viscid humour that has dried in it — and the treatment of each is evident. The most useful things in it are the oils and the cold moist juices, and drawing the humour out if present, after softening it with an oil or a juice, so that what ought not be extracted does not come out ⁦(the oil and juice names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.252}
