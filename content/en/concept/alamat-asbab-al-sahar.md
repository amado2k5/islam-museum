## Signs
The sign of what comes from simple dryness, without matter and without heat accompanying, is lightness of the senses and of the head, dryness of the eye, tongue and nostril, and that no heat or cold is felt in the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94} What comes from heat has the signs of dryness with inflammation and burning, and may come with thirst and burning at the root of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94} What comes from the nitrous quality of the humours has as its sign wetness in the nostril, rheum (ramas) in the eye, a slight sense of heaviness, quick waking from sleep and starting up, and it is inferred from the past regimen and the age {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94} What comes from the lighting of the place or from food has its cause as its sign; what comes from a melancholic swelling has the signs mentioned many times; and what comes from pain, distressing thoughts or acute fevers has its cause as its sign {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94}
