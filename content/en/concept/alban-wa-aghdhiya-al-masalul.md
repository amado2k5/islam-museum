## Milks and foods of the consumptive
And the fittest of the milks is women's milk, suckled from the breast; then donkey's milk, and goat's milk — especially for constipation, in goat's milk; and mare's milk is also among what cleanses and eases expectoration, though it does not glutinate to that degree, as he opines. As for cow's and sheep's milk, there is thickness in it — if it could be managed that it is sucked from the udder it would be preferable — and the milked animal must be fed the plant needed for its action. The plucked-claw crabs, washed much with water, are among the cleansing foods in this chapter; the trotters are good for their viscidity; and the ash — and especially the cold vegetables — among the foods (lines labelled, carrying animal milks and meats on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.368}
