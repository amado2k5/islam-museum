## Nature
It is well known; when washed with the ash of its branches and leaves it is like tutiya; its rubb keeps because of the soundness of its astringency, whereas the rubb of the apple turns sour because of the cold watery moisture in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Selection
The roasted is lighter and more beneficial {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Temperament
Cold at the end of the first degree, dry at the start of the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Actions and properties
It is astringent and strengthening, and its flower is also astringent and so is its oil; the sweet is less astringent, its seed softening without astringency, and it prevents the flow of residues to the viscera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Cosmetics
It holds back sweat, and its oil benefits cracks from cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Swellings and pustules
Its oil benefits herpes well {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its oil is for malignant ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Joints
Eating much of it generates nerve pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Ocular organs
The roasted fruit is placed on hot swellings of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Organs of the chest
Its juice is useful against orthopnoea and asthma and prevents spitting of blood; its seed benefits roughness of the throat and softens the windpipe, and its mucilage also moistens dryness of the windpipe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits vomiting and hangover, quenching thirst and strengthening the stomach that receives residues when drunk; its infusion and its decoction are taken after wine and prevent hangover; a syrup made from it greatly strengthens a fallen appetite; and raw it strengthens the stomach and prevents phlegmatic vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.608}
