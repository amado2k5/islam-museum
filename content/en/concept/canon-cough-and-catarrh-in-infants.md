## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions cough and nasal catarrh in infants, with the directions to pour much hot water over the head, smear the tongue with honey and press its base so that phlegm is vomited, or a mixture of gum and tragacanth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.213-214}.

## Vomiting to relief
Cough and catarrh may befall them, and a treatment is directed: much hot water poured on the head of the child so afflicted, its tongue smeared with plenty of honey, then the base of the tongue pressed with the finger so that much phlegm is vomited and it is relieved {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.213}.

## The mixture
Or gum arabic, tragacanth, quince seed, liquorice rob and fanid are taken, and a little of this is given each day in fresh milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.213-214}.
