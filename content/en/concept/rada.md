## Overview
Foster-kinship (*mahramiyya al-rida'*) is the subject of the forty-fourth hadith of Ibn Rajab's additions: "Fostering makes unlawful what birth makes unlawful." In another wording: "What is unlawful by lineage is unlawful by fostering." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.864}

## Its effect
[[person/ibn-rajab|Ibn Rajab]] relates the consensus of the scholars on acting upon it in general. Foster-kinship establishes the same kind of forbidden degree in marriage that birth establishes, so that what would have been forbidden to the suckling child had he been a son becomes forbidden to him, both through the nurse and her relatives and through the owner of the milk. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.864}

The division in the commentary of the prohibited degrees into those permanently forbidden on their own and others applies to fostering as it applies to lineage. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.864}
