## Summary
Al-Battani states that his rule holds until a long span of time has passed in which the place of the Sun's apogee that he found on the zodiac changes much, and with it what arises from the variation in the Sun's motion; he added some minutes to the Moon's mean place in the base of the reckoning, and took the share of each degree of the signs from the amount of the inequality of days with their nights and entered it in the tables of ascensions of the right sphere, in the column following the ascensions in each sign {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.

## How the table is used
When the entry opposite the Sun's true degree is taken, its amount in mean hours is known; if it is subtracted from the unequal days, what remains is the mean days from which the motions are taken from the tables, and if it is added to the mean days, what gathers is the unequal days found by measurement {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}. The number of minutes added to the Moon's mean place is disturbed in the printed text and is not given. This follows the remark that [[concept/zij-sabi-day-inequality-is-felt-in-the-moon|the inequality is felt in the Moon]] {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}. Chapter 29 ends here and chapter 30, on the Moon's spheres, begins on the next leaf {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.79-80}.
