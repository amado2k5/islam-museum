## Summary
Al-Battani opens chapter 12 with the means of finding the meridian line, the line of the south, in any country and at any season, when the Sun's place is not known. The first step is to prepare the ground: a site with an open horizon, a level surface that does not slope, a circle drawn on it of any size, and an upright rod with a pointed head fixed at its centre. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.38}.

## The site and the circle
He says: if you do not know the Sun's place, go to a spot with an open horizon, safe on its face, level on its surface and not sloping, and draw a circle on it of whatever size you please. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.38}.

## The rod and its length
He adds: fix at the centre of the circle a straight upright rod with no bend and a pointed head; the best length is a quarter of the circle's diameter. Then measure with one and the same distance from the farthest parts of the circle to the tip of the rod, so that its standing true on the centre is confirmed; that is, the distances must agree for the rod to stand true on the centre. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.38}.
