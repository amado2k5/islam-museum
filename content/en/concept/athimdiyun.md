## Nature and selection
The book gives no detailed identity for this drug {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}.

## Temperament and actions
It cools very intensely, with watery moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}.

## Uses by organ
Chest organs: it keeps the breast firm in its shape {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Excretory organs: the book says that drinking it makes the drinker sterile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. This is the book's listing and is logged for the founder's ruling.
