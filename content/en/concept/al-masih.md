## Summary
The Messiah Jesus son of Mary is mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the prophet and messenger of God, who descends in the last days to break the cross, kill the pig, kill the Dajjal, judge the people justly, and lead or follow the Mahdi in prayer. His descent is a major sign of the Hour, and he is among the greatest who ward off the people's evil from the Dajjal and the trials he resolves.
