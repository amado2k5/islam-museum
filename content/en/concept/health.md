## Overview
The first books of the Canon divide the body's states, after Galen, into three: health, disease, and a state between them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.104}. Health is a state in which the human body is so constituted in temperament and composition that all its acts issue sound and whole {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.104}. Medicine's aim: to preserve health present and restore it lost {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.13}.
