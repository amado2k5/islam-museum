## The state
There are organs — it happens that a person is struck dumb so that he cannot be told from the dead, no breath or anything appears from him, and then he lives and is safe; Ibn Sina saw many people in this state, in whom the breath does not appear and the pulse falls completely {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.131}

## Its reason
It is likely that the innate heat in them is not in great need of ventilation, and the smoky vapour of it leads to abundant breath for the cold that has come over them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.131}

## Delaying burial
For this reason he recommends delaying the burial of the ambiguous dead until their state is clear — no less than seventy-two hours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.131}
