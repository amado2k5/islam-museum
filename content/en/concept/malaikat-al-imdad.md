## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an mentions the angels of aid in several places: at Badr [[passage/quran-8-9|al-Anfal 9]] [[passage/quran-8-12|al-Anfal 12]], in the promise of three thousand angels sent down [[passage/quran-3-124|Al 'Imran 124]], and at Hunayn [[passage/quran-9-26|al-Tawbah 26]].

## In hadith
Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi narrate in the hadith of the Isra' that Jibril told the Prophet God aided him at Badr with three thousand angels and at Hunayn with a thousand {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details that the angels of aid are those who descended to steady and aid the believers, noting their number was three thousand at Badr and a thousand at Hunayn {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some held the angels did not fight at Badr but only steadied the believers, others that they fought; the primary interpretation takes them as descending for aid and victory {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
