## Summary
Sha'rawi reads the verse 'We send down of the Quran that which is a cure and a mercy' as placing cure before mercy. When the Quran came, societies were full of ills such as injustice, the usurping of rights and the enslavement of people by people. The method comes first to heal, and mercy then keeps those ills from returning. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.26}

## The Quran as a pharmacy
Sha'rawi compares the Quran to a pharmacy: when people neglect God's method the ills return, and when they go back to the Quran they take its remedy and are healed. This is his reading and is attributed to him. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.26}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's reading and reasoning, attributed to him here. Other readings of the verse are not yet presented.
