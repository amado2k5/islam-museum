## Overview
The gathering ground is the earth on which God gathers creation on the Day; the Qur'an names the earth's changing: 'the day the earth is changed to another earth, and the heavens, and they come forth to God, the One, the Irresistible' [[passage/quran-14-48|the earth-changing verse]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the hadith
In Sahl ibn Sa'd's report in the two Sahihs: people are gathered on the Day on a white, pure land like fine flour, with no landmark for anyone {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}. Ibn Kathir at the verse records the earth changed for another earth, white and clean {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
