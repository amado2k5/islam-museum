## Overview
Ibn al-Nadim, in the *Fihrist*, records what reached him about the codices (*masahif*) of the Companions. In the arrangement of the codex of Ubayy ibn Ka'b, as transmitted by al-Fadl ibn Shadhan, there are one hundred and sixteen surahs, among them the two surahs of *al-Khal'* and *al-Hafd*. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}

In the codex of Ali ibn Abi Talib, as transmitted by Abd Khayr, he collected the Qur'an after the death of the Prophet. Ibn al-Nadim mentions that in his time he saw a codex in the hand of Ali, from which some leaves had fallen, in the possession of Abu Ya'la Hamza al-Husayni. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}

He also transmits the words of Ata' ibn Yasar on the number of surahs, verses, and words. These are reports that Ibn al-Nadim transmits, and their verification needs a further source. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim}
