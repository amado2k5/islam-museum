## Overview
God says «those who believe and do righteous deeds — tuba is theirs and a goodly return» {cite:tanzil-quran}. A body of the salaf, among them Ibn Abbas in some reports, held that Tuba is a tree in Paradise {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the exegesis
It is reported from Ibn Abbas that Tuba is a tree in Paradise whose trunk is in the Prophet's dwelling, with no branch whose tip does not hold a dwelling of one of the people of Paradise {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. Others read tuba in the plain sense of goodness and joy — which also belongs to Paradise.
