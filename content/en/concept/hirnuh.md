## Nature and selection
Nature: it resembles pepper except that it tends to yellow; it is a fragrance like aloes wood, carried from the lands of the Slavs. The book names hasht dihan as another name for it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
Temperament: moderate. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.451}

## Uses by organ
Digestive organs: strengthens the stomach, improves digestion, and strengthens appetite for food. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.451}
