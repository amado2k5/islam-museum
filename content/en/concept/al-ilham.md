## Summary
A casting into the heart without revelation or dream: a knowing God opens upon the heart of whom He wills, as He inspired Musa's mother to nurse him — a karama of the saints, not prophethood nor proof {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:aqida-tahawiyya}.
