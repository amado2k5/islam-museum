## Summary
"The model of training in general leadership" is the model in which [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] names eight roles that he holds are leadership itself, everything else being management to be delegated: direction, example, change, motivation, training, relations, intervention and empowerment. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:10:00} He set it out in episodes 19 to 27 of his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]], giving each role two elements. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:04:00} His method of training is to define the roles, then the competencies each role needs, then measure the gap between the role and the competence a person has; that gap is what training fills. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:03:30} In his earlier management course he had given the leader six roles; see [[concept/al-qiyada|leadership]].

## The roles and their elements
- **Direction**: vision and plan. Vision for him is prediction without fantasy, and ambition without the end justifying the means. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:13:30} For the plan he cites the Prophet's offering himself to Banu Shayban ibn Tha'laba, who offered to protect him against everyone except the Persians, which he refused. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:31:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:36:30}
- **Example**: credibility and presence. Credibility for him means "trustworthy and truthful", and he says studies make it the most important trait in a leader. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:40:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:40:30} Presence includes the Prophet's share in building the mosque at Quba and in digging the Trench. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:52:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep19 00:52:30}
- **Change**: creativity and challenge. The theory of the "superman leader" is over, he says; the modern leader makes up for his weakness through consultation. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:04:30} An instance of creativity, for him, is that when the words of [[person/abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Abdullah ibn Ubayy]] on the campaign against Banu al-Mustaliq reached the Prophet, he marched the army for a whole day until it was too busy to talk of them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:10:30}
- **Motivation**: encouragement, and appreciation both material and moral; moral appreciation includes titles such as al-Siddiq, the Sword of God and the Trustee of the Community, and the return of the key of the Ka'ba to Banu Shayba. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:31:00}
- **Training**: training oneself and training others, including sending Ali to Yemen as a judge with the advice to hear both parties. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:36:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep20 00:43:30}
- **Relations**: external and internal. He says 64% of family firms collapse in the second generation, one reason being, in his view, that the founder hands down money and work but not relationships. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:06:30} Citing scholars of Sharia-based statecraft, he lists five kinds of external relation, all of which the Prophet used: truce, as at Hudaybiyya; alliance, as with Khuza'a; war; giving help without conditions; and seeking help without return. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:10:00} One of his examples is the story of [[person/thumama-ibn-uthal|Thumama ibn Uthal]], who was captured and tied up in the mosque for three days, treated well, then set free, whereupon he became a Muslim, and later cut off grain to Mecca until the Prophet interceded for it. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:19:00}
- **Intervention**: the leader steps in only in two cases, when those with him cannot cope and when a problem has become chronic; his rule is that "delegation does not cancel responsibility". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:38:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:14:30} His example of the second is that Quraysh were a chronic problem through Badr, Uhud and the Trench, and the [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|Treaty of Hudaybiyya]] was the decisive intervention. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:40:00}
- **Empowerment**: participation and granting authority, without which there is centralisation; an example is Abu Bakr's charging [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]] with collecting the Qur'an. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:43:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep21 00:44:30}

## Competencies and skills
He then names two competencies for each role, with a skill that trains each:
- **Direction**: analysing organisations, trained through institutionalism by a formula of his own he calls "1-4-5-2": one element of planning, strategic planning; four pillars of work, namely leadership, members, method and system; five pillars of competition, namely training, technology, service and quality together, marketing, and creativity; and two elements of infrastructure, headquarters and money. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep22 00:26:00} The second competency is forecasting, trained through strategic planning; see [[concept/al-takhtit-al-istratiji|strategic planning]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep23 00:02:30}
- **Example**: values, trained through ethical leadership; then self-management, trained through what he calls "Arrange Your Life". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:39:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:41:30} He cites Kouzes and Posner's study in *The Leadership Challenge* of a million and a half people on five continents, in which, he says, credibility headed the traits wanted in a leader, at 83% in 1987 and 89% in 2010, followed by vision, motivation, competence and intelligence. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:06:00} He defines ethical leadership as moving followers towards the goal according to sound values, "sound" being set by the Sharia, then reason, then custom, then conscience; his model of it has five parts: thinking, emotions, convictions, talents and purposes. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep24 00:11:00}
- **Change**: managing change, trained through changing organisations; and creative thinking. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep25 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep25 00:11:00} Change for him is defining the present and moving to the desired future, and every change meets resistance that needs "resistance to the resistance". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep25 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep25 00:05:00}
- **Motivation**: analysing situations, trained through situational leadership; and influencing people. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep25 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep25 00:38:00} See [[concept/al-qiyada-al-mawqifiyya|situational leadership]].
- **Training**: analysing people, trained through personality types; and persuasion, trained through creative teaching. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:29:00} See [[concept/tahlil-disc|DISC analysis]].
- **Relations**: "people's keys", the idea that each person has something that moves him; and building synergy in teams. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:03:30} See [[concept/fariq-al-amal|teamwork]].
- **Intervention**: follow-up and oversight, trained through decision-making and problem-solving; then power and influence. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:22:30} Authority and responsibility belong to the post, he says, while influence belongs to the person, and he defines it as "the ability to bring about a change that is barred". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:28:00}
- **Empowerment**: the art of listening, his example being the Prophet's listening to [[person/utba-ibn-rabia|Utba ibn Rabi'a]] until he had finished; then delegation, which he says should go only to the keen and the competent. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:34:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:44:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:50:00}

At the end of episode 27 he declares the training in general leadership complete. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:52:30}
