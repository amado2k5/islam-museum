## Overview

Janin in the language is the child in the womb; in fiqh it names what forms there. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6576}

## Effect on the waiting period and inheritance

A divorced pregnant woman's waiting period ends with delivery; a widow's is disputed and detailed in the encyclopedia. The unborn can affect inheritance in certain cases, and its sex or number is left open for the rules of inheritance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6579} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6580}

## Assault, washing and burial

If an assault on the womb causes a dead birth, the majority hold a ghurra is due, one-twentieth of the blood-money. A child who cries out or moves is washed and prayed over; one under four months is wrapped in a cloth and buried. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6581} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6582}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
Episodes 7 and 30 of [[work/fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah|Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi]] explain Ibn Mas'ud's hadith on the stages of creation and Hudhayfa ibn Asid's hadith on the angel who enters upon the drop. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep30 00:00:00} The presenter sets out the stages of the embryo as embryology describes them, and says that the bones appear and are then clothed with muscle, as in Surat al-Mu'minun. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:04:30}

[[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Al-Sha'rawi]] says that the stages before the angel is sent are the material formation, and that the spirit comes only when the formation is complete, because it needs a vessel, as the light of electricity shows only in a bulb. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:12:30} He holds that the stage with which the verse of al-Mu'minun closes the stages of creation is the breathing in of the spirit, that before it the embryo is alive with vegetative and animal growth, and that the spirit is the secret of human, responsible life. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:15:00} He says that the embryo's provision is written while it is in its mother's womb, that it has its own provision, and he explains the stopping of menstruation in pregnancy as that blood being the embryo's food. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:17:00}

He reports that the scholars differ over harming the embryo: some said that before four months no human spirit has been breathed into it, while others saw it as a life fit to become human and forbade aborting it from the first day. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep07 00:21:30} In episode 30 he says that the angel asking whether it is male or female, a matter settled from the start, shows that the angel does not know the unseen. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep30 00:14:30} The presenter holds that physicians' knowledge of the womb is knowledge by observation, not encompassing knowledge, and that a doctor who sees the sex of the foetus by ultrasound has not thereby "known what is in the wombs". {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep30 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep30 00:08:00}
