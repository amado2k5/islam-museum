## Summary
Ihtiyal means seeking a stratagem, which is skill in arranging affairs until one reaches the aim. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1750} Usage has narrowed it to hidden paths by which a person reaches an aim that few would notice, as Ibn al-Qayyim said. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1750} A stratagem is forbidden when it breaks the rules of the law: for example, drinking wine or a sedative when the time of prayer comes so that its time passes, or giving away one's wealth before the pilgrimage to escape it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1751} The Malikis and Hanbalis forbid disposing of property before the year to escape zakat; the Hanafis differ, Muhammad disliking it and Abu Yusuf allowing it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1751}
