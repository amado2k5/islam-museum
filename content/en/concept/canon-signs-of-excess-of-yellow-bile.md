## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the signs of a dominant yellow bile in colour, mouth, tongue, thirst, breathing, vomiting, stools and dreams {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.

## The signs
Yellowness of the skin and eyes, bitterness in the mouth, roughness and dryness of the tongue, dry nostrils, pleasure in a cool breeze, strong thirst, quick breathing, weak appetite, nausea, yellow and green bilious vomiting, biting loose stools, and a shiver like the prick of needles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.

## Indications and dreams
Earlier regimen, age, temperament, habit, country, season and trade are weighed too, and dreams in which fires and yellow banners appear, things that have no yellow look yellow, and one sees inflammation and the heat of a bath or the sun {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.
