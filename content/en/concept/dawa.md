## Overview
Da'wa in Islamic usage is the conveying of Islam to people; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia lists 'conveying the Islamic call' among its entries {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia names da'wa and nida' among its related terms {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)

The Sheikh advises that whoever tries to persuade another person should enter from the ground that person stands on, and he points back to what he said in the Friday lesson about Abu Sufyan {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:31:30}. He says that the Prophet approached them through the door of pride, and that when Adi ibn Hatim came the Prophet honored him greatly and he entered Islam {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:32:00}. He relates that the chief of the Abd al-Qays in Bahrain, on hearing that the Messenger of God was coming, was not willing to meet him with the dust of travel, so he washed, perfumed himself and put on his clothes {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:32:00}.

The Sheikh says that after the Prophet was commanded to warn his nearest kin ([[passage/quran-26-214|Qur'an 26:214]]), he went to his relatives, who were preparing for their yearly summer journey to Sham; among them were Abu Talib, Hamza, al-Abbas and his other uncles {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:44:30}. He mentions that the Prophet found them busy preparing the journey and so did not raise the matter of the message with them; the Sheikh explains that the listener had not yet lent him his ear, and counts this as part of the Prophet's insight in calling to God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:44:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)

The Sheikh recalls the hadith that God guiding a single man through you is better for you than red camels, or than the world and all it contains {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:48:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 9)

The Sheikh relates that Umar asked a man who had missed the prayer with them, and the man answered that he was ill and pointed to the place of the pain in his side and neck; Umar then placed his right hand on the man's side and his left on his neck and recited the Fatiha {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:09:30}. He adds that the man then stood up as if released from a rope {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:10:00}.
