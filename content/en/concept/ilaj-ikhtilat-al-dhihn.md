## By cause
The treatment of melancholia he will give in the chapter on melancholia; as for the treatment of confusion from blood, evacuation of the blood should be hastened, with all that tempers the blood, cools it and mends its consistency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98}

## Yellow bile and the red
As for that from yellow bile and the red, its cure is to hasten and evacuate and change the temperament, either from the whole body or from the head in particular, using the regimens and moistenings stated in the Canon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98} If it grows severe and strong it is managed as mania is managed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98} When sleeplessness is present, he says all means of this kind are unhelpful, and the sharp may bring it on, so the sharp is not used and the gentle is followed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98}

## Where an organ shares
For what comes from the sharing of an organ, the strengthening of the head, its cooling and the drawing of the matter away are used, and all this is known from the earlier general and particular rules {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98}
