## Nature and selection
Known in red, black and white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}. The editor's footnote says bassad is red coral.

## Temperament and actions
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}. Its drying is stronger than its binding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: astringent and stops bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}.

Wounds and ulcers: cuts away proud flesh {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

Eyes: strengthens the eye by cleansing and drying the moisture lodged in it, especially the washed burnt form; it clears the marks of ulcers and suits tear flow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

Excretory organs: holds back spitting of blood and helps spitting; the black kind also does, especially the washed burnt form; it is one of the medicines that strengthen the heart and help palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

Digestion: taken in water for swelling of the spleen, it helps it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

Excretory organs: helps ulcers of the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.
