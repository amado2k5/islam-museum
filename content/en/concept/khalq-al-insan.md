## Summary
In the Sheikh's lessons, the creation of man follows successive stages, the soul is breathed in at the end, and he uses this as evidence for resurrection. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:27:00}

## The stages
He says that man was created from dust mixed with water and became clay, then was exposed to fire in its stages, then became fermented mud and then dried clay (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:27:00}

## The soul
He says the soul is breathed in at the end of creation, so it is the last thing formed in a person, and that it leaves at death (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:27:30}

## The coccyx
He relates a report about a bone at the base of the spine, the coccyx, from which the body is re-made on the Day of Resurrection, and that it does not decay (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:28:00}
