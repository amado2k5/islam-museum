## Summary
In chapter ten Ibn Sina compares women's urine with men's and describes that of pregnant women, women after childbirth, and men after intercourse. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.197}.

## Women and men
Women's urine is in every case thicker, whiter and less lustrous than men's, because of their more abundant waste, weaker digestion, the wideness of their outlets and what flows into the urinary organs from their wombs. Men's urine turns turbid when stirred, the turbidity going upward, while women's is not made turbid by stirring, owing to its little separation, and usually has a round foam on top. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.197}.

## After intercourse, in pregnancy and after childbirth
A man's urine after intercourse holds threads woven into one another. The urine of pregnant women is clear with a mist at the top, perhaps the colour of chickpea water, with something like teased cotton in the middle; if blue is very plain it is early pregnancy, and if red takes its place it is late. The urine of a woman after childbirth is mostly black, like ink and soot. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.197}.
