## Summary
Ibn Sina gives the nail four uses and says it was made of soft bones that keep growing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.57}.

## The four uses
First, to be a support for the fingertip so that it does not weaken when pressing on something. Second, to let the finger pick up small things. Third, to let it clean and scratch. Fourth, to be a weapon at times. The first three belong more to humans and the fourth to other animals {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.57}.

## Make-up
The nail was made round at its tip, of soft bones so that it gives way under what strikes it and does not split, and made to keep growing since it is exposed to rubbing and wearing away {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.57}.
