## Overview
The scrolls are the records of deeds kept by the noble scribes; on the Day they are spread and every soul is handed its record {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the texts
The Qur'an names the ways the record is taken: in the right hand, an easy reckoning and a joyful return [[passage/quran-84-7|the surat al-Inshiqaq verses]]; in the left or behind the back, a cry of ruin and a blaze [[passage/quran-84-10|what follows]]; and 'read your record — your soul suffices today as reckoner over you' [[passage/quran-17-14|the record-reading verse]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. In the hadith the believer first denies what is in his record, then admits it {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
