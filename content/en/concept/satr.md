## Overview
Veiling a Muslim (*satr*) is one of the deeds of helping a brother. It occurs in the thirty-sixth hadith of the Forty of al-Nawawi: "Whoever veils a Muslim, God will veil him in this world and the next." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## Its meaning
Ibn Rajab explains veiling as withholding people's view of a brother's nakedness in his religion or his worldly affairs, and not exposing his concealment. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## Its place in the hadith
Veiling is paired with relieving distress and easing the lot of one in difficulty, and with the principle "God is in the aid of the servant as long as the servant is in the aid of his brother." Veiling is a kind of help, and it is rewarded in kind in both abodes. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

It is connected with the concept of [[concept/ukhuwwa|brotherhood]].
