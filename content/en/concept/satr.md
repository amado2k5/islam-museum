## Overview
Veiling a Muslim (*satr*) is one of the deeds of helping a brother. It occurs in the thirty-sixth hadith of the Forty of al-Nawawi: "Whoever veils a Muslim, God will veil him in this world and the next." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## Its meaning
Ibn Rajab explains veiling as withholding people's view of a brother's nakedness in his religion or his worldly affairs, and not exposing his concealment. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

## Its place in the hadith
Veiling is paired with relieving distress and easing the lot of one in difficulty, and with the principle "God is in the aid of the servant as long as the servant is in the aid of his brother." Veiling is a kind of help, and it is rewarded in kind in both abodes. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.731}

It is connected with the concept of [[concept/ukhuwwa|brotherhood]].

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series Nafahat Ramadaniyya (the faults of the tongue)
The Sheikh counts failing to cover a Muslim's fault as a fault of the tongue, saying that whoever sees a fault in his brother with his eyes or hears of it with his ears must cover it; he cites Abu Hurayra's hadith that whoever covers a servant in this world God will cover on the Day of Resurrection, and the hadith 'the Muslim is the brother of the Muslim' as narrated by Muslim. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:01:30} He cites the verse of Surat al-Nur on those who love that indecency should spread. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:01:30}
He relates that [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] asked from the pulpit what if the Commander of the Faithful saw a man and a woman committing indecency, and [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] answered that unless he had three more witnesses to complete the four, the penalty for false accusation would fall on him (needs_source: the story is the Sheikh's narration). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:02:30}
He holds that covering helps the sinner to repent, while exposure may push him to persist in the sin and flaunt it, making the exposer Satan's helper against his brother; he mentions the hadith on pursuing people's faults. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:05:00} He holds that spreading what was hidden about observant people makes people lose trust in one another, and may encourage sinners to sin openly. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:06:30}
His alternative is private counsel, since counsel in public is exposure: either the sinner turns back and the adviser becomes the cause of his guidance, or he relapses and is gently advised again, or he refuses, and then one may ask the Friday preacher to speak about repentance without naming him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:09:30} He holds that affliction in religion is the worst affliction, so a Muslim stands by his sinning brother as one visits the sick. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:10:30}
He relates the answer of [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn ibn Ali]] to a man who complained that he kept returning to sin: let him disobey God in a land not His, in a place where He does not see him, and without eating from His provision; and the man repented (needs_source: the attribution to al-Husayn is the Sheikh's narration). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:11:30} He closes with [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]]'s prayer to be covered on the Day of Resurrection, and the report of Umar, who lost his ablution on the pulpit, went to renew it and then told the people, saying that disgrace in this world is lighter than disgrace in the next. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:13:30} See also: [[concept/tajassus-wa-tahassus|spying]].

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh tells an Israelite story: God withheld rain from the Children of Israel; Musa prayed and was told that a sinner among them was the reason and rain would not come until he left; no one left, for fear of exposure; then rain fell, and when Musa asked he was told the sinner had repented; he asked to be shown him so as to learn from him, and was told: shall I cover him while he sins and expose him once he repents? (needs_source: an Israelite story as the Sheikh tells it). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:07:00} He thanks God that sins have no smell, recalls Abu Bakr's prayer not to be exposed on the Day of Resurrection and the hadith of the bankrupt who comes with prayer and fasting but had insulted one and taken another's money, and is thrown into the Fire; and he mentions a prayer his teachers taught him more than forty years ago: what is between me and You, forgive it; what is between me and Your servants, bear its consequences for me. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep16 00:09:30}
