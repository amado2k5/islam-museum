## Summary
Al-Battani says that by this figure he worked out the variation degree by degree and entered it in the tables, starting from the point of greatest distance, and that by the same figure the Moon's single equation and the planets' mean equation are made, which is the radius of the epicycle of each of them {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.

## The method when it is needed
He says that whoever wants to do this by calculation should look at the parts the planet, Sun or Moon runs on the epicycle from the point of greatest distance, the argument named, and if it is less than a limit work with it, and if more take its complement. He then follows steps that give the half chord and the arc, set out in [[concept/zij-sabi-chord-rule-for-the-equation-at-any-anomaly|the chord rule for the equation at any anomaly]]. The numerals marking the limits are disturbed in the printed text, so the exact limit is not given here {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.
