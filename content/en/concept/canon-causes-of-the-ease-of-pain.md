## Summary
In chapter 21 Ibn Sina says pain eases either by what cuts off and evacuates its cause, as dill and flaxseed do as a poultice, or by what moistens and puts to sleep, like intoxicants, or by what cools and numbs, like narcotics, and he holds that the true soother is the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.150-151}.

## Three ways
Pain is eased either by what cuts off its cause and evacuates it, as dill and flaxseed when applied as a poultice to the painful place; or by what moistens and induces sleep, so that the sensing power sinks and leaves its work, as intoxicants do; or by what cools and numbs, like all narcotics {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.150-151}.

## The true soother
He says the true soother is the first, the one that removes the cause itself; the other two ease by dulling feeling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.
