## Summary
The cause of boring pain is thick matter or wind confined between the layers of a hard, thick organ such as the colon, tearing it and passing through as if drilled; the cause of piercing pain is that same matter, held back at the time of its tearing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## Boring
The cause of boring pain is thick matter or wind confined between the layers of a hard, thick organ, such as the body of the colon intestine; it keeps tearing it and passing through it, so that it feels as if drilled with a borer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## Piercing
The cause of piercing pain is that very matter in such an organ, except that it is held back at the time it is tearing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.
