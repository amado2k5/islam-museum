## Overview
an-Nur — The Light. It is name number 93 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-14-1|14:1]]، [[passage/quran-14-5|14:5]]، [[passage/quran-2-257|2:257]]، [[passage/quran-33-43|33:43]]، [[passage/quran-35-20|35:20]]، [[passage/quran-5-16|5:16]]، [[passage/quran-57-9|57:9]]، [[passage/quran-65-11|65:11]]، [[passage/quran-7-157|7:157]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/muslim-0179|179]].
