## Definition
Awqas are the plural of waqs, the gap between two prescribed amounts in zakat on camels, cattle and sheep; in lexicon waqs also means a short neck. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3686}

## Related terms
- Ashnaq: the gap between two prescribed amounts; some jurists restrict shanaq to camels and waqs to cattle and sheep. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3687}
- Afw: the same gap, named for being pardoned from zakat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3687}

## Ranks of the gaps
- Camels: five ranks, among them the four between five and ten, the ten between the bint makhad and the bint labun, and the nine between the bint labun and the hiqqa. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3687}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3688}
- Cattle: two counts, the nine between thirty and forty, and the nineteen between forty and sixty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3688}
- Sheep: three ranks, beginning with eighty, then seventy-nine, then ninety-nine. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3688}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3691}

## Zakat on the gaps
- Sheep: no zakat is due on the gaps, by agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3690}
- Camels: the Hanafis (Abu Hanifa and Abu Yusuf), the older view of al-Shafi'i and one Maliki view deny zakat; Muhammad, Zufar, the preferred Maliki view and a report of al-Shafi'i affirm it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3689}
- Cattle between forty and sixty: the majority hold no zakat is due; Abu Hanifa has three reports, one counting the excess in proportion, and one making nothing due until fifty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3689}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3690}
