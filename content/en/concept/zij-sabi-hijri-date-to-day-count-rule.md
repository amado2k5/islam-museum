## Summary
Al-Battani gives a rule for turning a Hijri date into a count of days: multiply the completed years by 354 and five-sixths of a day, add the days of the months that have passed, and the sum is the number of days since the start of the Hijra. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.

## Handling the fraction
If a fraction remains and is less than half a day it is dropped; if it is more than half a day a whole day is added. The reckoning thus deals with fractions without keeping a part of a day in the total. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.
