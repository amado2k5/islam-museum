## Nature and selection
Some, namely Paulus, said it is the gum of an herb like myrrh, sweet-smelling, and to be used with care. Others called this a mistake and said it is amber (kahraba); another said that this is lak but that lak in many qualities has the power of amber. The editor's note calls it the gum of an Indian plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Cosmetic uses
Strongly slimming {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It helps and strengthens the liver, and helps jaundice, dropsy, pains of the liver and obstructions of the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.
