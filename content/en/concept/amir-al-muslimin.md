## Origin
The Almoravids first assumed the title amir al-Muslimin, in contradistinction to amir al-mu'minin, which had been borne by the independent dynasties. They recognized the supremacy of the 'Abbasids and did not wish to take the caliphs' title, so they established a kind of sub-caliphate with a title of their own. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 467}

## Later use
Afterwards the African and Spanish princes bore either the one title or the other, according as they sought an independent caliphate or recognized any supremacy. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 467}
