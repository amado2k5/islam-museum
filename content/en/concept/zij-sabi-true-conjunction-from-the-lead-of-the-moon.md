## Summary
Al-Battani explains how the true conjunction is found from the mean: one looks at the difference between the places of the Sun and the Moon. If they coincide to within one minute, that is the true time of conjunction; if they differ, the difference in degrees and minutes is taken, with its sixth and eighth, depending on which is greater. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.141}.

## Adjusting the anomaly by the difference
A sixth and an eighth of the difference are added to or taken from the Moon's anomaly, according to whether the difference belongs to the Sun or the Moon; the result enters the equation tables, and the single equation is added to or taken from the motion in latitude. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.141}.
