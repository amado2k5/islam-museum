## Summary
Al-Battani says the earlier writers held that the crescent cannot be seen in less than a day and a night, which is the rule they worked by; he says the visibility found by observation is close to this limit, but on closer examination it is not seen exactly and is only approximate. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.129}.

## Applying the rule
He says that when the Moon moves its least and the Sun its greatest, that is when the Moon is at its farthest point and the Sun at its nearest on the epicycle, the arc of vision changes with that contrast. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.132}.
