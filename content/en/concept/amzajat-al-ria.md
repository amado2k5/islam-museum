## The lung's temperaments and their signs
The most particular proofs of the states of chest and lung are the breath — in its heat, cold, greatness, smallness, ease, difficulty, fetor and sweet smell — and likewise the voice, as the strangling voice shows the harm lies in the dilating muscles and the hoarse in the contracting. The lung neighbours the heart, so inference from the heart's states to it is strong; the pulse tells most of what lies toward the lung's nerve-branches and the cough of what lies toward the windpipe; the lung-flesh and the feeling of sediment is a special proof that the matter is in the lung, and the feeling of stinging and pricking that the matter is in the membranes and the muscles. If the spitting-up is by a light cough, the matter is near the windpipe's top; if it is spat only by a strong cough, the matter is deep and far {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.304}
