## The apogees from the epoch
The text says the apogees of the wandering planets, those of Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, the Sun and Mercury, are written at the head of their equation tables for the epoch year, which is the year 1191 of the era of Dhu al-Qarnayn. These apogees also move with the sphere of the fixed stars, one degree in every sixty-six solar years and one degree in every sixty-eight lunar years. The drift for the years between the epoch and the wanted year is taken, added to the apogee when the year is after the epoch and subtracted when it is before. The text does not give the conversion of this era to the Common Era. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.176-177}

## The conversion of the year is not given
The text does not give the conversion of the Dhu al-Qarnayn year to the Common Era, so no conversion is made here; it is left to the editor to document it from another source. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.177}
