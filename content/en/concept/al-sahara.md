## Summary
Pharaoh's sorcerers gathered to contest Musa; they threw their ropes and staffs which seemed to slither by their magic — Musa cast his staff and it swallowed their fabrications, and the sorcerers believed in the Lord of the worlds {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-farmawi-1997}.
