## Overview
The angels who bless the Prophet are the angels who bless the Prophet at every moment, as in [[passage/quran-33-56|Al-Ahzab 56]] "God and His angels bless the Prophet". They are among the clearest reported works of the angels in the Qur'an {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-33-56|Al-Ahzab 56]]: "God and His angels bless the Prophet; O you who believe, bless him and greet him with a worthy greeting" — in it that God and His angels bless the Prophet {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In the tafsir, "blessing" from God is His mercy and favour, and from the angels it is supplication for him of mercy and blessing {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- In a narration, "the angels" in the verse are those who bless the Prophet at every moment {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In hadith
- "Whoever blesses me, God blesses him tenfold" — in a narration of Muslim {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- "Whoever blesses me ten times, God blesses him a hundredfold; whoever blesses me a hundred times, God blesses him a thousandfold; whoever blesses me a thousand times, God admits him to Paradise" — in a narration of al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- "The first of people to reach me on the Day of Judgment will be those who bless me most" — in a narration of al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- "God and His angels and their prayers and the angels of the heavens..." — in it that blessing from God and the angels reaches the Prophet {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- "The angels do not tire of blessing you" — in it that they bless the believers {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## The meaning of blessing from the angels
- In the tafsir, "blessing" from God is His mercy and favour, and from the angels it is supplication for him of mercy and blessing {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- In a narration, "blessing" from God is not like the blessing of creatures, but is His mercy and favour {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- In a hadith, "blessing" from God is praise of Him, and from the angels it is supplication for him {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that "blessing" in [[passage/quran-33-56|Al-Ahzab 56]] from God is His mercy, and from the angels it is supplication for him of mercy and blessing {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. Al-Qurtubi holds that the angels bless the Prophet at every moment {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## Notes for researchers
- "Angels of blessing the Prophet" is a technical name; the Qur'an calls them "angels" in [[passage/quran-33-56|Al-Ahzab 56]] {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In it is evidence of the Prophet's greatness and his standing with God and the angels {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- Their companion in this topic is [[concept/malaika|the angels]] on its page.
