## In hadith
Al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and Muslim narrate that God and His angels send salawat on the Prophet, and that whoever sends salawat on him has ten recorded for him {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## What the angels' salawat means
Commentators detail that the angels' salawat on the Prophet is supplication for his mercy and blessing, among the reasons for his honour and exalted station {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some held 'they send salawat' here means supplication rather than the ritual prayer, others that it is literal prayer; the primary view takes it as supplication for mercy and blessing {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
