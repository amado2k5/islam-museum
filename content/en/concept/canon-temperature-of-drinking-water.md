## Summary
Ibn Sina says that very cold water is poor drinking unless taken after a sufficient meal, that lukewarm water brings nausea, and that heated water in quantity weakens the stomach but, drunk now and then, washes the stomach and loosens the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## Cold, lukewarm and heated
Drinking very cold water is poor, and if it cannot be avoided it should follow a sufficient meal. Lukewarm water brings nausea. Water heated beyond that, if taken in quantity, weakens the stomach, while drunk from time to time it washes the stomach and loosens nature {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.
