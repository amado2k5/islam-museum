## Summary
A maternal aunt's son by blood is the male blood child of a blood sister of the mother. By fostering, he is one in whom the foster tie takes the place of the blood tie. The word child covers male and female; the word son covers only the male. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1462}

## General ruling
The jurists agree that he is among those with blood-ties through a female who are not mahrams: the duty of kinship, permission to marry, the prohibition of being alone with them, and no duty of maintenance unless he is an heir apply to him. A foster maternal aunt's son shares most of these rules. They also agree that he is among those with blood-ties through a female, with special rules in inheritance, leading the funeral prayer and guardianship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1463}

## Where it is discussed
The rules are set out in the entry on kinship (arham) and in the chapters on marriage, maintenance, inheritance and funerals. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1463}
