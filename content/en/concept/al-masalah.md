## Summary
Asking people for help (al-masala) means asking others for gifts. Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy presents the Prophet's teaching to the beggar and the needy on this point. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:08:30}

## Poverty is not a trade
He says poverty is not a trade a person follows, and that Islam gives the needy their right to help, not the honour of poverty. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:09:00}

## The man with the cup and the mat
He recounts that a poor man asked the Prophet, who asked what he owned; the man said he had a cup to drink from and a mat, part of which he spread and part of which he wore. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:09:00} The Prophet sold them to whoever would pay more, gave the man two dirhams, and told him to buy food with one and an axe with the other. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:10:00} He was told to cut wood and sell it; he came back after fifteen days with much money. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:10:00}

## Asking only in real need
He says asking is lawful only for a person in extreme poverty, under a crushing debt, or facing a painful calamity, and that asking leaves its mark on the face of the one who asked on the Day of Judgement. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:10:30} (needs_source: the three categories are not clear in the automatic transcript.)

## Complaint is made to God
He warns against complaining to people about one's family, neighbours, the times or poverty, and says complaint is made to God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:11:00}
