## Summary
At the sacrum there are two bones, right and left, joined in the middle by a secured joint; they are like the foundation of all the bones above and carry and transmit to those below {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.57}.

## Its parts
Each of the two bones divides into four parts. The part toward the outer side is called the ilium, the flank bone; the part to the front, the pubic bone; the part to the back, the ischium; and the part below and inward is the socket of the thigh, since it holds the hollow into which the convex head of the thigh bone enters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.57-58}. On this bone lie noble organs such as the bladder, the womb, the seminal vessels in men and the anus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.58}.
