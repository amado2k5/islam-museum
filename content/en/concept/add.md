## Summary
In the lexicon, add is gripping a thing with the teeth and holding it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11648}.

## Liability for a bite without right
A person who bites another without right and causes a wound must pay the damages for the wound, assessed as a just award by experts {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11648}.

## When the bitten man pulls free and the biter's teeth fall out
Most jurists hold that there is no liability in that case, relying on a prophetic judgement in such a case. The Shafi'is limit this to a bitten man who used the easiest means of release; the preferred Maliki view requires compensation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11648} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11649}.
