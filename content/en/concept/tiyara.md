## Overview
Tatayyur: taking omens from birds, days, houses, a woman or a horse; among the Jahiliyya beliefs Islam abolished.

## The story
They used to take omens from birds when they startled them, and from days, towns, houses, a woman, a horse and a slave; tiyara is part of shirk {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

Islam came declaring: "No contagion and no tiyara", while liking the good omen, which is the good word {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
They believed a bird or a day could bring ill luck; Islam taught them this is of shirk, and that the good omen is good.
