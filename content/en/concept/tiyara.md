## Summary
Tiyara (omen-taking) means taking an ill omen from a thing. Originally, they would come upon a bird or a gazelle and startle it. If it went to the right, they proceeded with what they intended and considered it good; if it went to the left, they desisted and took it as an ill omen. They would also take ill omen if a bird or an animal crossed their path {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 2/851-857}.

## Related practices
Close to omen-taking are their hanging of a rabbit's ankle-bone, their taking ill omen from certain days, months, animals, houses and women, and their belief in contagion and in the [[concept/hama|hama]] {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38}.

## Not covered here
The hadith "There is no contagion and no omen-taking" and the rulings on omen-taking will be presented under their own entries. The author refers to Sahih al-Bukhari with the commentary of al-Saharanfuri {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38}.

## For young visitors
They believed that a bird or a day could bring misfortune. Islam taught them that this is superstition.

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] explains the hadith that 'iyafa, tiyara and tarq are of the jibt, describing the bird-scaring in which people released birds, taking a good omen if they flew right and giving up their plan if they flew left, and says all of it runs against trust in God and the certainty that nothing happens but by His command. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep16 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep16 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep16 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep16 00:05:00} He cites Mu'awiya ibn al-Hakam al-Sulami's question about people who take ill omens, and the Prophet's answer that it is something they find in their breasts and should not let it turn them back. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep16 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep16 00:09:30}
