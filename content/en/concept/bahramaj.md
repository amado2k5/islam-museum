## Nature and selection
One of the sweet herbs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}. The editor's footnote gives it as the balkhiyya {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}.

## Temperament and actions
Its liniment dissolves wind from every part {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}.

## Uses by organ
Head: its blossom is good for thick wind in the head, and smelling its leaf does the same {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}. Excretory organs: it loosens the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}.
