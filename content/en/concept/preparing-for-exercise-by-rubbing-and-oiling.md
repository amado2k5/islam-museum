## Summary
Ibn Sina says the one who will exercise begins with a rubbing by something rough, then anoints himself with a sweet oil and works the anointing gradually until the organ is pressed, though not too deeply, by many hands meeting it from different positions so as to reach every fibre of the muscle, then leaves off and goes into exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.224}.

## The order
The one who exercises should rub himself with something rough, then anoint himself with a sweet oil, then work the anointing by degrees until the organ is pressed without going very deep. This is done with many hands in varying positions of contact so that it reaches all the fibres of the muscle; then it stops and the rubbed person takes up the exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.224}.
