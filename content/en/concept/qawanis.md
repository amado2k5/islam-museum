## Actions and properties
The gizzards of birds are rich in nourishment, and those of hens are not digested quickly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
They claim that the inner layer of the gizzard is drying and benefits the mouth of the stomach and its pain, especially the gizzards of cocks; this is attributed to Ibn Masawayh {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}
