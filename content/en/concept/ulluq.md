## Summary
In the lexicon, ulluq means clinging and lodging; in the jurists' usage it is conception by intercourse or by emission {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11801}.

## Lineage through conception on the marriage bed
When a man able to father children has intercourse with his wife, no one else sharing it, and she bears a child within the period of gestation, the child is attributed to him by agreement {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11802}.

## The woman under a revocable divorce
The child she conceived is attributed to the husband even if the birth comes more than two years later, since her cleanliness and conception may have been delayed; intercourse during the waiting period restores the marriage, and birth is evidence of the return {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11802}.

## Effect on bequests and inheritance
Conception affects a bequest to an unborn child, and in inheritance the share is held until the birth {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11802}.

## Where to look further
Lineage is treated under «nasab» and return to a wife under «raj'a» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11802}.
