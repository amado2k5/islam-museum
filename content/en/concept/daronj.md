## Nature and selection
Woody root pieces about the size of a knot or smaller, white inside and grey outside, hard and dense {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}. The editor's footnote gives a Latin name, which is not used here.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}. Actions: it disperses winds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}.

## Uses by organ
Chest: it strengthens the heart and helps palpitation very much {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}.
Excretory organs: it disperses womb winds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}; a labelled book listing.
Poisons: it helps poisons and the stings of scorpions and tarantulas, drunk or as a poultice with fig {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}.
Substitutes: zarnabad, and cloves in two-thirds of its place {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.433}.
