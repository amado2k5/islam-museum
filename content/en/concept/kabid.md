## Actions and properties
The blood produced from livers is thick; the best is the liver of fattened duck and fattened hen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.530}.

## Head, ears and teeth
The liver of the goat, especially the he-goat, is said to expose the epileptic, who falls in a fit when he eats it. The liver of the gecko on eaten-away teeth quiets their pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.530}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
The liver of the wolf is said to help all pains of the liver. Galen said that he put it into the remedy of agrimony and found no added benefit over the remedy without it. The liver is slow to pass through the vessels, except the liver of fattened duck {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.531}.

## Poisons and bites
The liver of the rabid dog is given to drink and is said to help one it has bitten; they have said that it prevents the dread of water, and some of them lived, though they were also treated with other treatments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.531}.
