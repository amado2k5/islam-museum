## Summary
The transience of the world in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's teaching: this world is a temporary visit after which people return to their true abode, and it is fickle and does not last. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:24:00} He gives examples of a vanishing palace and of tyrants who became mere traces. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:18:30}

## The flawed palace
The Sheikh tells of a king who built a palace and admired it, until a man pointed out two defects in it: its ruin after a time, and the death of its owner. He then goes on to mention tyrants who became traces (needs_source: the parable is not named in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:17:00}

## The speaker's view: the fall of tyrants and the warning about water
The Sheikh refers to the pharaohs who have become traces in museums, and he expects the coming conflict to be over water and warns against its pollution (needs_source: the forecast of a conflict over water is the speaker's own and has not been verified). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:18:30}

## The world is an hour, and contentment
The Sheikh cites sayings handed down that call for making the world a means of obedience and for contentment, and he describes the world as fickle and not lasting (needs_source: the sayings are not attributed in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:19:00}

## The speaker's view: the world is a temporary visit
The Sheikh holds that people in this world are on a temporary visit, after which they return to the true place they set out for. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:24:00}

## The speaker's view: a person's span within the world
The Sheikh holds that what matters to a person is not the world's age of millions of years, but the period he lives in it, and so he should take account of his own life span. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep12 00:29:00}

## Noah asked about the world
The Sheikh tells that Noah was asked about the world and said that he found it like a house with two doors: he entered by one and left by the other, and that a person in it is like a rider who rested under a tree and then moved on (needs_source: the saying is not sourced in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep12 00:29:30}
