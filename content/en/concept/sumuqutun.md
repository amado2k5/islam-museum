## Nature
It is said to be the houseleek, or a kind of mandrake, or something else, and it is of two kinds, rocky and not rocky {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Temperament
Cold and dryness prevail in it, with a moderate hot moisture and a fineness by which it cuts, and a viscosity by which it dissolves and a quality by which it gathers and constricts; it has no scent and no sweetness, and it draws saliva {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Actions and properties
It joins the pieces of meat in the pot until they become one thing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Joints
Its decoction is for tearing of the nerves and muscles in their middles and ends, and it joins fresh wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.597}.

## Organs of the chest
It heals roughness of the throat and checks spitting of blood, and in honey water it cleans the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.597}.

## Excretory organs
It helps ulcers of the intestines and abrasion, watery hernia and pains of the kidney {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.597}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it checks the flow of the menses, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.597}.
