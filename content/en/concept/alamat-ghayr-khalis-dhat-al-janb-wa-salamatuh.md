## The signs of the non-true pleurisy and of its safety and badness
Its safety is indicated by the easy, quick, ripe expectoration — the white, smooth, even — and a pulse not too hard and sawy, littleness of pain and the rest of the symptoms, soundness of sleep and breath, acceptance of the treatment and the patient's bearing what is in him (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.345}
