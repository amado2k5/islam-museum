## Summary
Al-Battani explains how to reckon the conjunctions and oppositions of the Sun and the Moon in any month of the Roman calendar: one takes the years of Dhu al-Qarnayn, without counting the current year until February is over, and then looks up the same number of years in the combined tables of conjunction or opposition. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.138}.

## Leap years in the reckoning
He handles the leap year by taking the extra days of the leap year and counting them against the quarters; a remaining fraction is ignored, and in a leap year one day is removed from the quarter days unless February has already passed. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.139}.
