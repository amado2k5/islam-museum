## Summary
In the idiom of Qur'an and hadith, al-sayyi'at are «the evil deed» and its plural: every ugly act, sin and disobedience written against the servant and requited. They are paired with «al-hasanat», the righteous deeds, in God's promise and His writing: [[passage/quran-11-114|good deeds annul ill-deeds]]، the hadith qudsi «if My servant intends an evil deed…» narrated by Muslim in [[passage/muslim-0128|his Sahih]]، and its parallel in [[passage/bukhari-7501|Sahih al-Bukhari]]. This record gathers their theme in the museum, and the [[/en/sayyiat/|Sayyi'at Browser]] gathers it with every mention across the Qur'an، the Sunnah، the seerah and the harvested books.

## What is called sayyi'at
The lexicons and fiqh books gather under it what is named ithm، dhanb and ma'siya، and the books of zuhd and raqa'iq devote chapters to it: «righteousness is good character، and sin is what gnaws at your soul and you dislike people seeing» — as in Muslim's report at [[passage/muslim-2553|2553]] and al-Muwatta's parallel at [[passage/arbaun-27|Forty 27]]. Al-Nawawi makes «follow the evil deed with a good one and it will erase it» a comprehensive counsel in [[passage/arbaun-18|Forty 18]].

## Good deeds annul them
God says in [[passage/quran-11-114|Surah Hud 114]] that good deeds annul ill-deeds — the promise repeated across the repentance reports: «those who did evil in ignorance then repented and reformed — your Lord is thereafter Forgiving and Merciful» in [[passage/quran-16-119|al-Nahl 119]]. And «whoever does an evil deed will not be requited except the like of it» in [[passage/quran-40-40|Ghafir 40]].

## Small and great
The verse divides sins into what earns vast forgiveness and what does not: «those who shun the major sins and immoralities — except the slips» in [[passage/quran-53-32|al-Najm 32]]. The hadith qudsi records that when the servant intends an evil deed it is not written against him until he does it، and when he intends a good deed it is not written for him until he does it; whoever brings an evil deed is requited its like or forgiven — as in [[passage/muslim-2687|Muslim 2687]] and [[passage/ibnmajah-3821|Ibn Majah 3821]]. «Among the major sins is a man's reviling his parents» is counted among the clearest examples the tradition names in [[passage/muslim-0090|Muslim 90]].

## Repentance and seeking forgiveness
The story of repenting from sin recurs in the reports: «My servants، all of you are sinners except those I have spared — ask Me forgiveness and I will forgive you» in [[passage/muslim-2577|Muslim 2577]]، «My servant sinned a sin and said: my Lord، forgive me my sin» in [[passage/muslim-2758|Muslim 2758]]، Yusuf's seeking forgiveness for his brother in [[passage/quran-12-97|Yusuf 97]] and the believers' prayer «our Lord، forgive us our sins» in [[passage/quran-3-147|Al 'Imran 147]]. «He leaves neither small nor great but enumerates it» in [[passage/quran-18-49|al-Kahf 49]] — every small or great deed is recorded in the Book.
