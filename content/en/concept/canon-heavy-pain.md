## Summary
Heavy pain is caused by a swelling in an insensitive organ such as the lung, kidney or spleen, which sinks by its weight and drags the organ by its wrappings, or by a swelling in a sensitive organ whose own pain has abolished its sense, such as a cancer at the mouth of the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## An insensitive organ
The cause of heavy pain is a swelling in an organ that does not feel, such as the lung, kidney and spleen: by its weight the swelling is drawn downward and pulls the organ by the wrapping and the sheath as it sinks {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## Sense abolished by pain
Or it is a swelling in a sensitive organ whose pain has itself done away with its sense, as with a cancer at the mouth of the stomach: its weight is felt, but it does not hurt because it has abolished feeling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.
