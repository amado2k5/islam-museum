## Summary
Al-Battani holds that the Moon's motion has two inequalities. The first appears alone only at conjunction and opposition with the Sun; the second arises from the Moon's distance from the Sun. The two combine into one effect in the reckoning. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.

## Separating the two
He separates the single inequality, seen only at conjunctions and oppositions and tied to the Moon's place on its epicycle, from the second inequality, which follows the Moon's distance from the Sun and comes from the eccentric sphere. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.
