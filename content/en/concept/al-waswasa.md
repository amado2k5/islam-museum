## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that Satan began his enmity to Adam by causing him to slip, that his whisper seeks disobedience by any means, while the self's whisper wants one specific thing {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.266} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.267} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.268}. See [[passage/quran-20-117|Ta-Ha 117]].

## Enmity already known
He says the enmity had been declared before. Adam had seen Iblis's pride when he refused to prostrate, and that should have warned him {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.266}.

## Satan's whisper and the self's whisper
He distinguishes Satan's whisper, which wants you disobedient by any sin, so that if you refuse on one side he comes from another, from the self's whisper, which wants one particular thing {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.267} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.268}. He notes that Satan promised Adam a fruit that makes its eater everlasting and a kingdom that never ends, and holds that this lie did not succeed {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.268}. See [[passage/quran-7-20|al-A'raf 20]].

## Forgetting
He holds that what brought Adam into disobedience was heedlessness or forgetting, and that forgetting was a disobedience in earlier communities. He cites a hadith that lifts error and forgetting from this nation {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.268}. See [[passage/quran-20-115|Ta-Ha 115]].

## Not yet checked
This is Sha'rawi's reading. The hadith he cites is in the scan's footnote and has not been checked at its collection. No study of forgetting in the law of obligation is set out here, and none is checked.

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
Episode 5 of [[work/fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah|Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi]] explains the hadith of people asking until they ask who created God, with the instruction in its versions to declare faith in God or to seek refuge and stop; the presenter says Muslim records it from Anas, with versions from Abu Hurayra. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:00:30}

[[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Al-Sha'rawi]] says that the mind is tethered as a camel is tethered, and that each faculty has its field: the eye has a range, the ear a range, and reason a field it should not exceed. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:18:00} He argues that God is Creator and uncreated because no one has ever come forward claiming to have created, and a claim stands for its maker until someone contests it. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:20:30} He then sets out circularity and infinite regress (al-dawr wa-l-tasalsul): one from two, two from three, so that either you return to the first, which is circular, or regress without end; both are invalid, so there must be a stop that is neither. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:21:30} He holds that those who asked this question fell into atheism because they used reason on what is above it. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:23:00}

He says that a person cannot see, smell or taste the spirit that runs his body; if he cannot perceive a created thing within himself, how would he perceive its Creator? {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:26:30} He reads the version with seeking refuge as showing that the question comes from Satan, who enters at the weak spot, and notes that Satan named four directions and left out above and below: whoever keeps in mind the might of Lordship above him and the humility of servanthood below him is not reached by him. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep05 00:30:00}
