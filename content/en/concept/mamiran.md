## Nature and selection
A wood like knots leaning to black with a slight bend, sharper than the dyers' roots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry at the end of the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Actions and properties
Cleansing and purifying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Cosmetic uses
It cleanses the whiteness of the nails {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Its juice draws thick humidity from the head and purifies the superfluities of the brain, and its root helps toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Eyes
It purifies the whiteness in the eye and sharpens sight when used as kohl, and cleanses the thick humours, especially its juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its root helps jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Excretory organs
It helps colic and promotes urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.
