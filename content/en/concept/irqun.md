## Nature
Dioscorides claimed that irqun is a plant with a long, cleft leaf like that of the anemone and a round, pungent root that is eaten; he also mentioned another kind with fine branches carrying a leaf like the leaf of mallow, and at the tips of the branches something protruding like the head and beak of a crane. It has no scope in the craft of medicine but in another craft, which the author thinks it unfitting to name here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}

## Actions and properties
Drunk with wine it dissolves wind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as dissolving the winds that swell the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.622}
