## Nature and causes
He says that by the head he means the brain and its membranes, and that he does not deal here with diseases of the hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.9}

The brain suffers the kinds of ill-temperament, single ones and ones with matter, the matter being either vaporous or having body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.9} Diseases of moisture are frequent in it, since every brain has at the first creation a superfluous moisture that needs to be cleared, and if it is not cleared the trouble grows {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.9} All of these are in the substance of the brain, in its vessels or in its membranes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.9}

It suffers diseases of composition, either in size, being smaller or larger than it should be, or in shape, departing from the natural course, and an affliction in its acts follows {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.9}

Its channels and vessels may be blocked, the blockage being in the front ventricle, in the rear one or in both, partial or complete, or in the veins and arteries or in the roots of the nerves; or the ties of its membranes may be dislocated or a separation occur between two parts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.9-10}

It suffers diseases of continuity through the dissolving of continuity in the brain itself or in its arteries and veins or its membranes or the skull {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.10}

Swellings occur in its substance, in its thin or thick membrane, the net or the outer membrane, from a matter of one of the hot or cold humours; he says no disease of the brain will be found that does not go back to these or arise from them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.10}

## Its own diseases and diseases by sharing
Diseases of the brain are its own and by sharing, and the trouble in diseases of sharing sometimes grows until they become like the deadly diseases of its own {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.10}

For often, in pleurisy and the throat swellings called khawaniq, choking, deadly matters are driven to it, and a deadly apoplexy often strikes it because of harm in another organ that shares with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.10}

## Womb (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
He says the superfluous moisture of the brain at the first creation needs to be cleared either in the womb or after it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.9}
