## A small head and a large head
Ibn Sina says that when a small head comes from little matter together with strength of the first forming power, its shape is good and it is less bad than one that joins to the smallness of the head a badness of shape in the creation, which indicates weakness of the power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.18} Yet it is not free of some badness in the form of the brain, weakness in its powers and narrowness of room for the governing and natural powers in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.18}

He mentions that the physiognomists decided that such a person is quarrelsome, cowardly, quick to anger and bewildered in affairs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.18}

Galen said a small head is never without an indication of a bad form of the brain, while a large head is not always an indication of a good state of the brain unless it is joined by a good shape, a thick neck and a wide chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.18}

## Swelling of the head, and a sign of a good brain
An increase of the head in bone that is not natural may occur, as in boys, who suffer a swelling and enlargement of the head that is not of nature but by way of disease, the cause being a quantity of boiling matter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.18-19} It likewise befalls adults in severe pains of the head, and it may happen that the fontanelle shrinks and the temple flattens when erysipelas rises {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19}

One of the signs of a good brain is that it is not affected by the vapours of the drink (sharab), and is affected by its refining and its heat so that the mind sharpens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19}

## Seminal matter (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina says the cause of a small head in creation is little matter, as the cause of a large head is much matter, meaning the seminal matter distributed in the natural distribution to the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.18}
