## Overview
'Arjun names three signs that precede a society's decline: corruption at the summit — a rot from the top that heralds general ruin {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 40}; wallowing in luxury, which kills zeal and pride and makes vice familiar {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 40}; and inclining toward the wrongdoers {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 41}. He argues from al-Isra' and Hud that God takes a people for social corruption faster than for unbelief, the penalty of wrong and corruption being immediate in this world.
