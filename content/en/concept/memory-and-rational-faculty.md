## Summary
The third power physicians mention is the retentive and remembering one, a store for the meanings reaching estimation that are not the sensed forms, whose place is the back ventricle of the brain; what remains of the perceptive powers is the human rational one, which the physician does not examine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.101}.

## Retentive and remembering
The third of what physicians mention, the fifth or fourth on verification, is the retentive and remembering power, a store for the meanings in sensibles that reach estimation, other than their sensed forms, and its place is the back ventricle of the brain. Here is a place for philosophical inquiry into whether the retentive power and the remembering one that retrieves what has left memory from estimation's stores are one power or two, but the physician need not settle it, since the afflictions of either are the afflictions befalling the back ventricle of the brain, whether of temperament or of composition {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.101}.

## The rational faculty
What remains of the perceptive powers of the soul is the human rational one. Since the physicians' regard falls away from the estimative power, it falls away more from this one; their regard is confined to the acts of the three powers and no more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.101}.
