## Overview
After the private share and the public share, Zaydan Qa'dat allocates the remaining portion of production output to strengthening the enterprise's financial and material standing, since it is inconceivable that an enterprise's machinery could continue without renewal, expansion, or maintenance, and productive enterprises are the economic backbone of the nation {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 106}.

## Items
The author lists the main items on which this portion is spent {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 106}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 107}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 108}:
- Renewing and upgrading machinery in line with scientific progress, because the fruits of scientific civilization belong to all humanity.
- Maintaining tools and machinery, including wages for skilled workers, spare parts, and lubrication, for which a dedicated reserve is set aside.
- Providing raw materials, without which work halts and everyone dealing with the enterprise suffers losses.
- Expanding the enterprise, as expansion reduces costs, though it requires ongoing financial support for emergencies.

## The Example of Agriculture
The author gives the example of mechanized agriculture: a farmer who used to cultivate one hectare came to cultivate ten thanks to machinery, yet costs increased, so a reserve must be set aside for them if the enterprise is to continue {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 107}.
