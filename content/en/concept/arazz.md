## Nature and selection
A well-known grain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. The long footnote on the rice plant quotes a modern nutrition book and is not used for this entry.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry, with its dryness more evident than its heat; some say it is hotter than wheat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}{cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. It gives a good nourishment that tends to dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}.

## Uses by organ
Food organs: cooked with milk and almond oil it nourishes more and better; soaked overnight in bran water its drying and binding are lessened; it has a cleansing quality and cools slowly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. Excretory organs: cooked in water it binds to a degree; cooked in milk it increases the semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. The semen line is the book's listing and is logged for the founder's ruling.
