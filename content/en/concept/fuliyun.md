## Nature
Dioscorides claimed that fuliyun grows in rocky places; one kind is called bal'un, that is, the female, and resembles moss, its leaf is greener than the olive leaf and its stem thin and short, and it has a white flower and small seeds larger than poppy seed. Another is called aribu'ayun, that is, the one that begets a male; it resembles the first but differs in its seed, since its fruit is like the olive fruit in the shape of a cluster {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it is said that a pregnant woman who drinks of one of the two kinds will bear a male, and of the other a female; Fawastus the sage said so, although the book adds that it was tried and shown to people after the trial, and that it may well be only a saying. With this the letter fa ends {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}
