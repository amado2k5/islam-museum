## Summary
Ibn Sina sorts everything that comes upon the body, and between which and the body action and being acted upon pass, by how it is changed by the body and how it changes the body. What is changed by the body without changing it appreciably and is assimilated to it is food in the absolute sense; what is not assimilated is the moderate drug {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.133}.

## The other classes
What is changed by the body and changes it, and is finally changed by it, is a medicinal food if assimilated to the body and an absolute drug if not. What in the end alters the body and corrupts it is the poisonous drug. What is never changed by the body and changes it is absolute poison {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.133}.
