## Nature and selection
Well known, with a slight bitterness and pungency. Its seed is the most useful part {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Temperament
Dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Actions and properties
It opens obstructions and has, with its drying, a softening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Cosmetic uses
Drinking it and anointing with it turns the colour to yellow, and it enters remedies for vitiligo and leprosy; kneaded with honey it removes the livid mark of blood wherever it is {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps pus of the chest and turning of the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It helps the dampness of the stomach, calms nausea and turning of the soul, and is good for the cold liver and stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Excretory organs
Given with wine it provokes urine, removes difficulty of urination and expels the stone; in general it cleanses the kidneys and bladder and helps winds and colic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The womb is fumigated with it, with resin, and it cleanses it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Fevers
It helps very old fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Poisons and bites
Its decoction is poured on a scorpion's sting and calms it, and drunk for bites of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.
