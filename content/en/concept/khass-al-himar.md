## Nature
It is like the leaf of fine lettuce, many in number, tending to black and downy, its leaves clinging to the root and holding it, the colour of its root tending to red and staining the hand and the ground red, and it grows in good land, being of a watery and earthy substance, and it is al-shanjar, of which something has been said; the editor of the book says in his note that khass al-himar is al-shanjar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.709}

## Choice
The yellow is stronger, and the white is watery and weak {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the beginning of the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Actions and properties
Cleansing, opening and drying, its flower being stronger in that, the nature of its root near the nature of its seed, and the root stronger, especially the dry; the book quotes Paulus that it has a power of drawing from the depth, so that it draws al-sala' {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Swellings and pustules
It benefits hard swellings wherever they are {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Wounds and ulcers
Made up with cerate it heals, and likewise its water with cerate; it is used with cerate on fissures of the anus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Joints
With its roots it is a poultice on gout, and likewise with vinegar on sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Organs of the head
Its juice cleanses the head as a nasal drop, and with honey it is used on thrush and benefits as a smear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Ocular organs
Its dry form cleans the trace that remains in the eye and the thickness of the layers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Organs of nourishment
It cleanses the liver, and that pickled in vinegar benefits the spleen, eaten and as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it promotes menses strongly, brings out a dead foetus and kills a living one, that it benefits hard swellings in the womb as a pessary and by sitting in its water, and that it is the most promoting of menses and the most fitting; the amount is left out; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.710}
