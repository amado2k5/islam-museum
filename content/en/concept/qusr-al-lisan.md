## Its cause
The tongue's shortness may occur from the ligament beneath it being attached to the tongue's head and tip so it does not let it stretch out, and may occur by way of convulsion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.258}
## Its treatment in the book
The kind from convulsion has been spoken of; the kind from the short ligament is treated in the book by cutting that ligament a little from the tip side and seeing to the place with what stops the blood of this kind (the drug name left out) — the measure of needed cutting being the tongue's release to the palate and out of the mouth; and if cutting with iron is not dared for fear of much blood bursting, it is permitted to pass a piercing needle with thread under the ligament to perforate without cutting, laying on the organ what prevents adhesion — the sharp caustic drugs — and if the cutting is gentle with care of the vessels under the tongue, no flow harms them (a hand-work the book carries as description, not procedure; the drug names are left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.258}
