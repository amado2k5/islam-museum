## Nature and selection
An Indian woody drug, with some likeness in power to bahman {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}. The best is the thick, white, rough kind with many lines. The smooth, thin-stemmed and less white kind is poor, and the book says it is adulterated with a Berber imitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the second degree and dry in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}. Dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: it helps pains of the joints and gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}. Excretory organs: it increases desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}. Poisons: it helps against poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.400}.
