## Summary
Al-Battani faults Ptolemy for treating the Sun's diameter as fixed in the two eclipses he used, and holds that his own observations showed a clear change in the Sun's diameter between its farthest and nearest distances, though small in itself. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.84}.

## The amount of change
He says the Sun's diameter changes between its two distances by about two and a third minutes, and that with this the eclipse measures come right in his view; without it, he says, the result would be many times too large. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.88}.
