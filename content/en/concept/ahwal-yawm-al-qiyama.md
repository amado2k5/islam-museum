## Overview
The terrors are the hardships that meet creation on the Day before they settle into the reckoning; the Qur'an names it the day of fright, the day of regret and the day of meeting {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the texts
The Qur'an pictures the Day's terrors: the sun folded up, the stars scattered, the mountains moved [[passage/quran-81-1|the opening of surat al-Takwir]], the heaven split by cloud and the angels sent down [[passage/quran-25-25|the splitting-heaven verse]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. In the Sunnah people are gathered barefoot, naked and uncircumcised, Ibrahim the first clothed, and the believer sweats as God wills {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
