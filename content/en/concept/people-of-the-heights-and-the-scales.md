## Summary
Sha'rawi presents the reckoning by the scales as it appears in al-A'raf. Whoever's scales are heavy prospers, and whoever's scales are light has lost himself {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1233}.

## The people of the heights
He holds that the people of the heights are those whose good and bad deeds are equal. The Qur'an does not say this outright, but God's forgiveness comes before His wrath, so he reports forgiveness for them {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1233}.

## Accounting by record
He holds that God's reckoning is not by knowledge alone but by a clear record, and that each person reads his own book, citing the Isra verse {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1232} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1233}.
