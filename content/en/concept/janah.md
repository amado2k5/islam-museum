## Nature and selection
A bird's wing. The best is the chicken's wing; goose wings are easy to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.426}.

## Temperament and actions
Actions: the wing is lighter as food because of much movement and exercise, and heavier because of the meat in it and its nearness to the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.426}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it is said that the feathers of the dove's wing, mixed with the same amount of henbane, burnt and crushed, and put into bread like salt, dissolve scrofulous swellings of the neck without the knife; the same when put back on the bread {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
Excretory organs: it is said that the bread made so loosens the bowels and purges greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
