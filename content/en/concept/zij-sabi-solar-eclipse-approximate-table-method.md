## Converting the hours to local time
For an approximate reading from the tables the corrected true hours of conjunction are counted from local midday. If the conjunction is before midday the hours are subtracted from twenty-four; if after, the hours between are taken. The parallax in longitude is then read from the fourth table of parallax by clime and multiplied by the Moon's hourly motion, giving the first hours of parallax. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.170-172}

## Choosing between the two methods
The text says the table reading comes close to the full calculation and is used where a first approximation is needed, while the reckoning by angles and circles is more exact. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.172}
