## Nature
Dioscorides said that some people call it armiyun and also amiyun; it is of two kinds, the one wild and the other garden; of the garden kind some have a red flower and some a flower toward white; its leaf resembles the leaf of coriander but is thinner; the wild kind has a broader and harder leaf and a deep red flower; and the shaqa'iq has no tear and no poppy-head; the editor's note says that it is the shaqa'iq al-nu'man {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree and moist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Actions and properties
Cleansing and dissolving; and Galen said it is cleansing, washing, drawing and ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Cosmetics
It blackens the hair when mixed with walnut husks, and when its leaf and stems are used as they are or cooked it improves the hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Swellings and pustules
It is cooked and smeared on swellings that are not hard, and it purges on account of boils and hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its dry form benefits dirty ulcers and heals them, and peeling of the skin; it cleans ulcers thoroughly, being sufficient for peeling and for ulcerated scab, and it cleans very dirty ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.670}

## Organs of the head
Its juice as a snuff cleans the head and the brain, its root is chewed to draw the moistures from the head, and it removes ringworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Ocular organs
Its juice with honey is beneficial for dimness of the eye, its whiteness and the scars of its ulcers, and when cooked with tila' (boiled-down grape must) and applied as a poultice it heals hard swellings around the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Organs of the chest
When its leaf is cooked with its stems and the herb of thyme and eaten, it brings on milk as is fitting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it brings on the menses when taken up {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.671}
