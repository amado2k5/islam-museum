## Summary
To find a star's latitude the chord of its distance from the equator is multiplied by the chord of the complement of the declination of the degree in which the star was found, the result is divided by the chord of the complement of the full obliquity, and the quotient is turned into an arc. If the arc is greater than the declination of that degree the declination is taken from it, and if less it is taken from the declination; the remainder is the latitude of the star. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.60-61}.

## The direction of the latitude
The direction has its rule: if the arc is more than the declination of the star's degree the latitude is in the direction of that declination, and if less it is opposite to the direction of the declination of the star's degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.61}.
