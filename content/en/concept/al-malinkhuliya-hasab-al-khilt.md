## The kinds
We say: if the black bile that makes melancholia is bloody, it comes with joy and laughter and intense grief does not attend it; if it is from phlegm, it comes with laziness, little movement and stillness; if from bile, it comes with agitation and a touch of madness and is like mania; if it is pure black bile, thought in it is abundant and aggressiveness is less unless he is stirred, and then he becomes fretful and bears a grudge that is not forgotten {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.107}
