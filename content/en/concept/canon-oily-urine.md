## Summary
Ibn Sina describes oily urine, a yellow mixed with green like oil in viscosity, transparency and fatty sheen, holds that it mostly shows evil, and divides it into three kinds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.

## Description and meaning
One compound colour is the oily, a yellow mixed with green, like oil in viscosity and transparency with a fatty sheen. In most cases it shows evil and never good, ripeness or soundness; rarely it shows the emptying of fatty matter as a crisis, and then only if relief follows. The deadly kind is the one whose fattiness stinks, above all when passed little by little, and worse still when something like the washings of fresh meat is mixed in; it occurs mostly in dropsy, consumption and bad colic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.

## The three kinds
In acute diseases oily urine on the fourth day has often shown that the patient will die on the seventh. In general oily urine is of three kinds: all of it fatty, or only its lower part, or its upper part. It may also be oily in its colour only, as in consumption, especially at its start, or in its consistency only, or in both, as in kidney ailments and in the full and late stage of consumption {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.
