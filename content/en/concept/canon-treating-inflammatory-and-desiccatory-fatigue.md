## Summary
For inflammatory fatigue Ibn Sina sets three aims, to relax what is stretched, cool what has heated and evacuate the excess, and he puts the desiccatory kind on the healthy regimen with warmer bath water, then describes restorative exercise and a plunge into cold water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.

## The inflammatory kind
For inflammatory fatigue the aim of the regimen is threefold: to relax what is stretched, to cool what has grown hot and to evacuate the excess. This is done with abundant lukewarm oil, very gentle rubbing, a long stay in water inclined slightly to warmth, and rest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.

## The desiccatory kind and restorative exercise
Nothing is changed in the regimen of the healthy for the desiccatory kind except that the bath water is made hotter, because very hot water condenses the skin without the harm of cold water, which, though it condenses, risks its cold passing into a body that has grown thin. On the second day restorative exercise is used gently, the bath is as on the first day, and the person is told to plunge once into cold water to condense the skin, leaving at once, since staying in it is not safe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.
