## Swellings and pustules
It is put with rue on herpes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is put with rue on fistulas {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Organs of the head
The lethargic is fumigated with it; with oil the head of one with phrenitis and lethargy is rubbed with it; its moist juice is dropped into a suppurating ear; and it is very beneficial for headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits difficult breathing and asthma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its root benefits pains of the liver and benefits jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Excretory organs
It purges phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as benefiting suffocation of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.607}
