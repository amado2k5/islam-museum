## Organs of the head
It is said that when the raada is laid on the head of one with a headache it takes the headache away; Galen said he supposes it does so only while alive, and as for the dead one, he had tried it and it did none of this; it is the numbing fish {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Joints
Paul said that the oil in which this fish is cooked soothes recent pains of the joints when anointed with {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Excretory organs
If it is taken up as a suppository it tightens the anus that has come out and draws in the haemorrhoids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}
