## Summary
Sha'rawi explains the root *tawaffa* from its usage. He holds that the common sense, death, is not its only meaning. The basic sense is taking a thing in full, as a creditor who receives his due in full is said to have taken it in full {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1501}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1502}.

## Sleep and death
Sha'rawi cites two verses that call sleep *tawaffi*, [[passage/quran-6-60|al-An'am 60]] and [[passage/quran-39-42|al-Zumar 42]], and says that sleep is called so because it removes a person from the sense of life {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1501}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1502}.

## Death and killing
Sha'rawi separates death from killing. Killing breaks the body that the soul inhabits; death takes the soul while the body stays whole, and only God can do that. He gives the image of electricity and a lamp to show that the soul dwells only in a body with particular characteristics {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1503}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1504}.

## Taking and raising in Al Imran
In the address to Jesus in [[passage/quran-3-55|Al Imran 55]], Sha'rawi reads *tawaffi* as taking Jesus in full, in soul and body, so that no human can reach him. He says that the Arabic 'and' does not require order between events, so the raising need not come after the taking. He holds that mentioning the taking first is not a claim that Jesus escapes death, since death comes to all {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1504}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1505}.
