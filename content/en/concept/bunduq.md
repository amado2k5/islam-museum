## Nature and selection
A known nut, with more earth than walnut. It is more nourishing than walnut because it is denser, less oily and slower to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

## Temperament and actions
It leans toward heat and dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: it produces bile and has more binding power than walnut; it causes wind and bloating in the lower belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Adornment: its burnt shell stains the hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Head: roasted and eaten with a little pepper it ripens a cold; the book quotes Hippocrates that it increases the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Eyes: some claimed that rubbed on the crown of a blue-eyed child it removes the blueness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Breath: eaten with honey water it helps chronic cough and helps bring up phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Digestion: slow to digest, brings on vomiting, and is slower to digest than walnut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Excretory organs: its shell is astringent and binds the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Poisons: helps the bites of venomous creatures, especially with fig and rue for scorpion sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.
