## Actions and properties
Drying and cleansing, especially the khazaf of the oven, the subtlest of the khazaf being that of the sea crab, and the roof tiles are in the nature of sanbadhaj {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Cosmetics
The khazaf of the sea crab is drying and clears freckles and blotches {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Swellings and pustules
A cerate is made of khazaf for scrofula and benefits it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Wounds and ulcers
The ointment made of khazaf is strong in healing, benefits ulcers and clears mange, especially the khazaf of the sea crab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Ocular organs
The book says that the Chinese khazaf of al-'ada'ir, pounded, removes chronic pterygium, and that the khazaf of the sea crab with excavated salt benefits al-murra and removes the whiteness that comes from the healing of an ulcer; the ways of mixing are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Joints
The khazaf of the oven is applied on gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}
