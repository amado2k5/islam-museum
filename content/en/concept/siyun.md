## Nature
It is the qurrat al-ayn, found in standing waters, with an aromatic quality, and it was spoken of in the chapter of qaf {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Excretory organs
Cooked or uncooked it helps stone and promotes urine, and helps dysentery {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.
