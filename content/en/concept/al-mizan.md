## Summary
Sha'rawi reads the Rahman verse about the balance as a sign that higher things run by an exact measure, and that humans are bound to justice where they have choice {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1064} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1065}.

## What humans do not control
He says that the sun, moon, air and rain work in order because humans have no hand in them, and that corruption comes where humans act without God's method {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1064}.

## What humans control
He urges that people set a measure in every matter they choose, so they do not transgress, and he draws on the Ya Sin verse about the celestial order {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1065}.
