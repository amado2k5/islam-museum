## Summary
Naqia, in the lexicon, is food prepared for a traveller who returns. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16029}

## Rulings
The Hanafis and Hanbalis hold it lawful; the Malikis and Shafi'is hold it recommended. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16032}
Accepting the invitation is a sunna or recommended for most; some early scholars held it obligatory; the Malikis dislike attending all feasts except the wedding feast. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16033}
