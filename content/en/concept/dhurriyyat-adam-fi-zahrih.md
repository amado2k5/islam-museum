## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the plural address in the verse on creation shows that Adam's descendants were latent in his loins at creation, and that all of them were in him {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.239}.

## Continuity of life
He describes life as a continuous chain, in which each person takes from the one before and gives to the one after, and a break in the chain would stop life {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.240}. He says each person has a part of Adam, and that the lives of those now living are joined to Adam {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.240}.

## The prostration to the descendants
He holds that the angels prostrated to Adam and to his descendants until the Hour, because the descendants were latent in his loins, and that the verse adds a new part to the account of creation {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.240}.

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
Episodes 17, 18 and 19 of [[work/fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah|Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi]] explain the hadith of the covenant taken from Adam's back at Na'man, which the presenter says Ahmad records from Ibn Abbas. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:00:00} The presenter links it with the verse of the covenant in [[passage/quran-7-172|al-A'raf 172]] and mentions al-Fakhr al-Razi's question of how a covenant taken from rational beings could be forgotten, answering that forgetting is no proof that a thing did not happen, since a person forgets his whole infancy. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:02:00} He reads the mention of two deaths and two lives in [[passage/quran-40-11|Ghafir 11]] as making the first life the day of the covenant. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:04:30} He reports that scholars differ on where the covenant took place, India, between Ta'if and Mecca, or Batn Na'man beside 'Arafat, and the programme chooses the last because the hadith names it. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep18 00:06:00}

[[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Al-Sha'rawi]] says that the day of the covenant, when we were like tiny particles, is not the day of creation, when God shaped clay into a human form. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:17:30} He holds that no one remembers the day of the covenant because one remembers a thing when one needs it: when a person is alone in the darknesses of land and sea, with no father or mother near, he says "O Lord", and that is the covenant surfacing. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:19:30} For him the one in utter need, with none left but God, says "O He", and that for him is the meaning of God answering one whose need is extreme. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:21:00} He gives the example of a man who loses his money on the road and then remembers ten pounds at home, and his grief lightens. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep17 00:21:30}
