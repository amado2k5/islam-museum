## In the Qur'an and Hadith
The Qur'an mentions Hell's keeper without naming him, in [[passage/quran-32-11|al-Sajdah 11]] and [[passage/quran-39-71|al-Zumar 71]]; the hadith narrated in the Sunan of al-Tirmidhi and Musnad Ahmad names Malik as the keeper of Hell {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:musnad-ahmad}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir joins the hadith report with the Qur'an's silence, noting that the keeper is named Malik in hadith while the Qur'an leaves him unnamed {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some reports describe him with attendants and eyes over Hell, and one has him asking God for more punishment {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; later scholars count him among the angels charged with the Fire.
