## Overview
Ibn al-Nadim says that he read, in the handwriting of Ahmad ibn al-Harith al-Kharraz, the scholars' statement on the specialisations of the historians (*akhbariyyun*). {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.293}

## The division
Abu Mikhnaf surpasses others in the matter of Iraq and its reports and conquests. Al-Mada'ini in the matter of Khurasan, India, and Persia. Al-Waqidi in the Hijaz and the biography. They shared in the conquests of Syria. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.293}
