## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an mentions that an angel appeared to Maryam as a well-formed man and gave her tidings of a righteous son [[passage/quran-3-42|Al 'Imran 42]], and in Maryam that the angels gave her glad tidings of the birth of the Messiah [[passage/quran-19-16|19:16]].

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir interprets that the angel who appeared to Maryam was Jibril, noting that 'appeared to her as a well-formed man' is Jibril in the form God willed {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; Muslim narrates that Jibril came to Maryam in the form of a well-formed man {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Notes
Some held the angel was Jibril, others that it was another angel; the primary interpretation identifies him as Jibril {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
