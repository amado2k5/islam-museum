## Overview
Maryam's angel is the angel who appeared to her as a well-proportioned man and gave her glad tidings of a pure boy, as in [[passage/quran-3-42|Al 'Imran 42]], [[passage/quran-19-16|Maryam 16]], and [[passage/quran-19-17|Maryam 17]]. And he is, on the majority view, Jibril {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## The story in the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-19-16|Maryam 16]]: "And mention in the Book, Maryam, when she withdrew from her people to an eastern place * Then she withdrew from them, so he carried her, and she withdrew to a nearby place" — and in it the story of Maryam {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-19-17|Maryam 17]]: "So We sent to her Our spirit, and he assumed for her the likeness of a well-proportioned man" — and in it the angel's assuming human form for her {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-19-18|Maryam 18]], [[passage/quran-19-19|Maryam 19]], [[passage/quran-19-20|Maryam 20]], [[passage/quran-19-21|Maryam 21]], and [[passage/quran-19-22|Maryam 22]]: in the context of Maryam's story and the glad tidings {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-3-42|Al 'Imran 42]]: "When the angels said: O Maryam, indeed God gives you glad tidings of a word from Him whose name is the Messiah, 'Isa son of Maryam" — and in it the glad tidings of 'Isa {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-3-43|Al 'Imran 43]], [[passage/quran-3-44|Al 'Imran 44]], [[passage/quran-3-45|Al 'Imran 45]], [[passage/quran-3-46|Al 'Imran 46]], and [[passage/quran-3-47|Al 'Imran 47]]: in the context of Maryam's story in Al 'Imran {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In hadith
- In [[passage/quran-19-17|Maryam 17]] "Our spirit" assumed for her the likeness of a well-proportioned man — and in the transmitted tafsirs the spirit is Jibril {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- And in a narration that when Maryam saw the man she said "Indeed I seek refuge in the Most Merciful from you, if you are pious" — as in the context of the verses {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## What he announced to her
- "A word from Him whose name is the Messiah, 'Isa son of Maryam" in [[passage/quran-3-42|Al 'Imran 42]] — that is, 'Isa {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- "That He grants you a pure boy" in [[passage/quran-19-19|Maryam 19]] — that is, 'Isa {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- And in tafsir the angel who assumed form for her is Jibril {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that the angel who appeared to Maryam is Jibril, and mentions that "he assumed for her the likeness of a well-proportioned man" is Jibril in the form God willed {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. And al-Qurtubi held that the angels descended upon Maryam with the glad tidings {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## Is it Jibril or another angel?
- **The most famous view**: he is Jibril, because God sent Jibril to the prophets with revelation {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- **Another view**: he is another angel besides him, and in it is a problem {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02}.
- And the principle in tafsir is that they are Jibril {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes for researchers
- "The angel" in [[passage/quran-3-42|Al 'Imran 42]] is singular, and "the angels" in [[passage/quran-19-16|Maryam 16]] is plural — and in tafsir they are one angel {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- Their companion in the glad tidings is Ibrahim on [[concept/malaikat-ibrahim|the angels of Ibrahim page]].
- What is narrated about the details of their assuming form is from the books of seerah, and the principle is that their glad tidings is established in the Qur'an {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
