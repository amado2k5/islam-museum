## Overview
The tariqa in later usage is the organized Sufi path attached to a shaykh and his way — among the most visible terms of post-sixth-century-AH Sufism {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.

## In the sources
Trimingham describes the ramification of the main orders — Qadiriyya, Naqshbandiyya, Mawlawiyya and others — across the Islamic world {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998 pp. 31+}; bibliographical works attribute the orders to their eponyms {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}.
