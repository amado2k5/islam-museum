## Overview
The tariqa in later usage is the organized Sufi path attached to a shaykh and his way — among the most visible terms of post-sixth-century-AH Sufism {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998}.

## In the sources
Trimingham describes the ramification of the main orders — Qadiriyya, Naqshbandiyya, Mawlawiyya and others — across the Islamic world {cite:trimingham-sufi-orders-1998 pp. 31+}; bibliographical works attribute the orders to their eponyms {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Sharh Ketab Al-Fawaed lectures (episode 9)

The Sheikh relates a saying in spiritual wayfaring that between the servant and God and Paradise there is a bridge crossed in steps: a step away from the self in dealings with people, and a step away from the self with God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep09 00:33:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep09 00:34:00} ⁦(needs_source: the attribution is unclear in the recording)⁩
