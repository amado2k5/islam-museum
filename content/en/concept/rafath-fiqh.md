## Summary
Rafath in the lexicon is intercourse and what passes between a man and a woman, such as kissing, and it is also used for obscene speech. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8799} In worship it is forbidden: one who has intercourse deliberately in the day of Ramadan breaks his fast and owes a makeup and an expiation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8800} The jurists differ on forgetful intercourse: the Hanafis and Shafi'is do not void the fast, the Malikis and Hanbalis do. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8800} Intercourse breaks i'tikaf, and the Shafi'is differ on the forgetful. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8800} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8801} Rafath in the state of ihram is forbidden by agreement, and intercourse in the private part voids the pilgrimage by the majority when deliberate, requiring a makeup and an expiation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8801}
