## The rule
Its treatment is the treatment of melancholia itself, when it is from bile or burnt black bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.111}

## Completing the treatment
The book says he is to be moistened and his heart strengthened after the evacuation, and that the completion of his treatment is sleep; at the end of the chapter it then describes harsh procedures for when medicine and treatment do not avail {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.111} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.112}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. The harsh procedures named at the end of the chapter are also left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.111} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.112}
