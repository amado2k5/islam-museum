## What it is
Teeth-grinding in sleep comes of a weakness of the jaw muscles, like a convulsion of them; it befalls boys often and goes when they grow. If the grinding and squeaking (the editor glosses sarif as the teeth's sound) in sleep runs much, it warns of stroke, epilepsy or convulsion, or points to worms in the belly — the worm kind comes in spells. The sufferer is treated by cleansing the head and anointing the neck with the hot fragrant oils having astringent power, of this kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.278}
