## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Actions and properties
Pungent, cutting phlegm and cleansing; it dissolves winds and flatulence, and its purifying is thorough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Cosmetics
It cuts away inverted warts, moles, bahaq and baras especially {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Swellings and pustules
It is put with vinegar on the milky pustules, and dissolves phlegmatic and hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Ulcers
With vinegar on phlegmatic ulcers and ulcerated scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Organs of the head
It benefits catarrh, especially roasted and put in a linen bundle, and is smeared on the forehead of one with a cold headache; as a snuff it benefits long-standing pains of the head and facial palsy; it is among the drugs that inflate very strongly for blockages of the strainer; and its decoction in vinegar benefits toothache as a gargle, especially with pine wood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Ocular organs
Its powder is snuffed up with oil of irsa at the beginning of the water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Respiratory organs
It also benefits orthopnoea when drunk with natron {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Excretory organs
It kills worms and the tapeworm even as a smear on the navel, and is given to drink with honey and hot water for the stone in the bladder and the kidney {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.678}

## Fevers
It resolves phlegmatic and black-bilious fevers especially and takes them away {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.678}

## Poisons and bites
Vermin flee from its smoke, some people claimed that taking much of it kills, and it is among what benefits the sting of the tarantula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.678}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it brings on the menses when used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.678}
