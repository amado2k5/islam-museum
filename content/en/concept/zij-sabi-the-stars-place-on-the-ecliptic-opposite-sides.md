## Summary
If the star's distance from the equator and the declination of the part culminating with it are in two different directions, the way is to add them, the sum becoming the corrected distance; the chords are then multiplied and divided in another order giving the chord of the corrected declination and what follows from it, until the difference of the star comes out, and the first procedure is then applied until the part of the ecliptic in which the star stands is known. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.60}.

## A short account
This summary gives only the structure of the working, because the chain of chords in it is long and part of it is disturbed in the scan; whoever wants its exact formula should go to the text of the zij. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.60}.
