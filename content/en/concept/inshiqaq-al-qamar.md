## Overview
The splitting of the moon among the scholars is a sign of prophethood; Ibn Kathir says it is agreed upon among the scholars, and reports it split over Mecca twice {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
Ibn Kathir details under the verse the reports of the splitting from Ibn Abbas and others {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
