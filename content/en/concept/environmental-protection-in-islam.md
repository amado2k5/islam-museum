## Overview
Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad holds that the pollution of water, air and soil threatens the safety of persons and societies, and that international conference resolutions went unimplemented {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 119}. Islam, he presents, took care that people make good use of their natural environment and coexist with it in a way that preserves its constituents {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 120}, and the verses treating the cosmos invite reflection on man's relation to it {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar p. 121}. This is the author's reading, attributed to him.
