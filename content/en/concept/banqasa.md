## Nature and selection
Its power is like lentil's and harder to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}. The editor's footnote says it may be the baiqiyya of Ibn al-Baytar.

## Temperament and actions
Moderate, leaning to dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}. Astringent like lentil and produces black bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: good for the joints; applied to swellings and hernias in boys {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.

Excretory organs: binds the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.402}.
