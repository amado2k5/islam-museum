## Crater (al-Ka's)

The catalogue names the figure al-Ka's, the cup (poculum), and its heading gives the Greek krater. Its stars lie on the lip and the handles. The editor notes that a leaf of the Escorial manuscript, which carried the first stars of the Cup and the stars of the Ship and the Hydra, is missing. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.172}
