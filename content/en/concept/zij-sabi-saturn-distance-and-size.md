## Saturn
The text says Saturn's size varies between its nearest and farthest positions, that its mean distance is about fifteen thousand and five hundred, the last figure being garbled, and that its mean diameter is one eighteenth of the Sun's. Its diameter is about four and a sixth times the Earth's, and its bulk about seventy-nine times the Earth's. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.189}

## Reading the figures
Saturn's mean distance and size are read from a disturbed scan and need checking in the original. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.189}
