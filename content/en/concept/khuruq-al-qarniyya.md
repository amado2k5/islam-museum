## Kinds of the protrusion
Corneal ruptures come from an ulcer that has eaten through or from an outside cause like a blow or piercing impact, whereupon the uvea shows; if what shows of it is slight it is called the ant-like, mursharij or fly-like by its smallness or size; if more, until a grape-grain of uvea appears, the grape-like; greater still, the bladder-like; if the uvea comes out so far it bars the lids from meeting, the nail-like; and if the uvea turns white there is no cure {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.178} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.179}

## Distinguishing it from the bladders, and the lengthwise rupture
When the cornea ruptures lengthwise no white is seen but a cleft, as though the observer had lengthened; the rupture may be in all the cornea's coats — the protrusion then being of the uvea's substance — or in some coats only, the protruding then being of the cornea itself on the eating of part of its coats, resembling a bladder; it differs from the watery bladders and vesicles in that those carry pain and pulsation and yield under the probe, while a protrusion from the cornea itself is solid and stiff and does not yield; the kinds of protrusion caused by rupture of all the cornea's coats with all or part of the uvea coming out are four: the small fly-like and ant-like, which when small may resemble the bladder and vesicle but differ by keeping the uvea's colour — black, blue and grey — a bladder whose colour differs from the uvea being a bladder, though a white trim like embroidery may be seen round its base, that being the rupture's edge whitened on healing; the second the grape-like; the third the bladder-like and nail-like which prevents closing; the fourth greater still {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.179}

## Its treatment and the untreatable
The small protrusion is treated with the astringent fluids and fomenting and collyried with the astringent collyria ⁦(drug, collyrium, recipe and measure names are not carried)⁩; when it becomes a rupture it is treated as a rupture, and once the ulcer sets to protruding the eye keeps its dressing and recumbent rest; the nail-like has no treatment — some cut the mursharij protrusions for looks, but the sounder course is not to cut or disturb it, for the matter may pour and pass to the other eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.179} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.180}
