## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that compulsion governs bodies, while true faith is chosen. So God left belief and obedience to choice {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1076}.

## Compulsion and love
He says humans are compelled in many bodily matters and events, but love cannot be compelled. He gives the image of a gun forcing a man to bow, which cannot force love {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1076}.

## Iblis
He cites Iblis's oath by God's might. He holds that had God willed all to obey, none could disobey. He wanted to know who comes willingly, so that the servant lives between fear and hope {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1077}.
