## Its treatment by lifting and pressing
If heat and redness are there it is bled, then the gargles named in the past chapters are used, and it is lifted with what he described in the bit-like tool — as gently as possible; if there is no heat or redness the gargle of what he described is used and it is lifted with the stated tool. The strongest treatment is that it be pressed upward with the tool, stretched outward, with the astringent drugs or those mixed with the resolvents as is right; and it may be pressed with the finger smeared with what he described. Sucking is gentlest once there is no lesion of tumour or fullness; and boys' uvulas may be lifted with what he described, above all when it is painted on their crowns (the tool and the pressing carried as the book's descriptions, not procedures; the names of this kind left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.299}
