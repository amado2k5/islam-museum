## Nature and selection
Nature: well known. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.482}
Selection: the best wheat is medium in hardness and softness, large, plump, fresh and smooth, between red and white; black wheat is poor in nourishment. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.482}

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot, moderate in moisture and dryness, and its gruel tends to dryness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.482}
Actions: large and red wheat is more nourishing. Boiled wheat is slow to digest and windy, but its nourishment is much once it is digested. Flour is close to starch but hotter; wheat gruel passes slowly and causes much wind, and needs sweetness to move it, and is washed in hot water to remove the wind. Starch is cold, moist and sticky. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: wheat cleans the face; its flour and starch, especially with saffron, are a remedy for kalaf. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
Organs of digestion: wheat and barley gruel are heavy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
Excretory organs: raw wheat, and also wheat boiled whole without grinding or husking, like harisa, and harisa likewise, breed worms if eaten. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
Poisons: wheat ground fine and scattered on the bite of the mad dog helps; the chewed wheat taken fasting is better in his view. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
