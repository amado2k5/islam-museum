## Summary
"Knowing the mudtarib hadith" is the nineteenth type in [[person/ibn-al-salah|Ibn al-Salah]]: a mudtarib hadith is one whose narration varies, some relating it one way and others another way that contradicts it. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 93} {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 94}

## When the name applies
Ibn al-Salah says it is called mudtarib only when the two versions are equal; if one prevails so that the other cannot match it — because its narrator has better memory, kept longer company with the one he narrates from, or on another recognised ground — the ruling goes to the stronger, and the hadith is then neither called mudtarib nor treated as such. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 94}

## Where it occurs and its ruling
Inconsistency can occur in the text or in the chain, and it can come from a single narrator or from several. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 94} It makes the hadith weak because it signals that it was not well preserved. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 94} It is close to [[concept/illa|the defective (mu'allal)]]; in the preceding type Ibn al-Salah notes that some variants called defects are not damaging, such as a mursal version of a hadith that a trustworthy, precise narrator gives with a full chain. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 93}

## His example
Ibn al-Salah cites Isma'il ibn Umayya's hadith from Abu Amr ibn Muhammad ibn Hurayth from his grandfather Hurayth from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] that one who prays and finds no stick to set up before him should draw a line. Bishr ibn al-Mufaddal and Rawh ibn al-Qasim relate it from Isma'il in this form; [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]] from him from Abu Amr ibn Hurayth from his father; Humayd ibn al-Aswad from Isma'il from Abu Amr ibn Muhammad ibn Hurayth ibn Sulaym from his father; Wuhayb and Abd al-Warith from Isma'il from Abu Amr ibn Hurayth from his grandfather Hurayth; and Abd al-Razzaq from Ibn Jurayj says Isma'il heard it from Hurayth ibn Ammar — and, says Ibn al-Salah, there is more inconsistency in it than this. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 94} {cite:muqaddima-ibn-salah-itr p. 95}
