## Summary
Al-Battani takes the declination of the part that culminates with the star and the star's distance from the equator. If they are in one direction, the smaller is taken from the larger and the remainder is the corrected distance; a long chain of multiplications and divisions of chords then follows, bringing in the complements of the corrected distance and of the full obliquity, the longest chord and the added chord, and ends in an arc that is the difference of the star. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.59-60}.

## Applying the difference
If the culminating part lies from the start of Cancer to the end of Sagittarius and the distance is northern, the difference of the star is taken from the rising times of the culminating part in the right sphere, and if southern it is added; if the part lies from the start of Capricorn to the end of Gemini the working is reversed. The resulting times are brought into the rising times of the right sphere, and the degree of the signs against them is the degree and minute in which the star stands. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.60}.
