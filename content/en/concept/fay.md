## Overview
In al-Baladhuri, *fay'* is what God granted the Muslims of the property of the People of the Book without fighting. The reference verse for the property of the Banu al-Nadir is [[passage/quran-59-7|Surat al-Hashr 7]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.40-42}

## Fadak
After the conquest of Khaybar by force, the people of Fadak made peace with the Prophet on half of their land. Half of Fadak was therefore his alone, because the Muslims did not advance against it with horse or camel. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.40-42}

## The Prophet's *safaya*
It is reported from Umar that he said the Prophet had three *safaya* (choice portions). The land of the Banu al-Nadir was held in reserve for his contingencies; Khaybar was divided into three parts; and Fadak was for the wayfarers. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.40-42}

## The difference from booty
What was taken by advancing with horse and camel was divided among the fighters. What was granted as *fay'* without fighting belonged to the Prophet, who placed it where God had placed it. The distinction goes back to [[concept/fath-anwa|conquest by force]] and [[concept/fath-sulh|conquest by treaty]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.40-42}

The data note that the same report appears in al-Bukhari, in the words of Umar in the story of al-Abbas and Ali. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] says Ibn Abbas called al-Hashr "the sura of Banu al-Nadir", and that the Prophet gave most of the fay' to the Emigrants as compensation, with the Ansar's consent, and to only two of the Ansar who were in clear need ([[passage/quran-59-6|al-Hashr 6]], [[passage/quran-59-7|7]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:04:00} He says that for him the most important question in economics is how wealth is distributed, that the verse forbids wealth circulating only among the rich, and that this is the mark of unrestrained capitalism. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:14:00} He relates that Umar disagreed with some Companions over dividing the lands of the Sawad of Iraq and of Egypt, then found the answer in al-Hashr and made them an endowment for all generations of Muslims under a land tax (kharaj), and he applies the same reasoning to the Gulf's oil wealth. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:33:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep20 00:34:00}

## Fay' and land tax in Abu Yusuf's Kitab al-Kharaj

[[person/abu-yusuf-al-qadi|Abu Yusuf]] states in [[work/kitab-al-kharaj-abu-yusuf|Kitab al-Kharaj]] that fay', for him, is the land tax, citing the verses of Surat al-Hashr on fay' ([[passage/quran-59-7|al-Hashr 7]] and after) as giving a share in it to those who come after the Emigrants and Helpers until the Day of Resurrection. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.27}

He relates with his chains how Bilal and his companions asked [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] to divide what had been conquered in Iraq and Syria among the conquerors as an army's spoils are divided; Umar refused, recited these verses, and said God had given a share to those who would come later, and if he divided it nothing would remain for them. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.27} In a report from several scholars of Medina, Umar consulted the Companions: Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf favoured division, while Uthman, Ali, Talha and Ibn Umar shared Umar's view; he then called ten leading Ansar, five of Aws and five of Khazraj, and put to them his plan to keep the lands with their cultivators, placing land tax on the land and jizya on the persons, so that it would be fay' for the fighters, their families and those to come, to man the frontiers and pay the armies in the great cities; they answered: your view is right. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.28} {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.29} He then put Uthman ibn Hunayf in charge of surveying the Sawad, and a year before Umar's death the revenue of the Sawad of Kufa reached a hundred million dirhams. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.29} {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.30} In another report al-Zubayr and Bilal pressed hardest for dividing Syria, and Umar prayed "O God, spare me Bilal and his companions"; Abu Yusuf notes that the Muslims saw the plague of Amwas as the answer to his prayer. {cite:kitab-al-kharaj-salafiyya-1392 p.30} See [[event/umar-land-policy-al-jabiya|Umar's land policy]].
