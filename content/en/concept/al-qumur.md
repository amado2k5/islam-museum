## How it arises
Qumur may arise from dominant light and dominant whiteness, as when gazing long at snow prevails: things are not seen, or seen near but not far, from weakness of the spirit, and colours are imagined covered with whiteness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.216}
## Its treatment
He orders keeping the gaze on green and azure colours and hanging black colours before the sight; the eye may be fomented and collyriated, opened on vapour, or fomented with the known means ⁦(drug, vapour, fomentation and collyrium names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.216}
