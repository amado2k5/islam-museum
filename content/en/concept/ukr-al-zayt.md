## Nature
The lees of oil, when cooked in a vessel of Cyprian copper until it thickens and becomes like honey, is fit for what hudud is fit for and excels hudud {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.623}

## Organs of the head
Cooked with verjuice until it thickens and smeared on decaying teeth, it removes them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.623}

## Ocular organs
It sometimes enters the mixtures of drugs for the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Excretory organs
When aged it is better, and an enema useful for the stomach is prepared from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Joints
The fresh that has not been cooked, if pounded and poured on those with gout and those with pain in the joints, benefits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists the enema made from the aged lees as useful for ulcers of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}
