## Its cause
The tongue's enlargement may come from prevailing blood or from a great phlegmy relaxing irritating moisture; it may grow so great it leaves the mouth and the mouth cannot hold it; this greatness is given its own mention apart from the tumour chapter for the distinction proper to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.257}
## Its treatment
The sanguine one and the one from hot matter is treated by keeping up its rubbing with the cutting sour astringents of this kind; the one from moistures by keeping up rubbing of this kind after evacuations, until it returns to its size and the protruding part goes back in (the drug names are left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.257} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.258}
## The boys' relaxation
When the tongue's relaxation occurs in boys, the important task is the diet and feeding on birds and light meats {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.258}
