## Nature
It is well known and does the actions of butter, being stronger in ripening, relaxing and softening; what was said in the chapter of zay, where the butter was mentioned, is to be read and added to this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and moist in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Actions and properties
Ripening and dissolving, acting only in soft and middling bodies and not in hard ones {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Swellings and pustules
It ripens swellings, especially those at the base of the ear, particularly in children and women, and it cannot do the same in hard bodies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Organs of the head
It ripens the swellings behind the soft ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Organs of the chest
It softens the chest and ripens the residues in it, especially with honey, sugar and bitter almond {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Excretory organs
With bitter almond it may restrain the bowels through an astringency in it, and may loosen them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Poisons and bites
It is an antidote to poisons that are drunk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.
