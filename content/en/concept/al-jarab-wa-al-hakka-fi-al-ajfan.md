## The cause and its stages

The cause of itch and scab of the lids is a salty, acrid matter from sharp blood or another sharp humour that produces itching and then scabs over; it mostly follows ulcers of the eye. It begins as slight itching, then becomes roughness so the lid reddens, then ulcerating chaps, then the hard granular stage when the cracking in the itching and swelling grows severe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.197} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.198}

## The treatment

If the scab is accompanied by ophthalmia, the ophthalmia is treated first, then the scab is turned to without neglect; likewise if another illness is present the severer of the two is attended to. The advanced kinds require scraping, while the kind that admits medicine begins with repeated evacuation and venesection, frequent bathing, and avoidance of dust, smoke, shouting, tight fastenings, a narrow collar, anger, vexation, much talking, a soft pillow, long prostration, and all that drives matters up to the face ⁦(the scraping means, collyria, recipes, and the named bloods and gall are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.198}
