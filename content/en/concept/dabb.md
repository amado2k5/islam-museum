## Nature
The dabb is other than the waral found in our lands, although it resembles it and is close to it in conditions and powers, and the dabb is scarce except in the desert of the Arabs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Cosmetics
Its droppings are applied to freckles and spots and benefit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Ocular organs
Its dung is beneficial for whiteness of the eye and descent of water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}
