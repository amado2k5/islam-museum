## Nature and selection
Nature: the good kind of bua, finer than qaqulla. Temperament: hot in the first degree and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}

## Actions
Fine and light. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}

## Uses by organ
Digestive organs: strengthens the cold liver and stomach and aids digestion greatly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.448}
