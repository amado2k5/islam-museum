## Its definition
It is that the lids get difficulty moving toward closing from opening and toward opening from closing, with pain and redness, mostly without moisture; it often does not answer opening on waking from sleep. Most of it has separations of dry hard eye-sordes, and no flowing accompanies it except incidentally, for it is from dryness or a viscid humour inclining strongly to dryness — though pain and redness may be there. If there is itching with no matter flowing to it, it is called dryness of the eye; often there is a hot temperament and much thick matter needing evacuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.194}

## The treatment
Constant fomentation of the eye with a sponge soaked in tepid water is kept up, frequent bathing with moderate fresh water, a moistening application placed on the eye at sleep, and constant irrigation of the head with the moisteners, oils, douches and moistening snuffs of the named oils; if the states show a choleric matter with the dryness it is eased with what has that property; if a thick drying matter needing dissolution is suspected it is dissolved with the viscid softeners; and sometimes what draws tears is used — in its chronic eye-heat benefit comes from using the tearful kohls, for they dissolve the thick matter, set it flowing and draw of the fine moistures what softens it and dissolves it as they dissolve ⁦(the drug, oil, softener and kohl names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.194}
