## Food organs
The book says this earth binds and spoils the balance of the body, yet it strengthens the mouth of the stomach and removes the heaviness of food. The author does not like it to be used. It has a remarkable power to stop vomiting. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Book listing on desire (labelled, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that the claim that this earth cheers the spirit applies to those who crave it and long for it, and that the effect comes from an intense desire. It is recorded here as the book's report, not as advice. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}
