## Summary
Ratib means fixed and settled; its technical use keeps that meaning. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8568}

## Regular sunnas
These are the sunnas that follow the obligatory prayers. The jurists differ on their number: the majority say ten rak'as, and the Hanafis twelve, with the two before dawn prayer the most emphasised among them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8568} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8569}

## The regular muezzin
When a mosque has a regular muezzin, no one else calls the adhan before him unless he is absent and the time is at risk; if others dispute the call, the regular muezzin takes precedence. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8569} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8570}

## The regular imam
The regular imam leads over the others present even if someone else is more learned; the ruler, his deputy or the judge takes precedence when present. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8570} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8571}

## Repeating the congregation
The Hanafis, Malikis and Shafi'is dislike repeating the congregation in a mosque with a regular imam when it falls on the path of people and the imam has not permitted it; the Hanbalis allow it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8571}
There is no dislike in a second congregation when the mosque is in a market or on a road, when there is no regular imam, or when the imam permits it; this is agreed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8571}
