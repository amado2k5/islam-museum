## Definition
Anf is the nostril, plural anaf or unuf. Its rulings vary with its place. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3513}

## In purification and washing
- Washing the inside of the nose by sniffing water in is sunna; the outside is obligatory as part of the face. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3513}
- In full washing the outside is obligatory for all, and the inside is obligatory for the Hanafis and sunna for others. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3513}

## Prostration
Placing the nose with the forehead on the ground is sunna for the majority of jurists; the Hanafis hold it obligatory. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3513}

## Entry into the body through the nose while fasting
- If a fasting person applies medicine by the nose and it reaches the stomach, throat or brain, his fast is broken and he owes repayment; the Malikis hold it is broken only if it reaches the interior or the throat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3513}
- Excessive sniffing where water reaches the stomach or throat: the Hanbalis and Shafi'is have two views, and the Malikis hold the fast broken. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3513}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3514}

## Injury to the nose
- A deliberate injury calls for retaliation where an equal can be exacted without wrong, as the Qur'an states in sura al-Ma'ida (5:45); otherwise, or if the injury is accidental, blood money is due. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3514}
- Loss of the sense of smell alone carries blood money; loss of smell together with the cartilage carries two blood payments; cutting part of the nose carries a proportionate share. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3514}

## Where the nose is treated
Sniffing in purification, washing and the washing of the dead; pouring a nursing mother's milk into the nose in suckling; and making a nose of gold or silver in dress. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3514}
