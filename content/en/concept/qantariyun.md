## Nature
Dioscorides is reported saying that some say it is the Roman dari, called in Arabic the little luqa, and others named it lambisun; it resembles hyufariqun, the mountain pennyroyal, with a stem longer than a span, a flower red leaning to purple, small leaves, rather long, like rue leaves, a fruit like wheat and a small root of no use, and its taste is very bitter. It is of two kinds, small and large, both appearing at the end of spring, found in Persia and the land of the Romans; it is a leafy herb. The book spends a long passage on how its juice is extracted, which is not taken up here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Choice
The best is the fine, small one, leaning to yellow, that stings the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Actions and properties
It has clearing, astringency, acridity, a little sweetness and drying without stinging; it is said that if cooked with cut meat it joins it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Wounds and ulcers
It cleanses fresh wounds and seals old ulcers; the dried enters ointments and heals fistulas, deep ulcers and bad wounds, and a fistula may be filled with it and bound, and it mends it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Joints
It benefits tearing in the muscles and pus in them; the fine especially, an enema made from it benefits sciatica and pains of the nerves and bruising of them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Ocular organs
The juice of the fine with honey is beneficial for the whiteness that comes from the healing of an ulcer in the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits the spitting of blood by its astringency; the thick and the fine alike benefit difficulty of breathing, and it is given for cold pleurisy and spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits obstructions of the liver and hardness of the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Excretory organs
It kills worms and induces urine and benefits colic and the colon disease; the decoction of the small may loosen phlegm and raw yellow bile, and if too much is taken it loosens blood, especially the fine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it induces the menses and brings out the foetus, and that it is given for pains of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Fevers
It is beneficial for fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}
