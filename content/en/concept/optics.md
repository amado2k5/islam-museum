## Overview
The science of light and vision. The editor of the Kitab al-Manazir holds that optics was among the Greeks a branch of mathematics; Ibn al-Haytham made it a compound inquiry of the natural and the mathematical sciences, studying light's three conditions: rectilinear radiation, reflection and refraction {cite:manazir-sabra-1983 p.9}.
