## Summary
If the corrected anomaly is less than a certain figure, the Moon is faster than its mean motion; if it is more, the Moon is slower. Likewise when the anomaly falls in one quadrant the Moon's motion is less than its mean, and in another quadrant more; the Sun follows the same pattern. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.112}.

The figures marking the quadrant limits in this passage are wholly damaged in the scan, so only the structure is given here; the reckoning is to be re-run against the tables once the text is clear. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.112}.
