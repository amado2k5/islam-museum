## Opposition, square, trine and sextile
The zodiac is divided into twelve signs, and the measures used for aspects are four forms besides conjunction. Opposition spans six signs and two right angles, a hundred and eighty degrees, and it is named the strongest cause. The square is half of the opposition, spanning three signs and a right angle, ninety degrees. The trine spans four signs and a right angle and a third of an angle, a hundred and twenty degrees. The sextile is half of the trine, spanning two signs and two-thirds of a right angle, sixty degrees. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.198}

## Conjunction and its meaning
Conjunction is not a shape in the sense of the four others and is treated separately. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.198}
