## Overview
Imam Ahmad said in his epistle that the Qur'an is the speech of God, that what God has spoken is not created, and that what is in the codex and in people's recitation is the speech of God, uncreated. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.53} He added that whoever says the Qur'an is created is a disbeliever, and whoever does not declare him a disbeliever is also a disbeliever. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.53} The author notes that the scholars agreed that the Jahmiyya split into three groups over the question of the Qur'an. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.54} One group said the Qur'an is the speech of God and that it is created; another said the Qur'an is the speech of God and remained silent on whether it is created or uncreated; and a third said the words of the Qur'an are created. {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p.54}
