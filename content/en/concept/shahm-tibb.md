## Nature
Well known; every benefit of a fat named below is a report attributed to the book, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Temperament
The fat of the stallion is hotter and drier, then the fat of the gelding, and the fat of the aged is the worst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Actions and properties
Duck fat is very subtle and hotter than chicken fat, the fat of the cock is in between, deer fat is very hot, cow fat is between lion fat and goat fat, bear fat is subtle, the fat of the male is stronger in all of them, the fat of the aged is lighter, she-goat fat is the most astringent of all, and he-goat fat is the more dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Cosmetics
Bear fat and goose fat benefit alopecia, donkey fat benefits the marks of the skin, and goose fat greatly benefits cracks of the face and the lip {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.682}

## Swellings and pustules
Pig fat benefits swellings, and lion fat dissolves hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Ulcers
Donkey fat benefits burns by fire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Organs of the head
Goose fat soothes earache, and so does fox fat, which is very beneficial for that; chicken fat benefits roughness of the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Joints
Camel fat benefits spasm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Ocular organs
Fish fat benefits the water of the eye and sharpens sight with honey; fresh viper fat benefits dimness and the water descending in the eye and makes plucked hair of the eyelid grow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Excretory organs
Goat fat benefits the stinging of the intestines when used and benefits their ulcers; she-goat fat is stronger than pig fat in treating ulcers of the intestines because it congeals quickly, but pig fat soothes the stinging more; the camel's hump as a fumigation benefits haemorrhoids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Poisons and bites
Pig fat benefits the stings of vermin, elephant and deer fat when smeared drive vermin away, and goat fat benefits against cantharides {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that all the soft fats, such as chicken fat and others, benefit pains of the womb, that old fat is bad for it, and that goose fat likewise benefits the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}
