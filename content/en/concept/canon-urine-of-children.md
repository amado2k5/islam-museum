## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that the urine of children gives few signs, above all that of infants, and gives his reasons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.184}.

## Why the signs are few
The urine of children gives few signs, especially that of infants, because of their milky food, and because the dyeing matter in them is still and submerged; and in their natures there is such weakness and so much sleep that the signs of ripening die away {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.184}.
