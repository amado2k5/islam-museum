## Nature
It is the mouse onion, with a leaf like that of the iris and a flower inclining to black {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Actions and properties
Cutting, with some viscosity in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Cosmetics
Burnt and kneaded with honey it is put on alopecia and on the serpiginous scalp disease {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}

## Organs of the chest
It roughens the throat and hardens its flesh, and it is good for asthma, rattling in the throat and chronic cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.611}
