## Overview
In the terminology of the jurists, *tahara* (purity) is the lifting of the ritual impurity (*hadath*) and the removal of impurity (*najas*) by water or by dry ablution (*tayammum*). {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.489}

## In the hadith of Abu Malik al-Ash'ari
The hadith presents it in a great faith-related form: "Purity is half of faith," reported by Muslim (223). In the version of al-Tirmidhi it is "ablution is half of faith," and in the version of Mu'awiya ibn Sallam in Ibn Majah, "perfecting ablution is half of faith." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.489}

## With other deeds
In the same hadith it comes together with the balance of praise, the light of prayer, the proof of charity, the radiance of patience, and the argument of the Qur'an. In his commentary Ibn Rajab explains that it is a balance with which the servant weighs his deeds on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.490}

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] treats the command of the tooth-stick as an extension of bodily and inner purity, and says physical purity of body, clothes, shoes and place, in the mosque, the home and the office, is the way into inner purity and a help to humility in prayer, and that prayer, recitation of the Qur'an and circumambulation all call for purity in both senses. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep25 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep25 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep25 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep25 00:09:00} He lists the five practices of the natural way, circumcision, shaving the pubic hair, plucking the armpits, clipping the nails and trimming the moustache, as aids to physical purity. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep25 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep25 00:10:00}
