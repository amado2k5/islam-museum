## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy sets out what he calls the four states of the believer: giving thanks at a gift, patience at a withholding, remembrance at knowledge and recollection, and stopping from sin. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep07 00:17:00}
He holds that whoever is in obedience should give thanks, whoever is in disobedience should seek forgiveness, and whoever is in blessing should be humble, because thanks increases blessings. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep07 00:21:00}

## The four states
The Sheikh says that the believer gives thanks at a gift, is patient at a withholding, remembers at knowledge and recollection, and stops from sin. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep07 00:17:00}
He holds that thanks for a blessing is of its kind. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep07 00:17:00}
See also [[concept/shukr|Gratitude (shukr)]] and [[concept/sabr|Sabr]].

## Obedience, disobedience and blessing
The Sheikh states that whoever is in obedience should give thanks, whoever is in disobedience should seek forgiveness, and whoever is in blessing should be humble, because thanks for blessings increases them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep07 00:21:00}
