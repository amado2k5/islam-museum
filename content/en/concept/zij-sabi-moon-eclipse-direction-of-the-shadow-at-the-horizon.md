## The rising and setting points
The side from which the darkening begins and the side at which the eclipse ends are found by reference to the rising and setting points at each moment. The beginning is measured from the azimuth of the rising part of the ecliptic, the end from the azimuth of the setting part, in each case on the side opposite the Moon's latitude. The method uses the figure given at the front of the book. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.155-157}

## When the Moon is off the ecliptic
When the Moon's centre is not on the ecliptic and it has latitude to one side, the deviation of the darkness is measured from the rising or setting point according to that latitude, and the angle is read on the horizon. This is the case in which the text distinguishes the beginning, the start of stay and the end. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.155-156}
