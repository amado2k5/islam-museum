## Nature and selection
It is a plant close to hyssop. Dioscorides said: it is a plant with a flower like hellebore, with a purple tinge in its colour, from which eye salves are made, its flower like a lamp on the head of a green plant; there is another wild kind like the garden kind in all its qualities {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

Its seed is what is used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Actions and properties
It is opening, but astringency prevails in it, and it cuts bleeding however it comes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Greatly healing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Applied as a poultice it cuts nosebleed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Chest and breathing
It prevents spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Excretory organs
It helps ulcers of the intestines as an enema, and some claimed that the seed of the wild kind loosens the belly; the amount the book gives is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Poisons and bites
Its seed drunk with wine helps the sting and bite of the scorpion, and some claimed that the seed of the wild kind laid on scorpions numbs them, ends their action and leaves them as if dead {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.
