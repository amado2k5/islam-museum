## Its definition, cause and treatment

Jahr is not seeing by day. Its cause is the thinness and great scantiness of the spirit, which is dissolved by sunlight and gathers in darkness; or the cause may be slight, so that he sees in darkness and shade night and day and weakens in the light. Its treatment is increasing the moistening and thickening the blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.207}
