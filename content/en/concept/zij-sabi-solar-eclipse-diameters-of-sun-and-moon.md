## Correcting the two diameters
The Sun's anomaly for mid-eclipse is found, entered in the tables, and the minutes beneath it in the third table are taken; these are the minutes by which the Sun's diameter differs as seen from the Moon between its farthest and nearest distance. They are added to the diameter at the farthest distance. The Moon's diameter is found in the manner set out for the lunar eclipse, the two corrected diameters are added and halved, and the half-sum is the measure of the eclipse. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.165-166}

## A note on the page
The text adds that for a more exact check the two diameters may be corrected by the parallax in altitude, which it calls more correct for the reckoning. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.166}
