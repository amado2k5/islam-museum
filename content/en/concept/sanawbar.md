## Nature
A well-known tree; as for the pine nut, it was spoken of in the chapter of the letter ha, and now the book wishes to speak of the other parts of the pine tree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Temperament
The power of the bark of the large trees is stronger, and the bark of the kind called fufi is weaker {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Actions and properties
In its bark there is much pith, and the worm in it is certainly in the power of cantharides {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its bark benefits corrosive ulcers and has a healing power; its bark has enough astringency to heal abrasion when laid on as a poultice, and the powder of its bark benefits scalding by hot water; its leaf is stuck on wounds as a powder, its bark suits the places of a blow and heals, and its leaf suits this better because it is moister {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Organs of the head
Gargling with a decoction of its bark draws much phlegm, and the boiling of its bark in vinegar is good as a mouthwash for toothache, and with vinegar put in it and gargled it brings down phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Ocular organs
Its smoke benefits the loss of eyelashes and the eating away of the corners of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Organs of the chest
Its nut benefits old cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its bark and leaf, drunk, benefit pain of the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Excretory organs
Its nut binds the bowels, and its seed with cucumber seed in wine-syrup promotes urine and benefits ulcers of the kidney and the bladder, and its bark binds the bowels too {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Poisons and bites
The green worm that is in the pine is in the nature of cantharides {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}
