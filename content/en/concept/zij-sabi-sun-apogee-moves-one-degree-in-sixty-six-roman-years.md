## Summary
Al-Battani says the Sun's apogee moves with the motion of the fixed stars, which he found by observation, so it moves one degree every sixty-six Roman years, and likewise one degree in a set number of lunar years. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.108}.

## Its use in the reckoning
When the reckoning uses the Arab date, the apogee is treated the same way: the Sun's far point at any date is counted from the known apogee at the stated year, and subtracted from or added to it according to whether the date is before or after. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.108}.
