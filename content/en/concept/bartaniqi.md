## Nature and selection
Some say it is bustan afrouz; others say its leaf looks like the wild sour orange leaf, but darker and better {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

## Temperament and actions
Actions: its leaf is extremely astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: heals wounds and ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Head: its juice is the best for old ulcers in the mouth and thrush; a syrup made of it helps thrush very much {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.
