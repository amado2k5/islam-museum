## Summary
Ibn Sina warns that a man thirsty from sleep who drinks his fill at once may die on the spot, and advises making do with rinsing the mouth; if he cannot avoid drinking, he drinks sip after sip, best taking mixed rose oil and water before the water; one struck by heat sits in a cool place and washes his feet in cold water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.262}.

## Drinking by degrees
If a man is thirsty on waking from sleep he should make do with rinsing the mouth and not drink his fill, for then he may die on the spot; if he cannot avoid drinking, he drinks sip after sip, and only when his condition and the surge of thirst have settled does he drink; and it is sounder to start with mixed rose oil and water before drinking water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.262}.

## The one struck by heat
In sum, the one struck by heat should take his seat in a cool place and wash his feet with cold water; if thirsty he drinks cold water little by little and eats something quickly digested {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.262}.
