## Overview
The Greatest Terror is a Qur'anic phrase in surat al-Anbiya': 'the Greatest Terror shall not grieve them; the angels shall receive them — this is your day that you were promised' [[passage/quran-21-103|the verse of the Greatest Terror]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In exegesis
The exegetes differ on its exact referent — the severest fright of the Day, the blast itself, or the terror of the gathering in general — while agreeing that those for whom God's best has preceded are not grieved by it {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16}.
