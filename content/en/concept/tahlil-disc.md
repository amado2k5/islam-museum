## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents the DISC model for analysing leadership personalities — and its British counterpart Thomas — on two axes: task-focus versus people-focus, and fast versus gradual {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:28:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:47:30}.

## The five patterns
Top-left 'alif' is the dominant: task-focused and fast. Top-right 'ba' the influencer: people-focused and fast. Bottom-left 'jim' the steady: task-focused and gradual. Bottom-right 'dal' the cautious: people-focused and gradual. And 'ha' in the centre between them all — in his view the best pattern for middle leadership {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:29:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:30:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:46:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:42:30}.
Combining letters extends the division to one hundred and four classes: an 'alif-ba' is a strong, fast leader balancing task and people — among the best long-term leadership personalities — while a 'ba-dal' is an 'oscillating' pattern swinging between caring for people and tightening the work {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:40:30}.

## Rules of use
He states that every personality-analysis test carries an error margin; DISC's is fifteen per cent — excellent in this field — while older analyses reach forty per cent (needs_source: the figures as he gave them) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:45:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:46:30}.
That a person's pattern is shaped by the nature of his work — a long-serving accountant becomes a 'jim' even if he was not one — and that a person can reshape his pattern as he wills, though with strain and acting {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:49:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:50:30}.
He uses it in hiring (noting that Kuwait Finance House hires nobody without a Thomas test) and in forming work teams — a personnel section, for instance, is built of one 'alif-ba' head with two 'dal' and two 'jim' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:53:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:57:30}.
He cites that the Prophet distributed his Companions over tasks according to what suited each, and that rare people possess an unusual capacity to reshape themselves — whoever finds them should not let them go {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 02:00:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 02:01:00}.
