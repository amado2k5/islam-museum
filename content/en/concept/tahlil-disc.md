## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents the DISC model for analysing leadership personalities — and its British counterpart Thomas — on two axes: task-focus versus people-focus, and fast versus gradual {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:28:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:47:30}.

## The five patterns
Top-left 'alif' is the dominant: task-focused and fast. Top-right 'ba' the influencer: people-focused and fast. Bottom-left 'jim' the steady: task-focused and gradual. Bottom-right 'dal' the cautious: people-focused and gradual. And 'ha' in the centre between them all — in his view the best pattern for middle leadership {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:29:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:30:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:46:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:42:30}.
Combining letters extends the division to one hundred and four classes: an 'alif-ba' is a strong, fast leader balancing task and people — among the best long-term leadership personalities — while a 'ba-dal' is an 'oscillating' pattern swinging between caring for people and tightening the work {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:40:30}.

## Rules of use
He states that every personality-analysis test carries an error margin; DISC's is fifteen per cent — excellent in this field — while older analyses reach forty per cent (needs_source: the figures as he gave them) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:45:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:46:30}.
That a person's pattern is shaped by the nature of his work — a long-serving accountant becomes a 'jim' even if he was not one — and that a person can reshape his pattern as he wills, though with strain and acting {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:49:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:50:30}.
He uses it in hiring (noting that Kuwait Finance House hires nobody without a Thomas test) and in forming work teams — a personnel section, for instance, is built of one 'alif-ba' head with two 'dal' and two 'jim' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:53:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 01:57:30}.
He cites that the Prophet distributed his Companions over tasks according to what suited each, and that rare people possess an unusual capacity to reshape themselves — whoever finds them should not let them go {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 02:00:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep03 02:01:00}.

## In "Secrets of Prophetic Leadership": personality types in the Sira
In episode 26 of his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]], [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|al-Suwaidan]] says his model of "people's types" is taken from the well-known DISC model and recast on two axes, a horizontal one between a focus on relationships and a focus on achievement, and a vertical one between speed and gradualness; he makes it the skill that trains a leader to analyse people in the role of training. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:08:30} The latest version of his model, he says, has 29 types, of which he presents six with examples from the Companions: {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:09:30}
- **The dominant** (fast, achievement-minded): [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:09:30}
- **The influencer** (fast, relationship-minded): [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]], citing Umar's proposal at Hudaybiyya that Uthman go as envoy to Quraysh because Quraysh liked him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:15:00}
- **The persuader**: [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr al-Siddiq]], which he says is his own view and "may be mistaken". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:16:30}
- **The balanced**: [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:18:30}
- **The conservative**, gradual and relationship-minded: Abdullah ibn Abbas. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:20:30}
- A gradual, precise, achievement-minded type: [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]], chosen by Abu Bakr to collect the Qur'an and by Uthman for the single codex. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:24:30}

He stresses that this analysis has nothing to do with rank in merit: the scholars agree that the best of the Companions is Abu Bakr, then Umar, differ over Uthman and Ali, and agree that these four are the best of the Companions; "merit is one thing, leadership analysis another". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep26 00:20:00}

## In "The Academy for Preparing Leaders": the test, its equations and its limits
In two lessons of [[work/leadership-development-academy-1-program|the Leaders' Academy]] al-Suwaidan has the trainees take the DISC test from his course book. He says he is a licensed DISC practitioner. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:05:30} He stresses that the session concerns the leadership side of personality only, and that he is not a psychologist. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:04:00} The test has forty-four statements, scored from zero to five. They fall into four columns of eleven, so the highest possible score for any letter is fifty-five. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:08:30} It is better, he says, to be rated by others than by oneself, because people have "an extraordinary ability to deceive themselves". In his own management work he prefers eight raters, in the "360-degree" manner: the person, his superior, three colleagues and three subordinates, averaged. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep07 00:08:30}

He reads the scores by four equations. If the top score leads the next by four or more, the type is that one letter. If the gap between the highest and lowest is five or less, the type is the balanced one. If the second leads the third by four or more, the type is the first two letters. Otherwise it is the first three, leaning to the highest. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:17:30} On the grid, the right side cares for people and the left for work; the top is fast and the bottom gradual. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:25:00} He warns that the "cautious" type is not wary of people but careful not to err and not to upset anyone. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:28:30}

His portraits of the types include these:
- The dominant type tires of the same work after three to five years and suits short-term leadership. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:34:30}
- The influential type is obeyed because he is loved, while the dominant is obeyed out of respect and fear. The influential suits long-term leadership. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:46:30}
- The steady type is the rarest; he puts it at seventeen per cent of people. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:47:30}
- The first two types hold that rules were made to be bent, the last two that rules were made to be respected. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 00:52:30}
- Among combinations he calls dominant-influential "the best leadership" and steady-cautious the best supervisory leadership. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:04:00}

He also comments on the test's limits. He says DISC is about eighty-five per cent accurate, and that a type can change with age and experience. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:11:00} Each type suits certain tasks, and a team made only of dominant types fails. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:23:00} He warns against reading character from handwriting, palms, the shape of the eyes or favourite colours. People, he says, are more complex than any list of types. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep08 01:29:00}
