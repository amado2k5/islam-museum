## Summary
Al-Battani gives another way: take the rising times of the part that culminates with the star in the right sphere, add the half day arc of the star to them and also take it from them; the sum is the rising of the opposite of the degree at which the star sets, and the remainder is the rising of the degree with which it rises in the clime, and the degrees against them are then taken from the table. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.54}.

## The same procedure by another door
This way does not need the difference of the two days; it uses the star's half day arc directly on the right-sphere rising times and reaches the same result from another side. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.54}.
