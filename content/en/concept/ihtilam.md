## Summary
Ihtilam is emission of semen during sleep; it happens to both men and women. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1745} It is a chapter of [[concept/ahlam|dreams]] that fiqh binds to [[concept/ruya-fiqh|the fiqh of the dream]].

## Its meaning and definition
Ihtilam in the language is what the sleeper emits in sleep; in fiqh it is the exit of semen in sleep without intercourse or masturbation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1745}. The majority require the fluid to have left its passage — or reached its place in the woman, as detailed below. It is distinguished from madhy, wady and waking emission, each with its own ruling.

## In the woman
The jurists differ on how it is realised in a woman: the Hanbalis, the Hanafis in the apparent report, and the Shafi'is for a married woman hold that it occurs when the fluid reaches the outer part of the private parts; the Malikis hold that it occurs when it leaves the passage {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1745}.

The Muwatta has a chapter "the woman's ghusl when she sees in sleep what a man sees". Malik relates from Ibn Shihab from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr: I asked Umm Sulaym to ask the Prophet about a woman who sees in sleep what a man sees — should she perform ghusl? He said: "Yes, let her perform ghusl" {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.96} [[passage/muwatta-1-127|Muwatta 127]]. A'isha, objecting, said to her: "Fie on you — does a woman see that?" The Prophet said to her: "May your right hand be dusted, and whence does the resemblance come?" — meaning the child's resemblance to its mother {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.96}. In another report through Umm Salama, the Prophet's wife, Umm Sulaym the wife of Abu Talha the Ansari came to the Prophet and said: "God is not shy of the truth" and asked: is there ghusl on a woman who has ihtilam? He said: "Yes, when she sees the water" {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.96} [[passage/muwatta-1-128|Muwatta 128]].

## When he finds the trace but remembers no dream
Malik says of one who finds in his garment a trace of ihtilam and does not know when it was nor remembers seeing anything in his sleep: "Let him perform ghusl from the most recent sleep in which he slept; and if he prayed after that sleep, let him repeat what he prayed after it" {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.95} [[passage/muwatta-1-126|Muwatta 126]]. He argues from Umar's having repeated what he prayed after his last sleep and not what preceded it. The editor notes that a man may have ihtilam without seeing anything, and see without having ihtilam; when he finds water in his garment, ghusl is upon him {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.95}.

## Ghusl and doubt
If a man finds wetness and does not remember ihtilam, ghusl is obligatory on him by the majority; the jurists differ where he doubts between semen and madhy {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1746} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1747}. Their basis is that semen does not exit save with pleasure and flow, while madhy exits without pleasure — it nullifies wudu but does not oblige ghusl.

## Fasting and puberty
Ihtilam has no effect on fasting, by agreement {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1747}, and by agreement it marks puberty {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1748}. That is, ihtilam is among the signs of puberty for the majority; whoever reaches puberty by it owes the prayer, and on it hang the rest of the obligations.

## Between the bad dream and ihtilam
Ihtilam belongs to what occurs in sleep but is not the [[concept/al-hulm|bad dream]] from which refuge is sought; the bad dream is from Satan and nothing is built on it, while ihtilam is a purity event with its own ruling for those who see the semen emitted {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.547} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1745}.

## Perspectives
- **Ahl al-Sunna:** ihtilam is a sign of puberty and ghusl is obligatory on it by the majority; in the woman there is the detailed inter-madhhab difference set out above.
- **The jurists:** the difference between semen and madhy is pleasure and flow, and on it the rulings of purity, fasting and major ritual impurity are built.
