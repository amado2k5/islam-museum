## Summary
Ihtilam is emission of semen during sleep; it happens to both men and women. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1745} On how it is realised in a woman, the jurists differ: the Hanbalis, the Hanafis in the apparent report, and the Shafi'is for a married woman hold that it occurs when the fluid reaches the outer part of the private parts; the Malikis hold that it occurs when it leaves the passage. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1745} A man who finds wetness without remembering a dream must perform ghusl by the majority; where he doubts between semen and madhy the views differ. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1746} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1747} Ihtilam does not affect fasting, and by agreement it marks puberty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1747} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1748}
