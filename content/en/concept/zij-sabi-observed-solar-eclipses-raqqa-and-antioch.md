## Summary
Al-Battani records two solar eclipses that he observed close to his own time. The first, on 8 August at Raqqa, hid more than two-thirds of the Sun; the second, on 23 January at Antioch, hid more than half. He uses both to test the ratio of the shadow to the Moon's diameter. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.85}.

## Dating by the years of Dhu al-Qarnayn
The first eclipse is dated to the year 1211 of the years of Dhu al-Qarnayn and the second to 1212; the numerals are damaged in the scan, so they are given as read, with caution. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.85}.
