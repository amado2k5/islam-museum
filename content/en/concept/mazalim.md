## Overview
The *mazalim* (grievances) was an administrative judicial institution, which the caliph convened or for which he appointed a judge as deputy, to hear complaints of injustice that went beyond the work of ordinary judgement. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.76}

The Abbasid caliph al-Mahdi took an interest in it, and he had a house made with an iron window through which complaints were thrown. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.76}

Al-Muhtadi built a dome with four doors, which he called "the dome of grievances," where he sat to hear both the general public and the privileged. {cite:tarikh-dawla-abbasiyya-taqqush p.166}
