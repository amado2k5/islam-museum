## Summary
In summer Ibn Sina reduces food, drink and exercise, keeps to quiet and ease and to quenching things, uses vomiting for whoever can, and stays in shade and shelter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.

## The rule
In summer food, drink and exercise are reduced; one keeps to quiet, ease and quenching things, to vomiting for whoever can, and to shade and shelter, the word rendered shelter being glossed by the edition's annotator as embracing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.
