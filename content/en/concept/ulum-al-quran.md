## Summary
The Qur'anic sciences are the disciplines used to understand and interpret the Qur'an. [[person/badr-al-din-al-zarkashi|Al-Zarkashi]] says they cannot be bounded, and that earlier scholars had missed writing a book gathering their types, as had been done for hadith; so he wrote [[work/al-burhan-fi-ulum-al-quran-zarkashi|al-Burhan]] in forty-seven types. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.18} {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.19}

## Before and after al-Zarkashi
Layla Muhammad Mas'ud's thesis traces the stages of the field: before al-Zarkashi the exegete's learning was scattered through commentaries such as those of al-Tustari, al-Tabari, al-Zamakhshari and al-Qurtubi; in separate studies of abrogation, the occasions of revelation, the rare words of the Qur'an, its inimitability, its grammar and its rulings; and then in incomplete works, among them Ibn al-Jawzi's Funun al-Afnan fi Ulum al-Qur'an in the sixth century and al-Sakhawi's Jamal al-Qurra' wa-Kamal al-Iqra' in the seventh. {cite:thaqafat-al-mufassir-zarkashi-alexandria p.3} Then al-Zarkashi wrote al-Burhan in the eighth century, followed by [[person/al-suyuti|al-Suyuti]]'s [[work/al-itqan-fi-ulum-al-quran|al-Itqan]]; and in modern times came Subhi al-Salih's Mabahith fi Ulum al-Qur'an, Muhammad Abd Allah Draz's al-Naba' al-Azim and al-Zurqani's Manahil al-Irfan. {cite:thaqafat-al-mufassir-zarkashi-alexandria p.3} Al-Suyuti also wrote al-Tahbir fi Ulum al-Tafsir, in one hundred and two types, and Itmam al-Diraya li-Qurra' al-Nuqaya. {cite:thaqafat-al-mufassir-zarkashi-alexandria p.142}

## How many sciences, and their divisions
Muhammad Rizq Satur, in his foreword to al-Burhan, reports that al-Qadi Abu Bakr ibn al-Arabi in Qanun al-Ta'wil counted the Qur'anic sciences as the number of its words times four, and divided them into three: divine unity, which includes knowledge of creation and of the Creator through His names, attributes and acts; reminder, including promise and threat, Paradise and Hell; and rulings, including all obligations. {cite:burhan-dar-al-hadith-2006 p.11} How far this field extends is debated; see [[question/does-the-quran-contain-every-science|Does the Qur'an contain the roots of every science?]].
