## Cosmetics
Its blood cuts warts, and mouse dung on alopecia areata is beneficial, especially smeared with honey and especially burnt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Organs of the head
It is said that roasted, dried and given to a child to eat, it stops the dribbling of saliva from his mouth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Excretory organs
It is said that mouse dung drunk with frankincense and wine-honey breaks the stone, that its suppository loosens a child's bowels, and that cooked in water and sat in by one with difficulty in urination it benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Poisons and bites
People agreed that split open and placed on a scorpion's sting it benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}
