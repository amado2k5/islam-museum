## Summary
Ibn Sina gives northern dwellings the rulings of cold lands and seasons and details what befalls men, women and boys there {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.128}.

## General rulings
These dwellings follow the rulings of cold lands and seasons, in which diseases of retention and squeezing abound and the humours gather inside. Its consequences are good digestion and long life, and nosebleed is frequent among them because of fullness and little dissolving, so that the vessels burst. Epilepsy does not befall them because of the health of their interior and the abundance of their innate heat; if it does come it is strong, since it comes only from a strong cause. Sores heal quickly in their bodies because of their strength and the quality of their blood, and the great heat of their hearts gives them a predatory temper {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.128}.

## Women
Their women are not cleansed well by the menses, for their flow is not sufficient, so, as the authorities he reports said, they are barren because the wombs are not clean. He points out that this is the opposite of what is seen in the lands of the Turks, and says that the strength of their innate heat offsets what is lacking of the loosening and softening causes from outside. They said that miscarriage rarely befalls them, that their labour is hard because the birth organs are drawn together, that most miscarry from cold, and that their milk is scant and thick from cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.128}.

## Consumption, tetanus and the young
In this region, especially among the weak such as women, tetanus and consumption arise from hard labour, for they strain and the vessels of the chest, or parts of the nerve and fibre, split, giving consumption in the first case and tetanus in the second. Boys get hydrocele, which goes with age, and girls get dropsy of the belly and wombs, which goes with age. Ophthalmia is rare among them, but severe when it comes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.128}.
