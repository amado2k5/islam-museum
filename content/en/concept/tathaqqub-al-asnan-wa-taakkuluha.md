## Its use
Its use is mostly by stuffing: the tooth is stuffed with what he described of this kind, which bars the corrosion and calms the pain; camphor was tried in the stuffing and was most beneficial, barring the corrosion's increase and calming the pain (a tried report carried on the book's attribution). Salves of this kind are used too, a good drug whose recipe (left out), stuffing and smearing may be joined, a strong thing of the qalqandiyun kind set on the spot; among the mouth-held rinses a cooked thing of this kind benefits greatly; drops are used in the corrosion itself of this kind, and almond oil dripped beside the eaten tooth helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.274-275}
