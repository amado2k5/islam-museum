## Nature
Qulimiya may be made from gold and silver, and may be made from copper and from marqashita; it is a dross that rises over the smelting, or a smoke, and what settles of it is plate-like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Temperament
Near the qulimiya of gold, and colder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Actions and properties
It has drying and clearing in moderation without stinging, especially the washed form, which is best in ointments; its drying and clearing act in moderate bodies, not in the hard-fleshed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Wounds and ulcers
It benefits itch and stubborn and moist ulcers, in ointments, dusted on {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}
