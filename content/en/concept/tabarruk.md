## Overview

Tabarruk is the seeking of blessing through sanctioned means. The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats its forms: through the basmala and hamdala, the water of Zamzam, and certain times and places {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
