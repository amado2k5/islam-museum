## Nature
Galen is quoted as saying that qalqadis may turn into qalqatar; the editor's note says that qalqatar is yellow vitriol {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Actions and properties
It has a strong burning power, a binding of flows of blood and a drying; the burnt form dries more and bites less; with its much astringency it has much heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Swellings and pustules
It benefits the spreading sore and erysipelas when smeared with coriander water; it is sprinkled on the malignant and the creeping ulcer, burns away excess flesh and makes a scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Organs of the head
It benefits nosebleed, swellings of the gums and swellings of the glands {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Ocular organs
It enters eye salves for cleansing and for thinning the humour of the eyelids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it stops the flow of blood from the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}
