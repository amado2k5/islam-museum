## What it is and its causes
Thrush is an ulcer forming in the skin of the mouth and tongue with spreading and widening; it occurs much in children — most of what occurs in them occurs for bad milk or its bad digestion in the stomach. It may come from every humour: the white mostly from salty phlegm, the yellow bilious and the most inflamed of them, the black melancholic — the worst of all — and the bright-red sanguine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.262}
## Its kinds and Galen's naming habit
Some of its kinds corrode much and some are contained; it may come with a tumour or singly. Every ulcer occurring in the mouth's surface hurries to spread, for the inseparable inherent heat and its moist soft skin. Galen's custom is to call it thrush while it stays in the surface; when it putrefies and sinks he calls it not thrush but malignant ulcers — the ones needing caustic drugs (a habit ascribed to Galen). Thrush grows common when rains are many, and in the epidemic fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.262}
## The treatment
The dominant humour making the thrush is aimed at first, evacuated from the whole body if dominant, then from the vein in the mouth's neighbourhood of this kind (the venesection site carried as the book names it), its bleeding being useful in all hot material diseases of the mouth. Then the named pustule-medicines are used — the strong-mattered, much-moist suppurating one treated by the strong, the moderate by the moderate, the weak by the weak; if the sore nearly reaches the bone the very strong of this kind are needed, and all oils are to be avoided even olive-oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.262}
## Regimen of kinds and ages
The drugs are picked from the cold and hot pustule-medicines named in the first chapter; for the sanguine-red the fittest drugs at first have slight astringency and cooling, then afterwards the dissolvers; for what tends to lividity and yellowness the drug's cooling is increased; the rest need first what dries and polishes moderately at the start, then what dries and dissolves with strength — age regarded in all of it. Children's drugs must be weaker and their milk rectified; adults' stronger; children may profit by foods alone, and if they do not yet eat, the wet-nurse is fed them; the cold, astringent and dissolving are used for it by its kind, of this kind (the names, recipes and salves left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.262} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.263}
