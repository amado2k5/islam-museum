## Overview
Shaykh Shaltut presents spending in God's path as a pillar of building society, noting that the Qur'an in some suras mentions faith only coupled with spending {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 108}{cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 109}. Feeding the poor and destitute is the steep pass that must be climbed to happiness, and failing to urge it is a mark of denying the religion {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 110}. He draws three circles of spending: family and kin, the poor unable to work, and the public interests.
