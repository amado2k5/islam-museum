## Definition
Rada'a — suckling — in Islam is nursing the infant until weaning, and in law it is a second kinship running parallel to birth: 'what is forbidden through lineage is forbidden through nursing', in Aisha's report in the two Sahihs, so the nurse becomes a mother by milk, her children siblings to the nursed child, and intermarriage between them is barred as it is by birth. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}

## The nursing span and its rulings
The Qur'an sets nursing at two full years for whoever wishes to complete it, makes the nurse's provision and clothing due on the father in fairness, and conditions their weaning on mutual consent and counsel — so the ruling binds infant, nurse and both parents together, not the infant alone. {cite:tanzil-quran}
