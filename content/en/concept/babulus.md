## Nature and selection
It is what is called the wild and cream poppy, and it acts like the yatu' in its loosening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}. The footnote names it as the poppy; the Latin name is not used.

## Temperament and actions
Very hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: purges like the yatu'at {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.412}.
