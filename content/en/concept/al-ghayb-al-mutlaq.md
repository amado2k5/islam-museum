## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the unseen has two kinds: the relative, known to one party and not another, and the absolute, which has no precursors and which God alone knows {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.253}. See also [[concept/ghayb|al-ghayb]] for the creedal entry.

## The relative unseen
He gives the example of a stolen wallet: the thief and the one who helped him know what the owner does not, so it is relative unseen {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.253}. He also gives the example of a promotion or dismissal that its maker knows before the person concerned is told {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.253}.

## The absolute unseen
For him the absolute unseen is what surprises a person and those around him without any sign beforehand, and only God knows it {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.253}.

## Secret and what is more hidden
He distinguishes the secret, which you confide to another, from what you keep in your breast and tell no one. The latter is more hidden than the secret, and only God knows it {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.242}.

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] makes the bar on entering the absolute unseen one of the rules for the scientific miracle in the Sunna, counting in it the divine essence, the spirit, the life of the barzakh, the reckoning of the grave, the scale, the bridge, paradise and hell, and says a Muslim accepts what is reported of them in submission, without trying to prove it scientifically. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep01 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep01 00:08:00} He explains the hadith of the five keys of the unseen known only to God, dwelling on rain, which he says no power on earth can direct as to where, how much or when it falls, and on what the wombs absorb, saying a woman may conceive and lose the pregnancy without knowing. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:05:00}
