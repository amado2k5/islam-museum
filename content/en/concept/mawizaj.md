## Nature and selection
The mountain raisin, a black wrinkled grain like the black chickpea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Actions and properties
Burning, eating, sharp and acrid {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Cosmetic uses
It kills lice, especially with arsenic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Wounds and ulcers
With arsenic or alone, on scab and scaling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It is chewed so that phlegm and humidity run from the brain; boiled in vinegar it is used as a rinse for toothache and humidity of the gums, and with honey it heals bad mouth sores {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Given with the water of qaratin it makes one vomit sticky chyme; the quantity named is not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Excretory organs
Drinking it is dangerous, for it ulcerates the bladder; with correctives and in a moderate measure the book says it cleansed it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.
