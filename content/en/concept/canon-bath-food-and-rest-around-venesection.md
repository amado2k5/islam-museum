## Summary
Ibn Sina advises what comes before and after bleeding: a bath before it makes the bleeding hard, and after it the one bled moves up gradually in food, takes no exercise and no dissolving bath, and he says what is done if the arm swells after the bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.293}.

## Bathing before
Bathing before the bleeding may make it hard, by what it thickens of the skin, softens it and prepares it to slip, unless the one to be bled has very thick blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.293}.

## Food and movement afterwards
The one bled should not rush to fullness afterwards but advance gradually in food, taking it light at first; likewise he should not exercise after it but incline to lie down, and not bathe after it with a dissolving bath. Sleep close to the bleeding may bring a breaking in the limbs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.293}.

## A swollen arm
One who is bled and whose arm swells is bled from the other arm to the extent he can bear, a white-lead ointment is put on it and the parts around are smeared with strong coolants {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.293}.
