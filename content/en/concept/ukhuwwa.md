## Overview
Brotherhood among Muslims, in the thirty-fifth hadith of the Forty: "Be, servants of God, brothers. The Muslim is the brother of the Muslim: he does not wrong him, nor forsake him, nor lie to him, nor belittle him." Then the prohibition of shedding one another's blood, taking one another's property, and violating one another's honour. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.710}

## A brotherhood that protects rights
It is a brotherhood that protects rights, not a mere feeling. It forbids envy, inflating prices, hatred, turning away from one another, and selling over a brother's sale. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.710}

## The hadith of Ibn Umar
In the hadith of Ibn Umar, the thirty-seventh: "Whoever is in his brother's need, God is in his need." And it is enough evil for a Muslim that he belittle his brother. In his commentary Ibn Rajab establishes that brotherhood requires perfection in preferring the brother, loving him, and attending to his condition. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.711}

Connected to brotherhood are [[concept/hurmat-al-dam|the sanctity of blood]] and [[concept/satr|veiling a Muslim]].

## Brotherhood in Medina (al-Rahiq al-Makhtum, pp. 185–186)
The Prophet made brothers between the Muhajirun and the Ansar on the basis of mutual support, and made it a bond tied by blood and property. The tribal solidarities of the Jahiliyya melted away, and distinctions of lineage, colour, and homeland fell away ([[event/brotherhood-of-muhajirun-and-ansar|the brotherhood between the Muhajirun and the Ansar]]). {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.185} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.186}.
