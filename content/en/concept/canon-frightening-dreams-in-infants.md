## Summary
Ibn Sina explains a child's frightening dreams as coming from its fullness through greed: when food spoils and the stomach feels it, the harm passes to the moulding and imaginative powers, which show terrible dreams; he advises not putting it to sleep on a full stomach and giving honey to lick {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.

## The cause
A child may have dreams that frighten it in its sleep, mostly from its fullness through its great greed. When the food spoils and the stomach feels it, that harm passes from the sensing power to the moulding power and the imagination, which present bad, terrible dreams {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.

## The regimen
So it must not be put to sleep on a full stomach, and it should lick honey so that what is in its stomach is digested and sent down {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.
