## Principles of cure
If nothing prevents, he is bled first, then the matters are drawn downward, the patient is made to vomit and is lodged in a bright house, and kept from sinking into coma by pressing him to stay awake {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} Strong dissolving means and the rest of what is known from the Canon are used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.86}

## Food and the aftermath
When one of them is fed, he is fed with what is light, and care is taken lest the vapour be drawn upward {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.86} If the long course of the illness calls for a purgative it is done with regard to the fever and the patient's power, and when he forgets his stool or urine the bladder is attended to so that he may urinate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.86} Then, when the illness has been roused out, swinging and being carried are used, then slight exercise and the regimen of the convalescent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.86}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.85-86}
