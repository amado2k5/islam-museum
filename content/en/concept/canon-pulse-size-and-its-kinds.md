## Summary
Ibn Sina begins the genera with the extent of expansion: the pulse has three dimensions, length, breadth and depth, each with three simple states, and compound ones follow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.169}.

## The simple states
By size there are nine simple states: long, short and moderate; broad, narrow and moderate; low, high and moderate. A long pulse is one whose parts are felt along its length more than the natural amount, whether measured against true balance or against the particular person's own natural norm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.169}.

## The compounds
A pulse exceeding in length, breadth and depth is called large, one falling short in all three small, with the moderate between. One exceeding in breadth and height is called thick and one falling short in both fine; some compounds have names and some do not {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.169}.
