## An ascribed saying and the nurse's regimen
It has been said that a bitch's milk benefits strongly in this by a specific property (a saying ascribed in the book's wording, carried as an attribution and set aside for the founder's ruling). If the pain grows severe, what he described is smeared on — a warmed tepid oil of this kind; chewing anything firm is barred, and the wet-nurse puts her finger in his mouth when the eruption-pain begins and rubs his gum hard so the moisture runs out through the gum, then wipes with the named drugs; when the teeth show a little, head, neck and jaws are bound with wool soaked in tepid oil, and oil is dropped in his ear — something of this chapter was already given in Book I {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.276}
