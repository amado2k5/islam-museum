## Nature
It is the juice of cane cooked until it thickens, and fanidh is made of it; this is done in the land of Makran in the region of Kirman and it is carried from there to the lands, and fanidh is made only in the land of Makran and nowhere else {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Selection
The best is the white, thin Harrani {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Temperament
Hot and moist in the first degree, the white especially, which is moister {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Actions and properties
Thicker than sugar and much hotter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Organs of the chest
Good for cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Excretory organs
It softens the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as benefiting coldness of the womb and the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}
