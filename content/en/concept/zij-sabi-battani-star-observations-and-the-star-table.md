## The observation and the table
Al-Battani says he observed the fixed stars many times over successive years, and relied on an observation in the year 1191 of the era of Dhu al-Qarnayn. He measured their distances from the equator of the day and the signs of the zodiac on the meridian, and set out tables of their longitudes, latitudes and motions. He also reports observing a northern star between the eyes of the Kneeler, the Yemeni Sirius and Regulus in that year, and comparing them with Ptolemy's places. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.191-192}

## The interval between the two observations
The text puts the interval between the two observations at about one hundred and eighty-two years, from which the motion of one degree in sixty-six years is derived. The number is somewhat disturbed in the scan and is to be checked against the original. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.191-192}
