## Definition
Iltimas is seeking in the lexical sense; its frequentative form means seeking again and again. In the technical sense it is a request between equals, where the one who asks and the one asked are on the same footing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3314}

## Ruling
- Asked for: such as seeking the crescent of Ramadan, which is obligatory for the Hanafis and recommended for most jurists; seeking water before tayammum, which is obligatory; and seeking the Night of Power in night prayer, which is recommended. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3314}
- Forbidden: when the thing sought is forbidden by the Law, such as seeking wine or any other prohibited thing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3314}
