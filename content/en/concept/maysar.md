## Summary
Maysir and the arrows are a form of gambling close to seeking decisions by [[concept/azlam|arrows]]. They would divide the meat of the camels they slaughtered by means of the arrows {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38}.

## Gambling and generosity
The Sealed Nectar holds that their engagement in maysir was a result of their generosity. They regarded it as one path of generosity, since they would feed the poor with what they won, or with what remained after the winners' shares. That is why the Qur'an does not deny the benefit of wine and gambling, but says that their sin is greater than their benefit ([[passage/quran-2-219|al-Baqara 219]]) {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.47}. See [[concept/jahili-virtues|Virtues of the people of the Jahiliyya]].

## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an counts maysir along with wine, idol-stones and arrows as an abomination from Satan's handiwork ([[passage/quran-5-90|al-Ma'ida 90]]).

## For young visitors
Maysir is a gambling game with arrows played over the meat of slaughtered camels. God forbade it, and the Qur'an says its sin is greater than its benefit.
