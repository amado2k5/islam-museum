## Summary
Preliminaries to intercourse are kissing, touching and close contact before it. The jurists rule on their consequences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14917}

## Pilgrimage and fasting
The Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis hold that whoever does a preliminary of intercourse in ihram owes a sacrifice, and his pilgrimage is not void. For the Malikis, if he ejaculates the rule is that of intercourse. In fasting, kissing and touching do not break the fast unless they cause ejaculation, which breaks it by agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14918}

## Revocation, zihar and affinity
Touching or kissing without desire is not a revocation of divorce. The Hanafis and Malikis hold that revocation may be made by intercourse or its preliminaries with desire, the Shafi'is deny it altogether, and the Hanbalis allow it only by intercourse. In zihar the Hanafis, most Malikis and one report of Ahmad forbid the preliminaries before expiation, while the Shafi'is in the sounder view allow them. In affinity only the Hanafis hold that kissing and touching with desire create it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14918} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14919}

## Recommendation to the husband
A husband who wishes to have intercourse with his wife is recommended to caress her first, so that her desire rises and she gains as much pleasure as he does. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14919}
