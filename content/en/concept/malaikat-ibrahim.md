## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an tells the story of Ibrahim's guests in [[passage/quran-11-71|Hud 71]], [[passage/quran-15-51|al-Hijr 51]] and [[passage/quran-51-24|al-Dhariyat 24]], where they give him tidings of the birth of Ishaq and then of Ya'qub.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir interprets that the guests were angels who gave Ibrahim tidings of Ishaq's birth and then of Ya'qub's {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; in Qisas al-Anbiya' he notes the angels were messengers from God to Ibrahim {cite:qisas-al-anbiya-ibn-kathir-farmawi-1997}.

## Notes
Some held the guests were angels, others that they were men carrying a message from God; the primary interpretation takes them as angels {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
