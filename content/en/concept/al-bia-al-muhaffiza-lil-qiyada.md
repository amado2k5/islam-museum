## Summary
"The environment that fosters leadership" is the last model in [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]]'s programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]], set out in episodes 29 and 30. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:53:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:08:00} Its idea is that someone not raised in a leadership family still has a chance if an environment that fosters leadership is created around him; the model has six parts: flexibility, curiosity, freedom, values, balance and sponsorship. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:01:00} Without such environments, he says, the community loses its best resource, its people, and its talent emigrates. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:04:30}

## Flexibility
He defines it as "positive adaptation to intellectual and material change", and says that rigid environments and spineless ones alike fail to produce leaders. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:22:00} He cites the [[event/treaty-of-hudaybiyya|Treaty of Hudaybiyya]]: [[person/suhayl-ibn-amr|Suhayl ibn Amr]] objected to writing "the Merciful, the Compassionate" and "Messenger of God"; Ali would not erase them, so the Prophet erased them himself. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:12:30} His rule: never give up principles and values; positions and means can be given up. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:12:30} Gradualness is part of flexibility for him, and he relates from al-Shatibi the answer of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz to his son Abd al-Malik, who urged him to hurry his reforms. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:17:30} Flexibility means knowing when to be soft and when to be firm; his example of firmness is the poet Abu Azza, freed after Badr without ransom on condition he would not fight again, who fought at Uhud, was captured a second time, and was not freed. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:22:00}

## Curiosity
He defines it as the love of discovering the unknown, and sets three conditions for it to be positive: it concerns public, not personal, matters; it is useful; and it concerns the person himself. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:24:30} He sees a child's curiosity as a sign of intelligence, and advises parents to answer, be patient, and say "I don't know". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:25:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:27:00} Among his examples are the Prophet's scouts before Uhud, including [[person/hubab-ibn-al-mundhir|al-Hubab ibn al-Mundhir]], who went into the Quraysh camp. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:31:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:32:00}

## Freedom
He defines it as "saying and doing what you wish" on two conditions, courtesy and no harm; its limits for him are "disciplining morals and freeing thought and expression". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:36:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:47:30} He mentions that he explained freedom in his programmes "Life Taught Me" and "Winds of Change" and was criticised for it. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:37:30} He rejects two extremes: freedom as chaos, and the fear of freedom that multiplies prohibitions. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:40:30} He cites al-Shafi'i's saying that where there is disagreement, censure falls away, and the principle that things are permitted by default. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:41:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:42:30} Among his examples are the Ansar's rejection at the Trench of a settlement with Ghatafan over the fruit of Medina, which the Prophet then dropped, and Ibn Ubayy's withdrawal with three hundred men at Uhud, for which he was not punished. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:45:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep29 00:46:00}

## Values
Values for him are "the controls that govern behaviour" in word and deed, with favour to no one. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:05:30} One of his examples is the story of Zayd ibn Sa'na, the Jewish scholar who lent the Prophet dates to help a newly Muslim tribe struck by drought and then came to demand it roughly before it was due; the Prophet rebuked Umar, not him, and Zayd became a Muslim, saying he had been testing the trait of forbearance. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:18:00} Another is Abu Bakr's stance towards [[person/mistah-ibn-uthatha|Mistah]] after the affair of the slander, when he swore to stop supporting him and then resumed after a verse of Surat al-Nur came down ([[passage/quran-24-22|al-Nur 24:22]]). {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:21:00}

## Balance
He defines it as giving each thing its due, no more and no less, and avoiding both excess and neglect. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:23:30} He cites the story of Salman and Abu al-Darda', and that of the three men who wanted to fast without break, pray all night and keep away from women. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:33:00}

## Sponsorship
Sponsorship for him is "caring for a candidate for leadership materially, morally or both", and he says there is a sponsor behind every leader. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:37:00} His examples include the house of al-Arqam as the first "intensive courses", the twelve representatives at the second pledge of al-Aqaba, and the Badr captives who could not pay ransom being allowed to ransom themselves by teaching ten Ansari boys to write. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:38:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:38:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:41:00}

## The close
Al-Suwaidan ends the programme with three "illusions" he says he set out to dispel: that the lessons of the Sira are exhausted, that the community must stay backward, and that it can rise apart from its Islamic identity. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:46:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:48:00} His life, he says, is focused on two things, thought and leadership, and preparing leaders is methodical, hard and continuous work. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:49:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep30 00:49:30}
