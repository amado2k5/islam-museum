## Nature and temperament
Its strength is like that of the fuwa spice, but finer; it is brought from China. Some say that, with its heat, it has a cooling force, but in truth it is hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.519} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Actions and properties
It opens gently, but not to the point of standing in for cinnamon. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Wounds, ulcers and mouth
Good for septic ulcers in the soft organs, and good for foul thrush in the mouth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Chest and digestion
Held in the mouth, the book says, it clears the voice. It is strong in opening obstructions of the liver. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): it cleans the urinary passages, brings out gravel, and expels stone of the kidney and the bladder. The book says that the saliva of one who chews it pleases a sexual partner. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}
