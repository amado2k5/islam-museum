## Nature
Dioscorides describes a herb with many rod-like stems, shaped like the plant called kamadryus, with thin leaves like chickpea leaves. It grows much in Cilicia. Taken fresh, it is drunk with vinegar and water; when dry, its decoction is drunk. The editor's note calls it the spleen herb. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Spleen
Its decoction strongly dissolves swellings of the spleen. A poultice of it with fig and vinegar, applied to those who suffer in the spleen, helps them clearly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Poisons
A poultice with vinegar alone helps the bites of venomous creatures. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}
