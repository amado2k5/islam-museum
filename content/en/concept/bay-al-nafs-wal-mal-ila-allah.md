## Summary
In an episode of his series "Al Fawzu Al Moubeen", Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy takes up the meaning of a believer giving his self and his wealth in the way of God. He presents it as a contract of sale in which God is the buyer and Paradise is the price. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:01:00}
He explains the pillars of a sale, holds that the thing sold in this contract already belongs to the buyer, and advises the Muslim to renew this contract every day. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:47:30}

## The verse and the image of the bargain
The Sheikh recites a verse about giving self and wealth in the way of God in return for Paradise, and says he wishes to live with this verse in this episode. [[passage/quran-9-111|Qur'an 9:111]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:01:00}

## The pillars of a sale, and God as the buyer
The Sheikh explains the pillars of a sale: seller, buyer and price. He notes that God is the buyer in this contract, and that the verb is in the past tense to show that the contract is complete. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:01:30}

## The thing sold belongs to the buyer
The Sheikh says that the thing sold in this contract is stranger than imagination, because the buyer already owns it. He describes this as a divine kindness and God's tenderness towards His servants. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:03:00}

## The example of the car that stays with the seller
The Sheikh gives the example of buying a car that the buyer leaves with the seller after the contract. It is meant to bring closer the idea that God took ownership of self and wealth through the contract, then left them with His servant to use. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:04:30}

## A question about breach of the contract
A member of the audience asks about a contract that lasts for life: what happens if breach occurs after years, or many times? The Sheikh answers by speaking of faith and submission. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:34:00}

## Renewing the contract every day
The Sheikh advises the Muslim to renew the contract every day, saying that he has sold his self and his wealth to his Lord, and he prays for the people of the house and the children to be righteous. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep10 00:47:30}
