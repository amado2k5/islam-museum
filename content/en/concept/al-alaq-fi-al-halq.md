## The tricks and the mould-taking
Other tricks are played for it: the man is plunged in hot water or sits in a hot bath — above all having eaten what he described — then keeps taking iced cold water in his mouth time after time until the leech leaves the spot it clung to, fleeing the heat and leaning toward the cold; if one must bear that heat until faintness is feared he bears it, for it is a very good regimen for bringing it out, and often it suffices to eat what he described and sit in the sun, mouth open, beside iced cold water. And of the people is he who gives the leech-patient lentils and a kind of red blood-insect like the small hard ticks called in our country al-anjal (a saying ascribed to the people, carried on its attribution and set before the founder). Vinegar alone, if sipped, may bring it out of the throat, above all with salt. As for taking it with the mould: the swallower of the leech is stood in the sun, his mouth opened and his tongue pressed down with the tip of a spoon-like probe; when the leech is glimpsed the mould is set at the root of its neck lest it break — and that mould is what it is drawn out with (carried as the book's description, not a procedure) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.286}
