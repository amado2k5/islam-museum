## Warming and penetration
Most of the temperament-changers they need are the warming ones (the names of the plasters, oils and decoctions are not carried); know that in most nerve diseases the treatment aims at venesection of the back of the brain — except what is in the face — and then, after that, at the origin of the nerve which moves the member whose nerve is sick {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.136}

## What strengthens and what harms
The nerve may be harmed by some things and helped by some things, many of them recorded in the tables of simple drugs; here only what belongs to its own states and diseases is considered: the things that strengthen the nerves among drinks and foods (their names are not carried); the most suitable water for them is rain water, and moderate exercise and hot oils profit them. The things that harm the nerves: very frequent intercourse (a labelled line, held for the founder's ruling), sleeping on a full stomach, drinking icy cold water, the very sweet, much drinking for the sharp stinging of the drink and for its turning to acidity which moreover cools; every sour, flatulent and strongly cooling thing harms them, and much venesection harms them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.136}

## Why cold water harms
Know that cold water harms the nerve because it cannot digest the moistures in it, so it turns raw {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.136}
