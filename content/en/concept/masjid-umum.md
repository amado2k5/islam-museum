## Overview
The congregational house of worship for Muslims. From the Prophet's mosque in Medina its plan developed around the prayer area, the qibla, the pulpit and the courtyard. {cite:wikidata}

## For Sprouts
The mosque is the blessed house of worship where Muslims gather to pray and cooperate in kindness and goodness.

## For Explorers
The mosque is the sanctuary dedicated to Muslim prayer and communal devotion, serving as the heart of community life and education in cities and towns. Originating with the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, mosque architecture expanded globally to encompass minarets, domes, courtyards, and the mihrab pointing toward the Kaaba in Mecca.

## For Scholars
The foundational religious and communal institution in Islam, designated for daily congregational prayers, the Friday Juma, and spiritual retreat. Its functional archetype originated with the Prophet's Mosque in Medina as an open space combining worship, education, communal counsel, and welfare. Architecturally, it standardized core elements including the prayer hall, qibla wall, recessed mihrab, minbar, minaret, ablution facilities, and central sahn, adapting harmoniously to diverse geographical and cultural landscapes across the world.
