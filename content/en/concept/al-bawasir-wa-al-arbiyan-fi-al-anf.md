## Their kinds
The piles are superfluous flesh that grows: sometimes a soft white flesh with no pain — the easiest to treat; sometimes red and dusky, very painful — the hardest to treat, especially if foul pus flows from it. Sometimes there is among them a cancerous one that corrupts the nose's shape and hurts by its severe stretching — dusky of colour, of very bad formation, deep in the root; its way is palliation, not cutting and scraping. The cancerous may be told from the bad piles thus: the growing flesh, if it arises after illnesses and in the absence of flows, is cancer — especially if melancholic signs preceded it in the brain and its start was like a chickpea or hazelnut, then it kept growing and worked a hardness in the nose {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.248} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.249}
## The cancer's character and the hanging piles
The cancer is mostly without pus or flow to the throat — it is dry and hard. The piles may lengthen into hanging piles, even lengthening till they come out of the nose or the palate. All the drugs that benefit the arbiyan benefit the piles, though their strength may need to be broken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.249}
