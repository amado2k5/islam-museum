## Nature
Dioscorides said that some people call it sarish since it is a plant from which the glue sarish is made; it is well known, with a leaf like the Syrian leek, a smooth stem with a flower at its tip called anbariqun, and long round roots resembling large acorns in shape, their power being hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.608–609}

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Actions and properties
Warming {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Swellings and pustules
Mixed with suwiq it benefits hot swellings at their beginning {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Wounds and ulcers
As a poultice it benefits dirty malignant ulcers, ulcerated wounds and boils, and burns from fire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Cosmetics
Its ash grows hair in alopecia as a poultice after the place has been rubbed with a woollen cloth, and if white vitiligo is rubbed with a cloth in the sun and the root with vinegar is then smeared on it, it removes it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Organs of the head
Alone, or mixed with frankincense, honey, wine and myrrh and warmed, and dropped into the ear opposite the side of the aching tooth, it calms its pain; the water of its root mixed with old sweet wine and myrrh and cooked is a remedy for the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Ocular organs
This same compound is also an excellent remedy for applying to the various pains of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Organs of the chest
Drunk with tila' (boiled-down grape must) it benefits pain of the two sides, cough and weakness of the muscles, and its root cooked with the dregs of wine as a poultice is very good for swellings of the breast {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Poisons and bites
It benefits against the bites of vermin, its leaf is also useful against the bite of vermin as a poultice, and if its fruit and flower are drunk with wine it benefits greatly against the sting of the scorpion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as promoting the urine and the menses when drunk with tila' (boiled-down grape must) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}
