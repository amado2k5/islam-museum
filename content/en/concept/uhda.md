## Summary
In sale, 'uhda is the seller's guarantee of the price or of the title when the object is claimed by a third party. In pre-emption it is the return of ownership to the pre-emptor or buyer; in the option for a defect it is the guarantee when a defect appears {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11906}. In pre-emption the Shafi'is and Malikis place the warranty on the seller if the pre-emptor took the property from him; the Hanafis place it on the buyer when the property was sold by someone other than the seller {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11907}. For defects, the year and the three-day warranties differ: a defect arising in the buyer's hands and one present at the seller's both give a right of return {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11907} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11908}.
