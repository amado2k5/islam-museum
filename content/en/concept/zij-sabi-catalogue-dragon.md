## The Dragon

The entries for the Dragon follow those of the Bears. They begin with the star on the tip of its tail and include the star above its head and the star above its eye. Positions are given in longitude and latitude in a table, which is not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.246}
