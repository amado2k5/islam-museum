## Summary
Rukn in the lexicon is the strongest side and the great matter; technically it is what a thing cannot exist without, the part of its essence, as against the condition, which is outside the essence, and against the obligation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8955} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8956} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8957} A pilgrim who leaves a pillar of the pilgrimage does not complete his rite; one who leaves an obligation makes it good by a sacrifice, and the Hanafis and Hanbalis apply this to prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8957} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8958} The pillars of ablution, tayammum, prayer, fasting, i'tikaf and pilgrimage differ between the schools. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8958} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8959} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8960} In contracts the majority hold that each has three pillars, the formula, the two parties and the object, while the Hanafis hold the formula alone; the lack of a pillar makes the contract void. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8960} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8961} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8962} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8963} The sunna is to touch or kiss the Black Stone and the Yemeni corner, and to point to them from afar when crowded. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8963} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8964} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8965} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8966}
