## Nature and selection
Nature: a thing that twines round old oak trees, like fern but smaller and less finely cut; it has tangled roots, with sweetness together with pungency, bitterness and astringency with a rotting power. Temperament: strongly hot and dry. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.446}

## Actions
It thins hair, clears it away, and removes it by its rotting and sharpness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.447}

## Uses by organ
Joints: some claim it helps paralysis and facial palsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.447}
