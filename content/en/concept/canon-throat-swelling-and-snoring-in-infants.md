## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions a swelling of the throat between the mouth and the gullet, which may spread to the muscles and the vertebrae of the nape, treated by loosening the bowels with a suppository and then mulberry rob, and then loud snoring in sleep, treated by licking flaxseed or cumin with honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.

## Throat swelling
A swelling of the throat between the mouth and the gullet may befall a child, and may spread to the muscles and the vertebrae of the nape. The bowels should be loosened with a suppository, and then it is treated with mulberry rob and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.

## Loud snoring
A loud snoring may come on it in sleep, and it should lick pounded flaxseed with honey or pounded cumin kneaded with honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.
