## Summary
Al-Battani describes a test of whether the crescent will be seen: the Sun and the Moon are reckoned for the time of sunset on the second day after conjunction, that is the twenty-ninth of the Arab month; then their places and the parallax in longitude and latitude are found, which gives the visible place of the Moon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.130}.

## The final step
Once the visible distance is fixed, the Moon's distance from the Sun is taken in parts of setting times and compared with the corrected arc of vision; if it equals or exceeds the arc the crescent is seen, and if less it is not seen in that place. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.131}.
