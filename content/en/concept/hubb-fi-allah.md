## Summary
Love for God's sake is loving a brother for God's countenance rather than kinship or worldly benefit. Among the seven shaded by God are "two people who loved each other in God, gathering and parting upon it" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 660}, and Sahih Muslim records "whoever loves for God, hates for God, gives for God and withholds for God has completed faith" {cite:sahih-muslim}. Malik's Muwatta devotes a chapter to mutual love {cite:muwatta-malik}.
