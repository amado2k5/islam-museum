## Overview
In the section on the acts of servants, Abduh treats voluntary acts as one kind of existing thing, perceived by the senses and the mind as other beings are{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.68}. He holds that the distinction between beauty and ugliness applies to acts as it does to things; an act may be made beautiful by its effect, and a beautiful act may be made ugly by what accompanies it{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.69}{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.70}. The book does not give a comprehensive definition of good and evil at this point.
