## The great breath's causes and signs
The marks parting the causes of the whole chest's moving: if it is from much need and the power is strong, the breath is much in its drawing-in and its puffing, the breath's touch is hot and flaming, the pulse great and pointing to heat, and the signs of burning stand in the chest, the face, the eyes and the tongue in its colour and its roughness; if the power is not fallen, the cause is the narrowness; and if all the organs try to move yet do not open the full opening, leaning wholly on the nostrils with no puff at the return, the muscles' moving power is marred. If the narrowness is from a moisture in the windpipe and its neighbours, a rattling joins the signs and the man needs to clear his throat — an extra mark over the mark of the general narrowness; and if the rattling narrowness arises all at once, a matter of the catarrhs has flowed to the lung, or pus and matter has flowed first to it, then to the windpipe, from an organ suddenly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.309}
