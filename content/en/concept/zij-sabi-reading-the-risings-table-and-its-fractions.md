## Summary
Al-Battani teaches how to take the rising of any degree from the table: look for the wanted degrees in the common-number row of the risings table for the given clime or for the right sphere and take what stands opposite; it is the risings between the first of Aries and the degree in the clime, and between the first of Capricorn and the degree in the right sphere. Then the fractions of the degree are added in proportion to the difference of the figures, over a step of one degree or of ten. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.44-45}.

## Searching the common-number row
He says: if you wish to know the risings of any degree by the table, look for the number of degrees whose risings you want, of whichever sign, in the common-number row of the table of the risings of the signs in the given clime or in the risings of the right sphere, whichever you want, and take what stands opposite it among the rising times in the table of the sign to which that number belongs. If you worked with the clime's risings, they are the risings between the first of Aries and that degree; if with the right sphere's, between the first of Capricorn and that degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.44}.

## Minutes, and the step of one degree or of ten
Then he says: if there are minutes with the degrees, find their share of sixty when the step of the number is one degree: take that share of the difference between those risings and the risings that follow by one degree, and add it to the risings found for the whole degrees; the total is the risings of the degree and minute you wanted. If the step is ten degrees, look at the degrees and minutes left over beyond what you find in the table and see how large they are as a part of the ten degrees; take that part of the surplus of the risings in the table, that is, between the risings you found and those ten degrees greater, and add it to the risings under the tens; the result is the risings of that degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.44-45}.
