## Summary
Ibn Sina reports of Galen that he took every night a seasoned dish of lettuce, the lettuce for sleep and the seasoning to make good its cooling, since as an old man he profited from the moistening of sleep; he calls this the gentlest regimen for one whom sleep eludes, adding a bath after digestion and hot water poured over the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.

## Galen's saying
For this reason Galen mentioned that he took every night a seasoned dish of lettuce, the lettuce for its bringing sleep and the seasoning to make good its cooling. He said he was now eager for sleep, meaning that as an old man he was helped by the moistening of sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.

## The gentlest regimen
This is the softest regimen for one whom sleep denies, and if a bath is put before it once the food taken has finished digesting, with much hot water poured on the head, it is an excellent aid. A stronger regimen than this Ibn Sina leaves to the section on treatments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.
