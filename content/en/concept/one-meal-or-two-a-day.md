## Summary
Ibn Sina says the most suitable rhythm for a satisfying meal is one meal on one day and two, morning and evening, on the next; habit must be watched closely, for one used to two meals is weakened by being restricted to one, and one with weak digestion should take two meals and eat less at each {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.230-231}.

## The rule
The most suitable times for a filling meal are to eat once on one day and twice on the next, morning and evening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.230}.

## Heeding habit
Habit must be kept in view very closely, for one used to two meals is weakened and loses strength if held to one; indeed, if his digestion is weak, he should take two meals and reduce the food at each {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.230}.

## The reverse
One used to a single meal who doubles it suffers weakness, sluggishness and slackness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.230-231}.
