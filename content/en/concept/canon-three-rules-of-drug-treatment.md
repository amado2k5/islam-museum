## Summary
Ibn Sina sets treatment by drugs under three rules: the choice of the drug's quality, the choice of its quantity in weight and in degree, and the ordering of its time, and he states that disease is treated by the contrary while health is kept by the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.268}.

## Quality, quantity and time
The first is choosing the drug's quality, hot, cold, moist or dry, found by knowing the kind of disease: once the disease's quality is known, the drug chosen is the one opposed to it in quality. The second is choosing its quantity, divided into reckoning its weight and reckoning its quality, that is, its degree of heat or cold. The third is ordering its time {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.268}.
