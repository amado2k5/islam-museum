## General signs
Mania has general signs, and its kinds have signs; the signs of the whole are that the governing (siyasiyya) and motor acts change in the way stated, and the signs that warn of it are such as nightmare (kabus) with heat of the brain, and the two feet filling with blood and reddening, and a third that is set apart below as a labelled line, which point to movements that spoil the blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102} The first may point to that, and may point to its becoming a cause of the blood's spoiling in an organ with no strong innate heat, so that the blood is not well managed but spoils in it in a kind of spoiling that harms the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102}

## Outlook and the crisis
If the first sign appears at the end of mania it may point to its resolving, as varices do, and mania often comes in acute illnesses as a sign of the crisis (buhran); if the other signs witness to goodness it points to a crisis that will then come, and the strengthening of mania may be a sign of the crisis of mania itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102}

## By the matter
As for the sign of that from burnt black bile, know that its madness and beastliness is with thought and stillness that lasts a time, then when he moves and speaks he begins to act sensibly, thinking, then when it is repeated to him there is no freeing him of it nor silencing him; the leanness of the body in it is greater, the colour leans more to black, the dreams are worse, and he may vomit something sour that makes the ground boil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102} As for that from bilious black bile, the rising to evil is quicker and the subsiding from it quicker, he does not recall evil and rancour as the first recalls, his stillness is less, and his movement, weariness and agitation are more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102}

## Woman's breast (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Among the signs that warn of mania Ibn Sina names the blood congealing in a woman's breast {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.102}
