## Overview

A pregnant woman (hamil) is a woman carrying a child. The rules cover her blood, fasting, marriage, divorce, waiting period, maintenance, penalties and injury to the foetus. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6729}

## Blood and fasting

Blood is normally not discharged during pregnancy. Blood seen during pregnancy counts as irregular bleeding in the Hanafi and Hanbali views; the blood between two births is disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6730}

A pregnant woman may break her fast if she fears harm to herself or the child, and must make up the days; whether a ransom (fidya) is due is disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6730}

## Marriage and divorce

The Shafiis and Hanafis allow a pregnant woman of unlawful union to marry; the Hanbalis require repentance, and the Malikis forbid marriage before birth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6731} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6732}

A divorce of a pregnant woman is valid, revocable or final. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6732}

## Waiting period and maintenance

The waiting period of a pregnant woman ends with the birth, in the view of most jurists. A pregnant divorcee is maintained until she gives birth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6732} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6733}

## Penalties and injury

Penalties are not carried out on a pregnant woman until she gives birth. A blow that causes a stillborn foetus requires a ghurra, the value of a slave or a female slave. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6736} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6737}

## Death and burial

If a pregnant woman dies with a living foetus, the body may be opened to save the child, according to the Hanafi and Shafii views. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6738}

## Animals

A foetus is slaughtered by its mother's slaughter according to most jurists. A pregnant animal is not taken as zakat, and a foetus cannot be included in a sale as a separate item. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6740}
