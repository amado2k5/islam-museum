## Summary
Ihdad in the lexicon is giving up adornment to show grief; as a term it is a woman's abstention from adornment and similar things for a set period and in set circumstances, and from sleeping away from her own house. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1753} It is obligatory for a widow in the period of waiting after a valid marriage, by agreement, on the saying that a woman who believes in God and the last day may not mourn a dead man for more than three nights, except for a husband, four months and ten days. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1753} The mourning woman avoids what is adornment by law or custom, including scent, kohl and dyed clothes; the report names safflower and saffron dyes, which carry scent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1756} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1757} She may go out for her needs; if she is away from her marital home she must return to it, and travel for pilgrimage or a long journey is treated in detail. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1759} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1761} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1762}
