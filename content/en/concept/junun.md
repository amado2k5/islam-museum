## Overview

In law, insanity is a disorder of the mind that stops acts and words from having their proper effect. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6558}

## Effect on worship and transactions

The madman is not accountable, so his spoken transactions such as gifts, bequests and endowments are not valid, and he is not bound by bodily worship such as prayer. The majority also hold that no jizya is due from him. Zakat is due on his property, and his guardian pays it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6564} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6566} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6567}

## Retaliation and punishment

No retaliation is due from a madman who kills; the liability falls on his property, and the intentional act of a child or a madman counts as accidental for blood-money. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6561} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6574}
