## Summary
In chapter 2 al-Battani explains that each part of the circle is also called a degree, each degree divides into sixty parts called minutes, each minute into sixty called seconds, each second into sixty thirds, and what follows goes on the same plan to tenths and beyond. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.

## The ladder of orders
Al-Battani calls the fractional ranks genera. The degree is the base, and below it come minutes, seconds, thirds, fourths, fifths and sixths down to tenths. Each genus divides by sixty to give the one next to it, the sexagesimal division al-Battani attributes to the ancients. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.

## Part and degree
He says the single division of the 360 is called a part and also a degree, so part and degree are two names for one thing in this chapter, and the zij uses both words interchangeably, each with its sexagesimal fractions. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.
