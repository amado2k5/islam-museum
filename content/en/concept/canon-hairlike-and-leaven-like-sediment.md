## Summary
Ibn Sina says hairlike sediment is set in the kidney from a lengthened moisture worked on by heat, and that sediment like bits of soaked leaven shows weakness of stomach and bowels and poor digestion, perhaps from taking milk and cheese. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Hairlike
Hairlike sediment is from a lengthened moisture set by heat acting on it; it may be white or red and is set in the kidney, and it is said it can be spans long. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Like bits of leaven
Sediment like bits of soaked leaven shows weakness of stomach and bowels and poor digestion in them, and may be caused by taking milk and cheese. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.
