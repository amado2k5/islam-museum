## Definition
Umm al-dimagh in the lexical sense is the skull, or the thin skin that encloses the brain. For the jurists it is the membrane under the bone above the brain, called umm al-ra's (mother of the head) and khurita al-dimagh (the sac of the brain). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3401}

## Ruling
A wound that reaches the membrane without piercing it is called amma and ma'muma, carries a third of the diya, and has no retaliation, following the hadith of Abbas that there is no retaliation for a ma'muma, a jaifa or a munqila. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3401}
A wound that pierces it is called damigha; the jurists differ: some hold it carries the same as an amma, some add a fixed compensation, and some hold it carries the full diya for a life. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3402}
On the fast, some jurists hold that anything reaching the membrane breaks it, and others only if it reaches the brain itself. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3402}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3403}
