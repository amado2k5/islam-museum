## Overview
The Darafsh-i Kaviyan was a banner of leather raised by the followers of Afridun in their war against al-Dahhak. This is how the Persians name it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.439-440}

## Meaning
Al-Masudi notes that *darafsh*, in Pahlavi Persian, which is his first source, means banner, lance, and standard. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.439-440}
