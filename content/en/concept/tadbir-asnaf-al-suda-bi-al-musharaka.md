## The rule
In all kinds of headache by sharing of organs, care must be taken of those organs, they are evacuated by what is particular to them and their temperament changed, and in addition the head is strengthened by strengtheners so that it does not receive {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.70} When you have finished with the organ, consider whether the symptom has become a disease in itself and whether the cause of the headache has become settled in the head, learn the matter and the quality, and do what you have learned {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.70}

## The leg, vapours and the liver
The one by sharing with the leg, whose owner feels as if something rises from his legs, is treated according to the presence or absence of fullness, and the book names other measures that are not reproduced here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.70} The kind that is because of vapours rising from the organs of the body is helped by what prevents the vapours rising, and likewise the one by sharing with the liver, for which promoting urine is especially of use {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.70}

## The stomach
As for the kind by sharing with the stomach, what is because of its weakness, especially the weakness of its mouth, so that it receives matters and the chymes spoil in it, mostly stirs in emptiness, and its treatment is to strengthen the mouth of the stomach by what has astringency and a pleasant smell {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.70-71} What is because of humours in it, the first thing to hasten to is cleansing, and after that and with it to feed on fine, praised foods, light in digestion, giving a good chyme, and then to incline the quality toward what is due {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.71} If the humours are cold and strong winds are stirred from them, then stronger emetics and thinning means, and if these do not remove it a stronger evacuation; the book also names cuttings of arteries and strong cautery, which are not reproduced here; and wine is to be avoided in every case {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.71}

## Womb (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina says that for the headache by sharing with the kidney, the hypochondrium, the womb and the like, what he put at the start of the chapter suffices for its management {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.71}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.70-71}
