## Summary
Ibn Sina notes that for all its many benefits the bath has harms: it makes it easy for superfluities to flow to the weak organs, slackens the body, harms the nerves, dissolves the innate heat and removes the appetite for food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.141}.

## The harms
The bath eases the pouring of superfluities to the organs that are weak, relaxes the body and harms the nerve, dissolves the innate heat and removes the desire for food. He then adds that the bath also has excesses on the side of the waters it contains, which he takes up in the same chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.141-142}.
