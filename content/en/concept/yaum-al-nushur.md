## Summary
The Day of Spreading is mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the day people are spread from their graves and gathered for reckoning — a sign of the Hour, when God spreads them as He spreads those on the earth, a day of indescribable terror. It includes events beyond imagination: the blowing of the Trumpet, the emergence of people from their graves, their gathering to the Gathering Place, and their presentation for reckoning.
