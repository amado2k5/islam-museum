## Temperament
Cold in the second degree and dry in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.590}.

## Actions and properties
It stops blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.590}.

## Adornment
By its special property it stops the spreading of hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.590}.

## Substitutes
Its substitute is filzahraj and the roots of the reed; the proportions the book gives are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.590}.
