## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that when the raw humours are not in the vessels but in the principal organs, they are rubbed with relaxing viscous oils and given heating drinks that reach the skin, then bathed and given pennyroyal before food and exercise, and he names chamomile, dill and marjoram oils for anointing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.248-249}.

## In the principal organs
If you are sure that the raw humours are not in the vessels but in the principal organs, rub them, especially in the mornings, with relaxing viscous oils, give them heating drinks whose warmth reaches the skin, keep them at rest for long, then bathe them in water of moderate heat and give them pennyroyal without fear, though it must be before food and before exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.248}.

## Anointing with oils
Anointing with oil of chamomile, dill, marjoram and the like is useful to them, alone or with wax, or strengthened with fennel in twelve times its weight of oil. And if you learn that the humours are in the vessels and outside together, you aim at the greater and do not neglect the lesser {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.248-249}.
