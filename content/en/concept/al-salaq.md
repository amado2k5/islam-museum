## Its definition
Salaq — anyusima in the book's Greek — is a thickening in the lids from a thick, bad, eating, boracic matter; the lids redden from it, the lashes fall out and it leads to ulceration of the lid margins, eye corruption following; some of it is old and bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.193}

## The treatment
The recent benefits from softening cooling poultices and the bath is entered after them, and constant bathing is among its most beneficial treatments; the old chronic requires cleansing and evacuation with the bathing kept up; the local drugs are of the astringent polishing kind, and the one occurring after ophthalmia has a special collyrium ⁦(cupping and venesection sites, poultice and drug names and recipes are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.193}
