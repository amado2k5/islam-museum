## Its definition and treatment

Tahajjur is a small tumour that bleeds and turns stony; the hand's work may clear it, and then the desiccant medicines are used ⁦(a short chapter in the book; the medicine names are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.197}
