## Actions and properties
The ash of the frog, when placed on the place of blood, stops it; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Cosmetics
It is, as is said, an antidote (badzahr) for leprosy and for all vermin when eaten; its preparation is not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Swellings and pustules
Its broth is beneficial for swellings of the tendons when poured on them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Organs of the head
It was said that river frogs are used by rinsing with their boiled water for toothache, and I think it is of the tree-and-garden kind, for this is the kind that physicians attest to, and experienced laypeople say that they make the teeth of animals fall out when they get it in fodder and pasture; a saying attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Poisons
One who ate its blood or body, his body swelled and his colour darkened until he died; and it was said that when prepared and eaten it is an antidote (badzahr) for leprosy and vermin; a report attributed to the book, without any method {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
In the place on poisons the book says that one who ate its blood or body ejaculated semen until he died; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}
