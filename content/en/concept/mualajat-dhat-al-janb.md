## The treatments of pleurisy
And Hippocrates directs, in treating the pleurisy in which the pain is felt only when strongly inclined to the hypochondria, that he be evacuated with a drug of this kind (a direction attributed to Hippocrates, the name excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.359}
