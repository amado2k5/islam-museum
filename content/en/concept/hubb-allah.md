## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy says that love for God reaches the reality of faith when the heart holds God dearer than wealth and honour. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:25:00} He names the sources of love as pondering one's speech, eating lawful food and understanding God's favours, and its signs as doing good for God's sake, visiting a sick neighbour and the heart shivering at the Qur'an. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:59:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:06:00}

## The reality of faith: God before wealth and honour
The Sheikh says that love does not reach the reality of faith until the heart holds it dearer than wealth, and dearer than honour (needs_source: one word in the middle of this sentence is garbled in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:25:00}

## Sources of love
The Sheikh says that the first source of love is pondering one's speech, then seeking out lawful food, then understanding God's favours. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:59:30}

## Signs of love: doing good for God's sake
The Sheikh says that one sign of love is doing good to people for God's sake, not expecting their thanks, and not letting their return of harm stop one from doing good for God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:03:30}

## Signs of love: a sick neighbour, funerals and congratulations
The Sheikh lists among the signs of love visiting a sick neighbour, following a funeral, congratulating a Muslim and praying for him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:05:30}

## Sign of love: the heart shivers at the Qur'an
The Sheikh says that one sign of love is that the heart shivers when the Qur'an is recited. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:06:00}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
**Loving the meeting with God.** Episode 23 of [[work/fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah|Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi]] explains the hadith that matches a servant's love of meeting his Lord with God's love of meeting him. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:00:00} [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Al-Sha'rawi]] says that whoever hears something from the Prophet should not hide it, since faith is completed by loving for one's brother what one loves for oneself, and that conveying a ruling benefits the one who conveys it, because others' keeping to it protects him. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:20:30} He holds that for the one who loves meeting God, death is not grievous. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:23:30} The presenter cites al-Khattabi's explanation of loving the meeting with God as preferring the next life to this one, and relates the story of Ibrahim and the angel of death. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep23 00:25:30} (needs_source: the Ibrahim story has no chain in the programme.)

**Those who love one another for God.** In episode 28 al-Sha'rawi says that people love one another for interests, and interests differ; only God gathers all interests, so love in Him unites people around one source whose commands do not conflict. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:21:30} He defines love as the self reaching out to one in whom it finds what fulfils its desires, and divides it into emotional love without rational check, such as loving one's son even if he is dull, and rational love, such as loving bitter medicine. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:25:00} He relates the story of Umar saying the Prophet was dearer to him than everything except himself, and then saying that he was dearer to him than himself too. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:25:30} He holds that Umar's first answer showed honesty of faith, not hypocrisy, and that rational faith is the obligation, which may be refined into emotional faith. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:27:30} The presenter closes with [[passage/quran-3-31|Al 'Imran 31]], on love for God being shown by following the Prophet. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep28 00:32:30}

## In Abla al-Kahlawi's programme 'In the Love of al-Mustafa'
[[person/abla-al-kahlawi|Abla al-Kahlawi]] takes up love in the first three episodes of her programme [[work/fi-hub-al-mustapha|'In the Love of al-Mustafa']]. Love, she says, is pure and clean only when it comes from its true source, which is God: whoever loves his children, his wealth and all that God has given him for God's sake and through Him knows that what he loses was only on loan and will be taken back, and so is patient, content and tastes the sweetness of patience. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep02 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep02 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep02 00:09:30} Love is a gift God has bestowed on His servants to join their hearts; it is a love of souls that is not bound to a time, which is why later believers pray for those who preceded them in faith. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:02:30} She recalls the hadith of the three qualities by which a person tastes the sweetness of faith, among them that God and His Messenger be dearer to him than all else and that he love a person only for God's sake. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:05:00}

She tells how Abu Idris al-Khawlani saw in Syria a young man with a radiant face who was mostly silent but spoke abundantly when asked; he admired him and told him he loved him for God's sake, and the young man rejoiced and recalled the hadith qudsi that God's love is due to those who love one another for His sake. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:06:00} She recalls the hadith that when God loves a servant He calls Gabriel to love him, the people of heaven love him and acceptance is then laid down for him on earth, and the hadith of servants who are neither prophets nor martyrs yet are envied by prophets and martyrs because they loved one another by God's light without kinship or wealth between them, and who will be on pulpits of light. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:08:00} She ends with a call to reconciliation: that a brother go to his brother and apologise, that a husband make peace with his wife, and that everyone love his work, his country, his land and his tools, for God and in God. {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-abla-al-kahlawi fi-hub-al-mustapha/ep03 00:09:00}
