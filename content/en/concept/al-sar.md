## Definition
Epilepsy is an illness that bars the psychic organs from the acts of sensation, motion and standing upright, though not completely; this is because of an obstruction that occurs, and most of it is a general convulsion arising from a lesion that prevents the power of sense and motion from passing fully into the nerve and the organs, without cutting them off entirely, and it prevents the ability to stand, so the person cannot remain upright with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.118}

## Why there is convulsion in it
Every convulsion is either from fullness, or from dryness, or from contraction at something harmful, and so is epilepsy; epilepsy comes suddenly, and dry convulsion does not come suddenly, because the brain does not reach by dryness the point of convulsing for it or of disabling the body first; so its cause remains either a contraction of the brain to expel something harmful, or a vapour of bad substance, or an incompleteness that makes an obstruction in a ventricle of the brain or at the roots from which the nerves spring {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.118}
