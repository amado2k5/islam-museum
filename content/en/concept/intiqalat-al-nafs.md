## The passages between the breath-kinds
This rule sets largeness, swiftness and frequency on one ladder, each climbing into the next by the need and the bar, and descending as those descend; the unevenness, the slowness and the smallness are the rungs of the descent from the natural. The lung-stirring breath is a short chapter attached to it, pointing to the power's flagging, a severe choking, or a gathered pus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.311}
