## Nature and selection
The fruit of a tree, which is eaten at times despite its excessive astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.566}.

## Actions and properties
It has astringency and drying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.566}.

## Wounds and ulcers
The pungency of its rind with water on ulcerated scab; in sum its astringency is so great that its fruit heals thick wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.566}.

## Poisons and bites
The juice of murran with wine, drunk or bound on, helps the bite of the viper; and it is said that the sawdust of its wood kills if drunk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.
