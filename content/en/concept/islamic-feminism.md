## Overview
Islamic feminism is a modern discourse reading the sharia's texts egalitarianly on women's questions; Badran traces the term's emergence in twentieth-century Egypt {cite:badran-feminism-2009}.

## Perspectives
- **Sunni frame:** women's rulings are built on text and established scholarship within the inherited fiqh {cite:badran-feminism-2009}.
- **Academic:** a convergence of feminist and Islamic discourse that formed in the twentieth century before the label consolidated {cite:badran-feminism-2009}.
