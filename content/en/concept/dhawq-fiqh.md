## Summary
Dhawq is perceiving the taste of a thing through the moisture spread on the nerve over the tongue; it is one of the five senses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8501}

## Fasting
Tasting food does not break the fast unless it reaches the body inside, though it is better avoided. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8501}

## Injury to the taste
A full diya is due for injury that takes away taste, by agreement; the schools differ on retaliation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8501}

## Oaths about tasting
One who swears not to taste food or drink and then eats or drinks breaks the oath; one who swears not to eat or drink and then tastes does not, because all eating is tasting but not every tasting is eating. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8501}
