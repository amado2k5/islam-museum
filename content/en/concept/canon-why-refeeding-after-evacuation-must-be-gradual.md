## Summary
Ibn Sina warns against feeding those who have been evacuated their full need at once, because undigested food is drawn into the vessels for three reasons: the stomach is stingy with a little and its retaining power contends with the liver's drawing power, much food is not digested well in the stomach, and much sent to the vessels is beyond their power to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.250}.

## The three reasons
Beware of feeding them, after these evacuations, the full measure of their need at once, for the undigested food is then drawn into the vessels for three reasons. First, when food is little the stomach is stingy with it and its retaining power contends with the liver's drawing power, whereas when it is much it does not stint but may even help the liver's drawing with its expelling power, and so with every earlier vessel in relation to the one after it. Second, much food is not well digested in the stomach. Third, much sends a great deal of nourishment into the vessels, which are also unable to digest it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.250}.
