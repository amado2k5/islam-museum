## Summary
Dharq is a bird's droppings. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8418}
For birds whose flesh is eaten, such as pigeons and sparrows, the majority hold the droppings pure; the Shafi'is hold them impure. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8419}
For birds whose flesh is not eaten, such as the falcon and the crow, the droppings are impure by the majority, and this is the sounder Hanafi view. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8420}

## Excused amounts
Most jurists excuse a small amount of it because it is hard to avoid, and what counts as small is fixed by custom. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8420}
The Hanafis excuse less than a quarter of a garment for the lighter kind; Muhammad among them treats it as a heavy impurity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8421}
