## Summary
Ibn Sina explains that milk benefits those of the old who digest it and feel no stretching, itching or pain after it, since it nourishes and moistens; goat's and ass's milk suit best, ass's milk having the property of not curdling much and passing down quickly, provided the animals' pasture is watched {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.251-252}.

## For whom it is good
As for milk, those of the old benefit from it who digest it and find no stretching in the region of the liver or abdomen after it, no itching and no pain, for milk nourishes and moistens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.251-252}.

## Goat's and ass's milk
The most suitable is goat's and ass's milk. Ass's milk has the special property of not curdling much and of passing down quickly, especially with salt and honey. The pasture must be watched so that its plants are not astringent, pungent, sour or very salty {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.252}.
