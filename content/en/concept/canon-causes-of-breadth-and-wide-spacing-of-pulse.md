## Summary
Ibn Sina gives the causes of breadth, frequency and wide spacing in the pulse, alongside the causes of length {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.175}.

## Breadth, frequency and wide spacing
Breadth is produced either by emptiness of the vessels, so that the upper layer leans on the lower and widens, or by great softness of the instrument. Frequency is caused by weakness or by much need for heat. Wide spacing is caused by a power that has reached the need through greatness, or by severe cold that lessened the need. A garbled phrase follows in the scan and has been left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.175}.

## Length
Length is produced in truth by the causes of greatness when something prevents breadth and rise, such as hardness of the instrument preventing breadth, and by accident leanness may help it. A line in this place is unreadable and is not cited {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.175}.
