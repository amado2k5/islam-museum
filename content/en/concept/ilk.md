## Nature
We have spoken of the gum of the Anbat, the resin and the like in its place {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Temperament
The gum of the Anbat is hot, then the gum of the cypress, then the resin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Actions and properties
It is dissolving, and the resin and the gum of the cypress are not stronger in dissolving than the gum of the Anbat even though warmer than it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}
