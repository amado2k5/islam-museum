## Temperament
Hot and dry in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Actions and properties
It has much cleansing, softening and purifying, though it does not reach the bitter vetch, and it dissolves winds and phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Swellings and pustules
With strong vinegar on the start of a hot swelling; wetted with wine it is a poultice for hot swellings of the breast, and it disperses swellings of phlegm and wind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Wounds and ulcers
With strong vinegar on the ulceration of scab, applied as a warm poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Chest and breathing
It softens the chest by its cleansing, especially a gruel of its water with sugar and almond oil, and wetted with wine it helps swellings of the breast {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Excretory organs
It stirs the bowels to push out what is in them, and its gruel, when sipped, loosens the belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.

## Poisons and bites
As a poultice it helps the sting of the scorpion and the viper {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.578}.
