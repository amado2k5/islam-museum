## Nature and selection
Known. The oil made from its peel is strong; the oil made from its flower is weaker in every respect {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.367}. A footnote describes a tall tree with a fruit like large lemons, made of four parts: peel, flesh, juice and seed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.367}.

## Temperament and actions
The peel is hot in the first degree and dry at the end of the second. The flesh is hot and moist in the first; some say it is cold and moist in the first. The juice is cold and dry in the third, and the seed is hot in the first and drying in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.367}.

## Uses by organ
Properties: its flesh is flatulent; its leaves calm flatulence; its flower is subtler. Its juice is astringent and breaks bile; its seed and peel resolve; if its peel is put among clothes it prevents moth, and its smell corrects bad air and plague {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.367}. Ornament: its juice brightens the complexion and removes freckles; its ash-like burnt peel is a good salve for leprosy; its decoction sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Swellings and pustules: its juice as a salve helps herpes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Joints: its oil helps slackness of the nerves and paralysis, and its juice is bad for the nerves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Head: it helps facial palsy, and a decoction of the citron sweetens the breath greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Eyes: its juice used as kohl removes jaundice of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Chest: its juice calms hot palpitation; its preserve is good for throat and lung, but its juice is bad for the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Stomach: its flesh is bad for the stomach and flatulent and slow to digest, and should be eaten with preserve; its leaves strengthen the stomach and the bowels; its flower and peel aid digestion when added to food; its sour preserve tans the stomach, and its juice helps jaundice and calms bilious vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Excretory organs: its flesh causes colic; its juice binds the bowel and helps bilious diarrhoea; its seed helps piles; the juice of its sour part calms women's lust {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. Poisons: its seed, two drachms with wine, hot water or a salve, counters all poisons, especially scorpion venom, and the peel is similar; the seed drunk helps snake bites, and the peel is used as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}.
