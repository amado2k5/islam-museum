## Its definition
It is an instrumental disorder arising for the motive power's inability to keep the muscles moving continuously against the obstructing resistance mingled with its motion for the will's motion, so voluntary motions mix with involuntary motions, or voluntary rest with involuntary movings; it is a lesion in the motive power as numbness is a lesion in the sensitive power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.157}

## In power and instrument
This cause is either in the power, or in the instrument, or in both together {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.157}

## Its seats
Tremor may be in all the organs, may be in the hands, may be in the head alone, by the lesion's reaching muscles rather than the cord's muscles — rather in the nerve branches penetrating to the hands; or because the cause is in the cord's origin but shakes it off to the nearest places and sides, nature guarding the cord lest that cause pass into it to its utmost; or because the motive spirit in the lower body is stronger and harder for those organs' need, so the lower organs are strongly affected by causes that are not very strong, while the instrument, if affected, can overpower them — and the hand is not so; the prevalent cause producing the second tremor is a cold pleasing nerve and spirit together, or a moisture with a relaxing instrument short of the moisture that makes palsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.157} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.158}

## The hardest and the lasting
Know that the hardest tremor is what begins from the left, and tremor in the aged is not removed by treatment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.158}
