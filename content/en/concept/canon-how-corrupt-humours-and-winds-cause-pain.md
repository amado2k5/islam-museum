## Summary
In chapters 25 and 26 Ibn Sina says corrupt humours hurt by their quality, as they irritate, by their quantity, as they stretch, or by both, and that wind hurts by stretching, whether in the cavities of organs, in their layers, or around muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.

## Corrupt humours
Corrupt humours hurt either by their quality, as they irritate, or by their quantity, as they stretch, or by both together {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.

## Winds
Wind hurts by stretching. The stretching wind may be in the cavities and bellies of organs, like bloating in the stomach; or in the layers and fibre of organs, as in windy colic; or in the layers of muscles, or beneath membranes and above bones, or around the muscle between it and the flesh and skin, or lining an organ as the chest muscles are lined {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.
