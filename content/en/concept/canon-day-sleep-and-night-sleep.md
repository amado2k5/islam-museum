## Summary
Ibn Sina holds day sleep poor, since it brings moist diseases and catarrhs, spoils the colour, brings spleen trouble, slackens the nerve, makes sluggish, weakens appetite and brings tumours, and prefers night sleep as complete, continuous and deep, while one used to day sleep is not to give it up all at once {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.

## Day sleep
Day sleep is poor: it brings moist diseases and catarrhs, spoils the colour, brings trouble of the spleen, slackens the nerve, makes sluggish, weakens appetite and brings tumours. Among the causes of its ills is that it breaks off quickly and nature is dulled in what it was doing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.

## Night sleep
Among the merits of night sleep is that it is complete, continuous and deep; yet one used to sleeping by day should not give it up at once, without gradual steps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.
