## Overview
The Prophetic biography is the transmitted history of the life of the [[person/muhammad|Prophet]]: his birth, his upbringing, his call in Mecca, his migration, his campaigns, and his death, together with what relates to the state of his companions and his family. {cite:ibn-hisham-sira} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## The origin of the discipline
It arose out of the campaigns and the reports transmitted with chains of narration in the works of Ibn Ishaq and his students. It later became a discipline in its own right, recording a history of twenty-three years.

## Its relation to hadith
Biography and hadith go together in the transmission of the Prophetic period. See [[concept/al-hadith-al-nabawi|the Prophetic hadith]]. Further detail needs a further source.

## The Prophet's life in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episodes 4, 8, 9 and 10)
The Sheikh describes the Prophet entering a narrow cave in the Meccan desert, and says the Islamic state began from that cave two days after leaving for Yathrib (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:30:30}
He describes the Battle of the Trench, when the confederate Arabs came against Madina with hypocrites inside it, and says the hearts reached the throats (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:10:00}
He says the Banu Hashim guarded the Prophet until a verse promising protection from people was revealed (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:29:30} He says the hypocrites came late to prayer, and the Prophet told the people not to precede him in bowing and prostration (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:30:30}
He holds that the Prophet was a human like other humans, and that his miracle lies in that (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:29:00} He says Abu Lahab is named in the Qur'an while he was alive, and that he could easily have stopped Islam (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:33:30}
He says al-Walid ibn al-Mughira was among the early Muslims by rank, that Khalid became Muslim later, and that Quraysh would hand over its leaders (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:54:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:55:00}
