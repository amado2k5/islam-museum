## Nature
Of garlic there is the known garden kind, the karrai garlic and the wild garlic; in the wild there is bitterness and astringency and it is the one called snake garlic, and the karrai is compounded in power of garlic and leek {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.695-696}

## Temperament
Heating and drying from the third degree to the fourth, the wild more than that {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Actions and properties
It softens, dissolves wind greatly, ulcerates the skin and benefits a change of waters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Cosmetics
Taken with a decoction of mountain mint and rubbed on, it kills lice and nits; its ash applied with honey benefits bahaq and the bruised look of the eye, and benefits alopecia that comes from putrid matter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Swellings and pustules
It opens internal abscesses, and its ash is put on pustules {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Wounds and ulcers
It ulcerates the skin, its ash with honey is put on ringworm and ulcerating mange, and the wild garlic glues malignant wounds when laid fresh on them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Joints
Used as an enema it benefits sciatica because it loosens blood and bilious humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Organs of the head
Garlic gives headache, its decoction and roasted form calm toothache, and rinsing with its decoction also benefits toothache, especially when frankincense is mixed with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Ocular organs
It weakens sight and brings pustules to the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Organs of the chest
Cooked, it clears the throat, benefits chronic cough, chest pains and cold, and brings leeches out of the throat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits ascites, especially the dish that the book attributes to the Christians, of garlic, olives and carrots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Excretory organs
With honey water it brings out phlegm, it expels worms, it loosens the bowels and promotes urine; the amount is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that sitting in a decoction of garlic leaf and stem brings on menses and expels the afterbirth, likewise when carried as a pessary or drunk, and likewise the dish of the Christians made from it; of its action on desire it says that by its strong drying and dissolving it may harm, but if cooked in water until its sharpness is dissolved it is not unlikely that the matter of semen is generated from it, and that phlegmatic matters in phlegmatic temperaments are made into winds, and when these dissolve in the vessels as winds it is not unlikely that it alters the desire; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Poisons and bites
It benefits against stings of vermin and bites of snakes when given with a drink, the book saying that it tried this, and likewise against the bite of a rabid dog {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}
