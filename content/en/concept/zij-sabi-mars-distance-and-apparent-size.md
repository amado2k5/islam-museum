## Mars
The text states that Mars's apparent size varies between its farthest and nearest positions, and that its nearest size is seven times its farthest size. Its farthest distance is given as eight thousand and twenty-two times the Earth's radius and its middle distance as about four thousand, five hundred and eighty-four. Its apparent diameter is about two degrees and a minute, and its mean diameter is given as one twentieth of the Sun's. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.187-188}

## The figures to be confirmed
The middle distance of Mars and its apparent diameter need checking against the Latin text, as they were read from a disturbed scan. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.187-188}
