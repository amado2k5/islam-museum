## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy sets contentment (qana'a) against greed. He holds that people are by nature inclined to greed and do not settle for little, and that acceptance of what God has apportioned, and leaving off wishing for what others have, are among the sources of happiness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:15:30}.

## Greed in human nature
The speaker says that a person has greed and evil in his nature, and does not content himself with little {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:15:30}.
He gives a story which he takes as evidence that a person is greedy and does not accept little, and that when he persists in sin, his soul is given over to it (needs_source: the rest of the story is unclear in the transcript) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:16:00}.

## The story of Isa and the bread
The speaker tells a story in which Isa is with a man, food is shared out, and there are portions of butter, and he presents it as an illustration of greed (needs_source: the details are garbled and are not given verbatim) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:19:00}.

## Wishing and contentment with God's bounty
The speaker says that none of us should wish for what another has, but should ask God for His bounty; men should not wish for what women have, nor women for what men have {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:21:00}.
He gives examples of such wishing, such as the wish to sit on a board of directors or in parliament (needs_source: the transcript is partly garbled) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:21:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh marvels at those who take from the world both lawful and unlawful, says he does not condemn wealth, and holds that the grateful rich man is better with God than the patient poor man; but he warns against being like one who drinks sea water and only grows thirstier: a million, then another, then a billion, and nothing fills the son of Adam's belly but dust; he quotes: whoever wants riches, contentment suffices him, and whoever wants a preacher, death suffices him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep27 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep27 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep27 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep27 00:10:00}
