## Summary
Ibn Sina fixes the measure of exercise by three things: the colour while it keeps improving, the movements while they are light, and the state of the organs and their swelling while it keeps growing; when these begin to fall off and the steamy sweat turns into a running ooze, exercise should stop {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.224-225}.

## The three signs
Three things are watched in the measure of exercise: first the colour, which while it improves means it is still early; second the movements, which while light mean it is still early; third the state of the organs and their swelling, which while it grows means it is still early {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.224}.

## When to stop
When these states begin to fall off and the steamy sweat becomes a running ooze, exercise should be stopped {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.224-225}.
