## Summary
Khums is lexically a fifth; khammasa is to take a fifth; technically it is the fifth of spoil or booty, and takhmis is setting aside the fifth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7856} Related terms: mirba' (the quarter taken by the chief in pre-Islamic raids), safi (what the chief chose for himself before division, including what the Prophet chose, such as a sword, a horse or a slave-girl), nashita (what a chief took before division) and fudul (remnants too small to divide among an army). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7856}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7857} Ruling: setting aside a fifth of spoil is obligatory by agreement, on the Quranic verse of the spoil; setting aside a fifth of fay' is disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7857}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7858} Property subject to the fifth: spoil taken from unbelievers by force; spoil was lawful to the Prophet\'s community, and four fifths went to the warriors and the fifth to its beneficiaries. If the spoil is land conquered by force the division is disputed; movable spoil is divided after the salab and expenses are deducted. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7858} The Shafiis and Hanbalis give five shares: one for God and His Messenger (for horses, arms, the public good and the stipends of judges and scholars); one for the kin of Hashim and al-Muttalib (by descent, male and female, rich and poor by the majority); one for orphans (by the preferred view poor Muslims); one for the needy; one for the traveller; the beneficiaries must be generally given when enough is available. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7859}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7860}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7861} The Hanafis divide the fifth into three shares for orphans, the needy and the traveller, holding that God\'s and the Messenger\'s share lapsed at the Prophet\'s death; the Malikis leave it to the Imam; some divide it into six; Abu al-Aliya gives God\'s share to the Kaaba. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7861}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7862}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7863}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7864} Fay\' is not divided into a fifth by the Hanafis and Malikis; the Shafiis and Khiraqi divide it, the four fifths going to the frontier soldiers. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7865} Spoil: if the slayer takes it, the majority do not divide it; al-Awza'i, Makhul and Ibn Abbas held that it is divided; the Hanafis divide it unless the Imam granted it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7866} Buried treasure: the fifth is due, on the report of Abu Hurayra; its details are under zakat, buried treasure, mine and hoard. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7866}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7867}
