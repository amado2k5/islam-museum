## Summary
Khums is lexically a fifth; khammasa is to take a fifth; technically it is the fifth of spoil or booty, and takhmis is setting aside the fifth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7856} Related terms: mirba' (the quarter taken by the chief in pre-Islamic raids), safi (what the chief chose for himself before division, including what the Prophet chose, such as a sword, a horse or a slave-girl), nashita (what a chief took before division) and fudul (remnants too small to divide among an army). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7856}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7857} Ruling: setting aside a fifth of spoil is obligatory by agreement, on the Quranic verse of the spoil; setting aside a fifth of fay' is disputed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7857}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7858} Property subject to the fifth: spoil taken from unbelievers by force; spoil was lawful to the Prophet\'s community, and four fifths went to the warriors and the fifth to its beneficiaries. If the spoil is land conquered by force the division is disputed; movable spoil is divided after the salab and expenses are deducted. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7858}
