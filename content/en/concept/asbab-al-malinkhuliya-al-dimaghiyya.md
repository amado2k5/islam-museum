## Without matter
It is either from a cold, dry bad temperament without any matter, which carries the substance of the brain and the temperament of the bright pneuma over to darkness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}

## With matter
Or it is with a matter: the matter is in the vessels, having come to them from another place, or turning to blackness within them by the burning or the turbidity of what they hold, which is the most frequent; or the matter is soaked into the body of the brain; or it harms the brain by its quality and substance and pours into its cavities (butun) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}

## Passing over from epilepsy
It is often a passing over from epilepsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}
