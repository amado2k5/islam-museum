## The sign of sharing
Headache by sharing arises, ceases, grows strong and grows weak according to what befalls the sharing organ of pain; and were it not by sharing, there would be in the other acts of the brain such things as darkness in the eye, coma and constant heaviness, while the state of the other organs is sound {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52} When the affliction is in the membranes of the brain themselves and is strong, the passing of the pain to the roots of the eyes shows it; if the affliction is in the outer membrane or elsewhere, the pain does not pass to the roots of the eyes, and touching the skin of the head hurts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52}

## Stomach and brain: which came first
The one by sharing with the stomach is shown by distress and nausea, or little appetite or none, or bad digestion, or little digestion or none, after the earlier sign is found {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52} Headache may be because of something in the brain that brought about these states and afflictions in the stomach, by the stomach sharing with the brain, and not by beginning in the stomach with the brain sharing; so one must be sure in such a case and learn the state of each of the two organs in itself, and so judge which came first from which came after {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52} What shows it in the stomach especially is the difference of state in digestion and not in digestion, and in emptiness and fullness; for pain of the stomach, if from bile, is stirred in emptiness, and if from a cold humour it is less in emptiness and hunger quiets it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52} What comes out by vomiting may show it, and so does the difference of state with the difference of what enters the stomach; so when the headache stirs and they eat something and it quiets, that is a sign that it is by sharing with the stomach, and so too if it quiets when they cast up bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52}

## The side of the pain points to the sharing organ
It is inferred from the side of the pain: what is by sharing with the stomach mostly begins in the front part of the fontanelle, and may lean toward the middle of the fontanelle and then descend; what comes from the liver leans toward the right side; what comes from the spleen leans toward the left side; what comes because of the hypochondrium leans very far forward; and what comes from the kidney and the organs of the back leans very far back {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52} What comes by sharing with pains arising in other organs comes with their stirring and intensifying; and what comes with fevers and crises comes with them and quiets and weakens with their quieting and weakening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.52-53} The whitening of the urine with severe fever may show it, because the bilious humours incline upward; and fine-making things are often a cause of headache by opening the way of vapours to the brain even if they are not hot, and the same holds for the shaqiqa {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53}

## Womb and menses (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina says that the headache that comes because of the womb is in the very middle of the fontanelle and is mostly after birth or miscarriage or the holding back or scantiness of the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52}
