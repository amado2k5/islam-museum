## Its causes and significance
It may be from a sharp vapour, or a sharp catarrh that was or is to be, or a strongly flowing catarrh even if cold; it may be from pustules, or from the stirring of a nosebleed. It is among the foretokens of crisis and among the foretokens of smallpox and measles, as we shall mention in its place; and the treatment of each of these by the known principles is easy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.252}
