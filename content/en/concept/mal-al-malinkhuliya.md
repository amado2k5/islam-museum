## The safest and its resolution
The safest melancholia is what is from the dregs of blood and what has joy with it, and melancholia often resolves by piles and varicose veins {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.105}

## Signs of good and of death
The one among them whose black bile moves in the body is more ready for treatment than the one whose black bile is not so, and the one whose black bile moves is the one whose black bile shows in the vomit, the stool and the urine, in the colour of the skin, in vitiligo, blemishes, sores, scab, itch, varicose veins, elephantiasis and a flow from the anus; all this shows that it is 'qatil' for distinguishing it from blood (as printed, not interpreted), and when any of it shows in them it is a sign of good; if sores appear on their bodies, it points to a near death, and the falling away of appetite is bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.110} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.111}

## Convulsion after purging
When some of them are seized by a convulsion after purging and evacuation, they are more liable to it than others because of their dryness; the particulars of what is done for them are not reproduced here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.111}
