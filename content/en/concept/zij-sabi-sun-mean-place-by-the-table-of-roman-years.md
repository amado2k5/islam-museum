## Summary
Al-Battani explains how to find the Sun's mean place on the zodiac from a Roman date: the completed years are counted from the Dhu al-Qarnayn era and looked up in the table of Roman years arranged in steps of twenty; the months and days are then added, and the Sun's place from the first point of Aries comes out. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.106}.

## The arithmetic order
He adds the degrees, minutes and seconds from the years, the months and the days, carrying each sixty seconds into a minute and each sixty minutes into a degree; he then removes full circuits from the total degrees, and what remains under a full circuit is the place. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.106}.
