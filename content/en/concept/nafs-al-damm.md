## Overview
Al-Masudi notes that the Arabs in the Jahiliyya had doctrines about the soul (*nafs*). Some of them claimed that the soul is nothing but blood, and that the spirit (*ruh*) is the air within the body. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.467-468}

For this reason they called a woman who had just given birth *nafsa'* (one with blood), because of the blood that comes out of her. On this meaning the jurists of the cities disagreed about whether an animal with flowing blood that falls into water makes it impure. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.467-468}

He cites the words of Ta'abbata Sharran to al-Shanfara describing a slain man: "his soul flowed out in a flood." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.467-468}
