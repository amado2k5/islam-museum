## Summary
In the fifteenth chapter, on the pulse peculiar to women, Ibn Sina explains the pulse of the pregnant woman by strong need with a moderate power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.181}.

## Need and power
Need grows strong in them because the child shares in the air breathed in, so that the pregnant woman breathes as if for two needs and two lives. Power neither rises nor falls much, apart from the slight weariness of carrying the burden. The rulings of a moderate power and a strong need therefore prevail, and the pulse is great, quick and frequent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.181}.
