## Serpens (al-Hayya)

The catalogue names the serpent al-Hayya allati yumsikuha al-Hawi, with the Latin gloss serpens quem psyllus tenet, the serpent that the snake-charmer holds. Its stars run from the temple near the nostrils through the neck, and the tail continues onto the next page. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.151}
