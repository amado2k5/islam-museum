## Nature and selection
Nature: a known herb, water pepper; its leaves are like willow leaves but yellower, its stems are red, and its taste is that of ginger. The book reports that it kills dogs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.458}
Temperament: hot in the second degree and dry in the first. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.458}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: fresh and pounded with its seed, it clears marks on the face, kalaf and namash. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.458}
Swellings and pustules: fresh, it dissolves hard swellings when pounded with its seed and applied as a poultice. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.458}
