## The poultices and compresses of pleurisy
And the convalescent pleurisy patient must be guarded against the salty things, the acrid things, fullness, satiety, the sun, the wind, smoke, the loud voice, blowing, and coitus — for if he relapses he dies (a labelled line carrying the women-and-coitus line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.362}
