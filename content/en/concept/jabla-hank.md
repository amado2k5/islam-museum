## Nature and selection
Its action is close to that of hellebore {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}. Some say it is the seed of black turbith and the rind of its root; others say it is yellow turbith, described as Soghdian, but the best is the Indian kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.

## Temperament and actions
The entry gives no temperament; actions: it causes vomiting and purging, and has poisonous power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: some used to give it to paralysed patients, who recovered {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.
Food: causes vomiting, and at times kills by the force of the vomit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.
Excretory organs: purges; the book warns that too much is dangerous {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.
Poisons: has poisonous power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.
