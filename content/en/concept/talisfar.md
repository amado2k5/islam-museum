## Nature
Indian barks with some astringency, pungency and a little aroma; they contain more earthy substance and a little fineness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Temperament
In Galen's view no hot or cold quality is clear enough to count. Some say it is hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Properties
It has strong astringency and drying, and dissolving power; it is made of many substances, the earthy ones predominating. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Excretory organs
It helps diarrhoea, intestinal ulcers and piles. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}
