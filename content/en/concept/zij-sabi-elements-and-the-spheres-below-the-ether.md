## The elements and the ether
The text says that below the lunar sphere are the four elements, earth, water, air and fire, and above them the ether through which the planets move. These four are the principles of the earthly natures, and by them things differ as the Sun, Moon and stars act on them. It states the distance from the earth's centre to the ether in multiples of the earth's radius; those figures are garbled and are not given here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.185-186}

## The disturbed figures
The figures for the distances of the elements are disturbed and are not carried over; they are to be checked against the Latin text. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.185-186}
