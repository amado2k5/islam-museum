## Summary
Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim report the plague described as a torment God sent upon whom He willed and made a mercy for believers: who hears of it in a land does not enter, who is in it does not flee, and who dies of it patient and hoping for reward has a martyr's wage {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
