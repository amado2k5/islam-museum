## Overview
The book begins by defining economics from its linguistic root. In Arabic, qasd means moderation and balance, and the Qur'anic expression regarding walking belongs to this sense; this meaning was current among jurists and scholars, as in al-'Izz ibn 'Abd al-Salam's statement on moderation between the two extremes of negligence and extravagance {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 14}.

## From Terminology to Resources
The author observes that modern terminology has narrowed the meaning of economics to the study of allocating scarce resources, whereas Islamic economics remains connected to the sense of moderation in spending and living, and to preserving human beings in their religion, life, intellect, lineage, and property {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 15} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 16}.
