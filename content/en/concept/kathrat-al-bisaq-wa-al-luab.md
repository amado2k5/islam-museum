## Its causes
It may come from much heat and wetness, especially in the stomach, or from heat's sway; and there is one who lacks the constant spittle only until he eats, when it calms in him; it may also come from phlegm or from cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.263}
## If from heat
If it is from heat, the vein the book names must be bled first (the site carried as the book names it), the sour rububs and the cold astringent fruits used, the not-old wine in a mixed temperament, the food made of fish and the light meats like kids' flesh and birds, and rinsing kept up with the astringent decoctions made of this kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.263}
## If from cold and phlegm
If it is from cold and phlegm, vomiting is used as you know twice or thrice a week, a drug of this kind is given to drink weekly, and after it the hot theriacs and electuaries of this kind; his food is the spiced stewed chicks and their like, an evening taking of the known cake, hot water sipped, and the siwak before sleep; among the good shared treatments is taking a thing of this kind daily, then the small atrifil, keeping up the long siwak; and the roasted mouse was tried and found beneficial, especially for children (a line kept as a labelled listing for the founder's ruling; the recipes, quantities and names left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.264}
