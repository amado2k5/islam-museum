## The cusps and the risings
Knowing the measures on the ecliptic requires the risings of the signs between the cusps: the ascendant, the descendant, the midheaven and the nadir. The rising of Aries at the midheaven and at the nadir differs by a stated number of equal hours, and between each cusp and the next there are six equal hours, the hours of one quarter of the day or night. The hours of the quarters below the earth are night hours. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.202-203}

## The risings in the climes
The rising amounts differ in each clime according to how far the first degree of Aries lies from one of the cusps, which is computed in equal hours. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.202-203}
