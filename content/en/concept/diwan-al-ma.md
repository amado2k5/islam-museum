## Overview
The *diwan al-ma'* (the office of water) was an Abbasid office devoted to the water of al-Ray. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan mentions it, citing al-Khwarizmi in *Mafatih al-ulum*. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.372}

## Its work
It supervised the digging of canals, the maintenance of dams, and the regulation of irrigation. The author notes that it existed in Egypt, Iraq, Yemen, north-east Persia, and Transoxiana. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.372}
