## Summary
In the seventh chapter Ibn Sina compares the pulse of men with that of women {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.

## The pulse of males
The pulse of males, for the strength of their power and need, is much greater and stronger, and since their need is met by greatness, it is slower than women's and in most cases more widely spaced. Any pulse in which power holds and which becomes frequent must be quick, since speed comes before frequency; so as men's pulse is slower, it is also more widely spaced {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.

## Women
The text says men's pulse is slower than women's, so women's is quicker by comparison; this passage says no more than that in describing women's pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.
