## Summary
[[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] distinguishes general leadership, such as heading a state or a company, from specialised leadership in a particular field such as thought, politics, media or marketing. The general leader takes only a light share of specialised subjects; each specialism has its own list of skills, and so it needs specialised centres offering professional training. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:04:30} He devoted episode 28 of his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]] to it, after completing the training in general leadership. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:02:30}

## Examples from the Sira
The Prophet, he says, raised specialised leaders, and he gives examples:
- **Military**: [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]], in his view the foremost military commander, with Ali second. He cites Khalid's taking up the banner at [[event/battle-of-mutah|Mu'ta]] after the three commanders named by the Prophet had fallen, only months after his conversion, so leadership for him is tied neither to age nor to seniority. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:07:30} He says the Muslims at Mu'ta were three thousand against two hundred thousand, and that Khalid swapped the right and left wings and the van and rear by night and made the enemy think reinforcements had come, then withdrew the army. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:08:30}
- **Political**: [[person/amr-ibn-al-as|Amr ibn al-As]] in his mission to the people of Oman, where he began with the younger, gentler brother, Abd ibn al-Julanda, before the king his brother, until both brothers accepted Islam. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:13:00}
- **Diplomatic**: [[person/dihya-al-kalbi|Dihya al-Kalbi]], the first to carry the Prophet's letter to Heraclius. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:18:00}
- **Religious knowledge**: [[person/abdullah-ibn-masud|Abdullah ibn Mas'ud]], who, he says, took seventy suras from the Prophet's own mouth and was the first to recite the Qur'an aloud at the Ka'ba. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:19:30}
- **Economic**: [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-awf|Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf]], who declined his Ansari brother's offer and asked to be shown the way to the market. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:22:00}

## The "Mufakkir" institute as an example
Al-Suwaidan shows how specialised leaders are prepared through one of his own institutions: the "Mufakkir" (Thinker) institute for preparing leaders of thought, one of a series of online centres he says were under construction, including the "I'lami" academy for preparing media leaders. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:24:00} He describes it as non-profit, with all study done at a distance, 42 compulsory courses, an overall grade of at least "very good" required to pass, and about two years to complete; its director is Abdulaziz al-Awadi, and he says building its competencies and curriculum took more than three years. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:28:30} Its method has three steps:
1. **Defining the competencies**: aware thinking, discernment, self-learning, intellectual method, broad culture, tools for spreading ideas, and intellectual relationships. A cultured person, he says, reads two books a month; a thinker at least seven, and better ten. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:29:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:30:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:41:30}
2. **The curriculum**: methodical thinking, ideologies and religions, the humanities, history and civilisation, philosophy and logic, political thought, and the tools of public speaking, dialogue, writing and programme-making. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:43:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:50:00}
3. **The means**: courses, workshops, readings, assignments, research, interviews with thinkers, projects, debates and simulation, all online; he says its students come from more than 47 countries. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:50:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:51:00}

In his view leadership training in the Arab world happens in isolation, and trainees must meet other thinkers, including those who disagree with them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep28 00:43:00}
