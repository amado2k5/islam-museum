## Summary
Ibn Sina says much or little sediment shows much or little of the producing cause, and that colour speaks: black is bad, red shows blood and indigestion, yellow strong heat, white is praised or blamed by kind, and green is a road to black. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.195-196}.

## Quantity
Quantity shows through much and little, which show much and little of the efficient cause, and through the size of the pieces, as said of the scraped sediment. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Colour
Black is a bad sign for the kinds listed, safest when the sediment is black and the watery part is not. Red shows a sanguine state and indigestion; yellow, great heat and a malignant illness; white is praiseworthy in one kind and blameworthy in another, mucous, pus-like or frothy and opposed to maturation; and green is a road to black. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.195-196}.
