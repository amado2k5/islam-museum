## The subject of the chapter
The subject of this chapter is the treatment of the headache that arises from intercourse, and what Ibn Sina says in it is all in the labelled section below {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.66-67}

## Intercourse (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina says that this headache comes either because of the dryness that intercourse leaves, and its treatment is what was mentioned in the chapter on dry headache after inclining with moistening means, or because of a fullness in the body upon which the movement of intercourse, compounded of the bodily and the psychic, comes and stirs the bad vapours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.66} In the second, the one who is so affected and is full begins by drawing blood and then by loosening the bowels if needed; then the brain is strengthened, he is fed what is quickly digested and gives a good chyme, and he leaves intercourse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.66-67}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.66-67}
