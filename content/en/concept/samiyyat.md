## Definition
The samiyyat are matters on which hearing (revelation) depends, such as prophethood, or matters that depend on revelation, such as the return and the causes of felicity and wretchedness through faith, obedience, disbelief and disobedience. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9827}
They include the signs of the Hour, the punishment of the grave, resurrection, reckoning, the books, the bridge, the scales, intercession, the Pool, Paradise and Hell. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9827}

## The ruling
The ruling on them has two parts. The first concerns belief in them, their kinds and their evidence, treated under the entry on faith. The second concerns the ruling on one who denies them or any of them, and its consequence, treated under the entry on apostasy. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9827}
