## Definition
Dutifulness to the mother is doing her good in word and deed: feeding her, clothing her, honouring her, gentleness to her in her old age, praying for her and joining the ties she used to join — among the greatest devotions in the Sunnah. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}

## Your mother, three times
In Abu Hurayra's report in the two Sahihs a man came and said: Messenger of God, who most deserves my good companionship? He said: your mother. He said: then whom? He said: then your mother. He said: then whom? He said: then your mother. He said: then whom? He said: then your father — the mother is named three times, for what she bore of pregnancy, delivery, nursing and raising, as the commentators say. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}

## In her old age and after her
The Qur'an joins dutifulness to the parents with His worship, forbids even a sigh of impatience to them in their old age and commands the wing of humility and the prayer 'my Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small'; in the Sunnah Paradise lies beneath mothers' feet, and of continuing birr is joining the parents' loved ones after their death — as the Prophet did with Khadija's friend — and his care for Umm Ayman who had nursed him small. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:muwatta-malik}
