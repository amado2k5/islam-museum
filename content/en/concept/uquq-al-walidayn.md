## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy presents disobedience to parents as the opposite of honouring them, and says that Paradise is forbidden to one who disobeys his parents. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:07:00}

## Forms of disobedience
The Sheikh counts it as disobedience when a father or mother goes to sleep angry with his or her child. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:07:00}
He counts as disobedience a glance at father or mother that is harsh, full of anger or defiance, and cites a saying on this. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:09:00}
He says that however high a child's academic degrees, the parents are the ones who taught and raised him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:09:30}
He warns against calling a son who struck his father or disobeyed his mother a good son. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:10:00}
He recounts a son whose wife refused to live under one roof with his mother, and who took his mother and left her in a home for the elderly. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:21:30}

## Its consequences
The Sheikh says that Paradise is forbidden to the one who disobeys his parents. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:07:00}
He cites a saying about three people who do not enter Paradise, the disobedient child among them (needs_source: the list of three is not clear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:07:30}
He says God made causes for entering Paradise and for entering the Fire, and that disobedience is among the causes of the Fire (needs_source: the second half of this passage is not clear). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:08:00}
He says that whoever disobeys his parents has two gates of the Fire opened to him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:05:00}

## Between parents and children over marriage
The Sheikh describes parents who announce their refusal of a wife chosen by their son, or of a suitor for their daughter, even after the child has been educated. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:11:00}
