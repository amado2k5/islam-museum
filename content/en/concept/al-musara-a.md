## Summary
Ibn Ishaq narrates, through Ishaq ibn Yasar, that the Prophet invited Rukana ibn Abd Yazid of Banu al-Muttalib to Islam. The Prophet asked whether Rukana would know his claim to be true if he threw him; Rukana agreed; the Prophet threw him twice, and Rukana then said he was a sorcerer. {cite:sira-ibn-ishaq-zakkar p.276}

Ibn Hajar, in al-Isaba, gives the bout also through Abu Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Rukana from his father. Ibn Hibban found the chain doubtful, and Ibn Hajar judged that Muhammad ibn Rukana most likely dropped a link from the chain. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.499} {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.531}

Abu Nu'aym lists the bout in his index of Companion reports under the incipit «the Prophet wrestled Rukana»; the body text was not legible in the scan used. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym p.682}
