## The zodiacal and the northern and southern figures
The text says the stars named by Ptolemy in the Almagest number one thousand and twenty-two. They fall into figures: in the southern region fifteen figures and six zodiacal figures of the south, namely Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces, and in the northern region eighteen figures and the remaining six zodiacal figures. The total is forty-five figures. The zodiacal figures hold three hundred and forty-six stars, the northern figures three hundred and sixty, and the southern figures three hundred and sixteen. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.192-193}

## The sum and its check
The three counts given add up to one thousand and twenty-two, which agrees with the total; the southern count was read from a disturbed scan and needs checking. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.192-193}
