## Nature and selection
An Indian drug that acts like fawaniya {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}. The editor's footnote says it is a stone that eases childbirth; this comes from the footnote, not the text {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament for it in this entry.

## Uses by organ
Head: applied to the place where vapour rises, it is said to prevent epilepsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.
