# Emigration to the West — hope or pain (the brain drain)

## The episode and the votes
The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode debated emigration to the West — 'they imagine it paradise and meet racial discrimination, family dissolution and loss of identity' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:02:00}. The youth voted: 34% would emigrate immediately, 26% would hesitate, 40% would not; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:09:00} and 84% saw the phenomenon as large and dangerous {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:38:00}.

## Figures of the haemorrhage — as presented by the host
The host presented figures: 54% of state-funded scholarship students never return, 50% of doctors and 29% of engineers; and 75% of migrants to America are from the scientist class {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:17:00}. An objector noted emigrants are two million of 300 million — under 1% — but the reply: the emigrants are the leaders and thinkers; 'nations are developed by their 1-2%, the rest are followers' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:18:00}.

## The repelling environment and the attracting one
Panelists traced the phenomenon to converging causes: political suffocation and despotism, weak research and education — 'they ask not for higher salaries but merely an advanced library' — and the economic side {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:19:30}. And 'in an atmosphere of freedom ideas cross-pollinate; in an atmosphere of suffocation everyone is afraid' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:20:00}.

## The cure — a state that attracts, not expels
One panelist compressed the medicine: deepening good governance, a culture of human rights, and building a welfare state and a state of law — 'restoring the peoples' belonging to their systems and countries' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:17:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:17:30}. Another proposed linking emigrants through shares in small projects that invest their savings at home {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep11 01:16:00}.
