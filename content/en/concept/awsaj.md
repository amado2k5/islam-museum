## Nature
Some said that the awsaj is the ulayq. Dioscorides said that it is a tree growing in salt marshes with upright thorny branches like the tree called dawksawafibs in its rods and thorns, and a rather long leaf topped with a sticky moisture that clings to the hand. There is another kind of awsaj whiter than this, and another whose leaf is blacker and broader, inclining slightly to red, with long branches that are thornier and weaker, its thorns less sharp, and its fruit broad and thin as if in husks. The awsaj has a fruit like the mulberry that is eaten, and its growing place is more in the cold lands {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}

## Actions and properties
Some people claimed that when it is hung on doors or windows it nullifies the work of sorcerers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}

## Swellings and pustules
The leaf of all its kinds is useful for erysipelas and herpes as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}
