## Actions and properties
Its web stops the flow of blood when it is laid on wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}

## Wounds and ulcers
When its web is laid on ulcers and wounds it keeps them from swelling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}

## Organs of the head
When the thick-webbed white spider is cooked in rose oil and dropped in the ear it quiets the pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}

## Fevers
Some said that the web, mixed with certain ointments, spread on a linen cloth and fixed to the forehead or the temples, cures the tertian fever; some claimed that the web of the dense white kind, bound in a skin and hung on the neck or the upper arm, cures the tertian fever; and Dioscorides said that it cures the quartan fever. These are attributed statements that the book reports {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.617}
