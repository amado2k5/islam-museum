## Overview
Mahmud Hamdi Zaqzuq sets out an Islamic conception of peace in three interlocking circles: peace within the soul, then peace with God through faith, then peace with people and the world; Islam and salam share one root, and the Muslims' greeting and the prayer's closing taslim both express their wish of peace for the world {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 66} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 67}. Diversity of colors, races and tongues was meant for mutual acquaintance, not conflict, and the religions have one source {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 67} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 68}; justice is the shortest road to peace.
