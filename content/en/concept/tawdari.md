## Nature
Dioscorides said: a herb with leaves like the leaves of farasiyun and a square root, the root about half a cubit, with cones holding an elongated black seed, and this is what is used of tawdari; as for the wild, its seed is rolled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.686}

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree and moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.686}

## Actions and properties
It has a pungency like the pungency of harf, and some ulcerating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.686}

## Swellings and pustules
It benefits cancers that are not ulcerated, as an application with water and honey, benefits all hard swellings, and is used as a poultice on inflammation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.686}

## Joints
A poultice of it is put on the hardness of gout and benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Organs of the head
It benefits swellings at the roots of the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Ocular organs
When used as an eye salve with honey it cleanses ulcers of the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Organs of the chest
When it goes into linctuses it helps in bringing up humours; the book describes a way of preparing it by soaking, boiling and roasting, which is left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it benefits desire, especially when cooked with the sharab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}
