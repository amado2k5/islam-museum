## Overview
The qibla is the direction of the Kaaba which Muslims face in prayer — among the clearest markers of unity in the Islamic world. In the early Medinan period the Prophet and his companions prayed toward Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem); the turn to the Kaaba came in the verses of Surat al-Baqara {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In fiqh and creed
Facing the qibla is a condition of valid prayer in fiqh, and Fiqh al-Sunna details the rulings for one unable to determine it and the acts to which facing extends {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}. The Tahawiyya creed names the Muslims 'the people of our qibla' {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §73}, making the qibla a banner of the community's identity itself.
