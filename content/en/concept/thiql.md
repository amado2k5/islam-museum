## Choice
The best is the heavy dregs of saffron oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.697}

## Temperament
The dregs of olive pressings are in the first degree of heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.697}

## Actions and properties
The dregs of saffron oil dye the tongue and teeth with a stain that remains, as the book has said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.697}

## Wounds and ulcers
The dregs of olive pressings are among the healers of ulcers occurring in dry bodies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.697}
