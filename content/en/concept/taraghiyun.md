## Nature
A plant that grows in Crete, with leaves, stalk and fruit like those of the plant called akhinus but smaller, and a gum like gum arabic. Its leaf, fruit and gum have a drawing power. Another kind has leaves like those of the plant called saqulugandarion and a root like a wild radish. The editor's note calls the plant the goat's tree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Action on flesh
Dioscorides says that a wild goat struck by an arrow that grazes among this plant has the arrow fall out. A poultice of it with wine draws thorns and whatever is lodged out of the flesh. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Excretory organs
Drunk, it cures painful dribbling of urine and breaks the stone in the bladder. A small measure taken brings the menses, according to the book; this is a labelled listing held for the founder's ruling. The other kind, eaten raw or cooked, helps ulcers of the intestines. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}
