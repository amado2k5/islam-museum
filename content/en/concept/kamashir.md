## Nature, temperament and properties
It is like jawshir in its properties, but much stronger. Nature: strongly hot and dry in the second degree. Properties: dissolving, resolving and thinning. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.525}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): the book says it brings on urine and the menses, expels the foetus with a strength it has no equal in, and has no equal in purging watery matter. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.525}
