## Summary
Ibn Sina says the scraped, scaly sediment includes a sheet-like kind in large pieces, white and red, which shows detachment from parts near the passage of urine: the white from the bladder, the red fleshy from the kidney. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.194}.

## What the colour shows
The large white and red sheets usually show detachment from the urinary organs. White shows the bladder, from sores, scab or erosion in it; red and fleshy shows the kidney. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.194}.

## The worst kind and the cantharides report
Some sheets are dull and dark, or like fish scales; this is the worst of the kinds he lists, because it shows the scraping off of layers of the principal organs, whereas the first two kinds often do no harm and may even cleanse the bladder. He reports that a man given cantharides passed white scales like membrane that dissolved in water and dyed it red, and he recovered and lived. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.194}.
