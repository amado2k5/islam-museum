## Overview
The burqa in juristic usage is a garment by which the woman veils her face; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia lists it among the terms related to hijab {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia names burqa, niqab, khimar and litham among the terms related to the hijab entry {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
