## Overview
Shaykh 'Ali al-Khafif holds acting on the solitary report (khabar al-ahad) obligatory when probability outweighs that the Prophet said, did, or approved it — legal obligation hangs on likelihood, not certainty {cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 41}. Denying its authority was the doctrine of some people of passions — the Rafida, the philosophers, some Mu'tazila — and it would annul the whole Sunna, for apart from the mutawatir the entire practical Sunna of prayer, zakat, fasting and hajj rests on solitary reports {cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 84}. The Prophet himself relied on single informants in his missions.
