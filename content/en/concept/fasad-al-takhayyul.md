## Nature
It is the same, in causes and signs, as described in the other chapters, except that it is in the front of the brain; its corruption is either that he imagines what does not exist and sees things that have no existence, from the prevailing of bile on the front of the brain, or from the prevailing of a hot ill temperament without matter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.100} or that imagination falls short and is weak at imagining imaginable things, and he sees visions and dreams only rarely, and forgets them and forgets the forms of sensed things as they were, and does not imagine them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.100} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.101}

## How it differs from corruption of memory
Its cause is the same as the cause of deficiency of memory, except that corruption of memory is mostly from cold and moisture and least from dryness, and here it is the reverse, because this instrument was created soft so that it is quickly impressed by what it imagines, while that one is hard so that it is hard to clear of what was impressed in it; so things fall in them by opposites {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.101} Corruption of memory falls in the meanings of sensed things and because of their composition, and corruption of imagination in the likenesses of sensed things and their shapes; this is learnt from another art {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.101}

## Signs and treatment
The strongest indication that the cause is moisture or dryness is the state of sleep and sleeplessness, the dryness or moisture of the eye and nose, and the colour of the tongue and its moisture or dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.101} When the illness is corruption of imagination and not its deficiency, you can know that it is from black bile, yellow bile or a hot temperament alone by what was said in the earlier causes; the treatments follow that, except that the cure must be in the region of the origins of sensation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.101} The procedures that he names for the front of the brain are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.101}
