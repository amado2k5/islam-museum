## Summary
Khiyar al-ta'yin (option of specification): lexically singling out from a group; for the Hanafis it is buying one of two or three things with the buyer to choose which he wants; technically the right of a party to specify one of the things on which the contract fell in common, within a set term. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7895}
