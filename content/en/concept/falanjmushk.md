## Nature
Some claimed that falanjmushk is more nourishing than marjoram and mint and less dry. The editor's footnote says it is wild basil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Organs of the head
It opens obstructions that occur in the brain and the nostrils, by smelling, by anointing and by eating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits palpitation that comes from phlegm and black bile in the heart, when eaten {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Excretory organs
Good for piles, drunk and applied {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}
