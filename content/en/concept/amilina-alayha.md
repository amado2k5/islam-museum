## Overview
The third zakat category: the collectors, guards and registrars appointed by the imam to gather and distribute it; they are paid for their work from it even when wealthy, in the accepted view, for their wage is for labour not for poverty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
