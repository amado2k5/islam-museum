## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy says that wasting time comes from long hope, because a person assumes that tomorrow will come. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:20:00} He holds that following desire turns one from the truth, and that long hope makes one forget the Hereafter. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:20:30}

## Wasting time and long hope
The Sheikh says that wasting time comes from long hope, because a person assumes that tomorrow will come. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:20:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:20:30}

## Following desire and long hope
The Sheikh says that following desire turns a person from the truth, and that long hope makes him forget the Hereafter. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:20:30}

## Speaker's view: the aim is guidance and readiness to meet God
The Sheikh's view is that all of prayer lies in following guidance and preparing for the meeting with God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:21:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy says that hopes run opposite to lifespans: a life begins long and grows short, while hope grows with a person; he tells the joke of Juha, who began dreaming of what he would do if he grew rich, buying and selling, taking a second wife and having children, until what was in his hand was lost. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:14:00}
