## Summary
In the lexicon, uquba is a penalty, a noun from 'iqab; as a term it is the penalty fixed for a wrong {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11776}.

## Its divisions
Penalties are classed by kind (retaliation, fixed punishment and discretionary punishment), by whether the right is God's or people's or both, and by whether they are complete, incomplete or carry an element of worship {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11777}.

## Other penalties
These include the ghurra for a foetus, the arsh for an injury, and the killer's loss of inheritance and bequest {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11777} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11778}.

## Fixed and discretionary punishments
The fixed punishments are set by the law: illicit intercourse, false accusation, drinking, theft and brigandage, with apostasy and rebellion disputed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11778}. Discretionary punishment is not fixed; flogging, imprisonment and reproof are allowed, while maiming, slapping the face, burning and shaving the beard are not {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11778} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11779}.

## Combination and overlap
Some jurists allow penalties of different kinds for one offence; penalties of the same kind and cause overlap {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11779} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11780}.

## Where to look further
Retaliation is treated under «qisas», discretionary punishment under «ta'zir», and overlap under «tadakhul» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11779}.
