## Andromeda

The catalogue gives Andromeda, the Woman, and places some of her stars at her head. Her entries come on the page where the names of the fixed stars begin again, and the Triangle follows on the same page. Positions in longitude and latitude are not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.253-254}
