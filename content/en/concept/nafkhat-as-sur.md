## Overview
The Trumpet blasts open the Day of Resurrection in the Sunni sources: a first blast of swooning and a second blast of rising {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In the texts
The Qur'an describes the day of the blast: whoever is in the heavens and earth swoons, save whom God wills, then it is blown again and they stand, watching [[passage/quran-39-68|the verse of the two blasts]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. The hadith places forty between the two blasts, and has God send down rain from which people sprout like seedlings {cite:sahih-muslim}. Israfil is the angel of the Trumpet, already poised {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In creed
The two blasts are real events of the Day in Sunni creed: by the first, whoever remains dies; by the second, creation stands before the Lord of the worlds {cite:aqida-tahawiyya}.
