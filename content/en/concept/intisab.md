## Definition
Intisab in the lexical sense is ascribing oneself to someone, to a father, a tribe, a town or a craft. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3434}

## Kinds and rulings
- Ascription to one's parents by birth is obligatory when true and forbidden when false, following the hadith that a woman who brings into a people a child not theirs is not in God's keeping. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3434}
- Adoption is forbidden, citing the Qur'anic command to call adopted children after their fathers. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3434}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3435}
- Ascription by freed-slave patronage gives inheritance and the share in a blood payment when there are no blood relatives. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3435}
- The Hanafis recognise allegiance by agreement: a convert who is linked to a patron by agreement is inherited from and pays for his crimes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3435}
- Ascription to a craft, a tribe or a town is allowed, as with al-Qurashi, al-Tamimi, al-Bukhari and al-Qurtubi, and the community agrees. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3435}
- The child of a mutual curse is attached to his mother after the ruler has denied the father's paternity. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3435}
- Ascription through the mother has rulings on inheritance, guardianship, marriage, looking and travel. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3435}
