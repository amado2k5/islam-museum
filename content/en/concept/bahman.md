## Nature and selection
Dried, twisted and wrinkled woody pieces of root, in two kinds, white and red {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.387}. The heading is damaged in the scan, and the drug is identified from the editor's note, which likens it to a thick radish root cut and dried before use {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.388}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.387}. It fattens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.387}.

## Uses by organ
Chest: it strengthens the heart very much and helps palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.387}. Excretory organs: the book says it increases semen clearly. This is the book's statement on sexual vigour and is recorded as written {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.387}.

## Substitutes
Its substitute is one of the same kind, and the book also names a second substitute, the todari. The proportions are not given here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.387}.
