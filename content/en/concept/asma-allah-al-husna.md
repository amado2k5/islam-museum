## Overview
The most beautiful names of God (asma' Allah al-husna) are the names of perfection and majesty by which God named Himself; the famous hadith reports: "God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one — whoever enumerates them enters Paradise" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In the scholars' books
Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a section of Jala' al-Afham to the ninety-nine names, transmitting and commenting on them {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}; later writers expanded the subject into chapters on creed, remembrance and supplication, making it among the most-repeated topics in Islamic writing.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 3)

The Sheikh says that God is the Guide before there exists anyone who needs guidance, that is, from eternity, and that He is the First, the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:04:00}. He adds that God is the Provider before there exists anyone who needs provision {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:04:00}.

The Sheikh says these are among the most beautiful names of God, and when they settle in the servant's heart he gains full certainty in the sincerity of his reliance on God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:04:30}. He explains that God's guidance exists before the existence of those who need it, and that His provision exists before the servants are created {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:04:30}.

The Sheikh stresses again that God is the Provider before He creates those who need provision {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:05:00}. He then turns to the softening of hearts, likening it to a gradual process of making something supple, prays that God soften the hearts of those present, and links this to opening the Qur'an and reading it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:05:00}.
