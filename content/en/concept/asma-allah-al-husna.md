## Overview
The most beautiful names of God (asma' Allah al-husna) are the names of perfection and majesty by which God named Himself; the famous hadith reports: "God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one — whoever enumerates them enters Paradise" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In the scholars' books
Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a section of Jala' al-Afham to the ninety-nine names, transmitting and commenting on them {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}; later writers expanded the subject into chapters on creed, remembrance and supplication, making it among the most-repeated topics in Islamic writing.
