## Nature and selection
Armak is a fragrant wood from Yemen, resembling cinnamon in colour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament for it in this entry; its actions are listed by organ below {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: it sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Swellings and pustules: it helps hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Wounds and ulcers: it helps spreading ulcers, dries them without burning and prevents the flesh from rotting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Head: it strengthens the brain, firms the gums and helps diseases of the mouth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Eyes: eaten, it helps ophthalmia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Chest: it strengthens the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Excretory organs: it binds the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}.
