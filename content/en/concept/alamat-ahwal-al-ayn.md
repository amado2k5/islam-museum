## Signs of the sharing
The sign of eye disease shared with the brain is that the brain carries some proofs of its lesions; when the intermediary is the inner membranes pain and ache begin from the eye's depth — sneezing and nose-itch if the matter is hot, a felt cold flow if cold — and this sharing is seldom from a single distemper; when the sharing is with the outer membranes and matter moves from them there is a distension beginning in the brow and outer veins, and the harm shows most below the lid; when with the stomach the signs are those given under the brain's sharing of the stomach, and any imaginings from the stomach lessen in emptiness and increase in fullness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.164}

## Signs of the material disease in the eye itself
The sanguineous is told by heaviness, redness, tears, swelling, vessels running, pulsing temples, sticking, eye-rheum and hot touch — especially when joined by the head's sanguineous signs; the phlegmy by strong heaviness, a faint leaden-tinged redness, sticking, rheum, irritation and few tears; the choleric by pricking and inflammation with a redness toward yellow unlike the sanguineous redness, thin sharp tears and little sticking; the simple distempers by heaviness with dryness plus the signs given under discerning; the instrumental and shared diseases each get their own chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.164}
