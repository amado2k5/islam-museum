## Nature and selection
A plant with seed, flower and small broken twigs, sharp and pungent in taste, with red seed. Its power is like that of hasha, but weaker; some say it is of the same kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}. It helps melancholia and epilepsy, and purges black bile and phlegm strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.357}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry in the third degree according to Galen; Hunayn says it is hot in the third and dry at the end of the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.356}.

## Uses by organ
It calms flatulence, suits the old, and clears black-bile diseases {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.357}. It helps convulsions, melancholia and epilepsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.357}. Stomach: it distresses those whose yellow bile predominates and makes them vomit, and it causes thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.357}. Excretory organs: it purges black bile and phlegm strongly. The book gives measured doses; they are not repeated here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.357}.
