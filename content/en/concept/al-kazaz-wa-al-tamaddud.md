## The definition of extension
Extension is an instrumental disease barring the motive power from contracting the organs whose business is to contract, for a lesion in the muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.150}

## The uses of kazaz
They use it for different meanings: sometimes they say kazaz meaning what begins from the collar-bone muscles and extends them forward and backward, or in both directions together; sometimes they say kazaz for every extension; sometimes kazaz for convulsion itself; sometimes for the convulsion of the neck alone; sometimes they mean the extension arising from two extensions, one forward and one backward; and sometimes they single out the name kazaz for the extension from cold (the author's note in the margin glosses kazaz as the convulsion befalling from severe cold and from losing much blood, per the commentators) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.150}

## Opposition to convulsion
Extension in truth is the opposite of convulsion, and the two befall one cause in succession — only convulsion is toward one direction, so when two convulsions meet in two opposite directions they become extension, convulsion befalling it fore and aft together; since this extension is a doubled convulsion it must be sharper than the simple convulsion and its crisis quicker, and this doubled one may not be from one stretching but from two extensions, and convulsion in most cases is not free of severe pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.150}
