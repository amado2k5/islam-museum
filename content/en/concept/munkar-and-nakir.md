## In hadith
The hadith of the grave, narrated in Sunan Abi Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah and Musnad Ahmad, establishes that when the dead are buried two angels come to him — called Munkar and Nakir — who question him about his Lord, religion and Prophet {cite:sunan-abi-dawud} {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:sunan-al-nasai} {cite:sunan-ibn-majah} {cite:musnad-ahmad}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details their appearance at the grave and that whoever answers rightly is given glad tidings of Paradise while whoever fails is punished {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; the questioning occurs in the grave before resurrection.

## Notes
Scholars differ on the details of what they ask and on the nature of the grave's punishment, but agree the questioning is established in the Sunna {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
