## Overview
It was the custom of the Arabs of the towns to seek wet-nurses for their children among the desert people, to keep them away from the diseases of the towns, to strengthen their bodies and firm their nerves, and to master the Arabic tongue in their cradle. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.55} In keeping with this custom, [[person/abd-al-muttalib|Abd al-Muttalib]] sought wet-nurses for his grandson [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], and [[person/halima-al-sadiyya|Halima]] of [[group/banu-sad-ibn-bakr|the Banu Sa'd ibn Bakr]] was chosen to nurse him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.55} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/162}

## How it worked
From Halima's account, which the book transmits from Ibn Ishaq, it is understood that the women of the desert went out at a known season to Mecca to look for nursing children. Each woman chose an infant and returned with him to the dwellings of her people. They hoped for a reward from the child's father, and so they disliked the orphan. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.55} {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.56} {cite:ibn-hisham-sira 1/162-164} The child remained in the desert for two years until weaning, and the nurse might ask to keep him longer, until he grew sturdy, out of fear for him of the plagues of the town. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.56}

## Fosterage kinship
Fostering creates milk-siblings whom the scholars of biography count among a man's kin. As the book counts Abd Allah, Unaysa, and al-Shayma', the children of Halima, as brothers of the Prophet, and counts [[person/hamza|Hamza]] as his foster-brother in two respects. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.55} {cite:zad-al-maad 1/19} The ruling of this kinship in jurisprudence is treated under [[concept/rada|foster-kinship]].

## What is not recorded here
The book does not examine the origin of this custom, its spread beyond Quraysh, or the wages of the nurses. These need other sources.

## For young visitors
In the past, the people of Mecca used to send their infants to the women of the desert, so that they would grow up in fresh air and learn eloquent Arabic. In this way the young Prophet went to Halima in the desert of the Banu Sa'd.
