## Summary
Ihtitab is gathering firewood. Its legal ruling is that of ihtishash, but it differs on two points: cutting idhkhir and the 'awsaj inside the sanctuary is allowed for grass but not for wood, and some allow grass to feed animals but not wood. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1732}
