## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the solar equation taken from the table is for noon at Raqqa. If the Sun's anomaly is below half a circuit the equation is subtracted from the mean, and if above it is added, which gives the Sun's true place. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.107}.

## Minutes with the anomaly
When the anomaly carries minutes, the equation for the full degree is taken and kept, and the difference from the equation for the next degree is scaled by those minutes out of sixty, then added or subtracted according to the case. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.107}.
