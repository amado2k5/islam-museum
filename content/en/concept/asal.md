## Summary
In the lexicon, asal is the saliva of the bee, which God made a healing for people; the Arabs use the word for anything found sweet {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11602}.

## Honey as medicine
Using honey as medicine is permitted, and most scholars read the Qur'anic verse as saying that honey contains healing {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11603}.

## Zakat on honey
The Hanafis and Hanbalis hold that a tenth is due on honey; the Hanafis require that the bees be on tithe land {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11603} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11604}. The Malikis and Shafi'is hold that honey carries no zakat {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11605}. The Hanbalis and al-Zuhri set its threshold at ten faraq; Abu Hanifa requires a tenth on any amount {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11605}.
