## Its causes from bad temperament
Earache is either from bad temperament, or from a swelling or pustules, or from loss of continuity. The bad temperament comes from hot air and hot winds — especially passing to it from cold suddenly — or washing with hot water that enters the ear or water of hot-dominant waters; or hot with a sanguine or bilious matter; or cold without matter, from causes counter to those named — cold air or wind, especially passing to it from heat suddenly, or cold water or cold-dominant water; or cold with a cold windy matter or a sticky humoral one {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.222}
## Its causes from swellings and loss of continuity
What is from swellings or pustules: they are either hot or cold swellings. What is from loss of continuity: a stretching wind, ulcers and wounds; among the causes of earaches that break continuity are a wind breeding in the ear, water entering it, an animal reaching its meatus, worms breeding in it; it may follow a fall or a blow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.222}
## The worst of it
The worst earaches are from a deep-lying hot swelling, attended by a continued fever, especially when it brings confusion of mind; what lies in the outer cartilage has neither severe pain nor severe danger. The first-named may kill suddenly as apoplexy kills — deadlier to the young than the old, and quicker, sometimes killing on the seventh day; in most of the aged this swelling suppurates, but the young it often kills before suppuration — if it suppurates with good signs recovery is hoped. Earache may come with itch or without; the itch has its own chapter in its place; the signs are like those given in the chapter on hardness of hearing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.222}
