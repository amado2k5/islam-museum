## Definition
Motherhood in Islam is a womb-bond wrapped in care from pregnancy to custody: the mother bears her child in weakness upon weakness, nurses him two full years, is named in dutifulness three times before the father, and care for her is set in the Qur'an beside the worship of God Himself. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## The mother's rank
In Abu Hurayra's report a man asked: who most deserves my good companionship? He said: your mother. He said: then whom? He said: then your mother. He said: then whom? He said: then your mother. He said: then whom? He said: then your father. And in a well-known report Paradise lies beneath mothers' feet. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}

## The rulings of motherhood
Fiqh surrounds motherhood with the rulings of pregnancy, delivery, confinement, nursing, custody, maintenance, inheritance and prohibition: the mother is unmarriageable by lineage and by milk, custody is hers before others in early childhood for most jurists, and she has her three cases in inheritance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
