## The general rule
The reader knows that headache, like other ailments, requires that its cause be cut off and that it be met with its opposite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53} After that, among the things that help to remove headache are eating and drinking little, especially of the drink (sharab), and sleeping much, though excess in eating little is harmful in hot headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53} In treating the material kinds one must strive to draw the matters downward, and the strength must be supported so that it can evacuate from the region of the liver and the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53}

A regimen is reported from Filaghriyus that includes a little movement and leaving windy and slow-digesting foods, and also measures on the vessels and extremities that are not reproduced here; Ibn Sina adds an observation of his own {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53}

## What accompanies chronic headache and what opposes it
He says that sour foods do not suit those with headache, except for the headache that is by sharing with the stomach, where the food is of the kind that strengthens the mouth of the stomach and prevents bile pouring into it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.53-54} When chronic headache is accompanied by a troublesome symptom, direct your management toward it, for that symptom may be a cause of increase in the root on which it came, such as sleeplessness, which, when it comes because of the headache and grows strong, is one of the causes of the headache's increase {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.54} For every headache accompanied by catarrh, do not incline to cooling and moistening the head, but resort to evacuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.54} The pulsating headache, which the editor glosses as headache accompanied by throbbing and palpitation in the vessels of the head, may accompany hot and cold swellings; if the cause is hot, use coolers that have softness, and if it is cold, incline to what disperses and join with it what has strengthening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.54} Know that vomiting is not among the treatments of headache and does great harm to the one with headache, unless the headache is because of the stomach and by sharing with it, when vomiting helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.54}

## Intercourse (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
In listing what is to be left of everything that stirs, Ibn Sina names intercourse together with thought {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.53}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.53-54}
