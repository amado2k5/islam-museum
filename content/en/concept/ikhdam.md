## Definition
Ikhdam means, in the lexicon, giving a servant, and the jurists' usage keeps this meaning. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1972}
## General ruling
The giving of a servant may be made by a husband to his wife, if she is of a class that is served, or otherwise. The majority of jurists hold that a husband must provide a servant for his wife if she is of a class that is served, or maintain the servant she brings with her, since the purpose is achieved that way. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1972}
## Where the topic is treated
The jurists treat ikhdam in several places. A husband's providing a servant for his wife is treated under maintenance. Providing a servant for a bankrupt man for his needs, if he is chronically ill and needs a servant beyond his maintenance or if his rank requires one, is treated under bankruptcy, in the part on what is done with the property of one placed under restraint for insolvency. A servant for an imprisoned bankrupt is treated where the jurists discuss imprisoning the bankrupt until he confesses what he owes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1972}
