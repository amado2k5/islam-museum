## Overview
The Day of Resurrection carries many names in the Qur'an and Sunnah, each naming a face of it {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## The Qur'anic names
Among them: yawm al-qiyama [[passage/quran-2-85|the first mention]], yawm al-din [[passage/quran-1-4|Master of the day of religion]], yawm al-fasl [[passage/quran-37-21|the day of sorting]], yawm al-talaq [[passage/quran-40-15|the day of meeting]], yawm al-taghabun [[passage/quran-64-9|the day of mutual loss]], yawm al-hasra [[passage/quran-19-39|the day of remorse]], yawm al-jam' [[passage/quran-64-9|the day of gathering]], yawm al-wa'id [[passage/quran-50-20|the day of the threat]]. And the names born of its terror: al-Haqqa, al-Qari'a, al-Sakhkha, al-Ghashiya, al-Azifa and al-Waqi'a {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani}.
