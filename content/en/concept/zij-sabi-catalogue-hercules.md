## Hercules (al-Jathi)

The catalogue names the figure al-Jathi, the one kneeling, and lists its stars from the head down through the shoulders, arms, elbows and wrists to the knees. The notes after the list compare the magnitudes with other catalogues. The positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.148}
