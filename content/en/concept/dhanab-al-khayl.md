## Nature
A plant that grows in pits and ditches, with hollow stalks tending to red, rough and hard, knotted with interlocking joints, and at the joints like the leaves of idhkhir, thin and dense, clinging to what is near the trees, then many ends hang from it like the tail of a horse, and it has a hard root {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Actions and properties
Astringent, and its sap especially strongly drying without sting, very beneficial for bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Wounds and ulcers
It heals ulcers and wounds wonderfully, and even if a nerve is in them it heals them also {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Joints
Applied or laid as a poultice it also benefits bruising of the middle of the muscles, and it shrinks hernia of the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.719}

## Organs of nourishment
It benefits swellings of the stomach and liver, and dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.720}
