## Nature and selection
The best is white, pearly in colour, and pure {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and not strongly dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Actions and properties
A fine cleanser, dissolving and calming pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Joints
Good for pains of the flank and the back {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Chest and breathing
Helpful for fainting, drunk with honey water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.567}.

## Excretory organs
Helpful for colic and for stone in the kidney and bladder, and for the back drunk with honey water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.
