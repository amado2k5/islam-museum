## Nature
It is close in power to sadhinj, indeed stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.593}.

## Temperament
Cold and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.593}.

## Actions and properties
It is astringent, with something of the cooled white lead but much subtler, and it checks hemorrhages {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.593}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It is laid with a cerate on a burn from fire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.593}.

## Excretory organs
It stops the flow of blood strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.593}.
