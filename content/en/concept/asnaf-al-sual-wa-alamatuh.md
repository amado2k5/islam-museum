## The cough's kinds and signs
Something stony like a chickpea may come out of the cough, its cause a thick humour that the heat petrifies in it; Alexander has testified to it and Fulus has testified to it and mentioned that this kind came out in the expectoration, and we too have witnessed that (a testimony transmitted on the book's saying) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.328}
