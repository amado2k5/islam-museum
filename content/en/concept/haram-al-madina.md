## Overview
The sanctuary of Medina is the area that the Prophet declared sacred around [[place/medina|the city of Medina]], as Abraham declared Mecca sacred. It has known boundaries in the reports. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.92-118}

## Boundaries
The reports say that its boundary is "between Ayr and Thawr," or "up to Uhud" in most of the reports al-Samhudi transmits, and that it lies "between its two lava fields," that is, its two basalt plains to the east and west. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.92-118}

## The rulings mentioned
Al-Samhudi notes that hunting and cutting trees are forbidden within it except where excepted; that the blood-money for accidental killing there is made heavier; and that introducing innovations there, or sheltering one who introduces them, is forbidden. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.92-118}

## In the Sahih
Al-Bukhari relates that "Medina is a sanctuary from such a place to such a place; its trees may not be cut and no innovation may be introduced in it." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1867} In another report, "what lies between its two lava fields is made sacred." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1869} In the document of Ali it is said that "the protection of the Muslims is one." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 1870}

## The fire of 654
Al-Samhudi holds that the blessing of the sanctification turned away from the city a fire in the year 654, when it reached its sacred area. This is al-Samhudi's opinion. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.92-118}
