## The treatment of the voice's cutting-off
The chapter's rule is hastening the treatment before the harm settles, then the parting of the kind arising from a relaxing moisture with no pain and the kind arising from dryness — its mark the smallness with huskiness and no greatness (all the draughts, lohochs, foods, broths and powders named there with their names and measures are excluded per the register's rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.324}
