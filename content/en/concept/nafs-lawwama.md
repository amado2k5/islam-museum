## Summary
Sha'rawi speaks of three states of the soul in the context of God's law and people's drift from it: the self-reproaching soul, the soul that commands evil, and the community that turns a person back to what is right {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1525}.

## The self-reproaching soul
Sha'rawi says a person may be heedless of one part of the law and commit a wrong. His soul then wakes and reproaches him for leaving it, and he returns in repentance and seeks forgiveness. He explains this by a "faith-cell" in the soul that alerts its owner {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1525}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1526}.

## The soul that commands evil
When the self-reproaching soul does not do its work, the soul commands evil and inclines toward deviation. Around each soul there are other forces from the environment and society that try to set the crooked straight. If the community includes someone who guides a person to what is right, the drift does not close in on him {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1526}.

## General corruption
Sha'rawi asks what happens if all members of a society have come to command evil. His answer is that darkness then spreads, and God sends a messenger to rekindle the light of faith. He notes that God has promised the community of Muhammad that no prophet will come after him, so good remains in it through the self-reproaching soul and a believing community together {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1526}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1567}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh holds that when a person notices that his soul commands him to evil, such as sleeping through the night and dawn prayers, withholding zakat and harming people, and treats it by blaming it, it rises from the soul that commands evil to the self-reproaching soul, and that continued blame, rightly done, with a programme of reform raises it to the tranquil soul. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep10 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep10 00:04:00} See: [[concept/maratib-al-nafs|the degrees of the human soul]].
