## Nature
This is the earth of all places. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Temperament
All of it is cooling. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Properties
It dries and cleanses. The hot earth of sunny ground dries loose bodies without burning them, when it is not mixed with the burnt kind, as in potsherds and walls baked in the sun; it has a dissolving power. Washed again, it becomes a moderate drier, mild in heat and cold. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Adornment
It firms loose flesh. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Swellings and pustules
With a plaster, for swine-like and hard swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Organs of digestion
Those with dropsy and those with spleen trouble who are smeared with the earth of sunny ground benefit clearly, and it heals fleshy growths greatly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}
