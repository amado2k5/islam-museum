## The filling and the bursting
And some people lay on the chest and its sides a linen rag soaked in red clay diluted in water, and mark the spot that dries first — that is the place of the pus (a hand-work on the book's wording, which the author reports of people) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.353}
