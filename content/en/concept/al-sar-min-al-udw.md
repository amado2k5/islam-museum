## Vapour rising from an organ
Epilepsy whose cause lies in another organ comes either from vapours and harmful winds rising from it to the brain by quantity, gathering on their way up and then condensing into a matter of substance that acts by its substance or by the wind formed from it; or from a vapour or wind that harms not by quantity but by quality — by chilling, by burning, or by poisonousness and bad substance; or from a bare quality rising to it; or from what harms in both ways. The organ from which vapours rise in epileptic quantity is either the whole body, the stomach, or the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.120}

## Poison carried by way of a nerve
The cause may be blood or a humour held in an outlet where an obstruction has occurred, sending out periodically a vaporous matter or a poisonous quality, or some poison may fall on it and act on the nerve as a scorpion's sting acts on a nerve, so that its poisonousness is driven by way of the nerve to the brain and hurts it; the brain then contracts from it and convulses and its motions are disturbed, as happens to the stomach when something with a sting is taken on an empty stomach — and hiccup is a kind of convulsion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.120}

## Rising from the limbs
Galen and others related, and Ibn Sina saw after him, that often the epileptic would feel something rising from the big toe of his foot like a cold wind making for his brain, and when it reached his heart and brain he was seized; Galen said that if his leg was bound with a strong ligature before the fit it was prevented. Epilepsy beginning from a vertebra and epilepsy beginning from the shoulder have also been reported {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.121}

## Womb, semen and pregnancy (labelled listing, held for the founder's ruling)
A kind of epilepsy is compounded with syncope and physicians nearly take it out of the class of epilepsy, though it belongs there; a kind of it and of its family is called suffocation of the womb; the book records retention of the menses and retention of semen turning in the womb to a poisonous quality whose vapour rises to the heart, the like occurring for a man from semen gathering in its vessels and turning, and epilepsy occurring to a woman in pregnancy which ceases when she delivers and the bad menstrual matter is evacuated {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.121}

## From the stomach and the hypochondrium
It may be from the stomach and the hypochondrium through indigestions that leave obstructions in the vessels, so they do not receive the wholesome food and the humour corrupts in them, or the wholesome food stays choked by the obstructions and corrupts, and often returns corrupted to the stomach and corrupts the new wholesome chyme; vomiting of undigested food often follows from that; epilepsy may also come about on account of worms {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.121}
