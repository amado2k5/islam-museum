## From the stomach
If its cause is from the stomach, it is with loss of appetite or an affliction and spoiling of digestion, a palpitation and a slackening of the breath, a turning of the stomach, and an inclination of the harm to the front of the head, and the state of the pain varies, quieting at times and increasing at others with fullness and emptiness, and it comes from a fever that has passed; he also finds a pain in the stomach and a distension at times, the way of its sharing being the nerve, and he finds before it, and at its peak toward its end, a pain behind the fontanelle at the root of the sixth pair and in the regions of the nape {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.115}

## From the liver, the heart and all the organs
If the start is from all the organs, or from the source of food, the liver, or the source of the pneuma, the heart, its passage is in the veins and arteries that spring from them; as for what is behind the ear or in the nape, its sign is that it comes with a strong throbbing and tension of the vessels in the neck, though he finds no affliction in the neck and its nerves or in the rest of the nerve; the book describes a test for this, and it is not reproduced here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.116}

## From a changing bad temperament
What comes from a changing bad temperament is known by the lightness of the brain, the absence of the causes mentioned, and the falling of a sudden cold or heat from outside or from things taken that cool or warm all at once, and vertigo follows it; the one with sadar does not gain from the drink (sharab) as he gains from drinking water; know that when sadar and dawar last, the illness is 'badira' (as printed, not interpreted) and the sign of a crisis is evident {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.116}

## Womb and bladder (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina says that if it is from the womb, suffocation of the womb, holding back of sperm or of menstruation, or swellings in it come before it, and likewise if it is from the bladder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.116}
