## Summary
Ibn Sina says the child's management is moistening and ample moderate exercise, that it should not be made to walk or sit before it moves toward it naturally, that it be protected from harm, and how its gums are tended when the front teeth and canines come, with oil, honey, salt and liquorice root {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.210-211}.

## Moistening and exercise
The child's management is moistening, because its temperament suits that and it needs it for feeding and growth, together with plentiful moderate exercise. This is as if natural to children, as though nature itself demands it of them, above all once they pass from infancy into boyhood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.

## Sitting and moving
When it begins to stand and move it should not be allowed violent motion, nor may it be made to walk or sit before it is moved toward it naturally, or an affliction will strike its legs and back. When it first sits and creeps on the ground, its seat should be on a smooth leather mat so the roughness of the ground does not scratch it, wood, knives and anything that pricks or cuts are kept from its face, and it is guarded from slipping from a height {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.

## Canines and teeth
When the canines begin to split through, children are kept from anything hard to chew, so the matter from which the canines form is not dissolved by the chewing they are fond of; their gums are then anointed with rabbit's brain, their heads and necks with washed oil beaten with hot water, and some oil is dropped in their ears {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.210-211}. When the child can bite with them it is given a piece of liquorice root not yet very dry, or its rob, which helps with the sores and pains of the gums, and its mouth is rubbed with salt and honey so that these pains do not befall it; anointing the neck with sweet oil at the time the canines grow also suits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.211}. When they begin to speak the care of the roots of their teeth is kept up {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.211}.
