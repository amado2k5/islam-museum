## Summary
Al-Battani says that whoever wants to turn from the mean days that come out of the tables to the unequal days found does the opposite: the excess is added to the mean days if the number of times is the smaller, and taken from them if the number of times is the larger; what the mean days come to after the addition or subtraction is what is turned into the unequal days found {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.

## The working principle
He says that on the principle he laid down in his book, from the assumed place of the Sun, the number of times is always the smaller, until a long span of time has passed {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}. This remark gives the usual case in his day: that addition is the ordinary step and subtraction the exception, and it explains why the instruction begins with the addition when turning from mean days to unequal ones {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}. It is the counterpart of [[concept/zij-sabi-converting-unequal-days-to-mean-days|the rule for turning unequal days into mean days]].
