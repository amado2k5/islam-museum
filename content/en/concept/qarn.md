## Organs of the head
The burnt horn of the stag and the goat cleans the teeth strongly, firms the gums and calms their stirred pain; it must be burnt until it whitens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Ocular organs
The burnt, whitened horn of the stag, like washed salt, keeps matters from the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Respiratory organs
The burnt and washed horn of the stag benefits the spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It does not harm the stomach and benefits jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}

## Excretory organs
The burnt and washed horn of the stag benefits dysentery {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.658}
