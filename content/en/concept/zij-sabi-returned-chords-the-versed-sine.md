## Summary
Al-Battani defines the returned chord by a working rule: if the number whose returned chord is wanted is less than ninety degrees, it is subtracted from ninety, the chord of the remainder is found and subtracted from sixty, giving the returned chord; if more than ninety, the chord of the excess over ninety is found and added to sixty. He then gives the reverse rule to find the returned arc from its chord. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.20-21}.

## The returned chord from the arc
To get the returned chord from an arc: if the number is less than ninety degrees, subtract it from ninety, find the chord of the remainder by the rule before, then subtract that from sixty, the radius, and what remains is the returned chord of that arc. If the number is above ninety, find the chord of its excess over ninety and add it to sixty, and the total is the returned chord. In later terms this is the versed sine. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.20}.

## The returned arc from the chord
To find the returned arcs from these chords: if the chord whose arc you want is less than sixty, subtract it from sixty, find the arc of the remainder by the rule given, and subtract that arc from ninety, and what is left is the returned arc. If the chord is above sixty, drop sixty from it, find its arc and add it to ninety, and the total is the returned arc. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.21}.
