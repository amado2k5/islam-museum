## Summary
The single inequality of the Moon reaches at most five parts and one minute. The radius of the epicycle that matches it is five and a quarter parts against sixty for the radius, the ratio Ptolemy gives. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.

## What the ratio means
The ratio of sixty to five and a quarter means the epicycle's radius is small compared with the eccentric's radius, and from it the largest equation at conjunctions and oppositions is found. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.
