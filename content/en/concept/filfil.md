## Nature
Galen said that when its fruit first comes out it is long pepper, and then the grain of pepper separates from it, so that long pepper is moister and so eats and stings a little after its first taste. Its root resembles black costus and is more pungent, and the white is weaker in heat and moisture; some say that the black has dried so that its drawing power has fallen away and remains in the white, which has not reached extreme dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Actions and properties
It has drawing, dissolving and cleansing; chewed with raisins it removes phlegm, it uproots viscous phlegm, it is among what soothe pain, calms the nerve and suits the healthy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Cosmetics
With natron it clears vitiligo, and with natron it thins the body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Swellings and pustules
With pitch it dissolves scrofula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Joints
It warms the nerves and muscles with a warming that nothing else equals {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Organs of the head
With vinegar it benefits the teeth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Ocular organs
The white enters eye salves and clears {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Organs of the chest
Used in linctuses it suits cough and pains of the chest; with honey, rubbed on the palate, it is useful against quinsy, and it cleanses the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It aids digestion and stimulates appetite; drunk with fresh bay leaf it benefits wind and colic; with vinegar, drunk and applied, it is good for swelling of the spleen; the white is better for the stomach and strengthens it more, and long pepper brings the food down with ease {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Excretory organs
It promotes urine; much of it and little of it loosen, unlike scammony {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as bringing down the foetus, as strongly drying the semen, as strongly spoiling the seed after intercourse, and says that long pepper increases desire by its surplus moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Fevers
Rubbed on with oil it benefits shivering {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Poisons and bites
The white enters theriacs, and long pepper too is useful for the bites of vermin, also applied with oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}
