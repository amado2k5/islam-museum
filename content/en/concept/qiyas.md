## Overview
Qiyas in usul usage is extending a root case's ruling to a branch case for a shared cause; al-Shafi'i counts it among the proofs with the Book, the Sunna and ijma' {cite:risala-shafii}.

## In the sources
Al-Shafi'i devotes chapters of the Risala to qiyas, defining it and its conditions, and holding that knowledge proceeds by the Book, the Sunna, ijma' and qiyas {cite:risala-shafii}.
