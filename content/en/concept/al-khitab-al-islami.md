# The contemporary Islamic discourse — maturity versus reactivity

## The episode and the definition
The second 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode debated the contemporary Islamic discourse: mature or reactive? {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:01:00} It proceeded from the International Islamic Fiqh Academy's definition: 'the manner of expression conveying Islam's truths and laws' — the question concerns the conveyers, not the text {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:04:30}. The audience split almost evenly: 51% reactive, 49% mature {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:03:30}.

## Marks of the defect — method, reality, tradition, reaction
Salah al-Din al-Jurshi compressed the content problems into four: the method problem — 'starting from the conclusion, ruling pre-decided without foundations'; the gap between discourse and reality — the anecdote of a shaykh lecturing on divorce to bachelor conscripts; traditionalism — litigating questions of the Murji'a and Kharijites the age has passed, and the anecdote of a Colorado mosque lesson on the Qadianis whom nobody there had ever met; and dependence on reaction — events drag the discourse (Denmark, then the Pope) so no cumulative knowledge forms {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:18:00}.

## Self-criticism or a voice of optimism
Mahmoud Rashdan defended maturity: authorities like al-Qaradawi, Muhammad al-Ghazali and Abd al-Alim outweigh satellite loudmouths; and the Islamic movements practise democracy internally more than parties bearing its name {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:21:30}. He insisted the problem is alive and being addressed — 'the power of renewal in it exceeds the power of imitation and rigidity' — and every discourse, Western or nationalist, has its flaws {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:41:30}. The host noted Rashdan's habitual optimism {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:41:30}.

## The mosque and official discourse
Al-Jurshi called the mosque discourse 'mined', needing radical revision: excessive politicisation — imams appointed by governments to glorify their policies, or movements using it to justify their choices — and a historical mismatch: mosques built for homogeneous communities now facing pluralist ones {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:43:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:43:30}. Rashdan argued most inflammatory pulpits are 'imposed by the regimes' on the mosques {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:40:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:40:30}. Ma'mun Jarrar noted sharia colleges now admit the weakest-scoring students and so fail to produce great jurists and callers as before {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:53:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:54:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:54:30}.

## External pressure or internal necessity
The audience split: 51% saw the revision as an internal necessity, 49% as a response to outside pressure {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:47:30}. Sayyid al-Fayiz said external pressure 'opened horizons' in political discourse — dialogue, participation, pluralism, women's rights — while the rule remains that revision should be ours {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:52:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:52:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:53:00}. Mahmoud Sayyid Daghim held the discourse's growth reflects Muslims' deepening understanding of their religion {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:50:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:51:00}.

## One discourse or two
76% endorsed two different discourses for inside and outside — a result al-Jurshi found astonishing, warning against duplicity and tutelage over Western Muslims {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:56:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:57:30}. Khaled Hroub supported unity with two examples: pulpits cursing Jews and Christians while we preach love abroad, and the remote-village sermon now translated and broadcast {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:59:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 00:59:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 01:00:30}. Al-Fayiz detailed: content differs by addressee {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 01:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 01:02:30}. The host concluded: 'Islam is one with levels — as the Prophet sent Mu'adh to begin with tawhid' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 01:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep02 01:05:30}.
