## Nature and selection
The editor's note, citing al-Mu'tamad fi al-Adwiya al-Mufrada, gives it as the fruit of the elm; the identification is taken from the note alone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree and moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Actions and properties
Its leaf has astringency, cleansing and joining {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Its leaf heals and joins wet ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Joints
Its bark with vinegar is for bruising of the muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it increases desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.541}.
