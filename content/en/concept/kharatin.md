## Temperament
It must, as I estimate, be hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Ulcers
Wounds of the nerves are covered with its pounded form as a poultice and it is then very beneficial; the time the poultice stays on is not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}

## Organs of the head
Its decoction with goose fat benefits earache, and it may be dropped in with oil on the side opposite the aching tooth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}

## Organs of nourishment
It cures jaundice when drunk with boiled grape must (tila') {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}

## Excretory organs
Given with boiled grape must it promotes urine, and that also benefits stone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}
