## Summary
For Ibn Sina, what acts by its quality is that which, once in the human body, tends to grow hot or cold, so that it heats by its heat and cools by its cold without being assimilated to the body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.131}. On the next page he adds that for what acts by quality, its matter has no part in the action {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.132}.
