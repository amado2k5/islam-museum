## The snuff and the dressing
Among its treatments is to snuff and gargle with a drug of the known kind, used as snuff and otherwise for days; and among what it is treated with is to place in the nose a dressing with wax and an oil of the known kind, continually used till it heals ⁦(the drug, paste and oil names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.248}
