## Summary
Nuqra, in the lexicon, is a piece of gold or silver melted down. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15973}

## Rulings on nuqra
Zakat is due once it reaches the threshold and is combined with coins of the same metal. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15973}
Selling it for coin of its kind, or coin for it, follows the rules of exchange. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15973} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15974}
Partnership with nuqra as capital is valid for the Hanafis, Malikis and a Hanbali view if it circulates like coin; disallowed by the Shafi'i rival view and the Hanbali school. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15974}
