## Summary
To find the Moon's true place on the zodiac at any time, one takes the mean Sun, the mean Moon and the Moon's anomaly for the day and hour sought. The mean Sun is subtracted from the mean Moon, the remainder is the Moon's mean elongation, and the tables then give the equation. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.111}.

## Entering the anomaly and the elongation
The anomaly is entered in the two rows of numbers of the lunar equation tables; its counterpart is taken in the third table and the matching minutes in the fourth, each carried separately. What the third table gives is the equation of the anomaly. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.112}.
