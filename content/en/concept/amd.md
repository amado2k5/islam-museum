## Summary
In the lexicon, amd is the purpose and intention of a thing; its opposite is error (khata'), an act done without purpose {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11814}.

## In prayer and fasting
A deliberate omission of a pillar of prayer, or speech or eating in it, voids the prayer; a deliberate eating, drinking or intercourse breaks the fast by agreement {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11814} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11815}.

## Homicide, apostasy and divorce
Deliberate homicide is forbidden by agreement; the acceptance of the killer's repentance is disputed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11815}. A deliberate utterance of unbelief is apostasy, and a deliberate divorce takes effect {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11815} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11816}.

## Lying about the Prophet and oaths
Deliberate lying about the Prophet is among the gravest sins; the false oath is a major sin; breaking an oath deliberately falls under the five rulings {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11816} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11817}.

## Where to look further
Oaths are treated under «aymān», apostasy under «ridda», and retaliation under «qisas» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11817}.
