## Nature and selection
Nature: Dioscorides says some call it miyan, some asfaraʿs, and some mawqaniyus. Temperament: Galen says it is moderate, with no clear heating or cooling except the rocky kind, which is not far from heat. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.449}

## Actions
Its power cleanses and opens the blockages of all the viscera, especially the liver and kidney; it has a resolving quality, especially the rocky kind. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}

## Uses by organ
Joints: its decoction is drunk for back pain and sciatica. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
Head: its root boiled with vinegar, and the root itself and its seed, are good for toothache. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
Digestive organs: opens blockages of the liver, helps jaundice, and causes nausea. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
Excretory organs, book listing: Rufus claimed it binds the bowels; most say it helps phlegmatic and windy colic, its root decoction drives urine and helps difficult urination, increases semen and desire, helps difficult conception, and its seed when taken brings on the menses, and it opens blockages of the kidneys. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
Poisons: boiled with wine it helps the bite of a tarantula; it is said that asparagus decoction kills dogs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.450}
