## Summary
The Prophet forbade killing four kinds of creatures, the ant, the bee, the hoopoe and the shrike, according to a report in Abu Dawud from Ibn Abbas, with a sound chain as the jurists describe it {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## Significance
Jurists cite this prohibition in the chapters on hunting and slaughter, and they debate whether its reason is respect for the creature or simply the prohibition of killing what does no harm. The ant appears in the Quran in Surat al-Naml, where the valley of the ants is mentioned with Solomon, a passage recorded here by reference only [[passage/quran-27-18|Surat al-Naml, verse 18]].
