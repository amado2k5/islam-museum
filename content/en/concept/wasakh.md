## Nature and selection
Nature: furnace soot heats at the end of the second degree, the green is best; bath-wall soot heats moderately; wrestlers' grime is close to bath grime, and has two kinds: one gathers on their bodies after oiling mixed with dust, and the other on the walls from vapours and on the arena floor. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}

## Actions
Actions: both resolve and ripen gently; furnace soot cleanses gently and draws strongly; all of it draws out splinters and thorns. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: resolves abscesses; wrestlers' grime is good for breast swellings, and bath grime for blistering. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}
Wounds and ulcers: arena-wall grime is for old ulcers and head wounds; furnace soot clears ringworm greatly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}
Joints: wrestlers' grime helps sciatica when placed warm on the ointment, and helps hardening of the finger joints. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}
