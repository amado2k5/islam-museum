## Summary
'The faults of the tongue' is the title of the series Omar Abdelkafy gave in Nafahat Ramadaniyya, each episode on one fault. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep03 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:00:00} In the last episode he lists the faults treated so far: backbiting, talebearing, wrangling, disputation, ingratitude for blessings, harm, lying, mockery, reviling the Companions and calling on other than God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:00:00} He says at the close that these were thirty episodes, quick headings for a large subject (needs_source: the archive holds only six episodes of this series). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:10:30}

## The faults treated in the surviving episodes
- [[concept/mann|Reminding people of one's gifts (mann)]], which he calls a major fault of the tongue. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep01 00:00:00}
- Turning in supplication to other than God, which he counts as a form of associating partners with God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep02 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep02 00:12:00}
- [[concept/al-dua-ala-al-nafs-wal-awlad|Praying against oneself and one's children]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep03 00:00:00}
- [[concept/sabb-al-sahaba|Reviling the Companions]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:30}
- Failing to [[concept/satr|cover a Muslim's fault]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:00:30}

## The remedy, as he sees it
He holds that many of these faults are major sins, and that the first remedy is to hold God's sanctities in awe, then to reflect on the Qur'an's praise of those who remember God and use the tongue for good. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:01:30} Among the means of cure he lists: remembering death and [[concept/tul-al-amal|cutting short one's hopes]], {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:02:30} much prayer, night prayer and supplication before dawn, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:04:30} training oneself in silence for set hours of reflection, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:05:00} the company of people of knowledge and piety, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:05:30} seeking refuge from Satan, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:07:00} being busy with one's own faults rather than other people's, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:07:30} and many acts of obedience such as fasting Mondays and Thursdays and reading the Qur'an, and addressing one's tongue each day and bidding it fear God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:09:00}

## Knowing one's own faults
He says a person learns his own faults from four things: by presenting himself to a teacher learned in the Book and the Sunna and in people's hearts, to the Book of God, to the righteous brother who warns him, and from the tongues of his enemies, who may be right about one or two traits; he adds that what one finds ugly in others one should not do oneself. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:08:30}

## The fruit of guarding the tongue
He holds that if people held their tongues, societies would be set right and souls made clear; this is his view at the close of the series. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:10:30}
