## Summary
Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi holds that the Quran differs from earlier revelations in that its method and its miracle are one. Earlier methods were sent with the intention that they would be changed, and the miracles of earlier messengers were sensory and ended with those who saw them. The Quran was sent to last until the Day of Resurrection. This is his view and is attributed to him; other views are not yet presented. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.16} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.17}

## Method and miracle
Sha'rawi notes that the Torah was Moses' method with the staff as his miracle, and the Gospel was Jesus' method with the healing of the blind and the leper as his miracle. In his reading they were separate. The Quran joins them, so every follower can say that Muhammad is the messenger of God and that this is his miracle. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.17}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's view and reasoning, and it is attributed to him here. The views of other commentators and of the sciences of the Quran are not yet presented.
