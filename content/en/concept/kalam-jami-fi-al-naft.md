## A comprehensive discourse on the expectoration
In sum the expectoration indicates by its colour, by its consistency of thickness and thinness, by its shape of roundedness or otherwise, and by its measure in abundance and littleness (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.349}
