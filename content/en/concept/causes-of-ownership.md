## Overview
The author presents the grounds of ownership in the second chapter, beginning with prohibited means as a reminder that ownership cannot rest on an unlawful path {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 53}.

## Classification
The author draws on Ibn Juzayy's division of earnings into those without consideration and those with consideration {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 54} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 55}, and then explains that some grounds of ownership occur compulsorily and others voluntarily {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 54}.
