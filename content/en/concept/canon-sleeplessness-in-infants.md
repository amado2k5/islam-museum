## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions a child who does not sleep and keeps crying and murmuring so that putting it to sleep becomes necessary, treated with poppy husks and seed, lettuce oil and poppy oil on the temple, or a paste of many seeds with sugar, to which a little opium may be added {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.218}.

## The state
A child may not sleep, keep crying and murmuring, and there is a pressing need to put it to sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.218}.

## What makes it sleep
If possible it is put to sleep with poppy husks and seed, and with lettuce oil and poppy oil placed on its temple {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.218}. Otherwise sumana grain, kandam nut, white and yellow poppy, flaxseed, khuri grain, 'arfaj seed, plantain seed, lettuce seed, fennel seed, anise and cumin are taken, all boiled a little at a time and pounded, with one part of fried, unpounded psyllium seed added, the whole mixed with an equal weight of sugar, and the child given about two dirhams of it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.218}.

## A stronger form
If it is wanted stronger, some opium is put in it, about a third of a part or less {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.218}.
