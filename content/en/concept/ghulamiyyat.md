## Overview
The *ghulamiyyat* were close-cropped slave girls whom Umm Ja'far Zubayda kept, and people named them thus, according to al-Masudi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.671-672}

## The report
Zubayda saw her son al-Amin's passion for servants, so she had the girls' heads shaved and gave them forelocks, sideburns, and napes. She dressed them in tunics, shirts, and belts until they were mistaken for young men. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.671-672}

Then people imitated her, and called them the *ghulamiyyat*. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.671-672}
