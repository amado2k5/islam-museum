## Overview

Jahr (audible) and sirr (silent) in worship concern how loudly words are said, and the schools differ on the limits of each. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6639}

## Voice in prayer

The Malikis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis say the imam recites aloud in audible prayers; the Hanafis let him choose between loud and quiet. Most schools make silent seeking of refuge the sunna. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6640} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6642} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6643}

## Other places

The imam says the response to praise aloud, and jurists differ on whether a lone worshipper says the salutation aloud. Raising the voice in the sermon is recommended. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6644} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6646} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6653}
