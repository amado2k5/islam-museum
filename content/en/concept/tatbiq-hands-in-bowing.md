## Overview
Tatbiq is the worshipper placing the inner side of one palm on the inner side of the other and setting them between his knees and thighs in bowing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5305}

## Ruling
Most jurists dislike it, citing the report of Musab ibn Sad who prayed beside his father, joined his palms and was forbidden by him: «we used to do it and were forbidden, and we were ordered to place our hands on the knees». {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5306}

## Those who held it the sunna
Ibn Masud and his companions Alqama and al-Aswad held tatbiq to be the sunna; al-Nakha'i and Abu Ubayda took it up, and an-Nawawi explains that the abrogation had not reached them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5306}
