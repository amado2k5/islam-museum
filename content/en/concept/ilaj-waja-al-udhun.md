## The rule of dropping and evacuation
The rule to keep for whatever is dropped in the ear: that it be neither very hot nor very cold. If the cause is repletion in body or head, the head region is evacuated of that kind of repletion — if hot, by venesection and the evacuation by head-cleansers of the hot matter as known; if a sticky viscid humour, by the known means; if viscid matter settled in the ear region, then after the purging softening vapours and softening drops are used, then evacuation of the organ is sought again ⁦(drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.223}
## The hot, the cold and the windy
If the cause is extreme heat, the brain is cooled by the known extinguishers named in the brain's chapter, and the known drops are instilled; if throbbing and pain grow severe and convulsion is feared, relaxants are not persisted in — sometimes inserting a tube in the ear fitted onto a vessel of hot water so the vapour reaches the ear suffices, quieting it and sparing the narcotics; if a narcotic is needed, the known kind is used ⁦(drug and narcotic names are not carried; the milk line is a labelled listing for the founder's ruling)⁩. If the cause is a settled cold deep within or from outside, the drops are the hot oils; such a patient may drink a strong pure wine, sleep and wake with nothing wrong with him. If the cause is a cold wind, what is said in the ringing and buzzing chapter helps, and what was said for the viscid-humour and cold causes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.223} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.224}
## The deep-lying swelling, pustules and the narcotics
If the cause is pustules, what is said in the chapter on ear pustules; if worms, the chapter on worms breeding in the ear; if water or a pebble entering, what is said there. If a deep-lying hot swelling — a hazard for its nearness to the brain until it gathers and suppurates — then after venesection and evacuation the cooling lenitives are used first to the third day, then always fomentation with lukewarm oil in which a cotton wrapped on a fine probe is dipped and set in the ear time after time, with ripening lenitives dressed outside; when past the beginning, if not very strong, the known drops are used; if it turns to pus, what is said in its chapter. A swelling outside the ear is little danger and treated with the known ⁦(oil, fat, drug, ointment and poultice names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.224}
## The need for narcotics
Narcotics may be needed for severe earaches; that is delayed until fainting is feared, especially with cold humours for which it is very harmful; if harm occurs from narcotics, the known is used alone after; if there is a very painful ulcer, suppuration-hasteners and the known are used ⁦(narcotic, tablet and drug names and their measures are not carried; the woman's-milk lines are labelled listings for the founder's ruling)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.225}
