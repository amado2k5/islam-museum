## Its causes and its warning
It occurs for fine materials and vapours and for weakness and badness of digestion, as in sleeplessness and the sleepless fevers; it may be in the beginnings of dropsy and bad cachexia and for moist swellings like pneumonia and lethargus. When it occurs in convalescents it warns much of relapse, especially if wasting of the other organs surrounds it while the lids stay irritated and swollen. The treatment is cutting the cause and fomentation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.194}
