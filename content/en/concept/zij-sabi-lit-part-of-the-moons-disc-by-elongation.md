## Summary
Al-Battani measures the lit part of the Moon's disc seen from the Earth in parts of fifteen, so that full light is fifteen. He gives two ratios for how much elongation adds one part of light, and one of them is damaged in the scan. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.94}.

## The method
He counts the part of the lit disc that falls within the half facing the Earth, takes what is seen of it, and matches it against the elongation from the Sun. The exact figures at the end of the chapter are unreadable and are not recorded. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.93}.
