## The frequent breath
The rule for parting the one that comes from need and the one that comes from pain went before in the great-breath chapter; what this chapter adds is that Hippocrates' testimony makes it harmful, for the drying of the lung and the tiring of the breath's organs it entails (attributed on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.311}
