## Regimen, lying and the bursting
When the ulcer is in the right eye he sleeps on the left, and in the left on the right; his regimen must first be gentle; when the ulcer bursts the treatment turns to the extremities and the thighs, lest his strength weaken, the ulcer fail to close and his body's superfluities multiply — he must not be replete, must not sneeze so far as possible, and enters no bath before the disease ripens, and if he enters he must not stay long {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.178}

## Cleansing the head and the canon of drugs
The mainstay is cleansing the head by evacuations drawing downward; much cupping on the shank and venesection of the saphenous also help, with looseness of the bowels kept up every four days by what expels the thin hot superfluity ⁦(the venesection and cupping sites and the names of the decoctions and infusions are not carried)⁩; if there is ramad it is treated first by the evacuation given in its chapter, with drugs joining pain relief to ulcer work — such as women's milk in the eye; if there is flow, what has checking power is mixed in; in sum the canon of drug choice here is to choose all that dries without biting; if the ulcers carry filth it is cleansed with the cleansers; strong eating compels the strengthener; and once the ulcer is cleansed one turns to the dryers without biting ⁦(collyrium names, recipes, measures and the mineral and animal parts are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.178}
