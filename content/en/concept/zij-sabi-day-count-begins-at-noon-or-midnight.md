## Summary
Al-Battani says the start from the horizon circle changes and varies from place to place with the ascensions of the signs there, while the start from midday stays in one state through the evenness of the rising of the signs at the meridian circle in every land; therefore the beginning of the days in reckoning the planets and setting their places is made neither from sunrise nor sunset but from midday or midnight {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.

## A second reason
He adds a second reason: the motions set in the tables for the planets were set on mean days equal in time, so that what gathers between the days with their unequal nights and those with their mean nights must be worked out {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}. With these two reasons he justifies the rule of conversion in [[concept/zij-sabi-converting-unequal-days-to-mean-days|turning unequal days into mean days]] {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.
