## The routes
Some are made into a suppository-form with what he described and set on the aching tooth, some dripped into the ear on the aching side, some pastes held in the mouth, some fumed in the throat, and some given to drink so the sufferer sleeps on it (names and recipes left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.273}
