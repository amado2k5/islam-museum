## Nature
It is a manna that falls on the ushar, like pieces of salt, with a little astringency and bitterness along with sweetness; one kind is Yemeni and white, another Hijazi and tending to black {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Actions and properties
Cleansing, with astringency in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Organs of the eye
Sukkar al-ushar sharpens sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Organs of the chest
It is useful for the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is useful for dropsy with the milk of she-camels, and does not cause thirst like the other kinds of sugar because its sweetness is slight; it is good for the stomach and liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Excretory organs
It helps the kidneys and bladder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.
