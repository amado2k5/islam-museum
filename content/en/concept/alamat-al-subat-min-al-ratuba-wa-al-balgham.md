## Simple moisture
If the coma is from simple moisture, its sign is that the signs of blood are not seen, nor the heaviness of phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}

## Phlegm
As for that from phlegm, it is known from fullness and indigestion having come before, much drinking, softness of the pulse and waviness with breadth, from the depth and heaviness of the coma, whiteness of colour in the face, the eye and the tongue, heaviness of the head, a flush in the eyelids, coldness to the touch, the earlier regimen, the age, the country and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}
