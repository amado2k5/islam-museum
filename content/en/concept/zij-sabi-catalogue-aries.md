## Aries (al-Hamal)

The catalogue opens its zodiac group, headed Constellationes zodiaci, with Aries, al-Hamal, the ram. Its stars are listed from the horns and mouth through the neck, back, tail and hind leg, followed by a group the catalogue marks as not in the figure, above the head. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.155}
