## Overview
Iddah is the waiting period a woman observes after divorce or widowhood; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats it across the marriage and divorce chapters {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia examines the term of the iddah, the supervening iddah, residence in the iddah home, and the effect of miscarriage on the iddah {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## For Scholars
Idda is the term a woman observes after divorce or widowhood; the Kuwaiti fiqh encyclopaedia treats it in the marriage and divorce chapters, covering the term's length, the interim idda, dwelling in the idda house, and the effect of miscarriage on it.
