## Summary
Nukha', in the lexicon, is the white vein inside the neck running along the spine to the coccyx; mukh is the brain or marrow, and nukha' is narrower; faqra is the vertebra that holds it. A prohibition on nakh' (cutting the spinal cord) in slaughter is related on the authority of Ibn Abbas, which al-Bayhaqi judged weak, and the jurists differ on its ruling; the detail is under slaughter. The jurists also treat head wounds that reach the spinal cord, such as the hashima and the munaqqila, and give their rulings under diyya (blood money). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15545} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15546}
