## Nature and selection
Well known; it is less oily than the walnut, though oily enough to turn rancid, and the walnut digests faster and turns to bitterness faster. The gum of the sweet almond, as some claim, is close to gum arabic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Temperament
The sweet is balanced in both, leaning slightly to moisture; the bitter is hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Actions and properties
The gum of the bitter almond binds and warms; all kinds have cleansing, purifying and opening power, though the sweet is far weaker than the bitter in opening. It is said to have no astringency at all and its nourishment is small. A property of the bitter is that it kills the fox; the bitter is a drug, not a food, while the sweet gives a little good nourishment, and almond oil is lighter in body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Cosmetic uses
The bitter helps freckles and skin spots and smooths a facial spasm; the root of the bitter, boiled and laid on freckles, is a strong remedy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Swellings and pustules
The bitter with wine is good for urticaria {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It is spread with honey on the sa'iya and namla eruptions, and with vinegar or wine on ringworm; the bitter is more effective in all of these {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Good for earache and ringing in the ear, especially the bitter. A head washed with it and with wine is cleared of moisture and dandruff and drawn to sleep. Bitter almond drunk before wine prevents drunkenness; the wood of the bitter tree, pounded fine and mixed with vinegar and rose oil as a poultice on the forehead, helps headache, and so does the oil of the bitter almond {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Eyes
It strengthens sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Chest and breathing
Bitter almond with wheat starch is good for spitting of blood and helps chronic cough, asthma and pleurisy, especially the oil of the sweet; almond flour is useful for cough and spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It opens obstructions of the liver and spleen, especially the bitter, which opens those at the ends of the veins. The fresh almond eaten with its skin dries the stomach's wetness; it is hard to digest, of good humour and little nourishment, and eaten with sugar it goes down quickly. Its flour is heavy and stirs the yellow bile because of its sweetness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.544} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.

## Excretory organs
The bitter opens kidney obstructions, and its oil cleanses the kidney and bladder and breaks the stone, especially drunk with iris; it is sometimes applied as a poultice with iris and rose oil. It helps kidney pain and difficulty of urine. The sweet helps colic by its cleansing, the bitter more, and its oil is lighter than its substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it helps pains of the womb, its hot swellings, hardness and suffocation, and as a pessary brings on the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.

## Poisons and bites
It helps against the bite of a rabid dog {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.545}.
