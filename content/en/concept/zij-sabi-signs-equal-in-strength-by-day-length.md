## Equal by the day
Parts equidistant from the two solstice points are equal in strength, because the length of the day at each is the same. Ten parts of Cancer equal twenty parts of Gemini; ten of Aries equal twenty of Pisces; five of Sagittarius and twenty-five of Capricorn are likewise equal; and two bodies at the same distance from the solstices are equal. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.199-200}

## The growth of the day
The text says the growth of the day in the northern parts equals that of the corresponding southern parts, and that the measures may agree through one of the aspect forms as they happen to fall, so that the start of Sagittarius joins the start of Aquarius in a sextile. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.200}
