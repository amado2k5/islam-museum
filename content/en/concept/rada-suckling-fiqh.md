## Summary
Rada' is the sucking of the breast and drinking of milk; in law it is the arrival of a woman's milk, or what comes of it, into the belly of an infant under conditions. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8762} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8763} Nursing is a duty on the mother, in the Hanafi view a duty of religion and not of law; for the Shafi'is and Hanbalis the father must arrange it if the mother is not fit, and the nurse is paid the usual wage. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8763} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8764} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8765} The bar of kinship comes from a woman's milk and not a man's or an animal's; from the milk of a dead woman by the majority; and from a mixed milk where its effect shows. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8766} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8767} The schools differ on the number of sucks: the Hanafis and Malikis count any number, the Shafi'is and Hanbalis five. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8768} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8769} The period of suckling that bars marriage is two years by the Shafi'is, the Hanbalis, Abu Yusuf and Muhammad, and two and a half years in Abu Hanifa's view. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8769} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8771} By suckling the nurse and her line are barred to the child, and the nurse's children are his milk-siblings; the milk of the stallion (the husband of the nurse) bars by the majority. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8771} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8772} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8773} Suckling is proved by admission or evidence, and the schools differ on the number of witnesses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8776} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8777} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8778} The nurse has a claim on the one she nursed, and it is recommended to reward her. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8779} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8780}

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] says medical studies have recently shown that nursing in a baby's first two years builds its immune system through antibodies passed in the nurse's milk, and sees in this the wisdom of barring marriage between those nursed by one woman in those two years. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:01:30} He cites hadith that suckling forbids what kinship forbids, that there is no suckling except within two years, that only what fills the intestines before weaning forbids, that only what builds bone and grows flesh counts, and that one or two feeds do not forbid. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:04:00} He says at least three full feeds are needed "as some jurists held", that the jurists held only suckling under two years creates the bar, and lists the nurse's relatives who become forbidden. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep21 00:09:00}
