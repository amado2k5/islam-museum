## Overview
as-Salam — The Source of Peace. It is name number 5 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-10-25|10:25]]، [[passage/quran-4-94|4:94]]، [[passage/quran-5-16|5:16]]، [[passage/quran-59-23|59:23]]، [[passage/quran-6-127|6:127]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/bukhari-3326|3326]]، [[passage/muslim-0054|54]]، [[passage/muslim-0397|397]]، [[passage/muslim-0591|591]]، [[passage/muslim-2432|2432]]، [[passage/muwatta-1-064|1.64]]، [[passage/muwatta-1-240|1.240]]، [[passage/muwatta-1-241|1.241]].
