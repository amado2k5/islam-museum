## Summary
For Ibn Sina, what acts by its element is that whose element, changed from its substance by a power in the body, first takes the place of what has dissolved, second fans the innate heat by adding to the blood, and sometimes third also acts by the quality that remains in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.132}.

## What remains of the quality
Something of its first qualities stays in the element that has become like the body. Blood made from lettuce keeps a coldness greater than the temperament of a human being, even though it has become blood and fit to be part of a limb, and blood made from garlic is the opposite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.132}.
