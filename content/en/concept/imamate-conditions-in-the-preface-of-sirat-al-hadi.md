## Overview
This record sets out what the preface of [[work/sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq|Sirat al-Hadi ila al-Haqq]] says about the imamate, a preface transmitted by Ali ibn Muhammad from [[person/muhammad-ibn-sulayman-al-kufi|Muhammad ibn Sulayman al-Kufi]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.22} It is a Zaydi position, presented as attributed to its narrators and not as a judgement of the museum. The general record on the topic is [[concept/imama]].

## The duty of jihad and enjoining good
The preface opens with praise of God, then says God laid duties on his creatures, first knowing him and affirming his oneness, his promise and threat and his justice, then prayer, zakat, pilgrimage and the fast of Ramadan, then enjoining good and forbidding wrong, keeping apart from wrongdoers and fighting the transgressors alongside the just imams of the line of al-Hasan and al-Husayn. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.21} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.22} The book cites verses for this, among them [[passage/quran-61-10|al-Saff 61:10]], [[passage/quran-4-95|al-Nisa 4:95]], [[passage/quran-3-104|Al Imran 3:104]], [[passage/quran-3-110|Al Imran 3:110]], [[passage/quran-9-41|al-Tawba 9:41]], [[passage/quran-9-111|al-Tawba 9:111]], [[passage/quran-4-59|al-Nisa 4:59]], [[passage/quran-35-32|Fatir 35:32]], [[passage/quran-3-33|Al Imran 3:33]], [[passage/quran-16-43|al-Nahl 16:43]], [[passage/quran-15-9|al-Hijr 15:9]], [[passage/quran-22-41|al-Hajj 22:41]]. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.22} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.23} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.24} The text of the verses is not written out here and is left to their own records.

## Who the imam is, in this preface
The narrators hold that prophethood and the imamate stayed in the line of Abraham until Muhammad was sent and the imams were placed in his line through al-Hasan and al-Husayn, and that whoever is first to obey God and strive against the unjust is owed obedience. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.23} The imam, in their account, is whoever of the line of al-Hasan and al-Husayn rises calling to judge by the Book of God and the sunna of his Messenger, shows himself, draws his sword and risks his life; for whom near and far, noble and low are alike in the truth; who is scrupulous, ascetic, strong in God's religion, brave and generous, takes God's wealth from where it is due and puts it where it belongs, a terror to wrongdoers and a friend to the believers. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.27} One who sits at home with the door shut and the curtain drawn, under the rulings of the unjust, who does not change himself when wronged and neither enjoins good nor forbids wrong, who takes God's wealth to eat good food and wear soft clothes while the poor stand hungry behind his door, is not a rightful imam but, in the book's phrase, "an imam of whim and corruption". {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.27} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.28}

## The reports the narrators relate
The book gives reports here with chains beginning with Ali ibn Muhammad from Muhammad ibn Sulayman: one attributed to Ibn Mas'ud from the Prophet about rulers who say what they do not do {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.25}; one attributed to Ma'qil ibn Yasar about the "horn of injustice" and the "horn of justice" {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.25}; reports attributed to Ali ibn Abi Talib listing signs {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.25} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.26}; reports about the Prophet's family attributed to the Prophet, such as the report on holding to the Book and the kin and the report of the ark of Noah {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.26} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.27}; reports attributed to [[person/zayd-ibn-ali|Zayd ibn Ali]] that the imam of the family is the one who draws his sword and calls to the Book of his Lord, not the one who sits at home {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.28}; and a report attributed to Ali that whoever dies without a leader of the community dies a death of the Age of Ignorance, whose source the narrators say they examined and found sound. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.28} {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.29} The wording of these reports is not copied here and no grade is given to them.

## What the narrators say of the community
The narrators say the community went against its Prophet in this out of envy of his family, put others forward and sought knowledge from others, and that it is for the community to seek its religion in obedience to the family of its Prophet and to stand with whoever of them rises. {cite:sira-hadi-ila-al-haqq p.27} This is the narrators' verdict and not a judgement of the museum; the Sunni position on the imamate is set out in the general record [[concept/imama]].
