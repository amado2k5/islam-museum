## Definition
Inhilal in the lexical sense is coming apart. For the jurists it means invalidity, dissolution, rescission and cancellation; invalidity is the wider term, since dissolution applies only to what has been formed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3459}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3460}

## Where it occurs
- Oaths: an oath to do what is obligatory or to leave what is forbidden is dissolved in a way that is obligatory, and one about recommended or disliked acts is dissolved in a recommended way, following the hadith "when you swear to an oath and see that something else is better, do the better and atone for your oath". {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3460}
- Ways an oath is dissolved: the occurrence of the condition, the disappearance of the object, the fulfilment or breach, an exception, the ending of the marital bond by divorce for some jurists, and apostasy for some. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3460}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3461}
- Contracts: dissolved when a non-binding contract is ended, by rescission by agreement or by judgement, or by mutual cancellation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3461}
