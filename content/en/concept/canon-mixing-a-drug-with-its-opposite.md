## Summary
Ibn Sina shows that in changing a temperament one often has to use what strengthens that temperament mixed with its opposite: vinegar with the warming drugs for an organ, saffron in the cooling drugs for the heart, and wax with balsam oil to hold it on the organ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.272-273}.

## Examples
One often has to use what strengthens a temperament mixed with its opposite, such as vinegar with the warming drugs for an organ, so that their strength is made good, and saffron in the cooling drugs for the heart, to carry them to it. Often a drug that acts strongly in changing temperament is fine and does not stay long enough to do its work, so something is mixed in to thicken and hold it, though it may cause the opposite effect, as wax and the like are mixed with balsam oil to keep it on the organ for a while so that it can act {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.272-273}.
