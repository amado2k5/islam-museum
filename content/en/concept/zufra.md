## Nature and selection
Nature: Dioscorides says it is a plant that grows abundantly on the mountain of Aqabis, next to Egypt, and on high rough mountains shaded by trees, especially in damp places and small streams. Its stem is slender and knotted like dill, its leaf like the crown plant but softer and sweet smelling, its stem tip slender and crowned at the end; its seed is black, hollow, sharp to the taste, like fennel seed; its root is white. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Temperament: hot and dry. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}

## Actions
Actions: it dissolves flatulence, and warms. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}

## Uses by organ
Digestion organs: it aids digestion and helps the stomach against flatulence and phlegmatic swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Eye organs: its seed and root help darkness of the sight and clear it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Wounds and ulcers: it helps the pains and itch of scabies. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Excretory organs: its root and seed are similar in power to rue in drying semen; drunk, it brings the menses and urine; and when a woman takes its root in the way the book describes, it does the same. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Poisons: it helps scorpion and vermin stings, drunk in a sweet drink. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
