## Summary
Two corrupting nations confined by Dhu'l-Qarnayn behind a dam of iron and molten copper; they dig at it daily until God's promise comes and they surge forth from every height, drain the Sea of Tiberias, and are destroyed by the naghaf {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
