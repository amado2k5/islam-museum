## Nature
Al-darw is known, and rubb al-darw, which is its gum, is brought to Mecca and is called by this name {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree and moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Actions and properties
Cleansing, dissolving and drawing from the depth of the body; its gum is a gum in the kamkam tree, like al-la'dhan in strength, fragrant, entering women's perfume with halab, as the word is printed and not glossed here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Organs of the head
Rubb al-darw is very beneficial for flow of moisture from the mouth and its ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Excretory organs
In it is a power that binds the belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}
