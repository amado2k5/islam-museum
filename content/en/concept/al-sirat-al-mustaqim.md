## Its meaning for al-Tabari
Al-Tabari states that the people of interpretation are unanimous that "al-sirat al-mustaqim" is the clear road with no crookedness in it, and that this is its meaning in the language of all the Arabs {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 170}. He supports this with a line of Jarir ("the commander of the believers is upon a sirat", meaning a road of truth) and a line of Abu Dhu'ayb al-Hudhali {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 170}, then a rajaz verse, and says the evidence is more than can be counted {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 171}.

## Metaphor in Arabic usage
The Arabs borrow the word "sirat" for any saying or action described as straight or crooked, so that the straight is called straight and the crooked crooked {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 171}.

## The reading al-Tabari prefers
He holds that the best interpretation of "guide us to the straight path" is: grant us steadfastness upon what You have approved, and what You granted to those You blessed among Your servants, in word and deed. Whoever is granted what the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs and the righteous were granted has been granted Islam, belief in the messengers, holding fast to the Book, acting on what God commanded and refraining from what He forbade, and following the way of the Prophet and the way of Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali. All of that is the straight path {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 171}.

## Differences among the interpreters
Al-Tabari says that the interpreters of the Qur'an differ over what the straight path means, and that the reading he chose includes the meanings of all of them {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 171}. The reported views themselves are later in his commentary and are not yet recorded in this entry.

## Its relation to the verse after it
On "the path of those You have blessed", al-Tabari explains that this phrase is a clarification of, and a substitution for, "the straight path": the straight path is the path of those upon whom God bestowed favour {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 170}.
