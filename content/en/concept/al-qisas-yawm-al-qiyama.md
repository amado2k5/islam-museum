## Overview
Retaliation on the Day is God's justice among His creatures; no wrong is lost, even between animals {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## In the hadith
Al-Bukhari records from Abu Hurayra: whoever owes his brother a wrong of honour or anything else should clear it today before there is no dinar and no dirham — if he has good deeds they are taken in the measure of his wrong, and if not, the wronged's bad deeds are loaded onto him {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. Muslim records that when creatures stand for the reckoning, retaliation is taken for the hornless from the horned {cite:sahih-muslim}. Musnad Ahmad carries among the day's reports the claims of creatures until God decides between them {cite:musnad-ahmad}.
