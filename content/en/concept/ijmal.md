## Summary
For the mutakallimun among the usulis, mujmal is what its meaning does not make clear. For the Hanafis it is what cannot be understood without an explanation from the speaker, so that it is not found by reflection alone. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1700} Ambiguity can lie in words or deeds; an example of a deed is the Prophet's salam after two rak'as when it was unclear whether he forgot or the prayer was shortened. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1700} Anything explained leaves ambiguity by agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1700}

## Related term: mushkil
For the Hanafis, if the meaning can be reached by reflection, the word is not mujmal but mushkil, which is a different category. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1700}
