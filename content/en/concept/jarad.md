## Nature and selection
The best is the fat locust with no wings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: its legs remove warts, it is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
Food: a number of its round ones are taken, with the head and extremities removed, and a little dry myrtle is added; it is drunk as it is for dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
Excretory organs: it helps with difficult urination; fumigated with it, it relieves difficulty in urinating, especially in women, and it is fumigated for piles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
Poisons: the fat wingless ones are roasted and eaten for a scorpion sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.427}.
