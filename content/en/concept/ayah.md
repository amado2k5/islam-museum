## Overview
The aya in Qur'anic-sciences usage is the division of the sura bounded by separators; every sura comprises a number of ayat {cite:itqan-suyuti}.

## In the sources
The Itqan treats the meaning of aya alongside sura and Qur'an among the first tafsir terms {cite:itqan-suyuti}.
