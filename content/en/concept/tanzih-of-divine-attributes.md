## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the attributes God reports of Himself are taken as He states them, while denying any likeness {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1074}.

## Life and speech
He says God has life and we have life, but His is essential and ours is given, so ours is no measure for His. Likewise His speech is not like ours, and He can inform His messenger by any means {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1074} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1075}.

## Istiwa
He gives the image of a meal, whose quality varies with the host's rank. If descriptions differ among humans, God is more deserving of being declared free of likeness, so His istiwa is not like human sitting {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1074}.

## Time and place
He holds that "when" and "where" belong to events and do not apply to God, who is not an event {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1103}.
