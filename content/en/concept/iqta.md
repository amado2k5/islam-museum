## Overview
*Iqta'* (land grant), in al-Baladhuri's *Futuh al-buldan*, is when kings and caliphs assign land to whoever deserves it or asks for it. He notes that a request need not be granted. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Examples
Abyad ibn Hammal asked the Prophet for the salt-pans of Marib. He was told that they were like flowing standing water, and he refrained from granting them to him. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.99}

The Prophet granted Alqama ibn Wa'il al-Hadrami a piece of land in Hadramawt, as is related from his father. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.99}

Al-Zubayr was granted land at Khaybar, which contained palm trees and other trees. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.40-41}

The summary mentions that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz confirmed the heirs in land that the Prophet had given as a grant, after counting them. It gives no page for this in the data. It needs a further source.

## Conclusion
In these reports, the grant revolves between public utilities, which are not granted, and the giving of dead land or land reclaimed for cultivation.
