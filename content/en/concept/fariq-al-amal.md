## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan distils the good work team into seven criteria, the first being the choice of a leader — a boat with more than one head sinks — and the leader actually leads, not merely in name: he distributes tasks and roles, gives instructions and help, and makes sure the group moves towards the goal {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:10:00}.

## The seven criteria
Second is planning before work: fixing the goal and the way to reach it; third is participation — the leader makes sure no one is marginalised — fourth is motivation, 'for it stirs resolve'; fifth is distributing roles — a table, for instance, is received by one person and checked by another; sixth is managing the discussion, which includes protecting the weak from those who monopolise speech; and seventh is managing information {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:12:30}.

## The three kinds of information
In managing information he divides it into three: direct information, which is recorded, used and never reread; irrelevant information, which is struck out at once — in meetings it arrives as talk, and the chair must deflect it as 'not related, we defer it' — and indirect information, on which most of the time should be spent because linking it yields the conclusions {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:18:00}.

## Running and wrecking meetings
He also teaches the tricks of running and of wrecking meetings, so that the chair knows them and can control them: reading body language in meetings; ways of 'demolishing' a meeting such as provoking a particular person with a bad example about him ('I am watching out for you') or raising an unrelated dispute; and he tells how he handled an aggrieved man in a meeting he chaired by entering 'Bassam's reply to so-and-so' as an agenda item — preserving his right and the meeting together {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:17:00}.
The ordinary meeting method — agenda, approving the previous minutes, discussing the items, minutes — prevails and wastes time; he cites a Saudi study that a general manager or above attends on average a hundred and ninety-nine meetings a year (needs_source) — and the effective alternative he teaches is [[concept/al-majmua-al-shakliyya]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:47:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:49:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:50:00}.

## In "Secrets of Prophetic Leadership": building synergy
In episode 27 of his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]], [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|al-Suwaidan]] makes "synergy" the second competency of the role of relations: a team in which each member fills another's gap, trained through teamwork courses. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:08:00} Rather than labouring to fix his own weaknesses, he holds, a leader should find someone strong where he is weak, and surround himself with people who complete him, not people who agree with him. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:21:30} He cites the Hijra as a model of dividing roles: the Prophet leading; Abu Bakr on provisions; Ali in the bed and returning the deposits; A'isha and Asma' with food; Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr with news of Quraysh; Amir ibn Fuhayra with the sheep, covering the tracks; and Abdullah ibn Urayqit as guide. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:06:00} He also cites specialised teams: the teaching team betrayed at the [[event/incident-of-al-raji|incident of al-Raji']], and the political team sent at the Trench to check whether Banu Qurayza had broken their pact, told to report in a coded phrase the Prophet would understand so as to protect morale. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep27 00:12:30}
