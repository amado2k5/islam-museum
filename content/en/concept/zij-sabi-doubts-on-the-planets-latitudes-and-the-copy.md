## Summary
Al-Battani says the latitudes of Venus and Mercury differ widely from what Ptolemy's book gives, so he takes the method he found there and adjusts it to approximate and check what observation gives. He allows that the fault may lie with the translator or with the copy the book was translated from. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.100}.

## A matter for further observation
He leaves the matter open rather than judging, and treats the gap between observation and the book as something that more exact observation must settle; he does not declare Ptolemy wrong. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.100}.
