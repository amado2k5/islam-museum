## Its definition and treatment
It is a little pustular flesh growing in the eyeball; if at the canthus it is best pricked, then treated with the gharb's treatment or collyriated with what is used in the pterygium, especially the strongest collyrium; if accompanied by white and black, its treatment is the pterygium's ⁦(the kohl and collyrium names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.195}
