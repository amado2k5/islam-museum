## Summary
The first ten days of Dhu al-Hijja run from the first of the month to its tenth day {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11625}.

## Their merit and the multiplied reward
The jurists hold that its days and nights are noble and that deeds done in them are multiplied; striving in worship and doing good are recommended. Most commentators take the oath in the Surat al-Fajr to refer to these days {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11625}.

## Fasting
Fasting the first nine days is recommended; fasting on the tenth, the day of sacrifice, is not permitted by agreement {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11625}. Fasting on the day of Arafat is recommended for those who are not pilgrims, and the majority hold that it expiates minor sins but not major ones {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11625} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11626}.
