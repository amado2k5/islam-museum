## Summary
Ibn Sina closes chapter 19 by saying that soaking in oil helps people with fatigue, that cooking a fox or a hyena in it, as he will describe, is the best treatment for pains of the joints and gout, and that wetting the face and sprinkling water on it revives a slackened power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.143}.

## Soaking in oil
Soaking in oil and the like helps those with fatigue. If a fox or a hyena has been cooked in it, as he describes, it is the best treatment for those with pains in the joints and gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.143}.

## Sprinkling water on the face
Wetting the face and sprinkling water on it revives a power slackened by distress, by the burning of fevers and at fainting, above all with rose water and vinegar. It may correct appetite or arouse it, and harms those with catarrh and headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.143}.
