## Summary
Lubaʾ, in lexicon, is the first milk that comes after birth; Abu Zayd said it is usually three milkings and at least one. Fisah is the time when the lubaʾ has passed and the milk clears {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13562}.

## The general ruling
The Shafi'is hold that a mother must suckle her child with lubaʾ even if another nurse is found, since the child usually does not live or grow strong without it. The period is short: three days, or seven, or it is left to experts to fix {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13562}. She may claim a fee if such a service has a customary wage, just as a starving man must be fed at the going price {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13562}.

## Liability if the mother refuses
Ibn Abi Sharif holds that she is not liable if she refuses and the child dies, since no act of hers caused the death; this is compared with one who withholds food from a starving man, who is not liable {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13562}.
