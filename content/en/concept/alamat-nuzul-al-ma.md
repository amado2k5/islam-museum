## The signs
The sign heralding the water is the phantasms not from other causes — their matter was explained in the chapter on khayalat — a sensible turbidity arising with them especially if in one eye, and luminous things imagined doubled like lamps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.214}
## How it is told from the inner occlusion
The water may be told from the inner occlusion in that when one eye is closed the other widens in the water but not in the occlusion, for the cause of that widening is the spirit's being driven over; this is so in most cases unless the water is very thick though not an occlusion, and in intishar none of this occurs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.214}
## A transmitted report
He says: I saw a man of attainment and reason in whom the water occurred; he treated himself with evacuations, diet and lessening food, avoiding moist and rich foods, keeping to roast and fried foods and using dissolving lenitive collyria, and his sight returned to him well ⁦(a report transmitted as the book relates it, labelled a transmitted report)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.214}
