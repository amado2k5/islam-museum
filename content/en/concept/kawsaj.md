## Overview
The *kawsaj*, according to al-Masudi, is a man who comes out on the first day of Adharmah riding a mule, in Iraq and the land of Persia, but not in Syria, the Jazira, Egypt, or Yemen. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 507}

## The practice
He reports that for some days people eat walnuts, garlic, fatty meat, hot foods, and heated drinks. The kawsaj appears as something that drives away the cold; then cold water is poured over him and he feels no pain. He cries out in Persian "*kurma kurma*," which means "hot, hot." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 507}

## The occasion
Al-Masudi says that this is the time of the festival of the non-Arabs, in which they rejoice and show gladness. It is a Persian custom with no bearing on worship in Islam. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 507}
