## Summary
Raqm in the lexicon is the weaving of cloth; technically it is the mark that shows the price for which a sale takes place, or the price written on the cloth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8939} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8940} A sale by the written price is valid when both know the price; when both are ignorant it is void according to the majority, and the Hanbalis hold it valid if the ignorant party learns the price before they part. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8940} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8941} A hadith forbids images in houses but excepts a mark on cloth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8941}
