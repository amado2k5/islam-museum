## Nature
Small grains like small qaqulla, brought from al-safala {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}

## Actions and properties
Its strength is the strength of the clove, it cleanses and subtilises, and it is subtler than qaqulla {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}

## Organs of nourishment
It is good for the cold stomach and liver, better for the stomach than qaqulla, and it stops vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.718}
