## Definition
Iltifat is turning to the right or the left; the jurists use the word in its lexical sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3313} It differs from inhiraf, which is leaning away from a thing, since a person may lean while facing the same direction. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3313}

## Ruling
Turning is sometimes asked for and sometimes forbidden.
- It is recommended while turning to the right and the left at the hayya 'ala formulae of the adhan, as Bilal did, according to most jurists; some exempt one who calls the adhan for himself, for a small group, or for a newborn. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3313}
- Turning to the right and left is a sunna at the greeting that ends prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3313}
- Turning the face in prayer is disliked by the four Imams, following the hadith warning against turning in prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3314}
- Turning the chest or the whole body in prayer is disliked by some jurists, and some hold the prayer invalid if the feet are moved. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3314}
- The preacher is disliked from turning in the sermon; some add the same dislike for the listener. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3314}
