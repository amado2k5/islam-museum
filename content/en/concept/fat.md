## Nature
A Turkish drug; the editor's footnote says it is the emetic nut. The heading is as printed on the page {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.632}

## Poisons and bites
Good against drinking hemlock and the sting of vermin, given in cold water, and likewise against jawz mail and, greatly, against all poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.632}
