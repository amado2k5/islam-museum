## Definition
Aqta' in the lexical sense means a person with a cut-off hand. In the jurists' usage it refers to someone who has lost a hand or a foot, and to work that is incomplete or of little blessing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3226}

## Ruling and related matters
- A report says: every affair not begun with the basmala is aqta'. The chain is weak, according to Ibn Hajar as reported by al-Manawi. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3226}
- A person who has lost a hand or foot is excused from jihad where it is a communal duty, and some jurists treat certain diseases of the hand or foot as an excuse to avoid fighting. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3226}
- Someone who has lost a limb does not wash that limb in ablution or bathing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3226}
- Losing a hand or foot is a defect in a prayer leader, so some jurists disliked his leading and some forbade it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3226}
- Retaliation and the penalty for theft in cases involving such a person are treated in their own entries. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3226}
