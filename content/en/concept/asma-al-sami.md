## Overview
as-Sami — The All-Hearing. It is name number 26 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-10-65|10:65]]، [[passage/quran-12-34|12:34]]، [[passage/quran-17-1|17:1]]، [[passage/quran-2-127|2:127]]، [[passage/quran-2-137|2:137]]، [[passage/quran-21-4|21:4]]، [[passage/quran-26-220|26:220]]، [[passage/quran-29-5|29:5]]، [[passage/quran-29-60|29:60]]، [[passage/quran-3-35|3:35]]، [[passage/quran-40-20|40:20]]، [[passage/quran-40-56|40:56]]، [[passage/quran-41-36|41:36]]، [[passage/quran-42-11|42:11]]، [[passage/quran-44-6|44:6]]، [[passage/quran-5-76|5:76]]، [[passage/quran-6-115|6:115]]، [[passage/quran-6-13|6:13]]، [[passage/quran-8-61|8:61]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
