## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the shades of green in urine from pistachio to leek and states what each shows: pistachio shows cold, verdigris and leek severe burning, and green urine in children shows spasm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.185}.

## The shades of green
Next come the shades of green: urine leaning to pistachio, then verdigris, the aubergine-dark shade and then leek green. Pistachio shows cold, as does anything with green in it, except verdigris and leek, which show severe burning. Leek is safer than verdigris {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.185}.

## Verdigris and sky blue
Verdigris after exertion shows spasm, and green urine in children shows spasm. The sky-blue shade mostly shows severe cold and is preceded by green urine. It has been said to show the drinking of poison; with a sediment there is hope of survival, otherwise the patient is feared for. Verdigris is a strong sign of destruction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.185}.
