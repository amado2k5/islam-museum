## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy speaks of the heart's intimacy with God, and says that it shows both among people and in solitude. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:09:00}

## Losing intimacy among people
The Sheikh describes a person who lost his intimacy with God among people but found it in his solitude (needs_source: the phrasing of this description is garbled in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:09:00}

## Intimacy in both settings
The Sheikh says that whoever finds intimacy with God both among people and in solitude is beloved to God, and whoever lacks it in both is deprived of it (needs_source: the wording for the second case is unclear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:15:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:16:00}

## God's remembrance and a saying of Rabi'a
The Sheikh says that God fills hearts with His remembrance, and he quotes a saying of Rabi'a on intimacy with God, whose text is not reproduced here. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:16:00}
