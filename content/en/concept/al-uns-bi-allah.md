## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy speaks of the heart's intimacy with God, and says that it shows both among people and in solitude. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:09:00}

## Losing intimacy among people
The Sheikh describes a person who lost his intimacy with God among people but found it in his solitude (needs_source: the phrasing of this description is garbled in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:09:00}

## Intimacy in both settings
The Sheikh says that whoever finds intimacy with God both among people and in solitude is beloved to God, and whoever lacks it in both is deprived of it (needs_source: the wording for the second case is unclear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:15:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:16:00}

## God's remembrance and a saying of Rabi'a
The Sheikh says that God fills hearts with His remembrance, and he quotes a saying of Rabi'a on intimacy with God, whose text is not reproduced here. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:16:00}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ish al-Lahza'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] gives the seventh episode of his programme [[work/ich-allahda|'Ish al-Lahza']] to the moment of intimacy with God, which he describes as the moment of presence with God, when the veils between hearts and the Knower of the unseen are lifted; veils, he says, that people build with sins and attachment to other than God, until God manifests Himself to the heart and removes them. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:04:30} He says God is near and never leaves a person, that scholars call this moment 'the greatest wealth', and that its sign is tranquillity and calm. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:05:00} Its wisdom, for him, is raising the servant from presence with created things to presence with their Maker, and among its means he names seeking help through patience and prayer, and the Prophet's words to Abu Bakr on the day of the Hijra, not to grieve, for God was with them. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:06:30} He says intimacy in obedience is kept by reflecting on the name through which God manifests Himself, the Appreciative, the Generous or the Helper, and that what cuts it off is seeing oneself instead of God's favour. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:12:30} He relates from Ibn Qudama's 'Book of the Penitents' that a sinner of the Children of Israel was driven out to a ruin, and when death came and he found no one around him he asked his Lord to forgive him; God told Moses to witness the death of one of His friends, and said that had he asked forgiveness for all the sinners of the earth He would have granted it; it is an Isra'iliyyat report that he takes from the book. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:22:00}

## In Omar Abdelkafy's programme Al-Munjiyat (episode 13)
The Sheikh says true intimacy with God does not come through worldly wealth, status or tribe, but through the servant's intimacy with his Lord. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:01:00} He recounts the Children of Israel's saying to Pharaoh "we shall be seized" and Musa's reply "never — my Lord is with me, He will guide me", and sees in the reply two reasons: a heart full of God and God's companionship. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:04:00}
He holds that transmitted revelation is not put before reason, that the law is not offered to medicine but the physician follows it, and creed follows the transmitted text. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:05:00}
He mentions Yunus, cast into the sea in anger and swallowed by the whale, and says the darknesses of night, sea and whale's belly became light for him because his heart was intimate with God; his supplication 'glory be to You, I was among the wrongdoers'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:09:00}
He mentions Ya'qub, whose calamity at losing Yusuf was doubled a second time, and Yusuf's saying 'imprisonment is dearer to me than what they summon me to', and sees intimacy with God in both. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:12:30}
He says Ibrahim asked Hajar a single question — has God commanded you to this? — and when she stood firm she said 'He will not let us be lost'; he sees that the early generations asked one useful question while people today multiply useless ones. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:14:30}
He says the Companion who prayed two units when a thief came out at him in the desert and cried 'O Rescuer, O Rescuer' had an angel sent to him from the fourth heaven; he sees the distance between heaven and earth as an answered prayer for one intimate with God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:19:30}
He tells of Safina, the freedman of the Prophet's household, whom a lion met in the desert and who said 'I am Safina, the maula of the Messenger of God', so the lion walked with him to the caravan; then the report of the Hijra: Ali slept in the Prophet's bed, and Abu Bakr walked before him, behind him and at his right; he says the umma descended from its height when intimacy with God evaporated from the hearts of its members. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep13 00:22:30}
