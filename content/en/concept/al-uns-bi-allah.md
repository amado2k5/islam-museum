## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy speaks of the heart's intimacy with God, and says that it shows both among people and in solitude. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:09:00}

## Losing intimacy among people
The Sheikh describes a person who lost his intimacy with God among people but found it in his solitude (needs_source: the phrasing of this description is garbled in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:09:00}

## Intimacy in both settings
The Sheikh says that whoever finds intimacy with God both among people and in solitude is beloved to God, and whoever lacks it in both is deprived of it (needs_source: the wording for the second case is unclear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:15:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:16:00}

## God's remembrance and a saying of Rabi'a
The Sheikh says that God fills hearts with His remembrance, and he quotes a saying of Rabi'a on intimacy with God, whose text is not reproduced here. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 01:16:00}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ish al-Lahza'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] gives the seventh episode of his programme [[work/ich-allahda|'Ish al-Lahza']] to the moment of intimacy with God, which he describes as the moment of presence with God, when the veils between hearts and the Knower of the unseen are lifted; veils, he says, that people build with sins and attachment to other than God, until God manifests Himself to the heart and removes them. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:04:30} He says God is near and never leaves a person, that scholars call this moment 'the greatest wealth', and that its sign is tranquillity and calm. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:05:00} Its wisdom, for him, is raising the servant from presence with created things to presence with their Maker, and among its means he names seeking help through patience and prayer, and the Prophet's words to Abu Bakr on the day of the Hijra, not to grieve, for God was with them. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:06:30} He says intimacy in obedience is kept by reflecting on the name through which God manifests Himself, the Appreciative, the Generous or the Helper, and that what cuts it off is seeing oneself instead of God's favour. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:12:30} He relates from Ibn Qudama's 'Book of the Penitents' that a sinner of the Children of Israel was driven out to a ruin, and when death came and he found no one around him he asked his Lord to forgive him; God told Moses to witness the death of one of His friends, and said that had he asked forgiveness for all the sinners of the earth He would have granted it; it is an Isra'iliyyat report that he takes from the book. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ich-allahda/ep07 00:22:00}
