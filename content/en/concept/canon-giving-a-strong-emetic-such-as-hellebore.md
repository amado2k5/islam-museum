## Summary
Ibn Sina says a strong emetic such as hellebore is given on an empty stomach if nothing forbids, two hours into the day and after the dregs are cleared from the gut; the vomiting is helped with a feather, otherwise the bath, and if the drug acts too fast the patient is calmed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## When to give it
When a person is given a strong emetic such as hellebore, it should be given on an empty stomach if nothing prevents, two hours into the day and after the waste is removed from the gut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## If vomiting does not come
If he vomits with the feather, well; otherwise it is moved a little, and otherwise he is sent into the bath. The feather used should be wiped with something like henna oil. If cutting pain and distress occur, he is given hot water or oil to drink and either vomits or is purged {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}. Warming the stomach and the limbs also helps, for that brings on nausea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## If the drug acts too fast
If the emetic acts quickly and begins to work at once, the patient should be calmed, made to breathe in pleasant scents, have his limbs pressed, be given a little vinegar, and then take apple and quince with a little mastic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.
