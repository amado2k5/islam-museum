## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the word "father" in the Qur'an is also used for a grandfather and for an uncle, not only for the begetter. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.597}

## The evidence from Jacob's sons
Sha'rawi counts Abraham as a grandfather, Isma'il as an uncle and Isaac as a father, all named together as the fathers of Jacob's sons, and takes this to show that the word covers all three. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.598}

## Azar as Abraham's uncle, in Sha'rawi's reading
Sha'rawi reads the verse that names Azar (6:74) as meaning the uncle, not the begetter, comparing it to a child who asks about his father when he means his uncle. This is Sha'rawi's reading alone in these pages. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.598} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.599}
