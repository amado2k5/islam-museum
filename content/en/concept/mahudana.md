## Nature and selection
It is the one called habb al-muluk, and its tree in our country is called sisaban; its leaf resembles small fish, a finger long, and its fruit comes in threes like large hazelnuts, or smaller, with three black seeds in each fruit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Joints
Helpful by its purging for pains of the joints, gout and sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Helps dropsy, makes one vomit violently, and does not suit the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Excretory organs
It purges like the spurges, and its leaf is boiled in the broth of an old cock, which helps colic and promotes urine. The book says of taking its seeds that chewing them purges more strongly and swallowing them whole is gentler; the counts it names are not copied here. It purges bile and phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.
