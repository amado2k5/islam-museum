## Summary
Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi notes that "Quran" is a verbal noun from the root qara'a (to read), so the name points to recitation, while God calls it a Book, which points to writing. The Quran therefore has two modes of preservation and recitation: it is kept in hearts and recorded in lines. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.14}

## Author's view
This linguistic distinction is Sha'rawi's, attributed to him here. He draws no legal ruling from it, and it has not yet been compared with the lexical views in other works of Quranic sciences.
