## Actions and properties
It varies with the species and with the temperaments of persons, and its power as a whole is ripening and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.546}.

## Cosmetic uses
It clears freckles, skin spots and dead blood under the skin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.546}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Ringworm is rubbed with the saliva of a fasting person and camphor {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.546}.

## Head, ears and teeth
The saliva of a fasting person, dropped into an ear troubled by worms, kills them and brings them out at once {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.546}.

## Poisons and bites
Saliva resists poisons, and if a fasting person spits on a scorpion repeatedly it dies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.546}.
