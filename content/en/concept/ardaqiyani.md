## Nature and selection
A tree like caper, very sharp in smell, whose fruit is in husks {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}.

## Temperament and actions
The monk (al-rahib), as the book names him, said that it is stronger in nature than nightshade berries and kakanj {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it helps internal swellings, in the monk's account, and it is applied to hot external swellings, where it is very remarkable wherever the swelling is {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}.
