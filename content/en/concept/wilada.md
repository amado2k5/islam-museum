## Definition
Childbirth in the Qur'an is a sign: Mary delivered beneath the trunk of the palm tree and was told not to grieve, the mother of Moses was inspired to nurse him and cast him into the river when she feared for him, and every child is born on the fitra as in Abu Hurayra's report in the two Sahihs. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}

## The etiquette of the newborn
Of the established etiquette of the newborn are the adhan in his right ear as the Prophet did with al-Hasan, the tahnik with a chewed date, the good name, and the aqiqa on the seventh day with shaving the head and alms of silver to the weight of the hair. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}

## The rulings of delivery and confinement
Delivery establishes the rulings of lineage, inheritance and the waiting period: the widow's waiting period ends with the delivery, the blood of confinement suspends prayer and fasting until she is pure or forty days end for those who hold it, and the child inherits if he cries aloud for most jurists. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
