## Overview
The *hammam* is the public bathhouse. The data here cover the first bathhouses in Basra. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## The bathhouses of Basra
The first bathhouse built in Basra was that of Abd Allah ibn Uthman ibn Abi al-As al-Thaqafi. The second was the bathhouse of Fil, client of Ziyad. The third was the bathhouse of Muslim ibn Abi Bakra in Balalabadh. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Basra remained for a long time with only these bathhouses, and they could not be built except with the permission of the governors. Then they gave permission to a number of people, and they multiplied. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
