## Summary
Ihtidar is the presence of death; the dying person is one in the throes of death, whose signs include slackening of the feet, bending of the nose and sinking of the temples. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1726} Among the right instructions for the dying is to tell the family to follow the sunna in preparing and burying the body and to avoid innovations. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1727} The jurists differ on reciting the Qur'an beside the dying: al-Nawawi recommends it, while the Malikis dislike any recitation at death, after it, or at graves. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1728} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1729}

## In Ahmad Farid's lessons 'The Boat'
In [[work/the-boat-ahmad-farid|'The Boat']] [[person/ahmad-farid|Ahmad Farid]] gives two readings of the verse in which angels descend on those who said 'our Lord is God' and then stood firm, telling them not to fear or grieve: that they descend on them at all times, or that they descend at death, when a person is at his weakest, most afraid of this world and the next and most grieved over what he leaves, and remove from his heart the fear of what is coming and the grief over his children, wealth and world. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep06 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep06 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep06 00:04:30} In his account the soul of a believer given the glad tidings of God's pleasure flows out as a drop flows from the mouth of a waterskin, with a sweet scent and a light brighter than sunlight, while the soul of the disbeliever and the wicked, told of God's anger, scatters in the body as the angels strike the face and back and tell it to come out. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep07 00:01:30} Both souls, he says, watch the washing of the body and speak above the bier, the one asking to be sent forward and the other crying woe, and this is why the Sunna is to hasten the funeral. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep07 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep07 00:02:30}
