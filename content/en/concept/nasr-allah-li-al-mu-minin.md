## Overview
'Arjun holds God's victory for the believers conditional on their victory for Him — holding to His law and establishing His bounds {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 57} — and on unity of word and leadership, scattered commands meaning failure and the wind's passing {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 55}. Tamkin in the land means placing the law's authority in believers' hands with means of enforcement {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 59}. Badr, the Trench, Hunayn, the ridda wars, the Persian and Roman conquests, 'Ayn Jalut and Hattin all witness that victory went to those who unified the word though few in number.
