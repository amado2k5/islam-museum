## Overview
Al-Suyuti notes that Mu'awiya was the first to establish the *diwan al-khatim* (the office of the seal), and that this office continued under the Abbasid caliphs until the end of their days. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.332}

## The account
Mu'awiya appointed 'Ubayd Allah ibn Aws al-Ghassani over the office and handed him the seal, on whose bezel was inscribed: "For every deed there is a reward." {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.332}

According to the report, the reason for its establishment was that Mu'awiya ordered a man to be given a hundred thousand, and the man altered the document to make it two hundred thousand. When the account was raised, Mu'awiya denied it, and so he established the office so that letters would be sealed with it, and could not be opened or added to. {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.332}

## Retained copies and differentiated functions
Duri adds checked/sealed correspondence and a reference copy under Muawiya,placing the bureau alongside correspondence and post {cite:tdv-divan-duri early-Umayyad}. Adjacency is not equivalence: preparing,controlling and transporting a text are different operations. [[invention/muawiya-seal-copy-registry|The institutional arrangement]] explains their coordination without invention of writing or seals. A copy offers a comparison point in modern teaching,not a receipt for execution. Its own integrity requires controls; existence alone cannot prove every fact it records.

## Attributed roles and the purpose's limits
Duri distinguishes Muawiya's arrangement from an Iranian-influenced regional control attributed through Baladhuri to Ziyad {cite:tdv-divan-duri early-Umayyad}. These are attributed roles,not a full craft/staff inventory. The earlier report's prevention-of-opening/alteration language expresses purpose,not proof of impossible tampering or universal secrecy {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.332}. The named official does not establish a precise founding date,and100000/200000does not become a full budget or count of every alteration.

## Literary witness,not an original seal
Suyuti332is transmitted literary evidence,not the altered letter or its preserved seal; Duri's functional account is not contemporary establishment minutes {cite:tarikh-khulafa-minhaj-1434 p.332} {cite:tdv-divan-duri early-Umayyad}. The concept remains linked to sources and context with gaps visible. Functional resemblance to present signatures/archives is not a direct technical genealogy,and accession is not guessed as the office's opening day. Earlier wording remains attributed to its report; these additions explain its scope and limits.
