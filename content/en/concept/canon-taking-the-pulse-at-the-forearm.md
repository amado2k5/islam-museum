## Summary
Ibn Sina explains why the vessel at the forearm is chosen for feeling the pulse and sets out the position of the hand, the time of examination and the conditions for the patient {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.169}.

## Why the forearm
Three reasons decide the choice: it is easy to reach, little modesty hinders its uncovering, and it lies straight opposite the heart and near it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.169}.

## Hand and time
The hand should rest on its side, since a hand propped up adds to width and height and takes from length, especially in thin people, and a hand lying back adds to height and length and takes from width. The examination should be made when the person is free of anger, joy, exercise and all emotions, of heavy fullness and of hunger, and of the state of dropping or taking up habits; and the examiner should compare against the pulse of a balanced, excellent person {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.169}.
