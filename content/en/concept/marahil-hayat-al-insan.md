## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy says that human life has five stages (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:01:30} The first is the world of the dharr before the creation of Adam, when God took the covenant from the souls, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:01:30} the second is the world of the wombs, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:03:00} and the third is the world of witnessing, which is this life; {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:04:00} this stage ends with the start of what the opening of Surat an-Nazi'at addresses. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:04:30}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 8)
The Sheikh says that human life, whether the person is a believer or a disbeliever, obedient or disobedient, has five stages (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:01:30}. The first was before the creation of Adam, in the world of the seed (dharr), when God took the covenant from the souls {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:01:30}. In this stage the souls affirmed God's oneness; then we come to this world and divide into two parties, believer and disbeliever, the believer being the one whose deeds engaged with what he affirmed in the world of the dharr {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:02:00}. He explains kufr as covering: the disbeliever covered the testimony to God's oneness that he had affirmed, and in his view kufr is this covering {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:02:30}. He says the disbeliever covered the innate disposition to monotheism within him, and that his tongue may deny in this life while every atom of his being testifies that there is no god but God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:03:00}.

The second stage is God's moving us from the world of the dharr to the world of the wombs {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:03:00}. He mentions that the drop stays forty days, then a clot forty days, then a lump of flesh forty days, then God inspires the angel in charge of the wombs, who writes the creature's lifespan and provision; it remains in the darkness of the womb and belly until God wills that this darkness end and it comes out into the light of the world (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:03:30}. The third stage is the world of witnessing, with nursing, then childhood, boyhood, puberty, youth, manhood or womanhood and full maturity {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:04:00}. Then come middle age and the age of old frailty, and this stage we are in ends with the start of Surat [[passage/quran-79-1|an-Nazi'at 1]], that is, with a day on which departure begins {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08 00:04:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 9)
In a later episode the Sheikh says that Surat an-Nazi'at concerns the stage between this world and the hereafter, that is, the beginning of a person's movement into the fourth stage of his life {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:01:30}. He counts what came before as three rings: the world of the dharr, then the world of the wombs, then the world of witnessing in which we now live; the surah comes to speak of the start of the soul's rise and release from the body, and he asks what the souls would say if asked whether they are pleased with the bodies they are in, saying some of these vessels are good {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09 00:02:00}.
