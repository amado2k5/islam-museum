## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy presents the sound heart as the heart lit by faith, and describes kinds of heart by their states: an active heart, a heedless heart and a lazy heart {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:33:00}.

## The heart lit by faith
The speaker wants to explain the heart that faith has lit, and says such a heart is known by the light within it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:31:30}.

## Kinds of heart
The speaker mentions the heedless heart and the lazy heart when describing states of the heart {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:32:00}.
He says that a Muslim with a sound heart has an active heart, or a heart that is lazy in worship; the statement is his own division of the heart's states {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:33:00}.

## The heart and hypocrisy
The speaker says that the heart with the light of faith does not know hypocrisy, and that only hardened hearts know it; the wording at this point is unclear (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:31:00}.
