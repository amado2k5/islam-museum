## Summary
The fifth genus for Ibn Sina is the state of the organs: a hot temperament goes with a broad chest, large limbs, wide vessels and a large pulse, cold with the opposites, and dryness with roughness and showing joints and cartilages {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.161}.

## Hot and cold
A hot temperament goes with a broad chest, large and full-sized limbs without narrowness, wide and visible vessels, a large and strong pulse, and large muscles close to the joints, since all proportional acts and structural forms are completed by heat. Cold goes with the opposites, because it leaves the natural powers short of finishing the acts of making and shaping {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.161}.

## Dry
A dry temperament goes with roughness, showing joints, cartilages showing in the larynx and nose, and a straight nose {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.161}.
