## Each organ's making and use
By the throat he means the space holding the two passages of breath and food, with the appendages that are the uvula, the two tonsils and the epiglottis; the uvula is a fleshy body hung like a veil on the larynx's top whose use is to admit air gradually, keep off smoke and dust, and be a striking-board for the voice; the tonsils are two jutting nervous flesh bodies like two glands whose use is to scoop air at the windpipe's head like a tank; and the epiglottis a membrane-flesh clinging to the palate under the uvula, hanging applied on the windpipe's head with the fâ'iq above it (editor's glosses attributed) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.285}
