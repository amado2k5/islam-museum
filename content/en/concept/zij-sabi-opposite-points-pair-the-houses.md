## Summary
Al-Battani shows the houses pair off as opposites: the first of the eleventh is the opposite of the first of Aries, the first of the twelfth of Taurus, the first of the eighth of the second, and the first of the ninth of the third. The opposite is the point across the diameter of the circle, one hundred and eighty degrees away. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.111}.

## Use of the method
Once the first points of the twelve houses are known this way, all of them are points of the zodiac, and from them the ascendant at any hour can be read. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.111}.
