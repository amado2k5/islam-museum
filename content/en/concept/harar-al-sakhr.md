## Nature
Galen says this is a growth on the rock that resembles moss. It dries in two ways: its power cleanses and cools, and the cleansing and drying it takes from the rock, the cooling from the water. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}

## Properties
It dries and cools. Dioscorides says it cuts blood; the author does not hold this. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}
