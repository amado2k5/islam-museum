## Temperament
Cold and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Actions and properties
Astringent, subtle and binding, it stops the pouring of humours and calms heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach when given with myrtle water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Excretory organs
It binds the belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}
