## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the Qur'an draws humanity's attention to God's signs in the heavens and the earth so that reason and perception work on them, that sciences accumulate, and that each science rests on an earlier one {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.248} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.249}. The verse that rebukes turning away from the signs is [[passage/quran-12-105|Yusuf 105]].

## Accumulation of knowledge
He gives the example of steam, from evaporating water to the train, and the fallen tree that gave the idea of the wheel. He holds that every scientific advance springs from earlier knowledge and from capacities God gave {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.248}.

## Newton and the stone
He tells that Newton watched an apple fall and reached the law of gravity, and he uses the date stone as an example: a whole palm is present in the stone potentially {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.249}.

## Axioms
He holds that every science rests on theories, that its starting point is axioms God placed in the universe, and that all human discoveries are present potentially, waiting for reflection and work {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.249}.

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'Wa Yatafakkarun'
[[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] makes reflection on the signs of the cosmos the aim of his series [[work/wa-yatafakkarun|Wa Yatafakkarun]], saying that the Qur'an's cosmic references came as proofs of God's divinity, lordship and oneness and of the certainty of the hereafter, because the human mind in his view needs observation, induction, contemplation, experiment and inference and cannot take in the whole of things at once. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep11 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep11 00:02:30} He cites the verse 'and on the earth are signs for those with certainty' ([[passage/quran-51-20|al-Dhariyat 20]]) and says that the earth's distance from the sun, its size, the tilt of its axis and the make-up of its soil are reckoned so exactly that they show it did not come about by pure chance. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep16 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep16 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep16 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep16 00:07:30} He says the 'scholars' of the verse on reverent fear ([[passage/quran-35-28|Fatir 28]]) include for him the natural scientists who study water, plants, rocks, people and animals, and he describes human knowledge as a pyramid whose base is the pure and applied sciences, then the humanities, with revelation at its summit. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep22 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep22 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar wa-yatafakkarun/ep22 00:12:30}
