## Nature and selection
Nature: zarin darakht, which is zinzalakht in the colloquial, according to the editor's note. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}

## Uses by organ
Joints: it helps sciatica. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Excretory organs: the water of its leaves with maybakhtaj helps difficult urination, expels clotted blood from the bladder, and brings the menses. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
Poisons: it helps vermin stings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
