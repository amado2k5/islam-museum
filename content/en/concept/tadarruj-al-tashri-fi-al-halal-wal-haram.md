## Summary
Aisha reports that the first thing sent down of the Qur'an was a sura of the mufassal that mentions Paradise and the Fire, until, once people had turned to Islam, the lawful and the unlawful came down, and that had the ban on wine come first they would have said they would never give it up [[passage/bukhari-api-4993|Sahih al-Bukhari 4993]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4993}. The reports say that Umar asked for a healing explanation about wine, that the verse of al-Baqara came down and then the verse of al-Nisa' [[passage/nasai-api-5540|Sunan al-Nasa'i 5540]] {cite:sunan-al-nasai 5540}, and that when wine was forbidden people poured out what they had [[passage/bukhari-api-4620|Sahih al-Bukhari 4620]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4620}. The Prophet forbade the flesh of domestic donkeys on the day of Khaybar [[passage/tirmidhi-api-1478|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 1478]] {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 1478}, and said that a vessel makes nothing lawful or unlawful [[passage/tirmidhi-api-1869|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 1869]] {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 1869}. The Qur'an says that good things which had been lawful were forbidden to the Jews [[passage/quran-4-160|al-Nisa' 160]] {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the Qur'an
On wine, the Qur'an says that from the fruit of the date-palm and the vine people take an intoxicant and also good nourishment [[passage/quran-16-67|al-Nahl 67]] {cite:tanzil-quran}; that those who asked about wine and games of chance were answered that in both is great sin and some benefit for people, and that their harm outweighs their benefit [[passage/quran-2-219|al-Baqara 219]] {cite:tanzil-quran}; that the believers were told not to approach prayer while drunk, until they know what they say [[passage/quran-4-43|al-Nisa' 43]] {cite:tanzil-quran}; that wine, games of chance, idols and divining arrows are the handiwork of Satan and that they were told to leave them aside [[passage/quran-5-90|al-Ma'ida 90]] {cite:tanzil-quran}; and that Satan wants to cast enmity and hatred among them through wine and games of chance and to turn them from the remembrance of God and from prayer [[passage/quran-5-91|al-Ma'ida 91]] {cite:tanzil-quran}. On fasting, it says they were permitted to approach their wives on the night of the fast, that God knew they had been betraying themselves and turned to them in mercy, and that they were permitted to eat and drink until the dawn becomes clear [[passage/quran-2-187|al-Baqara 187]] {cite:tanzil-quran}. On earlier communities it says that good things which had been lawful to the Jews were forbidden to them because of their wrongdoing [[passage/quran-4-160|al-Nisa' 160]] {cite:tanzil-quran}; that Jesus came confirming the Torah before him, to make lawful for them some of what had been forbidden [[passage/quran-3-50|Al Imran 50]] {cite:tanzil-quran}; and that the unlettered Prophet makes lawful for them good things, forbids them foul things and lifts from them their burden and the fetters they wore [[passage/quran-7-157|al-A'raf 157]] {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the Sunnah
Aisha says in Sahih al-Bukhari that the first thing sent down of the Qur'an was a sura of the mufassal mentioning Paradise and the Fire, «until people turned to Islam, and then the lawful and the unlawful came down»; had the ban on wine come down first they would have said they would never leave it, and had the ban on adultery come first they would have said the same [[passage/bukhari-api-4993|Sahih al-Bukhari 4993]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4993}. Buraida reports that the Prophet said he had forbidden them the vessels, that a vessel makes nothing lawful or unlawful, and that every intoxicant is unlawful [[passage/muslim-api-5208|Sahih Muslim 5208]] {cite:sahih-muslim 5208} [[passage/tirmidhi-api-1869|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 1869]] {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 1869}. In the Muwatta, Abu Sa'id relates that the Prophet had forbidden them the flesh of sacrificial animals after three days and then said to eat, give in charity and store it; that he had forbidden steeping drinks and then permitted it, saying every intoxicant is unlawful; and that he had forbidden visiting graves and then said to visit them [[passage/muwatta-api-1035|Muwatta Malik 1035]] {cite:muwatta-malik 1035}. Al-Rabi' ibn Sabra reports that the Prophet said he had permitted them temporary marriage with women and that God had forbidden it until the Day of Resurrection [[passage/muslim-api-3422|Sahih Muslim 3422]] {cite:sahih-muslim 3422}. Jabir reports that on the day of Khaybar the Prophet forbade domestic donkeys, the flesh of mules, every beast of prey with a fang and every bird with a talon [[passage/tirmidhi-api-1478|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 1478]] {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 1478}. In al-Nasa'i, a man came to the Prophet and said they were vineyard owners and that God had sent down the prohibition of wine, and asked what they should do; he answered: make it into raisins [[passage/nasai-api-5735|Sunan al-Nasa'i 5735]] {cite:sunan-al-nasai 5735}.

## In the reports of the Companions, the Successors and the scholars
Ibn Umar reports that Umar said on the Prophet's pulpit that the prohibition of wine had come down and that wine was made from five things: grapes, dates, honey, wheat and barley, and that wine is what clouds the mind [[passage/bukhari-api-4619|Sahih al-Bukhari 4619]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4619}. In another report in Muslim, raisins are named in place of grapes [[passage/muslim-api-7559|Sahih Muslim 7559]] {cite:sahih-muslim 7559}. Abu Maysara reports from Umar that when the prohibition of wine came down he said: O God, give us a healing explanation about wine; the verse of al-Baqara came down, Umar was called and it was read to him, and he said the same; then the verse of al-Nisa' came down [[passage/nasai-api-5540|Sunan al-Nasa'i 5540]] {cite:sunan-al-nasai 5540}; Abu Dawud gives the same report beginning with the verse of al-Baqara [[passage/abudawud-api-3670|Sunan Abi Dawud 3670]] {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3670}. Anas relates that he was serving Abu Talha, Abu Dujana and Mu'adh ibn Jabal among a group of the Ansar when someone came in and said that the prohibition of wine had come down; they overturned it, and most of their wine then was a mixture of unripe and ripe dates [[passage/muslim-api-5135|Sahih Muslim 5135]] {cite:sahih-muslim 5135}; in al-Bukhari Abu Talha tells him to go and pour it out, and it ran in the lanes of Medina [[passage/bukhari-api-4620|Sahih al-Bukhari 4620]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 4620}. Al-Bara' relates that some Companions had died while drinking wine, and people asked about them after the prohibition, so a verse came down concerning them [[passage/tirmidhi-api-3051|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 3051]] {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 3051}. Al-Bara' also relates that one of them, if he slept before supper, was not permitted to eat or drink that night or the next day until sunset, until the verse on fasting came down [[passage/nasai-api-2168|Sunan al-Nasa'i 2168]] {cite:sunan-al-nasai 2168}. Abdallah ibn Abi Awfa says that hunger struck them on the day of Khaybar and they slaughtered donkeys while their pots were boiling, and the Prophet's herald called to overturn the pots and eat none of the donkey flesh; in one report they said he forbade it because it eats filth [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3192|Sunan Ibn Majah 3192]] {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3192}, in another because it had not been divided by the fifth [[passage/muslim-api-5010|Sahih Muslim 5010]] {cite:sahih-muslim 5010}.

## In the museum
In Ibn Rushd's Bidayat al-Mujtahid, in a scanned text, the Prophet forbade drinks except in skin vessels and then said to drink from any vessel but not to drink an intoxicant, and in Muslim's wording a vessel makes nothing lawful or unlawful and every intoxicant is unlawful {cite:bidaya-bakri-bayt-al-afkar p.57} {cite:bidaya-bakri-bayt-al-afkar p.61}. In Ibn Hisham's Sira, when the Prophet was settled in Medina, his brothers of the Muhajirun had gathered to him and the affair of the Ansar was united, the matter of Islam was firmly established: prayer was set up, zakat and fasting were made obligatory, the penalties were established, and the lawful and the unlawful were made obligatory {cite:ibn-hisham-sira p.236}.
