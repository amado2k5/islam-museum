## Nature
It is a dew that falls on the khilaf tree and on tragacanth at Herat; the editor's note says it is the tree of the bahramaj, which is the khilaf {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Actions and properties
Cleansing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Temperament
Tending to moderation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}

## Excretory organs
It is close to tarunjabin in its loosening and its actions, indeed stronger than it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.677}
