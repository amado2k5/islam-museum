## Summary
Nisyan in the lexicon is the opposite of remembering or retaining, and it also means leaving aside or delaying. As a term it is failing to recall a thing when one needs it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15686} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15687} It differs from error (khata'), which is an act the person did not intend. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15687}

## General ruling
The jurists differ on the effect of forgetting on accountability. The Shafi'is and Hanbalis hold, in the sound view, that the forgetful person is not accountable while forgetting. The Hanafis hold that forgetting does not cancel the duty owed to God and may be an excuse in God's rights, but not in the rights of people. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15687} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15688} All agree that forgetting removes sin in the next world. In this world an omitted command must be made up, and what was destroyed remains a liability. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15690}

## Where it is discussed
The applications of forgetting appear in ablution, prayer, tayammum, fasting, pilgrimage, slaughter, testimony, divorce, oaths and liability. Each is treated in its own entry. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15691} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15697} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15705}
