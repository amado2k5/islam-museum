## Nature and selection
Nature: copper flower, the scum that rises on copper when it is melted, according to the editor's note. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.465}

## Actions
Actions: astringent, eats away and stings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.465}

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: it eats proud flesh. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.465}
Head organs: it enters the drying remedies for ear sores; the white kind, ground and blown into the ear, removes chronic deafness, and it is rubbed on with honey for swellings of the throat and uvula. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.465}
Excretory organs: a measured dose purges thick humour, and purges yellow water; it is used in drying remedies for piles and ulcers of the anus, as is said. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.466}
