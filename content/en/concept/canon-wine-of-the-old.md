## Summary
In the third chapter Ibn Sina states that the best drink for the old is old red wine, since it promotes urine and warms together; they avoid new and white wine, except one who has bathed after a meal and is thirsty, who is given a thin wine of little nourishment in place of water; sweet, obstructing drinks are avoided {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.253}.

## Their best drink
Their best drink is old red wine, so that it promotes urine and warms together. They avoid new and white wine, unless they have bathed after taking food and are thirsty; then they are given a thin wine of little nourishment, in the place of water for them. Sweet drinks that obstruct are to be avoided. This is the physician's account of a medieval regimen, reported as the book states it and not as advice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.253}.
