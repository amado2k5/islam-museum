## Summary
A diwan is a register in which names or documents are entered, and which keeps what concerns the state's business, funds and officials. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8364}
Umar ibn al-Khattab was the first to set one up in Islam. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8365}

## Keeping a diwan
The Malikis allow the imam to keep a diwan, a register of the troops' names and pay. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8365}
The Shafi'is recommend the troops' register in their accepted view, and the Hanbalis say the imam should keep one to fix the fighters and their allowances. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8365} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8366}

## Parts of the state's diwan
For the army: enrolment and pay; a body of troops that refuses to fight when called loses its allowance, while a sick or insane man whose condition may improve stays on the register. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8366}
For the business of revenue: each district is described with its land, whether it pays tithe or land-tax; the non-Muslims and their poll tax are recorded; mines and frontier towns are noted. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8366} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8367}
For the officials: appointment, dismissal and stipends, with the conditions of a valid appointment as to district, duty and term. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8367} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8368}
The clerk of the diwan must be trustworthy and capable; he keeps the rules, collects dues, and hears complaints. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8368} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8369} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8370}

## The judge's diwan and others
A judge's diwan holds people's documents, records and minutes; a new judge takes it first and the predecessor hands it over. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8371}
The diwan of letters was set up when the language decayed, so that the rulers' letters could be written in eloquent words. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8371}
