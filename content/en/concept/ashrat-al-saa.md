## Summary
"We believe in the signs of the Hour: the emergence of the Dajjal, the descent of Jesus son of Mary from heaven; and we believe in the sun rising from its west, and the Beast of the Earth emerging from its place" {cite:aqida-tahawiyya p. 31}.

## In Ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi's Lum'at al-I'tiqad
Ibn Qudama mentions among the signs of the Hour: "the emergence of the Dajjal; the descent of Jesus son of Mary, who kills him; the emergence of Gog and Magog; the emergence of the Beast; the sun rising from its west; and the like of what sound transmission established" {cite:lumat-al-itiqad p. 31}.

## In al-Ash'ari's Maqalat al-Islamiyyin
In "the account of the sum of the saying of the people of hadith and sunna" in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari presents what the people of hadith and sunna hold, ending: "And in all we have mentioned of their saying we speak, and to it we go" {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin p. 350}. He relates there that they "believe in the emergence of the Dajjal, and that Jesus son of Mary kills him" {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin p. 348}.

## What is not covered here
This entry presents the positions of the 'Aqida al-Tahawiyya, Ibn Qudama's Lum'at al-I'tiqad, Ibn Taymiyya's al-Wasitiyya, and what al-Ash'ari related in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin of the people of hadith and sunna and declared his own — each attributed to its book. The sayings of the Maturidis and the remaining Sunni and non-Sunni schools from their own books have not yet been added; the entry needs specialist review before promotion.

## From the series Qissa wa 'Ibar
In the sixth episode, at the mention of Gog and Magog in the story of Dhu al-Qarnayn, Omar Abdelkafy says they are among the major signs of the Hour and counts six as he narrates them: the sun rising from the west, the beast, the smoke, the Dajjal, Gog and Magog, and before that the descent of the Messiah 'Isa. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:03:30} He says that the first, as he thinks, is the sun rising from the west, after which repentance no longer avails, and relates that the beast will mark the believer and the unbeliever so that each is known, leaving the details to hadiths for other sessions. The order and the count are his narration. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep06 00:04:30}

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] takes up three of the signs. In the sixth episode he reads the hadith that the Prophet's mission and the Hour are like two fingers as meaning the mission came near the end of the universe. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep06 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep06 00:05:00} In the seventeenth he explains the hadith of the fire that will come out of the land of the Hijaz and light up the necks of camels at Busra, linking it to the lava fields of western Arabia and the two lava fields of Rahat and Khaybar around Medina, which he says are still active, and says the signs belong to the unseen whose time only God knows, but that science confirms this sign is possible. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep17 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep17 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep17 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep17 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep17 00:08:00} In the thirtieth he takes up the sun's rising from the west and lists the ten major signs as he relates them; the episode breaks off before his argument is finished. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep30 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep30 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep30 00:06:00}
