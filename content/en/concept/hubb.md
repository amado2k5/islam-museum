## Overview
Loving for one's brother means that a believer loves for his brother and for people the good that he loves for himself. It has its root in the hadith of Anas: "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.282-283}

## Wording of the hadith
In Muslim's wording it is "for his neighbour or his brother." In Ibn Hibban's: "A servant does not attain the reality of faith until he loves for people the good that he loves for himself." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.282-283}

## Ibn Rajab's commentary
Ibn Rajab shows that this addition makes clear that the intent is the negation of attaining the reality and perfection of [[concept/iman|faith]], not the negation of its root. Faith is often negated because some of its pillars or duties are missing. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.282-283}

He relates from Abu Amr ibn al-Salah, from Abu Muhammad ibn Abi Zayd, that this hadith is one of four hadiths from which the branches of good manners spring. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.270}

The subject connects with [[concept/nasiha|sincere counsel]].
