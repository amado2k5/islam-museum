## Overview
Loving for one's brother means that a believer loves for his brother and for people the good that he loves for himself. It has its root in the hadith of Anas: "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.282-283}

## Wording of the hadith
In Muslim's wording it is "for his neighbour or his brother." In Ibn Hibban's: "A servant does not attain the reality of faith until he loves for people the good that he loves for himself." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.282-283}

## Ibn Rajab's commentary
Ibn Rajab shows that this addition makes clear that the intent is the negation of attaining the reality and perfection of [[concept/iman|faith]], not the negation of its root. Faith is often negated because some of its pillars or duties are missing. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.282-283}

He relates from Abu Amr ibn al-Salah, from Abu Muhammad ibn Abi Zayd, that this hadith is one of four hadiths from which the branches of good manners spring. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.270}

The subject connects with [[concept/nasiha|sincere counsel]].

## In Abd al-Rahman Mansour's programme 'Ahalina'
[[person/abd-al-rahman-mansour|Abd al-Rahman Mansour]] counts among "the keys to hearts" in his programme [[work/ahaleena|'Ahalina']] telling your brother that you love him for God's sake, which he says plants love for you in your brother's heart. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:10:30} He cites the hadith that whoever loves his brother for God's sake should tell him, for it makes closeness last longer and affection firmer, and another that he should go to his house and tell him of his love. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:11:00} He recalls that the Prophet declared his love for particular people, among them [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Mu'adh ibn Jabal]], and told the Ansar they were the dearest of people to him. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:12:30} He mentions a report that [[person/mujahid-ibn-jabr|Mujahid]] relates from [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]], in which Ibn Abbas reads what is in a man's heart towards him from what is in his own heart towards the man, with the hadith that souls are conscripted soldiers. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:13:00} He tells his colleagues at al-Nada channel, and every Muslim, that he loves them for God's sake. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:11:30}

He carries the same idea into the home: a husband should declare his love to his wife and a father to his child, and he counts withholding affection from children among "the destroyers", because a child who hears "I love you" understands that he is wanted, and his confidence and sense of safety grow. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep05 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep05 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep05 00:10:30}
