## Nature
It is well known; the book reports from Dioscorides that chard is of two kinds, black and white, both bad in chyme because of the natron in them, and from Istifan that he found in the Dijla al-Awra near Basra a wild chard with branches spreading from one root and a leaf the colour of rocket {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.597}.

## Temperament
Some say hot and dry in the first degree, though in truth it is compound in power, and some say cold; its root has moisture in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Actions and properties
It has a thinning borax-like quality and dissolving and opening stronger than the opening of the iris, and softening; the black has astringency, especially with lentils; all of it is bad in chyme and low in nourishment like the other vegetables {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Cosmetics
Its juice and the decoction of its leaf help chaps from cold, it helps alopecia, it helps freckles when the leaf is used as a poultice, its juice removes warts, and its juice kills lice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Swellings and pustules
Boiled and laid on swellings it dissolves and ripens them, it helps the mulberry-like warts as a poultice, and helps hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Wounds and ulcers
The leaf cooked is good for burns from fire, with honey as a smear it helps ringworm, and it is used on malignant ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Organs of the head
Its juice snuffed into the nose removes facial palsy and helps ulcers of the nose; its warm juice dropped in the ear calms pain, and the head washed with it loses dandruff {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its root is bad for the stomach and nauseating because of its stinging borax-like quality, its nourishment is slight, and its opening of liver blockages is stronger than that of mulukhiyya, especially with mustard and vinegar, and likewise the spleen; it ought to be eaten with murri and spices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.

## Excretory organs
It is said that the black restrains, especially with lentils, as the other softens; all of it breeds wind and rumbling and gripes, and it is good for colic when taken with spices and murri {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.598}.
