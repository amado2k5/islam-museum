## Overview
Amir silah, 'grand master of the armour', was an office of the Mamluk kingdom. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 467}

## Duties
The holder was in charge of the armour-bearers (silahdariyya) and supervised the arsenal (silahkhana). It was his duty to bear the sultan's arms in public ceremonies and to convey them to him in battle and on other occasions. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 467}

## Rank
In the early Mamluk period the office was not very high; compare the amir majlis. Under the Circassians it was the second office among the highest amirs of the kingdom, and the amir silah had the right of sitting as ra's al-maysara in the sultan's presence. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 467-468}
