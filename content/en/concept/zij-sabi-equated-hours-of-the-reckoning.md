## Summary
Equated hours, also called mean hours, are hours in which the parts of the day and night are equal by the equinox. Al-Battani holds that the reckoning hours of each place are these very hours, counted from noon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.108}.

## The hours in the book
He says the hours of the reckoning in his book are counted from noon at Raqqa, and the hours of each other place are then derived from them. That derivation is set out in chapter 34. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.108}.
