## Summary
Ibn Sina makes the aim in treating tension fatigue the loosening of what has hardened, by gentle rubbing, oil warmed in the sun and bathing in lukewarm water with a long stay, keeps the food small and moist, and treats a cause in thick superfluities by evacuation and one in distending wind with cumin, caraway and aniseed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.244-245}.

## Aim and means
He says the aim in treating tension fatigue is to loosen what has hardened, and this is done by gentle rubbing, oil warmed in the sun and bathing in lukewarm water, staying in it for a long time {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.244}.

## Repeats, food and causes
If it recurs, the tub may be used two or three times a day, with oiling after each bath, and the food is moist and small in quantity, for this kind needs less food than the ulcerous one. Exercise dissolves and drives out this fatigue. If it arises by itself from thick superfluities, evacuation cannot be avoided, and if from distending wind, things such as cumin, caraway and aniseed dissolve it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.245}.
