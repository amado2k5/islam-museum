## Nature and selection
Buwsir. The editor's footnote gives it as the Maghribi barbashka, or bear's ears {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

## Temperament and actions
Dissolving, especially the golden-flowered kind, and it cleanses moderately {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: the wild kind with its golden flower reddens hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Swellings and pustules: a decoction of its leaf helps swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Wounds and ulcers: applied with honey to sores and wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Joints: a decoction helps bruising of the muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Head: a gargle with the decoction helps toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Eyes: its decoction helps hot ophthalmia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Breath organs: its decoction helps chronic cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.

Excretory organs: the white-leaved and black-leaved kinds help diarrhoea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.401}.
