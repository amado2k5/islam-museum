## Nature
A plant with some resemblance to ushnan {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Actions and properties
It has saltness with astringency, its parts are not alike, and it has a little opening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Respiratory organs
It is gargled with milk, and so is its salt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Excretory organs
It loosens the yellow water, especially its seed and the juice of the plant; it drives urine and is a gentle purge of bile and the watery humour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it generates semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}
