## The compound temperaments
The signs of the hot dry temperament: absence of superfluities, clear senses, strength in staying awake, little sleep, quick growth of hair at first and its strength, blackness and curliness, very quick baldness, a hot and dry touch of the head with a plain redness in it and in the eye, changing and haste in resolves, strong understanding and memory and quick acts of the soul {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.24}

The signs of the hot moist temperament: if it is not very far from moderation, the colour is good, the vessels plain, the touch hot and soft, the superfluities more and riper, the hair lanker and tending to fair, not quick to bald, and heating and moistening come quickly to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.24} If it is far from it, the person is sickly, ready to be hurt by heat and cold and quick to putrefactive diseases in his substance, his senses heavy and murky, he cannot hold out against sleep and sees confused dreams {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.24}

The signs of the cold dry temperament: the head cold to the touch, the colour pale, the veins hidden in it and in the eyes, slow growth of hair, reddish and thin, slow baldness, the senses clear in the elderly, but when he advances in age he weakens quickly and ages, and convulsion, putrefaction and contraction show in the regions of his head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.24} He ages quickly and his health is unsteady, at times light of head with open passages, at times the opposite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.24}

In the cold moist temperament the person sleeps much and deeply, has poor senses, is lazy and dull, with much expelling of superfluities from the head, and slow baldness and quick coming of descents also indicate it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.24}
