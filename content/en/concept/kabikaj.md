## Nature
Dioscorides says there are four kinds. One is like coriander leaf, but broader and whitish; its flower is yellow and sometimes purple, it grows to two cubits, its root is slender and white, and it has branches like those of hellebore, growing by flowing water. A larger kind with a longer root and slashed leaves is called kurafs al-barr. A very small golden kind, and a fourth like the third but with a milky-white flower. The editor's note gives the name as warad al-habb. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry in the second degree. All kinds are hot, sharp, blistering, cleansing, scouring, irritant to the skin and resolving. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}

## Cosmetic use and skin
Its leaves and stems before they dry clear leukoderma, white nail marks and alopecia with slight contact. It removes itch very well, and disperses nail-shaped warts and clinging glands. Cooked, and its warm water applied to the favus, it helps. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}

## Organs of the head
Its dried roots are strong sneezing agents, and its powder helps the throbbing that comes on the teeth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}
