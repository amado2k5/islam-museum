## Summary
Ibn Sina explains the mouse-tail, pause, spasmodic and thrilling pulses by the states of power, instrument and need, and ranks the kinds of mouse tail by badness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.175-176}.

## The mouse tail
The cause of the mouse-tail pulse is a weak power that passes by degrees from effort to rest and from rest to effort. A pulse fixed in one state points more to weakness of power; the mouse tail and its like point to some power and to weakness not being at its extreme. The worst is the ending mouse tail, then the fixed, then the returning {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.175-176}.

## Pause, spasm and tremor
The cause of the pulse with a pause is the tiring and resting of the power, or a sudden event to which the soul and nature turn all at once. The cause of the spasmodic pulse is unnatural motions in the power and badness in the instrument's consistency. The thrilling pulse comes from a power, a hard instrument and a severe need; without these its trembling does not follow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.176}.
