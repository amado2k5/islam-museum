## Overview
Al-Firdaws is the highest station of Paradise, named in two suras: «for those who believe and do righteousness, the gardens of al-Firdaws are a lodging» in al-Kahf, and «who inherit al-Firdaws; they will abide therein» in al-Mu'minun {cite:tanzil-quran}. Ibn Kathir glosses al-Firdaws as the middlemost and noblest of Paradise {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the Sunnah
In Sahih al-Bukhari, from Abu Hurayra, the Prophet said: «When you ask God, ask Him for al-Firdaws, for it is the middlemost and highest part of Paradise; above it is the Throne of the Merciful, and from it the rivers of Paradise spring» {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. It is therefore the station a suppliant is taught to request by name.
