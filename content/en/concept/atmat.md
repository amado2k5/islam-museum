## Nature and selection
An Indian drug of the power of buzandan, which the book treats under ba. The book warns that it must be checked so that it is not atiyut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. The editor's note identifies it as the Indian nut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and moist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: the book says it increases the desire for intercourse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. This is the book's own listing and not a recommendation.
