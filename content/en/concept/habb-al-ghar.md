## Nature
It is the berry the book calls dimist, like small hazelnuts, with a thin, dark shell that splits when pressed into two hard yellowish halves, with a slight fragrance. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
Its actions are given in the letter ghayn at the entry on the laurel, and are not written here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
