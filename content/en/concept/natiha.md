## Summary
Natiha is an animal gored to death by another's horns. It shares with strangled, struck and fallen animals the status of carrion dead without lawful slaughter, and every gored animal is carrion but not the reverse. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15761}

## Ruling
The gored animal is carrion, unclean and forbidden to eat by the verse of the Ma'ida surah. The exception is an animal reached with a settled life and lawfully slaughtered, by cutting the windpipe and gullet with a sharp instrument. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15762}
Carrion is treated in full under its own entry. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15762}
