## Nature
It is well known {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Temperament
Cooling and drying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Actions and properties
Its dross is very astringent, and it has drawing and drying; its filings, mixed with other drugs, benefit viscous moistures {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Swellings and pustules
Very good for scab and itch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Organs of the head
Its filings, mixed with other drugs, are useful for foul breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Ocular organs
Applying kohl with a silver stick increases sight and clears the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}

## Organs of the chest
Its filings with other drugs are useful for palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.624}
