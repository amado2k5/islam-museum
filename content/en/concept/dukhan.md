## Nature and selection
Nature and choice: a fine earthy substance that varies with its kind; it dries its earthy substance and has a little fiery quality. The smoke of tar is the strongest, then moist pitch, storax, myrrh, frankincense and terebinth; the smoke of naphtha may be the strongest of all. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}

## Actions
Ripening and resolving. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}

## Uses by organ
Eyes: the smoke of frankincense and of terebinth enters the remedies for eye ulcers that have no inflammation with them. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}
