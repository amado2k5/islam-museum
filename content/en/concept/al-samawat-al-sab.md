## Summary
God created seven heavens in layers with no disparity; each has its affair and inhabitants, and gates that were opened to the Prophet on the Night of the Ascent, gate after gate, up to the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
