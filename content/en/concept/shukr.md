## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that remembrance and gratitude go together, and that gratitude for a blessing makes God increase it. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.646}

## Ascribing a blessing to its giver
Sha'rawi says that a blessing received with the remembrance of its giver and ascribed to Him is guarded by the giver's protection, while a blessing ascribed to oneself and to one's own strength leaves the person. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.647}

## Gratitude removes pride
Sha'rawi holds that gratitude removes pride, so that one does not say that the blessing was given for one's own knowledge. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.647}

## A practice not adopted
Sha'rawi recounts from unnamed righteous people a practice of drinking water in three draughts, with God's name before each and praise after. The passage does not name its source, so it is not adopted here. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.646}

## Limits of this batch
This is Sha'rawi's view in these pages. Jurists' rulings on gratitude and its limits, the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.647}
