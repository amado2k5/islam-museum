## Overview
The prostration of the angels to Adam is the prostration God commanded the angels when He created Adam and settled him in Paradise, as in [[passage/quran-2-34|Al-Baqara 34]], [[passage/quran-7-11|Al-A'raf 11]], [[passage/quran-15-29|Al-Hijr 29]], and [[passage/quran-17-61|Al-Isra' 61]]. It is among the clearest reported meetings of angels with humans in the Qur'an {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-2-30|Al-Baqara 30]]: "And when your Lord said to the angels: I am placing a vicegerent on earth" — in it God's address to the angels {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-2-31|Al-Baqara 31]], [[passage/quran-2-32|Al-Baqara 32]], [[passage/quran-2-33|Al-Baqara 33]], and [[passage/quran-2-34|Al-Baqara 34]]: in the context of creating Adam, settling him in Paradise, and commanding the angels to prostrate to him {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-7-11|Al-A'raf 11]] and [[passage/quran-7-12|Al-A'raf 12]]: "And when We said to the angels: prostrate to Adam, and they prostrated except Iblis who refused" — in it the angels' prostration and Iblis's refusal {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-15-29|Al-Hijr 29]], [[passage/quran-15-30|Al-Hijr 30]], and [[passage/quran-15-31|Al-Hijr 31]]: "So when I had fashioned him and breathed into him of My spirit, fall prostrate to him * So the angels prostrated, all of them together * except Iblis who refused to be with the prostrating" {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-17-61|Al-Isra' 61]]: "And when We said to the angels: prostrate to Adam, and they prostrated except Iblis who said: shall I prostrate to one You created of clay?" {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-18-50|Al-Kahf 50]], [[passage/quran-20-116|Ta Ha 116]], [[passage/quran-38-71|Sad 71]], [[passage/quran-38-72|Sad 72]], [[passage/quran-38-73|Sad 73]], [[passage/quran-38-74|Sad 74]], [[passage/quran-38-75|Sad 75]], and [[passage/quran-38-76|Sad 76]]: in the context of Iblis's refusal and his blame {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In hadith
- In a narration of al-Tirmidhi in the hadith "when God created Adam He settled him in Paradise... then He said to the angels: prostrate to Adam, and they prostrated" {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- In a narration "what prevented you from prostrating to Adam", in which is Iblis's dialogue with God {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- In a hadith that the angels prostrated to Adam in honour of him, not in worship of him {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- In a narration that "the angels" in the verse are those who have a task toward humanity, such as the guardians and recorders and those who carry out God's decrees on earth, and the command did not include the exalted among the Throne-bearers and the guards of heaven {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.255}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that the angels' prostration to Adam was obedience to God's command, not worship of Adam {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. Al-Sha'rawi holds that worship is obedience to God's commands and avoidance of His prohibitions, and that the prostration to Adam was obedience, not worship {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.254} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.256} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.257}.

## Who prostrated and who did not
- Those who prostrated are the angels who have a task toward humanity, such as the guardians and recorders and those who carry out God's decrees on earth {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.255}.
- The command did not include the exalted among the Throne-bearers and the guards of heaven {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.255}.
- Iblis refused, and he was of the jinn, and the jinn have choice, obeying or disobeying {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.256}.

## Pride and disbelief
- Al-Sha'rawi holds that Iblis's disobedience was at the summit, because he answered the command back to the Commander and thought himself better than Adam, and that his pride led him from one disobedience to another {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.257}.
- He relates an athar naming Iblis "the peacock of the angels" and comments that conceit was his door {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.257}.

## In the transmitted books of tafsir
- Al-Tabari holds in [[passage/quran-2-34|Al-Baqara 34]] that the angels' prostration to Adam was an honour to him and obedience to God, not worship of Adam, and relates from Qatada that the obedience was to God and the prostration to Adam {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p.512}.
- In a narration of Ibn Abbas that "the angels" in the verse are those who prostrated {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes for researchers
- "The prostration of the angels to Adam" is a technical name; the Qur'an calls them "the angels" in [[passage/quran-2-34|Al-Baqara 34]] {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In it is evidence that prostration to other than God is in some cases lawful if it is obedience to God's command {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.254}.
- Their companion in this topic is [[concept/malaika-khalq|the angels of creation]] on its page.
