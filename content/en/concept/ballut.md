## Nature and selection
It is well known and astringent. The sweet chestnut type is the least astringent. The strongest part for binding is its jiffa, the inner bark {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}. The footnote describes the tree from a modern reference, which is not used here.

## Temperament and actions
Oak is cold and dry in the second degree and cold in the first. The sweet chestnut has a little heat from its sweetness. Oak leaf is more astringent and less drying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: the sweet chestnut has a sheen; all kinds bring wind in the lower belly and bind, stop flows, especially the inner bark, and strengthen the organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

Food: the sweet chestnut is slow to digest and the best food of its kind; with sugar it is better food. Galen said it is more nourishing than all grains, nearly as much as bread grain, though the sweet chestnut is more nourishing for its sweetness. Its food is not praised for people in general; it may be praised for pigs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}, {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Swellings and pustules: with the fat of a kid or of salted pig it helps hard swellings; the acorn fruit helps hot swellings at their start {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Wounds and ulcers: burnt and used, it stops thrush and creeping ulcers; the crushed leaf sprinkled on wounds closes them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Head: it causes headache, because its vapour fills the head, and it constipates the body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Stomach: helps dampness of the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Excretory organs: binds, and helps abrasion and ulcers of the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.

Poisons: helps the poisons of vermin; a decoction of its bark with cow's milk helps the poison of Armenian arrows; oak-chestnut meat is good against poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.409}.
