## The two tubes and the sighting arm
To find the size of one of these diameters a sighting arm is made with two curved tubes set opposite each other. The hole nearest the eye is small, and the hole at the body's end is large enough to take in the whole disc of the body. The Sun is then measured with a second arm in the same way, and the two holes are compared, the measurement being taken at one place on the horizon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.189-190}

## One reference point
The text fixes the measurement to one place on the horizon, since the apparent difference changes with the place. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.190}
