## Summary
Ibn Sina says how the asilam is opened and the hand put in hot water so the blood flows, and mentions the artery on the back of the hand that Galen saw in a dream he should open for a pain in his liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.295}.

## Opening the asilam
It is opened until the blood ceases of itself, and the hand of the one bled must be put in hot water so the blood is not held back and comes out easily if it flows down weakly, as it does in most of those bled at the asilam. The best way of opening the asilam is lengthwise. The axillary has the rule of the basilic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.295}.

## The artery on the back of the hand
As for the artery that is opened in the right hand, it is the one on the back of the palm. Galen saw this in a dream, for a true dream is one of the parts of prophecy, as though a commander ordered him to do it for a pain in his liver; he did so and was healed. Another artery, inclined from it toward the inner palm and close to it in benefit, may also be opened {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.295}.
