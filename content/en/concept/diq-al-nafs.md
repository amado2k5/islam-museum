## The breath's narrowness and the signs of its causes
The sign of the tumour inside the lung itself is the heavy pain; in the chest's muscles and partitions the pain is stronger and sharper, and the outward kind is the weakest; in the lung's cartilages it is the pain with a strength in it — the editor glosses al-masis as strength ⁦(the editor's gloss)⁩ — and it may lead to coughing, and if they are hot, fever. The signs of the humours' fullness: if they are in the windpipe, spitting, a longing for the cough, and profiting by it, with a thing spat at the slightest cough; if in the space, a heaviness pouring from side to side with the change of lying, then the spitting appears, and no cough worth the name attends the breath's narrowness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.313}
