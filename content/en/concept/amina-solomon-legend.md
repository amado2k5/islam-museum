## The legend
In a legend known in the Islamic and Semitic traditions, Solomon once entrusted to his wife Amina the ring on which his dominion and his wisdom depended. She gave it to a demon who had taken Solomon's form, and the ring returned to the king only after a long time. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 460}
