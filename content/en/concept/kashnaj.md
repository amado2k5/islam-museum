## Nature and selection
It is something of the truffle kind, very deeply furrowed, which may grow in sands as truffles do; a very tasty fungus common in the author's lands, Transoxiana and also Khurasan. The author says it had not reached him that it harmed anyone as mushrooms and truffles do, and in taste compared with truffles it inclines slightly to sweetness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.528}.

## Temperament
Cold, but less cold than the other truffles and mushrooms; it is not free of a foreign moisture, with dryness in its substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.528}.

## Actions and properties
It is thick and quenching {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.528}.
