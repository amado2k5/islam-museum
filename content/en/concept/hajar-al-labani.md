## Nature
When rubbed with water a milky substance comes out of it; this stone is grey, sweet to taste, is ground with water, and what dissolves from it is kept in a lead box. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}

## Temperament
Moderate. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it helps the start of hot swellings; the rest of this sentence is unclear in the scan and is left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Organs of the eye: rubbed with water and used as kohl, it stops the flow of humours. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}
