## From the spleen
The sign of what comes from the spleen is abundant appetite, because the black bile pours into the stomach, with little digestion from the coldness of the temperament, abundant rumblings on the left, and swelling of the spleen, which does not leave them; a quartan fever may come with it, the bowel may be loose, and the sting of the black bile may cause pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}

## From the stomach
What comes from the stomach is known by the presence of the signs of a swelling of the stomach given in the chapter on diseases of the stomach, and by the illness increasing with indigestion and fullness; at the time of digestion, eating often stirs pains up until they continue, and then they quiet when digestion is complete; if it is hot, burning in the hypochondrium, vomiting of bile and thirst point to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}

## From the hypochondrium
Most of those with melancholia are 'mathul' (afflicted in the spleen, as printed); the sign of the hypochondriac kind is a heaviness in the hypochondrium, a pulling upward, constant retching, a foulness of the soul and spoiled digestion, finding pain in the stomach or between the shoulders, especially after food until it is fully digested; at times he brings up bilious phlegm, at times a sharp sour matter, and these symptoms come with the taking of food or, rather, a while after it, and his stool is phlegmatic and bilious; it eases with good digestion and increases with its lack; a swelling in the hypochondrium may come before it or with it, he finds a twitching in the hypochondrium at times, and the illness increases with indigestion and 'speed of digestion' (as printed) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.107}

## Desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Among the signs of the spleen kind Ibn Sina lists a strong shabaq (desire) 'for the puffing' (as printed) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.106}
