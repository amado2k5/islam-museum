## Overview
Tatfful is entering another person's place to eat his food without his leave or knowledge of his consent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5306}

## Definition and related terms
The tufayli is one who enters a people without being invited. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5306} A guest is one who attends another's meal by invitation or with his known consent; the tufayli is his opposite. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5307} A dhayfan is one who comes with a guest as an intruder; a fudooli acts for another without leave or authority, and the intrusion is most common in material things. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5307}

## Ruling
The Malikis, Shafiis and Hanbalis say it is forbidden to attend another's meal uninvited and without knowledge of consent, and it makes one a sinner if repeated, on the hadith «whoever is invited and does not come has disobeyed God and His messenger; whoever enters uninvited enters as a thief and leaves as a plunderer». {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5307} The Shafiis add that it is intrusion when a scholar or a Sufi is invited and his followers come without the host's leave. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5307}

## The testimony of the intruder
All agree that the testimony of a habitual intruder is rejected, since he eats what is unlawful and does base things that take away manliness; the repetition is required because he may have a doubt of leave until the host forbids him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5308}
