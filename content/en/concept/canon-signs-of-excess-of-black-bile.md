## Summary
Ibn Sina gives the signs of a dominant black bile in colour, blood, thought, the mouth of the stomach, appetite, urine, build and accompanying ailments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.

## The signs
Dry and dull colour, black and thick blood, more brooding and worry, a burning at the mouth of the stomach, false appetite, dark black or thick red urine, a dark and hairy body (black bile seldom arises in white, thinly haired bodies), frequent black vitiligo, bad ulcers and ailments of the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.

## Indications and dreams
Age, temperament, habit, country, trade, season and earlier regimen count too, and frightening dreams of darkness, pits, black things and fears {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.165}.
