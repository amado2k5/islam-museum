## Summary
Al-Battani says no retrograde motion shows in the Sun and the Moon, because the daily motion of each far exceeds the daily change in its equation. Although each does move in the nearer half of its epicycle, the motion is not perceived. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.98}.

## The test
He says he tested the motion of each of the planets, and that the five planets wander most at three places on their epicycles: the far point, the mean point and the near point. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.
