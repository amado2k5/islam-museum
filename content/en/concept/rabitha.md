## Temperament
Ibn Masawayh said it is hotter than the rubyan {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Beneficial for the stomach, it dries the moistures in it, above all when it is eaten with rue, nigella, celery and oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it is an excellent help to desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}
