## How it differs from confusion
The difference between confusion of the mind and ru'una and humq, though both are faults of the intellect and the cause producing both may fall in the middle belly of the brain, is that confusion of the mind is a fault in the acts of thought by way of change, while ru'una and humq are a fault by way of deficiency or abolition; its state is like al-kharafiyya and al-sababa (as printed, not interpreted), and you have learnt that the kinds of fault of the acts are three {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98}

## Causes
As for the causes of this illness, they are either simple coldness, or coldness with dryness enveloping the substance of the middle belly of the brain over long days and periods, or coldness with phlegmy matter in the cavities of its vessels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98} The cause of this kind is cold and not heat because this is a harm of abolition and deficiency, and heat is active for thought, which is a certain movement of the spirit, by which the front of the brain is moved to its back and the reverse; heat stirs movement and helps it, and stiffness prevents it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.98}
