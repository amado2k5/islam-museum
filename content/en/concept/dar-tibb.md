## Temperament
Cold and dry because of the large nerve in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Actions and properties
The nourishment of the udder filled with milk, when digested, is near the nourishment of meat, and the most praiseworthy is the one with milk in it, with spices, for they hasten its descent; it is from the animals of very good flesh, good in humour, thick and strong {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}
