## Summary
The Beast is mentioned in Surat an-Naml among the trials that occur before the Hour. It is described as a creature of the earth that speaks to people when trials are severe, that seals the faces of the unbelieving hypocrites, and that announces to every soul what it has earned. Hadith accounts place its appearance in the last days, when corruption is widespread, and say it does not speak except to the sincere believer.
