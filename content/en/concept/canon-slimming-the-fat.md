## Summary
Ibn Sina closes the fourth teaching with the fifth chapter, on slimming the fat; the regimen is to hasten the passage of food out of the stomach and intestines so the channels do not finish their sucking, to use food large in amount and low in nourishment, to bathe often before eating, to exercise briskly and use dissolving oils, among the electuaries the small itrifal, the lac remedy and theriac, and to drink vinegar with fermented sauce on an empty stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.257}.

## The regimen
The regimen is to hasten the passing of food out of the stomach and intestines so the channels do not complete their sucking, to use food large in amount but low in nourishment, to bathe repeatedly before the meal, to exercise briskly and use dissolving oils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.257}.

## Electuaries and vinegar
Among the electuaries are the small itrifal, the lac remedy and theriac, and drinking vinegar with fermented sauce on an empty stomach; the author promises the full account in the book of adornment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.257}.
