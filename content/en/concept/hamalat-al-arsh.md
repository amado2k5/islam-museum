## Overview
The Throne-bearers are the angels entrusted with carrying the divine Throne; they glorify with praise of their Lord and seek forgiveness for the believers, as in [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir 7]], [[passage/quran-69-17|Al-Haqqa 17]], and [[passage/quran-35-1|Fatir 1]]. They are among the greatest of the angels in station, and their number is eight on the most famous report in the tradition {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir 7]]: "Those who carry the Throne and those around it glorify with the praise of their Lord and believe in Him and seek forgiveness for those who have believed" — and in it their description with carrying, bearing, glorifying and seeking forgiveness {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-69-17|Al-Haqqa 17]]: "And your Lord is God who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then established Himself upon the Throne" — and in tafsir "established Himself" upon the Throne after creating it, and the Throne-bearers are those entrusted with it {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- [[passage/quran-35-1|Fatir 1]]: "Praise be to God, the Originator of the heavens and the earth" — and in it that the angels are God's messengers who carry revelation and the Throne {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-69-35|Al-Haqqa 35]] and [[passage/quran-85-15|Al-Buruj 15]] in the context of the Throne and the dominion — and in them scenes without naming {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In hadith and traditions
- It was related that God created eight angels who carry the Throne, as al-Tirmidhi narrated in "al-Shama'il" and al-Tabarani in "al-Awsat" — and in its chain is a problem according to some of them {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:mustadrak-al-hakim}.
- In a narration "God created eight of the angels... who raise the Throne" {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}.
- In a hadith "as if they were shining stars" in the description of the Throne and its bearers {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- In a narration that "there is no place in the heavens of four fingers except that in it is the forehead of a prostrating angel" — and in it the multitude of the angels {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Their description
- They are among the greatest of the angels in station, the ones entrusted with the Throne which is the greatest of creatures {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.
- They glorify with praise of their Lord night and day, and seek forgiveness for the believers {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In a narration their "feet are in the farthest reaches of the earth and their heads at the Throne" — and it is of the Isra'iliyyat, and the principle is that they are entrusted with the Throne {cite:itqan-suyuti} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that the Throne-bearers are the angels entrusted with carrying it, and mentions that their number is eight on the most famous report, even if another was narrated {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. And al-Qurtubi held that "and those around it" in [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir 7]] are the Throne-bearers and those with them {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## Notes for researchers
- "The Throne-bearers" is a Qur'anic name in [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir 7]], and the number eight is from the tradition, not from the Qur'an {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- Their companions in greatness are Israfil on [[concept/israfil|Israfil's page]] and Jibril on [[person/jibril|Jibril's page]].
- What is narrated about their cosmic description is of the Isra'iliyyat, and the principle is that they are entrusted with the Throne {cite:itqan-suyuti}.
