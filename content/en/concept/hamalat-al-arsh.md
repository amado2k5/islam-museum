## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an mentions them in Ghafir: 'Those who carry the Throne and those around it glorify their Lord, believe in Him and ask forgiveness for the believers' [[passage/quran-40-7|Ghafir 7]], in al-Haqqah [[passage/quran-69-17|69:17]], and in Fatir describes angels as God's messengers [[passage/quran-35-1|35:1]].

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details that the Throne-bearers are the angels charged with carrying the Throne, noting reports differ on their number, the common view being eight {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## Notes
Scholars differ on their number and the manner of their carrying, but agree they are great angels charged with the Throne {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
