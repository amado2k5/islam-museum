## Nature
It was said to be the dregs of saffron oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.648}

## Choice
The best is the fragrant, heavy, black one with no sticks in it; when dissolved it dyes the water the colour of saffron, and when chewed it dyes the teeth with a strong and lasting dye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Actions and properties
Heating and ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Ocular organs
Its power clears the eye and removes its darkness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Excretory organs
It induces urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}
