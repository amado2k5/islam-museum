## Overview
Tajwid is the science of articulating the letters from their points of articulation and performing the recitation properly; al-Shatibi composed 'Hirz al-Amani wa Wajh al-Tahani' — the Shatibiyya — as its comprehensive didactic poem {cite:matn-shatibiyya-ghawthani-2010}.

## In the sources
The Shatibiyya of al-Qasim ibn Firruh al-Shatibi is the poem gathering the foundations of the ten canonical readings {cite:matn-shatibiyya-ghawthani-2010}.
