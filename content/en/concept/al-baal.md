## Nature and selection
A sea drug that resembles qatt, which is the dried green fodder, identified in the editor's note as alfalfa (fasfasa). It also resembles the hindaquqi plant, with many stalks, and its seed is like carrot seed. It sprouts in spring {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.

## Uses by organ
Food organs: very helpful for the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}. Excretory organs: it drives out urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.375}.
