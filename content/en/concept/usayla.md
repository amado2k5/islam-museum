## Summary
In the lexicon, usayla means a drop of semen, a man's water, or the sweetness of intercourse; it is a euphemism for intercourse {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11606}.

## The condition for returning to a thrice-divorced wife
The jurists agree that a woman divorced three times may not return to her former husband until she has married another man and he has separated from her; the second marriage must include consummation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11606}.

## The minimum that suffices
The least that suffices is the penetration of the glans with an erection; al-Hasan al-Basri also required emission, and the rest of the jurists disagreed {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11607}.

## Dissent of Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab
Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab dissented on the requirement of consummation and, according to Ibn Abidin, later returned to the majority view {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11606}.
