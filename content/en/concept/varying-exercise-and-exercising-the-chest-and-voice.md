## Summary
Ibn Sina advises mixing strong and quick exercises with rests or lax exercises, varying the exercises and not keeping to one, each organ having its own; he details exercise of the chest by heavy and sharp voice and by blowing while holding the breath, and warns against prolonged loud voice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Variety
Strong and quick exercises are used mixed with pauses or lax exercises; one should vary the exercises and not stay with one, and each organ has its own exercise. The exercise of the hands and legs is plain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Exercising the chest and voice
The chest and the breathing organs are exercised sometimes by the heavy, large voice, sometimes by the sharp, and sometimes by a mixture, which also exercises the mouth, uvula, tongue and eye, improves the colour and clears the chest. They are exercised too by blowing with the breath held, which is a kind of exercise for the whole body and widens its passages {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Moderation in voice
Enlarging the voice for a very long time is a hazard, since it demands drawing in or sending out much air. He advises starting with soft reading and raising the voice by degrees; when the voice is strengthened, enlarged and prolonged, the time should be kept moderate, and it then clearly benefits, whereas a long stretch is risky for moderate healthy people {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.
