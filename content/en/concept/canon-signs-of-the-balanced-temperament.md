## Summary
In the fourth chapter Ibn Sina gathers the signs of the balanced temperament from touch, colour, build, hair, sleep and waking, character, functions, dreams and digestion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## The body
Among its signs are evenness of touch in heat and cold, dryness and moisture, softness and hardness; evenness of colour between white and red; evenness of build between plump and lean, with a lean toward plumpness, and vessels between sunken and riding on the flesh; evenness of hair in abundance, thinness and curliness, in the age of youth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## Sleep and soul
Also an even state of sleep and waking; ready response of the organs in their movements; ease and strength of imagination, thought and memory; a mean of character between excess and shortfall, that is between rashness and cowardice; completeness of all functions; and sound, good and swift growth with long standing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.

## Dreams and digestion
His dreams are pleasant and companionable, of sweet scents, delightful sounds and joyful gatherings; he is well liked, open of face, cheerful, moderate in appetite for food and drink, digests well in the stomach, liver and vessels, and is even in the way waste leaves him by the usual channels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.163}.
