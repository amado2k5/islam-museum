## Overview
The angels of the Night of Decree are the angels who descend on the Night of Decree by their Lord's permission with every matter of peace, as in [[passage/quran-97-4|Al-Qadr 4]] "the angels and the Spirit descend". They are among the clearest reported works of the angels in the Qur'an {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-97-1|Al-Qadr 1]], [[passage/quran-97-2|Al-Qadr 2]], [[passage/quran-97-3|Al-Qadr 3]], [[passage/quran-97-4|Al-Qadr 4]], and [[passage/quran-97-5|Al-Qadr 5]]: the whole surah of Al-Qadr, in which "the angels and the Spirit descend by their Lord's permission with every matter of peace" {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In the tafsir, "the Spirit" here is Jibril, and the angels descend with every matter {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- In a narration, "the angels" in the verse are those who descend with peace upon the people of the earth {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In hadith
- In a narration of al-Bukhari on the description of the Night of Decree "the angels descend on the Night of Decree..." {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- In the hadith "whoever stands in prayer on the Night of Decree in faith and seeking reward, his past sins are forgiven" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
- In a narration that Jibril descended upon the Prophet on the Night of Decree {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- In a hadith that "the angels" on the Night of Decree are those who record the deeds {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that "the angels" in [[passage/quran-97-4|Al-Qadr 4]] are those who descend with peace, and that "the Spirit" is Jibril {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. Al-Qurtubi holds that "with every matter of peace" in the verse means that whatever good and benefit God commands descends with the angels {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## Their description
- They descend on the Night of Decree by their Lord's permission with every matter of peace {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- "The Spirit" in the verse is Jibril in the view of the majority of exegetes {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- In a narration they descend with peace upon the people of the earth {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes for researchers
- "Angels of the Night of Decree" is a technical name; the Qur'an calls them "the angels" in [[passage/quran-97-4|Al-Qadr 4]] {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In it is evidence of the greatness of the Night of Decree, that it is better than a thousand months {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- Their companion in this topic is [[concept/malaika|the angels]] on its page.
