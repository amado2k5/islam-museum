## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an mentions that the angels and the Spirit descend on Laylat al-Qadr by their Lord's leave, with peace on every matter [[passage/quran-97-4|al-Qadr 4]], the night better than a thousand months [[passage/quran-97-3|al-Qadr 3]].

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir interprets 'the Spirit' here as Jibril, and that the angels descend with every matter, bringing peace to the earth's people {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some held 'the angels' in the verse are those who descend with peace, others that they are those who write deeds; the primary view takes them as God's angels charged with the matter {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
