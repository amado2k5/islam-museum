## Overview
al-Barr — The Beneficent. It is name number 79 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-10-22|10:22]]، [[passage/quran-17-67|17:67]]، [[passage/quran-17-68|17:68]]، [[passage/quran-17-70|17:70]]، [[passage/quran-2-177|2:177]]، [[passage/quran-2-189|2:189]]، [[passage/quran-27-63|27:63]]، [[passage/quran-29-65|29:65]]، [[passage/quran-3-92|3:92]]، [[passage/quran-30-41|30:41]]، [[passage/quran-31-32|31:32]]، [[passage/quran-5-2|5:2]]، [[passage/quran-5-96|5:96]]، [[passage/quran-52-28|52:28]]، [[passage/quran-6-59|6:59]]، [[passage/quran-6-63|6:63]]، [[passage/quran-6-97|6:97]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/arbaun-27|27]]، [[passage/muslim-2552|2552]]، [[passage/muslim-2553|2553]]، [[passage/muwatta-5-880|880]].

## In people's lives
And of kindness: Uways al-Qarni's devotion to his mother was such that his fame reached heaven — the Prophet told his Companions of him before they ever met, though Uways had never seen him. {cite:sahih-muslim}
