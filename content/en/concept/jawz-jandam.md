## Nature and selection
The footnote says the word is Persian in origin, written kawzkandam, meaning earth-fat, and is a kind of plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: Bulus says it has a cooling, quenching and slightly drying power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}. Actions: it stops bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: fattens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.
Wounds and ulcers: cures ringworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421}.
Excretory organs: stirs desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.421} [book listing].
