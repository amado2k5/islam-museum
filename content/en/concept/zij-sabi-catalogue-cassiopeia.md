## Cassiopeia

The catalogue gives Dhat al-Kursi, the Lady of the Chair, before Perseus. Its entries begin with the star on her head, then the star on her chest, the northern star on her side, and the star on her knee, followed by the stars in the middle of the chair and above its leg. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.250}
