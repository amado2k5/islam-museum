## Nature and selection
A tree as if it were the shubram, but taller, grey toward yellow in colour, which some people regard as one of the spurges {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Actions and properties
If some is thrown into a pool it stuns the fish {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Joints
Helpful for gout and the pain of sciatica, the joints, back and hip, and it scatters winds when put in purgative remedies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Excretory organs
It purges the thick humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.
