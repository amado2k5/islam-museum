## Definition
Indibat is not found in the older lexicons; in the modern dictionary its verb is the reflexive of dabata, to hold firm, and in usage it means falling under a rule or general judgement, so that the thing becomes known. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3506}

## Controllability in a cause
Among the conditions of a cause for the usulis is that it be a controllable feature of the wisdom, and not a bare wisdom that cannot be controlled. Hardship is not reckoned in each degree but is controlled by its likely cause, travel. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3506}
Objectors said suitable features such as hardship and deterrence cannot be controlled; the reply is that a suitable feature is controlled in three ways: by itself, such as certainty; by custom, such as benefit and harm; or by the law through its likely cause, such as travel or a fixed penalty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3507}

## Fiqh examples
- The controlled description of goods in a forward sale (salam), such as a mixed fabric whose parts are known in the trade. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3507}
- The controlled injury in retaliation for a wound short of death, such as a cut at the joint; where it cannot be controlled, as with a wound to the body cavity, no retaliation is due. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3507}
