## Summary
The paper by Qeddi Abdelmadjid and Bouzid Issam presents riba as a forbidden excess in financial dealings, divided into two kinds: riba of sales and riba of loans {cite:qeddi-bouzid-islamic-finance-concept p.10}.

## Riba of sales and riba of loans
The authors set out a diagram that separates "riba of sales" from "riba of loans". They say that sales are exchange contracts (muʿāwaḍāt) and that a loan is not an exchange contract {cite:qeddi-bouzid-islamic-finance-concept p.10}. Each kind has its own heading in the paper, with riba of loans first and riba of sales second {cite:qeddi-bouzid-islamic-finance-concept p.11}.

## Division of riba of sales
The authors say that riba of sales divides into riba of excess (ribā al-faḍl) and riba of deferment (ribā al-nasīʾa) {cite:qeddi-bouzid-islamic-finance-concept p.12}. The wording of the definitions on this page must be checked against the printed text.

## Basis of the prohibition
The authors cite the Qur'anic passage on riba in sura al-Baqara, including the command to leave what remains of riba {cite:qeddi-bouzid-islamic-finance-concept p.13}; see [[passage/quran-2-278|Al-Baqara 278]]. The paper also cites [[passage/quran-2-275|Al-Baqara 275]] on the permission of sale and the prohibition of riba; its page has not been confirmed on the printed text.

## What this entry does not cover
This entry does not cover the detailed rulings for each form of riba or the jurists' disagreements about them. The legible pages of the paper do not support that. What is recorded here is the authors' own classification and is attributed to them.
