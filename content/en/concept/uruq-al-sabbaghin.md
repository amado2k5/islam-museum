## Nature
It is well known {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Temperament
Hot and dry toward the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Actions and properties
It has a strong cleansing power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Organs of the head
Chewing it benefits toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Ocular organs
Its juice is very useful in sharpening the sight and in clearing the water and the white before the pupil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is useful against jaundice arising from obstructions, especially with anise and white wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.615}
