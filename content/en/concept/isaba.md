## Summary
In the lexicon, isaba comes from 'asb, a tight binding; the word is used for a turban and for what binds a wound {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11638}.

## Wiping over the turban
The Hanbalis and Malikis allow wiping over the turban in ablution, on the report of al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba, with the Malikis imposing conditions. The Hanafis do not allow wiping over it; they lift it and wipe the head {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11638}.

## Prostration on the turban
Prostration on the fold of the turban is disliked by the Hanafis, Malikis and Hanbalis unless there is an excuse; the Shafi'is do not allow it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11638} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11639}.

## What binds a wound
In cases of need, the jurists permit wiping over a dressing, plaster or splint in place of washing or dry ablution {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11639}.
