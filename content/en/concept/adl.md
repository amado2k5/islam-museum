## Summary
Linguistically, adl is the opposite of injustice: aiming at what is right and standing between excess and deficiency. In the jurists' usage, an adl is one whose good deeds outweigh his bad ones, a person of good standing who is not open to suspicion {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11512}.

## Related terms
Qist is wider than adl in meaning; zulm originally means injustice and going beyond a limit; fisq means going out of obedience and is wider than zulm {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11512} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11513}.

## General ruling
The jurists require uprightness in several matters and differ on some of them: leading the prayer, where the Hanafis and Shafi'is do not require it and the Hanbalis do {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11513}; the zakat collector, where all agree it is required {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11513}; sighting the crescent of Ramadan, the qibla, the purity or impurity of water, the marriage guardian, the executor, the trustee of an endowment, the guardian of a minor, the supreme imamate, judgeship, testimony and the transmission of hadith, with disagreement about whether the uprightness required is the outward or the inward kind {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11514} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11515} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11516} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11517} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11518}. For a narrator, a reasoned criticism is preferred over a bare commendation when both occur {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11519}. Justice in judgement, and justice between wives and between children in gifts, are also required {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11520} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11521}.

## Where it is discussed
Matters of uprightness are treated in their own entries: prayer leadership, judgeship, guardianship, testimony, endowments, the supreme imamate, the sighting of the crescent, and equality between wives and children {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11513} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11521}.
