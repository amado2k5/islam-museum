## Summary
Ibn Sina sets the child's daily order: a bath on waking and play, a little food, a longer play, a bath and a meal, no drinking of water on food; presenting the child gradually to a teacher at six; avoiding wine; and lessening violent exercise up to fourteen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}.

## Its day
When the child wakes from sleep it is better that it bathe, then be left to play for an hour, then be fed a little, then be allowed the longer play, then bathe, then take its meal; and as far as possible they avoid drinking water on food, so that it does not carry the food through them raw before digestion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}.

## Six years and the teacher
When six years of his state have passed he should be presented to the instructor and teacher, and led into this by degrees, and not condemned to keep to the schoolbook all at once. When they reach this age their rest is cut and their exertion before food is increased {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}.

## Wine and water
They are kept from wine, above all if one of them is hot of temper and moist, because the harm that remains from wine, the making of bile in those who drink it, comes quickly to them, and the benefit looked for from giving it, the driving of bile out or moistening of the joints, is not wanted in them, for their bile is not plentiful enough to be driven out in urine and their joints need no moistening. Cold, sweet, clean water is left free to them as they wish {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}.

## Up to fourteen
This is the course of their regimen until they reach their fourteenth year, keeping in view what belongs to them each day of the lessening of moistures and the drying and hardening of the body; they are led by degrees to less exercise and to leave the violent kinds between boyhood and adolescence, and keep to the moderate. After that age their regimen is that of growth and of keeping the body's health, and Ibn Sina turns to speak of the regimen of healthy adults, beginning with exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.220-221}.
