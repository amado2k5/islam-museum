## Summary
Al-Battani says the Moon's farthest distance from the Earth is sixty parts when the Earth's half-radius is one part; its distance from the Earth's surface follows from this. The epicycle's half-radius at this point is read with difficulty in the scan, so its figure is not recorded here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.116}.

## What the reckoning requires
When the epicycle's centre is at the far point, the Moon's farthest distance from the Earth's centre is the figure used for the view from the Earth's surface; this reckoning is a condition for completing the parallax in eclipses. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.116}.
