## Summary
The Khurasanian vault is a multi-ribbed vault that Timurid architects developed from earlier Ilkhanid and Seljuq stelliform vaults. Four large intersecting ribs create a central vaulted area; the square becomes an octagon and then a circle on which the dome rests {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.8}.

## Examples
Timurid examples include the Bibi Khanum Mosque in Samarqand and buildings at Herat. Under the Mughals it appears in the imperial hammam at Fatehpur Sikri and in the Barber's Tomb in Humayun's garden in Delhi {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.9}.
