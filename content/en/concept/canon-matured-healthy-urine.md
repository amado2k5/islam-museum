## Summary
In chapter eight Ibn Sina describes matured, healthy and excellent urine: moderate in consistency, fine in tint toward citron, with praiseworthy sediment and a moderate smell. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.197}.

## The description
It is moderate in consistency and fine in tint toward citron, with praiseworthy sediment of the described kind, white, light, smooth, even and round, and its smell is moderate, neither stinking nor dull. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.197}.

## Sign in an acute illness
If such urine is suddenly seen in an extremely acute illness, it shows a remission coming on the second day. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.197}.
