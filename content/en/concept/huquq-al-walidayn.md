## Summary
In a lesson of his series on rights, Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy sets out parents' rights over their children, beginning with the verses that command kindness to parents and join thanks to them with thanks to God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:01:00} [[passage/quran-17-23|The verse on kindness to parents]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:01:30} [[passage/quran-31-14|The verse on giving thanks to parents]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:02:00}
He says that kindness to parents has become rare in his time, and that many homes no longer know parents' rights. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:02:30}

## Kindness while they live and after they die
The Sheikh says that kindness to parents does not end with their death, whether they are alive or have gone under the earth. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:04:00}
He recounts a saying that whoever pleases his parents has two gates of Paradise opened to him, and whoever angers them has two gates of Fire opened to him; the ruling holds when one of them is still alive. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:29:30}

## The mother first
He says that the person most deserving of a man's companionship is his mother, and that for a woman it is her husband. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:18:30}

## The man who carried his mother
He recounts that a man carried his mother during the pilgrimage and asked Umar ibn al-Khattab whether he had discharged her due; Umar's answer, that it does not repay even one of the pains of her childbirth, is not clear in the transcript (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:20:00}

## Prayer for parents
He says that a servant's rank in Paradise is raised by the prayer of his righteous child. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:28:00}

## Pleasing one's parents
He says that whoever wishes to enter Paradise must please his father and mother, living and dead. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:22:30}
He says that a person who reached his parents without honouring them has made their presence a cause of entering the Fire rather than Paradise. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:23:30}

## Livelihood
He warns against children living in comfort while their parents live below that level. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:20:30}

## A child's commitment to the sunna
He complains that a father may grow angry when his son commits to the sunna, and says this is now called personal freedom. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:24:30}

## From the lectures: prayer, dutifulness, money and the Hajj
The speaker says that when children disobey their fathers and mothers, blessing is taken from them {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:03:00}.
He speaks of paradise and the fire in the context of dutifulness and disobedience to parents; one sentence is garbled and is noted only as a pointer (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:08:30}.
He tells a story of a father who ordered his son to divorce his wife, in which Abu Bakr and other Companions are mentioned; the detail of the narration has not yet been checked (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:13:00}.
He asks what dutifulness to a mother and family means; the answer is not clear in the transcript (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:30:30}.
He attributes to Abu Hanifa a view dividing a right between mother and father; the attribution has not yet been checked (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:26:30}.

## From the lecture series Al Naba Al Safi: kindness and manners at home
The Sheikh says that kindness to parents opens the gates of paradise, and that harsh speech and a rough manner push people away; he cites a hadith that righteousness is a gentle face and gentle speech. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep05 00:21:00}
He says that children should be taught to kiss the hands of their parents in front of them, so that they learn gratitude to the giver. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep06 00:11:30}
He gives the example of a parent who gives a child a phone, and the child uses it to reveal the parent's secrets, and he says that blessings must not be used to anger parents or God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep15 00:16:00}
He criticises young men who boast of their fathers' positions, holds that young people who scorn their parents' words as old-fashioned are mistaken, and says that values do not change with time. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep03 00:13:00}
He says that boasting of fathers and wealth is foolish, and describes a rich youth and a poor youth each boasting of his father. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep09 00:18:30}
He mentions what the Qur'an reports of Ishmael's reply to his father about the dream, in which Ishmael asks him to do what he is commanded and promises patience (needs_source: the sura is not named in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep19 00:09:30}
He presents the counsel of Abraham to his father as a model of gentle counsel based on knowledge. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep09 00:36:00}
He cites a hadith on responsibility for those under one's charge, and applies it to a child who spends his father's money on what displeases God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep15 00:15:30}

## What the Sheikh says parents owe their children
The Sheikh says that the success of children is a duty of parents, that lawful income and good conduct bring blessing, and that parents must be role models, speak kindly and build a home of mercy. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:15:00}
He calls mothers and fathers to gentleness and friendship with their children, and says that advice must not make children feel criticised. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:42:30}
He criticises a father who does not embrace his children and a mother who leaves them to television or the phone, and he quotes a line by Shawqi on the orphan whose mother is absent. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:29:30}
He criticises parents who blame others for their children's faults, who do not teach them daily prayers and remembrance of God, and who neither pray nor attend learning in front of them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep06 00:24:00}
He says that a mother should keep praying for children who do not pray, instruct them, and pray for them and for herself to be among those who keep the prayers. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep19 00:40:30}
He says that a child feels that his parents want the best for him, and that God's generosity is far greater. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep10 00:05:30}
He distinguishes an orphan who lost his father in childhood from a neglected child whose parents are alive but absent. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep10 00:44:00}
He says that a believer is not a fool, and that a lively child should not be called wretched, since the wretched are those in the Fire and barred from God's mercy. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep22 00:09:00}

## Marriage and the parents' consent
The Sheikh says that parents should think about what suits the young person and not impose their own preference, and that the wise path is balance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:44:00}
He says that parents should accept a suitable match from a family known for religion and character, that nobody is free of faults, and that faults should not be used as a pretext for rejecting a person. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep02 00:45:00}

## Teaching children gratitude
The Sheikh says that parents ask children who gave them the bread, so that they learn that God provided it, and parents teach them the formulas said at meals. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep06 00:22:30}
He says that children should be trained to thank their mother for the food she prepares, so that gratitude becomes a habit and grows into care for parents. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep06 00:23:30}
