## Nature and selection
It is well known; it is the coconut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}. The good one is fresh, very white and sweet in its water. If no water is found in it, that shows it is old; the inner skin of its kernel must be removed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot at the start of the second degree and dry in the first; it has some superfluous moisture that does not count for much, and the fresh kind is moist in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}. Actions: heavy, but not bad as food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: its old oil helps pains of the back and hips {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.
Food: heavy on the stomach though little harmful, and good as food; the kernel's skin does not digest and should be removed; no food should be eaten on it for an hour; its fresh oil is a better chyme than ghee, and does not clog or slacken the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.
Excretory organs: stirs desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422} [book listing]; its oil helps piles, especially the old oil; when old it kills gourd-worm and intestinal worms and purges when eaten {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.
