## Summary
In chapter 24 Ibn Sina says motion hurts because of the stretching, bruising or tearing that comes with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.

## The reasons
Motion hurts because of what comes with it, stretching, bruising or tearing; so it hurts not in itself but through these three that accompany it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.
