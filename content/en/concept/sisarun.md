## Nature
It is the wood of the shuniz, with bitterness and astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Actions and properties
It has dissolving power and a slight astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
A decoction of its root helps the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.
