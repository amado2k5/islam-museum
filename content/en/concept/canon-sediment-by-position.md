## Summary
Ibn Sina says smoothness and evenness are praised in good sediment and worse in bad, and that sediment floats, called cloud, hangs, or settles; the settled is more matured in good sediment and the lighter is better in bad. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.196}.

## Smoothness and scatter
Smoothness and evenness are more praiseworthy in good sediment and worse in bad; scattering shows winds and weak digestion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.196}.

## Floating, hanging and settled
The floating is called a cloud; the hanging stands in the middle and is more matured than the first, and the best hanging is that whose fringe turns downward; the settled at the bottom is best matured, this in good sediment. In bad sediment the lightest is the safest, as with black in acute fevers and likewise with phlegmatic or black-bile humour, so the cloud is better than the settled, unless the floating is caused by very much wind. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.196}.
