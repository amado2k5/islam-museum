## Its states
The disorder enters taste on the three known ways, all of it possibly by sharing and possibly from a disease of its own — bad temperament, organic or shared — inferred by what has been indicated {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.255}
## Its treatment
If by sharing, the brain's state is examined and set right as taught in the chapter of the brain's disorders, or the stomach's; if without sharing the tongue itself is treated. If the cause is fullness and a bad humour it must be evacuated — if sharp, by the countering kind, and if thick, evacuated with the gargles named in the chapter of the tongue's relaxation — and its owner fed the sharp foods of this kind (the drug and food names are left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.255}
