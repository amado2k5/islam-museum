## Summary
Ibn Sina completes the places of cupping in the lower limb, naming the thighs, the knee, the ankles, the hip, the space between the knees and the anus, and what each helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.301}.

## The thighs and the knee
Cupping on the front of the thighs helps swelling of the testicles and abscesses of the thighs and shins, and on the back of the thighs it helps swellings and abscesses arising in the buttocks. Below the knee it helps throbbing of the knee from sharp humours and bad abscesses and old ulcers in the shin and the foot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.301}.

## The ankles and the hip
Cupping on the ankles helps retention of the menses and sciatica. On the hip it helps sciatica and fear of dislocation. Between the knees it helps the hips, the thighs, piles, a person with hernia and gout {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.301}.

## The anus
Setting cups on the anus draws from the whole body and from the head, helps the bowels, heals disorder of the menses, and the body feels lighter with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.301}.
