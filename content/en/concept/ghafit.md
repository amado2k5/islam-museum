## Nature
One of the thorny herbs, with a leaf like the leaf of shahdanaj or the leaf of qantaflun and a flower like the water lily, which is what is used, or its sap; in the editor's footnote ghafit is the flea tree (shajarat al-baraghith) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Actions and properties
Subtle, cutting and cleansing, without drawing or evident heat, with slight astringency and harshness, and its bitterness is strong like the bitterness of sabir {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Cosmetics
Good from the beginning of da' al-tha'lab and da' al-hayya {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is applied with old fat to ulcers hard to heal; its sap is beneficial for scab and itch when drunk with the water of shahtaraj and sikanjabin, and so is its flower, the sap being stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Organs of nourishment
Beneficial for pains and obstructions of the liver and strengthens it, for hardness of the spleen, swellings of the liver and swellings of the stomach, as herb and as sap, and it benefits bad condition (su' al-qinya) and the signs of dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Excretory organs
It is given with wine and benefits ulcers of the intestine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Fevers
Beneficial for chronic and old fevers, especially its sap, and especially with the sap of wormwood (afsantin) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Substitutes
Its substitute is asarun with a portion of wormwood (afsantin); the weights are not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}
