## Heaviness and the matter
Heaviness of the head always indicates a matter in it, but the bilious matter makes less heaviness and more burning {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19} The black-bilious makes more heaviness than that and more obsession {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19} The sanguine makes heaviness stronger than both, with throbbing and pain at the roots of the eye from the passing of the humour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19} Phlegm makes heaviness more than all, pain less than the sanguine and the bilious, more sleep than the black-bilious, and dullness of thought, laziness and little briskness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19}

## Heat, cold and the kinds of pain
A hot touch, if it lasts, is a sign of a temperamental heat, and if it arises and harms, of an accidental one, and the cold is judged likewise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19}

Gnawing pains, which make one imagine something creeping and eating in the head, and stinging ones indicate a hot matter, and throbbing ones a hot swelling, their meaning being confirmed by constant fever {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19} Heavy, pressing ones indicate a heavy cold matter, and stretching ones a windy matter, and shifting confirms this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19} The pain that is as if struck with a hammer indicates something like the egg and the chronic shaqiqa {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.19}

The pain also indicates by its direction, as the pain by sharing with the stomach takes one form and that by sharing with the liver another {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.20}
