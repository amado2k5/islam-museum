## Summary
The Gathering Place is mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the place where people are gathered on the Day of Resurrection for reckoning: a single expanse where faces turn white or black, and people are gathered on foot in ranks. It is one of the greatest stations of the Day, where God gathers His creation to present them for reckoning, and the terror there is beyond description.
