## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the Prophet explained that the son of Mary would come again, and relies on a hadith reported by al-Bukhari and Muslim {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1505}. He holds that one who accepts the miraculous birth of Jesus should not find his miraculous end unlikely {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1505}.

## The hadith as Sha'rawi gives it
Sha'rawi recalls a hadith in which the Prophet asks his community how they will be when the son of Mary descends among them and their leader is one of them. He takes this as the Prophet's explanation of what the Qur'an says about Jesus {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1505}.

## The argument from the birth
Sha'rawi addresses those who accept that Jesus was born without a father but stop at the end of his life. He says that accepting the first marvel prepares the way for accepting the second {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.1505}. He does not present this as a proof for those who reject it, but as his own account of the question.
