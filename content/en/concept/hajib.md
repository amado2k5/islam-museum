## Overview

A hajib is the attendant at the door of a ruler or judge who admits people; the encyclopedia distinguishes him from the gatekeeper and the warden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6701}

## Appointing a chamberlain

A ruler or judge may appoint a chamberlain as needed. The Prophet had Anas ibn Malik in that role, and Abu Bakr had one too. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6704}

## Conditions and manners

He must be honest and must not take bribes; the ruler may also withdraw from the public when there is need. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6705}
