## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions small worms that grow in children's bellies, mostly near the rectum, some of them long and flat ones rarely; the long are treated with wormwood water in milk and poultices on the belly, and the small with rasan and yellow roots with sugar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.219-220}.

## Kinds
Small worms may grow in children's bellies and harm them, mostly about the rectum, and the long kind also grows in them. The broad kind rarely grows {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.219}.

## The long worms
The long are treated with wormwood water, a little given to them in milk according to their strength, and at times their bellies need a poultice of absinthe, kabuli myrobalan, ox gall and colocynth fat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.219-220}.

## The small worms at the rectum
For the small ones at the rectum, rasan and the yellow roots are taken, a part of each, and sugar equal to the whole, and given in water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}.
