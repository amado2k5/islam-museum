## Summary
The Moon can hide other stars and planets from sight when its apparent path matches theirs in longitude and latitude. Planets can likewise hide one another when the lower one passes beneath the higher. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.83}.

## The difference from an eclipse
Al-Battani places this hiding beside the solar eclipse: the small nearby body covers the large distant one. The Moon covers stars when it passes them, but only when their paths coincide, not at every passage. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.84}.
