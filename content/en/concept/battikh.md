## Nature and selection
A well-known fruit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.395}.

## Temperament and actions
Cold at the start of the second degree and moist at its end. If its seed is dried it is no longer moistening but drying in the first degree, and the plant's root is dried {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.395}. The ripe fruit is light and the unripe is dense. The unripe melon has the nature of the cucumber and opens in any case; the asparagus mixes best of all vegetables. Its flesh ripens and polishes, especially its seed, which polishes more strongly. It turns into whatever humour suits the stomach, and it leans more to phlegm than to yellow bile, and tends toward black bile. Asparagus does not change quickly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.395}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: it cleanses the skin, especially its seed and its inside, and helps freckles and white patches {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.395}. Eyes: its rind pressed on the forehead blocks the drops from reaching the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.395}. Food organs: it causes vomiting, especially its root; with wine it stirs vomiting without violence. If the melon is not digested well it produces a sudden flux {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.395}. Excretory organs: ripe and unripe, it promotes urine, and helps small stones in the kidney and bladder, especially stones of the kidney {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}. Poisons: if the melon spoils in the stomach it turns into a poisonous nature, so when it sits heavy it should be expelled quickly, and it is better to vomit it with what is possible {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}.
