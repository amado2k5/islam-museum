## Summary
The anwa' (star-risings) are part of the faith of the people of the Jahiliyya in the stars. They used to say "We were given rain by such-and-such a star-rising," attributing the rain to the rising or setting of a star {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38} {cite:sharh-sahih-muslim-al-nawawi 1/59}.

## In The Sealed Nectar's account
The author holds that believing the reports of astrologers is, in reality, faith in the stars, and that among their faith in the stars was faith in the anwa' {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38}. He refers to the chapter "Explanation of the disbelief of one who says 'we were given rain by the nawʼ'" in the Book of Faith in Sahih Muslim, with al-Nawawi's commentary {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.38} {cite:sharh-sahih-muslim-al-nawawi 1/59}. See [[concept/kahana|Soothsaying and astrology]].

## For young visitors
They thanked the stars for the rain instead of God. The Prophet made clear that rain comes from God's favour alone.

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme In the Courts of the Hadith Qudsi
Episode 3 of [[work/fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah|Fi rihab al-hadith al-qudsi]] explains the hadith of al-Hudaybiya on those who credited the rain to God's favour and those who credited it to a star-setting (naw'), which the presenter says al-Bukhari and Malik record from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:00:00} The presenter distinguishes ghayth, beneficial rain in its season, from matar, which may benefit or harm, and says the Qur'an mostly uses matar in the context of the destruction of nations. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:13:00} He criticises phrases such as "the will of the sky" and "the justice of the sky", holding that one should say "God's command" and "God's will". {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:27:30}

[[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Al-Sha'rawi]] says that those who speak of nature and the stars are as if saying that God exercised His power once and then left the matter to the stars. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:18:30} He holds that God is self-subsisting (qayyum), having given each thing its task while remaining over it: He made water fluid and can suspend that property, as when Musa struck the sea and it stood like a mountain. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:20:00} He asks those who credit the rain to the stars who assigned the stars this task: for him they are employees of the One who appointed them, with no power of their own. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:21:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy fi-rihab-al-hadith-al-qudsiyyah/ep03 00:22:00}
