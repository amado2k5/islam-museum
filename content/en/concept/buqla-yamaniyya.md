## Nature and selection
Dioscorides is quoted as saying that the yamani herb has no medicinal use at all. It is watery like the qitf, has no taste, and is more so than any other vegetable {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}. It moistens more than lettuce or gourd, its food value is small, and its passage through the body is not quick, since it has none of the boraxy quality {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}.

## Temperament and actions
Galen is quoted as saying that it is cold and moist in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings: a poultice for hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}. Wounds and ulcers: its root is applied to ulcers of the type called shahdiyya {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}. Head: its juice mixed with rose oil helps headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}. Chest: it helps and calms cough, especially as a decoction with almond oil and sweet pomegranate juice, and it likewise calms hot thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.391}.
