## Nature
The bark of sticks like fuwa, overlaid with dark colour. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Temperament and properties
Warm and moist at about the first degree. A softening agent that breaks the force of hot medicines such as gum. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}

## Cosmetic use
Said to fatten and improve the colour and skin. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.520}
