## Summary
In the lexicon, uqm is a dryness that prevents conception; a woman who cannot conceive is barren, and the word is used of a man who cannot father children {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11773} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11774}.

## Encouragement of offspring
A man who wants to marry is recommended to choose a fertile virgin; the law encourages offspring and forbids what blocks it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11774} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11775}.

## Sterility and the marriage contract
The majority hold that sterility does not dissolve a marriage; al-Hasan alone gives the option at the start of the marriage. The majority do not require disclosure of sterility before the contract {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11775}.

## Injury to the power to conceive
Full blood-money is due for injuring the power to conceive or to impregnate; cutting off offspring by medicine is forbidden {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11775}.
