## Summary
Ibn Sina says purging draws from above and uproots from below, so it suits both the opposite and the same-side draw, while vomiting does the reverse; those least in need of evacuation are people of good food and good digestion and the people of hot countries {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.276}.

## Drawing and uprooting
Ibn Sina explains that purging draws matter from above and uproots it from below, which makes it fit both the draw to the opposite side and the draw to the same side, and fit also once the matter has settled; if the matter lies below, purging draws it to the opposite side and also uproots it from where it is. Vomiting does the drawing and uprooting the other way round, and the effect of venesection depends on the place the blood is taken from. The people least in need of evacuation are those of good food and good digestion, and people of hot countries need it little {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.276}.
