## Nature and selection
Labakh is said to be the lote tree (sidr). It is a large tree that was brought to Egypt, where its taste changed. The book notes that if this is labakh, it belongs under the letter lam, and it treats it here on that understanding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}. Dioscorides says this is a tree found in Egypt with a fruit that is eaten; a kind of tarantula is sometimes found in it, especially in the part of the country towards Upper Egypt. Some claimed that the tree used to kill in Persia, and that after it was moved to Egypt its nature and taste changed, so that it is eaten and does no harm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament for it in this entry.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and bleeding: when the leaf of this tree is sprinkled on the places where blood flows, and applied to the organ, it stops the bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.373}.
