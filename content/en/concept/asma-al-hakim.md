## Overview
al-Hakim — The Wise. It is name number 46 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.
