## Summary
In the lexicon mumathala is resemblance, and it holds only between things that agree. Equality (musawat) may hold between things that differ. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15016} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15017}

## Equal exchange of ribawi goods
For a ribawi good to be sold for another of the same kind, with immediate exchange, the price and the thing sold must be certainly equal, on the saying: 'gold for gold, silver for silver ... salt for salt, like for like'. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15017}

## Retaliation
For retaliation to be due, the offender and the victim must be alike in several respects; if the offender exceeds in one respect, no retaliation is taken, though the jurists differ on some details. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15018}

## Recovering a monetary right
The owner of a right takes its thing if it exists, its like if it is a fungible good, or its value if it has no like. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15018}

## Shares
In the arithmetic of shares, equality means one of two numbers equals the other. The Qur'anic fixed shares are six: two thirds, a third, a sixth, a half, a quarter and an eighth. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15018} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15019}
