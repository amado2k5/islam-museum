## Summary
Al-Nu'man ibn Bashir reports from the Prophet that the lawful is clear, the unlawful is clear, and between them lie doubtful matters that many people do not know {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3330}. [[passage/bukhari-api-52|Sahih al-Bukhari 52]] adds that whoever guards against the doubtful clears his religion and honour, that whoever falls into it is like a herdsman grazing around a protected pasture who is close to entering it, and that in the body there is a morsel of flesh, the heart {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 52}. Ibn Rajab holds that the whole of religion returns to doing what is commanded, leaving what is prohibited and pausing before doubtful matters, and that the report of al-Nu'man contains all of this {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.42}. The same chapter also carries the saying to leave what makes you doubt for what does not {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 2518}.

## In the Sunnah
[[passage/bukhari-api-2051|Sahih al-Bukhari 2051]] reports from al-Nu'man ibn Bashir that the Prophet said the lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear and between them are doubtful matters; whoever leaves what is made doubtful for him of wrongdoing will leave more surely what is plain, whoever dares what he doubts of wrongdoing is close to committing what is plain, and acts of disobedience are God's protected pasture {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 2051}. [[passage/bukhari-api-52|Sahih al-Bukhari 52]] adds the comparison: every king has a protected pasture, and God's protected pasture on His earth is what He has forbidden; it then says that in the body there is a morsel of flesh which, when sound, makes the whole body sound, and when corrupt, makes the whole body corrupt, and that it is the heart {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 52}. The same meaning, with the herdsman, the protected pasture and the morsel of flesh, appears in [[passage/muslim-api-4094|Sahih Muslim 4094]] and [[passage/ibnmajah-api-3984|Sunan Ibn Majah 3984]] {cite:sahih-muslim 4094} {cite:sunan-ibn-majah 3984}. In [[passage/abudawud-api-3329|Sunan Abi Dawud 3329]] the narrator says that he heard al-Nu'man ibn Bashir and does not hear anyone after him say that he heard God's Messenger; he adds that the Prophet sometimes said the doubtful matter in the singular, and then the Prophet gives a comparison: God has set aside a protected pasture, God's pasture is what He has forbidden, and whoever grazes around it is close to mixing with it {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3329}. In [[passage/abudawud-api-3330|Sunan Abi Dawud 3330]] the doubtful matters are ones that many people do not know; whoever guards against them has cleared his honour and religion, and whoever falls into them has fallen into the unlawful {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 3330}. In [[passage/muslim-api-6516|Sahih Muslim 6516]] al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an asks the Prophet about righteousness and sin, and he answers that righteousness is good character and sin is what wavers in your breast and what you dislike people finding out {cite:sahih-muslim 6516}. [[passage/nasai-api-5398|Sunan al-Nasa'i 5398]] and [[passage/nasai-api-5397|Sunan al-Nasa'i 5397]] give a passage about judging: whoever faces a matter should judge by what is in God's Book, then by what His Prophet decided, then by what the righteous decided, and then use his own considered opinion without saying that he is afraid, since the lawful is clear, the unlawful is clear, and between them are doubtful matters, so leave what makes you doubt for what does not {cite:sunan-al-nasai 5398} {cite:sunan-al-nasai 5397}. [[passage/tirmidhi-api-2518|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 2518]] gives an answer about what the speaker had memorised from God's Messenger: leave what makes you doubt for what does not, for truth is reassurance and falsehood is doubt; al-Tirmidhi calls the report hasan sahih {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 2518}. In [[passage/tirmidhi-api-2451|Jami' al-Tirmidhi 2451]] Atiyya al-Sa'di reports that the Prophet said a servant does not reach the rank of the God-fearing until he leaves what is harmless out of caution for what is harmful; al-Tirmidhi calls it hasan gharib {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi 2451}.

## In the reports of the Companions, the Successors and the scholars
In [[passage/jami-ulum-hikam-halal-2-172|Jami' al-'Ulum wa-l-Hikam, vol. 2, p. 172]] Ibn Rajab makes this the sixth report in Jami' al-'Ulum wa-l-Hikam, from al-Nu'man ibn Bashir {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.172}. He gives the text of the report in [[passage/jami-ulum-hikam-halal-1-12|Jami' al-'Ulum wa-l-Hikam, vol. 1, p. 12]] {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.12}, and says in [[passage/jami-ulum-hikam-halal-2-42|Jami' al-'Ulum wa-l-Hikam, vol. 2, p. 42]] that the whole of religion returns to doing what is commanded, leaving what is prohibited and pausing before doubtful matters, and that the report of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir contains all of this {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.42}. He says in [[passage/jami-ulum-hikam-halal-2-186|Jami' al-'Ulum wa-l-Hikam, vol. 2, p. 186]] that whoever goes beyond the lawful and falls into doubtful matters has come as close as possible to the unlawful, and that this points to keeping far from forbidden things and setting a barrier between oneself and them {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.186}. Al-Shatibi cites in [[passage/muwafaqat-halal-2-612|al-Muwafaqat, vol. 2, p. 612]] the Prophet's likeness of the herdsman around the protected pasture who is close to entering it {cite:muwafaqat p.612}, and says in [[passage/muwafaqat-halal-2-673|al-Muwafaqat, vol. 2, p. 673]] that the Qur'an affirms ambiguous verses and that the Sunnah shows the law has doubtful matters, through the saying that the lawful is clear, the unlawful is clear and between them are doubtful matters; such a matter stays doubtful until it is made clear by independent judgement or by following, and then it becomes like the other clear matters {cite:muwafaqat p.673}. Al-Ghazali cites the report in Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din: in [[passage/ihya-halal-4-2481|Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din, vol. 4, p. 2481]] he states that the lawful is clear, the unlawful is clear and between them are doubtful matters {cite:ihya-shamela-dar-al-marifa p.2481}, and in [[passage/ihya-halal-4-5497|Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din, vol. 4, p. 5497]] he says that whoever dares what is doubtful is close to falling into the unlawful {cite:ihya-shamela-dar-al-marifa p.5497}.
