## Actions and properties
Its dung acts as donkey dung acts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Swellings and pustules
It is said that the skin of the foal, burnt and smeared with water on pustules, scatters them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.635}

## Organs of the head
It is said that the growths on the horse's knees, pounded and drunk with vinegar, cure headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}

## Excretory organs
The rennet of the horse especially suits chronic diarrhoea, ulcers of the intestines and dysentery {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.636}
