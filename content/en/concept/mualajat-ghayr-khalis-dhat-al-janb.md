## The non-true pleurisy kinds
And he is given the decoction of Yusuf al-Sahir, which he gives with an oil of this kind; and donkey's milk is beneficial for them especially; and what benefits in it is taking a drug of this kind as described (a decoction attributed to Yusuf al-Sahir, and a line labelled for an animal's milk) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.363}
