## Nature
A desert shrub about a cubit high, with pale leaves, not very white, whose fruit is like pepper, oily and milky. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
Some say it is the seed of another plant the book names in Greek, left out here. The editor's note identifies it as kardamana. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}

## Temperament
Hot, with a little moisture. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it fattens and beautifies. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
Organs of digestion: it is slow in the stomach, but when digested it gives much nourishment. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
Labelled listing, set aside for the founder's ruling: excretory organs: it increases semen and stirs desire. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.490}
