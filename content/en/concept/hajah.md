## Overview

Hajah in the language is want; as a technical term it is what people need to remove a hardship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6706}

## Where need counts

Need is taken into account in worship, such as the relaxed rules for the sick and the traveller, and in custom, dealings and offences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6709}

## Examples

Need permits what would not be allowed without it, such as the ariya sale of fresh dates for dried dates on the tree, and silver plating on a vessel for one who needs it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6710} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6716}

## Need as a basis

The schools differ on whether need is a basis of its own, apart from the text. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6707} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6708}
