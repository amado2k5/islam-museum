## Summary
In the lexicon, 'awl is rising and increase; in inheritance it is the enlargement of the common base of the shares, so that each sharer's portion falls when the base cannot cover the fixed shares; it is a lawful practice {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11943} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11944}. 'Umar was the first to apply it and the companions followed him; Ibn 'Abbas disagreed in the caliphate of 'Uthman, and his saying on the order of the shares is reported {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11944} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11945}. The bases of the shares are seven, and the detail belongs to inheritance {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11945}.
