## Nature
Two kinds, land and sea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Organs of the head
The blood of the land kind is said to help epilepsy when roasted, and the tortoise's gall is for thrush and is dropped into the nostrils of the epileptic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Organs of the chest
Its egg is for the cough of children, and its gall is a smear for quinsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.

## Poisons and bites
The blood of the sea kind with rennet is good against the bites of vermin and for one given spurge to drink {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.
