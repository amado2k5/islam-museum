## Summary
Ibn Sina closes the chapters on purgatives by sending the reader to the pharmacopoeia and the book of single drugs for the purgative and softening drugs, drunk or applied, by age, and for how each drug is corrected, remedied and given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.285}.

## What is sought in other books
He says the purgative and softening drugs, whether drunk or smeared on, are to be sought in the pharmacopoeia, the book of compound recipes, and according to age. In the book of single drugs one seeks how each single drug is corrected and remedied and how it is given to drink {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.285}.

## Pills
He adds that pills should be taken if they have not hardened through dryness, and not while they are still soft and sticky, but when they begin to dry and give slightly under the finger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.285}.
