## Its light treatment
The light treatment of it: the known oil is dropped in, especially at night, the bath entered and the ear laid on the hot ground so the dirt melts; blowing the known into it may also help ⁦(drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.229}
## Tents and what follows evacuation
Of them some is made into a tent set in the ear and drawn out on the third day bringing much dirt and a clear lightness after; some is poured, some dissolved and dropped and left an hour then the place washed. The strong tents are used only after evacuation. Some have claimed camphor is strongly beneficial in hardness of hearing, likely for the bilious kind; scorpion oil, when tried, cures deafness ⁦(drug and tent names and measures are not carried; the claim and the trial are transmitted as attributed sayings)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.229}
