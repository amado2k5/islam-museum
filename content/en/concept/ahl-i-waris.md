## Summary
Ahl-i waris is a term in general use among the Muslim peoples of Indonesia, with the meaning of the Arabic wārith, "heir". It came from Persian usage and reached the East Indian archipelago.

## Encyclopaedia of Islam account
The Encyclopaedia of Islam (2nd ed.) records the term as in general use among the Muslim peoples of Indonesia with the meaning of Arabic wārith. The word is taken from Persian usage and reached the archipelago by that route. The article's bibliography refers to a study of Arabic loanwords in Malay. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 289}
