## Summary
To find the Moon's parallax in the altitude circle, one takes the Moon's altitude at the time sought and its distance from the zenith, and by chords computes how much the altitude falls short of ninety degrees. The parallax then comes out in minutes on that circle. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.118}.

## The tables and the chords
He shows that the chord reckoning repeats what the tables give, that the tables are easier, and that the chord reckoning is needed when the table is not at hand. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.118}.
