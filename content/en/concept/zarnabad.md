## Nature and selection
Nature: roots of a plant resembling sa'd, but larger and less fragrant, grey in colour, brought from China. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Temperament: hot and dry in the third degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}

## Actions
Actions: it dissolves winds. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it fattens and removes the smell of wine, garlic and onion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Chest organs: it gladdens the heart. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Digestion organs: it stops vomiting. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Excretory organs: it binds the belly and helps the winds of the wombs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Poisons: it helps vermin bites greatly, nearly as much as jadhwar. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Substitutes: for vermin bites a like amount of the same is used, with durunj, wild tarkhashqauq and citron seed named alongside; the proportions are left out as measured quantities. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
