## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the Qur'an had two descents: a sending down, in one piece, to the lowest heaven on the Night of Power, and a gradual descent to the Prophet over twenty-three years as events required {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1263} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1264}.

## Sending down and gradual descent
He says the Torah and the Gospel came down once, while the Qur'an came down gradually and in step with events. The verses that join the two verbs, he holds, show the two descents {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1264}.

## Steadying and teaching
He holds that the gradual descent steadied the Prophet's heart, opened the way for the believers to ask, and corrected their mistakes as they arose {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1265}.

## Not yet checked
The theory of two descents is Sha'rawi's reading and has not been checked against the classical exegesis of the Qur'an's descent. His criticism of the word-origin of Torah and Gospel has not been checked in its sources. The academic view and any non-Sunni view have not been checked.
