## Summary
Ibn Sina advises that purging and vomiting be preceded by thinning the humour to be evacuated and widening and opening the channels, which spares the body fatigue, and counts habituating nature to ease and ready response before a strong drug among the successful measures {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.277}.

## Preparation
Before purging and vomiting the humour to be evacuated should be thinned and the channels widened and opened, since that spares the body fatigue. Habituating nature, before the strong drug, to softness and an easy response to the purge or vomit wanted is one of the measures that succeed. Purging and vomiting are hard, tiring and dangerous for people with a wasted hypochondrium {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.277}.
