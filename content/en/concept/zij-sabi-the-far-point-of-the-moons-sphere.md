## Summary
The far point from the Earth is a point on the inclined sphere, touching the zodiac's sphere, which is its highest place. In al-Battani's arrangement it is where the Moon is farthest from the Earth, and the inequality is measured from it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.76}.

## Its role in the reckoning
The centre of the eccentric sphere starts its motion from the far point, and the angles of the inequality that combines the two spheres are counted from it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.77}.
