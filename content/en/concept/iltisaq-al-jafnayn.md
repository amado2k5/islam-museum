## Its states and cause
The lid may come to adhere to the eyeball, either at the conjunctiva, the cornea or both; it may be on one side of the canthus, toward the middle, or total. Its cause is either recent ulcers, or the oculist's error when he excised a sabal from the ball, scraped a pterygium or rubbed a scab from the lid, then did not cauterise it with the drugs we mentioned as a strong cautery and did not watch every moment what had to be watched till it adhered ⁦(what the cautery is done with is named in the book and not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.195}
