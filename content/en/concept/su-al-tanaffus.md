## A general discourse on faulty breathing
The chapter's division: what departs from the natural in breathing comes either of a bad temperament, fullness, a blockage, a neighbouring presser, a hindrance to motion, ulcers in the diaphragm and the chest's quarters, or the power's falling — from a wasting disease, an acute or epidemic fever, or a drunk poison. The sign-rule: the material kind of it grows strong lying flat, middles on a side, and lightens upright {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.312}
