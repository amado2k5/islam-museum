## Summary
Hawam is the plural of hamma, a creature with venom that kills, and sometimes used of harmless insects. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16571} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16572}

## Sale
The jurists agree in general that the sale of creeping things with no benefit is not valid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16572}
The Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis hold that their sale does not conclude; the Malikis hold them pure, and the sale of anything pure and useful valid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16572}
The Shafi'is and Hanbalis permit the sale of bees, as do the Malikis, and the Shafi'is require delivery to be possible. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16572} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16573}
Some jurists permit the sale of silkworms and their eggs before they move, since they are pure and yield silk. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16573} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16574}

## Eating and killing
Most jurists forbid eating creeping things; the Malikis allow all kinds of insects. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16574}
Killing them is generally permitted outside the Sacred Precinct and for those not in ihram. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16574}
