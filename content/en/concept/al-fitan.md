## Summary
The trials are mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the ordeals and tests that pass over Muslims in the last days — a sign of the Hour, including trials beyond description, when God tests His servants. They include events beyond imagination: the blowing of the Trumpet, the emergence of people from their graves, their gathering to the Gathering Place, and their presentation for reckoning.
