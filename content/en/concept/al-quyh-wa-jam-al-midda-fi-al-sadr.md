## Pus and the gathering of matter
And the suppurated man may expectorate a great deal: the author says he has seen one who spat in a single hour near two mann by the lesser weight, or a mann and more than a half; and Galen bears witness that a suppurated man may vomit up near fifty ounces a day — about nine cotylae (a testimony on the book's wording, of the author's own witnessing and of Galen's saying) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.353}
