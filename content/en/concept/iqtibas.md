## Definition
Iqtibas is weaving a piece of the Qur'an or hadith into one's own speech so that it is not presented as a quotation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3155}

## Kinds
It is either where the borrowed words keep their meaning or where they are turned to a new sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3155}

## Ruling
Most jurists allow it in general for lawful purposes, but not in corrupt speech. The Malikis are known for forbidding it and some distinguish verse from prose. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3156}

## Al-Suyuti's division
Al-Suyuti, citing Ibn Hijja, gives three kinds: accepted in sermons, admonitions and covenants; permitted in love poetry, letters and tales; and rejected. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3157}
