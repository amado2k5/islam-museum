## Summary
When the planet is at the far point of its epicycle and the epicycle's centre is at the far point of the eccentric, the true and the mean apogee are one and there is no inequality between them. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.96}.

## Argument from the figure
Using the drawn lines he shows that the line through the centres of the spheres falls on the far point of the zodiac's sphere, and that the planet's place at that moment matches the mean. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.96}.
