## Choice
The strongest of the khabath in drying is the khabath of iron {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Temperament
The khabath of iron is dry in the third degree, that of copper near it, and the rest of the khabath less hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Actions and properties
All of them dry, and the strongest in drying is the khabath of iron {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Swellings and pustules
The khabath of iron dissolves hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Wounds and ulcers
The khabath of silver benefits mange and ringworm of the scalp, heals ulcers and checks the bleeding of "al-nawasib" (so printed; the sense is not settled) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Ocular organs
The khabath of iron benefits roughness of the eyelid, and the khabath of lead benefits ulcers of the eye in place of litharge {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Organs of nourishment
The khabath of iron strengthens the stomach, dries its excess and removes its laxity with old nabidh or tila' (boiled grape must), the substances named and the way of taking it left out; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Excretory organs
The khabath of iron checks the bleeding of haemorrhoids, and tightens the anus; its application with sakanjabin benefits against the harm of the drug called farintus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that the khabath of iron prevents conception and cuts menstrual bleeding and is the utmost in it, and likewise in urine (so printed, without saying what flows); a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}
