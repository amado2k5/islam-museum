## The stated regimen
He takes of the described drugs equal parts, kneaded after crushing with what he stated and used as a rub; and of the described flour an amount kneaded with honey, lozenged and set on a tile or potsherd in the bottom of an oven, or baked in the oven until it can be crushed and is near burning; once burnt it is crushed, the described items are thrown on it, and it is used as a dentifrice in the stated way (the names of this kind left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.282}
