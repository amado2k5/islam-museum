## Ophiuchus (al-Hawi)

The catalogue names the figure al-Hawi alladhi yumsik al-hayya, the one who holds the serpent, and its heading gives the Greek psyllus, the snake-charmer. Its stars are listed by part of the body: head, shoulder, hands, knee, leg and foot. Their positions are in the longitude and latitude table, which is not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.151}
