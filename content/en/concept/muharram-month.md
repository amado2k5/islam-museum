## Name and definition
Muharram is the first month of the lunar year, named for the prohibition of fighting in it among the Arabs before and after Islam; it is one of the four sacred months named in the verse: of them four are sacred. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}

## Its excellence
It is called in the hadith the month of God, and the best fasting after Ramadan is the month of God, Muharram, making it the best month for fasting after Ramadan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## 'Ashura
'Ashura is the tenth of Muharram. The Prophet fasted it and ordered its fast in distinction from the Jews; when told that the Jews and Christians honour it he said: if I live until next year I shall fast the ninth as well. Fasting it expiates the past year. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
