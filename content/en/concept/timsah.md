## Ocular organs
Its dung benefits whiteness of the eye; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.689}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it is said that what is taken from around its kidney, drunk with a drink, stirs the desire for intercourse, and that lettuce seed calms the desire it stirred; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.689}

## Poisons and bites
Its fat laid as a poultice on its bite calms the pain at once; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.690}
