## Nature and selection
Nature: according to the editor's note, zaj is zinc sulphate. The white, red, green and yellow vitriols, with qalqadis, qalqand, suri and qalqatar, all dissolve in water and in cooking except suri, which is very firm and sets hard. Qalqatar is the yellow one, qalqadis the white, qalqand the green, and suri the red. Galen is recorded as having supposed that red vitriol forms from qalqatar; the book doubts this. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Choice: the Egyptian green is stronger than the Cypriot, but in eye diseases the Cypriot is stronger; the unburnt is stronger, and the burnt is finer. The finest are qalqadis and the green, the most balanced is qalqatar, and the thickest is suri. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Temperament: hot and dry in the third degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}

## Actions
Actions: all of them burn and cause the eschar the book names khushkrisha; red vitriol is less stinging than qalqatar; the vitriol of al-asalifa is the most astringent of all, and qalqatar is moderately astringent. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: qalqatar helps redness and running swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Wounds and ulcers: all of them help moist itch and favus, and qalqatar most. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Joints: suri, injected with wine, helps sciatica. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Head organs: it helps nosebleed, especially qalqatar; all of them help gangrene and bad swellings of the gum. Honey-soaked as a wick in the ear, it helps ear sores and the discharge in it. The red one known as suri firms loose teeth and molars, and burnt vitriol pounded with sursinjan and placed under the tongue helps the ranula. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Eye organs: qalqatar especially, and the others in general, help hardness and roughness of the eyelids. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.459}
Respiratory organs: it dries the lung so much that it may kill. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.460}
Poisons: it has a poisonous power, because it dries the lung. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.460}
