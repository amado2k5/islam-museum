## Overview
Polygyny is the permissibility of one man being married to more than one wife, subject to conditions including justice between them; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia devotes sections to its rulings in the marriage chapters {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia's section sets out the conditions of permissibility, the limit on number, and what concerns justice between co-wives {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
