## The Flying Eagle

The catalogue gives the Flying Eagle, al-Nasr al-Ta'ir, after the stars of the Serpent. The entries follow the order of the bird's parts: the star on its head, the star in its neck, and then the stars of its body. The figures are not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.252}
