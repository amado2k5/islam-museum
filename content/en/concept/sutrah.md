## Overview

The sutrah in fiqh is the barrier a worshipper sets before himself to prevent passers-by crossing in front. The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia details its size, description, placement and the worshipper's stance toward it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
