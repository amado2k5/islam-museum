## Summary
At the end of chapter 5 al-Battani says the table of the risings of the signs in the right sphere begins at the first of Capricorn, that the risings of the signs at midheaven for each country are taken from it, and that he will explain how the risings are tabulated when he reaches the risings in the climes, so that he need not say the same thing twice. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.25}.

## What survives of chapter 5
Only the closing lines of chapter 5 appear on the first page of this stretch: the leaf carrying the start of the chapter, printed page 20 of the edition, is missing from this copy and a leaf of the star catalogue sits in its place. The wording of the closing is unclear in places in the scan, so this entry keeps to its general sense: that the table is arranged from Capricorn onward and that one number of parts is used in each country for what stands at midheaven and what rises. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.25}.
