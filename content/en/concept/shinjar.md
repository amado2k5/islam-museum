## Nature
It is the donkey's lettuce, of many kinds, with a leaf like the leaf of lettuce, pointed and prickly, toward black, and it reddens in summer; its stem is like blood so that it dyes the hand {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Choice
Its leaf is the weakest part of it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Actions and properties
The kind called anuqiliya is astringent with a bitterness in it, the kind called filusi is more astringent, the kind called anulus is more so than both and sharper, and the one without a name is close to it; in all of it there is astringency and drying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Cosmetics
A smear beneficial for bahaq and jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Swellings and pustules
It is laid as a poultice with fat and smeared on peeling, and with oil of thyme on the carbuncle, especially the kind called falus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Ulcers
It heals ulcers when used in the cerate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Organs of the head
It is the most beneficial thing for pains of the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits jaundice when drunk, especially anuqiliya, and especially pains of the spleen, and its bark tans the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Excretory organs
The one without a name, with qardamana, hyssop or harf, expels worms and the tapeworm, and the kind called anuqiliya is beneficial for pain of the kidneys {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Poisons and bites
The kind called yafusus is very beneficial for the bite of the viper when used as a poultice or a drink, and the one without a name is close to that {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}
