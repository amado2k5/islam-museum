## Overview
The Praised Station of 'it may be your Lord raises you to a praised station' is the station of the Great Intercession on the Day, with the majority of exegetes {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the hadith
In the long hadith of intercession in the two Sahihs the people come to the prophets one by one asking them to intercede with their Lord to relieve them; each excuses himself by his sin or by its not being his, until they come to Muhammad, who goes and prostrates under the Throne until it is said: O Muhammad, raise your head, intercede and you will be granted, ask and you will be given {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
