## Overview

Dhanb in the legal-religious terminology is sin and what incurs accountability. The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats repentance from sins, their expiation through istighfar, and the kaffarat of sins {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
