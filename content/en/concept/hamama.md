## Nature and selection
Nature: Dioscorides says it is a tree like a cluster of woody parts interlaced, with broad large leaves resembling those of fashra, a small flower, a golden colour and a fragrance; the wood is ruby-coloured. One kind grows in damp places and is weaker; another is neither long nor wide. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Selection: the best is the first, golden, fresh, Armenian, bitter and sweet-smelling; the second is green-wooded, weak in smell and poorer; the third best is the modern kind inclined to white and red, dense and smooth, sharp to the tongue, and avoiding crumbling. It is adulterated with a drug called armumis, which resembles it but has neither smell nor fruit; the test is to rub the crumbs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Actions: it thins and ripens, has some binding, and its strength is like that of wajj. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it ripens hot swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Joints: its decoction is drunk for gout, and the patient sits in it for the same. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Organs of the head: it weighs on the head, causes headache and sleep; it is one of the intoxicating and sleep-inducing drugs; some say that when applied to the forehead it removes headache. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Organs of the eye: its decoction is applied for hot ophthalmia. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Organs of the chest: helps the cold pleurisy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Organs of digestion: it opens obstructions of the liver, and its decoction is drunk for liver ailments; it digests more easily than wajj. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.476}
Excretory organs: it brings the urine, helps the pains and ulcers of the womb, is sat in for pain of the kidneys, is drunk for pains of the womb, and helps swellings of the viscera. The womb uses here are a labelled listing set aside for the founder's ruling. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.476}
Poisons: when applied with bazrawaj it helps the sting of the scorpion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.476}
