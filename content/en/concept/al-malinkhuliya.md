## Nature
Melancholia is the name for a change of the opinions and thought from the natural course into corruption, fear and badness, from a black-bile temperament that makes the pneuma of the brain feel alone from within and frightens it with its darkness, as outer darkness does {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103} For Ibn Sina a cold and dry temperament is hostile to the pneuma and weakens it, while a hot and moist temperament, like the temperament of the drink (sharab), suits the pneuma and strengthens it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}

## Relation to mania
If melancholia is left with fretfulness, agitation and fierceness it passes on and is called mania; the name melancholia is given to what arises from burnt black bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}

## Where the cause lies
The cause of melancholia is either in the brain itself or outside the brain; the two cases are set out in [[concept/asbab-al-malinkhuliya-al-dimaghiyya]] and [[concept/asbab-al-malinkhuliya-min-al-tihal-wa-al-miraq]] {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.103}
