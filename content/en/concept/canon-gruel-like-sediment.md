## Summary
Ibn Sina says the meal-like and gruel-like sediment comes mostly from burning of blood when red, and from the melting of organs when whitish; the black kind comes from burnt blood and is bad in acute diseases. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.194}.

## Source by colour
The meal-like and gruel-like is mostly from burnt blood when it leans to red; it may often come from melting and scraping of organs when it leans to white, and less often from a scabbed bladder. If it leans to black it comes from burnt blood, particularly in the spleen, kidney and urinary passages. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.194}.

## The judgement
All sheet-like sediment not caused by something in the bladder, kidney or urinary passages is bad and deadly in acute diseases. Matured urine shows the veins are sound, since kidney ailments do not prevent maturation of urine, that happening above them. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.194}.
