## Nature
It is the qatf, a well-known potherb of two kinds, one wild and one garden, and either may be cooked and eaten {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Temperament
Cold and moist in the first degree, and moderate according to some {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.
