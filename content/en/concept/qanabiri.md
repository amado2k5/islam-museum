## Temperament
Hot in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Actions and properties
Subtle, cleansing and cutting; it is reported from Paulus that it generates black bile, especially what is pressed with salt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Cosmetics
It clears freckles and vitiligo; the book says it is the most beneficial thing for leucoderma, eaten and as a poultice, and adds that this is something the Arabs know {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Wounds and ulcers
A poultice of its leaf benefits malignant ulcers of the breast {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Organs of the head
Its root, taken as a snuff, benefits the thick moistures in the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Respiratory organs
It opens obstructions of the lung and cleanses it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It opens obstructions of the liver and spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Excretory organs
Its juice loosens the bowel, it is a poultice for piles, removes colic and brings out the thick chymes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it dissolves hardness of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}

## Poisons and bites
Qanabiri is a poultice for the stings of all vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.652}
