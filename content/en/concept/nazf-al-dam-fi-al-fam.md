## Its treatment
If its coming out is from the mouth's substance and skin, its treatment is by the astringents named in the pustule chapter and others, and a decoction of twigs of this kind has great benefit; if it is from another place, a chapter — indeed chapters — have been set apart for it (the names left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.264}
