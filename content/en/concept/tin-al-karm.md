## Nature and choice
Dioscorides says this earth may be found in the land of Syria. It is black, like charcoal of cedar wood, and some pieces are smooth. The book advises choosing the black kind; the whitish grey kind that does not dissolve in water or oil when ground is poor. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Properties
It dries without much sting, has a little dissolving power, and a cooling power. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Adornment
It is put in eye salves that the book says make hair grow, and in dyes for the hair and eyebrows. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}

## Excretory organs
The vine is smeared with it until its leaves and shoots begin, to kill worms. Taken by mouth, the book says, it kills worms and snakes in the intestines. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.505}
