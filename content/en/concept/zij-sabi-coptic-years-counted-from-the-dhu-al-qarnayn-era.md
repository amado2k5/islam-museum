## Summary
Al-Battani converts a Coptic date from the years of Dhu al-Qarnayn: he subtracts 287 from the completed years, divides what remains by four for the quarter days, and when a fraction occurs and it is not the leap month the year is taken as leap, and the days are counted from Tut. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.103}.

## The constant 287
The constant 287 subtracted from the Dhu al-Qarnayn years in this rule is read from a damaged passage; it is recorded as read, with caution, and needs checking in the Latin translation. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.103}.
