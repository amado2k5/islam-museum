## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan presents his project of 'Islamic management': rewriting the science of management from the philosophy of tawhid and the principle of following the Prophet, rather than copying Western categories and pasting verses and hadiths onto them; he says he has begun writing it and has not published it because it has not yet matured in his view {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:12:30}.

## His critique of existing books (his view)
Al-Suwaidan holds — this is his personal view — that the 'Islamic management' books on the market are Western management coloured with verses and hadiths and not built on an Islamic philosophy: Western management has an individualist, materialist philosophy, and whoever wants to write Islamic management must master two fields — management in depth, its root and philosophy, and the Islamic side of creed, sharia, its purposes, sira and history {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:11:00}.
He says his starting point is that any science called Islamic must proceed from Islam's foundation, tawhid, so he asks: what effect does the creed of tawhid have on my management as a Muslim manager? {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:13:00}.

## His tool: classifying the Prophet's roles
In his project he relies on the book Af'al al-Rasul wa-Dilalatuha ala al-Ahkam al-Shar'iyya (The Prophet's Acts and Their Significance for Legal Rulings) by Dr Muhammad al-Ashqar — a doctoral thesis he judges the best written on its question — which classifies thirteen roles in which the Prophet acted, sometimes as army commander, head of state, adviser or judge, the Companions distinguishing between them and asking when they could not {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:14:00}.
He ties this to usul al-fiqh and the purposes of the sharia to rewrite Islamic management {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep01 00:16:30}.
