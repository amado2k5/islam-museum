## Summary
Ibn Sina sets out in brief what each humoral type needs of food: the melancholic much moistening and a little warming, the bilious what moistens and cools, one whose blood is hot cold foods of little nourishment, and one whose blood is phlegmatic foods of little nourishment with warmth and attenuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.229}.

## Melancholic and bilious
The melancholic needs food that is much moistening and little warming; the bilious needs what moistens and cools {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.229}.

## Hot-blooded and phlegmatic-blooded
One in whom the blood generated is hot needs cold foods of little nourishment; one in whom the blood generated is phlegmatic needs foods of little nourishment that carry warmth and attenuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.229}.
