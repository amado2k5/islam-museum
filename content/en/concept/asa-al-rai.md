## Nature
It is the batbat, male and female, and the male is the stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Actions and properties
It has astringency, but the watery part in it is large, and because it repels the descending matter so much it is thought to be drying, and likewise it prevents haemorrhages {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Swellings and pustules
As a poultice it is very beneficial for phlegmon, erysipelas, herpes and the swellings of ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Wounds and ulcers
It heals very fresh wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Organs of the head
Its juice kills worms of the ear and dries its ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Organs of the chest
Its water benefits spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is used as a poultice for inflammation of the stomach; it is cooling and beneficial {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Excretory organs
It heals ulcers of the intestines, and Dioscorides claimed that it promotes urine and relieves the one with retention {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as preventing the flow of blood from the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}
