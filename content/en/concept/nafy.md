## Summary
Nafy, in the lexicon, is banishment, driving out and removal. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15947}

## Basis and nature
Established by the Qur'an, the Sunna and consensus, with the reports of the Companions' practice of combining lashes and exile. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15949} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15950}
Required in banditry and for an unmarried free fornicator by most jurists; permitted as discretionary punishment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15950}

## Length and place
Fornication: a year by the majority, counted from arrival at the place of exile. Banditry: until repentance is evident, or death. Discretionary exile: as the judge sees fit. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15952} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15955}
Place: exile to a distance that shortens prayer, under supervision, or imprisonment, according to the schools. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15955} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15956}

## Ending, pardon and repentance
It ends with the term, death, or insanity (majority), and with a fatal illness (Muhammad's view). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15959} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15960}
Pardon removes exile when it is a private right but not for fornication; intercession is allowed in discretionary exile. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15960} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15961}

## Exile of women
The Shafi'is and Hanbalis exile a woman only with a mahram or trustworthy women; the Hanafis and Malikis do not exile women. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15957} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15959}
