## Summary
Ibn Sina sets out for each nursing: drawing off a little milk, helping the child by pressing, a lick of honey, nursing little by little, handling distension and wind, changing the nurse when she is ill or taking a drug, not rocking the cradle roughly, and that the natural period of nursing is two years {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.

## At each nursing
At each nursing, and especially the first, some milk should be drawn off and let run, and the child helped by pressing so that the strength of sucking does not force it to hurt the throat and gullet and parch it. A spoon of honey licked before each nursing is useful, and mixing it with a little wine is right {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.

## Little and often
It should not be given a great deal of milk at once but little, repeatedly, for nursing it to fullness at one time may bring distension, wind, much gas and whitish urine. If that happens it should not nurse, but be kept hungry or occupied with sleep until it digests. At most it nurses three times a day in the first days {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.

## The nurse's condition
If the nurse has a bad temperament, a painful illness, heavy diarrhoea or a harmful retention, it is better that another woman nurse until she recovers, and likewise when she must take a drug of strong effect {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.

## After nursing
If it sleeps after nursing it is not roughly shaken, with the cradle churning the milk in its stomach, but rocked gently. A little crying before nursing helps it, and the natural period of nursing is two years {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.210}.
