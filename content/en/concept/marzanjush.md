## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.563}.

## Actions and properties
Fine, opening and dissolving; the force of its oil is warming without restraint, and sharp {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Cosmetic uses
Its water is put in the cupping glass and the limb is painted with it after the cupping, which prevents the whiteness that comes at scarification after cupping; the dried herb is painted on blood bruising and its greenness, especially under the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Swellings and pustules
It is a paint on phlegmatic swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Joints
It enters the cerate that is painted on twisted sinew; it helps pain in the back and the groin, and with honey helps fatigue; its oil is also a poultice for the palsy that bends the neck backward and for other palsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It opens obstructions of the brain and helps migraine, headache and humidity, melancholic headache and thick winds, and helps earache as a fomentation and as drops. A piece dipped in marzanjush oil is put in the ear, which helps its blockage {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its decoction helps dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Excretory organs
Its decoction helps difficulty of urine and colic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Its oil warms and refines and helps the closing of the womb that leads to its suffocation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.

## Poisons and bites
With vinegar it is a poultice for scorpion sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.564}.
