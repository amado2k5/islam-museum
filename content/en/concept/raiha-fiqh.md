## Summary
Smell (raiha) is an accident perceived by the sense of smell, pleasant or foul; some use the word rih only for a pleasant scent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8564}

## General ruling
The ruling depends on the chapter of law in which the smell matters. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8564}

## Purity of water
If an impurity changes one of the attributes of water, most jurists say the water loses its purifying power, since it is no longer water in the plain sense; the Hanafis say it keeps it unless its nature as water is lost. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8565}
A scent that merely passes near the water without mixing with it does not take away its power. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8565}

## Scent for the pilgrim
A pilgrim in ihram may not use what is worn for its scent, such as musk and aloes, but may use what is not worn for its scent, such as apples or citron. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8565}

## Smells in mosques
Mosques are to be perfumed and kept free of foul smells such as garlic and onion; a person who has eaten them is kept away, and the Malikis allow releasing wind when there is need. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8565} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8566}

## Harm from smell
A person who runs a smelly workshop among homes and causes deaths by it is liable, because he has broken custom. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8566}

## Proof of drinking wine
Most jurists hold that the smell of wine on a person's breath does not prove the hadd penalty, because of other possibilities; the Malikis accept it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8566} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}

## Meat and milk of the jallala animal
Most jurists dislike eating its meat and drinking its milk when impurity has changed their smell; the Shafi'is forbid them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}

## The husband's right
A husband may forbid his wife what gives him an unpleasant smell, and may require her to remove foul odours from body and clothes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8567}
