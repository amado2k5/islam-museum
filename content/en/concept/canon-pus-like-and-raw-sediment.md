## Summary
Ibn Sina says pus-like sediment shows a burst ulcer, especially in the urinary organs, and he tells it from raw sediment by stench, a preceding swelling, and parts that gather and scatter easily. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Pus-like
Pus-like sediment shows a burst ulcer, especially in the urinary organs, particularly when a praiseworthy settled residue accompanies it. Some of it mixes thoroughly with the watery part and some separates. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## How it differs from raw matter
Pus-like sediment comes with stench and a prior sign of swelling, and its parts gather and scatter easily; raw sediment is turbid and thick and neither gathers nor scatters easily. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.
