## Summary
Sha'rawi separates the authority of compulsion, forcing a person to do what he does not want, from the authority of argument, persuading him. He holds that Satan has neither; he finds a desire in the self and follows it. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.36}

## Obedience and testing
Sha'rawi holds that obedience with no temptation offered does not show the strength of faith, as an honest employee's integrity is not tested unless he is offered a bribe. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.37}

## The directions of misguidance
Sha'rawi says that Iblis came from the four directions and not from above or below, because divine height and human submission in prostration are beyond his reach. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.33}

## Iblis's disobedience and Adam's
Sha'rawi separates Iblis's disobedience, which answered the command with refusal, from Adam's, which admitted weakness and asked for forgiveness. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.35}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's reading and is attributed to him. The scan gives no kalam or exegetical view on Satan's authority, and none has been checked. The edition is unverified.
