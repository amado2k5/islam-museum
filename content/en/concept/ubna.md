## Summary
Ubna is, in the jurists' usage, a condition in the inner rectum that makes a person desire the forbidden act of the people of Lot. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1466}

## General ruling
The afflicted person is asked to struggle against the desire and to refrain from its causes. If he commits the forbidden act, the rules of sodomy apply to him; if another accuses him of it, the rules of slander apply to the accuser, as a fixed punishment or a discretionary one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1467}

## Places of discussion
Jurists treat ubna in the context of following an imam in congregational prayer (where it can invalidate the following), in the option of defect in a sale, in slander, and in the rules of sodomy in the chapter on fixed punishments. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1467}
