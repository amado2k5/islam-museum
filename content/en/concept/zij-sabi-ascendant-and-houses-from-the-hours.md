## Summary
Al-Battani shows how to find the ascendant and the other eleven houses from the hours that have passed since sunrise or sunset. If the time is daytime the hours are taken from sunrise to the given hour; if it is night they are taken from sunset. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.109}.

## Equal and seasonal hours
If the hours are equal they are multiplied by a fixed figure; if they are seasonal the daytime hours are multiplied by the daytime hour-lengths and the night hours by the night hour-lengths, and the ascendant is then read from the rising-times in the tables. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.109}.
