## Overview
*Wilaya* (sainthood, friendship with God) is set out in Ibn Rajab's commentary on the thirty-eighth hadith of the Forty, a sacred hadith about God's friend and drawing near to Him. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.770-771}

## Who is the friend
The commentary says that the friend is one who has stood firm in the obligations, then advanced by supererogatory acts until he became beloved to God. The hadith says that his hearing, his sight, his hand, and his foot are by God. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.770-771}

## The two degrees of drawing near
There are two degrees of drawing near on which friendship rests. The obligations come first, and they are the most beloved of the approaches to God. Then the supererogatory acts follow. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.770-771}

## Enmity toward the friend
The hadith says that whoever is hostile to a friend, God declares war on him. The commentary considers this the greatest threat concerning those who love Him. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.770-771}

## In Ahmad Farid's lessons 'The Boat'
In [[work/the-boat-ahmad-farid|'The Boat']] [[person/ahmad-farid|Ahmad Farid]] counts God's guardianship as the first fruit of the tree of faith: God is the patron of those who believe, as they take Him as patron with love and support for His religion, and He brings them out of darkness into light. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep04 00:01:30} He reads the present tense of 'brings them out' as continual: at every moment they are brought out of the darkness of associating, ignorance, heedlessness, doubt and showing off into the light of oneness, knowledge, remembrance, certainty and sincerity. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep04 00:02:00} He then recalls the sacred hadith, which he gives from al-Bukhari through Abu Hurayra, in which God declares war on whoever is hostile to one of His friends, and draws from it that God's friends must be befriended and never shown enmity. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep04 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep04 00:03:00}
