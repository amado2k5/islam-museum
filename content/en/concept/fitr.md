## Nature
Dioscorides said that it is of two kinds, one eaten and one that kills; there are many reasons why a mushroom is deadly, among them its growing near rusty nails, rotten rags, the nests of some harmful vermin, or the roots of trees whose property is that the mushroom growing near them is deadly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.632-633}. A slimy moisture or a rot like a spider's web may be found on this kind, and when cut and picked it spoils at once and rots quickly. The other kind is used in broths and eaten, and is delicious, but if much is taken it harms and may kill because it is not digested, and may choke or bring on cholera, and it stirs the black-bile diseases {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Temperament
Cold at the end of the third degree, moist near it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Actions and properties
It engenders a thick, bad humour, and the book gives its correction as boiling it with fresh and dry pears and wild basil and drinking a strong wine after it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}. Of the kind known as qala'i the book says that it has killed no one but cholera may come of it, and the dried is less bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Organs of the head
It brings on numbness and apoplexy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Organs of the chest
From the kind that does not kill, choking may come {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
From the kind that does not kill, cholera may come if much is taken; it is hard to digest and much nourishing, and from the deadly kind come fainting and cold sweat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Excretory organs
It brings on difficulty in urination {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}

## Poisons and bites
A warning only: part of it is deadly, the kind that grows beside rusty iron, or rotten things, or near the dwelling of some vermin, or at some trees such as the olive; its sign is a slimy, rotting moisture on it and quick change and rot in it, and tightness of breath and fainting come from it, and it has often killed within the day. For its treatment the book names cutting drugs and oxymel with fudhanj, or much honey, and for the harm that comes from eating it, borax, natron, ash-water with vinegar and salt, or a barley decoction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.633}
