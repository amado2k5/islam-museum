## The motion of one degree in eighty years
Al-Battani reports from Ptolemy that the talisman-makers claimed the sphere has a slow shifting motion of one degree in every eighty years, which runs up to eight parts and then turns back. Their claim meant that the zodiac moves from west to east with the fixed stars by eight parts and then from east to west by eight parts, against the first motion. Al-Battani replies that one body cannot make two motions in two opposite directions. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.194-195}

## The date of the turning point
The text puts the turning point of the motion one hundred and twenty-eight Egyptian years before the reign of Augustus, that is the year one hundred and sixty-six of Alexander, and reckons the years since then at one degree per eighty years. This dating needs checking against the calendar named. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.194-195}
