## Its causes
Blood bursting from the ear may run the course of nosebleed in being a crisis; it may come of a repletion leading a vessel to split, break or open; or of a collision or a blow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.229}
## Its treatments
The crisis kind must not be checked unless it leads to weakness and fainting; the rest is checked — by astringents, caustics or coolants — and of each kind he names what is known, some of it tried ⁦(drug, juice and tried-remedy names and measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.229}
