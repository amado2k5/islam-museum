## Overview
Sadaqah in legal usage is what is given seeking the reward; the jurists treat its rulings in the chapters of zakat and vows {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}.

## In the sources
Fiqh al-Sunna details sadaqah in the zakat chapters and the rulings of vows and expiations {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}; the encyclopedia treats its related terms {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
