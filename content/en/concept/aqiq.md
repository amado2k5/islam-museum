## Summary
In the lexicon, al-aqiq is a valley cut by a torrent, and a red stone used for the settings of rings {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11780}.

## The valley as a station of ihram
The Shafi'is prefer that people of the east begin ihram from al-Aqiq; the Malikis and Hanbalis hold that the station for the people of Iraq is Dhat 'Irq {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11781}.

## The stone
The majority do not allow tayammum with it, while the Hanafis allow it as being of the kind of earth. The majority hold that it carries no zakat unless it is held for trade; the Hanbalis require zakat on it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11781}. It is not subject to riba; the schools differ on advance sale of it, and most jurists allow men to wear it as an adornment {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11782}.

## Where to look further
Stations of ihram are treated under «mawaqit», tayammum under «tayammum», and zakat under «zakat» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11781}.
