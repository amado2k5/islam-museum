## The grades of the ointments
At the start an oil of the known kind suffices alone, or with a wax and fat of the known kind; stronger is an ointment of the known kind, especially mixed with the mucilage of a seed of the known kind; if more drying is wanted, a dross of the known kind is put in, and the dross may be soaked alone in an oil of the known kind. If the ailment grows somewhat severe an ointment of the known kind is used; if it turns chronic and very severe, an ointment of the known kind is taken, and stronger than it a smear cooked in a copper vessel till it reaches the consistency of honey ⁦(the oil, fat, dross and smear names and measures are carried only by kind)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.247} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.248}
