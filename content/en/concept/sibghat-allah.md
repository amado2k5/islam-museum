## Summary
Sha'rawi reads the dye named in [[passage/quran-2-138|2:138]] as faith that penetrates a person the way dye penetrates a material, not a coating that can be removed. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.612}

## Dye and coating
Sha'rawi distinguishes dye from coating: henna is a dye that penetrates living matter and cannot be taken off at will, while nail varnish is an outer layer that can be removed moments after it is applied. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.612}

## The fitra as God's dye
Sha'rawi ties the dye to the fitra that God set in the heart, citing the hadith on the fitra as given in the scan's footnote to al-Bukhari, Muslim and others: a child born on the fitra stays on it if his parents are Muslim, and turns from it if they make him a Jew or a Christian. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.613}

## What gives no dye
Sha'rawi holds that the differences of human colour are part of God's dye in the very make-up of people, and that a baptismal rite gives no such dye, since religion comes from inside the person, and that faith moves the whole body. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.613}

## Limits of this batch
This is Sha'rawi's view in these pages. Other readings of the fitra and of the hadith in the footnote, the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.613}
