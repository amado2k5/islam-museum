## Summary
Al-Sha'rawi separates judicial ruling, which rests on evidence before a judge, from religious truth, which concerns a person before God. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2035}

## Judging by what appears
He holds that a judge rules on the evidence before him and cites the report of Umm Salama; the book's footnote names its transmitters, and its wording is not reproduced here. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2035}

## When the debtor has paid
He says that if the debtor has paid, heirs may not claim the debt again once they know their relative had settled it, because a judicial ruling may differ from the religious position. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2036}

## Forbearance is not permission
He says God's forbearance toward someone who does wrong in this world does not show that the act was lawful; it is respite, and punishment remains in the next life. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2036}
