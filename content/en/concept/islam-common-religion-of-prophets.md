## Overview
Muhammad 'Abd Allah Daraz shows that "islam" in the Qur'an names not one particular religion but the shared religion all prophets preached: Noah, Jacob, Moses and the Apostles count themselves among the muslimin {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 49}. The Qur'an gathers prophets and followers into one nation with one God and one law in its roots {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 50}. This religion is turning to God in pure submission unmixed with shirk, believing all that came from Him without distinguishing between book and book or messenger and messenger {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 50}.
