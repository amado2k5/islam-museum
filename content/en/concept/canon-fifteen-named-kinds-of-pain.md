## Summary
In chapter 20 Ibn Sina lists the kinds of pain that have names, counts fifteen genera, and then gives the cause of each {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## The list
The kinds of pain that have names are: itching, rough, pricking, pressing, tensive, tearing, breaking, loose, boring, piercing, dull, throbbing, heavy, fatigue and irritative. He says these are fifteen genera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## Where each is treated
Each genus gets its own line beginning with the cause of that pain. In the museum they are grouped into records: itching, rough and irritative; pricking; tensive and pressing; tearing, breaking and loose; boring and piercing; dull; throbbing; heavy; and fatigue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.149-150}.
