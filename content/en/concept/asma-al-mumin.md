## Overview
al-Mu'min — The Granter of Faith and Security. It is name number 6 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-59-23|59:23]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/muslim-0797|797]]، [[passage/muslim-2585|2585]]، [[passage/muslim-2664|2664]]، [[passage/muslim-2956|2956]]، [[passage/muslim-2999|2999]]، [[passage/muwatta-1-066|1.66]]، [[passage/muwatta-1-067|1.67]]، [[passage/muwatta-2-633|633]]، [[passage/muwatta-2-643|643]]، [[passage/muwatta-2-648|648]].
