## Nature
A plant at the edge of China, and the clove is the fruit of that plant; it resembles jasmine but is black, its male part is like an olive stone but longer and blacker, and its gum is in the strength of the terebinth gum {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}

## Choice
The best is the one like a dry, sweet stone, of a keen smell {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}

## Cosmetics
It sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}

## Ocular organs
It sharpens the sight and benefits the film over the eye, eaten and as kohl {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach and the liver and benefits vomiting and nausea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.642}
