## Summary
A house in the seventh heaven directly above the Ka'ba; seventy thousand angels enter daily, pray, and never return — Ibrahim leaned his back against it on the Night of the Ascent.

## In the texts
[[passage/quran-52-4|and the Frequented House]] — Surat at-Tur.

## Note
Set above the Sacred House; the hadith frames it in the account of the ascent.
