## Summary
A house in the seventh heaven directly above the Ka'ba; seventy thousand angels enter it daily to pray and never return. The Prophet saw it on the Night of the Ascent, with Ibrahim leaning his back against it {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.
