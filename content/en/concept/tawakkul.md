## Overview
*Tawakkul* (trust in God) is, in the forty-ninth hadith of Ibn Rajab's additions, the worship of the heart by which provision is obtained: "If you truly trusted in God as He should be trusted, He would provide for you as He provides for the birds: they go out hungry in the morning and return full in the evening." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.914}

## The meaning
True trust is the heart's reliance on God for obtaining what is sought, while taking the means, as the bird goes out in the morning, and not the abandonment of striving. The birds themselves go out and return, combining work with commitment. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.914}

It is connected with [[concept/iman|faith]] in the museum's classification.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 3)

The Sheikh holds that hope in God is closer than fear of Him, and says that God has counted everything in a book that neither errs nor forgets, whereas we forget {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:59:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)

The Sheikh mentions people who entrusted their affairs to God and then asks the listener what he himself does; he returns to four places that, as he said before, look dark and narrow yet hold all the good, and says a person has two kinds of sight, the sight of the eye and the sight of insight (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:29:00}.

He says that whoever has God with him has everyone with him, and whoever has God against him has everyone against him, and he notes that God is with those who are God-fearing and with the doers of good {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:31:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 6)

The Sheikh speaks of an employee's worry that his manager will be displeased with him or let him go if he falls short, then says we need a faith like children's faith, and gives the example of an infant who wakes and calls for his father and is reassured {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:28:30}.

He asks why a child of one or two years is at ease, and answers that God created his heart without muddy thinking, so he is like a fisherman whose provision comes while he waits patiently, and he asks the one who returns anxious whether he did not come back with a catch {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:29:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 10)

The Sheikh calls on a person to follow his Lord and not anyone else out of tribal loyalty, to leave the matter of the universe to its Creator and to entrust the affair to his Lord {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:55:30}.

He calls for relying on God rather than on a strong tribe, a prominent family or a minister, and mentions young men each boasting that his father is the chief of police, the head of an office, or some other figure {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:56:00}.
