## Nature
Nature: it is the dabb lizard; its nature is close to that of the monitor lizard, and it resembles the monitor in what it exceeds in. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.487}

## Uses by organ
Organs of the eye: its dung for whiteness and itching, and it sharpens sight. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.487}
