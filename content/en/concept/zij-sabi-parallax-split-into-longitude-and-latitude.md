## Summary
Al-Battani divides the parallax in the altitude circle into two angles, the angle of longitude and the angle of latitude. He takes the chord of each, multiplies it by the parallax and divides by sixty, which gives the parallax in longitude and the parallax in latitude. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.119}.

## Which parallax is applied
He says the parallax in longitude is added to or taken from the Moon's place depending on whether its distance from the ascendant is less or more than ninety degrees; in latitude the Moon's position relative to the zenith point decides the direction. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.121}.
