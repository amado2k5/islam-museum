## Summary
Ibn Sina divides black urine into kinds by the route taken to blackness and summarises what it shows: severe burning, severe cold, the death of the innate heat, or a crisis {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.185-186}.

## Three kinds
Of the shades of black, one goes to black by the saffron road, as in jaundice, and shows the thickening and burning of yellow bile, indeed black bile arising from yellow, and jaundice. Another comes from the dusk shade and shows sanguine black bile. A third comes from green and aubergine-dark and shows pure black bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.185-186}.

## What it shows in general
Black urine in general shows either severe burning, or severe cold, or the death and defeat of the innate heat, or a crisis and the expulsion by nature of black-bilious residues {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.186}.
