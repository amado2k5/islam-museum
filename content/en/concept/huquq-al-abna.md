## Summary
In his lesson "the rights of children over parents", Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy covers what a child is owed by his father and mother, before and after birth: choosing a righteous mother, a good name, aqiqa and tahnik, upbringing, and memorising the Qur'an. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:02:00}

## Before birth
The Sheikh says that the first right of a child over his father is that the father choose him a righteous mother, and that this is a right the child will claim on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:02:00}
He says that a righteous wife is not the father's right alone but the child's right too. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:02:30}

## A father, his son and Umar
He recounts that a man complained to Umar ibn al-Khattab of his son's disobedience; the son replied that his father had not chosen him a good mother. Umar told the father that he had wronged his son, and that the son had not wronged him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:03:30}

## A good name
The Sheikh says the Prophet changed the names of many Companions whose names had bad meanings, and that a father should choose a good name for his son. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:04:30}
He recounts the story of a man called Hazn who refused to change the name his father had given him, and says that the grief did not leave his house afterwards (needs_source: the name and details are not clear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:05:30}
He says a person will be called on the Day of Resurrection by his name and his father's name, so the choice should be made well. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:06:00}

## Aqiqa and tahnik
The Sheikh says the aqiqa is made on the seventh, fourteenth or twenty-first day: two sheep or rams for a boy and one for a girl (needs_source: the days and numbers are spoken once in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:07:00}
He says a boy is held in pledge by his aqiqa, and that the hair is shaved for a boy but not for a girl. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:08:00}
He describes tahnik on the seventh day: a date is chewed and rubbed on the newborn's palate with a clean finger. [[practice/tahnik|Tahnik]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:08:30}

## Upbringing and mercy
The Sheikh cites a saying on the stages of a child's upbringing: seven years of play, then seven years of discipline (needs_source: the third stage is not clear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:09:00}
He recounts that a man told the Prophet he had ten children (needs_source: the number is spoken once in the transcript) and had never kissed one of them, and that the answer was that whoever shows no mercy will not be shown mercy. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:10:00}
He urges tenderness towards daughters in particular. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:10:30}
He counts among the best things a parent can leave to his children that they memorise the Qur'an by heart, and says parents are honoured when their son or daughter does so. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:12:00} [[practice/hifz-al-quran|Memorisation of the Qur'an]]
He holds that from the age of fourteen (needs_source: the number is spoken once in the transcript) the father should guide his son and the mother her daughter, and should not leave them to companions without guidance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:12:30}
He cites a saying that whoever begins a good practice has its reward and the reward of those who follow it, and whoever begins a bad one bears its burden. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:13:00}

## Responsibility and belonging
He cites the saying that every shepherd is responsible for his flock: a man is a shepherd over his household, and a woman over her home. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:28:00}
He holds that among young people belonging should begin with religion, followed by belonging to the land, the family and the homeland. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:29:00}

## From the lectures: teaching children and parents' responsibility
The speaker counts it among children's rights that they be taught the history of their fathers and forefathers {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:16:30} (needs_source: partly garbled).
He sets out parents' duties towards their own parents, their sons and their daughters, and gives his reasons {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:33:30}.
He cites a survey of female students smoking at a Cairo university, attributed to "one of the universities" with no study named (needs_source: the figure of 74% has no traced source and should not be relied on before re-listening) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:38:30}.
