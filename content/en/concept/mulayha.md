## Nature and selection
Like the boxthorn; its leaf is like the olive's but broader, and it is eaten like greens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Actions and properties
Saltiness, astringency and a raw moisture that bloats {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Chest and breathing
Brings milk with the water of qaratun; the quantity named is not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
With the water of qaratin it calms colic; the quantity named is not copied here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.
