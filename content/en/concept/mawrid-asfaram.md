## Nature and selection
Flowers and thin separated twigs, grey and yellow; some say its force is like badhawurd, and there is a kind leaning more to white and a kind leaning more to yellow. Ibn Masa said: it is the wild myrtle. Others said it is a Roman afar. Ibn Masarjawayh said it is like badhawurd. Al-Khuzi said it is in the force of the poor wormwood and more astringent. The editor's footnote says it is the wild myrtle {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Helpful for epilepsy and humidities in the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Strengthens the stomach and liver and helps a fall upon the viscera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.

## Excretory organs
Inserted as a suppository for worms of the anus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.569}.
