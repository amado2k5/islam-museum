## Nature and selection
Its seed is larger than a chickpea and whitish, with a soft oily pith {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. The editor's note says the ban is a tall tree, and that its fruit is the fruit of a tree called yasr {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the third degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. The pith is purgative in particular; it cuts thick humours and, with vinegar and water, opens obstructions of the viscera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. The husk is more astringent, its oil has some astringency, and the whole has a cleansing and cutting quality {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: the seed helps freckles, patches and white marks on the skin, and scars of ulcers; the oil does too {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Swellings and pustules: it helps hard swellings when it goes into ointments, and it helps warts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Wounds and ulcers: with vinegar it helps peeling scabies, and it helps favus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Joints: it warms the nerves and softens spasm and hard nerves, especially its oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Head: with its astringency and its oil it stops nosebleed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.381}. Ear: it suits earache and noise in the ear, especially with duck fat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.382}. Teeth: a decoction of its root helps toothache when used as a gargle {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.382}. Food organs: it helps hardness of the liver and spleen when drunk in vinegar, and is used in a poultice on the spleen; the book says it is harmful to the stomach and causes nausea, and its juice drunk with honey brings on strong vomiting and purging {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.382}. Excretory organs: its seed with honey draws off raw phlegm, and its oil is used on a wick {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.382}.

## Substitutes
The book names substitutes: fawwa (madder root), cinnamon bark and bisbasa, in proportions that are not given here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.382}.
