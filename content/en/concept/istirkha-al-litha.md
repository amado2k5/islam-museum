## The regimen by its measure
If slight, rinsing with what the hot astringents, or the cold according to the temperament, were cooked in suffices; if much, the right in it is scarifying and letting the blood run, spitting what runs, then rinsing with the astringents' decoction in the way stated before; of the agreeable decoctions are what he described, and an adhesive smear is made of what he described; palate-rubbing with what he stated may help, rinsing after it, and the strong dentifrices are used; and venesection of the jaharik benefits it (the editor glossed al-tahnik as rubbing the palate; the venesection, scarification and dentifrices carried as the book names them, not procedures; the names of this kind left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.282}
