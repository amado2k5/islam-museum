## Nature
It is the Indian mash, like the seed of linseed, a little larger, inclining to dust colour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}

## Temperament
Cold in the second degree, moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It removes hiccup {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}

## Excretory organs
It breaks the stone of the kidneys and bladder, and is good for loosening the belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}
