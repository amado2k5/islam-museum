## Nature and selection
A kind of sea foam, solid and clinging to reeds, the reed being the qasab. It is a sharp drug, not drunk because of its sharpness, but applied as a salve after its sharpness is broken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. A footnote gives it as lake foam or al-afura {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.

## Temperament and actions
Very hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.

## Uses by organ
It changes a bad cold temperament into a good one {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Ornament: it helps melasma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Swellings and pustules: it helps milky pustules {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Wounds and ulcers: it helps scabies that has broken into ulcers, and the ulcers called qawabi {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Joints: a poultice of it helps sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.
