## Nature
There comes to the body, from the recurrence of fullness and the like in the muscles and vessels, a state like fatigue: the vessels stretch, yawning and stretching are frequent from the abundance of wind and vapour, the face and eye redden with it, and it calls for writhing and extension; when this is frequent in a person it points to fullness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.117}

## Starting point of treatment
The sanguine and bilious humour must then be evacuated; the book then names other means, among them a procedure whose danger the author himself warns of, and they are not reproduced here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.117}
