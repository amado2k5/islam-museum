## What it is
The cause of the teeth's crumbling and breaking is mostly their temper turning to moisture; it may also happen that they dry out severely — the two told apart by wasting and its opposite, a sign of colour-change or corrosion pointing to a moist temperament with matter. The first is treated by repelling the matter and strengthening the tooth with the named strong astringents and what he described as potent in it (names left out); if heated on top, only the strong thing he described avails; if from dryness, its treatment is the dryness-treatment already given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.275}
