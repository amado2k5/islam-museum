## Nature and signs
Most of the time this comes from putrid blood that swells the brain; it may split the shu'un and loosen the network, the head almost cracks and splits with it, the pain is intense, the eyes redden and bulge greatly and the cheeks redden greatly, vomiting and nausea may come with it by sharing of the stomach, and he is much inclined to lie on his back, contrary to his habit and to order {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.82} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83}

## Outcome
It kills for the most part in the third, and if he passes it there is hope; know that the illness is not extremely hard, otherwise an organ of this consistency and this nobility could not bear it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83}

## Treatment
Its treatment is the treatment of sarsam, and stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83} The sites of venesection and the methods of application are left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83}
