## Summary
Ibn Sina explains that pustules may come out on infants' bodies: the black ulcerous kind is deadly, the white safer and the red likewise, their coming out may bring benefit, and they are treated with gentle dryers in the washing water and with ointments when they ulcerate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.216-217}.

## Kinds
Pustules may come on their bodies; the black, ulcerous kind is deadly, the white safer, and the red likewise. Even thrush alone would be deadly, so how much more when it is joined by pustules, yet their coming out may bring many benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.

## Treatment
In every case they are treated with gentle dryers put in the water they are washed with, boiled in it, such as rose, myrtle, mastic leaf and tamarisk, and with the oils of these things too. Sound pustules are left until they ripen and then treated; if they ulcerate, an ointment with white lead is used, and at times the washing water with a little natron {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.216-217}.

## When they thicken
Likewise thrush: when it has thickened something stronger is needed, and they are then washed with borax water itself mixed with milk so that they can bear it. If their skin is speckled they are bathed in a decoction of idhkhir and mastic leaf, and the best of all this is to put right the nurse's food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.217}. A plant name in the bath's decoction is garbled in the scanned text and is left out.
