## Summary
The Abode of Recompense is mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the Hereafter where people are repaid for their deeds — paradise and fire — where God rewards every person for their deeds, good or evil. It is among the greatest things a Muslim believes in, containing bliss and torment known only to God.
