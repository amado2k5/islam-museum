## Summary
Ithar is preferring a brother's need over one's own even amid hardship. The Qur'an describes the Ansar by it: "they prefer them over themselves though poverty be theirs" — al-Hashr 59:9 [[passage/quran-59-9]]. The hadith states "none of you believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 13}, making ithar the summit of brotherhood's manners.
