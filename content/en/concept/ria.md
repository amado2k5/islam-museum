## Actions and properties
Its nourishment is little and inclines to the phlegmatic, and there is room for examination in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Wounds and ulcers
The lung of the camel heals the chafing from the sock when put on it warm, and the lung of pigs does likewise and prevents swelling from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Organs of the chest
The lung of the fox, when dried and drunk, benefits asthma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its digestion is easy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}

## Excretory organs
There is a binding in it for the belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.668}
