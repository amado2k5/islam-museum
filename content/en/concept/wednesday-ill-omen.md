## Overview
Al-Masudi relates that the barren wind came upon 'Ad on a Wednesday, so that no one of them survived before the following Wednesday. For this reason, in his account, people disliked Wednesday. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.457}

What is not recorded here: the position of Muslim scholars on ill omen from days. It needs a further source.
