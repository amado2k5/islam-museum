## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan orders the history of time management into four generations: the to-do list, where organising began around the nineteen-twenties and which remains its basis today, then scheduling, then prioritising, then the ordering of all of life — see [[concept/idarat-al-hayat]] {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:27:00}.

## The to-do list
A task board in columns — general calls, work calls, family and home, study or work, culture and activities, financial matters, and the year's goals carried over from ordering one's life — with one essential condition: only non-repeated items are written; recurring routines such as work hours and meals are left out {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:29:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:30:00}.

## The schedule
The proposed unit of work is the week, not the day or the month, and the day is divided into periods, not hours — he suggests the periods bounded by the prayers — unless one has an effective secretary managing appointments, in which case hours may be used {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:35:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:36:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:36:30}.
Filling begins with appointments one does not control — those bound to other parties like courses and doctors' times — then, from the to-do list, only tomorrow's tasks are picked; distributing tasks over more than a day is proven to fail since a person on average accomplishes half of what he plans in a day, and if one day slips the whole plan is not wrecked {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:37:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:41:30}.
He faults the old mechanical method — 'do such-and-such at such-and-such an hour' — which strips life of joy without proving effective {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:42:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:43:30}.

## Ranking priorities
The third generation is simpler than people imagine: three to five tasks in the to-do list — no more — are circled as the most important; when filling tomorrow's schedule one starts with them if possible; finished items are crossed out and new ones circled; and he warns to circle tasks from the 'year's goals' column lest they be deferred until the year ends undone {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:44:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:45:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:46:00}.
On the whole method he notes that prayer before, during and after planning is among the causes of blessing in time, success and inspiration {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:46:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:46:30}.
