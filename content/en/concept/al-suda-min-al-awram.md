## Swellings of the membranes of the brain
The swelling from which headache arises may be in the membranes of the brain. It may be hot and is called hot sarsam; it may be cold and is called lithargus, that is forgetfulness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} It may be compound, and the state of its owner is called the sleepless coma; and it may be hard {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48}

## Swellings of the substance of the brain
The swelling may be in the brain itself and its substance, and then it is either hot, a phlegmon or an erysipelas, or cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} The detail of all this comes shortly, and these swellings often dissolve through pus, sanies or a watery matter coming out of the head, by the ear and elsewhere {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48}
