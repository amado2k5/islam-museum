## Summary
A joint (mafsil) is, in the lexical sense, where two bones of the body meet; in the technical sense it is the place where one organ joins another by ligaments that link two bones. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14827}

## Ablution and washing
It is recommended to wash the hands up to the wrist three times before dipping them into the vessel. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14827}

## Retaliation
A wound short of death is subject to retaliation only if the cut can be made without excess, which holds only where the cut is made at a joint. Without a joint there is no retaliation, agreed by all. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14828}

## Blood-money
Each phalanx of a finger or toe carries a thirteenth of the full diya; the thumb has two phalanges, each carrying half a tenth of the diya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14828}

## Theft
Most jurists hold that a thief's hand is cut at the wrist, which is the joint of the palm, and his foot at the ankle joint. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14828}
