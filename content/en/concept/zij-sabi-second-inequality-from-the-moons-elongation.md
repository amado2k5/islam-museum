## Summary
The second inequality, which follows the Moon's elongation from the Sun, reaches about two and two-thirds parts in the greatest case the observers found. Added to the single inequality, the total comes to about seven parts and forty minutes. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.

## Observation and the sum
He says he observed many lunar eclipses, fixing their times and middles, and found the single inequality as described. He then shows that together the two inequalities come to about seven parts and forty minutes, the sum the tables use. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.
