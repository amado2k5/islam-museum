## Summary
Ibn Sina describes the compound colour like the washings of fresh meat, as if blood were mixed into water, and traces it mostly to a weak liver and less often to an excess of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.

## Description and cause
Among the colours of urine are compound colours, one being that like the washings of fresh meat, resembling blood mixed in water. It may come from a weak liver or from much blood, mostly from a weak liver under whatever bad temperament has gained the upper hand, and weak digestion and slackened strength point to it; if the strength is strong it can only be from much blood, beyond the amount the discriminating power can separate in full {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.
