## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh holds that purification of the soul, cleansing it of the impurities of sin and bad character, has obstacles that must be removed, as a child is protected from germs and polluted water so that he grows; among them he lists insincerity in word and deed, lying, little knowledge of the religious sciences, consuming the unlawful and doubtful, attachment to the world, extremism and severity, an inflated ego, many imagined enmities, and diseases of the heart such as envy, hatred and rancour. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:10:00}
His examples: a young man who hears two tapes of a scholar and then pronounces on [[person/ibn-taymiyya|Ibn Taymiyya]] and Ibn al-Qayyim though he cannot recite al-Fatiha correctly; one who works where the Companions would not work in his place; and the likeness of a darkened soul to a clouded mirror that does not reflect the sun. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:05:30} On extremism he holds that the Prophet, given a choice between two things, always chose the easier, that he was the easiest jurist and exegete in history, that the Companions after him were the easiest of people, that jurisprudence grew complicated afterwards, and he describes the religion as one of the middle way; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep24 00:07:00}
In the next episode he holds that self-blame that does not turn into action is useless, asks when one's eyes last filled with tears at the Qur'an, quotes [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]'s fear that he might be the one person left out of Paradise, and calls for avoiding [[concept/bida|innovations]] and treating love of the world. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep25 00:08:30} See [[concept/futur-al-nafs|slackening of the soul]].
