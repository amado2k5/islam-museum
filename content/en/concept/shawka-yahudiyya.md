## Temperament
Hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}

## Actions and properties
Subtle and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}

## Joints
It benefits tetanus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}

## Organs of the head
A gargle is made of its decoction for molar pain, it benefits all catarrhs, and so are the actions of its roots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}

## Respiratory organs
It benefits spitting of blood from the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its root benefits continuous vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its root suits chronic flows of moisture from the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.684}
