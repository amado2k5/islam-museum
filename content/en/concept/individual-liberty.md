## Overview
Muhammad al-Bahi presents individual liberty as a product of the European Renaissance in thought, politics and money — a reaction against the Middle Ages {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 144}. He then criticizes what he sees as its excesses: colonialism and tutelage justified in the name of independence; the interlocking of money, politics and thought in the democratic order; and capital concentrated in a few hands that stamp capitalism's character {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 145} {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 148} {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 150}. This is the author's own reading and critique of the Western model, attributed to him rather than stated as the museum's judgment.
