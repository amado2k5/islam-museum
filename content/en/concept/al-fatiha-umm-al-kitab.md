## Summary
The Fatiha is called the Umm al-Kitab, and prayer is not valid without it. Sha'rawi cites the hadith to that effect and a hadith qudsi that divides prayer between God and His servant, noting that it says "I divided the prayer", not "the Fatiha". {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.51}

## Repeated divine names
Sha'rawi holds that the repetition of the divine name and of "the Merciful, the Compassionate" in the basmala and the Fatiha does not repeat the meaning, since the speaker is God who places each word in its right place. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.51} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.52}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's reading and is attributed to him. The grading of the two hadiths is not checked here, and the scan gives no jurists' views on reciting the Fatiha behind an imam. The edition is unverified.

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] holds that the first revelation was the five opening verses of al-'Alaq, not al-Fatiha. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:02:00} He reports his teacher Muhammad Abdullah Draz of al-Azhar as calling al-Fatiha a request to God for the divine constitution, and says its plural "we", said even by someone praying alone seventeen times a day in the obligatory prayers, plants a sense of community and umma. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:26:30} He says the sura gathers the three parts of creed: divinity, the hereafter and prophethood. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep01 00:19:30} He holds that "the seven oft-repeated" are al-Fatiha, not the seven long suras, since al-Hijr is Meccan and the long suras Medinan ([[passage/quran-15-87|al-Hijr 87]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep07 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep07 00:11:00}
