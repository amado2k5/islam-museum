## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy distinguishes tahassus, checking a matter with one's senses, from tajassus, entering into people's affairs, which he says is forbidden. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:49:00} He narrates how Umar heard a strange voice, climbed a wall and found men drinking wine, and their reply to him, though the narration is partly garbled in the recording (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:47:30}

## Tahassus and tajassus
The Sheikh says that tahassus is checking a matter with one's senses, and he gives the example of checking doors. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:49:00}
He says that tajassus is entering in among people into their affairs, and that it is forbidden. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:49:00}

## Umar and the men drinking wine
The Sheikh narrates that Umar heard a strange voice, climbed a wall and found three men drinking wine. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:47:00} The men told him that he had disobeyed God in three ways, among them spying. The Sheikh also quotes a verse he does not name, about entering houses by their doors (needs_source: the narration is partly garbled in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep03 00:47:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series Nafahat Ramadaniyya (the faults of the tongue)
In an episode on covering a Muslim's faults, the Sheikh says that God forbade spying, and applies it to spouses: a wife who suspects her husband's conduct should not track his news and search his phone to confirm what is in her mind, nor the husband hers; he holds that spying turns back on the spy with what harms him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:04:30}
