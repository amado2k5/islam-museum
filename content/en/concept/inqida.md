## Definition
Inqida is the reflexive of qada, meaning passing away and ceasing; in another use, completion and exit from a thing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3541}

## Related terms
- Imda': the ending or carrying out of a thing; a woman "imda'ed" her waiting period, meaning she completed it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3541}
- Intiha': the same sense of ending; a term is "intaha" when it has run out. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3541}

## How contracts end
- Achievement of the aim: a hire for a fixed term or use, an agency to buy, a pledge when the debt is paid, a surety when he pays or is released. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3542}
- Invalidity: a contract that is invalid must be undone as a right of God, and a judge may compel the parties to undo it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3543}
- Termination by a party: rescission; undoing a non-binding contract such as agency, partnership, partnership of profit or loan; irrevocable divorce and khul' in marriage. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3543}
- Impossibility: the loss of a fungible object before delivery, the loss of the thing whose use was hired, or the death of a principal, agent or partner. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3543}

## Other applications
- Waiting period: ends with childbirth, the lapse of months or the cycles; return, inheritance and maintenance then cease. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3543}
- Custody: ends at puberty, and the schools differ in detail: the Hanafis at a girl's puberty and a boy's self-sufficiency; the Malikis at a boy's puberty and a girl's consummation by her husband. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3544}
- Vow of abstinence (ila'): ends by return, expiation of the oath, or the passing of four months; the Hanafis hold the wife separated by its lapse. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3544}
- Wiping over socks: ends with the obligatory washing, by tearing the sock, removing it, or the lapse of the period. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3544}
- The traveller's shortening of prayer: ends when the stay that allows it ends, on intention to complete, or on entering home. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3544}
- A term: ends by waiver or by lapse. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3545}

## Disputes over ending
Where the parties dispute whether something has ended, the word generally lies with the one who denies it, on oath: a woman claiming her waiting period has ended, or a buyer and seller disputing the term of payment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3545}
