## Overview
al-Wadud — The Most Loving. It is name number 47 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-85-14|85:14]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In people's lives
Among the righteous: Rabi'a al-Adawiyya is remembered for her prayers of love for God alone, becoming the tradition's figure of the servant who loves her Lord most sincerely. {cite:khulasat-al-siyar}
