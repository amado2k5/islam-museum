## The signs of consumption
And if the expectoration ceases at the consumption's end they may not outlast four days; their cough often strengthens and leads to a successive spitting of blood — if their cough is treated with the preventers of expectoration they perish amid a relief that befalls them, and if they are left coughing they die of bleeding, a swift death (a witnessing attributed to the author) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.357}
