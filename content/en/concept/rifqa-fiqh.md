## Summary
Rifqa in the lexicon is companionship; a rifqa is the company that travels with a man. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8822} It is recommended to travel with a company and disliked to travel alone. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8822} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8823} The company should be of righteous people and should appoint the best of them as leader; sharing one's food and money is advised only as far as it suits all. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8823} The duty of pilgrimage requires a company where the road is dangerous. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8823} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8824} For women the majority require a husband or a mahram to travel with them, while the Malikis allow a trusted company for a duty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8824} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8825} The company must bury and prepare any dead companion, and a traveller must ask his companions for water for ablution. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8825}
