## Summary
Sha'rawi argues that the Quran, the hadith qudsi and the prophetic hadith each have a distinct style, and that a person has one stylistic personality that he cannot leave for long. If the Quran came from the Prophet, his three forms of speech would resemble each other, so the difference is evidence that the Quran is from God. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.22}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's argument and is attributed to him. The views of the scholars of hadith and of the sciences of the Quran on the stylistic difference are not yet presented.
