## Nature
A well-known tree, of which fufi is a kind; qadm quraysh is the fruit of its tree, and wild pitch is made from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Actions and properties
As for its seed, which is qadm quraysh, its power is astringent and mildly heating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Swellings and pustules
The leaf of this tree is a poultice for hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its leaf and seed, when mixed with goose fat, murdasanj and fine frankincense, benefit outer ulcers; when mixed with wax and myrtle oil they benefit ulcers of delicate bodies and all hot and moist ulcers; its bark suits a wound as a dusting, and its leaf used on fresh wounds prevents their corruption {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Organs of the head
A gargle is made of it and of its decoction, especially with vinegar, for toothache, and its wood is sometimes split and cooked in vinegar for that {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Ocular organs
Its smoke enters eye salves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Organs of the chest
Its seed helps in bringing up phlegm from the chest, and the resin of tannub is of great benefit for very chronic cough, and it is a kind of pitch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits the afflicted liver with honey water; the book states a weight, left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Excretory organs
If drunk it binds and holds back urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}
