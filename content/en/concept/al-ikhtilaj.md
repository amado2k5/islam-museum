## Its definition and proofs
Ikhtilaj is a muscular motion, and the adjoining skin may move with it; it is from a thick blowing wind. The proof that it is from wind is the quickness of its dissolution, that it occurs only in cold bodies, cold teeth and on drinking cold things, and that warmers and penetration calm it; the proof that it is thick is that it does not dissolve except by moving the organ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}

## Its muscular seat
The proof that it is muscular, fleshy and nervous is that what is very soft, like the brain, does not develop it, nor does what is hard, like bone; it befalls mostly what is midway between hardness and softness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}
