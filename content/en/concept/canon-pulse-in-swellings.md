## Summary
In the seventeenth chapter Ibn Sina divides swellings into those that bring on fever and change the pulse of the whole body and those that do not and change the pulse of the organ, then sets out how a swelling changes the pulse by its kind, its time and its size {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.181-182}.

## Swellings with and without fever
Some bring on fever, by their size or the nobility of their organ, and change the pulse in the whole body, the change peculiar to fever that he will explain in its place. Others bring no fever and change the pulse peculiar to the organ in which they sit, and may change the rest of the body only incidentally, through the pain and not through being a swelling. A swelling changes the pulse by its kind, its time, its size, its organ or the symptom that follows it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.181}.

## By kind
A hot swelling by its kind turns the pulse to the saw-like, trembling, shivering, quick and frequent, unless a moistening cause opposes it, when the saw-like fades and the wave-like takes its place; the trembling, speed and frequency always remain. A soft swelling makes the pulse wave-like, and if it is very cold, slow and widely spaced; a hard one adds to the saw-like quality. An abscess once it has gathered turns the pulse from the saw-like to the wave-like through the moistening and softening that follow, and adds unevenness by its weight, while speed and frequency often ease as the accidental heat settles with ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.181-182}.

## By time and size
While a hot swelling is growing, the saw-like quality and the rest grow with it, and the pulse always grows harder from the added stretching and more trembling from the pain. Near the peak every symptom increases except what follows power, which weakens, so frequency and speed rise; if it drags on speed fails and the pulse turns ant-like. When the swelling declines, by dissolving or bursting, the pulse strengthens as the weight is lifted from power, and the trembling eases as the stretching pain lessens. As to size, a great swelling makes these states greater and more, a small one less and smaller {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.182}.
