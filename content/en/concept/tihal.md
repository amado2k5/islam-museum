## Choice
Among spleens, the best is the spleen of swine, though its chyme is poor. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Properties
It has some astringency and generates black blood. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Organs of digestion
It is slow to digest because of its astringency. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}
