## Overview
Al-Maraghi counts the crafts among the chief pillars of civilization and among the communal obligations (fard kifaya) whose absence indicts the whole community — citing al-Ghazali on farming, weaving and cupping {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 36}. The Qur'an itself points to David's craft of mail-coats and the shipbuilding commanded of Noah {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 37}. The craftsman is a hireling in what he is commissioned to make: owed sincerity, mastery of the work, and keeping his word {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 37}; the book cites "God loves that one of you, when he does a thing, perfect it," and Ahmad b. Hanbal's ruling that hiding a defect in merchandise is unlawful.
