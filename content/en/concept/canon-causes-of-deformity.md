## Summary
In chapter 5 Ibn Sina lists the causes of deformity of shape: faults in first creation where the shaping power fell short; those at birth from the womb and at the swaddling of the child; evident causes such as a fall or blow; and morbid causes such as leprosy, spasm, excessive fatness or leanness, tumours and poor healing of ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.144-145}.

## In creation and birth
Among the causes of deformity are those that occurred in first creation, so that the shaping or altering power in the semen fell short of completing its work; those that occur at separation from the womb; those at the swaddling and holding of the infant; and evident causes from outside, such as a fall or blow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.144}.

## Morbid causes
There are also causes tied to hurrying to movement before the limbs have hardened, and morbid causes such as leprosy and spasm. It may arise from excessive fatness, from excessive leanness, from tumours, from diseases of posture, or from poor healing of ulcers and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.144-145}.
