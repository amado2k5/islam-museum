## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that whoever walks the path of guidance that God showed him loses no good he could grieve over, since all good lies in God's method, and no evil he cannot ward off comes to him to fear {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.278}. See [[passage/quran-2-38|al-Baqara 38]] and [[passage/quran-47-17|Muhammad 17]].

## Guidance as signpost and increase
He notes that guidance has two senses: pointing to the way of good, and helping faith and increasing it {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.278}.

## Fear and grief
For him fear is expecting an evil that is coming and that one cannot ward off, and grief is losing what one loves and desires {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.278}. He holds that the upright do not live in fear, because the wrongdoer fears that his deed will be exposed {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.279}.

## Acceptance and wisdom
He holds that one who obeys God's will has his will submitted to that of his Creator, and that the universe hears from him only glorification and obedience, so he is at peace with himself, his Lord and society {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.279}. He cites the Prophet's weeping at the death of his son Ibrahim, who said that the eye weeps and the heart grieves but only what pleases the Lord is said {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.279} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.280}. The hadith is in the two Sahih collections, as the scan's footnote says.

## The doctor
He gives the example of a bone-setter who breaks a bone to mend it, so pain does not prevent the act from being good {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.280}.
