## Summary
Ramal is the brisk walk: hurrying in one's steps while swaying the shoulders. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8995} Its ruling is that it is a sunna of the tawaf, recommended in the first three circuits of every tawaf that is followed by sa'y, as the majority of jurists hold, and it is for men only, not women. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8995}
