## Overview

Jahl in fiqh is lack of knowledge of a thing; the encyclopedia distinguishes it from forgetfulness. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6657}

## Ignorance of a prohibition

Ignorance of a prohibition removes sin and the ruling in outward terms for someone who has recently become Muslim or is similar. Someone who knows a thing is forbidden is not excused for not knowing its consequences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6660} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6661}

## Its effect on worship and divorce

The encyclopedia discusses the prayer when the time or the opening chapter is unknown, fasting when Ramadan is uncertain, and a divorce pronounced in words the speaker does not understand, which does not take effect. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6662} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6663} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6664} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6665}

## In Ahmed Abdussalam's programme 'Osalli Alayh'
[[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]] gives the twenty-first episode of his programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']] to ignorance, citing the Prophet's parable of himself as a man who kindled a fire into which moths and grasshoppers fell, taking it for light, while he drove them away; he says the ignorant are those who fall in, and that many sins are a sign of ignorance of God, of His Fire and of His Paradise. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:03:00} He relates what he calls Jabir's hadith in Sahih Muslim: a man whose head was gashed on a journey woke in a state of major impurity and asked his companions whether he had a dispensation to use dry ablution; they said not while he could use water, so he washed and died, and when the Prophet heard he prayed against those who had given the ruling, and said the cure of not knowing is to ask. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:05:30} (needs_source: the speaker attributes it to Sahih Muslim; its place was not checked in this run.) He then tells the story of the man who killed ninety-nine, asked an ignorant worshipper who made him despair, killed him to make a hundred, then asked a scholar who pointed him to repentance; he says it was ignorance that killed the worshipper. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:07:00} He ends with the merit of seeking religious knowledge. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep21 00:08:00}
