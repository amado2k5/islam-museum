## Overview

Jahl in fiqh is lack of knowledge of a thing; the encyclopedia distinguishes it from forgetfulness. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6657}

## Ignorance of a prohibition

Ignorance of a prohibition removes sin and the ruling in outward terms for someone who has recently become Muslim or is similar. Someone who knows a thing is forbidden is not excused for not knowing its consequences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6660} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6661}

## Its effect on worship and divorce

The encyclopedia discusses the prayer when the time or the opening chapter is unknown, fasting when Ramadan is uncertain, and a divorce pronounced in words the speaker does not understand, which does not take effect. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6662} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6663} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6664} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6665}
