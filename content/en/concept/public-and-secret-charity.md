## Summary
On the verse that compares open and secret giving, Sha'rawi holds that the choice depends on the giver's condition and intention, and that God knows the intention in both cases {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1167}.

## The wealthy and their honour
He says that if a wealthy person does not give openly, people may credit his wealth to God's bounty without crediting him with giving, so he should give openly to protect his honour {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1167}.

## One whose wealth is not visible
One whose wealth is not visible should give in secret. Someone who gives openly to set an example, without showing off, is also praised {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1167}.

## Reports on merit
He cites Ibn Abbas that secret voluntary charity is seventy times better than its public form, and that obligatory charity given openly is twenty-five times better than given in secret. The chain was not checked {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1172}.
