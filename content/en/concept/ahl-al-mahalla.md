## Definition
Ahl al-mahalla are the people who settle in a place and make it their home. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3643}

## Related terms
- The 'aqila: the clan and kin of a person, however distant, who pay the blood-money. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3643}
- The tribe (qabila): descendants of one father; the people of a quarter need not be of one father. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3643}
- Ahl al-khitta: the place marked out by the imam for a people to settle. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3643}

## Their rules
- The imam of the people of a quarter has a better claim to lead prayer in their mosque, if he is fit to lead, by the majority; Ibn Umar declined to lead a prayer in another's mosque on this ground. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3644}
- Their call to prayer in their mosque suffices for those who hear it, by the majority; in the newer view of al-Shafi'i a worshipper is still recommended to call. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3644}
- The oath of the quarter: where a body is found with no known killer and there is a presumption against a particular person, the claimant swears fifty oaths; if he does not, the people of the quarter swear, and the Hanafis hold that they share the oath and then pay the blood-money. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3644}
