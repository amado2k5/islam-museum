## Actions and properties
It has a hot power, and its juice is drunk for pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Cosmetics
Its fresh form is applied with the sharab on bahaq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Ulcers
It closes chronic ulcers and is dusted on proud flesh {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Joints
It is applied with vinegar on gout, and a cerate is made of it for pain of the back {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Organs of the chest
A linctus for cough is made of it with sweet things {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is given with adhrumali for the stinging of the stomach; the book states a weight, which is left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Excretory organs
It is given with adhrumali for dysentery and difficulty of urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that when women use it as a pessary it brings on the menses gently, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.683}
