## Name and definition
Safar is the second month of the lunar year; it was named, it is said, because Mecca emptied of its people as they journeyed, or, by another account, for the yellowing of the season. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}

## The Jahiliyya superstition
The Jahiliyya Arabs saw Safar as unlucky: they would not marry in it nor undertake affairs in it. The Prophet abolished this in the saying: there is no contagion, no Safar and no owl. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## What happened in it
The seerah records expeditions and events in Safar, and the law assigns it no special worship and forbids no work in it. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
