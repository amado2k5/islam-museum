## Nature and selection
It is the drug called fas (axe), because it has two edges like an axe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. The editor's note says it is a plant called andrusaron, as the common people name it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in nature, with bitterness and astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. It opens the obstructions of the viscera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: it helps pains of the joints {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.
