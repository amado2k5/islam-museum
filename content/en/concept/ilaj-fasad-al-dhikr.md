## From dryness
That which accompanies heat and dryness is easier to cure, and is treated by what has been said many times {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99} For what comes from bare dryness, the sick person must be fed moistening foods, and exercise of the region of the head and movement of the hands and legs is used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.99}

## Cold moist matter
For what comes from a matter of cold and moisture, evacuate it after ripening by what you know, and let him dwell in a bright house, proceeding gradually in the evacuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.100} Do not hurry in drying him, but go gradually, and beware lest your drying reach the finishing of the original moistures, which cold and dryness of temperament would then follow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.100}

## What is avoided
They must avoid intoxication (sukr), the blowing of winds and fullness; a rule on washing is left out here, its reason being the slackening that the hot brings and, for the cold, what numbs and harms the sensing spirit; if fullness comes upon them, the regimen after it is lightened, and they must avoid foods that silence, weigh down, numb and give vapours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.100}

## Swellings of the brain
What comes from swellings of the brain is treated by what was said of qaranitus, lithargus and the sleepless coma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.100}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.99-100}
