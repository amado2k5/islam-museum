## Vapours
If the cause is vapours that gather and rise from the body in fevers, especially with pain of the lung and the swelling in it called dhat al-ri'a, and vapours from the stomach, you know each by its signs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} If it is from the stomach, it is preceded by sudar, dawar, dawiy, ringing (tanin) and imaginings (khayalat), and it eases with hunger and grows with fullness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} If it is from the region of the lung and chest, it is preceded by heavy pain or pain in the regions of the chest, tightness of breath, cough and the signs of pleurisy and of dhat al-ri'a {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} Likewise, if it is from the liver, signs of illness in the liver come before it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} That which comes from a blow to the crown or temple is known by its evidence {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}

## Womb (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina says that if it is from the womb, the illnesses of the womb and its fullness come before it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}
