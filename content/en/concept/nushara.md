## Temperament
Its temperament follows its tree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Actions and properties
The sawdust of worn wood cleanses, and has drying if its tree has it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Wounds and ulcers
The sawdust of worn wood heals, especially that from astringent trees such as some kinds of thorn; it is gathered with anise and wine, burned and then ground, and dusted on creeping ulcers it helps them (the proportion is not carried over here) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.
