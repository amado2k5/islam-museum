## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the causes of quick intoxication as a weak brain, a plenty of humours in it, the strength of the wine, scant food and poor regimen, and says that what comes of a weak head is treated as old catarrh is {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.239}.

## The causes
Quick intoxication comes from a weak brain, from many humours in it, from the strength of the wine, and from scant food and poor management of it and of what goes with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.239}.

## The weak head
What arises from weakness of the head is treated as old catarrh, with the plasters mentioned in that chapter, and such a person drinks only a little {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.239}.
