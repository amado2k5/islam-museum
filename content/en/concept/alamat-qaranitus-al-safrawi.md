## The signs
As for that from pure bile, he is much awake, his eyes dry very strongly, his tongue grows very rough, yellows first and then blackens, the fever is intense and he keeps wiping his eyes, and they fancy yellow things {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.79} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80} Fierceness (sab'iyya), hostility (suran) and eagerness for quarrel enter their temper, as if they were in the posture of one who wants to fight; their noses grow thin, especially at the tips, and their foreheads are strongly drawn upward {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80} The editor's footnote glosses sab'iyya as fierceness and suran as hostility, and says it is most likely a colloquial word {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80}
