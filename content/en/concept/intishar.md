## Definition
Intishar in the lexical sense is spreading; a report that spreads is "intashara", and the day that extends is "intashara". {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3436}

## Two senses in fiqh
- The stiffening of the male member. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3436}
- The spread of a thing, as the new moon is proven by a widely spread report. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3437}

## Effects of the first sense
- A triply divorced woman may be lawful again to her first husband only after a marriage and intercourse in which the member has stiffened, following the hadith of the wife of Rifa'a. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3436}
- Whether compulsion to fornication carries the hadd penalty is disputed: the Hanbalis and some Malikis require it, the preferred Shafi'i view does not, since the stiffening may be natural and not willed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3437}
- Rulings on wudu, ghusl, fasting, looking at a stranger and marriage bars by nursing depend on it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3437}

## Difference
Istifada is used only of reports; intishar applies to reports and to other things. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3436}
