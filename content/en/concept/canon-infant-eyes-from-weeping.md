## Summary
Ibn Sina says water in a child's head was treated in the chapters on diseases of the head, and mentions swelling of the eyes, whiteness in the pupil from much weeping and sores of the eyelid, treated with hudud and milk, chamomile decoction and nightshade juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.

## Water in the head
A child may have water in its head, and its treatment was given in the diseases of the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.

## Eyes and eyelid
Their eyes may swell; hudud in milk is smeared on them, then they are washed with chamomile decoction and basil water. Much weeping may cause a whiteness in the pupil, and they are treated with the juice of nightshade. A sore may come on the child's eyelid from weeping, and its treatment is likewise the juice of nightshade {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.216}.
