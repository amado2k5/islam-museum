## Summary
Ibn Sina says much weeping may bring a protrusion at the navel or one of the causes of rupture, and treats it by giving nankhwah in nabidh, then with the hot astringents such as myrrh and cypress bark kneaded with egg white and bound with a linen cloth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.217}.

## What happens
Much weeping may produce in them a protrusion at the navel, or one of the causes of rupture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.217}.

## Treatment
For it a treatment is directed: nankhwah is given in nabidh and a binding is put over it. Stronger than that are the hot astringents such as myrrh and cypress bark, kneaded with egg white, smeared over it and covered with a thin linen cloth; or burnt lupin, acacia and aloe are moistened and used, along with what is said in the chapter on rupture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.217}.

## Swelling at the cutting of the cord
A swelling may befall children, especially at the cutting of the cord; then shankal, which is fanjiyus, and terebinth gum are taken and dissolved in sesame oil, and the child is given some to drink and its navel is smeared with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.217-218}.
