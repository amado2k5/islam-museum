## The regimen and the tried
The drugs needed in the fissures' treatment join softening to astringency and drying; of the beneficial regimen is anointing the navel and the anus and painting them with what he described, and of the greases butter, marrow and fats; and of the tried drugs what he described, made like a salve, his food being made trotters and the named dish (the editor glossed the cerate as a salve, a word foreign to Arabic and perhaps Greek in origin; the names of this kind carried as the book names them and left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.283}
