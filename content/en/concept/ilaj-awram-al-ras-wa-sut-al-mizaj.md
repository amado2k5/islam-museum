## Swellings
As for treating hot swellings, one must begin first with the repelling coolers, the remedies mixed with them being changed if there is severe pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.38} After that treatment continues until the swelling reaches its end, and then it is dissolved {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.40} The evacuations that are required come first, according to the matter, and in feeding one with a bilious swelling light, moist foods are used in particular {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.40}

Cold swellings begin first, as with the others, with evacuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.40} Of the repellents at its start he limits to a few, mixing in the mild thinning ones, and then uses ripeners with a slackening and a little dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.40-41} Then at the end, for all, cold and hot, slackening remedies are used; for the cold the complete slackeners and the strong dissolvers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.41}

He says everyone who complains of a material illness in his head is harmed by wine and by lingering in the bath, and everyone with a disease in the membranes of the brain is harmed by very cold water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.41}

Recipes, quantities, the names of the veins and the sites of venesection and cupping, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.38-41}

## Ill-temperament and the management of feeding
As for the treatments of a hot ill-temperament alone, they are what has cooling in it, of the cold herbs and oils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.41} If the ill-temperament is cold, use the poultices and the waters made from the hot remedies, and the oils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.44} If with that it is black-bilious, whether natural black bile or phlegmatic, warm it with moistening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.44}

If it is burnt, avoid everything that dries or heats and limit yourself to the moistening ones, milks, oils, fomentations, poultices and foods {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.44} If dryness is with the cold, join moistening and warming; if moisture is with the cold, use the evacuants and the remedies that have drying with heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.44}

He says those with these complaints should be kept from inner movements of the soul and from the eye's repeated scanning of features, and should avoid looking at what glitters and gleams, and their hearing should likewise be eased {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.43}

When you feed those with material diseases of the head, rub the limbs, and then feed according to the amount and quality of the matter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.44}

Recipes, quantities, the names of the veins and the sites of venesection and cupping, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.41-44}
