## Summary
Al-Battani dates his eclipses by years he calls the years of Dhu al-Qarnayn, which he says are counted from the death of Alexander. This is the era in which the four eclipses in this chapter are dated. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.85}.

## Reckoning the dates
He gives each date by year, month and day, and each eclipse's middle time relative to noon at Raqqa or at Antioch. The dates are not converted to the Gregorian calendar here; any conversion would be derived and needs checking. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.85}.
