## General signs
Know that anyone who has severe pain and does not complain of it or feel it has confusion in him, and golden urine may in fevers point to confusion of the intellect {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97}

## Black bile
That from black bile comes with griefs and the supposing of something and the signs of melancholia that he gives in its chapter; if the black bile is bilious, beastliness and boldness are with it; if the black bile is sanguine, there is mirth and laughter with the veins in flow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97}

## Yellow bile and the red
That from yellow bile comes with inflammation, heat, weariness, ill temper, severe agitation, the imagining of fire and sparks, burning of the corners of the eyes, yellowness of colour, inflammation of the head, stretching of the skin of the forehead, sinking of the eyes and leaping to confront {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97} That from the red has these signs stronger and harder; of this sort is the confusion of the intellect in fevers, and most of it is in the epidemic ones {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97}

## Heat and dryness, and phlegm
That from simple heat and dryness has no heaviness with it and none of the signs of the matters mentioned {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97} That from phlegm that has putrefied and sharpened befalls its sufferers with sedateness along with the confusion, so that they lift their eyebrows with their hands at all times, their heads grow heavy and they fall into coma from the substance of the cold, as their minds are confused by the accident of heat; they do not let go of what they hold, and it may befall them to imagine themselves beasts and birds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97} In sum, when confusion of the intellect befalls from dry heat, sleeplessness points to it, and when from moist heat of blood or putrefied phlegm, coma points to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97}

## Vapour from an organ
That whose cause is vapour rising from an organ is known from the state of that organ, from its pain if it is one organ, or from the whole body if it is general, as in the all-embracing fevers; and it is known whether it is simple, or with matter, or vapour; the signs of all that are given in the chapter on headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.97}
