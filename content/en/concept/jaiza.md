## Overview
In al-Baladhuri, the *jaiza* (reward) is a gift that a commander gives to whoever ventures into a hard task. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## When the name first arose
He reports that the first day the name *jaiza* was used was the day al-Hajjaj appointed Qutn ibn Qabisa ibn Mukhariq al-Hilali governor of Fars and Kirman. He reached a river that his companions could not cross, and he said: "Whoever crosses shall have a thousand dirhams." They crossed, and he kept his word to them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

Al-Ju'af ibn Hakim recited a verse whose meaning is that it was they who established rewards among Mudar. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
