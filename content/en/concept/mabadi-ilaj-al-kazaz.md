## Its treatment is convulsion's, and hastening first
Its treatment is the very treatment of convulsion; and tetanus deserves hastening to its treatment before convulsion, for tetanus is hurtful, suffocating, killing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.153}

## Restoring heat and what is observed
Here the cupping-glasses are used for what restores heat, with scarification, especially on the neck muscles, the vertebrae and the ribs' tips; among what must be watched in the tetanus patient is that when his body sweats from the pain's severity or from the treatment he is not left to cool on it, for that harms him — he must be dried with a moistened wool, and may be sat in warmed oil, for it strongly dissolves (the named potions, clysters, ointments and their measures are not reproduced) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.153}

## Food and the befalling fever
When the tetanus patients are fed they must be given only very small weak mouthfuls and thin sips, for swallowing is hard for them and adds to their distress; the fever that befalls by nature is the best treatment for what is moist of it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}
