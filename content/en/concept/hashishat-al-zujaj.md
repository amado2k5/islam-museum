## Nature and actions
Nature: a plant used to polish glass. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.487}
Actions: binding with moisture, sticking, cleansing and softening. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}

## Uses by organ
Swellings: soothes swellings; its leaf is drunk for carbuncle, for burns from fire and for phlegmy swellings; its juice with white lead (asfidaj al-rasas) on nettle-rash and redness; it is used as a gargle for swelling of the tonsils. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}
Joints: a cerate on gout. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}
Organs of the head: its juice with rose oil for ear pain, dropped in, and its juice for swelling of the tonsils. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}
Organs of breathing: its juice is sipped for chronic cough. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}
Excretory organs: removes piles. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.488}
