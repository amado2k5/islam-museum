## Summary
Ihtiyat in the lexicon is taking the most prudent and secure course, and guarding against error. Jurists use it in these senses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1749} Scrupulousness (wara') is avoiding doubtful matters for fear of falling into what is forbidden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1749} One of the rules built on caution is that the forbidden prevails when the lawful and the forbidden are mixed; its scope is treated in the books of legal maxims. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1750} Whether caution applies to obligatory, recommended and forbidden acts is treated in the usul appendix. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1750} Note: the heading in the scan is spelled اختياط.
