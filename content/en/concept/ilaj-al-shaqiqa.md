## The principle of cure
Its cure is evacuation of blood as was taught for the bayda and the rest, then purging and drawing, each by its own measure as set down in the Canon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.74} When it comes in fits the body must be cleansed and the temperament changed after the cleansing; if the matter is hot, cooling means are used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.74} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.75}

## The long-standing kind
As for the long-standing kind, it is cold in every case, and needs dissolving and what warms strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.75}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, methods of application, and the cure he attributes to some earlier physicians are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.74} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.75}
