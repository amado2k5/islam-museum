## Overview
Islamic eschatology is the part of the creed concerned with death, the signs of the Hour, resurrection and reckoning; the Tahawiyya and its Sharh devote a chapter to belief in the signs of the Hour {cite:aqida-tahawiyya} {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.

## In the sources
The Sharh notes that scholars devoted well-known compilations to the hadiths of the signs, enumerating among them the mission of Muhammad, the seal of the prophets, and the events foretold after him {cite:aqida-tahawiyya}.
