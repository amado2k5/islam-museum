## The fatal kinds of pleurisy
And mostly, if he passes the seventh, he is saved; and often a redness shows between his shoulder-blades and his shoulders heat and he cannot sit; if his belly heats and a yellow stool comes out of him he dies, unless he has passed the seventh (a line on the book's wording, of Hippocrates' saying) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.346}
