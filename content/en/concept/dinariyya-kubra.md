## Summary
The Greater Dinar is another named problem in inheritance: the heirs are a wife, a mother or grandmother, two daughters, twelve brothers and one sister, and the estate is six hundred dinars. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8363}
The case is a just one from twenty-four: the wife takes an eighth, seventy-five dinars; each brother two dinars and the sister one, the rest divided by the rule that a male takes twice a female's share. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8364}
Other names are al-Rakabiyya, al-Shakiyya, al-Dawudiyya and al-Amiriyya, each tied to an anecdote about a judge or jurist who divided an estate. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8363} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8364}
