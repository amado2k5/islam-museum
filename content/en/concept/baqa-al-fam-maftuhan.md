## Its causes
The mouth stays open either from the great need for strong breathing, or for an inflamed inflammation, or for straitness and quinsy, or for weakness of the mouth's muscles so they do not do their work in sleep — and that in the acute diseases is a bad sign; as for the tongue's colours, they are told where the acute diseases are told {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.265}
