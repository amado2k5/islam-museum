## Actions and properties
Drying and cleansing, of bad humour. The editor's footnote says that sahnat is ground fish {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.639}

## Wounds and ulcers
It brings on scab and itch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Joints
It benefits phlegmatic pain of the hip {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Cosmetics
It removes the foul breath that comes from the stomach and its corruption {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It cleanses the moisture of the stomach and dries it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.640}
