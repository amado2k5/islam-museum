## Overview
al-Hayy — The Ever-Living. It is name number 62 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-10-31|10:31]]، [[passage/quran-2-255|2:255]]، [[passage/quran-25-58|25:58]]، [[passage/quran-3-2|3:2]]، [[passage/quran-3-27|3:27]]، [[passage/quran-30-19|30:19]]، [[passage/quran-40-65|40:65]]، [[passage/quran-6-95|6:95]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/muslim-0779|779]]، [[passage/muwatta-1-583|1.583]]، [[passage/muwatta-2-598|598]].
