## Overview
Alcazar is a Spanish word, from the Arabic al-qasr, meaning castle or citadel. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 380}

## Famous examples
The Alcazars of Seville, Cordova, Segovia and Toledo are the best known. The name is also frequent in place names: Alcazar de San Juan is a town in the Spanish province of Ciudad Real, and Alcazarquivir is the Spanish name of Ksar al-Kabir, a town in Morocco. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 380}
