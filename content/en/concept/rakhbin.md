## Temperament
Ibn Masawayh said that it is hot and dry in the second degree, poor in humour and good for the hot stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Excretory organs
It softens the belly if a suppository of it is taken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its nourishment is very slow to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.669}
