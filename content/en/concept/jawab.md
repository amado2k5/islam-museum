## Overview

A jawab is a reply to a question. If it cannot stand alone it follows the question's scope; if it can, it has its own effect. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6671} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6672}

## Effect of a reply

A reply may count as an acknowledgement, a divorce or a refusal, depending on wording and intention; some schools return to the speaker's intention to count the divorces. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6673} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6674}

## Refusing a required reply

A person who refuses a required answer, such as a mufti or a witness, is sinful. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6675}
