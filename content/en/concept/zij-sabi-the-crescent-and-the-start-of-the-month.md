## Summary
Al-Battani says that knowing when the crescent can be seen at the start and end of months completes what came before, since the Arab calendar and the starts of its months follow the sighting of the new moon. Yet it is hard from many sides: the Moon's nearness to and distance from the Sun, its latitude, the parallax, and the short or long rising times of the signs. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.129}.

## The inherited opinion
He says that some of the men of his time, falling short in the finer points, supposed that a body's distance from the equator and its latitude come from one arc, and that the parallax does not come from the altitude; he holds that this view is not required by reasoning. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.129}.
