## Summary
Al-Battani holds that the Sun's parallax is not perceptible, as it is with the other bodies named, and that Ptolemy set it at the ratio of one to 1210, which is the Sun's distance from the Earth's centre. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.116}.

## The visible and true places agree
He says the Sun's visible place agrees with its true place, because the Sun's parallax was already included in the Sun's reckoning at the time of observation, since the limit of the zodiac and its distance from the equator of the day were known from observation. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.116}.
