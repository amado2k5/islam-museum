## Signs
When the coma is from simple cold from outside, its sign is that it follows a severe cold that strikes the head from outside, or a cold inside the body and the brain; no flush is found in the face or the eyelids, the colour leans to green, and the pulse is stretched to hardness with great irregularity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.91}

## Outlook
All kinds of coma that come from cold in the substance of the brain, or from a drink taken, are followed by corruption in memory and thought {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92} Some have said that the coma of simple cold is lighter than the coma of moist matter; that is not a sound saying, for it may be very strong {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.92}
