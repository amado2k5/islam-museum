## Summary
Al-Battani takes the planets' equations from Ptolemy's book and corrects the apsides of Jupiter by what he found through the Moon at the observed times; the other planets' equations and their apsides he keeps as Ptolemy gives them. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.

## Jupiter
He says Jupiter's far point falls short of the figure in the book by a number of parts; the exact number in the text is garbled in the scan and is not recorded here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.99}.
