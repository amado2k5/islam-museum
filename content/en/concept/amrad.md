## Definition
Amrad is a youth whose beard has not grown and who has not reached the age at which it usually appears. The lexical root is smooth cheeks. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3391}

## Rulings
- Looking and being alone: a beardless youth who is not attractive is treated like any other man. A handsome one may be looked at without desire, a view most jurists allow; looking with desire is forbidden, and the Hanafis and Shafi'is count such looking as like looking at a woman. Being alone with him is nearer to harm. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3391}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3392}
- Shaking hands: most jurists forbid touching or shaking hands with a handsome beardless youth with desire; the Hanafis dislike it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3392}
- Breaking ablution by touching him with desire: the Malikis and one view of Ahmad hold it breaks ablution; the Shafi'is and the other view of Ahmad do not. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3392}
- Leading prayer: most jurists dislike praying behind a handsome beardless youth, since he is a source of temptation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3392}
- Dealing with him: one must take care and keep to what is needed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3392}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3393}
- The rule: whatever causes temptation is forbidden unless a greater interest outweighs it; the means to corruption must be closed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3393}
