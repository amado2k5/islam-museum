## Overview
al-Ali — The Most High. It is name number 36 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-2-255|2:255]]، [[passage/quran-22-62|22:62]]، [[passage/quran-31-30|31:30]]، [[passage/quran-34-23|34:23]]، [[passage/quran-40-12|40:12]]، [[passage/quran-42-4|42:4]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
