## Summary
Each of the five planets has four spheres arranged like the Moon's: one level with the zodiac's sphere, an inclined sphere over it, an eccentric sphere, and an epicycle whose centre travels along the eccentric. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.95}.

## What governs the motion
He says the irregular motion of the planets that the observer sees is accounted for by these spheres, each described by its centre, its direction and its tilt; the inclined sphere fixes the planet's latitude at each place. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.95}.
