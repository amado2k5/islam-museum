## The evident cause and the swellings
If it is from a twist, fall, blow or cut, the cause points to it; the cause of a cut may hide when the nerve is deep, and it is told by its coming on suddenly without benefitting from regimen; if from a hot swelling, stretching, pain and fever point to it; if from a hard swelling, touch and a knot felt in the nerve point to it with a preceding pain, for it mostly follows a blow, a twist or a hot swelling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.139}

## The soft swelling and the other causes
If from a soft swelling the inference is hard, yet in all states it does not lack slight pain, numbness, a mild fever, and waxing and waning of the pain with motions and foods, and its onset is not sudden; in all of this the patient feels, on willing motion, as though something were preventing him in that very spot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.139}

## Diffused moisture and gross nerve
The palsy arising from a diffused moisture is felt by its owner as a cause spread through the whole palsied organ; that arising from grossness of the nerve is told by the difficult return of the organ from a contraction the patient forces, or another makes for him, into extension and relaxation, and the organs are not soft as in absolute palsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.139}

## The sign of the matter, and the saying on the boy's urine
If the matter is with blood, the jugulars and the oft-repeated signs point to it; if from moisture, by flabbiness; if after colic or acute fevers, colic or moisture points to it — it does not come suddenly, there are no other signs, and it is judged by touch and the causes affecting the organ. It is said: if you see a boy's urine green, warn of palsy or convulsion from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.139}
