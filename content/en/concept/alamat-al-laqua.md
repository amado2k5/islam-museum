## The general signs
The signs are that the puff and the spit issue from one side, and wind and saliva are not retained from a side; headache often accompanies it, especially in the convulsive kind; to know the afflicted of the two sides: it is the one which, when stretched and set right by the hand, lets the other easily return by nature to its shape {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.155}

## Signs of the relaxed kind
The signs of the relaxed laqua are that motion weakens, the senses grow turbid, softness is felt in the skin and in the muscles, no stretching is felt, the lower eyelid hangs down, and half the membrane on the gum neighbouring that eye is seen relaxed too, moist and flaccid — shown by pressing the tongue downward and inspecting, the cause being this membrane's continuity with the membrane coming by way of the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.155}

## Signs of the convulsive kind
The signs of the convulsive are that the senses are mostly not turbid, the forehead's skin is stretched so the creases vanish, the face muscles are hard, this side's stretching runs to the neck, saliva and spit are mostly lessened, and the skin's drawing toward the neck's side is more decisive and harder to turn back; the signs of the moist and dry of the convulsive are as you know {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.155}

## Signs of onset
Among the signs of laqua's onset are that the man finds pain in the bones of his face, numbness in its skin, and much twitching of it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.155}
