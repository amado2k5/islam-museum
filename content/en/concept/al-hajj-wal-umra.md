## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that Hajj and 'Umra are two rites completed for God, and that the manners of the pilgrimage forbid obscenity, wickedness and disputing. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.835} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.844}

## Prevention and the offering
Sha'rawi says that one who is prevented is restrained by what offering is available, does not shave until the offering reaches its place, and that a sick person or one with an injury to the head pays a redemption by fasting, alms or a sacrifice; he orders these from the easiest to the most beneficial. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.839} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.840} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.841}

## Whoever cannot find the offering
Sha'rawi says that one who cannot find the price of the offering fasts three days during the pilgrimage and seven after returning. He warns that one who spends his money on gifts and then claims he cannot afford an offering deceives himself, and that it is better to begin the fast beforehand. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.841} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.842}

## Obscenity, wickedness and disputing
Sha'rawi holds that obscenity is forbidden in the pilgrimage, wickedness is forbidden at all times and more so in the sacred house, and disputing is lower in rank; in their place are the good that God knows and speech in gentleness. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.844} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.846}

## Provision and God's bounty
Sha'rawi says provision keeps the pilgrim from asking, that trade during the pilgrimage is permitted if it is God's bounty and does not exploit the pilgrims' need, and that humility belongs to God alone. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.846} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.847} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.848}

## 'Arafat and Muzdalifa
Sha'rawi holds that standing at 'Arafat is the greater Hajj, that the particle "then" indicates deliberation so the night at Muzdalifa is required, and that departing from where the people depart abolishes the Quraysh practice of standing apart. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.838} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.855}

## Remembrance after the rites
Sha'rawi says remembrance after the rites exceeds boasting of one's fathers; that honour should come from what a person does himself; and that supplication after the rites should aim at what lasts. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.857} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.858} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.859} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.860}

## Limits of this batch
The names of those who hold each view on the kinds of rite, the jurists' conditions of the offering, the academic view and other views are not entered. The stories of Adam, Eve and Abraham that Sha'rawi gives for the name 'Arafat are not checked against a source. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.839} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.854}
