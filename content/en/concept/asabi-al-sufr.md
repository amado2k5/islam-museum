## Nature and selection
Its shape is like a fist; it is mottled yellow and white, hard, with a little sweetness, and there is a yellow kind with a greyish tinge and no white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}. A footnote identifies it as turmeric {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry in roughly the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}.

## Uses by organ
It resolves very thick humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}. Joints: it has a particular virtue for nerve organs and their ailments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}. Head: it helps madness in particular {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}.
