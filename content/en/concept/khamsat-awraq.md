## Nature
It is qantaflun {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.716}

## Actions and properties
Strongly drying without sharpness, pungency or sting, and it is put as a poultice on bleeding and cuts it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Swellings and pustules
It is put as a poultice on abscesses, scrofula, phlegmatic hardnesses and felon; the decoction of its root is for spreading ulcers, and what is cooked of it with vinegar for namla; and it benefits jamra, felon and mange {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Joints
It benefits pains of the joints and sciatica, and benefits hernia drunk and as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Organs of the head
The decoction of its root is for an aching tooth when rinsed with it and for thrush, and its leaf with the drink for headache; the duration and amount are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Respiratory organs
It is gargled as a decoction for roughness of the throat, and the juice of its root is for pain of the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Organs of nourishment
The juice of its root is for liver pain and jaundice; the duration and amount are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Excretory organs
Its root benefits diarrhoea, ulcers of the intestines and haemorrhoids, and likewise the decoction of its root fevers, and its leaf with adhrumali or with the drink the quartan and the recurring fever {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}

## Poisons
The juice of its root is a deadly drug {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.717}
