## Definition
Inha is lexically informing and reporting, as in "I informed the ruler of the matter", and also completing and finishing. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3567}

## In judgement
The Malikis and Shafi'is use it to mean informing another judge of one's ruling so that he may carry it out, or of what came before one short of a ruling, such as a claim heard so that the other may complete it. It may be done in person, by letter, or before two witnesses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3567}
- The detail is treated under the entries on claim and judgement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3567}
