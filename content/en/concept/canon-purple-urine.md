## Summary
Ibn Sina says purple urine is bad and deadly because it shows the burning of both biles, and that red running into black shows compound fevers and fevers from thick humours, and pleurisy if it is clearer and the black lies nearer its top {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.188-189}.

## Purple and red running to black
Among the colours is the purple, which is bad and deadly because it shows the burning of both biles. There may also be a red with black running through it, which shows compound fevers and fevers from thick humours; if it is clearer and the black is nearer its top, it shows pleurisy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.188-189}.
