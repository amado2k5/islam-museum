## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree; the editor's note says it is the shah banak {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}

## Actions and properties
Very dissolving and subtilising; when placed under the pillow of boys it benefits the slobber of their mouths {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}

## Joints
It benefits palsy as a smear, as a snuff and as a drink taken with a sharab (drink) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}

## Organs of the head
When snuffed up as its water it cleans the brain, and it also benefits facial palsy and epilepsy when drunk with a sharab (drink) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits the moistures of the stomach, and, as they claimed, the slobber of the mouths of boys when placed under their heads {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it benefits the winds of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.676}
