## Summary
Al-Battani says it is not truly known whether the equator line is inhabited, since he knew of no one who had reached it in his day and Ptolemy does not mention it in his book; but people of understanding know its temperament is balanced, because the Sun never goes far from it nor do shadows lengthen there, so swift is the Sun's change of declination at that time, and summer and winter on it are both mild, something seen in lands near it such as places in Yemen. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.25-26}.

## What is not known and what is inferred
The text separates what was seen from what is inferred: he does not claim anyone reached or settled the line, does not credit Ptolemy with saying so, rests on what people of understanding say, and explains the mildness by the Sun's motion there. As evidence he points to lands near the equator in the Yemeni country that approach it, naming Sanaa, Aden and others; these are given as a general observation and not as measured figures.
