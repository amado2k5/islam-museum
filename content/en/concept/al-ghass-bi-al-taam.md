## What it is
If a body of some bulk sticks fast, one begins by cuffing the neck and what is between the shoulders strike after strike; if that does not avail he is helped by vomiting, and there may be danger in that (carried as the book's description, not a procedure) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.285}
