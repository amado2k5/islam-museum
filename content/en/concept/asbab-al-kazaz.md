## Likeness and unlikeness
Tetanus's causes are like convulsion's in one respect and unlike in another: like in that tetanus may come from repletion, dryness, an injury reaching the nervous organs, and swellings; unlike in that convulsion is rarely from wind while tetanus is often from an extending wind — indeed the tetanus composed of two convulsions may often be of a wind that seizes the body and is a hard illness with it, and if the single convulsion occurring in an organ over the whole body already held danger and a sign of death, how much more the doubled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.150} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.151}

## Where the matter falls in tetanus
The cause in material tetanus falls differently in the tissue: either the congealing moisture ran through the fibre then froze and stayed hard, so its return to contraction is hard; or it fell at once and filled the fibre without its proportion differing from the fibre's proportion — falling along the fibre's length, so it occurred without taking from the length, but keeps the length by its leaning to the gap; whereas in convulsion the effective matter is variously placed in the nerve's interstices, not penetrating them evenly or much, and the penetration of tetanus's matter in this manner seems like the penetration of relaxation's matter — except that matter is thin and relaxing, and this is frozen and hard, not letting the organ bend and contract {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.151}

## Swelling, the origin and the hurt
Or the matter in tetanus may not fall in the belly of the muscle, tendon or nerve but at its origin, digging the nerve or tendon lengthwise so it cannot contract; or there is a swelling; or the matter fell within the fibre so that when it contracts the fibre must be pressed for it, hurt and pained; or the paining hurtful cause is a matter or non-matter falling in the origins of the muscles or tendons, which flee from it lengthwise — as occurs in a kind of tetanus after violent vomiting and much evacuation for the hurt, for the tendons and nerves take hurt from the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.151}

## Dryness, wounding and after sleep
If the cause in tetanus is dryness, it is because the muscles, narrowed in width by the dissolution of moistures, grow in length and the passages contract from them, so the motive power's penetration is hard and they are too weak to carry the organs into contraction — especially if the hardening arising from dryness aids on the nerves; its like in dry convulsion may take from length and breadth together evenly, which is why the dry convulsion is worse than the dry tetanus; and as relaxation may befall for cutting, so extension may befall for a wound occurring so the muscles are hurt from contraction; and it may occur as convulsion occurs — for a repletive numbness blocking the spirit's paths, so the extended organs stay uncontracting as the contracted organs stay unextending until the spirit finds a way and passage, this often after sleep, for the spirit in it is gone further inward {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.151} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.152}
