## Summary
Ibn Sina divides fatigue pain by its cause into exertional fatigue, tensive fatigue from a stretching humour, inflative fatigue from wind and ulcerative fatigue from an irritating humour, and notes compounds such as the boric kind, a mix of tensive and ulcerative {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## The kinds
The cause of fatigue pain is either exertion, and that pain is called exertional fatigue; or a stretching humour, and what it brings about is called tensive fatigue; or wind, called inflative fatigue; or an irritating humour, called ulcerative fatigue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.

## Compounds
Compounds are formed from these, which he says he will explain in the place proper to them. Among them is the fatigue known as the boric, compounded of tensive and ulcerative {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.
