## Nature
It is called the strangler, and in Arabic it is also called al-jathum and al-nidalan; al-kabus is an illness in which a person on entering sleep senses a heavy image fall on him and squeeze him and narrow his breath, so that his voice and movement are cut off and he is nearly choked from the blocking of the pores, and when it passes from him he wakes at once {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.117}

## Forerunner of three illnesses
It is a forerunner of one of three illnesses, epilepsy, apoplexy or mania, when it comes from crowded matters and not from other, non-material causes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.117}

## Causes
But its cause in most cases is the vapour of thick matters, bloody, phlegmatic or black-bilious, rising to the brain all at once while the movement of wakefulness that dissolves the vapour is still; each humour is imagined by its colour, and the sign of each humour is evident by the earlier rules; it may also come from an intense cold striking the head all at once at sleep, which squeezes, condenses and contracts it and brings on those same imaginings, and that does not happen except with a weakness too in the brain as to its heat, or a bad temperament in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.117} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.118}
