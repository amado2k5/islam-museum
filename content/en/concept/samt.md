## Summary
In the lexicon samt means a direction or aim, and musamata means facing or being opposite; one who turns toward the qibla is said to face it (sammata al-qibla). The word also means following the truth and guidance, and the bearing of the good; tasmit means invoking blessing on one who sneezes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9822} The technical sense does not depart from the lexical one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9822}

## Related terms
Istiqbal (facing) and muhadhat (being in line with) are synonyms of musamata the qibla. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9823}

## Ruling
The jurists agree that facing the qibla is a condition for the validity of prayer for one able to do it, on the basis of the verse in al-Baqara (144) directing the face toward the Sacred Mosque. The details are in the entry on facing the qibla. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9823}
