## The Great Bear

The star catalogue at the end of al-Battani's Zij lists the stars of the Great Bear after those of the Lesser Bear, each with its position in longitude and latitude. The entries name the stars by part of the figure: the muzzle, the knee, the back, the lower belly, the root of the tail and the root of the thigh. The figures in the table are not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.245}
