## The voice's huskiness and harshness
The convalescent, the weak, and those feigning weakness for little power are as it were unable to handle much air, so they narrow the larynx till their voice sharpens; and if the weak man strains to widen his larynx and weigh down his voice he is not heard at all (a line on the book's wording). And if it is a tumour that has grown old, the sweet drink is drunk, on the book's wording (the rest of the chapter's soft pills, broths, gargles, lohochs and draughts, named with their forms, are excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.325}
