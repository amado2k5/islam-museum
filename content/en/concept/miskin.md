## Overview
The second zakat category; the miskin is one who has some property or earning that falls short of sufficiency — or nothing at all in the view of those who rank the faqir above him — and the definitional dispute with the faqir is recorded in every school. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
