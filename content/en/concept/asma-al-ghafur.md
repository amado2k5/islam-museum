## Overview
al-Ghafur — The Forgiving. It is name number 34 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-10-107|10:107]]، [[passage/quran-12-98|12:98]]، [[passage/quran-15-49|15:49]]، [[passage/quran-18-58|18:58]]، [[passage/quran-28-16|28:16]]، [[passage/quran-34-2|34:2]]، [[passage/quran-39-53|39:53]]، [[passage/quran-42-5|42:5]]، [[passage/quran-46-8|46:8]]، [[passage/quran-67-2|67:2]]، [[passage/quran-85-14|85:14]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
