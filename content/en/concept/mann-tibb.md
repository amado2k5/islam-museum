## Nature and selection
Mann is a dew that falls on a stone or a tree, turns sweet, sets as honey and dries as gums dry, like taranjabin, shir khishk and the honey brought from the mountains of Qasran at Rayy, each of which the book has treated in its own chapter; it takes a force from the nature of what it falls upon and adds that to what its softness and sweetness bring {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.
