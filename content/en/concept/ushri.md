## Overview
*Ushri* (tithe land) describes land whose people pay the tithe (*'ushr*) rather than the land tax (*kharaj*). It is the counterpart of [[concept/kharaj|the kharaj]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.419}

## In Futuh al-buldan
Al-Baladhuri mentions that what the Arabs cultivated of the lands of the springs of al-Taff after Dhu Qar and the conquest became tithe land. Likewise what was granted from the lands of those springs whose people left them after al-Qadisiyya and al-Mada'in. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.419}

He then mentions that the springs of al-Taff were made into Sawad lands in the governorship of Ishaq ibn Ibrahim ibn Mus'ab under al-Mutawakkil. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.419}

What is not recorded here: the detail of the rulings on the tithe among the jurists. It needs a further source.
