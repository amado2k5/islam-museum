## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the harms of excessive vomiting: it harms and weakens the stomach and exposes it to flows of matter, harms the chest, sight and teeth, may harm the liver, lung and eye and split some vessels, and he warns against those who fill themselves quickly and then turn to vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.288}.

## On the stomach and other organs
Excessive vomiting harms the stomach and weakens it and exposes it to the turning of matter toward it; it harms the chest, sight and teeth and chronic headaches, and its excess harms the liver, lung and eye, and sometimes splits some vessels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.288}.

## Filling then vomiting
Some people like to fill themselves quickly, cannot bear it, and run to vomiting; this habit leads to bad chronic diseases, so he should refrain from filling himself and balance his food and drink {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.288}.
