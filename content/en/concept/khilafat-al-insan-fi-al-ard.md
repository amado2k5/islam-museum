## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the human khilafa on earth means the succession of generations, and that God gave humanity the means of provision, so the universe was subjected to humanity by God's will and not by human will {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.241} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.242}.

## Successor and successive
He distinguishes "khalaf", a generation that comes after another, from "khalifa", which he takes to carry a higher meaning {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.241}. He holds that the word takes several meanings {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.242}.

## Subjection of the universe
He says God made the angels prostrate to Adam and made the universe subject to him, so that he is as it were God's khalifa on His earth {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.241} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.242}. He cites a sacred saying about devoting oneself to worship, which he explains as God filling the heart with richness {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.242}.
