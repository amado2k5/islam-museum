## Nature and selection
A cooked food made of dough, called rishta in the author's country, like straps or strands, boiled in water with meat or without {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. The editor's note calls it a flour food like threads {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot, and its moisture excessive {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. Slow to digest and to leave the stomach, because it is unleavened bread-dough; some find the version cooked without meat lighter, and the book doubts this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}. With pepper and almond oil its state improves a little, and once digested it gives much nourishment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}.

## Uses by organ
Respiratory organs: it helps the lung, cough and spitting of blood, especially when cooked with hamqa' greens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.380}.
