## Nature
Shih is of two kinds, Rumi and Turkish; the one has a cypress-like leaf and a hollow stem, and the other a tamarisk-like leaf; a third kind is also found, called the yellow Armenian sabirinun; Dioscorides said that some people call it sariqun and some call it the sea absinthe, and it grows abundantly on the mountain of Taurus and in Egypt in a place called Busir; and sheep that feed on it grow fat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Choice
The best of it is the Armenian {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree and dry in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Actions and properties
All its kinds cut and dissolve winds; it has an astringency less than that of absinthe, more heating, more bitterness, and a saltiness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Cosmetics
Its ash with oil or oil of almond is a beneficial smear for alopecia, and its oil makes a slow beard grow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Swellings and pustules
It soothes swellings and boils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Ulcers
It checks the akila and the sawda' {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Organs of the head
It causes headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Ocular organs
Its water is applied warm to ophthalmia and dissolves it, and its ash fills the hollow of the eye that comes from ulcer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Respiratory organs
It benefits difficulty of breathing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Harmful to the stomach, especially the third kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Excretory organs
It expels worms and the tapeworm and kills them and brings on urine, and is stronger in that than the other absinthe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Fevers
Its oil benefits the cold of the shivering fit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Poisons and bites
It benefits the stings of scorpions and the tarantula and poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it brings on the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.673}
