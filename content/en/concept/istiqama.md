## Overview
*Istiqama* (uprightness) is steadfastness in faith and obedience after saying "I believe in God." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 471-472}

## The hadith
In the hadith of Sufyan ibn Abd Allah al-Thaqafi, he asked the Prophet for a comprehensive saying about Islam, about which he would not ask anyone after him. He said: "Say, I believe in God, then be upright." In another version: "Say, my Lord is God, then be upright." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 471-472}

## Ibn Rajab's commentary
Ibn Rajab holds that the meaning of the hadith is taken from two verses in the surahs of Fussilat and al-Ahqaf. He relates from Abu Bakr three meanings of "then they were upright": they did not associate anything with God, they did not turn to any god other than Him, and they were upright in holding that God is their Lord. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 472}

## Guarding the tongue
In the continuation of the hadith in al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i, Sufyan asked what he should fear, and the Prophet took hold of his tongue. Ibn Rajab makes guarding the tongue among the most important things that uprightness must observe. In a report of al-Nasa'i from Anas, with a chain he described as weak, there is an addition to the meaning. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 471-472}
