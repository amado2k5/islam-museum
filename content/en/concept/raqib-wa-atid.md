## In hadith
Al-Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud narrate that the servant has two scribes at his right and left, called al-Raqib and al-Atid, who preserve his deeds {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}; one report has them both on his neck.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details that al-Raqib and al-Atid are the angels charged with writing, and notes that 'al-Raqib' is also a name of God in another context, while these two angels were given these names in hadith {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Scholars differ on their exact identity and relation to the noble scribes, but agree the hadith establishes angels who preserve every deed {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
