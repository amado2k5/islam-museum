## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that God testified to His oneness before any creature did, so the self's testimony to itself is the strongest, and the creatures testify after it {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.234}.

## Attributes before their objects
He notes that God's attributes existed before what they relate to: God is Creator before anything is created, and Provider before anyone is provided for. He argues that God created the universe with His attributes complete before any creature testified {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.233}.

## Encompassing knowledge
He notes that after creating the earth and the heavens, God says He has knowledge of everything, so no atom is hidden from Him, and the whole universe acts only by His permission and will. He cites the verse about the weight of a grain {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.234}.
