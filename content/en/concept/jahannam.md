## Overview
Jahannam in Sunni creed is the already-created abode of punishment in the hereafter — 'Paradise and the Fire are created, never perishing' {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §106-107}.

## In the sources
The Sharh notes the grave sinners of the community do not remain in the Fire forever if they die monotheists {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz §89}; the creed affirms God created the Fire before creation and made people for it — whoever He wills to it, by His justice {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §107}.
