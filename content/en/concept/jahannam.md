## Overview
Jahannam in Sunni creed is the already-created abode of punishment in the hereafter — 'Paradise and the Fire are created, never perishing' {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §106-107}.

## In the sources
The Sharh notes the grave sinners of the community do not remain in the Fire forever if they die monotheists {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz §89}; the creed affirms God created the Fire before creation and made people for it — whoever He wills to it, by His justice {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §107}.

## Jahannam in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episode 3)
The Sheikh mentions the verse that describes Jahannam as keeping watch over its people, and says that the name Jahannam is used for the fire in general and sometimes for the first of its seven levels. [[passage/quran-78-21|the verse describing Jahannam as keeping watch over its people]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:41:30}
He lists the levels of the fire: Jahannam, Laza, Hutama, Sa'ir and Hawiya, with the lowest level at the bottom, and distinguishes the Arabic word for a lower level from the word for a higher one (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:44:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:45:00}
He says that the first level holds the disobedient monotheists who died with debts owed to people (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:45:00}
He relates reports of necks coming out of the fire, of seventy thousand halters, and of three breaths of the fire, some of which could not be checked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:43:30}
He mentions a valley of insects that torments those who bring grief upon the Muslims (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:49:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:50:00}
