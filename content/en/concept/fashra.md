## Nature
Some said it is hazarjashan, which is the white vine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Actions and properties
Sharp and pungent, it cleanses, dries and attenuates, and warms with a moderate warming {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Cosmetics
Its root with bitter vetch and fenugreek cleanses the surface of the body strongly, purifies and clears it and removes freckles and the black marks left after ulcers; cooked with oil until it is soft it likewise removes the dark discolouration under the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Swellings and pustules
Its root cuts warts and milky pustules; with wine it calms the whitlow, dissolves hard swellings and bursts the abscess; drunk with vinegar it dissolves swellings of the spleen, and as a poultice with figs it also serves the spleen and calms its pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its root as a poultice with salt is used on bad ulcers, and it enters the ointments that eat away flesh; its fruit, smeared on scab, ulcerated or not, makes it peel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Joints
Its root as a poultice with wine brings out bones, and it is drunk for palsy, and for bruising of the muscle it is both applied and drunk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Organs of the head
It is drunk for epilepsy and vertigo, and sometimes it causes confusion in the mind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Organs of the chest
With honey a linctus is made from it for those who choke, for disorder of the breath, for cough and for pain in the side, and its juice drunk with cooked wheat makes the milk abundant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Galen said that whoever eats its tips when they first come up benefits the stomach by its astringency and pungency with a little bitterness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Excretory organs
The heart of this plant when it first comes up, eaten as it is or cooked, promotes urine and loosens the bowels; its juice loosens phlegm, and it is among the good drugs for the spleen; cooked with oil it benefits the fistulas of the anus, and the water it is cooked in, poured on swellings or sat in, cleanses them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its root kills the foetus, that used as a pessary it brings out the foetus, that sitting in its decoction cleanses the womb, and that the water it is cooked in, and its juice with honey, bring out the afterbirth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Poisons and bites
Its root benefits the bite of the viper, and likewise the sting of all vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}

## Substitutes
Its substitutes are doronicum and mace {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.628}
