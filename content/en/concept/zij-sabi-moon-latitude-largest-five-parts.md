## Summary
In al-Battani the Moon's latitude reaches about five parts. It is tabulated in the seventh table of equations, and the latitude correction at each place is taken from it. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.83}.

## Latitude in the reckoning
He says he found the Moon's latitude reaches this amount, and then subtracts the share belonging to the time between the mean and the true motion; what remains is the latitude the tables rely on. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.83}.
