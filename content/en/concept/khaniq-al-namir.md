## Nature
The book quotes Dioscorides that it is a plant with thin long stalks hard to crush, a leaf like the ivy leaf but softer and sharper at the tip, heavy in smell and full of a sticky yellow moisture, and a fruit like the pods of the bean, a finger in length, with small hard black seeds inside {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Actions and properties
The book relates that the leaf of this plant kills wolves, dogs, foxes and the nimr (leopard or tiger) and weakens their strength at the hour they eat it, and it is used neither inside nor outside; the way of giving it to the animals is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Poisons
A deadly poison; it is said that if brought near a scorpion it subdues it; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}
