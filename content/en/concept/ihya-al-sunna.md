## Definition
Ihya' of the Sunna (the reviving of practice). The sunna is the path followed in religion; reviving the sunna here means restoring the practice of a rite of Islam after it has been neglected. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1881}
General ruling. Reviving a neglected sunna is required by law: as a collective duty, which is the basis, or as a personal duty, or as recommended; the detail is in the entry on commanding right («amr bi-l-ma'ruf»). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1881}
