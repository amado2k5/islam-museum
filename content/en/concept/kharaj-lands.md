## Overview
Al-Baladhuri transmits the views of the jurists on lands taken by force, such as al-Sawad and Syria. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Division and return of the land
According to Abu Yusuf, if the imam divides the land among the victors, it becomes land of tithe (*ushr*) and its people are slaves. If he does not divide it, and returns it to the Muslims as Umar did with al-Sawad, then the people bear the *jizya* on their heads and the land bears the *kharaj*, and they are not slaves. This is the view of [[person/abu-hanifa|Abu Hanifa]], and a similar view is transmitted from Sufyan al-Thawri. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

## Points of disagreement
According to Malik and Ibn Abi Dhi'b, when an unbeliever embraces Islam his land remains in his possession, to cultivate it and pay its *kharaj*. They disagreed on combining *kharaj* and *zakat*: Abu Hanifa forbade it, while Malik, Ibn Abi Dhi'b, Sufyan, Ibn Abi Layla, and al-Awza'i permitted it. As for sowing the land more than once in a year, most scholars required only one *kharaj*, while Ibn Abi Layla and Ibn Abi Sabra demanded the *kharaj* of every crop. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

They nearly all agreed that whoever left land idle must be obliged to cultivate it or hand it to someone who will, and that the *kharaj* is waived in case of a disaster or flood. They disagreed about reviving dead land, and about irrigating it with the water of the *kharaj* or with rainwater. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}
