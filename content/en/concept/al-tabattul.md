## Overview
For Shaykh Mahmud Shaltut, tabattul is withdrawal from life and total renunciation of the world — perpetual fasting, abandoning marriage and earning, contentment with a hermitage or cave. Islam, he holds, confines man's relation to the world to two courses with no third, and absolute renunciation is neither {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 21}{cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 23}. This course paralyses the powers of thought and work God gave man, leaving the secrets of the universe unused {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 24}. To those who cite the texts blaming the world he answers: what is blamed is taking God's blessings as a path to passion — not life itself and its good.
