## Summary
Ibn Sina gives southern dwellings the rulings of hot lands and seasons and details the state of men, women and the middle-aged there {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.128-129}.

## General rulings
Their rulings are those of hot lands and seasons, and most of their waters are salty and sulphurous. The heads of their people are full of moist matter because the south wind does this, their bellies are constantly loose from what flows down to the stomach from the head, their limbs are slack and weak, their senses heavy and their appetite for food and drink weak. Their hangover from drink is great because of the weakness of their heads and stomachs, and their sores are hard to heal and grow flabby {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.128-129}.

## Women and men
Their women have much menstrual bleeding, conceive only with difficulty and mostly miscarry because of their many ailments and for no other reason. Men suffer dysentery, haemorrhoids and moist ophthalmia that dissolves quickly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.129}.

## The middle-aged
Those past fifty are struck by palsy from their catarrhs, and asthma, distension and epilepsy afflict most of them because of fullness of the head; they get fevers combining heat and cold and the long winter and night fevers, while hot fevers are rare among them because of their many loose motions and the dissolving of the fine part of their humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.129}.
