## Summary
Ibn Sina makes the bones of the sacrum three, the most tightly fitted and firmly jointed of the vertebrae, and builds the coccyx from three cartilaginous vertebrae without processes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.52}.

## The sacrum
The bones of the sacrum (ajuz) are three. They are the most closely fitted of the vertebrae, the firmest in joint and the broadest in wings, and are like the lumbar bones. The nerve leaves through holes that are not exactly at the sides, so that the hip joint does not crowd them; they are set much further in, toward the front and the back {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.52}.

## The coccyx
The coccyx (usus) is made of three cartilaginous vertebrae without processes. Its nerves grow through shared holes, as in the neck because of its smallness; from the end of the third a single nerve leaves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.52}.
