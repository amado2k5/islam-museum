## Nature and selection
Dioscorides claimed that manur is the Egyptian poppy, and the author says he will mention it in the chapter of kha. With it ends the discussion of the letter mim, in all fifty-four drugs. The editor's footnote says it is the poppy, given for identity only {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.574}.
