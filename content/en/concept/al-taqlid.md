## Overview
Shaykh Shaltut rejects taqlid — inheriting beliefs and opinions from fathers and grandfathers without inquiry or proof — noting the Qur'an blamed it in many verses {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 57}. Sticking to the ancients' opinions merely because they came earlier collides with the law of intellectual growth as with the growth of creatures, and robs man of his faculty of telling truth from falsehood {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 58}{cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 59} — a crime, he calls it, against human nature.
