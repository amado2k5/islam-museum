## Overview
The munafiq in legal-theological usage is one who outwardly shows Islam while concealing disbelief; the Qur'an devotes sura al-Munafiqun to them, whose qualities Ibn Kathir details {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
Ibn Kathir details in his tafsir the chapters on the munafiqin, their qualities and their account in Medina {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In al-Tabari's commentary
Al-Tabari describes the hypocrites of "and among the people are those who say: we believe" as those who show affirmation with the tongue contrary to their hearts to avert death and captivity. He sees in the verse proof against the Jahmiyya claim that belief is affirmation by word alone, since God denies them belief although they said it. This is his reading and is attributed to him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 271} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 272}
