## Overview
The munafiq in legal-theological usage is one who outwardly shows Islam while concealing disbelief; the Qur'an devotes sura al-Munafiqun to them, whose qualities Ibn Kathir details {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
Ibn Kathir details in his tafsir the chapters on the munafiqin, their qualities and their account in Medina {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
