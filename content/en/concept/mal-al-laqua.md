## The term of repair
Every laqua extending six months is fit not to be hoped for repair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}

## Warning of palsy and apoplexy
Know that laqua may warn of palsy, indeed often warns of apoplexy; so look whether the prodromes of epilepsy and apoplexy draw it, and then hasten with a strong evacuation. Some have claimed the laqua patient is feared for up to four days, and if he passes he escapes — it seems to be on account of apoplexy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}

## The dry convulsive kind
Ibn Sina saw one who had a dry convulsive laqua, whom some physicians treated with warm compresses and warm things taken by mouth, so that side of his face became worse than it was and his tongue grew heavy in speech, and his time lengthened; when Ibn Sina treated him with the opposite of that he recovered, after suffering through the treatment (a witnessed case carried in the book's own words) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.156}
