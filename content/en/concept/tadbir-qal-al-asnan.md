## Its danger and drawing by drugs
Drawing an unloose tooth is dangerous at many times: it may bare the jaw, putrefy the substance, stir severe pain and even stir eye-pain and fever. When drawing is known to be hard and unbearable, wiggling it hard is not right — it increases the pain — though it may happen that the lesion is not in the tooth and the loosening dissolves the matter beneath and calms the pain. Teeth may also be drawn by drugs: the sounder course is to scarify around the tooth with the lancet and set the drug on it of this kind, then draw (the names and recipes left out; the scarification and lancet carried as the book's description, not a procedure), the sound teeth shielded with wax {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.277}
## Crumbling the corroded tooth without pain
The chapter on crumbling the corroded tooth — like drawing without pain: flour kneaded with the plant-milk he named is laid on it for hours and crumbles it, the sharp great ivy leaf added to it; and the tree-frog's fat cuts and crumbles — the green frog that dwells in plants and trees and leaps tree to tree (an animal item carried as the book names it, set before the founder) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.277}
