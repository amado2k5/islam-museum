## The treatment

A desiccating dressing is used on ulcers of the lid; once the scab has fallen and the corrosion ceased, the healing medicines are used. A lacerated lid is treated by the treatment of lacerated skins given in its own chapter ⁦(the names and compounds are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.197}
