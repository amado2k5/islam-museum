## Summary
Ibn Sina gives the genera of the pulse by stroke, time, texture, fullness, touch and rest, each with three kinds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.169-170}.

## Stroke, time and texture
By how the motion strikes the fingers: strong, which resists the touch at expansion, weak, its opposite, and moderate between. By the time of each motion: quick, which completes the motion in a short while, slow, its opposite, and moderate. By the texture of the instrument: soft, which yields inward easily, hard, its opposite, and moderate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.169-170}.

## Fullness, touch and rest
By what it holds: full, in which a notable inclining moisture is felt in its hollow, and empty, its opposite. By touch: hot, cold and moderate. By the time of rest: frequent, with a short felt interval between the two strokes (also called overtaking and dense), and infrequent, its opposite (also called slack and rarefied), with the moderate between {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.170}.
