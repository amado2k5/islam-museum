## Nature and selection
Well known; its flower is dusty and inclines to green, sweet and aromatic in smell {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Temperament
Al-Dimashqi said marmakhur is hotter than marjoram and stronger, hot in the third degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Actions and properties
Fine, dissolving, soothing wind, and opening phlegmatic obstructions wherever they are {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Put in wine it intoxicates quickly and smelling it over the wine gives headache, but smelling it or bending over its steaming-bath dissolves all vapours and cold headache, like wormwood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach, opens obstructions of the viscera and dries the stomach's moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Excretory organs
It strengthens the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.
