## Nature and selection
This is the drug known as al-halibi, as the al-Mu'tamad lexicon gives it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}.

## Temperament and actions
It has the least cooling and no astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}. Its power is resolving, with cooling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: it helps swellings of the ureter, as a poultice and as an application {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.376}.
