## Nature
An Indian drug resembling ginger; the editor's note says it is the Indian quince {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Actions and properties
It is bitter, astringent and pungent, breaks winds, is in the power of honey, and has a marvellous dissolving and subtilising power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}

## Joints
Beneficial for the nerve and for dislocations {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.674}
