## Absolute and not absolute sharing
Headache by sharing is either by absolute sharing or by sharing that is not absolute. Absolute sharing is when nothing bodily at all passes from the sharing organ to the region of the brain except the harm itself; sharing that is not absolute is when something passes to the substance of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49} Of the first kind are the kinds of headache that occur in convulsion, tetanus and stretching, in pains of the joints, and as in severe gout and sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49}

## What passes from the sharing organ
What passes of the qualities of the sharing may be a plain one of the natural qualities, or a strange bad quality not ascribed to heat or cold, like the poisonous qualities; for in some organ there may be a humour poisonous and bad in substance, and its quality passes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.49-50} What passes of matters may be matters not foreign in their natures, which cause harm only through the intensifying of their qualities or the growth of their amounts; or it may be a foreign matter generated in some organ by a strange and corrupt generation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50} The harmful quality that passes may itself become the cause of a harmful matter, in two ways: that quality may corrupt the good matters it finds in the regions of the brain or the good nourishment that reaches them, or it may make the brain receptive to bad matters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50} This receiving is of two kinds: receiving by its own drawing, as when the brain grows hot and draws matters to itself by the heat; and receiving through weak resistance, for the reader has learned in the principles that an organ, when weak, receives what comes to it of matters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50}

## Sharing with the whole body
Sharing with the whole body is either because of a matter spread through the whole body, and the crisis headache is of that kind, or because of a quality spread through the whole body, as in fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50} When headache becomes severe in acute fevers, its severity is a bad sign, even a deadly one if the other bad signs accompany it; if it stands alone it indicates a crisis by nosebleed, and it may indicate a crisis by vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50}

## The organs that share with the head
The organs that share with the head: the first and the most fitting is the stomach, for humours may be in excess in the stomach, and bile may be generated in it in cycles or not in cycles, the ring of bile being such that bile pours from its thick vessel and not the thin into the stomach, as explained in its place; or winds are held in it or vapours rise from it, and headache comes of this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50} Then the hypochondrium, the liver, the spleen, the diaphragm, the kidney, all the extremities and the region of the back; the first to share with the brain is the membrane around it that covers the skull; and headache by sharing is often when the matter moves from swellings of the inner sharing organs upward {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50}

## Womb and intercourse (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Ibn Sina names congestion of the womb as an example of a foreign matter generated in an organ by a corrupt generation and passing to the brain, and names with it the man who has long been without intercourse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50} He counts the womb among the organs that share strongly with the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.50}
