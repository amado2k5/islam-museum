## Nature and selection
It is melissa, as the editor's note gives for its name {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. It helps all phlegmatic and black-bile disorders {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: it greatly sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Wounds and ulcers: it helps black scabies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Head: it helps obstructions of the brain and removes bad breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Chest: it gladdens and strengthens the heart and stops palpitation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Food organs: it aids digestion and helps hiccup {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.

## Substitutes
For ulcerations the substitutes are silk and citron peel, without their proportions and weights {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.399}.
