## Nature and temperament
Nature: a plant that most people know; a small thorny shrub with small fine leaves round the branches, small heads at the tips bearing a purplish flower; it grows mostly in rocky and high places; its flower is white inclined to red. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Temperament: hot and dry in the third degree; Rufus says it is drier than pennyroyal. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}

## Actions
It dissolves and cuts, even clotted blood, and it warms, so that its drink prevents winter shivering. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: dissolves warts. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Swellings: applied as a poultice with vinegar on phlegmy swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Joints: drunk for weakness of the nerve; with barley gruel and wine applied for sciatica; its drink helps the pains below the xiphoid cartilage. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Organs of the eye: mixed in food it keeps the strength of sight and removes its weakness, as Dioscorides attests. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Organs of the chest: cleans the chest and lung, helps expectoration, eases pains below the xiphoid when boiled and licked with honey, and its drying stops bleeding from the chest. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Organs of digestion: helps digestion; its drink removes bad digestion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Excretory organs: brings urine and the menses, expels worms; a measured amount taken, between two and four dirhams, purges phlegm without harm; the amount is left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
Poisons: drunk and applied as a poultice with honey it helps the bites of vermin; fumigation with it drives them off. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.477}
