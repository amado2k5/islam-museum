## The treatments of the lung
The rule of this chapter: what was said in the asthma chapter is measured onto whatever shares its cause; the right form of a chest drug is that its meeting with the breath's passages be long, not that it hasten down to the stomach; the exercise of the voice and the puffing of the breath refine the lung's residues; and the residues are inclined from the oesophagus's side by vomiting when nothing forbids (names, measures and formulae are left out, per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.305}
