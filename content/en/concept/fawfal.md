## Nature
The fruit of a plant in India whose shape resembles that of nutmeg, except that fawfal is red and hard to break, its parts crumbling on breaking; it has a good smell, the people of India take it for a sweet breath, it reddens the teeth, and its power is near the power of sandalwood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Temperament
Cold in the third degree and dry in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Actions and properties
Strongly cooling and astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Swellings and pustules
Good for hot, thick swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Ocular organs
Suitable for one with inflammation in his eye, and as a poultice it keeps matter from the eyelids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}
