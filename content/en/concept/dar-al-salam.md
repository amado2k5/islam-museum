## Overview
Dar al-Salam — the Abode of Peace — is one of Paradise's names, so called in two places: «for them is the abode of peace with their Lord; He is their protector for what they used to do», and «God invites to the abode of peace and guides whom He wills to a straight path» {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the exegesis
Ibn Kathir explains the name: al-Salam is God Himself, so it is the house of God prepared for His friends; others read it as the abode whose people are safe from every harm and affliction {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
