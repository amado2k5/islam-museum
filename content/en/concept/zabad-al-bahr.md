## Nature and selection
Nature: five kinds, as the book sets them out: one spongy in shape; a dense coastal kind; a light, long, soft, spongy kind with a moss-like smell; a rose-purple kind; a light kind like dirty wool; and a fifth, fungus-shaped, smooth outside and rough inside, with no smell. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.460}
The editor's note identifies it as a stone formed of magnesium silicates. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.460}
Temperament: hot and dry in the third degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.460}

## Actions
Actions: it cleans dirt, scours and burns; the third kind is the finest. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.460}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it burns, especially the third kind, for the alopecia the book calls the fox's disease; the fungus kind depilates; it is said to help white patches; the two spongy kinds enter washes and remedies for milky pustules, kalaf and marks on the face; the rest depilates. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Head organs: the smooth kind suits polishing the teeth, and in general it is strong for the teeth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Swellings and pustules: the smooth kind is for nail-like swellings, and the rose kind for scrofulous swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Wounds and ulcers: it helps ulcerating scabies and the qawabi, especially the spongy kinds. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Joints: the rose kind helps gout with wax and rose oil. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Digestion organs: the rose kind helps the spleen and dropsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Excretory organs: the rose kind helps difficult urination and clears sand from the bladder. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
