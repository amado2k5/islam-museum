## Summary
In the fifteenth chapter, on the causes that lie in what is eaten and drunk, Ibn Sina divides the action of food and drink on the human body into three: action by its quality alone, action by its element, and action by the whole of its substance. He notes that the three terms come close in ordinary usage and that he fixes a particular sense for each {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.131}.
