## Nature
It is the most oily of the seeds and therefore turns rancid easily; some said there is no benefit in its oil except for the melancholic, whom it warms and moistens, and arsimun is a kind of sesame of foul taste {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.604}

## Selection
Its substance is stronger than its oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Temperament
Hot in the middle of the first degree and moist at its end {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Actions and properties
It is emollient and softening with a moderate warming, and so are its oil and its decoction; it relaxes, its oil has a thickness, and the roasted is less harmful {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Cosmetics
It dissolves the greenness of a blow and clotted blood; it is useful for cracks and roughness and for the melancholic, drunk and applied; it fattens, especially the peeled; it lengthens the hair, especially the juice of its tree and leaf, softens it and removes scurf; and its oil in which myrtle has been cooked keeps the hair, strengthens it and firms it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is applied on burns from fire, and drinking its oil removes phlegmatic and sanguine itching, especially with an infusion of aloe and raisin water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Joints
A poultice of it is used for thickness of the nerves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Organs of the head
Its oil benefits burning headache, and the juice of its tree removes scurf {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is bad for the stomach, nauseating, putting down appetite and filling quickly; eaten with honey it loses its harm; its digestion is slow and it relaxes the viscera; the roasted is less harmful; its nourishment is very oily and it causes thirst, and it passes down quickly with its husk and more slowly once peeled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Excretory organs
It is good for the colon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Poisons and bites
It benefits against the bite of the horned viper {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists a soaking of sesame as strong in provoking the menses until it causes the foetus to be lost, and says that soaked and eaten with poppy seed and flaxseed in moderation it increases semen and desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.605}
