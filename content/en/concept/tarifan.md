## Nature
A plant that grows in spring, its seed like safflower. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.502}

## Poisons
Its decoction, poured on a viper bite, calms the pain; poured on a healthy limb, it causes the same pain a viper bite causes. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.502}
