## In hadith
Al-Bukhari and Muslim narrate from the Prophet that two angels from God's angels came to him; in one narration they asked him about a man who had killed a hundred souls, and one said to the other: 'Do you not look into him since he has repented?' and the other said 'I will not look into him, for he killed a hundred souls' {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details the story, noting the angels descended on the man questioning him, that he repented, and that God commanded them to leave him {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some held the story concerns a man who repented from a hundred souls, others that it concerns one who killed a hundred deliberately; the primary view takes it as about repentance and mercy {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
