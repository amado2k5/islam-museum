## Overview
The angels of the story of the hundred souls are the two angels who disputed over the soul of a man who killed ninety-nine souls then repented, in Sahih al-Bukhari {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. One of them is of the angels of mercy and the other of the angels of punishment, so they disputed until revelation descended to the Prophet concerning him {cite:sahih-al-bukhari} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## The story in hadith
- In Sahih al-Bukhari from Abu Hurayra that a man killed ninety-nine people then went out asking; he came to a monk and asked whether there was repentance, and he said no, so he killed him; he kept asking and a man told him: go to such-and-such a village {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- "And death overtook him while he was heading toward it, and the angels of mercy and the angels of punishment disputed over him" — in it that the angels of mercy say he repented and was told of the village, and the angels of punishment say he killed ninety-nine souls {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- "Until God revealed concerning them a verse... God said: measure what is between them" — in it that God commanded the weighing of what is between them {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- "So He placed this one on one side and that one on the other" — in it that God placed his follower on one side and his killer on the other {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- In a narration of Muslim the man repented and was commanded to the village, and God forgave him {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details the story, mentioning that the angels of mercy and the angels of punishment disputed over the man, and that God commanded the weighing between them {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. Al-Qurtubi holds that the dispute among the angels concerns the repentant {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## The meaning of "al-Munfarij" in the name
- "Al-munfarij" in the language is from "farraja", meaning to widen and relieve; in the hadith the angels disputed over the man until God relieved between them by the weighing {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.
- The name is a technical term for the story, and the Qur'an does not name them {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## Their description
- One of them is of the angels of mercy and the other of the angels of punishment, in al-Bukhari's narration {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- They disputed over the soul until revelation descended to the Prophet concerning him {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- In a narration of Muslim that God commanded the weighing of what is between them {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## Notes for researchers
- "Angels of the story of the hundred souls" is a technical name; the hadith is in Sahih al-Bukhari {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
- In it is evidence that repentance is accepted even after ninety-nine souls, and that God relieves between His angels with justice {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
