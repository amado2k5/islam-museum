## Summary
Capital (ra's al-mal) in the lexicon is the principal of money without profit or increase: the whole sum put into an enterprise. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8530}

## Where it is discussed
The jurists mention the term in zakat, partnership, mudaraba, salam, usury, loan, trust sales, murabaha, tawliya and hitta, and its rules are treated in those places. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8530}
