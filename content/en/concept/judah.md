## Overview

In fiqh, judah bears on deceptive sale: the encyclopedia lists ways of making goods look better than they are. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6688} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6689}

## Examples of deception

These include inflating meat after skinning, gathering the water of a mill before offering it for sale or hire, and leaving milk in the udder so the animal looks productive. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6689}

## Transfer of debt

The schools differ on the conditions for a transfer of debt (hawala); the Hanafis do not require that the debtor owe the transferor. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6690}
