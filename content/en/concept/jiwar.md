## Overview

Jiwar covers the rights a neighbour has over a neighbour. The Shafi'is and Hanbalis set its limit at forty houses on each side. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6676}

## Shared walls and screens

A shared wall may be used only to the extent of the partner's benefit. Roofs need a screen against sight into a neighbour's house, and the schools differ on compelling the owner to build one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6679} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6680} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6682}

## Passage and pre-emption

A neighbour must let people pass along a public watercourse. Pre-emption belongs to the adjoining neighbour in the Hanafi view; others disagree. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6683} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6684}

## Effect on what an owner may do

The neighbour's rights limit how an owner may use his property where it harms the neighbour. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6681}
