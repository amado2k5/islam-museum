## Nature and temperament
Nature: an Indian drug that resembles white surankan. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
Temperament: hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}

## Uses by organ
Joints: drinking it helps gout and joint pains. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
Excretory organs: purges phlegm, the crude humours, worms, pumpkin-seed worms and thick humours. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
