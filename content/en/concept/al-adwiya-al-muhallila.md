## Galen's saying and what is left out
Galen alleged that a viper's liver laid on the aching painful tooth calms its pain for the time (a saying ascribed to Galen in the book's wording, carried as an attribution and set aside for the founder's ruling); the tried smears and pastes of this kind are left out; also rubs, smokes and funnel fumigations, resolvent snuffs, ear-drops on the aching side, and stuffing for the corrosion applied gently, never packed hard, the hot cause stuffed with the cooling and the cold with the warming of this kind; and the extractors, for which a chapter is set apart and which are only lawful to use when the pain is in the tooth itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.272-273}
