## The signs
Ibn Sina adds to what he has given the other signs, compounded from the signs of the single ones, in the sign of the prevalence of the matters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.25}

As for the bilious: heaviness that is not excessive, stinging, inflammation, severe burning, dryness in the nostrils, thirst, sleeplessness and a yellowness in the colour of the face and eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.25}

In the sanguine matters, an increased heaviness indicates them, throbbing may accompany it, and with it swelling of the face and eyes, redness of colour, fullness of the vessels and coma {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.25}

In the cold phlegmatic matters: a felt cold, long distress and its crises, little redness of colour; the heaviness is more in the phlegmatic matter, with laziness, dullness, coma, forgetfulness and a leaden colour in the face, the eye and the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.25}

In the black-bilious matters: the heaviness is less, the sleeplessness is more, with obsessions and corrupt thoughts, and a dullness of colour of the face, the eye and all the organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.25}
