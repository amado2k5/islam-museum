## What it is
For the bleeding gum, what he described of the vinegar-quenched burnt thing beaten with salt helps, and what is sprinkled on it of this kind; also the ash of a burnt salted tariikh (the editor glosses it a kind of fish, carried as the book names it) burnt to ember and taken with what he described, mixed and used (the names and measures left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.281}
