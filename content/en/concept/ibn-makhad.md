## Summary
Ibn makhad is the young of a she-camel that has entered its second year. It is so named because its mother has joined the pregnant (makhad), even if she is not pregnant. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1466}

## Zakat
The basic rule is that it does not suffice. The Hanafis allow taking it at the value of a bint makhad, since in their view value suffices for every kind of zakat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1466}

## Blood money
It may be counted among the categories of the light diya according to the Hanafis, the Hanbalis and one Shafi'i view. The Malikis, and the accepted Shafi'i view, deny this. The jurists agree that it is not one of the categories of the heavy diya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1466} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1467}
