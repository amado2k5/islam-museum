## Nature
Its name in Greek is derived from the name of one of many benefits {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Temperament
Hot with moderation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Actions and properties
It has softening, ripening, relaxing and dissolving, and its seed and root are of like power, stronger, more drying and subtler {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Cosmetics
It is applied on bahaq with vinegar and one sits in the sun, its seed being stronger in that {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Swellings and pustules
It softens swellings and prevents them, dissolves the sanguine, ripens boils and benefits windy swellings and scrofula, and is put with sulphur and gum on scrofula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Joints
It calms joint pain, especially with goose fat, and benefits sciatica, tremor, bruising of the middle of muscles and stretching of the nerves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Organs of the head
Poulticed with it, it benefits swellings in the glands of the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Ocular organs
It dissolves the inflammation and puffiness that occur in the eyelids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Organs of the chest
Its seed is of benefit for hot cough, eases expectoration and prevents the spitting of blood through an astringent power in it, its leaf benefits swellings of the breast and enters poultices for pleurisy and the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its gum calms thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.701}

## Excretory organs
A decoction of its roots, drunk, is of benefit for burning of urine, burning of the intestine and swellings of the anus, and so is its leaf, and likewise for bad diarrhoea; a decoction of its root given with a drink benefits difficulty of urine and stone, especially its seed, and its gum holds the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.701-702}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it is carried with terebinth gum for hardness and contraction of the womb, and likewise its decoction alone, and that it cleanses the lochia; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}

## Poisons and bites
Applied with vinegar and oil it prevents the harm of vermin, and its decoction with diluted vinegar or a drink is of benefit applied to a bee sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.702}
