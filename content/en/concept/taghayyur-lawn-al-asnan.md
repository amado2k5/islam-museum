## What whitens at once
Among what whitens at once are ground clay (the editor: potter's clay), the magnet-stone, al-mashaquniya (the editor: stones cooked of glass and kohl-stone), al-sinbadhij (the editor: the whetstone) and al-qayshur (the editor: a light pumice-like stone floating on water, used as a foot-scrubber, the stone of the chiropodist) — carried as the book's names with the editor's glosses; the recipes are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.275-276}
