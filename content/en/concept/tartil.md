## Overview
Tartil is the measured, distinct articulation of recitation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia treats tartil and tarsil among the connected terms of tilawa {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
