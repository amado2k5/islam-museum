## Summary
Kirdar is a Persian word, called in Khwarazm the right of tenure. It names what is built or planted on land held in endowment on lease, and lands the ruler has taken for the public treasury and given out for sharecropping on half the yield {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13241}.

## Sale
Tenants of such endowed or similar land may sell what they built, planted or filled with earth, once the kirdar is known, because what they made is theirs and they hold the right of tenure in the land {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13241}. Pre-emption in it is treated in the entry on fixtures {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13241}.
