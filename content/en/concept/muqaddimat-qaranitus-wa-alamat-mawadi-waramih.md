## What comes before it
When you see the signs of acute diseases and hard fevers and the bowels are held, that warns of sarsam; before qaranitus come forgetfulness of the near thing, stubbornness without cause, bad dreams, much headache, heaviness and fullness, and for the most part a yellow face, long wakefulness and troubled sleep; these symptoms grow strong while the matters are turning toward the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78}

## As the swelling draws near
When they draw near it and the brain drinks the matter, they find the start of a pain from behind the head at the nape, especially in the bilious; when they fall into it and the brain swells, their eyes first dry hard, then begin to water, especially one of the two, and gather rheum, and their vessels often turn strongly red, and sometimes drops of blood from the nose follow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78} They often do not blink, and their hands fiddle so that they pick at straw and fluff, sometimes with the eyes closed and sometimes with staring and vexation; they may be too lazy for fluent speech and do no more than move the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78} Urine may drip from them, with their knowledge or without it, which in fevers is one of the strong indications of present sarsam, and they are heedless of pains in their organs, so that even rough touch on a painful organ goes unfelt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78}

## The places of the swelling
If the swelling falls in the front it spoils imagination, so they pick fluff from their clothes and straw and the like from the walls and imagine shapes that do not exist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78} If it is toward the middle it spoils thought, so he confuses what he knows and pours out much raving, and if it falls toward the back he forgets what he sees and does at that moment {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78} If the swelling takes in all sides, all these signs appear, and if the brain swells with it the face and eye redden and the eyes bulge strongly, or both turn red if the swelling matter is blood and yellow if it is pure bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78}
