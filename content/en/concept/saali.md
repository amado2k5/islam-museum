## Nature
It is of a hot substance and a watery substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Temperament
It is hot and pungent in moderation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Swellings and pustules
Its leaf bursts abscesses and dissolves them while they are beginning, and the fresh kind ripens swellings stubborn to ripen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Wounds and ulcers
The fresh kind removes ulcerating scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Organs of the eye
It enters the drugs that sharpen sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.

## Organs of the chest
It is said to be the best remedy for cough and orthopnoea, even by fumigation with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.596}.
