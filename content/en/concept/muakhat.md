## Summary
Mu'akhat is a bond between two people upon mutual support and affection. After the hijra the Prophet paired the Muhajirun with the Ansar so that each emigrant became the brother of a Medinan host who sheltered and aided him, and al-Bukhari dedicated a chapter of his Sahih — the Book of Brotherhood (kitab al-ikha') — to what is narrated about it {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}. In Sunan Abi Dawud he is reported saying "your brother is the one who supports you" {cite:sunan-abi-dawud}. Mu'akhat thus stands as the founding form of Islamic brotherhood: a visible pact to aid a brother, not merely a feeling.
