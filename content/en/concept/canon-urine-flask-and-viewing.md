## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that the vessel for urine is a clear body of pure substance such as clear glass or crystal, that it is viewed in light but screened from the direct ray, and that urine looks thicker the nearer it is held and clearer the farther {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.184}.

## The vessel and the viewing
The vessel for urine is the clear body of pure substance, such as clear glass and crystal. Urine grows thicker the nearer you bring it and clearer the farther you hold it, and by this it differs from other adulterations set before physicians as tests. A flask of urine must be guarded from the change that cold, sun and wind make in it, and looked at in the light without the ray falling on it, being screened from the ray, and then a judgement is made from the signs seen in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.184}.
