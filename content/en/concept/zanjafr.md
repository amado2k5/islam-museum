## Nature and selection
Nature: some say its power is that of isfidaj, and others that it is that of sadanj. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Temperament: the most correct view is that it is hot and dry, about the end of the second degree; anything said otherwise is from ignorance of it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}

## Actions
Actions: according to some, its astringency is stronger than its drawing power; according to others, its drawing power is stronger than its astringency. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: it heals wounds, grows flesh in ulcers, and prevents burns from fire and the burn the book calls hasaf. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
Head organs: it prevents the teeth from wearing away. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.461}
