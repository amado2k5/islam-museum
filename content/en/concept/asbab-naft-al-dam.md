## The causes of the spitting of blood
When sanguine fullness befalls, nature sets about pushing the matter to whatever side is open to it, the readiest or nearest to the place of the surplus — pushing it by spitting or by a flow or the like; and if the vessels are too strong to yield the blood, sudden death befalls from the blood's pouring into the vessels' hollows (lines on the book's wording; among them a labelled line in women) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.333}
