## Overview
The madhhab in juristic usage is the method of ijtihad traced to an imam and his school; the jurists enumerate the 'four madhhabs' — Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia applies 'madhhab' throughout to the imams and their four schools {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}; Fiqh al-Sunna presents the rulings across the madhhabs {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}.
