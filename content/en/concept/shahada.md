## Summary
"The two testimonies" are the testimony that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is God's messenger — the first thing the Prophet mentioned in his saying "Islam is built on five" {cite:sahih-ibn-khuzayma p.192}. They are one of [[concept/five-pillars|the five pillars of Islam]].

## In Gabriel's hadith
When the Prophet defined [[concept/islam-din|Islam]] for Gabriel he said: "That you worship God and associate nothing with Him" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.

## What is not covered here
The creed books treat the meaning of "there is no god but God", its conditions and the categories of tawhid; that will be added in a later batch from the creed sources with their authors' sayings, and is not presented here since it is not in the sources consulted.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

The Sheikh says that the highest branch of faith is the saying "there is no god but God", that the first of the branches is the testimony, and that Abu Jahl understood its meaning {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:39:00}. He says that one Companion treated trustworthiness, truthfulness and refusing false witness as part of the meaning of "there is no god but God", so that for him prayer was not separated from trustworthiness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep03 00:09:30}.

The Sheikh holds, as his own opinion, that the believer who prays carries the ethics of "there is no god but God" {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:15:30}, and that the words of the testimony that come out at a critical moment are a sign of a heart full of faith {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep11 00:17:00}.
