## Ways of evacuation
The eye's treatments answer its diseases: since the diseases are either material-temperamental, simple-temperamental, structural, or solutions of continuity, the material kind is treated by evacuating the matters — either by turning them away or by milking them out of her; the turning away is first from the body when full, then from the brain by its known cleansers, then diverting by way of the nose and the veins near the eye ⁦(the venesection sites are not carried)⁩; the milking out is by the tear-drawing drugs; the temper is changed likewise by propriety drugs; and the solution of continuity is treated by drugs of mild drying far from biting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.164} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.165}

## Regimen and the order of derivation
Know that for the eye's material diseases food must be lessened, what breeds the laudable humour taken, and every vaporous and ill-digesting thing shunned; if the matter rises from an organ that organ is venesected; if it comes by way of the outer membranes, cupping and repellents on the brow are used ⁦(their sites and names are not carried)⁩; the soundest place to draw the eye's matters off to is the nostrils, when not on the flow-path to the eye — and that derivation is by sternutatives and snuffs ⁦(the juice and drug names are not carried)⁩; and know that if with the eye's ills evacuation, cleansing and right regimen do not suffice, then there is a cold temper in the eye, or a lurking malign matter in the coats corrupting the nourishment reaching it, or a weakness in the brain whose catarrhs are discharged onto the eye from elsewhere {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.165} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.166}
