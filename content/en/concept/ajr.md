## Summary
For jurists, ajr is the consideration for work, whether it comes from God or from people, though a reward from God is a bounty. It also means the price of a benefit, whether of a building, such as lodging in a house, or of a movable, such as riding in a car. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1592}

## Qur'anic usage
The Qur'an calls what is given to a nursing woman an ajr, and calls a woman's dowry an ajr. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1592}

## Where the topic is treated
Jurists treat questions of ajr for work and benefit within the lease. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1592}
