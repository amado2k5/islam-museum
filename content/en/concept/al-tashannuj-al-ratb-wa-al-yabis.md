## The moist
The repletion-moist convulsion's own cause is the moisture, cold aiding it by congealing and thickening so it does not extend; the matter making the convulsion convulses only by entering the fibre's substance in a spreading, benefiting entry, yet crowding in the gap — as though convulsion were the epilepsy of a member as epilepsy is the convulsion of the whole body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.143}

## The dry
Of the dry convulsion is what follows the purging medicine — very bad — and likewise after every evacuation; some of it follows the burning fevers, or especially in the fevers of phrenitis, and after violent bodily and psychic motions like sleeplessness, grief and fear, and that is a thing from which escape is lost {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.144}

## The dry from dryness of brain and cold
Of this dry kind is what comes and passes quickly, its cause the brain's dryness for weakness, the nerves' dryness following it: if the slightest drying cause strikes the brain it calls back moisture from the nerves and cord, the nerves contract, then when nature tends to supply the brain enough moisture the organs return obedient to extension with effort; and as it occurs from severe cold, convulsion often occurs for the brain's coldness with the muscles sharing in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.144}

## Nursing women and the drunk (labelled listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Of the moist convulsion is what befalls nursing women from the breast's nearness and the milk-nature moistening the tendons and the milk congealing in them, and of it what befalls the drunk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.143}
