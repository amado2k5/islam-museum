## Nature and selection
It is the kazmarik {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.422}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: its heat is like the balanced mean, or at the start of the first degree; its drying is at the end of the first degree or above it; some say it is cold in the first degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423}. Actions: it is good for stopping bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423}.

## Uses by organ
Head: a vinegar gargle for toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423}.
Food: its decoction in water and vinegar is very good for a hard spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423}.
