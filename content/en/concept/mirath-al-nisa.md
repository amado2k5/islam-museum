## Summary
Al-Sha'rawi says the verse "it is not lawful for you to inherit women against their will" ends the pre-Islamic practice of denying women inheritance and of inheriting them by force. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2077}

## The pre-Islamic custom
He says the Arabs denied women inheritance and denied it to anyone who had not taken part in defending the tribe, and that Islam gave this weak group its right in a fixed share. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2046}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2047}

## Free women and slave women
He holds that "women" in the verse refers to free women, because slave women were treated as owned, and that slave women fall under a different ruling. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2077}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2078}

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] says that before Islam a woman was inherited rather than inheriting, and that among the Arabs inheritance went to those who rode horses and bore arms, until Islam fixed a share for women ([[passage/quran-4-19|al-Nisa' 19]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep03 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep03 00:05:30} He says the Qur'an sets out inheritance in detail in three verses because it is a matter that does not change with time and place ([[passage/quran-4-11|al-Nisa' 11]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep03 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:06:00} With a worked example of an estate in Qatar he argues that a son's larger share is offset by the dower and gifts he pays and his sister receives, so he sees no favouring of men, and he rejects calls to abolish the rule on the ground that the extended family is gone. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:19:30} He considers the obligatory bequest for grandchildren in some Arab laws a correct piece of ijtihad. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep04 00:14:00}
