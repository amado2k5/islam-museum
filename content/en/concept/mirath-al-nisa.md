## Summary
Al-Sha'rawi says the verse "it is not lawful for you to inherit women against their will" ends the pre-Islamic practice of denying women inheritance and of inheriting them by force. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2077}

## The pre-Islamic custom
He says the Arabs denied women inheritance and denied it to anyone who had not taken part in defending the tribe, and that Islam gave this weak group its right in a fixed share. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2046}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2047}

## Free women and slave women
He holds that "women" in the verse refers to free women, because slave women were treated as owned, and that slave women fall under a different ruling. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2077}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2078}
