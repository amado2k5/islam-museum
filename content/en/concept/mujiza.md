## Overview
Ibn Khaldun holds that the extraordinary events that happen to prophets are evidence of their truthfulness: acts that humans cannot match, and so they are called a *mu'jiza* (miracle). {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p.91-93}

In his view, the theologians say that they occur by the power of God and not by the act of the prophet, and that the prophet has in them the challenge by God's leave; this is what distinguishes them from the marvel of a saint (*karama*) and from magic. The philosophers hold that the extraordinary event is from the act of the prophet, by the properties of his prophetic soul. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p.91-93}

The greatest of the miracles, in his view, is the Qur'an, because it is the claimed revelation and is itself the extraordinary, inimitable event. {cite:muqaddima-ibn-khaldun p.91-93}

## In Ahmad Farid's lessons 'The Boat'
In [[work/the-boat-ahmad-farid|'The Boat']] [[person/ahmad-farid|Ahmad Farid]] presents the Prophet's miracles as a cause of the growth of faith in him and love for him. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:04:00} His life story is itself among his miracles, he says, but the greatest is the Qur'an, and he recalls the hadith that every prophet was given signs on which people believed, while what the Prophet was given was revelation. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:03:30} Against the view that he had fewer sensory miracles than earlier prophets he quotes [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]], that God gave no prophet anything He did not give Muhammad, answering the raising of the dead by [[person/isa-ibn-maryam|Jesus]] with the weeping palm trunk, and he carries the comparison further himself: the splitting of the moon answering the parting of the sea by [[person/musa|Moses]], and water from between his fingers answering the twelve springs from the rock, a greater wonder in the speaker's view since water from stone is known in many places. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep11 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:01:30} He adds the stone in Mecca that greeted the Prophet, the trees that came at his word, and the rain that came and stopped at his prayer during a Friday sermon. {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmad-farid the-boat/ep12 00:04:00}
