## Headache that comes with fevers and acute diseases
Of this kind some comes with the intensifying of the disease or the fit and then passes, and some remains after the disease has gone or the fit has ceased; the one that comes in fevers may so trouble the patient that it adds to its cause, the fever {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68} It may be shown by the urine whitening at once and turning to resemble the urine of donkeys, but since it resembles the urine of donkeys it may show that it is present now and may show its dissolving, so one must go back to the other signs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68} The rising of the matters in it is held back at the beginning; if the severity of pain forces the use of narcotics and sleep-inducers, that is done with care and caution; and the regimen of the hangover is used in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.68-69} The headache may remain after the fever and after acute diseases, and its treatment is cooling and moistening the foods and strengthening the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.69}

## Crisis headache
As for crisis headache, one looks to the direction toward which nature inclines with the matter, and helps it there according to the signs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.69} If the sick person finds nausea, turning of the stomach, twitching in the lip and giddiness, in short the signs of the nature's inclining with the matter upward, he is helped toward vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.69} If he finds rumbling and puffing in the two flanks, in short the signs of the nature's inclining with the matter downward, he is helped toward loosening the bowels; if he finds heaviness in the region of the kidneys and under the back ribs, in short the signs of the matter's inclining to the way of the urine, it is treated by promoting the urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.69} If he sees radiance and redness before the eyes and imaginings, followed by the printed words safr aw tatawul (left as printed and not interpreted here), and no nosebleed comes, he is helped toward a nosebleed; if he finds a slack pulse and softness in the skin, he is helped toward sweat; and if he finds something like a sting somewhere, drawing means are used on it; the particular measures for each are not reproduced here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.69}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.68-69}
