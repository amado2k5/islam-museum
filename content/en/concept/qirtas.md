## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Actions and properties
Its burnt form prevents the spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Swellings and pustules
Its burnt form benefits tinea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}

## Organs of the head
Its burnt form prevents nosebleed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.654}
