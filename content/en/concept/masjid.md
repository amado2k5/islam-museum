## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 1)
The Sheikh relates a hadith to the effect that when God wills punishment for the people of the earth, He looks to those who build and frequent the mosques and lifts the punishment from them (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:00:30}. He contrasts the condition of the believer in the mosque with that of the hypocrite in it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:00:30}.
