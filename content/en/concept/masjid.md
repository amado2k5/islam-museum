## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 1)
The Sheikh relates a hadith to the effect that when God wills punishment for the people of the earth, He looks to those who build and frequent the mosques and lifts the punishment from them (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:00:30}. He contrasts the condition of the believer in the mosque with that of the hypocrite in it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01 00:00:30}.

## In Abdul Hamid Kishk's sermons
[[person/abdul-hamid-kishk|Abdul Hamid Kishk]] holds that those who fight Islam strike at the mosques, not with shells but by making people afraid to enter them; he cites the phrase 'watch your children in the mosques' and says it was said in '65 (needs_source: a date he gives without detail; his opinion). {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:17:00} He says that in the Prophet's time the mosque was at once the seat of leadership and of worship, a gathering place in the widest sense. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:19:30}
