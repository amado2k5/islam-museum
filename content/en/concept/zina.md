## Overview
Zina in juristic usage is among the fixed hudud penalties; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia's 'hadd al-zina' entry details the conditions of its enforcement {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia's hadd-al-zina entry details how the penalty is carried out and the flogging rulings among the jurists {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## Sha'rawi on publicity and strictness
Sha'rawi holds that strictness in verifying the act of adultery is required, since error causes anxiety and doubt about lineage, and that the penalty is carried out openly so that a group of believers witness it and are deterred. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.753} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.754}
