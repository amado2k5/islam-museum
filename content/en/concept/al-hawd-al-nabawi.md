## Summary
In works of creed, the Basin is a pool to which the Prophet's community comes on the Day of Resurrection; [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] gives it a chapter in the Book of the Sunna of the [[work/sunan-abi-dawud|Sunan]], after the chapter on the creation of Paradise and the Fire and before the chapter on the questioning in the grave. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 72} {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 74} The annotator notes that Ibn al-Qayyim, in his commentary on al-Mundhiri's abridgement, listed forty Companions who related reports of the Basin. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 72}

## Its size
[[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|Ibn 'Umar]] relates from the Prophet: "Before you is a basin whose two sides are as far apart as [[place/jarba|Jarba']] and [[place/adhruh|Adhruh]]." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 72} The annotator identifies Jarba' as a town of Syria and Adhruh as one of the nearer towns of Syria, or, some say, in Palestine. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 72}

## Those who come to it
[[person/zayd-ibn-arqam|Zayd ibn Arqam]] relates that they halted with the Prophet at a stopping place and he said, "You are not one part in a hundred thousand of those who will come to me at the Basin"; they were then seven or eight hundred. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 73}

## The Basin and al-Kawthar
[[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas]] relates that the Prophet dozed, then raised his head smiling and said that a sura had just been revealed to him, and recited [[passage/quran-108-1|Surat al-Kawthar]]; then he said, "Do you know what al-Kawthar is?… It is a river my Lord promised me in Paradise, with much good in it; on it is a basin to which my community will come on the Day of Resurrection, its vessels as many as the stars." {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 73} In Qatada's version from Anas, when the Prophet was taken up he was shown in Paradise a river whose banks were of hollowed gemstone (yaqut), and the angel told him, "This is [[concept/al-kawthar|al-Kawthar]]"; [[person/abu-sulayman-al-khattabi|al-Khattabi]] explains *mujayyab* as "hollow". {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 73}

## The account of Abu Barza
Abu Talut 'Abd al-Salam ibn Abi Hazim relates that he saw [[person/abu-barza-al-aslami|Abu Barza]] enter upon [[person/ubayd-allah-ibn-ziyad|'Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad]], who made a remark slighting the old man; Abu Barza understood and said, "I never thought I would live among people who taunt me with having been a Companion of Muhammad." 'Ubayd Allah then asked him about the Basin, and he said, "Yes — I heard him mention it not once, nor twice, nor three, four or five times; whoever denies it, may God not give him to drink from it," and went out angry. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 73-74} The annotator notes that its chain contains an unnamed man. {cite:sunan-abi-dawud-ibn-hazm-1997-v5 p. 74}

## Not covered here
Al-Khattabi does not discuss the Basin as a doctrine in this chapter; the views of the theologians and others on it are to be added from their sources.
