## What it is
Al-daras is a numbness befalling the tooth from a roughening thing — either astringent or acid — from what touches the tooth coming from outside or vomited, or what rises to it from the stomach when a sour humour is there; it may also follow the imaginary fancy at seeing someone crunch something very sour with relish. Chewing what he described of the greens and nuts helps it, and mouth-rinsing with the described milk and oil kinds; and salt, for opposing the sourness, is very beneficial against it (the names of this kind carried as the book names them) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.278}
