## Summary
Ibn Sina sets out what the patient does once he has finished vomiting: wash mouth and face with vinegar mixed with water, drink a little mastic in apple water, abstain from food and water, rest, oil the soft part under the ribs, enter the bath, and if he must be fed, eat something pleasant, of good substance and quick to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.287}.

## The steps
When the one who vomits has finished, he washes his mouth and face with vinegar mixed with water, to remove the heaviness that may come to the head, drinks a little mastic in apple water, refrains from eating and from drinking water, keeps to rest and oils the soft parts under his ribs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.287}.

## Bath and food
He enters the bath, washes quickly and leaves; if he must be fed, the food should be pleasant, of good substance and quick to digest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.287}.
