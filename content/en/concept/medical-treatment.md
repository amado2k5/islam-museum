## Overview
The fourth fann of the Canon's first book classifies the modes of treatment by general diseases in thirty-two chapters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.267}. For Ibn Sina treatment is accomplished by three things: regimen and diet, the use of drugs, and the use of manual operations {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.267}. Regimen has rules parallel to those of drugs in quality; food has rules of quantity — it may be forbidden, reduced, altered, or increased {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.267}.
