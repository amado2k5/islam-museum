## Summary
The descent of Jesus son of Mary in the last days is a major sign the Prophet foretold: he descends near the white minaret on the east side of Damascus, breaks the cross, kills the pig, kills the Dajjal at the gate of Ludd, judges the people justly, and leads or follows the Mahdi in prayer. Reports say he brings the people out of the trials of the Dajjal and of Gog and Magog.
