## Summary
In his series "Al Fawzu Al Moubeen", Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy takes up God's punishment of nations that rebelled against His way, naming wind, the shout, earthquakes and volcanoes among the means. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:07:30}
He sets out a scientific explanation of earthquakes and then objects that the believer sees in them a divine reminder. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:14:30}
He answers a question about a hadith linking abandoning jihad to afflictions, holding that the outcome is tied to sins, and then analyses Umar's stance on the shaking of the earth. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:35:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:36:00}

## The speaker's view on the punishment of nations
The Sheikh mentions nations that God punished with wind, the shout, earthquakes and volcanoes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:07:30}
He states that Iceland has thirty-six volcanic craters (needs_source: volcano-count). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:07:30}

## Earthquakes between science and divine reminder
The Sheikh presents the scientific explanation of an earthquake as the movement of the earth's layers, and then objects that the believer sees in it a reminder from God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:14:30}
This view is recorded here as the speaker's own; the museum does not weigh it against the scientific explanation.

## A question on the hadith about abandoning jihad
A listener asks about a hadith which says that abandoning jihad brings afflictions, sinking into the earth and transformation. The Sheikh answers that the outcome is tied to sins (needs_source: hadith-unnamed). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:35:00}

## The speaker's view on Umar's stance
The Sheikh analyses the stance of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]], that the earth shakes because of the deeds of those who forgot God. He lists examples of individual sins that he regards as a departure from the path. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep06 00:36:00}
