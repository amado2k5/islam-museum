## Taurus (al-Thawr)

Taurus, al-Thawr, the bull, follows Aries in the zodiac group. The list opens with the northern one of four stars in one part of the figure and continues through the figure. The Pleiades, al-Thurayya, follow a few pages later under their own heading. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.155}
