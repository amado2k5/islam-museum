## The causes of the stench
Either putrid vapours rising to it from the region of the chest, the lung and the stomach, having caused ulcers — a foul-smelling putrefaction whose smell may make its way above and be sensed; or a putrid humour in the belly, or in the whole of the brain, its front, or the part adjoining the nose; or a putrefaction and corruption befalling those bones themselves, whose treatment is hard; or putrid piles in the nose {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.245}
