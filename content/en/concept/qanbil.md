## Nature
Sandy seeds topped with a redness less than the redness of wars {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Actions and properties
Ibn Masawayh said: it has strong astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Excretory organs
It kills worms and tapeworm and brings out the tapeworm, drunk and smeared, as it is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}
