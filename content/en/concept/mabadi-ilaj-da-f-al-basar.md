## Treatment for dryness and for moisture

If the cause is dryness, whey-water and the moisteners benefit, drinking milk, moistening oils on the head especially in convalescents, sleep and rest and moistening snuffs; what lies in a layer is hard to treat. If it is from moisture, what dissolves is used after the evacuations; thin vomiting is among the beneficial things, especially for the elderly, and the stale harms greatly; gargles, mouth-soaks and sternutatories benefit. Exercise of the limbs benefits, especially the lower ones, and their rubbing must be used; if the cause is a thickening it is treated with the clearing medicines, and the astringents are used with them ⁦(the medicines, collyria, recipes and measures are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.204}

## Regimen and preserving the power

Among beneficial regimen is using the comb on the head, for it draws the matter upward and moves it away from the eye, especially for the elderly — it should be used several times a day; and opening the eyes as much as possible preserves the eye's health and strengthens it, especially in the young; and one who complains of stomach vapours and the harm of moisture should take before food what loosens the residues in the stomach. The book reports that eating turnip always, roasted and cooked, strengthens the sight greatly so that it removes long-standing weakness, and that some medicines make the eye so that gazing at the sun's disc does not harm it ⁦(their recipes are not reproduced)⁩; and it mentions a medicine for one whose sight weakened from coitus ⁦(the coitus line is carried as a listing held for the founder's ruling)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.205} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.206}
