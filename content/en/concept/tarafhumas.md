## Nature
Dioscorides says some people call it adbar. It grows where the Persian herb grows and resembles the plant called fartis. Its leaves are very long and thin, like lentil leaves, set opposite each other on hard, thin, blackish-brown stems. It is thought to act like the Persian herb in all its actions. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}
