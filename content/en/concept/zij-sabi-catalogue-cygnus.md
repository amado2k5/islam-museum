## The Hen

The catalogue gives the stars of al-Dajaja, the Hen, as a section of its own. It begins with the star on the beak, then the star in the middle of the neck and the star in the breast, and the bright star on the tail. The entries continue through the wings and the legs. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.249}
