## Definition
In language sifa is an adornment or description. For grammarians it is a noun showing a state of the thing; for jurists it is a description so fixed that only a slight variation remains after it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10403}

## For the usulis
For the usulis, sifa is the restriction of a word with several meanings by another, specific word; they do not mean only the grammatical adjective. Its concept includes attaching a ruling to a thing by one of its qualities, as in the saying that a free-grazing flock owes zakat. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10403} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10404}

## Its effect on contracts
Description enters the conditions of salam and of sale on description. If the described quality is not found, the buyer has the option of the lost attribute. The attribute has no share of the price, since it follows the contract and enters without being named. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10404}
