## The evacuation first, and the means
Whatever bad humour has gathered must first be evacuated if it lies outside the nasal fossa; then the local means — tents, snuffs, insufflations and the like. For the tried tents it is best to wash the nose with wine first, then use them; among those tents is one of the known kind, and many sorts of tents made of these drugs in differing weights ⁦(their names and weights are not carried)⁩. Among the snuffs is a snuff of a juice of the known kind; and the best and most useful of snuffs, the book says, are donkey urines, for they do not fail ⁦(the book's saying, carried as attributed)⁩. Among the well-tried: tablets of the known kind dissolved in wine and dripped into the nose, curing; and a decoction of the known kind in wine, very good, used for days by inhalation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.245}
## Liniments, insufflations, inhalations and the cautery
Among the liniments: smearing its inside with a thing of the known kind, and a leaf of the known kind heated then ground with water and smeared on the nose, and a drug of the known kind ⁦(names and weights not carried)⁩. Among the insufflations: blowing into it a thing of the known kind; among the inhalations: a tried decoction in water or wine used for days. And among what has been tried in its treatment — especially when the putrefaction is in the brain or its front — two cauteries on the right and left of the fontanelle, beside the ears inclining to the temples, or a cautery on the middle of the head ⁦(the cautery sites carried as the book's description, not as instruction)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.246}
