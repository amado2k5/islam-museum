## The sign
As for the sign of the headache claimed to be generated from worms, al-Hindi said: the sign of the headache from worms is that it is gnawing and severe, with a foul smell, and that the headache grows strong with movement and quiets with stillness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.52}

## Treatment
In treating the headache claimed to be due to worms, one must begin by cleansing the body and the brain, then use what is mentioned in the chapter on foulness of the nose and what kills worms in the belly, followed by what cleanses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.69-70} In the headache that stirs after sleep and drowsiness, the body and the head must be cleansed in the ways the reader knows; a poultice is also named, which is not reproduced here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.70}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.52-70}
