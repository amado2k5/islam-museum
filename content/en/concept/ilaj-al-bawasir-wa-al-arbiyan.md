## The hand-work and the thread-saw
What is of the first kind is cut with a fine knife, then scraped with the scraper gently; what is of the second kind is best cauterised — either with the drugs we name ⁦(carried by kind)⁩ or with small fine irons — or cut with scrapers taking out all the growths and refuse in the nose. The best of scrapers is the tubular one; then vinegar and water are poured into the nostrils. If the breath runs freely after that and the occlusion is gone, done; otherwise a remnant remains in the depth, and then the thread-saw is needed. Its description: take a thread of hair or silk, tie it in knots that make it like a toothed saw, pass it on a bent lead needle, introducing it through the nostril till it comes out at the palate, then saw the rest of the flesh by pulling it from both sides as with a saw; then take a tube of the known kind, wrap a cloth on it dusted with the piles drugs, so the place of breathing stays open. If a file-like but tubular scraper is made, the aim of cleansing can be reached by it. When the instruments of cutting and scraping or the corrosive drugs are used on the piles, sneezing must then be induced so all putrefaction and sawdust is scattered ⁦(the hand-work, the instruments and the thread are carried as the book's description, not as instruction)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.249}
## The drugs for the light and the chronic, and the treatment of the arbiyan
The drugs treating the light kind: a tried tent of the known kind — slow in benefit — and other tents and insufflations of the known kind; those treating the chronic kind: tents, dusting powders and ointments of the known kind — if they fail, the qalqandiyun ⁦(as printed)⁩. It has been said that a seed of the known kind heals the nose's piles, and that a juice of the known kind, a tuft of wool made to drink of it and put into the nostrils, removes the superfluous flesh and the cancer ⁦(a 'it has been said' carried as in the book; the names are not carried)⁩. As for the arbiyan: it is best treated by the hand-work, after the fullness has been shaken off body and head; if light, the strong ulcer-drugs of the known kind are used. The drug Galen chose is taken of the known kind, made into long suppositories introduced into the patient's nose and left there, then he is given relief at times by taking them out while the nose and the palate are smeared with the two juices, keeping at this regimen — beneficial for the ulcers and the piles, and among its benefits that its pain is inconsiderable ⁦(attributed to Galen as in the book; the measures and names are not carried)⁩. Galen said they sometimes add a little of the known kind to it. If the arbiyan is cut, among the blood-staunchers is clay wetted with cooled water till it is a thick mud, well chilled, smeared on the nose {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.249} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.250}
