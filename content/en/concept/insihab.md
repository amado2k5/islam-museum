## Definition
Insihab is lexically the reflexive of sahb, to drag. In usul and fiqh it means the conventional extension of an act over successive moments of time. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3473}

## Related terms
- Istishab: lexically keeping one thing company with another; in usul, leaving an established state as it was while nothing changes it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3473}
- Injirar: lexically the same as insihab; in the jurists' usage on patronage (wala'), the transfer of patronage from one patron to another after the first lapses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3473}

## Ruling
- In usul: where a duty has a flexible time, the whole time is time for performance; each part requires either the act or resolve to act, and resolve need not be renewed at each part. Resolve at the start carries through the rest until the time narrows. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3473}
- In fiqh: a worship of several acts is generally begun with one intention, which carries over its acts. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3474}
- Among the Hanafis, an act of worship with several acts carries its intention over all of them. Ibn 'Abidin excepts the tawaf of ifada in hajj, which needs its own basic intention of tawaf because it is independent worship, as well as a part of hajj. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3474}
