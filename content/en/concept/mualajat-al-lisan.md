## Ways of treatment
The tongue's treatment may be with a partner — head or stomach — set right by what is taught in each chapter, or it may be a specific treatment by drinks evacuated through loosening, which are more useful than the emetics, the temperament-changers, the astringents and the dissolving-cutting-mollifying ones whose strength reaches the tongue when drunk; such drinks are best taken after food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.255}
## Forms and the caution
It is also treated with rinses, rubs, gargles, oils held in the mouth, and mouth-held pills made from the drugs of the named powers as needed, best made finely ground; and in using mouth-and-tongue drugs of the kind that harms the throat and the lung care must be taken lest some of their flow trickle down to them (the forms are named without recipes or drug names) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.255}
