## Nature
Some claimed that rawand is the roots of bahman in China, brought from there to the lands; it may be adulterated by being boiled, its watery part taken and the sap dried, then its substance dried afterwards and sold as it is, but then it is dense and more astringent, while the pure is more porous and less astringent, saffron-coloured when chewed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Actions and properties
The substance of its tree is a mixture of the watery and the airy, with a bitter earthy part from the action of the fiery in it; its softness and its astringency likewise come from its earthy part, and the pure is less astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Cosmetics
It benefits melasma and the marks left on the skin when smeared on with vinegar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Swellings and pustules
Hot swellings are poulticed with it together with certain moist substances {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Wounds and ulcers
Smeared on with vinegar it benefits ringworm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Joints
Very beneficial for a fall and a blow, and for sprains when drunk with a fragrant drink (sharab rayhani), and likewise when rubbed with its oil for strain of the muscles, their pains and stretching; and it benefits hernia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Organs of the chest
Beneficial for asthma and spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.663}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Beneficial for the liver and the stomach, their weakness and their pains, and for inner pains and hiccup, and it shrinks the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.663-664}

## Excretory organs
It benefits diarrhoea, colic, dysentery, pain of the liver and the bladder, and loss of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it benefits pains of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Fevers
Beneficial for chronic fevers and those with periods {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}

## Poisons and bites
Beneficial for the bites of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.664}
