## Nature and selection
A tall, well-known tree whose leaf does not fall in autumn and winter but stays green because of its strength; its taste has a slight sharpness and pungency and much bitterness, its astringency more than its bitterness; its heat and sharpness are according to how deep its power sinks, and it carries astringency without sting; it differs from the other warming drugs in that it does not draw {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second; some claimed it very cold and judged that its power is compound, its heat being as much as its astringency shows in the organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Actions and properties
Its leaf and cone are astringent, with a dissolving that dissolves moistures; its cone is stronger in everything than its leaf, with a gluing and a stopping of blood so that it removes putrefaction; it is thought that the smoke of the cone, branches and leaf drives away bugs outright {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.586}.

## Adornment
Cooked with vinegar and lupin and smeared on the nails it removes their marks; its leaf removes vitiligo, and it blackens the hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Its leaf, twigs and cone, when fresh and soft, heal wounds in the hard organs and help the creeping ulcer and erysipelas, especially with barley flour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Joints
Its fresh leaf and cone are good for hernia as a poultice, help erysipelas and the like with barley flour, strengthen the sinews, shrink the hydrocele as a poultice, and strengthen and tighten slackness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Head, ears and teeth
If the cone is pounded fine with milk and made into a wick in the nose it cures excess flesh, and its decoction with vinegar calms toothache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Eyes
It helps swellings of the eye as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Chest and breathing
Its cone is given with wine for spitting of blood, difficulty of breathing, orthopnoea and old cough, and its decoction too is very helpful {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Excretory organs
Its leaf is drunk with boiled grape juice and helps difficulty of urine and the flow of residues to the bladder, and also ulcers of the intestines and belly to which residues flow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Substitutes
Its substitutes are pomegranate rind and red anzarut; the proportions the book gives are left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.
