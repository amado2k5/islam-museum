## Nature
Like a small walnut, slightly elongated; lines come from its end, and other parallel lines oppose them; they cross, and small shining flakes remain. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
The editor's note identifies it as the Israeli olive. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}

## Uses by organ
Organs of digestion: it weakens the stomach, does not suit it, and takes away appetite. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Excretory organs: it helps kidney stone and expels it, taken in hot water; the dose is left out. Some claimed it helps bladder stone, which is not so; it is said to stop bleeding from the anus. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
