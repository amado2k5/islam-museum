## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an describes the angels as God's messengers [[passage/quran-35-1|Fatir 1]], sent down with the spirit from His command [[passage/quran-16-2|al-Nahl 2]], who never disobey [[passage/quran-66-6|al-Tahrim 6]], glorify night and day without flagging [[passage/quran-21-20|al-Anbiya' 20]], each has a known station [[passage/quran-37-164|al-Saffat 164]], and all in heaven and earth and the birds glorify Him [[passage/quran-24-41|al-Nur 41]].

## In creed books
Sunni jurists note that the angels' creation is hidden and not precisely known, some mention they were created of light, and that they are pure and never disobey God {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3} {cite:aqida-tahawiyya}.

## Among the commentators
Ibn Kathir details their attributes, noting they are angels charged with affairs and possess no power of harm or benefit for themselves {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Notes
Some held they were created of light, others that this comes from Isra'iliyyat; the primary view is that they are created of a kind other than humankind, and their creation is not grasped as ours is {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
