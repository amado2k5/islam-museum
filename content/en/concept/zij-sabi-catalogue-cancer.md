## Cancer

The catalogue gives the stars of the figure of al-Saratan, Cancer, in its own section of the zodiac belt, listed in order. Cancer is one of the twelve signs into which the Zij divides the ecliptic. Its positions are in a table not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.257}
