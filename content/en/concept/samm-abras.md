## Nature
It is the wall lizard, also called khalaq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Cosmetics
Pounded and laid on thorns and splinters it draws them out, and on warts and nail-warts it removes them; the dried kind, mixed with oil, is said to grow hair on a scald {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Actions and properties
Its urine and blood are said to be wonderfully helpful in the hernia of children when they are sat in its decoction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Organs of the head
Its liver is said to calm toothache, and its head pounded and put on the decayed places of the teeth calms the pain at once {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.600}.

## Poisons and bites
It is split open and laid on a scorpion's sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.601}.
