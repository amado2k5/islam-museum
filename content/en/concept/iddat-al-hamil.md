## Definition
The waiting period of the pregnant woman is the span in which a pregnant woman holds back from marriage after the parting of the husband by divorce or death, and for the scholars it ends with the delivery of the pregnancy — not by the count of months or cycles. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The root of the chapter
God says in Sura al-Talaq: and the term of those with child is that they deliver their burden — making the pregnant woman's term her delivery, soon or late. It is related that Subay'a al-Aslamiyya delivered nights after her husband's death and the Prophet permitted her to remarry, so the jurists agree that the idda of the divorced pregnant woman ends with the delivery. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The widow
The widow who is not pregnant waits four months and ten days; the pregnant widow's term, for the majority, is the delivery even a day after the death, while Ahmad in one report takes the farther of the two terms — the remainder of the four months and ten if she delivers first, the delivery if she delivers later. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
