## Pisces (the Two Fishes)

The figure is as-Samakatan, the two fishes, in the Latin heading duo pisces. Its stars are listed at the mouth of the front fish, which the Latin also calls the southern one, and then the head, back, belly and tail. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.166}
