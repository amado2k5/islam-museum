## Definition
Imtina' in the lexical sense is refraining from a thing; one may also say of a man that he was strong with his people and could not be overcome. Both senses are kept in the technical use. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3379}

## Ruling
- Refraining from what is forbidden is obligatory: refraining from fornication or wine, and a menstruating woman's refraining from prayer and from touching the Qur'an. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3379}
- Refraining from what is obligatory is forbidden: a fit person who refuses prayer, fasting or pilgrimage, and one who refuses to save a person about to die when he can. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3379}
- Refraining from what is recommended is disliked, such as a sick man who refuses treatment when he can take it; refraining from what is disliked is recommended. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3379}
- Refraining from what is lawful is lawful, such as a woman refusing to enter until her prompt dower is paid, or a seller refusing delivery until he is paid. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3380}
