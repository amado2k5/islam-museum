## Summary
Ibn Sina says the first indication of urine is of the state of the liver, the water passages and the vessels, and through them it points to other diseases {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.184}.

## The primary indication
It should be known that the primary indication of urine is of the state of the liver, the water passages and the vessels, and through them it points to other diseases, above all to the liver and the state of its service. A phrase after this in the scan is garbled and has been left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.184}.
