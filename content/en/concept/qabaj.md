## Nature
Well known, and tayhuj shares its attributes; the editor's footnote says that qabaj is al-hajal {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Actions and properties
Its flesh is the most subtle of meats {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Cosmetics
Its flesh fattens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Respiratory organs
Its flesh cleanses the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
The flesh of qabaj benefits dropsy and benefits the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that their flesh is light, that they bind, and that they add to desire; the pronoun in the book is dual, for qabaj and what is paired with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.660}
