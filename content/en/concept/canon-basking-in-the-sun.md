## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that basking in strong sun, above all with vigorous movement such as walking fast or running, dissolves superfluities powerfully, brings sweat, dissolves swellings of flabbiness and dropsy and helps asthma and chronic cold headache, but that if prolonged it thickens and parches the body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.142}.

## Its benefits
Basking in hot sun, especially in vigorous movement such as hurrying or running, dissolves superfluities strongly and induces sweat, dissolves flabby and dropsical swellings, helps asthma and orthopnoea, dissolves long-standing cold headache and strengthens a brain of cold temperament. If the ground beneath is not wet but the place is dry, it helps pains of the hip, cautery, the pains of leprosy, choking of the blood and the clearing of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.142}.

## Its harm
If a person stays long exposed to the sun it condenses and dries the body, darkens it and acts like cautery on the mouths of the pores, preventing dissolving. Staying still in one place in the sun burns the skin more than moving about in it, and holds back dissolving more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.142}.
