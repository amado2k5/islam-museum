## Nature
The fruit of a large tree in some lands; some found on its tree is fresh, small, knobbed and compact and not perforated, and is called amghafantus because it is fresh, and some is smooth, light and perforated {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.615–616}

## Selection
The best is the unripe, the heavy and the hard, while the yellow soft one is of little power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}

## Actions and properties
Its astringency is strong, it prevents moistures from flowing, and its substance is earthy and cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}

## Cosmetics
Its water and the water in which it has been washed blacken the hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is applied with vinegar to ringworm and removes it, and if its powder is sprinkled on excess soft flesh it shrinks it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}

## Organs of the head
It prevents corrupt moistures from flowing to the tongue and the gums, benefits thrush, especially in children and especially with vinegar, and is useful when put in a remedy for caries of the teeth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}

## Excretory organs
Its powder is sprinkled on water and drunk for ulcers of the intestine and chronic diarrhoea, and likewise when put into food it is good for this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.616}
