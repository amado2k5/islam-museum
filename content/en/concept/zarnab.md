## Nature and selection
Nature: fine round stems, between the thickness of a needle and of a pen, black to yellowish, with little taste and little smell; the little smell it has is citron-like and fragrant. Its power is that of jawzabwa, but a little finer, and it is said to stand in for cinnamon. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.462}
Temperament: hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.462}

## Actions
Actions: it has astringency and dissolves winds. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.462}

## Uses by organ
Head organs: snuffed with water, and with rose oil for cold headache. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.462}
Digestion organs: it helps the cold liver and stomach, a very clear benefit. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.462}
Excretory organs: it binds the belly, as is said. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.462}
