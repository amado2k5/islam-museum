## Nature
Dioscorides describes a plant with leaves like those of the garden nightshade, many branches, small dark flowers, and a seed like millet in a husk shaped like the Syrian carob. It has three or four white roots about a span long, sweet-smelling and warming. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Womb and milk (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that a drink of it cleanses the womb, and that when placed in a pad and drunk it brings milk, as is said. These are recorded as the book's report only; the amount is left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}
