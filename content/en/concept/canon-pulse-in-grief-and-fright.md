## Summary
In the eighteenth chapter Ibn Sina describes the pulse of grief and of sudden and of prolonged fright {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.182}.

## Grief and fright
In grief the heat is stifled and sinks and power weakens, so the pulse must be small, weak, widely spaced and slow. Sudden fright makes the pulse quick, trembling, uneven and irregular, while fright that is drawn out and gradual changes the pulse as worry does {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.182}.
