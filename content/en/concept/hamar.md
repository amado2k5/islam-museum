## Nature
Wild and tame; both are well known. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: the ash of the flesh and liver of the ass with oil is very good for chilblain cracks. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Swellings and pustules: ash of the ass's liver with oil on scrofula. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Ulcers: the book says it cures leprosy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Joints: the one stiff from dryness sits in the broth of its flesh. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Organs of the head: its roasted liver helps epilepsy, and likewise its burned hoof. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
Excretory organs: it is said that its urine helps kidney pains, and the urine of the wild ass breaks stone in the bladder. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.495}
