## Summary
Al-Sha'rawi says Islam ended the ascription to an adopter of a child not born of his loins, and that this ruling came by revelation. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2100}

## The phrase "of your own loins"
He reads the phrase "wives of your sons who are of your own loins" as showing that "sons" was used in pre-Islamic practice for adopted sons, and that Islam ended this. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2100}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2101}

## The story of Zayd and Zaynab
He tells how Zayd ibn Haritha, whom the Prophet had adopted, married Zaynab bint Jahsh, and the marriage did not last. The verses of Surat al-Ahzab were revealed, and he says God is the one who gave the marriage, so that there would be no fault for believers regarding the wives of their adopted sons. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2104}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2105}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p. 2106}
