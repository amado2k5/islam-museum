## Summary
Ibn Sina says the harm of hot foods is made good with oxymel, above all the seed oxymel; cold foods are followed by honey water and its syrup and kammuni; and after thick food the hot-tempered take a strong seed oxymel while the cold-tempered take a little of falafili and pennyroyal {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## Hot foods
The harm of hot foods is made good with oxymel, above all the seed oxymel, the most useful kind if made with sugar; if made with honey, the plain one suffices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## Cold and thick foods
Cold foods are followed by honey water and its syrup and by kammuni; thick food is followed, for the hot-tempered, by a strong seed oxymel, and for the cold-tempered by a little falafili and pennyroyal {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.
