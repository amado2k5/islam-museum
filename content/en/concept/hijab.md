## Overview
The hijab in juristic usage is that which veils the woman; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia gives it a dedicated entry distinguishing its material senses — veiling the 'awra, screening during prayer — from its figurative ones {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The jurists detail the rulings of covering {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}; the encyclopedia's hijab entry names khimar and niqab among related terms {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
