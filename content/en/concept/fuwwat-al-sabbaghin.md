## Nature
It is astringent in taste, of the taste of oak gall {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Actions and properties
It cleanses moderately {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Cosmetics
Applied with vinegar to ringworm it cures it, smeared with vinegar on white vitiligo it cures it too, and it cleanses the skin of every mark {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Joints
Given with the water of qaratin it benefits sciatica and the palsy that comes with an impairment of sensation, and it is given with Chinese rhubarb for a blow or a fall {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its fruit is given with oxymel for swellings of the spleen, and it cleanses the liver and opens the obstructions of both, which is its special property {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.625}

## Excretory organs
It promotes urine strongly, so that it may even make one pass blood, and the one who drinks it must bathe every day, the book says {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Poisons and bites
Its branches with its leaves benefit the bites of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it, taken as a suppository, as promoting the menses and bringing down the foetus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.626}
