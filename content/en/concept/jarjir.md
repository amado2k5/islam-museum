## Nature and selection
Known; there is a wild and a garden kind; the seed used in cooking in place of mustard {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}. The footnote gives two kinds and cites a modern nutrition book, which is not used.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot in the third degree and dry in the first; its moisture is moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}. Actions: it causes flatulence and softens the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.

## Uses by organ
Marks: its water with ox gall for marks of ulcers; its seed or water washes freckles and kalaf {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.
Head: it causes headache, especially when eaten alone; lettuce prevents this harm, and so do chicory and purslane {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.
Chest: it increases milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.
Food: it aids digestion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.
Excretory organs: the wild kind is a diuretic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.
Poisons: eaten and drunk with rayhani wine, it is a remedy for the weasel's poison and others {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.430}.
