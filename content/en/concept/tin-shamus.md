## Nature
Galen says we use what is called the star of Shamus. The editor's footnote identifies it as the earth of the island of Samos. Some think it is the talq, but those who collect drugs say talq reaches the Greek lands from the island of Cyprus. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Properties
Galen says it is like the sealed earth in stopping blood and in other matters, but it is airier than the sealed earth, and lighter, indeed very light; it is gummier and stickier than the sealed earth, and the sealed earth is stronger. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Temperament
It is a gummy, sticky, bitter substance that needs no washing; its cooling is slight and its soothing is great, it is said. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Swellings and pustules
It prevents hot swellings at their start more strongly than the other earths, though it helps; it has no roughness like the sealed earth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Wounds and ulcers
Because of its gumminess it does not help burn ulcers as much as the sealed earth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Joints
It helps the start of gout when applied. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Eye
It helps the bubbles that form on the cornea. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Chest and head
It helps swellings of the breasts and behind the ears. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}

## Excretory organs
It helps the bursting of blood from the womb (a book listing, labelled) and bloody flux. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.504}
