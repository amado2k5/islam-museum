## Summary
"The model of leadership upbringing" is the second model in the making of a leader for [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]], coming after [[concept/istikshaf-al-qada|discovery]] and before training; he set it out in episodes 14 to 18 of his programme [[work/asrar-al-qiyada-al-nabawiyya-program|"Secrets of Prophetic Leadership"]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:01:30} For him it is the way to move a person from ordinary to effective; moving him from effective to leader is the work of training. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:04:30} It has five elements, each with five parts that he calls "quintets": convictions, interests, skills, relationships and role models. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:03:00} Upbringing, he says, is not checking homework, food, sleep and clothes, but changing convictions, adjusting interests, developing skills, building relationships and choosing role models. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:02:30}

## Convictions
A conviction for him is what stands behind every behaviour and decision; negative convictions show in words like "impossible" and "I can't". Adults' information and skills change easily, he holds, but their convictions only with difficulty, unlike children's. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:08:00} Convictions have five parts: creed, principles, values, understanding of life, and ambitions. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:10:30} He defines creed as all the firm rational and emotional convictions that a religion or idea plants in its followers, and says the early Muslim victories were victories of creed, with some thirty thousand bringing down the Persian state, in his account. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:14:30} Principles are settled convictions that become a method governing conduct; his example is the Prophet at Ta'if, who, offered that the two mountains of Mecca be brought down on its people, refused, hoping that from their descendants would come people who worship God. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:25:30} For values he cites Stephen Covey's *The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People*. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:30:30} Understanding of life is awareness of what is happening locally and globally "without illusions". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:37:30} Ambition is a distant, large goal; his example is the Prophet's announcement at the Trench of the opening of Syria, Persia and Yemen. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:44:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep14 00:46:00}

## Interests
Interests are what occupies a person's mind, especially in free time, and they show in how he spends his weekend; what is needed is balance between them, because leadership is exhausting. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:04:30} They are five: spirit, mind, emotion, body and money. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:06:30} For the spirit he cites the Prophet's standing in night prayer until his feet swelled; for the mind, that reason is the basis of moral responsibility; and he counts blind imitation as the greatest obstacle to thinking. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:18:30} He defines emotion as a psychological readiness that leads to feelings and behaviour, and holds that a leader must keep his personal feelings out of his decisions, though without feeling he will sacrifice people carelessly. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:29:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:35:30} The body, in Islamic thought as he puts it, is a trust, not a possession. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep15 00:46:00}

## Skills
A skill for him is "an ability its holder practises with ease and mastery", a definition he says is his own. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:04:00} Skills are of five kinds: mental, physical, social, technical or special, and leadership-administrative. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:07:30} For memory he cites al-Shayma' bint al-Harith, the Prophet's foster-sister, who made herself known to him among the captives of Hawazin by a bite he had given her as a child, whereupon he spread his cloak for her. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:11:00} For intelligence, the ruse of [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman]] on the night of the Trench; for planning, the chain of steps that led up to the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|conquest of Mecca]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:20:30} For a special skill, [[person/zayd-ibn-thabit|Zayd ibn Thabit]], who learned the Jews' script at the Prophet's request at fifteen or sixteen. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:40:00} Leadership skills, he says, can be taught to children. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep16 00:46:00}

## Relationships
Relationships have five rules for him: choosing, building, depth, ending and repair. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:40:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:47:00} Choosing goes by temperament, cultural level, social background, hobbies and difference of age, and his example is the Prophet's choice of Abu Bakr as companion; building passes through circles beginning with oneself, then the mother, the father, kin, society, the community and the world. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:20:30} On depth he cites the hadith on moderation in love, and says the Prophet's love for Abu Dharr did not lead him to give him office. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:34:30} On ending, he says relationships are built gradually and ended at once, citing the marriage of Zayd and Zaynab bint Jahsh to show that two good people may still not make a good match, and the story of Barira and Mughith. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:40:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:44:00} On repair, he holds that ties of kinship are kept even when they go bad. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep17 00:47:00}

## Role models
A role model for him is "the applied model of principles and values", someone whose deeds match his words; the Prophet is the model in everything, and each Companion is a model in his own field. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:07:30} Role models have five rules, among which he names: choosing by righteousness, good character, words matching deeds and mastery of the field; close contact with the model and following his books and media; judging the model only within his field; and diversity, since there is no absolute model but the Prophet. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:35:00} One may follow Steve Jobs in creativity, he says, but not in how he treated people. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan asrar-al-qyada-al-nbawya/ep18 00:09:30}
