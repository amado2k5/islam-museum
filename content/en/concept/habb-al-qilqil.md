## Nature
The white kind is larger than safflower, not perfectly round, and breaks into an oily, pleasant kernel. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}
Some say it is wild pomegranate seed. The editor's note identifies it as pomegranate seed. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}

## Actions
The roasted kind is lighter. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}

## Uses by organ
Joints: it strengthens slack bodies. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}
Adornment: it fattens. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}
Organs of the head: it causes headache, especially when taken with aged wine. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}
Organs of digestion: too much of it causes repletion and indigestion; eaten with sugar candy, sugar and honey it digests better; the roasted kind is better; the small kind stings the stomach strongly. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.491}
