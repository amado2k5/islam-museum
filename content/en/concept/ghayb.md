## Overview
Al-ghayb in the creed is what lies beyond sense and perception of what God has informed; the Sharh al-Tahawiyya treats the unseen matters and warns against transgressing Qur'an and hadith in them {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.

## In the sources
The Sharh notes that the exposition of unseen matters is clear in the Book and Sunna, and that exceeding them is where the warning lies {cite:aqida-tahawiyya}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)
The Sheikh says the surah sets out nine scenes a person can see, so as to show the power of God, who made everything in the world of the witnessed, to create the hereafter for him in the world of the unseen {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:14:30}. He says that Paradise and the Fire are unseen as far as we are concerned, while for God nothing is unseen {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:14:30}. He holds that the unseen is unseen only to the one who is veiled, and he cites a line by a poet to the effect that the hearts of those who know God have eyes that see what onlookers do not (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:15:00}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh relates that [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik]] saw the angel of death in a dream and asked when he would take his soul, and the angel pointed with five fingers; Malik asked [[person/muhammad-ibn-sirin|Ibn Sirin]] for the interpretation, and he said these were the five things only God knows: the knowledge of the Hour, the sending down of rain, what is in the wombs, what a soul will earn tomorrow, and in what land it will die (needs_source: the story as the Sheikh tells it; a meeting of Malik with Ibn Sirin is not established). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:07:00} He digresses to criticise the number of dream interpreters today, saying there were five or six up to the third century and now seventy in every alley, because the community is asleep and its state has turned into dreams; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep26 00:06:00}
