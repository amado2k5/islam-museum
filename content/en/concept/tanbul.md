## Nature
The leaves of a tree that grows in India and in a place called al-Nagr, its leaf like the lemon leaf and its branches likewise; the people of India take it with nura and fawfal, chewing stains the teeth red and it has a pleasant scent, and they love to take it, keep to it most of their time and take pride in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.690}

## Cosmetics
It sweetens the breath, removes mouth odour and reddens the teeth; the juice of its leaf with a drink is said to clear bahaq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.690}

## Organs of the head
It strengthens the gums and firms them, and the people of India chew it for this always {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.690}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the mouth of the stomach and digestion, breaks winds and sweetens belching, which is why the people of India chew it always {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.690}
