## Nature
It is said that shirzq is the "waraq" of its milk, and it is also said, of its urine; the word waraq is clear on the printed page and is left untranslated here because its sense in this place is not settled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Temperament
In shirzq there is a cleansing of strong heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Ocular organs
Its brain with honey benefits the beginning of cataract in the eye, its ash sharpens sight, and shirzq benefits pterygium and whiteness; a report attributed to the book, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that the oil of the bat prevents the breasts of virgins from growing large and prevents the growth of hair, as is said, and then says that it is not correct; a report attributed to the book together with its rejection, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.711}
