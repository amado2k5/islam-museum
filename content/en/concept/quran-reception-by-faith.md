## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the Quran's gift is equal for all people, but each takes from it according to faith and how he receives it. He cites [[passage/quran-47-16|Q. 47:16]], on those who listen and then ask those given knowledge what was just said, and [[passage/quran-41-44|Q. 41:44]], on the Quran as guidance and healing for believers and as deafness in the ears of those who do not believe. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.28} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.29}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's commentary and is attributed to him. Other readings of these verses are not yet presented.
