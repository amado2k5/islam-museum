## Nature and selection
It is what turns to ash of stony and ceramic bodies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Temperament
The one that water has not reached, and the one just reached by water, both burn; if the slaked lime stands for a time it then no longer burns but only warms, and the washed is moderate and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Actions and properties
It cuts the flow of blood; the washed kind dries without sting, and lime boiled with ointments becomes ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It eats excess flesh; the washed kind heals and helps fire burns greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.580}.
