## Overview
The science of computing star positions, the zodiac sphere and the stations and motions of the planets. The Sabian Zij defines its subject as the computation of the stars, the zodiac sphere and the stations of the planets and the like, covering the sphere's graduation, chord computations, inclination, rising-times, latitudes, shadow, altitude and distances {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.1} {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.5}.
