## Nature
A herb of bitter taste {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Temperament
Arbiyasiyus said that its heating and drying are by two powers, and another said that it is hot in the second degree and dry in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Actions and properties
Opening, it cleanses, removes, dissolves and cuts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Organs of the head
Its juice is for chronic ear pain; it cleanses and opens the passages of hearing and removes the old pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Ocular organs
Its juice with honey is for sharpening the sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Organs of the chest
It cleanses the chest and the lung by expectoration {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It greatly opens the obstructions of the liver and spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it brings down the menses and cleanses the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}

## Poisons and bites
With salt it is a poultice for the bite of a rabid dog {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.630}
