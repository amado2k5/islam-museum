## Summary
Al-Battani says he observed in the city of al-Raqqa, and that one of his autumn observations, the one he relies on and trusts for its soundness since it was made with the instrument, found the Sun crossing the autumn equinox point in the year one thousand one hundred and ninety-four of the years of Dhu'l-Qarnayn, on the nineteenth of Aylul in the Roman months, which is the eighth of Bakhun in the Coptic, about four hours and a half and a quarter hour before sunrise {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.67}.

## The difference between Alexandria and al-Raqqa
He adds that the meridian of Alexandria precedes that of al-Raqqa by about two-fifths of a mean hour, and corrects the interval between his observation and Ptolemy's by it. The year is given in the text by the era of Dhu'l-Qarnayn only and is read as shown, with the caution that numerals in the printed text are disturbed {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.67}. The observing place was already met in [[concept/zij-sabi-battani-obliquity-observation-at-raqqa|the obliquity observation at al-Raqqa]].
