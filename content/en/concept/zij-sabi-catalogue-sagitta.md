## Sagitta (an-nasl)

The catalogue gives the arrow the Greek name bistos and the Arabic an-nasl, the point of the arrow. It lists a solitary star in the shaft and one at the tip, and the section comes just before Aquila. The positions in the table are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.152}
