## Summary
The seventh pair arises from the boundary between brain and spinal cord, and most of it spreads in the muscles of the tongue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.81}.

## Its course
Most of it goes spread through the muscles that move the tongue and those shared between the thyroid cartilage and the hyoid, and the rest may happen to spread through other neighbouring muscles, though not always. Since the other nerves were given other duties, and it was not fitting to multiply holes in front or below, it was best that the movement of the tongue should come by a nerve from this place, its sense having come from another {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.81}.
