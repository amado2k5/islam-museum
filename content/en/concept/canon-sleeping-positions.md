## Summary
Ibn Sina says the best way to sleep is to begin on the right and then turn to the left, that beginning on the belly helps digestion, and that lying on the back is poor, preparing for apoplexy, paralysis and nightmare, being the habit of the weak among the sick because of the weakness of their muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.

## Right, left and belly
The best manner of sleeping is to begin on the right and then turn to the left, which Ibn Sina calls fitting in medicine and in the law alike. Beginning on the belly gives good help to digestion, since it confines the innate heat and holds it in, so that it increases {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.

## Lying on the back
Lying on the back is a poor sleep that prepares for grave diseases such as apoplexy, paralysis and nightmare, because it tilts the superfluities backward and holds them from their channels, which lie to the front, such as the nostrils and the palate. Sleeping on the back is the habit of the weak among the sick, because of the weakness that comes to their muscles: one side cannot bear the other, and they hasten to lie on the back; likewise they sleep with the mouth open for weakness of the muscles that close the jaws. For this there are two chapters already given in the particular books {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.241}.
