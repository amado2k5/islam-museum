## The sign of extension absolutely
The signs of extension absolutely are that the organ does not answer to contraction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.153}

## Forward tetanus
Forward tetanus shows the man as though strangled, face and eye suffused; it may be imagined he is smiling for the stretching of his face muscles, his head drawn forward and thrust out with the neck full, unable to turn; sometimes he cannot urinate for the stretching of the belly muscles and the weakness of the expeller; sometimes he urinates without will, for his bladder muscle is stretched not contracted; sometimes he urinates blood for the bursting of the vessels from the hardness of the compression; hiccup may befall him {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.153}

## Backward tetanus
In backward tetanus head, shoulders and muscle are found drawn backward, befalling for the belly muscles' extension backward by sharing, and the stretching of the muscle of the seat; he cannot hold what is in the straight intestine nor bring down what is in the small intestines; the two share suffocation, sleeplessness and pain; the signs of the moist, dry, swelling-kind and that from injury are as said under convulsion, and colic often strikes them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.153}

## What precedes it, and a witnessed case
Tetanus is often preceded by twitching of the body, its heaviness, heaviness of speech, hardness in the muscles and in the nape's side down to the tail-bone, and difficulty swallowing; when general tetanus begins the mouth locks, the face reddens, pain grows severe, he cannot swallow what he gulps, the blinking multiplies and the eye waters. Ibn Sina saw a woman in whom general tetanus began: her mouth locked, her face yellowed, her teeth ground, then after a long time her face greened, she could not open her mouth and stayed a long while stretched out lying, unable to turn; then the tetanus loosened from her, she turned to her sides, spoke and slept till morning (a witnessed case carried in the book's own words, not our testimony) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.152}
