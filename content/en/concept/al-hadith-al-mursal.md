## Overview
The second kind of drop in [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar]]'s [[work/nuzhat-al-nazar|*Nuzhat al-Nazar*]] is the loss of the link at the end of the chain, after the Successor: this is the *mursal*. Its form is a Successor, senior or junior, saying: "The Messenger of God said such-and-such", or "did such-and-such", or "such-and-such was done in his presence". {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 78}

## Why it counts among rejected reports
The *mursal* is listed among rejected reports because the omitted narrator is unknown. He might be a Companion or a Successor; if a Successor, he might be weak or trustworthy; if trustworthy, he might have taken it from a Companion or from another Successor; and so the possibilities multiply. In theory they are endless; in practice, by survey, they reach six or seven, the most found of Successors transmitting from one another. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 78}

## The dispute over using it as proof
If the Successor is known to omit only trustworthy narrators, scholars differed: {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 78-79}

:::perspectives
- **Sunni: most hadith scholars, and one of Ahmad's two views**: judgement is suspended, since the uncertainty remains. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 78}
- **Sunni: the Malikis and the Kufans, and Ahmad's second view**: it is accepted outright. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 78}
- **Sunni: al-Shafi'i**: it is accepted if supported by its coming through another route distinct from the first, with a full chain or loosely attributed, making it likelier that the omitted narrator was in fact trustworthy. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 78-79}
:::

Abu Bakr al-Razi of the Hanafis and Abu al-Walid al-Baji of the Malikis report agreement that if a narrator omits both trustworthy and other narrators, his *mursal* reports are not accepted. {cite:nuzhat-al-nazar-itr-bushra-2011 p. 79}
