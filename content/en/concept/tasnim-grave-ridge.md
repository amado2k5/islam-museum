## Summary
Tasnim is raising a grave above the ground by about a span or a little more; its opposite is tasteeh, making the grave flat and level. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5113} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5114}

## General ruling
All agree that raising the earth over a grave by about a span is recommended, so that the grave is known, avoided and prayed for. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5113} The Hanafis, Malikis and Hanbalis recommend the ridge and dislike leveling; the Shafi'is recommend leveling and hold it better. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5114}

## Where it is discussed
If a Muslim dies in a land outside the abode of Islam and cannot be moved, the better course is to level and conceal the grave, for fear it will be dug up. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5115}
