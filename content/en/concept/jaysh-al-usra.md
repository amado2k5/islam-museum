## Summary
The name given by Ibn Hisham for the army of the Tabuk expedition, in a report of Uthman ibn Affan's spending a thousand dinars on it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In Ibn Ishaq's Sira
- Ibn Hisham said: one whom I trust related to me that Uthman ibn Affan spent a thousand dinars on the Army of Hardship in the Tabuk expedition. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}
- Ibn Ishaq described the expedition's time as a time of hardship for the people, fierce heat, and drought in the lands. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/159}
