## Summary
Ibn Sina states that the strongest sands for drawing moisture from the skin are the sands of the seas, which may be sat on while hot, buried in, or scattered over the body a little at a time, dissolving the pains and diseases named under the sun, and that on the whole sand dries the body severely {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.142-143}.

## Use
The strongest sands in drawing moisture out of the areas of the skin are sea sands. A person may sit on them while hot, or be buried in them, or have them sprinkled over the body little by little, and they dissolve the pains and diseases listed in the section on the sun. In sum, sand dries the body severely {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.142-143}.
