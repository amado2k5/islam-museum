## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that after a strong purgative it is better to sleep before it acts, so that it works better, while after a weak one one should not sleep, since nature digests it, and that once it begins to act one should neither sleep nor move but stay still so that nature takes hold of it and works on it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.279-280}.

## Sleep and movement
When someone has drunk a purgative, if the drug is strong it is better for him to sleep on it before it acts, for then it works better, but if it is weak he should not sleep, since nature digests the drug. Once the drug begins to act it is better not to sleep in any case, and he should not move right after drinking it but remain still so that nature surrounds it and works on it, for unless nature works on the drug the drug will not work on nature {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.279-280}.
