## Summary
Sha'rawi separates provision, a gift of Lordship that believer and disbeliever both receive, from the imamate and the values of the way, a gift of divinity that only believers receive. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.582} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.583}

## Abraham's prayer for the land
Sha'rawi reads Abraham's prayer for the land's safety as a request that the blessing last, since God had already made the House a place of security. He says that «make this a land» asks for two things: that the place become a known settlement, and that it be safe. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.581} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.582}

## Provision for those whom God called to life
When Abraham asked provision for «those among them who believe», Sha'rawi says God corrected him: the gift of divinity is not the same as the gift of Lordship. Leadership is not gained by wrongdoing, while provision is given to all whom God called into life, believer or disbeliever, as the sun and the air are. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.583}

## Little enjoyment
Sha'rawi holds that however varied the enjoyments of this world, they are few. He says the disbeliever is driven to the punishment of the Fire in the next life not by his own choice, when his limbs bear witness against him. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.584}

## Limits of this block
These are Sha'rawi's views alone, from these pages. The exegetes' accounts of provision for disbelievers and the limits of enjoyment, the academic view and other views are not yet entered.
