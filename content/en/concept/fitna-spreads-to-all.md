## Overview
'Arjun holds that God's punishment of the corrupt may engulf the innocent when corruption spreads — men being distinguished on Resurrection Day by their intentions {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 50}. He who accepts evil shares its doing; abandoning the forbidding of wrong makes the silent partners in the curse {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 51} — the book qualifying that salvation belongs to those who forbid the evil {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar}.
