## Summary
In the Sheikh's lessons, forbearance is first a quality of God: He is forbearing and does not hasten punishment even for criminals. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:49:30} He urges the believer to be forbearing with people, and gives as an example an Arab who stayed calm when he was provoked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:00}

## Forbearance as a quality of God
The Sheikh says that God is forbearing and does not hasten punishment even for criminals. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:49:30}

## Forbearance with people
He urges the believer to be forbearing with people. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:49:30}
He recalls that the Prophet praised a man of Abd al-Qays for forbearance and deliberation. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:32:30}

## The wager story
He tells of people who wagered that they could provoke an Arab man: they slaughtered a sheep and skinned it before him, and he stayed still (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:43:00}
He says that they offered him a hundred camels and then named sums reaching thousands, and that the man was not provoked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:44:30}
He says that favour belongs to God and not to oneself (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:44:00}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

The Sheikh recounts that God describes what the idolaters said of the Prophet, calling him mad, a sorcerer, a soothsayer and a poet, and that the Prophet bore it patiently and did not answer in kind {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep06 00:40:00}. The Sheikh holds, as his own opinion, that people need softness of heart, that harshness towards a father, a brother or a neighbour gains nothing, and that a harsh neighbour hides good and spreads evil {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep09 00:34:30}.

He recounts that Umar ibn al-Khattab kept silent towards Abbas when Abbas angered him, and that later Abbas gave his house to the House of God (needs_source: the details of the episode) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep09 00:54:00}. He says that if a neighbour does not refrain or accept counsel, one should bear with him and advise him gently {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep09 01:14:00}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy relates that a man insulted Abu Bakr in the Prophet's presence; Abu Bakr kept silent once and twice, and when he answered back the third time the Prophet rose, saying that an angel had been answering for him, but when he answered himself the devil came, and the Prophet does not sit in a gathering the devil attends. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:25:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:26:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh notes that the glad tidings of Isma'il were of a 'forbearing' (halim) boy and those of Ishaq of a 'knowing' ('alim) boy, saying the first is the forefather of the Arabs and the second of the Jews; he holds that knowledge is not all good, since knowledge without morals becomes destruction, giving examples: drones wiping out villages, drug trials on the poor in Africa, and great powers dumping surplus food in the sea; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:04:00} He mentions that forbearance is an attribute of God, and that the Qur'an ascribes it to Ibrahim, Isma'il and Shu'ayb. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep08 00:05:00}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ahl al-Janna'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] gives the sixteenth episode of his programme [[work/ahl-al-jannah|'Ahl al-Janna']] to the forbearing and the hot-tempered: he recalls the Prophet's prayer that God enrich him with knowledge and adorn him with forbearance, and says forbearance is the adornment of character. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep16 00:03:30} He makes [[person/ibrahim|Abraham]] the forbearing person's neighbour in Paradise, recalling from Surat Maryam that his father threatened to stone him and drove him out. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep16 00:04:00} He explains that 'aql in Arabic means a tether, so one who loses his reason in anger falls from the rank of a human being. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep16 00:07:30} He quotes psychologists as saying that temper has recurring triggers that take a person out of his calm. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep16 00:13:30}
