## Summary
In the Sheikh's lessons, forbearance is first a quality of God: He is forbearing and does not hasten punishment even for criminals. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:49:30} He urges the believer to be forbearing with people, and gives as an example an Arab who stayed calm when he was provoked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:00}

## Forbearance as a quality of God
The Sheikh says that God is forbearing and does not hasten punishment even for criminals. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:49:30}

## Forbearance with people
He urges the believer to be forbearing with people. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:49:30}
He recalls that the Prophet praised a man of Abd al-Qays for forbearance and deliberation. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:32:30}

## The wager story
He tells of people who wagered that they could provoke an Arab man: they slaughtered a sheep and skinned it before him, and he stayed still (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:43:00}
He says that they offered him a hundred camels and then named sums reaching thousands, and that the man was not provoked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:44:30}
He says that favour belongs to God and not to oneself (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep05 00:44:00}
