## Overview
Philosophy in Islamic civilisation. The data here present two examples from Zirikli's *al-A'lam*: Ibn Bajja and Ibn Rushd. {cite:al-aalam}

## From al-A'lam
Zirikli mentions Ibn Bajja among the philosophers of Islam, and as one ascribed to the school of the sages. {cite:al-aalam p.868}

Ibn Rushd devoted himself to the words of Aristotle, translated him into Arabic, and wrote about fifty books on him. {cite:al-aalam p.849}

The subject needs a further source to define philosophy and its history.
