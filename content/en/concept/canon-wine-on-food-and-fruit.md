## Summary
Ibn Sina holds drinking wine on any food to be poor and puts it off until the food is digested and has descended, singling out food of bad chyme and fruit, melon above all, while allowing two or three cups to the accustomed and after bloodletting for the healthy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## After digestion
Taking wine on any food is poor, so it should be drunk only after the food is digested and has gone down. With food of bad chyme, wine drunk while eating it or after it is digested is poor, because it drives the bad chyme to the far parts of the body; the same holds for fruit, melon above all {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.

## The cups
Beginning with the small cups is better than with the large, but if two or three cups are drunk on food they do the accustomed no harm, and likewise after bloodletting in the healthy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.237}.
