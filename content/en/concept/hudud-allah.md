## Summary
Al-Sha'rawi reads the "limits of God" in inheritance, marriage and other rulings as separators that stop one right from merging with another, or with wrong. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2036}

## Ownership limit and neighbourly limit
He separates a limit that must not be crossed in ownership from a limit set to prevent harm to a neighbour, such as leaving a gap in one's land so that water does not reach a neighbour's field. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2037}

## Command and prohibition
He says that "these are the limits of God, do not transgress them" concerns commands, and "do not approach them" concerns prohibitions, so a prohibition is kept by staying away from its occasions. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2037}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2038}
