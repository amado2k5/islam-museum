## Its cause and treatment
The ear's fleeing from great sounds: the cause is a weakness in the psychic power in the brain or in what overflows to hearing; the brain must be treated with what strengthens it, as has been taught ⁦(with this Fann 4 ends and Fann 5 on the states of the nose follows)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.233}
