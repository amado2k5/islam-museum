## Summary
Ibn Sina says some humours answer vomiting quickly, as bile does, others resist it, as black bile does, and others go either way, as phlegm does, that for a feverish person purging is sounder than vomiting, and that vomiting is impossible for those whose humour runs downward, as with the lientery {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.278}.

## How the humours differ
Some humours, such as bile, mostly respond quickly to vomiting; some, such as black bile, resist it; and some, such as phlegm, are sometimes one and sometimes the other. For a feverish person purging is sounder than vomiting, and for those whose humour is running downward, such as sufferers of lientery, vomiting cannot be done {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.278}.
