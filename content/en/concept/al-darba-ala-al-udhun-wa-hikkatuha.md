## The ailment befalling the ear and the blow
Hippocrates holds it should be treated with nothing; those after him treat it by taking the known and making smears of it ⁦(the view is transmitted as attributed to Hippocrates; drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.230}
## The ear's itch
For the ear's itch the known is taken and poured in with some oils, or boiled in oil and dropped ⁦(drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.230}
