## Digits and fall
The absolute half-sum of the radii is multiplied by itself and the square of the visible latitude is subtracted from it; the root of the remainder gives the absolute minutes of fall. Divided by the Moon's hourly motion they give the hours of fall, which subtracted from the hours of mid-eclipse fix the absolute beginning, and added fix the absolute end. The digits are the difference between the half-sum and the latitude, multiplied by twelve and divided by the Sun's diameter. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.165-166}

## The stay in a solar eclipse
The text states that where there is a stay it is found by the same method as for the lunar eclipse, and that the disturbed numbers are not reproduced. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.166-167}
