## Summary
The epic signs are mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the great events of the last days among trials and wars — a sign of the Hour, including events beyond imagination, which God decrees. They include events beyond imagination: the blowing of the Trumpet, the emergence of people from their graves, their gathering to the Gathering Place, and their presentation for reckoning.
