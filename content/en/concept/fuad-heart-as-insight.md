## Overview
The Qur'an uses fu'ad and qalb (heart) for an inner faculty of insight that sees and understands, through which a person reaches aspects of reality the senses alone do not reach. [[work/reconstruction-religious-thought-islam|Muhammad Iqbal]] places it beside sense-perception in Lecture I and holds that it is a way of dealing with reality, not a special hidden faculty, with no part played by physiological sensation. {cite:iqbal-reconstruction-religious-thought p.11} {cite:iqbal-reconstruction-religious-thought p.12}

## His reading
Iqbal holds that what the fu'ad reports is not false when properly interpreted. This is his reading of the text, not a ruling of the museum. {cite:iqbal-reconstruction-religious-thought p.12}
