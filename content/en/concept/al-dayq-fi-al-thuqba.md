## Its definition and causes

Dayq is the uveal pupil being narrower than usual; if natural it is wholesome, if morbid it is bad, worse than widening, and may lead to occlusion. Its causes: either a parching dryness of the cornea drawing it together so the pupil contracts and narrowing results, or a severe dryness of the egg-white humour diminishing it while the layer helps it to the shrinking and gathering opposed to the state of protrusion; it mostly arises from dryness, and the narrowing of the pupil may also come from a widening of the hollow nerve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.212}

## The treatment

The dry kind is treated by the moisteners — drops, snuffs and poultices of moist juices — and soft fatty foods; at times there is no avoiding something with heat to draw the moist matter to the eye, with successive short rubbing of the head, face and eye to draw, for pure moisteners may also harm, and when attractive collyria are used the moisteners are resumed. The moist kind is treated by the known collyria named under weakness of sight, the water and the khayalat ⁦(the recipes are not reproduced; and the book's author reports that he treated a narrowing that arose after a non-deep corneal ulcer healed, with the clearing things dissolved in stated matters, so it healed and he saw things as before — the book's own report of its author)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.212} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.213}
