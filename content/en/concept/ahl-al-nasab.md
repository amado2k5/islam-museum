## Definition
Ahl in origin means household and kin; a man's ahl are those closest to him and his clan. Nasab means kinship, common through the father, and on another view through both parents. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3645}

## For the jurists
The jurists count nasab as kinship through the father; the rule is the same in all schools, and the phrase "ahl al-nasab" is used by the Hanafis. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3645}
- A bequest to the kin of the testator goes to those who trace descent to him through the father. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3645}
- An endowment to those called after the founder excludes the children of daughters by the Shafi'is and Hanbalis, on the Qur'anic phrase that they are to be called after their fathers (sura al-Ahzab, 33:5). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3645}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3646}
- The Shafi'is include the children of daughters where the founder is a woman, since mention of the descent is for describing the reality. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3646}
- The Malikis include them in one view, and in another include them only if they are named expressly. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3646}
