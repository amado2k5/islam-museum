## Summary
Ijhad in the lexicon is the early delivery of a child, and as a term it is the expulsion of a foetus before its time. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1705} After ensoulment, at about a hundred and twenty days, abortion is forbidden by consensus. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1706} Before ensoulment the jurists differ: some allow it only for an excuse, as the Hanafis do; some allow it at the earliest stage, as one Hanbali view does; and the Shafi'is forbid it once the drop has settled. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1707} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1708}
