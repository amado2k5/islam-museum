## Selection and temperament
The best are bird brains, especially those of mountain birds; among four-footed animals, the camel's brain and then the calf's {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.436}. Temperament: cold and moist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.

## Actions
It generates phlegm and thick humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.

## Uses by organ
Head: the chicken brain helps a membrane nosebleed; a camel brain, dried and given with wine vinegar, helps epilepsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Food: it is heavy in digestion, causes nausea, dulls appetite, must be eaten with spices, and clings to the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Excretory organs: it softens the belly; duck brain is among the remedies for swellings of the anus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Poisons: brains are good for poisons drunk and for animal bites when eaten {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
