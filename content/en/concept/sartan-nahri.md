## Nature and selection
It is an animal hard to digest and rich in nourishment, and cooking with mung beans improves it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.587}.

## Actions and properties
It draws out splinters and thorns, and the sea kind is finer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Adornment
Its ash with cooked honey is good for cracked feet from cold, and the burnt form enters remedies for vitiligo and freckles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves hard swellings when it is laid on them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Chest and breathing
Its flesh helps consumption, especially with the milk of she-asses, and its broth too {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Excretory organs
Its ash with honey is good for fissure of the anus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Poisons and bites
It helps the sting of scorpions and the tarantula as a poultice and as food; its ash with honey is drunk for the bite of a rabid dog; a well-known remedy for the bite of a rabid dog is made from it with gentian, and the way to treat with it is learnt in the chapter on poisons; it is claimed that if it is brought near a scorpion with basil the scorpion dies on the spot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.
