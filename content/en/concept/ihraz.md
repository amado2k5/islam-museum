## Summary
Ihraz in the lexicon is keeping a thing and guarding it from being taken. Al-Nasafi defines it as placing a thing in a safe store. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1763} It is treated under the conditions of theft and in contracts that carry liability, such as deposit. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1764}
