## Overview
The person who calls to prayer from the mosque. Bilal ibn Rabah was the first muezzin in Islam. {cite:wikidata}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam defines the muezzin as a Muslim man who delivers the adhan, the summons to prayer, in Arabic from the minaret five times a day. Nowadays, it says, a recording played through loudspeakers fixed to the minaret often replaces a living caller, giving the call greater reach and volume. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e1569}
