## Overview
Al-Masudi mentions that the cities of the people of Lot were in the land of Jordan and the lands of Palestine, and that they were five cities, of which the seat of the kingdom and the greatest city was Sodom (*Sudum*). {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.428}

## The kings
In Syria there were kings in the lands of Madaba in the region of al-Balqa'. The mark of every king who ruled Sodom was "*bari'*." He says that their names are mentioned in the Torah. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.428}
