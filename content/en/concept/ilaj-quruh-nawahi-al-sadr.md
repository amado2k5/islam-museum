## Ulcers of the chest regions
And if the chest ulcers grow old, it benefits to give the patient a small spoonful of tar once in the morning, or some liquid myrrh with honey. And if there is a heat, and the hot cleansers are feared and the cold ones give no benefit — take a fox's lung and a seed of this kind, gathered in a thickened water: it is the utmost. And kinds of fumigations may be used in this disease, with which it is dried and cleansed in a funnel: of them, arsenic with what is gathered with it; of them, sweet olive leaf, mountain-cow dung, cow-kidney fat, arsenic, goat-kidney fat and sheep fat; of them, arsenic with what is gathered with it in honey and clarified butter; also pine in the dregs of tar; also yellow arsenic in shiraj (lines labelled, carrying animal dung, fats and a lung, the spoonful of tar, and the arsenic lines — the names excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.367}
