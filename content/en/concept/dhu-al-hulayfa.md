## Summary
Dhu al-Hulayfa was a water of the Banu Jushan and then the name of the place; it is the miqat of the people of Medina. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8498}
It lies six miles from Medina and two hundred miles from Mecca less two miles. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8498}

## Prayer there
The Prophet, going out to Mecca, prayed at the mosque of the tree; returning, he prayed at Dhu al-Hulayfa in the valley and spent the night there. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8498}
It is one of the stations for entering the state of ihram for hajj and umra, and it carries the rules of the stations. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8498}
