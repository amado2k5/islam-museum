## Summary
Al-Battani reckons the mean conjunction from the days that have passed in the month and the equated hours from noon at Raqqa to the time of conjunction or opposition by their mean motions. The mean Sun, the mean Moon, the Moon's anomaly and its motion in latitude are then taken from the four tables for that time. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.139}.

## Reckoning by the Coptic date
When the reckoning uses the Coptic date, the years are counted from Dhu al-Qarnayn with the current year, and two hundred and eighty-seven is taken away; the remainder is looked up in the tables, and the Coptic days are counted from Tut. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.139}.
