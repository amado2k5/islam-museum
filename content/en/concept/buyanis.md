## Nature and selection
Most of the time its root is used. It also has a gum and a juice, and the gum is stronger than the juice. It can be mixed with oil and myrrh and a little wine, then beaten until it thickens, and the quality is judged by how well it is thickened {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the third degree and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. It dissolves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: because of its strong drying it scales away diseased bone and cleanses ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Joints: it suits the nerves very well {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Chest: it helps thick humours, as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}. Food organs: it helps hardness of the spleen when applied as it is, or dissolved in hot water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.393}.
