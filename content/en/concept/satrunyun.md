## Nature and selection
Dioscorides said: some people call it trifali, which means three-leaved, because it mostly grows with three leaves inclined toward the ground, like the leaves of sorrel or the flower of the iris in their leaning, though its leaf is smaller than the sorrel leaf and a deeper red, inclining to blood; its stem is thin, about a cubit long, and its flower is like the flower of the white iris; it has a root like the bulb of an onion the size of an apple, red outside and white within like the white of an egg, sweet in taste. Another plant resembles it and bears its name, with a seed like flax seed, and the skin of its root thin and red, white within and sweet in taste; it grows in mountain places that face the sun {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Joints
Drunk with black astringent wine it helps the palsy that bends the head and neck backward, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It is said that the root of this plant, if a man holds it in his hand, moves him to intercourse at once, and drunk with wine it rouses desire like the skink {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.589}.
