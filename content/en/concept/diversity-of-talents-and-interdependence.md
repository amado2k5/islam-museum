## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that God distributed talents among creatures and did not gather them in one person, so that society completes itself and each person needs the talents of others {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1143}.

## Complementarity, not favour
He says this joining is not a favour from the rich to the poor but a necessary coexistence, since each needs what the other produces, and difference of rank is needed for complementarity {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1144}.

## A community with equal talents
He holds that a group whose talents are the same will compete and destroy itself, that people are best when they differ, and that the small peak is carried by the broad base, and the one who reaches the top should be helped to stay there {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1144}.
