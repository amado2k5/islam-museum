## Summary
The first hour is counted from the noon that begins the day; the sixth hour falls at sunset, the twelfth at the change to night, and the count then runs from sunrise to the next noon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.108}.

## Seasonal and equal hours
To turn seasonal hours, which change with the length of the day, into equal hours, each daytime hour is multiplied by the time of a daytime hour, and each night hour by the time of a night hour, and the method of the chapter is then followed. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.108}.
