## Nature
Known. The river kind is aquatic and earthy; the sea kind is more astringent. As for the rock moss, which is the harar al-sakhr, it has been described above. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Temperament
Cold. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Properties
It stops blood wherever it is applied; the sea kind is stronger. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Swellings and redness
It is put on hot swellings and redness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Joints
It helps hot gout and hot joint pain; when boiled in old oil, it softens the sinews. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}

## Excretory organs
It is applied as a poultice to an intestinal rupture to shrink it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.501}
