## Overview
The juz' in masahif usage is one of the thirty divisions into which the masahif is divided; the scholars of Qur'anic sciences record that they divided the masahif into thirty ajza' {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt}.

## In the sources
Tahdhib Manahil al-Irfan records that the reciters divided the masahif into thirty ajza' and marked its parts in the text {cite:manahil-irfan-sabt}.
