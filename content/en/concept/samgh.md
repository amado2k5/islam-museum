## Choice
The best is the clear Arabian with little wood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.639}

## Temperament
All kinds of gums are very hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.639}

## Actions and properties
Astringent and glutinous with drying and strengthening; acacia gum is much stronger, and for that reason it enters theriacs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.639}

## Organs of the chest
It softens hot cough, repels the harm of ulcers of the lung and clears the voice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.639}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.639}
