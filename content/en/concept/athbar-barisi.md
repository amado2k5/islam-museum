## Nature and selection
It is the zirishk, barberry. One kind is round, red and smooth; another is long, black and sandy or from the mountains, and it is the stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. Cold and dry at the end of the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}.

## Temperament and actions
Cold and dry at the end of the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}.

## Uses by organ
Properties: when drunk it strongly suppresses yellow bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. Swellings and pustules: it helps hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. Stomach: it strengthens the stomach and the liver and strongly quenches thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. Excretory organs: it checks, helps excoriation, and when drunk it helps a long-standing discharge from the uterus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. The book states that if a pregnant woman drinks of the root of this plant three times, or has it applied, she expels the foetus; this is the book's listing, not a recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.360}. It helps bleeding from below {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.360}.
