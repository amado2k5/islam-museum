## In hadith and sources
It is narrated that God appointed an angel over Paradise called Ridwan, its keeper, who gives its people tidings of it before they enter {cite:sahih-ibn-hibban} {cite:mustadrak-al-hakim}. Ibn Kathir notes that 'Ridwan' in the Qur'an names divine pleasure, while the hadith keeper is known by this name {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## Among the commentators
Commentators transmit that some held 'Ridwan' to be the keeper's name and others rejected it as a description of divine pleasure, not a proper name {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; it is therefore treated in tafsir more as a divine attribute than a fixed angelic name.

## Notes
Reports differ on the details of his service and how the people of Paradise receive him; later scholars count him among the angels charged with Paradise.
