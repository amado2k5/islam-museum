## Nature
This is a dew, falling mostly in Khurasan and Transoxiana, and falling mostly in our lands on al-hajj {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Choice
The best is the fresh white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Temperament
It is moderate, tending to heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Actions and properties
Softening, good for cleansing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits cough and softens the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It calms thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}

## Excretory organs
It loosens yellow bile gently, its loosening being by a property in it; the book states the amount of a draught, left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.687}
