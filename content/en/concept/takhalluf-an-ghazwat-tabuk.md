## Summary
Ibn Ishaq's classification of those who did not go out with the Prophet to Tabuk: the excuse-makers among the Bedouin; the Weepers who found nothing to carry them; those who stayed back without doubt, like Abu Khaythama; and those who stayed back of the hypocrites and people of suspicion, by his description. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In Ibn Ishaq's Sira
- Ibn Ishaq mentioned classes: the excuse-makers among the Bedouin — whom God did not excuse, in his wording; it was mentioned to him that they were a band of Banu Ghifar. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}
- The seven Weepers who came to the Prophet asking for mounts, and he found nothing to carry them. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}
- The men of the Muslims whose resolve lagged, so they stayed back without doubt or misgiving — among them he names Ka'b ibn Malik, Murara ibn al-Rabi', Hilal ibn Umayya and Abu Khaythama. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}
- Those who stayed back of the hypocrites and people of suspicion — among them Abdullah ibn Abi, by Ibn Ishaq's account. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/162}
