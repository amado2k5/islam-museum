## Uses by organ
Adornment: rubbing it on the breast and the testicle so that they do not grow large. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}
Swellings and pustules: its rubbing is good for hot swellings of the breast. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}
