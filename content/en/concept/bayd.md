## Nature and selection
An ordinary food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}. The best is the fresh egg of the hen; after the hen's egg come the eggs of birds that are like it, such as the partridge and the tadarj. The duck's egg and the like are poor {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}.

## Temperament and actions
The nature is moderate: the white is cold, the yolk is hot, and both are moist, especially the white. The driest are the goose and ostrich eggs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}. There is astringency, especially in the roasted yolk. The white calms sharp pains because it coats them. The best is the one called nimbarsht, which passes quickly through the body, and the more compact kind is slower to digest and gives more food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: the white applied on the skin prevents sunburn and removes it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}. Swellings and pustules: it belongs among the remedies that stop swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.396}. Wounds and ulcers: in injections for ulcers and swellings, and the burn is anointed with oil; it helps wounds of the anus and the groin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. Joints: they soften the nerves and help all pains of the joints {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. Head: it belongs among drugs that stop bleeding of the membrane of the brain, and helps colds. The yolk of hen's egg helps hot swellings in the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. Eyes: its white calms pain of the eye; its yolk with saffron and rose oil helps throbbing of the eye greatly, and with barley flour as a poultice it blocks drops from reaching the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. Chest: it helps roughness of the throat, cough, pleurisy, consumption, hoarseness from heat, shortness of breath and spitting of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. Food organs: the boiled egg, as it is, in vinegar, prevents matter from settling in the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. Excretory organs: its boiled form stops diarrhoea, especially when eaten raw; the roasted form on ash, eaten with astringents and sour grape juice, helps looseness of the bowels, helps roughness of the intestine and bladder, and an injection of its white with crown of kings helps ulcers of the bowels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. The book says all eggs, especially sparrow eggs, increase desire. It is also said that goose egg mixed with oil and dropped warm into the womb brings on the menses after four days. These are the book's statements, recorded as written and not as advice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}.
