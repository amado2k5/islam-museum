## Overview
Tatfif is the shortfall in measure and weight by which the seller betrays, the opposite of full measure, and it is forbidden. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5308}

## Definition
In the lexicon tatfif is short-changing in measure and weight; tawfiya is giving a thing in full, and tatfif is its opposite. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5308}

## Ruling
Tatfif is a kind of betrayal and of eating wealth falsely, with the loss of manliness. God ordered full measure in several verses and pronounced woe upon the short-changers in the surah of the defrauders. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5308} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5309}

## The warnings
In the hadith of the five-for-five: no people break a covenant without God giving their enemy power over them; no people short-change measure without being denied plants and struck by years of drought. Ibn Umar would pass a seller and say: fear God, and give full measure and weight. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5309}

## A major sin
Ibn Hajar reports the scholars' statement that it is among the major sins, and he favours it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5309}

## Prevention and measures
The market inspector must prevent short measure and cutting in measures, scales and weights; he warns the measurers and weighers, and punishes and exposes whoever is caught so that others are deterred. If a dispute leads to denial, the judges have the better right to hear it than the inspectors. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5309} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5310}

## Cross-reference
Tatahhur and tathhir refer to the entry on purity and have no entry of their own. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5310}

## Short measure in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episode 2)
The Sheikh tells of two merchants, one of whom saw the other cheat in weighing, and he describes cheating on the scale between buyer and buyer (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:12:30} He says that whoever tastes fruit in the markets without reckoning should hold himself to account, and that betrayal in small things is among the causes of fear (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:13:00} He says a small sin does not excuse its doer (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:13:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh pauses at the opening of [[passage/quran-83-1|Surat al-Mutaffifin]], holding that the Qur'an's wonders never wear out however often it is read, and that short measure is not only material, in measure and weight, but there is a moral short measure in dealings that is worse. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:02:30} His examples: a brother who keeps ties with his siblings and stands by them in their crises while they give him nothing and treat it as his duty, and a tender sister who meets only refusal from her sister. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:03:30}
For him it is short measure when the mother of a son who does not pray and is an addict seeks a devout girl who knows the Qur'an by heart, and he recalls that [[concept/kafaa|suitability]] in marriage is also a matter of faith. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:04:30} So is a wife who welcomes her own family and frowns at her husband's. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:05:30} So is a father who favours the dutiful child in gifts; he holds that a child's undutifulness does not deprive him of his share of inheritance, and mentions that whoever deprives an heir of his inheritance God will deprive of his inheritance in Paradise (needs_source: the hadith is not referenced in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:06:00} He cites what Yusuf's brothers did when [[person/yaqub|Ya'qub]] showed his love for Yusuf, and holds that a father must treat his children equally and not show his heart's preference. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:07:30} He closes: treat people as you would wish them to treat you. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep04 00:08:00}
