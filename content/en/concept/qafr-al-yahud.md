## Nature
Dioscorides is reported as saying that qafr is found in the land of Ifriqiya, in the city of Sayalun and the city of Aqrish, and also in the land of Sicily; some of it wells out of certain mountains and some floats on the waters of springs, and people use it in the lamp instead of oil. The black and dirty is bad, being adulterated with pitch mixed into it, for when chewed it gives the taste of tar, though it crumbles; it is light black pieces {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Choice
The best is the purple, glossy, strong, heavy; the black and dirty is bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree and dry up to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Actions and properties
Its power is near the power of pitch; it strengthens the organs and, when drunk, dissolves congealed blood in the belly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Cosmetics
It benefits whiteness of the nails, smeared on {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Swellings and pustules
It ripens scrofula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is smeared on ringworm and on the swelling of wounds and benefits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Joints
It is a poultice for gout, and is drunk and smeared for sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits cough and ulcers of the lung, helps spitting and brings pus out of the chest, and benefits swellings of the tonsils and quinsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Excretory organs
When used as an enema with barley water it benefits dysentery {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it benefits hardness of the womb, and that when it or its smoke is carried it benefits protrusion of the womb and its pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}
