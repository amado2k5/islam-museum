## Nature and selection
It is al-hawk, which is well known. Its oil is like the oil of marjoram but weaker, and it has opposing powers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.403}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the first to the second degree, dry at the start of the first, with a surplus moisture that nearly reaches the second degree in moisture, though not in its substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: it binds and loosens; it binds unless it meets a ready surplus, and then it loosens. It warms, opens and causes wind, spoils quickly and makes bad black humour; its seed helps those in whom black bile arises {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Swellings and pustules: with vinegar and rose oil, applied to hot swellings, it helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Head: its juice, taken as a drop, helps nosebleed, especially with vinegar, wine and camphor on a wick; it cures deafness, and eases or stirs sneezing according to temperament {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Eyes: a poultice helps throbbing of the eye; eaten, it dims the sight because of its thick moisture; its juice as a kohl strengthens the sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Breath and chest: strengthens the heart very much and dries the lung and chest; its water is good for spitting blood, and the book says it brings milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Digestion: hard to digest, quick to rot, bad for the stomach, especially the water of its leaf {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Excretory organs: it binds; if it meets a ready humour it loosens; its seed helps difficult urination {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.

Poisons: applied to the stings of wasps, scorpions and sea monsters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.404}.
