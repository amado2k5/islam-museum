## Summary
Ibn Sina adds further bodily signs: a face reddened and swollen with a dusky tinge, if it persists, foretells leprosy; heaviness of the body with distended vessels calls for venesection; irritation spreading over face, eyelids and limbs calls for care of the liver lest dropsy follow; and a fiercely foul stool foretells fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.

## Leprosy and venesection
If the face reddens, swells and turns dusky and this lasts, it foretells leprosy; if the body is heavy and tired and the vessels are distended, one lets blood lest a vessel burst and apoplexy and sudden death follow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.

## Liver, stool and urine
When irritation spreads in face, eyelids and limbs, the state of the liver is to be remedied lest dropsy follow; when the stool is very foul, putrefaction is removed from the vessels lest fevers follow, the urine being a stronger indication still; and when you see exhaustion and aching, suspect a fever to come {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.
