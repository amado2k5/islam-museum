## How it happens
Water may enter the ear if the bather and washer do not protect it; it hurts, swells the ears' roots and gives severe pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.230}
## Its treatments
What helps: drawing it out with a tube in one pull, then pouring in the known; cough and sneeze may expel it; or a known stick is taken, a third of one end wrapped with cotton, dipped in oil, the other end fitted in the ear, the man laid down and the cottoned end lit and left to burn until warmth creeps inside the ear — then it is pulled out at once and what is in the ear comes out with it ⁦(the hand-work is carried as the book's description, not as a procedure)⁩. Also helpful, especially at the start: a palm of water fills the ear, then the man turns over hopping on one leg until it all comes out; it may also be drawn out with the known instruments; for a little of it, pouring oils in the ear and warm milks several times running may suffice, especially if pain remains; if it pains severely the ear is dressed with the known ⁦(drug and dressing names are not carried; the woman's-milk line is a labelled listing for the founder's ruling)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.231}
