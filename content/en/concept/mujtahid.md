## Overview
The mujtahid is one who attains the rank of deriving rulings from their proofs {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia presents the age of ijtihad, the ranks of mujtahids and jurists, and the question of the closing of the gate of ijtihad {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
