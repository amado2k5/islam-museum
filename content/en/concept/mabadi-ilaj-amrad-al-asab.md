## Purifying the nerves
The way of treatment lies in purifying the nerves and changing their temperaments; what most needs to be wholly evacuated from them is the cold materials, their evacuants being the strong drugs (their names are not carried), while among their gentle evacuants are the dry bath and moderate exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.135}

## Changing the temperament by exercise
Exercise after purification is the best changer of its temperament, each organ having its own; one must begin with the gentlest and pass by degrees to what has moderate force {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.135}
