## Nature and selection
The azadaraxt is a known tree with a fruit like the nabq. In Rayy it is called the myrobalan tree and kanar; in Tabaristan it is called batahak. It is a large tree among the big trees {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. A footnote identifies it as zanzalakht or zarin-darakht {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.

## Temperament and actions
Its flower is hot in the third degree and dry at the end of the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}.

## Uses by organ
Its flower opens obstructions {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Ornament: the water of its leaves kills lice and lengthens hair; its roots especially, when used with wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Head: its flower opens the obstructions of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Chest: its fruit is very harmful to the chest and deadly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Stomach: its fruit is bad for the stomach and causes distress {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Fevers: it is said that a decoction of its bark with shahatraj and myrobalan is a strong purge that helps phlegmatic fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.363}. Poisons: the juice of its tips with honey counters all poisons, and its fruit has sometimes killed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Substitutes, for lengthening hair: the leaves of the grape-seed plant, myrtle and lote-tree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}.
