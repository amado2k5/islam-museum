## Cepheus

The catalogue gives this figure under the name al-Mutahhib, which the text identifies as Cepheus. Its stars come after those of the Dragon and before those of the Guardian of the North, in the order of the printed pages. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.247}
