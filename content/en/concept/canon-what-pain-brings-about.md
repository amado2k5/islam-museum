## Summary
In chapter 22 Ibn Sina says pain loosens the power and keeps organs from their proper acts, and may first heat an organ and finally cool it, through what it dissolves and what it defeats of spirit and life {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.

## Loosening the power
Pain loosens the power and keeps organs from the acts proper to them, to the point of stopping a breathing person from breathing, or troubling his action, or making it broken or frequent, in general moving it off its natural course {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.

## Heat, then cold
It may heat the organ at first and chill it in the end, by what it dissolves and by what it defeats of spirit and life {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.151}.
