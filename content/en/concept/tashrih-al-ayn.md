## The seeing spirit and the ice-like humour
The power of vision and the matter of the seeing spirit reach the eye through the two hollow nerves; when the nerve and its attendant membranes descend to the orbit each one's end widens, fills and spreads so as to surround the moistures in the eyeball, of which the central is the ice-like humour — a pure moisture like ice, round, its front flattening short of a sphere; it was flattened so that refraction in it is more ample and small visibles get a full share refracted in it, which is why its rear tapers a little to set well into the bodies taking it up; and it was set in the centre as the safest place {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.161}

## The glass-like and the white-of-egg humours
Behind it is set another moisture coming to it from the brain to nourish it, for between it and pure blood there is a gradation; this moisture resembles molten glass, whose colour is clearness inclining to a little redness — clear because it feeds the clear, slightly red because it is of blood's substance not yet wholly assimilated; it covers the ice-like's rear half to its greatest circle; before the ice-like is a third moisture like white of egg, the white-of-egg humour, like the superfluity of the ice-like's substance — and the clear's superfluity is clear; it was set in front for an antecedent reason and a completing one: the antecedent, that the superfluity's side faces the nourishment's side; the completing, that it stage the light's conveyance onto the ice-like and be its shield {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.161}
