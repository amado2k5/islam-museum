## Overview
Abduh presents the general mission as a pillar of faith: the sending of human messengers to convey beliefs and rulings{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.82}. He links it to the miracle, which he takes as the proof of a claim, possible for reason, given only together with the challenge; and he holds that messengers are human in all matters apart from the conveying of the message{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.83}{cite:abduh-risalat-tawhid-imara-1994 p.84}.
