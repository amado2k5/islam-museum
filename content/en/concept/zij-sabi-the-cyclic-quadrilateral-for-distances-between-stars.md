## Summary
Chapter 26 begins with finding the distances between the stars as they appear in the sphere and the places of one to another, by which the true distance of the Moon from the Sun is known according to the Moon's latitude. It is preceded by a geometrical proof: in every four-sided figure inscribed in a circle, the products of the two pairs of opposite sides, added together, equal the product of one diagonal by the other. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.61}.

## The case of two parallel sides
If two of its sides are parallel the other two are equal, its diagonals are likewise equal, and the product of the diagonals is the product of the two parallel sides plus the product of the two opposite sides. This principle is what the following example rests on. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.61}.
