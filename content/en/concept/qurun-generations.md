## Definition
In the speech of the Arabs the qarn is the people joined in one age, and the word came to be used for the span of time itself; jurists and historians reckoned it at about a hundred years. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}

## The best of generations
In the two Sahihs: the best of generations is mine, then those who follow them, then those who follow them — meaning the excellence of the Companions, then the Followers, then their followers over all later ages. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## Generations in the Qur'an
The Qur'an tells of the destruction of the wrongdoing generations such as 'Ad and Thamud, and that God never destroyed a generation before sending messengers to it. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}

## Generations for the historians
Historians divided time into ages and generations, and al-Biruni in al-Athar al-Baqiya set out the measures of time and the names of the eras among the nations. {cite:athar-baqiya-thaqafa-2008}
