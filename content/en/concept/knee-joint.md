## Summary
The knee joint arises from the entry of the two processes at the end of the thigh bone. They are secured by a winding ligament, a binding ligament in the depth and two strong ligaments at the sides, and the front is fitted by the patella, the eye of the knee {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.59}.

## The patella
The patella is a bone close to round. Its use is to resist what is feared in kneeling and squatting, tearing and dislocation, and to support the joint burdened by moving the body. It was set in front because most of the violent bending that reaches the knee comes forward, in rising, kneeling and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.59}.
