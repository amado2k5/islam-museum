## Overview
Islamization is societies' turning to Islam in belief and institutions; conquest literature records populations' entry into Islam, while academic study describes the process as gradual {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} {cite:bulliet-conversion-1979}.

## Perspectives
- **Classical sources:** al-Baladhuri's Futuh al-Buldan records the garrison towns' treaties, jizya and entry into Islam {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.
- **Academic:** Bulliet argues conversion proceeded gradually over centuries through social networks, not at the moment of conquest {cite:bulliet-conversion-1979}.
