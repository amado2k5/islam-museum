## Increase and decrease
This flesh may enlarge till it blocks sight, and may decrease till it hides and no longer bars the tears; most of it comes at the physician's error in cutting the pterygium. The increase is treated with the pterygium drugs and is not extirpated, lest tearing occur; the decrease caused by cutting has no treatment; if it is from another cause it may perhaps be treated with the flesh-growing drugs in which there is astringency and drying, and wine itself benefits especially when a thing of astringent power is cooked in it ⁦(the named drugs are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.183} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.184}
