## Nature
Dioscorides claimed that azn is a plant with a leaf like the leaf of the small lentil, only longer, with a short stem, a red flower and a small root, growing in idle, neglected places, and this plant is found in some countries {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.623}

## Actions and properties
A poultice of its leaf brings sweat when applied with oil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.623}

## Swellings and pustules
Pounded and applied as a poultice it dissolves abscesses and inflamed pustules {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.623}

## Excretory organs
Drunk with wine it cures dribbling of urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.623}
