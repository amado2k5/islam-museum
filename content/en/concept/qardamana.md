## Nature
A tree that grows in Armenia and the lands called Qum'ayna, and also in the land of India and the land of the Arabs; the qardamana is taken from that plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Choice
The best is what is brought from India and Armenia, what is hard to crush, full and compact; what is contrary to this is rejected; so too what has a diffusing smell and an acrid taste with some bitterness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Actions and properties
Its power is heating and reddening, it has a dissolving power, and its property is strengthening the inner organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Wounds and ulcers
It benefits itch and ringworm, smeared with vinegar {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Joints
It benefits diseases of the nerves and pain of the hip from phlegm, and it benefits palsy and bruising of the muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Organs of the head
It benefits epilepsy, drunk in water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Organs of the chest
It cleanses the chest and soothes the cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Excretory organs
It benefits colic, worms and tapeworm; with wine it is for pain of the kidneys and difficulty of urine, and it is given with the root bark of laurel for the stone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its smoke kills the foetus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Poisons and bites
It benefits the sting of the scorpion and bites of every kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Substitutes
Its substitute is harmal or idhkhir {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}
