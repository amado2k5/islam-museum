## Overview
Shaykh Shaltut presents scrambling for the world as the course opposite tabattul: a man concentrating his powers in serving his private material existence, amassing and hoarding within his own circle, heedless of virtue and of gratitude {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 29}. This material individualism severs the natural bonds between people, plants avarice, and breeds enmity and hatred.
