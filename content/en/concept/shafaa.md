## Summary
"The intercession He has stored for them is true, as related in the reports" {cite:aqida-tahawiyya p. 16} — in the context of his saying that the Basin by which God honoured His prophet, to succour his umma, is true {cite:aqida-tahawiyya p. 16}.

## The people of major sins
"The people of major sins of Muhammad's umma are not in the Fire forever if they died as monotheists, even unrepentant, after meeting God knowing and believing" {cite:aqida-tahawiyya p. 22}. They are under His will and judgement: if He wills He forgives them by pardon and favour; if He wills He punishes them in the Fire by His justice, then brings them out of it "by His mercy and the intercession of the intercessors among the people of His obedience, then sends them to His Paradise" {cite:aqida-tahawiyya p. 23}.

## In Ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi's Lum'at al-I'tiqad
Ibn Qudama says: "Our prophet intercedes for those of his umma's people of major sins who entered the Fire, and they come out by his intercession after they have burned and become embers and cinders"; "the rest of the prophets, the believers and the angels have intercessions"; he cites His word that they intercede only for whom He approves, and "the intercession of the intercessors avails the unbeliever nothing" {cite:lumat-al-itiqad p. 34}.

## In Ibn Taymiyya's al-'Aqida al-Wasitiyya
Ibn Taymiyya says the Prophet has three intercessions: the first, "he intercedes for the people of the Standing until judgement is passed among them"; the second, "he intercedes for the people of Paradise to enter Paradise — and these two intercessions are his alone"; the third, "he intercedes for those who deserve the Fire" — his and the rest of the prophets', the siddiqs' and others': interceding that the deserving not enter it and that entrants be brought out {cite:aqida-wasitiyya p. 20}. "And God brings people out of the Fire without intercession — by His favour and mercy" {cite:aqida-wasitiyya p. 20}.

## In al-Ash'ari's Maqalat al-Islamiyyin
In "the account of the sum of the saying of the people of hadith and sunna" in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari presents what the people of hadith and sunna hold, ending: "And in all we have mentioned of their saying we speak, and to it we go" {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin p. 350}. He relates there that they "affirm the Messenger of God's intercession, and that it is for the people of major sins of his umma" {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin p. 347}; and they believe "that God brings out of the Fire a people of the monotheists, as the reports from the Messenger of God have come" {cite:maqalat-islamiyyin p. 347}.

## What is not covered here
This entry presents the positions of the 'Aqida al-Tahawiyya, Ibn Qudama's Lum'at al-I'tiqad, Ibn Taymiyya's al-Wasitiyya, and what al-Ash'ari related in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin of the people of hadith and sunna and declared his own — each attributed to its book. The sayings of the Maturidis and the remaining Sunni and non-Sunni schools from their own books have not yet been added; the entry needs specialist review before promotion.

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]], explaining the Throne Verse, rejects the pagan idea of intercession in which intercessors impose their will on God, and says it passed from pagans to some of the People of the Book. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep03 00:20:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep03 00:21:00} He notes that the Mu'tazila of old and, in our time, Dr Mustafa Mahmoud denied intercession in the hereafter as favouritism, and holds that alongside justice God has favour and mercy. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep03 00:21:30}

## In Ahmed Abdussalam's programme 'Osalli Alayh'
In his programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']], [[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]] ties intercession to invoking blessings on the Prophet, citing the hadith that those nearest the Prophet on the Day of Resurrection are those who invoke blessings on him most, which he reads as nearest to his intercession and to his heart's love, on the day a man flees from his brother, mother and father. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:08:00} In the fifteenth episode he relates the hadith of the great intercession: people go from prophet to prophet, each declining, until they come to the Prophet, who accepts and prostrates beneath the Throne, and is told to raise his head, ask and be given, intercede and have his intercession accepted; he notes that the Prophet asked not for his daughter, his uncle or his aunt, but for his community. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep15 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep15 00:01:30}

In his commentary on [[passage/quran-2-48|verse 48]], al-Tabari holds that the verse is general in wording but particular in meaning: the denial that intercession is accepted refers to one who died in disbelief without repenting, while the reports from the Prophet affirm his intercession for the people of major sins among his community. This is his own view. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 32} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 33}
