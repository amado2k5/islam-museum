## Summary
In his commentary on the verse about the covenant of leadership ([[passage/quran-2-124|Al Baqara 124]]), Sha'rawi asks who inherits religious leadership: is it passed by descent and offspring, or by applying the law? He holds that the inheritance here is an inheritance of deeds {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1529}.

## Abraham's request and its answer
Sha'rawi says Abraham fulfilled his duty completely, and God made him a leader for the people. Abraham then asked that leadership remain among his offspring. The answer was that the covenant does not reach the wrongdoers among them, because leadership is not inherited the way property is. It passes to whoever applies the law completely {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1530}.

## Salman and the household
Sha'rawi cites the Prophet's saying to Salman al-Farisi that Salman is "of us, the household". He reads it as the Prophet placing Salman within the inheritance of prophethood, which is the application of the law, rather than within a nation or lineage {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1530}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1531}. He says the report appears in al-Hakim's Mustadrak and al-Tabarani's Mu'jam al-Kabir; those references have not yet been checked against the originals (needs_source).

## Noah's son
Sha'rawi notes that Noah asked for his son to be saved, and was told that the son was not of his family because his deeds were not righteous. God did not say that he was not a righteous doer, but attributed the matter to deeds. From this he concludes that belonging to a prophet is by following his law {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1531}.
