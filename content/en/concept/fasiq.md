## Overview
Fasiq in the legal-religious terminology is one who departs from obedience through a grave sin or through persistence; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats fisq and its effects in the chapters on testimony, prayer leadership and interdiction {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia's discussions include praying behind a fasiq, the effect of an imam's fisq on his authority, and interdicting a fasiq {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
