## Nature and selection
Its kinds: the finest is the white, called sparrow dung; the sharpest is the green {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}.

## Temperament and actions
It cleanses, scours and opens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: a small measured weight of it relieves difficult urination. The book states that a larger measure expels the foetus, alive or dead, and brings on menses, with measures given; this is the book's listing, not a recommendation. It also helps the watery kind of dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}. Poisons: the book says a large measure is a deadly poison, and the smoke of the green kind drives vermin away {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.361}.
