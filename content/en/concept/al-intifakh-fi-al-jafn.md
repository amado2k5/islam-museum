## Its definition and kinds

Intifakh is a cold swelling with itching; the dominating element may be wind, or a thin phlegmy residue, or a watery residue, or a melancholic residue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.198}

## Signs and treatment

The windy kind comes on suddenly and extends toward the canthus like a fly's bite, befalling in summer and in the elderly, without heaviness. The phlegmy is colder and heavier and keeps the mark of pressing for an hour. The watery keeps no mark of pressing and has no pain. The melancholic spreads over lid and eye with hardness and stretching reaching the brows and cheeks, no pain worth mention, a dusky colour, and mostly follows ophthalmia. Treatment begins with evacuating the body and cleansing the head, then using what suits the dominant kind ⁦(the names are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.198} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.199}
