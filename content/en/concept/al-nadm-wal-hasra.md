## Overview
Regret and remorse are scenes of the Day: the Qur'an names it the day of remorse [[passage/quran-19-39|the warning verse of the day of remorse]] {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In exegesis
The exegetes mark the places of regret: the wrongdoer biting his hands on the Day [[passage/quran-25-27|the hand-biting verse]], the disbeliever wishing he were dust [[passage/quran-78-40|the wishing verse]], and the hands and feet telling what they did {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.
