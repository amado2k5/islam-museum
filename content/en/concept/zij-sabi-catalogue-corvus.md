## Corvus (al-Ghurab)

The catalogue names the figure al-Ghurab, the crow. One of its stars lies in the beak, which the list places near the Hydra, called ash-Shuja', the serpent. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.172}
