## Nature
It grows in the land of India {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Choice
The best is what is of ruby colour with close knots, which when crushed breaks into many splinters, its tube full of something leaning to white like a spider's web, viscous when chewed, astringent, with some acridity, and its powder fragrant, leaning to yellow and white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Actions and properties
Thinning, with a little astringency along with its acridity; its substance has an earthy and an airy part well mixed toward moderation, its drying is the greater, and it has a fine substance as in all the spices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Cosmetics
It benefits the darkness of dead blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Joints
It benefits crushing of the muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Ocular organs
It clears the sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Organs of the chest
It is used as a fumigation through a funnel into the throat and benefits the cough, alone or with terebinth gum {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.644}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
With honey and celery seed it benefits swelling of the liver and the stomach, and it is beneficial against the harm of cheese {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Excretory organs
With celery seed it is beneficial for the kidneys and for dribbling of urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that its decoction benefits pain of the womb, drunk and sat in, and is drunk with honey, and that celery seed benefits swellings of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.645}
