## The five means for the inverted lash

In sum, the treatment of the inverted lash is one of five means: adhesion, cautery, threading with the needle, shortening the lid by cutting, and preventing plucking ⁦(the craft of each means and what is used in it are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.200}

## The superfluous lash

The superfluous lash is generated from much putrid moisture gathering in the lids. Its treatment is cleansing the body, head and eye as known, then using the sharp collyria that cleanse the lid, especially when there is tearing or a humoural symptom; if that does not avail, it is treated by plucking with an application on the root that prevents regrowth ⁦(the names of the collyria, galls, bloods and compounds are not reproduced, among them one the book itself says experience did not confirm)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.201}
