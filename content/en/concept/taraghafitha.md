## Nature
Dioscorides describes a broad, rough root, the thorn of tragacanth. It sends up short, strong stems with many thin leaves, and among the leaves a fine, white, stiff thorn standing upright. The editor's note identifies the plant as tragacanth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}

## Gum
When the root is cut, a moisture appears at the place of the cut and turns into gum. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Chest and breath
Kneaded with honey and placed under the tongue, it helps cough and roughness of the chest. When it is melted and dissolved, it is drunk in a sweet drink. The measure is left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}

## Excretory organs
Mixed with burnt and washed deer horn, or a little Yemeni alum, it helps kidney pain and burning of the bladder. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.508}
