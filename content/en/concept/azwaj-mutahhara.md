## Overview
God promised the believers purified spouses in Paradise, in one repeated wording across three suras — al-Baqara, Al Imran and al-Nisa: «and for them therein are purified spouses, and they abide therein forever» {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In the exegesis
Ibn Kathir explains the purified as cleansed of urine, excrement, discharge, menstruation and every hurt and defilement {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}; none of the worldly imperfections of their like belongs to them.
