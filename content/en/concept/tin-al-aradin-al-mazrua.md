## Nature
Dioscorides says that every earth used in medicine has a general power that is astringent, softening, cooling and drying, and each has a special use. Among the earths of cultivated land some are very white and some grey; the grey is better and softer than the white. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.506}

## Properties
Its power is astringent and cooling, and it softens only gently. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.506}

## Wounds and ulcers
It fills ulcers with flesh and binds wounds at their first appearance. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.506}
