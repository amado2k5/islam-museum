## Summary
A solar eclipse happens when, at conjunction, the Moon comes between the eye and the Sun. Its centre lies on the zodiac belt and it is nearer to the Earth than the Sun is, so the small nearby body hides the large distant one. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.84}.

## Partial and total eclipse
He says that if the Moon strays from the Sun's path in latitude, it lies between the Sun and the eye only at less than a half circle, so only part of the Sun is hidden; the nearer its centre to the Sun's path, the greater the eclipse. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.83}.
