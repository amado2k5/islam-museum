## Nature
Well known {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Actions and properties
Its seed is heating and softening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Respiratory organs
Its seed is very good for the chest and benefits cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Excretory organs
Its seed softens the belly, and the juice of its leaf benefits the diarrhoea of children {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}
