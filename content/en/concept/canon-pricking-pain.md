## Summary
For Ibn Sina pricking pain is caused by something that stretches a membrane across, so that it resembles a separation of its continuity; it may be even or uneven in feeling, and he explains the unevenness by what the membrane stretches over, or by the state of the organ's sense {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## The cause
The cause of pricking pain is a cause that stretches the membrane across, as though breaking its continuity. The pain may be equal in sensation or not {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## Why it may be unequal
It is unequal either because what the membrane is stretched over and touches is not uniform in hardness and softness, like the collarbone for the membrane lining the ribs when the swelling of pleurisy draws it upward, or because it is not uniform in its movement, like the diaphragm for that membrane. Or the organ's sense is not uniform, by nature or because a defect has struck some parts and not others {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.
