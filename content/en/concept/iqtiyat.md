## Definition
Iqtiyat means eating the staple food (qut), the food that keeps a person alive, such as wheat or rice. Staple foods are those fit to sustain bodies on a regular basis, unlike things that sustain them only occasionally. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3183}

Jurists use the word in its lexical sense. Al-Dusuqi defines a staple as what sustains the body so that it does not decay when one is limited to it. Foods in general are a wider category. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3183}

## Where the jurists treat it
- Zakat: there is no disagreement that zakat is due on crops and fruit that are staple and storable. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3183}
- Sale of ribawi goods: most jurists do not count being a staple as the cause of riba; the Malikis count staple and storable food together as the cause. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3183}
- Hoarding: the jurists agree that hoarding staples is forbidden, most of them holding it unlawful, and most hold that hoarding applies only to staples. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3183}

The detail on hoarding is in the entry on it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3183}
