## Summary
The Sheikh paraphrases a passage on the reversal of values, in which the self takes the place of the intellect, desire that of right guidance, injustice that of justice, wrongdoing that of good, ignorance that of knowledge, show that of sincerity, falsehood that of truth, and flattery that of counsel (needs_source: the origin of the passage is not named in the recording, and the last pairs are garbled in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 00:13:00}

## Those pointed to in a time of reversal
The Sheikh says that the people with these reversed qualities are the ones pointed to and hold dominance in such a time, whereas before it was the opposite, and the people of the praiseworthy qualities were the ones pointed to. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep02 00:13:30}
