## Its signs
An insect's entry into the ear is known by severe pain with scratching and movement to the animal's measure; worms are felt with a tickling {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.231}
## Its treatments
What covers all of it: dropping the known in the ear — it stills the animal's movement at once and kills it before long, especially the small; pouring roast-meat drippings in is good too; oil set in the ear while sitting in the sun may help. Of juices, especially for worms, the known are named — all stronger and surer in worms; a tried one for worms: the known mixture is set in the ear on a wool plug dipped in it filling the ear, the sufferer leans on it without sleeping, then it is snatched out at once and many worms come out ⁦(drug, juice and compound names and measures are not carried; the trial is transmitted as the book's report)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.231} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.232}
