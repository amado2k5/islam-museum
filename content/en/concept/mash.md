## Nature and selection
Close in substance to the broad bean; the best time to use it is summer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Temperament
Moderate in moisture and dryness; hulled it is moderate, and unhulled it leans to dryness because its husk is astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Actions and properties
It lacks the bloating of the broad bean, and though it has a slight bloating it is less; it lacks the broad bean's cleansing and the lentil's coolness, and when a little safflower is put with it, it is improved {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Joints
A poultice for pain of the limbs, especially with grape juice and cooked wine with saffron, put on bruises and sprains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Its chyme is praised, especially hulled; it lacks the slow descent of the broad bean, and cooked with sweet almond oil it gives a better humour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Excretory organs
Cooked in water after water in which it was cooked and poured off, it binds the bowel, especially when soured with pomegranate seed and sumac {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
And it harms sexual power, as some have said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.570}.
