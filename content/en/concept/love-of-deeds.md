## Summary
Sha'rawi considers the verse that makes God's love go to whoever keeps his covenant and is conscious of God ([[passage/quran-3-76|Al Imran 76]]). He holds that the love attaches to the deed, not to the person {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1550}.

## Love for the attribute, not the person
Sha'rawi says that someone who reads the verse as meaning God loves the worshipper's person might think the love is fixed to him and stop working. In his reading, the love is for the presence of the attribute, so a person should strive to keep the attribute, to remain loved by God {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1550}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1551}.

## Lineage and deeds
Sha'rawi says persons are not multiplied from one origin by descent in God's sight. Lineage has no worth, and worth lies in righteous deeds. He cites the story of Noah and his son: when Noah asked for his son to be saved, he was told the son was not of his family because his deeds were not righteous {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1550}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1551}.
