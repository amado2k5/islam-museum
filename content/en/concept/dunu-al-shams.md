## Overview
The drawing-near of the sun is a scene of the gathering ground: the sun comes within the measure of a mile of creation, and people sweat according to their deeds {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In the hadith
Muslim records from al-Miqdad that the Prophet said: on the Day of Resurrection the sun is drawn near creation until it is the measure of a mile from them, and people stand in their sweat according to their deeds — some to the ankles, some to the knees, some to the waist, and some bridled by it {cite:sahih-muslim}. Al-Nawawi's commentary places this sweat before the reckoning, on the gathering ground {cite:sharh-sahih-muslim-al-nawawi}.
