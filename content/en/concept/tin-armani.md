## Nature
A reddish-grey earth, known, which goldsmiths use in colouring gold; the earth called alani is close to it in action. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Properties
It stops blood, because its drying is extreme. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Swellings and pustules
It helps plague, taken as a drink and applied, and it prevents the spreading of putrefaction in the organs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is remarkable in the treatment of wounds. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Organs of the head
It prevents catarrh and helps thrush. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Organs of the breath
It is good for spitting of blood and helps consumption and descending humours. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Excretory organs
It is good for ulcers of the bowels and for diarrhoea, and for bleeding of the womb (a book listing, labelled). {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}

## Fevers
It helps consumptive and epidemic fevers in particular; some people were spared a great plague by habitually drinking it in a thin wine. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.503}
