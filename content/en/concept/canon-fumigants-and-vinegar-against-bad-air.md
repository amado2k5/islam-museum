## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the fumigants that mend the putrefaction of airs, namely sa'd, frankincense, myrtle, rose and sandalwood, adds that vinegar in an epidemic is a safeguard against its afflictions, and refers the rest of the subject to the particular books {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.259}.

## Fumigants
The fumigants that set right the putrefaction of airs are sa'd, frankincense, myrtle, rose and sandalwood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.259}.

## Vinegar and the cross-reference
The use of vinegar in an epidemic is a safeguard against its afflictions, and Ibn Sina says he will give in the particular books the remainder of what should be said on this subject {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.259}.
