## Nature
It is more drying than qalqatar although less stinging, and so finer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627}. The later page gives it as a drug for scab made from litharge and qalqadis, and gives a method of making it that is left out here {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Actions and properties
More drying than qalqatar and less stinging {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Wounds and ulcers
It removes scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.627} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}
