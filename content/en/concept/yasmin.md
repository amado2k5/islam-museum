## Nature and temperament
The white kind is hotter than the yellow, and the yellow hotter than the purple. Taken as a whole, it is hot and dry in the second degree, the book says. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.511}

## Properties
It dissolves the humours. Its oil benefits the elderly when applied. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.512}

## Adornment
Washing the face with it in the bath, wet or dry, removes freckles. The book adds that frequent smelling of it produces yellowness of the complexion, a claim recorded as stated. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.512}

## Joints
Its oil helps the cold diseases of the nerves. The rest of the line is garbled in the text and is left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.512}

## Head
Its smell causes headache, yet it dissolves the headache of hot origin when it is smelled, the book says. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.512}
