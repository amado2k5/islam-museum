## Summary
Funerals in the jurisprudence are the rules for the dead from the approach of death to burial: closing the eyes, covering the body, announcing the death, settling debts, preparation, washing and shrouding, carrying, the funeral prayer and burial. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6464}

## The dying and the dead
It is recommended to close the eyes of the dead, soften the joints, cover the body with a cloth and inform neighbours and friends. Debts should be settled promptly. Wailing, shouting and tearing garments are disliked; weeping without a sound is allowed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6465} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6466} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6467} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6469}

## Carrying and following the bier
Carrying the bier is a collective duty. Hurrying it short of a trot is recommended; carrying between two poles is disliked except when needed. Walking in front of or behind the bier differs between the schools. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6470} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6472} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6473}

## The funeral prayer
The funeral prayer is a collective duty. Its pillars, recommended acts and the imam's position differ between the schools. It has four takbirs according to the majority and includes a supplication for the dead. Priority to lead it belongs, in most schools, to the closest male relative in order of agnation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6477} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6486} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6498}

## Mosque and cemetery
The Hanafis dislike praying over the dead in a mosque where congregational prayer is held; the Shafi'is allow it if the mosque is not soiled. Opinions on praying in a cemetery differ. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6495} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6496}

## After burial
Lingering at the grave and praying for the dead are recommended. The schools differ on instructing the dead after burial. Hosting meals by the bereaved family is disliked; neighbours may prepare food for them. The majority hold that the reward of charity and supplication reaches the dead. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6501} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6503} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6505}
