## Nature and selection
Close in nature to the myrobalan, and its kernel is sweet and near the hazelnut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.

## Temperament and actions
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. It has a light cleansing power and an astringent power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.

## Uses by organ
Food organs: it strengthens the stomach by tanning and binding, and helps its looseness and moisture; nothing tans the stomach better {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Excretory organs: it sometimes binds the belly, and according to some only softens it, which is the apparent view; it helps the straight intestine and the anus greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.
