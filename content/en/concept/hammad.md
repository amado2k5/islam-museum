## Nature and temperament
Nature: Dioscorides says this plant has many kinds. One grows in fat soil with long leaves that have sharp tips, and may grow in gardens; when boiled it has a pleasant taste. Another grows in thickets. A wild smooth kind resembles lamb's tongue, and another has leaves like thyme. The wild kind is called wild silq, and the wild kind is stronger in everything. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}
Temperament: cold and dry in the second degree; its seed is cold in the first and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.483}

## Actions
It has binding; in its mild form it has a slight dissolving action; the sour kind is stronger, and the one that is not very sour is more nourishing. It resembles endive, and all of it checks yellow bile; mixed with other things it is a sound remedy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: its roots with vinegar peel nails; when boiled in wine its poultice helps leprosy-like white patches and the patch the book calls qawba. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
Swellings: applied to swellings in pigs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
Wounds: its roots with vinegar for ulcerating scabies and qawabi; its decoction in hot water on itching. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
Organs of the head: its juice used as a mouthwash for a painful tooth, and its decoction in wine likewise; it helps swellings under the ear. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
Organs of digestion: helps black jaundice with wine, eases nausea; when boiled in vinegar and applied to the spleen it dissolves the swelling. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
Excretory organs: the plant and its seed bind, especially the seed of the larger kind; some say the leaf of all its kinds, boiled and eaten, loosens the bowels. Its ground roots help flow of the womb; drunk in wine they break up kidney stone; for its thickness they help excoriation. Seed drunk with water or wine helps ulcers of the bowel and chronic diarrhoea, and taken ground it stops chronic flow of humours from the womb; boiled in wine and drunk, it breaks up bladder stone and brings the menses strongly. The womb and menses claim is a labelled listing for the founder's ruling. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
Poisons: helps the sting of the scorpion, especially the wild kind; if the seed is taken before a sting of vermin or scorpion, the sting does no harm. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.484}
