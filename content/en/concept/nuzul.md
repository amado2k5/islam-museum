## Summary
Nuzul, in the lexicon, is descent from a height to a lower place. In law there are several rulings. On what the preacher does after finishing his sermon on Friday: the Hanafis have the caller give the call to prayer after he descends; the Malikis have him ask forgiveness and then descend to pray; the Shafi'is have him begin to descend as the caller finishes the second call; and the Hanbalis have him descend when the caller says that the prayer has begun. The descent of a delegation of unbelievers into a mosque is allowed by the Hanbalis for need. Most jurists do not require a rider to dismount for the prostration of recitation, but gesture instead; the Shafi'is and Hanbalis allow the preacher to descend for it, the Hanafis make it obligatory, and the Malikis do not prostrate. Deliberate emission of semen by desire invalidates the fast according to most jurists. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15647} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15648} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15649}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme "Liqa' al-Iman"
In episode 27 of [[work/likaa-al-iman|Liqa' al-Iman]] [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|al-Sha'rawi]] is asked how long the revelation of the Qur'an took. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep27 00:07:30} He reconciles its descent on the Night of Power with its descent in the month of Ramadan by saying the Qur'an was in the Preserved Tablet, came down all at once to the lowest heaven on the Night of Power, and was then spread over time with the Messenger's mission, coming down whenever a ruling was revealed. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep27 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep27 00:08:30} In episode 28 he holds that the way of life came before the creation of man, as a maker sets the purpose of his product before making it, pointing to the opening of Surat al-Rahman, where teaching the Qur'an is placed before creating man. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep28 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep28 00:04:30}
