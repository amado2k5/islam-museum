## Overview
Muhammad al-Bahi argues in *al-Islam Fitrat Allah* that wealth is necessary to human life and that seeking and owning it belongs to the fitra — so banning private property outright is no natural course {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 27}. Yet wealth carries temptation and trial: the Muslim takes care in how he earns and grows it, avoiding what harms him and others {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 28}. The first principle: real ownership is God's, man is His trustee — bound by God's limits in acquiring and spending, giving freely in His path {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 69} {cite:al-islam-fitrat-allah-azhar p. 70}, and God's path is the public good of the community.
