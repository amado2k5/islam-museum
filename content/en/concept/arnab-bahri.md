## Nature and selection
A reddish shell-bearing animal, with parts inside that resemble tooth leaves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}. The book lists it as the marine rabbit (arnab bahri), separate from the wild rabbit (arnab barri) that appears earlier under the letter alif {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament for it in this entry; its blood is said to be hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: its blood is hot and clears freckles and vitiligo; its head, burnt, makes hair grow in alopecia, especially with bear and snake fat; and when it is poulticed whole, it removes hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}. Eyes: it clears the sight as a poultice and as a kohl {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}. Poisons: it is counted among the poisonous drugs and kills by ulcerating the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}.
