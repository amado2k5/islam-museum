# Human rights — the violations file in the Arab world

## The episode and the votes
The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode debated the human-rights file in the Arab world: the scale of violation and its causes — the culture of despotism or political tension — and when the 'dawn visitors' saga will end {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:02:00}. The youth voted: 69% judged international covenants practically worthless and 19% of limited use; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:07:00} 72% judged the Arab person's situation worse than others' and 10% saw no difference; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:36:30} and only 9% endorsed international intervention while 65% saw the solution as internal {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:57:00}.

## Worthless covenants — and the double standard
Panelists traced the youth's scepticism to the double standard: their example was international envoys rushing to one victim's site while ignoring 220 dead on the other side {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:29:30}. They added that the big international organisations answer to powers hostile to Arabs and Muslims — though independent rights organisations remain worth resorting to {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 01:04:30}.

## Rights untaught — entertainment media
They blamed ignorance of treaty-guaranteed rights on their absence from curricula and on officially controlled media preferring entertainment: 14 of the top-20 most-watched programmes in the Arab world are serial dramas — 'where then do people acquire their culture?' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:32:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:32:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:33:00}.

## The way — from within, gradually
Panelists advised starting with non-political rights — an honest judiciary and protection of property — before political rights, to avoid collision; {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:30:30} and agreed international intervention is no answer — reform is internal even if sincere pressures assist {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 00:57:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep10 01:04:30}.
