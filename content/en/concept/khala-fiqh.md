## Summary
Khala' is lexically an empty place, then by custom the building for relief, called the privy, the place of ease or the toilet; the verb takhalla means to relieve oneself. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7745} Among the manners the jurists give: enter with the left foot saying the formula of seeking refuge from evil spirits, following the report of Anas; details of relief are under the entry on relieving oneself. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7746}
