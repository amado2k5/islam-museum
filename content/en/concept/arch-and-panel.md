## Summary
The arch-and-panel system is an Iranian way of organising walls and facades in which the arch is the primary theme and the rectangular panel secondary. Repeated arches unify the surface and the voids and govern the decoration {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.7}.

## Five relations
The author lists five relations: alignment, empanelling, multiplication, enframing and intersecting {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.7}.

## In India
The system first appears in Sultanate architecture (the Alai Darwaza at the Quwwatul Islam complex) and gains prominence under the Mughals. On the Buland Darwaza and the Taj Mausoleum it is modified by fluted or octagonal quoin shafts {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.7} {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-2002 p.8}.
