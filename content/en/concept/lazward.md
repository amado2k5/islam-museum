## Nature and selection
Its power is that of the gold-solder, a little weaker. The editor's note calls it the stone azurite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree and dry in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.

## Actions and properties
It has a stinging, putrefying and cleansing power with sharpness and slight astringency, and it burns and ulcerates {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.

## Cosmetic uses
It makes warts fall away {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.

## Eyes
It improves and thickens the eyelid margins; this is said to be a property in it, and also said to come from its emptying the bad humours that stop hair from growing well {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps shortness of breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.

## Excretory organs
It brings on urine well, drunk or as a pessary, loosens black bile and everything mixed with the blood that is thick, and helps kidney pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.538}.
