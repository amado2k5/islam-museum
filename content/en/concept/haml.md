## Definition
Pregnancy in the Qur'an is weakness upon weakness: his mother carried him reluctantly and delivered him reluctantly, and his bearing and weaning are thirty months; in another verse his mother carried him in weakness upon weakness and his weaning is in two years. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## The rulings of pregnancy
Lineage is established by pregnancy, maintenance is due to the pregnant woman, she may break the Ramadan fast when she fears for herself or her child for most jurists and makes up what she missed, retaliation and the hadd are deferred until she delivers and the child can do without her milk for those who hold it, and the widow's waiting period ends with the delivery. {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## Its term for the jurists
The shortest pregnancy is six months, from the thirty months of bearing and weaning together with the two years of nursing; the longest is two years for Malik, al-Shafi'i and Ahmad in one narration, four for Abu Hanifa, and five recorded in Ahmad's school. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
