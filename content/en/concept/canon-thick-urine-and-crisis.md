## Summary
Ibn Sina says thick urine can be a favourable crisis when it bursts out at once with ease, and tells the bursting of swellings by the pus, the stinking smell, the flakes passed with it and the signs that went before {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.191}.

## Favourable thick urine
If urine is thick and then thins by degrees with abundance, that is praiseworthy. Sometimes abundant urine follows scanty, thick and turbid urine and is a good sign: when the thick, turbid urine that had been passed little by little bursts out at once, plentifully and easily, the illness is often resolved by it, whether an acute fever or another disease of fullness. Thick urine that is a crisis for spleen diseases and mixed fevers is not expected to turn even {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.191}.

## The bursting of swellings
Thick urine showing the bursting of swellings is known by what is mixed with it and what went before. What is mixed is pus, shown by the stinking smell and the scrapings passed with it as white or red flakes or bran; what went before is a sign of a swelling or sore in the bladder, kidney, liver or chest. If the pus is ripened it is praiseworthy; if the thick urine inclines to black with pain on the left side, it is from the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.191}.
