## Nature and selection
Nature: the seed of wild carrot; its full account is given in the chapter on wild carrot. Temperament: hot in the third degree and dry in its first degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}

## Actions
Very opening. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs, book listing on the menses: it drives urine and the menses, and the book says it helps both. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}
