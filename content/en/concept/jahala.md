## Overview

In contracts, jahala is ignorance of the thing contracted for, of the person in whose favour an acknowledgement is made, or of the claim. The encyclopedia gives three degrees; the gross kind, which leads to dispute, voids the contract. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6626} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6628}

## Sales

The sales of touching and throwing are forbidden and void. The sale of goods by lot without weighing is allowed out of need, despite the uncertainty. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6630} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6632}

## Term, acknowledgement and claim

Most jurists hold that an unknown term voids the contract. An acknowledgement needs a known beneficiary, and a claim needs a known object. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6633} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6637} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6638}
