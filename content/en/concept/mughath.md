## Nature and selection
Some said it is the root of the wild pomegranate, which does not agree with the report that its seed suits sexual capacity and stirs it strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Temperament
Hot to the second degree and moist in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Actions and properties
It strengthens the organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Cosmetic uses
It fattens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Joints
Applied as a poultice it helps sprain, fracture and weakness of muscle; it helps gout and spasm and is good for hardness of the joints {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Chest and breathing
It softens hardnesses of the throat and lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it stirs sexual desire, especially its seed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.559}.
