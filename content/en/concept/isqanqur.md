## Nature and selection
A water lizard caught in the Nile of Egypt; they say it is of the crocodile's line and that, placed out of the water, it grows outside it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. The best is that caught in spring and at the time of its arousal; the best of its parts is the navel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}. A footnote gives the name as al-saqanqur {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.368}.

## Temperament and actions
The passage gives no temperament for it at this point.

## Uses by organ
Joints: it helps cold ailments of the nerves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.369}. Excretory organs: its salt stirs desire, how much more its flesh, especially the flesh of its navel region and near its kidney, and above all its fat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.369}.
