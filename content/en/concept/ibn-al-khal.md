## Summary
A maternal uncle's son is the son of the mother's brother. He is either a blood cousin, which is the meaning when used alone, or a foster cousin. The word child covers male and female; the word son covers only the male. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1462}

## General ruling
The jurists agree that he is within the non-prohibited kin: marriage to him is permitted, a woman may not be alone with him, and maintenance is not due from him unless he is an heir. A foster maternal cousin shares most of these rules. They also agree that he is among those with blood-ties through a female, with rules in inheritance, the funeral prayer, kinship and guardianship. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1462}

## Where it is discussed
The rules are set out in the chapters on marriage and elsewhere, and in the entries on prohibited kin (mahram) and kinship (arham). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1462}
