## Nature
A known plant, of the endive kind. The editor's footnote identifies it as wild endive. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.499}

## Temperament
Its cold is greater than its moisture, though it has some moisture. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.499}

## Properties
It cools and opens. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.499}

## Organs of the eye
Its milk clears white films on the eye. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.499}

## Organs of digestion
Its juice helps dropsy greatly and opens obstructions of the liver. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.499}

## Poisons
It resists poisons, and it is applied as a poultice for stings, especially the sting of the scorpion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.499}
