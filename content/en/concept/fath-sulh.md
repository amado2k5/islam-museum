## Overview
In al-Baladhuri, *fath sulhan* (conquest by treaty) is the taking of a town by agreement and peaceable settlement, without fighting. His first example of it is Fadak. It is contrasted with [[concept/fath-anwa|conquest by force]]. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.41}

## The example of Fadak
On his return from Khaybar, the Prophet sent Muhayyisa ibn Mas'ud al-Ansari to invite the people of Fadak to Islam. They made peace with him by giving up half the land and its produce, and he accepted it from them. Its people remained on it during the lives of the Prophet and Abu Bakr, until Umar expelled them. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.41}

## The effect
Those who made peace remain on their land under the terms of the treaty, and their property stays with them except what was stipulated. Because the Muslims did not advance against it with horse or camel, the half of Fadak given by treaty became [[concept/fay|fay']] (property returned to the community), belonging solely to the Prophet. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.41}
