## Summary
Ibn Sina lists forms of turbid urine and what they show: turbid urine shows collapse of strength, wine-like or chickpea-water urine belongs to pregnant women and those with long hot swellings, and urine like that of asses shows corruption of the humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.191}.

## Turbid urine and the fall of strength
A healthy person at ease who has left off exercise may pass urine like pus and matter, whereby his body is cleansed and its flabbiness goes. Turbid urine often shows the fall of strength, and when strength falls cold takes over. Turbid urine like the colour of bad wine or chickpea water occurs in pregnant women and in people with long hot swellings in the viscera {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.191}.

## Urine like that of asses
Urine that resembles the urine of asses and beasts, as if shaken up by its heavy pustules, shows corruption of the body's humours, mostly a raw humour on which some heat has worked, bringing about a thick wind; so too it may show a headache present or impending, and, if it lasts, a tremor {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.191}.
