## Overview
Al-Maraghi holds that Islam forbids only work that aggresses against reason, life, wealth or honor {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 17}. Among the prohibited: wrongful killing or hiring oneself to it; making and trading wine, and narcotics likewise; gambling in every form including what tortures animals; theft; sorcery, soothsaying, fortune-telling and palm- and cup-reading; and false witness {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 17} {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 18} {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 19}. On begging he dislikes asking people for profit and permits it only in necessity.
