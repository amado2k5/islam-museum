## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that Islam covers every activity in life that fulfils the human vice-regency on earth and its building, and that the pillars are a foundation that is not enough by itself {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1089}.

## Pillars and building
He says prayer, fasting, zakat and the pilgrimage are the pillars on which Islam is built, and that one cannot make Islam nothing but these pillars, just as a foundation alone is not a house {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1089}.

## Time and work
He notes that prayer takes an hour a day, zakat a day a year, fasting a month, and the pilgrimage one time in a life. He asks what a person does with the rest of his time, and answers that bread needs knowledge, movement and energy, so work becomes worship {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1089} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1090}.

## Idleness
He rebukes those who say they are created only to worship and do not work. A person should not take the fruit of another's work without effort, and doing a task well is part of good worship {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1090}.
