## Nature and temperament
Its force is close to that of the winter cherry, especially its leaf. Its nature is cold and dry to the second degree. The editor's note: the garden winter cherry. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its juice keeps ulcers from spreading, and it removes the hardness of fistulas and chronic ear sores. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}

## Chest, digestion and excretory organs
It helps asthma and difficult breathing. It helps jaundice. It helps ulcers of the urinary passages. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.521}
