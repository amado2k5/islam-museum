## Nature and temperament
Nature: Dioscorides says it is a tree whose leaves grow on its branches, like olive leaves but wider, softer and greener; it has a small white fragrant flower and a black seed; it is brought from hot countries. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}
Temperament: cold in the first degree and dry in the second. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}

## Actions
It has a dissolving, binding and drying action without harm; it dissolves, opens and clears the mouths of the veins. Its oil is strongly warming and very softening. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: henna with the water of kendus, applied to the hair, reddens it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}
Swellings: its decoction helps hot swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}
Wounds and ulcers: its decoction helps burns from fire. It is said to act on wounds like dam al-akhawain, and it is applied to fractures alone or with cerate. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}
Joints: it helps pains of the nerves; it enters ointments for paralysis and spasm; its oil dissolves exhaustion, softens the nerves, and helps a fracture. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.474}
Organs of the head: applied to the forehead with vinegar for headache, and it helps sores of the mouth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Organs of the chest: suits the sharp pleurisy and enters ointments for quinsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
Excretory organs: labelled listing, set aside for the founder's ruling: it suits pains of the womb. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.475}
