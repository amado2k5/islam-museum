## Nature
It is a kind of gum of the pine tree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}

## Temperament
Hot to the third degree, dry in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}

## Actions and properties
A grower of flesh in hard bodies, but it stirs pain in tender bodies; ulcers may be healed by it and by jullanar and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.665}
