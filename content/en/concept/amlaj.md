## Nature and selection
Amlaj is well known; its preserve is weaker than preserved myrobalan and on its way, and when it is steeped in milk it is called shir amlaj {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.353}.

## Temperament and actions
According to al-Yahudi it is hot, according to many of them cold in the second degree, and according to Sharak al-Hindi it has some warming; perhaps the truth is that it is dry with a little cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.353}. It quenches the heat of the blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}.

## Uses by organ
It strengthens the root of the hair and darkens the hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It helps the nerve and the joints greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It strengthens the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It strengthens the heart, kindles it and increases understanding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. It strengthens the stomach and tans it, soothes thirst and vomiting and whets the appetite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}. Some hold that it binds the bowel, but its preserve loosens the bowel without hardship, and it helps piles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}.

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it stirs desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.354}; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation.
