## Summary
In acute diseases Ibn Sina compares red, watery and white urine and distinguishes blood-red from bile-red, saying that deep red in kidney diseases is bad and that urine that stays red with no sediment is fearful {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.

## The ranking
In acute diseases a red dye is better than a watery urine, the white for its consistency is also better than the watery, and blood-red is safer than bile-red; bile-red is not so fearful if the bile is still, and fearful if it is moving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.

## Kidney and head
Deep red urine in kidney diseases is bad, since it mostly shows a hot swelling, and in headaches it warns of confusion. If in acute diseases the urine begins red and stays so without a sediment, death is feared and a swelling of the kidneys shown; if it is turbid with the redness and stays so, it shows a swelling in the liver and a weakening of the innate heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.188}.
