## Summary
Mutual hatred and estrangement are brotherhood's contrary: envy, desertion, bad suspicion and backbiting. "Do not envy one another, do not hate one another, do not turn away from one another — be servants of God, brothers" {cite:sahih-muslim 2559}; it was called "the shaver" that shaves not hair but religion {cite:sunan-abi-dawud 4915}, and a Muslim is forbidden to shun his brother beyond three days {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
