## Nature and selection
Nature: hanzal is known, and there is a male and a female kind. The male is fibrous; the female is soft, white and smooth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
Selection: the preferred is the white, very white and soft kind; the black is bad, and so is the hard. Its fat is not taken out from inside when gathered, but left in place, because it weakens if this is done. It is not gathered until it has turned yellow and its green has fully gone; otherwise it is harmful and bad. When there is only one fruit on the tree, it is bad and deadly. The fibrous male is stronger than the soft female. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.480}
It must be crushed very finely, and one must not be satisfied that it is fine: its small part, when it meets moisture, swells, clings to the stomach and the folds of the intestines and inflames. So it should be wetted with honey water, dried and crushed; and correcting it and removing its harm is better done with tragacanth than with gum, because gum overpowers the strength of the drug. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: hot in the third degree and dry. Al-Kindi claimed it is cold and moist, a claim the book calls far from the truth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Actions: dissolves, cuts, and draws from a distance; its fresh leaf stops bleeding. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: rubbed on for leprosy and elephantiasis. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Swellings: the fresh leaf dissolves and ripens swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Joints: helps pains of the nerves and joints, sciatica, and very cold gout. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Organs of the head: cleans the brain; its root boiled with vinegar is used as a gargle for toothache, or a hollowed root is set in hot ash with vinegar; cooked in oil the oil becomes a useful ear drop against buzzing in the ear, and it eases pulling teeth. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Organs of breathing and chest: purging with it helps severe breathlessness. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Organs of digestion: its root helps dropsy, and it is bad for the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Excretory organs: purges thick phlegm from the joints and nerves in particular, purges bile, helps wet and windy colic, sometimes purges blood, and if taken it kills the foetus; because it leaves the bowel quickly it does not reach the effects expected from its bitterness. It helps kidney and bladder diseases. The measured dose and preparation are left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
Poisons: the fruit gathered green purges excessively and vomits excessively, and it may cause fatal faintness; the root alone, left on its stem, can kill in measured amounts; the root helps the bite of vipers and is among the most useful remedies for the scorpion sting when applied as a plaster. The lethal amounts are left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.481}
