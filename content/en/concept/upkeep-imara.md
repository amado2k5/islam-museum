## Summary
In the lexicon, imara is what a thing is made to flourish by, and it also means building. As a term it is the doing of what keeps land or a building in good condition, such as reviving land and repairing a building {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11805}.

## Duty of upkeep
The supervisor of an endowment must keep it in repair from its income or from what the founder stipulated; a guardian must keep his ward's real estate in repair; a restrained person must keep his property in repair. A free adult is not obliged to keep his property in repair, though neglecting a ruin is disliked {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11806}.

## The mosque
Upkeep of mosques is a communal duty, kept by worship, teaching and cleaning {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11806}.

## Where to look further
Mosques are treated under «masjid» and endowments under «waqf» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11806}.
