## What befalls the teeth
Their diseases include relaxation, loosening, falling out, protrusion and change of colour in substance and coating; pain, corrosion, putrefaction and breaking; severe pains, itching and al-daras — a kind of their pains; inability to chew the sweet and the sour and hurt from hot and cold with little patience to meet one or both {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.266}
## Growth and the reading of temperament
The teeth were created able to grow and increase always, to stand in place of what wears away, so that the tooth adjoining the place of a fallen or drawn one grows longer when the increase reaches it and no wearing opposes it; the temperament is inferred from the gum and its colour — yellow bilious, white phlegmatic, red sanguine, or tending to the dark melancholic dullness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.266}
