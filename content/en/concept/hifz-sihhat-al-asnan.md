## The tooth-stick and what is kept up
Used with moderation the tooth-stick cleanses and strengthens the teeth, strengthens the gums, prevents the hollowing (the editor explains: the qalih scale hollowing the tooth roots between gum and tooth, outside and in) and sweetens the breath; the best wood for it has astringency and bitterness. The teeth are kept anointed at sleep with what suits, cooling or warming, of this kind; best first to rub with honey when there is cold, or with sugar on a leaning to cold or little heat — each gathers praised properties of cleansing, rubefaction, warming and cleansing-through, sugar below honey in all of it. Among what keeps their health is a twice-monthly mouthwash of a decoction of this kind and tooth-sticks of the burnt and smooth things he described (the names left out, some of them animal items carried as the book names them) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.267-268}
## When prone to descending matter
If the tooth is liable to descending matter, a decoction of the astringent things is held long in the mouth and the burnt things of this kind are kept sprinkled on it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.268}
