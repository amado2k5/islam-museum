## Overview
The jurist's exertion of his utmost effort to derive the legal ruling from the legal evidences {cite:nashat-al-fiqh-al-ijtihadi-azhar p. 11}. Al-Sayis holds it began in the age of revelation; its aim is to uncover God's ruling in the case, not to create legislation — binding on the mujtahid and his followers, bound by the Shari'a's general rules {cite:nashat-al-fiqh-al-ijtihadi-azhar p. 24, 26}. Its ways are many: taking the ruling from the texts' literal sense or from their rationale.
