## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the things that make the pulse weak, hard or soft {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.175}.

## Weakness
Among the changing causes of a weak pulse are grief, sleeplessness, evacuation, change of place, bad humour, excessive exercise, the stirring of the humours and their meeting organs that are keen of sense and next to the heart, and everything that dissolves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.175}.

## Hardness and softness
The causes of hardness are dryness of the vessel's substance, its great stretching, or severe congealing cold; the pulse may harden in carpenters through the strain of their labour and the stretching of their limbs toward the direction of nature's pushing. The causes of softness are moistening causes that are natural, like food, or morbid, like dropsy, or neither natural nor morbid, like bathing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.175}.
