## Overview

Jawaz in the language is possibility and permission; in fiqh it often means permission, and at times a wider sense that covers the obligatory and the recommended, depending on context. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6685} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6686}

## Whether a contract is binding

When a contract is considered binding, its harms are weighed, as in the reward for a task, the bequest and the profit-sharing partnership. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6687}
