## Summary
Ibn Sina traces itching pain to a pungent or salty humour, rough pain to a rough humour, and irritative pain to a humour with a sharp quality {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.149-150}.

## Itching and rough
The cause of itching pain is a pungent or salty humour; the cause of rough pain is a rough humour. The editor's note in the printed edition explains pungent as having a biting taste like red pepper, and salty as tasting of salt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.149}.

## Irritative
Irritative pain comes from a humour with a sharp quality {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.150}.
