## The opening of the chapter and its division

The book opens its fourth discourse, on the states of the visual power and its acts, with the chapter on weakness of sight: the weakness and its lesion are produced either by a general temperament in the body — a predominating dryness, a predominating humoural or temperamental moisture without matter, or a vapour rising from the body and especially the stomach, or a cold with or without matter, or a predominating material or immaterial heat — or it follows a cause in the brain itself, or something belonging to the visual spirit itself and the members next to it, such as the hollow nerve, the moistures, the layers and the visual spirit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.201} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.202}

## Its other sections

This chapter sets out in detail the causes of the weakness on the side of the body, brain, spirit, layers, moistures, pupil and nerve, and its signs and the principles of its treatment, in their own records {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.202} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.203}
