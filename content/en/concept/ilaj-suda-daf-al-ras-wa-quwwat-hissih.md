## Weakness of the head
Its treatment is to change the ill-temperament that is in it and strengthen it with the strengtheners of the head, among the fragrant medicines that have refining and astringency {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68} Often the active cause that goes with the passive cause of weakness is a gathering of bad humours, hot or not hot, in the stomach, so we must evacuate by what suits it and supply a food that joins to its goodness the dissolving power and the readiness for digestion that arise from it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68} Hippocrates allows them the drink (sharab) without condition, while Galen prefers it mixed, or thin and fragrant, or both {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68}

## Strength of the sense of the head
Its treatment is to dull the sense a little by foods that thicken the nourishment of the brain, if the digestion is strong {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68} Something of the narcotics is sometimes used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are not reproduced here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.68}
