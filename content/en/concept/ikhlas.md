## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that sincerity is doing the act for God alone, and that intention alone is not enough: there must be action with the intention. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.615} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.617}

## Deed and intention
Sha'rawi reads the hadith "actions are by intentions" as showing that the act goes with the intention: the intention benefits its owner alone, while the deed benefits people. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.618}

## Reward follows the intended aim
Sha'rawi holds that God rewards intentions by their sincerity, not bare deeds. A charity benefits the poor whether the giver wanted fame or secrecy, but the reward follows sincerity. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.618}

## The builder and the disbeliever
Sha'rawi gives the example of a man who builds a building many people benefit from, and holds that someone who does a useful deed for other than God's sake has no reward with God. This is his view alone in these pages. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.618}
