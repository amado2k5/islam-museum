## Nature and selection
The root of anjudan, below hiltit in strength and benefits; what was said in the chapter on anjudan should be carried over to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Actions and properties
Softening and ripening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is hard to digest and harms the stomach, unless the stomach is cold, which is then strengthened by it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.568}.
