## Summary
Ibn Sina describes good milk by its consistency, colour, smell, taste, quantity and evenness of parts, and names two tests: a drop on the fingernail, and a little myrrh put in a glass and stirred to learn how cheesy or watery it is {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.207}.

## The qualities
As to the quality of her milk: its consistency should be balanced and its amount balanced, its colour leaning to white, not dull, green, yellow or red, its smell good with no weakness or rot, its taste leaning to sweet with no bitterness, saltiness or sourness, and its parts alike, so that it is neither thin and running, nor very thick and cheesy, nor uneven, nor much frothed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.207}.

## The tests
Its consistency is tried by dripping it on the nail: if it runs it is thin, if it stops running it is thick. It is also tried in a glass by throwing in some myrrh and stirring with the finger, so that one learns how cheesy and how watery it is, for good milk is balanced between cheesiness and wateriness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.207}.
