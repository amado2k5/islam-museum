## The breath of pain, of the straitened, of the pus-possessed and of the quinsy
Four short chapters; what gathers them is the parting of the breath's states in the ill: the breath of pain smaller and shorter; the straitened's and the asthmatic's breath small, narrow, frequent in making-up; the pus-possessed's a labouring with no abiding power behind it; and the quinsied's a great expansion with swiftness and frequency and no puff {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.315}
