## Overview
Awra in fiqh denotes the parts of the body that must be covered; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats it across the chapters on dress, prayer and looking {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
Questions the encyclopedia examines include whether the ear counts as awra, displaying the awra, and relaxation of covering it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
