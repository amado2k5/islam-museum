## Its causes and kinds
Protrusion may occur either from severe swelling of the eyeball by its heaviness and repletion, or from severe pressing outward, or from severe relaxation of its suspensory and of the muscles preserving that relation. Where it occurs from severe swelling by heaviness and repletion, the matter is either in the eye itself — windy or moist-humoral — and the repletion may be special to it or with the brain's or the body's sharing. The kind from severe pressing outward is as occurs in choking, severe headache and after vomiting and shouting. The kind from muscle relaxation: the muscle surrounding the hollow nerve, if relaxed, does not weigh down the eyeball, which inclines outward. Protrusion may be from muscle relaxation alone so sight is not lost, or with its violation so sight is lost; it mostly comes with a visible greasiness and swelling of the cornea ⁦(the book's lines on women, labour, the lochia and the foetus are kept here as labelled listings put to the founder's ruling)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.190}

## The signs
What is from much matter gathered in the pupil comes with enlargement of the protrusion; what is from pressing may come with enlargement if matter assists or without; in both a pushing distension is felt from behind and it is known from its cause; what is from muscle relaxation — the pupil does not enlarge with it, no strong inner distension is felt, and the pupil is restless {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.190}

## The treatment
The light protrusion is sufficed by an inward-pressing bandage, sleeping on the back, lightening food, little motion and constant closing. The strong one, if there is matter, needs its cleansing from body and head with the laxatives and evacuations you know; in sum, diarrhoea is among the most beneficial things for its kinds; the poultice must be kept up at the start, and the face sprinkled with cold water or cold salt water, especially boiled with astringents; if it is not from repletion all benefit from this management at all times; if there is repletion, after the start the matter must be dissolved; if from relaxation the great electuaries, gargles, snuffs and known fumigations are used, then the strong astringents ⁦(venesection and cupping sites and drug names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.190} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.191}
