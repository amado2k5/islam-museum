## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions a hot swelling that befalls the brain of infants, which he calls al-'utas; its pain often reaches the eye and the throat and the face turns yellow, and it is treated by cooling and moistening the brain with peels, juices and oils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.215}.

## Signs
A hot swelling may befall the brain of children, called al-'utas, whose pain often reaches the eye and the throat and the face turns yellow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.215}.

## Treatment
Then its brain must be cooled and moistened with gourd and cucumber peels, nightshade water, and the juice of purslane especially, with rose oil and a little vinegar, and egg yolk with rose oil, changing from one to another all the time {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.215}.
