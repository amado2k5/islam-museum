## Nature and selection
The excellent meats are mutton, which has a gentle pungency, the young of the goat, and calves. The flesh of the young is more receptive to digestion and finer in nourishment; the kid has fewer residues than the lamb, and the flesh of a suckling nursed on good milk is good. The flesh of an old sheep is bad, and so is a lean one; dark meat is lighter and tastier, and so is the male. Soft flesh with no sinew on it can be tasty, and its nourishment when digested is good, though it is mostly phlegmy. The best of the birds is the pheasant, and the hen is finer but not more nourishing. The flesh of the he-goat is altogether bad, and the flesh of beasts of prey is bad. The best of game is the flesh of gazelles, though it leans toward black bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.550}.

## Temperament
All bird meat is drier than that of quadrupeds; beef is drier than goat; goat meat is dry and harder to digest than mutton; camel meat is thick in nourishment and strongly heating; hare is hot and dry; the meat of large birds, geese and crows is thick. Duck and water birds are very moist and close to mutton in that. Fat meat and tail fat are hot and moist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.551}.

## Actions and properties
Meat is a food that strengthens the body and is the nearest to turning into blood; the food of roasted and grilled meat is drier, and that of boiled is moister. Fat and tallow are poor in nourishment and slight, and only a small amount suits, enough to give pleasure. Salted meat, though moist in origin, becomes drier than any meat and gives little nourishment. Fat meat loosens the bowels while giving little nourishment. Tail fat is worse than fat meat, hard to digest and poor in nourishment. Beef is much nourishment but thick, dark and bad, and breeds black-bile diseases; the best is calf. Duck meat is much nourishment but not as good as chicken, its gizzards are tasty and its liver is good. The meat of the roller breaks wind and is the least liable to rot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.551}.

## Cosmetic uses
Beef breeds vitiligo; the fat of the wild ass is good applied to freckles, and so is the fat of a fattened duck {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.551}.

## Swellings and pustules
Beef breeds cancer, as do thick meats, and it dissolves hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.551}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Beef breeds scab and bad ringworm, as do thick meats {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.551}.

## Joints
Beef breeds leprosy, elephantiasis and varicose veins, as do thick meats and clarified butter. Tail fat as a poultice is good for a stiff nerve. The broth of hare meat is sat in by one with gout or joint pains, its action being close to that of fox broth. The meat of the weasel is used as a poultice on joint pains; the fat of the wild ass with costus oil is a good rub for backache and thick winds. The meat of the viper is for leprosy, as said in its chapter, and hedgehog meat too is good for leprosy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.551}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Beef and the other thick meats breed black bile and evil thoughts by drying, and weasel meat is mixed with wine and drunk for epilepsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.

## Eyes
The ash of lamb meat is for the white of the eye, and the meat of beasts of prey and clawed birds helps and strengthens the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.

## Chest and breathing
The river crab is good for those with consumption, and the meat of chicks stirs quinsy unless stewed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Thick meats thicken the spleen, but beef sakbaj with dried coriander and saffron stops matters flowing to the stomach. Sand-grouse meat helps corrupt temperament, dropsy and obstructions of the liver and spleen, and for dropsy it is better made as a sour dish so as not to stir thirst. Some praise the meat of beasts of prey for a cold, moist, weak stomach. The meat of the pig, wild and tame, is said to digest and descend most quickly, and to be strong, sticky and thick in nourishment. Deer meat, despite its thickness, descends quickly. Hedgehog with oxymel helps dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.

## Excretory organs
Beef stops yellow bile flowing into the intestines. Roast hare is good for ulcers of the intestines. Dried hedgehog meat with oxymel is good for kidney pain, and the broth of an old rooster for colic and black-bile diseases. The fat of the wild ass with costus oil is good for kidney pain with thick wind. The meat of beasts of prey and clawed birds is good for piles. A beef sakbaj broth is good for bilious diarrhoea. Bird meat, roasted or not, binds the bowels, especially partridge and francolin, and more strongly sand-grouse and larks. Deer meat is diuretic, and fatty meats loosen the bowels more than others {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.

## Fevers
Beef, deer, ibex and large birds produce quartan fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.

## Poisons and bites
Dried weasel meat given in wine helps against poisons; burnt lamb meat is for the stings of snakes and scorpions, and with wine for the rabid dog; frog meat is listed against the stings of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.552}.
