## Summary
Al-Battani lists the Coptic months from Tut to Abib and Bisra, gives each month thirty days, and notes five days added after the months called al-mulhaq. The Coptic year is 365 and a quarter days, with one day added every fourth year. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.

## The added days
The five added days are called al-mulhaq; they come after the twelve months at the end of the Coptic year, and one more day is added in every fourth year, which al-Battani counts in the reckoning of the days. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.
