## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that salat means supplication in the language, that God's blessing on His servants is mercy and grace, that the angels' blessing is forgiveness, and that believers' blessing is supplication. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.666}

## Blessing from God, the angels and believers
Sha'rawi says that God's blessing is the gift of mercy and grace, the angels' is forgiveness, and believers' is supplication. He holds that believers and disbelievers alike live by God's mercy in this world, and that the believer is increased by blessing and tranquillity. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.666}

## Prayer for the Prophet returns to the community
Sha'rawi holds that every rank the Prophet attains returns to his community, so that to pray for him with good and mercy is to pray for oneself. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.666} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.667}

## Limits of this batch
Sha'rawi states the number of blessings on the Prophet and their reward in the text without naming a source; the originals were not checked here. Other exegetes' views, the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.667}
