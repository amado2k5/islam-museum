## Treatments for the chest-side tumours
Galen said: and if the fever is very severe, beware the purgative and keep to venesection, for there is no danger in it — or its danger is less — while in purging there is great danger: it may stir, or may fail to purge, or may overdo; and the stupefying drugs should be kept from them as far as possible, for they prevent ripening and expectoration (a saying attributed to Galen, and a line labelled for the stupefying drugs) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.357}
