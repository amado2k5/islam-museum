## Nature
A gum of unpleasant taste brought from the land of the Arabs; some claimed that it is sandarus, which is not established; it is also used as a fumigation with myrrh and maya {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Actions and properties
It has a slight gluing power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Cosmetics
It cleanses the marks of ulcers quickly, and it has a wasting power when drunk with sakanjabin or water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Organs of the head
Nothing equals it in removing toothache and the receding of the gums {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Ocular organs
It clears the sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Respiratory organs
It benefits asthma with honey water, and wrestlers use it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Drunk with sakanjabin, it greatly wastes the spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it brings on the menses with honey water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.653}
