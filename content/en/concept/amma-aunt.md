## Summary
'Amma is the father's sister, and the word also covers the grandfather's sister {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11844}.

## Marriage
Marriage to an aunt is forbidden by blood and by suckling, and a woman may not be married together with her aunt {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11844}.

## Inheritance, custody and maintenance
Her inheritance depends on whether distant kin inherit; her custody comes after those with a prior right; her maintenance is disputed between the schools {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11844} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11845}.

## Where to look further
Custody is treated under «hadana», maintenance under «nafaqa», and inheritance under «irth» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11845}.
