## Nature
It is of the power of hasha, and its drink is like the drink of hasha {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.591}.

## Selection
The strongest is the wild {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.591}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.591}.

## Actions and properties
Dissolving, peeling and thinning {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.591}.

## Joints
It helps pains of the hips {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.591}.

## Organs of the head
Chewed, it calms toothache and heals the slack gums, through its burning power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.591}.

## Organs of the chest
Its oil helps the chest and lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.592}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It helps the liver and stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.592}.

## Excretory organs
It expels worms and gourd-seed worms greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.592}.
