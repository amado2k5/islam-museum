## Summary
Ibn Sina says closely mixed clotted sediment shows weakness of the liver, less mixed shows a wound in the urinary passages, and separate clots come mostly from the bladder and penis; he defers detail to the section on bloody urine. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## What it shows
If closely mixed it shows weakness of the liver; if less so, a wound and break of continuity in the urinary passages; if separate, mostly the bladder and penis, to be treated fully in the section on bloody urine. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## Red clots and bladder ailments
When the urine holds red clots like leeches and the patient is splenic, his spleen has shrivelled. Not much blood comes out in bladder ailments, because its vessels are narrow, few and buried in its substance. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.
