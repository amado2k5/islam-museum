## Summary
Al-Battani says the Roman and Coptic reckoning goes back to the year of Alexander's death, the king of Macedon, and that the Dhu al-Qarnayn years are counted from it so that the Roman and Egyptian days agree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.

## The need for one reckoning
He says the calculation by the Dhu al-Qarnayn years is needed to extract the planets' motions shown in the tables, and that the first month shown in the tables is not included in the count of months. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.101}.
