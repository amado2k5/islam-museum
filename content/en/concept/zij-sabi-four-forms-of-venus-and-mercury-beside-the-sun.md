## The four forms
For Venus, when the corrected anomaly runs over one range she rises in the evening, when she is on the western horizon and at her fastest. Over the next range she sets in the evening as she slows, turns back and is overtaken by the Sun; over the next she rises in the morning at her slowest; over the last she sets in the morning until the Sun overtakes her and hides her in the rays, when she is again fastest. Mercury follows the same forms as Venus. The range boundaries are given in abjad letters that are partly garbled. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.180-181}

## The limits of the forms
The limits of the forms are written in abjad letters, some of them unclear, so the four forms are described and the exact limits are not reproduced. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.180-181}
