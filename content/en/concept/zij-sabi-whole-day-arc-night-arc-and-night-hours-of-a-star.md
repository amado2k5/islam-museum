## Summary
The half arc is doubled to give the whole day arc of the star above the earth, and this is taken from three hundred and sixty to leave its whole night arc below the earth. The times of its day hours are taken from thirty, leaving the times of its night hours below the earth. A star that has no latitude has the day arc of the degree it stands in. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.53}.

## Why the degree's own arc
The text gives as the reason that a star without latitude runs exactly as the Sun does, on the ecliptic itself, so that its rising and setting do not differ from those of its degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.53}.
