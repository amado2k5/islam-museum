## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan calls 'life management and time organisation' the most important subject he has ever taught; ordering one's life is the fourth generation of time management — one does not begin by scheduling the day but by ordering life {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:26:00}.

## Choosing a field
The first step is choosing a single field for one's life — not necessarily an academic specialisation — holding that whoever works two fields weakens his output; he cites Khalid ibn al-Walid, whose field was one: warfare, recounting what is told of him — 'jihad kept me from the Qur'an' — when asked about his slips in reciting the short suras {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:50:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:52:00}.
The choice runs on three criteria gathered under 'rifq': desire — 'work in a field you do not love and you may produce, but you will not excel' — opportunity — he tells of a Kuwaiti student who specialised in forestry engineering and found no work — and ability — some fields demand a strong memory, a language or a fine voice, and he recounts turning a young man away from the qira'at specialisation because his voice would not serve it {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:24:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:29:00}.
The criteria are scored in a table: ten points per criterion per candidate field, filled column by column; a field that stands out by a wide margin is chosen; if scores are close and one's current field is among the leaders, do not switch — the numbers are not one-hundred-per-cent exact {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:30:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:33:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:34:30}.
His rule for the university student: one who discovers in the first or second year that his field lies elsewhere should switch even at the cost of a year; one who has reached the third or fourth should finish and switch at postgraduate level — he himself changed field after his doctorate {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:35:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:35:30}.
The highest investment of energy, to him, is when specialisation, job and volunteering align {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:22:30}.
His doctorate equation: whoever wants the theoretical life — research, authorship, teaching — takes the doctorate seriously and from a serious university; whoever wants the practical life finds it a waste of time and money that makes him 'overqualified'; he excepts two cases — the politician and the media presenter — suggesting they take a token doctorate from a 'struggling university' if found (the speaker's advice, labelled as his own view) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:43:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:44:30}.

## Life projects
After the field, a person sets three to ten great projects to give his life to — each needing fifteen to twenty years — written in measurable wording, to be proud of in this world and the next {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:49:30}.
His example is Abd al-Rahman al-Sumayt, who turned his whole life to serving Africa through the Direct Aid society (needs_source for the cited figures) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:48:30}.
The fifty-per-cent rule: the field takes fifty per cent of one's time, informal relationships, reading and output {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:52:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:54:30}.

## The roles of life
Beside the field stand roles, each with written, measurable goals: the faith and worship role — for the Qur'an he proposes three completions: a reading completion, a tafsir completion (even over years), and a completion extracting the sunan and laws which he estimates takes fifteen years; and he places Ghazali's Ihya' Ulum al-Din above Ibn al-Qayyim's Madarij al-Salikin for the worship of hearts {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:55:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:57:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 00:58:00}.
The social role in order: parents, then family and children, then kin, then friends; he faults the saying 'dads are for fun, moms mean business' — a father's time with his children is for transferring experience and guidance, not amusement only {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:00:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:02:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:03:00}.
The health role aims at reaching a level then keeping it — not endless improvement — through four elements: diet (the plate rule: half vegetables and fruit, a quarter starches and fats, a quarter proteins; and reducing fats, soft drinks, red meat and sweets), sleep (no fixed number of hours; fix the waking time not the sleeping time, and sleep only when drowsy), exercise (half an hour daily or an hour three times a week; swimming first, then trampoline, then running, walking the lowest acceptable), and a target weight (height in centimetres minus a hundred, for men) (the speaker's distillation of Western nutrition books; figures needs_source) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:12:30}.
The financial role with four goals: doubling income every three years, doubling assets every five years, saving twenty per cent of every revenue — except one whose weak income would force him to stint his family, supported by the hadith preferring the dinar spent on one's family — and a fixed charity he advises converting into endowments 'for the waqf is what built the Muslims' civilisation' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:14:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:18:00}.
And the skill-acquisition role: planning, leadership and delegation, decision-making, feasibility study, basic computer skills, improving one's Arabic, and mastering English — he states that eighty per cent of the world's research is in English (needs_source) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:18:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep05 01:19:00}.

## In "The Academy for Preparing Leaders": choosing a field and writing the plan
In the second and third lessons of [[work/leadership-development-academy-1-program|the Leaders' Academy]] al-Suwaidan calls managing one's life the most important subject he teaches, out of all his books and albums. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:02:00} The trainees fill in a printed worksheet as he speaks. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:25:30} He separates a field of life from a university major. He himself studied engineering, he says, but his field in life is not engineering. He adds that eighty per cent of American graduates work outside their degree. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:07:00} The greatest use of a young person's energy, he says, comes when study, job and volunteering all lie in the same field. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:10:00} He quotes [[person/stephen-covey|Stephen Covey]]: "To say no, you must have a bigger yes". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:14:00}

A field is chosen by three criteria, which together spell the Arabic word "rifq": desire (raghba), opportunity (fursa) and ability (qudra). {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:16:00} His aphorism on desire: "Whoever works at what he does well produces, but he will create only if he works at what he loves." {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:16:30} He tells the young not to wait to be employed but to make opportunities and projects. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:23:00} Each candidate field is scored on the three criteria. He then gives rules for reading the totals, including when a student should change major and when he should finish and move over at graduate level. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:25:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:36:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:37:00} For "a special few" he adds five more criteria for choosing projects: spread, continuity, depth of impact, monthly income and ownership. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:43:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:45:30} On ownership he quotes a Western maxim: "One percent ownership is better than a hundred percent management". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep02 00:52:30}

In the third lesson the plan takes shape. Each trainee writes three to ten life projects in his field. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:02:00} It is never too late, he says: [[person/izz-al-din-ibn-abd-al-salam|al-Izz ibn Abd al-Salam]] devoted himself to learning at fifty-one and died at eighty-three. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:06:00} He sets a rule for focus. One field gets half of one's time, relationships, reading and output. A second love becomes a hobby with about ten per cent. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:08:30} Beside the field come the roles of life: faith, worship, social, intellectual, health, financial and skills. In each, a minimum is reached and then raised. He likens this to the "aspiring soul" ascribed to [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:12:00} For the worship of the heart he recommends living with certain books, among them [[work/ihya-ulum-al-din|al-Ghazali's Ihya']]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:16:00} For the intellectual role his minimum is two books a month, three courses a year of fifteen hours each, regular conferences, specialist journals, and following two leading specialists. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:32:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:33:00} At the end the goals and roles are ranked and fitted on one page, and the same is done for five years and for one year. His motto: "focus". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep03 00:50:30}
