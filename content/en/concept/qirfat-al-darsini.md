## Nature
It is said to be from cinnamon, and it is said rather that it is of another kind. It is hard like cinnamon; some of it is not hard, some is striped, some is white, some crumbles quickly, and it is weaker than cinnamon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.643}
