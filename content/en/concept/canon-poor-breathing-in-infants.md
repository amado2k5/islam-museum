## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions poor breathing in a child and its treatment: oiling the roots of the ears and the base of the tongue, making it vomit, pressing the tongue down, dripping hot water into the mouth and a lick of flaxseed with honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.214}.

## Treatment
A child may suffer poor breathing, and then the roots of its ears and the base of its tongue should be oiled and it made to vomit; pressing its tongue down is also very useful; hot water is dripped into their mouths, and they are given a little flaxseed with honey to lick {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.214}.
