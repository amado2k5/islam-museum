## The root of it
The seven isti'la letters «خص ضغط قظ» are made heavy by the raising of the tongue, and the itbaq letters among them are the strongest in heaviness — "the letter of isti'la make heavy, and single out the itbaq as the strongest, like qal and al-asa", says Ibn al-Jazari — who in the chapter of tarqiq warns against making the alef heavy {cite:ar-wikisource}.

## The ra letters
The ra is made heavy when it carries fath or damm or is still after a fath or damm, and light "when it is kasr'ed, likewise after a kasr wherever it is still" — unless the kasr is incidental or an isti'la letter precedes it, in which case the Jazariyya records the disagreement at the places of "separation" (فرق) {cite:ar-wikisource}. The matn also lightens the ra's takrir when it is doubled.

## The lam of the divine name
The lam of the divine name is made heavy after fath or damm, "like Abd Allah", and light after kasr — a rule known to the readers and kept in their chapter of the lams {cite:ar-wikisource}.
