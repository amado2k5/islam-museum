## Summary
Ibn Sina says that in acute burning diseases the colour of urine leans toward saffron and fire, that deepening yellow shows rising heat and increasing clearness shows falling heat, and he treats blood-like, abundant and thin urine before the crisis {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.185}.

## Colour and heat
In acute burning diseases the colour of the water leans toward saffron and fire. If thinness is present it shows a degree of ripening that has begun but does not yet appear in the consistency. When the yellow deepens to the fiery limit, heat has increased greatly; if the urine grows clearer, heat is declining {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.185}.

## Bloody and abundant urine
In acute sanguine diseases urine like blood itself may come without any vein opening, showing excessive fullness of blood. If it is passed little by little with a stench it is a dangerous sign, for a rush of blood to the throat is feared. If passed abundantly it may be a good sign in acute and mixed fevers, since it is often a sign of crisis, unless it turns thin all at once before the time of crisis, when it signals a relapse, as it also does if it does not thin by degrees after the crisis {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.185}.
