## Nature
Pieces of wrinkled wood, about a finger thick, of varying length, astringent in taste and dull in colour, with a power like that of pomegranate rind. It is said to be brought from the desert. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.500}

## Properties
It is an astringent that, it is said, stops the movement of blood in all the organs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.500}

## Joints
It strengthens a loosened joint. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.500}

## Organs of digestion
It helps loosening of the stomach and the liver. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.500}

## Excretory organs
It holds back bleeding, and it holds back the flow of blood when drunk in boiled goat's milk. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.500}
