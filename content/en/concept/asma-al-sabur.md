## Overview
as-Sabur — The Patient. It is name number 99 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In people's lives
Among the Companions' stories of patience: Bilal ibn Rabah enduring torture in Mecca while repeating 'Ahad, Ahad', until Abu Bakr freed him. {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}
