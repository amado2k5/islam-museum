## Summary
In the lexicon ra's is the top of anything, and by metaphor the chief of a people. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8529}

## General ruling
Wiping the head in ablution is required by agreement of the jurists. A man in the state of ihram for hajj or umra may not cover his head, and whoever does owes a ransom. For prayer, a man is recommended to cover his head with a turban or the like, and a woman must cover her head. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8529} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8530}

The majority hold that the head of a person being punished by a fixed penalty or a discretionary one is not to be struck; Abu Yusuf disagrees and allows it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8530}
