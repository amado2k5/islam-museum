## The breath of the ages and the states
Four short chapters: one on the breaths of the ages; one on the breath of the full of food and those like him — pregnancy and dropsy ⁦(the pregnancy line carried on the book's wording and set out as a labelled line)⁩; one on the bather's breath in its two kinds; and one on the sleeper's — all as set out in the summary {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.314}
