## Summary
Ibn Sina says greasy sediment shows the melting of fat, flesh and body-butter, the most severe resembling liquid gold, and its source is judged from its quantity, mixing and separation. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.

## The source
If it is abundant and separate, suppose it comes from the kidney region through the melting of its fat; if less and closely mixed, from a more distant place; and a white piece like a pomegranate seed is from the kidney's fat. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.195}.
