## Noon and the hours
The hour points of Capricorn are joined to one another, and the hour points of Cancer to one another, by crossing lines on the marble, and each point of the first hour of Capricorn is joined to the first hour of Cancer. The count starts from the western side, and the first, second and third hours are written under each point of Capricorn up to the eleventh. It is not feasible to read from the dial more than the hours from the first to the eleventh, because the shadow stretches too far at the ends of the day. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.208-209}

## Fractions and calibration
The text says halves and thirds can be marked between the hours for any fraction that falls between them, and that this is not difficult. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.209}
