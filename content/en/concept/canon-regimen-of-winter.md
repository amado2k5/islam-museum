## Summary
In winter Ibn Sina increases toil and generosity of food; for a southerner exercise is raised and food reduced, winter bread is made of stronger wheat than summer bread, and vegetables such as cabbage, chard and celery are taken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.258-259}.

## Toil and bread
In winter toil is increased and food is spread generously, unless the person is a southerner, in which case exercise is increased and food reduced; the wheat of winter bread should be stronger and more compact than that of summer bread, and meats and roasts follow the same measure {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.

## Vegetables
His vegetables are such as cabbage, chard and celery, and the text names with them qatf, yamaniyya, purslane and chicory {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.258}.
