## Summary
The second section of the third teaching opens with urine and stool, in thirteen chapters, and its first chapter begins with the general signs of urine and the conditions for relying on them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.183}.

## Place in the work
After the pulse chapters of the first section of the third teaching of the second Fann, the second section deals with urine and stool in thirteen chapters, the first on the signs of urine in general and the second on its colours. Ibn Sina says one should not trust the ways of reading urine until certain conditions are kept, which the next entries set out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.183}.
