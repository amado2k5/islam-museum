## Summary
Ibn Sina mentions an abrasion that befalls a child's thigh, treated by dusting it with ground myrtle, iris root, rose, sa'd, barley flour or lentil flour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}.

## Treatment
A child may have an abrasion of the thigh, and ground myrtle, ground iris root, ground rose, sa'd, barley flour or lentil flour should be dusted on it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.220}. With this the third chapter, on children's diseases, ends.
