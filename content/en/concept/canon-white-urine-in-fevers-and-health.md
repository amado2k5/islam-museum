## Summary
Ibn Sina lists what white urine shows in acute fevers and in health: a fever shifting to a quartan, that leaden urine without sediment is bad and milky urine deadly, and that bile may have turned toward an organ or the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.187}.

## In fevers
If the urine is white throughout the fever it is likely to pass into a quartan. Leaden urine without sediment is very bad, and milky urine in an acute fever is deadly. Whiteness of urine in acute fevers, however it appears once the dye is gone, shows that bile has turned to an organ that will swell, or to a loose stool, and most often to the region of the head. So too if the urine is thin in a fever and then suddenly white, mental confusion is to come {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.187}.

## In health and the tallowy kind
If urine stays white in a state of health it shows lack of ripening. The tallowy kind, like oil, in acute fevers warns of death or of consumption {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.187}.
