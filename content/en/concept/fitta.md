## Nature
Dioscorides is reported as saying that it is the gum of a plant like the reed in form that grows in the land of Suriya, meaning al-Sham; some people call it makaniyun, and it is adulterated with resin and with the flour of chickpea and broad bean. It is of two kinds: a frothy one, light in weight and whiter, and the other denser and heavier. The book gives no identification here beyond the description {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Choice
The better of the two is the denser, like frankincense, which is pounded by hand, with not much wood in it and some seed of its plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree and drying in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Actions and properties
Its power is softening and dissolving, dispersing winds, and it is among the things that spoil flesh; it has heating, inflaming, drawing and dissolving in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Cosmetics
It removes lentigo spots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Swellings and pustules
It benefits scrofula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Wounds and ulcers
It is smeared with vinegar on milky ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Joints
It benefits fatigue, tetanus and cramp of the muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Organs of the head
It benefits headache and epilepsy, for if the epileptic smells it he revives; it benefits vertigo and at once benefits toothache and the eaten tooth; it benefits cold pains in the ear and dissolves their swellings and pains without harm, when put in iris oil, warmed and dropped in {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits asthma and chronic cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Excretory organs
It removes difficulty of urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it induces the menses strongly, brings out foetuses and causes them to be lost when carried, and benefits suffocation of the womb, given to drink with wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.649}

## Poisons and bites
It is an antidote of the poisons that are given to arrows, when given with wine, and for the poisons of snakes and scorpions; its smoke drives off vermin, and when a person anoints himself with it they do not come near him; it withstands every poison short of the resistance of sakbinaj {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Substitutes
Its substitute is sakbinaj {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}
