## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the nafs is formed when the ruh joins matter and gives it life, that matter and ruh each glorify God before they join, and that when they meet, desires stir and disorder begins {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.321}.

## Matter and ruh
He says that matter alone, before the ruh joins it, is subdued and obedient, while the ruh glorifies God. So one should not speak of a spiritual life and a material life as opposites, since both glorify God {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.321}. He holds that death takes the ruh to its world of subjection and the body to its world {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.321}.

## Witness of the senses
He holds that the senses bear witness on the Day because they are no longer at a person's service in disobedience. They are at his service in obedience, and when he uses them in disobedience they curse him {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.322}. See [[passage/quran-24-24|al-Nur 24]].

## Angelic ruh
He says the word ruh is used for the angelic ruh and for the ruh of revelation, which is pure and clear {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.322}. See [[passage/quran-91-7|al-Shams 7]] and [[passage/quran-42-52|al-Shura 52]].
