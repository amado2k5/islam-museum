## Nature
It is said to be shahsfarm al-hamahim; and in the editor's footnote daymaran is habaq al-ma' {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Temperament
Ibn Masawayh said: there is heat in it and it is dry in the second degree; many people say it is cold since none of hot temperament was harmed by its heat, rather al-hamam, cold in the first (as the word is printed); the sounder view is that its power is compounded of heat with coldness, and it may be that coldness predominates in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Actions and properties
Beneficial for those of hot temperament, especially when rose water is sprinkled on it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Ulcers
It is laid as a poultice on burning {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Organs of the head
Very beneficial for qala' and al-hamahim, and opening to obstructions of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}

## Excretory organs
Its seed is given for chronic diarrhoea; the way of preparing it and the quantities are not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.721}
