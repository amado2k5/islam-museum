## Summary
Al-Battani says that Ptolemy showed and made clear that uneven motions are seen and supposed in two ways: in one, a sphere is imagined for the planet whose centre is the centre of the zodiac, with a second, small sphere carried upon it that does not enclose the earth, its centre running on the circle of the great sphere while the planet moves in the small one; in the other, a further sphere is imagined whose centre is not the centre of the first and which cuts its circle in two places {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.71-72}.

## The meaning is the same
He says the meaning is the same in both: whether the sphere turns the planet or the planet turns upon it, the variation that appears is one. In the first picture the planet moves in the epicycle either toward the leading or the following direction, or the small sphere turns it, and this motion is the planet's own variation; in the second the planet is on the eccentric sphere. Al-Battani starts with the first and draws a figure for it, and then presents the second {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.72}. The two presentations are [[concept/zij-sabi-epicycle-model-for-the-suns-equation|the Sun's epicycle]] and [[concept/zij-sabi-eccentric-model-for-the-suns-equation|the Sun's eccentric sphere]].
