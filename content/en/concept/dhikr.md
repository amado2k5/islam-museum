## Overview
Ibn Rajab discusses *dhikr* (remembrance of God) in the fiftieth hadith, which closes his additions to the Forty, as the comprehensive principle to hold on to when the laws have multiplied. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.927}

## In Ibn Rajab's commentary
The text of the hadith is: "Let your tongue remain moist with the remembrance of God." In the hadith of Mu'adh: "that you die while your tongue is moist with the remembrance of God." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.927}

Ibn Rajab says that many of the virtues of remembrance were mentioned earlier, scattered through the book, and that here he mentions the excellence of persisting in it and doing it often. He cites the verses that command much remembrance and that praise the men and women who remember, with forgiveness and a great reward. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.927}

He holds that remembrance is the easiest and most honourable of deeds, and the nearest to God, since it needs no place, no ablution, and no turning toward the qibla. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.927}

## Remembrance in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons (episodes 2, 3, 6 and 9)
The Sheikh says remembrance covers every act done for God's sake, from the needle-maker's needle to the astronaut's flight, and lists its kinds: of the tongue, eye, ear, heart and limbs (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 01:16:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 01:17:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 01:17:30}
He holds that counting glorification on the fingers is better than the rosary, and ties the heart to the fingers (his view). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:14:00} He mentions that whoever turns away from God's remembrance has a straitened life (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:49:30}
He claims that a believer who performs ablution and recites a particular chapter sincerely in distress is relieved, a claim that could not be checked (needs_source). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep06 00:18:30}
