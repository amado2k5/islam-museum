## Summary
Al-Battani computes the parallax of the Moon together with the Sun in the altitude circle. He takes the Sun's parallax from the second table and adds it to the Moon's, or subtracts it according to whether the distance is the farthest or nearest; the total is the parallax used in observing. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.120}.

## The order of the tables
He enters the two results in the rows of the four tables that follow the table of the Sun, the third, fourth, fifth and sixth, and adds the matching minutes of each, scaled by their share of sixty. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.121}.
