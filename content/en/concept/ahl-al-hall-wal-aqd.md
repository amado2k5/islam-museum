## Definition
Ahl al-hall wa al-'aqd are the men of power among scholars and notables through whom the purpose of authority is achieved; the term comes from loosing and binding affairs. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3611}

## Related terms
- Ahl al-ikhtiyar: a group among them entrusted with choosing the imam; they may be all of them or some of them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3611}
- Ahl al-shura: their mark is learning, whereas the mark of ahl al-hall wa al-'aqd is power. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3611}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3612}

## Qualities
Justice that meets the conditions for testimony, knowledge by which the one entitled to the imamate is known, judgement and wisdom, a power that people follow, and sincerity toward the Muslims. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3612}

## Choosing the electors
- By the caliph naming them, as Umar named six. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3612}
- By presence, when the caliph names none: whoever is present among them may conclude the allegiance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3612}

## Their work
- Appointing the caliph, which is agreed upon. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3612}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}
- Renewing allegiance to a designated successor who was not qualified when he was designated. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}
- Summoning a designee who is absent, or appointing a deputy if the absence is long. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}
- Dismissing the imam when the cause arises. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}

## Number
- The Hanbalis require most of them in every town; a report from Ahmad says the imam is the one all agree upon. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}
- Another view: at least five, or one with the consent of four. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}
- The Hanafis and Shafi'is set no fixed number, and accept the appointment by a group of them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3613}
