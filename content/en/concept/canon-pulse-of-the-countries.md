## Summary
In the tenth chapter Ibn Sina measures the pulse of countries by the pulse of the seasons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.178}.

## Countries and their seasons
Some countries are temperate and spring-like, some hot and summer-like, some cold and winter-like, some dry and autumn-like, so the rulings of the pulse in them follow what has been learned of the pulse of the seasons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.178}.

## The rule
In this chapter Ibn Sina names no country; he gives the rule that the physician applies according to the climate of the place {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.178}.
