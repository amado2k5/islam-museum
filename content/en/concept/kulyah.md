## Nature and selection
Known. The best as food is the kid's kidney {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.530}.

## Temperament
Moderate toward dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.530}.

## Actions and properties
Its humour is poor; the best is the kid's kidney {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.530}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Hard to digest, greasy, slow to descend {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.530}.
