## Nature
The book says this plant is also called wild abrun and wild purslane. Its stalk and leaves resemble those of purslane. From the base of each leaf grow branches with six or seven small shoots, and when rubbed a sticky moisture appears. It has a white flower and grows between vines. The editor's note calls it the herb of leprosy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}

## Temperament
Cold and moist. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}

## Adornment and leprosy
A leaf poultice on leprosy, left on for a time and followed by barley flour, is said to be a good treatment. Ground and applied to vitiligo, then left in the sun to dry and wiped off, it heals greatly, the book says. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.507}
