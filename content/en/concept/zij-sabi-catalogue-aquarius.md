## Aquarius

The third fixed-star table gives the figure of al-Dalw, the Bucket, under its own heading and lists its stars in order along the outflow of water: the first, the middle and the last. Their positions in the longitude and latitude table are not transcribed here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.279}
