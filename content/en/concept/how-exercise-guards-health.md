## Summary
Ibn Sina holds that exercise is the surest check on the gathering of the first causes of fullness: it revives the innate heat, accustoms the body to lightness, raises a gentle heat that dissolves each day's surplus, hardens joints and tendons, readies the organs to take food, and whoever leaves it often falls into wasting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.221-222}.

## How it stops residues gathering
Exercise is the surest check on the gathering of the first causes of fullness when the rest of the regimen is right, because it raises a gentle heat that dissolves what gathered of each day's surplus, and the movement helps to slide it toward its outlets, so that over the days no sizeable surplus collects {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.221}.

## What it does for the organs
It builds up the innate heat and hardens joints and tendons, so the body is strong in acting and safe from being acted on; it prepares the organs to receive food by reducing their surplus, so the drawing faculty stirs, the knots in the organs loosen, the organs soften, the moistures thin and the pores widen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.221-222}.

## The harm of neglecting it
Often the person who gives up exercise falls into wasting, because the organs' powers weaken once the movement that brings them the innate spirit, the instrument of each organ's life, is dropped {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.222}.
