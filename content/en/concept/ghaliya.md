## Nature
A known drug; the editor's note says it is a compound drug {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## Swellings and pustules
It softens hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## Organs of the head
It is dripped into the aching ear, and smelling it benefits the epileptic and revives him, and likewise the maskut; it calms cold headache, and when some of it is put in the drink (sharab) it intoxicates {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## Organs of the chest
Smelling ghaliya gladdens the heart {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## Womb, menses and conception (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It is beneficial for cold pains of the womb and for its hard and phlegmatic swellings, it promotes the menses, it brings down the womb that is suffocated or tilted, cleanses it and prepares it greatly for conception; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}
