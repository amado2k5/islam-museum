## Nature and selection
Nature: there is a mountain kind and a garden kind. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Temperament: hot and dry in the third degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}

## Actions
Actions: subtle like thyme. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: drinking it improves the complexion, and washing the face with it clears marks on the face. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Swellings and pustules: it dissolves hard swellings when drunk with wine. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Head organs: boiled in vinegar it calms toothache; the vapour of its decoction with figs helps ringing in the ear when taken through a funnel. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Eye organs: boiled and then applied as a poultice, it helps the eyelid complaint called tarfa and blood that has gone dead under the lid. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Chest organs: it helps the chest and the lung, asthma and orthopnoea, and gargling with it also helps. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Digestion organs: with fig and borax it is a poultice for the spleen, and drunk it helps the spleen and dropsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
Excretory organs: it purges phlegm, the seeds of the gourd (habb al-qara') and worms; mixed with qirdmana and irsa its purge is stronger. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.457}
