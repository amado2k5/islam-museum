## Summary
Raghaib is the plural of raghiba, a great gift or a good deed urged upon the believer; for the Malikis it is what the Law favours without its being done in congregation, and it may name the Fajr sunna. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8795} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8797} The Hanafis and Shafi'is hold that the special prayer on the first Friday of Rajab or on mid-Sha'ban, with a fixed manner or number, is a reprehensible innovation, and the traditions for it are judged fabricated. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8796} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8797} For the Malikis the raghiba is the Fajr sunna, which the Hanafis count among the strongest sunnas and the Shafi'is and Hanbalis among the regular sunnas. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8797}
