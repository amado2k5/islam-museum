# Islamophobia (the phobia of Islam)

## The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode — debating the phenomenon and the term
Tareq al-Suwaidan devoted the first episode of his panel programme 'Al-Wasatiyya' to the question: is the West's fear of Islam a real widespread phenomenon or an inflated phantom? {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:01:00} The audience vote split 51% real versus 49% limited and media-inflated {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:05:30}. Adel Darwish distinguished the clinical 'phobia' — absent, he reported, from British and American medical dictionaries — from the press-political phenomenon, noting the Runnymede Trust coined the term in the context of racism and cultural pluralism {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:10:00}.

## On its spread — three positions
Darwish held it is not widespread: publics are more susceptible than governments which understand the equation, and Western intellectuals — including Jews — signed statements against it, likening it to antisemitism {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:13:30}. Oraib al-Rantawi called it a 'manufacture' inflated by power centres and elites for conflict agendas, rising after the Cold War when political Islam ceased to be an implicit ally against communism {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:24:00}. A physician-researcher called it a growing sickness that demonises the Muslim and strips his humanity — citing the post-First-Crusade committee's description 'an odious and evil people, fit only for extermination', NATO secretary-general Werner's naming Islam the next enemy, and his daily experience of trainees posting Abu Hamza al-Masri's image in a British hospital as 'Islam' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:25:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:26:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:29:00}.

## On its causes — the vote's surprise
The audience result surprised the host: 55% judged 'Jewish fingers' the greatest cause, 25% the conduct of some Muslims, 20% the historical legacy {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:32:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:32:30}. Emad Hussein separated old hatred (the historical stock) from a fear that resurfaced as Ottoman armies approached central Europe and with the Islamic awakening; he cited a conference finding every Western curriculum maligning Islam {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:36:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:36:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:37:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:37:30}. Ali al-Sawa argued Islam's comprehensive theory of life naturally collides with other ideologies, drawing shock and counter-propaganda, while irresponsible acts by some Muslims 'fuel' rather than originate it {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:40:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:41:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:42:00}. Nur Issa traced an old defamation in English literature — over 1200 years in fiction and poetry — depicting the Muslim as a sword-bearing sensualist {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:43:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:44:30}.

## The media — and Arab media
The host cited American statistics: only 2% know what Islam is, 10% believe Muslims worship the moon, and 25% that Islam teaches violence {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:46:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:46:30}. The audience judged Arab media 'noticeably improving' by 57% against 43% seeing it still weak — a shift the host noted would have differed ten years earlier {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:49:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep01 00:50:00}.
