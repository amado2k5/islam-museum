## Summary
In the chapter 'the prohibition of torturing a cat and the like of an animal that does no harm' in Sahih Muslim, Ibn Umar and Abu Hurayra transmit from the Prophet that a woman was punished because of a cat she had shut up until it died; she entered the Fire, since she neither fed it nor gave it drink while she held it, nor let it go to eat from the vermin of the earth {cite:sahih-muslim ch-taḥrīm-taʿdhīb-al-hirra}.

## Significance
The hadith is the basis for the duty to care for an animal in one's keeping, and for the view that withholding food and drink from a confined animal is a sin.
