## Nature
The land one is known; the mountain one is the dildil, with arrow-like spines, near the land one in nature; the sea one is a kind of shelled fish {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Actions and properties
Its fat keeps matters from pouring to the viscera, and so does its dried liver; in the ash of the land and the sea one is a cleansing, a dissolving and a drying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Cosmetics
The salted flesh of the land hedgehog benefits elephantiasis; the flesh of the land one benefits leprosy for its strong dissolving and drying; the burnt skin of the land hedgehog, mixed with pitch, benefits alopecia {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Swellings and pustules
The skin of the sea hedgehog benefits in medicines for itch, and its flesh is very beneficial for scrofula {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Wounds and ulcers
The ash of its skin benefits dirty ulcers and consumes excess flesh, and its flesh is very beneficial for scrofula and hard knots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Joints
The salted flesh of the land one benefits palsy, spasm, all diseases of the nerve and elephantiasis {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Respiratory organs
The flesh of the land hedgehog benefits consumption {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
The flesh of the land one benefits bad temperament, its salted form with sakanjabin is good for dropsy, and so is its liver dried in the sun on a cloth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Excretory organs
The sea hedgehog is good for the stomach, softens the belly and drives urine; the salted flesh of the land hedgehog with sakanjabin benefits pain of the head and kidneys; its flesh benefits boys who wet the bed, though persisting in eating it may make urination difficult {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Fevers
The flesh of the land one benefits chronic fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}

## Poisons and bites
Its flesh benefits the bites of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.659}
