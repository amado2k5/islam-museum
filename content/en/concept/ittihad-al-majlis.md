## Summary
Unity of the session is the single sitting in which a word or an act takes place. It is wider than the seat, since it can hold while standing and when place or posture changes. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1475} Hanafis distinguish a real session, where the parties move more than two steps, from a constructive session, where the custom treats an act as breaking it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1477}

## Contracts and exchange
Hanafis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis require one session for a contract to form, and all schools agree that turning away from an offer ends it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1478} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1479} Exchange of ribawi goods must be made hand to hand within the session. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1479} The three schools require the capital of a salam contract to be handed over in the session, while the Malikis allow a delay of three days. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1480} On marriage there are three views: one session without immediacy, immediacy, or validity despite separation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1481}

## Worship
On renewing ablution in one session there are three views: disliked, recommended once, or disliked when repeated many times. Most jurists recommend renewal before each prayer whatever the session. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1476} For prostration of recitation, Malikis and Hanbalis repeat it with each occurrence of the verse, while Hanafis do not repeat it when session and verse are the same. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1476}

## Divorce, ila and zihar
Repeating divorce in one session with intent to repeat yields three divorces in the view of the four schools; when the words are repeated without qualification the usual result is three among the Hanafis, Malikis and Hanbalis. Ibn Abbas and others held that triple divorce pronounced at once counted as one in the early period. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1484} Repeated oaths of ila in one session are one oath for the Hanafis when intended as emphasis. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1486} In zihar, the expiation is not multiplied by repetition in one session among most jurists unless renewal is intended. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1486}

## Ihram
Expiations for prohibitions in ihram overlap only when they are of one kind. A repeated act in one place and in one continuous session gives one fidya in the majority view. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1483}

## Where it is discussed
Each chapter of the law treats its own session rules; the cause-unity question is treated under ittihad al-sabab. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1475}
