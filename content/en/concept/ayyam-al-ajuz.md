## Overview
Seven days at the end of February and the start of March, when the embers fall, are called by the Arabs *Sinn*, *Sinbar*, *Wubar*, *Amir*, *Mu'tamir*, *Mu'allal*, and *Mutfi' al-jamr* (the extinguisher of embers). They are the last days of very great cold before the year turns with the sun's entry into Aries in the middle of March. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.506}
