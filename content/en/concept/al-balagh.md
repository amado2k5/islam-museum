## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that the Prophet is a bringer of good news and a warner, charged with delivering the message; guidance is in God's hand, and no one is compelled to believe. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.559} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.560}

## Good news and warning
Good news, he explains, is the report of something that will please when its time comes; warning is the report of something that will grieve, which one may still avert. Good news goes to the believer and warning to the disbeliever, and both presuppose a message that is delivered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.559}

## Not answerable for the people of the Fire
Sha'rawi holds that the Prophet is not responsible for those who throw themselves into the Fire; his task is delivery. He says that had God wished to force belief He would have compelled all creation, and that He wants hearts that love Him, not hearts forced into belief. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.559} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.560}

## Limits of this block
