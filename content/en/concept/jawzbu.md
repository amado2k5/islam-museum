## Nature and selection
It is a nut the size of a gall-nut, easy to break, thin-shelled, fragrant and sharp {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.416}. The editor's footnote gives it as nutmeg {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.416}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: Masih says hot and dry at the end of the second to the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.416}. Actions: it is astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.416}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: clears freckles and sweetens the breath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.416}.
