## Nature and selection
A plant that resembles the gourd. The book says that al-Khuz know it by this name {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}. The editor's note identifies it as Abutilon avicennae, called sheep's thorn {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament for it in this entry.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: it is said to be the most useful thing for fresh wounds, since placed on them it closes and knits them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}.
