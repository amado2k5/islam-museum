## Nature
A stone brought from the land of Abyssinia, tending to yellow. Rubbed from it, it gives a sharp rubbing for the tongue, like milk. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}

## Organs of the eye
It helps a film over the eye when no swelling is with it, helps ophthalmia, helps the marks left by ulcers in the eye, and helps the soft zufra, an eye membrane. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}
