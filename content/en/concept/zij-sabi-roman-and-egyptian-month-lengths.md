## Summary
Al-Battani lists the Roman months as the Greeks and the people of Egypt use them, from Aylul to Ab, gives the days of each, and says the year is intercalated every fourth year, when February takes an extra day. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.100}.

## Intercalation and the Roman year
He holds that the Roman year, counting the months and the intercalation, comes to 365 and a quarter days, and that the leap year has twenty-nine days in February. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.100}.
