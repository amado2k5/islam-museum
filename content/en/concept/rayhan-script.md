## Summary
Rayhan is one of the [[concept/al-aqlam-al-sitta|six pens]]; for Maryam Ekhtiar it is a smaller variant of [[concept/muhaqqaq-script|muhaqqaq]], with which it forms a large–small pair. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 25} {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 26}

## Uses
The author notes that rayhan, with [[concept/tawqi-script|tawqi']] and [[concept/riqa-script|riqa']], was preferred for chancery documents and colophons, and to embellish book covers and carpet pages. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 26} She also lists it among the scripts that emerged for administrative use in the chanceries. {cite:ekhtiar-how-to-read-islamic-calligraphy-2018 p. 26}
