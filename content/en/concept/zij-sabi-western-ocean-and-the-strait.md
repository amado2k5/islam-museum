## Summary
Al-Battani says of the western ocean Oceanus, the encircling ocean, that only its western and northern parts are known and no ships run in it; that its six islands opposite Abyssinian land are the Inhabited Islands, also called the Islands of the Blessed; that another island opposite al-Andalus called Gadira lies at the strait from which it flows to the sea of the Romans, seven miles wide between al-Andalus and Tangier; and that in its north lie twelve British islands. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.30}.

## What is known and what is not
The text treats this sea as one of which only the parts toward the west and north are known; beyond that it runs far from any settlement, and no one knows what it is like or what is in it. It is one more case of his keeping the known and the unknown apart, and the island names are given as the scan reads them, some of them uncertain. See [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]]. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.30}.
