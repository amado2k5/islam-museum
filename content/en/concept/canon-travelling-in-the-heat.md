## Summary
Ibn Sina gives a chapter to guarding against heat on a journey: if travellers do not order themselves, they weaken, their powers dissolve, thirst overcomes them and the sun harms their brains; so they take care to cover the head, protect the chest with a coating of psyllium mucilage and purslane juice, and take barley meal or fruit drink before setting out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.262}.

## Head and chest
They must take great care to cover the head from the sun, and the traveller guards his chest and coats it with such as psyllium mucilage and purslane juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.262}.

## Before setting out, and oils
Travellers in heat need something to take before riding, such as barley meal and fruit drink, since if they ride with empty bowels dissolution weakens them greatly; they then wait until it passes down from the stomach and no longer sloshes; they bring rose and violet oil to apply every so often to the crown of the head, and if harm from travel in the heat befalls them they recover by swimming in cold water, though it is sounder not to hurry into it but to proceed by degrees {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.262}.
