## Summary
The hadith «Shoot, sons of Isma'il, for your father was an archer» is recorded in Bukhari's index with a report from Salama ibn al-Akwa'. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari p.1207}

In Tirmidhi's Virtues chapter, Ali reports that on the day of Uhud the Prophet told Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas «Shoot; may my father and mother be your ransom», and Tirmidhi grades it hasan sahih. {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi p.853}

A prize in a race is allowed only for arrows, hoof and nasl, on the hadith «there is no prize except in hoof, hoof or nasl». {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229}
