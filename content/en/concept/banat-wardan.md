## Nature and selection
Beetles and cockroaches {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}. The editor's footnote gives the sense as beetles and cockroaches.

## Temperament and actions
No separate temperament is given in this entry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: helps pains of the womb and kidneys after its dissolving is broken with oil, wax and egg yolk; moves urine and the menses; the book's text also says it expels [the foetus] {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}. With qardumana it helps haemorrhoids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Fevers: helps the shivering fit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Poisons: helps poisons of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

Substitutes: its substitute is the qaysur {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.
