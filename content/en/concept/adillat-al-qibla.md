## Overview
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari devoted a fourth chapter of his *Masalik al-absar* to the qibla and the signs by which it is known. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 pp. 11, 128-129}

## Methods of orientation
Al-Umari arranges the determination of the direction of the Ka'ba by four signs: the stars, then the winds, then the mountains, then the rivers. He describes them as the methods relied on by the people of the cities before precise astronomical constructions. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 pp. 11, 128-129}

The details of each sign are not recorded here and would need a further source.
