## Summary
Khul\' is lexically drawing off or stripping; for the Hanafis it is taking money from the wife in return for the marriage bond, by the word khul\'; for the majority it is separation for a consideration due by the wife, by the word talaq or khul\'. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7758}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7759} It is near to reconciliation (sulh, ending a quarrel), to divorce (lifting the marriage tie), to ransom (fidya, the sum paid to be free), to mubara\'a (another name for it, in which the wife drops her claims on the husband), and to annulment (faskh, dissolving the contract). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7759}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7760} Is khul\' divorce or annulment? The Hanafis in the chosen view, the Malikis, the Shafiis in the new view and the Hanbalis in one report hold it divorce; the Shafii in the old view and the Hanbalis in the best-known report hold it annulment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7760}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7761} Those who call it divorce say it is a final single divorce, and if the husband intends more, more falls, by the Malikis, Shafiis, Hanbalis and Zufar; the Hanafis count three if he intends three. Those who call it annulment cite the verse on two divorces then the khul\', then the divorce after it, and the report of the wife of Thabit ibn Qays who was told to wait one menstrual period. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7761}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7762} Results of the two views: one who has khul\'ed twice and marries her again is free to do so on the annulment view; the Hanafis and Malikis treat khul\' as an exchange on the wife\'s side and an oath on the husband\'s, the Malikis, Shafiis and Hanbalis as an exchange on both sides. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7763} Ruling: khul\' is lawful in general; the Hanbalis divide it into three: lawful when the wife hates the marriage and fears she cannot keep its duties, when the husband is encouraged to accept; disliked without cause while the marriage is sound; forbidden when the wife is harmed to make her pay. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7764}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7765}
