## Summary
Ibn Sina lists what wards off the harm of changing waters: water with wine counters its corruption, water is mixed with vinegar when scant, salty water is drunk with vinegar or oxymel with carob, myrtle seed and hawthorn thrown in, alum-like astringent water is followed by what softens the bowels, bitter water by fatty and sweet things, thick turbid water by garlic and cleared by Yemeni alum, and onion is a theriac against the changing waters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.265-266}.

## Wine, vinegar and salty water
Drinking water with wine wards off its corruption if the fault is of the kind that lacks penetration; when water is scant it is drunk mixed with vinegar, especially in summer, which spares drinking much; salty water is drunk with vinegar or oxymel, and carob, myrtle seed and hawthorn are thrown into it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.265}.

## Alum-like, bitter and stagnant water
After alum-like astringent water one drinks whatever softens the bowels, and wine helps too; with bitter water fatty and sweet things are used and it is mixed with julep; chickpea water drunk before it, and eating chickpeas, wards off its harm; with standing marshy water one uses astringent cold fruits and vegetables such as quince, apple and rhubarb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.265-266}.

## Turbid water, onion and the water of home
With thick turbid water one takes garlic, and Yemeni alum clears it; onion wards off the harm of changing waters and is a theriac for them, above all onion with vinegar, and garlic too; a good regimen for one who moves between different waters is to carry some water of his own land and mix it with the next water he meets, taking from each halt's water for the next and mixing it, until he reaches his goal, and likewise to carry the clay of his own land, mixing it into whatever comes, shaking it and leaving it to clear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.266}.

## The strainer and sour syrups
The water should be drunk through a strainer so that leeches are not swallowed by mistake nor foul residues gulped down; and carrying sour fruit syrups to mix with every one of the changing waters is a good regimen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.266}.
