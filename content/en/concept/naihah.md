## Summary
In the lexicon, a na'iha is a woman who weeps over the dead and recounts their virtues. The jurists hold that she is disciplined and held until she repents. A report related by al-Awza'i says that Umar struck a mourning woman, saying she troubles the dead in their graves and the living in their houses, forbids patience which God commands, and urges despair which God forbids. Al-Shayzari says the market inspector disciplines a wailing woman and bars her from funerals and graves. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15427}
