## Summary
Naqi, in the lexicon, is a drink of raisins, dates or other fruit steeped in water without boiling. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16028}

## Ruling
The Malikis and Shafi'is forbid it once it has fermented; the Hanafis dislike it as forbidden; the Hanbalis forbid it once it has fermented and boiled, even if it does not intoxicate. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16028}
