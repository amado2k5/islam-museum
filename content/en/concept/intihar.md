## Summary
Al-Sha'rawi reads "do not kill yourselves" as forbidding a person to take his own life or to bring himself to ruin, and ties the prohibition to trust in God. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2146}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2148}

## Trust in God
He holds that a person whose means are exhausted should not despair, because a believer does not isolate himself from his Creator, and he cites Moses' words at the sea that his Lord is with him. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2147}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2148}

## The report he cites
He cites a report about one who hastens his own death by taking his life, that God forbids him paradise, and names its source in the book's notes without reproducing the full wording here. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2147}

## Killing others
He holds that the prohibition covers believers killing one another, since they are one faith community, and that retaliation is life. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2148}{cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p. 2149}
