## Summary
Nasl means offspring or progeny. The jurists use it of the young of a human or an animal, and of the foetus. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15682} Offspring is one of the aims of the Law and one of the necessities to be preserved, since the human kind depends on it for its continuation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15682} The jurists drew rulings from this on marriage, coitus interruption, castration, abortion, harm, usurpation, mortgage, waqf and forward sale. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15683} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15684} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15685} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15686}

## General ruling
Multiplying offspring is encouraged. Castration is forbidden because it cuts off offspring, and the jurists differ on coitus interruption. Abortion is forbidden after ensoulment. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15683} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15684} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15685}

## Where it is discussed
The effect of offspring on waqf, mortgage and forward sale is treated under those headings; coitus interruption and abortion are treated in their own entries. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15683} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15685}
