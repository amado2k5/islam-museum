## Summary
Ibn Sina says that someone who wants to fatten enters the bath after eating if he is safe from blockages, someone who wants dissolving and thinning bathes fasting and sits long in it, someone who only wants to keep health enters after the stomach has digested, and the hot-tempered, bilious person is advised bread soaked in fruit water or rose water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.226}.

## By aim
Whoever seeks to put on flesh should enter the bath after eating, if he is safe from blockages. Whoever wants dissolving and thinning should bathe fasting and sit long in it. Whoever wants only to keep health should enter after what is in the stomach is digested, and if he bathes on an empty stomach he should take something light beforehand {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.226}.

## The hot-tempered
The hot-tempered, bilious person may find no way around this, and for one like him entering the hot room is forbidden. The best thing for these people to busy themselves with is bread soaked in fruit water or rose water {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.226}.
