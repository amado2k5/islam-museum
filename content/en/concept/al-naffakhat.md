## Its definition and differences
Watery bladders may arise in some of the cornea's coats — four by some, three by the rest — the water congesting between two of these coats, their sites necessarily differing; they differ by increase and decrease in size, by their quality, their colour and consistency, and by their mildness, sharpness and eating; the deepest is the worst; the one toward the first coat shows black; the sunken one bars perception of the uvea, being farther than the ray's reach, and is seen as white; the copious sharp watery one is bad, hurting by both distending and eating, and the deeper it is the wider its distension and eating spread; the part level with the rest harms vision, especially if it eats and ulcerates {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.176} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.177}

## Its treatment
While small it is treated with the drying drugs ⁦(the compound's recipe and measures are not carried)⁩; when large it is treated by iron — lancing with the scalpel; the book says: I have treated with the scalpel one afflicted with this disease, and the water gathered under the cornea came out and the cornea's surface levelled ⁦(a witnessed report in the book, transmitted as the author's own)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.177}
