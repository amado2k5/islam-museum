## Summary
If the distance of the Moon's degree from the ascendant is less than ninety degrees, the parallax in longitude is added to the Moon's true place; if more, it is subtracted, because the Moon is then nearer the eastern or western horizon as the case may be. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.126}.

## The case near mid-heaven
When the Moon is within an hour or less of mid-heaven, the parallax in longitude is small, and in the hour after noon it is less than in the first hour; there the longitudinal parallax almost disappears. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.126}.
