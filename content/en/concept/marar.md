## Nature
It is the mountain cypress, some of it small and some large {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Temperament
It leans to heat and dryness, and its fruit is hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Actions and properties
It is warming, thinning and dispersing, and its fruit also has astringency, which is not found in the other parts of its tree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Joints
It is good for a crushing of the muscle {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.609}

## Organs of the chest
It is good for pains of the chest and for cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It cleanses and opens obstructions in the stomach and liver, is good for the stomach when drunk, and is very useful for flatulence in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Poisons and bites
It wards off the harm of the stings of vermin, and fumigation with either kind, from any part of their trees, drives away vermin and flies {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as promoting the urine and the menses, and as good for suffocation of the womb and its pains {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.610}
