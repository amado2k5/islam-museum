## Burnt bile
As for that from burnt bile, the bad and deadly one, the first of its signs is that its common symptoms come with madness and vexation, a large breath and fiddling; their eyes are turbid, and it resembles sabar, as printed, or is as if it were it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80}

## Signs of transfer
If it is passing into lethargus, which is the more hopeful for them, you see the eye sink in, the closing of the eyes persist, the saliva run, and the pulse slow and soften {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80} The signs of its passing into safaqulus and the cerebral swelling are that the sign of safaqulus appears, the black of the eye disappears and the white shows at times, he refuses to lie except on his back, his belly swells, his hypochondria stretch and the trembling of his organs is frequent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80} The sign of its passing into consumption is sinking of the eyes, a calming of the fever, a drying of the body and a small, hard pulse; the signs of its passing into convulsion he has already given in the chapter on convulsion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.80}
