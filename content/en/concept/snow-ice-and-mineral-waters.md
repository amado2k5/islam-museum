## Summary
Ibn Sina says waters mixed with a mineral substance, and leech waters, are all poorer, though some have benefits. Snow and ice, when pure and not mixed with a bad power, are fit, whether melted into water, used to cool water from outside, or dropped into it; but they are coarser than other waters and harm one with nerve pain, and when boiled they return to being fit {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.137}.

## Kinds
Water in which the power of iron prevails strengthens the viscera and rouses the appetites. Alum water helps against the flow of menstrual surplus, the spitting of blood and the flow of haemorrhoids, but strongly provokes fever in bodies prone to it. Iron water assists sexual capacity, and copper water suits a corrupted temperament. When different waters are mixed, the strongest prevails {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.137-138}.
