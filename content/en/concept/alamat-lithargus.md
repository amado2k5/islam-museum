## The phlegmatic kind
The sign: a light headache and a soft fever, for fever must be in every swelling from a putrid humour, and by this it is told from coma, but the fever is soft because the matter is phlegmatic, and this fever may not be felt {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.84} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} With it a heavy coma, so that whenever its sufferer opens his eye he shuts it, and there is forgetfulness and a loose, very slow and weak breath with slight tightness and spittle, much yawning and opening and closing of the mouth, and his mouth may stay open after the yawn or the like, from forgetting that it should be closed or from laziness about it, though he wants it; with hiccup from sharing of the stomach, whiteness of the tongue, laziness in answering and in moving the lids, and confusion of mind; the stool is for the most part moist, and if it dries it dries moderately, and the urine is like the urine of donkeys {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85}

## How it differs from qaranitus and dhat al-ri'a
Trembling and sweating of the extremities may come upon them, and unlike the sufferers of qaranitus they have splitting headache; the pulse is large, spaced, slow, tremulous and wavy, closer to the pulse of dhat al-ri'a but less in breadth and length, slower, more spaced and less varying, because the heart is less hurt by it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} More of its pulse falls in the middle, because the vital power in it is safer, and the fever with it is less from its distance from the heart, and its coma is greater because the matter here is in the brain itself, whereas in dhat al-ri'a it rises from the swelling of the lung {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85}

## The black-bilious kind and the place of the illness
As for what is called lithargus when it is black-bilious, its sign is that the pain is stronger and vexation and raving come with it, and the eye is open and stunned {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} When the lithargus is in the substance of the brain the coma is stronger, the movements harder, the whiteness of the tongue very strong, the eye inclined to bulging and hard movement, and the pain toward slackness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} If it is in the membrane the pain is stronger and the movements lighter, and retention of urine often falls in it, from forgetfulness and from weakness of the urinating muscles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85}

## Outlook
Among the signs of a person's passing into lethargus is frequent twitching of his head with laziness and heaviness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85} When the symptoms of lithargus grow strong and sweat becomes very abundant, it is deadly, for the sweat drops the power; when the breath widens and improves and the symptoms decline, it is toward safety, especially if swellings appear behind the ear, for many of its crises come by them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.85}
