## Her definition and cause
It is an instrumental disorder in the face by which a side of the face is drawn toward an unnatural direction, so its natural shape changes and the good meeting of the lips and eyelids of one side is lost; its cause is either a relaxation or a convulsion of the muscles of the eyelids and face, and you have known both and known their origins {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}

## The relaxed and the convulsive
What arises from relaxation: if one side inclines it draws the second side with it, relaxing it and changing its shape if strong, or relaxing alone if weak; what arises from convulsion — the majority — because when one side convulses it draws the second side to it, its cause being the cause of convulsion, and the first kind's cause is the cause of palsy, with evacuations from deviant vomiting and bad nosebleed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}

## The dispute over the afflicted side
Some hold that the relaxation is in the sound side, it being what draws the crooked — not to be relied on, Paulus among them; some said the diseased side in laqua is the one seen as sound while the cause is in the other and the healthy side tries to draw it level — this is not sound in most cases; anatomy and what you know of the state of the face's muscles shows you the falsity of this generally, for sensation lapses with it in those of them in whom it lapses on the side of the laqua {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}

## What accompanies it
Many a man is struck by a swelling in the muscles of the suffocating parts and is taken by laqua from it; a palsy reaching the hands also strikes them, for the nerve that waters the hand muscles with motive power is likewise rooted in the neck's vertebrae; every laqua extending six months is fit not to be hoped for repair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.154}
