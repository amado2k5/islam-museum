## Summary
Al-Battani holds that knowing this parallax matters chiefly for solar eclipses, and that they cannot be fully grasped without knowing its amount at every point of the horizon. Lunar eclipses need no such correction, since the Earth's shadow is the same for every observer. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.115}.

## Solar and lunar eclipses compared
He says a lunar eclipse happens when the Earth screens the Sun from the Moon, while a solar eclipse is the Moon screening the Sun, so it depends on where the observer stands on the Earth; this is why solar eclipses need the parallax correction. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.115}.
