## The short voice
He must graduate into lengthening the breath, as by lengthening the stay in the hot bath and in all that calls for the breath and hastens it; let him hold his breath and do all that, exercise and bathe, and after leaving the bath he must drink the drink, for the drink is more nourishing to the spirit, and likewise after food, and let it be much at one breath; and sleep benefits them (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.326}
