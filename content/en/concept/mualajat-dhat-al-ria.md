## The treatments of peripneumonia
And if the nature in it is bound they must be given once every two days of a drink of this kind; if the nature is lax to weakening, robs and astringents of this kind are given (a line on the book's wording, the names excluded per the rule) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.363}
