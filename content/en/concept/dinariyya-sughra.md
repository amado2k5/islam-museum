## Summary
The Lesser Dinar is a named problem in inheritance that jurists set in an estate going to seventeen women: three wives, two grandmothers, four maternal sisters and eight full or paternal sisters. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8361}
Its base is twelve, raised by awl to seventeen; it is called the Dinar case because a deceased left seventeen dinars and each heiress received one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8362}
It has other names, including the seventeen-case, the mother of widows, the mother of wombs and al-Minbariyya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8362}
