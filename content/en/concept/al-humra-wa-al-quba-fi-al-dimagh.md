## Nature and signs
Erysipelas and al-quba may also come in the brain itself, the pain and the inflammation being intense, but the face turns cold from the hiding of the heat, and small for that reason, especially the eye, and then warms all at once and reddens; for the most part it tends to yellowness and cold, the dryness in the mouth is intense, and there is not with it the coma that there is in the phlegmon, but the symptoms in it are more dreadful and the fever stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83}

## Outcome and in children
Its treatment is the treatment of sabari, and most of it kills in the third, and if it does not kill he escapes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83} Erysipelas in the brain comes to children, and with it the fontanelle and the eyes sink, the eye yellows and the whole body dries {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83} The remedies and applications mentioned there are left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.83}
