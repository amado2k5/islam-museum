## Summary
Ibn Sina states that the amount of a drug is known by artful conjecture from the nature of the organ, the size of the disease and the things that indicate by their fitness, namely sex, age, habit, season, country, craft, strength and complexion, and that knowing the nature of an organ takes in four matters: its temperament, its creation, its position and its strength {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.268-269}.

## Temperament
Once the organ's natural temperament and its diseased temperament are known, artful conjecture shows how far it has strayed from its natural one and so how much is needed to bring it back. For example, if the healthy temperament is cold and the disease hot, it has strayed far and needs much cooling; if both are hot, slight cooling is enough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.268}.
