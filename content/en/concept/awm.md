## Summary
In the lexicon, awm is swimming, and a 'awwam is one skilled in it; the technical sense does not differ {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11945}. Swimming is among the skills Islam urged, with horsemanship and archery, to strengthen the body and drive away idleness; a report from Ibn 'Umar urges teaching children to swim {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11945}.
