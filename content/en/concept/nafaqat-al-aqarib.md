## Summary
For the author, the financial maintenance of relatives rests on three legal grounds: ownership, kinship, and marriage; the focus here is the maintenance of persons rather than the upkeep of owned property {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 301}. It is among the earliest forms of obligatory redistribution presented in the book, appearing before zakat {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 301}.

## Definition and Scope of Maintenance
As defined by jurists, legal maintenance (nafaqah) is providing a dependent with what sustains and benefits them—that is, what wards off illness and strengthens them for worship and work {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 301}. The book lists what it encompasses: food, medicine, clothing, housing, and marriage—the basic necessities—provided there is neither extravagance, nor stinginess, nor anything unlawful {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 302}. The amount is determined either by mutual agreement or by judicial ruling, taking into account the dependent's sufficiency, the provider's means, and prevailing custom, and it increases or decreases according to circumstances {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 302}.

## Maintenance, Zakat, and Inheritance as One System
The author views the systems of maintenance, zakat, and inheritance as integrated frameworks, none of which can be fully understood except in relation to the others {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 302}. Zakat may not be paid to those whose maintenance is obligatory—such as a child, a parent, or a wife—and an inheriting relative bears the duty of maintenance toward their kin because liability accompanies benefit {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 302}. Maintenance carries both a sense of responsibility and social solidarity: if a father becomes impoverished, his maintenance becomes obligatory upon his wealthy son, and if a son becomes impoverished, it becomes obligatory upon his wealthy father {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 302}. A wife is entitled to maintenance without poverty being a condition {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 302}.
