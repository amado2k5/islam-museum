## Signs of the state
Its common signs for its true kinds are a continued dry fever that mostly grows strong at midday; raving that is excessive at times and stops at others, from dislike of speech and laziness about it; confusion of mind, mostly near the fourth; plucking of the limbs; a disturbed breath that is irregular but large; much stretching of the hypochondria upward; and tremor of the organs with it and before it, which warns of it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.77} Often troubled sleep comes with it, from which they wake shouting; they sleep at times and lie awake at times, their sleep disturbed and confused, with frightening fancies and corrupt dreams {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.77} There is boldness, daring and anger beyond the usual; they hate the light and turn from it; their tongues are greatly disturbed and rough and they bite them, and sometimes they swell; the voice is often cut off; they long for water but drink little of it; and their extremities often chill with no cold from outside to cause it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.77} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78}

## Urine and pulse
Their urine tends toward thinness and fineness. Their pulse is hard, because the swelling is in a nervous organ and hard from the hardness of the vessel, and because the power is weak and pressed by the matter, though their pulse has some strength until they near danger, since dryness gathers and tightens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78} Its sawlike quality is never without some waviness, because the brain is a moist substance {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78} The pulse may become broad at times, or large as need requires, or frequent, or varying and trembling, which warns of fainting, unless it is a kind of variation and trembling caused by the hardness of the vessel and the strength of the power, in which case it does not warn; the pulse may also become convulsive, and then it warns of convulsion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.78}
