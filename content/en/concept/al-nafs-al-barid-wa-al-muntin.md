## The cold breath and the fetid
The cold is among the surest signs of the inborn heat's dissolution, and the fetid among the most particular signs of rottenness in the breathing organs, since its stench is bound to the out-breath and nothing else {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.311}
