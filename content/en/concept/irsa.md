## Nature and selection
The root of the violet-coloured iris, a plant with a stem, bearing a flower of many colours: white, yellow, violet and purple. It is called irsa, meaning rainbow {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. The best is hard, dense, juicy and reddish, with a sweet smell that does not smell wild, and it brings on sneezing strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry at the end of the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}.

## Uses by organ
Warming, thinning, ripening, opening, clearing and cleansing. Its juice with honey water clears and expels thick phlegm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Ornament: with its like, the spurge-type plant, it cleanses freckles and spots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Swellings and pustules: its boiled form softens hard and thick swellings, scrofula and pustules {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Wounds and ulcers: it helps filthy ulcers, and grows flesh in fistulas, even as a powder, and covers bone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Joints: its oil dissolves fatigue; drunk with vinegar or with wine it helps convulsions, and its enema helps sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Head: it induces sleep and removes long-standing headache; its oil with rose oil and vinegar prevents headache and makes one sneeze; rinsing with its decoction calms toothache, and its oil with vinegar stills ringing in the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Eyes: it draws tears {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.364}. Chest: it calms pain of the side and helps cough from thick moisture, pleurisy, laboured breathing and quinsy, and it clears matter held in the chest by subtle, opening action; it is taken in chest complaints with mead {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.365}. Stomach: it calms pain of the cold liver and spleen when drunk with vinegar, especially the spleen, and helps dropsy by drink and by poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.365}. Excretory organs: it opens the mouths of piles, removes gripes, urethral discharge and frequent nocturnal emissions, and promotes menses when taken in wine. Its oil is good for the uterus; sitting in its decoction is used for hardness of the womb and its cold pains. Used as a pessary with honey it expels phlegm and bile and purges yellow water and bile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.365}. Fevers: its oil removes chill and shivering {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.365}. Poisons: taken with vinegar it helps against all poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.365}.
