## Summary
In lexicon dhaqn is the joint of the two lower jaw-bones on which the lower teeth grow; it is used for the whole face. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8422}
The chin is part of the face and must be washed in ablution. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8423}

## Washing a beard
If the beard is thin, the skin beneath it must be washed; if it is thick, the outer hair must be washed but not the skin beneath, because the water does not reach it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8423} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8424}

## The two jaw-bones
The Shafi'is and Hanbalis set a full diya if both jaw-bones are removed, and half for one; the teeth on them carry their own diya. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8424}
