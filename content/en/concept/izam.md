## Joints

## Organs of the head
It is said that the bones of human beings cure epilepsy, and Galen said that a man used to give this to people in secret and removed their epilepsy, and that man was found out. These are attributed reports that the book gives, not advice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.622}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment

## Excretory organs
