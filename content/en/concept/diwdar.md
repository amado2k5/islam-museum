## Nature and selection
A kind of abhal, called the Indian pine; its sticks resemble zarnabad sticks, with a slight sharpness, and its milk is hot, pungent and thirst-causing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: dry in the third degree, more than hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}. Actions: its milk is sharp enough to burn; the substance vomited from it is astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: good for relaxation of the nerve, paralysis and facial palsy; the book says nothing is better {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}.
Head: it helps cold diseases in the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}.
Food: its milk causes thirst {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}.
Excretory organs: it breaks up stones in the kidney and bladder, binds the bowel, and removes relaxation of the anus when one sits in its decoction {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}.
