## Overview
Bab al-Rayyan is one of the eight gates of Paradise, reserved by God for the fasting, whom no one rivals through it. It is named from al-rayy — satiety of drink, the opposite of thirst — a pointer to the fasters' thirst in this world {cite:sharh-sahih-muslim-al-nawawi}.

## In the Sunnah
In the two Sahihs, from Sahl ibn Sa'd, the Prophet said: «In Paradise there is a gate called al-Rayyan; the fasting enter through it on the Day of Resurrection and none but they enter it. It is said: where are the fasting? They rise, and none but they enter; when they have entered, it is shut and none enters after them» {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
