## Nature
Its seed is in the shape of small hazelnuts with thin black husks that split under pressure into two halves from a seed black tending to yellow, good in taste and smell, aromatic; its leaf is like the myrtle leaf but larger, its fruit is red, it grows in mountainous places, and its strength is in its fruit and leaf; in the editor's footnote al-ghar is al-dahmasht {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Temperament
Its seed is hotter, its husks less hot, and as a whole it is hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Actions and properties
In its seed there is relaxing, and in all of it heating, its seed is hotter than its leaf, the heating and drying of its parts are stronger, the seed is the more extreme, the bark is weaker and less hot, and its oil is hotter than the oil of the walnut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Cosmetics
It is applied to bahaq with drink (sharab) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Swellings and pustules
With bread and sawiq it benefits hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Joints
It benefits all pains of the nerve, and its oil dissolves fatigue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Organs of the head
Its oil also dissolves headache, and likewise for cold pains of the ear, it restores hearing and benefits ringing in the ear and catarrhs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Organs of the chest
Beneficial for shortness of breath and orthopnoea as a lick (la'uq) with honey or boiled grape must, and likewise for the flow of residues to the lung, and a lick is made of it with honey for ulcers of the lung and orthopnoea, especially its seed being beneficial {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Organs of nourishment
Its oil is beneficial for pain of the liver when given with a fragrant drink, and so is its husk, but it and its seed relax the stomach and move vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Excretory organs
Its oil causes nausea and vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
In it there is a promoting of menses and of urine, the decoction of its leaf benefits diseases of the bladder and womb even as a sitting bath, and when its husk is drunk it breaks up stone and kills the foetus because of its bitterness exceeding the bitterness of others, and its seed also breaks up stone; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation or quantities {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Fevers
Its oil as an embrocation benefits shivering {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Poisons
It is given with wine for the sting of the scorpion, the fresh is a good poultice for hornets and bees when they sting, and in sum it is a theriac for all drunk poisons; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Substitutes
Its substitute is the leaf of namam {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}
