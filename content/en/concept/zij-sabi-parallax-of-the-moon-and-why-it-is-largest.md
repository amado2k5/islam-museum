## Summary
The parallax is the amount by which the visible place of a body differs from its calculated true place. It is greatest in the Moon because the Earth's size, as seen from the Moon's sphere, is far larger than from the spheres of the other bodies, so the half-radius of the Earth shows clearly. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.114}.

## Earth's centre and surface
The true view is the place seen from the centre of the Earth; the view of the eyes is the place seen from the Earth's surface. The difference between them is the Earth's half-radius set against the Moon's distance, and this is what makes the parallax. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.114}.
