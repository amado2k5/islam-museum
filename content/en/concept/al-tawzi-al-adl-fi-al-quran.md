## Overview
After production, Zaydan Qa'dat moves to the distribution of wealth, stating that its fundamental principle is directed toward those working in the enterprise, and that rights encompass all partners in the production process: namely, private right, public or divine right, and the right of land and materials {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 99}. He grounds this principle in the Qur'anic injunction to give the due right on the day of harvest without waste, which he indicates by [[passage/quran-6-141|al-An'am 141]] {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 99}.

## Critique of Capitalism and Communism
The author argues that capitalism focused on the rights of the individual and forgot the rights of the other partners, thereby entrenching injustice, selfishness, and exploitation {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 99}. And communism focused on material rights and the element of materials and land, while forgetting the divine right and private right, so the outcome of both systems was the same {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 99}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 100}. This critique is the author's own, presented as his assessment of the two systems.

## Three Principles
In his view, the Islamic system upholds three principles {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 100}:
- The main factors of production belong to no one: the earth belongs to God, every human being has the right to benefit from a part of it, and therefore every person must be given an opportunity to work on it {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 100}.
- Employment and work opportunities are guaranteed and assured for all, so once the principle of equal opportunity is in place, justice must take its course in distributing the fruits {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 100}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 101}.
- Distribution among individuals is according to work, as a scale for evaluating tasks is established by agreement among the members of the enterprise {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 101}.

## Private Right
The author divides human work in the enterprise into manual work, intellectual work, and technical and skilled work, and whoever contributes capital receives in proportion to his work and the amount he contributed {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 101}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 102}. Procedural matters are left to the members of the enterprise through consultation (shura) {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 102}.

## Public Right and the Right of the Enterprise
What remains is divided into [[concept/al-haqq-al-amm|public right]], which goes to public interests, the vulnerable, and the defense of the homeland and religion, and [[concept/haqq-al-mashru|the right of the enterprise]], which keeps the enterprise running {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 102}, {cite:qadan-manhaj-iqtisad-quran p. 106}.
