## Overview
*Dai al-duat* ("chief of the missionaries") was a senior office, entrusted with the leadership of the Ismaili mission under the [[polity/fatimid-caliphate|Fatimids]]. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.405-406}

## The office
Hasan Ibrahim Hasan notes that it ranked just below the chief judge (*qadi al-qudat*) and wore his dress, and that the two offices were often combined in one man. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.405-406}

Twelve *naqibs* assisted him in spreading the teachings, and he had deputies in the lands. He was thus the link between the caliph and his followers in the [[group/ismaili-dawa|Ismaili mission]]. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.405-406}

## His duties
- Taking the oath of allegiance from novices, directly or through his deputies.
- Supervising the lectures in the assembly of the mission, which were presented to the caliph before they were delivered.
- Collecting the contributions (*najwa*) from the believing men and women, set at three dirhams and a third. Whoever paid thirty-three dinars was given a Fatimid badge, which was a slip bearing the caliph's signature.

{cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.405-406}
