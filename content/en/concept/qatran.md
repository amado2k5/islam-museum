## Nature
It is the sap of a tree called al-sharbin; the force of its smoke is like the smoke of pitch, and an oil is made from it that is separated from it with wool as it is separated from pitch {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Actions and properties
It preserves the body of the dead, reddens and cauterises {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Cosmetics
It benefits lice and nits and kills them, even on livestock {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Wounds and ulcers
It strengthens soft flesh and benefits itch, even the itch of animals, especially its oil for four-footed beasts, dogs and camels {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Joints
It benefits crushing of the muscles and the gathering of blood and pus in them, and it is a remedy for elephantiasis and varicose veins, as a lick and as a smear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Organs of the head
It is the greatest thing for soothing cold headache, smeared on the head; it is dropped in the ear and kills ear worms, dropped in both ears with hyssop water for ringing and buzzing, and with hyssop water also for the aching tooth, soothing its pain and benefiting eaten teeth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Ocular organs
It sharpens the sight and clears the scars of ulcers in the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Organs of the chest
It is smeared on the throat for the tonsils and their pain; licked, it benefits ulcers of the lung and heals them, and it benefits old cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
The fruit of its tree is bad for the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Excretory organs
It kills worms in the intestines, especially an enema with it, which kills all the worms, and it benefits dribbling of urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it induces the menses, kills the foetus and spoils the semen, that when the penis is smeared with it before intercourse it prevents conception, and that when used as an enema it draws out the foetus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}

## Poisons and bites
It is applied as a poultice on the bite of the horned viper and heals with tila' (boiled grape must); it is given with tila' for one given sea-hare to drink, and when melted in camel fat and rubbed on the limbs, vermin do not come near them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.647}
