## Definition
Muyawama means dealing with a person by the day, or hiring him for a day. It is the fixing of a period of use to one day or several days. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15347}

## Related terms
A fixed term (ajal) is the future period to which a matter is attached; it may be set in days, months or longer, and its object need not be use. A time limit (ta'qit) makes a matter effective now and ending at a fixed time. Monthly hiring (mushahara) fixes use by the month, as muyawama does by the day. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15347} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15348}

## Ruling
The jurists agree that it is lawful when both parties accept it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15348}

## Length of the day
The legal day runs from the second dawn to sunset, unlike the daylight (nahar), which runs from sunrise to sunset. Custom may also fix it: the Majalla holds that a worker hired for a day works from sunrise to the afternoon or sunset, according to local practice. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15348} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15349}
