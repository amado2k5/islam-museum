## Summary
Ibn Sina prescribes for ulcerous fatigue with its humour in the vessels barley, wheat, light bird meat, honey oxymel, honey water and thin white wine, then turns to evacuation if this regimen does not suffice, and warns against it when the strength is weak {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.248}.

## Food and drink
Such foods as barley and hulled wheat and the meat of birds of light flesh; of drinks, honey oxymel, honey water and thin white wine, and he is not to be kept from wine of this kind, for it ripens and promotes urine. He should begin with what has a little sourness and then move gradually to the thin white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.248}.

## Evacuation
If this regimen does not suffice, there is a humour there, and the predominant one is evacuated: if blood predominates or is present, venesection is done, and otherwise a purge or cupping, as the state of the blood suggests. Do none of this when the strength seems weak {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.248}.
