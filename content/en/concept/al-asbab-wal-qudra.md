## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that a person sees things respond to him and thinks he has mastery over them: the earth, electricity, the aircraft, television. He forgets that God created their laws and properties. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.139} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.140}

## Self-sufficiency belongs to God
Sha'rawi holds that self-sufficiency belongs to God alone, since a person starts in need and ends in need, things around him change without his will, and strength passes with old age. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.139} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.140}
