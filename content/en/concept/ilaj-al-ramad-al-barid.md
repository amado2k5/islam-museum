## Evacuating the cold humour and the mild repellents
Ramad arising from cold causes: the cold humour must be evacuated, and repetition of a drink, clyster or gargle may be needed; treatment opens with repellents that are not too cold but carry some attenuation, and the collyrium used with some of the temperate waters is sound ⁦(the collyrium, water and infusion names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.174} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.175}

## Smearing, collyria and regimen
The brow is smeared at the start with the drying agent ⁦(its name is not carried)⁩, especially when the matter's path is the outer membranes, and washing the face with water in which the same was steeped is also admissible; smearing the lids at the start with the tried strengthening liniments is good, and drinking the strengthener of their kind is also useful — tried in this are the poultices ⁦(their names and the pharmacopoeia tablets are not carried)⁩; the waters and mucilages useful dripped into the cold-ramad eye ⁦(their names are not carried)⁩; then the dissolving collyria ⁦(their names are not carried)⁩; and the gentle regimen, the bath and pure white wine benefit all of these {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.175}
