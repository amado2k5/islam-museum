## The mean of the planet and the mean of the Sun
To find where a wandering planet lies on the ecliptic, its mean is found for the required day and the given hour, under either the Arab or the Roman dating, and the Sun's mean is found for the same moment. For Saturn, Jupiter and Mars, the planet's mean is subtracted from the Sun's mean and the remainder is the anomaly used. For Venus and Mercury the table gives the planet's anomaly together with the Sun's mean, and these two are used in place of the planet's own mean and the Sun's mean. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.175-176}
