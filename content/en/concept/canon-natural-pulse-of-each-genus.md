## Summary
In the fourth chapter Ibn Sina states what is natural in each genus of the pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## Genera that admit more and less
For each of the genera named that allow a difference of increase and decrease, the natural is the moderate, except strength, where the natural is the greater. If one of the other kinds increased only as a consequence of greater strength, becoming larger for instance, it is natural because of strength {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.

## Genera that do not admit more and less
For the genera that do not admit the greater and the lesser, the natural is the even and the regular. The rest of the chapter is cut off on this page and runs into the next, so it has not been transcribed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.173}.
