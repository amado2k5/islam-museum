## Overview
Muhammad Shawqi al-Fanjari distinguishes the line of sufficiency (kifaf) — the barest minimum that keeps a person alive — from the line of adequacy or wealth, the level fitting the time, place and the person's work and responsibility; Islam, he argues, defines the poor by the second {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 170} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 171}. The economic problem within one society is imbalance among its members; in the world it is the gap between developing and advanced states — he cites the Hashr verse that wealth must not circulate only among the rich {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 171}. He quotes sayings attributed to 'Umar and 'Ali and to al-Sarakhsi and al-Shatibi on the imam's giving.
