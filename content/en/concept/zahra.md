## Nature and selection
Nature: a plant with lentil-like leaves, upright branches and a fine root, with little leaf; it grows in salt ground and tastes salty. There is another kind of better colour, purplish. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}

## Actions
Ulcers: it heals them. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}

## Uses by organ
Organs of the head: it thins the superfluities, so that the second kind helps epilepsy when drunk in oxymel. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}
