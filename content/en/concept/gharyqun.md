## Nature
Dioscorides said: it is male and female; some gharyqun resembles the root of anjudan, but its outside is not as compact as the outside of the root of anjudan; some say it is generated in decaying trees by way of putrefaction; in its taste there is heat, acridity and astringency, and its substance is watery, airy and earthy and subtle; the difference between male and female is that inside the female are found straight layers, while the male is round without layers, being one thing; the two are alike in temperament, in their taste there is first sweetness and then it changes until some bitterness appears in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Choice
The best is the smooth, white, quickly crumbling, very compact one with smooth ends in whose bitterness sweetness is found; the one that breaks apart with splinters is the female; the male is not good; the hard and the black are very bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Actions and properties
Dissolving, cutting thick humours, opening all obstructions and subtilising; some say there is an astringent power in it, at the start of its taste like sweetness and then bitterness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Swellings and pustules
Beneficial for all swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Joints
It is given with sikanjabin for sciatica, and it is among what purifies the residues of the nerve by a property in it, and it benefits weakness of the muscle and a fall {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Organs of the head
It benefits the epileptic and purifies the residues of the brain by a property in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits asthma and ulcer of the lung when given with boiled grape must (tila'), and when drunk with water it benefits spitting of blood from the chest; the quantities are not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Organs of nourishment
It benefits jaundice and is given with sikanjabin for swelling of the spleen; chewed alone or swallowed it benefits pain of the stomach and sour belching, and it is given for pain of the liver {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Excretory organs
It loosens thick humours of different kinds, from black bile and phlegm, especially with the water of qaratin, it may help the loosening drugs and carry them to the extremities of the body, and it calms pain of the kidneys; the quantities are not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.723-724}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It promotes urine and menses, and benefits suffocation of the womb; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Fevers
It benefits rigor and old thick fevers; given with wine it kills worms and prevents rigor; the quantity is not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}

## Poisons
It is applied as a poultice for stings of vermin, and when given with wine it is of very great benefit for that, and it is applied as a poultice for stings of the vermin of cold poisons; a report attributed to the book {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.724}
