## Definition
Jam' in the lexical sense means gathering what is scattered and bringing things close. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3229} In the grammarians' sense it is a noun that denotes several units meant together, with a change in the letters of the singular. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3229}

## Views on its least number
- Grammarians: the plural is not applied to one or two; the paucal range runs from three to ten. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3229}
- Jurists and usulis: most companions, jurists and language scholars hold the least plural to be three; al-Ghazali and Sibawayh held it two in fact. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3229}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3230} The dispute is about the forms that carry the name, not the word itself. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3230}
- Experts in inheritance: apart from Ibn Abbas, they take two as the least, and al-Farra' is reported to have said that the plural begins with the dual. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3230}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3231}

## Consequences
- In fiqh outside inheritance, rulings are built on three; for example a bequest of expiation for oaths. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3230}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3231}
- In endowments the income goes to three of the nearest persons, and the list is completed beyond the first rank if fewer are found. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3231}
- An acknowledgement of "dirhams" owed obliges three dirhams. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3231}
- An oath to abstain for days, months or years binds for three. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3231}
- In inheritance the least is two; two siblings reduce the mother to a sixth. Ibn Abbas disagreed, and the exchange with Uthman is reported. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3231}
- In usul, a general term that is an indefinite plural may be restricted down to three. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3232}
