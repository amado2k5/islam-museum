## Summary
The khala (maternal aunt) is the mother's sister, the mother being whoever bore one, in fact or by extension. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7534} A maternal or milk-aunt is forbidden in marriage by consensus, by the Quranic list of the forbidden and the saying that what is forbidden by kinship is forbidden by suckling. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7534} As a kinswoman by blood she is among the kin who are not sharers or agnates, and her inheritance is disputed: the Shafiis and Malikis deny it, the Hanafis and Hanbalis grant it when agnates and sharers are absent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7535} She has the right of custody; the majority deny maintenance to her, the Hanafis oblige maintenance to a blood aunt; a milk-aunt has neither custody nor maintenance. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7535}
