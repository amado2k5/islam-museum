## Overview
Shaykh Shaltut holds religious brotherhood a bond of hearts uniting Muslims in direction and aim — the basis of social solidarity in which each bears his brother's burdens {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 81}{cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 84}. Religious brotherhood outranks blood kinship when the two conflict; the Muslim shields his brother, neither wrongs him nor hands him over {cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 82}{cite:azhar-quran-building-society-shaltut p. 83}. He divides solidarity into a moral branch — counsel and guidance — and a material one of mutual aid in needs.
