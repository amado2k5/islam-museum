## The causes

The eyelashes shed either on account of the matter or on account of the place. The matter either becomes scant, as at the end of severe acute diseases, or corrupts from what mixes with it at the root, as happens in alopecia where a sharp, salty or acrid moisture does not show on the lid yet harms the hair. The local cause is a manifest lesion: hardness and thickness through which the vapour generated from the hair finds no outlet, or a swelling, or a corrosion; redness and strong stinging point to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.199}

## The treatment

What comes from a local lesion is treated by treating that lesion as stated in its chapter; what comes from a failure of matter is treated by restoring and nourishing the body and using the medicines that draw the matter of hair to the lids; and what comes from corrupt moisture is treated by cleansing the head and the member ⁦(the tried remedies, collyria, recipes and measures are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.199} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.200}
