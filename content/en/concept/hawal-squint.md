## Summary
Hawal (squint) is lexically a whiteness at the far corner of the eye with the black at the near side; the jurists apply the word to one who has it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7424} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7425} Awar is loss of sight in one eye, ashan is poor sight by night or day, and zufr is a whiteness appearing in the pupil. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7424} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7425} In marriage, the majority hold that squint gives no right to annul unless safety was stipulated; if safety was stipulated and found absent, the Malikis and one Hanbali view give the right to annul, while the Hanafis do not give it for a missing stipulated quality. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7425} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7426} A squint-eyed sheep may be sacrificed unless the squint prevents sight, and an injury to the eye that causes squint carries assessed compensation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7426}
