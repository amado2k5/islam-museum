## Definition
The maternal milk-aunt is she who takes the ruling of the blood maternal aunt through suckling rather than birth: the sister of the woman who nursed the child, and likened to her is the paternal milk-aunt, the sister of the nursing woman's husband to whom the milk belongs. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The root of the chapter
God forbade in Sura al-Nisa the mothers who suckled you and your sisters through suckling, and the Prophet said that what is forbidden by suckling is forbidden by lineage; on this the jurists built that a woman's milk gives the child motherhood, brotherhood, maternal and paternal aunthood on the side of suckling as on the side of lineage, in what is forbidden. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

## Its rulings
The milk-aunt is forbidden to her milk-sister's child as the blood aunt is forbidden to her sister's child by birth — and likewise the paternal milk-aunt, the milk-mother, the milk-sister and the milk-daughter. With the prohibition comes the mahram standing in privacy and gaze for the jurists who hold it, but not inheritance and not lineage. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
