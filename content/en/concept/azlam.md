## Overview
Azlam: featherless arrows by which the Jahili consulted the unknown before resolving on a matter; among the idolatrous practices the Qur'an forbade.

## The story
When they intended something they would cast the three arrows — "Do", "Do not" and "Deferred" — and take whatever came out; these arrows were kept at Hubal inside the Ka'ba {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

The Qur'an then counted them among the ansab and defilement: "Wine, gambling, the ansab and the azlam are filth of Satan's work" {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
They used to draw lots on arrows inscribed "Do" and "Do not" to decide their affairs; Islam forbade that.
