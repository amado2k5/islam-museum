## Summary
The Lesser Resurrection is mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the death of every person and their raising in the barzakh — a sign of the Hour and a day of indescribable terror. It includes events beyond imagination: the blowing of the Trumpet, the emergence of people from their graves, their gathering to the Gathering Place, and their presentation for reckoning.
