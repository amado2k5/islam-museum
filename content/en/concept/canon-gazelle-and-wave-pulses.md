## Summary
Ibn Sina begins the third chapter, on the compound pulses that have names of their own, with the gazelle pulse and the wave pulse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.172-173}.

## The gazelle pulse
The gazelle pulse is the one uneven in a single part, slow at first, then it breaks off and quickens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.172}.

## The wave pulse
The wave pulse is uneven in the largeness and smallness or the rise of the parts of the vessels, in breadth and in earliness and lateness at the start of the pulse's motion, with softness in it; it is not very small and has some breadth, as if waves following one another in a straight line, differing among themselves in rise, fall, speed and slowness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.172-173}.
