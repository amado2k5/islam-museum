## Nature and selection
The Indian cucumber is like the large cucumber, but it is bitter and resembles ginger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}. The editor's footnote also gives bull as the Indian cucumber {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.397}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry in the second degree, and in the third according to some {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Astringent, and it strengthens the organs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: it helps hardness and dampness of the nerves and their cold disorders, such as paralysis and facial palsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Food organs: it kindles the stomach's fire, helps vomiting, and enters the electuaries {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}. Excretory organs: it binds the belly and dispels wind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.398}.
