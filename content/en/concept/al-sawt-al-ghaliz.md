## The coarse voice
It may befall those who practise much blowing into the reed-pipes, and into the trumpets especially, for the cutting of their breath and its withholding in the lung that befalls, so that the passages widen (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.326}
