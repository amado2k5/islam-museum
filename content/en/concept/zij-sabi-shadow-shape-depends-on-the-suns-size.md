## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the shadow is neither a cylinder of equal width nor an endlessly widening cone. Because the Sun is larger than the Earth the shadow narrows to a point in the air; had the Sun been equal to the Earth the shadow would keep one width, and had it been smaller the shadow would widen. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.84}.

## Effect on the eclipses
It follows that the lunar eclipse varies with the Moon's position in the shadow, and not every planet is eclipsed at each opposition, since the shadow does not reach all their centres. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.84}.
