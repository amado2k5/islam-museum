## Nature and selection
Choice: the lees of old wine are the best and safest, then those like them. Vinegar lees are very strong; they are dried, ground fine and burned until whitened, then sprinkled. Old lees are weak, so the moist kind is used, kept in vessels away from the air; it may be washed as zinc is washed. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}

## Actions
The vinegar lees are the stronger of the two; their power cleanses and astringes, and the burned kind corrodes and rots by another power. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: the burned kind, used with resin on whitened nails, improves them. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Swellings and pustules: the unburned kind is good for irritation alone and with myrtle, and it deflates pustules that have no ulcer. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Chest: the unburned kind cools the heat of a breast congested with blood. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Digestive organs: the unburned kind stops matter from flowing down to the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
Book listing, excretory organs, on the menses: the book says that when the womb is poulticed from outside with the unburned kind, it stops the bleeding of the menses. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.440}
