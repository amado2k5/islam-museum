## Summary
Al-Battani records two nearby lunar eclipses. The first, on 23 Tammuz at Raqqa, covered more than half and a third of the Moon's diameter; the second, on 2 August, covered less than its full diameter, and its middle is given in hours after noon. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.86}.

## Comparison with Ptolemy
He says Ptolemy's reckoning would make the first eclipse more complete and would bring its middle earlier by about half an hour and a quarter; for the second, its middle would be earlier by about half an hour and a third. He takes the gap as proof that the reckoning needs correcting. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.87}.
