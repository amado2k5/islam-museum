## Summary
Three land sinkings — east, west, and the Arabian peninsula — great collapses counted among the major signs.

## In the texts
A khasf is the earth swallowing people or armies from above; scholars affirm it will happen, its time and places are unseen.

## Note
From the hadith of Hudhayfa ibn Usayd in Sahih Muslim.
