## Summary
The centre of the epicycle travels on the eccentric from the far point at the planet's mean rate of longitude per day, and the planet moves on its epicycle from the apogee point at its own rate each day. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.95}.

## The figure drawn
He draws the circle of the zodiac sphere, the inclined circle and the eccentric circle, then places the epicycle's centre on a point of the eccentric and measures the longitudinal motion from it, using four marked points to show what happens to the planet. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.96}.
