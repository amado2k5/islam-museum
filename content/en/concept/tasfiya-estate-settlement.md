## Overview
Tasfiya is the whole of the work whose aim is to list the dead person's rights and obligations and to pay the rights attached to the estate to those who hold them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5247}

## The term
It is a modern term that lawyers adopted; the jurists did not treat it under this heading. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5247}

## What the jurists say
The jurists took great care over the rights that the estate has or owes, and over the rights of minors, to secure those who hold them, and especially to secure the claims of creditors and of legatees on part of the estate. These are set out in the entries on the estate, inheritance and bequest. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5247}
