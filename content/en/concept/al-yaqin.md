## Summary
Sha'rawi sets certainty in three degrees: knowledge of certainty, sight of certainty and truth of certainty. For him, certainty is the settling of a matter in the heart so that it admits no doubt. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.556}

## Knowledge of certainty
Certainty is knowledge when the report comes from someone one trusts. Sha'rawi illustrates it with Abu Bakr, who accepted the account of the night journey, saying that if the Prophet had said it, then it was true. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.556}

## Sight and truth of certainty
Certainty rises to sight of certainty when the matter is seen with the eye, and then to truth of certainty when one enters into the reality of the thing. He uses the warning of the Fire in al-Takathur to show the move from report to sight. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.557}

## Limits of this block
These are Sha'rawi's words alone, from these pages. The positions of Sufi writers and theologians on the stations of certainty, the academic view and other views are not yet entered.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh holds that there is a space in the heart that only certainty in God can fill, and quotes [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]]: 'my certainty in God makes me certain' (yaqini billah yaqini), explaining that the first 'yaqini' is a noun, a premise and a cause, and the second a verb, a result and a fruit. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:02:00} He quotes the 'people of hearts' that the degrees of certainty begin with tranquillity in God's promise, citing the strengthening of the heart of Musa's mother, and end with the servant's acknowledgement that God suffices him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:03:30}
Among the signs of certainty he names: the heart's rest in trust in God; submission to God's command, citing the verse of Surat al-Nisa' on taking the Prophet as judge and the verse of Surat al-Ahzab that the believer has no choice once God and His Messenger have decided; and then awe at God's prior knowledge of one's sins. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:05:00}
He holds that a person of certainty whose daughter falls ill knows the illness is a purification and a raising of rank, and that doors of provision are opened by it for the doctor, the nurse, the pharmacist, the drug and packaging factories, transport and laboratories; and he distinguishes the one who thinks, who sees that the loaf will fill him, from the one who reflects, who sees how many people worked so the loaf could reach him, from farmer to miller to kneader to baker. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep18 00:08:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh holds that in hardships a person has two views: one looks through 'the narrow keyhole of the visible world', like Musa's companions saying 'we shall surely be overtaken', and one looks from 'the wide horizons of the unseen world', like Musa's 'no, my Lord is with me, He will guide me' and the Prophet's words to Abu Bakr in the cave: what do you think of two whose third is God? {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:08:30} He holds that people form a committee from which another committee springs and it fails, while God's command is carried out by His mere will, and applies this to the patient whom doctors cannot help and the debtor in straits. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep07 00:09:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
## The steps of the station of certainty
The Sheikh holds that the great, when hardship struck, were marked by the station of certainty: that the servant knows he belongs to God, that God chooses for him only what is best, and that he is limited in thought and means; he says this station has nine steps: [[concept/tawba|repentance]], then [[concept/zuhd|renunciation]], then [[concept/sabr|patience]], then [[concept/shukr|gratitude]], then [[concept/khawf|fear]], then contentment, then [[concept/raja|hope]], then [[concept/tawakkul|trust]], then love. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:10:00} He gives the example of Pharaoh's sorcerers, whom he numbers at sixty thousand, the strength of their hearts once they believed, and the fear Musa felt within himself until he was told 'do not fear' (needs_source: the number as the Sheikh gives it). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep13 00:03:30}
