## Summary
Ibn Sina describes washing in cold water after exercise: the rubbing before it is made firmer and the exercise kept moderate though a little quicker than usual, then the person enters the water all at once and stays as long as he feels lively and before shivering; he starts on the hottest summer day at noon, avoids it after intercourse, food or vomiting, and says it drives the innate heat inward and then strengthens it many times over {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.

## The method
It may be used after exercise, in which case the rubbing before it should be firmer than usual, the oil anointing as usual, and the exercise after the rubbing and anointing moderate and a little quicker than usual. After exercise he goes into the cold water at once so it reaches all his organs together, and stays in it as long as he feels lively and able, and before shivering seizes him {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.

## Starting and guarding
Whoever wants to use it should proceed by degrees and begin the first time on the hottest day of summer at noon, taking care there is no wind. He should not use it after intercourse, after food, while food is undigested, after vomiting from weakness of body or stomach, nor after exercise, except for someone very strong {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.

## Its effect
Washing in cold water in the ways described drives the innate heat inward all at once, then strengthens it, many times over, to withstand and come out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.
