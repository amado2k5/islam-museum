## Nature
A kind of truffle and mushroom which dries and so contracts like cartilage; its shape is the shape of a cup on a small, shrivelled stomach (kirsh); clothes are washed with it, and it is eaten among the sours, and its pleasure is like the pleasure of cartilages and more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Temperament
It is not in the coldness of the rest of the truffles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Actions and properties
It is not bad in humour as the truffle is, and there was in its nature a fermentation (takhmir) or an alkalinity (qalawiyya) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}
