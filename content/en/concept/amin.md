## Meaning and use
Amin means "safe" or "secure". In this and the more frequent form amin, it is used like the Hebrew and Syriac amen, by Muslims as by Jews and Christians, as a confirmation of prayers, in the sense of "answer" or "so be it". {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 458}

## In prayer
It is said after the opening sura of the Qur'an, without being part of the sura. According to a hadith the Prophet learned it from Gabriel at the end of that sura. In the prayer the imam says it aloud or, according to others, softly after the Fatiha, and the congregation repeats it. It is said to be God's seal on the believers because it protects them from evil. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 458}
