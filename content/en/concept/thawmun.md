## Temperament
Its seed is strongly hot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Excretory organs
It loosens blood and bilious humours and expels worms; the amount of the draught is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says that it promotes flow, the printed line not saying what flows, and brings out a dead foetus; a report attributed to the book, without recommendation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.696}
