## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that provision comes by gain, which is increase and earning, and by averting harm, which is warding off heavy costs and affliction {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1151}.

## The example of the sick child
He gives the image of a child who is ill. If the father can afford a simple remedy, God heals the child. If the father is struck by a long illness and costly treatment, then averting that burden is itself provision, because something greater has been turned away {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1152}.

## Blessing
He says provision by averting harm may carry more blessing than provision by gain, and that the believer thanks God for what is turned away {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1152}.

## Not yet checked
The division into gain and averting is Sha'rawi's own reasoning and has not been checked against the tafsir or fiqh books. The academic view and any non-Sunni view have not been checked.
