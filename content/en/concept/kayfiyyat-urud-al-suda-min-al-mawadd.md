## By essence: changing the temperament
Matters are a cause of headache either by essence or by accident, and by essence means that they change the temperament by essence or break the continuity by essence {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} Change of temperament by essence is of two kinds, by neighbourhood and by what the book prints as al-tahlif {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} By neighbourhood is when the humour is mixed, hot or cold, and heats or cools; when it parts from what it mixed with, it fades and vanishes and does not stay for any notable time {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} As for al-tahlif, it is when the humour has set and fixed its effect, so that even if it departs by evacuation or dissolving the quality remains set {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48}

## By essence: breaking continuity
As for their causing headache by essence through breaking continuity, that is by their movement and penetration, or by their biting and eating {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} Most that causes headache by moving does so by stirring up winds, and most that does this are cold matters struck by a sudden heat, or windy foods mixed with heat; the biting, eating ones are the hot humours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48}

## By accident: the obstruction
As for the headache that comes from them by accident, it is when an obstruction arises, from a swelling or not; the obstruction is followed by a change of temperament, as the reader knows, and by a break of continuity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} This is because the matters that nature moves in the body, either to shake it off or to sort and apportion it as nourishment, are moved through natural passages; when these are blocked they are held back, and when held back they resist; resisting brings distension, and distension brings a break of continuity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48} Obstructions may occur in the substance of the brain, in the veins within it, in its arteries, and in those two in its membranes {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.48}

The obstruction comes from the humours through their viscosity, their thickness or their abundance. Viscosity is found only in phlegm, thickness in phlegm and black bile; phlegm blocks by viscosity, thickness and abundance, black bile by thickness or abundance, and bile blocks by abundance, and so does blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.48-49}

## Crisis headache and headache after digestion
Crisis headache is of the kind whose cause is a natural movement of shaking off, and the headache that comes after the digestion of food is of the kind whose cause is a natural movement of sorting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49} The editor's footnote glosses the crisis headache as a headache that results from the crisis, the change that comes suddenly in fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49}
