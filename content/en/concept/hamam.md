## Nature
A well-known bird. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.492}

## Temperament
The nestlings have heat and a surplus of moisture; the fledged birds are lighter; their eggs are very hot. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.492}

## Actions
In the nestlings the surplus moisture is thick. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.492}

## Uses by organ
Organs of the head: dove's blood stops the nosebleed that comes from the membrane of the brain. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.492}
Organs of digestion: the fledged birds digest more lightly and make better humour than the nestlings; the hot-humoured are to eat them with unripe grape, coriander and cucumber pulp, the book says; its eggs are heavy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.492}
Organs of the eye: pigeon dung helps the white spot that follows the healing of an ulcer in the cornea. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.492}
