## Summary
Ibn Sina says dry stool shows fatigue and dissolution, much urine or fiery heat, that the natural colour is lightly fiery, and he sets out what intense, pale, white, black and green stool show. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.199}.

## Dry stool
Dry stool shows fatigue and dissolution, much passing of urine, fiery heat, dry foods or a long stay in the bowel. If moisture mixes with the hard dry stool, its dryness comes from long retention among moistures that stop it coming out; where there is no long retention, the cause is a biting, purulent surplus poured from the liver. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.199}.

## Colour
Its natural colour is fiery to a slight degree; if intensified it shows much bile, if reduced it shows rawness and lack of maturation, and if white it may be from a block in the bile passage, showing jaundice, while white with pus smelling of corruption shows a burst abscess. A very strongly fiery colour often shows maturation at the end of a disease, and often a bad state. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.199}.
