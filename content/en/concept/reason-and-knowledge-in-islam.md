## Overview
Shaykh Mahmud Shaltut opens *Hadha Huwa al-Islam* with Islam as the religion of reason and knowledge: the Qur'an urges reflection on the dominion of heavens and earth, places the people of knowledge third after God and the angels, and condemns blind imitation {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 11} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 12} {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 13}. Knowledge in the Qur'anic sense is not confined to the law — it is every faculty that helps man build the earth: farming, industry, health, wealth, defense of homelands {cite:hatha-huwa-al-islam-azhar p. 20}; and the first revelation descended as a command to read.
