## The upright breath
What makes it bad is that this man's breathing tools cannot bear lying down, since his channels, narrow at root, are blocked when the parts lean on one another — hence his being driven to sit with a stretched neck and a level chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.314}
