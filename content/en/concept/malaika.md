## Overview
Angels in Islam are creatures of God, and belief in them is a pillar of faith for the Sunnis {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §72}.

## In the creed
The Tahawiyya states it plainly: "we believe in the angels and the prophets and the books sent down to the messengers, and we bear witness that they were upon manifest truth" {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §72}; Ibn Abi al-Izz's commentary elaborates what belief in them entails and their attributes {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.
