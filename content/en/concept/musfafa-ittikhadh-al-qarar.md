## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan teaches 'the matrix' as one of the best decision-making methods: it does not fit every decision but fits most of them, and software exists that computes the decision directly from the conditions and the alternatives {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:52:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:54:30}.

## Its steps
Conditions are divided into essential and preferential and kept wholly separate {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:55:00}.
On the essential conditions, offending alternatives are excluded outright — like a KPI: a per-person cost one hair above sixty dinars and the hotel falls — beginning with the conditions easiest to check (the conference dates, distance from the airport, room count) and leaving the hardest to compute (cost) for last, saving effort {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:56:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:58:30}.
The surviving alternatives are then scored on the preferential conditions, and the winner is the highest scorer; in a deeper variant the conditions carry weights (three hundred points for price, twenty for a preferential condition) computed only after the essentials are passed {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 00:59:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 01:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 01:05:30}.

## Companion rules
He states that a decision improves as alternatives multiply: whoever wants to hire interviews not three but twenty; whoever buys a house sees not two but thirty or forty — and he tells of a trainee who used the matrix to buy a car the day after the course and came out quickly with what he wanted {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 01:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 01:03:30}.
He applies it to hiring: the essential and preferential conditions are written before applications arrive, applications failing an essential are set aside before any interview, and interviews serve only to compare preferentials; his rule is 'never hire out of pity' (the speaker's view) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 01:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep04 01:03:00}.
