## Overview
Prophethood in Sunni creed is a pillar of faith: belief in the prophets and in the books sent down to the messengers, and bearing witness that they were upon manifest truth {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §72}.

## In text and commentary
The Tahawiyya creed affirms that God took Abraham as intimate friend and spoke to Moses directly {cite:aqida-tahawiyya §71}; Ibn Abi al-Izz's commentary details prophethood's place in belief {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}. The 'proofs of prophethood' literature — al-Bayhaqi's book among them — treats its signs, miracles and tokens {cite:dalail-al-nubuwwa-bayhaqi}.
