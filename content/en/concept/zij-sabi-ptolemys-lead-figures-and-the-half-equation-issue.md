## Summary
Al-Battani says Ptolemy computed the Moon's lead on the basis of the larger of the two equations, when the Moon's equation is five parts and the Sun's two parts and twenty-three minutes; the difference then comes to about seven parts and five and a half minutes, giving a lead of nearly an hour. He notes this does not hold when the Moon's equation is three parts. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.144}.

## The more exact reckoning
Al-Battani holds that the better and more exact method takes half the difference and half its sixth, adds it to or subtracts it from the corrected anomaly as the difference belongs to the Sun or the Moon, so that the Moon's anomaly is right for the midpoint between the true and the mean conjunction. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.145}.
