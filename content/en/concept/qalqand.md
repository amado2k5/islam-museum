## Nature
The editor notes in a footnote that qalqand is green vitriol {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Temperament
Hot and dry to the fourth degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Actions and properties
Drying, hardening, condensing the body and corrosive, with astringency and burning in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Wounds and ulcers
It benefits fistulas of the nose {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Organs of the head
It prevents nosebleed, and when a drop of it dissolved in water is dropped into the nose it cleanses the head; it is among the drugs that cleanse the ear and benefit its cold pains, and it kills the worms in the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Excretory organs
With honey it is given for worms and tapeworm. The measure is left out {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Poisons and bites
It repels the harm of mushrooms {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}
