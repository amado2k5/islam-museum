## Definition
Sha'ban is the eighth month, named for the tribes dispersing to their raids after Rajab, or for branching between Rajab and Ramadan. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## Its fasting
The Prophet fasted so much of Sha'ban that it was said he did not break fast; it is the month in which deeds are raised, and he liked to be fasting when his deeds were raised. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## The middle night
Reports tell of the merit of keeping the night of mid-Sha'ban and of God's pardon of His servants in it, save the idolater and the rancorous; scholars disputed its grade, some strengthening it and others weakening it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}

## Its end
It is forbidden to anticipate Ramadan by fasting a day or two before it, except for one whose habit of fasting preceded, and fasting the day of doubt is disliked by many jurists. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}
