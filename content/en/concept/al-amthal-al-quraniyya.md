## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that Quranic parables bring the unseen closer to understanding through the sensible. God's parables cover monotheism, light, disbelief, hypocrisy and Paradise. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.165} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.166}

## The fire and the rain
Sha'rawi reads the parable of the one who kindled a fire, applied to the hypocrites, as God taking away their light and leaving them in layered darkness. He reads the rain-cloud of [[passage/quran-2-19|Al-Baqara 19]] with darkness, thunder and lightning, and the frightened who put their fingers in their ears against death. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.171} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.177} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.179}

## The light
Sha'rawi discusses the parable of light in the niche, lamp and glass, and says it brings the meaning near and is not a description of God's light. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.168} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.169}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's reading and is attributed to him. No other exegetical reading of the parables is given in these pages, and none has been checked.
