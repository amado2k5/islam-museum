## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy relays from Ibn Abbas that sitting with one who makes you fear God, so that you find safety on the Day of Resurrection, is better than sitting with one who reassures you, so that you find fear on that Day (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}. He says God does not combine two securities or two fears on His servant: whoever fears in this world is given security in the next {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}. He holds that Islam is simple and that we have complicated its matters, and he explains not hoping for the reckoning as not fearing it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:50:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)
The Sheikh relays from Ibn Abbas an answer to someone who asked about sitting with people who make them fear God: sitting with one who makes you fear, so that you find safety on the Day of Resurrection, is better than sitting with one who reassures you, so that you find fear on that Day (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}.

He says that God does not combine two securities or two fears on His servant: whoever fears in this world is given security in the next. He points to God's promise of two gardens for the one who fears standing before his Lord {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)
The Sheikh holds that we have complicated matters and that the answers are simpler than we imagine; he prays that God grant us understanding of His Book and application of His Prophet's Sunna. He explains the saying of God about a people who did not look forward to the reckoning as meaning that they did not fear it, and then gives a mocking exchange in which the speaker declares that he does not fear God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:50:30}.

He gives the sense of a saying whose speaker is not named in this passage (needs_source: no source is given in the transcript): whoever fears Hell as he fears poverty escapes all of it, and whoever fears the Creator as he fears a created being prospers in this world and the next {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:19:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Sharh Ketab al-Fawaed lectures (episode 5)
The Sheikh holds that fear and hope are directed only to the One in whose hand the whole matter is, since created beings own neither harm nor benefit for themselves, and God alone disposes of everything (his view) (needs_source: he uses wording close to a verse that he does not name) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 01:19:00}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lecture series Al Dinu Al Qayem

The Sheikh says that fear and hope balance each other, and that good comes between them {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:08:00}. He holds that a believer who feels God's presence does not fear anyone else, and that security settles within him through faith {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep02 00:12:00}.

The Sheikh holds, as his own opinion, that a person who fears dismissal from work when he tries to enjoin good and forbid evil needs patience and resolve, and he counts this as a price that must be paid {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep04 01:11:00}. He says that provision comes from God, not from the employer or the spouse, so fear of losing a job is misplaced {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep12 00:23:00}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh answers a question often put to him: should we live by fear or by hope, especially one who sins often? He holds that the matter needs balance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:02:30} He cites how [[person/al-khidr|al-Khidr]] could not bear three objections from Musa and said 'this is the parting between me and you', whereas a servant errs with God hundreds of times a day and God does not say that to him, because He is forbearing and does not hasten punishment. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:04:00}
He quotes the scholars' likeness of the believer's heart to a bird whose head is love, of God, His Messenger and the Qur'an, and whose wings are fear and hope: cut off the head and the bird dies; break one wing and it cannot fly. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:06:00}
He holds that most of what people fear is worldly: a child's long illness and its costs, death in surgery, being let go by one's employer; he calls this a negative fear that lessens as faith grows strong, because the term of life and provision are in God's hand, and provision is written while one is still in the womb. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep12 00:08:30}
The true fear, in the following episode, is to put other people's sins under the microscope while forgetting God's covering of one's own; to come on the Day of Resurrection with good deeds and be set upon by creditors, those whose wealth one consumed, whom one backbit or wronged or failed to help when one could; and to belittle a sin. He quotes the scholars: do not look at the smallness of the sin but at the greatness of the One you disobey. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep13 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep13 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep13 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep13 00:08:30} See also: [[concept/raja|hope]].
