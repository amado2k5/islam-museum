## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy relays from Ibn Abbas that sitting with one who makes you fear God, so that you find safety on the Day of Resurrection, is better than sitting with one who reassures you, so that you find fear on that Day (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}. He says God does not combine two securities or two fears on His servant: whoever fears in this world is given security in the next {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}. He holds that Islam is simple and that we have complicated its matters, and he explains not hoping for the reckoning as not fearing it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:50:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 2)
The Sheikh relays from Ibn Abbas an answer to someone who asked about sitting with people who make them fear God: sitting with one who makes you fear, so that you find safety on the Day of Resurrection, is better than sitting with one who reassures you, so that you find fear on that Day (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}.

He says that God does not combine two securities or two fears on His servant: whoever fears in this world is given security in the next. He points to God's promise of two gardens for the one who fears standing before his Lord {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02 00:16:30}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)
The Sheikh holds that we have complicated matters and that the answers are simpler than we imagine; he prays that God grant us understanding of His Book and application of His Prophet's Sunna. He explains the saying of God about a people who did not look forward to the reckoning as meaning that they did not fear it, and then gives a mocking exchange in which the speaker declares that he does not fear God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:50:30}.
