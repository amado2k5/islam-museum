## Nature
The Indian kind is small, thin pieces of wood and bark like the bark of cinnamon, red and black where broken; it grows on old walls and where it does not snow, and has a leaf like the leaf of harf; it has no smell, and is like harf, its taste and smell resembling qardamana and its power the same {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Temperament
Hot and dry at the end of the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Actions and properties
Cleansing and ulcerating; its taste and smell resemble qardamana, and so does its power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Cosmetics
Smeared with vinegar it benefits bahaq and baras {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Wounds and ulcers
Smeared with vinegar on peeling and scab, it removes it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Joints
It is drunk for pain of the joints and benefits greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Smeared on the spleen, it wastes it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Excretory organs
When its root is hung on the ear of one with pain of the bladder it soothes it, as is said {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}

## Substitutes
Its substitute is fuwwa {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.672}
