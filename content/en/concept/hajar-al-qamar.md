## Nature
Called the saliva of the moon and the foam of the moon. It is gathered when the moon waxes, is found in the lands of the Arabs, and is light. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}

## Organs of the head
It cures epilepsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}
