## Nature
It is the Indian hazelnut, a fruit in a rattling shell of the hazelnut, which splits open on a seed like the coconut {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Temperament
Hot and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Swellings and pustules
Smeared on scrofula with vinegar it benefits it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Wounds and ulcers
It benefits scab and itching {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Joints
It breaks the harmful winds in the back {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}

## Organs of the head
It is used as a snuff for facial palsy and the benefit of it is great, and likewise it benefits migraine and headache, and is a useful snuff for vertigo, epilepsy, madness and melancholia; its snuff has been tried for facial palsy and moisture and much phlegm ran from the nostrils; and the book says the one with palsy must keep to a dark house {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.662}
