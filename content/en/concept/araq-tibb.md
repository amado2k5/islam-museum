## Nature
Sweat is the wateriness of the blood mixed with a bilious serum; what should be used of it is what has not yet dried but still has moisture, and it is riper than urine, for it comes from a surplus of softness and moisture after the last digestion, while urine comes from the surplus of the second digestion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.622}

## Actions and properties
It is riper than urine and differs according to the animal, and it has no little dissolving power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.622}

## Swellings and pustules
The sweat of wrestlers with henna oil benefits a swelling of the groin, indeed dissolves it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.622}

## Organs of the chest
The dried sweat of wrestlers with henna oil is laid on swellings of the breast and dissolves them, and with rose oil on the clotting of milk in the breast {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.622}
