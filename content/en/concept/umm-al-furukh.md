## Definition
Umm al-furukh is a named case in inheritance with a husband, a mother, two sisters of the full or paternal side, and two or more uterine siblings. It is named for the many shares that rise in it, like a bird surrounded by its young. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3402}
It is also called al-balja', for its clarity, and al-shurayhiyya, after the judge Shurayh, in whose time it arose; some say the name applies to any case that rises to ten. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3402}

## Shares
The husband takes a half, the two full or paternal sisters take two thirds, the mother takes a sixth, and the uterine siblings take a third; the total is ten, and the base is six by the majority view. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3403}
