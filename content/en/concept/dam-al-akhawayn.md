## Nature and selection
Nature: a known red juice. Temperament: its heat is not great, though some say it is cold; its dryness is in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}

## Actions
It binds and stops bleeding. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: it sticks fresh ulcers and wounds together. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}
Digestive organs: it strengthens the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}
Excretory organs: it binds the bowels and helps abrasion and fissure of the anus. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}
Substitute: some say lettuce, in all its actions. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.441}
