## Summary
Riq (bondage) is a person's being owned by another; freedom is the original state, and the jurists set out its rules at length. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8858} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8859} Slaves enter ownership by capture in war, by the offspring of a slave-woman, by purchase, gift, bequest and inheritance; a free Muslim may not be enslaved. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8859} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8860} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8861} A non-Muslim may not keep a Muslim slave, and must sell or free him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8862} Owning a relative within the prohibited degrees frees him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8863} The owner may command the slave in what is not sinful, but the work must be within his strength; he must provide food, clothing and care, and may not torment or disfigure him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8864} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8865} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8866} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8867} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8868} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8869} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8870} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8871} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8872} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8873} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8874} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8875} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8876} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8877} The owner may sell, pledge or give the slave, and the schools differ on sale on condition of freedom, on separating relatives in sale, and on the slave's property. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8878} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8879} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8880} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8881} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8882} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8883} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8884} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8885} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8886} In worship the slave is lighter: Friday is not obligatory, the owner pays his zakat, and his vowed fast and i'tikaf need leave;
