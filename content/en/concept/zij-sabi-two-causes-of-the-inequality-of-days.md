## Summary
Al-Battani says this variation is compounded from two sources: one is the variation in the Sun's motion, the equation, and the other the variation in the passage of the signs across mid-heaven, since they do not all rise there by one amount; the most that gathers from the variation of the Sun's motion is close to three parts, a quarter and a tenth, and the most from the passage of the signs in mid-heaven close to four parts, a quarter and a fifth {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.

## The total
What gathers from the two sources together is seven parts and forty-eight minutes, which is half an hour and a fifteenth of a mean hour, approximately. This agrees with the sum of the two figures above, three parts, a quarter and a tenth plus four parts, a quarter and a fifth, and the text also names where the deficiency and the excess lie by the limits of two signs, written in a damaged form in the printed text and not given here {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}. This maximum is what gives the inequality of days its weight in [[concept/zij-sabi-day-inequality-is-felt-in-the-moon|the case of the Moon]] {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.78}.
