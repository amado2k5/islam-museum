## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that retaliation is a prescription written on the killer, the slain and the heir of blood, which quells vengeance and protects society. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.744}

## Free for free
Sha'rawi reads the verse on free for free and slave for slave ([[passage/quran-2-178|2:178]]) as completing the equation, not as limiting the killing to the same class: the killer's penalty is of the kind of his act. He holds that the law brought the pre-Islamic feud back to a minimum, and that excess in revenge, such as killing the chief of the other family, never quenches enmity. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.745} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.746}

## The heir of blood: retaliation or pardon
Sha'rawi says the heir of blood may retaliate or pardon; a pardon turns the killer's life into a gift and changes enmity into affection, and blood-money is paid with kindness, since a pardon is repaid with something better. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.749} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.750}

## Brotherhood in faith
Sha'rawi holds that the verse calls the heir of blood a brother to soften hearts, that brotherhood in faith is above brotherhood by descent, and relates the story of Mus'ab ibn Umayr and his brother Abu Aziz at Badr, given without a chain in the text. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.747} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.748}

## Retaliation as life
Sha'rawi holds that retaliation is life because it deters one who wants to kill, and that penalties are legislated to prevent rather than to happen. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.752} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.753}

## Limits of this batch
The jurists' disagreement on a free man killed for a slave and a man for a woman, the academic view and other views are not entered. Sha'rawi's comparisons with the Torah and the Gospel are not checked here. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.745} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.750}
