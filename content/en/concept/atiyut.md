## Nature and selection
Atiyut is the Indian hazelnut, as the editor's note gives it, and it appears under this name in al-Mu'tamad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the second degree and moist in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}. It has a polishing power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}.

## Uses by organ
Beauty: it clears vitiligo strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.377}.
