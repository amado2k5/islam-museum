## Summary
Rukun in the lexicon is inclining, settling and feeling at ease; in law it is the inclination toward a suitor and the appearance of the woman's or her guardians' consent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8982} Retraction from it is allowed for a valid reason, since marriage is a lasting contract whose harm endures; retraction without a valid reason is disliked. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8982}
