## Summary
In lexicon dhurriyya means offspring, and it is sometimes used for ancestors. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8416}
The majority hold that dhurriyya covers sons and daughters, so that an endowment to one's dhurriyya includes the children of daughters. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8417}
Al-Kharaqi of the Hanbalis holds that the children of daughters are not included. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8417} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8418}
