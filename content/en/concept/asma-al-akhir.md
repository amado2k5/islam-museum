## Overview
al-Akhir — The Last. It is name number 74 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}. Reports naming this name include: [[passage/arbaun-15|15]]، [[passage/muslim-0047|47]]، [[passage/muslim-1354|1354]]، [[passage/muwatta-14-1529|1529]]، [[passage/muwatta-15-1747|1747]].
