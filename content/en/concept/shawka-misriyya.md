## Temperament
Cold in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.685}

## Actions and properties
Drying and cutting catarrhs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.685}

## Wounds and ulcers
Its root, and especially its seed, is strongly healing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.685}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits swelling of the throat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.685}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It benefits swelling of the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.685}
