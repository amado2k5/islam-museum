## Summary
Isa ibn Maryam descends at the white minaret east of Damascus between two ochre garments, his hands on two angels' wings, kills the Dajjal at the gate of Ludd, and rules with justice until wealth overflows {cite:sahih-muslim} {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
