## The management
The first and best is that it be stuffed from inside, then evened from outside, and the stuffing taken out little by little until it settles ⁦(as the book describes)⁩. As for the useful embrocation in it: what must be laid on the break is a little of the known kind ⁦(the embrocation names and parts are not carried)⁩ — though we may return to mention this chapter in the book of fracture and setting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.248}
