## Summary
Imam Ahmad's madhhab is taken from his sayings, deeds and answers; when two sayings of his exist on a question, his companions first try to reconcile them by the ways of usul — carrying the general on the specific or the absolute on the qualified {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 126}. When reconciliation fails and the dates are known, his companions differ: it was said the second saying is his madhhab; it was said both together; and it was said the first, even if he recanted it {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 126}. If the dates are unknown, his madhhab is the saying nearest to the proofs or to his madhhab's principles {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 127}. Ala al-Din al-Mardawi approved the first position in Tas'hih al-Furu' {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 126}.

## The positions
Some of Imam Ahmad's companions said: the second saying is Imam Ahmad's madhhab {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 126}.
Others said: both sayings together are Imam Ahmad's madhhab {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 126}.
A group said: the first saying is Imam Ahmad's madhhab, even if he recanted it {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 126}.
