## On the measure of tremor's treatment
The treatment is the very one given under tremor; but when the numbness is from prevailing blood and the proofs of repletion stand — full veins, swelling jugulars, heaviness of body, sleep, redness of face and eye — a thorough venesection is due, for it mostly removes the numbness alone, with correction of regimen and drying of food {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.159}

## Treating the origin, not the spot alone
When numbness shows in an organ from an antecedent or incidental cause — such as a cold striking the nerve's origin — treatment must not stop at the spot: the origin of the nerve reaching it is treated ⁦(the cautery site is not carried)⁩, and the nerve's origin is likewise treated {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}

## Exercising the organ
Among the treatments useful for numbness is exercising that organ and keeping it in motion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}
