## Old vapours
What comes from a person turning on himself, or from his looking at things that turn, or that are bright or high, is known by itself, as is what comes from a blow or a fall; what comes from the confining of old vapours in the brain, or vapours generated in the brain itself, is a constant illness not following an illness in some organ, not stirring with fullness and quiet with emptiness, preceded by pains of the head, a humming and a ringing, and a heaviness in the head; he finds the dimness of his sight fixed, finds a shortfall in the senses, even in taste and smell, feels a strong throbbing in the arteries in front, and a heaviness in smell {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.115}

## By humour
If the humour in the brain, or elsewhere, from which the vapours stir is phlegm, there are heaviness, cowardice, abundant sleep, difficulty of movement and the signs of phlegm; if it is bile, there are sleeplessness and a burning felt without great heaviness, and yellow, golden imaginings; if it is blood, the vessels are swollen, the face, head and eye red and hot, with heaviness, fatigue, sleep and throbbing; if from black bile, there is a measured heaviness, sleeplessness, imagining hairs, black plates and smoke, corrupt thought and the other signs mentioned {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.115}
