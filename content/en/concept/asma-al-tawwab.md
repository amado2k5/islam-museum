## Overview
at-Tawwab — The Accepter of Repentance. It is name number 80 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-2-128|2:128]]، [[passage/quran-2-160|2:160]]، [[passage/quran-2-37|2:37]]، [[passage/quran-2-54|2:54]]، [[passage/quran-9-104|9:104]]، [[passage/quran-9-118|9:118]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
