## Summary
For one who has vomited too much Ibn Sina orders sleep by every means, binding the limbs and strengthening, astringent poultices on the stomach, and, if he brings up blood, milk mixed with wine, then oxymel cooled with snow and purslane juice with Armenian bole {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.288-289}.

## Sleep and binding the limbs
The one who has vomited excessively is put to sleep, by every device, with his limbs tied as they are tied to hold back diarrhoea, and his stomach treated with strengthening and astringent poultices {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.288}.

## If blood is brought up
If the vomiting goes so far that he empties blood, stop it with milk mixed with wine, four qutulat; it weakens the force of the emetic, stops the blood and softens the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.288}. To clear the chest and stomach of blood so that it does not clot there, give him oxymel cooled a little with snow in small sips; drinking the juice of purslane with Armenian bole also helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.288-289}.

## The emetic drugs
He says the emetic drugs, in their grades, and how each should be given, hellebore above all, are to be sought in the pharmacopoeia and the book of single drugs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.289}.
