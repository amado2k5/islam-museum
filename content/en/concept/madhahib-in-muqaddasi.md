## Summary
In the chapter "On the schools and the dhimma" of the [[work/ahsan-al-taqasim|Ahsan al-taqasim]], [[person/al-muqaddasi|al-Muqaddasi]] classifies the schools "in use in Islam today, which have followers high and low, preachers and congregations" in his time, the fourth century AH, as twenty-eight, in seven groups of four. The classification, names and judgements are all his; the museum reports them as he gave them. {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.47}

## The classification
- **In law**: the Hanafis, Malikis, Shafi'is and Dawudis.
- **In theology (kalam)**: the Mu'tazila, Najjariyya, Kullabiyya and Salimiyya.
- **With both law and theology**: the Shi'a, Kharijites, Karramiyya and Batiniyya.
- **The hadith folk**: the Hanbalis, Rahawiyya, Awza'iyya and Mundhiriyya.
- **Extinct**: the Ata'iyya, Thawriyya, Ibadiyya and a fourth whose name is uncertain in the copy read.
- **Rural**: the Za'faraniyya, Khurramdiniyya, Abyadiyya and Sarakhsiyya.
- **Absorbed by four of their kind**: the Ash'aris over the Kullabis, the Batinis over the Qarmatis, the Mu'tazila over the Qadariyya, the Shi'a over the Zaydis, and the Jahmiyya over the Najjariyya.

{cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.47} He says these are the main schools, which then branch into countless sects. {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.47}

## The labels
He then lists names and labels that, he says, add nothing to the above: four "nicknames", al-Rawafid, al-Mujbira, al-Murji'a and al-Shukkak; four "names of praise", Ahl al-Sunna wal-Jama'a, Ahl al-Adl wal-Tawhid, al-Mu'minun and Ashab al-Huda; and four "disowned" labels, where each party denies a name thrown at it: the Kullabis deny *jabr*, the Hanbalis deny *nasb*, those who affirm the divine attributes deny *tashbih*, and those who deny them reject *ta'til*. {cite:muqaddasi-ahsan-taqasim-bga-1877 p.47}

## Note
This is a fourth-century author's classification of what he saw in his time; words such as "Rawafid" and "Mujbira" are, as he says himself, nicknames that groups threw at one another. No assessment of the classification from another source has been added here.
