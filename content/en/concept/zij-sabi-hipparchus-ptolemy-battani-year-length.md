## The corrections to the year
The text says Hipparchus worked on a year of whole days and a quarter, the day count being garbled in the scan, that Ptolemy made a correction of about one day in roughly three hundred years, and that al-Battani observed after Ptolemy and found the year to be a number of whole days and somewhat less than a quarter, the count being garbled, correcting Ptolemy over about seven hundred and fifty years. The exact figures are partly disturbed in the scan. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.195}

## The later values
Al-Battani's exact fraction of the day and his corrected solar motion in the Egyptian year are disturbed, so only the clear figures are carried over and the others go back to the Latin text. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.195}
