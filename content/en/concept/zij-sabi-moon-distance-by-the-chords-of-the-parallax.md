## Summary
The Moon's distance is computed by chords: the chord of its distance from the zenith point is multiplied by the radius of the circle and divided by the chord of the parallax, which gives the distance in parts, adding one minute for each degree of the chord of altitude. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.128}.

## A check by proportion
Al-Battani repeats the reckoning with a correction and says the figures it gives come close to what the observation shows; he ties the distance to the ratio between the Earth's half-radius and the radius of the sphere. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.128}.
