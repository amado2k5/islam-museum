## Summary
Nutq, in the lexicon, is speech. Mantiq is a related word, and ibara (expression) is narrower than nutq. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15759}

## Rulings on speech
Faith: speaking the two testimonies is required of one able to do so; one who believes and dies before being able to speak is a believer by agreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15759} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15760}
Transactions: speech is a condition for contracts such as marriage, sale and pledge, and for releases such as divorce and annulment; a sign stands in for speech when a person is unable to speak. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15760}
Loss of speech: where an injury to the tongue or head destroys speech entirely, the full blood-money is due; partial loss is treated under blood-money. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.15760}
