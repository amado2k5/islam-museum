## Summary
Ibn Sina counts as signs of complete cleansing that the evacuated humour changes into another, that sound sleep follows the purge or vomit and that thirst grows strong, while agitation means the drug is drawing out something other than the surplus, and a scraping or black stinking matter is bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.278}.

## The signs
As long as the drug is evacuating the surplus there is no agitation with it; once agitation begins it is evacuating something other than the surplus. When the evacuated humour changes by vomiting or purging into another humour, that shows the body is clean of the humour meant to be evacuated, but if it changes into scrapings or something black and stinking, that is bad. Heavy sleep after the purge or vomit shows the evacuation has cleansed the body thoroughly and to good effect, and strong thirst during it shows it has gone far, reached its end and cleansed well. Someone who is purged or made to vomit when his body is already clean cannot avoid dizziness, colic and distress {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.278}.
