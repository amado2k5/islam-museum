# Arab satellite channels — evaluating the experiment

## The episode and the votes
The 'Al-Wasatiyya' episode evaluated the Arab satellite experiment — the channels having 'become the number-one cause in changing minds and values' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:01:30}. On daily viewing: 50% watch three channels or fewer, 25% four to nine, 25% ten or more {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:08:00}. On evaluation: 75% judged the contribution bad, 21% excellent {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:33:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:34:00} — a guest noted the figures reflect impression, not the whole truth {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:34:00}.

## Space-age figures — as presented by the host
From the episode's statistics: 79 variety channels and 37 direct government channels {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:15:00}; and 52% of televised violence falls within children's slots — Tom and Jerry a leading example {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 01:05:00}. The question was put: have the channels corrupted children, 'making them live an era not their own'? {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 01:03:00}

## The new role models — stars, not scholars
Panelists described the role-model shift: talent-show stars like 'Star Academy' displaced scholarly and religious exemplars, while youth simply do not watch cultural or guidance programming {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:59:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 01:00:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 01:01:00}.

## Regulation — moral, not closure
On oversight, 76% voted for moral regulation only, 21% for broader control, and 3% against any control — a centrist equation: open the space with checks, not bans {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:50:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan al-wassitiya/ep12 00:51:00}.
