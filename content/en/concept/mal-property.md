## Overview
The author examines wealth (mal) at the beginning of the second chapter on ownership. For him, it is what is owned—that is, the object of ownership—and from this derive the definitions of the legal schools {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 50} {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 51}.

## Valuable and Non-Valuable Property
The Hanafi school distinguishes between legally valuable property (mutaqawwam) and non-valuable property, and wine belonging to a dhimmi is legally valuable property under the ruling the author cites {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 52}.
