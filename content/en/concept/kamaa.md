## Nature
Dioscorides says it is a rounded root without stalk or fibre, greyish like cotton, found in spring under the ground; some people eat it raw and cooked. It has more earthy substance and less watery, a little airiness and slight fineness, and it is without taste. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}

## Choice and properties
The best is the white sandy kind without a bad smell; dry is worse than moist. The worst kinds are the mushroom, especially those growing under trees and in poor land. It is very thick and gives a thick black-bile food that nothing rivals, and its antidote is pure wine and spices. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.527}

## Organs
Paralysis and apoplexy are feared from it. For the eye, its water as it is clears the sight, a report the book attributes to the Prophet and to al-Masih al-tabib and others. Digestion: it is slow to digest, harmful, heavy on the stomach, thick in its chyme, and slow to pass; Galen says in one place that its chyme is not bad. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.527}

## Excretory organs
It brings on colic and difficult urination. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.527}
