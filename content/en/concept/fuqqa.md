## Nature
Known {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Choice
The best is what is made from refined-flour bread with mint and celery, for what is made from baked bread is not like what is made from unleavened dough bread {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Actions and properties
It is windy, engenders bad humours and is bad food, and its harm to the organs of living creatures is such that ivory steeped in it is softened and becomes easy to work; the kind made from refined bread, celery and mint has good chyme and suits the hot-tempered greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Joints
It greatly harms the nerve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Organs of the head
It harms the membranes of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
The kind made from refined bread is good for a hot stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}

## Excretory organs
The kind made with barley promotes urine and harms the kidneys and the bladder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.637}
