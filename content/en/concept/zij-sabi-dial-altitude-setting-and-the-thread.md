## Setting the dial
The dial is set by a known altitude on the given side. The altitude is fixed, and when it reaches the right amount the dial is turned until the shadow of the gnomon falls on the line from east to west; when that holds the dial is level, its face being plumbed and not tilted. For an altitude at one or two hours the reckoner computes it, then turns the dial until the shadow lies on the hour line. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.209-210}

## The fine thread
If the shadow does not reach the rim of the circle, a fine thread is tied to the end of the gnomon and stretched at the azimuth from the east or the west point. The dial is turned until the middle of the shadow of the gnomon falls on the thread, and the dial is then level and the sixth-hour line lies parallel to the noon line on its azimuth. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.210}
