## Summary
Once the wet nurse meets her conditions, her food must be of good quality: wheat, old wine, meat of lambs and kids, fish whose flesh is neither rotten nor hard, with lettuce, almonds and hazelnuts, while the worst herbs for her milk are rocket, mustard and basil, and mint has some of that force {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.205-206}.

## What is praised
When she meets her conditions, her food must be made excellent: it is drawn from wheat, old wine, meat of lambs and kids, and fish whose flesh is neither putrid nor tough. Lettuce is a praised food, and almonds and hazelnuts as well {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.205}.

## What spoils milk
The worst herbs for her are rocket, mustard and basil, which spoil the milk, and mint has some of that force {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.206}.
