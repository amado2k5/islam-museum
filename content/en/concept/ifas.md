## Summary
In the lexicon, ifas is the container in which money is kept, of leather or cloth, and it is also the cap of a bottle. As a term it is the container in which found property is kept {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11668}.

## The ifas as a mark
The ifas is one of the marks by which found property is recognised, on the basis of a prophetic instruction to know the tie and the container, announce the find for a year, and then keep it or let it go {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11669}.

## Knowing the marks
Most jurists hold that knowing the ifas alone does not suffice; it must be joined with the other marks, such as the tie, the weight, the number and the kind, or most of them {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11669} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11670}.

## The Maliki view
A claimant who knows the ifas and the tie is given the find without oath, on the prevailing view; one who knows only the ifas is not given it at once, and Asbagh requires an oath from one who knows the count and weight {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11670}.

## Where to look further
Found property is treated under «luqta» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11670}.
