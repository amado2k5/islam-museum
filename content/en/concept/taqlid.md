## Overview
Taqlid in usul usage is accepting another's saying without proof; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia gives it a dedicated entry {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia's taqlid entry treats its senses and rulings among the jurists {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}; the founding of usul in al-Shafi'i's Risala is the frame in which the taqlid–ijtihad contest was later fought {cite:risala-shafii}.

## Sha'rawi on imitation of the fathers
Sha'rawi holds that imitation is a natural beginning in the child, who copies those around him across generations, yet fathers may have drifted from the way, so following them in that is not right. He separates reason from knowledge: one who reasons derives, while one who knows takes what another knows. He reads [[passage/quran-2-170|2:170]] as denying the fathers both reason and guidance. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.701} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.702} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.708} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.709}
