## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy says that no punishment is greater for a servant than hardness of heart and distance from God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:08:00} He says that the heart is harmed by four things when they exceed their limit, and that it is revived by remembrance and watered by reflection. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:14:30}

## Causes of hardness: food, sleep, talk and mixing
The Sheikh says that the heart is harmed by four things: excess food, excess sleep, excess talk and excess mixing with people. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:11:30}
He compares this to the body: just as food and drink do not help a sick body, a heart sick with desire is not helped by [a word unclear in the transcript] (needs_source: the closing phrase is garbled in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:11:30}

## A saying on preferring God to desire
The Sheikh says that whoever wants his heart purified should prefer God to his desire, and he gives this as a saying whose speaker he does not name in the recording (needs_source: speaker of the saying not named). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:12:00}

## Remembrance and reflection: reviving the heart
The Sheikh says that the heart is revived by remembrance, watered by reflection and cleansed of weeds, meaning harmful growth. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep05 00:14:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim on the signs of a dead heart: not grieving over acts of obedience one has missed and not regretting the slips one has made. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:54:30} He gives the example of the man who enters the mosque only when carried in to be prayed over, and of one who repeatedly leaves the prayer. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:46:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
In al-Munjiyat Omar Abdelkafy speaks of what the scholars gathered of the things that corrupt the heart, among them knowledge not acted upon, deeds without sincerity or following the Prophet's example, and a heart empty of love and longing for God, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:13:30} and says that whoever does not act on what he knows loses his way in what he does. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:04:00}
