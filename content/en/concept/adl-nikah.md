## Summary
In the lexicon, adl means to prevent. The jurists use it of preventing a marriage, and in divorce for ransom of harming the wife so that she will pay to be released {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11650}.

## The ruling
For a guardian to prevent his ward's marriage to a suitable husband is forbidden, as is a husband's harming his wife to make her ransom herself; a guardian may refuse for the woman's own benefit {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11651}.

## When a guardian is an obstructer
He is one when a woman asks for a suitable husband, or a suitable man proposes, and he refuses without a valid reason. The schools differ on refusing a dower below the usual amount; the Malikis do not count a compelling father's rejection of suitors as obstruction unless he intends harm {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11651}.

## The effect of obstruction
Once obstruction is proven, the judge orders the marriage. If the guardian still refuses, the Hanafis, Shafi'is and Malikis pass guardianship to the ruler, and the main Hanbali view passes it to the next guardian. If all guardians obstruct, the judge contracts the marriage {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11652}.

## Where to look further
Divorce for ransom is treated under «khul'», and guardianship under «wilayat al-nikah» {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11651}.
