## Temperament
Cold at the start of the first degree, dry at the end of the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Actions and properties
It halts every flow and is less astringent and binding than za'rur, it suppresses bile pouring into the viscera, and when taken as a dessert it slows intoxication {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.722}

## Organs of the chest
It benefits hot cough {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Organs of nourishment
It stops vomiting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}

## Excretory organs
It benefits bilious dysentery and binds the belly and vomiting, and likewise za'rur benefits excessive urine, and its flour is less binding to the belly than za'rur; both bind the belly and neither binds urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.723}
