## Overview
For the jurists of usul the Sunna is the second root of rulings after the Book: obedience to the Prophet is binding in what he commanded and forbade, and the Muslims agreed on this in his lifetime and after {cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 40}{cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 41}. The source distinguishes what is certain — the mutawatir — from the ahad reports acted on by preponderant likelihood: no single hadith is ascribed to the Prophet with certainty unless mutawatir, while what inquiry into isnad and matn makes probable must still be followed {cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 41}{cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 42}. The doubts raised against the Sunna's authority — its variants, its late recording — are dismissed as undetailed claims.

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] says the Sunna is revelation in its meaning, its wording being the Prophet's, who was given concise comprehensive speech, and that the Qur'an and the Sunna are the two basic sources of Islam. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:04:00} He sets out the arguments of those he calls the orientalists, that the Qur'an is mass-transmitted and was written in the Prophet's lifetime while the Sunna was compiled after him and admitted forgeries, and answers that the hadith scholars sorted the sound and good from the weak and forged and "taught the whole world what documentation means". {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:03:00} He says the ban on writing anything but the Qur'an belonged to the beginning of revelation, after which the Prophet allowed writing, cites the hadith of the man on his couch content with the Qur'an alone, and holds that "whoever demolishes the Sunna demolishes the Qur'an". {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep02 00:12:00}
