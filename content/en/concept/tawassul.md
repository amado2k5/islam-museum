## Overview

Tawassul is making a means in supplication. The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia details its kinds — through God's names and attributes, through faith and righteous works, and through the Prophet in this life and on Judgment Day — and records three scholarly opinions on tawassul through him after his death {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
