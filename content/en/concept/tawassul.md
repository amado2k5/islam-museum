## Overview

Tawassul is making a means in supplication. The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia details its kinds — through God's names and attributes, through faith and righteous works, and through the Prophet in this life and on Judgment Day — and records three scholarly opinions on tawassul through him after his death {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh tells the hadith of the three Israelites whom a rock shut inside a cave, each of whom appealed to God by his most sincere deed: the first by his devotion to his parents, waiting with the cup of milk until they woke while his children whimpered at his feet; the second by refraining from a widowed neighbour he had tried to seduce and then giving her the money for God's sake; the third by a hired man who left his wage, which he grew into a herd filling the valley and then gave him in full; and the rock moved away.
