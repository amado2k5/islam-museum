## From dryness
For what comes from dryness, the sufferer should use moistening food, and must put away thought and fatigue and use stillness and rest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.95}

## From heat
For what comes from heat besides, the regimen is an increase in the regimen already given and its use {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.95}

## Sounds
Among the things that induce sleep is pleasant, gentle singing with no disturbance in it, whose rhythm is heavy or an even hazaj; for that reason the murmur of water and the rustling of trees induce sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.95}

## From pain
For what comes from pain, the regimen is the soothing of the pain, treating it by what belongs to each pain in its chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.95}

## Salty nitrous moisture
For what comes from salty nitrous moisture, what adds to this moisture must be avoided, and a fine food is taken, and evacuation is made {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.95} If this kind befalls in old age, its cure is hard {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.95}

## Lesser sleeplessness
One whose sleeplessness is not so excessive may need only to tire himself and exercise, then eat, and he sleeps a moderate sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.96}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.95-96}
