## Summary
Al-Battani closes chapter 12 with a geometrical proof of the altitude whose azimuth does not deviate: from the Sun's point he draws a perpendicular on the horizon diameter, the chord of the midday altitude, and makes a right-angled triangle with known sides similar to another, so that the ratios between their sides are equal; he then multiplies and divides to find the chord of the required altitude, whose arc in the example comes out, on the scan's reading, at forty-two degrees thirty-two minutes. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.42}.

## The two right-angled triangles and the ratios
He says: further, we draw from a point a perpendicular to the diameter line, parallel to another line, which is the chord of the midday altitude, so a line remains that is the chord of the arc which is the complement of the altitude, and we want to find the line that is the chord of the altitude with no deviation of azimuth. He then shows that the right-angled triangle has become known in its sides and is proportional to another triangle, since one of its angles equals an angle of the other and the right angle is common to the two, so that the ratio of one side to another is like the ratio of the corresponding sides. The figure's letters are unclear in the scan and are not given. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.42}.

## Multiplication, division and the result
He then says that when we remove from one line the ratio to another, there remains for us the ratio of one line to another, and the outcome is that one line is multiplied by another, giving a large number of parts which is divided by the sum of two chords, and the length of the required line results, the arc corresponding to it being the altitude with no deviation of azimuth. In the example the result, as the scan reads it, came to forty-two degrees thirty-two minutes; the number words are partly damaged, so this is read with caution, and the intermediate products are not given because they cannot be read. He ends: that is what we wished to show, God willing. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.42}.
