## The rule
The inference taken from the kind of acts: if the acts are sound they help to indicate soundness of the brain, and if they are afflicted they indicate an affliction in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.11} The afflictions of acts are three, weakness, change and confusion, and then failure {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.11}

The general statement for inference from acts is that their lessening and failure come from cold, and from thickness of the spirit through moisture and blockage, and do not come from heat unless it grows great enough that the power collapses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.11} Confusion, or what resembles movement, may come from heat and may come from dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.11}
