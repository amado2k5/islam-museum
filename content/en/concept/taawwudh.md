## Overview
Ta'awwudh is seeking refuge with God from Satan before and during recitation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia examines the refuge formulas and the best of them, pausing on the formula, and repeating it when recitation is interrupted {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
