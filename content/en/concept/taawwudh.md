## Overview
Ta'awwudh is seeking refuge with God from Satan before and during recitation {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia examines the refuge formulas and the best of them, pausing on the formula, and repeating it when recitation is interrupted {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In Ahmed Abdussalam's programme 'Osalli Alayh'
[[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]] gives three episodes of his programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']] to seeking refuge in God. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep19 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep20 00:00:30} He relates from Ahmad's Musnad, graded sound by al-Albani as he says, that devils gathered against the Prophet one night and Gabriel taught him to seek refuge in God's perfect words from the evil of created things in the sky and on the earth and from the trials of night and day, and their fire went out. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:04:30} He says the night comes first in the refuge because its trial is harder, thieves and highwaymen being abroad then, and recommends reciting the three suras of refuge three times morning and evening. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep18 00:08:00} He then explains refuge from four things, the hardship of trial, being overtaken by misery, an evil decree and the gloating of enemies, saying it was the Prophet's way to seek refuge from trial before it came. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep19 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep19 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep19 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep20 00:00:30}
