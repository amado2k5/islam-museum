## Its definition and treatment

The tuta is soft flesh arising inside the eyelid, from which red, black and green blood keeps flowing. Its treatment is cleansing with eating desiccants; once it has eaten the tuta away, the medicines that grow flesh are used as stated under the ulcers of the lids, and in sum its treatments are the treatments of the corneal itch and scab ⁦(the names are not reproduced)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.197}
