## Summary
In Ka'b's account at Ibn Ishaq, "God's repentance" upon the three is declared after the boycott; Ka'b counts as part of his repentance matters touching his wealth and the truthfulness of his speech. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/180-181}

## In the story of the three
The Prophet declared God's repentance upon them after the dawn prayer, and the people congratulated him on it: "Rejoice in God's repentance upon you" ([[event/repentance-of-the-three-accepted|the event]]). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/180}

## What Ka'b counted of his repentance
Ka'b said part of his repentance was to strip off his wealth as alms — the Prophet ordered him to keep some of it — and part of it was to speak only truth as long as he lived ([[event/kaab-offers-his-wealth-and-vows-truthfulness|the event]]). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/180}

## Notes
- This page describes the concept only as it appears in Ka'b's account; the general legal definition of repentance and its rulings need the fiqh and creed sources (needs_source).
