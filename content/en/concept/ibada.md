## Overview
Ibada is the comprehensive name for the acts of the heart and limbs sanctioned by the sharia; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats the rulings of worship from its opening books {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia's discussions include the intention that vitiates worship, seeking others' help in worship, and the role of worship in pre-Islamic Arabia {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
