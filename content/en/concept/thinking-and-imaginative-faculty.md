## Summary
The second of the inner powers is what physicians call the thinking faculty and the verifiers call at times imaginative and at times thinking; it works on what imagination has stored by composing and dividing, and its seat is the middle ventricle of the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.100}.

## Its name
Physicians call it the thinking faculty, and the verifiers sometimes call it imaginative and sometimes thinking: if the animal estimating power uses it, or it rises by itself to its act, they call it imaginative; if the rational power turns to it and directs it to what is of use, it is called thinking {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.100}.

## Its act
The difference between it and the first is that the first receives or keeps the sensed forms that reach it, while this one works on what is stored in imagination by composing and dividing, bringing forth forms like those that came from sense and forms unlike them, such as a flying man or a mountain of emerald; imagination brings forth only what it received from sense. Its seat is the middle ventricle of the brain, and it is a tool for the power that is in truth the inner perceiver in animals, estimation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.100}.
