## Summary
Ibn Sina says light foods better guard health but give less help to strength and endurance, and thick foods the reverse; those who exercise and labour much bear thick food better, helped by deep sleep, though heavy sweating may strip their livers of food not yet digested and prepare deadly illnesses late or early in life {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## Light and thick
Light foods preserve health better and help strength and endurance less, and thick foods are the reverse; one who needs endurance and so needs foods strong in chyme should wait for severe hunger and take of them not too much, so that it digests {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## Those who exercise
Those given to exercise and much toil bear thick foods better, and what helps them digest is the strength and depth of their sleep {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.

## The danger
But because they sweat much and their bodies dissolve, their livers may be stripped of food not yet digested, which readies them for deadly illnesses at the end of life or at its start; their digestion, which depends on sleep, fails if sleeplessness follows one night on another, especially if they bathe {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.233}.
