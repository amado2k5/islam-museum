## Choice
The best is the golden, clustered one, ash-coloured and soft, and the plate-like is coarser {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Temperament
Moderate, leaning to dryness in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.650}

## Actions and properties
It and its washed form are finer than silver qulimiya, and it has drying and clearing in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Wounds and ulcers
It fills wounds, cleans their dirt, eats their proud flesh and heals malignant ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}

## Ocular organs
It benefits whiteness of the eye and the beginning of the water, and strengthens the eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.651}
