## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that lowering the gaze protects insight, and that many incidents begin with a single glance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:30:00}

## The Qur'anic command
The Sheikh cites the Qur'anic command to believers to lower their gaze and guard their chastity, in [[passage/quran-24-30|the verse in Surat al-Nur]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:30:30}

## An innocent glance
The Sheikh tells the story of a man whose innocent glance went unnoticed until he struck a wall and bled. He uses it as a lesson that a glance can lead to what follows. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:30:00}

## The glance takes insight
The Sheikh holds that a glance takes away the insight of the one who looks, and that many incidents begin with a glance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:31:00}

## Rejecting the passing thought
The Sheikh holds that a passing thought should be rejected from the start. If it is left to grow, it becomes an intention and then a deed. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:31:30}

## Insight of the heart
The Sheikh says that whoever lowers his gaze, God gives him insight (needs_source: the saying is not sourced in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep13 00:30:30}
