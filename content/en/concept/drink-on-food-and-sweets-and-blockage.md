## Summary
Ibn Sina says drink taken on top of food is among the most harmful things, since it digests and passes quickly and carries the food on undigested, bringing blockages, rot and sometimes scab; sweets quickly cause blockages, which lead to many illnesses such as dropsy; where heavy air and water, especially in summer, spoil food, a mixed cup or hot water boiled with aloeswood and mastic does no harm {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.232}.

## Drink on food
Drink on food is among the most harmful things, because it digests and passes quickly and drives the food through undigested, bringing blockages, putrefaction and, at times, scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.232}.

## Sweets and blockage
Sweets quickly bring blockages because nature draws them before digestion; blockages lead to many illnesses, among them dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.232}.

## Heavy air and water
Heavy air and water, above all in summer, spoil food, so there is no harm in drinking over it a mixed cup, or hot water in which aloeswood and mastic have been boiled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.232}.
