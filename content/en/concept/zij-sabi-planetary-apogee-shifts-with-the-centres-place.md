## Summary
When the epicycle's centre lies between two points of the eccentric and less than half of its circle, the true far point of the planet differs from the mean one; the true place is then above or below the mean, depending on where the centre is. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.97}.

## Effect on the equation
It follows that when the planet is in the half nearer the Earth the equation of the centre is added to the equation, and when it is in the other half it is subtracted; this fixes each equation's share of increase and decrease. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.97}.
