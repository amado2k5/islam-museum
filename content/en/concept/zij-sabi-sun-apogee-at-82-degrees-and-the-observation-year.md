## Summary
Al-Battani reckons that the point of greatest distance of the eccentric lies about seven parts and forty-three minutes before the summer solstice point, toward the leading direction of the zodiac, that is at eighty-two parts and seventeen minutes from the first of Aries, in the observation he relied on, the year one thousand one hundred and ninety-four of the years of Dhu'l-Qarnayn {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.70-71}.

## How he arrives at it
He compares the line between the centres with the radius, finding the radius about twenty-eight and a half and a third times that line, and from this ratio and the sine of the angle he reaches an arc of the apogee of about seven parts and forty-three minutes. He says the observation he worked from was made in the year one thousand one hundred and ninety-four, when he observed the Sun's course from the first of Aries to the first of Cancer and to the first of Libra {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.71}. The result is the place from which [[concept/zij-sabi-sun-equation-tabulated-by-degrees|the tables of the equation, reckoned for each degree]], are drawn {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.71}.
