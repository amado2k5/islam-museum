## Summary
In chapter 6 Ibn Sina says washing in cold water suits only someone whose regimen is thorough in every respect, with age, strength, physique and season fitting, and who has no indigestion, vomiting, looseness, sleeplessness or catarrh, who is neither child nor old man, at a time when the body is lively and movements come readily {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.

## The conditions
It suits only a person whose regimen is thorough in every respect, whose age, strength, physique and season are fitting, who has no indigestion, vomiting, looseness, sleeplessness or catarrh, and who is neither a child nor an old man, at a time when his body is lively and his movements ready {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.

## After hot water
It may be used after hot water to strengthen the skin and shut in the innate heat; if that is wanted the water should not be very cold but moderate {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.227}.
