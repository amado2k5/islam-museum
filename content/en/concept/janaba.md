## Overview

Janaba in Islamic fiqh is the major ritual impurity obliging ghusl before prayer and other acts conditioned on purity. The Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats its causes and what lifts it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
