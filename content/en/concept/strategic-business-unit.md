## Summary
Tareq al-Suwaidan distinguishes the line of business (LOB) from the strategic business unit (SBU): the line of business is the strategic axis — 'what do you sell?' — and the SBU is any part of the work that can stand alone, provided it belongs to the core business {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 00:49:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:01:30}.

## Lines of business
He holds that a company's natural state is a single line of business, and it should not exceed three unless they are related — training, consulting and education are related; training, timber and a restaurant are not. He recounts that his company bought a profitable Malaysian timber firm and lost it within a year because it lay outside their core work, selling it at a ten per cent loss (the speaker's experience) {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 00:50:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 00:52:30}.

## The strategic business unit
He defines it as any part of the work able to stand alone, provided it is of the core business: it has its own plan, management, budget and decisions, without being wholly independent of funding and the master plan {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:03:30}.
There is no limit to the number of units under one line of business, provided each is treated as independent; while IT, legal affairs, personnel and accounting are not of the core business and count as 'support units' {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:05:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:07:00}.
His rule for placing support units: if a service will occupy an employee full-time it is attached to the unit, otherwise it stays shared or central — each service is judged separately, since accounting is daily while legal counsel is occasional {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:11:00}.

## Independence grows a business
He states that whoever wants a business to grow must not subordinate it to another business but grant it independence — its own management, plan, budget and decisions; he recalls that his company Ibda' al-Fikri reached the revenues of Ibda' al-Khaliji within three or four years precisely because it was not made part of it {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:12:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:13:00}.
He also holds that the organisation chart changes whenever the plan changes — the chart is formed to support the plan — and that fixing the chart while changing the plan is a grave error in many organisations {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan administrative-processus-abilities/ep02 01:32:30}.

## In "The Academy for Preparing Leaders": lines of business and units
In the strategic-planning lessons of both filmed leaders' academies, al-Suwaidan places strategic business units after the line of business. ([[work/leadership-development-academy-1-program|first academy]], lesson 14; [[work/leadership-development-academy-2-program|second academy]], session 5.) A line of business is an axis that holds certain of the organisation's products or services. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep14 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:28:30} His main rule is one line of business for each organisation, or at most two or three closely related ones. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep14 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:29:30} Governments, holding companies and broad movements he treats as umbrellas over specialised bodies. Holding companies, he says, "own and do not govern". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:33:00} He defines a strategic business unit as "every product or service that is of the core of the work and able to become independent in the future". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep14 00:36:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:40:00} As an example of when a product line becomes a unit he cites 3M's rule of forty million dollars in sales. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep14 00:39:30} Whatever is likely to grow, he advises, should be given its own unit from the start. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:42:30} Support services, he says, should not be merged into one shared unit; he calls that the worst option. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep14 00:48:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:43:00} The structure follows from the units and lines of business and is adjusted to fit the plan. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep14 00:52:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-2/ep05 00:43:00}
