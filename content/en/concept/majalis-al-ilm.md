## Summary
Study circles are gatherings in which people come together to receive knowledge. Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that the Friday sermon is never enough to give the heart a charge of faith that keeps it at its level all week, so the heart needs a study circle. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:02:00}

## No tape or channel replaces them
He says that some people stop attending study circles and make do with a recorded tape, the radio or television channels, and that this never replaces attending the circle. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:02:00}

## Their merit in the Sheikh's words
He speaks of the merit of one who purifies himself at home and then sets out for one of the houses of God, says that the angels surround a study circle, and likens their lowering of their wings for the seeker of knowledge to the carpet rolled out for an honoured guest at an airport. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:05:00}
He counts his own lesson on the Hikam as one of these gatherings, in which the heart finds rest in the remembrance of God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:08:30}
