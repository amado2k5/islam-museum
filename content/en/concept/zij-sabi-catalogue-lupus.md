## Lupus (al-Sab')

The catalogue names the figure al-Sab', the beast (bellua). Its section follows Centaurus, and the notes after it discuss a star that other Arabs call rijl Qintawrus, the foot of the Centaur, which the editor cites Baily on as unidentified. Positions are not transcribed here. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum pp.174}
