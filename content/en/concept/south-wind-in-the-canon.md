## Summary
In the chapter on the causes of winds Ibn Sina describes what the south wind does to the body {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.126}.

## What it does
The south wind slackens the power, opens the pores, stirs the humours and moves them outward, and weighs down the senses. It spoils sores, brings diseases back and weakens, causes itching on sores and in gout, stirs headache, brings sleep and breeds putrid fevers, though it does not roughen the throat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.126}.
