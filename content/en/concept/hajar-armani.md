## Nature
A stone with a little of the quality of lapis lazuli, but not its colour or its density; it has some grit in it. Painters and dyers sometimes use it in place of lapis lazuli. It is soft to the touch. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}

## Organs of digestion
It is bad for the stomach. The washed stone does not cause vomiting; the unwashed stone does. In every case it is bad for the stomach. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}

## Excretory organs
It purges black bile with a strong purge, stronger than the purge of lapis lazuli. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}
