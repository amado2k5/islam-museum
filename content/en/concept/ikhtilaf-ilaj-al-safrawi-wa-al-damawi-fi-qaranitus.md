## The difference
The bilious needs in his treatment more purging of bile and less bleeding, and at bleeding he is not taken near fainting but bled a fair amount with care against that, then evacuated by purging, and his remedies are cold and moist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.82} The foods of the sanguine are cold and may be astringent once the purging is done, but these do not suit the bilious {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.82} Know that the bilious needs more quenching and the sanguine more dissolving; one is not so wary of cooling in the bilious as one is in the sanguine, and cold water is not so shunned for him, and care for sleep must be greater {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.82} Where the bile of the bilious is burnt, care for moistening is multiplied as far as possible {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.82}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.82}
