## Nature and selection
Its power is close to fenugreek's {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the first degree, moderate in moisture and dryness. Some say the boiled flax is the boiled green flax, and it has surplus moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: ripens and cleanses, and bloats from its surplus moisture; roasted, it binds; unroasted, it is moderate and mixed with softening. It eases pain, less than chamomile {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Adornment: with natron and fig, a poultice for freckles and milky pustules {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Swellings and pustules: softens hot swellings, inside and outside, swellings behind the ear treated with ash-water, and hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Joints: helps cramp, especially cramp of the nails when mixed with wax and honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Head: its smoke helps a cold, as does the smoke of flax itself {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Breath: helps phlegmy cough, especially the roasted kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Digestion: bad for the stomach, slow to digest, low in food value {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.

Excretory organs: roasted, it binds the bowel; unroasted, it is moderate, and its urine-moving power is weak but strengthened by roasting; taken with honey and pepper it stirs desire. The book says the decoction injected into the womb, with sitting in it, helps without burning or swelling there, and seed injected with rose oil greatly helps the ulcers of the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.410}.
