## Summary
Ihtibas in the lexicon is restraint and holding back. It differs from hajr, interdiction, in that restraint serves the one who holds back, while interdiction serves the person interdicted. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1716} It differs from hasr, confinement with constriction, in that restraint need not include constriction. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1716} Whoever detains a person or an animal owes its maintenance; this is why a wife held back is owed maintenance, and a private worker is owed his wage simply by being held available. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1717} Prayer with wind or excrement held back is disliked, and the rain prayer is sunna when rain is withheld. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1717}
