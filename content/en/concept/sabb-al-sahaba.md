## Summary
The Sheikh counts reviling any of the Prophet's Companions as a very grave fault of the tongue, and hopes that whoever is afflicted with it repents before leaving this world. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:30}

## The Companions as friends of God
He cites a hadith qudsi in the two Sahihs about whoever shows enmity to a friend of God, and says: if the Companions are not God's friends, then God has no friend on earth. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:30} He quotes [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] as saying that God's friends are the scholars, and that if the scholars are not, God has no friend; then he asks: how much more the Companions of the Prophet? {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:01:00}

## Hadiths forbidding reviling them
He mentions the hadith forbidding the reviling of the Companions, in which spending gold the size of Mount Uhud does not reach a handful of theirs or half of it, and the hadith warning against making them a target; he likens the words 'God, God, regarding my Companions' to someone crying 'fire, fire!' in warning. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:02:00} He mentions a hadith from Anas about those who revile the Companions, and a report forbidding eating, sitting, intermarrying and praying with those who disparage them, as these reached him (needs_source: neither report is referenced in the recording). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:05:30} He mentions Ibn Mas'ud's hadith on holding back when the Companions are mentioned. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:05:30}

## Why he holds it grave
He holds that the Companions were the first to believe, strove, spread the religion and taught people the obligations and the sunna, and that without them neither a root nor a branch of the religion, nor a single hadith of the Prophet, would have reached us. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:03:30} He judges that whoever attacks the Companions leaves the religion as an arrow passes through its target, and that reviling them shows the rancour in the heart against the best people after the Prophet. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:04:00} This judgement is his; the museum reports it as his.

## Examples of their merits, as he tells them
- [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]]: he stopped supporting [[person/mistah-ibn-uthatha|Mistah ibn Uthatha]] when Mistah took part in the slander against Aisha, then resumed when the verse on pardon in Surat al-Nur came down; he also mentions the Qur'an's description of him as the companion in the cave. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:07:30}
- [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]]: equipped the army of hardship and married two daughters of the Prophet. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:08:00}
- [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]]: he mentions the hadith of the station (like Harun to Musa). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:08:00}
- [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd]]: he mentions the Prophet's words 'this is my maternal uncle'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:08:00}
- The Emigrants and the Helpers: he mentions the Qur'an's praise of the Helpers' self-preference. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:08:30}
