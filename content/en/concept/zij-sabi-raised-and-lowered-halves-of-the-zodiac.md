## The raised and the lowered
The parts that fall opposite to one another are called raised and lowered. The parts of the southern half, from the beginning of Libra to the end of Pisces, are called the lowered; the parts of the northern half, from the beginning of Aries to the end of Virgo, are called the raised. The growth of the day in the northern parts equals that of their opposite parts. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.199-200}

## Reading the names
The name lowered is read from a disturbed phrase on page 199, and the reading given is the closest to the context. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.199}
