## Summary
This page is a single table of sixty of the most-cited books in the sciences of Arabic, twelve for each of dictionaries, grammar, morphology, prosody and rhetoric, as they appear in a research summary supplied to the museum. The books are in chronological order within each science, and the last column gives what the summary says of the book's presence in the Shamela library: yes, no, or partly where the summary says the book has no entry of its own but something related to it is indexed. Each title opens the museum's record of the book, with the documenting pages the summary named.

## What is not known
The records of the first twenty-four books (dictionaries and grammar) and of the other thirty-six were made together, and all of them say what they rest on: their facts are the summary's as it stands, the museum did not open the pages the summary draws on, the founder approved using the summary without re-verification on 21 November 2026, and the museum has read these books only where a book's record says it read pages of it, namely al-Idah of al-Qazwini, Miftah al-Ulum of al-Sakkaki, the Badi of Ibn al-Mu'tazz and Dala'il al-I'jaz of al-Jurjani.

## Basis
This page rests on a research summary supplied to the museum; the museum did not open the pages it cites, and the founder approved using it without re-verification on 21 November 2026. The facts are as the summary reports them, and each book's record names its documenting pages.
