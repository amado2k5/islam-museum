## Gradation of treatment
Recurrent ikhtilaj is fomented with heated compresses; if it clears, well; if not, dissolving oils are used, beginning from the weaker toward the stronger; if it clears, well; if not, the laxative is given, and afterwards anointing the organ with warming drugs is kept up ⁦(the names of the oils and drugs and their reckoned properties are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}

## What is avoided, and the fann's close
He does not take iced water, nor much wine, nor what is flatulent and cooling; its treatment approaches the treatment of its sisters, and with these words the book closes the speech on the postural disorders: as for the tumours, the solutions of continuity and the rest, they are deferred to the Fourth Book ⁦(the book's own closing of Fann 2)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}
