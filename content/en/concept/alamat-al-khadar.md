## The signs are the causes
The very signs are the causes and are evident, as was said under tremor; the numbness's increasing as a cause increases and lessening as it lessens points out the cause {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.159}

## Signs of the sanguineous numbness
If the numbness is from prevailing blood there stand the proofs of vein-fulness, swelling jugulars, heaviness of body, sleep, redness of face and eye and the like {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.159}
