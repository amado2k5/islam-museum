## Summary
Once he knows what has turned of the sphere, al-Battani adds it to the rising times of the Sun's degree in the clime and so finds the ascendant in the way set out at the beginning of the book. A second way uses the returned arc, which is the Sun's distance from the midheaven line: he divides it by the times of the hours of the day and subtracts it from six hours or adds it to them, then subtracts the arc from the right-sphere risings or adds it to them and so finds the ascendant and midheaven. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.49}.

## The ascendant from the arc that has turned
He says: if you want to find the ascendant from what has turned of the sphere, add what has turned to the rising times opposite the Sun's degree in the clime; with the sum you know the ascendant in the way whose source we set out at the beginning of the book. The ascendant here is the degree of the ecliptic that is rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of measurement, found by the taqwis of the risings as explained in [[concept/zij-sabi-degree-from-risings-taqwis-al-matali|converting risings to degrees]]. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.49}.

## The returned arc as the Sun's distance from midheaven
Then he says: if you wish, take the returned arc that results, which is the Sun's distance from the midheaven line, divide it by the times of the hours of the day, and subtract the result from six hours if the measurement was before midday or add it to six hours if after midday; the total is how many seasonal hours of the day have passed, and you can turn them into equal hours if you wish. If you want the ascendant from this returned arc, subtract it from the risings of the Sun's degree in the right sphere if the measurement was before midday and add it to the risings if after; with the resulting risings you know the ascendant and midheaven, by taking the taqwis of these risings in the clime and in the right sphere and noting the degrees of the signs that match them, in the way stated in the chapter on knowing the degrees of the signs from the rising times. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.49}.
