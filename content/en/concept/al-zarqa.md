## Its causes
Blueness occurs either from a cause in the coats or in the humours: if the vitreous is clear, near to the outside and moderate or little in amount, the eye is blue from it if the coat does not oppose; if the humours are turbid, or the icy humour little and the vitreous much, it darkens like deep water; if the icy is sunken the eye is kohl-dark. The cause in the coats is in the uvea: if black the eye is kohl-dark from it, if blue it makes the eye blue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.191}

## Why the uvea becomes blue
The uvea becomes blue either from unripeness — like a plant which when it first sprouts does not show its dye but is whitish, then greens as it ripens — and for this reason children's eyes are blue and shuhl, a blueness from extreme moisture; or from dissolution of the dye-following moisture when it is over-ripe — like a plant whitening as its moisture dissolves — a blueness from dominant dryness; the sick and the aged get shuhl eyes for this reason, for the aged have much foreign moisture and dissolving innate moisture. Or it may be a colour that occurred in creation, known by the goodness or badness of sight; of blueness some is natural and some incidental. The shahla arises from the meeting of the kohl causes and the blue causes, a thing composed between kohl and blue; were the shahla from the fiery element as Empedocles supposed, the blue eye would be harmed by lacking the fire which is the instrument of sight ⁦(the supposition transmitted as a saying attributed to Empedocles, which the book answers)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.191} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.192}

## The difference of sight in them
If the eye is blue from littleness of vitreous moisture it sees better by night and in darkness than by day, for the light stirs the little matter and keeps it from discerning; the kohl-dark from moisture sees less by night, for that needs focusing and moving the matter outward, and copious matter is slower than little; the kohl from the coat gathers sight more strongly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.192}

## The treatment
Collyriation with prepared drugs has been tried; saffron itself and its oil are among what blackens the pupil, as is what blackens the blue-eyed pupil; and the book adds that the foster-nurse, if black or Abyssinian and nursing the boy, removes the blueness ⁦(drug names and measures are not carried; the foster-nurse line transmitted as a labelled listing for the founder's ruling)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.192} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.193}
