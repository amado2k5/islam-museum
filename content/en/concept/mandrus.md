## Nature and selection
Dioscorides said: it is the gum of a tree found in the lands of the Arabs and India, with a slight likeness to myrrh, disagreeable in taste; people take its smoke and perfume clothes with its smoke together with myrrh and storax, and those gums are cooked on the fire and become sandrus {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Actions and properties
It has astringency, and its special property is that it stops blood; wrestlers use it to be light and strong and not to be left breathless {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Adornment
It has a strong thinning power when it is drunk each day {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It dries fistulas when it is used as a fumigation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Its smoke prevents catarrhs, its benefit in calming toothache is very great and nothing equals it, and it mends the gums {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps palpitation as amber does, prevents the flow of blood, and prevents moist asthma by drying it, which is why wrestlers use it so as not to be left breathless {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Eyes
It clears marks in the eye quickly, and cures weak sight when dissolved in wine and used as an eye salve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It is given to those with disease of the spleen and helps {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.585}.

## Excretory organs
It is good for long-standing diarrhoea, and its smoke helps haemorrhoids {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.585}.
