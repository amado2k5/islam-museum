## Its causes
Squint may be from relaxation of some of the muscles moving the eyeball, so it inclines from that side to the opposite side, or from convulsion of some of them so the eyeball inclines toward them; either way it may be from moisture and may occur from dryness as in the acute diseases. Where the cause is convulsion of the muscles, it is either a convulsion of brain cause like epilepsy, or a convulsion of the holding muscles in their origin so that no lesion shows — rather it benefits greatly. Squint often occurs after brain diseases like qaranitus and cataract and the like, from burning and dryness or also repletion. Know that the eye's turning upward and downward is what shows one thing as two, while sideways does not harm sight appreciably {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.189}

## The treatment
The congenital does not heal, except in very moist childhood where healing may be hoped for, especially if recent: in such a case the cradle should be levelled and the lamp placed on the side opposite the squint so that the child always exerts turning toward it; likewise a thread with something red should be tied facing the squint side, or something red stuck at the opposite temple or the ear — all so that it costs him the least effort in looking — for that tasking may succeed in straightening the eye and sending blood so as to make the gaze straight. As for those afflicted after maturity and in old age, whose cause is relaxation or a moist convulsion, they must use cleansing of the brain with the evacuations we mentioned by the great electuaries and the like, soften the regimen and use the dissolving bath; if its occurrence is from dry convulsion the moistening douches must be used; if there is no fever they are given the milks with very moistening oils — in sum their regimen must be moistened ⁦(the named drugs, bloods and milks are not carried; the footnote on the milks is the editor's)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.189} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.190}
