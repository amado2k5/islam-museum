## Overview
Israfil is the angel charged with blowing the trumpet, according to books of tafsir and hadith. He is not named in the Qur'an; rather the trumpet and blowing in it are mentioned in places such as [[passage/quran-39-68|Az-Zumar 68]], [[passage/quran-78-18|An-Naba 18]], [[passage/quran-101-1|Al-Qari'a 1]], and [[passage/quran-82-1|Al-Infitar 1]]. The name "Israfil" is related in books of tafsir and hadith, not in the Qur'an {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the Qur'an
- [[passage/quran-39-68|Az-Zumar 68]]: "and the trumpet is blown, and whoever is in the heavens and on earth is struck, except whom God wills, then it is blown again and they stand up looking" — in it the two blasts {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-78-18|An-Naba 18]]: "a day when the trumpet is blown and you come forth in groups" — in it blowing the trumpet {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-101-1|Al-Qari'a 1]], [[passage/quran-101-2|Al-Qari'a 2]], [[passage/quran-101-3|Al-Qari'a 3]], [[passage/quran-101-4|Al-Qari'a 4]], and [[passage/quran-101-5|Al-Qari'a 5]]: the surah of Al-Qari'a on the blast and the striking {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-82-1|Al-Infitar 1]], [[passage/quran-82-2|Al-Infitar 2]], [[passage/quran-82-3|Al-Infitar 3]], and [[passage/quran-82-4|Al-Infitar 4]]: on the cleaving, in which "when the heaven is cleft" and "when the stars are scattered" {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- [[passage/quran-69-13|Al-Haqqa 13]], [[passage/quran-69-14|Al-Haqqa 14]], [[passage/quran-69-15|Al-Haqqa 15]], and [[passage/quran-69-16|Al-Haqqa 16]]: on the Resurrection, in which are scenes of the Day of Judgment {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## In hadith
- "Israfil" is not named in the two Sahihs and the well-known Sunan; rather he is known by the description "the holder of the trumpet" or "the angel charged with blowing" in books of tafsir and hadith {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir} {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.
- In a narration that the Prophet saw Israfil on the Mi'raj, in which Israfil was standing ready, placing the horn to his mouth and awaiting the command without turning his gaze from it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:20:30}.
- In a narration that the Prophet feared being commanded to blow {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:22:00}.
- In a narration that the first blast destroys whoever is in the heavens and on earth except whom God wills, then the second comes and people stand up looking {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:30:00}.
- In a narration that the blast makes the mountains move before it like clouds {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:22:00}.
- In a narration that God commands the Angel of Death to take the souls until none remains but him, then Israfil is revived for the second blast {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:29:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:29:30}.

## About the name "Israfil"
- The name "Israfil" is related in books of tafsir and hadith, and some hadith scholars question it {cite:mizan-itidal-azimabadi-v1} {cite:nazarat-mawdu-iyya-azhar}.
- The principle in the Qur'an is that the trumpet and blowing in it are mentioned without a name {cite:tanzil-quran}.
- In the transmitted books of tafsir it is mentioned that "Israfil" is the non-Arabic name of the angel charged with blowing {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## With the exegetes
Ibn Kathir details that "the trumpet" is the horn in which the angel charged with it blows, and mentions that the blowing is twice {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}. Al-Qurtubi holds that "the blowing" in [[passage/quran-39-68|Az-Zumar 68]] is the first blast in the trumpet {cite:mufhim-qurtubi-v7}.

## His description and work
- He places the horn to his mouth and awaits the command, not turning his gaze from it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:19:30}.
- He blows twice: the first destroys whoever is in the heavens and on earth except whom God wills, and the second God raises people with it {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:29:30}.
- In a narration the blast makes the mountains move before it like clouds {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03 00:22:00}.

## Notes for researchers
- "Israfil" is a name related in books of tafsir and hadith; the Qur'an does not name him {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
- In it is evidence that the trumpet and blowing in it are among the greatest things mentioned of the Resurrection {cite:tanzil-quran}.
