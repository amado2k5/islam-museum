## Power, matter and psychical events
The causes of ikhtilaj are a cooled power and a moist matter; ikhtilaj often arises from psychical events, especially joy, and likewise from grief and anger and the like, for the spirit's motion can dissolve matters into winds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}

## What it warns of
Generalised over the body it warns of apoplexy or tetanus; persistent in the temples it warns of melancholy; persistent in the face it warns of facial palsy; and twitching of the eyelid may be followed by a swelling in the diaphragm, being among its consequences {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.160}
