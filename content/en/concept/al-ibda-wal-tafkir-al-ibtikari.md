## Summary
"Creativity and innovative thinking" is the subject of two lessons by [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] in [[work/leadership-development-academy-1-program|the Leaders' Academy]]. He says he normally teaches it over five days. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:02:00} He adds his own definition to the more than two hundred he says exist: "creativity is the process of bringing about something new". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:02:30} He chose "bringing about" rather than "thinking of" on purpose. Thinking of something new is innovative thinking, and it becomes creativity only when it is carried out. He also calls creativity a process with seven steps, not a sudden flash. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:04:30} The models, percentages and laws below are his teaching. He credits the "creativity machine" to an American method that he says he reworked.

## Terms and laws
He separates creativity from neighbouring terms: invention, discovery, imagination, intelligence, talent, giftedness and genius. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:12:00} He draws a chain in which imagination becomes a passing thought, the thought becomes an idea, the idea a project, and the project an execution. Only at that last step is it creativity. On the passing thought he cites [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]] and his [[work/sayd-al-khatir|Sayd al-Khatir]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:17:00} He also says no scientific link between intelligence and creativity has been proven. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:14:30}

His laws include "look for the other right answer", "there is a better way to do any work", and "creativity happens only in lateral thinking". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:29:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:30:30} He says about ninety-eight per cent of our thinking time is spent in sequential, logical thinking and only two per cent in lateral thinking. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:30:30} He says ninety per cent of five-year-olds are highly creative, but by forty-five only two per cent remain so. He blames schooling and impatience with children's questions. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:34:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:36:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:36:30} The mind, he says, has thirty-one distinct abilities, and each can be strengthened with its own exercises, like a muscle. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:37:30}

## The creative person
He gives the trainees a sheet of thirty traits of the creative person to rate in themselves. The traits include:
- solving a problem before knowing how;
- asking many questions;
- patience;
- daydreaming;
- loving travel and play;
- strange ideas;
- working only at what one loves.
{cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:39:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:42:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:49:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep11 00:54:30} The list continues in the second lesson with forgetfulness, many interests, optimism, high ambition, dislike of routine, resistance to group pressure and boldness. He gives [[person/al-jahiz|al-Jahiz]] as an example of the forgetful creative mind. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:12:30} Twenty-three traits or more mark a person as creative. He says about ninety per cent of this group passed that mark. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:13:30}

He then lists ten places where creative ideas tend to come, which he says are in his book "Principles of Creativity". At the top are the shower and the drive to work; they also come before and after sleep, in a boring meeting, while reading or exercising, and on night walks. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:25:30}

## The creativity machine
The second half of the lesson is a workshop on the "creativity machine". He says it was invented in 1992 and is American in origin, and that he changed seventy per cent of its content. He presents it in his book "Creativity Step by Step". {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:26:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:31:00} He states a rule, "imitate the best that exists and modify it", and says a change of more than fifteen per cent can be registered as new. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:28:00} He takes the trainees through its stages: preparation with trigger words; investigating an object's parts, materials and uses; transformation by changing, reversing, dividing, combining or rotating it; illumination, when one new, realistic and simple idea is chosen and named; evaluation by a partner; and implementation. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:38:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:46:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:52:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:57:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 01:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 01:03:00} He calls combining things that have nothing to do with each other one of the strongest words of creativity. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 00:54:30}

Implementation is the hardest stage. He says only three per cent of patented ideas are ever carried out. Most failure, he says, lies in marketing rather than production. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 01:04:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 01:11:00} He ends by saying creativity is not hard, that the session only opens a door, and that one should pray before, during and after the work. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan leadership-development-academy-1/ep12 01:11:30}
