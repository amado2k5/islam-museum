## Its signs
It mostly occurs in the conjunctival coat: severe pain, distension of the eye's vessels, a strong pricking that reaches the temples especially as the patient moves, redness of the eye's coats, headache and loss of appetite for food, and pain at everything in which there is heat; it is a thing whose cure is not hoped for, though its soothing is hoped for. Cancer in no organ pains as it does when it occurs in the eye. The use of sharp drugs harms its owner and provokes an unbearable pain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.181}
