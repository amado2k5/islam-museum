## Drawing out the stuck thing
In thorns and splinters of wood and bone it must be looked: if the sense perceives it or a tool reaches it, it is pulled and drawn out; if it escapes the sense the slippery broths are sipped over it, failing which the hiccup and vomit are stirred; a strong thread may be tied to what he described, swallowed, then pulled so the stuck thing comes out — likewise what he described of the dried fruit tied on a thread, chewed a little then swallowed; and the throat may be poulticed from outside with poultices holding a light ripening and opening (the tool-and-thread drawing carried as the book's description, not a procedure; the names of this kind left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.286}
