## Summary
In the lexicon, ird means the self and lineage; a person of clean honour is free from blemish. When it is paired with self, blood or property, it means only lineage {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11558}.

## Related terms
For the jurists, hasab means the distinction inherited from forebears and the nobility of descent, and it is the usual sense of ird in their use {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11559}.

## Ruling in brief
The law protects life, honour and property, and prescribes punishments and retaliation for them. The jurists agree that defending self, honour and property against an aggressor is lawful {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11559}. They agree that defending honour in the sense of chastity is obligatory, and that whoever abandons it sins {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11560}. A husband who kills a man he finds committing adultery with his wife incurs neither retaliation nor blood-money {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11560}. The majority require defence of the self and permit defence of property, while some permit both {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11560} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11561}.
