## Overview
'Arjun sets out three factors by which God preserves human society from fragmentation: the blood-brotherhood of shared origin and kind {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 33}; the affection and mercy God made the foundation of the first family and then of society {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 34}; and mutual acquaintance among mankind's branches, drawing close what lineage and habitat set apart {cite:sunan-allah-fi-al-mujtama-azhar p. 35}. When a society strays from brotherhood and affection, selfishness and atomization seize it, and the stages of decline begin.
