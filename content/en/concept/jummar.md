## Nature and selection
The heart of the palm tree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.425}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: cold in the second degree and dry in the first {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.425}. Properties: astringent {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.425}.

## Uses by organ
Respiratory organs: helps roughness of the throat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.425}.
Excretory organs: stops diarrhoea and bleeding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.425}.
Poisons: helps a wasp sting as a poultice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.425}.
