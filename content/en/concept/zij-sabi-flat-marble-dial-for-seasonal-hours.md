## The flat dial and the shadow of the sun
The astronomer makes a simple instrument, called the marble, by which the seasonal hours of the day that have passed can be known in each place. A marble or a flat copper plate is taken, a centre is marked at the middle of its length, a circle is drawn, and it is quartered by two lines at right angles. The shadow of the first degree of Cancer and of Capricorn is found for each hour, with its azimuth on the horizon, and is drawn from the centre by a ruler. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.206-207}

## The altitude of the sun and its proportion
The shadows are found from the altitude of each hour of Capricorn and Cancer, and the gnomon is to be of a length equal to or more than the shadow of the head of Capricorn. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.207}
