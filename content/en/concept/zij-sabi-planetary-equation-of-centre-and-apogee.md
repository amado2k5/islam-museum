## Centre and equation
The apogee distance of the planet is subtracted from its mean, leaving the centre. This centre is looked up in the two rows of the table of the planet's equation, and the matching value is taken from the third table, which gives the equation of anomaly and centre. If the centre is less than qaf, that is 180 degrees, the equation is subtracted from the centre and added to the anomaly; if more, the equation is added to the centre and subtracted from the anomaly. The corrected centre then enters the fourth table of distance minutes. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.176}

## The value qaf
The dividing value is written in abjad letters as qaf, which stands for 180 degrees, the half circle. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.176}
