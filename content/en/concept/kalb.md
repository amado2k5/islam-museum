## Cosmetic uses
The urine of the dog is used on warts. What is claimed for its milk, that it prevents plucked hair from growing back, is false, as Galen asserts in several places {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.

## Actions and properties
Galen calls false the saying that the dog's blood prevents plucked hair from growing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Galen calls false the saying that its blood expels the foetus; the book records this as a rejected claim {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.

## Poisons and bites
The blood of the rabid dog is for its bites and for the poison of Armenian arrows {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.535}.
