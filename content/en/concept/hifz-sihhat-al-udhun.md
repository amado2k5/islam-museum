## The care owed it
The ear must be cared for: guarded from heat, cold, winds and extreme foreign things lest water or animals enter; its dirt cleaned; then the known oil must keep being dropped in once a week — it is wondrous; and watch kept lest swellings, pustules and ulcers breed in it, for they corrupt the ear ⁦(the drop names are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.217}
## What harms it
If pustules are feared, the known drops are used once a week — a safeguard against catarrhs descending into it. What harms the ear and all the senses is indigestion and repletion, above all sleeping on repletion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.217} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.218}
