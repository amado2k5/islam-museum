## Its matter and whom it afflicts
The matter of the lice is a putrid moisture nature drives toward the skin, and the predisposing power for its generation an unnatural heat; those most afflicted are people of much food variety, little exercise, unclean and not using the bath {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.193}

## The treatment
It begins with cleansing the body and the head toward the eye as you know, especially with gargles made of astringent kinds; then washing the eye and sprinkling it with sea salt and sulphurous waters; and the lid edge is smeared with a drug made of astringent drugs ⁦(the drug and gargle names and measures are not carried)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.193}
