## Summary
Ruju' in the lexicon is turning back; in the law it is a return to what was, or the undoing of an act. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8651} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8652} It may be obligatory, as a return to the Qur'an and the Sunna when people dispute; recommended, as the mutual cancellation of a sale; permitted in revocable contracts; forbidden, as taking back a charity; or disliked, as taking back a gift in the Hanafi view. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8652} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8653} A judge or mufti may change his ruling when his ijtihad changes; a few Malikis disagreed, but most held that the earlier judgment is not annulled by a later ijtihad. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8656} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8657} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8658} Revocable contracts, such as loan of use, bequest, partnership, agency and deposit, may be revoked; binding contracts may be revoked only by mutual consent. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8659} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8660} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8670} A return is lost by remission, by the death of a party, or by the waiver of a right. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8670} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8671} In testimony, witnesses who retract after judgment pay the money, and the penalty is not carried out; in confession, a retraction removes the hadd penalty by the majority. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8673} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8674} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8675}
