## Summary
Dhakar is the known male organ, and the opposite of female. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8424}
The Malikis, Shafi'is and one Hanbali view hold that touching it with the palm breaks ablution; the Hanafis and another Hanbali view hold that it does not. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8425}
Retaliation is due for cutting a sound male organ according to the majority, and a full diya is due when retaliation does not apply; the schools differ on the organs of the impotent and the eunuch. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8425} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8426} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8427}

## Other rulings that depend on it
Rulings depending on the male organ include the obligation of ghusl when the glans disappears in the female organ, the settling of the dower by consummation, the status of chastity in a valid marriage, and the hadd for unlawful penetration. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8427}
