## Nature
This is a herb the Arabs called lisan al-thawr, the ox-tongue, and the Persians call kazwan. The editor's note identifies it as the ox-tongue plant. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}

## Properties
Its property is to gladden and remove gloom. The book defers the full account, and gives its uses under ox-tongue in the letter lam. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.526}
