## The voice's harms and their causes
On the conveyor's side the voice changes by the lung's fierce heat or cold or moistness, by pus flowing to it from tumours, by catarrhs flowing to it, or by its dryness: heat makes the voice great, dryness makes it harsh and like the cranes' voices, moistness husks it, and smoothness sets it right and smooths it; if the lung fills with moisture and the windpipe is not pure, a high or clear voice is impossible, that being by the measure of the lung's and larynx's clearness. It differs in heaviness and lightness by the windpipe's and the larynx's width and narrowness; and when the named harms grow strong in the urging and conveying organs the voice ceases — the book telling of two men: one treated by the uncovering of a place with iron, and his voice went; and another treated for scrofula, and one of the two returning nerves was cut, and half his voice was cut ⁦(two reports on the book's wording)⁩. If the harm is in the bending muscles the voice turns hoarse; if in the moving dilating muscles it turns strangling, and a strangling may even arise from it; if in the moving constricting muscles it turns puffing; if their act ceases the voice ceases; and if an incomplete loosening and a tremor-like state arises in them the voice trembles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.323}
