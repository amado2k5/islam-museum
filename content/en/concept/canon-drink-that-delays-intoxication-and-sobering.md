## Summary
Ibn Sina gives a mixture that slows intoxication, of white cabbage water, sour pomegranate juice and vinegar, and pills of salt, rue and black cumin, then lists what sobers the drunk, water and vinegar, whey and sour curd, camphor and sandalwood and cooling applications on the head, leaving the hangover to the particular books {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.239}.

## The slowing drink
Take one part of white cabbage water, one part of sour pomegranate juice and half a part of vinegar, boil them briefly, and drink an ounce before the wine. Another is a pill made of salt, rue and black cumin, dried and taken one pill after another. Another takes the seed of the Nabataean cabbage, cumin, peeled bitter almond, pennyroyal, wormwood, naphtha salt, ajowan and dried rue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.239}.

## Sobering the drunk
What sobers the drunk is to give him water and vinegar three times in succession, or whey water and sour curd, to let him smell camphor and sandalwood, or to put cooling repellents on his head, such as rose oil with wine vinegar. The treatment of the hangover Ibn Sina leaves to the particular books {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.239}.
