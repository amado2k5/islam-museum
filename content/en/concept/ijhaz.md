## Summary
In the lexicon ijhaz means haste, and to finish off a wounded man is to complete his killing; jurists use the word in this sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1704} Finishing off wounded enemy combatants is lawful, and that of wounded rebels is lawful where they have a group; it is obligatory for one sentenced to death for a fixed penalty or retaliation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1704} For an animal that may not be killed at the outset, such as a donkey, the Hanafis and Malikis allow ending its suffering after injury, and the Shafi'is and Hanbalis forbid it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1705}
