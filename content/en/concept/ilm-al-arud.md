## Summary
'Arud is the science of the metres of Arabic poetry, and its setting down is ascribed to [[person/al-khalil-ibn-ahmad-al-farahidi|al-Khalil ibn Ahmad al-Farahidi]]. The page gives what Ibn Khallikan writes of it in Wafayat al-A'yan, two explanations of how it began, each reported as "it is said", and the works Ibn Khallikan lists for al-Khalil. The page does not choose between the two explanations.

## What Ibn Khallikan says
In his entry on al-Khalil, Ibn Khallikan says he was an imam in grammar and that he "deduced the science of 'arud and brought it into existence", and confined its parts in five circles from which fifteen metres are derived. Al-Akhfash later added another metre, which he called al-khabab {cite:wafayat-ihsan-abbas 2 p.244}.

## Two explanations of its beginning
Ibn Khallikan reports in the form "it is said" that al-Khalil prayed in Mecca that God give him a knowledge no one had preceded him in and that would be taken only from him, and that on returning from that pilgrimage the science of 'arud was opened to him. He adds that al-Khalil had knowledge of rhythm and melody, and that this knowledge produced 'arud for him, since the two are close in origin {cite:wafayat-ihsan-abbas 2 p.244}.

Al-Dhahabi in Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' quotes Ibn Khallikan and gives another explanation, also as "it is said": that al-Khalil passed the coppersmiths and took 'arud from the beat of a hammer on a basin {cite:siyar-risala-1985 7 p.431}. Each explanation is ascribed here to whoever reports it, and the page does not decide between them.

## The works of al-Khalil
Ibn Khallikan lists among al-Khalil's books Kitab al-Ayn, Kitab al-'Arud, Kitab al-Shawahid, Kitab al-Naqt wa-l-Shakl, Kitab al-Nagham and a book on the 'awamil. He then says that most scholars who know the language say Kitab al-Ayn, ascribed to al-Khalil, is not his composition: he began it, arranged its opening and named it al-Ayn, then died, and his pupils completed it, among them al-Nadr ibn Shumayl {cite:wafayat-ihsan-abbas 2 p.246}. The book has its own page, [[work/kitab-al-ayn|Kitab al-Ayn]], and the science in which al-Khalil was an imam, [[concept/ilm-al-nahw|Arabic grammar]], has its own as well.

## What this page does not reach
Al-Khalil's Kitab al-'Arud was not read, nor any other book of 'arud, so what the five circles, the fifteen metres and the khabab are is not explained here; their names are given as they stand in Ibn Khallikan. No account was read here of anyone who preceded or followed al-Khalil in this science. The story of al-Khalil and Ibn al-Muqaffa' in his entry was not given.
