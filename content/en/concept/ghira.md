## Temperament
Hide glue is hot and dry in the first degree, and fish glue is less hot but dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Actions and properties
Every glue has an adhesive, drying power {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Cosmetics
Fish glue enters the ghumra and enters the drugs for baras; hide glue and cow-hide glue, after a preparation the book mentions, stand in the place of tutiya in the treatment of sanan; the preparation is not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.725}

## Ulcers
Hide glue is applied to sa'fa and prevents blistering of a burn, and likewise fish glue and cow-hide glue, when applied with vinegar to qauba and to scaling scab, if it is not very deep, benefit, and when applied with honey and vinegar to wounds they benefit; fish glue enters the salves for ulcerated scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.725-726}

## Organs of the head
Fish glue enters the salves for ulcers of the head {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}

## Organs of the chest
Fish glue is given with vinegar for spitting of blood; the wording after it, on entering the 'ahsha', is unclear and is not given {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.726}
