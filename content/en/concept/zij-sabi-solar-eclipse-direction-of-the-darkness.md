## Rising and setting points
The direction of the darkness at the beginning is measured from the azimuth of the setting part of the ecliptic at that moment, toward the side of the Moon's visible latitude; at the end of totality it is measured from the azimuth of the rising part toward the same side. At mid-eclipse the darkness lies at a right angle to the ecliptic, bounded on the horizon by the arc through the poles of the ecliptic, the Moon's centre and the horizon, according to the distances of the Sun and Moon from the equator of the day. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.169-171}

## The tables and the shape of the darkness
The text says that the parts of deviation are taken from the third table at the rising point for the start, matched with the start and end of the solar eclipse in the second table, and then measured on the drawn circles from the rising and setting points. The disturbed letters on pages 169 to 171 are not reproduced. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.169-171}
