## Overview
The Companions and Successors differed over transmitting hadith by meaning: some forbade it and refused to narrate but in the wording heard, others permitted it {cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 80}. The permitters argued that the Prophet legislated in every time and place and it was impossible that word-perfect hearers always attend him; that people must convey what was legislated; that the Prophet's envoys rendered his messages by meaning faithfully; and that Ibn Sirin would hear one hadith from ten men differing in wording with a single meaning {cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 81}{cite:makanat-al-sunna-azhar p. 82}.
