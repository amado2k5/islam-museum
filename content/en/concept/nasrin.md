## Nature and selection
It is like jasmine in power and weaker, and like narcissus; its oil is near jasmine oil in power and weaker {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.

## Temperament
Hot and dry in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.

## Actions and properties
All its kinds cleanse and thin, and its flower more especially {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.

## Joints
It is said to help cold of the nerve {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It kills worms in the ears, helps tinnitus and ringing and toothache; the wild kind is smeared on the forehead and calms headache. Its kinds open obstructions of the nostrils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps swellings of the throat and the tonsils {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Drunk, it calms vomiting (the stated measure is not carried over here) and calms hiccup, especially the wild kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.576}.
