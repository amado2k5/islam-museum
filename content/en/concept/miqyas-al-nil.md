## Overview
Al-Masudi describes the Nile gauge and what its cubits indicate. The Nile's rise begins in the months of Baunah, Abib, and Masra. When it reaches sixteen cubits, the land tax is complete and the land is fertile, and the full rises are seventeen. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.301-302}

When it reaches eighteen, a quarter of the land of Egypt is flooded and this is harmful, and if it rises further, a plague occurs. It reached nineteen cubits in the year 99 AH, in the caliphate of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.301-302}

The two cubits at which water is drawn are the thirteenth and the fourteenth, and they are called "*munkar*" and "*nakir*" (Munkar and Nakir). The four canals are opened on the feast of the Cross. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.301-302}
