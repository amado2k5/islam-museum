## Nature and selection
A seed like barley tending to red; its flower is longer and finer, dark and bitter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}. The editor's footnote gives only a Latin name; the Arabic identity is queued for outside research.

## Temperament and actions
Ibn Masawayh is cited as saying it is cold; the correct view is that it is dry to the degree of heat in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}. Actions: astringent, and it keeps date wine from turning sour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings: it is a good softener of hardness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
Head: strengthening {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
Excretory organs: it binds, and is very useful for pains of the anus and its relaxation when one sits in its decoction; taken with oil by licking it helps piles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.436}.
Poisons: it helps poisons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.436}.
Substitutes: almond and abhal for softening hardness, except that abhal is not used for pregnant women {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.436}.
