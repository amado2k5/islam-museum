## From the series Al Naba Al Safi: naming bribery by other names
The speaker says bribery is a major sin, and that the one who gives and the one who takes are both cursed {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep11 00:20:00}. He asks whether bribery has disappeared, criticises people who call a bribe a gift or a tip, and says a neighbour who is not safe is a sign of failure {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep19 00:32:00}. He says people call the unlawful by other names and then expect answers to their prayers, and urges a person to re-examine his repentance {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep19 00:32:30}. He says gifts to officials are bribery, and criticises people who justify bribery under various headings {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep21 00:30:30}.

## The effect on the family and on wealth
The speaker says the family of a bribe-taker suffers, and that wealth gained unlawfully brings no blessing {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep11 00:20:30}. He criticises officials who use their position to wrong the weak and deny them their rights, and says the weak have only God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep15 00:24:00}.

## Gifts tied to office
He cites a hadith that gifts offered to officials are a form of dishonesty, and recounts Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz refusing a gift (needs_source: the anecdote is not sourced in the recording) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep11 00:21:00}. He cites a hadith about an official whom the Prophet sent, who came back with gifts, and the Prophet's anger; he sets against it Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz refusing a gift {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep21 00:31:00}. He asks why a giver offers a gift, and says gifts within the family are fine, but gifts tied to office are not {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-naba-al-safi/ep21 00:31:30}.
