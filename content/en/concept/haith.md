## Overview

Haith is the wall, and the encyclopedia treats the rules of a shared wall and of setting beams on it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6690}

## The shared wall and a partner's duty

When a shared wall has collapsed, the Hanafis hold that the other partner may be compelled to share in rebuilding it, where the foundation cannot be divided. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6692}

## Setting beams on a neighbour's wall

The Hanbalis and the older Shafi'i view hold that a neighbour may be compelled to allow his neighbour to set beams into his wall. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6691}

## Demolition

When a shared wall is feared to fall, it must be pulled down, and the partner who does so may claim his share from the other. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6695}
