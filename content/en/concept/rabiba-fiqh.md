## Summary
A rabiba is the daughter of a wife, and the daughter of her son or daughter however far down, by blood or fostering. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8617} She is forbidden to the husband when he has entered her mother, and most jurists do not require that she be in his custody, though Umar and Ali did. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8617} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8618} The death of the wife before entry does not take the place of entry for most jurists, while a Hanbali view counts it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8618} The daughters of the rabiba and their daughters are forbidden by consensus. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.8618}
