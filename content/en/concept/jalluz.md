## Nature and selection
The large pine nut is a better food than the walnut but slower to digest; it is made of a watery and an earthy substance, with little air {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.419}. The full discussion is to be sought under the letter sad at the pine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.419}.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: balanced, with a little heat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.419}. Actions: it nourishes strongly and thickly without being bad food; it suits the humours that have rotted in the bowels; it digests slowly, and its digestion is helped for cold people with honey and for hot people with sugar candy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.419}. Soaked in water it loses its sharpness and sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.419}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: eases pains of the nerve, the back and sciatica {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.420}.
Respiratory and chest: clears the lung greatly and brings out its pus and thick humour {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.420}.
Excretory organs: stirs desire, especially the preserved kind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.420} [book listing]; helps stone in the bladder {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.420}.
Poisons: with fig or date it helps a scorpion sting {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.420}.
