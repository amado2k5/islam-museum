## Nature
The date of the adfal, which is qasb among the people of the Hijaz, while the people of Najd call it al-'irq and al-yarsum. The editor notes that it is the name of a date found in Iraq {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Temperament
Moderate in heat and dry; it was said to be hot in the second degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Actions and properties
It has astringency in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}

## Excretory organs
It stops the bowel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.646}
