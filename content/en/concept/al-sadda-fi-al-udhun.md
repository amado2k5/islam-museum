## Its causes
This occlusion may be in creation — a membrane created over the hole; from dirt; from clotted blood; from extra flesh or a wart; from a pebble or pip fallen in, or an animal entering and dying in it; or with a viscid humour blocking the aperture or the nerve's passages so a man always feels his ear stopped; it may happen after a violent wind {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.229}
## Its treatments
What is of membranes or flesh blocking the passage in original creation: the deep-lying is harder to treat, the outward easier; the inner is managed by a fine instrument cutting it, then keeping flesh from growing back as he will say shortly; the outward should be split with the known blade, then a tent inserted with what keeps pus from breeding. If the occlusion is something stuck in it, oil is dropped in the ear; if what stuck is an animal that died in it, an oil is poured in to soften it then it is drawn out gently with the known instrument. If the occlusion is from extra flesh or a wart, it is washed and what burns the flesh is dropped, then the ulcer treated. What a man imagines of his ear being stopped benefits from dropping the known; if the occlusion is of dirt it is treated as said in the chapter on dirt occlusions ⁦(drug, instrument, oil and tent names are not carried; the splitting and cutting works are transmitted as the book's description, not as procedures)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.230}
## The congenital occlusion
The occlusion in creation is the ear being created unpierced and stopped within from creation; hand-work may be tried — if scraping and boring through reaches the inner meatus it benefits — or it may not benefit by any device at all ⁦(the hand-work is transmitted as the book's description, not as a procedure)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.230}
