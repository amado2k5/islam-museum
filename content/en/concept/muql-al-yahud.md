## Nature and selection
Muql al-yahud has a Slavic kind and an Arab kind, which is not the muql of dudum; both are among the gums, while the Meccan is the fruit of the doum palm. The best of the two gums is blue, clear, bitter in taste, clean of twigs, easily dissolved and sweet in smell, its smoke smelling of laurel; when muql al-yahud grows old it passes from softening to drying {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Temperament
The Meccan is cold and dry; the other is hot at the end of the first degree and softening, especially the Slavic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Actions and properties
It dissolves even clotted blood, softens, ripens and breaks wind; the Slavic is more softening and the Arab drier, except when fresh {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556}.

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves hard swellings, especially dissolved in the saliva of a fasting person, and all other cold swellings; the Arab kind, which is muql al-yahud, removes scrofula and is drunk boiled for inner and hard swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.556} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.

## Wounds and ulcers
It is spread with vinegar on scalp scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.

## Joints
It helps tearing of muscles, spasm, and the hardness and knotting of nerves {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.

## Chest and breathing
It helps pains and swellings of the windpipe, chronic cough and pains of the side; the Arab kind helps swellings of the larynx and pharynx {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.

## Excretory organs
It helps piles drunk, as a suppository and as a fumigation, and stops their blood; it helps kidney stone, prevents excoriation when put into purgatives, and brings on urine. The Meccan is also thought to bring on urine, though it undoubtedly binds the bowels and breaks stone {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists it as bringing on the menses, and says that both kinds open the closed mouth of the womb, send down the foetus and cleanse the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.

## Poisons and bites
Useful against stings of vermin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.557}.
