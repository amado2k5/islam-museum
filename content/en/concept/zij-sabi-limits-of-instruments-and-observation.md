## Error in the instrument and the observation
Al-Battani closes the book by saying that the causes of things and the derivations of the reckoning, which follow geometrical proof, do not change in any age. What is known only by measurement, observation, trial and reflection can be corrected by later work. If the error lies in the instrument and the instrument stays the same, the error is the same at both times; if the instrument changes over time through expansion, contraction or bending, the error changes with its size. Human error stays small with care and diligence. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.213-214}

## A note on the close
At the close the text points out that people may claim to grasp what cannot be grasped, and then introduces the instruments it has described. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.213-214}
