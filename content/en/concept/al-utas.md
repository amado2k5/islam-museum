## What it is and how it happens
The sneeze is a heated movement from the brain expelling a humour or another annoyance, aided by the inhaled air, driving through the nose and the mouth; the sneeze is to the brain as the cough is to the lung and what adjoins it. Some have thought the brain only empties into the sneeze once the annoying humour turns into air, which the inhaled air then expels — but that is not necessary; the air is driven out only that the body be full of air continuous with the air drawn to the humour's quarter, so that when the whole air is shaken by the chest's and diaphragm's muscles in a violent movement and leaps from inside out, digging out what lies further than the chest of its parts, it aids the expulsion and uprooting — for the shaking of the neighbouring air follows, helping the driving force to kill the matter and shake it out ⁦(the people's supposition is carried as a report the book rejects)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.250} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.251}
## Its harm, benefit and testimony
The sneeze is very harmful at the start of a nazla or zukam, since the humour needing ripening needs stillness; in fevers and their like it may multiply until it fells the strength and fills the head, or stirs a severe nosebleed — so its staunching must be hastened; yet it resolves the material hiccup by its shaking. Some sneezes occur at the onset of fever fits. The Indians claimed — and it is not upheld as right ⁦(the book's own judgement)⁩ — that the sneezer's best posture for the head is facing forward level with the chest, neither turning aside nor bending, so no harm befalls him. The sneeze is the most useful of things for drying the head when the matter is little and can be shaken out though unripened, or windy; if the matter is much or vaporous, the sneeze is the most useful thing for the vaporous fullness in the head; or thick but ripened. If it is more than that it points to the brain's strength — which is why one near death cannot sneeze, and one of them who is made to sneeze by sternutatories and does not has no recovery to be hoped for at all. It is among what helps shake out the retained wastes, and it eases childbirth and the exit of the afterbirth ⁦(a labelled listing set aside for the founder's ruling)⁩, and calms the head's heaviness; but it harms one in whose head is a matter that must rest to ripen, whose neighbourhood must be neither heated nor moved for fear other matter be drawn to it, and it harms one in whose chest is much or crude matter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.251}
