## Nature and selection
A plant nearly the size of a tree that grows near water, with firm stems and a leaf like the olive's but softer. Its stalks are not used in medicine; its flower, leaf and fruit, and what is used of it, have lightness, sharpness and astringency, and it is weaker than dry rue {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot in the first degree and dry in the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

## Uses by organ
Actions: thins, dissolves and releases wind; it causes no bloating at all, and opens with binding {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Adornment: clears the complexion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Joints: its leaf is applied for twisting of a nerve and to remove weariness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Head: taken as a drink it causes headache and sleep; applied, it helps headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.406}.

Chest: the book says it increases milk and reduces semen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}.

Digestion: opens the blockages of the liver and spleen; very good for a hard spleen, and helps dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}.

Excretory organs: sitting in its decoction helps pain of the womb and its swellings; it dries semen; a few of its stalks spread under the back prevent nocturnal emission and erection; fumigation is used for women at strong desire; it moves flow, and its seed especially helps fissure of the anus; applied with ghee to a hard testicle {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}.

Poisons: helps the bite of insects and snakes; its leaf smoke drives away vermin strongly; applied, it helps the bite of a mad dog and of beasts {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.407}.
