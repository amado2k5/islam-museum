## Summary
Ibn Sina says fast settling after voiding is a good sign of maturation and slow settling a sign of its lack; floating or hanging sediment at the start of illness that persists shows a crisis by abscess; and he warns against greasy floaters like cobwebs. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.196}.

## Floating sediment early in illness
Separate sediment floats in thick urine, especially when light, and settles in thin urine, especially when heavy. When hanging and floating sediment appear at the start of illness and last, the crisis comes by abscess, though in the lean the illness may end with good floating or hanging sediment. Greasy floating or hanging sediment like a spider's web or heaped egg white is a bad sign. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.196}.

## Time and mixing
A floating residue that looks poor often causes fear, yet it is the beginning of maturation and turns to good, then hangs, then settles. If sediment settles quickly once voided, it is a good sign of maturation; if slow or absent it shows lack of maturation in proportion to its state. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.196}.
