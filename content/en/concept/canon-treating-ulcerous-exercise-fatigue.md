## Summary
Ibn Sina opens the fourteenth chapter, on treating exercise fatigue, by saying that care for it is a safeguard against many illnesses, fevers among them, and then sets out the regimen for the ulcerous kind: less exercise, evacuation, rubbing with oil, then restorative exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.244}.

## The principle
Ibn Sina says that care in treating exercise fatigue is a safeguard against many illnesses, fevers among them. For the ulcerous fatigue, exercise should be reduced as soon as it shows, if exercise is its cause; if an abundance of humours accompanies it, the humours are reduced; and if there is recent indigestion, its harm is met with hunger and evacuation {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.244}.

## Rubbing and restorative exercise
What has gathered in the region of the skin is dissolved by abundant gentle rubbing with an oil that has no astringency, up to the third day, and then restorative exercise is used. On the first day the person is fed in the usual kind of food, and on the second with moistening foods. The printed text is garbled at the words on the quantity of food, so they are not taken {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.244}.
