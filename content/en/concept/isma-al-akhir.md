## Summary
The names of the Hereafter are mentioned in Qur'an and Sunnah as the days, states and stations by which the Day of Resurrection, the barzakh, paradise and fire are named. Many names are given, such as the Day of Resurrection, the Day of Spreading, the Day of Reckoning, the Day of the Scale, the Day of the Bridge, the Day of the Basin, the Day of Gathering, the Day of Religion, the Day of Separation, the Day of Regret. All these names point to one day — the Day of Resurrection — and differ according to what each name emphasizes.
