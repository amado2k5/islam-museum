## Summary
Hilal in the lexical sense is the moon in a particular state; technically it is the first sighting of the lit part of the moon on the first night. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16552}

## The crescents as times
The lawgiver made the crescents times for people: deferred dealings, the rites (the pilgrimage, fasting and its breaking), and matters with legal effects such as the waiting period, the nursing period and the period of the oath of abstention. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16552} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16553}

## Worship and transactions
Only the lawgiver fixes the times of worship; the times of transactions are agreed by the parties, and an unqualified month is taken as a lunar month. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16553} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16554}

## The size of the crescent
The size of the crescent is no guide to the start of the month; what counts is the night of sighting. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.16554}
