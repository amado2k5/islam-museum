## Nature and selection
Nature: the largest of the geckos (wazagh) and of the kind called sawam abras, with a long tail and a small head. It is not the dabb, which is rare except in the desert; its head, body and tail differ from the warl, though its temperaments are close. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}
Temperament: its flesh is very hot. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}

## Actions
Actions: it has a power to draw out splinters and thorns. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: its dung helps kalaf and namash. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}
Swellings and pustules: its powdered dung pulls out warts. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}
Eye organs: its dung, like the dung of the dabb, helps whiteness of the eye, as is said. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.455}
