## Summary
Ibn Sina records that the ranks of drugs have been set at four, according to their action by quality {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.133}.

## The ranks
In the first rank, the taker's effect on the body by its quality is imperceptible, as when it heats or cools so slightly that it is noticed only when repeated or taken in quantity. In the second the effect is stronger but does no clear harm to the functions and alters their natural course only incidentally, or when repeated and abundant. In the third the effect by its nature causes clear harm without reaching destruction. In the fourth it reaches destruction and ruin, which is the mark of poisonous drugs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.133}.
