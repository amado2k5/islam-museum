## Overview
For Ibn Sina a temperament (mizaj) is a state arising from the interaction of contrary qualities held at their bound {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.19}. The primary qualities in the elements are four: heat, cold, moisture and dryness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.19}. The temperament is treated in the third lesson of the first fann, in three chapters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.19}.
