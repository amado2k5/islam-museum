## Overview
Al-Masudi mentions that the title of *wizara* (vizierate) first occurred in the Abbasid state for Abu Salama Hafs ibn Sulayman al-Khallal al-Hamadhani. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.256-257}

He was called "the vizier of the family of Muhammad," just as Abu Muslim was called "the trustee of the family of Muhammad." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.256-257}
