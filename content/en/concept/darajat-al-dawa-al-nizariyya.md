## Overview
Hasan Ibrahim Hasan describes seven degrees that the missionaries of the [[group/ismaili-dawa|Nizari mission]] followed in winning over the one they invited. The author's description is of what he sees as the missionaries' weapons. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.264-265}

## The degrees
1. Probing (*tarassum*): the missionary's inward reading of the invitee's thoughts, and the strength of his will.
2. Reassurance (*ta'nis*): instilling security in him and satisfying his inclinations.
3. Doubt (*tashkik*): unsettling his belief, which the author considers the most dangerous of the steps.
4. Suspension (*ta'liq*): leaving him in suspense, longing to learn the doctrine.
5. Deception (*tadlis*): concealment and false claims, in the author's description.
6. Foundation (*ta'sis*): fixing information in his mind until it is settled.
7. Dismissal (*khal'*): removing him from the Sunni schools entirely by discarding the obligations through interpretation.

{cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.264-265}

This is the account of a single author, and the description needs a further source. It would be well to compare it with what the Ismailis themselves wrote.
