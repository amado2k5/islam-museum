## Nature and selection
When sea crab is said, we do not mean every crab from the sea, but a special kind stony in all its parts. One whose word we trust said that this crab in the sea of China comes out of the sea water and enters another water beside the sea that is not sea water; when it enters that water it dies in the water or on leaving it and becomes hard stone; one who had witnessed this many times in China told me this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Actions and properties
Its burnt form is finer than other burnt things {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Adornment
Its burnt form polishes the teeth and removes freckles and spots {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Its burnt form dries ulcers and helps scab {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.

## Eyes
It stops tears; rubbed with salt it cures the pterygium; an eye salve is made from it and rubbed on the scab of the lid; it clears the eye greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.588}.
