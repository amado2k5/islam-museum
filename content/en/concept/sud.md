## Nature and selection
Dioscorides said: it is the root of a plant with leaves like leek but longer, thinner and harder, and a stem that is not straight but bent at angles like the stem of the lemon grass, with small leaves and seed growing at its tip; its roots are like olives, some long and some round and tangled together, black, sweet in smell with some bitterness; it grows in flooded places and damp land, and is found in the country of Tarsus and in Syria, in the islands called Qauqaladas, and in India and Kufa. Stephen claimed that some oils are prepared with galls or astringent things and then perfumed with it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.583}.

The best is the dense, heavy kind that is hard to crush, fragrant, with short herbage and strong pungency; it enters ointments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Adornment
It improves colour and sweetens the breath; the Indian kind, it is said, removes hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Swellings and pustules
It heals ulcers that are hard to heal, the fibrous and the eating kinds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Joints
With the oil of the green seed it is for pain of the flank, and it firms the back; too much of it brings leprosy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It helps foulness of the nose and mouth, thrush and slackness of the gums, greatly adds to memory, and helps eating ulcers of the mouth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Excretory organs
It expels the stone and provokes urine, helps dripping of urine and weakness of the bladder greatly and its coldness strongly, does the same for the kidneys, and helps dropsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Fevers
It helps old fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Poisons and bites
It helps greatly against the sting of the scorpion and of insects {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It helps the cold of the womb greatly, and helps haemorrhoids and closure of the mouth of the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.584}.
