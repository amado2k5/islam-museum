## Nature and selection
Close to anjudan in nature and inferior to it; its vinegar is the better form to use {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. A footnote gives its name as camel thorn, a word of Persian origin {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}.

## Temperament and actions
Hot and dry at the end of the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}.

## Uses by organ
Stomach: its vinegar is good for the stomach; it cleanses and strengthens it and sharpens appetite, while the substance itself causes nausea by its burning and stays long in the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}. Fevers: its particular virtue is that it helps quartan fevers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.359}.
