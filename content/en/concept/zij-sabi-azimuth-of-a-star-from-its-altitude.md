## Summary
Chapter 23 is headed with finding the azimuth of any star from its altitude and its place in the sphere. The altitude of the star at that time, its distance from the equator and the latitude of the place are found, and the course described in the earlier chapter on the azimuth of an altitude is followed without change, except that the star's distance from the equator is used in place of the declination of the Sun's degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.57}.

## Use for the Moon and the planets
If the Moon's azimuth is wanted, it is done with its visible distance from the equator so that its azimuth in the circle of altitude is right, and likewise for any star whose azimuth from the horizon circle is wanted. In this way one chapter on azimuth serves the Sun and all the stars. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.58}.
