## Summary
A daughter's son by blood is the male blood child of a blood daughter; by fostering, he is one in whom the foster tie takes the place of the blood tie. Without qualification the word means the blood relation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1461}

## General ruling
He is not included in the word "my children" in a waqf, by agreement. The jurists differ on whether he is included in "children of my children", "my offspring", "my descendants" and "my issue". He is a mahram, so the rules of mahrams apply to him, including the prohibition of marrying his grandmother and the permission to keep her company and to travel with her. He is among those with blood-ties through a female, with rules on inheritance, exclusion, guardianship, custody, maintenance, the revocation of a gift and liability for offences. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1461} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1462}

## Where it is discussed
The Encyclopedia says the detailed rules are treated in their places in the books of fiqh, and refers the rules on those with blood-ties through a female to its entry on kinship (arham). {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1462}
