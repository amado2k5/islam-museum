## Overview
The jinn in Islamic conception are a hidden, accountable creation; Ibn Kathir reports they inhabited the earth before mankind, and that among them are disbelievers and Muslims {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## In the sources
Ibn Kathir transmits that the jinn were on the earth before Adam's creation, and that Iblis was of them {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
At the end of one lesson Omar Abdelkafy answers questions about jinn and possession, saying that the matter of the jinn is one people talk about a great deal, and that a house in which the Qur'an is recited is not approached by sorcery. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 01:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 01:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 01:09:00}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme "Liqa' al-Iman"
In episode 5 of [[work/likaa-al-iman|Liqa' al-Iman]] the presenter asks about a man who claims to command hundreds of thousands of jinn, healing illnesses and meeting needs through them. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:00:30} [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|Al-Sha'rawi]] says it is all fraud, in which claimants exploit people in need who have run out of options, like a drowning man clutching at anything. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:01:00} He holds that Satan does not possess a person or enter him but only whispers, that whispering comes from devils among jinn and humans, and that Satan gains hold only over the weak. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:02:00} He cites the dispute on the Day of Resurrection, when Satan tells those he misled that he had no power to force them to act nor power to convince them by argument; he only called them and they answered. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:02:30} He says Satan comes during prayer because it is the believer's most important time with his Lord, and that he does not sit at the tavern door but on the straight path, since whoever has left the path has no need of him. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:06:00} He holds that jinn are of two kinds: the obedient, occupied with his Lord, who does not come to you, and the disobedient, who will not obey your command; he cites the verse relating that some humans who sought refuge with some jinn only gained more burden from them, and says the jinn were made subject to no one but Sulayman. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep05 00:10:00} The denial that jinn enter the human body is his view, attributed to him, and scholars differ on the question. ## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Ahl al-Janna'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] speaks of the jinn in the second episode of his programme [[work/ahl-al-jannah|'Ahl al-Janna']]: Satan, he says, has a name and an attribute, Iblis from despair and shaytan from shatana, being far from God; the devils are the unbelievers among the jinn, and the jinn include Muslims and unbelievers and are addressed by the Sharia like humans. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep02 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep02 00:16:00} He recalls the company of jinn who listened to the Qur'an from the Prophet and went back to their people as warners. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep02 00:16:00} He says the jinn were created from 'marij of fire', which he explains, citing [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]]'s commentary on Sahih Muslim, as the red flame of fire mixed with its black. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep02 00:17:00} He gives two views of the scholars on how fire can punish beings made of fire: that they were changed into another substance, as man is no longer clay, or that they remain fire and the fire of the hereafter is another fire. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny ahl-al-jannah/ep02 00:17:30}
