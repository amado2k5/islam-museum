## The chapter
Al-Jamzuri says: "and the mim, when still, comes before the letters — no soft alef for the pilgrim — its rules are three for one who masters them: ikhfa, idgham and izhar only" {cite:ar-wikisource}.

## The three rules
Ikhfa at the ba — called shafawi because the ba issues from the two lips; idgham into its like when a mim follows, called the small idgham; and izhar at the rest of the letters, likewise called shafawi — with the matn's warning against hiding the mim at the waw and the fa because of the nearness and oneness of their makhraj {cite:ar-wikisource}. In the Jazariyya Ibn al-Jazari states the ikhfa with ghunna at the ba "on the choice of the people of performance", and izhar at the rest, with the same warning {cite:ar-wikisource}.
