## Summary
Ibn Sina closes chapter 3 with a practical rule: once a person has found in the first day the measure of his exercise and his meal and knows how much food he bore, he changes nothing the next day but sets food and exercise at the same measure {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.225}.

## The rule
If on the first day you hit upon the measure of your exercise and your meal and learned the quantity of food you could bear, change nothing on the second day, but set the day's food and exercise at their measure on the first day {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.225}.
