## Meaning and office
Amir akhur, in Persian mir akhur, "high equerry", was one of the highest officials in the courts of Oriental princes. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 464}

## Under the Mamluks
Under the Mamluks the amir akhur supervised the royal stables. He was generally an amir of a thousand, and had three amirs of forty under his orders. In the Circassian period he ranked fourth among the grand amirs. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 464}
