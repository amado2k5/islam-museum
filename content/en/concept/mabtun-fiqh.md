## Summary
Mabtun, in lexicon, is one ill in the belly; in technical use one who has a stomach disease, or dropsy, or who dies of a stomach ailment in general, or who has diarrhoea or colic {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13828}. The mabtun is sick and excused, so the rules for the sick and the excused apply to him {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13829}. The jurists count one who dies of his belly ailment as a martyr, on the hadith of Abu Hurayra listing five kinds of martyr: the one who dies of plague, the mabtun, the drowned, the one killed by a collapse, and the one who dies in God's way; they agree he is a martyr of the next world, and that he is washed for burial though counted a martyr {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13829}.
