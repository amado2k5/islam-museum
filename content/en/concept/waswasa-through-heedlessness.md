## Summary
Sha'rawi recounts the story of Iblis and Adam and holds that whispering enters through heedlessness. Iblis's sin began when he refused to prostrate and turned the command back on its giver. He then tempted Adam with immortality and kingship in a tree God had forbidden, as in [[passage/quran-20-120|Q. 20:120]]. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.29} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.30}

## Entering through heedlessness
Sha'rawi cites Iblis's oath, 'By Your might I will mislead them all', in [[passage/quran-38-82|Q. 38:82]], and holds that Satan sees the human being while the human being does not see him, as in [[passage/quran-7-27|Q. 7:27]]. When the whispering is exposed by recognising the known enmity, Satan flees. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.29} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan p.31}

## Author's view
This is Sha'rawi's commentary and is attributed to him. Other readings of the story of Adam and Iblis are not yet presented.
