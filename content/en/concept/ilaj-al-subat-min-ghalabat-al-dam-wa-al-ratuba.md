## Prevailing blood
For what comes from prevailing blood, the evacuation of blood must be hastened and the food lightened {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93}

## Simple moisture
For what comes from prevailing simple moisture that is not with matter, the food is lightened, and care is taken with whatever moistens, since the moistening may overcome the power of the remedy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.93} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94}

## Moisture with phlegm
If the moisture is with a matter of phlegm, evacuation is made first, a way is contrived for him to vomit, for most of it is from phlegm in the stomach, and the stomach is cleared of it by what benefits phlegm, as he states in its place; the ripening means and the rest of what is known in the Canon are used {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94}

## Moving the soul
Among his treatments is that the patient is made to hear and see what grieves him, for grief in illnesses like these, in which thought weakens and congeals, moves the soul and returns it to soundness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.94}

## Note
Recipes, quantities, the names of drugs and plants, the names of veins and the sites of venesection, cupping and cautery, and methods of application are left out here. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.93-94}
