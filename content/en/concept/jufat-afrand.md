## Nature and selection
A thing shaped like a pine cone, with two spines at its head; it is said to resemble the almond, and sometimes it splits open {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423}. The footnote says the word is Persian and that it is a plant {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423}.

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: stirs desire greatly {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.423} [book listing].
