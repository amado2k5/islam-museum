## Nature and temperament
Nature: a plant that the editor's footnote gives as the plant called ears of the goat (adhan al-atr). {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}
Temperament: hot and dry, perhaps at the start of the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}

## Actions
Actions: it is said to dissolve irritation. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}

## Uses by organ
Excretory organs: Galen tested it and found that its decoction breaks up kidney stone. Some said it helps intestinal ulcers and colic, and helps hernias. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}
Labelled listing on the womb, set aside for the founder's ruling: it helps pains of the womb and brings away what the womb releases. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}
Poisons: a measured amount taken helps against the poison of the sea hare, opium and other things; the amount is left out. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.472}
