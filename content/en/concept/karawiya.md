## Nature and temperament
Dioscorides says karawiya is the seed of a known plant, whose stems and leaves resemble purslane but are more dusky in colour, and whose force is close to that of anise. Its nature: hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.524}

## Properties and digestion
It expels wind and dries; it is less fine than cumin. Organs of digestion: when drunk it stops the vomiting that comes from food rising, warms the stomach and aids digestion. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.524}

## Eye and chest
It enters eye remedies and sight-sharpening powders, and taken in excess it weakens sight. Chest: it helps hiccup and palpitation. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.524}

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling): the book says that the decoction of the plant and its seed, when drunk, bring on urine and calm colic and cut semen; that women who used its decoction found benefit for pains of the womb; that its burnt seed, applied to growing piles, removes them; and that taken as grain or seed it kills worms. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.524}
