## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the kinds of unnatural sediment: the scraped, bran-like, vetch-like and gruel-like; the fleshy, greasy, pus-like and mucous; that like bits of soaked leaven; and the clotted, hairlike, sandy-gravelly and ashen. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.193-194}.

## The division
The unnatural sediment is scraped, bran-like, vetch-like or gruel-like, resembling red arsenic or deep yellow; or fleshy, greasy, pus-like or mucous; or like pieces of soaked leaven; or bloody and clotted, hairlike, sandy and gravelly, or ashen. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.193-194}.
