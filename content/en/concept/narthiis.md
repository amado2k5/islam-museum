## Nature and selection
A hot drug, with a green, astringent fat in its interior, and with oil it provokes sweat. The name is as read from the page image; the extracted text gave a different first letter, and the plant is not identified {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Head, ears and teeth
It is blown into the nostrils and cuts nosebleed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Chest and breathing
Its fresh pith brings up the blood that gathers in the chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Excretory organs
Its pith stops long-standing diarrhoea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.

## Poisons and bites
Drunk with wine, it helps the bite of the viper {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.579}.
