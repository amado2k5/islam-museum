## Summary
The Qur'an teaches that God's measure of time is not man's: a day with your Lord as a thousand years of your reckoning, and a day of ascent measuring fifty thousand years — from which scholars taught that our count of time is relative, not absolute {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir}.
