## Nature and selection
Anaghalis has two kinds, one with a yellow flower and the other with a violet-blue flower {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}. The editor's note identifies it as the leech herb, or the leech-killer {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}.

## Temperament and actions
The book gives no temperament for it in this entry.

## Uses by organ
Wounds and ulcers: both kinds suit wounds, prevent their swelling and prevent ulcers from spreading {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}. Head: used as a gargle or as a snuff, the water of either draws much phlegm from the head and calms the pain of the tooth beside the cleft {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}. Excretory organs: taken with wine, it helps kidney pain; some claim that the blue-flowered kind supports a protruding anus and the red-flowered kind makes it protrude more {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}. Poisons: taken with wine, it helps a viper's bite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.378}.
