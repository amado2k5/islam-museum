## Summary
Al-Battani finds the position of the northern node, called the head of the dragon, from the mean for the time sought, using the method for finding the means. Full circuits are removed and the rest is counted from the first point of Aries. The tail falls on the diameter of the sphere, the point opposite the head, degree for degree. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.113}.

## The term and the place
He notes that the northern node, called the head of the dragon, and the tail are the two places where the inclined sphere meets the zodiac, already described in the chapter on the Moon's spheres. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.113}.
