## Nature
It is of the kind of the soft flesh among the organs of animals {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.703}

## Choice
The best are those of the young and of the grown such as he-goats and the like of them among rams; that of the bull is not digested, and they are not like those of cocks, especially the fattened, which are very good {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.703}

## Actions and properties
They lack the goodness of the nourishment of the breasts except the fattened cock's, which is good and abundant in nourishment; and all kinds, once digested, especially those harder to digest, nourish much {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.703}

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Most of them are hard to digest and much nourishing, especially those of a large animal with thick flesh {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.704}
