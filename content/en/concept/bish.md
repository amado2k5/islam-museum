## Nature and selection
A deadly poison {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

## Temperament and actions
Extremely hot and dry {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: applied and taken, in the electuary called jawarishna al-bizarjali, it helps leprosy and white leprosy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.

Poisons: a poison that destroys the drinker; the book says that even less than the usual portion kills; its antidote is the bish-mouse, which feeds on it and is not killed, and the musk-based electuary counters it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.408}.
