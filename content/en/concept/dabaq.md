## Nature and selection
Known. Its fruit is like black chickpea, not perfectly round, wrinkled and broken, and it makes the hand sticky; it grows in oak, apple and pear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.434}. The best is soft, smooth, green outside; it is pounded, washed and then boiled {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.434}. The footnote gives only a Latin name; the Arabic identity is queued for outside research.

## Temperament and actions
Temperament: it heats only after a long stay, and it is hot and dry in the third degree {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.434}. Actions: it dissolves thick humours from the depths because of its strong drawing power, and softens {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.434}.

## Uses by organ
Swellings and urticaria: it dissolves cold swellings, especially with lime, and helps urticaria {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
Wounds: it softens old ulcers and bad wounds {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
Head: it helps cold swellings behind the ears when mixed with resin and wax {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
Joints: it softens joints, with an equal part of resin and wax {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
Food: it dissolves the spleen when applied with strengthening substances, such as lime {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.435}.
