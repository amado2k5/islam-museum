## Nature
There are two sunbuls: sunbul al-tib, which is the sparrows' sunbul, and the nardin, which is the Roman sunbul; the aqliti is weaker than the Indian. The book reports from Dioscorides two kinds, one called Indian and the other Syrian, not because it is found in Syria but because the mountain on which it is found lies partly toward Syria and partly toward the land of India {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.602}.

## Selection
The best has plentiful hair and tends to yellowish, is of good scent like su'd, small in spike and clings to the tongue, and this is the Syrian; the Indian is weaker, longer and with more spikes, the black Indian is better than the red, and the best nardin is fresh, fragrant, many-rooted, full and not crumbling; the book warns of adulteration by soaking it or by antimony {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Temperament
Hot in the first degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Actions and properties
Opening and dissolving; the Indian has much astringency and less heat; a powder of sunbul al-tib checks excessive sweat, and the clay of sunbul is a good fragrant wash {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Swellings and pustules
It dissolves swellings and dries the moisture flowing from ulcers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Organs of the head
It checks flux to the head and strengthens the brain {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Organs of the eye
It grows the eyelashes when it enters collyria, and the nardin is stronger in this, as I reckon {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Organs of the chest
All of it helps palpitation, cleans the chest and lung and checks the pouring of matter to the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It opens blockages of the liver and stomach and strengthens them, all of it helps jaundice, with wine it helps the spleen, and with cold water it calms nausea {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Excretory organs
All of it promotes urine, the aqliti being stronger because it is thinner and less astringent; it helps pains of the kidneys and checks the flowing of matter to the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it helps all swellings of the womb by sitting in its decoction, and that it has a special property of checking excessive flow from the womb {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.603}.
