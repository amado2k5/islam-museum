## Excretory organs
It is said to break up a bladder stone; Galen denies it. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}

## Poisons
It is said that a suspension steeped from it is used for a viper bite. Galen said that a trustworthy man told him so. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.497}
