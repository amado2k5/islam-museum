Yalamlam (also alamlam or yarmarm) is a mountain of Tihama south of Mecca, two stages away, and the miqat of the people of Yemen. The jurists' sense is the lexical one. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18004}

General ruling: its status as a miqat is fixed by a text. Ibn 'Abbas reports that the Prophet set Dhu al-Hulayfa for the people of Medina, al-Juhfa for Syria, Qarn al-Manazil for Najd, and Yalamlam for Yemen, saying that these places are for their people and for whoever passes through them who is not of their people. Al-Nawawi explains that the miqat of Yemen is the miqat of its Tihama, since Yemen includes Najd and Tihama. The rules of the pilgrim's state of ihram are under ihram. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18005}
