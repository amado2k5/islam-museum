## Which temperaments hurt
The hot, the cold, the dry and the moist temperament may produce pains in the way the universal principles have taught {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.46-47} The dry temperament has little effect in causing pain, and the moist temperament, as moist, does not hurt unless there is a moist matter that hurts by vaporising or producing wind, and so brings about a break of continuity {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} The hot-dry and the cold-dry hurt by the two qualities and also by the movements that break continuity; the hot-moist and the cold-moist hurt only as hot and as cold, not as moist, except in the way mentioned {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47}

## Causes of the hot temperament
The hot temperament has as its cause either a hot matter, bloody or bilious or compound, sharp and inflamed, that acts by its quality; or a hot wind and vapour; or a heating movement of the body or the soul; or meeting fire or the burning of the sun; or taking a heating food or medicine; or the nearness of organs that have grown hot, and their sharing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} The causes of the cold temperament that causes headache are the opposites of these {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47}

## Causes of the dry temperament
The causes of dryness are either dryers from outside, by dissolving and burning, such as poisonous winds and hot poultices; or congealers, natural or accidental, sudden or not sudden, that keep nourishment from reaching the head so that its organs dry through the cutting off of supply; or the dissolving of the moisture inside, by dissolving it or by evacuating it, or because its power is drying, or because the nourishment made from it is dry or low in moisture {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} Dry organs nearby and their sharing dry it, and excessive movements of the soul and the body dry by way of evacuation and dissolving, and so do the passing of urine, loss of blood and strong exercise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} Some evacuations are in organs other than the organs of the head, which the head shares in, such as evacuations of the whole body; some are in the organs of the head, such as nasal catarrh, catarrh and nosebleed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47} Among the causes of dryness is the cutting off of the sources of moisture even without evacuation, such as fasting and leaving food or having none {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47}

## Intercourse (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
In listing the things that dry by evacuation and dissolving, Ibn Sina names intercourse {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.47}
