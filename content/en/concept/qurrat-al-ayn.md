## Nature
It is water-cress, also called water celery; it is of fragrant smell and grows in still waters {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.655}

## Actions and properties
Heating and dissolving {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.656}

## Excretory organs
It drives urine and breaks the stone in the kidneys, eaten raw or cooked, and benefits ulcers of the intestines {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.656}

## Womb, menses, foetus and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book lists that it brings on the menses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.656}
