## Nature
A berry of very pleasant taste, which grows in Shahrazur. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Temperament
Hot in the second degree and moist. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it fattens. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
Labelled listing, set aside for the founder's ruling: excretory organs: it greatly increases semen. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.489}
