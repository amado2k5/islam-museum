## Summary
Ibn Sina says each person has an exercise to suit him, that the gentle kinds such as swinging suit those weakened by fevers, convalescents, those weakened by hellebore and those with a disease of the diaphragm, and that swinging on a bed suits half-tertian and mixed or phlegmatic fevers, dropsy, gout and kidney diseases {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Swinging for the weak
Each person has the exercise that fits him. The gentle kinds, such as swinging, suit those weakened by fevers and made unable to move, convalescents, those weakened by drinking hellebore and the like, and those with a disease of the diaphragm. Used gently it brings sleep, dissolves winds, helps the remains of head diseases such as inattention and forgetfulness, stirs the appetites and rouses the innate faculty {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Swinging on a bed
Swinging on a bed suits those with a condition like the half-tertian, mixed and phlegmatic fevers, those with dropsy, those with the pains of gout and kidney diseases, for this swinging readies the matters to be uprooted, gentle for what is softer and strong for what is stronger {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.
