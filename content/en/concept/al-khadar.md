## The variance of the word
The word khadar is used variously in the books; sometimes the word khadar is made synonymous with the word tremor, but we and many people use it in this sense {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.158}

## Its definition with Ibn Sina
Khadar is an instrumental disorder producing a lesion in the tactile sense, either abolition or lessening, with tremor if — (the end of what this packet covers of the chapter; the rest in the next packet) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.158}
