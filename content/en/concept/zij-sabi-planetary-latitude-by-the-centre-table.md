## The adjustment before entering the table
The corrected centre of the planet from the third table is entered in the two rows, six parts apart, of the tables of latitude for the five wandering planets. For Saturn fifty is added to the centre, for Jupiter twenty is subtracted, and Mars is left unchanged. The matching minutes of latitude are then taken from the ninth table, the table of latitude parts for all the planets. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.178-179}

## North and south
If the entry falls in the upper half of the rows the northern table is used, and if in the lower half the southern table. The text names the two tables by garbled Greek names which are not reproduced here. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.178-179}
