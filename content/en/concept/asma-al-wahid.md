## Overview
al-Wahid — The One. It is name number 66 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Qur'an
The name occurs in the Qur'an, among them: [[passage/quran-12-39|12:39]]، [[passage/quran-13-16|13:16]]، [[passage/quran-14-48|14:48]]، [[passage/quran-38-65|38:65]]، [[passage/quran-39-4|39:4]]، [[passage/quran-40-16|40:16]].

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.
