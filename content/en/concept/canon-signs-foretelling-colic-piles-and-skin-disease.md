## Summary
Ibn Sina closes the chapter of warning symptoms with signs in the bowels and the skin: loss of appetite with vomiting, flatulence and pain in the limbs foretells colic; itching of the seat, unless small worms are the cause, foretells piles; many boils and cysts foretell many abscesses; ringworm foretells the black leprosy and white vitiligo the white {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.260-261}.

## Colic and piles
Loss of appetite with vomiting, flatulence and pain in the limbs foretells colic; itching in the seat, if it is not small worms there, foretells piles {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.260}.

## The skin
Many boils and cysts foretell many abscesses; ringworm foretells the black form of leprosy, and white vitiligo foretells the white form {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.261}.
