## Summary
The seven days from Saturday to Friday; the Qur'an names them in the six days of creation and in the appointed Friday.

## In the texts
The hadith says the best day the sun rises on is Friday: on it Adam was created, entered the Garden and left it, the Hour rises only on a Friday, and in it is an hour in which no Muslim asks without being given.
