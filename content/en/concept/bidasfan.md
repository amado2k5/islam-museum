## Nature and selection
It is a substitute for kasht barkasht; the Zanj make bracelets from it, and they are wooden {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}. The editor's footnote gives its name as the wazal {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.

## Temperament and actions
The entry gives no temperament or actions {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.414}.
