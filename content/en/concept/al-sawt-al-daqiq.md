## The fine voice
Its treatment is that he lay down the voice, keep to moderate fattening exercise and moderate foods, enter the bath every morning, and abandon the astringents, the driers and the waters (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.326}
