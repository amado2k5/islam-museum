## Summary
A son's son is the male first descendant of a son, by blood or by fostering. He is called a grandson, and the word son is used of him figuratively. He is an agnate: the higher son excludes him, and he excludes those below him, and he makes agnate his sisters and his paternal female cousins who stand level with him. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1458} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1459}

## General ruling
The jurists agree that a son's son is an agnate. They differ on whether he may contract his grandmother in marriage: the majority hold that he has this right, and the Shafi'is disagree. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1459}

## Where it is discussed
His share in inheritance is treated under the fixed shares when agnates are reached; his right in marriage under those who have the guardianship of marriage; and the rules of exclusion in their places. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1459}
