## Summary
On the verse about God's hand (48:10), Sha'rawi sets out the approach of the salaf and that of the khalaf, and gives his own view {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1104}.

## The salaf approach
He says the salaf take the word as God said it and place its mode and image within "there is nothing like Him" {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1104}.

## The khalaf approach
He reports that some interpret the attribute as an established one, saying the hand here means power, as God's power is affirmed by the same verse {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1104}.

## His view
He holds that the attribute is taken from God, who knows His own self, and is referred to the denial of likeness, as God has knowledge and sight not like ours {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-vol-2 p.1104}.
