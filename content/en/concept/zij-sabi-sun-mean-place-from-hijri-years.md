## Summary
Al-Battani shows that the Sun's mean place can also be found from Hijri years: the Hijri year is taken with the current year and looked up in the table of the Sun's motion, in the row of combined Arab years; the earlier procedure is then followed. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.107}.

## A check on the method
He says the motion over the four divisions, after the full circuits are removed, gives the Sun's mean place, and that the mean is the same whichever of the two dates is used; this serves as a model for finding the planets' means. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.107}.
