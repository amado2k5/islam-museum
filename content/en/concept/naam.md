## Nature and selection
Some physicians build a great structure on its meat {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.583}.

## Temperament
Some physicians have said that its meat is hot and fatty, spreads the food, strengthens the body and improves it, but is coarse and is not digested {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.583}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It increases desire {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.583}.
