## Nature and selection
Well known; it has broad, long leaves like the leaf of the marzuwan, and grows only in hot lands {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.573}.

## Actions and properties
It nourishes a little and is loosening; too much of it breeds obstructions and increases bile and phlegm according to the temperament {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.573}.

## Chest and breathing
Helpful for burning of the throat and chest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.573}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
Heavy on the stomach, and much of it weighs on it greatly; the hot-tempered should take seed oxymel after it, and the cold-tempered honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.573}.

## Excretory organs
It suits the kidneys and provokes urine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.573}.

## Womb, menses and desire (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
It increases semen and suits it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.573}.
