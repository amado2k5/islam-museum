## Summary
Al-Battani gives a rule for turning unequal days into mean days: he takes the distance between the Sun's first assumed mean place and the second place it reaches by the mean motion and keeps it, and then the distance between its first true place and the second by its true motion, in times of the ascensions of the signs in the right sphere; if the number of these times exceeds the kept parts of mean motion, the excess is found in mean hours and added to the assumed unequal days, and if less it is subtracted {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}.

## The result
Whatever the days come to after the addition or subtraction is what is turned from unequal days into mean days, from either starting point, that is from midday or from midnight, whichever is used for the beginning of days {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.79}. From them the mean motions are taken from the tables, and this ties to what he said [[concept/zij-sabi-day-count-begins-at-noon-or-midnight|on beginning the count at midday]] {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.78-79}.
