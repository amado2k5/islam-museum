## Summary
For someone who dislikes vomiting but cannot be spared it, Ibn Sina advises first preparing and accustoming him, softening, enriching and sweetening his food and resting him from exercise, then giving fats and oils with wine and good food before he brings it up {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.285}.

## The preparation
For one who does not like to vomit but must: the physician first prepares and accustoms him, softens his food, makes it fatty and sweet, lets him rest from exercise, then uses the emetic and gives him fats and oils with wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.285}.

## Food before the bringing up
He feeds him good foods before the vomiting, especially if he vomits with difficulty, for he may fail to vomit and nature may overcome him; to be dissolved by good food is better than to be dissolved by bad {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.285}.
