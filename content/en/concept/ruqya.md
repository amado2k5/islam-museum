## Overview
Ruqyah is the lawful counter-incantation: reciting Qur'an and permissible supplications over the sick or afflicted to seek healing and God's protection. The Prophet himself performed it when bewitched, as in al-Bukhari's Book of Medicine. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

The Muwatta devotes whole chapters to ruqyah: ruqyah for the eye-ailment, reciting the protective suras over children, the prohibition on ruqyah containing shirk or ignorance, and the line between lawful ruqyah and reliance on other than God.
