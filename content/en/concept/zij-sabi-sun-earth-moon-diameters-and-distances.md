## Summary
Al-Battani puts the Sun's distance from the Earth's centre at about 1210 Earth radii. He makes the Sun's diameter about eighteen and four-fifths times the Moon's, and about five and a half times the Earth's. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.90}.

## Earth and Moon diameters
He sets the Earth's diameter against the Moon's in fractional numbers and uses these ratios to derive the Sun's distance and the Earth's shadow. Some figures in the passage are wholly damaged and are not recorded. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.90}.
