## Overview
Quranism is a modern current restricting religious authority to the Qur'an alone and questioning hadith's authority; scholarship dates it to the modern prophetic-authority debate {cite:musa-hadith-scripture-2008}.

## Perspectives
- **Sunni frame:** the Sunna is an authoritative second source inseparable from the Book, as al-Shafi'i's Risala argues {cite:risala-shafii}.
- **Academic:** a position born of the nineteenth- and twentieth-century authority debates, not a classical school {cite:musa-hadith-scripture-2008}.
