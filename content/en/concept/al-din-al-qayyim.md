## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy holds that God described this religion as "al-qayyim", because it has the highest value, a value no other value equals, and because it sets human life straight. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:13:30}

## The meaning of al-qayyim
The Sheikh explains that al-qayyim is that which has the greatest or highest value, a value that no other value equals. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:11:00}
He gives the name this reason: the religion comes from God, so it sets human life straight, and it is the highest method a person can reach. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:13:30}

## One measure
The Sheikh holds that people measure things by different standards, and so their methods differ. If the unit of measure were one, the nation would have a single reference, the Book and the Sunna, to which disagreements are returned. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:11:30}

## Uprightness and God's limits
The Sheikh holds that whoever stays within God's limits lives in security and ease, and that whoever crosses them is worn down. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:14:00}
He says that the owner of the method is the Creator, who knows what suits a person and what harms him, so no one else can set a human life straight. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:14:30}

## Successive laws
The Sheikh holds that God gave religion as successive laws, each prophet bringing one part of them, until they were completed with the seal of the prophets. He illustrates this with the image of a house built well except for the place of one brick, which he applies to the prophets. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:10:00}

## The questions of the Companions and of today
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's view (opinion): the Companions asked few questions because they were busy acting, while people today ask many questions and act little. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-dinu-al-qayem/ep01 00:13:00}
