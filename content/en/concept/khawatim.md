## Overview
The endings of deeds are a meaning that Ibn Rajab explains through the hadith of Ibn Mas'ud: "Deeds are only by their endings." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

## The meaning according to Ibn Rajab
He holds that what counts is the state in which a deed ends, whether good or bad. A bad ending comes from a hidden inner trait in the servant that people do not see. He says that "endings are the inheritance of earlier deeds." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

## The fear of the early generations
He notes that the early generations greatly feared a bad ending. They feared that the lesser hypocrisy would prevail over them at the end, leading them into the greater hypocrisy. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

He reports that the Prophet often prayed: "O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm upon Your religion." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series "Qissa wa Ibra"
He devotes episode twenty to "deeds end with their endings": he recounts from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] that two men of the Children of Israel were brothers in God, one diligent in worship and the other sinful, and when the diligent one told the sinner "by God, God will not forgive you nor admit you to Paradise," both were taken and met before God — the sinner entered Paradise and the diligent one entered the Fire, for a word can reach the degree of God's displeasure or pleasure that it admits its owner to Paradise or Fire. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:02:30}
He says deeds end with their endings, and that Pharaoh's sorcerers who believed in their last moment entered Paradise with a single prostration; a good ending comes from a pure heart, God accepts repentance even at the death-rattle, there is no door to God and no doorkeeper, and the answered call is near. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy silsilat-qissa-wa-ebra/ep20 00:07:00}
