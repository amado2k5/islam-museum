## Overview
The endings of deeds are a meaning that Ibn Rajab explains through the hadith of Ibn Mas'ud: "Deeds are only by their endings." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

## The meaning according to Ibn Rajab
He holds that what counts is the state in which a deed ends, whether good or bad. A bad ending comes from a hidden inner trait in the servant that people do not see. He says that "endings are the inheritance of earlier deeds." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

## The fear of the early generations
He notes that the early generations greatly feared a bad ending. They feared that the lesser hypocrisy would prevail over them at the end, leading them into the greater hypocrisy. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}

He reports that the Prophet often prayed: "O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm upon Your religion." {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p. 148-154}
