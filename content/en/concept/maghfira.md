## Overview
Forgiveness in the forty-second hadith, the last of the Forty of al-Nawawi, is among the widest of God's promises to His servants. In it, God forgives whoever calls on Him and hopes in Him, however much he has done. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.830-831}

Ibn Rajab draws from the hadith three causes by which forgiveness is obtained: supplication with hope, seeking forgiveness, and meeting God without associating anything with Him. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam p.830-831}

Forgiveness is part of the chapter on [[concept/tawba|repentance]]. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
