## Overview
The bay'a in legal usage is the contract of submission and obedience; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia treats its entries from the contract of bay'a to its oaths {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The encyclopedia covers the bay'a contract to two imams and the bay'a oath among its entries {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
