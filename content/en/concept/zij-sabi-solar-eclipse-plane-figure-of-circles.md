## The geometric figure of the solar eclipse
A circle of the sum of the radii is drawn around a centre, with the solar circle inside it on the same centre. Both circles are divided by two crossing lines at right angles and the horizon directions are marked. The visible latitudes at the beginning, the middle and the end are then laid off on the dividing line, and the circles of the Moon are drawn through them; the part of the solar circle that falls inside the middle Moon circle is the part eclipsed. The circles for the beginning and the end touch the solar circle. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.173-175}

## The letters and the places
The points of the figure carry abjad letters. The text says the figure gathers the whole explanation on one face, but the disturbed letters on pages 173 to 175 are not reproduced. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.173-175}
