## The arc of appearance
The text says the arc by which a star of the first magnitude rises into view and disappears is fifteen degrees, for stars such as Sirius, Regulus, the Pleiades and Arcturus. As the magnitude diminishes, the arc grows larger, until the smallest stars appear and disappear by a fixed amount close to the sun. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.185}

## The figure and its use
The arc is used in the reckoning as described for the planets' visibility, and the text treats the appearance values as varying with the size of the star. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.185}
