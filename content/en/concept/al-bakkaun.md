## Summary
Seven men of the Ansar and others came to the Prophet asking for mounts to Tabuk; he found nothing to carry them, so they turned back, their eyes overflowing with tears of sorrow. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}

## In Ibn Ishaq's Sira
- Ibn Ishaq names them "al-Bakka'un" (the Weepers) and counts seven: Salim ibn Umayr, Ulba ibn Zayd, Abu Layla Abd al-Rahman ibn Ka'b, Amr ibn Humam ibn al-Jumuh, Abdullah ibn al-Mughaffal al-Muzani, Harami ibn Abdullah, and Irbad ibn Sariya. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}
- There is a dispute over the names which the editors, and Ibn Ishaq himself, noted, concerning Abdullah ibn al-Mughaffal (or Abdullah ibn Amr al-Muzani). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/161}
