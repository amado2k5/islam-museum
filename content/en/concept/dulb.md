## Temperament and actions
Its bark and nut are very dry, cold in the first degree; the nut and bark dry strongly; the dust of the leaf is bad for the senses {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.

## Uses by organ
Adornment: its bark has polishing and drying strength, and may help the white leprosy spot {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Swellings: its leaf helps phlegmatic swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Wounds: its ash is put on flaking and dirty wounds, and they heal; the bark boiled in vinegar helps a burn {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Joints: the leaf helps joint pain and hot swellings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Head: bark boiled in vinegar is good for toothache; its dust is bad for hearing and the ear {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Eyes: the dust of the leaf harms the eye; the fresh leaf, washed, boiled and applied as a poultice, checks the falling of humours into the eye and helps inflammation and sore eye {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Chest: its dust harms the lung and the voice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
Poisons: the fresh fruit with wine helps pest bites; its nut with fat is a poultice for bites and stings {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}; the book says it is poison to beetles, which die from its leaf and bark {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.437}.
