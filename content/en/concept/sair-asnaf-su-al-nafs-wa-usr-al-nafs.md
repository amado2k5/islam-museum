## The rest of the bad-breath kinds and the hard breath
For the nervous kind of the hard breath the book describes a regimen including the calx of the leech found under the jars, burnt until it turns to ash (a labelled line for the leech, set before the founder); and the saffron itself is the utmost in benefit for the weakness of the nerves' roots, on the book's wording (the formulae, measures and names are excluded) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.322}
