## Nature
A sweet-smelling drug, its colour the colour of the quince {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## Actions and properties
It congeals milk, and its power is drying with a little sharpness; its flower is beneficial for the bursting forth of blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## Wounds and ulcers
It may be thought that this drug heals from a burn {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}

## End of the letter
This is the end of the discussion of the letter ghayn, and the total of the drugs we mentioned in this chapter is eleven in number, and it is the end of the discussion of Book II; the book then turns to Book III {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.727}
