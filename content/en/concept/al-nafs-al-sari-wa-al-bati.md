## The swift breath and the slow
The yield of the two chapters: swiftness follows the need's intensity where largeness falls short of sufficing or is barred in the instrument or the power; slowness is its contrary, and pain may bring it on in care for the breathing organ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.311}
