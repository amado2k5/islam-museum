## Nature
It is the stone that grinds grain, according to the editor's note. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}

## Uses by organ
Swellings and pustules: the vapour of vinegar over it stops bleeding and prevents hot swellings. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.496}
