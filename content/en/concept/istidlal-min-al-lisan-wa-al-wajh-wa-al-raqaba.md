## Tongue, face, neck and nerves
The tongue often indicates by its colour the state of the brain, as it indicates by its whiteness lethargus, by yellowness first and blackening second faranitus, and by a prevalence of yellow with greening of the vessels beneath it the epileptic condition of its owner {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 pp.20-21} Inference from the colour of the tongue is not like inference from the colour of the eye, which is very particular to the brain; the colour of the tongue may be used to infer the states of the stomach {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.21}

As for the face, fatness and redness indicate the prevalence of blood, leanness with yellowness the prevalence of yellow bile, leanness with dullness the prevalence of black-bilious dryness, and flushing the prevalence of blood and wateriness, provided these are accidental states and not original ones {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.21}

The neck, if strong and thick, indicates a strength of the brain's powers and its abundance, and if short and thin, the opposite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.21} If it is ready to receive scrofula and swellings, the cause is not weakness in it but weakness of the digesting power in the brain and strength of the expelling power, since the regions of the neck receive what the brain expels, with the soft glandular flesh in them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.21} The state of the uvula, tonsils and teeth is likewise {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.21}

As for the nerves, when they grow strong and thick and the paths they run along are strong, they indicate the strength of the brain, and the opposite indicates the opposite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.21}
