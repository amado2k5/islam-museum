## The gathering of water in the lung
And it is indicated by a chilliness, a soft fever, a swelling in the extremities, bad breathing, a thin watery expectoration, and a state like the state of the dropsical (a line on the book's wording) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.352}
