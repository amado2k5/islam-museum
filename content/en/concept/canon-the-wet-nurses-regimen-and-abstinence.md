## Summary
Ibn Sina bids the nursing woman take moderate exercise and food of good chyme, and not to have intercourse at all, because it stirs the menstrual blood, spoils the smell of the milk and lowers its amount, and she may conceive, which harms both children {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.209}.

## Exercise and food
The nursing woman should be told to take moderate exercise and be fed with food that makes good chyme {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.209}.

## Intercourse and pregnancy
She should not have intercourse at all, for it stirs her menstrual blood, which spoils the smell of the milk and reduces its quantity, and she may even conceive, with great harm to both children: to the nursling because the fine part of the milk turns to feeding the embryo, and to the embryo because it gets little food, as the other needs the milk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 pp.209-210}.
