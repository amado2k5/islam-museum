## Summary
Ibn Sina divides unevenness within one beat into unevenness across its many parts, that is the places of the fingers, and unevenness in one part, and then details what lies in the position of the parts and in their motion {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.172}.

## In the position of the parts
Unevenness in the position of the parts is a difference in the relation of the parts of the vessel to the directions, and since the directions are six, so are the differences that fall in them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.172}.

## In the motion of the parts
Unevenness in motion is either in speed and slowness, or in lateness and earliness, meaning that a part moves before or after its time, or in strength and weakness, or in size and smallness. All of it runs either in an even order or in an uneven order by increase and decrease, over two, three or four of the finger places, and the physician is left to combine them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.172}.
