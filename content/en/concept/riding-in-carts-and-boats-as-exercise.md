## Summary
Ibn Sina says riding in a cart stirs more than swinging, and riding it facing backward greatly helps weak sight; riding in small boats and ships near the shore helps leprosy, dropsy, apoplexy and cold and bloated stomach, and going out on the open sea is stronger against these diseases because of the joy and sorrow that alternate in the soul {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Riding in a cart
Riding in a cart may do the same work but stirs more than swinging, and a cart may be ridden facing backward, which greatly helps weak and dim sight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.

## Riding in boats and ships
Riding in small boats and ships helps leprosy, dropsy, apoplexy and a cold, bloated stomach, when it is done near the shores. Riding in ships out on the swelling sea is stronger at uprooting these diseases because of the joy and sorrow that come by turns upon the soul {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.223}.
