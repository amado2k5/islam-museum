## Summary
Tasarri is a master's lawful relations with a woman he owns, allowed by the Qur'an, the Sunna and agreement, subject to conditions, of which ownership and the absence of a bar are the main ones. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5065} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5066}

## Definition
In the language, tasarri is taking a slave woman as a concubine. In the Hanafi sense it is preparing a woman to be lain with; it is complete there with two things, housing her and lying with her. This entry treats it as lying with an owned woman in general. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5065} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5066}

## Basis
Jurists cite the verses on the permitted kinds of women (surah al-Nisa and surah al-Mu'minun), the report that the Prophet gave a slave woman to Hassan ibn Thabit, and the practice of the Companions; the community agreed on it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5067} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5068}

## Conditions
Ownership is required: one may not lie with a woman owned by another, a part-freed woman, or a woman owned jointly. The woman must be a Muslim or a People of the Book, and must not be barred by a temporary or permanent bar, such as a close kinswoman. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5069} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5070}

## Waiting period, number and choice
A man who acquires a woman may not lie with her before her waiting period is complete: if she is pregnant, until she gives birth; if not, until she has one menstrual period with him. The number of concubines is not limited. It is recommended to choose one of religion, beauty and sense. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5070} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5071}

## Its effects
Lying with an owned woman makes her mother and daughters forbidden to him for ever, and makes her forbidden to his father and sons; kinship bars are established. Two sisters may not be joined in relations at once: the other is forbidden until the first is freed or sold. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5070}
If an owned woman bears a child by her master she becomes an umm walad and is freed at his death; the details are under that entry. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5071}

## Descent of the child
The jurists differ. The Hanbalis and Malikis hold the child descends from the master if it is possible he is the father, on the hadith that the child belongs to the bed. The Hanafis hold it descends only by acknowledgement. Al-Hasan and al-Sha'bi hold it descends and he may not disown it once acknowledged. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5071} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5072}
