## Nature and selection
The laurel tree; its berry is used, and its leaf and berry are the strongest, then the root bark {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}. The editor's footnote says it is the laurel {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.438}; the book refers the rest to the letter ghayn.

## Temperament
Hot in the third degree and dry in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.

## Uses by organ
Joints: good for relaxation of the nerve, paralysis and facial palsy {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.
Head: its powder causes sneezing {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.
Food: it helps tumours of the liver and spleen {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.
Excretory organs: it helps colic {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.439}.
