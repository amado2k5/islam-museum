## Summary
Al-Battani uses the cube, that is length times breadth times depth, to compare the volumes of bodies. He shows that the Sun's body is about one hundred and sixty-six and three eighths times the Earth's volume, and compares it with the Moon in the same way. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.90}.

## The Earth's cube as unit
He takes the Earth's cube as the unit of measure and computes the Moon's and the Sun's cubes from the stated diameters. Several large figures are damaged, so they are recorded as read, with caution. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.91}.
