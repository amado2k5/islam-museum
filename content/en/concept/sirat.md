## Overview
The sirat in the creed is the bridge stretched over Jahannam which people cross after the standing; the Sharh describes it as finer than a sword's edge, slippery {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.

## In the sources
The Sharh's sirat chapter describes a bridge over Jahannam crossed after the standing, 'like a sword's edge, slippery' {cite:sharh-tahawiyya-ibn-abi-al-izz}.

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 4)

The Sheikh cites a hadith that on the Sirat there are hooks like the thorn of the sa'dan, a thorn that grows in the Arabian Peninsula and is eaten by camels. He likens them to curved hooks like those a chandelier hangs from, and says each hook seizes the person whose sin it is assigned to {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:40:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep04 00:40:30}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
Omar Abdelkafy mentions what some scholars say, that on the Sirat there are seven bridges at which the servant is held and questioned: the bridge of God's oneness, then of prayer, fasting, zakat, pilgrimage, enjoining good and forbidding wrong, and lastly of wrongs done to others, at which those who wronged people are held. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep10 00:15:00}
