## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy says that the bearer of the Qur'an is known by his night when people sleep, by his weeping when people laugh, by his silence when people engage in idle talk and by his humility when people are proud. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:10:30} He holds that he should be wise and forbearing, neither harsh nor careless. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:11:00}

## Signs of the Qur'an bearer
The Sheikh quotes a saying, whose speaker he does not name in the recording, that the bearer of the Qur'an is known by his night when people sleep and by his day when people slacken (needs_source: the word is unclear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:10:00}
He says that he weeps when people laugh, is silent when people engage in idle talk, and is humble when people are proud. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:10:30}

## Wisdom, forbearance and avoiding harshness
The Sheikh says that the bearer of the Qur'an should be wise and forbearing, and should be neither harsh nor careless. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:11:00}

## Humility raised, pride lowered
The Sheikh quotes a saying, not named in the recording, that whoever is humble God raises and whoever is proud God lowers. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep04 00:11:00}
