## Summary
A hired worker is one who works for a wage. The law orders that his wage be paid before his sweat dries. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1714} When a worker is competent to contract, meets the conditions, can deliver the benefit and the work is lawful, he must perform the contract. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1714} A private worker must surrender himself to his employer for the term and not work for others, except for the obligatory prayers; a shared worker must do and deliver the work and is paid on delivery. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1714}
