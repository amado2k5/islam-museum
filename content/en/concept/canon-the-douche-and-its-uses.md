## Summary
Ibn Sina counts douches good treatments for what must be dissolved from the head and other organs and for what needs its temperament changed, and orders their use hot first and then cold water to firm the part when no superfluities are flowing down, and the reverse when the case is the opposite {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.289}.

## Its benefits
Douches are good treatments for what needs to be dissolved from the head and other organs, and for what needs its temperament changed, and for organs needing a hot or a cold douche {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.289}.

## The order of use
If there are no superfluities flowing down, the warmed douche is used first and then cold water so that the part firms; if the matter is the opposite, he begins with the cold {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.289}.
