## Summary
Al-Battani divides the circle of the sphere into twelve parts of thirty degrees each, names each sign after the figure next to it, lists the names in order from Aries to Pisces, and notes that these figures can shift with long time from the divisions named after them. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.

## Three parts to each season
He says anything with an extent has a middle and two ends, so each of the four seasons divides into three parts, hence the divisions of the sphere's circle must be twelve. These are the twelve signs, each of thirty parts, together 360. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.

## The names of the signs
Each sign is named from the figure next to it: first Aries, then Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces. The naming holds throughout the zij, so a degree of Aries or Libra means a division of the sphere and not particular stars. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.

## The figures leaving their places
Al-Battani notes that the figures may move away in time from the divisions named after them, so separating the sign, a division of the sphere measured from the equinox point, from the figure, the group of stars it is named for. The remark prepares the ground for chapter 51 on the motion of the fixed stars by observation. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion p.12}.
