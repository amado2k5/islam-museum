## Nature and selection
Nature: Dioscorides says pitch, also called ighra', has two kinds: a black sea pitch that flows and enters ointments, of the kind of bitumen, and a mountain pitch. The mountain pitch is the flowing resin of the fir tree and other pines; it is moist at first and is then dried by boiling, and most of it comes from the fir. Pitch oil is close to tar and is made by distilling its moist form while it is boiled. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}

## Actions
Actions: it ripens thick humours, cleans and warms; the moist kind ripens more, the dry kind dries more, and it enters ointments. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: it removes whiteness of the nails and draws blood to the organs so that they fatten, especially when applied and pulled off again with force. Smeared on fissures of the foot and other organs it mends them, and as a poultice it grows hair in the alopecia the book calls the fox's disease. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Swellings and pustules: it softens hard swellings, especially the moist kind; with barley flour it is used on scrofulous swellings; mixed with sulphur or with fir bark it prevents the creeping of ants, the book says; and it helps abscesses of all the glands. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Wounds and ulcers: it clears scabs and grows flesh in deep ulcers, especially with frankincense powder and honey; it cleans ulcers full of rotten humours, and the dry kind dries more in wounds. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Joints: it helps swellings of the muscles. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Head organs: both the dry and the moist kinds are good for ulcers of the head. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Eye organs: the smoke of pitch improves the eyelashes, grows the lids, prevents tearing, fills ulcers in the eye and strengthens the sight. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Chest organs: it helps dry cold cough, especially with almonds and sugar, and likewise pleurisy and pneumonia; it eases expectoration and ripens; with almond oil its ripening is greater, alone its cleansing is less than its ripening, and with sugar the opposite. It prevents spitting of blood; licked with honey it helps the vomiting of pus; and wet pitch rubbed on the throat is good for quinsy. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Excretory organs: it is a mild laxative, and much of it purges; given as an enema for hot swellings on fissures of the anus, it cures them. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
Poisons: it counters poisons, and when applied it helps viper bites. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.463}
