## Summary
"Ihsan" is what the Prophet defined in Gabriel's hadith, saying: "That you worship God as though you see Him; if you do not see Him, He sees you" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.

## Its place in the hadith
Ihsan comes third, after the two questions on [[concept/iman|faith]] and [[concept/islam-din|Islam]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}. The hadith is what al-Bukhari included in the "Book of Faith" in the chapter of Gabriel's questioning the Prophet {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.

## Explaining the word
The editor explains the phrase "as though you see Him" as presence of mind, emptiness of self and gatheredness of heart — as though the worshipper were beholding the divine presence {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.

## What is not covered here
Ihsan is connected to Sufism and spirituality in Islamic history; those sayings will be added from their sources in a later batch, and nothing not present in the hadith is attributed to it here.
