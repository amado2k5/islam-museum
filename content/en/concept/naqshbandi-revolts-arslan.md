## Overview
In the chapter on the Islamic union, Shakib Arslan presents the spread of the Naqshbandi order from Central Asia eastward as far as China. {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}

He mentions that the Muslims of Chinese Turkestan and Yunnan rose in great revolts in the nineteenth century, and that revolts in the Dutch East Indies broke out in the same period. {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}

Arslan holds that they shared hostility to the West and lacked organisation and the linking of their parts. This is his own assessment, and the sequence of events needs a further source. {cite:stoddard-arslan-hadir-1923}
