## Nature
Dioscorides said that some people call the azaa sura; it is an animal like the gecko, except that it is green, slow in movement and of varied colours, and some claimed that if it enters fire it is not burnt. Its power is weak, and it is stored as the blister-beetle is stored, its entrails taken out and its hands and feet cut off, and kept in honey {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.620}

## Wounds and ulcers
It benefits scab as the blister-beetle does, and it enters corrosive and soothing ointments {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.620}

## Cosmetics
Its tail, cooked in oil until it falls apart, removes hair {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.621}
