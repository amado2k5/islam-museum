## Temperament
It has a little heat and cold, such that its heat is slackened, as though it were not strongly cold; its cold is at the end of the first degree and its dryness is strong, to the third {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Actions and properties
Astringent to a degree, its root more strongly; it goes into theriac to firm the limbs, and its juice is as astringent as rose seed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Wounds and ulcers
Its dried leaf heals and helps old ulcers, and its flower is stronger in all this {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Head, ears and teeth
Its root is among the remedies that cleanse earwax, dry the ear's ulcers and help deafness {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Chest and breathing
Its flower, leaf or root, whichever, drunk with barley water, helps ulcers of the lung, and its juice is for spitting blood {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Digestion and the organs of nourishment
It strengthens the stomach and stops matter from flowing into it, especially its juice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.539}.

## Excretory organs
It is the strongest remedy for ulcers of the intestines when its flower in particular, or its juice, is drunk with wine {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.540}.

## Excretory organs (labelled book listing, held for the founder's ruling)
The book says it helps bleeding of the womb, applied or drunk {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.540}.
