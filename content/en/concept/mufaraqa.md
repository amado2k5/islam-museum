## Summary
Mufaraqa means separation. In prayer it is a follower leaving his imam, or a worshipper leaving the congregation. This part of the entry covers pages 14805 to 14811 and the discussion continues beyond them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14805}

## Leaving without an excuse
The Hanafis, Malikis, Hanbalis and the older Shafi'i view hold that a follower may not leave his imam without an excuse, because joining the prayer binds him from the start, citing the hadith that the imam is appointed to be followed. On these views the prayer of one who leaves without excuse is void. The main Shafi'i view, and a second Hanbali view, hold it valid but disliked. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14805} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14806} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14807}

## Leaving with an excuse
Most jurists (Malikis, Shafi'is and Hanbalis) allow a follower to intend to pray alone for an excuse. They cite the story of Mu'adh ibn Jabal, whom the Prophet did not order to repeat his prayer. A widely agreed excuse is the imam's recitation being so long that the follower cannot bear it. The Shafi'is add that the imam omits a recommended act such as the first tashahhud or the qunut. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14807} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14808}

## When leaving is required
Leaving is obligatory in several cases: when the imam turns away from the qibla, according to most jurists; when the imam becomes involved in something that voids his prayer, such as impurity, loss of ritual purity, or drunkenness; and when he misreads the opening chapter in a way that changes its meaning. The Shafi'is also require it if the imam stands for a fifth rak'a. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14809} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14810} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14811}

## Leaving in the Friday prayer
The Shafi'is and Hanbalis allow a worshipper to leave the congregation at the second rak'a of the Friday prayer. They do not allow it at the first, because congregation is a condition there. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.14811}
