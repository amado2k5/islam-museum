## Nature and selection
Nature: a deep red thing resembling ground saffron, brought from Yemen; it is said to be scraped from its trees. Temperament: hot and dry in the second degree. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}

## Actions
Astringent. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}

## Uses by organ
Adornment: helps freckles and dark spots; when drunk it helps white patches. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}
Swellings and pustules: helps pustules. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}
Wounds and ulcers: helps scabies and itching. {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.454}
