## How the harmful matter comes to be in the organ
The harmful matter comes to be in the organ either as long established and held back, or as nutritive, that is generated at the time from food as a chyme bad in its substance and quality, through a fault in the food itself, in its order, its amount or its digestion, or in the other ways of its corruption mentioned in its chapter {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49} Of this kind is the headache of eating garlic, onion and mustard, and the hangover headache {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49}

## Winds
Wind is among the matters that cause headache, and it does so by distension, when a natural passage narrows upon it, one made narrower than it should be for its time, or when it seeks to make an unnatural passage {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49}

## Vapours
Vapour is also among these and acts either by its quality or by crowding the humours in their places and so moving them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49} Winds and vapours may be generated in the body and in the brain itself, or be breathed in from outside, or come by the pores and then be pent up in the brain, so that headache comes; of this kind are the headache of stench and the headache of perfume {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49} Know that phlegmatic winds and vapours are heavy, slow-moving and held back; the black-bile ones are dreary and fixed, less in amount or worse in quality; the sharp humours do not stir winds but vapours; the bloody vapours are sweet and less harmful, rather doing most of their harm by quantity; and the bilious are sharp and inflamed {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.49}
