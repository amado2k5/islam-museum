## Nature and selection
Afyus of the pupil, which is something that tightens the pupil {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. The editor's note says it is turnip {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. It is not the same as afyun, the juice of the poppy, which has its own entry in this book.

## Temperament and actions
Galen said: cold in the second degree and drying in the first; its fruit is hot and astringent at the start of the first degree, and drying in the second {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}. It keeps the pubic area of boys free of hair for a time {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.

## Uses by organ
Food organs: its fruit helps jaundice {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.379}.
