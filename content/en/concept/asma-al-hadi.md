## Overview
al-Hadi — The Guide. It is name number 94 in the enumeration of God's most beautiful names in the hadith of Abu Hurayra at al-Tirmidhi {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}; Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya devoted a chapter of Jala' al-Afham to explaining these names {cite:jala-afham-ibn-qayyim}.

## In the Sunna
Abu Hurayra reports: 'God has ninety-nine names — a hundred less one; whoever enumerates them enters Paradise' {cite:sahih-muslim}.

## In people's lives
And among the seerah's manifestations of guidance: Salman al-Farisi's journey seeking the truth — from Zoroastrianism to Christianity — until God guided him to Islam in Medina. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
