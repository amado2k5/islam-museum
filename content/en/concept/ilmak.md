## Definition
Imlak in the lexical sense is marriage, the contract of the nikah. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3396}

## Ruling
The feast of the contract is a sunna for the Shafi'is and Hanbalis, and accepting the invitation to it is a sunna for the Shafi'is. The Shafi'is hold that one feast is enough, the feast of consummation being the same. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3396}{cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.3397}
