## Its states
This may occur in the fevers — especially the sleepless — and after evacuations, insomnia, grief and care. In the insomniac kind the eye is sleepy, heavy and hard to move in the lid, not the pupil; in grief the pupil is still. It is told that some people had a difference between the two halves in severe cold and severe heat, and the eye on the cold half sank and shrank ⁦(the anecdote is transmitted as a report in the book, not endorsed)⁩ {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.191}
