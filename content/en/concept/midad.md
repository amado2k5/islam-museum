## Nature and selection
Well known. Selection: the best is the lightest in weight and the blackest {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.563}.

## Temperament
All of it hot and drying except the Indian, which the Indians and Paulus count among the coolers {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.563}.

## Actions and properties
All of it dries {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.563}.

## Swellings and pustules
Some claimed that the Indian, put on hot swellings, helps them {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.563}.

## Wounds and ulcers
The kind made from the smoke of pine wood with gum and muql is put on a burn from fire and left until it falls away {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.563}.
