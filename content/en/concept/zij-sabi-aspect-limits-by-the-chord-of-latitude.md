## The full chord and the second side
To find how many parts a body throws its ray of sextile or trine onto the ecliptic when it has a latitude, the chord of the remainder of the second side is taken from the half-chord tables, squared, and the full chord of the latitude is found and squared as well. The corrected second side is obtained by subtraction from the square of the full chord of the aspect, and the corrected chord is then turned into an arc, giving the sextile and trine on the ecliptic. {cite:zij-sabi-nallino-byblion pp.200-201}
