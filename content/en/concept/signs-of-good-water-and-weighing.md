## Summary
Ibn Sina says good water is sweet to the taste so that it seems sugary, takes little wine when mixed with it, is light in weight, cools and warms quickly because of its looseness, is cold in winter and warm in summer, is dominated by no taste or smell, passes quickly down from the lower ribs, and quickly cooks what is cooked in it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.135}.

## Weighing
Weight is one of the successful guides for judging water, and the lighter is in most cases better. It may be found by a measure, or by wetting two cloths or two cottons of equal weight in two different waters, drying them thoroughly and weighing them: the water whose cotton is lighter is the better {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.135}.
