## Summary
Sha'rawi holds that la'na is expulsion and removal from God's mercy; a curse with anger is an expulsion with no return, and a disciplinary curse is removal without anger. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.673}

## Angry curse and disciplinary curse
Sha'rawi says that the word la'na occurs forty-one times in the Qur'an, that expulsion in anger leads to eternity in the Fire, and that discipline is not done in anger, since the one who disciplines is not angry with the one he disciplines. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.673}

## The curse of God, the angels and people
Sha'rawi reads the verse on those who conceal the truth as wider than the curse of the Hereafter, since all creatures share in cursing those through whom they were deprived of benefit. In the Hereafter the deceived disown the deceivers and the deceivers the deceived, and they curse one another. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.674}

## The removal of those who stayed behind from Tabuk
Sha'rawi reports that the Prophet ordered the Muslims not to speak to the three who stayed behind, which was a disciplinary removal, and that their release came ten days later with the words of [[passage/quran-9-118|9:118]]. He holds that the door of return is never closed. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.675} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.676} {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.677}

## Limits of this batch
The images of plants, animals and places cursing are not adopted, since they name no source, and appear here only as Sha'rawi's exposition. Other exegetes' views, the academic view and other views are not yet entered. {cite:tafsir-sharawi-khawatir-scan-pp-501-1000 p.674}
