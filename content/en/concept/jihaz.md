## Overview

Jihaz in the language is what a fighter or traveller needs, and what a bride takes to her husband's house. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6624}

## Equipping the fighters

Equipping the registered fighters is obligatory on the Muslims and is met from the public treasury. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6624}

## The bride's trousseau

The bride is not obliged to furnish her trousseau from her dower. It belongs to her alone, and the dower is not treated as its price. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6625}
