## Overview
For Ibn Sina the elements are simple bodies that do not divide into bodies of different forms — from them creatures are compounded by mixture; they are four: fire and air are light, water and earth heavy; earth he describes as cold and dry, water as cold and moist {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.17}. The physician takes this on trust from natural philosophy and does not prove it in his own craft {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.17}.
