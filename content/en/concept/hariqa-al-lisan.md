## Its causes
It may come from a heat in the mouth of the stomach or the brain short of being a fever, or from taking sharp, salty, bitter and sweet things and severe thirst, and it may come from greater causes such as the hot fevers and the inward tumours {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.260}
## Its treatment
Its treatment in sum: the complainant — especially the sick — is kept from sleeping on his back and from lasting mouth-gaping; pills made of the cooling seeds of this kind are held to, the kernels and the known mucilages and cooling moistening juices are held in the mouth, it is wiped if there is a viscid oily humour, then oils and rinses of this kind are kept up; and some people treat it by rubbing it with mint (a saying ascribed to some people; the other names left out) {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v2 p.260}
