## What the texts say
In [[passage/quran-2-30|al-Baqara 30]] God tells the angels that He will place a vicegerent on the earth; they ask about placing one who will spread corruption and shed blood, and He answers that He knows what they do not. The verse does not say who was on earth before that vicegerent, nor how the angels knew of corruption and bloodshed. Everything said about "the dwellers of the earth before Adam" is transmitted exegesis around that question, not an explicit Qur'anic statement. Other verses speak of Adam created from clay and of the jinn created from fire [[passage/quran-15-27|al-Hijr 27]] [[passage/quran-55-15|al-Rahman 15]], and another speaks of a span of time in which man was nothing worth mentioning [[passage/quran-76-1|al-Insan 1]].

## Ibn Abbas's report in al-Tabari
Al-Tabari gives a chain through al-Dahhak to Ibn Abbas: the first to dwell on earth were the jinn; they spread corruption, shed blood and killed one another, so God sent Iblis with an army of angels, who drove them to the islands of the seas and the edges of the mountains; then Adam was created and settled there, which is why God spoke as He did. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF p. 454} A second report, from al-Rabi' ibn Anas on the authority of Abu al-Aliya, says God created the angels on Wednesday, the jinn on Thursday and Adam on Friday; a group of the jinn disbelieved, and the angels descended to fight them, which brought bloodshed and corruption. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 454-455} In al-Dahhak's longer version al-Tabari has Iblis, from a clan of angels, grow conceited after defeating the jinn. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 459-460} Ibn Kathir repeats the report from al-Dahhak and adds one from Mujahid on the authority of Abd Allah ibn Amr: the jinn, "the sons of al-Jann", were on earth two thousand years before Adam, corrupted it, and God sent a force of angels who struck them until they were pushed to the islands of the seas. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 p. 70}

## How scholars weighed it
Al-Tabari did not adopt this reading, and gave his reason: knowledge of past events reaches us only by a report that excludes excuse, and that is not found in al-Dahhak's report from Ibn Abbas or in Ibn Zayd's. Yet he did not call it corrupt or impossible, and said that the report is "not invalid" as an account of the angels asking by way of inquiry. He himself held that the angels were asking for information. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 473-475} Ahmad Shakir, his editor, notes that al-Tabari's chain here is weak, and that al-Hakim in al-Mustadrak has a similar report through Mujahid from Ibn Abbas that al-Hakim called sound in its chain though the two Shaykhs did not include it, with al-Dhahabi agreeing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF p. 454} I did not read the Mustadrak text myself; that grading is relayed from the editor's footnote. Ibn Kathir sets out the reports without an explicit grading of al-Dahhak's report at this point, but cautions that the well-known chain of al-Suddi carries many Israelite elements, and calls two other reports about the angels' question strange, objectionable and taken from the People of the Book. {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 pp. 71, 76-77} Al-Qurtubi places the report among the views and gives it, in question form, to Ahmad ibn Yahya Tha'lab. {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-alam-kutub-bukhari p. 274}

:::perspectives
- **Sunni view: the jinn held the earth before Adam.** Ibn Abbas by al-Dahhak's chain in al-Tabari, with parallels from al-Rabi' ibn Anas, Mujahid and Abd Allah ibn Amr; on this view the angels measured the new vicegerents against those before them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF p. 454} {cite:tafsir-ibn-kathir-alexandria-024188 p. 70}
- **Sunni view: the angels knew because God told them.** From Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas and other Companions in al-Suddi's account: God told them the vicegerent would have offspring who corrupt and kill; al-Tabari treats it as possible while criticising the internal inconsistency of some wording, and himself reads the question as a request for information. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 456, 465-466}
- **Sunni view: they reasoned from the meaning of vicegerency.** Reported by al-Qurtubi: hearing the word "vicegerent" they understood that some of Adam's kind would corrupt, and they generalised. {cite:jami-ahkam-quran-alam-kutub-bukhari p. 274}
- **Sunni view: vicegerency means one generation after another.** Attributed to al-Hasan al-Basri; here the corruption comes from the vicegerents themselves. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF p. 455}
- **Academic note.** The editor's notes in Shakir's al-Tabari observe that al-Tabari sometimes relates weak reports to illustrate the sense of a word, as one cites poetry, not to establish doctrine. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 PDF pp. 457-458}
:::

## What the sources do not say
The sources give no duration of the jinn's residence before Adam except Mujahid's two thousand years from Abd Allah ibn Amr, for which I found no evaluation in al-Tabari or Ibn Kathir. The texts I read name no cities or remains from that age. Nor do they establish that these reports mean a human people before Adam; that is another question, treated in [[concept/adam-before-adam-in-later-readings|later readings of earlier Adams]].
