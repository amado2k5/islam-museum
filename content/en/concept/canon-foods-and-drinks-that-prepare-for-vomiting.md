## Summary
Ibn Sina lists the foods and drinks that prepare for vomiting, and advises that those about to vomit be present, exercise and tire themselves and then vomit at midday, with the eyes covered and the belly bound moderately {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## The preparing things
The things that prepare for vomiting are rocket, radish, citron, soft mountain pennyroyal, onion, leek, barley water with its dregs and honey, a broth of broad beans made sweet, sweet wine, almonds with honey, and similar things such as unleavened bread made in fat, melon and cucumber and their seeds, or some of their roots soaked in water and pounded with sweetness, and radish broth {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## Intoxicating drink
One who drinks an intoxicating drink in order to vomit and does not vomit on a little should drink much. Barley beer, drunk with honey after the bath, makes one vomit and purges. One who wishes to vomit should not make use of hard chewing close to it {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.

## Time and posture
When the emetic is taken, they should be present, exercise and tire themselves, and then vomit, at midday. At the time of vomiting he should cover his eyes with a pad, then bind his belly with a soft bandage, moderately tight {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print-v1 p.286}.
