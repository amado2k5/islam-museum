## Overview
A book by Imam Mahmud Shaltut, former shaykh of al-Azhar, on Islam as creed and shari'a: an introduction to Islam's nature, the basic doctrines, the way doctrine is established, and worship and transactions; introduced and annotated by Dr Muhammad 'Imara in the Islamic Research Academy's edition (1439/2018), 204 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
