## Overview
The fourth volume of Muhammad Rashid Rida's Tafsir al-Manar, from the exegesis of Al 'Imran 93 to al-Nisa' 23, edited by Ibrahim Shams al-Din; it includes the discourses on Abraham's creed, the first of houses, the sanctuary's sanctity and the hajj's institution, with the author's marginalia on the Hijaz's conditions in his time.
