## Overview
Al-Aqida al-Tahawiyya: Bayan Aqidat Ahl al-Sunna wa al-Jama'a by al-Tahawi, Dar Ibn Hazm, Beirut, 1st edition 1416 AH / 1995 CE. It contains the text of the creed of Abu Ja'far al-Tahawi (d. 321 AH), with a short biography of the author, numbered clauses from 1 to 134 and short linguistic notes. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
