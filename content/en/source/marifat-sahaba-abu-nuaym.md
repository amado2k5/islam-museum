## Overview
Ma'rifat al-Sahaba by Abu Nu'aym, a dictionary of the Companions by Abu Nu'aym al-Asbahani (d. 430 AH), arranged alphabetically, with chains of narration and hadiths. It is taken from a local photographic copy; the page numbers given are the local file's (scanner's), not the printed edition's. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
