## Overview
Futuh al-Buldan by al-Baladhuri, Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir (d. around 279 AH), on the conquests. Mu'assasat al-Ma'arif edition, Beirut, dated 1 Dhu al-Hijja 1407 AH, corresponding to 26 July 1987 CE. The editors are not named on the scanned pages. One volume, followed by a detailed index of persons and places. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
