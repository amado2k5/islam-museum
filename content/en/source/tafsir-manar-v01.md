## Overview
The first volume of Muhammad Rashid Rida's Tafsir al-Manar: the editor Ibrahim Shams al-Din's presentation, then the tafsir's introduction drawn from Muhammad 'Abduh's lectures in paraphrase with the author's expansion, then the beginning of the Fatiha's exegesis.
