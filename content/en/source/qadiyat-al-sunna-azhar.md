## Overview
A treatise by Shaykh Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision. It answers those who hold the Sunna non-binding, arguing from the verses of obedience, the Prophet's role in expounding the legislation, the rulings the Sunna alone supplies for prayer, zakat and hajj, and the precedent of Uhud; 56 pages, including a foreword by the shaykh of al-Azhar and an introduction by the secretary-general.
