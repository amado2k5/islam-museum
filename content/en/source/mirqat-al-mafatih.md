## Overview
Mirqat al-Mafatih by Ali al-Qari, Dar al-Fikr, Beirut, 1422 AH. A commentary by Ali al-Qari on Mishkat al-Masabih. Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it in the chapter on soothsaying. The list of references gives a volume 2 under al-Mubarakpuri's own name (Lucknow, 1378 AH). Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
