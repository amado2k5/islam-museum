## Overview
Fiqh al-Sunna by Sabiq, Dar al-Fikr for Printing, Publishing and Distribution. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. A work of Islamic jurisprudence that sets out the matters of worship and dealings together with their evidence from the Qur'an and the Sunna, by al-Sayyid Sabiq, in five volumes, Dar al-Fikr edition.
