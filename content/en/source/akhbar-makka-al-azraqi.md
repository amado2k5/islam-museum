## Overview
Akhbar Makka by al-Azraqi, printed by al-Majidi Press, Mecca. Akhbar Makka and the monuments recorded in it, by Abu al-Walid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah al-Azraqi. Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it in his history of the idols and the Kaaba. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
