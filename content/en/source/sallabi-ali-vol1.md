## Overview
The first part of al-Sallabi's study of 'Ali b. Abi Talib's life from birth to martyrdom — his lineage, family, conversion and deeds in Mecca and Medina under the first Rashidun caliphate. The author is of the Sunni scholars, writing from their method and declaring so in his preface; the work includes rebuttals of reports he deems weak or forged and a chapter critiquing what he calls Rafidi deviation. The museum cites it as a declared school sira.
