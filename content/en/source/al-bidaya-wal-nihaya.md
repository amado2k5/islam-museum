## Overview
Al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya by Isma'il ibn Kathir, Matba'at al-Sa'ada, Egypt, 1932 CE. The well-known history of Ibn Kathir of Damascus. One of the sources of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
