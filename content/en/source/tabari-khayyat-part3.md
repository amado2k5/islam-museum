## Overview
Tarikh al-Rusul wa al-Muluk by al-Tabari, Khayyat Library, Bayn Street, Beirut. The third part of al-Tabari's history in the Khayyat Library edition in Beirut. It is a scanned copy from the Bibliotheca Alexandrina (AAlexandrina-223581), covering the reign of al-Mu'tasim, al-Mazyar and al-Afshin. References here use the scan's page number, not the printed numbering, because the printed numbering cannot be read reliably by OCR. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
