## Overview
Muhadarat fi Tarikh al-Umam al-Islamiyya (The Umayyad State) by al-Khudari, Dar al-Arqam bin Abi al-Arqam, Beirut. A collection of lectures given by Muhammad Bey al-Khudari to students of the Egyptian University on the history of Islamic peoples, gathered in a single book and edited by Shaykh Muhammad al-Uthmani. The part on the Umayyad state covers the period from Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan to Marwan ibn Muhammad. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
