## Overview
Al-Muwatta' of the imam of the city of migration, Malik ibn Anas, in the narration of Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi, Dar al-Gharb al-Islami, Beirut, 2nd edition, 1417 AH / 1997 CE, edited by Dr. Bashshar Awwad Ma'ruf. Volume one of a digital PDF copy (Noor-Book). The classical text of the source is in the public domain; the editor's work and notes remain protected, so only the page numbers and references are taken from it. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
