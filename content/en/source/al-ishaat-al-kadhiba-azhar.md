## Overview
A book by Dr Muhammad Sayyid Tantawi, shaykh of al-Azhar, on false rumors in the history of the prophets and nations and their effect, and on the means Islam used to combat them — first of all the verification of reports; printed by Dar al-Shuruq, Cairo (1421/2001 as printed on the publication page). Cited page numbers are the PDF file's, two more than the printed pages.
