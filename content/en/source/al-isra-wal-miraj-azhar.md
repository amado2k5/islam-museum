## Overview
A book by Shaykh Muhammad Mutawalli al-Sha'rawi in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, on the Isra' and Mi'raj: the verse of the Isra', the miracle of the night journey and ascent, the difference between ru'ya and ir'a, what the Prophet saw in the Mi'raj, and the obligation of prayer; 114 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision. Its content is reflections delivered as questions and answers.
