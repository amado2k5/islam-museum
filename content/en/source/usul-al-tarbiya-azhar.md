## Overview
A book by Shaykh Ahmad Muhammad al-Barri, an Azhar scholar, on eight foundations of education and teaching drawn from the Qur'an and the Prophetic Sunna, 224 pages; published by the Islamic Research Academy in 1447/2025 with a foreword by its secretary-general, resting on the author's manuscript at the Faculty of Arabic Language.
