## Overview
Al-Dawla al-Umawiyya min al-Milad ila al-Suqut by Qabbani, Dar Wahy al-Qalam. A short booklet in the series of concise treatises that presents the Umayyad caliphs, their events and conquests, and the causes of their fall. It includes a foreword by Dr. Adnan al-Attar. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
