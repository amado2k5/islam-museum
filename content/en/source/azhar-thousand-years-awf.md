## Overview
A book by Dr Ahmad Muhammad 'Awf in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, on the Azhar mosque's history across a thousand years: its leadership, what was said of it, and the age of its building; 158 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
