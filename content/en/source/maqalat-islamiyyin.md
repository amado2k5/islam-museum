## Overview
Maqalat al-Islamiyyin wa Ikhtilaf al-Musallin, part one, by al-Ash'ari, al-Maktaba al-Asriyya, Sidon and Beirut, 1411 AH / 1990 CE. Part one of Maqalat al-Islamiyyin by Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari, edited by Muhammad Muhyi al-Din Abd al-Hamid, with the account of the statements of the people of hadith and the Sunna at its end. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
