## Overview
Tafhim al-Qur'an by al-Sayyid Abu al-A'la al-Mawdudi, Markazi Maktaba Jama'at-e-Islami, India.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for the history of Yemen and the Abyssinians, and for the story of Dhu Nuwas and the People of the Trench {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
