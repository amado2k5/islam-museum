## Overview
Ikhbar al-Kiram bi-Akhbar al-Masjid al-Haram by Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Asadi al-Makki, al-Matba'a al-Salafiyya at Benares, 1396 AH.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for the rank of Dar al-Nadwa over Quraysh {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
