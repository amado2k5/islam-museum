## Overview
Khulasat al-Siyar by Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Abd Allah al-Tabari, Delhi, India, 1343 AH. A summary of the biography of the Messenger of God by al-Tabari (d. 674 AH). One of the sources of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
