## Overview
Tarikh al-Umam wal-Muluk (al-Tabari's History) by Ibn Jarir al-Tabari, al-Matba'a al-Husayniyya al-Misriyya.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for the prophetic lineage's stopping at Adnan {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
