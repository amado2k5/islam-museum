## Overview
Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' by al-Dhahabi, Institute of Arabic Manuscripts in association with Dar al-Ma'arif, Egypt (edited by Ibrahim al-Abyari). Volume two of Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' by al-Dhahabi (d. 748 AH), edited by Ibrahim al-Abyari. It contains the biographies of Companions and women Companions at the start of the volume. Taken from a photographic copy in the local repository; the numbers refer to the printed pages. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
