## Overview
Al-Aqida al-Wasitiyya followed by al-Munazara fi al-Aqida al-Wasitiyya by Ibn Taymiyya, al-Matba'a al-Salafiyya, Cairo. It contains the text of the Wasitiyya creed of Taqi al-Din Ibn Taymiyya, preceded by a note from the publisher and followed by the record of the debate held in Damascus in 705 AH. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
