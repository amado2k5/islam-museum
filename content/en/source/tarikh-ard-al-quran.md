## Overview
Tarikh Ard al-Qur'an by Sayyid Sulayman al-Nadwi, Ma'arif Press, Azamgarh, India, fourth edition 1955 CE.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for Yemen's history, al-Hira's kings, Sabianism and Zoroastrianism, and for the preference tracing the Ghassanids, Aws and Khazraj to Nabat's house {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
