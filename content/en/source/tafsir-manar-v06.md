## Overview
The sixth volume of Muhammad Rashid Rida's Tafsir al-Manar, from the comment on al-Nisa' 148 to the opening of al-Ma'ida. Its pages treat aloud speech of evil and the wronged, the distinction between God and His messengers, the People of the Book's demand for a book descending from heaven, the covenant with the Children of Israel and their Sabbath affairs, then al-Ma'ida's connection to al-Nisa' and its opening on fulfilling contracts.
