## Overview
A book by Shaykh Muhammad al-Khidr Husayn, former shaykh of al-Azhar, in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series. It treats freedom in Islam: its lexical and technical sense, the two bases of consultation and equality, then freedom in property, honor, life and religion and its effect on language and literature; 108 pages, under the supervision of the Academy's secretary-general Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad.
