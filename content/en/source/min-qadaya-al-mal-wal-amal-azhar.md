## Overview
A treatise by Shaykh Abu'l-Wafa' Mustafa al-Maraghi — senior Azhar scholar and former assistant secretary of the Islamic Research Academy — in the Academy's scholarly series, supervised by secretary-general Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad with an introduction by Muhammad 'Abd al-Rahman Bisar. It examines work, its kinds and what is forbidden in it, women and work, the worker's relation to the employer, and wealth and its functions: spending, zakat, taxation and expenditure on the public good; appended are the resolutions of the Academy's second conference (1965).
