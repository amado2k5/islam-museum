## Overview
Tarikh al-Khulafa' by al-Suyuti (d. 911 AH), a biography of the caliphs from al-Siddiq to his own time, arranged chronologically with the events of their days. Second corrected and revised edition, Dar al-Minhaj, Jeddah, in cooperation with Dar al-Awqaf for Islamic Publishing, under the supervision of the Ministry of Islamic Affairs, funded by the General Endowments Authority of the State of Qatar, 1434 AH / 2013 CE, supervised by Muhammad Uthman Nusuh al-Husayni. Six manuscripts were used, two of them in the hand of the author's pupils. One volume of 864 pages. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
