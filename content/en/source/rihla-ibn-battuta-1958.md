## Overview
Tuhfat al-Nuzzar fi Gharaib al-Amsar wa Aja'ib al-Asfar by Ibn Battuta, al-Maktaba al-Tijariyya al-Kubra, Cairo, 1958 CE (1377 AH). The approved Arabic edition of Ibn Battuta's travels, reviewed by a committee of men of letters. The pages given are the printed numbering of the book. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
