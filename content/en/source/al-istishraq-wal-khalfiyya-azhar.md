## Overview
Dr Mahmud Hamdi Zaqzuq's book in the Scientific Series of al-Azhar's Islamic Research Academy — on the origins and development of Orientalism, the orientalists' works, aims, categories and methods, and the Muslim stance toward them; 212 pages per the cataloguing data, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision. The author critiques a number of orientalists and ascribes his judgments to himself.
