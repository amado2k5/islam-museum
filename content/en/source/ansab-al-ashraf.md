## Overview
Ansab al-Ashraf by Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir al-Baladhuri (d. 279 AH), an encyclopaedia of lineage and reports on Quraysh and the Arab tribes. Digitised scans from the local al-Hasad library, read by automated OCR (Apple Vision). Primary source, book, in Arabic. It mentions, among the Prophet's grandmothers, the Fawatim and the Awatik: Fatima bint Amr al-Makhzumiyya, mother of Abd Allah; Fatima bint Sa'd al-Azdiyya, mother of Qusayy; and Atika bint Murra al-Sulamiyya, mother of Hashim {cite:ansab-al-ashraf p. 533-534}
