## Description
A chapter by Syed Ali Nadeem Rezavi in A Shared Heritage: The Growth of Civilizations in India and Iran, edited by Irfan Habib (2002). It traces Iranian and Timurid borrowings in Indo-Muslim architecture, covering forms, plans and decoration, and how they were adapted in India.
