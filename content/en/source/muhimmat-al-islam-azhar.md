## Overview
Muhammad Farid Wajdi's book (d. 1373/1954) on the mission of the Islamic religion in the world, in the Scientific Series of al-Azhar's Islamic Research Academy under the Academy secretary-general's supervision; with an introduction by Dr Muhammad Rajab al-Bayumi and three sections: the mission of Islam in the world, the declaration of general fraternity among peoples, and the ideal in nation-building.
