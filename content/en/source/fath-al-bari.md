## Overview
Fath al-Bari, the commentary on Sahih al-Bukhari by Ibn Hajar (773–852 AH), al-Matba'a al-Salafiyya wa Maktabatuha, al-Rawda, Cairo. One of the sources of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
