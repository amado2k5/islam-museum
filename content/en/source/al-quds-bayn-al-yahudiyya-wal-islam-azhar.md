## Overview
A book by Dr Muhammad 'Imara in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, 188 pages: a study and presentation answering a Jewish Defense League document on Jerusalem's Islamic character, including in its appendix the report of the International Wailing Wall Commission formed by the League of Nations in 1930; under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
