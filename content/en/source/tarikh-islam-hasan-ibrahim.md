## Overview
Tarikh al-Islam al-Siyasi wa al-Dini wa al-Thaqafi wa al-Ijtima'i by Hasan, Dr. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. Dr. Hasan Ibrahim Hasan's encyclopaedia, in four volumes, is one of the best-known modern university references on Islamic history.
