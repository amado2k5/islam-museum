## Overview
A book by Dr Muhammad al-Bahi (d. 1982), member of the senior scholars' body, in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series at al-Azhar al-Sharif, on Islam's orientation toward man, wealth, property, inheritance and individual liberty; 152 pages, introduced by Mahmud Hamdi Zaqzuq under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
