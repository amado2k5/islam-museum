## Overview
Al-Itqan fi Ulum al-Qur'an by Jalal al-Din Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr al-Suyuti (d. 911 AH); a primary source — a book — in Arabic. It is the author's encyclopedia of the Qur'anic sciences, arranged as an ordered sequence of "types" running past one hundred in the scanned text: from the modes of revelation, isnad and recitation, the rare and arabized vocabulary, through abrogation, conciseness and prolixity, tawriyya, iltifat, rhymed endings, to the etiquette of the reader, teacher and exegete, the knowledge of commentators, the writing of the Qur'an, the naming and ordering of suras and verses, the ambiguous expressions and the names of those about whom the Qur'an was revealed.

## Reliance on it
This source is cited in 4 museum records, among them the concepts of aya, sura, juz' and qira'at in Qur'anic-sciences terminology.
