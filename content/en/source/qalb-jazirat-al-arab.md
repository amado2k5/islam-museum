## Overview
Qalb Jazirat al-Arab by Fuad Hamza, al-Matba'a al-Salafiyya and its library at al-Rawda, Egypt, 1352 AH.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for the charge of the House after Isma'il's sons, the duration of Jurhum and Khuza'a, and the year of Qusayy's takeover of Mecca {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
