## Overview
Tarikh al-Rusul wa al-Muluk by al-Tabari, Dar al-Ma'arif, Egypt (Dhakha'ir al-Arab series), 2nd edition, edited by Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, in eleven volumes. It is the edition cited in this entry. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
