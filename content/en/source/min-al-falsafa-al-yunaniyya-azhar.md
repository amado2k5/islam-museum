## Overview
A book by Dr Sulayman Sulayman Khamis, professor of philosophy at al-Azhar's Faculty of Usul al-Din, on the history of philosophy from the ancient East and Greece to Islamic philosophy and the thought of Ibn Sina, al-Ghazali and Ibn Rushd, then modern philosophy in Bacon and Descartes; first edition printed by the Islamic Research Academy in Cairo, 1447/2025, 383 pages, with a foreword by the Academy secretary-general.
