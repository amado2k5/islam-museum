## Overview
A volume in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series (year 49, 1439/2018), 170 pages, recording the Grand Imam Ahmad al-Tayyib's visit to Indonesia in February 2016, with a country profile, Egypt's relations with it, al-Azhar's activities there and the visit's press coverage; prepared and introduced by Mahmud Hamdi Zaqzuq under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
