## Overview
A book by Shaykh 'Ali al-Khafif, former member of the Islamic Research Academy, in the Academy's scholarly series. It clarifies the Sunna's meaning among muhaddithun and usulis, its place in expounding the Qur'an's rulings and the obligation to act by it, then answers what was raised about its transmission and authority: its writing, transmission by meaning, and the solitary report; 86 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
