## Overview
Kashf al-Zunun 'an Asami al-Kutub wa al-Funun by Hajji Khalifa, Mustafa ibn Abd Allah, known as Katip Celebi (d. 1067 AH), edited by Ekmeleddin Ihsanoglu and Bashshar Awwad Ma'ruf (Furqan Foundation for Islamic Heritage, 2004). The first volume is a digitised scan from the local al-Hasad library, read by automated OCR (Apple Vision). It contains the author's well-known introduction and the letter alif and the letters ta and tha (about two thousand entries). Primary source, book, in Arabic.
