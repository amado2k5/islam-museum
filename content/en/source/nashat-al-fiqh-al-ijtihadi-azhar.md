## Overview
A book by Shaykh Muhammad 'Ali al-Sayis (d. 1396/1976), former dean of the Faculty of Shari'a, in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series. It traces juristic ijtihad from the Prophetic age through the caliphs', Umayyads' and 'Abbasids' eras, then presents the bases of the four madhhabs and the scholars' work after the age of ijtihad; 116 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
