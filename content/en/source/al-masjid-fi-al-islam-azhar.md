## Overview
A book by Dr Muhammad Rajab al-Bayyumi in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, on the mosques' role in education and scholarly culture, with chapters on the Prophet's Mosque, the Sacred Mosque and 'Abd Allah b. 'Umar; 166 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
