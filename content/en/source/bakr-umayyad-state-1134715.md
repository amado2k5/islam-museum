## Overview
Al-Dawla al-Umawiyya by Bakr, Markaz al-Raya lil-Nashr wa al-I'lam. A book on the history of the Umayyad state after the Rightly Guided caliphs. It begins with a brief account of the Rightly Guided caliphate, then narrates the lives of the Umayyad rulers from Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan to Marwan ibn Muhammad, in the manner of annals, with biographies of notable figures and records of the years. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
