## Overview
A collective volume in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series at al-Azhar al-Sharif, gathering fifteen studies by Azhar scholars — among them Mahmud Shaltut, Muhammad 'Abd Allah Daraz, Mahmud Hamdi Zaqzuq, Ahmad 'Umar Hashim and Muhammad Shawqi al-Fanjari — on reason, knowledge, peace, unity, homeland, poverty and brotherhood in Islam; 210 pages, under the Academy secretary-general Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad.
