## Overview
The Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra of Taj al-Din 'Abd al-Wahhab b. 'Ali al-Subki (d. 771 AH); used from scanned copies in the local repository of the Husayniyya Egyptian Press edition, with numbers referring to the printed pages. The volumes available locally are 1, 2, 3, 10 and 11.
