## Overview
A book by Muhammad al-Sadiq 'Arjun, former dean of al-Azhar's Faculty of Usul al-Din, in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series (book twelve of year fifty-seven), on God's ways in human society as drawn from the Qur'an; 80 pages, with a foreword by the Academy's secretary-general.
