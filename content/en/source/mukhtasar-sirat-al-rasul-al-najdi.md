## Overview
Mukhtasar Sirat al-Rasul by Abd Allah ibn Muhammad al-Najdi Al al-Shaykh (d. 1342 AH), al-Matba'a al-Salafiyya and its Maktaba al-Rawda, Egypt, 1379 AH. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. One of the sources of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum.
