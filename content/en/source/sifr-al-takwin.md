## Overview
The Book of Genesis, the first book of the Torah; it appears in al-Raheeq al-Makhtum's list of references.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for Isma'il's seniority in age over Isaac, and for the length of Isma'il's life {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
