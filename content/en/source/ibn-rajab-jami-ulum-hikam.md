## Overview
Jami' al-Ulum wa al-Hikam fi Sharh Khamsin Hadithan min Jawami' al-Kalim by al-Hanbali, the commentary of Imam Ibn Rajab (d. 795 AH) on fifty hadiths from Jawami' al-Kalim. Dar Ibn Kathir, Damascus–Beirut, 1st edition 1429 AH / 2008 CE, annotated and edited by Dr. Mahir Yasin al-Fahl, one volume of 956 pages. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
