## Overview
The eighth volume of Muhammad Rashid Rida's Tafsir al-Manar, from the comment on al-An'am 111 to that sura's end, with the opening of al-A'raf. It treats the satans of men and jinn, the ways of God in the prophets' enmities, the Qur'an as judgement and miracle, the completion of God's word, and the earlier scriptures' alteration — then al-A'raf's opening and the letters at the suras' heads.
