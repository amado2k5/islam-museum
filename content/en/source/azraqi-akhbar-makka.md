## Overview
Akhbar Makka wa ma Ja'a fiha min al-Athar by al-Azraqi (d. after 250 AH), one of the oldest books on the reports and monuments of Mecca. The page numbers given are the scanner's page numbers for this copy. Notes attributed to the editor may be contemporary with the editor rather than the author. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
