## Overview
Sifat Jazirat al-Arab by al-Hasan ibn Ahmad ibn Ya'qub al-Hamdani (d. 334 AH), edited by David Heinrich Muller (Leiden, 1884 CE). The most complete surviving geography of the Arabian Peninsula before and after Islam. Digitised scan from the local al-Hasad library, read by automated OCR (Apple Vision). Primary source, book, in Arabic.
