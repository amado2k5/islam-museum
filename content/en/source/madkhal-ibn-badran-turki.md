## Overview
Al-Madkhal ila Madhhab al-Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal by Ibn Badran, Mu'assasat al-Risala. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. Abd al-Qadir ibn Badran al-Dimashqi (d. 1346 AH) on the Hanbali school, its principles, scholars and books, corrected by Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Muhsin al-Turki, 2nd edition, Mu'assasat al-Risala, Beirut, 1401 AH / 1981 CE.
