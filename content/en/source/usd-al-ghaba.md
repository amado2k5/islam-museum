## Overview
Usd al-Ghaba fi Ma'rifat al-Sahaba by Ibn al-Athir, Dar Ibn Hazm, Beirut, 1st edition 1433 AH / 2012 CE. Primary source, book, in Arabic. A biographical dictionary of the Companions by Izz al-Din Ibn al-Athir (d. 630 AH), arranged alphabetically, gathering earlier material with chains and criticism. The edition used here is Dar Ibn Hazm, Beirut, 1st edition 1433 AH / 2012 CE, from a photographic copy in the local repository; page numbers are those of the printed book.
