## Overview
Lum'at al-I'tiqad al-Hadi ila Sabil al-Rashad by Ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi, Dar Rakaiz, Riyadh (distributed by Dar Atlas al-Khadra). Primary source, book, in Arabic. The study and critical edition by Dr. Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Farhan ibn Uraiyir, with its introduction on the author and a description of the manuscripts, 2nd edition (Riyadh, 1443 AH / 2022 CE). It is used here for the introduction, the biography and the types of creedal writings, and the text itself is not quoted from it.
