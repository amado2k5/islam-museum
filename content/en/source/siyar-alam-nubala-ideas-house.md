## Overview
Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' by al-Dhahabi, Dar al-Afkar al-Dawliyya, Lebanon. Primary source, book, in Arabic. The collection of Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' by al-Dhahabi (d. 748 AH) in a single photographic copy from the local repository, about 4683 pages; the printed numbers on the pages match the file's page numbers, and references point to these pages. The biography of Malik ibn Anas (number 4664) is on pages 3145–3152.
