## Overview
A book by Dr Muhammad Ibrahim al-Fayyumi, member of the Islamic Research Academy, in the Academy's scholarly series. It sets out the Sunna's rank in legislation, its definition among muhaddithun and usulis, the history of its writing, the motives for its fabrication, the rules of isnad and of jarh and ta'dil, and the grades of hadith classification down to the sahih; 56 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
