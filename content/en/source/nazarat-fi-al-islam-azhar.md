## Overview
A book by Dr Muhammad 'Abd Allah Daraz, member of the senior scholars' body, in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series (year 49, 1439/2018), 176 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision. It comprises an introduction to Islam's message and chapters on doctrine, worship, social life, conduct, peace and international law.
