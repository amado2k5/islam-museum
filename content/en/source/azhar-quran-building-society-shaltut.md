## Overview
A book by Imam Mahmud Shaltut, former shaykh of al-Azhar, in three chapters: knowledge's standing in Islam, the Islamic acts of worship, and the Muhammadan teachings and their connection to the cosmos; printed by the Islamic Research Academy in its scholarly series (1439/2018), 216 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
