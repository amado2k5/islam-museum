## Overview
Muhadarat Tarikh al-Umam al-Islamiyya by Shaykh Muhammad al-Khudari Bek, al-Maktaba al-Tijariyya al-Kubra in Egypt, eighth edition 1382 AH.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri's most-used reference in the section on the Arabs before Islam: the Qahtani and Adnani tribal migrations, and the kings of al-Hira and Syria {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
