## Overview
Rahma lil-'Alamin by Judge Muhammad Sulayman Salman al-Mansur Puri, Deoband, India.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it for Hagar's story and that she was free, not a slave; for Ma'add's journey to Syria; and for the generations between Adnan and Abraham {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
