## Overview
A book by Muhammad Farid Wajdi (d. 1373/1954), in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, analyzing the principles of the Islamic religion: religion and revelation, Islam and the authority of reason and science, and Islam's shari'a; 152 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
