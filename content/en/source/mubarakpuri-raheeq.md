## Overview
Al-Raheeq al-Makhtum: Bahth fi al-Sira al-Nabawiyya by al-Mubarakpuri, Muslim World League (Prophetic Biography Competition). Secondary source, book, in Arabic. Winner of the first prize in the Muslim World League's biography competition (1399 AH / 1979 CE). It is the most widely read modern biography, relying on classical sources with notes on almost every page.
