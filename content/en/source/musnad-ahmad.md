## Overview
Musnad of Imam Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Hanbal al-Shaybani (d. 241 AH). The catalogue of this edition records the date as 164 AH, which is a mistaken reading in the copy; Imam Ahmad died in 241 AH. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
