## Overview
The third part of Dar al-Kutub al-'Ilmiyya's edition of the Qanun fi'l-Tibb, with Muhammad Amin al-Dannawi's marginalia; it contains Book Four (scans 3–393) and the beginning of Book Five on the compounding of drugs (from scan 395), the text breaking off at scan 590 before the book's end.
