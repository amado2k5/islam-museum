## Overview
A scanned, OCR-processed copy of al-Tha'labi's book on the stories of the prophets; the title page names the fourth edition, and its publisher, place and date have not yet been verified. Citations in the entries use the scan-marker page numbers (p.N), not the printed pages, since the offset between them is not constant.
