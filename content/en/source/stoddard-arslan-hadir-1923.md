## Overview
Hadir al-Alam al-Islami by Stoddard and Arslan, Isa al-Babi al-Halabi and Partners Press, Cairo. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. An Arabic translation of Lothrop Stoddard's 1921 book on the Muslim world after the First World War, with extensive commentary by Shakib Arslan.
