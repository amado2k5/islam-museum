## Overview
Al-Isaba fi Tamyiz al-Sahaba by al-Asqalani, al-Maktaba al-Asriyya, Sidon–Beirut, 1st edition 1433 AH / 2012 CE. A biographical dictionary of the Companions, men and women, by Ibn Hajar (d. 852 AH), arranged alphabetically in sections for men, women and kunyas. It gathers what is in al-Isti'ab and Usd al-Ghaba and others, with the chains of the reports and a critique of the companionship of those who are disputed. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
