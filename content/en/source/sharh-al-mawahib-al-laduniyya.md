## Overview
Sharh al-Mawahib al-Laduniyya by al-Zurqani, an old copy lacking its opening pages. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. Al-Zurqani's commentary on al-Qastallani's al-Mawahib al-Laduniyya. One of the sources of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum.
