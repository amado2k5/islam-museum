## Overview
Ahsan al-Taqasim fi Ma'rifat al-Aqalim by Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Muqaddasi al-Bashshari (d. around 380 AH). It is among the greatest regional geographies, describing routes, cities, customs and crafts. The copy used is a digitised scan from the local al-Hasad library, read by automated OCR (Apple Vision). Primary source, book, in Arabic.
