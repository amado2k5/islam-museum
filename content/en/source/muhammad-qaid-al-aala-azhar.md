## Overview
A book by Dr 'Abd al-Majid Ibrahim Hindi (d. 1981), issued by the Islamic Research Academy at al-Azhar al-Sharif, first edition 1447/2025, 198 pages. It reads the Prophet's political and military leadership across twelve stations of the sira, from Badr to Tabuk, joining historical analysis with a maqasidi reading; foreword by the Academy's secretary-general Muhammad 'Abd al-Da'im al-Jindi.
