## Overview
A collection of articles and addresses by Dr Muhyi al-Din 'Afifi Ahmad, secretary-general of the Islamic Research Academy, in the Academy's scholarly series (1439/2018), 252 pages, on contemporary human questions: peace and dialogue, waqf and scientific research, equality, monopoly, environmental protection, the culture of difference, coexistence, violence and extremism, the Rohingya, and Jerusalem.
