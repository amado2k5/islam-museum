## Overview
A book by Dr 'Abbas Shuman, deputy of al-Azhar al-Sharif, in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series (year 49, 1439/2018), 544 pages. It collects newspaper articles he published on renewal in Islamic thought: theoretical entries on its nature and controls, then questions of worship, family, transactions, non-Muslim rights and extremism, and the fiqh of governance. Cited page numbers are the PDF file's; the printed page is one less.
