## Overview
Tabaqat al-Fuqaha' by al-Shirazi, Dar al-Ra'id al-Arabi, Beirut. The biographical classes of Shafi'i jurists by Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi (d. 476 AH), edited by Dr. Ihsan Abbas, Dar al-Ra'id al-Arabi, Beirut, from a local photographic copy (the title page and the editor's introduction include the author's biography). The file's page numbers (PDF) are used, not the printed numbers; one page of the file may be a double spread carrying two printed numbers. Primary source, book, in Arabic.
