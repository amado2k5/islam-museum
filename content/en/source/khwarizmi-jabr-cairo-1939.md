## Overview
The Cairo 1939 edition of al-Khwarizmi's book on algebra (al-jabr wa'l-muqabala), presented by 'Ali Mustafa Musharrafa and Muhammad Mursi Ahmad of the Egyptian University's Faculty of Science; the copy is a Columbia University scan read by OCR, and only the title and introduction pages were read.
