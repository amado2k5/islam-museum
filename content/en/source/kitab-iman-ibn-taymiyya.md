## Overview
Kitab al-Iman by Shaykh al-Islam Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Taymiyya; a primary source — a book — in Arabic. It is his major treatise on the reality of faith and the theologians' dispute over it. The harvested copy is the Maktab al-Islami fifth edition, Beirut–Damascus–Amman, 1416 AH / 1996 CE, with hadith documentation by Shaykh Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani. In this edition the publisher completed the verses Ibn Taymiyya cited only by their openings, and added to al-Albani's notes bracketed references to the hadith discussions in his other works.

## Reliance on it
This source is cited in one museum record, on the concept of the believer (mu'min).
