## Overview
Sharh al-Tahawiyya fi al-Aqida al-Salafiyya by Ibn Abi al-Izz al-Hanafi (731 to 792 AH), Dar al-Hadith, Cairo (1421 AH / 2000 CE). Primary source, book, in Arabic. Ibn Abi al-Izz al-Hanafi's commentary (731–792 AH) on al-Tahawi's creed, in the Dar al-Hadith edition, Cairo, 1421 AH (2000 CE). In it he reports the views of the people on each question and discusses them.
