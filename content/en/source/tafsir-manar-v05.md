## Overview
The fifth volume of Muhammad Rashid Rida's Tafsir al-Manar, opening with the exegesis of al-Nisa' 24; it contains the discourses on the married women, what the right hands possess, and captives in religious war, edited by Ibrahim Shams al-Din.
