## Overview
Al-Dawla al-Umawiyya: 'Awamil al-Izdihar wa Tada'iyat al-Infisal by al-Salabi, Dar Ibn Kathir. Secondary source, book, in Arabic. Volume two of al-Salabi's book on the Umayyad state, 1st edition 1426 AH / 2005 CE, Dar Ibn Kathir, Damascus and Beirut. Volume two covers the conquests, the financial and administrative system, the caliphate of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, and the caliphates of Yazid and Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik.
