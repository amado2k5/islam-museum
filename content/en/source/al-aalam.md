## Overview
Al-A'lam by Khayr al-Din al-Zirikli, Cairo, second edition 1945 CE.

## Reliance on it
Al-Mubarakpuri relied on it in verifying the names of al-Hira's kings and other men of the Jahiliyya {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
