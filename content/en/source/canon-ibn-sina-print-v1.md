## Overview
The first part of Dar al-Kutub al-'Ilmiyya's edition of the Qanun fi'l-Tibb, with Muhammad Amin al-Dannawi's marginalia; it contains the author's preface and his division into five books (scans 9–10), the whole of Book One (scans 11–311), and the beginning of Book Two from scan 313 to scan 728.
