## Overview
Nisa' Hawl al-Rasul: Tarajim wa Qisas by al-Qutb, al-Da'uq and al-Dumi, al-Maktaba al-Asriyya, Sidon–Beirut (Sharikat Abna' Sharif al-Ansari for Printing, Publishing and Distribution), 1436 AH / 2015 CE. A collection of biographies and stories of the Prophet's mothers, wives and daughters, granddaughters and a number of Companions, compiled by Muhammad Ali al-Qutb, Muhammad Umar al-Da'uq and Ahmad Abd al-Jawad al-Dumi; 464 pages. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
