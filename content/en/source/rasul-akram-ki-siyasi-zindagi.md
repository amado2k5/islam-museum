## Overview
Rasul Akram ki Siyasi Zindagi by Dr. Muhammad Hamidullah, Paris Salim Company, India, 1963 CE. The political biography of the Prophet in Urdu, by Dr. Hamidullah. One of the sources of al-Raheeq al-Makhtum. Secondary source, book, in Arabic.
