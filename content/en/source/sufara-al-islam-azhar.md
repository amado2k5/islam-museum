## Overview
A book by Muhammad Farid Wajdi in the Islamic Research Academy's scholarly series, containing the Academy's foreword, memoirs of the author by Tahir al-Tanahi, and the epistle Wajdi wrote for a congress of religions alleged to convene at Tokyo in 1907 — affirming Islam as the shared religion of the prophets, not a new religion; 52 pages, under the Academy secretary-general's supervision.
