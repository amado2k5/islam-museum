## Overview
The Danishmendid state, a Turkish dynasty founded in Cappadocia. Its head, Jamshtakin ibn Danishmend, consolidated his power in Sivas, Caesarea and Malatya, and defeated the Franks at Malatya, according to Hasan Ibrahim Hasan. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

## In the Crusades
The author describes the role of its rulers in the Crusades as splendid. He names Ghazi ibn Jamshtakin in 499 AH, then Muhammad II, Dhu al-Nun and Yaghi Arslan ibn Ghazi. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

## Merging
The dynasty quickly merged into its great Seljuk neighbour, the Seljuks of Rum. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}
