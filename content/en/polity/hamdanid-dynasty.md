## Overview
The Hamdanid state, the state of the Banu Hamdan ibn Hamdun ibn al-Harith al-Taghlibi, of the Banu Taghlib ibn Wa'il, people of al-Jazira. It was founded by [[person/abu-l-hayja-ibn-hamdan|Abu al-Hayja ibn Hamdan]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Beginning and expansion
It began with leadership over Mosul, when al-Muktafi appointed Abu al-Hayja Abd Allah ibn Hamdan over it. It then extended to al-Jazira and Syria. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

It settled with its great emirs: Nasir al-Dawla over Mosul, and Sayf al-Dawla over Aleppo. Ibn Khaldun describes it as having been autocratic in the Abbasid call. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
