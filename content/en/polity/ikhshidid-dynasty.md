## Overview
The Ikhshidid state of the Banu Tughj, in Egypt and Syria, founded by [[person/muhammad-ibn-tughj-al-ikhshid|Muhammad ibn Tughj al-Ikhshid]]. He was succeeded by his two sons, and by their tutor Kafur al-Ikhshidi. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The end
It died out when Jawhar al-Siqilli entered Egypt in 358 AH (969 CE), and the call there turned to the Alawids. Jawhar laid out the city of Cairo. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
