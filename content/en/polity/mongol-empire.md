## Overview
Ibn Khaldun presents the Tatar state as peoples of the Turks from beyond the Khita who settled on the borders of China. Genghis Khan, king of the Mughal, rose against them and overcame the realms of Islam. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1529-1531}

## Impact
They wrested the seat of the caliphate in Baghdad, and then their state split into states: the kings of Transoxiana, Juqiyya, the House of Jochi in the steppe, and the Ilkhans of Iraq. They later embraced Islam. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1529-1531}

## Founder
In the data the state is attributed to [[person/genghis-khan|Genghis Khan]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar} The date of its founding, about 602 AH, is approximate and disputed.
