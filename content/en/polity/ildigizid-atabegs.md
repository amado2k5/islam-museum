## Overview
The Ildigizid atabegate of Adharbayjan, a state whose origin goes back to Shams al-Din Ildiguz, a mamluk from the lands of the Qipchaq who belonged to the Seljuk sultan Mas'ud in Iraq. His state was founded in 531 AH and spread through the mountains, Adharbayjan and Iraq Ajam. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.88-89}

## Succession
Muhammad Bahlawan Jahan ruled after him in 568 AH, then Qizil Arslan Uthman in 587 AH, then Abu Bakr. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.88-89}

## The end
Khwarazmshah Ala al-Din Muhammad removed the state in 622 AH, at the hands of Uzbek Bahlawan, the last of its emirs. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.88-89}
