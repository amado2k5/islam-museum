## Overview
The Begtiginid atabegate of Erbil, named after Bektekin, a mamluk of the Seljuk army who was made governor of Erbil. It lasted in al-Jazira until it came under the Ayyubids and then the Mongols, according to Hasan Ibrahim Hasan. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}

## Its most notable rulers
The most notable was Zayn al-Din Ali Kuchuk ibn Bektekin, governor of Mosul. Then his son al-Muzaffar Kukburi ibn Zayn al-Din, lord of Erbil and Harran. Kukburi leaned towards Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi and became one of his senior emirs. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim}
