## Overview
The Saffarid state, the state of the Banu al-Saffar, kings of Sistan. It arose from a volunteer group that gathered in the lands of Sistan to fight the Kharijite Shurat, when the affair of the state at Baghdad was unsettled after the killing of al-Mutawakkil. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1108-1112}

## Origins
The volunteers first gathered around Salih ibn Nasr al-Kinani. Then [[person/yaqub-ibn-layth|Ya'qub ibn al-Layth al-Saffar]] led them, and he is the founder of the state in Ibn Khaldun's account. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1108-1112}

The origins began around 861 CE, an approximate date in the data. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Expansion and end
Ya'qub ruled Herat, Kerman, Fars and Khurasan, and the caliphs submitted to him and gave him these lands. He died in 265 AH and was succeeded by his brother Amr. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1108-1112}

Isma'il the Samanid captured Amr at Balkh, and their great state died out. The data place this in 287 AH (about 900 CE); it is a derived year and needs documentation from this same source. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
