## Overview
The state of the sons of Jochi (Batu Khan), son of Genghis Khan, in the Qipchaq steppe, from Filaq to Bulghar; it is the Golden Horde. It was founded by [[person/jochi-ibn-genghis|Jochi]], according to Ibn Khaldun. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Berke
Jochi's sons were Nuqaya and Berke. Berke embraced Islam at the hands of a shaykh of the companions of Najm al-Din Kubra in Bukhara. He asked his brother Mengu-Timur for leave to seize the lands of the caliphate. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Sarai and Uzbek
Their state was in Sarai until Uzbek ruled. Islam appeared among them, and they intermarried with the kings of Egypt. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Encyclopaedia of Islam note: the name
Altin Orda is the modern Turkish imitation of the Russian term Zolotaya Orda, "Golden Horde". The article refers the reader to the Batu'ids. {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 446}
