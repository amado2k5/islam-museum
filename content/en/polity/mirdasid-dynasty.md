## Overview
The Mirdasid state of the Banu Salih ibn Mirdas, of the Banu Kilab ibn Amir ibn Sa'sa'a, in Aleppo and the lands between it and Ana. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1074-1075}

## Beginning
It was begun by [[person/salih-ibn-mirdas|Salih ibn Mirdas]] with the kingship of al-Rahba. Then he took Aleppo from the hands of Ibn Sha'ban al-Kutami in 424 AH. His rule then extended from Baalbek to Ana. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1074-1075}

## The caliphs
Salih died in 429 AH, and his sons Nasr (Shibl al-Dawla) and Thumal held power in turn, until their state died out with the rise of the Seljuk kings in Syria. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1074-1075}
