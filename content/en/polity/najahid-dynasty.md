## Overview
The Najahids of Zabid were clients of the Banu Ziyad. They took Zabid after the killing of al-Sulayhi in 473 AH, and their affair ended when Ali ibn Mahdi took it in 554 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1038-1039}

## The three sons
When al-Sulayhi took Zabid, Najah had three sons: Ma'arik, Sa'id and Jayyash. Sa'id and Jayyash went to the island of Dahlak to study the Qur'an and literature. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1038-1039}

## The killing of al-Sulayhi
Ali had al-Sulayhi ambushed at al-Lujum while he was on his way to Mecca, his army broke, and Jayyash himself killed him in 473 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1038-1039}

## Rule
They went to Zabid and took it. They gave the khutba for the Abbasids, while the Sulayhids gave it for the Fatimids. Ibn Khaldun describes Jayyash as just. He died at the start of the fifth century AH, and his son al-Fatik succeeded him. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1038-1039}

## The end
The line of the Najahids died out, and Ali ibn Mahdi al-Kharijī took Zabid in 554 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1038-1039}
