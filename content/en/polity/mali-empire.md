## Overview
The kingdom of Mali, the greatest of the Muslim kingdoms of the Western Sudan in Ibn Khaldun's account. It was founded by Mari Jata after he took power from the hands of Sosso. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1655-1657}

## Foundation
Mari Jata overcame Sosso and took all that they held of the old kingdom of the people of Ghana ([[polity/ghana-empire|the Ghana kingdom]]). The date is approximate, around 632 AH, and the conversion is derived. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1655-1657}

## Height
The kingdom reached its height in the eighth century AH, and its rule extended from the sources of the Niger to the ocean. Its capital was "Bilad Bani" (Niani), on the cultivated land and the buildings. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1655-1657}

## End
Ibn Khaldun did not record the end of the kingdom. The data speak of its collapse in the eleventh century AH before the people of Kukku (Gao), and describe the date of its end as a matter of dispute. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1655-1657}
