## Overview
A state in Southeast Asia — an archipelago of 17,508 islands per the travel-book, with some 238 million people, its capital Jakarta on Java {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 24}. Its motto is Unity in Diversity; its official language Bahasa {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 26}. Islam entered it, per the book, in the late second and early third century AH through Arab traders {cite:rihla-imam-akbar-indonesia-azhar p. 26}; a country of historic ties to the Muslim world.
