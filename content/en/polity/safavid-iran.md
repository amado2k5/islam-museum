## Overview
The Safavid state, an empire founded in 1501 CE and ended in 1736 CE, recorded in the data as the successor of the [[polity/timurid-empire|Timurid state]]. {cite:wikidata}

## Not covered here
The two dates come from Wikidata, and that is a single source; a second source is needed. The data contain no shahs and no events so far.
