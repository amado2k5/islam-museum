## Overview
The atabegate of Fars, known as the Salghurid state, ruled the province of Fars for a century and a half from Shiraz. Sankar ibn Mawdud seized Fars in 543 AH (1148 CE), and their affair died out in 686 AH (1287 CE). {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.95-96}

## Name and origin
The state is named after Salghur, the commander of one of the Turkmen tribes that attacked Khurasan. Salghur joined Tughril, who appointed him at his court. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.95-96}

## The rulers
Sankar ibn Mawdud founded the state, extended his influence over Kerman, and made Shiraz the seat of his kingdom. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.95-96}

Sa'd ibn Zangi seized Isfahan and extended his influence over Iraq Ajam. He met the armies of Khwarazmshah at al-Ray and was defeated, but Khwarazmshah honoured him and sent him back to his lands, satisfied with the khutba being given in his name. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.95-96}

His son Abu Bakr then extended the territory to Bahrain and the islands of the Gulf. He sent gifts to Genghis Khan, and Genghis Khan did not harm his lands. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.95-96}
