## Overview
The Burid state, the state of the atabeg [[person/tughtakin-zahir-al-din|Tughtakin]] and his sons in Damascus. It began in 497 AH (1104 CE) and ended in 549 AH (1154 CE), when Nur al-Din Mahmud ibn Zangi entered Damascus. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Tughtakin
Tughtakin was a mamluk of Tutush. He became independent in power when Duqaq died in 497 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

He fought the Franks until he met their kings at Marj al-Safar in 520 AH. He died in 522 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## The Banu Buri
His son Taj al-Dawla Buri ruled after him, then his sons. Their state came to an end when Nur al-Din Mahmud ibn Zangi took Damascus in 549 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
