## Overview
The Marinid dynasty, a ruling house founded in 1215 CE and ended in 1465 CE, recorded as the successor of [[polity/almohad-caliphate|the Almohad caliphate]]. {cite:wikidata}

## Not covered here
The two dates come from Wikidata, and that is a single source; a second source is needed. The data contain no sultans and no events so far.
