## Overview
The Seljuk state, an empire founded in 1037 CE and ended in 1194 CE, with its capital at [[place/nishapur|Nishapur]]. {cite:wikidata}

## Dating
The data attribute the end of the state to 19 March 1194 CE. These dates come from Wikidata alone, and a second source is needed before relying on them. {cite:wikidata}

## Not covered here
A second source is needed for the sultans, the branches and the events. For one of its branches, see [[polity/seljuk-kirman|the Seljuks of Kerman]].
