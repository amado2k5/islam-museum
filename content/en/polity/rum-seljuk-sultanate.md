## Overview
The state of Qutlumush and his sons from the Seljuks, kings of Konya and the land of Rum. Qutlumush entered the land of Rum and took Konya, Aksaray and their districts, and rebelled against Alp Arslan, who defeated him and killed him in 456 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1331-1333}

## Sulayman
His son [[person/sulayman-ibn-qutlumush|Sulayman]] acted on his behalf and conquered Antioch from the Romans in 477 AH, then was killed in his war with Tutush in 479 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1331-1333}

## Kilij Arslan
His son Kilij Arslan ruled, struggled with the Franks, and took Mosul and Diyar Bakr, until he drowned in the Khabur in his war with Jawali. His son Mas'ud then took the land of Rum. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1331-1333}

## The end
The rule of his sons remained in Konya until their state died out at the hands of the Tatars. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1331-1333}

## Not covered here
The approximate start date of about 1071 CE is approximate and derived.
