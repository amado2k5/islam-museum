## Overview
The Rashidun caliphate, a caliphate founded in 632 CE and ended in 661 CE. Its two capitals, as the data give them, were [[place/kufa|Kufa]] and then [[place/medina|Medina]]. {cite:wikidata}

## For Sprouts
The Rashidun caliphate began after the Prophet's death and lasted from 632 to 661. The four caliphs who ruled in it are Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali.

## For Explorers
After the Prophet's death, the four caliphs whom Muslims call the rightly guided ruled: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali. Their caliphate was between 632 and 661, and cities such as Medina and Kufa were its centres.

## For Scholars
A caliphate that began in 632 CE after the Prophet's death and ended in 661 CE. It was held by the four caliphs Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali, whom historians call the Rashidun. Medina and then Kufa were among its chief centres, and its conquests reached Syria, Egypt and Persia. The reports differ on how each caliph was chosen and on the events of the strife under Uthman and Ali, so each detail needs a source. This entry presents the caliphate as the history books relate it, and it does not judge the people or the strife.
