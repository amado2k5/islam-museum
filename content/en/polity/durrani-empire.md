## Summary
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam describes the Durrani as an Afghan dynasty founded by Ahmad Khan Abdali after the death of Nadir Shah in 1747. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e568}

## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam calls the Durrani an Afghan dynasty. The Iranian ruler Nadir Shah died in 1747, and in the struggles that followed, it says, Ahmad Khan Abdali, a former commander in Nadir Shah's army, came out as master of much of Afghanistan. He took the title Durr-i Dowran, "pearl of pearls", and the dynasty he founded took its name from it. The Dictionary says his clan supplied Afghanistan's rulers for more than two centuries, among them Abd al-Rahman Khan, who secured Afghanistan's place internationally in the closing years of the 1800s, and its final monarch, Muhammad Zahir Shah, who was removed by an army coup in 1973. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e568}
