## Overview
The Mazyadid state, of the Banu Mazyad of the Banu Asad, kings of al-Hilla in Iraq. Their domain ran from Baghdad to Basra to Najd. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1078-1086}

## Leading men
Their senior was Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Mazyad, who seized the Dubaysi district. In his lifetime the rule passed to his son Dubays Nur al-Dawla. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1078-1086}

## The height
The state reached its height under his grandson Sadaqa ibn Mansur, who built al-Hilla and took the title King of the Arabs. He was killed in his war with Sultan Muhammad ibn Malik Shah in 501 AH, and their affair died out after his sons. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1078-1086}
