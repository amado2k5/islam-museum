## Overview
The Umayyad caliphate, founded in 661 CE and ended in July 750 CE, with its capital at [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. It follows the [[polity/rashidun-caliphate|Rashidun caliphate]] in the data. {cite:wikidata}

## For Sprouts
The Umayyad caliphate was founded in 661 CE after the Rashidun caliphate, its capital was Damascus, and it ended in 750 CE.

## For Explorers
After the Rashidun caliphate came the Umayyad caliphate, with its centre in Damascus. The Banu Umayya ruled until 750, and their state reached from al-Andalus to Central Asia.

## For Scholars
A caliphate founded in 661 CE and ended in 750 CE, with its capital at Damascus. It followed the Rashidun caliphate under Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan and became a hereditary rule within one family. Its conquests stretched from al-Andalus to Central Asia, and its administration was Arabised in its time. The sources record many events of strife and war among the Muslims, and each needs a precise source. This entry presents the caliphate by its history and centre, and does not judge its caliphs.
