## Overview
The Umayyad caliphate, founded in 661 CE and ended in July 750 CE, with its capital at [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. It follows the [[polity/rashidun-caliphate|Rashidun caliphate]] in the data. {cite:wikidata}

## Dating
The dates come from Wikidata alone, and a second source is needed before relying on them. {cite:wikidata}

## Not covered here
A second source is needed for the caliphs and the events. The state's record lists the book "The Umayyad State from Birth to Fall" among its sources, without pages in the data. {cite:umayyad-birth-to-fall-2006}
