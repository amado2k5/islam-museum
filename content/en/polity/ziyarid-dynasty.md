## Overview
The Ziyarid state of Mardavij ibn Ziyar and his brother Vashmgir and their sons, of the Jil, brothers of the Daylam. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222-1223} It was founded, in the source's account, by [[person/mardavij-ibn-ziyar|Mardavij ibn Ziyar]].

## Origin
They were commanders of the Daylam for al-Atrush, and then commanders for the Alawids, whom they relied on for support. When the state of al-Atrush died out at a time when the Abbasid state was failing, they went through the regions seeking power, scattered. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222-1223}

## Rule
Mardavij ruled Isfahan and al-Ray. He sent for his brother Vashmgir from the land of Gilan in 320 AH, and relied on him for support. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222-1223}

After Mardavij was killed, Vashmgir ruled Tabaristan and Jurjan, and disputed al-Ray and Isfahan with Rukn al-Dawla ibn Buya. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1222-1223}

## Dating
The data put the beginning at about 928 CE (about 316 AH), an approximate date. The data include no end date, and a second source is needed. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
