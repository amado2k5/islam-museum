## Overview
The Musafirid state of Muhammad ibn Musafir, one of the great Daylam, lord of al-Tarm, and his sons in Adharbayjan. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226-1227}

## The sons
He had many sons, among them Salar, Sa'luk, Wahshudan and al-Marzuban. His brothers disputed Adharbayjan with Dusaym ibn Ibrahim al-Kurdi, until [[person/marzuban-ibn-muhammad|al-Marzuban]] took it in 330 AH. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226-1227}

## The Rus at Bardha'a
In their days the Rus came down on Bardha'a in 332 AH, and al-Marzuban was victorious over them. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar p.1226-1227}
