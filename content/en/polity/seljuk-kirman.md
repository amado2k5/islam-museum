## Overview
The Seljuks of Kerman, a branch of the Seljuk house, founded by Qawurt Bek, Imad al-Din Qara Arslan, in 433 AH (1041 CE). Their emirate in Kerman lasted until it died out at the end of the sixth century AH. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.90-91}

## The emirs
After the founder a number of emirs succeeded him. The source names them as follows: Kermanshah in 465 AH, then Hasan, Qizil Arslan, Uzbek, Sultan Shah Rukn al-Din, Turan Shah in 477 AH, Imiran Shah in 490 AH, Arslan Shah in 494 AH, and Muhammad I Mughith al-Din in 536 AH. {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.90-91}
