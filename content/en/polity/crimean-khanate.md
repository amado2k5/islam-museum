## In The Oxford Dictionary of Islam
The Oxford Dictionary of Islam says Muslim Tatars and Turks founded the Crimean Khanate in the middle of the fifteenth century. It was not wholly free of the Ottoman sultans, yet it built its own political and social institutions, combining the customs of the Tatar steppe with Ottoman administrative and dynastic habits. Trade carried most of its economy: slaves from the Slavic settlements to the north, foodstuffs and high-quality manufactured goods, with Jews and Christians holding significant economic roles. Russian expansion in the eighteenth century, together with the decline of both the khanate and the Ottomans, ended in Russian victories over it in the years 1768 to 1774, and in the reign of Catherine II Russia annexed the whole of the peninsula, adding it to its empire. {cite:oxford-dictionary-of-islam e470}
