## Overview
The Zengid dynasty, a ruling house founded in 1127 CE and ended in 1250 CE, with its capital at [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. {cite:wikidata}

## Dating
The two dates come from Wikidata alone, and a second source is needed before relying on them. The state belongs to the age of the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks, according to the museum's classification. {cite:wikidata}
