## Overview
The kingdom of al-Hira (the Manadhira): Lakhm's house, Persia's wardens over Iraq's Arabs and the desert — from Amr ibn Adi to the last al-Nu'man, 632 CE.

## In the sira presentation
Al-Mubarakpuri devotes a chapter to government and rule among the Arabs before Islam, dividing the peninsula's rulers into non-independent crowned kings (Yemen's kings, the Ghassanid house and al-Hira's kings) and independent tribal chiefs {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.
