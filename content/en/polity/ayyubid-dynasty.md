## Overview
The Ayyubid dynasty was a ruling house founded in 1171 CE and ended in 1341 CE, with its capital at [[place/cairo|Cairo]]. {cite:wikidata}

The two dates come from Wikidata, and that is a single source. A second source from a history book is needed, along with a documented narrative of the dynasty's events.
