## Overview
The Ghurid state, the state of the Banu Husayn, kings of Ghur, who held power after the Banu Subuktakin according to Ibn Khaldun. It was founded by [[person/ala-al-din-husayn-ghuri|Ala al-Din Husayn]]. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Rise
They were kings over the land of Ghur in the days of Subuktakin, and their rule grew strong from Firuzkuh. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

Bahram Shah the Ghaznavid killed Muhammad ibn Husayn and crucified his brother Shuri at the gate of Ghazna. Their brother Ala al-Din Husayn marched on, took Ghazna in 547 AH and burned it. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}

## Ghiyath al-Din and Shihab al-Din
The state settled with his two nephews, Ghiyath al-Din and Shihab al-Din. They subdued Khurasan and the lands of India, until Shihab al-Din granted Delhi as an iqta' to Qutb al-Din Aibak. {cite:tarikh-ibn-khaldun-ibar}
