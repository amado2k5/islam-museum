## Overview
'The martyrs of Hunayn and Awtas', a dataset in the museum: a table of the Muslim martyrs at the battle of Hunayn and the expedition of Awtas (Shawwal, 8 AH), their lineages and places of death, taken from the Life of Ibn Hisham (part 4, pp. 97–101). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/hunayn-martyrs.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
