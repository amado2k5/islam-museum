## Overview
'The numbers of the battles of the Prophetic age', a dataset in the museum: a table of the numbers of the two armies and of the dead and captives in the battles of the Prophetic age, taken from al-Rahiq al-Makhtum with its classical sources. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/prophetic-battles.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
