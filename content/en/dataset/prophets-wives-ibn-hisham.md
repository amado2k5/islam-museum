## Overview
'The wives of the Prophet according to Ibn Hisham: order, dowries and notes', a dataset in the museum: the list of wives that Ibn Hisham gathered in the fourth part of the Life: thirteen wives, eleven of whom the Prophet consummated his marriage with, among them Khadija and Zaynab bint Khuzayma, who died before him; with the dowry as Ibn Hisham gives it, and the notes on the reports that he alone recorded. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/prophets-wives-ibn-hisham.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
