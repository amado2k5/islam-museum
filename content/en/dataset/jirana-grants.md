## Overview
'The grants to those whose hearts are to be reconciled, at al-Ji'rana', a dataset in the museum: a table of the amounts of gifts and money the Prophet distributed at al-Ji'rana (Shawwal, 8 AH) to the leaders of Quraysh and the chiefs of the tribes of Najd to reconcile their hearts, taken from the Life of Ibn Hisham (part 4, pp. 135–138). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/jirana-grants.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
