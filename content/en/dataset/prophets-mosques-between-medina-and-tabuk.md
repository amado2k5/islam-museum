## Overview
'The mosques of the Prophet between Medina and Tabuk', a dataset in the museum: the list of the seventeen places which Ibn Ishaq says had known named mosques of the Prophet between Medina and Tabuk (part 4, pp. 174–175). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/prophets-mosques-between-medina-and-tabuk.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
