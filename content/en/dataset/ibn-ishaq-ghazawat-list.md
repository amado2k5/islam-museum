## Overview
'The total of the Prophet's raids according to Ibn Ishaq', a dataset in the museum: Ibn Ishaq's list, in the narration of Ziyad al-Bakka'i from Ibn Hisham, of the raids the Prophet took part in himself: twenty-seven raids from Waddan to Tabuk in his order, of which he fought in nine: Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Qurayza, al-Mustaliq, Khaybar, the Conquest, Hunayn and al-Ta'if. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/ibn-ishaq-ghazawat-list.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
