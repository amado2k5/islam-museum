## Overview
'The roster of the emigrants to Abyssinia according to Ibn Ishaq', a dataset in the museum: a table of the names of the emigrants to Abyssinia, their clans and their fates as Ibn Ishaq counted them in the narration of Ibn Hisham (part 4, pp. 3–11): those carried in the two boats (sixteen men), the rest of the emigrants (thirty-four men), the women emigrants (sixteen women), and those born in Abyssinia (five men and five women). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The tabular data are in the file `datasets/abyssinia-muhajirun-roster.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
