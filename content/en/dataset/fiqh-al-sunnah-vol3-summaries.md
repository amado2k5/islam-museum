## Overview
'Summaries of Fiqh al-Sunna, volume three', a dataset in the museum: independent summaries in own words of the chapters of the third volume of Fiqh al-Sunna, from jihad to bequests and inheritance, with the printed page range of each chapter. {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/fiqh-al-sunnah-vol3-summaries.csv`.
