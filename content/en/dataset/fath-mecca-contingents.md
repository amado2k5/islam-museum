## Overview
'The numbers and divisions of the army of the Conquest of Mecca', a dataset in the museum: a table of the tribes and numbers of fighters in the army of the Conquest of Mecca (ten thousand fighters), taken from the Life of Ibn Hisham (part 4, pp. 42, 63) edited by Mustafa al-Saqqa. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/fath-mecca-contingents.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
