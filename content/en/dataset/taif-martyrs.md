## Overview
'The martyrs of the siege of al-Ta'if', a dataset in the museum: a table of the Muslim martyrs at the siege of the fortress of al-Ta'if (Shawwal, 8 AH), their lineages and tribes, taken from the Life of Ibn Hisham (part 4, pp. 128–129). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/taif-martyrs.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
