## Overview
'The participants and absentees in the expedition of Tabuk', a dataset in the museum: a table of those whom Ibn Ishaq named in the expedition of Tabuk by category: those who wept, those who stayed behind without doubt, those who made excuses, the hypocrites who stayed behind, those left as deputies in Medina, those who spent, those who pursued, and those who died or were reconciled (part 4, pp. 159–173). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/tabuk-participants-and-absentees.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
