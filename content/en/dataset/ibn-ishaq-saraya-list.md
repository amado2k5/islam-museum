## Overview
'The total of the Prophet's dispatches and detachments according to Ibn Ishaq', a dataset in the museum: Ibn Ishaq's list of the Prophet's dispatches and detachments, thirty-eight in all, as he narrates them on page 257 onward, interspersed with accounts of some of them: from the raid of Ubayda ibn al-Harith below the Pass of al-Murra to the dispatch of Usama ibn Zayd; with Ibn Ishaq's note that some people place the raid of Hamza before the raid of Ubayda. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} {cite:muruj-al-dhahab}

The tabular data are in the file `datasets/ibn-ishaq-saraya-list.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
