## Overview
'Summaries of Fiqh al-Sunna, volume one', a dataset in the museum: independent summaries in own words of the chapters of the first volume of Fiqh al-Sunna, from purification to pilgrimage, with the printed page range of each chapter. {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/fiqh-al-sunnah-vol1-summaries.csv`.
