## Overview
'The stages of the story of the three who were left behind', a dataset in the museum: the order of the stages of the story of Ka'b, Murara and Hilal as they appear in Ka'b's narration in Ibn Ishaq, from the prohibition of speaking to them to the announcement of their repentance (part 4, pp. 175–181). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/three-left-behind-fifty-nights.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
