## Overview
'Summaries of Fiqh al-Sunna, volume two', a dataset in the museum: independent summaries in own words of the chapters of the second volume of Fiqh al-Sunna, from marriage to offences, with the printed page range of each chapter. {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/fiqh-al-sunnah-vol2-summaries.csv`.
