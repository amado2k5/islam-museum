## Overview
'The builders of the Mosque of Discord (Masjid al-Dirar)', a dataset in the museum: the list of the twelve men whom Ibn Ishaq named as builders of the Mosque of Discord, with the lineage of each as it appears (part 4, p. 174). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Content
The tabular data are in the file `datasets/builders-of-masjid-al-dirar.csv`. The related time period: [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
