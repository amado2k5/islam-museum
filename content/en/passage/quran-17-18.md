## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "whoever desires the immediate world, We hasten for him therein whatever We will for whom We will, then We make for him Hell to enter it, blamed and driven away": Whoever seeks the immediate world, works for it, strives for it, and seeks it, without doubting of the Hereafter or hoping for reward or punishment from his Lord for his deeds. "We hasten for him therein whatever We will for whom We will," meaning: Allah hastens for him in this world whatever He wills from spreading it for him, or stinging it for whom He wills to do it, or destroying it as He wills His punishments. "Then We made Hell for him to be prayed for," meaning: Then We made him pray at the time he comes before us in the Hereafter, Hell, "blameworthy" for his lack of gratitude to Us and for his bad deeds in what we have done before him in this world, "rejected," meaning he is dismissed: distant: cast out in the Fire. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
