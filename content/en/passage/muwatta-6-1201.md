## Summary
It reached Yahya ibn Said that Umar ibn al-Khattab went out on the day after the day of sacrifice when the day was high, and said the takbir, and the people said the takbir after him. He went out a second time after the day was high, and a third time when the sun had passed its zenith, and the people were saying the takbir with him, until the takbir joined up and reached the House, so people knew that Umar had gone out to stone. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.540}
