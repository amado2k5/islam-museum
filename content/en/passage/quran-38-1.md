## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 1 to 2 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Sadah and the Qur'an with remembrance": Abu Ja'far said: The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of Allah's saying: "Sad," some said: It is from the analogy, whoever caught so-and-so, and it is one of that, as if its meaning is: by your deeds, the Qur'an is opposed to it. Whoever says this is its interpretation, reads it with a kasra on the 'dal' because it is a command. It is also narrated from Al-Hasan mentioning the narration accordingly: and from Qatadah, he said: Al-Hasan said: He invented the Qur'an. I narrated from Ali ibn Asim, from Amr ibn Ubayd, from Al-Hasan, regarding the phrase "Sad," he said: oppose the Qur'an by your deeds. I narrated from Abd al-Wahhab, from Sa'id, from Qatadah, from al-Hasan, regarding the verse: 'Ṣ al-Quran' (the Qur'an), he said: He opposed the Qur'an. Abd al-Wahhab said: 'Present it to your deeds, and see where your deeds are in relation to the Qur'an.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, al-Dhahhak, and al-Hasan.
