## Summary
Malik says: the matter on which there is no dispute among us is that whoever sells a slave-girl, or any animal, with a foetus in its belly, the foetus belongs to the buyer, whether he stipulated it or not. Palms are not like animals, and fruit is not like a foetus in its mother's womb. Malik said: what makes this clear is that people practise pledging the fruit of palms, not the palms themselves; and no one pledges a foetus in the belly of a slave or animal. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.272}
