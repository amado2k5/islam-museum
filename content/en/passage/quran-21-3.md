## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Their hearts are distracted and they have confided the private conversations of those who have wronged. Is this but a human like you? Do you come to magic while you see?" He explains: "Their hearts are distracted" and heedless. He says: These people whose attributes this Qur'an describes do not listen except while their hearts are heedless of it, they do not contemplate its rulings nor reflect on the proofs God has placed upon them. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "Their hearts are distracted," he means: their hearts are heedless. And His saying: "And they kept the private conversation of those who wronged" means: And they confided those people from whom the Hour approached while they were heedless, turning away, their hearts were distracted, the whispering among them, meaning: And they made their intimacies public among themselves and said: Is this the one who claims to be a messenger from God sent to you "except a human like you"? They say: Is he but a human like you in your forms and creation? They mean by that Muhammad. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
