**Commentary on verse 103 (p. 284)**

On "most people, however eager you are, are not believers", al-Tabari says: most of the polytheists of your people, Muhammad, however eager you are that they believe you, and so confirm you and follow what you have brought them from your Lord, will not be those who confirm you or follow you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 284}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
