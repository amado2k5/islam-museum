## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And on the Day when He will gather them and what they worship besides Allah, and He will say, 'Did you mislead these My servants, or did they go astray from the way?': And the Day We will gather those who denied the Hour, worshippers of idols and those who worship besides Allah—angels, mankind, and jinn." Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid regarding the saying of Allah: "And the Day when they will be gathered and they will not worship besides Allah," he says: "Have you led My servants astray?" He said: Jesus, Uzair, and the angels. And he said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, and similar. Reciters differed in their reading; Abu Ja'far al-Qari and Abdullah ibn Kathir read it: "And the Day He will gather them and what they worship besides Allah and say" with a ya, all with a ya, meaning: and the Day your Lord will gather them and gather what they worship without and say. Most Kufan reciters read it as: "We will gather them" with a nun, "so we say." Nafi' also read it as such. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
