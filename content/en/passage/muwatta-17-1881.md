## Summary
Malik says: the agreed practice among us is that wheat is not sold for wheat, dates for dates, wheat for dates, dates for raisins, wheat for raisins, nor any food at all, except hand to hand. If any of that involves a term, it is not proper and is forbidden. Nor is any condiment sold except hand to hand. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.173}
