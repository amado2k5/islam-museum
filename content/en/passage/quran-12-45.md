**Commentary on verse 45 (pp. 119-122)**

On "and the one of the two who was saved said, remembering after a time" al-Tabari says: the one saved from being killed, of the prison's two companions who asked Joseph to interpret, "remembered", that is recalled what he had forgotten of Joseph's affair and request to the king, which Joseph asked him to mention with the words "mention me to your lord", "after a time" meaning after a while. Reports 19339 to 19353, from Ibn Abbas, Abu Razin, al-Hasan, Mujahid, Ibn Kathir, al-Suddi and Ikrima: after a while, through Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Abd al-Rahman, Sufyan, Asim, Abu Kurayb, Waki', Abd al-Razzaq, al-Thawri, Abu Bakr ibn Ayyash, Abu Nu'aym, Bishr, Yazid, Sa'id, Affan, Ibn Jurayj and others; one report from Ibn Abbas has after years, and one from Ikrima after a span of time. Al-Tabari says this interpretation goes with the reading with a damma on the alif and a doubled mim, the reading of the readers of the Muslim cities. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 119}

It is reported of a number of early scholars that they read it with a fat-ha on the alif and the mim lightened, meaning after forgetting, and some said the Arabs say of a man that he "forgot" with that verb, and those who read so interpret it so. Reports 19354 to 19361, from Ibn Abbas, Ikrima and Qatada, that he read it so and explained it as after forgetting, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Affan, Hammam, Ibn Humayd, Bahz ibn Asad, Abu Ghassan Malik ibn al-Khalil al-Yahmadi, Ibn Abi Adi, Abu Harun al-Ghanawi, Ya'qub, Ibn Waki', Ibn Ulayya, Abd al-Wahhab, Sa'id, Bishr, Yazid, Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar. The editor notes that Abu Ghassan Malik ibn al-Khalil al-Yahmadi al-Azdi was a teacher of al-Tabari and that al-Nasa'i and others narrated from him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 122}

We stop here at the foot of p. 122, in the middle of the commentary on verse 45 (at the reading meaning "forgetting", with report 19362 begun at the end of the page); the next batch begins at p. 123. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 122}
