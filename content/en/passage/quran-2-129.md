## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "our Lord, send among them a messenger from among them who will recite to them Your signs" al-Tabari says this is the prayer of Abraham and Ishmael for our Prophet Muhammad in particular, the prayer of which the Prophet used to say "I am the prayer of my father Abraham and the glad tidings of Jesus"; he relates from Khalid ibn Ma'dan that some of the Companions said, Messenger of God, tell us about yourself, and he said I am the prayer of my father Abraham and the glad tidings of Jesus. In a footnote the editor says the chain is mursal, since Khalid ibn Ma'dan does not say he had it from a Companion, and that it is established as connected through Ibn Ishaq in al-Hakim's collection. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 82}
