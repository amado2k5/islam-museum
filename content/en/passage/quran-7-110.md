**Commentary on verse 110 (pp. 19-20)**

Al-Tabari says "knowing" means a sorcerer who knows sorcery, and "he wants to drive you out of your land" means the land of Egypt, you company of Copts. Then Pharaoh said to the chiefs, "What do you command?", meaning: what would you command us to do about him, and what do you advise? He notes that this speech is reported as Pharaoh's without Pharaoh being named, which seldom happens in speech; a parallel is the saying of the governor's wife in sura Yusuf ([[passage/quran-12-51|12:51]], [[passage/quran-12-52|12:52]]), where "that he may know that I did not betray him in secret" is Joseph's speech though Joseph is not named; similarly one may say "I said to Zayd, stand, for I am standing", meaning "Zayd said, I am standing". The editor refers to al-Farra's "Ma'ani al-Qur'an" for the detail. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 20}
