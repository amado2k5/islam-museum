## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Dinar that he saw Abdullah ibn Umar make two prostrations in Surat al-Hajj. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq (5891) and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani (271) transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.283}
