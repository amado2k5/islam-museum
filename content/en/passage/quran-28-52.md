## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We have certainly conveyed to them the word that perhaps they may remember": And indeed We have reached your people from Quraysh and the Jews from the Children of Israel the news of the past and the news of what We have done for them of Our power, for they denied Our messengers and about what We are doing to those who followed their footsteps, followed in disbelief in God, and denied His messengers like them, so that they may remember, be considered, and take heed. Its origin is: 'Connecting the ropes to each other,' and from this is the poet's saying: 'Say to Banu Marwan, what is the matter with a covenant and a weak rope that still connects and transmits in the segment sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, Sufyan, and Atiyyah.'
