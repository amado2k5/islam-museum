## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab and Sulayman ibn Yasar used to say that the waiting period of a slave-woman, when her husband dies, is two months and five nights. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.109}
