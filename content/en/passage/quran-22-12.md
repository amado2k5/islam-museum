## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They invoke besides Allah that which does not harm him and what does not benefit him. That is the distant misguidance": And if this one who worships Allah with a letter of discord is afflicted by a trial, apostatizes from the religion of Allah, and calls upon gods besides Allah that will not harm him if he does not worship them in this world and will not benefit him in the Hereafter if he worships them. "That is distant misguidance," he says: His apostasy by calling besides Allah is taking a wrong path and deviating from the religion of Allah far away. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase: "He calls upon besides Allah that which does not harm him and does not benefit him," he disbelieves after his faith: "That is the distant misguidance." In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
