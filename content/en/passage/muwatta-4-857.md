## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman, that he heard Aisha, wife of the Prophet, say: I used to owe fasting from Ramadan, and I could not fast it until Shaban came. The editor notes that Abu Musab (834), al-Baghawi, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi in Abu Dawud and al-Jawhari transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.414}
