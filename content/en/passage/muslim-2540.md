## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the prohibition of insulting the Companions, in the Book of the Virtues of the Companions in Sahih Muslim: spending gold equal to Uhud does not reach a mudd or half a mudd of a Companion. {cite:sahih-muslim}
