## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 19 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “By the sky and the night-comer”: Our Lord swears by the sky and by the night-comer who appears at night, from among the bright stars, and is hidden by day. Everything that comes at night is said to have “appeared.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
