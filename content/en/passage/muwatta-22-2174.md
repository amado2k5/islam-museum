## Summary
Malik transmits from Amr ibn Yahya al-Mazini, from his father, that he said: there was in the orchard of his grandfather a water channel belonging to Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf. Abd al-Rahman wanted to move it to one side of the orchard, nearer to his land. The orchard's owner refused him. Abd al-Rahman spoke with Umar ibn al-Khattab about it, and Umar judged for Abd al-Rahman that he may move it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.291}
