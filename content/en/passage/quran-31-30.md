## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because Allah is the Truth, and that what they call upon besides Him is falsehood, and that Allah is the Most High, the Great": This is what I have told you, Muhammad, that Allah did by entering night into day, day into night, and other great powers by His power. He did it as Allah as truth, not what these polytheists claim to do, and that He can do it only by Him. Divinity is only suitable for those who do so by His power. And His saying: "And that what they invoke besides Him is falsehood," the Exalted says: And that what these polytheists worship besides Allah is falsehood that diminishes, which perishes and perishes, "And that Allah is the Most High, the Great." Allah says: And that Allah is the Most High, He says: He is the Most High, He says: the Exalted above everything, and everything below Him has a submissive and submissive one. The Great One below whom everything is diminished.
