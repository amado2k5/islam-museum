## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab asked about making up Ramadan. He said: I prefer that making up Ramadan not be spread out, but made consecutive, that is, followed. The editor notes that Abu Musab (822) and Suwayd (470) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.409}
