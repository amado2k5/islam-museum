## Summary
A ruling of Malik transmitted by Yahya in the chapter on performing ablution: whoever forgets to rinse his mouth and blow his nose and then prays does not have to repeat his prayer, but should rinse and blow his nose for the prayers to come. The editor refers to the disagreement of the jurists on this matter in al-Tirmidhi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.53} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.54}
