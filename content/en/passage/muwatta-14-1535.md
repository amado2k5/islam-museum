## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he used to say a slave-woman may not be married on top of a free woman unless the free woman agrees. If she agrees, she is entitled to two-thirds of the turn. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.43}
