## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told that Abdullah ibn Abbas, Ubada ibn al-Samit, al-Qasim ibn Muhammad and Abdullah ibn Amir ibn Rabi'a prayed the witr after dawn, in the chapter on the witr after dawn. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.185}
