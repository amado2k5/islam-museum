## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from his father, that Umar ibn al-Khattab led the people at Mecca in two rak'as; when he finished he said: people of Mecca, complete your prayer, for we are a people on a journey. Then he prayed two rak'as at Mina, and it did not reach him that he said anything to them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.539}
