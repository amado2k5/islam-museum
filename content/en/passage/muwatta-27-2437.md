## Summary
Malik said: a hired worker, or a man who is among a group serving them, who steal from them, owes no cutting, because their condition is not that of a thief; their condition is that of a betrayer, and there is no cutting on a betrayer. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.406}
