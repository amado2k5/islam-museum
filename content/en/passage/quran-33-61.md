## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation came within the interpretation of verses 60–61. Al-Tabari explains that the hypocrites, those with disease in their hearts, and those who are shaken in Medina, if they do not cease, God will impose His authority on them and then they will not be near him there except for a few, cursed wherever they are, they will be taken and killed.
