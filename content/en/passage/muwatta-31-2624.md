## Summary
Yahya narrated to me from Malik that it reached him that it used to be said: praise be to God who created everything as it should be; nothing that comes to it hastens, and He decreed it; God is sufficient for me; God hears whoever calls; beyond God there is no goal. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.483}
