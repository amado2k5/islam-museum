## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the pilgrimage on behalf of another. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, that al-Fadl ibn Abbas was riding behind the Messenger of God when a woman from Khath'am came to ask him for a fatwa; al-Fadl began to look at her and she looked at him, so the Messenger of God turned al-Fadl's face to the other side. She said: Messenger of God, God's obligation of the pilgrimage has reached my father, an old man who cannot sit steady on the mount; may I make the pilgrimage on his behalf? He said: yes. That was in the Farewell Pilgrimage. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1199) transmitted it from Malik, and that this hadith is in the two Sahihs. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.483}
