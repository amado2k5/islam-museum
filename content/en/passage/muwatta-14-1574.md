## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha that he heard Anas ibn Malik say that a tailor invited the Messenger to a meal he had prepared. Anas went with him, and the bread was of barley, with a broth containing gourd. Anas saw the Messenger follow the gourd around the dish, and he has loved gourd ever since. The editor notes that the chains of this report strengthen one another. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.55}
