## Summary
Verse 22 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 22 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "on the day we gather them all, then say to those who associated: where are your partners whom you claimed?" al-Tabari says: these inventors of lies against God and deniers of his signs do not prosper today, in this world, nor on the day we gather them all, that is, in the next; so something is omitted from the sentence, the mention of what is shown making what was dropped unnecessary. The sense is: the wrongdoers do not prosper today in this world, "and on the day we gather them all", and "on the day we gather them" is returned to what is meant in the sentence, since even if omitted it is as though it were there, the hearers knowing its sense; "then we say to those who associated: where are your partners" means: then we say, when we have gathered these inventors of lies against God who claimed in his dominion a partner and the deniers of his signs and messengers, and brought them all together on the day of resurrection: where are your partners whom you claimed were gods besides God, by invention and lying, and whom you called lords besides him? Bring them, if you are truthful. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 297}

The editor refers for "gathering" and "trial" to p. 89 and volume 10.

Verse 22 closes here at p. 297, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-23|6:23]].
