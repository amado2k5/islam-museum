## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 30 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'You used to come to us from the right hand': The humans said to the jinn: 'Indeed, you jinn, you used to come to us before the religion and the truth, and you deceive us with the strongest faces.'
