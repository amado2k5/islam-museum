## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 76 and 77 in one passage: "And it is indeed a way to stay," meaning that this city—the city of Sodom—is a clear, resident road, visible to those passing through it, not hidden from it, and no one leaves its place so that the person with the knowledge of its matter is unaware — according to Mujahid and Al-Dahhak as a guide, Qatadah as a clear path, and from Ibn Zayd the path. "Indeed, in that is a sign for the believers": Indeed, in God's action with the people of Lot is a clear sign and indication for those who believe in God to take revenge on the disbelievers and save him from His punishment from those who befall him—and he narrates from Sa'id ibn Jubayr that the verse is like a sign like a man sending his seal to his family, so they know it is true.
