## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 35 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And those who are faithful to their trusts and their covenants”: Except for those who are faithful to the trusts of God that He has entrusted to them of His obligations, and the trusts of His servants that they have been entrusted with, and the covenants that He has taken from them to obey Him in what He has commanded and forbidden, and the covenants of His servants that He has given them in accordance with what He has made binding upon Himself. They are mindful of these things, and they preserve them and do not neglect them, but rather they fulfill them and pledge to them according to what God has obligated them to preserve.
