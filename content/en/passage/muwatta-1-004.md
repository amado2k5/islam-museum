## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman from Aisha, in the chapter on the times of prayer: the Prophet prayed the dawn prayer at the start of its time, until women left wrapped in their cloaks and could not be recognised in the remaining darkness of night. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.35} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.36}
