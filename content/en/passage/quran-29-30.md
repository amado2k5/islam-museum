## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 31 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "My Lord said, 'Help me against the corrupting people'": "And when Our messengers Abraham brought the glad tidings" from God through Isaac, and from behind Isaac Jacob, "They said, 'Indeed, we will destroy the people of this town.'" He means: The messengers of God said to Abraham: We will destroy the people of this town, the village of Sodom, which is the village of the people of Lot. "Indeed, its people were wrongdoers," meaning its people were wrongdoers by disobeying God and denying the Messenger of God. My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase "And when Our messengers Abraham came with glad tidings..." And in the Maqta' he transmits statements from Ibn Abbas.
