**Commentary on verse 88 (p. 414)**

Al-Tabari says: these hypocrites whose stories I related did not strive against the polytheists, but the Messenger Muhammad and those who believed God and His Messenger with him are the ones who strove against the polytheists with their wealth and their persons, spending their wealth in their striving and wearying and giving up their persons in fighting; "and those", that is, for the Messenger and for those who believed with him, who strove with their wealth and persons, "are the good things", the good things of the hereafter, which are its women, its gardens and its bliss, and the discussion continues on the next page. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of striving. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 414}

We stop here at the foot of p. 414, in the middle of the commentary on verse 88 (before the words "and those are the successful"); the next batch begins at p. 415. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 414}
