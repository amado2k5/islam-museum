## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, We have sent down clear verses, and Allah guides whom He wills to a straight path": We have sent down, O people, clear signs indicating the path of truth and the path of guidance. "And Allah guides whom He wills to a straight path," meaning: Allah guides whom He wills of His creation by His guidance, guiding them to the religion of Islam, which is the straight path and the straight path without deviation.
