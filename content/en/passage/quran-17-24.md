## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say, 'My Lord, have mercy on them as they raised me when I was small': And be humble for them out of mercy from You, obeying them in what He commands you to do, which is not disobedience to Allah, and do not delay them in what they love." In the passage, he quotes from Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
