## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 81 to 82 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And take gods besides Allah that they may be a source of honor": And take, O Muhammad, these polytheists from your people as gods they worship besides Allah, so that these gods may have honor, preventing them from Allah's punishment, and making their worship a refuge with Allah. And His saying: "No," the Exalted says: The matter is not as they thought and hoped from these gods they worship besides Allah, that they would save them from Allah's punishment, save them from it, and from evil if their Lord wills it for them. And His saying: "They will disbelieve in their worship," the Exalted says: But the gods will disbelieve in the Hereafter by the worship of these polytheists on the Day of Resurrection, and their disbelief in it is said to their Lord: We disown You for what they used to worship, but they denied being their servants or commanded them to do so, and disowned them, which is their disbelief in their worship. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
