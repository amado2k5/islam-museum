## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that a man found lost property, and came to Abdullah ibn Umar and said: I found lost property; what do you think about it? Abdullah said: announce it. He said: I have. He said: more. He said: I have. Abdullah said: I do not order you to eat it; if you wished, you need not have taken it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.304}
