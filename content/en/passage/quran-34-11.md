## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 10–11. Al-Tabari explains that God gave David a favor from Him and said to the mountains, 'Obey with Him,' meaning glorify Him with Him. He explains the meaning of 'Tawib' in the language, that the birds were subjected to him as well, and that the iron was given to him by God: 'Do the severity and measure in narration, and do righteousness. Indeed, I am Seeing of what you do.'
