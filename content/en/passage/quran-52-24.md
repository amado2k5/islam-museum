## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And young men of theirs will circumambulate them as if they were hidden pearls": and those whose attribute is described in Paradise will be circumambulated as if they were pearls in its whiteness and clarity, hidden in its purity, meaning: preserved in a vagina, so it is purer for it and purer for its whiteness. What he meant by this is that these young men circumambulate these believers in Paradise with cups of drinks whose quality the Exalted has described as such. And Qad: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And two young men will circle over them as if they were hidden pearls," he mentioned to us that a man said: O Prophet of Allah, this servant, how about the servant? He said: "By Him in whose hand is the soul of Muhammad, the superiority of the servant over the servant is like the superiority of the moon on the night of the full moon over all the other planets." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
