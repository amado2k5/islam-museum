## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34-37 as a single passage. He interprets the phrase, “So today those who believed will laugh at the disbelievers”: It was reported to us that Ka’b used to say: “Between Paradise and Hell there are openings. If a believer wishes to look at an enemy he had in this world, he looks through one of these openings.” God, may His praise be exalted, said: “Then he looked and saw him in the midst of Hellfire,” meaning in the center of the fire. It was reported to us that he saw the skulls of the people boiling. On the authority of Ma’mar, on the authority of Qatadah, Ka’b said: “Between the people of Paradise and the people of Hell there are openings. No man from the people of Paradise wishes to look at another from the people of Hell except that he does so.” In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
