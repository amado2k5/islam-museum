## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among the people is he who buys idle talk, to mislead from the way of Allah without knowledge and takes it as a mockery. Those will have a humiliating punishment": The interpreters differed regarding the interpretation of His saying: "And among the people is he who buys idle talk." Some said: Those who buy the well-known purchase at a price. They narrated a report from the Messenger of Allah, which is: from Khallad al-Saffar, from Ubaydullah ibn Zuhr, from Ali ibn Yazid, from al-Qasim, from Abu Umamah, who said: The Messenger of Allah said: "It is not permissible to sell, buy, trade, or sell in them, nor their prices.
