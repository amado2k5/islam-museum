## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40 to 42 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Indeed, the Day of Separation is their appointed time for all of them": The Day of Judgment will be judged between His creation regarding what they have done in this world, whether good or evil, by which the doer of good will be rewarded with goodness, and the wrongdoer with evil will be rewarded with all their time: He says: the time for their gathering together. Also: from Qatadah, His saying: "Indeed, the Day of Separation is their appointed time for them all," a day when people are judged by their deeds. And His saying: "The Day when a master will not avail a master of any avail" means: no cousin will defend his son from a cousin, nor a companion from his companion, from the punishment of Allah that has befallen them from Allah, "nor will they be helped," meaning: Nor will they support one another, so they seek refuge from those who received punishment as they used to do in this world. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "The day when no master avails a master of any benefit to a master"... He quotes in the Maqta' from Qatadah.
