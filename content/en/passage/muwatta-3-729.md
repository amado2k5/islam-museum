## Summary
A saying of Malik with which he closes the chapter: if a man has scattered properties, or shares in scattered properties, none of which reaches the threshold on which zakat is due, but when some are added to others they reach the threshold, he gathers them and pays their zakat. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.366}
