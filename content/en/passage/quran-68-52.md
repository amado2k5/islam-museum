## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50-52 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "And it is nothing but a reminder to the worlds" as meaning: Muhammad is nothing but a reminder by which God reminds the worlds of both jinn and humankind. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Kalbi, Ibrahim, and Ata.
