## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not devote an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 36–37. Al-Tabari mentions that those who disbelieve will have the Fire of Hell not decreed for them so that they die, nor will its punishment be lightened for them, while they cry out therein: Our Lord, we have brought us out doing righteousness. It will be said to them, "Did We not give you a life in which you remember who remembers? And there has come to you the warner."
