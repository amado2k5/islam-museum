## Summary
A saying of Malik transmitted by Yahya, who heard it from him, in the chapter on not reciting behind the imam in prayers he recites aloud: the practice of the people of Medina is that a man recites behind the imam in prayers in which the imam does not recite aloud, and leaves off reciting in prayers in which he does. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.138} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.139}
