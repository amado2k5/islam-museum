## Summary
A report that Malik says reached him: Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote to his governors that they should waive the jizya from those among the People of the Protected Covenant who become Muslims at the time they convert. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.376}
