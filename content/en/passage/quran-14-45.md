## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse as addressed to those who dwelt in the homes of earlier nations that wronged themselves. Qatada names among them the people of Noah, 'Ad and Thamud; Ibn Zayd names Madyan and al-Hijr. He glosses the lessons as likenesses, and says that God sent His messengers and set out examples, so only the deaf fail to hear them.
