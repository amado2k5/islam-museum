## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains: "And you dwelled in the dwellings of those who wronged themselves": You dwelled in this world the dwellings of the disbelieving nations that were before you—and from Qatadah: the dwellings of the people of Noah, 'Aad, Thamud, and many generations in between, among those who perished; and from Ibn Zayd: Their villages were Midian, Al-Hijr, and the towns whose people Allah punished. "And it became clear to you how We dealt with them": You knew how We destroyed them when they disobeyed their Lord and persisted in their transgression. And "We set forth for you examples": We have set forth examples for you regarding what you used to be of associating partners with likes—from Mujahid, the parables are the likes—but you neither repented nor repented until punishment befell you. Your question for delay now is unacceptable. And from Qatadah: He sent His messengers, revealed His books, and set forth parables, so no one deafens in them, and only the deaf is disappointed.
