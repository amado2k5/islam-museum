## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra from Malik from al-A'raj in the chapter on the prohibition of suspicion and spying, in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. It is a root principle of social ethics against bad suspicion and spying. {cite:sahih-muslim}
