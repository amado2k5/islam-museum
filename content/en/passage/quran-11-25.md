**Commentary on verse 25 (p. 293)**

On [[passage/quran-11-25|11:25]] al-Tabari says: We sent Noah to his people: I am to you, people, a warner from God, warning you of His might for your disbelief in Him, so believe in Him and obey His command; 'clear' means making clear to you what he was sent with of God's command and prohibition. The readers differed on 'inni': most readers of Kufa and some Medinans read 'inna' with kasra as a fresh start, since 'sending' has the sense of 'saying'; some readers of Medina, Kufa and Basra read 'anna' with fatha, with 'sending' governing it, as if the sense were: We sent Noah to his people with 'I am to you a clear warner'. Al-Tabari says the right view is that they are two readings agreeing in meaning, each read by a group of readers, so whichever the reader reads he is right. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 293}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
