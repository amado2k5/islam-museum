## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And among the people is he who disputes about Allah without knowledge and follows every devil who desires": He mentioned that this verse was revealed about Al-Nadr ibn Al-Harith. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj: "And among the people is he who disputes about God without knowledge," he said: Al-Nadr ibn Al-Harith. By His saying: "Whoever disputes about God without knowledge," he means one who disputes about God, claiming that God is incapable of reviving someone who has been afflicted and turned to dust, without knowledge He knows, but out of ignorance of what He says. "And he follows" in his statement and argument about God without knowledge: "Every devil who desires."
