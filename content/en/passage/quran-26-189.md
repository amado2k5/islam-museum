## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 189 to 190 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "My Lord said, 'My Lord is most knowing of what you do.'" Shu'ayb said to his people: "My Lord is most knowing of what you do," meaning He encompasses their deeds, nothing of which is hidden from Him, and He repays you your reward for them. "So they denied him." He means: his people denied him, "and the punishment of the Day of the Canopy seized them," meaning by the canopy, a cloud that shaded them. When they were covered beneath it, a fire blazed over them and burned them, thus the traces were obtained. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
