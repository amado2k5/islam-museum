## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 64 to 65 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Allah is Who made for you the earth a firmament and the heaven a structure and shaped you and shaped you well, so He made your forms excellent and provided you with good things. That is Allah, your Lord. So blessed is Allah, Lord of the worlds": "Allah" who has pure divinity, O people, "Who made for you the earth" on which you dwell, a "resting place," on which you settle and dwell on it. "And the sky is a structure": He built it and raised it above you without pillars that you see for your benefit and the foundation of your worldly life until you reach your appointed time. "And He shaped you and made the best of your forms," meaning He says: And He created you and perfected your character. "And provided you with good things," meaning: He provided you with lawful provision, and the delicacies of restaurants and drinks. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
