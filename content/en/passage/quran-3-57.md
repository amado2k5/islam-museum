## Summary
Verse 57 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 57 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The believers and the statement on wrongdoing.** Al-Tabari says those who believed are those who affirmed Jesus, acknowledged his prophethood and what he brought from God, took on the Islam with which he was sent, and acted on the obligations, laws and practices God set through him. Report 7156 (al-Muthanna, from Abdullah ibn Salih, from Muawiya, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas) says it means they performed His obligations. Paying their rewards in full means giving them the recompense of their good deeds complete, with nothing withheld. On the close of the verse he says God denies love for whoever wrongs another of his right or puts something where it does not belong, and that this denies that He wrongs His servants by rewarding the disbelieving wrongdoer as the believing doer of good, or the reverse. Although this is cast as a statement, al-Tabari reads it as a warning to those who disbelieve in God and His messengers and a promise to the believers, since God told both groups that He will not deprive the believer of his right or put his honor on one who disbelieved. The editor notes two textual points: the scribe wrote a word badly so the printed wording stands, and in a second place the manuscript's wording is an error and the printed one is right. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 465} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 466}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
