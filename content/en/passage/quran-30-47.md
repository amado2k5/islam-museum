## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly sent messengers before you to their people, but they brought them clear proofs, so We took revenge on those who had wronged. And it is incumbent upon Us to help the believers." He says, delighting His Prophet with the harm he encountered from his people with what he received before him from His messengers from their people, and His teacher His Sunnah regarding them and their people, and that He followed him and his people with His Sunnah among them and among their nations: "And indeed We sent messengers before you to their disbelieving people, just as We sent you to your people who worship idols besides Allah." "So they brought them clear proofs," meaning: With clear proofs of their truthfulness and that they are messengers to Allah, just as you brought your people with clear proofs, but they denied them as your people denied you, and they rejected them what they brought from Allah, just as they rejected to you what you brought them from your Lord. "So take revenge on those who have committed crimes," meaning: "So take revenge on those who have committed sins and acquired evil from their people. We do this likewise with the criminals of your people." "And it was incumbent upon Us to help the believers."
