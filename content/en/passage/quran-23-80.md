## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He is the One who gives life and causes death, and to Him is the difference of night and day. Will you not then understand": And Allah, who gives life to His creation, says: He makes them alive after they were a dead drop of sperm, by breathing the soul into them after the moments that come upon them. "And He causes death" means: and causes them to die after He has brought them back to life. "And He has the difference of night and day," meaning: He is the One who made the night and the day different, as it is said in speech: You have the manna and the bounty, meaning: You are the favor and the favor. And His saying: 'Will you not reason' means: Do you not understand, O people, that the one who did these acts from the beginning without a foundation cannot be prevented from reviving the dead after their destruction and creating whatever He wills to be executed after their creation.
