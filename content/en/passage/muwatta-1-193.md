## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, who used to say, in the chapter on the call while travelling: whoever prays in a desert wilderness has an angel on his right and an angel on his left, and when he calls and sets the iqama, behind him are angels like mountains. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik, and that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ibn Uyayna from Yahya ibn Sa'id. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
