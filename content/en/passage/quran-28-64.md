## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And it was said, 'Call upon your partners,' but they called upon them, but they did not respond to them, and they saw the punishment if they had been guided." And it was said to the polytheists of Allah, the gods and the equals in the world, 'Call upon your partners' whom you used to invoke besides Allah, but they called upon them, but they did not respond to them. "And they saw the punishment": meaning: and witnessed the punishment "if only they had been guided," meaning: they would have died when they saw the punishment if they had been guided to the truth in this world.
