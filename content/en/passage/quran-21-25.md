## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We did not send before you any messenger except that We revealed to him that there is no deity except Me, so worship Me." He explains: And We did not send, O Muhammad, any messenger before you to a nation from among the nations except that We revealed to him that there is no deity worthy of worship in the heavens and the earth except Me. "So worship Me," meaning: be sincere in worship for Me and devote divinity for Me. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
