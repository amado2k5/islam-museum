## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And worship your Lord until certainty comes to you": God Almighty says to His Prophet: "And worship your Lord until death comes to you, which is certain of it." It is said: certainty, which is certain of it, just as it is said: old wine, which is freed. In the excerpt, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
