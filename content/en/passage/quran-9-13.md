**Commentary on verse 13 (pp. 158-159)**

Al-Tabari says God addresses the believers in God and His messenger, urging them to fight their polytheist enemies: will you not fight, believers, these polytheists who broke the covenant between you and them, attacked your religion and backed your enemies against you, and "intended to expel the Messenger" from among them, and did expel him, and "were the first to begin" the fighting against you, meaning what they did on the day of Badr, or, it was said, their fighting the Messenger's allies of Khuza'a; "do you fear them", that is fear for yourselves and so leave off fighting them out of fear, when God is more worthy of your fear, more fitting that you fear His punishment for abandoning struggle against them and beware His anger at you, rather than these polytheists who hold no harm or benefit for you except by His leave, "if you are believers", that is if you acknowledge that fear of God is more fitting than fear of these polytheists. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 158}

Report 16535, from al-Suddi, through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal and Asbat: they intended to expel him and did, and they began the fighting against you first. Report 16536, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: the fighting of Quraysh against Muhammad's allies; reports 16537 and 16538 are similar. Report 16539, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: God told His messenger to fight the polytheists who had broken the particular covenant and those of the general covenant after the four months He set as their term, unless one of them transgressed within it and was killed for his transgression, and then said the verse. The editor refers to Ibn Hisham's Sira and explains that the printed and manuscript wording was garbled and was corrected from the Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 159}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
