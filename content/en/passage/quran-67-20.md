## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Or who is it that could be an army for you to help you against the Most Merciful? The disbelievers are only in delusion,” is explained to the polytheists of Quraysh: Who is this who could be an army for you, O disbelievers in Him, to help you against the Most Merciful if He intends evil for you, and repel from you what He intends for you of that? “The disbelievers are only in delusion.” God Almighty says: The disbelievers in God are only in delusion because of their thinking that their gods bring them closer to God, and that they benefit or harm.
