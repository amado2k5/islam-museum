## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki that a man proposed to another man for his sister, and it was said that she had done something improper. When this reached Umar ibn al-Khattab, he struck the man, or nearly did, and then said: what does this have to do with the report? {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.57}
