## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Pharaoh's wife said, 'She has an eye for me and for you; do not kill him, perhaps he will benefit us or we will take him as a child while they do not perceive.'" He said: "And Pharaoh's wife said," this is "a delight of an eye for me and for you." O Pharaoh, the nominative clause of the eye is in the case of this or him. And the phrase: "Do not kill him" is a question from Pharaoh's wife not to kill him. It is mentioned that when the woman said this to Pharaoh, Pharaoh said: 'As for you, yes, and as for me no,' so it was. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
