## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “Then when the prayer has been concluded, disperse throughout the land and seek the bounty of God, and remember God often that you may succeed,” is explained as follows: When the Friday prayer is concluded on Friday, disperse throughout the land if you wish; this is a dispensation from God. The passage also quotes statements from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
