## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And who is more unjust than one who fabricates lies against Allah while being called to Islam, and Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people": And who is more unjust and aggressive than one who fabricates lies about Allah, which is the saying of those who say to the Prophet: He is a magician and what he brought is magic. Likewise, his false accusation against Allah while he is called to Islam is that when he is called to Islam, he says: When he is invited to enter Islam, he says a lie about Allah, and falsehood is fabricated against him. "And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people," meaning: And Allah does not guide the people who wronged themselves by disbelieving in Him to strike the truth.
