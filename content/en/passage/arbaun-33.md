## Summary
Hadith of Ibn Abbas: "If people were given according to their claims, people would claim the wealth and blood of others; but the proof is upon the claimant and the oath upon the one who denies". Ibn Rajab explains that this is the great principle of judgment in disputes. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
