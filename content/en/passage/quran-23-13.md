## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Then We made him a drop of fluid in a firm place," meaning He mentioned him by saying: "Then We made him a sperm-drop in a firm position," then We made the man whom We made from clay a drop of sperm in a firm position, which is where the man's sperm from the woman's womb settled in him. He described him as 'firm' because He made him capable and prepared for him to settle in it until he reached His command, which He made a permanent place for him. And His saying: "Then We created the drop into a clot," he means: Then We made the sperm-drop which We made in the stable position of Maqin al-'alqa, which is a piece of blood. "So We created the lump into a lump," meaning: So We made that blood into a lump, which is a piece of flesh. And His saying: "So We created the lump into bones," he says: So We made that lump of flesh into bones. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Al-Rabi', and Ata.
