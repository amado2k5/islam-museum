## Place in the museum

**Commentary on verse 126: God's ownership and His encompassing knowledge (p. 252, notes 2-3 on p. 252).** Al-Tabari says that God took Abraham as a friend because of his obedience to his Lord, his sincere worship of Him and his hastening to His pleasure and love, and not out of any need on His part of Abraham or of his friendship. How could He need him, he asks, when the ownership of everything in heaven and earth, small and large, is His; and the owner is the one his property needs, not the one who needs it. So Abraham's need is for God, and God does not take him out of need; He took him because he hastened to His pleasure and love. God is saying: so hasten to My pleasure and love, and I will take you as My allies. On the closing words, that God is ever encompassing everything, he says God has always counted whatever His servants do, of good or evil, knowing it, so that nothing of it is hidden from Him and not an atom's weight escapes Him. The editor notes that a word for "He says" is in the manuscript but not the print, and refers to earlier explanations of the verb "was" in the sense of "has always been" and of encompassing (p. 193, note 1). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 252}
