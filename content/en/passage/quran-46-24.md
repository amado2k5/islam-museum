## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when they saw it passing by the direction of their valleys, they said, 'This is the raining cloud, but it is what you hastened for, a wind in which is painful punishment.'" So when the punishment of God came to them, which they hastened, they saw it as passing clouds in one of the parts of the sky, "the future of their valleys." The Arabs call the clouds seen in some parts of the sky evening, then the next day they become level, and they love each other in a passing way, because they are present in certain parts of the sky when they arise, as Al-A'sha said: O you who see a rain-bearing rain that has passed the night, it is as if lightning is on its edges. "They said, 'This is a raining rain-fed rain.' They thought upon seeing it that rain had come to them to revive them, so they said: This is what Hud used to prepare for us, and it is the rain."
