## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And say, 'Praise be to Allah; He will show you His verses and you will know them. And your Lord is not unaware of what you do.'" He explains: "Say," O Muhammad, to those who say to you from among the polytheists of your people: "When will this promise be fulfilled, if you are truthful? Praise be to Allah" for His blessing upon us by guiding us to the truth from which you are untrustworthy. Your Lord will show you the signs of His punishment and wrath, and you will know by them the truth of what He has called you to of righteousness. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
