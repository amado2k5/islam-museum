## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 55 to 56 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So on the Day no soul will be wronged in the least, nor will you be recompensed except for what you used to do": "So today" means the Day of Resurrection, "no soul will be wronged in the least." Likewise, our Lord will not wrong a soul in the least, so He will not recompense it for its good deeds, nor will He bear the burden of any other upon it. Rather, He will repay every soul for what it has done of good deeds and will only punish it for what it has earned and earned. "And you will not be recompensed except for what you used to do," meaning: You are only rewarded for the deeds you have done in this world.
