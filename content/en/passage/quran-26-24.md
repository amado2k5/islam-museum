## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And that is a favor that I have bestowed upon me that I have enslaved the Children of Israel": He, the Exalted, informs us of the saying of His prophet Moses to Pharaoh, "And that is a blessing He has bestowed upon me," meaning by saying: And that is the upbringing of Pharaoh to him, meaning: "And your upbringing to me, and your leaving me enslaved, just as I enslaved the Children of Israel a favor from You, you rightfully granted to me."
