## Summary
A report of Abu Mas'ud al-Ansari in the chapter on the merit of relieving the distressed and the merit of jihad, in the Book of Leadership of Sahih Muslim. Whoever points to good shares in the reward of the one who does it. {cite:sahih-muslim}
