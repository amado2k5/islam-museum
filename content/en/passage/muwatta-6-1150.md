## Summary
Abu Asma, client of Abdallah ibn Jafar, tells that he was with Abdallah ibn Jafar when they left Medina and passed Husayn ibn Ali, who was ill at al-Suqya; Abdallah stayed with him until he feared missing the pilgrimage. He sent for Ali ibn Abi Talib and Asma bint Umays, who were in Medina, and they came to him; Ali ordered his head shaved and a camel slaughtered for him at al-Suqya. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.520}
