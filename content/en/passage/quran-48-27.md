## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, Allah has spoken the truth to His Messenger (Al-Rayya) with the truth, so that you may enter the Sacred Mosque safely, if Allah wills, with your heads shaved and trimmed. Do not fear. So He knew what you did not know, so He made a near conquest besides that." Allah confirmed His Messenger Muhammad's vision, which I showed him, that he and his companions would enter the Sacred House of Allah in safety, not fearing the people of polytheism, some of them lowering their heads and some shaving. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
