## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Abbad ibn Tamim from his paternal uncle Abdullah ibn Zayd al-Mazini, in the chapter on practice in the congregational prayer: the Prophet was seen lying on his back in the mosque with one leg over the other. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.245}
