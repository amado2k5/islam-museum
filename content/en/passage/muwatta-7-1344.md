## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that the Messenger of God was seen wiping his horse's face with his cloak, and was asked about it; he said: I was reproached tonight about horses. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said this report is transmitted from Malik with a chain through Yahya ibn Said from Anas, and is not sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.601}
