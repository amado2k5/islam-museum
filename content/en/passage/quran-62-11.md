## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “And when they see a caravan of trade or amusement, they rush to it and leave you standing. Say, ‘What is with Allah is better than amusement and trade, and Allah is the best of providers,’” explains that when the believers see a caravan of trade or amusement, “they rush to it,” meaning they hasten to the trade, “and leave you standing.” This refers to the Prophet, Muhammad, being left standing on the pulpit. The trade they saw, to which the people rushed, leaving the Prophet standing, was oil brought by Dihyah ibn Khalifah from Syria. The passage also includes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
