## In al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says that, once they knew the command came from God, the Israelites asked Moses to ask his Lord to make clear what the cow was, though God had spared them this, having told them to slaughter any cow they pleased with no limit. Their rough manners and coarse nature led them into captious questioning of their prophet, and God, in his reading, punished them by limiting the slaughter to one kind, which he reports from Ibn Abbas. He explains "la farid" as an old cow, citing Arabic verse for the sense of age. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 189} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 190}
