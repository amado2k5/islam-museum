## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra: the Prophet forbade prayer after the afternoon prayer until the sun sets, and prayer after the dawn prayer until the sun rises. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Ishaq al-Tabba' in Ahmad, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi in al-Jawhari, Ibn al-Qasim (96), Qutayba in al-Nasa'i (1/276), al-Shafi'i (872), and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Naysaburi in Muslim (2/206) transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.303}
