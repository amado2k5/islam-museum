## Summary
Malik transmits from Muslim ibn Abi Maryam, from Abu Salih al-Sammam, from Abu Hurayra, that he said: the deeds of people are presented twice every week, on Monday and Thursday, and every believing servant is forgiven except a servant who has enmity with his brother; it is said: leave these two until they make peace. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.496}
