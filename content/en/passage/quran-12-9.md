**Commentary on verse 9 (pp. 563-564)**

On [[passage/quran-12-9|12:9]] al-Tabari says: Joseph's brothers said to one another, kill Joseph or cast him out on some land, meaning some place on the earth, 'your father's face will be free for you', meaning your father's face will be free of his preoccupation with Joseph, who has preoccupied him from us and turned his face from us to him, 'and you will be a righteous people after him', meaning that they would repent of killing Joseph and of their sin in it, and by repenting of killing him after Joseph's death would be a righteous people; report 18798, from al-Suddi: you repent of what you did. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 564}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
