**Commentary on verse 185 (pp. 290-291)**

By "he is only a plain warner" he means he is only a warner who warns you of God's punishment for your unbelief in Him if you do not turn to belief in Him, and "plain" means he has made clear to you, people, his warning of what he warns you of, God's might against your unbelief. In the next verse he says: have those who deny God's signs not looked at God's dominion and sovereignty in the heavens and the earth and at everything He created in them, to ponder it, take lesson from it and know that it belongs to One without peer or likeness and is the doing of One to whom alone worship and pure religion are due, so that they believe in Him, trust His messenger, turn to obedience to Him, cast off rivals and idols, and beware that their terms may have drawn near so that they perish in their unbelief and come to God's torment. And "in what discourse after it will they believe" means by what frightening and warning after Muhammad's warning and threat, which he brought them from God in the verses of His book, will they believe if they do not believe this book that he brought them from God? The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "warner", "plain", "dominion", "term" and "discourse". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 290} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 291}
