## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah who made the night for you to rest therein, and the day [as seeing]; indeed, Allah is bountiful to the people, but most people do not give thanks": Allah, to whom divinity is not suitable except Him, and worship is not worthy of anyone else, described as having made the night a place of tranquility for you, so you may be calm from distractions and disorder for livelihood, and the reasons you used to act during your day, "and the day is a sighting." He says: And He made the day a sight for those who are troubled in their livelihood and seeking their needs, as a blessing from Him for that. "Indeed, Allah is the possessor of bounty over the people," meaning: Allah is gracious to you, O people, with unmatched bounty. "But most people do not give thanks." He means: but most of them do not thank Him through obedience to Him, sincerity of divinity and worship of Him, and no hand has come forward to Him that obligates him to be grateful.
