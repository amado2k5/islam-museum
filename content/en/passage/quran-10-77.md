**Commentary on verse 77 (p. 156)**

On [[passage/quran-10-77|10:77]], the words 'and the magicians do not prosper' mean the magicians do not succeed and do not remain. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'prospering'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 156}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
