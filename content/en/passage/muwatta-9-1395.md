## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki, from Jabir ibn Abdullah, that they sacrificed with the Messenger of God in the year of al-Hudaybiyya, a camel for seven and a cow for seven. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.624}
