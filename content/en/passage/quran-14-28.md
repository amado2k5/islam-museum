## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse as describing Quraysh's rejection of the Prophet Muhammad, sent among them as a mercy from God, which they turned into a cause of disbelief. Several narrations name the people meant as the clans of Banu Umayya and Banu Makhzum, and explain the abode of ruin as Hell. One narration from Umar ibn al-Khattab reads it as two wicked clans of Quraysh.
