## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that the address in "Have You Not Noticed" is addressed to the Prophet, and that those who changed God's favor in disbelief changed what God had bestowed upon them, making it disbelief; their alteration was against the Prophet, as God favored Quraysh by expelling him from them and sending him among them as a messenger of mercy for them, but they disbelieved in him and denied him. "Dar al-Buwar" is interpreted as the abode of destruction, from their saying "the righteous of something burs a burr" when it perishes and becomes nullified, citing a verse by Ibn al-Zub'ari, and it is said to Abu Sufyan ibn al-Harb ibn Abd al-Muttalib. He then mentions that "Hell" is a translation of the abode of destruction and clarification: "And wretched is the settled one who prays it." It is narrated from Umar ibn Al-Khattab that the 'dawn' of Quraysh refers to Banu al-Mughira and Banu Umayyah. The Banu al-Mughira were uprooted by God on the day of Badr, and Banu Umayyah was appointed to them for a while; similarly, it is narrated from Ali, and in another narration from him: Banu Asad and Banu Makhzum, and it is said: Banu Umayyah and Banu Makhzum.
