## Summary
Malik says there is no harm in buying a cloth of flax, or of Shatawi or Qasabi kind, for garments of Itribi, Qissi, Zayqa, Harawi or Marawi kind, or for Yemeni cloaks and checked cloths, and the like, one for two or three, hand to hand or on a term, even if they are of one kind. If delay enters into it, there is no good in it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.187}
