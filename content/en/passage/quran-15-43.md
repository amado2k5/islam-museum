## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 43 and 44 in one passage in God's response to Iblis: "And indeed, Hell is for all their appointments," meaning the appointment of all those who followed Iblis. "It has seven gates": Hell has seven cups, "for each gate is a portion," meaning each of Iblis's followers has a portion and a divided portion. He narrates from Ali ibn Abi Talib that the gates of Hell are layers one above the other, not like the gates of this world. In another narration: the first fills, then the second, then the third, then all of them are filled.
