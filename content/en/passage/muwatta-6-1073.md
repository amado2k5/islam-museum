## Summary
A saying of Malik: as for running between al-Safa and al-Marwa, the breaking of his ablution does not interrupt it for him, and he enters running only in a state of purity by ablution. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1296) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.495}
