## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And indeed Joseph came to you before with clear proofs, but you remained in doubt about what he brought to you until when he perished, you said, 'Allah will never send a messenger after him.' Thus Allah leads astray those who are extravagant and doubtful." And indeed Joseph, son of Ya'qub, came to you, O people, before Moses with clear proofs from Allah, as in Al-Suddi: "And indeed Joseph came to you before," he said: before Moses. And His saying: "So you remained in doubt about what He brought to you," means: You continued to doubt what Joseph brought you from your Lord, not convincing hearts of his true reality, "until he perished." He means: Until when Joseph died, you said, O people: God will not send to you after Joseph a messenger to call to the truth. "Thus does God lead astray those who are transgressors and doubtful," meaning: This is how God prevents those who disbelieve, doubt, doubt the truth of the truth, from reaching the truth and the way of the way.
