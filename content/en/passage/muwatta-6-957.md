## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz set out from Mina on the day of Arafat, and heard the takbir loudly; he sent the guards to shout among the people: people, it is the talbiya. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1095) and Suwayd (504) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.456}
