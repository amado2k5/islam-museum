## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And to Midian, their brother Shu'ayb, who said, 'Stand up, worship Allah and hope for the Last Day, and do not spread corruption on the earth.": And I sent to Midian their brother Shu'ayb, and he said to them: O my people, worship Allah alone, humble yourselves to Him in obedience, and submit to Him in worship. 'And hope for the Last Day,' meaning: 'And hope for the Last Day,' meaning, 'And hope for the reward of the Hereafter by worshiping Me, and that will be on the Day of Resurrection.' 'And do not spread corruption on the earth,' meaning: Do not increase disobedience to Allah on earth, nor stand firm upon it, but repent to Allah and repent. Some scholars of Arabic speech interpreted his phrase: "Hope for the Last Day" as meaning: fear the Last Day. Other Arabic scholars denied this and said: We did not find hope in the Arabic language meaning fear except when compared to denial.
