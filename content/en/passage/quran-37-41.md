## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 41 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Those are the ones for whom is a known provision": These are the sincere servants of Allah, to whom is a known provision, and that known provision is the fruits that Allah created for them in Paradise, as: from Qatadah, "Those will have a known provision" in Paradise. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase: "Those will have a known provision," he said: in Paradise. In the verse, he quotes from Qatadah.
