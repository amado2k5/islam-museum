## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the merit of praying at night in Ramadan, in the Book of the Prayer of Travellers in Sahih Muslim. In an earlier wording, "whoever prays Ramadan in faith and seeking reward": fasting the month, praying in it and the Night of Power are all promised forgiveness. {cite:sahih-muslim}
