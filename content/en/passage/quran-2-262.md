## Summary
Verse 262 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 262 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

In ([[passage/quran-2-262|2:262]]) from p. 517, al-Tabari says the verse concerns one who gives his wealth to the fighters in God's way as support for their fighting God's enemies, that is, one who helps the fighters by spending on them and on their mounts and other provisions, and then does not follow what he spent with reproach or harm to them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 517} Reproach is that he shows them he has done them a kindness by his act and gift by which he strengthened them, expressing it in speech or deed; harm is his complaining about them for what he gave and spent on them in God's way, that they did not do their duty in the fighting, and similar speech that hurts the one spent on. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 517} He explains that this condition is set on the one who spends in God's way, and the reward is made due for the one who neither reproaches nor harms, because spending in God's way is what is done seeking God's face and what is with Him; this is his explanation. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 517} The editor notes a correction to the singular verb in place of the plural in the printed text and the manuscript, and keeps the manuscript reading in the second note. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 517} We stop here, before the rest of his argument. These reports are given as transmitted, al-Tabari's choice and explanation are attributed to him, and the corrections are the editor's, with no verdict from the museum.
