**Commentary on verse 17 (pp. 45-46)**

He then explains "Who does greater wrong than one who invents a lie against God or denies His signs? The guilty do not prosper". God tells His prophet: say to these polytheists who charged you with lying in what you brought them from your Lord: what creature is more transgressing and puts his words more out of place than one who fabricates a lie against God and invents falsehood about Him, "or denies His signs", that is His proofs, messengers and the verses of His book? Say to them: what you ascribe to me is no greater than your lying against your Lord, inventing about Him and denying His signs; "the guilty do not prosper" means those who committed disbelief in the world will not succeed on the Day of Resurrection when they meet their Lord and will not attain success. The editor refers to the glosses on wrongdoing, invention, sign, success and guilt, and notes where the printed text garbled a phrase the unpointed manuscript preserves. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 46}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
