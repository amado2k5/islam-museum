## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32-33 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase, “Such is the punishment, and the punishment of the Hereafter is greater, if only they knew,” as referring to worldly punishment. Qatadah said, regarding God’s statement, “Such is the punishment,” meaning worldly punishment, “and the punishment of the Hereafter is greater, if only they knew.” Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase, “Such is the punishment,” that the punishment in this world is the destruction of their wealth, meaning worldly punishment. His statement, “If only they knew,” means: If these polytheists knew that God’s punishment for those who associate partners with Him is greater than His punishment for them in this world, they would have been deterred, repented, and turned back to Him. But they are ignorant and do not know. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
