## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger forbade the sale of habal al-habala, which was a sale that the people of the Jahiliyya practised: a man would buy a camel meat for a term until the she-camel gives birth, and then the one in her belly gives birth. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.182}
