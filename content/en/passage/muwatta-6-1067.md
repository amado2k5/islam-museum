## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: he heard some people of knowledge recommend that when the one circumambulating raises his hand from the Yemeni Corner, he places it on his mouth. The editor notes that Ibn Wadah dropped the word 'Yemeni' from Yahya's transmission; that Ibn Abd al-Barr saw the 'Yemeni' reading as more correct; and that the hadith is connected in the two Sahihs from Zayd ibn Aslam from his father. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.493}
