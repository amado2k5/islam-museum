## Summary
Malik says that the emphatic oath (tawkid) is a person swearing about one matter, repeating oaths one after another, such as 'by God I will not reduce it by so-and-so', swearing this several times, three or more; the expiation for it is one expiation, like the expiation for an oath. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.614}
