## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Rather, they are clear signs in the breasts of those who were given knowledge, and none deny Our signs except the wrongdoers": The interpreters differed on the meaning of the phrase: "Rather, it is clear signs in the breasts of those who were given knowledge." Some said: the Prophet meant by it, and others said: the meaning of the statement is that the existence of the People of the Book in their books is that Muhammad is neither written nor read, and that he is illiterate, clear signs in their hearts. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Al-Hasan.
