## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 43 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and I have made you for Myself": "And I have made you for Myself." I have bestowed upon you, O Moses, these blessings, and I have bestowed upon you these favors, as a choice from Me to you, as a choice for My message, to convey on My behalf, and to carry out My command and prohibition. "Go, you and your brother." Aaron says: with My signs and proofs, go to Pharaoh with them; he has rebelled against his misguidance and misguidance. So convey My messages to him: "And do not be weak in remembering Me," meaning: Do not weaken in reminding Me of what I have commanded you and forbidden, for if you remember Me, it strengthens your resolve and strengthens your feet, because when you remember Me, you remind Me of abundant blessings upon you, and from Us Countless abundances. It is said: 'And so-and-so was forgiven in this matter.' Regarding this matter: when he weakened, and he is 'neni' and 'nenia' as Al-'Ajjaj said: 'So Muhammad has not forgiven him since God forgave him for what has passed and what has passed.'
