## Summary
A saying of Malik about a man who returns from his journey while not fasting, and his wife, not fasting, has become clean from her menses in Ramadan: that her husband may lie with her if he wishes. The editor notes that this section has been repeated in this chapter. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.398}
