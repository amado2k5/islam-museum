## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So when Moses came to her, he was called from the right bank of the valley in the blessed spot of the tree, saying, 'Indeed, I am Allah, Lord of the worlds.'" So when Moses came to the fire, which is "Anas from the side of the mountain, he was called from the right bank of the valley," meaning by the shore: the shore, which is the side of the valley and its enemy, and the shore combines two shores and two shores. Al-Shatt: the shore. And the right: a description of the shore to the right of Moses. In the Maqta, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Ibn Ishaq.
