## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd ibn Qays al-Makki, from a man called Dhafif, that he asked Ibn Abbas about coitus interruptus. Ibn Abbas called his slave-girl and said: tell them. She seemed shy, so he said: that is it; as for me, I do it, meaning that he practises withdrawal. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.112}
