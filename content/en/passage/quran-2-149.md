## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and from wherever you come out, turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque; it is the truth from your Lord, and God is not unaware of what you do" al-Tabari says: from whatever place you leave to whatever place you head, Muhammad turn your face, that is change it, toward the Sacred Mosque; "turning" here is facing it, and he has explained the meaning of "shatr" before. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 198} On "it is the truth from your Lord" he says facing it is the truth about which there is no doubt from your Lord, so keep to it and obey God in facing it. On "God is not unaware of what you do" he says God does not overlook your deeds nor is He unaware of them, but He counts them for you until He repays you for them on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 198}
