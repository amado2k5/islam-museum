## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We will test you until We know the fighters among you and the patient and test your news": He says: So that the believers among you may know the mujahideen among you, meaning that my party and my allies know the people of striving in the cause of Allah among you, and those who are patient in fighting their enemies, so that this becomes clear to them, and so that those with insight in his religion may be known from those who doubt and are confused about it, and the people of faith from the hypocrites. We will test your news, so that we may distinguish the truthful from the liar. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
