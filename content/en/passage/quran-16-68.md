## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And your Lord inspired the bees to take from the mountains houses and from the trees and from what they build (roofs)": And your Lord, O Muhammad, inspired the bees with inspiration: "Take from the mountains houses and from the trees and from what they build (roofs)," meaning: from the roofs they build, so they raised them by building. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
