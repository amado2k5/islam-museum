## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, 'Bring a Book from Allah, which is more guided than them, to follow if you are truthful'": Say, O Muhammad, to those who say the Torah and the Gospel: they are magical in appearance: bring a Book from Allah, which is more guiding than them to the path of truth and the path of righteousness, "Follow it if you are truthful," in your claim that these two books are magical and that the truth lies in others. In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
