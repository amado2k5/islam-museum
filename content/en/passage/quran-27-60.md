## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Is it He who created the heavens and the earth and sent down to you water from the sky and caused to grow thereby gardens of delight? It is not for you to grow their trees. Is there no god with Allah? Rather, they are a people who act justly": Allah says to those who associate with Him among Quraysh: Worship what you worship of idols that neither harm nor benefit good, or worship of those who created the heavens and the earth? "And He sent down for you water from the sky" means rain, and it may be intended by the springs that gushed forth on the earth, because all of that is from His creation "so We grew by it," meaning the water that was sent down from the sky is "gardens," which are the plural of gardens. The garden is a garden with a fenced wall, and if there were no wall on it, it would not be a garden. And His saying: "with joy" means: with a beautiful appearance. And it is said that with monotheism. It is also called gardens, as it said: "And to Allah belong the best names," and I have explained this previously.
