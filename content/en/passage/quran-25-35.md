## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 36 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We certainly gave Moses the Scripture and made his brother Aaron with him a minister": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, threatening the polytheists of His people for their disbelief in Allah, their denial of His Messenger, and their fear of His wrath upon them, similar to what befalls those before them of the nations whose messengers denied it: "And indeed We have given," O Muhammad, "Moses the Book," meaning the Torah, just as We gave you from the Furqan, "and We made with him his brother Aaron a minister," meaning: Supporter and supporter: "So We said, 'Go to the people who denied Our signs.' He means: So We said to them: Go to Pharaoh and his people who denied Our knowledge and proofs, and We destroyed them completely." In the speech, it is left unnecessary to do with the indication of what was mentioned, which is: They went and denied them, so We destroyed them then.
