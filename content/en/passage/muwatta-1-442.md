## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on waiting for prayer and walking to it: the Prophet told that a person remains in prayer as long as the prayer holds him in the mosque, and nothing keeps him from returning to his family except waiting for it. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Ahmad and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.228}
