## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that a man asked Umar ibn al-Khattab about a locust he killed while in ihram; Umar said to Kab: come, let us judge. Kab said: a dirham. Umar said to Kab: you find the dirhams; a date is better than a locust. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.555}
