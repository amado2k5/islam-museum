## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie against Allah or denies the truth about what has come to him? Is there not in Hell a abode for the disbelievers?" He explains: And who is more unjust among the people than one who fabricates a lie against Allah, and they say, when they commit an immorality, we found our fathers upon it, and Allah commanded us to do so. And Allah does not command immorality. "Or he denies the truth when it comes to him," meaning: or he lies about what Allah sent His Messenger Muhammad with regarding His oneness and disassociation from gods and equals when this truth came to him from Allah. "Is there not in Hell a abode for the disbelievers?" He says: Is not in the Fire a dwelling place and dwelling place for those who disbelieve in Allah, deny His oneness, and deny His Messenger? This is a declaration, not a question; rather, it is like the saying of Jarir: Are you not better than the one who rides mounts and the most devoted of the worlds to the wombs of the worlds? He only informed that the disbelievers of Allah will have a dwelling place in the Fire and a dwelling place to dwell in.
