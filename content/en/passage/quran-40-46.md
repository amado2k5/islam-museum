## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The Fire will be exposed to it morning and evening, and on the Day the Hour will be established, they will admit the family of Pharaoh to the severest punishment": Allah Almighty says, explaining the severe punishment that befell these wretched people of Pharaoh, that which befell them from the evil punishment of Allah. "The Fire will be presented to it." Indeed, they perished and drowned in it. Allah placed their souls in the hollows of black birds, so they are exposed to the Fire twice every day, morning and evening, until the Hour is established. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
