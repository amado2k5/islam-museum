## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of Bara'a (at-Tawba) 9:122, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Tabi'i generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «وما كان المؤمنون لينفروا كافة».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">
