## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 1 to 3 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Tasm": Abu Ja'far said: We have mentioned the differences among those who differ regarding the beginning of the opening chapters of the Qur'anic surahs with the letters of the alphabet, and what each of them derived from their statement and doctrine of the cause. We have clarified what is more correct than what is more correct than what has been said in our previous book in what makes it unnecessary to repeat it, and he mentioned about them the difference in the phrase: "Tasm" and "Tas," similar to what was mentioned about them in: Alam, Al-Murr, and Al-Mas. He said: Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase "Tasm," he said: It is a division divided by God, and it is one of the names of God. A name among the names of the Qur'an.
