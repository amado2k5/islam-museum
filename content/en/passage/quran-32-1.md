## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that the 'Al-Ma'am' was previously explained, and that the revelation of the Book revealed to Muhammad is undoubtedly from the Lord of the Worlds, Lord of the Two Weighty Things, jinn and humans. The Qur'an is neither poetry nor soothsayer's rhyme nor anything fabricated by Muhammad. This refutes the claim of those who claimed to have fabricated it, for it is the truth from its Lord to warn a people to whom no warner came before Him, perhaps they might be guided.
