## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik about a man in ihram: he may take back his wife if she is still within her waiting period from him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1181) and Suwayd (331) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.469}
