## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And seek, in that which Allah has given you, the home of the Hereafter, and do not forget your share of this world, and do good as Allah has done good to you. And do not seek corruption in the land. Indeed, Allah does not like corrupters": Allah says, referring to the saying of the people of Qarun: Do not seek from your people, O Qarun, with your abundant wealth, and seek from what Allah has given you of wealth the goodness of the Hereafter, by acting in obedience to Allah in this world. And His saying: 'And do not forget your share of this world,' means: Do not abandon your share and share of this world, so that you may take your share of the Hereafter and act in it in what will save you tomorrow from Allah's punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
