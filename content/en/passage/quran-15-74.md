## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 74 and 75 in one passage: "So We made its highest place at its lowest and rained upon them stones of Sijil." God made their land its lowest and rained upon them stones of Sijjal—from Ikrimah, meaning from clay. "Indeed, in that are signs for those who seek it": In what He did to the people of Lot regarding their destruction are signs and indications for those who are deceitful and aware of God's signs and guidance about the consequences of the sinners and disbelief in Him. This only means that the people of His Prophet from Quraysh have a valid account of what befell the people of Lot. He narrates from Mujahid for the deceitful, from Ibn Abbas and Al-Dahhak for the onlookers, and from Qatadah for those who reflect.
