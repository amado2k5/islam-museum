## Summary
Malik says: if a man is owed a debt by another and asks him to keep it with him as profit-sharing, that is disliked until he receives his money, and then he may enter profit-sharing with him afterwards, or keep it. That is for fear that he has become poor in his money, and wants to postpone it on condition that he increases it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.223}
