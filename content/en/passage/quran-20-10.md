## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And has the story of Moses come to you": God Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad that he is comforted by the hardships he faces from the polytheists of his people, and knows what will happen to him and their affairs, that he is superior to them and weakens the schemes of the disbelievers. He urges him to be serious in his affairs, to be patient in worshiping him, to remember what he encounters among his enemies from the polytheists and others, and in his efforts in obedience to what his brother Moses, repented from his enemy, then from his people, and from the Children of Israel and what he faced from trial and hardship as a small child, then as a young man, then as a frail youth, then as a full man: "And did he come to you?" "O Muhammad," the hadith of Moses, son of Imran, "when he saw a fire"? It is mentioned that this happened in winter at night, and that Moses was the most misguided of the road. When he saw the light of the fire, he said to his family, what he said. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Ishaq.
