## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that the Messenger of God made the lesser pilgrimage only three times, one of them in Shawwal and two in Dhu al-Qadda. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1104), Suwayd (517) and al-Shaybani (449) transmitted it from Malik, and refers to al-Tamhid (22/289). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.460}
