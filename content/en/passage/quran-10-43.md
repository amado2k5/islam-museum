**Commentary on verse 43 (p. 96)**

On [[passage/quran-10-43|10:43]] he says: some of these polytheists look at you, Muhammad, and see your signs and proofs of your prophethood, but God has taken away their success and they are not guided, and you cannot guide them, as you cannot make for a blind man sight with which to find the way. "Can you guide the blind, even if they do not see?" means: will you produce eyes for them if they were blind? As you cannot do that and none but He can, so you cannot make them see the way of right guidance. This is God's consolation to His Prophet over a company of his people who disbelieved in him, turned away and denied, and a comfort to him about them, with a command to put aside his hope of their turning to belief. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 96}
