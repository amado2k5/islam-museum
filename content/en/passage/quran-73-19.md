## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19-20 as a single section. He interprets the statement, “Indeed, this is a reminder, so whoever wills may take a path to his Lord,” meaning that God, may He be exalted, says: These verses, which mention the Day of Resurrection and its terrors, and what He will do to the disbelievers, are a “reminder,” meaning a lesson and admonition for whoever reflects upon them and takes heed. “So whoever wills may take a path to his Lord,” meaning: whoever among creation wills may take a path to his Lord by believing in Him and obeying Him. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Sufyan.
