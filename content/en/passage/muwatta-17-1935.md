## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that the Messenger forbade two sales in one sale. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the report is connected and supported from the reports of Ibn Umar, Abu Hurayra and Ibn Masud from the Prophet, by sound routes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.192}
