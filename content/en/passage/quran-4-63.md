## Summary
Verse 63 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 63 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Al-Tabari's explanation of the verse (p. 515, note 1).** Al-Tabari says those meant are the hypocrites he has described; God is aware of what their hearts hold when they take their cases to the taghut, leave judgement by you and withdraw from you, namely hypocrisy and deviation, even if they swear otherwise. He tells his prophet to turn from them, that is, to leave them without punishment of their bodies, and to admonish them by warning of God's might coming upon them and His punishment descending on their dwellings and by warning them against the evil of their doubt about God and His messenger, and to speak to them about themselves in words of deep effect, bidding them to fear God and believe in Him, in His messenger and in His promise and threat. In his note the editor explains the construction of the sentence. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 515}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 515}
