## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if We had not kept you steadfast, you would have almost abandoned them a little": "Had We not made you, O Muhammad, firm by Our protection from what these polytheists have called you to from the trial," he says: You almost inclined towards them and felt a little, and that was what they intended to do, that some of what they had asked him to do. The Messenger of Allah said what was mentioned when this verse was revealed: And from Qatadah, regarding the verse "And if We had not kept you steadfast, you would have almost left them a little," the Messenger of Allah said: "Do not leave me to Myself even for the blink of an eye." In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
