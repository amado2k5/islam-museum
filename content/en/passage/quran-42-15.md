## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So call upon and remain upright as you have been commanded, and do not follow their desires. And say, 'I have believed in what Allah has revealed of the Book and I have been commanded, that I will do justice between you. Allah, our Lord, and your Lord, so that we may have our deeds and you have your deeds. There is no argument between us and you. Allah will gather us, and to Him is the [return]': So to that religion which He legislated for you, and which Noah commanded and revealed to you, O Muhammad. So call upon the servants of Allah, be steadfast in acting upon it, do not deviate from it, and remain steadfast in it as your Lord commanded you to remain upright." It was said: Therefore, call upon her, and the meaning: so on. I placed the lam instead of 'to,' as it was said: 'that your Lord revealed to her,' and we have explained this elsewhere in this book. Some Arabs interpreted the meaning of this in their saying: 'Therefore call' to this meaning, and say: the meaning of the speech is: to this Qur'an, supplicate and be steadfast. What was said from this statement is close to what we have said, except that what we have said is more appropriate to interpret the speech, because it is in the context of Allah's report about what was legislated for you of the religion for His Prophet Muhammad by establishing it, and nothing has come from the speech indicating that he turned away from it to others. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
