## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger of God said: when a Jew greets one of you, he is only saying: death be upon you; so say: and upon you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.549}
