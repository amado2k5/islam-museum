**Commentary on verse 3 (p. 388)**

Al-Tabari says: those who perform the prescribed prayer within its limits and spend of the wealth God provided them where God commanded them to spend it, of zakat, striving, pilgrimage, the lesser pilgrimage and spending on those whose maintenance is due from them, thus paying their dues; "those", who do these things, "are the believers", not those who say with their tongues that they have believed while their hearts hold the opposite in hypocrisy, who do not keep up prayer or pay zakat. The interpreters said the like, and report 15695, from Ibn Abbas: "who keep up prayer" means the five prayers, "and spend of what We provided them" means the zakat on their wealth, "those are the believers truly" means they are clear of unbelief; then God described hypocrisy and its people, saying [[passage/quran-4-150|4:150]] and [[passage/quran-4-151|4:151]] up to "those are the unbelievers truly"; so God made the believer a believer truly and the unbeliever an unbeliever truly, and that is His words [[passage/quran-64-2|64:2]]. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "keeping up prayer", "provision", "spending" and "truly". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 388}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
