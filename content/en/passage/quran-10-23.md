**Commentary on verse 23 (pp. 53-54)**

He then explains "But when He saved them, they transgress in the land without right. People, your transgression is only against yourselves, a passing enjoyment of the life of this world; then to Us is your return and We shall tell you what you used to do". When God saved those who thought at sea that they were encircled from the distress they were in, they broke what they had promised God and transgressed in the land, going beyond what God permitted them, in disbelief in Him and in acts of disobedience on its surface. God says: people, the wrong you commit is committed against yourselves, and it is yourselves you wrong; and what you are in is "the enjoyment of the life of this world", a provision you reach your goal with in your near-term worldly life. The editor refers to the glosses on "transgression" and "enjoyment". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 53}

On this interpretation "transgression" is nominative by the pronoun that refers back to it in "against yourselves", and "enjoyment of the life of this world" is nominative in the sense "that is the enjoyment of this life", as in the word "a delivery" in Surat al-Ahqaf meaning "this is a delivery". It may also mean: your transgression in the life of this world is against yourselves, since by your disbelief you earn God's anger, "enjoyment" being the nominative of "transgression" and "against yourselves" completing it. The readers read "enjoyment" in the nominative except Abd Allah ibn Abi Ishaq, who put it in the accusative as a state. The editor says the reading in our printed copies of the mushaf today, in Egypt and elsewhere, is the accusative, the other reading al-Tabari mentions, though he has explained the nominative one in what precedes; he refers to al-Farra. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 54}

"Then to Us is your return" means after that your return and destination are to Us, after death; "We shall tell you what you used to do" means on the Day of Resurrection We will inform you of what you did in the world of disobedience to God and repay you for the deeds you sent ahead in the world. The editor refers to the glosses on "return" and "informing". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 54}
