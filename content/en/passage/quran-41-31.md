## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "We are your allies in this life and in the Hereafter, and you will have therein whatever yourselves desire, and you will have therein whatever you call upon": He, the Exalted, says, informing about the saying of His angels who descend upon those believers who remained steadfast in obedience to Him at their death: "We are your allies," O people, "in this worldly life," we used to take you as guardians in it, and he mentioned that they were the guardians who recorded their deeds.
