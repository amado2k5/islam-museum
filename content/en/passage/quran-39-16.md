## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 18 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "They will have shades above them from the Fire, and beneath them shades. That is what Allah fears His servants. O servants, so fear Allah": Allah Almighty says to these losers on the Day of Resurrection in Hell: "Above them are shades of fire," similar to the form of shadows built from the Fire. "And beneath them are shades," meaning: and beneath them is the Fire, until what is above them becomes shade beneath them, similar to His saying, Glorified and Exalted, to them: "From Hell is a bed, and above them is a mud (mud)" that covers them with what is beneath them from the bed. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
