**Commentary on verse 40 (pp. 317-319)**

On [[passage/quran-11-40|11:40]] al-Tabari says: 'until, when Our command came' means Noah makes the ark until Our command came, which We promised would come to his people, the flood that drowns them. The interpreters differed on 'and the oven boiled over'. Some said the water burst from the face of the earth, the 'oven' being the face of the earth. Reports 18143 and 18144, from Ibn Abbas and al-Dahhak, and 18145 and 18146, from Ikrima: the oven is the face of the earth; he was told, when you see the water on the face of the earth, ride, you and those with you; the Arabs call the face of the earth the oven of the earth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 318}

Others said it is the brightening of the dawn, from 'the dawn brightened'. Reports 18147, 18148 and 18149, from Ali b. Abi Talib: it is the brightening of the dawn; report 18150, from him: the dawn rose; report 18151, from him: when the dawn rose. Others said the meaning is that the highest of the earth, its most eminent place, boiled over with water, saying the oven is the loftiest part of the earth. Report 18152, from Qatada: we were told it was the highest and loftiest of the earth and was a sign between Noah and his Lord; report 18153, from him: the loftiest and highest of the earth, from which the water gushed. The commentary on the verse continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 319}
