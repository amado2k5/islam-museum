**Commentary on verse 40 (pp. 317-327)**

On [[passage/quran-11-40|11:40]] al-Tabari says: 'until, when Our command came' means Noah makes the ark until Our command came, which We promised would come to his people, the flood that drowns them. The interpreters differed on 'and the oven boiled over'. Some said the water burst from the face of the earth, the 'oven' being the face of the earth. Reports 18143 and 18144, from Ibn Abbas and al-Dahhak, and 18145 and 18146, from Ikrima: the oven is the face of the earth; he was told, when you see the water on the face of the earth, ride, you and those with you; the Arabs call the face of the earth the oven of the earth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 318}

Others said it is the brightening of the dawn, from 'the dawn brightened'. Reports 18147, 18148 and 18149, from Ali b. Abi Talib: it is the brightening of the dawn; report 18150, from him: the dawn rose; report 18151, from him: when the dawn rose. Others said the meaning is that the highest of the earth, its most eminent place, boiled over with water, saying the oven is the loftiest part of the earth. Report 18152, from Qatada: we were told it was the highest and loftiest of the earth and was a sign between Noah and his Lord; report 18153, from him: the loftiest and highest of the earth, from which the water gushed. The commentary on the verse continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 319}

Others said it is the oven in which bread is baked. Report 18154, from Ibn Abbas: when you see the oven of your household with water coming out of it, that is the destruction of your people; report 18155, from al-Hasan: it was a stone oven that belonged to Eve until it came to Noah, and he was told, when you see the water boil from the oven, ride, you and your companions; reports 18156 to 18159, from Mujahid: the water burst from it, a sign that he should ride with his family and those with him in the ship; report 18160, from Mujahid: the water gushed in the oven, his wife learned of it and told him, and that was in the region of Kufa; report 18161, from al-Sha'bi, who used to swear by God that the oven boiled over only in the region of Kufa; report 18162, from Ibn Abbas: the oven boiled over in India; report 18163, from al-Dahhak: it was a sign for Noah, and when water came out of it destruction and drowning had reached the people. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 321}

Ibn Abbas used to say that 'boiled' means gushed, report 18164. Al-Tabari says the boiling of water is the surge of its rush; one says 'the water boiled' when its rush moves. He says the soundest of these sayings on 'the oven' is that of those who said it is the oven in which bread is baked, because that is what is known in Arab speech, and God's speech is directed only to the commonest and best-known of its meanings among the Arabs unless proof arises against it, which is then accepted, since God addressed them with what He addressed them so that they would understand its meaning. 'We said' to Noah when Our punishment came on his people which We had promised Noah We would punish them with, and the oven boiled over whose boiling with water We made the sign of the coming of Our punishment between Us and him for his people's destruction, 'carry in it', meaning in the ark, 'two of every kind', that is, of each male and female. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 322}

Reports 18165 to 18169, from Mujahid: a male and a female of each kind; report 18170, from Qatada: two of each kind; report 18171, from al-Dahhak: by the two he means a male and a female. Some of the Kufan people of knowledge of Arab speech said that 'zawjan' in Arab speech is two; one says 'he has zawja na'l' when he wears two sandals, and not 'zawj na'l', and likewise 'zawja hamam' for two doves, and he cites [[passage/quran-53-45|53:45]] as meaning two. Some Basrans said 'the two kinds' are made the males and females, and mention Yunus on a line by an unknown poet that calls the wolf a man. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 323}

Another of them said 'zawj' is a colour and every kind is called a colour, citing a line of al-A'sha in praise of Hawdha b. Ali al-Hanafi and a line of Labid describing plants after rain like striped cloths. It is related that al-Hasan said on [[passage/quran-51-49|51:49]] that heaven is a pair, earth a pair, winter a pair, summer a pair, night a pair and day a pair, until the matter comes to God, the One, to whom nothing is like. His words 'and your family, except those against whom the word has gone' mean carry your family also in the ark, by family meaning his children, women and wives, 'except those against whom the word has gone', of whom I said I shall destroy them with those I destroy of your people. They then differed on whom God excepted of his family: some said it is one of Noah's wives; report 18172, from Ibn Jurayj: the punishment, she is his wife, who was among those left behind in the punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 324}

Others said it is his son who drowned, report 18173, from al-Dahhak. His words 'and those who believed' mean carry with them whoever affirmed you and followed you of your people; God says 'and none believed with him except a few', that is, none of his people acknowledged God's oneness with Noah but a few. They differed on the number of those who believed with him and were carried. Some said eight: report 18174, from Qatada, it was told us that only Noah, his wife, his three sons and their wives were in the ship, eight in all; report 18175, from al-Hakam: Noah, his three sons and four daughters-in-law; report 18176, from Ibn Jurayj: I was told that Noah carried his three sons, three women of his sons and Noah's wife, eight with their wives, the sons' names being Japheth, Shem and Ham, and that Ham lay with his wife in the ship, so Noah prayed that his seed be changed and it brought the blacks, as it is transmitted. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 325}

Others said they were seven: report 18177, from al-A'mash: Noah, three daughters-in-law and three sons. Others said ten besides their women: report 18178, from Ibn Ishaq: when the oven boiled over Noah carried in the ark whom God ordered, few as God said, his three sons Shem, Ham and Japheth and their wives and six people who had believed, ten with Noah and his sons and their wives. Others said they were eighty souls: report 18179, from Ibn Abbas: Noah carried eighty people with him in the ship; report 18180, from Sufyan: some said they were eighty, meaning the 'few'. Report 18181, from Abu Nuhayk, begins at the foot of the page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 326}

Report 18181, from Abu Nuhayk: I heard Ibn Abbas say there were eighty men in Noah's ship, one of them Jurhum. Al-Tabari says the right view is to say as God said, 'and none believed with him except a few', describing them as few without fixing their number by any measure, there being no sound report from the Messenger of God; so God's limit should not be exceeded, since the number has no limit in God's Book or in a report from the Messenger of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 327}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
