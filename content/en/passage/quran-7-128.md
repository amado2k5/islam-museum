**Commentary on verse 128 (p. 42 onward)**

Al-Tabari says: Moses said to his people of the Children of Israel, when Pharaoh said to the chiefs of his people "we shall kill the sons of the Children of Israel and spare their women": "Seek help from God" against Pharaoh and his people in whatever befalls you, "and be patient" in the hardships you suffer in yourselves and your sons from Pharaoh. Six hundred thousand of the Children of Israel had followed Moses, according to report 14972, from Ibn Abbas through Abd al-Karim, Ibrahim ibn Bashshar, Sufyan, Abu Sa'd and Ikrima: when the sorcerers believed, six hundred thousand of the Children of Israel followed Moses. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 42}

On "the earth belongs to God; He gives it as inheritance to whom He will among His servants" he says: the earth is God's, and it may be that God will give you as inheritance, if you are patient under the hardship you meet in yourselves and your children from Pharaoh, count it with Him and keep to the right course, the land of Pharaoh and his people, by destroying them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 42}

The sentence is completed by "and make you successors in it", since God gives His land as inheritance to whom He will of His servants; and "the final outcome belongs to the God-fearing" means the praiseworthy outcome belongs to whoever fears God and watches Him, avoiding His disobediences and performing His obligations. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "outcome". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 43}
