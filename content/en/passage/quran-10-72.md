**Commentary on verse 72 (p. 152)**

On [[passage/quran-10-72|10:72]] al-Tabari says God reports the saying of His prophet Noah to his people: if you turn away from me, people, after my call and the delivery of my Lord's message, and refuse what I called you to of the truth, the acknowledgement of God's oneness, pure worship of Him and giving up setting up gods beside Him, then that is your neglect and failing in what God's right owes you and has nothing to do with me, for I asked you no wage or compensation for what I called you to. 'My wage is only upon God' means my reward and the recompense of my work are upon my Lord alone, not on you or anyone else. 'I was commanded to be of those who submit' means my Lord commanded me to be one who yields to Him in obedience, submits to His commands and prohibitions and humbles himself to Him; for that reason I call you to Him and by His command bid you give up worshipping idols. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'turning away', 'wage' and 'Islam'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 152}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
