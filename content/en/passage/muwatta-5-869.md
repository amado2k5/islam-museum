## Summary
A saying of Malik: a man is not in seclusion until he avoids what those in seclusion avoid: visiting the sick, praying over the dead, and entering a house except for a human need. The editor notes that Abu Musab (864) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.420}
