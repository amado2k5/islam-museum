## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger forbade najash. Malik said: najash is that you offer for someone's goods more than their price, while you have no intention of buying them, so that others follow you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.217}
