## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He will guide them and set their minds right": God Almighty will grant His mention success in acting in what pleases and loves those who fought in His cause. "And will set their mind right": He will set right their affairs and condition in this world and the Hereafter. He also quotes in the passage from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
