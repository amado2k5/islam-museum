## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra from al-Zuhri in the chapter showing that major sins negate the completeness of faith, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It is a central text in the disputes with the Kharijites and the Murji'a and in creed books. {cite:sahih-muslim}
