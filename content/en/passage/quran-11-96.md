**Commentary on verse 96 (pp. 465-466)**

On [[passage/quran-11-96|11:96]] al-Tabari says: We sent Moses with Our signs for Our oneness and a proof that shows whoever sees it and reflects with a sound heart that it points to God's oneness, belies everyone who claims lordship apart from Him, and refutes whoever joins another with Him in divinity. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 465}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
