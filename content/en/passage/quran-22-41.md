## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who, if We enable them to do so, establish prayer, give zakah, enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong, and to Allah belongs the outcome of all matters": Permission is given to those who fight because they have been wronged, those who, if We enable them to do so, establish prayer. And "those who" here respond to "those who fight." And by His saying: 'If We establish them in the land,' he means that they have a homeland in the lands, so they defeated the polytheists and overpowered them, and they are the companions of the Messenger of Allah. He means: If We help them against their enemies and they defeat the polytheists of Mecca, they obey Allah, so they establish prayer within its limits. 'And give zakat' means they give zakat from their wealth from what Allah has designated for them. 'And enjoin what is right,' meaning: and call people to the oneness of Allah and to act upon His obedience, which the people of faith in Allah know. 'And forbid evil' means: and forbid associating partners with Allah and committing His sins, which the people of truth and faith in Allah deny. In the passage, he quotes statements about the spring.
