## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So We sent among them a messenger from among themselves, saying, 'Worship Allah; you have no deity other than Him. Will you not fear Him?'" He calls them, "If you worship Allah," O people, and obey Him instead of gods and idols, for worship is only worthy of Him. "You have no deity besides Him," meaning: You have no deity worthy of worshiping anyone besides Him. "Do you not fear Him": Do you not fear the punishment of Allah for your worship of anything besides Him, which is the God for whom you have no deity?
