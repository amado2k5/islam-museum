## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that the Prophet ruled on a foetus killed in its mother's belly: a ghurra, a slave or slave-girl. The one ruled against said: how shall I pay for what did not drink, eat, speak or cry out, and whose like is nullified? The Prophet said: this is from the brethren of soothsayers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.424}
