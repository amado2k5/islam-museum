## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase “Does he not know that God sees?” as follows: Did Abu Jahl not know, when he forbade Muhammad from worshipping his Lord and praying to Him, that God sees him and should fear His might and punishment? It has also been said: “Have you seen the one who forbids a servant from praying? Have you seen if he is on the right path?” The phrase “Have you seen?” is repeated three times as an alternative. The meaning is: Have you seen the one who forbids a servant from praying, while he is a denier and has turned away from his Lord? Does he not know that God sees him? And his statement, “No! If he does not desist,” means: It is not as he said, that he will trample Muhammad’s neck. He means: He is not capable of doing so, nor can he reach him. His statement, “If he does not desist,” means: If Abu Jahl does not desist from Muhammad, “we will surely seize him by the forelock,” meaning: We will seize him by the front of his head, and we will subdue him and humiliate him. It is said of him: “I seized him by the hand,” meaning: I took hold of his hand. And in the passage, statements are quoted from Ibn Abbas.
