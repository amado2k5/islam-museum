## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman from Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn, who said: Fatima bint the Messenger of God weighed the hair of Hasan and Husayn and gave in charity silver equal to its weight. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.646}
