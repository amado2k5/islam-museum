## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And this is a blessed reminder We have sent down. Will you then deny it": He, the Exalted, says: And this Qur'an which We sent down to Muhammad is a reminder for those who remember it, and a reminder for those who take heed of it. "Blessed is that We have sent it down" just as We revealed the Torah to Moses and Aaron as a reminder for the righteous. "Are you denying it?" Allah says: Are you, O people, denying this Book which We revealed to Muhammad, and you say, 'It is a false dream; rather, it is a fabrication; rather, it is a poet. So bring us a sign as the ancients were sent.' What We have given of that is a reminder for the righteous, just as We gave Moses and Aaron as a reminder for the righteous. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
