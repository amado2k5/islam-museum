## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 11 as a single section. He interprets the words, “So I said, ‘Ask forgiveness of your Lord. Indeed, He is ever Forgiving,’” as follows: I said to them, “Ask your Lord for forgiveness of your sins, repent to Him from your disbelief and from worshipping other than Him, and acknowledge His Oneness and devote your worship to Him alone. He will forgive you, for He is ever Forgiving of the sins of those who turn to Him and repent of their sins.” He then quotes statements from Mujahid and Sufyan in this section.
