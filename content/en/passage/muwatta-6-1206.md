## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to pray the noon and afternoon prayers, and the sunset and night prayers, at al-Muhassab, then enter Mecca at night and circumambulate the House. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.541}
