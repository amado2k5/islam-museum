## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And if the people were not one nation, We would have made for those who disbelieve in the Most Merciful a roof of silver and ladders on which they appear": "And if the people were not one nation": one group. Then the interpreters differed on the meaning that their gathering did not believe in: if He did what He said, exalted be He, and what He did not do for His sake, some said: That is their gathering in disbelief. He said: The meaning of the statement is: Were it not for people being one nation upon disbelief, all of them becoming disbelievers: "We would have made for those who disbelieve in the Most Merciful a roof of silver for their houses." In the passage, statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd are also quoted.
