## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who were given knowledge will see what has been revealed to you from your Lord, which is the truth, and guide to the path of the Mighty, the Praiseworthy": This is established in a clear Book, so that He may reward those who believe and those who strive in Our verses what has been made clear to them, and so that those who were given knowledge may see it and see it in the accusative case as a conjunction to His saying: 'to recompense those who believe,' meaning those who were given knowledge: The Muslim of the People of the Book, like Abdullah ibn Salam and his counterparts who read the books of Allah revealed before the Criterion, as Allah said: Let those who were given knowledge of the Book of Allah, which is the Torah, see the Book that was revealed to you, O Muhammad, from your Lord, the truth. It is said: He meant those who were given knowledge the companions of the Messenger of Allah. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
