## Summary
A report that Yahya transmits from Malik from his paternal uncle Abu Suhayl ibn Malik from his father Malik ibn Abi Amir, in the chapter on the call to prayer: he said he knows nothing that people held to that has stayed as it was except the call to prayer, a sign of how he saw people's conditions had changed. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.120}
