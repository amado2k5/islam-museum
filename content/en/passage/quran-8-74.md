**Commentary on verse 74 (pp. 88-89)**

Al-Tabari says: those who believed, emigrated and strove in God's way, and those who sheltered the Messenger and the emigrants with him and aided them and aided God's religion, are the believers in God and His messenger in truth, not one who believed but did not leave the land of polytheism, stayed among the polytheists and did not fight their enemy with the Muslims. "Forgiveness" for them means God's covering of their sins by pardoning them, and "generous provision" means that in Paradise they have pleasant, generous food and drink that does not change in their bellies into waste but becomes a sweat like musk. The editor cites the report from Jabir in Muslim and Abu Dawud that the people of Paradise eat and drink without passing waste, their food being a belch and sweat like musk, and refers to the earlier explanations of the wording. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 88}

Al-Tabari holds that this verse shows his reading to be sound, that "allies of one another" and "nothing of their alliance" mean aid and support rather than inheritance, since God followed them with praise of the Emigrants and Helpers and what they have with Him, unlike those who did not emigrate; had the earlier verses been meant to settle the ruling on inheritance, what followed them would only have been an urging to carry it out, and in that he sees clear evidence that nothing in these verses abrogates or is abrogated. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 89}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
