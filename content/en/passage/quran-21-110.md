## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 110 to 111 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, He knows the public speaking and knows what you conceal": Say to these polytheists, Allah knows the public speech they openly express and knows what you conceal, so do not speak openly with Him, whether it is hidden or apparent or secret or public. Nothing is hidden from Him. If He delays His punishment for what you conceal of polytheism or openly speak, I do not know the reason why it delays it from you. Perhaps His delay in this from you is despite his promise to you of a trial He wants you to take, so that you may enjoy your life for a term He has made for you to convey it, then His punishment will descend upon you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ata.
