## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah sets forth an example of a slave who is possessed and has no power over anything. And whoever We have provided from Us with good provision, he spends from it secretly and openly. Are they equal to praise be to Allah? Rather, most of them do not know": and He likened to you, O people, the likeness of the disbeliever among His servants and the one who believes in Him among them. As for the example of the disbeliever: he does not act in obedience to Allah, does not do good, and does not spend his wealth in the way of Allah because Allah has overwhelmed him, like a slave who cannot afford anything and spends it. As for the believer in Allah, he acts in obedience to Allah and spends his wealth in His cause, like a free person whom Allah has given wealth, who spends from it secretly and openly, saying: with knowledge from people and without knowledge. "Are they equal?" He says: Is it equal to a servant who owns nothing and cannot afford it, and this free man whom Allah has granted good sustenance to spend as described? Similarly, the disbeliever who acts against Allah's disobedience and the believer who acts in obedience to Him are not equal. In this passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
