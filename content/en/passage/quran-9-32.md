**Commentary on verse 32 (pp. 213-214)**

Al-Tabari says: these who took their rabbis, their monks and the Messiah son of Mary as lords want to extinguish God's light with their mouths, meaning they try to nullify it by denying the religion of God with which He sent His messenger and by barring people from it with their tongues, that being the light God made as illumination for His creation; "God refuses but to perfect His light" means His religion will be raised high, His word made manifest and the truth with which He sent His messenger Muhammad perfected; "though the unbelievers dislike" God's perfecting of it, meaning those who deny it and call it a lie. Report 16644, from al-Suddi, through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal and Asbat: they want to put out Islam with their talk. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 214}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
