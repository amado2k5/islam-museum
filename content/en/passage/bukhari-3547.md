## Summary
In the Book of the Virtues of the Companions, the chapter on the description of the Prophet: Anas ibn Malik's well-known description of his physique, of medium height, with a fair complexion and hair between curly and straight. It also records the age at the call and the periods in Mecca and Medina, the number of grey hairs, and Rabi'a's report that his hair reddened from perfume. It is one of the finest texts on his shama'il in the Sahih. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
