## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He has subjected to you whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, all of it—from Him. Indeed, in that is a sign for a people who reflect": "And He has subjected to you whatever is in the heavens"—the sun, the moon, the stars, and whatever is on the earth—animals, trees, mountains, inanimate objects, and ships for your benefit and interests—all of it is from Him. Allah Almighty says: All that I have mentioned to you, O people, of these blessings is a blessing upon you from Allah, bestowed upon you and a favor from Him, bestowed upon you. So be grateful to Him, no one else, for He has not shared in bestowing these blessings upon you, but He alone bestowed them upon you, and all of them are from Him. From His blessings, do not make Him a partner in your gratitude but dedicate to Him in gratitude and worship, and dedicate to Him devotion, for there is no god but Him. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
