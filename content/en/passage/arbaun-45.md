## Summary
Hadith of Jabir ibn Abdullah: at the conquest of Mecca the Prophet said, "God and His Messenger have forbidden the sale of wine, carrion, pigs and idols", and on the question of fat from carrion he said no, and that God cursed the Jews who melted fat and sold it. It is the third of the hadiths Ibn Rajab added. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
