## Summary
Hadith of Ibn Abbas, a qudsi report from God: God records good and bad deeds, and whoever intends a good deed and does not do it has one full good deed recorded; whoever does it has ten up to seven hundred times recorded. Whoever intends an evil deed and does not do it has one good deed recorded; whoever does it has one evil deed recorded. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
