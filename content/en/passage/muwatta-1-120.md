## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the ablution of one in sexual impurity who wants to sleep or eat before bathing: when Abdullah ibn Umar was in sexual impurity and wanted to sleep or eat, he washed his face and hands to the elbows, wiped his head, and then ate or slept. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik, in al-Bayhaqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.93}
