## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, you have a good example in Abraham and those with him, when they said to their people, 'We are disassociated from you and from what you worship besides Allah. We have disbelieved in you, and enmity and hatred have appeared between us and you forever, until you believe in Allah alone.' Except for Abraham's words to his father, 'I will ask forgiveness for you, and I have no power for you from Allah. Our Lord, in You we rely, and to You we will return. And to You is the [return]': Allah says, the Exalted, For the believers among the companions of the Messenger of Allah: You have a good example for you, O believers: He says: A good example in Ibrahim, the Friend of the Merciful, to follow him and those who are with him among the prophets of Allah. As Ibn Zayd said regarding the saying of Allah, the Almighty: "You have a good example in Abraham and those with him," meaning those who were with him the prophets. And His saying: "When they said to their people, 'Indeed, we are disassociated from you and from what you worship besides Allah,'" means: when they said to their people who disbelieved in Allah and worshiped Taghut: O people, indeed we are disassociated from you and from those you worship besides Allah, from the gods and equals. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
