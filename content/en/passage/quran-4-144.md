**Commentary on verse 144: the prohibition on taking unbelievers as allies (pp. 336-337)**

God forbade the believers to take on the character of the hypocrites, who take unbelievers as allies instead of believers, lest they be like them in committing what He forbade, namely allying with His enemies. He threatened whoever took unbelievers as allies that He would join him to the hypocrites' allies, the people for whom the Prophet was told to announce painful punishment, if he did not desist. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 336}

Al-Tabari says a clear authority (sultan mubin) is clear proof. The sense is: do you want to give God proof against you by taking unbelievers as allies instead of believers, so that you deserve what the hypocrites deserve? Do not expose yourselves to God's anger by making proof against yourselves through disobeying what He forbade. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 337}

Report 10737, from Qatada, says God is the sovereign over His creatures, but He calls the clear demonstration a clear excuse. Report 10738, from Ikrima, says every 'sultan' in the Qur'an is proof; reports 10739 and 10740, from Mujahid through Ibn Abi Najih and Shibl, say the same. The editor notes that these reports show al-Tabari was abridging, since he did not give the evidence for reading sultan as proof in its earlier place. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 337}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
