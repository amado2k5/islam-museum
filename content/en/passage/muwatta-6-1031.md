## Summary
A saying of Malik: as for birds that harm, one in ihram may not kill them, except those the Prophet named, the crow and the kite; if one in ihram kills a bird other than those two, he ransoms it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1188) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.481}
