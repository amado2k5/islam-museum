## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 65 to 66 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, 'Say, I am only a warner, and there is no deity except Allah, the One, the Subduer.'" He explains: "Say," O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people: "I am only a warner." For you, O people of Quraysh, before a severe punishment is coming. I warn you of Allah's punishment and wrath that it may befall you for your disbelief in Him. So beware of it and hasten its arrival upon you with repentance. "There is no deity except Allah, the One, the Subduer." He means: There is no deity worthy of worship and worthy of lordship except Allah, to whom all things are subdued and all creation worships Him, the One who should not be He should have a partner in His dominion, and He should not have a mistress, the Subduer of all that is beneath Him by His power, the Lord of the heavens and the earth. He says: The Owner of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them of creation. He says: This is the One whose attribute is this, the God who has no deity but Him, not the One who owns nothing, neither harms nor benefits.
