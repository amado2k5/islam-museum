## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 36 to 37 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds": "So all praise is due to Allah" for His blessings and His hands in His creation. So be grateful to Him, O people, for all your blessings are from Him, beyond what you worship besides Him of gods and idols, and less than what you take as Lord besides Him, and associating partners with Him. "Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth" means: the Owner of the seven heavens and the Lord of the seven earths, and the Lord of the worlds says: The owner of all that is in them of all kinds of creation, and He holds pride in the heavens and the earth. He says: "To Him belongs greatness and authority in the heavens and the earth, unlike all the gods and rivals." "And He is the Almighty" in His punishment from His enemies, the Subduer of all that is beneath Him, and nothing can overcome Him. "The Wise" in managing His creation and managing them as He wills as He wills. And Allah knows best.
