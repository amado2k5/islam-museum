## Summary
A saying of Malik about a man in ihram for whom game was hunted, which was prepared for him, and he ate from it knowing that it had been hunted for him: he owes the expiation for all of that game. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1149) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.476}
