## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abd al-Hamid ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn al-Khattab, from Abdallah ibn Abdallah ibn al-Harith ibn Nawfal, from Ibn Abbas, that Umar ibn al-Khattab set out for Syria, and when he reached Sargh the military commanders, Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah and his companions, met him and told him that the plague had broken out in Syria. Umar consulted the first emigrants, who disagreed; then the Ansar, who disagreed; then the elders of Quraysh among the emigrants of the conquest, who agreed to return. Umar called out to people: I am setting off in the morning on the back of my mount, so set off in the morning. Abu Ubayda said: are you fleeing from the decree of God? Umar said: we flee from one decree of God to another. Then Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf came and said: I have knowledge of this; I heard the Messenger of God say: if you hear of it in a land, do not enter it; if it breaks out in a land where you are, do not leave it to flee from it. Umar praised God and went away. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.472}
