## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab say about the money-belt worn by one in ihram under his clothes: there is no harm in it, if he attached at both ends straps that tie one to another. Malik said: this is the most beloved to me of what I heard on it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1046) and Suwayd (490) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.439}
