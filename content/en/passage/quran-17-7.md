## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "If you do good, do good to yourselves; but if you do evil, it is for it. But when the promise of the Hereafter comes, they will disgrace your faces and enter the mosque as they entered it the first time, and they may disregard what they have exalted in honor." Allah says to the Children of Israel regarding what He decreed for them in the Torah: "If you do good," O Children of Israel, then obey Allah and set your affairs right, and adhere to His commands and prohibitions, "You have done well," and you have done what you have done "for yourselves," because you benefit yourselves by doing what you do, in this world and the Hereafter. As for this world, Allah will repel from you those who oppress you in harm, increase your wealth, and increase your strength. As for the Hereafter, Allah will reward you with His gardens: "And if you do wrong," He says: 'But if you disobey Allah and commit what He has forbidden you then, you will harm yourselves, because you are displeased with yourselves, your Lord. Your enemy will be imposed upon you in this world, and He will prevent you from wrongdoing, and will immortalize you in the Hereafter in humiliating punishment.'
