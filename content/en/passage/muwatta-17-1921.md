## Summary
Malik says: the explanation is that a man says to another: I take your goods for such and such, on condition that you advance me such and such. If the sale is concluded on this basis, it is not permitted. If the one who stipulated the advance drops the stipulation, then that sale is permitted. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.187}
