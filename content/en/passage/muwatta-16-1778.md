## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, and from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Aisha, mother of the believers, that the Messenger said: what birth forbids, fosterage forbids too. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the correct chain is Sulayman ibn Yasar from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, and that the mention of the conjunction in Yahya's book is an error; the report was also transmitted by Makhul al-Shami from Urwa. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.126}
