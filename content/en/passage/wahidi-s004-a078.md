## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nisa 4:78, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «أينما تكونوا يدرككم الموت».
