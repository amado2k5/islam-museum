## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 16 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “A soul will know what it has brought forth”: At that time, the soul will know what it has brought forth of good, which will lead it to Paradise, or evil, which will lead it to Hell. He says: At that time, what it was ignorant of will become clear to it, and what was beneficial for it will be revealed. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Rabi’, and Sufyan.
