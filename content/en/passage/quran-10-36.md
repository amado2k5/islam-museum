**Commentary on verse 36 (p. 89)**

On [[passage/quran-10-36|10:36]]: most of these polytheists follow only conjecture, that is, what they have no knowledge of in its truth or soundness, but are in doubt and suspicion about it. "Conjecture is of no avail against the truth": doubt does not take the place of certainty or avail where certainty is needed. "God is aware of what they do": God knows what these polytheists do in following conjecture and denying the certain truth, and He is watching them at a time when their conjecture will avail them nothing before Him. The editor refers to the glosses on "conjecture", "avail" and "aware". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 89}
