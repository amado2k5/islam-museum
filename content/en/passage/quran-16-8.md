## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "and horses, mules, and donkeys for you to ride and as adornment, and He creates what you do not know": He created horses, mules, and donkeys for you as well, "so that you may ride them, and as adornment," meaning: He made for you adornments with which you adorn yourself along with the benefits they contain, for riding and other things. The accusative of horses and mules is a conjunction with the letter 'h' and 'alif' in the phrase: "He created them." The accusative of adornment is with an implicit verb as explained, even if it does not have a waw. The speech was: "You would have put it in the accusative case that precedes it and is connected to it, but the inclusion of the waw indicates that it has a verbal pronoun and is disconnected from the verb before it." In the passage, statements are quoted from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
