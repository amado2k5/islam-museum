## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He it is Who created you from dust, then from a drop of fluid, then from a clot, then He brings you forth as a child, then you may reach your maturity, then you will be elderly, and among you will die before, and you will reach an appointed term that you may understand." Allah Almighty commands His Prophet Muhammad to warn the polytheists of His people about His arguments about His oneness: Say, O Muhammad, to your people: I was commanded to submit to the Lord of the Worlds, whose attributes are these, namely that He created your father Adam "from dust, then He created you" from a drop of fluid, then from 'Alaqah' after you were sperm, "then He will bring you forth as a child" from your mothers' wombs as young, "then you will reach your maturity," so your strength will be perfected, your youth will be complete, and your creation will be perfected as elders, "and among you will die before," to reach old age, "and you will reach an appointed term," meaning: and that you will reach a fixed term of your life, a limited term that you do not exceed, and do not advance before it, "and that you may understand."
