**Commentary on verse 29 (pp. 300-301)**

On [[passage/quran-11-29|11:29]] al-Tabari says: this too is God's report of Noah's saying to his people: my people, I ask you no wealth as a wage for my counsel to you and my call to God's oneness and pure worship of Him, so that you would accuse me in my counsel and think I act to seek something of the world's goods; 'my wage is only on God' means the reward of my counsel to you and my call is only upon God, who will repay and reward me for it; 'I am not one to drive away those who believe' means I will not thrust out whoever believed in God, acknowledged His oneness, cast off idols and renounced them because they are not among your elite and nobles; 'they will meet their Lord' means these whom you ask me to drive away are going to God, and God will ask them about what they did in the world, not about their rank and descent. Report 18112, from Ibn Jurayj: they said to him, Noah, if you wish us to follow you then drive them off, or we will not accept being equal with them in this matter; and he said 'I am not one to drive away those who believe, they will meet their Lord', who will ask them about their deeds. Reports 18113, 18114 and 18115, from Mujahid: 'my wage' is my recompense. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 301}

His words 'but I see you a people who are ignorant' mean: but I see you, people, ignorant of what is due from you of God's right and what is binding on you of His obligations; and through your ignorance you asked me to drive away those who believed in God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 301}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
