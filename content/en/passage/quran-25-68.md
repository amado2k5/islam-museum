## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 68 to 71 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that the servants of the Most Merciful do not worship any other god besides Allah and devote their worship to Him, nor do they kill the soul that Allah has forbidden to kill except by truth, which is disbelief after Islam, adultery after chastity, or killing a soul, and they do not commit adultery. Whoever does this will face sin, that is, punishment and retribution, which will double his punishment on the Day of Resurrection and he will remain humiliated therein. It is narrated from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid that some polytheists had increased killings and asked the Prophet about expiation for what they had done.
