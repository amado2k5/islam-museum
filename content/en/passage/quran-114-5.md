## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 10 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind": Say, O Muhammad, seek refuge in "the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind," and He is the Sovereign of all creation: humans, jinn, and others, informing him that whoever honored people and the believers their Lord was the king of those who honor Him, and that this is in His sovereignty and authority, His power flows upon Him, and that He is more worthy of honor and more deserving of worship than those who honor Him and worship Him than others.
