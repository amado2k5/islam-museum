## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And I will not call you to salvation and you call Me to the Fire": He, the Exalted, informs him of what this believer said to his people from the disbelievers: "Why do I call you to salvation?" from God's punishment and punishment by believing in Him, following His Messenger Moses, and believing in what He brought you from his Lord. "And you invite me to the Fire," meaning: and you invite me to the deeds of the people of the Fire. In this passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
