## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And as for those who disbelieved, were My verses not recited to you so that you acted arrogantly and were a criminal people": As for those who denied the oneness of Allah and refused to attribute His divinity in this world, it is said to them: Were not My verses recited to you in this world? If someone says: Or is it not a mother answered with a 'fa', then where is it? The answer is to say: it is the 'fa' in the phrase 'are I successful'.
