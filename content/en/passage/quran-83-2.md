## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 7 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Woe to those who give less than due measure” as: the valley that flows with the pus of the inhabitants of Hell at its lowest point, for those who give less than due measure, meaning: those who shortchange people and deprive them of their rights in their measures when they measure for them, or in their weights when they weigh for them, giving them less than what is due to them in full. The origin of this is something trifling, which is something small and insignificant. The one who gives less than due measure is the one who reduces the right of the one entitled to it from what is due to him in fullness in measure or weight. From this comes the saying for people who are equal in counting or number: they are equal like the overflowing measure, meaning: like the overflowing measure that is close to being full but not quite full. In the section, he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas.
