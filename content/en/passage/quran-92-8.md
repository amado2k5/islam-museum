## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 17 as one section. He explains His saying “By the night when it covers”: God Almighty says, swearing by the night when it covers the day with its darkness, so it takes away its light, and its darkness comes: “By the night when it covers” the day, and in the section he quotes sayings of Qatadah and Ibrahim.
