## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah takes the souls at the time of their death and those that did not die in their sleep, then He holds the one for whom death has ended and sends the others to a specified term. Indeed, in that are signs for a people who reflect." Among the indications that divinity is for Allah, the One, the Subduer, is pure and exclusive to all else, is that He causes death, gives life, does whatever He wills, and nothing else can do that. He made this a warning to them about His great power, saying: "God takes souls at the time of their death," so He takes them at the end of their term and the end of their life. He also takes away those who did not die in their sleep, just as those who died at their death, "and He holds the one whom death has decreed." It is mentioned that the souls of the living and the dead meet in dreams, and they become acquainted as much as God wills. If all of them want to return to their bodies, God seizes the souls of the dead with Him, imprisons them, and sends the souls of the living to return to their bodies for a specified period, until the end of their lives. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
