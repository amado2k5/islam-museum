## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And protect them from evil deeds. And whoever fears evil deeds that Day, mercy will be upon him. And that is the great attainment." Allah Almighty says about the saying of His angels: 'Warn them: turn away from them the evil consequences of their evil consequences that they committed before their repentance and repentance.' They say: Do not hold them accountable for that, so you punish them by it. "And whoever avoids evil deeds that Day, mercy will be granted to him." He means: Whoever avoids the evil consequences of his evil deeds on the Day of Resurrection, You will have mercy on him, and You will save him from Your punishment. "And that is the great attainment," because whoever is saved from the Fire and admitted to Paradise has indeed succeeded. There is no doubt that it is a great win. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
