## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, from Aisha, wife of the Prophet, that she used to leave off the talbiya when she returned to the standing place. The editor notes that one copy reads 'went' instead of 'returned', and that Abu Musab (1091), Suwayd and al-Shaybani (390) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.455}
