## Summary
Malik transmits from Urwa ibn Udhayna al-Laythi that he went out with a grandmother of his who had vowed to walk to the House of God; when they were on part of the road she grew weak, and she sent a client of hers to ask Abdullah ibn Umar. He went out with him and asked him, and Ibn Umar said to him: tell her to ride, and then walk from where she grew weak. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.607}
