## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah set forth an example of two men, one of whom is mute and unable to do anything, while he is all dependent on his Lord wherever He directs him, but he does not bring any good. Is he equal and he who enjoins justice while he is on a straight path": This is the parable Allah set for Himself and the gods worshipped besides Him, as He said: "And Allah set forth an example of two men, one of whom is mute and unable to do anything." This means that the idol hears nothing and does not speak, because it is either carved wood or made copper that cannot It benefits those who serve him and does not pay harm for him. "And he is all dependent on his master." He means: He is dependent on his cousin, his allies, and his guardians. Likewise, an idol is dependent on those who worship it, needing to carry, place, and serve it, like the mute person who cannot do anything, so he is all over his guardians from his uncles and others. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, and Ibn Jurayj.
