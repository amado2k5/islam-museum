## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 91 to 93 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, everyone in the heavens and the earth will come to the Most Merciful as a servant": All who are in the heavens, angels, humans, and jinn on earth—except that the Most Merciful will come to him as a servant, meaning: will his Lord come on the Day of Resurrection as a servant, humble and submissive, acknowledged in servitude, with no relation between him and him. And His saying: "He came to the Most Merciful" is only the doer of the one I come, so I come to him.
