## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 60 to 62 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Are our gods better, or is it they who argued against you except as a dispute? Rather, they are a people of disputes": And the polytheists of your people, O Muhammad, said: Are our gods whom we worship better, or Muhammad? So we worship Muhammad and abandon our gods? He mentioned that this is in the reading of Abu ibn Ka'b: "Are our gods better or this?" The narration is mentioned accordingly: from Qatadah that in the letter of Abu ibn Ka'b, 'and they said, 'Is our god better or this?' meaning Muhammad. Others said: Rather, what I mean by that is: 'Are our gods better or Jesus?' In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
