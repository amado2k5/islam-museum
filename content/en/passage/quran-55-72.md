## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 72 to 75 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "houris confined in tents": He, the Exalted, informs about these good things, "hour," meaning by the word "houris: white, which is the plural of houris, and houris means white." We have previously explained the meaning of houris with sufficient evidences that do not require repeating them here. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Rabi', and Sufyan.
