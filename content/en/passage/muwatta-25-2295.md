## Summary
Malik said of a man who had intercourse with a woman under his contract of manumission: if she becomes pregnant, she has the choice; if she wishes she becomes an umm walad, and if she wishes she remains under her contract. If she does not become pregnant, she remains under her contract. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.346}
