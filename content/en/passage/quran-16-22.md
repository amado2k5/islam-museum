## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Your God is one God, but those who do not believe in the Hereafter have hearts in denial and are arrogant": Your deity is the one who deserves worship and dedication to Him alone, not all other things, because worship is only suitable for Him. So dedicate obedience to Him and dedicate worship to Him and do not associate with Him any partner. "So those who do not believe in the Hereafter, their hearts are detestable." Allah says: Those who do not believe in Allah's promise and warning and do not acknowledge the Resurrection after death, their hearts are detestable. Allah says: They deplore what Allah's power, greatness, and beauty have befallen them, and that worship is only suitable for Him and divinity is for nothing else. He says: They are arrogant in devouring Allah alone and acknowledging His oneness, following what their predecessors did of associating partners with Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
