**Commentary on verse 3 (pp. 18-19)**

He then explains "Your Lord is God, who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then established Himself on the Throne, directing the matter; there is no intercessor except after His permission". Your Lord, to whom all worship belongs and to no one else, is the One who created the seven heavens and the seven earths in six days, alone, with no partner or helper, then established Himself on His throne, directing affairs and deciding in His creation what He wills; no one opposes His decree, no one reviews His ordering, and no flaw enters His affairs. The editor refers to the earlier volumes for "establishing" and "the Throne". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 18}

On "there is no intercessor except after His permission": no intercessor will intercede with Him on the Day of Resurrection for anyone except after He permits the intercession; the editor refers to the glosses on "intercession" and "permission". "That is God, your Lord" means the One with these qualities is your master and patron, not the gods and idols that neither hear, see, direct nor decide; "so worship Him" means be sincere in worship of Him and make divinity and lordship His alone, in humility before Him and not before your idols and whatever else you join with Him in worship; "will you not remember" means will you not take heed of these signs and proofs, turn to acknowledge your Lord's oneness, put away the rivals and renounce them? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 19}

The interpreters said the same. Reports 17543, 17544, 17545, 17546 and 17547, all from Mujahid by several routes, say that "directing the matter" means He decides it alone; report 17546 opens its chain with dots, marking it as the same beginning as the one before. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 19}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
