## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He created you from a single soul, then made from it its mate, and sent down to you from the cattle eight pairs. He creates you in the wombs of your mothers as a creation after being created in three darknesses. That is Allah, your Lord. To Him belongs the dominion; there is no deity except Him. So how will you act?" "He created you, O people, from one soul," meaning from Adam, "then made from it its mate." He means: Then He made Adam His wife Eve, because God created her from one of His ribs. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
