## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Did you fear that you would give charity before your private conversations? But when you did not, and Allah has accepted your repentance, then establish prayer and give zakah and obey Allah and His Messenger. And Allah is Acquainted with what you do": It is difficult for you, O believers, that you would give charity to the Messenger of Allah for need. The root of compassion in Arabic speech is fear and caution, and its meaning here is: you feared giving charity to the needy and needy. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
