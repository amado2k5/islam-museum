## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abdallah ibn Amir ibn Rabia, that Umar ibn al-Khattab set out for Syria. When he reached Sargh he was told that the plague had broken out in Syria. Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf told him that the Messenger of God said: if you hear of it in a land, do not enter it; if it breaks out in a land where you are, do not leave it to flee from it. Umar turned back from Sargh. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.476}
