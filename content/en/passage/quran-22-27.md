## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 29 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And proclaim to the people the pilgrimage; they will come to you on foot and on every camel coming from every deep pass": We also know that he proclaimed Hajj among the people by saying: "And he proclaimed" the most knowing and calling among the people to perform Hajj, O people, the Sacred House of Allah. "They come to you on foot" means: people come to the House you command them to perform Hajj on foot, "and on every camel (on every camel)" means: and mounts on every camels, which are the camels that are the slack. "They come from every deep pass" means: these camels come from every deep pass, meaning from every road, place, and distant path. It was said: "they come," so the plural because by each 'dhamir' is meant camels. The meaning of 'all' is plural, so it is said: "they come." Al-Farra' claimed it is rare in Arab speech: 'I passed by every standing man,' which is correct, and God's saying: 'And on every close one they come' indicates the validity of its permissibility. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibrahim, and Ata.
