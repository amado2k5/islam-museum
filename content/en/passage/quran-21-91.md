## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And the one who guarded her private parts, so We breathed into her of Our spirit and made her and her son a sign for the worlds": And remember the one whose private parts were fortified, meaning Mary bint Imran. And by "she protected," he means she preserved and withheld her private parts from what Allah had forbidden her to permit. There is disagreement about the private parts that Allah, Glorified and Exalted, meant that she protected him; some said: He meant her private parts by protecting her from immorality. Others said: This means that the cab of her armor was prevented from Gabriel before she knew he was the messenger of her Lord and before she confirmed him in a certain way. They said: What indicates this is the saying: "So We breathed into her," followed by the phrase: "And the one whose private parts were fortified." They said: It was understood from this that the meaning of the phrase "the one whose pocket was fortified is 'so We breathed into her of Our spirit.'
