**Commentary on verse 98 (pp. 466-467)**

On [[passage/quran-11-98|11:98]] al-Tabari says: Pharaoh will go before his people on the Day of Resurrection, leading them and going with them to the Fire until he brings them to it and has them burn in its blaze; 'and wretched is the place to which they come'. Report 18531, from Qatada: Pharaoh will go before his people on the Day of Resurrection, walking ahead of them until he leads them into the Fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 466}

Report 18532, from Qatada: he leads his people; 18533, from Ibn Abbas: he misled them and brought them to the Fire; 18534, from Ibn Abbas: the coming is the entering; 18535, from al-Dahhak: Ibn Abbas used to say there are four comings in the Qur'an, in Hud 'wretched is the place to which they come', in Maryam 'there is none of you but will come to it' in [[passage/quran-19-71|19:71]], in al-Anbiya' 'you are fuel for Hell, you will come to it' in [[passage/quran-21-98|21:98]], and again in Maryam 'We drive the wrongdoers to Hell as thirsty herds' in [[passage/quran-19-86|19:86]]; he said all this is entering, and by God every righteous and every wicked will come to Hell, then We save those who feared, as in [[passage/quran-19-72|19:72]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 467}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
