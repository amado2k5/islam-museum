## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Until when he comes to us," he says, "If only between me and you after the two easts, then what a wretched companion": Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase: "until when he comes to us," but most reciters of the Hijaz except Ibn Muhaysin, some Kufans, and some Syrians read it as "until when he comes to us" in the dual form meaning: until this one who is distracted from the mention of the Most Merciful, and his companion who is set for him among the devils. Most of the reciters of Kufa and Basra, as well as Ibn Muhaysin, read this as: until when He comes to us upon monotheism, meaning: until this tenth of the children of Adam comes to us from the mention of the Most Merciful. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
