## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Muhammad ibn Umara, from Abu Bakr ibn Hazm, that Uthman ibn Affan said: when boundaries are set in the land, there is no pre-emption in it; and there is no pre-emption in a well, nor in a palm stallion. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256}
