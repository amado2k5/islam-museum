## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 141 to 145 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Thamud denied the messengers": Thamud denied the messengers of Allah when Salih, their brother, called them to Allah, and said to them: Do you not fear Allah's punishment, O people, for your disobedience to Him and your disobedience to His command? By obeying the matter of the corrupters on Allah's earth, "Indeed, I am to you a messenger" from Allah. He sent me to you warning you of His punishment for disobeying you. He commanded 'Amen' regarding His message which He sent with me to you: 'So fear Allah, O people, and beware of His punishment and obey Him in my warning you.' Your Lord commanded to follow His obedience 'and I ask you for no reward for it,' meaning: And I do not ask you for my advice to you and to warn you of reward or recompense. "My reward is only with the Lord of the worlds," meaning: My reward and recompense are only with the Lord of all that is in the heavens, whatever is on the earth, and all that is between them of creation.
