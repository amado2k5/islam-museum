## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is Jesus, son of Mary, the word of truth in which they are tested": This is the one whose attribute I have shown you and informed you of, from the matter of the boy whom Mary carried is Jesus, son of Mary, and this is his attribute, and this report is his report, which is the "statement of truth," meaning that this report which I told you is the word of truth, and the words I recited to you are the word and report of God, not any other report, which is subject to doubt, increase, and subtraction, as God Almighty used to say: So say about Jesus, O people, this is the statement that Allah informed you about, not what the Jews said, who claimed he was immature, that he was a lying magician, nor what the Christians said, that he had a son for God, that God did not take a son, and that it should not be for Him. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
