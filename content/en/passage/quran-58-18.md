## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "the Day Allah will resurrect them all and they will swear to Him as they swear to you and think they are against something, but they are the liars": those whom He mentioned are the companions of the Fire, the Day Allah will resurrect them all, and the Day of the Companions of the Fire. And what he meant by His saying, 'On the Day when Allah will resurrect them all,' from their graves alive as they were before their death, they will swear to Him as they swear to you liars and false therein, as from Ma'mar, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: 'and they will swear to Him,' he said: The hypocrite will swear to him on the Day of Resurrection as he swore to his allies in this world. And from Qatadah, regarding the saying: 'The Day Allah will resurrect them all'... The verse, and Allah swore to their Lord on the Day of Resurrection, just as they swore to His allies in this world. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Samak, and Sufyan.
