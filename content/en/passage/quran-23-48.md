## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 48 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "so they denied them and were among the destroyers": Pharaoh and his followers denied Moses and Aaron, and they were among those whom God destroyed as He destroyed those before them of nations by denying their messengers.
