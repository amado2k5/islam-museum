## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 172 to 175 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We sent down upon them rain, but evil was the rain of the warners": That is why God sent upon them stones from the sky. "So evil is the rain of the warners." He means: How wretched is that rain of the people to whom their prophet warned but denied it.
