## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, when it is said to you to make room in the assemblies, then make room; Allah will make room for you. And when it is said, 'Turn away, then disobey.' Allah raises those of you who have believed and those who have been given knowledge, degrees. And Allah is Acquainted with what you do": O you who have spoken the truth of Allah and His Messenger, "When it is said to you to make room in the assemblies," it means by saying 'make room' (make room) in their words, a spacious place if it is spacious. The interpreters differed regarding the gathering in which Allah commanded the believers to be open; some said: That was the Prophet's gathering specifically. In the excerpt, statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
