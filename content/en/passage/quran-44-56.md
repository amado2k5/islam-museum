## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 59 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Thus and We married them to the seas of 'Ayn": Just as We gave these righteous in the Hereafter honor by admitting them to Gardens and clothing them in the gardens of beauty and adorning them, so too We honored them by marrying them there to houris among the pure white women, whose singular is Hoora. Mujahid used to say about the meaning of houris: and from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "And We married them to the seas of 'Ayn," he said: We married them to Hoor. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
