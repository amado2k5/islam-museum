## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions the difference among the reciters regarding 'Uswa' with damma and kasra, and explains that it is a reproach from Allah to those who lag behind the Prophet, as they had a good example for those who hope in Allah and the Last Day. He then mentions that when the believers saw the confederates, they said, 'This is what Allah and His Messenger promised us, and Allah and His Messenger have spoken the truth,' and it only increased them in faith and submission.
