## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 93 to 95 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Yahrun, what prevented you when you saw them go astray?" Moses said to his brother Aaron when he finished addressing his people and reviewing them for their mistake: "O Aaron, what prevented you when you saw them go astray from their religion? They disbelieved in Allah and worshipped the swift that you should not follow me." Interpreters differ on the meaning of what Moses punished his brother for abandoning his followers. Some said: He was excused for abandoning him by following those who followed him as he was promised. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
