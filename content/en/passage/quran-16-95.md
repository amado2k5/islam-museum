## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 95 to 96 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And do not exchange for the covenant of Allah for a small price; it is only better for you with Allah, if you only knew." By breaking God's covenant in this world and the Hereafter, there is great reward in the Hereafter for fulfilling it. Then Allah Almighty explained the difference between the two rewards and the virtue between the two rewards, saying: What you have, O people, of what you possess in this world, even if it is abundant, will perish. And what Allah has for those who fulfill His covenant and obey Him of good deeds will remain but will perish. So when it is with Him, then act upon the everlasting remainder that never perishes, so be keen.
