## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, O people, I am only to you a clear warner": Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people who dispute with you about Allah without knowledge, following every devil who desires them: "O mankind, I am only to you a clear warner." I warn you that God's punishment will come upon you in this world and His punishment in the Hereafter by reaching Him (Mubabin). He says: I will make clear to you my warning and make it clear so that you may repent from your polytheism and beware of what I have warned you about. I have no other power over you. As for hastening and delaying the punishment you are hastening with, that is not for me and I am not able to do it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
