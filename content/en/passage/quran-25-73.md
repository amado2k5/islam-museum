## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who, when they are reminded of the signs of their Lord, do not fall upon them deaf and blind": And those who, when reminded of God's proofs, are not deaf and do not hear, nor blind who do not see them. But they awaken hearts, they understand what Allah reminds them of, and they understand from Him what alerts them to Him, so they are aware of admonition with ears that have heard it and hearts with its awareness. In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
