## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on how to deal with someone overcome by blood from a wound or a nosebleed. Yahya transmits from Malik from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father that al-Miswar ibn Makhrama told him he entered on Umar ibn al-Khattab on the night he was stabbed. Umar woke him for the dawn prayer, saying yes, and that there is no share in Islam for one who leaves prayer; he prayed while his wound was bleeding. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it through several chains that do not go through Malik, including that al-Miswar and Ibn Abbas entered on Umar when he was stabbed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.81}
