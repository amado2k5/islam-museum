**Commentary on verse 49 (p. 100)**

On [[passage/quran-10-49|10:49]] he says: say, Muhammad, to those who hurry the threat of God and ask when the promise will come: I do not own for myself harm or benefit, in worldly affairs or in religion, except what God wills me to own and bring about by His leave. Since I cannot do that except by His leave, I am all the more unable to reach knowledge of the unseen and of when the Hour comes, except by His will and permission. "For every nation there is a term": every people has a time at which its span ends, and when the time of their end and the close of their lives comes, they are not delayed an hour or brought forward, because God decreed that it would not come before the time He set. The editor refers to the glosses on "owning", "nation" and "term". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 100}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
