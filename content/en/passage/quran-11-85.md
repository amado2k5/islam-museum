**Commentary on verse 85 (pp. 446-447)**

On [[passage/quran-11-85|11:85]] al-Tabari relates Shu'ayb's words to his people: give people full measure and weight 'with justice', that is, give those owed what is measured or weighed their rights in the full amount due, without cheating or shortfall. 'And do not defraud people of their things' means do not diminish the rights you must pay them in measure, weight or otherwise; report 18473, from 'Ali b. Salih b. Hayy: do not reduce them; 18474, from Qatada: do not wrong people of their things. 'And do not act corruptly on the earth, spreading mischief' means do not go about the earth working God's disobedience in it; report 18475, from Qatada: do not go about the earth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 446}

Report 18476, from al-Dahhak: do not strive on the earth as corrupters, meaning short measure and weight. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 447}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
