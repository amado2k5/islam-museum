**Commentary on verse 33 (p. 85)**

On [[passage/quran-10-33|10:33]]: just as these polytheists have been turned from the truth to error, so "the word of your Lord has come due" upon those who act wickedly, meaning His decree and judgement in His prior knowledge became due upon those who left obedience for disobedience and disbelieved; "that they do not believe" means they do not accept God's oneness or the prophethood of His Prophet. The editor refers to the gloss on "wickedness". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 85}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
