## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And when the people are gathered together, they will be enemies to them and disbelievers in their worship": And when the people are gathered on the Day of Resurrection for the Day of Reckoning, these gods they call upon in this world will be enemies to them, because they disown them, "and they were disbelievers in their worship." Allah says: Their instruments which they worshiped in this world were ungrateful because they will say on the Day of Resurrection: We did not command them to worship Us nor to recognize their worship of Us; we disassociated ourselves from You, our Lord.
