## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 76 in a single passage. He explains the verse: "He said, 'O Iblis, what prevented you from prostrating when You were created by My hands, were you arrogant or were you among the highest?': Allah said to Iblis, when he did not prostrate to Adam and disobeyed His command: 'O Iblis, what prevented you from prostrating?' He says: What prevented you from prostrating 'when You were created with My Hand?' He says: 'To the creation of My hand,' Allah Almighty informs that He created Adam with His own hands, as the servants of the office told me, he said: I heard a Mujahid narrate from Ibn Umar, who said: God created four with His hand: the Throne, 'Aden, the Pen, and Adam. Then He said, 'Be, for everything, and it was.'
