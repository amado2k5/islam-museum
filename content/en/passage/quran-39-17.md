## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 16–18. Al-Tabari describes the state of the losers in Hell; above them are shades of the Fire and shades beneath them. This is what Allah fears His servants. O servants, fear Him. And those who avoid Taghut (false gods) and turn to Allah will have glad tidings, so give glad tidings to the servants of those who listen to the word and follow the best of it.
