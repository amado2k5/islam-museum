## Summary
A saying of Malik: if a pregnant woman comes from enemy land and gives birth in Arab land, the child is hers. It inherits from her if she dies, and she inherits from it if it dies, and its inheritance is in God's book. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.24}
