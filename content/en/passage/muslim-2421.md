## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra and al-Bara' ibn Azib in the chapter on the virtues of al-Hasan and al-Husayn, in Sahih Muslim. The Prophet prayed that God love al-Hasan and carried him on his shoulder. It is the text on the Prophet's love for the two grandsons. {cite:sahih-muslim}
