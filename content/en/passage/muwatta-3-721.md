## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Umar ibn al-Khattab drank milk and liked it, and asked the one who gave it to him where this milk was from. He told him it came from a water-point, where he found camels of the charity herd being watered, and they milked some for him and he put it in his waterskin. Umar put his hand in and vomited it out. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.362}
