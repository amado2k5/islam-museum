## Summary
A report of Abd al-Rahman ibn Samura in the chapter on the recommendation of making expiation for an oath and doing good instead of it, in the Book of Oaths of Sahih Muslim. Two well-known counsels: do not seek leadership, and when you swear and then see better, do the good and make the expiation. {cite:sahih-muslim}
