## Summary
Verse 42 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 42 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "those who believe and do righteous deeds, We charge no soul beyond its capacity, those are the companions of Paradise, abiding in it", God says: those who affirmed God and His Messenger and acknowledged what came to them of His revelation, His sending down and the laws of His religion, and did what God commanded them, obeying Him, and avoided what He forbade them, "We charge no soul beyond its capacity" means We charge no soul with deeds except what it can bear, with no hardship in it; "those" means these who believed and did righteous deeds, "are the companions of Paradise", that is, they are the people of Paradise, who are its people, not others who disbelieved in God and did evil deeds; "abiding in it" means they stay in Paradise, their stay in it everlasting, never leaving it or being stripped of its bliss. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 437}
