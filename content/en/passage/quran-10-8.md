**Commentary on verse 8 (pp. 26)**

On "those, their refuge is the Fire": those with these qualities have as their refuge, that is their destination, the fire of hell in the next life "for what they used to earn" in the world of sins and crimes and evil deeds they committed. The editor refers to the glosses on "refuge" and "earning". The Arabs say "so-and-so does not hope for so-and-so" when he does not fear him, and the Qur'anic "What is the matter with you that you do not hope for dignity from God" in Surat Nuh is of that kind; he cites a line by Abu Dhu'ayb al-Hudhali on the same sense, about a man not afraid of being stung by bees (described, not quoted), which the editor traces to the ninth volume. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 26}
