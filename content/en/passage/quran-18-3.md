## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 3 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "they will remain therein forever" as eternal beings, neither departing from it nor transmitting it, and in the accusative case of "those who remain in the state" from the phrase: "that they will have a good reward" in this state, if they remain in that reward. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
