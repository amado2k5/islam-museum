## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 26-28 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “So when they saw it, they said, ‘Indeed, we are lost’”: When these people reached their garden and saw its crops burned, they did not recognize it and doubted whether it was their garden or not. Some of them said to their companions, thinking they had lost their way to their garden and that what they saw was another: “O people, we are lost to the path to our garden.” But those who knew it was their garden and that they had not gone astray said: “Rather, O people, we are deprived; we have been deprived of the benefit of our garden because its crops have been destroyed.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
