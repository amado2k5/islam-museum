**Commentary on verse 132 (p. 49)**

Al-Tabari says: Pharaoh's people said to Moses, whatever sign and proof you bring us "to bewitch us with it", meaning to turn us by it from the religion of Pharaoh we follow, "we shall not believe in you", that is, we shall not hold you true that you are right in what you call us to. He has shown the meaning of "sorcery" earlier; the editor refers to p. 27. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 49}

Report 14988, from Ibn Zayd through Yunus (the editor restores a bracketed link in the chain which he calls a copyist's slip): "whatever sign you bring us" is originally "if what you bring us of a sign", with an added "what". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 49}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
