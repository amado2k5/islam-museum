## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Allah has heard the words of the one who disputes with you concerning her husband and complains to Allah, and Allah hears your dialogue. Indeed, Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing." He explains: "Allah has heard," O Muhammad, "the one who disputed with you about her husband," and the woman from the Ansar used to argue with the Messenger of Allah about her husband. Scholars differ regarding her lineage and name; some said: Khawlah bint Tha'labah, others said: Her name is Khuwaylah bint Tha'labah. Others said: She is Khuwaylah bint Khuwaylid. Others said: She is Khuwaylah bint al-Samit. Others said: She is Khuwaylah, daughter of al-Dulayj, and the Messenger of Allah disputed her husband, and her husband was Aws ibn al-Samit, her confrontation with him about his matter, and what he said to her: 'You are like my mother's back to me.' And he debated her about this, and this is what the interpreters said, and the narration supported it. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
