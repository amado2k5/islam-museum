## Summary
Yahya transmits that it reached him that a man came to Abdullah ibn Umar and said: I gave my wife authority over her affair, and she divorced herself. What do you think? Ibn Umar said he thought it was as she said. The man said: do not do that, Abu Abd al-Rahman. Ibn Umar said: me do it? You did it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.62}
