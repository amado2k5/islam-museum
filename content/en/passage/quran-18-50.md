## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And [mention] when We said to the angels, 'Prostrate to Adam,' and they prostrated except Iblis; he was of the jinn and disobeyed the command of his Lord. Will you take him and his descendants as allies besides Me? And they are to you an enemy evil for the wrongdoers in return": Allah Almighty reminds these polytheists of the envy of Iblis their father and their teacher of his arrogance and arrogance towards him when he commanded him to prostrate to him, and that it is out of enmity and envy towards them over the same as he was to their father: And remember, O Muhammad, "When We said to the angels, 'Prostrate to Adam,' they prostrated except Iblis," which these polytheists obey, follow his command, and oppose God's command, for he did not prostrate to him out of arrogance toward God and envy of Adam, "he was among the jinn." Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase "he was from the jinn." Some said: He belonged to a tribe called the jinn. Others said: Rather, he was from the reservoir of Paradise, so he was attributed to Paradise. Others said: Rather, it was said to be from the jinn, because he was among the jinn who were protected from the eyes of the children of Adam. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, Al-A'mash, and others.
