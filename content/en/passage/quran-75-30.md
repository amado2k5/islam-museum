## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30-34 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Nay, when the soul reaches the collarbones" as follows: The matter is not as these polytheists think, that they will not be punished for their polytheism and disobedience to their Lord. Rather, when the soul of one of them reaches the collarbones at the time of death and rattles in the throat, he will be punished. Ibn Zayd said regarding God's statement, "Nay, when the soul reaches the collarbones," that the collarbones refer to the soul itself. Ibn Zayd also quotes in this section statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, al-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd, Simak, and Sufyan.
