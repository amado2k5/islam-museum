## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Did they not travel through the land and see how was the end of those before them? They were stronger and more influential in the land, so Allah seized them for their sins, and they had no protection from Allah": Or did these dwellers not travel in their polytheism with Allah, the deniers of His Messenger from Quraysh, in the lands? "So they will see what was the end of those before them?" He means: So see what was the end of the nations of those before them who followed their path in disbelief. By God, and denying His messengers: "They were stronger than them." He says: Those nations before them were more oppressive than them and left traces on earth. Their strength and the strength of their bodies did not benefit them, as the command of God came to them, seizing them for what they had committed and what they had earned from their sins, but He wiped out their gatherings, and their dwellings became empty of them because of their wrongdoing. "And they had no protection against God," meaning: And they had no punishment from God when it came to them, any shield to protect them and repel it, as in the passage, and in the passage he quotes from Qatadah.
