## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Allah sends down water from the sky and revives the earth after its death. Indeed, in that is a sign for a people who listen." He says, the Exalted, alerting His creation to His proof against His oneness, and that no divinity is worthy except for Him, and that worship is not suitable for anything else: O people, your deity is the One to whom worship is above all else. "He sent down water from the sky," meaning rain. He says: So He caused the dead earth to grow by what He sent down from that water from the sky, which has neither cultivation nor grass nor vegetation "after its death." After it is dead and nothing in it—'Indeed, in that is a sign.' Allah says: 'Indeed, in Our resurrection the earth after its death by sending down water from the sky as a clear proof and a conclusive proof. Excuse for those who think about it, 'for a people who hear,' meaning: for a people who hear this statement, reflect upon it, understand it, and obey Allah by what He has shown them.
