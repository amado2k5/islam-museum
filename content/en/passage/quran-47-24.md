## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Do they not reflect on the Qur'an, or upon hearts that have closed it": Do these hypocrites not reflect upon the admonitions of God that He admonishes them in the verses of the Qur'an revealed to His Prophet, and reflect on the proofs He has made clear to them in its revelation, so they know by them the error of what they are steadfast? "Or are they locked upon their hearts?" He says: Or is God closed upon their hearts so they do not understand what God revealed in His Book of admonitions and lessons? In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
