## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 30 to 32 in a single passage: When God created mankind and breathed spirit into him after He had made him do so, all the angels prostrated except Iblis, who refused to be with those who prostrated to Adam out of arrogance, envy, and arrogance. God said to him, "O Iblis, why should you not be with those who prostrate?" meaning what prevented you from being with them. In the grammar of "an," two opinions are presented: according to some grammarians of Kufa, it is in the case of a lowered case, and according to some people of Basra, it is accusative with the loss of the lowering vowel.
