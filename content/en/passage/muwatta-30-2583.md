## Summary
Malik said: in deliberate killing, the claimants swear the oath of fifty only if they are two or more; the oaths are returned to them until they swear fifty oaths, and then they are entitled to the blood. That is the matter among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.456}
