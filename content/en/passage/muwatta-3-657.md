## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: no zakat is due on property until a year has passed over it. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, al-Shafii and al-Shaybani transmitted it; that it is reported raised but is not sound; and cites al-Daraqutni (2/90). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.336}
