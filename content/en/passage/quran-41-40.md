## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who deviate in Our verses are not hidden from Us. Is there anyone who will be cast into the Fire better, or will he come in safety on the Day of Resurrection? Do whatever you wish. Indeed, He is Seeing of what you do": He means by saying: "Indeed, those who deviate from the truth in Our proofs and evidences, and turn away from them, denying and denying them." I have previously explained the meaning of 'burial' with its evidences that do not require repeating it here. We will mention some differences among those who differ regarding what is meant by it and its meaning in this context. Interpreters differ on what is meant by it in this context, with some saying: It is meant to oppose the polytheists to the Qur'an with confusion and whistling in mockery of it. In the passage, statements are quoted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
