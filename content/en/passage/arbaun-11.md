## Summary
Hadith of al-Hasan ibn Ali, called the grandson and fragrance of the Messenger of God: "Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt; truthfulness is peace and lying is doubt". Its transmission is disputed, and Ibn Rajab reads it as a call to avoid doubtful matters. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
