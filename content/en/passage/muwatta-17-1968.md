## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger said: the delay of a rich man is injustice, and if one of you is referred to a creditworthy person, let him accept the referral. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.205}
