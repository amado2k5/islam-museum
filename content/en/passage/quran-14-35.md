## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains Abraham's prayer that God make the sacred territory, the Haram, a safe land, and keep him and his sons far from worshipping idols. He defines an idol (sanam) as a carved image, and anything not carved as a wathan. Mujahid reports that the prayer was answered, and that none of Abraham's descendants worshipped an idol after it.
