## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 35 and 36 together: He mentions that 'this city' means the sanctuary. He prayed to Abraham that God would make it safe, his family, and its inhabitants. He explains 'ajnabni' linguistically: I kept evil away from him with a major impurity, 'I avoided him with a janab' (a jiban), and 'janabat janaban' (a jibanuhu). He cites a poet's verse, meaning 'distance me and build from idol worship'; idols are plural of idols, which is the depicted statue, and he cites a verse by Ru'bah ibn al-'Ajjaj. Mujahid narrates that God answered Abraham's call regarding his son, so none of them worshipped an idol after him, and that an idol is a depicted statue, and anything that is not an idol is an idol; and that He also responded to it, provided for the city, provided its people with fruits, made him an imam, made his descendants establish prayer, accept his prayers, see his rituals, and repent to him. He narrates from Ibrahim Al-Taymi in his stories: Who will be safe from affliction after God's friend Abraham while praying for himself and his children with that?
