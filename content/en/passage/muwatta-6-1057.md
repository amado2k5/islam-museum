## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on hastening (ramal) in circumambulation. Malik transmits from Jafar ibn Muhammad, from his father, from Jabir ibn Abdullah, who said: I saw the Messenger of God hasten from the Black Stone until he reached it for three rounds. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1281) transmitted it from Malik, that it is in Muslim's Sahih from Ibn Umar and Jabir, and in Ahmad, Ibn Khuzayma, al-Shafii and others. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.489}
