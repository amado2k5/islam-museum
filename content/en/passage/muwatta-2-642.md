## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said that the earth consumes every son of Adam except the coccyx bone; from it he was created, and from it he will be reassembled. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik, followed by Ibn Hibban, Suwayd, al-Qanabi in Abu Dawud, Ibn Wahb in al-Tahawi, and Qutayba in al-Nasai. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.328}
