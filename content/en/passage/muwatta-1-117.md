## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say that the bath becomes obligatory when the circumcised part passes the circumcised part, in the chapter on what makes the bath obligatory when the two circumcised parts meet. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ibn Jurayj and from Abdullah ibn Umar al-Umari, and Ibn Abi Shayba from Ubayd Allah ibn Umar, all from Nafi'. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.92}
