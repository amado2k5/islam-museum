## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And indeed, in the cattle you have a lesson: We will give you to drink from what is in their bellies, from between the filth and blood, pure milk that flows for the drinkers": And indeed, O people, you have a lesson regarding the cattle We give you to drink from what is in their bellies. Reciters differed in the interpretation of the phrase: "We will give you drink," but the majority of the people of Mecca, Iraq, Kufa, and Basra read it except for Asim, and from the people of Medina Abu Ja'far: "We will give you water" with a damma on the nun. Meaning: He gave them constant drink. Al-Kisai used to say: The Arabs say: We gave them a river and we gave them milk when you made it a constant drink. If they wanted to give them a drink, they would say: We gave them water, so we give them water without an alif. Most of the reciters of Medina except Abu Ja'far recited this, and from the people of Iraq, Asim: 'We water you' with a fatha on the nun, whoever is watered by Allah is given water. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Al-Kisai.
