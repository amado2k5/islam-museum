## Summary
Verse 123 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 123 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 123, "thus We made in every town its chief wrongdoers to plot in it; they plot only against themselves and do not perceive": al-Tabari says: as We adorned for the unbelievers what they did, so We placed in every town its great ones as wrongdoers, meaning people of idolatry and disobedience, to plot in it with deceptive speech or false deeds against God's religion and His prophets; "they plot only against themselves" means their plotting recoils only on them, because God is behind punishing them for barring His path; "and they do not perceive" means they do not know what painful punishment God prepared, so they carry on in their error. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 93}

He transmits from Mujahid (13847 and 13848) and Qatada (13849) that "chief wrongdoers" means its great ones; and from Ikrima (13850) that it came down about the mockers, and that "to plot in it" means against God's religion, His Prophet and His believing servants. Al-Tabari explains that "akabir" is the plural of "akbar" as "afadil" is of "afdal", and that it would also be right to take it as the plural of "kabir"; the Arabs were heard to say "akabira" and "asagira", and likewise "ahamir" and "ahamira", "asawid" and "asawida". He cites a verse of al-A'sha that mentions the wealth consumed by three red things; the editor's note discusses the variants of the line, the word "mubaqqa'" and the habit of saffron use at weddings. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 94}

As for "makr", it is deception and scheming against the one plotted against, through treachery, so that the schemer entangles him in something hateful. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 95}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
