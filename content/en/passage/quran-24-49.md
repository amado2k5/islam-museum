## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And if they have the right, they come to it submissively": And if the truth belongs to those who call to Allah and His Messenger to judge between them, but they refuse and refuse to respond to that, before those who call them to Allah and His Messenger, they come to the Messenger of Allah submissively. He says "submissive," submissive to his judgment, acknowledging it willingly and not reluctantly. It is said: so-and-so has acknowledged his right: when he acknowledges it willingly without coercion and submits to him and submits to him. Mujahid used to say about him in what was mentioned about him, he said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "They come to him humbly," he said: quickly. And His saying: "Is there a disease in their hearts"? Allah says: Is there in the hearts of those who turn away when called to Allah and His Messenger to judge between them? There is doubt about the Messenger of Allah that he is Allah's messenger, so they refuse to respond to his judgment and accept it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
