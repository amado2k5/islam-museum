**Commentary on verse 93 (pp. 198-200)**

On [[passage/quran-10-93|10:93]] al-Tabari says: We settled the Children of Israel in places of sound abode. It was said that Syria and Bayt al-Maqdis were meant, and it was said that Syria and Egypt were meant. Report 17882, from al-Dahhak on 'a sound abode': places of truth, Egypt and Syria. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 198}

Report 17883, from Qatada on 'a sound abode': God settled them in Syria and Bayt al-Maqdis; report 17884, from Ibn Zayd: Syria, and he recited [[passage/quran-21-71|21:71]]. His words 'and We provided them with good things' mean We provided the Children of Israel with lawful provision, which is the good. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 199}

His words 'they did not differ until knowledge came to them' mean that those We did this for among the Children of Israel did not differ until there came to them what they knew. Before Muhammad was sent they were agreed on his prophethood and on acknowledging him and his mission, not differing about him over the description they found written with them; when what they knew came to them, some disbelieved in him and some believed, and the believers among them were few. So the sense is that they did not differ until there came to them the known one whom they knew to be a prophet of God, 'knowledge' being put in place of 'the known'. Some took 'knowledge' here to be God's book and revelation. Report 17885, from Ibn Zayd: knowledge is the book of God He sent down and His command to them; and did they differ except out of rivalry among themselves? Those of these passions, did they fight except from rivalry? Rivalry has two kinds: the kind of worldly vying and those who fight over it, and rivalry in knowledge, where one sees another as ignorant and mistaken and himself as right and learned, and so is hostile to that mistaken one by his own rightness and learning. The editor says the printed text and manuscript add 'rivalry among themselves' to the verse, which is not part of its recitation, so he removed it, and that its like are in [[passage/quran-3-19|3:19]], [[passage/quran-42-14|42:14]] and [[passage/quran-45-17|45:17]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 199}

His words 'your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection concerning what they used to differ about' mean God tells His prophet Muhammad that your Lord, Muhammad, will judge between those of the Children of Israel who differ about you, on the Day of Resurrection, in what they differed about of your matter in the world, by sending those who denied you to the Fire and those who believed in you to Paradise; that is His judgement that day on what they differed over of Muhammad's affair. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'judgement'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 200}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
