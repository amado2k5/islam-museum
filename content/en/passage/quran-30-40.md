## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah is the One who created you, then provided for you, then caused you to die, then gives you life. Is there any of your partners who does any of that among you? Glory be to Him, exalted is He above what they associate with Him": Allah says to the polytheists, recognizing the ugliness of their deeds and the wickedness of their deeds: Allah, O people, worship is not suitable except for Him, and it should not be for anyone else. He is the One who created you and you were nothing, then provided for you and granted you authority, and you did not possess before, then He causes you to die after He created you alive, then He gives you life after your death For the resurrection of the Resurrection, as in the narration of Qatadah, His saying: "Allah who created you and provided for you, then caused you to die, then gives you life again" refers to resurrection after death. And His saying: "Are any of your partners who do any of that from them?" Allah says: Are there any of your gods and idols whom you make partners in your worship of Allah? Who does any of that, whether he creates or provides sustenance, or causes death, or spreads it? This is a rebuke from Allah to these polytheists. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
