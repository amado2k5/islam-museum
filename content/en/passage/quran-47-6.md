## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And He will admit them to Paradise, which He has made known to them": and Allah will admit them to His Paradise, which He has made known. He says: He made it known and explained it to them, so that a man may enter his house from it as he would have entered his house in this world, and this is not difficult for him. Also: from Mu'ammar, from Qatadah, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, who said: When Allah saves the believers from the Fire, they are confined on a bridge between Paradise and Hell, and He will avenge from one another many wrongs they had in this world, then they will be permitted to enter Paradise. He said: The believer will never be more deprived of his abode in this world than his own abode in Paradise when he enters it. And from Qatadah, His saying, "And He will admit them to Paradise, He will make it known to them," he said: meaning their abodes therein. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
