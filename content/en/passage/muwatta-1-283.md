## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on one who has a nosebleed on Friday: whoever bows one rak'a with the imam on Friday, then has a nosebleed and goes out and returns after the imam has prayed both rak'as, builds on with another rak'a as long as he has not spoken. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.162}
