## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 93 to 95 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "By Your Lord, let us question them all together": By Your Lord, O Muhammad, let us ask those who made the Qur'an in this world a portion in the Hereafter about what they used to do in this world, what We commanded them to do, and what We sent you to them of My Book which I revealed to them, and of what We called them to in acknowledging it, of My monotheism, and disassociation from equals and idols.
