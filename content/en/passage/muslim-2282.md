## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on the likeness of the guidance and knowledge with which the Prophet was sent, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim. A good land, barren ground and flat plains depict three kinds of people in relation to revelation. {cite:sahih-muslim}
