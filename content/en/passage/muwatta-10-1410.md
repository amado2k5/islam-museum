## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Abu Murra, client of Aqil ibn Abi Talib, that he asked Abu Hurayra about a sheep that was slaughtered and some of it moved. He told him to eat it. Then he asked Zayd ibn Thabit, who said that even a dead animal moves, and forbade him to eat it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.632}
