## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And how many prophets We sent among the first ones": "And how many prophets did We send"—O Muhammad, in the earlier generations that passed before your generation in which you were sent, just as We sent you among your people from Quraysh. "And no prophet came to them except that they mocked him." He means, no generation or nation from those earlier nations would come to us from a prophet calling them to guidance and the path of truth, except that those to whom that came from those nations mocked their prophet whom he sent to them mocked them, just as your people mocked you, O Muhammad. He says: So do not exalt what your people do to you, nor be you, for they have only followed the path of their ancestors and the methodology of their predecessors who disbelieved in Allah in mocking you.
