## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 62 to 64 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Why do we not see men whom we used to count among the wicked?": The tyrants whose attributes were described in these verses, and as Abu Jahl and Al-Walid ibn al-Mughira and their families mentioned: "Why do we not see men with us?" He says: Why do we not see men with us in the Fire? "We used to count them among the wicked," meaning: We counted them among our wicked in this world, and they meant what Suhayb, Khabab, Bilal, and Salman meant in what was mentioned. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Al-Dahhak.
