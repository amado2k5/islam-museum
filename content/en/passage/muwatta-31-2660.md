## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: when one of you puts on his sandals, let him begin with the right; when he takes them off, let him begin with the left; the right is put on first and the last to come off. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.503}
