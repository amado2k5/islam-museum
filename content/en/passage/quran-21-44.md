## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Rather, We granted enjoyment to these people and their forefathers until life became prolonged for them. Will they not see that We are coming to the land and reducing it from its edges? Understanding is better understood by the prevailing ones": These polytheists have no gods to prevent them besides Us, nor any neighbor to protect them from Our punishment. If We intend their punishment, they rely on that, and disobey Our messengers as reliance on that. But We granted them enjoyment in this worldly life and their forefathers before them until their life was prolonged, and they remain steadfast in their disbelief. No admonitor from Us comes to them as a warning of punishment or retribution for their disbelief and disbelief. We commanded and worshipped idols and idols, forgetting our covenant and ignorant of the place of our favor upon them, and they did not know the place of gratitude. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
