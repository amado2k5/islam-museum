## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 97 to 98 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Bring me a piece of iron until it equals the two shells." He said, "Blow until he makes it fire." He says, the Exalted, said: Dhul-Qarnayn said to those who asked him to make a barrier between them and Gog and Magog, "Atoni," meaning bring me a piece of iron, which is the plural of 'zabra,' and 'zabra' means a piece of iron. Also: he said: Muawiyah told us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: 'Zabar al-Hadid' means to cut the iron. My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: 'Give me the Zabar al-Hadid' he said: cut the iron. From Isma'il, from Abu Salih, regarding the phrase: 'Zabar al-Hadid' he said: cut the iron. And Isra'il told us, from Abu Yahya from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: 'Give me the Zabar al-Hadid' he said: cut the iron. And from Qatadah: 'Give me the Zabar al-Hadid': meaning the hadith was split. Muammar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "Give me the iron rod," he said: cut the iron. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibrahim.
