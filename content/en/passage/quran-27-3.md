## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 5 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Tas, those are the verses of the Qur'an and a clear Book": Abu Ja'far said: We have explained the previous statement from our book regarding the letters of the dictionary at the beginnings of the surahs, so the phrase "Tas" is from that. It was narrated from Ibn Abbas that the phrase "Tas": a section that Allah divided is one of the names of Allah. He said: Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas. Therefore, it is obligatory for this statement to mean: 'And the All-Hearing, the Gentle.' These verses that I have revealed to you, O Muhammad, are the verses of the Qur'an, and the verses of a clear book: It says: It makes clear to those who reflect upon it and reflect upon it with the understanding that it is from Allah. He revealed it to you, you did not hide it or say it, nor did anyone else among God's creation, for no one among creation can produce anything like it, even if jinn and humans pretend to do so. He reduced His saying: 'and a clear Book' as a conjunction with the Qur'an. And His saying: 'Guidance' is an attribute of the Qur'an. In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
