## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “Say, O you who are Jews, if you claim that you are allies of Allah to the exclusion of other people, then wish for death, if you should be truthful,” explains: Say, O Muhammad, to the Jews: “O you who are Jews, if you claim that you are allies of Allah to the exclusion of other people,” meaning other than yourselves, “then wish for death, if you should be truthful” in your claim that you are allies of Allah to the exclusion of other people. For Allah does not punish His allies, but rather He honors and blesses them. And if you are truthful in what you say, then wish for death so that you may find relief from the hardships of this world. And its worries and sorrows, and they will pass on to the spirit of Paradise and its bliss through death. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the verse, “Say, O you who are Jews,” that it means, “Say, O you who have repented,” addressed to the Jews. Moses said, “Indeed, we have turned to You in repentance.” The passage also includes statements attributed to Ibn Zayd.
