## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those We sent before you among Our messengers were asked: Did We appoint besides the Most Merciful gods to be worshiped": The interpreters differed on the meaning of the phrase: "And ask who We sent before you among Our messengers?" Among those whom the Messenger of God commanded to be questioned about, some of whom he commanded to be questioned by the Messenger of God said, "The Messenger of God is the believers of the People of the Two Books: the Torah and the Gospel." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
