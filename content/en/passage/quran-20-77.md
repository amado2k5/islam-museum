## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We revealed to Moses, 'Take My servants captive, so set for them a road in the sea, dry and you will not fear.'" He explains: "And We revealed to our prophet Moses when we followed his arguments against Pharaoh, but he refused to respond to his Lord's command, transgressed, and persisted in his tyranny." He says: He made for them a dry road by sea. And 'al-Yabas' and 'al-Yabas': it is pluralized as 'aybas', as you say: they stood in 'aybas' on the earth. 'Al-Yabas' is a diluted form: it is pluralized as 'yabous'. In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
