## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah wrote to Umar ibn al-Khattab mentioning gatherings of the Romans and what he feared from them; Umar wrote to him that whenever a believing servant is struck by a hardship, God will bring relief after it, and that no hardship will overcome two eases; and he cited a verse in Surah Al Imran about patience, perseverance, guard duty and piety. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.574}
