## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Those are given their reward twice for what they patiently endured, and they ward off evil with good and spend from what We provided them": those whose attributes have been described are given twice the reward of their deeds for their patience. The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of patience that Allah promised what He promised. Some said: He promised them what He promised, Glorified and Exalted, by their patience upon the first Book, following Muhammad, and their patience in that. This is the view of Qatadah, which we have mentioned before. Others said: Rather, he promised them patience by their faith in Muhammad before he was sent and that they would follow him when he was sent. This is the statement of al-Dahhak ibn Muzahim, and we have also mentioned it before, and among those who agreed with Qatadah was Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd. Ibn Zayd said regarding the saying: "Indeed, we were Muslims before him" on the religion of Jesus. When the Prophet came, they embraced Islam, and they received their reward twice: for their patience the first time, and they embraced Islam with the Prophet. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
