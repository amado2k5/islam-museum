## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that he said: the Messenger of God sacrificed on his behalf and his household only one camel or one cow. Malik said: I do not know which of the two Ibn Shihab said. The editor notes that some transmitted it from al-Zuhri from Amra from Aisha, and it appears al-Zuhri did not hear it from Amra. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.626}
