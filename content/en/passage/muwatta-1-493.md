## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab that he told him people were ordered to eat on the day of breaking the fast before going out. Malik said: I do not think this applies to people on the festival of sacrifice. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.252}
