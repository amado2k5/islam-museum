## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21-24 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “Eat and drink in good health because of what you did in the past days,” as meaning: These days of yours are fleeting days, leading to eternal days. So work during these days and do good deeds if you can, and there is no power except with God. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase, “because of what you did in the past days,” that it refers to the days of this world and what they did in them. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
