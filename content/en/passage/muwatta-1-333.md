## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id, in the chapter on the witr after dawn: Ubada ibn al-Samit led a people in prayer. One day he went out for the dawn prayer, the caller called the iqama, and Ubada silenced him until he had prayed the witr, and then led them in the dawn prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.185}
