## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 39 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And do not walk through the land in rejoicing. Indeed, you will never pierce the earth nor reach the mountains in height": "And do not walk on the earth arrogantly and arrogantly." "Indeed, you will never pierce the earth," meaning: you will never cut the earth apart by your own destruction, as Ru'bah said: "And the depths are empty, and the penetrator means the penetrator means the cut-off 'and you will never reach the mountains in height" by your pride and arrogance. Rather, this is a prohibition from God to His servants from arrogance, pride, and boasting, and He previously informed them that they do not attain anything by their arrogance and pride that others fall short of. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Jurayj.
