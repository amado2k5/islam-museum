**Commentary on verse 26 (p. 430)**

On "God extends provision to whom He wills and straitens it; they rejoice in the life of this world, and the life of this world in comparison with the hereafter is only an enjoyment", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: God makes ample the provision of whom He wills of His creatures, extending it to him, because some of them are set right only by that, "and straitens", that is, restricts the provision and living of whom He wills of them, narrowing it for him, because only restriction sets him right; "and they rejoice in the life of this world" means those to whom provision was extended in the world despite their disbelief in God and disobedience to Him rejoice in what was extended to them in it, ignorant of what God has for the people of obedience and faith in the hereafter of honour and bliss. Then He, mighty be His praise, told the measure of that in the world against what the people of faith have with Him in the hereafter and let His servants know how small it is, saying "and the life of this world in comparison with the hereafter is only an enjoyment": all that these were given in the world of ease and the extension of provision and comfortable living, compared with what God has for the people of obedience in the hereafter, is "only an enjoyment", small, paltry and passing. Report 20353, from Mujahid, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Shababa, Waraqa and Ibn Abi Najih: "only an enjoyment", he said, small and passing. Reports 20354 and 20355, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl, and Ishaq, Abd Allah and Waraqa: "and the life of this world in comparison with the hereafter is only an enjoyment", he said, small and passing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 430}
