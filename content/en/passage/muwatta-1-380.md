## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from a man he trusted, from Bukayr ibn Abdullah ibn al-Ashajj, from Busr ibn Sa'id, from Ubayd Allah ibn al-Aswad al-Khawlani, who was raised in the household of Maymuna, the Prophet's wife: Maymuna used to pray in a shirt and headscarf with no waist-wrap on her. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.204} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.205}
