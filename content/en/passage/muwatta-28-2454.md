## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Wa'la al-Misri, that he asked Abdullah ibn Abbas about what is pressed from grapes. Ibn Abbas said: a man gave the Prophet a waterskin of wine as a gift. The Prophet said to him: do you not know that God has forbidden it? The man said: no. Then a man beside him whispered to him, and the Prophet said: what did you whisper to him? He said: I told him to sell it. The Prophet said: he who forbade drinking it forbade selling it. So the man opened the two waterskins until what was in them was gone. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.414}
