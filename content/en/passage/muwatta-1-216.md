## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on what is done in recitation: when Abdullah ibn Umar missed part of a prayer with the imam in which the imam recited aloud, he stood after the imam's salam and recited for what he had to make up, aloud. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.132}
