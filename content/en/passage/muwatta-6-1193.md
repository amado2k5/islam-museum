## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Adi ibn Thabit al-Ansari, that Abdallah ibn Yazid al-Khatmi told him that Abu Ayyub al-Ansari told him that he prayed with the Messenger of God the sunset and night prayers together at Muzdalifa during the Farewell Pilgrimage. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.537}
