## Summary
Malik says he heard some people of knowledge say that the pebbles with which the pillars are stoned are like the pebbles of the sling; Malik said that something a little larger is more pleasing to him. The editor notes that stoning with pebbles like sling pebbles is reported raised in Muslim's Sahih from Jabir. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.543}
