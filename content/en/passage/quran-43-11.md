## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And He who sent down water from the sky in measure, and We spread with it a dead land, thus you will emerge": "And He sent down rain from the sky, exalted be He, in measure," meaning: what rain descends from the sky by measure: He says: according to your need for it, and He did not make it like the flood, so it would be a punishment like what was sent down upon the people of Noah, nor did He make it small, so plants and crops do not grow from its scarcity, but He made it rain as a helper and revived the dead earth, giving life to the dead earth. "So We spread with it a dead land." He, the Exalted, says: So We revived a dead town from your land, meaning barren with no plants or crops, studied from drought and rotted from drought. "Thus you will emerge." Allah says: "Just as We brought forth this water which We sent down from the sky from this dead town after its drying and drought, plants and crops." Likewise, O people, you will come out after your destruction and destiny on earth as remains of water and in the passage of the Qur'an. In the Maqta, there are sayings from Qatadah.
