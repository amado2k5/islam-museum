## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the sun and the moon as constantly alternating for the good of people and their livelihood. He records another view, from Ibn Abbas, that they are constant in obeying God. Night and day alternate: the day is for work and livelihood, the night for rest.
