## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that Allah subjected the sun and the moon to people, who take turns day and night for the betterment of themselves and their livelihood. In 'Da'ibin,' there are two opinions: that they are diligent in their differences and succession over people, and there is a narration from Ibn Abbas through Muqatil ibn Hayyan from Ikrimah that it means their diligence in obeying Allah. He explains 'And He subjected the night and the day to you' by their difference in succession: when one goes, the other brings their benefits and the righteousness of their means. The day is for managing livelihood, and the night is for tranquility and comfort, all of which is mercy from Him to His servants.
