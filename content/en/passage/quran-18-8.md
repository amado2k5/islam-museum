## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 10 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Perhaps you will destroy yourself over their traces if they do not believe in this hadith with regret": He means that He may be reminded of you by that: "Perhaps you will kill yourself and destroy it because of the traces of your people who said to you, 'We will not believe in you until you cause us to gush forth from the earth,' as a rebellion against their Lord, if they did not believe in this Book that I revealed to you, and they would believe it is from Allah out of sorrow, longing, and longing, by turning away from you, turning away from what You brought them, and abandoning faith in You." It is said: So-and-so shook his soul and struck it with a smile, and from it is the saying of Dhu al-Rummah: 'O this one who has found himself for something carved by the mighty ones,' meaning: he carved it and lightened it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
