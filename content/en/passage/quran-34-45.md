## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 44–45. Al-Tabari explains that God did not give these people books to study nor sent them a warner before the Prophet, and that those before them denied and did not reach a tenth of what God had given them, so they denied My messengers, so how denial was that.
