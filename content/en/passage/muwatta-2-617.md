## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he prayed over the dead, would say the salam until those next to him heard it. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Abd al-Razzaq, al-Shaybani and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.315}
