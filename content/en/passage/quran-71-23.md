## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse within a single section that includes the verses [[passage/quran-71-23|71:23]] to [[passage/quran-71-24|71:24]] In summary: Al-Tabari mentions that these were a group of people from the children of Adam whose images were worshipped after them. He narrates on the authority of Muhammad bin Qais that they were righteous people who made images of them to make people long for worship, and then those who came after them worshipped them.
