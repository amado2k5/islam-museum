## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Prophet said: no one may milk another's livestock without his permission. He gives the example of a man who would not want his upper room entered, his storeroom broken into, and his food taken; and explains that the udders of livestock are kept for their owners' sustenance. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.564}
