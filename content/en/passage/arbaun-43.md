## Summary
Hadith of Ibn Abbas: "Give the shares of inheritance to their owners; what remains goes to the nearest male man". It is the first of the eight hadiths Ibn Rajab added to the Forty Hadith. Scholars disagree on whether the sister with a daughter takes the residue. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
