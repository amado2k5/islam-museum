## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "and do not mix truth with falsehood" al-Tabari says "labs" is mixing: one says "labastu ʿalayhi the matter" when one has confused it for him, citing a verse of surah al-An'am and al-Ajjaj, and he distinguishes it from "lubs", wearing clothing, with a line of al-Akhtal. He asks how they could mix truth with falsehood when they were disbelievers, and answers that some among them were hypocrites who showed belief in Muhammad while hiding disbelief, and that most of them said he was a prophet who had been sent. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 566} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 567}

Al-Tabari continues on how they mixed truth with falsehood. The hypocrite's mixing was showing the truth with the tongue, acknowledging Muhammad openly, and mixing that outward truth with what he hid within. The mixing of one who acknowledged that Muhammad was sent to others but denied he was sent to them was to hold the truth, that he was sent to others, together with the falsehood, that he was not sent to them, when God sent him to all creation. He reports from Ibn Abbas that it means do not mix truth with lying; from Abu al-Aliya, do not mix truth with falsehood and give sincere counsel to God's servants about Muhammad; from Mujahid, Judaism and Christianity with Islam; and from Ibn Zayd, that the truth is the Torah God sent to Moses and the falsehood what they wrote with their own hands. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 567} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 568}
On "and conceal the truth while you know" he gives two readings: that it is a further prohibition joined to "do not mix", hence jussive, which is Ibn Abbas's view; or a statement about them that they conceal the truth they know, hence in the subjunctive by "sarf", which is the view of Abu al-Aliya and Mujahid, illustrated by a line "do not forbid a trait and do the like of it". The editor refers to al-Farra'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 569} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 570}
On what truth they concealed he reports from Ibn Abbas that it was the knowledge they had of God's messenger and what he brought, which they found in the books they held; from Mujahid that the People of the Book concealed Muhammad while finding him written with them in the Torah and Gospel; and from al-Suddi that the truth is Muhammad. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 571}

Al-Tabari reports that Abu al-Aliya said they concealed Muhammad's mission while finding it written with them, and Mujahid says the same. The meaning of the verse for al-Tabari is: do not confuse people, scholars, about Muhammad by claiming that he was sent to some peoples and not others or by dissembling about him when you know he was sent to all of you and to all peoples, and do not conceal what you find in your scripture of his description, since you know he is a messenger and that belief in him is part of the covenant I took from you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 571} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 572}
