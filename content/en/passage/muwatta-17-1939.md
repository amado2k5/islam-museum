## Summary
Malik says of a man who buys goods from another for one dinar in cash, or for a described sheep on a term, with the sale due under either price: this is disliked and should not be done, because the Messenger forbade two sales in one sale, and this is two sales in one sale. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.193}
