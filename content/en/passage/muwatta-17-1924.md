## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that he said: I heard Abdullah ibn Abbas, and a man asked him about a man who advanced payment for unginned cotton, and wanted to sell it before taking possession. Ibn Abbas said: that is silver for silver, and disliked it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188}
