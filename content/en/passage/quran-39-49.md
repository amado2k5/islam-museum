## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when a person is afflicted by harm, he calls upon Us; then when We grant him a favor from Us, he says, 'We have only given him knowledge of it, but it is a trial,' but most of them do not know." So when a person is afflicted with misery and hardship, he calls upon Us for help from the harm that befell him. "Then when We grant him a favor from Us," he means: Then when We grant him relief from the hardship he was in, by replacing it with hardship with ease and abundance, and with illness with health and wellness, he said: I was only given what I was given of prosperity and abundance in life, health in the body, and well-being, with knowledge from me, meaning with knowledge from Allah that I am worthy of my honor and pleasure through my deeds, meaning in what I have, as it is said: You are the best in this matter with me, meaning in what I think and think. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
