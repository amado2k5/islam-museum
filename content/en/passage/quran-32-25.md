## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection concerning that over which they used to differ": Indeed, your Lord, O Muhammad, He will clarify all His creation on the Day of Resurrection regarding what they used to differ in in this world, differing in matters of religion, resurrection, reward, punishment, and other causes of their religion. He will separate them by a decisive decree by granting Paradise to the people of truth and Hellfire to the people of falsehood.
