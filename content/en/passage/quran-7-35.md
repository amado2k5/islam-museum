## Summary
Verse 35 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 35 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "Children of Adam, if messengers come to you from among you, relating My signs to you, then whoever is God-fearing and acts rightly, there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve", God informs His creatures of what He prepared for His party, the people of obedience to Him and faith in Him and His Messenger, and for the party of Satan and his allies and those who disbelieve in Him and His messengers: "children of Adam, if messengers come to you from among you" means if My messengers come to you, whom I send to you calling you to obey Me and to desist at My command and prohibition, "from among you", meaning from your own selves, clans and tribes, "relating My signs to you" means reciting the verses of My Book to you and making you know My proofs and signs of the truth of what they bring you from Me, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 405}

and the reality of what they call you to of My oneness; "whoever is God-fearing and acts rightly" means whoever of you believed what My messengers brought him of My signs that they related to him and affirmed it, and feared God by doing what He commanded and desisting from what He forbade through His messenger, "and acts rightly" means set right his deeds which he had corrupted before with disobedience of God, by shunning it; "there is no fear for them" means no fear on the Day of Resurrection of God's punishment when they come to Him, "nor will they grieve" over what they missed of their world which they left, and the desires they avoided in obedience to His prohibition, when they see of God's honour what they see there. He gives (14554) from Abu Sayyar al-Sulami that God placed Adam and his descendants in His palm and said, children of Adam, if messengers come to you from among you, relating My signs to you, then whoever is God-fearing and acts rightly, there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve; then He looked at the messengers and said what is in [[passage/quran-23-51|23:51]], "O messengers, eat of the good things and act rightly", and then scattered them. The note says the chain is obscure and it could not explain it, and that it did not know Abu Sayyar. If someone asks, what is the answer to "if messengers come to you from among you"? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 406}

The answer is that the Arabists differ on this. Some said the answer is implied, indicated by what appears of the speech, that is "whoever is God-fearing and acts rightly", for in saying that it is as if He said, so obey them. Others said the answer is "whoever is God-fearing", for the meaning is whoever of you is God-fearing and acts rightly, and what shows it is the partitioning of the speech, for the partition makes mention of "of you" unnecessary. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 407}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
