## Summary
Malik was asked whether a man from Mecca may call out for the lesser pilgrimage from the heart of Mecca; he said: rather he goes out to the lawful territory and enters ihram from there. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1088) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.457}
