## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah has made for you from your houses a place of residence and made for you from the skins of cattle houses which you would humiliate on the day you enter and on the day you reside, and from their wool, its wool, its hair, and their poetry, as furniture and enjoyment for a time": "And Allah has made for you, O people, from your houses, which are of stone and rain, 'a dwelling' place where you will dwell during your days in your homes and lands." "And He made for you houses of the skins of the cattle," which are houses made of camels and fabrics made of hair, wool, and hair. "You underestimate them," meaning: you underestimate carrying and transporting them, "the day they leave you" from your lands and regions for your travels, "and the day you reside" in your lands and regions. "And from its wool, its nests, and its poetry are furniture." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
