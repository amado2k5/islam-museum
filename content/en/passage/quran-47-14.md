## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Is he who is upon clear evidence from his Lord like one whose evil deeds are made attractive to him and they follow their desires": "Is he who" is on proof, proof, and clarifying "who" commands his Lord and knows His oneness? He worships Him with insight from Him, that he has a Lord who rewards him with Paradise for his obedience and for his wrongdoing and disobedience to him, "like one whose evil deeds are made attractive to him," meaning: like one for whom Satan makes his evil deeds good and evil good, and he sees it beautiful, so he remains steadfast in acting upon it. "And they followed their desires" and followed what their souls have called them to in disobedience to God and worship. idols without having any proof or proof for what they do. It was said: The one who means by the phrase: "Is he who is upon clear proof from his Lord" is a prophet, and the one meant by the phrase: "like one whose evil deeds is made attractive to him" are the polytheists.
