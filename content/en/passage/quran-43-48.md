## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We do not show them any sign except that it is greater than its sister, and We seized them with the punishment that they might return": We do not see Pharaoh and his full sign is a sign, meaning: his proof against us is the truth of what our Messenger Moses calls him to: "Except that she is greater than her sister," meaning: except that which We show him is greater in proof against them and more confirming than the previous verses, and it indicates the truth of what Moses commanded him regarding the oneness of God. And His saying: "And We seized them with punishment" means: And We sent down punishment upon them, which is like Allah's remembrance of them by years, a decrease in fruits, locusts, lice, frogs, and blood. And His saying: "Perhaps they will return" means: that they may turn away from their disbelief in God to His oneness and obedience, and repentance from what they have been committed in from their sins. Also: from Qatadah, His saying: "And We seized them with punishment that perhaps they would return," meaning they repent or remember. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
