## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Blessed is He who placed constellations in the sky and placed in them a lamp and a shining moon": the sanctification of the Lord who placed constellations in the sky, and by constellations means palaces, according to some. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Abraham, and Atiyah.
