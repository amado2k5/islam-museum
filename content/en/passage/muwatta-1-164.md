## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the urine of a boy. Yahya transmits from Malik from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Aisha: a boy was brought to the Prophet, and he urinated on his garment. He asked for water and poured it over the place of the urine without washing it thoroughly. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i, al-Tahawi and al-Bayhaqi, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.109}
