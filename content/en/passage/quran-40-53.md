## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 53 to 55 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave Moses the guidance and made the Children of Israel inherit the Scripture": He says, "And We certainly gave Moses" the clarification of the truth with which We sent him, just as We gave that to Muhammad, but Pharaoh and his people denied it, just as Quraysh denied Muhammad. "And We bequeathed the Children of Israel the Scripture," meaning: "And We taught them it and revealed it to them," meaning a guidance explaining their religion and what We obligated them of its obligations. "And a reminder for those of understanding," meaning a reminder from Us to the people of pilgrimage and the minds among them therein.
