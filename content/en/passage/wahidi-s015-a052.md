## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Hijr 15:52, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «وما أرسلنا من قبلك من رسول ولا نبي».
