## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As in the chapter on the call to prayer in Sahih Muslim. It combines answering the muezzin, blessing the Prophet, and asking for the intermediary: the basis of the prayer said after the call. {cite:sahih-muslim}
