## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Umar ibn al-Khattab used to say: O God, do not make my death at the hand of a man who prayed one prostration to You, who will dispute with me before You on the Day of Resurrection because of it. The editor notes that the report is cut off, and that al-Layth transmitted it from Hisham from Zayd ibn Aslam from his father from Umar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.593}
