## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 45–47. Al-Tabari mentions the differences among reciters regarding 'Our servants' in the plural and 'Abduna' in monotheism, and explains that Allah commands the mention of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the people of hands and eyes. Indeed, We have saved them with the sincere remembrance of the abode, and that they are among the chosen among the righteous.
