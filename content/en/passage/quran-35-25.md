## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 24–26. Al-Tabari explains that God sent His Prophet with the truth as a bringer of good tidings and a warner, and that there is no nation without a warner. If they deny it, then those before them denied it. Their messengers came to them with clear proof, the pen, and the illuminating Book. Then God seized those who disbelieved, so how denial was he?
