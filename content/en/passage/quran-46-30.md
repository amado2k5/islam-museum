## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "They said, 'We stand up, indeed we have heard a Book sent down after Moses, confirming what was before it, guiding to the truth and to a straight path.'" He says, exalting the Almighty about the saying of those who were sent to the Messenger of God from the jinn to their people when they turned to them from the Messenger of God: "O our people" from the jinn, "Indeed, we heard a Book sent down after him," the Book of Moses confirming what was before him, saying: He believes what came before him from the books of God that He revealed to His messengers. And His saying: 'He guides to the truth' means: he guides to the right path and indicates what pleases Allah. 'And to a straight path,' he says: to a path without deviation, which is Islam. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
