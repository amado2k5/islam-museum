## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "I hear them and see the day they come to Us, but the wrongdoers today are in clear error": Allah Almighty informs about the condition of those who disbelieve in Him, those who make him equals and claim that he will have a child on the Day of Resurrection: If they are blind in this world to the sight of the truth and to look at the proofs of Allah that indicate His oneness, they are deaf to hearing the verses of His Book and what the messengers of Allah called them to in it regarding acknowledgment of His oneness and what His prophets sent with. How will I hear them on the day they come to their Lord in the Hereafter, and see them that day when sight and hearing will not benefit them? In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Al-Rabi'.
