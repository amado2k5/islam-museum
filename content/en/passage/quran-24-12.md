## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Why did you not think well of the believing men and women when you heard it, and they said, 'This is a clear slander': This is a reproach from Allah Almighty mentioned by the believers regarding what happened to them in their hearts, of the trembling of those who trembled about Aisha because of what they were stirred up by." He says to them: 'O people, when you heard what the people of the slanderers said about Aisha, the believers among you and the believing women thought of themselves well.' He says: You thought of those among you who were disgusted by it with good, but you did not think that he committed immorality. He said: "by themselves" because all the people of Islam are like one soul, since they are followers of one religion. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
