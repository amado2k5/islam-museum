## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Allah makes clear to you the verses, and Allah is Knowing and Wise": Those who love that adultery be spread among those who are truthful about Allah and His Messenger and that it becomes clear among them, "for them is a painful punishment," meaning: they will have a painful punishment in this world, to the extent that Allah set as a limit for those who throw chaste and chaste women if they accuse them of it, and in the Hereafter is the punishment of Hell if he dies insisting on it without repenting. As Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "They love that immorality spread," he said: It appears in the case of Aisha. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "Indeed, those who like that immorality should spread among those who believe will have a painful punishment," he said: the wicked Abdullah ibn Abi ibn Salul, the hypocrite, who spread the falsehood he spread against Aisha, "for them is a painful punishment." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
