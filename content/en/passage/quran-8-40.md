**Commentary on verse 40 (pp. 544-545)**

Al-Tabari says on "if they turn away, know that God is your protector, an excellent protector and an excellent helper" [[passage/quran-8-40|8:40]]: if these polytheists turn away from what you called them to, believers, of belief in God and His Messenger and leaving fighting you over their unbelief, and refuse but to persist in unbelief and fight you, then fight them and be sure that God helps you against them and gives you victory; "excellent protector" He is to you, meaning an excellent helper to you and His friends, "and an excellent helper" being the supporter. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "turning", "protector" and "helper". Report 16086, from Ibn Ishaq: if they turn away from your command to their unbelief, God is your protector who made you mighty and gave you victory over them at Badr, despite their many numbers and your few; it is in the Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 544} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 545}

We stop here at the foot of p. 545, with the commentary on 8:40 complete; the commentary on 8:41 begins on this page and is in the 8:41 file. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 545}
