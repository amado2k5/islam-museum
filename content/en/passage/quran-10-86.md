**Commentary on verse 86 (p. 171)**

On [[passage/quran-10-86|10:86]] al-Tabari says: save us, our Lord, by Your mercy, and free us from the hands of the disbelieving people, Pharaoh's people, because they enslaved them and put them to the filthy tasks of their service. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 171}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
