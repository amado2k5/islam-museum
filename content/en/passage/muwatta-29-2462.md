## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that Ibn Shihab used to say: for the blood-money of deliberate killing when accepted: twenty-five she-camels in their fifth year, twenty-five in their sixth year, twenty-five in their fourth year, and twenty-five in their fourth year (jadhaa). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.418}
