## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 91 to 93 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And it is not fitting for the Most Merciful to take a child": It is not appropriate for God to take a child, because He is not like creatures who are overcome by desires and compelled by pleasures to have intercourse with females, and no child is born except from a female. God is exalted above being like His creation, as Ibn Ahmar said: "At the head of creatures from honored phoenix, there is no plain or mountain below it," meaning: it is neither fit nor can it be. "Indeed, everyone in the heavens and the earth will come to the Most Merciful a servant," meaning: none of the angels in the heavens, nor on the earth—humans, humans, and jinn—will come to the Most Merciful a servant. He means: Will his Lord not come on the Day of Resurrection as a servant to Him, humble, submissive, acknowledged in servitude, with no relation between him and him. And His saying: "He will come to the Most Merciful" is only the doer of whoever I bring to him, so I will come to him.
