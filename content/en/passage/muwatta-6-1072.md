## Summary
A saying of Malik: whoever has his ablution broken while circumambulating the House or running between al-Safa and al-Marwa, if he has circumambulated part or all of it and has not yet prayed the two rak'as of circumambulation, then he performs ablution and begins the circumambulation and the two rak'as again. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1295) and Suwayd (550) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.494}
