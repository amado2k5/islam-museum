## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 71 to 72 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they say, 'When is this promise, if you are truthful?'" He explains: And the polytheists of your people, O Muhammad, say, "They denied what you brought them from your Lord." "When" will this "promise" we have prepared of the punishment that is upon us in what you say, "if you are truthful," in what you promise us? "Say, perhaps it will be a remedy for you." God Almighty says: Say to them, O Muhammad: Perhaps our affection "some of those you are hastening" from God's punishment will be nearer to you. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
