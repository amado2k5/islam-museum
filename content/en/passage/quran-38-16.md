## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 17 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "And these people do not look except one shout, with no hiccups": "And these people" of the polytheists from Quraysh "do not look except one shout," meaning by a single shout: the first blast in the trumpet "has no hiccups," meaning: that shout has no hiccups, meaning neither sluggishness nor cessation. In the segment, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
