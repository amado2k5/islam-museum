**Commentary on verse 150: disbelief in God and His messengers and separating them (pp. 352-354)**

Al-Tabari says those who disbelieve in God and His messengers are the Jews and Christians, who want to separate God from His messengers by calling the messengers God sent with revelation liars who invented against their Lord; this is their wish to separate them, by making the messengers liars and inventing falsehood about them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 352}

The sense of 'we believe in some and disbelieve in others' is that the Jews accept one and reject another: they reject Jesus and Muhammad, and in their view accept Moses and the prophets before him, and the Christians reject Muhammad and accept Jesus and the prophets before him in their view. They want to take a path between these, that is, a road to error and the innovation they devised, to which they call the ignorant. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 352}

Al-Tabari then says true unbelief belongs to one who accepts some of the books and messengers and rejects others, for the believer in the books and messengers is one who affirms all of the book he claims to accept and all the messenger brought. Whoever accepts some and rejects some denies the prophethood of those whose message he rejects; whoever denies a prophet's prophethood is a denier of him. Such are truly unbelievers, and for them We have prepared a humiliating punishment, a torment that humiliates whoever suffers it by his eternal dwelling in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 353}

Report 10765, from Qatada: the Jews believed in the Torah and Moses and disbelieved in the Gospel and Jesus; the Christians believed in the Gospel and Jesus and disbelieved in the Qur'an and Muhammad; they took Judaism and Christianity, two innovations not from God, and abandoned Islam. Report 10766, from al-Suddi: the Jews said Jesus was not a messenger of God, and the Christians said Muhammad was not, so they separated God from His messengers. Report 10767, from Ibn Jurayj: the Jews believed in Uzayr and disbelieved in Jesus; the Christians believed in Jesus and disbelieved in Uzayr. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 354}
