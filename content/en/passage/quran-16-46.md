## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46 to 47 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Or He seizes them in their change, and they are not miracles": He means that God Almighty mentions him by saying: "Or He seizes them in their change" or destroys them in their management in the lands and their hesitation in their travels. "They are not miraculous," says He, Glorified and Exalted: They are not powerless to do that if God wills to seize them in that way. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibrahim.
