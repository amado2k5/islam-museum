## Summary
Malik transmits from Ismail ibn Abi Hakim, that he heard Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz say: it was said that God, blessed and exalted, does not punish the general public for the sin of the elite; but when the wrong is done openly, they all deserve the punishment. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.591}
