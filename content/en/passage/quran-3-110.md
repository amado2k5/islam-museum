## Summary
Verse 110 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 110 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The opening of the dispute about the praised community (p. 100).** Al-Tabari notes that the interpreters differed over who is meant by the verse's description of this community as the best brought forth for people. Some said: those who emigrated with God's messenger from Mecca to Medina. This view continues on the next page.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 100 in the commentary on verse 110 and continue from p. 101. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 100}
