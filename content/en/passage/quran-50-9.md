## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 11 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We sent down blessed rain from the sky and caused with it to grow gardens and grain of harvest": "And We sent down water from the sky" as blessed rain, causing us to grow orchards of trees, grain of harvested crops from wheat, barley, and all kinds of grains. Also: from Qatadah, "and the grain of harvest" means this barley and barley. From Mu'ammar, from Qatadah: 'and the grain of the harvest' he said: it is wheat and barley. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid: 'and the love of the harvest' he said: wheat. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Samak.
