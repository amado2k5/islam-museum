## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Aisha used to say that whoever fears he will sleep until morning should pray the witr before sleeping, and whoever hopes to wake at the end of the night should delay his witr, in the chapter on the command to pray the witr. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.183}
