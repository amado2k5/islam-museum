## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 79 to 80 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "You do not hear the dead nor hear the deaf supplication when they turn away," meaning: You cannot understand the truth from someone whom God has sealed upon his heart and caused him to die, because God has sealed upon him that he will not understand it. "And the deaf do not hear the prayer." He means: And you cannot hear that from someone whom God has deafened from hearing, "when they turn away, they turn away," meaning: if they turn away and turn away from Him, they do not listen to Him because the religion of disbelief dominates their hearts, nor listen to the truth, nor do they contemplate it, nor listen to it to those who say it, but they turn away from it, deny speaking about it, and refusing to listen to it.
