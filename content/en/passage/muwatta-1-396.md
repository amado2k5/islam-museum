## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Salim ibn Abdullah, that Abdullah ibn Umar shortened the prayer on a full day's journey. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq and Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted it by other channels. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.211}
