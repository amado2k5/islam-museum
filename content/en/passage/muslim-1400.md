## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Masud in the Book of Marriage of Sahih Muslim, narrated by Alqama when Uthman offered to marry a young slave-girl to Ibn Masud. It is the root of the encouragement to hasten marriage. {cite:sahih-muslim}
