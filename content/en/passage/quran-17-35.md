## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And give full measure when you have exercised; weigh with the just scale." That is better and best in interpretation: "And" He ruled that "give full measure" to people "when you give them their rights" before you, and do not belittle them. "Weigh with the just balance," meaning: "And judge or also weigh for them if you weigh them with the straight balance," which is justice free of distortion, deceit, or deception. Interpreters have differed regarding the meaning of 'al-qistas'; some said: it is al-Qaban. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, and Ibn Jurayj.
