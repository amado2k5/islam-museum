## Summary
Malik asked Abdallah ibn Dinar what Abdullah ibn Umar did with the covers of his offering animals when the Kaaba was covered with that covering. He said: he gave them in charity. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1211), Suwayd (526) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.511}
