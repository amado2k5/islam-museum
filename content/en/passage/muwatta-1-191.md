## Summary
A report that Yahya transmits from Malik from Hisham ibn Urwa, that his father Urwa said to him: when you are on a journey, if you want to give the call and the iqama, do so; if you want, give only the iqama and do not call. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik, and Ibn Abi Shayba from Hatim ibn Isma'il from Hisham. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.121} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
