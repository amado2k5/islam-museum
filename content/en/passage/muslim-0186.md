## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Masud in the chapter on intercession and bringing monotheists out of the Fire, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It describes the last to enter Paradise and his rank: ten times the size of the world. {cite:sahih-muslim}
