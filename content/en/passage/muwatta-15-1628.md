## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, that a freed woman of the Banu Adi, called Zabraa, told him she had been married to a slave while she was herself a slave. When she was freed, Hafsa, wife of the Prophet, sent for her and said: I will tell you something and I do not like you to act on it. Your affair is in your own hands so long as your husband has not touched you; if he has touched you, you have no say in the matter. She said she replied: it is divorce, divorce, divorce, and she separated from him three times. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.72}
