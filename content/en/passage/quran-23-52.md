## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And indeed, this is your nation as one nation, and I am your Lord, so fear [God]": Reciters differed in reading His saying: "And indeed, this is your nation as one nation." The majority of the readers of Medina and Basra read this as: "andan" with a fatha, meaning: "Indeed, I am Knowing of what you do," and that this nation of yours is one nation. According to this interpretation, "أن" is in a lowered position, and it is attached to "ma" in His saying: "by what you do," and it may be in the accusative case if read as such, and the meaning of the speech then is: and know that this is in the accusative case, and its accusative is implied. Most of the Kufan reciters read this with a kasra: "andin" is as a form of renewal.
