## In al-Tabari's commentary: "they seek to deceive God and those who believe"
Al-Tabari explains the hypocrite's deception of his Lord and the believers as showing by his tongue affirmation contrary to the doubt and denial in his heart, to ward off God's ruling of death and captivity. To the objection that one who hides his belief only out of caution is not really deceiving, he replies that the Arabs call a person who gives with his tongue what differs from his inner thought, to escape what he fears, a deceiver of the one he escapes from. He adds that the hypocrite also deceives himself, thinking he does it good while leading it to punishment, which he takes to be the sense of "they deceive only themselves, though they do not perceive". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 272} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 273}
