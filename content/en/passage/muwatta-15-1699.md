## Summary
Malik says that the practice among us for a slave who divorces his slave-woman, when she was a slave and is later freed, is that her waiting period is that of a slave-woman. Her emancipation does not change her waiting period, whether he had a right of return or not. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.95}
