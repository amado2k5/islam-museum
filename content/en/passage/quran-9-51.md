**Commentary on verse 51 (pp. 290-291)**

Al-Tabari says God is instructing His Prophet Muhammad to say to these hypocrites who stayed behind from him, doubters in their religion: nothing will befall us except what God has written for us on the Preserved Tablet and decreed for us; He is our protector, meaning our helper against His enemies; and "on God let the believers rely", for if they rely on Him, hope for help from no one else and fear nothing but Him, He will suffice them in their affairs and help them against whoever wrongs them and plots against them. The editor refers to earlier explanations of writing, protector and reliance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 291}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
