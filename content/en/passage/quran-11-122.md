**Commentary on verse 122 (p. 544)**

On [[passage/quran-11-122|11:122]], 'and wait, we are waiting', it means wait for what Satan promised you, for we wait for what God promised us of your defeat and our victory over you; report 18765, from Ibn Jurayj: wait for the promises of Satan to you of what he makes attractive to you, 'we are waiting'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 544}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
