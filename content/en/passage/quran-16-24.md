## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when it is said to them, 'What has your Lord revealed?' They say, 'Legends of the ancients': And when it is said to those polytheists who do not believe in the Hereafter: 'What has your Lord revealed?' What has your Lord revealed?" They said: What was revealed by the earlier generations of falsehood. And that was as follows: And from Qatadah, His saying: "What did your Lord send down? They said, 'The legends of the ancients,' meaning: the hadiths of the ancients and their falsehood." A group of Arab polytheists used to sit on the path of the Prophet of Allah, and when one of the believers passed by them, he meant the Prophet of Allah, they said to them: the legends of the ancients, meaning the hadiths of the ancients and their falsehood. He said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: 'legends of the ancients,' meaning the hadiths of the ancients. And in the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
