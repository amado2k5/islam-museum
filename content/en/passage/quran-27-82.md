## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 81 to 82 together in one place. Al-Tabari mentions two readings: 'with the guidance of blindness' in the addition, and the Kufa reading 'Guidance to the blind' in the accusative of 'blindness.' He considers both to be similar in meaning and both are correct. The Prophet does not guide the blind from their misguidance; if only those who believe in the signs of Allah hear, they are Muslims.
