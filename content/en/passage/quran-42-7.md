## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And thus We have revealed to you an Arabic Qur'an to warn the Mother of the Cities and those around it and warn of the Day of Assembly, about which there is no doubt, a group in Paradise and a group in the Blazing Fire": Thus have We revealed to you, O Muhammad, an Arabic Qur'an in the language of the Arabs, because those to whom We sent you are Arabs. So We revealed this Qur'an to you in their tongues, so that they may understand the proofs and remembrance of Allah in it, for We do not send a messenger except in the language of his people, to make clear to them "that you may warn the Mother of Cities," which is Mecca and its surroundings, as He says: And those around Umm al-Qura from all the other people.
