## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "When the young men sought refuge in the cave and said, 'Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and prepare for us from Our command guidance guidance': 'Or did you think that the companions of the cave and the number were among Our signs wonderful?'" When the young men took refuge in the mountain cave, fleeing their religion to Allah, they said: "Our Lord, grant us mercy from You," wishing to their Lord to provide them with mercy from Him. And His saying: "And prepare for us from our affair guidance reason," he means: And they said: It is easy for us what we seek and what we seek of Your pleasure and fleeing from disbelief in You, and from the idol worship to which our people invite us. "Righteousness" means: to act upon what you love. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
