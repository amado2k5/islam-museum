## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 103–106. Al-Tabari mentions that when Abraham and his son submitted to God, He commanded them to be sent to Him, and He raised them to the forehead, and we called out, 'O Abraham, the vision has come true. Indeed, We reward the doers of good. This is the clear trial.'
