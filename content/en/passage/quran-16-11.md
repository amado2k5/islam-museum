## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Your Lord will cause your crops to grow by what He sent down from the sky, your crops, your olives, your palm trees, and your grapes." "And of all fruits," meaning from all fruits besides that, sustenance, sustenance, and fruit, a favor from Him upon you in that and favor, and a proof against those among you who disbelieve in it. "Indeed, in that there is a sign." He says: "Indeed, in Allah's bringing forth from the sky what He descends from the sky of water is what has been described to you." "A sign" means a clear indication and a clear sign, "for a people who reflect," meaning a people who consider God's admonitions and reflect on His proofs, so they remember and repent.
