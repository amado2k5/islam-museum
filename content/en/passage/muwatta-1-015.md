## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on whoever catches one rak'a of the prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet made whoever catches one rak'a of the prayer to have caught it. The editor cites Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban and al-Bukhari's Juz' al-Qira'a, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.41} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.42}
