## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 15 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “When the earth is shaken with its [final] earthquake” as follows: “When the earth is shaken” for the Day of Judgment, “with its [final] earthquake,” meaning it will be violently shaken. The word “earthquake” (zilzal) is a verbal noun when the letter “z” is pronounced with a kasra (i.e., “zilzal” is pronounced with a kasra), and when it is pronounced with a fatha (i.e., “zilzal” is pronounced with a fatha). The earthquake is attributed to the earth, which is its attribute, just as one might say, “I will honor you with your honor,” meaning, “I will honor you with honor.” This is fitting in the context of its earthquake, as it aligns with the rhymes of the verses that follow. He cites Ash'ath, on the authority of Ja'far, on the authority of Sa'id, who said: “The earth was shaken” during the time of Abdullah, and Abdullah said to it, “What is wrong with you? If it could speak, the Hour would come.” He also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid in this section.
