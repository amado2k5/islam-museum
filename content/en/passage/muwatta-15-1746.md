## Summary
Malik says a man may not practise coitus interruptus with a free wife without her permission, but there is no harm in practising it with his slave-woman without her permission, and whoever has a slave-woman belonging to people may not do so without their permission. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.112}
