## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-10 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase “And by Him Who created the male and the female” as having the two possible meanings described in the verse “And by the heaven and what He constructed, and by the earth and what He spread out.” The first meaning is that “what” is taken to mean “who,” in which case it is an oath by God, may His praise be exalted, by the Creator of the male and the female, which is that creation. The second meaning is that “what” and what follows it are taken to mean the verbal noun, in which case it is an oath by His creation of the male and the female. It has been reported that Abdullah ibn Mas’ud and Abu al-Darda’ recited it as “And by the male and the female,” and Abu al-Darda’ attributed this reading to the Messenger of God. The report mentions this: On the authority of Abu Ishaq, he said: In Abdullah’s reading: “By the night when it covers, and by the day when it shines, and by the male and the female.” The passage also quotes statements from Qatadah and Ibrahim.
