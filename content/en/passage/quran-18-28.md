## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And be patient with those who call upon their Lord morning and evening, seeking His face. And do not turn your eyes away from them, seeking the adornments of worldly life, and do not obey one whose heart We have made heedless of Our remembrance and who follows his desire and his affair is neglectful." "And be patient," O Muhammad, "with your companions, those who call upon their Lord morning and evening," by mentioning Him with glorification, praise, declaration, supplication, and righteous deeds such as obligatory prayers and others. They "intend" to do so "His face" does not want a display of worldly display. We have mentioned the differences of opinion regarding the phrase "They call upon their Lord morning and evening" in Surah Al-An'am, and the correct view is ours, so it is more necessary to repeat it here. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
