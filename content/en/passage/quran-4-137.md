**Commentary on verse 137: turning from belief to unbelief (p. 314)**

Al-Tabari gives the views on those who believed, then disbelieved, then believed, then disbelieved, then increased in disbelief. One view is that these are the Christians, who believed in Moses then disbelieved in him, then believed in Jesus and disbelieved in him, then increased in disbelief toward Muhammad; God will not forgive them nor guide them to the way. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 314}
