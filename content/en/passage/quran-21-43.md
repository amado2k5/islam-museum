## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Or do they have gods who prevent them from Us? They cannot help themselves, nor are they from us companions": Are these those who hasten their Lord punishment gods who prevent them? If We have made Our punishment lawful for them and sent down upon them Our might, instead of Us? And meaning: Or do they have gods besides Us who prevent them from us? Then He, the Exalted, described the gods as weak and humiliated, and what their attributes are, saying: How can their gods, whom they call upon instead of Us, prevent them from us when they cannot support themselves? And His saying: "Nor are they of us accompanying them." Interpreters differed on the meaning of this and on the meaning of "they accompany." Some said: What was meant is the gods, and that they are not accompanied by good from God. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
