## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, Allah knows the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is Seeing of what you do": Indeed, O Bedouins, it is not hidden from Him whether among you the truthful is the liar, and from those who are inward in the religion of Islam out of desire for it, and from within it is awe of our Messenger Muhammad and his soldiers. So do not teach us your religion or the consciences of your hearts, for Allah knows what your hearts hold and speak to yourselves, and He knows what has escaped you. So hide in the secrets of the heavens and the earth, nothing of that is hidden from Him. "And Allah is Seeing of what you do," meaning: And Allah is the All-Seeing of the deeds you do—whether you do it openly or secretly. Are you obedience or sin? And He is your recompense for all of that: if good, then good; if evil, then evil, and He is competent.
