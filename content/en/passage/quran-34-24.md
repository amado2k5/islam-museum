## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Who provides for you from the heavens and the earth?' Say, 'Allah, and indeed, or you, are upon guidance or in manifest error.'" Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists with their Lord, idols and idols: Who provides for you from the heavens and the earth by sending down rain upon you from it, life for your crops, and for your livelihoods, and subjecting the sun, moon, and stars for your benefit, your sustenance, and the earth by bringing forth from it your sustenance and the food of your livestock? He omitted the report from the people's response out of necessity to make the statement meaningless, then mentioned it, which is: If they say: We do not know, then say: What provides for you is Allah. "And we or you," O people, "perhaps we are guided or in clear error," meaning: Say to them: We are on guidance or in error, or you are on error or guidance. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ikrimah.
