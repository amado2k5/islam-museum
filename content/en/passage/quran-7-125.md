**Commentary on verse 125 (p. 35)**

Al-Tabari says the sorcerers answered Pharaoh, when he threatened them with cutting off hands and feet on opposite sides and with crucifixion: "to our Lord we shall return"; by turning back to God he means returning to Him and ending up with Him. The editor refers to the explanation of "turning back" on p. 32. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 35}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
