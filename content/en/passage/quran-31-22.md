## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And whoever submits his face to Allah while he is a doer of good, has held fast to the most trustworthy handhold, and to Allah is the outcome of all matters": Whoever worships His face, humbling himself in servitude, acknowledging His divinity "while he is doer of good," means: He has held fast to the most trustworthy handhold, meaning he has held fast to the most trustworthy handhold, which is not feared to be broken by those who hold to Him. This is an example, which means that he has held firmly out of Allah's pleasure by submitting his face to Him while he is a doer of good, so that he will not fear Allah's punishment on the Day of Resurrection. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Sufyan.
