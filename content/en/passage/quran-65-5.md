## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “That is the command of Allah which He has revealed to you. And whoever fears Allah - He will remove from him his misdeeds and grant him a great reward,” explains: This that I have clarified for you concerning the rulings on divorce, reconciliation, and the waiting period is the command of Allah which He has given you, revealing it to you, O people, so that you may obey it and act upon it. His statement, “And whoever fears Allah - He will remove from him his misdeeds,” means: Whoever fears Allah and is mindful of Him by avoiding His prohibitions and fulfilling His obligations, Allah will erase his sins and the evil of his deeds. “And grant him a great reward” means: He will bestow upon him abundant reward for his actions and piety. Part of this great reward is that He will admit him to His Paradise, where he will abide eternally.
