## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if you ask them who created the heavens and the earth, they will surely say, 'Allah, say, 'Have you seen what you invoke besides Allah? If Allah intends harm for me, will they remove His harm or intend me with mercy? Will they withhold His mercy?' Say, 'Sufficient for me is Allah. In Him do you rely.'" And if you ask, O Muhammad, these just polytheists of Allah, idols and idols: Who created the heavens and the earth? They will say: Allah created them. When they say that, say: Have you considered, O people, this whom you worship besides Allah of idols and gods? "If Allah intends harm for me," he means: severe in my livelihood. Are they relieving me of what my Lord is causing me harm? "Or does He intend mercy for me"—meaning: If my Lord wills for me to grant me abundance in my livelihood, abundant wealth, prosperity, and well-being in my body, are they withholding from me what He intends to bestow upon me of that mercy? He omitted the answer to make the listener self-sufficient to know this and to indicate what has appeared in the discourse about him. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
