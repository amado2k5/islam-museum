## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim: six rights of a Muslim over his brother are returning greetings, accepting invitations, giving sincere advice, responding to a sneezer, visiting the sick and following funerals. It is a charter of social brotherhood. {cite:sahih-muslim}
