## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on whoever calls to guidance or to error, in the Book of Knowledge of Sahih Muslim: a rule that the one who calls to guidance gets a continuous reward, and the one who calls to error gets a continuous burden. {cite:sahih-muslim}
