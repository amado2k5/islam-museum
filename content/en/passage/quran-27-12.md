## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And put your hand into your pocket and it will come out white without harm." In nine verses to Pharaoh and his people, indeed, they were a defiantly disobedient people.' Allah Almighty says, informing about His saying to His prophet Moses: "And put your hand into your pocket." It was mentioned that Allah commanded him to put his hand into his pocket, but rather to put it in his pocket, because what he was wearing that day was a woolen armored vehicle. Some said: It had no sleeve. Some said: Its sleeve was part of his hand. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
