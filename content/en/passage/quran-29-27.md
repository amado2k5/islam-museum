## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We granted him Isaac and Jacob and made among his descendants prophethood and the Book, and We gave him his reward in this world, and indeed, in the Hereafter, he is among the righteous": We gave him from Us Isaac a son, and Jacob after him a son. Also: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "And We granted him Isaac and Jacob," he said: They were the children of Abraham. And His saying: "And We placed prophethood and the Book among his descendants" means the plural, referring to the books, but it is derived from their statement: the dirham and dinar increased with so-and-so. And His saying: "And We gave him his reward in this world," the Exalted means: "And We gave him the reward of his trial among us in this world," and yet he is "among the righteous in the Hereafter." He will also receive the reward of the righteous there, without diminishing his share of the reward given in this world for his trial in Allah, compared to what he has with him in the Hereafter. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Sufyan.
