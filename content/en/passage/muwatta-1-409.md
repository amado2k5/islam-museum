## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman used to pray voluntary prayers on a journey, in the chapter on voluntary prayer on a journey. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.214}
