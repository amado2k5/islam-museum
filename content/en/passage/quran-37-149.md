## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 149 to 151 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And We sent him to one hundred thousand or more": So We sent Yunus to one hundred thousand people, or they increase over one hundred thousand. It is mentioned from Ibn Abbas that he used to say: the meaning of the word "or" is: rather, they increase. The narration mentioned this: And from Mansur, from Salim ibn Abi al-Ja'd, from Al-Hakam ibn Abdullah ibn al-Azwar, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "And We sent him to one hundred thousand or more," he said: Rather, they increase, they were one hundred and thirty thousand. From Ja'far, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, regarding the phrase: "One hundred thousand or more," he said: They increase by seventy thousand, and the punishment was sent upon them. When they separated the women and their children, the cattle and their children, and fled to Allah, the punishment was lifted from them, and the sky rained down blood. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
