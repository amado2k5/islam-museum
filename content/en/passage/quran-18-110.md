## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, I am only a human like you, revealed to me. Indeed, your God is one God. So whoever hopes to meet his Lord, let him do righteous work and not associate anyone in the worship of his Lord." Say to these polytheists, O Muhammad: I am only a human like you among the children of Adam; I have knowledge except what Allah has taught me. And indeed Allah reveals to me that your deity whom you must worship and associate nothing with is one deity, with no equal, no partner. "So whoever hopes to meet his Lord," he says: So whoever fears his Lord on the Day of Meeting, watches over His sins, and hopes for His reward for obedience to Him—let him do righteous deeds—he says: let him dedicate his worship to Him and set aside Lordship for him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, Al-A'mash, Al-Rabi', and others.
