## Summary
Malik said: the explanation is that she shares with them in the wound that exposes the bone and the wound that displaces it, and what is below the head wound and the abdominal wound and similar wounds, in which a third of the blood-money or more is due; when it reaches that, her blood-money is half of a man's blood-money. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.422}
