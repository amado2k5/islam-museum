## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "and He taught Adam" al-Tabari reports from Ibn Abbas and Said ibn Jubayr that God sent the Angel of Death (in another place Iblis, as the editor notes) who took from the surface of the earth, sweet and salty, and Adam was made from it, hence the name Adam, from the "adim" of the earth. He reports from Ali that Adam was made from the surface of the earth, with the good, the sound and the bad in it, and all that is seen in his children, the editor noting the chain is weak. He gives, through al-Suddi, that Adam's children come in different kinds because the earth taken was red, white and black. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 480} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 481}
He reports from the Prophet, by a chain the editor calls sound, from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari: God made Adam from a handful taken from all the earth, so Adam's children came according to the earth, red, black, white and between, the soft and the rough, the bad and the good. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 481} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 482}
Al-Tabari says that on this reading "Adam" is originally a verb-form name given to the father of mankind, like "Ahmad" and "As'ad", which is why it is not declined, meaning he reached the "adama" of the earth, its visible surface. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 482}
On "all the names" he records differences over which names Adam was taught. He reports from Ibn Abbas that they are the names by which people know things: human, animal, earth, plain, sea, mountain, donkey and the like; from Mujahid that He taught him the name of everything; and from Said ibn Jubayr that this included camel, cow and sheep. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 482} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 483}
