## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari lists the meanings given to the hurrying described in 14:43: rushing toward the summoner, as in Qatada and Sa'id ibn Jubayr; or keeping the gaze fixed without blinking, as in Ibn Abbas and al-Dahhak, which some describe as a constant staring.
