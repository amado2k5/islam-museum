## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He is Allah, the Creator, the Creator, the Fashioner, to whom belongs the Most Beautiful Names. Whatever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him, and He is the Mighty, the Wise": He is the Creator, for whom worship is worthy of worship, and there is no creator besides Him. The Creator who created creation by His power, the Imager, created them as He wills, and as He wills. And His saying: "To Him belong the best names," He, the Exalted, says: To Allah belong the beautiful names, which are the names by which Allah named Himself, which He mentioned in these two verses: "All that is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him," meaning: all that is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Him, and He prostrates to Him willingly and unwillingly, "and He is the Mighty." He means: He is the Most Severe in vengeance against His enemies, the "Wise," in managing His creation and directing them to what is good for them.
