**Commentary on verse 8 (p. 408)**

Al-Tabari says on "that He may establish the truth and bring falsehood to naught, though the guilty dislike it": God wills to cut off the last of the unbelievers so that the truth be made true, so that God alone be worshipped and not the gods and idols and Islam be honoured, which is the establishing of the truth; "and bring falsehood to naught" meaning He nullifies the worship of gods and idols and unbelief, though those dislike it who sinned and earned wrongs and burdens among the unbelievers. Report 15733, from Qatada: they are the polytheists. It is also said that "the truth" in this place is God, mighty and glorious. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "guilty". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 408}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
