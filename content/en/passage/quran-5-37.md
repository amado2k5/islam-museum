**Commentary on verse 37: "they will wish to come out of the Fire but will not leave it", pp. 293-294**

Al-Tabari says those who disbelieved in their Lord will want on the Day of Resurrection to come out of the Fire after entering it, and will not leave it, and theirs is lasting punishment, constant and abiding, never ending or changing, and he cites a line by a poet whom the editor did not identify, meaning a punishment lasting for them. The interpreters spoke likewise, and report 11906, from 'Ikrima, says Nafi' ibn al-Azraq told Ibn 'Abbas that whoever claims some people leave the Fire contradicts God's "nor will they come out of it", and Ibn 'Abbas said: woe to you, read what is before it, this is about unbelievers.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 294}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
