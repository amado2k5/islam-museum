## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar shrouded his son Waqid ibn Abdallah, who died in al-Juhfa in ihram; he covered his head and face and said: if we were not in ihram, we would have perfumed him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1048) and Suwayd (495) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.440}
