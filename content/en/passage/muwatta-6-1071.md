## Summary
A saying of Malik: whoever doubts his circumambulation after he has prayed the two rak'as of circumambulation should repeat and complete his circumambulation with certainty, and then repeat the two rak'as, because there is no prayer for a circumambulation until the seven are complete. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1294) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.494}
