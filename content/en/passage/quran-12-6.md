**Commentary on verse 6 (pp. 559-561)**

On [[passage/quran-12-6|12:6]] al-Tabari relates Jacob's words to his son Joseph when he told him his dream, 'and so your Lord will choose you', that is, as your Lord showed you the stars, sun and moon prostrating to you, so He will choose you; report 18789, from Ikrima: select you; 18790, from Qatada: He chose and selected him and taught him the interpretation of events, which is 'the interpretation of narratives'. As for 'and teach you the interpretation of narratives', it means your Lord will teach you of the knowledge of what people's narratives come to about what they see in their dreams, which is interpreting dreams; 18791, from Mujahid: the interpretation of dreams; 18792, from Ibn Zayd: the interpretation of speech is knowledge and speech, and Joseph was the best interpreter of people, and he recited 'when he reached his full strength We gave him judgement and knowledge' in [[passage/quran-12-22|12:22]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 560}

As for 'and complete His favour on you', by choosing you and teaching you the interpretation of narratives, 'and on the house of Jacob', that is, on the people of Jacob's religion and creed, his descendants and others, 'as He completed it on your two fathers before, Abraham and Isaac', by taking this one as a friend and saving him from the fire and ransoming that one with a great sacrifice; report 18793, from Ikrima: His favour on Abraham was to save him from the fire and on Isaac to save him from the slaughter. As for 'your Lord is knowing, wise', it means your Lord knows where favour belongs and who is fit for choosing and favour, and is wise in His ordering of His creatures. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 561}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
