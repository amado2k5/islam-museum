## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: the woman in ihram should not wear a face-veil, nor put on gloves. The editor notes that al-Bukhari hung it from Malik, that al-Layth ibn Saad transmitted it from Nafi from Ibn Umar from the Prophet, and that al-Bukhari mentioned corroborations for the face-veil and gloves by Musa ibn Uqba, Ismail ibn Ibrahim, Juwayriya and Ibn Ishaq. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.440}
