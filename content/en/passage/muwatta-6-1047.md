## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Salim ibn Abdallah, from Abdullah ibn Umar, who said: one held back from the House by illness does not release himself until he circumambulates the House and between al-Safa and al-Marwa. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1165) and Suwayd (566) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.486}
