## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth, like His light like a niche with a lamp in a bottle, like a bright planet lit from a blessed olive tree, neither eastern nor western, whose oil almost glows, and if it is not touched by fire, light upon light. Allah guides to His light whom He wills. And Allah sets forth parables for mankind, and Allah is Knowing of all things": meaning, the Exalted, by saying: "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth." Hadi explains: Those in the heavens and the earth, by His light they are guided to the truth, and by His guidance they hold firmly from the confusion of misguidance. The scholars of interpretation differed in interpreting this, with some saying it as we said. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Al-Rabi'.
