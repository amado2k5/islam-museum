## Summary
Malik transmits from Sumayy, client of Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman, from Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman, from Aisha and Umm Salama, the wives of the Prophet, that they said: the Messenger of God would rise in a state of major impurity from intercourse, not a wet dream, and then fast. The editor notes that this was discussed in detail under the previous hadith. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.392}
