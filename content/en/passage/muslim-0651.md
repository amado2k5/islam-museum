## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the merit of congregational prayer in the Book of Mosques of Sahih Muslim: the isha and fajr prayers weigh heavily on the hypocrites, and the Prophet considered acting against those who missed them. {cite:sahih-muslim}
