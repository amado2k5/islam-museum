## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 36 to 38 in a single passage: Iblis said, "My Lord, watch me until the Day they are resurrected," meaning, when You took me out of the heavens and cursed me, delayed me until the Day You raise Your creation from their graves and gather them for the Day of Resurrection. God said to him, "Indeed, You are among those who look to the Day of the appointed time," meaning one who delays his destruction until the appointed time for the destruction of all His creation, when there will be no homes left on earth among the children of Adam.
