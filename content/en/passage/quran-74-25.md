## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 32 as a single section. He explains His saying, “Indeed, he thought and deliberated”: This One whom I created alone, thought about what He revealed to His servant Muhammad of the Qur’an, and deliberated about what He says in it. “So may he be destroyed for how he deliberated” means: Then he cursed how he deliberated what was revealed in it. “Then he looked” means: Then he narrated about that. “Then he frowned” means: Then he closed his eyes. “And scowled” means: His face became grim. From this is the saying of Tawbah ibn al-Humayr: “And I was troubled by her aversion that I saw, and her turning away from my need and her scowling.” And in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
