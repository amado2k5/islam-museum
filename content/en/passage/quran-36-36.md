## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Glory be to Him who created all pairs from what the earth grows, from themselves, and from what they do not know": Allah Almighty says that He created all the different colors of the earth and from themselves. He says: And He created from their offspring, males and females, and also from things they did not know, and He also created pairs from what these polytheists add to and describe as partners and others.
