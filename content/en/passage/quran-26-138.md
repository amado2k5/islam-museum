## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 136 to 138 together in one place. Al-Tabari explains the response of the people of 'Ad to Hud that his admonition and abandonment with them are the same, so they will not believe in him. He mentions two readings in 'Khalq al-Awliyn' with a damma on the khā and lam, a fatḥa on the khā, and a sukun on the lam: the first meaning the custom of the ancients, and the second meaning their lies and hadiths.
