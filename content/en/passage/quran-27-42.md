## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "When she came, it was said, 'Is this your throne?' She said, 'It is as if it were him, and we were given knowledge before her while we were Muslims': When the companion of Sheba came to Solomon, he brought out her throne and said to her: 'Is your throne like this?' She said, likening him to him: 'As if it were him.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
