## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The night enters the day, and the day enters the night, and the sun and the moon are subjected, each running for a name. That is Allah, your Lord; to Him belongs the Sovereign, and those whom you invoke besides Him have whatever they possess of the Qatmir": the night enters the daytime, and that is what is diminished in the night, which he brings into the day and increases it, and the day enters the night, and what is diminished in the parts of the day, increases in the parts of the night, so he brings it into them, as: And from Qatadah, His saying: "The night enters the day and the day enters the night" means adding this to the deficiency of this, and the deficiency of this in the addition of that. My father narrated to me, he said: My uncle narrated to me, he said: My father narrated to me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "The night enters the day and the day enters the night," meaning: it is a diminution of one of them from the other. And His saying: "And He subjected the sun and the moon, each running for a specified term," means: He made the sun and the moon for you a blessing from Him upon you, and mercy from Him to you, so that you may know the number of years and the reckoning, and know night from day. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Atiyah.
