## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "So take revenge on them, for they are with a clear leader." So God took revenge on the darkness of the people of the Ike, and "they" refers to the city of the people of the Ike and the city of the people of Lot, and "to the clear Imam" refers to a penguin they lead and guide on their journey, showing the uprightness of those who follow it. He made the path an imam because he leads and follows.
