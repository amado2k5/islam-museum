**Commentary on verse 174 (p. 252)**

Al-Tabari says: as We set out for your people, Muhammad, the verses of this surah and made clear in it what We did to the earlier nations before your people and the exemplary punishments We brought down on them for their unbelief and their associating others in worship with Me, so We set out other verses and make them clear to your people, that they may be deterred and restrained, turn to obedience to Me and repent of their association and unbelief, and return to belief and the acknowledgement of My oneness, giving obedience to Me alone and abandoning the worship of anything else. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "setting out" and "verse". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 252}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
