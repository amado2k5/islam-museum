## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on wiping the head and the ears: he was asked about a man who performed ablution, forgot to wipe his head, and his ablution dried. He said he thought he should wipe his head, and if he had prayed he should repeat the prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.75}
