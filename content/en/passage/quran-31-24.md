## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 23–24. Al-Tabari mentions that whoever disbelieves, the Prophet should not be saddened by his disbelief, nor should his soul be saddened by regret, because their return is to God, who informs them of their deeds and rewards them for them, and that God knows what their hearts conceal.
