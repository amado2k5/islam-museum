## Summary
Malik says: as for dressing, sewing, dyeing and the like, it is like the cloth: profit is counted on it just as it is counted on cloth. If he sold the cloth and did not specify any of what I named, then no profit is counted on it. If the cloth is lost, the hire is counted, and no profit is counted. If the cloth is not lost, the sale is undone between them, unless they agree on something permitted between them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.198}
