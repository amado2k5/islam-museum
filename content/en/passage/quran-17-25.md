## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Your Lord knows best what is in your souls if you are righteous, for indeed, He is Forgiving to those who repent": Your Lord, the Exalted, says: O people, He is "more knowledgeable" than you "of what is in your souls" of honoring the affairs of your fathers and mothers, honoring them, honoring them, and belittling their rights, disobedience to them, and other consciences in your hearts. Nothing of this is hidden from Him, and He repays you for both good and bad deeds, so beware of harboring ill grievances against them and making them disobedient. And His saying, 'If you are righteous,' it means: If you purify your intentions regarding them, obey Allah in what He commands you to do to them and fulfill their rights upon you, after a slip or slip in a duty they owe you, along with fulfilling what He has obligated you in other obligatory duties, then He is forgiving for those who repent after a slip and those who repent after a slip. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ata.
