## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "But We established generations, and life prolonged for them, and I did not dwell among the people of Midian, reciting Our verses to them, but We were messengers": meaning that Allah exalted Him by saying: "But We would have established generations," but We created nations and then created them after that, "and life became prolonged for them." And His saying: 'And I was not a resident among the people of Midian,' it means: I was not a resident among the people of Midian. It is said: I stayed in a place where I stayed with a place of resting there. A'sha Tha'labah said: He stayed and shortened his night to increase his time, then he went and left a meeting of the martyred man. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
