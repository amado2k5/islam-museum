## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr died in his sleep, and Aisha freed many slaves on his behalf. Malik said: this is the most beloved to me of what I have heard on this. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.332}
