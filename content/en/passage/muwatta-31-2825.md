## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from his father, that Umar ibn al-Khattab entered upon Abu Bakr al-Siddiq while he was pulling his tongue. Umar said to him: stop, may God forgive you. Abu Bakr said: this has brought me to the watering places. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.586}
