## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Nothing is said to you except what was said to the messengers before you: Indeed, your Lord is Forgiving and Severe in punishment": These polytheists, who deny what you brought to them from your Lord, say to you only what was said before them from the nations before you. He says to him: So be patient with what befell you from them, just as the people of resolve among the messengers were patient, "and do not be like the companion of the fish." He also quotes in the passage from Qatadah.
