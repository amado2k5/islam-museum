## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "O you who have believed, do not enter houses other than your own until you have found comfort and greeted their inhabitants; that is better for you so that you may remember": The interpreters differed on this, with some saying: Its interpretation: O you who have believed, do not enter houses other than your own until you have asked permission.
