**Commentary on verse 170 (pp. 216-217)**

Al-Tabari says the readers differed over "those who hold fast": some read it with a light, silent second letter, from one verb for holding, and others with a doubled letter, from a stronger form of the same verb. He means those who act by what is in God's book; "and establish prayer" with its limits, without wasting its times; "We do not waste the reward of the reformers": whoever of My creation does this, I do not waste the reward of his good work. Report 15329, from Ibn Zayd: the book of God that Moses brought. Report 15330, from Mujahid: from the Jews or the Christians. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "establishing prayer". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 216} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 217}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
