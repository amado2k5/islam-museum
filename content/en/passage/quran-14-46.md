## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions several narrations about the management of the oppressors, including from Ali and Ibn Mas'ud: that a king took eagle chicks, fed them meat until they grew up, then tied a box to their legs and hung the meat over it, causing them to rise until the mountains became like smoke. Then he ordered the staff to be straightened and it descended. Some link this king to those who pleaded with Abraham for his Lord, and Mujahid mentions that some attributed it to the Persians, others to Bakhtinasr.
