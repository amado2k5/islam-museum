## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari gives several narrations about the scheme of the wrongdoers. In one, from Ali and from Ibn Mas'ud, a king raised young eagles on meat, tied a box to their legs with meat hung above it, and they rose until the mountains looked like smoke, until he was told to point the staff and they came down. Some narrations link this king to the one who argued with Abraham about his Lord. Mujahid reports that some attributed the story to the Persians and others to Nebuchadnezzar.
