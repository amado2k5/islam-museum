## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 55 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "so that they may deny what We have given them, and enjoy yourselves, for you will know": to deny Allah's favor in what He has given them of removing harm from them. "So enjoy yourselves, and you will know." This is a warning from Allah to those whose attributes He described in these verses and a warning to them. He, the Exalted, says to them: Enjoy in this worldly life until your appointed term reaches you, and you reach the appointed time for your life and enjoyment therein. Through that, you will return to your Lord, know the consequences of meeting Him, and know the consequences of what your hands have earned, and you will know the bad consequences of your affairs, and regret when regret does not benefit you.
