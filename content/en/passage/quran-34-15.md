## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, for Sheba in their dwelling was a sign of Paradise, on the right and to the left. Eat from the provision of your Lord and be grateful to Him, a good land, and a Forgiving Lord": The children of Sheba in their dwelling had a clear sign and clear proof that they had no lord except the one who had bestowed upon them the blessings they were in. And Saba from the Messenger of God was given the name of the father of Yemen.
