## Summary
Malik says of a slave-woman with a man who has had intercourse with her, and who then wants to have intercourse with her sister: she is not lawful to him until her sister is made forbidden to him by marriage, manumission, a writing of freedom, or the like, or until he marries her to his slave or to another. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.46}
