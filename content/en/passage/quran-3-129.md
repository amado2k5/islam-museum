## Summary
Verse 129 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 129 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The meaning (p. 203).** Abu Ja'far says: you have no say in the matter, Muhammad; to God belongs everything between the ends of the heavens and the earth, from the sunrise to its setting, apart from you and from them; He rules among them as He wills and decides as He pleases, so He accepts the repentance of whom He wishes among those of His creatures who disobey His command and prohibition and then forgives them, and punishes whom He wills for their offence and takes vengeance on them. He is the Forgiving who covers the sins of those He wishes to cover from His creation, out of His favour, by pardon and overlooking, and the Merciful to them in not hastening their punishment despite the gravity of the sins they commit. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 203}
- From Ibn Humayd, Salama, Ibn Ishaq (7822): He forgives sins and has mercy on His servants despite what is in them. The editor notes it is in Ibn Hisham's Sira (3: 115) and follows reports ending with 7804. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 203}
