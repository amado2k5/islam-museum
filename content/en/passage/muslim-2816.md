## Summary
A report of Aisha in the Book of the Description of the Resurrection in Sahih Muslim: entry into Paradise is by God's mercy and not by deeds alone, together with the command to be steady and moderate. It is a comprehensive text on deeds and mercy. {cite:sahih-muslim}
