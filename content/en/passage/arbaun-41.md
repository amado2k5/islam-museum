## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As: "None of you believes until his desire is subject to what I have brought". An-Nawawi calls it sound, and it is transmitted through Nu'aym ibn Hammad, whose narration Ibn Rajab finds hard to accept as sound. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
