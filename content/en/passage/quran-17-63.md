## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "He said, 'Go, so whoever follows you among them, indeed, Hell is your recompense with a full recompense.'" Allah said to Iblis when he said to him, "If you delay until the Day of Resurrection, I will surely have his descendants except a few." Go, for you have delayed you. Whoever follows you among them, meaning from the descendants of Adam and obeys you, then Hell is your reward and theirs. He says: Your reward is for your prayer to them for my disobedience, and their reward for following you and for their disobedience. My command is a "full recompense": He says: a complete and abundant reward. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying, "He said, 'Go, for whoever follows you among them, for Hell is your full recompense,' the punishment of Hell is their recompense, and it is a wrath from Allah against His enemies, so nothing will be equivalent to its punishment for them." He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid: "Indeed, Hell is your reward with a full recompense," he said: abundant. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
