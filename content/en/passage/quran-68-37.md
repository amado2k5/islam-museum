## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 39 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Or do you have a book in which you study?” as follows: God Almighty says to the polytheists of Quraysh: Do you, O people, have a book revealed to you by God, through a messenger from among His messengers, that allows you to choose what you want, and in which you study what you say? He then quotes statements attributed to Ibn Zayd in this section.
