## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on dry ablution: a man in sexual impurity who performs dry ablution may read his portion of the Qur'an and perform voluntary prayers as long as he finds no water, in a place where he may pray with dry ablution. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.100}
