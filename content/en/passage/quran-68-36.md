## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 36 as a single unit. He interprets His saying, “What is the matter with you? How do you judge?”: Do you equate those of His servants who obey God with those who disobey Him in terms of honor? He, may His praise be exalted, says: Do not equate them, for they are not equal in the sight of God. Rather, the one who obeys Him receives lasting honor, and the one who disobeys Him receives lasting humiliation.
