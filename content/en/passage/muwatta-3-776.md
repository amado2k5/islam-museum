## Summary
A saying of Malik: all expiations, the fast-breaking zakat and the tithe zakat are all reckoned by the smaller mudd, the mudd of the Prophet, except for the expiation for zihar, which is reckoned by the mudd of Hisham, the larger mudd. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.383}
