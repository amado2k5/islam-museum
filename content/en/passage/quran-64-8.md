## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “So believe in Allah and His Messenger and the light which We have sent down. And Allah is All-Aware of what you do,” explains: So believe in Allah and His Messenger, O polytheists who deny the Resurrection, and in His informing you that you will be resurrected after your death, and that after your affliction you will be raised from your graves. “And the light which We have sent down” means: And believe in the light which We have sent down, which is this Qur’an which Allah sent down to His Prophet Muhammad. “And Allah is All-Aware of what you do” means: God Almighty says: God is All-Aware of your deeds, O people, encompassing them, recording them all, nothing of them is hidden from Him, and He will reward you for all of them.
