**Commentary on verse 105 (p. 285)**

On "and how many a sign in the heavens and the earth they pass by while turning away from it", al-Tabari says: how many a sign, lesson and proof of God there is in the heavens and the earth, like the sun, the moon and the stars and the like among the signs of the heavens, and like the mountains, seas, plants and trees and the rest among the signs of the earth; "they pass by it" means they see them and pass by turning away from them, not taking a lesson from them or reflecting on them and on what they point to of the oneness of their Lord, and that divinity is fitting only for the One, the Overpowering, who created them and created everything and ordered them. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "ka'ayyin". The interpreters said the same: report 19953, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "how many a sign in the heavens and the earth they pass by", which in Abd Allah's codex is "they walk over it"; heaven and earth are two great signs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 285}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
