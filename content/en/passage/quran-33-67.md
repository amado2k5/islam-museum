## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 68 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Our Lord, indeed we obeyed our leaders and our elders, and they led us astray from the way': The disbelievers will say on the Day of Resurrection in Hell: Our Lord, indeed we obeyed our leaders in misguidance and our leaders in polytheism," so they led us astray from the way. He means: so they removed us from the path of truth, the path of guidance, faith in You, acknowledgment of Your oneness, and sincere obedience to You in this world. "Our Lord, give them double the punishment," meaning: He punished them with punishment equal to our torment that we did. "And curse them with a great curse," meaning: And he humiliated them greatly. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
