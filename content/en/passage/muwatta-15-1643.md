## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that in the Prophet's time a man performed the mutual curse with his wife and disowned her child. The Messenger separated them and assigned the child to the mother. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.78}
