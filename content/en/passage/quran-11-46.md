**Commentary on verse 46 (p. 340)**

On [[passage/quran-11-46|11:46]] al-Tabari says: God said, Noah, the one you drowned and destroyed, whom you mention as of your family, is not of your family. The interpreters differed on the sense of 'he is not of your family': some said it means he is not of your offspring but of another, and said it came from wrongdoing; the editor explains 'hinth' as sin and disobedience and 'children of hinth' as children of fornication. Report 18208, from al-Hasan: he was not his son; report 18209, from Abu Ja'far: his wife's son; report 18210 begins at the foot of the page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 340}

We stop here at the foot of p. 340, in the middle of the commentary on verse 46; the next batch begins at p. 341. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 340}
