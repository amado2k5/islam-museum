## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman from Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Harith al-Taymi, who said he heard his father say: the aqiqa is recommended, even if it is only a sparrow. The editor notes that some copies say "it is recommended". {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.646}
