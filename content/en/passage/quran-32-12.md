## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And if you could see when the criminals lowered their heads before their Lord, our Lord, We saw and heard, and then we returned to do righteousness. Indeed, we are certain": If you could see, O Muhammad, those who say, "When We have led astray in the land, are we in a new creation?" when they lowered their heads before their Lord out of shame before their Lord, because of those who preceded them in their sins in this world, they say: "Our Lord, have made us see" what we used to deny about as punishment for Your sinners, and "We hear" from You the confirmation of what Your messengers used to command us in this world. "So we returned," meaning: So we returned to the world to act in obedience to You, and that righteous deed, 'Indeed, we are certain,' it says: We now know what we were ignorant of in this world of Your oneness, and that it is not fitting to worship anyone but You, nor should there be any Lord besides You. You give life and cause death, raise those in the graves after death and annihilation, and do as You wish. And as we said regarding the phrase: "They lowered their heads," the interpreters said. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
