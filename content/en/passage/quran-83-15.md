## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15-17 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Nay, indeed, they will be veiled from their Lord that Day,” as meaning: The matter is not as these deniers of the Day of Judgment claim, that they have a close relationship with God. Rather, on that Day, they will be veiled from their Lord, neither seeing Him nor experiencing any of His favor. The scholars of interpretation differed in their understanding of the phrase, “Indeed, they will be veiled from their Lord that Day.” Some said it means they will be veiled from His favor. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
