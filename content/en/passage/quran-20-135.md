## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Every one who is in waiting, so wait for you, and you will know who are the companions of the straight path and who is guided.": Say, O Muhammad: All of you, O polytheists of Allah, are lying in wait, saying: I await who will be successful, and what will happen to my affairs and your affairs is suspended, waiting for the cycles of time. "So wait," he means: wait and wait, and you will know from the people of the straight, moderate path that has no crookedness when God's command comes and the Day of Resurrection is established. Is it us or you? Whoever is guided says: And you will then know who is the one who follows the Sunnah of the path, the one who is aiming and not going unjustly from us and from you.
