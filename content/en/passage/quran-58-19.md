## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Satan seized them and made them forget. The remembrance of Allah—those are the party of Satan." Unquestionably, the party of Satan are the losers: meaning He mentioned him by saying: "Satan has seized them," Satan overcame them, "so he made them forget the remembrance of Allah. Those are the party of Satan," meaning his soldiers and followers. "Indeed, the party of Satan are the losers." He means: Indeed, the soldiers of Satan and his followers are the doomed and deceived in their deal.
