## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "When he said to his father, 'Do you not worship that which neither hears nor sees, nor avails you at all?': Remember him when he said to his father, 'O my father, why do you worship that which cannot be heard?' He means: What do you do by worshiping an idol that neither hears nor sees, nor does it avail you at all?' He means: 'And nothing harm can be averted from you.' Rather, it is a figurative image that neither harms nor benefits. He says, 'What do you do with worship? What is this description of?' Worship the One who, when called upon hears, hears your supplication; when you are surrounded, He sees you and supports you; and when harm befalls you, it is repelled from you.
