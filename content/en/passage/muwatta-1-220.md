## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id and Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, in the chapter on recitation at dawn: al-Furafisa ibn Umayr al-Hanafi said he had memorised the surah of Yusuf only from Uthman ibn Affan's recitation of it in the dawn prayer, from how often he repeated it. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Ibn Wahb in al-Tahawi, al-Shafi'i and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.133}
