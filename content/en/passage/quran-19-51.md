## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And mention in the Book Moses; indeed, he was sincere and was a messenger and a prophet": He says to His Prophet: "And remember, O Muhammad, in Our Book which We sent down to you Moses son of Imran, and tell your people that he was sincere." Reciters differed in their interpretation; the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra and some Kufans read it as: "Indeed, he was sincere" with a kasra on the lam from the sincere, meaning: he was sincere in worship of Allah and dedicated Him to divinity, without making Him a partner in it. Most of the reciters of Kufa read this excluding Asim: "Indeed, he was sincere" with a fatha on the lam from 'mukhlis,' meaning: Moses was sincere and chosen for His message, and made him a messenger. Abu Ja'far said: The correct view in my view is that he was sincere in worshiping God, sincere to the message and prophethood. So, whichever one reads, the reader is correct.
