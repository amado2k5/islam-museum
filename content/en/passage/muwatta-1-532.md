## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Atika bint Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl, the wife of Umar ibn al-Khattab, that she used to ask Umar's permission to go out to the mosque, and he was silent. She would say: by God, I will go out unless you forbid me, and he did not forbid her. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.274}
