## Summary
Malik said: this applies to anyone who is freed: his inheritance goes to the nearest of the men who freed him, from among his children or agnatic kin, on the day the freed person dies after his manumission, and he becomes inheritable by clientage. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.359}
