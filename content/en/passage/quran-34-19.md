## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So they said, 'Our Lord, distance between our journeys,' and they wronged themselves, so We made them as hadiths and torn them apart. Indeed, in that are signs for every patient and grateful person": Reciters differed in reciting the phrase: "Our Lord, distance between our journeys," so the majority of the reciters of Medina and Kufa read it as: "Our Lord, distance between our journeys" in the case of supplication and asking with the alif, and some people of Mecca and Basra read it as "after" with a stressed 'ayn' on supplication as well. It is mentioned from the earlier scholars that he used to recite it: 'Our Lord, distance our journeys,' as a predicate from God that God did that to them. It is narrated from another that he recited it: 'Our Lord, after,' also in the form of the predicate, except that the Lord is a caller. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
