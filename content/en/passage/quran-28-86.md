## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And you did not hope that the Book would be sent to you except as a mercy from your Lord, so do not be a supporter for the disbelievers": And you did not expect, O Muhammad, that this Qur'an would be revealed to you, so you would learn the news and reports about those who came before you and the events after you, from things that have not yet happened, what you have not witnessed and will not witness, then recite it to your people from Quraysh, except that your Lord has mercy on you and revealed it to you. Thus, His saying: "Except as a mercy from your Lord" is a broken exception. And His saying: "So do not be a supporter for the disbelievers," means: So praise your Lord for bestowing upon you His mercy upon you by sending down this Book upon you, and do not be a helper to those who disbelieve in your Lord in disbelief in Him. It is also said: This is from the 'Mu'taar' meaning to come forward, and the meaning of the lam is: 'Indeed, He who has decreed the Qur'an upon you, so He revealed it upon you,' and you did not expect it to be revealed to you, so you would be a prophet before that, for He would have sent you to a Hereafter.
