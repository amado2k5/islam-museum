## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “Do not move your tongue with it to hasten it,” as meaning: O Muhammad, do not move your tongue with the Quran to hasten it. Scholars of interpretation differed on the reason why he was told, “Do not move your tongue with it to hasten it.” Some said it was because whenever something was revealed to him, he would hasten to recite it, wanting to memorize it out of his love for it. So he was told, “Do not hasten it, for We will preserve it for you.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Sufyan.
