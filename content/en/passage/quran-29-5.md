## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 6 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Whoever hopes for the meeting of Allah, then the appointed time of Allah is coming, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing": Whoever hopes for the Day of Meeting Him and hopes for His reward, then the appointed time of Allah, which He has postponed to resurrect His creation for recompense and punishment, is coming near. "And He is the Hearing," meaning: By Allah, the one who hopes for the reward of meeting him, the All-Hearing, because He says: We believe in Allah, the All-Knowing, with the truth of his statement, that whoever denies him has believed. And His saying: "And whoever strives [in jihad] only strives for himself," means: Whoever strives against his enemy among the polytheists is only striving for himself, because he does so seeking reward from Allah for his struggle and to escape punishment. Allah has no need for him to do that, because Allah is self-sufficient from all His creation; to Him belongs the dominion, creation, and command.
