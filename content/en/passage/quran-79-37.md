## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 41 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "But as for him who transgresses": As for him who rebels against his Lord, disobeys Him, and is too proud to worship Him. On the authority of Ibn Abi Najih, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding the word "transgresses," he said: He disobeyed. He then quotes statements from Mujahid in this section.
