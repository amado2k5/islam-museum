## Summary
Yahya said that Malik said about the hired worker on campaign: if he witnessed the fighting, was with the people at the battle, and was free, he has his share; if he did not do so, he has no share. Malik holds that shares are given only to free men who witnessed the fighting. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.581}
