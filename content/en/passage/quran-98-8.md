## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Their reward with their Lord is Gardens of Eden beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever, God being pleased with them and they with Him. That is for whoever fears his Lord,” explains that the reward of those who believed and did righteous deeds with their Lord on the Day of Resurrection is “Gardens of Eden,” meaning gardens of permanent residence, wherein rivers flow beneath their trees, “wherein they will abide forever.” This means they will remain there eternally, never leaving or dying. “God being pleased with them” means He is pleased with their obedience to Him in this world and their efforts to save themselves from His punishment. “And they are pleased with Him” means they are pleased with what He has given them. The reward on that Day is for their obedience to their Lord in this world, and He will reward them with honor for it. And His saying: “That is for whoever fears his Lord” means that this good which I have described and promised to those who believed and did righteous deeds on the Day of Resurrection is for whoever fears his Lord, meaning whoever fears God in this world in secret and in public, and is mindful of Him by performing His obligations and avoiding His prohibitions. And with God is all success.
