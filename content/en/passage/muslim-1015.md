## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the obligation to spend from good things, in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim. It depicts the traveller who raises his hands to heaven, dishevelled, while all he eats and wears is unlawful. {cite:sahih-muslim}
