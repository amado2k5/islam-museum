## Summary
It reached Malik that Uthman ibn Affan, when he made the lesser pilgrimage, sometimes did not dismount from his mount until he returned. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1128) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.466}
