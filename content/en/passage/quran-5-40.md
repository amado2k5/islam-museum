**Commentary on verse 40: "do you not know that God has dominion over the heavens and the earth", pp. 300-301**

Al-Tabari says God asks His prophet whether those who said the Fire would touch them only for a few days, claiming to be God's sons and beloved, do not know that God directs what is in the heavens and on earth, turning and creating it, and nothing in them resists what He wills, since all is His dominion and His to command; there is no kinship between Him and anything in them for Him to favour it through relation by saving it from His punishment when it disbelieves in Him and opposes His command and prohibition, or to put in the Fire one who obeys Him because of distance of kinship; rather He punishes whom He wills of His creation in this world for disobedience by killing, swallowing up by the earth, transformation and other kinds of His punishment, and forgives whom He wills of them by turning to them from their unbelief and disobedience, saving them from destruction.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 301}

"God has power over all things" means He is able to punish whom He wishes to punish for disobedience and to forgive whom He wishes to forgive by saving from destruction through repentance, and all else, because creation is His creation, dominion His dominion and servants His servants. The words "do you not know" are addressed to the Prophet while those meant are the groups of the Children of Israel in his city and around it, and he had shown earlier that the Arabs use a like form of speech.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 301}
