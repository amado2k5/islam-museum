## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 34 as one section. He explains His saying “I will cast him into Hell”: God Almighty means by His saying: “I will cast him into Hell” I will bring him to one of the gates of Hell called Saqar and Saqar is not declined because it is one of the names of Hell and in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Zayd bin Aslam and Sufyan.
