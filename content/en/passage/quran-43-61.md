## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 61 to 62 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Indeed, it is knowledge of the Hour, so do not hesitate thereby and follow this is a straight path": The interpreters differed regarding the 'ha' in the phrase "indeed," what it means, and who mentioned what it is. Some said: it is from the mention of Jesus, and it refers to him. They said: The meaning of the statement is: that Jesus' appearance is knowledge by which he knows the coming of the Hour, because his appearance is one of its signs and his descent to earth is evidence of the annihilation of this world and the arrival of the Hereafter. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
