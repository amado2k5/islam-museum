After the title pages (p. 147 the title of the commentary on Surat Al Imran, p. 148 blank), p. 149 begins with the opening formula, a prayer for ease and the words 'Abu Jafar Muhammad ibn Jarir ibn Yazid reported to us'; the editor notes that the print adds a prayer for him after the name and that he kept the manuscript's wording. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 149} On the separate letters that open the sura, al-Tabari says he has already given their meaning, enough not to repeat it here, and likewise for the name of God; the editor refers to part 1. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 149}
