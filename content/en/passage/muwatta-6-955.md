## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that he used to say: Abdullah ibn Umar did not call the talbiya while he was circumambulating the House. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1093), Suwayd (503) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi 5/43) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.455}
