## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 2 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "The reckoning of the people has drawn near while they are heedless, turning away": The reckoning of people is near: the reckoning of people is near, for their deeds they did in this world, and for the blessings He bestowed upon them in their bodies, their bodies, their food, their drinks, their clothes, and other blessings with them. He asks them what they did in them, whether they obeyed him in them, and whether they followed His command and forbidden them all, or did they disobey Him and disobey His command in all of them? "And they are heedless, turning away"—meaning: they are in this world because of what Allah will do to them on the Day of Resurrection, and because of the imminent accountability of them among them, and His approach to them out of forgetfulness and heedlessness. They turned away from that, so they abandoned reflection, preparation, and preparedness, out of ignorance of what they would face at that time of great affliction and severe terrors.
