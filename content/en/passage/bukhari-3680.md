## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar: Abu Hurayra's report of the Prophet's dream of Umar's palace in Paradise, and of his reluctance to look at it because of Umar's well-known jealousy. It is among the best-known virtues of Umar in the Sahih. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
