## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 43–44. Al-Tabari mentions that God relieved Job and granted him his family and their like as mercy from Us and a reminder for those of understanding, and commanded him to take a small piece of cloth in his hand and strike with it and not to break it. Indeed, We found him patient. What an excellent servant, indeed he repents.
