## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The Gardens of Eden flow beneath them rivers, abiding therein forever, and that is the reward of those who purify themselves." He explains: "And whoever comes to him as a believer who has done righteous deeds, those will have the highest degrees." Then he clarified what those higher levels are, saying: They are "Gardens of Eden," meaning: gardens of residence from which they disappear, never end, and no annihilation (rivers flow beneath them), meaning: rivers flow beneath their trees, "abiding therein forever," meaning: They remain there indefinitely without limitation, for the gardens from the phrase 'Gardens of Eden' are raised by refuting degrees, as he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj regarding the verse: 'And whoever comes to him as a believer who has done righteous deeds—those will have the highest degrees,' he said: 'Eden.' In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Jurayj.
