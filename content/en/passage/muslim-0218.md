## Summary
A report of Ibn Abbas in the chapter on the number of the people of Paradise and the seventy thousand, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Those who enter without reckoning are marked by giving up incantations and omens together with reliance on God. {cite:sahih-muslim}
