## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The living will emerge from the dead, and the dead will emerge from the living, and the earth will come to life after its death. And thus you will emerge": Pray at these times, O people, to Allah who brings the living out of the dead, and He is the living human from dead water, and He brings forth dead water from the living person, "and gives life to the earth after its death," so He makes it grow, and its crops come forth after it is ruined and its soil, "and thus you come out." He says: Just as He revives the earth after its death, bringing forth its plants and sowing, He also revives you after your death, bringing you back alive from your graves to the station of reckoning. We have previously explained before interpreting His statement: "The living emerge from the dead, and the dead emerge from the living," and we mentioned the differences among interpreters on this matter, which is more necessary to repeat it here. However, we will mention some of the reports we did not mention there, God willing. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
