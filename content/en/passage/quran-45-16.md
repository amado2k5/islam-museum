## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave the Children of Israel the Scripture and wisdom and prophethood and provided them with good things and preferred them over the worlds": "And indeed We given," O Muhammad, "the Children of Israel" means the Torah and the Gospel, and wisdom means understanding the Book and knowledge of the laws that were not revealed in the Book. "And prophethood" means "and made from them prophets and messengers to creation." "And provided them with good things," he means: "And We fed them of the good things of Our provisions, and that is what He fed them of manna and quail." "And preferred them over The worlds" says: And We preferred them over the scholars of their time during the days of Pharaoh and his era in Egypt and the Levant.
