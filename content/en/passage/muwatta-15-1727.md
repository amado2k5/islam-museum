## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Miswar ibn Makhrama, that he told him Subayah al-Aslamiyya gave birth a few nights after her husband's death. The Messenger told her: you are free, marry whom you wish. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.105}
