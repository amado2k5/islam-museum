## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Aswad Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman, who was an orphan in the care of Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Urwa, from Aisha, that the Messenger of God made the pilgrimage single. Ibn Abd al-Barr says this hadith is extracted from the previous hadith; Malik cited it as a proof for his school that single pilgrimage is more excellent. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.451}
