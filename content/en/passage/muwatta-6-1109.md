## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu Jafar al-Qari that Abdallah ibn Ayyash ibn Abi Rabia al-Makhzumi offered two camels, one of them Bakhtiyya. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1203) and Suwayd (523) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.509}
