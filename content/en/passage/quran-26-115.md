## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 114 to 116 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, I am but a clear warner": I am only a warner to you from your Lord, warning you of His severity and His authority over your disbelief in Him, which is clear: He says: A warner whose warning has become clear to you, and his advice has not been concealed from you. "They said, 'If you do not desist, O Noah, you will surely be among the stoned.'" He means: He said to his people: "If you do not desist, O Noah, from what you say, and call to Him, and criticize our gods, you will be among the cursed." He says: We will insult you.
