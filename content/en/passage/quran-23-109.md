## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, there was a group of My servants who said, 'Our Lord, we believe, so forgive us and have mercy on us, and You are the best of the merciful'": "Indeed," and this 'inna' in His saying 'inna' is the 'ha' that the people of the unknown Arabic call it. I have explained its meaning previously and its inclusion in the discourse, which is necessary to repeat it here. "There was a group of My servants," he says: They were a group of My servants, who are the people of faith in God, who say in this world: "Our Lord, we believe" in You and Your messengers, and what they brought from You. "So forgive us our sins and have mercy on us," and You are better than the womb of those afflicted, so do not punish us with Your punishment.
