## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And how many of a town is more powerful than your city that expelled you? We destroyed them, and there was no helper against them": And how many cities, O Muhammad, are there that are more powerful than your town? Its people say it is more powerful and more numerous, and I counted many of the people of your village, which is Mecca, and the report was reported from the town, meaning its people. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ikrimah.
