## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "that they may deny what We have given them, so enjoy yourselves, and you will come to know": Allah Almighty warns those polytheists about whom He informed that if harm is revealed to them, they deny it, so that they may deny what We have given them. He says: Then they associate partners with their Lord so that they may disbelieve: meaning they deny the blessing You have bestowed upon them by removing the harm they were in, and replacing that with prosperity, fertility, and well-being. That prosperity and abundance is what He has given them, the Exalted, who said: "for what We have given them." And His saying "so enjoy" means: So enjoy, O people, what We have given you of ease and abundance in this world, "for you will know" if you come to your Lord what you will receive of His punishment and the great punishment for your disbelief in Him in this world. Some have read: "So they will know" with the ya, meaning: to deny what We have given them. They have enjoyed pleasure in the predicate sense, so they will know.
