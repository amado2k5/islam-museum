## Summary
Malik was asked whether one in ihram may eat food that contains saffron; he said: what fire has touched of that, there is no harm in the one in ihram eating it; but what fire has not touched, the one in ihram does not eat. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1056) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.444}
