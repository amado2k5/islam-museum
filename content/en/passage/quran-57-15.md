## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So on the Day no ransom will be taken from you, nor from those who disbelieved. Your refuge is the Fire; it is your protector, and wretched is the destination." He says, exalted be He, informing about the believers' saying to the hypocrites, after distinguishing between them on the Day of Resurrection, "So today," O hypocrites, "no ransom will be taken from you," meaning: in exchange and replacement, He says: It will not be taken from you instead of your punishment and punishment, and He will save you from the punishment of Allah, "nor from those who disbelieve." He says: No ransom is taken from those who disbelieve. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
