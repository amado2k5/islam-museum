**Commentary on verse 86 (pp. 411-412)**

Al-Tabari says: when a surah of the Qur'an is sent down to you, Muhammad, telling these hypocrites "believe in God", that is, affirm God, "and strive with His Messenger", that is, fight the polytheists with the Messenger of God, "the men of wealth among them ask your leave", meaning the owners of riches and property among them ask your leave to stay behind from you and sit with their families, "and they say, leave us", that is, they say to you let us be of those who sit in their houses with the weak among the people and the sick and those unable to go out with you on the journey. The editor refers to earlier explanations of striving, wealth, leaving and sitting. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 411}

What the interpreters said agrees with what we said on the meaning of "wealth". Report 17061, from Ibn Abbas, through Ali ibn Dawud, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: "the men of wealth ask your leave", he said, meaning the people of riches; report 17062, from him, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: "the men of wealth among them", meaning the rich. Report 17063, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: among them were Abdallah ibn Ubayy and al-Jadd ibn Qays, and God denounced that against them. The editor notes that Ibn Hisham mentions Ibn Ubayy alone and does not mention al-Jadd ibn Qays. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 412}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
