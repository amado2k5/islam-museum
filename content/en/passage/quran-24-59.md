## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And when the children among you reach puberty, let them ask permission as those before them were permitted. Thus Allah makes clear to you His verses. And Allah is Knowing and Wise": when the children among your children and relatives reach puberty, and by "from you" means "from among your free men," the dream means to have a wet dream, and they have a dream. "Let them seek permission" means: They should not enter upon you at any time except with permission, neither during the times of the three private parts nor at any other time. And His saying: "As those before them sought permission" means: just as the elders sought permission from the son of a man and his free relatives. Allah Almighty specifically mentioned in this verse children in remembrance and explained their ruling to His servants in seeking permission, without mentioning what our right hands possessed. The previous verse previously defined the ruling on free children and mamluks because the ruling on what your right hands possess is the same, whether it concerns the ruling of their elders or children in that they are permitted during the three hours mentioned by Allah in the previous verse. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
