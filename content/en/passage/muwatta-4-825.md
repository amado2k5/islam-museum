## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on fasting on the feast of breaking the fast, the feast of sacrifice and the whole year. Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet forbade fasting two days: the day of breaking the fast and the day of sacrifice. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik, and through him Abu Ahmad al-Hakim; Rawh ibn Ubada in Ahmad; Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi, Ibn Wahb, Ibn al-Qasim in al-Nasai; and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Nisaburi in Muslim; and that it will come later under number 1103. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.403}
