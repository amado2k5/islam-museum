## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 49 to 51 in a single passage: "And you will see the criminals that Day" — they are those who disbelieved in God and committed polytheism in this world, the Day the earth will be changed from the earth. And "paired in chains": their hands and feet are chained to their necks with chains, which are the bonds of a chain and a chain, singular Safad — according to Ibn Abbas in 'Withaq,' from Al-Dahhak the chains, from Qatadah the chains and chains, and from Al-A'mash and Ibn Zayd similarly. He elaborates on the exact wording: 'I chained him in Safad' as Safadan and Safad, citing the verse of Amr ibn Kulthum, and whoever makes the singular Safad is Safadan, its plural is Safadan; in 'At'a' it is said 'Asfadtuhu Isfadan' as Al-A'sha said, and it is said Safadani Safadan as Al-Nabigha Al-Dhubyani said.
