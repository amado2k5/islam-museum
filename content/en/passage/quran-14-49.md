## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains that the sinners will be bound together in shackles, their hands and feet fastened to their necks, citing Ibn Abbas, al-Dahhak and Qatada. He takes the verse to describe the wrongdoers on that day, the day the earth is changed and the Resurrection is established.
