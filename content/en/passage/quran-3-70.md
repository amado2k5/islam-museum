## Summary
Verse 70 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 70 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of verse 70.** Al-Tabari says the address is to the People of the Book among the Jews and Christians, and the meaning is: why do you deny what is in God's book that He sent down to you on the tongues of your prophets, of its signs and proofs, when you bear witness that it is true from your Lord. This is a rebuke from God to the people of the two books for disbelieving in Muhammad and denying his prophethood while they find him in their books, even as they testify that what is in their books is true and from God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 502}

**Report 7219 (begun).** Al-Tabari gives (Bishr, from Yazid, from Said, from Qatada) a comment on this verse beginning with the word for bearing witness, which continues on p. 503. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 502}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at the beginning of report 7219 on p. 502 and continue from p. 503.
