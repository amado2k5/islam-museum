## Summary
Verse 70 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 70 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of verse 70.** Al-Tabari says the address is to the People of the Book among the Jews and Christians, and the meaning is: on what ground do you reject what God's book contains, which He sent down to you through your prophets, of its signs and proofs, when you yourselves attest that it is true from your Lord. This is a rebuke from God to the people of the two books for disbelieving in Muhammad and denying his prophethood while they find him in their books, even as they testify that what is in their books is true and from God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 502}

**Report 7219 (begun).** Al-Tabari gives (Bishr, from Yazid, from Said, from Qatada) a comment on this verse beginning with the word for bearing witness, which continues on p. 503. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 502}

**The end of report 7219 and reports 7220-7222.** Qatada (Bishr, from Yazid, from Said) completes his comment: the addressees acknowledge that the description of Muhammad stands in their scripture and then reject him and refuse to believe, though they find it with them in the Torah and the Gospel; he points to the verse about the unlettered prophet, [[passage/quran-7-157|7:157]]. Al-Tabari gives report 7220 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Ibn Abi Jafar, from his father, from al-Rabi) to the same effect. In report 7221 (Muhammad, from Ahmad, from Asbat, from al-Suddi) the signs of God are Muhammad himself, and their acknowledgement is their own testimony that he is the right one whom they find established with them. In report 7222 (al-Qasim, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Ibn Jurayj) the point is the acknowledgement that Islam is the religion God approved alone and that He has none besides it, as in [[passage/quran-3-19|3:19]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 503}

**The editor on the chain of report 7222.** He says the printed text and the manuscript both dropped the words "al-Qasim told us, he said" from the chain, and he restored them because it is a chain that recurs from the beginning of the commentary, the nearest case being report 7200, and it comes shortly after in its right form in report 7226. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 503}
