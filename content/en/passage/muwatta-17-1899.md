## Summary
Malik transmits from Yunus ibn Yusuf, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Umar ibn al-Khattab passed by Hatib ibn Abi Baltaa, who was selling raisins of his in the market. Umar said to him: either raise the price, or leave our market. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.180}
