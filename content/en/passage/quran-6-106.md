## Summary
Verse 106 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 106 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

God says to His prophet Muhammad: follow, Muhammad, what your Lord has commanded you in His revelation to you, act on it and refrain from what He forbade you in it, and leave aside what the polytheists of your people invite you to of worshipping idols and images, for there is no god but He, that is, no object of worship deserves from you sincere worship but God, who splits the grain and the date-stone, splits the dawn, made the night for rest and the sun and the moon for reckoning; "and turn away from the polytheists": leave aside disputing and quarrelling with them (the editor refers to the earlier note on "a'rid"). Then God abrogated this by what He said in Bara'a (al-Tawba 9:5) about fighting the polytheists, as in report 13736 from Ibn Abbas: as for "turn away from the polytheists" and the like, by which God commanded the believers to pardon the polytheists, it was abrogated by the verse in Bara'a. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 32}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
