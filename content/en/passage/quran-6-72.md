## Summary
Verse 72 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 72 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "and: establish the prayer and fear him; and it is he to whom you will be gathered" al-Tabari says: and we are commanded to establish the prayer. It is said "and that you establish" joined by "an" to the lam of "that we submit", because the sense of "that we submit" is "that we submit", and "and that you establish" is attached to the sense of "that we submit", since the lam goes only with the future of verbs and "an" is among the particles that show the future, so it is joined to it by the agreement of their senses; "an" is therefore in the accusative by being joined to the lam. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 456}

A Basran grammarian said it is either "we are commanded that we submit to the Lord of the worlds, and that you establish the prayer", meaning in order that we submit, as in [[passage/quran-10-104|10:104]], "and I am commanded to be of the believers", and then "and that you establish the prayer and fear him", meaning we are commanded to establish the prayer; or that the verb is connected by the lam and the sense is "I am commanded to be", as the verb is connected by the lam in [[passage/quran-7-154|7:154]], "those who stand in awe of their Lord". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 457}

The editor says the printed text and manuscript have a wording that is not a verse of God's book, the verse being the one he gave, and that another wording is an error with the right form as he gave it; he refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an and to earlier passages on "establishing the prayer", "the gathering" and "the worlds".

So the sense is: we are commanded to establish the prayer, which is performing it within the limits laid on us; "and fear him" says: fear the Lord of the worlds to whom we are commanded to submit, dread him and beware his wrath by performing the prayer laid on you, submitting to him in obedience and making worship pure for him; "and it is he to whom you will be gathered" says: your Lord, the Lord of the worlds, is he to whom you will be gathered on the day of resurrection, and each worker among you will be repaid for his work and every soul be paid in full what it earned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 457}

Verse 72 closes here on p. 457, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-73|6:73]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
