**Commentary on verse 88 (pp. 453-454)**

On [[passage/quran-11-88|11:88]] al-Tabari says: Shu'ayb said to his people, my people, consider, if I stand on clear proof from my Lord in what I call you to, worshipping God and disowning the worship of idols and images, and in what I forbid you of ruining wealth, 'and He has provided me with fair provision from Him', meaning lawful and good, 'and I do not wish to go against you to what I forbid you', that is, I do not wish to forbid you something and then do the opposite; I do only what I command you and refrain only from what I forbid you; report 18496, from Qatada: I would not forbid you what I do myself. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 453}

'I want only reform' means I want, in what I command and forbid you, only your reform and the reform of your affairs, 'as far as I am able', that is, as far as I can reform it, lest a punishment that makes an example of you come on you from God for going against His command and disobeying His Messenger; 'my success is only with God' means my reaching the truth in trying to reform you is only by God, who helps in that, and without His help I would not reach it. 'In Him I trust' means I commit my affair to God, in whom is my confidence and on whom I rely; 'and to Him I turn' means I turn to Him in obedience and return in repentance; reports 18497 to 18501, from Mujahid: I return. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 454}
