## Summary
Yahya narrated to me from Malik, from Amr, client of al-Muttalib, from Anas ibn Malik, that when Uhud appeared to the Prophet, he said: this is a mountain that loves us and we love it. O God, Abraham made Mecca sacred, and I make sacred what lies between its two lava fields. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.467}
