## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We have certainly sent down to you clear signs and examples from those who passed away before you and an admonition for the righteous": And We have certainly sent down to you, O mankind, clear indications and signs, meaning: the detailed explanations of truth from falsehood, and clarifications of that. The reciters differed in their reading; the majority of the reciters of Medina, some Kufans, and Basrans read it as "mubaynat" with a fatha on the ya: meaning 'mufassālāt' (mufāsālāt), and that Allah separated them and made them clear to His servants, so they are Mufāsālī mu'aṭānāt. The general recitation of Kufa reads this as "mubāninat" with a kasra on the ya, meaning that the verses clarify the truth and correctness for people and guide them to the truth. The correct view in our view is that they are two well-known readings, and scholars of each recitation have read each with similar meanings.
