**Commentary on verse 36 (pp. 306-308)**

On [[passage/quran-11-36|11:36]] al-Tabari says: God inspired Noah, after the word had fallen due on his people and God's command had overshadowed them, that none of your people will believe, Noah, in God, affirming His oneness and following you in what you call to, 'except those who have believed', affirming it and following you; 'so do not grieve' means do not be humbled or sad 'at what they used to do', for I shall destroy them and rescue you from them and those who follow you. God inspired this to him after Noah had prayed against them for destruction, as in [[passage/quran-71-26|71:26]]. 'Tabta'is' is a form from 'bu's', hardship, one says 'so-and-so was distressed at the matter'; he cites a line of Labid b. Rabi'a on a daughter or wife and her state after his death. Reports 18121 and 18122, from Mujahid: do not grieve; report 18123, from Ibn Abbas: do not grieve; report 18124, from Qatada: do not be distressed or sad; report 18125, from Qatada: that was when he prayed against them; report 18126, from al-Dahhak: then he prayed against his people when God made clear to him that none would believe but those who had. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 307}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
