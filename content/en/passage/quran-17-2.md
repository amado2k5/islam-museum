## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We gave Moses the Scripture and made it a guidance for the Children of Israel, that you will not take anyone other than Me as a guardian": Glory be to Him who took His servant by night and gave Moses the Scripture. The speech was returned to "And We came," beginning with the phrase "Asri" for what we have mentioned earlier about the actions of the Arabs in similar cases, starting with the report about the absent, then returning to the discourse and its likes. And the book given to Moses means: the Torah. "And We made it a guidance for the Children of Israel," meaning: And We made the Book, which is the Torah, an explanation of the truth and a proof for them of the correct argument in what was obligatory upon them, commanded them, and forbade them from it. And His saying: "Do not take anyone other than Me as a guardian" has differed in their interpretation; the majority of the reciters of Medina and Kufa read it as "do not take" with a 'ta' meaning: And We gave Moses the Scripture saying, 'Do not take, O Children of Israel, a guardian besides Me.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
