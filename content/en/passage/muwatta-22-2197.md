## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the agreed practice among us, on which there is no dispute: whoever gives a charity to his son, which the son took possession of, or which was in the custody of his father when he witnessed the charity for him, has no right to take any of it back, because one does not take back anything of a charity. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.301}
