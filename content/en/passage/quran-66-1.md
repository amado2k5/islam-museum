## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “O Prophet, why do you prohibit what Allah has made lawful for you, seeking to please your wives? And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful,” is explained as follows: O Prophet, who forbids for yourself what Allah has made lawful for you, seeking thereby to please your wives, why do you forbid for yourself what Allah has made lawful for you, seeking by your prohibition to please your wives? Scholars differed regarding the lawful thing that Allah, may His praise be exalted, had made lawful for His Messenger, which he then forbade for himself to please his wives. Some said it was his Coptic slave, Maria, whom he forbade himself by swearing that he would not approach her, seeking thereby to please his wife, Hafsa bint Umar, because she was jealous that the Messenger of Allah had been alone with her on her day and in her room. The passage also quotes statements from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Zayd ibn Aslam.
