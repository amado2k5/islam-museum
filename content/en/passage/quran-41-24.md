## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "If they are patient, then the Fire is their abode; but if they seek reproach, then they are not among the reproachers": If those who are gathered to the Fire on the Fire are patient, then the Fire is their dwelling and abode. "And if they seek reproach," he means: and if they ask for reproach, which is their return to those they love by lightening the punishment from them, "they are not among the reproached." He means: They will not be like the people who are taken to Paradise, and the punishment they are in will be lightened, as He, Glorified and Exalted, said, informing about them: "They said, 'Our Lord, our hardship has overcome us,' up to His saying, 'And do not speak,' and like their saying to the keeper of Hell: 'Pray to your Lord to lighten for us one day of punishment,' up to His saying: 'And the supplication of the disbelievers is only in error.'
