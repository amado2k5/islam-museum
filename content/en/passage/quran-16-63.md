## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "By Allah, indeed We have sent [Allah] to nations before you, so Satan made their deeds seem attractive to them. He is their guardian of the Day, and for them is a painful punishment": Allah Almighty says, swearing by Himself to His Prophet Muhammad: By Allah, O Muhammad, We have certainly sent messengers before you to their nations just as We sent you to your nation of supplication, to the oneness of Allah, to sincere worship of Him, to submit to Him in obedience, and to remove equals and gods. "So Satan made their deeds seem good to them," meaning: Satan made good for them what they had been of disbelief in Allah and idol worship, so they remained so that they denied their messengers and rejected what they brought to them from their Lord. "So he is their ally today," meaning: Satan is their helper today in this world, and how evil is the Helper. "And they will have a painful punishment" in the Hereafter when they return to their Lord. Then Satan's guardianship will not benefit them, nor will it benefit them in this world, but it harms them therein, and it will be even more harmful for them in the Hereafter.
