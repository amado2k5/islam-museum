## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We have certainly set forth for mankind in this Qur'an every parable, and if You bring them a sign, they will surely say, 'Those who disbelieved. Indeed, you are nothing but falsehoods.'": And We have set forth for mankind in this Qur'an every parable as a proof against them and as a reminder to them about the oneness of Allah. And His saying, 'And if you bring them a sign,' it means: 'And if you come, O Muhammad, these people with a sign,' he says: indicating the truth of what you say, 'Those who disbelieve will say, 'Indeed, you are but deniers.' He means: those who denied your message and denied your prophethood would say: Indeed, you, O believers, Muhammad in what he has brought to you, will only invalidate what you bring us regarding these matters.
