## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who love that immorality should be spread among those who believe will have a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter. And Allah knows while you do not know." It means: Those who love that adultery be spread among those who believe in Allah and His Messenger and it becomes evident among them, "will have a painful punishment," meaning: they will have a painful punishment in this world, to the extent that Allah set as a punishment for those who accuse chaste and chaste women if they accuse them of it, and in the Hereafter is the punishment of Hell if he dies persistently in it without repenting. As he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "They like that immorality becomes widespread," he said: it appears regarding Aisha. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "Indeed, those who like that immorality should spread among those who believe will have a painful punishment," he said: the wicked Abdullah ibn Abi Ibn Salul, the hypocrite, who spread the slander against Aisha about her, "for them is a painful punishment." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
