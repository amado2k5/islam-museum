## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the saying, "O my son, establish prayer and enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong, and be patient over what befalls you. Indeed, that is of the determination of affairs": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of Luqman to his son: "O my son, establish prayer" within its limits, "and enjoin good," meaning: He commanded people to obey Allah and follow His command, "and to avoid evil." He says: "And that people should avoid disobedience to Allah and intimacy with His prohibitions." "And be patient with what befalls you," meaning: And be patient with what befalls you among the people concerning Allah if you enjoin them to do good and forbid them from evil, and let not what befalls you from them prevent you from that. "Indeed, that is of the determination of matters," meaning: that is among the matters Allah has commanded with resolve. And as we have said about this, the scholars of interpretation have said.
