## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that he asked Salim ibn Abdullah whether the noon and afternoon prayers may be combined on a journey. He said yes, there is no harm, and cited the people's prayer at Arafat. In the chapter on combining two prayers at home and on a journey. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.208}
