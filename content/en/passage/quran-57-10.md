## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And why do you not spend in the way of Allah? And to Allah is the inheritance of the heavens and the earth. No one among you is equal to those who spent before the conquest and fought those who spent after and fought. And each has promised Allah the best. And Allah is Acquainted with what you do." And what is the matter with you, O people, that which Allah has provided you in the cause of Allah, and to Allah is the rest of your wealth if you do not spend it in the way of Allah, for to Him belongs the inheritance of the heavens and the earth. Rather, the Exalted, urges them. Thus, it was their share. He said to them: Spend your wealth in the way of Allah, so that it may be a treasure for you with Allah before you die. You cannot afford it, and the wealth becomes an inheritance for the One who owns the heavens and the earth. And His saying: "Those of you who spent before the conquest and fought are not equal." The interpreters differed in their interpretation of this, and some said: Its meaning is: among you, O people, is not equal to those who believed before the conquest of Mecca and emigrated. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Sufyan.
