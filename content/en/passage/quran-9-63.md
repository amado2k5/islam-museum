**Commentary on verse 63 (pp. 330-331)**

Al-Tabari says: do these hypocrites who swear falsely by God to the believers to please them, while persisting in hypocrisy, not know that whoever wages war against God and His Messenger and opposes them, setting himself against them in dissent, "for him is the Fire of Hell" in the hereafter, "abiding in it", that is, staying in it without end; "that is the great disgrace", that is, its melting in the Fire of Hell and his abiding in it is the great humiliation and abasement. The editor refers to earlier explanations of abiding and disgrace. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 330}

The reciters read "that for him" with a fatha on the particle, meaning do they not know that for whoever opposes God and His Messenger is the Fire of Hell, with "know" governing it, as if they made the second particle a repetition of the first and relied on it since the predicate is with it and not the first. Some of the Basran grammarians preferred the kasra, as a beginning, because of the entry of the particle "fa", which for them shows that it is the answer to a condition, and when it answers a condition the preferred form is the beginning. Al-Tabari says the reading he allows no other than is the fatha on both particles, because it is the reading of the great cities and for the reason he gave from Arabic usage. The editor notes that the printed text and the manuscript both have a garbled phrase, and gives the correct one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 331}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
