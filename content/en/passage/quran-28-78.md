## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He said, 'I have only been given him with knowledge from Me.' Did he not know that Allah destroyed before him those who were stronger and more numerous than him? And He will not question the criminals about their sins." Qarun said to his people who admonished him: I was given these treasures because of the virtue of knowledge with me, which God taught me from me, and He preferred me with this wealth over you, because He knew of My favor over you. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
