## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So Allah made them taste disgrace in the life of this world, and the punishment of the Hereafter is greater if they only knew": So Allah hastened for those nations who denied their messengers humiliation in this world and punishment before the Hereafter, and did not look at them when they disobeyed the command of their Lord. "And the punishment of the Hereafter is greater." He means: And Allah's punishment for them in the Hereafter when He casts them into the Fire and punishes them with it, greater than the punishment with which He punished them in this world, if only they knew. He means: If only these polytheists from Quraysh knew this.
