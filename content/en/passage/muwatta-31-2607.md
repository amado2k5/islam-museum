## Summary
Malik narrated to me from Ibn Shihab that the Messenger of God said: two religions shall not coexist in the Arabian Peninsula. Malik said: Ibn Shihab said: Umar ibn al-Khattab investigated this until knowledge and certainty came to him that the Messenger of God had said it, and he expelled the Jews of Khaybar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.471}
