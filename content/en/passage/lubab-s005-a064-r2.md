## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Ma'ida 5:64, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Companion generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «وقالت اليهود».
