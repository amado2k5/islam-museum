**Commentary on verse 105 (p. 13 onward)**

Al-Tabari says that the readers differed in reading "worthy that I say nothing about God but the truth". A group of the readers of Mecca, Medina, Basra and Kufa read the word "on" with the letter ya left unstressed (and without doubling), giving the sense "I am worthy of not saying about God anything but the truth", taking "on" in the sense of "with", as one says "I shot with the bow" or "on the bow", and "I came in a good state" with either preposition. The editor refers to earlier passages and to al-Farra's "Ma'ani al-Qur'an". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 13}

We stop here at the foot of p. 13, in the middle of the commentary on verse 105 (the readings of the word translated "worthy"); the next batch begins on p. 14.
