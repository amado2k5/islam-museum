## Summary
Verse 21 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 21 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "and he swore to them: I am for you one of the sincere advisers", he says: "qasamahuma" means he swore to them, as He said in [[passage/quran-16-49|16:49]], "they swore to one another by God", and as Khalid ibn Zuhayr, cousin of Abu Dhu'ayb, said in a line in which he says that he swore to her by God earnestly, meaning he swore by God to her, and as A'sha of Banu Tha'laba said in a line about two nursed at one breast who swore together by the black night never to part, meaning they swore to each other. The note gives the story of Abu Dhu'ayb and Khalid ibn Zuhayr and what passed between them over the woman he took from him, with the verses they exchanged, and explains "asham". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 349}

"I am for you one of the sincere advisers" means one who gives you sincere counsel in his advice to you and in his bidding you to eat the fruit of the tree you were forbidden to eat, and in his telling you what he told you, that if you ate it you would be angels or immortal; he gives (14396) from Qatada that he swore to them by God until he deceived them, and a believer may be deceived by God; he said, I was created before you and know more than you, so follow me and I will guide you; and some of the people of knowledge used to say, whoever deceives us by God, we are deceived. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 350}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
