## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, He would have almost led us astray from our gods, had we not been patient with them, and they will know when they see the punishment who is more astray in the way": Allah Almighty says, informing about those polytheists who used to mock the Messenger of Allah: They say, when they see him, 'This has almost led us astray from our gods whom we worship, and he will turn us away from worshiping them, if not for our patience with them and our steadfastness in worshiping them.' "And they will know when they see the punishment." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: It will be made clear to them when they witness the punishment of God that has befallen them for their worship. "Who is more astray in the way?" He means: Who rides on a path other than guidance, and the one who seeks the path of disguise? You are more deluded. And similarly to what we said in the interpretation of the phrase "If only We had not been patient with it," the interpreters said.
