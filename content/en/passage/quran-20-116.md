## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 116 to 117 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And when We said to the angels, 'Prostrate to Adam,' and they prostrated except Iblis, who refused": Allah Almighty says, referring to His Prophet Muhammad, that Adam did not neglect his covenant and knew that his children would not be on His path except those whom Allah protected from among them: And remember, O Muhammad, "When We said to the angels, 'Prostrate to Adam,' they prostrated except Iblis, who refused to prostrate to him." So We said, 'O Adam, indeed this is an enemy to you and to your wife.' Therefore, out of hatred he did not prostrate to you, disobeyed My command in that, and disobeyed me, so no obey him in what he commands, as he commands, and He will expel you both by your disobedience to your Lord and your obedience to Him.
