## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 97 to 100 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "They said, 'Build him a structure and throw him into Hell': When Abraham said to them, 'Do you worship what you carve? Allah created you and what you do?' They built a structure for Abraham, mentioning that they built him a structure resembling a furnace, then transported wood to him and kindled it over him, and they cast him into Hell." Among the Arabs, 'Hell' means embers of fire, some on top of others, and fire upon fire. And His saying: "So they intended a plot against him," the Exalted means: So the people of Abraham intended a plot against Abraham, and that is what they intended by burning him with fire. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibrahim.
