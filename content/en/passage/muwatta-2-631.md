## Summary
The first report in the chapter on seeking reward in misfortune. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said that no Muslim loses three children and bears them with patience without the fire touching him, except the fulfilment of an oath. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Ibn Hibban, al-Bughawi, Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari al-Adab al-Mufrad, Suwayd, al-Qanabi, Ibn Wahb, Ibn al-Qasim, and Maan in al-Tirmidhi, Yahya ibn Said al-Qattan in Ahmad, and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Nisaburi in Muslim transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.322}
