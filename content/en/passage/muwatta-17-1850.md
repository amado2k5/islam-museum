## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said the same: do not sell gold for gold except equal for equal, with no excess between them, and do not sell any of them absent for present. If he asks you to wait until he enters his house, do not wait for him, for I fear riba for you; riba is rama. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.160}
