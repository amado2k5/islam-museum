## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And why do I not worship Him who created me and to Him you will be returned": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of this believing man, "And why do I not worship Him who created me": meaning, what is wrong with me that I do not worship the Lord who created me? "And to Him you will return," meaning: to Him you will return, O people, and you all will return. This was when he revealed to his people his faith in God and His oneness, as reported by Ibn Ishaq in what he conveyed, from Ibn Abbas, from Ka'b al-Ahbar, and from Wahb ibn Munabbih who said: He called them, meaning he called his people contrary to their idol worship, and he revealed to them his religion and worship of his Lord, telling them that he has no benefit or harm except Him. He said: "And why do I not worship the One who created me and to Him you will return? Should I take gods besides Him?" Then he reproached her, saying: "If the Most Merciful intend harm," and severely, "Their intercession does not avail me at all, nor does it benefit me. In the passage, sayings from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq are quoted.
