## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar: the Prophet's dream in which he drank milk until it flowed from his nails, then gave the rest to Umar, and he interpreted it as knowledge. It is one of the texts on Umar's virtue in knowledge. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
