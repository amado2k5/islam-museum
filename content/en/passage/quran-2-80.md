## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "they say: the Fire will touch us only for a few numbered days", al-Tabari says the speakers are the Jews, and that the number is not given in the revelation because they knew it. The commentators differ on how many days: Ibn Abbas through al-Dahhak, Qatada, al-Dahhak, al-Suddi and Abu al-'Aliya say forty, matching the days they worshipped the calf; al-Suddi adds they said it would be forty nights and then every circumcised person would be brought out; another report from Ibn Abbas has them finding in the Torah that Hell's two sides are forty years' journey apart, as far as the Zaqqum tree; 'Ikrima and Ibn Zayd report that they disputed with the Prophet saying others would take their place, to which he replied that they would remain in it, the editor noting both are mursal reports on which no argument stands. Others said the world lasts seven thousand years and they would be punished one day for every thousand, which Ibn Abbas and Mujahid report. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 273} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 274} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 275} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 276} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 277} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 278}
On "Say: have you taken a covenant with God?", the sense is: have you received a pledge from God, for God does not break his pledge, or do you say of God what is false out of ignorance and boldness, as reported from Mujahid, Qatada and Ibn Abbas through al-Dahhak, the latter taking the covenant to be saying "there is no god but God" without association. Al-Tabari holds that God's pledge is that whoever believes and obeys he saves from his Fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 278} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 279} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 280}
