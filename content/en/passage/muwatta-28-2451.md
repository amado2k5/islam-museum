## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Ibn Shihab, from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman, from Aisha, that she said: the Messenger was asked about al-bitac (a strong drink), and he said: every drink that intoxicates is forbidden. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.412}
