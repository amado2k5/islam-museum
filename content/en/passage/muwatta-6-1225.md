## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi and Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that Umar ibn al-Khattab preached to the people at Arafat and taught them the rites of the pilgrimage; among what he said was: when you come to Mina, whoever has stoned the pillar, it is lawful for him what was forbidden to the pilgrim, except women and perfume, until he circumambulates the House. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.547}
