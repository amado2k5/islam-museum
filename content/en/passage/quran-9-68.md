**Commentary on verse 68 (pp. 339-340)**

Al-Tabari says: God promised the hypocrites, men and women, and the unbelievers in God the Fire of Hell, that He will make them all burn in it, "abiding in it", staying in it forever, neither living in it nor dying; "it is enough for them" means it suffices them as punishment and recompense for their unbelief in God; "and God cursed them" means He drove them far and away from His mercy; "and theirs is a lasting punishment" means that for both parties, the people of hypocrisy and unbelief, there is with God a lasting punishment, enduring, which neither ends nor perishes. The editor refers to earlier explanations of abiding, sufficiency and lastingness. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 340}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
