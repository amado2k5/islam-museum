## Summary
It reached Malik that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ruled about a conditionally freed slave who wounds someone: his master may hand over what he owns of him to the wounded person, who makes him serve and offsets his wound against his blood-money; if the master pays before he dies, the slave returns to him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.375}
