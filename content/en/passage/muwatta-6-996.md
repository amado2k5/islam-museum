## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the marriage of one in ihram. Malik transmits from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that the Messenger of God married his client Abu Rafi and a man of the Ansar to Maymuna bint al-Harith, while the Messenger of God was in Medina before he set out. The editor notes that the hadith is traced connected through Hammad ibn Zayd from Mutar al-Warraq in Ahmad and elsewhere; that al-Tirmidhi said Malik transmitted it relayed; and the editor prefers the relayed version, after Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that Mutar erred, since Abu Rafi died shortly after the killing of Uthman, Sulayman did not hear from him, and Maymuna was his client. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.467}
