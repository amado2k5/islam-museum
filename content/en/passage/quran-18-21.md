## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And thus We sought them out so that they might know that the promise of Allah is true and that the Hour is unquestionable, when they dispute among themselves over their affair. They said, 'Build upon them a structure.' Their Lord is most knowing of them." He said, "Those who prevail over their affair, We will surely take for them a mosque." And just as We sent them after their long sleep, as they appeared for an hour, they slept to question among themselves, so that they would gain greater insight by the greatness of Allah's authority and the sound defense of His allies. "Thus We found them," he says: We also informed them of the other group who doubted God's ability to raise the dead, and doubted the creation of the bodies of His creation, like their appearance on the day they died after affliction, so they knew that God's promise was true and were certain that the Hour was coming without doubt. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
