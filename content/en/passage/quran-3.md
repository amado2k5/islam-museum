## Summary
Surat Al 'Imran: surah 3 in the order of the Mushaf, Medinan, 200 verses, and revelation order 89 among the surahs. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Content
Its verses are recorded in the museum one by one under the records passage/quran-3-‹verse number›.

## In Tantawi's al-Tafsir al-Wasit
In [[work/al-tafsir-al-wasit|al-Tafsir al-Wasit]], [[person/muhammad-sayyid-tantawi|Tantawi]] says that Al Imran is the third sura in the order of the mushaf, that it has two hundred verses, and that it is Medinan by agreement of the scholars. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.680} It is named for the family of Imran because their story is told in it in a detail found nowhere else; by this family he understands [[person/isa-ibn-maryam|Jesus]], John, [[person/maryam|Mary]] and her mother, Imran being Mary's father. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.680} He lists other names for it: al-Zahra' (the radiant), al-Aman (safety), al-Kanz (the treasure), al-Mujadala (the disputation), because more than eighty of its verses came down on the Prophet's debate with the Christian delegation of Najran, and Tayba (the good). {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.680}

He quotes [[person/al-qurtubi|al-Qurtubi]] that its opening came down on the occasion of [[event/delegation-of-najran|the Najran delegation]], who came to Medina, prayed in the mosque facing east and stayed some days debating with the Prophet about Jesus; the opening of the sura, some eighty-odd verses, came down about them, until he invited them to the mutual curse (mubahala). {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.680} {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.681} Of its second half, about sixty verses came down after [[event/battle-of-uhud|the battle of Uhud]]. {cite:tafsir-tantawi-wasit p.681}
