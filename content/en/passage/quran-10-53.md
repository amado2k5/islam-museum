**Commentary on verse 53 (p. 102)**

On [[passage/quran-10-53|10:53]] he says: these polytheists of your people, Muhammad, ask you to tell them whether what you say is true, and what you promise of God's punishment in the next abode as repayment for the disobedience we earned in the world. Say to them: yes, by my Lord, it is true, with no doubt, and you cannot make God helpless when He wills it for you by flight or refusal; you are in His grip, authority and dominion, so fear God for your own sakes. The editor refers to the glosses on "news" and "making helpless". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 102}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
