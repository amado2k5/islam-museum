## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say every divorced woman is entitled to a consolation payment, except one who was divorced after a dower was set for her but before she was touched; for her, half of what was set is sufficient. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.85}
