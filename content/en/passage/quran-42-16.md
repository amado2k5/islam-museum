## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who dispute concerning Allah after His proof has been answered, their argument will be refuted before their Lord, and upon them is wrath, and for them is a severe punishment": And those who dispute concerning the religion of Allah, which His Prophet Muhammad was sent with after the people responded to him, and entered it among those who inherited the Scripture, "their argument is refuted," meaning: their disputes in it are invalid and will go before their Lord, "and upon them is wrath." He means: And upon them is the wrath of Allah, and for them in the Hereafter is a severe punishment, which is the punishment of the Fire. He mentioned that this verse was revealed about a group of Jews who quarreled with the companions of the Messenger of Allah over their religion, hoping to turn them away from it and turn them away from Islam to disbelief. In the excerpt, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
