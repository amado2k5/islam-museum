## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 55 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Lot, when he said to his people, 'Do you commit immorality while you see?": Allah says, the Exalted, and We sent Lot to his people when He said to them, 'O my people, do you commit immorality while you see,' that it is an immorality, because you know that no one has preceded you to what you do. And His saying, 'Do you desire men from among yourselves,' does not mean the private parts of women whom Allah has permitted for you to marry, and His saying, 'Rather, you are a people who do not know,' He says: That is nothing but that of you, foolish and ignorant of the great right of Allah over you, so you disobeyed His command and disobeyed His Messenger.
