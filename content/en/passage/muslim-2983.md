## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the reward of one who supports an orphan, in the Book of Asceticism of Sahih Muslim. The one who supports an orphan is placed next to the Prophet in Paradise, shown by two fingers. {cite:sahih-muslim}
