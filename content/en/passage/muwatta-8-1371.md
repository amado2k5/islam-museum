## Summary
Malik says that the best he heard on the exception (thunya) is that it belongs to its owner as long as he does not cut off his speech; what follows in sequence, one part after another, before he is silent. When he is silent and cuts off his speech, there is no exception for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.613}
