## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The example of Paradise which the righteous have promised: rivers of water without any hardship, rivers of milk whose taste does not change, rivers of wine a delight for those who drink, and rivers of refined honey, and they will have therein all fruits and forgiveness from their Lord as if he is eternal in the Fire. And they will drink hot water and he will cut off their intestines": the description of Paradise promised to the righteous, who are those who fear Him in this world, will receive His punishment by fulfilling His obligations and avoiding His sins. From water that is not stagnant," Allah Almighty says about this Paradise which: He mentioned rivers of water whose wind does not change. It is said: The water of this well has become stable: when the wind of its water changes and becomes foul, it is called 'Yasan Asna.' Likewise, it is said to a man when a foul wind is struck: he has become stubborn and is called 'Yassan.' As for when the water becomes mad and changes, it is called 'assan', so it is yasan and ya'asan asun, and water is asna. In the maqta', statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah are quoted.
