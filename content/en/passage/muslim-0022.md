## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Umar in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim: the pillars of protection are the two testimonies, prayer and zakat, and the blood and property of a person are protected except by right. He read it against God's words "You are only a reminder; you are not over them a controller". {cite:sahih-muslim}
