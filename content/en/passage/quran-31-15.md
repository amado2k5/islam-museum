## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if they strive with you to associate with Me that of which you have no knowledge, then do not obey them, while their companion in this world is in an honorable manner, and follow the way of the one who turned to Me for succession, then to Me is your return, and I will inform you of what you used to do." And if your parents strive for you, O human, to associate You with Me in worship with others who do not know that I have a partner and no partner to Him, exalted is He, exalted be He, do not obey them in what they want you to associate with Me in this world, "and their companion in this world is known," meaning: And their companion in this world is obedient to them in matters where there is no responsibility for you between you and your Lord, nor sin. And His saying: "And follow the path of the one who turns to me," means: and follow the path of one who repents from his polytheism, returns to Islam, and follows Muhammad. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
