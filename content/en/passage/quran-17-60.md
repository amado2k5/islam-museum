## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when We said to you, 'Indeed, your Lord encompasses the people, and We did not make the vision which We showed you except as a trial for mankind and the accursed tree in the Qur'an, and We warn them, but it only increases them in great transgression.'" This is an encouragement from Allah, the Exalted, His Prophet Muhammad, to convey His message and inform him that He has previously stated that He will prevent him from all who transgress against Him in evil and destruction. He, Glorified and Exalted, says: And remember, O Muhammad, when We told you that your Lord encompasses the people with power, but they are in His grip and cannot escape His will, and we are preventing you from them, so do not fear any of them, and proceed with what We have commanded you to deliver Our message. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, and Ibn Jurayj.
