([[passage/quran-2-238|2:238]]) begins on p. 167. Al-Tabari says the command to guard means keeping to the obligatory prayers at their times, tending and holding to them, and the middle one among them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 167} Masruq: guarding them is guarding their time and not neglecting them, and neglect is leaving their time (5378, 5379). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 168} They then differed over the middle one; some said it is the afternoon prayer, from 'Ali (5380). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 168} The editor says the chain of this report is stopped at 'Ali and very weak because of al-Harith al-A'war, and that the report comes from 'Ali by chains some of which are sound; this is the editor's grading, not al-Tabari's. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 168}
