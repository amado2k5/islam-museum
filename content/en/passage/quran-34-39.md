## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 41 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, 'My Lord extends provision for whom He wills of His servants and restricts it for him. And whatever you spend of anything—He will replace it, and He is the best of providers.'" And on the Day We gather these disbelievers all together by Allah, then We will say to the angels: Were these the ones worshipping you besides Us? The angels disassociate themselves from them, saying, "Glory be to You, our Lord," purifying You and disowning them from what these partners and rivals have attributed to you: "You are a protector besides them," and we do not take any protector besides You, "but they used to worship the jinn." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
