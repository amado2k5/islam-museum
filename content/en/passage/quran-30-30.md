## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So set your face toward the religion, inclining to truth. Allah has created [the fitrah] upon which He created mankind. There is no change in the creation of Allah. That is the upright religion, but most people do not know": so direct your face towards the path your Lord has directed you to, O Muhammad, to obey Him, which is religion. "Upright" means upright to His religion and obedience, "the innate nature of Allah upon which He created mankind." He means: the craft of Allah upon which He created mankind and was set in the verbal noun, from the meaning of the phrase "So set your face towards the religion, inclining to truth," which is that the meaning of this is: God created people naturally for that. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
