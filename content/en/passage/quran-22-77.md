## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "O you who have believed, bow down, prostrate and worship your Lord and do good that you may succeed": O you who have believed Allah and His Messenger, "bow" to Allah in your prayer, "and prostrate" to Him in it, "and worship your Lord," meaning: humble yourselves to your Lord and submit to Him in obedience. "And do good" which your Lord has commanded you to do, "that you may succeed," meaning: so that you may succeed, and by Him you may attain your requests with your Lord.
