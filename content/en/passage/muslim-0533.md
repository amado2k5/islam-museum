## Summary
A report of Uthman ibn Affan when he built the Prophet's mosque, while people disliked his changes, in the chapter on building mosques in the Book of Mosques of Sahih Muslim. It is the text that endowment projects have hung at the thresholds of mosques for centuries. {cite:sahih-muslim}
