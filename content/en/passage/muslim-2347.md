## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on the description of the Prophet, his mission and his age, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim. It gives his full physical description, his call at forty, ten years in Mecca and ten in Medina, and his death at sixty. It is the comprehensive text on his description and biography. {cite:sahih-muslim}
