## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said and from Yazid ibn Abdallah ibn Qusayt al-Laythi, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he said Umar ibn al-Khattab said: any woman who is divorced, and has had one or two menstrual periods, then her menstruation stopped, waits nine months. If a pregnancy appears, that is the answer; otherwise she observes three months after the nine months, and then she is free. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.96}
