## Summary
A saying of Malik: if a man had camels, cattle or sheep, on each kind of which charity is due, and then acquired a camel, a cow or a sheep, he pays charity on it with his livestock when he pays. Malik said: this is the most beloved to me of what I have heard on this. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.354}
