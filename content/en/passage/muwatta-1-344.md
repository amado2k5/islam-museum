## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubayd Allah, from Busr ibn Sa'id, that Zayd ibn Thabit said, in the chapter on the merit of congregational prayer over praying alone: the best prayer is your prayer in your houses, except the prescribed prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it as a stopped report, and that it was transmitted raised from Busr from Zayd from the Prophet. Al-Tirmidhi (450) reports that Musa ibn Uqba and Ibrahim ibn Abi al-Nadr transmitted it raised from Abu al-Nadr, and that the raised version is sounder. He cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Khuzayma and Ibn Hibban for the raised form. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.189} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.190}
