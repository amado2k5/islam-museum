## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-7 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Has there come to you the account of the overwhelming event?” as follows: “Has there come to you,” O Muhammad, “the account of the overwhelming event?” meaning its story and details. Scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning of “the overwhelming event.” Some said it refers to the Day of Resurrection, which will overwhelm people with terrors. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
