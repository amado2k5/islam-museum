## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And indeed Iblis confirmed his suspicion against them, so they followed him except for a group of the believers": The readers differed in reading the phrase: "Indeed, Iblis confirmed his suspicion to them," so the majority of the Kufan readers read this as: "Indeed, he has spoken it" with a stressed letter of 'sadiq,' meaning he said it in his own way: "And you will not find most of them grateful," and he said: "By Your might, I will surely mislead them all except Your sincere servants among them." Then his assumption was confirmed about them, so he realized it through them and by following it. This was read by the majority of the reciters of Medina, Sham, and Basra: 'And indeed he has truthed' with a lightening of the 'd', meaning: and his suspicion has been confirmed upon them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
