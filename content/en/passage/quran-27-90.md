## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 89 to 90 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that whoever brings the oneness of God, faith in Him, and the phrase 'There is no god but Allah' with certainty in his heart will have better than it on the Day of Resurrection, which is that God rewards him with Paradise and secures him from the great cry, which is the blowing of the trumpet. Whoever brings polytheism and denial of oneness will have their faces buried in the fire of Hell.
