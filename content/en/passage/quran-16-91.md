## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And fulfill the covenant of Allah when you have made a covenant and do not break oaths after confirming it, and you have made Allah a guarantor over you. Indeed, Allah knows what you do": And fulfill the covenant of Allah when you trust Him, and the covenant of Allah when you make a covenant with Him, and you have made it obligatory upon yourselves as a right for the one you have contracted with and entrusted to Him. "And do not break oaths after they have been confirmed," meaning: "And do not violate the matter in which you contracted oaths," meaning after you have tightened the oaths upon yourselves, then break your oaths, lie about them, and nullify them after they have been concluded. It is said: "So-and-so has made his oath firmly emphatic," meaning if he tightens it, which is the language of the people of Hijaz. As for the people of Najd, they say: "I have confirmed it, I confirm it with emphasis." And His saying: "And you have made God a guarantor over you," it means: "And you have made God, by fulfilling what you have contracted upon yourselves, a guardian who protects those among you who fulfill God's covenant that He has made a covenant to fulfill," and the one who breaks it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
