## Summary
It reached Malik that Luqman the Wise advised his son: my son, sit with the scholars and press against them with your knees, for God gives life to hearts with the light of wisdom, as He gives life to dead earth with the heavy rain of the sky. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.602}
