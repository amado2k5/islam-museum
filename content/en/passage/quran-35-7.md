## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Those who disbelieve, for them is a severe punishment, and those who believe and do righteous deeds—for them is forgiveness and a great reward": "Those who disbelieve" in Allah and His Messenger will have a severe punishment from Allah, and that is the punishment of the Fire. And His saying: "And those who believe" means: those who are truthful to Allah and His Messenger, and do what Allah has commanded them, and refrained from what Allah forbade them, "for them is forgiveness" from Allah for their sins, "and a great reward," and that is Paradise, as from Qatadah: "For them is forgiveness and a great reward," which is Paradise. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
