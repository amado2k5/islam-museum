## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind": Say, O Muhammad, seek refuge in "In the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind," and He is the Sovereign of all creation: humans, jinn, and others, informing him of those who honored people by honoring the believers their Lord, that He is the King of those who honor Him, that this is in His sovereignty and authority, His power applies to Him, and that He is more worthy of reverence and more deserving of worship than those who honor and worship Him than others.
