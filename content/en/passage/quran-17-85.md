## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they ask you about the Spirit. Say: The spirit is by the command of my Lord, and you have not been given of knowledge except a little." And the disbelievers of Allah among the People of the Book ask you about the Spirit. What is it? Say to them: The Spirit is by the command of my Lord, and you and all people have not been given knowledge except a little. He mentioned that those who asked the Messenger of Allah about the Spirit, and this verse was revealed by asking him about it, were among the Jews. The narration mentioned this: And from Ibrahim, from Alqamah, from Abdullah, who said: I was with the Prophet in a plow in Medina, and he had a horse to lean on, and he passed by a group of Jews. Some said: Ask him about the spirit, and some said: Do not ask him. So he stood leaning on his horse, so I stood behind him, thinking he was being inspired to him. He said: "And they ask you about the spirit. Say: The spirit is by the command of my Lord, and you have been given of knowledge except a little." Some of them said to each other: Did I not tell you not to ask him? In the passage, he quotes from Ikrimah, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
