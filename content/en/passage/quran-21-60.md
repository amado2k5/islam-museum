## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 59 to 61 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "They said, 'We heard a young man remind them of them called Ibrahim.'" Those who heard him said, "By Allah, surely your idols will be remembered after you have turned away." We heard a young man remind them of a defect called Ibrahim. As he said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj: "They said, 'We heard a young man remind them.' Ibn Jurayj said: He reminds them of their faults." From Ibn Ishaq, regarding His saying: "We heard a young man mention them called Ibrahim." We heard him insult, criticize, and mock her; we have not heard anyone say this except him, and we think he is the one who did this to her. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
