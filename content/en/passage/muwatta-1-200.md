## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Salim ibn Abdullah, that Abdullah ibn Umar used to glorify God in prayer at each lowering and raising, in the chapter on opening the prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.126}
