**Commentary on verse 118 (p. 31)**

Al-Tabari says: the truth became manifest and clear to whoever witnessed and was present at the affair of Moses, that he was a messenger of God calling to the truth; "what they were doing came to nothing" means the falsehood of sorcery, its lies and its illusions. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 31}

Reports 14950 to 14953 are from Mujahid: "the truth took place" means it appeared; in 14951 (through al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz and Isma'il ibn Ibrahim ibn Muhajir from his father), the truth appeared and the falsehood they had been working went; in 14953 (through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl and Ibn Abi Najih), Moses appeared. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 31}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
