## Summary
Verse 132 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 132 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 132, "for all there are ranks according to what they did, and your Lord is not unaware of what they do", al-Tabari says: for every doer, in obedience to God or disobedience, there are stations and ranks from his deed that God brings him to and rewards him with, good for good and evil for evil; "your Lord is not unaware of what they do" means all of that from their deeds is within the knowledge of your Lord, Muhammad, who counts and records it for them with Him so as to repay them for it at their meeting with Him and their return to Him. The note refers to the earlier explanation of "daraja". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 125}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
