## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 66 to 67 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "He said, 'Do you worship besides Allah that which benefits you nothing and does not harm you?": Abraham said to his people: Do you worship, O people, that which benefits you and does not harm you? And you know that she did not restrain herself from those who intended harm to her, nor could she speak if asked about someone who harms her, and she would inform about it. Are you not ashamed of worshipping what was like this? Also: from Ibn Ishaq: "He said, 'Do you worship besides Allah that which benefits you nothing and does not harm you...' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq and Ibrahim.
