## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth glorifies God, the Sovereign, the Holy, the Exalted in Might, the Wise,” explains: Everything in the seven heavens and everything on the earth glorifies God, and magnifies Him willingly or unwillingly. “The Sovereign, the Holy” is He who has dominion over this world and the Hereafter and their authority, whose command is effective in the heavens and the earth and what is in them. “The Holy” is He who is pure from everything that the polytheists attribute to Him and describe Him with that which is not from His blessed attributes. “The Exalted in Might” means He is severe in His vengeance against His enemies. “The Wise” is He who manages His creation and directs them in what He knows best of their interests.
