## Summary
A saying of Malik: scholars hold that she owes making up, as God, mighty and majestic, said: whoever of you is sick or on a journey, a number of other days (Al-Baqara [[passage/quran-2-184|Al-Baqara 184]]); and they consider that a kind of illness, with fear for her child. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik (467). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.413}
