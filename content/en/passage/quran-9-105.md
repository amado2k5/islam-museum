**Commentary on verse 105 (pp. 462-463)**

Al-Tabari says to His Prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, to these who confessed their sins to you of those who stayed behind from striving with you, "work" for God with what pleases Him of obedience to Him and performing His obligations; "God will see your work, and His Messenger", meaning God will see your work if you work, and His Messenger and the believers will see it in this world; "and you will be returned" on the Day of Resurrection to Him who knows your secret thoughts and your open ones, from whom nothing of the inward or outward of your affairs is hidden; "and He will tell you what you used to do" means He will inform you of what you used to do, what of it was sincere and what for show, what of it obedience and what disobedience to God, and will repay you for all of that your recompense, the doer of good for his good and the doer of evil for his evil. The editor refers to earlier explanations of the Knower of the unseen and the seen and of tidings. Report 17173, from Mujahid, through Ibn Waki', Ibn Yaman, Sufyan and a man: "say, work, and God will see your work", he said, this is a warning. The editor notes that at this point the eleventh part of his manuscript ends, with the copyist's closing note giving the month of Sha'ban in the year 715 as the time of copying, and that the twelfth part follows, beginning with the commentary on the verse about others whose affair is deferred. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 463}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
