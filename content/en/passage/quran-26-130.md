## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 128 to 130 together in one place. Al-Tabari explains renting as any place raised above the ground, road, or valley, which are two dialects with a kasra on the ra, citing Dhu al-Rumma and al-A'sha, and narrates from Ibn Abbas that it is all honor.
