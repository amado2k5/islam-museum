**Commentary on verse 21 (p. 288)**

On [[passage/quran-11-21|11:21]] al-Tabari says: these of whom this is the description are those who cheated themselves of their share of God's mercy, 'and what they used to invent has failed them', that is, their lie, falsehood and invention about God, by claiming partners for Him, has come to nothing; what they called a god besides God took a different course from theirs and a different way, and so was lost to them, since it led them to Hell, and their gods became nothing, since they were stones, wood or copper in the world, or were a friend of God and went to Paradise, which also differs from their course and is also loss to them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 288}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
