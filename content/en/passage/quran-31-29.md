## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah merges the night into the day and merges the day into the night and subjects the sun and the moon, each running for a specified term? And Allah is Acquainted with what you do": "Have you not seen", O Muhammad, with your own eyes: "That Allah merges the night into the day" means: He increases the reduction of the night's hours during the day, and "and causes the day to enter the night": He adds: He increases what is reduced in the hours of the day. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah merges the night into the day"—the reduction of the night in the increase of the day, and "and the day merges into the night"—the decrease of the day in the increase of the night. And His saying: "And He subjected the sun and the moon, each running for a specified term," the Exalted says: "And He subjected the sun and the moon to the interests and benefits of His creation," "each one runs"—meaning: all of that runs by His command until a known time, and a limited time when it reaches it, the sun and moon are cut off.
