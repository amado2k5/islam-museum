## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, that Aisha, wife of the Prophet, let in those whom her sisters and nieces had nursed, but did not let in those whom the wives of her brothers had nursed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
