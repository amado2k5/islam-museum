## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Abd al-Rahman ibn Hormuz al-A'raj from Abdullah ibn Buhayna, in the chapter on one who stands up after completing a prayer or after two rak'as: the Prophet led them in the noon prayer and stood up after two rak'as without sitting, then when he finished he made two prostrations and then gave salam. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Tahawi, al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.152}
