## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that Aisha used the same formula as the previous report in the tashahhud, with the addition "and I testify" before the testimony that Muhammad is the Messenger. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.145}
