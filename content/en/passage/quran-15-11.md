## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues his statement in the section: "And no messenger comes to them except that they mock him," meaning that the early followers—their nations—would come to them from a messenger whom God sent to them by calling for His oneness and submitting to his obedience, except that they mocked the messenger He sent to them out of rebellion and rebellion against their Lord. In the verse before it, "the Shi'" was interpreted as referring to the nations according to Ibn Abbas and Qatadah, and that the mention of the messengers was omitted as sufficiency to indicate "We sent" upon him.
