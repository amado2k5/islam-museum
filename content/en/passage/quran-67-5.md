## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, “And We have certainly beautified the nearest heaven with lamps and have made them missiles for the devils and have prepared for them the punishment of the Blaze”: “And We have certainly beautified the nearest heaven with lamps,” which are the stars, and He made them lamps to illuminate it, and likewise the morning is only called morning because of the light that illuminates for people during the day. “And have made them missiles for the devils,” He says: And We made the lamps with which We beautified the nearest heaven missiles for the devils to be stoned with. And it is narrated from Qatadah regarding the verse, “And We have certainly beautified the nearest heaven with lamps and made them missiles for the devils” (57:30): God, may His praise be exalted, created these stars for three purposes: to adorn the nearest heaven, to repel the devils, and to serve as signs by which to be guided. Whoever interprets them otherwise has spoken based on his own opinion, missed his opportunity, lost his share, and presumed to know what he does not. The passage then quotes statements attributed to Qatadah.
