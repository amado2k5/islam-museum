## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among the people is he who worships Allah with a letter; if good befalls him, he is assured of it; but if a trial befalls him, he turns his face away from him losing this world and the Hereafter. That is the clear loss." He means by saying: "And among the people is he who worships Allah on a letter," meaning Bedouins who used to come before the Messenger of Allah, emigrants from their desert. If they attained prosperity in life after migration and entering Islam, they would remain in Islam; otherwise, they would apostatize on their heels. Allah said: "And among the people is he who worships Allah" with doubt, "If good befalls him, he is assured by it," which means abundance of livelihood and similar worldly means. "He is reassured by it," meaning: he is firmly established in Islam and steadfast upon it. "And if a trial befalls him," which refers to the hardship of living and similar worldly causes, "he turned away," meaning: he turned back and turned back on his face, which he was on from disbelief in God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
