## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Praise be to Allah, to whom belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and to Him belongs all praise in the Hereafter, and He is the Wise, the All-Aware." This means complete thanks and complete praise to the One who is the Owner of all that is in the seven heavens and whatever is on the seven earths, above all that they worship, and above everything else. There is no owner of any of that except Him. The meaning: He who owns it all, and to Him belongs all praise in the Hereafter, meaning: And He has full thanks in the Hereafter, just as He has that in this immediate world, because from Him are all blessings upon everyone in the heavens and the earth in this world, and from Him it will be in the Hereafter. So, praise be to Allah sincerely, excluding everything else in the immediate world and the Afterlife, because all blessings come before Him, and no one else shares them except Him. He is the Wise in managing His creation and directing them in His estimation, aware of them, what benefits them, what they do, and what they do, encompassing all of that. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
