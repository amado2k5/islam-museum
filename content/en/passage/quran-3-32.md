## Verse 32 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, p. 325)

Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-32|3:32]]) says the sense is: say, Muhammad, to this delegation of Najran Christians: obey your Lord and His messenger Muhammad, for you know with certainty that he is His messenger to His creation, sent with the truth, whom you find written in your Gospel; and if they turn away from what you called them to, tell them God does not love whoever disbelieves and denies what he knew of the truth, and that they are among those, by their denial of your prophethood. Ibn Humayd, from Salama, from Ibn Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr (6850) says the same: you know him and find him in your book, and if they turn away in their unbelief, God does not love the unbelievers. The editor says this is from Ibn Hisham's Sira and the remainder of the reports ending at 6849, and that the printed text altered a phrase where he kept the manuscript's. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 325}
