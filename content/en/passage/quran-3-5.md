## Summary
Verse 5 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 5 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

([[passage/quran-3-5|3:5]]), p. 166. Al-Tabari says the meaning is that nothing, earthly or heavenly, escapes God's knowledge, and he addresses the Prophet: how could it be hidden from Me, when I know everything, what these Najran Christians who dispute with you about God's signs are imitating in what they say about Jesus son of Mary? In 6566, Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr (through Ibn Humayd, Salama and Muhammad ibn Ishaq): He knows what they want, what they plot and what they imitate in their saying about Jesus when they made him lord and god, while they have from His knowledge something other than that, out of jealous zeal for God and disbelief in Him; the editor says it is the remainder of the reports ending at 6564, from the biography of Ibn Ishaq. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 166} These are reports as transmitted, and the wording 'imitated' belongs to al-Tabari and his narrators as a description of the Christians' saying as they see it, with no museum verdict.
