## Summary
Malik transmits from Ziyad ibn Saad, from Ibn Shihab, that he heard him say: the Messenger of God let his forelock hang as God willed, then parted it afterwards. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the transmitters narrated it from Malik as relayed (mursal), and that Hammad ibn Khalid connected it in error. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.536}
