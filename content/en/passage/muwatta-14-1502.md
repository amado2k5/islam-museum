## Summary
A saying of Malik about a father who marries off his daughter and stipulates as her dowry a gift to be given: whatever condition by which the marriage is concluded belongs to his daughter if she seeks it. If her husband separates from her before consummating, he has half of the gift by which the marriage was concluded. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.32}
