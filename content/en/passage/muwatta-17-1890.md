## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Maryam, that he asked Said ibn al-Musayyab: I am a man who buys food from the certificates in the granary; I often buy from him for a dinar and a half of a dirham, should I give half of it in food? Said said no. Rather give a dirham, and take the rest in food. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.176}
