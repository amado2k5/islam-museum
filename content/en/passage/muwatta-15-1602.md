## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Said ibn al-Musayyab and Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman used to say of a man who makes ila against his wife that when the four months pass it counts as one divorce, and he has the right of return while she is in her waiting period. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.65}
