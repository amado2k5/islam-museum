## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "then We pardoned you after that" al-Tabari says it means We refrained from hastening punishment upon you after you took the calf as a god, reporting this from Abu al-Aliya. In "that you might give thanks" he takes "la'alla" in the sense of "so that", meaning that you might thank Me for pardoning you, since pardon calls for thanks from people of understanding. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 69}
