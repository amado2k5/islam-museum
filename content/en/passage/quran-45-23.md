## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Have you seen one who takes his desire as his god and whom Allah leads astray with knowledge, seals his hearing and heart, and places a veil over his sight? So who guides him after Allah? Will you not then remember?" The interpreters differ in the interpretation of the phrase: "Have you seen the one who takes his desire as his god?" Some said: The meaning is: "Have you seen the one who takes his religion according to his own desires, and he desires nothing except his riding, because he does not believe in Allah, nor forbids what He has forbidden, nor permits what He permits; rather, his religion is what his soul desires, and he acts upon it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
