## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-8 as a single section. He explains the phrase "He created man from a clot" as meaning from blood. He says, "from a clot," meaning from a blood clot, because he uses the plural form, just as one says "tree" and "trees," or "reed" and "reeds," and similarly "clot" and "clot." He uses "from a clot" and "man" in the same word because it conveys a plural meaning, even though it is in the same word. Therefore, it is said "from a clot." He then quotes statements from Qatadah in the section.
