## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "that He will admit them to an entrance they will be pleased with, and indeed, Allah is Knowing and Forbearing": that Allah will admit the killed among the emigrants and the dead among them to an entrance they are pleased with, and that entrance is Paradise. "And indeed, Allah is Knowing" regarding those who emigrate for His cause among those who leave their homes seeking spoils or offering worldly offers. "Humble" refers to the sinners of His creation, by refraining from dealing with them with punishment and torment.
