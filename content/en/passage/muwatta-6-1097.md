## Summary
Malik transmits from Jafar ibn Muhammad, from his father, from Jabir ibn Abdullah, that when the Messenger of God came down from al-Safa and al-Marwa he walked, and when his feet reached the bottom of the valley he ran until he came out of it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1314), Ishaq al-Tabba, Suwayd (544) and others transmitted it, and that it is part of the lengthy hadith of Jabir. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.502}
