## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains that the one who made his desire a god is the one who worships a stone and then throws it at him if he sees something better than it, so his idol is what he chooses for himself. And the Prophet asks: Would you be a memorizer of such things while he is great in his ignorance, or do you think that most of them hear and understand?
