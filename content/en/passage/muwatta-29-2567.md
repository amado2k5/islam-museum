## Summary
Malik said: if a man is killed deliberately and the proof is established, and the dead man has sons and daughters, and the sons pardon while the daughters refuse to pardon: the pardon of the sons is valid against the daughters, and the daughters have no say with the sons in claiming the blood or pardoning it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.448}
