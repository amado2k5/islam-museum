## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Allah created every creature from water. Among them is he who walks on his belly, some who walk on two legs, and some who walk on fours. Allah creates whatever He wills. Indeed, Allah is over all things competent": The reciters differed in the interpretation of His statement: "By Allah, He created every creature from water." The majority of Kufa's reciters except Asim interpreted it as: "And Allah is the Creator of every creature." Most reciters of Medina and Basra, as well as Asim, recited it: "By God, He created every creature" in the accusative case of "all," and "created" as in the example of "fa'l." These are two well-known readings with similar meanings, because the addition in the reading of the one who reads it as "Khaliq" indicates that the meaning is to pass. So, whichever one reads the Quran, he is correct. And His saying: "He created every creature from water" means: from a drop of fluid. "Among them are those who walk on their bellies," like serpents and similar ones.
