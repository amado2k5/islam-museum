## Summary
Malik was asked whether it is easier for a man to perform voluntary circumambulation by joining two weeks or more together, and then to pray what is due for those weeks of prayer. He said: that is not fitting; the Sunna is to follow every seven with two rak'as. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1292) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.494}
