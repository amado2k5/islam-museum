## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Today We seal over their mouths and make their hands speak and their feet bear witness to what they used to earn": He means His mention by saying: "Today We seal over their mouths": Today We will seal the mouths of the polytheists, and that will be on the Day of Resurrection. "And their hands will speak about what they did in this world of sins against Allah," and "their feet will testify." It is said: the one who speaks from their legs is their thighs on the left leg "because of what they used to earn" in this world of sins. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-A'mash.
