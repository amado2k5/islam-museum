## Summary
A saying of Malik transmitted by Yahya, who heard it from him, in the chapter on the call while travelling: there is no harm in a man giving the call while riding. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik, and refers to Ibn Abi Shayba and al-Bayhaqi on the call of the rider. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
