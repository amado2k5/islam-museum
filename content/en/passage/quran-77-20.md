## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 24 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Did We not create you from a despised fluid?” as follows: “Did We not create you, O people, from a despised fluid?” meaning from a weak sperm, as narrated by my father, who said: My uncle narrated to me, who said: My father narrated to me, on the authority of his father, on the authority of Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase “Did We not create you from a despised fluid?” meaning by “despised”: weak. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and al-Dahhak in this section.
