## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 16 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “On that Day, man will be informed of what he put forth and what he left behind”: On that Day, meaning the Day when the sun and moon are gathered and rolled up, man will be informed of what he put forth and what he left behind. Scholars of interpretation differed in their understanding of the phrase, “what he put forth and what he left behind.” Some said it means what he did in the past, good or evil, in this world before his death, and what he left behind after his death, good or bad, or evil that others would do after him. In this section, he quotes interpretations from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
