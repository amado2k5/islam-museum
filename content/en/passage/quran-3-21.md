## Verse 21 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, p. 283)

Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-21|3:21]]) says the sense of those who disbelieve in God's signs is that they deny God's proofs and marks and call them false, among the two peoples of the Books, the Torah and the Gospel. In 6776, Muhammad ibn Ja'far ibn al-Zubayr (Ibn Humayd, Salama, Ibn Ishaq): then He gathered the two peoples of the Books together and mentioned what they innovated and invented. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 283}

We stop at the end of p. 283, within report 6776, before its continuation on p. 284. These are reports as transmitted, al-Tabari's choices and arguments are his, and the editor's notes and references are his, with no museum verdict.
