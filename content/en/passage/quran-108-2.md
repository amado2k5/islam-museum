## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Indeed, We have given you al-Kawthar" as: "Indeed, We have given you," O Muhammad, "al-Kawthar." Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of al-Kawthar, some saying: it is a river in Paradise that God gave His Prophet Muhammad. Ata ibn al-Sa'ib narrated to us, from Muharib ibn Duthar, from Ibn Umar: He said: al-Kawthar is a river in Paradise, with banks of gold and silver, flowing over pearls and rubies, its water whiter than milk and sweeter than honey. From Ata, from Muharib ibn Dathar al-Bahili, from Ibn Umar, regarding the phrase: "Indeed, We have given you al-Kawthar," he said: A river in Paradise whose banks are gold, its course on pearls and rubies, its water whiter than snow, sweeter than honey, and its soil more fragrant than the scent of musk. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sufyan, and Ata.
