## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among the people is he who says, 'We believe in Allah.' But when he is harmed in the cause of Allah, He makes the trial of the people like the punishment of Allah. But if help comes from your Lord, they will say, 'Indeed, we were with you.' Or Allah is not most knowledgeable of what is in the breasts of the worlds." And among the people is he who says: We acknowledge Allah and unite Him. But if the polytheists harm him by acknowledging Allah, he makes the trials of the people in this world like the punishment of Allah in the Hereafter, so he averts his faith in Allah, returning to disbelief in Him. "And if help comes from your Lord, O Muhammad, people of faith." By it, these apostates of their faith, who have caused the trial of people like the punishment of God, will say: 'Indeed, we were,' O believers, 'with you,' supporting you against your enemies, lying and slandering. Allah says: "Or is Allah not most knowledgeable?" O people, of each one of them "regarding what is in the breasts of the worlds"—all His creation, who say, 'We believe in Allah and others.' So if someone is harmed for the sake of Allah, he apostatizes from Allah's religion. So how can he deceive someone who is not hidden from Him or conceals from Him secretly or openly? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
