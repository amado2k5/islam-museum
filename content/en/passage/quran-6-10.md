## Summary
Verse 10 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 10 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad, consoling him with the threat against those who mock him and the penalty for the hurt of mockery and contempt he meets for God's sake: take it lightly, Muhammad, what you meet from these who mock you and hold your right to obedience to me in contempt, and go on with what I ordered you, the call to my oneness, acknowledging me and submitting to obey me; if they persist in their error and insist on their unbelief, the way of their predecessors among all the other peoples will be taken with them, of hasty vengeance and exemplary punishments descending on them. Peoples before you mocked messengers I sent them with the like of what I sent you with to your people, and did what your people did to you: "so there encompassed those who mocked among them what they used to mock". By "encompassed" he means it came down upon and surrounded those who ridiculed their messengers, "what they used to mock", that is, the punishment they laughed at and denied would come upon them as their messengers warned them. One says "haqa bihim hadha al-amr yahiqu haqan wa-huyuqan wa-hayaqanan". Report 13095, from al-Suddi: there fell on them the punishment they mocked. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 272}

Verse 10 closes here at p. 272, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-11|6:11]].
