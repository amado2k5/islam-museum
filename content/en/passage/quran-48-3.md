## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 5 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We have given you a clear opening" as meaning that by His saying, the Exalted, to His Prophet Muhammad, "Indeed, We have granted you a clear opening," he means: Indeed, We have judged for you, O Muhammad, a judgment for whoever hears it or reaches it against those who oppose you and appoint you among the disbelievers of your people, and We have decreed victory and victory for you, so that you may thank your Lord and praise Him for His blessing by granting you judgment over them and granting you the victory granted to you, and so that you may glorify Him and seek His forgiveness. Your Lord will forgive you by your actions. What your sins passed before He opened it for you is what He opened, and what was delayed after His conquest for you. That is what you thanked and sought forgiveness.
