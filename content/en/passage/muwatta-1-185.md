## Summary
A saying of Malik transmitted by Yahya in the chapter on the call to prayer: the dawn prayer has always been called before dawn, while for the other prayers he did not see a call made until its time had come. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.120}
