**Commentary on verse 40 (p. 94)**

On [[passage/quran-10-40|10:40]] he says: among your people, Muhammad, of Quraysh, there are those who will in time believe in it, that is, will accept the Qur'an and affirm that it is from God, and those who will never believe in it; your Lord knows best the corrupters, meaning those who deny and will never accept, none of whom is hidden from Him and who stand beyond His punishment, while those written to believe He will turn to in mercy. The editor refers to the gloss on "corruption". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 94}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
