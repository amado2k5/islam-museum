## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have they not seen that Allah, who created the heavens and the earth and did not realize their creation, is capable of giving life to the dead? Indeed, He is over all things competent": Or did these deniers not consider that Allah revived His creation after their death and raised them from their graves after their trial, saying to their fathers and mothers, "Woe to you, why do you promise to bring me out when centuries have passed before me?" They did not raise the eyes of their hearts, so they see and know that Allah created the seven heavens and the earth and created them from nothing, not comprehending their creation, thus failing about their invention and creation "capable of bringing the dead back to life," bringing them out alive after their affliction in their graves as they were before their death.
