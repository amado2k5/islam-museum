## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "The heavens and the earth did not weep for them, nor did they look": So it did not weep for those whom God drowned in the sea, Pharaoh and his people, the sky and the earth. It is said that the crying of the sky is the redness of its edges. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ata.
