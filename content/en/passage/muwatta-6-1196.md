## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that the Messenger of God prayed two rak'as at Mina, and that Abu Bakr, Umar and Uthman prayed them at Mina as two rak'as, and that Uthman prayed them in full for half of his rule, then completed them afterwards. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.538}
