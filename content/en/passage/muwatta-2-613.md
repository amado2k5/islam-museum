## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar said: the funeral prayer is said after the afternoon and after the dawn when these two prayers are said at their proper times. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.314}
