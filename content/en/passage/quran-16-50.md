## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "they fear their Lord above them and do what they are commanded": These angels in the heavens and on the earth fear their Lord above them that He will punish them if they disobey His command. "And they do what they are commanded" means: they do what Allah commands them, fulfilling His rights and avoiding His wrath.
