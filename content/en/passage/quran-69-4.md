## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-4 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Thamud and 'Ad denied the Striking Calamity" as follows: Thamud, the people of Salih, and 'Ad, the people of Hud, denied the Hour, which will strike the hearts of people with its onslaught. "Al-Qari'ah" is also one of the names of the Day of Resurrection. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
