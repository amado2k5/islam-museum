## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So exalted is Allah, the True Sovereign, and do not hasten the Qur'an before its revelation has come to you. And say, 'My Lord, increase me in knowledge': So the One to whom worship is elevated from all His creation, the Sovereign whose authority has overwhelmed every king and might, is the truth from what the polytheists behind him describe it. 'And do not hasten the Qur'an before its revelation has been decreed to you.' He says to His Prophet Muhammad: 'And do not hasten, O Muhammad, with the Qur'an, so your companions or recite it to them before its meanings are revealed to you, so you are reproached for writing and dictating it.' What Allah would have revealed to him from His Book was written by those who wrote it before His meanings had been clarified. It was said: Do not impose it on anyone, nor dictate it to anyone, until We make it clear to you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
