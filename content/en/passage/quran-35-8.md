## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Is he for whom the evil of his deeds is made fair and he sees it good? Indeed, Allah misleads whom He wills and guides whom He wills. So let not yourself be deterred against them. Indeed, Allah is Knowing of what they do": Is it one for whom Satan has pleased his evil deeds, such as disobedience to Allah, disbelief in Him, and worshipping gods and idols below Him? So he sees it as good, and considers that evil good, and thinks his evil is beautiful, to beautify Satan for him, your soul has lost regret over them and omission from the speech: Your soul has lost regrets over them, suffice with the meaning of His saying: "So do not let your soul suffer regrets over them." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
