**Commentary on verse 60 (p. 367)**

On [[passage/quran-11-60|11:60]] al-Tabari says: 'Ad, the people of Hud, were followed in this world by anger from God and on the Day of Resurrection by a like wrath, a curse upon the curse that went before for them from God in the world; 'surely 'Ad disbelieved their Lord; away with 'Ad, the people of Hud' means God put them far from good. One says 'so-and-so disbelieved his Lord' and 'disbelieved in his Lord', and 'I thanked you' and 'I thanked to you'. It is also said that 'they disbelieved their Lord' means they were ungrateful for their Lord's favour. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 367}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
