**Commentary on verse 80 (p. 394)**

Al-Tabari says to His Prophet: pray to God for forgiveness for these hypocrites whose descriptions I gave in these verses, or do not pray for it for them. This is speech in the form of a command whose sense is a statement, meaning whether you ask forgiveness for them, Muhammad, or do not ask it for them, God will not forgive them. The words "if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times, God will not forgive them" mean that if you ask that their sins be covered with His pardon of them and their disgrace be avoided, God will not cover them or pardon them but will disgrace them for them before all the witnesses on the Day of Resurrection; "that is because they disbelieved in God and His Messenger", the Almighty says, this act of God toward them, His not pardoning their sins, is because they denied the oneness of God and the message of His Messenger; "and God does not guide the transgressing people" means God does not grant success for belief in Him and in His Messenger, and the discussion continues on the next page. The editor refers to earlier explanations of asking forgiveness, forgiveness and guidance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 394}

We stop here at the foot of p. 394, in the middle of the commentary on verse 80 (before the explanation of "God does not guide the transgressing people" is complete); the next batch begins at p. 395. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 394}
