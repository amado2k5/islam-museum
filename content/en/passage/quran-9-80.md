## Summary
Verse 80 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/death-of-abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Death of Abdullah ibn Ubayy]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

**Commentary on verse 80 (pp. 394-397)**

Al-Tabari says to His Prophet: pray to God for forgiveness for these hypocrites whose descriptions I gave in these verses, or do not pray for it for them. This is speech in the form of a command whose sense is a statement, meaning whether you ask forgiveness for them, Muhammad, or do not ask it for them, God will not forgive them. The words "if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times, God will not forgive them" mean that if you ask that their sins be covered with His pardon of them and their disgrace be avoided, God will not cover them or pardon them but will disgrace them for them before all the witnesses on the Day of Resurrection; "that is because they disbelieved in God and His Messenger", the Almighty says, this act of God toward them, His not pardoning their sins, is because they denied the oneness of God and the message of His Messenger; "and God does not guide the transgressing people" means God does not grant success for belief in Him and in His Messenger those who preferred unbelief in Him and departure from obedience to Him over belief in Him and in His Messenger. The editor refers to earlier explanations of asking forgiveness, forgiveness and guidance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 394}

It is reported of the Messenger of God that when this verse came down he said "I shall add to asking forgiveness for them beyond seventy times", hoping that God would forgive them, and the verse of Surat al-Munafiqun came down, "it is the same for them whether you ask forgiveness for them or do not, God will never forgive them", which is [[passage/quran-63-6|63:6]]. Report 17023, from Urwa, through Ibn Waki', Abda ibn Sulayman and Hisham ibn Urwa: Abdallah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul said to his companions "if you did not spend on Muhammad and his Companions, they would scatter from around him", and he is the one who said "if we return to Medina the mighty will drive out the weak", [[passage/quran-63-8|63:8]]; and God sent down "ask forgiveness for them or do not ask forgiveness for them", and the Prophet said "I shall add to the seventy", and God sent down "it is the same for them whether you ask forgiveness for them or do not", and God, blessed and exalted, refused to forgive them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 395}

Report 17024, from al-Sha'bi, through Ibn Humayd, Ibn Waki', Jarir, Mughira and Shabak: Abdallah ibn Abdallah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul called the Prophet to his father's funeral, and the Prophet said to him "who are you?", and he said "Hubab ibn Abdallah ibn Ubayy", and the Prophet said to him "rather you are Abdallah ibn Abdallah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul, for Hubab is the devil", and then the Prophet said "it has been said to me, 'ask forgiveness for them or do not ask forgiveness for them; if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times God will never forgive them', so I shall ask forgiveness for them seventy and seventy and seventy", and the Prophet clothed him in his shirt while he was sweating. The editor explains that the word Hubab means a snake and is also applied to the devil. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 395}

Reports 17025, 17026, 17027 and 17028, from Mujahid, by the routes of Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa, Ibn Abi Najih, al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl, Ishaq, Abdallah, Warqa', al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: the Prophet said "I shall add to seventy askings of forgiveness", and God sent down in the surah in which the hypocrites are mentioned "God will never forgive them", as a firm decision, meaning an emphasis and a binding truth. Report 17029, from al-Sha'bi, through al-Husayn, Hushaym and Mughira: when Abdallah ibn Ubayy was gravely ill his son went to the Prophet and said "my father is dying, and I would like you to attend him and pray over him", and the Prophet said "what is your name?", and he said "al-Hubab ibn Abdallah", and he said "rather you are Abdallah ibn Abdallah ibn Ubayy, for Hubab is the name of a devil"; he said, and he went with him until he attended him and clothed him in his shirt while he was sweating, and prayed over him, and it was said to him "do you pray over him when he is a hypocrite?", and he said "God said 'if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times God will never forgive them', and I shall ask forgiveness for him seventy and seventy", and Hushaym said I doubt the third. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 396}

Report 17030, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: "ask forgiveness for them or do not" to the words "the transgressing people", and the Messenger of God said when this verse came down "I hear that my Lord has given me latitude about them, so by God I shall ask forgiveness for them more than seventy times, perhaps God will forgive them", and God, in the intensity of His anger with them, said "it is the same for them whether you ask forgiveness for them or do not, God will never forgive them; God does not guide the transgressing people", which is in Surat al-Munafiqun. Report 17031, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: the Prophet of God said "my Lord has given me a choice, so I shall add to seventy for them", and God sent down "it is the same for them whether you ask forgiveness for them", the verse; report 17032, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar, is similar. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 397}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
