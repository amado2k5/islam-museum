## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, I only warn you by the revelation, and the deaf do not hear the supplication when they are warned": Say, O Muhammad, to those who say, 'Let him bring us a sign as the ancients sent: I only warn you, O people, of the revelation of Allah which He reveals to those with Him, and I warn you of His severity by it.' Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "Say, I only warn you by revelation," meaning by this Qur'an. And His saying: 'And the deaf do not hear the supplication'—reciters differed in its reading; the majority of the reciters of the regions read it as 'wa wa ya'ma' with a fatha on the ya' from 'ya', meaning it is a verb for the deaf, and 'al-sam' is then nominative. It is narrated from Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Sulami that he used to recite: 'wa la tasma' with a ta' and a damma on it, so 'al-sam' in this reading is nominative because the phrase 'wa la tasma' is not named for its doer, and its meaning in this recitation is: 'And Allah does not let the deaf hear the supplication.' In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
