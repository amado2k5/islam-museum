## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "O My servants who have believed, indeed My earth is vast, so worship Me": Allah Almighty says to those who believe in Him among His servants: "O My servants who have united with Me and believed in Me and My Messenger Muhammad, indeed My earth is vast." Interpreters differed regarding the meaning intended by the statement about the vastness of the earth. Some said: What is meant by this is that it has not become difficult for you, so stay in a place where you are not fit to stay. But if one acts in a place of disobedience against Allah and cannot change it, then flee from it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Ata.
