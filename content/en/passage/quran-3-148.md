## Summary
Verse 148 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 148 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Reward of this world and of the next (p. 275).** Al-Tabari: God gave those He described, who kept patient in obeying Him after the killing of their prophets, in struggle with their enemy, in seeking His help and in following their leader's way, a reward in this world, which is victory over their enemy and His, triumph, conquest and a firm place in the land, and the best reward of the next world for their good deeds, which is Paradise and its delights. Qatada (7994): by God, God gave them conquest, ascendancy, establishment and victory in this world, and in the next the Garden. Al-Rabi' (7995): the same. Ibn Jurayj (7996): the reward of this world is victory and spoils, and of the next world God's good pleasure and mercy. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 275}

**Ibn Ishaq and the doers of good (p. 276).** Ibn Humayd from Salama from Ibn Ishaq (7997): the reward of this world is ascendancy over their enemy, and of the next the Garden and what is prepared in it. Al-Tabari: God did that for them because of their doing good, and He loves the doers of good, who are those who do as was described of them when their prophet was killed. The editor notes that the printed text and manuscript differ over a word of the report and that Ibn Hisham has a different preposition, and traces it to the Sira (3: 119) as a continuation of 7993, abridged. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 276}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
