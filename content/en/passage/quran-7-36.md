## Place in the museum

Then, on "but those who deny Our signs and are arrogant about them, those are the companions of the Fire, abiding in it", God says: as for whoever denies the coming of My messengers whom I sent to him, rejects My oneness, disbelieves in what My messengers brought and is arrogant about affirming My proofs and evidences, "those are the companions of the Fire, they will abide in it" means they will stay in the fire of Hell and never leave it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 407}
