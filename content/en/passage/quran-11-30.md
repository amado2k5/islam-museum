**Commentary on verse 30 (p. 302)**

On [[passage/quran-11-30|11:30]] al-Tabari says: my people, who will help me against God, protecting me from Him, if He punishes me for driving away the believers who affirm God's oneness, if I drive them away? 'Will you not reflect?' means will you not think about what you say, know its error and stop it? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 302}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
