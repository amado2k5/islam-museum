## Summary
It reached Yahya ibn Yahya from Malik that a slave of Abdullah ibn Umar ran away, and a horse of his was taken; the polytheists seized both, and then the Muslims took them as booty and returned them to Abdullah ibn Umar, before the shares were distributed. The editor notes that Bukhari connected this report through narrations from Nafi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.583}
