## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih from his father from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on general rules of ablution: when a Muslim washes his face, every sin his eyes looked at leaves with the water or the last drop; when he washes his hands, every sin his hands seized leaves them, until he comes out clean of sins. The editor notes that al-Baji said the phrase "or the believer" is a doubt of the narrator, and that mention of two men in some copies is not from Yahya's transmission but Ibn Wahb's addition from Malik, as Ibn Abd al-Barr said. He cites Muslim, al-Tirmidhi, al-Darimi, Ibn Khuzayma and Ibn Hibban. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.70} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.71}
