## Summary
Malik said: Umar ibn al-Khattab expelled the Jews of Najran and Fadak. The Jews of Khaybar left with no share of the fruit or the land. The Jews of Fadak had half the fruit and half the land, because the Messenger of God had made that treaty with them; Umar gave them the value of half the fruit and half the land in gold, silver, camels, ropes and saddles, then paid them the value and expelled them from it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.471}
