## Summary
Verse 21 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 21 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "who is more wrong than he who invents a lie against God or denies his signs? The wrongdoers do not prosper" al-Tabari says: who is more transgressing, more mistaken in act and more erring in word than he who invents a lie against God, that is, who fabricates a false saying against God and forges a lie from himself against him, claiming that he has a partner from his creation and a god worshipped besides him, as the idol-worshipping polytheists said, or claims a son or wife for him, as the Christians said; "or denies his signs" means denies his proofs, marks and indications that he gave his messengers for the truth of their prophethood, as the Jews denied them; "the wrongdoers do not prosper" means that those who say falsehood against God do not prosper or win to stay in the gardens, nor do those who invent a lie against him or reject the prophethood of his prophets. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 296}

The editor refers for "invention", "sign" and "prosperity" to earlier notes, and says that "ikhtaraqa", "ikhtalaqa" and "iftara" all mean to devise a lie, as in [[passage/quran-6-100|6:100]], "they ascribed to him sons and daughters without knowledge".

Verse 21 closes here at p. 296, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-22|6:22]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
