## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that he heard Abdullah ibn Umar on al-Safa supplicating: O God, You said, call upon Me and I will answer you, and You do not break Your promise; and I ask You, as You guided me to Islam, not to take it from me until You take me while I am a Muslim. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1313) and Suwayd (543) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.500}
