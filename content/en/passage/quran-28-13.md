## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We returned him to his mother so that her eyes would be satisfied and she would not grieve, and she would know that the promise of Allah is true, but most of them do not know": "So We returned Moses to his mother after the Pharaoh's family had picked him up, so that her eyes would be satisfied with her son, as he returned to her safe from Pharaoh's killing. "And do not grieve" over his separation from her, "and you will know that the promise of Allah which He promised her is when He said to her: 'So when you fear for him, cast him into the sea, and do not fear or grieve...' The verse is true.
