## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22-24 as a single unit. He interprets the words, “Indeed, We have sent down to you the Qur’an in stages”: We have sent down this Qur’an to you, O Muhammad, in stages, as a trial and test from Us. “So be patient for the judgment of your Lord,” meaning: Be patient with what your Lord has tested you with, with His obligations, the delivery of His messages, and the fulfillment of what He has obligated you to do in His revelation that He has inspired to you. “And do not obey from among them a sinner or an ungrateful one,” meaning: Do not obey in disobedience to God any of the polytheists of your people who are sinful, meaning those who commit sins, or ungrateful, meaning those who deny His blessings and favors upon them, for they disbelieve in Him and worship other than Him. It has been said that the one meant by this statement is Abu Jahl. The passage quotes statements from Qatada and Ibn Zaid.
