## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that he used to say: whoever swears an oath and emphasises it, then breaches it, owes freeing a slave or clothing ten needy people; and whoever swears an oath without emphasising it, then breaches it, owes feeding ten needy people, a mudd of wheat for each; whoever cannot find it, fasts three days. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.615}
