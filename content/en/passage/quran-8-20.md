**Commentary on verse 20 (p. 457)**

Then al-Tabari says on "you who believe, obey God and His Messenger and do not turn away from him while you hear" [[passage/quran-8-20|8:20]]: you who trusted God and His Messenger, obey God and His Messenger in what He commanded you and forbade you; "do not turn away from him" meaning do not turn from the Messenger of God, opposing his command and prohibition; "while you hear" his command and prohibition to you, and you believe in him. Report 15852, from Ibn Ishaq: that is, do not oppose his command while you hear his word and claim you are of him. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "turning" and notes that the printed edition has "you claim you are believers" while he follows the manuscript, which agrees with Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 457}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
