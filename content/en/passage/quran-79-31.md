## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 34 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And He darkened its night and brought forth its morning light”: “And He darkened its night,” God Almighty says: He darkened the night of the sky. He attributed the night to the sky because the night is the setting of the sun, and its setting and rising are within it. It is attributed to the sky because it is within it, just as the stars of the night are said to be there, since rising and setting are within it. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
