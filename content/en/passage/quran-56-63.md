## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 62 to 64 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have you seen what you sow": Have you seen the plow you sow? "Do you sow it, or are we the sowers?" He means: Do you make it a crop, or do we make it so? And it is said: And from Hashim, from Muhammad, from Abu Huraira, who said: The Messenger of Allah said: "Do not say, 'I sown,' but say, 'I have sowed.'" Abu Huraira said: Have you not heard of God's saying: 'Have you seen what you sow, are you sowing, or are we the sowers?' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
