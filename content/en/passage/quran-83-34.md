## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 37 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "And they were not sent as guardians over them" as follows: "So today," meaning on the Day of Resurrection, "those who believed" in God in this world "will laugh at the disbelievers" therein, "on thrones, looking on." He says: on their couches in the canopies, they will look at them, and they are in Paradise, while the disbelievers are in Hell being punished. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
