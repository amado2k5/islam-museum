## Summary
Malik said: the best that has been heard about a man who buys the contract slave of another: he may not sell him, if his master contracted him for gold or silver, except for goods which he pays at once and does not delay, because if he delays it, it becomes debt for debt, and debt for debt has been forbidden. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.353}
