## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they do not intercede except for whom He is pleased with, and they are fearful of Him": He knows what is before His angels that which they have not reached and what they are in it, and what they say and do, "and what is behind them" means: and what has passed before today, what they left behind of them of times and ages, what they did in it. They said: All of that is accounted for and upon them, and nothing of that is hidden from Him. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
