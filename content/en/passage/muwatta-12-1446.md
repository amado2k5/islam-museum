## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Ali ibn Abi Talib performed the aqiqa for his two sons Hasan and Husayn. The editor notes that Abu Dawud transmitted with a sound chain from Ikrima from Ibn Abbas that the Messenger of God performed the aqiqa for Hasan and Husayn, one ram each. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.647}
