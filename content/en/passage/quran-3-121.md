## Summary
Verse 121 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 121 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Link to the preceding verses (p. 159).** Al-Tabari says that if the believers are patient and godwary the scheming of these Jews will not harm them at all, but God will help them against them as he helped them at Badr when they were weak; and if they oppose his command, do not hold to the duties he laid on them and do not guard against what he forbade, what befell them at Uhud will befall them. So he reminds them of that day when their Prophet went out early to place the believers in their positions for battle. He holds that the report of what happens if they are not patient is left unstated because the flow of the speech indicates it: it mentions what God does for them in turning aside their enemies' scheming if they are patient, then follows with a reminder of the trial at Uhud, when some of them opposed the command of the Messenger of God and disputed among themselves. The address is outwardly to the Prophet, but those meant are the ones forbidden to take the Jews as intimates, and the particle at the start has its construction from the sense of the speech as he has explained. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 159}

**The differing view on which day is meant (pp. 159-160).** Some said it is the day of Uhud; others said it is the day of the Confederates. Those who said Uhud: {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 159}

- From Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa, Ibn Abi Najih, Mujahid (7708): the Prophet walked on his feet that day, placing the believers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}
- From Bishr, Yazid, Sa'id, Qatada (7709): it is the day of Uhud; the Prophet of God went out from his family to Uhud to place the believers in their battle positions. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}
- From Ammar, Ibn Abi Ja'far, his father, al-Rabi' (7710): the same. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}
- From Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle, his father, his grandfather, Ibn Abbas (7711): it is the day of Uhud. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}
- From Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal, Asbat, al-Suddi (7712): this is the day of Uhud. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}
- From Ibn Humayd, Salama, Ibn Ishaq (7713): this is among what was revealed on the day of Uhud; the editor says the report is abridged from the Sira of Ibn Hisham (3: 112). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}

Those who said the Confederates are introduced at the foot of p. 160 with the report of Muhammad ibn Sinan al-Qazzaz from Abu Bakr al-Hanafi, which continues on p. 161.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 160 in the commentary on verse 121 and continue from p. 161. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 160}
