**Commentary on verse 31 (pp. 302-303)**

On [[passage/quran-11-31|11:31]] al-Tabari says: 'I do not say to you that I have the treasuries of God' is joined to 'my people, I ask you no wage for it'; the sense is: I ask you no wage, and I do not say I have the treasuries of God that nothing exhausts, so that I should call you to follow me for them; nor do I know the unseen, meaning what is hidden of servants' secrets, which only God knows, so as to claim lordship and call you to worship me; nor do I say I am an angel sent to you, which would be a lie in my claim, but I am a mortal like you as you say, ordered to call you to God, and I have delivered to you what I was sent with. 'Nor do I say to those whom your eyes despise, God will never give them good' means I do not say to those who followed me, believed in God and affirmed His oneness, whom your eyes hold in contempt and whom you called your lowliest, that God will never give them good, that is, belief in God; 'God knows best what is in their souls' means God best knows the hidden thoughts of their breasts and the belief of their hearts, and He is the guardian of their affair in that, and mine is only what appears; they showed belief in God and followed me, so I will not drive them away nor think it lawful; 'then I would be among the wrongdoers' means if I said to those who showed belief in God and affirmed me that God will never give them good, and judged their hidden thoughts against what their tongues showed me, without knowledge of what is in their souls, and drove them out by it, I would be one of those who do what is not theirs to do and transgress what God ordered them, and that is wrong. Report 18116, from Ibn Jurayj, says likewise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 303}
