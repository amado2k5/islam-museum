## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 58 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "they and their wives are in shade, reclining according to Your opinions": He means by saying: "They" are the companions of Paradise "and their spouses" from the people of Paradise, as Ibn Abi Nujayh narrated from Mujahid, His saying: "They and their wives are in shade," meaning their shelters are in shade. Reciters differed in reading this; some read it as "in shade" meaning the plural of shade, just as the garment plurals are garments. Others read it as "in shades," and if it is read this way, it has two interpretations: one is that it means the plural of shadows, which means 'kan', so the meaning of the word then is: they and their wives in a 'kan' do not sacrifice for the sun as the people of the world do, because there is no sun in it. The other is that it is meant to be the plural of a shade, so the plural of it is similar, similar to their plural of 'hillah' in abundance: 'al-khallal', and 'qawla' means 'few'. In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
