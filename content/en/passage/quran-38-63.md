## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 62–64. Al-Tabari mentions that the tyrants, and some say Abu Jahl, al-Walid ibn al-Mughira, and their likes, say, 'Why do we not see men whom we considered wicked? Did we take them as mockery or have our eyes deviated from them? Indeed, that is the true dispute of the people of the Fire.'
