## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who display [their wives] and then return to what they said—then freeing a slave before they touch—that is what you admonish. And Allah is Acquainted with what you do": He, the Exalted, says: And those who say to their wives: You are like the backs of our mothers. And His saying: "Then they return to what they said." Scholars differ regarding the meaning of repetition when it comes to manifestations. Some say it means retracting what he forbade himself from his wife, who was lawful to him before he showed off, and he permits her after he forbade her to himself by deceiving, unconsciously deceiving, and having intercourse with her. In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
