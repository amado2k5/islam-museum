## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from a Muhajir man whom he saw no harm in, in the chapter on practice in the congregational prayer: Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As was asked whether one may pray at the watering place among the camels' resting place, and he said no, but pray in the sheepfold. The editor notes that Yunus ibn Bukayr transmitted it raised from Hisham, but Yunus is not to be relied on when he differs from Malik, and that Ahmad raised it from Ibn Lahi'a by a weak chain, though its text is sound from al-Bara', Abdullah ibn Mughaffal, Jabir ibn Samura and others. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.240} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.239}
