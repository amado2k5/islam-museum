## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 21 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Indeed, what you are promised is bound to occur": By the winds sent forth in succession, what you, O people, are promised is bound to occur, and it will inevitably come to pass. He means by this the Day of Resurrection, and what God has mentioned that He has prepared for His creation on that day of reward and punishment. He then quotes in this section statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
