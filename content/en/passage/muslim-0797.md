## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on the merit of the reciter of the Qur'an, in the Book of the Prayer of Travellers in Sahih Muslim. A simile compares the believer and the hypocrite to a citron, a date, basil and bitter colocynth. {cite:sahih-muslim}
