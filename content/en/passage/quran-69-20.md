## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “I was certain that I would meet my reckoning” as meaning: I knew that I would meet my reckoning when I appear before my Lord on the Day of Judgment. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
