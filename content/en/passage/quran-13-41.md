**Commentary on verse 41 (p. 493)**

On "do they not see that We come to the land, diminishing it from its borders? God judges; none can repel His judgment, and He is swift in reckoning", al-Tabari says: the interpreters differed over its interpretation. Some said its meaning is: do these polytheists of Mecca who ask Muhammad for signs not see that We come to the land and open it for him, land after land around their own land? Do they not fear that We open their land for him as We opened others for him? Report 20514, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Muhammad ibn al-Sabbah, Hushaym, Husayn and Ikrima: "that We come to the land, diminishing it from its borders", he said, have they not seen that We open the land for Muhammad, land after land? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 493}

We stop here at the foot of p. 493, in the middle of the commentary on verse 41 (after report 20514, on "diminishing it from its borders"); the next batch begins at p. 494 (marker 502). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 493}
