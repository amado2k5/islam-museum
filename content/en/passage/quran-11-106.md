**Commentary on verse 106 (p. 479)**

On [[passage/quran-11-106|11:106]] al-Tabari says: 'as for those who are wretched, they are in the Fire, there they will have sighing and sobbing': 'zafir' is the beginning of the donkey's bray and the like, and 'shahiq' is the end of its braying, when it draws it back inside as it finishes; he cites a line of Ru'ba b. al-'Ajjaj on a wild ass's cry, and the editor explains its words. The reports follow on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 479}
