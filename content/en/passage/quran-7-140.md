**Commentary on verse 140 (p. 84)**

Al-Tabari says Moses told his people: "Shall I seek for you a god other than God", that is, look for you something to worship, "when it is He who has favoured you over the worlds" of your age and time? That is: shall I seek for you something to worship that neither benefits nor harms you, and you leave the worship of the One who favoured you over creation? This from you is ignorance. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "seek" and "worlds". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 84}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
