**Commentary on verse 138: the good news of painful punishment for the hypocrites (p. 318)**

Al-Tabari says that 'announce good news' here means informing: God informs the hypocrites that for their hypocrisy they will have a painful punishment on the Day of Resurrection, that is, the torment of hell. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 318}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
