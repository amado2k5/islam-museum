## Summary
Verse 69 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 69 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "those who fear God are not accountable for them in anything, but it is a reminder, that they may fear" al-Tabari says: whoever fears God and obeys him in what he commanded and avoids what he forbade has, in not turning away from these who plunge into God's signs while they plunge, no burden between himself and God, if his not turning away is not satisfaction with what they are in and he fears God; nor does their sin lay any blame on him, but let them turn away from them then as a reminder of God's command, "that they may fear" means that they may guard themselves. The sense of "dhikra" is "dhikr". "Dhikra" may be in the accusative, in the sense: but let them turn away from them as a reminder, or in the nominative, in the sense: but their turning away is a reminder of God's command, that they may fear. It was also said that the Prophet was told to get up from the polytheists when they plunged into God's signs because his rising from them was something they disliked, so God told him: when they plunge into God's signs, rise from them, that they may guard against plunging into them and stop it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 439}

The editor says the manuscript also has "fearing for his rights" and he thinks "fearing him" more likely, but did not change it as the other is nearly permissible, and refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an.

13396, from Ibn Jurayj: the polytheists used to sit by the Prophet wanting to hear him, and when they heard they mocked, so the verse came down; when they mocked he got up, and they took care and said do not mock or he will rise; that is "that they may fear". "Those who fear God are not accountable for them in anything" came down for those who sat with them, but you do not sit. Then this was abrogated by God's words at Medina, [[passage/quran-4-140|4:140]], which abrogated "those who fear God are not accountable for them in anything". 13397, from al-Suddi: in anything of the reckoning of the unbelievers, "but it is a reminder": when you remember, get up; "that they may fear" your displeasure: when they saw you not sitting with them they were ashamed before you and held back from you. Then God abrogated it afterward and forbade sitting with them ever, and he read the verse of al-Nisa. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 440}

13398 and 13399, from Mujahid: they are not accountable for them in anything if they sit, but you do not sit. 13400, from Abu Malik: what is it to you if they plunge into God's signs, if you do that. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 441}

Verse 69 closes here on p. 441, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-70|6:70]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
