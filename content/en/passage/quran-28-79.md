## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So he went out to his people in his adornment, saying, 'Those who desire the life of this world, would we have had like what Qarun was given; indeed, he is one of great fortune.'" Qarun went out to his people in his adornment, which is mentioned as purple garments. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Hasan, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Samak.
