## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he entered ihram from Mecca, did not circumambulate the House or run between al-Safa and al-Marwa until he returned from Mina; and when he circumambulated around the House after entering ihram from Mecca, he did not hasten. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1304), Suwayd (553) and al-Shaybani (520) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.490}
