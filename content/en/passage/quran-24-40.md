## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Or like darkness in a deep sea, covered by waves above it, waves above it, clouds of darkness, some layers upon others. When He puts out His hand, he can hardly see it. And whoever Allah does not make light for him, then he has no light." This is another example Allah gave for the deeds of the disbelievers. Allah says: The example of the deeds of these disbelievers is that they acted in error, corruption, misguidance, and confusion among their agents and without guidance, like darkness in a raging sea. The sea is attributed to 'al-Lijjah' to describe it as deep and abundant in water. And 'the depths of the sea': most of it. 'Covered by waves' means: the sea is covered by waves; 'above it are waves': he says: above the wave, another wave covers him, 'above it, clouds': he says: above the second wave that covers the first wave with clouds. In the exclusion, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Al-Rabi'.
