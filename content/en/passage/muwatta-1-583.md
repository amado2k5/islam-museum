## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abu al-Darda' used to rise in the middle of the night and say: the eyes have slept and the stars have set, and You are the Living, the Self-Subsisting. This is the last hadith in the chapter on practice in supplication. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.300}
