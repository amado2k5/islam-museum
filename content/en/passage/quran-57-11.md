## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Who is it that will lend Allah a goodly loan and multiply it for him and will have a generous reward": Who is this who spends in the way of Allah in this world, seeking reward in his expenses, seeking what is with Allah? That is the good loan. He says: So his Lord multiplies for him his loan which He lent him by spending in His cause, and He makes for him seven hundred at a time. Some grammarians from Basra used to say regarding the phrase: "Who is it that will lend Allah a good loan?" It is like the Arabs' saying: I have a loan with you sincerely, and a bad loan if he does good with it. He recited this verse by Al-Shanfari: "We will reward Salaman ibn Mufarrij for her loan with what their hands have given and it removes." "And he has a generous reward," meaning: He has a generous reward and recompense, meaning by that reward: Paradise. We have previously mentioned the narration from the interpreters about this in a way that suffices to repeat it.
