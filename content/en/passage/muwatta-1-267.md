## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Sa'id ibn Abi Sa'id al-Maqburi from Abu Hurayra, who used to say, in the chapter on the ritual washing on Friday: washing on Friday is obligatory on every adult male, like the bath from sexual impurity. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.157}
