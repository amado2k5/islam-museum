## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who take besides Him as allies of Allah, a guardian over them, and you are not a guardian over them": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, "And those who take," O Muhammad, among the polytheists of your people, "besides Allah" are gods they take as guardians and worship them. "Allah is their guardian," who records their deeds and records their deeds, so that He may recompense them on the Day of Resurrection with their recompense. "And you are not a guardian over them." He means: You are not the guardian over them, O Muhammad, but rather you are a warner. So convey to them what you have sent to them, for it is only upon you to convey the message, and upon Us is the account.
