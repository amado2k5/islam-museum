## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And thus We have revealed to you a spirit of Our affair. You did not know what the Book was or faith, but We made it a light by which We guide whoever We choose from Our servants. And indeed, You guide to a straight path": He means that He mentioned it by saying: "And thus We have revealed to you a spirit of Our command." Just as We revealed to you concerning Our messengers, so too have We revealed to you, O Muhammad, this Qur'an, a spirit of Our command: He says: And may He grant us peace and mercy. The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of 'spirit' in this context; some said: it meant mercy. In the section, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
