## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra from Qays ibn Abi Hazim in the chapter on disliking asking people, in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim. Working with firewood is better than asking, and the hadith ends by preferring the upper hand and beginning with those one supports. {cite:sahih-muslim}
