**Commentary on verse 59 (p. 111)**

On [[passage/quran-10-59|10:59]] he says to His Prophet: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists, consider, people, what God sent down to you of provision, meaning what He created of provision for you and made yours, which is the food you eat; "and of it you made some forbidden and some permitted" means you made some of it lawful for yourselves and forbade some, as they forbade of their crops what they set aside for their idols, as God describes them at [[passage/quran-6-136|6:136]], and of livestock what they forbade by slitting the ear and setting free and the like, which he treated earlier in the book. Then: say, Muhammad, has God given you leave to forbid what you forbade, or do you invent against God, that is, speak falsehood and lie? The interpreters spoke in the same sense, and report 17689 begins, from Ibn Abbas: the people of the Jahiliyya forbade things God had made lawful, of clothing and other things; it continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 111}
