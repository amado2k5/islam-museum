**Commentary on verse 59 (pp. 111-112)**

On [[passage/quran-10-59|10:59]] he says to His Prophet: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists, consider, people, what God sent down to you of provision, meaning what He created of provision for you and made yours, which is the food you eat; "and of it you made some forbidden and some permitted" means you made some of it lawful for yourselves and forbade some, as they forbade of their crops what they set aside for their idols, as God describes them at [[passage/quran-6-136|6:136]], and of livestock what they forbade by slitting the ear and setting free and the like, which he treated earlier in the book. Then: say, Muhammad, has God given you leave to forbid what you forbade, or do you invent against God, that is, speak falsehood and lie? The interpreters spoke in the same sense, and report 17689 begins, from Ibn Abbas: the people of the Jahiliyya forbade things God had made lawful, of clothing and other things; it continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 111}

The rest of report 17689, from Ibn Abbas: the people of the Jahiliyya forbade things of clothing and other things, and this is what the verse meant; so God sent down what is at [[passage/quran-7-32|7:32]]. Report 17690, from Ibn Abbas: they are the people of polytheism. Report 17691, from Ibn Abbas: the crops and livestock, and from Mujahid: the slit-eared and the freed animals; likewise report 17692, from Mujahid: about the slit-eared and the freed. Report 17693, from Qatada: every provision I did not forbid you forbade yourselves, of your women, your wealth and your children; has God given you leave, or do you invent against God? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 112}

Report 17694, from Ibn Zayd, in which he recited [[passage/quran-6-138|6:138]] and [[passage/quran-6-139|6:139]], then [[passage/quran-6-143|6:143]] and [[passage/quran-6-144|6:144]], and said that this is its meaning: He gave them provision and they made some of it forbidden and some permitted, forbidding part and permitting part; and he asked which of the two He forbade to those who speak so and allowed to these. Report 17695, from al-Dahhak: it is what God said at [[passage/quran-6-136|6:136]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 113}
