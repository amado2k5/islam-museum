**Commentary on verse 13 (pp. 259-260)**

On [[passage/quran-11-13|11:13]] al-Tabari says: this Qur'an is enough as proof of the truth of what you brought them and evidence of the soundness of your prophethood, beyond all other signs, since signs are a proof of truthfulness for whoever is given them because the whole creation is unable to bring the like, and the whole creation is unable to bring the like of this Qur'an. If they say 'he invented it', that is, forged and falsely made it, then tell them to bring ten surahs like it, invented, that is, made up and forged, if what I brought you is an invention and not a miraculous sign like the other signs asked of me, such as the treasure you said should have been sent down on him or the angel you said should have come with him as a confirming warner. You are my people, speakers of my tongue, and I am a man from among you, and it is impossible that I could forge alone a hundred and fourteen surahs while all of you together cannot invent and forge ten like them, especially when you have the help in it of whomever of creation you wish. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 259}

'And call on whom you can besides God' means other than God, among the gods, to invent and forge it. If you cannot invent ten surahs like it, it is clear to you that you are liars in saying 'he invented it', and the truth of what I brought you, that it is from God, is proven to you; and you have no right to pick signs against your Lord when the proof has come to you of the truth of what you deny, of the same kind as you ask for. 'If you are truthful' attaches to 'bring ten surahs like it', and the sense is: say, bring ten surahs like it, invented, if you are truthful that Muhammad invented this Qur'an, and call on whom you can besides God for that, of gods and rivals. Report 18008, from Ibn Jurayj: 'or do they say he invented it', they have said it; 'say, then bring ten surahs like it, invented, and call your witnesses', he said they would testify that they are like it; the editor says 'and call your witnesses' is not in this verse but in [[passage/quran-2-23|2:23]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 260}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
