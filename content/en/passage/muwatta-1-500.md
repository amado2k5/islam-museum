## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, that he used to pray on the day of breaking the fast before the prayer in the mosque, in the chapter on permission to pray before and after the two festivals. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256}
