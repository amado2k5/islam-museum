## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "The Day the earth will split open for them hastened, that will be gathered for us, easy": He says, Glorified and Exalted, "and to us is their fate on the day the earth splits open." So today is from the connection of destiny. And His saying: "The earth will split open from them" means: the earth will crack from them. And His saying "hasra'" and the accusative 'hasaan' is in the accusative case of the letter 'ha' and 'mim' in his saying about them. The meaning: the day the earth will split open for them, and they will leave it quickly. He sufficed with the meaning of His words: "the day the earth will split open for them," based on what he mentioned. And His saying: "That will be gathered for us, easy," means: gathering them together in the position of reckoning, easy for us.
