## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46 to 47 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So when they brought them Our signs, they laughed at them": When Moses came to Pharaoh and filled him with Our proofs and evidence of the truth of his words in what he called them to in terms of the oneness of God and disassociation from the worship of gods, Pharaoh and his people laughed at what Moses brought them of signs and lessons, just as your people mocked what You brought them of signs and lessons. This is a consolation from God Almighty to His Prophet from what he was receiving from his people's polytheists and a warning from Him that his people would never be more than polytheists Like all other nations who followed their path in disbelief in God and denying His messengers, and his Prophet advised him to be patient with them by the ways of the resolute messengers, and he informed him that the outcome of their rebellion would be ruin and destruction, just like his way of those who rebelled against him before them, and his victory over them and elevating his authority, just as he did with Moses and his people who believed in him by showing them to Pharaoh and his full authority.
