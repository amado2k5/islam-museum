## Summary
Malik transmits from Alqama ibn Abi Alqama, from his mother, from Aisha, that it reached her that people of a household in her house were residents there, and had backgammon. She sent to them: if you do not remove it I will expel you from my house; and she disapproved of it for them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.548}
