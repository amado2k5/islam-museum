## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu Bakr ibn Nafi, from his father, that a niece of Safiyya bint Abi Ubayd gave birth at Muzdalifa, so she and Safiyya stayed behind until they came to Mina after the sun had set on the day of sacrifice; Abdullah ibn Umar ordered them to stone the pillar when they came, and saw no fault in them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.546}
