## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He it is Who created for you hearing, sight, and hearts. How little do you give thanks": And Allah who introduced for you, O deniers, the hearing with which you hear, the sights with which you see, and the hearts with which you understand. So how can the One who created this be unable to begin his habit after its absence and loss, when He is the One who creates all of that if He wills and annihilates it if He wills? "You give little thanks," meaning: You thank you, O deniers. God has informed of your giving of hearing, sight, and hearts a little.
