## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who were given knowledge said, 'Woe to you! The reward of Allah is better for those who believe and do righteous deeds, and none will meet it except the patient.'" And those who were given knowledge of Allah said to those who said, 'Oh, if only we had the like of what Qarun was given: Woe to you! Fear Allah and obey Him.' So the reward and recompense of Allah for those who believed in Him and His messengers, and acted upon what His messengers brought of good deeds in the Hereafter, is better than what Qarun was given of his adornment and wealth. And His saying: 'And none will meet it except the patient,' means: 'And he will not meet it': that is, it is not suitable for this word, which is: 'The reward of Allah is better for those who believe and do righteous deeds,' and the 'ha' and 'alif' are metaphors for the word.
