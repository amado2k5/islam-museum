## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "if you slip after the clear proofs have come to you, know that God is Almighty, Wise": al-Tabari means, if you miss the truth, stray from it and contradict Islam and its laws after My proofs and clear signs of My guidance have come to you and the path of Islam has become plain by the evidence that cut off your excuse, believers, then know that God has might that nothing prevents from taking vengeance on you, and is wise in what He does of punishing you after establishing the proof. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 259} A number of interpreters said the clear proofs are Muhammad and the Qur'an, which is close to what he said, but he holds his own reading better, because God argued against the rabbis of the People of the Book by what He committed to them in the Torah and the Gospel of the charge to follow him, besides the proofs binding on them through Muhammad and the Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 259} From al-Suddi: "if you slip" means if you go astray (4027); from Ibn 'Abbas: the slip is associating partners with God (4028). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 259} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 260} On "after the clear proofs have come to you": al-Suddi, after Muhammad has come to you (4029); Ibn Jurayj, Islam and the Qur'an (4030). From al-Rabi': Almighty in His vengeance, Wise in His command (4031). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 260}
