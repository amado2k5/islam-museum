## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 8 as a single unit. He interprets the words, “O mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?” as: O disbelieving mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous? Man is deceived by his enemy, who has power over him. As Qatadah said, “What has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Most Generous?” This enemy, Satan, deceived the son of Adam. And the words, “Who created you and proportioned you,” mean: He who created you, O mankind, and proportioned your creation, “and made you upright.” The reciters differed in their reading of this verse. The majority of reciters from Medina, Mecca, Syria, and Basra read it as “fa-addalaka” with a shaddah on the dal, while the majority of reciters from Kufa read it with a sukun on the dal. Those who read it with the shaddah seem to interpret the meaning as meaning that He made you upright, balanced, and well-proportioned. Those who read it with the sukun seem to interpret the meaning as meaning that He changed you and inclined you to whatever form He willed, whether to a beautiful form, an ugly form, or the form of one of His relatives. The passage also includes statements attributed to Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
