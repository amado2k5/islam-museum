## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So set your face toward the upright religion before a day comes without any turning from Allah, that Day they will be defeated": Direct your face, O Muhammad, toward the face to which your Lord has directed you, "for the upright religion" to obey your Lord, and the upright religion that does not deviate from the truth "before a day comes without avert from Allah." Allah says: Before the coming of a day of Allah, there will be no turning back, because Allah has decreed its coming, so it will inevitably come. "On that Day, they will be defeated." He says: The Day when the people will split apart, he says: the people will split into two groups, as in their saying: The sheep will split into two groups: one in Paradise and one in the Blaze. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
