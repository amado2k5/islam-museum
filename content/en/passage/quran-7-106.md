**Commentary on verse 106 (p. 14)**

Al-Tabari says Pharaoh answered him, "If you have brought a sign", that is, an argument and a mark attesting the truth of what he says, "then bring it, if you are of the truthful." The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "clear proof" and "sign". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 14}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
