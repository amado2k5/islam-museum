## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and fight them until there is no persecution and the religion is God's", al-Tabari says God tells His Prophet: fight the polytheists who fight you until there is no fitna, meaning until there is no shirk toward God and none is worshipped besides Him, the worship of idols, gods and rivals fades, and worship and obedience are to God alone. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 570} From Qatada, Mujahid, al-Suddi and Ibn 'Abbas: fitna is shirk, and fight until there is no shirk. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 570}
