## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, that Ata ibn Yasar told him from Abu Qatada about the wild donkey, like the hadith of Abu al-Nadr, except that in Zayd ibn Aslam's hadith the Messenger of God said: is there any of its meat with you? The editor notes that Abu Musab (1137), Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari (5490), Suwayd and others transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.471}
