**Commentary on verse 57 (p. 365)**

On [[passage/quran-11-57|11:57]] al-Tabari reports Hud's saying to his people: 'if you turn away' means if you turn your backs in refusal of what I call you to, God's oneness and giving up worship of idols, 'then I have conveyed to you' what I was sent to you with, and the Messenger has only to convey; 'my Lord will make a people other than you successors' means my Lord will destroy you and then replace you with another people who affirm His oneness and make worship pure for Him; 'you will not harm Him at all' means you cannot do Him any harm when He wills your destruction. It is also said that your destruction does not harm Him when He destroys you, you take nothing from Him, as it is the same to Him whether you are or not. 'My Lord is Guardian over all things' means over all His creation He is possessed of guarding and knowledge, and He is the one who guards me from your reaching me with harm. The editor says the right form would be 'if you turn away' and takes this to be a slip of Abu Ja'far. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 365}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
