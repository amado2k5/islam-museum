## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Amr ibn Hazm, from his father, that Abu al-Bada ibn Asim ibn Adi told him from his father that the Messenger of God allowed the herdsmen of camels to spend the nights away from Mina, stoning on the day of sacrifice, then stoning the next day, and for the two days after that, and then stoning on the day of departure. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.545}
