## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 19 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "If He wills, He will remove you and bring you a new creation": If He wills, He will destroy you, O people, your Lord, because He created you from what you need, and "and brings a new creation" means: He brings another creation who obey Him, obey Him, and refrain from what He forbids them, as in Qatadah's saying: "If He wills, He will remove you and bring a new creation": meaning He brings others among you.
