## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala, from Amr ibn Shuayb, from his father, from his grandfather, that the Prophet said: a rider is a devil, two riders are two devils, and three are a company. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.574}
