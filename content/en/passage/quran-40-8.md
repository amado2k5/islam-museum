## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Our Lord, admit them to the gardens of Eden which You have promised them, and those of their fathers, spouses, and descendants. Indeed, You are the Exalted in Might, the Wise." He, the Exalted, says, informing about the prayer of His angels to those who believe in Him in worship, saying: "O our Lord, admit them to the gardens of Eden," meaning: the gardens of residence which You promised them, meaning the one You promised the people of repentance to obey You, that You admit them to them. "And those who make peace among their fathers, their spouses, and their offspring," meaning: And admit with those who repented and followed Your way the Gardens of Eden by those who made peace with their fathers, spouses, and offspring. So they did what pleases you of righteous deeds in this world. It is mentioned that a man will enter Paradise with his parents, children, and wife, even if they did not perform his deeds by the grace of Allah's mercy upon him. As Saeed said: A man will enter Paradise and say: Where is my father, where is my mother, where is my son, where is my wife?
