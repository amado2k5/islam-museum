**Commentary on verse 56 (pp. 297-298)**

Al-Tabari says: these hypocrites swear to you by God, believers, falsely and vainly, out of fear of you, that they are of you in religion and community, and God calls them liars, saying "they are not of you", that is, they are not of your religion and community but people of doubt and hypocrisy; "but they are a people who are afraid", that is, they fear you, and so out of fear of you they say with their tongues "we are of you", to be safe among you and not be killed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 298}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
