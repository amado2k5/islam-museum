## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22-24 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase, “Indeed, this is your reward, and your striving has been appreciated”: It will be said to these righteous people then: This honor We have bestowed upon you is a reward for the good deeds you performed in this world. “And your striving has been appreciated” means: Your deeds in this world were appreciated; your Lord praised you for them and was pleased with them, so He rewarded you with the honor He bestowed upon you. Qatadah said regarding the phrase, “Indeed, this is your reward, and your striving has been appreciated”: He forgave them their sins and appreciated their good deeds. On the authority of Mu'ammar, on the authority of Qatadah, who said: Qatadah recited, "And your striving will be appreciated," and said: God has appreciated a small effort. And in the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
