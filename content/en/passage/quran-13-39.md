**Commentary on verse 39 (pp. 477-479)**

On "God erases what He wills and confirms, and with Him is the Mother of the Book", al-Tabari says: the interpreters differed over its interpretation. Some said: God erases what He wills of the affairs of His servants and changes it, except wretchedness and happiness, which are not changed. Report 20461, from Ibn Abbas, through Abu Kurayb, Bahr ibn Isa, Ibn Abi Layla, al-Minhal and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: "God erases what He wills and confirms, and with Him is the Mother of the Book", he said, God manages the affair of the servants and erases what He wills except wretchedness, happiness, life and death; in a note the editor discusses the chain of this report and thinks its correct form is "Abu Kurayb told us, Bakr told us, from Isa, from Ibn Abi Layla, from al-Minhal". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 477}

Report 20462, from Ibn Abbas, through Ibn Bashshar, Ibn Abi Layla, al-Minhal ibn Amr and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: "God erases what He wills and confirms, and with Him is the Mother of the Book", he said, everything except happiness and wretchedness, for those two have been finished with; the editor notes that the chain has a gap, since Ibn Abi Layla died in 148 and Ibn Bashshar was born in 167. Report 20463, from Ibn Abbas, through Ali ibn Sahl, Yazid, Ahmad, Abu Ahmad, Sufyan, Ibn Abi Layla, al-Minhal and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: except wretchedness, happiness, death and life. Report 20464, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abu Nu'aym al-Fadl ibn Dukayn, Qabisa and Sufyan: the same. Report 20465, from Ibn Abbas, through Amr ibn Ali, Waki' and Ibn Abi Layla: except life and death, wretchedness and happiness. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 478}

Report 20466, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Amr ibn Awn, Hushaym, Ibn Abi Layla, al-Minhal and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: God decrees the affair of the year in the Night of Power, except wretchedness, happiness, death and life. Report 20467, from Mujahid, through Amr ibn Ali, Abu Asim, Sufyan and Mansur: except life, death, happiness and wretchedness, for these two do not change. Reports 20468 and 20469, from Mujahid, through Umar, Abd al-Rahman, Mu'adh ibn Uqba and Mansur, and Ibn Bashshar, Abu Ahmad and Sufyan: the same. Report 20470, from Mujahid, through Abu Ahmad, Sufyan and Mansur: I asked Mujahid about the transmitted supplication in which a servant asks his Lord to confirm him if He has written him happy and to erase him if He has written him wretched; he said, wretchedness and happiness have been finished with; the editor refers to the report of Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud on the supplication. Report 20471, from Mujahid, through Ahmad, Abu Ahmad, Sufyan and Mansur, and Sa'id ibn Sulayman and Sharik: "God erases what He wills and confirms", he said, God sends down everything of the year on the Night of Power and erases what He wills of terms, provisions and decrees, except wretchedness and happiness, which are fixed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 479}

We stop here at the foot of p. 479, in the middle of the commentary on verse 39 (after report 20471, on erasing and confirming); the next batch begins at p. 480 (marker 488). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 479}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
