## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Have they not seen the birds being subjected in the sky to the sky, which only Allah holds them? Indeed, in that are signs for a people who believe": Allah says to these polytheists: Have you not seen, O polytheists of Allah, to the birds being subjected in the sky of the sky, meaning in the air of the sky between it and the earth, as Ibrahim ibn Imran Al-Ansari said: Woe to its mother from the air of the sky, seeking nothing like it on earth, meaning: In the air of the sky. "No one can hold them except by Allah" means that they do not fly in the air except by Allah and by His subjugation of them by that, and if He had taken them away, He would not have been able to rise high. And His saying: "Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe," he says: In Allah's subjugation of birds and enabling them to fly in the sky are signs and indications that there is no god but Allah alone without partner, and that He observed idols and idols in divinity. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibrahim.
