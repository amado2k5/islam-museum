## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Say, ‘Have you considered: if your water were to become sunken [into the earth], then who could bring you flowing water?’” is explained as follows: “Say,” O Muhammad, to these polytheists, “Have you considered,” O people who associate partners with God, “if your water were to become sunken [into the earth],” meaning: so deep that buckets could not reach it, “then who could bring you flowing water?” meaning: who could bring you flowing water, meaning: water that is visible to the eyes. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
