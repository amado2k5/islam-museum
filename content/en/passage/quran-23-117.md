## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And whoever calls upon Allah another god for whom he has no proof, then his reckoning is only with his Lord. Indeed, the disbelievers will not succeed": Whoever calls upon the deity whose worship is not suitable except for Him another deity, he has no proof for what he says or does of that, nor any proof. Also: from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "He has no proof for it," he said: Evidence. And he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid: "He has no proof of it," he said: Proof. From Anbasa, from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman, from al-Qasim ibn Abi Bazzah, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: 'He has no proof for it,' he said: There is no proof. And His saying: 'His reckoning is only with his Lord,' it means: the reckoning of his evil deed is with his Lord, and He will reward him if he comes upon it. 'Indeed, the disbelievers will not succeed.' He means: the people of disbelief in Allah will not succeed with Him, nor will they realize eternity and permanence in bliss. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
