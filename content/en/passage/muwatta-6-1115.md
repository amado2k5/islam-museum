## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to cover his offering animals with brocade, striped cloth and fine garments, then send them to the Kaaba, where he clothed it with them. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1210), Suwayd (525) and al-Bayhaqi transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.510}
