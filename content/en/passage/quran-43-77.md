## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 77 to 78 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And they called out to Malik, that your Lord may end us," he said, "Indeed, you will remain." He called these criminals after Allah admitted them to Hell, and He inflicted upon them the affliction that befell them. Malik, the keeper of Hell, said: "O Malik, let your Lord destroy us." He said: "Why did your Lord kill us, and finish our death?" He mentioned that Malik does not respond to them when they are told to him, and leaves them a thousand years later, then answers them, saying: "Indeed, you will remain." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
