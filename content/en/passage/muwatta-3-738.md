## Summary
Malik's saying on God's words in Surah al-An'am [[passage/quran-6-141|al-An'am 141]] about giving the due of the crop on its harvest day: he said it is zakat, God knows best, and he had heard some people say so. The editor notes that 'God knows best' is missing in copy M, but is in the other copies and in Abu Musab's transmission from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.368}
