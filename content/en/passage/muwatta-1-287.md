## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on the imam who alights in a village on Friday while on a journey: if the imam, while travelling, alights in a village where the Friday prayer is obligatory, preaches and leads them in it, then the people of that village and others join him in the Friday prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.163}
