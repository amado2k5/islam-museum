## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Be patient with what they say, and remember Our servant David, the possessor of the hands; indeed, He is Ever-Returning." He explains: Be patient, O Muhammad, with what your people say to you about what you dislike what they say. Indeed, We tested you with hardships, as were all Our messengers before you. Then they made you elevated, elevated, and victorious over those who denied you and made Your tradition difficult regarding the messengers We sent to Our servants before you, including Our servant Ayyub and Dawud son of Isha. So mention the One of the Hands, and by His saying: "The One of the Hands," he means the one with great strength and oppression in the presence of God and patience in obeying Him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
