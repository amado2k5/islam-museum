## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, that Umar ibn al-Khattab decided concerning a woman, when a man married her, that once the curtains were let down the dowry was due. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.33}
