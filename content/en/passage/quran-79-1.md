## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-9 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By those who draw out violently”: Our Lord, may His glory be exalted, swears by those who draw out souls. Scholars of interpretation differed regarding this phrase: What are they? And what do they draw out? Some said: They are the angels who draw out the souls of the children of Adam, and what is drawn out are the souls of the humans. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-A'mash, Sufyan, and Ata.
