## Summary
Verse 104 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 104 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari explains "when it is said to them, come to what God has sent down and to the messenger, they say, enough for us is what we found our fathers following; even if their fathers knew nothing and were not guided?": God says that when it is said to those who slit the bahiras and let the sa'ibas loose, who do not understand that by attributing the forbidding of these to God they invent lies about Him, come to the revelation of God and the verses of His book and to His messenger, so that the falsehood of what you say in attributing to God your forbidding of these things becomes plain to you, they answer whoever calls them to this by saying: enough for us is what we found our fathers before us doing, and they say, we follow them and they are our leaders and guides, we are content with what we took from them and satisfied with what they held of forbidding and permitting. God says to His Prophet: even if the fathers of those who say this knew nothing? That is, they did not know that what they attributed to God of forbidding the bahira, sa'iba, wasila and ham is a lie and invention against God with no truth in it, because they were followers of the inventors who began the forbidding, and in what they practised of it they were not upright or correct but astray and in error. The reports on verse 104 end here, on p. 137. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 137}
