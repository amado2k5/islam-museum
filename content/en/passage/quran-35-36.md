## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 36 to 37 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieve" will have the Fire of Hell; it will not be made up for them until they die, nor will its punishment be lightened for them. Thus We recompense every disbeliever: "And those who disbelieve" in Allah and His Messenger, "for them is the Fire of Hell," meaning: for them is the Fire of Hell, abiding therein, with no share in Paradise nor its bliss, as narrated from Qatadah: "For them is the Fire of Hell, which will not be made for them" by death, and they will die, because if they died, they would find rest. "And He does not lighten for them its punishment" means: He does not lighten their punishment from the fire of Hell by killing them, so that eases their burden, as in Qatadah from Abu al-Sawda, who said: The poor people of the Fire do not die; if they died, they would rest. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
