## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Sad ibn Zurara, that it reached him that Hafsa, wife of the Prophet, killed a slave-girl of hers whom she had bewitched, and she had made her conditionally free; she ordered her put to death, and she was killed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.444}
