**Commentary on verse 121 (p. 544)**

On [[passage/quran-11-121|11:121]] God says to His Prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, to those who do not believe you and do not acknowledge God's oneness, 'act according to your position', that is, in your leisure and ability, what you are doing, 'we are acting' as we are doing, of the deeds God commanded us. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 544}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
