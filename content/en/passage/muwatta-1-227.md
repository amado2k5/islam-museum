## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Ruman that Nafi' ibn Jubayr ibn Mut'im recited behind the imam in prayers in which the imam does not recite aloud. Malik says that this is the most beloved of what he has heard on the question, in the chapter on reciting behind the imam in silent prayers. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.138}
