## Summary
Malik says: the difference between irrigation partnership in palms and in bare land is that the owner of the palms cannot sell their fruit until it shows ripeness, while the owner of bare land rents it when it is bare and has nothing in it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.244}
