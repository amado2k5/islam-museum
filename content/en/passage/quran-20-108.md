## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "On that Day, they will follow the caller without any deviation, and voices will humble themselves to the Most Merciful, so they will hear nothing but a whisper": On that day, people will follow the voice of the caller to God who calls them to the station of the Resurrection, and he will gather them to it. "There is no deviation from it," meaning: they will not deviate from it or deviate, but they will hasten to it. It is said: there is no deviation from it, and the meaning is: they will not deviate from it, because the meaning of the speech is what we mentioned: that they will neither turn away from him nor from him. But they believe in Him and come to Him, as it is said in speech: So-and-so called me a call from which I have no deviation: meaning I do not deviate from it. And His saying, "And the voices humbled themselves before the Most Merciful," the Exalted says: "And the voices of creation remained silent for the Most Merciful," describing the voices as humility. The meaning is for their people that they all submitted to their Lord, so you do not hear any of them speak logically except for those whom the Most Merciful permits, as he said: Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "And the voices humbled themselves before the Most Merciful," meaning: they remained calm. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, Ikrimah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and others.
