## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And do not be [t]he [saying], 'And do not be [[it] that you have broken its yarn after the power of its reversal, taking your oaths as a means of entry among yourselves, that it be a nation superior to another nation. Allah tests you by it, and that He may make clear to you on the Day of Resurrection that over which you used to differ.': Allah Almighty says, forbidding His servants from breaking oaths after they have been affirmed, commanding the fulfillment of covenants, and representing the annulment of that by nullifying their yarn after its conclusion and breaking it after its firmness: And do not be, O people, in breaking your oaths after they have been confirmed and God has given you to fulfill those covenants and covenants "like those whose spinning was broken after a strong one," meaning after a conclusion. Some Arabs used to say: 'Al-quwwa' means that he does not flirt with a single power and does not repeat. It is said that the one who did this was a foolish woman known in Mecca. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
