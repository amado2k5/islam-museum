## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha al-Ansari, from Anas ibn Malik, that the Messenger of God said: a good dream from a righteous man is one part of forty-six parts of prophethood. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.545}
