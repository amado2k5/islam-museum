## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God forbade a man to marry a woman together with her paternal aunt, or together with her maternal aunt. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.38}
