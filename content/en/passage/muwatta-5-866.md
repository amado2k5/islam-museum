## Summary
The first hadith in the Book of Seclusion, in the chapter on mentioning seclusion. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, from Aisha, wife of the Prophet, that she said: when the Messenger of God was in seclusion he would bring his head close to me and I would comb it, and he would not enter the house except for a human need. The editor notes that Abu Musab (860), Ishaq al-Tabba, Suwayd, Amir ibn Salih and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Nisaburi (in Muslim) transmitted it, and refers to his commentary on al-Tirmidhi (2/157). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.419}
