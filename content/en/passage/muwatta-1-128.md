## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, from Zaynab bint Abi Salama, from Umm Salama, the Prophet's wife, in the chapter on a woman bathing when she sees in a dream what a man sees: Umm Sulaym, the wife of Abu Talha al-Ansari, came to the Prophet and said that God does not shy from the truth, and asked whether a woman must bathe if she has a wet dream. He said yes, if she sees the water. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Ibn Hibban, Ibn Khuzayma, al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi, and notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the Muwatta transmitters gave it this chain except al-Qa'nabi, who sent it from Hisham from his father; Ibn Shihab sometimes related it from Urwa as a mursal and sometimes from Aisha. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.96} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.97}
