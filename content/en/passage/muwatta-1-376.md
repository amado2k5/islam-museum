## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told that Jabir ibn Abdullah reported that the Prophet said whoever does not find two garments should pray in one garment wrapped around him, and if the garment is short, wrap it as a waist-wrap. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik, and that Jabir prayed in a single garment because he saw the Prophet do it, as in his hadith in Muslim, Ahmad and Ibn Khuzayma. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.203} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.204}
