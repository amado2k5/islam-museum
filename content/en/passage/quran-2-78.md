## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and among them are unlettered ones", al-Tabari says those meant are among the Jews, reported from Abu al-'Aliya, al-Rabi' and Mujahid; "ummiyyun" are those who neither write nor read. He cites the saying "We are an unlettered community, neither writing nor reckoning", which the editor calls a sound report. He reports the same from Ibrahim and Ibn Zayd, and from Ibn Abbas through al-Dahhak a differing view, that they are people who believed no messenger and no scripture God sent and wrote a book with their own hands. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 257} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 258}
