## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that he used to cup while fasting and not break his fast, and he said: I never saw him cup except while fasting. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shaybani (357) transmitted it from Malik, and that al-Shaybani transmitted it with the wording: I never saw my father cup except while fasting. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.401}
