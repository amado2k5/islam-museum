## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ruled that the blood-money of a Jew or a Christian, when one of them is killed, is like half the blood-money of a free Muslim. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.434}
