## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We certainly established them in that which We had established you, and We made for them hearing, sight, and hearts, but what availed them of their hearing, their sight, and their hearts was of no avail, for they used to deny the signs of Allah, and that which they used to mock has encompassed them": Allah Almighty says to the disbelievers of Quraysh: "And We certainly empowered, O people, those whom We destroyed by their disbelief in what We did not enable you from this world, and We gave them of it what We did not give you from among them of abundant wealth, the splendour of bodies, and the hardness of bodies." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
