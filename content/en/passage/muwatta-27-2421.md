## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the matter among us regarding one who steals goods of people that are placed in the markets, safely stored, which their owners have secured in their containers and gathered together: if he steals from the protected place an item whose value reaches what requires cutting, he owes the cutting, whether the owner of the goods was at his goods or not, by night or by day. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.400}
