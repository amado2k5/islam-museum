## Summary
Malik says of a man who performs the mutual curse with his wife, then withdraws and retracts his accusation after an oath or two oaths, before he has completed the fifth: if he withdraws before completing the mutual curse, he is given the prescribed punishment and they are not separated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.80}
