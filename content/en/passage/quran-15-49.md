## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Inform My servants that I am the Forgiving, the Merciful." Tell My servants, O Muhammad, that I am the One who covers their sins when they repent and repent by leaving their disgrace and punishment for it. The Most Merciful to them is that I will not punish them after their repentance. It is narrated from Qatadah that the Prophet said: If a servant knew the extent of Allah's forgiveness, he would not fear what is forbidden, and if he knew the extent of His punishment, he would have humbled himself. It is narrated from a man among the Prophet's companions that the Prophet went out to them through the Banu Shaybah gate, then returned to them and said: When I left, Gabriel came and said: O Muhammad, Allah says My servants have not despaired. Inform My servants that I am the Forgiving, the Merciful, and that My punishment is the painful punishment.
