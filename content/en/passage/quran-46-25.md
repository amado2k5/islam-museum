## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They destroy everything by the command of their Lord, so they became visible only their dwellings. Thus We recompense the criminal people": and His saying, "They destroy everything by the command of their Lord": Allah says: You destroy everything, throwing some of it upon others and destroying it, as Jarir said: And you had like the firstborn of Thamud, when it roared at the back and destroyed them utterly, meaning by saying: 'They destroyed them': some of them fell upon others and perished. What he meant by His saying: "Everything was destroyed by the command of its Lord" was sent with its destruction, because it did not destroy Hud and those who believed in him. From Zaidah, from Al-A'mash, from Al-Minhal, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn Abbas, who said: God sent upon 'Ad from the wind only the measure of this seal, so he removed his ring. And His saying: "So they woke up and saw nothing except their dwellings," he says: The people of Hud woke up having perished and perished, and nothing was seen in their land except their dwellings that they used to live in. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Al-A'mash.
