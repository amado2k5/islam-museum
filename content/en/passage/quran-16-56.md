## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they assign, for what they do not know, a share of what We have provided them, that you may be questioned about what you used to fabricate": These polytheists are idol worshippers, and because they do not know of any harm or benefit, He makes a 'share' (share) meaning a share and recompense from what We have provided them of wealth, as a partnership from them in what they know He created them, and He benefits and harms them and not anything else. Like the one who said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "And they assign for what they do not know a share of what We have provided them," he said: They know that Allah created them, harms them, and benefits them, then they assign to what they do not know harms them or benefits them a share of what We have provided them. And from Qatadah, His saying: "And they assign for what they do not know a share of what We have provided them," and they are the polytheists of the Arabs; they have given their idols a share of what We have provided them, and a portion of their wealth they assign to their idols. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
