## Summary
Malik says: whoever hires a particular slave, or rents a particular mount, until a term, and takes possession of the slave or the mount until that term, has done what is not proper, for he has neither taken possession of what he rented or hired, nor advanced a debt that makes him a guarantor for its owner until he receives it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.155}
