## Summary
Malik transmits from Wahb ibn Kaysan, from Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Ata, that he was sitting with Abdullah ibn Abbas when a man from Yemen came in and said: peace be upon you, and God's mercy and blessings, and then added something more. Ibn Abbas, whose sight had gone, said: who is this? He was told: this is the Yemeni who visits you. Ibn Abbas said: peace has reached its end in blessing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.549}
