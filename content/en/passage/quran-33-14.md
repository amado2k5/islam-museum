## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 13–14. Al-Tabari mentions that a group of hypocrites said, 'O people of Yathrib, there is no place for you to stay, so go back.' He explains the reading of 'Maqam' with a fatha, meaning there is no place for you to stand there, and that a group of them sought the Prophet's permission under the pretext that their houses are 'awrah' and that they are not 'awrah, but they only want to flee.
