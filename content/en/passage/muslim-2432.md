## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the virtues of Khadija, in Sahih Muslim: Gabriel conveyed greetings to Khadija from her Lord and gave her good news of a house in Paradise with no clamour or toil. It is the most specific text on her virtue in the collections. {cite:sahih-muslim}
