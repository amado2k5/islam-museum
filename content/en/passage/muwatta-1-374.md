## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Jabir ibn Abdullah used to pray in a single garment, in the chapter on permission to pray in a single garment. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.203}
