## Summary
Verse 98 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 98 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Address to the People of the Book (p. 52).** Al-Tabari says God is addressing the Jews of the Children of Israel and others who profess a religion based on books God sent down, who disbelieved in Muhammad and denied his prophethood, asking why they reject the proofs of God that He gave Muhammad in their books and elsewhere, which are established against them for his truthfulness, prophethood and proof, while they know his truthfulness. God thus reports of them that they deliberately disbelieve in God and His messenger with knowledge and recognition. The editor says he is in doubt about this wording of al-Tabari, though it is nearly sound, and that al-Tabari apparently slipped, leaving the rest of the verse without interpretation and explaining in its place a phrase that is not part of this verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 52}

**First reports (7522-7523, p. 52).** Al-Tabari relates from al-Suddi that the signs of God are Muhammad (7522), and from al-Hasan that the addressees are the Jews and the Christians (7523); the report continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 52}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 52 in the commentary on verse 98 and continue from p. 53. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 52}
