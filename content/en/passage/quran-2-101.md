## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and when a messenger came to them from God confirming what they had, a party of those given the Book threw God's Book behind their backs as if they did not know", al-Tabari says the messenger is Muhammad (from al-Suddi), who confirms the Torah as the Torah confirms that he is a prophet sent; "a party threw" means they denied and rejected him after having acknowledged him, from envy and transgression. Those meant are the scholars of the Jews given knowledge of the Torah, and "God's Book" is the Torah. "Behind their backs" is a figure used of anyone who rejects a matter, meaning he turned away from it. He reports from al-Suddi that when they confronted him with the Torah and the Torah and the Qur'an agreed, they threw the Torah aside and took up the book of Asaf and the magic of Harut and Marut. "As if they did not know" means as if they did not know what the Torah says of following Muhammad, a report that they denied the truth knowingly; Qatada says they knew but corrupted their knowledge, denied and concealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 403} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 404}
