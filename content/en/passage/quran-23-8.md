## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 10 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "And those who are faithful to their trusts and their covenant": "And those who are to their trusts" which they entrusted with and their covenant, which are their contracts contracted with people "guardians," meaning they are guardians who do not neglect, but they fulfill all of that. Reciters differed in their reading; most reciters of the regions read it except Ibn Kathir: "and those who are to their trusts" in the plural. Ibn Kathir read it as "for their trusts" in the singular. The correct reading in this regard for us is: 'for their trusts,' due to the consensus of the argument among the reciters on them. And his saying: He quotes in the Maqta' from Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
