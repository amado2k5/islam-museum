## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Qabisa ibn Dhu'ayb, that Umar ibn al-Khattab assigned the grandfather the share that people assign him today. The editor notes that this report is in the Muwatta of Abu Mus'ab. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.12}
