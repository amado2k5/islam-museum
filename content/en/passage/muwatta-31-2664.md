## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, that he said: Anas ibn Malik said: I saw Umar ibn al-Khattab, who was then Commander of the Faithful, with a patch of three layers between his shoulders, one layered on top of another. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.504}
