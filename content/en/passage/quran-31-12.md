## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave Luqman wisdom: 'Be grateful to Allah. And whoever gives thanks is only for [the benefit of] his own soul. And whoever disbelieves, then indeed, Allah is Free of need and Praiseworthy.": And indeed We gave Luqman jurisprudence in religion, reason, and precision in speech. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
