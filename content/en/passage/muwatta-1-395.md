## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' from Ibn Umar, that he used to travel to Khaybar and shorten the prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.211}
