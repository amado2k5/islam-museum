## Summary
The famous hadith of Ibn Umar in the chapter "Islam is built on five" in the Book of Faith: the founding instruction that made the testimony, prayer, almsgiving, pilgrimage and fasting the five pillars of religion. Muslim's other version places "that God is worshipped and what is beneath that is disbelieved" first. {cite:sahih-muslim}
