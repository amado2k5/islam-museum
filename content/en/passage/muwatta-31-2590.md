## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha al-Ansari, from Anas ibn Malik, that the Prophet said: O God, bless them in their measure, and bless them in their sa' and their mudd, meaning the people of Medina. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.461}
