## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "woe to every sinful sinner who is a liar": the flowing valley of the pus of the people of Hell, for every liar who has sins in his Lord, slanders against him, "He hears the verses of Allah recited to him," meaning: he hears the verses of the Book of God recited to him, "then insists" on his disbelief and sin, so he remains upon him without repenting, and no one turns back from him, "arrogant" before his Lord, to obey His command and prohibition "as if he did not hear them," meaning: as if he did not hear the verses of God recited to him due to his persistence in his disbelief, "so give him glad tidings of a painful punishment." He says: So give glad tidings, O Muhammad, this wicked liar whose attribute is a punishment from God for him. "Aleem": meaning one who will suffer in the fire of Hell on the Day of Resurrection.
