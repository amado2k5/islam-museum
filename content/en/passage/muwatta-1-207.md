## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on recitation in the sunset and night prayers. Yahya transmits from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Muhammad ibn Jubayr ibn Mut'im from his father Jubayr ibn Mut'im: he heard the Prophet recite the Surat al-Tur in the sunset prayer. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Khuzayma and Ahmad, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.128}
