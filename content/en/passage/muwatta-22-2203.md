## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar inherited the house of Hafsa bint Umar. He said: Hafsa had let the daughter of Zayd ibn al-Khattab live in it as long as she lived. When Zayd ibn al-Khattab's daughter died, Abdullah ibn Umar took possession of the dwelling, believing it was his. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.303}
