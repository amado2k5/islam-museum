## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Isma'il ibn Abi Hakim, that a Christian freedman of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz died, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz ordered his property to be placed in the public treasury. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.23}
