## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Evil is it for them: Did you warn them or not, they do not believe": And the same, O Muhammad, regarding those who have the right to say, that is, whether you have warned them or neglected warning, they do not believe, because Allah has decreed upon them so. And His saying: "You only warn those who follow the Reminder," the Exalted says: Your warning is only beneficial, O Muhammad, to those who believe in the Qur'an and follow the rulings of Allah in it. "And fear the Most Merciful" means: fear Allah when it is hidden from the sight of those who observe, not the hypocrite who belittles the religion of Allah when he abandons it and shows faith in public, nor the polytheist whose heart Allah has sealed. And His saying: "So give him glad tidings of forgiveness," he means: So give glad tidings, O Muhammad, this one who follows the Reminder and fears the Most Merciful the unseen with forgiveness from Allah for his sins. "And a generous reward," meaning: and a generous reward from him in the Hereafter, which is to grant him that Paradise for his deeds. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
