## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And you see the sun, when it rises, it looks away from their cave on the right, and when it sets, it lends to them the one on the left, while they are in a gap in it. That is among the signs of Allah. Whoever Allah guides, then He is the guided. And whoever He leads astray, you will not find for him a protector or guide." Allah says: "And you see the sun," O Muhammad, "When it rises, it will look away from their cave on the right." He means by saying: 'tazawar': to straighten and bend, from 'zor': which is crookedness and inclination. It is said: in this land, 'zor' means if there is a crookedness, and in so-and-so from so-and-so 'azurar' means he turned away from him. From this is the saying of Bishr ibn Abi Khazim: 'The water of palm trees leads the way, and in it from the two buildings of Azurar,' meaning turning away and rejection. Reciters have differed in reading this; the general reciters of Medina, Mecca, and Basra read it as 'tazawar' with a stressed zā', meaning: 'tazawār' with two tā's, then merged one of the ta'ā into the zay, as it was said: 'tazāwār' (to show their arrogance). In the clip, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Sufyan.
