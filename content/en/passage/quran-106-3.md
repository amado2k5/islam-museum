## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 6 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "for the familiarity of Quraysh": the reciters differed in pronunciation: "For the familiarity of Quraysh, their familiarity," so most reciters of the lands read it as a 'ya' after a hamza for 'Ilafa' and 'Ilafuham,' except for Abu Ja'far, who agreed with others in saying 'for Ilafa,' so he pronounced it with a 'ya' after a hamza, and differed regarding the phrase 'Ilafuham.' It was narrated that he used to read it as 'ilfham' as a verbal noun derived from 'alif' (to alif), without a ya. Some narrated from him that he read it as 'ilahum' without a ya, the kasura-e-alif. The correct reading in this regard for me: whoever reads it is: 'For the familiarity of Quraysh is their familiarity' with the affirmation of the ya' after the hamza, whoever familiarizes something with it is familiar with it, due to the consensus of the scholars on it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
