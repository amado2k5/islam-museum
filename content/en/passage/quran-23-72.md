## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 72 to 73 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or do you ask them for a khara, and the expiation from your Lord is better, and He is the best of providers": Or do you ask these polytheists, O Muhammad, from your people for a khara, meaning a reward for what you brought them from Allah of advice and truth? "So the expulsion from your Lord is better": so the reward from your Lord for fulfilling His command and seeking His pleasure is better for you. He did not ask them for what He brought them from Allah as a reward, but He said to them as Allah told him and commanded him to say to them: "Say, I do not ask you for any reward for it except affection for near relatives." The meaning of the statement is: Or do you ask them for a reward for what you brought them? So they turn back on their heels when you recite it to them, arrogantly in the sanctuary. The outcome of your Lord's reward is better. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Al-Hasan.
