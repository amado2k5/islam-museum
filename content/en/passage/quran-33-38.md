## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "There was no hardship upon the Prophet in what Allah has ordained for him in what Allah has prescribed for him the Sunnah of Allah concerning those who passed away before, and Allah's command is a decreed decree": There was no hardship for the Prophet in what Allah made lawful for him in marrying the wife of the one he adopted after separating from her, as Qatadah narrated: "There was no hardship for the Prophet in what Allah has decreed for him": meaning Allah made lawful for him. And His saying: "The way of Allah concerning those who passed away before" means: Allah Almighty would not have sinned His Prophet in what He permitted for him, like His action with those before him among the messengers who passed before him, in that He did not sin them for what He permitted them; His Prophet would not have feared people in what He commanded or permitted for him. His phrase "Allah's way" is in the accusative to mean: a right from Allah, as if he said: We did that as a Sunnah from us. And His saying: "And the command of Allah was a decree decreed" means: and Allah's command was a decree. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
