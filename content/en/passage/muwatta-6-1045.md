## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that it reached him from Aisha, wife of the Prophet, that she used to say: nothing releases one in ihram except the House. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1163) and Suwayd (566) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.485}
