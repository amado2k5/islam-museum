## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed in that is a sign, and most of them were believers": Indeed, in Our growth on earth from every noble spouse is a sign. He says: This is a sign for those polytheists who deny resurrection, of its reality, and that the power with which God caused that plant to grow on earth after its harvest will not prevent Him from spreading the dead after their death, alive from their graves. And His saying: "And most of them were not believers," means: And most of those who denied the resurrection, who denied your prophethood, O Muhammad, believed in what you brought them from Allah of remembrance. He, Glorified and Exalted, says: And it has been previously mentioned in my work that they do not believe, so most of them do not believe in you because of the predecessors among them.
