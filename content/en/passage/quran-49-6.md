## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, if a wicked person comes to you with news, investigate it, lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful": O you who have believed Allah and His Messenger, "If a wicked person comes to you with news" from a people, "then investigate." Reciters differed in the recitation of the phrase "so clarify," so the majority of the reciters of Medina read it as "فتتُت" with the letter 'ث', and it was mentioned that it is in Abdullah's Mushaf with a dotted letter with a 'ث'. Some reciters read this and clarified with the 'b', meaning: 'Wait until you know its authenticity, do not rush to accept it, as well as the meaning of 'so be firm.' The correct view is that they are well-known readings with similar meanings; whichever one the reader reads is correct. He mentioned that this verse was revealed regarding Al-Walid ibn Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt. In the section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
