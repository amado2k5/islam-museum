## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah who subjected for you the sea so that the ships sail therein by His command and that you may seek of His bounty, and that you may be grateful": Allah, O people, to whom deity is deserved, who has bestowed upon you these blessings, which He has explained to you in these verses, namely, "He subjected the sea for you to sail through it by His command" for your livelihood and management in the lands to seek His bounty therein, and to thank your Lord for subjecting that to you, so you may worship Him and obey Him in what He commands you and forbids you from Him.
