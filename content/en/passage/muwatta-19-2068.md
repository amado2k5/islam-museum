## Summary
Malik says: the profit-sharer is likewise in this position, not proper. If an excess enters into irrigation partnership or profit-sharing, it becomes hire. What enters hire is not proper, and hire should not be made for an uncertain matter, which may or may not happen, or be small or large. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.245}
