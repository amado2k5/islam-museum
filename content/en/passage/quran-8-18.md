**Commentary on verse 18 (pp. 449-450)**

Al-Tabari says, on "that, and God weakens the plot of the unbelievers" [[passage/quran-8-18|8:18]]: by "that" he means this deed, the killing of the polytheists, the throwing at them until they were routed and the trying of the believers with a fair trial by victory over them and enabling them to kill and capture them: We did what We did; "and God weakens the plot of the unbelievers" meaning, know that God besides that weakens the unbelievers' plotting, that is their scheming, until they are humbled and submit to the truth or are destroyed. As to the opening of "anna", the forms are those in [[passage/quran-8-14|8:14]], which he explained there. The reciters differed over the reading of "muhin": most reciters of Medina and some Meccans and Basrans read it with a doubled letter, from "I weakened the thing", and most Kufan reciters read it from "I made it weak". Abu Ja'far said: the doubled form pleases me more, because God was undoing what the polytheists were contriving against the Messenger of God and his companions, knot after knot and thing after thing, though the other is a sound form. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "weakness" and "plot". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 449} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 450}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
