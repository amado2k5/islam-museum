## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah does not forbid you from those who have not fought you because of religion and have not expelled you from your homes from being kind to them and being just towards them. Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly": "Allah does not forbid you from those who have not fought you because of religion" from the people of Mecca, "and did not expel you from your homes, that you should be kind to them and be just towards them." He means: be just in their way by your kindness to them and your kindness to them. The interpreters differed regarding those who referred to this verse; some said: it meant those who believed in Mecca and did not migrate, so Allah permitted the believers to be dutiful to them and to be kind to them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
