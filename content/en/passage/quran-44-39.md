## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 39 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "We did not create them except in truth, but most of them do not know": We did not create the heavens and the earth except by the truth, by which management is not right. What Allah means by this is to warn about the validity of resurrection and recompense. He says: We did not create creation in vain by speaking to them and giving them life as We willed, then annihilating them without testing them by obedience, command, and prohibition, nor by recompensing the obedient for obedience and the disobedient for disobedience. Rather, We created that to test whom We willed to test those of Our creation with what We willed of testing Him in command and prohibition: "We will surely recompense those who have done evil for what they have done, and We will reward those who do good with good." "But most of them do not know." Allah says: But most of these polytheists do not know that God created that for them; they do not fear punishment for what they bring from God's wrath, nor do they hope for good if they do
