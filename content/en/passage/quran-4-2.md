## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 2 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The start of the commentary on verse 2 (p. 524).** Abu Ja'far al-Tabari says the address is to the guardians of orphans, to whom He says: give the orphans their wealth, guardians, once they reach puberty and sound judgement is perceived in them, and there the sentence breaks off, continuing on p. 525. Two notes by the editor: the manuscript and printed edition omit the second word for orphans, which the context requires, so he supplies it in brackets, the copyist having been misled by the repetition; and the second refers to the explanation of the verb of giving in the language indexes and of the word for orphans earlier (3: 292). These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 524 in the commentary on verse 2 of sura 4 and continue from p. 525. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 524}
