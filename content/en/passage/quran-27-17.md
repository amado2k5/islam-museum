## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And gathered for Solomon his soldiers from the jinn, humans, and birds, and they will be distributed": He gathered for Solomon his soldiers from jinn, humans, and birds on a march for them, so they distributed. The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase "they are distributed," with some saying it means they confine the first of them to the last until they gather. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
