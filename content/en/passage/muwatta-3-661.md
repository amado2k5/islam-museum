## Summary
A saying of Malik regarding a man who has one hundred and sixty full-weight dirhams, when the exchange in his town is eight dirhams to a dinar: zakat is not due on them; it is due only on twenty dinars in coin or two hundred dirhams. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.337}
