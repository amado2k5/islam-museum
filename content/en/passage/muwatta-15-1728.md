## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that Abdullah ibn Abbas and Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf disagreed about a woman who gave birth a few nights after her husband's death. Abu Salama said she is free once she gives birth to what is in her womb; Ibn Abbas said the later of the two terms. Abu Hurayra came and said: I am with my nephew, meaning Abu Salama. They sent Kurayb, a client of Abdullah ibn Abbas, to Umm Salama, wife of the Prophet, to ask her. He came back and told them she said Subayah al-Aslamiyya gave birth a few nights after her husband's death, and she told the Messenger, who said: you are free, marry whom you wish. Malik said this is the matter the scholars here have always followed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.105}
