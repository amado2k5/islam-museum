## Summary
A saying of Malik: if one kind among these reaches five wasqs, it carries zakat; if not, there is no zakat on it. The explanation: a man harvests five wasqs of dates even though their names and colours differ; these are gathered together and zakat is taken from them. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.369}
