**Commentary on verse 125 (p. 578)**

Al-Tabari says: as for those with sickness in their hearts, hypocrisy and doubt about God's religion, the surah that came down added defilement to their defilement, because they doubted that it was from God and neither believed nor affirmed it; this was an added doubt about a revelation they were bound to believe, and they were rather suspicious of it, an added stench in their deeds to the like they had already shown of stench and hypocrisy. "They died while unbelievers" means these hypocrites perished disbelieving in God and His signs. The editor refers to earlier discussion of sickness and defilement. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 578}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
