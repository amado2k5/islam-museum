## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 40 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Nay, by the moon,” meaning that God Almighty, may He be exalted, says, “Nay,” meaning that the statement is not as those who claim that the keepers of Hell are sufficient for their polytheistic companions to repel them from it. Then our Lord, may He be exalted, swears, saying, “By the moon and the night when it departs,” meaning: by the night when it has passed and gone. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
