## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 18 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Is He who creates like one who does not create? Will you not then remember?" He says: God Almighty says to the worshippers of idols and idols: "Is He who creates these wondrous creatures which We have enumerated for you and bestows upon you these great blessings, like the one who creates nothing and does not bestow upon you a small or great blessing?" He says: Do you associate this with the worship of another? This is known to them by their great ignorance, their poor self-esteem, and their lack of gratitude to the One who has bestowed upon them the blessings He has counted on them, which no one else can count. He, the Exalted, reproached them, said to them: "Do you not remember?" O people, He says: Do you not remember the blessings of Allah upon you, His great authority and power over what He wills, and the weakness, weakness, and humiliation of your idols, and that they neither bring benefit to themselves nor repel harm? So do you realize the error of what you are steadfast in worshiping them and acknowledging their divinity? In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
