## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Or you should have a house of ornamentation or ascend to the sky, and We will not believe in Your ascent until You send down to us a Book to recite: Say, 'Glory be to my Lord? Was I but a human being as a messenger?'" He says, informing about the polytheists whose matter We have mentioned in these verses: "Or you should have, O Muhammad, a house of gold, which is ornamentation." Also: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, 'Or you have a house of ornamentation,' meaning: a house of gold. And he said: All from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase 'from ornamentation,' he said: from gold. And he said: Hajjaj told me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. And from Qatadah: 'Or you have a house of ornamentation,' and 'al-zukhruf' here: gold. Of gold. Mujahid said: We did not know what the ornament was until we saw it in the recitation of Ibn Mas'ud: 'Or you have a house of gold.' And from Al-Hikam, from Mujahid, he said: I did not know what the ornamentation was until we heard in the recitation of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud: "A house of gold." In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
