## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And provoke whomever you can among them with your voice, and bring upon them your horse and foot, and share with them in wealth and children, and their promise. And Satan does not count them except as delusion": God Almighty says in His saying, "And provok," and "insult and belittle," from their saying: provoke so-and-so such and such, so he provokes him, "whoever you can among them with your voice." The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the sound intended by His Exalted Praise in the phrase "And provoke whomever you are able among them with your voice," and some said: it means the sound of singing and playing. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
