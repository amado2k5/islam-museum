## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 63 to 64 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And when Jesus came with clear proofs, he said, 'I have come to you with wisdom and to make clear to you some of that over which you differ, so fear Allah and obey.'" And when Jesus came to the Children of Israel with clear proofs, he meant the clear proofs. It was said: By clear proofs, he meant the Gospel. In the verse, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
