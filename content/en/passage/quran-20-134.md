## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if We had destroyed them with a punishment before it, they would have said, 'Our Lord, why not sent to us a messenger so we would follow Your verses before We were humiliated and disgraced': If We had destroyed these polytheists who deny this Qur'an before We had sent it down to them, and before We had sent a caller calling them to what We have imposed upon them with a punishment We would have sent down upon them for their disbelief in Allah, they would have said on the Day of Resurrection, when they came to us, and We intended their punishment: Our Lord, have You not sent us a messenger inviting us to obey? "So we may follow Your verses," meaning: so we may follow Your proof and evidence and what You reveal to Him of Your command and prohibition before we humiliate us by punishing Us and disgrace Him. As narrated from Fudayl ibn Marzuq, from Atiyah al-Awfi, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, from the Prophet who said: "Three people will be argued against Allah on the Day of Resurrection: the one who perishes in the period, the one who is intellectually overwhelmed, and the young child. The one who is overwhelmed by reason will say: You did not give me a mind to benefit from, and he says and quotes in the passage about Atiyah.
