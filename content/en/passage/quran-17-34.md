## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not approach the orphan's wealth except in the best manner until he reaches maturity. And fulfill the covenant. Indeed, the covenant is ever accountable." He also ruled that you should not approach the orphan's wealth with food, wastefulness, or haste to grow up, but rather approach him with the best deed and the best lineage, meaning that you should manage it for him with fruitfulness, reform, and caution. Qatadah used to say about this: And from Qatadah, regarding His saying: "And do not approach the property of an orphan except in a way that is best." When this verse was revealed, this became especially severe for the companions of the Messenger of Allah, who did not mix with them in food, food, or anything else. So Allah, the Blessed and Exalted, revealed: "And if you do mix with them, then they are your brothers, and Allah knows the corrupter from the righteous." This was a concession for them. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
