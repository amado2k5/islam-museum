## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 16 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, is that better or the eternal Paradise which the righteous were promised, for them is a reward and a destination?" Say, O Muhammad, to those who deny the Hour: Is this the Fire that your Lord described to you as the description of its inhabitants is better, or the eternal garden whose bliss endure and never perishes, which promised those who fear it in this world to obey Him in what He commanded and forbade? And His saying: "It was for them a reward and a destination," he means: The eternal Paradise was for the righteous as the reward for their deeds for God in this world by obeying Him, the reward for their piety in Him, and a destiny for them. He means: And a destination for the righteous, to which they will reach in the Hereafter. And His saying: "They will have therein whatever they wish," means: for these pious ones are in the eternal Paradise which Allah has promised them, whatever they desire from what souls desire and what the eyes delight. "Eternal" therein, meaning: they will remain therein forever, never leaving it, nor will its bliss depart from them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
