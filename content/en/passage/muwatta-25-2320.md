## Summary
Malik said: if they are young and cannot bear to work, one does not wait for them to grow up, and they remain slaves to their father's master, unless the contract slave left what would pay their instalments on their behalf until they undertake to work; if they pay, they are freed, and if they become unable, they are enslaved. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.357}
