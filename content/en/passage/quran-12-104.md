**Commentary on verse 104 (pp. 284-285)**

On "you ask no reward of them for it; it is only a reminder for the worlds", al-Tabari says: God says to Muhammad: you do not ask, Muhammad, those who deny your prophethood and refuse to confirm you and to acknowledge what you have brought them from your Lord, for what you call them to of sincere worship of your Lord, leaving the worship of idols and obeying the Merciful, "any reward", meaning any recompense or payment from them; rather your recompense and the reward of your work is with God. He says: you do not ask them for a recompense for that, so that they could say to you, you only want by calling us to follow you that we give up our property to you when you ask us. And since you do not ask them that, it was their duty to know that you call them to what you call them to in following your Lord's command and as sincere advice to them, and not to suspect you. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "ajr". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 284}

On "it is only a reminder for the worlds", he says: what your Lord has sent you with, Muhammad, of prophethood and message is only a reminder, an admonition and reminding for the worlds, that they may take heed and remember by it. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "dhikr". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 285}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
