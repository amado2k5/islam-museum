## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And clasp your hand to your wing; it will come out white without harm" (another verse): "And clasp your hand, O Moses, and place it under your forearm, and the two wings are the hands." This is also narrated from Abu Huraira and Ka'b al-Ahbar. As for the people of Arabic, they say: they are the two madmen. Some cite the statement of al-Rajiz: "Attach it to the chest and the wing," and in the passage he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, and Ibn Ishaq.
