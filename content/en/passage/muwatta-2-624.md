## Summary
Malik transmits from several narrators he trusts that Saad ibn Abi Waqqas and Said ibn Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl died at al-Aqiq, were carried to Medina and buried there. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and Man ibn Isa transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.318}
