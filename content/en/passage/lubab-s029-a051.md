## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of al-'Ankabut 29:51, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247).

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «أولم يكفهم».
