## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 28 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah sends down water from the sky and We bring forth with it fruits of varying colors? And from the mountains are new white and red of different colors and black crows": Have you not seen that Allah sends down rain from the sky, and We bring forth with it fruits of varying colors, meaning: We watered it with trees in the earth, and We brought forth from those trees fruits of different colors, some red, some black, yellow, and other of their colors. "And from the mountains are new white and red," as Allah says: Among the mountains are the new ones, and these are the new ones. These are the plans that occur in the mountains in white, red, and black, like the roads, whose singular is Jeddah. From this is the saying of Imru' al-Qais regarding the description of a donkey: As if his navel and his bed are like a dilis running over them, meaning by 'jaddah': the black plan is in the donkey's back. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Dhahhak.
