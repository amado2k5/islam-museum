## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is because they preferred the life of this world over the Hereafter, and that Allah does not guide the disbelieving people": Allah's wrath befell these polytheists and they deserved a great punishment, because they chose the adornment of worldly life over the bliss of the Hereafter, and because Allah does not guide those who deny His verses despite their persistence in denying them.
