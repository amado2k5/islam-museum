## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46-49 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “And when it is said to them, ‘Bow down,’ they do not bow down”: When these criminals who deny God’s warnings are told, “Bow down,” they do not bow down. Scholars of interpretation differ on when this will be said to them. Some say it will be said in the Hereafter when they are called to prostrate but are unable to. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
