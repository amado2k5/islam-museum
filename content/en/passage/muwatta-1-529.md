## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr from Abbad ibn Tamim from Abdullah ibn Zayd al-Mazini, that the Prophet said what lies between his house and his pulpit is a garden of the gardens of Paradise, without the addition about the pool in Abu Hurayra's report. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and al-Qa'nabi transmitted it, al-Tinisi in al-Bukhari (1195), and Qutayba in Muslim (4/123) and al-Nasa'i (2/35). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.273}
