## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And who is more unjust than one who is reminded of the signs of his Lord and then turns away from them? Indeed, we are among the criminals [avengers]": And which people is more unjust to himself than the one whom Allah admonished with His proofs, His Book, and His messengers, then turned away from all of that, and did not take heed of his admonitions, but rather became arrogant about them. And His saying, "Indeed, we are among the criminals who take revenge," it means: We are among those who have acquired sins and committed evil deeds, seeking revenge. Some used to say: By criminals in this context, he meant the people of predestination.
