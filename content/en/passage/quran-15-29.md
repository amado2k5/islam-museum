## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "So when I shaped him," meaning I shaped him and shaped him, "and breathed into him of My spirit," and he became a living human. "They fell down in prostration before him," a prostration of greeting and honor, not a prostration of worship. It is narrated from Ibn Abbas that when God created the angels, He told them that He created a human from clay, so when He created him, they prostrated to him. But those people refused, so He sent fire upon them and burned them, then He created other angels who refused, and I burned them. Then He created angels and they said, 'We hear and obey, except Iblis, who was among the first disbelievers.'
