## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37-39 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "a reward from your Lord, a gift, a reckoning" as follows: God, may His praise be exalted, means that He gave these righteous people what is described in these verses as a reward from their Lord for their deeds, for their obedience to Him in this world. His statement "a gift" means that this reward is a favor from God upon them, as He rewarded them tenfold for each deed in some instances, and seven hundredfold for each deed in others. This increase, though a reward, is a gift from God. His statement "a reckoning" means that they will be held accountable for their deeds to God in this world. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
