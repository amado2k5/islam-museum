## Summary
Malik said: the wounds of Jews, Christians and Magians in their blood-money are reckoned like the wounds of Muslims in their blood-money: the head wound that exposes the bone is a twentieth of its blood-money, the wound that reaches the brain is a third of its blood-money, and the abdominal wound is a third of its blood-money; their wounds are all reckoned on that basis. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.435}
