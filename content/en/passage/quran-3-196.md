## Summary
Verse 196 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 196 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 196: moving about in the land (p. 493, reports 8371-8372).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: let not the going to and fro of those who disbelieved in the earth, and their striking about in it, deceive you, Muhammad. Muhammad ibn al-Husayn (8371), through Ahmad from Asbat from al-Suddi: their striking about in the lands. He then says that God forbade His Prophet to be deceived by their travelling about the lands and by God's respite to them despite their polytheism, their denial of His favours and their worship of others, and that the address came to the Prophet while the one meant is others among his followers and companions, as he explained earlier, the Prophet being one who proclaimed by God's command and called to the truth. In the editor's note he fears something has dropped out of this sentence, though the meaning is clear, and that the original probably added that the Messenger of God was not entrusted to the people of polytheism and disbelief with anything of God's command; for the word for "moving about" he refers to an earlier place. In the same sense said Qatada (8372), through Bishr from Yazid from Sa'id: by God, they did not deceive the Prophet of God, nor was anything of God's command entrusted to them, until God took him in that state. These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 493}
