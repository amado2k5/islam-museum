## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 16 as a single section. He explains His saying, “They say, ‘Are we indeed to be returned to our former state?’”: These disbelievers of the Resurrection from among the polytheists of Quraysh say, when it is said to them: You will be resurrected after death: Are we indeed to be returned to our former state before death, returning alive as we were before our destruction and before our death? This is from their saying: So-and-so returned on his hoof: when he returned from where he came. And from it is the saying of the poet: Hoofs upon baldness and gray hair. God forbid from foolishness and recklessness. And in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
