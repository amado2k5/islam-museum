## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40 to 41 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So perhaps my Lord will give you something better than your Garden and send upon it a reckoning from the sky, and you will become a smooth and slippery slope": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of the believer certain of the return to Allah to the disbeliever who doubts the Hour: If you ring, O man, I have the least wealth and children in this world, then perhaps my Lord will provide me with something better than this orchard of yours, and send upon it, meaning the Garden of the disbeliever to which He said: I do not think these will ever perish. "We thought from heaven," he says: a punishment from heaven that you throw and throw. Al-Hisban: the plural of Hisbana, which is the means of the land.
