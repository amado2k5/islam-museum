## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "O Zechariah, indeed we give you good tidings of a boy named Yahya; We have not given him a name before": His Lord answered him and said to him: O Zechariah, we give you good tidings of our gift to you a boy named Yahya. Qatadah used to say: God named him Yahya only to revive him with faith. And from Qatadah, His saying, "O Zechariah, we give you good tidings of a boy named Yahya," is a servant whom God revived in faith. And His saying: 'We have never given him a name before,' scholars of interpretation differed in its interpretation; some said: Its meaning is that no barren person has ever begotten like him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
