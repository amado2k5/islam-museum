## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and indeed, Allah is the Self-sufficient, the Praiseworthy": To Him belongs the dominion of whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth; they are His servants, owners, and creation; He has no partner in that or in any of it. Indeed, Allah is independent of all that is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth of His creation, and they are in need of Him. The Praiseworthy is with His servants in His favor upon them and His hands with them.
