## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 38 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And a sign for them is the night; We remove the day from it, and behold, they are darkened": It is also evidence of God's ability to do whatever He wills, "The night from which We remove the day," meaning: We remove the day from it. The meaning of "from it" in this context is from it, as if it was said: We remove the day from it, bring darkness to it, and go away by the day. From it is His saying: "And recite to them the news of what We have given and then detach from it," meaning he left it and left it, so likewise the night departs from the day. And His saying: "Then behold, they are darkened," it means: Then they have become in darkness with the coming of night. Qatadah said about this: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And a sign for them is the night; We peel off the day, but behold, they are oppressed," he said: The night enters the day, and the day enters the night. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibrahim.
