## Summary
Malik transmits from his uncle Abu Suhayl ibn Malik, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, who said: do you see it as red as this ember of yours? It is blacker than pitch. The editor explains that pitch is tar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.593}
