## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 107 to 108 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, those who believed and did righteous deeds will have gardens of Paradise as a lodging": Those who believed in Allah and His Messenger, affirmed the oneness of Allah and what He revealed, and acted upon His obedience, will have gardens of Paradise, and Paradise means the majority of Paradise. As Umayyah said: Their dwellings then were clearly paradises, with two onions, and some interpreters differing on the meaning of Paradise, with some saying it meant the best and middle Paradise. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, Al-A'mash, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Ata.
