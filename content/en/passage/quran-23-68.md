## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 68 to 70 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Did they not reflect on the word, or did it come to them until their forefathers came": Did these polytheists not contemplate the revelation of God and His speech, so they would know the lessons it contained, and know the proofs of God that He used as evidence against Him? "Or did something come to them that did not come to their forefathers?" He says: Or did a matter come to them that did not come before them from their predecessors, so they arrogantly and turned away? The messengers came before them and revealed the scriptures with them. It is possible that 'um' in this context means 'rather,' so the interpretation of the speech is: Did they not reflect on the speech? Rather, what came to them before their first fathers came, so they abandoned that reflection and turned away from it, as those who came before them did not have that. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
