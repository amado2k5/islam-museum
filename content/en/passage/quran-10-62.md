**Commentary on verse 62 (p. 118)**

On [[passage/quran-10-62|10:62]] he says: the helpers of God have no fear in the next life of God's punishment, for God is pleased with them and has made them safe from it, and they do not grieve for what they lost of the world. "Friends" is the plural of the word for helper. The editor notes that here there follows a detail on the meaning of "friends of God" for which there is no earlier parallel in al-Tabari's commentary. The commentary continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 118}
