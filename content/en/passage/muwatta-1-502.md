## Summary
A question in the chapter on the imam going out on the festival day and waiting for the sermon: Malik was asked whether a man who prayed with the imam on the day of breaking the fast may leave before he hears the sermon. He said he should not leave until the imam leaves. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256}
