## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that when the Messenger of God returned from a raid or the pilgrimage or the lesser pilgrimage, he exclaimed the takbir three times on every height of land, then said: there is no god but God alone, without partner; His is the dominion and His the praise, and He is powerful over all things; returning, repenting, worshipping, prostrating to our Lord, praising; God has fulfilled His promise, supported His servant, and defeated the factions alone. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.562}
