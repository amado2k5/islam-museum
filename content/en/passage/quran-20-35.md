## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 31 to 35 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, you were with us insight": You were the one with insight over us, and nothing is hidden from you from our deeds. It was mentioned from Abdullah ibn Abi Ishaq that he used to recite: "Ashdad biha azri" with a fatha on the alif from Ashadad, and "and associate him in my affair" with a damma on the alif of "ashrakhu," meaning the news from Moses himself that he did this, not in the form of supplication. If this is read as such, he is affirmed and shadda wa shirak for the reward or the answer to the supplication, and this is a reading I do not see a proper reading, although it has an understandable reason, because it is a different reading that is not permissible. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
