## Summary
Malik transmits that he asked Ibn Shihab about a man whose slave-woman was his wife, whom he had divorced once and then bought. Ibn Shihab said she is lawful to him by ownership so long as the divorce was not final; if it was final, she is not lawful to him by ownership until she marries another. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.44}
