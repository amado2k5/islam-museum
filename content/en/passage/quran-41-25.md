## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We appointed for them companions, so they adorned for them what was before them and what was behind them. And it is true for them to say about nations that passed away before them of jinn and mankind. Indeed, they were losers": He means that He mentioned Him by saying: "And We made for them companions," and We made them for them companions, paired with them to adorn for them the evils of their deeds, and they made that attractive to them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
