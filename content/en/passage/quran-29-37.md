## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "so they denied him, and trembling seized them, and they became lying in their homes": The people of Midian, Shu'ayb, denied what he brought them from God's message, so the trembling of punishment seized them, and they became in their homes lying dead, some dead on top of others. As narrated from Qatadah: "So they became dead in their homes," meaning dead.
