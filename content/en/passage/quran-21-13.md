## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Do not run and return to that in which you have been comforted and your dwellings, that you may be questioned": Do not flee and return to what you have been comforted with: He says: to what you have been blessed with of your life and dwellings, as in my father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas regarding the phrase: "Do not run, but return to what you enjoyed and your dwellings, that you might be questioned," meaning whoever was afflicted by punishment in this world among the nations who disobeyed God. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: 'Do not run': do not run away. And he said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. And from Qatadah: 'And return to what you have been distracted about,' meaning: return to your worldly life in which you have been distracted. From Qatadah: 'And return to what you have been distracted by' he said: to what you have been distracted about in your world. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
