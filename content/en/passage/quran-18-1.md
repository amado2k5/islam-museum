## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Praise be to Allah who sent down to His servant the Book and did not make it crooked": Abu Ja'far said: Allah Almighty says: Praise be to Allah who singled Muhammad with His message and chose him to convey it on his behalf, sent him to His creation as a messenger, sent down His Book to him, and made it clear and did not make it crooked. And what is meant by His saying, the Exalted, is: "upright," moderate and upright. It is also said: what is meant by this is that He is the one who established all the books, confirms and memorizes them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, and Ibn Ishaq.
