## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on keeping ties of kinship and the prohibition of cutting them, in Sahih Muslim. It describes kinship speaking near the Throne, and God makes the reward of joining it return to those who join and cut it. He cites a verse of Surat Muhammad. {cite:sahih-muslim}
