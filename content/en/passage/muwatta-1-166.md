## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on urinating standing and otherwise. Yahya transmits from Malik from Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari as a mursal: a Bedouin entered the mosque, uncovered his private parts to urinate, and the people shouted at him until their voices rose. The Prophet ordered them to leave him, and when he had urinated, he ordered a great bucket of water poured on that place. The editor explains dhanub as a large bucket, and notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the report is raised and connected through Yahya ibn Sa'id from Anas by several sound channels, and that it is in the Sahihayn from Anas (al-Bukhari 221, Muslim 1/163) and from Abu Hurayra in al-Bukhari (6010). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.110}
