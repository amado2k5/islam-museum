## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 10 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By the winds sent forth in succession”: The scholars of interpretation differed in the meaning of God’s words, “By the winds sent forth in succession.” Some said: It means the winds sent forth, one following the other. They said: “The winds sent forth” refers to the winds. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-A’mash, and Sufyan.
