## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz flogged a slave eighty lashes for false accusation. Abu al-Zinad said: I asked Abdallah ibn Amir ibn Rabia about it, and he said: I knew Umar ibn al-Khattab, Uthman and the caliphs after them, and I never saw anyone flog a slave for false accusation more than forty. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.390}
