## Summary
Hadith of Shaddad ibn Aws: "God has prescribed excellence in everything". Ibn Rajab gives two readings of what is prescribed and to whom, and notes that writing in the Qur'an usually means something obligatory by law or fixed by decree. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
