## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, O Allah, Creator of the heavens and the earth, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. You judge between Your servants concerning that over which they used to differ": Say, O Muhammad, Allah is the Creator of the heavens and the earth, the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, which the eyes do not see, nor the eyes perceive, and the witnesses of His creation do not notice. "You judge between Your servants," and you separate them by truth on the Day You unite them to separate judgment between them "in what they used to be" in this world. They differ in their speech about You, Your greatness, Your authority, and other differences among them. On that Day, you will judge between Us and those polytheists whose hearts are filled with remorse when you alone are mentioned, but when you are mentioned besides you, they rejoice in the truth.
