**Commentary on verse 40 (p. 257)**

Al-Tabari says this is an announcement from God to the Messenger's Companions that He is the one charged with helping His messenger against the enemies of his religion and making him prevail over them without them, whether or not they protect him, and a reminder from Him of His doing so for him when he was few in number and the enemy many; how then when he is many and the enemy few? He says to them: if you do not go out, believers, with My messenger when he calls you out, so as to help him, God is his helper and supporter against his enemy and makes him independent of you and of your help and support, as He helped him "when the unbelievers drove him out", the unbelievers in God of Quraysh, from his homeland and home, "second of two", meaning they drove him out as one of the two. The Arabs say "he is the second of two" meaning one of the two, and "third of three" and "fourth of four" meaning one of the three or four, which differs from their saying "brother of six" or "boy of seven", since the brother and the boy are not among the six and the seven. By "second of two" God meant the Messenger and Abu Bakr, who left fleeing from Quraysh when they planned to kill the Messenger and hid in the cave. His words "when the two were in the cave" mean when the Messenger and Abu Bakr; the sentence continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 257}
