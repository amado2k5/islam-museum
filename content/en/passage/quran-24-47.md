## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 48 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And they say, 'We believe in Allah and the Messenger and we obey.' Then a group of them will take turns after that, and those will not be the believers": The hypocrites say: We have believed in Allah and the Messenger and we have obeyed Allah and we have obeyed the Messenger. "Then a group of them will take over," he says: then each group will reflect after they have said this statement about the Messenger of Allah, and call for trial against someone else. "And those are not the believers," he says: and not those who said this, meaning His saying: "We believe in Allah and the Messenger and obey" refers to the believers, because they abandon referring to the Messenger of Allah and turn away from him when they call to him. And His saying: "And when they are called to Allah and His Messenger," it means: And when these hypocrites are called to the Book of Allah and to His Messenger "to judge between them" in what they dispute by Allah's judgment, "then a group of them refuse" to accept the truth and accept the judgment of the Messenger of Allah.
