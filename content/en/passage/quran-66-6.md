## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains his saying, “O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones, over which are [appointed] angels, harsh and severe; they do not disobey Allah in what He commands them but do what they are commanded”: O you who have believed in Allah and His Messenger, “protect yourselves” means: Teach one another what you will use to protect those you teach from the Fire, and repel it from them if they act upon it in obedience to Allah, and act in obedience to Allah. His statement, “and protect your families from the Fire,” means: Teach your families to obey God in order to protect themselves from the Fire. The passage then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
