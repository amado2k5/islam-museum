## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "until when We open for them a gate of severe punishment, in which they are in a state of distress": the interpreters differed in interpreting this, and some said: it means: until when We open the door to fighting for them, they were killed on the day of Badr. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
