## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to wash for his entering ihram before he entered it, for his entry into Mecca, and for his standing at Arafat in the evening. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shafii in his Musnad transmitted it from Malik, and that Ibn Abi Shayba and al-Bayhaqi transmitted similar reports from Ibn Umar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.434}
