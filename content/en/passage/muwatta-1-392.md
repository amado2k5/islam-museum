## Summary
The first report in the chapter on when shortening the prayer is required. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he set out on pilgrimage or lesser pilgrimage, shortened the prayer at Dhu al-Hulayfa. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.210}
