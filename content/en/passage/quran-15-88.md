## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Do not strain your eyes toward that We have granted enjoyment as spouses among them, nor grieve over them, and lower your wing to the believers": Do not wish, O Muhammad, for what We have made of the adornments of this world as enjoyment for the rich among your people who do not believe in Allah and the Last Day, for indeed, behind them is severe punishment. "And do not grieve over them" means "and do not grieve over what they enjoyed, so hasten for them, for you will have in the Hereafter something better than it, along with the one We have hastened for you in this world of honor by granting us the seven oft-repeated verses. The great Qur'an is said to be about it: so-and-so extended his eye to so-and-so's wealth: when he desired it, wished for it, and wanted it." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Jurayj.
