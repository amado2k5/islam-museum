## Summary
Verse 136 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 136 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The meaning (p. 227).** Abu Ja'far says "those" means the God-fearing, for whom he said He prepared the Garden as wide as the heavens and the earth, who are described as He described them. He then says their reward is the recompense for the works He said they did: God's pardon of them from punishment for their earlier sins together with the good of their obedience, gardens (orchards) with rivers running among their trees and beneath them as recompense for their righteous deeds, where they stay for ever; and excellent is the recompense of those who work for God, these gardens. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "recompense" (2: 27-28 and 6: 314, as printed), "gardens" (1: 384 and 5: 535, 542) and the running of rivers (5: 542). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 227}
- From Ibn Humayd, Salama, Ibn Ishaq (7866): that is, the reward of the obedient. The editor traces it to Ibn Hisham's Sira (3: 116), continuing the reports that end at 7865. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 227}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 227}
