## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, they are but names which you and your forefathers have named. Allah has not sent down any authority thereby. They follow nothing but conjecture. And what do souls desire. And indeed, guidance has come to them from their Lord": What are these names you have named, namely Al-Lat, Al-Uzza, and Manat, the third? Are these names you and your forefathers have named, O polytheists of Allah, and your forefathers before you? Allah did not reveal them, meaning by these names. He says: God did not permit that for you, nor did He permit it. Also: Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "From authority..." to the end of the verse. And His saying: "They follow nothing but conjecture," the Exalted says: These polytheists do not follow these names by which they named their gods except conjecture that what they say is true, not certainty. "And what souls desire" means: and they desires themselves, because they did not receive this from a revelation that came to them from Allah, nor from the Messenger of Allah who informed them, but rather it was infiltrated by themselves, or they took it from their forefathers who were disbelievers in Allah as they are in it. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd.
