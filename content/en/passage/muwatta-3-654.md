## Summary
It reached Malik that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote to his governor in Damascus about charity: charity is only on crops, on silver, and on livestock. Malik said: charity is only due on these three things: crops, silver and livestock. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.335}
