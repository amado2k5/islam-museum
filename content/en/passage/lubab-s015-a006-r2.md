## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Hijr 15:6, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Tabi'i generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «يا أيها الذين آمنوا لا تقدموا بين يدي».
