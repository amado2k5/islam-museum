## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among His signs are your sleep by night and day and your desire of His bounty. Indeed, in that are signs for a people who hear." Among His proofs against you, O people, is the estimation of hours and times, and the contrast between night and day. He made the night a place of rest for you in which you may rest and sleep, and He made the day a bright place for you to manage your livelihood and seek provision from you. "Indeed in that are signs for a people who listen." Allah says: Indeed, in Allah's action as well, there is a lesson, a reminder, and evidence that nothing can prevent the doer of this, intending for a people who listen to Allah's admonitions, take heed, and take heed, and understand Allah's proofs against them.
