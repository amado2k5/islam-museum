**Commentary on verse 197 (p. 323)**

Al-Tabari says: this too is a command from God to His prophet to say to the polytheists: tell them that God is my helper and backer, and those you call upon, polytheists, besides God, of the gods, cannot help you, nor can they, in their powerlessness to help you, help themselves. Which of the two is the better to be worshipped and the more fit for godhead: one who helps his friend and protects himself from whoever wants him, or one who cannot help his friend and is powerless to protect himself from whoever wants him and wrongs him with harm? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 323}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
