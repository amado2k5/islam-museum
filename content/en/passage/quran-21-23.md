## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He is not questioned about what He does while they are being questioned": No one asks the Lord of the Throne about what He does to His creation, from their conduct to what He wills of life, death, honor, humiliation, and other forms of His judgment over them, because they are His creation and His servants, all of them are in His dominion and authority, and judgment is His judgment. Nothing above Him asks Him what He does, and He says to Him, Why did you not do it? Why did you not do it? "And they are questioned," says He, Glorified and Exalted: All those in the heavens and the earth among His servants are responsible for their deeds and accountable for their deeds, and He is the one who questions them about that and holds them accountable for it because they are above them and their Owner, and they are under His authority. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Jurayj.
