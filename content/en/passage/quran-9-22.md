**Commentary on verse 22 (p. 175)**

Al-Tabari says: "abiding in it" means staying in the gardens forever, with no end or limit; "with God is a great reward" means that with God, for these believers He has described, is a reward, a recompense for their obedience to their Lord and performance of what He charged them with, and great, being the bliss He promised to give them in the hereafter. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 175}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
