## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nisa 4:49, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Companion generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «ألم تر إلى الذين يزكون».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
