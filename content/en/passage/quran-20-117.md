## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 116 to 117 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So we said, O Adam, indeed this is an enemy to you and your spouse, so do not expel you from Paradise and cause you misery": Therefore, whoever hates him did not prostrate to you, disobeyed My command in that, and disobeyed me, so do not obey him in what he commands as he does, and he will expel you with your disobedience to your Lord and your obedience to Him. "From Paradise and you will suffer," meaning: your livelihood will be from the toil of your hands, for that is the misery he warned him about, as from Ja'far, from Sa'id, who said: A red bull descended to Adam, plowing it and wiping sweat from its forehead. It was the one whom Allah Almighty said: "So let not let you both be expelled from Paradise and you should suffer." That was his misery.
