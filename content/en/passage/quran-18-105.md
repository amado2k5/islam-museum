## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Those who disbelieved in the signs of their Lord and the meeting with Him, so their deeds became worthless, so We will not assign for them any weight on the Day of Resurrection": These are the ones whose description We have described, the most losing in deeds, those who disbelieved in the proofs and proofs of their Lord and denied meeting Him. "So their deeds became worthless," meaning: their deeds were nullified, and they would have no reward benefiting their owners in the Hereafter. Rather, they will have a long punishment and disgrace. "So We will not assign for them any weight on the Day of Resurrection." Allah says: So we do not give them any weight. What he meant by this is that their scales are not weighed down by them, because the scales are only weighed down by good deeds, and they have no good deeds, so their scales are weighed down by them. In the excerpt, he quotes from Al-A'mash.
