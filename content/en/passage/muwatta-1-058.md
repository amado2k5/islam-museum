## Summary
A report that Malik was told that Ali ibn Abi Talib and Abdullah ibn Abbas did not perform ablution from food touched by fire. The editor notes that al-Bayhaqi transmitted reports to this effect from both, and Abd al-Razzaq gave similar reports in his Musannaf. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.62}
