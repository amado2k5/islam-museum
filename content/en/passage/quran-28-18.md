## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So he entered the city fearful, waiting, and when the one who had supported him yesterday cried out to him, Moses said to him, 'You are a clear and clear speaker.'" Moses spent the night in Pharaoh's city, fearful of his crime and the killing of the soul he had killed, lest he be taken and killed by it. "Waiting" means he waits for the news: that is, waiting for what the people will say, what they are doing about him and his killing. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Al-Suddi.
