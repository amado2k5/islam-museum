## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not approach adultery; indeed, it was an immorality and an evil way": He also ruled that "do not approach," O people, "adultery, indeed it was an immorality," meaning: adultery was an immorality and an evil way. He says: The path of adultery is evil, because the path of the people of disobedience to Allah and those who oppose His command, so the worst path is a path whose owner will bring the fire of Hell.
