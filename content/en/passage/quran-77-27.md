## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27-30 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Have We not made the earth a receptacle?”: God, may He be exalted, reminds His servants of His blessings upon them: “Have We not made the earth a receptacle for you, O people?” He explains that it means a container. You say, “This is a receptacle for this,” and “I have made it a container for it,” meaning it is its container. The meaning of the statement is: Have We not made the earth a receptacle for your living and your dead? It contains your living in dwellings and homes, sheltering and gathering them therein, and your dead within its depths in the graves, where they are buried. It is also possible that by “receptacle for the living and the dead” he meant that it contains their harm during their lives and their decay after their deaths. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Rabi’, and Sufyan.
