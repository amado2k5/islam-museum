## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 96 to 98 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Repel [that which is the best of evil]; We are most knowing of what they describe": God Almighty says to His Prophet: Repel, O Muhammad, with the best of allies, which is to overlook and pardon the ignorant polytheists and to be patient with their harm, and this is what He commanded him before He commanded him to fight them. By 'evil,' he means the harm caused to him by the polytheists and their denial of him regarding what he brought them from God. He says to him: Be patient with what you receive from them concerning the essence of God. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
