## Summary
Verse 60 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 60 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of verse 60.** Al-Tabari says it means that what God told the Prophet of Jesus, that his likeness is Adam's, is the truth from his Lord, so he should not be among the doubters. Report 7167 (Bishr, from Yazid, from Said, from Qatada): be in no doubt about Jesus, that his likeness is Adam's, God's servant and messenger, His word and spirit. Report 7168 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Abdullah ibn Abi Jafar, from his father, from al-Rabi): be in no doubt about what We related to you, that Jesus is God's servant and messenger, a word from Him and a spirit, and that his likeness before God is Adam's. Report 7169 (Ibn Humayd, from Salama, from Ibn Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr): what reached you of the account of Jesus is the truth from your Lord, so do not doubt it. Report 7170 (Yunus, from Ibn Wahb, from Ibn Zayd): the doubters are those who are in doubt. Al-Tabari says doubt, uncertainty and suspicion mean the same, as one says "give me", "hand me" and "come here". The editor notes that 7169 is in Ibn Hisham's Sira (2:231-232) as a continuation of 7165, that al-Tabari uses it to give what he had abridged, and that Ibn Hisham's wording at its end is better; he refers to an earlier explanation of the word for doubting. (The number 7170 is unreadable in the scan and is inferred from the sequence.) {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 472} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 473}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
