## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "ask the Children of Israel how many clear signs We gave them": al-Tabari means, ask, Muhammad, the Children of Israel, who wait on nothing before turning in obedience to Me and acknowledging your prophethood but that I come in canopies of cloud and with My angels and decide between you and those who believe in you, how many signs and marks came to them before you, in support of their truthfulness, plainly from Me, and they rejected My proofs, denied My messengers, changed My favour and altered My covenant. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 270} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 271} From Mujahid: the sign is what God mentioned in the Qur'an and what He did not, and they are the Jews (4040); from al-Rabi': the staff of Moses and his hand, the sea cut open for them and their enemy drowned as they watched, the cloud shading them, manna and quails sent down; then they contradicted God's command, killed His prophets and altered His covenant (4041). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 271} Al-Tabari says God told His Prophet these things and commanded him to be patient with those who deny him, informing him that earlier communities did the same to their prophets, and that the Jews among them are remnants of those with such habits. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 271} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 272}

On "whoever changes God's favour after it has come to him, God is severe in punishment": by "favour" he means Islam and the laws of the religion He prescribed, and whoever changes what he covenanted with God regarding that favour, of acting on it and entering it, so that he disbelieves in it, will be punished as threatened. He reads the verse as: you who believed in the Torah and accepted it, enter Islam entirely and leave disbelief and what Satan calls you to, since the clear proofs have come to you through Muhammad and the proofs God showed by his hand, so do not alter My covenant with you in your scripture that he is My prophet and messenger. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 272} A group said the same: from Mujahid, he disbelieves in it (4042, 4043); from al-Suddi, whoever changes it into disbelief (4044); from al-Rabi', whoever disbelieves His favour after it has come to him (4045). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 272} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 273}
