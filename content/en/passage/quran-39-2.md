## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 1–2. Al-Tabari explains that the revelation of the Book is from Almighty God in His wise revenge for His management, not from anything else, so the Prophet is not in doubt about this.
