## Summary
Verse 102 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 102 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari explains "a people before you asked about them and then became disbelievers on account of them": a people before you asked for signs, and when God gave them these they came to deny them, rejecting that they were evidence for the truth of what they were offered as proof of, or proof of the soundness of what they were made proof to confirm; like the people of Salih, who asked for a sign, and when the she-camel came to them as a sign they hamstrung her, and like those who asked Jesus for a table to come down on them from heaven, and when they were given it, disbelieved in it, and the like. So God warned the believers in His prophet against following the way of the nations before them that perished through their disbelief in God's signs when these came to them at their asking, and said to them: do not ask for signs, and do not enquire into things which, if shown to you, would vex you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 115}

Report 12817, from Ibn Abbas: He forbade them to ask the kind of question the Christians asked about the table, so that they became disbelievers on account of it, and God forbade that. Report 12818, from al-Suddi: "a people before you asked about them": a people before you asked for signs, and that was when he was told, turn al-Safa into gold for us. The reports on verse 102 end here, on p. 116. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 116}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
