## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: by Him in whose hand my soul is, I would love to fight in God's path and be killed, then be given life and be killed, then be given life and be killed; and Abu Hurayra used to say this three times, swearing by God. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.592}
