## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 10 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “As for him who is self-sufficient” as meaning: As for him who is self-sufficient with his wealth, you should approach him, hoping that he will embrace Islam. He quotes Sufyan as saying, regarding the verse “As for him who is self-sufficient, you should give him your full attention,” that it was revealed concerning al-Abbas. He also quotes Ibn Abi Najih, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding the phrase “As for him who is self-sufficient,” that it refers to Utbah ibn Rabi’ah and Shabiyah ibn Rabi’ah. He then quotes statements from Mujahid and Sufyan in this section.
