## Summary
Verse 112 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 112 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 112 (pp. 500-505)**

Al-Tabari says: God bought from the believers who repent and worship their selves and wealth; the phrase is in the nominative because it opens a new clause after a complete one like it, as the Arabs do, and he points to his earlier explanation at [[passage/quran-2-18|verse 2:18]]. "The repenters" are those who return from what God disliked and was angered by to what He loves and is pleased with. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 500}

Report 17272 is from al-Hasan: they repented to God of all sins. Reports 17273 and 17274 are from al-Hasan reciting the verse: they repented of polytheism and were free of hypocrisy; reports 17275 and 17276 give the same from him. Report 17277 is from Qatada: they repented of polytheism and then did not act hypocritically in Islam. Report 17278 is from Ibn Jurayj: those who repented of sins and did not return to them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 501}

"The worshippers" are those who were humbled in awe of God and devoted themselves to serving Him. Report 17279 is from Qatada: people who took from their bodies by night and by day. Report 17280 is from al-Hasan: they worshipped God at all times, in ease and in hardship; report 17281 from him: worshippers of their Lord. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 501}

"The praisers" are those who praise God for everything He tests them with, good or bad. Report 17282 is from Qatada: people who praised God in every state. Report 17283 is from al-Hasan: those who praised God at all times, in ease and hardship; report 17284 from him: the praisers for Islam. The editor refers to earlier discussion of praise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 502}

"The sa'ihun" are the fasters. Reports 17285 and 17286 are from Ubayd ibn Umayr: the Prophet was asked about the sa'ihin and said, "They are the fasters." The editor notes that Ibn Kathir called this a good mursal report and that al-Suyuti cited it through Ubayd ibn Umayr from Abu Hurayra. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 502}

Report 17287 is from Abu Hurayra: the Messenger of God said to him that the sa'ihun are the fasters; report 17288 from him: the sa'ihun are the fasters. The editor notes that the first is raised to the Prophet and the second stops at Abu Hurayra, and that Ibn Kathir held the latter sounder. Reports 17289 and 17290 are from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, and 17291 from Abu Abd al-Rahman: sa'ihun means fasters, and siyaha is fasting. Reports 17292 to 17295 are from Ibn Abbas through several chains: the sa'ihun are the fasters. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 503}

Report 17296 is from Abdullah, the same; 17297 from Abu Abd al-Rahman; 17298 from Ibn Abbas: he means the fasters; reports 17299 and 17300 from Mujahid: they are the fasters. Report 17301 is from Ibn Abbas: wherever God mentions siyaha in the Qur'an, it means the fasters. Report 17302 is from Abu Amr al-Abdi: those among the believers who fast continually; report 17303 from al-Hasan: the fasters. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 504}

Report 17304 is from al-Hasan: the fasters of the month of Ramadan. Reports 17305 to 17308 are from al-Dahhak: the sa'ihun are the fasters, and everything in the Qur'an called sa'ihun means the fasters. Reports 17309 and 17310 are from Ata: the fasters. Report 17311 is from Wahb ibn Munabbih: siyaha existed among the Children of Israel; a man who wandered for forty years saw what earlier wanderers had seen; the son of an adulteress wandered forty years and saw nothing, and asked his Lord whether it was because his parents had done wrong while he did right, and then he saw what they had seen. Ibn Uyayna said that one who gives up food, drink and women is a wanderer. Report 17312 is from Qatada: people who took fasting from their bodies for God. Report 17313 begins here and continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 505}

We stop here at the foot of p. 505, in the commentary on verse 112 (as report 17313 begins); the next batch begins at p. 506. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 505}
