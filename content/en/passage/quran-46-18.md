## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Those upon whom the word is due concerning nations that passed away before them of jinn and mankind. Indeed, they were losers": These are those whose attribute is this, those upon whom God's punishment is deserved, and upon whom His punishment and wrath have befallen them. Among those who received God's punishment is like what befell those nations who passed before them, among the jinn and humans, who denied God's messengers and disobeyed the command of their Lord. And His saying: "Indeed, they were losers," the Exalted means: They were the ones who were misled by selling guidance for misguidance and bliss for punishment. And from Qatadah, from Al-Hasan, who said: The jinn do not die. Qatadah said: I said, "Those are the ones against whom the word is due concerning nations that have passed away..." The verse. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
