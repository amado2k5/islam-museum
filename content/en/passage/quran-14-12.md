**Commentary on verse 12 (p. 539)**

On "why should we not rely on God, when He has guided us in our ways? We will bear patiently whatever harm you cause us; and in God let the reliant rely", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise, reporting what the messengers said to their nations: "why should we not rely on God", trusting in Him and in His sufficiency and His defending us from you, "when He has guided us in our ways", he says, when He has shown us the way of deliverance from His punishment and made it clear to us, "and we will bear patiently whatever harm you cause us" in God, and whatever unpleasantness we meet from you in Him because of our calling you to what you shun, the disowning of idols and images and the devoting of worship to Him; "and in God let the reliant rely", he says, let whoever of His creation has trust in Him rely on God; as for whoever disbelieves in Him, his patron is Satan. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 539}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
