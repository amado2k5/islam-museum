## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 169 to 171 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "My Lord, save me and my family from what they do": He sought help from Lot when his people threatened to expel him from their land if he did not stop forbidding them from riding immorality. He said, "My Lord, save me and my family" from Your punishment for what they do of the coming of the males, "so We saved him and his family" from the punishment with which We punished the people of Lot, "all except the old in the past," meaning the rest, due to the long passing of years. She became old, for she was destroyed among the people of Lot because she used to guide her people to guests. It has been said: It was said to be from the Ghabirun because she did not perish with her people in their village, and that she was only struck by the stone after leaving their village with Lot and his two daughters. She was among the Ghabirun after her people, then Allah destroyed her with the rain He rained on the remnants of Lot's people from stones. We have previously explained this with sufficient evidence to support her return.
