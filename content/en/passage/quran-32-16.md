## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Their south turns away from beds, calling upon their Lord out of fear and hope, and they spend from what We have provided them": the south of those who believe in the signs of Allah, whose attributes have been described, rises from their beds where they lie down, and they do not sleep. "They call upon their Lord out of fear and hope" in His forgiveness of them, and His favor over them with His mercy and forgiveness, "And from what We have provided them, they spend" in the way of Allah, fulfilling from Him the rights that Allah has made obligatory upon them. And 'tajafa': it reacts from estrangement, and 'jafa' means prophethood, as the poet said: 'And my companions are the dust of Damascus, and Ibn Malat is 'muttajaf' is gentle, meaning: their generosity is a trait of Ibn Malat. Allah described them as their south's avoidance of beds because they left lying down to sleep and were occupied with prayer. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, Sufyan, and Ata.
