## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We placed over their hearts hardships that they might understand, and in their ears deafness. And when you mention your Lord alone in the Qur'an, they turn over their backs aversion." And We placed over the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter when you recite the Qur'an to them, which is the plural of Kenan. This is what Allah covers them with forgetting from understanding what is recited to them. "And in their ears is deafness," meaning: We placed in their ears deafness from hearing it, and deafness. Al-Waqr with a fatha in the ear means heaviness. Al-Waqr with a kasra: carrying it. And His saying: "And when you mention your Lord alone in the Qur'an," he says: And if you say: There is no god but Allah in the Qur'an while reciting it, "Turn away from their backs with aversion," it means: Move away, and leave your saying out of arrogance and reverence for Him to be one-dimensional to Allah. And with what we have said about this, some interpreters have said. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
