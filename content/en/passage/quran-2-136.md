## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "say: we believe in God and in what was sent down to us and what was sent down to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes, and what was given to Moses and Jesus, and what was given to the prophets from their Lord; we make no distinction between any of them, and to Him we submit" al-Tabari says: say, believers, to these Jews and Christians who told you "be Jews or Christians and you will be guided", we believe, that is we hold true, in God and in the Book sent down to our Prophet Muhammad, the address of the sending down being put to them because they follow him and are commanded and forbidden by it; and in what was sent down to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes (the prophets from Jacob's descendants); and in the Torah God gave Moses, the Gospel He gave Jesus and the books of all the prophets, affirming that all of it is truth, guidance and light from God and that all the prophets He named were on truth and guidance, each confirming the other on one path in calling to God's oneness and acting in obedience to Him. On "we make no distinction" he says we do not believe some prophets and disbelieve others, as the Jews disowned Jesus and Muhammad and accepted others, and the Christians disowned Muhammad and accepted others; rather we testify for all that they were messengers and prophets of God sent with truth and guidance. "And to Him we submit" means we are humble to Him in obedience and yielding in servitude. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 109} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 110}

He relates from Ibn Abbas that a group of Jews came to the Messenger of God, among them Abu Yasir ibn Akhtab and others, and asked him which messengers he believes in; he said, I believe in God and what was sent down to us, up to "and to Him we submit", and when he mentioned Jesus they denied his prophethood, saying we do not believe in Jesus nor in whoever believes in him, and God sent down "say: People of the Book, do you take revenge on us only because we believe in God". Qatada said: God commanded the believers to believe and hold true all His prophets and messengers and make no distinction between any. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 110} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 111}

On "the tribes" he says they are twelve men of Jacob's descendants, each of whom had a nation born of him, so they were called asbat; from Qatada and al-Rabi', Joseph and his brothers the sons of Jacob; from al-Suddi, naming Joseph, Benjamin, Reuben, Judah, Simeon, Levi, Dan and Qahat. In a footnote the editor says the last is probably a corruption of Naphtali and that al-Tabari names only eight here. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 111} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 112}

Al-Tabari relates from Ibn Ishaq on the tribes that Jacob son of Isaac, who is Israel, had by his wife Leah Reuben (the eldest), Simeon, Levi, Judah, Zebulun, Issachar and a daughter Dinah; by Rachel Joseph and Benjamin; and by two slave-women Dan, Naphtali, Gad and Asher, so Jacob's sons were twelve men from whom God spread twelve tribes whose number and descent only God knows, citing "and We divided them into twelve tribes, nations". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 112} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 113}
