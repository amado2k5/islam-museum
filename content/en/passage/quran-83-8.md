## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 12 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Nay, indeed, the record of the wicked is in Sijjin,” as follows: Nay, meaning the matter is not as these disbelievers think, that they will not be resurrected nor punished. Rather, their record, in which their deeds in this world are written, is “in Sijjin,” which is the seventh lowest earth. The word “Sijjin” is derived from the root “s-j-n,” just as “sikkir” is derived from “s-k-r” (intoxication), and “fisiq” from “fisq” (wickedness). The scholars of interpretation have differed in its meaning, with some saying the same as what we have said. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
