## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 16 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "The people of Noah and the companions of the Ars and Thamud denied before them": God Almighty says, "I lied" before those polytheists who denied Muhammad from his people, "the people of Noah and the companions of the Ars," as we mentioned before the matter of the People of the Ars, that they were a people who anchored their prophet in a well. From Sufyan, from Abu Bakr, from Ikrimah regarding this. I narrated from Al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say regarding the phrase: "the people of the ark," and the word 'Rass' is a well in which the companion of Ya-Yes was killed. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "the people of the ark," he said: a well. The people of the well, and the 'Ikah': the trees that are wrapped around the tree, and the people of the ark were two nations, so Allah sent to them one prophet, Shu'ayb, and punished them with two punishments: 'Thamud, 'Ad, Pharaoh, the brothers of Lot, and the companions of the thicket.' They were the people of Shu'ayb, and their story had been mentioned before: 'and the people of Tub'. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Ishaq, and Sufyan.
