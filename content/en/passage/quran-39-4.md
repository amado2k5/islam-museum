## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 3–4. Al-Tabari explains that God does not guide those who are liars and disbelievers, and that those who have taken besides Him allies say, 'We only worship them so that they may bring us closer to God in a spiritual way.' And if God wanted to take a son, He would have chosen from among what He wills. Glory be to Him, He is Allah, the One, the All-Subduer.
