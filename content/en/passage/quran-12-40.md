**Commentary on verse 40 (pp. 105-106)**

On "what you worship besides Him are only names you have named, you and your fathers" al-Tabari says: he means what you worship besides God. He said "you worship" in the plural though he began by addressing two, "companions of the prison", because he meant the one addressed and those of Egypt who persist in associating others with God; so he told the one addressed, what you and those like you worship of idols are only names that you and your fathers named, that is, their calling their idols gods and lords, which is shirk, likening them in their names to God, who is exalted above having any like or equal. "God has sent down no authority for them" means they named them with names He did not allow them to use, and no indication or proof established that those names are theirs, but they are an invention and fabrication of theirs. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "authority". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 105}

On "judgement belongs only to God; He has commanded that you worship none but Him" he says: it is He who commanded that you and all His creatures worship none but God, whose is divinity and worship purely, apart from everything else; report 19293, from Abu al-Aliya, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Abd Allah ibn Abi Ja'far, his father and al-Rabi' ibn Anas: religion is founded on sincerity to God alone with no partner. On "that is the upright religion" he says: what I called you to, disowning the worship of idols and giving worship sincerely to God the One, the Overpowering, is the straight religion in which there is no crookedness and the truth in which there is no doubt, "but most people do not know", meaning idolaters are ignorant of it and do not know its reality. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "upright". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 106}
