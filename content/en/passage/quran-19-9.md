## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 10 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Your Lord said, 'He is easy to me, and I created you before and you were nothing.'" Allah said to Zakariya in response, "He said so," meaning: This is the matter as you say, that your wife is barren, and that you have reached extreme old age. But your Lord says: He created what I gave you of the boy whose name I mentioned to you was that his name was Yahya Ali Hayn. Therefore, this is a metaphor for creation. And His saying: "And I created you before, and you were nothing," the Exalted says: "It is not the creation of what I promised you to give you of the boy I mentioned to you with your old age, and the barrenness of your wife with something more wonderful than your creation. Indeed, I created you, and I raised you as a good human being before My creation as I did not give you the gift of a child, and you were nothing. Likewise, I created for you the child I promised you from your barren wife, along with your old age, weakened bones, and the burning of your gray hair." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
