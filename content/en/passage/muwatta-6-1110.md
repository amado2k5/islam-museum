## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: when a she-camel gives birth, let her young be carried until it is slaughtered with her; if no carrier is found, let it be carried on its mother until it is slaughtered with her. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1204), Suwayd (528) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.509}
