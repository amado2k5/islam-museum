## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "We do not extend these and those from the giving of your Lord, and the giving of your Lord is not forbidden": Your Lord, O Muhammad, extends both groups of the seekers of the immediate and the seekers of the Hereafter, who strive for it and believe in this world of His giving. He provides for them all of His provision until they reach their term and fulfill their appointed term. Then their conditions differ after death, and their sources diverge after the arrival of the sources. The group of the seekers of the immediate will go to Hell, and the group of those seeking the Hereafter will go to Paradise, their final destination is "and the giving of your Lord is not forbidden." He says: And the gift of your Lord, which He gives to whom He wills of His creation in this world, is not withheld from those who have spread it upon him; no one among His creation can withhold it, and Allah has given it to him. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
