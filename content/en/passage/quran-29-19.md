## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have they not seen how Allah begins creation and then repeats it? Indeed, that is easy for Allah": or have they not seen how Allah resumes the creation of things as a small child, then a young boy, then a gathered man, then an old man. It is said: 'I began' and 're', and 'begun' and 'aad' are two languages with the same meaning. And His saying: 'Then He will return it' means: Then He will return it after its annihilation and affliction, just as He first began it as a new creation, not impossible for it. 'Indeed, that is easy for Allah' as easy as it used to be for Him to begin with. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
