## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "God is Forgiving, Merciful" al-Tabari says: forgiving, if you obey in your Islam and avoid what is forbidden and stop following Satan in what you forbade in your pre-Islamic time, for the error and sin that went before in your disbelief; He pardons you and leaves your punishment, and is Merciful to you if you obey Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 327} On "those who conceal what God has sent down of the Book and sell it for a small price" he says they are the Jewish rabbis who hid from people the matter and prophethood of Muhammad though they found it written in the Torah, for bribes given them, with Qatada, al-Rabi', al-Suddi and 'Ikrima saying it was revealed about the Jews, as was the verse of Al 'Imran. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 327} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 328} "Sell it" means they bought with their concealment a small price of this world's goods; from al-Suddi, they hid Muhammad's name and took a small gain for it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 328} On "those eat nothing into their bellies but the Fire, and God will not speak to them on the Day of Resurrection, nor purify them, and theirs is a painful punishment" he says what they ate of bribes for distorting God's verses is only what leads them to the Fire, citing the verse of al-Nisa' on eating orphans' property; from al-Rabi', what they took as payment; and he answers that the Arabs say "I was hungry in other than my belly". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 329} "God will not speak to them" means not with what they love and desire, though He will speak to them with what grieves them, as in the verse of al-Mu'minun, "Be gone in it and do not speak to Me"; "nor purify them" means not cleanse them of the filth of their sins and disbelief; and "painful" means causing pain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 330}
