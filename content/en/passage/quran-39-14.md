## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 15 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, 'Allah, I worship Him sincerely in religion': Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people: I worship Allah sincerely, singularly to Him my obedience and worship. I do not make Him a partner in that, but I separate Him with divinity and disassociated from others of equals and gods. So worship yourselves, O people, whatever you wish of idols, idols, and other things you worship of His creation. You will know the consequences of your worship when you meet your Lord." And His saying: "Say, 'Indeed, the losers are those who have lost their souls,' Allah Almighty says: Say, O Muhammad, to them: Indeed, the doers are those who deceived themselves and were destroyed by the punishment of Allah, their families along with themselves, so when they entered the Fire, they had no family, even though they had family in this world." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
