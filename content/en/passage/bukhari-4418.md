## Summary
In the Book of Campaigns, the chapter on the hadith of Ka'b ibn Malik: the longest hadith in the Sahih on its subject, told by Ka'b in his own words. He stayed behind from Tabuk without excuse, spoke the truth to the Prophet, and he and his two companions were boycotted by people for fifty nights, until a letter from the king of Ghassan reached him. Their repentance was announced after the dawn prayer. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
