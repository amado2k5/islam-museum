## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains: "And We have certainly placed constellations in the heaven": God made in the lowest heaven stations for the sun and moon, which are stars that descend — from Mujahid as planets, and from Qatadah whose stars appear. And "He adorned it for the onlookers": He adorned the sky with stars for those who looked at it and saw it.
