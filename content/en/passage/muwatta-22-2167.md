## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Salim ibn Abdallah, from his father, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: whoever revives a dead land, it is his. Malik said: the practice among us follows that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.288}
