## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Ibn Umar used to say that a woman in ihram, when she leaves ihram, does not comb her hair until she takes from the hair of her head; and if she has an offering with her she takes nothing from her hair until she slaughters her offering. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.519}
