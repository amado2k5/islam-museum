## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse within a single section that includes the verses [[passage/quran-65-1|65:1]] to [[passage/quran-65-3|65:3]] In summary, Al-Tabari says in “If you divorce women, divorce them for their waiting period” that the meaning is to divorce them during their period of purity, which they count as part of their waiting period, pure and without intercourse, and do not divorce them during menstruation, which they do not count as part of their menstrual cycles.
