## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and among the people is he who sells himself seeking God's pleasure": al-Tabari means: among the people is he who sells himself for what God promised the believers who fight in His way, and for which He bought their selves in "God has bought from the believers their selves and their wealth in return for Paradise" (9:111); he has shown elsewhere that "shara" means "sold". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 246} "Seeking God's pleasure" is in the accusative governed by "yashri", as if "for the sake of seeking"; he reports the opinion of some grammarians that it is accusative by the verb after the lam is dropped, citing a verse of Hatim, and another opinion on a verbal noun standing in the place of the cause, with the editor's notes. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 246} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 247} The interpreters then differed over whom it was revealed about. Some said the Emigrants and the Helpers, meaning the fighters in God's way, from Qatada (4000). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 247} Some said particular men among the Emigrants; from 'Ikrima: it was revealed about Suhayb ibn Sinan and Abu Dharr al-Ghifari, Jundub ibn al-Sakan; Abu Dharr's people seized him and he slipped away and came to the Prophet, and when he returned as an emigrant they confronted him at Marr al-Zahran and he escaped again; Suhayb's people seized him and he ransomed himself with his wealth, then left as an emigrant and Qunfudh ibn 'Umayr ibn Jud'an overtook him, so he gave him what remained of his wealth and he let him go (4001). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 248} The editor corrects the printed "Munqidh" to "Qunfudh ibn 'Umayr", who became a Companion and was made governor of Mecca by 'Umar, then dismissed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 248} From al-Rabi': a man of Mecca had become Muslim and wanted to emigrate to Medina, but they stopped and held him; he said, I will give you my house and my wealth and all I own, so let me go; they refused, then one of them said take what he has and let him go, and they did, and he left; God sent down the verse on the Prophet at Medina; when he neared Medina 'Umar met him with some men and said, the sale has profited; he said, and your sale will not lose; what is that? 'Umar said, such and such was revealed about you (4002). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 248}
