## Summary
In the Book of Campaigns: Aisha reports that in his final illness the Prophet felt the pain of the food he had eaten at Khaybar, the poisoned sheep a Jewish woman had brought him, until he said he felt the cutting of his aorta. It is among the texts on the poisoning at Khaybar and its lasting effect. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
