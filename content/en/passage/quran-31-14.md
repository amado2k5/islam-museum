## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We commanded man to be dutiful to his parents. His mother carried him here upon weakness, and his weaning in two years: 'Thank Me and your parents, to Me is the [end]': And We commanded man to be dutiful to his parents." He means: weakness upon weakness, and strength upon hardship. Among these is the saying of Zuhayr: 'They will not say, 'With a weak rope created, if your people perished because of it.' He also quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Samak.
