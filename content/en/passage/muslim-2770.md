## Summary
The hadith of Umm Zar' related by Aisha in the Book of Repentance, which she tells in full as the story of the slander. Her absence after her necklace fell, the defence of Sa'd ibn Mu'adh and Sa'd ibn Ubada, and the Prophet's recitation of the verses of innocence from Surat al-Nur. It is the longest autobiographical account in the two Sahih works of a single event. {cite:sahih-muslim}
