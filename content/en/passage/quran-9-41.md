**Commentary on verse 41 (p. 262)**

The interpreters differed over the meaning of the "light" and the "heavy" for which God ordered whoever was one or the other to go out with him. Some said the lightness God meant here is youth and the heaviness old age. Report 16734, from al-Hasan, through Ibn Humayd, Hukkam and Anbasa: the old and the young; report 16735, from al-Hasan, through Ibn Waki, Hafs and Amr: elders and youths; report 16736, from Abu Talha, through Ibn Uyayna, Ali ibn Zayd and Anas: on "go forth, light and heavy" he said the middle-aged and the young, and that he had never heard God excuse anyone; he went to Syria and fought until he died. The editor says Ali ibn Zayd is weak in hadith. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 262}
