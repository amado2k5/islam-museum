## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd al-Tawil, from Anas ibn Malik, that Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf came to the Messenger with a yellow mark on him. The Messenger asked, and he said he had married. The Messenger asked how much dower he had given, and he said the weight of a date-stone in gold. The Messenger said: hold a feast, even with a single sheep. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.54}
