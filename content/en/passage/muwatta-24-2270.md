## Summary
Malik transmits from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam bought a slave and freed him, and that slave had sons by a free woman. Al-Zubayr claimed their clientage, and the clients of their mother also claimed it. They brought the dispute to Uthman ibn Affan, who ruled the clientage of the sons for al-Zubayr. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.337}
