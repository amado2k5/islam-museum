**Commentary on verse 67 (pp. 164)**

On "and he said, O my sons, do not enter by one gate but enter by different gates; I cannot avail you anything against God; judgment belongs to God alone; in Him I have put my trust, and in Him let the trusting put their trust", al-Tabari says: Ya'qub said to his sons, when they wished to leave him for Egypt to buy food: O my sons, do not enter Egypt by one road, but enter by different gates. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 164}

We stop here at the foot of p. 164, at the opening of the commentary on verse 67 (Ya'qub's counsel to his sons to enter by different gates); the next batch begins at p. 165. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 164}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
