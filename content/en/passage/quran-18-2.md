## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 3 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Standing firm to warn of a severe punishment from Him and give glad tidings to the believers who do righteous deeds that they will have a good reward": He revealed to His servant the Qur'an in moderation and upright, without any crookedness, to warn you, O people, of a severe punishment from Allah. By hardship, he meant immediate punishment, present punishment, and authority. And His saying: "from Him" means: from Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
