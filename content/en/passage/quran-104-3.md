## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 11 in a single passage. He explains His saying, 'Woe to every hamza with a hint of a hint': He means His mention by saying: 'Woe to every hamzah'—the valley flows from the pus and pus of the people of Hell; 'for every hamza': He says: to every one who backbites people, backbites them, and angers them, as Ziyad al-Ajam said: 'You recite with my own pleasure when you meet me lying, and if I am absent, you are the hamz al-lamzah.' By 'lamzah' he means the one who criticizes people and stabs them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Rabi', and Sufyan.
