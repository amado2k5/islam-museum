## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the segment: "They do not believe in it," meaning they do not believe in the mention revealed to the Prophet — from Qatadah: When they deny, God has chosen in their hearts not to believe in it. And "The way of the ancients has passed": the way of God passed among their predecessors among the polytheists before them, from the people of 'Aad and Thamud and their tribes who denied His messengers and did not believe until God's wrath came upon them and they perished. So your people will not believe in this Qur'an until they see the painful punishment taken from them by the way of those peoples; and from Qatadah: The events of God concerning those before you among the nations.
