## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: "What I have forbidden you, avoid; what I have commanded you, do as much of it as you can". The hadith of the man who asked each year about the pilgrimage shows that one should not ask about what is not needed, or ask to test or mock. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
