## Summary
A saying of Malik regarding a man who has sheep on which no charity is due, and then buys many more sheep or inherits them: charity is not due on all the sheep until a year passes from the day he acquired them by purchase or inheritance; because the livestock a man has on which no charity is due does not count as a threshold of property until each kind of it contains what charity is due on. The threshold is that with which he pays for what his fellow acquired, little or much. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.353}
