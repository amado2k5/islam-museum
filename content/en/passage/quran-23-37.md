## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 36 to 37 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "It is nothing but our worldly life; we die and live, and we are not resurrected." He also said: He says, "It is neither the end nor resurrection." They deny resurrection, saying: "This is only our life, then we die but do not live." These people die and those live. They say: We are like a crop; this one reaps and that one grows. They say: These people die and others come. He recited: "And those who disbelieve said, 'Shall We guide you to a man who will inform you when you are torn apart? Indeed, you are in a new creation.'" He recited: "The Hour will not come to us. Say, yes, by my Lord, it will come to you." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
