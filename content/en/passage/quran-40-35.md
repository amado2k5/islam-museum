## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who dispute about the verses of Allah without authority, arrogance has come upon them with hatred in the sight of Allah and those who believe. Thus Allah seals upon every arrogant and tyrant heart": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of the believer from the family of Pharaoh: "Those who dispute the signs of Allah without authority has come to them." The phrase "those who dispute" is refuted by "who" in the phrase "who is extravagant," and the interpretation of the statement: Thus Allah misleads the people of extravagance and exaggeration in their misguidance by disbelief in Allah and their daring to commit His sins, those who doubt the news His messengers, those who dispute over the arguments that His messengers brought to them to refute falsely as arguments "without authority," meaning they came to them without any authority. He means: without a proof that came to them from their Lord, by which they refute the truth of the arguments brought by the messengers. And "those who," if the meaning of the statement is what we mentioned in the accusative case, in response to "who." In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Mas'ud.
