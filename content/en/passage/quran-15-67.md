## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "And the people of Medina came with good tidings," meaning the people of Sodom, the people of Lot, came when they heard that a guest had been hosted by Lot, rejoicing at their stay in their city, hoping to commit immorality — from Qatadah, who were glad of Lot's guests when they came down because they wanted to bring them evil.
