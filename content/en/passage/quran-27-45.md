## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 45 to 46 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed We sent to Thamud their brother Salih [saying], 'Worship Allah.' But behold, they are two groups disputed": "And indeed We sent to Thamud their brother Salih, saying, 'Worship Allah' alone, with no partner, and do not make with him any other deity. 'Then behold, they are two parties disputing.' He means: When Salih came to them calling them to Allah, his people from Thamud became two groups in what he called them to: one group believing in righteousness and believing in it, and one party denying what he brought. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
