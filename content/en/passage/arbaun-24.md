## Summary
Qudsi hadith from Abu Dharr: God says, "O My servants, I have forbidden injustice to Myself and made it forbidden among you, so do not wrong one another", and describes the needs of His servants and His sufficiency. Ibn Rajab explains God's exemption from injustice, and the distinction between wronging oneself and wronging others. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
