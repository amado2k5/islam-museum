## Summary
The comprehensive qudsi hadith, a report of Abu Dharr from the Prophet, related from his Lord, in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. God forbids injustice to Himself and makes it forbidden among His servants, then the doors of guidance, provision and forgiveness. It is among the greatest texts on justice and mercy. {cite:sahih-muslim}
