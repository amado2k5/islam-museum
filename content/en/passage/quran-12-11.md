**Commentary on verse 11 (p. 568)**

On [[passage/quran-12-11|12:11]] al-Tabari says: Joseph's brothers, having plotted among themselves and agreed to part him from his father Jacob, said to their father Jacob 'father, why do you not trust us with Joseph', leaving him with us when we go out of the city to the desert, 'we are his well-wishers', guarding and protecting him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 568}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
