**Commentary on verse 88: "Eat of what God has provided, lawful and good", pp. 522-523**

Al-Tabari says God tells the believers He forbade to make the good things lawful for them unlawful: eat, believers, of God's provision that He gave you and made lawful, lawful and good. Report 12355, from Ikrima, says it means the food God made lawful for them. "Fear God" means fear going beyond His limits by making lawful what is forbidden to you and forbidden what is lawful, and beware of going against Him, lest His anger come down or you earn His punishment; "in whom you believe" means the One whose oneness you acknowledge and whose lordship you accept.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 523}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
