## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 19 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'This is nothing but clear magic.'" These polytheists from Quraysh said to Muhammad: What is this that you have brought to us except clear magic? He says: It clarifies to those who reflect on it and see that it is a magic: 'When we die and we will be dust and bones? Are we sent again?' They say, denying that Allah sent them after their affliction: 'Are we going to be resurrected alive from our graves after our death, and our destiny is dust and bones, whose flesh has been lost?' He also quotes Qatadah in the passage.
