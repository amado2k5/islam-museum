## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He said, 'Did you see, when We took refuge to the rock, indeed I forgot the whale, and only Satan made him forget it, that I should mention it and take its way into the sea in a wondrous way?'" The young man of Moses said to Moses when he said to him: Give us our lunch so we may eat: Did you see when we take refuge at the rock, for I forgot the whale there, and 'and none made me forget it except Satan,' meaning: 'And the whale made me forget except Satan,' meaning 'to mention it.' This is in the accusative case as a response to the fish, because the meaning of the phrase is: And no one has forgotten me to mention the whale except Satan, who preceded the whale to the verb, and the phrase "to mention it" was replied to, and it is mentioned that this is in Abdullah's Mushaf: "And I have not forgotten him to mention him except Satan." And from Qatadah, Al-Abbas ibn Al-Walid told me: I heard Muhammad ibn Ma'qil narrate from his father that the rock where Moses took refuge was the rock below the Wolf River on the road. "And he took his way by sea in a wondrous way" which amazes him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
