## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 2 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "O Prophet, fear Allah and do not obey the disbelievers and hypocrites. Indeed, Allah is ever Knowing and Wise": "O Prophet, fear Allah by obeying Him, fulfilling His obligatory duties, and His rights upon you, refraining from His prohibitions, and violating His limits." "And do not obey the disbelievers" who say to you: "Drive away your weak followers of the believers until we sit with you." "And the hypocrites" who show you faith in Allah and advise you, yet they do not deceive you, your companions, or your religion. So do not accept their opinion, nor consult them seeking their advice, for they are your enemies. "Indeed, Allah is All-Knowing, Wise." He says: Allah is All-Knowing of what their souls harbor, and what they intend by their showing you advice, along with what they rely on. He is wise in managing your affairs, the affairs of your companions, your religion, and other matters related to all His creation.
