## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Saad ibn Abi Waqqas and Abdullah ibn Umar used to cup while they were fasting. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shaybani (356) transmitted it from Malik, and that Abd al-Razzaq (7540) transmitted it from Mamar from al-Zuhri. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.401}
