## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "We and our forefathers promised this before. Indeed, this is nothing but the legends of the ancients": They said: We have been promised this promise which you promised us, O Muhammad, and the promise of our forefathers before us is a people who mentioned that they were messengers to Allah before you. We have not seen the truth that this one says: What is this that has been promised to us of resurrection after death, except the legends of the ancients? He means: what the ancients recorded in their books of hadiths and reports that have neither authenticity nor truth.
