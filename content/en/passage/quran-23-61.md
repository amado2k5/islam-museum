## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 61 to 62 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And those who give what they give while their hearts are awe, that they will return to their Lord": meaning the Exalted in His saying: "And those who give what they give," and those who give to their rightful shares of charity what Allah has ordained for them in their wealth. "What they give" means: what they give them of charity, and they fulfill God's rights over them in their wealth to its rightful owners. "And their hearts are fearful"—meaning: fearing that they will return to their Lord, so that what they have done will not save them from Allah's punishment. They are afraid of returning to Allah for that, as Al-Hasan said: The believer is the combination of kindness and compassion. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Jurayj.
