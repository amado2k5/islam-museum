## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm, from his father, that he was with Aban ibn Uthman when people of Juhayna and of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj disputed before him over the clientage of the clients of a woman of Juhayna who died leaving property and clients. Her son and her husband inherited her, then her son died. Aban ruled the clientage of the clients for Juhayna. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.339}
