## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because they said to those who dislike what Allah has revealed, 'We will obey you in some matters, and Allah knows their secrets.'" Allah dictated to these hypocrites and abandoned them, and Satan incited them, so he did not grant them guidance, because they "said to those who dislike what Allah has revealed" regarding the command to fight the hypocrites of polytheism: "We will obey you in some matters," which contradicts the command of Allah, the Blessed and Exalted, and the command of His Messenger. Also: And from Qatadah: "That is because they said to those who disliked what Allah has revealed, 'We will obey you in some matter.'" These hypocrites, "And Allah knows their secrets." Allah says: And Allah knows the secrets of these two hypocritical parties, contrary to the command of Allah and His Messenger, as they confront each other by disbelief in Allah and disobedience to the Messenger, and neither this nor any other matter is hidden from Him. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
