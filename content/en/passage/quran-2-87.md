## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "We gave Moses the Book and followed him with messengers", the Book is the Torah, "qaffayna" means We sent one after another, from "qafa" the nape, and "the messengers" are the prophets, meaning that all who were sent after Moses until the time of Jesus were sent to uphold the Torah on his path. On "and We gave Jesus son of Mary the clear proofs", they are what God showed by his hands of proofs, such as raising the dead and healing the born blind; he reports from Ibn Abbas raising the dead, fashioning from clay the likeness of a bird that became a bird by God's leave, healing illnesses and telling of what people stored in their homes. "We strengthened him" means We made him strong and helped him, reported from al-Dahhak, with a verse of al-'Ajjaj. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 317} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 318} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 319}
