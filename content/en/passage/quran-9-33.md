**Commentary on verse 33 (pp. 214-215)**

Al-Tabari says: God, who refuses but to perfect His religion even if those who deny it dislike it, is He who sent His messenger Muhammad with guidance, meaning the explanation of God's obligations on His creation and all that is required of them, and the religion of truth, which is Islam, "to make it prevail over all religion", that is to raise Islam above all creeds, though the polytheists dislike it prevailing. The interpreters differed on the sense of making it prevail over all religion. Some said that this is at the coming out of Jesus, when all the creeds become one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 214}

Report 16645, from Abu Hurayra, through Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Qattan, Shaqiq, Thabit al-Haddad Abu al-Miqdam and an elder: at the coming out of Jesus son of Mary; report 16646, from Abu Ja'far, through Ibn Waki, Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman and Fudayl ibn Marzuq: when Jesus comes out, the people of every religion follow him. Others said the sense is that He teaches him all the laws of religion and lets him know them. Report 16647, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: God makes His prophet master the whole matter of religion, giving it all to him with nothing hidden from him, and the polytheists and Jews disliked that. The editor says Thabit al-Haddad Abu al-Miqdam is Thabit ibn Hurmuz al-Kufi. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 215}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
