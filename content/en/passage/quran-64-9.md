## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “The Day He will gather you for the Day of Gathering - that is the Day of mutual loss and gain. And whoever believes in Allah and does righteous deeds - He will remove from him his misdeeds and admit him to gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever. That is the great attainment,” means: Allah is All-Aware of what you do. “The Day He will gather you for the Day of Gathering” refers to the gathering of all creation for judgment. “That is the Day of mutual loss and gain” means: the gathering is the day when the people of Paradise will be the losers.
