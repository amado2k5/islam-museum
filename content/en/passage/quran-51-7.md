## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "By the heaven, the possessor of your love": and the heaven of good character. And by His saying: 'Dat al-Habak': the one with the paths, and the breaking of everything means its weaving, which is the plural of 'habbak' and 'habikah'. It is said to break the curly hair: 'your love', and for sand, when the still wind or standing water passes through it, and the 'arra' is made of iron: 'He loved you.' From this is the saying of al-Rajiz: 'As if the weaver has made it a breath in its waving, your weaving is a breath in its shavings, the blowing and the sand have removed it.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Hasan, al-Rabi', Sufyan, and Ata'.
