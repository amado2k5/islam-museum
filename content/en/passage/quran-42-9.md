## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 9 to 10 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or do you take allies besides Him? For Allah is the first protector, and He gives life to the dead, and He is over all things competent": Or did these polytheists take allies besides Allah to take them as protectors? "For Allah is the protector," meaning: Allah is the protector of His allies, and let them take them as allies, not gods or idols, nor what has power over them to harm or benefit. "And He gives life to the dead." He means: By Allah, He gives life to the dead after their death, and will gather them on the Day of Resurrection. "And He is over all things competent," meaning: And Allah is capable of reviving His creation after their death and beyond; He is capable of all things. And His saying: "And whatever you differ over is the judgment of Allah," Allah says: And what you differed over over it, O people, and you disputed among yourselves, its judgment is with Allah. He says: Indeed, Allah is the one who judges between you and decides the judgment therein. Also. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
