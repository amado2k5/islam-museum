## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya: Malik said the practice among us is that the obligatory prostrations of the Qur'an are eleven, and none of them is in the mufassal. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik, and that the words "Yahya said" are not in one copy. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.284}
