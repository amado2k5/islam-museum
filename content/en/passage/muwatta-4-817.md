## Summary
A saying of Malik: I heard people of knowledge say: one who broke his fast on a day of making up Ramadan, by having intercourse with his wife in the daytime or otherwise, does not owe the expiation mentioned from the Messenger of God for one who has intercourse in the daytime in Ramadan; he owes only making up that day. Malik said: this is the most beloved to me of what I have heard on it. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (806). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.400}
