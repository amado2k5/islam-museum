**Commentary on verse 79: "They did not forbid one another any wrong they did", p. 496**

Al-Tabari says: these Jews whom God cursed did not stop any wrong they committed, and did not forbid one another; "wrong" means the sins by which they disobeyed God. "How evil was what they did" is God's oath that their conduct was evil in not refraining from His disobediences, committing His prohibitions and killing His prophets and messengers. Report 12313, from Ibn Jurayj, says their souls did not restrain themselves once they had fallen into unbelief.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 496}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
