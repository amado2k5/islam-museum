## Summary
Verse 49 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/al-jadd-ibn-qays-asks-to-stay-behind|Al Jadd ibn Qays Asks to Stay Behind]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 49 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 49 (pp. 286-287)**

Al-Tabari says it is reported that this verse came down about al-Jadd ibn Qays. By "and among them" God means among the hypocrites, "who says give me leave", that is let me stay and not go out with you, "and do not tempt me", that is do not try me with the sight of the women of Banu al-Asfar and their daughters, for I am infatuated with women and would go out and sin by it. The reports agree on this explanation. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 286}

Report 16785, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: the Messenger said fight at Tabuk and you will take the daughters of Banu al-Asfar and the women of Rome, and al-Jadd said give us leave and do not tempt us with women; report 16786 is the same through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj. Report 16787, from Ibn Abbas: "give me leave and do not tempt me", he said it is al-Jadd ibn Qays, who said the Helpers know that when I see women I cannot restrain myself until I am tempted, but I will help you with my wealth. Report 16788, from Ibn Ishaq, from al-Zuhri, Yazid ibn Ruman, Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr, Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada and others: one day, while he was making his preparations, the Messenger said to al-Jadd ibn Qays, brother of Banu Salima, "would you this year fight Banu al-Asfar?", and he said Messenger of God, will you give me leave and not tempt me, for by God my people know no man more taken with women than I, and I fear that if I see the women of Banu al-Asfar I will not restrain myself; the Messenger turned away from him and said "I have given you leave"; and about al-Jadd ibn Qays this verse came down, "among them is one who says give me leave and do not tempt me", that is, if he fears temptation only from the women of Banu al-Asfar, which is not his case, the temptation into which he has fallen by staying behind from the Messenger and preferring himself to the Messenger's self is greater. The editor refers to Ibn Hisham's Sira. Report 16789 begins, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb, and continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 287}
