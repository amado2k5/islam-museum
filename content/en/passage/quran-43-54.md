## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 55 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So his people disliked him and they obeyed him; indeed, they were a corrupt people": Pharaoh belittleed a people among his people from among the Copts, by saying that Allah, the Exalted, informed them that He told them, so they accepted this from him and obeyed him, and they denied Moses. Allah said: They only obeyed and responded to what the enemy of Allah called them to believe in Him and deny Moses, because they were a people who disobeyed Allah by forsaking them and imprinting it on their hearts. Allah Almighty says: "So when they forgave us," He means: they made us angry. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
