## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 53 to 55 in a single segment. He explains His saying: "So be patient; indeed, the promise of Allah is true, and ask forgiveness for your sin, and glorify the praise of your Lord in the evening and in the morning." So be patient, O Muhammad, for the command of your Lord, carry out what He has sent you with the message, inform your people and those you have commanded to convey to him what has been revealed to you, be certain of the truth of God's promise He promised you of your support, and support those who are truthful and believe in you against those who denied you and denied what you brought from your Lord. Indeed, the promise of Allah is a truth with no breaker and it is fulfilled. "And ask forgiveness for your sin," meaning: He granted him forgiveness of your sins and His pardon for you. "And glorify the praise of your Lord," meaning: He prayed with thanks from you to your Lord "in the evening," from the sun's zenith until night, and early morning, from the second dawn until sunrise. The people of Ibkar have directed that it is from sunrise until the rising of Duha and the time of Duha, and the first opinion is known among the Arabs.
