## Summary
Verse 80 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 80 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-80|7:80]], al-Tabari says: We sent Lot; it would also be a possible reading to take it as "remember Lot, Muhammad, when he said to his people". "When he said to his people" means to his people of Sodom, to whom Lot was sent: "do you commit the foul deed", and the foul deed for which God punished them was intercourse between males; "no one in the worlds has preceded you in it" means nobody before them had done it. (14835) from Amr ibn Dinar that no male had been seen on a male until Lot's people. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 548}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
