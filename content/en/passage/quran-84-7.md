## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 10 as a single section. He interprets the words, “O mankind, indeed you are laboring toward your Lord with [great] exertion, and you will meet Him”: O mankind, you are working toward your Lord with an action, and you will meet Him with it: whether that action is good or evil. He says: So let your action be that which saves you from His wrath and earns you His pleasure, and let it not be that which angers Him and causes you to perish. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
