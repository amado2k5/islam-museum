## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains "and the earth We stretched out," meaning he spread it out, and he mentions that it was narrated from Qatadah in another verse, "And the earth after that He spread it," and that he mentioned to them that the mother of the cities, Mecca, was swept away by the earth. And "We cast into it My mountains": mountains firmly on their backs, and he explained the meaning of anchoring in the past. And "We grew therein of everything measured": determined by a known limit—from Ibn Abbas, from Ikrimah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Abu Salih or Abu Malik, and Al-Hakam ibn Utaybah with measure and capability, and from Mujahid with a measure of measure.
