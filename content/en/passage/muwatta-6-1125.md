## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: he heard people of knowledge say that the owner of the offering does not eat any of the penalty or the rite. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1228) and Suwayd (540) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.513}
