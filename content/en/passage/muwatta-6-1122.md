## Summary
Malik transmits from Thawr ibn Zayd al-Dili, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, the same as Said ibn al-Musayyab's view about the camel that is harmed. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1217) and Suwayd (527) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.513}
