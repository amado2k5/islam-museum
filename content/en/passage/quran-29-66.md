## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 66 to 67 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that when God saved these polytheists from the fear of drowning in righteousness, they associated with God, the gods, and the equals to deny what He had given them, meaning to deny His favor in themselves and their wealth, and to enjoy themselves. He mentions two readings: "let them enjoy" with a kasra on the lam among the people of Medina and Basra, meaning to enjoy themselves, and "let them enjoy" its tranquility among the Kufans as a warning and rebuke, so they will know and remind them that God has made a safe sanctuary and kidnaps people around them. {cite:altafsir-tabari} — https://www.altafsir.com/Tafasir.asp?tMadhNo=0&tTafsirNo=1&tSoraNo=29&tAyahNo=66&tDisplay=yes&UserProfile=0&LanguageId=1
