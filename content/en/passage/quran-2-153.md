## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "O you who believe, seek help through patience and prayer; God is with the patient" al-Tabari says the verse is God's exhortation to obey Him and bear what is hard in it on bodies and wealth: seek help through patience and prayer in keeping My obedience and performing My duties, in turning from what I abrogate of My rulings to what I newly give you and submitting to My command, even if something hard reaches you from what your enemies say, from strain on your bodies or loss in your wealth, and in striving against your enemies in My cause, and then fly in whatever grave matter befalls you to prayer. By patience you reach My good pleasure, by prayer your requests are granted, and I am with the patient in performing My duties and leaving My disobediences, helping and watching over them until they obtain what they hoped for from Me. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 213}
