## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He said, 'This is a mercy from my Lord.' So when the promise of my Lord comes, He will make it a miracle, and the promise of my Lord is true": When Dhul-Qarnayn saw that Gog and Magog could not show what had been built of the rubble and could not dig it, he said: This is what I built and made a barrier between this nation, and without the rubble, it is a mercy from my Lord, with which He showed mercy to the people, so He helped me with His mercy to them until I built and leveled it so that I would stop this nation from being angry with them. And His saying: "So when the promise of my Lord comes, He makes it a swell," it means: When the promise of my Lord comes, which He made a time for the appearance of this nation and its emergence from behind this piling for them, He made it a slaughterhouse, meaning: He fixed it to the ground, so he held it to it, from their saying: a she-camel with a flat back, no hump. The meaning of the speech is that He made it stable, so it was said: slaughter. In the verse, he quotes from Qatadah.
