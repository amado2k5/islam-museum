## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd Rabbih ibn Said, from Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham, from Aisha and Umm Salama, the wives of the Prophet, that they said: the Messenger of God would rise in a state of major impurity from intercourse, not from a wet dream, in Ramadan, and then fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab (777), al-Qa'nabi in Abu Dawud (2389), and Ahmad through Ibn Mahdi from Malik from Sumayy and Abd Rabbih ibn Said also transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.390}
