## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So would you like, if you turned away, that you would cause corruption on the earth and sever your kinship": Allah Almighty says to those whom He described as saying that when a decisive surah was revealed mentioning fighting in it, they looked at the Messenger of Allah with the gaze of the unconscious "So could you perhaps", O people? He says: Perhaps if you turn away from the revelation of Allah, Glorified and Exalted be He, and abandon the rulings of His Book, and turn away from Muhammad and what He brought to you, "that you will cause corruption on earth"—meaning: To disobey God on earth, disbelieve in Him, shed blood, and sever your kinships, and return to the state you were in in your ignorance, of dispersion and division after God united you with Islam and united your hearts through it. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
