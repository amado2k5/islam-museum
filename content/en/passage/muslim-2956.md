## Summary
The first hadith in the Book of Asceticism and Softening the Heart of Sahih Muslim: a report of Abu Hurayra, followed by Jabir's report that the Prophet passed by a market while people were around him. It is a concise picture of where the world sits in the believer's heart in the Prophetic text. {cite:sahih-muslim}
