## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Evil women are for the evil men, and the wicked are for the evil women, and the good men are for the good women. Those are free from what they say, forgiveness and a generous provision." The interpreters differed in interpreting this, and some said: Its meaning is: the evil women speak to the wicked among men, the evil men to the evil men in speech, and the good women in the speech of the good people, and the good men to the good men in speech. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
