**Commentary on verse 63 (pp. 370-371)**

On [[passage/quran-11-63|11:63]] al-Tabari says: Salih said to his people of Thamud, 'my people, do you see, if I am on a clear proof from my Lord', that is, on a demonstration and explanation from God which I know and am sure of, 'and He has given me mercy from Him', that is, given me prophethood, wisdom and Islam, 'who will help me against God if I disobey Him?' means who will repel His punishment from me if He punishes me when I disobey Him, and free me from it; 'you would only add to my loss' means by your excuse that you worship what your fathers worshipped you only add to your own loss, through the loss of your share of God's mercy. Report 18285, from Mujahid: you only increase in loss. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 371}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
