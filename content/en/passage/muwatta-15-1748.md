## Summary
Zaynab bint Abi Salama said: then I entered upon Zaynab bint Jahsh, wife of the Prophet, when her brother died. She called for perfume and touched some, then said: by God, I have no need of perfume, but I heard the Messenger say the same. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.113}
