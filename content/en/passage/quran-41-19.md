## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And on the Day the enemies of Allah will be gathered into the Fire and they will be divided": And the Day these polytheists will gather the enemies of Allah into the Fire, into the fire of Hell, they will be confined to the first of them and their end, as in Al-Suddi, "so they will be divided," he said: the first of them will be confined to the last. And from Qatadah, "So they will be divided," he said: They will be divided over the first of them and the last. And His saying: "Until, when they come to it, their hearing and sight testify against them," he means: until when they come to the Fire, their hearing will testify about what they used to hear in this world, listen to it, and their eyes with what they used to see and look at in this world, "and their skins because of what they used to do." It has been said: The term 'skins' here refers to the furs. In the section, he quotes from Qatadah.
