## Summary
He said: I also heard Malik say of someone who bequeaths a bequest and mentions that he had given one of his heirs something he did not take possession of, and the heirs refuse to allow it. That returns to the heirs as inheritance under God's book, because the dead man did not want any of it to fall in his third, and the bequest-holders do not share in his third with any of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.316}
