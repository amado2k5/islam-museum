## Summary
The first book of Faith in Sahih Muslim: Gabriel came in the form of a man with very white clothes and very black hair. He asked the Prophet about Islam, faith, excellence and the Hour, and explained excellence as "that you worship God as if you see Him, and if you do not see Him, He sees you". It appears as Umar's report within the hadith of destiny (Muslim 1) and as a separate report from Abu Hurayra. {cite:sahih-muslim}
