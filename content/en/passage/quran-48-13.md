## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And whoever does not believe in Allah and His Messenger, then indeed, We have prepared for the disbelievers a Blaze." He says: Allah Almighty says to these hypocrites among the Bedouins. Whoever does not believe, O Bedouins, in Allah and His Messenger from among you and others, should confirm what he was informed of and acknowledge the truth he brought from his Lord. Indeed, We have prepared for them all a fire of fire that will blaze against them in Hell when they return it on the Day of Resurrection. Among this is said: 'I burned the fire' when I kindled it, and it is said: 'I set it up as a price.' It is said: 'I also burned it when you stir it.' It is called 'Mas'ar' because it stirs fire, as they say: 'It is a mas'ar harb' (war torch): it refers to its burner and its agitator.
