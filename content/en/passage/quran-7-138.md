**Commentary on verse 138 (p. 80 onward)**

Al-Tabari says: We took the Children of Israel across the sea after the signs We had shown them and the lessons they had witnessed at the hands of God's prophet Moses; yet those signs did not restrain them and those lessons and proofs did not admonish them, so that, despite seeing arguments by which even cattle should have been reminded, they said, when they passed a people devoted to idols of theirs, that is, standing before images of theirs they worshipped besides God: "Moses, make us a god", meaning an image we worship and an idol we take as a god, as these people have idols they worship. Worship is not fitting for anything besides the One, the Overpowering God. Moses said: you are a people who do not know God's greatness and the duty He is owed by you, and do not know that worship is not allowed to anything besides God, to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "devotion" and glosses "images". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 80}

Report 15053, from Ibn Jurayj, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn and Hajjaj: "idols of theirs" means images of cattle; when the Samaritan's calf came, it was made to appear to them to be one of those cattle, and that was the beginning of the affair of the calf. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 80}

It is also said that the people devoted to their idols were some of Lakhm. Report 15054, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Bishr ibn Umar, al-Abbas ibn al-Mufaddal and Abu al-Awwam: "on Lakhm". The editor gives biographies of the chain's transmitters and says of "al-Abbas ibn al-Mufaddal" that he thinks it is al-Abbas ibn al-Fadl al-Ansari al-Waqifi, whose transmissions are discarded. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 81}

It is also said that they were of the Canaanites whom Moses was commanded to fight. Report 15055, from al-Zuhri, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: Abu Waqid al-Laythi said, we went out with the Messenger of God before Hunayn and passed a lote tree; I said, Prophet of God, make this a tree to hang things on for us as the unbelievers have; the unbelievers used to hang their weapons on a lote tree and stay around it. The Prophet said, "God is greatest! This is like what the Children of Israel said to Moses: 'make us a god as they have gods'; you will follow the ways of those before you." The editor explains "lote tree", "hang" and the hanging-things, and says the report is mursal because al-Zuhri did not give its full chain, that al-Tabari gives it by four routes of which this is the first, and its sourcing follows at the next. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 81}

We stop here at the foot of p. 81, in the middle of the commentary on verse 138 (where report 15056, from Abd al-Razzaq, begins); the next batch begins at p. 82.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
