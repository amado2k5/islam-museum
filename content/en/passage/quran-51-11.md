## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "the killing of the Kharasun": curses the speculators who fabricate lies and falsehood and think of it. Interpreters differ regarding those who referred to the phrase "killing the Kharasun," with some saying: it refers to the doubters. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
