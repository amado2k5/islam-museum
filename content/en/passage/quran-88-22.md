## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse in a single passage that includes [[passage/quran-88-21|88:21]] to [[passage/quran-88-26|88:26]] In summary, he interprets "al-Musaytir" as "the all-powerful ruler" who compels them to do as he pleases, and says that God asks His Prophet to entrust them to him. He quotes Ibn Zayd as saying that God does not force them to believe, and that this was abrogated by the verses on fighting, while the reminder remains.
