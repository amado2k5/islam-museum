## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when it is said to them, 'Prostrate to the Most Merciful,' they say, 'And what is the Most Merciful that I prostrate to what you commanded us?'" And when it is said to those who worship besides Allah what neither benefits nor harms them: 'Prostrate to the Most Merciful,' meaning make your prostration to Allah sincere, excluding gods and idols, they say: 'I prostrate to what you command us.' The reciters differed in their reading; the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as: 'Lahma Ta'amurana' meaning: 'Should we prostrate, O Muhammad, when you command us to prostrate to Him?' The majority of Kufa's reciters read it as: 'Lama yu'amurna' with a ya, meaning: 'Should we prostrate to what the Most Merciful commands?' Some mentioned that Musaylimah was called the Most Merciful, and when the Prophet said to them: Prostrate to the Most Merciful, they said: Should we prostrate to what the Most Merciful commands us? They mean Musaylimah prostrating to Him.
