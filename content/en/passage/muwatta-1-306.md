## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, in the chapter on night prayer in Ramadan: Dhakwan Abu Amr, who was a slave of Aisha and freed by her on a deferred arrangement, used to stand and recite for her, that is, lead her in night prayer in Ramadan. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.172} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.173}
