## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Aisha, the mother of the believers, in the chapter on the Prophet's witr prayer: the Prophet prayed thirteen rak'as at night, then when he heard the call to dawn prayed two light rak'as. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i in al-Kubra, Ahmad and al-Tahawi, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.178}
