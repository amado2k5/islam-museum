## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11-13 as a single section. He interprets the words, “And leave Me with the deniers, those of ease and luxury, and give them respite for a little while”: God Almighty means by His words, “And leave Me with the deniers,” meaning, O Muhammad, leave Me with those who deny My signs, “those of ease and luxury,” meaning those who enjoy worldly comforts, “and give them respite for a little while,” meaning, delay the punishment I have decreed for them for a little while until the appointed time arrives. It is mentioned that the time between the revelation of this verse and the Battle of Badr was short. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, and Sufyan.
