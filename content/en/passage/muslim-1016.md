## Summary
A report of Adi ibn Hatim in the chapter urging charity even with half a date, in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim. In its full form God speaks to the servant without an interpreter, until the viewer thinks he looks upon the Fire. {cite:sahih-muslim}
