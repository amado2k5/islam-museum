## Summary
In the Book of Funerals: Jabir ibn Abdullah reports that the martyrs of Uhud were buried two by two in one garment, with the one who knew more Qur'an placed first in the grave, and that they were buried in their blood without washing or funeral prayer. It is among the most firmly established texts on the excellence and rules of the martyrs of Uhud. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
