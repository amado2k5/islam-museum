## Summary
Verse 154 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 154 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says that 'We raised the Mount over them' means the mountain, when they refused to act on what was in the Torah and to accept what Moses brought in it; 'for their covenant' means the pledge and bond they gave God: that they would act on what is in the Torah. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 361}

'We said to them, enter the gate prostrating' refers to the Gate of Hitta, when they were told to enter it prostrate; they entered crawling on their buttocks. 'Do not transgress on the Sabbath' means do not go beyond what is permitted on that day to what is not permitted. Report 10773, from Qatada, says the gate was one of the gates of the Holy House. Al-Tabari says God commanded them not to eat fish on the Sabbath or go out to them, and made lawful for them what was beyond that. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 361}

The readers differ on 'do not transgress'. Most readers of the cities read it with a light ayn, from 'adā fī al-amr, to go beyond the truth in a matter. Some readers of Medina read it with a sukun on the ayn and a shadda on the dal, meaning to commit aggression; the t is assimilated into the d. 'We took from them a firm covenant' means a solemn and strong pledge that they would act on what God commanded and refrain from what He forbade in the Torah. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 362}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
