## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who have believed and those who are Jews, and the Sabians and the Christians and the Magians and those who associated others with Allah, Allah will separate them on the Day of Resurrection. Indeed, Allah is over all things Witness." This means that the separation between these hypocrites who worship Allah by a letter, those who associate partners with Allah and worship idols and idols, and those who are Jews, Sabians, Christians, and Magians who magnified the fire and served it, and those who believed in Allah and His messengers to Allah, and on the Day of Resurrection He will separate them with justice and judgment Entering the fire of all parties and heaven is the believers in him and his messengers, that is the separation from God between them. Qatadah used to say about this: Ma'mar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the verse: "Indeed, those who believed and those who were Jews and the Sabians and the Christians and the Magians and those who associated others with Allah," he said: The Sabians are people who worship the angels, pray to the Qibla, and recite the Psalms. And the Magians: they worship the sun, the moon, and fire. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
