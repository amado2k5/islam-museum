## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 13 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And the Day the Hour is established, the criminals will be paralyzed": "And the Day will come when Allah will separate His creation, and the dead will be gathered from their graves, and will be gathered to the place of reckoning." "The criminals will be exhausted," meaning: Those who associated others with Allah and acquired in this world the worst deeds of every evil will despair, and they will be depressed and regretful, as Al-Ajjaj said: O friend, do you know a fixed script? He said: Yes, I know it, and Iblis. He also quotes in the passage from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
