**Commentary on verse 37 (pp. 534-535)**

Al-Tabari says on "that God may separate the bad from the good, and place the bad one on another and pile them all together and put them in Gehenna; those are the losers" [[passage/quran-8-37|8:37]]: God gathers these who disbelieved in their Lord and spend their wealth to bar from His path to Gehenna to separate between them, they being the people of badness as He called them, and the believers in God and His Messenger, they being the good as He called them; He separated them by lodging the people of faith in His gardens and settling the people of unbelief in His fire. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "bad" and "good". Report 16067, from Ibn Abbas: He separated the people of happiness from the people of wretchedness; report 16068, from al-Suddi: He separates the believer from the unbeliever and puts the bad on one another. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 534} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 535}

By "place the bad one on another" He means He puts the unbelievers one above another, "pile them all together" meaning He makes them a heap, which is joining them to one another until they are many, as He said of the clouds [[passage/quran-24-43|24:43]], meaning gathered and thick; report 16069, from Ibn Zayd: He gathers it all, one on another. "Put them in Gehenna" means He puts the bad all together in Gehenna; the report about them is in the singular because "the bad" is singular, then He said "those are the losers" in the plural and did not say "that is the loser", turning back to the start of the report. By "those" He means those who disbelieved, that is these who spend their wealth to bar from God's path are the losers, those whose bargain is cheated and whose trade is lost, since they bought God's punishment in the hereafter with their wealth and hastened by spending it on fighting the Prophet and the believers shame and abasement. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "loss". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 535}
