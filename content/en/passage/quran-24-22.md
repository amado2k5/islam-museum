## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those of bounty among you and those of wealth do not swear by those who are of favor and the needy, and those who emigrated, in the cause of Allah, and that they may pardon and overlook. Do you not like that Allah should forgive you? And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful": And those of bounty among you do not swear by Allah, meaning those of favor and abundance, meaning: and those of grandmother. The reciters differed in the reading of his phrase: 'wa la ya'tal' (and 'wa la ya'til') as the general recitation of the region: 'wa la ya'atal' meaning 'yaf'al' from the instrument, which is the oath by Allah except for Abu Ja'far and Zayd ibn Aslam, for he mentioned that they read it as 'wa la yata'al' meaning: 'yaf'al', from the instrument. The correct reading in this regard is my reading, according to the reading of those who read: 'wa la ya'atal' meaning 'yaf'aliyah' meaning 'yaf'aliyah' (to be made from the instrument) because this is also in the script of the Mushaf, and the other reading is different from the script of the Mushaf, so following the Mushaf with the recitation of the group and the authenticity of what is recited is preferable to all other things. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
