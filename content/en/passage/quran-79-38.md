## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 41 as a single section. He explains His saying, “And he preferred the life of this world”: He preferred the pleasures of this worldly life over the honor of the Hereafter and what God has prepared therein for His allies. So he worked for this world and strove for it, and neglected working for the Hereafter. In the section, he quotes sayings of Mujahid.
