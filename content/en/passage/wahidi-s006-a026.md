## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-An'am 6:26, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «وهم ينهون عنه وينأون عنه».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">
