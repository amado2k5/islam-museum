## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, that he heard Sulayman ibn Yasar mention that a wound to the face is like a wound to the head, except that if it disfigures the face, blood-money is added for it up to the blood-money of half a head wound, so that it carries seventy-five dinars. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.428}
