## Summary
Verse 16 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 16 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

The readers differed on "he from whom it is turned away on that day": most readers of the Hijaz, Medina and Basra read "yusrafu" with damma on the ya and fatha on the ra, meaning from whom the punishment is turned away on that day; most readers of Kufa read "yasrifu" with fatha on the ya and kasra on the ra, meaning from whom God turns the punishment away on that day. Al-Tabari holds the better of the two readings to be "yasrifu", because the words "he has had mercy on him" show it to be right and show the reading to be one that names the doer; had it been "yusrafu", with the doer unnamed, the right form of "he has had mercy on him" would have been "he has been shown mercy", doer unnamed, and the naming of the doer in "he has had mercy on him" is plain proof that it is so in "from whom he turns away". So the sense is: he from whom God turns away his punishment on that day, God has had mercy on him; "and that is the clear triumph" means by "that" God's turning punishment from him on the day of resurrection and his mercy to him, "triumph" meaning rescue from ruin and attainment of the aim, and "clear" meaning plain to whoever sees it that it is gaining the need and reaching the aim. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 286}

The editor refers for "triumph" to p. 245 and for "clear" to p. 265.

The interpreters said much the same: report 13115, from Qatada: from whom the punishment is turned away. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 287}

Verse 16 closes here at p. 287, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-17|6:17]].
