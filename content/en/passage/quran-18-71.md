## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So they set sail until, when they board the ship, it breaks through." He says, "I have broken it to drown its people. You have come to a command." Moses and the scholar set out, traveling to find a ship to board, until they found it in the ship. When they boarded, the scholar broke through the ship. Moses said to him: "You broke it after we entered the sea, 'to drown its people. You have come with something terrible.' He means: You have come with something great and have done something wrong." And from Qatadah, His saying: "You have come to something, a man": Meaning, how strange, a people stormed their ark and it was torn apart, as if we were in dire need of it. But knowledge of that is what the Prophet Moses did not know from the knowledge of God that He gave him. He said to the Prophet Moses: "If you follow me, do not ask me about anything until I bring you a reminder of it." Muammar informed us, from Qatadah: "You have come to something by command," meaning it is invalid. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah and Ibn Jurayj.
