## Summary
Verse 199 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 199 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 199: who is meant? (pp. 496-497, reports 8376-8378).** Al-Tabari says the interpreters differed over whom this verse refers to. Some said it refers to Ashama al-Najashi (the Negus), and that it came down about him.

**Report 8376.** 'Isam ibn Rawwad ibn al-Jarrah, from his father, from Abu Bakr al-Hudhali, from Qatada, from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, from Jabir ibn 'Abd Allah, that the Prophet said: go out and pray over a brother of yours; he led us in prayer with four takbirs and said: this is al-Najashi, Ashama; and the hypocrites said: look at this man, praying over a Christian foreigner he never saw! and God sent down this verse, [[passage/quran-3-199|3:199]]. In the editor's note, "'ilj" is a man from the unbelieving non-Arabs; 'Isam ibn Rawwad's biography and reliability came earlier; the printed edition adds the name "Ziyad" in his lineage with no basis and the manuscript, rightly, omits it; his father Rawwad ibn al-Jarrah appeared earlier with his weak grading; Abu Bakr al-Hudhali was shown to be very weak; al-Suyuti (2: 113) mentions the report and attributes it to no one but al-Tabari; Ibn Kathir (2: 330) gives it from al-Tabari with a different wording in places and without the start of the chain, so he may have taken it from another place in al-Tabari. The editor says this report is weak, as one sees, and that al-Tabari's remark on p. 499, "it was said: that is a report whose chain has something to consider", is to follow; the weakness is only in this chain for Jabir's report, whereas the basic matter, that the Prophet prayed the funeral prayer over the Negus in his absence, is in the editor's judgement established and sound beyond doubt, transmitted by the two Shaykhs and others from Jabir and from Abu Hurayra, and he refers to al-Muntaqa (nos. 1821-1824).

**Report 8377.** Ibn Bashshar, from Mu'adh ibn Hisham, from his father, from Qatada, that the Prophet said: your brother al-Najashi has died, so pray over him; they said: he prays over a man who is not a Muslim! and the verse came down. Qatada said: they also said that he did not pray towards the qibla, and God sent down the verse of al-Baqara, [[passage/quran-2-115|2:115]].

**Report 8378 (Qatada).** Bishr, from Yazid, from Sa'id, from Qatada on the verse; p. 497 breaks off as the report begins with the word "it was mentioned", and its text continues on p. 498. These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
