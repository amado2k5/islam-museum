## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Then after them came successors who neglected prayer and followed desires, and they will meet oppression": It is narrated after those who were mentioned among the prophets upon whom You bestowed favor and described in this surah, that they left behind evil who left them on earth and neglected prayer. Then the interpreters differed regarding the description of their neglecting of prayer, and some said: Their neglect was delaying it and wasting its time. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Abraham.
