## Summary
Malik transmits from his uncle Abu Suhayl ibn Malik, from his father, that he heard Uthman ibn Affan preach: do not burden a slave-girl with earning, if she has no craft, for if you burden her with that, need will drive her to earn money with her body. Do not burden a young child with earning, for if he finds no work he will steal. Forgive, since God has forgiven you, and eat of the good food that is lawful. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.577}
