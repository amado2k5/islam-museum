## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads this as Abraham's prayer for his parents' forgiveness. His plea for his father's forgiveness rested on a promise he had made, and once it became clear to him that his father was an enemy of God, he disowned him. He reads 'the believers' as those who follow his religion, and the mention of the reckoning as standing for the people who stand before it.
