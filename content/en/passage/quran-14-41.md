## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that this is a prayer from Abraham to his parents for forgiveness and seeking forgiveness for them, and it calls for His saying in Surah At-Tawbah: Abraham's seeking forgiveness for his father was only for a promise he had made to him. When it became clear to him that he was an enemy of God, he disowned him, saying that he was between the time when he disowned him in the past. He explains "and to the believers" as "And to the believers in God who follow him on the religion he follows and obey him in his command and prohibition." And "the Day the reckoning is established": the day people stand for accounting, and they suffice with mentioning the reckoning among the people, as its meaning is understood.
