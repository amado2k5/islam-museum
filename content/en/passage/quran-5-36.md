**Commentary on verse 36: "those who disbelieve, if they had all that is on earth and as much again", pp. 292-293**

Al-Tabari says those who denied their Lord's lordship and worshipped another, whether the Children of Israel who worshipped the calf or others who worshipped idols and statues, and perished so before repenting, even if they had the whole earth's possessions and as much again with it to ransom themselves from God's punishment on the Day of Resurrection, and ransomed themselves with all that, God would not accept it as ransom and exchange for their punishment, but He will punish them with a painful punishment.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 292}

This is God's notice to the Jews who lived near the Prophet's place of emigration that they and the other polytheists are alike with Him as to the painful punishment awaiting them, since they used to say the Fire would touch them only for a few days, deluded about God and lying against Him; He gave the lie to them by this verse and the next and cut off their hope: do not hope, unbelievers, that a ransom will be accepted from you, or that you will leave the Fire through your fathers' standing with Me, if you die in your unbelief; rather repent to God with sincere repentance.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 293}
