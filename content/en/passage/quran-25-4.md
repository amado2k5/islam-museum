## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieve say, 'This is nothing but a fabrication that I fabricated and others helped him against it, but they have come unjustly and in falsehood.'" And these disbelievers in Allah who took gods besides Him said: What is this Qur'an that Muhammad brought to us, 'except a falsehood,' meaning: nothing but lies and slander, 'He fabricated' it and distorted it by saying, 'And other people helped him against it.' It is mentioned that they used to say: Muhammad only knows this which the Jews bring to us. That is His saying: "And other people helped him against it," meaning: and he helped Muhammad against this slander, which the Jews fabricated. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
