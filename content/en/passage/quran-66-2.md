## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “God has ordained for you the expiation of your oaths. And God is your Protector, and He is the All-Knowing, the All-Wise,” explains: God Almighty has clarified for you the expiation of your oaths and defined it for you, O people. “And God is your Protector,” He will protect you with His victory, O believers. “And He is the All-Knowing,” of your interests, “the All-Wise,” in His management of you and His directing you in what He knows best.
