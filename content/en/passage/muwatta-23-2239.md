## Summary
Malik said: the practice among us is that whoever gives his young son gold or silver, then dies while he is in charge of it, the son has nothing of that, unless the father set it aside by its own identity, or handed it to a man and placed it for his son with that man. If he does that, it is valid for the son. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.321}
