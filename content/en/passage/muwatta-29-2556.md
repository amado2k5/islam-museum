## Summary
Malik said: the matter agreed upon among us, on which there is no dispute: when a man strikes another with a stick or throws a stone at him or strikes him deliberately and he dies from it, that is deliberate killing, and retaliation is due for it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.444}
