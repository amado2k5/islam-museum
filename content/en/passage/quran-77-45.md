## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 45 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Woe on that Day to the deniers,” as: Woe to those who deny God’s word concerning what He has told them about His honoring of these righteous people with what He has bestowed upon them on the Day of Resurrection.
