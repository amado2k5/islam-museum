## Summary
Verse 128 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[person/sawda-bint-zama|Sawda bint Zam'a]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:nisa-hawl-alrasul}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 128 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 128, the first clause: a wife who fears ill-treatment (pp. 267-268, notes on pp. 267-268).** Al-Tabari says the sense is that if a woman fears from her husband, meaning she knows it of him, a withdrawal, meaning his raising himself above her to another and preferring someone else, whether from dislike or from displeasure at something about her, such as plainness or age, or a turning away from her with his face or with some of the benefits she had from him, there is no blame on the two of them, that is, on the woman who fears this, if they make a settlement between them. That settlement is that she gives up her day to him, or lowers some of what is owed to her, seeking his kindness and to remain in his marriage and in the contract between them; the settlement by giving up part of the right to keep the bond and the marriage, he says, is better than seeking separation and divorce. He says the interpreters said the same. The editor notes the earlier explanations of the words for the husband, the verb of fearing as knowing, the withdrawal, the turning away and blame, that the print had a frightening reading where the manuscript was not understood, and explains that the word for causes originally means a rope and was later used for whatever links to something. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 268}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
