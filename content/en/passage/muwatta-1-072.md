## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told the Prophet ordered steadfastness, with the reminder that encompassing all deeds is beyond human power, and made prayer the best of deeds, and said that guarding ablution is a sign of faith. The editor explains "Be steadfast and you will not number them" as not turning aside from what is prescribed for them, though they cannot encompass all deeds. Abu Mus'ab transmitted it the same way. The report joins a connected chain from Thawban in al-Tayalisi, Ahmad, al-Darimi, Ibn Majah and Ibn Hibban, and from Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As in Ibn Majah, and its text is sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.73}
