## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 6 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And I feared the mawwali behind me, and my wife was barren, so grant me a guardian from Yourself." He says: 'After me, they will inherit me,' and it is said that by 'from behind me' he meant 'from before me and from before me,' and I have previously explained the permissibility of that. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
