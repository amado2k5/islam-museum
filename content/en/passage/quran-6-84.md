## Summary
Verse 84 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 84 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "and we gave him Isaac and Jacob, each we guided; and Noah we guided before, and of his offspring David, Solomon, Job, Joseph, Moses and Aaron; thus we repay the good-doers" al-Tabari says: so we repaid Abraham for his obeying us, his making his Lord's oneness pure and his parting from the religion of his people who associate others with God, by raising his degree in the highest places and giving him his reward in this world and giving him children we singled out for prophethood and descendants we honoured from ourselves with favour and preferred over the worlds, among them his son Isaac and his son's son Jacob; "each we guided" says: we guided all of them to the way of right guidance and gave them success in the true and right among religions; "and Noah we guided before" says: we guided to the like of what we guided Abraham, Isaac and Jacob to of truth and right, giving success in it to Noah before Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 507}

"And of his offspring David": the pronoun in "his offspring" refers to Noah, because God mentions in the course of the verses following this one Lot, and it is known that Lot was not of Abraham's offspring; since he is joined to the names of those we named of his offspring, it is certain that were Abraham's offspring meant, Jonah and Lot would not be included, while Lot is of Noah's offspring; so the pronoun in "offspring" must refer to Noah. So the sense is: Noah we gave success in the truth and right before Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and we guided also of Noah's offspring David and Solomon. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 507}

The editor refers for "gave", "each" and "offspring" to earlier passages.

David is David ibn Isha, Solomon his son Solomon ibn David, Job is Job ibn Mus ibn Razih ibn Esau ibn Isaac ibn Abraham, Joseph is Joseph ibn Jacob ibn Isaac ibn Abraham, Moses is Moses ibn Imran ibn Yashar ibn Qahath ibn Levi ibn Jacob, and Aaron is Moses's brother. "Thus we repay the good-doers" says: we repaid Noah for his patience in what he was tried with for us by guiding him and giving him success in hitting the truth from which we left alone those of his people who disobeyed us and went against our command and prohibition, and we guided of his offspring after him those of his prophets he mentioned to the like of what we guided him to; and as we repaid these for the goodness of their obedience to us and their patience in trials for us, so we repay with goodness every good-doer. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 508}

The editor says David is called "Yassa" in the people's book and in the earlier commentary "ibn Ishay", and that "Ruh" in print and manuscript should be "Razih", from al-Tabari's history.

Verse 84 closes here on p. 508, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-85|6:85]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
