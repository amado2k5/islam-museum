## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar did not fast while travelling. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani (359) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.398}
