## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Rijal Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman, from his mother Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, that she told him the Messenger said: the sediment of a well is not withheld. The editor notes that the report is mursal in all Malik's narrators, and that Ibn Abd al-Barr cited its connected chain from Abu Qurra from Malik and weakened it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.289}
