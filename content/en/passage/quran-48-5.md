## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "That the believing men and believing women may enter gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding eternally therein, and that will be expiated from them their misdeeds. And that is with Allah a great success." This is: Indeed, We have opened for you a clear victory, so that you may thank your Lord and praise Him for it, and He will forgive you what your past and future sins have passed. And so that the believers may praise their Lord and thank Him for bestowing upon them the conquest He has granted them, which He has granted them and judged between them and their enemies among the polytheists, by showing them to them. Thus, He will admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will remain until Except for the end, and to expiate for them the evil deeds of the good deeds they used to do, as a form of gratitude from them to their Lord for what He has decreed for them, and He bestowed upon them it. "And that was with Allah a great success." He says: And what Allah promised them of this number was that He would admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, and to expiate their bad deeds for the good deeds they did with Allah, for them was a "great success." He says: a victory from them for what they used to do. He also quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas.
