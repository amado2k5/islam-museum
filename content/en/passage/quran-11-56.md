**Commentary on verse 56 (pp. 363-364)**

On [[passage/quran-11-56|11:56]] al-Tabari says: I have put my trust in God, who is my Owner and yours and who stands over all His creation, against your striking me, you and others of creation, with harm; for there is nothing that moves on the earth but God owns it, and it is in His grasp and authority, humble and submissive to Him. If someone asks why 'He holds it by its forelock' singles out the forelock for the taking and not other parts of the body, the reply is that the Arabs used this in describing one they described as humbled and submissive, saying 'so-and-so's forelock is only in the hand of so-and-so', meaning he obeys him and turns him as he wills, and when they took a captive and wished to free him and show him favour they cut his forelock so as to count it a boast over him in boasting; God addressed them with what they knew in their speech. His words 'my Lord is on a straight path' mean my Lord is on the way of the truth, repaying the good-doer of His creation for his good and the wrong-doer for his wrong, wronging none of them at all, and accepting from them only submission and belief in Him. Reports 18278 to 18281, from Mujahid: the truth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 364}
