**Commentary on verse 36 (pp. 94)**

On "and two young men entered the prison with him" al-Tabari says this points to something left out, namely that they imprisoned him and put him in the prison and two young men entered with him, the words relying on this to omit the mention of their putting Joseph in. The two youths were, it is said, two servants of the great king of Egypt, one his cupbearer and the other his baker. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "fata". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 94}

We stop here at the foot of p. 94, at the beginning of the commentary on verse 36 (at the two youths, the cupbearer and the baker); the next batch begins at p. 95. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 94}
