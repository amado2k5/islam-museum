## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if you turn away from them seeking mercy from your Lord that you hope for, then speak to them an easy word": And if you turn away, O Muhammad, from those whom I have commanded you to give their rights if you find a way to them in your face when they ask you, you will not find a way to it, out of modesty and mercy from them. "Seeking mercy from your Lord" means: waiting for provision you await from your Lord, hoping for Allah to make it easy for you. Do not despair of them, but speak to them in an easy manner. He says: But they promised them a beautiful promise, by saying: 'Allah will provide for you, so I will give you,' and similar gentle but not harsh words, as He, the Exalted, said: 'As for the one who asks, do not be repelled.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Ibn Jurayj, and others.
