## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day who will ally with Allah and His Messenger, even if they were their fathers, or their sons, or their brothers or their tribe. Those are the ones in whose hearts faith is inscribed, and He will support them with a spirit from it, and admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever. Allah is pleased with them and pleased with Him. Those are the party of Allah. Indeed, it is the party of Allah who are the successful": meaning, glorified be He, in His saying: "You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day affection for those who oppose Allah and His Messenger." You will not find, O Muhammad, a people who believe in Allah, acknowledge the Last Day, and befriend those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, oppose them, and oppose Allah's command and prohibition. "Even if they were their fathers," meaning: "Even if those who opposed Allah and His Messenger were their fathers, or their sons, or their brothers, or their tribe." Rather, Allah, Glorified and Exalted, informed His Prophet with this verse: "Have you not seen those who turned to a people upon whom Allah is angry?" They are not among the people of faith in Allah or the Last Day. Therefore, They took over those Jews who took them as their guardians. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
