## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And whoever is victorious after being wronged—those are the ones who have no way against them": "And whoever is victorious among those who oppress him after wronging him, they have no way," meaning: those who are victorious among them have no way to overcome them with punishment or harm, because they were victorious from them by right, and whoever takes his right from those who are obligated to do so without transgression has not been wronged, so it will be a way for him. Interpreters differed on what is meant by this; some said: It refers to every victor among those who wronged him, whether Muslim or a disbeliever. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
