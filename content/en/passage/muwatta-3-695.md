## Summary
The first report in the chapter on hoarding (kanz). Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar that he heard Abdullah ibn Umar, when asked what hoarding is, say: it is property from which zakat is not paid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.348}
