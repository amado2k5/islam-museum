## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 90 to 95 together in one place. Al-Tabari explains that Paradise was brought to the righteous who feared God's punishment by obeying Him, and that Hell was revealed to the misguided who stray from the straight path. They are asked, 'Wherever you worshiped, will they help you or will they be victorious?'
