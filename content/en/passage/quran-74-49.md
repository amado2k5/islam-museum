## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46 to 49 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "So what is the matter with them that they turn away from the Reminder?" as meaning: Why do these polytheists turn away from God's reminder to them through this Quran, refusing to listen to it, learn a lesson, and take heed? In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
