## Summary
A report that Malik transmits as a mursal from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Aisha, in the chapter on the two dawn rak'as: the Prophet made the two dawn rak'as so light that Aisha wondered whether he recited the Opening chapter in them. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and that it is connected in the Sahihayn through Yahya ibn Sa'id from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Asad ibn Zurara from Amra from Aisha. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.186}
