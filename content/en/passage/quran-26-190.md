## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 190 to 191 in a single syllable. He explains His saying, "Indeed in that is a sign, and most of them were believers": Indeed, in our torment is the people of Shu'ayb the punishment of the Day of the Shade, by their denial of their prophet Shu'ayb, as a sign for your people, O Muhammad, and a lesson for those who take heed, if they take heed, if they consider that Our custom is against them by denying You. Our custom is with the companions of the pack, "and most of them were not believers" in our previous knowledge about them: "And indeed, your Lord," O Muhammad, "is the Almighty" in his punishment against those who took revenge on His enemies, the "Merciful," by those who repented from His creation and turned to obedience.
