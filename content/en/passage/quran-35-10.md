## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever desires honor, then to Allah belongs all honor. To Him ascends good words and righteous deeds He raises them. But those who plot evil deeds will have a severe punishment, and the plotting of those will be destroyed": The interpreters differed on the meaning of the phrase: "Whoever desires honor, to God belongs all honor." Some said: The meaning is: whoever desires honor by worshiping gods and idols, for all honor belongs to God. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
