## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm, from his father, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, from Aisha, that she told the Messenger of God that Safiyya bint Huyayy had menstruated; he said: perhaps she holds us back; did she not circumambulate the House with you? They said yes; he said: then go out. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.551}
