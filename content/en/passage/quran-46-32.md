## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And whoever does not oblige to call upon Allah is not a miracle on earth, nor will he have protectors besides Him. Those are in clear error": Allah Almighty says, informing about what these people said to their people: 'And whoever is not answered, O people, is the Messenger of Allah, Muhammad, and his caller to what he was sent by calling to Him by calling to Him and acting upon His obedience, He is not a miracle on earth.' He says: He is not a miracle to his Lord by fleeing, if He intends to punish him for denying His caller and abandoning his belief, even if he goes on earth fleeing, because wherever he is, he is under His authority And his fist, 'and he has no protectors besides Him,' meaning: He who does not respond to the call of God besides his Lord has no helpers who will support him from God if his Lord punishes him for his disbelief and denial of his caller.
