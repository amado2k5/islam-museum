**Commentary on verse 91 (pp. 457-459)**

On [[passage/quran-11-91|11:91]] al-Tabari says: Shu'ayb's people said to him, 'Shu'ayb, we do not understand much of what you say', that is, we do not know the truth of much of what you say and tell us, 'and we see you weak among us'; it is reported he was blind, and so they said that. Reports 18507 to 18511, from Sa'id b. Jubayr: he was blind; the editor gives notes on some narrators of the chain and is unsure of the name of one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 457}

Report 18512, from Sufyan: he was weak of sight, and Sufyan said he used to be called the orator of the prophets; 18513, from Sa'id: he was blind. As for 'were it not for your kinsmen we would have stoned you', they mean, were you not among your clan and people we would have stoned you, meaning abused you, and some said, killed you; report 18514, from Ibn Zayd: they said, were we not wary of your people and kin we would have stoned you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 458}

As for 'you are not mighty over us', they mean you are not one honoured among us, whose abasement and humiliation would weigh on us; rather it is easy for us. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 459}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
