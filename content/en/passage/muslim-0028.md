## Summary
A report of Ubada ibn al-Samit in the chapter on calling to the two testimonies and the laws of Islam in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It is the broad testimony that enters through any of the eight gates of Paradise, and it contains the Islamic description of Jesus son of Mary. {cite:sahih-muslim}
