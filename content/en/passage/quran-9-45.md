**Commentary on verse 45 (pp. 275-276)**

Al-Tabari says God says to His prophet: only those ask your leave, Muhammad, to stay behind against you and abandon striving with you without clear excuse who do not believe in God or acknowledge His oneness, "and whose hearts doubt", that is whose hearts are in doubt about the truth of God's oneness and about the reward of those who obey Him and the punishment of those who disobey; "so in their doubt they waver" means they are bewildered in their doubt and waver in the darkness of confusion, not knowing truth from falsehood so as to act with insight, and this is the description of the hypocrites. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 275}

A number of scholars held that these two verses are abrogated by the verse mentioned in the sura of Light. Report 16769, from Ikrima and al-Hasan al-Basri, through Ibn Humayd, Yahya ibn Wadih, al-Husayn and Yazid: "those who believe in God do not ask your leave" up to "so in their doubt they waver" were abrogated by the verse in the sura of Light, [[passage/quran-24-62|24:62]]. Al-Tabari says he has explained abrogation enough not to repeat it here. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 276}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
