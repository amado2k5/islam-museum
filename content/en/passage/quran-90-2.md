## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-7 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "And you are free in this city" (meaning Mecca): God, may His praise be exalted, says to His Prophet Muhammad: "And you, O Muhammad, are free in this city," meaning Mecca. He says: "You are free to do as you please in it, whether it be killing whomever you wish to kill, or taking captive whomever you wish to take captive. This is entirely permissible for you." It is said of this: "It is permissible," "It is lawful," "It is sacred," "It is forbidden," "It is lawful," "It is prohibited," "We have made it permissible," and "We have made it forbidden." In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Sufyan, and Ata.
