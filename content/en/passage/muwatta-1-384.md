## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' from Abdullah ibn Umar, in the chapter on combining two prayers at home and on a journey: when the Prophet was hurried on his journey, he combined the sunset and night prayers. The editor cites Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad, al-Tahawi and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.207}
