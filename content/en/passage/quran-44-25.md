## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 28 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "How many gardens and springs they left behind": How many gardens and springs did Pharaoh and his people leave after their destruction and God's drowning of them of orchards and trees, which are gardens and springs, meaning: the sources of what used to burst forth in their gardens and the crops standing in their fields, and "a noble sanctuary," meaning: a noble, noble place they used to erect. Then interpreters differed about the meaning of God's description of that shrine as generosity; some said He described it so for His honor, because it is the place of kings and princes. They said: It only meant pulpits. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
