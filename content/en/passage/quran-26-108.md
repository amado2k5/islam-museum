## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 108 to 110 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "So fear Allah and obey Him": So fear Allah's punishment, O people, for your disbelief in Him, and obey Me in my advice to you, and I command you to fear Him. "And I do not ask you for any reward for it." He means: I do not ask you for my advice to you, and I command you to avoid Allah's punishment by obeying Him in what He has commanded you and forbidden, whether reward or recompense. "My reward is only with the Lord of the worlds" without you and all of Allah's creation. So fear Allah's punishment for your disbelief in Him, and fear the inflicting His wrath upon you for denying His messengers. And obey: He says: Obey Me in my advice to you, and command you to worship your Creator sincerely.
