**Commentary on verse 20 (p. 173)**

Al-Tabari says this is God's judgment between the groups who boasted, one of the watering, another of the custodianship, and another of belief in God and striving in His way: those who believed in God and affirmed His oneness from among the polytheists, emigrated from the homes of their people and strove against the polytheists in God's religion with their wealth and their selves are higher in rank with God and loftier in standing than the waterers of the pilgrims and tenders of the Sacred Mosque who associate others with God, and these are the ones who triumph, with the Garden, saved from the Fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 173}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
