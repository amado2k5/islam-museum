**Commentary on verse 13 (p. 433)**

Al-Tabari says, on "that is because they opposed God and His Messenger; whoever opposes God and His Messenger, God is severe in punishment" [[passage/quran-8-13|8:13]]: by "that is because they" he means this deed, the striking of these unbelievers above the necks and the striking of every fingertip of them, as repayment for their opposing God and His Messenger and punishment for it. The sense of "they opposed God and His Messenger" is that they separated from the command of God and His Messenger, disobeyed them and obeyed the command of Satan; and of "whoever opposes God and His Messenger" that whoever goes against the command of God and His Messenger and departs from obeying them, "God is severe in punishment" to him; the severity of His punishment of him in this world is His bringing on him the vengeance that used to fall on His enemies and in the hereafter abiding in the Fire of Gehenna, the word "to him" being dropped from the speech because the speech indicates it. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "opposition". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 433}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
