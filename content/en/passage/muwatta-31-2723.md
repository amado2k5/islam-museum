## Summary
It reached Malik from Jabir ibn Abdallah that the Messenger of God said: when a man visits a sick person he wades into mercy, and when he sits with him it settles on him, or something like this. The editor notes that this hadith is relayed (mursal) in the Muwatta; Ma'n ibn Isa transmitted it connected from Aisha, and Ibn Wahb transmitted it connected outside the Muwatta. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.534}
