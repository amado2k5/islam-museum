## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Marwan ibn al-Hakam was brought a man who had snatched goods, and he wanted to cut his hand. He sent to Zayd ibn Thabit asking him, and Zayd said: there is no cutting for snatching. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.405}
