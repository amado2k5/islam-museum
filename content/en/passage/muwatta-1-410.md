## Summary
A ruling of Malik transmitted by Yahya in the chapter on voluntary prayer on a journey: he was asked about voluntary prayer on a journey, and said there is no harm in it by night or by day, and that he had heard that some people of knowledge did so. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.214}
