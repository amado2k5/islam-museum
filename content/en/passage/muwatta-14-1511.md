## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, from Abd al-Malik ibn Abi Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham al-Makhzumi, from his father: when the Messenger of God married Umm Salama and spent the morning with her, he said to her: there is no humiliation for you with your family; if you want, I will stay with you seven nights and with them seven, and if you want, I will stay with you three nights and turn to them. She said: three. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.34}
