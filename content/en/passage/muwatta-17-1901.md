## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Salih ibn Kaysan, from al-Hasan ibn Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, that Ali ibn Abi Talib sold a camel of his called Asayfir for twenty camels, on a term. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.180}
