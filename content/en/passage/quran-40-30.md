## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 31 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And he who has believed said, 'Indeed, I fear for you the day of the Confederates'": The believer from Pharaoh's family said to Pharaoh and his people: "O my people, I fear for you by killing Moses if you kill him like the Day of the Confederates when they were united against the messengers of Allah, Noah, Hud, and Salih." But Allah destroyed them because of their audacity against him, so He will destroy you as He destroyed them. And His saying: 'The example of the people of Noah' means: He will do that to you and destroy you like His way with the people of Noah, 'Aad, and Thamud, and His treatment of them. We have explained the meaning of 'diyā' in what has been mentioned with its evidences, which do not require repeating it, along with the opinions of the interpreters on it. And he said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas: 'Like the habit of the people of Noah,' meaning: like the condition of the people. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase: 'Like the habit of the people of Noah,' he said: like what befell them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
