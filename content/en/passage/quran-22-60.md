## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is for whoever is punished with the like of what he was punished for and then oppressed him, Allah will surely help him. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Forgiving": He means that He mentions Him by saying: "That is for those who emigrated in the cause of Allah, then were killed or died. And they also have the same reason that Allah promises them victory over the polytheists who transgressed against them and expelled them from their homes." Also: He said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj: "That is the one who is punished with the same as he was punished," he said: They are the polytheists who transgressed against the Prophet, so Allah promised to support him, and he also said about retribution.
