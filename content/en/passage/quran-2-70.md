## In al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says "make clear to us what it is" reports a third instance of their ignorance: had they slaughtered any cow to hand it would have sufficed, and once the age was given any cow of that age would have done; since they insisted on having it defined by its marks, God made it hard for them because of their many questions and their disputing with their prophet. He cites a saying he gives from the Prophet, "Leave me as I leave you; those before you were destroyed only by their many questions and their disagreeing with their prophets", and the editor notes it is originally from Abu Hurayra and that the wording here is faulty. He then reports from Ibn Abbas, 'Ubayda and Ikrima that had they taken any cow it would have sufficed, but they pressed and God pressed them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 203} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 204}
