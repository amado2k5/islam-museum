## Summary
Malik thinks that in an ostrich egg there is a tenth of the price of a camel, as in the foetus of a free woman there is a slave's indemnity, male or female; the value of the indemnity is fifty dinars, which is a tenth of its mother's blood-money. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.555}
