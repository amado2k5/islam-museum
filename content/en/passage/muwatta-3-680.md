## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that he bought property for his brother's sons, who were orphans in his care, and that property was later sold for a large amount of money. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.342}
