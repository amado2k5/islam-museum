## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not rush your cheek before people and do not walk on the earth jokingly. Indeed, Allah does not love every boastful and deceitful person": Reciters differed in reciting the phrase "Do not be arrogant," and some reciters from Kufa, Medina, and Kufan read it as: "Do not be arrogant" as an example of 'taf'al'. Some Meccan scholars and the general reciters of Medina, Kufa, and Basra read this as "wa la ta'sa'ar" as an example of interaction. The correct view is to say that these are two readings, each recited by scholars among the reciters, so whichever reciter is correct. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
