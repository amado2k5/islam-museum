## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We did not send before you any messenger or prophet except that when he wished, Satan casts into his wish. Then Allah abrogates what Satan casts. Then Allah judges His verses. And Allah is Knowing and Wise." It is said: The reason this verse was revealed to the Messenger of Allah was that Satan cast on his tongue some of what Allah had revealed to him of the Qur'an that Allah had not revealed to him, so this intensified the Messenger of Allah and he was distressed by it, so Allah forgave him for what he had done With these verses. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
