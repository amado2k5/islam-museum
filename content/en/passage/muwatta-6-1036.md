## Summary
Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Maryam that he asked Said ibn al-Musayyab about a nail that broke off for a man in ihram; Said said: cut it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1196) and Suwayd (581) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.482}
