**Commentary on verse 57 (pp. 152)**

On "and the reward of the Hereafter is better for those who believed and were mindful of God", al-Tabari says: the reward of God in the Hereafter is better for those who believed God and His messenger than what Joseph was given in this world by being established in the land of Egypt; "and were mindful" means they were mindful of God, fearing His punishment for going against His command and treating His prohibitions as lawful, and so they obey Him in what He commands and forbids. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 152}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
