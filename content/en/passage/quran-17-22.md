## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Do not make any other god with Allah and remain blameworthy and forsaken": Do not make Muhammad a partner with Allah in His divinity and worship, but be sincere in worship to Him and dedicate to Him, for there is no god but Him. If you place a god with Him and worship others besides Him, you will remain blameworthy, as if you are blamed for what you have wasted of thanking Allah for the blessings He has bestowed upon you, and you will be thanked for others who have given you kindness, and in associating in praise those who did not share the blessing upon you. Others, disappointed, your Lord has handed you over to those who wrong you unjustly, and if your Lord, who is the helper of His allies, surrenders you, you will have no protector besides Him to support and defend you. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying, 'Do not place any other god besides Allah and then sit in blame and forsaken,' means: blamed for the blessing of Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
