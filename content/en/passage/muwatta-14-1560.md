## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abdallah and al-Hasan, the sons of Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, from their father Ali ibn Abi Talib, that the Messenger forbade temporary marriage on the day of Khaybar, and the eating of the flesh of domestic donkeys. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.50}
