**Commentary on verse 21 (p. 174)**

Al-Tabari says: their Lord gives these who believed, emigrated and strove in God's way the good news of mercy from Him, that He has had mercy on them by not punishing them, and of His good pleasure, that He is pleased with them for their obedience and fulfilling what He charged them with, and of gardens in which they will have lasting bliss that does not pass or perish but stays forever. Report 16567, from Jabir ibn Abdullah, through Ibn Bashshar, Abu Ahmad al-Zubayri, Sufyan and Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir: when the people of Paradise enter it, God says I give you better than this; they ask what could be better, and He says My good pleasure. The editor notes that this report has come with its chain and wording earlier, where its text was corrected. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 174}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
