## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 62 to 64 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And He has certainly led astray among you many mountains. Would you not then reason": He means His mention by saying: "And indeed, He has led astray among you many mountains": And Satan has certainly averted many of you from my obedience and deity until they worshiped him and took gods besides me, as reported by Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "And indeed, He has led astray among you a mountain," he said: creation. Reciters differed in its reading; the majority of Medina reciters and some Kufans read it as "Jabla" with a kasra on the jīm and a shadda on the lam. Some Meccan scholars and the general reciters of Kufa read it as "Jabla" with a damma on the jim and ba, and a lightening of the lam. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid.
