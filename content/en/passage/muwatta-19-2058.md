## Summary
Malik says: the sunna in irrigation partnership among us is that it applies to the root of every palm, vine, olive, fig, pomegranate, date-plum, or similar roots: it is lawful and not harmful, on condition that the owner has half the fruit, or a third, a quarter, or more, or less. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.243}
