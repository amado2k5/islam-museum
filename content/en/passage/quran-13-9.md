**Commentary on verse 9 (p. 366)**

On "Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Great, the Exalted", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: God is the Knower of what is absent from you and from your sight, which you have not seen, and what you have witnessed and seen with your eyes; nothing is hidden from Him, because they are His creation and His ordering. "The Great" is the One beneath whom everything is, and "the Exalted" is the One who is above everything by His power; it is the form "mutafa'il" from "'uluw", like "mutaqarib" from "qurb" and "mutadani" from "dunuw". The editor refers to earlier discussions of these terms. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 366}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
