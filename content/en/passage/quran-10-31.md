**Commentary on verse 31 (pp. 83-84)**

On [[passage/quran-10-31|10:31]], al-Tabari says God tells His Prophet to say to these polytheists: who provides for you from the sky the rain, brings up its sun for you, darkens its night and brings out its morning light, and from the earth your sustenance and the food it grows for you and the fruit of its trees? Or who owns your hearing and sight, able to strengthen them or take them away so that you become deaf and blind? And who brings the living thing out of the dead and the dead thing out of the living? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 83}

He adds that he has already given the views of the interpreters on this, and his own choice with its proofs, in the commentary on Surat Al Imran, which spares repeating it here. The editor refers to that earlier place. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 83}

"And who directs the affair?": say to them, who directs the affair of the heaven and the earth and what is in them, your own affair and the affair of creation? "They will say, God": they will soon answer that the one who does all this is God. "So say, will you not be mindful?": will you not fear God's punishment for your associating and for claiming a lord other than the one with these attributes, and for worshipping beside Him one who gives you no provision, owns no harm or benefit for you and does nothing? The editor refers to the earlier gloss on directing the affair. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 84}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
