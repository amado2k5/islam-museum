## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 6 as a single unit. He explains the phrase “I do not swear by the Day of Resurrection”: The reciters differed in their reading of the phrase “I do not swear by the Day of Resurrection.” The majority of reciters in the various regions read it as “I do not swear,” with “la” separated from “aqsim,” except for al-Hasan and al-A’raj, who, it is reported, read it as “la-uqsimu bi-yawmi al-qiyamah,” meaning: I swear by the Day of Resurrection, then the “lam” of the oath was added. The reading that I consider permissible in this context is “la” separated, with “aqsim” beginning a new clause, as is the reading of the major reciters, due to the consensus of the authoritative reciters on this point. Those who read it in the way we have chosen to interpret it differed. Some said that "no" is redundant, and that the meaning of the statement is: I swear by the Day of Resurrection. The passage quotes statements from Qatadah, al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
