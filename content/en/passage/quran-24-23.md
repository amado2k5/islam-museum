## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, those who accuse chaste, unaware believing women are cursed in this world and the Hereafter, and for them is a great punishment": "Indeed, those who accuse" immorality "chaste women" means chaste "heedless" women, "believing women" in God and His Messenger, and what has been brought from God, "are cursed in this world and the Hereafter," meaning: "They are kept away from God's mercy in this world and the Hereafter." "And for them" in the Hereafter is a great punishment, which is the punishment of Hell. The interpreters differed regarding the chaste women for whom this ruling applies. Some said: This is only for Aisha, and it is a ruling from Allah regarding her and those who threw her, excluding the other women of the Prophet's nation. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
