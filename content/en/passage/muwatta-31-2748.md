## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, from Zafar ibn Sasaa ibn Malik, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that when the Messenger of God finished the dawn prayer he would say: has any of you seen a dream last night? He said: nothing of prophethood remains after me except good dreams. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.546}
