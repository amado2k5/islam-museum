## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra at the start of the Book of Fasting in Sahih Muslim. In another wording, "the gates of mercy are opened, the gates of Hell are closed, and the devils are chained": a cosmic picture of the fasting season. {cite:sahih-muslim}
