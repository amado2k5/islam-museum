## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 0-3 as a single phrase. He interprets the phrase “that the blind man came to him” as meaning “because the blind man came to him.” It has been reported that some reciters lengthened the alif in “that he came to him,” saying “that he came to him,” as if the meaning was: “Because the blind man came to him, he frowned and turned away?” Similarly, some reciters read “that he was a man of wealth and sons” with a lengthened or shortened alif in “that he came to him.” It has been mentioned that the blind man mentioned by God in this verse was Ibn Umm Maktum, for whom the Prophet was reproached. In this passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
