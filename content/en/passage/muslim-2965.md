## Summary
A report of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in the chapter on God loving the pious, hidden, rich servant, in the Book of Asceticism and Softening the Heart of Sahih Muslim. The context is Sa'd's answer to his son, who blamed him for leaving people to fight over power. {cite:sahih-muslim}
