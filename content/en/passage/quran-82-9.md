## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 13 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Nay, but you deny the religion” as follows: “O disbelievers, the matter is not as you claim, that you are right in worshipping other than God, but rather you deny reward and punishment, recompense and reckoning.” In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
