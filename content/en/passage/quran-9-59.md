**Commentary on verse 59 (p. 304)**

Al-Tabari says: if these who find fault with you, Muhammad, over the alms had been content with what God and His Messenger gave them and the share assigned to them, and said "God is enough for us", meaning He suffices us, "God will give us of His bounty, and His Messenger from the alms and otherwise, and to God we turn in longing", that is, we long that God widen His bounty on us and make us need neither the alms nor the gifts of people nor having to turn to them. The editor refers to earlier explanations of sufficiency, giving and bounty. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 304}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
