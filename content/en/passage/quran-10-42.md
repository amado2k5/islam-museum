**Commentary on verse 42 (p. 95)**

On [[passage/quran-10-42|10:42]] he says to His Prophet: among these polytheists are some who listen to what you say; "can you make the deaf hear, even if they do not understand?" means, will you create hearing for them, when they have no hearing with which to understand, or will I? This is an announcement from God to His servants that success in belief is in His hand and no one else's: as you cannot make hear one whose hearing He has taken, so you cannot make understood My command and prohibition to a heart from which He has taken that understanding, for He has sealed that it will not believe. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 95}
