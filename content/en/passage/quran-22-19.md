## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 22 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "These are two adversaries who disputed about their Lord, so as for those who disbelieve, garments of fire will be cut off from their heads, from whose heads hot hot is poured": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of these two adversaries mentioned by Allah. Some said: One group is the people of faith, and the other group is the idol worshippers from the polytheists of Quraysh who fled on the Day of Badr. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ata.
