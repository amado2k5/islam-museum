## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if two groups of believers fight, then reconcile between them. But if one of them transgresses against the other, then fight the one who seeks until it fulfills the command of Allah. But if it does, then reconcile between them with justice and be just. Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly." And if two groups of believers fight, then reconcile between them by calling to the ruling of the Book of Allah and being content with what is for them and against them. That is reconciliation between them through justice: "If one of them oppresses the other." He says: If one of these two groups refuses to comply with the ruling of Allah's Book for him, and accordingly transgresses what Allah has made just among His creation, and the other answers: "So fight the one who transgresses," meaning: fight the one who transgresses, and refuses to respond to Allah's judgment "until you return to Allah's command," meaning you return to Allah's judgment which He judged in His Book among His creation. "But if you refuse, then reconcile between them with justice," meaning: If the rebel returns after you fight them to the satisfaction with God's judgment in His book, then reconcile it with the other group it fought. In the passage, statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd are quoted.
