## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "to God belong the East and the West, so wherever you turn there is the face of God", al-Tabari says: to God belong their dominion and ordering, as one says "this house is so-and-so's", meaning it is his possession; so the sense is that they are His in dominion and creation. "Mashriq" is the place of sunrise. Asked whether God has only one East and one West, he answers that the meaning is: to God belong the East from which the sun rises each day and the West in which it sets each day, that is what lies between the two ends of the East and between the two ends of the West, since the sun rises each day from a place to which it returns only after a year. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 526}
