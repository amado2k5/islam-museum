## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And on the Day We will raise from every nation a witness, then those who disbelieved will not be permitted to do so," meaning they will recognize God's favor and then deny it today and denounce "the Day We will raise a witness from every nation as a witness," which is the witness to what they answered God's call, and He is their Messenger sent to them. "Then those who disbelieved will not be permitted to make excuses, so they will excuse themselves from what they used to deny by God and His Messenger." "Nor do they seek refuge," so they abandon returning to the world and repent, as Allah Almighty said: "This is a Day when they will not be pronounced nor permitted to be excused." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
