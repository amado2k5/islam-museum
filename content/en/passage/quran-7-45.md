## Summary
Verse 45 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 45 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "those who bar from God's path and seek to make it crooked, and in the Hereafter they disbelieve": the announcer between the people of Paradise and the Fire says that God's curse is on the wrongdoers who disbelieved in God and barred from His path, "and seek to make it crooked", meaning they tried to change God's path, which is His religion, and alter it from the straightness God gave it, "and in the Hereafter they disbelieve", meaning they deny the coming of the Hour, the resurrection, and reward and punishment in the Hereafter. The Arabs call inclination in religion and in a road "iwaj" with kasra on the ayn, whereas what is a bodily shape in a person is called "awaj" with fatha, and he cites a line whose poet is unknown. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 448}
