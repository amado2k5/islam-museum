## Summary
It reached Malik that the Messenger of God used to say: no, by Him who turns the hearts. The editor notes that this report is supported by a sound route from Musa ibn Uqba from Salim from his father, and that it is in al-Bukhari. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.617}
