## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And not equal are the blind and the seeing, and those who believe and do righteous deeds, nor the evil touched by them a little, you do not remember": nor is the blind person who sees nothing, and he is like the disbeliever who does not contemplate God's proofs with his eyes, so he reflects on them and takes them into consideration, knowing His oneness and ability to create whatever He wills, believing in them, and being truthful. And the all-seeing one who sees with his own eyes what is prescribed for them and sees with him. This is like the believer who sees with his own eyes the proofs of Allah, reflects on them, takes heed, and knows what they indicate regarding the oneness of His Maker, the greatness of His authority, and His ability to create whatever He wills. He, Glorified and Exalted, says: Thus the disbeliever and the believer are not equal.
