## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24-25 as a single unit. He interprets the words, “Say, ‘It is He who has created you on the earth, and to Him you will be gathered’”: Say, O Muhammad, God “who has created you on the earth” means: God who created you on the earth, “and to Him you will be gathered” means: and to God you will be gathered, and you will be assembled from your graves for the Day of Reckoning.
