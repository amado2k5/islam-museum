## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or did you take intercessors besides Allah? Say, 'Were they those who possessed nothing and did not understand?": Or did these polytheists take besides Allah their gods whom they worship intercessors to intercede for them with Allah in their needs? And His saying: "Say, 'Or if they possess nothing and do not understand,' Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: Say, O Muhammad, to them: Will you take these gods as intercessors as you claim, even if they had no power to benefit or harm you, nor could they understand anything? Say to them: If you worship them for that and intercede for you with Allah, then dedicate your worship to Allah and dedicate it to divinity, for all intercession is his; only the one who is permitted and is pleased with in word, intercedes for Him. And when you dedicate your worship to Him, call upon Him, and He will intercede for you." To Him belongs the Sovereign of the heavens. And the earth," he says: He has dominion over the heavens and the earth and dominion over them, and what do you worship, O polytheists, besides Him has a dominion to Him, saying: Worship the dominion, not the one who is the king, and in the passage he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
