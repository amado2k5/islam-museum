## Summary
Verse 134 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 134 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 134, "what you are promised is sure to come, and you cannot frustrate it", al-Tabari says to the idolaters who set idols and statues equal to God: what your Lord threatens you with of His punishment for persisting in your unbelief will befall you; "you cannot frustrate it" means you will not defeat your Lord by fleeing from Him in the earth so as to escape Him, for wherever you are you are in His grip, and He is able over you and over punishing you for your disobedience to Him; so beware of Him and turn back to obeying Him before the calamity comes down on you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 128}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
