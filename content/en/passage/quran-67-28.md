## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Say, ‘Have you considered: if Allah destroys me and those with me, or has mercy on us, then who can protect the disbelievers from a painful punishment?’” explains: “Say,” O Muhammad, to the polytheists among your people, “Have you considered,” O people, “if Allah destroys me,” meaning if He causes me to die, “and those with me, or has mercy on us,” meaning if He extends our lives, “then who can protect the disbelievers” from a painful punishment, namely, the punishment of Hellfire? He says: Neither our death nor our life will save the disbelievers from Allah’s punishment. Therefore, there is no need for you to hasten the coming of the Hour and the descent of the punishment, for that will not benefit you; rather, it will be a great calamity for you.
