## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab used to go out to the prayer ground after praying the dawn prayer, before sunrise, in the chapter on leaving off prayer before and after the two festivals. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.255}
