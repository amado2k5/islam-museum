## Summary
A report from al-Zuhri on Abu Hurayra in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It gives the context of Abu Bakr's fight against those who withheld zakat in the wars of apostasy and Umar's persuasion. It is a comprehensive text on judging by the outward and the obligation of zakat. {cite:sahih-muslim}
