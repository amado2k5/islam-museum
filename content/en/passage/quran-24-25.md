## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "On that Day, Allah will fulfill their true debt and they will know that Allah is the Clear Truth": "On the Day their tongues, hands, and feet will bear witness against them for what they used to do." Allah will fulfill their reckoning and recompense them with the truth for their deeds. And religion in this context: reckoning and reward, as Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "On that Day, Allah will repay their debt with the truth," meaning: their reckoning. Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase "the truth," and the majority of the reciters of the cities read it as "their true religion" as an accusative for religion, as if he said: God will repay them their deeds as truth. Then the definite article was added to the definitive and the lam, accusative with the accusative of religion. It was mentioned from Mujahid that he read that: "Allah will fulfill their true debt" by raising the case of "haqq" as if it were attributed to God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
