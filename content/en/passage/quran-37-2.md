## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 1–3. Al-Tabari mentions that God swore by the 'Saffat,' the 'Zajirat,' and the 'Tala'at' by mention, and that 'al-Saffat' are the angels who swear by their Lord in the heavens, as well as the 'Zajirat' following by the interpreters, and that the oath is that your God belongs to one.
