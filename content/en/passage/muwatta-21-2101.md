## Summary
Malik says: there is no pre-emption among us for a slave, a slave-girl, a camel, a cow, a sheep, or any animal, nor for cloth, nor for a well with no open land. Pre-emption applies only to what can be divided and in which boundaries of land are set. What cannot be divided has no pre-emption. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.258}
