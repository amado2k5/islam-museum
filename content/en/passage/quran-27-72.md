## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 71 to 72 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, perhaps some of those you are hastening have come close to you." And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "Perhaps it will be a hurry for you," he said: "Perhaps it will be a hurry for you." He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "Say, perhaps it is a repelling for you some of what you are in a hurry," he said: It is a warning. I narrated from Al-Husayn, he said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say regarding the phrase: "A repelling for you," meaning he has come near to you. The Arabs differed regarding the inclusion of the lam in the phrase: "Radaf lakum," and the well-known Arabic saying: 'Radaf is an imperative, and he followed it, as it is said: he followed and followed him. Some grammarians from Basra said: insert the lam in that, and added the verb to it, as it is said: for dreams you interpret, and for their Lord they fear.' In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
