## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the segment: "Their garments" are their garments they wear, singular sarbal, citing a verse by Imru' al-Qais, and from Ibn Zayd al-sarbil al-qams. And "min qtaran": the tar with which camels are comforted — from al-Hasan al-Khadkhaddh means the joy of camels and qatran of camels — and it has dialects: qatran with a kasra on the qaf, qatran with a fatha and a sukoon on the tā. It is said that Isa ibn Umar read "qatrān" with a kasra on the qaf and a sukun on the tā, and two verses by Abu al-Najm are cited with kasra. Some said: Qatran is copper — from Mujahid and Ibn Abbas through the route of Ibn Jurayj. And "their faces are covered by fire": their faces are covered by fire, so that nothing is seen of them except what covered them.
