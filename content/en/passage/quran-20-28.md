## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 34 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed": "Go," O Moses, "to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." He means he has transgressed and rebelled against his Lord. We have explained the meaning of transgression with what has passed and made it no longer be necessary to repeat it. In this place and in the passage, it is omitted, and it is sufficient for the listener to understand what was mentioned from him, which is: "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." Invite him to the oneness of God and obey Him, and to send the Children of Israel with you. He quotes in the passage from Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Zayd.
