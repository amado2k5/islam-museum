## Summary
Malik says: it is not lawful for a slave to buy himself from his master on the condition that he may take as client whomever he wishes, because clientage belongs to the one who frees. Even if the master permitted his freedman to take as client whomever he wished, that would not be lawful; and if it were lawful for the master to stipulate it or permit it, that would be a gift of clientage, which the Prophet forbade. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.336}
