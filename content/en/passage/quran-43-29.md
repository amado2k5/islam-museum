## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 30 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Rather, I enjoyed these and their forefathers until the truth came to them and a clear messenger": "Rather, I enjoyed," O Muhammad, these polytheists from your people and their forefathers before them with life, so I did not hasten to punish them for their disbelief "until the truth came to them," meaning, glorified be He, with the truth: This Qur'an says: I did not destroy them with punishment until I revealed the Book to them and sent among them a clear messenger.
