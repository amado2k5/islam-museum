## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that food or drink, even medicine, was never brought to him without his saying, when he ate or drank it: praise be to God who guided us, fed us, gave us drink and blessed us; the prayer is completed with asking for the completion of the blessing, thanks for it, and blessing in what is provided. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.524}
