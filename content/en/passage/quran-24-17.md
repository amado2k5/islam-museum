## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah admonishes you to return to the like of Him forever, if you are believers": Allah reminds you and warns you with the verses of His Book, so that you do not return to the same act you did regarding Aisha, from receiving the slander that was narrated against her with your tongues, and saying with your mouths what you have no knowledge of. "If you are believers" means: if you take heed of Allah's admonitions, obey His command, and refrain from what He has forbidden you. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
