## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 88 to 89 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, in whose hand is the dominion of all things, while he protects and is not protected over it, if you know": Say, O Muhammad: In whose hand are the treasures of all things? Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding God's saying: "The dominion of everything," he said: the treasures of everything. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Mujahid regarding the saying of Allah: "Say, in whose hand is the dominion of all things," he said: the treasures of everything. And His saying: "And He will defend" is the one who intends evil from those who intend it. "And no one is protected against him" means: no one withholds from the one whom He intends evil and removes His punishment and punishment. "If you know" is among his attributes, for they say: The dominion of all things and the power over all things belong to Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
