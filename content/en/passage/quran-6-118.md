## Summary
Verse 118 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 118 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "so eat of that over which the name of God has been mentioned, if you believe in His signs", God says to His prophet Muhammad and to His servants who believe in Him and His signs: so eat, believers, of what you have slaughtered of your sacrificial animals, in the slaughter I showed you makes the animal lawful for you; that is, what was slaughtered by those who believe in Me among the people of your religion, the religion of truth, or by those of the People of the Book who adhere to My oneness, and not what was slaughtered by idolaters or by those without a book, such as the Magians. "If you believe in His signs" means: if you accept as true the proofs and signs of God that have come to you, in His permitting what He permitted and forbidding what He forbade of foods and eatables; and leave aside the embellishment which the devils inspire to one another in adorned speech to you, and the muddying of your religion for you by deception. Report 13790, from Ata' (through Ibn Jurayj): He commands mentioning His name over drink, food and slaughter, and He commands everything that indicates mentioning Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 67}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
