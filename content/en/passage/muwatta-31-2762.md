## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, from Anas ibn Malik, that he heard Umar ibn al-Khattab, when a man greeted him, return the greeting, then asked the man: how are you? He said: I praise God to you. Umar said: that is what I wanted from you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.551}
