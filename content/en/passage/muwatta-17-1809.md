## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Rijal Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Harithah, from his mother Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, that the Messenger forbade selling fruit until it is safe from blight. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.141}
