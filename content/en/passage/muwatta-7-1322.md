## Summary
Malik transmits from Thawr ibn Zayd al-Dili, from Abu al-Ghayth Salim, client of Ibn Mutiyy, from Abu Hurayra, that he said: we went out with the Messenger of God in the year of Khaybar, and we did not win gold or silver, only goods, clothes and property. Rifa'a ibn Zayd gave the Messenger of God a black slave called Mid'am; he was sent to Wadi al-Qura; while Mid'am was unloading the Messenger of God's saddle, a stray arrow came and killed him. The people said: happiness for him, Paradise! The Messenger of God said: no, by Him in whose hand my soul is; the cloak he took on the day of Khaybar from the booty, which had not been divided, is burning on him with fire. When the people heard that, a man came with a thong or two, and said: a thong or two of fire. Ibn Abd al-Barr did not know it traced connected through any route. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.591}
