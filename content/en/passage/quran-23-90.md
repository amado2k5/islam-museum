## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 90 to 92 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "But We gave them the truth, and they are liars": What is the matter, as these polytheists claim, that the angels are the daughters of Allah and that the gods and idols are gods besides Allah? "Rather, We brought them the truth" is certainty, which is the religion with which Allah sent His Prophet, and that is Islam, and nothing is worshiped except Allah because there is no deity besides Him. "And indeed, they are liars"—he means: And the polytheists are liars in what they attribute to Allah and reclaim from Him of children and partners.
