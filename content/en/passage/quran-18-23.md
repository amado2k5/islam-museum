## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 24 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And do not say to any doubt that I will do that tomorrow": This is a discipline from Allah, the Almighty, to His Prophet, who was promised not to assert that what happens is inevitable unless it is connected to it by Allah's will, because nothing happens except by Allah's will. He was only told this as far as we have reached because he promised his questioners about the three issues we mentioned earlier, one of which was the question about the matter of the young people from the cave that he would answer them tomorrow, and he did not make exceptions. The revelation withheld fifteen from him for this reason, until he was saddened by his delay. Then Allah revealed the answer to him, and his Prophet knew the reason for the revelation being withheld from him, taught him what should be used in his account, and informed him of matters that had not come to him by revelation. He said: "And do not say, O Muhammad, 'for anything, that I will do that tomorrow,' as I said to them." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Al-Rabi'.
