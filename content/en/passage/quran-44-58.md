## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 58 to 59 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So We have only made it easy in Your tongue that they may remember": We have only made it easy to recite this Qur'an which We have revealed to you, O Muhammad, in your tongue, so that those polytheists to whom We have sent you may remember its lessons and proofs, take heed from its admonitions, and reflect on its verses when you recite it to them, so they may call upon obedience to their Lord and submit to the truth when it becomes clear to them. Also: and from Qatadah, His saying: "We have only made it easy with your tongue": meaning this Qur'an "perhaps they will remember." Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "We have only made it easy with your tongue," he said: the Qur'an, and We made it easy: he let his tongue loose with it. And his saying: He quotes in the segment from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
