## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "You see the wrongdoers fearful of what they have earned, while it will befall them. And those who believe and do righteous deeds will have whatever they wish with their Lord. That is the great bounty." He explains: You see, O Muhammad, the disbelievers in Allah on the Day of Resurrection "fearful of what they earned," meaning: "And two are afraid of the punishment of Allah for what they earned in this world of evil deeds." "And it is upon them," he means: those who are fearful of Him from the punishment of Allah that has descended upon them, and they will surely taste it. And His saying: "And those who believe and do righteous deeds in the gardens of Paradise," He, the Exalted, says: And those who believe in Allah and obey Him in what He commands and forbids in this world, in the gardens of the gardens in the Hereafter. By 'rawdat' he means: the plural of a garden, which is the place where its growth increases. The Arabs do not say places of trees as gardens, hence the view of Abu al-Najm. And 'naghd' is like the manly man, the gardens of the meadows that have not been permitted, meaning by 'rawd': the plural of a meadow. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
