## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "To Him is the knowledge of the Hour, and what comes forth of fruits from its sleeves, and what a female carries and which is not delivered except by His knowledge. And the Day He will call out to them, 'Where are my partners?' They will say, 'Your ears have heard you; none of us is a witness.'" The knowledge of the Hour is returned to Allah, for no one knows what its status will be, "and what fruits will come forth from its sleeves." He means: and what appears from the fruit of a tree from its sleeves that it is absent from, and it emerges prominently "and no female carries." He means: And she does not carry a female when she is pregnant, and she does not give birth to her child except with knowledge from Allah, and nothing of this is hidden from Him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
