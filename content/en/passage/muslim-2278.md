## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter "the preference of our Prophet over all creation", in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim. In it the Prophet affirms his lordship on the Day of Resurrection, that his grave is the first to split open, and that his intercession comes first. It is among the clearest texts on the Great Intercession in Sunni belief. {cite:sahih-muslim}
