## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "No disaster has befallen the earth or yourselves except in a book before We restore it. Indeed, that is easy for Allah": No calamity has befallen you on the earth with its drought, drought, the loss of its crops, and its corruption. "Nor among yourselves" with afflictions, pains, and illnesses, "except in a Book," meaning in the Mother of the Book, "before We heal it," meaning: before We heal souls, meaning before We create them. It is said: God has created this thing, meaning: He created it, so He is its Creator. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
