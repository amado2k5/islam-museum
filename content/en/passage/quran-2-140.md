## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "or do you say that Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes were Jews or Christians? Say: are you more knowing, or God?" al-Tabari says there are two ways of reading it: "am taqulun" with ta', joined to "do you dispute with us about God", so it means say to them, do you argue with us about God, or do you say that Abraham; and "am yaqulun" with ya', as a question beginning anew, like "or do they say he invented it". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 122}
