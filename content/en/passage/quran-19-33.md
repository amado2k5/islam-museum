## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 33 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Peace be upon me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am resurrected alive": And the trust from Allah upon me is from Satan and his soldiers on the day I am born, that they will receive from me what they do from those who are born at birth, from stabbing him, the day I die, from the horror of the dawn, and the day I am resurrected alive on the Day of Resurrection, that the terror that people experience upon witnessing the horrors of that day will befall me, as narrated from Ibn Ishaq, from those I have known, from Wahb ibn Munabbih: "Peace be upon me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am resurrected alive." He tells them in his story about himself that he has no father, that he will die and then be resurrected alive. God Almighty says: "That is Jesus, son of Mary, the word of truth in which they are deceived." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
