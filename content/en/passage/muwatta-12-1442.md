## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ja'far ibn Muhammad from his father, who said: Fatima bint the Messenger of God weighed the hair of Hasan and Husayn, and of Zaynab and Umm Kulthum, and gave in charity silver equal to its weight. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from Ibn Jurayj that Fatima had no child born to her without having it shaved and giving silver in charity equal to the weight of its hair. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.645}
