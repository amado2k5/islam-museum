## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 7 as one section. He explains his saying, “He said, ‘My Lord, indeed I called my people night and day’”: Noah said, when he had delivered his Lord’s message to his people, or warned them of what He had commanded him to warn them of, but they disobeyed him and rejected what he had brought them from Him: “My Lord, indeed I called my people night and day” to Your Oneness and worship, and warned them of Your might and power, “but my call only increased them in aversion.” He says: My call to them to what I called them to of the truth with which You sent me to them only increased them in aversion. He says: Only in turning away from it, fleeing from it, and turning away from it. And it was narrated on the authority of Ma'mar, on the authority of Qatadah, regarding the verse: "But my call only increased them in flight," that he said: We were told that a man would take his son to Noah and say to his son: Beware of this man lest he lead you astray. For I saw my father take me to him when I was like you, and he warned me as I warned you. The passage also includes statements attributed to Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
