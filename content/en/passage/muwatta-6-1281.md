## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Aisha, Mother of the Believers, that she used to say: fasting is for one who combines the lesser pilgrimage with the pilgrimage, if he does not find an offering, between entering ihram for the pilgrimage and the day of Arafat; if he does not fast, he fasts the days of Mina. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.569}
