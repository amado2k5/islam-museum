## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 41 as a single section. He explains the phrase "then indeed, Hell is the abode" as meaning that God's fire, which is called Hell, is his home, his abode, and the destination to which he will return on the Day of Resurrection. He then quotes statements attributed to Mujahid in this section.
