## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr that his paternal aunt told him, from her grandmother, that she had vowed to walk to the mosque of Quba; she died and did not fulfil it; Abdullah ibn Abbas gave a fatwa to her daughter that she should walk on her behalf. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.605}
