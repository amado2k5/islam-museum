## Summary
A saying of Malik: there is no harm in a man anointing himself with an oil that has no perfume before he enters ihram, and before he departs from Mina after stoning the pillar. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1055) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.444}
