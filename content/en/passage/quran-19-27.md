## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So her people brought him carrying him. They said, 'O Mary, you have come to a certain matter.'" When Jesus said this to his mother, she reassured herself, submitted to God's command, and carried him until her people brought him. Also: from those who do not accuse, from Wahb ibn Munabbih, who said: He forgot her, meaning Mary, the suffering of affliction and the fear of people, and what she heard from the angels about the glad tidings of Jesus, until when he spoke to her, meaning Jesus, and she came to fulfill what God had promised her, she bore it and then brought it to her people. Al-Suddi said about this: And from Al-Suddi, he said: When she gave birth to him, Satan went and told the Children of Israel that Mary had given birth. They came with great enthusiasm, so they left her, "and her people brought him to carry him." And His saying: "They said, 'O Mary, you have brought something to the devil,'" Allah says: When they saw Mary and saw with her the child she had born, they said to her: O Mary, you have come with a wondrous event and caused a great event. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
