## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: 'They are favorable to you to embrace Islam. Say, 'Do not be kind to me of your Islam; rather, Allah is favoring you by guiding you to faith, if you are truthful.' These Bedouins are favoring you, O Muhammad, to embrace Islam. 'Say, do not be favorable to me to your Islam, but Allah is favoring you by guiding you to faith.' He means: Rather, Allah bestows upon you, O people, that He grants you success in believing in Him and His Messenger (if you are truthful). If you are truthful in your words, 'We believe,' then Allah is the One who has bestowed upon you by guiding you to Him, so do not entrust me with your Islam. He mentioned that these Bedouins from Banu Asad were kind to the Messenger of Allah, saying: We have believed without fighting, and we did not fight you as others fought you. Allah revealed these verses about them: In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
