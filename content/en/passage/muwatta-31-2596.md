## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr, from Sufyan ibn Abi Zuhayr, that he said: I heard the Messenger of God say: Yemen will be conquered, and people will come who travel and carry their families and those who follow them; Medina is better for them, if they only knew. Syria will be conquered, then Iraq, in the same way. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.465}
