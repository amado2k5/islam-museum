## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So he called upon his Lord, that these are a criminal people": Moses called upon his Lord when they denied him, did not believe in him, and the servants of Allah did not lead him to Him, and they intended to kill him, meaning Pharaoh and his people are "a criminal people," meaning they are polytheists and disbelievers in God. And His saying: "So take My servants on a journey" and in the omission is self-sufficient with the indication of what was mentioned about it, which is: His Lord answered him by saying: So take a secret when the matter was like that concerning My servants, who are the Children of Israel. The meaning of the speech is: So take My servants who believed in You and believed in You, and followed You instead of those among them who denied you and refused to accept the advice You brought them. Those who were in this state at that time were the Children of Israel. And He said: "So take My servants by night," because the meaning of that is: to take them by night before morning. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
