## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, do not take as allies to a people upon whom Allah is wrath; they have despaired of the Hereafter just as the disbelievers despaired of the companions of the graves": Allah Almighty says to those who believe in Him among the companions of the Messenger of Allah: "O you who have believed, do not take as allies of a people upon whom the wrath of Allah has fallen" from the Jews. "They have despaired of the Hereafter just as the disbelievers despaired of the companions of the graves." The interpreters differed in interpreting His saying: "They have despaired of the Hereafter just as the disbelievers despaired of the companions of the graves." Some said: The meaning is that those people upon whom God is angry with the Jews have despaired of God's reward in the Hereafter and will be resurrected, just as the living disbelievers despaired of their dead who are in the graves to return to them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
