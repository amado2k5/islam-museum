## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 131 to 133 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Peace be upon the family of Yasin" as a security from God for the family of Yasin. Reciters differed in the interpretation of his phrase: "Peace be upon Ilyasin." The majority of the reciters of Mecca, Basra, and Kufa read it as: "Peace be upon Ilyasin" with a kasra on the alif of Ilyasis. Some said it was the name of Elias, and they said he was called by two names: Ilyas, and Ilyasin is like Ibrahim. Abraham supports this by saying that everything in the surah regarding "Salam" means peace upon the Prophet mentioned over his family, and so on Ilyasin, he is only peace upon Elias without his family.
