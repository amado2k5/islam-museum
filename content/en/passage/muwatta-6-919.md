## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from Fatima bint al-Mundhir, who said: we used to cover our faces while in ihram, when we were with Asma bint Abi Bakr al-Siddiq. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1050) and Suwayd (494) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.441}
