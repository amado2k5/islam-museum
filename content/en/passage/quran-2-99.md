## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "We have sent down to you clear signs", al-Tabari says they are clear marks of Muhammad's prophethood, namely what God's book contains of the hidden knowledge of the Jews, the secrets of their reports and those of their forebears, and of what they distorted and altered of the rulings of the Torah; God disclosed these in His book, and so they were clear signs for whoever is fair to himself and not driven by envy and transgression to his own destruction, since he brought them without learning from any human. He reports from Ibn Abbas through al-Dahhak that he recites it to them morning and evening, being unlettered and having read no book, telling them what they hold exactly as it is; and from Ibn Abbas through Ibn Ishaq that Ibn Suriya said to the Messenger of God: you have brought us nothing we know, and God has sent down on you no clear sign for us to follow, so the verse came down (the editor notes the narration differs on whether the name is Ibn Suriya or Ibn Salawba). On "none disbelieves in them but the transgressors" he says disbelief is denial and transgression is leaving one thing for another: none denies these signs but one who has left his religion and abandoned its obligations in the book he professes to believe, whereas Jews who hold to their religion affirm them, namely those who believed in God and in Muhammad. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 397} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 398} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 399}
