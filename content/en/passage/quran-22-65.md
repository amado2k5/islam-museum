## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah has subjected to you whatever is in the earth and the ships that sail through the sea by His command and holds the heavens to fall upon the earth except by His permission? Indeed, Allah is to the people Kind and Merciful": Have you not seen that Allah has subjected to you, O people, whatever is on the earth of animals and beasts? All of that is for you to spend according to your needs. "And the ships sail in the sea by His command," meaning: He subjected the ships to sail in the sea by His command, meaning by His power, and His subjugation of them is also for you. Reciters differed in the recitation of the phrase: "and the ark moves," so most reciters of the cities read it as "and the orbit" in the accusative, meaning He subjected to you what is on the earth, and the ark is a conjunction to "what," and to repeat the word "an" and that the stars move. It is narrated from Al-Araj that he recited this as the nominative as a beginning. The accusative is the accusative reading in this regard due to the consensus of the reciters on it. "And He holds the sky so that it falls on the earth" means: and He holds the sky by His power so that it does not fall on the earth except by His ear.
