## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "with clear proofs and the dimmals, and We have sent down to you the Reminder that you may make clear to the people what has been revealed to them and that they may reflect": We sent men with clear proofs and the dial to whom We revealed. If someone says: And how is it said with clear proofs and the distress? And what brings this 'b' to the phrase "with clear proofs": if you say: it brings it to the verse "We sent," which is from his connection, is it permissible for the conjunction of "ma" before "except" after it? And if you say: He brought it to something else, what is it? And where is the verb that brought it? It is said: The people of Arabic differed on this; some said: the 'ba' in the phrase 'with clear evidence' is from the conjunction 'We sent us,' and said: 'except' in this place, with denial and interrogation in each place, meaning 'other.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
