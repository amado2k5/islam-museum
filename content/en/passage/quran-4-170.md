**Commentary on verse 170: the messenger has come with the truth from your Lord (p. 412)**

Al-Tabari says 'O people' means the idolaters of the Arabs and all kinds of disbelievers: the Messenger, Muhammad, has come to you with the truth from your Lord, that is, with Islam, which God has chosen as a religion for His servants. So believe, it is better for you: affirm him and what he brought you of your Lord's religion, for belief is better for you than unbelief. If you deny his mission and reject it, it will not harm anyone but you, and the harm of that returns to you alone; to God belongs what is in the heavens and earth in dominion and creation, and your disbelief does not diminish His dominion and authority by anything, and God is knowing and wise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 412}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
