## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And warn them of the Day of Regret when the matter has been decided while they are heedless and do not believe": And warn, O Muhammad, those who share with Allah on the Day of their regret and regret, for what they neglected in the presence of Allah, and their dwellings in Paradise were inherited by the people of faith in Allah and obedience to Him. They were admitted to the dwellings of the people of faith in Allah from the Fire. Both parties were certain of eternal eternity and a life after which there is no death. What a regret and regret it is. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-A'mash.
