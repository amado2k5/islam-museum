## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “They say, ‘If we return to Medina, the more honorable will surely expel the more humble from it. But honor belongs to Allah and to His Messenger and to the believers, but the hypocrites do not know,’” explains this: These hypocrites, whose characteristics were described before, “If we return to Medina, the more honorable will surely expel the more humble from it,” are saying this. By “more honorable,” He means the stronger and more powerful. Allah, may His praise be exalted, said, “And honor belongs to Allah,” meaning strength and power, “and to His Messenger.” And for the believers in God, “but the hypocrites do not know.” It was mentioned that the reason for this statement by Abdullah ibn Ubay was because a man from the Muhajireen had struck a man from the Ansar. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
