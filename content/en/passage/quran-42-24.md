## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Or do they say, 'He fabricated lies against Allah, and if Allah wills, He seals your heart, wipes out falsehood, and makes truth true by His words. Indeed, He is Knowing of the essence of the hearts': Or do these polytheists say: 'Muhammad fabricated a lie against Allah,' and he brought what he recites to us fabricated by himself. And His saying: "If Allah wills," O Muhammad, is sealed on your heart, so forget this Qur'an that was revealed to you. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
