## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Allah is Who sent the winds and stirs up clouds, so We made them flow to a dead land and revived the earth after its death. Likewise, the resurrection." And Allah is the One who sent the winds and stirs the clouds to the living and the rain. "So We caused it to flow to a dead land," meaning: We caused it to be driven to a land that was barren for its people, the place of the land, a vast land where neither growth nor crop grows. "So We revived the earth after its death," meaning: We made fertile with the rain of that clouds, the land to which We watered after its drought, and caused crops to grow therein after the place. "Likewise resurrection." Allah Almighty says: Thus God scatters the dead after their affliction in their graves, reviving them after their destruction, just as We revived this earth with rain after its death. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
