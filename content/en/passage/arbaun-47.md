## Summary
Hadith of al-Miqdam ibn Ma'dikarib: "No human fills a vessel worse than his stomach; a few bites suffice to keep his back straight. If he must, a third for food, a third for drink, and a third for breath". It is the fifth of the hadiths Ibn Rajab added, and he cites the variant reports and their chains. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
