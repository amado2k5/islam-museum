**Commentary on verse 4 (p. 232)**

On [[passage/quran-11-4|11:4]] al-Tabari says: to God, people, is your return and destination, so beware His punishment if you turn away from what I call you to, repentance toward Him from worshipping the gods and images, for He will keep you in the Fire of Hell if you perish in your polytheism before repenting; 'and He is powerful over everything' means He is able to give you life after your death and to punish you for setting up idols with Him and whatever else He wills for you and for others. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'return' and 'powerful'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 232}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
