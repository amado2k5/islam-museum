## Summary
Yahya ibn Said narrated to Malik that Isa ibn Maryam met a pig on the road and said to it: go in peace. He was asked: do you say this to a pig? He said: I fear that I would accustom my tongue to speak evil. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.580}
