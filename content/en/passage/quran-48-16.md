## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say to those who stayed behind among the Bedouins: You will be called to a people of great might, to fight them or to surrender. If you obey, Allah will give you a good reward; but if you turn away as you turned away before, He will punish you with a painful punishment": "Say," O Muhammad, "to those who lagged behind among the Bedouins" from marching with you, "you will be called to" fight a people of great might in fierce battle. The interpreters differed regarding those whom Allah Almighty informed that these retreated Bedouins were called to fight them, with some saying: They are the people of Persia. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and others.
