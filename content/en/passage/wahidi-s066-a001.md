## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of at-Tahrim 66:1, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «1».

## Full report (Arabic source text)

وعشرين ليلة، فأنزل الله: {لم تحرم ما أحل الله لك} الآية.
