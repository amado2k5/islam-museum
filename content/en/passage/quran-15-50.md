## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "And that My punishment is the painful punishment," meaning he also informed the servants that God's punishment is for those who persist in their sins, persist in them, and do not repent, the painful punishment unlike any other. He mentions that this is a warning from God to His creation to overcome His sins and commands them to turn back and repent, and he links the verse with the story of Gabriel, "My servants have not despaired," and the Prophet's statement about the extent of God's forgiveness and punishment for Qatadah.
