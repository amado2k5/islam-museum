## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And how many animal does not carry its provision? Allah provides for it and you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing": Allah Almighty says to those who believe in Him and His Messenger among the companions of Muhammad: 'Emigrate and strive in the cause of Allah, O believers, His enemies, and do not fear any family or need. How many animals need food, food, and drink that cannot carry their provision, meaning their food they cannot carry, so they raise it in the day and tomorrow because they are unable to do so. Allah provides for it and you' day by day, and He is the Hearing, according to your sayings: We fear by our separation from our homelands, the family (the All-Knowing) what is within yourselves, what is the fate of your affairs, and the command of your enemy to humiliate them, to support you over them, and other matters of yours are hidden from His creation. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Sufyan.
