## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single syllable. He explains His saying, "And when they cast a narrow place from it, folded together, let there be a resting place": And if these deniers of the Hour are cast into a narrow place of the Fire, their hands are chained around their necks, "Leave there a bury." Interpreters differed on the meaning of the verse, with some saying: it is woe. In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
