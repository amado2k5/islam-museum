## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, [being] sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. And that is the correct religion,” means that Allah did not command these Jews and Christians, who are the People of the Book, except to worship Him, devoting their religion sincerely to Him. That is, to devote their obedience solely to Him, without mixing their obedience to their Lord with any form of idolatry. The Jews associated partners with their Lord by saying that Ezra was the son of God, and the Christians did the same by saying the same about the Messiah, and by denying the prophethood of Muhammad. Regarding His statement, “inclining to truth,” we have already explained the meaning of “inclining to truth” with sufficient evidence, but we will mention some of the relevant narrations that we did not previously discuss. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
