## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id, that Abdullah ibn Ayyash ibn Abi Rabi'a al-Makhzumi told a slave of his to slaughter an animal. When he was about to slaughter it, he said: mention God. The slave said: I have mentioned Him. He said: mention God, woe to you. The slave said: I have mentioned God. Abdullah ibn Ayyash said: by God, I will never eat it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.630}
