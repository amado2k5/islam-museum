## Summary
Malik transmits from Yaqub ibn Zayd ibn Talha, from his father Zayd ibn Talha, from Abdullah ibn Abi Mulayka, that a woman came to the Prophet and told him she had committed adultery while pregnant. He told her to go until she gave birth, then until she nursed, then until she entrusted her child, and then he ordered her stoned. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr counted it as mursal in the report of Yahya and those with him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.382}
