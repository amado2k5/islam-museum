## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "My Lord, judge by truth, and our Lord is the Most Merciful, the Helper against what you describe": Say, O Muhammad: O Lord, separate between me and those who deny me among the polytheists, disbelievers, and servants of others, by invoking Your punishment and retribution upon them. This is the truth that Allah Almighty commanded His Prophet to ask his Lord to judge by, similar to His saying, Glorified and Exalted: Our Lord, open between us and our people the truth, and You are the best of conquerors. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
