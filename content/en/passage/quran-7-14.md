## Summary
Verse 14 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 14 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "He said: grant me respite until the day they are raised. He said: you are among those granted respite", he says: this is another of his wicked ignorances; he asked his Lord for what he knew no creature has a way to, for he asked for a respite until the coming of the Hour, which is the day creation is raised, and had he been given what he asked, he would have been given eternity and a lasting that has no end, since there is no death after the resurrection. God said, as in [[passage/quran-15-38|15:38]] and [[passage/quran-38-81|38:81]], you are among those granted respite until the day of the known time, which is the day on which God has decreed his perishing, death and extinction, because nothing remains and does not perish except our Lord, the Living who does not die. God says, as in [[passage/quran-3-185|3:185]], every soul shall taste death. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 330}

"Respite" in Arab speech is delay; one says "I gave him respite in my right over him". If someone asks, God said to him, when he asked for respite until the day they are raised, "you are among those granted respite", so did He not grant what he asked? The answer is that it is not so; He would have granted what he asked only if He had said, you are among those granted respite until the time you asked, or until the day of the resurrection, or until the day they are raised, or the like, which would show He granted the respite he asked. His words "you are among those granted respite" contain no indication but for the other verse in which He stated the length of the respite, "until the day of the known time"; for if He respited him one day or less or more, he enters the number of those respited and God's true promise is fulfilled, but He stated the length of that period as we mentioned. As al-Suddi used to say, and (14360) is given from him, He did not respite him until the day of the resurrection, but until the day of the known time, which is the day the Horn is blown the first blast, when those in the heavens and on the earth are struck down and die. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 331}

So the meaning is: Iblis said to his Lord, grant me respite, that is, delay me, defer me, extend my term and do not let me die until the day they are raised, that is, the day creation is raised; and the Exalted said, you are among those granted respite until the day the Horn is blown, when those in the heavens and on the earth are struck down except those God wills. If someone asks, is anyone but Iblis granted respite until that day, so that he can be told "you are among them"? The answer is yes: whoever of His creation God has not taken the soul of until that day, of those on whom the Hour rises, they are among those respited by their terms until it; therefore it was said to Iblis "you are among those granted respite", meaning you are among those God will not let die until that day. The note says the Musa ibn Harun al-Hamdani is in the chain and that the printed text and manuscript's "Yunus ibn Harun" is a plain error. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 332}
