## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and that I may complete My favour upon you, and that you may be guided" al-Tabari says: from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque, and you, Muhammad and the believers, turn your faces toward it and take it as your qibla, so that no one has an argument against you except the Quraysh polytheists, and so that by it, by My guiding you to the qibla of My friend Abraham whom I made a leader for people, I complete My favour, perfecting My grace upon you and completing the rites of your upright, submitting creed which I enjoined on Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus and the other prophets. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 207} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208} "That you may be guided" means that you be directed to the right qibla, and it is joined to "that I may complete My favour", which is joined to "so that there be no argument". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208}

On "as We sent among you a messenger from among you, reciting to you Our verses, purifying you, teaching you the Book and wisdom, and teaching you what you did not know" he says the meaning is: that I complete My favour upon you by explaining the rites of your upright creed and guide you to the religion of My friend Abraham, making for you his supplication and request, which is what Abraham asked in Surat al-Baqara, that they be made submitters to Him and from their offspring a submitting community, and that a messenger from among them be sent among them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208}
