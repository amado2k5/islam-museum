## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and that I may complete My favour upon you, and that you may be guided" al-Tabari says: from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque, and you, Muhammad and the believers, turn your faces toward it and take it as your qibla, so that no one has an argument against you except the Quraysh polytheists, and so that by it, by My guiding you to the qibla of My friend Abraham whom I made a leader for people, I complete My favour, perfecting My grace upon you and completing the rites of your upright, submitting creed which I enjoined on Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus and the other prophets. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 207} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208} "That you may be guided" means that you be directed to the right qibla, and it is joined to "that I may complete My favour", which is joined to "so that there be no argument". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208}

On "as We sent among you a messenger from among you, reciting to you Our verses, purifying you, teaching you the Book and wisdom, and teaching you what you did not know" he says the meaning is: that I complete My favour upon you by explaining the rites of your upright creed and guide you to the religion of My friend Abraham, making for you his supplication and request, which is what Abraham asked in Surat al-Baqara, that they be made submitters to Him and from their offspring a submitting community, and that a messenger from among them be sent among them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208}

Al-Tabari completes the sense: I make for you Abraham's supplication as I made for you his request "Our Lord, send among them a messenger from among them", so I sent from among you My messenger whom Abraham My friend and his son Ishmael asked Me to send from their descendants. He holds that "kama" is joined to "that I may complete My favour" and not to "so remember Me and I will remember you". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 208} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 209} He notes that some said the meaning is "remember Me as We sent among you a messenger, and I will remember you", by transposition, and rejects it as far-fetched and not the most eloquent Arabic, the answer "I will remember you" showing it breaks off from what precedes. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 209} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 210} He reports from Mujahid and Ibn Abi Najih that the meaning is "as I did, remember Me", so that "as We sent" would answer "remember Me". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 210}

He says "as We sent among you a messenger from among you" means the Arabs: God said to them, keep to My obedience and face the qibla I commanded, so the Jews' argument ends and I complete My favour on you and you are guided, as I began My favour to you by sending among you a messenger from among you, who is Muhammad, from al-Rabi'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 210} "Reciting Our verses" means the verses of the Qur'an; "purifying you" cleansing you from the stain of sins; "the Book" is the Furqan, teaching them its rulings; "wisdom" is the sunna and understanding of the religion; and "what you did not know" is the reports of the prophets, the stories of past nations and news of what is and will be, which the Arabs did not know. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 210} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 211}
