## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the branches of faith in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It lists the degrees of faith, from the testimony to removing harm from the road and modesty. It is a comprehensive text that faith is speech and action. {cite:sahih-muslim}
