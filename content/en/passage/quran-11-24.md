**Commentary on verse 24 (p. 291)**

On [[passage/quran-11-24|11:24]] al-Tabari says: the likeness of the two parties, of disbelief and belief, is as the blind who sees nothing with his eyes and the deaf who hears nothing: so the party of disbelief does not see the truth so as to follow it and act on it, occupied with its disbelief in God and overcome by God's abandoning, not hearing God's caller to right guidance so as to answer and be guided, remaining in its error and wavering in its bewilderment; and the hearing and the seeing are the party of belief, who saw God's proofs and acknowledged what they point to of God's oneness, the renouncing of gods and rivals and the prophethood of the prophets, and heard God's caller and answered him and worked in obedience to God. Report 18102, from Ibn Jurayj from Ibn Abbas, begins at the foot of the page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 291}
