## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 68 to 70 in one passage: Lot said to his people, "These are my guests, so do not expose them," meaning those whom you have come to want immorality from them as my guest. It is a man's right to honor his guest, so do not disgrace my guest, and honor me by refraining from insulting them with dislike. "And fear Allah" — they feared Him and within themselves that His punishment would befall them. "Do not disgrace Him," nor humiliate Him nor disgrace Him among them by harming them. "They said, 'Or did We not disgrace the worlds?'" His people said to him, "Or did We not disgrace you to add anyone from the worlds?" — from Qatadah, did We not disgrace you to add anyone?
