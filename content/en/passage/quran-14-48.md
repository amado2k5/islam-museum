## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reports that the day of the earth's change may mean that the earth on which people live now becomes a white earth like silver, where no blood is shed and no sin is committed. He gives this from Ibn Mas'ud by several chains, one adding that the first matter judged among people will be bloodshed.
