## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says that "the day the earth will be changed to the earth and the heavens" is connected to the "revenge" in the previous verse: God will take revenge that day against those who disbelieved among the polytheists of Quraysh and all those who denied the prophethood of His messengers. He narrates the meaning of change from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud through various chains from Amr ibn Maymun: A pure white land like silver was exchanged as if it were a silver bar, where no unlawful blood was shed, no sin was committed therein, the caller hears them and the sight pierces them, barefoot and naked, standing as they were created until sweat restrains them, and the first judgment among people there is blood on it. Al-Tabari lists the chains of transmission of these narrations and the differences in their wording, and in some of them "the land of Paradise." Then "And they appeared to Allah, the One, the Subduer": they appeared to Him, not a concealer, the One, the Subduer of His creation.
