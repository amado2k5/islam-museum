## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 99 to 102 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We did not mislead except the criminals": God Almighty says, informing about the saying of these misguided in Hell: "And We have not led astray except the criminals," meaning by criminals Iblis and the son of Adam who prescribed killing. Also: He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Ikrimah, regarding the phrase "And We did not mislead except the criminals," he said: Iblis and the son of Adam the killer.
