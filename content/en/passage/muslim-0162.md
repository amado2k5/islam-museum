## Summary
The longest account of the Night Journey and Ascension in the two Sahih works: a report of Thabit al-Bunani from Anas from Malik ibn Sa'sa'a from Abu Dharr, in the Book of Faith. It includes al-Buraq, the opening of the heavens' gates, meeting Adam, John, Jesus, Joseph, Idris, Aaron, Moses and Abraham, the Lote-tree of the Boundary, and the obligation of prayer, reduced from fifty to five through Moses's return. {cite:sahih-muslim}
