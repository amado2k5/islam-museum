## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains his statement, “And Mary, the daughter of Imran, who guarded her chastity, so We breathed into her [womb] through Our spirit, and she believed in the words of her Lord and His scriptures and was among the devoutly obedient.” He says: “And God presents an example for those who believed: Mary, the daughter of Imran, who guarded her chastity.” He says: She who prevented Gabriel from entering the opening of her garment. And everything that was in the garment, whether a tear or a rupture, is called a “farj.” Likewise, every crack and split in a wall, or a gap in a roof, is a “farj.” His statement, “Then We breathed into her of Our spirit,” means: We breathed into the opening of her garment, which is her private part, from Our spirit, from Gabriel, which is the Spirit. The passage also quotes statements attributed to Qatadah.
