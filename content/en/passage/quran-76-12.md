## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12-13 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "And He will reward them for their patience with Paradise and silk" as meaning: God will reward them for their patience in this world in obeying Him and doing what pleases Him with Paradise and silk. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
