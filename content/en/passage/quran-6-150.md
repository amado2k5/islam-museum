## Summary
Verse 150 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 150 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "say: bring your witnesses", God tells His Prophet to say to these inventors of lies against their Lord among the idol worshippers, who claim that God forbade them what they forbid of their crops and cattle: bring your witnesses who testify against God that He forbade you what you claim He forbade. Al-Tabari then gives the usage of "halumma": the people of the high country of Tihama keep it unchanged for one, two or many, male or female, and cite a line of al-A'sha where it is used for a group (the editor refers to al-A'sha's Diwan and Abu Ubayda's Majaz al-Qur'an); the people of the lower country of Najd inflect it, one form for a man, another for a woman, and further forms for two and for plurals, and the editor refers to Abu Ubayda's text for this. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 213}

"If they testify, do not testify with them" means that if they bring witnesses claiming that God forbade what they claim, do not testify with them, for they are liars and false witnesses against God. The address is to the Prophet but those meant are his companions and the believers. "Do not follow the desires of those who deny Our signs" means: do not go along with them in denying God's revelation, in forbidding and permitting, but follow what was revealed to you from your Lord's Book, which falsehood does not approach from before it or behind it. "And those who do not believe in the Hereafter" means: do not follow their desires and deny God's giving life to His creation after death and raising them after their perishing. "And they equate others with their Lord" means that, though denying the resurrection and the coming of the Hour, they equate idols and images with God, making them rivals that they worship besides Him. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "equating". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 214}

He gives (14133) from al-Suddi on "bring your witnesses": say, show me those who testify that God forbade this of what the Arabs forbade and said God commanded; God said to His Messenger "if they testify, do not testify with them"; and (14134) from Mujahid that "this" is the bahira and sa'iba animals. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 214}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
