## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So they set out until, when they meet a young man and kill him, he says, 'I have killed a pure soul other than a soul; I have brought something reprehensible.'" The scholar said: "So they set out until, when they meet a young man, he kills him." Moses said to him: "I killed a pure soul." Reciters differed in their interpretation of this; the majority of the reciters of Hijaz and Basra read it as: "I killed a pure soul," and they said it means: purified one who has no sin and has never sinned due to its small age. The general recitation of the people of Kufa read this as: 'Nafsa Zakiyah,' meaning: the repentant one whose sins are forgiven. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Jurayj.
