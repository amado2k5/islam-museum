## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 53 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "so they fill their bellies with their bellies": they fill their bellies with the trees of Zaqqum: and the Arabs differ regarding the feminine form of the tree in the phrase: "so they fill their bellies" meaning from the tree, "they drink on it" because the tree is feminine and masculine, and feminine because it is carried on the tree, since the tree may indicate everyone. The Arabs say: A bitter tree grew before us with a bad scarcity, and they mean everyone.
