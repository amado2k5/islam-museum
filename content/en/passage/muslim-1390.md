## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on what lies between the grave and the pulpit being a garden of the gardens of Paradise, in the Book of Pilgrimage of Sahih Muslim. It is the origin of the name "the Noble Garden" in the Prophet's mosque. Abdullah ibn Zayd's report in the same section says "what lies between my house and my pulpit is a garden of the gardens of Paradise". {cite:sahih-muslim}
