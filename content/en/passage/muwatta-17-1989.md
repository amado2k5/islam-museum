## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that Umar ibn al-Khattab said of a man who lent another man food on condition that he would give it to him in another city: Umar disliked that and said: then where is the carriage? meaning its transport. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.214}
