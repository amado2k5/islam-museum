## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30-31 as a single unit. He interprets the words, “He admits whom He wills into His mercy; but for the wrongdoers He has prepared a painful punishment”: Your Lord admits whomever He wills among you into His mercy, accepting their repentance until they die repentant from their misguidance, forgiving their sins, and admitting them to His Paradise. “But for the wrongdoers He has prepared a painful punishment” means: those who wronged themselves and died in their polytheism, He has prepared for them in the Hereafter a painful and agonizing punishment, which is the punishment of Hell. The word “the wrongdoers” is in the accusative case because the conjunction “and” is an adverbial phrase modifying “He has prepared,” and the meaning is: He has prepared for the wrongdoers a painful punishment. It was mentioned that in Abdullah’s reading: “And for the wrongdoers He has prepared for them” with the repetition of the letter “lam,” and the Arabs do that, and some of them recite: I say to her if she asks for a divorce, “Why are you rushing to separate from me?” And another recites: “So they became not asking him about what he was doing, whether he was ascending in the vainness of passion or descending?” with the repetition of the letter “ba,” and the statement is: “They do not ask him about what he was doing.”
