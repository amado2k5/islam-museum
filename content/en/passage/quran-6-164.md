## Summary
Verse 164 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 164 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "say: shall I seek a lord other than God, when He is the Lord of everything? No soul earns except against itself, and no bearer bears the burden of another", God tells His Prophet to say to those who equate idols with their Lord and call you to worship images and follow Satan's steps: shall I seek a lord other than God, that is, shall I seek a master other than God who would be my master? "When He is the Lord of everything" means He is the master of everything besides Him, its manager and its reformer. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 285}

"No soul earns except against itself" means no soul commits a sin except against itself, that is, no one is taken for the disobedience of God another has committed and the wrong he has ridden; every sinner is punished for his own sin and taken for his own offence. "No bearer bears the burden of another" means no sinful soul is made sinful by another soul's sin, but it is sinful by its own and is punished for it, not for that of another. This is meant for the idolaters whom God commanded His Prophet to say it to: tell them, we are not taken for your sins, upon you is the punishment of your crimes and for us the reward of our deeds, as He commanded him elsewhere to say what is in [[passage/quran-109-6|109:6]]. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "seeking", "Lord" and "earning". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 286}

He gives (14307) from al-Rabi' that in that time the devout scholars had no way out except one of two courses, one better than the other: either command and call to the truth, or withdrawal, not sharing with people of falsehood in their deeds, performing the obligations between yourself and your Lord, loving for God and hating for God and sharing in nobody's sin; and he said a precise verse was revealed on this, namely this one, and on it God said in [[passage/quran-98-4|98:4]] that those given the Book divided only after the clear proof came to them. Al-Tabari gives the conjugation of "wizr" and the note records where the printed text differs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 286}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
