## Summary
Verse 84 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 84 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**First part of the verse (p. 579, notes 1-5).** Al-Tabari says God commands His prophet Muhammad to strive against God's enemies among the polytheists in His religion, which He legislated for him and which is Islam, and to fight them in it himself. The limiting of his charge to himself means that God does not burden him, in the duty of striving against His enemy, except with what He laid on him and not on others, that is, he is held to what he himself earned, not what others earned. Then He orders him to urge the believers to fight those he was commanded to fight with them, with the hope that God will keep off the fighting of those who disbelieved, denied His oneness and rejected his message, from him and from them and their harm; he adds that "it may be" from God is binding, as he has explained before. The editor says print and manuscript have a different opening for one sentence and the context requires his reading; refers to earlier explanations of the road of God and of charging (page numbers garbled and not given); explains that "and their harm" is joined to "the fighting of one who disbelieved"; and says he could not find the place al-Tabari refers to and fears it is not meant for that (the rest of the note is garbled in print and is not asserted). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 579}

**Last part of the verse (p. 580, notes 1-2).** Al-Tabari says the sense is that God is more severe in harming His enemy among the disbelievers than they are toward you, Muhammad, and your companions, so do not hold back from fighting them, for He watches them with might, harm and punishment to weaken their scheming and strength and to raise the truth above them. The noun for such punishment comes from saying "I made an example of so-and-so" when one pains him with a penalty. The editor explains that a certain verb means to hold back from fear, and refers to the earlier explanation of the word for exemplary punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 580}

**Report 10014 (p. 580).** Bishr b. Mu'adh (10014, from Yazid b. Zuray', from Sa'id) reports from Qatada that the noun means punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 580}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 580}
