## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 62 to 66 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "Is that better for a dwelling or the tree of Zaqqum?" as: Is this the one whose attribute I described as a miracle in Paradise, and I provided them with good bliss therein, or what I prepared for the people of the Fire from Zaqqum? By 'nazl' I mean the bounty, and it has two languages: nazl and nazl. The food that has a profit is called food that has a dwelling and a swelling. And His saying: "Or the tree of Zaqqum," mentioned that when Allah Almighty revealed this verse, the polytheists said: How can trees grow in the Fire when the Fire burns the trees? Allah said: "Indeed, We have made it a trial for the wrongdoers," meaning those polytheists who said what they said, then informed them of the nature of this tree, "and said, 'It is a tree that emerges at the root of Hell.'" In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
