## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "When he called upon his Lord with a secret call": He says when he called upon his Lord and asked Him with a secret call, meaning: He was secretly asking Him for His dislike of showing off, as in Qatadah, His saying: "When he called to his Lord in a hidden call," meaning secretly, and God knows the pure heart and hears the hidden voice. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj regarding the phrase: "When he called his Lord with a hidden call," he said: He did not intend to show off. And from Al-Suddi, he said: Zakariya desired a child, so he stood up and prayed, then prayed secretly to his Lord, saying: "My Lord, I am weak in my bones..." In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
