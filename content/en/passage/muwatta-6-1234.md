## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Aisha, that the Messenger of God mentioned Safiyya bint Huyayy; it was said to him that she had menstruated; he said: perhaps she holds us back. They said: Messenger of God, she has already circumambulated; he said: then there is no problem. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.551}
