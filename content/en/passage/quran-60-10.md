## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, when believing women come to you as emigrants, test them. Allah knows best by their faith. But if you teach them to believe, do not return them to the disbelievers. They are not lawful for them, nor are they lawful for them. And give them what they spent. And there is no blame upon you if you marry them if you have given them their dues. And do not hold fast to the protection of the disbelievers. And ask about what you have spent, and ask about what they spent. That is the ruling of Allah." He judges between you, and Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.' Allah says to the believing companions of the Messenger of Allah: 'O you who have believed, when the women who have believed, when he comes to you, the believing women are emigrants' from the land of disbelief to the land of Islam, 'so test them.' The ordeal of the Messenger of Allah was to them when they came as emigrants, as reported by Qays ibn al-Rabi', from al-Aghur ibn al-Sabah, from Khalifah ibn Husayn, from Abu Nasr al-Asadi, who said: Ibn Abbas was asked: How was the test of the Messenger of Allah for women? He said: He used to test them by Allah what I did out of hatred for a husband, by Allah I did not leave out of desire from land to land, by Allah I did not seek worldly gain, and by Allah I did not go out except out of love for Allah and His Messenger. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
