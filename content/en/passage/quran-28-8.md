## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So the people of Pharaoh caught him so that he might be an enemy and a source of sorrow for them. Indeed, Pharaoh, Haman, and their soldiers were sinners": the people of Pharaoh caught him, struck him, and took him. Its origin is from the catch, which is what he found lost and caught. The Arabs say about what came upon him suddenly without his request or will: I caught him by catching, and I met so-and-so with catching, as in the saying of the poet: 'And the spring that came to him by catching, I did not meet when I came to him in a desert,' meaning suddenly. Interpreters differ on the meaning of the phrase 'the family of Pharaoh' in this context, and some said: He meant by that: the concubine of Pharaoh's wife. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
