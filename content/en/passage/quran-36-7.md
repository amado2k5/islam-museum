## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 6–7. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among interpreters regarding 'to warn a people whose forefathers did not warn': some said, 'To warn a people whose forefathers did not warn before them, so they are heedless,' and others say otherwise, then clarifies that this is true for most of them, as they do not believe.
