## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, from his father, that in the letter the Prophet wrote to Amr ibn Hazm about blood-money: for a soul, a hundred camels; for a nose cut off entirely, a hundred camels; for a head wound that reaches the brain, a third of the blood-money; for an abdominal wound, the same; for an eye, fifty; for a hand, fifty; for a foot, fifty; for each finger, ten camels; for a tooth, five; for a wound that exposes the bone, five. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that there is no disagreement from Malik in his transmission of it as mursal; it is a well-known book among the historians of the campaigns, and it is transmitted connected by a sound route. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.417}
