## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on the merit of congregational prayer over praying alone: the Prophet told that congregational prayer surpasses the prayer of one who prays alone by twenty-five parts. The editor notes that all transmitters in the Muwatta gave it with this chain, though some transmitted it from Malik through al-Zuhri from Abu Salama, and al-Shafi'i and Rawh through Malik from Abu al-Zinad from al-A'raj (al-Tamhid). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.189}
