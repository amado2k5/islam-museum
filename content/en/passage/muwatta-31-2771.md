## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, that Rafi ibn Ishaq, client of al-Shifa, told him: I and Abdallah ibn Abi Talha entered upon Abu Said al-Khudri to visit him when he was ill. Abu Said told us: the Messenger of God told us that the angels do not enter a house with images or pictures in it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.555}
