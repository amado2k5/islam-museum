## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 80 to 81 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He who made for you from the green trees a fire, so when you are kindled from it": Say, He who created it first will revive it, "Who made for you from the green trees a fire," meaning: He who brought forth for you from the green trees a fire that burns the trees, unable to do what He willed, nor failing to revive the bones that were thrown and restore them as a normal human being, and a new creation as He began them the first time. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
