**Commentary on verse 124 (pp. 577-578)**

Al-Tabari says: when God sends down a surah of the Qur'an on His Prophet, some of these hypocrites mentioned in this surah ask, "People, which of you has this surah increased in faith?", that is, in belief in God and His signs. As for those who believed, among those so addressed, the surah increases them in faith, and they rejoice at the faith and certainty God gave them. If it is asked whether faith in Arabic is not belief and acknowledgement, so how did the surah increase their belief and acknowledgement, it is said it increased them in faith when it came down, because before it came down they were not bound to acknowledge it and act on it specifically, only through their general belief that all their Prophet brought from God is true; when God sent it down, they became bound to acknowledge that this very surah is from God and to believe its rulings, limits and obligations, and that was the increase. The editor refers to earlier discussion of rejoicing and faith. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 577}

Report 17488 is from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father and uncle: whenever a surah came down they believed in it and God increased them in faith and belief, and they rejoiced. Report 17489 is from al-Rabi', through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Ibn Abi Ja'far and his father: "it increased them in faith", he said, in awe. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 578}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
