## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: a man asked for a short counsel, and the Prophet said "Do not become angry", repeated. Ibn Rajab takes this as showing that anger is the root of evil and restraint from it the root of good. It is one of the four hadiths on which the rules of good conduct are built. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
