## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Do you know Allah through your religion while Allah knows what is in the heavens and what is on the earth, and Allah is Knowing of all things?': 'Say,' O Muhammad, to those Bedouins who say, 'We believe,' and when faith enters their hearts: 'Do you know Allah?' O people, by your religion, meaning by your obedience to your Lord, 'And Allah knows what is in the heavens and what is on the earth,' meaning: By Allah, whom you know that you are believers, the Knower of all that is in the seven heavens and the seven earths, nothing is hidden from Him. So how can you teach Him by your religion, which you believe in, It is not hidden from Him, whether in the heavens or on the earth, so what you are on in religion is hidden from Him. "And Allah is All-Knowing of everything," meaning: God is aware of all that is, whatever is being, and of what He possesses knowledge. Rather, this was a precedent from God to these Bedouins by forbidding them from lying and saying anything different from what they are in their religion.
