## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 42–43. Al-Tabari mentions that the polytheists swore by Allah with all their oaths that if a warner came to them, they would surely be more guided than one of the nations. But when a warner came to them, it only increased their aversion, arrogance on earth and evil plotting, and evil plotting only befalls its people. Do they look beyond the tradition of the ancients?
