## Summary
Malik reported that it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab and Sulayman ibn Yasar were asked: is the blood-money made heavier in the sacred month? They said: no, but it is increased because of its sanctity. It was said to Said: is the blood-money increased for wounds as it is for the soul? He said: yes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.439}
