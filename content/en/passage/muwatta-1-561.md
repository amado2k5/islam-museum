## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Sumayy, client of Abu Bakr, from Abu Salih al-Samman from Abu Hurayra: the Prophet said that whoever says "Glory be to God and praise be to Him" a hundred times in a day has his sins removed, even if they are like the foam of the sea. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Ibn Hibban, al-Baghawi, Ibn Majah and al-Tirmidhi from Malik's transmitters. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.288}
