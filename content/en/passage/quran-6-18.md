## Summary
Verse 18 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 18 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

By "he is the all-subduing above his servants, and he is the wise, the aware" al-Tabari means God himself, that is, God prevails above his servants; by "the subduer" he means the one who humbles his creatures and makes them servants, and is above them, and he said "above his servants" because he described himself by his overpowering them, and part of the description of whoever overpowers something is that he is above it. So the sense is: God overcomes his servants, abasing them, and is above them by abasing them and creating them; he is above them by overpowering them and they are beneath him; "he is the wise" means wise in his height above his servants, in overpowering them by his power and in all his ordering, "the aware" of the good and harm of things, from whom the ends and beginnings of matters are not hidden, in whose ordering there is no flaw, and in whose judgement no defect enters. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 288}

The editor keeps "God prevails" from the manuscript and refers for "wise" and "aware" to the language indexes.

We stop here at the foot of p. 288, after the commentary on verse 18, and al-Tabari begins verse 19 on p. 289.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
