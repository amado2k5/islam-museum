## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 as one section. He explains His saying, “And for those who disbelieve in their Lord is the punishment of Hell, and wretched is the destination”: “And for those who disbelieve in their Lord,” who created them in this world, “is the punishment of Hell” in the Hereafter, “and wretched is the destination.” He says: And wretched is the destination, the punishment of Hell. His statement, “When they are thrown into it,” means when the disbelievers are thrown into Hell, “they will hear from it,” meaning from Hell, “a groaning sound.” By “groaning” he means the sound that comes out of the chest with great force, like the braying of a donkey, as Ru’bah said in describing a donkey: “It rattled in its chest, a snarl, or a groan, until it was said, ‘It is braying,’ though it did not bray.” And his statement, “While it is boiling,” means it is bubbling. The passage also quotes statements from Mujahid and Sufyan.
