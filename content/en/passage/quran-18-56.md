## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We do not send the messengers except as bringers of good tidings and warners. And those who disbelieve in falsehood argue with falsehood to refute the truth and take My verses; but what they warn is in ridicule": The Almighty says: "And We do not send except to give glad tidings to the people of faith and belief in Allah of His great reward in the Hereafter, and to warn the disbelievers of His great punishment and painful torment, so they desist from associating partners with Allah and refrain from disbelief in Him and His sins." "And those who disbelieve argue with falsehood to refute the truth," meaning: And those who deny Allah and His Messenger argue with falsehood, as they said to the Prophet: Tell us about the story of young men who went away at the beginning of time without knowing their fate, about the man who reached the east and west of the earth, about the soul, and similar things they used to dispute with, seeking to overthrow him, meaning him. Allah said to them: We do not send our messengers to you for argument and disputes, but rather we send them as bringers of Paradise and warners of the disbelievers of the Fire, while you dispute with them falsely
