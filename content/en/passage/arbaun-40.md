## Summary
Hadith of Ibn Umar: the Prophet took him by the shoulders and said, "Be in this world as a stranger or a passer-by", and Ibn Umar added, "When you are in the evening do not wait for morning, and in the morning do not wait for evening; take from your health for your sickness and from your life for your death". Ibn Rajab takes it as the root of shortening hope. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
