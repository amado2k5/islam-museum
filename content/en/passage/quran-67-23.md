## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Say, ‘It is He who created you and made for you hearing, sight, and hearts. Little are you grateful,’” is explained as follows: Say, O Muhammad, to those among the polytheists who deny the Resurrection: God is the One who created you and made you hear, sight, and hearts. “Little are you grateful,” meaning: You are very ungrateful to your Lord for these blessings He has bestowed upon you.
