## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, Allah commands justice, good deeds, giving to relatives, and forbids immorality, wrongdoing, and oppression. He admonishes you so that you may be reminded": Indeed, Allah commands in this Book that He revealed to you, O Muhammad, justice, which is fairness. Among the fairness is acknowledging the One who has bestowed His favor upon us, thanking Him for His favor, and giving praise to His people. If that is justice, and idols and idols among us have no hand worthy of praise, then it is ignorance of us to praise and worship them, as they neither benefit nor benefit and are worshiped. Therefore, we must bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. Therefore, those who said: justice in this context is a testimony that there is no god but Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
