## Overview
The Book of Sales (Buyu') is the seventeenth book of the [[work/muwatta-malik|Muwatta]] in the transmission of [[person/yahya-ibn-yahya-al-laythi|Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi]] from [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik ibn Anas]], in the edition of Bashshar Awwad Ma'ruf (1997), in the second volume. It occupies pages 129 to 220 in the PDF pagination, and contains 226 numbered texts (1781–2006) in forty-six chapters. It concerns [[concept/bay|sale]], its conditions and its defects, and [[concept/riba|usury]], [[concept/salaf|advance payment]] and [[concept/gharar|uncertainty]]. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.129} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.220}

## What the book gathers
- **The first sales, the warranty and defects** (1781–1800): the earnest-money sale, the property of a slave, the warranty on the sale of slaves, and the defects of slaves and their return. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.129} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.138}
- **Fruit, crops, calamity and the ariya** (1801–1825): the prohibition of selling fruit before it is ripe, the sale of ariya, calamity to fruit, the exception of fruit on the tree, and the sale of dates for dates. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.139} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.147}
- **Exchange, mutual weighing and 'inah** (1826–1861): gold for silver, muzabana and muhaqala, mutual weighing of coins, 'inah, and the sale of food before it is taken into possession. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.148} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.167}
- **Animals, meat, dogs and advance payment** (1862–1925): the sale of animals, the sale of an animal for meat, the price of a dog, and advance payment on animals and goods. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.181} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188}
- **Uncertainty, touching and throwing, murabaha, option, and insolvency** (1926–2006): sale of uncertainty, the touching and throwing sales, murabaha, the barnamaj, the sale with option, usury on debt, partnership and rescission, the insolvency of a debtor, advance payment, and haggling and najash. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.194} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.220}

## Malik's method in the book
Malik sets down "the matter agreed upon among us" on many questions, distinguishes sale from advance payment and 'inah by possession and term, and disapproves of any sale that leads to usury or uncertainty, even where its outward form is sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.169} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.194}

## The editor's notes
The editor quotes Ibn Abd al-Barr on the chain of the hadith forbidding uncertain sales, on the hadith of touching and throwing that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted with a connected chain, and on the hadith "no deception". He shows that some hadiths of the book are cut off in the Muwatta, and that some are connected in other collections. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.197} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.209}

## Its place in the museum
Each text of the book has its own record. It names the highest narrator and the chapter, gives the substance of the report in the museum's own wording, and records the editor's takhrij where it is needed. See [[concept/bay|Sale]] for the places where its rulings appear in the book. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.129}
