## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger said: none of you should prevent his neighbour from fixing a wooden beam into his wall. Then Abu Hurayra said: why do I see you turning away from it? By God, I will hurl it between your shoulders. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.290}
