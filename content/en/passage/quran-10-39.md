**Commentary on verse 39 (p. 93)**

On [[passage/quran-10-39|10:39]] he says: it is not your denial, Muhammad, that troubles these polytheists, but their denial of what they did not grasp in knowledge of what God sent down to you in this Qur'an, the warning of what awaits their disbelief; "and its fulfilment has not yet come to them" means that what that warning leads to has not yet come. "Thus did those before them deny": as these deny God's warning, so did the nations before them deny His warning about denying their messengers. "See how the wrongdoers ended": see, Muhammad, how the disbelief of those who disbelieved ended; did We not destroy some by the quake, some by being swallowed in the earth and some by drowning? The end of those among your people who deny you will be like the end of earlier disbelieving nations unless they turn from disbelief and hasten to repent. The editor refers to the glosses on "grasping", "fulfilment" and "outcome". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 93}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
