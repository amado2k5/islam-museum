## Summary
Yahya said: Malik said: the matter on which there is no disagreement among us is that no woman swears in the oath of fifty for deliberate killing, even if the victim has no heirs but women; women have neither oath nor pardon in deliberate killing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.456}
