## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 21 as one section. He explains His saying, “But when He tries him and restricts his provision, he says, ‘My Lord has humiliated me’”: His saying, “But when He tries him and restricts his provision,” means: But when his Lord tests him with poverty, “and restricts his provision,” he says: He makes his provision scarce and limited, so his wealth does not increase, nor is it made abundant for him, “and he says, ‘My Lord has humiliated me’,” he says: That person says: My Lord has humiliated me, he says: He has humbled me with poverty, and he does not thank God for what He has given him of sound limbs and the provision of health in his body. And Qatadah said regarding the verse, “But when He tries him and restricts his provision, he says, ‘My Lord has humiliated me’”: How quickly the son of Adam becomes ungrateful! Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase, “and restricts his provision,” that it means He makes it difficult. The passage also includes statements from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
