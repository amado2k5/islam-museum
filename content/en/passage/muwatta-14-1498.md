## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu Hazim ibn Dinar from Sahl ibn Sa'd al-Sa'idi: a woman came to the Messenger of God and said she had given herself to him. He stood a long time, and a man stood and said: marry her to me, if you have no need of her. He asked: do you have anything to give her as a dowry? The man said he had nothing but his waist-wrap. The Prophet said: if you give it to her you will sit with no waist-wrap; look for something. He looked and found nothing, and the Prophet asked: do you have any of the Qur'an with you? He said yes, some surahs he named. The Prophet said: I have married her to you for what you have of the Qur'an. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.30}
