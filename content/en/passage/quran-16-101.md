## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when We substitute a verse for another, and Allah knows best what He sends," they say, "You are only a fabricator." Rather, most of them do not know. And if We abrogate the ruling of one verse and replace it with another, "And Allah knows best what He sends," meaning: And Allah knows best what is best for His creation in what He changes and alters His rulings. "They say, 'You are only a fabricator.' He means: The polytheists who denied Allah, His Messenger, said to His Messenger: 'You are but a fabricator, O Muhammad, that is, a liar, you are a liar by saying falsehood against Allah.' Allah Almighty says: Rather, most of those who say to you, O Muhammad, are but fabricators, ignorant, that what you bring to them from Allah is abrogating and abrogated, and they do not know its true authenticity. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
