## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The people ask you about the Hour. Say, 'Its knowledge is only with Allah, and what will He know you? Perhaps the Hour is near.'" He explains: "People ask you, O Muhammad, about the Hour." When will it be established? Say to them: The knowledge of the Hour is only with Allah; no one knows its time except Him. "And what do you know? Perhaps the Hour is near." He means: What makes you aware, O Muhammad, that perhaps the Hour will be near to you, the time of its establishment is near, and its time is near.
