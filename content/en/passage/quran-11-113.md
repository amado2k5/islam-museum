**Commentary on verse 113 (pp. 500-501)**

On [[passage/quran-11-113|11:113]] al-Tabari says: do not incline, people, to the words of those who disbelieved in God, accepting from them and approving of their deeds, 'lest the Fire touch you' through doing so, 'and you have no protectors besides God', no helper to help you and no guardian over you, 'then you will not be helped', for if you do that God will not help you but leave you without His help and give your enemy power over you. Report 18602, from Ibn Abbas: inclining is inclining to polytheism; 18603, from Abu al-'Aliya: do not approve of their deeds. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 500}

Report 18604, from Abu al-'Aliya: inclining is approving; 18605, from him, likewise; 18606, from Ibn Abbas: do not lean towards those who did wrong; 18607, from Qatada: do not join polytheism, which you have left; 18608, from Ibn Zayd: inclining is compromise, and he recited from [[passage/quran-68-9|68:9]] 'they wish you would compromise so they would compromise'; he said, you incline to them and do not condemn what they said, when they have said the gravest of their disbelief in God, His Book and His messengers; this is for the people of disbelief and polytheism and not for the people of Islam, for as to sinners among the people of Islam God knows best their sins and deeds, and it is not for anyone to make peace over any of God's disobediences nor incline to it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 501}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
