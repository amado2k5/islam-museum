## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among His signs is the creation of the heavens and the earth and the diversity of your tongues and colors. Indeed, in that are signs for the worlds." Among his arguments and evidence is also that nothing can prevent Him, and that if He wills, He causes the death of those who are alive among His creation. Then, if He wills, He can spread it and restore it as it was before He died. He created the heavens and the earth without anything He created, but by His power, which nothing can prevent Him. "And the difference of your tongues," meaning he says: And the difference in the logic of your tongues and their languages, and your colors—he says: And the difference in the colors of your bodies—'Indeed, in that are signs for the worlds.' He says: In doing so are lessons and proofs for His creation, who understand that it is not a fault in him to return them to the form they were in before their death after their death. We have explained the meaning of the worlds in the past.
