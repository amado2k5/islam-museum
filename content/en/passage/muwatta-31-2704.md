## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger of God wore a gold ring; then the Messenger of God rose, threw it away, and said: I will never wear it again. So people threw away their rings. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.525}
