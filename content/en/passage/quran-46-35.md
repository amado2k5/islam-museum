## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So be patient as those of firm resolve among the messengers were patient, and do not hasten against them, as if they were the Day they saw what they were promised, but they did not remain but an hour of the day of the message. So will none perish except the defiantly disobedient people?" The Exalted says to His Prophet Muhammad, affirming his commitment to carrying the burden of the message and the heavy burdens of prophethood, and commanding him to be determined to influence that by those with resolute resolve before him among His messengers who were patient with the great hardships they faced from their people, and the harm and hardships they suffered from them. "So be patient, O Muhammad, for what befalls you in the cause of Allah from the harm inflicted on your deniers among your people to whom We sent you with warning, as those who are determined to be patient in fulfilling Allah's command and ending in obedience to His messengers who did not forbid them from interfering with His command, no matter how severe it befell them." It is also said: The resolute among them were those who were tested by trials in the presence of Allah in this world, and trials only increased them greatly in the matter of Allah, such as Noah, Abraham, Moses, and similar ones. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
