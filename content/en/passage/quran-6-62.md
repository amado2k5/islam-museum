## Summary
Verse 62 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 62 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "then they are returned to God, their true master; his is the judgement, and he is the swiftest of reckoners" al-Tabari says: then the angels who took them and seized their selves and souls are returned to God, their true lord; "his is the judgement" says: judgement and decree are his alone, apart from all the rest of his creation; "he is the swiftest of reckoners" says: he is swifter than any to reckon your number, your deeds, your terms and the rest of your affairs, people, counting them and knowing their measures and totals, for he does not reckon by tying a knot with the hand but knows it, nothing of it hidden from him, and not the weight of an atom in the heavens or the earth, nor anything smaller or greater, escapes him but is in a clear book. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 413}

The editor says this incorporates a verse of Surat Saba, [[passage/quran-34-3|34:3]], and refers for "master", "judgement" and "reckoning" to earlier passages.

We stop here at the foot of p. 413, after the commentary on verse 62, and al-Tabari goes on to verse 63 on p. 414.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
