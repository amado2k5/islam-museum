## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And those who accuse their wives and have no witnesses except themselves, the testimony of one of them is four testimonies by Allah; indeed, he is among the truthful." "And those who accuse" their wives of immorality, accusing them of adultery, "and they have no witnesses" who testify to the truth of what they accused them of immorality. "So the testimony of one of them is four testimonies by Allah; indeed, he is of the truthful." The reciters differed in their interpretation; the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "four testimonies" in the accusative case, and their accusative is twofold: one is that the testimony in his saying: "So the testimony of one of them" is in the nominative case before it, and the "four" is accusative in the sense of testimony. Thus, the interpretation of the statement is: one must testify four testimonies of God.
