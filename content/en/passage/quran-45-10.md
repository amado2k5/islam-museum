## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Behind them is Hell, and what they have earned and what they have taken as allies besides Allah will not avail them": and behind these who mock God's signs means those who are before them. We have explained the reason for which it is said that what is before you is behind you, in what has been said about what is beyond repetition, he says: 'From before them is the Fire of Hell, which they have returned, and what they have earned will not avail them at all.' He means: 'Nor will they benefit from the punishment of Hell if they are punished by what they have earned in this world of wealth and children at all.' And His saying: 'Nor will they take besides Allah as allies,' meaning: nor their gods whom they worshipped besides Allah, nor their leaders, who are those whom they obeyed in disbelief in Allah and took as helpers in this world, will be of any avail to them that Day from the punishment of Hell. 'And for them is a great punishment,' meaning: 'And for them is a great punishment from Allah on that Day.'
