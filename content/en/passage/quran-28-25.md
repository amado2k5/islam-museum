## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Suddenly, one of them was walking shyly, saying, 'My father is calling you to reward you for what you gave us.' When she came and told him the stories, he said, 'Do not be afraid, you have escaped from the wrongdoing people.'" So one of the two women for whom Moses was given water came walking shyly before Moses, covering her face with her garment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq and Sufyan.
