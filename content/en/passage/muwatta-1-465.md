## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he came to the mosque after people had prayed, began with the obligatory prayer and prayed nothing before it. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.239}
