## Place in the museum

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-70|7:70]], al-Tabari says: the people of Ad said to Hud, have you come to threaten us with God's punishment for the religion we follow, so that we should worship God alone and give Him pure obedience, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 520}

and abandon the gods and idols our fathers worshipped and disown them? We will not do it and we will not follow you in what you call us to, so bring upon us the punishment you promise for our refusing to devote ourselves to God's oneness and for worshipping what we worship besides Him, if you are truthful in what you say and promise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 521}
