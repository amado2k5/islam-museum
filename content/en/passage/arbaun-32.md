## Summary
Hadith of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri: "There should be no harm and no reciprocal harm". An-Nawawi calls it good; Ibn Rajab shows that the chain is disputed between the connected and the broken forms, and that scholars read it as forbidding harm to oneself and others and answering harm with harm. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
