## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 50 to 51 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that these nine betrayed Salih by going to him at night to kill him and his family without realizing it. So God plotted against them, meaning He took them with His punishment suddenly without realizing it. He referred the meaning of God's plot to the past, that He took those who took him out of heedlessness or lured him into disbelief.
