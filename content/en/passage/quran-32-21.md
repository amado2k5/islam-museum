## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We will make them taste the lesser punishment than the greater punishment that they might return": The interpreters differed on the meaning of the lesser punishment that Allah promised these wicked people would taste. Some said: That is the calamities of this world in lives and wealth. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Ibrahim, and others.
