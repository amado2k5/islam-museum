## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Allah is gentle with His servants, providing for whom He wills, and He is the Strong, the Mighty": Allah is kind to His servants, He provides for whom He wills and is generous to whom He wills among them. "And He is the Powerful," whom no one can overcome due to His severity, nor can He withhold His punishment by His power, the "Almighty" in retribution. If He takes revenge on those who committed sins: "Whoever desires the plowing of the Hereafter, We will increase for him in his crop." Allah Almighty says: Whoever intends for the Hereafter through his deeds, We will increase for him in his crops: He says: We will increase for him in his good deeds, and We will make for him in one number of increases, until our Lord wills of increase. "And whoever desires the plowing of this world, We will grant him from it," meaning: whoever desires by his deeds this world and strives for it, not for the Hereafter, We will grant him from it what We have allotted for him. "And he will have no share in the Hereafter." He means: It is not for those who seek this worldly deeds and Allah did not intend them in the reward of Allah for the people of deeds. He also quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
