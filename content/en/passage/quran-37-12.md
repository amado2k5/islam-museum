## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 13 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "So ask them who are more powerful in creation or than We created. Indeed, We created them from sticky clay": So ask them, O Muhammad, those polytheists who deny resurrection after death and resurrection after trial: He asks them: Is they more of a stronger character? He says: Are they created more strong? Or does He create from among us His creation from angels, devils, heavens, and the earth? He mentioned that this is in the recitation of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud: "More of a stronger character or more than our number"? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, and Al-A'mash.
