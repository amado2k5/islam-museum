**Commentary on verse 108 (pp. 291-292)**

On "say, this is my way; I call to God with insight, I and those who follow me; glory be to God, and I am not of the polytheists", al-Tabari says: God says to His prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, this call to which I call and the way I am on, of calling to God's oneness, sincere worship of Him and not of the gods and idols, and reaching obedience to Him and abandoning disobedience to Him, is "my way", my path and my call; I call to God alone, who has no partner, "with insight" into it and certain knowledge of it on my part, and those who follow me, believe me and have faith in me call to Him with insight too; "glory be to God", He says to him: say it, declaring God exalted above having a partner in His dominion or any object of worship besides Him in His authority, "and I am not of the polytheists", that is, I am free of the people who associate others with Him, I am not of them and they are not of me. The editor refers to the earlier discussions of "sabil", "basira" and "subhan". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 291}

The interpreters said the same: report 19981, from al-Rabi' ibn Anas, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Ibn Abi Ja'far and his father: "say, this is my way; I call to God with insight", he says, this is my call. Report 19982, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: "this is my way", this is my affair, my custom and my method, "I call to God with insight, I and those who follow me"; he said: and by God it is the duty of whoever follows him to call to what he called to, to remind with the Qur'an and admonition and to forbid disobedience to God. Reports 19983 and 19984, from al-Rabi' ibn Anas, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Abu Ja'far, and Ibn Humayd and Hakkam: this is my call. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 292}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
