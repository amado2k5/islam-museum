## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if you asked them who created the heavens and the earth and subjected the sun and the moon, they would say, 'Allah, then how can they deceive?": And if you asked, O Muhammad, about these polytheists who created the heavens and the earth, He made them and subjected the sun and the moon to His servants, running constantly for the interests of Allah's creation, to say, 'He who created and did that, Allah did, so how can they deceive?' He says: How can they turn away from the One who made that, so they turn away from the one who made it, so they turn away from sincerity in worship to Him? Also: And from Qatadah, "so how can they turn away": meaning they are just.
