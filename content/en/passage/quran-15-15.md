## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari presents the disagreement among the interpreters regarding 'so they continued to ascend therein': The first is that if We opened a gate from the sky for the polytheists and the angels kept ascending through it, the children of Adam would see them with their eyes and say, 'Our eyes have been closed; rather, we are a people bewitched'—from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dahhak, and Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj points out that this refers to their claim to the angels in the previous verse, and that Quraysh is the one who said this. The second is that the ascending are the polytheists themselves, and if a door were opened for them and they ascended, they would say, 'Our eyes are closed.' And from Ibn Abbas that meteors do not kill but burn and cause madness.
