**Commentary on verse 123 (p. 33)**

Al-Tabari says: Pharaoh said to the sorcerers when they believed in God, that is, held His messenger Moses to be true after seeing God's great power and authority: "You believed in him", meaning you held Moses true and acknowledged his prophethood, "before I gave you leave" to believe in him; "this", that is your holding him true and acknowledging his prophethood, "is a plot you have plotted in the city", a deception you practised on those in our city "to drive its people out of it"; "you will soon know" what I shall do to you and the punishment of mine that you will meet for this deed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 33}

Report 14955, from al-Suddi, in a narrative he related from Abu Malik, from Ali ibn Abi Talha from Ibn Abbas, from Murra from Ibn Mas'ud, and from some Companions of the Messenger, through Musa ibn Harun, Amr and Asbat: Moses met the chief of the sorcerers and asked him, if I overcome you, will you believe in me and testify that what I brought is true? The sorcerer said: tomorrow I shall bring a sorcery that no sorcery can beat; by God, if you overcome me I shall believe in you and testify that you are right. Pharaoh was watching them, and so his saying "this is a plot you have plotted in the city" refers to their meeting, to join together and drive its people out. The editor says the report is part of a long one al-Tabari gives in his History (vol. 1, p. 213). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 33}
