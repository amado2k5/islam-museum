## Summary
Verse 155 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 155 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says the sense is: then because of these People of the Book's breaking of their covenant, that is, the pledges they made to God to act on what is in the Torah; and their disbelief in God's signs, that is, their denial of His proofs by which He argued against them for the truth of His prophets and messengers; and their killing of the prophets without right after the proof had been set against them, with no sin deserving death. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 363}

Their saying 'our hearts are wrapped' means they say there is a covering and wrapping over them against what you call us to, so we do not understand what you say; al-Tabari has explained the sense of 'wrapped' and the reports on it earlier. 'God sealed them for their disbelief' means that they lied in saying our hearts are wrapped; they are not wrapped, nor have they coverings, but God set a seal upon them for their disbelief in Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 363}
