## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad Abdullah ibn Dhakwan, from al-A'raj Abd al-Rahman ibn Hormuz, from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on performing ablution: the Prophet told the one who performs ablution to put water in his nose and blow it out, and whoever cleans himself with stones to make the number odd. The editor explains istijmar as wiping with small stones, and cites al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Hibban. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.51}
