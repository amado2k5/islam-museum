## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. He creates whatever He wills. He grants females to whom He wills and grants males to whom He wills." He grants to whom He wills of the seven heavens and the earths. He does with His authority whatever He wills and creates what He loves for His creation. He grants to whomever He wills of His creation, females, not males, by making every child His wife carries a female from him. "And He grants males to whom He wills." He means: He grants males to whom He wills among them, by making every lamb carried by his wife a male, not a female among them. And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "Or he marries them to males and females," he said: He mixes them up, saying: Marriage is when a woman gives birth to a boy, then gives birth to a girl, then gives birth to a boy, then gives birth to a girl. In the Maqta, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
