## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam from his father Aslam, client of Umar, in the chapter on ablution after madhi: Umar ibn al-Khattab said he finds madhi running from him like a small bead, and ordered whoever finds it to wash his penis and perform ablution for prayer. The editor explains kharaza as the diminutive of kharza, and notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted from al-A'raj that Umar said from the pulpit that something runs from him like pearls or beads, which he did not mind. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.83}
