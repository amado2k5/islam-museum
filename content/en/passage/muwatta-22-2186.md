## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: if a man buys a garment which has a defect, from burning or something else, which the seller knew, and which he testified to or acknowledged, and then the buyer cut the garment, reducing its price, and then the buyer learned of the defect: he returns it to the seller, and the buyer is not liable for its cutting. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.297}
