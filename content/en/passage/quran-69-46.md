## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 46 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Then We would have severed his aorta” as referring to the artery of the heart. My father told me, saying: My uncle told me, saying: My father told me, on the authority of his father, on the authority of Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase “Then We would have severed his aorta,” that it means a artery in the heart, and it is also said to be a cord in the heart. On the authority of Ibn Abi Najih, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding the phrase “the aorta,” he said: the cord of the heart that is in the back. On the authority of Qatadah, regarding the phrase “Then We would have severed his aorta,” he said: the cord of the heart. It was narrated to me on the authority of Al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Ad-Dahhak say regarding the phrase “We would have severed his aorta,” that it refers to the aorta of the heart, which is a artery in the heart, and if it is severed, a person dies. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Saeed bin Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
