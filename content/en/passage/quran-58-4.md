## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So whoever cannot find means to fast two consecutive months before they continuate, then whoever cannot afford to fast for sixty needy people, that you may believe in Allah and His Messenger. And those are the limits set by Allah. And for the disbelievers is a painful punishment." So whoever among you cannot find a slave from his wife to free her, he must fast for two consecutive months before they consecutive. The two consecutive months are those that are not separated by breaking the fast during the day except for an excuse. If breaking the fast is due to an excuse, there is disagreement among scholars. Some said: If breaking the fast is for an excuse, then the excuse is removed based on the previous fast. Others said: Rather, it should be resumed, because whoever breaks the fast with or without an excuse does not continue fasting for two months. In the Maqta, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ata.
