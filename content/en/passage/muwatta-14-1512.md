## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Humayd al-Tawil from Anas ibn Malik, who used to say: for a virgin, seven nights; for a previously married woman, three. Malik says that is the practice among us. The editor notes that the hadith is raised in the Sahihayn through Abu Qilaba from Anas. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.35}
