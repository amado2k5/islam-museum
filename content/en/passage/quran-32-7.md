## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 6–8. Al-Tabari explains that God is the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, meaning what is hidden from sight and what is seen, the mighty in taking revenge on those who disbelieve in Him, and merciful to those who repent. He then mentions the difference among readers regarding 'He created Him' (with a sukun on the lam and a fatha on it) regarding His saying, 'He who perfected everything He created,' and explains that God perfected the creation of everything and began to create man from clay.
