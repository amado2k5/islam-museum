## Summary
Verse 73 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 73 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (p. 540).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: if God gives you victory over your enemy and you take booty from them, this hypocrite who held Muslims back from jihad will speak as though no tie of friendship joined him to you, wishing he had been with them to win a rich prize from the booty they took. He says this reports of the hypocrites that they attend war with Muslims, if they do, to seek booty, and stay behind, if they do, from the doubt in their hearts, hoping for no reward for attending and fearing no punishment from God for staying behind. Qatada and Ibn Jurayj used to say that what these hypocrites said when victory was the Muslims' came from envy of them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 540}

**Reports 9940-9941 (p. 540).** Bishr b. Mu'adh (9940, from Yazid, from Sa'id) reports from Qatada that this is the speech of an envier. Al-Qasim (9941, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj) reports from Ibn Jurayj that the bounty is the Muslims' prevailing over their enemy and taking booty, and what is then said is the speech of the envier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 540}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 540}
