## Summary
A saying of Malik: the practice among us is that if a man stipulates to a woman, even at the moment of the marriage contract, that he will not marry another over her and will not take a concubine, that is nothing, unless it involves an oath of divorce or manumission, in which case it is binding on him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.36}
