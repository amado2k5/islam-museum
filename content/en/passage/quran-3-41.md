## Summary
Verse 41 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 41 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The request for a sign.** Al-Tabari says Zechariah told his Lord that if this call and the voice he heard were the voice of His angels and a good news from Him, He should give him a sign that it was so, to remove what Satan had whispered and cast into his heart, that the voice was other than the angels' and the news from other than God. Report 7004 (Musa, from Amr, from Asbat, from al-Suddi) says he said that if the voice was from Him, He should give him a sign. Al-Tabari says he has already explained the meaning of the word for sign, that it is a mark; the editor refers to the earlier passage and to the language index. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 384}

**The omission of the hamza in the word for 'sign'.** Al-Tabari says the Arabic scholars differed on why the Arabs leave the hamza out of this word, when they normally put it on every ya that follows a silent alif. Some said it was 'ayya', and the doubling was heavy for them so they changed it to alif because the letter before it has fatha, as they say 'ayma fulan'. Others said it is a shortened 'fa'ila' form; the questioners asked why the Arabs diminish it as 'uyayya' and not 'uwayya', and they replied as with Fatima, 'Futayma'; they were answered that the Arabs diminish the 'fa'ila' form to 'fu'ayla' only when it is a name in the sense of 'so-and-so', not otherwise. Others said it is a 'fa'la' form whose first ya became alif as in 'haja' and 'qama'; they were answered that the Arabs do that only in triliteral nouns, and one who rejected the view said that if it were so, the word for 'date-stone' would be 'naya' and for 'life' 'haya'. The editor says the first speaker is al-Kisa'i and his companions, the questioners are al-Farra and his companions, refers to Lisan al-Arab, thinks all these replies are al-Farra's as the text of the Lisan shows, finds some disorder in al-Tabari's text, suspecting the copyist dropped or moved something, explains 'sons of three' as the triliteral noun, and says both witnesses wrote a word in a corrupt form and the right one has the ya doubled. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 384} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 385}

**The start of al-Tabari's comment on the sign.** Al-Tabari says God punished Zechariah, as is reported, for asking the sign after the angels had addressed him with the glad tidings face to face, so He made his sign, to confirm that the news he heard was from God, and the comment continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 385}
