## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And you see them presented to it humbled in humiliation, looking from a hidden aspect." And He said, "Indeed, the losers are those who have lost themselves and their families on the Day of Resurrection." Indeed, the wrongdoers will be in a lasting punishment.' And you see, O Muhammad, the wrongdoers will be presented to the Fire, humbled and submissive. Also: Ibn Zayd said: Humility means fear and reverence for Allah, the Almighty, and he read Allah's saying: 'When they saw the punishment...' up to His saying: 'humble from humiliation,' he said: The fear that befell them humiliated them, and they humbled themselves. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase 'humble,' he said: humble from humiliation. And His saying: 'They look from a hidden glance' means: these wrongdoers look at the Fire when they are presented to it from a hidden glance. The scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning of the phrase: 'from a hidden glance,' and some said it means: from the side of a humiliated person. As if the meaning of the speech is: from a side that has been hidden from humiliation. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
