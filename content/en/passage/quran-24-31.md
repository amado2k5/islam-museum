## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "And tell the believing women to lower their gaze and guard their private parts and not display their adornment except what is apparent thereof and to draw their veils over their bosoms and not display their adornment except to their husbands or their fathers or their husbands' fathers or their husbands or their sons or their husbands' sons or their brothers or their brothers or their sisters or their wives or what their right hands possess or the followers who are not of legal authority Men or children who do not show the private parts of women and do not strike their feet so that what they conceal of their adornment is known, and repent to Allah all together. O believers, perhaps you will succeed." "And say," O Muhammad, to the believing women of your nation, "to lower their gaze" from what Allah hates to look at, which He forbids you from looking at, "and guard their private parts." He means: "And guard their private parts so that those who do not allow them to see them may see them, by wearing what conceals them from their eyes." And His saying: "And they shall not display their adornment," means that Allah says: 'And they should not display their adornment to people who are not in a mahram for them.' They are two adornments: one is what is hidden, such as anklets, bracelets, earrings, and necklaces. The other is what is apparent from them, and this differs in its meaning in this verse. Some used to say: the adornment of apparent garments. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
