## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, 'Have you considered, if it is from Allah, then you disbelieve in it? Who is more astray than one who is in distant discord": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, 'Say,' O Muhammad, to those who deny what you have brought them from your Lord of this Qur'an, 'Have you, O people, said, 'If it is' that you deny, 'from Allah, then you disbelieve in it?' Are you not separated from the truth and far from the truth? He placed the place of separation with the report, saying: "Who is more misguided than one who is in a distant discord?" if its meaning is understood. And His saying: "Who is more astray than one who is in distant discord," means: Tell them who is more devoted to the right path and who takes a path other than the right path, who is separated from God's command and fears Him, far from guidance.
