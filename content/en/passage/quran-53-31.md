## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "To Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, so that He may recompense those who do evil for what they have done and reward those who do good with good": "And to Allah belongs the dominion of whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth," and He misleads whom He wills, and He knows them best. "To recompense those who did evil for what they did," meaning: to recompense those who disobeyed Him among His creation and wronged Him by their disobedience, so He rewards them with the Fire. "And He rewards those who did good with the best reward," meaning: And He may reward those who obey Him and do well in their obedience to Him in this world with the best reward, which is Paradise, and He will reward them thereby. It is also said: This refers to the people of polytheism and faith. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Zayd ibn Aslam.
