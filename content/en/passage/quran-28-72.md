## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Have you considered, if Allah makes the day for you eternal until the Day of Resurrection, who is there to bring you a night in which you may rest? Will you not see?": "Say," O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people, "Have you, O people, said, 'If Allah makes the day for you everlasting,' there will never be night with Him forever, 'until the Day of Resurrection, from a god other than Allah,' from an idol other than the One who worships everything, 'He will come to you at night in which you will dwell,' so you will settle and find peace therein. 'Will you not see?' He says: Do you not see with your eyes the difference between night and day for you, a mercy from Allah to you and a proof from Him against you? So know that worship is only suitable for those who have blessed you with that and not others, and for those who have the ability to differ between them.
