## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Ata ibn Yasar, when someone selling in the mosque passed by him, would call him and ask what he had and what he wanted. If he said he wanted to sell it, Ata would tell him to stick to the market of this world, for this is the market of the Hereafter. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.248}
