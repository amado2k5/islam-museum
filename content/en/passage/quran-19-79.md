## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 79 to 80 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "No, We will record what he says and prolong for him of the punishment in length": He means mentioning him by saying "No," he means that it is not so; He has not seen the unseen, so He knows the truth of what he says and the reality of what he mentions, and He has not taken a covenant with the Most Merciful to believe in Allah and His Messenger or to act upon His obedience, but rather he lied and disbelieved. Then He, the Exalted, said: "We will record what he says": meaning We will record what this disbeliever says with his Lord, the one who says, "We will not be given" in the Hereafter, "wealth and children, and We will extend for him from the punishment a length"—meaning: We will increase his punishment in Hell by his words of lies and falsehood in this world, in addition to his punishment for disbelief in Allah. And His saying: "And We will inherit from him what he says," the Exalted says: And We will take away this one who says: We will take away this one who says: We will be given wealth and children in the Hereafter, his wealth and children, and his wealth and children will be less than him, and he will come to us on the Day of Resurrection alone, with no wealth or children. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
