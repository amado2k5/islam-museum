## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 51 to 53 in one passage: "And inform them of the guest of Abraham." Tell me, O Muhammad, My servants, about Abraham's guest, the angels who entered upon Abraham, the friend of the Merciful, when their Lord sent them to the people of Lot to destroy them. "When they entered upon him and said, 'Peace': the guest said to Abraham 'Salaam'; 'He said, 'We are of you and a gentleman': Abraham said, 'Indeed, we are among you, fearful'—and he clarified the accusative in 'Salamana', the reason for Abraham's reverence, the differences among those who differed, and supported the correct view in the past. It is noted that 'they said peace' is the plural of the predicate about the guest, which is in one wording, as 'guest' is a noun for one and two, and the plural is like meter, diameter, and justice. And 'They said, 'Do not delay; indeed, we give you good tidings of a knowledgeable boy.'
