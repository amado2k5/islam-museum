**Commentary on verse 60 (pp. 113-114)**

On [[passage/quran-10-60|10:60]] he says: what do those who fabricate lies against God think, attributing to Him the forbidding of provisions and foods He has not forbidden them and made their sustenance, that God will do to them on the Day of Resurrection for their lie and invention? Do they suppose He will pardon and forgive them? Never; He will burn them in the blaze, abiding in it forever. "God is full of bounty to people" means that He is gracious to His creation in not hastening punishment on one who lies about Him in this world and in giving him respite until he comes before Him at the resurrection; "but most of them do not give thanks" means most people do not thank Him for this bounty and for His other favours. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 113}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
