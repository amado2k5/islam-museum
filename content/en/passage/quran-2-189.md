## Summary
Verse 189 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[concept/hums|the Hums]]. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 189 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "they ask you about the crescents; say, they are times for the people and for the hajj", al-Tabari says it was reported that the Prophet was asked about the waxing and waning of the crescents and the change in their states, and the verse was revealed as their answer. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 553} From Qatada, they asked the Prophet of God why these crescents were made, and God made them for the fasting and breaking of the fast of the Muslims, for their rites and pilgrimage, for the waiting period of their women, and for the falling due of their debts. From al-Rabi', the like; the editor discusses the form of "hall duyunihim". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 553} From Qatada, they are times for people in their pilgrimage, fasting, breaking the fast and rites. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 554} From Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn 'Abbas, and the time of their pilgrimage, the waiting period of their women, and the falling due of their debts. From al-Suddi, they are the times of divorce, menses and hajj. From al-Dahhak, the falling due of their debts, the time of their hajj and the waiting period of their women. From Ibn 'Abbas in another report, by them they know when their debts fall due, their women's waiting period and the time of their pilgrimage. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 554} From 'Ali, asked about "times for the people", he said, "they are the times of the month, thus and thus and thus", folding his thumb, "so when you see it fast, and when you see it break the fast, and if it is overcast for you complete thirty"; the editor says Jabir al-Ju'fi in the chain is very weak and that he could not identify his shaykh 'Abd Allah ibn Yahya. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 554}

Al-Tabari then gives his own interpretation: they ask you, Muhammad, about the crescents and the change in their states by waxing and waning; say that your Lord made them differ from other things so that they would be times for you and other children of Adam in their livelihoods, by which you watch for when your debts fall due, when the term of those you hired ends, when your women's waiting periods run out, and the time of your fasting and breaking of the fast. As for "and the hajj", it means "and for the hajj", that He made them also the time of your pilgrimage, by which you know the time of your rites. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 555}

On "it is not righteousness to enter houses from their backs", al-Tabari notes that it was said to have been revealed about people who, when in ihram, would not enter their houses by their doors. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 555}

On the occasion of "it is not righteousness to enter houses from their backs", al-Tabari gives reports. From al-Bara', when the Ansar had performed the hajj and returned they would not enter houses except from the back; a man of the Ansar came and entered by his door, was asked about it, and the verse was revealed; and from him, in the jahiliyya when they entered ihram they would come to houses from the backs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 556} The editor traces the report to al-Tayalisi and al-Bukhari and quotes al-Suyuti's attribution of it to others. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 556} From Qays ibn Hubtar, people in ihram would not enter an orchard or house by its door; the Prophet and his companions entered a house, and Rifa'a ibn Tabut of the Ansar climbed the wall and came in, and when the Prophet went out by the door Rifa'a went out with him; the Prophet asked what made him do it and he said "I saw you go out by it so I went out by it", and the Prophet said "I am a man of the ahmas", and Rifa'a said "if you are of the ahmas, our religion is one", and the verse was revealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 556} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 557} The editor says the chain is a weak mursal, corrects the name "Hubtar", and explains the "hums" as Quraysh and those allied with or born to them, who were strict in their religion. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 557}

From Mujahid, righteousness is not to come to houses through openings in their backs and doors in their sides, as the people of the jahiliyya made, so they were forbidden to enter by them and commanded to enter by the doors. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 557} From Ibrahim, some people of the Hijaz, once in ihram, would not enter by their house doors but entered from the back. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 557} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 558} From Mujahid in another report, when a polytheist entered ihram he would cut an opening in the back of his house and make a ladder to enter by; the Prophet came with a polytheist, entered by the door, and the man went to enter by the opening, saying "I am of the ahmas", and the Prophet said "and I am of the ahmas". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 558} From al-Zuhri, some of the Ansar when they declared ihram for 'umra would let nothing come between them and the sky, so a man who returned for a need would not enter by the door of the room and would break open the wall behind him; in the year of al-Hudaybiyya the Prophet declared ihram for 'umra and entered a room, and a man of the Ansar of Banu Salima entered after him; the Prophet said "I am of the ahmas", and the Ansari said "and I am of the ahmas", meaning "I am of your religion", and the verse was revealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 558} From Qatada, this tribe of the Ansar in the jahiliyya, when one of them declared ihram for hajj or 'umra, would not enter a house by its door unless he climbed a wall, and they became Muslims in that state; God revealed the verse, forbade them, told them their practice was no part of righteousness and commanded them to come to houses by their doors. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 558} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 559}

From al-Suddi, some Arabs when on hajj did not enter their houses by their doors; when the Prophet made the Farewell Hajj a man of them who was a Muslim stayed behind him at the door of the house and said "I am of the ahmas", meaning in ihram, and the Prophet said "and I too am of the ahmas, so enter", and he entered. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 559} From Ibn 'Abbas, some men of Medina, when one of them feared something from his enemy, would enter ihram and be safe, and once in ihram would not pass by the door of his house but make a breach at the back; the Prophet entered an orchard by its door and such a man entered with him, and a man behind him called "you are in ihram and have entered!", and he said "I am of the ahmas", and the man said "Messenger of God, if you are in ihram I am in ihram, and if you are of the ahmas I am of the ahmas", and the verse was revealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 559} From al-Rabi', the people of Medina and others, in ihram, would enter houses only from the backs; the Prophet entered the house of one of the Ansar and a man in ihram entered after him and the people objected, and when the Prophet asked he said "I saw you enter so I entered after you", and the Prophet said "I am of the ahmas", Quraysh being called the hums then, and the Ansari said "my religion is your religion", and the verse was revealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 559} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 560} From 'Ata', the people of the jahiliyya came to houses from the backs and considered it righteousness; and from Mujahid, in Ibn Jurayj's report through 'Abd Allah ibn Kathir, the verse concerned the Ansar, who did this to be righteous. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 560}

Al-Tabari gives his interpretation: it is not righteousness, people, to come to houses from the backs when you are in ihram, but righteousness is that of one who fears God, avoids what He has forbidden and obeys Him by performing the obligations; coming to houses from their backs holds no righteousness toward God, so come to them from where you wish, doors or otherwise, so long as you do not hold it forbidden under any condition to come by the doors, since it is not permitted to hold that belief, as God has not forbidden it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 560}
