## Summary
Verse 189 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[concept/hums|the Hums]]. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 189 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "they ask you about the crescents; say, they are times for the people and for the hajj", al-Tabari says it was reported that the Prophet was asked about the waxing and waning of the crescents and the change in their states, and the verse was revealed as their answer. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 553} From Qatada, they asked the Prophet of God why these crescents were made, and God made them for the fasting and breaking of the fast of the Muslims, for their rites and pilgrimage, for the waiting period of their women, and for the falling due of their debts. From al-Rabi', the like; the editor discusses the form of "hall duyunihim". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 553} From Qatada, they are times for people in their pilgrimage, fasting, breaking the fast and rites. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 554} From Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn 'Abbas, and the time of their pilgrimage, the waiting period of their women, and the falling due of their debts. From al-Suddi, they are the times of divorce, menses and hajj. From al-Dahhak, the falling due of their debts, the time of their hajj and the waiting period of their women. From Ibn 'Abbas in another report, by them they know when their debts fall due, their women's waiting period and the time of their pilgrimage. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 554} From 'Ali, asked about "times for the people", he said, "they are the times of the month, thus and thus and thus", folding his thumb, "so when you see it fast, and when you see it break the fast, and if it is overcast for you complete thirty"; the editor says Jabir al-Ju'fi in the chain is very weak and that he could not identify his shaykh 'Abd Allah ibn Yahya. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 554}

Al-Tabari then gives his own interpretation: they ask you, Muhammad, about the crescents and the change in their states by waxing and waning; say that your Lord made them differ from other things so that they would be times for you and other children of Adam in their livelihoods, by which you watch for when your debts fall due, when the term of those you hired ends, when your women's waiting periods run out, and the time of your fasting and breaking of the fast. As for "and the hajj", it means "and for the hajj", that He made them also the time of your pilgrimage, by which you know the time of your rites. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 555}

On "it is not righteousness to enter houses from their backs", al-Tabari notes that it was said to have been revealed about people who, when in ihram, would not enter their houses by their doors. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 555}
