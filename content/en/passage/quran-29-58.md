## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 57 to 59 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that Allah commands the believers among the companions of His Prophet to migrate from the land of polytheism Mecca to the land of Islam, Medina, for His land is vast, and to be patient in worshiping Him, for they are dying and will become to Him because every soul shall taste death, then to Him you will be returned. Then He informs them of the honor prepared for the patient with Him, of chambers in Paradise beneath which rivers flow.
