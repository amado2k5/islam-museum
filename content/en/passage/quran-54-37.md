## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 38 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And He warned them of Our oppression, so they turned away with the warning": And Lot warned his people of Our oppression that We had oppressed before, "so they rebelled with the warning," meaning: they denied his warning of what He warned them about, doubting them about it. And His saying: "So they disobeyed" they reacted from Al-Murayah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
