## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said the Hour will not come until a man passes by the grave of another and wishes he were in his place. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Ibn Hibban, Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari, Suwayd, al-Qanabi, Ibn Mahdi in Ahmad, and Qutayba in Muslim transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.330}
