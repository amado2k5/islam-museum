## Summary
Hadith of Sahl ibn Sa'd al-Sa'idi: a man asked for a deed that makes God and people love him, and the Prophet said, "Be detached from the world and God will love you; be detached from what people have and they will love you". Ibn Rajab shows that the chain is seriously disputed, and that the sense is correct in other texts. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
