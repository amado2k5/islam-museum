## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The adulterer shall only marry a fornicator or a polytheist woman, and an adulteress shall not marry except an adulterer or polytheist, and this is forbidden for the believers": The interpreters differed in interpreting this. Some said: This verse was revealed regarding some of those who sought permission from the Messenger of Allah to marry women who were known for adultery among the polytheists, and who were known to have banners and disliked themselves. So Allah revealed the prohibition of these women upon the believers, saying: The adulterer among the believers may only marry an adulteress or a polytheist woman, because they are, and the adulteress among those prostitutes is only married to an adulteress among the believers, polytheists, or polytheists like her, because they were polytheists. "And He forbade that for the believers." So Allah forbade their marriage according to the opinion of the people of this article with this verse.
