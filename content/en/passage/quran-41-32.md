## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "a descent from the Forgiving, the Merciful": "Your Lord has given you that as a descent from a Lord who is forgiving for your sins, merciful to you to punish you after your repentance." The accusative is a revelation on the verbal noun from the meaning of the verse: "And for you therein is what your souls desire, and for you therein is whatever you call upon," because in that is an interpretation of "Your Lord sent you down with what you desire of bliss."
