## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse "And man leaves evil with his supplication for good, and man is impatient": Allah Almighty reminds His servants of His hands with them, and He prays for evil for himself, his children, and his wealth, saying: O Allah, destroy him and curse him in his time of boredom and anger, like his prayer for good: He says: like his prayer to his Lord to grant him well-being and grant him safety in himself, his wealth, and his children. He says: If he were to respond to his prayer for himself, his wealth, and his children with evil as he would in good, he would perish. But by His grace, Allah does not respond to him in that. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibrahim.
