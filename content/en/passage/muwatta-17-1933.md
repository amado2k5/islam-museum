## Summary
Malik says: the practice among us for what is measured or weighed, which is not eaten or drunk, such as safflower, nuts, acacia leaves, catechu and the like, is that there is no harm in taking two for one of each kind hand to hand, but two for one of one kind on a term is not allowed. If the two kinds differ and the difference is clear, there is no harm in taking two for one on a term. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.191}
