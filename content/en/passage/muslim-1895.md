## Summary
A report of Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani in the chapter on the reward of whoever equips a fighter or cares for his family well, in the Book of Leadership of Sahih Muslim. Equipping the fighter and caring well for his family both have the reward of the raid. {cite:sahih-muslim}
