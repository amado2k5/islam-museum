## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Harith al-Taymi, that Aisha said: I was sleeping beside the Prophet when I missed him in the night. I touched him with my hand and put my hand on his feet while he was prostrating and saying: I seek refuge in Your pleasure from Your anger, in Your pardon from Your punishment, and in You from You. I cannot number the praises I give You; You are as You praised Yourself. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, al-Baghawi, Suwayd and Ma'n in al-Tirmidhi (3493), who called it sound good, transmitted it. Ibn Abd al-Barr says it is broken and unconnected in the Muwatta among its transmitters, and it is connected through al-Aaraj from Abu Hurayra from Aisha in Muslim, Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah and al-Nasa'i, and through Urwa from Aisha in Ibn Khuzayma and Ibn Hibban. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.294}
