## Summary
Verse 36 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 36 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "only those who hear respond; and the dead God will raise, then to him they return" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: let not the turning away of those who turn from you and from answering your call weigh on you when you call them to their Lord's oneness and acknowledging your prophethood, for none answers your call to what you call to but those whose ears God has opened to listen to the truth and for whom he has eased following right guidance, and not those on whose hearing he has set a seal, who understand of your calling them to God and to following the truth only what cattle understand of their herdsmen's calls; they are as God describes them in [[passage/quran-2-171|2:171]], "deaf, dumb, blind, so they do not understand"; "and the dead God will raise" means the unbelievers God will raise with the dead, counting them among the dead who hear no sound, grasp no call and understand no word, since they do not reflect on God's proofs, take no lesson from his signs and do not remember and so desist from the denial of God's messengers and opposition to them in which they are. The interpreters said so... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 341}

The editor refers for "response" to earlier volumes 7 and 3 and corrects "and they do not remember so they desist" to the form he gave.

We stop here at the foot of p. 341, at al-Tabari's saying that the interpreters said the like on "only those who hear respond", before the reports, which continue on p. 342.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
