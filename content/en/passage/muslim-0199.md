## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the Prophet's prayer for his community, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Every prophet has a prayer that is granted, and Muhammad saved his prayer as intercession for his community on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:sahih-muslim}
