**Commentary on verse 32 (pp. 505-509)**

Then he says on "when they said, God, if this is the truth from You, rain stones on us from the sky or bring us a painful punishment" [[passage/quran-8-32|8:32]]: remember, Muhammad, also what befell whoever said that, when I plotted against them and brought them a painful punishment, that punishment being their killing by the sword at Badr. It was said this verse also came down about al-Nadr ibn al-Harith: report 15981, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and reports 15982, 15983 and 15984, from Mujahid, that it is al-Nadr ibn al-Harith ibn Alqama ibn Kalada of Banu Abd al-Dar, the editor showing the difference over his lineage and preferring the manuscript's. Report 15985, from Ata: a man of Banu Abd al-Dar called al-Nadr ibn Kalada said what he said, and God sent down about it [[passage/quran-38-16|38:16]], [[passage/quran-6-94|6:94]] and [[passage/quran-70-1|70:1]], and Ata said a dozen or so verses of God's book came down about him; report 15986, from al-Suddi, is like it with [[passage/quran-70-1|70:1]]; report 15987, from Mujahid, on the same verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 505} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 506} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 507}

Report 15988, from Qatada: the fools of this community and its ignorant said that, and God returned with His kindness and mercy on the fools of this community and its ignorant; the editor explains the plural of "fool" and corrects the printed edition by the manuscript. Report 15989, from Ibn Ishaq: then He mentioned the delusion of Quraysh and their asking for the decision against themselves when they said what they said, meaning what Muhammad brought, "rain stones on us" as You rained them on the people of Lot, "or bring us a painful punishment" meaning some of what You punished nations before us with; it is in the Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 507}

Then he says the people of Arabic differed on why "he" enters the words "if this is the truth". Some Basrans said "the truth" is accusative because "he" was turned into an extra in the sentence, an emphasising link like the extra "ma", and it is added only where a verb cannot do without a predicate; it is not a description of "this", for if you said, I saw this he, it would not be speech, and the hidden pronoun is not a description of the apparent one but of the hidden, as in [[passage/quran-43-76|43:76]] and [[passage/quran-73-20|73:20]]; it may also run as a noun and make what follows nominative in the language of Banu Tamim. Some Kufans said the "support" he is brought in only for a sound sense, as if one said, Zayd is standing, and you said, rather Amr is the one standing. The editor explains that "support" is the Kufans' term, the Basrans saying "pronoun of separation", corrects readings of the printed edition and notes a blank in the manuscript between the brackets. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 507} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 508}

The Kufan adds that the "support" he in speech is an emphasis like their saying, I found him himself, and is not a description like "the clever" or "the wise", unlike the "he" that enters as an extra. The editor refers to the section on the support pronoun in al-Farra's Ma'ani and to earlier commentary. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 509}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
