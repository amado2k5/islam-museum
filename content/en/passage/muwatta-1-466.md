## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on practice in the congregational prayer: Abdullah ibn Umar passed a man praying and greeted him, and the man replied with words. Ibn Umar came back to him and said that whoever is greeted while praying should not speak, but should gesture with his hand. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.239}
