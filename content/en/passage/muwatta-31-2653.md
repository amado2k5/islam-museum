## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Ibn Shihab, that the Messenger of God rose at night and looked at the horizon of the sky and said: what treasures have been opened tonight, and what trials have occurred? How many clothed women in this world will be naked on the Day of Resurrection! Wake the companions of the chambers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.500}
