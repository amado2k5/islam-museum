## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And indeed, messengers before you were mocked, so what they mocked befell those who mocked them," means: If those who say to you, O Muhammad: Is this anything but a human like you, do you come to magic while you see? When they saw you, they mocked and said: This is the one who mentions your gods, disbelief on their part and audacity against Him. He mocked the messengers of Our messengers whom We sent before you to their nations, saying: So it was obligatory and revealed to those who mocked them and mocked them of their peoples "as they used to mock." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: What they used to mock befell them, mocking the affliction and punishment that their messengers feared would descend upon them. They mocked: He, the Exalted, says, 'These mockers of You will not be like their ancestors among the nations whose messengers denied their messengers, and they will be subjected to God's punishment and wrath by mocking you like what was sent down upon them.'
