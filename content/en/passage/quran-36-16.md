## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 15–17. Al-Tabari mentions that the villagers said to the messengers, 'You are nothing but human beings like us, and the Most Merciful has revealed nothing. Indeed, you deny.' The messengers said, 'Our Lord knows that we are to you as messengers, and our duty is only to deliver the clear message.'
