## In al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says "that was a community" means Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes, and reports this from Qatada and al-Rabi'; he refers back to his earlier explanation that "umma" means a group. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 128}

He explains the sense as the Prophet being told to say to those Jews and Christians who dispute about God, having concealed the testimony they hold about Abraham and those named and claimed they were Jews or Christians: they are a community that has passed on to its Lord with its deeds and hopes, with what it earned of good and against it what it earned of evil, and only its good deeds benefit it and only its evil harms it. He holds that the Jews and Christians are even likelier to gain only from what they have sent ahead, and so urges them to hasten to repentance and stop relying on the merits of fathers and forefathers, since each soul is asked on the Day of Resurrection about what it earned, not about what another earned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 128} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 129}
