## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 36 to 38 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Enter it in peace, that is the Day of Eternity": He means His mention by saying: "Enter it in peace," meaning enter this Paradise in safety from worry, anger, and punishment, and what you used to encounter in this world of hardship. Also: And from Qatadah, regarding His saying: "Enter it in peace," he said: They were safe from the punishment of Allah, and And His saying: "That is the Day of Eternity" means: This is what I have described to you, O people, as the attribute of entering Paradise from those who admit it, is the day people enter Paradise, where they will remain indefinitely. Also: According to Qatadah, "That is the Day of Eternity," they will live forever by Allah, so they will not die, they will stand in prayer and will not be down, and they will be blessed and will not be harmed. And His saying: "They will have whatever they wish therein" means: These pious people will have what they desire in this Paradise, which has been revealed for them of all that their souls desire and their eyes delight. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
