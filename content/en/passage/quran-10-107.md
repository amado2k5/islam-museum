**Commentary on verse 107 (p. 219)**

On [[passage/quran-10-107|10:107]] al-Tabari says: if God afflicts you, Muhammad, with hardship or trial, none can remove it but your Lord who afflicted you, not what these polytheists worship of gods and rivals; 'and if He intends good for you' means if your Lord intends for you ease, blessing, well-being and joy, 'none can repel His favour', meaning no one can come between you and that, turn you from it or deny it to you, for in His hand are ease and hardship, not in the hands of the gods and idols or anything else. 'He strikes with it whom He wills' means your Lord, Muhammad, strikes with ease and trial, joy and hardship whom He wills and intends 'of His servants, and He is the Forgiving' of the sins of one of His servants who repented and turned from disbelief and polytheism to belief in Him and obedience, 'the Merciful' to those who believed in Him and obeyed Him, in not punishing them after repentance and turning. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'touching', 'harm', 'removing', 'good', 'striking', 'Forgiving' and 'Merciful'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 219}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
