## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on the merit of Medina, in the Book of Pilgrimage of Sahih Muslim. He says of Mount Uhud, "a mountain that loves us and that we love", and in the same context, "this is Taba". {cite:sahih-muslim}
