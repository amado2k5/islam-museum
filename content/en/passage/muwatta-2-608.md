## Summary
Malik says he asked Ibn Shihab about a man who catches part of the takbir over a funeral and misses part of it, and he said: he makes up what he missed of it. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.312}
