## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Prophet said: whoever says to his brother, unbeliever, one of them has earned it. The editor notes that the words 'O unbeliever' are established in other transmissions but are not in the transmission of Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.579}
