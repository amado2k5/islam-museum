## Summary
Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Khusayfa, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, that he heard Aisha say: the Messenger of God said: no believer is struck by a misfortune, even a thorn, except that he is cleansed by it or his sins are expiated by it. Yazid does not know which of the two Urwa said. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.529}
