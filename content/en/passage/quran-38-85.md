## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 87 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "He said, so the truth and the truth I say": Reciters differed in the reading of His saying: "He said, so the truth and the truth I say," some people of Hijaz and the majority of Kufans read it as the first in the nominative case, and the second in the accusative. In the first case of the first nominative right, if read as such, there are two interpretations: one is nominative with the pronoun 'lillahi al-haq,' or 'I am the truth and I say the truth.' The second: It must be nominative by interpreting the phrase "to fill it," so the meaning then is: "Indeed, I will fill Hell from you," as He says: "A sincere resolve to come to you." So he raised a determination with an interpretation of "I will come to you," because its interpretation is to come to you, as He said: "Then it appeared to them after they saw the signs to imprison him." Therefore, the phrase "appeared to them" must be in the nominative form, which is implicit in meaning. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
