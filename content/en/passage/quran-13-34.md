**Commentary on verse 34 (p. 468)**

On "for them is punishment in the life of this world, and the punishment of the hereafter is harder, and they have no protector against God", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: for these disbelievers whose description He gave in this sura is punishment in the life of this world, by killing, captivity and the afflictions with which God strikes them, "and the punishment of the hereafter is harder", that is, God's punishing them in the abode of the hereafter is more severe than His punishing them in the world; "ashaqq" is the "af'al" form from "mashaqqa". On "and they have no protector against God" he says: these disbelievers have no one to protect them from God's punishment when He punishes them, neither kin, nor ally, nor helper, because He, mighty be His majesty, has no rival who could overpower Him and so rescue them from His punishment by force, and none intercedes with Him except by His leave, and He does not permit anyone to intercede for one who disbelieved in Him and died in his disbelief before repenting of it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 468}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
