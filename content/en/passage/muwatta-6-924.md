## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr and Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik asked Salim ibn Abdallah and Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit, after he had stoned the pillar and shaved his head and before he departed, about perfume; Salim forbade him, and Kharija permitted it for him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1059) and Suwayd (493) transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.443}
