## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 21 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And those who believe say, 'Why is it not that a surah is not revealed? So when a decisive surah is revealed and fighting is mentioned in it, you see those in whose hearts is a disease looking at you with the gaze of one who is fainted from death, so it is more appropriate for them.": Those who believe Allah and His Messenger say: Why not a surah from Allah commanding us to fight the enemies of Allah from the disbelievers? "So when a clear surah is revealed," it means: it is clear in clarity and obligations. He mentioned that this is in Abdullah's recitation: "So when a newly established surah is revealed." And His saying: "And He mentioned fighting in it" means: He mentioned the command to fight the polytheists. Qatadah used to say about this: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And those who believe say, 'Why is a surah not revealed? When a decisive surah is revealed and fighting is mentioned in it,'" he said: Every surah that mentions jihad is decisive, and it is the harshest Qur'an against the hypocrites. From Mu'ammar, from Qatadah: "And he mentioned fighting in it," he said: Every surah that mentions fighting is decisive. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
