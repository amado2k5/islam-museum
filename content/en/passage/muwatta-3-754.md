## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, who said: I asked Said ibn al-Musayyab about charity on horses of the barbarian breed, and Said said: is there any charity on horses at all? The editor notes that Said's name is missing in copy M; Abu Musab, al-Shaybani and al-Shafii in his Musnad transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.374}
