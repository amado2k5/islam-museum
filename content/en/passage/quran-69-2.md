## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-4 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase “What is the Inevitable Hour?” as meaning: What is the Inevitable Hour? It is reported that the Arabs say: When did the Inevitable Hour come? And the word “Inevitable Hour” is pronounced with a kasra (short i vowel) and has the same meaning in all three dialects. They also say: Something is due to him when it becomes obligatory, so he is entitled to rights. The first “Inevitable Hour” is in the nominative case because of the second, as the second is a metaphor for it, as if he were astonished by it and said: What is the Inevitable Hour? Just as one says: What is Zayd? The second “Inevitable Hour” is in the nominative case because of “what,” and “what” is in the nominative case because of the second “Inevitable Hour.” A similar example in the Quran is: “And the Companions of the Right - what are the Companions of the Right?” and “The Striking Calamity - what is the Striking Calamity?” “What” is in the nominative case because of the second “Striking Calamity,” and the first is in the nominative case because of the following clause. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
