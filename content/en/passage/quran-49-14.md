## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The Bedouins said, 'We believe.' Say, 'You have not believed, but say, 'We have submitted.' And when faith enters your hearts, and if you obey Allah and His Messenger, He will not burden you from your deeds at all. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." The Bedouins said: We have spoken the truth by Allah and His Messenger, for we are believers. Allah said to His Prophet Muhammad: Say to them, O Muhammad, 'You have not believed,' and you are not believers, 'But say, 'We have submitted.' He mentioned that this verse was revealed about the Bedouins of Banu Asad. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
