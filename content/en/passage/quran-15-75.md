## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Indeed, in that are signs for those who doubt," meaning that what God did to the people of Lot—their destruction and the punishment He imposed upon them—are signs and indications for those who are deceitful, who recognize God's signs and His lesson of the consequences of the affairs of the people of sin and disbelief in Him; he only means the people of His Prophet from Quraysh. He narrates from the warrior of the deceitful and deceitful — you have been impressed with goodness — and from Ibn Abbas and Al-Dahhak for those who observe, and from Qatadah for those who take heed.
