## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 68 to 69 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He is the One who gives life and causes death, and when He decrees a matter, He only says to Him Be, and it is": Say to them, O Muhammad: "He is the One who gives life and causes death." He says to them: Among His attributes, Glorified and Exalted, is that He gives life to whomever He wills after death, and He causes death to whomever He wills among the living after life. "When He decrees a matter," He says: and when He decrees the existence of a matter He wants to create, "He only says to Him Be," meaning for the one who intends to create it, Be, so what He intends to create exists without suffering or burden. And His saying: "Have you not seen those who dispute the signs of Allah how they turn?" He says to his Prophet Muhammad: Have you not seen these polytheists among your people who dispute with you regarding God's proofs and His signs? "How do they turn away?" He means: what way they divert from the truth and deviate from the right path, as in Qatadah: "How they turn away": how they lie and deviate. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
