## Summary
Verse 88 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 88 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-88|7:88]], al-Tabari takes "the chiefs" to mean the company of men, and "those who were arrogant" to mean those too proud to believe in God, to stop at His command and to follow His messenger Shuayb when he warned them of God's might for their opposing their Lord's command and disbelieving in Him. They said, "we shall expel you, Shuayb", and whoever follows you, believes you and believes in you, "from our town, or you will return to our creed", that is, you and they will go back to our religion and what we follow. Shuayb answered, "even if we are unwilling?" The sense is: will you expel us from your town and bar us from God's way even though we dislike it? The interrogative alif was then placed before the "wa" of "wa law". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 561}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
