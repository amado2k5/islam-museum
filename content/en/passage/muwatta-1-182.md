## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on the call to prayer: he was asked about the caller greeting the imam and calling him to prayer, and who was first greeted this way. He said he had not heard that the greeting was practised in the earliest times. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.119}
