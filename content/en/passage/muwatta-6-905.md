## Summary
A saying of Malik: people of knowledge said there is no harm in the one in ihram washing his head with a cleanser after stoning the pillar of the pass and before shaving his head, because once he has stoned the pillar, killing lice, shaving hair, casting off untidiness and wearing clothes become lawful for him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1037) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.436}
