## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15-17 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Then it will be said, ‘This is what you used to deny’”: God Almighty says: Then it will be said to these deniers of the Day of Judgment: This punishment you are experiencing today is the punishment you used to say in this world you would taste, yet you denied and rejected it. So taste it now, for you have suffered it. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
