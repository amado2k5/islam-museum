## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, every one who denied the messengers deserves punishment": He also narrated that all of these people denied the messengers, so punishment was due upon them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, and Al-Rabi'.
