## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So that the People of the Book may not know that they have no power over any of Allah's bounty, and that bounty is in the hand of Allah; He grants it to whom He wills. And Allah is the possessor of great bounty": Allah Almighty says to those who believe in Him and Muhammad among the People of the Book, 'Your Lord will do this to you so that the People of the Book may know that they are not capable of anything of the bounty of Allah which He has given you and has singled you out, because they believed that Allah had favored them over all creation, so Allah, Glorified and Exalted, made known to them that He had given the nation of Muhammad bounty and honor,' What was not given to them, and that the People of the Book envied the believers when His saying: "O you who have believed, fear Allah and believe in His Messenger. He will grant you two doubles of His mercy and make for you a light by which you walk and forgive you. And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." Allah Almighty said: I did this so that the People of the Book would know that they have no power over any of Allah's bounty. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Sufyan, and Ata.
