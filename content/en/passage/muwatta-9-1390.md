## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Bushayr ibn Yasar, that Abu Burda ibn Niyar slaughtered his sacrifice before the Messenger of God slaughtered his on the day of sacrifice; he claimed that the Messenger of God ordered him to return with another sacrifice. Abu Burda said: Messenger of God, I only find a jadha' (a young animal of the year). He said: even if you find only a jadha', slaughter it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.621}
