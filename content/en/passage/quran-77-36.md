## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 40 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “nor will they be permitted to offer excuses” as referring to the sins they committed in this world. If someone were to ask, “How can it be said, ‘This is a Day when they will not speak,’ when we know from God’s account that they will say, ‘Our Lord, bring us out of it,’ and ‘Our Lord, You caused us to die twice and You gave us life twice,’ and similar statements that God and His Messenger have reported they will make?” the answer is that this applies to some situations but not others. The phrase “This is a Day when they will not speak” informs us that they will not speak during certain times on that Day, not that they will not speak for the entire day.
