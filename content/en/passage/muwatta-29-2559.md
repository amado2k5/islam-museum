## Summary
It reached Malik that Marwan ibn al-Hakam wrote to Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan that a drunk man had been brought who had killed a man. Muawiya wrote back: kill him in retaliation for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.445}
