## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 6 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And they were amazed that a warner came to them from among themselves, and the disbelievers said, 'This is a lying magician.'" These polytheists from Quraysh were amazed that a warner came to them warning them of God's wrath for their disbelief in Him from themselves, and no angel from heaven came to them with it. "And the disbelievers said, 'This is a lying magician.'" He means: The deniers said about the oneness of God: This means Muhammad, a lying magician. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
