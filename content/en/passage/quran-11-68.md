**Commentary on verse 68 (p. 381)**

On [[passage/quran-11-68|11:68]] al-Tabari says: 'as though they had never lived in it' means as though they had never dwelt in it or built it up; reports 18295 and 18296, from Ibn Abbas and Qatada: as though they never lived in it. 'Surely Thamud disbelieved their Lord' means they disbelieved in their Lord's signs and rejected them, 'away with Thamud' means may God put Thamud far, for the coming of the punishment on them. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'living' and 'distance'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 381}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
