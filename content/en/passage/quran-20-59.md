## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 59 to 60 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Your appointment is the Day of adornment, and that the people will be gathered at noon': Moses said to Pharaoh, when he asked him to set a meeting time between him and him, 'your appointment' for gathering, 'the day of adornment,' meaning a day of a festival they had or a market where they adorned themselves, and that people would be gathered." He means: and that people would be led from every corner and direction of 'Duha,' for that is the time between you and me for the meeting. In this passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
