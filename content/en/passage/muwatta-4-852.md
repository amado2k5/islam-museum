## Summary
A saying of Malik: I do not consider it obligatory, and what I prefer is that one who is strong enough does it. Whoever gives a ransom feeds, for each day, a mudd by the mudd of the Prophet. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (810). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.413}
