## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 154 to 156 in a single passage. He explains the verse "You are only a human like us, so bring a sign if you are of the truthful": Allah Almighty says, informing about Thamud saying to their prophet Salih: "You are not," O Salih, "except a human like us" from the children of Adam. You eat what we eat and drink what we drink, and you are neither a lord nor an angel. So why should we follow you? If you are truthful in what you say, and that Allah sent you to us, 'then bring a sign,' meaning: as evidence and proof that you are right in what you say, if you are among those who are truthful in their claim that Allah sent him to us. And it was narrated from Ikrimah, from Ibn Abbas, that Salih the Prophet Allah sent him to his people, and they believed in him and followed him. Salih died, so they turned away from Islam. Salih came to them and said: I am Salih. They said: If you are truthful, then bring us a sign. He brought them the she-camel, but they denied him and dismembered her, and Allah punished them. And His saying: And in the passage, sayings from Ibn Abbas are quoted.
