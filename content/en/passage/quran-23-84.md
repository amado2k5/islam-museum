## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 84 to 85 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, to whom is the earth and all that is in it, if you knew": Say, O Muhammad, to those who deny the Hereafter among your people: To whom is the dominion of the earth and all creation in it, if you know who owns it? Then inform him that they will acknowledge that it belongs to Allah as an dominion, excluding all other things. "Say, do you not remember?" He means: Say to them when they answer you so: Do you not remember? Do you not know that whoever was able to create that in the first place is able to revive them after their death and restore them to a sound creation after their annihilation?
