## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 38 in a single passage. He interprets His saying, "He created man from hastening; I will show you My signs, so do not be hasty." "He created man" means Adam "in haste." Interpreters differed in its interpretation; some said: its meaning is: whoever hastened in his structure and creation was from haste, and in haste. In the maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
