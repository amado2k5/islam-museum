## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "If these were gods, they would not have brought them and all of them are eternal herein": Allah Almighty says to those polytheists whose attribute He described as that no mention from their Lord comes to them except that they listen to it while they play, and they are the polytheists of Quraysh: You are the polytheists, and what you worship besides Allah is sent to Hell. And if what you worship besides Allah were gods, they would not have rejected it. Rather, it would have prevented those who wanted to bring you down to it while you were its worshippers in this world. But since it was of no benefit to themselves or Then she would be protected from harm on her behalf, so it is more likely that this would be for others in her view, and whoever is so will be clear from divinity after him, and God is the one who can do whatever He wills and cannot do anything over Him. As for someone who is able to do so, it is not permissible to be a god. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
