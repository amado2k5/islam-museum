## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 15 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Then We made him a sperm-drop in a firm place": He means His mention by saying: "Then We made him a sperm-drop in a firm position," then We made the man whom We made from clay a sperm-drop in a firm position, which is where the man's sperm from the woman's womb settled. He described him as 'Makin' because He made it possible and prepared for him to settle in it until he reached His decree which He made for him. And His saying: "Then We created the drop into a clot," he means: Then We made the sperm-drop which We made in the stable position of Maqin al-'alqa, which is a piece of blood. "So We created the lump into a lump," meaning: So We made that blood into a lump, which is a piece of flesh. And His saying: "So We created the lump into bones," he says: So We made that lump of flesh into bones. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Al-Rabi', and Ata.
