## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 59 to 60 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, the Hour will not come, there is no doubt about it, but most people do not believe": The Hour in which Allah will bring the dead to life for reward and punishment is a refuge. O people, there is no doubt about its coming. He says: So be certain of its coming, and that you will be resurrected after your death, and you will be rewarded for your deeds, so repent to your Lord. "But most of the people do not believe." He means: but most of the Quraysh do not believe in its coming. And His saying: "And your Lord said, 'Call upon Me; I will respond to you,'" Allah says: And your Lord says, O people, call upon Me: He says: Worship Me and be sincere in worship to Me, excluding those you worship besides Me, idols, and others. 'I respond to you,' meaning: I answer your supplication and forgive you and have mercy on you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, and Al-A'mash.
