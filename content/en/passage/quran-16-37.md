## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "If you are keen on their guidance, then indeed Allah does not guide those who misguide, and they will have no helpers": If you are keen, O Muhammad, to guide these polytheists to believe in Allah and follow the truth, "then Allah does not guide those who go astray." Reciters differed in their interpretation; the majority of Kufan readers read it as: "Indeed, Allah does not guide those who go astray" with a fatha on the ya from "he guides" and a damma on "he misguides." Readers also differed about the meaning of this. Some grammarians from Kufa claimed that its meaning: 'Indeed, Allah, whoever is misguided, does not be guided,' and said: The Arabs say: 'A man has been guided,' meaning 'he has been guided,' and 'guided' means 'guided' means 'guided' means 'guided' and 'he is guided.' Others claimed that its meaning: 'For Allah does not guide those whom He misguides,' meaning: 'Indeed, whoever Allah leads astray, Allah does not guide him.'
