## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “From what thing did He create him?”: From what did his Lord create the disbelieving human being so that he might become arrogant and disdainful of obeying his Lord and acknowledging His Oneness? Then He, may His praise be exalted, clarifies from what He created him, saying: And in the section, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
