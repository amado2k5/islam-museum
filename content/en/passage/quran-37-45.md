## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 45 to 50 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "fruits while they are honored" as a response to the known provision, which is why it was raised. And His saying: "and they are honored" means: they are among those who have the known provision in Paradise, honored by the honor of God with which He honored them. He also quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Dhahhak.
