## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abdullah ibn Abbas, when he had a nosebleed, went out, washed off the blood and came back to build on what he had prayed, in the chapter on nosebleeds. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.80}
