## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "She said, 'When kings enter a town, they corrupt it and make the honorable of its people humiliated, and thus they do'": The lady of Sheba said to the people of her people, when they offered themselves to fight Solomon, if she commanded them to do so: "When kings entered a town" by force and dominance, "they ruined it," meaning they destroyed it "and made the honorable of its people humiliated," by enslaveing the free and enslaved them. The news of this is not reported to the kings at this point, so God said: "And so they do," says He, the Exalted: "And as the Lady of Sheba said, kings do when they enter a city by force." In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
