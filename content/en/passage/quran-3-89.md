## Summary
Verse 89 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 89 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 89: the exception of those who repent.** Al-Tabari says that God excepted those who repented from among these who turned back after their faith, that is, those who repented after their turning back, returned to faith in God and His messenger, confirmed what their prophet brought them from their Lord, and did good deeds. He says that to whoever does this after his disbelief God is one who covers over, that is, covering the sin he committed of turning back, leaving its punishment and its public exposure on the Day of Resurrection, and not holding him to account for it if he dies in repentance from it, and one who shows mercy, turning to him with mercy. (This is what al-Tabari transmits, and the museum presents it as his, without a verdict.) {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 578}
