## Summary
Verse 107 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 107 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

God says to His prophet: turn away from these polytheists, and leave aside disputing, quarrelling and abusing them; "and had God willed, they would not have associated": had your Lord wanted to guide them and save them from their error, He would have dealt kindly with them by granting them success, so they would have associated nothing with Him and would have believed in you, followed you and credited the truth you brought them from your Lord; "and We have not made you a guardian over them": I sent you to them only as a messenger who conveys, not as a keeper over what they do, counting it against them, for that is for Us and not for you; "nor are you a trustee over them": you are not their steward to see to their provision and sustenance, or to guard them, for what was not given into your charge of their affair. Report 13737, from Ibn Abbas: He says, glory to Him, had I willed I would have gathered them all on guidance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 32}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
