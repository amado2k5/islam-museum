## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "those who believe and do good deeds", al-Tabari says they are those who accepted what Muhammad brought and obeyed God, keeping his limits, fulfilling his obligations and avoiding what he forbade; and that this verse and the one before it report the permanence of the Fire and its people and of Paradise and its people, contradicting the Jews' claim that the Fire would touch them only for a few days. He reports this from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 287} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 288}
