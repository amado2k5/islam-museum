## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The believers are only those who have believed in Allah and His Messenger and then did not doubt and strive with their wealth and their lives in the cause of Allah; those are the truthful." Allah Almighty says to the Bedouins who said, 'We believe,' and when faith enters their hearts: 'The believers are but those who have believed Allah and His Messenger and then did not doubt.' He says: Then they did not doubt the oneness of Allah or the prophethood of His Prophet, and committed themselves to obeying Allah and His Messenger, and to act upon what was obligatory upon them of Allah's obligations without doubt about the obligation of that upon Him. 'And strive accordingly.' With their wealth and their lives in the way of Allah," he says: They fought the polytheists by spending their wealth and exerting their efforts in their jihad, according to what Allah commanded them to strive, and that is His way so that the word of Allah would be the highest and the lowest word of those who disbelieved. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
