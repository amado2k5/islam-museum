## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We have certainly created above you seven paths, and We were never oblivious to creation": And We have certainly created above you, O people, seven heavens, one above another, and the Arabs call everything above something a path. The seven heavens are called seven paths because they are one above the other, so each of them is a path. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
