## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He said, 'Knowledge is only with Allah, and He has conveyed to you what I have sent with, but I see you as a people who are ignorant.'" Hud said to his people 'Ad: 'Knowledge is only with the time of the coming of what He promised you of the punishment Allah has promised you for your disbelief with Allah. I know nothing of that except what He has taught me, and I convey to you what I have been sent with.' He means: I am only a messenger to you from Allah, conveying to you what He sent me with of the message. 'But I see you as a people who are ignorant.' The places of your own fortunes, so you do not know what harm it causes to your worship except and in hastening His punishment.
