**Commentary on verse 109 (pp. 491-492)**

On [[passage/quran-11-109|11:109]] God says to His Prophet Muhammad: do not be in doubt, Muhammad, about what these polytheists of your people worship of gods and idols, that it is error and falsehood and associating others with God; 'they worship only as their fathers worshipped before', that is, only like their fathers' worship of them before them. He tells that they worshipped what they worshipped of idols only by following their fathers' way and tracing their steps in worshipping them, not by God's command nor by any proof they saw that made it binding on them. Then He told His Prophet what He will do to them for that worship, saying 'We shall pay them their share in full', their portion of what I promised to pay them of good or evil. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 491}

'Undiminished' means I shall not reduce what I promised them but complete it for them in full. Reports 18595, 18596 and 18598, from Ibn Abbas: what they were promised of good or evil; 18597, from Ibn Abbas: what was decreed for them of good and evil; 18599, from Ibn Zayd: their share of the punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 492}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
