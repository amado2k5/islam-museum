## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "those are they who bought the life of this world at the price of the Hereafter", al-Tabari says they are those who believed in part of the Book and disbelieved in part, having bought leadership over the weak of their community in this world with the belief that would have given them permanent stay in Paradise, content with this world in place of the bliss of the next; he reports from Qatada that they preferred little of this world to much of the next. "Their punishment shall not be lightened" because only one who has a share in the bliss is relieved, and "nor will they be helped" means no one will turn God's punishment from them, by strength or by intercession. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 316} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 317}
