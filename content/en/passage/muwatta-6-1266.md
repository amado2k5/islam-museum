## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Isa ibn Talha, from Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As, that he said: the Messenger of God stood for people at Mina while they asked him; a man came and said: I shaved before I sacrificed. He said: sacrifice, and there is no blame. Another came and said: I sacrificed before I stoned. He said: stone, and there is no blame. He was not asked about anything that was done early or late without his saying: do it, and there is no blame. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.562}
