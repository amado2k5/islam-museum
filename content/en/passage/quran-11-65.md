**Commentary on verse 65 (pp. 372-373)**

On [[passage/quran-11-65|11:65]] al-Tabari says: Thamud hamstrung God's she-camel; a phrase is left out in the speech because the plain words show it, namely 'they denied him' and 'they hamstrung her'; then Salih said to them 'enjoy yourselves in your home three days', that is, enjoy your life in the world for three days; 'that is a promise not belied' means this term I have given you is a promise from God that at its end destruction and punishment will come to you, not belied, that is, the one who told you did not lie to you in it. Report 18286, from Qatada: it was told us that when Salih told them that the punishment had come upon them they put on hides and cloaks, and it was said to them that the sign of it is that your colours turn yellow on the first day, red on the second and black on the third. It was told us that when they had hamstrung the she-camel they regretted it and said, where is the foal? and the foal went up the mountain, the 'qara', and when the third day came it faced the qibla and said, my Lord, my mother, three times, and the cry was sent at that. Ibn Abbas used to say, had you climbed the qara you would have seen the foal's bones; Thamud's dwellings were at al-Hijr between Syria and Medina. Reports 18287 and 18288: 'three days' means the rest of their terms. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 373}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
