## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who disbelieved in the Remembrance [[of the Quran] because of what came to them, and indeed, it is a Mighty Book": Indeed, those who denied this Qur'an and denied it because of what came to them, and he meant the Qur'an, as Qatadah narrated His saying: "Indeed, those who disbelieved in the Remembrance when it came to them" disbelieved in the Qur'an. And His saying: "Indeed, it is a mighty Book," the Exalted says: And this mention is a Book of Mighty by the might of Allah and His protection from anyone who seeks to alter, distort, or alter it, whether humans, jinn, or devils. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
