## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Whoever brings a good deed will have something better than it, and whoever brings an evil deed will not be recompensed except for what they used to do." Whoever comes to Allah on the Day of Resurrection with sincere monotheism, he will have good, and that good is Paradise and everlasting bliss. Whoever brings evil, which is associating partners with Allah, is explained. Also: from Qatadah, His saying "Whoever brings a good deed will have a good share of it," meaning he will have a good share of it, and a good deed means sincerity, and bad is polytheism. We have explained this despite the differing opinions and have indicated the correct opinion regarding it. And His saying: "Those who do evil deeds will not be rewarded" means: those who do evil deeds will not be rewarded for their evil deeds "except for what they used to do," meaning: except for the recompense of what they used to do.
