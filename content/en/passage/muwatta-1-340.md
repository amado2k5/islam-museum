## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad that he did the same as Ibn Umar, making up the two dawn rak'as after sunrise, in the chapter on the two dawn rak'as. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.187} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188}
