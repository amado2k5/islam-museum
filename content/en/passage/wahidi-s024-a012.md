## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nur 24:12, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «ولولا إذ سمعتموه قلتم ما يكون لنا أن نتكلم بهذا».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
