## Summary
A report of Ibn Umar in the chapter on the merit of congregational prayer and the warning to those who miss it, in the Book of Mosques of Sahih Muslim. It is the best-known text on preferring congregational prayer, and Abu Hurayra's report in the same book says it is twenty-five times more. {cite:sahih-muslim}
