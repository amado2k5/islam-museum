## Summary
A report that Yahya transmits from Malik from Yahya ibn Sa'id, that Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan wrote to Zayd ibn Thabit asking about the grandfather. Zayd wrote back: you wrote asking me about the grandfather, and God knows best. This is a matter on which only the commanders, meaning the caliphs, judged, and I attended the two caliphs before you, who gave him half with one brother and a third with two. If the brothers increased, they did not reduce his third. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.11}
