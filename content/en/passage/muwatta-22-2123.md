## Summary
Yahya said Malik said about a man who dies leaving a debt that he is owed by one witness, and he owes debts to people who have one witness, while his heirs refuse to swear for their rights with their witness. Malik said: the creditors swear and take their rights. If something remains over, the heirs have no share of it, because the oaths were offered to them before and they refused them; unless they say they know of no surplus for their companion, and it is known they left the oaths for that reason. I think they should swear and take what remains after his debt. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.267}
