**Commentary on verse 153: the People of the Book ask for a book from heaven (p. 356)**

Al-Tabari says the People of the Book, meaning the Jews of the Torah, asked Muhammad to send down to them a written book from heaven, as Moses had brought the Torah written. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 356}

Report 10768, from al-Suddi: the Jews said, if you are truthful that you are God's messenger, bring us a written book from heaven as Moses brought. Report 10769, from Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi: some Jews came to the Prophet and said, Moses brought the tablets from God, so bring us tablets from God, and we will believe you! God then revealed the verse on their request for a book sent down from heaven, down to what is mentioned of their slander against Mary. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 356}

We stop here after report 10769 at p. 356, in the middle of the commentary on verse 153, and will continue from p. 357. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 356}
