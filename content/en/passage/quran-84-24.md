## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22-25 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “So give them tidings of a painful punishment”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: So give, O Muhammad, the tidings of a painful punishment from God for those who deny His signs, a punishment that will befall them from God, “except for those who believe and do righteous deeds.” He says: Except for those among them who repent and believe, and acknowledge His Oneness, the prophethood of His Prophet Muhammad, and the resurrection after death. “And do righteous deeds” He says: And they fulfill God’s obligations and refrain from what God has forbidden them to do. And His saying, “For them is a reward uninterrupted,” He, may He be exalted, says: For those who believe and do righteous deeds, there is a reward that is neither measured nor diminished. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
