## Summary
A report of Aisha in the chapter on supplication in prayer, in the Book of Remembrance and Supplication of Sahih Muslim: a comprehensive seeking of refuge from the trials of the Fire, the grave, wealth and poverty, and the Dajjal, and asking that sins be washed away with the water of snow and hail. {cite:sahih-muslim}
