## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever does a sin and is not recompensed except for its like." Whoever does righteous deeds, whether male or female, while he is a believer - those will enter Paradise and be provided for there without reckoning.' Whoever acts in disobedience to Allah in this worldly life, Allah will only recompense him in the Hereafter for an evil like it, and that is to punish him accordingly. "And whoever does righteous deeds, whether male or female," he means: Whoever acts in obedience to Allah in this world, obeys His commands, and refrains from what He forbids him, whether man or woman, and is a believer in Allah, "those will enter Paradise" means: those who do this among the servants of Allah will enter Paradise in the Hereafter. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
