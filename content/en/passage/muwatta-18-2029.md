## Summary
Malik says of a man who transgressed by borrowing from the profit-sharing money in his hands, and bought goods with it for himself. Malik said: if he profits, the profit is according to their stipulation in the profit-sharing; if there is a loss, he is liable for the loss. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.229}
