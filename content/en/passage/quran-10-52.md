**Commentary on verse 52 (p. 102)**

On [[passage/quran-10-52|10:52]] he says: then it is said to those who wronged themselves by disbelieving in God, taste the punishment of eternity, drink down God's punishment that stays with you forever, without end or ceasing; "are you repaid except for what you earned?" means they are told to consider whether they are recompensed except for what they did in their lives before death of disobedience to God. The editor refers to the glosses on "tasting", "eternity", "recompense" and "earning". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 102}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
