## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Allah created you and then causes you to die, and among you is he who is returned to the most disgraceful life, so that he knows nothing after knowledge. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and All-Powerful." This means: And Allah created you, O people, and created you, and you were nothing, not the gods you worship besides Him, so worship Him who created you and not anyone else. "Then He will take you away," he means: Then He will take you. "And among you is he who returns to the worst of life," meaning: And among you is he who becomes old and becomes the worst of life, which is the worst of it. It is said: a man is despicable and disgraceful, he despises a vile and a degraded one. It is also said: He becomes like that in seventy-five years. From Sa'd ibn Tarif, from Al-Asbagh ibn Nabatah, from Ali, regarding the verse: "And among you is he who returns to the lowest age," he said: seventy-five years. And His saying: "So that he does not know anything after he knows," means: We only return him to the worst age so that he returns ignorant as he was in his childhood and youth.
