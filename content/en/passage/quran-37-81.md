## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 78–82. Al-Tabari explains that God kept Noah a beautiful reminder and good praise among others. Peace be upon Noah in the worlds. Indeed, We reward the doers of good. Indeed, He is one of Our believing servants, then We drowned the others.
