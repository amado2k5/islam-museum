## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “And when it is said to them, ‘Come, the Messenger of Allah will ask forgiveness for you,’ they turn their heads away, and you see them turning away while they are arrogant,” explains that when these hypocrites are told, “Come to the Messenger of Allah, he will ask forgiveness for you,” they turn their heads away. This means they move and shake their heads in mockery of the Messenger of Allah and his seeking forgiveness. The reading with the emphatic “waw” in “lawwa” indicates that they repeatedly shook and moved their heads, except for Nafi’, who read it with a light “waw” as “lawaw,” implying that they did it only once. The correct reading is that of those who pronounce it with the emphatic “waw,” as this is the consensus of the authoritative reciters.
