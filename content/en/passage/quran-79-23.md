## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Then he gathered and called out”: He gathered his people and followers and called out to them, “I am your Lord, the Most High,” before whom every other lord is less than me. The fool lied. Those who interpret the Quran have said the same thing. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
