## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-4 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And what will make you know what the Inevitable Reality is?” as meaning, “What will make you know and inform you what the Inevitable Reality is?” He quotes Sufyan as saying, “The verse in the Quran does not say ‘And what will make you know,’ but it does say ‘And what will make you know,’ because it does inform.” He also quotes Qatadah as saying, “And what will make you know what the Inevitable Reality is?” to emphasize the Day of Resurrection, as you have heard. He then quotes Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan in this section.
