## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He has only forbidden you carrion, blood, and the flesh of swine, and that which is suitable for other than Allah. So whoever is forced by necessity, neither willingly nor willing, then indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful": Allah Almighty says, denying the polytheists who used to forbid what We have mentioned of seas and other things: Allah has forbidden you O people except carrion, blood, and the flesh of swine, and what is slaughtered for the sacrificial animals, so names are made upon them other than Allah, for that is among the sacrifices of those whose slaughter is not lawful. Whoever is compelled to do that or any of it due to famine, it becomes lawful. He ate it, 'neither desiring nor transgressing, for Allah is Forgiving and Merciful,' meaning: a person with a concealment should be held accountable for eating it in cases of necessity; it is merciful to him that he should punish him for it. We have explained the differences among those who differ regarding his statement: 'He did not intend nor did he hostile,' and the correct view for us is to say this based on his previous evidences, which would suffice to repeat it. And from Qatadah, his statement: 'Death and blood are only forbidden to you'... In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
