## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains: "And We created man from clay from sturdy clay": Adam — man — created from clay. Regarding the meaning of clay, interpreters differ: from Qatadah, the dry clay that is called clay when it is pecked; from Ibn Abbas, the thin clay from which pottery is made, and that it was created from three parts: sticky clay, which is good stickiness; and muslin clay, which is clay containing sludge; and in another narration from him: water falls on the good ground and then cracks and becomes like thin clay, and he was called a human because he was entrusted with it and then forgot; and from Mujahid: dry soil, and from Al-Dahhak, hard clay mixed with dunes. Others said the foul clay is from their saying, 'Pray the flesh when it rots.' This is also narrated from Mujahid.
