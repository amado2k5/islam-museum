## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the time of the pre-dawn meal, from the call. Yahya transmits from Malik from Abdullah ibn Dinar from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Prophet said Bilal calls at night before dawn, and so he allowed the fasting person to eat and drink until Ibn Umm Maktum calls. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad, al-Bayhaqi and al-Tahawi, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
