**Commentary on verse 14 (p. 573)**

On [[passage/quran-12-14|12:14]] al-Tabari says: Joseph's brothers said to their father Jacob, if the wolf were to eat Joseph in the desert while we are eleven men with him guarding him, who are the group, 'then we would be losers', that is, helpless and ruined. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 573}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
