## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on dress, stepping over necks and facing the imam on Friday: the practice of the people of Medina is that on Friday people face the imam when he wants to preach, whether they are nearest the qibla or not. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.167} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.168}
