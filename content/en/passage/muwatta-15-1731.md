## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, that it reached him that Sayib ibn Khabbab died, and his wife came to Abdullah ibn Umar, told him of her husband's death, and mentioned that they had a field at Qana. She asked whether it was lawful for her to spend the night there. He forbade it. She used to leave Medina before dawn and spend the day in their field, then return to Medina in the evening and sleep in her house. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.107}
