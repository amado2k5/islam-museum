## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And they said, 'O magician, call upon us your Lord by what He has promised you; indeed, we are rightly guided.'" Pharaoh and his full voice said to Moses: "O magician, call upon us your Lord by what He has promised you," and they referred to their saying "by what He has promised you." They meant by the covenant which was entrusted to you: If we believe in you and follow you, He will remove the falsehood from us. Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the words of Allah, the Almighty, 'with what He promised you,' He said: 'If we believe, He will remove the punishment from us.' If someone says to us: What is the reason for their saying, O magician? Call your Lord for us with what He has promised you. And how did they call him a magician when they ask Him to pray for them to relieve them of the punishment? It is said: For them, 'magician' meant 'scholar,' and magic was not a condemnation for them; rather, they called him by this name, because its meaning among them was: 'O scholar.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
