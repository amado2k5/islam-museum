## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So when you and those with you are established on the ark, say, 'Praise be to Allah who saved you from the wrongdoing people': meaning His mention is with His saying, 'So when you and those with you are established on the ark,' meaning when you and those with you are on board the ship, you and those who carried it with you from among your family, riding high above it, 'then say, 'Praise be to Allah who saved us from the wrongdoing people,' meaning from the polytheists.
