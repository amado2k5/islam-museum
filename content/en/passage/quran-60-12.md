## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O Prophet, when believing women come to you, they pledge allegiance to you not to associate anything with Allah, nor to steal, nor to commit adultery, nor to kill their children, nor to commit a false slander they fabricate before their hands and feet, nor to disobey you in a kind manner. So pledge allegiance to them and ask forgiveness for them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." "O Prophet, when believing women come to you" by Allah, they pledge allegiance to you on the condition that they will not associate anything with Allah, nor steal, nor They commit adultery and do not kill their children, nor do they come with a false fabrication they fabricate before their hands and feet." He says: They do not come with lies they lie about a newborn that is in their hands and feet. The meaning of the statement is: they should not be joined to their wives except their children. In the passage, statements from Ibn Abbas, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Sufyan are also quoted.
