## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Should I invent a lie against Allah, or is there a Paradise for him? Rather, those who do not believe in the Hereafter are in punishment and far error": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of those who disbelieved in Him and denied resurrection after death, admiring the Messenger of Allah in his promise to them: Do you see this who promises us after we tear every torn in a new creation before Allah as a lie, thus creating falsehood about him and disclosing the false statement, 'Or is he a paradise?' He says: Or is he insane and speaks meaninglessly? In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
