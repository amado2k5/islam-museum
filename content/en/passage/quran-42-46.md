## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46 to 47 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and they had no protectors to support them besides Allah. And whoever Allah leads astray, then he has no way": These disbelievers, when Allah punishes them on the Day of Resurrection, will have no protectors to prevent them from Allah's punishment, nor will they be given victory against their Lord against what they suffered besides Allah. "And whoever Allah misguides, then he has no way." He says: And whoever forsakes him from the path of truth, he has no way to reach it, for guidance and misguidance are in His hands, and not for anyone else. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
