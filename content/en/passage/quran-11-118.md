**Commentary on verse 118 (pp. 531-534)**

On [[passage/quran-11-118|11:118]] al-Tabari says: had your Lord willed, Muhammad, He would have made all people one community, one creed and one religion; report 18699, from Qatada: He would have made them all Muslims. As for 'they will not cease to differ', it means people do not cease differing 'except those your Lord has mercy on'. The interpreters differ over the difference described: some said it is difference in religions, so the sense is that people will not cease to differ over various religions, Jew, Christian, Magian and the like, God excepting those He has mercy on, who are the people of faith; report 18700, from 'Ata': Jews, Christians and Magians, and the hanifs are those your Lord has mercy on. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 531}

Report 18701, from 'Ata', likewise; 18702, from al-Hasan: people differ on various religions except those your Lord has mercy on, and those He has mercy on do not differ; 18703, 18704 and 18705, from Mujahid: 'they will not cease to differ' are the people of falsehood, 'except those your Lord has mercy on' the people of truth; 18706, from al-Hasan, asked about 'and for that He created them': He created these for His Paradise and those for His Fire, these for His mercy and those for His punishment; 18707, from Mujahid: the people of falsehood and the people of truth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 532}

Reports 18708 and 18709, from Mujahid: the people of truth and of falsehood, 'except those your Lord has mercy on', the people of truth; 18710, from Ibn al-Mubarak: the people of truth, among whom there is no difference; 18711, from Ikrima: Jews and Christians, 'except those your Lord has mercy on', the people of the qibla; 18712, from Ibn Abbas: the people of falsehood, 'except those your Lord has mercy on', the people of truth; 18713, from Ikrima: they will not cease to differ in desire; 18714, from Qatada: the people of God's mercy are people of unity though their homes and bodies scatter, and the people of disobedience are people of division though their homes and bodies gather; 18715, from al-A'mash: whom He put on Islam. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 533}

Report 18716, from al-Hasan: the people of falsehood, 'except those your Lord has mercy on', the editor discussing a name in the chain; 18717 and 18718, from Mujahid: the people of falsehood and of truth. Others said the sense is they will not cease differing in provision, this one poor, that one rich; report 18719, from al-Hasan: differing in provision, some made to serve others. Some said differing in forgiveness and mercy. Al-Tabari says: the best of these views is that people do not cease differing in their religions and desires, on various religions, creeds and desires, except those your Lord has mercy on, who believed in God and held His messengers true, for they do not differ in God's oneness, belief in His messengers and what came to them from God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 534}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
