## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So taste the meeting of your Day because you forgot. Indeed, We have forgotten you, and taste the punishment of eternity because of what you used to do": It is said to those polytheists of Allah when they enter the Fire: "Taste the punishment of Allah for forgetting the meeting of this Day of yours in this world." "Indeed, We have forgotten you," meaning: We have left you today in the Fire. And His saying: "And taste the punishment of eternity" means: it is also said to them: taste a punishment in which you will abide forever "because of what you have been" in this world "you do" from the sins of God. Similarly, the interpreters said about this. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
