## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, and it reached him from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, that the two used to say the same as Said ibn al-Musayyab about women: a woman shares with a man's kin up to a third of a man's blood-money; when her share reaches a third of a man's blood-money, her blood-money is half of a man's. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.422}
