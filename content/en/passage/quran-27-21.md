## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 22 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And he inspected the birds and said, 'My owner, do I not see the hoopoe, or was it among the unseen?": "And he searched" Solomon, "the bird, and he said, 'My owner, I do not see the hoopoe.'" The reason for his inspection of the birds and his question about the hoopoe, especially among the birds, was: He said: I heard Imran from Abu Majliz, who said: Ibn Abbas sat with Abdullah ibn Salam and asked him about the hoopoe: Solomon did not lose him among the birds. Abdullah ibn Salam said: Solomon descended at a station on his journey, but he did not know what came after the water. He said: Who knows what is beyond the water? They said: The hoopoe, that is when you check it. And from Abu Majaliz, from Ibn Abbas and Abdullah ibn Salam in a similar manner. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
