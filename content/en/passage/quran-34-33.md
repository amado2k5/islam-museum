## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who were weak said to those who were arrogant, but the plotting of the night and the day when you command us to disbelieve in Allah and to make for Him equals and confiscat regret when they saw the punishment and put shackles around the necks of those who disbelieved. Will they be recompensed except for what they used to do?" "And those who were weak said" are among the disbelievers in the world, so they followed their leaders in error "for those who were arrogant" therein, so they had leaders "but indeed plotted" how much. By 'night and day,' we turned away from guidance: 'When you commanded us to disbelieve in Allah and to set for Him' examples and similar examples in worship and divinity, so cunning was added to night and day. The meaning is what we mentioned of the cunning of the arrogant against the oppressed day and night, despite the Arabs' breadth in what its meaning is known from its logic, transferring the attribute of something to another, and saying to a man: O so-and-so, your day is fasting and your night is standing, as the poet said: 'And I slept, and the night of the mount was resting and similar things have been explained elsewhere in this book.' In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Zayd.
