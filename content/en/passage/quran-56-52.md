## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50 to 52 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "so they fill the bellies from it": they fill their bellies from the trees of Zaqqum: and the Arabs differ regarding the feminine form of the tree in the phrase: "so they fill their bellies" meaning from the tree, "and they drink on it" because the tree is feminine and masculine, and feminine because it is carried on the tree, since the tree may indicate everyone. The Arabs say: A bitter tree grew before us with a bad scarf, and they mean everyone.
