## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if the truth had followed their desires, the heavens and the earth and all in them would have been corrupted. Rather, We have brought them their remembrance, and they turn away from their remembrance." If the Lord had acted upon His remembrance as these polytheists desire, and carried out the plan according to their will and desire, and abandoned the truth which they hate, the heavens and the earth and all that is in them would have been corrupted, because they do not know the consequences of matters or what is right and what is corrupt. If matters were to proceed according to their will and desires, with most of them preferring falsehood over truth, the heavens and the earth and those within them would not be accepted by God's creation, because that was established by the truth. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
