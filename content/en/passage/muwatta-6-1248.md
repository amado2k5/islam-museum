## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that a man came to Umar ibn al-Khattab and said: Commander of the Faithful, I struck some locusts with my whip while I was in ihram; Umar said: feed a handful of food. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.555}
