**Commentary on verse 44 (pp. 96-97)**

On [[passage/quran-10-44|10:44]] he says: God does not do to His creatures what they do not deserve; He punishes them only for disobeying Him and tortures them only for disbelieving in Him, but it is people who wrong themselves, by committing what brings upon them God's anger and displeasure. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 96}

This is an announcement from God to His Prophet and the believers that He did not take faith from those He reported would not believe as a first act, without an earlier offence on their part; He tells that He took it from them because they had earned it by sins they committed, so their Lord's word was due upon them and their hearts were sealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 97}
