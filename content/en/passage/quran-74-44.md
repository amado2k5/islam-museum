## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 51 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Every soul is held in pledge for what it has earned" as meaning that every soul is commanded and prohibited by what it has done of disobedience to God in this world, and is held in pledge in Hell, "except for the companions of the right," for they are not held in pledge, but rather "in gardens, questioning one another" about the criminals. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
