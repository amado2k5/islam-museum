## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And when he learns of any of Our signs that He takes them as a mockery, they will have a humiliating punishment": "And when this sinful slanderer knows" of the signs of Allah, "He takes them as a mockery": he says: take those verses he taught as mockery, mocking them, as Abu Jahl did when it was revealed: "Indeed, the tree of Zaqqum is food for the sinner," when he called upon dates and foam and said: You are annoyed by this. Muhammad does not count you except as witnesses, and similar actions of theirs. And His saying: "Those are the ones for whom is a humiliating punishment," the Exalted says: These are the ones who do this act, and they hear the verses of Allah recited to them and then persist in their disbelief out of arrogance, taking the signs of Allah which they were taught as mockery. On the Day of Resurrection, they will have a humiliating punishment from Allah that will humiliate them and humiliate them in the fire of Hell, because they were in this world, arrogant in obeying Allah and following His signs. Rather, Allah said: "those people," and he gathered.
