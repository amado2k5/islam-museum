## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, that Aban ibn Uthman and Hisham ibn Ismail used to mention in their sermons the warranty of slaves, for three days from the time a slave or a slave-girl is bought, and the warranty of a year. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.134}
