## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We saved those who believed and were righteous": And saved us from Our punishment which We bestowed upon Thamud, our messenger, Salih, and the believers in it, "and they were righteous"—meaning: And they were righteous by their faith and by believing in righteous who befell their people from Thamud as punishment from Allah. Thus do We save you, O Muhammad and your followers, when We impose Our punishment on the polytheists of your people from among their backs. He mentioned that Salih, when Allah permitted his people what was lawful, he and the believers went out with him to the Levant, and he settled in the sands of Palestine.
