## Summary
Yahya ibn Said narrated to Malik: it reached me that Asad ibn Zurara was cauterised in the time of the Messenger of God for quinsy, and died. The editor notes that the correct name is Asad, not Sad as in some copies, and that quinsy is a disease that affects the throat. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.533}
