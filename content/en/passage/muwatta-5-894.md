## Summary
Ziyad reports from Malik from Humayd al-Tawil, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: the Messenger of God came out to us in Ramadan and said: I was shown this night, until two men quarrelled and it was lifted; so seek it on the ninth, the seventh and the fifth. The editor notes that Anas narrated this hadith raised from Ubada ibn al-Samit, and that it is in Ibn Abd al-Barr's al-Tamhid (2/200). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.429}
