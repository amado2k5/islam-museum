## Summary
Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Sasaa, that he heard Abu al-Hubab Said ibn Yasar say: I heard Abu Hurayra say: the Messenger of God said: whomever God wants good for, He afflicts. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.530}
