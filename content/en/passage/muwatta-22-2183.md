## Summary
He said: and I heard Malik say of a dyer who is handed a cloth, mis-delivers it to another man, who wears it before the one who handed it to him: the wearer owes nothing, and the launderer owes the cloth's owner, if the wearer wore the cloth without knowing it was not his. If he wore it knowing it was not his, he is liable for it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.296}
