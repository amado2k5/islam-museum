## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46-49 as a single section. He explains the phrase, “Woe on that Day to the deniers,” meaning: Woe on that Day to the deniers who rejected God’s message informing them of what He will do to them in this verse. And the phrase, “And when it is said to them, ‘Bow down,’ they will not bow down,” means: When it is said to these criminals who deny God’s warnings, those who disbelieve in Him, “Bow down,” they will not bow down. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the time when this will be said to them. Some said: It will be said in the Hereafter when they are called to prostrate but are unable to. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
