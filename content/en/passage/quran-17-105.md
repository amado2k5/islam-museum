## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 105 to 106 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And with truth We sent it down, and with the truth it was revealed. And We have not sent you except as a bringer of good tidings and a warner": and with truth We sent this Qur'an: He says: We revealed it enjoining justice, fairness, beautiful morals, and good and worthy matters, and forbidding injustice, ugly matters, bad morals, and blameworthy deeds. "And with truth it was revealed," meaning: and thus it was revealed from God to His Prophet Muhammad. And His saying: "And We have not sent you except as a bringer of good tidings and a warner," Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: "And We did not send you, O Muhammad, to those to whom We sent you from among Our servants except as bringers of good tidings of Paradise for those who obeyed Us, and ended up with Our command and prohibition, and as a warner to those who disobeyed and opposed Our command and prohibition." "And We have divided it so that you may recite it" — reciters differed in this reading; the majority of the reciters of the cities read it as "We divided it" with a lightened 'ra' from 'we separated it,' meaning: We made it firm, separated, and clarified. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-Rabi'.
