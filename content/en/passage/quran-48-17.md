## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "There is no blame upon the blind, nor upon the lame, nor upon the sick. And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger will be admitted to Paradise beneath which rivers flow. And whoever turns away, He will punish him with a painful punishment": There is no distress upon the blind among you, nor upon the lame, nor upon the lame, nor upon the sick, that they should refrain from jihad with the believers, and those witnessing war with them when they meet their enemy, due to the ailments they have and the reasons that prevent them from witnessing them. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
