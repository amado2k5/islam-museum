## Summary
Verse 61 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 61 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Al-Tabari's explanation of the verse (p. 513, notes 2-3).** Al-Tabari says the address is to Muhammad, to take note of the hypocrites who claim to accept what was sent to him and of the People of the Book who claim to accept what came before him, who mean to seek judgement before the taghut; when they are told to hasten to the ruling God sent down in His book and to the Messenger so that he may judge between them, you see them refuse to come to you for judgement and hold others back in the same way. The editor refers to earlier explanations of the call "come" and of turning away (the page numbers are scrambled in the scan). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 513}

**Ibn Jurayj's report and the close of the matter of the caller (p. 514, report 9903).** Al-Qasim (9903, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj) reports from Ibn Jurayj that the Muslim called the hypocrite to God's messenger to judge between them and the hypocrite turned away. Al-Tabari says the interpretation of those who make the Jew the one calling to the Prophet, and the hypocrite the one called, is as he showed before. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 514}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 514}
