## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if you ask them who sends down water from the sky and revives the earth with it after its death, they will say, 'Allah, say, Praise be to Allah.' Rather, most of them do not understand." And if you ask, O Muhammad, those who disassociated others with Allah from your people sent down water from the sky, which is the rain that Allah sends down from the clouds, "and He revived the earth by it," meaning: He gave life to the earth by the water that sends down from the sky, and reviving it: Its plant germinates in it "after its death," after its drought and drought. And His saying: "to say Allah" means: to say, 'Who did that, Allah, to whom all things are worshiped.'
