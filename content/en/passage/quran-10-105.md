**Commentary on verse 105 (p. 218)**

On [[passage/quran-10-105|10:105]] al-Tabari says: 'I was commanded to be among the believers' and 'and that you set', the second 'that' being joined to the first. By 'set your face to the religion' he means set yourself on the religion of Islam, 'as a hanif', upright on it and not turned aside from it to Judaism, Christianity or the worship of an idol; 'and do not be among the polytheists' means do not be among those who join gods and rivals in the worship of their Lord and so be among the lost. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'face' and 'hanif'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 218}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
