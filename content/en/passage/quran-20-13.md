## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And I chose You, so listen to what is revealed": Reciters differed in its reading. The majority of the reciters read it as "Waana" with a stressed 'nun' and 'Ana' with a fatha on the alif of 'Ana' in response to 'Nu'ud, O Moses,' as if the meaning of the speech is: 'Nu'nu wa Musa. Indeed, I am your Lord, and I have chosen You.' With this reading, the majority of Kufa's reciters read it. As for the general reciters of Medina and Basra and some people of Kufa, they read it: 'wa ana chotitaka' with a lightened 'n' as a sign from Allah Himself that He chose Him. Abu Ja'far said: The correct view in my view is that they are two readings, each recited by scholars of the Qur'an, with their meanings agreed. Whichever of them the reciter recited, the correct view is correct. The interpretation of the statement: We have chosen You. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibrahim.
