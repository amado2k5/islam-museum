## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “That is because they believed, then disbelieved, so their hearts were sealed, and they do not understand,” explains that evil was the conduct of these hypocrites who used their oaths as a shield because they believed in God and His Messenger, then disbelieved by doubting and denying them. His statement, “so their hearts were sealed,” means that God placed a seal of disbelief upon their hearts, preventing them from believing. We have already explained the nature of this sealing of the heart, along with its supporting evidence and the statements of scholars, elsewhere, so there is no need to repeat it here. His statement, “so they do not understand,” means that they cannot distinguish right from wrong, truth from falsehood, because God has sealed their hearts. Qatadah said regarding this: “That is because they believed, then disbelieved, so their hearts were sealed, and they do not understand.” They professed that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God, but their hearts were in denial and rejected it. The passage then quotes Qatadah’s words.
