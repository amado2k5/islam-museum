## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, from Aisha, who said: I came to Mecca while menstruating, and did not circumambulate the House or run between al-Safa and al-Marwa; I complained of that to the Messenger of God; he said: do what the pilgrim does, except that you may not circumambulate the House or run between al-Safa and al-Marwa until you are clean. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr thought the hadith in the Muwatta is from the transmission of the group of narrators from Ibn Shihab from Urwa from Aisha. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.549}
