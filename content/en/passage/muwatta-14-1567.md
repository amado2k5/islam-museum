## Summary
Malik says Ibn Shihab said that they had not been told of any woman who emigrated to God and His Messenger while her husband was a disbeliever living in the land of disbelief, without her emigration separating her from her husband, unless her husband emigrated before her waiting period ended. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.53}
