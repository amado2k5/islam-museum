## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And indeed We sent messengers before you; some of them We told stories against you, and among them are those We did not relate to you. And it is not for a messenger to bring a sign except by permission of Allah. But when the command of Allah comes, it is decided with truth, and there are losers among the falsehoods": "And indeed We sent," O Muhammad, "messengers before you" to their nations. "Among them are those to whose stories We sent to you," meaning: "Among those to whose nations We sent stories We told you were their news, and among them were those whose stories We did not relate to you." It was mentioned from Anas that they are eight thousand. The narration is mentioned accordingly: And from Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir, from Yazid ibn Aban, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: The Prophet was sent after eight thousand prophets, including four thousand from the Children of Israel. From Utbah ibn Utaybah al-Basri al-Abdi, from Abu Sahl, from Wahb ibn Abdullah ibn Ka'b ibn Sur al-Azdi, from Salman, from the Prophet, who said: "Allah sent four thousand prophets." In the Maqta', he quotes from Wahb.
