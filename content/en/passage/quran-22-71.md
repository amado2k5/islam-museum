## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they worship besides Allah that of which He has not sent down authority, and of which they have no knowledge, and for the wrongdoers is any helper": These polytheists worship besides Allah that by which no proof from the heavens has been sent down to them in a book of His Books sent down to His messengers, that they are gods whose worship is fit and they worship them, that Allah has permitted them to worship them, and that they have no knowledge that they are gods. "And the wrongdoers have no helper." He says: And what helper will the disbelievers in Allah who worship these idols have to support them on the Day of Resurrection, save them from Allah's punishment, and avert His punishment from them if He wills to punish them?
