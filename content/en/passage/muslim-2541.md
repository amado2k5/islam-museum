## Summary
A report of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri in the chapter on the prohibition of insulting the Companions, in the Book of the Virtues of the Companions in Sahih Muslim. The context is a dispute that arose between Khalid ibn al-Walid and Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf. {cite:sahih-muslim}
