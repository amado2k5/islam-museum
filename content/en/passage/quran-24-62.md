## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The believers are only those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, and when they are with him on a comprehensive matter, they do not go until they ask His permission. Indeed, those who ask your permission are those who believe in Allah and His Messenger. So when they ask your permission for some of their affairs, then permit whom you will among them and ask forgiveness for them. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." The true believers are not believers, except those who are truthful to Allah and His Messenger. "And if they are with him," he says: and if they are with the Messenger of God, "on a comprehensive matter," he says: on a matter that unites them all from a battle that was present, or a prayer that was gathered for it, or a consultative matter that was revealed, "they did not go," meaning they did not depart from what they had gathered for in the matter, until they sought the permission of the Messenger of God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
