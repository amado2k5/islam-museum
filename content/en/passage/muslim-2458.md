## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the virtues of Bilal in Sahih Muslim: the Prophet heard the sound of Bilal's sandals ahead of him in Paradise, and Bilal said his work was keeping up prayer with every ablution. It is among the best-known texts announcing Paradise. {cite:sahih-muslim}
