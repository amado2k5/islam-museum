## Summary
Malik transmits that Said ibn al-Musayyab and Sulayman ibn Yasar were asked about the divorce of a drunk man. They said: if a drunk man divorces, his divorce is valid, and if he kills, he is killed for it. Malik said this is the practice among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.104}
