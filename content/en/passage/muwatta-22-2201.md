## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, that he heard Makhul al-Dimashqi ask al-Qasim ibn Muhammad about the life-estate and what people say about it. Al-Qasim said: I found people only keeping to their conditions regarding their property and what they gave. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.302}
