## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, those who believe and do righteous deeds will have gardens of bliss": "Indeed, those who believe" in Allah and unite Him, believe His Messenger and follow him, "and do righteous deeds," meaning: so they obey Allah, act upon what He commanded them in His Book and on the tongue of His Messenger, and refrained from what He forbade them. "For them are gardens of bliss," meaning they will abide therein forever. He means: They will remain there indefinitely. "Allah has promised them truly," meaning: Allah has promised them a true promise, without doubt or break, "and He is the Mighty." He says: He is the one who takes severe revenge on the polytheists and those who turn away from His path, the 'Wise' in managing His character.
