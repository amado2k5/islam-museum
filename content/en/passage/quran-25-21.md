## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who do not expect to meet Us said, 'Why did not the angels come down to us or we see our Lord? Indeed, they have become arrogant among themselves and have become very arrogant.": And the polytheists who do not fear Our meeting and do not fear Our punishment said, 'Why did Allah not send down to us angels to tell us that Muhammad is right in what he says, and that what he has brought to us is true, or we see our Lord and He informs us of it, as He, Glorified and Exalted, said informing us about them:' And they said, 'We will not believe in you until you gush forth a spring for us from the earth.' Then He said: 'Or bring Allah and the angels to the tribe.' Allah will say: The speakers of this statement have become arrogant in themselves and exalted. 'Be cautious and be mighty in a great way.' He means: And they transgressed arrogance by saying that this is its limit.
