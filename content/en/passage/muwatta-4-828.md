## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: beware of continuous fasting, beware of continuous fasting. They said: Messenger of God, you yourself fast continuously? He said: I am not like you; I spend the night and my Lord feeds and gives me drink. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi in Abu Dawud and Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim and others transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.404}
