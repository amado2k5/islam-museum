## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd ibn Qays and Thawr ibn Zayd al-Dili that they told him from the Messenger of God that he saw a man standing in the sun, and said: what is this? They said: he vowed not to speak, not to shade himself from the sun, not to sit, and to fast. The Messenger of God said: order him to speak, to shade himself, to sit, and to complete his fast. Ibn Abd al-Barr said the hadith is connected from the Prophet through several routes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.609}
