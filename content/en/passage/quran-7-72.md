## Summary
Verse 72 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 72 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-72|7:72]], al-Tabari says: We saved Noah, so the printed text reads although the context is the people of Hud, and those with him among his followers, by a mercy from Us, and we destroyed all those who denied Our proofs to the last of them, leaving none. (14809) from Ibn Zayd that "cut off the root" means: we uprooted them; and he refers back to what he said on [[passage/quran-6-45|6:45]]. "They were not believers" means they did not believe in God or in His messenger Hud. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 524}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
