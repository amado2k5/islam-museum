## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 15 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "And the earth and the mountains were lifted up and crushed with a single crushing" as meaning they were shaken with a single earthquake. Ibn Zayd said regarding this: Ibn Zayd said, concerning the phrase "And the earth and the mountains were lifted up and crushed with a single crushing," that they became dust. It was said: “So they were crushed.” He mentioned before the mountains and the earth, and it is a collective noun. He did not say: “So they were crushed,” because he made the mountains like one thing, as the poet said: “They are two masters who claim, and they only rule us if their sheep are easy to fatten.” And as it was said: “The heavens and the earth were joined together.” And in the passage he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
