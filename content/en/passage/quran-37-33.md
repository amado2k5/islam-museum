## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 34 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "for on that Day they will share in the punishment": Indeed, the mankind who disbelieved in Allah and their spouses, and did not worship besides Allah, and those who led mankind among the jinn on the Day of Resurrection in the punishment, will all share in the Fire, just as they shared in disobedience to Allah in this world. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "Indeed, they will be shared that Day in the punishment," he said: they and the devils. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
