## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is for whoever honors the sanctities of Allah, it is better for him in the sight of his Lord. And the cattle have been lawful to you except for what is recited to you, so avoid impurity from idols and avoid false speech": He means that He mentioned Him by saying "that": This is what He commanded regarding performing prayers, fulfilling vows, and circumambulating the Old House, which is the obligatory duty upon you, O people, during your Hajj. "And whoever honors the sanctities of Allah, it is better for him in the sight of his Lord." He means: Whoever avoids what Allah commanded him to avoid during the state of Ihram out of respect for Allah's limits and forbids him from making them lawful, then that is better for him in the Hereafter. As he said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, who said: Mujahid said regarding the phrase: "That is and whoever honors the sanctities of Allah," he said: The sanctity includes Mecca, Hajj, Umrah, and all the sins Allah has forbidden. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, similarly. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
