## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6-8 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "when they were sitting around it" as referring to the fire with its fuel, since these disbelievers, the People of the Ditch, were sitting around it, meaning around the fire. He says "around it," meaning they were sitting on the edge of the ditch. It is said "on the fire," meaning at the very edge of the ditch, so the listeners understand its meaning. Qatadah said something about this: Regarding Qatadah's statement, "the fire with its fuel, when they were sitting around it," he meant the believers. This interpretation, which Qatadah offered, is based on the view that the People of the Ditch were killed from among the believers. We have already demonstrated that the correct interpretation is different from the one Qatadah offered. He then quotes Qatadah in the section.
