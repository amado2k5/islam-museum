**Commentary on verse 92: "Obey God and obey the Messenger, and beware", pp. 574-575**

Al-Tabari says: after God said that wine, gambling, altars and arrows are an abomination of Satan's work, to be avoided, He commanded obedience to Himself and His messenger in avoiding them and in following His command to desist from what He forbade, and to oppose Satan in his command to disobey God, for he only wants enmity and hatred between them through wine and gambling. "Beware" means fear God and be mindful that He may see you at what He forbade or miss you at what He commanded, so you destroy yourselves. "If you turn away" means if you do not act on what we commanded and abstain from what we forbade, and go back from faith and acceptance of God and His messenger and following what your Prophet brought, "know that the duty of Our messenger is only clear delivery", that is, he is only to deliver the message plainly enough to show the way of truth, while punishment for turning away falls on the one to whom he is sent, not on the messengers. This is a warning to those who turn from His command and prohibition, so they should expect His punishment and beware His anger.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 575}
