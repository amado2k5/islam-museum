## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 71 to 74 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "except the sincere servants of Allah": Allah says: So see how was the end of the warners, except the servants of Allah whom We sincerely chose to believe in Allah and His messengers, and He excluded the servants of Allah from the warners, because the meaning is: So see how We destroyed the warners except the believing servants of Allah. Therefore, it is good to exempt them from them.
