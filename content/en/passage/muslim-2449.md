## Summary
A report of al-Miswar ibn Makhrama in the chapter on the virtues of Fatima, in Sahih Muslim: the hadith of the "piece of flesh", in which the Prophet makes harm to her his own harm. It is among the central texts on her standing in Sunni belief. {cite:sahih-muslim}
