## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 65 to 66 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that Allah calls the polytheists and says, 'What did you answer the messengers concerning His oneness and disassociation from idols? Then the news was made blind to them that day, meaning the news was made hidden from them. The meaning is that the proof was blinded to them, so they did not know what they were arguing for, because Allah has conveyed to them the excuse and they do not question. {cite:altafsir-tabari} — https://www.altafsir.com/Tafasir.asp?tMadhNo=0&tTafsirNo=1&tSoraNo=28&tAyahNo=65&tDisplay=yes&UserProfile=0&LanguageId=1
