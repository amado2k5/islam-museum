**Commentary on verse 78 (p. 381)**

Al-Tabari says: do these hypocrites who disbelieve in God and His Messenger in secret and show belief in them to the people of belief openly not know that God knows their secret, which they conceal in themselves of unbelief in Him and His Messenger, "and their private talk", when they confer among themselves slandering Islam and its people and mentioning them in a way they should not be mentioned, so that they should fear God's punishment lest He bring it down on them and His might lest He strike them for their unbelief in God and His Messenger and their disparagement of Islam and its people, and give it up and repent of it; "and that God is the knower of the unseen" means: do they not know that God knows what is hidden from the hearing, sight and senses of His creatures, what their souls conceal and does not show on their outward limbs, so that it should keep them from deceiving His friends with hypocrisy and lies and warn them off hiding other than what they display and displaying the opposite of what they believe? The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "knower of the unseen". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 381}
