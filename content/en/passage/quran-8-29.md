**Commentary on verse 29 (pp. 487-491)**

Al-Tabari says on "you who believe, if you fear God He will give you a criterion, wipe out your evil deeds and forgive you; God is of great bounty" [[passage/quran-8-29|8:29]]: you who believe God and His Messenger, if you fear God by obeying Him, performing His obligations, avoiding His disobediences and leaving betrayal of Him, of His Messenger and of your trusts, He will give you a criterion, that is a separation and distinction between your right and the falsehood of whoever wishes you harm among your polytheist enemies, by His helping you against them and giving you victory; "wipe out your evil deeds" means He erases from you what is past of your sins between you and Him; "and forgive you" means He covers them and veils them for you so that He does not hold you to account for them; "God is of great bounty" means He who does this for you has great bounty on you and on others of His creation, and His doing it is a recompense from Him to His servant for obeying Him, since it is He who enables His servant to the obedience he earned until he deserved from his Lord the recompense He promised for it. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "criterion", "wiping out", "evil deeds", "forgiveness" and "bounty". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 487} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 488}

The interpreters differed in the wording of the interpretation of "He will give you a criterion": some said a way out, some said deliverance, some said a separation, and all are close in sense though the wordings differ, as al-Tabari has shown earlier. For those who said its meaning is the way out: reports 15936 to 15941, 15943, 15944 and 15947, from Mujahid, with 15939 reading "a way out in this world and the hereafter"; report 15942, from Ibn Abbas; reports 15945 and 15946, from al-Dahhak; and report 15948, from Ikrima. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 488} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 489}

For those who said its meaning is deliverance: report 15949, from Ikrima; report 15950, from a man, from Ikrima and Mujahid, Ikrima saying the way out and Mujahid deliverance; report 15951, from al-Suddi; report 15952, from Ibn Abbas; report 15953, from Qatada. For those who said a separation: report 15954, from Ibn Zayd, a criterion that separates in their hearts between truth and falsehood until they know it and are guided by that criterion, the editor noting that the chain of this report is missing in the manuscript and that the printed edition's publisher joined the words without a sign; report 15955, from Ibn Ishaq: a separation between truth and falsehood by which your truth is made to appear and the falsehood of whoever opposes you is hidden. "Criterion" in the speech of the Arabs is a verbal noun from their saying, I separated one thing from another, separating them with a separation and a criterion. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 489} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 490} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 491}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
