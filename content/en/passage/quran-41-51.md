## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when We bestow favor upon a person, He turns away and distances himself from his side; and when evil touches him, He is a Person of Wide Supplication": And when We bestow favor upon the disbeliever, and We remove what harm is upon him, grant him wealth and abundance, grant him physical health and well-being, turn away from what We called him to in obedience, and turn away from him, and He turned away from him. "And distanced himself from his side," meaning: after responding to what We called him to, meaning beside him in his direction.
