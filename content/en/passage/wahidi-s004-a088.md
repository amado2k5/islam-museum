## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nisa 4:88, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «فما لكم في المنافقين فئتين».

## Full report (Arabic source text)

فرقة: لا نقتلهم، فنزلت هذه الآية.
