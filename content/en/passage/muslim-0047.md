## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim: speaking good or keeping silent, and honouring the neighbour and the guest, are parts of faith. It is one of the best-known texts on the ethics of speech and neighbourliness. {cite:sahih-muslim}
