## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "These are the verses of Allah which We recite to you in truth. So in which hadith after Allah and His signs will they believe?" These verses and proofs, O Muhammad, from your Lord over His creation, We recite them to you with truth: He says: We inform you about them in truth, not falsehood, just as the polytheists tell your people about their gods falsely, that they bring them closer to Allah in a false manner. So in which hadith after Allah and His verses do you believe? Allah says to the polytheists about it: So in what hadith, O people, after this hadith of Allah that He recites to you, and after His proofs against you and the proofs by which He has shown you of His oneness, Indeed, you have no lord other than Him. Believe so, if you deny His speech and verses. This interpretation follows the reading of those who recite 'you believe' in the manner of Allah's address to the polytheists, and this is the general reading of the reciters of Kufa.
