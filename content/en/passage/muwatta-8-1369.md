## Summary
Malik says that whoever swears to a thing while knowing he is sinning, swearing to a lie knowingly, to satisfy someone, or to excuse himself to someone he asks pardon from, or to cut off property with it, this is too grave for there to be an expiation for it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.612}
