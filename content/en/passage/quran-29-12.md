## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieved said to those who believed, 'Follow Our way, and We will bear your sins, and they bear no of their sins. Indeed, they are liars.'" And those who disbelieved in Allah from Quraysh said to those who believed in Allah among them: "Follow Our way," meaning: They said: Be as we are, denying resurrection after death, denying reward, and punishment for deeds. "And let us bear your sins." He says: They said, 'If you follow our way in that, you will be resurrected after death, and you will be rewarded for deeds, for indeed, we bear the consequences of sins.' Your sins then. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
