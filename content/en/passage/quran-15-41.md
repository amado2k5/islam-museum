## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 41 and 42 as a single passage, which is God's response to Iblis. In "He said, 'This is the path of Ali straight,' the general recitation of the reciters of Hijaz, Medina, Kufa, and Basra is presented: 'The path of Ali,' meaning this is a path that returns to Me, so reward each with his deeds, similar to the saying of the one who threatens him with your path to me, and about the true warrior who returns to God and follows his path without astray." And "Indeed, My servants, you have no authority over them except those who follow you among the misguided": Satan has no authority over the believing servants of God; rather, his authority is over the misguided who choose to follow him.
