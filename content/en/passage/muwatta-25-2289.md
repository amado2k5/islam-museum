## Summary
Malik said: some scholars explained the 'giving' in the verse of al-Nur as: a man contracts with his slave, then reduces for him some of the final instalment of the contract. Malik said: this is the best of what he has heard from scholars, and what people have always followed in his view. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.344}
