## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And thus We sent it down as an Arabic Qur'an and diverted therein from the warning that they might become righteous or that He might make for them a reminder": Just as We encouraged the people of faith to do good deeds by promising them what We promised them, likewise We warned the disbelievers with warning against the punishment of Our disobedience and disbelief in Our verses. So We revealed this Qur'an in Arabic, when they were Arabs, "and We diverted therein from the warning," explaining it: He says: And We warned them therein with various kinds of warnings "that they might become righteous," meaning: So that they may fear us by reflecting on the warning we have directed toward it, "or He will make it a reminder for them," meaning: or this Quran will inform them of a reminder, so they will heed and take heed from our actions against the nations that denied the messengers before them, and they will turn away from what they are standing on in disbelief in God. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
