## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari concludes the passage that began with verse 23: "And indeed, your Lord will gather them," meaning God will gather the creatures—those who came forth and those who came after—on the Day of Judgment for reckoning and recompense. "Indeed, He is Wise, All-Knowing": He is Wise in His management of His creation and His judgment among them, Knowing of their deeds and who He brings forth and who He delays. He mentioned in the verse before it the statements of the interpreters regarding the foregoing and the later generations: those who have passed from the nations and those who did not create from Ikrimah and Qatadah, those who died and those who remained from Mujahid and Muhammad ibn Ka'b.
