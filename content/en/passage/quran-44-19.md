## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And that you do not rise above Allah; indeed, I will bring you clear authority": and a noble messenger came to them, saying, 'Submit to Me the servants of Allah, and do not rise above Allah.' What he meant by His saying: "Do not rise above Allah" is that you do not transgress and seek against your Lord, so you disbelieve in Him and disobey Him, and disobey His command. "Indeed, I bring you clear authority," meaning: I bring you proof of the truth of what I call you to and proof of its truth, making it clear to those who reflect on and reflect on it that it is proof for me of the truth of what I am telling you.
