## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And how many a town I was appointed to while it was wrongdoer, then I took it, and to Me is the final destination": "And how many a town I was appointed to do," meaning: I gave them respite and delayed their punishment, while they are partners with Allah and opposing His command. That was their injustice that Allah, Glorified and Exalted, described them for, so I did not hasten their punishment. "Then I took it," meaning: Then I took it with punishment, and I punished it in this world by imposing Our punishment on them. "And to Him is the final destination," He says: And to Me is their fate after their destruction. They will be met with punishment that never ends, as He, the Exalted, says: "So is the state of those who hasten to the punishment among the polytheists of your people. And if You delay for them until their appointed terms which You have postponed for them, I will seize them with punishment, so fight them with the sword, then to Me will be their fate after that, and their pain will be a punishment for the sins they have committed."
