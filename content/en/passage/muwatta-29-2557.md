## Summary
Malik said: deliberate killing among us is when a man intends a man and strikes him until his soul leaves. It is also deliberate when a man strikes another in a dispute between them, then leaves him alive, and he dies from the blow; in that case there is an oath of fifty (qasama). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.445}
