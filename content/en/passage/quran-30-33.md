## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when harm touches the people, they call upon their Lord, turning to Him in repentance. But when He makes them taste mercy from Him, a group among them associates with their Lord." And when those polytheists who set up another god besides Allah afflicts them with hardship, drought, and drought, "Leave their Lord," meaning: Be sincere to their Lord, dedicate monotheism to Him, dedicate themselves to Him in supplication and supplication, and seek help from Him, repenting to Him from their polytheism and disbelief. "Then when He makes them taste mercy from Him," he says: Then when their Lord, the Exalted, removes that harm from them, relieves them, and brings them prosperity, fertility, and abundance, "when a group of them associates partners with their Lord," meaning they worship gods and idols with Him.
