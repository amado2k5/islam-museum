**Commentary on verse 94 (pp. 424-425)**

Al-Tabari says: these hypocrites who stayed behind, opposing the Messenger of God and leaving striving against the polytheists with you, believers in God, make excuses to you with falsehoods and lies when you return to them from your journey and your striving; "say" to them, Muhammad, "make no excuses, we shall not believe you", meaning we shall not credit what you say; "God has informed us of your news" means God has told us of your reports and let us know of your affair what has let us know your lying; "God and His Messenger will see your work" means God and His Messenger will see hereafter your work, whether you repent of your hypocrisy or persist in it; "then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the seen" means then you will go back after your deaths to the Knower of the unseen and the seen, meaning He who knows the secret and the open, from whom the inward and outward of your affairs are not hidden; "and He will tell you what you used to do" means He will inform you of all your deeds, bad and good, and repay you for them, the good with good and the bad with bad. The editor refers to earlier explanations of tidings and of the Knower of the unseen and the seen. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 425}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
