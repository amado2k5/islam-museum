## Summary
Malik says: likewise, a man who slanders a free man and receives the prescribed punishment, then a man and two women come and testify that the man he slandered was a slave, which makes the punishment lapse from the slanderer after it fell on him. The testimony of women is not admissible in slander. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.266}
