**Commentary on verse 56 (p. 104)**

On [[passage/quran-10-56|10:56]] he says: God is the giver of life and death, and nothing He wills is beyond Him, whether to bring these polytheists back to life after their death or to make them die; to Him they come after dying, and they then see what they used to deny of God's warning and punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 104}
