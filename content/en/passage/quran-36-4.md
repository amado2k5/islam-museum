## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 1–4. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among interpreters regarding 'Yasin': some said it is an oath by which Allah swore and it is among the names of Allah, and others say otherwise. Then he clarifies that Allah swore by the Holy Qur'an that Muhammad is among the messengers on a straight path.
