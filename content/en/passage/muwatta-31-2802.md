## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, who said: the Prophet said: the devil attacks one or two, but if they are three he does not attack them. The editor notes that the hadith is relayed (mursal) by agreement of Malik's transmitters. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.574}
