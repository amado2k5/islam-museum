## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Amr ibn Muadh al-Ashhali al-Ansari, from his grandmother, who said: the Messenger of God said: O women of the believers, let none of you despise her neighbour, even a burnt sheep's hoof. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.596}
