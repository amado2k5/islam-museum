## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: what is above the chin of the head, the one in ihram should not cover. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1051), Suwayd (495), al-Shaybani (418) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.440}
