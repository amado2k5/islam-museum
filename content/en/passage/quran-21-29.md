## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And whoever among them says, 'I am a god besides Him,' We will recompense him with Hell. Thus do We recompense the wrongdoers." He explains: And whoever among the angels says, 'I am a god besides Allah,' 'that is,' that among them who say that, 'We will recompense him with Hell.' He says: We will recompense him for saying that that is Hell. 'Thus do We recompense the wrongdoers,' meaning: Just as We recompense those among the angels who say, 'I am a god besides Allah, Hell.' So too do We recompense whoever wrongs himself and disbelieves in God and worships others. It was said: What I mean by this verse is Iblis. And those who say that said: We only said that because none of the angels said that I am a god besides Allah
