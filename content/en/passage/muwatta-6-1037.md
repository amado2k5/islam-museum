## Summary
Malik was asked about a man who has an earache: may he drop into his ear oil that is not perfumed while in ihram? He said: I see no harm in it; and if he put it in his mouth I would see no harm in that. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1197) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.482}
