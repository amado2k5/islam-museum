## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 55 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then when harm is removed from you, a group of you associates partners with their Lord": Then when your Lord grants you well-being, removes from you what afflicted you of illness in your bodies and hardship in your livelihood, and relieves the affliction from you, "When a group of you associates partners with their Lord," it means: a group of you associate partners with Allah in their worship, worshipping idols and slaughtering sacrifices for them in gratitude to others who have been granted relief from their distress. "To deny what We have given them" means: to deny Allah's favor in what He has given them of removing harm from them.
