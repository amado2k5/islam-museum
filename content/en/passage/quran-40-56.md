## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who dispute about the verses of Allah without authority has come to them. Indeed, in their breasts is nothing but arrogance, which they cannot fully reach. So seek refuge in Allah. Indeed, He is the Hearing, the Seeing." Indeed, those who dispute with you, O Muhammad, regarding what you brought them from your Lord of the signs "He came to them without authority," he says: without any argument that came to them from Allah by arguing with you about them, "Indeed, in their breasts is nothing but arrogance," meaning: There is nothing in their hearts but pride, for which they are proud to follow You, and to accept the truth You brought them out of envy of them for the favor God has given you, and the honor He has honored you with through prophethood. "They are not capable of attaining it." He says: What they envied you for is something they neither grasp nor attain, because that is the grace of God that He grants to whom He wills, and it is not something that is attained by wishful thinking. It has been said that its meaning is that in their hearts is nothing but greatness, which they do not attain because God humiliates them. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
