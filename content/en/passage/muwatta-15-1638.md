## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Rubayyi bint Muawwidh ibn Afra came with her paternal uncle to Abdullah ibn Umar, and told him she had redeemed herself from her husband in the time of Uthman ibn Affan. The news reached Uthman, and he did not object. Abdullah ibn Umar said her waiting period is that of a divorced woman. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.75}
