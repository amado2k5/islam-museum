## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Amr ibn al-Sharid, that Abdullah ibn Abbas was asked about a man with two wives, one of whom nursed a boy and the other a girl. He was asked whether the boy may marry the girl. He said no, the milk is one. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.121}
