## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "She said, 'How can I have a boy when no human has touched me and I am not an adulteress?': Mary said to Gabriel: 'I will have a boy.' From what perspective would I have a boy?" Is he before a husband whom I marry and provide for from him, or does God begin in His creation with the phrase 'and no human has touched me' from the descendants of Adam with a lawful marriage 'and I have not been 'but I', since none of them have ever touched me in a lawful manner 'out of prostitution' (out of indecency) and I did so in a forbidden way, so I took him out of adultery, as in Al-Suddi's 'and I was not a prostitute,' he says: an adulteress. "He said, 'Thus he said, 'Your Lord is easy to me.'" Allah Almighty says: Gabriel said to her: This is the matter as you describe, that you were not touched by a human and that you were not unjust, but your Lord said: He is easy to me: meaning the creation of the boy whom you said I would give to you to Ali is easy; it is impossible for me to create him and give him to you without a stallion to open for you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
