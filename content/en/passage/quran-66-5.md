## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “Perhaps his Lord, if he divorces you, will give him in exchange wives better than you—submissive [to Allah], believing, devoutly obedient, repentant, worshipping, traveling [for His cause], previously married and virgins,” is interpreted as: “Perhaps the Lord of Muhammad, if he divorces you, O wives of Muhammad, will give him in exchange wives better than you.” It has also been said that this verse was revealed to the Messenger of God as a warning from God to his wives when they gathered against him in jealousy. The passage also includes statements attributed to Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
