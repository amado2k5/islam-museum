**Commentary on verse 80: "You see many of them taking the unbelievers as allies", pp. 496-497**

Al-Tabari says: you see, Muhammad, many of the Children of Israel taking the unbelievers as allies, that is, taking the idol-worshipping polytheists as allies and opposing God's friends and messengers. "How evil is what their souls sent ahead for them" is an oath that what they sent ahead to their return in the hereafter is evil, namely God's anger with them for what they did; the clause "that God was angry with them" stands in the nominative as a substitute for "what". "In the punishment they will abide" means in God's punishment on the day of resurrection their stay is permanent.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 497}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
