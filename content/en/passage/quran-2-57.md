## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "and We shaded you with the clouds" al-Tabari takes it as joined to "then We raised you up after your death", and "ghamam" as what covers the sky, whether cloud, murk or anything veiling it, the Arabs calling anything covered "maghmum". He records that it was said the "ghamam" was not ordinary cloud: Mujahid says it was not cloud but the "ghamam" in which God comes on the Day of Resurrection, though in another report he says it was like cloud; Ibn Abbas says it was a cloud cooler and sweeter than this, which was with them in the wilderness. Al-Tabari holds that what God shaded the Israelites with is no more to be described as cloud than as anything else that covers the face of the sky, and notes the saying that it was the white of the clouds. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 90} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 91}
