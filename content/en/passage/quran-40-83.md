## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when their messengers came to them with clear proofs, they rejoiced in what they had of knowledge, and what they used to mock overcame them": When those nations who came before Quraysh, the denial of their messengers, their messengers whom Allah had sent to them with clear proofs, meaning the clear proofs of Allah, the Almighty, "rejoiced in what they had of knowledge," meaning: they rejoiced in their ignorance of the knowledge they had, and they said: We will not be resurrected, nor will Allah punish us. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
