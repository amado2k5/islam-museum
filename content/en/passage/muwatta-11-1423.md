## Summary
A report that Malik says he heard some people of knowledge say about the falcon, the eagle, the hawk and the like: if the bird is trained and understands as trained dogs understand, there is no harm in eating what it kills when it hunts, provided the name of God was mentioned when it was released. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.637}
