## Summary
Hadith of Abu Dharr: some of the Companions said the wealthy had taken the rewards. The Prophet named many kinds of charity: every glorification, magnification, praise and declaration is charity, enjoining good is charity, and a man's intercourse with his wife is charity. Ibn Rajab explains that all good works are charity, and that the reward for intercourse depends on the intention. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
