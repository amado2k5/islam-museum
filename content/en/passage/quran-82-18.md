## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 19 as one section. His statement, “Then what will make you know what the Day of Judgment is?” explains: Then what will make you aware of the Day of Recompense and Reckoning, O Muhammad? This is to emphasize its importance. Then, may His praise be exalted, He explained some of its aspects, saying: “The Day when no soul will have power to do anything for another.” He says: That Day, the Day when no soul will have power, the Day when no soul will be able to help another in any way, to ward off any calamity that has befallen it, nor to benefit it in any way. In this world, it used to protect it and repel any harm that might befall it, but that will cease on that Day, because the command will belong to God, whom no one can overcome, and no one can subdue. On that Day, kingdoms will vanish, and leaderships will disappear, and dominion will belong to the Almighty King. This is what He says: “And the command that Day will be entirely with God.” He says: And all the command that Day, meaning the religion, will belong to God alone, not to any of His creation. No one among His creation has any power over it. On that day, he had no authority to command or forbid. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
