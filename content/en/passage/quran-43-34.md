## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 35 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And for their houses doors and couches upon which they recline": We made for their houses doors of silver and couches of silver. As he said: Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, and a couch of silver said: a couch of silver. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "And their houses have doors and couches on which they recline," he said: the doors are of silver, and the couches are of silver on which they lean, meaning they recline on the couches. And His saying: 'and ornamentation' means: And We made for them adornment along with that, which is gold. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
