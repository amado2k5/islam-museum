## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 137 to 138 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And indeed, you will pass by them in the morning": Allah says to the polytheists of Quraysh: "And indeed, you will pass by the people of Lot, whom We destroyed when you entered the morning and at night," as Qatadah narrated: "And indeed, you will pass by them in the morning," they said: Yes, by Allah, morning and evening, they cross it by footsteps. Whoever travels from Medina to the Levant, he takes over Sodom, the village of the people of Lot. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase: "You will pass by them in the morning," he said: in your travels. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
