## Summary
Malik transmits from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that a man of Aslam said: I did not sleep this night. The Messenger of God said: why? He said: a scorpion stung me. The Messenger of God said: if you had said when you reached evening: I seek refuge in the perfect words of God from the evil of what He created, it would not have harmed you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.541}
