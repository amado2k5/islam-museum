## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: none of you should pray while pressing his thighs together, that is, while he holds back the need to relieve himself. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.227}
