## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 37 as a single section. He explains the phrase “nor does he urge the feeding of the poor”: God Almighty informs us about this wretched person who will be given his record in his left hand: that in this world he did not urge people to feed the poor and needy. And His saying, “So he will have no friend here today,” means that on the Day of Resurrection, he will have no friend here, meaning in the Hereafter, a close relative to defend him and help him in his affliction, as Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase “So he will have no friend here today.” The word “close relative” in Arabic is used in this context. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
