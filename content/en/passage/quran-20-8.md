## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And if you speak aloud, He knows the secret and conceals it": And if you speak aloud, O Muhammad, or fear it, it is equal with your Lord, who owns whatever is in the heavens and what is on the earth, "for He knows the secret," meaning: indeed, what you have concealed within yourself is not hidden from Him, so do not reveal it with your limbs, nor speak with your tongue, nor utter it, and conceal. Then the scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of the phrase "and concealed," with some saying its meaning is 'and more concealed than secret.' They said: 'What is more hidden than the secret' is what a person has told himself but did not do. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, Samak, and others.
