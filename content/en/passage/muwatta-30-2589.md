## Summary
Malik said: if a slave kills a slave, deliberately or by mistake, the master of the slain slave has no oath of fifty, and his master is entitled to it only with a just proof, or with a witness whom he swears with. Yahya said: Malik said: this is the best I have heard. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.459}
