## Summary
A report of al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Tasting the flavour of faith depends on a threefold contentment: with God as Lord, with Islam as religion, and with the Prophet as Messenger. {cite:sahih-muslim}
