## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Umar, the eighth in the Forty Hadith: the Prophet was ordered to fight people until they testify that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is the Messenger of God. Ibn Rajab explains that whoever came to Islam with only the two testimonies was protected, and answers Sufyan ibn Uyayna's view that this was only at the beginning of Islam. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
