## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that the Messenger of God said: give to the one who asks, even if he comes on a horse. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that there is no disagreement among the Muwatta transmitters that this hadith is relayed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.595}
