## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 19–20. Al-Tabari explains that whoever is deserving of punishment before God's knowledge, you will not save those in the Fire, but those who fear their Lord will have chambers above which rivers flow, and God's promise is that God will not fail in His promise.
