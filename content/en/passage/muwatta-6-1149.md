## Summary
Malik says that whoever owes an offering for killing game or for something else, his offering can only be in Mecca; but what the offering is commuted to, such as fasting or charity, is done outside Mecca, wherever its owner wishes to do it. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.520}
