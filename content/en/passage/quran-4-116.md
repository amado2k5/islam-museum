## Summary
Verse 116 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 116 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 116: shirk and what is less than it (pp. 206-207, note 1 on p. 206 and note 1 on p. 207).** Al-Tabari says the sense is that God does not forgive Tu'ma, since he associated others with God and died on his association, nor any other of His creatures for their association and disbelief, and forgives what is below association, of sins, to whom He wills. He explains that had Tu'ma not associated and died on it, he would have been under God's will in respect of his past betrayal and disobedience, his punishment or pardon being God's affair; and the same holds for everyone who commits a crime, whose matter is God's unless his crime is association with God and disbelief, in which case it is decreed that he is among the people of the Fire if he dies on it, and God has forbidden Paradise to one who dies on his association, and his abode is the Fire. Al-Suddi's saying is given in report 10429 (Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, from Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal, from Asbat, from al-Suddi): the words "for whom He wills" mean those of the Muslims who avoid the grave sins. On the second part, whoever sets up a partner with God in His worship has gone off the road of truth and departed from the right course, a long way and a great departure, since in associating others with God in worship he has obeyed Satan and taken his road and abandoned obedience to God and the way of His religion; that is the far-off error and the manifest loss. The editor explains that the sentence between dashes in al-Tabari's words is parenthetical to the flow, the sense running on from "God would have had his matter" to "as for when he died on it"; the first printer misread it and so made the whole passage muddled; and on p. 207 that the printed text and the manuscript read "has obeyed" with a superfluous particle. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 207} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 207}
