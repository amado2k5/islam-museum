## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, I am Forgiving for whoever repents, believes, does righteous deeds, and then is guided": and whoever is subject to My wrath, He descends upon him. He desires, he says, he has deteriorated and suffered, as: he said: Muawiyah narrated to me, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "He desired" means: He has been miserable. And His saying: "Indeed, I am Forgiving to those who repent" means: I am the One who forgives whoever repents from their polytheism, so he returned to faith for Me and "believe," meaning: He is sincere to divinity for Me and has not associated anyone in His worship except Me. "And did righteousness," meaning: He fulfilled the obligations I made obligatory upon him and avoided sins. "Then he was guided," meaning: then he adhered to that, remained steadfast, and did not lose anything of it. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Al-Kalbi, and Al-Rabi'.
