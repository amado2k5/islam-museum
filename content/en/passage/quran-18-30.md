## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, those who believe and do righteous deeds, We do not let the reward of those who do good deeds be lost": Indeed, those who are truthful to Allah and His Messenger, act in obedience to Allah, and adhere to His command and prohibition, We do not let to waste the reward of whoever does good, obeys Allah, follows His command and prohibition, and rather recompense them with their obedience and good deeds like gardens of Eden beneath which rivers flow.
