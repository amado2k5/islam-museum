## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the prohibition of wronging, forsaking, despising, and of a Muslim's blood, honour and property, in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. It gathers the sanctity of a Muslim's blood, property and honour, and says that piety is in the heart. {cite:sahih-muslim}
