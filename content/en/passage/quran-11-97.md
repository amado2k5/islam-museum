**Commentary on verse 97 (p. 466)**

On [[passage/quran-11-97|11:97]], 'to Pharaoh and his chiefs', he means the nobles of his troops and followers; 'but they followed Pharaoh's command', that is, Pharaoh and his chiefs denied Moses, rejected God's oneness and refused what Moses brought from God, and Pharaoh's chiefs followed Pharaoh's command rather than God's, obeying him in denying Moses and rejecting what he brought. God says 'and Pharaoh's command was not right', meaning that Pharaoh's command guides none who accept it from him, in denying Moses, to any good, nor leads him to right, but leads him to the fire of Hell. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 466}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
