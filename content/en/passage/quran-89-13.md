## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 15 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “So your Lord poured upon them a scourge of punishment” as: “O Muhammad, your Lord inflicted His punishment upon them and brought down His wrath upon them because of the corruption they spread in the land and their transgression against God therein.” It has also been said: “So their Lord poured upon them a scourge of punishment.” Rather, these were calamities that befell them, either a wind that destroyed them, or an earthquake that shook them, or a drowning that perished them, without being struck by a whip or a stick. For among the most painful punishments inflicted upon the people addressed by this Quran was flogging. So much so that the people frequently used the expression, describing the severity of the torment inflicted upon a man, "So-and-so was flogged," until it became a proverb. They used it for anyone subjected to a severe form of punishment, saying, "A whip of torment was poured upon him." The passage also includes statements attributed to Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Dahhak, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and others.
