## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Dinar that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say the sunset prayer is the witr of the daytime prayers, in the chapter on the command to pray the witr. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.184}
