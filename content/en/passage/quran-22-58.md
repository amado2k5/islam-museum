## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who emigrated in the way of Allah and then were killed or died - Allah will provide them with good provision. And indeed, Allah is the best of providers": those who left their homelands and tribes and abandoned that for the pleasure of Allah, obedience to Him, and striving against His enemies, then were killed or died while they were so, "so that Allah may provide them" on the Day of Resurrection in His Gardens "a good provision." By good, it means noble, and by good provision it means abundant reward. "And indeed, Allah is the best of providers," meaning: and Allah is the best of those who extend His bounty upon His obedient and honored people. It was mentioned that this verse was revealed about a group among the companions of the Messenger of Allah who differed regarding the ruling on those who died in the cause of Allah. Some said: The same applies to the one killed among them and the dead, while others said: The one who is killed is better. So Allah revealed this verse to His Prophet, teaching them that the affairs of the deceased are equal in His cause, and that the one killed in it is rewarded with Him.
