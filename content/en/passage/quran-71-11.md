## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 11 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “He will send down abundant rain upon you” as meaning: Your Lord will provide you with rain if you repent, acknowledge His Oneness, and devote your worship to Him alone. He will send down continuous, abundant rain upon you. It is narrated that Umar ibn al-Khattab went out to pray for rain, but he did nothing more than seek forgiveness. When he returned, people said, “O Commander of the Faithful, we did not see you pray for rain.” He replied, “I sought rain with the means by which rain is brought down from the heavens.” Then he recited, “Seek forgiveness from your Lord. Indeed, He is ever Forgiving. He will send down abundant rain upon you.” He then recited the verse from Surah Hud until he reached, “And He will increase you in strength upon strength.” In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Sufyan.
