## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then We sent them to know which of the two parties was most counted for how long they had remained for a long time": neither of the two groups had knowledge, neither for their disbelievers nor for their believers. As for the phrase "for a while," the interpreters differed on its meaning; some said: it means: distant. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
