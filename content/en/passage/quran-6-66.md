## Summary
Verse 66 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 66 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "see how we vary the signs that they may understand" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: look, Muhammad, with the eye of your heart at our repeating our proofs against these who deny their Lord and reject his favours and our varying them among them, "that they may understand" says: that they understand it and take heed, and be reminded and held back from what they persist in that angers God, worshipping idols and images and denying God's book and his messenger. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 433}

The editor says that Ahmad also related report 13380 through Waki from Abu Jafar al-Razi; that at this point a part of the old division from which his manuscript was copied ends, and the manuscript has a closing in which the copyist prays a blessing on the Prophet and his family, after which the next part begins with the basmala and the words "my Lord, make easy"; and refers for "varying the signs" and "understanding" to earlier passages.

On "your people denied it though it is the truth; say: I am not a guardian over you" he says: your people, Muhammad, denied what you say and report and threaten of the threat, "and it is the truth": the threat we threatened them with for persisting in associating others, the sending of punishment from above them or beneath their feet or confusing them in sects and making some taste the might of others, is the truth that is surely to happen if they do not repent and turn from persisting in disobeying God and associating others with him to obeying him and believing in him; "say: I am not a guardian over you" says: say to them, Muhammad, I am not a keeper or watcher over you; I am only a messenger conveying what I was sent with to you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 434}

Verse 66 closes here on p. 434, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-67|6:67]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
