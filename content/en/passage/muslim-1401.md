## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the Book of Marriage of Sahih Muslim. The hadith answers a tendency among some Companions toward monastic abstention and sets the practical line of the Sunna. It is followed by the Prophet's rejection of celibacy for Uthman ibn Maz'un. {cite:sahih-muslim}
