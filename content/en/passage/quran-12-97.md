**Commentary on verses 97 and 98 (pp. 261-262)**

On "they said, O our father, ask forgiveness for our sins for us; we were sinners. He said, I will ask my Lord's forgiveness for you; He is the Forgiving, the Merciful" (verses 97 and 98, which al-Tabari explains together and which are therefore gathered here under verse 97), al-Tabari says: Ya'qub's sons who had separated him from Joseph said: O our father, ask your Lord to pardon us and veil for us our sins which we committed against you and Joseph, so that He does not punish us for them on the Day of Resurrection, "we were sinners" in what we did to him; we have confessed our sins. "He said, I will ask my Lord's forgiveness for you": Ya'qub said, I will ask my Lord to pardon your sins that you committed against me and Joseph. Then the people of knowledge differed over the time to which Ya'qub put off praying for his sons' forgiveness of their sin. Some said he put it off to the pre-dawn hour. Report 19870, from Muharib ibn Dithar, through Abu al-Sa'ib, Ibn Idris and Abd al-Rahman ibn Ishaq: an uncle of mine used to come to the mosque and heard someone say: O God, You called me and I answered, You commanded me and I obeyed, and this is the pre-dawn hour, so forgive me. He listened to the voice and it came from the house of Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud; he asked Abd Allah about it and he said: Ya'qub put his sons off to the pre-dawn hour with his words "I will ask my Lord's forgiveness for you". The editor says that Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud's narrator Abd al-Rahman ibn Ishaq ibn Sam' al-Wasiti, Abu Shayba, was called by Ahmad "nothing, a rejected narrator" and weakened by the rest, while Muharib ibn Dithar is reliable. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 261}

Report 19871, from Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud, through Ibn Waki', Ibn Fudayl, Abd al-Rahman ibn Ishaq and Muharib ibn Dithar: he put them off to the pre-dawn hour. Report 19872, from Ibrahim al-Taymi, through Abu Sufyan al-Himyari and al-Awwam: he put them off to the pre-dawn hour; the editor says Abu Sufyan al-Himyari is Sa'id ibn Yahya ibn Mahdi, who is truthful. Report 19873, from Amr ibn Qays, through Amr and Khallad al-Saffar: in the night prayer. Report 19874, from Ibn Jurayj, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn and Hajjaj: he put that off to the pre-dawn hour. Others said he put it off to Friday night. Report 19875, from Ibn Abbas, from the Messenger of God, through al-Muthanna, Sulayman ibn Abd al-Rahman Abu Ayyub al-Dimashqi, al-Walid, Ibn Jurayj, Ata' and Ikrima: "I will ask my Lord's forgiveness for you", he says, until Friday night comes, and it is the saying of my brother Ya'qub to his sons. The editor notes that Sulayman ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Tamimi Abu Ayyub al-Dimashqi is reliable but related rejected reports and is criticised for his narrations from the unreliable, and that al-Walid ibn Muslim al-Dimashqi al-Qurashi is reliable and was used by the compilers of the six books. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 262}

We stop here at the foot of p. 262, in the middle of the commentary on verses 97 and 98 (after report 19875, on the time of the prayer for forgiveness); the next batch begins at p. 263. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 262}
