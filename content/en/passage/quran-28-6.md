## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We want to bestow favor upon those who were weakened in the land and make them leaders and make them inheritors": His saying: "And We want" is a conjunction to "a group of them will be oppressed." The meaning is that Pharaoh exalted himself in the land and made its people from the Children of Israel a sect, weakening a group among them. "And we" "want to bestow favor upon those whom Pharaoh oppressed among the Children of Israel, and make them leaders." In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
