## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that whoever dawn overtakes on the night of Muzdalifa without having stood at Arafat has missed the pilgrimage, and whoever stood at Arafat on that night before the dawn rose has caught the pilgrimage. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.523}
