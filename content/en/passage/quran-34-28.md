## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We have not sent you except as a recompense to the people as a bringer of good tidings and a warner, but most people do not know": We did not send you, O Muhammad, to these polytheists from your people exclusively, but We sent you all to all people, Arabs and non-Arabs, red and black, as a bringer of good tidings for those who obey you and a warner of your lies. "But most people do not know" that Allah sent you as well to all mankind. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
