## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11-14 as a single phrase. He interprets the phrase “And Pharaoh and those before him and the overturned cities came with sin” as meaning: “And Pharaoh of Egypt came.” The reciters differed in their reading of the phrase “and those before him.” The majority of the reciters of Medina, Kufa, and Mecca, except for al-Kisa’i, read it as “and those before him” with a fatha on the qaf and a sukun on the ba, meaning: and those before Pharaoh from the nations who disbelieved in God’s signs, such as the people of Noah, ‘Ad, Thamud, and the people of Lot, came with sin. The majority of the reciters of Basra and al-Kisa’i read it as “and those before him” with a kasra on the qaf and a fatha on the ba, meaning: and those from his own country, Egypt, came with Pharaoh, the Copts. The correct view, in my opinion, is that both readings are well-known and have sound meanings, so whichever reading a reciter chooses is correct. His statement, “And the overturned cities, with their sin,” means: the cities that were overturned with their inhabitants, so that their tops became their bottoms, “with their sin,” meaning with their transgression. Their sin was: their practice of sodomy. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
