## Summary
Verse 163 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 163 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "He has no partner; this I was commanded and I am the first of the Muslims": "no partner" in any of that from His creation and none of them has a share in it, as it is not fitting that this be anything but His alone; "this I was commanded" means my Lord commanded me this; and "I am the first of the Muslims" means I am the first of this community to acknowledge, submit and humble himself to his Lord that it is so. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "Islam". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 283}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
