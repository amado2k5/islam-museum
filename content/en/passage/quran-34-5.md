## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And those who strive in Our verses in vain, those will have a painful punishment of false accusation": He affirmed this in the Book, so that He may recompense the believers for what was described, and to recompense those who strive in Our verses in vain, helping. They think they are preceding Us, so that We cannot overcome them. "Those will have a punishment," meaning: these will have a severe painful punishment, meaning the painful one. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
