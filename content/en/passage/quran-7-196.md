**Commentary on verse 196 (p. 323)**

God says to His prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, to the idol-worshipping polytheists, "my ally", my helper, supporter and backer against you, "is God who sent down the Book" to me with the truth, "and He takes the righteous as allies", for He is the one who takes as an ally those of His creatures whose works are made right by obeying Him. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "ally". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 323}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
