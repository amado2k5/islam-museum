## Summary
Malik says that the matter on which there is no disagreement among them is that the imam does not recite aloud in the noon prayer on the day of Arafat; that he preaches to the people on the day of Arafat; and that the prayer on the day of Arafat is the noon prayer, even if it coincides with Friday, but it is shortened because of travel. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.535}
