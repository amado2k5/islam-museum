## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "those whose eyes were covered with a cover from My remembrance and could not hear": Allah says: And We offered Hell that day to the disbelievers who did not look at the signs of Allah, contemplating them without contemplating His proofs, but they heed them, remembering and turning to the oneness of Allah, and submitting to His command and prohibition. "And they could not hear" meaning: they could not bear to hear the remembrance of Allah which He reminded them of, and His clarification clarified for them in any Book, due to Allah's abandonment of them, the dominance of misery upon them, and their distraction from disbelief in Allah and obedience to Satan. They take heed from it and reflect on it, and they know guidance from misguidance and disbelief from faith. Mujahid used to say about this: He said: All of them are from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "They cannot hear," he said: They cannot reason. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
