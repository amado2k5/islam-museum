## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 25 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And they say, ‘When will this promise be fulfilled, if you are truthful?’”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: The polytheists say: When will what you promise us of resurrection to God be fulfilled, if you are truthful in your promise to us?
