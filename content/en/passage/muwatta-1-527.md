## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the Prophet's Mosque. Yahya transmits from Malik from Zayd ibn Rabah and Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Abdallah, both from Abu Abdallah al-Aghar, from Abu Hurayra: the Prophet said that prayer in his mosque is better than a thousand prayers in any other mosque, except the Sacred Mosque. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik, and from him Ibn Majah (1404); al-Tinisi transmitted it in al-Bukhari (1190); Qutayba and Ma'n in al-Tirmidhi (325); and that one copy calls al-Aghar "Salman al-Aghar". {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.272}
