## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas used to pray one rak'a of witr after the night prayer. Malik comments that the practice in Medina does not follow this, and that the least of the witr is three. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.184}
