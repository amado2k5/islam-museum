## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 38 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "But He is Allah, my Lord, and I do not associate anyone with my Lord": As for me, I do not disbelieve in my Lord, but I am Allah my Lord. It means that he says: But I say: He is Allah, my Lord, and I do not associate anyone with my Lord. There are two interpretations of this: one is 'But He is Allah my Lord' with a stressed 'n' and the alif omitted in the case of a joining, as it is said: 'Ana Qa'im' and the alif is omitted from 'Ana', which is the general reading of the reciters of Iraq. As for pause, the entire recitation has the alif affirmed, because the nun is stressed to merge the nun from 'lakon,' and it is silent in the nun that is from 'ana,' as the hamza in 'ana' is dropped. When it is paused, the alif in 'ana' appears. It is said: 'lakna,' because in pausing it it is said to affirm the alif in 'ana' rather than dropping it.
