## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, in the chapter on the woman with irregular bleeding: the irregularly bleeding woman need only bathe once, then perform ablution after that for each prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.108}
