## Summary
A report of Jubayr ibn Mut'im in the chapter on the names of the Prophet, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim: five prophetic names that gather the call, the gathering to disbelief, the Resurrection and the seal of prophethood. {cite:sahih-muslim}
