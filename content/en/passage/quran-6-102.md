## Summary
Verse 102 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 102 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "That is God your Lord, there is no god but He, creator of all things, so worship Him; He is guardian over all things", al-Tabari says: the one who created everything and knows everything is God your Lord, you who set up gods and idols beside God and make the jinn partners to Him; your gods own neither benefit nor harm. This is God's denial of those who claimed that the jinn are partners to Him: nothing deserves divinity and worship except the one who created everything and knows everything, so your worship and the worship of all who are in the heavens and the earth should be His alone, with no partner; He is the creator and maker of all things, and it is the due of the made to single out its maker for worship. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 12}

He glosses "so worship Him" as: be humble to Him with obedience, worship and service, and submit to Him in that; and "wakil" as one who, over everything He has created, is watcher and guardian, taking charge of its provision, sustenance, governance, management and direction by His power. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 13}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
