## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 8–9. Al-Tabari explains that God placed shackles around the necks of the disbelievers, which are up to the necks of the disbelievers, so they are stubborn, and He placed a barrier before them and a barrier behind them, but He covered them so they could not see. He explains the meaning of 'shackles,' and that it is like the state of one who withholds from faith.
