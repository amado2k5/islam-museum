## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Do not make the Messenger's supplication among you like the supplication of one another. Allah may know those who sneak away from among you, so let those who disobey His command beware lest trial befall them or a painful punishment." Allah Almighty says to the companions of His Prophet Muhammad: "Do not," O believers, "make the Messenger's supplication among you like the supplication of one another." The interpreters differed on the meaning of this. Some said: Allah forbade the believers with this verse from responding to the Messenger's supplication against them, and He said to them: Fear His supplication upon yourselves, by doing what angers Him, and he will pray for it against you and you will perish. So do not make his supplication like the supplication of others, for his supplication is accepted. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
