## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And mention in the Book Abraham; indeed, he was a truthful prophet": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: "And remember," O Muhammad, in the Book of Allah, "Abraham, the friend of the Most Merciful." So relate to these polytheists his stories and those of his father. "Indeed, he was truthful," meaning he was truthful in his speech, reports, and appointments, never lying, and the truthful is the active act of truthfulness. We have explained this previously, which is sufficient to repeat it here. "Prophet" means: God had informed him and revealed to him. And His saying: "When he said to his father," he says: Remember him when he said to his father, "O father, why do you worship what is not heard?" He means: what do you do by worshiping an idol that cannot hear or see anything, "and it does not avail you at all," meaning: it does not avert any harm from you; it is a meager image that neither harms nor benefits.
