**Commentary on verse 118: the cursed devil and the determined portion (pp. 212-213, note 1 on p. 212 and notes 1-3 on p. 213).** Al-Tabari says that the words that God cursed him mean He disgraced him, cast him out and removed him; so the sense is that they call only a rebellious devil whom God has cursed and removed from all good. The words that he said he would take a determined portion from God's servants mean that the rebellious devil said this to his Lord when he was cursed; the word for determined means known, as in report 10444 (al-Muthanna, from Abu Nu'aym, from Sufyan, from Juwaybir, from al-Dahhak): known. If someone asks how Satan takes a determined portion from God's servants, the answer is that he takes that portion by seducing them from the right road, calling them to obey him and adorning for them error and disbelief until he turns them from the path; whoever answers his call and follows what he has adorned for him is of his known portion and allotted share. And God reported what He reported of Satan's saying so that those who broke away from the Messenger after the guidance became clear to them might know that they are of the portion of Satan, whom God has cursed, and are among those on whom his conjecture came true. Al-Tabari adds that he has already shown the meaning of cursing and does not repeat it. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of portion (volume 4, p. 206 and volume 8, p. 58, note 4) and of the word for determined (volume 8, p. 50; volume 7, pp. 597-599; volume 5, p. 120; volume 4, p. 121), notes that the "determined" word describes Satan's portion, that the printed text and the manuscript read "that he is of those" where the context requires the plural, and refers to the earlier explanation of cursing (p. 57, note 4). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 213} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 213}
