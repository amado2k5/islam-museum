## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Show me those whom you have joined as partners with Him.' No! Rather, He is Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise." Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists with Allah, the gods and idols: Show me, O people whom you have joined with Allah and made them partners in your worship of Him: What have they created of the earth, or do they have any association in the heavens? 'No,' Allah says: 'They lied. The matter is not as they described, nor as they made and said, that Allah has a partner, but He is the deity who has no partner, and it is not fitting for Him to have a partner in His dominion.' The Almighty in taking revenge on those who associated others with Him among His creation, the Wise in managing His creation.
