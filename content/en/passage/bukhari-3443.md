## Summary
In the Book of the Stories of the Prophets, the report on Jesus: the Prophet's relation to Jesus is framed as a brotherhood through one religion with different mothers. It is the text of the prophetic connection on which the section on the stories of the prophets is built. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
