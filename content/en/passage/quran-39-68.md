## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He blew the trumpet and struck whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth except whom Allah wills. Then He blew into it again, and behold, they stood and looked": and Israfil was blown in the horn. We have explained the meaning of the images previously with their evidences, mentioned the differences among scholars on it, and the correct view is to say it with its evidences, so it is necessary to repeat it here. And His saying, "Then He struck whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth," means: He died, and that was in the first blow, as in Al-Suddi: "And he blew the trumpet and struck whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth," he said: He died. And His saying: "Except whom Allah wills," interpreters differed regarding what Allah meant by the exception in this verse; some said it referred to Gabriel, Michael, Israfil, and the Angel of Death. In the passage, he quotes from Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
