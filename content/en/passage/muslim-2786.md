## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Masud in the Book of the Description of the Resurrection, Paradise and Hell of Sahih Muslim: a Jewish scholar describes God's grasp of creation on the Day of Resurrection, and the Prophet confirms him and laughed. The verse is recited, "They did not measure God with His true measure" (Al-Zumar 67). {cite:sahih-muslim}
