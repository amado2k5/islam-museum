## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'My Lord, indeed I fear that they will lie'": "Moses said to his Lord, 'My Lord, indeed I fear' from the people of Pharaoh, whom You commanded me to come to 'that they will lie,' by telling them: You sent me to them, and 'And my chest will be constricted' because of their denial of Me if they deny me. He raised His saying: 'and My chest will be constricted' in conjunction with 'Akhaf', and with the nominative in it, it was read by most reciters of the lands, meaning: 'And my chest is constricted.' And His saying: 'And my tongue does not let go' means: 'And he does not start with the phrase from what you sent me to them,' because of the reason that was in his tongue. And His saying: 'And my tongue does not let go' is a phrase attached to 'yadiq'. And His saying: 'So he sent to Aaron' means his brother Aaron, and he did not say: 'So Harun sent to me to support and assist me,' as the meaning of the speech was understood, like the saying: 'If a calamity had come down, we would have been afraid of you,' meaning: We would have been afraid of you with our two curses. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
