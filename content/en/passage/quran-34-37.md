## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And what is your wealth or your children that brings you near to Us in the sanctuary, except for those who believe and do righteous deeds; those will have the recompense of weakness for what they did, while they are safe in chambers": He, the Exalted, says: And what is your wealth that you boast about, O people, before the people, nor your children whom you are arrogant about by that which brings you closer to Us in nearness? In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
