## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, avoid much suspicion. Indeed, some suspicion is sin. Do not spy and do not backbite one another. Do any of you like to eat the flesh of his dead brother? So you should be misled by him. And fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is Accepting of Repentance and Merciful." This means that you do not approach too much suspicion of the believers, for suspicion is not justified. He, Glorified and Exalted, said: "Avoid much suspicion," and did not say: all suspicion, as He had permitted the believers to think of each other of good, saying: "Why did the believing men and women not think good of themselves when you heard it, and they said, 'This is a clear lie.'" So Allah, Glorified and Exalted, permitted the believers to think of each other of good and to say it, even if they were not certain of what was said. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
