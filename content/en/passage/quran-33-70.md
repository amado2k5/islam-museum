## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 70 to 71 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "O you who have believed, fear Allah and speak appropriate words": O you who have believed Allah and His Messenger, fear Allah lest you disobey Him, and thus deserve His punishment. And His saying: "And speak appropriate words" means: speak about the Messenger of Allah and the believers with intentional and unlawful words, true not false, as reported by Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "And speak sound words," meaning with sound judgment. From al-Kalbi: 'And speak sound speech,' he said: truthful. And from Qatadah, His saying: 'Fear Allah and speak sound words,' meaning just. Qatadah said: He means it in his logic and all his actions, and 'al-sadid' means truthfulness. From al-Hakam ibn Aban, from Ikrimah regarding the saying of Allah: 'And speak sound words,' say: There is no god but Allah. In the maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and al-Kalbi.
