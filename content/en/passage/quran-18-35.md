## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 36 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And he entered his Paradise while he wronged himself," he said, "I do not think this will ever perish": This is the one for whom We made two gardens from the grapes "He entered his Paradise," which is his garden, "while he wronged himself." He wronged himself: his disbelief in resurrection, his doubt about the Resurrection, and his forgetting the return to God Almighty, thus incurring God's wrath and painful punishment. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
