**Commentary on verse 68: "Say, People of the Book, you stand on nothing", p. 473**

Al-Tabari says: this is God's command to His prophet to tell the Jews and Christians who lived around Medina that they stand on nothing of what they claim from the religion of Moses and Jesus until they uphold the Torah, the Gospel and what was sent down to them from their Lord, namely what Muhammad brought of the Furqan. They must act on all of it, believe what it holds about believing in Muhammad and accepting him, and not separate between God's messengers, believing some and rejecting others, for rejecting one of them is rejecting all, since God's books confirm one another.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 473}

Report 12284, from Ibn Abbas, begins with a group of Medinan Jews, among them Rafi ibn Haritha, Sallam ibn Mishkam, Malik ibn al-Sayf and Rafi ibn Hurayma, who came to the Prophet and asked whether he did not claim to follow the creed and religion of Abraham.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 473}

We stop here at the foot of p. 473, at the start of report 12284 from Ibn Abbas on the coming of a group of Medinan Jews; the rest of verse 68 follows in the next batch. The pages of commentary end at p. 585, before the indexes beginning at p. 587.
