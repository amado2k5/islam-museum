## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "and when We gave Moses the Book and the Criterion" al-Tabari takes "the Book" as the Torah and "the Criterion" as the separation of truth from falsehood, reporting this from Abu al-Aliya, Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj. He also gives a report from Ibn Abbas that "furqan" is the common name of the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms and the Qur'an, and one from Ibn Zayd that it is like the "Day of the Criterion", the day of Badr, meaning the judgement by which God separated truth from falsehood; so He did between Moses and Pharaoh by victory. His own choice is the view of Ibn Abbas, Abu al-Aliya and Mujahid that the Criterion describes the Book, the Torah by which God separated truth from falsehood, since it is better joined to what immediately precedes it. "That you may be guided" means that you might be guided by it and follow the truth in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 70} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 71} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 72}
