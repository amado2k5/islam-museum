## Summary
Malik says: the practice among us is that among risk and uncertainty is buying what is in the bellies of female women and animals, because one does not know whether it will come out or not. If it comes out, one does not know whether it will be fine or ugly, complete or defective, male or female, and all of that varies. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.195}
