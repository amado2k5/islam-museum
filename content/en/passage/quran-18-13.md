## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "We have reduced to you their news in truth. Indeed, they were young men who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance." He explains: We have reduced to you the news of those youths who took refuge in the cave with truth, meaning: with undeniable truth and certainty. "Indeed, they were young men who believed in their Lord." He means: the youths who took refuge in the cave whom I asked you about the news of those who took refuge among the polytheists of your people, were young men who believed in their Lord. "And We increased them guidance," meaning: We increased them in their faith in their Lord and insight into their religion, until they endured the abandonment of their people's homes, fleeing from among the most outwardly gifted of their religion to Allah, and the separation from what they had been in of reduced and soft livelihood, to the harshness of staying in the mountain cave. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
