## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger of God said: God, have mercy on those who shave; they said: and on those who shorten, Messenger of God? He said: God, have mercy on those who shave; they said: and those who shorten? He said: and those who shorten. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.529}
