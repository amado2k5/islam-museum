## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 158 to 160 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they placed between him and Paradise a lineage, and indeed, Paradise has known that they are among those who will be present": These polytheists made a lineage between God and Paradise. Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the lineage that God informed them that they made for God Almighty. Some said: It means they said they said the enemies of God are brothers.
