**Commentary on verse 51 (pp. 357-358)**

On [[passage/quran-11-51|11:51]] al-Tabari reports Hud's saying to his people: my people, I ask you no wage or reward for what I call you to, pure worship of God, casting off idols and renouncing them; 'my wage is only upon Him who created me' means my reward and recompense for my counsel to you and my call to God is only upon Him who created me; 'will you not reason?' means will you not reason that if I sought by my calling you to God anything other than counsel for you and your good in the world and the next, I would have sought from you some of the world's goods for it and asked you for wage and reward? Report 18260, from Qatada: 'who created me'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 358}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
