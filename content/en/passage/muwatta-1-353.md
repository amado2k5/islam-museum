## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say that whoever has prayed the sunset or dawn prayer and then catches them with the imam should not repeat them, in the chapter on repeating prayer with the imam. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.194}
