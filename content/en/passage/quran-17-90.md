## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they said, 'We will not believe in you until you gush forth a spring for us from the earth': And He said, O Muhammad, the polytheists of Allah from your people are to you: We will not believe you until you gush forth for us from this land a spring that springs forth for us with water." And the phrase "Yanbu'" is derived from the saying: 'The spring of water' means when it appears and a mouse, it springs and springs, which is what springs. Also: According to Qatadah, His saying 'until a spring gushes forth for us from the earth' means until springs gush forth for us from the earth: meaning in this town of ours. Springs. From Mu'ammar, from Qatadah, similarly. He said: All from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid 'a spring' he said: 'Eyes.' He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. In the Maqta', he transmits statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibrahim.
