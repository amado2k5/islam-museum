## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said from Said ibn al-Musayyab that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: whoever twists his hair, plaits it, or stiffens it with gum, has become obliged to shave. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.533}
