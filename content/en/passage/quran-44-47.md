## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 48 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Take him and drive him to the worst of Hell": "Take him" means this sinful one with his Lord, who informed the Almighty that he has food from the Zaqqum tree, "so climb it." Allah Almighty says: "So drive it and drive it." It is said: 'atla' means to drive it by pushing and pulling, as in the saying of Al-Farazdaq: 'The noble do not have your own heels until you return to a gift that is driven by pushing and pulling.' And His saying: "to the level of Hell": to the middle of Hell. The meaning of the statement: On the Day of Resurrection, it will be said: Take this sinner and drive him away by pushing him into the middle of the fire. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
