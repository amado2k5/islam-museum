## In al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari takes God's words in the verse ([[passage/quran-2-228|2:228]]) on divorced women to mean those divorced after their husbands have consummated the marriage with them, if they have menstrual cycles and times of purity, that they wait for themselves, refraining from marrying again, three quru'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 499} The interpreters differed over the meaning of the word quru' that God meant. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 499}
