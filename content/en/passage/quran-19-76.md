## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah increases those who are guided as guidance, and the lasting righteous deeds are better with your Lord in reward and better in return." He explains: Allah increases those who follow the path of pilgrimage, are guided to the path of guidance, believe in their Lord, believe in His signs, act upon what He commanded, and refrained from what He forbade as guidance through renewed faith in the obligatory obligations imposed upon them. He acknowledges the obligation to oblige him and acts upon it. This is an increase from Allah in guiding him through His verses as guidance upon His guidance, similar to His saying: "And when a surah is revealed, among them is he, saying, 'Which of you has increased this in faith? As for those who believe, it has increased them in faith while they are rejoicing.'" Some interpreted this: 'And Allah increases guidance for those who are guided by the abrogated and abrogated Qur'an, so they believe in the abrogator, just as they believed before in the abrogated.
