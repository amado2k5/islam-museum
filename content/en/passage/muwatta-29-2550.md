## Summary
Malik said: the practice among us is that when a slain person is found among a people in a village or elsewhere, the nearest people to him in house or place are not held liable, because a slain person may be killed and then placed at the door of a people to smear them with it; no one should be held liable for such a thing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.443}
