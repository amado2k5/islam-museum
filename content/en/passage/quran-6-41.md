## Summary
Verse 41 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 41 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "no, it is him you call on, and he removes what you call on him for if he wills, and you forget what you associate" al-Tabari says, denying these who hold idols equal to him: you are not, polytheists who make gods and rivals partners to God, if God's punishment comes to you or the Hour comes to you,... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 353}

We stop here at the foot of p. 353, at the start of the commentary on "no, it is him you call on", at "if God's punishment comes to you or the Hour comes to you", which continues on p. 354.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
