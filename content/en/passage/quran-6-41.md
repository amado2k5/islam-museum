## Summary
Verse 41 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 41 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "no, it is him you call on, and he removes what you call on him for if he wills, and you forget what you associate" al-Tabari says, denying these who hold idols equal to him: you are not, polytheists who make gods and rivals partners to God, if God's punishment comes to you or the Hour comes to you,... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 353}

The rest: you would not seek shelter in anything but God in the hour of terror that comes on you, from gods, idols and images, but you would call there on your Lord who created you, cry to him for help and take refuge in him alone of all things; "and he removes what you call on him for" means he relieves you, when you cry to him and humble yourselves to him, of the great trial that has come on you, if he wills to relieve it, for he is able to do all things and owns all things, unlike what you call a god among idols and images; "and you forget what you associate" means that when God's punishment or the Hour with its terrors comes on you, you forget what you join with God in your worship of him, making it a rival to him, of idol, image and whatever else you worship besides him and call a god. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 354}

Verse 41 closes here at p. 354, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-42|6:42]].
