## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is their conveyance of knowledge. Indeed, your Lord is most knowing of who has strayed from His way, and He knows best who is guided": This is what those who do not believe in the Hereafter among the angels say about naming them by the female name, "their conveyor of knowledge." He says: They have no knowledge except this disbelief in Allah and associating partners with Him in a conjecture without certainty of knowledge. Ibn Zayd used to say about this: Ibn Zayd said regarding the verse: "So turn away from those who turn away from Our remembrance and desire nothing but the worldly life; that is the extent of their knowledge," he said: He means they have no knowledge except what they are involved in regarding disbelief in the Messenger of Allah and their plotting against what has come from Allah. He said: These are the people of polytheism. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd.
