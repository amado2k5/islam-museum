## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 9 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree” as meaning: We sent down this entire Quran in one go to the lowest heaven on the Night of Decree, which is the night of judgment when God decrees the year. This is derived from the expression, “God decreed this matter for me,” meaning He decrees. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Sufyan.
