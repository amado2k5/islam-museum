## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 86 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them except in truth. And indeed, the Hour is coming, so forgive is the Most Gracie pardon." He explains: We did not create all creation, its heaven and its earth, what is in them, and what is between them, meaning by saying: "and what is between them," what is in the dishes of that. "Except by the truth" means: except by justice and fairness, not by injustice and injustice. What Allah means by this is that He did not wrong any of the nations whose stories are cut in this surah and the stories of his destruction by hastening the punishment upon him for his disbelief in it, so He punishes and destroys him unjustly because He did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them unjustly and unjustly, but He created that with truth and justice. And His saying: "And indeed, the Hour is coming, so pardon with gracious forgiveness," Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: And the Hour, which is the Hour in which the Resurrection will be established, is a refuge. So be pleased with it for the polytheists of your people who denied you and returned to you what you brought them of the truth. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahak, and Sufyan.
