## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among His signs is that you see the earth humbled, but when We send down upon it the water, it shakes and swells. Indeed, He who gives it life to the erasure of the dead. Indeed, He is over all things competent." Among Allah's proofs and proofs is also His ability to disperse the dead after their affliction, and to restore them to their former form after their destruction. You see, O Muhammad, the earth scattered and dusty, with no plants to grow on it, as: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And among His signs is that you see the earth humbled," meaning dusty and shattered. And from Al-Suddi: "And among His signs is that you see the earth humbled," he said: dry and shattered. "So when We send down water upon it, it trembles." He says: "So when We send down rain from the sky upon this humble earth, it shakes with plants," meaning: it moved with it, as: he said: 'Warqa', all from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "It shook"—he said: with plants. "And it swelled"—he says: it swelled, as in Al-Suddi: "And it swelled." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
