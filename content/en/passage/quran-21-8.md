## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We did not make them bodies that did not eat food, nor were they everlasting": We did not make the messengers We sent before you, O Muhammad, to the nations before you, "bodies that do not eat food," meaning: We did not make them angels who did not eat food, but We made them bodies like you that eat food. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And We did not make them bodies that they did not eat food," he says: We did not make them bodies except that they might eat food. I narrated from Al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say regarding the verse: "And We did not make them bodies in which they do not eat food," he said: I did not make them bodies without souls in them that do not eat food, but We made them bodies in which souls eat food. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
