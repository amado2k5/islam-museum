## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban, and from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger forbade the touching sale and the throwing sale. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.196}
