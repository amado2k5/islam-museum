## Summary
Malik says: one should not buy a debt owed by an absent or present man, except with acknowledgement from the one who owes it, nor a debt owed by a dead man, even if the heir knows of it. Buying such a debt is uncertainty: one does not know whether it will be completed. The explanation of what is disliked is that if he buys a debt owed by an absent or a dead man, he does not know what debts the dead man may incur that were not known. If another debt befalls the dead man, the price the buyer paid is lost. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.207}
