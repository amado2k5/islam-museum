## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 31 as one section. He explains His saying, “So whoever seeks beyond that, then those are the transgressors”: Whoever seeks for his private parts a partner other than his wife, or his slave-girl, then those who do that are the transgressors, who have transgressed what God has made lawful for them to what He has forbidden for them, and they are the ones to be blamed.
