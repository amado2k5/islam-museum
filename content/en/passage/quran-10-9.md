**Commentary on verse 9 (pp. 27-29)**

He then explains "Those who believe and do righteous deeds, their Lord guides them by their faith; rivers flow beneath them in the gardens of delight". Those who believed God and His messenger and did righteous deeds, which is acting in obedience to God and keeping to His command, their Lord guides them, by their faith in Him, to Paradise. The editor refers to the gloss on "righteous deeds". Report 17558, from Qatada, who says it reached them that the Prophet of God said that when the believer comes out of his grave his deed is shaped for him in a fair form and says: I see you are a man of truth; he asks: who are you? and it answers: I am your deed, and it becomes a light and a leader for him to Paradise; as for the unbeliever, his deed is shaped for him in an ugly form and bearing, and he says: I see you are a man of evil; it answers: I am your deed, and it goes off with him until it puts him into the Fire. The editor notes that the printed text and the manuscript differ on the word for "bearing". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 27}

Reports 17559, 17560 and 17561, from Mujahid, on "their Lord guides them by their faith": it will be a light by which they walk. Report 17562 is the same from Mujahid, and Ibn Jurayj said that his deed is shaped for him in a fair form with a sweet scent, meets its companion and gives him good news of every good thing; he asks who it is and it says: I am your deed, and it becomes a light before him until it brings him into Paradise; the unbeliever's deed is shaped for him in an ugly form with a foul smell and clings to its companion until it throws him into the Fire. The editor explains the verb used for clinging as keeping close to, attaching to and sticking to. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 28}

Others said the meaning is that by their faith their Lord guides them to His religion, that is, He guided them by their belief; the list of those who said this then begins. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 28}

The editor notes that after the words "the list of those who said this" al-Tabari gives nothing, and that the margin of the manuscript has a word marking it ("thus"), a sign of an old omission. Then he explains "rivers flow beneath them": the rivers of Paradise run beneath these believers "in the gardens of delight", the delight with which God blessed the people who obeyed Him and believed in Him. The editor refers to the gloss on "gardens of delight" in the tenth volume. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 29}

He raises an objection: how is it said "beneath them" when elsewhere in the Qur'an God describes the rivers of Paradise as flowing beneath the gardens, and how can they flow beneath them unless the people are above the ground while rivers run under it, which is not how Paradise's rivers are described, since they run on the surface of the earth with no trenches? He answers that the meaning is that the rivers run in front of them, from lower ground, to what is before them, like "your Lord has placed beneath you a stream" in Surat Maryam, where it is known the stream was not placed under her while she sat upon it, the stream being a brook, but placed below her and before her; likewise Pharaoh's words, "these rivers flow beneath me", meaning before me. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 29}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
