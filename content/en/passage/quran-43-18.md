## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "or one who grows up in the ornament while in dispute is not clear": or one who grows in the ornament and adorns himself with it "while he is in dispute"—meaning: he is in dispute with someone who disputes him during the dispute but is not clear, from his opponent with evidence and argument, due to his weakness and weakness. You made him a part of Allah of His creation and claimed that he was his share among them. In the speech, it is left to suffice, and the indication of what was mentioned is what I mentioned. Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase: "Or one who grows up in adornment while the dispute is not clear," and some said: it refers to the concubines and women. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
