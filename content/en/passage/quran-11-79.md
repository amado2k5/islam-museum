**Commentary on verse 79 (pp. 417-418)**

On [[passage/quran-11-78|11:78]], 'is there not among you a right-minded man', al-Tabari says: is there no man of sound judgment among you to forbid whoever means to commit the shameful act against a guest and stand between them and it? Report 18384, from Ibn Ishaq: a man who knows the right and forbids what is wrong. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 417}

On [[passage/quran-11-79|11:79]] al-Tabari says: Lot's people said to Lot, you know, Lot, that we have no right in your daughters, since they are not our wives; report 18385, from Ibn Ishaq: that is, as wives. 'And you know what we want' means our need lies elsewhere than your daughters, and what we want is what you forbid us; report 18386, from al-Suddi: we want the men; report 18387, from Ibn Ishaq: our desire is for something else. When they would not desist, his words did not turn them back and they accepted nothing of what he offered about his daughters, he said 'if only I had strength against you or could take refuge in a strong support'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 417}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
