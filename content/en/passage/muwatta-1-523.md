## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Aisha, in the chapter on forbidding spitting toward the qibla: the Prophet saw spit, or mucus, or phlegm on the wall of the qibla and rubbed it off. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.270} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.271}
