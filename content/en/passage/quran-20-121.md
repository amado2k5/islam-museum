## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 121 to 122 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So they ate from it, and their evils became apparent to them, and they began to spread over them the leaves of Paradise, and Adam disobeyed his Lord and led astray": Adam and Eve ate from the tree they forbade eating, obeyed Iblis, and disobeyed their Lord's command, "and their faults became apparent to them." He means: so their faults were exposed to them, and they were hidden from their eyes, as in Al-Suddi. He said: He meant, meaning Iblis, by saying: "Shall I guide you to the tree of eternity and a king who does not wear out?" to reveal to them what had hidden from them of their evil deeds, by removing their clothes, and he knew that They were disgraced for what they were reading from the books of the angels, and Adam did not know this. Their clothing was nail-stained, so Adam refused to eat from it, so Eve came forward and ate and said: O Adam, eat, for I have eaten, but it did not harm me. When Adam ate, their evils became apparent to them. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
