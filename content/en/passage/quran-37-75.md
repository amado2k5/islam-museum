## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 77 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed Noah called to us, so how excellent are those who respond": Noah called us with his request for the destruction of his people, saying: "My Lord, indeed I called my people night and day, but My supplication only increased them to flee," up to His saying: "My Lord, leave no houses upon the earth among the disbelievers," and His saying: "How excellent are the responders," meaning: "How excellent are the responders." We were to him when he called us, and We answered his prayer, so We destroyed his people. "And We saved him and his family," meaning: the people of Noah who boarded the ark with him. We have mentioned them previously and explained the differences of scholars regarding their number. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
