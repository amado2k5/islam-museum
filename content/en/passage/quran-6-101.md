## Summary
Verse 101 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 101 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says: the God to whom these unbelievers attributed the jinn as partners and for whom they invented sons and daughters without knowledge is "the originator of the heavens and the earth", that is, the one who brought them into being after they were not (the editor refers back to the commentary on "bad'i"). Report 13693, from Ibn Zayd: He originated their creation and created them when they were nothing before Him. On "how can He have a child when He has no consort?" he says a child comes only from a male and a female, and God cannot have a consort so as to have a child; and since He created everything, when there is nothing but what God created, how could He have a child with no consort from whom it could be? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 11}

On "He created everything and He knows everything", he says God created all things and there is no creator besides Him; everything you claim, you who set up the idols beside God, whether what you call a lord and say has a child, or a jinn or a human, is His creation and His servant by ownership. Nothing of what He created is hidden from Him, and not an atom's weight escapes Him; He knows your number and your deeds and the deeds of those you called a lord or made a child of God, and He counts them against you and against them until He repays each according to his work. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 12}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
