## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads this verse along with the one before it, saying that the 'abode of destruction' in the first verse is the abode of destruction, then Allah describes it by saying, 'Hell they will burn, and wretched is the place of resolve.' That is, wretched is the settled place; it is Hell for those who pray it. It is mentioned that their 'people' are the polytheists of Quraysh, who brought down that abode by disbelief and denial of the Prophet, after his mission had been a blessing upon them. He transmits narrations that call the two intended ones: from Umar ibn al-Khattab: they are the 'dawn' of Quraysh, the Banu al-Mughira and the Umayyads. As for the Banu al-Mughira, Allah cut off their dwellings on the day of Badr, and as for the Umayyads, they enjoyed it for a while; and from Ali the same, and in another narration from him: the two dawns from Banu Asad and Banu Makhzum. He lists the chains of transmission to these statements as is his custom.
