## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 218 to 222 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "If they disobey you, then say, 'Indeed, I am innocent of what you do.'" It is: If you disobey your closest relatives whom you were commanded to warn about, and refuse to remain in idol worship and associating partners with the Most Merciful, say to them: "Indeed, I am disassociated from what you do" from idol worship and disobedience to the Creator of mankind. "And rely on the Almighty" in His punishment from His enemies, the "Most Merciful," through those who turn to Him in repentance and repent from their sins. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
