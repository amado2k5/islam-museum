## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Umar ibn al-Khattab used to say: O God, I ask You for martyrdom in Your path and death in the city of Your messenger. The editor notes that its chain is cut off, since Zayd ibn Aslam did not meet Umar, and that al-Bukhari connected it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.595}
