## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "the revelation of the Mighty, the Most Merciful": The reciters differed in reading His phrase: "The Revelation of the Mighty and the Merciful." The majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "the revelation of the Aziz" with the nominative nominative of revelation. The nominative approach in this is in two ways: one is to make it a predicate, so the meaning of the speech is that it is the revelation of the Mighty, the Merciful. The other is with the beginning, so the meaning of the speech then is: You are among the messengers. This is the revelation of the Mighty, the Most Merciful. Most reciters of Kufa and some people of the Levant read it as "Tanzil" in the accusative case from His saying: "Indeed, you are among the messengers," because the sending is only about the revelation, so it seems it was said: for the revelation of the Almighty, the Most Merciful, truly. The correct view in my view is that they are two well-known readings among the reciters of the regions, with similar meanings. Whichever one the reciter reads, the correct one is correct.
