## Summary
Yahya said Malik said, reporting from Jamil ibn Abd al-Rahman the muezzin, that he would attend Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz as he judged between people. When a man came claiming a right against another, he examined it: if there was dealing or contact between them, he made the accused swear; if there was no such thing, he did not make him swear. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.268}
