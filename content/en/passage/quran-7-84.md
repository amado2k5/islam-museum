## Summary
Verse 84 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 84 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-84|7:84]], al-Tabari says: We rained on the people of Lot, who denied him and did not believe in him, a rain of stones of baked clay with which We destroyed them. "See how the end of the wrongdoers was": see, Muhammad, the end of those of Lot's people who denied God and His messenger, who committed God's disobediences, ran to foul deeds and made lawful what God forbade, how it was and what it came to; was it anything but ruin and destruction? That, or the like of it in punishment, is the end of those of your own people who deny you and are too proud to believe in God and to confirm you, if they do not repent. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 553}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
