**Commentary on verse 162 (p. 179)**

Al-Tabari says that those among them who disbelieved in God changed the words God commanded them to say, and in place of "this is a hittah" they said the equivalent of "a grain of wheat in a barley grain". What they said was other than what they were told to say. "So We sent upon them a punishment from heaven" means He sent a chastisement that destroyed them because they kept altering what they were commanded, doing the opposite of what God ordered and saying other than what He ordered them to say. He refers to his earlier commentary on the parallel verse and to his explanation of the meaning of "rijz". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 179}
