## Summary
The first report in the comprehensive chapter on funeral prayer. A report from Malik that it reached him that Uthman ibn Affan, Abdullah ibn Umar and Abu Hurayra used to pray over the dead in Medina, men and women, placing the men nearest the imam and the women nearest the qibla. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (968). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.315}
