## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We responded to him and saved him from the landing, and thus We save the believers": "So We answered" Jonah's prayer to Us, when he called us in the belly of the whale and saved him from the distress he was in by imprisoning him in the belly of the whale and saddened him with his sin and guilt. "Thus do We save the believers." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: Just as We saved Jonah from the distress of being trapped in the belly of the whale in the sea when he called us, so too do We save the believers from their distress when they seek help from Us and call upon us.
