## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, that he heard al-Qasim ibn Muhammad say: a man came to Ibn Abbas and said: I have an orphan who has camels; may I drink from the milk of his camels? Ibn Abbas said: if you seek the lost of his camels, and tend their mange, and clean their trough, and water them on the day they come to water, then drink, without harming the offspring and without overmilking. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.523}
