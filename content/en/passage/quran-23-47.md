## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 48 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'We believe in two human beings like us, and their people are worshippers of us.'" Pharaoh and his followers said: "We believe in two human beings like us," so we follow them, and their people, from the Children of Israel, "worshippers to us," meaning they are obedient to them, submissive to them, obedient to their commands and submissive to them. The Arabs call anyone who submits to the king a worshiper. Among these, the people of al-Hirah were called the servants, because they were obedient to the non-Arab kings. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
