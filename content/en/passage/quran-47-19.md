## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So know that there is no deity except Allah, and ask forgiveness for your sins and for the believing men and believing women. And Allah knows your changes and your resting place." So know, O Muhammad, that there is no deity for whom divinity should be deserved or suitable for you, and it is permissible for you and creation to worship Him except Allah, who is the Creator of creation and the Owner of everything. He owes to Him by Lordship. "And ask forgiveness for your sins." And ask your Lord for forgiveness for your past and their sins, and for the sins of the people of faith in You, men and women. "And Allah knows your changes and your abode," meaning: Indeed, Allah knows how you will deal with your actions while you are awake, and your abode when you lie down in your bed to sleep at night. Nothing of this is hidden from Him, and He will reward you for all of that.
