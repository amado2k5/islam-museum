## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He will forgive you your sins and admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow and good dwellings in the Gardens of Eden. That is the great attainment." He means: "Your Lord will cover your sins for you if you do that, then He will pardon and pardon you." "And admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow," meaning: "And admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow," and "good dwellings." He means: He will also admit you to good dwellings "in the Gardens of Eden," meaning gardens of residence, from which they do not abandon them. And His saying, "that great victory," means: that great salvation from the punishment and horrors of the Hereafter.
