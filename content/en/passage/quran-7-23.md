## Summary
Verse 23 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 23 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "They said: our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we shall be among the losers", he says: this is a report from God of Adam and Eve's answer to Him, their confession of sin against themselves and their asking His forgiveness and mercy, unlike the cursed Iblis's answer to Him. The meaning of "our Lord, we have wronged ourselves" is that Adam and Eve said to their Lord, our Lord, we have done wrong to ourselves by sinning against You and going against Your command, and by obeying our enemy and Yours in what we had no right to obey him in, eating the tree You forbade us to eat; "if You do not forgive us" means if You do not veil our sin and cover it for us, leaving our exposure by punishing us for it; "and have mercy on us" by Your compassion toward us and Your not taking us to account for it; "we shall be among the losers" means among the perished. Al-Tabari says he explained the meaning of "loser" with evidence earlier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 357}

He gives (14411) from Qatada that Adam said, Lord, what if I repent and seek Your forgiveness? He said, then I will bring you back into Paradise; Iblis did not ask for repentance but for respite, and each was given what he asked; and (14412) from al-Dahhak that "our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us" are the words Adam received from his Lord. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 357}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
