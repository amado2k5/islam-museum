## Al-Tabari's commentary

Or did you think, O Muhammad, that the companions of the cave and the rainbow were among Our signs marvelous? For what You created of the heavens and the earth and all the wonders in them is more wondrous than the matter of the People of the Cave. My argument for all this is firm against these polytheists from your people and others from My servants. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
