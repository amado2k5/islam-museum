## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if We had made it a foreign Qur'an, they would have said, 'Why had its verses not been explained in detail? It is a foreign and Arabic verse. Say, 'It is for those who believe as guidance and healing; and for those who do not believe, in their ears is deafness, while it is upon them blindness.' Those are calling from a distant place." If We had made this Qur'an which We revealed to Muhammad, a foreign word, your people from Quraysh would say: "Had We not explained its verses in detail," meaning: Would You not clarify its proofs and what is in it, so that we could understand it and know what it is and what is in it? 'Ajamiya,' meaning they used to say in denial of it: Is this Quran foreign and the tongue upon which an Arabic was revealed? In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-Hasan, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
