## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 40 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Woe on that Day to the deniers,” as: Woe on that Day to those who deny God’s word concerning these people, and what He will do to them on the Day of Resurrection. And His saying: “This is the Day of Judgment. We have gathered you and the former peoples.” God Almighty says to these deniers of the Resurrection on the Day they are resurrected: This is the Day of Judgment on which God will judge with truth between His servants. “We have gathered you and the former peoples.” He says: We have gathered you on it for your appointed time which We promised you in this world, the gathering on it between you and all those who were before you from the perished nations. We have fulfilled that for you. “So if you have a plan, then plan against Me.” He says: God will fulfill for you what He promised you in this world of punishment for your denial of Him that you will be resurrected for this Day. If you have a trick to devise to escape His punishment on this Day, then devise one.
