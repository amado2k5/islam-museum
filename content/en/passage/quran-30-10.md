## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Then the end of those who did evil was that they denied the signs of Allah and mocked them": Then was the last matter of those who disbelieved among those who raised the earth and built it, and their messengers brought them clear proofs about Allah, and they denied their messengers, so they wronged their actions. Al-Sawa': means the disbelief that is worse than what they did. As for this world, destruction and destruction, and in the Hereafter, the Fire from which they will not be taken out nor do they take responsibility. In the passage, statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah are quoted.
