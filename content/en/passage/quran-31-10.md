## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He created the heavens without pillars that you see, and cast upon the earth firm pillars to support you, and spread therein from every mount, and We sent down from the sky water and caused therein to grow from every noble pair": and among His wisdom is that He "created the seven heavens" "without pillars you see." I have previously mentioned the difference among interpreters regarding the meaning of the phrase "without pillars you see them," and clarified the correct view on this with us. And it is narrated from Imran ibn Hudayr, from Ikrimah, from Ibn Abbas: "You see them without pillars" he said: Perhaps they are with pillars that you do not see. And from Hamad ibn Salamah, from Humayd, from al-Hasan ibn Muslim, from Mujahid, who said: They are pillars you do not see. And from Sharik, from Samak, from Ikrimah, from Ibn Abbas, who said: Perhaps they are pillars that you do not see. From Samak, from Ikrimah in this letter: "He created the heavens without pillars you see," he said: You see them without pillars, and they are pillars. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, and Samak.
