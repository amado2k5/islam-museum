## Summary
Malik transmits from Ziyad ibn Saad, from Amr ibn Muslim, from Tawus al-Yamani, that he said: I met people of the Companions of the Messenger of God who said: everything is by decree. Tawus heard Abdullah ibn Umar say: the Messenger of God said: everything is by decree, even incapacity and cleverness, or cleverness and incapacity. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.480}
