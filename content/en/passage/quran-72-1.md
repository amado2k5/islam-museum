## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 3 as one section. He explains His saying, “Say, ‘It has been revealed to me that a group of jinn listened and said, “Indeed, we have heard a wondrous Qur’an.”’” He says, may His praise be exalted, to His Prophet Muhammad: Say, O Muhammad, God has revealed to me that “a group of jinn listened” to this Qur’an “and said” to their people when they heard it, “Indeed, we have heard a wondrous Qur’an. It guides to the right path,” meaning it indicates the truth and the way of righteousness, “so we have believed in it,” meaning we have affirmed it, “and we will never associate with our Lord anyone” of His creation. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Sufyan.
