## Summary
A saying of Malik: I heard that one who kills game in the sanctuary while not in ihram is judged by the same ruling as one in ihram who kills game in the sanctuary while in ihram. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1157 and 1158) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.478}
