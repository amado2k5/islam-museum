## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say of sacrificial animals and camels: the one in its second year and above. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1212), Suwayd (525) and al-Bayhaqi transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.511}
