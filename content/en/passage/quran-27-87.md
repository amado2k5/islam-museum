## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And on the Day when the trumpet will be blown, whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth will be terrified except whom Allah wills, and all will come to it humbly": The interpreters differed in the interpretation of the phrase: "And the day the trumpet will be blown." We have mentioned their differences previously, and we have clarified the correct view on this matter with its evidences, except that we mention here some reports that were not mentioned there. Some said: It is a horn that is blown into it. Some of those who were not mentioned before mentioned in the report about this said: All of them, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid regarding the phrase: "And the day the trumpet is blown," he said like the form of a trumpet. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid.
