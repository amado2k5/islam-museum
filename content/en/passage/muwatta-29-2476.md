## Summary
Malik said he heard Ibn Shihab say: it was the Sunna that when a man wounds his wife, he owes the blood-money of that wound, and he is not subject to retaliation. Malik said: this applies to accidental acts, where a man strikes his wife and his blow hurts her without his intending it, such as striking her with a whip and putting out her eye. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.422}
