## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We have distributed it among them that they may be reminded, but most people refused except as ungrateful." And We have distributed this water which We sent down from the sky as purity to revive the dead from the earth among My servants, so that they may remember My favors upon them and thank My favors to them and My kindness to them. "But most people refused except ungrateful," meaning: except as a form of worship to bestow favors upon them and to grant them blessings. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
