## Summary
A report of Jarir ibn Abdullah al-Bajali in the chapter urging charity and the reward of whoever starts a good practice in it, in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim. It is set as the Prophet's sermon when barefoot men of Mudar came, and people followed with charity. {cite:sahih-muslim}
