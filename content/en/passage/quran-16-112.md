## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah sets forth an example of a town that was secure and secure, its provision coming to it abundantly from every place, but it denied the favors of Allah, so Allah made it taste the garment of hunger and fear because of what they used to do": Allah Almighty says: And the parable of Allah is the example of Mecca, whose inhabitants were people of polytheism with Allah, which was the town that was safe and secure. Its security was that the Arabs would hostile and kill and enslave each other, while the people of Mecca neither zealed nor fought in their own land, so that was their security. And His saying: 'Mutna' means: a settlement with its people, whose people do not need a settlement as the desert dwellers did. 'Its provision comes to it in abundance,' meaning: its people come to their livelihoods and abundance and abundance. And His saying: 'from every place' means: from every part of this village and from every side within it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
