## Summary
Verse 87 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 87 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-87|7:87]], al-Tabari means by "and if a party of you believed" a group and faction of you who believed what I was sent with, pure worship of God and giving up disobedience, wronging people and cheating them in measures and weights, and followed me in it; "and a party did not believe" is another group that did not affirm it or follow me; "so be patient until God judges between us" means wait on God's decision that separates us and you; "and He is the best of judges" is the most just who decides, because there is no leaning toward anyone in His judgment and no favouritism. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 561}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
