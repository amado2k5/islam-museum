## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger and fears Allah and fears Allah and fears Him - those are the winners": "And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger" in what He commands and forbids, submits to their judgment for him and upon him, fears the consequences of disobeying Allah and warns Him, and fears Allah's punishment by obeying Him in His command and prohibition. "So those are the ones who do that, the ones who will be pleased with Allah on the Day of Resurrection and will be safe from His punishment."
