## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And what prevented the people from believing when guidance came to them and asking forgiveness of their Lord except that the way of the former came to them or the punishment came to them beforehand": The Almighty says: "And what prevented these polytheists, O Muhammad, from believing in Allah when guidance came to them as the clear explanation of Allah, and they knew the truth and reality of what you call them to, and seeking forgiveness for what they are committed to from their polytheism, except that they came to us as a Sunnah among nations like them from nations whose messengers denied before them, or that punishment came to them beforehand." Interpreters differed in interpreting this; some said: Its meaning is: or punishment will come to them suddenly. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
