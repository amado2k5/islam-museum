## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God saw a man driving a camel for sacrifice, and said: ride it. The man said: Messenger of God, it is a sacrificial camel. He said: ride it, woe to you, on the second or third time. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1203), Ishaq al-Tabba and Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari (2755) and others transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.508}
