**Commentary on verse 36 (pp. 473-474)**

On "those to whom We gave the Book rejoice in what has been sent down to you, but among the factions are some who deny part of it. Say: I am only commanded to worship God and not to associate anything with Him; to Him I call and to Him is my return", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: those to whom We sent down the Book, of those who believed in you and followed you, Muhammad, rejoice in what was sent down to you of it; "but among the factions are some who deny part of it", that is, among the people of the communities banded against you, who are people of various religions, are some who deny part of what was sent down to you; say to them: I am only commanded, people, to worship God alone to the exclusion of all else, "and not to associate anything with Him", making a partner for Him in my worship and worshipping gods and idols with Him, but to devote religion to Him as a hanif, a submitter; "to Him I call", that is, to obeying Him and devoting worship to Him I call people; "and to Him is my return", that is, my destination, "ma'ab" being the "maf'al" form from "aba ya'ubu awban wa ma'aban". The interpreters said the same: report 20454, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "those to whom We gave the Book rejoice in what has been sent down to you", those are Muhammad's Companions, who rejoiced in God's Book and His Messenger and believed in it; and "but among the factions are some who deny part of it" means the Jews and the Christians. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 473}

Report 20455, from Mujahid, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Shababa, Waraqa and Ibn Abi Najih: "but among the factions are some who deny part of it", he said, of the People of the Book. Report 20456, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Abd Allah and Waraqa: the same. Report 20457, from Mujahid, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "those to whom We gave the Book rejoice in what has been sent down to you, but among the factions are some who deny part of it", of the People of the Book; "the factions" are the people of the Books whom their banding together draws close, and he cited "and if the factions come" [[passage/quran-33-20|33:20]], for their banding against the Prophet; Ibn Jurayj said, and he said from Mujahid, "deny part of it", he said, part of the Qur'an. Report 20458, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: "and to Him is my return", to Him is the destination of every servant. Report 20459, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: "those to whom We gave the Book rejoice in what has been sent down to you", he said, this is whoever of the People of the Book believed in the Messenger of God, and they rejoice in that; and he recited "some of them believe in it and some of them do not" [[passage/quran-10-40|10:40]]; and of "but among the factions are some who deny part of it", he said, "the factions" are the nations, the Jews, the Christians and the Magians, some of whom believed in it and some of whom denied it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 474}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
