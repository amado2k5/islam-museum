## Summary
Verse 171 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 171 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 171 (pp. 398-399, report 8232).** Al-Tabari explains the gladness at God's blessing and favour as what He honoured them with on coming before Him, and the favour and ample reward He gave for their earlier obedience to God and His Messenger and their fighting His enemies. Ibn Ishaq (8232), through Ibn Humayd from Salama, says it is what they saw of the fulfilment of the promise and the greatness of the reward; the note places it in Ibn Hisham's Sira (3: 126), completing the reports ending at 8229. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 398}

**The reciters differ on the last "anna" (pp. 398-399).** Some read it with fatha, linked to what precedes: they rejoice at a blessing and favour and that God does not nullify the reward of the believers. Others read it with kasra as a fresh sentence, arguing that in the reading of 'Abdallah it comes without the particle (with the conjunction and the divine name instead), which they take as proof that it is separate from what precedes; the note refers to al-Farra' (1: 247). Al-Tabari glosses the non-wasting as that He does not void the recompense for the deeds of one who believed His messenger, followed him and acted on what came from God. His own choice (p. 399) is the fatha reading, because of the consensus of the authoritative body of reciters. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 398} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 399}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 399}
