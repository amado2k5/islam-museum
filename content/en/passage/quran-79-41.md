## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 41 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “then Paradise is the abode” as meaning that Paradise is his dwelling place and home on the Day of Resurrection. We have already mentioned the interpretations of the scholars regarding the meaning of the phrase “and for him who fears the standing before his Lord” in previous sections, which makes it unnecessary to repeat them here. In this section, he quotes statements attributed to Mujahid.
