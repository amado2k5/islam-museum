## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He is the One who sends down rain after they have despaired and spreads His mercy, and He is the Praiseworthy Protector": And Allah is the One who sends down rain from the sky and provides you with it, O people, "after they have despaired," meaning: after despairing of its descent and coming, and "spreads His mercy," meaning spreads His mercy among His creation, and by mercy it means the rain that He sends down from the sky. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
