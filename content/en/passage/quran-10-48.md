**Commentary on verse 48 (pp. 99-100)**

On [[passage/quran-10-48|10:48]] he says to His Prophet: these polytheists of your people, Muhammad, ask when this promise will be, which you tell us will come from God, meaning the coming of the Hour, if you and those who follow you are truthful in what you threaten us with. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 99}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
