## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "From it We created you, and into it We will return you, and from it We will bring you forth again": From the earth We created you, O people, and We created you as speaking bodies, "and into it We will return you," meaning: And on the earth We will return you after your death, and We will make you dust, just as you were before you were created as humans, "And from it We will bring you out," meaning: And from the earth We bring you out as you were alive before your death, and We will raise you up from it, just as We created you the first time. And His saying: 'another time' means: again, as in Qatadah, 'and from it We will bring you out another time,' he says: again. Ibn Zayd said, regarding 'another time,' he said: 'Once again,' he said: 'Once again,' the other creation. Abu Ja'far said: The interpretation is: We brought you out of the earth and you were not a proper creation, and We will bring you out of it after your death again, just as We brought you out of it the first time. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
