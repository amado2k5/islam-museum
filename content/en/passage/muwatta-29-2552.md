## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Umar ibn al-Khattab killed a group, five or seven, for one man whom they killed by secret murder (ghila). Umar said: if the people of Sanaa had conspired together against him, I would have killed them all. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.443}
