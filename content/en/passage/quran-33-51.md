## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "You hope for whom you wish among them and you shelter whom you will, and whoever you seek from among those you have been removed, there is no blame on you. That is more deserved that their eyes be pleased and not grieve, and that they be content with what You have given them. And Allah knows what is in your hearts, and Allah is Knowing and Forbearing." The interpreters differed in the interpretation of the phrase: "You hope for whom you will among them and you shelter whom you will." Some said: What he meant by His saying: You hope: It delays, and by saying: it shelters: it is enclosed. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
