## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on God giving the wrongdoer respite, in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. It is respite followed by a seizure from which there is no escape, linked to God's words, "Such is the seizure of your Lord when He seizes the towns while they are wrongdoing". {cite:sahih-muslim}
