## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 59 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The command to obey God and the Messenger (p. 495, report 9851, note 3).** Al-Tabari says the address is to the believers to obey their Lord in what He commanded and forbade, and to obey His messenger Muhammad, since obeying him is obeying their Lord, for they obey him on God's command to obey him. Ibn Humayd (9851, from Jarir, from al-A'mash, from Abu Salih) reports from Abu Hurayra that the Prophet said: whoever obeys me has obeyed God, whoever obeys my commander has obeyed me, whoever disobeys me has disobeyed God, and whoever disobeys my commander has disobeyed me. The editor notes that Ahmad gave it in the Musnad many times by different routes, that the two Sahih compilers and others gave it, and that Ibn Kathir calls it agreed upon as sound, and it is as he says. Al-Tabari then says the interpreters differed over the sense of the command to obey. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 495}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 495 in the commentary on verse 59 of sura 4, at the opening of the interpreters' disagreement over the sense of the command to obey, and continue from p. 496. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 495}
