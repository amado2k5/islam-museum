## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation was included in the interpretation of verses 158–160. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among interpreters regarding the lineage that the polytheists made between God and Paradise: Some said it is their saying that God and Iblis are brothers, and Paradise has known that they are among the Present. Glory be to God above what they describe except the sincere servants of God.
