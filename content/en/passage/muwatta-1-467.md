## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say that whoever forgets a prayer and does not remember it until he is with the imam, when the imam gives salam should pray the forgotten prayer, then pray the other after it. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Hafs ibn Ghiyath in Ibn Abi Shayba, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Abu Amir al-Aqdi in al-Tahawi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.239} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.240}
