## Summary
He said: if the master of a contract slave contracts his slave for goods of camels, cattle, sheep or slaves, it is valid for the buyer to buy them for gold, silver, or other goods that differ from what he was contracted for, and to pay at once and not delay. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.354}
