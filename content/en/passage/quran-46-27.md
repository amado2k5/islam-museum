## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 28 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And We destroyed the towns around you and turned away the verses that perhaps they might return": God Almighty warns the disbelievers of Quraysh of His might and dominance to befall them in their disbelief. "And indeed We destroyed," O people, the towns around your town, like the stone of Thamud, the land of Sodom, Marib, and the like. We warned their people with examples, and destroyed their homes, leaving them empty on their thrones. And His saying: "And We diverted the verses" means: We admonished them with various admonitions, reminded them of various forms of remembrance and arguments, and explained that to them. Also: Ibn Zayd said regarding the verses: "And We diverted the verses," he explained them "perhaps they will return," meaning to return from what they were established in disbelief in God and His signs. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
