**Commentary on verse 19 (pp. 47-48)**

He then explains "People were only one community, then they differed; and had it not been for a word that preceded from your Lord, it would have been decided between them in what they differ about". People were only one religion and one way, then they differed in their religion and the ways parted with them; "had it not been for a word that preceded from your Lord" means had it not been foreordained by God that He destroys no people except after their term has run out, "it would have been decided between them" by destroying the people of falsehood among them and saving the people of truth. He says he has set out how those who differ understood this in Surat al-Baqara, on "People were one community, so God sent the prophets", with the right view and its evidence, so he need not repeat it; the editor refers to the fourth volume and to the gloss on "decided". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 47}

Reports 17589, 17590 and 17591, from Mujahid, on "People were only one community, then they differed": when one of the two sons of Adam killed his brother. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 48}
