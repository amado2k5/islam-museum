## Summary
Verse 49 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 49 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "those who deny our signs, punishment will touch them for their transgression" al-Tabari says: as for those who denied those of our messengers to whom we sent them, opposed our command and prohibition and resisted our proof, our torment and punishment will reach them for their denial of the proofs they denied, "for their transgression", that is, for their lying. Ibn Zayd used to say: every "fisq" in the Qur'an means lying; this is 13251 from him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 370}

The editor refers for "touching" to p. 287 and for "transgression" to p. 206 and reports 12983 and 12103, and notes on p. 370 that a part of the old division from which his manuscript was copied ends here, with the closing: the saying on the interpretation of "say: I do not say to you that I have the treasures of God, nor do I know the unseen, nor do I say to you that I am an angel; I follow only what is revealed to me; say: are the blind and the seeing equal? Will you not reflect?" follows; blessing upon Muhammad the prophet and his family, then: in the name of God, the compassionate, the merciful, my Lord help.

Verse 49 closes here at p. 370, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-50|6:50]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
