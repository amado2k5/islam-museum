## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Did He not guide for them how many generations We destroyed before them, walking in their dwellings? Indeed, in that are signs for those who admonish": Did He not guide your people who associated others with Allah? The meaning of guidance is to clarify. He says: Has He not made clear to them the abundance of what We destroyed before them of the nations before them, who walk in their dwellings and homes, and see the consequences of Our punishments that We have imposed upon them as the severe consequences of their disbelief in Our signs? They take heed, take heed, and call upon submission, and believe in Allah and His Messenger, fearing that their disbelief in Allah might befall them as it befell them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
