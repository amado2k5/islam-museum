## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "To Him belongs the keys of the heavens and the earth; He extends provision for whom He wills and decrees it. Indeed, He is of all things Knowing": He means His mention by saying: "To Him belongs the keys of the treasuries of the heavens and the earth, and in His hand are the chains and keys of good and evil. Whatever mercy is opened, no one can hold it, and whatever He holds is not sent to it after it." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
