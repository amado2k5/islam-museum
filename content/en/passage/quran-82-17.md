## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 19 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And what will make you know what the Day of Judgment is?”: “And what will make you know, O Muhammad, what will make you aware of what the Day of Judgment is?” He says: “What is the Day of Reckoning and Recompense?” He then exalts the matter, may His name be glorified, by saying this. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
