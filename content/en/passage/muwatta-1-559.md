## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, that Humayd told him that Surat al-Ikhlas is a third of the Qur'an, and that Surat al-Mulk pleads for its reader. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and that the copy and al-Tamhid say "equal to a third". Ibn Abd al-Barr said such a thing cannot be said by opinion, and must come by directive, and that it is raised from the Prophet by many channels, including from Anas, Ibn Abbas, Uqba ibn Amr, Abu Hurayra and Umm Kulthum bint Uqba (al-Tamhid). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.287}
