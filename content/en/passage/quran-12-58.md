**Commentary on verse 58 (pp. 153-154)**

On "and Joseph's brothers came and entered upon him; he recognised them, but they did not know him", al-Tabari says: Joseph knew them while they did not know him. The cause of their coming to Joseph, as he was told, is in report 19463, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: when Joseph was settled in his rule and out of the affliction he had been in, and the fertile years in which he had told them to prepare for the years he said were coming had passed, the people were in hardship everywhere and made for Egypt seeking provisions from every town. When Joseph saw the hardship that had struck the people he treated them alike, giving a man only one camel-load and never two to a single man, apportioning fairly between people and easing matters for them; his brothers came among those who came seeking provisions, and he knew them while they did not know him, because God willed to bring Joseph to what He willed. The editor notes differences between the printed text and the manuscript in some words, and glosses "asa baynahum" as treating them equally and "taqsit" as apportioning. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 153}

Report 19464, from al-Suddi, through Ibn Waki', Amr and Asbat: hunger struck the people until it reached the land of Ya'qub, who sent his sons to Egypt and kept back Joseph's brother Benjamin. When they came in to Joseph he knew them and they did not know him. Looking at them he said: tell me your affair, for I find your condition strange. They said: we are people from the land of al-Sham. He said: what brought you? They said: we came to buy food. He said: you lie, you are spies; how many are you? They said: ten. He said: you are ten thousand, each of you a commander of a thousand; tell me your news. They said: we are brothers, sons of a truthful man; we were twelve, and our father loved a brother of ours who went with us into the wilderness and was lost to us there, and he was the dearest of us to our father. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 153}

He said: and to whom did your father turn after him? They said: to a brother of ours younger than he. He said: how do you tell me your father is truthful when he loves the younger of you and not the elder? Bring me this brother of yours so that I can look at him; if you do not bring him, you have no measure with me and you shall not come near. They said: we will seek him from his father, and we will do it. He said: then put some of you as a pledge until you return; and they left Shim'un. Report 19465, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: "and they did not know him" means they did not recognise him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 154}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
