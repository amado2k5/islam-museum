## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains the verse: "They said, 'Did your messengers not come to you with clear proofs?' They said, 'Yes, they said, so call upon them. And the supplication of the disbelievers is only in error.'" The keeper of Hell said to them: "Or did your messengers not come to you in this world with clear proofs for the oneness of Allah, so that you may unify Him, believe in Him, and disown what is besides Him among the gods?" They said: Yes, our messengers have brought us that. And His saying: "They said, 'So call upon them,' He, the Exalted, says: The treasure said to them: 'So call upon the permission of your Lord, whom the messengers have brought you with the supplication to believe in Him.' And His saying: 'And the supplication of the disbelievers is only in error,' it means: They have called upon them, and their supplication is only in error, because it is a prayer that does not benefit them nor is it answered. Rather, they are told: 'Be disgraceful in it and do not speak.'
