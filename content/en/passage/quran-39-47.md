## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if those who wronged had whatever is on earth all together and the like of it with it, they would have been redeemed by it from the worst punishment on the Day of Resurrection, and it would appear to them from Allah what they did not expect." If only those polytheists with Allah on the Day of Resurrection, and they were the ones who wronged themselves, would have "whatever is on the earth" in this world of its wealth and adornments (and the like of it with Him) multiplied, and accepted that from them as a recompense of themselves, thus redeeming themselves all in exchange, so that they might be saved from the evil punishment of Allah, which He will torment them with that Day: "And it will appear to them from Allah" means: And on that day appeared to them the command and punishment of Allah which He had prepared for them, which they had not previously expected to have prepared for them.
