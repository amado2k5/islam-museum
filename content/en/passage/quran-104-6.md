## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 14 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "Woe to every hamza with a hint of a hint": He means His mention by saying: "Woe to every hamza," meaning the valley flows from the pus and pus of the people of Hell. "For every hamza": He says: to every one who backbites people, backbites them, and angers them, as Ziyad al-Ajam said: "You will recite with my pleasure if you meet me lying, and if I am absent, you are the hamz al-lamzah." By al-lamzah, he means the one who criticizes people and stabs them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Rabi', and Sufyan.
