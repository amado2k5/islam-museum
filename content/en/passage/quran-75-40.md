## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37-40 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase, “Is not He Who created man from a sperm, then a clot, until he became a complete human being with sons and daughters, able to resurrect the dead from their death, bringing them back to life as they were before their passing?” He says: It is known that He Who was able to create man from a sperm, until he became a complete human being, is not incapable of resurrecting the dead after their death. And when the Messenger of God recited this, he would say, “Yes.” And it was narrated from Qatadah, regarding the phrase, “Is not He Who created man from a sperm, then a clot, until he became a complete human being,” that when the Prophet of God recited it, he would say, “Glory be to You, and yes.” The passage quotes statements from Qatada.
