## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Call upon those whom you claim besides Allah do not possess the weight of an atom's weight in the heavens or on the earth, and they have no share in them, nor does He have any backing from them.'" This is what We did to us and those who obeyed us, David and Solomon, upon whom We bestowed upon them unsatisfactory blessings when they thanked us. And that We did to the insults of those We did to them, when they boasted of Our favor, denied Our messengers, and denied Our hands. So say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists among your people who deny Our favors to them: Pray, O people whom you claim to have a partner besides Allah, ask them to do to you some of Our deeds, those whose affairs We have described as 'An'am or Despair.' If they are unable to do so, then know that you are false, because associating partners in Lordship is neither valid nor permissible. Then He described those who invoke besides Allah, saying: They do not possess even an atom's weight in the heavens or the earth of good, evil, harm, or benefit. So how can a god be one who is like that? In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
