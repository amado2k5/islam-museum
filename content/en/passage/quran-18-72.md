## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 72 to 73 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Did I not say that you will not be able to have patience with me?': The Almighty says: 'He said' The scholar said to Moses when he said to him, 'Did I not say that you will not be able to have patience with me?' regarding what you see of my actions, because you see what you have not been able to contain. Moses said to him: 'Do not hold me accountable for what I forgot.' The interpreters differed on the meaning of this. Some said: This statement from Moses to the scholar was opposition, not that he had forgotten his covenant or what he had previously said when he accompanied him, saying: "If you follow me, do not ask me about anything until I mention it to you." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
