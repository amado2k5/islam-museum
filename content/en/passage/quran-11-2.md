**Commentary on verse 2 (p. 228)**

On [[passage/quran-11-2|11:2]] al-Tabari says: then they were set out so that you worship none but God alone, without partner, and cast off gods and rivals. Then God tells His prophet: say, Muhammad, to the people, I am to you from God a warner, warning you of His punishment for disobedience and worship of idols, and a bearer of glad tidings, giving you news of the great reward for obeying Him and for pure worship and divinity to Him. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'warner' and 'bearer of glad tidings'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 228}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
