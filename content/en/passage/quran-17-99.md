## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have they not seen that Allah, who created the heavens and the earth, is able to create the like of them and assign for them a term of no doubt, but the wrongdoers refused except as disbelievers": Or did these polytheists not look at them with the eyes of their hearts, knowing that Allah who created the heavens and the earth, created them from nothing, and established them by His power, is capable by that ability to create their likes and likes after their destruction and before that, and that Whoever is able to do so cannot prevent them from being recreated anew, after they have become bones and remains. And His saying, "And He appointed for them a term in which there is no doubt," means Allah, the Exalted, saying: And Allah has appointed for these polytheists a term for their destruction and a time for their punishment, about which there is no doubt. He says: There is no doubt that He has come to them that term.
