## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah glorifies whoever is in the heavens and the earth and the birds, each knowing His prayer and glorification? And Allah is Knowing of what they do": Have you not looked, O Muhammad, with the eye of your heart and know that Allah prays for Him from whoever is in the heavens and the earth, whether angels, mankind, or jinn? "And the birds are also suffocating" in the air, glorifying Him. "Everyone who knows his prayer and glorification" means glorifying Him as prayer, so it is said: It was said: Prayer is for the children of Adam and glorification is for other creatures, so he separated between them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
