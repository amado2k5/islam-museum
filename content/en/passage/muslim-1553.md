## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on the merit of planting, in the Book of Irrigation of Sahih Muslim. Jabir reports similar texts, including the hadith of Umm Mubashshir al-Ansariyya. It makes sowing and planting an ongoing charity whose benefit reaches birds and animals. {cite:sahih-muslim}
