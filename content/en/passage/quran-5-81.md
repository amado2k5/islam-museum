**Commentary on verse 81: "Had they believed in God and the Prophet and what was sent down", pp. 497-498**

Al-Tabari says: if those of the Children of Israel who take the unbelievers as allies believed God, acknowledged Him and affirmed His oneness, accepted that His prophet Muhammad was a sent prophet and acknowledged what was sent down to him of the verses of the Furqan, they would not have taken them as companions and supporters instead of the believers; but many of them are disobedient, that is, they leave obedience to God for disobedience and treat as permitted what God forbade them in word and deed. Report 12314, from Mujahid, says that those meant are the hypocrites.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 498}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
