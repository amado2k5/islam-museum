**Commentary on verse 117 (p. 530)**

On [[passage/quran-11-117|11:117]] al-Tabari says: your Lord, Muhammad, would not destroy the towns He destroyed, whose story He told you, unjustly while their people were doing right in their deeds and not wrongdoing, so that destroying them while they did right and obeyed their Lord would be injustice; rather He destroyed them for their people's disbelief in God, persisting in their error, denying their messengers and committing evil deeds. It was also said that the sense is that He would not destroy them for their polytheism, 'unjustly' meaning polytheism, 'while their people were doing right' among themselves, not wronging one another but dealing in truth between them, though they were polytheists, and He destroys them only when they wrong one another. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 530}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
