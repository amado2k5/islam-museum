## Summary
Verse 110 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 110 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "when God said: Jesus son of Mary, remember my favour to you and to your mother, when I strengthened you with the holy spirit" al-Tabari says: beware the day God gathers the messengers and asks what answer your peoples gave you in the world, when God said to Jesus what he said; "when" is tied to "you were answered", as if it meant: what answer did the peoples to whom Jesus was sent give Jesus. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 212}

If one asks how the messengers were asked about the peoples' answer in Jesus' time, when there were few messengers then, the answer is that God may have meant by "what answer were you given" the messengers sent in Jesus' time, so the report came in the form of all of them while those of Jesus' time are meant, as in [[passage/quran-3-173|3:173]], where one man is meant though the wording is of all the people. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 213}

The editor prefers the manuscript's "few of that" and drops the "from" that the printed text added, and refers to earlier pages of volume 7.

The sense is "when God said", at the time he said "Jesus son of Mary, remember my favour to you and to your mother, when I strengthened you with the holy spirit", that is, remember my kindnesses to you and your mother in strengthening and helping you with the holy spirit. The grammarians differed on the verb "ayyadtuka": some said it is the form fa''altuka from "ayd" as you say "qawwaytuka" from "quwwa"; others that it is fa'altuka from "ayd"; and a reading is reported from Mujahid, "a'yadtuka", of the pattern af'altuka from strength. "With the holy spirit" means Gabriel, that is, when I helped you with Gabriel; he explained that and what "holiness" means earlier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 213}

The editor notes that this detail on "ayyadtuka" is one his earlier explanation in volume 2 omitted, which is one of his ways of abridging the commentary and shows his method of composing it; he refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an and to the earlier explanations of "favour", "ayd" and "holy spirit".

He then explains "you speak to people in the cradle and as a grown man; when I taught you the book, the wisdom, the Torah and the Gospel; when you made from clay the shape of a bird by my leave": God reports what he said to Jesus, remember my favour to you and your mother when I strengthened you with the holy spirit, while you spoke to people in the cradle and as a grown man; that is, God strengthened him with the holy spirit as an infant in the cradle and as an adult, "grown man" being joined to "in the cradle" because the sense of that is "as a child", as in [[passage/quran-10-12|10:12]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 214}

Then "when I taught you the book, the wisdom, the Torah and the Gospel": remember also my favour when I taught you the book, that is writing, and the wisdom, which is understanding the meanings of the book I sent you, the Gospel; "when you made from clay the shape of a bird" is the form of a bird, "make" meaning work and shape, "by my leave", my help in that and knowledge from me of it; "you breathe into it" into the form, which becomes a bird by my leave; "you heal the blind" who sees nothing, whose sight is effaced, "and the leper by my leave". He explained the meanings of these words earlier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 215}

The editor notes that at the dots there is a blank in the manuscript with the letter "ta" in the margin marking a faulty place, which he kept, and thinks al-Tabari's wording ran as he supplies; he refers to the earlier explanations in volumes 6 and 10 of "cradle", "grown man", "book", "wisdom", "healed", "blind" and "leave", and says "create" and "shape" in this sense and "leper" were not explained earlier.

"And when I held back the children of Israel from you when you brought them the clear signs": remember also my favour in holding back the children of Israel from you when they had resolved to kill you, when you brought them the proofs and wonders showing your prophethood and the truth of what I sent you with; "those of them who disbelieved said": those who denied your prophethood and called you a liar among the children of Israel, "this is nothing but plain magic". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 216}

The readers differed. The readers of Medina and some of Basra read "sihr mubin", plain magic, that is, it shows whoever sees it that it is magic with no reality; most of the readers of Kufa read "sahir mubin", meaning "this", that is Jesus, "is nothing but a plain magician", showing by his acts and the wonders he brings that he is a magician and not a true prophet. Al-Tabari holds that these are two known readings, both sound in sense and in agreement, for whoever is described with the act of magic is a magician, and whoever is described as a magician... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 216}

The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "holding back", "the clear signs" and "plain", and keeps "the truth of what I sent you" from the manuscript.

We stop here at the foot of p. 216, in the middle of al-Tabari's argument that the readings "sihr" and "sahir" agree in sense, at "and whoever is described as a magician", which continues on p. 217.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
