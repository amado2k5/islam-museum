## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: whoever relieves a believer of a worldly distress, God relieves him of a distress on the Day of Resurrection; whoever eases one in hardship, God eases him; whoever covers a Muslim, God covers him; and whoever seeks knowledge, God makes the way to Paradise easy for him. Ibn Rajab notes the chain disputes in the report of Al-A'mash. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
