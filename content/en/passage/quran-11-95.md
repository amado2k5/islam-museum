**Commentary on verse 95 (pp. 464-465)**

On [[passage/quran-11-95|11:95]], 'as though they had never lived there', al-Tabari says: as though Shu'ayb's people, whom God destroyed with His punishment, had not lived in their homes before they lay there, nor dwelt in them, from the saying 'I lived in such a place' for dwelling in it; he cites a line of al-Nabigha, and the editor refers to its explanation earlier. Reports 18528, from Ibn Abbas, and 18529 and 18530, from Qatada: as though they had never lived in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 465}

As for 'away with Midian, as Thamud were put away', it means God put Midian far from His mercy by bringing His vengeance on them, as Thamud before them were put far from His mercy by the coming of His wrath. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 465}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
