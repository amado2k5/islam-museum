## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah will admit those who believe and do righteous deeds into gardens beneath which rivers flow. Indeed, Allah does what He wills": Indeed, Allah admits those who are truthful to Allah and His Messenger, who do what Allah has commanded them in this world, and refrained from what He forbade them. In them, there are "gardens" meaning gardens, "beneath which rivers flow," meaning: rivers flow beneath their trees. "Indeed, Allah does what He wills," so He grants whatever He wills of His honor to those who obey Him, and whatever He wills of humiliation to those who disobey Him.
