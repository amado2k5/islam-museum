## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 69 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And cast down what is in your right hand and seize what they have done; they have only devised the plot of a magician, and the magician does not succeed wherever he comes." And His statement: "They only made the plot of a magician," the readers differed in their interpretation of his statement. The general reciters of Medina and Basra, and some reciters of Kufa, read it as "They only made the plot of a magician" with the nominative 'kayd' and with the alif in 'sahir' meaning: what these magicians made was a plot by a magician. The general recitation of Kufa read: "They only made a magical plot" by raising the scheming and without the alif in magic, meaning that what they did was a magical scheme.
