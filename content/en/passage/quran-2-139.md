## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "say: do you dispute with us about God, when He is our Lord and your Lord, and ours are our deeds and yours your deeds, and to Him we are sincere" al-Tabari says: say, Muhammad, to the Jews and Christians who said to you and your companions "be Jews or Christians and you will be guided", claiming their religion is better than yours and their book better than your book since it came before it, and that for this reason they are nearer to God than you: do you dispute with us about God, who is our Lord and yours, in whose hand is the good, to whom belong reward, punishment and recompense for deeds, and claim you are nearer to God than we because your prophet is before ours and your book before ours, while each side has what it did and earned and is repaid for it, not for lineage or the antiquity of a religion and book. "Do you dispute with us" means do you quarrel and argue with us, from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd and Ibn Abbas. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 120} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 121}

"And to Him we are sincere" means sincere in worship and obedience, associating nothing with Him, as the idol-worshippers worshipped idols with Him and the people of the calf the calf. Al-Tabari sees in this God's rebuke to the Jews and an argument for the believers, so they should say to the Jews and Christians: do you dispute with us about God's religion, when our Lord and yours is one, just, never unjust, and repays His servants only for what they earned; you claim to be nearer to God for the antiquity of your religion, book and prophet, while we are sincere in worship to Him and associate nothing with Him, but you have associated partners in worshipping Him, some of you worshipping the calf and some the Messiah, so how are you nearer to Him than we? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 121} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 122}
