## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 27 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Stop them; they are to be held accountable": "Stop them": "Restrain them": that is, imprison, O angels, these polytheists who wronged themselves and their spouses, and did not worship besides Allah of the gods, "Indeed, they will be held accountable." The interpreters differed on the meaning that Allah commands them to be stopped because of their questioning. Some said: He asks them whether they like the arrival of the Fire. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
