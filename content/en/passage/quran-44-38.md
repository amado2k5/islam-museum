## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 39 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them as plays": "And We did not create the heavens" — the seven heavens, the two earths, and what is between them as play. And His saying: "We did not create them except in truth" means: We did not create the heavens and the earth except by truth, which is not the best of management. What Allah means by this is to remind us of the validity of resurrection and reward. He says: We did not create creation in vain by telling them and giving them life as We willed, then annihilating them without testing them by obedience, command, and prohibition, nor by recompensing the obedient for obedience and the disobedient for disobedience. Rather, We created that to test whom We will, of Our creation with what We will, of His trial of command and prohibition: "We will surely recompense those who have done evil for what they have done, and We will reward those who do good with good."
