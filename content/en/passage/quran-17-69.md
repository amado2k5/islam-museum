## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Or do you believe that He will return you therein another time, sending upon you a torrent of the wind and drowning you for what you disbelieved, and then you will find no way to sell it against you": Or do you, O people, believe, from your Lord, when you disbelieved in Him after He bestowed His favor upon you? The blessing you know will return you to the sea another time: he says: again, and the 'ha' in the phrase "in it" is from the mention of the sea. Also: And from Qatadah, "to bring you back to it again": meaning at sea again, "and He sends upon you a storm of wind," which is what blows what it has passed, shattering and striking it. From their saying: "So-and-so bombed the back of so-and-so": when he breaks it, "He will drown you because of what you disbelieved," meaning: God will drown you with this wind that blows because of what you disbelieved. He means: by your disbelief in it. "Then you will find no merchant against us for it," meaning: then you will find no follower over us who will follow us for what we have done to you, nor a vengeful vengeance for our destruction of you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
