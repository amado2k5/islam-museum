## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on combining two prayers at home and on a journey: when the rulers combined the sunset and night prayers in rain, Abdullah ibn Umar combined them with them. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.208}
