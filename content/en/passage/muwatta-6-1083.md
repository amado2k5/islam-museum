## Summary
A saying of Malik: if a man were ignorant that the last of his time at the House should be the circumambulation until he had departed, I would not hold anything against him, unless he was close, in which case he returns and circumambulates the House and then departs once he has departed. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1446) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.497}
