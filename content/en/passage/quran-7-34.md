## Summary
Verse 34 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 34 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "for every community there is a term; when their term comes, they cannot delay it by an hour nor advance it", God says, as a threat to the idolaters of whom He reported that when they committed an indecency they said "we found our fathers on it and God commanded us to", and a warning of what He holds for them for their lying about Him, their persistence in shirk and their staying in disbelief, reminding them of what He inflicted on their likes among the nations before them: "for every community there is a term", that is, every group that united in denying God's messengers, rejecting their counsel and associating partners with God, despite their following of their Lord's proofs against them, has a time for punishments to descend on them and exemplary penalties to fall on them for their shirk; "when their term comes" means when the time God set for their destruction and the descent of punishment on them comes, "they cannot delay it by an hour nor advance it" means they cannot be delayed by remaining in the world, nor be allowed to enjoy life in it beyond the time of their destruction and the hour their end comes, by an hour of time, "nor advance it" means nor advance it before the time God set for their destruction. The note says the printed text has "threatening" where the manuscript has what al-Tabari wrote. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 405}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
