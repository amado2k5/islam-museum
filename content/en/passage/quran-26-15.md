## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 15 to 17 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that God restrained Moses by saying, 'No,' meaning Pharaoh's people will not kill you, and He commanded him and his brother to take His signs and proofs, and that God is with them listening to what they say and responding, and that they come to Pharaoh and say, 'He sent the Children of Israel with us.'
