## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He is Allah, besides whom there is no deity, the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Most Merciful, the Most Merciful": The One whose fear causes the mountain to crack, O people, is the deity to whom worship and divinity are deserved, the Knower of the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and the witness of what is seen and perceived in them. "He is the Most Merciful, the Most Merciful," meaning: He is the Most Merciful in this world and the Hereafter, merciful to those who believe in Him.
