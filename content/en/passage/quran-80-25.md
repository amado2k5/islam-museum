## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 30 as a single section. He explains the phrase “that We poured down water in abundance”: The majority of reciters in Medina and Basra read it with a kasra on the alif of “that,” as a new beginning, while the majority of reciters in Kufa read it with a fatha on the alif, meaning: “Let man look at ‘that,’” placing “that” in the genitive case, with the intention of repeating the preposition. It is also permissible for it to be in the nominative case if it is pronounced with a fatha, with the intention of “His food, that We poured down water in abundance.” In my opinion, the correct view is that both are well-known readings, and whichever reading the reciter uses is correct. His statement, “that We poured down water in abundance,” means: We sent down rain from the sky in abundance, and poured it down upon it in abundance. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Sufyan.
