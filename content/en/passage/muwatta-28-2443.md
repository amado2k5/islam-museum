## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that he was asked about the prescribed punishment of a slave for wine. He said: I was told that he is owed half the prescribed punishment for a free man for wine, and that Umar ibn al-Khattab, Uthman ibn Affan and Abdullah ibn Umar flogged their slaves half the prescribed punishment for a free man for wine. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.409}
