**Commentary on verse 115 (pp. 536-537)**

Al-Tabari says: God would not judge you as astray, for asking forgiveness for your dead polytheists, after He gave you guidance and enabled you to believe in Him and His Messenger, until He has told you in advance to refrain and you then fail to refrain. Before He made clear His dislike of it by forbidding it, He does not pronounce you astray, since obedience and disobedience belong to one who is commanded or forbidden, and one not commanded or forbidden is neither obedient nor disobedient in what was not commanded or forbidden. "God knows all things" means He knows what stirred in your hearts when He forbade you to ask forgiveness for your dead polytheists, your distress over what you had done before He told you, and the hidden matters of your affairs, so He showed you His forbearance in order to lift the weight of grief from you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 536}

Report 17419 is from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: God's making clear to the believers is about asking forgiveness for polytheists in particular, and in His making clear obedience and disobedience in general: do or leave. Reports 17420 to 17422 give the same from him through several chains. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 537}
