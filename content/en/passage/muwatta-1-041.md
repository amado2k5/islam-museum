## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, in the chapter on the ablution of one who sleeps when he rises for prayer: Umar ibn al-Khattab said that whoever sleeps lying down must perform ablution. The editor notes that Zayd ibn al-Hubab transmitted it from Malik in Ibn Abi Shayba, and Suwayd ibn Sa'id and Abd al-Razzaq in his Musannaf. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.54}
