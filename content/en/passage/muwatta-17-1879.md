## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that he told him that Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Aswad ibn Abd Yaghuth ran out of fodder for his animal, and he said to his slave: take food from your family's wheat and buy barley with it, and take only the same measure. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.173}
