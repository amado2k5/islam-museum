## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "These are two adversaries who disputed concerning their Lord, so as for those who disbelieve, garments of fire will be cut off from their heads, with hot meat pouring over their heads": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of these two disputants mentioned by Allah. Some said: One group is the people of faith, and the other group is the idol worshippers from the polytheists of Quraysh who fled on the Day of Badr. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ata.
