**Commentary on verse 14 (pp. 433-434)**

Then al-Tabari says on "that, so taste it, and for the unbelievers is the punishment of the Fire" [[passage/quran-8-14|8:14]]: this punishment I hastened for you, unbelievers who oppose God and His Messenger, in this world, of striking above the necks and striking every fingertip, at the hands of my friends the believers, so taste it now, and know that you have in the later and the return the punishment of the Fire. For the "anna" in "and that for the unbelievers" there are two grammatical forms: the nominative and the accusative. The nominative is in the sense of "that, so taste it, and that, and for the unbelievers is the punishment of the Fire", with the intention of repeating "that"; the accusative is in two ways: one, "that, so taste it, and know or be sure that for the unbelievers", its accusative being by the intention of an unexpressed verb, and he cites a line of a poet describing how his wife saw her husband in battle girded with a sword and a spear, meaning "and carrying a spear"; the other, "that, so taste it, and because for the unbelievers is the punishment of the Fire", the preposition then dropped and the accusative resulting. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "tasting" and to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 433} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 434}
