**Commentary on verse 86: "Those who disbelieved and denied Our signs are the people of Hell", p. 513**

Al-Tabari says: as for those who denied God's oneness, rejected Muhammad's prophethood and denied the signs of His book, they are the people of the blazing Fire, meaning its inhabitants who remain in it; the blaze is the fiercest heat of the fire.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 513}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
