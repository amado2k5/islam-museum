## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if they do not respond to you, then know that they only follow their own desires. And who is more astray than one who follows his desires without guidance from Allah? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people": If those who say the Torah and the Gospel are magical and pretentious, claiming that the truth lies with others, from the Jews, O Muhammad, until they bring you a book from Allah that is more guided than them, then know that they only follow their desires, and that what they speak and say in the two books is falsehood and falsehood, with no truth. Perhaps someone who says it will say: Or did the Prophet not know that what the Jews and others said about the Torah and the Gospel of falsehood and falsehood, called so-called magic, is false in speech unless they do not respond to it until they bring them a book that is more gifted than them?
