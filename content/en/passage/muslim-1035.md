## Summary
A report of Hakim ibn Hizam in the chapter on the prohibition of asking people in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim. Wealth taken with a willing soul is blessed, and wealth taken with greed is like eating without being satisfied. The Prophet gave him three times and then forbade him to ask. {cite:sahih-muslim}
