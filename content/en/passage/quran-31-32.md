## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when waves like shadows overshadow them, they call upon Allah, sincerely devoting religion to Him. But when He saves them to the land, among them is one who is moderate. And none denies Our signs except every chosen one who disbelieves." And when those who invoke gods and idols besides Allah overflow the sea when they board the ship, waves like a shadow, which is the plural of a canopy, likened to waves in the intensity of the blackness of the water. Nabigha Bani Ja'da said about the description of the sea: They walk green, with shades on its edges, like the splitting of the danan, and the wave-like shape, which is one of the shadows, and it is a plural, because waves come from one thing after another, and some of them join each other like the shape of shadows. And His saying: "Invite Allah, sincere to Him in religion," Allah says: And when these people are overwhelmed by waves like shadows, they fear drowning, so rush to Allah by supplicating sincerely to Him, devoted to Him in obedience, associating nothing with Him, calling upon no one besides Him, and seeking no help from anyone else. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
