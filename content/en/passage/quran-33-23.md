## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 24 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Among the believers are men who have been true to their covenant with Allah; among them are those who have fulfilled their vow, and among them are those who await, and they did not alter their vows in the least": Allah Almighty says: "Among the believers" in Allah and His Messenger, "men who have fulfilled their covenant with Allah," meaning: They fulfilled what they promised them to be patient in the face of hardship and adversity, and when hardship occurs, "among them are those who have fulfilled their vow," He says: Among them are those who have completed the duty that Allah had vowed and made obligatory upon themselves, and some were martyred on the Day of Badr, some on the Day of Uhud, and some in other matters The citizen, "and among them are those who await" his decree and completion of it, just as those who have passed have fulfilled their covenant with God, received victory from God, and triumphed over their enemy. And 'al-nahb' means vows in Arabic speech. In the maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Ishaq, and Sufyan.
