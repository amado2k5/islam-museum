## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abu Said al-Khudri, that the Messenger said: do not sell gold for gold except equal for equal, and do not give one more than the other; do not sell silver for silver except equal for equal, and do not give one more than the other; and do not sell any of them absent for present. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.157}
