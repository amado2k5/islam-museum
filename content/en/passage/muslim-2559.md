## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik from al-Zuhri in the chapter on the prohibition of envy, hatred and turning away from one another, in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. It is the charter of relations among Muslims in four prohibitions and one command. {cite:sahih-muslim}
