## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever disbelieves, it is upon him to disbelieve; and whoever does righteousness, then for themselves [they will be prepared]": Whoever disbelieves in Allah, it is upon him, or increases his disbelief and sins of his denial, the blessings of his Lord. "And whoever does righteousness," he means: whoever obeys Allah, acts according to what He commanded in this world, and refrains from what He forbids in it, "then their souls will be prepared." He means: for themselves, they prepare and stretch out their beds to be safe from the punishment of their Lord and to be saved from His punishment, as the poet said: Prepare for yourself, for illness and destruction have come, and do not waste a soul that has no successor. In the passage, sayings about Mujahid are quoted.
