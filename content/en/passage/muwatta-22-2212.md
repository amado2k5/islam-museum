## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Aisha, wife of the Prophet, that a man said to the Messenger: my mother died suddenly, and I think if she had spoken she would have given charity; should I give charity on her behalf? The Messenger said: yes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.306}
