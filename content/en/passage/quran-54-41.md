## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed, the people of Pharaoh brought the warning": And indeed, the followers of Pharaoh and his people came to warn us of punishment for their disbelief in us and our messenger Moses. "They denied all Our signs." He, Exalted be He, says: The people of Pharaoh denied Our proofs that came to them from Us, and Our proofs that came to them that there is no god but Allah alone, all of them. "So We seized them with a seizure of power, mighty and powerful." God Almighty says: So We punished them for their disbelief in God with a severe punishment that cannot be overcome, capable of whatever He wills, not powerless nor weak. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
