## Summary
Verse 48 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 48 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "we send the messengers only as bringers of good news and warners; whoever believes and acts rightly, no fear on them, nor shall they grieve" al-Tabari says: we send our messengers only with the good news of Paradise and the clear triumph on the day of resurrection for those who obey us, as our reward to them for obeying us, and with the warning, to those who disobey us and oppose our command, of our punishing them for their disobedience on the day of resurrection, as our recompense for it, so that we give them an excuse and whoever perishes perishes on a clear proof; "whoever believes and acts rightly" means whoever trusts the warnings of those of our messengers we sent to him, accepts from them what they brought from God and does good in the world, "no fear on them" on coming to their Lord, of his punishment and torment that God prepared for his enemies and the people of disobedience to him, "nor shall they grieve" then for what they left behind in the world. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 369}

The editor refers for "good news", "warner" and the like of this verse to earlier volumes 9, 1, 2 and 7.

Verse 48 closes here at p. 369, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-49|6:49]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
