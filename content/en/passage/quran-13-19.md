**Commentary on verse 19 (p. 418)**

On "is he who knows that what has been sent down to you from your Lord is the truth like one who is blind? Only people of understanding remember", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: is this one who knows that what God sent down to you, Muhammad, is the truth, so believes in it, affirms it and acts by what is in it, like the one who is blind, who does not recognise where God's proof lies against him in it and does not know what God has bound him to of His obligations? The interpreters said the same: report 20329, from Qatada, through Ishaq, Hisham, Amr and Sa'id: these are a people who benefited from what they heard of God's Book, understood it and retained it; God said "like one who is blind", he said, blind to good so that he does not see it. On "only people of understanding remember" he says: only those with minds take admonition from God's signs and learn from them; they are the "albab", the singular of which is "lubb". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 418}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
