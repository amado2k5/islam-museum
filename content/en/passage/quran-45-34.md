## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And it was said, 'Today We will forget you as you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours, and your refuge is the Fire, and you will have no helpers.'" And it was said to those disbelievers whose attributes were described: Today We will leave you in the torment of Hell, just as you abandoned your work to meet your Lord this Day. As he said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "And it was said, Today We will forget you," meaning we will leave you. And His saying: "And your refuge is the Fire," it means: and your refuge is the Fire of Hell, "And you have no helpers," it means: And you have no helper to save you today from the punishment of God, nor any helper to help you from the one who torments you and delivers you from it. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
