## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We caused the earth to swallow with him and his house, and he had no group to support him besides Allah, nor was he among the victors": So We caused Qarun and his people to swallow his household. It is said: and his household, because it was mentioned that when Moses commanded the earth to take him, he commanded him to take him, and he took those who were with him from among his companions in his house, and they were sitting with him, and they were like the hypocrisy and support he was in to harm Moses. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
