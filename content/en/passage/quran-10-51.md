**Commentary on verse 51 (p. 101)**

On [[passage/quran-10-51|10:51]] he says: is it then, when God's punishment has fallen on you, polytheists, that you believe in it, meaning accept it at a time when acceptance does not help you? And it is said to you then: now you believe, when before you were hurrying it while denying it would come down? Taste now what you used to deny. The sense of the particle here is "is it there", and it is not the one that means "then" as a connective. The editor notes that al-Qurtubi says in his commentary that it is a particle of place meaning "there", which is al-Tabari's view, and that Abu Hayyan said that what al-Tabari said is a claim. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 101}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
