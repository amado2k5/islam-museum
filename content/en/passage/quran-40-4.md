## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 5 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "None disputes about the verses of Allah except those who disbelieve, so let not their turning in the lands deceive you": no one disputes God's proofs and evidence for His oneness by denying them, except those who deny His oneness. And His saying: "So let not their wandering in the lands deceive you," He says: So let not their management of the lands, their stay, and stay in them deceive you, while disbelieving in their Lord. You think they were only granted respite and turned around, so they acted in the lands while disbelieving in Allah, and did not hasten the punishment and punishment for their disbelief because they were on some part of the truth. We did not give them respite for that, but so that the Scripture would reach its appointed time and the word of punishment would come upon them, the punishment of your Lord, as in Qatadah: "So let not their wandering in the lands deceive you," their travels there, their comings and goings. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
