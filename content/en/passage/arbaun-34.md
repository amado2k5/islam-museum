## Summary
Hadith of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri: "Whoever sees an evil among you, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, with his tongue; if he cannot, with his heart, and that is the weakest of faith". Ibn Rajab explains the stages of changing wrong according to ability, and the link to the report about the sermon on the Eid. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
