## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17-19 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase "crystal goblets of silver, precisely measured" as follows: "The 'goblets' are of the purest silver, the silver being of the purest white." He cites Abu Raja' as saying: Al-Hasan said, regarding the verse "They were crystal goblets of silver," that the purity of the goblets lies in the whiteness of the silver. He also cites Abu Raja' on the authority of Al-Hasan, regarding God's statement "crystal goblets of silver," that the whiteness of the silver is in the purity of the goblets. Ibn Abi Khalid informed us, on the authority of Abu Salih, regarding the verse "They were crystal goblets of silver," that its dust was of silver. And regarding the phrase "crystal goblets of silver," he said: The purity of the glass lies in the whiteness of the silver. Regarding Qatadah's interpretation of the verse, "Crystal goblets, crystal goblets of silver," he said: "If the people of falsehood needed to make a silver vessel whose contents could be seen from behind, just as what is in crystal goblets can be seen, they would not be able to do so." The passage also includes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
