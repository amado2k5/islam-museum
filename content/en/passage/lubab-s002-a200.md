## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Baqara 2:200, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Companion generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «فإذا قضيتم».
