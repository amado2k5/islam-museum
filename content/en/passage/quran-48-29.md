## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains the description of the Prophet and his companions in his saying: Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, and those with him are severe against the disbelievers, merciful among themselves. You see them bowing and prostrating, seeking bounty from Allah and His pleasure, which is their mark on their faces from the effect of prostration.
