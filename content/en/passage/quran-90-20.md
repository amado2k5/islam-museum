## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “upon them will be a fire closed in” as meaning that the fire of Hell will be completely sealed upon them on the Day of Resurrection. The word “mustabakah” (closed in) is derived from this root. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
