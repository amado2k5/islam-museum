## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 2 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "O mankind, fear your Lord; indeed, the shaking of the Hour is a great thing": Abu Ja'far said: Allah Almighty says: O people, beware of the punishment of your Lord by obeying Him, so obey Him and do not disobey Him, for the punishment of those who punish Him on the Day of Resurrection is severe. Then the Exalted described the horror of the signs of that day and its manifestation, saying: "Indeed, the shaking of the Hour is a great thing." Scholars differ regarding the time when the earthquake, which He, the Exalted, described as severe; some said: It exists in this world before the Day of Resurrection. In the passage, he quotes from Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and Ata.
