## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Dinar that he saw Abdullah ibn Umar urinate standing, in the chapter on urinating standing and otherwise. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Ibn Wahb transmitted it from Malik, in al-Bayhaqi, and Ibn Abi Shayba by another channel from Abdullah al-Rumi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.110}
