## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the call while travelling and without ablution. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi': Abdullah ibn Umar gave the call to prayer on a cold, windy night and then said, "Pray in your camp dwellings", and reported that the Prophet ordered the caller on cold rainy nights to say this. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.121}
