## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Read your books; it is sufficient for yourself today as a Reckoner over you": "And We will produce for him on the Day of Resurrection a book that he will receive scattered," and it will be said to him: "Read your book; it is sufficient for yourself today as a Reckoner." He omitted mentioning the phrase: so we say to him, suffice with the indication of the speech on it. And by His saying: "Read your book," he means: read the book of your deeds that you have done in this world, which our scribes used to write, and we will count it upon you. "Sufficient is yourself today as a reckoner over you," meaning: Today your soul is sufficient against you as a reckoner to account your deeds, and he will count them against you. We seek no witness other than these, nor do we ask you to account for anything else. And from Qatadah: "Read your book; it is sufficient for yourself today as a reckoner." On that day, he will read it by those who were not a reader in this world. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
