## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Amr ibn Sad ibn Muadh, from his grandmother, that the Messenger of God said: O women of the believers, let none of you despise her neighbour even if it is a burnt sheep's hoof. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.520}
