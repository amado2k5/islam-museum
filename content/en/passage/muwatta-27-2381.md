## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Ubayd Allah ibn Abdallah ibn Utba, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, that he said: I heard Umar ibn al-Khattab say: stoning is a right over whoever commits adultery, men and women, if they are married, when witnesses testify, or there is pregnancy, or confession. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.384}
