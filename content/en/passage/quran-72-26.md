## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 26 to 29 as one section. He explains His saying, “Say, ‘I do not know whether what you are promised is near or whether my Lord will set for it a longer period’”: God Almighty says to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists of your people: I do not know whether what your Lord promises you of punishment and the Day of Judgment is near, “or whether my Lord will set for it a longer period” meaning: a known end date whose duration is long. His statement, “He is the Knower of the unseen, and He does not disclose His unseen knowledge to anyone except to a messenger whom He has chosen,” means that He knows what is hidden from the sight of His creation, and they have not seen it. He does not disclose His unseen knowledge to anyone, teaching it or showing it to them, except to a messenger whom He has chosen, and He reveals it to him as He wills. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ad-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
