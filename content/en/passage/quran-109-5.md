## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 10 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, O disbelievers": God Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, and the polytheists among His people were offered to worship Allah for a Sunnah, on the condition that the Prophet of Allah worship their gods for a Sunnah. So Allah revealed His clear answer to them: "Say" O Muhammad to those polytheists who asked you to worship their gods Sunnah, on the condition that they worship your God in the Sunnah "O disbelievers" by Allah. He quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas.
