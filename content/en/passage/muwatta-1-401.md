## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the traveller's prayer as long as he has not resolved to stay: Ibn Umar stayed ten nights in Mecca, shortening the prayer, unless he prayed it with the imam, in which case he prayed it complete with him. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.212}
