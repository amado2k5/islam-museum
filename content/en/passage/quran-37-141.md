## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 139 to 142 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "So he participated and was among those who refuted": It was narrated: The ship was blocked, and the people learned that it was only a story they had narrated, so they participated. Yunus blew and threw himself, and the whale struck him. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase "he contributed," he said: He knocked out. And His saying: 'So he was among the detractors' means: he was among the oppressed and defeated. It is said: Allah refuted the proof of so-and-so and it refuted, meaning he nullified it and it became invalid. Al-Dahdhd is originally slippery in water and clay, and it is mentioned about them: Allah refuted His proof, which is few. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
