## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "he only commands you to evil and indecency and that you say about God what you do not know" al-Tabari says it is Satan who commands; "evil" (su') is sin, what grieves the doer, and "indecency" (fahsha') is whatever is held shameful to mention and ugly to hear; it is said that evil is acts of disobedience, so called for their bad outcome, and that indecency is fornication, and from al-Suddi, evil is disobedience and indecency fornication. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 303} Saying about God what they do not know is their forbidding the bahira, sa'iba, wasila and hami and claiming God forbade them; he cites the verse of al-Ma'ida that God made no bahira, sa'iba, wasila or hami, and says their claim is the lie Satan commands, following their misguided forefathers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 303} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 304}
