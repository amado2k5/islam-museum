## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Did they not travel through the land and see how was the end of those before them, and they were stronger than them? And Allah could not overcome Him of anything in the heavens or the earth. Indeed, He is Knowing and Powerful": "Or did these polytheists not travel through the land which We destroyed its people by their disbelief in Us and their denial of Our messengers? Indeed, they are merchants traveling the way of the Levant." "And they will see how was the end of those before them" among the nations they used to pass through. Did We not destroy them and destroy? Their dwellings, and We make them an example for those who come after them, so they take heed from them and turn away from their worship of gods by associating partners with Allah, and they know that what He did to those people was not done, and they were stronger and more oppressive than them. He will not be able to do to them what He did to them, hastening punishment and punishment. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
