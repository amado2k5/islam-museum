## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “No calamity befalls except by the permission of Allah. And whoever believes in Allah - He will guide his heart. And Allah is Knowing of all things,” is explained as follows: No one among creation is afflicted with a calamity except by the permission of Allah. He says: except by the decree and predestination of Allah. “And whoever believes in Allah - He will guide his heart.” He says: Whoever believes in Allah and knows that no one is afflicted with a calamity except by His permission, his heart will be guided. He says: Allah will grant his heart success in submitting to His command and being content with His decree. The passage also includes statements attributed to Ibn Abbas and Al-A'mash.
