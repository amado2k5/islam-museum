**Commentary on verse 10 (p. 100)**

Al-Tabari says: 'those who disbelieved' are those who denied God's oneness and broke his covenant and the pledges they made to him; 'and denied our signs' means they denied God's proofs and evidences of his oneness which the messengers and others brought; 'those are the people of the Blaze' means the people of the Fire, who will remain in it and never leave. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 100}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
