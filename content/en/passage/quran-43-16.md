## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 17 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or take from that which He creates daughters and purify you with sons": a rebuke to them for saying that, so it is clear that his rebuke to them for that is only what He informed about them from what they said about the addition of daughters to Allah, Glorified and Exalted be He. And His saying: "Indeed, man is a clear ungrateful" means: Man is a denial of the blessings of his Lord which He has bestowed upon him. It is clear that his ingratitude makes His blessings upon him clear to those who reflect on Him with the thought of their heart and reflect on their state. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
