## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As: "Four qualities make someone a hypocrite: when he speaks he lies, when he promises he breaks it, when he disputes he is abusive, and when he makes a covenant he betrays it". It is the sixth of the hadiths Ibn Rajab added. He records a report attributing an interpretation to Ata, and rejects it as a lie. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
