## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So the response of his people was only that they said, 'Expel the family of Lot from your town; indeed, they are people purifying themselves.'" He explained the people of Lot as he forbade them from what Allah commanded them to forbid from approaching men, except that some said to others: "Bring out the family of Lot from your town; indeed, they are people purifying themselves." What we do by approaching the remembrances in their backs. Also: I heard al-Hasan ibn 'Amarah mention from al-Hakam, from Mujahid, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "People who purify themselves," he said: from men and women coming to their backs. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "They are people who purify themselves," he said: from the backs of men and women mocking them. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, who said: "They purify" from the backs of men and women, mocking them for saying that. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
