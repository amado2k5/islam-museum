## Summary
A report of Abu Dharr al-Ghifari in the chapter on the sanctity of a believer's blood, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It tells of Gabriel's good news for whoever dies holding to monotheism, and the Prophet's exception for major sins but not for polytheism. {cite:sahih-muslim}
