## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 69 to 71 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'Burn him and support your gods if you are doers': Some of Abraham's people said to others: Burn Abraham with fire," and "Support your gods, if you will do," meaning: if you are their supporters and do not want to abandon their worship. It is said that the one who said this was a man from the Kurds of Persia. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Hasan, Ibn Ishaq, and Ibrahim.
