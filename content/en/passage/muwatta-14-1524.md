## Summary
Malik says of a man with a wife who marries her mother and has intercourse with her: both the wife and the mother become forbidden to him permanently, and he must separate from both. If he did not have intercourse with the mother, the wife is not forbidden to him, and he separates from the mother. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.39}
