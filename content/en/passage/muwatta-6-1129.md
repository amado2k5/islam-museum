## Summary
A saying of Malik: what spoils the pilgrimage or the lesser pilgrimage, so that the offering becomes due in it, is the meeting of the two circumcised parts, even without a gushing fluid. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1234) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.515}
