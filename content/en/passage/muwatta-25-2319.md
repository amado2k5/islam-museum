## Summary
It reached Malik that Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and Sulayman ibn Yasar were asked about a man who made a contract for himself and his sons, then died: do his sons work to pay their father's contract, or are they slaves? They said: no, they work to pay their father's contract, and nothing is remitted from them by the death of their father. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.356}
