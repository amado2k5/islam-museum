## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 6 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, O disbelievers": He says to His Prophet Muhammad, and the polytheists among His people, as mentioned, offered to worship Allah for a Sunnah, so that the Prophet of Allah would worship their gods for a Sunnah. So Allah revealed His answer to them: "Say" O Muhammad to those polytheists who asked you to worship their gods Sunnah, on the condition that they worship your God in the Sunnah, "O disbelievers," by Allah. He quotes in the stanza from Ibn Abbas.
