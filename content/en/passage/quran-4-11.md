## Summary
Verse 11 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 11 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Start of the commentary on verse 11 (p. 30).** Abu Jafar says that the opening verb here means God entrusts you (the editor refers to the earlier commentary on the word), that is, your Lord charges you when one of you dies and leaves male and female children, then his male and female children take his whole inheritance among them, the male receiving twice the share of the female, if he has no other heir, the same for his young and grown children and his daughters, in that all of it is divided among them in that proportion. In the editor's note the manuscript has a form "and his grown ones" and the printing's is better. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 30} These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 30 in the commentary on verse 11 of sura 4, after the opening of the commentary, and continue from p. 31. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 30}
