## Al-Tabari's commentary

Allah commands the believers to remember Him frequently with hearts, tongues, and limbs, and to glorify Him morning and evening, meaning the dawn and afternoon prayers according to al-Tabari. Then He clarifies that He is the One who sends blessings upon them and His angels to bring them out of darkness into light, and He is merciful to the believers, greeting them on the Day they meet Him with peace, and He has prepared for them a generous reward.
