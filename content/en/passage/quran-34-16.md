## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 17 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "So they turned away, so We sent upon them a torrent of 'al-'Arim and replaced them with their two gardens: eating of Khamat, Athal, and a little lote tree": So Saba turned away from obedience to her Lord and turned away from following what her messengers had called her to that He is her Creator. As Muhammad ibn Ishaq narrated to us, from Wahb ibn Munabbih al-Yamani, who said: Allah sent thirteen prophets to Sheba, but they denied them. "So We sent upon them the flood of 'Aram." Allah Almighty says: We pierced them when they refused to believe our messengers, their dam that was restraining them from floods. And 'al-'arm' is the snake that holds water, singular: 'aramah', and al-'A'sha meant it by saying: 'In that is for al-Mu'tasi, an example.' And 'Marib' pardoned him by 'al-'Arim, a stone he built for them: when their water came, they did not throw 'aim'. And 'al-'arm, as mentioned, was built by Balqis. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
