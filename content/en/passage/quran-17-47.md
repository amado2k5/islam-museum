## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "We are most knowledgeable of what they listen to when they listen to you, and when they are whispering when the wrongdoers say, 'If you follow except a man bewitched': We know, O Muhammad, what those who do not believe in the Hereafter listen to from the polytheists of your people, when they listen to you while you recite the Book of Allah, 'And when they are whispering.'" Some Arabs from Basra used to say: Najwa is their doing, so he made them the ones who whisper, just as he says: They are a people of contentment, but rather satisfaction is their action. And His saying, "When the wrongdoers say, 'You will only follow a bewitched man,'" he means: when the polytheists say to God, 'You will only follow a bewitched man.' And what he meant in what was mentioned in the confiscated conversation is those who consulted about the matter of the Messenger of God in the gathering place. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
