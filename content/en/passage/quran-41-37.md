## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And among His signs are the night and the day, and the sun and the moon. Do not prostrate to the sun or the moon, but prostrate to Allah who created them, if you should worship Him." Among Allah's proofs for His creation and His indication of His oneness and great authority is the difference of night and day, and the punishment of each for its companion. The sun and the moon do not surpass the moon. "Nor does the night outstrip the day, and everyone in an orbit swims." Do not prostrate to the sun or to the moon, for even if they move in the orbit for your benefit, they only move by it for you. By Allah's actions, they are obedient to Him in their running and movement, not because they themselves are capable of walking and running without Allah managing and directing them, or that they can benefit or harm you. Rather, Allah has subjected them to you for your benefits and interests. So prostrate to Him, and worship Him besides them, for if He wills, He can obliterate their light, leaving you confused in darkness, unable to find the right path and see nothing.
