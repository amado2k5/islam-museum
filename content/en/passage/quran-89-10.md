## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 15 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the people of ‘Ad?”: “Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the people of ‘Iram?” God Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: “Have you not looked, O Muhammad, with the eye of your heart, and seen how your Lord dealt with the people of ‘Ad?” The scholars of interpretation differed in their interpretation of “Iram.” Some said it was the name of a city, and those who held this view differed as to which city it referred to. Some said it was Alexandria. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
