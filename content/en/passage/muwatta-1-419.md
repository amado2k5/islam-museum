## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the total of the forenoon voluntary prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Ishaq ibn Abdullah ibn Abi Talha from Anas ibn Malik: his grandmother Mulayka invited the Prophet to a meal. He ate some and said, "Get up, and I will pray for you". Anas went to a mat of theirs that had blackened from long wear, sprinkled it with water, and the Prophet stood on it. Anas formed a row with an orphan behind him and the old woman behind them, and he prayed two rak'as for them and then left. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Darimi, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.218} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.219}
