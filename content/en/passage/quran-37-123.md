## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 123 to 129 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed, Elias is among the messengers": And that Elias, who is Elias son of Yasin son of Fannhas son of Al-Eizar son of Harun son of Imran, is from Ibn Ishaq. And His saying: "Among the messengers," He, the Exalted, says: a messenger from among the messengers, and in the segment he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
