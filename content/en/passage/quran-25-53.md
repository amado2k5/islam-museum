## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains the meadow by mixing and abandoning. God is the one who made the two seas meet: one is fresh, very sweet, and the other is very salty, and He placed a single barrier between them that neither wants to overlap. He explains the sweet Euphrates as very sweet, which is the water of rivers and rain, and salt, which is bitter, which is the water of the seas.
