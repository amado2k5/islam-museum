## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Then a clear shooting star follows him," meaning the devil who eavesdrops to hear a visible shooting from the fire, showing its effect by his confusion, corruption, or burning — and narrates from Ibn Abbas that meteors do not kill but burn, madness, and come out without being killed. He narrates the image of eavesdropping: the devils ascend in groups, the genie rises from them, rises, and throws the shooting star and strikes his forehead or wherever God wills and it ignites. He informs his companions, the soothsayers come and multiply his lies, and when they see something they said, they believe what they have brought.
