## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "On the Day their faces are turned in the Fire, they will say, 'If only we had obeyed Allah and obeyed the Messenger.'" These disbelievers will find no protector or helper on the Day their faces will be turned into the Fire one after another. They will say, 'That is their state in the Fire: 'If only we had obeyed Allah' in this world and obeyed His Messenger in what He brought us regarding His command and prohibition. We would have been with the people of Paradise in Paradise. What a regret and regret it is, how great and noble it is.
