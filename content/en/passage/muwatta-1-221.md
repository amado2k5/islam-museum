## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on recitation at dawn: when Abdullah ibn Umar travelled he would recite at dawn the first ten surahs of the mufassal, in each rak'a the Opening chapter and a surah. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, al-Shafi'i, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.134}
