## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and spend in the way of God, and do not throw yourselves by your own hands into destruction, and do good; God loves those who do good", al-Tabari says the commentators differed over its interpretation and over who is meant by "do not throw yourselves into destruction". One group said the meaning is: spend in the way of God, His path that He commanded be followed to His enemy among the polytheists to fight them, and do not leave off spending in the way of God, for God will compensate you for it; the editor says he fears "ajran" in the printed text is a corruption of "ajilan". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 583} From Hudhayfa, "do not throw yourselves into destruction" means in leaving off spending, and the like is reported by several routes from Shaqiq from Hudhayfa. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 583} From Ibn 'Abbas, spend in the way of God even if you have nothing but a mishqas or, in Shu'ba's doubt, an arrow; the editor explains the mishqas as a long, not broad, arrowhead. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 584} From Ibn 'Abbas, destruction is not that a man be killed in the way of God but withholding from spending in the way of God. From 'Ikrima, it was revealed concerning spending in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 584} From Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi, the people were in the way of God and a man took provisions, one having better provisions than another, and the poor man spent from his provisions until none was left, wanting to share with his companion, and God revealed the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 584} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From Ibn 'Abbas, let none of you say "I find nothing"; if he finds only a mishqas let him equip with it in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From 'Amir, some provision was held back from the Ansar and they had spent funds, so they thought ill and withheld, and the verse was revealed; the destruction was their ill thought and withholding. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From Mujahid, do not let fear of poverty prevent you from spending in a right cause. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From Qatada, from al-Hasan, they used to travel and raid without spending from their wealth, so God commanded them to spend on their expeditions in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585}

Further reports that the meaning is leaving off spending: from Qatada, do not hold back your hands from spending in the way of God. From al-Suddi, spend in the way of God even a hobble-rope, and "do not throw yourselves into destruction" means saying "I have nothing". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 586} From 'Ikrima, when God commanded spending some said "we spend, and our wealth goes and nothing is left to us", and He said, spend and do not throw yourselves into destruction, spend and I will provide for you. From al-Hasan, it was revealed about spending; God commanded spending in His way and told them that leaving off spending in the way of God is destruction. From 'Ata', spend in the way of God little or much, and from 'Abd Allah ibn Kathir, it was revealed about spending. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 586} From Ibn 'Abbas, let no man say "I find nothing, I am ruined"; let him equip even with a mishqas; and in another report, spend whatever it is, little or much, and do not give up, nor spend nothing and so perish. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 586} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 587} From al-Dahhak, destruction is that a man holds back himself and his wealth from spending in jihad in the way of God; from al-Hasan, so that you abandon spending in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 587}

Others, among those who took the interpretation to be about spending, said the meaning is: spend in the way of God and do not throw yourselves into destruction by going out in the way of God without provision or strength; from Ibn Zayd, if you have nothing to spend, do not go out without spending or strength and so throw yourself into destruction with your own hands. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 587}

Others said rather that it means: spend in the way of God, and do not, over the sins you have committed, throw yourselves into ruin by despairing of God's mercy, but hope for His mercy and do good deeds. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 587} From al-Bara' ibn 'Azib, it is the man who commits sins and then throws himself into destruction, saying "there is no repentance for me". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 588} From Abu Ishaq, a man asked al-Bara', "if I charge the polytheists alone and they kill me, will I have thrown myself into destruction?" He said no, destruction is in spending; God sent His messenger and said "so fight in the way of God, you are held responsible only for yourself". From him also, it is the man who commits a sin and says "God will not forgive him"; and in other reports from him, when the questioner asked if it is the man who advances and fights until killed, he said no, but destruction is that a man commits a sin, surrenders, and says "no repentance will be accepted from me". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 588} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 589} From 'Ubayda al-Salmani, by several routes through Ibn Sirin, a man would commit a sin and throw himself in, destroying himself or saying there was no repentance for him; and from him, it is despair. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 589}

Others said rather that the meaning is: spend in the way of God and do not abandon jihad in His way. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 590} From Aslam Abu 'Imran: we campaigned against "the city", meaning Constantinople, with 'Uqba ibn 'Amir over the people of Egypt and 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Khalid ibn al-Walid over the whole force, and the Romans had their backs against the wall of the city; a man of us charged the enemy and the people said "stop! there is no god but God, he is throwing himself into destruction!" Abu Ayyub al-Ansari said, "you interpret this verse so, that a man charged seeking martyrdom or showing his mettle! It was revealed about us, the Ansar: when God aided His Prophet and made Islam prevail we said among ourselves, hidden from the Messenger of God, 'we left our families and wealth to stay and tend them until God aided His Prophet; come, let us stay with our wealth and tend it', and God revealed the verse; so throwing ourselves into destruction is staying in our wealth, tending it and leaving jihad." Abu 'Imran said that Abu Ayyub never ceased to fight in the way of God until he was buried at Constantinople. The editor identifies Haywa ibn Shurayh and Aslam Abu 'Imran, whom he calls a trustworthy Successor. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 590}
