## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and spend in the way of God, and do not throw yourselves by your own hands into destruction, and do good; God loves those who do good", al-Tabari says the commentators differed over its interpretation and over who is meant by "do not throw yourselves into destruction". One group said the meaning is: spend in the way of God, His path that He commanded be followed to His enemy among the polytheists to fight them, and do not leave off spending in the way of God, for God will compensate you for it; the editor says he fears "ajran" in the printed text is a corruption of "ajilan". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 583} From Hudhayfa, "do not throw yourselves into destruction" means in leaving off spending, and the like is reported by several routes from Shaqiq from Hudhayfa. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 583} From Ibn 'Abbas, spend in the way of God even if you have nothing but a mishqas or, in Shu'ba's doubt, an arrow; the editor explains the mishqas as a long, not broad, arrowhead. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 584} From Ibn 'Abbas, destruction is not that a man be killed in the way of God but withholding from spending in the way of God. From 'Ikrima, it was revealed concerning spending in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 584} From Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi, the people were in the way of God and a man took provisions, one having better provisions than another, and the poor man spent from his provisions until none was left, wanting to share with his companion, and God revealed the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 584} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From Ibn 'Abbas, let none of you say "I find nothing"; if he finds only a mishqas let him equip with it in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From 'Amir, some provision was held back from the Ansar and they had spent funds, so they thought ill and withheld, and the verse was revealed; the destruction was their ill thought and withholding. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From Mujahid, do not let fear of poverty prevent you from spending in a right cause. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585} From Qatada, from al-Hasan, they used to travel and raid without spending from their wealth, so God commanded them to spend on their expeditions in the way of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 585}
