## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Who will punish you by night and day? Rather, they turn away from the remembrance of their Lord.": Say, O Muhammad, to those who hasten to punishment, those who say: When will this promise be made if you are truthful? 'Who will punish you?' O people, it means: Who will protect you and guard you at night when you sleep, and by day when you act against the Most Merciful? He says: Who commands the Most Merciful if He descends upon you, and from His punishment when He befalls you? He omitted the mention of 'the command,' and it was said 'min al-rahman,' as a partial meaning known to the listeners as meaning from his mention. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
