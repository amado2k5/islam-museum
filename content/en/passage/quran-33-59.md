## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O Prophet, tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to draw their garments close over them so that they may be recognized and not harmed." And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful. This is: O Prophet, tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers not to imitate slave women in their clothing when they leave their homes for their needs. They should uncover their hair and faces, but draw their cloaks closer to them so that no disobedient person will approach them if they know they are free in harm from speech. Then the interpreters differed regarding the description of the intimacy that God commanded them to perform. Some said: it is to cover their faces and heads, so that only one eye is visible. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
