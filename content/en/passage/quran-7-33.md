## Summary
Verse 33 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 33 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "Say: my Lord has forbidden only indecencies, what is open of them and what is hidden, and sin and aggression without right", God tells His Prophet to say to these idolaters who strip off their clothes for circling the House and forbid themselves the good things God made lawful to them of His provision: people, God did not forbid what you forbid, but made it lawful for His believing servants and good for them; my Lord forbade only ugly things, which are "indecencies", "what is open of them", being public, "and what is hidden", being secret and concealed. It is reported of Mujahid on this, (14551), that "what is open of them" is the Jahiliyya's circling the House naked and "what is hidden" is zina. Al-Tabari says he mentioned the commentators' disagreement over this, with reports, earlier and did not want to repeat it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 402}

As for "sin", it is disobedience, and "aggression" is overbearing toward people; God says that my Lord forbade the indecencies along with sin and aggression against people. He gives (14552) from al-Suddi that "sin" is disobedience and "aggression" is to act wrongfully against people without right; and (14553) from Mujahid that He spoke of "sin", which is all acts of disobedience, and reported that the aggressor's aggression falls on himself. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 403}

Then, on "and that you associate with God that for which He sent down no authority, and that you say about God what you do not know", God says: my Lord forbade only indecencies and shirk with Him, that you worship another god with God, "that for which He sent down no authority", meaning your Lord forbade you to make a partner with Him in worshipping anything for which He gave you, in associating it with Him in worship, no proof or evidence, which is the "authority"; "and that you say about God what you do not know" means that you say God commanded you to strip and go bare for circling the House, and forbade you to eat these cattle you forbade, set free, and made wasila and hami, and the like, of which you do not know that God forbade, commanded or permitted them, so that you ascribe to God its forbidding, restricting and commanding; for that is what God forbade you, rather than what you claim God forbade or say He commanded, out of ignorance of the truth of what you say and ascribe to God. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "authority". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 404}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
