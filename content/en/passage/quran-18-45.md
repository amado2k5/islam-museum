## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And present to them the example of the life of this world: a trunk which We sent down from the sky, and the plants of the earth mingled with it, and it became dry sand scattered by the winds. And Allah is over all things competent": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: "And strike for the life of those arrogant ones who said to you: 'Drive away from you those who call upon their Lord morning and evening.' If We bring you the world from them as an example, it says: It is like water We sent down from the sky," meaning: Like rain We sent down from the sky, "and the plants of the earth mixed with it," meaning: the plants of the earth mixed with water, "and it became barren," meaning: the plants of the earth became dry, fragmented, and the winds scattered it. It means the winds scatter and disperse it. It is said: the wind scattered it, scattered it, and its seed scattered, and its seed scattered, and the scattered it was scattered, as the poet said: I said to him, 'Turn it ahead, and do not strain it,' so it drops the other of the scat, and it slips. It is said: I scattered the man from the beast and the camel: If you cast it away from him.
