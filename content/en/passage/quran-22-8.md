## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among the people is he who disputes about Allah without knowledge, guidance, or a luminous Book": And among the people is he who disputes about the oneness of Allah and His deity without knowledge of what he disputes. "Nor guidance" means: without clarification or proof with what he says. "Nor a clear Book" means: and without a Book from Allah that came to him because of the validity of what he says. Munir says he illuminates his proof, but he only says what he says out of ignorance and calculation. He mentioned that he meant by this verse and the one after which was Al-Nadr ibn Al-Harith from Banu Abd al-Dar.
