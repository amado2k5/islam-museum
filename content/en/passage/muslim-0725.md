## Summary
A report of Aisha through Sa'd ibn Hisham in the chapter on the merit of the two rak'as before the dawn prayer, in the Book of the Prayer of Travellers in Sahih Muslim. They are the supererogatory prayer the Prophet most kept to. {cite:sahih-muslim}
