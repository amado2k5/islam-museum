## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 76 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And there is nothing hidden in the heavens and the earth except in a clear Book": "And there is nothing" that is concealed or concealed from the eyes of those who observe "in the heavens and the earth except in a Book," which is the mother of the Book in which our Lord has confirmed everything that exists from the moment His creation began until the Day of Resurrection. By "clear," he means that He makes it clear to those who look at it and read what is in it, in which our Lord, Glorified and Exalted, has firmly established it. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
