## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Masud in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim, completing the hadith with the three levels of struggle: whoever struggles against wrong with his hand is a believer, with his tongue is a believer, with his heart is a believer, and there is no faith beyond that the size of a mustard seed. {cite:sahih-muslim}
