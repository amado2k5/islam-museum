## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then We created after them another generation": Then We created after the destruction of the people of Noah another generation and created them. "So We sent among them a messenger from among themselves," calling them, "If they worship Allah," O people, and obey Him instead of gods and idols, for worship is only worthy of Him. "You have no deity besides Him," meaning: You have no deity worthy of worship besides Him. "Do you not fear Him": Do you not fear the punishment of Allah for your worship of anything besides Him, which is the God for whom you have no deity?
