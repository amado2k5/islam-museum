## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on the recommendation of voluntary prayer at home, in the Book of the Prayer of Travellers in Sahih Muslim. The likeness makes houses where God is remembered living, and empty houses dead. {cite:sahih-muslim}
