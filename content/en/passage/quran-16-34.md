## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "so the evils of what they did befell them, and what they used to mock befell them": Those who did from the past nations did the same as those polytheists from Quraysh who did the sins they did, meaning the punishment for their sins and the retribution of the sins they had earned. "And befell them what they used to mock for," meaning: the punishment of God befell them what they used to mock and mock when warned by the messengers of God, and it was revealed to them over others who believed in God.
