## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed, all of these generations that We destroyed, those We did not perish, and others with Us on the Day of Resurrection, all will be prepared," as Sa'id narrated from Qatadah, "And indeed, all of these generations that We have destroyed and those We have not destroyed and others will be prepared with Us on the Day of Resurrection," meaning they will be prepared on the Day of Resurrection. Readers differed in reading this; the general recitation of Medina and Basra and some Kufans read it as: "And inna kull-lamma" with a lighter indication that this is "ma" to which the lam was inserted as an answer, because the meaning of the speech is: "and everyone is present to us." The general recitation of the people of Kufa read this as: "lamma" with a stressed mim. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
