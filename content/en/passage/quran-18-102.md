## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Do those who disbelieve think that they will take My servants besides Me as allies? Indeed, Hell has been prepared for the disbelievers as a resting place": He, the Exalted, says: "Do those who disbelieve in Allah think of the servants of the angels and the Messiah that they will take My servants whom they worship besides Allah as allies?" He says, "No, rather, they are enemies to them." In the passage, he quotes from Ikrimah and Ibn Jurayj.
