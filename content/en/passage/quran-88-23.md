## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21-26 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “except those who turn away and disbelieve” as having two meanings: First, remind your people, O Muhammad, except those among them who turn away from you and disregard God’s signs and disbelieve. The word “except” then serves as an exception to those who were being reminded, even if they did not heed the reminder, just as one might say, “So-and-so went and called out, except to those whose response is not expected,” meaning, “He called out to the people, except to those whose response is not expected.”<sup>1</sup> In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
