## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20-24 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Nay, indeed, the record of the righteous is in the highest heavens” as follows: “Nay, indeed, the record of the righteous is in the highest heavens.” The righteous are those who are dutiful to God by performing His obligatory duties and abstaining from His prohibitions. Al-Hasan used to say: They are those who do not harm anything, not even an ant. A sheikh narrated from al-Hasan that he was asked about the righteous, and he said: They are those who do not harm an ant. Al-Sari ibn Yahya narrated from al-Hasan that he said: The righteous are those who do not harm an ant. Regarding the phrase “in the highest heavens,” the scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning of “the highest heavens.” Some said it is the seventh heaven. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, al-A'mash, and Atiyyah.
