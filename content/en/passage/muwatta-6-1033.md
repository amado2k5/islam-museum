## Summary
Malik transmits from Alqama ibn Abi Alqama, from his mother, that she heard Aisha, wife of the Prophet, asked about the one in ihram: may he scratch his body? She said: yes, let him scratch and press hard; even if my hands were tied and I had only my feet, I would scratch. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1194), Suwayd (581) and al-Shaybani (435) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.481}
