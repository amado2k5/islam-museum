## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on prostration in the Qur'an: it is not the practice that the imam, when he recites a prostration while on the pulpit, comes down and prostrates. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.284}
