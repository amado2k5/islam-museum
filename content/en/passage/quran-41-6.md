## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Say, I am only a human like you, revealed to me. Indeed, your God is one God, so stand firm to Him and ask His forgiveness. And woe to the polytheists." Say, O Muhammad, to those among your people who turn away from the signs of Allah: O people, I am nothing but a human from among the children of Adam like you in kind, form, and appearance. I am not an angel (revealed to me) who inspires Allah that there is no deity worthy of you except one deity whose worship is worthy of worship, "so stand steadfast towards Him," meaning: So stand firm to Him in obedience, and turn your faces to Him with desire and worship rather than the gods and idols. "And ask His forgiveness," meaning: and ask Him to forgive you for your sins that have been committed by repentance from your polytheism. He may repent to you and forgive you. And His saying: "Woe to the polytheists who do not pay zakat while they are disbelievers in the Hereafter," Allah says: 'And the pus of the people of the Fire, and what flows from them to those who call to Allah as partners with the worshippers and idols, besides Him is those who do not pay zakat.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ikrimah.
