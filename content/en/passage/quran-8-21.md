**Commentary on verse 21 (pp. 458-459)**

Al-Tabari says on "and be not like those who said, we hear, and they do not hear" [[passage/quran-8-21|8:21]]: God says to those who believe in God and His Messenger among the companions of the Prophet of God: do not be, believers, in opposing the Messenger of God like the polytheists who, when God's book is recited to them, say "we have heard" with their ears, "and they do not hear" meaning they take no lesson from what they hear with their ears and do not benefit from it because they turn away from it and leave their hearts without taking it in and pondering it; so God, since they did not benefit from the admonitions of the Qur'an though they heard them with their ears, put them in the position of those who did not hear. He says to the companions of the Messenger of God, do not be, in turning away from the Messenger of God's command and not coming to it while you hear it with your ears, like these polytheists. Ibn Ishaq used to say, in report 15853: that is, like the hypocrites who show him obedience and hide disobedience; reports 15854 and 15855, from Mujahid on "and they do not hear": disobedient. Abu Ja'far said: what Ibn Ishaq said has a ground, but the words "be not like those who said, we hear" come in the course of the stories of the polytheists and are followed by a report about them blaming them, His words "the worst of beasts with God are the deaf, the dumb, who do not reason", so that what lies between be a report about them is better than that it be a report about others. The editor gives the reading of the manuscript and printed edition in places of the commentary. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 458} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 459}
