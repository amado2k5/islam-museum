## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Those from whom We accept the best of what they have done and overlook their bad deeds among the companions of Paradise, the promise of truthfulness which they were promised": Those whose attribute is this trait are those from whom the best of good deeds they have done in this world is accepted, and He rewards them and rewards them for it, and "We overlook their sins," meaning: and He forgives them for the bad deeds they did in this world, so He does not punish them for them among the companions of Paradise, as he says: We do that to them; we did the same to the people of Paradise and its people who are its people. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
