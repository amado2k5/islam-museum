## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54-56 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “So whoever wills may remember it”: Whoever among God’s servants whom God has reminded of this Quran will remember it, take heed, and act upon what it contains of God’s commands and prohibitions. “And they do not remember unless God wills”: God, may He be exalted, says: They do not remember this Quran, take heed from it, and act upon what it contains, unless God wills that they remember it, for no one has power over anything except by God’s will, enabling them to do it and granting them the ability to do it. And His saying, “He is worthy of being feared and worthy of granting forgiveness”: God, may He be exalted, says: God is worthy of His servants fearing His punishment for their disobedience to Him, so they avoid His sins and hasten to obey Him. “And worthy of granting forgiveness”: He says: He is worthy of forgiving their sins if they do so, and He does not punish them for them after they repent from them.
