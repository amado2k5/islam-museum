## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Did they not travel through the land and see how was the end of those before them? They were more powerful than them and more powerful and more influential in the land. What they used to earn was not more beneficial than them": Was it not easy, O Muhammad, those who dispute the signs of Allah among the polytheists of your people in the lands? They are travelers to the Levant and Yemen, their journey in winter and summer. They look among the lands they have set foot in, at Our events, with whom We sent upon them from among the nations before them, and see how We have permitted them of Our punishment by denying Our messengers and their denial of Our signs. How The consequence of their denial was to be denied. "They were more than them," he says: those who were before these deniers from Quraysh were more numerous, more oppressive, stronger, and left traces on the earth, because they used to carve houses from the mountains and establish factories. Mujahid used to say about this: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "And traces in the land," meaning walking on their feet. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
