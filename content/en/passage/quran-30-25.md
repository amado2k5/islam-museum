## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And among His signs is that the heavens and the earth stand by His command, then when He calls you to a call from the earth, [saying], 'Then when He calls you from the earth, when you come out,'" he says: "Then when He calls you from the earth, you will come out." He means: when He calls you out of the earth, you will be answered by His call.
