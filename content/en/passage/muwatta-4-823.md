## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, that he heard Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan on the pulpit on the day of Ashura in the year he performed the pilgrimage, saying: people of Medina, where are your scholars? I heard the Messenger of God say of this day: this is the day of Ashura, and God did not make its fasting obligatory for you, but I am fasting, so whoever wishes may fast and whoever wishes may break the fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik, and that Abu Musab and Malik make it part of the Musnad of Aisha and others. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.402}
