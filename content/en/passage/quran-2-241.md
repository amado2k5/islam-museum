## Summary
Verse 241 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 241 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On ([[passage/quran-2-241|2:241]]) al-Tabari says that what is meant is what a divorced woman enjoys from her divorcer, such as clothing, maintenance, a servant and the like, and that the meaning of the provision and the scholars' disagreement over it have been explained earlier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 262} Those meant are disputed. Some said they are the women who were consummated with, since the right of those not consummated with has been set out in earlier verses; so the verse concerns the consummated, and this is 'Ata' and Mujahid (5590, 5591). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 262} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 263} Others said the verse shows every divorced woman has a provision and was sent down for the added meaning over the other provision verses, which treat the unconsummated; this is Sa'id b. Jubayr (5592). Al-Zuhri on a slave wife divorced while pregnant said she observes her wait at home and that he had heard nothing on provision for a slave woman (5593); 'Ata' said no provision for a slave woman married to a free man or a free woman with a slave, while 'Amr b. Dinar said yes (5594). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 263} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 264} A third group said it came down because a man among the Muslims, when the command of provision for the good-doers came down, said he would do it if he did good and not if he did not wish to; so this was sent and made it obligatory, from Ibn Zayd (5595). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 264} The editor notes in the margin a confusion of a transmitter's name in the source and corrects it; that is his estimate. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 264}

Al-Tabari chooses the view of Sa'id b. Jubayr, that God sent it as a pointer for His servants that every divorced woman has a provision, because in the other verses He singled out groups of women, the unconsummated in ([[passage/quran-2-236|2:236]]) and in ([[passage/quran-33-49|33:49]]), and the women offered the choice among the Prophet's wives, leaving the rulings for young girls divorced after consummation, unbelieving women and slave women; this place covers all of them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 264} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 265} For the sense of "right" and its grammatical form he refers back to ([[passage/quran-2-236|2:236]]), and he says the pious are those who feared God in His command, prohibition and limits and kept them out of dread of Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 265} This is al-Tabari's choice and argument, and not a legal ruling by the museum.
