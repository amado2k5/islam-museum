## Summary
Verse 42 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 42 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "we sent to communities before you and seized them with hardship and affliction that they might humble themselves" al-Tabari says, threatening these who hold idols equal to him and warning them lest, if they persist in their error, the way of those before them of the peoples who took their way be taken with them, in God's hastening his punishment on them in this world, and reporting to his prophet his way with the peoples before them who followed their path of denying the messengers: "we sent", Muhammad, "to communities", meaning groups and generations "before you, and we seized them with hardship", that is, we commanded them and forbade them, they denied our messengers and went against our command and prohibition, so we tried them with trial, "by hardship", which is the severity of poverty and narrowness of livelihood, "and affliction", which is the illnesses and sicknesses that befall bodies; he has made this clear with evidence and the ways of its grammar in Surat al-Baqara. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 354}

By "that they might humble themselves" he says: we did that to them so that they would humble themselves to me, devote worship to me and single out their desire for me alone, abasing themselves to me in obedience and submitting to me in turning back. Something is omitted in the sentence, the apparent sense making its mention unnecessary: the cause of his seizing them was their denial of the messengers and their opposition to his command, not the sending of the messengers; the sense is: we sent to communities before you messengers and they denied them, so we seized them with hardship. "Tadarru'" is the form "tafa''ul" from "dara'a", which is abasement and submission. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 355}

The editor refers for "community", "hardship" and "affliction" to p. 341 and to volumes 4, 7 and 3, and keeps the manuscript's wording "by what the apparent sense shows of mentioning it", fearing some words fell from the copyist.

Verse 42 closes here at p. 355, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-43|6:43]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
