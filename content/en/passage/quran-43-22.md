## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Rather, they said, 'Indeed, we found our fathers upon a nation, and indeed, we are guided by their footsteps.'" These who say: 'If the Most Merciful had willed, we would not have worshipped these idols by commanding their worship,' a book from Us. Rather, they said: We found our fathers before us worshipping them, so we worship them as they used to worship them. And He, Glorified and Exalted, meant by saying: 'Rather, we found our fathers upon a nation,' rather, we found our fathers following a religion and a religion, and that is their idol worship. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
