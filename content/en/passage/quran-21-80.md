## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We taught him the craft of clothing for you to protect you from your might, so will you then be grateful?" He explains: And We taught David the craft of clothing for you. Among the Arabs, clothing means all weapons, whether armor, or a sword, or a spear. This is indicated by Al-Hudhali's saying: "And I have a garment for the garment as if it was a spear on the forehead of a sheep with a spear." As for this place, the interpreters said: 'I mean the shields.' In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
