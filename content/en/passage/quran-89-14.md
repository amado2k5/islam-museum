## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 15 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Indeed, your Lord is ever watchful”: “O Muhammad, your Lord is ever watchful over those whose stories I have related to you, and over others like them among the disbelievers, observing their deeds in this world and the Hereafter, on the bridges of Hell, to hurl them into it when they arrive on the Day of Resurrection.” Scholars of interpretation differed in their understanding of this verse. Some said that the meaning of “ever watchful” is that He sees and hears. In this section, he quotes interpretations from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Dahhak, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and others.
