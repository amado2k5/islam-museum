## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 151 to 153 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And do not obey the command of the extravagant": He, the Exalted, informs about a righteous saying to His people from Thamud: Do not obey, O people, the command of the extravagant against themselves in their persistence in disobeying Allah and daring to rebel against Him. They are the nine tribes who used to spread corruption in the land and did not reform among Thamud, whom Allah, Glorified and Exalted, described by saying: "And there were in Medina nine tribes who spread corruption on earth but did not reform." He means those who strive in the land of Allah through His sins but do not reform. He means: They do not reform themselves by acting in obedience to Allah. And the phrase: 'You are only among the bewitched'—interpreters differed in its interpretation; some said: it means you are only among the bewitched. In the section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
