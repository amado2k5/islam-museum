**Commentary on verses 23-24 (p. 423)**

On "gardens of Eden which they will enter, together with those who were righteous among their fathers, their spouses and their offspring; and the angels will enter upon them from every gate: peace be upon you for your patience; excellent is the final abode", al-Tabari says: "gardens of Eden" is a "tarjama", an explanatory restatement, of "the final abode", as one says "excellent the man, Abd Allah", where Abd Allah is the man of whom "excellent the man" is said. The meaning of the speech is: those have, following their obedience to their Lord, the abode that is the gardens of Eden. We have explained the meaning of "Eden", that it means a stay with no departure in it. On "together with those who were righteous among their fathers, their spouses and their offspring" he says, mighty be His praise: the gardens of Eden are entered by these whose description He gave, who are those who fulfil God's covenant, those who join what God commanded to be joined and fear their Lord, and those who were patient seeking the face of their Lord, established prayer and did the deeds He, mighty be His praise, mentioned in these three verses, "and those who were righteous among their fathers, their spouses", their women and families, "and their offspring"; their righteousness is their faith in God and their following His command and the command of His messenger. The editor notes that the "tarjama" of the Kufans is the "explanatory apposition" and, for the Basrans, the "substitution". Then report 20338 begins, from al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, through Shababa and Waraqa. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 423}
