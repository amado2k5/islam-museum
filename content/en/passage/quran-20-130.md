## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 130 to 131 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And if it were not for a word that preceded your Lord, it would have been binding and a specified term": "And if it were not for a word that preceded your Lord"—O Muhammad, that whoever decrees a term for him does not respect it before reaching his term. "And a specified term." He means: a designated time with your Lord that He named for them in the Mother of the Book and wrote it in it, they exaggerated and fulfilled it. "It would have been necessary." He means: they would have been bound to perish soon, which is a verbal noun derived from the saying: So-and-so must be connected to him continuously and inseparably: if he does not leave him, and he precedes the phrase: "It would have been necessary" before his phrase "a specified term." The meaning of the phrase is: If it were not for a word from your Lord and a specified term, it would have been binding. So be patient with what they say. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
