## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "the Day We will strike the greatest oppression; indeed, We will take retribution": Indeed, O polytheists, if the punishment that will befall you and the distress that will befall you, and then you return to your disbelief and break the covenant you made with your Lord, I will take revenge on you on the day I struck you with my greatest oppression in the immediate aftermath of this world, destroying you, and Allah removed them, and they returned, and the Exalted is His great oppression in this world struck them with the sword. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the great oppression; some said: It is God's oppression of the polytheists of Quraysh on the day of Badr. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and others.
