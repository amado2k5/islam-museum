## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “They have taken their oaths as a shield and thus averted [people] from the way of Allah. Indeed, evil is what they were doing,” is explained as follows: The hypocrites took their oaths as a shield, meaning their oaths. As Qatadah said, “They have taken their oaths as a shield,” meaning their oaths were a shield. Ibn Abi Najih narrated from Mujahid regarding Allah’s statement, “They have taken their oaths as a shield,” that he said: They come with them. He said this because they believed and then disbelieved. It was narrated to me from al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu’adh say: I heard al-Dahhak say regarding His statement, “They have taken their oaths as a shield,” that he said: Their oaths by Allah were a shield for you. His statement, “a shield,” means a covering with which they protect themselves, just as one protects oneself with a shield in war and combat, thus defending themselves, their families, and their possessions, and repelling attacks against them. The passage also quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
