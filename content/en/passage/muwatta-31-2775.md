## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abu Umama ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, from Khalid ibn al-Walid, that he entered with the Prophet into the house of Maymuna. A lizard roasted on hot stones was brought, and the Prophet reached out his hand to it. Some women said: tell the Messenger of God what he wants to eat, for it is a lizard. He lifted his hand. Khalid asked him: is it forbidden? He said: no, but it is not in the land of my people, and I find I dislike it. Khalid ate it while the Prophet watched. The editor notes that Ibn Abbas was present at the story, which is supported by the hadith of Ibn al-Munkadir from Abu Umama. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.560}
