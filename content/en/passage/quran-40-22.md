## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because their messengers used to come to them with clear proofs, but they disbelieved, so Allah seized them. Indeed, He is Powerful and severe in punishment": This is what was done to those nations whom the polytheists of Quraysh destroyed with their sins. We did to them by having Allah's messengers come to them with clear proofs, meaning by the verses that indicate the true meaning of what calls them to the oneness of Allah and ultimately obedience to Him. "So they disbelieved," meaning: they denied its message, denied the oneness of Allah, and refused to obey Allah. "So Allah took them." He says: So Allah seized them with His punishment and destroyed them. "Indeed, He is Powerful and severe in punishment." He says: Allah is Powerful; nothing can overcome Him, nor can He be overcome by anything. He wills His severe punishment against those who punish those of His creation. This is a warning from Allah, the polytheists of Quraysh, the deniers of His Messenger Muhammad. He says to them, the Exalted: Beware, O people, that you follow their path in denying Muhammad, denying the oneness of Allah, and opposing His command and prohibition, so He will hasten destruction with you.
