## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "in retaliation there is life for you, O people of understanding, so that you may become God-fearing" al-Tabari says: for you, O people of reason, in the retaliation I made obligatory in lives, wounds and head wounds, there is what restrained some of you from killing others, so you have life by it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 381} He reports that the interpreters differed. Some said much the same: retaliation is a deterrent and a limit, from Mujahid; from Qatada and al-Rabi', God made retaliation life, deterrence and a warning for the foolish, and many a man who planned a calamity was held back by fear of retaliation; from Ibn Zayd, life and survival, since one who fears being killed holds back; and from Abu Salih, survival. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 382} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 383} Others said: in retaliating against the killer there is survival for others, since no one but the killer is killed for the slain in God's ruling, whereas in the pre-Islamic time they killed a male for a female and a free man for a slave, from al-Suddi. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 383} "People of understanding" are people of reason, whom God addressed because they are the ones who grasp His command and prohibition and reflect on His signs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 383} "So that you may be God-fearing" means you guard against retaliation and so refrain from killing, from Ibn Zayd. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 384}
