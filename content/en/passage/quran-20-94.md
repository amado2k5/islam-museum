## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 94 to 96 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Yahrun said, 'What prevented you when you saw them go astray?': Moses said to his brother Aaron when he finished addressing his people and consulting them about their mistake: 'O Aaron, what prevented you when you saw them go astray from their religion? They disbelieved in Allah and worshipped the swift not to follow me.' Interpreters differ on the meaning of which Moses excused his brother for abandoning his followers; some said: He was excused for abandoning him by following those who followed him as he was entrusted with it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
