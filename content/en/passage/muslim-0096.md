## Summary
A report of Usama ibn Zayd in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim, about an expedition against the Huraqat of Juhayna. It makes the word of monotheism a protection for life even in battle, and it is among the sharpest reproaches in Sahih Muslim. {cite:sahih-muslim}
