## Summary
A report from Malik that it reached him that Aisha, wife of the Prophet, used to give the property of orphans to someone who would trade with it. The editor notes that the phrase 'in whose care she was' is not in the Muwatta copy, and that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.342}
