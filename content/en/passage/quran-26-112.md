## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 111 to 113 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "He said, and I do not know what they used to do": Noah said to his people: And I do not know what my followers used to do. I only have the apparent meaning of their affairs from them, not the hidden ones, and I have not obliged them to know their inner meaning, but I have only been tasked with the outward. Whoever appears good, I think of him as good, and whoever manifests bad, I think of him as bad.
