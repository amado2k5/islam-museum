## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "But it comes to them suddenly and they are confused, and they cannot repel it, nor do they look": This fire, which envelops the faces of these disbelievers described in this surah, does not come to them knowingly at its time, but it comes to them as a surprise they do not perceive its coming. "So they are stunned," meaning: it envelops them suddenly, and their faces become enveloped in a scene like a man staring at his face, until the stunned remains as if bewildered by it. "So they cannot repel it," meaning: they cannot bear it when it overwhelms them, and their faintness repels them. "Nor do they look," meaning: nor do they, even if they cannot repel it from themselves, delay the punishment with it for repentance they bring and repent, because it is not a time of action or a time of repentance and repentance, but an hour of reward and recompense.
