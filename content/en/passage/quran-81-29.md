## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 29 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And you do not will except that Allah, Lord of the worlds, wills”: “And you, O people, do not will to be upright on the truth, except that Allah wills it for you.” It was mentioned that the reason for which this verse was revealed was: On the authority of Sufyan, on the authority of Saeed bin Abdul Aziz, on the authority of Suleiman bin Musa, when the verse “For whoever wills among you to be upright” was revealed, Abu Jahl said: “That is up to us; if we will, we will be upright.” So the verse “And you do not will except that Allah, Lord of the worlds, wills” was revealed. On the authority of Sa`id ibn `Abd al-`Aziz, on the authority of Sulayman ibn Musa, who said: When this verse was revealed: “For whoever among you wills to take a straight path,” Abu Jahl said: “The matter is up to us; if we wish, we will take a straight path, and if we wish, we will not.” So God revealed: “And you do not will except that God, Lord of the worlds, wills.” The passage also includes statements attributed to Mujahid and Sufyan.
