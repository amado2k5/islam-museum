## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Pharaoh said, 'O you who filled it up, I have not known you of any god other than Me, so light for me two deceitful ones on the earth, so make for me a monument so that I may look at the God of Moses. Indeed, I think he is of the liars.'" Pharaoh said to the nobles and leaders of his people: "O nobles, I have not known for you any god other than Me," so worship him and believe the words of Moses in what he brought to you that you have a Lord other than Me and worship besides Me, "So kindle for me, O Haman, on the clay," meaning: He made a brick for me, and it was mentioned that he was the first to cook and build bricks with it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
