## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Why was this Qur'an not revealed to a man from the two cities, a great one?': These polytheists from Quraysh said when the Qur'an came to them from Allah: This is magic. If it were true, then why not it was revealed to a great man from one of these two cities, Mecca or Taif?" There was disagreement about the man they described as great, so they said: Why was this Qur'an revealed to him? Some said: Why not it was revealed to Al-Walid ibn al-Mughira al-Makhzumi from the people of Mecca, or Habib ibn Amr ibn Umair al-Thaqafi from the people of Taif? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
