## Summary
The first report in the chapter on how to deal with a nosebleed. Yahya transmits from Malik from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala al-Aslami: he saw Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab have a nosebleed, so that the blood came out until his fingers were stained with it from his nose, and then he prayed without performing ablution. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ma'mar from Abu al-Zinad from Ibn al-Musayyab, and Ibn Abi Shayba from Yahya ibn Sa'id from him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.80} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.81}
