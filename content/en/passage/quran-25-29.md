## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 31 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And the Day when the wrongdoer will bite his hand and say, 'I wish I had taken a way with the Messenger.'" He will say: "And the day the wrongdoer will bite himself with his Lord with regret and regret for neglecting his favor and resolving himself to disbelieve in Him in obedience to his beloved who prevented him from the way of his Lord." He will say: "Oh, I wish I had taken a way in this world with the Messenger, meaning a path to salvation from God's punishment." And the phrase "Woe to me, I wish I had not taken so-and-so as a close friend," interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase: "so-and-so." Some said: By the oppressor, I mean Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt, because he apostatized after embracing Islam, seeking the pleasure of Abu ibn Khalaf, and they said: So-and-so is my father. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ata.
