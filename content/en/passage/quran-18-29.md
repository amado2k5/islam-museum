## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And say, 'The truth is from your Lord. So whoever wills, let him believe; and whoever wills, let him disbelieve.' Indeed, We have prepared for the wrongdoers a Fire whose tents will surround them. And if they seek help, they will be relieved, with water like a moisture that roasts faces. Wretched is the drink, and evil is the companion." And say, O Muhammad, to those whose hearts We have made heedless of Our remembrance and who follow their desires: The truth, O people, is from your Lord, and to Him belongs success and misguidance. In His hand is guidance and misguidance. He guides whom He wills to righteousness, so he believes, and He misguides whom He wills from guidance and disbelieves. I have nothing of that. I am not a pursuer of your desires those who follow the truth, and by Allah and what was revealed to me, I believe. If you wish, then believe; and if you will, disbelieve. Indeed, if you disbelieve, your Lord has prepared for you against your disbelief a Fire whose tents will surround you. If you believe in Him and act upon His obedience, then you will have what Allah has described for those who obey Him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
