## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So the crowd said to those of his people who disbelieved, 'This is nothing but a human like yourselves who wants to bestow favor upon you. And if Allah had willed, He could have sent down angels. We have not heard of this concerning our forefathers.'" The group of the noble people of Noah, who denied and denied the oneness of Allah, said to their people: Noah, O people, is only a human like you; he is only a human like you and some of you. "He wants to show favor to you," meaning: He wants to have favor over you, so he becomes a follower and you follow him. "And if Allah willed, He would have sent down angels"—meaning: and if Allah had willed that we worship nothing besides Him, He would have sent angels, meaning: I would have sent angels to deliver his message to what Noah calls you to. And His saying: "We have not heard of this" which Noah calls us to, that we have no god but Allah in past centuries, and they were their early forefathers.
