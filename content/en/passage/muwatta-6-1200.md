## Summary
Malik says that whoever arrives at Mecca for the new moon of Dhu al-Hijja and enters ihram for the pilgrimage completes his prayer until he leaves Mecca for Mina, then shortens it, because he has resolved to stay more than four nights. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.539}
