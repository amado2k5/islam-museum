## Summary
Malik transmits from Umar ibn Husayn, from Aisha bint Qudama, from her father Qudama ibn Mazun, that he said: whenever I came to Uthman ibn Affan to collect my stipend, he asked me: do you have property on which zakat is due? If I said yes, he took the zakat on that property from my stipend; if I said no, he gave me my stipend. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.336}
