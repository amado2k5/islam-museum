## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He is Allah, there is no deity except Him, the Sovereign, the Holy, the Peace, the Faithful, the Sustainer, the Mighty, the Mighty, the Compeller, the Arrogant. Glory be to Allah above what they associate with Him": He is the deity to whom worship is not worthy except Him, the Sovereign above whom there is no king, and nothing besides Him, the Holy, it is said: He is the Blessed One. I have previously explained the meaning of sanctification with its evidences, and mentioned the differences among those who differ on it, which is sufficient to repeat it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
