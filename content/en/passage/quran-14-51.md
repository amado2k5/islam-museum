## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari concludes the segment that begins with verse 49: "That Allah may recompense every soul for what it earns," as an explanation for the criminals' admitting to the Fire chained in chains and their faces covered with fire: That is to repay every soul for what it has done in this world, of polytheism and sins. If good, it is good, and if evil, evil. And "Indeed, God is swift in reckoning": no one's account is distracted from another's, nor does He slow down His reckoning due to the large number of people He holds accountable. Al-Tabari detailed the parts of the verse in the two previous verses: the binding shackles, the garments made from camel tar, and it is said copper.
