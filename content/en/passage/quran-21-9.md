## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Then We made the promise true to them, and We saved them from whom We willed, and destroyed the transgressors": Then We confirmed Our messengers whom their nations denied and asked them for signs. So We gave them what they asked for of that, then they persisted in denying it and insisted on denying its prophethood after it had brought them of the signs of its Lord. Our promise to them of destruction is to keep them in disbelief in their Lord after the verse they asked for has come. This is like His saying, Glorified and Exalted: "So whoever among you disbelieves, I will surely punish him with a punishment I will not punish anyone among the worlds," and likewise: "And do not touch it with evil, lest a near punishment seize you," and similar promises that the nations have promised upon the coming of the verses. And His saying: "So We saved them," God Almighty says: So We saved the messengers when their nations insisted on denying them after the verses, "and whoever willed," and they are their followers who believed in them and believed in them. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
