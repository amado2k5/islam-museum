## Summary
Malik said: if this was unknown until the contract slave paid, or before he paid, the one who contracted him returns to his partner what he received from the contract slave, and they divide it according to their shares, and the contract is void, and he is a slave for them both in his former state. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.346}
