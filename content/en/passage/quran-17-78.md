## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Establish prayer for the setting of the sun until the darkness of the night and the Qur'an of dawn; indeed, the Qur'an of dawn was witnessed": "Establish prayer," O Muhammad, "for the setting of the sun." Interpreters differ regarding the time Allah meant by the setting of the sun; some said: it is the time of its setting, and the prayer commanded to be performed at that time is the Maghrib prayer. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
