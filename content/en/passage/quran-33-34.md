## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse "And remember what is recited in your houses of the verses of Allah and wisdom. Indeed, Allah is ever Subtle and Acquainted": Allah Almighty says to the wives of His Prophet Muhammad: "And remember the favor of Allah upon you by placing you in houses where the verses of Allah and wisdom are recited. So thank Allah for that, praise Him, and He meant by saying: 'And remember what is recited in your houses of the verses of Allah's verses and wisdom." By wisdom, he means: What was revealed to the Messenger of Allah of the rulings of the religion of Allah, and no Qur'an was revealed to it, and that is the Sunnah. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
