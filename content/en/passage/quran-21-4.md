## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "My Lord said, He knows the speech in the heavens and the earth, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing": The reciters differed in the recitation of His saying: "Say, my Lord," so the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra, and some Kufans, read it as "Say, my Lord" as the actual wording. Some reciters of Mecca and the general reciters of Kufa read it as "My Lord said" as a predicate form. It is as if those who read it in its proper context intended by its interpretation: Say, O Muhammad, to those who say, 'Do you come to magic while you see?' My Lord knows the words of every speaker in the heavens and the earth, nothing is hidden from Him, and He is the All-Hearer of all that and the lies they say. He is the Knower of my truthfulness and the truth of what I call you to, the falsehood of what you say, and all other things. It is as if those who read this said, in the form of the report, they meant: Muhammad said: My Lord knows the statement as a report from Allah about the answer of His Prophet to them.
