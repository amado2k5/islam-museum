## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Waqi'a 56:28, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «في سدر مخضود».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

فأعجبهم سدره، فقالوا: يا ليت لنا مثل هذا، فأنزل اللههذه الآية.

</div>
