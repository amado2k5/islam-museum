## Summary
A report of Aisha, through Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, in the chapter on the merit of gentleness in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. It makes gentleness a divine attribute for which God rewards more than He rewards violence. {cite:sahih-muslim}
