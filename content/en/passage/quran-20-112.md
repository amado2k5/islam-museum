## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And whoever does righteous deeds while he is a believer should not fear injustice or diminishment": Allah Almighty says: Whoever does righteous deeds, as it is said, fulfills the obligations of Allah which He has imposed upon His servants, "while he is a believer," meaning: he is affirming Allah, and that He is the one who accepts His obedience and sins for their sins. "So he does not fear injustice." He means: He does not fear Allah wronging him, so He attributes the sins of others to him, punishing him (nor digesting them). He means: He does not fear that his good deeds will be diminished, so his reward is diminished. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Samak.
