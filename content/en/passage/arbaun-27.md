## Summary
Two hadiths gathered by al-Nawawi in the twenty-seventh place. The first, from al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an: "Righteousness is good character, and sin is what troubles your soul and you dislike people seeing". The second, from Wabisa ibn Ma'bad: "Ask your heart; righteousness is what the heart is at rest with". Ibn Rajab reviews the chains and finds both weak in parts, though they have sound supporting reports. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
