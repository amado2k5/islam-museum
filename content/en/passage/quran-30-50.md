## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So look at the effects of Allah's mercy, how He gives life to the earth after its death. Indeed, that is the erasure of the dead, and He is over all things competent": The reciters differed in His saying: "So look at the effects of Allah's mercy." The majority of the reciters of Medina, Basra, and some Kufans read it as "to the effect of Allah's mercy" on monotheism, meaning: So look, O Muhammad, at the effect of the rain that Allah has brought to His servants, how that rain revives the earth after its death. The general recitation of Kufa reads this: "So look at the effects of God's mercy" on intercourse, meaning: look at the rain that God has bestowed upon those who have afflicted Him, how He revives the earth after its death.
