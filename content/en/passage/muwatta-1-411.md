## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Nafi' reported that Abdullah ibn Umar saw his son Ubayd Allah ibn Abdullah pray voluntary prayers on a journey and did not object, in the chapter on voluntary prayer on a journey. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.214} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.215}
