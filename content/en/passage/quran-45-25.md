## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when Our verses are recited to them in clear evidence, their argument is only that they say, 'Bring our forefathers if you should be truthful'": And when Our verses are recited to these polytheists who deny the resurrection, that Allah is the originator of His creation after their death, then He will gather them on the Day of Resurrection with Him for reward and punishment "clear and obvious" meaning clear and evident proofs that remove doubt from the hearts of those who believe in Allah in this matter: "Their proof was only that they said, 'Bring our forefathers if you should be truthful.'" He, the Exalted, says: They had no argument against our Messenger who recited this to them except that they said to him: Bring us our forefathers who perished alive, and spread them for us if you are truthful in what you recite to us and tell us, so that we may believe the truth of what you say, that God sold us alive after our destruction.
