## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, fear Allah and believe in His Messenger. He will grant you two bundles of His mercy and will make for you a light by which you walk, and He will forgive you. And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful": O you who have believed Allah and His Messenger from the People of the Two Scriptures, the Torah and the Gospel, fear Allah by obeying Him, avoiding His sins, and believe in His Messenger Muhammad. Also: My father told me, my uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas: 'O you who have believed, fear Allah and believe in His Messenger,' meaning those who have believed from among the People of the Book. I narrated from Al-Husayn, he said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say, regarding His saying: 'O you who have believed, fear Allah and believe in His Messenger,' meaning: those who believed from among the People of the Book. And His saying: "He will give you two doubles of His mercy" gives you double the reward for your faith in Jesus, and the prophets before Muhammad, and then your faith in Muhammad when he was sent as a prophet. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Sufyan, and Ata.
