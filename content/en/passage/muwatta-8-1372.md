## Summary
Yahya said Malik said about a man who says 'I disbelieve in God' or 'I associate others with God' and then breaks the oath: there is no expiation owed, and he is neither an unbeliever nor a polytheist unless his heart harbours polytheism and disbelief; let him ask God's forgiveness and not return to any such thing, though what he did was a bad thing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.613}
