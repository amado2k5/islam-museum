## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 27 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, he is but a man with Paradise, so wait for him for a while": meaning he mentioned him as an informant of the saying among the people of Noah who disbelieved "Indeed, he is but a man with Paradise": Noah was only a man with madness. It is also possible to say jinn Paradise, so the name and the verbal noun coincide, and "he" comes from the phrase: 'Indeed, it is a metaphor for the name Noah.' And His saying: 'So wait for him until a while' means: so you will remain with him and watch him until he says: until a certain time. They did not mean a known time by that; rather, it is like the saying of the one who says: leave it until a day, or until a certain time. And his saying: And in the Maqta' he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
