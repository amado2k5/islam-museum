## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The only saying of the believers when they were called to Allah and His Messenger to judge between them was to say, 'We hear and we obey, and those are the successful': The words of the believers when they were called to the judgment of Allah and His Messenger to judge between them" and their adversaries should have been "to say, 'We hear'" what was said to us, "and we obey" whoever called us to that. Bacan did not mean here the news about a matter that has passed and is to be decreed, but rather a rebuke from God, for whom this verse was revealed, and a punishment for others from it. And His saying: "And those are the successful," God Almighty says: "And those who, when called to God and His Messenger to judge between themselves and their adversaries, say: We hear and obey." "The successful": He means they are the successful ones who fulfill their desires by doing so, eternal in the gardens of God.
