## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They want to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths, and Allah will perfect His light, even if the disbelievers dislike it": These who say about Muhammad mean: this is a clear magician, "to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths," meaning: they want to nullify the truth with which Allah sent Muhammad with their mouths, meaning by saying he is a magician, and what he brought is magic. "By Allah, He perfects His light." He means: Allah is the Manifester of the Truth, the manifestation of His religion, and He supported Muhammad against those who oppose Him. That is the completion of His light, and in this context, he meant Islam. Ibn Zayd used to say: He meant by the Qur'an. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths," he said: the light of the Qur'an. Reciters differed in the recitation of the verse: "And Allah is the Perfect of His light," so the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra, and some Kufans, read it as "the completion of His light" in the accusative case. In the section, he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd.
