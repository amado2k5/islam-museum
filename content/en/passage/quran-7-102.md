**Commentary on verse 102 (pp. 10-11)**

Al-Tabari says: we did not find in most people of these destroyed townships "any covenant", that is, any fidelity to what God had charged them with: His oneness, following His messengers, acting in obedience, avoiding sins and abandoning the worship of idols. "Covenant" means a charge, as he has explained before. "We found most of them transgressors" means they were transgressors against their Lord's obedience, abandoning His covenant and charge. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "covenant" and "transgression". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 10}

He gives reports 14905 to 14908. Report 14905 is from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: "most of them" are the past generations. Report 14906 is from Mujahid, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: the past generations, and His covenant is the one He took from the children of Adam in Adam's back, which they did not fulfil. Report 14907 is from Ubayy ibn Ka'b: in the covenant He took in Adam's back. Report 14908 is from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: God destroyed the townships only because they did not keep what He charged them with. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 11}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
