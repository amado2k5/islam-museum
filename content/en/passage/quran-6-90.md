## Summary
Verse 90 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 90 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "those are they whom God guided, so follow their guidance" al-Tabari says: those, these people to whom we entrusted our signs and who do not disbelieve in them, are those whom God guided to his true religion, to keeping what they were charged with keeping of the verses of his book, to upholding its limits, following its lawful and forbidden, acting on what God's command is in it and refraining from what he forbids in it, and God, glorified be his praise, gave them success in that; "so follow their guidance"... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 518}

We stop here at the foot of p. 518, in the middle of al-Tabari's sentence on "so follow their guidance" in verse 90, and he continues on p. 519.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
