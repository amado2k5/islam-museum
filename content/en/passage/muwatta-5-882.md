## Summary
Ziyad reports from Malik that the one who is voluntarily in seclusion and the one for whom seclusion is obligatory have the same rule regarding what is permitted and forbidden to them; and it had not reached him that the Messenger of God's seclusion was anything but voluntary. The editor notes that Abu Musab (878) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.425}
