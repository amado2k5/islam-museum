## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 35 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And if you obey a human like yourselves, then indeed, you will be losers": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of the public among a people who are righteous to their people: "And if you feed a human like yourselves," then follow him and accept what he says and believe him. "Indeed," O people, "when you lose": He says: They said: Then you are being denied your chances of honor and elevation in this world by following it.
