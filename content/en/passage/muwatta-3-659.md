## Summary
A saying of Malik: the Sunna on which there is no dispute among us is that zakat is due on twenty dinars, as it is due on two hundred dirhams. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.336}
