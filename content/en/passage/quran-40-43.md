## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "There is no sin except that you invite me to Him; there is no call in this world or the Hereafter, and that we will return to Allah, and that the transgressors are the companions of the Fire." It is truly that the idol you call me to has no call in this world or the Hereafter, because he is inanimate objects that neither speak nor understand anything. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
