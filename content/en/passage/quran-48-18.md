## Summary

## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Indeed, Allah was pleased with the believers when they pledged allegiance to you under the tree, so He knew what was in their hearts, so He sent down tranquility upon them and rewarded them with a near victory." It means that Allah, O Muhammad, was pleased with the believers "when they pledged allegiance to you under the tree," meaning the pledge of allegiance to the companions of the Messenger of Allah, the Messenger of Allah, in Hudaybiyyah, when they pledged allegiance to him to fight Quraysh in battle, and not to flee or turn their backs under the tree. Their pledge to him was there, as mentioned, under a tree. The reason for this pledge was what was said: the Messenger of Allah sent Uthman ibn Affan with his message to the mullahs of Quraysh. Uthman delayed him somewhat, thinking he had been killed, so he called on his companions to renew the pledge against their war as described, so they pledged allegiance to him accordingly. This pledge is called the Pledge of Ridwan. Those who pledged allegiance to him were, as mentioned, one thousand four hundred, some saying five hundred, and some saying one thousand three hundred. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Ibn Ishaq.
