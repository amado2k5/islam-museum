## Summary
Verse 92 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 92 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-92|7:92]], al-Tabari says: He destroyed those who denied Shuayb and did not believe in him, wiped them out, and their town was left empty and desolate, "as if they had never dwelt in it", that is, as if they had never settled or lived there when they perished. One says "ghaniya fulan bi-makan kadha, yaghna bihi ghaniyan" when he settled and lived in a place, as in a line of Abid ibn al-Abras from his poem in which every half-line ends with the definite article. The editor adds lines of al-Ansari and al-Akhtal on dropping the nun of the masculine plural and a line of Ruba using "maghna". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 570}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
