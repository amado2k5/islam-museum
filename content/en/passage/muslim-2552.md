## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Umar in the Book of Righteousness and Kinship in Sahih Muslim, in the story of a Bedouin who met Ibn Umar on his donkey and was given the donkey and the turban, because his father had been a friend of Umar. It widens righteousness to include maintaining the ties of those who loved one's parents. {cite:sahih-muslim}
