## Summary
In the Book of the Stories of the Prophets, the chapter on the excellence of Maryam: two women who were not prophets are placed at the highest level of human perfection, and the excellence of Aisha is linked in its own chapter. It is one of the best-known hadiths on the ranks of women. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
