## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 55 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Do you have sexual relations with men of desire instead of women? Rather, you are a people who do not know": this is excluding the private parts of women whom Allah has permitted for you to marry, and His saying, "Rather, you are a people who do not know," He says: That is nothing but that of you, a foolish people ignorant of the great right of Allah over you, so you disobeyed His command and disobeyed His Messenger.
