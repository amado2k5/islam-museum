## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Ata ibn Yasar, that the Messenger of God said: nothing of prophethood will remain after me except the good tidings. They asked: what are the good tidings, Messenger of God? He said: a good dream that a righteous man sees, or is seen for him, is one part of forty-six parts of prophethood. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.546}
