## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Did We not cause you to be confused among us as a child and you stayed among us for years of your life?'" In this passage, it is omitted, which is self-sufficient to indicate what appears to it, which is: They came to Pharaoh and conveyed to him the message of their Lord to him. Pharaoh said: Did we not see you among us, O Moses, a child, and you stayed among us for years of your life? This is because he stayed with him before killing the one who killed the Copt, and you did what you did, meaning: the soul that was killed by the Copt. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
