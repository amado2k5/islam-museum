## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth, who took no son and has no partner in dominion, and created everything, so He decreed it": "Blessed is He who sent down the Furqan... Who owns the dominion of the heavens and the earth." "Al-hadhi" is an adjective of the first "who is," and both are in the nominative position. The first is by the phrase "blessed," and the second is attributed to it. By His saying: "To Him belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth," he means the One who has dominion over the heavens and the earth, executing His command and decree in all of them, and proceeding with all His rulings. He means: It is incumbent upon whoever is like this that the people of his kingdom and those in his authority obey him and do not disobey him. He says: So do not disobey My warner to you, O people, and follow him and act according to what He brought you of the truth. "And He did not take a son" means a denial of the one who attributed a child to him. And the angels are the daughters of God: He who sent down the Furqan upon His servant did not take a son. Whoever attributes a child to him has lied and slandered his Lord.
