## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 80–81. Al-Tabari explains that the One who first created the bones of the decayed bones revives them, and He is the One who made the green tree fire, so nothing can overcome Him. He is the One who created the heavens and the earth, and He is able to create the like of them, and He is the All-Knowing, Creator.
