## Summary
Malik transmits from al-Ala ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Yaqub, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet forbade steeping in the gourd and in the bitumen-lined jar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.411}
