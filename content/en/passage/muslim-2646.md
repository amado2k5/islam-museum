## Summary
A report of Ibn Masud, supported through Amir ibn Wathila by Hudhayfa ibn Usayd's hearing from the Prophet, in the chapter on how the human is created and the beginning of the matter in the Book of Destiny of Sahih Muslim. Wretchedness and happiness are written in the womb. {cite:sahih-muslim}
