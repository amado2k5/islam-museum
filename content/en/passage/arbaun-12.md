## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: "Part of the goodness of a person's Islam is leaving what does not concern him". Its chain is disputed, and Ibn Rajab calls it a great principle of good manners, which includes leaving unlawful, doubtful and reprehensible matters and superfluous permitted ones. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
