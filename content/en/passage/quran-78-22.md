## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22-26 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Indeed, Hell has been lying in wait" as meaning that God, may He be exalted, is saying that Hell has been lying in wait for its inhabitants, those who denied it in this world and the return to God in the Hereafter, as well as for those who believed in it. The meaning is that Hell was ever watchful, observing and lying in wait for those who crossed it. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Sufyan.
