**Commentary on verse 96 (pp. 204-205)**

On [[passage/quran-10-96|10:96]] al-Tabari says: those against whom, Muhammad, your Lord's word has been fulfilled, which is His curse on them as in [[passage/quran-11-18|11:18]], so that it became fixed on them; one says 'such a thing is due upon so-and-so' when it is fixed on him and is binding. Report 17895, from Mujahid, and report 17896, from Qatada: God's anger became due on them for their disobedience. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'due'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 205}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
