## Summary
A saying of Malik about one who makes the lesser pilgrimage from al-Tanim: he stops the talbiya when he sees the House. The editor notes that in copy M 'entered ihram' appears instead of 'made the lesser pilgrimage', and that Abu Musab (1123) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.461}
