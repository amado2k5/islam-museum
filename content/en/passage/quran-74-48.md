## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46-49 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "So the intercession of intercessors will not benefit them" as meaning that those whom God has allowed to intercede for the sinners among the monotheists will not benefit from their intercession. This verse clearly indicates that God, may He be exalted, allows some of His creation to intercede for others. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
