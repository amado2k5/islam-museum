## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 97 to 100 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And he said, 'Indeed, I am going to my Lord, He will guide them.'" And when Abraham was paralyzed over his people and saved him from their scheme, he said: "I am going to my Lord," meaning: I am migrating from the town of my people to God, meaning to the Holy Land and to leave them, so I withdraw from them to worship God. Qatadah used to say about this: And from Qatadah: "And he said, 'I am going to my Lord, He will guide me.'" means he goes with his deeds, heart, and intention. Others said about this: Abraham only said, 'Indeed, I am going to my Lord,' meaning they would throw him into the Fire. In the passage, statements are transmitted from Qatadah and Ibrahim.
