## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 24 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We certainly sent Moses with Our signs and clear authority": God Almighty says, amusing His Prophet Muhammad about what he was receiving from the polytheists of his people from Quraysh, by informing him of the denial Moses received from those sent to him, and his informant that he was a superior to them and making the circle of evil difficult for those who were harsh and difficult, like his practice with Moses, when he destroyed his enemy Pharaoh. "And We certainly sent Moses with Our signs": meaning with his evidences. "And a clear authority," as in Qatadah, "and a clear authority": meaning he is excused. He says: and his clear proofs for those who see them as a proof that confirms what Moses calls for, and he quotes in the passage from Qatadah.
