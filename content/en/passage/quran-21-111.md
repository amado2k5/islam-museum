## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 110 to 111 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And if I know, perhaps it is a trial for you and a provision for a time": Perhaps what is closer to you of punishment and the Hour is that it will be delayed for your time and enjoyment for a while, so my words become a trial for you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ata.
