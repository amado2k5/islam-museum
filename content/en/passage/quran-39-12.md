## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 13 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And I was commanded to be the first of the Muslims": He says: My Lord, Glorified and Exalted, commanded me to do this, so that by doing so, I would be the first among you to embrace Islam, submit to Him in monotheism, dedicate my worship to Him, and disassociate myself from all other gods below Him. And His saying, the Exalted: "Say, I fear if I disobey my Lord the punishment of a great Day": He said: He said to them, "O Muhammad, indeed I fear if I disobey my Lord in what He commands me to worship, sincerely obeying Him, and singularly dedicating to Him in Lordship." "The punishment of a great day": meaning the punishment of the Day of Resurrection, that is the day that will be magnified for it.
