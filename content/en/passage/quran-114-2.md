## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind": Say, O Muhammad, seek refuge in "the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind," and He is the Sovereign of all creation: humans, jinn, and others, informing him of this: those who honored people and the believers their Lord, informing him that He is the King of those who honor Him, and that this is in His kingdom and authority, His power flows over Him, and that He is more worthy of reverence and more deserving of worship than those who honor and worship Him than others. And his saying: In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
