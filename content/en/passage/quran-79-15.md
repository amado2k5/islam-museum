## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 18 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Has there come to you the story of Moses?”: Has the story of Moses, son of Imran, reached you, O Muhammad? Have you heard of him when his Lord spoke to him in the sacred valley? By “sacred,” he means purified and blessed. We have already mentioned the opinions of scholars on this matter, so there is no need to repeat them here. We have also explained the meaning of the word “Tuwa” and what the scholars of interpretation have said about it, but we will mention some of that here. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the word “Tuwa.” Some said it is the name of the valley. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
