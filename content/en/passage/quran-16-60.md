## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "For those who do not believe in the Hereafter is an evil example, and to Allah is the highest example, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise": This is a report from Allah, Glorified and Exalted, that His saying: "And when one of them is given glad tidings of a female, his face will be darkened and he is oppressed," and the following verse illustrates Allah as a punishment for those polytheists who have made daughters for Allah. He clarified by His saying: "For those who do not believe in the Hereafter is the evil like," it is an example, and He meant by His saying, Glorified and Exalted: "For those who do not believe in the Hereafter" for those who do not believe in the Hereafter, reward, and punishment among the polytheists, "the bad example," which is the ugliest example, and what is bad is the one for whom that example is made. "And to Allah belongs the highest example." He says: And to Allah belongs the highest example, which is the best, the best, the best, and most beautiful, and that is monotheism and submission to Him that there is no god but Him. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
