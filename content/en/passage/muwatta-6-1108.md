## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz offered a camel during the pilgrimage or the lesser pilgrimage. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1201) and Suwayd (523) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.509}
