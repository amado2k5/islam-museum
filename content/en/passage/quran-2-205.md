## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and when he turns away he strives in the land to spread corruption": by "tawalla" al-Tabari means this hypocrite turns his back and leaves you, Muhammad. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 237} From Ibn 'Abbas: when he goes out from you, he strives (3980); some said it means when he is angry, from Ibn Jurayj (3981). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 237} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 238} Al-Tabari takes the sense to be that when this hypocrite leaves you angry he acts in the land in what God forbade him, seeking disobedience of God, highway robbery and the corruption of the road for God's servants, as was recounted of al-Akhnas ibn Shariq al-Thaqafi, about whom al-Suddi said the verse was revealed, burning the Muslims' crops and killing their donkeys. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 238} "Sa'y" in Arabic speech is work, and he cites a verse of al-A'sha about Qays ibn Ma'dikarib al-Kindi, with the editor noting the correct form in the Diwan. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 238} From Mujahid: "sa'a" means he worked (3982). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 238}
