## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "I have only been commanded to worship the Lord of this city, who has forbidden it and to Him everything, and I have been commanded to be among the Muslims": O Muhammad, "Say, I have only been commanded to worship the Lord of this city," which is Mecca, "the One who has forbidden it." It is forbidden for His creation to shed unlawful blood, or to wrong anyone, or hunt its game, or abandon its place except for the idols you worship, O polytheists.
