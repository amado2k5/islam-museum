## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40-41 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Ask them which of them is responsible for that" as: Ask, O Muhammad, these polytheists which of them has oaths binding upon us, binding them to their judgment until the Day of Resurrection. "Responsible" means: one who guarantees it. The word "responsible" among the Arabs refers to the guarantor and spokesperson for the group, as in: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, on the authority of his father, on the authority of Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase "which of them is responsible for that," he says: Which of them is responsible for that? And on the authority of Qatadah, regarding the phrase "Ask them which of them is responsible for that," he says: Which of them is responsible for that? And he quotes in the section statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
