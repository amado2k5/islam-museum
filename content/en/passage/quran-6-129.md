## Summary
Verse 129 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 129 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 129, "thus We make some of the wrongdoers allies of others for what they used to earn", the commentators differed on "nuwalli". Some said We make some of them allies of others in unbelief in God; he gives (13893) Qatada: God makes allies among people according to their deeds, so the believer is the ally of the believer wherever he is and the unbeliever the ally of the unbeliever wherever he is; faith is not by wishing nor by adornment. Others said it means We make some follow others in the Fire, from "muwalat", succession, given (13894) from Qatada. Others said We give some wrongdoers power over others; he gives (13895) Ibn Zayd: the wrongdoers of the jinn and of mankind, who recited "whoever turns away from the remembrance of the Merciful, We assign to him a devil" (43:36) and said We give power to the wrongdoers of the jinn over those of mankind. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 119}

Al-Tabari's choice: the soundest saying is that of those who said the meaning is that We make some of the wrongdoers allies of others. Before this verse God had mentioned the idolaters' saying "our Lord, some of us profited from others" and reported that some of them are allies of others, and then followed this with a report that their being allies of one another is through His placing them so. So as We made some of these idolaters among jinn and mankind allies of one another, profiting from one another, so We make some of them allies of others in all matters, "for what they used to earn" of disobedience to God that they commit. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 120}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
