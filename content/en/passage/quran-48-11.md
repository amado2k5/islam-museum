## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The Bedouins will say to you, 'You have occupied us with our wealth and our families, so ask forgiveness for us.' They will say with their tongues what is not in their hearts. Say, 'Who can grant you anything from Allah if He intends harm for you or intends benefit for you? Rather, Allah is Acquainted with what you do.'" He will say to you, O Muhammad, those whom Allah left behind among their families about your companionship, your departure on your journey, and your journey that you set out for Mecca on Umrah, visiting the Sacred House of Allah when you returned to them, and you reproached them for Neglecting you, you have distracted us from going out with you to manage our wealth and improve our livelihoods and our families. So ask our Lord for forgiveness for our delay in you. Allah, Glorified and Exalted, said, denying their claim of this: These Bedouins who have left you say with their tongues what is not in their hearts, and that is what the Messenger of Allah asked for forgiveness for them. He said: They ask Him without repentance from them and no regret for their previous disobedience to Allah in their neglecting the companionship of the Messenger of Allah and traveling with him. "Say, 'So who can grant you anything against Allah?'" Allah says to His Prophet: Tell those Bedouins who ask you to ask forgiveness for them because you are left behind, and in the passage he quotes sayings from Mujahid and Ibn Ishaq.
