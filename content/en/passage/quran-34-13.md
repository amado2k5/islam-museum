## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "They make for him whatever He wills of mihrabs, statues, and lids, like the prayer girdles and pots of the pillars. Do it, O family of David, in thanks, and a few of My grateful servants": This means: the jinn make for Solomon whatever He wills of mihrabs, which are the plural of a mihrab. The mihrab is the front of every mosque, house, and prayer place. From this is the saying of 'Adi ibn Zayd: 'Like ivory in the mihrabs' or like white flowers in the meadow, its flower is illuminated. He also quotes in the Maqta' from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Dahhak, al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
