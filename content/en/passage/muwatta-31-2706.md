## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr, from Abbad ibn Tamim, that Abu Bashir al-Ansari told him that he was with the Messenger of God on one of his journeys; the Messenger sent a messenger: let no necklace of cord or any necklace remain on a camel's neck without being cut. Yahya said: I heard Malik say: I think this is about the evil eye. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.526}
