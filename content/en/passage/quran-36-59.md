## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 59 to 61 in a single segment. He interprets the phrase "and they distinguished yourselves today, O criminals": meaning: by saying: "and they distinguished themselves," which is invented, from 'mazāzī ymizāzī' (to distinguish yāmāzī), a verb that distinguishes from it: to distinguish yāmāzī yātāzī. In the māṣāṇa, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
