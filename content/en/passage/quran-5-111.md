## Summary
Verse 111 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 111 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "when I inspired the disciples: believe in me and in my messenger; they said we believe, and bear witness that we are Muslims" al-Tabari says: remember also, Jesus, when I cast it to the disciples, who were Jesus' helpers in his religion; he has explained earlier what the word means and why they were called disciples. The interpreters' wording of "I inspired" differs though the sense agrees. Report 12992, from al-Suddi: I cast it into their hearts; others said: I inspired them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 217}

The editor refers for "inspired" and "the disciples" to earlier volumes 9 and 6.

So the sense is: when I put it to the disciples that they trust in me and in my messenger Jesus, and they said we believe, that is, we accept what you commanded us to believe, our Lord, "and bear witness" against us "that we are Muslims", that is, that we are submissive to you in humility, hearing and obeying your command. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 218}

Verse 111 closes here at p. 218, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-5-112|5:112]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
