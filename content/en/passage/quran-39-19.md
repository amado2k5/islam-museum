## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Is it upon whom the word of punishment is justified? Will you save those in the Fire?" meaning: He means by saying: "Is there anyone upon whom the word of punishment is deserved before your Lord, O Muhammad, by disbelief in him?" as in Qatadah's statement: "Is the one upon whom the word of punishment is justified?" by his disbelief. And His saying: "Will you save those in the Fire?" Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: "Will You save, O Muhammad, the one who is in the Fire who deserves the word of punishment? You will save him." He sufficed with the phrase: "Save those in the Fire" from this. Some grammarians from Kufa used to say: This is meant as a single question, so the interrogation is preceded by a different place, and the interrogation is returned to its place that belongs to it. The meaning, and Allah knows best, is: Will you save the one in the Fire upon whom the word of punishment is deserved? And he said: The same without the interrogative word: He repeated "innakum" twice. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
