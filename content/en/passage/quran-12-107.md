**Commentary on verse 107 (p. 290)**

On "do they feel secure that a covering of God's punishment will come upon them, or that the Hour will come upon them suddenly while they are unaware?", al-Tabari says: do those who do not acknowledge God as their Lord except while associating others with Him in their worship of Him, feel secure "that a covering of God's punishment will come upon them", covering them with God's chastisement and punishment for their shirk, or that the Resurrection will come upon them suddenly while they persist in their shirk and their disbelief in their Lord, and God, mighty and exalted, makes them abide in His fire while they do not know of its coming and arrival. The editor refers to the earlier discussions of "ghashiya", "sa'a" and "bagh'a". The interpreters said the same: reports 19974 to 19977, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim and Isa; al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Shababa and Waraqa; al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl; and Ishaq, Abd Allah and Waraqa, all from Ibn Abi Najih: "a covering of God's punishment", he said, it covers them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 290}

We stop here at the foot of p. 290, in the commentary on verse 107 (after report 19977); the next batch begins at p. 291. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 290}
