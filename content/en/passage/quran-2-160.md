## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "except those who repent, set right and make clear; those I turn toward, for I am the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful" al-Tabari says: God and the cursers curse those who conceal what they know of Muhammad's prophethood and description, except one who turned back from that concealment and returned in repentance by believing in Muhammad, acknowledging his prophethood and affirming him, set his own state right through righteous deeds, and made clear what he knew of God's revelation without concealing it; those I turn toward, "and I am the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful", who brings back the hearts of His servants who turned away from Him and has mercy on those who turn to Him, pardoning the great wrongs they committed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 259} To the question of how one who has repented is turned toward, he answers that neither the repenting nor the one turned toward is without the other. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 259} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 260} From Qatada, they set right what is between them and God and made clear what came to them from God without concealing it; from Ibn Zayd, they explained what is in God's book to the believers and what they asked them about the Prophet, and all this concerns the Jews. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 260} He notes that some claimed "made clear" means they made their repentance clear by sincerity of deed, and rejects it since the reproach was for concealing what God sent down about Muhammad; he says those God excepted are 'Abd Allah ibn Salam and his like among the People of the Book who became Muslims and did so well and followed the Messenger of God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 260} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 261}
