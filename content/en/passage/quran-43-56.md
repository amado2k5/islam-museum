## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 57 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "So We made them predecessors and an example for others": The reciters differed in reading this, so most of the reciters of Kufa, excluding 'Asim, read it as 'So We made them predecessors' with a damma on the sin and lam, referring from them to a predecessor of people, which is the preceding plural of people. Al-Farra' narrated that he heard al-Qasim ibn Ma'n mention that he heard the Arabs say: 'A predecessor from the people' has passed. The general recitation of the reciters of Medina and Basra, and Asim read: 'So We made them predecessors' with a fatha on the 's' and 'lam'. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
