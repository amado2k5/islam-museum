**Commentary on verse 149: pardoning evil and showing good (pp. 350-352)**

Al-Tabari says the sense of 'if you show good or hide it, or pardon evil' is: if you speak kindly to one who has done you good and show your thanks, or leave off showing it; or if you pardon one who wronged you and do not call out evil against him. God is forgiving and powerful: He has always pardoned His creatures and overlooks those who disobey Him, though He is able to take vengeance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 350}

So you too, people, pardon one who wrongs you, do not call out evil against him even though you can harm him, just as your Lord pardons you though He is able to punish you while you disobey Him and oppose His command. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 351}

Al-Tabari finds a clear indication in this verse that the reading 'God does not love public evil, except from one who was wronged' is not as Zayd ibn Aslam took it, that it means God does not love public evil against the hypocrites except from one who persists in hypocrisy. For it is not sensible that believers should be told to pardon hypocrites for their hypocrisy, nor forbidden to call a hypocrite by his name: pardon is for a right one has against another, and naming a hypocrite by his name is no right of anyone to be pardoned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 351}
