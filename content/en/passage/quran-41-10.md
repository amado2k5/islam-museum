## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And He placed therein mountains above it, blessed therein, and determined therein its sustenance in four days, equal for those who ask": And He placed on the earth which He created in two days mountains of steeples, which are the fixed places on the earth above it, meaning: from above the earth on its back. And His saying: "And blessed it" means: And blessed the earth and made it everlasting goodness for its people. It was mentioned from Al-Suddi in this regard: And from Al-Suddi: "And blessed it," he said: He made its trees grow. "And measured in it its sustenance." The interpreters differed on this; some said: He estimated in it the sustenance of its people meaning their livelihood and livelihood. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
