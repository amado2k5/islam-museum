## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abdullah ibn Mas'ud used to walk bowing until he reached the row, in the chapter on what a person does when he comes while the imam is bowing. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq reported from Qatada Ibn Mas'ud's saying that there is no harm in bowing before the row, and from Zayd ibn Wahb that he entered the mosque with Ibn Mas'ud while the imam was bowing, and they bowed, then walked until they were level in the row. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.233}
