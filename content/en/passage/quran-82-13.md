## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 13 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Indeed, the righteous will be in bliss”: God Almighty says: Those who are righteous in fulfilling God’s obligations and avoiding His prohibitions will be in the bliss of Paradise, enjoying its delights. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
