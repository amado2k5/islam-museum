## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 55 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And turn to your Lord and submit to Him before the punishment comes to you and then you will not be helped": "And turn to your Lord in repentance, return to Him in obedience, respond to Him in what He calls you to in monotheism, dedication to divinity to Him, and sincere worship of Him," as Qatadah said: "And turn to your Lord," meaning turn to your Lord. And from Al-Suddi, "and repent," he said: respond. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "And turn to your Lord," he said: Turning back means returning to obedience and turning away from what they used to be. Do you not see him saying: "We repent to Him and fear Him," and His saying: "And submit to Him," meaning: submit to Him in obedience and acknowledgment of the upright religion "before the punishment comes to you" from Him for your disbelief in Him, "then you will not be helped," meaning: Then no helper will help you, and He will save you from His punishment that will befall you. In the Muqta', he quotes from Qatadah, Al-Suddi, and Ibn Zayd.
