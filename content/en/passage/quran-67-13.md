## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 13 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And conceal your speech or publicize it; indeed, He is Knowing of that within the breasts”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: Conceal your speech and words, O people, or declare them and make them public, “Indeed, He is Knowing of that within the breasts.” He says: He is All-Knowing of the secrets of the breasts that have not been spoken, so how about what has been uttered and spoken, whether concealed or public, because He from Whom the secrets of the breasts are not hidden, then other things are even less likely to be hidden from Him.
