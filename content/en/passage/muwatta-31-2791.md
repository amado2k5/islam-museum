## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd al-Tawil, from Anas ibn Malik, that the Prophet was cupped, and Abu Tayba cupped him; he ordered him a sa' of dates, and ordered his family to lighten his tax on him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.568}
