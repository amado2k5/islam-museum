## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 34 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And who is better in speech than one who calls to Allah and does righteous deeds and says, 'Indeed, I am of the Muslims?': And who is the best in speech, O people, among those who say, 'Our Lord is Allah,' then firmly believes in Him, adheres to His command and prohibition, and calls the servants of Allah to what he said and acted upon." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
