## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "They only look at one cry that seizes them while they are quarrelling": These polytheists, who hasten to warn them, await only one shout to seize them, which is the breath of terror at the Hour of Judgment. In this passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
