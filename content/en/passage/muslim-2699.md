## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Remembrance of Sahih Muslim: a comprehensive text that begins with relieving distress, passes through seeking knowledge and the gatherings of Qur'an recitation. It is among the greatest texts on the merit of knowledge. {cite:sahih-muslim}
