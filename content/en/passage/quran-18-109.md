## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, if the sea were ink for the words of my Lord, the sea would run out before the words of my Lord run out": The Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: "Say," O Muhammad: "If the sea were ink," the pen with which "the words of my Lord are written, the water of the sea would run out" before the words of my Lord run out. And if We could bring the like of it as reinforcement, he says: "And if We extended the sea with the like of the water in it, it would be extended," as in the saying: I have come to you as support, and that is the meaning of increase. It has been mentioned from some of them: 'If we were to bring a similar amount of ink, it is as if the reader of that would have meant: the sea would run out before the words of my Lord run out, and if we were to add the same ink with which it is written with ink.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
