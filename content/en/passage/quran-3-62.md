## Summary
Verse 62 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 62 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of verse 62.** Al-Tabari says what God told the Prophet of Jesus, that he is His servant, messenger and word and a spirit from Him, is the true narrative and report, and that there is no object of worship deserving worship from creation by virtue of owning them except the Prophet's own object of worship. He is the Almighty in taking vengeance on whoever disobeys Him and claims another god with Him or worships another lord, the Wise in His ordering, which no weakness enters and no flaw reaches. The editor refers to earlier explanations of "mighty" and "wise". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 476}

**Reports 7176 to 7179.** Report 7176 (Ibn Humayd, from Salama, from Ibn Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr): the account you brought of Jesus is the truth about him. Report 7177 (al-Qasim, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Ibn Jurayj): what We have said of Jesus is the true account. Report 7178 (Yunus, from Ibn Wahb, from Ibn Zayd): it is not for Jesus to go beyond this and be other than God's word and a spirit from Him and God's servant and messenger. Report 7179 (Muhammad ibn Saad, from his father, from his uncle, from his father, from his father, from Ibn Abbas): what We have said of Jesus is the truth. The editor notes that 7176 continues, in Ibn Hisham, the reports that ended with 7172, and that the manuscript reading is correct at one point in 7178. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 476} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 477} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 478}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
