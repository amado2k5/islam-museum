**Commentary on verse 82 (p. 162)**

On [[passage/quran-10-82|10:82]] al-Tabari says God reports Moses telling the magicians: 'God verifies the truth', that is, God establishes the truth I have brought you from Him, raising it over your falsehood and confirming it, 'by His words', meaning His command, 'even if the criminals dislike it', meaning those who earned sin against their Lord by disobeying Him. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'verifies the truth by His words' and 'sinful toward his Lord'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 162}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
