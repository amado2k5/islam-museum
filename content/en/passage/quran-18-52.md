## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And on the Day when He will say, 'Call upon My partners whom you claimed, but they call upon them, but they will respond to them and We will place between them a barrier": Allah Almighty will say, "And the Day He will say" Allah, the Exalted, to the polytheists with Him, the gods and the opponents, "Call upon My partners whom you claimed," He will say to them: Call upon those whom you claimed to be My partners in worship to support you and prevent you from me. "So they called upon them, but they did not respond to them." He means: They called for help from them, but they did not help them. "And We placed between them a destroyer." The interpreters differed on the meaning of this, and some said: Its meaning is: And We placed enmity among these polytheists, and what they used to call upon besides Allah, partners in the world that day. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and others.
