## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who lower their voices before the Messenger of Allah are the ones whose hearts Allah has tested for piety; for them is forgiveness and a great reward": those who suffice to raise their voices before the Messenger of Allah. The root of 'ghaddh' is softness. Among them is: lowering the gaze, which is refraining from looking, as Jarir said: 'Lower your gaze, indeed you are from Namir, neither a heel nor a dog.' And His saying: 'Those are the ones whose hearts Allah has tested for piety,' Allah says: These are the ones whose voices are lowered before the Messenger of Allah. They are the ones whose hearts Allah tested by testing them, so He chose and purified them for piety, meaning to avoid Him by performing His obedience and avoiding sins, just as gold is tested by fire, purifying its good and nullifying its impurity. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
