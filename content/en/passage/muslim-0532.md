## Summary
A report of Jundub ibn Abdullah al-Bajali in the Book of Prayer of Sahih Muslim, which he heard from the Prophet five days before his death. It equates Abu Bakr's special love for the Prophet with the Prophet's divine friendship, and the text continues, "The one who has shown me most gratitude in companionship and wealth is Abu Bakr". {cite:sahih-muslim}
