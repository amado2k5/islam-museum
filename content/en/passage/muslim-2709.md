## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on seeking refuge in God's perfect words, in the Book of Remembrance and Supplication of Sahih Muslim: the man stung by a scorpion who said that the evening seeking of refuge had protected him. {cite:sahih-muslim}
