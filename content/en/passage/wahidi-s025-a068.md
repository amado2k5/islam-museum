## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Furqan 25:68, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «والذين لا يدعون مع الله إلها آخر».
