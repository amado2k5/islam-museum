## Summary
The first text in the chapter on what carries no zakat from fruit, fresh herbs and vegetables. Malik says: the matter on which there is no dispute among us, and what he heard from people of knowledge, is that there is no charity on any fruit at all: pomegranates, apricots, figs and the like, and anything else that counts as fruit. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.372}
