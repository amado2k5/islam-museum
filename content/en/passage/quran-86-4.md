## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-10 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Indeed, every soul has a guardian over it”: The reciters differed in their reading of this. Abu Ja’far, from the reciters of Medina, and Hamza, from the reciters of Kufa, read it as “lamma ‘alayha” with a shaddah on the mim. It was mentioned that al-Hasan also read it this way. And it was narrated from Harun, on the authority of al-Hasan, that he used to read it as “Inn kullu nafsin lama ‘alayha hafiz” with a shaddah, and he would say: “except that it has a guardian over it,” and thus everything in the Qur’an is read with a shaddah. 1 2 3 4 5 6 He then quotes in this section the sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
