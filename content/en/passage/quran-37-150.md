## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 150 to 152 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Or did We create the angels female while they are witnesses": meaning: Or did those polytheists who say: The angels are daughters of Allah who created the angels and I created them females? They witnessed this testimony and described the angels as female. And His saying: 'Indeed, they are the ones who fabricated them,' Allah says: 'Indeed, these polytheists, among their lies, 'will say, 'He is the son of Allah,' and they are liars.' In their statement, as in Qatadah, and in the passage he quotes from Qatadah.
