## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Thawr ibn Zayd al-Dili from Abdullah ibn Abbas: he was asked about the slaughter of the Christian Arabs and said there is no harm in it, and recited a verse of Surat al-Ma'ida about allying with the People of the Book. The editor notes that the chapter here is about the slaughter. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.631}
