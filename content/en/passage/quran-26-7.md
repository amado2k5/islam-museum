## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Have they not seen to the earth how much We have grown in it of every noble spouse": or have these polytheists who deny the resurrection and spread to the earth not seen how much We have grown there after it was dead and no plants in it? "From every generous spouse" means the noble, just as a good palm tree is called a noble lamb, and as it is said of a sheep or she-camel when they are abundant and their milk increases: a noble she-camel and a noble sheep. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
