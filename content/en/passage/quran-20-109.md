## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 109 to 110 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "on that Day when intercession will only benefit the one whom the Most Merciful permits and is pleased with": Allah Almighty says, "On that Day, intercession will only benefit from the intercession of the one to whom the Most Merciful permits" means to intercede "and be pleased with his word." He also included in his speech evidence to add the word to the metaphor of "who," like the other saying: "I am pleased with your deeds and pleased with you." The place of "except for those who are permitted" is accusative because it is the opposite of intercession. And His saying, "He knows what is before them and what is behind them," the Exalted says: "Your Lord, O Muhammad, knows what is before those who follow the caller regarding the matter of the Resurrection, and what reward and punishment they will attain," "and what is behind them." He means: "And He knows what they left behind in worldly matters," as from Qatadah: "He knows what is before them" regarding the matter of the Hour and "and what is behind them" of worldly matters. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
