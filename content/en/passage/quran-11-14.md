**Commentary on verse 14 (pp. 261-262)**

On [[passage/quran-11-14|11:14]] al-Tabari says God tells His prophet: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists: if those you call besides God do not answer you to bring ten surahs like this Qur'an, invented, and you and they are unable to produce that, then know and be certain that it was sent down from heaven on Muhammad only by God's knowledge and leave, that Muhammad did not invent it nor could he, 'and that there is no god but He', that is, be certain also that there is no deity who deserves divinity over creation but God, to whom creation and command belong, so cast off rivals and gods and make worship His alone. It has also been said that 'if they do not answer you' is God's address to His prophet, as if He said: if these disbelievers do not answer you, Muhammad, then know, polytheists, that it was sent down by God's knowledge; al-Tabari calls that an interpretation far from what is understood. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 261}

His words 'will you then submit?' mean will you yield to God in obedience and make worship pure for Him, after the proof has been established against you? Mujahid used to say that the Companions of Muhammad are meant by this saying, and reports 18009, 18010 and 18011, from Mujahid, say: to the Companions of Muhammad. It says 'if they do not answer you' in the plural though the address at the start was to one, 'say, bring', and not 'if they do not answer you', in line with what al-Tabari showed before about addressing the head of a people and the master of their affair: the Arabs sometimes make his address like that of a plural, since to address him is to address his followers and troops, and sometimes like that of a single person, since he is one in himself. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 262}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
