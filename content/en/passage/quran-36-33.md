## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 34 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And a sign for them is the dead earth; We revived it and brought forth from it love, so from it they may eat": This is an indication for these polytheists of Allah's power over whatever He wills, and of His resurrection of those who died from His creation and return him after his destruction, as he was before his death, reviving the dead earth, which neither grows nor cultivates with the rain He sends down from the sky until its crops come out, then He brings forth from it grain which is food and nourishment, from which they eat. And His saying: "And We made therein gardens of palm trees and grapes," means: And We made in this land which We revived after its death gardens of palm trees and grapes, "and We caused springs to gush forth therein," meaning: And We caused springs to gush forth therein from springs.
