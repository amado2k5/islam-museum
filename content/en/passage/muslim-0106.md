## Summary
A report of Abu Dharr in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. The Prophet recited the verse three times, and Abu Dharr said, "They are lost and ruined, who are they, Messenger of God?" He named them: those who trail their garments in pride, those who give only to remind, and those who sell goods with false oaths. {cite:sahih-muslim}
