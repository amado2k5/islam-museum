## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "A parable is set forth for you from yourselves. Do you have of those who share what your right hands possess in what We have provided you? But you are equal in it. You fear them as you fear yourselves. Thus We detail the verses for a people who reason": An example for you, O people, is your Lord an example from yourselves. "Do you have from what your right hands possess?" meaning: "Among your dominions are partners," "in what We have provided you" of wealth, "and you are equal in it," and they are. He says: If you are not pleased with that for yourselves, then how can you accept that your gods, whom you worship Me, are partners in your worship of Me, while you are My servants and Mamluks, and I am the owner of all of you? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
