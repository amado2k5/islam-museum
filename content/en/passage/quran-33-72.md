## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, We offered the trust to the heavens and the earth and the mountains, but they refused to bear it and feared it. But man undertook it. Indeed, he was unjust and ignorant": interpreters differed on the meaning of this. Some said: It means that God presented His obedience and obligations to the heavens, the earth, and the mountains as if they were good and rewarded, and if they were wronged, they were punished. They refused to bear it out of compassion and not fulfilling their duties, and Adam carried it "indeed, he was unjust" to himself, "ignorant" of what was destined for him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Al-Dhahhak.
