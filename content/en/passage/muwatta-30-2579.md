## Summary
Yahya said: Malik said about a group of people who have a number, who are accused of blood, and whose oaths the heirs of the victim return to them: each one of them swears fifty oaths for himself; the oaths are not divided by their number, and they are not cleared until each of them swears fifty oaths for himself. Malik said: this is the best I have heard on this. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.455}
