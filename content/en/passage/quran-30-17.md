## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 18 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So exalted be Allah when you enter the evening and when you enter the morning": So glorify Allah, O people: meaning send blessings upon Him when you reach the evening, and that is the Maghrib prayer, and when you enter the morning, and that is the morning prayer. "And to Him belongs all praise in the heavens and the earth," meaning: "And to Him belongs all praise from all His creation, excluding others in the heavens, from its inhabitants among the angels, and the earth from its inhabitants, from all kinds of His creation therein." "And in the evening," he means: and glorify Him also in the evening, and that is the Asr prayer "and when you appear," meaning: when you enter at the time of Dhuhr. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
