## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 46 as one section. He explains His saying “a revelation from the Lord of the worlds”: But it is “a revelation from the Lord of the worlds” that was revealed to him “even if he had fabricated against Us” Muhammad had fabricated some false sayings and lied about Us. In the section, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Saeed bin Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
