## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "that was a nation which has passed away; theirs is what they earned and yours what you earned, and you will not be asked about what they did" al-Tabari says those meant are Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and their children, and that God tells the Jews and Christians to leave off speaking of them other than as they deserve and not to ascribe Judaism and Christianity to them; "umma" here is the group and generation of people, and "khalat" means it passed on its way. He says those of God's prophets and messengers whom you ascribe it to have what they earned, you have the like of what you did, and you will not be held to account for what they did of good and evil, so leave off claiming them, because claims do not avail you with God; what avails is what you sent ahead of good works. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 100} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 101}
