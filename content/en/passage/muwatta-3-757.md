## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Aslam, client of Umar ibn al-Khattab, that Umar set the jizya on the people of gold at four dinars and on the people of silver at forty dirhams, in addition to the provisions of the Muslims and hospitality for three days. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Abd al-A'la ibn Muhsir and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.375}
