**Commentary on verse 89 (pp. 455-456)**

On [[passage/quran-11-89|11:89]] al-Tabari relates Shu'ayb's words to his people, 'my people, let not my opposition lead you', that is, let not enmity to me, hatred of me and parting from the religion I follow lead you to persist in your disbelief in God, idol worship, cheating people in measure and weight and refusing to turn and repent, so that there strike you 'the like of what struck the people of Noah', drowning, 'or the people of Hud', punishment, 'or the people of Salih', the quake, 'and the people of Lot', whose land was overturned, 'are not far from you', their destruction; will you not take warning and learn? Report 18502, from Qatada: let not parting from me lead you; 18503, from Qatada: let not my opposition lead you; 18504, from Ibn Jurayj: my enmity, hatred and parting. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 455}

Report 18505, from Qatada: 'the people of Lot are not far from you', for they were recent, meaning the people of Noah, 'Ad, Thamud and Salih, though the editor thinks the right reading is the people of Noah, Hud, Salih and Lot; 18506, from Qatada: they were of recent date, after Noah and Hud. Al-Tabari says it could also be taken to mean that the abode of Lot's people is not far from you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 456}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
