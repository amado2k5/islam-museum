## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20-22 as a single unit. He explains the phrase, “Say, ‘I only call upon my Lord, and I do not associate anyone with Him’”: The reciters differed in their reading of the phrase, “Say, ‘I only call upon my Lord.’” The majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra, and some of the Kufans, read it as a statement of fact, “He said,” with the alif. Those who read it this way considered it a statement from God on the authority of His Prophet Muhammad, that he said, so the meaning of the statement is: When the servant of God stood up to call upon Him, they crowded around him, so he said to them, “I only call upon my Lord, and I do not associate anyone with Him.” Some of the Medinan reciters and the majority of the Kufan reciters read it as a command from God Almighty to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to the people who were about to crowd around you, “I only call upon my Lord, and I do not associate anyone with Him.” The correct view is that both are well-known readings, and whichever one the reciter chooses is correct. His statement: “And he quotes in the passage statements from Qatadah and Sufyan.”
