## Summary
Malik says of a man who marries a woman, then marries her mother and has intercourse with her: the mother is never lawful to him, nor to his father or his son, and the daughter is not lawful to him, and his wife becomes forbidden to him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.40}
