## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So the cry seized them with the truth, so We made them a source of filth, and a distance for the wrongdoing people": So We took revenge on them, so We sent upon them the cry and seized them with the truth, because God punished them by deserving their punishment by their disbelief in Him and denying their Messenger. "So We made them like filth," meaning: We made them like the scum, which rises on a flood and the like, just as it is of no benefit to anything. This is merely a metaphor, and the meaning: So We destroyed them and made them like something useless. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
