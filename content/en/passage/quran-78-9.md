## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 11 as one section. He explains His saying, “And We made your sleep a rest”: We made your sleep for you a rest and tranquility, by which you find peace and rest, as if you were dead and unaware, while you are alive and the spirits have not departed from you. Saturday and rest: is stillness, and that is why Saturday is called Saturday, because it is a day of rest and tranquility. “And We made the night a covering.” God Almighty says: We made the night a covering for you, its darkness enveloping you, just as a garment covers its wearer, so that you might find rest therein from the activities you engage in during the day. This is similar to the poet’s saying: “When they donned the night, or when she set her ears to it, and it was coming.” By “they donned the night,” he means they entered into its darkness and were concealed by it. The passage also quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
