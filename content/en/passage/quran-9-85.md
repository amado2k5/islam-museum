**Commentary on verse 85 (pp. 410-411)**

Al-Tabari says to His Prophet Muhammad: let not the wealth and children of these hypocrites impress you, Muhammad, so that you pray over one of them when he dies and stand at his grave because of the abundance of his wealth and children, for I gave him what I gave of that only to punish him with it in this world, with griefs and worries over the burdens, expenses and zakat laid on him in it and the misfortunes and calamities that befall him in it; "and their souls depart" means he dies and his soul leaves his body, parting from the wealth and children I gave him, so that this is a regret to him at his death and a ruin to him then, and a ruin to him in the hereafter, since he died denying the oneness of God and the prophethood of His prophet Muhammad. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of departing. Report 17060, from al-Suddi, through al-Muthanna, Suwayd ibn Nasr, Ibn al-Mubarak and Sufyan: "and their souls depart" in the worldly life. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 411}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
