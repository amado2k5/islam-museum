## Summary
Verse 95 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/prophets-return-and-excuses-of-the-stragglers|Prophets Return and Excuses of the Stragglers]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 95 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 95 (pp. 425-428)**

Al-Tabari says: they will swear to you, believers in God, these hypocrites who rejoiced in their staying behind opposing the Messenger of God, "when you return to them", meaning when you come back to them from your campaign, "that you may turn away from them", so that you do not rebuke them; "so turn away from them", the Almighty says to the believers, leave off rebuking them and leave them with what they chose for themselves of unbelief and hypocrisy; "they are filth, and their refuge is Hell" means they are unclean, and their refuge is Hell, meaning they will end up in Hell, which is the dwelling where they will take shelter in the hereafter; "a recompense for what they used to earn" means a reward for their works which they used to do in the world of disobedience to God. The editor refers to earlier explanations of returning, turning away, filth, refuge, recompense and earning. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 425}

It is mentioned that this verse came down about two of the hypocrites who said. Report 17090, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: "they will swear by God to you when you return to them that you may turn away" to "for what they used to earn", for it was said to the Messenger of God "will you not campaign against Banu al-Asfar, perhaps you will take the daughter of the great man of the Romans, for they are beautiful?", and two men said "Messenger of God, you know women are a temptation, so do not tempt us with them, give us leave", and he gave them leave; and when they left, one of them said "he is only a piece of fat for the first to eat", and the Messenger of God marched on and nothing was sent down about it; when he was part-way along the road, at some water, there came down to him "had it been a near gain and a moderate journey they would have followed you, but the distance seemed far to them", which is [[passage/quran-9-42|9:42]], and "God pardon you, why did you give them leave", [[passage/quran-9-43|9:43]], and "those who believe in God and the Last Day do not ask your leave", [[passage/quran-9-44|9:44]], and "they are filth and their refuge is Hell, a recompense for what they used to earn". The editor explains that Banu al-Asfar are the Romans, that the piece of fat is a piece of a camel's hump which the eater is quick to take, and cites a line of Zufar ibn al-Harith al-Kilabi and the proverb "not every white thing is fat". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 426}

A man who was campaigning with the Prophet heard it and came to them behind, saying "do you know that Qur'an has come down on the Messenger of God after you left?", and they said "what did you hear?", and he said "I do not know, except that I heard him saying 'they are filth'", and a man called Makhshi said "by God, I wish I were flogged a hundred lashes and were not with you!"; and he came to the Messenger of God, who said "what brought you?", and he said "the face of the Messenger of God scorched by the wind while I am in the shade!", and God sent down to him "among them is one who says give me leave and do not tempt me", [[passage/quran-9-49|9:49]], "and they said do not go out in the heat", [[passage/quran-9-81|9:81]], and about the man who said "I wish I were flogged a hundred lashes" there came down God's words "the hypocrites fear that a surah be sent down about them", [[passage/quran-9-64|9:64]]; and a man with the Messenger of God said "if these are as they say, there is no good in us", and it reached the Messenger of God, who said to him "are you the one who said the word I heard?", and he said "no, by Him who sent down the Book to you", and God sent down about him "they said the word of unbelief and disbelieved after their Islam", [[passage/quran-9-74|9:74]], and "among you are listeners to them, and God knows the wrongdoers", [[passage/quran-9-47|9:47]]. The editor says Makhshi is Makhshi ibn Humayyir al-Ashja'i. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 427}

Report 17091, from Ka'b ibn Malik, through Yunus, Ibn Wahb, Ibn Shihab, Abd al-Rahman ibn Abdallah ibn Ka'b and Abdallah ibn Ka'b: when the Messenger of God came back from Tabuk he sat for the people, and when he did so the stay-behinds came and began to make excuses to him and swear to him, and they were some eighty men, and the Messenger of God accepted their outward profession, took their pledge, asked forgiveness for them and left their inner thoughts to God; and my account of myself was true. Ka'b said, "by God, God has never favoured me with a favour after guiding me to Islam greater in my own eyes than my truthfulness to the Messenger of God, in that I did not lie to him and so perish as those who lied perished; for God said to those who lied, when the revelation came down, the worst thing He said to anyone, 'they will swear by God to you when you return to them that you may turn away from them; so turn away from them; they are filth and their refuge is Hell, a recompense for what they used to earn', to His words 'God is not pleased with the transgressing people'". The editor notes that this is abridged from the long report of Ka'b ibn Malik's repentance, related by Muslim. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 428}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
