## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He is the One who restrained their hands from you and your hands from them in the valley of Mecca after I had given you victory over them, and Allah is ever Seeing of what you do": Allah Almighty says to His Messenger: 'And those who pledged allegiance of pleasure,' "and He is the One who restrained their hands from you," meaning that Allah restrained the hands of the polytheists who had gone out against the army of the Messenger of Allah at Hudaybiyyah, seeking their pledge to strike them. The Messenger of Allah sent and brought them captive, and the Messenger of Allah was released from them and those who were against them and did not kill them. Allah said to the believers: He is the one who restrained the hands of these polytheists from you, and your hands from them in the belly of Mecca, after I had defeated you.
