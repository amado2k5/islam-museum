## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 34 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'My Lord, indeed I have killed a soul from among them so that I fear they will be killed': Moses said: 'My Lord, indeed I have killed' from Pharaoh's people 'a soul, so I fear.' If I come to them, I will not defend myself under the pretext of 'that they will be killed,' because I have a knot in my tongue, and I cannot clarify what I intend to say. 'And my brother Aaron is more eloquent than me,' meaning: he is more eloquent than what he wants to clarify, 'so send him with me as a repella,' meaning help. "He believes me": meaning he explains to them what I am speaking to them about me. Also: from Ibn Ishaq: "And my brother Aaron is more eloquent than me, so send him with me a reply confirming me": meaning he explains to them what I speak to them about, for he understands what they do not understand. It is said: Moses only asked his Lord to support him with his brother, because when the two agreed on the report, the soul would be inclined to believe them, and the more calm it would be to believe the one report. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
