## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase: "And who is more astray than those who call upon besides Allah those who will not respond to him until the Day of Resurrection while they are heedless of their supplication": And what servant is more astray than a servant who calls upon gods besides Allah who will not respond to him until the Day of Resurrection? He says: His supplication will never be answered, because it is stone, wood, or the like. And His saying: "And they are heedless of their supplication," the Exalted means: "And their gods whom they call upon from their supplication are heedless, because they do not hear, speak, or understand." By describing her as heedless, he meant likening her to a person who is negligent about what is said to him, as she does not understand anything from what is said to her, just as a heedless person does not understand what he is unaware of. Rather, this is a rebuke from Allah to these polytheists for their bad opinion and the ugliness of their choice in worship, those who do not understand anything, and who abandon them worship from all that He bestows upon them, and who calls upon them for help when they are afflicted with needs and calamities.
