## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on practice in congregational prayer: Nafi' stood behind Abdullah ibn Umar in a prayer when no one else was with him. Ibn Umar signalled to him with his hand and made him stand level with him on his right. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.195}
