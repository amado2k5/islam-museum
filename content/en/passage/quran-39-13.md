## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 13 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, I fear if I disobey my Lord the punishment of a great Day": He says: He said, O Muhammad, to them, indeed I fear if I disobey my Lord in what He commands me to worship, sincerely obeying Him, singularly by Lordship. "The punishment of a great day": means the punishment of the Day of Resurrection; that is the Day He is magnificent.
