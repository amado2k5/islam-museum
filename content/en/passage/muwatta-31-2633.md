## Summary
It reached Malik that the Messenger of God said: I was sent to perfect good character. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the hadith is connected through sound routes from Abu Hurayra and others, and that the report of Muhammad ibn Ajlan from Abu Hurayra is good but reaches soundness only by corroboration. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.490}
