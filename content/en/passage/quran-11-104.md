**Commentary on verse 104 (p. 478)**

On [[passage/quran-11-104|11:104]] al-Tabari says: We do not delay the Day of Resurrection from coming to you except for a term decreed for it, which He counted and numbered, so it comes only at that term, neither earlier nor later. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 478}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
