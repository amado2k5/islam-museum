## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Then the cry seized them east," meaning the people of Lot were struck by the thunderbolt of punishment, which is the shout of the sunrise, meaning when the sun rose; he explains that the accusative of "eastern" and "musbahin" in the case means when they entered the morning and when they rose.
