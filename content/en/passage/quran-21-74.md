## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Lot, We gave him judgment and knowledge, and We saved him from the town that used to commit evil. Indeed, they were a wicked, defiantly disobedient people": And We gave Lot a "judgment," which is the decisive judgment between the disputants. "And knowledge" means: We also gave him knowledge of his religion and what is obligatory upon him for God's obligations. In the accusative case of "Lot," there are two types: it is accusative because the conjunction of the waw is attached to the verb, as we said: "And We gave Lot," and the other is implicit, meaning: and mention Lot. And His saying: "And We saved him from the town that used to commit evil," means: And We saved him from Our punishment which We punished the people of the town that committed evil, namely the city of Sodom, to whose people Lot had been sent. The evils they committed included having males on their backs, people distracting them, quarreling with them in their clubs, and other evil things they were doing. God sent him out when He intended to destroy them in the Levant.
