## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Damra ibn Sa'id al-Mazini from Aban ibn Uthman, in the chapter on leaving ablution for food touched by fire: Uthman ibn Affan ate bread and meat, then rinsed his mouth and washed his hands, wiped his face with them, and prayed without performing ablution. The editor notes that a similar report is raised to the Prophet from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab from Uthman, with a good chain, in Abd al-Razzaq, Ahmad and al-Bazzar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.62}
