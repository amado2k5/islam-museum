## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, from Abu Hurayra, that he used to say: if I saw gazelles grazing in Medina I would not startle them, because the Prophet said: what lies between its two lava fields is sacred. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.467}
