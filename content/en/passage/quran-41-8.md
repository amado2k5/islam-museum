## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, those who believe and do righteous deeds will have a reward without expectation": Those who are truthful to Allah and His Messenger, and do what Allah and His Messenger have commanded them, and refrained from what He has forbidden them, and that is the righteous deeds, "will have a reward without being denied." He says: Whoever does this will have a reward not diminished from what He promised them to reward them for. The interpreters have differed regarding the interpretation of this, and we have explained it previously in a way that suffices to repeat it here. And Qad: And from Al-Suddi: 'They have a reward that is not ungrateful,' some said: not diminished. And some said: They are not grateful to them. And he said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: 'a reward that is not ungrateful,' means it is not diminished. And he said: praise and gentleness, all from Ibn Abi Nujayh, His saying: 'They have a reward that is not ungrateful,' he said: It is considered calculated. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
