## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 68 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Those who will be enemies to each other that Day, except for the righteous": those who will be enemies to others on the Day of Resurrection for sins against God in this world, some will disown one another, except for those who used to fear God in that passage. In this passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
