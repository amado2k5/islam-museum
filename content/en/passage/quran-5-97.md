## Summary
Verse 97 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 97 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari explains "God has made the Ka'ba, the sacred House, a support for people, and the sacred month, the offering and the garlands": God made the Ka'ba, the sacred House, a support for people who have no support in a chief who restrains their strong from their weak, their wrongdoer from their doer of good, their oppressor from the oppressed, and likewise the sacred month, the offering and the garlands; by each of these He restrained some of them from others, since they had no other support, and He made them landmarks of their religion and benefits for their affairs. He notes that the Ka'ba, it is said, is called "Ka'ba" for its squareness. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 89}

Report 12780, from Mujahid: the Ka'ba is so called because it is square. Report 12781, from Ikrima: it is called Ka'ba for its squareness. Al-Tabari explains that "qiyam" with ya is of the roots with waw, the waw turned to ya because of the kasra on the qaf, as the verbal noun of "I stood" is "qiyam" and of "I fasted" is "siyam", when the origin is "qiwam" and "siwam"; it has come in the original form with waw in the verse of the rajaz poet Humayd al-Arqat, who calls something the support of the world and the support of religion. God made the Ka'ba, the sacred month, the offering and the garlands a support to those of the Arabs who held these sacred and honoured them, in the position of the chief by whom the affairs of his followers stand. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 90}

We stop here at the foot of p. 90, in the middle of the commentary on verse 97, after the note on the form of "qiyam".
