## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "The greatest panic will not grieve them, and the angels will meet them on this Day of yours, which you were promised": The interpreters differed regarding the greatest panic—which is the panic? Some said: That is the fire when it encompasses its people. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
