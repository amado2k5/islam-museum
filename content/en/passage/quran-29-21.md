## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 22 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "He punishes whom He wills and has mercy on whom He wills, and to Him you will turn": Then God creates the afterlife of His creation after their destruction, punishing whom He wills for the crimes committed during their lifetime, and He has mercy on whomever He wills among them who repent, believe, and do righteous deeds. "And to Him you turn," meaning: to Him you will return and be returned. As for His saying: "And you are not miracles on earth or in the heaven," Ibn Zayd said about this: Ibn Wahb informed him, who said: Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase "And you are not miracles on earth or in the heaven," he said: The inhabitants of the two earths on earth nor the inhabitants of the heavens will not be able to do so if they disobey Him. He recited: "An atom's weight in the heavens or on the earth, nor smaller or greater than that except in a clear Book." Some Arabs from Basra said about this: "You are not miracles in the 'A' and quote in the verse from Ibn Zayd."
