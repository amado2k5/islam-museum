## Summary
A saying of Malik: the Sunna has stood that there is no jizya on the women or the children of the People of the Book, and that jizya is taken only from the men who have reached puberty. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.376}
