## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 80 to 81 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "O Children of Israel, We have saved you from your enemy and promised you the side of the right Mount and sent down upon you manna and quails": When Moses and his people were saved from the sea, and Pharaoh covered his people from the sea, we said to the people of Moses: "O Children of Israel, We have saved you from your enemy." Pharaoh: "And We promised you the right side of the Mount and sent down upon you manna and quails." We have also mentioned how God's appointment with Moses and his people was on the right side of the mountain. We have explained manna and salwa according to the differences of opinion among those who differ, and we have mentioned the evidence confirming the correct opinion on this matter earlier, which would not be necessary to repeat it here. In the passage, statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah are also quoted.
