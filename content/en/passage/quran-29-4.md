## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Or is it appropriate for those who commit evil deeds to precede Us, evil is what they judge": Or is it considered for those who associate partners with Allah and worship others with Him, and they are the ones meant by the phrase "those who commit evil to surpass Us," meaning: that they overpower Us and cause us to lose their lives, so we cannot overcome them and take revenge on them because of their associating partners with Allah? In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
