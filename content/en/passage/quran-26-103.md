## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 103 to 104 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed in that is a sign, and most of them were believers": Among the arguments Abraham used against his people that we mentioned for him is clear evidence for those who took heed, that God's way in His creation is to follow the way of Abraham's people of worshiping idols and gods, and to follow them in that as prescribed for them in the Hereafter, such as their slapping and worshipping besides Him with the soldiers of Satan in Hell. Most of them were believers before His knowledge. And indeed, your Lord, O Muhammad, is severe in vengeance on those who worshipped besides Him and did not repent from his disbelief until he perished. The Merciful to those among them who repent will punish them for what was before their repentance of sin and offense.
