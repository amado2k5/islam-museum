**Commentary on verse 50 (p. 357)**

On [[passage/quran-11-50|11:50]] al-Tabari says: We sent to the people of 'Ad their brother Hud, who said to them 'my people, worship God' alone, without partner, apart from the gods and idols you worship besides Him, 'you have no god but He' meaning you have no object of worship who deserves worship from you but Him, so make worship pure for Him and give Him divinity alone; 'you are only inventing' means you, in setting up gods and idols with Him, are only people of falsehood who lie and forge untruth, since there is no god but He. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'invention'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 357}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
