## Summary
Verse 43 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 43 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**What the command to devotion means.** Al-Tabari says God means, reporting what His angels said to Mary, that she should make her obedience sincere to her Lord alone; he says he showed the sense of the word for devotion with its attestations earlier, and the editor refers to volume 2 and later pages, adding that the disagreement among the commentators here is like the one there. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 401}

**First view: prolonged standing.** Report 7038 (Muhammad ibn Amr, from Abu Asim, from Isa, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid) says the sense is to prolong the standing; so do 7039 (al-Muthanna, from Abu Hudhayfa, from Shibl) and 7040 (al-Qasim, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, in prayer). Report 7041 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Abdullah ibn Idris, from Layth, from Mujahid) says that when she was told this she stood until her ankles swelled; 7042 repeats it through al-Qasim and al-Husayn, and 7043 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Abd al-Razzaq, from al-Thawri, from Ibn Abi Layla, from Mujahid) gives the prolonged standing. Report 7044 (from Ammar, from Ibn Abi Ja'far, from his father, from al-Rabi) says the word means staying still, that is standing for God in prayer; 7045 (Muhammad ibn Sinan, from Abu Asim, from Sufyan, from Layth, from Mujahid) says she prayed until her feet swelled; and 7046 (Ibn al-Barqi, from Amr, from al-Awza'i) says she stood until pus ran from her feet. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 401} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 402} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 403}

**Second view: sincerity.** Report 7047 (al-Muthanna, from al-Himmani, from Ibn al-Mubarak, from Sharik, from Salim, from Sa'id) gives the sense as to be sincere toward her Lord. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 403}

**Third view: obedience.** Report 7048 (al-Hasan ibn Yahya, from Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from Qatada) gives the sense as to obey her Lord, and so does 7049 (Musa, from Amr, from Asbat, from al-Suddi). Report 7050 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Harb, from Ibn Lahi'a, from Darraj, from Abu al-Haytham, from Abu Said al-Khudri, from the Prophet) says every place in the Qur'an where devotion is mentioned means obedience to God; the editor says this is another chain for the earlier report 5518, through al-Rabi ibn Sulayman, from Asad ibn Musa, from Ibn Lahi'a. Report 7051 (Muhammad ibn Sinan, from Abu Bakr al-Hanafi, from Abbad ibn Mansur, from al-Hasan) gives the sense as to worship her Lord. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 403}

**Bowing and prostration.** Al-Tabari says he also explained the sense of bowing and prostration with proofs of its soundness: both mean humility before God and submission to Him in obedience and servitude. The editor refers to earlier passages and notes that the printed text had a different word for servitude, that he kept the reading of the manuscript, and that al-Tabari often uses it. The sense of the verse, in al-Tabari's view, is therefore: make the worship of your Lord sincere for His face alone, and be humble in obedience and worship together with those of His creation who are humble before Him, in thanks for the honour He gave her in choosing her, purifying her of impurities and preferring her over the women of the world of her time. This is his own choice. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 404}
