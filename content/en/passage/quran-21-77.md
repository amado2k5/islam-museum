## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 76 to 77 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We supported him against the people who denied Our signs. Indeed, they were an evil people, so We drowned them all together": And We helped Noah against the people who denied Our proofs and Our evidences, and We saved him from them, and We drowned them all together. "Indeed, they were a bad people." Allah says: Indeed, the people of Noah, who denied Our signs, were a bad people, misdeeds, disobeying Allah and disobeying His command.
