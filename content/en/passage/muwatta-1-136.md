## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on dry ablution: he was asked about a man who performed dry ablution, whether he may lead his companions who are in ablution. He said it is more beloved to him that someone else lead them, though if he led them he would not see any harm in it. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.100}
