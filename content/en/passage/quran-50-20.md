## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And blow the trumpet on the Day of Warning": Our explanation of the meaning of the trumpets and how to blow them by mentioning the differences of opinion. Which is the most correct opinion among us, as it suffices to repeat it here. And His saying: "That is the Day of Warning" means: This day on which the blowing is the day of warning that God promised the disbelievers to punish them.
