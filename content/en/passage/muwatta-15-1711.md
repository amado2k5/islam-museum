## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that Umar ibn al-Khattab, Abdullah ibn Umar, Abdullah ibn Masud, Salim ibn Abdallah, al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, Ibn Shihab and Sulayman ibn Yasar all used to say that if a man swears a divorce of a woman before marrying her and then breaks the oath, it binds him if he marries her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.98}
