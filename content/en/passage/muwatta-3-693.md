## Summary
A saying of Malik: for property a man holds for trade, from which the owner receives no cash that zakat is due on, he fixes a month of the year when he values the goods he has for trade, and counts the gold or silver he has; when all of it reaches what zakat is due on, he pays zakat. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.347}
