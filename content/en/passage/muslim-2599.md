## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim: when asked to curse the polytheists, the Prophet answered that he was sent as a mercy and not as a curser. It is one of the clearest texts on the mercy of his mission. {cite:sahih-muslim}
