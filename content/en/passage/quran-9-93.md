**Commentary on verse 93 (pp. 423-424)**

Al-Tabari says: the blame by punishment is not on the people of excuse, Muhammad, but on those who ask your leave to stay behind opposing you and to leave striving with you, while they are people of wealth, strength and capacity for striving and campaigning, from hypocrisy and doubt in God's promise and warning; "they are content to be with those who stay behind" means content to sit after you with the women, who are the stay-behinds behind the men in the houses, and to leave campaigning with you; "and God has sealed their hearts" means God set a seal on their hearts because of the sins they earned; "so they do not know" the evil end of their staying behind from you and leaving striving with you, and the ill repute they bear in the world and the great trial in the hereafter. The editor refers to earlier explanations of the way, those who stay behind and the sealing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 424}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
