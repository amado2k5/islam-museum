## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger said: do not intercept riders to buy from them; do not let one of you sell over another's sale; do not compete in bidding up; do not let a town dweller sell for a desert dweller; do not tie up the udders of camels and sheep. Whoever buys them after that has the choice of two options, after he has milked them: if he is pleased with them he keeps them, and if he is displeased he returns them with a sa' of dates. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.216}
