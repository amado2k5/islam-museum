## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 15 to 16 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, the Hour is coming; I will almost conceal it, and every soul will be rewarded for what it strives." The hour when God will resurrect creatures from their graves for the Day of Resurrection is "I will almost conceal it." Thus, the pronunciation of the alif from its hidden is the recitation of all the reciters of the cities of Islam, meaning: I will almost conceal it from myself, so that no one will see it. This is the interpretation of most scholars. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
