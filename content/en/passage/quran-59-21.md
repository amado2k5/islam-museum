## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "If We had sent down this Qur'an upon a mountain, you would have seen it humble and cracked out of fear of Allah. These are parables We present to mankind that they might reflect": and His saying: "If We had sent down this Qur'an upon a mountain, you would have seen it humble and cracking out of fear of Allah," He, the Exalted, says: If We had sent down this Qur'an upon a mountain, which is a stone, you would have seen it, O Muhammad, humbled, saying: humbled, cracked out of fear of Allah despite His harshness, warning not to fulfill the duty of Allah to honor the Qur'an, which was revealed to the son of Adam He is despised about it, and he is turned away from the lessons and reminders he contains, as if he did not hear them, as if he had deafness in his ears. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
