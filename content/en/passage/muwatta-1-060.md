## Summary
A report that Yahya transmits from Malik from Abu Nu'aym Wahb ibn Kaysan, that he heard Jabir ibn Abdullah al-Ansari say he saw Abu Bakr al-Siddiq eat meat and then pray without performing ablution, in the chapter on leaving ablution for food touched by fire. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq and Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted reports to this effect from Abu Bakr. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.63}
