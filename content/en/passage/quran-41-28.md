## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is the recompense of the enemies of Allah; the Fire will have them in eternal abode as a recompense for what they used to deny Our signs": This is the reward with which those polytheists of Quraysh who disbelieved are rewarded as the enemies of Allah. Then the Exalted is He, the Exalted, began to report the description of that reward, and what is it? He said: It is the Fire. The Fire is an explanation of the reward and a translation of it, and it is nominative in response to it. Then He said: "For them is the eternal abode therein," meaning for those who commit partners with God in the Fire, the eternal abode means the abode of staying and staying without end or duration. The abode that He, the Exalted, informed them that they will have in the Fire is the Fire. This is good because of the difference in the two terms, as it is said: You have a righteous abode from your town, and from Kufa a noble abode. Al-daur is Kufa and the town. This is good due to the difference in wording. It has been mentioned to us that in Ibn Mas'ud's recitation: "That is the recompense of the enemies of God, the eternal abode." This corrects what we have said regarding the interpretation
