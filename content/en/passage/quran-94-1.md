## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 8 as one section. He explains His saying, “Have We not expanded for you your breast?”: God Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, reminding him of His favors upon him and His kindness to him, urging him thereby to be grateful for what He has bestowed upon him, so that he may deserve more of it: “Have We not expanded for you,” O Muhammad, for guidance, faith in God, and knowledge of the truth, “your breast,” so that We soften your heart and make it a vessel for wisdom. In the section, he quotes sayings of Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
