## Summary
Malik was asked about one who sent with someone an offering to slaughter in the pilgrimage, while he himself entered ihram for the lesser pilgrimage: does he slaughter it when he leaves ihram, or does he delay it until he slaughters it in the pilgrimage and leaves ihram from his lesser pilgrimage? He said: rather he delays it until he slaughters it in the pilgrimage, and he leaves ihram from his lesser pilgrimage. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.520}
