## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 77 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Have you seen what you used to worship?": Abraham said to his people: Have you considered, O my people, what you used to worship of these idols, you and your forefathers? By 'the ancients' means the foremost among those whom Abraham addressed, and they were the first before them among those who were on the same path as those whom Abraham spoke to in worshiping idols, for they are an enemy to me except the Lord of the worlds. Someone says: How can wood, iron, and copper be described as the enmity of the son of Adam? The meaning of this is: they are enemies to Me if I worship them on the Day of Resurrection, as He, the Exalted, said: "And they took gods besides Allah to be a source of honor for them. No! They will deny their worship and will be opposites to them." And His saying: "Except the Lord of the worlds" is accusative as an exception, and 'adw' means plural, and it is unified because it took the verbal articulation point, such as sitting and sitting. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Abraham.
