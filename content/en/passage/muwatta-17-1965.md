## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that he said: riba in the Jahiliyya was that a man had a right over another man for a term, and when the term fell due, he said: will you pay or will you increase it? If he paid, he took it; if not, he increased his right and postponed the term for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.204}
