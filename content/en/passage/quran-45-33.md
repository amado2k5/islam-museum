## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And the evils of what they did became apparent to them, and what they used to mock befell them": And those who used to disbelieve in the signs of Allah in this world realized the evils of their deeds in this world. He says: Their evils and evils became apparent to them when they read the books of their deeds that the memorizers used to copy in this world. "And befell them for what they used to mock for," meaning: And befell them from the punishment of God then that which they used to mock when it was said to them: Indeed, Allah will replace those who denied Him for the sins of the deeds they used to do.
