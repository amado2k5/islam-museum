Al-Tabari addresses verses 29 to 31 together in one place. Al-Tabari mentions that the hoopoe threw the book to the queen of Sheba, who said to her people, 'I am delivering a noble book.' Wahb ibn Munabbih narrates the text of the book: In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful: From Sulayman son of Dawud to Bilqis bint Dhi Sarh and her people.
