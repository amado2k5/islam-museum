## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And he and his soldiers were arrogant in the land without right, and they thought they would not return to Us": Pharaoh and his soldiers in the land of Egypt were arrogant in believing in Moses and following him in what he called them to regarding the oneness of God and acknowledging servitude to Him "without right," meaning transgression and disobedience to their Lord. "And they thought they would not return to Us." He means: They thought that after their death they would not be resurrected, nor would they receive reward or punishment, so they rode their desires, not knowing that God was watching over them and that He was justifying their evil deeds.
