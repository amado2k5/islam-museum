## Summary
Malik says of a man who bought a slave-girl or an animal that bore young while with him, and then the buyer became insolvent: the slave-girl or animal and its young belong to the seller, unless the creditors wish to have it and give him his full right, and keep it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.213}
