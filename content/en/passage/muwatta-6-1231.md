## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, from Aisha, that Safiyya bint Huyayy menstruated, and that was mentioned to the Prophet; he said: perhaps she will hold us back? It was said to him: she has already circumambulated; he said: then there is no problem. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.550}
