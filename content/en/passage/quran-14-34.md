## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says: And He gave you, along with His favor, upon you to subjugate what He subjected and provided for you of everything you asked for and desired for something. He explains that the second object is the omission of sufficiency with 'ma,' since 'min' partially removes what comes after it and stopped mentioning it, and similarly 'wa ta'ayt min everything.' Then he presents the sayings: It is said to be about the plural, like 'so-and-so knows everything,' meaning part of it; and it is said there is nothing except that some people asked him, which is the saying of some grammarians of Basra; and some grammarians of Kufa said: And he came to you from all that you asked if you had asked him. And from Mujahid: From all that you asked and desired for him, and from al-Hasan the similar; and from Rukanah ibn Hashim: What you asked and what you did not ask. Others read 'mil kul' with tanween kul meaning something you neither asked nor sought, like the sun and the moon.
