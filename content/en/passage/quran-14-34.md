## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari sets out views on what the phrase about asking means: that God gave what they asked for and desired (Mujahid and al-Hasan); that He gave what they asked and what they did not ask (Rukana). Grammarians note that 'all' can mean a great many, or that the sense is 'whatever you would ask, were you to ask'.
