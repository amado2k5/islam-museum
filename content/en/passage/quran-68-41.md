## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40-41 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Or do they have partners? Then let them bring their partners, if they are truthful,” as meaning: Do these people have partners in what they say and describe of the matters they claim to possess? Then let them bring their partners in that, if they are truthful in what they claim about partners. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
