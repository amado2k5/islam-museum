## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if Allah were to punish people for their injustice, He would not delay them for a specified term, but when their term comes, they will not delay it for an hour nor delay it": "And if Allah were to be held accountable" means the sinners of Adam for their sins, "He did not leave upon it" meaning on the earth "from a creature" that would swarm upon it. "But he delays them," he says: but by his forbearance, he delays these oppressors and does not hasten their punishment, "until a specified term," meaning until their appointed time. "When their appointed time comes," he says: when the time set for their destruction comes, "they do not delay" their destruction by an hour and are given respite, "nor delay" it until they complete their term. In the passage, he quotes statements from Al-A'mash.
