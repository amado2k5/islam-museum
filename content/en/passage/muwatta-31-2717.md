## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, that Abu Bakr al-Siddiq went in to Aisha while she was ill, and a Jewish woman was reciting over her. Abu Bakr said: recite over her with the Book of God. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.532}
