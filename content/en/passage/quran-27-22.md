## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So he stayed not far and said, 'I encompassed what you did not encompass and I came to you from Sheba with certain news': He means His mention by saying: 'So he stayed not far.' So Solomon stayed not long from the time he was asked about the hoopoe until the hoopoe came. Reciters differed in the pronunciation of the phrase 'fa maqas'; most of the reciters of the regions except Asim read it as 'fa maqath' with a damma on the kaf, and Asim read it with fatha. Both readings are correct to us because they are well-known languages, although the damma in them is more impressive to me, as it is the most famous and eloquent of the two languages. And His saying: 'He said, 'I encompassed what you did not encompass with'—he says: The hoopoe said when Solomon asked him about his backwardness and occultation: 'I have learned what you have not encompassed, O Suleiman.' Also: Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: 'I encompassed what you did not encompass it,' he said: what you did not know. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd and Ibn Ishaq.
