## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, “He is the One Who created you, and among you are those who disbelieve and among you are those who believe. And Allah is Seeing of what you do.” God “who created you,” O people, and this is from the mention of the name of God, “and among you are those who disbelieve and among you are those who believe.” He says: Among you are those who disbelieve in their Creator and that He created them, “and among you are those who believe.” He says: Among you are those who believe in Him and are certain that He is their Creator or Maker. “And Allah is Seeing of what you do.” He says: And God who created you is Seeing of your deeds and knows them. Nothing of them is hidden from Him, and He will reward you for them. So fear Him, lest you disobey Him in His command or prohibition, and He punish you. On the authority of Abu Tamim al-Jayshani, on the authority of Abu Dharr: “When the semen remains in the womb for forty nights, it comes to the angel of souls, who ascends with it to the Almighty in His palm, and says: ‘O Lord, is this Your servant male or female?’ Then God decrees to it what He decrees. Then he says: ‘O Lord, is he wretched or blessed?’ Then He writes what he will receive.” He said: And Abu Dharr recited the opening of Surah At-Taghabun, five verses.
