## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who, when they spend, do not waste nor are stingy, and there is a way between that." Then the interpreters differed regarding the spending that Allah referred to in this context, and what is extravagance and stinginess. Some said: Extravagance is spending in disobedience to Allah, even if it is few. And he said: And Allah has forgiven me, and he called it extravagance. They said: Stinginess means withholding from the right of Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibrahim.
