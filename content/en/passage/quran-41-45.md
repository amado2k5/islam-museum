## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave Moses the Scripture, but there was differing about it. And if it were not for a word that had preceded your Lord, He would have judged between them, and indeed, there is doubt concerning it." He explains: "And indeed We gave Moses" the Book, O Muhammad, meaning the Torah, as We gave you the Criterion. "So he differed about it," meaning: He differed in the action concerning those who were given it from the Jews. "And if it were not for a word that preceded from your Lord, He would have judged between them," meaning: Were it not for the previous decree and judgment of God regarding them, that He delayed their punishment until the Day of Resurrection, "He would have judged between them," meaning: To hasten the separation between them in what they differed over by destroying the invalids among them, as in Al-Suddi, regarding His saying: "And if it were not for a word that preceded your Lord," he said: delay until the Day of Resurrection. And His saying: 'And they are doubtful about it,' means: And the invalidated group among them is doubtful about what they said 'doubtful,' meaning: they doubt their statement about what they said, because they said it without evidence, and they only said it based on conjecture.
