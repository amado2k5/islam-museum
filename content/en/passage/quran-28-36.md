## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when Moses came to them with Our clear signs, they said, 'This is nothing but fabricated magic, and we have not heard of this concerning our forefathers.'" When Moses came to Pharaoh and filled him with Our proofs and proofs that testify to the truth of what Moses brought from his Lord, they said to Moses: What is this that you brought us except magic that you fabricated before you and fabricated falsely and falsely? "And we have not heard of this" to which you invite us of worshiping the one you call us to worship in our ancestors and our forefathers who passed before us.
