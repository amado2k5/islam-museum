## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Qarun, Pharaoh, and Haman. And Moses brought them clear proofs, but they were arrogant in the land and were not foremost." And remember, O Muhammad, Qarun, Pharaoh, and Haman. Indeed, Moses all came with clear proofs, meaning clear verses. They were arrogant on earth to believe in the clear proofs of the signs and to follow Moses, "And they were not foremost." God Almighty says: "They did not precede us by themselves, so they would pass us away; rather, we were able to overcome them."
