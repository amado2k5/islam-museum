## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 87 to 88 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, it is but a reminder for the worlds": Say to these polytheists among your people: "Indeed, it is," meaning: this Qur'an is "only a reminder," meaning: except a reminder from God "to the worlds" among the jinn and mankind, their Lord's remembrance is a will to save those among them who believe in Him from destruction. And His saying: "And you will know its news at a certain time" means: And you will know, O polytheists of Allah from Quraysh, its news, meaning: the news of this Qur'an, which is its report, meaning the reality of the promise and warning it contains after some time. And similar to what we have said about this, the scholars of interpretation said. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Hasan, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
