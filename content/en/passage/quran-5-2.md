**Commentary on verse 2: do not violate God's symbols (pp. 462-464)**

Al-Tabari sets out the views on 'O believers, do not violate God's symbols'. Some say: do not violate God's sanctities or transgress His limits; report 10938, from Ata, who was asked about the symbols: God's sanctities, avoiding His prohibitions and following His obedience. Others say: do not violate God's sanctuary, that is the marks of God's sacred territory among the lands; report 10939, from al-Suddi: the symbols of God are His sanctuary. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 462}

Others say: do not violate the rites of the pilgrimage and so waste them; report 10940, from Ibn Jurayj through Ibn Abbas: the rites of the pilgrimage. Report 10941, from Ibn Abbas through Ali ibn Abi Talha: the idolaters used to make the pilgrimage to the Sacred House, bring offerings, honour the sanctity of the places, and trade in their pilgrimage; the Muslims wanted to raid them, and God said: do not violate God's symbols. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 463}

Report 10942, from Mujahid through Ibn Abi Najih: the symbols are al-Safa and al-Marwa, the offering animals and the camels; all this is among God's symbols; report 10943, from Mujahid through Shibl, the same. Others say: do not make lawful what God has forbidden you in the state of pilgrimage; report 10944, from Ibn Abbas: the symbols are what God forbade you to touch while you are in pilgrimage. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 464}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
