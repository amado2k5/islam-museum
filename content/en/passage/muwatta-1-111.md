## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the procedure for the ritual bath: when Abdullah ibn Umar bathed from sexual impurity, he began by pouring water over his right hand and washing it, then washed his private parts, rinsed his mouth and blew his nose, washed his face and splashed water into his eyes, then washed his right hand and then his left, washed his head, and then poured water over his whole body. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, al-Shafi'i and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ibn Jurayj from Nafi'. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.89}
