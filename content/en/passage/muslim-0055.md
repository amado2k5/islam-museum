## Summary
A report of Tamim al-Dari in the chapter "Religion is sincere advice" in the Book of Faith, through Suhayl ibn Abi Salih from Ata ibn Yazid al-Laythi. It is from the hadith on which al-Bukhari said the whole matter turns. Next in the chapter is the pledge of Jarir ibn Abdullah "to establish prayer, pay zakat and give sincere advice to every Muslim". {cite:sahih-muslim}
