## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say to those who have believed, forgive those who do not hope for the days of Allah, that He may recompense a people for what they used to earn": Say, O Muhammad, to those who have believed Allah and follow you, forgive those who do not fear Allah's might, events, and wrath when they are afflicted with harm and adversity. "That He may recompense a people for what they used to earn." He means: Allah may recompense those who harm them among the polytheists in the Hereafter, and He will afflict them with His punishment for what they used to earn in this world, and then for their harm to the people of faith in Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
