## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'If there were angels on earth walking in safety, We would have sent down upon them from the heaven an angel as a messenger.'" He says to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to those who have rejected you and your belief in what You brought them from Me, rejecting that Allah would send down a messenger from among mankind: If there were angels on earth walking in tranquility, We would have sent down to them from the heaven an angel as a messenger, because the angels see only their own like them, and whoever Allah has chosen from among the children of Adam to see them, they are not able to Seeing it, so how can messengers be sent to them from among the angels when they cannot see them in the forms in which Allah created them? Rather, a messenger is sent to humans from among themselves, as if angels were walking on earth in reassurance. Then We sent to them a messenger whom We sent from among themselves as an angel like them.
