## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We forbade nursing for him before, so she said, 'Shall I guide you to a household who will guarantee him for you and who are sincere to him?': And We forbade Moses, the nursing mother, from being nursed by his mother." It is mentioned that Moses' sister was the one who said to Pharaoh's family: "Shall I guide you to a family who will guarantee it for you and who are sincere advisors to him?" In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Ibn Ishaq.
