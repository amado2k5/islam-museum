## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly sent Noah to his people, and he stayed among them for a thousand years minus fifty years, and the flood seized them while they were wrongdoers." This is a warning from Allah mentioned by those polytheists from Quraysh, who said to those who believed: Follow Our way, and let us bear your sins. He says to His Prophet Muhammad: Do not be grief-stricken, O Muhammad, what harm you and your companions have caused to these polytheists, for even if I dictate to them and prolong their decree, their fate will be ruin, and the fate of your affairs and your companions' will be to rise and conquer them and be saved from what befalls them of The punishment, just as we did with Noah, when We sent him to his people, and he stayed among them for a thousand years minus fifty years, calling them to monotheism and separating from gods and idols. This did not increase their call to Allah and their acceptance of the advice he gave them except as a flee.
