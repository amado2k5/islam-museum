## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Pilgrimage of Sahih Muslim: the fighter in God's path, the pilgrim and the one who makes the minor pilgrimage are the guests of God. He calls them and they answer, they ask and He gives. {cite:sahih-muslim}
