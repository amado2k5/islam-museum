## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40-42 as a single section. He interprets the words, “So I swear by the Lord of the East and the West that We are indeed able,” meaning: I swear by the Lord of the east and west of the earth, “that We are indeed able to replace them with better than them.” He says: We are indeed able to destroy them and bring forth better than them from among creation, who will obey Me and not disobey Me. “And We are not to be outrun.” He, may He be exalted, says: None of them can escape Us in any matter We intend for them, so that they cannot evade Us. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
