## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 17 as one section. He explains his statement “Nay, indeed it is a reminder”: “Nay,” the matter is not as you do, O Muhammad, in frowning at the one who comes to you striving while he is fearful, and turning away from the one who is self-sufficient. “Indeed it is a reminder,” he says: this admonition and this surah are a “reminder,” he says: an admonition and a lesson. And in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A’mash and Sufyan.
