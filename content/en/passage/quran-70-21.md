## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 25 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Indeed, mankind was created anxious” as meaning that “man,” the disbeliever, “was created anxious,” and anxiety is intense distress coupled with extreme greed and restlessness. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Saeed ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
