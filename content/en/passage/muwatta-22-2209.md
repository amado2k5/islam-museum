## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said, with his back against the Kaaba: whoever takes a stray has gone astray. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.305}
