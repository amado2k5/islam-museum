## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 28–30. Al-Tabari mentions that humans say to the jinn, 'You used to come to us from the right side, that is, from the side of religion and truth, and you deceive us with power and power.' The jinn replied, 'You were not believers, and we had no authority over you; rather, you were a tyrannical people.'
