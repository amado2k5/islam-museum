## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "and they disbelieved in him before and cast the unseen from a distant place": "And they disbelieved in him," meaning: they disbelieved in what they ask their Lord when the punishment descended upon them, and their witnessing of His removal, which is faith in Allah, in Muhammad, and in what He brought them from Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
