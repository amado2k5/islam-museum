## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "So We made them predecessors and an example for others": The reciters differed in reading this, so most reciters of Kufa, except Asim, read it as "so We made them predecessors" with a damma on the sin and lam, referring from them to a predecessor of the people, which is the preceding plural before the people. Al-Farra' narrated that he heard al-Qasim ibn Ma'n mention that he heard the Arabs say: 'A predecessor from the people' has passed. Most reciters of Medina and Basra, and Asim read it: "So We made them predecessors" with a fatha on the sin and the lam. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
