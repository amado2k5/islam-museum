## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We have certainly given you seven of the oft-repeated and the great Qur'an": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the seven that Allah sent His Prophet from the oft-repeated parts. Some of them referred to me as the seven chapters at the beginning of the Qur'an, known by their length. Those who say this statement differ regarding the two oft-repeated; some said: the oft-repeated are the seven, and they are named so because they contain proverbs, reports, and lessons. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
