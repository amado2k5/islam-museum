## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 37 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "none will eat it except the sinners" as meaning that only sinners, those whose sins amount to disbelief in God, will eat the food made from the discharge of wounds. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
