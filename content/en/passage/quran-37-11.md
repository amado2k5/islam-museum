## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So ask them who are more powerful in creation or of Our creation. Indeed, We created them from sticky clay": So ask them, O Muhammad, those polytheists who deny resurrection after death and resurrection after trial: He asks them: Is he more of a stronger character? He says: Did He create them more severely? Or does He create from among us His creation from the angels, devils, the heavens, and the earth? He mentioned that this is in the recitation of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud: "More of a stronger character or more than our number"? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, and Al-A'mash.
