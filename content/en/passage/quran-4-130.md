**Commentary on verse 130: separation and God's bounty (pp. 293-294)**

Al-Tabari says that if the wife from whom her husband has turned away refuses the settlement, claims her right to division, maintenance and what God has ordained for her, and the husband refuses the kindness God urged on him, and does not treat her equally with the other wife in division, maintenance and company, then the two part. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 293}

God enriches each from His bounty: God gives the husband and the divorced wife of His grace; to her, a husband better for her than the first, or wider provision and chastity; to him, wide provision and a wife better for him than the divorced one, or chastity. God is wide, giving to both and to the rest of His creatures, and wise in the parting and divorce He decrees between them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 294}

Reports 10672 and 10673, from Mujahid through Ibn Abi Najih and Shibl, say that the parting in the verse is divorce. The editor notes that the printed text adds words absent from the manuscript, and that the Durr al-Manthur gives Mujahid's word as simply 'divorce'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 294}
