## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 132 to 134 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Peace be upon the family of Yasin" as a trust from God to the family of Yasin. Reciters differed in the reading of his phrase: "Peace be upon Ilyasin." The majority of the reciters of Mecca, Basra, and Kufa read it as "Peace be upon Eliasin" with a kasra on the alif of Ilyasis. Some said it was the name of Elias, and they said he was called by two names: Elias, and Ilyasin is like Ibrahim. Abraham supports this by saying that everything in the surah about "peace" means peace upon the Prophet mentioned over his family, and so is Eliasin; it is only peace upon Elias without his family.
