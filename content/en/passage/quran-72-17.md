## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17-18 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “And if they had remained steadfast upon the right path, We would have provided them with abundant water,” meaning that if these unjust people had remained steadfast upon the path of truth and righteousness, “We would have provided them with abundant water.” He says: If We had expanded their provision and made life easy for them in this world, We would have tested them with it. The scholars of interpretation differed in their understanding of this, with some saying something similar to what we have said. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
