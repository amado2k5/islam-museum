## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Gather those who have wronged, and their spouses, and those who used to worship": In this statement, it is left unnecessary to suffice with the indication of what was left out, which is: it is said: gather those who have wronged, meaning gather those who disbelieved in God in this world and disobeyed Him, their wives, and their followers according to what they used to be of disbelief in God and what they worshipped besides God among the gods. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Suddi, Ibn Zayd, and Samak.
