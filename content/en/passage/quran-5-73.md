**Commentary on verse 73: "Those who say God is the third of three have disbelieved", pp. 481-483**

Al-Tabari says: this too is God's report of another party of the Israelites described in the preceding verses, who in unbelief and association said that God is the third of three. This was the saying of most Christians before the Jacobites, Melkites and Nestorians split; as it reached al-Tabari, they said that the eternal God is one substance comprising three persons: a father who begets and is not begotten, a son begotten who does not beget, and a spouse that follows between them.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 482}

God denies them with "there is no god but one God": you have no object of worship, people, but one, who neither begets anything nor is begotten, being the creator of every begetter and begotten. "If they do not stop what they say, a painful punishment will touch those of them who disbelieved" covers both those who hold this saying and those who say He is the Messiah son of Mary, because both parties are unbelieving polytheists. For that reason the threat is made general and does not say "will touch them", since then it would have been limited to those who say third of three and left out those who say the Messiah is God. The pronoun in "of them" returns to the Children of Israel, so the sense is that painful punishment touches those who say the Messiah is God, those who say third of three, and every unbeliever who followed their path.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 483}

Report 12294, from al-Suddi, says the Christians said "He, the Messiah and his mother", which is the point of the verse near the surah's end; report 12295, from Mujahid, is to the same effect.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 483}
