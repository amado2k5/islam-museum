## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And he had no authority over them except that We would know who believes in the Hereafter among those who are in doubt from it, and your Lord is over all things Guardian." He explains: Satan had no argument against these people whose attributes he described as a proof by which He misled them except that We would impose it upon them, so that Our party and allies would know "whoever believes in the Hereafter," meaning: whoever believes in resurrection, reward, and punishment "from among those who are doubtful of it" will not be certain of the Hereafter, nor will he believe in reward or punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
