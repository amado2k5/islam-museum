## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 31 to 32 together in one place. Al-Tabari explains that 'they turn to Him' means those who repent, returning to Allah, turning to Him. He quotes Ibn Zayd that the one who turns to Allah is the one who turns to Him and returns to His obedience, who returns to what he was, commands piety of Him, establishing prayer, and not being among the polytheists, those who have divided their religion and become sects, each party rejoicing in what they have. {cite:altafsir-tabari} — https://www.altafsir.com/Tafasir.asp?tMadhNo=0&tTafsirNo=1&tSoraNo=30&tAyahNo=32&tDisplay=yes&UserProfile=0&LanguageId=1
