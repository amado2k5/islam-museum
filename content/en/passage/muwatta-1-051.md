## Summary
A ruling of Malik transmitted by Yahya in the chapter on what does not require ablution: he was asked whether one who vomits food must perform ablution. He said no; he should rinse his mouth and wash his mouth. The editor refers to the jurists' views in Abd al-Razzaq and al-Tirmidhi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.60}
