## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15-18 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase, “When his Lord called to him in the sacred valley of Tuwa,” as meaning: “We used to say that it was sanctified twice, and the name of the valley is Tuwa.” Others said: Rather, its meaning is: “Tread the earth barefoot.” Some of those who said this mentioned, on the authority of Sufyan, on the authority of Ibn Jurayj, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding the verse, “Indeed, you are in the sacred valley of Tuwa,” that it means: “Tread the earth with your foot.” Others said: Rather, its meaning is that the valley was sanctified twice. We have already explained all of this and its various interpretations previously, which makes repeating it unnecessary here. Al-Hasan recited it with a kasra on the ta’, and said: “Blessing and sanctification were bestowed upon it twice.” This was also narrated on the authority of Awf, on the authority of Al-Hasan. The reciters differed in their reading of it. The majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "ṭuwā" with a ḍammah and did not pronounce it with a kasra. Some of the people of Syria and Kufa read it as "ṭuwan" with a ḍammah on the ṭāʾ and tanwīn. He also said: “And in the passage, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.”
