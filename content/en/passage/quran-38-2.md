## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 1–2. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among interpreters regarding the meaning of 'Sad': Some said it is from 'Masada', meaning he opposed the Qur'an with your deeds, and others said otherwise. Then he explains that Allah swore by the Qur'an with the Reminder that those who disbelieved are in honor and discord.
