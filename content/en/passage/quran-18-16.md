## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And [mentioned] when you withdrew from them and worshipped none but Allah, then took refuge in the cave; your Lord will spread for you of His mercy and prepare for you of your affair an easement": Allah Almighty says, informing about some young men saying to others: "And when you withdraw, O young men, your people who took gods besides Allah" (and they worship none but Allah) means: And when you withdraw from your people who worship gods besides Allah, then "Ma" means in the accusative case as a conjunction to the 'h', and the meem in His saying: 'And when you withdrew from them.' In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
