## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Bukayr ibn Abdallah ibn al-Ashajj, from al-Numan ibn Abi Ayyash al-Ansari, from Ata ibn Yasar, that a man came asking Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As about a man who divorced his wife three times before touching her. Ata said: I told him the divorce of a virgin is one. Abdullah ibn Amr said to me: you are only a storyteller. One divorce separates, and three forbid her until she marries another husband. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.82}
