## Summary
Malik said: the practice among us is that a man's manumission is not valid when he has a debt that covers his property. The manumission of a boy is not valid until he reaches puberty or reaches the age of puberty, and the manumission of someone under guardianship over his property is not valid even if he reaches puberty, until he takes charge of his property. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.328}
