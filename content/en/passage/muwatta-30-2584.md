## Summary
Malik said: if a group beats a man to death under their hands, they are all killed for him. If he dies after their beating, the oath of fifty applies. If the oath of fifty applies only to one man, and no one else is killed, we know of no oath of fifty that applied to more than one man. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.457}
