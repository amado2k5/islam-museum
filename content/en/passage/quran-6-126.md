## Summary
Verse 126 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 126 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 126, "this is the path of your Lord, straight; We have detailed the signs for a people who remember", al-Tabari says: this that We have made clear to you, Muhammad, in this sura and others is your Lord's path, His way and His religion that He chose for Himself as religion and made straight without crookedness; so stand firm on it, forbid what I forbade you and allow what I allowed you, for We have made the signs and proofs clear for its truth and soundness, "for a people who remember", that is, whoever recalls the signs and lessons God argued with and takes warning from them; He singled out those who remember because they have discernment and understanding, wit and virtue. He gives (13883) Ibn Abbas: the path of your Lord means Islam. The notes refer to the earlier explanation of "the straight path" and of "fassala". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 113}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
