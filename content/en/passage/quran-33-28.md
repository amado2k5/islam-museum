## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 29 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "O Prophet, tell your wives, if you desire the life of this world and its adornments, then come, I will entertain you and release you in a beautiful manner": "Say," O Muhammad, "For your wives, if you desire the life of this world and its adornments, then come, I will give you enjoyment." He says, "I am the most entertaining for you." He says: "And grant enjoyment to those who are bound by the extent of their status, and to the one who is stingy, as a kind enjoyment for the doers of good." And His saying: "And He will release you in a beautiful manner," he means: and He will release you according to what Allah has permitted and refined His servants by saying: "When you divorce women, then divorce them for their waiting period." "And if you seek the pleasure of Allah and His Messenger," he means: And if you seek the pleasure of Allah and His Messenger and obey them, then obey them, "Indeed, Allah has prepared for the doers of good among you."
