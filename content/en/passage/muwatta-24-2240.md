## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger said: whoever frees his share in a slave, and has property worth the slave's price, the slave is valued at a fair price, and he gives his partners their shares, and the slave is freed by him; otherwise the part he freed is freed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.323}
