## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 16–17. Al-Tabari mentions that Sheba turned away from obeying her Lord and from following His messengers, so God sent upon them the flood of 'Arim and replaced them with two gardens of food, with a small amount of lote tree, as a reward for their disbelief, and only the disbelievers will be punished.
