## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nahl 16:101, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «وإذا بدلنا آية مكان آية».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

نزلت حين قال المشركون: إن محمداسخر بأصحابه يأمرهم
