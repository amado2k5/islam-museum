## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 39–41. Al-Tabari mentions that God will gather all the disbelievers and then say to the angels, 'Are these those whom you used to worship?' The angels disassociate themselves and say, 'Glory be to You! You are our protector besides them.' Rather, they used to worship the jinn, most of whom were believers in them.
