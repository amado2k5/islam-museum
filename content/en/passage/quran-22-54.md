## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So that those who have been given knowledge may know that it is the truth from your Lord and believe in it, and their hearts humble to Him. And indeed, Allah is the Guide to the Straight Path." So that the people of knowledge may know that what Allah revealed of His signs which He made clear for His Messenger and abrogated what Satan cast into it is the truth from your Lord, O Muhammad, "so that they may believe in it," meaning they believe in it. "So their hearts humbled themselves to Him," meaning their hearts submitted to the Qur'an, submitting to its belief and acknowledgment of what is in it. "And indeed, Allah guides those who believe to a straight path." And indeed, Allah guides those who believe in Allah and His Messenger to the truth, abrogating what Satan cast into the wish of his Messenger, so that Satan's plotting and falsehood on the tongue of their Prophet will not harm them.
