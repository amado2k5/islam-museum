## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id, in the chapter on voluntary prayer on a journey: Anas ibn Malik was seen on a journey praying on a donkey facing away from the qibla, bowing and prostrating by gesture, without placing his face on anything. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it, and that al-Nasa'i reported by a good chain from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Anas that he saw the Prophet pray on his donkey, mounted, toward Khaybar with the qibla behind him. In the Sahihayn, from Anas ibn Sirin, that he saw Anas pray on a donkey facing away from the qibla and said: had I not seen the Messenger of God do it, I would not do it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.216}
