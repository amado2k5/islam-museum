## Summary
Malik transmits from Ziyad ibn Abi Ziyad, client of Abdullah ibn Ayyash ibn Abi Rabia, from Talha ibn Ubaydallah ibn Kariz, that the Messenger of God said: the best supplication is the supplication of the day of Arafat, and the best that I and the prophets before me have said is: there is no god but God alone, without partner. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.564}
