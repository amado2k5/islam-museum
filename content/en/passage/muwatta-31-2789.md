## Summary
Yahya ibn Said narrated to Malik that the Prophet said to a milch camel: who will milk this? A man stood and asked him his name; he said: Murra. He said: sit down. Another stood and said: Harb; he ordered him to sit. A third stood and said: Yaish; he said to him: milk. The editor notes that al-Bukhari in al-Adab al-Mufrad transmitted it raised from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha from Anas, with a chain the editor considers sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.567}
