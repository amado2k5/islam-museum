**Commentary on verse 30 (pp. 201-202)**

The interpreters differed over who said that Uzayr is the son of God. Some said it was one man, Finhas. Report 16619, from Abdullah ibn Ubayd ibn Umayr, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: one man said it, whose name they say was Finhas, the one who said what is at [[passage/quran-3-181|3:181]], that God is poor and they are rich. Others said it was the saying of a group of them. Report 16620, from Ibn Abbas, through Abu Kurayb, Yunus ibn Bukayr, Muhammad ibn Ishaq, Muhammad ibn Abi Muhammad and Sa'id ibn Jubayr or Ikrima: Sallam ibn Mishkam, Nu'man ibn Awfa, Shas ibn Qays and Malik ibn al-Sayf came to the Messenger and asked how they could follow him when he had left their qibla and did not claim that Uzayr was the son of God, and God sent down the verse about their saying. The editor refers to Ibn Hisham's Sira and notes variations in the names there. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 202}

Report 16621, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his grandfather: they said he was God's son because Uzayr was among the People of the Book, who had the Torah, and they acted on it as long as God willed, then neglected it and acted without right, the Ark being among them; when God saw that they had neglected the Torah and followed whims, He lifted the Ark from them, made them forget the Torah and erased it from their breasts, and sent a sickness on them so that their bowels loosened until they forgot the Torah and it was erased from their breasts, Uzayr being among them; they stayed as God willed after it was erased, and Uzayr, who had been one of their scholars, prayed to God and begged Him to return what had been erased from his breast of the Torah; and as he prayed, pleading, a light came down from God and entered his body, and what had gone from within him of the Torah returned; the report continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 202}

We stop here at the foot of p. 202, in the middle of the commentary on verse 30 (within report 16621); the next batch begins at p. 203. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 202}
