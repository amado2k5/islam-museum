## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among His signs is that He sends the winds as bringings of good tidings and that He may make you taste of His mercy and that the ships may sail by His command and that you may seek of His bounty, and that you may be grateful." Among His proofs of His oneness and proof against you is that He is the God of everything: "That He sends the winds as bringings" of rain and mercy, "and that He may make you taste of His mercy." He means: "And He will send down upon you from His mercy, which is the rain by which He revives lands, and ships sail through the seas by His command," and "and you may seek of His bounty," meaning: And to seek from His sustenance and your livelihood which He has distributed among you, "and that you may be grateful," meaning: "And be grateful to your Lord for that. He sent these winds as good tidings." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
