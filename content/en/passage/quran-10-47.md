**Commentary on verse 47 (p. 99)**

On [[passage/quran-10-47|10:47]] he says: every nation before you, people, had a messenger whom I sent to them as I sent Muhammad to you, calling them to God's religion and obedience; "when their messenger comes" means in the next life. Report 17666, from Mujahid: the Day of Resurrection. "It is judged between them with justice": judgement is made between them then with fairness, "and they are not wronged" in any of the repayment for their deeds; the good-doer is repaid for his good, the wrongdoer among the believers is either punished by God or pardoned, and the disbeliever abides in the Fire; that is God's justice between them. Report 17667, from Mujahid: with fairness. The editor refers to the gloss on "justice". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 99}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
