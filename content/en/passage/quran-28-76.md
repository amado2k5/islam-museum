## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Qarun was from the people of Moses, but he transgressed against them, and We gave him treasures. His keys are not to be carried by a group of people of strength." When his people said to him, 'Do not rejoice; indeed, Allah does not love joy.' He said: "Indeed, Qarun," who is Qarun ibn Yashar ibn Qahith ibn Levi ibn Yaqub, "was from the people of Moses," meaning he was from the tribe of Moses ibn Imran, the prophet, and he was his paternal cousin, because Qarun is Qarun ibn Yashar ibn Qahath. And Moses: He is Musa ibn Imran ibn Qahith, and this is how Ibn Jurayj lineaged. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj regarding His saying: "Qarun was from the people of Moses," he said: his cousin, the nephew of his father. For Qarun ibn Yasfar, this is what al-Qasim said, and he is only Yashar ibn Qahith, and Musa ibn Umar ibn Qahith, and 'Umar in Arabic means Imran. As for Ibn Ishaq, it is Ibn Humayd. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibrahim, Samak, and Sufyan.
