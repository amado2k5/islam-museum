## Summary
Malik transmits from al-Ala ibn Abd al-Rahman that he heard him say: charity has never decreased wealth; God adds to a servant who pardons only honour; and no servant humbles himself without God raising him. Malik said: I do not know whether this hadith is raised to the Prophet or not. The editor notes that it is raised in the hadith of al-Ala from his father from Abu Hurayra in Muslim and elsewhere. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.599}
