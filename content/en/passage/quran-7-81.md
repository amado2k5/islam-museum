## Summary
Verse 81 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 81 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-81|7:81]], al-Tabari reports that Lot said to his people, rebuking them for what they did: you, people, come to men from behind out of lust for it instead of what God made lawful for you among women; "no, you are a people who transgress" means you do what God has forbidden you and disobey Him by this act, and that is the transgression meant here. "Shahwa" is a verbal noun from "shahiytu" (I desired) a thing. He cites two lines by a poet the editor could not identify, about a travelling companion, dishevelled, who longs for sleep, and notes that the second line occurs in al-Akhtal as well. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 548}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
