## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We saved him and the companions of the Ark and made it a sign for the worlds": So We saved Noah and the companions of his ark, who carried them in his ark from his sons and their wives. We have explained this before and mentioned the narrations about it, so it is more necessary to repeat it here. "And We made it a sign for the worlds" means: We made the ark in which We saved him and his companions a lesson and admonition for the worlds, and a proof against them. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
