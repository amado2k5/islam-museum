## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 41–44. Al-Tabari explains that among the verses is the carrying of their descendants in the loaded ship, meaning the filled ark, which is Noah's Ark, and that God created for them from the like what they ride, and if it arises, He drowns them, so they neither cry out nor save them except as mercy from Us and a temporary enjoyment.
