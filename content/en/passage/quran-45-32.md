## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when it is said, 'Indeed, the promise of Allah is true, and the Hour is undoubted,' you say, 'We do not know what the Hour is; we think nothing but assumption, and we are not certain.'" They are then told: 'And when it is said,' to you, 'Indeed, the promise of Allah,' who promised His servants, that He will revive them after their death and resurrect them from their graves, is 'true,' and the Hour He told them He would establish for their gathering, gathering them for reckoning and reward for obedience and punishment for disobedience, with 'no doubt' in it, as he says: There is no doubt about it, meaning in the Hour, and the 'ha' in His saying: 'in it' is from the mention of the Hour. The meaning of the statement: 'By the Hour, there is no doubt about its occurrence. So fear Allah, believe in Allah and His Messenger, and act upon what will save you from Allah's punishment during it.' You said, 'We do not know what the Hour is,' denying Allah's promise, rejecting His news, and denying His ability to bring you back to life after your death.
