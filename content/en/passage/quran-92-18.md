## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 25 as a single section. He explains the phrase, “And what will his wealth avail him when he falls?” meaning, may His praise be exalted, that what wealth will avail this person who was stingy with his wealth and considered himself independent of his Lord will be protected on the Day of Resurrection “when he falls.” Then the scholars of interpretation differed in their interpretation of “when he falls.” Some said its interpretation is: when he falls into Hell, meaning he falls into it and plummets. In the section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
