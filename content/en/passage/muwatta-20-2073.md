## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman, from Handhala ibn Qays al-Zuraqi, from Rafi ibn Khadij, that the Messenger forbade renting out farmland. Handhala said: I asked Rafi ibn Khadij: for gold and silver? He said: as for gold and silver, there is no harm. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.249}
