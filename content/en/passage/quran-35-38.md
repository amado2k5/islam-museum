## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, Allah is the Knower of the unseen of the heavens and the earth. Indeed, He is Knowing of the Essence of the hearts": "So taste the fire of the punishment of Hell which you have prayed, O disbelievers in Allah." So for the wrongdoers is there no helper. He means: So what is it for the disbelievers who wronged themselves and earned the wrath of Allah by disbelief in Allah in this world? Who will help them from Allah to save them from His punishment? And His saying: "Indeed, Allah is the Knower of the unseen of the heavens and the earth," He, the Exalted, says: Allah is Knowing what you conceal within yourselves, O people, and conceal it, and what you do not conceal or intend, of what you intend, and what is hidden from your eyes in the heavens and the earth. So fear Him lest He may appear upon you, while you harbor doubt within yourselves about the oneness of Allah or in the prophethood of Muhammad, other than what you reveal with your tongues. "Indeed, He is Knowing of what is in the hearts."
