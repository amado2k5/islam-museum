## Summary
Malik transmits from Dawud ibn al-Husayn, from Abu Sufyan, client of Ibn Abi Ahmad, from Abu Said al-Khudri, that the Messenger forbade muzabana and muhaqala. Muzabana is buying dates for dates on the palm tops; muhaqala is renting land for wheat. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.149}
