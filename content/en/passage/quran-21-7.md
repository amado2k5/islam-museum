## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We did not send before you except men to whom We revealed. So ask the people of the Reminder if you do not know": He says to His Prophet: And We did not send before you, O Muhammad, a messenger to a nation that passed before you, except men like them. We reveal to them what We intend to reveal to them of Our command and prohibition, not angels. So why do they deny that We sent you to them, while you are a man like the other messengers before you to their nations? In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
