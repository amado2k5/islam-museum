## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 33 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'If I had brought you a clear thing': Moses said to Pharaoh when his Lord recognized him and that He is the Lord of the East and the West, and called him to worship Him and to devote deity to Him. Pharaoh replied, 'If you take a god other than Me, I will surely make you among the imprisoned.' Would you make me among the imprisoned? 'And if I brought you something clear,' it would clarify to you the truth of what I say, O Pharaoh, and the reality of what I call you to? He only said this to him, because among people's morals is to remain calm for fairness and to respond to the truth after clarification. When Moses told him what he said, Pharaoh said to him: Bring what is truly clear about what you say, for We will not imprison you then if you take a god other than Me if you are among the truthful. He says: If you are truthful in what you say and truthful in what you describe and report. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
