## Summary
The first report in the comprehensive chapter on seeking reward in misfortune. Malik transmits, as a mursal report, from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq, that the Prophet said: may the Muslims be consoled in their calamities by the calamity of me. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik, citing al-Tamhid (19/322). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.323}
