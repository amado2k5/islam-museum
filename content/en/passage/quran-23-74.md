## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 74 to 75 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And those who do not believe in the Hereafter will turn away from the path": those who do not believe in the resurrection after death, the Hour will be established, and God's recompense for His servants in the Hereafter. "We will turn away from the path," meaning they will turn away from the path, meaning they will turn away from the path, and that is the religion of God that He has chosen for His servants for the just. It is said: so-and-so turned away from such-and-such: if he turned away from him, and he turned away from him, meaning he turned away from him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ata.
