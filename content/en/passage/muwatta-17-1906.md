## Summary
Malik says: the explanation of what is disliked is that a camel is taken for two camels with no difference between them in nobility or in riding. When it is as you have described, two of them should not be bought for one on a term. There is no harm in selling what you bought from them before taking possession, to someone other than the one you bought from, once you have paid its price. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.181}
