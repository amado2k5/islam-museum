## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 172 to 175 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then We destroyed the others": Then We destroyed the others from the people of Lot by destruction, "and We rained down upon them rain," and that was because God sent stones from the sky "How terrible was the rain of the warners." He says: How wretched was that rain of the people whom their prophet warned about, but they denied it. "Indeed, in that is a sign." God Almighty says: Indeed, in Our destruction is the destruction of the people of Lot, which We described as denying Our Messenger. It is a lesson and admonition for your people, O Muhammad, to take heed in their denial of you, and to return to you what you brought them from your Lord of the truth. "And most of them were not believers" in the previous knowledge of Allah.
