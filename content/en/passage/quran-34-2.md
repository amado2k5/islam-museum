## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He knows what enters the earth and what comes out of it, what descends from the sky and what ascends therein, and He is the Merciful, the Forgiving": He knows what enters the earth and what disappears therein, from their saying: 'Waljat' means to enter it, as the poet said: I saw the rhymes swollen, sludge, so that needles would swallow them, meaning by saying: 'They swallow in such-and-such': they enter the entrances "and what comes out of it," meaning: What comes out of the earth, "and whatever descends from the sky and what ascends therein" means: what ascends in the heaven. This is a sign from Allah, the Knower to whom nothing in the heavens and the earth is hidden, from what appears in them and what is hidden. "And He is the Most Merciful, the Forgiving." He is the Most Merciful to the repentant servants to punish them after their repentance, the Most Forgiving of their sins if they repent from them.
