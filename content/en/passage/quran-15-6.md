## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 6 and 7 together: The polytheists among your people said to you, O Muhammad, 'O you upon whom the Reminder has been revealed, indeed you are mad'—and the Qur'an, in which Allah mentioned the admonitions of His creation, according to Al-Dahhak, is the Qur'an—is mad in your supplication to us to follow you and abandon our gods. And 'If you had not brought us the angels, if you were among the truthful': Would you not come to us with the angels as witnesses to the truth of what you say? If you are truthful, Allah sent you as a messenger and sent down a book upon you. The Lord who did what you say would not be able to send you an angel as proof. Al-Tabari notes that the Arabs place 'luma' instead of 'lo'la' ('lola'), and vice versa, citing a verse by Ibn Muqbil.
