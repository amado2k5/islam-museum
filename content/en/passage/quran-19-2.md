## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Mention the mercy of your Lord to His servant Zechariah": The people of Arabic differed regarding the nominative mention and the predicate for the servant. Some grammarians from Basra said about this meaning as if he said: What is insufficient for you is the mention of the mercy of your Lord to His servant, and the servant is accusative with mercy as you say: the mention of Zayd's striking for a lifetime. Some grammarians of Kufa said: I raised the mention with a kahi's 'a's, and if you wish, you may implicate it; this is the mention of your Lord's mercy. He said: The meaning is to mention your Lord His servant by His mercy before and after. Abu Ja'far said: The correct view in my view is that the mention is nominative with an implicit omitted form, and this is as He did in other surahs, such as Allah's saying: "Disassociation from Allah and His Messenger," and His saying: "A surah We have revealed," and the like. The servant is accusative with mercy, and Zakariya in the accusative case, because it is an indication of the servant. So the interpretation is: This is the mention of the mercy of your Lord, His servant Zechariah. And His saying: and in the Maqta' he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
