## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 40 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “This is the Day of Judgment; We have gathered you and the former generations”: God Almighty says to these deniers of the Resurrection on the Day they are resurrected: This is the Day of Judgment on which God will judge with truth between His servants. “We have gathered you and the former generations” means: We have gathered you on this day for the appointed time that We promised you in this world, the gathering on this day between you and all those who came before you from the perished nations. We have fulfilled that promise to you.
