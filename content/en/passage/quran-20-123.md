## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He said, 'Go down from it, all of you, enemies to others. But if guidance comes to you from Me, then whoever follows My guidance will neither go astray nor suffer.'" Allah Almighty said to Adam and Eve: "Go down, all of you," to the earth, "Some of you are enemies to others," meaning: You are the enemy of Iblis and his descendants, and Iblis is your enemy and the enemy of your descendants. And His saying: "But when guidance comes to you from Me," it means: If guidance comes to you, O Adam, Eve, and Iblis, it means guidance from Me: it means clarification of My path and the religion I have chosen for My creation. "So whoever follows My guidance," he means: Whoever follows My guidance and acts upon it, and does not deviate from it, "let him not go astray," meaning: He will not deviate from the proof of the truth, but he will be guided in this world and guided, "and will not suffer" in the Hereafter by God's punishment, because God will admit him to Paradise and save him from His punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ikrimah, and Ata.
