## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that he did not gather two sets of seven together without praying between them; rather, after every seven he prayed two rak'as, and sometimes he prayed by the Station or elsewhere. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1291) and Suwayd (554) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.494}
