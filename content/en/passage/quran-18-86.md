## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Until when he reaches the setting of the sun and finds it setting in the spring of Hamiyah, and there is a people there. We said, 'When the two centuries come, either to be punished or to treat them well.'" "Until" the two Qarnayn reached the setting of the sun in the spring of Hamiyah. The reciters differed in their interpretation. Some reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "in the spring of Hamiyah," meaning it sets in a water source with mud. A group of the reciters of Medina and the general reciters of Kufa interpreted it as follows: "In a hot spring" means it sets in a hot spring. The scholars of interpretation differed in their interpretation of this, just as the reciters differed in its reading. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
