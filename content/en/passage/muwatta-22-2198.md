## Summary
He said: I also heard Malik say: the agreed practice among us regarding someone who gives his child a gift, or a grant that is not a charity: he may take it back, so long as the child has not incurred a debt that people have lent him on the security of that gift from his father. If he has incurred debts, the father may take none of it back. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.301}
