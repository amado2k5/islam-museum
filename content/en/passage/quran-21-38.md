## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 39 in a single passage. He interprets His saying, "He created man from hastening; I will show you my signs, so do not be hasty." He interprets the verse as "He created man," meaning Adam "from hastening." Interpreters differed in its interpretation; some said: its meaning is: whoever hastened in his structure and creation was from hastening, and upon hastening. In the verse, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
