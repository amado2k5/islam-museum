## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 104 to 105 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, shall we inform you of those who are the losers in deeds": "Say," O Muhammad, to those who seek your disgrace, argue with you falsely, and debate with you about matters from among the People of the Two Scriptures: the Jews and the Christians, "shall we inform you"—O people, "who are the losers in deeds"—meaning those who tire themselves in a task seeking profit and virtue, and have suffered damage and destruction without realizing their demand, like a buyer of a commodity hoping for bounty and profit, so his hope fails, his sale is lost, and the one whose bounty is cursed. The interpreters differed regarding those who meant this; some said: It referred to monks and priests. In the Maqta', statements from Abraham are also transmitted.
