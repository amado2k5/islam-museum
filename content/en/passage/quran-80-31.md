## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 42 as a single section. He explains the phrase “and fruits and herbage”: “Fruits” refers to what people eat from the fruits of trees, and “herbage” refers to what animals eat from grass and plants.
