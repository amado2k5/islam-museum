## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 117 to 120 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'My Lord, indeed my people lie': Noah said: 'My Lord, indeed my people denied' regarding the truth You brought them from You, and they returned my advice to me: 'So open between Me and them a victory,' meaning: Judge between Me and them by a judgment from Yourself, by which You destroy the falsehood, and take revenge on those who disbelieved in You, denied Your oneness, and denied Your Messenger. Also: 'So judge between Me and them by judgment.' Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase "So open a victory between Me and them," he means: Judge between me and them, and "and save me," meaning: and save me from that punishment which you bring as a judgment between me and them. "And those who are with me among the believers," he means: those with me among the people of faith and affirmation of Me. And his saying, and in the Maqta' he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
