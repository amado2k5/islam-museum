## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd ibn Qays al-Makki, from Mujahid, that he said: Abdullah ibn Umar borrowed some silver dirhams from a man, then repaid him better dirhams than them. The man said: Abu Abd al-Rahman, these are better than my dirhams that I lent you. Abdullah ibn Umar said: I know, but I am content with that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.213}
