## Summary
In the Book of the Beginning of Creation, the chapter on the creation of Adam and his descendants: the Islamic greeting is set in the form of the first creation, and the rule that whoever enters Paradise will be in the image of Adam. It is the Sahih text on Adam. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
