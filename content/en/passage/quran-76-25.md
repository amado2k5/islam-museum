## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 27 as one section. He explains His saying, “And remember the name of your Lord morning and evening”: “And remember,” O Muhammad, “the name of your Lord,” so call upon Him by it in the morning in the dawn prayer, and in the evening in the noon and afternoon prayers. “And during the night prostrate to Him,” he says: And during the night prostrate to Him in your prayer, so glorify Him for a long night, meaning: most of the night, as He, may His praise be exalted, said: “Stand [in prayer] at night, except a little - half of it, or subtract from it a little, or add to it.” And in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
