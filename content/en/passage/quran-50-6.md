## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Rather, they denied the truth when it came to them, so they are in a difficult matter": What happened to these polytheists who said, "If we die and we are dust, that is a distant return," in their statement, "Rather, they denied the truth," which is the Qur'an "when it came to them" from God. For example: And from Qatadah, "Rather, they denied the truth when it came to them," meaning they lied about the Qur'an: "They are in a matter of Murij," meaning: they are in a mixed matter that is confused to them, they do not know its truth from its falsehood. It is said: the matter of people is mixed and neglected. The expressions of the interpreters have differed in their interpretation, although their meanings are similar. Some said: it means they are in a reprehensible matter, and they said: Al-Marij is the reprehensible thing. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
