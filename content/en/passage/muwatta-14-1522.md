## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Zayd ibn Thabit was asked about a man who married a woman and divorced her before consummation, and whether her mother was lawful to him. Zayd said no: the mother is not conditioned, and the condition applies only to the stepdaughters. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.39}
