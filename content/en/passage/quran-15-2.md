## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari balances the Basran school of thought, who interpret 'ma' as meaning something so that the meaning is 'lord of affection,' and the school of the Kufans, who believe that 'Lord' does not appear in Arabic in the future except as a metaphorical use in terms of promise and warning, citing examples from the Qur'an and poetry. He then explains the verse as saying that those who deny the oneness of God wish in this world that they were Muslims.
