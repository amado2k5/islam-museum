## Summary
Verse 95 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 95 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-95|7:95]], al-Tabari says: then We changed, for the people of the town whose people We had seized with misery and hardship, the evil, which is the misery and hardship, for the good. It was called evil because it grieves people, while the good is ease, blessing and breadth of living; "until they increased" means until they multiplied, and anything that multiplies is said to have "afa", as in a line of Labid. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 573}

(14879) from Qatada that "in place of the evil, the good" is ease in place of hardship; (14880) and (14881) from Mujahid that the evil is harm and the good is ease, wealth and children, or good; (14882) from Ibn Abbas, ease in place of hardship; (14883) from Ibn Zayd that We exchanged what they disliked for what they loved in this world until they were released from that punishment. They differed on "until they increased"; some said what we have said. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 574}

(14884) from Ibn Abbas, until they multiplied and their wealth multiplied; (14885) from him, until they flourished; (14886) and (14887) from Mujahid, their wealth and children multiplied; (14888) from al-Suddi, until they multiplied; (14889) from Ibrahim, until they flourished and multiplied; (14890) from Ibn Abbas, until they flourished; (14891) from al-Dahhak, they flourished and multiplied; (14892) from Mujahid, their wealth and children multiplied; (14893) from Ibn Zayd, they multiplied as plants and feathers multiply, "rish" being goods and property, and then He seized them suddenly while they did not perceive. The editor explains "jamma" and "istajamma" as multiplying. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 575}
