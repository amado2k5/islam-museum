## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "but if they desist, God is forgiving, merciful", al-Tabari says: if the unbelievers who fight you cease from fighting you and from their disbelief in God, leave that and repent, God is forgiving of the sins of those of them who believed and repented, and merciful to him in his hereafter; from Mujahid, "if they desist" means if they repent. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 569}
