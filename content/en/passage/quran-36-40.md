## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 39–40. Al-Tabari mentions the differences among the reciters regarding the raising and setting of the moon, explaining that God decreed the moon in stages until it returned like the old cage, and neither the sun should perceive the moon nor the night before the day, and each in an orbit swims.
