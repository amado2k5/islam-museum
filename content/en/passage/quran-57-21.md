## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Hasten to forgiveness from your Lord and a Garden as wide as the width of the heavens and the earth, prepared for those who believe in Allah and His messengers. That is the bounty of Allah that He grants to whom He wills. And Allah is the possessor of great bounty": "Race yourselves, O people, to a deed that obliges you to a deed that obliges you to seek forgiveness from your Lord and a Garden as wide as the width of the heavens and the earth prepared again." This is Paradise "for those who believe in Allah and His messengers," meaning those who have united Allah and believed in His messengers. And His saying: "That is the bounty of Allah; He grants it to whom He wills," the Exalted says: This is the Paradise which He has offered as the width of the heavens and the earth which Allah has prepared for those who believe in Allah and His messengers. Allah's bounty He bestowed upon the believers, and Allah grants His bounty to whomever He wills of His creation. He is the possessor of great bounty upon them, by abundant provision in this world, granted them blessings, and recognized them in the place of gratitude, then rewarded them in the Hereafter for obedience as He has prepared for them.
