## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said from Sulayman ibn Yasar that he said: I found people who, when they gave for the expiation of an oath, gave a mudd of wheat by the small mudd, and considered that sufficient for them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.616}
