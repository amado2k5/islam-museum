## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 5 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant": Have you not looked, O Muhammad, with the eye of your heart, and you see with it "how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant?" Those who came from Yemen intending to destroy the Kaaba from Abyssinia, led by Abraha al-Habashi al-Ashram. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Hasan.
