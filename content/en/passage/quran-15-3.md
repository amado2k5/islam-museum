## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says that the address to the Prophet is: Leave these polytheists to eat in this world what they have eaten and enjoy its pleasures and desires until their appointed time, and distract them from hope from taking their share of obedience to Allah in it and from seeking their reward in ways that bring them closer to their Lord. And "they will know": They will know tomorrow, when they come to Allah having perished because of their disbelief and polytheism, when they witness His punishment, that they were in loss and recovery in the pleasures and desires they enjoyed.
