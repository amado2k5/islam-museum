## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So he called her from beneath it, 'Do not grieve; your Lord has made beneath you a secret.'" Reciters differed in its reading; the majority of the reciters of Hijaz and Iraq read it as "and he called her from beneath it," meaning: Gabriel called her from before her, though some differ in its interpretation. Some interpret it when it is read "from beneath it," and others interpret it as Jesus, and that he called her from beneath it after she gave birth to him. Some reciters from Kufa and Basra read this: "He called her from beneath her" with a fatha on the two under her, meaning: the one beneath her called her, meaning that the one beneath her called out to her, and that he was the one who called his mother. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and others.
