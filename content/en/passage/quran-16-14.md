## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And it is Who subjected the sea so that you may eat from it tender flesh and extract from it ornaments to wear and see the stars pierced in it and seek of His bounty, and perhaps you will be grateful": He who did these things for you and bestowed upon you, O people, these blessings, who subjected the sea to you, which is every river, salty or fresh. "So that you may eat from it tender flesh," which is the fish from which one catches. "And you extract from it an ornament to wear," which is pearls and coral. As Hisham informed us, from Amr, from Sa'id, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "And He it is He who subjected the sea to eat from it tender flesh," he said: both of them. "And you extract from it an ornament to wear," he said: This is the pearl. And from Qatadah: "So that you may eat from it tender meat," meaning sea whales. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
