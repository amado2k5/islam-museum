## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Rather, We throw the truth upon falsehood, and it will be destroyed, and behold, it will vanish. And woe to you is what you describe": But We send down the truth from Us, which is the Book of God and His revelation upon disbelief in it and its people. "And He will stamp it," meaning: He will destroy it as a man would stamp a man by slapping a sword on his head that reaches the brain, and if the sword reaches that stubbornness, he will have no life afterward. And His saying, 'and behold, he is perishing,' means: 'and behold, he is perishing, decaying,' as from Ma'mar, from Qatadah: 'and when he is destroy,' he said: perished. And from Qatadah: 'And when he is destroy,' he said: he is gone. In the Maqta, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
