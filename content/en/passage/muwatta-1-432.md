## Summary
The first report in the chapter on wiping the pebbles during prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Abu Ja'far al-Qari: Abdullah ibn Umar, when he bent to prostrate, wiped the pebbles at the place of his forehead with a light wipe. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.223}
