**Commentary on verse 10 (pp. 536-537)**

On "their messengers said: is there doubt about God, Creator of the heavens and the earth? He calls you to forgive you some of your sins and to defer you until a named term. They said: you are only human like us, wanting to bar us from what our fathers used to worship; so bring us a clear authority", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: the messengers of the nations to whom their messengers came said, "is there doubt about God", that He is the One entitled to divinity and worship from you, people, to the exclusion of all {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 536}

His creation, "Creator of the heavens and the earth", that is, their maker, "He calls you to forgive you some of your sins", he says, He calls you to His oneness and obedience, "to forgive you some of your sins", that is, to veil some of your sins by pardoning them so as not to punish you for them, "and to defer you", he says, to postpone your terms, so as not to punish you at once and destroy you but to defer you to the time written in the Mother of the Book at which He will take you, which is the term He has named for you. The nations said to them, "you are only", people, "human like us" in form and appearance, and not angels, and you only want by this you say to us "to bar us from what our fathers used to worship", he says, you only want to turn us by your words from worshipping the idols that our fathers used to worship, "so bring us a clear authority", he says, bring us a proof of what you say that makes its truth and soundness clear to us, so that we know that you are right in what you say. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 537}
