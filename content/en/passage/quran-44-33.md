## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 33 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We gave them of the signs in which there is a clear trial": And We gave them lessons and admonitions that are tests that make it clear to those who reflect that it is a test with which God tested them. The interpreters differed regarding this affliction, with some saying: He tested them with His blessings with them. In the verse, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
