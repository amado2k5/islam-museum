## Summary
Malik said: the sale of a conditionally freed slave is not lawful, and no one may buy him, unless the conditionally freed slave buys himself from his master, which is lawful for him, or someone gives money to the master of the conditionally freed slave and the master who conditionally freed him frees him, which is lawful too. His clientage belongs to the master who conditionally freed him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.374}
