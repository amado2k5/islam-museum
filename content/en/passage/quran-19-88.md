## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 88 to 90 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Have the Most Merciful taken a son?'" and these disbelievers in God said, "The Most Merciful has taken a son. Indeed, you have come with a [something]," meaning that He created those who say: 'You have indeed come to you, O people, with a great and reprehensible word.'
