## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Those are upon guidance from their Lord, and those are the successful": these are the ones whose attributes have been described as clear from their Lord and light. "And those are the successful." He means: These are the successful ones who attain what they hoped for and hoped for from their Lord's reward on the Day of Resurrection.
