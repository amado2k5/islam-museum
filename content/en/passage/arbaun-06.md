## Summary
Hadith of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir: the lawful is clear and the unlawful is clear, and between them are doubtful matters that many people do not know. Whoever avoids doubtful matters protects his religion and honour, and whoever falls into them falls into the unlawful, like a shepherd grazing near a protected pasture. The body is set right by one lump of flesh, the heart. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
