## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He it is Who sent down tranquility into the hearts of the believers that they might increase in faith along with their faith. And to Allah belong the hosts of the heavens and the earth. And Allah is Knowing and Wise": He means that His glory be to Him by saying: "He is the One who sent down tranquility into the hearts of the believers." Allah sent down tranquility and tranquility in the hearts of the believers in Allah and His Messenger to faith, and the truth with which Allah sent you, O Muhammad. The difference of the interpreters regarding the meaning of tranquility has already been mentioned, and the correct view is based on sufficient evidence, not repeating it here. "So that they may increase in faith along with their faith," meaning: to increase by believing in what God renewed of the obligations that were obligatory for them, which were not necessary for them. "Faith along with their faith," meaning they may increase in their faith with the obligations that were necessary for them before. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
