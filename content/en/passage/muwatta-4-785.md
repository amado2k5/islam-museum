## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik about a man who sees the new moon of Ramadan alone: he fasts, and he should not break his fast while knowing that this day is in Ramadan. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.387}
