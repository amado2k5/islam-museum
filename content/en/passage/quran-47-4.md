## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when you meet those who disbelieve, strike the necks until you have made them heavy and tighten the binds. Either from us after or ransom until the war has put its burden on you. And if Allah had willed, He could have won among them, but let some of you test others by means of trial. And those who are killed in the way of Allah, their deeds will never go astray." Allah says to the group of believers in Him and His Messenger: "So when you meet those who disbelieve" by Allah and His Messenger among the people of war, strike their necks. And His saying: "Until, when you have tied them up, bind them," it means: until when you overcome them and conquer those whose necks you do not strike, and they become captives in your hands, "bind them," he means: bind them in bondage so that they do not kill you, and they flee from you. And His saying: "Either after us or ransom," it means: If you capture them after the bondage, either you should do them a favor by releasing them from captivity and freeing them without compensation or ransom, or they will ransom you by giving you a ransom by giving you a ransom so that you can release them and release them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
