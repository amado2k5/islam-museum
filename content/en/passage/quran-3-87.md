## Summary
Verse 87 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 87 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 87: their recompense.** Al-Tabari says the meaning is these who turned back from their faith after they acknowledged that the messenger is true, and that their recompense is the reward of the deed they did. He explains the curse as the distancing and banishment that comes down on them from God, and from the angels and people the invocation against them of the punishment that harms them, the sense being that the cursing is by all of them and not only by some of the angels and people mentioned. God made this the reward of their deed because their deed was disbelief in God. He says he has described the cursing of the unbeliever by people elsewhere, and the editor refers to volumes 2 and 3. In two notes the editor records that the verb stood in the past form in the manuscript and printed text while the sentence requires the present, and that the phrase 'except what harms them' is a misreading of what al-Tabari wrote, 'the invocation of what harms them', the letters having been confused by the copyist. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 577}
