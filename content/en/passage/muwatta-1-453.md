## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu Ja'far al-Qari, in the chapter on turning the head and clapping when needed in prayer: he was praying with Abdullah ibn Umar behind him without knowing it. He turned his head, and Ibn Umar nudged him in disapproval. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.232} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.233}
