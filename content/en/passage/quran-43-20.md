## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 21 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And they say, 'If the Most Merciful had willed, we would not have worshipped them. They have no knowledge of that; they only humble.'" These polytheists from Quraysh said: If the Most Merciful had willed, We would not have worshipped our idols besides Him. Rather, no punishment has befallen us for our worship of them out of His pleasure with us worshipping them. Also: From Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "If the Most Merciful had willed, We would not have worshipped them" refers to idols. Allah Almighty says: "They have no knowledge of that," meaning: they have no knowledge of what they say, but they say it emptily and lying, because they have no knowledge or proof from me about it. They only say it by assumption and calculation: "Indeed, they are only mute," meaning: they are only mumbled. This is what they said, and that is their saying, "If the Most Merciful had willed, We would not have worshiped them." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
