## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 53 to 56 as one section. He explains His saying, “as if they were startled donkeys”: What is wrong with these polytheists who turn away from the reminder, turning away from it like startled donkeys? And in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-A’mash, Sufyan, and Ata.
