## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa that his father Urwa used to pray in the open desert without a screen, in the chapter on the screen for one who prays on a journey. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.223}
