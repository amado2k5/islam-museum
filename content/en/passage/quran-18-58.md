## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And your Lord is the Forgiving, the Possessor of Mercy. If He were to take them to account for what they earned, they would hasten the punishment. Rather, they have a fixed time from which they will find no refuge." And your Lord, O Muhammad, is the Protector of the sins of His servants by His pardon of them when they repent from them. "The Owner of mercy upon them, if He had taken responsibility for what they earned," those who turn away from His verses when they are reminded of their sins and wrongdoings, "He hastened the punishment for them," but it is because of His mercy to His creation that He does not do that for them until their appointed time and term. "Rather, they have a fixed time," meaning: But they have a fixed time, which is the appointed time for their punishment, which is the Day of Badr. "They will not find a refuge besides it," meaning Allah Almighty says: 'These polytheists will not find a refuge, even if the punishment in this world is not hastened for them except the appointed time for their punishment, a refuge to turn to, and a savior to be saved with.' It means they will not find a stronghold to detain them from Allah's punishment. It is said: 'And I turned from such and such to that,' like 'Wa'ul' and 'Awwal,' like 'Wa'ul' and from it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
