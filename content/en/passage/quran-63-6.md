## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “It is all the same to them whether you ask forgiveness for them or do not ask forgiveness for them; God will never forgive them. Indeed, God does not guide the disobedient people,” explains: O Muhammad, it is all the same to these hypocrites who were told, “Come, let the Messenger of God ask forgiveness for you,” whether you ask forgiveness for their sins or do not ask forgiveness for them; God will never forgive them. He says: God will not pardon their sins, but will punish them for them. “Indeed, God does not guide the disobedient people.” He says: God does not grant faith to the people who lie about Him, who disbelieve in Him, and who deviate from His path. Obeying him. The passage quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas.
