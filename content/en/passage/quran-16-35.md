## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who associate others with Allah said, 'If Allah had willed, we would not worship besides Him of anything, neither we nor our forefathers, nor have We deprived anyone besides Him.' This is as those before them did. So is there for the messengers anything but a clear message": And those who associated others with Allah and worshipped idols and idols besides Allah said: We worship these idols except because Allah has approved our worship of them, and we do not forbid what we have been deprived of of the seas and the abundant things except that Allah wills from us and from our forefathers to prohibit them and is pleased with Him. Were it not for that, He would have changed. This is by some of His punishments or by guiding us to other actions. Allah Almighty says: Thus did those before them among the polytheistic nations who deviated from their tradition, saying the same as they said, and followed their path in denying the messengers of Allah and following the misguided deeds of their forefathers.
