## Summary
In the Book of the Virtues of the Qur'an: Hudhayfa's warning from Azerbaijan, the borrowing of Hafsa's pages, the committee of four, the instruction that the text follow Quraysh's dialect, the sending of codices to the regions, and the burning of other versions. It is the full text on the unification of the codex. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
