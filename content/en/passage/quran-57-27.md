## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Then We stood in their footsteps with Our messengers, and We stood firm with Jesus, son of Mary, and gave him the Gospel. And We placed in the hearts of those who followed him compassion, mercy, and monasticism. They invoked it. We did not prescribe it for them except seeking the pleasure of Allah. But they did not care for it as it deserved. So We gave those of them who believed their reward, but many of them were defiantly disobedient." Then We followed in their footsteps with Our messengers whom We sent with clear proofs, following in the footsteps of Noah, Abraham, with Our messengers, and We followed with Jesus, son of Mary: 'And We placed in the hearts of those who followed him' meaning: those who followed Jesus on his way and law, 'compassion,' which is the most severe mercy. 'And mercy and monasticism they invented,' meaning: 'They introduced it as We did not prescribe it upon them.' He means: We did not impose that monasticism upon them except seeking the pleasure of Allah. He means: But they invented it seeking the pleasure of Allah, 'so they did not care for it as it deserved.' Interpreters differed regarding those who did not properly observe monasticism; some said: They are the ones who invented it, did not establish it, but rather changed and opposed the religion of God that Jesus was sent with: so they converted and became Jews. In the passage, statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Sufyan, and Ata.
