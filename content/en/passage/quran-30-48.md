## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah is the One who sends the winds and stirs up clouds and spreads them in the sky as He wills and makes them an eclipse, so you see the rain emerge through it. And when it strikes whoever He wills among His servants, they rejoice": Allah sends the winds and stirs up clouds. He means: the winds create clouds, which is the plural of 'haha'ah' (cloud), meaning 'He spreads them in the sky as He wills.' He says: 'Allah spreads them and gathers them in the sky as He wills.' He said: He expanded it, unified the hā, and removed the articulation point of masculine metonymy, and 'al-sahab' is the plural as I described in response to the word 'al-sahab,' not to its meaning, as it is said: 'This is a good date.' The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
