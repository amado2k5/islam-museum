## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Mujabbar, in the chapter on how to deal with a nosebleed: he saw Salim ibn Abdullah ibn Umar have blood come out of his nose until his fingers were stained, then he twisted it off and prayed without performing ablution. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.81}
