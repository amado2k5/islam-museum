## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from al-Araj, from Abu Hurayra, that he used to say the worst food is the feast to which the rich are invited and the poor are left out. Whoever does not answer the invitation has disobeyed God and His Messenger. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.55}
