## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "You refuse; indeed, knowledge has come to me that which has not come to you, so follow me, I will guide you to a straight path." Abraham said to his father: O father, God has given me knowledge that which He has not given you, so follow me. He says: So accept my advice from me. "I will guide you to a straight path," meaning: I will guide you to the straight path in which you do not stray if you hold to it, which is the religion of God, which is free of deviation. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Abraham.
