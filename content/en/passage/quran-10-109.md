**Commentary on verse 109 (p. 221)**

On [[passage/quran-10-109|10:109]] al-Tabari says: follow, Muhammad, God's revelation that He reveals to you and His sending down that He sends down on you, and act on it, and be patient with what has struck you in God's cause from the polytheists of your people of harm and hardships and with what you have met from them, until God decides His affair concerning them and you by a clear act, 'and He is the best of judges', the best of deciders and the most just of those who separate. He judged between him and them at Badr and killed them by the sword, and ordered His prophet concerning those left among them to treat them as those of them who were destroyed, unless they repent and turn to His obedience. Report 17914, from Ibn Zayd on 'you are not a guardian over them; and be patient until God judges, He is the best of judges': he said this is abrogated; 'until God judges' means God ruled by fighting them and ordered harshness toward them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 221}

The editor notes that at this point a part of the old division of the commentary ends, and that his manuscript here has the words: end of the commentary on Surat Yunus, praise to God alone; it is followed by the commentary on the surah in which Hud is mentioned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 221}

We stop here at the foot of p. 221, where the commentary on Surat Yunus closes in this volume; the next batch begins at p. 222, with the commentary on Surat Hud. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 221}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
