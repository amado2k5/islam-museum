## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 75–76. Al-Tabari mentions that God said to Iblis, 'What prevented you from prostrating to what You created with My hands?' And that this indicates that Adam was created with His hands. Were you arrogant or were you among the high? He said: I am better than him; You created me from fire and created him from clay.
