## Summary
A report of Ma'qil ibn Yasar al-Muzani in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. He narrated it to Ubayd Allah ibn Ziyad on his deathbed, after holding it back for a long time. It is one of the heaviest texts on the leader's responsibility for those he governs. {cite:sahih-muslim}
