## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Among them are women whose gaze is limited, whom no man before them has touched nor touched": He says about these beddings whose linings are made of sparks "women who have limited their gaze to their husbands and do not look at other men." In this section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
