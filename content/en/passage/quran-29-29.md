## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Are you going to come to the men and cut off the way and come to your place in your wrongful community?" The only response of his people was that they said, "Bring us the punishment of Allah if you should be of the truthful": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of Lot to his people, "Are you the people?" "To bring men to their backs and cut off the way," meaning "and cut off the travelers upon you by your evil act," because in what was mentioned about them, they used to do this to travelers who passed by them, those who came to their lands from strangers. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd and Samak.
