**Commentary on verse 200 (pp. 332-333)**

By "and if an incitement from Satan incites you" God means if an anger from Satan angers you that keeps you from turning away from the ignorant and moves you to repay them, "then seek refuge in God", that is, take shelter with God from his incitement; "He is Hearing, Knowing" means that God, with whom you seek refuge from Satan's incitement, hears the ignorance of the ignorant against you and your seeking refuge with Him from his incitement and the other speech of His creatures, nothing of it hidden from Him, and knows what removes Satan's incitement from you and the other affairs of His creatures. Report 15553, from Ibn Zayd: when "take what is easy, enjoin the customary good and turn away from the ignorant" came down, the Messenger of God said, how is it with anger, Lord? and "if an incitement from Satan incites you, seek refuge in God, He is Hearing, Knowing" came down; report 15554, from Qatada: God knew that this enemy is formidable and rebellious. The root of "nazgh" is corruption, and one says "Satan incited between the people" when he corrupted them and set some of them against others; the verb has two forms. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "turning away", "ignorance", "seeking refuge", "hearing" and "knowing". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 332} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 333}
