## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 33 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "She said, 'O the nobles, give me a ruling concerning my affair; I have not been decisive in a matter until you bear witness.'" The Queen of Sheba said to the nobles of her people: "O nobles, give me a ruling concerning my matter," meaning: Advise me concerning my matter, which I have already attended regarding the matter of the author of this letter that was presented to me, so she made the advice a fatwa. And His saying: "I was not decisive in a matter until you testified," it means: I was not a judge of a matter until you testified, so I consulted you about it. Also: Ibn Zayd said: She invited her people to consult them, "O people, give me a fatwa on my matter; I was not decisive in any matter until you testified," meaning in the speech: I would not have decided a matter without you, nor would I have decided a matter, so she said: "I was not decisive in any matter," meaning: a judge. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Ata.
