## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari covers verses 146 to 150 together in one place. Al-Tabari mentions Salih's saying to his people: 'Does your Lord leave you in this world, safe and fearing nothing, in orchards, springs, crops, and palm trees whose leaves are thin, and the leaves are disbelievers.' Their differences are explained about 'al-hidhim': it is said 'the ripe and ripe,' which is Ibn Abbas's view, and it is said 'the shattered, fragmented.'
