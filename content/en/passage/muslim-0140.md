## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim, a question and answer about a man who refuses to give up his property. Its meaning appears in a fragment of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As: "Whoever is killed defending his property is a martyr". {cite:sahih-muslim}
