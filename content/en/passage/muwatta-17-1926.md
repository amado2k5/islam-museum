## Summary
Malik says: whoever advances payment for goods on a term, the buyer should not sell those goods back to their owner for more than what he advanced for them, before receiving what he advanced for. The result is that he returns what he advanced and adds something of his own. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188}
