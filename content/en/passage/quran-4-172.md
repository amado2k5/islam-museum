**Commentary on verse 172: the Messiah does not disdain to be God's servant (pp. 424-425)**

Al-Tabari says 'the Messiah will not disdain to be a servant of God' means he will not be too proud or too haughty; likewise the angels brought near do not disdain to acknowledge God's servanthood. Report 10856, from Qatada: the Messiah is not ashamed to be God's servant, nor are the angels. Report 10857, from al-Ajlah: al-Dahhak said of the brought-near ones that they are the nearest to the second heaven; the editor notes that al-Bazzuri is not known and suggests Ja'far ibn Muhammad al-Wasiti. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 425}

Whoever disdains His worship and is proud, God will gather them all to Him, that is, raise them on the Day of Resurrection and bring them together for their appointment with Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 425}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
