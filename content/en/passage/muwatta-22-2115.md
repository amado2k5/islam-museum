## Summary
Malik says: this applies only to property, and does not apply to any prescribed punishments, or to marriage, divorce, manumission, theft or slander. If someone says manumission is a matter of property, he is mistaken; it is not as he says. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.264}
