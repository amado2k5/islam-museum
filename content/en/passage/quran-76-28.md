## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28-29 as a single section. He interprets the words, “We created them and strengthened their joints, and if We will, We can replace them with others like them”: “We created” these polytheists who disobey God’s commands and prohibitions, “and strengthened their joints”: We strengthened their creation, from the expression, “This man was created and his creation was perfected,” meaning: He was created and his creation was perfected. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
