## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He said, 'We will strengthen your support through your brother and appoint for you authority so that they will not bring upon you Our signs, you and those who follow you who are the victors.'" Allah said to Moses, "We will strengthen your support," meaning we will strengthen you and help you with your brother. The Arabs say: 'If a man honors a man, helps him, and prevents him from those who want him unjustly': so-and-so has tightened his support on so-and-so's arm, and he is the one who supported him on his command: if he helped him. Among them is Ibn Muqbil's saying: 'I supported it with a stick, not a weak one, as if he were standing on ivory, hidden and hidden,' meaning: a bow supported by an arrow. In 'al-'adud' there are four languages: the best of them: 'al-udud', then 'al-'udd, then al-'udud', and al-'udud. All of these are pluralized into 'adadd'. And His saying: 'And We will give you authority over you' means: and we will make for you a proof. Also: From Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, the phrase 'for you two is authority' is a proof. And from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. And from Al-Suddi, 'And We appoint for you authority as authority'; and the authority is the proof. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
