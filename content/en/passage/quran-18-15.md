## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "These are our people, taken gods besides Him, if they did not bring upon them clear authority. So who is more unjust than one who invents a lie against Allah": He, the Exalted, informs about the saying of the young people among the People of the Cave: These are our people, who took gods instead of Allah to worship them instead of Him, "Why did they not bring upon them clear authority?" He says: "Why did they not come to worship them with clear evidence?" In the discourse, it is omitted, and what appears to be omitted is derived from what is omitted, as in His saying: "If they had not brought upon them a clear authority," the letters 'ha' and 'm' refer to them as referring to the gods. The gods are not subject to authority, nor is the ruler questioned about them; rather, their worshippers are questioned by the authority for their worship. It is known, given this fact, that the meaning of the statement is: 'If only they had not come to worship them and made them gods besides Allah with clear authority.' Similarly, as we have said regarding the meaning of authority, the interpreters said. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
