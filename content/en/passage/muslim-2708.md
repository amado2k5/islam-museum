## Summary
A report of Khawla bint Hakim al-Sulamiyya from Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in the chapter on seeking refuge in God's perfect words, in the Book of Remembrance and Supplication of Sahih Muslim. It is the response to one who alights in a dwelling. {cite:sahih-muslim}
