## Summary
In the Book of the Virtues of the Qur'an: the dispute between Umar and Hisham ibn Hakim over their recitation before the Prophet, which contains the well-known text on the seven ahruf. It is the basis of the chapters on the readings, and later Uthman's unification of the text. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
