## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Until, when they come to the valley of the ants, an ant will say, 'O ants, enter your dwellings lest Solomon and his soldiers destroy you while they do not perceive."' This means that Allah mentioned him by saying: "Until, when they come to the valley of the ants," until Solomon and his soldiers came to the valley of the ants, "An ant said, O ants, enter your dwellings so that Solomon and his soldiers do not destroy you," meaning: Solomon and his soldiers will not break you or kill you, "while they do not perceive." He says: And they don't know they're breaking you. They said from Al-A'mash, from a man called Al-Hikam, from Awf, regarding the verse: "An ant said, O ants," he said: The ants of Solomon son of Dawud were like flies. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Al-A'mash.
