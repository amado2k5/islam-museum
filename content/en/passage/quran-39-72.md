## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "It was said, 'Enter the gates of Hell to abide therein forever, so wretched is the abode of the arrogant.": The treasure of Hell will say to those who disbelieved: "Enter the gates of Hell" which are as many as your dwellings therein, "abiding therein forever," meaning: they will remain therein and will not be transferred from it to any other. "So wretched is the abode of the arrogant," meaning: wretched is the dwelling of those who are arrogant against Allah in this world, to unify Him and separate divinity for Him, Hell on the Day of Resurrection.
