## Summary
Verse 68 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 68 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "when you see those who plunge into our signs, turn away from them until they plunge into another talk; and if Satan makes you forget, do not sit after the reminder with the wrongdoing people" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: when you see, Muhammad, the polytheists who plunge into our signs that we sent down to you and our revelation that we revealed to you, their plunging in them being their mocking them, their reviling of the one who sent them down and spoke them, and their denying them, "turn away from them" means turn your face from them, get up from them, and do not sit with them, "until they plunge into another talk" until they take to talk other than mocking God's signs in their conversation; "and if Satan makes you forget" says: if Satan makes you forget our forbidding you to sit with them and turn from them while they plunge into our signs, and then you remember that, get up from them and do not sit after remembering with the wrongdoing people who plunged into what they had no right to plunge into, in what they plunged into; and that is the sense of their wrongdoing here. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 436}

The editor refers for "plunging", "turning away" and "wrongdoing" to earlier passages.

13386 and 13387, from Qatada: God forbade him to sit with those who plunge into God's signs, denying them, and if he forgot, not to sit after remembering with the wrongdoing people. 13388, from Abu Malik and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: those who plunge into our signs are those who deny our signs. 13389, from al-Suddi: when the polytheists sat with the believers they fell on the Prophet and the Qur'an, reviled him and mocked him, so God told them not to sit with them until they plunged into other talk; and "if Satan makes you forget" says: our prohibition, and you sat with them, then when you remember, get up. 13390, from Mujahid: they deny our signs. 13391, from Abu Jafar: do not sit with people of disputation, for they are those who plunge into God's signs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 437}

The editor says the printed text has a different word for "reminder", that he kept the manuscript's, which is entirely right as will appear on p. 439; that al-Suddi's wording is confused in print and manuscript, and he inferred the right form from the commentary on the verse earlier; and that "our prohibition" is the object of "makes you forget" in the early interpreters' habit of abridging.

13392, from Ibn Abbas on this verse and on [[passage/quran-6-159|6:159]], [[passage/quran-3-105|3:105]], [[passage/quran-42-13|42:13]] and the like in the Qur'an: God commanded the believers to unity and forbade disagreement and division, and told them that those before them perished only by wrangling and disputation in God's religion. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 438}

13393, from Mujahid: they mock them, and God's messenger was forbidden to sit with them unless he forgot, and when he remembered to get up; and from Ibn Jurayj: the polytheists used to sit by the Prophet wanting to hear him, and when they heard they mocked, so the verse came down. 13394, from Mujahid: they deny. 13395, from Abu Malik: it means the polytheists, and if you forget and then remember, do not sit with them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 438}

Verse 68 closes here on p. 438, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-69|6:69]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
