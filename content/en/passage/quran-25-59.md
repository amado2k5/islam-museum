## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Who created the heavens and the earth and all that is between them in six days, then established Himself on the Throne of the Most Merciful and was asked about it as an Expert": "And put your trust in the Ever-Living and the One who never dies" "Who created the heavens and the earth and what is between them in six days," so He said: "And what is between them." He mentioned the heavens and the earth, and the heavens in the plural, because he referred to both types and things, as Al-Qatami said: "Did you not grieve that the ropes of Qays and Taghlib have become disjointed?" He means: And ropes that prevailed and bent them, and the plural of ropes was because he meant the two things and types. And His saying: 'in six days' it is said that the beginning of that was on Sunday, and completion was on Friday, 'Then He established Himself on the Throne of the Most Merciful,' meaning: Then He established on the Throne of the Most Merciful and was elevated upon it, and that was on Saturday, as it is said.
