## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Amir ibn Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr from Amr ibn Sulaym al-Zuraqi from Abu Qatada al-Ansari, in the chapter on waiting for prayer and walking to it: the Prophet ordered whoever enters the mosque to pray two rak'as before he sits. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Khuzayma, al-Darimi and Ahmad from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.230}
