## Summary
Malik says it is not lawful for anyone to shave his head until he slaughters his offering, and no one should slaughter before dawn on the day of sacrifice. All the work is on the day of sacrifice: the slaughter, putting on clothes, casting off the untidiness, and shaving; none of that is before the day of sacrifice. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.529}
