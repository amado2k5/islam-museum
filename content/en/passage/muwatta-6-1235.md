## Summary
Malik mentions the saying of Hisham, Urwa and Aisha, and we mention it: he says, then did the people not bring their women if this would not benefit them? If what they say were true, there would be more than six thousand menstruating women in Mina in the morning, all of whom had circumambulated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.552}
