## Summary
It reached Malik from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: whoever breaks the fast because of illness or on a journey must fast Ramadan in consecutive days. The editor notes that Abu Musab (819) and Suwayd (469) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.408}
