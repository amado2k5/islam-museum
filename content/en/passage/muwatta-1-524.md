## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the qibla. Yahya transmits from Malik from Abdullah ibn Dinar from Abdullah ibn Umar: while people at Quba were praying the dawn prayer, a messenger came and said that the Prophet had received a Qur'anic revelation that night and had been ordered to face the Ka'ba, so they faced it, for their faces had been toward Syria, and they turned to the Ka'ba. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad, Ibn Hibban, Ibn Khuzayma, Abu Awana, al-Shafi'i, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and al-Bayhaqi, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.271}
