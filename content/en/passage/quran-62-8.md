## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Say, ‘Indeed, the death from which you flee – indeed, it will meet you. Then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, and He will inform you of what you used to do,’” explains: “Say,” O Muhammad, to the Jews, “Indeed, the death from which you flee,” which you abhor and refuse to wish for, “will meet you,” and will befall you, “then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed.” Then your Lord will return you, after your death, to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Knower of the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and the witnessed: meaning what is witnessed and appears to the eye, and is not hidden from sight. The observers. On the authority of Mu`mar, he said: Qatadah recited: “Then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed.” He said: God humbled the son of Adam with death, and I know of no other way to explain it except that He raised him up. “Then He will inform you of what you used to do.” He says: He will then inform you of what you used to do in the world, both good and bad deeds, because He encompasses them all. Then He will reward the righteous for their good deeds and punish the wicked as they deserve.
