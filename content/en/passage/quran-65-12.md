## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Allah is He Who created seven heavens and of the earth, their like. The command descends between them so that you may know that Allah is All-Powerful over everything and that Allah has encompassed all things in knowledge,” explains: “Allah is He Who created seven heavens,” not the idols and false gods worshipped by the polytheists, which are incapable of creating anything. And His statement, “and of the earth, their like,” means: He created of the earth their like because each of them contains a similar creation to that found in the heavens. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Saeed bin Jubayr, Ibrahim, and Al-Rabi’.
