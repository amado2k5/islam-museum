## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "They will say, 'Three, a fourth of them are their dogs,' and they will say, 'Five, the sixth of them are their dogs, stoning by the unseen.' They will say, 'Seven, and the eighth of them are their dogs.' Say, 'My Lord knows best their number; He knows only a few, so do not pass through them except in apparent appearance, and do not ask anyone about them for question.': Some of those involved in the matter of the young from the Cave will say: There are three, a fourth of whom is their dog, and some say: They are five, the sixth of whom are their dogs (stoning unseen): He says: 'Throwing suspicion without certainty of knowledge,' as the poet said: 'And make the truth from me a hidden and stoned concealment.' He quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, Samak, and others.
