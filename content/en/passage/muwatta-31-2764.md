## Summary
Yahya ibn Said narrated to Malik that a man greeted Abdullah ibn Umar: peace be upon you, and God's mercy and blessings, and the morning and evening visitors. Abdullah ibn Umar said: and upon you a thousandfold. Then he seemed to dislike it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.552}
