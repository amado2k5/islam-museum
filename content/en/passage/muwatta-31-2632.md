## Summary
Yahya ibn Said reported that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab say: shall I tell you of something better than much prayer and charity? They said: yes. He said: making peace between people. Beware of hatred, for it is the shaver. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.489}
