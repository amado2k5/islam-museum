## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, in the chapter on permission to pray in a single garment: Abu Hurayra was asked whether a man may pray in one garment, and said yes. He was asked whether he did so himself, and said he prayed in a single garment while his clothes hung on the rack, a stick on which garments are hung. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.203}
