## Summary
Malik transmits from Fudayl ibn Abi Abdallah, a client of al-Mahri, that al-Qasim ibn Muhammad and Salim ibn Abdallah used to say that when a woman is divorced and enters blood in the third menstrual period, she has separated from him and is free. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.91}
