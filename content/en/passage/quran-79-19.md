## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And I will guide you to your Lord so that you may fear Him”: Say to Pharaoh: Would you like me to guide you to what pleases your Lord, which is the upright religion? “So that you may fear Him” means: So that you may fear His punishment by performing what He has obligated you to do and avoiding what He has forbidden you to do. He then quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd in this section.
