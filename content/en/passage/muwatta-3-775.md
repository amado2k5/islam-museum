## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to give nothing for fast-breaking charity but dates, except once when he gave barley. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shafii in his Musnad transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.383}
