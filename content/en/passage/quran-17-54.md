## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Your Lord is most knowing of you; if He wills, He will have mercy on you, or if He wills, He will punish you. And We have not sent you as a guardian." He says to those polytheists from Quraysh who said, "Then We were bones and remnants. Indeed, We will be sent as a new creation." "Your Lord," O people, "He knows best about you; if He wills, He will have mercy on you," so He will repent to you by His mercy, so that you may repent from what you are upon of disbelief in Him and the Last Day. "And if He wills, He will punish you" by dissuading you from faith, so you die in your polytheism, and on the Day of Resurrection, He will punish you by your disbelief in Him.
