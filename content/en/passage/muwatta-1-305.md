## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr, who heard his father Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm say, in the chapter on night prayer in Ramadan: because their standing was so long, people would hurry the servants with food on leaving the night prayer for fear of dawn. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it, and al-Baghawi cited it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.172}
