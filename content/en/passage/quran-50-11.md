## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 13 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We sent down blessed rain from the sky, and We caused with it to grow gardens and grain of harvest," meaning: "And We sent down water from the sky" as blessed rain, causing orchards of trees to grow, grain of harvested crops from barley, barley, and all kinds of grains. Also: According to Qatadah, "and the grain of harvest" means this barley and barley. From Mu'ammar, from Qatadah: 'and the grain of the harvest' he said: it is wheat and barley. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid: 'and the love of the harvest' he said: wheat. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Samak.
