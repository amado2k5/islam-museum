## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 107–111. Al-Tabari interprets "and We ransomed him with a great sacrifice" by saying that God made the place of his slaughter the slaughter of a great ram and saved him from slaughtering. He also addresses the differences among interpreters regarding the deceased among the sons of Abraham: some said it was Isaac and narrated their narrations, mentioning other opinions in their proper place, and that God left Abraham with a mention of the others. Peace be upon Abraham.
