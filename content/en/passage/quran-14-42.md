## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses the verse to the Prophet: God is not unaware of what his wrongdoing people do, and He only delays them until a day when eyes stare fixed. He reports Maymun ibn Mihran's view that the verse is a warning to the wrongdoer and a consolation to the wronged.
