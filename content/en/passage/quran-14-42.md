## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 42 and 43 together: "And do not think that Allah is unaware of what the wrongdoers do." He forbade the Prophet from thinking that God is heedless of what these polytheists among His people do; rather, He is aware of them, indicating their deeds upon them so that He may recompense them at the time previously known to Him. He narrates from Maymun ibn Mihran that it is a warning to the oppressor and a consolation for the oppressed. And "He only delays them for a Day when eyes will be stalled": He delays their punishment and the punishment sent upon them until the Day of Resurrection, when the eyes of creation will be fixed and will not turn back to them, as narrated from Qatadah. In 'Muhta'in' there are two opinions: the first is hastening—according to Sa'id ibn Jubayr that it is the offspring, which is the 'khabab' or something lower, and from Qatadah, they are hasty, deliberately setting out to the caller—and the second is perpetual gazing without turning away, which is constant stamiz, according to Ibn Abbas, Abu al-Duha, and al-Dahhak.
