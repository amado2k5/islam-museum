## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 97 to 98 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed, We know that your chest is tightened by what they say": And indeed, we know, O Muhammad, that your heart is tightened by what these polytheists among your people say about their denial of you and their mockery of you and what you have brought to them, and that that embarrasses you. "So glorify the praise of your Lord," meaning: Be distressed in what befalls you of any matter you dislike from them, to thanking Allah, glorifying Him, and praying. Allah will suffice you from that as long as you are concerned. This is similar to the report narrated from the Messenger of Allah: "That when he was in a state of disbelief, he would rush to prayer."
