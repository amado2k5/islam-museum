## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the saying, "O you who have believed, do not be like those who harmed Moses, and Allah acquitted him of what they said, and he was in high esteem with Allah." He says to the companions of the Prophet of Allah: "O you who have believed in Allah and His Messenger, do not harm the Messenger of Allah with words he dislikes from you, nor with an action he dislikes from you, nor be like those who harmed Moses, the Prophet of Allah, and threw him with a false and false fault." "So Allah acquitted him of what they said," which contains lies and falsehood by clearly proving their falsehood. "And he was respected with Allah," meaning: Moses was an intercessor in the sight of God regarding what he asked, holding a standing and status with Him by obeying Him. Then the interpreters differed regarding the harm caused to Moses, whom God mentioned in this place, saying: He was accused of being worse. A report was narrated from the Messenger of God regarding this. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ikrimah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
