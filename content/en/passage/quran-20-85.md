## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 86 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "He said, 'Indeed, We tested your people after you and led them astray by the Samaritan.'" He says: God Almighty says to Moses: "Indeed, O Moses, We have tested your people after you by worshiping the calf, and that was their trial after Moses." And by "after you," he means: after you separated them. God Almighty says: "And the Samiri led them astray," and the Samaritan led them astray as his prayer to them to worship the calf. And His saying: "So Moses returned to his people" means: Moses turned to his people from the Children of Israel after the forty nights had passed, "angry and sorrowful," resentful toward his people, saddened by what they had done after him of disbelief in God. Also: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas regarding the phrase: "angry and sorrowful," meaning sad. And he said in Al-Zukhruf: "So when they regretted us," he says: they angered us, and the word 'sad' comes in two ways: anger and sadness. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
