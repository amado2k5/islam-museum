**Commentary on verse 8 (pp. 252-255)**

On [[passage/quran-11-8|11:8]] al-Tabari says: if We delay the punishment from these polytheists of your people, Muhammad, and do not hasten it for them but defer their terms 'for a counted nation', that is, a fixed time and known years. The root sense of 'umma' is a group of people agreed on one way and religion, which is then used in many senses that go back to that root; the counted years and the time are called an 'umma' because a community lives in them, and the meaning is: if We delay the punishment until another community comes and another before it passes away. Reports 17991, 17992 and 17993, from Ibn Abbas: to a fixed term; report 17994, from Qatada: a counted term; report 17995, from al-Dahhak: a counted term; reports 17996 to 17999, from Mujahid: for a time; report 18000, from Ibn Abbas: a known term. The editor notes he skipped number 18001 in the numbering by oversight. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 253}

His words 'they will surely say, what holds it back?' mean these polytheists will say what restrains it, what prevents the hastening of the punishment he threatens us with, denying it and thinking it was delayed only because the threatener lied; report 18002, from Ibn Jurayj: out of denial of it or saying it is nothing. His words 'surely, on the day it comes to them it will not be turned from them' confirm His threat: the punishment they deny, when it comes, no one will turn from them nor repel, but it will come down on them and destroy them; 'and what they used to mock at will encircle them' means the punishment of God they used to jeer at came down on them. Their mockery was their saying before it came, what holds it back, and why does He not bring it to us. Report 18003, from Mujahid: what their prophets brought of the truth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 255}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
