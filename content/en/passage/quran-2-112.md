## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "yes, whoever submits his face to God while doing good" he says it is not as the claimants said, but whoever submits his face to God while doing good is the one who enters and enjoys Paradise, from al-Suddi. "Islam of the face" is humbling oneself to obey Him and submitting to His command; the root of "islam" is "istislam", and the Muslim is called so for the yielding of his limbs to the obedience of his Lord, from al-Rabi' (he was sincere to God), and he cites a verse of Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl. The face is singled out because it is the noblest of a person's members and the most sacred to him, so if he yields it, the rest are more fit to yield; and the Arabs ascribe a thing to "its face" meaning the thing itself, as in a verse of al-A'sha and one of Dhu al-Rumma. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 510} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 511}
