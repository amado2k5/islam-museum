## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 117–122. Al-Tabari explains that God gave Moses and Aaron the clear Book, which is the Torah, guided them to the straight path, and left them with the others. Peace be upon Moses and Aaron. Indeed, We reward the doers of good; indeed, they are among Our believing servants.
