## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So gather your plots and then come in rows, and the day has succeeded from the exalted": Reciters differed in reading His saying: "So gather your schemes." Most reciters of Medina and Kufa read it as "so gather your plot" with the hamza of the alif of "so gather them." They interpreted this as: "So tighten your plot and resolve upon it," as in their saying: "So-and-so agreed to go out," as it is said: "I intend against him," and from the poet's saying: "If only my poetry and wishes would not be of any use, would I one day become a united matter," meaning in his saying: "Majma'" has been judged and resolved to follow, including the Prophet's saying: "Whoever does not unite to fast at night, there is no fast for him." Some reciters from Basra read this as: "So gather your schemes" with the connecting of the alif and omitting its hamza, meaning to gather something, as if it was directed to the meaning: "So do not invoke anything of your plot except that you bring it." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
