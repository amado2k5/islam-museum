## Summary
A report of Jabir ibn Abdullah in the chapter on the sanctity of a believer's blood, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Asked about the two things that bring about destiny, the Prophet answers that monotheism brings Paradise and polytheism brings the Fire. {cite:sahih-muslim}
