## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "There is no deity except Him; He gives life and causes death, your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers": There is no deity for you except the Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, so worship is not suitable for anyone else, nor should worship be for anything other than Him. He gives life and causes death. He says: He gives life as He wills and causes death as He wills from what He was alive. And His saying: "Your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers" means: He is your owner and the owner of those who came before you from your early fathers.
