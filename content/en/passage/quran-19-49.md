## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "So when I withdrew from them and what they worship besides Allah, We gave him Isaac and Jacob, and each of them We made a prophet." So when Abraham withdrew from his people and the idols they worshipped besides Allah, we became aware of their longing and replaced him with one better and more honored to Allah than them. We gave him his son Isaac and his great-grandson Jacob son of Isaac. "And each of them We made a prophet," meaning: And We made them all, meaning by all Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob prophets. And Allah the Exalted said: "And each of them We made a prophet," so He unified, not called prophets, to unify the wording of each "And We granted them from Our mercy." He says: "And We provided them all with Our mercy," meaning Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob from Our mercy. The One who granted them of His mercy was what He expanded for them in the immediate days of His provision and enriched them with His bounty. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
