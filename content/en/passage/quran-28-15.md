## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Ali entered the city while unawaited from its people and found two men fighting there, one from his followers and the other from his enemy. The one from his followers called for help against the enemy of his enemy, so Moses struck him and killed him. He said, 'This is the work of Satan; indeed, he is a clear misguided enemy.'" Moses entered the city of Memphis in Egypt "while its people were unaware," and that was at midday. Scholars differ on the reason why Moses entered this city at that time. Some said: He entered it following in Pharaoh's footsteps, because Pharaoh rode while Moses was unseen. When he arrived, he learned of his ride, rode and followed his footsteps, and Al-Maqil caught up with him in this city. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd and Ibn Ishaq.
