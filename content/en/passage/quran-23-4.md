## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And those who are the doers of zakat": those who are responsible for the zakat of their wealth, which Allah has made obligatory upon them, and their deeds are described as their own payments. And His saying: "And the debtors are for their private parts, guarding except for their wives," means: those who are for their private parts. And by 'charaw' in this context, he means the private parts of men, which is their inclination. 'They guard' it from their deeds in some parts of the private parts. Except for their husbands} He says: except for their wives whom Allah has permitted for men by marriage. "Or what their right hands possess" means their slave women. And "Ma" in the phrase: "or what their right hands possess" is in the case of a deductive case to refer to their husbands. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
