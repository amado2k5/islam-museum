## Summary
It reached Malik that a slave-girl belonging to Ubaydallah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab was dressed like free women; Umar saw her and went in to his daughter Hafsa and said: did I not see the slave-girl of your brother walking among people, dressed like free women? Umar disapproved of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.577}
