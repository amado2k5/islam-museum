**Commentary on verse 8 (p. 528)**

On "and Moses said: if you disbelieve, you and all who are on the earth, God is Self-sufficient, Praiseworthy", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: Moses said to his people, if you disbelieve, people, denying God's favour that He gave you, you, and all on the earth do the like of what you do in that, God is independent of you and of them, of all His creation, with no need of your thanks to Him for His favours from any of you; "Praiseworthy", possessing praise from His creation for the favours He gave them. Report 20589, from Ali, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Abd Allah ibn Hashim, Sayf, Abu Ruq and Abu Ayyub: "God is Self-sufficient", he said, independent of His creation, "Praiseworthy", he said, seeking praise from them; the editor notes that "istahmada" is not recorded in the great dictionaries though al-Zamakhshari records it in the Asas. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 528}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
