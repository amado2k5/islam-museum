## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains 'what precedes a nation's term, and what they delay': the destruction of a nation does not come before the appointed time that God has set as a time for its destruction, nor does it delay it. He narrates from Al-Zuhri that when the term of a nation comes, it is neither delayed nor advanced, but what does not come to its appointed time, God delays what He wills and advances whatever He wills.
