## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We certainly gave Moses the Book after We destroyed the earlier generations as a clear insight for mankind, and guidance and mercy that they may remember." He says: "And We gave Moses the Torah after We destroyed the nations before it, like the people of Noah, 'Aad, Thamud, the people of Lot, and the people of Midian." "Insight for the people," meaning: a light for the Children of Israel regarding their need regarding their religion. "And guidance," meaning clarification for them and mercy for those among them who acted upon it, "that they might remember." He says: So that they may remember God's blessings upon them by this, thank Him for them, and not deny them.
