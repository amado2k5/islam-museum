## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, if I go astray, I only stray against myself. And if I am guided, then by what my Lord reveals to me. Indeed, He is Hearing, Near." Say, O Muhammad, to your people: If I stray from guidance and follow the path other than the truth, then my misguidance is only upon myself. He says: If I stray from guidance harms myself, "and if I am guided," he means: If I remain steadfast on the truth "in what my Lord reveals to me," he says: By the revelation of God who reveals to me, and His guidance to steadfastness on the path of truth and guidance. And His saying: "Indeed, He is All-Hearing, Near," it means: My Lord is All-Hearing of what I say to you, a guardian of it, and He is my interpreter for my truthfulness in that, and that is not far from me, so it is impossible for Him to hear what I say to you, what you say, or what others say, but he is close to every speaker who hears everything he says, closer to him than a jugular vein.
