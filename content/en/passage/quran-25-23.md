## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 24 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We brought forth what they had done of deeds and made them scattered waste": "And We advanced" and deliberately followed what these criminals did, "of deeds," as in the verse of Al-Rajiz: "And the Kharijites brought misguidance to the servants of their Lord and said, 'Your blood is lawful for us,' meaning by saying: 'He deliberately.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Hasan, Samak, and Ata.
