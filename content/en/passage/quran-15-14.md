## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari presents the difference among interpreters regarding the pronoun regarding 'they continued to ascend therein.' Some believe, and narrate from Ibn Abbas, that the angels ascend through the open gate of the sky and see it with their own eyes, while others believe that the polytheists themselves ascend through it.
