## Summary
A report of Ali in the chapter on the virtue of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in Sahih Muslim: the Prophet never gathered his two parents as a ransom for anyone except for Sa'd on the day of Uhud. It is the Prophet's text honouring Sa'd, the archer of Badr. {cite:sahih-muslim}
