## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, let not a people ridicule a people; perhaps they may be better than them, nor any women among women; perhaps they may be better than them. And do not insult one another and do not call each other by nicknames. Wretched is the name of wickedness after faith. And whoever does not repent - then it is those who are the wrongdoers." The one who is mocked among them is better than the mockers. "Nor are women of women." He says: And believing women are not mocked by believing women, perhaps the mocked among them is better than the mockers. The interpreters differed regarding the mockery that Allah forbade the believers in this verse. Some said: it is the mockery of the rich for the poor, forbidding mocking the poor because of their poverty. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
