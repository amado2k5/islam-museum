## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra: the Messenger of God said that none of you should propose on the proposal of his brother. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and others transmitted it, with sound connections in the Sahihayn. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.27}
