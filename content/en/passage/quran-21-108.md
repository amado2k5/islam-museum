## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, it is only revealed to me that your God is one God, so will you then be Muslims": Say, O Muhammad, my Lord is revealed to me only that there is no deity for you. It is permissible to worship only one God, and worship is not suitable except for Him, nor should it be for anyone else. "Are you Muslims?" He means: Are you submissive to Him, O polytheists who worship idols and idols, by submitting to that, and disassociating yourselves from worshipping those of your gods below Him?
