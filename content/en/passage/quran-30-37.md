## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have they not seen that Allah extends provision for whom He wills and restricts it? Indeed, in that are signs for a people who believe": Or have they not seen those who rejoice in prosperity and fertility, and who despair of relief when hardship befalls them, with the eyes of their hearts, knowing that hardship and prosperity are in the hand of Allah, and that Allah extends provision for whom He wills among His servants, expands it for them, and restricts it for whomever He wills but restricts it for them: "Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe," meaning: His extension of this to those He has granted it to him, and His decree over those He has power over Him, and its opposition between those among His servants who differ from Him regarding wealth and poverty, is a clear indication of those who believe in God's proofs and acknowledge them when they are observed and seen.
