## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase: "Or do they say, 'I fabricated it?' Say, 'Indeed, I have fabricated it, so you have no authority over me from Allah. He is most knowing of what you overflow into. Sufficient is a witness between me and you, and He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.'" Or do they say, 'These polytheists of Allah from Quraysh fabricated this Qur'an, so he fabricated it and fabricated it as a lie? Say to them, O Muhammad, if you fabricate it and fabricate it against Allah as a lie, then you have no authority.' He means: Do not avail me from Allah if you punish me for my fabrication against him, and you conceal anything against him, and you cannot repel any harm from me. He hit me with it. And His saying: "He knows best what you overflow in," means: My Lord knows better than anything else what you say among you in this Qur'an, and the 'ha' is more than His saying: 'You increase in it' from the mention of the Qur'an. In the section, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
