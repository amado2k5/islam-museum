## Summary
Verse 81 of [[passage/quran-11|Surat Hud]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 81 of 123 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 81 (pp. 423-432)**

On [[passage/quran-11-81|11:81]] al-Tabari says: the angels said to Lot, when he said what he said to his people and they saw the distress he suffered on their account, 'Lot, we are your Lord's messengers', sent to destroy them; they will not reach you or your guest with harm, so ease your heart; 'travel with your family in a part of the night', that is, leave from among them with your family in what remains of the night. One says 'asra' and 'sara' of travelling by night. The readers differ on 'fa-asri': most readers of Mecca and Medina join the alif, from 'sara', and most of Kufa and Basra pronounce it, from 'asra'; al-Tabari holds they are two readings, each read by leaders in reading, two well-known dialects of one meaning, so whichever the reader takes is right. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 423}

As for 'except your wife', most readers of the Hijaz, Kufa and some of Basra read it in the accusative, meaning take your family except your wife, Lot being told to journey with his family apart from his wife and to leave her with her people; some Basrans read it in the nominative, meaning let none of you turn around except your wife, so Lot had brought her with him and was told that no one should turn but she, and she turned and perished. 'What befell them will befall her' means what befell your people of punishment will befall your wife; 'their appointment is the morning' means the destruction of your people is at dawn. Lot found that slow and said, hasten it for them, and they said 'is not the morning near?', that is, the punishment comes on them at morning; report 18407, from Ibn Ishaq: it comes on them with the dawn of this very night, so go on as you are told. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 424}

Report 18408, from Sa'id: the messengers went from Abraham to Lot, and when they came, and what God has related of their affair took place, Gabriel said, Lot, we are to destroy the people of this town; Lot said, destroy them now; Gabriel said, their appointment is the morning, is the morning not near? He told Lot to travel with his family in part of the night, none turning but his wife; he went, and when the hour of their destruction came Gabriel put his wing under the town and raised it until the dwellers of heaven heard the crowing of cocks and barking of dogs, then turned it upside down and rained on it stones of baked clay; Lot's wife heard the crash and cried, oh my people, and a stone overtook and killed her. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 425}

Report 18409, from Shamir b. 'Atiyya: Lot had taken a promise from his wife not to divulge any of his guests' secret; when Gabriel and those with him came in she saw them in a form such as she had never seen, and went hurrying to her people, came to the assembly and gestured with her hand; they came rushing, and when they reached Lot he said to them what God relates in His Book; Gabriel said 'Lot, we are your Lord's messengers, they will not reach you', and signalled with his hand and blotted out their eyes, and they felt along the walls searching for them, unseeing. Report 18410, from Hudhayfa: when the old woman of evil, his wife, saw them she went and warned them; they came rushing, Lot shut the door, they struggled with it; Gabriel asked his Lord's leave to punish them and He gave it, and he struck them with his wing, leaving them blind and wandering through the worst night they had known; they told him 'we are your Lord's messengers, travel with your family'. It was told us that Lot's wife was with him when he left the town, then heard the sound and turned, and God sent a stone on her. The editor restores a wording from the manuscript and the History. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 426}

Report 18411, from Qatada: his wife, when she saw the messengers, went to her people and said, tonight guests have come to him whose like I have never seen, of fairer faces and sweeter scent; they came rushing, Lot hurried to jam the door against them and said 'these are my daughters if you will act', and they said 'did we not forbid you the worlds', which is a borrowing from [[passage/quran-15-70|15:70]] and [[passage/quran-15-71|15:71]]; they went in to the angels, who took hold of them and blotted out their eyes; they said, Lot, you brought us sorcerers who bewitched us, wait until morning. Gabriel lifted Lot's four towns, a hundred thousand in each, on his wing between heaven and earth until the lowest heaven heard their cocks, then overturned them. Report 18412, from Qatada from Hudhayfa: when they entered, his old woman of evil went to her people; they came hurrying; an angel shut the door fast; Gabriel asked leave to punish them and struck them with his wing, leaving them blind, and they passed the worst of nights; then the messengers said 'travel with your family'; we heard that she heard a sound, turned, and a stone struck her, and she lay apart from the people, her place known. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 427}

Report 18413, from Hudhayfa, similarly. Report 18414, from al-Suddi: when Lot said 'if only I had strength', Gabriel spread his wings and put out their eyes, and they went out trampling on one another blind, saying, flee, flee, for in Lot's house are the greatest sorcerers on earth; that is the saying of [[passage/quran-54-37|54:37]]. They said to Lot 'we are your Lord's messengers, they will not reach you; travel with your family in a part of the night', 'and follow behind them', that is, go with them, 'and go where you are commanded', and God brought them out to Syria. Lot said, destroy them now, and they said we were commanded only for the morning, is the morning not near? At the last of the night Lot went out with his family, except his wife, and that is the saying of [[passage/quran-54-34|54:34]], 'except Lot's household, whom We saved at dawn'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 428}

Report 18415, from Wahb b. Munabbih: the people of Sodom, among whom was Lot, had done without women for men; when God saw this of them He sent the angels to punish them, and they came to Abraham; when they gave Sarah the good news of a son they rose, and Abraham rose and walked with them and asked, why were you sent, what is your errand? They said, we are sent to the people of Sodom to destroy it, a wicked people; he said, what if fifty righteous men are in it? They said, then we shall not punish them; he kept lowering until he said, one household; they said, if one righteous household is in it; he said, what of Lot and his household? They said, his wife's heart is with them; and when Abraham despaired he turned back. They went to the people of Sodom and came to Lot, and when his wife saw them their beauty pleased her and she sent word to the people of the town that guests had come such as no one had seen; they spread the news, surrounded Lot's house on every side and climbed the walls, and Lot met them and said, my people, do not disgrace me in my guest, I shall marry my daughters to you, they are purer for you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 428}

They said, if we wanted your daughters we know where they are; he said 'if only I had strength against you or could take refuge in a strong support', and the messengers were displeased with him and said, your support is strong indeed, and a punishment which cannot be turned back will come to them; one of them wiped over their eyes with his wings and blotted out their sight, and they said, we are bewitched, take us back until we return to him; and what followed was as God has related in the Qur'an. Then Michael, who has charge of punishment, put his wing down to the lowest earth and overturned it, and stones fell from heaven that followed those of them who were outside the town wherever they were; God destroyed them and saved Lot and his family but his wife. Report 18416, from Hudhayfa, with the accounts of several merged: Abraham used to come to them and say, woe to you, I forbid you from exposing yourselves to God's punishment, and they did not obey, until the term was reached and their punishment fell due. The angels came to Lot as he worked on land of his and he invited them to be guests; God had bound Gabriel not to punish them until Lot bore witness against them three times; as Lot took them to his house he recalled the evil his people did, walked with them a while, turned and said, do you not know what the people of this town do? I know none worse on the earth, where am I taking you, to my people, the worst God created? Gabriel said to the angels, take note, that is one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 429}

He walked on a while, and in the middle of the town, pitying them and ashamed before them, said the same, and Gabriel said, note, that is two; at the door of his house he wept from shame and pity and said it again, and Gabriel said, note, that is three, the punishment is due. When they entered, his old woman of evil went up, waved her garment, and the sinners came running; she said guests had come to Lot, none of fairer faces or sweeter scent; they hurried to the door, Lot met them there and they pushed against him a long while, he inside and they outside, as he pleaded with them in God's name and said 'these are my daughters, purer for you'; an angel then stood and shut the door fast, and Gabriel asked leave to punish them and God gave it. He stood in the form he has in heaven and spread his wing, wearing a sash of strung pearls, with shining teeth and a radiant brow; he said 'Lot, we are your Lord's messengers, they will not reach you', move from the door and leave me and them; Lot stepped aside, Gabriel went out to them, spread his wing and struck their faces with a blow that crushed their eyes. The editor comments on the pointing of some words in the print and the manuscript. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 430}

So they were blind, knowing neither the road nor their houses; then Lot was commanded to take his family out that night, 'travel with your family in a part of the night'. Report 18417, from Ibn Ishaq: when Lot said to his people what he said, with the messengers hearing him and seeing his distress, they said 'Lot, we are your Lord's messengers, they will not reach you', that is, with anything you dislike; 'travel with your family…', that is, the punishment falls on them only at the dawn of this night, so go on as you are told. Report 18418, from Muhammad b. Ka'b al-Qurazi: at that the messengers struck the faces of the men of his people who had come asking for his guest, and they went back blind, which is [[passage/quran-54-37|54:37]]. Reports 18419 from Ibn Abbas, 18420 from Qatada and 18421 from Ibn Abbas: 'a part of the night' is a portion of the night, Ibn Abbas adding the depth of the night; 'follow behind them' means behind your family, 'let none of you turn', as in [[passage/quran-15-65|15:65]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 431}

Mujahid says in report 18422: let none look behind him, 'except your wife'. It is related of Abdallah b. Mas'ud that he read 'travel with your family in a part of the night, except your wife' in the accusative, and report 18423, from Harun, gives this in Ibn Mas'ud's codex; al-Tabari says this shows the soundness of the accusative reading. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 432}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
