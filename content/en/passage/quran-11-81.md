**Commentary on verse 81 (pp. 423-428)**

On [[passage/quran-11-81|11:81]] al-Tabari says: the angels said to Lot, when he said what he said to his people and they saw the distress he suffered on their account, 'Lot, we are your Lord's messengers', sent to destroy them; they will not reach you or your guest with harm, so ease your heart; 'travel with your family in a part of the night', that is, leave from among them with your family in what remains of the night. One says 'asra' and 'sara' of travelling by night. The readers differ on 'fa-asri': most readers of Mecca and Medina join the alif, from 'sara', and most of Kufa and Basra pronounce it, from 'asra'; al-Tabari holds they are two readings, each read by leaders in reading, two well-known dialects of one meaning, so whichever the reader takes is right. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 423}

As for 'except your wife', most readers of the Hijaz, Kufa and some of Basra read it in the accusative, meaning take your family except your wife, Lot being told to journey with his family apart from his wife and to leave her with her people; some Basrans read it in the nominative, meaning let none of you turn around except your wife, so Lot had brought her with him and was told that no one should turn but she, and she turned and perished. 'What befell them will befall her' means what befell your people of punishment will befall your wife; 'their appointment is the morning' means the destruction of your people is at dawn. Lot found that slow and said, hasten it for them, and they said 'is not the morning near?', that is, the punishment comes on them at morning; report 18407, from Ibn Ishaq: it comes on them with the dawn of this very night, so go on as you are told. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 424}

Report 18408, from Sa'id: the messengers went from Abraham to Lot, and when they came, and what God has related of their affair took place, Gabriel said, Lot, we are to destroy the people of this town; Lot said, destroy them now; Gabriel said, their appointment is the morning, is the morning not near? He told Lot to travel with his family in part of the night, none turning but his wife; he went, and when the hour of their destruction came Gabriel put his wing under the town and raised it until the dwellers of heaven heard the crowing of cocks and barking of dogs, then turned it upside down and rained on it stones of baked clay; Lot's wife heard the crash and cried, oh my people, and a stone overtook and killed her. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 425}

Report 18409, from Shamir b. 'Atiyya: Lot had taken a promise from his wife not to divulge any of his guests' secret; when Gabriel and those with him came in she saw them in a form such as she had never seen, and went hurrying to her people, came to the assembly and gestured with her hand; they came rushing, and when they reached Lot he said to them what God relates in His Book; Gabriel said 'Lot, we are your Lord's messengers, they will not reach you', and signalled with his hand and blotted out their eyes, and they felt along the walls searching for them, unseeing. Report 18410, from Hudhayfa: when the old woman of evil, his wife, saw them she went and warned them; they came rushing, Lot shut the door, they struggled with it; Gabriel asked his Lord's leave to punish them and He gave it, and he struck them with his wing, leaving them blind and wandering through the worst night they had known; they told him 'we are your Lord's messengers, travel with your family'. It was told us that Lot's wife was with him when he left the town, then heard the sound and turned, and God sent a stone on her. The editor restores a wording from the manuscript and the History. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 426}

Report 18411, from Qatada: his wife, when she saw the messengers, went to her people and said, tonight guests have come to him whose like I have never seen, of fairer faces and sweeter scent; they came rushing, Lot hurried to jam the door against them and said 'these are my daughters if you will act', and they said 'did we not forbid you the worlds', which is a borrowing from [[passage/quran-15-70|15:70]] and [[passage/quran-15-71|15:71]]; they went in to the angels, who took hold of them and blotted out their eyes; they said, Lot, you brought us sorcerers who bewitched us, wait until morning. Gabriel lifted Lot's four towns, a hundred thousand in each, on his wing between heaven and earth until the lowest heaven heard their cocks, then overturned them. Report 18412, from Qatada from Hudhayfa: when they entered, his old woman of evil went to her people; they came hurrying; an angel shut the door fast; Gabriel asked leave to punish them and struck them with his wing, leaving them blind, and they passed the worst of nights; then the messengers said 'travel with your family'; we heard that she heard a sound, turned, and a stone struck her, and she lay apart from the people, her place known. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 427}

Report 18413, from Hudhayfa, similarly. Report 18414, from al-Suddi: when Lot said 'if only I had strength', Gabriel spread his wings and put out their eyes, and they went out trampling on one another blind, saying, flee, flee, for in Lot's house are the greatest sorcerers on earth; that is the saying of [[passage/quran-54-37|54:37]]. They said to Lot 'we are your Lord's messengers, they will not reach you; travel with your family in a part of the night', 'and follow behind them', that is, go with them, 'and go where you are commanded', and God brought them out to Syria. Lot said, destroy them now, and they said we were commanded only for the morning, is the morning not near? At the last of the night Lot went out with his family, except his wife, and that is the saying of [[passage/quran-54-34|54:34]], 'except Lot's household, whom We saved at dawn'. Report 18415, from Wahb b. Munabbih, follows on p. 428. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 428}
