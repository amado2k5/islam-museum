## Summary
A saying of Malik about the rabid dog ordered killed in the sanctuary: anything that bites people, attacks them and frightens them, such as the lion, the tiger, the leopard and the wolf, is a rabid dog. As for beasts that do not attack, such as the hyena, the fox, the cat and the like, one in ihram may not kill them, and if he kills one he ransoms it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1187), Suwayd (628) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.480}
