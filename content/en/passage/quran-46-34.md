## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And on the Day those who disbelieved will be presented before the Fire, will they not say, 'Yes, by our Lord?' He will say, 'So taste the punishment for what you used to disbelieve.'" And on the Day these deniers will be presented with the resurrection, and the reward of Allah will be presented to His servants for their righteous deeds, and His punishment will be upon them for their evil deeds, to the Fire, the fire of Hell. It will be said to them: Is not this the punishment you are suffering today, while you used to deny it in this world, as a rebuke from Allah for their denial of it? In this world, 'They will say, Yes, by our Lord,' meaning: These disbelievers immediately respond by saying, 'Yes, it is the truth.' Allah said: 'So taste the punishment for what you used to disbelieve.' The decreed said to them: 'So taste the punishment of the Fire now for what you used to deny in this world, deny it, and refuse to acknowledge it if you are called to believe in it.'
