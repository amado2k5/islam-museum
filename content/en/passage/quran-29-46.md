## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not argue, O People of the Scripture, except with what is better, except for those among whom who have wronged. And say, 'We believe in what has been revealed to us and revealed to you, and our God and your God is One, and to Him we submit.'" "And do not argue," O believers in God and His Messenger, the Jews and Christians, who are "People of the Book, except in what is best." He means: except with beautiful speech, which is supplicating to God with His signs and pointing out His proofs. And His saying: 'Except those who were wronged among them' has differed among interpreters, and some said: Its meaning is: except those who refuse to acknowledge paying the jizya to you and have waged war against you for that, for they are oppressors. Those argued with them with the sword until they surrendered or paid the jizya. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
