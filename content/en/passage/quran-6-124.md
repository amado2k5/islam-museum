## Summary
Verse 124 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 124 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 124, "when a sign comes to them they say we will not believe until we are given the like of what God's messengers were given; God knows best where to place His message", al-Tabari says: when a sign, meaning a proof from God for the truth of what Muhammad brought them, comes to these idolaters who dispute with the believers with adorned speech over what God forbade them, they say they will not believe what Muhammad called them to until God gives them miracles like those given to Moses, the splitting of the sea, and to Jesus, bringing the dead to life and healing the born-blind and the leper. Then "God knows best where to place His message" means that the signs of prophets and messengers are not given to any human except a sent messenger, and those who set idols and statues equal to their Lord are not among them; He knows best the places of His messages and who is fit for them, and the idolaters have no right to choose, since the choice of the messenger belongs to the one who sends, not the one sent to. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 96}

Then "those who did wrong will be struck by humiliation before God and a severe punishment for their plotting". God tells His Prophet what He will do with these rebels: those who earned sin by joining others with God and worshipping other than Him will be struck, Muhammad, by "saghar", meaning abasement and disgrace; he gives (13851) al-Suddi: "saghar" is abasement. It is a verbal noun from "saghira, yasghar, saghar and saghar", the severest abasement. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "isaba". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 96}

As for "saghar 'inda Allah", it means abasement will strike them from God, as one says "provision will come to me from God", meaning what is mine with God will come to me; so what is with God of abasement will strike them for denying His messenger. This is not like "I came 'inda 'Abd Allah" meaning from him; the editor refers to al-Farra'. And "a severe punishment for their plotting" means that along with abasement a severe punishment will strike these deniers of God and His Messenger, who count lawful what God forbade them of carrion, for the scheming against Islam and its people by false argument and adorned speech to deceive the people of God's religion and obedience. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 97}

At this point on p. 97 a part of the old division from which the manuscript was copied ends; the copyist carried over the closing formula and the opening of the next part with the basmala. Verse 124 thus closes here, and the commentary resumes from p. 98 with verse 125. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 97}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
