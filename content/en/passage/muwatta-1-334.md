## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim that he heard Abdullah ibn Amir ibn Rabi'a say that he prays the witr while he hears the iqama, or after dawn, and Abd al-Rahman is unsure which he said, in the chapter on the witr after dawn. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.185}
