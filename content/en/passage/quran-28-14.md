## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when he reached maturity and was established, We gave him wisdom and knowledge, and thus We reward the doers of good": "And when he reached maturity," Moses "matured," meaning his body and strength were tightened, and that was done with him. We have explained the meaning of "al-ashda" in previous contexts with its evidence, so that suffices without repeating it here. And His saying: "and he was established" means: his youth ended, his creation was complete, and he became firm. There is disagreement about the number of years of elevation, with some saying: That is in forty years. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
