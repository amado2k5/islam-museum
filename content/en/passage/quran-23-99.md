## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 99 to 100 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "until, when death comes to one of them, he says, 'My Lord, return'": Until when one of these polytheists comes to death and witnesses the descent of God's command upon him, he says to the great one who witnesses what comes before of God's punishment, feeling regret for what he has missed and eager for what he neglected before in obedience to God and asking Him for removal: "My Lord, return me to this world, return me to it," "that I may do righteous deeds," meaning: "So that I may do righteous deeds in what I left before today, which I have neglected and neglected." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd and Ibn Jurayj.
