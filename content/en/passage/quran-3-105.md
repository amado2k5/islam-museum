## Summary
Verse 105 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 105 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The warning against division (pp. 92-93).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: do not be, believers, like the People of the Book who differed over God's religion, His command and prohibition, after the proofs of God had come to them on what they differed about; they knew the truth and deliberately opposed it, disobeyed God's command and broke His covenant out of boldness toward Him, and for them there is a great punishment from God. So do not divide in your religion as they did, nor follow their way, lest what struck them strike you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 92}

**Al-Rabi, Ibn Abbas and al-Hasan (7598-7600, pp. 92-93).** From al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Ibn Abi Ja'far, his father, al-Rabi (7598): those meant are the People of the Book, and God forbade the people of Islam to divide and differ as they did. From al-Muthanna, Abdullah ibn Salih, Mu'awiya ibn Salih, Ali ibn Abi Talha, Ibn Abbas (7599): God commanded the believers to keep to the community, forbade them to differ and divide, and told them those before them perished only through disputation and quarrelling in God's religion. From Muhammad ibn Sinan, Abu Bakr al-Hanafi, Abbad, al-Hasan (7600): they are the Jews and the Christians. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 92} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 93}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 93}
