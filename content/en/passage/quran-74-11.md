## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 15 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Then when the Trumpet is blown" as meaning, may His praise be exalted, "Then when the Trumpet is blown," that will be a day of great tribulation. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Al-Rabi', and Atiyyah.
