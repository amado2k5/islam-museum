## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "This is Our Book, which speaks to you in truth. Indeed, We used to reproduce what you used to do": For every nation called on the Day of Resurrection to its Book which it dictated to its keepers in this world, "Today you will be recompensed for what you used to do." So do not be afraid of our reward for that, for it will be spoken to you if you deny it in truth, then recite it: "Indeed, We used to reproduce what you used to do," meaning: We used to record your deeds with our memorizers, so you would record them in the books and write them down. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ata.
