## Summary
Verse 47 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 47 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "say: do you see, if God's punishment comes to you suddenly or openly, will any be destroyed but the wrongdoing people?" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, to these who hold idols equal to their Lord and deny that you are my messenger to them: tell me, if God's punishment and penalty come to you for what you join with him of idols and rivals and for your denial of me after the proof you have seen of the truth of my word, "suddenly", that is, all at once, unawares, "or openly", that is, God's punishment comes to you while you see it and look at it, "will any be destroyed but the wrongdoing people?", that is, will God destroy any of us or you but whoever worships other than him who deserves our worship and abandons worship of him who deserves it? He has explained the sense of "jahra" elsewhere, that it comes from "ijhar", showing a thing to the eye. 13249 and 13250, from Mujahid: "suddenly" means all at once, in security, "or openly" while they look. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 368}

The editor refers for "do you see" to pp. 351 to 353, for "suddenly" to pp. 325 and 360, and for "openly" to volumes 9 and 2.

Verse 47 closes here at p. 369, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-48|6:48]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
