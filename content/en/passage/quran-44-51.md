## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 51 to 53 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Indeed, the righteous are in a trustworthy station": Those who fear Allah by performing His obedience and avoiding His sins in a place of residence are safe in that place from what they feared in worldly stations of calamities, illnesses, stone alms, and sorrows. Reciters differed in the recitation of His saying: "in a trustworthy station," but the majority of the reciters of Medina read it as "in the place of trustworthiness" with a damma on the meem, meaning: in the standing of a trustworthy one from the dwelling. The majority of Egyptian reciters read it. Kufa and Basra are 'in a maqam' with a fatha on the 'm' in the meaning we described, indicating that they are in a safe place. The correct view is that they are extensive readings in the recitation of the regions, with correct meanings; whichever one the reader reads, he is correct. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ikrimah.
