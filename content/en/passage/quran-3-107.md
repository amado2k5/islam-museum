## Summary
Verse 107 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 107 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Those with whitened faces (p. 96).** Al-Tabari says the whitened faces are those who held to God's covenant and compact, did not change their religion and did not turn on their heels after affirming oneness and witnessing to their Lord's divinity and that there is no god but Him; they are in God's mercy, that is, in His garden and its bliss and what God prepared there for its people, abiding there forever without end or limit. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 96}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 96}
