## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "This is the creation of Allah, so show me what He created besides Him; rather, the wrongdoers are in clear error." This is what I have prepared for you, O people, that I created in this verse the creation of Allah, who has the divinity of all things, and the worship of every creation, which is not suitable for worship except Him, and nothing else is appropriate for worship. So show me, O polytheists, in your worship of Him besides Him, of gods and idols, what kind of gods and idols He created besides Him, until worship became worthy of you, so you worshipped it instead of Him, just as your Creator and Creator of these things that I have enumerated for you.
