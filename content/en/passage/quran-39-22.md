## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Is he who opens his heart to Islam and is upon a light from his Lord? Woe to those whose hearts harden from the remembrance of Allah. Those are in clear error": Is he whose heart Allah has opened to know Him, acknowledge His oneness, submit to His Lordship, and submit to His obedience? "So he is upon a light from his Lord?" He means: He is with insight into what he is and certain of what he is and certain of the enlightenment of the truth in his heart. Therefore, he follows Allah's command and refrains from what He forbids in what pleases Him, like one whose heart Allah hardens, frees him from His remembrance, and restricts him from hearing the truth, following guidance, and acting Right? He omitted mentioning the one whose heart Allah hardened, and answering the question is a brief way for the listeners to know the intended meaning of the speech, as he mentioned one of the two types, and instead of mentioning the other, he placed the report about him by saying: "Woe to the hardened hearts from the remembrance of Allah." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
