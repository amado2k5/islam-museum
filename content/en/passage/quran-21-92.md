## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, this nation of yours is one nation, and I am your Lord, so worship Me": This is your religion as one religion, and I am your Lord, O people, so worship me separately from gods, idols, and all that you worship besides Me. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
