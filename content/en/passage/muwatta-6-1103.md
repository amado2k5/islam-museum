## Summary
Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet forbade fasting two days: the day of breaking the fast and the day of sacrifice. The editor notes that this hadith was given earlier in the Book of Fasting, number 825. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.505}
