## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 3 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We have given you a clear opening," meaning that by His saying to His Prophet Muhammad, "Indeed, We have given you a clear opening," he means: We have judged for you, O Muhammad, a judgment for whoever heard it or conveyed it to those who opposed you and appointed you among the disbelievers of your people, and We have decreed victory and victory for you, so that you may thank your Lord and praise Him for His favor by decreeing for you over them and granting you the opening of what He has opened for you, and that you may glorify Him and seek His forgiveness. Your Lord will forgive you by your actions. What your past sins before He opens them for you is what He opened, and what is delayed after His conquest is what you thanked and sought forgiveness.
