## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Eat and graze your livestock. Indeed in that are signs for those who forbid." He explains: Eat from the good things We have brought forth for you from the rain which We have sent down from the sky to the earth, of its fruits and food, and whatever is your sustenance and sustenance. And be grazeful of what is the sustenance of your livestock and their sustenance. "Indeed, in that are signs." He means: Indeed, in what is described in this verse of the power of your Lord and the greatness of His authority, there are signs: indications and signs indicating the oneness of your Lord, and that you have no god but Him. "Those of wisdom," meaning the people of pilgrimage and intellect. And 'al-nahy' is the plural of prohibition, just as al-kashshi: the plural of 'kashyyah'. Abu Ja'far said: 'al-kashshi' is a fat that is inside the lizard's stomach, resembling a navel, and Allah specified that this is a sign for those who forbid, because they are people of reflection and reflection, and people of reflection and admonition.
