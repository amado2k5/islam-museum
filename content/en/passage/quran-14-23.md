**Commentary on verses 23-25 (p. 566)**

On "and those who believed and did righteous deeds will be admitted to gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein by their Lord's leave; their greeting therein is peace. Have you not seen how God strikes a parable: a good word like a good tree, its root firm and its branches in the sky, giving its fruit every season by its Lord's leave? God strikes parables for people that they may remember", al-Tabari says, mighty be His mention: those who believed God and His Messenger will be admitted, acknowledging God's oneness and the mission of His messengers and that what they brought from God is true, "and did righteous deeds", he says, acted in obedience to God and stopped at God's command and prohibition, "gardens beneath which rivers flow", orchards beneath which rivers flow, "abiding therein", he says, remaining in them for ever, "by their Lord's leave", he says, they entered by God's command to them to enter, "their greeting therein is peace", and that, God willing, is as Ibn Jurayj relates in report 20657, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn and Hajjaj: "their greeting therein is peace", he said, the angels greet them in Paradise. On "have you not seen how God strikes a parable: a good word like a good tree" He says, mighty be His mention, to His Prophet Muhammad: have you not seen, Muhammad, with the eye of your heart {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 566}

We stop here at the foot of p. 566, in the middle of the commentary on verses 23-25 (at "have you not seen how God strikes a parable"); the next batch begins at p. 567 (marker 575). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 566}
