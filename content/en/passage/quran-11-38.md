**Commentary on verse 38 (pp. 310-317)**

On [[passage/quran-11-38|11:38]] al-Tabari says: Noah builds the ship, and whenever a group of the great men of his people passed him they mocked him, that is, they ridiculed Noah and said, have you turned carpenter after prophethood, and make a ship on dry land? Noah says to them, 'if you mock us' today, 'we shall mock you' in the next life as you mock us in the world; 'you will soon know', when you see God's punishment, which of us was doing wrong to himself. Report 18133, from Aisha, that the Messenger of God said: had God shown mercy to any of Noah's people, He would have shown it to the child's mother. Noah stayed among his people a thousand years less fifty calling them to God until, at the end of his time, he planted a tree which grew great and spread in every direction; then he cut it and began to make a ship, and they would pass and ask him and he would say I am making a ship, and they would mock and say, a ship on dry land, how will it run? He would say, you will soon know. When he finished it and the oven boiled over and the water filled the lanes, the child's mother feared for him, whom she loved intensely, and went out to the mountain until she reached a third of it; when the water reached her she went out until two-thirds; when the water reached her she went until she stood on the mountain; when the water reached her neck she raised him in her hands until the water took her away. Had God shown mercy to any of them, He would have shown it to the child's mother. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 310}

The editor says he finds the chain of this report good, that al-Hakim relates it and calls the chain sound while al-Dhahabi says its chain is obscure and Musa b. Ya'qub is not strong, that Ibn Kathir calls it strange by this route and says the story of this child and his mother is related in similar form from Ka'b al-Ahbar and Mujahid, and that al-Haythami cites it. Report 18134, from Qatada: it was told us that the ship was three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide and thirty cubits high, with its door in its width; report 18135, from al-Hasan: the length of Noah's ship was a thousand and two hundred cubits and its width six hundred. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 311}

Report 18136, from Ibn Abbas: the disciples said to Jesus son of Mary, if you would raise for us a man who saw the ship, to tell us about it. He took them to a mound of dust, took a handful of it and said, do you know what this is? They said God and His Messenger know best. He said, this is the heel of Ham son of Noah. He struck the mound with his staff and said, rise by God's leave, and there he stood shaking the dust from his head, having gone grey. Jesus said to him, did you perish like this? He said no, I died young, but I thought it was the Hour, and so I went grey. He said tell us of Noah's ship. He said: its length was a thousand and two hundred cubits and its width six hundred, and it had three decks, one with the beasts and wild animals, one with people and one with birds. When the beasts' dung grew much God inspired Noah to tap the elephant's tail, and he did, and a pig and a sow fell out and turned to the dung; when the mouse gnawed at the ship's timber, God inspired Noah to strike between the lion's eyes, and from its nostril came a tomcat and a cat, which turned on the mouse. Jesus asked how Noah knew the land had drowned; he said he sent the raven to bring him news, and it found a carcass and settled on it, so he prayed against it with fear, and so it does not keep to houses; then he sent the dove, which came with an olive leaf in its beak and mud on its feet, so he knew the land had drowned, and he ringed it with the green on its neck and prayed that it be in company and safety, and so it keeps to houses. They said, Messenger of God, shall we not take him to our people to sit and talk with us? He said, how would one follow you who has no provision? And he said to him, return by God's leave, and he went back to dust. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 312}

Report 18137, from Ubayd b. Umayr al-Laythi, that it reached him that Noah's people used to seize him and throttle him until he fainted, and when he recovered he would say, O God, forgive my people for they do not know. When they persisted in disobedience and their wrongdoing grew great on earth, his burden grew heavy and he waited generation after generation, each more wicked than the one before, Noah complained of it to God as in [[passage/quran-71-5|71:5]] to [[passage/quran-71-27|71:27]]. God inspired him to make the ark under His eyes and not to address Him concerning the wrongdoers after that day. Noah turned to the work, cutting wood and beating iron and preparing the ship's gear, while his people passed and mocked him, and he said, if you mock us we shall mock you; they said, Noah, you have become a carpenter after prophethood, and God made women's wombs barren so that no child was born. The people of the Torah claim that God ordered him to make the ark of teak, tarred inside and out, eighty cubits long, in three decks, lower, middle and upper, with windows; and Noah did as God ordered. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 314}

God then charged him that when His command came and the oven boiled over, he should carry in it two of every pair, and made the oven a sign between them. When the oven boiled over Noah loaded the ark, those with him being few as God said, and carried two of every pair that has life and of the trees, male and female, with his three sons Shem, Ham and Japheth and their wives and six people who believed, ten in all; his son Yam, an unbeliever, stayed behind. Report 18138, from Ibn Abbas: the first animal Noah loaded was the ant and the last the donkey; when the donkey put in its head and breast Iblis hung on its tail so its legs could not rise, and Noah said, woe to you, enter, even if Satan is with you, a word that slipped from his tongue; so Satan let go, and the donkey entered and Satan with it. Noah said, what brought you in to me, enemy of God? He said, did you not say 'enter, even if Satan is with you'? He said, get out. He said, you cannot but carry me; and he was, they claim, at the back of the ark. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 315}

When Noah was settled in the ark with those who believed, in the month of the year in which he entered, after six hundred years of his life, on the seventeenth night of the month, the springs of the great deep moved and the doors of the sky were opened, as in [[passage/quran-54-11|54:11]] and [[passage/quran-54-12|54:12]]; Noah and those with him entered and he covered it over them with its lid. Between God's sending the water and the water's lifting the ark were forty days and forty nights, then, as the people of the Torah claim, the water lifted it and rose and grew strong; 'We carried him on a thing of planks and nails' in [[passage/quran-54-13|54:13]], 'dusur' being iron nails; so the ark ran with him and those with him in waves like mountains. Noah called to his son who was among those who perished, standing apart, when Noah saw the truth of his Lord's promise, 'my son, ride with us and be not with the disbelievers'; he was wretched and had hidden disbelief, and said 'I shall take refuge on a mountain that will protect me from the water', for he had known mountains as a shelter from rain and thought it would be as he knew. Noah said 'today there is no protector from God's command but whom He has mercy on', and the wave came between them and he was among the drowned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 316}

The water rose until it overflowed and stood fifteen cubits above the mountains, as the people of the Torah claim, so everything on the face of the earth with life or of trees perished, and nothing remained but Noah and those with him in the ark and, as the People of the Book claim, Uj b. Unuq; between God's sending the flood and the water's subsiding were six months and ten nights. Report 18139, from Yusuf b. Mihran: when people's dung troubled Noah in the ark he was ordered to wipe the elephant's tail, and two pigs came out and he was spared it; and mice multiplied in the ark and when they troubled him he was ordered to have the lion sneeze, and from its nostrils came two cats that ate the mice for him; reports 18140 and 18141, from Ibn Abbas, to the same effect. Report 18142, from al-Dahhak: Sulayman al-Qarasi said Noah worked on the ship for four hundred years and grew the teak for forty years until it was four hundred cubits tall. The editor says these reports are all conjecture from the people of earlier books, of no good, that no one of the people of knowledge counts them as anything or as a ground to believe them, that Ali b. Zayd b. Jud'an was weakened by the imams, and that the report belongs to the remnants of the Israelite lore. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 317}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
