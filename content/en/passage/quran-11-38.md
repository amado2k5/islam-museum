**Commentary on verse 38 (pp. 310-312)**

On [[passage/quran-11-38|11:38]] al-Tabari says: Noah builds the ship, and whenever a group of the great men of his people passed him they mocked him, that is, they ridiculed Noah and said, have you turned carpenter after prophethood, and make a ship on dry land? Noah says to them, 'if you mock us' today, 'we shall mock you' in the next life as you mock us in the world; 'you will soon know', when you see God's punishment, which of us was doing wrong to himself. Report 18133, from Aisha, that the Messenger of God said: had God shown mercy to any of Noah's people, He would have shown it to the child's mother. Noah stayed among his people a thousand years less fifty calling them to God until, at the end of his time, he planted a tree which grew great and spread in every direction; then he cut it and began to make a ship, and they would pass and ask him and he would say I am making a ship, and they would mock and say, a ship on dry land, how will it run? He would say, you will soon know. When he finished it and the oven boiled over and the water filled the lanes, the child's mother feared for him, whom she loved intensely, and went out to the mountain until she reached a third of it; when the water reached her she went out until two-thirds; when the water reached her she went until she stood on the mountain; when the water reached her neck she raised him in her hands until the water took her away. Had God shown mercy to any of them, He would have shown it to the child's mother. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 310}

The editor says he finds the chain of this report good, that al-Hakim relates it and calls the chain sound while al-Dhahabi says its chain is obscure and Musa b. Ya'qub is not strong, that Ibn Kathir calls it strange by this route and says the story of this child and his mother is related in similar form from Ka'b al-Ahbar and Mujahid, and that al-Haythami cites it. Report 18134, from Qatada: it was told us that the ship was three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide and thirty cubits high, with its door in its width; report 18135, from al-Hasan: the length of Noah's ship was a thousand and two hundred cubits and its width six hundred. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 311}

Report 18136, from Ibn Abbas: the disciples said to Jesus son of Mary, if you would raise for us a man who saw the ship, to tell us about it. He took them to a mound of dust, took a handful of it and said, do you know what this is? They said God and His Messenger know best. He said, this is the heel of Ham son of Noah. He struck the mound with his staff and said, rise by God's leave, and there he stood shaking the dust from his head, having gone grey. Jesus said to him, did you perish like this? He said no, I died young, but I thought it was the Hour, and so I went grey. He said tell us of Noah's ship. He said: its length was a thousand and two hundred cubits and its width six hundred, and it had three decks, one with the beasts and wild animals, one with people and one with birds. When the beasts' dung grew much God inspired Noah to tap the elephant's tail, and he did, and a pig and a sow fell out and turned to the dung; when the mouse gnawed at the ship's timber, God inspired Noah to strike between the lion's eyes, and from its nostril came a tomcat and a cat, which turned on the mouse. Jesus asked how Noah knew the land had drowned; he said he sent the raven to bring him news, and it found a carcass and settled on it, so he prayed against it with fear, and so it does not keep to houses; then he sent the dove, which came with an olive leaf in its beak and mud on its feet, so he knew the land had drowned, and he ringed it with the green on its neck and prayed that it be in company and safety, and so it keeps to houses. They said, Messenger of God, shall we not take him to our people to sit and talk with us? He said, how would one follow you who has no provision? And he said to him, return by God's leave, and he went back to dust. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 312}
