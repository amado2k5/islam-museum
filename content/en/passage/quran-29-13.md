## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And let them bear their burdens and burdens with their burdens, and on the Day of Resurrection they will be questioned about what they used to fabricate": And let these polytheists bear the burden of those who believe in Him, saying, 'Follow Our way, and We will bear your sins, the burdens of themselves and their sins, and the burdens of those who went astray and turned away from the way of Allah along with their burdens.' And they will be questioned on the Day of Resurrection about what they used to deny in this world by their false promises. And it will be said to them: Follow Our way, and We will bear your sins, so they will fabricate lies about that. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
