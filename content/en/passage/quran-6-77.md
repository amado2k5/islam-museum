## Summary
Verse 77 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 77 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "when he saw the moon rising he said this is my Lord; and when it set he said: if my Lord does not guide me I shall be among the people who go astray" al-Tabari says: when the moon rose and Abraham saw it rising, which is its "buzugh". One says "the sun rose" with "bazaghat" and likewise the moon. "He said this is my Lord; when it set" says when it was gone, "Abraham said: if my Lord does not guide me" and give me success in hitting the truth in affirming his oneness, "I shall be among the people who go astray", that is, among the people who missed the truth in that and did not find guidance and worshipped other than God. Al-Tabari has explained the sense of "error" elsewhere. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 486}

Verse 77 closes here on p. 486, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-78|6:78]].
