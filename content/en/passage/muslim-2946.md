## Summary
A report of Imran ibn Husayn in the chapter on the Dajjal in the Book of Trials and Signs of the Hour of Sahih Muslim: the trial of the false Messiah, the Dajjal, is magnified. Another wording says, "a matter greater than the Dajjal". {cite:sahih-muslim}
