## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 22 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And indeed, in the cattle you have a lesson; We will give you to drink from what is in their bellies, and you will have many benefits from them, from which you will eat": "And indeed, O people," "in the cattle are lessons," by which you will recognize the hand of God with you and His power to do as He wills, and that He is the One who cannot withhold anything He wills and nothing can prevent Him. "We will give you water from what is in their bellies" from the milk that comes out from between the fur and blood. "And for you," yet "in it" means in livestock, "many benefits," like the camels that are carried on and ridden on their backs and drank from their wealth.
