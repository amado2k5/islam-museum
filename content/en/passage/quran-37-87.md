## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 87 to 92 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So what did you think of the Lord of the worlds": He, the Exalted, says, informing about what Abraham said to his father and his people: "So what do you think of the Lord of the worlds?" He says: What do you think, O people, that would be done to you if you met Him while you worshipped someone else? As Qatadah said, "So what did you think of the Lord of the worlds?" He says: When you meet Him while you worship someone else. And His saying: "He looked at the stars and said, 'I am sick,' mentioned that his people were people of astrology. He saw a star rising, tied his head, and said: I am stabbed. His people were fleeing from the plague, so he wanted to leave him in the house of their gods and leave it, so he would oppose them and break it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
