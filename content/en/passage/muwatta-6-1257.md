## Summary
Malik transmits from Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani, from Said ibn Jubayr, that Abdullah ibn Abbas said: whoever forgets or omits something of his rites should shed blood. Ayyub said: I do not know whether he said 'omits' or 'forgets'. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.559}
