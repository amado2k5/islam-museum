**Commentary on verse 106 (pp. 218-219)**

On [[passage/quran-10-106|10:106]] al-Tabari says: do not call, Muhammad, besides your object of worship and Creator, anything that neither benefits you in this world nor the next nor harms you in religion or the world, meaning the gods and idols; do not worship them hoping for their benefit or fearing their harm, for they neither benefit nor harm. 'If you do so', calling on them besides God, 'you will then be among the wrongdoers', meaning among those who associate others with God and wrong themselves. The editor says the printed text and manuscript have 'the one who wrongs himself', which the context does not suit, so he replaced it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 219}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
