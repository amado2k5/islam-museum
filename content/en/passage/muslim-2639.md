## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on a person being with whom he loves, in the Book of Righteousness, Kinship and Manners of Sahih Muslim. In it Anas says, "I love God and His Messenger, Abu Bakr and Umar, and I hope to be with them even if I do not act as they did". {cite:sahih-muslim}
