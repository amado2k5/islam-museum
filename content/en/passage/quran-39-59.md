## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the saying, "Yes, My signs have come to you and you denied them and were arrogant, and you were among the disbelievers": He says, denying the one who says: "If Allah had guided me, I would have been among the righteous," and to the one who said: "If I had a ball, I would be among the doers of good." What is the saying as you say, "Yes, it has come to you": O you who wish for Allah to return to this world so that you may be among the doers of good, My verses means: My proof has come to you from among a messenger I sent to you, and a book I have revealed to you reciting to you what is in it of promise, warning, and reminder. "So you lied" with My verses "and became arrogant." Regarding its acceptance and following "And I was among the disbelievers," he says: And I was among those who do the deeds of the disbelievers, adhere to their Sunnah, and follow their methodology. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
