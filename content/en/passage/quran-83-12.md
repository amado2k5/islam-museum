## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And none denies it except every transgressor and sinner”: And none denies the Day of Judgment “except every transgressor” who transgresses against God in his speech, and disobeys His command, “sinner” against his Lord, as: And on the authority of Qatadah, “Woe on that Day to the deniers,” God said: “And none denies it except every transgressor and sinner”: that is, the Day of Judgment, except every transgressor in his speech, sinner against his Lord. “When Our verses are recited to him,” God Almighty says: When Our proofs and evidences, which We have made clear in Our Book that We revealed to Muhammad, are recited to him, “he says, ‘Legends of the former peoples!’” He says: He says, “This is what the former peoples wrote down and recorded,” from the hadiths and stories. The passage also quotes statements from Qatadah.
