## Summary
A report of Aisha in the chapter on the virtues of Uthman, in Sahih Muslim. The Prophet sat up for Uthman's entry, arranged his clothes and said, "Shall I not be shy before a man before whom the angels are shy?" It is the best-known text on Uthman's modesty in Sunni tradition. {cite:sahih-muslim}
