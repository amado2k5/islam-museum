## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And what We have revealed to you of the Scripture is the truth, confirming what was before it. Indeed, Allah is with His servants All-Aware, All-Seeing": "And what We have revealed to you of the Book" (O Muhammad), which is the Qur'an that Allah revealed to him, "it is the truth," meaning: it is the truth upon you and upon your nation to act upon it, and to follow what is in it and not other scriptures revealed to you, "confirming what was before it," meaning: He believes what has passed before him, so before him are the books that were revealed to those before you among the messengers, as in Qatadah, His saying: "And what We have revealed to you of the Book is the truth, confirming what was before it" for the previous books. And His saying: "Indeed, Allah is All-Aware and All-Seeing" means: Indeed, Allah is knowledgeable and experienced of His servants, All-Seeing in what He does best. In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
