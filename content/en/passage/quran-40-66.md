## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Say, I have been forbidden to worship those you invoke besides Allah, when clear proofs came to me from my Lord and I was commanded to submit to the Lord of the Worlds.'" Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people from Quraysh, "Indeed, I have been forbidden," O people, "to worship those you invoke besides Allah" from gods and idols, "when clear proofs came to me from my Lord." He means: when clear signs came to me from my Lord, and those are the verses of the Book of Allah which He revealed, "And I was commanded to submit to the Lord of the worlds," meaning: My Lord commanded me to humble myself before the Lord of everything, the Owner of every creation with submission, and to submit to Him through obedience and not to other things.
