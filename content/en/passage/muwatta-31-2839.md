## Summary
Malik transmits from Amir ibn Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr that when he heard thunder he would stop speaking and say: glory to Him whom the thunder glorifies with His praise, and the angels from fear of Him. Then he would say: this is a severe warning to the people of the earth. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.591}
