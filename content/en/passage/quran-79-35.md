## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 36 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "On the Day when man will remember what he strove for" as meaning: when the great calamity comes, on the Day when man will remember what he did in this world, both good and evil, and that is his striving. "And Hell will be brought forth" means: Hell will be made manifest, and it is the fire of God for those who see it. He says: for the eyes of those who look. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
