## Summary
Malik says that if a man lawfully marries a woman during her waiting period and has intercourse with her, she becomes forbidden to his son as a wife, because his father married her lawfully, and a child born of that union is attributed to the father. As she is forbidden to the son, so the father's stepdaughter is forbidden to him if he has intercourse with her mother. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.40}
