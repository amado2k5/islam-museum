## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, We have sent down to you the Book for mankind in truth. So whoever is guided, it is for [the soul] of his own soul; and whoever goes astray—he only goes astray against it. And you are over them a guardian." He means: "Indeed, We have sent down to you, O Muhammad, the Book as clarification for mankind in truth." "So whoever is guided, it is for his own soul." He means: "So whoever acts upon what We have revealed to him and follows it, it is for himself." He says: "He has only done so for himself, and he sought it for good, not anything else," because he granted it the pleasure of God, the attainment of Paradise, and salvation from the Fire. "And whoever goes astray" means: Whoever deviates from the Book which We have revealed to you and the clarification We have made clear to you, deliberately deliberately deviates from the right path, is only unjust to himself, and to it he is driven to corruption and destruction, because he earns it the wrath of God, the painful punishment, and constant disgrace. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
