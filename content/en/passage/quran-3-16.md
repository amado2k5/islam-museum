## Verse 16 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, pp. 263-264)

Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-16|3:16]]) says the sense is: say, shall I tell you of something better than that, for the God-fearing, those who say in their prayer that they have believed and ask their Lord for forgiveness and for protection from the punishment of the Fire. He notes that this relative clause allows two cases: the genitive as following the first relative clause, and the nominative as a fresh start, since it opens a verse other than the one with the first. He compares its nominative with al-Tawba ([[passage/quran-9-111|9:111]]), where the purchase is mentioned and the next verse ([[passage/quran-9-112|9:112]]) begins with the description of the penitent and the worshippers in the nominative, and says that the genitive would also have been allowed. The editor refers to al-Farra, Ma'ani al-Qur'an (1: 198). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 263}

Al-Tabari then explains their prayer: they say they believe in God, in His prophet and in what he brought from Him, and ask Him to cover their sins by pardoning them and leaving them unpunished for them, and to keep His punishment of them by fire from them, that is, not to punish them with it. They singled out protection from the Fire because whoever is moved away from it that day has won deliverance from God's punishment and has His good return. The imperative of asking for protection comes from 'God protected so-and-so from such', meaning He warded it off him, and one who asks says 'protect me from such'. The editor refers to his commentary in the fourth part. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 263-264}
