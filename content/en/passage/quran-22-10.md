## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "The second of his conjunction to lead him astray from the way of Allah; for him in this world is disgrace, and on the Day of Resurrection we will make him taste the punishment of the fire": He argues with someone who argues about God without knowledge "the second of his conjunction." Interpreters differ regarding the meaning for which it was described as 'to conjugate His conjunction' and what is meant by describing it by that. Some said: He was described by that to exaggerate and boast about it. It is mentioned about the Arabs that they say: So-and-so came to me with a second conjunction: when he came with arrogance, and in the Maqta' he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
