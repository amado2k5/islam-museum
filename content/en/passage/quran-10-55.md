**Commentary on verse 55 (pp. 103-104)**

On [[passage/quran-10-55|10:55]] he says: all that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth belongs to God, with no part of it for anyone but Him, so this disbeliever has nothing that day to own and offer as ransom from his Lord's punishment, for all things belong to the one whose punishment is at hand. Even if he had what is in the earth and ransomed himself with it, it would not be accepted from him in place of the punishment; how then when he has nothing to ransom with? "The promise of God is true" means that the punishment He threatened these polytheists for their disbelief is true, and they need not hurry it, for it will fall on them without doubt; "but most of them do not know" means they do not know that it will truly fall on them, so in their ignorance of it they deny it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 103}
