## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar that Ka'b al-Ahbar said, in the chapter on the strictness against passing in front of one who prays: if the passer-by knew what was upon him, being swallowed up by the earth would be better for him than passing in front of him. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.221}
