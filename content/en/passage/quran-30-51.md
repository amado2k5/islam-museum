## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if We send a wind and they see it yellowish, they will continue to deny after it": And if We send a corrupting wind, the rain We sent down from the sky produced it. Those whom Allah struck with that rain with which He revived their land, with which their crops grew grass and sprouted, saw that what they produced from that rain of yellow crops was spoiled by that wind We sent, and after its greenness became yellow, they continued to deny their Lord after their glad tidings and joy.
