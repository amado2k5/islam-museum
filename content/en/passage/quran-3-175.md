## Summary
Verse 175 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 175 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 175 (pp. 416-418, reports 8256-8261).** Al-Tabari says that the one who said to the believers what he said, frightening them with the hosts of their enemy and their advance, was acting on Satan's behalf, who put it on the tongues of those who spoke, frightening them with his allies among the polytheists, Abu Sufyan and his companions of Quraysh, so that they would dread them and shrink from them. On this: Qatada (8256), through Bishr from Yazid from Sa'id: God has the believer frightened by the unbeliever and made to dread him; Mujahid (8257), through al-Qasim from al-Husayn from Hajjaj from Ibn Jurayj: he frightens the believers with the unbelievers; Ibn 'Abbas (8258), through Muhammad ibn Sa'd from his father, his uncle, his father and his grandfather: Satan frightens the believers with his allies; Ibn Ishaq (8259), through Ibn Humayd from Salama: those men, that is the party of 'Abd al-Qays who said to the Prophet what they said, with what Satan put on their tongues, meaning he terrifies you with his allies (the note: Ibn Hisham, 3: 128, completing the reports ending at 8253); and Salim al-Aftas (8260), through Yunus from 'Ali ibn Ma'bad from 'Attab ibn Bashir, freedman of Quraysh: he frightens you by his allies. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 416}

**The other view (p. 417).** Others said the meaning is that Satan magnifies the matter of the polytheists in your own selves, you hypocrites, so that you fear them; al-Suddi (8261), through Muhammad from Ahmad from Asbat: he spoke of the polytheists and magnified them in the eyes of the hypocrites, so he magnifies his allies in their breasts and they fear them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 417}

**A point of Arabic usage (p. 417).** Al-Tabari raises an objection: how can it be said that Satan frightens his allies, when he frightens others by means of them? He answers that this parallels the wording of [[passage/quran-18-2|18:2]], whose meaning is a warning of God's severe might, since might is not warned but warned of; the note refers to al-Farra' (1: 248) and says the editor added bracketed words which he thinks the copyist dropped. He mentions that some Basran grammarians said the meaning is that Satan frightens people with his allies, as one says "he gives dirhams and clothes garments", meaning he gives people dirhams and clothes them, with the object dropped as unneeded. Al-Tabari replies that the comparison is not apt: in that saying, it is obvious that the dirhams are the thing given, whereas here the allies are not those frightened but the ones from whom fright comes to others, so the two differ. In the two notes the editor keeps the manuscript text with a bracketed "from" and spells out the syntax. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 417}

**On forbidding the believers to fear (p. 418).** Al-Tabari explains the last part of the verse as God telling the believers not to fear the polytheists, not to let their affair weigh on them or dread their host, so long as they obey Him and follow His command, and that He guarantees them victory and success; rather, they should fear Him and beware of disobeying and opposing His command lest they perish, if they believe in His Messenger and what he brought from Him. The note says the manuscript reads "mutakallif" ("taking on a burden"), a gross error, since God is exalted above burdening Himself with anything and is the Able to whom nothing is heavy, and that the publisher of the earlier edition was right in what he did. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 418}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 418}
