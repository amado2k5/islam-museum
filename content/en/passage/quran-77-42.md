## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41-45 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And fruits of whatever they desire”: They will eat of them whenever they desire, without fear of harm or unpleasant consequences. And His saying, “Eat and drink in good health for what you used to do”: God Almighty says: It will be said to them, “Eat, O people, of these fruits, and drink from these springs whenever you desire, in good health.” He says: There will be no disturbance or annoyance to you in what you eat and drink, but it will be constant and pleasant for you, causing no harm to your bodies.
