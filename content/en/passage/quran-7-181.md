**Commentary on verse 181 (pp. 285-286)**

Al-Tabari says: "of those We created there is a community", meaning a group, "who guide by the truth", meaning they are guided by the truth, "and by it they act justly", meaning they judge by the truth and deal fairly with people. Report 15458, from Ibn Jurayj: it was mentioned to us that the Prophet of God said, this is my community; by the truth they take and give and judge. Report 15459, from Qatada, gives the verse, the editor putting dots because the report is incomplete, either dropped by the copyist or the chain of the next report. Report 15460, from Qatada: it reached us that the Prophet of God, when he read it, would say this is for you, and the people before you were given the like of it, [[passage/quran-7-159|7:159]]. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "community", "guide" and "act justly". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 285} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 286}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
