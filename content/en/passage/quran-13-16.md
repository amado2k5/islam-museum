**Commentary on verse 16 (pp. 405-408)**

On "say: who is the Lord of the heavens and the earth? Say: God. Say: have you then taken protectors besides Him who have no power to benefit or harm even themselves?", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise, to His Prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists, who is the Lord of the heavens and the earth and their ruler? They will say, God. And God commanded His Prophet to say "God", saying to him: say, Muhammad, their Lord who created and originated them is the One to whom alone worship is fitting, and He is God. Then He said: when they answer you so, say to them, have you taken besides the Lord of the heavens and the earth protectors who cannot own any benefit to bring to themselves nor any harm to ward off from themselves? And since they cannot own that for themselves, their owning it for others is further off, yet you worshipped them and left the worship of the One in whose hand are benefit and harm, life and death and the ordering of all things. Then He, mighty be His praise, struck them a parable and said "say: are the blind and the seeing equal?" {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 405}

On "say: are the blind and the seeing equal, or are the darkness and the light equal, or have they assigned God partners who created as He created so that the creation seemed alike to them? Say: God is the Creator of everything, and He is the One, the Subduer", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise, to His Prophet Muhammad: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists who worshipped besides God, in whose hand is their benefit and harm, what neither benefits nor harms, "are the blind equal", who sees nothing and is not guided to a path he can follow unless he is guided, "and the seeing", who guides the blind man to the highway of the road he does not see? They are surely not equal. He says: so too the believer, who sees the truth and follows it and knows guidance and walks in it, is not equal to you, polytheists, who know no truth and see no right guidance. On "or are the darknesses and the light equal" he says: are the darknesses in which the path cannot be seen to be followed nor the road seen to be ridden equal to the light by which things are seen and whose brightness clears the dark? These are surely not equal; so too disbelief in God leaves its owner in bewilderment, always wading in a flood that returns him to no reality, while faith in God leaves its owner in brightness, acting with knowledge of his Lord and knowing that He has one to reward him for his good, one to punish him for his evil, one to provide for him and one to benefit him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 406}

Report 20305, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl and Ibn Abi Najih: as for "the blind and the seeing", they are the disbeliever and the believer, and as for "the darknesses and the light", they are guidance and error. On "or have they assigned God partners who created as He created so that the creation seemed alike to them?" he says to His Prophet: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists, did your idols, which you took as protectors besides God, create as God created so that their affair confused you about what they created and what God created, and you made them His partners for that reason, or is it only ignorance in you and straying from what is right? It is not hidden to anyone with reason that worshipping what neither harms nor benefits is ignorance, and that worship is fitting only for the one whose benefit is hoped and whose harm is feared; just as that error and the ignorance of whoever does it is not obscure, so too the ignorance of one who joins in the worship of the One who provides for him, sustains him and supplies him with one who cannot harm or benefit him is not obscure. Some interpreters said the same. Reports 20306 to 20308, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl; Ishaq, Abd Allah and Waraqa; and al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "or have they assigned God partners who created as He created", that led them to doubt about the idols. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 407}

Report 20309, from Mujahid, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Shababa, Waraqa and Ibn Abi Najih: the same. Report 20310, from Ibn Kathir, through Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: I heard Mujahid say, "or have they assigned God partners who created as He created so that the creation seemed alike to them", it was struck as a parable. On "say: God is the Creator of everything" he says to His Prophet: say to these polytheists, once they admit to you that their idols, which they set up as partners in the worship of God, create nothing: God is your Creator, the Creator of your idols and the Creator of everything, so what ground is there for your associating what neither creates nor harms? On "and He is the One, the Subduer" he says: He is the Single, who has no second, and "the Subduer" is the One who deserves divinity and worship, not the images and idols that neither harm nor benefit. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 408}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
