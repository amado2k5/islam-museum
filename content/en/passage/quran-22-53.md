## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So that He may make what Satan casts a trial for those in whose hearts is disease and hardness of hearts. And indeed, the wrongdoers are in a distant discord": So Allah abrogates what Satan casts, then He judges His verses to make what Satan casts in His Prophet's wish falsehood, as the Prophet said: "Those are the highest thresholds, and their intercession is hoped for." "Fitna" means a test by which those in their hearts are tested with a disease of hypocrisy, and that doubt about the repulsion of the Messenger of Allah and the truth of what he tells them. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
