## Summary
A saying of Malik: likewise all raisins, black and red; when a man picks five wasqs of them, zakat is due; if it does not reach that, there is no zakat. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.369}
