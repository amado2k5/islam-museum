## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd al-Tawil, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: we travelled with the Messenger of God in Ramadan, and he did not blame the one who fasted for the one who broke the fast, nor the one who broke the fast for the one who fasted. The editor notes that Abu Musab and, through him, Ibn Hibban and al-Baghawi transmitted it, as did Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi (al-Bukhari 1947), al-Jawhari and al-Shafii, and refers to al-Tamhid (2/169). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.396}
