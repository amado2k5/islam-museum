## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that he had a vow to walk, then he was seized by a pain in his side, so he rode until he came to Mecca. He asked Ata ibn Abi Rabah and others, and they said: an offering is owed by you. When he came to Medina he asked, and they told him to walk again from where he grew weak, and he walked. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.607}
