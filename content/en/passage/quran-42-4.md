## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 5 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and He is the Most High, the Most Great": Allah has the dominion of "whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth" of all things, "and He is the Most High." He means: He is the possessor of exaltation and exaltation above everything, and all things are below Him, because they are under His dominion, His power flows over them, and His will "the Great" proceeds within them, who has greatness, pride, and determinism. And His saying: "The heavens are almost splitting open from above them," the Exalted says: The heavens are almost splitting open from above the earth, due to the greatness and majesty of the Most Merciful. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
