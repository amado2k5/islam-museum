## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 48 to 50 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, it has been revealed to us that the punishment is upon those who denied and turned away": Allah says to His messenger Moses and Aaron: "And it is said to Pharaoh: Indeed, your Lord has revealed to us that His punishment will never be exhausted, and there will be no cessation upon those who deny what we call to of Allah's oneness and obedience, and the response of His messengers." 'And turn away,' meaning: turn away from what We brought of the truth, as in Qatadah, His saying: 'Indeed, the punishment is upon whoever lies and turns away,' is a lie about the Book of Allah and turned away from obedience to Allah. And His saying: "He said, 'Who is your Lord, O Moses?'" is omitted in this statement, leaving it out of sufficiency to indicate what was mentioned about him, which is His saying: "So they came to him." They told him what their Lord had commanded them and conveyed His message. Pharaoh said to them, "Who is your Lord, Moses?" He addressed Moses alone, saying: "O Moses," and before that, the speech was directed to Moses and his brother. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
