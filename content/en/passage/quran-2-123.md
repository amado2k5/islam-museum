## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and fear a day when no soul will avail another soul at all, nor will any ransom be accepted from it, nor will any intercession benefit it, nor will they be helped", al-Tabari says this verse is a warning from God to those whom He had exhorted in the preceding verse: fear, company of the Children of Israel who altered My Book, changed its meaning and denied My messenger Muhammad, the punishment of a day on which no soul decides anything for another soul nor avails it at all if you perish in your disbelief in Me and denial of My messenger and die in it; for it is a day on which no ransom is accepted from a soul for what is due from it, no intercessor intercedes for a right owed by it, and no helper from God helps it when He takes vengeance on it for disobeying Him. He refers to the explanation already given of all the senses of this verse in its parallel earlier. Here the second volume of al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir edition) ends, to be followed by the third, which begins with the commentary on "and when his Lord tested Abraham with words". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 574}
