## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Masud on the creation of a person: forty days as a drop, a clot and a lump, then an angel is sent to breathe in the spirit and is told four matters: his provision, his deeds, his term and whether he is wretched or blessed. A person may act like the people of Paradise until the writing overtakes him. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
