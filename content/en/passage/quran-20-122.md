## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 121 to 122 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then he chose his Lord, so he accepted his repentance and was guidance": His Lord chose him after he disobeyed him, so He provided him with the means of returning to what He was pleased with and acting upon His obedience, and that was His repentance, which He had repented to. And His saying: "And He guided" means He guided him to repentance, so He guided him to it. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
