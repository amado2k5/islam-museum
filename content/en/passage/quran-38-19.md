## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 17–20. God commands His Prophet to be patient with what they say and reminds His servant David, the Possessor of Hands, that he repented. He subjected the mountains with him, glorifying them in the evening and the sunrise, and the birds are crowded, and We strengthened His dominion and gave him wisdom and decisive discourse.
