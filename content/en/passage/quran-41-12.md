## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So He decreed them as seven heavens in two days and revealed to each heaven its command and adorned the lowest heaven with lamps and preserved it. That is the decree of the Almighty, the All-Knowing": So He finished creating them seven heavens in two days, on Thursday and Friday, as reported by Al-Suddi, who said: He rose to the heaven, which is smoke from the breath of water when he breathed, so He made it one heaven, split it apart, and made it seven heavens in two days, on Thursday and Friday. It is called Friday because it combines the creation of the heavens and the earth. And His saying: "And He revealed in every heaven its command," means: He cast into each of the seven heavens whatever creation He willed. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
