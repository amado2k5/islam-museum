**Commentary on verse 62 (pp. 369-370)**

On [[passage/quran-11-62|11:62]] al-Tabari says: Thamud said to Salih their prophet, 'Salih, you were among us a man of hope', that is, we hoped you would be a chief among us before this saying you said to us, that we have no god but God; 'do you forbid us to worship what our fathers worshipped?' means do you forbid us to worship the gods our fathers worshipped; 'and we are in grave doubt about what you call us to' means they did not know the soundness of what he called them to, of God's oneness and that divinity belongs to Him alone. 'Raising suspicion' means causing blame, from 'arabtuhu fa-ana uribuhu' when you do to him a thing that gives him doubt; he cites a line of Khalid b. Zuhayr al-Hudhali about the nephew of Abu Dhu'ayb, whose story the editor explains. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 370}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
