## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: whichever slave-girl bears a child to her master, he may not sell her, give her away, or bequeath her; he may enjoy her, and when he dies she is free. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.327}
