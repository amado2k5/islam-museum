## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those before them have plotted, so Allah has sent their structure from the foundations, a roof of boasting over them, and the punishment has come to them from where they do not perceive." Those who have plotted before these polytheists who hinder those who seek to follow Allah's religion, so they sought to overcome Allah by building His children, claiming to ascend to the heavens to fight those within it. The one who intended this, as mentioned to us, was a mighty one of the Nabataean tyrants. Some said: He is Nimrod son of Canaan, and others said: He is Bakhtinasr. I have mentioned some of their stories in Surah Ibrahim. It is said that what is mentioned here is the one Allah mentioned in Surah Ibrahim. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Zayd ibn Aslam.
