## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And He said, 'You have only taken besides Allah idols out of affection among you in the life of this world. Then on the Day of Resurrection, some of you will disbelieve in one another and curse one another, and your refuge will be the Fire, and you will have no helpers.'" Allah says, informing about what Abraham said to his people: "And Abraham said to his people: O my people, you have only taken idols besides Allah." Reciters differed in the recitation of the phrase: "affection among you." The majority of the reciters of Medina and the Levant, as well as some Kufans, read it as "Mawaddah" in the accusative case of affection without the genitive between you in the accusative. Some Kufans read this as: "affection between you" as the accusative and added to the phrase "between you," and it was reduced to "baynakum."
