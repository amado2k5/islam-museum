## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And know that among you is the Messenger of Allah. If he were to obey you in many matters, you would be cursed. But Allah has endeared faith to you and beautified it in your hearts and hated disbelief, wickedness, and disobedience to you. Those are the rightly guided ones." To the companions of the Prophet of Allah: And know, O believers in Allah and His Messenger, that among you is the Messenger of Allah. So fear Allah lest you speak falsehood and spread lies, for Allah informs him of your news, knows him of your news, and guides him to the right path in his affairs. And His saying: "If he had obeyed you in many matters, you would have been cursed," the Exalted says: If the Messenger of Allah had acted according to your opinions in matters and accepted what you said to you and obeyed you, "you would have been cursed," meaning hardship and hardship in many matters by obeying you. Beware of obeying you, for he would have erred in his actions, as if he had accepted from Al-Walid ibn Uqbah his statement about Banu al-Mustaliq: They apostatized, withheld charity, and gathered the masses to invade the Muslims. He attacked them and killed some of them, and their blood and wealth were damaged. He had been killed, and you killed those who do not know. In this section, there are statements from Qatadah.
