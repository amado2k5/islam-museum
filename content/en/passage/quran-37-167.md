## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 165–169. Al-Tabari mentions that the angels say, "Indeed, We are the righteous, and indeed, We are the glorifyers," and he mentions the narration from the Prophet and the sayings of interpreters, and that the polytheists used to say, "If we had a male among the former peoples, we would be sincere servants of Allah."
