## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 40 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “So what is the matter with those who disbelieve, rushing towards you?” (48:37): “What is the matter with those who disbelieve in God, rushing towards you, O Muhammad?” We have already explained the meaning of “rushing” and what the scholars of interpretation have said about it previously, which makes repeating it here unnecessary. However, we will mention here some of what was not mentioned there. Qatadah said regarding it: On the authority of Qatadah, regarding the phrase, “So what is the matter with those who disbelieve, rushing towards you?” he said: “Deliberately.” Ibn Zayd said regarding it: On the authority of Ibn Zayd, regarding the phrase, “So what is the matter with those who disbelieve, rushing towards you?” he said: “The one who rushes is the one who does not blink.” Some of the scholars of Arabic from Basra said: Its meaning is: “Hastening.” It was narrated in it on the authority of al-Hasan: And on the authority of al-Hasan, regarding His saying: “So what is the matter with those who disbelieve, that they are rushing towards you?” he said: “Going forth.” And on the authority of al-Hasan, the same. And his saying: And he quotes in the section sayings on the authority of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan and Ibn Zayd.
