## Summary
Ziyad reports from Malik from Abdallah ibn Dinar from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God said: seek the Night of Decree in the last seven nights. The editor notes that Abu Musab (888) and Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari (2015) transmitted it, and that in Malik this hadith is from the transmission of Abdallah ibn Dinar from Ibn Umar, unlike hadith 895. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.428}
