## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar: Jabir reports through two chains from the Prophet about the death of the chief of the Aws after his judgement on Banu Qurayza. It corrects Jabir's narration about the shaking of the bier and explains its cause. It is among the best-known virtues of the Companions. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
