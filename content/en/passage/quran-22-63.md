## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Have you not seen that Allah sends down water from the sky and the earth becomes green? Indeed, Allah is Gentle and All-Aware": "Have you not seen," O Muhammad, "that Allah sends down water from the sky" meaning rain, "and the earth becomes green" with the plants that grow within it. "Indeed, Allah is subtle" by extracting plants from the earth with that water and other innovations He wills to innovate. "Knowledgeable" about what happens about that seed of love and with it. He said: "Then the earth will become," and he raised it, and his preceded saying: "Did you not see?" It was said so because the meaning of the speech is the predicate, as if it was said: Do you know, O Muhammad, that God sends down water from the sky and the earth becomes the earth? A similar example of this is the poet's saying: Did you not ask the old quarter so that it speaks? And will you tell you today about the valley of Samlak, because its meaning is: I asked him and he spoke.
