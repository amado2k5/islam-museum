## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Hamza and Salim, sons of Abdullah ibn Umar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Prophet said: the ill omen is in the house, the woman and the horse. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.566}
