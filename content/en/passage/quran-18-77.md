## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So they set out until, when the people of a town came to feed its people and refused to add them, they found a wall in it that wanted to be broken, so he erected it. He said, 'If you wished, you could take a reward for it.'" Moses and the scholar set out, "until, when they came to the people of a town, they fed its people" of food, but they did not feed them, and he hosted them. "They refused to add them and found a wall in it that wanted to be broken." He says: They found a wall in the village that wanted to collapse and collapse, from which it is said: The house collapsed: when it collapsed and fell, and from it the star collapsed, which means its fall and disappearance from its place. From this is the saying of Dhu al-Rummah: 'So it collapsed like the shining star, reaching down.' It was narrated from Yahya ibn Ya'mar that he read this: 'He means to be destroyed.' In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
