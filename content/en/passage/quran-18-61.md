## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So when they reached a gathering between them, they forgot their whales and took their way into the sea, a flock": meaning: When Moses and his young daughter reached the gathering of the two seas, as he said: all together from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase "gathered between them," he said: between the two seas. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. And His saying: "They forgot their whales" means by saying: they forgot: they left them, as he said: they all forgot from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid "they forgot their whales," he said: they misled them. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, who said: He misled him. Some Arabs said: The fish was with Joshua, and he forgot it, so forgetfulness was added to them, as he said: "Pearls and coral come out of them," and it comes from salt, not sweet. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Jurayj.
