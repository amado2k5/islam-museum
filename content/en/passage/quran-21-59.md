## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 59 to 61 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'Who did this to our gods? Indeed, he is among the wrongdoers': The people of Abraham, when they saw their gods had gone to hell, except for the one with whom the axe was tied, Abraham said: Who did this to our gods? The one who did this to our gods is among the wrongdoers, meaning those who do it to those who did not do it." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
