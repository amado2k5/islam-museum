## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab used to say: if a man enters upon a woman in her house, the dowry is confirmed for her; if she enters upon him in his house, it is confirmed for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.34}
