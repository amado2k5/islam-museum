## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And intercession will not benefit with Him except for one whom He has permitted, until, when it terrified from their hearts, they say, 'What did your Lord say?' They said, 'The truth, and it is the Most High, the Great.'" The intercession of an intercessor will not benefit anyone who intercedes for whom He intercedes, except that he intercedes for whom Allah has permitted intercession. Allah says: "So if intercessions benefit no one with Allah except for whom Allah has permitted intercession, and Allah does not permit any of His allies to intercede for anyone who disbelieves in Him, and you are people of disbelief in Him, O polytheists, then how can you worship those you worship besides Allah, thinking that you worship Him, so that He may bring you closer to Allah and intercede for you with your Lord?" "So who?" This is the meaning of the phrase in the phrase "except for those to whom He permits": the one who intercedes for him. Readers differed in the reading of the phrase: "He permitted him," so the majority of readers read it with a damma on the alif of "he permitted him" in a way that does not specify its doer. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibrahim.
