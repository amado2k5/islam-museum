**Commentary on verse 84: "Why should we not believe in God and the truth that has come to us", pp. 511-512**

Al-Tabari says: this is God's report of the people He described in these verses, that when they hear what was sent down to His messenger of His book they believe in it and accept it, and say: why should we not affirm God's oneness and what came to us from Him of His book and revealed verses, when we hope, through believing, that our Lord will admit us with the righteous people, namely the believers in God who obey Him and have earned Paradise by obedience, that is, enter us where they enter in His Garden on the day of resurrection and join our ranks to theirs. Report 12335, from Ibn Zayd, says the righteous people are the Messenger and his Companions.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 512}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
