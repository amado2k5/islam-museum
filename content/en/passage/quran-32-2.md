## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 1–3. Al-Tabari mentions that the 'Alm' was previously explained, and that the revelation of the Book revealed to Muhammad is unquestionable, and that he is from the Lord of the worlds, Lord of the two weighty things, jinn and mankind, and that the Qur'an is neither poetry, nor soothsayer's rhyme, nor anything Muhammad fabricated. This refutes the claim of those who claimed to have fabricated it, for it is the truth from his Lord, to warn a people to whom no warner had come before Him, so that they might be guided.
