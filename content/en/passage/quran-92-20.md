## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20-22 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And no one has with him any favor for which reward is due”: Some Arab scholars interpreted this to mean: “And no one among God’s creation has with this person who gives his wealth in the way of God to purify himself any favor for which reward is due.” This means: he does not spend what he spends, nor give what he gives, as a reward to someone for a favor done to him, nor as recompense for a favor previously bestowed upon him. Rather, he gives it in accordance with God’s rights, seeking God’s pleasure. In this section, he quotes statements attributed to Qatadah.
