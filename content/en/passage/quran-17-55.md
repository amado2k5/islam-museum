## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And your Lord knows best who is in the heavens and the earth. And We certainly preferred some prophets over others, and We gave David the Psalms": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: And your Lord, O Muhammad, knows best who is in the heavens and the earth and what reforms them, for He is their Creator, their Provider, and their Manager. He knows best who is worthy of repentance and mercy, and who is worthy of punishment. He guides to the truth those who have already received mercy and happiness from me, and who has misery and abandonment ahead of me, as He says: So do not exaggerate that to you, for that is what I have done to them because I preferred some prophets over others, by sending some to one creation and some to all, and elevating some of them above others. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Jurayj.
