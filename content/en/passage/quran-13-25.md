**Commentary on verse 25 (pp. 428-429)**

On "those who break God's covenant after its confirmation, sever what God commanded to be joined, and spread corruption on earth, for them is the curse and for them is the evil abode", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: as for those who break God's covenant, their breaking it being their going against God's command and acting in disobedience to Him, "after its confirmation", that is, after they bound themselves to God to act on what He enjoined on them, "and sever what God commanded to be joined", that is, cut the kinship God commanded them to join, "and spread corruption on earth", their corruption in it being their acting in it in disobedience to God, "for them is the curse", these have the curse, which is distance from His mercy and exclusion from His gardens, "and for them is the evil abode", that is, what distresses them in the abode of the hereafter. Report 20349, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abd Allah ibn Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: the greatest of the major sins is associating partners with God, because God says "whoever associates partners with God, it is as if he fell from the sky and the birds snatched him" [[passage/quran-22-31|22:31]], then breaking the covenant and cutting kinship, because God, exalted, says "for them is the curse and for them is the evil abode", meaning the evil outcome. Report 20350, from Ibn Jurayj, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn and Hajjaj: {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 428}

on "and sever what God commanded to be joined", he said, it reached us that the Prophet said: if you do not walk on foot to your kinsman and do not give him of your wealth, you have cut him off. Report 20351, from Mus'ab ibn Sa'd, through Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna, Muhammad ibn Ja'far, Shu'ba and Amr ibn Murra: I asked my father about this verse "say: shall we inform you of the greatest losers in their deeds, those whose effort has been lost in the life of this world" [[passage/quran-18-103|18:103-104]], are they the Haruriyya? He said no; rather the Haruriyya are "those who break God's covenant after its confirmation, sever what God commanded to be joined and spread corruption on earth, for them is the curse and for them is the evil abode"; and Sa'd used to call them the transgressors. The editor notes that al-Bukhari transmitted it in his Sahih and al-Hakim in the Mustadrak. Report 20352, from Mus'ab ibn Sa'd, through Ibn al-Muthanna, Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi, Shu'ba and Amr ibn Murra: I used to hold the copy of the Qur'an for Sa'd and he came to this verse, and then he gave the like of the previous report. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 429}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
