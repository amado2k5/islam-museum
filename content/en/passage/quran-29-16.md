## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Abraham, when he said to his people, 'Worship Allah and fear Him; that is better for you if you only knew.": And also remember, O Muhammad, Ibrahim, the Friend of the Merciful, when he said to his people: Worship Allah, O people, and not other idols and idols, for you have no deity except Him. 'And fear Him,' meaning: and beware of His wrath by fulfilling His obligations and avoiding His sins. 'That is better for you if you only knew.' What is better for you than what is bad for you.
