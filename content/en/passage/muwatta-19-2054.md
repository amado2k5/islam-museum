## Summary
Malik says: if all the costs and expenses are on the owner of the orchard, and nothing falls on the partner who enters the partnership except that he works with his hands, being merely a labourer for part of the dates, that is not proper, because one does not know what his wage is, when nothing known is named for him to work on, and one does not know whether it will be little or much. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.241}
