## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “O Prophet, strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites and be harsh upon them. Their abode is Hell, and wretched is the destination,” is explained as follows: God Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, “O Prophet, strive against the disbelievers” with the sword “and the hypocrites” with threats and words. Qatadah said regarding this: Regarding the verse, “O Prophet, strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites,” Qatadah said: God commanded His Prophet to strive against the disbelievers with the sword and to be harsh upon the hypocrites with the prescribed punishments. “And be harsh with them,” meaning: be severe with them for the sake of God. “And their abode is Hell,” meaning: their dwelling place is Hell, and their destination is the fire of Hell. “And wretched is the destination,” meaning: and wretched is the place to which they will go, Hell. The passage also quotes statements from Qatadah.
