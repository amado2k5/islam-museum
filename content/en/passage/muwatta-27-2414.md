## Summary
It reached Malik that al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, Salim ibn Abdallah and Urwa ibn al-Zubayr used to say: if a runaway slave steals what requires cutting, his hand is cut off. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.397}
