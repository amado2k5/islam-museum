## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya: Malik was asked about one who recited a prostration while a menstruating woman listened, and whether she may prostrate. He said neither a man nor a woman prostrates unless both are pure. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.284} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.285}
