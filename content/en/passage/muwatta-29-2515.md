## Summary
Malik said: the practice among us is that the front teeth, the molars and the canines have equal blood-money, because the Prophet said: for a tooth, five camels; a molar is a tooth among the teeth, and none is preferred over another. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.432}
