## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Abdullah ibn Qusayt al-Laythi from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, who used to say: the slaughter of what is in the belly of a slaughtered animal is in the slaughter of its mother, if its creation is complete and its hair has grown. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.633}
