## Summary
In the Book of the Call to Prayer: Aisha reports that the Prophet ordered Abu Bakr to lead people in prayer during his final illness. She and Hafsa asked that Umar lead instead, fearing Abu Bakr would weep, but he refused and said, "You are like the women of Joseph". It shows Abu Bakr leading prayer in the Prophet's last days; Sunni readers cite it in support of his precedence in the caliphate. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
