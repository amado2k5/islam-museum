## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The example of those who took gods and idols besides Allah as allies, hoping for its help and benefit when they needed it, is like a spider in its weakness, ugliness, and poor choice of themselves, like a spider in its weakness and little deception for itself. It took a house for itself to be a place to stay, but nothing availed it when it was needed. Likewise, these polytheists were not helped when it was revealed. By Allah's command, and upon them came the wrath of their allies who took them as anything besides Allah, and they did not repel from them what Allah permitted of His wrath through their worship of them. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
