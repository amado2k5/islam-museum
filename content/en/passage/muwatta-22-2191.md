## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the practice among us regarding someone who gives another a gift without seeking its return, with witnesses to it, is that it stands for the one given it, unless the giver dies before the recipient takes possession of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.299}
