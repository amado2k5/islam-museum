## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have they not traveled through the land so that they have hearts with which they can reason, or ears with which they hear? Indeed, it is not the eyes that are blinded, but rather the hearts within the breasts are blinded": Did these deniers of God's signs and those who deny His power not travel through the lands, looking at the battles of their strikers among the deniers of God's messengers who came before them, like 'Aad, Thamud, the people of Lot, and Shu'ayb, and their homelands and dwellings, so they reflect on them, consider them, and know by their contemplation of their affairs and the affairs of their people. It is God's law regarding those who disbelieve, worship others, and deny His messengers, so they turn away from They are arrogant and disbeliever, and when they reflect on this, take heed of it, and turn to the truth, they will have "hearts with which they reason" will be God's proofs over His creation and His power over what we have shown. "Or ears with which they hear," meaning: or ears that listen to the truth and understand it and distinguish it from falsehood.
