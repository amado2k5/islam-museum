## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Solomon inherited David and said, O people, teach us the understanding of birds and give us of everything. Indeed, this is the clear bounty": "Solomon inherited" his father David the knowledge that God had given him during his lifetime and the kingdom He granted him over all his people, so He made it for him after his father David all the descendants of his father. "And he said, 'O people, teach us the logic of birds.'" He says: Solomon said to his people: O people, teach us the logic of birds, meaning we understood their words and made it from birds like the logic of a man among the children of Adam, as he understood it from them. He said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Abu Ma'shar, from Muhammad ibn Ka'b: "And he said, 'O people, teach us the logic of birds.'" He said: We heard that Solomon's army was one hundred farsakhs: twenty-five for humans, twenty-five for jinn, twenty-five for wild animals, and twenty-five for birds.
