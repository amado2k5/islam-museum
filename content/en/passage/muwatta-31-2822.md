## Summary
It reached Malik that Aisha, wife of the Prophet, used to send to some of her household after the night prayer, saying: will you not let the scribes rest? The editor notes that some copies add that 'the scribes' means the angels who record deeds. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.584}
