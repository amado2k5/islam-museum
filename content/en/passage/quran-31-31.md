## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Have you not seen that the ships sail in the sea by the favor of Allah to show you of His signs? Indeed in that are signs for every grateful patient": Have you not seen, O Muhammad, that ships sail in the sea as a blessing from Allah upon His creation? "To show you of His signs and proofs against you." Indeed, in "That are signs for every grateful patient": He says: The movement of the ships in the sea indicates that Allah, who conducted them, is the Truth, and that what they call upon besides Him is falsehood. "For every patient patient is grateful." He says: Whoever patiently resists what Allah has forbidden and thanks Him for His blessings without disbelief. And from Qatadah, he said: Mutarrif used to say: Among the most beloved servants of Allah to him is the cactus of gratitude.
