## Summary
Malik says about a woman who has never made the pilgrimage, that if she has no male relative to go with her, or has one but he cannot go with her, she should not leave God's obligation of the pilgrimage upon her, and she should go out in a group of women. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.569}
