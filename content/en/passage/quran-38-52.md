## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 54 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And with them are women whose gaze is limited to their husbands": Among those pious whom God honored by the description in this verse of their dwelling in the Gardens of Eden are "women whose gaze is limited to their husbands, so they do not turn away from others nor extend their eyes to others." As Qatadah said, "And with them are women whose gaze is limited to their husbands, so they do not want anyone else." And regarding Al-Suddi, 'those with limited glances,' he said: They have restricted their sight, hearts, and ears to their husbands, so they do not want anyone else. And His saying: 'Atrub' means: one tooth. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
