## Summary
Malik transmits from Jafar ibn Muhammad, from his father, from Ali ibn Abi Talib, that the Messenger of God slaughtered some of his offering with his own hand, and had others slaughter the rest. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the correct chain is through Jabir, and that Ali's hadith came from Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Layla from him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.528}
