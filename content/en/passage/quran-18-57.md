## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And who is more unjust than one who is reminded of the verses of his Lord and turns away from them and forgets what his hands have sent forth? Indeed, We have placed barriers over their hearts so that they may understand it, and in their ears there is deafness. And if you invite them to guidance, they will never be guided." The Almighty says: "And which people have been placed to turn away and avert in their proper place than those who mentioned His signs and proofs, guided him by them to the path of righteousness, and guided him to the path of salvation, turning away from His signs and evidences by which he argued for salvation from destruction?" His hands" means: He forgot what his hands had sent before him of deadly sins, so he did not repent, nor did he repent, as in Qatadah, regarding his saying: "And he forgot what his hands had presented": meaning he forgot what came before of sins. And His saying: "Indeed, We have placed over their hearts veils that they may understand and in their ears deafness," Allah says: Indeed, We have placed veils over the hearts of those who turn away from the signs of Allah when they are mentioned so that they do not understand Him, because the meaning is that they understand what they are reminded of. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
