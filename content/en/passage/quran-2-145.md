## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "even if you brought those given the Book every sign, they would not follow your qibla; nor will you follow their qibla; nor do some of them follow the qibla of others" al-Tabari says: even if you brought the Jews and Christians every proof and argument that the truth is what you brought them, the duty of turning from the qibla of Jerusalem to the Sacred Mosque, they would neither affirm it nor follow your qibla. He explains that "la'in" is answered with the past tense by analogy with "law", the first lam standing as an oath and the second as its answer. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 184} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 185} On "nor will you follow their qibla" he says: you have no way to follow their qibla, since the Jews face Jerusalem and the Christians the east, so how could you follow their qibla given their differing directions? So keep to your qibla. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 185}

On "nor do some of them follow the qibla of others", from al-Suddi and Ibn Zayd, the Jews do not follow the Christians' qibla nor the Christians the Jews', and the verse came down when the Prophet was turned to the Ka'ba and the Jews said Muhammad longed for his father's land and birthplace, and had he stayed on our qibla we might have hoped he was our awaited one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 185} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 186} Al-Tabari explains that the Jews and Christians do not agree on one qibla while each party holds to its creed, so the Prophet was told not to hope for their pleasure, for there is no way to it: following the Jews' qibla would anger the Christians and following the Christians' would anger the Jews; so he should call them to unite on his upright, submitting creed and the qibla of Abraham and the prophets after him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 186}

On "and if you followed their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you would then be among the wrongdoers" he says: if you sought the pleasure of the Jews and Christians who said to you and your companions "be Jews or Christians and you will be guided", and followed their qibla, returning to it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 186} "After the knowledge that came to you" means after what reached you by My informing you that they hold to falsehood and obstinacy against the truth while knowing that the qibla I directed you to is the one I made obligatory on Abraham's house and the messengers among his descendants; and "you would then be among the wrongdoers" means among My servants who wrong themselves, oppose My command and abandon My obedience. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 186} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 187}
