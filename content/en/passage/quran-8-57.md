## Summary
Verse 57 of [[passage/quran-8|Surat al-Anfal]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 57 of 75 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 57 (pp. 22-24)**

Al-Tabari says: God says to His Prophet Muhammad, if you meet in war these Qurayza with whom you made a covenant, who broke your covenant time after time, and take them, "scatter those behind them", meaning do to them what will scatter those behind them, their like with whom you have a pact and treaty; scattering is driving off, dispersing and separating. The Prophet was commanded, when he had the power over one who broke the covenant, to deal with him so as to frighten those behind him who had a covenant with the Messenger of God, so that they would not dare do what these dared in breaking the covenant. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "meet". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 22}

Report 16212, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abd Allah ibn Salih, Mu'awiya ibn Salih and Ali: "scatter those behind them" means make an example of them for those after them; report 16213 from him, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father and his uncle: make an example of them for those behind them. Report 16214, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: admonish by them others of mankind. Report 16215, from al-Suddi, through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal and Asbat: make an example of them for those after them among the enemy, that they may beware of breaking their pledge lest you do the same to them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 23}

Report 16216, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr, Ma'mar and Ayyub: warn those behind them by them. Report 16217, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj, Ibn Jurayj and Ata al-Khurasani: make an example of them for those after them; Ibn Jurayj added from Abd Allah ibn Kathir: for those behind them. Report 16218, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: make an example of them for those behind them, that they may understand. Report 16219, from al-Dahhak ibn Muzahim, through al-Husayn ibn al-Faraj, Abu Mu'adh and Ubayd ibn Sulayman: make an example for those after them. Report 16220, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: frighten them by what you do to these; and he recited the verse of al-Anfal about "others besides them whom you do not know, God knows them" (8:60). The editor notes that report 16218 is in Ibn Hisham's Sira and traces the earlier reports from it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 24}

On "that they may take heed" he says: so that they take warning from what you did to these and beware of breaking the pact between you and them, fearing that what befell these when they broke it will befall them from you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 24}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
