## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the same passage: "And We have certainly taught those who have been brought forth among you, and We have certainly taught those who came later." The scholars of interpretation have the following opinions: from Ikrimah, those who have been brought forth from creation and those who have not yet been created; and in a narration from him, they are all the creations of God, who knows who among them was created until today and who is his Creator after today; from Mujahid who died and who remains; from Qatadah who passed and those who remained among the loins of men. He narrates the story from Awn ibn Abdullah ibn Utbah that he interpreted it among the rows of men and women in prayer, as Muhammad ibn Ka'b said: those who bring forth the dead, the slain, and those who are later to be joined by them.
