## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 169 to 171 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "except an old man among the two who are old," meaning among the rest, because of the long years that passed on her, and she became old, for she was destroyed among the people of Lot because she used to guide her people to the guests. It has been said: It is said to be of the old ones because she did not perish with her people in their village, and that she was only struck by the stone after leaving their village with Lot and his two daughters, so she was among the old after her people. Then God destroyed her by raining stones on the remnants of Lot's people, and we have previously explained this with evidence that does not require her return.
