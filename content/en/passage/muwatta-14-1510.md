## Summary
A saying of Malik: I think this applies to consummation: if he enters upon her in her house and she says he has consummated her, and he says he has not, she is believed; if she enters upon him in his house, and he says he has not consummated her and she says he has, he is believed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.34}
