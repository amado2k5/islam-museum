## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "No soul knows what was hidden for them of the delight of the eyes as a recompense for what they used to do": No soul knows what Allah has concealed for those whose attributes are described in these two verses, from what their eyes will acknowledge in His gardens on the Day of Resurrection "as a reward for what they used to do," meaning: a reward for their deeds which they used to do in this world. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Sufyan.
