## Summary
The first report in the chapter on one who catches a rak'a of the Friday prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Ibn Shihab, who used to say: whoever catches one rak'a of the Friday prayer should add another rak'a to it. Ibn Shihab added that this is the sunna. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.161}
