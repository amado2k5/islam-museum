## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 41–44. God commands the believers to remember Him frequently with hearts, tongues, and limbs, and to glorify Him morning and evening, that is, the morning and afternoon prayers according to Al-Tabari. Then He clarifies that He is the One who sends blessings upon them and His angels to bring them out of darkness into light, and He is merciful to the believers, greeting them on the Day they meet Him with peace, and He has prepared for them a generous reward.
