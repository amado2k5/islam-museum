## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 34 to 37 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that Pharaoh said to the nobles around him when he saw Moses' argument: This is a knowledgeable magician, who enchanted his staff until I weaved it into a serpent, intending to drive you out of your land with his magic.
