## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17-18 as a single section. He interprets the words, “So how will you protect yourselves, if you disbelieve, from a Day that will make children white-haired?”: God Almighty says to those who associate partners with Him: How will you fear, O people, a Day that will make children white-haired if you disbelieve in God and do not believe in Him? It is mentioned that this is also the reading of Abdullah ibn Mas’ud. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
