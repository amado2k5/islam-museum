## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He created the heavens and the earth in truth, exalted is He above what they associate with Him": Allah Almighty says, defining His creation as His proof against them in His oneness, and that divinity is only suitable for Him: Your Lord created the heavens and the earth with justice, and He is the Truth alone in their creation; He did not associate partners in their creation and its creation, nor did He have a helper over Him. So how can He have a partner? "Elevated above what they associate with Him," the Exalted is He, says: 'Your Lord, O people, has elevated yourselves from your polytheism and your calling to God besides Him. So He has risen beyond having equals, partners, or supporters, for there is no god except the One who creates and creates by His power the likes of the heavens and the earth, and invents bodies and creates them from nothing. This is not in the power of anyone except Allah, the One, the Subduer, for whom worship is deserved, and divinity is not suitable for anything else.'
