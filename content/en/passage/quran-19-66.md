## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 66 to 67 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And man says, 'When I die, I will surely be brought out alive': 'And man says,' the disbeliever who does not believe in resurrection after death: 'Bring him out alive,' so he will be resurrected after death and after trial and annihilation, as a denial of that. Allah Almighty says: "Or does He not mention man"? What is astonishing about this evil is Allah's ability to revive him after his demise and his creation after his absence in the creation of Himself, that Allah created him before his death, creating him as a proper human being from nothing, and "and he was nothing" before creating him. Thus, he considers it and knows that whoever created him from nothing cannot bring him back to life after his death or create him after his annihilation.
