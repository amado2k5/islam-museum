## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains praising Abraham as his Lord, who granted him two children: Ishmael and Isaac, despite his old age. And "Indeed, my Lord is the Hearer of supplication": the Hearer of my supplication by which I call upon Him, and say: Make this land safe, avoid me and the worship of idols, and other supplications of mine and others are not hidden from him. Nothing is hidden from him from all that a speaker utters. It is narrated in Al-Sa'di's tafsir that he heard an old man narrate Sa'id ibn Jubayr who said: Abraham gave glad tidings after seventeen hundred years.
