## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 27 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “then they called out to one another at daybreak”: These people, the owners of the garden, called out to one another. He says: They called out to one another “at daybreak,” meaning after morning, “Go early to your field,” that is, the crop, “if you are going to harvest it,” meaning if you are going to reap your crop. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and others.
