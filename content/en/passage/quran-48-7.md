## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "To Allah belong the hosts of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is Exalted in Might, Wise": He, the Exalted, says: "And to Allah belong the hosts of the heavens and the earth, supporters against His enemies. If He commands them to destroy them, He will destroy them, and they hasten to that by obeying Him." "And Allah is ever Exalted in Might, Wise." Allah says: "And Allah is always the possessor of might; no overpower can overpower Him, nor can He withhold from Him what He intended, due to the greatness of His authority and power, and He is Wise in managing His creation."
