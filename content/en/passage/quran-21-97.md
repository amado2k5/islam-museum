## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And the true promise drew near, and behold, it was fixed with the eyes of those who disbelieved. Woe to us! We were heedless of this; rather, we were wrongdoers." Until, when Gog and Magog were opened, the true promise drew near, and that is God's promise that He would resurrect them from their graves for recompense, recompense, and punishment, and it is undoubtedly true, as He, Glorified and Exalted, said. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
