**Commentary on verse 15 (pp. 40-41)**

He then explains "When Our clear verses are recited to them, those who do not hope to meet Us say: bring a Qur'an other than this, or change it. Say: it is not for me to change it of my own accord; I follow only what is revealed to me; I fear, if I disobey my Lord, the punishment of a great day". When the verses of God's book that We sent down to you, Muhammad, are recited to these polytheists, clear and plain, pointing to the truth, those who do not fear Our punishment, do not believe in the return to Us and do not credit resurrection say to you: bring a Qur'an other than this, or alter it; say to them, Muhammad, it is not for me to change it from myself, that is from my own side. The change they asked for, it is reported, was that a verse of warning be turned into a verse of promise and a verse of promise into warning, the forbidden made lawful and the lawful forbidden; God told His prophet to tell them that this was not his to do but belongs to Him whose judgement is not turned back and whose decree is not reviewed, and that he is only a messenger who conveys and one commanded who follows. The editor refers to the glosses on reciting, clear proofs, hoping, changing and "of my own accord". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 40}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
