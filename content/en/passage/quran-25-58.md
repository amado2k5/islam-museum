## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And rely upon the Ever-Living who never dies, glorify His praise, and suffice Him with the sins of His servants, Acquainted": And rely, O Muhammad, on Him who has eternal life without death. So trust in Him in the affairs of your Lord, entrust to Him, submit to Him, and be patient with what He has afflicted you with. His saying: "And glorify Him with His praise" means: And worship Him in gratitude from You for what He has bestowed upon you. His saying: "And sufficient is He for the sins of His servants, as an Aware," meaning: It is sufficient for You by the Ever-Living, who never dies aware of the sins of His creation, for none of them is hidden from Him, and He has secured them all against them until He recompenses them with them on the Day of Resurrection.
