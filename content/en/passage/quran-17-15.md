## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever is guided, only for [his own benefit] is guided; and whoever goes astray - he only goes astray against it. And no bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another. And We never punish until We send a messenger": Whoever is upright on the path of truth and follows it, and that is the religion of Allah with which His Prophet Muhammad, was sent. "He is guided only for his own benefit." He means: His adherence to uprightness and faith in Allah and His Messenger will not benefit anyone other than himself. "And whoever goes astray with purpose, takes a path without guidance, and disbelieves in Allah, Muhammad, and what he has brought from Allah of truth." No one harms his misguidance and his path except himself, because by doing so he incurs God's wrath and painful punishment. What he meant by His saying "for he is only misguided against her" means that he gains the sin of his misguidance on her and not on anyone else. And His saying: "And no bearer bearer bears the burden of another" means: And no bearer bears the burden of another other than sins. He said: "A bearer of another burden" because its meaning: no soul bears the burden of another soul. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
