## Summary
Verse 8 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 8 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says: these deniers of my signs who hold rivals and gods equal to me say to you, Muhammad, even if you called them to my oneness and the acknowledgement of my lordship, brought them the signs and lessons you brought and argued against them in a way that cut off their excuse: why has no angel been sent down to you from heaven in his own form to confirm what you brought us and bear witness to the truth of your claim that God sent you to us! As in [[passage/quran-25-7|25:7]], where he reports the polytheists saying this of his prophet. "Had we sent down an angel the matter would have been decided, and they would not be respited" means: had we sent an angel as they asked, and they then disbelieved and did not believe in me and my messenger, punishment would have come to them at once, not later, and they would not have been given a respite, put off from the penalty so that they might repent, as I did with the peoples before them who asked for signs and then disbelieved after they came, hastening the vengeance and leaving no respite. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 267}

Report 13078, from al-Suddi: the punishment would have come to them. 13079, from Qatada: had we sent them an angel and they still not believed, they would not have been respited. 13080, from Mujahid: "why is no angel sent down to him" in his form, "the matter would have been decided": the Hour would have come. 13081, from Ikrima: the Hour would have come. 13082, from Qatada: had God sent down an angel and they not believed, he would have hastened the punishment for them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 267}

Others said otherwise: 13083, from Ibn Abbas: had an angel come to them in his own form they would have died, with not a moment's delay. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 268}

The editor refers for "why not" to volumes 10 and 2 and to p. 343 further on, and for "decided" and "respite" to earlier volumes 9, 4, 2, 6 and 3.

Verse 8 closes here at p. 268, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-9|6:9]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
