## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9-10 as a single section. He interprets the words, “And we used to sit there in places to listen, but whoever listens now will find a flaming meteor lying in wait for him”: God Almighty says: We, the jinn, used to sit in places in the heavens to listen to what was happening and what was going on there. “But whoever listens now” from among us “will find a flaming meteor lying in wait for him,” meaning: a fiery meteor lying in wait for him. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and al-Kalbi.
