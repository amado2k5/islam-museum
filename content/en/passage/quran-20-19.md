## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Cast it away, O Moses': Allah said to Moses: 'Cast your staff that is in your right hand, O Moses.' Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: 'So Moses threw it.' Then Allah made it a serpent that would scurry. Before that, it was dry wood and a staff on which Moses leaned and struck his sheep. It became a snake by Allah's command, as reported by Ikrimah, from Ibn Abbas who said: When it was said to Moses: 'Cast it, O Moses,' he threw it, 'and behold, it was a serpent running,' and before that it was not a snake, he said: It passed by a tree and ate it, and passed by a rock and swallowed it. Moses heard the rock crash into its hollow and said: 'So turn to a retreat.' We called out, 'O Moses, take it,' but he did not take it. Then the second call was made: 'Take it and do not be afraid.' But he did not take it. In the third time, it was said to him: 'You are among the safe,' so he took it. And from Al-Suddi, he said: He said to him, meaning to Moses, his Lord: "Throw it away, O Moses," meaning he also transmits in the Maqta' statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
