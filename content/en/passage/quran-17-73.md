## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if they were about to tempt you about that which We revealed to you, you would invent against us something else, and behold, they would take you as a close friend": The interpreters differed regarding the trial that the polytheists nearly tempted the Messenger of Allah with from what Allah revealed to him to others. Some said: It is familiarity with the gods, because the polytheists invited him to that, so the Messenger of Allah understood it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
