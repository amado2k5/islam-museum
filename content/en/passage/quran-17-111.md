## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And say, 'Praise be to Allah, who did not take a son, had no partner in the kingdom, had no protector out of humiliation and magnification,' as follows: 'And say,' O Muhammad, 'Praise be to Allah who did not take a son,' so he would be raised with usury, because the Lord of lords should not have a son and 'He had no partner in the kingdom,' so he would be helpless, in need of help from others, weak, and not a god who would be in need of a helper as he tried and who was not alone in kingship and authority. He will have a guardian of humiliation," he says: He had no ally who swore to him because of the humiliation he endured, because whoever needed to support others is humiliated and humiliated, and whoever is humiliated and humiliated does not need a helper to be obeyed "and magnify Him with magnification," meaning: "And magnify your Lord, O Muhammad, by what We have commanded you to honor Him in word and deed, and obey Him in what He commands and forbids." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
