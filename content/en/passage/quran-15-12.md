## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 12 and 13 together: "Thus do We conduct it in the hearts of the criminals," meaning just as Allah practiced disbelief in the hearts of the early Muslims by mocking the messengers, so He treats the hearts of the polytheists of your people, O Muhammad, who committed disbelief; and the 'ha' in "Nasaluka" refers to mockery and denial — according to Ibn Jurayj as denial, and from Qatadah when they lie, Allah made their hearts not to believe, and from Al-Hasan, and in another narration from him: 'deeds they will do but did not,' and from Sufyan we attribute it, and from Ibn Zayd: 'They misled and were prevented by faith.' He clarifies the language of the wording: 'He followed it as a path, and I followed him as a path,' citing the two verses of 'Adi ibn Zayd and another.
