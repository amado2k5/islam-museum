## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar hastened for three rounds from the Black Stone to the Black Stone, and walked four rounds. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1283) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.490}
