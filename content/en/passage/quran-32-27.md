## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have they not seen that We drive water to the land of the Jaraz and bring forth with it crops from which their livestock and themselves will eat? Will they not see?" Or have these deniers not seen the resurrection after death and the spread after annihilation? By our power, We direct the water to the dry, thick land where there is no vegetation. Its origin is from their saying: 'A she-camel of Jaraz' means if she eats everything, and likewise the land of Jaraz: on whose back nothing remains except that it spoils it, similar to the she-camel eating all it finds. Among them is their saying to the eating person: Grouse, as the Rajz said. Eat Grouse and Hurt...... From this, it is said of the sword if it leaves nothing except its cut: a sword of a raz. It has four languages: the land of a jarz, jarz, jarz, and jarz, and the word 'conquest' for Banu Tamim in what I have reached. In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Dhahhak, and Ibn Zayd.
