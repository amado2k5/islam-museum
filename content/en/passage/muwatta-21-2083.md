## Summary
Malik says: whoever gives a share of a jointly owned house or land as a gift, and the recipient gives a return gift in cash or goods, the partners may take it by pre-emption if they wish, paying the recipient the value of the return, in dinars or dirhams. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.253}
