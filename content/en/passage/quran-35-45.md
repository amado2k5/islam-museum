## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if Allah were to take people to account for what they have earned, He would not leave behind them of the habit they have left behind, but He delays them for a specified term. But when their term comes, then indeed, Allah is ever Seeing of His servants": Even if Allah were to hold people accountable. He says: Even if Allah punishes people and recompenses them for the sins and transgressions they have committed, and they commit sins, He would not leave any creature on their backs to swarm them. "But He delays them for a specified term." He means: but He delays their punishment and accountability for what they have earned until a known term with Him, a limited time they do not fall short of it, and do not exceed it when they reach it. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
