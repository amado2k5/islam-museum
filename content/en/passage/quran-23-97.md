## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 96 to 98 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And say, my Lord, I seek refuge in You from the hamzats of the devils": And say, O Muhammad, my Lord, I seek refuge in You from the strangling of the devils and their hamzas. The hamza is the wink, and from this it is said that hamza in speech is hamzah, and hamzats are the plural of hamza. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
