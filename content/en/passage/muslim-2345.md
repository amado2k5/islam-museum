## Summary
A report of al-Sa'ib ibn Yazid in the chapter on the affirmation of the seal of prophethood, its description and its place on his body, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim. The boy saw the seal between the shoulders like the knob of a bridal tent, in agreement with al-Bukhari's report. {cite:sahih-muslim}
