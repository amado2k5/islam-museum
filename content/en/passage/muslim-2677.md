## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on God's names and the merit of whoever counts them, in the Book of Remembrance of Sahih Muslim: ninety-nine names, and whoever counts them enters Paradise. He is one and loves the odd. {cite:sahih-muslim}
