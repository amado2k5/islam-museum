## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abu Bakr and Umar used to do this, that is, pray the festival before the sermon, in the chapter on ordering the prayer before the sermon at the two festivals. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.251}
