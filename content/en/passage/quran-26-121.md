## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 121 to 122 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed in that is a sign, and most of them were believers": Indeed, in what We did, O Muhammad, to Noah and those with him among the believers in the charged ark, when We sent down Our power and power upon His people who denied Him, is a sign for you and your people, the believers among them and the deniers, that our Sunnah is to save our messengers and their followers, when Our wrath descends upon those who deny them among their people, and the destruction of those who deny Allah. Likewise, my Sunnah is upon you and your people. "And most of them were not believers," he says: "And most of your people were not among those who believed you in the decree of Allah that they would not believe." "And indeed, your Lord is indeed exalted in mighty" in taking revenge on those who disbelieved in Him and disobeyed His command, the "Most Merciful" to repent among them, to punish him after his repentance.
