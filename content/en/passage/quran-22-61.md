## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because Allah enters the night into the day and the day into the night, and that Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing": He means that He mentions Him by saying: "That is the victory He granted over whoever oppresses against the aggressor, for I am capable of whatever He wills." Among His power is that God "brings the night into the day," meaning: what is diminished in the hours of the night enters the hours of the day, and what is lacking from this adds to that. "And the day enters into the night," and what is diminished in the hours of the day is included in the hours of the night. What decreases in the length of this one increases the length of the other. By the ability to do this, he grants victory to Muhammad and his companions over those who transgressed against them and expelled them from their homes and wealth.
