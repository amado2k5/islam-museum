## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 108 to 109 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Those upon whose hearts, hearing, and sight Allah has sealed, and those are the heedless": These polytheists whose description has been described to you in these verses, O people, are the people whose hearts Allah has sealed with His seal, so they neither believe nor are guided, whose hearing is deaf so they do not hear Allah's call to guidance, and their eyes are blind so they do not see Allah's proofs with reliable and instructive insight. "And those are the heedless," he says: And those in whom Allah has placed these actions are the ones who are heedless of what Allah has prepared for their likes among the disbelievers and what is intended for them.
