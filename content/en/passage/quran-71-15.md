## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 18 as a single section. He explains the statement, “Have you not seen how God created seven heavens in layers?”: God Almighty, may He be exalted, informs us of what Noah said to his people who were polytheists, arguing with them using God’s proofs of His Oneness: “Have you not seen,” O people, so that you may consider, “how God created seven heavens in layers,” one above the other? The word “layers” (ṭibāq) is a verbal noun derived from the verb “to be layered” (ṭabāqa). What is meant by this is: how God created seven heavens, one above the other, layered. And His statement, “And made the moon a light therein,” means: “And made the moon a light in the seven heavens,” and “And made the sun” therein “a lamp.” The passage quotes statements from Qatada.
