## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 55 to 60 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "This and indeed, for the transgressors is an evil return": He means mentioning Him by saying: "this" is what was described for these righteous ones. Then the Exalted and Mighty resumed the news about those who disbelieved in Him who transgressed against Him and transgressed, saying: "And indeed, the transgressors" are those who rebelled against their Lord, disobeying His command despite His kindness to them, "for an evil return," meaning an evil return and a destiny they will reach in the Hereafter after leaving this world, as in Al-Suddi: "And indeed, the tyrants have an evil return." He said: an evil return. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
