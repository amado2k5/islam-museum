## Summary
A report that Malik says reached him: Said ibn al-Musayyab, Salim ibn Abdallah and Sulayman ibn Yasar were asked about marriage by the one in the state of ihram, and they said: the one in ihram does not marry, nor is he married to. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (1180). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.469}
