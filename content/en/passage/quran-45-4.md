## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And in your creation and what He sends out of habit are signs for a people who are certain": And in God's creation, beware of you, O people, and His creation is what is scattered on the earth of any creature that moves upon it from other than your kind. "Signs for a people who are certain," meaning: proofs and proofs for a people who are certain of the realities of things, acknowledge them, and know their authenticity. Reciters differed in the reading of His saying: "Signs for a people who are certain." In those that followed, the general reciters of Medina and Basra, and some of Kufa's reciters, read it as verses in the nominative case as a preposition, leaving their refutation of His saying: "Signs for the believers." The general reciters of Kufa read it as "Ayat," reducing the accusative interpretation in response to His saying: "Signs for the believers."
