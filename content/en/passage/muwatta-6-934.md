## Summary
Malik transmits from Musa ibn Uqba, from Salim ibn Abdallah, that he heard his father say: this desert of yours, about which you lie against the Messenger of God, the Messenger of God did not enter ihram except from the mosque there, meaning the mosque of Dhu al-Hulayfa. The editor notes that the hadith is in the two Sahihs from Ibn Umar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.447}
