## Summary
Malik transmits from Safwan ibn Sulaym that it reached him that the Prophet said: I and the one who cares for an orphan, whether his own or another's, will be in Paradise like these two, if he is God-fearing; and he pointed with his two fingers, the middle and the one next to the thumb. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.537}
