## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who disbelieve and hinder [others] from the way of Allah and the Sacred Mosque which We have made for mankind are nothing but those who persist in it and those who turn to it with falsehood and injustice, We will make them taste a painful punishment." He means: Those who denied the oneness of Allah, denied His messengers, and denied what came to them from their Lord, "and hinder from the way of Allah." He means: and prevent people from entering the religion of Allah, and from the Sacred Mosque, which Allah has made for all people who believe in it, not specifying some of it above others. 'And the bad one' means: moderate in his obligation, such as honoring the sanctity of the Sacred Mosque, performing his rituals there, and descending in it wherever the one who stays in it wishes, and the one who stays there, and the bad: the one who returns to it from others. The scholars of interpretation differed in interpreting this; some said: Its meaning is: the one who stays there, the resident therein, and the one who is destroyed, in that neither is more entitled to the dwelling therein than the other. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
