## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah has made for you from yourselves mates and made for you from your wives sons and grandchildren, and provided for you from good things. Do you believe in falsehood? And in the favor of Allah, they deny": "And Allah" who "made for you, O people, from yourselves mates," means that He created Adam His wife Eve, "and He made for you from your wives sons and grandchildren," as Qatadah said: "And Allah made for you from yourselves mates," meaning, God created Adam, then created his wife from him, then made for you sons and grandchildren. Interpreters differed regarding what is meant by grandchildren; some said: they are the two sisters, the two sisters of a man over his daughters. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and others.
