## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We placed between them and the towns which We blessed therein visible towns and determined therein travel; they will travel therein by night and by safe days." He says, exalted be He, informing them of His favor which He bestowed upon those people who wronged themselves. And We placed between their land and the villages We blessed, namely the Levant, visible villages. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
