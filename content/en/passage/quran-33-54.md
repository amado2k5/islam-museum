## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "whether you reveal something or conceal it, for Allah is ever Knowing of everything": if you show with your tongues something from watching women, or anything else He has forbidden you or harmed the Messenger of Allah by saying: 'I will marry his wife after his death,' 'or conceal it,' meaning: or conceal it within yourselves, for Allah is All-Knowing of everything. He means: Allah is All-Knowing of all that and in all your affairs and the affairs of others, Knowing and nothing is hidden from Him, and He will reward you for all of that.
