## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And you did not recite before it any book, nor write it with your right hand lest the falsifiers doubt": "And you were not," O Muhammad, "reciting" means reading "before it," meaning before this book which was revealed to you, "from a book, and do not write it with your right hand." It means: You were not writing with your right hand, but you were illiterate. "Then the falsifiers would not have doubted." He means: If you had read the book before it was revealed to you, or written it with your right hand, then to doubt: He says: Then there is doubt because of that in your matter and what you brought them from your Lord of this book that the invalidators recite to them, claiming it is rhyme and soothsaying, and that it is the legends of the ancients. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
