## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And thus We have sent down to you the Book. So those to whom We gave the Scripture believe in it. And among them is the one who believes in it. And none deny Our verses except the disbelievers." He explains: Just as We sent down the scriptures to those before you, O Muhammad, among the messengers. Thus We have sent down to you—this is the Book. So those to whom We gave the Book, before you, from the Children of Israel, "believe in it, and among them is he who believes in it." He says: And among those who are among you today are those who believe in it, like Abdullah ibn Salam, and those who believe in His Messenger from the Children of Israel. And His saying: "And none denies Our signs except the disbelievers," the Exalted means: "And none denies Our proofs and proofs except the one who denies Our blessings upon Him and denies Our oneness and lordship with knowledge of Him out of stubbornness toward us." Also: Qatadah said: "And none deny Our signs except the disbelievers," he said: The denial only occurs after knowledge.
