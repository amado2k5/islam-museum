## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains his saying, “Say, ‘He is the Most Merciful. We have believed in Him, and upon Him we have relied. And you will know who is in clear error’”: Say, O Muhammad: Our Lord is “the Most Merciful. We have believed in Him.” He says: We have believed in Him. “And upon Him we have relied.” He says: And upon Him we have depended in our affairs, and in Him we have trusted in them. “And you will know who is in clear error.” He says: So you will know, O polytheists, who is going astray from the truth, and who is not on a straight path, from among us and from among you, when we come to Him and are all gathered together.
