## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 76 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Did I not tell you that you will not be able to have patience with me?': The scholar said to Moses, 'Did I not tell you that you will not be able to have patience with me?' regarding what you see of my deeds, which you have not been informed about. Moses said to him: 'If I ask you about anything after that,' he means: after this time, 'do not accompany me.' He means: So leave me, so do not be my companion. 'I have reached an excuse from me,' meaning: I have reached an excuse concerning me. Reciters differed in reading this; the majority of the reciters of Medina read it as: "From my side is an excuse" with a fatha on the lam, damma on the dal, and lightening the nun. The majority of the reciters of Kufa and Basra read it with fatha on the lam, damma on the dal, and shadda on the nun. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
