**Commentary on verses 19-20 (p. 556)**

On "have you not seen that God created the heavens and the earth in truth? If He wills He will do away with you and bring a new creation; and that is not hard for God", al-Tabari says, mighty be His mention, to His Prophet Muhammad: have you not seen, Muhammad, with the eye of your heart, so as to know that God brought the heavens and the earth into being in truth, alone in bringing them into being with no supporter or helper; "if He wills He will do away with you and bring a new creation", he says, the One who alone created and brought that into being with no helper or partner, if He wills to do away with you and put an end to you, He will do away with you and put an end to you and bring another creation other than you in your place, renewing their creation; "and that is not hard for God", he says, your being done away with and ended and the bringing into being of another creation in your place is not impossible or hard for God, since He is able to do what He wills. The readers differed in reading "have you not seen that God created". Most of the readers of Medina and Basra and some of the Kufans read "khalaqa", in the verb form, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 556}
