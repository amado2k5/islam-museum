## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah has sent back those who disbelieved in His anger but did not attain any good. And Allah sufficed the believers in fighting, and Allah is Powerful and Exalted in Might": "Allah has sent back those who disbelieved" with Him and His Messenger from Quraysh and Ghatafan, "with their anger," meaning: by their distress and distress, by missing out on what they hoped for of victory, and their disappointment in what they had hoped for in terms of dominance. "They did not attain any good," meaning: they did not harm the Muslims in wealth or captivity. "And Allah sufficed the believers in fighting" with soldiers from the angels and the wind He sent upon them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
