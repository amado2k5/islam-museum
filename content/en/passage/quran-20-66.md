## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 66 to 67 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'Moses, either you throw or we will be the first to throw'": the magicians gathered their scheme, then they came to a row and said to Moses: "O Moses, either you throw or we will be the first to throw." He left this out of the discussion to suffice with the implication of the statement. There is disagreement about the number of magicians who came that day in a row; some said: They were seventy thousand magicians, each with a rope and a staff. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
