## Summary
Yahya narrated to me from Malik, from a man who said: Zayd ibn Thabit came in to me while I was at al-Asawif, having hunted a bird of the kind called al-nahs; he took it from my hand and released it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.468}
