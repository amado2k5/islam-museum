## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told a man asked al-Qasim ibn Muhammad that he often doubted in his prayer and that it troubled him a lot. al-Qasim told him to go on with his prayer, since the whisperings would not leave him until he turned away, still saying he had not completed his prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.156}
