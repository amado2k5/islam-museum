## Summary
Verse 11 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

Then, on "We created you, then fashioned you, then said to the angels: prostrate to Adam; they prostrated, except Iblis, who was not among those who prostrated", the commentators differ. Some said the meaning is: We created you, people, in Adam's back, then fashioned you in women's wombs as a made creature and a pattern formed on Adam's form. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 317}

He gives (14338) and (14339) from Ibn Abbas that "We created you" means Adam and "We fashioned you" his offspring; (14340) and (14341) from al-Rabi' that "We created you" is Adam and "We fashioned you" is in the wombs, in your mothers' bellies; (14342) from al-Suddi that He created Adam and then fashioned the offspring in the wombs; and (14343) from Qatada that God created Adam from clay and then fashioned them in their mothers' bellies, creation after creation: a clot, then a morsel, then bones, then clothed the bones with flesh, then brought it forth as another creation. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 318}

He gives (14344) from Qatada that God created Adam and then fashioned his offspring after him; and (14345) and (14346) from al-Dahhak that "We fashioned you" is his offspring, and the note says Umar ibn Harun al-Balkhi is spoken against and impugned. Others said the meaning is rather: We created you in your fathers' loins, then fashioned you in your mothers' bellies: he gives (14347) and (14348) from Ikrima that He created them in men's loins and fashioned them in women's wombs; and (14349) the same from al-A'mash. Others said the meaning is rather: "We created you" means Adam, "then fashioned you" means in his back, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 319}

and he gives (14350), (14351) and (14352) from Mujahid that "We created you" is Adam and "We fashioned you" is in Adam's back; and (14353) from him, in Adam's back, for what you will come to of reward in the Hereafter. Others said the meaning is: We created you in your mothers' bellies, then fashioned you in them; he gives (14354) from Ma'mar, from one he mentioned, that God created the human in the womb and then fashioned him, splitting his hearing, sight and fingers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 320}

Al-Tabari says the soundest of the views is that of those who said its interpretation is: We created Adam, then We fashioned you by fashioning Adam, as he explained earlier of the Arabs' addressing a man with deeds attributed to him when his forebear is meant, and as He said to the Jews among the believers in the time of the Messenger of God, in [[passage/quran-2-63|2:63]], of taking the covenant and raising the mountain over them, and the like, speech addressed to the living and present while the departed forebears are meant; so it is here, and the meaning is: We created your father Adam, then fashioned him. He says it is the soundest because what follows it is "then We said to the angels: prostrate to Adam", and it is known that God commanded the angels to prostrate to Adam before fashioning his offspring in their mothers' wombs, indeed before creating their mothers. "Thumma" in Arab speech comes only to signal that what follows is cut off from what precedes, as one says "I stood, then I sat", where the sitting comes only after the standing, and so in all speech; had the joining been with "wa", what follows could have come before what precedes, as in "I stood and sat". The editor refers to earlier places. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 321}

Therefore, he says, the words "We created you, then fashioned you" can only be interpreted as he said. If someone thinks that the Arabs sometimes say "thumma" in place of "wa" out of poetic necessity, as one of them said in a line asking Rabi'a who is best, father then mother, meaning father and mother, and that this could be a parallel, that is contrary to what he thinks: the Book of God came down in the most eloquent dialects of the Arabs and none of it may be turned to their anomalous forms when the eloquent, best-known form has an understood sense and a known aspect. Some whose knowledge of Arab speech is weak turned it to a case of what is delayed in wording but advanced in sense and claimed that the meaning is: We created you, then We said to the angels prostrate to Adam, then We fashioned you; but that is not allowed in Arab speech, because they do not bring "thumma" meaning that it precedes what comes before it in the report, though they sometimes advance it when there is an indication that its sense is delayed; but when it is said "Abd Allah stood, then Amr sat", Amr's sitting cannot have been except after Abd Allah's standing, if the report is true, and God's words "We fashioned you, then We said to the angels prostrate" are parallel to this. As for His words to the angels "prostrate to Adam", God says: when We fashioned {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 322}
