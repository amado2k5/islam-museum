## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30-31 as a single unit. He interprets the words, “And you do not will except that Allah wills. Indeed, Allah is ever Knowing and Wise”: “And you do not will” – meaning, you do not choose the path to your Lord, O people – “except that Allah wills” for you, because the matter is His, not yours. And in the reading of Abdullah, as mentioned, it is: “And you do not will except what Allah wills.” And His words, “Indeed, Allah is ever Knowing and Wise,” mean that none of you will deviate from what He has already decreed for you in His knowledge. And His saying: “He admits whom He wills into His mercy” means: Your Lord admits whomever He wills among you into His mercy, and He accepts his repentance until he dies repentant from his error, and He forgives his sins and admits him to His Paradise. “And for the wrongdoers He has prepared a painful punishment” means: Those who wronged themselves and died in their polytheism, He has prepared for them in the Hereafter a painful and agonizing punishment, which is the punishment of Hell.
