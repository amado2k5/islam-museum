## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Until the known day," meaning until the time known to God for the destruction of all His creation, when no homes remain on earth among the children of Adam—which is the appointed time for Iblis whom I am looking at, not until the Day they are resurrected as he asked.
