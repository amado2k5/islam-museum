**Commentary on verse 32 (pp. 303-304)**

On [[passage/quran-11-32|11:32]] al-Tabari says: Noah's people said to Noah, you have disputed with us and multiplied your disputing, so bring us what you promise us of punishment if you are truthful in your promises and in your claim that you are God's messenger, meaning he would be unable to do any of it. Reports 18117 to 18120, from Mujahid: 'you have disputed with us' means you have argued with us; Ibn Jurayj said it was in denial of the punishment and that it is false. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'disputation'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 304}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
