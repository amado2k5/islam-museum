## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 81 to 82 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "but they said as the ancients said": These polytheists did not consider the signs of God and did not reflect on the arguments and evidence against Him to do whatever He wills, but they said as their predecessors among the nations whose messengers before them said liars. "They said, 'When will we die and we will be dust and bones?'" He means: When we die and return to dust after which our bodies have been afflicted and our bones cleansed of our flesh. "Are we to be resurrected?" He means: We will be resurrected from our graves alive as we have appeared before death? This is for something that does not exist.
