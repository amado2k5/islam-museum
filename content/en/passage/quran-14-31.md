## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says that Allah commands His Prophet to tell His servants who believe in Him and believe in what He has brought them: Let them perform the five obligatory prayers within their limits, and spend from what Allah has provided them and granted them of His bounty, both secretly and openly, fulfilling the rights obligated upon them, both openly and secretly. He explains 'before the day comes when there is no sale' as a ransom or compensation for a soul that is liable to Allah's punishment for its sins in this world, so no ransom is paid or abandoned; he called ransom 'substitute' because he took compensation from Mu'taad. And 'no displacement': there is no 'khalil' (khalal) for its owner to pardon those who deserve punishment, but rather justice and equity; 'khalal' is derived from the phrase 'I left so-and-so with khakhala and khalan,' and he cites a verse from Imru' al-Qais.
