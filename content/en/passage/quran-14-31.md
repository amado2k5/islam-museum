## Al-Tabari's commentary

On the report of Ibn Abbas, al-Tabari takes prayer in the verse to mean the five obligatory daily prayers, and spending to mean giving the zakat on wealth. He reads the denial of trade and friendship on that Day as meaning that no ransom will be accepted. Qatada says that friendships not made for God's sake will come to an end.
