## Summary
Malik transmits from Ismail ibn Abi Hakim, that he heard Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz say: among the last things the Messenger of God said was: God fight the Jews and the Christians, who took the graves of their prophets as mosques; there shall not remain two religions in the land of Arabia. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.470}
