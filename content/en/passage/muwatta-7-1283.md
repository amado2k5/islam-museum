## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: the likeness of the one who strives in God's path is like one who fasts, stands in prayer and never slackens from prayer or fasting, until he returns. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.571}
