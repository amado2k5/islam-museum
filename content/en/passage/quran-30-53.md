## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 52 to 53 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that the Prophet does not hear the dead, meaning he cannot understand those polytheists whose hearing Allah sealed and robbed them of their understanding of admonitions. Just as the deaf cannot hear the supplication when they turn away, and likewise, he cannot guide the blind from their misguidance.
