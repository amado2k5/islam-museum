## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse in a single passage that includes [[passage/quran-84-16|84:16]] to [[passage/quran-84-21|84:21]] In summary, the verse is considered an oath by the twilight, and several interpretations of twilight are mentioned: the redness on the horizon towards the west, which is the view of a group of scholars from Iraq; the daytime, which is the view of Mujahid; and it is a name for redness and whiteness, being opposites.
