## Summary
Yahya said: Malik told us, from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih al-Sammah, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that Saad ibn Ubada said to the Messenger: Do you see, if I find a man with my wife, should I give him time until I bring four witnesses? The Messenger said: yes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.281}
