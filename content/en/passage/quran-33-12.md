## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 10–12. Al-Tabari describes the arrival of the confederates from above and below the Muslims, mentions that Abu Sufyan of Quraysh and his companions came from below, that eyes were deviated and hearts reached their throats, and the believers had suspicions about God, that the believers were tested and shaken by a severe earthquake, and that the hypocrites said, 'We have not promised God and His Messenger except delusion.'
