## Summary
A saying of Malik: there is no harm in a man circumambulating a single round after the dawn prayer and after the afternoon prayer, without exceeding one seven, and delaying the two rak'as until the sun rises, as Umar ibn al-Khattab did; and delaying them after the afternoon prayer until the sun sets, and when the sun has set he prays them if he wishes, or delays them until he prays the sunset prayer if he wishes. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1301) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.496}
