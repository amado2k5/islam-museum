**Commentary on verse 43 (pp. 569-572)**

Al-Tabari says on "when God showed them to you in your sleep as few; and had He shown them to you as many you would have lost heart and quarrelled over the matter; but God saved you; He knows what is in the breasts" [[passage/quran-8-43|8:43]]: God, Muhammad, hears what your Companions say and knows what they hide, when He shows you your enemy and theirs in your sleep as few, meaning He shows them to you in your sleep as few and you tell them, until their hearts grew strong and they dared to fight their enemy; and had your Lord shown you your enemy and theirs as many, your Companions would have lost heart, been cowardly and shrunk back, unable to fight the people, and would have quarrelled over it, but God saved them from that by what He showed you in your dream; He knows what the breasts hold, nothing of what hearts hide being concealed from Him. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "hearing", "knowing", "losing heart", "quarrelling" and "what is in the breasts" and explains "shrunk back". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 569} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 570}

Some claimed that "in your sleep" means in your eye with which you sleep, making "sleep" the eye, as if He meant, when God showed them to you in your eye as few; the editor says it is Abu Ubayda in Majaz al-Qur'an. The interpreters spoke as al-Tabari did; reports 16150, 16151 and 16152, from Mujahid: God showed them to him in his sleep as few, and the Prophet told his Companions, which steadied them; report 16153, from Ibn Ishaq: the first thing He showed him of that was a favour from God to them, with which He emboldened them against their enemy and held back from them what was feared for them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 570}

The rest of report 16153: and He held back from them what was feared for them of their weakness, for He knew what was in them. The editor shows the reading of the phrase in the Sira and that Ibn Hisham said the word "feared" replaced a word Ibn Ishaq used and he did not remember. The interpreters differed over "but God saved". Some said its meaning is: God made the believers' affair safe for them until He made them prevail over their enemy, report 16154, from Ibn Abbas. Others said its meaning is rather that God handed over His affair among them, report 16155, from Qatada. Abu Ja'far said: the better of the two views, to my mind, is what Ibn Abbas said, that God saved the people, by what He showed His prophet in his sleep, from loss of heart and quarrelling until their hearts grew strong and they dared to fight their enemy; for "but God saved" follows His words "had He shown them to you as many you would have lost heart and quarrelled over the matter", so what is better reported is that He saved them from what was feared from it had He not shown His prophet the people as few in his sleep. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 571} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 572}

We stop here at the foot of p. 572, with the commentary on 8:43 complete; the commentary on 8:44 begins on this page and is in the 8:44 file. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 572}
