**Commentary on verse 173 (p. 251)**

Al-Tabari says: we bear witness against you, who acknowledge that God is your Lord, lest you say on the Day of Resurrection that we were unaware of this, that is, we did not know it and were heedless of it, or lest you say that our fathers associated before us and we were their offspring after them and followed their way; will You destroy us for the associating of those of our fathers who associated, and our following their way in ignorance of the truth? By "what the falsifiers did" he means what those did who falsified in claiming a god other than God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 251}

The reciters differed over the reading: some Meccans and Basrans read it with the letter ya, meaning "we witnessed lest they say", as a report about the absent; the general run of the reciters of Medina and Kufa read it with ta, as address from the witnesses to those witnessed against. Al-Tabari chooses that they are two readings sound in meaning and agreeing in interpretation though differing in wording, because the Arabs do this in narration, as in the two readings in [[passage/quran-3-187|3:187]], and he has already given parallels at a length that makes repetition unnecessary. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 251}
