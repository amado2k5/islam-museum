## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 57 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Do not think those who disbelieved are miracles on earth, and their abode is the Fire, and what an evil destination": Do not think, O Muhammad, those who disbelieve in God are miracles on earth if He intends to destroy them. "And their refuge" after their destruction is "the Fire, and what an evil destination" to which they will become that refuge. Some used to say: "Do not consider those who disbelieve" with the ya. This is a weak view among the Arabs because "tahasas" requires accusative cases. If one reads "yahasban," it only applies to one accusative, but I think that the one who says the yaa thought he acted in "mu'jimin," and that his second accusative is in "earth," which makes no sense if that is intentional.
