## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "When those who disbelieved made in their hearts the zeal of ignorance, then Allah sent down His tranquility upon His Messenger and upon the believers and made upon them the word of piety, and they were more deserving of it and its people. And Allah is of all things Knowing": This means that He mentioned Him by saying: "When those who disbelieved made in their hearts the zeal of ignorance," when Suhail ibn Amr placed zeal in his hearts, refusing to write in the Book of Prosecution written before the Messenger of Allah and the polytheists: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, and that it should be written in it: Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, and he and his people refrained from entering the Messenger of Allah in that year.
