## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And the intoxication of death came with truth; that is what I used to deviate from": And in His saying: "And the intoxication of death came with truth," there are two interpretations: one: the intoxication of death came, which is its intensity and dominance over a person's understanding, like the intoxication from sleep or drinking with the truth of the Hereafter, which the person clarifies until he firmly establishes and knows it. The second: The intoxication of death came with the reality of death. It was mentioned from Abu Bakr al-Siddiq that he used to recite: 'And the intoxication of truth came with death.' The narration was mentioned accordingly: And from Wasil, from Abu Wa'il, who said: When Abu Bakr was reciting, Aisha said this, as the poet said: If you are suffocated one day and your chest tightens, Abu Bakr said: Do not say that, but as Allah Almighty said: 'And the intoxication of death comes with the truth, that is what you used to deviate from.' It was mentioned that this is also in the recitation of Ibn Mas'ud.
