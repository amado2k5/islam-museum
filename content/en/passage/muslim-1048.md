## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on "if the son of Adam had two valleys, he would seek a third", in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim. It describes a greed that only dust fills, and ends with the acceptance of repentance. {cite:sahih-muslim}
