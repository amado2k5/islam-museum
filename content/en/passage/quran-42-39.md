## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And those who, when oppression strikes them, they will be victorious": And those who, when oppression befalls them, they will be victorious. Then interpreters differed regarding the aggressor whom God praised, who is victorious after oppressing him. Some said: He is the polytheist when he oppresses a Muslim. In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
