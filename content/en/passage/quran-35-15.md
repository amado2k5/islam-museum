## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "O mankind, you are the poor to Allah, and Allah is the Self-sufficient and Praiseworthy": O people, you are in need and poverty to your Lord, so worship Him, and in His pleasure hasten; He will enrich you from your poverty and your needs may succeed with Him. "And Allah is the Self-Sufficient" from your worship of Him, your service, and other things, whether from you or others. "Al-Hamid" means the one praised for His blessings. For every blessing is from you and others, so to Him belongs all praise and thanks in every case.
