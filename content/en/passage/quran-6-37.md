## Summary
Verse 37 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 37 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "they say: why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord? Say: God is able to send down a sign, but most of them do not know" al-Tabari says: these who hold others equal to their Lord and turn from his signs said "why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord?", that is, why has no sign come down on Muhammad from his Lord, as the poet Jarir says in a line whose sense is "why not the armoured warrior"; "ayah" is a mark. They said [[passage/quran-25-7|25:7]], "what is this messenger, who eats food and walks in the markets? Why has no angel been sent down to him to be a warner with him, or a treasure thrown to him, or a garden for him to eat from?" God said to his prophet: say, Muhammad, to those who say this to you, "God is able to send down a sign", that is, a proof of what they want and ask, "but most of them do not know", that is, most of those who say this and ask you for a sign do not know what trial would fall on them in the sign if he sent it down, nor the reason it is left unsent to you; had they known the cause for which I did not send it, they would not say so or ask you for it, but most of them do not know that. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 343}

The editor refers for "why not" to volumes 11, 10 and 2, says the line is Jarir's and was given earlier with its sources, explanation and right attribution, refers for "sign" to the language indexes, and corrects "most of those who say" from the printed text.

Verse 37 closes here at p. 343, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-38|6:38]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
