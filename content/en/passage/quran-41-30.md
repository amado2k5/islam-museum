## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who say, 'Our Lord is Allah,' and then remain steadfast, the angels descend upon them, saying, 'Do not fear and do not grieve, but give good tidings of Paradise which you were promised.'" means: "Indeed, those who say, 'Our Lord is Allah' alone with no partner, and they disassociated themselves from the gods and equals, 'then remained steadfast' in the oneness of Allah, not mixing the oneness of Allah with associating others with Him, and they concluded with obedience to Him in what He commanded and forbade." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Sufyan.
