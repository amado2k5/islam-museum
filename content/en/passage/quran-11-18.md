**Commentary on verse 18 (pp. 282-284)**

On [[passage/quran-11-18|11:18]] al-Tabari says: who is more severely punished than one who made up a lie about God and so lied against Him? 'Those will be presented to their Lord, and the witnesses will say, these are those who lied about their Lord': they will be presented on the Day of Resurrection to their Lord, who will ask them what they used to do in the world. Report 18080, from Ibn Jurayj: the disbeliever and the hypocrite; 'those will be presented to their Lord' and He will ask them about their deeds. His words 'and the witnesses will say' mean the angels and prophets who witnessed them and kept record of what they did, 'ashhad' being the plural of 'shahid' like 'ashab' of 'sahib'; 'these are those who lied about their Lord' means these witnesses will testify in the next life against these who forged against God in the world and say, these are those who lied in the world about their Lord. God says 'surely the curse of God is on the wrongdoers', that is, God's anger on the transgressors who disbelieved in their Lord. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 282}

Reports 18081, 18082, 18083, 18085, 18086 and 18087, from Mujahid, Qatada, Ibn Jurayj and al-A'mash: the witnesses are the angels; report 18084, from Qatada, says creatures or angels, and report 18083 says they testify against the children of Adam to their deeds. Report 18088, from al-Dahhak: it means the prophets and messengers, as in [[passage/quran-16-89|16:89]], and they say, our Lord, we came to them with the truth and they denied it, so we testify against them that they lied against You. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 283}

Report 18089, from Safwan b. Muhriz al-Mazini: while we were at the House with Abd Allah b. Umar, who was circling, a man met him and said, Ibn Umar, what did you hear the Messenger of God say of the private converse? He said I heard the Prophet of God say: the believer draws near to his Lord until He puts His covering on him and has him confess his sins, saying, do you know this? He says, Lord, I know, twice, until when He has taken him as far as God wills He says, I covered them for you in the world and I forgive them for you today, and he is given the record of his good deeds, or his book, in his right hand. As for the disbelievers and hypocrites, they are called before the witnesses, 'these are those who lied about their Lord; surely the curse of God is on the wrongdoers'. Report 18090, from Ibn Umar, from the Prophet, to the same effect; report 18091, from Qatada: we were told that no one will be disgraced that day without his disgrace being plain to every creature. The editor refers to the same report earlier as number 6497. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 284}
