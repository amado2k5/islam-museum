## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "If you call upon them, they will not hear your supplication, and even if they heard, they would not respond to you. And on the Day of Resurrection they will deny your polytheism, and no one will inform you like an Aware," meaning: "If you call upon them, they will not hear your supplication, and if they heard, they would not respond to you." Allah Almighty says: If you call upon these gods you worship besides Allah, they will not hear your supplication, because they are inanimate objects that do not understand what you say. "And if they had heard, they would not have responded to you," meaning: If they had heard your prayer to them and understood that it was your saying, that He had given them a hearing to hear with, they would not have responded to you, because it is not a speaker, and not every listener has a word that can be answered. Allah Almighty says to the polytheists about Him, the gods and idols: So how can you worship besides Allah from this attribute of His, when He is of no benefit to you to Him, nor has the power to harm you, yet you claim to worship the One in whose hand He benefits you and harms you, when He is the One who created you and bestowed favor upon you. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
