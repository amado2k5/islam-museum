## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, that the Messenger of God offered as a gift a camel that had belonged to Abu Jahl ibn Hisham, for a pilgrimage or a lesser pilgrimage. The editor notes that Yahya mentioned 'from Nafi' in the chain, which is a clear error according to Ibn Abd al-Barr, who said Suwayd erred in the chain from al-Zuhri from Anas; and that Abu Musab (1199), Suwayd (522) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.507}
