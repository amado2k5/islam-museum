**Commentary on verse 63 (pp. 123-124)**

On [[passage/quran-10-63|10:63]] he says: those who believed in God and His Messenger and what he brought from God, and who were mindful of God by performing His obligations and avoiding disobedience. "Those who believed" describes the friends, so the sense is: the friends of God, who believed and were mindful, have no fear and do not grieve. If one asks whether it is in the nominative or accusative, it is nominative, because it comes after the predicate of "friends"; the Arabs do so especially after the particle, when the description of the noun it governs comes after the predicate is complete, and they put it in the nominative, saying "your brother is standing, the elegant", as at [[passage/quran-34-48|34:48]] and [[passage/quran-38-64|38:64]]. The grammarians differed over the reason though they all agree that what he says is sound Arabic, and this is not the place to set out the reasons. The editor refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 124}
