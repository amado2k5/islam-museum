**Commentary on verse 194 (p. 321)**

God says to these idol-worshipping polytheists, rebuking them for worshipping idols that neither harm nor benefit them: "those you call upon", polytheists, as gods "besides God" and worship, in association with Him and unbelief in God, "are servants like you", meaning they are owned by your Lord as you are His possessions. If you are truthful that they harm and benefit and deserve your worship for benefiting you, then let them answer your call when you call them; and if they do not answer because they do not hear your call, then be sure that they neither benefit nor harm, for harm and benefit come only from one who, when asked, hears the request of the one asking and gives and bestows, and who, when something is complained of to him, hears, harming whoever deserves punishment and benefiting whoever does not deserve harm. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "answering". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 321}
