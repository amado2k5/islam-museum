## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 70 to 74 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Those who denied the Book and that with which We sent Our messengers—they will come to know": Have you not seen those who dispute about the verses of Allah how they turn away those who denied the Book of Allah, which is this Qur'an, and the second "al-dhayn" is in a diminutive form to respond to the first "al-dhayn" as an adjective: "And by what We sent Our messengers," meaning: "And they also lied, along with their denial of the Book of Allah by which We sent Our messengers, sincerity in worship of Allah, disassociation from those worshipped besides Him of gods and equals, and acknowledgment of resurrection after death for reward and punishment." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
