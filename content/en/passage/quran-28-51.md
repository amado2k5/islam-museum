## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 52 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We have certainly conveyed to them the word that perhaps they will remember": And indeed We have conveyed, O Muhammad, to your people from Quraysh and to the Jews from the Children of Israel the news of the past and the news of what We have done to them of our power, for they denied Our messengers and about what We are doing to those who followed their footsteps, followed in disbelief in God, and denied His messengers like them, so that they may remember, be considered, and take heed. Its origin is: 'Connecting the ropes to each other,' and from this is the poet's saying: 'Say to Banu Marwan, what is the matter with a covenant and a weak rope that still connects and transmits in the segment sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, Sufyan, and Atiyyah.'
