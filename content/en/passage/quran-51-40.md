## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We took him and his soldiers and cast them into the sea while he was blameworthy": Pharaoh and his soldiers seized us angrily and regrettably, "so we cast them into the sea," meaning we cast them into the sea, and drowned them in it, "and he is a blameworthy," meaning: Pharaoh is a blameworthy one, and a single penny is the one who has fulfilled what is blamed for his actions. Qatadah used to say about this: And from Qatadah, His saying: "and it is a blame" means a blame for God's blessing. From Ma'mar, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase "and it is a blame," he said: a blame for the servants of God. He mentioned that this is in Abdullah's recitation: "So We took him and his soldiers and rejected him." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
