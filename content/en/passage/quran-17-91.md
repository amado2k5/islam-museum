## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Or you will have a garden of palm trees and grapes, and the rivers will gush forth therein with a gushing": He mentions his Prophet Muhammad: And He said to you, O Muhammad, your polytheists: We will never believe you until you bring forth a spring from our land, flowing with water or gushing, or you have an orchard, which is Paradise, made of palm trees and grapes, and the rivers will gush forth during it in a blast on our land that we live in, meaning through palm trees and vineyards. By "in it, there will be a burst of fire," meaning in its origins it will explode because of its buildings.
