**Commentary on verse 17 (pp. 577-578)**

On [[passage/quran-12-17|12:17]], 'they said, father, we went racing and left Joseph with our things and the wolf ate him; you will not believe us even if we are truthful', it is said that the sense of 'nastabiq' is we compete in shooting, from racing; report 18841, from al-Suddi, begins here and continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 577}

Report 18841, from al-Suddi: they came to their father in the evening weeping, and when he heard their voices he was alarmed and said, what is the matter, my sons, has something befallen your flock? They said, no; he said, then what of Joseph? They said 'father, we went racing and left Joseph with our things and the wolf ate him'; the old man wept and cried out loudly and said, where is the shirt? They brought him the shirt with false blood on it, and he took it and laid it on his face and wept until his face was stained with the blood of the shirt. As for 'you will not believe us', they mean you will not hold us true in our saying that the wolf ate Joseph, even if we are truthful; report 18842, from al-Suddi: you will not hold us true. If someone asks, how could they say 'even if we are truthful', which is either a report that they are untruthful, which would be their own denial of themselves, or a report from them that their father would not believe them even if they told him truth, when you know that had they told their father the truth he would have believed them? The answer is that neither is the sense; rather it means you will not hold us true even if we were of the truthful who are not doubted, because of your bad opinion of us and your suspicion of us. The editor thinks something has dropped from al-Tabari's words and that he did not finish the verse's commentary as is his custom. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 578}
