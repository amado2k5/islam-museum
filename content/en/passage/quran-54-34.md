## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 35 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, We sent upon them a besieger except the family of Lot; We saved them by magic." And His saying: "Except the family of Lot, We saved them by magic," means: except the family of Lot, who believed in him and followed him in his religion, so We saved them from the punishment with which We punished his people who denied him. And the besieger with whom We besieged them with magic: a blessing from Us: He says: a blessing We bestowed upon Lot and his family, and a honor by which We honored them from Us. And His saying: "Thus do We recompense those who are grateful," it means: Just as We strengthened Lot and his family, and bestowed favor upon them, and saved them from Our punishment by obeying Us, so too do We reward those who are grateful for Our favor upon them, so they obeyed us and concluded with Our command and forbade all of our creation. He used his phrase with magic because it is inexplicable, and if they say: I did this magic without a 'b', they do not perform it.
