## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who settled in the house and believed before them love those who emigrated to them and find in their breasts no need of what they were given and prefer over themselves, even if they were in need. And whoever guards his own greed, then it is those who are the successful": "And those who took the house and faith" means they took Medina as the city of the Messenger and built it as dwellings. "And faith" in God and His Messenger "from before them" means "from the emigrants," "they love those who emigrated to them": They love those who leave their homes and move to them from others, meaning the Ansar love the emigrants. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
