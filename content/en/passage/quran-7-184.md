**Commentary on verse 184 (pp. 289-290)**

Al-Tabari says: have those who denied Our signs not reflected, ponder with their minds and know that Our messenger whom We sent to them has no madness or derangement, and that what he called them to is the sound view, the straight religion and the manifest truth? This verse was revealed, it is said, as in report 15461, from Qatada: it was mentioned to us that the Prophet of God was on al-Safa and called Quraysh, addressing them clan by clan, "sons of so-and-so, sons of so-and-so", and warned them of God's might and God's days, and one of them said this companion of yours is mad, he cried out until morning; so God revealed "have they not reflected that there is no madness in their companion; he is only a plain warner". The editor says he added the bracketed words from the context, that he thinks the right reading is "stood on al-Safa" as in the reports on the verse in al-Shu'ara, [[passage/quran-26-214|26:214]], and explains "tafkhidh" as calling them thigh by thigh, the thigh being a division among the divisions and clans in the order people, tribe, sub-tribe, kindred, belly, thigh. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 289} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 290}
