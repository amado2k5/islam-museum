## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Mujabbar that Salim ibn Abdallah gave a slave-girl to his son and said: do not go near her, for I had wanted her and did not feel at ease with her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.47}
