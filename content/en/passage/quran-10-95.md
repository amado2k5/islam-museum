**Commentary on verse 95 (p. 204)**

On [[passage/quran-10-95|10:95]] al-Tabari says God tells His prophet: do not be, Muhammad, among those who denied the proofs and evidences of God, so that you would be one whose share is cheated and who sold God's mercy and pleasure for His anger and punishment. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'sign' and 'loss'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 204}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
