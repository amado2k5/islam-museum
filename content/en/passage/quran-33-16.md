## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 17 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, fleeing will not benefit you if you flee from death or killing and then you enjoy only a little": "Say," O Muhammad, to those who ask your permission to leave you and say, 'Our homes are shameful': "Fleeing will not benefit you if you flee from death or killing," meaning: because that, or what Allah has decreed, will reach you in every case, whether you dislike it or love it. "And when you do not enjoy life except for a little," he says: And when you flee from death or killing, your fleeing does not increase in your lifespans and lifespans; rather, you enjoy in this world until the time decreed for you, then what is destined for you and upon you will come to you. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-A'mash, Ibn Ishaq, Al-Rabi', and Sufyan.
