## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman from more than one of their scholars, that no one inherited from those killed at the Battle of the Camel, at Siffin and at al-Harra. Then came the day of Qudayd, and no one among them inherited from his companion, except one known to have been killed before his companion. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.24}
