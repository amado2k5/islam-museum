## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So whoever does righteous deeds while he is a believer, there is no denial of his effort. And indeed, We are His recorders": Whoever among those who have become divided in their religion by what Allah has commanded of righteous deeds, obeying Him in His commands and prohibitions, and acknowledging the oneness of Allah, confirming His promise and warning, and disassociating from equals and gods, "there is no denial of his efforts," meaning: Allah thanks his deeds which he has done, obeying them, and believing in them. He will reward him in the Hereafter with the reward He promised the people of His obedience, and He will not deny him or deprive him of His reward for his righteous deeds. "And indeed, We are His recorders," meaning: We record all his righteous deeds and leave nothing of them, so that He may reward him for all that small and large, and whether he is small or large.
