## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "There is no blame upon the blind, nor upon the lame, nor upon the sick, nor upon the sick. Nor is it upon yourselves that you eat from your houses, or the houses of your fathers, or the houses of your mothers, or the houses of your brothers, or the houses of your brothers, or the houses of your brothers, or the houses of your uncles, or the houses of your aunts, or the houses of your maternal uncles, or the houses of your maternal uncles, or whatever you own or your friend. There is no blame upon you for eating all of them, or in dispersion. "You entered houses and greeted yourselves with a greeting from Allah, a blessed and good blessing. Thus Allah makes clear to you the verses that you may understand." Abu Ja'far said: The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of this verse in which it was revealed. Some said: This verse was revealed as a concession for Muslims to eat with the blind, the lame, the sick, and the people of the time from their food, because they had refrained from eating with them from their food, fearing that they had brought with them some of their food that Allah had forbidden them He said: "O you who have believed, do not consume one another's wealth unjustly except as a trade by mutual consent." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
