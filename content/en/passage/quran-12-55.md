**Commentary on verse 55 (pp. 148-150)**

On "he said, put me in charge of the storehouses of the land; I am a knowing guardian", al-Tabari says Joseph said to the king: put me in charge of the storehouses of your land; "khaza'in" is the plural of "khizana", and the definite article entered "al-ard" in place of the possessive construction, as the poet said (the editor says he is al-Nabigha al-Dhubyani). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 148}

Al-Tabari cites a verse in which the poet praises a people for a quality time has given to no one else among men, together with a mind that does not stray, and the editor refers to its earlier place and sources. This is a request from Joseph that the king appoint him over the food of his country, its revenue and the means of running his country, and the king did that for him, as far as al-Tabari was told. Report 19453, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: Pharaoh had many storehouses besides food, and he handed all his authority to him and gave him the judging, so that his command and judgment were carried out. Report 19454, from Shayba al-Dabbi, through Ibn Humayd and Ibrahim ibn al-Mukhtar: over guarding the food. The editor notes that Ibrahim ibn al-Mukhtar al-Tamimi is one whose reports are treated with caution, especially in Muhammad ibn Humayd's transmission from him, and that Shayba ibn Nu'ama al-Dabbi is weak in hadith and is not used as proof, and refers to the sources on their biographies. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 149}

On "I am a knowing guardian" the interpreters differed. Some said: I am guardian of what you entrust to me, knowing of what you have charged me with. Report 19455, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: I am keeper of what you entrust to me, knowing of what you have charged me with; the king said, I have done it. Report 19456, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: guardian of what I have been put over, knowing of its affairs. Report 19457, from Shayba al-Dabbi, through Ibn Humayd and Ibrahim ibn al-Mukhtar: guardian of what you entrust to me, knowing of the years of famine. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 149}

Others said: I am keeper of accounts, knowing of languages (report 19458, from al-Ashja'i, through Ibn Waki' and Amr). Al-Tabari says: the more correct of the two views, in our opinion, is that of those who said the meaning is that I am keeper of what you entrust to me, knowing of what you have charged me with, because it comes right after "put me in charge of the storehouses of the land" and his asking the king to entrust the storehouses to him, so informing him that he has experience and competence in that is a better fit than informing him of his keeping of accounts and knowledge of languages. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "hafiz" and to the language indexes for "alim". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 150}

We stop here at the foot of p. 150, with the commentary on verse 55 complete; the next batch begins at p. 151 with verse 56 (whose text opens at the end of p. 150). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 150}
