## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa that he heard a man ask Urwa ibn al-Zubayr about a man who said to his wife: every woman I marry over you while I live is to me like my mother's back. Urwa said that for this he must free one neck. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.70}
