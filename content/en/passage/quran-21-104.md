## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The Day We fold the heaven, is like folding a scroll for the books, as We began the first creation. We will repeat it as a promise to Us. Indeed, We will do [this]": They will not be saddened by the greatest terror on the Day We fold the sky. So "day" is from the connection of "grief them." Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the record mentioned by God in this context; some said: it is the name of an angel from among the angels. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
