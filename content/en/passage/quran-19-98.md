## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And how many generations did We destroy before them? Do you feel from them any or hear them at all?" He explains: And many of us, O Muhammad, were destroyed before your people from the polytheists of Quraysh, from Qarn, meaning from a group of people who followed my disagreements and committed sins. Do you feel any of them from anyone? He says: Do you feel any of them, O Muhammad? So you see and observe him. "Or do you hear from them a focus?" He says: Or you hear their voice, but they have perished and perished, their homes have been emptied, their homes have been desolate, and they have become a home where only good deeds benefit them. Likewise, your people will reach what those people have reached if they do not hasten repentance before destruction. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Sufyan, and Ata.
