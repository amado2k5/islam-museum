**Commentary on verse 59 (pp. 366-367)**

On [[passage/quran-11-59|11:59]] al-Tabari says: these on whom We brought down Our vengeance and punishment, 'Ad, denied God's signs and proofs and disobeyed His messengers whom He sent to them to call them to His oneness and obedience to His command, 'and followed the command of every obstinate tyrant', meaning every one arrogant against God, swerving from the truth, who neither submits to it nor accepts it. One says 'he was obstinate against the truth' and 'a man is 'anid'; hence a vein that bursts and does not stop is called an obstinate vein, and he cites a rajaz by an unknown speaker about a man too old to bear obstinate camels. Report 18282, from Qatada: 'every obstinate tyrant' is the polytheist. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 367}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
