**Commentary on verse 8 (pp. 562-563)**

On [[passage/quran-12-8|12:8]] al-Tabari says: in Joseph and his brothers there were signs for whoever asks of their affair, when Joseph's brothers said 'Joseph and his brother', by his mother, 'are dearer to our father than we are, though we are a group', meaning a company of number, eleven men. The 'usba of people is ten and more, said up to fifteen, with no singular from its wording, like 'nafar' and 'rahṭ'. 'Our father is in clear error' means our father Jacob is mistaken in what he does in preferring Joseph and his brother by the same mother over us in love, and 'clear' means an error that shows itself as error to whoever considers and looks at it; report 18795, from al-Suddi: they meant Benjamin, and they were ten; 18796, from al-Suddi: in error about our affair; 18797, from Ibn Zayd: the 'usba is the group. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 563}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
