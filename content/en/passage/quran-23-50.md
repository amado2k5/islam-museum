## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50 to 51 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We certainly gave Moses the Scripture that they might be guided": And We gave Moses the Torah, so that his people from the Children of Israel might be guided by it and know what is in it. "And We made the son of Mary and his mother" meaning: And We made the son of Mary and his mother a proof for us against those among them and over our ability to create bodies without origin, just as We created Jesus without a father. Also: Muammar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "And We made the son of Mary and his mother," he said: She bore him without a father, who is his. Therefore, the verse is unified, and it mentioned Mary and her son. And His saying, "And We sheltered them to a hill," means: We united them and made them into a hill, it is said: So-and-so took shelter to such and such, so he shelters him. If he comes to him, as in the example of "I did it," he shelters him.
