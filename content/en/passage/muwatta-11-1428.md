## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam from Sa'd al-Jari, client of Umar ibn al-Khattab, who said: I asked Abdullah ibn Umar about fish that kill one another, or die from cold, and he said there is no harm in them. Sa'd said: I then asked Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As, and he said the same. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.639}
