## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: a man should not pray over the dead unless he is in a state of purity. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shaybani transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.316}
