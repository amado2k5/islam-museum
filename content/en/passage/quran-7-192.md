**Commentary on verse 192 (p. 319)**

Al-Tabari says: do these polytheists associate in the worship of God what creates nothing of God's creation and cannot help them if God wants harm for them or sends down punishment on them, and is not able, if harm is wanted for it, to help itself or ward off a harm from itself? The worshipper worships what he worships to draw a benefit from it or to ward off a harm from himself; their gods, whom they worship and associate in the worship of God, neither benefit nor harm them, indeed they do not draw a benefit to themselves or ward off a harm from themselves, and are further still from benefiting others or warding off harm from them. The Blessed and Exalted makes His creatures marvel at the great error of those who associate another with God in their worship. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 319}
