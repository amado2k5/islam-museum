## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is that when Allah alone is invoked, you disbelieve, and if He associates others with Him, you believe. The judgment belongs to Allah, the Most High, the Great." In this statement, it is left unnecessary to emphasise the apparent meaning of its mention of it, which is: Answer that there is no way to that. This is the punishment for you, O disbelievers, "that when Allah alone is invoked, you disbelieve," so you denied that divinity is exclusive to Him, and you said, "Should I make the gods one God?" "And if He associates others with Him, you will believe," meaning: And if He makes a partner for Allah, give charity to whoever He has made that for Him. "The judgment belongs to Allah, the Most High, the Most Great," meaning: the judgment belongs to Allah, the Most High, over everything, the Great and everything below Him is humbled today.
