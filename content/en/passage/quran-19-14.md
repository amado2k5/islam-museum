## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 15 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He was dutiful to his parents and was not arrogant in disobedience": He was dutiful to his parents, hastening to obey and love them, not disobedient to them, and he was not arrogant in obeying his Lord or his parents, but he was humble and submissive to God and his parents, obeying what He commanded and refraining from what He forbade, not disobeying his Lord or his parents. And the phrase "disobedience" means that he is disobedient, from the saying of the one who says: So-and-so disobeyed his Lord, so he disobeys Him disobediently. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
