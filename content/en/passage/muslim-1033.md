## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Umar in the Book of Zakat of Sahih Muslim: the upper hand that gives is better than the lower hand that asks. In Hakim ibn Hizam's version, "begin with those you support". It is a text on giving and self-restraint. {cite:sahih-muslim}
