## Summary
Malik transmits from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that he used to say: the ghurra is valued at fifty dinars or six hundred dirhams, and the blood-money of a free Muslim woman is five hundred dinars or six thousand dirhams. Malik said: the blood-money for the foetus of a free woman is a tenth of her blood-money, and a tenth is fifty dinars or six hundred dirhams. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.424}
