## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Forebek, We will surely gather them and the devils, then We will gather them around Hell in their knees": "Then we will gather those who say: When we die, we will be brought out alive on the Day of Resurrection from their graves, linked to their friends among the devils, and then We will gather them around Hell in their knees." The phrase is the plural of the restless. Also: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "Then We will bring them kneeling around Hell," meaning: sitting, which is like His saying: "And you will see every nation kneeling," and in the Maqta', he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
