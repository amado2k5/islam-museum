## Summary
A ruling of Malik: he was asked about a sheep that fell and broke, and its owner reached it and slaughtered it. Blood flowed from it and it did not move. Malik said: if he slaughtered it while its soul was still flowing and it was blinking, let him eat it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.632}
