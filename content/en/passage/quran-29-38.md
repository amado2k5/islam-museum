## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And 'Aad and Thamud, and indeed their dwellings have become clear to you. And Satan has made their deeds pleasing to them and they turned away from the way, while they were insightful": And remember, O people, 'Aad and Thamud, and it has become clear to you from their dwellings their ruin and their devotion from them by Our events over them and the arrival of Our dominion over them all. "And Satan has made their deeds pleasing to them," meaning: and good for them is their disbelief in Allah and their denial of His messengers. "He turns them away from the way." He means: He turned them back by adorning them with what He had made pleasing to them of disbelief, from the way of Allah, which is faith in Him. And His messengers, and what they brought to them from their Lord, "and they were insightful," meaning: they were enlightened in their misguidance, admiring it, thinking they were on guidance and correctness, but they were misguided. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
