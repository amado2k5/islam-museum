## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And thus We sent them to question among themselves. Someone among them said, 'How long did you stay?' They said, 'We stayed a day or part of a day.' They said, 'Your Lord knows best what you have stayed behind. So send one of you with this paper of yours to the city and let him see which is purest food, and let him bring you provision from it, and let him be gentle with you, and let no one perceive you.'" Just as We laid these young men in the cave, preserving them from reaching them, and keeping an eye on them to look at them, and We protected their bodies from affliction on The length of time, and their rotting clothes over the days by our power, so We raised them from their sleep and awakened them from their sleep to recognize the greatness of Our authority and the marvelous nature of Our creation, and to increase their insight into their affairs based on their innocence from worshiping the gods and their sincerity to worship God alone without partner, if they realize the length of time upon them while they were in their state when they slept. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ikrimah.
