## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Whoever is in error, let the Most Merciful extend for him a period until, when they see what they are promised—either the punishment or the Hour—they will know who is worse in position and weaker in soldier.": Say, O Muhammad, to those polytheists with their Lord, who say: When Our verses are recited to them, meaning the two groups from us and you are better in status and better in rivalry, whoever among us and you is in misguidance is unjust from the path of truth. A traveler other than the path of guidance, "Let the Most Merciful extend for him a length," meaning: May God prolong his misguidance and dictate it to him. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
