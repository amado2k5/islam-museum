## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and when it is said to them: believe in what God has sent down, they say: we believe in what was sent down to us", al-Tabari says those meant are the Jews of the Children of Israel living around the prophet's place of emigration: told to believe in what God sent down of the Qur'an on Muhammad, they say they believe in what was sent down to them, the Torah. On "and they disbelieve in what is beyond it", he says "wara'ahu" here means "other than" and "after", that is, other than the Torah and the books of God after it, reported from Qatada, Abu al-'Aliya and al-Rabi'. On "and it is the truth confirming what they have", he says the Qur'an is meant, from al-Suddi, and that it is called confirming what they have because God's books confirm one another: the Gospel and the Qur'an enjoin following Muhammad and believing in him just as the Torah of Moses does. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 348} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 349}
