## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who disbelieved in the signs of Allah and His meeting—those have despaired of My mercy, and for them is a painful punishment." And those who disbelieved in Allah's proofs, denied His proofs, and denied His meeting and incoming upon Him on the Day of Judgment, "Those will despair of My mercy." Allah says: Those will despair of My mercy in the Hereafter when they witness the punishment prepared for them, and those will have a painful punishment. If someone says: How can he object to these verses from His saying, 'And if you deny, then nations before you have lied...' up to His saying, 'Indeed in that are signs for people who believe,' and omitting the pronoun of 'So it was not the answer of his people,' which is from the story of Abraham. And His saying, 'Indeed, those you worship besides Allah...' He also quotes sayings from Abraham in the passage.
