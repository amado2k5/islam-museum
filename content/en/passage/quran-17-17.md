## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And how many generations after Noah We destroyed. And sufficient is your Lord for the sins of His servants, Acquainted and Seeing": This is a warning from Allah Almighty mentioned by those who denied His Messenger Muhammad among the polytheists of Quraysh, and threatened them with punishment, and informed them that if they do not desist from what they are upon, they will remain steadfast in denying His Messenger that His wrath will be upon them, and His punishment will be sent down upon them as was sent down to those before them among the nations who followed disbelief in Allah and the denial of His messengers' path.
