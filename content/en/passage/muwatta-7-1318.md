## Summary
Malik says he does not regard barbarian horses and mixed-breed horses as anything but horses, and cites two verses in Surahs al-Nahl and al-Anfal about horses, mules and donkeys; he regards barbarian and mixed-breed horses as horses if the governor permits them. Said ibn al-Musayyab was asked about barbarian horses, whether there is charity on them; he said: is there any charity on horses? {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.588}
