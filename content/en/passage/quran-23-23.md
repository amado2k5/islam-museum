## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We certainly sent Noah to his people, and he said, 'Stand up, worship Allah; you have no deity other than Him. Will you not then fear Allah?'" He said: "And indeed, We sent Noah to his people," calling them to obedience to Us, to our oneness, and to disassociate themselves from every deity except us. "So Noah said to them: 'O my people, worship Allah.' He said: He said to them: Humiliate yourselves, O people, for Allah in obedience. "You have no deity besides Him" means: You have no deity that you may worship besides Him. "Do you not fear Him?" He means: Do you not fear that by worshiping someone other than Him, the punishment will befall you?
