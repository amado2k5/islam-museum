## Summary
The last text in the Book of Seclusion. Ziyad reports from Malik that it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab used to say: whoever attends the night prayer on the Night of Decree has taken his share of it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (891) and Suwayd (452) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.431}
