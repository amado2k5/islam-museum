## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the command to pray the witr: Abdullah ibn Umar would give salam between the two rak'as, or between a rak'a and the next in the witr, so much that he would tell someone to bring something he needed in between. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi transmitted it from Malik's transmitters. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.184}
