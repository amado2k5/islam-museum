## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on wiping over socks: Abdullah ibn Umar urinated in the market, then performed ablution, washing his face and hands and wiping his head. When called to a funeral prayer on entering the mosque, he wiped over his socks and then prayed over the dead. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.78}
