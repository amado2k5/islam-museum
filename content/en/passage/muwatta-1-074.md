## Summary
A report transmitted by Yahya from Malik, in the chapter on wiping the head and the ears: Jabir ibn Abdullah al-Ansari was asked about wiping over the turban, and he said no, until the hair is wiped with water. The editor cites al-Tirmidhi's report from Abu Ubayda ibn Muhammad ibn Ammar ibn Yasir, who asked Jabir about wiping over the socks and he said it is the sunna, nephew; and about wiping over the turban, and he said touch the hair with water. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.74}
