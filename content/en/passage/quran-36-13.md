## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And give them an example of the people of the town when the messengers came to it": And the example of the polytheists of your people is the people of the town, mentioning that it is Antioch, "when the messengers came to it." Scholars differ about these messengers and about who sent them to the people of the town. Some said: They were messengers of Jesus son of Mary, and Jesus who sent them to them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
