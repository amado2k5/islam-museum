## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Aisha, Mother of the Believers, that she said: the Messenger of God used to kiss some of his wives while fasting, and then she laughed. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi (al-Bukhari 1928), al-Jawhari and al-Shafii transmitted it from Malik, and refers to al-Tamhid (22/139). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.393}
