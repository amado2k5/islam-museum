## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, Pharaoh exalted himself in the land and made its people into sects, oppressing a group of them, slaughtering their sons and dishonoring their women. Indeed, he was among the corrupters": Pharaoh was arrogant and arrogant in the land of Egypt, and he elevated and oppressed its people until they acknowledged servitude to him. Also: from Al-Suddi, "Pharaoh exalted himself in the land," he says: He is arrogant in the land. And from Qatadah: "Indeed, Pharaoh exalted himself in the land," meaning he rebelled in the land. And His saying: "and made its people into sects" means by sects: division; he says: and made its people scattered sects. Also: from Qatadah, "and made its people into sects": meaning a group that slaughters a group among them, shames a group, tortures another, and enslaves another. God Almighty said: "He slaughters their sons and shames their women; indeed, he was among the corrupters." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
