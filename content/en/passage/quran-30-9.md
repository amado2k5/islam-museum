## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Did they not travel through the land and see how was the end of those before them? They were stronger than them and raised the earth and built it more than they had built it. And their messengers came to them with clear proofs, but Allah would not wrong them, but they wronged themselves." Or did these deniers of Allah, the heedless of the Hereafter, from Quraysh not travel in the lands they travel in a hurry, and look at the traces of Allah among the nations before them, and what was the outcome of their affairs in Their messengers denied it, for they were stronger than them. "And they stirred up the earth," meaning: they extracted the land, plowed it, and cultivated it more than they had ever built. So Allah destroyed them through their disbelief and denial of their messengers, so they could not refuse, despite the severity of their strength from what Allah punished them, and the land they cultivated did not benefit them. Their messengers came to them with clear proofs of signs, but they denied them, so Allah imposed His wrath upon them. Allah would not wrong them by punishing them for denying His messengers and denying His signs, but they wronged themselves by disobeying their Lord. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
