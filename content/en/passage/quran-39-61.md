## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 61 to 62 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And Allah saves those who fear Him by their disappearance; evil will not touch them, nor will they grieve": And Allah saves those who fear Him from Hell and its punishment by fulfilling His obligations and avoiding His sins in this world, by their salvation: meaning by their success, which is effected by it. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
