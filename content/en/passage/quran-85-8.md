## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6-8 as a single section. He interprets the words, “And they resented them only because they believed in God, the Exalted in Might, the Praiseworthy”: These disbelievers who persecuted the believers with the threat of Hellfire found no fault with the believers, nor did they do what they did, except because they believed in God. He says, “except because they believed in God,” because the meaning is “except because of their belief in God.” Therefore, “believed” is appropriate in this context, as belief was their attribute. “The Exalted in Might” means severe in His vengeance against those who seek His vengeance. “The Praiseworthy” means the One praised for His benevolence towards His creation. In this section, he quotes statements attributed to Qatadah.
