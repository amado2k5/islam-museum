**Commentary on verse 57 (pp. 104-105)**

On [[passage/quran-10-57|10:57]] al-Tabari says God addresses His creation: people, an admonition has come to you from your Lord, a reminder that makes you recall God's punishment and warns you of His threat; the editor refers to the gloss on "admonition". The commentary continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 104}

He then explains "from your Lord": from your Lord, which Muhammad did not invent and no one fabricated, so that you might say we are not safe from its having no soundness; he means by this the Qur'an, which is the admonition from God. "A healing for what is in the breasts" means a remedy for the ignorance in the breasts, by which God heals the ignorance of the ignorant, cures their sickness and guides by it whom He wills; "guidance" is the explanation of what God permits and forbids and a pointer to obeying and disobeying Him; "mercy" is that by which He has mercy on whom He wills, rescuing him from error to guidance and from ruin. He made it a mercy to the believers and not the disbelievers, since for one who disbelieves in it it is blindness, and his repayment in the next life for disbelieving is abiding in the blaze. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 105}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
