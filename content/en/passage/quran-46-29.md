## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And [mention] when We directed to you a group of jinn listening to the Qur'an." When they were present at it, they said, "Listen." But when the matter was decided, they turned to their people as warners: Allah Almighty reminds the disbelievers of Quraysh for their disbelief in what the jinn believed in. "And when We turned to you," O Muhammad, "a group of jinn listening to the Qur'an," it is mentioned that they were sent to the Messenger of Allah regarding the incident of stoning them with shooting fire. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
