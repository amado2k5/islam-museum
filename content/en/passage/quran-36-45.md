## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 45 to 46 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And when it is said to them, 'Beware of what is before you and what is behind you, that you may receive mercy.'" And when it is said to those polytheists of Allah, who denied His Messenger Muhammad: Beware of what has passed before you of Allah's wrath and His likeness against those nations before you that the like may befall you by your polytheism and your denial of His Messenger. "And what is behind you" means: And what comes after your destruction from what you are facing if you perish because of your disbelief upon which you are, "perhaps you will be merciful," meaning: Your Lord may have mercy on you if you warned against that, and you fear Him by repenting from your polytheism and believing in Him, and by obeying Him in what He has made obligatory upon you of His obligatory duties. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
