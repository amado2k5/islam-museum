## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And the heart of Umm Musa became empty, if she were about to reveal it, if We had not bound her heart so that she would be among the believers": The interpreters differed on the meaning that Allah meant that the heart of Umm Musa became empty. Some said: What Allah meant was that the heart of Umm Musa became empty: everything except the mention of her son Musa. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
