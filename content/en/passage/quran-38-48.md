## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 48 to 49 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And remember Ishmael, Elisa, Dhul-Kifl, and all of the righteous": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: "And remember, O Muhammad, Ishmael, Elisa, and Dhul-Kifl, and what they did in obeying Allah. So take refuge in them and follow their path in patience with what you have attained in Allah and in delivering His message." We have previously explained from the accounts of Ishmael, Elisa, and Dhul-Kifl in our previous book that would not need to be repeated here. In Arab speech, 'kifl' means luck and grandeur. And His saying: "This is a reminder," the Exalted says: This Qur'an which We have revealed to you, O Muhammad, is a reminder for you and your people. We have reminded you and them by it.
