## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Or did We send down to them authority so that it speaks about what they used to associate with Him": Or did We send down to those who associate partners with Us gods and idols a book confirming what they say and the reality of what they do? "It speaks about what they used to associate with Him." He means: that book confirms the validity of their polytheism. Rather, the Exalted is He, He means that He did not reveal what they say and do, nor did He send a messenger with it. Rather, it is something they fabricated and fabricated, following their own desires.
