## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Busr: a man said the rules of Islam had become many, so tell me one thing to hold; the Prophet said, "Let your tongue stay moist with the remembrance of God". It is the eighth of the added hadiths and ends the fifty. Ibn Rajab cites verses that praise much remembrance. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
