## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth; He may know what you are on, and the Day they will return to Him and inform them of what they did. And Allah is Knowing of all things": "Indeed, Allah belongs the King" over all the heavens and the earth. He says: It is not appropriate for a slave to disobey his owner's command and disobey him, thus deserving his punishment. He says: So likewise, O people, it is not appropriate for you to oppose your Lord, who is your Owner, so obey Him and obey His command, and do not turn away from His Messenger if you are with him on a comprehensive matter except by His permission. And His saying: "He may know what you are upon" is from your obedience to Him in what He commanded you and forbade you to do, as Ibn Zayd said regarding the saying: "He may know what you are upon" is also your doing. "And the Day they return to Him" He means: And the day those who disobey His command will return. "He informs them" means: He then informs them "about what they have done" in this world, then recompenses them for what they previously did, regarding their opposition to their Lord. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
