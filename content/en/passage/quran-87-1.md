## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse in a single passage that includes [[passage/quran-87-1|87:1]] to [[passage/quran-87-7|87:7]] In summary, the meaning of glorification (tasbih) is explained in several ways: It means exalting the Most High Lord, and it was reported that Ibn Umar, Ali, Ibn Abbas, and Ubayy used to say, upon reciting it, "Glory be to my Lord, the Most High"; it means declaring His name free from being used to refer to idols like al-Lat and al-Uzza; it means declaring Him free from what the polytheists say; it means remembering Him only with reverence; and it means praying with His name invoked.
