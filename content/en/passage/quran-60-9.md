## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Allah only forbids you from those who fought you because of religion and expelled you from your homes and made manifestations in your expulsion from allegiance to them, and those who take allies with them—those are the wrongdoers." He says: "And assist those who expelled you from your homes in your religion, from the disbelievers of Mecca." He says: "And help those who expelled you from your homes to take you as allies." He says: And whoever makes them allies from among you or from others, "then those are the wrongdoers." He means: those are the ones who took a guardianship other than what is permissible for them, placed their authority in a place other than it deserved, and disobeyed God's command in that matter. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
