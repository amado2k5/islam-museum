## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 80 and 81 in one passage: "And indeed, the companions of Al-Hijr denied the messengers." The inhabitants of Al-Hijr—the city of Thamud—denied and made its people their inhabitants because they lived and resided there, just as the people of Paradise made them its companions. It is narrated from Ibn Shihab from Salim from Abdullah ibn Umar that they passed by the Hijr with the Messenger of God and said to them: Do not enter the dwellings of those who wronged themselves except when you weep, beware, lest the same befall you as it befell them. Then he rebuked and hurried to follow it; and it is narrated from Jabir that the Messenger of God said while he was in the Hijr: These are righteous people whom God destroyed except for a man who was in the sanctuary of God, whom God prevented from God's punishment — it was said: Who is it? Abu Raghal said. "And We gave them Our signs, but they turned away from them": I show them His proofs and proofs regarding the reality of what His Messenger sent righteously, so they turned away from His signs, neither heeding them nor taking heed.
