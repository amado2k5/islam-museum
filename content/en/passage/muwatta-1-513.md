## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on asking for rain. Yahya transmits from Malik from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Amr ibn Shu'ayb as a mursal: when the Prophet asked for rain he said, "O God, give rain to Your servants and Your animals, spread Your mercy, and revive Your dead land". The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that Malik transmitted it as a mursal, followed by al-Mu'tamir ibn Sulayman and Abd al-Aziz ibn Muslim al-Qasmali, while a group from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Amr ibn Shu'ayb from his father from his grandfather transmitted it connected, including Hafs ibn Ghiyath, al-Thawri, Abd al-Rahim ibn Sulayman and Salam Abu al-Mundhir. Abu Dawud (1176) gave al-Thawri's connected version and al-Bayhaqi Abd al-Rahim's, and Abu Hatim al-Razi preferred the mursal report, which the editor agrees with. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.265}
