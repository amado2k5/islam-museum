## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Pharaoh called out to his people, saying, 'O people, do I not have the kingdom of Egypt, and these rivers flow beneath me? Will you not see?": "And Pharaoh called out to his people" from the Copts, saying, "O my people, do I not have the kingdom of Egypt while these rivers flow beneath me? Will you not see?" He means: "beneath me," meaning "from beneath me" means those before me in the gardens. Also: And from Qatadah, "And these rivers flow beneath me," he said: They had gardens and rivers of water. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
