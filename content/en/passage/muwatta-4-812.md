## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on what one does when he returns from a journey or intends to, in Ramadan. It reached Malik that Umar ibn al-Khattab, when he was on a journey in Ramadan and knew that he would enter Medina early in the day, entered while fasting. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.398}
