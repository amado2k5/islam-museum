## Al-Tabari's commentary

Explains His saying: "And [mention] when Jesus, son of Mary, said, 'O Children of Israel, indeed I am the Messenger of Allah to you, confirming what was before me of the Torah and giving glad tidings of a messenger to come after me, whose name is Ahmad.' But when he brought them clear proofs, they said, 'This is clear magic.'" And also remember, O Muhammad, when Jesus, son of Mary, said to his people from the Children of Israel, "O Children of Israel, indeed I am the Messenger of Allah to you, confirming what was before me of the Torah" that was revealed to Moses, "and a bringer of good tidings." "A messenger will come after me named Ahmad."
