**Commentary on verse 103 (pp. 476-478)**

On [[passage/quran-11-103|11:103]] al-Tabari says: in Our seizing those We seized of the people of the towns whose account We have told you, people, 'is a sign', a lesson and admonition for whoever of His servants fears God's punishment and torment in the next life, a proof against him for his Lord and a restraint to hold him from disobeying God and going against His command and prohibition. It was also said that the sense is that in it is a lesson for whoever fears the punishment of the next life, in that God will fulfil His promise to him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 476}

Report 18561, from Ibn Zayd: We shall fulfil to them what We promised them in the next life, as We fulfilled to the prophets that We would help them. As for 'that is a day for which people will be gathered', that day, the Day of Resurrection, God gathers people for it from their graves, assembling them for requital, reward and punishment; 'and that is a day witnessed' means creation witnesses it, none being absent, and God then takes vengeance on whoever disobeyed Him, went against His command and denied His messengers. Reports 18562, from Mujahid, and 18563, from Ikrima: the Day of Resurrection. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 477}

Reports 18564 and 18565, from Ibn Abbas: the witness is Muhammad and the witnessed is the Day of Resurrection, then he recited the verse; 18566, from al-Dahhak: that is the Day of Resurrection, when all creatures gather and the dwellers of heaven and earth witness it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 478}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
