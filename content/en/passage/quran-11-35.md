**Commentary on verse 35 (pp. 305-306)**

On [[passage/quran-11-35|11:35]] al-Tabari says: do these polytheists of your people, Muhammad, say that Muhammad invented this Qur'an and this report about Noah? Say to them: if I invented it, guessing and forging it, then upon me is my crime, that is, my sin in what I invented; the commentary on the verse continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 305}

'And I am free of what you commit' means: upon me is my sin in what I invented against my Lord, apart from you; you are not taken for my sin or my crime nor am I taken for your sin, and I am free of what you sin and incur against your Lord by your invention against Him. One says 'ajramtu ijraman' and 'jaramtu ajrimu jarman', and he cites a line by the thief al-Hayradan al-Sa'di: outcast of his clan, hostage of a sin, for what my hand earned and my tongue wrought. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 306}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
