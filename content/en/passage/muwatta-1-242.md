## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim from his father from Aisha, the Prophet's wife, in the chapter on the tashahhud in prayer: she recited a formula beginning with "the greetings, the good blessings and the prayers belong to God", then the two testimonies, then peace upon the Prophet and upon the righteous servants of God, then "peace upon you". The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.145}
