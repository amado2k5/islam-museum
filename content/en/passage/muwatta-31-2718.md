## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that a man in the time of the Messenger of God was wounded, and the wound was blocked by blood. He called two men of Banu Anmar, who looked at him. They claimed the Messenger of God said to them: which of you is the better physician? They said: is there any good in medicine, Messenger of God? Zayd claimed the Messenger of God said: God sent down the medicine that sent down the diseases. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the report is broken in the transmission of the majority; Asim ibn Umar transmitted its first half connected from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih from Abu Hurayra. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.532}
