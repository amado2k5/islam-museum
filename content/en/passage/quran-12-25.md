## Summary
Verse 25 of [[passage/quran-12|Surat Yusuf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum

**Commentary on verse 25 (pp. 50-52)**

Al-Tabari says: Joseph and the Aziz's wife raced to the door of the room, Joseph fleeing from committing the indecency, restrained by the proof of his Lord, and the woman seeking him to get from him what she had solicited; she overtook him, caught hold of his shirt and pulled him toward her, preventing him from going out of the door, and tore it from behind, that is, split it from the back and not the front, because Joseph was the one fleeing and she the one pursuing. The editor refers to the explanations of "racing" and "back". Report 19091, from Qatada: he and the woman raced to the door and she tore his shirt from behind; report 19092, from Ibn Ishaq: when he saw the proof of his Lord he slipped away from her fleeing, she followed, took his shirt from behind and tore it on him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 51}

On "and they found her master at the door" he says: they met her master, the woman's husband, at the door. Report 19093, from Mujahid, through al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz and al-Thawri from a man: her master is her husband, and "lada al-bab" is at the door. Report 19094, from Zayd ibn Thabit: the "master" is the husband. Report 19095, from Qatada: at the door. The editor refers to the explanations of "found", "master" and "lada". Report 19096, from al-Suddi: he was sitting at the door with her cousin; when she saw him she said, what is the reward for one who intended evil toward your wife? He solicited me and I pushed him off and tore his shirt; Joseph said: rather she solicited me, I fled from her and she overtook me and tore my shirt; her cousin said: look at the shirt, if it is torn from the front she has spoken the truth and he is a liar, and if it is torn from the back she has lied and he is truthful; the shirt was brought, he found it torn from the back and said what he said in [[passage/quran-12-28|12:28]] and [[passage/quran-12-29|12:29]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 51}

Report 19097, from Ibn Ishaq: Itfir stood at the door of the room, and she said in fear of him, what is the reward of one who intended evil toward your wife, and at once put the blame on him, afraid her master would suspect her of the foul deed; he said, and the report agreed with him: she solicited me. On "she said, what is the reward of one who intended evil toward your wife" he says: the Aziz's wife said to her husband when they met him at the door, fearing he would accuse her of immorality: what is the recompense of a man who sought adultery with your wife but that he be imprisoned or a painful punishment, that is, one that hurts. He said "that he be imprisoned or a painful punishment" because "an yusjana" has the sense of "but imprisonment", and "punishment" is coordinated with it, for "an" with what it governs stands in the place of a noun. The editor refers to the explanation of "reward". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 52}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
