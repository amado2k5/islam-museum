## Summary
Malik said: the agreed practice among us is that when a doctor performs circumcision and cuts off the glans, he owes blood-money; this is an accidental act that the kin bears. Whatever the doctor errs in or exceeds, if he did not intend it, blood-money is due. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.422}
