## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "I refuse, indeed I fear that a punishment from the Most Merciful will seize him and you will become an ally for Satan": O my father, I know that if you die worshipping Satan, he will be a protector from God's punishment, "and you will be an ally for Satan." He means: You will be his protector instead of Allah, and God will disown you, and you will perish. Fear here means knowledge, just as fear means knowledge, in the saying: "So we feared that He would burden them with tyranny and disbelief."
