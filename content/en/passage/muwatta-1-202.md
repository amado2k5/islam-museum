## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu Nu'aym Wahb ibn Kaysan from Jabir ibn Abdullah, in the chapter on opening the prayer: Jabir taught them the takbir in prayer and told them to say it at each lowering and raising. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya al-Qattan in Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.126} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.127}
