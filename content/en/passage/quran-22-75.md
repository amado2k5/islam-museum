## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah chooses messengers from among the angels and from among the people. Indeed, Allah is Hearing and Seeing": Allah chooses messengers from among the angels like Gabriel and Michael, whom He sends to His prophets, and whoever He wills among His servants and from among the people, like His prophets whom He sent to His servants from among the children of Adam. The meaning of the statement: Allah chooses messengers from among the angels, and also messengers from among the people. It has been said: This verse was revealed only when the polytheists said: The Reminder has been sent down to him from among Us. Allah said to them: That is for Me and in My hand, not for My creation. I choose from among them whom I wish for the message. And His saying: "Indeed, Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing," means: God is All-Hearing of what the polytheists say about Muhammad and what he brings from his Lord. He is All-Seeing of whom He chooses for His message.
