## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 7 as one section. He explains his statement, “Indeed, We sent Noah to his people, [saying], ‘Warn your people before there comes to them a painful punishment’”: “Indeed, We sent Noah,” who is Noah, son of Lamech, “to his people, [saying], ‘Warn your people before there comes to them a painful punishment’” he says: We sent him to them to warn your people, so “that” is in the accusative case according to some of the Arabs, and in the genitive case according to others. The reasons for each of these groups have been explained, and what we consider to be the correct view on this matter has already been discussed in this book, making it unnecessary to repeat it here. In the reading of Abdullah, as mentioned, it reads: “We sent Noah to his people, [saying], ‘Warn your people,’” without the conjunction “that.” This is permissible because “sending” here means “saying.” It is as if it were said: We said to Noah, “Warn your people before a painful punishment comes upon them.” That painful punishment is the flood with which God drowned them. The passage also includes statements attributed to Qatadah.
