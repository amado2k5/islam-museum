## Summary
Verse 4 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 4 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says: there does not come to these unbelievers who hold their idols and gods equal to their Lord "a sign from the signs of their Lord", that is, a proof, mark and indication of the proofs, indications and marks of their Lord to his oneness, the truth of your prophethood, Muhammad, and the truth of what you brought them from me, "but they turned away from it", that is, from the sign, refusing to accept it or to acknowledge what it testified to and showed to be sound, from ignorance of God and being deceived by his forbearance toward them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 262}

The editor refers for "sign" to the language indexes.

Verse 4 closes here at p. 262, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-5|6:5]].
