## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Umar ibn al-Khattab asked the Messenger of God about the kalala, and the Messenger told him that the verse revealed in summer at the end of Surat al-Nisa' suffices him on that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.17}
