## Summary
Verse 98 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 98 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari explains "know that God is severe in punishment and that God is Forgiving, Merciful": know, people, that your Lord, who knows what is in the heavens and on the earth and from whom nothing of the secrets or the open parts of your deeds is hidden, and who counts them against you to repay you for them, is severe in punishing whoever disobeys Him and rebels against Him, and is forgiving of the sins of whoever obeys Him and turns to Him, covering him and leaving off exposing him for them, and merciful to him in not punishing him for his past sins after his turning and repentance from them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 95}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
