## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5-9 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “About what are they asking one another?” as meaning: What are these polytheists from Quraysh asking about, O Muhammad? This was said to him because Quraysh, as has been reported, were arguing and disputing about what the Messenger of God called them to: acknowledging his prophethood, believing in what he brought from God, and believing in the Resurrection. So God said to His Prophet: What are these people asking and arguing about? In this context, “about” and “concerning” have the same meaning. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
