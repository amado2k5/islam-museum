## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "He is the One who shows you His signs and sends down to you provision from the sky, and He does not remember except those who repent": He who shows you, O mankind, His proofs and proofs of His oneness and lordship, "and sends down to you provision from the sky," meaning He sends down to you from your provisions from the sky by sending down rain with which He brings forth your sustenance from the earth and the nourishment of your livestock upon you. "And none remembers except those who repent." He means: He does not remember the proofs of Allah, which He has made as proofs of His oneness, so he considers them, takes heed, takes heed, and knows the truth of what they indicate, except for the one who repents. He says: Except for those who return to His oneness and turn to obey Him, as: regarding Al-Suddi, 'except for those who repent,' he said: those who turn to obedience to Allah.
