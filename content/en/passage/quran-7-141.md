**Commentary on verse 141 (p. 85)**

Al-Tabari addresses the Jews among the Children of Israel who were living around the Messenger's place of emigration: remember, along with this saying you said to Moses after seeing the signs and lessons and after the favours that had gone before from Me to you, and your deed that you did, "when We saved you from Pharaoh's people", who were the ones that followed his path and way in unbelief in God among his people, "who were afflicting you with evil torment", meaning laying on you the ugliest and worst punishment. He has already explained what that torment was. "Killing your sons", the males among their children, "and sparing your women", that is, keeping alive their females, "and in that was a great trial from your Lord", meaning in their afflicting you with evil torment was a test from God for you and a great blessing. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "people", "affliction", "sparing" and "trial", and notes that the printed text has a meaningless word where he put the right one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 85}
