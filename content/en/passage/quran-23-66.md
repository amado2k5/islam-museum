## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 66 to 67 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "My verses used to be recited to you, but you were turning back on your heels": Allah Almighty says to these polytheists from Quraysh: Do not be disturbed today while the wrath and punishment of Allah has descended upon you because of what your hands have earned and which you demanded by your disbelief in the verses of your Lord. "My verses used to be recited to you" means: the verses of the Book of Allah. He says: The verses of My Book were recited to you, but you would deny them and turn away from them when you heard them, out of dislike for hearing them. Likewise, it is said to anyone who returns from where he came: so-and-so retreated on his heel. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
