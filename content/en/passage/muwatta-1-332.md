## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, that Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said he did not care if the dawn prayer were called while he was praying the witr, in the chapter on the witr after dawn. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.185}
