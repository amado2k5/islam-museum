## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 62–66. He asks God whether that is better for a resting place or the tree of Zaqqum, which He has made a trial for the wrongdoers. It is a tree that emerges from the root of Hell as if it were the heads of devils, for they eat from it and fill their bellies with it. Al-Tabari explains the meaning of dwelling.
