## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 76 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "they cannot help them, and they are prepared for them": these gods cannot help them from God if He intends harm for them, nor can they avert harm from them. And His saying: "And they are prepared for them an army," it means: and these polytheists have prepared soldiers for their gods. The interpreters differed on the interpretation of the phrase "prepared" and where their presence is with them. Some said: What is meant by that is: they have an army prepared at the time of reckoning. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
