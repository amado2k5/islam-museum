## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 13 as one section. He explains His saying, “He who fears will remember”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: O Muhammad, if you remind those whom I commanded you to remind, he who fears God and dreads His punishment will remember. “And the most wretched will avoid it”: He says: and he avoids the reminder. “The most wretched” means: the most wretched of the two groups. And in the section he quotes sayings of Qatadah.
