## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He created man from a drop of fluid, and behold, he is a clear adversary": Among his arguments against you are also the people that he created man from a sperm-drop, then created from a humiliating fluid into a wondrous creation, his heart was created in a series of moments after creation in three darknesses, then he brought him out to the light of the world after he was created and breathed into the spirit, nourishing him and sustaining him with sustenance and growth, until when he settled on his calf, he denied the blessing of his Lord, denied his planner, worshiped the One who neither harms nor benefited, and disputed his God, saying, "Who gives life to bones while they are remains?" He forgot the One who created him and made him a sound creation from humiliating water. By 'clarified' means that he clarifies his opposition with his logic and argues with his tongue, so that is his clarification. And by 'man' he means all people, derived from the word 'one,' which means all.
