## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 16 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Whoever thinks that Allah will not support him in this world and the Hereafter, let him stretch out his rope to the sky, then cut it off, and let him see if his plotting will remove what angers": The interpreters differ regarding the meaning of the 'h' in the phrase: "that Allah will not help him." Some said: It meant the Prophet of God. So he interpreted it as some who say that: Whoever among the people thinks that God will not support Muhammad in this world and the Hereafter, let him stretch out a rope, which is the cause, to the sky: meaning the sky of the House, which is its roof, then cut off the cause after being suffocated by it. Let him see whether they remove his suffocation and cut off the cause after the suffocation that angers him. He says: Will they remove what angers him and quote in the passage from Ibn Zayd.
