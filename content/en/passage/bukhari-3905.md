## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar, the chapter on the emigration of the Prophet and his Companions to Medina: the longest hadith on the Meccan period in the Sahih, related by Aisha from Urwa from Ibn Shihab. It covers the beginning of Islam in Abu Bakr's house, Abu Bakr's emigration to Abyssinia and the protection of Ibn al-Dughunna, the mosque in Abu Bakr's yard, the permission to emigrate, the night in the cave, Suraqa, Umm Ma'bad, and the entry into Medina with people saying "the Prophet has come". {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
