**Commentary on verse 130 (pp. 45-46)**

Al-Tabari says: We tried Pharaoh's people and his followers, in their error, "with years" meaning drought year after year and dearth (one says "the people suffered a lean year" when they have drought); "and a shortage of fruits" means We tried them, besides the droughts, with the loss of their fruits and produce except a little; "perhaps they will take heed" as an admonition and reminder, so that they would stop their error and turn to their Lord in repentance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 45}

Report 14976, from Abd Allah (ibn Mas'ud) through Ibn Waki', Yahya ibn Adam, Sharik, Abu Ishaq and Abu Ubayda: the years of hunger. Report 14977, from Mujahid: "years" is calamity, and "shortage of fruits" is less than that; report 14978, from Mujahid, is the same. Report 14979, from Raja' ibn Haywa through al-Qasim ibn Dinar, Ubayd Allah ibn Musa, Shayban and Abu Ishaq: it was when the palm bore only a single fruit. Report 14980, from Ka'b: a time will come to people when the palm bears only one fruit; report 14981, from Raja' ibn Haywa, is the same. The editor gives in a footnote the biography of al-Qasim ibn Dinar (al-Qasim ibn Zakariyya ibn Dinar al-Qurashi, Abu Muhammad al-Tahhan), who was reliable. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 46}

Report 14982, from Qatada through Bishr ibn Mu'adh, Yazid and Sa'id: God seized them with years, with hunger, year after year; the "years" were in their desert country and among the owners of livestock, and the "shortage of fruits" in their towns and villages. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 46}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
