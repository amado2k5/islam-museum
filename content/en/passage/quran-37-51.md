## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 53 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Some of them said, 'Indeed, I had a companion.'" He said: Someone from the people of Paradise, when they approached one another, wondering: "Indeed, I had a companion," the interpreters differed regarding the companion mentioned in this place. Some said: That companion was Satan, and he was the one who used to say to him: "Are you among the believers" about resurrection after death. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
