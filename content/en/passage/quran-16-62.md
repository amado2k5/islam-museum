## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they assign to Allah what they dislike, and their tongues describe falsehood, that they will have the best. Indeed, for them is the Fire, and they are transgressors": and these polytheists attribute to Allah what they dislike for themselves. "And their tongues describe lies," he says: and their tongues say lies and fabricate it, "Indeed, they have good," for this is in the accusative case, because it is a translation of lying. Interpretation of the statement: They attribute to Allah what they dislike for themselves, claiming that they have the good that they hate for themselves. They make daughters for Allah the Exalted, and they claim that the angels are the daughters of Allah. As for the good deeds they set for themselves: the males are among the children, because they used to marry females among their sons and surpass the males among them, saying: We have sons and to Allah the daughters. It is like His saying: "And they make for Allah daughters, and for them whatever they desire." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
