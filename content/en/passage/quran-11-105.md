**Commentary on verse 105 (pp. 478-479)**

On [[passage/quran-11-105|11:105]] al-Tabari says: on the day the Day of Resurrection comes, people, and the Hour is established, 'no soul will speak except by His leave', by its Lord's leave. The readers differ on 'yawma ya'ti': most readers of Medina keep the ya; some of Basra and some Kufans keep it when continuing and drop it when stopping; a group of Kufans drop the ya whether continuing or stopping. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 478}

Al-Tabari says: the correct reading with me is 'yawma ya'ti' without the ya, in continuing and in stopping, following the script of the codex and because it is a known dialect of Hudhayl; he cites a verse, and the editor explains the sense of the word in it. 'Tukallamu' is for 'tatakallamu', one of the two ta's being dropped, the other showing it. As for 'some of them wretched and some happy', it means among the souls which will not speak on the Day of Resurrection except by their Lord's leave are the wretched and the happy; it returns to 'soul', one in form, by naming them all. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 479}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
