## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who believe and do righteous deeds, We will surely expiate from them their misdeeds and We will surely reward them according to the best of what they used to do." This means that those who believed in Allah and His Messenger were firmly established when Allah tested them and tested them, and they did not avert from their religions by the harm of the polytheists to them. "And they did righteous deeds to expiate from them their misdeeds" that had been preceded from them in their polytheism, "And We will surely reward them according to the best of what they used to do." He means: "And We will surely reward them for the good deeds of their deeds in their Islam, the best of what they used to do while they were associating partners with Allah."
