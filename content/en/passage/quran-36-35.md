## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "to eat of its fruit and what their hands have done; will they not be grateful": We established these gardens on this earth so that My servants may eat of its fruit, and what their hands have done, meaning: to eat of the fruit of the gardens We have created for them, and of what their hands have planted and planted. And the 'ma' in His saying: 'and what their hands have done' is in a diminutive case as a conjunction with fruit, meaning: 'And from what I worked,' which in Abdullah's recitation mentioned: 'and from what I did it' with the 'ha' in this meaning, so the 'ha' in our reading is implied, because the Arabs sometimes implicate it, and it appears in the prayers of: min, ma, and al-dhiya. If it were said: 'ma' meaning the verbal noun, it would be a doctrine, so the meaning of the speech would be: and whoever their hands did. And if it was said: it means denial and has no place, it is also a doctrine, so the meaning of the speech would be: to eat from its fruit and their hands did not make it.
