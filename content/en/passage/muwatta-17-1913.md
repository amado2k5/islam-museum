## Summary
Malik transmits from Dawud ibn al-Husayn that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab say: among the gambling of the people of the Jahiliyya was selling an animal for meat, a sheep for two sheep. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.184}
