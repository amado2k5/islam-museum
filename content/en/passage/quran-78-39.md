## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 as one section. He explains His saying, “That is the true Day, so whoever wills may take a path to his Lord”: “That Day” means: the Day of Resurrection, which is the day when the Spirit and the angels will stand in rows. “The true”: He says: It is an existing truth, there is no doubt about it. And His saying: “So whoever wills may take to his Lord a place of return” means: So whoever wills of His servants may take, by believing in this true Day, preparing for it, and doing what will save him from its horrors, a place of return, meaning: a place of return, and it is a place of return, from their saying: So-and-so returned from his journey, as Ubaid said: Every absent one returns, and the one absent by death does not return. And in the passage he quotes sayings from Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Sufyan.
