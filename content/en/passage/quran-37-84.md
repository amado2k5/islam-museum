## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 83 to 86 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "when his Lord came with a sound heart": when Abraham came to his Lord with a sound heart free from polytheism, sincerely dedicating monotheism to Him, as narrated from Qatadah: "When his Lord came with a sound heart," and God is free from polytheism. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase: "When his Lord came with a sound heart," he said: free from polytheism. From Layth, from Mujahid "with a sound heart," he said: There is no doubt about it. Others said about this: Hadqla: and from his father, he said: O my sons, do not be cursed. Have you not seen that Abraham was never cursed at all? Allah said: "When his Lord came with a sound heart." And His saying: In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
