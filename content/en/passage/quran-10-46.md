**Commentary on verse 46 (p. 98)**

On [[passage/quran-10-46|10:46]] he says: whether We show you, Muhammad, in your lifetime some of the punishment We promise these polytheists of your people, or We take you in death before showing you that in them, "to Us is their return", their destination in every case is to Us; "then God is witness over what they do" means that I am witness of their deeds in the world, aware of them, with nothing hidden from Me, and I will repay them when they come to Me with what they deserve. Reports 17663, 17664 and 17665, from Mujahid: some of the punishment We promise them in your lifetime, or We take you before that, and to Us is their return. The editor refers to the glosses on "taking in death", "return" and "witness". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 98}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
