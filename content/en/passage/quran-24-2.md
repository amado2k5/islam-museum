## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The adulteress, and the adulterer, flog each one of them with one hundred lashes, and let no mercy prevent you from them concerning the religion of Allah if you believe in Allah and the Last Day, and let a group of the believers witness their punishment": whoever commits adultery among men or women while he is free and virgin and not married to a husband, then flog him with one hundred lashes as a punishment for what he has done and committed of disobedience to Allah. "And let no mercy seize you for the sake of Allah." Allah, the Exalted, says: Do not take the adulterer or the adulteress, O believers, out of compassion, which is gentle mercy in the religion of Allah, meaning in obeying Allah in what He commanded you to do, including the enforcement of the legal punishment upon them as He obligated you to do. The interpreters differed regarding what the believers forbade from taking compassion towards them. Some said: it means abandoning the enforcement of Allah's punishment upon them. But if the punishment is carried out upon them, they do not take it out of compassion for the religion of Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibrahim, and Ata.
