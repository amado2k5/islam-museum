## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abu Hurayra used to say, when he woke and the people had been rained on: we were rained on by the rising of conquest, and then he recited the verse about divine mercy that no one can withhold, from the start of Surat Fatir (2). The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.267} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.268}
