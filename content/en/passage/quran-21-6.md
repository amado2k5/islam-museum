## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "I did not believe before them of any town We destroyed. Do they understand that they will believe": No one before these deniers believed Muhammad among the polytheists of his people who said, 'So let Muhammad bring us a sign as the messengers before him from the people of a city We punished with destruction in this world, when our Messenger came to them with a miraculous sign.' "Do they understand that they believe?" He says: Do those who deny Muhammad who asked him for the verse believe in him if a sign comes to them, and their ancestors among the empty nations We destroyed with their messengers did not believe? In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
