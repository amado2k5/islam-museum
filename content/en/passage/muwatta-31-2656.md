## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Dinar, and from Zayd ibn Aslam, all reporting from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God said: God will not look on the Day of Resurrection at one who drags his garment out of pride. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.501}
