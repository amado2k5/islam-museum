**Commentary on verse 44 (pp. 572-573)**

Al-Tabari says on "when you met, He showed them to you as few in your eyes and made you few in their eyes, that God might accomplish a matter that was to be done; to God all matters return" [[passage/quran-8-44|8:44]]: God is hearing, knowing, when God shows His prophet the polytheists in his sleep as few, and shows them the believers when they met as few in their eyes though their number is great, and makes the believers few in their eyes so that they leave preparing against them, which makes their strength easy for the believers. Report 16156, from Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud: they were made few in our eyes at Badr until I said to a man beside me, do you see them as seventy? He said, I see them as a hundred; then we captured one of them and asked how many they were, and he said a thousand; report 16157, from him, is like it; report 16158, from Ibn Jurayj: Ibn Mas'ud said they were made few in our eyes until I said to a man, do you see them as a hundred? The editor identifies Ibn Bazi' al-Baghdadi and corrects the manuscript's reading. Report 16159, from al-Suddi: some of the polytheists said the caravan has gone back, so return; and Abu Jahl said, now that Muhammad and his Companions have come out to you, do not go back until you uproot them, and he said, people, do not kill them with weapons, take them alive and bind them with ropes, saying it out of the power he felt in himself. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 571} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 572} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 573}

The words "that God might accomplish a matter that was to be done" mean: I made you few, believers, in the polytheists' eyes and showed them to you few in your eyes, until God decreed between you what He decreed of your fighting one another and of your prevailing, believers, over your enemies the polytheists and gaining victory over them, so that God's word is the highest and the word of those who disbelieved the lowest, a matter God was doing and carrying out His command in. Report 16160, from Ibn Ishaq: to bring them together on war, for vengeance on whom He wished to take vengeance on and favour to whom He wished to complete His favour on among His friends, as in the Sira. "To God all matters return" means the return of all matters is to Him in the hereafter, when He recompenses their people as they deserve, the doer of good by his good and the doer of evil by his evil. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 573}
