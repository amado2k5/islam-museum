**Commentary on verse 83 (pp. 437-443)**

On [[passage/quran-11-83|11:83]], 'marked with your Lord', al-Tabari says: marked with God, who marked them; 'marked' describes the stones, hence its accusative. Reports 18440, 18441 and 18442, from Mujahid: marked. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 437}

Report 18443, from Mujahid, likewise, Ibn Jurayj adding that they do not resemble earthly stones; 18444, from Qatada and Ikrima: ringed, with a trace of redness; 18445, from Qatada: bearing a known mark, and someone who saw them said they were ringed stones with a trace of redness, unlike your stones; 18446, from al-Rabi': marked with lines; 18447, from al-Suddi: sealed. As for 'and they are not far from the wrongdoers', God threatens the polytheists of Quraysh: these stones sent on Lot's people are not far from the polytheists of your people, Muhammad, if they do not repent of their polytheism. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 438}

Report 18448, from Mujahid: that what befell those people befall them; 18449, from Mujahid: He frightens with them whom He wills, and 18450, 18451 and 18452 are the same; 18453, from Qatada: God has not sheltered any wrongdoer from them since Lot's people; 18454, from Qatada and Ikrima: He has left no wrongdoer free of them since; 18455, from Qatada: He means the wrongdoers of this community, and by God He has not sheltered a wrongdoer from them since. The editor vouches for a narrator in the chain and discusses a reading in the manuscript. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 439}

Report 18456, from al-Suddi: it means the wrongdoers of the Arabs, if they do not repent and are punished by them; 18457, from Abu Bakr al-Hudhali: not far from the wrongdoers of your community, so no wrongdoer is safe from them. The angels overturned the land of Sodom: report 18458, from Mujahid: Gabriel took Lot's people from their pastures and homes, lifted them with their cattle and goods until the dwellers of heaven heard their dogs bark, then tipped them over; 18459, from Mujahid: Gabriel put his wing under the lowest earth beneath Lot's people, took them with his right wing from their pastures and cattle, and raised it; 18460, from Mujahid: when they woke Gabriel went early to their town, tore it from its foundations, put his wing under it and carried it on the small feathers of his wing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 440}

Report 18461, from Ibrahim b. Abi Bakr, which Ibn Abi Najih did not hear from Mujahid: he carried it with all in it on the small feathers of his wing, rose with it to heaven until the dwellers of heaven heard their dogs, then overturned it, and the first to fall were its nobles; Mujahid said no people were struck as they were, for God blotted out their eyes, then overturned their town and rained on them stones of clay. Report 18462, from Qatada: it reached us that Gabriel took the middle town by its handle and bore it to heaven until the dwellers of heaven heard the barking of their dogs, then smashed parts on each other, making the top the bottom, and sent stones after them; and they were four million. Report 18463, from Qatada, likewise, adding that stones followed the stragglers, and that they were three towns called Sodom, between Medina and Syria. The editor explains rare words. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 441}

It was told us that Abraham would look out and say, Sodom, a day of ruin; the editor discusses a variant wording in the History. Report 18464, from al-Suddi: when morning came Gabriel came down and tore the land from seven earths and carried it to the lowest heaven until its dwellers heard their dogs and cocks, then overturned it and killed them; that is the saying of [[passage/quran-53-53|53:53]], 'and the overturned town He cast down', the one overturned when Gabriel cast the land down, tearing it up with his wing; those who had not died when the land fell were rained on with stones while under the ground, and any who were straggling on earth, and he pursued them through the villages, so that a man would be talking and a stone would come and kill him. Report 18465, from Qatada: it reached us that at morning Gabriel spread his wing and swept up their land with its palaces, animals, stones, trees and all else, gathered it into his wing and rose with it to the lowest heaven until its dwellers heard the voices of the people and the dogs, four million, then turned it upside down and sent it earthward overturned, parts crushing parts, and followed it with stones of baked clay. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 442}

Report 18466, from Muhammad b. Ka'b al-Qurazi: it was told me that the Prophet said, God sent Gabriel to the overturned town, Lot's town in which Lot lived, and he lifted it on his wing and rose with it until the dwellers of the lowest heaven heard the barking of its dogs and the voices of its hens, then tipped it on its face and God sent stones after it; God destroyed it and the overturned towns around, five in all, Sanʿa, Saʿwa, Athra, Duma and Sodom, Sodom being the great town, and God saved Lot and his family with him, but not his wife, who was among the lost. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 443}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
