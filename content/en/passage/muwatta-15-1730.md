## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd ibn Qays al-Makki, from Amr ibn Shuayb, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Umar ibn al-Khattab used to turn back widows from al-Bayda, preventing them from making the pilgrimage. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.107}
