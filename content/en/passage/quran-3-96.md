## Summary
Verse 96 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 96 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 96: the first house (pp. 19-21).** Al-Tabari says the interpreters differed over its interpretation.

**First view: the first house in which God is worshipped (7422-7427).** Some said the sense is that it is the first house in which God is worshipped, as a place of blessing and direction for all nations, the one at Bakka, and they said it is not the first house placed on the earth because many houses were before it. Hannad ibn al-Sari relays from Abu al-Ahwas, from Simak, from Khalid ibn Ar'ara that a man stood before Ali and said: will you not tell me about the House, is it the first house placed on the earth? He said: no, but it is the first house in which blessing was placed, the Abraham's place of standing, and whoever enters it is secure (7422). The editor says it is an abridgement of an earlier report; that the manuscript and printed text have here, as there, 'placed in the blessing'; that he corrected it from al-Mustadrak and al-Durr al-Manthur to 'the blessing in it', but returns to judge that what stood there is right and that 'the Abraham's place of standing' is in the genitive as a substitute for 'the blessing'. Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna relays from Muhammad ibn Jafar, from Shu'ba, from Simak that he heard Khalid ibn Ar'ara say that he heard Ali, who was asked whether the house placed first for people is the first house that was on the earth, say: no; where were Noah's people then, and where were Hud's people? but it is the first house set up for the public as a place of blessing and direction (7423). Yaqub relays from Ibn Ulayya, from Abu Raja that Hafs asked al-Hasan, and I was listening, and he said: it is the first mosque in which God was worshipped on the earth (7424). Abd al-Jabbar ibn Yahya al-Ramli relays from Damra, from Ibn Shawdhab, from Matar that there were houses before it but it is the first house placed for worship (7425); the editor says he found no entry for al-Tabari's teacher Abd al-Jabbar, who will come again as 7446. Muhammad ibn Sinan relays from Abu Bakr al-Hanafi, from Abbad, from al-Hasan that it is the first house in which God is worshipped (7426), and al-Muthanna from al-Himmani, from Sharik, from Salim, from Said that it was placed for worship (7427).

**Second view: it is the first house set up for the public, and their differing on how it was placed.** Others said that it is indeed the first house set up for the public. Those who said so then differed on the manner of its being placed first.

*Created before the earths, which were then spread from beneath it (7428-7432).* Some said it was created before all the earths and then the earths were spread out from under it. Muhammad ibn Umara al-Asadi relays from Ubayd Allah ibn Musa, from Shayban, from al-A'mash, from Bukayr ibn al-Akhnas, from Mujahid, from Abd Allah ibn Amr that God created the House two thousand years before the earth, and, when His throne was on the water, it was a white foam, and the earth was spread out from beneath it (7428); the editor says that the foam is the portion of the white froth of water that rises on it. Muhammad ibn Abd al-Malik ibn Abi al-Shawarib relays from Abd al-Wahid ibn Ziyad, from Khusayf that he heard Mujahid say that the first thing God created was the Ka'ba, then He spread the earth from beneath it (7429). Muhammad ibn Amr relays from Abu Asim, from Isa, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid that it is like the wording of [[passage/quran-3-110|3:110]] (that is, that 'for people' means for all) (7430). Muhammad relays from Ahmad, from Asbat, from al-Suddi that on the day the earth was water there was a foam on the earth, and when God created the earth He created the House with it, so it is the first house placed on the earth (7431). Al-Hasan ibn Yahya relays from Abd al-Razzaq, from Mamar, from Qatada that it is the first house God placed, around which Adam circumambulated and those after him (7432).

*The site of the Ka'ba is the site of the first house (7433).* Others said that the site of the Ka'ba is the site of the first house God placed on the earth. Bishr relays from Yazid, from Said, from Qatada, who said: it was mentioned to us that the House came down with Adam when he came down, and God said: a house of Mine has come down with you, to be circled as My throne is circled; so Adam circled it and those after him of the believers, until in the time of the Flood, when God drowned Noah's people, God raised it and purified it from the punishment of the people of the earth touching it, and it became a frequented house in the heaven. Then Abraham followed some trace of it afterwards and built it on an ancient foundation that had been before it (7433). (These are views and reports al-Tabari transmits, and the museum presents them as they were reported, without a verdict.) {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 21}
