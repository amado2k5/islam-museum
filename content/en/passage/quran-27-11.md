## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 9 to 11 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that God informed Moses that He is the Almighty God in His punishment, wise in His management, and that the 'h' in 'inha' is the 'h' of support or the pronoun of the matter. In the text, omitted is that he threw down his staff, and it became a serpent trembling as if it were a jinn, a species of serpent, so he turned away as a snake and did not follow back. The messengers do not fear him except those who wrong and then change good after evil.
