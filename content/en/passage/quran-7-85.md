## Summary
Verse 85 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 85 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-85|7:85]], al-Tabari says: We sent to the children of Midian, who are the descendants of Midian son of Abraham the Friend of the Merciful, as (14843) from Ibn Ishaq says; Midian is thus a tribe, like Tamim. Ibn Ishaq also claims that the Shuayb whom God sent to them was of the line of this Midian, that he was Shuayb son of Mikil son of Yashjur, and that his name in Syriac was Yatrun; the editor says the manuscript is unclear on how to vocalise these names. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 554}

On Ibn Ishaq's account, the meaning is: We sent to the children of Midian their brother Shuayb, calling them to obey God, to stop at His command, and to give up spreading corruption in the land and barring from His way. Shuayb said: my people, worship God alone with no partner; you have no god deserving your worship other than the God who created you, in whose hand lie your benefit and harm. "A clear sign has come to you from your Lord" means a mark and proof from God for the truth of what I say; "so give full measure and weight" means give people their due by the measure you use and the weight you weigh with; "do not diminish people's things" means do not wrong them of their rights or cut them short. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 555}

"Bakhs" is wronging, as in the proverb "you take her for foolish, but she is wronging", and the editor gives the story behind it: a man of Banu al-Anbar took his neighbour to be simple and proposed to mix his goods with hers so as to divide them and take the better, but she disputed with him until he paid her what she wanted to be free of her. Hence "bakhs" in "a paltry price" means poor in quality. (14844) from al-Suddi and (14845) from Qatada that it means do not wrong people of their things. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 555}

"Do not make mischief in the land after its reform" means: do not act in God's land by disobeying Him, nor by what you did before God sent His prophet to you, worshipping other than God, associating partners with Him and cheating people in measure and weight, after God has set the earth right by sending the prophet among you to forbid what is not lawful for you. "That is better for you" means what I have told you and commanded, pure worship of God, giving people their rights and leaving corruption, is better for you in your life now and your life to come; "if you are believers" means if you believe what I say and convey from God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 556}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
