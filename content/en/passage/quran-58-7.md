## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah knows what is in the heavens and what is on the earth? There is no private conversation of three except that He is their fourth, nor five but He is their sixth, nor less than that, nor more than that, but He is with them wherever they are, then He will inform them of what they did on the Day of Resurrection. Indeed, Allah is Knowing of all things." Glory be to Him: How can it be hidden from someone whose attribute is the deeds of these disbelievers and their disobedience to their Lord? Then He described His closeness to His servants, hearing their private conversations, and what people conceal of their conversations, which they speak secretly among themselves. He said: "There are not three private conversations of His creation except that He is the fourth of them," hearing their secrets and private conversations, and nothing of their secrets is hidden from Him. "And not five except that He is their sixth." He says: And there is no one who whispers to five except that he is the sixth of them. Likewise, 'there is no less than three,' and no more than five, 'except that He is with them,' when they converse 'wherever they are.' He says: In the passage, he quotes from Al-Dahhak and Muqatil.
