## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who wished for His place yesterday will say, 'And it is as if Allah extends provision for whom He wills of His servants and decrees [it]. Were it not for [Allah] upon us, He would have caused us to be swallowed up! And it is as if the disbelievers will not succeed.'": And those who wished for His place yesterday in this world, its wealth, abundant wealth, and what was spread for him yesterday—meaning before the wrath and punishment of Allah descended upon him—they say: And it is as if Allah... There is disagreement about the meaning of 'and it is as if Allah wills'. As for Qatadah, two opinions have been narrated from him regarding this: one is 'Ma' and from Qatadah, he said regarding the phrase 'and it is' he said: Did you not see that it is. And from Qatadah 'and it will be as if it is': or you do not see that it is. And he said: Muammar narrated to me, from Qatadah: 'And it is as if' he said: Did you not see that it is. And the other opinion is Ma: And from Ma'mar, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase 'And it is as if Allah would expand provision,' he said: or did not know that Allah 'and He would make provision abundant,' or he did not know that He did.
