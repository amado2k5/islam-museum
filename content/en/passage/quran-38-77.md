## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 77 to 79 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'So get out of it, for you are accursed': Allah says to Iblis: 'So get out of it,' meaning from Paradise, 'for you are accursed.' He says: 'So you are cast among the people, cursed, and cursed,' as from Qatadah, His saying: 'So get out of it, for you are accursed,' he said: 'The accursed' is the accursed. I narrated from al-Muharibī, from Juwaybir, from al-Dahhak with a similar narration. And His saying: 'And indeed, upon you is My curse,' he says: And for You is my expulsion from Paradise until the Day of Judgment, meaning: until the Day of recompense and accountability of the servants. 'He said, 'My Lord, then watch me until the Day they are resurrected.' Allah says: Iblis said to his Lord: 'My Lord, when You cursed me and expelled me from Your Paradise, then look at me,' meaning: delay me in the term, and do not destroy me until the Day they are resurrected, meaning: until the Day You resurrect Your creation from their graves. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and al-Dahhak.
