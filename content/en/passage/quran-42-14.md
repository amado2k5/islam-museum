## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And they did not split except after knowledge had come to them out of enmity among them. And if it were not for a word that preceded your Lord for a specified term, the matter would have been decided between them. And indeed those who inherited the Book after them are in doubt about it, and it is doubtful." The polytheists did not become divided in their religions and became confederates except after knowledge came to them, that what Allah commanded them and sent Noah to do was to establish the true religion and not to become divided in it. From Muammar, from Qatadah: "And they did not split except after knowledge had come to them," he said: Beware of division, for it is destruction "out of enmity among them," meaning: envy among you against others, envy, and enmity over the pursuit of worldly matters. "And if it were not for a word that has preceded from your Lord for a specified term," the Exalted says: "And if it were not for a previous saying from your Lord, O Muhammad, He would not hasten the punishment upon them, but delayed it to a specified term, and that is the appointed term mentioned as the Day of Resurrection." He quotes in the passage from Qatadah.
