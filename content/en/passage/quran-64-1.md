## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth glorifies God. To Him belongs dominion, and to Him belongs praise, and He is over all things competent,” means that all that is in the seven heavens and on the earth of His creation prostrates to Him and glorifies Him. And His statement, “To Him belongs dominion,” means that He, may He be exalted, possesses the dominion of the heavens and the earth, and His authority is absolute and His decree is carried out. And His saying: “And to Him belongs all praise” means: To Him belongs the praise of all that is in it of creation, because all of that creation does not know good except from Him, and they have no provider other than Him, so to Him belongs the praise of all of them. “And He is over all things competent” means: He is over all things, the One with power, He says: He creates what He wills, and He causes to die whom He wills, and He enriches whom He wills, and He impoverishes whom He wills, and He honors whom He wills, and He humiliates whom He wills. Nothing that He wills is impossible for Him, because He is the One with perfect power, with which nothing is impossible for Him.
