## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads Abraham's words as an acknowledgement that God knows what is kept secret and what is made public, and that nothing on earth or in heaven is hidden from Him. He takes Abraham's aim in the prayer to be God's pleasure, and that his offspring be among those who obey Him.
