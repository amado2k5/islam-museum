## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that Abraham's saying, "Our Lord, indeed You know what we conceal and what we reveal" is a testimony to God regarding what he intended and intended in his previous supplication — his supplication to make the city safe and to avoid idol worship — and that his intention was to please God in His love so that his children would be among His obedient and sincere worshippers. He explains the meaning: Know, O Lord, what our hearts conceal when it comes to our questions and in all our states, and what we declare of our supplications and other deeds, and nothing is hidden from You on earth or in the heavens, for all of this is apparent to You, for You are its Manager.
