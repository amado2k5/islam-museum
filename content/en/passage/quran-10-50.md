**Commentary on verse 50 (p. 101)**

On [[passage/quran-10-50|10:50]] he says: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists, tell me, if God's punishment came upon you by night or day and the Hour came and resurrection stood, could you ward it off from yourselves? What do the criminals who disbelieved in God hurry of the descent of the punishment, they who alone will burn in its heat, when they cannot ward it off from themselves? The editor refers to the glosses on "by night" and "hurrying". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 101}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
