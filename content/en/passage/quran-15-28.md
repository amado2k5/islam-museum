## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 28 and 29 together: Remember, O Muhammad, when your Lord said to the angels, 'Indeed, I created a human from clay of aged clay.' "So when I made him": He formed him and shaped his form, "and breathed into him of My spirit," and he became a living human. "So fall down in prostration before him": a prostration of greeting and honor, not a prostration of worship. It is narrated from Ibn Abbas that when Allah created the angels, He told them that I created a human from clay, and when I created him, they prostrated to him, but they refused, so He sent fire upon them and burned them. Then He created other angels and they refused, and I burned them. Then He created other angels and they said, 'We hear and obey, except Iblis, who was among the first disbelievers.'
