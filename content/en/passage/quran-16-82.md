## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 82 to 83 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "But if they turn away, then upon you is only the clear message": If these polytheists turn away, O Muhammad, from what I sent you to them from the truth, and they do not respond to you and turn away from it, then you have no blame or excuse because you have fulfilled your duty in that; your only duty is to convey to them what You sent with. By His saying "the clarified" means that it clarifies to those who hear it so that they understand it. As for the phrase: "They recognize the blessing of Allah and then deny it," interpreters differ regarding the meaning of the blessing that Allah informed about these polytheists that they deny despite knowing it. Some said: He is the Prophet; they recognized his prophethood, then denied it and denied it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
