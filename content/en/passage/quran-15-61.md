## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 61 to 63 in one passage: "When the messengers of Lot came," when the messengers of Allah came, Lot denied them but did not recognize them, saying, "Indeed, you are a people who deny you," meaning we reject you but do not recognize you—from Mujahid whom Lot denied. The messengers said to him, "Rather, We brought you with what they used to endure as they used to endure," meaning we brought you the messengers of God with what your people suspected was the punishment of God for their disbelief — from Mujahid regarding the punishment of the people of Lot.
