## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, I only advise you of one thing: that you stand up for Allah, both in pairs and alone, then reflect on what your companion has in Paradise. Indeed, he is only a warner to you before a severe punishment": Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists among your people: I only advise you, O people, of one thing, which is obedience to Allah, as narrated from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "I only advise you of one thing," he said: obedience to Allah. And His saying: "That you stand for Allah in pairs and individually" means: And that singular phrase I admonish you is that you stand for Allah in pairs, and "and individually" individually, for in a diminutive case, it is a translation of the singular. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
