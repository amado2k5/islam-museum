## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 33 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." He explains: "Go," O Moses, "to Pharaoh; he transgressed." He means he has transgressed and rebelled against his Lord. We have explained the meaning of transgression with what has passed and made it no sufficiency to repeat it. In this passage, the passage is omitted, and it is sufficient for the listener to understand what was mentioned, which is: "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." Invite him to the oneness of God and obey Him, and to send the Children of Israel with you. He quotes in the passage from Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Zayd.
