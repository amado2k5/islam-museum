## Summary
Verse 37 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 37 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "who does greater wrong than one who invents a lie about God or denies His signs? Those will receive their portion of the Book", God says: who is more mistaken in deed, more ignorant in speech and further away from the truth and what is right than one who invented a lie about God, that is, forged a false saying about God and said, when committing an indecency, that God commanded it; "or denied His signs" means denied His proofs and evidences that show His oneness and His prophets' prophethood, denying their reality and rejecting their soundness; "those" means whoever did that, inventing a lie about God and denying His signs, "will receive their portion of the Book", meaning their share of what God wrote for them in the Preserved Tablet will reach them. The commentators then differ on the nature of that "portion" which is theirs in the "Book", and what it is. Some said it is the punishment God prepared for the people of disbelief in Him: he gives (14555) and (14556) from Abu Salih that it means of punishment, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 408}

(14557) from al-Suddi that it is what was written for them of punishment; and (14558), (14559) and (14560) from al-Hasan that it is of punishment. Others said the meaning is that they will receive their portion of what preceded for them of wretchedness and happiness, and the note points to the like in [[passage/quran-11-105|11:105]], "some of them wretched and some happy": he gives (14561) from Sa'id ibn Jubayr that it is of wretchedness and happiness; (14562) from Mujahid that it is wretched and happy; and (14563) from him that it is what preceded, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 409}

and (14564) and (14565) from Mujahid that it is what was written for them of wretchedness and happiness; (14566) from Ibn Abbas that it is of wretchedness and happiness; (14567) from Mujahid that it is what has preceded of the Book; (14568) from Atiyya that it is what preceded for them in the Book; (14569) from Sa'id that it is of wretchedness and happiness; (14570) from Mujahid that it is what was decreed or ordained for them; (14571) from Ibn Abbas that it is what was written against them of deeds; and (14572) from Mujahid that they are a people who do deeds they must do, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 410}

and the note records the biography of Isma'il ibn Sumay' and Bakr al-Tawil in the chain. Others said the meaning is that they will receive their portion of their book that was written for them or against them through the deeds they did in this world of good and evil: he gives (14573) from Ibn Abbas that it is their portion of deeds, whoever did good is rewarded for it and whoever did evil is repaid for it; (14574) from Mujahid that it is of the rulings of the Book according to the measure of their deeds; (14575) from Qatada that they receive their portion in the Hereafter of the deeds they did and sent ahead; (14576) from him that it is their deeds, the evil deeds they did and sent ahead; and (14577) from Qatada that it is of their deeds that they did. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 411}

He gives (14578) from al-Dahhak that it means their portion of the deed: if the portion he did was good he is repaid good, and if he did evil he is repaid the like. Others said the meaning is that they will receive their portion of what they were promised in the Book of good or evil: he gives (14579) from Ibn Abbas that it is of good and evil; (14580), (14581) and (14582) from Mujahid that it is what they were promised; (14583) from Ibn Abbas the like; (14584) from al-Dahhak that it is what they were promised in it of good or evil; and (14585) from Mujahid that it is what they were promised in it, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 412}

(14586) from Mujahid that it is what they were promised of good or evil; and (14587) from him that what preceded for them of the Book will reach them. Others said the meaning is that they will receive their portion of the Book which He wrote: he gives (14588) from Ibn Abbas that what was written against them reaches them, that is, it was written that the face of whoever forges against God is blackened. Others said the meaning is that they will receive their portion of what was written for them of provision, lifespan and deed: he gives (14589) from al-Rabi' ibn Anas that it is of what was written for them of provision; and (14590) from al-Qurazi that it is his deed, provision and lifespan, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 413}

and (14591) from Ibn Zayd that it is of deeds, provisions and lifespans, and when they have finished all these things, Our messengers come to take them. Al-Tabari says the soundest of these views to him is that of those who said the meaning is: those will receive their portion of the Book, of what was written for them of good and evil in this world, and provision, deed and term. That is because God followed it with His words "until when Our messengers come to take them, they say: where are those you used to call on besides God?", and by following it with that after "those will receive their portion of the Book" He showed that what reaches them is only what was decreed for them in the world to reach them, since He reported that it reaches them until the time His messengers come to take their souls; had it been their portion of the Book or of what is prepared for them in the Hereafter, it would not be limited to reaching them until the coming of God's messengers for their death, because God's messengers do not come to them for death in the Hereafter, and their punishment in the Hereafter has no end and no ending, since God decreed their abiding in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 414}

Then, on "until, when Our messengers come to take them, they say: where are those you used to call on besides God? They say: they have strayed from us; and they will bear witness against themselves that they were disbelievers", God says that "until when Our messengers come" means until Our messengers come to them: these who forged a lie about God or denied the signs of their Lord receive the shares God wrote for them and His knowledge preceded for them, of provision, deed, term, good and evil in the world, until Our messengers come to take their souls. When Our messengers come, meaning the angel of death and his helpers, "to take them" means to receive their full number from the world to the Hereafter; "they say: where are those you used to call on besides God" means the messengers said, where are those you used to call on as allies besides God and worshipped, who do not ward off from you what has come of the command of God, who is your creator and theirs, and the great trial that has come down on your yard? Why do they not relieve you from the distress you are in and rescue you from it? The wretched answered, our allies whom we used to call besides God have strayed from us; by "strayed" he means they went astray and took a way other than ours and left us in our need of them and did not benefit us. God says that the people then bore witness against themselves that they had been disbelievers in God, denying His oneness. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 415}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
