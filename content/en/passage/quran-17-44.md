## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 45 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Exalted is He above what they say, a great exaltation": This is a disqualification from Allah Himself, which He mentioned above the polytheists, who make gods other than Him and host daughters to Him. He said: Exaltation is for Allah and exalted above what you say, O people, of falsehood and lying. What you attribute to these matters is not His attribute, nor should it have any attribute. Also: Qatadah: "Exalted is He above what they say, exalted is He great," glorifying himself when slandered was said about him. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Ibrahim.
