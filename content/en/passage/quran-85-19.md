## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 22 as one section. He explains His saying, “But those who disbelieve are in denial”: What is wrong with these people who deny God’s warnings is that they have not received news from the nations before them who denied God’s messengers, such as Pharaoh and his people, Thamud and their like, and what God brought down upon them of punishments for their denial of the messengers, but they are in denial of God’s revelation and sending down, preferring their desires and following the ways of their forefathers. In the section, he quotes sayings of Mujahid and Qatadah.
