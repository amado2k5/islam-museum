## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on the Prophet's miracles, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim: water sprang from between the Prophet's fingers until the people performed ablution with it. It is among the best-known texts on tangible miracles in the collections. {cite:sahih-muslim}
