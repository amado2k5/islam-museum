## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "Children of Israel, remember My favour which I bestowed on you and that I preferred you over the worlds" al-Tabari says this verse is an exhortation from God to the Jews who lived near the Messenger's place of emigration and a reminder of His earlier gifts to their forebears, to move them toward His religion and holding His Messenger true, so He said: Children of Israel, remember the favours in your hands and My deeds toward you, My rescuing you from the hands of Pharaoh and his people, My sending down manna and quails on you in your wandering, My establishing you in the land after you were abased, My choosing messengers from among you and My preferring you over the world among which you lived while you obeyed Me by following My messenger to you and holding him and what he brought you true, and leave persisting in error and wrongdoing. He refers to the earlier account of the favours and of the world over which they were preferred. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 573}
