**Commentary on verse 161 (p. 178)**

Al-Tabari says God is addressing His Prophet Muhammad: remember also, Muhammad, another error of these people, their opposition to their Lord, their disobedience to their prophet Moses, and their changing of the words they were told to say when God told them "dwell in this town", which is the town of Bayt al-Maqdis. "Eat from it" means from its fruit, grain and plants, "wherever you will". "And say: hittah" means say that this act is a "hittah" that lays down our sins. "We will forgive you" means your Lord will cover your past sins, pardon them and not hold you to account for them. "We will increase the doers of good" among you, who are those obedient to God, beyond the forgiveness of sins that He promised. He notes that he has already given the reports on all this, the differences among those who differed and the view he holds to be sound, in his commentary on the parallel verse, and the editor refers to the earlier explanations of "town", "hittah", "forgiveness" and "doing good". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 178}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
