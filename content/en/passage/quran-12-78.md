**Commentary on verse 78 (p. 202)**

On "they said, O Aziz, he has a father, a very old man, so take one of us in his place; we see you are among the doers of good", al-Tabari says: Joseph's brothers said to Joseph "O Aziz", that is, O king, "he has a father, a very old man", devoted to loving him, meaning Ya'qub, "so take one of us in his place", that is, take one of us instead of Benjamin and let him go, "we see you are among the doers of good" in your deeds. Report 19614, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: we see that as kindness from you if you do it. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "al-Aziz". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 202}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
