## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 30 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or do those in whose hearts is a disease that Allah will not bring out their grudges": Consider these hypocrites whose hearts are doubtful about their religion and weak in their certainty. They are confused in knowing the truth that Allah will not bring out the grudges in their hearts from the believers, so He reveals it to them and reveals it to them until they know their hypocrisy and confusion in their religion. "And if We willed, We would show you them." God Almighty says: And if We willed, O Muhammad, We would have made you known these hypocrites so that you may recognize them from the saying: I will show you what I do, meaning I will teach you. And His saying: "So I will recognize them by their features," means: Let them know by the signs of hypocrisy evident in the content of their speech and their actions. Then Allah, the Exalted, will make him known to them. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
