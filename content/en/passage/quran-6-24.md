## Summary
Verse 24 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 24 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "see how they lied against themselves, and what they used to invent failed them" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: see, Muhammad, and know, how these polytheists who held idols and images equal to their Lord lied against themselves in the next world at the meeting with God, by their saying "by God, our Lord, we were not polytheists", and used there the character they had in the world, of lying and slander. "See" here means seeing with the heart, not with the eye; its sense is to become aware and know how they lied in the next world. He said "they lied" though it means "they will lie", because since the report had already passed in the verse before, it became as if it had been and come to be. "And what they used to invent failed them" means the rivals and images left them and they disowned them, taking another path than theirs, since they perished, and those who worshipped them were returned... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 301}

The editor keeps "with which they were characterised" from the manuscript, and notes that what he bracketed in the printed text differs from the manuscript.

The rest: then they were taken to account for what they used to invent, their sayings about the idols against God, their worship of them and their making them partners in God's dominion; they failed them and those who worshipped them were punished for their invention. He has already explained that the meaning of "dalal" is taking a path other than guidance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 302}

The editor notes that in the manuscript the word "ya'budunaha" is without dots and he did not find the right reading, fearing a line or part of one has fallen out, so he put the printed text in brackets, doubting it, and refers for "dalal" to volume 10.

It has been reported that these polytheists say this when they see the breadth of God's mercy on that day. Report 13140, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr: a man came to Ibn Abbas and said: I hear God say "by God our Lord we were not polytheists" and in another verse, [[passage/quran-4-42|4:42]], "they will conceal no word from God". Ibn Abbas said: as for "by God our Lord we were not polytheists", when they saw that only the people of Islam enter Paradise they said, come, let us deny, and said "by God our Lord we were not polytheists"; so God sealed their mouths and their hands and feet spoke, "and they conceal no word from God". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 302}

The editor says this report came earlier as 9520 in volume 8, that Abu Jafar abridged Ibn Abbas's reports there, giving two others that show the questioner was Nafi' ibn al-Azraq, who used to come to Ibn Abbas to ask about the ambiguous verses of the Qur'an, and that this is one of his ways of abridging his commentary.

Reports 13141 and 13142, from Mujahid: the saying of the people of polytheism when they saw sins forgiven and that God does not forgive a polytheist; "see how they lied against themselves" by God's calling them liars. 13143, from Ibn Abbas: "by God our Lord we were not polytheists", then [[passage/quran-4-42|4:42]], "they conceal no word from God", by their limbs. 13144, 13145 and 13146, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr: they swore and made excuse, saying "by God our Lord". 13147, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr: when it was ordered that men of the people of oneness be taken out of the Fire, the polytheists in it said, come, let us say there is no god but God, perhaps we shall come out with these; they were not believed, so they swore "by God our Lord we were not polytheists", and God said "see how they lied against themselves". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 303}

Report 13148, from Qatada: "what they used to invent failed them", that is, "associate". 13149, from Ibn Abbas: when the polytheists saw that only a Muslim enters Paradise they said, come, when we are asked let us say "by God our Lord we were not polytheists"; they were asked and said so, so God sealed their mouths and their limbs bore witness to their deeds against them, and those who disbelieved wished, when they saw it, that the earth were levelled with them and they concealed no word from God. 13150, from Mujahid: an hour will come on the people on the day of resurrection when the polytheists see the people of oneness forgiven and say "by God our Lord we were not polytheists". 13151, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr: he used to read "wa-llahi rabbi-na" in the genitive, saying they swore and excused themselves. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 304}

The editor says he does not know the Hisham from whom Hamza al-Zayyat related, identifies Sufyan ibn Ziyad al-Usfuri, keeps "associate" in 13148 without the printed text's addition and "when the people of polytheism saw" in 13150 as a permitted dialect form, and found no biography of Muslim ibn Khalaf, fearing his name is distorted.

Verse 24 closes here at p. 304, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-25|6:25]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
