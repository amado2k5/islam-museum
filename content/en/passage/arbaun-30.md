## Summary
Hadith of Abu Tha'laba al-Khushani: God has set obligations, so do not neglect them; set limits, so do not transgress them; forbidden some things, so do not violate them; and kept silent about some things out of mercy, not forgetfulness, so do not search for them. Ibn Rajab reviews the chain, the dispute on raising the report, and related reports. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
