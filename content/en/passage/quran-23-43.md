## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 42 to 43 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "No nation precedes its appointed time nor delays it": the destruction of a nation that We established after Thamud precedes before the appointed time for its destruction, nor does its destruction be delayed by the time We postponed its destruction or the time we have set for its annihilation, but it perishes because of its coming. This is a warning from God to the polytheists of the people of our Prophet Muhammad, and a warning from Him to delay it in their appointed times while disbelieving in it and denying His Messenger, so that they may reach the appointed time and His punishment will befall them, just as He did with those before them among the previous nations.
