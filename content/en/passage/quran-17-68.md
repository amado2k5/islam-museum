## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Are you secure that He will not swallow you up the side of the land or that He will send upon you a retractor and then you will find no protector?" "Are you secure, O people, from your Lord, when you have denied His favor by saving you from the terror of what you were in at sea, and the great destruction you had foreseen? So when He saved you and you came to righteousness, you disbelieved and associated others in His worship with Him, meaning the side of righteousness that He would swallow you up," meaning the side of the land, "or He will send a harvester upon you," meaning: Or He rains stones from the sky to kill you, as He did with the people of Lot: "Then you will find no protector for you," meaning: Then you will find nothing to defend you from His punishment or to prevent you from it. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Jurayj.
