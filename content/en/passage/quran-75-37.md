## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 40 as a single unit. He explains the phrase, “Was he not a drop of semen emitted?” as follows: Was this denier not God’s power to resurrect him after his death and bring him back into existence after his annihilation? “A drop of semen” means a small amount of water in the loins of the man. The reciters differed in their reading of the word “emitted.” The majority of the reciters of Medina and Kufa read it as “emitted” (with a “t”), meaning “emitted the sperm.” Some reciters of Mecca and Basra read it as “emitted” (with a “y”), meaning “emitted the semen.” The correct view is that both readings are well-known and have the same meaning, so whichever reading a reciter chooses is correct. And His saying: “Then he was a clot” means that God Almighty says: Then he was blood after he was a sperm, then a clot, then He fashioned him into a complete human being, speaking, hearing, and seeing. And in the passage He quotes sayings on the authority of Qatadah.
