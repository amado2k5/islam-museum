## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, that Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf bought a slave-girl, found that she had a husband, and returned her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.139}
