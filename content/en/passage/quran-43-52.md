## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or am I better than this one who is humiliating and hardly explains": Allah Almighty says, informing about Pharaoh's words to his people after he argued with them about his kingship and authority, clarifying his tongue and the perfection of his character, and the virtue between him and Moses with the attributes he described himself. Moses said: I am the best, O people. My description is this attribute that I described to you. "Or is this one who is humiliated?" He has no kingdom or wealth despite the ailment in his body and the affliction on his tongue, for which he can hardly explain his words. There is disagreement about the meaning of the phrase "mother" in this context; some said: its meaning is: Rather, I am good, and they said: That is a report, not an interrogative one. In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
