**Commentary on verse 101 (pp. 214-215)**

On [[passage/quran-10-101|10:101]] al-Tabari says: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists of your people who ask you for signs on the truth of what you call them to of God's oneness and giving up rivals and idols: look, people, at what is in the heavens of signs pointing to the truth of what I call you to of God's oneness, of its sun and moon, the alternation of its night and day and the sending down of the rain with the provision of servants from its clouds, and in the earth its mountains, its splitting with plants and the sustenance of its people and the rest of its kinds of wonders; in this for you, if you reason and reflect, there is an admonition and a lesson; the commentary on the verse runs on to the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 214}

These signs are also a pointer that all this is the work of One who may have no partner in His dominion and no helper in His governing and keeping, and they make you need no other signs. God says: 'and signs and warnings do not avail a people who do not believe', that is, proofs, lessons and warning messengers do not avail God's servants against His punishment for a people for whom misery has gone before from God and for whom it was decreed in the Mother of the Book that they are of the Fire; they will not believe in any of it nor affirm it, even if every sign came to them until they see the painful punishment. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'availing' and 'warner'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 215}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
