## Summary
Malik says he regards camels, cattle and sheep as food, of which Muslims may eat when they enter enemy land; he sees no harm in what is eaten of them in a manner that is customary and needed; and he does not think anyone should store any of that to take back to his family. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.582}
