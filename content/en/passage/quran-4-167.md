**Commentary on verse 167: those who disbelieve and bar others from God's way (pp. 410-411)**

Al-Tabari says that those who deny Muhammad's mission, knowing it, from the People of the Book, and deny that God revealed His Book to him, 'barred from the way of God', that is, from God's religion which He sent His Prophet with. Their barring was their saying to the idolaters who asked about Muhammad: we do not find his description in our book; and their claim that prophethood can only be in the line of Aaron and the seed of David, and the like, by which they discouraged people from following the Prophet. 'They have strayed far' means they strayed far from the straight road and fell from the right path; whoever denies Muhammad's mission and bars from what he was sent with has lost his way away from God's religion. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 410}
