## Summary
A report that Malik transmits about his teacher Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman, whom he saw vomit repeatedly in the mosque. He neither left nor performed ablution, but prayed. The editor explains qalas as what comes out of the stomach, a mouthful or less, and not vomit. Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.59} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.60}
