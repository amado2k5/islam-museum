## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever does righteous deeds, it is for his own soul; and whoever does evil, it is against it. And your Lord is ever unjust to His servants." Whoever acts in obedience to Allah in this world, obeys His command, and refrains from what He forbids is for himself. He means: for his soul does that good deed, because his reward will be rewarded, so in the Hereafter he will be rewarded by Allah for Paradise and salvation from the Fire. "And whoever does wrong, it is against it." He means: Whoever acts against Allah's sins therein has done a harm upon himself, because by doing so he earned Allah's wrath and painful punishment. "And your Lord is not unjust to the servants." Allah Almighty says: And your Lord, O Muhammad, does not punish anyone for the sin of a sin that is not earned by it. Rather, He does not punish anyone except for the crime he has earned in this world, or for the reason he deserved it from him. And Allah knows best.
