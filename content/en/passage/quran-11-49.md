**Commentary on verse 49 (p. 356)**

On [[passage/quran-11-49|11:49]] al-Tabari says God speaks to His prophet Muhammad: this story I told you of Noah, his report and the report of his people, is 'of the tidings of the unseen', that is, of the unseen reports you did not witness so as to know them, 'which We reveal to you', We reveal them to you and so make them known to you, 'neither you nor your people knew them before this' revelation We reveal to you; 'so be patient' in carrying out God's command, conveying His message and what you meet from the polytheists of your people, as Noah was patient; 'the end is for the God-fearing' means the good outcome of matters is for whoever feared God, performing His obligations and avoiding disobedience to Him, so they are the winners of the bliss they hope for in the next life and the victors in the world in their quest, as the end of Noah was when he was patient with God's command, that He saved him from destruction with those who believed in him, gave him in the next life the honour He gave, and drowned and destroyed all who denied him. Report 18259, from Qatada: 'those are of the tidings of the unseen' is the Qur'an, and Muhammad and his people would not have known what Noah and his people did but for what God made clear to him in His Book. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 356}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
