**Commentary on verse 22 (pp. 560-565)**

On "and Satan will say, when the matter is decided: God promised you a true promise, and I promised you, then failed you; I had no authority over you except that I called you and you responded to me; so do not blame me, blame yourselves; I cannot help you, nor can you help me; I disbelieved in your associating me before; for the wrongdoers is a painful punishment", al-Tabari says, mighty be His mention: Iblis said "when the matter is decided", meaning when the reckoning was finished, the people of Paradise entered Paradise and the people of the Fire the Fire, and each party settled in its abode, "God promised you", followers, the Fire, and I promised you help, then failed my promise to you, while God fulfilled His promise to you; "I had no authority over you", he says, I had no proof over you, in what I promised you of help, that established the truth of my word; "except that I called you", a disconnected exception from the first, as you say "I did not strike him except that he is a fool", the meaning being: but I called you and you responded to me. He says, except that I called you {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 560}

to obey me and disobey God, and you responded to my call; "so do not blame me" for your responding to me, "but blame yourselves" for it; "I cannot help you", he says, I am not your helper, "nor can you help me", nor are you helpers for me against God's punishment so as to deliver me from it; "I disbelieved in your associating me before", he says, I denied being a partner with God in what you associated me in, of your worship, "before", in the world; "for the wrongdoers is a painful punishment", he says, for the disbelievers in God is a painful punishment from God. One says "asrakhtu al-rajul" when you help him, "israkhan", and "sarakha al-sarikh, yasrukhu and yasrakhu", the latter rare, and the noun is "sarikh" and "surakh". The interpreters said the same: report 20641, from Amir al-Sha'bi, through Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna, Abd al-A'la and Dawud: on this verse "I cannot help you, nor can you help me; I disbelieved in your associating me before", he said, two speakers will stand on the Day of Resurrection, Iblis and Jesus son of Mary; Iblis will stand among his party and say this, and Jesus will say "I said to them only what You commanded me: worship God, my Lord and your Lord; I was a witness over them while I was among them, and when You took me to Yourself You were the Watcher over them, and You are witness over all things" [[passage/quran-5-117|5:117]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 561}

Report 20642, from al-Sha'bi, through Ya'qub ibn Ibrahim, Ibn Ulayya and Dawud: he said, two speakers will stand on the Day of Resurrection, one Jesus and the other Iblis; Iblis will stand among his party and say "God promised you a true promise", and Dawud recited until he reached "in your associating me before", and I do not know whether he completed the verse or not. As for Jesus, it will be said to him "did you say to people, take me and my mother as two gods besides God?" and he recited until he reached "You are the Almighty, the Wise" [[passage/quran-5-116|5:116-118]]. Report 20643, from Amir, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Ali ibn Asim and Dawud ibn Abi Hind: he said, two speakers will stand on the Day of Resurrection before the people; God, mighty and exalted, will say "Jesus son of Mary, did you say to people, take me and my mother as two gods besides God?" to His saying "this is a day on which the truthful benefit from their truth" [[passage/quran-5-116|5:116-119]]. He said, and Iblis will stand and say "I had no authority over you except that I called you and you responded to me, so do not blame me, blame yourselves; I cannot help you, nor can you help me", I am not your helper and you are not my helpers. Report 20644, from al-Sha'bi, through al-Husayn, Sa'id ibn Mansur, Khalid and Dawud: "I cannot help you, nor can you help me", he said, two speakers will stand on the Day of Resurrection; Iblis will say this, and Jesus will say "I said to them only what You commanded me". Report 20645, from Uqba ibn Amir from the Messenger of God, through al-Muthanna, Suwayd ibn Nasr, Ibn al-Mubarak, Rishdin ibn Sa'd, Abd al-Rahman ibn Ziyad and Dukhayn al-Hajari, who related the hadith: he said, Jesus will say, that is the unlettered Prophet; so they will come to me, and God will permit me to stand, and from my seat will rise the sweetest fragrance anyone has smelled, until I come to my Lord and He lets me intercede and makes for {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 562}

me light upon light from the hair of my head to the nail of my foot; then the disbelievers will say, the believers have found someone to intercede for them, so stand and intercede for us, for you are the one who led us astray; so he stands, and from his seat rises the foulest stench anyone has smelled, and then he is made to loathe Hell, and says at that, "God promised you a true promise, and I promised you, then failed you", the verse; the editor notes that the chain of this report has Rishdin ibn Sa'd, weak and abandoned, and Abd al-Rahman ibn Ziyad, rejected in hadith, that the report is of weak chain and does not stand, and that he doubts the word "yu'azzamu" and thinks it is "yuqattamu li-jahannam", that is, he is made to find it repugnant and frown. Report 20646, from al-Hasan, through Ibn Waki', his father, Sufyan and a man: on "I had no authority over you", he said, on the Day of Resurrection Iblis will stand as a speaker on a pulpit of fire and say "God promised you a true promise, and I promised you, then failed you" to "nor can you help me", meaning my helpers, "I disbelieved in your associating me before", he said, in your obeying me in the world. Report 20647, from Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi, through al-Muthanna, Suwayd, Ibn al-Mubarak and an unnamed narrator: on "Satan will say when the matter is decided: God promised you a true promise", he said, Iblis stood to speak to them and said "God promised you a true promise" to "I cannot help you", meaning avail you anything, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 563}

"nor can you help me; I disbelieved in your associating me before"; he said, when they heard his words they loathed themselves, and were called "God's loathing is greater than your loathing of yourselves", the verse [[passage/quran-40-10|40:10]]. Report 20648, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "I cannot help you, nor can you help me", I am not your helper, nor are you my helpers; and "I disbelieved in your associating me before", he says, I disobeyed God before you. Report 20649, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd: "I cannot help you, nor can you help me; I disbelieved in your associating me before", he said, this is Iblis's saying on the Day of Resurrection, saying you do not benefit me and I do not benefit you; "I disbelieved in your associating me before", he said, his "partnering" is his being worshipped. Reports 20650 to 20653, from Mujahid: "bi-musrikhiyya", he said, my helpers. Report 20654, from al-Rabi' ibn Anas, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Abu Ja'far al-Razi: I am not your deliverer and you are not my deliverers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 564}

Report 20655, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: he said, the evil speaker, truthful Iblis; have you seen a truthful one whose truth did not benefit him? "God promised you a true promise, and I promised you, then failed you; I had no authority over you" by which I might subdue you, "except that I called you and you responded to me", he said, you obeyed me, "so do not blame me, blame yourselves" when you obeyed me; "I cannot help you", I am neither your helper nor your rescuer; "nor can you help me", you are neither my helpers nor rescuers, nor for Me; "I disbelieved in your associating me before; for the wrongdoers is a painful punishment". Report 20656, from Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi, through al-Muthanna, Suwayd, Ibn al-Mubarak, al-Hakam and Umar ibn Abi Layla: "Satan will say when the matter is decided", he said, Iblis stood at that, meaning when the people of Hell said "it is the same for us whether we are impatient or patient, we have no way of escape", and spoke to them, saying "God promised you a true promise, and I promised you, then failed you" to "I cannot help you", meaning avail you anything, "nor can you help me; I disbelieved in your associating me before". He said, when they heard his words they loathed themselves, and were called "God's loathing is greater than your loathing", the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 565}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
