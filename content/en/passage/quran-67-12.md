## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 13 as a single section. He explains His saying, “Indeed, those who fear their Lord unseen will have forgiveness and a great reward”: Those who fear their Lord unseen, he says: and they have not seen Him, “will have forgiveness,” he says: they will have pardon from God for their sins, “and a great reward,” he says: and a great reward from God for their fear of Him unseen. And His saying: “Whether you conceal your speech or make it public,” He, may His praise be exalted, says: Conceal your speech and words, O people, or make it public and reveal it. “He is All-Knowing of what is in the hearts,” He says: He is All-Knowing of the secrets of the hearts that have not been spoken, so how about what has been spoken and uttered, whether it was concealed or made public, because He who is not unaware of the secrets of the hearts is more likely to not be unaware of anything else.
