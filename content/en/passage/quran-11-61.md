**Commentary on verse 61 (p. 368)**

On [[passage/quran-11-61|11:61]] al-Tabari says: We sent to Thamud their brother Salih, who said to them, my people, worship God alone, without partner, and make worship pure for Him apart from other gods; you have no god but He who deserves worship from you, and divinity is allowed only to Him; 'He brought you into being from the earth' means He began your creation from the earth, said because He created Adam from the earth, so the address came to them as it was His act toward the one from whom they came; 'and made you settle in it' means made you its inhabitants, so the sense is that He lodged you in it during your lives, from the saying 'so-and-so gave so-and-so his house for life, and it is his 'umra'. The editor explains the 'umra' as the practice of the Jahiliyya of giving a house for life, which returned to its owner at the grantee's death; the commentary on the verse continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 368}
