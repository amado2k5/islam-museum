## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 1 to 3 together in one place. Al-Tabari refers in 'Tasm' to the previous explanation of their interpretation and the differences among interpreters regarding it, and interprets 'these are the verses of the clear Book' as the verses of the Book that God revealed to His Prophet, the clear one who came from Him and did not say it.
