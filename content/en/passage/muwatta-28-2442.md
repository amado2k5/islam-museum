## Summary
Malik transmits from Thawr ibn Zayd al-Dili that Umar ibn al-Khattab consulted about the wine a man drinks. Ali ibn Abi Talib said: we think you should flog him eighty lashes, for when he drinks he becomes intoxicated, when intoxicated he raves, and when he raves he slanders. Umar flogged eighty lashes for wine. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.409}
