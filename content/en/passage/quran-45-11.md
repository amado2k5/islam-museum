## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "This is guidance, and those who disbelieved in the verses of their Lord will have a painful punishment of a painful falsehood": This Qur'an, which We revealed to Muhammad is guidance, says: clarification and evidence of the truth, guiding to a straight path, whoever follows it and acts upon it. "And those who disbelieved in the signs of their Lord" means: those who denied the verses in the Qur'an that indicate the truth, and did not believe in them, and acted upon them, will have a painful punishment on the Day of Resurrection.
