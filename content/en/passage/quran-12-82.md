**Commentary on verse 82 (pp. 212-213)**

On "ask the village where we were and the caravan with which we came; we are truthful", al-Tabari says: if you suspect us and do not believe what we say, that your son stole, "ask the village where we were", which is Egypt, that is, ask those of its people who live there, "and the caravan with which we came", the convoy we were in and came from with it, about the news of your son and the truth of what we told you of his theft, for you will learn the confirmation of it, "and we are truthful" in what we told you of his case. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "al-'ir" and explains the verbal noun of "saraqa". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 212}

The interpreters said the same: report 19641, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "the village where we were" is Egypt. Report 19642, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: they mean Egypt. Report 19643, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: Rubil had understood, in how his words came back to him from his brothers, that they were under suspicion with their father because of what they had done to Joseph, and their saying to him "ask the village where we were and the caravan with which we came" meant that they know what we know and witnessed what we witnessed, if you do not believe us, "and we are truthful". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 213}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
