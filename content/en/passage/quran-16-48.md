## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Have they not seen what Allah has created, covering its shade from the right and left sides, in prostration to Allah while they are humbled": The reciters differed in their reading, and the majority of the reciters of the Hijaz, Medina, and Basra read it as "or they did not see" with a yaa as a predicate about those who plotted evils. Some of the Kufan reciters read this as "or you did not see" with a taa on the letter. The first of the two readings, in my opinion, is the correct reading of those who read with the ya' as a predicate about those who plotted evils, because that is in the context of their stories and the reports about them, followed by the report about their abandonment of God's proof against them and their refusal to consider and consider their evidences. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, and Ibn Jurayj.
