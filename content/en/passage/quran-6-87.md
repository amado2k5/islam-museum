## Summary
Verse 87 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 87 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "and of their fathers, their offspring and their brothers; we chose them and guided them to a straight path" al-Tabari says: and we guided also, of the fathers of these whom he named, "their offspring and their brothers", others besides them whom he did not name, to the truth and the pure religion in which there is no association, and gave them success in it; "we chose them" says: we selected them for our religion and for conveying our message to those we sent them to, like those we chose among those we named. One says "so-and-so chose for himself such a thing" when he selects and prefers it. 13516 and 13517, from Mujahid: "we chose them" means we made them sincere; "we guided them to a straight path" says: we directed them and guided them to a way that is not crooked, which is God's religion in which there is no crookedness, Islam, which God approved for his prophets and commanded his servants. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 513}

The editor refers for "chose" and "the straight path" to earlier passages.

Verse 87 closes here on p. 513, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-88|6:88]].
