## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 104 to 105 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who do not believe in the signs of Allah, Allah will not guide them, and they will have a painful punishment": Allah says: Indeed, those who do not believe in Allah's proofs and proofs and believe what they indicate, "Allah will not guide them," meaning: Allah will not guide them to the truth nor guide them to the right path in this world, "and for them will be in the Hereafter." And with Allah, if they are brought upon on the Day of Resurrection, they will face a painful and painful punishment. Then Allah informed the polytheists who said to the Prophet: You are only fabricators, that they are the people of fabrication and lying, not the Prophet of Allah and the believers in him. His Prophet and his companions disassociated themselves from this, saying: Lies are only fabricated and falsehood is said, those who do not believe in Allah's proofs and known sources because they do not hope for reward for truthfulness nor fear punishment for lying. They are the people of slanderers and fabricators of lies, not those who hope from Allah for great reward for truthfulness and fear severe punishment for lying.
