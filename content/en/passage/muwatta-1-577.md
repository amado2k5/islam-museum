## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on practice in supplication. Yahya transmits from Malik from Abdullah ibn Dinar, who said: Abdullah ibn Umar saw me supplicating with two fingers pointed, one from each hand, and forbade me. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.298}
