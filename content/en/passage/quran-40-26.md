## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Pharaoh said, 'Kill Moses and let him call upon his Lord. Indeed, I fear that he will change your religion or that corruption may appear on earth.'" He said, to fill it out: "Let me kill Moses and let him call upon his Lord," who claims to have sent him to us and prevent him from us. "I fear that he will change your religion," he says: I fear that he will change your religion by his magic. Reciters differed in the interpretation of his phrase: "or that corruption appears on earth." The majority of the reciters of Medina, Syria, and Basra read this as: "and that corruption appears on the earth" without an alif, and similarly in the Qurans of the people of Medina. The majority of Kufa's reciters read it as "or" with an alif, and similarly in their Qur'ans, "it appears in the land" with a fatha on the yaa and the nominative of corruption. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
