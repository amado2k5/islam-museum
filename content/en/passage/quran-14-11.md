**Commentary on verse 11 (pp. 537-538)**

On "their messengers said to them: we are only human like you, but God favours whom He wills of His servants; and it is not for us to bring you an authority except by God's leave; and in God let the believers put their trust", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: the messengers said to the nations to whom the messengers came, "we are only human like you", you spoke truly in your saying "you are only human like us", for we are only human beings of the children of Adam, men like you, "but God favours whom He wills of His servants", he says, God bestows favour on whom He wills of His creation, guiding him, enabling him to the truth and preferring him over many of His creatures; "and it is not for us to bring you an authority", he says, it is not for us to bring you a proof and demonstration of what we call you to, "except by God's leave", he says, except by God's command to us to do so; "and in God let the believers put their trust", he says, and let whoever believes in Him and obeys Him have confidence in Him, for we have confidence in Him and put our trust in Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 537}

Report 20610m, from Mujahid, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "so bring us a clear authority", he said, the "clear authority" is the proof and the evidence, and of "of which He sent down no authority" [[passage/quran-3-151|3:151]] he said, evidence and proof. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 538}
