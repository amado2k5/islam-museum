## Summary
Malik was asked whether a man may cut grass for his mount from the sanctuary; he said no. The editor notes that he probably said this because of the saying of the Prophet that the trees of the sanctuary may not be cut and its grass may not be mown. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.568}
