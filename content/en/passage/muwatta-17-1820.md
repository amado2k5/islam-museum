## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr that his grandfather Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm sold the fruit of an orchard called al-Afraq for four thousand dirhams, and kept back dates to the value of eight hundred dirhams. The editor notes that al-Afraq is a place in Medina. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.144}
