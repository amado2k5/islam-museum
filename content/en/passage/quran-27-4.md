## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 5 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who do not believe in the Hereafter—We have made their deeds attractive to them, and they are blind." He means: Those who do not believe in the Hereafter, the Resurrection, and the return to God after death, reward, and punishment—"We have made their deeds attractive to them," meaning: We have made their evil deeds beloved to them, and We have made that easy for them, "so they are blind." He means: They hesitate in the misguidance of their evil deeds which We have adorned for them, thinking they are doing good. And His saying: "Those are the ones who will have the worst punishment," Allah Almighty says: Those who do not believe in the Hereafter will have the worst punishment in this world, and they are the ones who were killed at Badr among the polytheists of Quraysh. "And in the Hereafter, they will be the greatest losers," meaning: on the Day of Resurrection, they will be the most successful in trade and have made it worse by buying misguidance with guidance. "So their trade did not profit, nor were they guided."
