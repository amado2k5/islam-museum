## Summary
Malik says of the woman who enters ihram for the lesser pilgrimage and then enters Mecca at the time of the pilgrimage while menstruating and unable to circumambulate: if she fears missing it, she enters ihram for the pilgrimage and offers, and she is like one who combined; one circumambulation suffices for her. A menstruating woman who has already circumambulated the House and prayed before she menstruated runs between al-Safa and al-Marwa, stands at Arafat and Muzdalifa, and stones the pillars, except that she does not depart until she is clean from her menses. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.550}
