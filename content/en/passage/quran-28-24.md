## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So he gave water for them, then turned to the shade and said, 'My Lord, indeed I have not sent down to me any good that is poor": Moses gave the two women their livestock to drink, then he went to the shade of a tree he mentioned was dark-skinned.
