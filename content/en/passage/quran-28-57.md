## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "And they said, 'If we follow the guidance with you, we will be snatched from our land. Have We not established for them a secure sanctuary to which the fruits of all things as provision from Us are gathered? But most of them do not know": The disbelievers of Quraysh said: If we follow the truth that you brought us with you and disown the equals and gods, people will snatch us from our land unanimously against us and our war. Allah says to His Prophet: Say, "Or have We not established for them a sanctuary?" He means: Or have We not set foot in a city for them where we forbade people from shedding blood, prevented them from mistreating its inhabitants, and protected its people from being raided, killed, or taken captive? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
