## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "and cut off their affairs among them, all to Us we shall return": the people became divided in their religion which Allah commanded them and called them to, forming in it in groups, so the Jews became secluded, the Christians converted, and idols worshipped. Then He informed the Exalted is His praise about what they are about to turn to, and that the reference of all the people of religions to Him threatens the people of deviation and misguidance, and He taught them that He is in the vigil for them, and that He is metaphorical for all of them the reward of the good for his kindness and the wrongdoer for his wrongdoing. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
