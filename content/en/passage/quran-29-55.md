## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "On the Day when the punishment will envelop them from above them and from beneath their feet, and He will say, 'Taste what you used to do': 'And indeed, Hell will encompass the disbelievers.' The Day the punishment will envelop the disbelievers from above them in Hell and from beneath their feet." As narrated by Qatadah: "On the Day the punishment will envelop them from above them and from beneath their feet": meaning in the Fire. And His saying: "And He says, 'Taste what you used to do,' says: And Allah says to them: Taste what you used to do in this world of disobedience to Allah and what displeases Him therein." And with the yaa in "wa waqawī tasteu," most of the reciters of the cities read it except Abu Ja'far and Abu Amr, for they read it with the nūn: "and we say." And the reading, which is the reading with the ya, is due to the consensus of the scholars on it.
