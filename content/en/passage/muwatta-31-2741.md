## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Maamar, from Abu al-Hubab Said ibn Yasar, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: God, blessed and exalted, will say on the Day of Resurrection: where are those who love one another for My majesty? Today I will shade them in My shade, on a day when there is no shade but Mine. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.542}
