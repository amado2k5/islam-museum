## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4-7 as a single section. He explains the phrase “He frowned and turned away”: God Almighty means by “He frowned” that he drew his face in displeasure, and “turned away” that he averted his gaze. “Because the blind man came to him” means because the blind man came to him. It has been reported that some reciters lengthened the alif in “because the blind man came to him,” saying “because the blind man came to him,” as if the meaning for them was: “Because the blind man came to him, he frowned and turned away?” Similarly, some reciters read “because he was wealthy and had sons” with a lengthened or shortened alif in “because he came to him.” It has been reported that the blind man mentioned by God in this verse was Ibn Umm Maktum, for whom the Prophet was reproached. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
