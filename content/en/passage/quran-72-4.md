## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 6 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “And that our foolish one used to say about God an outrageous falsehood”: God Almighty informs us of what the group of jinn who listened to the Quran said, “Our foolish one used to say,” referring to Iblis. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
