## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And to Him belongs those in the heavens and the earth and those with Him; they are not arrogant in worshiping Him nor are they despised": How is it permissible for Allah to take a desire, when He has the dominion of all that is in the heavens and the earth, while those who are with Him of His creation neither refuse to worship Him nor are aware of their long service to Him? You know that He does not enslave the father of His child or his wife, and everyone in the heavens and the earth is His servants. How can He have a wife and son who says: First, do you reflect on what you fabricate about your Lord? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
