## Summary
A saying of Malik: among these also, a paternal aunt and her nephew die, or a niece and her uncle, and it is not known which of them died first. If it is not known which died first, the uncle does not inherit from his niece, and the nephew does not inherit from his paternal aunt. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.25}
