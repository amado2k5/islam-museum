## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the radiant bright-limbed, in the Book of Purity of Sahih Muslim. It makes the traces of ablution a sign on the Day of Resurrection by which the community is recognised at the Pool. {cite:sahih-muslim}
