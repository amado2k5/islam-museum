## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Know that the life of this world is but play, amusement, adornment, and boasting among you, and increasing in wealth and children, like rain whose vegetation the disbelievers admire, then it stirs its yellowish period and becomes ruin. And in the Hereafter is severe punishment and forgiveness from Allah and His pleasure. And the life of this world is nothing but the enjoyment of delusion." Know, O people, that the enjoyment of this worldly life is nothing but play and amusement with which you distract, adornment to adorn yourselves, and boasting among yourselves. Some of you boast about the wealth bestowed upon them from their wealth, "and they increased in wealth and children." Allah says: "And some of you boast to one another about their wealth and children." "Like rain whose vegetation the disbelievers admired, then it stirred." Allah says: Then that plant withered, "and you saw it yellow" after it had been green and fresh. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
