## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah set an example of a man who has quarrelsome partners and a man who is submissive to another. Are they equal? Praise be to Allah? Rather, most of them do not know": The example of Allah is an example for the disbeliever in Allah who worships various gods and obeys a group of devils, and the believer who worships none but the One Allah. Allah says: Allah sets an example for this disbeliever, a man who has partners. He says: He is among a group of quarrelsome owners, meaning they differ and dispute, with bad character. From their saying: a man of bad character, and each of them uses him according to his share and ownership in it, and a man is a man of peace with another. He says: a man of sincerity means a believer, a monotheist who devotes his worship to God, who worships no one but Him and does not owe anything else to Lordship. Reciters differ in the interpretation of His saying: "and a man of peace." Some reciters from Mecca and Basra read it as "a man who is pure" and interpreted it as meaning: a man sincere to a man. This was also narrated from Ibn Abbas. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
