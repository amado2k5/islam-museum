**Commentary on verse 87 (pp. 412-414)**

Al-Tabari says: these hypocrites, who when told "believe in God and strive with His Messenger" had their men of wealth ask your leave to stay behind from the campaign and from going out with you to fight God's enemies among the polytheists, were content to be in their houses like the women on whom the obligation of striving does not fall, who sit in their dwellings and houses; "and a seal was set on their hearts" means God sealed the hearts of these hypocrites, "so they do not understand" God's admonitions so as to take heed of them. We have already explained the meaning of the seal and how the sealing of hearts occurs, which spares repeating it here. The editor refers to earlier explanations of those who stay behind and of understanding. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 413}

What the interpreters said agrees with what we said on the meaning of those who stay behind. Report 17064, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abdallah ibn Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: the stay-behinds are the women; report 17065, from him, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: meaning the women. Report 17066, from Shamir ibn Atiyya, through Ibn Waki', Hubuya Abu Yazid, Ya'qub al-Qummi and Hafs ibn Humayd: the women; report 17067, from al-Dahhak, through al-Muharibi and Juwaybir: with the women. Report 17068, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: that is, with the women. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 413}

Report 17069, from Qatada and al-Hasan, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: the women; reports 17070 and 17071, from Mujahid, say the same, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl and Ibn Abi Najih and through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj; report 17072, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: with the women. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 414}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
