## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Urwa ibn al-Zubayr said of the children of li'an and the children of fornication: when one of them dies, his mother inherits her share in God's book, his maternal brothers have their shares, the remainder goes to the clients of his mother if she was a freedwoman, and if she was an Arab woman, she inherits her share, his maternal brothers have their shares, and what is left is for the Muslims. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.26}
