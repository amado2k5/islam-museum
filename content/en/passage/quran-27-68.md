## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 68 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "We have promised this to us and our forefathers before. Indeed, this is nothing but the legends of the ancients": We were promised this before Muhammad, and they promised it to our forefathers, but we did not see any truth for it, nor did we verify its authenticity. "Indeed, this is nothing but the legends of the ancients." He says: They said: This promise is nothing but what the ancients wrote down in their books, affirming it and speaking about it without any truth.
