## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 112 to 113 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'How many years did you stay on the earth?': The reciters differed in reading the phrase: 'How many years did you stay on the earth,' and regarding His saying: 'We stayed a day or part of a day.' The majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra and some people of Kufa read this as a report: 'He said, how long did you stay,' and likewise His saying: 'He said, if you stayed longer.' These interpreted the words by saying that Allah said to these wretched people of the Fire while they are in the Fire: "How many years have you stayed on earth?" They answered Allah by saying: "We stayed a day or part of a day." The wretched forgot, due to the great affliction and torment they were in, the length of their stay in this world, and the short duration of their stay was due to Allah's wrath that befell them, to the extent that they thought they had only stayed a day or part of it.
