## Summary
Malik says it is not proper to make an unborn foetus in its mother's womb an exception when she is sold, because that is uncertainty: one does not know if it is male or female, beautiful or ugly, defective or complete, alive or dead, and that lowers the price. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.130}
