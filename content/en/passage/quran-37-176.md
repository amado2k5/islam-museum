## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 174 to 177 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Will they hasten Our punishment": and His Prophet commanded that the coming of His time be presented to them. The interpretation of the statement is: Turn away from them, O Muhammad, until Our punishment comes and descends upon them.
