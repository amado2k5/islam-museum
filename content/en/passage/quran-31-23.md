## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "We give them enjoyment a little, then compel them to a severe punishment": We give them respite in this world a little respite in which they will enjoy themselves, "then We compel them to a severe punishment," meaning: Then We will bring them to a severe punishment against their compulsion, which is the punishment of the Fire. We seek refuge in Allah from it and from any deed that brings them nearer.
