**Commentary on verse 27 (p. 190)**

Al-Tabari says: then, after His punishment by which those of them who perished by the sword were destroyed, God favours with success in repentance and turning to Him whom He wills, that is, He turns in mercy to whom He wills of the living and brings them to His obedience; God is forgiving of the sins of whoever turns to Him in repentance among them or others, and merciful to them, not punishing them after their repentance or holding them to account for it after they turn back. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 190}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
