## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 89 to 91 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Just as We sent down to those who divided": the example of the affliction and punishment that God Almighty sent down upon those who divided the Qur'an, making it into pieces. Then interpreters differed regarding what was meant by His saying: "the dividers," and some said: He meant it. The Jews and Christians, and he said: Their division was that they divided the Qur'an and its members, believing in some of it and disbelieving in others. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Al-A'mash.
