## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We certainly entrusted Adam before, but he forgot, but We found for him no resolve": And if those to whom we are warning in this Qur'an go to waste, disobey My command, abandon my obedience, follow the command of their enemy Iblis, and obey him in opposition to My command, then their father Adam did that long ago. "And We entrusted him with a covenant," he says: "Indeed, we enjoined Adam and said to him: 'Indeed, this is an enemy to you and your husband, so let him not expel you from Paradise.' So Satan whispered to him, so he obeyed him and disobeyed my command, so whatever punishment was permissible befell him." And He, Glorified and Exalted, by saying: 'before,' He meant those to whom He informed that He sent the threat in this Qur'an, and His saying: 'So he forgot,' meaning: so he abandoned My covenant, as He said: Muawiyah narrated to me, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the saying: 'And We made a covenant with Adam before, but he forgot,' he says: so he abandoned. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, Sufyan, and others.
