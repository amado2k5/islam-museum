## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the general rules of the ritual bath: Abdullah ibn Umar used to sweat in his garment while in sexual impurity and then pray in it, which shows that the sweat of one in sexual impurity is pure. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Ibn Wahb in al-Bayhaqi, Ibn Mahdi in Ibn Abi Shayba, and Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.97} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.98}
