## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 17 in a single passage. He explains the verse "He said, 'My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself, so forgive me, so forgive him. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful.'" He says, informing about Moses' regret for killing the soul he killed, his repentance to him for it, and asking for His forgiveness for that: "My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself" by killing the soul that You did not command me to kill, so forgive my sin, conceal it from me, and do not hold me accountable for it and punish me for it.
