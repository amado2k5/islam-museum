## Summary
Malik transmits that Abu al-Zinad told him that an agent of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz took people for brigandage, who had killed no one. He wanted to cut off their hands or kill them, and wrote to Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, who wrote back: if you had taken a lighter course than that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.399}
