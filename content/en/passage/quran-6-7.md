## Summary
Verse 7 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 7 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

This is God's report to his prophet Muhammad about these who hold idols, gods and images equal to their Lord: how would they understand the signs, or how would they use God's proofs, signs and indications against the falsity of the unbelief they persist in and of their rejecting your prophethood, when out of their stubbornness against the truth and distance from right guidance, were the revelation I sent you, Muhammad, sent down to you in a written sheet they could see, touch with their hands, look at and read from, hung between heaven and earth as proof of the truth of what you call them to and of the soundness of the oneness and revelation you bring, those who hold another equal to me would say: this that you have brought us is only magic with which you have bewitched our eyes, without reality or soundness; "plain" meaning plain to whoever reflects and ponders it that it is magic with no reality. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 265}

A group of interpreters said the like: report 13073, from Mujahid, on "a book on a sheet, and they touched it with their hands": they touched it and looked at it and did not believe it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 265}

The editor refers for "sheet", "touching" and "plain" to earlier volumes 8, 10 and 2.

We stop here at the foot of p. 265, after report 13073 from Mujahid on "they touched it with their hands" in the commentary on verse 7, which continues on p. 266.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
