## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So when they crossed, he said, 'Give us our lunch.' Indeed, we have encountered from this journey a gift": "When Moses and his daughter passed through the gathering of the two seas, Moses said to his daughter," Joshua said, "Give us our lunch," meaning: We brought our lunch and gave it to him, and he said: Give us our lunch, as it is said: "The lunch came and I gave it," like gold and I took it. "We have encountered from this journey a gift," meaning: We have encountered hardship and fatigue from this journey. Moses said this after he crossed the Rock, when hunger was cast upon him to remember the fish and return to his desired place.
