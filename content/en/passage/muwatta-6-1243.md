## Summary
Malik says about a man of Mecca who enters ihram for the pilgrimage or the lesser pilgrimage and has pigeon chicks of Mecca in his house, which he shuts in so that they die: he thinks he should ransom each chick with a sheep. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.554}
