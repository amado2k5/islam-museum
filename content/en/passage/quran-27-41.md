## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They said, 'They denied her her throne; should we wait to see if she will be guided, or will she be among those who do not guide?": When Solomon came to the throne of Bilqis, the owner of Sheba, and she came to him, he said to his soldiers: 'Change this woman's bed.'
