## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from al-Saib ibn Yazid, that Umar ibn al-Khattab went out to them and said: I found the smell of drink on a man; he claims that he drank talaa (boiled grape juice). I will ask what he drank: if it intoxicates, I will flog him. Then Umar flogged him the full prescribed punishment. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.409}
