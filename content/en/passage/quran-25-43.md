## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have you seen whoever takes his deity as his deity? Will you be a guardian over him": He means the Exalted is: "Have you seen," O Muhammad, "whoever takes his deity as his desire," because the man among the polytheists used to worship the stone, and when he saw something better than him, he would throw it at him, and the other would worship him, so his idol and god would choose for himself. Therefore, He, the Exalted, said: "Have you seen whoever takes his desires as his god? Will you be a guardian over him?" He, the Exalted, says: Are you, Muhammad, a guardian of this in his actions despite his great ignorance? "Or do you think," O Muhammad, that most of these polytheists "hear" what is recited to them, so they understand or understand what they see from God's proofs?
