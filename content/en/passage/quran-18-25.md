## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. The interpretation of the phrase "And they remained in their cave for three hundred years and increased by nine": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase "and they remained in their cave three hundred years and increased by nine," some said: That is a report from Allah, the Exalted, about the People of the Book that they say so, and they cited the validity of their statement by saying: "Say: Allah knows best what they have stayed." They said: If that were news from Allah about a decree that they would have stayed in the cave, then the saying "Say: Allah knows best what they have remained in" would not have been understood, and Allah has already known He created it as a measure for their persistence in it and its value. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
