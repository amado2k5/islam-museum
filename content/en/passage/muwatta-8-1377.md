## Summary
Malik says that the matter among them concerning a woman's vow is that it is binding on her without her husband's permission, and it is obligatory and established if it concerns her own body and does not harm her husband; but if it harms her husband he may prevent her, and it stays owed by her until she fulfils it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.615}
