**Commentary on verse 76: "Do you worship besides God what has no power over harm or benefit", pp. 486-487**

Al-Tabari says: this too is God's argument for His prophet against the Christians who say what was described of the Messiah. God has him say to them: do you worship, besides God who owns your harm and benefit, who created you, provided for you, and gives you life and death, something that has no power over harm or benefit for you? The Messiah, whom some of them claim is God and others God's son, cannot ward off harm if God brings it upon them, nor bring a benefit God has not decreed for them. How can one with that description be lord and god? The lord who is worshipped is the One in whose hand is everything and who has power over everything. "God is the Hearing, the Knowing" means He hears their asking forgiveness if they ask it for what they said, and knows their repentance if they repent.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 487}
