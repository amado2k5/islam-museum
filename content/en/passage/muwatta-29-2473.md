## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he used to say: a woman's kin share with a man's kin up to a third of the blood-money; her finger is like his finger, her tooth like his tooth, her wound that exposes the bone like his, and her wound that displaces bone like his. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.422}
