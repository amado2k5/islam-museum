## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So those are their houses empty because of what they wronged. Indeed in that is a sign for a people who know": He means His mention by saying: "Those are their houses empty," so their dwellings are empty of them, with no one in them. Allah has destroyed them and destroyed them "because of what they wronged": Allah says: by wronging themselves, associating partners with Allah, and denying their Messenger. "Indeed, in that is a sign for a people who know." Allah says: Indeed, in what We did with Thamud is what We have narrated to you, O Muhammad, as a reminder to those who know what We did to them from your people who deny you. In what I brought them from your Lord and a lesson.
