## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 28 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So how when the angels take their souls and strike their faces and backs": And Allah knows the secrets of these hypocrites, so how could He not know their condition when the angels take them while they are striking their faces and backs? He says: Their condition is also not hidden from Him at that time, and by 'adrab' means miracles. We have already mentioned this narration earlier. And His saying: "That is because they followed what angered Allah," Allah says: The angels do what is described as these hypocrites because they followed what angered Allah, so He angered Him from obeying Satan. "And they disliked His pleasure," meaning: and they hated what pleased them by fighting the disbelievers after what He had imposed upon them. And His saying: "So He nullified their deeds," meaning: Allah nullified the reward of their deeds and removed it, because they were done without His pleasure or love, so they became nullified and did not benefit their doer.
