## Summary
Malik says: there is no good in two for one of one kind on a term. If the two kinds differ, and the difference is clear, there is no harm in taking two for one on a term. But if the kind resembles the other kind, even if the names differ, such as lead, tin, pewter and brass, I dislike taking two for one on a term. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.190}
