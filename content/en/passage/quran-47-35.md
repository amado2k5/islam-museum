## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So do not weaken and call to peace, for you are the superior, and Allah is with you and will never abandon your deeds": So do not weaken, O believers in Allah, in fighting the polytheists and be cowardly in fighting them. Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, "Do not weaken," he said: Do not weaken. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase "Do not weaken," do not weaken yeah. And His saying: "And call to peace while you are the superior" means: Do not weaken against them and call them to peace and reconciliation, while you are the conquerors against them and those who are superior to them. "And God is with you," it means: God is with you in your victory over them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
