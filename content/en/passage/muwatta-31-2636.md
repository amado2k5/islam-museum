## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, that a man came to the Messenger of God and said: teach me words by which I may live, and do not make them many, lest I forget. The Messenger of God said: do not become angry. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.492}
