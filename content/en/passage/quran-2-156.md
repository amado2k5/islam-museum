## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "who, when a misfortune strikes them, say: we belong to God and to Him we return" al-Tabari says: give good news, Muhammad, to the patient who know that all the favour they have is from God, so they acknowledge His lordship and His oneness, believe in the return, submit to His decree, hope for His reward and fear His punishment, and when He tests them they say we are the property of our Lord and our God while alive and His servants, and after our death we go to Him, in submission to His decree and contentment with His rulings. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 221} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 222}
