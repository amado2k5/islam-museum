## Summary
The first report in the chapter on the leniency of not performing ablution after madhi. Yahya transmits from Malik from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab: a man asked him whether, if he finds a wetness while praying, he should turn away. Sa'id said that even if it ran down his thigh he would not turn away until he completed his prayer. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted a similar report from Ibn Uyayna and al-Thawri from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Sa'id. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.83} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.84}
