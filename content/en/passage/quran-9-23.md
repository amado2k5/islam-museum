**Commentary on verse 23 (pp. 175-176)**

Al-Tabari says God addresses those who believe in Him and His messenger: do not take your fathers and brothers as confidants and friends to whom you disclose your secrets and show the weak points of Islam and its people, and prefer to stay among them over emigrating to the land of Islam, "if they prefer unbelief to belief", that is choose unbelief in God over affirming Him and His oneness; "whoever of you takes them as allies" means takes them as confidants rather than the believers and prefers to stay with them over emigrating to God's messenger and the land of Islam, "they are the wrongdoers", who went against God's command, put allegiance where it does not belong and disobeyed God in His order. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 176}

It was said that this came down as a prohibition from God against allying with kin who had not emigrated from the land of polytheism to the land of Islam. Report 16568, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: they were ordered to emigrate, and al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib said he was the waterer of the pilgrims and Talha, brother of Banu Abd al-Dar, said he was keeper of the Kaaba so they would not emigrate, and the verse came down, up to the words "until God brings His command", meaning the conquest, in His order to them to emigrate; all this was before the conquest of Mecca. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 176}
