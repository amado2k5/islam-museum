## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever does righteousness, whether male or female, while he is a believer, We will surely give him a good life and will surely reward them according to the best of what they used to do": whoever acts in obedience to Allah and fulfills Allah's covenants when he makes a covenant with any human being male or female "while he is a believer," he says: He is affirming the reward of Allah, who promised His followers to obey Him, and by warning those who disobey Him against disobedience: "So We will surely lead him to a good life." The interpreters differed regarding what Allah meant by the good life that He promised these people to live. Some said: He means that He will give them life in this world as long as they live with lawful sustenance. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, and Sufyan.
