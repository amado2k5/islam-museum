## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We certainly created man from clay": "And indeed We created man from clay"—We derived him from it, for lineage is derived from every soil, and therefore Adam was created from dust taken from the earth's crust. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Al-A'mash.
