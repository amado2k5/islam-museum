## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 90 to 92 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Take the Most Merciful as a son.'" These disbelievers in God said, "The Most Merciful has taken a son. You have indeed come with a [correct] thing." God Almighty says to those who say that of His creation: "Indeed, you have come with a great and reprehensible word." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
