## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, who said: Umar ibn al-Khattab was asked about locusts, and said: I wish I had a pouch of them to eat. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.522}
