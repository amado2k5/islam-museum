## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Ubayd Allah ibn Abdallah ibn Utba ibn Masud, from his father, that Umar ibn al-Khattab was asked about a woman and her daughter held in ownership, and whether one may be had after the other. Umar said he did not like to inform both, and forbade it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.45}
