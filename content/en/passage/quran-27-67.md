## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 68 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieved said, 'If we are dust and our forefathers, are we going to be brought out?'" He said: "Those who disbelieved in Allah, are we going to be brought out of our graves alive, like our appearance after our death, after we were dust in which we were afflicted?" He says: We promised this before Muhammad, and they promised it to our forefathers. We have not seen any truth for it, nor have we verified its authenticity. "Indeed, this is nothing but legends of the ancients," meaning: They said: This promise is nothing but what the ancients wrote down of lies in their books, affirming it and speaking about it without it being true.
