## Summary
Verse 68 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 68 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (pp. 529-530, notes 3-4).** Al-Tabari says that guiding them to the way with no crookedness means the upright religion of God, which he chose for his servants and laid down for them, which is Islam, and that guidance here means granting them success in following it. The editor refers to the earlier interpretation of the upright way (al-sirat al-mustaqim) and of guidance, and to the language indexes (page numbers scrambled in the scan). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 530}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 530}
