## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "And indeed, upon you is the curse until the Day of Judgment," meaning that if God's wrath upon you by expelling you from the heavens and expelling you from them will remain upon you until the Day of Judgment, and that will be on the Day of Judgment. He mentions that he explained the meaning of the curse in another place, which is unnecessary to repeat here.
