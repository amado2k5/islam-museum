## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "He said, O Iblis, why do you not be with those who prostrate?" Meaning what prevented you from being with those who prostrated to Adam, which is a rebuke and rebuke to him for his pride, after all the angels prostrated. It is mentioned that "in" in "will not be" is in the case of a lowered case according to some grammarians of Kufa, while for some people of Basra it is in the accusative case with the loss of the lowering participle.
