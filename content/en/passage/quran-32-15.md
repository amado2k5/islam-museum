## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Only those who believe in Our signs who, when mentioned therein, fall down in prostration and glorify the praise of their Lord without being arrogant": None believe in Our proofs and the verses of Our Book except the people who, when reminded of them and admonished, "prostrate" to Allah in humility, out of humility and submission to His greatness, and acknowledgment of His servitude. "And glorify the praise of their Lord" means: "And glorify Allah in their prostration with His praise," thus disavowing Him from what the disbelievers describe him as, and adding to him the companions, children, partners, and equals "while they are not arrogant." He says: They do this, and they are not arrogant in prostrating to Him and glorifying Him, nor refraining from submitting to Him and submission. It was said that this verse was revealed to the Messenger of Allah because a group of hypocrites used to leave the mosque when the prayer was established. This was mentioned by Hajjaj from Ibn Jurayj.
