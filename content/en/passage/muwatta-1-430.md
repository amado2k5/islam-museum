## Summary
The first report in the chapter on the screen for one who prays on a journey, a transmitted report. Yahya transmits from Malik that he was told Abdullah ibn Umar used to shelter himself with his mount when he prayed. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted a similar report from Ibn Jurayj from Nafi' from Ibn Umar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.223}
