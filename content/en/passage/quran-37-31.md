## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 34 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So the word of our Lord has come true upon us; indeed, we will taste it": The word of our Lord has come upon us, and the punishment of our Lord is due upon us. Indeed, we and you will taste the punishment because of our past sins and disobedience in this world. This is a report from God regarding the saying of jinn and humans, as in Qatadah: "So the word of our Lord is due upon us"... The verse, he said: This is the statement of the jinn. And his saying: In the segment, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
