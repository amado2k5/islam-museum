## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Were it not for the favor of Allah upon you and His mercy, and that Allah is Accepting of Repentance and Wise": Were it not for Allah's favor upon you, O people, His mercy upon you, and that He is accustomed to His creation with His kindness and patience, wise in managing them and His policy towards them, He would hasten to punish you for your sins and expose the sinners among you with their sins. But He concealed your sins and left your disgrace to you quickly, out of His mercy and favor upon you. So be grateful for His blessings and refrain from advancing from what He forbade you from His sins. Refraining from answering this is sufficient to know the listener who is meant by it.
