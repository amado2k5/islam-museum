## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Aisha, in the chapter on practice in the congregational prayer: the deed most beloved to the Prophet was the one its doer kept up. The editor notes that al-Bukhari transmitted it from Malik's transmitters, that it is in the Sahihayn from Abu Salama from Aisha, and in Muslim from Qasim from her, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.247}
