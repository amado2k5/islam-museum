## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not devote an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 35–36. Al-Tabari mentions that the arrogant people from every village said, 'We have more wealth and children, and we are not punished,' thinking that Allah only gives them to please them, and Allah responds to them by expanding provision and restricting it to whom He wills.
