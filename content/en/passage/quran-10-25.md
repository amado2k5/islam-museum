**Commentary on verse 25 (pp. 59-62)**

He then explains "God calls to the abode of peace and guides whom He wills to a straight path". God says to His servants: people, do not seek the world and its adornment, for its end is perishing and passing away like the end of the plants He gave as a likeness for it, but seek the lasting next life and work for it, and seek what is with God by obeying Him; for God calls you to His abode, which is His gardens that He prepared for His friends, so that you are safe from cares and griefs in it and secure from the ending of the delight and honour in it prepared for those who enter, and He guides whom He wills of His creation, granting him success in finding the straight road, which is Islam, which God made a means to reach His good pleasure and a road to His gardens and honour for whoever travels it. The editor refers to the glosses on "guidance" and "straight path". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 59}

Reports 17604 and 17605, from Qatada: "God" is Peace, and His abode is Paradise. Report 17606, from Abu Qilaba, that the Prophet said: it was said to me, let your eye sleep, your heart understand and your ear hear; my eye slept, my heart understood and my ear heard; then it was said: a lord built a house, made a banquet and sent a caller; whoever answered the caller entered the house, ate from the banquet and the lord was pleased with him, and whoever did not answer neither entered nor ate and the lord was not pleased with him; the lord is God, the house is Islam, the banquet is Paradise and the caller is Muhammad. The editor says this report is mursal and a similar one comes with a connected chain in the tracing of report 17609. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 60}

Report 17607, from Qatada: it was mentioned to us that in the Torah is written "you who seek good, come; you who seek evil, stop". Report 17608, from Abu al-Darda, from the messenger of God: no day dawns without two angels at its two sides calling, heard by all God's creation except men and jinn: people, come to your Lord; what is little and enough is better than what is much and distracts; and that was sent down in the Qur'an in "God calls to the abode of peace". In the note the editor says this report has a sound chain, that Ahmad gave it at greater length in his Musnad through Humam from Qatada from Khulayd al-Asri with the angels' further call, "O God, give the spender a replacement and give the withholder loss", and that al-Suyuti lists it in al-Durr al-Manthur; he sets out the narrators' standing and explains the word for "side". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 61}

Report 17609, from Jabir ibn Abd Allah: the messenger of God came out to us one day and said: I saw in a dream Gabriel at my head and Michael at my feet, one saying to the other, give him a likeness; he said: hear, may your ear hear, and act, may your heart understand: your likeness and your community's is that of a king who took a house, built a room in it, set a banquet in it and sent a messenger to call people to his food, some answering the messenger and some leaving him; the king is God, the house Islam, the room Paradise, and you, Muhammad, are the messenger; whoever answers you enters Islam, whoever enters Islam enters Paradise and whoever enters Paradise eats what is in it. The editor says it is a mursal report from Jabir which al-Hakim connected in the Mustadrak through Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Husayn from Jabir, calling the chain sound with al-Dhahabi agreeing, notes that Sa'id ibn Abi Hilal did not meet Jabir, and says he kept the manuscript's wording in the text. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 62}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
