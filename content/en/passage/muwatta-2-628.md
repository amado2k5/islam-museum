## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu Bakr ibn Uthman ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf that he heard Abu Umama ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf say: we used to attend funerals, and the last of the people would not sit until they were told to. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.320}
