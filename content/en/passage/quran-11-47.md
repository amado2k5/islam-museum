**Commentary on verse 47 (p. 352)**

On [[passage/quran-11-47|11:47]] al-Tabari reports to His prophet Muhammad Noah's turning in repentance to Him from his slip in the request he made of his Lord about his son: 'he said, my Lord, I seek refuge in You', that is, I ask Your protection from taking on myself asking You what I have no knowledge of, of what You keep to Yourself and have withheld the knowledge of from Your creation, so forgive me my slip in asking You what I asked about my son, and if You do not forgive it for me and have mercy on me and save me from Your anger, 'I shall be among the losers', of those who cheated themselves of their share and perished. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'seeking refuge' and 'loss'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 352}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
