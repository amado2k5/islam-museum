## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "But if they turn away, then We have not sent you against them as a guardian. Indeed, you have nothing but to convey [the message]. And when We let man taste mercy from Us, he rejoices in it. But if evil befalls them because of what their hands have put forth forth, then man is ungrateful." If these polytheists, O Muhammad, turn away from the truth You brought them and call them to it from wisdom, and they do not respond to you or refuse to accept it from you, then leave them, for We will not send you to them as a watcher over them, to record their deeds and record them. "All you have to do is convey the message," meaning: All you have to do, O Muhammad, is to convey to them the message We have sent you with. Once you reach them, you have fulfilled your obligation. "And indeed, when We make man taste mercy from Us, he rejoices."
