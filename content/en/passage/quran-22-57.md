## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 57 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And those who disbelieve and deny Our signs—those will have a humiliating punishment" as against Allah and His Messenger. "And they lied" in the verses of His Book and His revelation, saying: That is not from Allah; rather, it is a fabrication fabricated by Muhammad and supported by other people. "So those will have a humiliating punishment." He says: Those whose attribute is this will be for them with Allah on the Day of Resurrection will have a humiliating punishment, meaning a humiliating punishment in Hell.
