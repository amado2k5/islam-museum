## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they assign to Allah daughters, exalted be He, and for them whatever they desire": And among the ignorance of these polytheists, the wickedness of their deeds, and the ugliness of their temptation against their Lord is that they attribute to the One who created them, plotted them, and bestowed favor upon them. Thus, thanks to His blessings upon them, and He deserved praise upon them, "daughters." And Allah should not have a son, male or female, exalted be He. By this, Glorified and Exalted is He, He distinguishes Himself from what they attribute to Him and attribute of daughters, so they are not satisfied with their ignorance by adding to Him what should not be attributed. He should not have a son who would add to him what they desire for themselves and love for her, but they should add to him what they dislike for themselves and would not accept from daughters who kill her if she is theirs. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, and Ibn Jurayj.
