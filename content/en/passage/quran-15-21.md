## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains: "And there is nothing but with Us its treasures, and We do not send it down except in a known measure." He narrates from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud: There is no land more rainy than a land, but God approves it on earth. There is no year rainier than a year, but God directs and distributes it wherever He wills. It is said that rain is especially here, and from Al-Hakam ibn Utaybah: It rains for some people and forbidden others, and it may be in the sea. They have heard that more angels descend with the rain, counting every drop where it falls and what grows.
