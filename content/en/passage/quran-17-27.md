## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 28 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And give to the relative his due and the needy and the traveler, and do not waste wastefully": The interpreters differed on the meaning of the phrase "and give to the relative." Some said: It means the kinship of the deceased through his father and mother. Allah, Glorified and Exalted, commanded His servants to maintain this connection. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ikrimah, and Ibn Jurayj.
