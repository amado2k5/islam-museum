## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 18 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Allah who sent down the Book in truth and the balance, and you do not know, perhaps the Hour is near": "Allah who sent it down"—this "Book" means the Qur'an "with truth and balance." He says: "And He sent down the Balance, which is justice, to judge between people with fairness and to judge them by the judgment of Allah which He commanded in His Book." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid and Qatadah.
