## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 14 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By those who draw out violently”: Our Lord, may His glory be exalted, swears by those who draw out souls. Scholars of interpretation differed regarding this, what they are, and what they draw out. Some said they are the angels who draw out the souls of the children of Adam, and that what is drawn out are the souls of the humans. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-A'mash, Sufyan, and Ata.
