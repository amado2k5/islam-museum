## Summary
The first hadith in the Book of Pilgrimage and in the chapter on ritual washing for entering ihram. Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, from Asma bint Umays, that she gave birth to Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr at al-Bayda; Abu Bakr mentioned that to the Prophet, who said: order her to wash, then enter ihram. The editor notes that the hadith is relayed in the view of most Muwatta transmitters; some of them said 'from Asma that she gave birth'; and that Muslim transmitted it through a route from Aisha mentioning post-natal bleeding. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.433}
