## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "and prevented her from worshipping besides Allah; indeed, she was of a disbelieving people": and this woman from Sheba was prevented from worshipping "what she worshipped besides Allah," and that was her worship of the sun from worshiping Allah.
