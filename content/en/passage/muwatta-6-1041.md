## Summary
A report that Malik says reached him: the Messenger of God and his companions released themselves at al-Hudaybiyya; they sacrificed the offerings, shaved their heads, and released themselves from everything before they circumambulated the House, and before the offerings reached it; then it is not known that he ordered any of them to make up anything. The editor notes that the root of this report is in the hadith of al-Miswar ibn Makhrama that the two Shaykhs transmitted. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.484}
