## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 72 to 73 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "They said, 'We will not prefer you over what we have brought of clear proofs, and by what We have created, so judge what You are the Judge of; you only judge this worldly life.'" The magicians said to Pharaoh when he threatened them with what he promised: "We will not favor you," so we follow you and lie for you. Moses "regarding the clear proofs that came to us," meaning the proofs and proofs for the truth of what Moses called them to. "By Him who created us" means: they said: We will not prefer you over what has come to us of clear proofs, nor over what created us. And by His saying: "By Him who created us," he means that the phrase "And He created us" is reduced to "what came to us." It is possible that His saying: "By Him who created us" is a reduction in oath, so the meaning is: We will not prefer you over what has come to us of clear proofs, by God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
