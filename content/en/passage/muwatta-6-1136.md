## Summary
Ibn Abi Rabah transmits from Abdullah ibn Abbas that he was asked about a man who had intercourse with his wife at Mina before dispersing, and he ordered him to slaughter a camel. The editor notes this is in the Muwatta as reported. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.516}
