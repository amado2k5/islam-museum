**Commentary on verse 91 (pp. 245-246)**

On "they said, by God, God has preferred you over us, and we were sinners", al-Tabari says: Joseph's brothers said to him: by God, God has favoured you over us and preferred you with knowledge, forbearance and merit, "and we were sinners", meaning that in what we did to you, separating you from your father and brother and the rest of what we did to you, we were only in the wrong, meaning mistaken. One says "khati'a fulan yakhta'u khat'an" and "akhta'a yukhti'u ikhta'an". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 245}

Of this is a verse of Umayya ibn al-Askar, mentioning two emigrants who sheltered him and who had erred and sinned; the editor refers to its earlier source and notes that "haba" means sinned, from "hawb", sin. The interpreters said the same: report 19793, from al-Suddi, through Ibn Waki', Amr and Asbat: when Joseph said to them "I am Joseph and this is my brother" they apologised to him and said "by God, God has preferred you over us, and we were sinners", in what we had done to you. Report 19794, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "by God, God has preferred you over us", this being after he made himself known to them; he says: God made you a forbearing man. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 246}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
