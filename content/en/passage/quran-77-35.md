## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 40 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “This is a Day when they will not speak”: God Almighty says to these deniers of God’s reward and punishment: “This is a Day when they will not speak” – those who deny God’s reward and punishment.
