## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 46 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "and between them is a veil, and on the heights are men who know each by their mark": by "between them is a veil" He means between Paradise and the Fire there is a barrier, which is the wall God mentioned in Surat al-Hadid, and it is the heights of which He says "and on the heights are men". He gives (14671m) from Mujahid that the heights are a veil between Paradise and the Fire; and (14672) from al-Suddi that "between them is a veil" is the wall, and it is the heights. As for "al-a'raf", it is a plural whose singular is "urf", and any raised place of the earth is an "urf" to the Arabs; the cock's comb is called "urf" because it is higher than the rest of its body, and he cites a line by al-Shammakh about wild asses. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 449}
