## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to forbid kissing and intimate contact for the fasting person. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and Abd al-Razzaq (7423 and 7438) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.395}
