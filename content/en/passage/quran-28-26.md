## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "One of them said, 'She refuses to hire him; indeed, it is better to hire the strong and trustworthy.'" One of the two women for whom Moses was given water told her father when Moses came to him. One of the women was named Safur, the other Lia, and it was said: Sharafan as well. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, and Wahb ibn Sulayman al-Ramadi informed me, from Shu'ayb al-Juba'i, who said: The names of the two maidservants were Liya and Safura, and the wife of Musa Safur, daughter of Yathrun, a priest from Midian, and the priest is a scribe. From Ibn Ishaq, he said: One of them was Safur, daughter of Yathrun, and her sister Sharaban, and it is said: Liya, and they were the two who defended him. As for their father, there is a difference in his name; some said: his name was Yathrun. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-A'mash, and Ibn Ishaq.
