## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Aisha, in the chapter on the imam praying while seated: the Prophet prayed while ill, seated, and some people behind him prayed standing. He gestured to them to sit. When he finished he told them the imam is appointed to be followed: when he bows, bow; when he rises, rise; when he prays seated, pray seated. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.196} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.197}
