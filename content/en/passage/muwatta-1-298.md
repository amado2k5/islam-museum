## Summary
A report that Malik transmits as a mursal from Ja'far ibn Muhammad al-Sadiq from his father Muhammad al-Baqir, in the chapter on the Friday recitation and whoever leaves it without excuse: the Prophet preached two sermons on Friday and sat between them. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the Muwatta transmitters generally gave it as a mursal, and that it connects through established channels outside Malik's hadith. Al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi transmitted it from Ja'far from his father from Jabir, and it is in al-Bukhari and Muslim from Ibn Umar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.169}
