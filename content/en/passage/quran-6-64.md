## Summary
Verse 64 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 64 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "say: God delivers you from it and from every distress; yet you associate others" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: say to those who hold other gods equal to their Lord, when you ask them whom they seek help from when distress falls on them on land and sea: God, who is able to relieve you when distress befalls you, delivers you from the great trouble that comes upon you on land and sea, the worry of going astray and the fear of perishing, and from every other distress and worry, not your gods whom you join to him in worship nor your idols, who cannot benefit or harm you; yet after his favour in lifting the distress you set your gods and images equal to him and join them in your worship of him, and that is ignorance of what he is due from you, ingratitude for his favours, and exposing yourselves to the swift coming of his punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 415}

Verse 64 closes here, on p. 416, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-65|6:65]].
