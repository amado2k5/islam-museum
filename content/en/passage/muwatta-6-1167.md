## Summary
Amra bint Abd al-Rahman tells that she heard Aisha say: they went out with the Messenger of God five nights before the end of Dhu al-Qadda, and they did not think it was anything but the pilgrimage. When they drew near Mecca, he ordered whoever did not have an offering to release himself after circumambulating and running; so he came to them on the day of sacrifice with beef. Yahya ibn Said says he mentioned that to al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, who said that she had brought the report in its true form. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.527}
