## Summary
Verse 9 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 9 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "whose scales are light, those are the ones who have lost themselves, because they used to wrong Our signs", God says: whoever's scales of righteous deeds are light, not made heavy by acknowledging God's oneness, believing in Him and His Messenger and following His command and prohibition, those are the ones who cheated themselves of their share of God's great reward and honour, "because they used to wrong Our signs", that is, because they used to reject God's proofs and evidences, neither acknowledging their soundness nor being certain of their reality. He gives (14337) from Mujahid that "whose scales are light" means his good deeds. "Those" is said though "whoever" is singular in form, because its sense is plural; had it come in the singular, it would have been correct and eloquent. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "loss" and "wrong" and to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 315}
