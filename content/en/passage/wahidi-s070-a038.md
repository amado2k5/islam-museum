## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Ma'arij 70:38, narrated in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «أيطمع كل امرئ منهم أن يدخل جنة نعيم كلا».
