## Summary
A saying of Malik about the lesser pilgrimage from al-Tanim: whoever wishes to leave the Sacred Area and then enter ihram, that suffices for him, God willing; but the better course is to enter ihram from the station the Prophet set, or from a place farther than al-Tanim. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1133) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.467}
