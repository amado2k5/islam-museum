## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "those are the ones who bought error for guidance and punishment for forgiveness" al-Tabari says they took error and left guidance, and took what brings God's punishment and left what brings His forgiveness and pleasure; naming the punishment and forgiveness suffices without naming their cause; he has explained earlier the sense of "bought error for guidance". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 330}
