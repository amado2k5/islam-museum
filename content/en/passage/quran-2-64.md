## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "then you turned away after that" al-Tabari says it means you turned aside; "tawalla" comes from "he turned his back on him" and is then used for anyone leaving obedience, citing the verse of surah al-Tawba and a line of Abu Khirash al-Hudhali. He takes the sense to be that they left acting with earnestness on what they had pledged after giving the pledges, casting it behind their backs, "that" standing for all that precedes it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 162} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 163} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 164}
On "were it not for God's favour on you and His mercy" he says: had God not favoured you with accepting repentance after you broke the covenant, shown you the favour of Islam and His mercy, and passed over your fault, you would have been among the losers. He notes that although this addresses the People of the Book living around the Prophet's place of migration, it reports on their forebears, in the form used for those spoken of, as explained earlier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 164}
