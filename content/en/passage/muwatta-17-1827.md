## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger forbade muzabana. Muzabana is selling fruit on the palm for dates by measure, and selling a vineyard for raisins by measure. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.148}
