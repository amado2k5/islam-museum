## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 78 to 79 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Pharaoh led his people astray and did not guide": He says: Pharaoh and his people deviated from the straight path and took them on an unjust path, because he led them to the path of the people of Hell, commanding them to disbelieve in Allah and denying His messengers. "And He did not guide them." He means: He did not guide them to the straight path, because he forbade them from following the Messenger of God Moses and believing in him, so they obeyed him, but he did not guide them by his command to them, nor did they follow him.
