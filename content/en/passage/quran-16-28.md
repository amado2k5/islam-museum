## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those whom the angels take to death while wronging themselves, so they threw away peace. Evil was not what we used to do. Indeed, Allah is Knowing of what you used to do": Those who were given knowledge said: Shame on this day and evil are upon those who disbelieve in Allah and deny His oneness. "Those whom the angels take their lives" means those whose souls are taken by the angels, "wrongs themselves," meaning: they are in their disbelief and associating partners with Allah. It is said that he meant by that the one who was killed from Quraysh at Badr, and he was brought there by force. He said: Sufyan ibn Uyaynah narrated to me from Amr ibn Dinar from Ikrimah, who said: There were people in Mecca who embraced Islam but did not migrate, so he was forced to take them to Badr, and some of them were killed. Then Allah revealed about them: "Those whom the angels take to death while wronging themselves." And His saying: "So they surrendered to His command and submitted to Him when they witnessed death that had descended upon them." In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ikrimah.
