**Commentary on verse 62 (p. 44)**

Al-Tabari says: if these whom you were commanded to throw back to on equal terms if you feared treachery, and to make peace with if they inclined to peace, want to deceive you and plot against you, "God is enough for you", meaning God suffices you against them and against their deceiving you, since He has taken on Himself to make your religion prevail over the religions and has undertaken to make His word the highest and the word of His enemies the lowest; "He it is who strengthened you with His help" means God who strengthened you by helping you against His enemies, "and with the believers", meaning the Ansar. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of deceit, "enough" and "strengthened". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 44}

Report 16253, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: "if they want to deceive you" he said, the Qurayza. Report 16254, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: "God is enough for you" means He is behind that. Report 16255, from al-Suddi, through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal and Asbat: "He it is who strengthened you with His help" he said, with the Ansar. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 44}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
