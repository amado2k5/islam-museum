**Commentary on verse 93 (pp. 462-463)**

On [[passage/quran-11-93|11:93]] al-Tabari relates Shu'ayb's words to his people, 'my people, act according to your position', that is, your ability. One says a man acts at his own pace, meaning his deliberateness, and 'makuna' the man 'yamkunu' of power, rank and place; some interpreters said 'your position' means your places. The sense is: my people, act as far as you are able in the work you do, I am acting with deliberation in mine, 'you will come to know' which of us wronged himself, erred against himself, and which was right in his deed and did good to himself. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 463}

'Whom a punishment will come to disgrace him' means whichever of us or you, people, a punishment comes to, it humiliates and abases him, 'and who is a liar' means the liar in his speech and report, from us or from you, is disgraced too; 'and watch', that is, wait and look out, from 'ruqba', one says 'raqabtu so-and-so' I watched him; 'I am watching with you' means I too am watching for that punishment with you and looking to see on which of us and you it comes down. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 463}
