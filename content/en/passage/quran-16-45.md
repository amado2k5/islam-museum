## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Do those who plot evil deeds believe that Allah will cause the earth to swallow them up or punishment will come upon them from where they do not perceive?" He explains: Do those who wronged the believers among the companions of the Messenger of Allah believe, so they wished to tempt them from their religion, from among the polytheists of Quraysh, who said, when it was said to them, 'What has your Lord revealed?' The legends of the ancients, to prevent those who deliberately believed in Allah from suffocating the earth for their disbelief and polytheism, or that Allah's punishment would come to them from a place they do not perceive and from where they do not know? Mujahid used to say: Nimrod ibn Canaan meant this. And from Warqa all from Ibn Abi Najih from Mujahid: "Do those who plot evils believe that Allah will cause the earth to swallow them up"... Up to His saying: "Or seize them out of fear," he said: He is Nimrod ibn Canaan and his people. And he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
