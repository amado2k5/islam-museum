## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, “And those who disbelieved and denied Our signs - those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide eternally therein, and wretched is the destination.” Those who denied the Oneness of God and denied His proofs, arguments, and the verses of His Book which He revealed to His servant Muhammad, “those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide eternally therein.” He says: They will remain therein forever, they will not die therein, nor will they leave it. “And wretched is the destination.” He says: And wretched is the thing to which one is led - Hell.
