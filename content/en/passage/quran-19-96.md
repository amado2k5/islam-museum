## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 96 to 97 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who believe and do righteous deeds - the Most Merciful will appoint for them affection": Those who believe in Allah and His Messenger and believe in what has come to them from their Lord, and act upon it, and make lawful and forbidden what is forbidden, the Most Merciful will make for them affection in this world, in the hearts of His believing servants. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Jurayj.
