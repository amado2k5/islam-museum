## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Safiyya bint Abi Ubayd, mourning her husband Abdullah ibn Umar, complained of sore eyes and did not apply kohl until her eyes were nearly festering. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.116}
