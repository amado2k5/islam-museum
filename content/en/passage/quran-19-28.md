## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "O sister of Aaron, your father was not a bad man, nor was your mother unjust": interpreters differ on the reason why she was called 'O sister of Aaron.' And who was this Aaron mentioned by Allah and informed that they attributed Mary to his sister? Some said: She was called 'O sister of Aaron,' referring to her righteousness, because the righteous among them used to call him Aaron, not Aaron, the brother of Moses. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Samak.
