## Summary
It reached Malik that Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan entered ihram from the mosque of Dhu al-Hulayfa when his mount stood level with him, and that Aban ibn Uthman advised him to do so. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1070) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.449}
