## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when clear signs are recited to them, they say, 'This is nothing but a man who wants to turn you away from what your forefathers used to worship.' And they say, 'This is nothing but a fabricated slander.' And those who disbelieved in the truth said, 'When it came to them, this is nothing but clear magic.'" And when the verses of our Book are recited to these polytheists, 'clear proofs,' he says: They are clear and true from Us. "They say, 'This is nothing but a man who wants to turn you away from what your forefathers used to worship.'" He explains: They said at that time: Do not follow Muhammad, for he is nothing but a man who wants to turn you away from what your fathers worshipped among idols and to change your religion and the religion of your forefathers. "And they said, 'What is this but a fabricated fabrication?' Allah says: These polytheists said: What is this that you recite to us, O Muhammad, meaning the Qur'an, except a fabrication? He says: Except a fabricated lie.
