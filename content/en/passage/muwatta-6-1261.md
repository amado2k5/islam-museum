## Summary
Malik says that he heard some people of knowledge say: if one in ihram throws something and hits some game he did not intend, and kills it, he must ransom it; likewise the one not in ihram who throws something in the sanctuary and hits game he did not intend and kills it must ransom it, because intentional and mistaken killing are alike in this. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.561}
