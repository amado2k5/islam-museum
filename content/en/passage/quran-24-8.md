## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and they ward off the punishment that she testifies four testimonies by Allah that he is of the liars": He means that the Exalted is mentioned in His saying: "and the punishment is averted from her": and the punishment is averted from her. Scholars differ regarding the punishment Allah intended in this context that He removes her four testimonies. Some said similarly to what we said about this, such as the punishment for one hundred lashes if she is a virgin, or stoning if she is a married woman who has been married. Others said: Rather, that is imprisonment, and they said: What is obligatory upon her is if she does not witness the four testimonies after the husband's four testimonies. And 'ta'na' means imprisonment without the prescribed punishment.
