## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 48 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And when they were arguing in the Fire, the weak will say to those who were arrogant, 'Indeed, we were to you followers. Will you then be enriched from us a share of the Fire?'" He explains: "And warn them of the Day of Trouble, when hearts are at the throats tightened," "And when they were arguing in the Fire," he says: And when they quarrelled in the Fire. What I mean by this is: when those whom the Messenger of Allah was warned about among the polytheists of his people in the Fire are arguing. The weak among them, who are those who follow associating partners with Allah, say, 'Indeed, we were followers for you.' You say to their leaders who followed them in misguidance: 'Indeed, we were for you in this world followers in disbelief in Allah.' 'Are you then free of benefit?' Today, 'We have a share of the Fire,' meaning a share so you can lighten it for us. We used to hasten to love you in this world, and before you came to us. Were it not for you, we would have been believers in this world. We would not have been afflicted today with this affliction and following. It is one and a group, according to some of the Basra grammarians and some of the Kufa synagogues.
