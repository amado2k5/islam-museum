## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him and to parents in kindness." Whether one or both of them reach maturity with you, do not say to them 'uff' and do not repel them, but speak to them a noble word.' This means that by this, the Exalted is He, your Lord, O Muhammad, has decreed that you worship none but Allah, for no one else should be worshipped. The words of the interpreters differed regarding the interpretation of the phrase "and your Lord has decreed," even though their meaning is the same. He mentioned what they said about this: He said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, "And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him," meaning: He commanded. He said: A man came to Al-Hasan and said: He divorced his wife three times. He said: You disobeyed your Lord, and your wife became clear from you. The man said: God has decreed that upon me. Al-Hasan said, eloquently: What Allah has decreed, meaning what Allah has commanded, and he recited this verse: "And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him," and the people said: Al-Hasan spoke about predestination. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and others.
