## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who disbelieve will call out to Allah, the hatred of Allah is greater than your hatred of yourselves when you call to faith and then disbelieve." It means that those who disbelieve in Allah will call out to the Fire on the Day of Resurrection when they enter it, but they hated entering it themselves, when they saw the kinds of punishment Allah has prepared for them therein. It will be said to them: Allah hates you, O people in this world, when you call upon believing in Allah and then disbelieve. The hatred of you today is greater than your own self-displeasure because of Allah's wrath upon you. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Suddi, and Ibn Zayd.
