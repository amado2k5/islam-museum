**Commentary on verse 91 (p. 194)**

On [[passage/quran-10-91|10:91]] al-Tabari says God lets Pharaoh know the ugliness of what he did in his life and the harm he did to himself in his health, by persisting in his tyranny and disobeying his Lord, when he took refuge in Him at the moment His anger came upon him and His punishment fell, asking for protection from the punishment that struck him; for he called out when the waves of the sea were over him and the pangs of death covered him, 'I believe that there is no god but the One in whom the Children of Israel believe, and I am of those who submit', yielding in lowliness and confessing servitude. God says to him: now you acknowledge servitude to God, yield to Him in lowliness and give Him pure divinity, when you disobeyed Him before His vengeance came on you and angered Him against yourself, and were among the corrupters in the land who barred people from His path? Why did you not, while you had respite and the door of repentance was open to you, acknowledge what you now acknowledge? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 194}
