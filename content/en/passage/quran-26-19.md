## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And I did your deed which you did while you were among the disbelievers": He said: I did it when I was among the misguided. He said: Killing a soul. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. It was said "and did your action" because it is once, and it is not permissible to break the fa if this meaning is intended. It is mentioned from Al-Sha'bi that he recited that: "and I performed your action" with a kasra on the fa, which is a different reading for reciters from the people of the cities. And his saying: "And you are among the disbelievers" The interpreters differed on its interpretation; some said: The meaning is: And you are among the disbelievers in our religion. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
