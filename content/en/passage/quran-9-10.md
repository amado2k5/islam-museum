**Commentary on verse 10 (p. 151)**

Al-Tabari says: these polytheists whom God told you, believers, to kill wherever you find them would hold back, if they could, from no killing of a believer, respecting neither "ill" nor "dhimma", so do not spare them, believers, as they would not spare you if they prevailed over you; "they are the transgressors" means those who overstep against you to what is not theirs by wrong and aggression. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 151}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
