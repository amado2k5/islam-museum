## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 33 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We granted to David Solomon the best of servants; indeed he turned back": God Almighty says, "And We gave to David Solomon his son a son," meaning "Yes, the servant" means "yes, the servant Solomon" means he returned to obedience to God and repented from what he disliked. It is also said that it meant that he frequently remembered God and obeyed Him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
