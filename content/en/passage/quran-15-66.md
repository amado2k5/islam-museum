## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 66 and 67 in one passage: "And We decreed for him that the return of these people is cut off in the morning." God finished the matter for Lot and revealed to him that the last of his people, the first of them, was a spark uprooted by morning and night — according to Ibn Abbas the eradication of their destruction in the morning, and from Ibn Zayd it was revealed to him — and he explains in the grammatical "an" that it is in the accusative case in response to the command to decree its decree or the loss of the diminutive one, and mentions Abdullah's recitation: "And we said, 'These are the deceased ones in the morning,' and 'in the morning' when they wake up or when they come." And "The people of Medina came to rejoice": the people of Sodom—the people of Lot—came when they heard that a guest had been hosted by Lot, rejoicing at their arrival in their city, hoping to ride immorality—from Qatadah, who rejoiced at Lot's guests when they wanted to bring them from evil.
