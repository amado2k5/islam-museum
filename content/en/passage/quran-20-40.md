## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "When your sister walked and said, 'Shall I guide you to someone to guarantee him?' Then We returned you to your mother so that her eyes might be comforted, and she did not grieve. And you killed a soul, and We saved you from the mine and tempted you with trials. So you stayed for years among the people of Midian, then you came upon the fate of Moses." The interpreters differed in the interpretation of the phrase "And it will be done upon My eyes." Some said: it means: 'And you will be nourished and raised on My love and will.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd and Ibn Ishaq.
