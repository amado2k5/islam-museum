## Summary
The first report in the chapter on zakat on the property of orphans and trading with it. A report from Malik that it reached him that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: trade with the property of orphans, lest zakat consume it. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik, that it is reported connected and stopped on Umar through a flawed route in al-Daraqutni (2/110), and cites Talkhis al-Habir. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.342}
