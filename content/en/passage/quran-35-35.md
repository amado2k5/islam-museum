## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Who has made the abode of the Abode of Paradise untouchable, not touched by the stable, nor touched by any doubt": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of those who entered Paradise: "Indeed, our Lord is Forgiving, Grateful to Him who has made the abode of the Aboon." Meaning, our Lord who sent down this abode means Paradise. The abode of the Abode means the abode of residence, which is the abode of residence with no transfer from it, and the letter 'mim' is not transferred from the maqamah; if it is included, it is part of the residence. If opened, it is from the gathering and the place where it is stayed. The poet said: Two days: a day of stations and clubs, and a day of marching to the enemies as a reinforcement. In the passage, statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah are quoted.
