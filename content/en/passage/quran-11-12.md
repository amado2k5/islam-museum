**Commentary on verse 12 (pp. 258-259)**

On [[passage/quran-11-12|11:12]] al-Tabari says God speaks to His prophet Muhammad: perhaps, Muhammad, you will leave out some of what your Lord reveals to you to convey, of what He ordered you to deliver, and your breast will be narrowed by what is revealed to you so that you do not deliver it to them, for fear that they will say, why has no treasure been sent down on him, or why has no angel come with him to confirm that he is God's messenger. He says: deliver to them what I revealed to you, for you are only a warner, warning them of My punishment and My might for their disbelief in Me; the signs they ask you for are with Me and in My power, and I send them down when I will, and you owe only conveying and warning. 'God is guardian over everything' means He is the one who stands over everything and has its ordering in His hand, so go on with what I commanded you and do not let their asking you for signs keep you from conveying My revelation. Report 18007, from Mujahid: God said to His prophet perhaps you will leave out some of what is revealed to you to act on it as you were ordered and call to it as you were sent; they said, why has no treasure been sent down, we do not see money with him, where is the money, or an angel come to warn with him; you are only a warner, so convey what you were ordered. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 259}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
