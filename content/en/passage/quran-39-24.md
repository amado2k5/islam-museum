## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Is he who will fear the worst punishment on his face on the Day of Resurrection and it will be said to the wrongdoers, 'Taste what you used to earn": The interpreters differed regarding the description of how this misguided person feared the worst punishment on his face. Some said: it means that he will be thrown into Hell with his face crushed, as that is his avoidance of it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
