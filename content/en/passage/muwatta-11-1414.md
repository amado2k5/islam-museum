## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that he said: I struck two birds with a stone while I was at al-Jurf, and hit both. One died, and Abdullah ibn Umar threw it away. The other Abdullah ibn Umar went to slaughter with an adze, and it died before he slaughtered it, and Abdullah threw it away too. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.635}
