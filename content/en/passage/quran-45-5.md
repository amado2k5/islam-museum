## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And the alternation of night and day, and whatever Allah sends down from the sky of provision and revives the earth after its death and the direction of the winds, signs for people who reason": Allah says: "And in the alternation of night and day, O people, they will be punished for you, one with its darkness and darkness, and this with its light and light." "And Allah does not send down from the sky of provision," which is the rain by which the earth produces the sustenance and sustenance of the servants and revives the earth after its death: He says: So what rain sent down from the sky sprang forth from the dead earth, until it shook the plants and crops after their death, meaning: after their drought, drought, and fate, they were a cycle with no plants or crops. And His saying: 'and the discharge of the winds' means: and in His directing the winds to you once to the north, and to the south at other times, sometimes to the wind, and sometimes to the wind for your benefits. It has been said: by its directing it he meant mercy sometimes and punishment at other times. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
