## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: God laughs at two men, one of whom kills the other, and both enter Paradise: this one fights in God's path and is killed; then God accepts repentance from the killer, who fights and is martyred. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.592}
