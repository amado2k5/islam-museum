## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on wiping over socks: he was asked about a man who performed ablution with his socks on, forgot to wipe over them until his ablution dried, and prayed. He said he should wipe over his socks and repeat the prayer, but not repeat the ablution. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.79}
