## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8-9 as a single section. He interprets the words, “It almost bursts apart with rage. Every time a group is thrown into it, its keepers ask them, ‘Did there not come to you a warner?’” as meaning, “Hell almost bursts apart,” meaning it is torn apart and disintegrates, “with rage” against its inhabitants. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
