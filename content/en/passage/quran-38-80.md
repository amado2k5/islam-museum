## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 80 to 83 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Indeed, you are among the seekers': Allah said to Iblis: You are among those whom I have seen until the appointed day, the time that Allah has appointed as an end for its destruction." I have explained the time of that in the past despite the differing scholars on it, and He said: "So by Your might, I will mislead them all together." Allah says: Iblis said: By Your might, authority, and subjugation of those below You, "We will mislead them all," meaning: I will mislead all the children of Adam, and in this segment, he quotes from Qatadah.
