## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he used to say: if a tooth is struck and turns black, its full blood-money is due; if it is removed after it has turned black, its full blood-money is also due. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.432}
