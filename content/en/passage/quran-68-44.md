## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44-45 as a single unit. He interprets the words, “So leave Me with those who deny this message. We will gradually lead them on from where they do not perceive” (5:45): “O Muhammad, entrust the matter of these deniers of the Quran to Me.” This is similar to someone threatening another, saying, “Leave him to me,” meaning, “I will deal with him.” The word “whoever” in “whoever denies this message” is in the accusative case because the meaning is as mentioned. It is similar to the expression, “If you were left to your own devices, you would not succeed.” The Arabs use the accusative case for “your own devices” because the meaning is, “If I entrusted you to your own devices, you would not succeed.” And His saying, “We will gradually lead them on from where they do not expect,” means, may His praise be exalted, that He will plot against them from where they do not expect. This will be done by granting them worldly pleasures until they think they have been given something better in the sight of God, thus becoming more entrenched in their transgression.
