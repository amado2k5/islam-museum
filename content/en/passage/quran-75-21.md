## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 26 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Nay, but you love the immediate life”: God Almighty says to His servants addressed by this Quran, who prefer the adornments of this worldly life over the Hereafter: The matter is not as you say, O people, that you will not be resurrected after your death, nor will you be rewarded for your deeds. Rather, what has led you to say that is your love for this immediate world and your preference for its desires over the eternal bliss of the Hereafter. So you believe in the immediate life and deny the eternal life. As for Qatadah’s statement, “Nay, but you love the immediate life and neglect the Hereafter,” most people have chosen the immediate life, except those upon whom God has mercy and whom He has protected. And he quotes in the section statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
