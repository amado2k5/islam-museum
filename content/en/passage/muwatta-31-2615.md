## Summary
Malik said: I was told that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: a dwelling at Rukba is more beloved to me than ten dwellings in Syria. Malik said: he meant for long life and survival, and because of the severity of the plague in Syria. The editor notes that Rukba is a valley in the hills of al-Ta'if. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.477}
