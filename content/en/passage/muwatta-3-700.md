## Summary
A saying of Malik regarding a man who has sheep and goats: they are gathered together for him in charity; if there is among them what charity is due on, it is taken, because they are all sheep; he cites the letter of Umar ibn al-Khattab: for grazing sheep, when they reach forty, one sheep. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.351}
