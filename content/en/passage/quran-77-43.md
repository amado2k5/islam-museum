## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 45 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Eat and drink in good health because of what you used to do”: It will be said to them: Eat, O people, of these fruits, and drink from these springs whenever you desire. “In good health” means: There will be no disturbance or unpleasantness to what you eat and drink, but it will be constant and pleasant for you, causing no harm to your bodies. And His saying, “because of what you used to do,” means, may His praise be exalted, it will be said to them: This is a reward for what you used to do in the world in obedience to God, and for striving in what would draw you closer to Him.
