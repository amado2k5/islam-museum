## Summary
Verse 155 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 155 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "as guidance and mercy, so that they may believe in the meeting with their Lord", al-Tabari says: We gave Moses the Book as completion and detailing of everything, "guidance", meaning setting them upright on the straight path and showing them the ways of right so they do not go astray, and "mercy", meaning mercy and kindness from Us to rescue them from error and the blindness of bewilderment. As for "that they may believe in the meeting with their Lord", it means that giving Moses the Book was completion of God's honour to Moses for his good, detailing of his religion's laws, guidance for those who followed him and mercy for any of them who was astray, that God might rescue him from error by it, and that he might believe in meeting his Lord when he hears God's admonitions to His creation, desist from the disbelief in Him and in meeting Him after death in which he persists, obey his Lord and affirm what his Prophet Moses brought him. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "guidance" and "mercy". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 238}

Then, on "and this is a blessed Book that We have sent down, so follow it and be mindful, that you may receive mercy", he says it means this Qur'an that We sent down to our Prophet Muhammad, a blessed Book; "so follow it" means make it an imam that you follow and act on its contents, people. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "blessed" and "following". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 238}

We stop here at the foot of p. 238, in verse 155 after "so follow it" in "and this is a blessed Book that We have sent down"; the next batch continues from p. 239.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
