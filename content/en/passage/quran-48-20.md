## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 21 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Allah has promised you many spoils that you will take, so He hastened this for you and restrained the hands of people from you, and that it may be a sign for the believers and guide you to a straight path": Allah Almighty says to the people of the pledge of pleasure: "Allah has promised you, O people, many spoils you will take." The interpreters differed regarding these spoils that Allah mentioned promised to these people, meaning the spoils of war. Some said: They are all the spoils that Allah has granted to the believers from the wealth of the polytheists since this verse was revealed through His Prophet. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
