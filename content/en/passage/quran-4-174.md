**Commentary on verse 174: proof and clear light (pp. 427-428)**

Al-Tabari says that 'O people' means the people of all religions, Jews, Christians and idolaters: proof has come to you from your Lord, that is, a proof from God demonstrating the falsity of your religions, namely Muhammad, whom God made a proof by which He closed off your excuse; and We sent down to you with him a clear light, that is, which makes plain to you the clear proof and the paths guiding to salvation from God's punishment; that clear light is the Qur'an. Report 10858, from Mujahid: the proof is the argument; report 10859, the same chain, says the same. Report 10860, from Qatada: a clear evidence from your Lord, this Qur'an. Report 10861, from al-Suddi: an argument. Report 10862, from Ibn Jurayj: the proof is the clear evidence, and the light is the Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 428}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
