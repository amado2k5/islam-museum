## Summary
Malik transmits from a trustworthy man with him, that Abdullah ibn Umar entered ihram from Iliya. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1062), Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq (5073), al-Shaybani (386) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.445}
