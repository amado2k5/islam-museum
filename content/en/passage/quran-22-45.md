## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So how many towns We destroyed while it was unjust, for it is empty on its thrones, a broken well, and a built palace": And how many cities, O Muhammad, have destroyed its people while they are wrongdoers, meaning: They worship others who should be worshipped, and disobey those whom they should not disobey. And His saying: "It is empty on its thrones" means: its people were destroyed and deserted, and its inhabitants were destroyed, destroyed, collapsed, and fell on its thrones, meaning on its buildings and roofs. Also: from Juwaybir, from Al-Dahhak: "It is empty on its thrones," he said: Its emptiness means its ruins, and its thrones are its roofs. From Mu'ammar, from Qatadah: "It is empty," he said: a ruin with no one in it. Mu'ammar informed us, from Qatadah. Likewise. And His saying: 'And a well that is inactive,' Allah says: 'So how can it be from a town that We destroyed, and from a well We shut down, by annihilating its people and destroying its inhabitants, and then it was buried and inactive, so there is no one to enter or drink from it?' In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dahhak, Sufyan, and Ata.
