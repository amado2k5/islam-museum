## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who believe and do righteous deeds - We will admit them to the righteous": "And those who believe" in Allah and His Messenger and "do righteous deeds" are among the deeds, meaning that they fulfill Allah's obligations and avoid His prohibitions "so that We will admit them among the righteous" at the entrance of the righteous, and that is Paradise.
