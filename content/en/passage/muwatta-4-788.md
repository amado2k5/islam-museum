## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on one who resolves to fast before dawn. Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say: only one who resolved to fast before dawn may fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani (372) and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik, as in al-Bayhaqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.388}
