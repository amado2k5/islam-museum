## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And you think they are awake while they are asleep, and We turn them into those on the right and those on the left, and their dog spreads its arms with hunting. If you looked at them, you would have seen them fleeing and filled with terror." And you think, O Muhammad, these boys whose story We have told you about, if you saw them while We were striking their ears in their cave to which they took refuge awake. And 'al-iqadh': the plural of 'yaq', and from it is the saying of the poet: 'And they found their brothers awake, and the sword of ghiyaz for them is ghiyaz', and His saying: 'while they are asleep' means: they are asleep. And 'al-ruqud': plural of 'asleep', like sitting: plural of sitting, and 'al-qu'id' is the plural of sitting. And His saying: "And We turn them into the right and the left," the Exalted says: We turn these young men in their sleep once to the right side and once to the left side, as Qatadah said: "And We turn them into the right and the left side," and this is the turning in their first sleep. He said: It is mentioned to us that Abu Iyad said: They have two turning each year. In the Maqta, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Jurayj.
