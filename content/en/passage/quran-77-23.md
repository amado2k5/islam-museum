## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 24 as a single unit. He explains the phrase “So We decreed, and how excellent a Decider We are!”: The reciters differed in their reading of this. The majority of the reciters of Medina read it as “So We decreed” with a doubled consonant. The majority of the reciters of Kufa and Basra read it with a single consonant. The correct view on this matter is that both are well-known readings, and whichever the reciter chooses is correct. However, I prefer the lighter pronunciation, as in the phrase "they are indeed capable," since the Arabs sometimes combined both forms, as in the verse, "So give respite to the disbelievers. Grant them a little respite," where the heavy and light pronunciations are used. Similarly, al-A'sha said: "She denied me, and what she denied of the events was nothing but gray hair and baldness." It is also possible that the meaning is the same in both the heavy and light pronunciations. This is reported from the Arabs: "Death was decreed for him," and it was decreed with both the light and heavy pronunciations. The passage also includes statements attributed to Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and al-Dahhak.
