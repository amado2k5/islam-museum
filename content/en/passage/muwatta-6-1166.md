## Summary
It reached Malik that the Messenger of God said: at Mina this is the place of sacrifice, and all of Mina is a place of sacrifice; and about the lesser pilgrimage he said: this is the place of sacrifice, meaning al-Marwa; and all the paths and roads of Mecca are places of sacrifice. The editor notes that the hadith is reported connected from Jabir with a good chain. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.526}
