## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the forenoon prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Musa ibn Maysara from Abu Murra, client of Aqil ibn Abi Talib, that Umm Hani bint Abi Talib told him the Prophet prayed eight rak'as in the year of the conquest of Mecca, wrapped in a single garment. The editor cites Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi, Uthman ibn Umar in Ahmad and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.216} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.217}
