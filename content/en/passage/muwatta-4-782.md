## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Prophet said: the month is twenty-nine days; do not fast until you see the new moon, and do not break your fast until you see it; but if it is hidden from you, then estimate it. The editor notes the many transmitters of the hadith from Malik, including Abu Musab, al-Baghawi, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi (al-Bukhari 1907), al-Tahawi, Rawh ibn Ubada in al-Bayhaqi, and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Nisaburi in Muslim. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.385}
