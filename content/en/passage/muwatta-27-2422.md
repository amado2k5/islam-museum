## Summary
Malik said of one who steals what requires cutting, then the goods he stole are found with him and returned to their owner: his hand is still cut off. If someone says: how can his hand be cut off when the goods were taken from him and handed to their owner? It is like the drinker in whom the smell of wine is found, but who is not intoxicated, and who is flogged the prescribed punishment, because he drank it in order to be intoxicated; likewise the thief's hand is cut off for the theft from which he was taken, because he stole in order to carry it off. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.400}
