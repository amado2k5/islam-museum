## Summary
Verse 113 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 113 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari means by this: the disciples said, answering Jesus' words "fear God if you are believers" about their question whether your Lord can: we only said it and asked you to ask your Lord for us so that we might eat from the table and know for certain his power over all things; "and our hearts be at ease" means be calm and settle on his oneness and on his power over all he wills; "and we know that you have told us the truth" means we know you did not lie to us in your report that you are a sent messenger and prophet of God; "and we be witnesses to it" means among those who bear witness that God sent it down as a proof for himself against us of his oneness and his power over what he wills, and for you of your truthfulness in your prophethood. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 224}

The editor refers for "ease of heart" to earlier notes in volumes 9 and 5 and for "witness" to the language indexes.

Verse 113 closes here at p. 224, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-5-114|5:114]].
