## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 61 to 62 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And [mention] when We said to the angels, 'Prostrate to Adam,' but they prostrated except Iblis. He said, 'Should I prostrate to whom You created of clay?': And remember, O Muhammad, the persistence of these polytheists in their error and apostasy, rebelling against their Lord by intimidating them, their fulfillment of the words of their enemy and their father's enemy, when his Lord commanded him to prostrate to him, but he disobeyed him and refused to prostrate to Him, out of envy and arrogance: 'If you delay until the Day of Resurrection, I will surely have his descendants except for a few.' And how they believed his assumption about them, disobeyed their Lord's command, obeyed Him, and followed the command of their enemy and their father's enemy." And by His saying "And when We said to the angels": 'And remember, when We said to the angels, 'Prostrate to Adam and prostrate except for Iblis,' he was arrogant and said: "Should I prostrate to one whom I created from clay," meaning: to whom I made from clay. When the word 'min' was omitted, the phrase 'created' became accusative, and the ignorant boast about it that he was created from fire, and Adam was created from clay. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
