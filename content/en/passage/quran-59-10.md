## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who came after them say, 'Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and do not place in our hearts any resentment towards those who believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Kind and Merciful'": And those who came after those who took over the house and faith before the early emigrants "say, 'Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith' from the Ansar." He meant by those who came after them the emigrants that they seek forgiveness for their brothers from the Ansar. And His saying: "And do not place in our hearts any resentment towards those who believe" means overwhelming and resentful. It is said: He meant those who came after them as those who embraced Islam after those who occupied the house. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
