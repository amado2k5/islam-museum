## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Then on the Day of Resurrection, He will disgrace them and say, 'Where are My partners with whom you used to dispute?' He will say to those who were given knowledge. Indeed, disgrace of the Day and evil is upon the disbelievers." Allah did to those who plotted, whom Allah, Glorified and Exalted, described as His affair by hastening punishment for them in this world, and retributing them through their disbelief and denial of His oneness. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, He will disgrace them and humiliate them with a painful punishment, and it will be said to them upon their return to Him: "Where are my partners you used to joke with?" Its origin: Whoever challenges so-and-so is challenging me, meaning that each of them does to the other in a way that is difficult for him. In the Maqta, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
