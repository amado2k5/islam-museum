## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He hears the verses of Allah recited to him, then persists arrogantly as if he did not hear them, so he gives him glad tidings of a painful punishment": He hears the verses of the Book of Allah recited to him, "then insists" on his disbelief and sin, and he remains upon them without repenting, and does not turn away from him "arrogantly" before his Lord, to submit to His command and prohibition "as if he did not hear them." He means: as if he did not hear the verses of God recited to him due to his persistence in disbelief. "So he gave him glad tidings of a painful punishment," meaning: So give glad tidings, O Muhammad, this wicked liar whose attribute is a punishment from God for him. "Aleem": meaning one who will suffer in the fire of Hell on the Day of Resurrection.
