## Summary
Verse 118 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 118 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says: if you punish these who said this, by letting them die in it, they are your servants, submissive to you, unable to ward off what you intend for them or to protect themselves from harm; "and if you forgive them" by guiding them to repent of it and so covering them, "you are the mighty" in vengeance on whom he wills to take vengeance, none able to hold it off, "the wise" in guiding those of his creatures he guides to repentance and granting success to those he enables to the path of escape. Report 13037, from al-Suddi: and if you forgive them, you take them out of Christianity and guide them to Islam; and this is Jesus' word in the world. Report 13038, from Qatada: by God, they were no revilers or cursers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 241}

The editor refers for "servants", "forgiveness", "mighty" and "wise" to the language indexes.

Verse 118 closes here at p. 241, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-5-119|5:119]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
