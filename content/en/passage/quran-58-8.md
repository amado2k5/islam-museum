## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase: "Have you not seen those who forbade private conversation, then they return to what they forbade and engage in sin, aggression, and disobedience to the Messenger? And when they come to you, they greet you with what Allah has not made you alive, and they say about themselves, 'Why not Allah punishes us with what we say? Hell is sufficient for them, and they will enter it. What a terrible destination': 'Have you not seen those who forbade private conversation?' among the Jews, 'Then they return.' Allah Almighty has forbidden them from it, and they engage among themselves in sin. And aggression and disobedience to the Prophet. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
