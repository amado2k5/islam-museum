## Summary
A report of Jubayr ibn Mut'im in the chapter on keeping ties of kinship in Sahih Muslim: a general report that the narrator from al-Zuhri interprets as referring to one who cuts kinship. It is among the strongest texts forbidding severing ties. {cite:sahih-muslim}
