## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that he used to cut off the talbiya in the lesser pilgrimage when he entered the Sacred Area. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1121) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.461}
