## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He cast into the earth [mine] [that He cast] upon you [mountains] [that He cast] upon you [the Messengers]": And among His favors upon you, O people, is that He cast upon the earth My mountains, which are the plural of 'Rasiya', which are the fixed mountains on earth. And His saying: "To support you" means: that it does not support you, as in His saying: "Allah makes clear to you that you will go astray," meaning that you will not go astray. This is because He, Glorified and Exalted, established the earth with mountains so that His creation on its back would not be extended, even though it was a table before it was settled on it. Also: And from Qatadah, from Hasan, from Qays ibn 'Abbad: When Allah, Blessed and Exalted, created the earth, it began to grow up. The angels said: This is not a place on its back, so it became dawn and has its mountains. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
