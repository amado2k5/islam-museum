## Summary
Yahya said: Malik said: the matter among us regarding a man who steals repeatedly and is then brought to complaint: he owes only that his hand be cut off for all of those he stole from, if the prescribed punishment has not been applied to him. If the prescribed punishment was applied to him earlier and then he steals what requires cutting, it is cut off also. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.399}
