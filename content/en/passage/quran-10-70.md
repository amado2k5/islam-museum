**Commentary on verse 70 (p. 146)**

On [[passage/quran-10-70|10:70]] al-Tabari says: but they have enjoyment in this world, which they are given to enjoy, and a provision to reach the term in which their end is written. 'Then to Us is their return' means that when the term written for them ends, their destination and place of turning is to Us. 'Then We shall make them taste the severe punishment' is their burning in Hell, 'for their disbelief' in God in this world, denying His messengers and rejecting His signs. 'Enjoyment' is in the nominative through an implied word before it, either 'that' or 'this', and the editor refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 146}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
