## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They said, 'Indeed, we are spreading with you, but if you do not desist, we will stone you and let a painful punishment touch you from Us.'" The people of the village said to the messengers: "We are spreading our swords about you," meaning: We have become pessimistic about you, and if a calamity befalls us, it is for you, as Qatadah narrated: "They said, 'We are flying with you,' they said: If evil befalls us, it is only for you." And His saying: 'If you do not desist, We will stone you,' it means: If you do not stop what you mentioned that you were sent to us disassociating from our gods and forbidding our worship, we will stone you. It was said: I meant by that we would stone you with stones. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
