## Summary
Verse 26 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/battle-of-hunayn|Battle of Hunayn]]. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 26 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 26 (p. 189)**

Al-Tabari says: then, after the earth had narrowed for you for all its breadth and you had turned your backs to the enemy, God lifted the affliction that had come on you by sending down His tranquillity, which is security and calm, upon you; "and sent down hosts you did not see" are the angels mentioned in the reports already given; "and punished the unbelievers" means God punished those who denied His oneness and the message of His messenger Muhammad by killing, the captivity of their families and children, the taking of their goods and humiliation; "that is the recompense of the unbelievers" means that what He did to them of killing and captivity is the reward of the unbelievers, the requital of those who deny His oneness and the message of His messenger. Report 16588, from al-Suddi, through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal and Asbat: He punished the unbelievers by killing them with the sword; report 16589, from Sa'id: by defeat and killing; report 16590, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: those of them who remained. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 189}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
