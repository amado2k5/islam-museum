## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse in a single passage that includes [[passage/quran-84-10|84:10]] to [[passage/quran-84-15|84:15]] In summary, he mentions two readings: “yuṣallā” (يُصلّى) with a ḍamma on the yā’ and a shadda on the lām, as used by most reciters in Mecca, Medina, and Syria, meaning that God will repeatedly cast them into the fire; and “yaṣlā” (يَصلى) with a fatḥa on the yā’ and a light lām, as used by reciters in Kufa, Basra, and some Medina, meaning that they will return to it and be burned therein.
