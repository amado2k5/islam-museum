## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And you were not near Al-Tur when We called out, but as a mercy from your Lord to warn a people to whom no warner came before you, that they might take heed." Those who follow the unlettered Prophet..." The verse, as narrated by al-A'mash, from Ali ibn Mudrik, from Abu Zur'ah, regarding the saying of Allah: "And you were not near the Mount when We called out," he said: He called out, O nation of Muhammad, I gave you before you asked Me, and I answered you before you called upon Me. And from Qatadah, His saying: "And you were not near Mount when We called out," he said: They called out: O nation of Muhammad, I gave you before you asked Me, and I answered you before you called Me. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
