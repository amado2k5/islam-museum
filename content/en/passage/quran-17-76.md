## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And if they were to provoke you from the land, they would surely drive you out of it, but they would not remain with you except for a little while": Allah Almighty says: And if these people were about to provoke you from the land, He said: They would try to hide you from the land you are in to expel you from it. "But behold, they will not remain dissent from you except for a little while," meaning: even if they drive you out of it, they would not remain there except for a short while, until He destroyed them with a swift punishment. The interpreters differed regarding those who nearly provoked the Messenger of Allah to expel him from the land and the land from which they wanted to expel him. Some said: Those who nearly provoked the Messenger of Allah from that Jews, and the land from which they wanted to expel him from Medina. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
