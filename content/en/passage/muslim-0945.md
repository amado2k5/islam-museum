## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the reward of attending a funeral, in the Book of Funerals of Sahih Muslim: one qirat for whoever prays over the dead, and two qirats for whoever follows the body until burial. Ibn Umar said, "We have neglected many qirats". {cite:sahih-muslim}
