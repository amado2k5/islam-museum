## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who believed and their descendants followed them with faith—We joined them with their descendants, and We did not grant them of their deeds of anything—every man [for what he earned] as a pledge": the interpreters differed in interpreting this. Some said: It means: those who believed and We followed their descendants in faith, We admitted to them their descendants the believers in Paradise, even though they did not reach the ranks of their forefathers through their deeds, as an honor for their believing forefathers. And their believing forefathers did not give us any reward for their deeds. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Sufyan.
