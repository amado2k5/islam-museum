## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 23–24. Al-Tabari explains that God gave Moses the Torah just as He gave Muhammad the Criterion, so there is no doubt about meeting Moses. He mentions Qatadah's statement that the meeting was meant on the night of Isra, and he mentions that God made the Torah a guidance for the Children of Israel.
