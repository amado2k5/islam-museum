## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O mankind, if you are in doubt about the resurrection, then indeed We created you from dust, then from a drop of fluid, then from a clot, then from a clotted lump formed and uncreated, to make clear to you and to confirm for you the wombs as We will for a specified term. Then We bring you forth as a child, then you may reach the oldest of you. And among you is he who dies, and among you is he who returns to the most disgraceful life, so that he will not know anything after knowing it, and you will see the earth lifeless. So when We sent down upon her rain that trembled, nurtured, and grew from every joyful spouse.' Among you are those who die before reaching their full maturity, and among them are those who are forgotten in their term and live until they reach old age, then after reaching the end of their youth and reaching the peak of their maturity, they return to the worst of their age, that old age, until they return to their former state of youth, and beyond their original intellect, they cannot comprehend anything. The meaning of the statement: And among you is he who returns to the lowest age after reaching his maturity, "so that he may not know after knowledge," he used to teach him "something." And His saying: "And you see the earth barren," God Almighty says: And you see the earth, O Muhammad, barren, with traces of plants and crops studied.
