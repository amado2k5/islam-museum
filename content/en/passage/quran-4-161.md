**Commentary on verse 161: the firm in knowledge among the People of the Book (pp. 392-393)**

Al-Tabari says that 'We prepared for the disbelievers among them a painful punishment' means that We made for those Jews who disbelieved in God and His messenger Muhammad a painful torment, that is, the agonizing torment of hell, which they will burn in on the Hereafter when they come to their Lord, and He will punish them with it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 392}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
