## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "How many generations did We destroy before them, and they called out and turned away when they turned away": Many were destroyed by these polytheists from Quraysh who denied our Messenger Muhammad regarding what He brought them from Us of the truth, meaning from the nations before them, who followed their path in denying their messengers what they had brought them from God. "So they called out," meaning: They hastened to their Lord, and cried out and sought help for repentance to Him when the wight of God descended upon them, and they witnessed His punishment as fleeing from His punishment and fleeing from His painful torment. When it comes to repentance," he says: This is not a time to flee or escape punishment by repentance, for the word of punishment has been fulfilled upon them, and they repented when repentance does not benefit them, and they resigned at a time other than the time of dismissal. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
