## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And I sought refuge with my Lord and your Lord to grant you translation": "Indeed, I held fast to my Lord and your Lord, and I sought courage from you to be translated." Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of stoning from which Moses, the Prophet of God, sought refuge with his Lord; some said: it is insulting with the tongue. In this section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
