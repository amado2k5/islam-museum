**Commentary on verse 43 (pp. 331-333)**

On [[passage/quran-11-43|11:43]] al-Tabari says: Noah's son said, when Noah called him to ride with him in the ship for fear he would drown, 'I shall take refuge on a mountain that will protect me from the water', meaning I shall go to a mountain to fortify myself against the water so that it keeps it from drowning me; 'protect' means keep off, like the 'isam' of a waterskin, the cord that ties its top and stops the water from spilling. His words 'there is no protector today from God's command except whom He has mercy on' mean there is none today to keep off God's command which has come down on creation of drowning and destruction, except one on whom He has mercy and so saves him from it, for He is the one who keeps off whom He wills of His creation; 'whom' is nominative, since the sense is: there is no protector today from God's command but God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 332}

The people of Arabic differed on the place of 'man' here. Some of the Kufan grammarians said it is accusative, since the protected is the opposite of the protector and the one given mercy is protected, as the accusative in [[passage/quran-4-157|4:157]], 'only following conjecture'; they said the nominative is not allowed in 'man', but that if 'protector' were taken as 'protected' the nominative of 'man' would be allowed, and it is not denied that the object form comes in the active form, as in 'gushing water' meaning poured and 'a pleasing life' meaning pleased with. Some Basrans said it is 'but whom He has mercy on', or that it may be 'none having protection', meaning protected, with 'except whom He has mercy on' nominative as a substitute for the protector. Al-Tabari says there is no ground for these sayings, since God's speech is directed to the most eloquent and best-known usage of those in whose tongue it came down where a way is found, and nothing forces us to make 'protector' mean 'protected' or 'except' mean 'but', as we find for it in the well-known speech of the Arabs a sound route, namely what we said: Noah said there is no protector today from God's command except one who had mercy on us and saved us from His punishment, as one says 'there is no saviour today from God's punishment but God' and 'there is none to feed today from Zayd's food but Zayd'.
