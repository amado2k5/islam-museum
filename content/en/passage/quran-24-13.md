## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "If they had not brought four witnesses against him, then they did not bring the witnesses, then those are the liars in the sight of Allah": Why not come those group who brought the slander and slandered Aisha, with four martyrs who testify to their statement about her and what they accused her of? If they do not bring the four martyrs based on the truth of what they have fabricated, "then those are the liars in the sight of Allah." He means: the group whom they threw with that before Allah are the liars in what they brought of the slander.
