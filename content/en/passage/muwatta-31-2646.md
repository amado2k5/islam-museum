## Summary
Malik transmits from Ayyub ibn Abi Tamima, from Muhammad ibn Sirin, who said: Umar ibn al-Khattab said: when God gives you abundance, be generous to yourselves; a man then gathered his clothes on himself. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.498}
