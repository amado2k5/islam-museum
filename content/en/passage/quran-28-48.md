## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when the truth came to them from Us, they said, 'Why was I not given the like of what Moses was given? Didn't they disbelieve in what Moses was given before?' They said, 'Two magicians to show off,' and they said, 'Indeed, we are disbelievers.'" So when those who did not come before you, O Muhammad, came as a warner, We sent you to them as a warner: "The truth is from Us," and he is Muhammad with the message from God to them, they said, rebelling against God and persisting in error: Shall I not be given the one who was sent to us, who is Muhammad, like Moses bin Imran was given from the Book? Allah, Blessed and Exalted, says to His Prophet Muhammad: Say, O Muhammad, to your people from Quraysh, who say to you, 'Why had I not been given the like of what Moses was given?': Or had those who knew this proof among the Jews not denied what Moses was given before you? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
