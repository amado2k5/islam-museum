## Summary
Malik said of a contract of manumission slave whose master settles with him, so that he is freed, and writes what remains of the settlement as a debt on him: then if the contract slave dies owing debts to people, his master does not share with his creditors in what remains of the settlement, and the creditors have the right to proceed against him first. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.351}
