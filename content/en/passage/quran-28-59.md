## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And your Lord would not destroy the towns until He sent to their mother a messenger reciting Our verses to them. And We never destroyed the towns except that their people were wrongdoers": "And your Lord, O Muhammad, destroyed the towns around Mecca in your time and era." "Until a messenger is sent to her mother," he means: until a messenger is sent to Mecca, which is the mother of cities, reciting to them the verses of our Book, and the messenger is Muhammad. In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
