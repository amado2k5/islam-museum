## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And to Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. He forgives whom He wills and punishes whom He wills. And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." This is explained by Allah, the dominion of the heavens and the earth. No one, O hypocrites, can repel Him from what He intends for you of torment for your hypocrisy if you persist in it, or prevent Him from forgiving you. If He forgives, if you repent from your hypocrisy and disbelief. This is from Allah, Glorified and Exalted, urging those Bedouins who lag behind the Messenger of Allah to repent and return to Allah's command in obedience to His Messenger, saying to them: Hasten to repent from those who have stayed behind with the Messenger of Allah, for Allah forgives those who repent. "And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." He says: And Allah is always the One who pardons the punishment of those who repent to Him for their sins and disobediences, and He is merciful to them to punish them for their sins after they repent from them.
