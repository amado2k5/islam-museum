**Commentary on verse 64 (pp. 124-125)**

On [[passage/quran-10-64|10:64]] he says: the glad tidings from God in the life of this world and in the next belong to the friends of God who believed and were mindful. The interpreters then differed about these glad tidings and their nature. Some said they are the good dream that a Muslim sees or that is seen for him, and in the next life Paradise. Report 17717, from Abu al-Darda', that he asked the Prophet about the verse and was told: the good dream that the believer sees or that is seen for him. Report 17718, from Ubada b. al-Samit, that he asked the Prophet about the verse and was told: you have asked me about something no one asked me before you; it is the good dream that the righteous man sees or that is seen for him. In a note the editor lists the routes of the hadith of Abu al-Darda' and of Ubada, explains that the weakness of the chain of 17717 is the unknown shaykh from whom Abu Salih al-Samman related, and points to the later reports in which each route comes.
