## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: people of knowledge said that whoever has begun a pilgrimage single and then decides to enter ihram for the lesser pilgrimage after it is not permitted. Malik said: this is what we found the people of knowledge in our city following. The editor notes that Abu Musab and al-Shaybani (1080) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.452}
