**Commentary on verse 38 (pp. 103-104)**

On "and I followed the creed of my fathers, Ibrahim, Ishaq and Ya'qub" al-Tabari says: I followed their religion, not that of the idolaters; "it was not for us to associate anything with God" means it was not permitted us to give God a partner in worship and obedience, but we must single Him out in divinity and worship; "that is of God's bounty to us" means that my following the creed of my fathers in Islam and leaving the creed of a people who do not believe in God and deny the hereafter is of God's bounty to us; "and to the people" means that it is also of God's bounty to people that He sent us to them as callers to His oneness and obedience; "but most people do not give thanks" means whoever disbelieves in God does not thank Him for it, as he does not know who granted it nor recognise the Bountiful. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 103}

Report 19287, from Ibn Abbas, through Ali, Abd Allah and Mu'awiya: "of God's bounty to us" that He made us prophets, "and to the people" that He sent messengers to them. Report 19288, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: we were told Abu al-Darda' used to say, how many a person is thankful for a blessing though not the one blessed and does not know, and how many a bearer of understanding is not a man of understanding. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 104}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
