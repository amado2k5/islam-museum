## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse in a single passage that includes [[passage/quran-98-1|98:1]] to [[passage/quran-98-4|98:4]] In summary, the word “munfakin” (they who are separated) is interpreted as meaning they ceased their previous actions until the Quran came to them, or that they did not abandon the description of Muhammad in their scriptures until he was sent.
