## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 102 to 104 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So whoever has heavy scales, those are the successful": "So whoever has scales weighed down": the scales of his good deeds and the scales of his bad deeds lighten. "Those are the successful," meaning the eternal ones in the Gardens of bliss. "And whoever has a light scale" means: and whoever has a light scale, his good deeds weigh out. "So those are the ones who have lost themselves," meaning: they have deprived themselves of their hopes for God's mercy. "In Hell they will abide forever," meaning: they are in the fire of Hell. And His saying: "Their faces will be blazing with fire," means their faces will be scorched by the fire. As he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, who said: Ibn Abbas said: "Their faces will be blazing with fire," he said: They will be blazing. In the Maqta' section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
