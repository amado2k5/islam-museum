## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He said, 'I want to marry you to one of my two daughters on the condition that you hire me for eight reasons. If you complete ten, then from You. And whatever I intend to make difficult for you, you will find me, God willing, among the righteous.'" The father of the two women for whom Moses was given water for Moses said: "I want to marry you to one of my two daughters on the condition that you hire me for eight reasons," meaning: "On the condition that you hire me." On the condition that you reward me by marrying her to graze my livestock, eight reasons, from the saying: God rewarded you, so He rewarded you, meaning: God rewarded you, and the Arabs say: I rewarded the worker his reward, meaning: I gave him that, as it is said: I took him, so I take him. Some Arabs from Basra narrated that the Arabic language is: 'I rented my servant, so he is rewarded,' and 'I hired him' means 'I did it.' He said: And some said: 'I hired him,' meaning he is a tenant, meaning the dower of it, as if her father to me made the dowry of his daughter, whom Moses married, Moses grazed over him, and his livestock were eight pilgrims. In the maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Ishaq.
