## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr, from his father, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, that in the time of Uthman a thief stole a citron. Uthman ordered it valued; it was valued at three dirhams, at the exchange of twelve dirhams to a dinar, and Uthman cut off his hand. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.394}
