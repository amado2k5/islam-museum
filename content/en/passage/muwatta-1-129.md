## Summary
The first report in the chapter on the general rules of the ritual bath. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say there is no harm in bathing with the leftover water of a woman, as long as she is not menstruating or in sexual impurity. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it, and refers to Abd al-Razzaq's Musannaf and Ibn Abi Shayba. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.97}
