## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 19 as a single section. He explains the phrase "Faces on that Day will be radiant": "Faces on that Day" means: on the Day of Resurrection, "radiant" means: they will be radiant with the blessings God bestows upon them in His gardens, and they are the people of faith in God. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah in this section.
