## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 2 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "the revelation of the Book from Allah, the Mighty, the Wise": "The revelation of the Book" which We have revealed to you, O Muhammad, is "from Allah, the Almighty" in His revenge on His enemies, the "Wise" in managing His creation, not from anyone else. So do not be in doubt about that, and He elevated His saying: "Revelation" by saying: "From Allah." And the interpretation of the statement: from Allah, the Mighty, the Wise, is the revelation of the Book. It is permissible to raise it by implicitly saying this, as it is said: "A surah We revealed," but the nominative in His saying: "the revelation of the Book" with what follows it is better than the raising of a surah with what follows it, because revelation, even if it is a verb, is closer to definite definition, as it is added to definiteness, so it is good to raise it with what comes after it, and this is not the good in "surah," because it is indeterminate. In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
