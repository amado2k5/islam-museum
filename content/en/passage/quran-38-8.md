## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Should the reminder be sent down to him from among us? Rather, they are in doubt about My remembrance, but they will taste a punishment": God Almighty says, informing about the saying of these polytheists from Quraysh: "Should the reminder be sent down to Muhammad from among us and be exclusive to him? He is not nobler than us in rank." And His saying: "Rather, they are in doubt of My remembrance," the Exalted says: What is wrong with these polytheists that they are not people of knowledge that Muhammad is truthful, but they are in doubt about whom We revealed to him, and in this Qur'an that We revealed to him that it is from Us, "But when they taste a punishment," He says, rather, it was not sent down upon them by Our might, so they taste the consequences of their denial of Muhammad and their doubt about Our revelation of this Qur'an upon him. And if they taste the punishment for that, they would know and be certain of the truth of what they deny when their knowledge does not benefit them
