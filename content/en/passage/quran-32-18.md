## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Is one who is a believer like one who is a defiantly disobedient and not equal": Is this disbeliever who denies God's promise and warning, who opposes God's commands and prohibitions, like this believer in God, who believes in His promise and threat, obeys Him in His commands and prohibitions? No, they are not equal before God. He says: The disbelievers and those who believe in Him are not just in His sight regarding what He will do to them on the Day of Resurrection. He said: "They are not equal," so it is pluralized. Before that, two were mentioned: a believer and a sinner, because by 'believer' it does not mean one believer, and by 'sinner' it means one sinner, but it refers to all the sinners and all believers in Allah. If both are not supported by them, the Arabs adopted the plural approach. He mentioned that this verse was revealed about Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased with him, and Al-Walid ibn Uqbah. In the Maqta', he quotes from Qatadah and Ata.
