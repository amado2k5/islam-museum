## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 27–29. Al-Tabari explains that Allah did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them in vain, but to act upon them in obedience to Him. That is what those who disbelieve assumed—woe to those who disbelieved from the Fire. Or shall We make those who believe like the corrupters? This is a blessed Book We have revealed to you so that they may reflect upon its verses.
