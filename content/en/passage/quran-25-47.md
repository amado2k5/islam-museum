## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He it is Who made the night for you a garment and sleep for you and made the day a gathering": He who stretched out the shade and then made the sun a sign over it, He is the One who made the night a garment for you, O people. Indeed, the Exalted said: "He made the night for you a garment" because He made it a paradise for His creation to gather and dwell in, so it became for them a cover to hide with, just as they cover themselves with the clothes they clothe themselves with. And His saying, "And sleep is slumber," means: He made for you sleep a rest by which your bodies rest and your limbs may be calmed. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
