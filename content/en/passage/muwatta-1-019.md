## Summary
The first report in the chapter on the decline of the sun and the darkness of night, two phrases from the verse of the Night Journey on establishing prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar interpreted the decline of the sun as its leaning from the middle of the sky, that is, noon. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.42}
