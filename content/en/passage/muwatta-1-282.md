## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on one who has a nosebleed on Friday: whoever has a nosebleed on Friday while the imam is preaching, goes out and does not return until the imam has finished his prayer, should pray four rak'as. The editor explains nosebleed as blood coming out of the nose, and notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.162}
