## Summary
Verse 71 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 71 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (pp. 536-537, notes 2-3).** Al-Tabari says those addressed are those who believed God and his Messenger; taking one's precaution means taking shields and weapons to guard against the enemy when going to fight them; and the word for the groups is the plural of thubah, a band, so the sense is: go out against your enemy band after band, armed. He cites a line of Zuhayr using the word thubah (describing a company of generous drinkers), says the word is also pluralised as thubin, and says going out all together means going out with their Prophet to fight. The editor says both manuscript and print have a phrase "he means by this" where the context does not call for it and he printed what the context requires; gives the sources of the line (Zuhayr's diwan, Abu Ubayda's Majaz al-Qur'an, Lisan al-Arab); says it comes from lines describing drinkers at the height of their intoxication, living in ease for the day; and gives further lines. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 537}

**Reports 9929-9934 (pp. 537-538).** Al-Muthanna (9929, from Abdullah b. Salih, from Mu'awiya, from Ali b. Abi Talha) reports from Ibn Abbas that the groups are bands, meaning detachments scattered apart, and "all together" means all of you. Muhammad b. Amr (9930, from Abu Asim, from Isa, from Ibn Abi Najih) reports from Mujahid that they are companies, little by little; Bishr b. Mu'adh (9931, from Yazid, from Sa'id) from Qatada, that they are companies; al-Husayn b. Yahya (9932, from Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar) from Qatada, likewise; Muhammad b. al-Husayn (9933, from Ahmad b. Mufaddal, from Asbat) from al-Suddi, that it is the band, and that "all together" is with the Prophet. Al-Tabari reports (9934, from something told to him from al-Husayn b. al-Faraj, from Abu Mu'adh, from Ubayd b. Sulayman) from al-Dahhak that they are bands scattered apart. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 538}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 538}
