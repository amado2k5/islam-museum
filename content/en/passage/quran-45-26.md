## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, Allah will give you life and then cause you to die, then gather you until the Day of Resurrection. There is no doubt about it, but most people do not know": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, 'Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists who deny the resurrection, who say to you, 'Bring us our fathers if you are truthful.' Allah, O polytheists, gives you life as He wills to give you life in this world, then causes you to die therein if He wills, then gathers you until the Day of Resurrection, meaning He gathers you all, the first and the last of you, your young and your old alike, 'until the Day of Resurrection.' He says: For the Day of Resurrection, meaning that He will gather you all alive for the Day of Resurrection "there is no doubt about it," meaning: there is no doubt about it, meaning: do not doubt it, for the matter is as I described to you, "but most people do not know," meaning: but most people who deny the resurrection do not know the truth of it, and that God will resurrect them after their death.
