## Summary
Malik said he asked Ibn Shihab about a one-eyed man who puts out the eye of a man with two sound eyes. Ibn Shihab said: if the sound man wishes to take retaliation from him, he has the right to retaliation; if he wishes, he has the blood-money: a thousand dinars or twelve thousand dirhams. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.426}
