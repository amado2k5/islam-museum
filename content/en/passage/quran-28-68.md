## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And your Lord creates what He wills and chooses what will be for them the best. Glory be to Allah and exalted is He above what they associate with Him": "And your Lord, O Muhammad, He creates what He wills" to create it, and "chooses" for His guardianship the best of His creation, and whoever precedes him in happiness. Indeed, the Exalted said, "And He chooses what is best for them," meaning: what was described, because the polytheists, as mentioned, chose their wealth and made it for their gods. God said to His Prophet Muhammad: "And your Lord, O Muhammad, He creates what He wills to create and chooses for guidance, faith, and righteous deeds from His creation. What He already knew was that He chose them, just as these polytheists had the best of their wealth. Likewise, my choice for myself. And my selection for my guardianship, my selection for my service and obedience, is the choice of my kingdom and my character." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
