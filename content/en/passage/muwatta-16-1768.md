## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Salim ibn Abdullah ibn Umar told him that Aisha, mother of the believers, sent for him while he was still nursing, to her sister Umm Kulthum bint Abi Bakr, and said: nurse him ten sucklings so that he may come in to me. Salim said: Umm Kulthum nursed me three times, then she fell ill and nursed me only three times. So I did not come in to Aisha, because Umm Kulthum did not complete ten sucklings for me. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.121}
