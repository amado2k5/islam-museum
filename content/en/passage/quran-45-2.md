## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Ham" (Hamighty): We have already explained the meaning of the phrase "Hamim." As for the phrase "The revelation of the Book from Allah," its meaning is: this is the revelation of the Qur'an from Allah, the "Mighty" in His vengeance on His enemies, the "Wise" in managing the affairs of His creation. And His saying: "Indeed, in the heavens and the earth are signs for the believers," God Almighty says: Indeed, in the seven heavens from which rain descends, and the earth from which creation emerges, O people, "are signs for the believers." He means: proofs and proofs for those who believe in proofs when they have clarified and seen them.
