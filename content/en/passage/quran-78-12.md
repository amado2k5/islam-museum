## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And We have built above you seven strong heavens” as follows: “And We have built above you” means: We have roofed over you. He uses the term “building” because the Arabs called the roofs of houses, which were their ceilings, “buildings.” The sky was the roof of the earth, so He addressed them in their language, as the revelation was in their language. He said, “seven strong heavens” because they were firmly constructed, without cracks or fissures, and the passage of nights and days would not wear them down. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan in this section.
