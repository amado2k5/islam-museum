## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave David from Us bounty, like the mountains of Obi with him, and the birds, and We gave him iron": And We gave David from Us bounty, and We said to the mountains: "Obi with him": "Praise with him when he glorifies Him." Among the Arabs, 'taw' means returning, and a man sleeping in his home and family. From this is the poet's saying: 'Two days, days are stations and clubs, and one day of going to the enemies, returning.' Some used to recite it as: 'Obey with him,' meaning 'turn back with him,' meaning: 'deal with him,' and that is a reading I do not permit reading it because it differs from the reading of proof. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
