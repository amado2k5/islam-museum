## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse "And they said, 'Our hearts are in veils of what you invite us to, and in our ears there is reverence, and between us and you is a barrier, so act; indeed, we are doing.'" And these polytheists who turned away from the signs of Allah from the polytheists of Quraysh, when Muhammad, the Prophet of Allah, called them to acknowledge the oneness of Allah and to believe in what is in this Qur'an of Allah's command and prohibition, and all that was revealed in it, 'Our hearts are in veils,' meaning: in the veils of 'what you invite us, O Muhammad, to Him' of the oneness of Allah and your belief in what you have brought us, we do not understand what you say, 'and in our ears.' 'And qar' is the weight; we do not hear what you call us to out of heaviness and dislike for what it calls for. The explanation has already been given about the meanings of these letters with their supporting evidence, and what the interpreters said about it, so here we repeat that here. Muhammad ibn Amr narrated to me from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "Our hearts are in a veil," he said: They have covers like a quiver for the nobles. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
