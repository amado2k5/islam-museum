## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that the daughter of Said ibn Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl was married to Abdullah ibn Amr ibn Uthman ibn Affan, who divorced her irrevocably, and she moved out. Abdullah ibn Umar objected to her doing so. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.93}
