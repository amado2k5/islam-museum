**Commentary on verse 37 (p. 475)**

On "and thus We sent it down as an Arabic judgment; if you follow their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you will have against God no protector or defender", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: as We sent down the Book to you, Muhammad, and some of the factions denied it, so too We sent down the judgment and the religion as an Arabic judgment; He made it "Arabic" and described it so because it was sent down on Muhammad, who is an Arab, so the religion is attributed to him since it was sent down on him, and the factions denied it. Then He, mighty be His praise, forbade him to leave what was sent down to him and to follow the factions, and threatened him over it should he do so, saying "if you follow", Muhammad, "their desires", the desires of these factions, their pleasure and their love, and move from your religion to theirs, you will have no one to protect you from God's punishment if He punishes you for following their desires and no helper to help you and rescue you from God if He chastises you; he says, so beware of following their desires. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 475}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
