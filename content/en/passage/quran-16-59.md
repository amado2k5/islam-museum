## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He hides from the people the evil of what he gave him, whether he holds him on a low level or buries him in the dust. Wrong, what they judge is wrong." This bearer of good news hides from the birth of a female from a child to him from the people, so he disappears from their sight "from the evil of what he was promised of," meaning: from the bad of what he was promised to him, it is a burden, whether he catches him on a low level or on a lowly level. This is also the language of Quraysh in what was mentioned to me, they say about humiliation: The mortar, and from it is the saying of al-Hutay'ah: When I feared the mortar and the caravan holding it despite its will, the rope fixed its hoof, and some Banu Tamim made the mortar a source of something light. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Jurayj.
