## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And say, 'My Lord, forgive and have mercy, for You are the best of the merciful': And say, O Muhammad, my Lord, cover my sins by Your pardon them, have mercy on me by accepting Your repentance, and leaving My punishment for what You have honored." "And You are the best of the merciful," meaning: And say, O Lord, You are the best of those who have mercy on a sinner, so he accepted his repentance and did not punish him for his sin.
