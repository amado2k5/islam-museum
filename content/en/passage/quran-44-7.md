## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 9 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Lord of the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them, if you are certain": Reciters differ in the reading of His phrase: "Lord of the heavens and the earth." The majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "Lord of the heavens" by following the grammatical of the Lord, the parsing of the All-Hearing, All-Knowing. Most reciters of Kufa and some Meccan readers read it as "Lord of the heavens" as a refutation of the Lord's words, Glorified and Exalted: "A mercy from your Lord." The correct view is that these are well-known readings with correct meanings; whichever one the reader reads is correct. By His saying: "Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them," He says, the Exalted, who revealed this Book to you, O Muhammad, and sent you to these polytheists as a mercy from your Lord, the Owner of the seven heavens and the earth and all that is between them.
