## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and when Abraham raised the foundations of the House, and Ishmael" al-Tabari says: remember when Abraham raised the foundations of the House, the "qawa'id" being the plural of "qa'ida", the base of the House. He then sets out the interpreters' disagreement on whether the two of them made the foundations or they existed before them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 57}

Some said they are the foundations of a house Adam built at God's command, which was effaced until God settled Abraham there and he built it, from 'Ata' and Ibn Abbas; in 'Ata''s report people say it was built from five mountains (Hira', Tur Zayta, Tur Sina', Lebanon and al-Judi). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 57} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 58}

Others said they are the foundations of a house God sent down to Adam from heaven, which he circled as the Throne is circled in heaven, and which was raised up in the days of the flood, so Abraham raised the foundations of that house, from 'Abd Allah ibn 'Amr, 'Ata', Qatada and Aban. In the editor's footnotes, al-Suyuti and al-Haythami mention the first report and the editor thinks it may be among what Abdullah ibn 'Amr heard of the reports of the People of the Book. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 58} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 59} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 60}

Others said the place of the House was a red mound like a dome, because when God wished to create the earth foam rose on the water at the place of the Sacred House and He then spread the earth out from beneath it, from Mujahid, 'Ata', 'Amr ibn Dinar and Ibn Abbas. From 'Ata' ibn Abi Rabah, people found in Mecca a stone with writing on it that attributes words to God mentioning the House, and the editor compares it in a footnote with the versions of al-Azraqi and Ibn Ishaq. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 60} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 61}

From Mujahid and others among the people of knowledge, through Ibn Ishaq: when God settled Abraham at the place of the House he set out for it from Syria with Ishmael and his mother Hajar, with Gabriel showing the place, at which time the House was a red mound; Abraham left them at the place of the Hijr, told Hajar to make a hut, and said "our Lord, I have settled some of my offspring". Ibn Ishaq adds that an angel came to Hajar before the foundations were raised, pointed to the House and said it is the first house set on earth and that Abraham and Ishmael will raise it for the people, and he says this is what they claim and God knows best. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 61} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 62}
