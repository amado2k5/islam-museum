## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 8 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “As for Thamud, they were destroyed by the overwhelming calamity”: “As for Thamud,” the people of Salih, God destroyed them by the overwhelming calamity. Scholars differed on the meaning of the overwhelming calamity with which God destroyed Thamud. Some said it was their transgression and disbelief in God. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
