## Summary
A saying of Malik: one in ihram does not raise his voice in calling out in the congregational mosques so that he hears himself and those next to him, except in the Sacred Mosque and the mosque of Mina, where he raises his voice in both. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1073) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.450}
