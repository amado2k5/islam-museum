## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19-20 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Then as for him who is given his record in his right hand, he will say, ‘Here, read my record!’”: As for him who is given the record of his deeds in his right hand, God Almighty will say, “Read my record!” As Ibn Zayd said, regarding God’s words, “Here, read my record!” he said: “Come.” And on the authority of Qatadah, he said: Some scholars used to say: I found the wisest of people to be those who said, “Here, read my record!” And he quotes in the section statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
