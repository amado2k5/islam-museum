## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Zayd ibn Abdallah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab, from Abdallah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq, from Umm Salama, wife of the Prophet, that the Messenger of God said: he who drinks from silver vessels only swallows the fire of Hell into his belly. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.512}
