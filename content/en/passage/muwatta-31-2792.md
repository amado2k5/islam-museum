## Summary
It reached Malik that the Prophet said: if there is a medicine that reaches the disease, then cupping will reach it. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the meaning of this hadith is preserved from Abu Salama on Abu Hurayra's authority, from Humayd on Anas's authority, and from Samura, with differing wording. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.569}
