## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Sa'id ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Ruqaysh, in the chapter on wiping over socks: Anas ibn Malik came to Quba, urinated, was brought water, and performed ablution, washing his face and hands to the elbows, wiping his head and wiping over his socks, and then came to the mosque and prayed. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq and Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted it from Asim al-Ahwal from Anas. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.78}
