## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, O disbelievers": He says to His Prophet Muhammad, and the polytheists among His people were offered to worship Allah Sunnah, on condition that the Prophet of Allah worship their gods Sunnah. Allah revealed His answer to them: "Say" O Muhammad to these polytheists who asked you to worship their gods Sunnah, on the condition that they worship your God Sunnah, "O disbelievers," by Allah. He also quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas.
