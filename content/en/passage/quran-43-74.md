## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 74 to 76 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Indeed, the criminals will abide eternally in the punishment of Hell": God Almighty says, "Indeed, the criminals" are those who committed disbelief against Allah in this world, so they committed it in the Hereafter, "in the punishment of Hell, they will abide forever," meaning: they will remain therein, "it will not be removed from them," meaning the punishment will not be lightened for them. The root of weakness is weakness "while they are in it," meaning they will be in the punishment of Hell, and the 'h' in it is from the mention of punishment. It is mentioned that this is mentioned in Abdullah's recitation: "and they will be in Hell, in which they will be deceitful." The 'al-Mublis' here is the one who has despaired of salvation and has surrendered to punishment and affliction. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
