## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Why should he not bring us a sign from his Lord? Has there not come to them clear proof of what is in the earlier scriptures?": These polytheists whose characteristics were described in the verses before: 'Why should not Muhammad bring us a sign from his Lord, just as his people Salih brought the she-camel, and Jesus brought the dead to life, and the dumb and the leper were healed? Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: Or has there not come to them the clarification of what is in the scriptures before this Book of the news of nations before them, which We destroyed when they were asked for the verses and they disbelieved in them when they came to them? How did We hasten for them the punishment and sent down We are wrong with their disbelief in it. He says: So what will they believe if the verse comes to them, that their condition will be like that of those? In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
