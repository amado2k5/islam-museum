## Summary
A report of Jabir ibn Abdullah in the chapter on the virtues of Sa'd ibn Mu'adh in Sahih Muslim: the Throne shook at the bier of the chief of the Aws. It is among the greatest texts on his virtues, agreed upon with al-Bukhari. {cite:sahih-muslim}
