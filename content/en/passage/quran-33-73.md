## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah will punish the hypocrites and the hypocrites, the polytheists, and the polytheists, and Allah will accept the repentance of the believing men and believing women. And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." He said: And man bears the trust so that Allah may punish the hypocrites therein who appear to be fulfilling Allah's commandments, believing in them, while they conceal their disbelief in them. "And the hypocrite men and the polytheists" in their worship of Allah, the gods and idols, "and the polytheists, and Allah accepts the repentance of the believing men and believing women," which brings them back to obedience to Him and fulfilling the trusts He has entrusted them with. So that they may perform it, and 'And Allah is Forgiving' for the sins of the believing men and women, by concealing them and leaving their punishment for them 'merciful,' to punish them for it after their repentance. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
