## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or is it for them the dominion of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, so that they may rise in the means": Or is it for these polytheists who are in honor and discord "the dominion of the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them"? He does not oppose me or rival me with whoever is in my kingdom and authority. And His saying: 'Let them ascend in the causes,' means: If they have dominion over the heavens and the earth and what is between them, let them ascend through the gates and paths of the heavens; and if they possess dominion over something, it is not impossible for them to supervise, inspect, or care over it. Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the means mentioned by Allah in this context, and some said: They mean the gates of heaven. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Ibn Zayd, and Al-Rabi'.
