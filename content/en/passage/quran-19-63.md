## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is the Paradise which We will inherit from among Our servants who are righteous": This is the Paradise whose attribute has been described to you, O people; it is the Paradise that We will inherit. He means: We will inherit the dwellings of the people of Hell therein. "Among Our servants is the one who is God-fearing," meaning: whoever fears God's punishment by fulfilling his obligations and avoiding His sins.
