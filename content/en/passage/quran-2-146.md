## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "those to whom We gave the Book know it as they know their sons" al-Tabari says it means the Jewish rabbis and Christian scholars, who know that the Sacred House is their qibla, the qibla of Abraham and of the prophets before you, as they know their sons. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 187} He reports from Qatada, al-Rabi', Ibn 'Abbas, al-Suddi, Ibn Zayd and Ibn Jurayj that the meaning is that they know the Ka'ba and the Sacred House is the qibla, and from Ibn Zayd that the Jews know that it is Mecca. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 187} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 188} He then begins "and indeed a party of them conceal the truth while they know", saying they are the Jews and Christians, and that Mujahid said they are the People of the Book. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 188}

On "they conceal the truth" al-Tabari says the truth is the qibla to which God directed His Prophet, the one the prophets before Muhammad faced; the Jews and Christians concealed it, some facing east and some Jerusalem, rejecting what God commanded them, and with it they concealed the matter of Muhammad though they found it written in the Torah and Gospel; God informed His Prophet and his community of this, and that they do it knowing that the truth is otherwise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 188} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 189} He reports from Qatada and Mujahid that they concealed Muhammad though they found him written with them in the Torah and Gospel, and from al-Rabi' that the meaning is the qibla. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 189} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 190}
