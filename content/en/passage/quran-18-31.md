## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those are the Gardens of Eden, beneath which rivers flow, where they will be adorned with bracelets of gold, and they will wear green garments of fine silk and Ibroq, reclining therein upon Your opinions. Excellent reward and excellent hospitality." For those who believed and did righteous deeds, there are gardens of Eden, meaning gardens of residence in the Hereafter. "Rivers flow beneath them," meaning: rivers flow beneath them, and from their hands rivers. And He, Glorified and Exalted, said: "beneath them," meaning: beneath them and in front of them, "they adorn them with bracelets," meaning: they wear gold bracelets in them, and bracelets are the plural of bracelets. And His saying: 'They wear green clothes made of sandas'—and 'al-sandas': its singular plural is 'sandasa', which is what is made of thin dibaj: and al-istabiraq: what is thick and thick of it. It is also said: al-istabiraq is silk, and from it is the saying of al-Marqash: 'You bet they wear hair once,' and 'istabraj' is sometimes worn by the dibaj, meaning: the thick brocade. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
