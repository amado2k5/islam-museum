## Summary
A report of Aisha, heard from Safiyya bint Shayba, in the chapter on the virtues of the People of the House of the Prophet, in Sahih Muslim. The Prophet put al-Hasan, al-Husayn, Fatima and Ali under a striped cloak and then recited the verse of purification (Al-Ahzab 33). It is one of the clearest texts in Sunni tradition on who the People of the House are. {cite:sahih-muslim}
