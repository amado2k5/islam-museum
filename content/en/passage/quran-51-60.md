## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So woe to those who disbelieved from their Day which they are promised": the valley flowing in Hell, made of pus and pus, for those who disbelieved in Allah and denied His oneness from their Day on which they are promised the punishment of Allah will descend upon them. What affliction and effort will they throw into it?
