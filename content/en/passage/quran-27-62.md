## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Is He who responds to the desperate when he calls upon Him and removes evil and makes you successors on earth? Is there a deity with Allah for a little you remember?" means: Or do you not associate anything with Allah with good, or is it He who responds to the desperate when he calls upon Him and removes the evil that befalls him? As he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj regarding His saying: "And He removes evil," he said: hardship. And His saying: "And He will make you successors on earth" means: He appoints living caliphs among you after your rulers on earth to succeed them. And His saying: "Will He be with Allah?" He says: "Will He be with Allah"—does He do these things to you and bestow these blessings upon you? And His saying: "You do not remember little" means you remember a little of the greatness of Allah and His hands with you, you remember and consider Allah's proofs for you easy, so you associate others with Allah in His worship.
