## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah has not placed for a man two hearts within him, nor has He made your wives of Allah to be among them your mothers, nor has He made your claimants your sons. That is your speech with your mouths, and Allah speaks the truth while He guides to the way": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of Allah's saying, "Allah has not placed for a man from two hearts within his body." Some said: This means the denial of a group of hypocrites who described the Prophet of Allah as having two hearts, but Allah denied that from His Prophet and denied them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Sufyan.
