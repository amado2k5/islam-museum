## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And if those who disbelieve had killed you, they would have turned their backs and found no protector or helper": Allah Almighty says to the believers among the people of the pledge of pleasure: "And if those who disbelieve fought you" by Allah, O believers in Mecca, "if they had turned their backs," He says: We would have fled from you, so they will have left you in their miracles. And likewise, the defeated of his century in battle will do "then they will find no protector or helper," meaning: Then these disbelievers who have been defeated from you, who have turned their backs and will not find any protector to support them in your war, nor any supporter to support them against you, because Allah Almighty Mention it with you, and Hezbollah will not defeat its supporter.
