**Commentary on verse 46 (pp. 575-576)**

Al-Tabari says on "obey God and His Messenger and do not quarrel, or you will lose heart and your strength will go; and be steadfast, for God is with the steadfast" [[passage/quran-8-46|8:46]]: he says to the believers: obey, believers, your Lord and His Messenger in what He commanded and forbade you, and do not go against them in anything; "do not quarrel or you will lose heart" means do not differ and so split and your hearts differ, and you grow weak and cowardly; "and your strength will go", which is a figure of speech, for one says of a man when what he loves and is glad of comes to him, the wind is coming toward him; among that is Ubayd ibn al-Abras in a line saying they protected their man at the day of al-Na'f from Shatab and that excellence belongs to the people, of wind and of number, meaning force and multitude. What is meant here is that your power and might go, so that you weaken and frailty and defect enter you. "Be steadfast" means be steadfast with God's Prophet when you meet your enemy, do not be routed from him and leave him; "God is with the steadfast" means be steadfast for I am a judge. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "quarrelling", "losing heart" and "with", gives lines from Ubayd's Diwan before this, corrects the Diwan's editor's handling, and explains "shrank back", "al-Na'f" and Shatab, a mountain in the land of Banu Asad. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 575} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 576}

The interpreters spoke likewise; reports 16163, 16164 and 16165, from Mujahid: "your strength will go" means your victory, and the strength of Muhammad's Companions went when they disputed with him at Uhud, with Ibn Jurayj's version when they left him at Uhud; report 16166, from al-Suddi on "do not quarrel or you will lose heart and your strength will go", he said... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 576}

We stop here at the foot of p. 576, in the middle of report 16166 in the commentary on 8:46; the next batch begins at p. 577. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 576}
