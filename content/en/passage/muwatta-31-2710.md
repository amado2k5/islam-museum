## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that Urwa ibn al-Zubayr told him that the Messenger of God entered the house of Umm Salama, wife of the Prophet, and there was a child crying in the house. They told him the child had the evil eye. He said: will you not seek a cure for him from the evil eye? {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.528}
