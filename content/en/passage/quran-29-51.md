## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Was it not enough for them that We sent down to you the Book to be recited to them? Indeed, in that is a mercy and a reminder for a people who believe": Or were these polytheists, O Muhammad, who say: Were it not enough for them that a sign was not revealed to Muhammad from his Lord, among the signs and proofs "Indeed, We have revealed to you" this is "the Book recited to them," meaning it is recited to them, "Indeed, in that is mercy," meaning: In this Book which We have revealed to them is a mercy for those who believe in it and a reminder to remember what it contains of lesson and admonition. He mentioned that this verse was revealed because a group of the Companions of the Messenger of Allah copied some of the books of the People of the Book.
