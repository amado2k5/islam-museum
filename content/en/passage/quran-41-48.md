## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 48 to 49 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And what they used to invoke before has gone astray from them, and they think they have no escape": On the Day of Resurrection, these polytheists will have their gods they used to worship in this world, so they will take a path other than theirs, but it will neither benefit them nor avert from them anything of the punishment of Allah that befalls them. And His saying: "And they will think that they will have no refuge," means: And they will be certain then that they have no refuge from the punishment of Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
