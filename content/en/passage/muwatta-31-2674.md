## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, who said: the Messenger of God said: the Muslim eats in one intestine, and the unbeliever eats in seven intestines. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.511}
