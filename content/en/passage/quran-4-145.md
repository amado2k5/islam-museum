**Commentary on verse 145: the lowest rank of the fire (pp. 337-339)**

Al-Tabari says that the hypocrites being in 'the lowest rank of the fire' means that they are in the lowest layer of the layers of hell. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 337}

Each layer of hell is called a 'darak', which has two forms, with a fatha or a sukun on the r; its plural for the few is 'adrak' and for the many 'duruk'. Most readers of Medina and Basra read it with a fatha, and most Kufan readers with a sukun; both are well known. The grammarians say the fatha is the more common in Arabic, and cite the saying: give me a rope (darak) by which I may reach the bottom of the well. The editor notes that Abu Ubayda raised this in Majaz al-Qur'an, and that there the word means the rope, not the layer, so it is no proof for this sense. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 338}

Report 10741, from Ibn Mas'ud through Khaythama and Salama ibn Kuhayl: the hypocrites are in coffins of iron closed upon them, locked tight; report 10742, from Ibn Mas'ud through Shu'ba: in iron coffins locked on them in the fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 338}

Report 10743, from Abu Hurayra through Asim and Dhakwan: in coffins that are locked on them; report 10744, from Ibn Abbas through Ali ibn Abi Talha: the lowest part of the fire; report 10745, from Abd Allah ibn Kathir: we have heard that hell has layers, dwellings; report 10746, from Ibn Mas'ud through Sufyan and Salama: coffins of fire closed over them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 339}

As for 'you will find no helper for them', the sense is that you will find for these hypocrites, Muhammad, no helper from God when He puts them in the lowest rank of the fire, to rescue them from His punishment or ward off the pain of His penalty. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 339}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
