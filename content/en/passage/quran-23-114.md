## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 114 to 115 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Indeed, you stayed except a little, if only you knew': The reciters differed in the interpretation of His saying: 'He said, 'Indeed, you stayed except a little.' Their difference in reading His saying: 'He said, how long did you stay?' and our view on this matter here is similar to the one we explained earlier regarding His saying: 'How long did you stay.' And the interpretation of the speech according to our reading: Allah said to them: You did not remain on the earth except a little while, if you only knew the destiny of your dwelling therein. And His saying: "Did you think that We created you in vain?" He says: Did you think, O wretched ones, that We created you only when We created you as playful and useless, and that you are to your Lord after your death, not living and recompensed for what you used to do in this world? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Jurayj.
