## Summary
Hadith of Jabir ibn Abdullah: a man asked, "If I pray the obligatory prayers, fast Ramadan, make lawful what is lawful and unlawful what is unlawful, and add nothing, will I enter Paradise?" He said yes. Ibn Rajab explains the making lawful and unlawful as acting on the lawful and avoiding the unlawful. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
