## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and what is between them and beneath the earth": To Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and what is on the earth and what is between them, and what is beneath the earth, His dominion, and He manages all of it and drains it all. By 'thara' he means: dew. Moist and wet soil is called 'thra minquus' (mud of the earth), from which it is said: 'thra'it al-'arti' (earth) is 'thara' (dust is incomplete), and 'thra' is a verbal noun. In the maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
