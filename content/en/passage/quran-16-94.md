## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And do not take your oaths as a means of encroachment among you, lest a foot slip after it has been established, and taste evil because of what you have prevented from the way of Allah. And for you is a great punishment": And do not take your oaths among yourselves as an entry or deception, deceiving people with them. "So your feet slip after they have been established," meaning: "And perish after you were safe from destruction." This is only a parable for anyone afflicted after recovery, or fallen into trouble after safety, and similar things: "His foot slipped," as the poet said: "You will be prevented from being ahead if you are ahead and you will be struck if your sandals slip." And His saying: "And taste the evil" means: "And you taste the evil," and that evil is the punishment of God with which He punishes the people of His sins in this world, and that is part of what the people of disbelief were punished with. "Because you turned away from the way of God" means: because you tempted those who wanted to believe in God and His Messenger from faith. "And for you is a great punishment" in the Hereafter, which is the fire of Hell. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
