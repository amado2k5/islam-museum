## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 107 to 108 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Our Lord, bring us out of it; but if we return, indeed, we are wrongdoers": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of those whose scales of righteousness will be feared on the Day of Resurrection in Hell: Our Lord, bring them out of the Fire. But if We return to what You dislike from us, then indeed, we are wrongdoers. And His saying: "He said, 'Disgrace in it,' Allah says: The Lord, Glorified and Exalted, said to them, responding: 'Disgrace them,' meaning 'sit in the Fire.' It is said: 'So-and-so was disgraced, I humiliated him,' and 'khas'a' means 'humiliation,' and 'khasa' means 'humiliated,' meaning 'he was not humiliated,' and indeed he was humiliated. In the passage, he quotes from Al-A'mash.
