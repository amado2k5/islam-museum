## Summary
It reached Malik that Umar ibn al-Khattab sent to al-Harith ibn Hisham: tomorrow is the day of Ashura, so fast and order your family to fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik, and that Abd al-Razzaq (7838) and Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted by other routes that Umar sent to Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith on the night of Ashura, telling him to eat suhur and fast in the morning, and he fasted in the morning. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.403}
