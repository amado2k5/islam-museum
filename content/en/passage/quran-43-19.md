## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they made the angels, who are the servants of the Most Merciful, females. They testified to their creation; their testimony will be recorded and they will be questioned": And He made those who associate partners with Allah His angels, who are the servants of the Most Merciful. The reciters differed in their reading; the majority of the reciters of Medina read it as "those who are with the Most Merciful" with a 'nun,' as if they interpreted the words of Allah, Glorified and Exalted be He: "Indeed, those who are with your Lord do not act arrogantly." The interpretation of this reading is: They made the angels of Allah who are with Him to glorify and sanctify Him female, saying: They are the daughters of Allah, out of ignorance of the right of Allah, and boldness in speaking falsehood and falsehood. This was read by most reciters of Kufa and Basra: 'And they made the angels, who are the servants of the Most Merciful, female,' meaning: the plural of servants. The meaning of the discussion regarding these readings is: They made the angels of Allah, who are His creation and servants, the daughters of Allah, and they made them female, describing them as females.
