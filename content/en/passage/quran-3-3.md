## Summary
Verse 3 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 3 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

([[passage/quran-3-3|3:3]]), pp. 160 to 161. Al-Tabari addresses the Prophet: your Lord, the Lord of Jesus and the Lord of everything is the one who sent down the scripture to you, meaning the Qur'an, and sent it with truth about what the people of the Torah and the Gospel disagreed over and what your Najran Christian disputants and other polytheists contested with you; it confirms the earlier books God sent to His prophets and messengers and makes good what His messengers brought, because the sender of all of it is one, so there is no disagreement in it, and had it been from anyone else there would be much disagreement. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 160} On p. 160 the editor refers to al-Farra' (Ma'ani al-Qur'an 1: 110) and notes that the manuscript slipped in some wording on the page, the print's reading being right.

In 6554, Mujahid (through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih): confirming what came before, a book or a messenger. In 6555 the same from Mujahid (through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl and Ibn Abi Najih). In 6556, Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr (through Ibn Humayd, Salama and Ibn Ishaq): He sent it down with truth in what they disagreed on; the editor says it is the remainder of the earlier reports that ended at 6553. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 161} In 6557, Qatada (through Bishr, Yazid and Said): it is the Qur'an, confirming the books that had gone before it. In 6558, al-Rabi' (through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Ibn Abi Jafar and his father): confirming what came before of scripture and messenger.

These are reports as transmitted, with no museum verdict.
