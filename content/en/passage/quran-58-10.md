## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Whispering is only from Satan so that those who believe may grieve, and nothing harms them except by permission of Allah. And upon Allah let the believers rely": intimate conversation is only from Satan. Then scholars differed regarding the whispering that Allah informed was from Satan, meaning that it is. Some said: What is meant is the intimate conversation of hypocrites with one another. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
