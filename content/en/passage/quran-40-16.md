## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 17 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "The One of the highest ranks, the possessor of the Throne, who casts his soul of His command upon whomever He wills among His servants to warn of the Day of Meeting": He is of high ranks, and the nominative of the phrase "lofty degrees" is the beginning, even if it is in the accusative to respond to the saying: So call upon Allah, it is correct. "The Possessor of the Throne" means: the One of the throne surrounding what is below it. And His saying: 'He casts the spirit of His command upon whomever He wills among His servants,' means: revelation descends from His command to whomever He wills among His servants. The scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning of the spirit in this context; some said: it refers to revelation. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, and Ibn Zayd.
