## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 28–30. Al-Tabari mentions that the polytheists ask the Prophet when this conquest will occur, and their meaning differs: it is said to be the judgment between them on the Day of Resurrection, and it is said to be the conquest by victory. God commands His prophet to tell them that the faith of the disbelievers will not benefit those who disbelieve, nor do they look, and that He should turn away from them and wait, for they are waiting.
