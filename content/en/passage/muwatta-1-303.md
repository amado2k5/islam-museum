## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Ruman, in the chapter on night prayer in Ramadan: in Umar ibn al-Khattab's time people stood in Ramadan with twenty-three rak'as. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it, and al-Baghawi cited it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.172}
