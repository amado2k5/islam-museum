## Summary
Malik said: if people are contracted together in one contract, with no kinship between them by which they inherit from each other, and some of them become unable while others work until all are freed, those who worked return to those who became unable, in proportion to what they paid on their behalf, because some of them are guarantors for others. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.357}
