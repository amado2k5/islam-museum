## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 10 to 11 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And [mention] when your Lord called to Moses, "Bring to the wrongdoing people": And remember, O Muhammad, when your Lord called to Moses son of Imran, "[Say] the wrongdoing people," meaning the disbelievers, the people of Pharaoh. The accusative of the second group is a translation of the first, and the phrase "do not fear God" means: do they not fear God's punishment for their disbelief in Him. The meaning of the speech is: the people of Pharaoh, so tell them: Do not fear Him. And he omitted the expression, so say to them because the speech indicates it. It was said: Do not fear the letter ya, but not say do not fear the letter ta', because the revelation was before the address, and if the reading were to be read with the ta, it would be correct, as it was said: Say to those who disbelieve that they will be overcome, and 'you will prevail.'
