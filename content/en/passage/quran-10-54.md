**Commentary on verse 54 (p. 103)**

On [[passage/quran-10-54|10:54]] he says: if every soul that disbelieved in God, its wrong here being its worship of one who does not deserve worship and its leaving obedience to the one it must obey, had all that is on the earth, little or much, it would offer it as ransom from God's punishment once it saw it. "They hide their regret when they see the punishment" means that the leaders of these polytheists hide their regret from their lowly followers when they see God's punishment surrounding them and are sure it will fall on them; "it is judged between them with justice" means God judges that day between followers and leaders fairly; "and they are not wronged" because He punishes none of them except for his own offence and holds none for another's sin, and punishes only one to whom He gave excuse in the world, warned and sent proof after proof. The editor notes that the printed text changed a word of the manuscript, and refers to the glosses on "ransom" and "justice". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 103}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
