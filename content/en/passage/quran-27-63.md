## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Is it He who guides you in the darkness of land and sea, and who sends the winds as human beings before His mercy? Is there a deity with Allah, exalted is He, above what they associate with Him?" Also: He said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, regarding His saying: "Who guides you in the darkness of the land and the sea?" And darkness on the land, His misguidance is the path, and the sea is its misguidance its path and direction and what is in it. His saying: "And whoever sends the winds as a scattering for the dead before His mercy," it means: And He who sends the winds as a scattering for the dead of the earth before His mercy, meaning: the rain that gives life to the dead of the earth.
