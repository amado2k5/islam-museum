## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 18 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And mention in the Book Mary when she withdrew from her family in an eastern place": "And remember, O Muhammad, in the Book of God which He revealed to you in truth, Mary, daughter of Imran, when she withdrew from her family and separated from them." It is a verbal form of 'nadhdh' (to reject), and 'nadd' means to cast off, as we have previously explained with its evidence. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
