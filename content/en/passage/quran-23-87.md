## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 86 to 87 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "They will say to Allah, say, 'Will you not then fear Allah?'" He interprets this as the majority of the reciters of the Hijaz, Iraq, and the Levant: "They will say to Allah" except for Abu Amr, who differed from them and read it as: "They will say Allah" in this place and the next one, following the script of the Mushaf. This is the case in the Qur'ans of the cities except in the Mushaf of the people of Basra, where in both places it is with an alif, so they read the entire alif following the script of their Mushaf. As for those who recite it with an alif, there is no hardship in their recitation as well, because they performed the answer at the beginning and responded with the nominative over the raised. This is because the meaning of the speech regarding their recitation is: Say, Who is the Lord of the seven heavens and the Lord of the Mighty Throne? They will say, 'The Lord of that is Allah.' There is no hardship in reading that as well.
