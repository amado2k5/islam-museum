## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Allah will not attain its flesh nor its blood, but rather, it will attain it the righteous among you. Thus He has subjected it to you, that you may magnify Allah over what He has guided you and give good tidings to the doers of good": The flesh of your body or its blood will reach Allah, but it will be through your avoidance of Him if you fear Him therein, intend His face with it, act upon it as He has enjoined upon you and commanded you in its affairs, and honor His sanctities with it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
