**Commentary on verse 35: "O you who believe, fear God and seek the means to Him", p. 289**

Al-Tabari says God means: O you who have believed God and His messenger in what they were told of reward promised and punishment threatened, "fear God", that is respond to God in what He commanded and forbade by obeying Him in it, and make real your faith and your belief in your Lord and your prophet.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 289}
