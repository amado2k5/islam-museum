## Summary
Verse 35 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 35 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 35: "O you who believe, fear God and seek the means to Him", p. 289**

Al-Tabari says God means: O you who have believed God and His messenger in what they were told of reward promised and punishment threatened, "fear God", that is respond to God in what He commanded and forbade by obeying Him in it, and make real your faith and your belief in your Lord and your prophet.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 289}

"And seek the means to Him" means seek nearness to Him through works that please Him. The word is a form from "I approached so-and-so by such a thing", meaning drawing near; al-Tabari cites two lines of verse, one of 'Antara saying men have a means to a woman, and another on the return of closeness between lovers once the slanderers are heedless, and the editor explains the occasion of the first and refers to Abu 'Ubayda's Majaz al-Qur'an. The interpreters spoke in the same sense.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 290}

Reports 11899 to 11904, from Abu Wa'il, 'Ata', al-Suddi, Qatada, Mujahid, al-Hasan and 'Abdallah ibn Kathir, say the means is nearness, in al-Suddi's report the asking and the nearness, and in Qatada's drawing near to Him by obeying Him and acting in ways that please Him. Report 11905, from Ibn Zayd, says it is love, that is, endear yourselves to God, and he recited the verse of [[passage/quran-17-57|17:57]].  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 291}

**Commentary on verse 35, continued: "and strive in His way so that you may prosper", p. 292**

Al-Tabari says God tells the believers: strive against My enemies and yours in My way, that is, in His religion and the law He laid down for His servants, which is Islam; weary yourselves in fighting them and in working to enter the pure upright religion, "so that you may prosper" meaning so that you succeed and attain lasting existence and eternity in His gardens. He had explained the meaning of success earlier.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 292}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
