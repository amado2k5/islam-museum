**Commentary on verse 54 (p. 20)**

Al-Tabari says: these idolaters, killed at Badr, changed their Lord's favour to them in sending Muhammad from among them and in their midst, calling them to guidance, by denying him and fighting him; "like the custom of the people of Pharaoh" means like their way, their habit and their dealing with Moses, the prophet of God, in denying him and setting out to fight him, and the habit. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "custom" and "people" and notes that the publisher changed a word and the manuscript's is right. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 20}

The sentence continues: and the habit of the nations before them who denied their messengers and what they did; "so We destroyed them for their sins", some by the earthquake, some by the swallowing of the earth and some by the wind, "and We drowned the people of Pharaoh" in the sea, "and all were wrongdoers", meaning all these nations We destroyed were doing what they had no right to do, in denying God's messengers and rejecting His signs. So We destroyed those We destroyed at Badr, since they changed God's favour to them, by killing with the sword, and humbled some of them by capture and enslavement. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 21}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
