## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Indeed, We have given you a clear victory" meaning: by His saying to His Prophet Muhammad, "Indeed, We have given you a clear opening," he means: We have judged for you, O Muhammad, a judgment against whoever heard it or conveyed it to you against those who opposed you and appointed you among the disbelievers of your people, and We decreed victory and victory for you, so you may thank your Lord and praise Him for His blessing by decreeing for you over them, granting His conquest of what He opened for you, glorifying Him and seeking His forgiveness, so that your Lord will forgive you by your actions. What your sins have passed before He opened for you, and what is delayed after His conquest is what you have thanked and sought forgiveness.
