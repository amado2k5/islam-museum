## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And to the sky, how it was raised”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: Do they not also look at the sky, how He raised it, He who informed you that He has prepared for His allies what He described, and for His enemies what He mentioned, so that they may know that His power is the power that nothing He wills can prevent Him from doing? And he quotes in the section statements from Qatadah.
