## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim: Islam began as a stranger and will return to being strange, so blessed are the strangers. It is one of the best-known texts on the trials of the end times. In Ibn Umar's version, it returns "between the two mosques as a snake returns to its hole". {cite:sahih-muslim}
