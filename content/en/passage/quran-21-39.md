## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "If only those who disbelieved knew, when the Fire would not escape their faces or their backs, nor would they be helped": If these disbelievers, who are hastening to the punishment of their Lord, knew what affliction they have when the Fire ignites their faces, and they are like a burning flame therein, so they do not cease from their faces or from their backs, so they repel it with their own selves. "Nor will they help." He means: nor will they have any helper to help them, who will save them from the punishment of Allah when they have remained in disbelief in Allah and hasten to repentance from him and faith in God, and when they hastened the affliction upon themselves.
