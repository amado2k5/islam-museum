## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they said, 'When did we go astray in the land? Are we not of a new creation? Rather, they are disbelievers in meeting their Lord.": And the polytheists of Allah, the deniers of resurrection, said: "When did we go astray in the land?" That is, our flesh and bones became dust on the earth. It contains two languages: 'dala'na (We misled' and 'ذلانونة'), with a fatha on the lam and a kasra and reading on its fatha, which is 'juda', and with it we read. It was mentioned from Al-Hasan that he used to read: 'idha salla' with a 'sad,' meaning: 'you,' from their saying: 'Pray the meat,' and the origin is: when you swell. These polytheists meant: 'When we went astray in the land,' meaning when our bodies perished in the earth, because everything else prevailed over it until it was hidden in what prevailed, for it was lost in it. The Arabs say: 'When water in milk prevailed' when it overwhelmed it so it became unclear. From this is the saying of Al-Akhtal by Jarir: 'I was like a scum in a foaming wave, throwing away the favor of what was brought in misguidance.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
