**Commentary on verse 7 (pp. 353-354)**

On "those who disbelieve say, why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord? You are only a warner, and for every people a guide", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: "those who disbelieve say", Muhammad, of your people, "why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord?", why has no sign been sent down to Muhammad from his Lord, meaning a sign and proof of his prophethood, which is their saying "why has no treasure been sent down to him, or an angel come with him?" [[passage/quran-11-12|11:12]]. God says to him: Muhammad, "you are only a warner" to them, warning them of God's might coming upon them for their polytheism; "and for every people a guide" means for every people an imam whom they follow and a guide who goes ahead of them and leads them either to good or to evil; its root is from "hadi al-faras", the horse's neck, which leads the rest of its body. The interpreters said the same, differing over who is meant by the guide here. Some said it is the Messenger of God. Report 20138, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "those who disbelieve say, why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord?", this is the saying of the polytheists of the Arabs; God said "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", for every people a caller who calls them to God. Report 20139, from Ikrima and Abu al-Duha, through Abu Kurayb, Waki', Sufyan, al-Suddi and Mansur: {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 353}

"you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", they said, Muhammad is the "warner" and he is the "guide". Reports 20140 and 20141, from Ikrima, through Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Abd al-Rahman, Sufyan and al-Suddi, and al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz, Sufyan and his father: the same. Others said that the "guide" in this place is God. Report 20142, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Abu Kurayb, Waki', Sufyan and Ata' ibn al-Sa'ib: "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", he said, Muhammad is the warner and God is the Guide; the editor notes that the end of the report is missing from the manuscript, which stops at "guide", and that it seems to have been completed from the next. Report 20143, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Ibn Bashshar, Abu Asim, Sufyan and Ata': Muhammad is the "warner" and God the "Guide". Report 20144, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Abu Kurayb, al-Ashja'i, Sufyan and Ata': "you are only a warner", he said, you, Muhammad, are a warner and God is the Guide. Report 20145, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Amr ibn Awn, Hushaym, Abd al-Malik and Qays: "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", he said, the "warner" is the Prophet, "and for every people a guide", he said, God is the guide of every people. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 354}

We stop here at the foot of p. 354, in the middle of the commentary on verse 7 (after report 20145, on who is meant by "the guide"); the next batch begins at p. 355. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 354}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
