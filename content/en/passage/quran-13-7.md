**Commentary on verse 7 (pp. 353-358)**

On "those who disbelieve say, why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord? You are only a warner, and for every people a guide", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: "those who disbelieve say", Muhammad, of your people, "why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord?", why has no sign been sent down to Muhammad from his Lord, meaning a sign and proof of his prophethood, which is their saying "why has no treasure been sent down to him, or an angel come with him?" [[passage/quran-11-12|11:12]]. God says to him: Muhammad, "you are only a warner" to them, warning them of God's might coming upon them for their polytheism; "and for every people a guide" means for every people an imam whom they follow and a guide who goes ahead of them and leads them either to good or to evil; its root is from "hadi al-faras", the horse's neck, which leads the rest of its body. The interpreters said the same, differing over who is meant by the guide here. Some said it is the Messenger of God. Report 20138, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "those who disbelieve say, why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord?", this is the saying of the polytheists of the Arabs; God said "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", for every people a caller who calls them to God. Report 20139, from Ikrima and Abu al-Duha, through Abu Kurayb, Waki', Sufyan, al-Suddi and Mansur: {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 353}

"you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", they said, Muhammad is the "warner" and he is the "guide". Reports 20140 and 20141, from Ikrima, through Muhammad ibn Bashshar, Abd al-Rahman, Sufyan and al-Suddi, and al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz, Sufyan and his father: the same. Others said that the "guide" in this place is God. Report 20142, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Abu Kurayb, Waki', Sufyan and Ata' ibn al-Sa'ib: "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", he said, Muhammad is the warner and God is the Guide; the editor notes that the end of the report is missing from the manuscript, which stops at "guide", and that it seems to have been completed from the next. Report 20143, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Ibn Bashshar, Abu Asim, Sufyan and Ata': Muhammad is the "warner" and God the "Guide". Report 20144, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, through Abu Kurayb, al-Ashja'i, Sufyan and Ata': "you are only a warner", he said, you, Muhammad, are a warner and God is the Guide. Report 20145, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Amr ibn Awn, Hushaym, Abd al-Malik and Qays: "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", he said, the "warner" is the Prophet, "and for every people a guide", he said, God is the guide of every people. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 354}

Report 20146, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd: "you are only a warner, and for every people a guide", he says: you, Muhammad, are a warner, and I am the guide of every people. Report 20147, from al-Dahhak, through al-Husayn, Abu Mu'adh and Ubayd ibn Sulayman: the "warner" is Muhammad and the "guide" is God, mighty and exalted. Others said that "the guide" in this place means a prophet. Report 20148, from Mujahid, through Ibn Bashshar, Abu Asim, Sufyan and Layth: the "warner" is Muhammad, "and for every people a guide", he said, a prophet. Report 20149, from Mujahid, through Ibn Humayd, Hakkam, Anbasa, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman and al-Qasim ibn Abi Bazza: a prophet. Reports 20150 and 20151, from Mujahid, through Jarir and Layth, and al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Asbat, Abd al-Malik and Qays: for every people a prophet, and the warner is Muhammad. Reports 20152 and 20153, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Abd al-Malik and Qays, and Shababa, Waraqa and Ibn Abi Najih: {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 355}

"and for every people a guide", he means for every people a prophet. Report 20154, from Mujahid, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl: a prophet. Report 20155, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: a prophet who calls them to God. Report 20156, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: for every people a prophet; the "guide" is the Prophet and the "warner" is also the Prophet; he cited [[passage/quran-35-24|35:24]] and [[passage/quran-53-56|53:56]] and said, a prophet among the prophets. Others said it is rather: for every people a leader. Report 20157, from Abu Salih, through Abu Kurayb, Jabir ibn Nuh and Isma'il ibn Abi Khalid: you, Muhammad, are only a warner, and every people has leaders. Report 20158, from Abu Salih, through al-Ashja'i: every people has leaders. Report 20159, from Abu al-Aliyah, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Abd Allah ibn Abi Ja'far, his father and al-Rabi': the "guide" is the leader, the leader is the imam, and the imam is the deed. Report 20160, from Yahya ibn Rafi', through al-Hasan, Muhammad ibn Yazid and Isma'il: {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 356}

a leader. Others said: it is Ali ibn Abi Talib. Report 20161, from Ibn Abbas, through Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Sufi, al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn al-Ansari, Mu'adh ibn Muslim, Ata' ibn al-Sa'ib and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: when the verse was sent down the Prophet put his hand on his chest and said, I am the warner, "and for every people a guide", and gestured with his hand to Ali's shoulder, saying, you are the guide, Ali; through you the guided are guided after me. In a note the editor says that al-Hasan ibn al-Husayn al-Ansari was one of the leaders of the Shi'a, not truthful and not to be relied on as evidence, that Mu'adh ibn Muslim is unknown, and that the report is ruined on every side; he adds that al-Dhahabi and Ibn Hajar mention it and say the fault lies with Mu'adh, while he judges the fault lies with both. Others said: its meaning is that every people has a caller. Report 20162, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abd Allah, Mu'awiya and Ali: "and for every people a guide", he said, a caller. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 357}

Al-Tabari says he has already explained the meaning of "guidance", that it is the followed imam who goes before the people. That being so, it may be God, who guides His creation, whose creatures follow His guidance and take His command and prohibition as their imam; it may be God's prophet, whom his community follows; it may be one of the imams who is followed, whose way and path his companions take; and it may be a caller, whether to good or to evil. That being so, he holds there is no opinion more correct than to say as He, mighty be His praise, said: that Muhammad is the warner of those to whom he was sent with the warning, and that every people has a guide who guides them, whom they follow and take as their imam. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 358}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
