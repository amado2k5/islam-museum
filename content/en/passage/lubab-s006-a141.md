## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of al-An'am 6:141, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247).

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «وآتوا حقه يوم حصاده ولا تسرفوا».
