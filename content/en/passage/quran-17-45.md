## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when you recite the Qur'an, We have placed between you and those who do not believe in the Hereafter a hidden veil." And when you recite the Qur'an to those polytheists who do not believe in resurrection and do not acknowledge reward and punishment, We have placed between you and them a veil that prevents their hearts from understanding what you recite to them and thus benefiting from it, as a punishment from Us for their disbelief. And the veil here: it is the covering, as in Qatadah's saying: "And when you recite the Qur'an, We place between you and those who do not believe in the Hereafter a hidden veil." The hidden veil is a shield for their hearts to understand and benefit from. They obeyed Satan and he took control over them. From Ma'mar, from Qatadah, he said: it is the veil. In the Maqta, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
