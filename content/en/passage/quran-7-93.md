## Summary
Verse 93 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 93 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-93|7:93]], al-Tabari says: Shuayb turned away from them, leaving them when God's punishment came, and said, once sure the punishment had fallen on the people who denied him, in sorrow for them: my people, I conveyed my Lord's messages and delivered what He sent me with, warning you of His wrath for your persisting in unbelief and wronging people of their things, and I advised you by commanding you to obey God and forbidding disobedience; "how then should I grieve?" means: how can I grieve and ache for the destruction of a people who rejected God's oneness and denied His messenger? (14875) from Ibn Abbas and (14876) from al-Suddi that "asa" means "grieve"; (14877) from Ibn Ishaq that Shuayb was struck by sorrow for his people when he saw God's vengeance on them, and then said, consoling himself, what God tells of him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 572}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
