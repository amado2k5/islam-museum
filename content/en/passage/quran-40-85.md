## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "but their faith did not benefit them when they saw Our wrongdoing, the way of Allah which had been established among His servants, and the disbelievers were lost": Their belief in the oneness of Allah in this world would not benefit them when they saw His punishment descending and His punishment had come, because they believed when believing is no longer effective, as Allah's ruling had already passed from His knowledge, that whoever repents after the punishment from Allah to deny it will not benefit his repentance. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
