## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar: Abu Hurayra's report of the Prophet's love for the Ansar and their loyalty to him. Abu Hurayra follows it with, "No one has wronged them; may my father and mother be sacrificed for them: they gave him shelter and helped him". It is among the best-known texts on the virtue of the Ansar. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
