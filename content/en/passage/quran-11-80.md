**Commentary on verse 80 (p. 418)**

On [[passage/quran-11-80|11:80]] al-Tabari says: Lot said to his people, when they would go on with what they came for and he despaired of their answering anything he had offered, 'if only I had strength against you', helpers to aid me against you, 'or could take refuge in a strong support', that is, join a protecting clan that would keep you from me, I would stand between you and what you want of me in my guests; the answer to 'if' is omitted because the speech points to it. Report 18388, from al-Suddi: a strong support means a strong force, I would fight you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 418}
