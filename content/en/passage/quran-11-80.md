**Commentary on verse 80 (pp. 418-422)**

On [[passage/quran-11-80|11:80]] al-Tabari says: Lot said to his people, when they would go on with what they came for and he despaired of their answering anything he had offered, 'if only I had strength against you', helpers to aid me against you, 'or could take refuge in a strong support', that is, join a protecting clan that would keep you from me, I would stand between you and what you want of me in my guests; the answer to 'if' is omitted because the speech points to it. Report 18388, from al-Suddi: a strong support means a strong force, I would fight you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 418}

Reports 18389 and 18390, from Qatada, and 18391, from al-Hasan: a strong support is the clan, or a support among people; 18392, from Ibn Jurayj: it reached us that no prophet was sent after Lot except among a strong following of his people, down to the last Prophet; 18393, from Ibn Ishaq: a clan to protect me or partisans to help me, and I would stand between you and this; 18394, from Qatada: the clan. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 419}

Report 18395, from al-Hasan: when the verse came down the Prophet said that God had mercy on Lot, who had been taking refuge in a strong support; 18396, from al-Hasan, similarly, with the words, so why did he give way? 18397, from Abu Hurayra, from the Prophet: God's mercy on Lot, he was indeed taking refuge in a strong support when he said that to his people, and God sent no prophet after him except among a strong following of his people, 'strong following' meaning numbers and protection. Reports 18398, 18399 and 18400 are the same by several chains from Abu Hurayra, one being al-Zuhri's from Abu Salama and Sa'id b. al-Musayyab. The editor comments on the chains, calls the hadith of Muhammad b. 'Amr from Abu Salama sound, and notes that al-Hakim gave it in the Mustadrak. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 420}

Reports 18401, the same, and 18402, from Abu Hurayra: the Prophet said of 'or take refuge in a strong support' that he was taking refuge in a strong support, meaning God, and God sent no prophet after him except among a strong following of his people; 18403, from Abu Hurayra, with the same prayer for Lot; 18404, from Abu Hurayra, likewise, the editor noting that the chain is sound and that al-Bukhari relates it in his Sahih by this route. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 421}

Report 18405, from Qatada: it was told us that when the Prophet read this verse, or came to it, he would say that God had mercy on Lot, who had been taking refuge in a strong support, and that God sent no prophet after Lot except among a strong following of his people, until He sent your Prophet among his. One says 'awaytu ilayka' for I came to you and joined you, and al-Tabari cites a rajaz line. It was said that when Lot said this the messengers were displeased with him; report 18406, from Wahb b. Munabbih: Lot said 'if only I had strength against you', and the messengers were displeased and said, your support is strong indeed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 422}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
