**Commentary on verse 112 (pp. 499-500)**

On [[passage/quran-11-112|11:112]] God says to His Prophet Muhammad: stand firm, Muhammad, in your Lord's command and the religion He sent you with and the call to it, as your Lord commanded you, 'and those who turned with you', that is, whoever returned with you to obedience to God and to doing what his Lord commanded after his disbelief, 'and do not transgress', that is, do not go beyond His command to what He forbade you; 'He sees what you do' means your Lord, people, sees all you do, its obedience and its disobedience, with knowledge of it, nothing hidden from Him. So fear God, people, that your Lord should find you acting against His command, for He knows what you do and is watching you. Ibn 'Uyayna said of 'stand firm as you were commanded' in report 18600: stand firm on the Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 499}

Report 18601, from Ibn Zayd: transgression is opposing God and committing disobedience to Him; that is transgression. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 500}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
