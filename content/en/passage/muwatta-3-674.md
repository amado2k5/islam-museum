## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to adorn his daughters and his slave-girls with gold and did not pay zakat from their jewellery. The editor notes that Abu Musab, al-Shafii in his Musnad, al-Bayhaqi, al-Shaybani and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.341}
