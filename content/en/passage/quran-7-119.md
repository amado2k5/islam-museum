**Commentary on verse 119 (p. 32)**

Al-Tabari says: Moses overcame Pharaoh and his hosts "there", that is at that point, "and they turned about humbled", that is, they left that place in abasement, subdued. One says "the man became small, sagheera", in several noun forms. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "turned about" and "abased". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 32}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
