**Commentary on verse 18 (p. 416)**

On "for those who responded to their Lord is the best reward; and those who did not respond to Him, if they had all that is on earth and its like with it, would offer it as ransom; those have an evil reckoning, and their refuge is Hell, and wretched is the resting place", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: those who responded to God, believing in Him when He called them to faith in Him, obeying Him, following His messenger and believing him in what he brought from God, "for them is the best reward", which is Paradise. Report 20326, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "for those who responded to their Lord is the best reward", which is Paradise. On "and those who did not respond to Him, if they had all that is on earth and its like with it, would offer it as ransom" he says: as for those who did not respond to God when He called them to His oneness and to acknowledge His lordship, who did not obey Him in what He commanded and did not follow His messenger so as to believe him in what he brought from his Lord, if they owned all that is on earth of anything and its like with it, and the like of it were accepted from them, and accepted in place of the punishment God has prepared for them in the Fire of Hell and as compensation, they would ransom themselves with it from it. God says "those have an evil reckoning" {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 416}
