## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And indeed, among you except that if he brings it, it will be upon your Lord to be decreed": And among you, O people, except that the one who will enter Hell, it is incumbent upon your Lord, O Muhammad, to bring it to them as a decree and decree. That has been decreed and obligatory in the Mother of the Book. Scholars differ regarding the meaning of the arrival mentioned by Allah in this context, with some saying: the entrance. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Jurayj, and Ata.
