## Summary
Malik transmits from Musa ibn Maysara, from Said ibn Abi Hind, from Abu Musa al-Ashari, that the Messenger of God said: whoever plays backgammon has disobeyed God and His Messenger. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.548}
