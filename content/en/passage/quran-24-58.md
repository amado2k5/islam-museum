## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, let those who have your right hands possess your right hand ask your permission. And those of you who have not reached puberty three times before the dawn prayer, and when you put down your garments at noon, and after the evening prayer, there are three awrahs for you, and upon them there is no blame upon you or upon them. After them, they circumambulate you, some of you over others. Thus Allah makes clear to you the verses, and Allah is Knowing and Wise": The scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning. By saying: "So that those whose right hands possess your right hand may ask your permission," some said: I mean men and not women, and they forbade entering upon them at these three times except with permission. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
