## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 11 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And We made the day for livelihood”: We made the day a source of light for you to spread out in it for your livelihood, to act in it for your worldly affairs, and to seek God’s bounty therein. He, may His praise be exalted, made the day, since it is the means for His servants to seek their livelihood, a means of livelihood, as in the poet’s saying: “And the one burdened with worries, when worries gather at the darkness of night, his pillow is his only rest, he does not sleep.” He makes the pillow the one who does not sleep, and the meaning is for the one who uses the pillow. On the authority of Ibn Abi Najih, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding the phrase “the day for livelihood,” he said: They seek God’s bounty therein. In this section, he quotes sayings from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
