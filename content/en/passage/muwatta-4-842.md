## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: whoever spreads out the making up of Ramadan need not repeat it, and it suffices him; but what I prefer is that he follows it consecutively. The editor notes that Abu Musab (823) and Suwayd (470) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.409}
