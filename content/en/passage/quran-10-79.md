**Commentary on verse 79 (p. 159)**

On [[passage/quran-10-79|10:79]] al-Tabari says: Pharaoh said to his people, bring me every magician skilled in magic. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'magic' and 'skilled'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 159}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
