## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if you deny, then nations before you have lied. And the duty of the Messenger is only to deliver the clear message." And if you deny, O people, our Messenger Muhammad regarding what he called you to in worshiping your Lord who created you and provided for you, and disassociating himself from idols, then groups before you denied their messengers regarding what the messengers called them to of the truth. Allah's wrath descended upon them, and His punishment descended upon them immediately. Your path is their way regarding what has been revealed to you by your denial of it. "And the duty of the Messenger is only to deliver the clear message," meaning: Muhammad only has to deliver his message to you and deliver to you what he commanded his Lord to deliver to you. By 'clear communication' means: it clarifies to the one who heard it what is intended and understands what is meant by it.
