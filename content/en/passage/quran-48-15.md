## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who lagged behind will say, 'When you set out to take spoils to take them, leave us to follow you.' They want to alter the words of Allah. Say, 'You will not follow us.' Allah said before. They will say, 'Rather, you envy us.' Rather, they understood only a little.": He will say, O Muhammad, those who are left behind among their families about your companionship. When you and your companions set out on your journey, that is to what Allah has promised you and upon them of the spoils of war, 'to take it.' And that is what Allah promised the people of. Hudaybiyyah is one of the spoils of Khaybar. "Leave us to follow you" to Khaybar, and we witness with you the fighting of its people. "They want to change the words of God," meaning they want to change God's promise that He promised the people of Hudaybiyyah, because God made the spoils of Khaybar their own and promised them that as compensation for the spoils of the people of Mecca if they turned away from them on peace and did not harm them at all. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
