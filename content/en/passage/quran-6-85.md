## Summary
Verse 85 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 85 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "and Zechariah, John, Jesus and Elijah, each of the righteous" al-Tabari says: and we guided also, to the like of what we guided Noah to of guidance and right, of his offspring Zechariah ibn Iddo ibn Berechiah, John ibn Zechariah, Jesus ibn Mary daughter of Imran ibn Yashim ibn Amun ibn Hezekiah, and "Elijah". They differed over Elijah: Ibn Ishaq said he is Elijah ibn Yasa ibn Phinehas ibn Eleazar ibn Aaron ibn Imran, nephew of Moses; others said he is Idris, among whom Abdullah ibn Mas'ud is reported, and 13515, from Ibn Mas'ud: Idris is Ilyas and Israel is Jacob. The genealogists say Idris is the grandfather of Noah ibn Lamech ibn Methuselah ibn Enoch, Enoch being Idris ibn Jared ibn Mahalalel, and so it is reported from Wahb ibn Munabbih. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 508}

The editor says of the genealogies in this verse that Zechariah is "Zechariah ibn Berechiah ibn Iddo" in the people's book and was named from his grandfather Iddo because his father Berechiah died in the prime of youth and so, by custom, he was named after the grandfather who was better known than his father; that print has "Barkiya" and he kept al-Tabari's history; that "Imran ibn Ashim ibn Amur" in print is an error, the right form being from earlier and from the history; that the history has "ibn Yasin" for "Yasa"; and, in 13515, he identifies Ubayda ibn Rabi'a al-Kufi, who related from Ibn Mas'ud and Uthman and from whom al-Sha'bi and Abu Ishaq al-Sabi'i related, says "Ibn Ishaq" in manuscript and print is a plain error for "Abu Ishaq", and that al-Bukhari mentioned the report in suspended form and Ibn Hajar said Abd ibn Humayd and Ibn Abi Hatim connected it with a good chain.

What the genealogists say is nearer the right, since God ascribed Elijah in this verse to Noah and made him of his offspring, and Noah is the son of Idris according to the people of knowledge, and it is impossible that the grandfather of his father be ascribed as being of his offspring. As for "each of the righteous", it says of those we mentioned of these we named, among the righteous, meaning Zechariah, John, Jesus and Elijah. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 510}

The editor refers for "each" and "righteous" to earlier passages.

Verse 85 closes here on p. 510, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-86|6:86]].
