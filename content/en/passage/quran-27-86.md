## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 86 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have they not seen that We made the night to rest therein and the day to see? Indeed in that are signs for people who believe": Have these deniers of Our signs not seen Our division of night and day, and Our opposition between them by making this a dwelling place for them to dwell in, and to soothe their bodies from the fatigue of movement and fluctuations during the day? This is a luminous light in which they see things and observe them, turning in it for their livelihood, contemplating it, reflecting on it, and knowing that the source of that is the God who is beyond nothing, and who is not able to cause the death of the living or to bring the dead back to life After death, just as it was not possible for him to go during the day and come at night, and to come during the day and go at night despite their differing states: "Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe."
