## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses this verse in a single passage that includes [[passage/quran-85-19|85:19]] to [[passage/quran-85-22|85:22]] In summary, the Preserved Tablet is interpreted as the inscription of the Quran. Two readings are mentioned: the lowered form of "preserved" (mahfuz) as an adjective for the Tablet, according to the scholars of Hijaz, Kufa, and Basra; and the nominative form as an adjective for the Quran, according to Nafi' and Ibn Muhaysin. Both readings are considered correct.
