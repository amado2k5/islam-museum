## Summary
Verse 11 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 11 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "say: travel in the land, then see what was the end of the deniers" al-Tabari says: say, Muhammad, to these who hold idols and rivals equal to me, who deny you and reject the truth of what I brought them: travel in the land, that is, go about in the lands of those before them who denied their messengers and rejected my signs, their like and counterparts among people, then see what was the end of the deniers, that is, how their denial ended for them in destruction and ruin, the disgrace and shame of this world, and God's wrath that came upon them, of perishing and desolation... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 272}

We stop here at the foot of p. 272, within al-Tabari's explanation of "travel in the land, then see what was the end of the deniers", at "of perishing and desolation", which continues on p. 273.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
