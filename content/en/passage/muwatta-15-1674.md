## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd Rabbihi ibn Said, from Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Harith al-Taymi, that Nufay asked Zayd ibn Thabit, saying he had divorced a free woman twice. Zayd said: she is forbidden to you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.86}
