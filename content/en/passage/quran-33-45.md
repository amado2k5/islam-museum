## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 45 to 48 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "O Prophet, indeed We have sent you as a witness, a bringer of good tidings, and a warner": O Muhammad, "Indeed, We have sent you as a witness" over your nation by conveying to them what We sent you with of the message, and giving them glad tidings of Paradise if they believe you and act upon what you brought them from your Lord, and "as a warner" of the Fire, that they will enter it and be punished by it if they deny you and contradict what you brought them from Allah. And what we have said about this is said by the people of interpretation. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
