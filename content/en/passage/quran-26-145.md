## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 141 to 145 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And I do not ask you for any reward for it. "Indeed, my reward is only from the Lord of the worlds," meaning: My reward and recompense are only with the Lord of all that is in the heavens, whatever is on the earth, and all that is between them of creation.
