## Summary
Abdullah ibn Umar said: whoever vows a camel garlands it with two sandals and marks it, then slaughters it at the House or at Mina on the day of sacrifice, and it has no place of release other than that. Whoever vows a camel or a cow may slaughter it wherever he wishes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.529}
