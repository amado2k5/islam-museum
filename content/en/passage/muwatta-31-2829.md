## Summary
It reached Malik that Abdullah ibn Masud used to say: hold to truthfulness, for it leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise; beware of lying, for it leads to wickedness, and wickedness leads to the Fire. The editor notes that the two Shaykhs transmitted its meaning raised, from the hadith of Abu Wa'il Shaqiq ibn Salama from Ibn Masud. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.588}
