## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: the offering is what is garlanded and marked and stopped with at Arafat. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1208), Suwayd (525) and Ibn Wahb (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.510}
