## Summary
Malik said it reached him that the full blood-money is due for each of the pair in a human, and the full blood-money is due for the tongue, and for the two ears if their hearing is lost, whether they are shaved off or not, and for the male organ the full blood-money, and for both testicles the full blood-money. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.426}
