## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he broke his fast in Ramadan and intended the pilgrimage, did not take anything from his head or beard until he made the pilgrimage. Malik said: this is not for people. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.531}
