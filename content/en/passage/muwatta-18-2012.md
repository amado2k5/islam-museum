## Summary
Malik says of a man who gives money in profit-sharing to a man and to his slave, both working with it: that is lawful and not harmful, because the profit is the property of the slave, and does not belong to the master unless he takes it from him; it is like the rest of his earnings. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.223}
