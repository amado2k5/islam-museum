## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 4 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Indeed, to Allah belongs the pure religion, and those who have taken besides Him as allies. We worship them only to bring us nearer to Allah. Indeed, Allah judges between them concerning that in which they differ. Indeed, Allah does not guide whoever is a liar and disbeliever." This means that "Allah does not guide" to the truth and His religion, Islam, and acknowledging His oneness. Thus, "whoever is a liar," fabricated against Allah, accuses falsehood, attributes falsehood to Him, attributes to Him what is not His attribute, and claims to have a son slandered against Him, disbelievers in His blessings, and deny His Lordship. And His saying: "If Allah had willed to take a child," the Exalted says: If Allah had willed to take a child, and it is not appropriate for Him, He would have chosen from among what He creates as He wills. He means: He would have chosen from His creation whatever He wills.
