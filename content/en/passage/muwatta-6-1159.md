## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Salim and Ubaydallah, the two sons of Abdallah ibn Umar, that their father used to send his household and children from Muzdalifa to Mina, so that they would pray the dawn prayer at Mina and throw the pebbles before people came. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.524}
