## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Sulayman ibn Yasar said Umar ibn al-Khattab, Uthman ibn Affan and Zayd ibn Thabit assigned the grandfather a third alongside the brothers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.12}
