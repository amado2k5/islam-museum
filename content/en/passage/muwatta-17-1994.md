## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger said: let none of you sell over another's sale. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the addition 'and do not intercept the goods until they are brought down to the markets' is preserved from the report of Abu al-Zinad, not from Yahya's transmission. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.216}
