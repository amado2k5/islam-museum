## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Or say, 'If Allah had guided me, I would have been among the righteous': 'And turn to your Lord, O people, and submit to Him.' That one soul on the Day of Resurrection should not say: 'Oh, regret for what I neglected in the sake of Allah, in the matter of Allah,' and not say: 'If Allah had guided me to the truth and granted me guidance to righteousness, I would have been among those who feared Him by obeying Him and following His pleasure,' or not to say anything else when you see Allah's punishment and witness it, saying: 'If only I had a chance to return,' you would say: 'If only I could return to this world, I would be among the doers of good,' those who did good in obeying their Lord and acting upon what the messengers commanded them. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
