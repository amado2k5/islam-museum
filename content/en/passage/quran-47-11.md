## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "That is because Allah is the protector of those who believe, and the disbelievers have no protector": This act we did to these two groups: the faith group and the disbelief group, from supporting us to the faith team in God, strengthening their feet, and destroying the disbelief group "that God is the protector of those who believe." He says: because God is the protector of those who believe in Him and obeys His Messenger. Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "That is because Allah is the protector of those who believe," he said: their guardian. It has been mentioned to us that this is in Abdullah's recitation: "That is because Allah is the protector of those who believe" and "that" on the table in our Qur'ans: "Indeed, your guardian is Allah and His Messenger": "Your protector is only Allah" in his recitation. And His saying: "And that the disbelievers have no protector" means: that the disbelievers have no protector or supporter. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
