## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 28 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "That is because they followed what angered Allah and disliked His pleasure, so His pleasure was nullified": The angels do what they described as hypocrites because they followed what angered Allah, so He angered Him from obeying Satan, "and they hated His pleasure," meaning: they hated what pleases them by fighting the disbelievers, after what He has imposed upon them. And His saying: "So He nullified their deeds," means: God nullified the reward of their deeds and removed them, because they were done without His approval or love, so they were nullified and did not benefit the doer.
