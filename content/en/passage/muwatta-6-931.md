## Summary
A report that Malik says reached him: the Messenger of God entered ihram for the lesser pilgrimage from al-Jiranah. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said he only knows this traced from the hadith of Muharrish al-Kabi al-Khuza'i, that the hadith is sound through the transmission of the people of Mecca, and that al-Tirmidhi (935) said it is good and strange. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.446}
