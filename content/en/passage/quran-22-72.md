## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when Our verses are recited to them with clear proofs that appear in the faces of those who disbelieve, they almost seize those who recite Our verses to them. Say, 'Shall I inform you of good news of that? Of that is the Fire. Allah has prepared it for those who disbelieve, and wretched is the destination": And when what is recited to the polytheists of Quraysh, worshippers besides Allah, what has not been revealed by authority is "Our verses," meaning: the verses of the Qur'an, "clear proofs," meaning their clear proofs and evidences in what was revealed about them. "You can recognize in the faces of those who disbelieve in evil" means: it is clear in their faces what the people of faith in God deny in terms of their alteration, because they hear the Qur'an. And His saying: "They almost overpower those to whom Our verses are recited," it means: They almost attack those who recite verses of the Book of God to them from the Prophet's companions, because they strongly dislike hearing the Qur'an and reciting to them. And similarly, as we said in the interpretation of the phrase "they set," the interpreters said. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
