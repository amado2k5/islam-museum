## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if it were not for you, when you heard it, you said, 'Glory be to You, this is a great slander': 'And were it not?' 'And if not,' O you who are involved in the slander, brought by a group of you, 'when you heard it' from those who brought it, 'you said,' 'It is not permissible for us to speak about this, nor should we utter it.' 'Glory be to You, this is a great slander,' a purification for You, O Lord, and a disavowal to You from what these people have brought. 'This is a great slander,' meaning: This statement is a great slander.
