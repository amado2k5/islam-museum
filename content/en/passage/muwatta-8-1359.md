## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the matter among us about one who says 'a walk to the House of God is upon me' is that if he grows weak he rides, then returns and walks from where he grew weak. If he cannot walk, he walks as far as he can and then rides, and an offering is owed by him, a camel, cow or sheep, if he finds nothing but that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.608}
