**Commentary on verse 74: "Will they not turn to God and ask His forgiveness", pp. 483-484**

Al-Tabari says: will these two unbelieving parties, one saying God is the Messiah son of Mary and the other that God is the third of three, not turn back from what they said, repent of their unbelief and ask their Lord for forgiveness? God forgives the sins of those who repent and return to obedience after disobedience, and is merciful to them in accepting their repentance, passing over their earlier offences.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 484}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
