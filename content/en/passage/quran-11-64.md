**Commentary on verse 64 (p. 371)**

On [[passage/quran-11-64|11:64]] al-Tabari reports Salih's saying to his people of Thamud when they said 'we are in grave doubt about what you call us to' and asked him for a sign of what he called them to: 'my people, this is God's she-camel, a sign for you', a proof, token and indication of the truth of what I call you to; 'so leave her to graze on God's earth' for her sustenance and cost are not on you; 'and do not touch her with harm' means do not kill her or wound her by hamstringing; 'or a near punishment will seize you' means if you touch her with harm a punishment from God, not far off, will seize you and destroy you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 371}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
