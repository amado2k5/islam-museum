**Commentary on verse 72: "Those who say God is the Messiah son of Mary have disbelieved", p. 480**

Al-Tabari says: this is God's report of one of the trials by which He tested the Israelites who thought there would be no trial. Among the things He tried them with, and in which they broke His covenant and changed His pledge not to worship any but Him, was His servant Jesus son of Mary, whom He created and at whose hands He worked what He worked at the hands of many of His messengers; and in unbelief they said "He is God". Al-Tabari says this is the saying of the Jacobites among the Christians. When God tried them, he says, they associated a partner with Him, saying it of a servant like themselves, a human of known lineage, born of humans, who called them to His oneness.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 480}

**Commentary on verse 72, continued: "Worship God, my Lord and your Lord", p. 481**

Al-Tabari goes on: Jesus acknowledged to them that God was his Lord and theirs and forbade them to associate anything with Him, yet they said "He is their God", out of ignorance of God and unbelief; and it is not fitting for God to beget or be begotten. "Worship God, my Lord and your Lord" means give worship and humility to the One to whom everything is humbled; "my Lord and your Lord" means my owner and yours, who created me and you. "Whoever associates others with God, God has forbidden him Paradise" means forbidden him to dwell in it in the hereafter, and "his refuge is the Fire" means his return and place is the Fire of Hell; the wrongdoers have no helpers to save them from God on the day of resurrection when He brings them to Hell.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 481}
