## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 14 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By the sun and its brightness”: “By the sun and its brightness,” this is an oath by which our Lord, may He be exalted, swears by the sun and its brightness. The meaning is: I swear by the sun and by the brightness of the sun. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of “its brightness.” Some said: It means the sun and the day, and he used to say: “The brightness” refers to the entire day. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
