## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So be patient. Indeed, the promise of Allah is true. So whether We show you some of what We have promised or take you at your death, to Us they will be returned." So be patient, O Muhammad, with what these polytheists dispute with you regarding the verses of Allah that We have revealed to you, and against their denial of you. Indeed, Allah has fulfilled for you in them what He promised you of victory over them, superiority over them, and the imposition of punishment upon them, just as We did regarding Moses son of Imran and those who denied him. "So whether We show you some of what We have promised," He says, the Exalted: So either We show you, O Muhammad, some of the punishment and curse that these polytheists promise will befall them, or We will take you before that befalls them, "and to Us they will return," meaning: to us is your fate and theirs. Then we judge between you and them with truth by immortalizing them in the Fire and honoring you by our presence in the gardens of bliss.
