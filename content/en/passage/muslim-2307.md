## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on the Prophet's courage, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim: he was first to reach the source of the alarm on Abu Talha's horse, with his sword on his neck. It is the best-known scene of his courage in the collections. {cite:sahih-muslim}
