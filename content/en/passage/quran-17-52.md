## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 53 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "The Day He calls you, and you respond with His praise and think that you have stayed a little longer": Say, perhaps your resurrection, O polytheists, is near, that is the Day your Lord will call you to leave your graves to the station of the Resurrection, "and you will respond with His praise." Interpreters differ regarding the meaning of the phrase: "So you respond by praising Him," and some said: You respond by His command. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Jurayj.
