## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single section. He explains the phrase "that We may bring forth thereby grain and vegetation" as follows: We bring forth grain with the water We send down from the rain-bearing clouds to the earth. Grain encompasses all that is contained within the sheaths of the harvested crops, and it is the plural of grain, just as barley is the plural of barley grain, and dates are the plural of date. As for vegetation, it refers to the fodder that is grazed, such as grass and crops. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
