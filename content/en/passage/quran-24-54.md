## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, 'Obey Allah and obey the Messenger.' But if you turn away, then upon him is only what is burdened, and upon you is what you are burdened. And if you obey him, you will be guided. And the Messenger is not above him except the clear message." "Say," O Muhammad, to those who swear by Allah with the effort of their oaths, for if you command them, they will go out, and others from your nation: "Obey Allah, O people, in what He has commanded you and forbidden you to do." "And obey the Messenger," for obeying him is obedience to God. "But if you turn away," he means: if you turn away and reflect on what the Messenger of God commanded you or forbade you to do, and refuse to submit to his judgment for you and upon you. "Then he is only responsible for what he has been told," meaning: He is only responsible for doing what he was commanded to do, conveying God's message to you, according to what he has been tasked with delivering. "And upon you is what you have been told," he means: And upon you, O people, to do what is obligatory and obligatory upon you in following His Messenger and ending obedience to him in what he commanded you and forbade.
