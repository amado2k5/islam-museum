## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 31 to 32 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that Moses was called to throw down his staff, and he threw it down, and it became a serpent striving for it. When he saw it trembling, that is, moving and restlessly as if it were a jinn, a type of great serpent, it turned away and fled. It was called to accept and not fear, and to move his hand into his pocket that came out white without harm, and to fold his wing from awe.
