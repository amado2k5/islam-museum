## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 11 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind": Say, O Muhammad, seek refuge in "the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind," and He is the Sovereign of all creation: humans, jinn, and others, informing him that whoever honored people and the believers their Lord was the king of those who honored Him, and that this is in His dominion and authority, His power prevails over Him, and that He is more deserving of reverence and more deserving of worship than those who honor and worship Him than others. And his saying: In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
