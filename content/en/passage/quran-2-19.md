## In al-Tabari's commentary: "or like a rainstorm from the sky"
Al-Tabari derives "sayyib" from "saba al-matar yasubu", the rain coming down, its original form being "sayyub" with the waw turned to ya and doubled, and cites lines by Alqama and others; he gives Ibn Abbas and Ata that the sayyib is rain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 333} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 334}
