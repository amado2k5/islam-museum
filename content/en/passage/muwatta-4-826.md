## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: people of knowledge said there is no harm in fasting all the year if one breaks the fast on the days the Prophet forbade fasting: the days of Mina, the day of sacrifice and the day of breaking the fast, as it reached us. That is what is most beloved to him on this. The editor notes that Abu Musab (859 and 894) and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.404}
