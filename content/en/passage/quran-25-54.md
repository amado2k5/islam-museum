## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He it is Who created from the water a human and made him a lineage and a mate, and your Lord is Powerful": And Allah is the One who created from the sperm a human and made him lineage, which is seven, and a marriage, which is five. Also: I narrated from Al-Husayn, he said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say regarding the phrase "So He made him lineage and in-law": lineage is seven, His saying: "Your mothers have been forbidden to you..." Up to His saying: 'And the daughters of the sister' and the son-in-law are five, His saying: 'And your mothers who nursed you...' Up to His saying: 'And the widowed daughters of your sons from your descendants,' and His saying: 'And your Lord is Powerful,' meaning: And your Lord, O Muhammad, is capable of creating whatever He wills of creation and directing them as He wills and wills.
