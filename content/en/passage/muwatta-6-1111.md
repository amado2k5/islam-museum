## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa that his father said: if you are compelled to use your sacrificial camel, ride it without overburdening it; and if you are compelled to take its milk, drink after its young has had enough; and when you slaughter it, slaughter its young with it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1205), Suwayd (528) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.509}
