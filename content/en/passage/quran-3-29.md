## Verse 29 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, p. 318)

**What is hidden and what is shown.** Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-29|3:29]]) says the sense is: say, Muhammad, to those you told not to ally with the unbelievers, whether you conceal allegiance to the unbelievers or show it by your tongues and deeds, God knows it and nothing is hidden from Him; so do not harbour affection for them or show allegiance, lest you meet a punishment you cannot bear, for He counts it against you to repay good with good and evil with its like. Musa, from Amr, from Asbat, from al-Suddi (6839), says He told them He knows what they concealed and what they showed. Al-Tabari adds that if nothing in heaven or earth is hidden from Him, how could what is in the hearts of those who take unbelievers as allies be hidden, whether inclination toward them in love or the help they show in word and deed? God's power, he says, means that He can hasten punishment for their allegiance and do whatever He wills without difficulty. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 318}
