## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieve said, 'Why was the Qur'an not sent down to him all at once?' Thus We would have strengthened your heart and recited it with great reverence": "And those who disbelieve" said, by Allah, 'Why had the Qur'an not been sent down to him,' meaning: Was the Qur'an not revealed to Muhammad 'all at once' just as the Torah was revealed to Moses in one sentence? Allah said: 'Thus so that We may strengthen your heart'—His revelation to you verse after verse, and the thing after the thing, so that We may strengthen your heart by it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ibrahim.
