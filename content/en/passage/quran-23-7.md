## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 7 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So whoever seeks after that - those are the enemies": so the adulterer is called from the aggressors. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "Those are the transgressors," he said: those who exceed the lawful to the unlawful. From Ata, from Abu Abd al-Rahman, regarding the phrase: "Whoever seeks beyond that, those are the transgressors," he said: Whoever commits adultery is 'Ad.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
