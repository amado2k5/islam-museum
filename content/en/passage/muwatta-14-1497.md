## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, Salim ibn Abdullah and Sulayman ibn Yasar used to say that if a father marries off a virgin daughter without her consent, the marriage binds her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.30}
