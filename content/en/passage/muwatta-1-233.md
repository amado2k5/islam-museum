## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on saying amen behind the imam: the Prophet told that whoever says amen and whose amen coincides with the amen of the angels in heaven is forgiven his past sin. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad, al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.141}
