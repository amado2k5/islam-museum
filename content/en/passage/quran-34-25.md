## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, 'Do not ask about the blindness of our crime, nor do we ask about what you do': Say to these polytheists: One of our groups is guided and the other is misguided. You will not be questioned about what we have committed of crime or what we have carried in sin, nor will we be questioned about what you do. Say to them: Our Lord will gather us together with Him on the Day of Resurrection, then open the truth between us. He says: Then He judges between us with justice, and then it becomes clear which one among us is guided from the misguided (and He is the Opener, the All-Knowing). He says: By God, the Judge, the All-Knowing in judging between His creation, because nothing is hidden from Him, and He does not need witnesses to distinguish the right from the false. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
