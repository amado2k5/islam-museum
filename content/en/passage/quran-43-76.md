## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 74 to 76 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We did not wrong them, but they were the wrongdoers": And We did not wrong these criminals by what We did to them. We did not inform you, O people, that We did to them the torment of Hell, "but they were the wrongdoers" by worshiping Him in this world, except for those who worshipped Him, disbelieved in God, and denied His oneness.
