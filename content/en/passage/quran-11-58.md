**Commentary on verse 58 (p. 366)**

On [[passage/quran-11-58|11:58]] al-Tabari says: when Our punishment came to Hud's people We saved from it Hud and those who believed in God with him, 'by mercy from Us', that is, by His favour and blessing to them, 'and We saved them from a harsh punishment', meaning We saved them also from a harsh punishment on the Day of Resurrection, as We saved them in the world from the anger We sent on 'Ad. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'harshness'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 366}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
