## Summary
Malik transmits from a trustworthy man with him, from Bukayr ibn Abdallah ibn al-Ashajj, from Busr ibn Said, from Abu Said al-Khudri, from Abu Musa al-Ashari, that the Messenger of God said: seeking permission is three times; if you are given permission, enter; otherwise return. The editor notes that the trustworthy man in Malik's transmission here is said to be Makhrama ibn Bukayr, and that Ibn Main rated him trustworthy. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.553}
