## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that Sulayman ibn Yasar said: the fodder of Saad ibn Abi Waqqas's donkey ran out, and he said to his slave: take some of your family's wheat and buy barley with it, and take only the same measure. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.173}
