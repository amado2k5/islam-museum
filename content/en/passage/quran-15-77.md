## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Indeed, in that is a sign for the believers," meaning that in God's act against the people of Lot, there is a clear sign and indication for those who believe in God to take revenge on the disbelievers and save the believers from His punishment. He narrates from Sa'id ibn Jubayr that the sign is a sign, like a man telling his family that a sign between me and you is that I will send you my ring; and from Ibn Abbas, it is like a man sending his ring to his family, and when they see it, they know it is true.
