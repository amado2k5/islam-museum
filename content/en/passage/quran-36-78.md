## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 77–79. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among interpreters regarding who referred to man in 'Did he not see that man, We created him from a drop of fluid?': Some said, 'He is Abu ibn Khalaf,' and he is a clear opponent, and he gave us an example and forgot his creation. He said, 'Who gives life to the bones while they are decayed?'
