## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46-47 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Or do you ask them for a reward, so they are burdened with debt?”: “O Muhammad, do you ask these polytheists for a reward for the advice you brought them and the truth you called them to? ‘So they are burdened with debt’ means they are burdened by the cost of that reward.
