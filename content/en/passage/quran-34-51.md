## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if you see when they are terrified, do not miss it, and they will be taken from a nearby place": 'And if you see, O Muhammad, when they are frightened.' Interpreters differed regarding what is meant by this verse. Some said: It refers to those polytheists whom Allah described by saying: "And when clear proofs are recited to them, they say, 'This is nothing but a man who wants to turn you away from what your forefathers used to worship.'" He means: "When they were terrified, do not be lost, and they took from a nearby place" at the time of Allah's wrath upon them in this world. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
