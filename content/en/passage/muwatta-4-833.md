## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on vowed fasting and fasting on behalf of the dead. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he was asked about a man who vowed to fast a month: may he also observe voluntary fasts? He said: let him begin with the vow before the voluntary fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik (472). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.406}
