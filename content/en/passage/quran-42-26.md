## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He will respond to those who believe and do righteous deeds and increase them of His bounty; but the disbelievers will have a severe punishment": Those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, do what Allah has commanded them, and refrain from what He has forbidden them, will answer each other. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibrahim.
