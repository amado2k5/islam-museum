## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 21–22. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among reciters regarding 'iswah' with damma and kasra, and explains that it is a reproach from God to those who lag behind the Prophet, as they had a good example for those who hope in God and the Last Day. He then mentions that when the believers saw the confederates, they said, 'This is what God and His Messenger promised us,' and God and His Messenger spoke the truth, and it only increased their faith and submission.
