**Commentary on verse 11 (pp. 152-153)**

Al-Tabari says: if these polytheists whom I told you, believers, to kill return from their unbelief and polytheism with God to belief in Him and His messenger and turn to His obedience, establish the prescribed prayer, performing it with its limits, and pay the obligatory zakat to its recipients, they are your brothers in the religion God commanded you, which is Islam; "We detail the verses" means We make clear God's proofs and evidences to His creation "for a people who know", who understand what is made clear to them, so We explain them in detail, unlike the ignorant who do not grasp His explanation or His firmly established verses. Report 16516, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: if they leave al-Lat and al-Uzza and testify that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is God's messenger, they are your brothers in religion. Report 16517, from Ibn Abbas through a man, through Ibn Waki, Hafs ibn Ghiyath and Layth: this verse made the blood of the people of the qibla sacred. Report 16518, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: prayer and zakat were made obligatory together, not separated; he recited the verse and refused to accept prayer without zakat, saying God have mercy on Abu Bakr, how discerning he was. Report 16519, from Abdullah, through Ahmad ibn Ishaq, Abu Ahmad, Sharik, Abu Ishaq and Abu Ubayda: you were commanded to establish prayer and pay zakat, and whoever does not pay zakat has no prayer. Al-Tabari adds that "your brothers" is in the nominative by an implied "so they are your brothers", since they had already been mentioned, as in [[passage/quran-33-5|33:5]], and the editor refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 153}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
