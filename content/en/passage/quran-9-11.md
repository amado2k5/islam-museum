**Commentary on verse 11 (p. 152)**

Al-Tabari says: if these polytheists whom I told you, believers, to kill return from their unbelief and polytheism with God to belief in Him and His messenger and turn to His obedience, establish the prescribed prayer, performing it with its limits, and pay the obligatory zakat to its recipients, they are your brothers in the religion God commanded you, which is Islam; "We detail the verses" means We make clear God's proofs and evidences to His creation "for a people who know", who understand what is made clear to them, so We explain them in detail, unlike the ignorant who do not grasp His explanation or His firmly established verses. Report 16516, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: if they leave al-Lat and al-Uzza and testify that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is God's messenger, they are your brothers in religion. Report 16517 begins, from Layth, through Ibn Waki and Hafs ibn Ghiyath. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 152}
