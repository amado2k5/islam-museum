## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 16 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Taste your trial, that which you were hastening": He means His mention by saying: "Taste your trial," meaning they are told: "Taste your trial," and they are called 'Taste your trial' as the meaning of the speech about it. And by His saying: "Your trial," he means your punishment and your fire. Interpreters differed on this, with some saying what we have said. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
