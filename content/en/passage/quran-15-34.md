## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "So get out of it," meaning come out of the heavens, "for you are accursed." The cursed is derived from the object to the active participle, which is the cursed — according to Qatadah the accursed man, and from Ibn Jurayj the accursed and stoning in the Qur'an is curse. This was a response to Iblis' saying: "I would not prostrate to a human whom I created from clay from aged sludge," which is made of clay and I am of fire, and fire eats clay.
