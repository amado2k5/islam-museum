## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that Thabit ibn al-Dahhak al-Ansari told him that he found a camel at al-Harra, tied it, then mentioned it to Umar ibn al-Khattab. Umar ordered him to announce it three times. Thabit said: it has kept me from my own property. Umar said: let it go where you found it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.305}
