## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 24 as a single section. He explains the phrase “He will be in a life of contentment”: “The one whose situation I have described, the one who will be given his book in his right hand, will be in a life of contentment, or a life in which there is contentment. The life is described as content and pleasing because this is praise for the life, and the Arabs do this in praise and blame, saying: ‘This is a sleeping night,’ ‘A secret kept,’ and ‘Gushing water,’ thus directing the action to the subject, which is originally the object of the intended praise or blame. One who says this would not be permitted to call the one who strikes ‘struck,’ nor the one who is struck ‘striker,’ because there is neither praise nor blame in that.” He then quotes statements from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd in the section.
