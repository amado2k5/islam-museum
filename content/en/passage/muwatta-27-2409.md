## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, from Aisha, that she said: it has not been long for me, and I have not forgotten: the cutting is for a quarter of a dinar or more. The editor notes that the report is connected in the two Sahihs from al-Zuhri. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.395}
