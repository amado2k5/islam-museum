## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say that whoever places his forehead on the ground should place his palms on what his forehead rests on, and when he rises he should raise them too, because the hands prostrate as the face does. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.231}
