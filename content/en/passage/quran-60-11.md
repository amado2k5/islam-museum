## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if you miss any of your wives to the disbelievers and punish them, then give to those whose wives have gone like what they spent and fear Allah, in whom you are believers": He, the Exalted, says to the believers among the companions of the Messenger of Allah: "And if you miss," O believers, "if you miss any of your wives to the disbelievers," then catch up with them. The interpreters differed regarding the disbelievers who were referred to by the phrase "to the disbelievers"? Some said: They are the disbelievers who had no treaty with the Messenger of Allah. They said: The meaning is: And if you miss any of your wives, to those with whom there is no treaty among the disbelievers. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
