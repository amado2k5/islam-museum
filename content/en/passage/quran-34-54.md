## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And there was a deception between them and what they desired, as He did with their followers before; indeed, they were in doubtful doubt": and there were deceptions among these polytheists when they were frightened, so they did not miss it, and they took it from a nearby place and said, "We believe in it," and at that time they believed in what they had in this world before that, disbelief and no way to it. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
