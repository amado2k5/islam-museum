## Summary
Verse 15 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 15 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "say: I fear, if I disobey my Lord, the punishment of a great day" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: say to these polytheists who hold others equal to God and call you to worship their idols: my Lord has forbidden me to worship anything but him, "and I fear, if I disobey my Lord", by worshipping them, "the punishment of a great day", meaning the punishment of the day of resurrection, described as great because of its great terror and the horror of its matter. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 285}

Verse 15 closes here at p. 285, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-16|6:16]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
