## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "those upon whom are blessings (salawat) from their Lord and mercy, and they are the guided" he says "salawat" means forgiveness, God's blessings on His servants being His forgiveness of them, and he cites what is reported from the Prophet, "O God, bless the family of Abu Awfa", meaning forgive them; mercy with forgiveness is mercy and compassion from God, and they are the guided who hit upon the way of truth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 222} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 223} He reports from Ibn 'Abbas that when a believer entrusts the matter to God and says the formula of return at a misfortune, three good things are written for him: God's blessing, mercy and the realisation of the path of guidance; and from the Prophet, whoever says it at a misfortune, God mends his misfortune, improves its outcome and gives him a good successor he is pleased with. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 223} From al-Rabi' the blessings and mercy are on those who were patient and said the formula, and from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, no one has been given what this community was given in saying it, and had anyone been given it Jacob would have been, for he said "O my sorrow for Joseph". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 223} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 224}
