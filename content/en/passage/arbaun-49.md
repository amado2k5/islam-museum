## Summary
Hadith of Umar ibn al-Khattab: "If you rely on God as He should be relied upon, He would provide for you as He provides for birds, which go out hungry and return full". It is the seventh of the hadiths Ibn Rajab added, and its chain is sound. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
