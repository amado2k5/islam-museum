## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Dawud ibn al-Husayn from Abu Sufyan, client of Ibn Abi Ahmad, from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on one who ends the prayer after two rak'as by forgetfulness: the Prophet prayed the afternoon prayer and gave salam after two rak'as. Dhu al-Yadayn stood and asked whether the prayer was shortened or whether he forgot. The Prophet said neither had happened, and Dhu al-Yadayn said some of it had. The Prophet turned to the people and asked whether Dhu al-Yadayn spoke the truth; they said yes, and he completed what remained of the prayer and then made two prostrations after the salam while seated. The editor cites Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Khuzayma, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.148}
