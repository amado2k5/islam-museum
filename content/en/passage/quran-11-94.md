**Commentary on verse 94 (p. 464)**

On [[passage/quran-11-94|11:94]] al-Tabari says: when Our decree came on Shu'ayb's people with Our punishment, 'We saved Shu'ayb', Our messenger, and those who believed in him and held true what he brought from their Lord, with Shu'ayb, from Our punishment sent on his people, 'by mercy from Us' to him and to whoever believed him and followed him; 'and the cry seized those who did wrong', a cry from heaven that stilled them and destroyed them for their disbelief in their Lord. It was said that Gabriel cried at them with a cry that took their souls from their bodies; 'and by morning they lay in their homes', on their knees, laid low in their courtyards. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 464}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
