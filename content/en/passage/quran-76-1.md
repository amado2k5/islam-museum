## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 2 as one section. He explains His saying, “Has there not come upon man a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?”: He, may His praise be exalted, means by His saying, “Has there not come upon man,” that man has indeed come. And “has” in this context is a statement, not a denial, like when someone asks another, confirming his statement: “Did I honor you?” and he did honor him, or “Did I visit you?” and he did visit him. And it may be a denial in other contexts, like when someone asks another: “Does anyone do such a thing?” meaning: No one does that. The human being whom God, may His praise be exalted, refers to in this passage as, “Has there not come upon man a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?” (76:1). Qatadah said, regarding the verse, “Has there not come upon man a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned?” (76:1), that Adam was created “a period of time when he was nothing to be mentioned.” He explained that humankind was created recently, and that no other creation of God’s creation came after humankind. The passage also includes statements attributed to Qatadah and Sufyan.
