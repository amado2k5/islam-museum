## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We have not sent down to you the Book except that you may make clear to them that over which they differed and as guidance and mercy for a people who believe": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: And We have not sent down to you, O Muhammad, Our Book and sent you as a messenger to Our creation except that you may make clear to them that over which they differed of the religion of Allah, so that you may know to them what is right from it and truth from falsehood, and to establish for them the proof of Allah who sent you with it. And His saying: "And guidance and mercy for a people who believe" means: guidance is a clarification of misguidance, meaning by that book, "and mercy for a people who believe in it," so they believe in what it contains, acknowledge what Allah contains of its command and prohibition, and act upon it. He conjoined guidance to the position of "to clarify" because its position is accusative. The meaning of the statement is: And We have not sent down to you the Book except as clarification for people concerning that over which they differ, guidance and mercy.
