## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Anas ibn Malik, that milk mixed with water from a well was brought to the Messenger of God, with a Bedouin on his right and Abu Bakr al-Siddiq on his left. He drank, then gave the cup to the Bedouin and said: the one on the right, then the one on the right. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.514}
