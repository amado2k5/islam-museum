## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on who owes the zakat of fast-breaking (zakat al-fitr). Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar gave the zakat of fast-breaking on behalf of his slaves who were in Wadi al-Qura and Khaybar. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shafii, in al-Bayhaqi, transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.380}
