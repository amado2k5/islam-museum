**Commentary on verse 111 (p. 498)**

Al-Tabari says: God bought from the believers their selves and their wealth for Paradise; "a promise binding on Him in truth" means He promised them Paradise, the Almighty, a promise binding on Him that He will fulfil to them in His revealed books, the Torah, the Gospel and the Qur'an, if they fulfil what they covenanted with God, fighting in His way and in support of His religion against His enemies, killing and being killed; "and who is more faithful to his covenant than God" means who is better in fulfilling what he guaranteed and stipulated than God; "so rejoice" He says to the believers: rejoice, believers who affirmed God in what you covenanted, in your sale of your selves and your wealth for what you sold them to your Lord for, for that is the great triumph; and then the reports follow on the next page. The editor refers to earlier explanations of the words of this verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 498}

We stop here at the foot of p. 498, in verse 111 (before the reports begin); the next batch begins at p. 499. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 498}
