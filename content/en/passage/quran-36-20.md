## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 19–21. Al-Tabari explains that the messengers said, 'Your bird is with you,' meaning your deeds and share of good and evil are written upon you, not from our misfortune. If you remember, you are a people who are extravagant. A man came from the farthest reaches of Medina seeking to seek, saying, 'O my people, follow the messengers, follow those who do not ask you for reward, and they are guided.'
