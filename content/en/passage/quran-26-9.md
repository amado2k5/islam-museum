## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And indeed your Lord is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful": And indeed, your Lord, O Muhammad, He is the Exalted in His punishment; no one who seeks revenge can withhold from Him. Allah Almighty says: And if I permit those who deny You, O Muhammad, turning away from what comes to them from My remembrance, My punishment is by denying You, so no one will prevent them from me, for I am the Mighty, the Merciful, meaning that He is the Possessor of mercy to those who repent from His creation from their disbelief and disobedience, to punish them for their past crimes after repentance. Ibn Jurayj used to say about the meaning of this: he said: Al-Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj that everything about the poets from His saying 'Mighty and Merciful' is what He destroyed among the nations that passed by. Aziz says, when He took revenge on His enemies, He was merciful to the believers, when He saved them from what He destroyed His enemies.
