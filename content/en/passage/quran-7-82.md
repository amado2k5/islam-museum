## Summary
Verse 82 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 82 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-82|7:82]], al-Tabari says: the answer of Lot's people, when he rebuked them for their foul deed, was only that they said to one another, expel Lot and his family; "expel them" is in the plural although only Lot had been mentioned before, or it may mean "expel Lot and those of his religion", the mention of Lot at the start sufficing for his followers, and then the plural, as in [[passage/quran-65-1|65:1]]. "They are people who keep themselves pure" means Lot and those who follow him are people who hold themselves aloof from what we do in approaching men from behind. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 549}

(14836), (14837) and (14838) from Mujahid: they keep pure from the backs of men and the backs of women; (14839) from him from Ibn Abbas the same; (14840) from al-Suddi that they hold back out of scruple; (14841) from Qatada that they faulted them without a fault and blamed them without cause. The editor notes the standing of Hani ibn Said al-Nakhai in the first chain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 550}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
