## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that he used to travel in Ramadan and we travelled with him; Urwa would fast and we broke our fast, and he did not order us to fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.398}
