## Summary
A report that Malik says reached him: the Messenger of God made the lesser pilgrimage three times: in the year of al-Hudaybiyya, the year of the settlement, and the year of al-Jiranah. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1103) and Suwayd (517) transmitted it from Malik, and that this report is transmitted through routes Ibn Abd al-Barr mentioned (al-Tamhid 24/410). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.460}
