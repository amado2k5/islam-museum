## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And the servants of the Most Merciful who walk upon the earth humbly, and when the ignorant address them, they say peace": "And the servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk upon the earth humbly" with forbearance, tranquility, and dignity, not arrogant, nor arrogant, nor seeking corruption or disobedience to Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
