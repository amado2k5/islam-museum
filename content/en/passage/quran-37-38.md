## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 41 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, you will taste the painful punishment": God Almighty says to these polytheists from the people of Mecca, who say to Muhammad: "A mad poet: 'Indeed, you, O polytheists, will taste the painful punishment' in the Hereafter, 'and you will not be rewarded.' He says: 'And you will not be rewarded in the Hereafter when you taste the painful punishment therein, except for the reward of 'what you used to do' in this world, the sins of Allah. And His saying: He quotes in the passage from Qatadah.
