## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id and Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that al-Qasim ibn Muhammad recited behind the imam in prayers in which the imam does not recite aloud, in the chapter on reciting behind the imam in silent prayers. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.138}
