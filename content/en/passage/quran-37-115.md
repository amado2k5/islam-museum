## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 114 to 116 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We saved them and their people from great distress": and it was narrated as from drowning. And from Qatadah, "And We saved them and their people from the great distress," meaning from the family of Pharaoh. And His saying: "And We helped them" means We helped Moses and Aaron and their people against Pharaoh and his family by drowning them, "and they were the victors" for them. Some Arabs said: The letter 'h' and 'm' in His saying: 'And We helped them' is meant by Moses and Aaron, but it was narrated on a pronunciation of the plural, because the Arabs refer to the leader like the prophet and the prince and liken him to the plural with his soldiers and followers, and to monotheism because it is originally one. Similarly: 'out of fear of Pharaoh and their fullness,' and elsewhere: 'and his fullness.' In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
