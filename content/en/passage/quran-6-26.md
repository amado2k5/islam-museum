## Summary
Verse 26 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 26 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

The interpreters differed on "they forbid it and keep away from it". Some said it means: these polytheists who deny God's signs forbid people to follow Muhammad and accept him, "and keep away from it" means keep far from him. Report 13159, from Ibn al-Hanafiyya: they hold back from the Prophet and do not answer him, and forbid people to follow him. 13160, from Ibn Abbas: they forbid people to believe in Muhammad, "and keep away from him", staying far from him. 13161, from al-Suddi: they forbid that Muhammad be followed while they themselves keep away from him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 311}

At 13159 the editor identifies Hani ibn Sa'id al-Nakha'i, sound in hadith, Hajjaj ibn Arta'a, Salim ibn Abi al-Ja'd and Ibn al-Hanafiyya, Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib.

We stop here at the foot of p. 311, after report 13161 from al-Suddi on the first view of "they forbid it and keep away from it" in verse 26, and al-Tabari gives the remaining views on p. 312.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
