## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21-26 as a single section. He interprets the words, “So remind, for you are only a reminder”: “So remind, O Muhammad, My servants of My signs, admonish them with My proofs, and convey My message to them. ‘For you are only a reminder’ means: I have only sent you to them as a reminder, to remind them of My favor upon them, to make them aware of what is required of them, and to admonish them.” And the words, “You are not a controller over them,” mean: You are not a ruler over them, nor are you a tyrant who compels them to do as you please. He says: Entrust them to Me, leave them to Me and My judgment over them. It is said: So-and-so has taken control of his people: when he has authority over them. In this section, he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
