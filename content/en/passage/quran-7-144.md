**Commentary on verse 144 (p. 105)**

Al-Tabari says: God said to Moses, I have chosen you above the people, that is, selected you over them, for My message with which I sent you to My creation and for My speech, with which I spoke to you and conversed with you and with no one else of My creatures; so take what I have given you of My command and prohibition, hold to it and act on it, and be among the thankful to God for the message He gave you and the conversation He reserved for you, by obeying Him in His command and prohibition and hastening to His good pleasure. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "choosing", notes a corrupt word in the middle clause of both the printed text and manuscript which he could not repair and so left as dots, and refers to the explanation of "take what We gave you with strength" in sura al-Baqara. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 105}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
