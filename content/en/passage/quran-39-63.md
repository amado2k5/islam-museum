## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "To Him belong the keys of the heavens and the earth, and those who disbelieve in the signs of Allah; those are the losers": He holds the keys to the treasuries of the heavens and the earth, from which He opens them to whomever He wills and holds them from whomever He loves among His creation. The singular is Muqallid. As for Al-Iqlid: it is the singular of the Iqlid. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
