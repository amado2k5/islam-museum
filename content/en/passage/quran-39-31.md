## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 33 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Indeed, you are dead, and they are surely dead": Indeed, you are, O Muhammad, dead from a little, and indeed those who deny you from your people, and the believers among them are dead. He quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, Ibrahim, and Al-Rabi'.
