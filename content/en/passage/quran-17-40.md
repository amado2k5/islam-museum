## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "Has your Lord given you children and taken from among the angels females? Indeed, you are saying a great word": Allah Almighty says to those who among the polytheists of the Arabs that the angels are the daughters of Allah. "Did He purify you, O people"? "Your Lord has chosen you with sons": "And He took from the angels females", while you do not approve of them for yourselves, but rather you give them and kill them. So you have made for Allah what you do not approve of yourselves. "Indeed, you say a great word." Allah says to those polytheists who have spoken of slandering Allah. What we have mentioned: You people, by your own words, say: 'The angels are the daughters of Allah,' a great statement, and you fabricate a lie against Allah from you. Qatadah used to say about this: from Mu'ammar, from Qatadah: "And He took from the angels females," he said: The Jews said: The angels are the daughters of God. In the Maqta, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
