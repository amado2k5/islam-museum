**Commentary on verse 34 (pp. 519-520)**

A note on arrangement: al-Tabari's commentary on verses 33 and 34 together is filed in one place, under [[passage/quran-8-33|8:33]] (pp. 509 to 519); only its last part, on "they were not its guardians", is here. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 509} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 519}

Then al-Tabari says on "they were not its guardians; its guardians are only those who fear God, but most of them do not know" [[passage/quran-8-34|8:34]]: what is there for these polytheists that God should not punish them while they bar from the Sacred Mosque and were not God's guardians, "its guardians" meaning God's guardians are "only those who fear God", meaning those who fear God by performing His obligations and avoiding His disobediences, "but most of them do not know", that is most of the polytheists do not know that God's guardians are the God-fearing, but suppose themselves God's guardians. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "guardian" and "piety". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 519} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 520}

The interpreters spoke likewise; report 16018, from al-Suddi: they are the Companions of the Messenger of God; reports 16019 and 16020, from Mujahid: whoever they are and wherever they are; report 16021, from Ibn Ishaq: those who hold its sanctity sacred and keep up prayer at it, that is you and whoever believed in you. The editor corrects the manuscript's and printed edition's wording from Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 520}

We stop here at the foot of p. 520, with the commentary on 8:34 complete; the next batch begins with the commentary on 8:35, which starts on p. 521. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 520}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
