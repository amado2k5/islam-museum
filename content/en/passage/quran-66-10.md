## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “God has set forth an example for those who disbelieve: the wife of Noah and the wife of Lot. They were under two of Our righteous servants, but they betrayed them, so they availed them not against God at all, and it was said, ‘Enter the Fire with those who enter,’” explains that God has given the example of Noah’s wife and Lot’s wife as a model for those who disbelieve among mankind and all of creation. They were under two of Our servants, Noah and Lot, but they betrayed them. It is mentioned that Noah’s wife betrayed her husband because she was an unbeliever and used to tell people that he was insane. The treachery of Lot's wife lies in the fact that Lot would keep a guest's secret, and she would betray him. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, al-Dahhak, Ikrimah, and Sufyan.
