## Summary
A report of Ubada ibn al-Samit on his deathbed in the chapter on calling to the two testimonies in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. He gives out what he had kept to himself last: the two testimonies forbid the Fire. {cite:sahih-muslim}
