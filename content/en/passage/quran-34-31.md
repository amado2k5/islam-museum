## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieve say, 'We will never believe in this Qur'an or in what was before it.' And if you could see when the wrongdoers are standing before their Lord, some of them will return to one another. The saying is, 'Those who have become weak will say to those who have been arrogant, if not for you, we would surely be believers': 'And those who disbelieved' among the Arab polytheists said: 'We will not believe in this Qur'an' which Muhammad brought to us, nor in the Book brought by others from before him, as follows: And from Qatadah, regarding His saying: "We will not believe in this Qur'an or in what is before it," he said: The polytheists said: We will not believe in this Qur'an, nor in what is before it of books and prophets. And His saying: "And if you could see when the wrongdoers are being held before their Lord," they blame each other, conversing with one another. The oppressed say: "The oppressed were in this world for those who were upon them, arrogant: If it were not for you, O leaders and great people in the world, we would have believed in Allah and His signs." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
