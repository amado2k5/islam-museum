**Commentary on verse 26 (pp. 293-294)**

On [[passage/quran-11-26|11:26]] al-Tabari says: whoever vowels 'inni' with kasra makes 'We sent' govern the 'an' in 'that you worship none but God', and the sense becomes: We sent Noah to his people that they worship none but God, and say to them I am to you a clear warner; and whoever vowels it with fatha joins the 'an' in 'that you worship none' to it, and the sense is: We sent Noah to his people with 'I am to you a clear warner', with 'that you worship none but God'. By this he means leave the worship of gods and idols and setting them up in His worship, make God's oneness alone and your worship pure for Him, for He has no partner in His creation; the editor says there is an evident omission in the sentence and puts what he judges right. His words 'I fear for you the punishment of a painful day' mean: I, people, if you do not make worship God's alone, make Him one and cast off the rivals and idols besides Him, fear for you from God the punishment of a day whose chastisement and punishment is painful to whoever is punished in it. 'Painful' is made an attribute of the day though it belongs to the punishment, because the punishment is in it, as in [[passage/quran-6-96|6:96]], where 'a rest' is of what one rests in and not the night. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 294}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
