## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase: "Is He who begins creation and then repeats it? And who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? Is there a deity with Allah? Say, bring your proof if you should be truthful." Or do you associate with Him, O people, better? Or is He who begins creation, then repeats it, creates it from its original source, innovates it, then annihilates it if He wills, then returns it as He wills before annihilating it? And He who provides you from the sky and the earth, sends down from this rain, and grows from this plant for your sustenance and the food of your livestock. "Is there any god with Allah?" besides Allah, who does that? And if they claim that there is a god other than Allah doing that or something of it, and "Say" to them, O Muhammad, "Bring your proof": meaning your proof that nothing but Allah does that, "if you are truthful" in your claim. And "min" in "Aman" and "ma" is a subject in His saying: "Do they not associate partners with Him," and the verses after it reach His saying: "And who provides for you from the heaven and the earth" meaning "who," not in the sense of interrogative, because interrogative does not enter into interrogation.
