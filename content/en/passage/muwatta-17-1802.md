## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say a man may have intercourse with a slave-girl, and if he wishes he may sell her, if he wishes give her away, if he wishes keep her, and if he wishes do with her whatever he wishes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.138}
