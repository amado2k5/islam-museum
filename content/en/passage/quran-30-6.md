## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The promise of Allah, Allah does not break His promise, but most people do not know": Allah, Glorified and Exalted, promised, He promised that the Romans would defeat a knight after a knight had defeated them. The accusative 'Waad Allah' is in the verbal noun of the phrase "And they will be defeated after their defeat," because that is a promise from Allah to them that they will prevail, as if He said: Allah has promised the believers that He promised. "Allah does not break His promise." Allah says: Allah fulfills His promise to the believers that the Romans will defeat Persia. He does not break His promise, because He does not fail in His appointments. "But most people do not know," He says: But most of the Quraysh, who deny that Allah fulfilled His promise to the believers, such as the Romans defeating Persia, do not know that this is the case, and that it is not permissible for Allah's promise to be broken.
