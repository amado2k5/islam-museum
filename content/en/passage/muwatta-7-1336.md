## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, from Anas ibn Malik, that the Messenger of God, when he went to Quba, entered upon Umm Haram bint Milhan, and she fed him; she was married to Ubada ibn al-Samit. He entered upon her one day and she fed him; she sat delousing his head; he slept, then woke up laughing. She asked him, and he said: some people of my community were shown to me, undertaking a raid in God's path, riding the back of this sea, kings on thrones or like kings on thrones. She prayed to be among them; he said: you are among the first. She sailed the sea in the time of Muawiya, and was thrown from her mount when she came out of the sea, and died. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.597}
