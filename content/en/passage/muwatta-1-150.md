## Summary
The first report in the chapter on the end of menstruation. Yahya transmits from Malik from Alqama ibn Abi Alqama from his mother, a client of Aisha: women used to send Aisha the cloth with cotton on which the yellow of menstrual blood showed, asking her about prayer. She told them not to hurry until they see the white discharge, meaning the end of the menses. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ma'mar from Alqama. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.104}
