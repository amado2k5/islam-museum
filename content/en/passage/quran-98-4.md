## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-4 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “And those who were given the Scripture did not become divided until after clear evidence had come to them” (10:11): “The Jews and Christians did not become divided concerning Muhammad, and thus disbelieved in him, until after clear evidence had come to them.” That is, after the “clear evidence” came to these Jews and Christians—meaning the explanation of Muhammad’s mission, that he was a messenger sent by God to His creation—he says: “When God sent him, they became divided concerning him; some disbelieved in him, and some believed. Before he was sent, they were not divided concerning his prophethood.” In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
