## Summary
A report of Abu Ayyub al-Ansari in the chapter on the prohibition of shunning a Muslim for more than three days without excuse, in Sahih Muslim. It fixes the limit of lawful shunning and makes the one who starts the greeting the better of the two who meet. {cite:sahih-muslim}
