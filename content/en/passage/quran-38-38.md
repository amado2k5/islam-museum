## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 36–40. Al-Tabari explains that God responded to Solomon by granting him an angel that no one after him deserves, and He subjected the wind to blow by His command wherever it struck, and the devils are all builders, divers, and others bound in chains. This is our gift, so believe or hold without reckoning, and with us He has a good destination and a good return.
