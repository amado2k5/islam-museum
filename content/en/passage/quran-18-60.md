## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And [mention] when Moses said to his turn, 'I will not leave until I reach the gathering of the two seas or pass through ages': He says to His Prophet: 'And remember, O Muhammad, when Moses son of Imran said to his daughter Joshua: 'I will not depart,' meaning: I will not continue to walk until I reach the gathering of the seas," as Ibn Zayd said, regarding 'I will not leave,' he said: I will not end. It is also said that by 'the gathering of the seas,' he meant the meeting of the sea of Persia and the Romans, and the gathering is a verbal noun derived from their saying: a plural that gathers. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
