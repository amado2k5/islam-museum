## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say that if a slave divorces his wife twice she is forbidden to him until she marries another husband, whether free or slave. The waiting period of a free woman is three menstrual cycles, and that of a slave-woman two. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.86}
