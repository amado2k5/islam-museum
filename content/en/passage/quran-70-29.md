## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 31 as one section. He explains His saying, “And those who guard their private parts”: “And those who guard their private parts” means that they guard their private parts from everything that God has forbidden them to do with them, “except” that they are not blamed for not guarding them “with their wives or those whom their right hands possess” from among their female slaves. It was said: “They guard their private parts except from their wives,” and this was not preceded by a denial, because the statement, “for they are not to be blamed,” indicates that the statement contains a denial meaning.
