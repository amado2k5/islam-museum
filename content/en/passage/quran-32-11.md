## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, the Angel of Death, who entrusted you with death, will take you away, then to your Lord you will be returned": Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists with Allah: the Angel of Death will take your life. He means: Your number will be fulfilled by taking your souls, the Angel of Death who was entrusted with taking your souls. From this is the statement of the Rajiz: The sons of Al-Adram are not of anyone, and Quraysh did not take them in number. "Then to your Lord you will be returned," meaning: After the Angel of Death takes your souls, you will be returned alive to your Lord on the Day of Resurrection, as you were before your death. The doer of good among you will be rewarded with kindness, and the wrongdoer with his wrongdoing. And from Qatadah: "Say, the Angel of Death who has been entrusted with you will take you," he said: The Angel of Death will take you away, accompanied by assistants from the angels. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid regarding the phrase "The Angel of Death will take you," he said: The earth was contained for him, and it was made for him like a bowl from which he could take wherever he wished. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
