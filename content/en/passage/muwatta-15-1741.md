## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubayd Allah, from Amir ibn Saad ibn Abi Waqqas, from his father, that he used to practise coitus interruptus. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.110}
