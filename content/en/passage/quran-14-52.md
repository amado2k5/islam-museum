## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse as describing the Qur'an as a message to mankind, through which God makes His case and gives warning, so that people are warned, know that God is one, and people of understanding take heed. He reports Ibn Zayd identifying the message as the Qur'an itself.
