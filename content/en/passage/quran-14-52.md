## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains the end of the surah: "This is a message to the people," meaning this Quran is a message conveyed to them by God, and the argument against them was established and excused for what was revealed in it of admonitions and lessons. And "And to warn them": to warn them of God's punishment and punishments through it. And "And to know that He is only one God": to know what was argued against them that He is one God, not multiple gods as the polytheists say, and He is the One who subjected the sun, the moon, the night, and the day, sent down water, brought forth fruits, and subjected the stars and rivers. And "And let the people of understanding be reminded": so that the people of pilgrimage and intellect may take heed of the proofs in the Qur'an and refrain from associating partners with Him, for they are people of consideration and intellect, unlike those without intellect, they are like cattle, or even more misguided; and Ibn Zayd reported something similar.
