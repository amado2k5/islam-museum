## Summary
Malik transmits from al-Walid ibn Abdallah ibn Sayyad, that al-Muttalib ibn Abdallah ibn Hanatib al-Makhzumi told him that a man asked the Prophet: what is backbiting? He said: that you mention of a person what he dislikes to hear. He said: Messenger of God, even if it is true? He said: if you say something false, that is slander. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that the correct name of the narrator is Hanatib, not Huwaytib as in Yahya's transmission, and that the hadith is connected through al-Ala ibn Abd al-Rahman from his father from Abu Hurayra in Muslim and elsewhere. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.585}
