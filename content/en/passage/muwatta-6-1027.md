## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger of God said: five animals; there is no sin on one in ihram who kills them: the scorpion, the mouse, the crow, the kite and the rabid dog. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1184), al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud and others transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.479}
