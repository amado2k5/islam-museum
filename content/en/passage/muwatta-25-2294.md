## Summary
Malik said of a contract of manumission slave whose master contracts him: it is considered; if he intended to show favour to his slave, known for easing his burden, that is not lawful; if he contracts him seeking wealth and help with completing his contract, that is lawful for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.345}
