## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse as a reassurance to the Prophet: God does not break His promise to His messengers, and vengeance comes on those who deny them, as it did to the nations before them. The Mighty is the One whom nothing can prevent from punishing whom He wills, and the Lord of vengeance avenges on those who reject His messengers. He notes that one grammatical reading is far from sound Arabic.
