## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 45 to 46 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then We sent Moses and his brother Aaron with Our signs and clear authority": Then We sent after the messengers whose description was described before this verse, Moses and his brother Aaron to Pharaoh and the nobles of his people from the Copts "with Our signs," meaning "by Our proofs," "but they were arrogant" in following them and believing in what God brought to them. "And they were a lofty people," meaning: they were exalted people over their people and those in their land from the Children of Israel and others in oppression, overcoming them. Ibn Zayd used to say about this: Ibn Zayd said, and regarding His saying: "And they were a lofty people," he said: they were exalted above their messengers and disobeyed their Lord. That is their superiority. And he recited: "That is the Hereafter"... the verse. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
