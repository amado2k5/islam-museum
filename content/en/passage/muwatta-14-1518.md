## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told al-Qasim ibn Muhammad was asked about a man who divorced his wife irrevocably, then she married another man who died before consummating. May her former husband take her back? Al-Qasim said her former husband may not take her back. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.38}
