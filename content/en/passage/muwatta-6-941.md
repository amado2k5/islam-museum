## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: he heard some people of knowledge recommend the talbiya after every prayer, and on every height of land. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1074) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.450}
