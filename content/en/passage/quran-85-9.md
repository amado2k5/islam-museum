## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9-10 as a single section. He interprets the words, “He to Whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth, and God is Witness over all things”: He who has authority over the seven heavens and the earths and all that is in them, “and God is Witness over all things,” meaning that God, may He be exalted, is witness to the actions of these disbelievers, the People of the Ditch, against the believers whom they persecuted, and to all their other deeds and the deeds of all His creation, and He will recompense them accordingly. And His words, “Indeed, those who persecuted the believing men and believing women,” mean that those who tested the believing men and women of God by torturing them and burning them in fire. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-Rabi’.
