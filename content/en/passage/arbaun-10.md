## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: God is good and accepts only what is good. Ibn Rajab takes "good" to mean pure, free of corruptions such as showing off and conceit, and lawful in money. Deeds, words and beliefs are divided into good and bad. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
