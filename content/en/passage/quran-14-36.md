## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues Ibrahim's supplication in the second verse of the section: "My Lord, indeed they have led many people astray," meaning idols and idols, meaning they have led many people astray from the path of guidance and the path of truth until they worshipped them and disbelieved in Allah, and he quotes this from Qatadah. And in "So whoever follows me is of me," meaning whoever follows him in faith in Allah, sincere worship of Him, and separating from idol worship, is adhering to his Sunnah and acting according to his deeds. And in "And whoever disobeys Me, indeed You are Forgiving and Merciful": Whoever opposes His command and associates partners with Allah, Allah is Forgiving of the sins of those who sinned by His grace, Merciful to His servants, pardoning whom He wills. He narrates from Qatadah the amazement at Abraham's character in this statement—as he was not one of the cursed or cursed ones—and he pairs it with the words of Jesus son of Mary in entrusting the affairs of his people to Allah in Surah Al-Ma'idah.
