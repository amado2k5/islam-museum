**Commentary on verse 61 (pp. 156)**

On "they said, we will seek him from his father, and we will do it", al-Tabari says: Joseph's brothers said to Joseph, when he said "bring me a brother of yours from your father", "we will seek him from his father", asking him to let him go with us so that we bring him to you; "and we will do it" means we will do what we told you we would, seeking our father's consent for our brother, and we will strive. Report 19469, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: "we will do it", we will strive. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "muradawa". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 156}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
