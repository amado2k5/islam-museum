**Commentary on verse 109: who will argue for them on the Day of Resurrection (p. 193, notes 2-4).** Al-Tabari says the sense is that those who argued, group of those who argued for Banu Ubayriq, have argued for them in this life, and the pronoun in the verse refers to the traitors. Who then will contend with God for them on the Day of Resurrection, the day people rise from their graves to be gathered, and ward off from them what God will do to them and punish them with? He says the meaning is that though those who defend these traitors themselves defend them in the nearer world, they will go in the later world to One before whom none can defend them against the painful torment and exemplary punishment that befalls them. As for the second question, who will be an agent over them, he says it means who will be the agent of these traitors on the Day of Resurrection, that is who will take up their cause in their dispute with their Lord; and he says he has already explained that agency is taking charge of the affair of the one for whom one acts. The editor refers to the language indexes for his explanations of the particle phrases used in this verse (volume 7, pp. 150-151), of the Day of Resurrection and of the agent. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 193} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 193}
