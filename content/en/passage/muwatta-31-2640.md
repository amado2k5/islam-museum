## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: beware of suspicion, for suspicion is the most false of speech; do not spy, do not pry, do not compete, do not envy, do not hate, do not turn your backs on one another, and be servants of God as brothers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.494}
