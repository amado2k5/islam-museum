## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 29 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said to those around him, 'Do you not listen?'" means that He mentioned him by saying, "He said to those around him, 'Do you not listen?'" Pharaoh said to those around him regarding His saying: Do you not listen to what Moses says? Moses informed the people of the answer to Pharaoh's question and said to him, "And what is the Lord of the worlds?" So Pharaoh's people would understand Pharaoh's words and his answer to what he asked. Pharaoh said to them, "Do you not listen?" to Moses' words, and the one to whom you were invited and to worship Him said, "Your Lord" who created you, "and the Lord of your forefathers." Pharaoh said when Moses told them that and informed them about what Pharaoh and his people were calling for: "Indeed, your messenger who was sent to you is mad," meaning that this messenger of yours who claims to have been sent to you is overwhelmed by reason, because he speaks words we do not know nor understand.
