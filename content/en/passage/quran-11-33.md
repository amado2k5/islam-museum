**Commentary on verse 33 (pp. 304-305)**

On [[passage/quran-11-33|11:33]] al-Tabari says: Noah said to his people, when they hastened him for the punishment: my people, what you hasten of punishment is not up to me; it is up to God and not another, who will bring it to you if He wills; 'and you cannot frustrate Him' means you, when He wills to punish you, cannot escape Him by flight, because wherever you are you are in His dominion, authority and power, and His rule over you runs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 305}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
