## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that people used to walk going and returning when they stoned the pillars, and the first to ride was Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.544}
