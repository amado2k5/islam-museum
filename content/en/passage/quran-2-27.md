## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "those who break God's covenant after its confirmation" al-Tabari says it describes the transgressors whom alone God misleads by the parable. He records disagreement over what the covenant is: some say it is God's charge to His creatures, His commands and prohibitions in His books and through His Messenger, and breaking it is leaving off acting on it; others say these verses came down about the unbelievers among the People of the Book and their hypocrites. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 410}
