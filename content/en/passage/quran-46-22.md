## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They said, 'You have come to us to distance ourselves from our gods, so bring us what you promise us, if you should be of the truthful': 'Aad said to Hud, when he said to them: 'Do not worship except Allah: Indeed, I fear for you the punishment of a great Day. You have come to us, O Hud, to divert us from worshiping our gods to worship what you call us to, and to following you according to your words.' In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
