## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 221 to 223 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "It descends upon every sinful slanderer": and it is narrated: a liar among the people. Muammar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "Every sinner of you is sinful," he said: They are the priests who steal the jinn from hearing, then bring it to their human allies. He said: I was with Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr when it was said to him: Al-Mukhtar claims to be revealed to him. He said: It is true, then he recited: "Shall I inform you about whom the devils descend, and upon every sinful slander?" And His saying: "They give hearing," the Exalted says: The devils cast listen, which is what they hear from what they have stealed their hearing from the moment it was revealed from the heavens, to "every sinner is sinful" among their allies among the children of Adam. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
