## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubaydallah, that Aisha bint Talha told him that she was with Aisha, wife of the Prophet, when her husband Abdallah ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq came in while fasting. Aisha said to him: what prevents you from approaching your wife, kissing her and playing with her? He said: should I kiss her while I am fasting? She said: yes. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.394}
