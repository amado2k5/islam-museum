## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We established for you therein gardens of palm trees and grapes, where you will have many fruits and from them you will eat": So one groves of palm trees and grapes "you will have in them," meaning: you will have many fruits in the gardens. "And from them you will eat"—he means: among the fruits you eat. It is possible that the 'h' and 'alif' refer to the gardens, or it could be from the mention of palm trees and grapes. He specifically praised the gardens mentioned in this place, describing them as palm and grape trees but not as the other fruits of the earth, because these two types of thama were the greatest fruits of the Hijaz and those closest to it. Thus, the palm trees belonged to the people of Medina, and the grapes to the people of Taif. He reminded the people of what they knew of the blessings Allah bestowed upon them, the fruits He bestowed upon them.
