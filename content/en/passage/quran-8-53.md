**Commentary on verse 53 (pp. 19-20)**

Al-Tabari says: We seized these who disbelieved in Our signs, the idolaters of Quraysh at Badr, for their sins, and We did that to them because they changed the favour God had given them in sending His messenger from among them and in their midst, by driving him out from among them, denying him and fighting him; so We changed Our favour to them by destroying them, as We did to those who went before them who rebelled against Us and disobeyed Our command. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 19}

Report 16209, from al-Suddi, through Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal and Asbat: God's favour is Muhammad, whom God favoured Quraysh with, and they disbelieved, so He moved him to the Ansar. On "and God is hearing, knowing" he says: nothing of His creatures' speech is hidden from Him; He hears the speech of every speaker, in good or in evil, and is "knowing" of what their breasts hide; He will requite and reward them for what they say and do, good for good and evil for evil. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 20}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
