## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And what prevented the people from believing when guidance came to them except that they said, 'I have sent Allah as a human as a messenger': O Muhammad, what prevented your people from believing in Allah and in what you brought them of truth," when guidance came to them. He means: when the clear message came to them from Allah with the truth of what you call them and the truth of what you brought to them, except their ignorance of their saying, 'Allah has sent a human as a messenger.' The first case is in the accusative case as a prohibition against it, and the second in the nominative case, because the verb is for it.
