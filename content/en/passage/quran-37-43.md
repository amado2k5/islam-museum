## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 48 in a single segment. He interprets the phrase "fruits while they are honored": His saying fruits is a response to the known provision, which is why it was raised. And the phrase "they are honored" means they are with those who have known provision in Paradise, honored by the generosity of God with which He has honored them. He also quotes in the segment from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Dahhak.
