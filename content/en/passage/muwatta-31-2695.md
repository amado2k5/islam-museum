## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: I saw Umar ibn al-Khattab, who was then Commander of the Faithful, having a measure of dates placed before him, and he would eat until he ate its dry ones. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.522}
