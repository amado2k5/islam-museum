## Summary
A report of Sufyan ibn Abi al-Zubayr al-Shana'i in the Book of Pilgrimage of Sahih Muslim: a warning against leaving Medina when Yemen, Syria and Iraq are conquered. It is among the texts on Medina's merit and the standing of the Prophetic city. {cite:sahih-muslim}
