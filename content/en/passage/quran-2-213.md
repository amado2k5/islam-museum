## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "mankind was one community; then God sent the prophets as bearers of good news and warners, and sent down with them the Book in truth to judge between people in what they differed over": the interpreters differed over the meaning of "umma" here and over who the "people" are whom God so described. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 275} Some said they were those between Adam and Noah, ten generations all on a law of truth, who differed afterwards; from Ibn 'Abbas: between Noah and Adam were ten generations all on a law of truth; they differed and God sent the prophets, and so it is in the reading of 'Abdallah, "mankind was one community, then they differed" (4048), the editor adding that al-Hakim narrated it in the Mustadrak and graded it sound on the condition of al-Bukhari, with al-Dhahabi agreeing; from Qatada: they were all on guidance and then differed, so God sent the prophets, and the first prophet sent was Noah (4049). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 275} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 276} Al-Tabari says "umma" on this view means religion, as in al-Nabigha's line "can one who has a religion be trusted while obedient", meaning one of religion; the root sense of "umma" is a group gathered on one religion, and the report about the umma suffices for a report about the religion, as in "had God willed, He would have made you one community" (5:48); so Ibn 'Abbas directed the verse to people having been of one religion until they differed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 276}

Others said Adam was on the truth, an imam to his descendants, and God sent the prophets among his children; they took "umma" as obedience to God and calling to His oneness, as in "Abraham was an umma, devoted to God, upright" (16:120), meaning a leader in good who is followed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 276} From Mujahid: "mankind was one community", he said, Adam (4050, 4051), and in one report: between Adam and Noah there were ten prophets, and God sent the prophets as bearers of good news and warners; Mujahid said Adam was a community by himself (4052). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 277} Al-Tabari remarks that whoever said this allowed naming one person by the name of a group for the gathering in him of the good qualities found in a scattered group, as one says "so-and-so is a community by himself", or because he was the cause of gathering on his religion those of his descendants who gathered on it until they differed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 277}

Others said mankind was one community on one religion on the day God brought Adam's descendants out of his loins and presented them to him; from Ubayy ibn Ka'b: they were one community when they were presented to Adam, when He created them on Islam and they acknowledged servanthood to Him, then they differed after Adam; Ubayy read "mankind was one community, then they differed, then God sent the prophets", and God sent messengers and revealed books only at the time of difference (4053); from Ibn Zayd: when He brought them out of Adam's back, they were never one community other than on that day, and "God sent the prophets" is when the communities split up (4054). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 277} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 278} Al-Tabari says the sense of the verse on this view is like Ibn 'Abbas's, except that the time when people were one community differs from the time Ibn 'Abbas gave. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 278}

Others said "mankind was one community" means they were on one religion, then God sent the prophets, from Ibn 'Abbas (4055). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 278}

Al-Tabari says the soundest interpretation is that God told His servants that people were one community on one religion and one creed. From al-Suddi: one religion, the religion of Adam; they differed and God sent the prophets as bearers of good news and warners (4056). The religion they were on was the true religion, as Ubayy ibn Ka'b said, and al-Suddi reports that in the reading of Ibn Mas'ud it is "they differed from it", that is from Islam (4057); they differed in their religion, so at their difference God sent the prophets as bearers of good news and warners and sent down the Book with them to judge between people in what they differed over, as mercy to His creatures and as an excuse before them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 278} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 279} He allows that the time they were one community may have been from Adam to Noah, as 'Ikrima reported from Ibn 'Abbas and as Qatada said, or when Adam was shown his offspring, or another time, and there is no indication in God's Book or an established report as to which, so nothing may be said beyond what God said, that people were one community and when they differed God sent among them prophets and messengers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 279}
