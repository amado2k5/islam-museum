## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when We decreed death upon him, none indicated his death except the beast of the earth devouring his soul. But when He fell, the jinn realized that if they had known the unseen, they would not have remained in the humiliating punishment." So when We decreed Solomon to die, he died, "He did not indicate his death." He means: The jinn did not indicate Solomon's death "except the beast of the earth," which was the earth that fell into his staff, on which he was leaning, and it ate it. This is the saying of Allah, the Almighty: "His soul devours him." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
