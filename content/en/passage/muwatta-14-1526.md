## Summary
Malik says that adultery does not make forbidden any of these relations, because God forbade only what was made by marriage. Any marriage contracted in lawful form, where the husband has intercourse with his wife, is like a lawful marriage. This is what Malik heard, and what the people here follow. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.40}
