## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "They have denied you what you say, so you have no power to turn or help. And whoever among you wrongs, We will make him taste a great punishment": Allah Almighty says to the believers: "And whoever wrongs you," he means by saying: "And whoever wrongs Allah," and whoever associates others with Allah and wrongs himself, We will make him taste a great punishment, just as We have mentioned that We will make those who deny the Hour taste it. In the passage, he quotes from al-Hasan.
