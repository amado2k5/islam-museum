## Summary
Verse 67 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 67 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "for every tiding there is a settled time, and you will know" al-Tabari says: for every report there is a settled place, meaning a resting point at which it comes to rest and an end to which it reaches, where its truth and honesty are told from its falsehood and vanity; "you will know" says: you will know, you who deny the truth of what he tells you of God's threat to you, polytheists, its reality when his punishment falls on you; they saw that and witnessed it, and on that day he had them killed at the hands of his friends among the believers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 434}

The editor says the printed text has a different word for "reality", and refers for "guardian" and "tiding" to earlier passages.

We stop here at the foot of p. 434, after this passage of the commentary on verse 67, and al-Tabari continues on p. 435.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
