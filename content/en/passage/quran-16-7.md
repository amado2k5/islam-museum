## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And you will have beauty in them when you rest and when you rest": and you will have in these livestock and livestock that He created for you "beauty when you rest," meaning: you return them in the evening from their pastures to their pastures and dwellings where they take shelter. That is why the place is called Al-Marah because it rests to him in the evening and he takes refuge there. It is said: So-and-so rested his livestock, so he rested it in rest. And His saying: 'And when you are released,' it means: 'And when you take them out in the morning from their pastures to their pastures,' it is said: So-and-so let his livestock be released in the morning, meaning he takes them out to graze in the morning. And 'sarhat' means when they go out to pasture, they go out to graze and graze, so grazing is in the morning and resting in the evening. From this is the poet's saying: 'It is as if the remains of the donkey are on its mats, like the bear on the pure while it is roaming.' In this passage, sayings are transmitted from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Jurayj, and Samak.
