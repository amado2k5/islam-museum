## Summary
Verse 95 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 95 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 95: following the creed of Abraham (pp. 17-18).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: say, Muhammad, that God has spoken the truth in what He told us, that all food was permitted to the descendants of Israel, that He did not forbid them the veins or the flesh and milk of camels, that this was only something Israel kept from himself and his children without a prohibition from God in the Torah, and in all that He reported to His servants, not you; and you, Jews, are the liars in attributing the prohibition of it to God in the Torah and the inventors of falsehood against Him in your claim. So follow the creed of Abraham if you are right in your claim that you are on the religion God approved for His prophets and messengers, for you know that it is the truth God approved from His creation as a religion and sent His prophets with, which is the hanifiyya, meaning steadfastness upon Islam and its laws, not Judaism, Christianity or polytheism. The editor says the conjunction stands in the manuscript and printed text with differences in two places and that it should be kept as in the manuscript, the sentence being that 'you' is the subject and 'the liars' and 'the inventors' its predicates, both nominative.

**The sense of Abraham's not being among the polytheists (p. 18).** Al-Tabari says the sense of Abraham's not having been of the polytheists is that he did not associate any of God's creation with Him in worship; so you also, Jews, are not to take one another as lords besides God and obey them as Abraham obeyed his Lord, and you, idol-worshippers, are not to take idols and statues as lords nor worship anything besides God, for Abraham, the friend of the Merciful, had as his religion the sincere worship of his Lord alone, with no one associated with Him in it; so you also are to give Him sincere worship and associate no one with Him in it. For all of you acknowledge that Abraham was on truth and straight guidance, so follow what all of you agree is right of his hanifi creed and leave what you differ over of the other communities, you factions, for those are innovations you have invented beside what you have agreed is true; what you have agreed is right and true of Abraham's creed is the truth God approved and sent His prophets and messengers with, and all the rest is the falsehood that He accepts from none of His creatures who brings it on the Day of Resurrection. Al-Tabari takes 'not of the polytheists' to mean that he was not of their number and their allies, since the polytheists are of one another in backing each other in their disbelief and in aiding one another, so God declared His friend Abraham free of being one of them or of their helpers and the people of their patronage. The editor says the addition between brackets makes the sentence sound. Al-Tabari says that by the polytheists here are meant the Jews, the Christians and the rest of the religions other than the hanifiyya, that is, that Abraham was not of these religions, which he calls polytheist, but was hanif and Muslim. (This is what al-Tabari says in his interpretation of the verse, presented by the museum as his, without a verdict.) {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 18}
