## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Then He shaped him and breathed into him of His spirit and made for you hearing, sight, and hearts. Few are you grateful": Then He reformed man who began his creation from clay, a sound and balanced creation, "and breathed into him of His spirit," so he became alive and speaking. "And He gave you hearing, sight, and hearts. Little do you give thanks," meaning: "And blessed be upon you, O mankind, your Lord by giving you hearing by which you hear sounds, and eyes with which you perceive people and hearts, discerning good from evil, so you may thank Him for what He has granted you." And His saying: 'You give little thanks,' means: while you give thanks a little of your Lord for what He has bestowed upon you.
