## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And He made the Book, and you see the criminals fearful of what is in it, and they say, 'Woe to us! This Book leaves no small or large except that it is enumerated.' And they find what they did present, and your Lord wrongs no one." He says: "And Allah placed that Day the record of His servants' deeds in their hands, and He took one in his right hand and the left hand." "And you see the criminals fearful of what is in it." He says: "You see the criminals who associate partners with Allah fearful." He says: Fearful and clear of what is written about their evil deeds in this world, that they will be held accountable for it{ and they say, 'Woe to us! This Book does not leave behind a small or large one except that He has enumerated them.'} That is, when they read their Book and saw what had been written against them in it—their minor and major sins—they called out for woe when they became certain of God's punishment, and they were upset by what they knew of their evil deeds that their Book had enumerated, and they could not deny their authenticity, such as: And regarding Qatadah, his statement: "What about this book does not leave behind a small section and quote in the section sayings from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah?
