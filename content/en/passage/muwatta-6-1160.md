## Summary
Ata ibn Abi Rabah transmits from a freedwoman of Asma bint Abi Bakr that she came with Asma to Mina in the darkness before dawn. She was told: they came to Mina in the dark. Asma said: they used to do that with someone better than she is. The editor notes that the hadith is in the two Sahihs from Ibn Umar raised. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.524}
