## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 16 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “But as for him who is given his record behind his back”: “As for him who is given his record from among you, O people, on that Day behind his back, that is, his right hand will be placed to his neck, and his left hand behind his back, so he will receive his record with his left hand from behind his back. That is why God, may His praise be exalted, sometimes describes them as being given their records in their left hands, and sometimes as being given them from behind their backs.” In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
