## Summary
Hadith of Aisha bint Abi Bakr: "Whoever does an action that is not in accordance with our matter, it is rejected". Ibn Rajab takes it as a great principle and a standard for outward deeds, as the hadith of intentions is a standard for inward ones. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
