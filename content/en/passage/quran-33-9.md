## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, remember the favor of Allah upon you when there came to you armies and We sent upon them a wind and hosts you did not see. And Allah is Seeing of what you do": "O you who have believed, remember the favor of Allah upon you" which He bestowed upon your people, which He bestowed upon your people, when the Muslims were besieged with the Messenger of Allah during the days of the Trench: "When armies came to you": the soldiers of the Confederates: Quraysh, Ghatafan, and the Jews of Banu Nadir "so We sent upon them a wind," which is mentioned as: The breeze of youth. Also: And from Ikrimah, he said: The south said to the north on the night of the Confederates: Go and support the Messenger of Allah. The north said: The pure land does not travel at night. He said: The wind that sent dawn upon them was.
