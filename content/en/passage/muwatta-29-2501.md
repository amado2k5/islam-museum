## Summary
Malik said: the practice among us is that there is no blood-money for wounds smaller than the wound that exposes the bone, until it reaches the wound that exposes the bone; blood-money is due for the exposing wound and above, because the Prophet, in his letter to Amr ibn Hazm, reached the exposing wound and set five camels for it; and the imams did not rule blood-money for anything smaller. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.429}
