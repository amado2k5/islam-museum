## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains His saying: 'And did the enemy receive news from you when you fenced the sanctuary?': And did the news of the opponent come to you, O Muhammad? It is said that by 'adversary' in this place, I mean two angels, and it is derived from the word 'one,' because it is a verbal noun like 'false' and 'safar', neither dual nor pluralized. From this is the saying of Labid: 'And the opponents are considered as if they are the corners of the sanctuary, all of Mus'ab's Azhar.' And His saying: 'When you fenced the sanctuary,' means: they entered it from outside the door of the sanctuary, and the mihrab was the foremost and most honorable of every gathering and house.
