## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to perform the ritual bath on the day of the festival of breaking the fast before going out to the prayer ground, in the chapter on performing the ritual bath for the two festivals. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, al-Shafi'i and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.250}
