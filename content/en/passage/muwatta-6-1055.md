## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that Aisha, Mother of the Believers, said: I do not mind whether I prayed in the Hijr or in the House. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1279) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.489}
