## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Aisha, mother of the believers, that she told him Aflah, brother of Abu al-Qu'ays, came asking to enter, and he was her paternal uncle through fosterage, after the veil had been imposed. She said she refused to admit him, and when the Messenger came she told him what she had done. He told her to admit him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.120}
