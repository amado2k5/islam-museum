**Commentary on verse 46: "We followed them with Jesus son of Mary, confirming the Torah before him", p. 373**

Al-Tabari says "We followed" means We sent after: We sent Jesus son of Mary in the footsteps of the prophets who submitted before you, Muhammad, as a prophet confirming Our book sent to Moses before him as true, and that acting by what the Gospel did not abrogate of it is binding; "and We gave him the Gospel" means We sent down to him the book named the Gospel, "in it guidance", which is clarity about what people did not know of God's ruling in his time, "and light", brightness against the blindness of ignorance, "confirming what was before it" of God's books, "and guidance and counsel for the God-fearing", clarifying the ruling God approved for His God-fearing servants in Jesus' time, an admonition turning them from what God dislikes to what He loves and alerting them to it.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 373}
