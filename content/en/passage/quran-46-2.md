## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Hami" (Praise): Our explanation has already been given in the meaning of the phrase: "Hamim* the revelation of the Book," which is sufficient to repeat it here. And His saying: "We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them except in truth," the Exalted says: "There is no one between them of the world's categories except by truth," meaning: except to establish truth and justice in creation. And His saying: "and a specified term" means: Otherwise, there is a term for all of that known to Him, which he annihilates once it reaches it, and annihilates it after it existed by its creation. And His saying: "And those who disbelieve in what they were warned will turn away," the Exalted says: And those who denied the oneness of Allah are turning away from Allah's warning, they do not take heed from Him, nor do they reflect, and they are heeded.
