## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm used to pray in a single shirt, in the chapter on permission to pray in a single garment. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.203}
