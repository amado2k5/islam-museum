## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is for whoever honors the symbols of Allah, for indeed, they are of the piety of hearts": This is what I have mentioned to you, O people, and commanded you to avoid impurity from idols and to avoid false speech, which is the truthfulness of Allah, and the glorification of Allah's symbols. This is the preference for the body, its well-being, and performing the rites of Hajj as Allah commanded, the Glorified and Exalted, from the piety of your hearts. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
