## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, that Sulayman ibn Yasar used to say: the blood-money of a Magian is eight hundred dirhams. Malik said: this is the practice among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.435}
