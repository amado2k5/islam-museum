## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when it is said to them, 'Follow what Allah has revealed,' they say, 'Rather, we will follow what we found our forefathers upon.' If Satan was calling them to the punishment of the Blaze," and if those who argue about the oneness of Allah out of ignorance of His greatness are told: 'Follow what Allah revealed to His Messenger and believe it,' he distinguishes between the rightful among us and the falsehood, and separates the misguided from the rightly guided. They said: Rather, we follow what we found our ancestors in the religions of, for they were people of truth. Allah Almighty said, "Or if Satan were calling them" by embellishing their evil deeds, following him in their misguidance, disbelieving in Allah, and abandoning them from following what Allah revealed in His Book to His Prophet, "to the punishment of the blazing fire," meaning: the punishment of the fire that roars and burns.
