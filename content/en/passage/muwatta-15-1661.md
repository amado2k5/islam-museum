## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Talha ibn Abdallah ibn Awf, who was the most knowledgeable of them about this, and from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, that Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf divorced his wife irrevocably while he was ill, and Uthman ibn Affan made her his heir after her waiting period ended. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.83}
