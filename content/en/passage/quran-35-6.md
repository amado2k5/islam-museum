## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Satan is an enemy to you, so take him as an enemy." They only invite his party to be among the companions of the Blaze: "Indeed, Satan," whom I have forbidden you, O people, from being deceived by his arrogance against Allah, "You have an enemy, so take him as an enemy." He means: So bring him down from yourselves the enemy's house among you, and beware of him in obeying Allah and seeking refuge with Him. Beware of your enemy whom you fear for yourselves, so do not obey him nor follow his steps, for he is only calling his party, meaning his followers, and those who obey him to obey and accept Him, and disbelieve in Allah "so that they may be among The companions of the Blaze" means: to be among those eternally eternal in the fire of Hell, which burns upon its people. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
