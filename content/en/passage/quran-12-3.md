**Commentary on verse 3 (pp. 551-553)**

On [[passage/quran-12-3|12:3]] al-Tabari says: God says to His Prophet Muhammad, 'We relate to you', Muhammad, 'the best of stories' by Our revelation to you of this Qur'an, telling you in it of past events, the news of earlier nations and the books We sent down in times gone by, 'though before it you were among the heedless', that is, though before We revealed it to you, Muhammad, you were among the heedless of that, not knowing it or any of it; report 18772, from Qatada: of past books and God's affairs with nations of old. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 551}

It is reported that this verse came down to the Messenger of God because his Companions asked him to tell them stories; reports 18773, from Ibn Abbas, and 18774, from 'Amr b. Qays: they said, Messenger of God, if only you would tell us stories, and 'We relate to you the best of stories' came down; the editor mentions the weakness of one narrator and points to Sa'd's report; report 18775, from 'Awn b. Abdallah: the Companions of the Messenger of God grew weary and said, tell us, and God sent down 'God has sent down the best of speech' in [[passage/quran-39-23|39:23]], then they grew weary again and said, tell us something above speech and below the Qur'an, meaning stories, and God sent down these verses, so they wanted speech and He guided them to the best of speech and wanted stories and He guided them to the best of stories; the editor explains that the report is mursal. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 552}

Report 18776, from Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas: the Qur'an was sent down to the Prophet and he recited it to them for a time, and they said, Messenger of God, if only you would tell us stories, and God sent down 'Alif Lam Ra, those are the verses of the clear Book' to 'so that you may understand'; then he recited it to them for a time and they said, if only you would speak to us, and God sent down 'God has sent down the best of speech' in [[passage/quran-39-23|39:23]]; Khallad said another man added that they said so and God sent down 'is it not time for the believers that their hearts humble themselves at the remembrance of God' in [[passage/quran-57-16|57:16]]. The editor gives notes on the narrators and says al-Hakim relates it by this route and judged it sound, with al-Dhahabi agreeing, and cites al-Suyuti and others. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 553}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
