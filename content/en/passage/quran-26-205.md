## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 205 to 207 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have you seen that We have granted them enjoyment for years": Then came to them the punishment they had been promised for their disbelief in Our verses and their denial of Our Messenger, "What did not benefit them?" He says: What benefit is the delay that delayed their lives, and the enjoyments We provided them with of life, when they did not repent from their polytheism? Did We only increase their favor by misunderstanding? Did it benefit them at all? Rather, it harmed them by increasing sins and acquiring them from crime, so that if they did not enjoy, they would not have acquired it. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase "Have you seen that We have enjoyed them for years?" up to his saying, and he quotes in the Maqta' statements from Ibn Zayd.
