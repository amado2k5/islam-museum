## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Safiyya bint Abi Ubayd told him that Abu Bakr al-Siddiq was brought a man who had intercourse with a virgin slave-girl and made her pregnant, then confessed to adultery, and he had not been married. Abu Bakr ordered the prescribed flogging against him, then he was exiled to Fadak. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.388}
