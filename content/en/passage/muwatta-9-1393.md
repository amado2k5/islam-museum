## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr from Abdallah ibn Waqid that he said: the Messenger of God forbade eating the meat of sacrifices after three days. Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr mentioned that to Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, who said: he is right; I heard Aisha say: people from the desert came into the presence at the time of the Sacrifice in the time of the Messenger of God; he said: store for three days, and give in charity what is left. Later it was said to him: people used to benefit from their sacrifices; he said: and what is that? They said: you forbade the meat of sacrifices after three days. He said: I only forbade you because of the crowd that came upon you; so eat, give in charity, and store. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.623}
