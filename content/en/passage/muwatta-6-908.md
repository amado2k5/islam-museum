## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on dyed garments in ihram. Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he said: the Messenger of God forbade the one in ihram to wear a garment dyed with saffron or wars, and said: whoever cannot find sandals may wear socks and cut them below the ankles. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it, and through him Ibn Majah and Ibn Hibban, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi in Abu Dawud (1824), al-Tinnisi in al-Bukhari (5852), al-Shafii, and Yahya ibn Yahya al-Nisaburi in Muslim. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.438}
