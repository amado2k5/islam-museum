## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 139 to 142 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "When he remained to the charged ark": when he fled to the ark, which is the ship, the loaded one, as al-Muqaddah, as narrated by Qatadah, "to the charged ark," we used to say that it is the "loaded ark" from the ark. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase "the charged ark," he said: the honored one. And the phrase "he contributed" means he competed. In the segment, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
