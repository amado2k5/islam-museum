## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 83 to 87 in a single passage. He interprets His saying, "Is this hadith you are being deceived?" as: Is it by this Qur'an whose story I have informed you and whose matter I have told you, O people, that you are softening the word for those who deny it, as a leniency on your part to deny it and disbelieve? The scholars of interpretation differed in its interpretation, with some saying similar to our view on this matter. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
