## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "When the two receivers receive from the right and from the left," he explains: "And We are closer to a person than the vein in his throat when he receives the two angels, which are the receivers 'on the right and from the left, Qa'id." It is said that by 'Qa'id' he meant the observation. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
