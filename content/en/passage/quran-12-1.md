**Commentary on verse 1 (pp. 549-550)**

Surat Yusuf begins in this volume with its title on p. 547 and the text on p. 549. On [[passage/quran-12-1|12:1]] al-Tabari says he has already given the interpreters' differences on 'Alif Lam Ra, those are the verses of the Book' and the view he prefers, which spares repeating it here. As for 'those are the verses of the clear Book', the interpreters differ on its sense; some said it means the clear Book that makes plain its lawful and unlawful, its right guidance and its guidance; report 18768, from Mujahid: it makes plain its lawful and unlawful; the editor weakens a narrator of the chain severely. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 549}

Reports 18769 and 18770, from Qatada: God made plain its guidance and right direction. Others said what report 18771, from Mu'adh, says: it made plain the letters that fell from the tongues of non-Arabs, six letters; the editor says the report is spoiled by its narrator al-Walid b. Salama. Al-Tabari says: the correct view with me is that the sense is these are the verses of the Book that is clear to whoever recites it and ponders its lawful and unlawful, its prohibition and the other kinds of meaning it holds, because God said it is clear and did not limit its clarity to part of what is in it rather than all. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 550}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
