## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, “They are the ones who say, ‘Do not spend on those who are with the Messenger of Allah until they disperse.’ And to Allah belong the treasures of the heavens and the earth, but the hypocrites do not understand.” He, may He be exalted, says, “They are the ones who say,” meaning those who say to their companions, “Do not spend on those who are with the Messenger of Allah,” from among his companions, the emigrants, “until they disperse,” meaning until they separate from him. His statement, “And to Allah belong the treasures of the heavens and the earth,” means that everything in the heavens and the earth belongs to Allah, and the keys to those treasures are in His hands. No one can give anything to anyone except by His will. “But the hypocrites do not understand” that this is so, and that is why they say, “Do not spend on those who are with the Messenger of Allah until they disperse.” The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
