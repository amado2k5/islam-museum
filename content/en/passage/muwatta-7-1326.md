## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: by Him in whose hand my soul is, no one is wounded in God's path, and God knows best who is wounded in His path, except that he comes on the Day of Resurrection with his wound flowing blood, its colour the colour of blood and its smell the smell of musk. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.593}
