## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 73 to 74 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "O mankind, set forth an example, so listen to it. Indeed, those you invoke besides Allah will never create a fly, even if they were gathered together for Him. And if the flies should steal from them something from which they cannot rescue it, the weak seeker and the sought will be weak": O people, Allah has set forth an example and remembrance. The meaning of 'to strike' in this place is: 'to make' from their saying: 'He imposed the authority upon the people of resurrection,' meaning: He imposed upon them. And he imposed the jizya on the Christians, meaning he placed that upon them, and likeness: the likeness. He, the Exalted, says: He made for me a likeness, O people, meaning by likeness and likeness: the gods. He says: He made for me the polytheists and idols like them, so worship them with Me and associate them in My worship. 'So listen to Him,' meaning: So listen to the state of what they liked, and make it in their worship like and attribute to Him. "Indeed, those you invoke besides Allah will never create a fly." He means: All the gods and idols you worship besides Allah, if they were collected, they would not have created a fly in its small size and scarcity, because it cannot bear or power that, even if all His creation were combined. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
