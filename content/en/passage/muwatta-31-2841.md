## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: my heirs shall not divide a single dinar; what I leave after the maintenance of my wives and the wages of my workers is charity. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.593}
