## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, who used to say: one in ihram does not cup unless compelled to it by something he cannot do without. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1190), Suwayd and al-Shaybani (416) transmitted it from Malik, and that al-Bukhari transmitted it in suspension from Malik's route. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.470}
