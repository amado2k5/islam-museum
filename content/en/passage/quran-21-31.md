## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We placed on the earth firm mountains that you might extend [in them], and We made therein plains of roads that they might be guided": Or did these disbelievers not see Our proofs against them and all Our creation, "Did We place firm mountains on the earth?" Al-Rawasi: plural of 'rasiyah,' which is established as: According to Qatadah, His saying: 'And We placed mountains upon the earth' means mountains. And His saying: 'to extend with them' means: not to rely on them. He, Glorified and Exalted, says: So We placed these mountain peaks on this land, and We fixed them so that they would not be supported by people, and they would be able to stand firm on their backs. Also: And from Qatadah, he said: They used to be on the earth with dates that did not settle down, and they woke up having God made the mountains, which are the mountains, the pegs of the earth. "And We made therein paths paths," meaning paths, singular faj. Also: from Qatadah, His saying: "And We made in them mountains" means banners. And His saying: "Sabla" means paths, which is the plural of the path. He also quotes in the Maqta' from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
