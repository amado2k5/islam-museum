**Commentary on verse 19 (p. 285)**

On [[passage/quran-11-19|11:19]] al-Tabari says: the curse of God is on the wrongdoers who bar people from belief in Him, from acknowledging servitude to Him and making worship pure for Him apart from gods and rivals, among the polytheists of Quraysh, who were tempting away from Islam whoever entered it; 'and seek to make it crooked' means they look for God's way, which is Islam to which Muhammad called people, with swerving and deviation from uprightness; 'and they, in the next life, are disbelievers' means they deny the resurrection after death, together with their barring from God's way and seeking to make it crooked. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'barring', 'rebellion', 'God's way' and 'crookedness'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 285}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
