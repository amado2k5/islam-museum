## Summary
Malik was asked about a man who vowed to go on a raid and prepared himself, but when he wished to set out his parents, or one of them, forbade him. He said: I do not think he should overpower them; he should postpone it to another year. As for his equipment, he keeps it until he sets out with it; if he fears it will spoil, he sells it and keeps the price until he buys with it what suits the raid. If he is wealthy and can find equal equipment when he sets out, he may do with his equipment whatever he wishes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.579}
