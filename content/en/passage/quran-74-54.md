## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54-56 as a single section. He explains His statement, “Nay, indeed, it is a reminder,” meaning that God, may His praise be exalted, says, “Nay, indeed, it is a reminder.” The matter is not as these polytheists claim about this Qur’an, that it is magic that has an effect, and that it is merely the word of man, but rather it is a reminder from God to His creation, reminding them of it.
