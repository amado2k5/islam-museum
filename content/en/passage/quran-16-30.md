## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And it was said to those who feared Allah, 'What has your Lord revealed?' They said, 'What good has He sent down in this world? And the home of the Hereafter is good. And excellent is the home of the righteous.'" And it was said to the other group who are people of faith and piety of Allah: 'What has your Lord revealed? They said good.' He says: They said: He sent down good. Some of the Arabs from Kufan used to say: The Bedouins differed regarding his saying: "They said the legends of the ancients," and his saying: "good." The issue before both answers is the same, which is: "What has your Lord revealed?" because the disbelievers denied the revelation. When they heard it, they said: the legends of the ancients, meaning this is what the legends of the ancients brought and nothing from it was revealed. As for the believers, they believed in the revelation, saying good, meaning that it revealed good, so they stood by the fact that the action from God is good. Therefore, they separated and began the report, saying: "For those who do good in this world, there is good." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
