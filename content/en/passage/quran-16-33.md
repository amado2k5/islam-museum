## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Do they wait until the angels come to them or the command of your Lord comes, as did those before them? And Allah did not wrong them, but they were wronging themselves": Are these polytheists waiting until the angels come to them to take their souls, or do they receive the command of your Lord to gather them at the station of the Resurrection? "Thus did those before them," says He, Glorified and Exalted: "Just as those who await the angels of Allah to take their souls or carry out Allah's command do the actions of their predecessors who disbelieved in Allah, because that is in every polytheist with Allah." "And Allah did not wrong them," says He, Glorified and Exalted: "nor did Allah wrong them by placing His wrath, but they wronged themselves" by disobeying their Lord and disbelieving in Him, until they deserved His punishment, so He hastened for them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
