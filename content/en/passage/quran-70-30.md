## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 31 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “except with their wives or those whom their right hands possess, for indeed, they are not to be blamed” as referring to their female slaves. It has also been said that the phrase “guarding their private parts, except with their wives” is not preceded by a denial, because the phrase “for indeed, they are not to be blamed” implies a denial.
