## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 36 as one section. He explains His saying, “Go forth to that which you used to deny”: God Almighty says to these deniers of these blessings and the proofs with which He argued against them on the Day of Resurrection: “Go forth to that which you used to deny” in the world, of God’s punishment for the people of disbelief in Him. “Go forth to a shadow of three branches,” meaning God Almighty: to a shadow of smoke of three branches, “not providing shade,” because smoke rises from its fuel, as has been mentioned, and when it rises, it splits into three branches, and that is what He says: “of three branches.” On the authority of Ibn Abi Najih, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding the verse, “to a shadow of three branches,” he said: “It is the smoke of Hell.” The passage also includes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
