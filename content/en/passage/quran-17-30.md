## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, your Lord extends provision for whom He wills and restricts [it] for whom He wills. Indeed, He is Acquainted with His servants, Aware and Seeing": Indeed, your Lord, O Muhammad, expands His provision for whom He wills among His servants, expands it for him, and restricts it for whom He wills. He says: "Indeed, He is Acquainted with His servants." He says: Your Lord is experienced with His servants, and who is it that abundance in provision improves and corrupts, and who is it that stinginess and hardship improve and destroy. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
