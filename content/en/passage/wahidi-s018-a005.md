## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Kahf 18:5, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «فمن كان يرجوا لقاء ربه».
