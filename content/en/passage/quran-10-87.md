**Commentary on verse 87 (pp. 171-174)**

On [[passage/quran-10-87|10:87]] al-Tabari says: We inspired Moses and his brother to take houses for their people in Egypt. One says 'so-and-so took a house for himself' when he took one, and 'I took him a house' when I took one for him. 'And make your houses a qibla' means make your houses places of prayer in which you pray. The interpreters differed on this. Some said what al-Tabari said. Report 17793, from Ibn Abbas: mosques; report 17794, from him through Ikrima: they were ordered to take them as mosques; report 17795: they were afraid of Pharaoh and his people and being barred, so he told them to make their houses mosques in which to pray. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'taking a house' and 'qibla'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 171}

Reports 17796 and 17797, from Ibrahim al-Nakha'i: they were afraid and were ordered to pray in their houses; report 17798, from Ikrima from Ibn Abbas, likewise; report 17799, from Mujahid: they prayed only in synagogues and only in fear, so they were ordered to pray in their houses; report 17800, from him: they were afraid, so they were ordered to pray in their houses. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 172}

Report 17801, from Abu Malik: the Children of Israel feared Pharaoh, so they were ordered to make their houses mosques in which to pray; report 17802, from al-Rabi' b. Anas: mosques; report 17803, from Ibrahim: they prayed in their houses in fear; report 17804, from al-Dahhak, on 'take houses for your people in Egypt': mosques; report 17805, from Ibrahim, likewise; report 17806, from Ibn Zayd from his father: put your mosques in your houses and pray in them, that is the qibla. The editor says the printed text alone has 'he said, he said, my father Zayd' and he kept the manuscript's reading. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 173}

Others said the meaning is: make your mosques face the Ka'ba. Report 17807, from Ibn Abbas: meaning the Ka'ba; report 17808, from him: the Children of Israel said to Moses we cannot show our prayer in the presence of the pharaohs, so God allowed them to pray in their houses and they were ordered to make their houses face the qibla; report 17809, from Mujahid from Ibn Abbas: turn your houses, your mosques, toward the qibla, and does He not say [[passage/quran-24-36|24:36]]; report 17810, from Mujahid: facing the qibla; reports 17811 and 17812, from Mujahid: toward the Ka'ba, when Moses and those with him feared Pharaoh, that they would be barred from the great churches, so they were ordered to make mosques in their houses facing the Ka'ba and pray in them in secret. Report 17813, from Mujahid, begins at the foot of the page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 174}

We stop here at the foot of p. 174, in the middle of the commentary on verse 87; the next batch begins at p. 175. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 174}
