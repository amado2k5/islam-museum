## In al-Tabari's commentary: "deaf, dumb, blind, so they do not return"
Al-Tabari takes "God took away their light and left them in darkness" as a report of what God will do to the hypocrites in the Hereafter, when their veils are torn, their secrets exposed and their lights taken, and takes "deaf, dumb, blind" as a postponed phrase whose sense is placed earlier: those who bought error with guidance... are deaf, dumb, blind, so they do not return, their likeness being that of one who kindled a fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 328} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 329}
He sets out the grammar: the nominative in two ways, as a fresh clause of censure or by repeating "those", and the accusative in two ways, as a circumstantial from "those" or from "alladhina", with a third as censure. He chooses the nominative because it agrees with the script of the Muslims' codices. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 329} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 330}
He explains the verse as saying that by buying error they were not guided: they are deaf to the truth and do not hear it, dumb and do not speak it, blind and do not see it, because God has sealed their hearts for their hypocrisy. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 330}
