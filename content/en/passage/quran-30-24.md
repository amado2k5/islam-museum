## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among His signs is that He shows you the lightning out of fear and hope, and He sends down water from the sky and revives the earth after its death. Indeed, in that are signs for people who reason." Among His proofs is "He shows you fear for you if you are traveling, that you may rain and be harmed by it, and you will be greedy" if you are in a state of residence to rain, so you will live and fertile. "And water will fall from the sky," meaning: rain will fall from the sky, and the water will revive the dead earth, so it will grow and its crops will grow after its death, meaning its barrenness and sprouting. "Indeed, in that are signs," he says: Indeed, in doing so are lessons and proofs for a people who reason—their arguments and proofs about God.
