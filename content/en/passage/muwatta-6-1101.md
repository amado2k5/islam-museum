## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on fasting on the days of Mina. Malik transmits from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubaydallah, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that the Messenger of God forbade fasting on the days of Mina. The editor notes that the relayed version in the Muwatta is the preserved one from Malik; that Sufyan al-Thawri connected it through Salim Abu al-Nadr and Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr from Sulayman ibn Yasar from Abdullah ibn Hudhafa al-Sahmi; that Ibn Maʿin called it relayed; and that Ibn Abd al-Barr considered it connected through other routes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.504}
