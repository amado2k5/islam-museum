## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And you will see every nation kneeling, every nation called to its Book today; you will be recompensed for what you used to do": And you will see, O Muhammad, the Day when the Hour will be established, the people of every nation and religion will be kneeling: He says: gathered and provoked on their knees because of the horror of that Day. Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid regarding the phrase: "And you will see every nation kneeling," he said: provoked. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "And you will see every nation kneeling," he said: This is on the Day of Resurrection kneeling on their knees. I narrated from Al-Husayn, he said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say, regarding the phrase: "And you will see every nation kneeling," he says: on the knees at the time of reckoning. And His saying: "Every nation is called to its Book" means: every people of a sect and religion is called to its Book, which it has dictated to its memorizer. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
