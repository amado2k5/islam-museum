## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “And Allah presents an example for those who believed: the wife of Pharaoh, when she said, ‘My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise and save me from Pharaoh and his deeds and save me from the wrongdoing people,’” explains: Allah presents an example for those who believed in Him and His Oneness, the wife of Pharaoh who believed in Allah and His Oneness, and believed in His Messenger Moses, even though she was married to an enemy of Allah, an unbeliever. Her husband’s disbelief did not harm her, for she was a believer in Allah. It is Allah’s decree for His creation that no soul shall bear the burden of another, and that every soul is responsible for what it has earned, as she said, “My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise and save me from Pharaoh and his deeds and save me from the wrongdoing people.” “A house in Paradise,” so God answered her prayer and built her a house in Paradise, as narrated by Sulayman al-Taymi, on the authority of Abu Uthman, on the authority of Salman, who said: Pharaoh’s wife was tortured by the sun. When it left her, the angels shaded her with their wings, and she could see her house in Paradise. Also narrated by Sulayman al-Taymi, on the authority of Abu Uthman, who said: Sulayman said: Pharaoh’s wife… and he mentioned something similar. The passage also includes statements attributed to Qatadah.
