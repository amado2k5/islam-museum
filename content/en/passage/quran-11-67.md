**Commentary on verse 67 (pp. 380-381)**

On [[passage/quran-11-67|11:67]] al-Tabari says: the cry struck those who did what they had no right to do, hamstringing God's she-camel and disbelieving in Him, 'and in the morning they were prostrate in their homes', fate having cut them down and left them still in their courtyards. Report 18294, from Qatada: 'prostrate in their homes' means they were destroyed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 381}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
