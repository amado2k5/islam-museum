## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 36 in a single passage. He explains the verse: "And set forth for them two men: We made for one of them gardens of grapes and surrounded them with palm trees and placed between them crops." And give to these polytheists of Allah, who asked you to drive out those who call upon their Lord morning and evening, seeking His face, "for example" like "Two men, one of whom We made two gardens," meaning We made for him gardens of vineyards, "and surrounded them with palm trees," meaning: "And We surrounded these two gardens with palm trees." And His saying: "And We placed between them a plantation" means: And We placed a seedling between these two orchards. And His saying: "Both gardens yielded their fruit," means: both gardens were nourished with their fruit and the plantations in them of palm trees, vineyards, and various types of crops. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
