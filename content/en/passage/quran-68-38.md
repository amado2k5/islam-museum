## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37-39 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Indeed, you have therein whatever you choose”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: Indeed, you have therein whatever you choose for yourselves. This is a command from God, a rebuke and reproach to these people for the falsehood they uttered and the false hopes they harbored. And His saying, “Or do you have oaths upon Us that extend to the Day of Resurrection?” means: Do you have oaths upon Us that will extend to the Day of Resurrection, that you have what you judge? That is, that you have your judgment. However, the alif in “inna” is broken because the lam is added to the predicate: meaning, do you have oaths that you have your judgment? In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd.
