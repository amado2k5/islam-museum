## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 118 to 120 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And that you will neither thirst in it nor sacrifice therein": that you will not be afflicted by thirst or heat in it. My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, His saying: "And that you neither thirst in it nor sacrifice," meaning: no heat or harm will affect you. He said: My father narrated to me, from Khasif from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, "Do not thirst in it nor sacrifice," he said: The sun will not touch you. And from Qatadah, "And do not sacrifice," he said: The sun will not touch you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
