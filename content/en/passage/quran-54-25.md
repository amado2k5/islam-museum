## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Should the reminder be cast upon him from among us? Rather, he is a liar and evil": Allah Almighty says, informing him about the saying of those who denied His messenger Salih from his people, Thamud: "Should the reminder be cast upon him from among us?" They mean: "The revelation was sent down and he was exclusively granted prophethood among us, and he is one of us," denying that God would send a messenger from among the children of Adam. And His saying: 'Rather, he is a wicked liar,' he says: They said: That is not so; rather, he is a wicked liar. By 'Ashr' they mean 'joyful with arrogance and pride,' and 'joy' comes from activity. I said to Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Hammad: What is the most evil liar? He said: He who does not care what he says, and with a kasra on the shin in the ashr and a lightening the rā, I read the reciters of the cities. It was mentioned from Mujahid that he used to read it as 'kadhib ashr' with a damma on the shin and a lightening of the rā', similar in speech to 'al-hadhar' and 'al-haadar', 'ajjal' and 'ajl'. He quotes in the passage from Mujahid.
