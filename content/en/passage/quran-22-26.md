## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "And [mention] when We showed Abraham the place of the House, [saying], 'Do not associate anything with Me and purify My House for those who perform Tawaf and those who stand, and those who bow and prostrate.'" Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, His great teacher, who assembled His people from Quraysh in particular and excluded all His creation, by worshiping them in its sanctuary, and the House which Abraham commanded his friend to build and purify from corruption, doubt, and polytheism: 'And remember, O Muhammad, how We began this House in which Your people worship others except Me, when We made it for our friend Abraham,' meaning by His saying: "Bu'ana": We set foot in the place of the house. Also: from Qatadah, regarding His saying: "And [mention] when We placed the House with Abraham at the place of the House," he said: God placed the House with Adam when Adam descended to the earth, and his landing was in the land of India, with his head in the sky and his feet on the earth, so the angels feared him, and he was reduced to sixty cubits. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, Sufyan, and Ata.
