**Commentary on verse 123 (pp. 544-545)**

On [[passage/quran-11-123|11:123]] God says to His Prophet Muhammad: to God, Muhammad, belongs the dominion of all that is hidden from you in the heavens and the earth, which you have not known of; all that is in His hand and His knowledge, nothing hidden from Him, and He knows what the polytheists of your people do and where their affair is heading, whether persisting in polytheism or leaving it for repentance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 544}

'To Him the whole matter returns' means to God every doer and his deed returns, and He repays them all for their deeds; report 18766, from Ibn Jurayj: and He judges between them by His judgement with justice. 'So worship Him' means worship your Lord, Muhammad, 'and put your trust in Him', entrust your affair to Him and rely on Him and His sufficiency, for He suffices whoever trusts in Him. As for 'your Lord is not unaware of what you do', it means your Lord, Muhammad, is not heedless of what these polytheists of your people do, but encompasses it, nothing escapes Him, and He is watching them; so let their turning away from you and their denying what you brought of the truth not grieve you, go on with your Lord's command, for you are before Our eyes. Report 18767, from Ka'b: the end of the Torah is the end of Hud. After the commentary on Hud the manuscript ends with praise to God alone and a note that the commentary on the chapter in which Yusuf is mentioned follows and begins the twelfth volume of the manuscript's end, with a blessing for His Prophet and his family and Companions. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 545}

Here Surat Hud ends in the Shakir edition at p. 545; Surat Yusuf begins at p. 547, with the text from p. 549. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 545}
