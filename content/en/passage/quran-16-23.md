## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah knows what they ease and what they declare; indeed, He does not like the arrogant": meaning Allah truly knows what facilitated these polytheists in denying what we mentioned in this surah, their belief in denying our saying to them: 'Your God is one God,' their arrogance against Allah, and their openly disbelief in Allah and their oppression against Him. 'Indeed, He does not love the arrogant.' He means: Allah does not love the arrogant to unite Him and remove all gods and equals besides Him. Also: According to a man: Al-Hasan ibn Ali used to sit with the poor and then say: "He does not like the arrogant."
