## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O mankind, indeed We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted." This verse also narrates sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
