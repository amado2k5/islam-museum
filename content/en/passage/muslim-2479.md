## Summary
A report of Nafi' from Ibn Umar in the chapter on his virtues in Sahih Muslim: the dream, and the Prophet's words, "What a man Abdullah is, if only he prayed at night". After that he was keen to pray at night. It is the text of his goodness and understanding. {cite:sahih-muslim}
