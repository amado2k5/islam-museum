## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on dress, stepping over necks and facing the imam on Friday: Abdullah ibn Umar, when he went to the Friday prayer, would oil himself and perfume himself, unless he was in a state of ihram. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.167}
