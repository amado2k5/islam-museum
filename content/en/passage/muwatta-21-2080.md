## Summary
Malik says it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab was asked whether there is a precedent about pre-emption; he said yes: pre-emption applies to houses and lands, and exists only among partners. Malik said: this is the established sunna among us, with no dispute. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.252}
