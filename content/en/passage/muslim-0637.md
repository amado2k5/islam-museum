## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari through his son Abu Bakr in the chapter on the forenoon prayer and the merit of keeping the prayers, in Sahih Muslim. The two cool prayers are the dawn and afternoon prayers, and before them is "whoever prays before sunrise and before sunset shall not enter the Fire". {cite:sahih-muslim}
