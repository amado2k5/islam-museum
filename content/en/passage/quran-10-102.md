**Commentary on verse 102 (pp. 215-216)**

On [[passage/quran-10-102|10:102]] al-Tabari says God speaks to His prophet Muhammad, warning the polytheists of his people of the swift onset of His vengeance in their courtyard like that which befell their likes before them among all the nations that passed, who took the way of denying God's messengers and rejecting their Lord's oneness: do these polytheists of your people, Muhammad, who deny what you brought from God, wait for anything but a day on which they see of God's punishment the like of the days of their forebears who were as they are in polytheism and denial, who passed before them, among the people of Noah, 'Ad and Thamud? Say to them, Muhammad, if that is what they await: wait then for God's punishment of you and for His anger to come down on you; I am among those awaiting your destruction and ruin by the punishment that will come on you from God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 215}

Report 17912, from Qatada: God's battles against those who passed before them, the people of Noah, 'Ad and Thamud; report 17913, from al-Rabi' b. Anas: He warned them of His punishment, vengeance and chastisement, and then told them that when any of that occurs God saves His messengers and those who believed with them, and then he recited [[passage/quran-10-103|10:103]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 216}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
