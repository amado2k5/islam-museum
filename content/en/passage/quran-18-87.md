## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "As for one who wrongs us, We will punish him, then he will be returned to his Lord and He will punish him with a disgraceful punishment": He says, "As for the one who wrongs, We will punish him," he means: As for the one who disbelieves, We will kill him, as Muammar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "As for the one who wrongs us, We will punish him," he said: It is killing. And His saying: "Then he will be returned to his Lord and He will punish him with a disliked punishment," means: Then he will return to Allah after his death, and He will punish him with a great punishment, which is denial, and that is the punishment of Hell. In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
