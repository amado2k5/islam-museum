## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Do they not know that Allah extends provision for whom He wills and restricts it? Indeed, in that are signs for people who believe": Or did they not know, O Muhammad, those from whom We revealed their harm, and they said: We have only given it with knowledge that hardship, prosperity, abundance, hardship, and affliction are in the hands of Allah, not for anyone else. He extends provision to whom He wills, expands it for him, and decrees it for whomever He wills among His servants, restricting it. This is among Allah's proofs over His servants, so that they may take Him into consideration, remember, and know that desire and awe for Him is not for the gods and equals. "Indeed, in that are signs," he says: Indeed, in Allah's provision is extended to whom He wills, and in His restriction upon whomever He wills, meaning: indications and signs of "for a people who believe," meaning they believe in the truth, and acknowledge it when they clarify it and know its true reality, that the one who does this is Allah and not everything else.
