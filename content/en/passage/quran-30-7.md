## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "They know the apparent meaning of the worldly life while they are heedless of the Hereafter": These deniers know the truth of God's report that the Romans will overcome Persia apparently from their worldly life, their livelihoods, what benefits them, and they are unaware of their Hereafter and what will save them from God's punishment. There, they are heedless and do not reflect on it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Hasan, Ikrimah, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
