## Summary
The first report in the chapter on zakat on goods. Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Ruzayq ibn Hayyan, who was at the customs post of Egypt in the time of al-Walid, Sulayman and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote to him: take from Muslims passing your post, from what they trade with, one dinar from each forty dinars, and in proportion below that until twenty dinars; if it falls short by a third of a dinar, leave it. From the people of the protected covenant (dhimmis) take one dinar from each twenty dinars, and in proportion until ten dinars; if it falls short by a third of a dinar, leave it; and write them a letter for what you take, valid for the next year. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.346}
