## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And the rules are among the women who do not hope for marriage, so there is no blame upon them if they put on their garments without adornment, but they remain chaste. And Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing." "Those who do not hope for marriage" means: those who have despaired of a wife and should not covet their husbands. "So there is no blame upon them if they put on their clothes," meaning: there is no blame or sin upon them to put on their clothes, meaning their jilabiya, which is the veil that is over the khimar and the robe that is over the clothes. There is no blame on them to wear this in front of mahrams, men and non-mahram strangers, not adorned with adornment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
