## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the practice among us regarding an umm walad who commits a crime: her master is liable for what lies between her and her value, and he may not hand her over, and he is not required to bear more of her crime than her value. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.287}
