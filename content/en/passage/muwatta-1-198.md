## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Sulayman ibn Yasar as a mursal, in the chapter on opening the prayer: the Prophet raised his hands in prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik, and Ibn Abd al-Barr says it is mursal for everyone who transmitted it from Malik, as Shu'ba also transmitted it from Yahya ibn Sa'id. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.125} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.126}
