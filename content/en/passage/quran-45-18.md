## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Then We set you upon a law of affairs, so follow it and do not follow the desires of those who do not know": Then We made you, O Muhammad, after what We gave the Children of Israel, whose attributes You described to you, "on a law of affairs," meaning: according to the way, tradition, and methodology of Our command that We commanded before you from Our messengers. "So follow it," meaning: "So follow that law which We have made for you." "And do not follow the desires of those who do not know," meaning: And do not follow what the ignorant of Allah have called you to, who do not distinguish truth from falsehood, so act upon it, and you will perish if you act upon it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
