## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And indeed, We wrote in the Psalms after the remembrance that the earth will be inherited by My righteous servants": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the Psalms and remembrance in this context. Some said: By the Psalms, I mean all the books of the prophets that Allah revealed to them, and by remembrance, I mean the Mother of the Book which He has in heaven. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
