## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So give the relative his due, and the needy and the traveler—that is better for those who seek the face of Allah, and those are the successful." So give, O Muhammad, the relative of yours his due from the relationship, the needy, the poor, and the traveler, as Allah has prescribed for them in that, as from Awf, from Al-Hasan: "So give the relative his due, the needy, and the traveler," meaning to give them their due if it is with ease, and if not with you, then speak to them with ease, say to them good. And His saying: "That is better for those who seek the Face of Allah," the Exalted says: Giving these people their rights that Allah has obligated His servants to is better for those who seek to be given that by giving them, "and those are the successful." He means: Whoever does that seeking the Pleasure of Allah through it, those are the successful, those who fulfill their requests with Allah, the successful in what they seek and seek to give them what they have been given. In the passage, he quotes from Al-Hasan.
