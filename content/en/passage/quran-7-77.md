Then, on [[passage/quran-7-77|7:77]], al-Tabari says: Thamud hamstrung the she-camel God had made a sign for them, "and defied their Lord's command", that is, were proud and overbearing toward following God and rose above the truth. (14826) from Mujahid that "atau" means they rose above the truth, unseeing; (14827) from him that they rose in falsehood; (14828) that they defied in falsehood and left the truth; (14829) that they rose in falsehood. It is from the saying "a jabbar atin", one high in his tyranny. Then "they said, Salih, bring us what you promise" means: they said, bring us, Salih, what you threaten us with. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 543}
