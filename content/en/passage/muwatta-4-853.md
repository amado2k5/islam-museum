## Summary
It reached Malik that Abdullah ibn Umar was asked about a pregnant woman who fears for her child and finds fasting hard; he said: she breaks her fast and feeds one needy person for each day a mudd of wheat by the mudd of the Prophet. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (811). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.413}
