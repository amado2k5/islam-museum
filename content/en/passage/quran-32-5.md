## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He manages the matter from the heavens to the earth, then ascends to Him in a day whose measure is a thousand years from what you count": Allah is the One who manages the matter of His creation from the heaven to the earth, then ascends to Him. The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase "Then it ascends to Him in a day whose measure was a thousand years of what you count," and some said: Its meaning is that the matter descends from the heaven to the earth and ascends from the earth to the sky in one day, and that is a thousand years of the days you count in this world, because the distance from the earth to the sky is five hundred years, and the distance from the sky to the earth is the same, so that is a thousand years. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Ikrimah, Samak, and others.
