## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "As for the ship, it belonged to poor people working at sea, so I wanted to disgrace it, and behind them was a king who would take every ship by force": As for what I did to the ship, because it belonged to a poor people "working at sea, I wanted to disgrace it" with the breach I had torn, as reported by Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid regarding the words of Allah, the Almighty: "So I wanted to disgrace her," he said: "I breached it." And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, similar. He said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, the same. And His saying: "And behind them was an angel who seized every ship by force," and there was an angel in front of them and in front of them. As Muammar told us, from Qatadah: "And behind them was an angel." Qatadah said: In front of them. Don't you see that he says: "Behind them is Hell," which is before them. And from Qatadah, he said: It was in the Qur'an: There was a king before them who would seize every valid ship by force. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Jurayj.
