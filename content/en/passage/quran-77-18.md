## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 19 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Thus We deal with the criminals,” as follows: Just as We destroyed these people for their disbelief in Me and their rejection of My messengers, so too is My way with similar disbelieving nations; We destroy the criminals for their crimes when they transgress and oppress.
