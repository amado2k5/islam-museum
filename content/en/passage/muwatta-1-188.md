## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the call to prayer: Abdullah ibn Umar heard the iqama while he was in al-Baqi' and hurried his walk to the mosque. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and Waki' transmitted it, the last in Ibn Abi Shayba. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.120}
