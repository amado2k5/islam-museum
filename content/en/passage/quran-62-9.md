## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “O you who have believed, when the call to prayer is made on Friday, then proceed to the remembrance of Allah and leave off trade. That is better for you, if you only knew,” explains that Allah, may He be exalted, says to His believing servants: O you who have believed in Allah and His Messenger, “when the call to prayer is made on Friday”—and this is the call, the call to prayer for Friday prayer when the Imam sits on the pulpit for the sermon—the meaning of the statement is: when the call to prayer is made on Friday, “then proceed to the remembrance of Allah.” He says: Go forth To remember God, and to work for Him. The essence of striving in this context is work, and we have already mentioned the evidence for this. The passage also quotes statements from Qatadah, Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
