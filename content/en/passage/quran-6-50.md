## Summary
Verse 50 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 50 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "say: I do not say to you that I have the treasures of God, nor do I know the unseen, nor do I say to you that I am an angel; I follow only what is revealed to me; say: are the blind and the seeing equal? Will you not reflect?" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: say to these who deny your prophethood: I do not say to you that I am the Lord who has the treasures of the heavens and the earth, so that I know the hidden things that none knows but the Lord from whom nothing is hidden, and that you deny me in what I say of that; for none should be a lord but he who owns all things, in whose hand are all things and from whom nothing hidden is hidden, and that is God, besides whom there is no god; "nor do I say to you that I am an angel", for it is not fit for an angel to appear in his form to the eyes of humans in this world, such that you reject what I tell you; "I follow only what is revealed to me" means: say to them, I follow in what I say to you and call you to only God's revelation that he reveals to me and his sending down that he sends down on me, and I carry out his revelation and obey his command; I have come to you with proofs from God that cut off your excuse for the soundness of my word in that, and what I say of it is not something your minds reject or cannot be, but with the existence of proof of its reality it is perfect wisdom, so what ground have you to deny it? That is God's pointing his prophet to the place of his argument against those of his people's polytheists who deny his prophethood. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 371}

The editor refers for "the unseen", "angel" and "revelation" to pp. 238, 263 and 290 and to volumes 4 and 1, and keeps "and I obey his command" from the manuscript where the printed text has "and I command to his command".

"Say: are the blind and the seeing equal?" says: say, Muhammad... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 371}
