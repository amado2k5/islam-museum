## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the saying, "Allah expands provision for whom He wills among His servants and restricts it for whomever He wills among His servants." Indeed, Allah is Knowing of all things: Allah expands His provision for whom He wills among His creation, and restricts it so He restricts it for whom He wills among them. He says: Your sustenance and its distribution among you, O people, is in My own hands, and not for anyone except Me, it is simpler for whom You will, and I am more stingy to whom You wish. So do not let migration and your enemy's struggle be prevented from the fear of family. "Indeed, Allah is Knowing of everything." He means: Allah is Knowing of your interests, and whoever is only suitable for abundant provision, and whoever is only worthy of being spacious, and He knows that.
