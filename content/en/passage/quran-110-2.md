## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 4 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "When the victory of God and the conquest comes": when the victory of God comes to you, O Muhammad, against your people from Quraysh. The conquest of Mecca means "and you saw the people" from the various types of Arabs and their tribes, the people of Yemen among them, and the tribes of Nizar "enter the religion of God in groups," meaning: in the religion of God with which He sent you, and Your obedience to which He called them "groups," meaning groups, groups upon groups. In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
