## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: whoever calls out for the pilgrimage from Mecca should delay circumambulating the House and running between al-Safa and al-Marwa until he returns from Mina, and so did Abdullah ibn Umar. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1086) and Suwayd (500) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.457}
