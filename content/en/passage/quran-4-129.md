**Commentary on verse 129: unequal love between wives (pp. 284-287)**

Al-Tabari says the verse means that men cannot make themselves equal between their wives in the love of the heart, even if they try, because that is not in their power; so the heart will hold no more love for one than for the other. They are then told not to incline wholly to the one they love, leaving the other as though she were neither married nor unmarried. Report 10626, from Mujahid through Ibn Abi Najih, says that being unable to be equal between them is an unavoidable fact. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 284}

Reports 10627 to 10633, from Ubayda through Ibn Sirin and others, give the equality as being of the self in love and intercourse, or in intercourse, or in love, or in affection; al-Hasan says it is love. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 285}

Report 10634, from Ibn Abbas, says you cannot be equal in desire between them, however much you try. Report 10635, from Qatada, says that it was reported that Umar used to say: O God, as for my heart I have no control; in other things I hope to be just. Report 10636, from Ibn Abbas, says the sense is love and intercourse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 286}

Report 10637, through Ayyub from Abu Qilaba, says that the Prophet divided among his wives and was just, then said: O God, this is my division in what I possess; do not blame me for what You possess and I do not. The editor notes that Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah and al-Tirmidhi transmit it by several routes, one through A'isha; al-Tirmidhi judged a mursal version through Hammad ibn Zayd more sound than the one through Hammad ibn Salama. Al-Tirmidhi and some scholars explain what he does not possess as love and affection; Abu Dawud says it is the heart. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 286}

Reports 10638 to 10642: through Ibn Abi Mulayka, the verse was revealed about A'isha (10638); through al-Dahhak, from Juwaybir, the sense is desire and intercourse (10639 and 10640); from Sufyan, love and intercourse (10641); from Ibn Zayd, what happens in his body and heart, which he cannot possess (10642). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 287}

We stop here after report 10642 at p. 287, and will continue from report 10643 in the commentary on verse 129 of sura 4. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 287}
