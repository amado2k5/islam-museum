## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And the people of Noah, when they denied the messengers, We drowned them and made them a sign for the people and prepared for the wrongdoers a painful punishment." And the people of Noah, when they denied Our messengers and returned to them what they brought with them of the truth, We drowned them in the flood. "And We made them a sign for mankind." He means: We made Our drowning them and Our destruction a lesson and lesson for people to take into account. "And We prepared for the wrongdoers a painful punishment," meaning: We prepared for them from among the disbelievers in the Hereafter a painful punishment, except for what befell them of the immediate punishment in this world.
