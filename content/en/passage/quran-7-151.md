**Commentary on verse 151 (p. 133)**

Al-Tabari says: Moses said, when his brother's excuse became clear to him and he knew that he had not failed in the duty God's command laid on him in doing what the ignorant worshippers of the calf did: Lord, forgive me, asking forgiveness for what he did to his brother, and my brother for anything past between him and God; cover our sins with a veil from You; "and admit us into Your mercy" means have mercy on Your believing servants with Your wide mercy, for You are more merciful to Your servants than anyone who shows mercy to anything. The editor refers to the explanation of "forgiveness" and notes that the printed text drops a word the manuscript has. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 133}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
