## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they hasten you with punishment, and if it were not for a specified delay, the punishment would have come to them suddenly, not realizing it": And those among your people are hastening you, Muhammad: If a verse from his Lord had not been revealed to him concerning punishment, they say: "O Allah, if this is the truth from You, then rain upon us stones from the sky." And if He had not postponed His name to them, He would not have destroyed them until they complete it and reach it, the punishment would have come to them quickly. And His saying: "And let them come to them suddenly while they do not perceive," it means: And the punishment will come upon them suddenly while they do not perceive its coming time before it comes.
