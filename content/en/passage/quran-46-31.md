## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "O you who establish us, respond to the call of Allah and believe in him; He will forgive you of your sins and save you from a painful punishment": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of these people from the jinn: "O our people, respond to the call of Allah." They said: Respond to the Messenger of Allah Muhammad to what he calls you to of obedience to Allah. "And believe in him," meaning: "And be truthful in what He has brought to you and his people regarding the command and prohibition of Allah, and other things that have called you to believe in Him." He says: Your Lord will cover your sins for you and will not expose you with them in the Hereafter by punishing you for them. "And He will protect you from a painful punishment," meaning: He will save you from a painful punishment if you repent from your sins, and turn from your disbelief to faith in God and His call.
