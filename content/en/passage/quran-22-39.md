## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Permission is given to those who fight because they have been wronged, and indeed Allah is capable of their victory": Allah has permitted the believers who fight the polytheists in His cause that the polytheists wronged them by fighting them. Reciters differed in their reading; the majority of the reciters of Medina read it as: "idhan" with a damma on the alif, "yuqatilun" with a fatha on the tā by omitting the active participle in "udhān" and "yāqilān" all. Some Kufans and the general reciters of Basra read this: 'adhaan' to omit the active participle, and 'yuqatilun' with a kasra on the ta, meaning those authorized to fight the polytheists. The majority of Kufan reciters and some Meccans read this: 'idhan' with a fatha on the alif, meaning: 'Allah permitted' with a kasra on the ta, meaning: 'Yaqatilun' with a kasra on the ta, meaning: those whom Allah has authorized to fight fight the polytheists. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
