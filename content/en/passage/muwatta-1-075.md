## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, in the chapter on wiping the head and the ears: his father Urwa ibn al-Zubayr would take off his turban and wipe his head with water. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan, Ma'n ibn Isa and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.74}
