**Commentary on verse 69: "Those who believe, the Jews, the Sabians and the Christians", pp. 476-477**

Al-Tabari says: those who believed God and His messenger, the people of Islam, the Jews, the Sabians whose case he had already set out, and the Christians, whoever among them believed in God and the Last Day, accepting the resurrection after death, and did good works for the return, have no fear of the terrors of the resurrection they are coming to, nor will they grieve over what they left behind of this world after seeing the honour God gives them in reward. He refers back to his earlier account of the grammar of the verse and holds that it need not be repeated.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 476}
