## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the words of Iblis: "Except for Your sincere servants among them," meaning except those whom God has sincerely guided by His guidance, and that is from those over whom Iblis has no authority or power — according to Al-Dahhak, the sincere are the believers, and from Qatadah that this is God's praise. Al-Tabari mentions the recitation of "the sincere" with a fatha on the kha for those who are sincere in obedience, so there is no way for Iblis to do so.
