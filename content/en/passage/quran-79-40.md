## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 41 as a single section. He explains His saying, “But as for him who feared standing before his Lord and restrained himself from [following] his desires,” meaning: But as for him who feared God’s questioning of him when he stood before Him on the Day of Resurrection, so he feared Him by performing His obligations and avoiding His sins, “and restrained himself from [following] his desires,” he says: And he restrained himself from his desires in what God dislikes and does not approve of from him, so he rebuked it for that and went against its desires to what his Lord commanded him to do. And in the section he quotes sayings of Mujahid.
