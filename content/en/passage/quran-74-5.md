## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 11 as a single section. He explains the phrase “O you who are wrapped up”: God Almighty says, “O you who are wrapped up,” meaning, O you who are wrapped up in your clothes when you sleep. It is mentioned that this was said to the Prophet of God while he was wrapped in a velvet blanket. In this section, he quotes sayings attributed to Abraham.
