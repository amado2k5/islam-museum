## Summary
A saying of Malik closing the question: no one who owes two consecutive months of fasting by the Book of God may break his fast except because of an illness or menstruation; and he may not travel and break his fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (815). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.405}
