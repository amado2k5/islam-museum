## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "then he showed him the greatest sign": Moses showed Pharaoh the greatest sign, meaning the greatest proof that he was a messenger of God sent to him. That sign was Moses' hand, which he brought forth white for all to see, and his staff, which transformed into a manifest serpent. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
