## Summary
A report of Abu Shurayh al-Adawi in the chapter on the sanctity of Mecca, in the Book of Pilgrimage of Sahih Muslim. It is the sermon of the conquest of Mecca on its sanctity, its blood and its trees, and that the permission granted to the Prophet lasted one hour of a day before the sanctity returned. {cite:sahih-muslim}
