## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "The people of Noah, 'Aad, and Pharaoh with the stakes lied before them": I lied before these polytheists from Quraysh, who said: 'I make the gods one god, their messengers, the people of Noah, 'Aad, and Pharaoh with the stakes.' Scholars differ on the reason why Pharaoh was called 'the one with stakes.' Some said: It was said to him because he had playgrounds made of stakes on which he played for himself. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, and Al-Rabi'.
