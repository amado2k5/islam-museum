## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 48 to 49 together in one place. Al-Tabari mentions that God is the one who sent the winds as glad tidings before His mercy, sent down pure water from the clouds to revive barren land that does not grow, and to water the many creatures and people He created. He explains that 'Anasi' is the plural of 'man' and explains the use of 'dead' because it meant a place.
