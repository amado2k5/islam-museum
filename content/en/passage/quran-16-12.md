## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He subjected to you the night and the day, and the sun and the moon and the stars, subjected to His command. Indeed in that are signs for a people who reason." And from His favor upon you, O people, along with what He mentioned before He subjected the night and the day to you, they take turns over you. This is to manage your livelihood and this is to dwell therein, "and the sun and the moon," to know the times of your times, your months, your years, and the righteousness of your lives. "And the stars are subjected to you" — by Allah's command, they run in their orbit so that you may be guided by them in the darkness of land and sea. "Indeed in that are signs for a people who reason"—Allah says: Indeed, in Allah's subjugation of that over what He has subjected to clear indications for a people who understand Allah's proofs and understand His warning to them.
