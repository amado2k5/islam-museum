## Summary
It reached Malik from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubaydallah, that the Messenger of God said of the martyrs of Uhud: I bear witness over these. Abu Bakr al-Siddiq said: are we not, Messenger of God, their brothers? We became Muslim as they did, and we strove as they strove. The Messenger of God said: yes, but I do not know what you will do after me. Abu Bakr wept, then wept again, and said: shall we be living after you? The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said it is relayed in all Muwatta transmitters, and that its meaning is supported by many sound routes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.594}
