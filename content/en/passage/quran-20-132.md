## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And enjoin your family to pray and be steadfast in it; We do not ask you for provision; We provide for you, and the outcome is for piety": "And command," O Muhammad, "to pray and be steadfast in it," meaning: and be patient in performing it and performing it within its limits. "We do not ask you for provision," meaning: We do not ask you for money, but rather burden you with physical work, and we give you a great reward and great recompense. "We provide for you," it means: We give you wealth and earn it, and we do not ask Him. And His saying: "And the outcome is for piety," it means: The righteous outcome is from the deeds of every doer who is pious and fears God, not one who fears punishment for him and does not hope for reward. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Zayd ibn Aslam.
