## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 2 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "al-mām": Abu Ja'far said: We have explained the meaning of Allah's saying, the Almighty, mentioned "lam" (Al-Mam), and we have mentioned the opinions of the interpreters regarding its interpretation, which is more correct than their statements according to us, with its previous evidences, which is sufficient to repeat it here. As for His saying: "Do the people think that they will be left to say, 'We believe,' and they will not be tested," its meaning: 'Do you think those who have gone out, O Muhammad, from your companions will be harmed by the polytheists,' that We will leave them without testing or testing, by saying: We believe in you, O Muhammad, so we have believed in you in what you brought us from Allah.
