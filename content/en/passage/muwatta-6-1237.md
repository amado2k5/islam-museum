## Summary
Malik says that a woman who menstruates at Mina must stay until she circumambulates the House, which is necessary for her; and if she has departed and menstruates after departure, let her return to her land, for it reached him that there is a permission from the Messenger of God for menstruating women. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.552}
