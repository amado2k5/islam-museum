## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 15 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We remove the punishment a little; indeed, you will return": Allah Almighty says to those polytheists about whom He informed that they seek help from the descending smoke and the torment that befalls them from exertion. He informed them that they promise Him that if the punishment is removed from them, they will believe, indeed We will remove the punishment: meaning the harm that befalls them with the fertility We are telling them: "A little, indeed you will return." He means: O polytheists, if He removed from you what harm befell you, you would not fulfill what you promised and promised your Lord of faith, but you will return to your misguidance and your error, and you were not before it was revealed to you. Qatadah used to say: Its meaning is: You will return in the punishment of Allah. From Ma'mar from him. In the Maqta, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
