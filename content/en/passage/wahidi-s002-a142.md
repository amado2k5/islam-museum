## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Baqara 2:142, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «سيقول السفهاء من الناس».

## Full report (Arabic source text)

الله- يحب أن يتوجه نحو الكعبة، فأنزل الله:
