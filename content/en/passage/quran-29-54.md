## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They hasten you with punishment, and indeed, Hell is encompassing the disbelievers": These polytheists hasten you, O Muhammad, with the coming and descending of punishment, and the Fire surrounds them, leaving them only to enter. It is said: That is the sea. In the passage, he quotes from Samak.
