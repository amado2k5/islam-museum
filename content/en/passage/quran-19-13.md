## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Yahya, take the Book with strength, and We gave him wisdom as a boy": Zakariya was born to Yahya, and when he was born, God said to him: O John, take this Book firmly, meaning the Book of God that He revealed to Moses, which is the Torah. Strongly, he says: seriously. As Ma'mar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "Take the Book with strength," he said: seriously. And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, "Take the Book with strength," he said: seriously. He said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. Ibn Zayd said about this: Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "O Yahya, take the Book firmly," he said: Strength means to do what Allah commanded and avoid what Allah forbidden. Abu Ja'far said: I have explained the meaning of this with its evidences in previous parts of our book, in Surah Al-Imran, so that makes it necessary to repeat it here. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and others.
