## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "This brother of mine has ninety-nine ewes, and I have one." He said, 'Sponsor her and honor me in the speech.' This is like the enemy who struck David in his sanctuary, because David had ninety-nine wives, and the man who invaded him was the one who killed him. When he was killed, he married his wife, as David mentioned. One of them said to him: 'This is my brother.' He says: My brother is on my religion, as from Ibn Ishaq, from some scholars, from Wahb ibn Mabnah: "This is my brother": meaning according to my religion, "He has ninety-nine ewes and I have one ewe." In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Ibn Ishaq.
