## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We did not send to a town any warner except that its wealthy said, 'Indeed, we are disbelievers in what you have sent for.'" Meaning: And We did not send to the people of a town a warner warning them of Our severity that it would descend upon them for their disobedience to Us, except that its leaders and leaders in misguidance said, as the people of Pharaoh among the polytheists said to him: 'Indeed, by what you have been sent as a warning and with which you have been sent of the oneness of Allah and disavowal from the gods and their rivals, we are disbelievers.' In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
