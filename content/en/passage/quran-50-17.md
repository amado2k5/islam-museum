## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 18 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "when the recipients receive a 'qa'id' on the right and on the left": 'We are closer to a person than the vein in his throat, when he receives the two angels, which are the receivers, 'on the right and on the left, Qa'id.' It is also said that by 'qa'id' he meant 'observation.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
