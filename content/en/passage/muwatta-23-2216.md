## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm, from his father, that Amr ibn Sulaym al-Zurqi told him that it was said to Umar ibn al-Khattab: there is a young Ghassani boy here, a youth who has not reached puberty, whose heir is in Syria, and who has property, and has here only a female paternal cousin. Umar said: let him bequeath to her. He bequeathed to her property called Bir Jushm. Amr ibn Sulaym said: that property was sold for thirty thousand dirhams, and his paternal cousin to whom he bequeathed it was the mother of Amr ibn Sulaym al-Zurqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.310}
