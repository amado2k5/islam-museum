## Summary
Malik says that the binding of an oath is a man swearing that he will not sell his garment for ten dinars and then selling it for that, or swearing that he will beat his slave and not beating him, and the like. Such a person's oath is expiated, and there is no expiation for an idle oath. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.612}
