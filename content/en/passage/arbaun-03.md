## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Umar on the pillars of Islam, the third in the Forty Hadith and in Ibn Rajab's commentary. Islam is built on five pillars as a building on its supports, and faith in God and His Messenger is included within Islam. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
