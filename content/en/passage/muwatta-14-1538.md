## Summary
Malik transmits that Said ibn al-Musayyab and Sulayman ibn Yasar were asked about a man who gave his slave his slave-woman in marriage; the slave divorced her irrevocably, then her master gave her to him. Could he have her by ownership? They said no, not until she marries another. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.44}
