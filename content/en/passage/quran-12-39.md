**Commentary on verse 39 (pp. 104-105)**

It is said that Joseph spoke these words to the two youths who entered the prison with him because one of them was an idolater, and so he called him by them to Islam and to leaving the worship of gods and idols. "Companions of the prison" means those in the prison; he called them his companions because they were in it, as God names the people of Paradise and of the Fire its companions because they are in them. On "are many lords better, or God the One, the Overpowering" he says: is worshipping many scattered lords and gods who neither help nor harm better, or worshipping the one object of worship who has no second in power and authority, who overpowered everything and subdued and subjected it so that it obeys willingly or unwillingly? The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "companion" and "the Overpowering". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 104}

Report 19289, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: when God's prophet Joseph knew that one of them would be killed, he called them to their share from their Lord and their portion of their hereafter. Reports 19290 and 19291, from Mujahid: Joseph says it, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl, Ishaq, Abd Allah ibn Abi Ja'far, Waraqa and Ibn Abi Najih. Report 19292, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: then he called them to God and to Islam, saying, is it better to worship one God or scattered gods that avail you nothing? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 105}
