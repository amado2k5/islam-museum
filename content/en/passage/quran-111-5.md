## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 9 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "May the hand of Abu Lahab be blessed," meaning: the hand of Abu Lahab was lost, and he himself lost. What he meant by his saying: "May the hand of Abu Lahab be blessed" means his deeds will be blessed. Some Arabic scholars used to say: His saying: "May the hand of Abu Lahab be blessed" is a prayer from God upon him. As for his saying: "And repent," it is a predicament. It is mentioned that this is in Abdullah's recitation: "May the hand of Abu Lahab be repented and he has repented." The entry of 'Qad' indicates that it is a predicate, and this is illustrated by the saying to another: 'God destroyed you,' 'He destroyed you,' 'made you righteous,' and 'He made you righteous.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
