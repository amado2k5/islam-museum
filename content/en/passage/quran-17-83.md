## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when We bestow favor upon man, he turns away and distances himself from his side; and when evil touches him, he despairs": And when We bestow favor upon man, We save him from the Lord of what he is in at sea, which He has brought upon him from destruction by the wind blowing him to land, and other of Our blessings, he turns away from mentioning us, having been a helper to us and excluding everyone except us in the time of hardship he was in, "and he withdrew from his side," meaning: And 'He was far from us beside him,' meaning by himself, as if he had not called us to harm before his harm, as in Mujahid, regarding the phrase: 'and he distanced himself from him,' he said: He distanced himself from us. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. The recitation is done by making the hamza in 'na'a' before the alif, which is the classical language, and it is used to read. In the maqta, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
