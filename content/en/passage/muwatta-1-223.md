## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu Nu'aym Wahb ibn Kaysan that he heard Jabir ibn Abdullah say, in the chapter on the Mother of the Qur'an: whoever prays a rak'a without reciting the Opening chapter has not prayed, unless he is behind the imam. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Isma'il ibn Musa al-Fazari, Suwayd, Ibn Wahb and Qutayba transmitted it in al-Tirmidhi (313), and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it as a stopped report. Yahya ibn Salam al-Basri, who is weak, transmitted it raised from Malik in al-Tahawi, which is not sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.135} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.136}
