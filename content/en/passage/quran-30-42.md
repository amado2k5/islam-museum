## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Travel through the land and see how was the end of those before you; most of them were polytheists.": Say, O Muhammad, to those who associate partners with Allah among your people: Travel through the lands, and look at the dwellings of those who disbelieved in Allah before you and denied His messengers. How was their final affair, and what was the outcome of their denial and disbelief? Did We not destroy them with a punishment from Us and make them an example for those who came after them? "Most of them were polytheists," he says: We did that to them because most of them were polytheists like them.
