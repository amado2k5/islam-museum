## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 42 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "So I swear by what you see" as follows: No, the matter is not as you, the deniers of God's Book and His Messengers, claim. I swear by all things, both those you see and those you do not see. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
