## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 10 as a single section. He interprets the words, “And remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion”: “And remember,” O Muhammad, “the name of your Lord,” so call upon Him by it, “and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion.” He says: devote yourself entirely to Him for your needs and worship, to the exclusion of all else. This is derived from the expression, “I devoted myself to this matter,” and from this comes the name given to the mother of Jesus, son of Mary, “al-Batul” (the Virgin), because of her complete devotion to God. It is also said of a worshipper who has turned away from the world and its causes to worship God: “He has devoted himself.” From this comes the hadith narrated from the Prophet, “that he forbade devotion.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
