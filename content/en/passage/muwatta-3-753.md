## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, who said: a letter came from Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz to my father while he was at Mina, that he should not take charity on honey or on horses. The editor notes that Abu Musab, al-Shaybani and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.373}
