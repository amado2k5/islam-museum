## Summary
In the Book of the Obligation of the Fifth: the account of al-Abbas and Ali asking Umar about the Prophet's inheritance from the lands of Banu al-Nadir, Khaybar and Fadak. It contains the rule "we do not inherit; what we leave is charity", and Umar's decision on the three estates of the Prophet and how they were to be spent. It is one of the best-known texts on the dispute between al-Abbas and Ali, and then between them and Abu Bakr and Umar. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
