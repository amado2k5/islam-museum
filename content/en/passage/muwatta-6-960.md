## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: people of Mecca and others call out for the pilgrimage only when they are there; and whoever is a resident in Mecca, not of its people, from the heart of Mecca, does not leave the Sacred Area. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1085) and Suwayd (500) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.456}
