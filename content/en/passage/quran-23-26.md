## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 27 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'My Lord, help me by what they used to deny'": Noah called upon his Lord, seeking help from him against his people, when his matter and their command prolonged and they persisted in their misguidance: "My Lord, help me over my people because of what they used to deny," meaning that they denied Me, in what You conveyed to them of Your message and their call to Him of Your oneness. And His saying: "So We revealed to him that He made the ark with Our eyes and Our revelation," he says: So we said to him when we sought help against the disbelievers of his people: Make the ark, which is the ship "with our eyes," meaning in sight and sight, and "and We revealed" means: by teaching you that I made it. "When Our command comes," he means: when Our decree comes upon your people, with their punishment and destruction, "and the furrow of the oven." We have previously mentioned the differences among those who differ regarding the description of the furnace, but the correct view is to say it with its evidence, which is sufficient to repeat it here. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
