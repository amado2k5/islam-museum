## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "Moses came to you with clear proofs, then you took the calf after him while you were wrongdoers", al-Tabari begins by saying that the clear proofs are the signs Moses brought pointing to his truthfulness and prophethood, such as the staff that turned into a plain serpent. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 354}

Al-Tabari goes on that among the clear proofs were Moses's hand, which he brought out white for the beholders, the splitting of the sea and its floor becoming a dry road, and the locusts, lice and frogs; God called them clear proofs because they show those who look that they are miracles no human can bring without God's making it so, "bayyinat" being the plural of "bayyina" as "tayyibat" is of "tayyiba". In "then you took the calf after him" the pronoun refers to Moses, that is, after he left them for his Lord's appointment, though it may refer to the coming, "after the coming of the proofs". "While you were wrongdoers" means that they worshipped the calf, which was not theirs to do, since worship is not fitting for any but God. He reads this as a rebuke: if they took the calf for a god after knowing what their Lord did through Moses, which neither Pharaoh nor his host could do, they are the quicker to deny Muhammad and reject the description in their books, given how far they are from the time of Moses. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 354} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 355} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 356}
