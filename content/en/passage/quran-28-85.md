## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, He who has ordained the Qur'an for you will return you to a return. Say, 'My Lord knows best who comes with guidance and who is in clear error.'" He said: Indeed, what was revealed to you, O Muhammad, is the Qur'an. As he said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase "Indeed, He who made the Qur'an obligatory upon you," he said: He who gave you the Qur'an. And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding Allah's saying, 'Indeed, He who ordained the Qur'an upon you' said: He who gave it to you. Interpreters differed in the interpretation of His saying: 'He will return you to the Hereafter.' Some said: Its meaning is: your destiny to Paradise. In the passage, they quote from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Hasan, Ikrimah, Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and others.
