## Summary
A saying of Malik about Umar's words: for the last rite is the circumambulation of the House: that is in our view, God knows best, because of God's words: whoever honours the rites of God, it is from the piety of hearts (Al-Hajj 32), and His words: then their release is at the Ancient House (Al-Hajj 33). So the release from all the rites and their end is at the Ancient House. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1443) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.496}
