## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We certainly sent to every nation a messenger, saying, 'Worship Allah and avoid Taghut. Among them is he who guides Allah, and among them is he upon whom error is justified. So travel through the land and see how was the end of the deniers.'" And We have sent, O people, in every nation before you a messenger just as We sent among you, 'Worship Allah alone without partner, dedicate obedience to Him, and dedicate worship to Him.' 'And avoid Taghut' (Taghut), meaning: And stay away from Satan, and beware that he misleads you and prevents you from the way of Allah, lest you go astray. "Among them are those who are guided by Allah," meaning: among those who were granted pardon to believe His messengers, accept from them, believe in Allah, and act upon His obedience, so they succeeded, succeeded, and were saved from Allah's punishment. "And among them is the one upon whom error is bound." He means: Among those who deliberately deliberately deviated from the path, disbelieved in Allah, denied His messengers, and followed Taghut. So Allah destroyed them with His punishment and sent down upon them His might, which cannot be averted from the criminal people.
