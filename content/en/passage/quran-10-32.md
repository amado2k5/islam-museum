**Commentary on verse 32 (p. 84)**

On [[passage/quran-10-32|10:32]] al-Tabari says: the one who does these things, provides for you from the heaven and the earth, owns hearing and sight, brings the living out of the dead and directs the affair, is God your Lord, the Truth, without doubt. "What is there after the truth but error?": what is there besides the truth but error, which is deviation from the right path? If the truth is this, then claiming another as god and lord is error and straying from the truth. "How then are you turned away?": by what route are you turned from guidance and truth, taking another course, while you admit that what you are turned away from is the truth? The editor refers to the earlier glosses on "error" and "turning away". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 84}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
