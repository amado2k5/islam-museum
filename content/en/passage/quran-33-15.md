## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they had made a covenant with Allah before, not turning their backs, and the covenant of Allah was a question": Those who sought the permission of the Messenger of Allah to turn away from him, saying, 'Our houses are shameful,' had made a covenant with Allah beforehand, that they would not turn their backs on their enemy. Their words in the Mashhad of the Messenger of Allah were with them, and they did not fulfill their covenant. 'And the covenant of Allah is accountable,' meaning: Allah will ask whoever gave it to him of himself. It is mentioned that this was revealed to Banu Haritha because of their actions in the trench after the one among them was with Uhud. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
