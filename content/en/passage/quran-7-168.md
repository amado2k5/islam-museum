**Commentary on verse 168 (pp. 208-209)**

Al-Tabari says of "and We cut them up in the earth into nations" that God scattered the Children of Israel in the earth as various separate groups. Report 15311, from Ibn Abbas: in every land that a group of Jews enters; report 15312, from Mujahid: the Jews. He refers back to the earlier explanations of "cutting up" and "nation", and the editor notes that Ishaq ibn Ismail in the chain is Abu Yazid Hubuwayh. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 208}

On "some of them are righteous", he says that among these people whom God described from the Children of Israel there are righteous ones, who believe in God and His messengers, "and some are less than that", that is, less than righteous. He says God described them so before their apostasy and disbelief in their Lord, before Jesus son of Mary was sent to them. "We tried them with good things and bad, that perhaps they would return" means He tried them with ease of living, comfort and wide provision, which are the "good things", and with hardship, narrow means and calamities in their wealth, which are the "bad things", so that they might return to obedience to their Lord, turn to it and repent of their disobedience. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 208} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 209}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
