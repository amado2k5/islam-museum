## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and who turns away from the religion of Abraham" al-Tabari says: which of the people holds the religion of Abraham in disregard and leaves it, turning away from it to something else? He says God meant the Jews and the Christians, for choosing Judaism and Christianity over Islam, because the religion of Abraham is the upright, submitting faith (hanifiyya), as in "Abraham was neither a Jew nor a Christian, but was upright, submitting". From Qatada and al-Rabi': the Jews and Christians turned away from his religion, devised Judaism and Christianity, which are not from God, and left the religion of Abraham, which is Islam. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 89}

On "except he who has made himself foolish" he says folly is ignorance, so the sense is that none turns away from the religion of Abraham except a fool ignorant of where his own share lies in what helps or harms it in its return; from Ibn Zayd, only one who missed his share. On the accusative of "nafsahu" he says it is as a specifier, because folly belongs by origin to the self and when carried over to "man" the self is put in the accusative, as in "he is the widest of you in house". He mentions the view of some Basra grammarians that "safiha" is treated like "safih" and made transitive to "nafsahu". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 90}

On "and We chose him in this world" he says Abraham is meant, and that choosing is selecting and picking him for friendship and making him in this world an example to follow for those after him. He sees in the verse a report that whoever opposes Abraham in what he established for those after him opposes God, and a notice that whoever opposes what Muhammad brought opposes Abraham, because God reported that He chose him for His friendship and made him a leader for the people and that his religion was the upright, submitting one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 91}

On "and in the Hereafter he will be among the righteous" he says Abraham in the next world is among the righteous, the righteous among the children of Adam being the one who fulfils the rights of God upon him; so God reported of His friend that in this world he is chosen and in the next a friend, and that he comes to the watering places of His friends who keep His covenant. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 91} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 92}
