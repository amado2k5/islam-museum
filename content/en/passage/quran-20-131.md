## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not let your eyes strain upon that by which We have granted enjoyment to spouses from among themselves, the flower of the life of this world, that We may test them therein. And the provision of your Lord is better and more lasting." He means: We have made for those who turn away from the signs and forms of their Lord a pleasure in their worldly life, which they enjoy, from the flower of the immediate and radiant world "to test them thereby." He means: to test them by what We have granted them enjoyment and to test them, for that is fleeting, and deceit and deceiving. "And the provision of your Lord, which He promised you in the Hereafter until you are satisfied, and it is His reward to Him." Better for you than what we have given them of the flower of this worldly life. "And he remained" means 'and lasted,' because it is not interrupted or penetrated. He mentioned that this verse was revealed to the Messenger of Allah because the Messenger sent a Jew to borrow food from him, but he refused to lend it except as a proof. In the Maqta, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
