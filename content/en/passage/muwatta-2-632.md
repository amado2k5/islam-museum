## Summary
Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, from his father, from Abu al-Nadr al-Sulami, that the Prophet said that no Muslim loses three children and seeks reward for them without them being a shield for him against the fire. A woman with him said: or two? He said: or two. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Qanabi transmitted it from Malik, and that its chain is weak because Abu al-Nadr al-Sulami is unknown among the Companions and the Successors and is known only through this report, though the text is sound from another route, citing al-Tirmidhi (1059). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.322}
