## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 13 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “So remind, if the reminder benefits” as follows: “Remind the servants of God, O Muhammad, of His greatness, admonish them, and warn them of His punishment. ‘If the reminder benefits’ means: If the reminder benefits those whose faith you have despaired of, then the reminder will not benefit them.” He explains that “So remind” is a command from God to His Prophet to remind all people, and then adds: “If the reminder benefits those whose faith you have despaired of.” He then quotes statements attributed to Qatadah in this section.
