## Summary
Malik transmits from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman, from Abu Said al-Khudri, that he came back from a journey; his family brought him meat; he said: check whether this is from the meat of the sacrifices; they said: it is from it. Abu Said said: did the Messenger of God not forbid it? They said: there was an order after you from the Messenger of God; Abu Said went out to ask, and was told that the Messenger of God said: I forbade you the meat of the sacrifices after three days; eat, give in charity and store; I forbade you the drink of nabidh, so drink; every intoxicant is forbidden; I forbade you visiting graves, so visit them, and do not say evil. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said Rabia did not hear from Abu Said, and that the person Abu Said asked was Qatada ibn al-Numan, his maternal half-brother. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.623}
