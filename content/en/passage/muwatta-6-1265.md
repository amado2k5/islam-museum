## Summary
Malik says about one who forgets or is ignorant and does not fast the three days of the pilgrimage, or falls ill during them and does not fast until he returns to his land: he offers if he finds an offering; otherwise he fasts three days with his family, and seven after that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.561}
