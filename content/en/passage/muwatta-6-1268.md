## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibrahim ibn Uqba, from Kurayb, client of Ibn Abbas, that the Messenger of God passed a woman in her litter; she was told: this is the Messenger of God; she took hold of the arm of a child she had with her, and said: does this one have a pilgrimage? He said: yes, and you have a reward. The editor notes that the hadith is relayed in the Muwatta according to most of its transmitters, and that a group of transmitters connected it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.563}
