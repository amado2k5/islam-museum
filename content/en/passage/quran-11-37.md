**Commentary on verse 37 (pp. 308-309)**

On [[passage/quran-11-37|11:37]] al-Tabari says: He inspired him that none of his people will believe but those who have, and 'make the ark', which is the ship. Report 18127, from Mujahid: the ark is the ship. 'Under Our eyes' means under God's eye and His inspiration as He orders you; report 18127m, from Ibn Abbas: he did not know how to build the ark, so God inspired him to build it like the breast of a bird; reports 18128, 18129 and 18130, from Mujahid: 'Our inspiration' means as We order you; report 18130, from Ibn Abbas: by God's eye; report 18131, from Qatada: by God's eye and His inspiration. 'And do not address Me concerning those who did wrong, they will be drowned' means do not ask Me for pardon for these who wronged themselves of your people, earning for themselves by their disbelief in God destruction by drowning; they will be drowned by the flood. Report 18132, from Ibn Jurayj: do not answer Me back; he said it had been ordered earlier that he not intercede for them with Him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 309}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
