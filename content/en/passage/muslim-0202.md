## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As in the chapter on the Prophet's prayer for his community, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It is the intercession prayer after the recitation of Abraham's words and Jesus's words, and the promise of satisfaction for the community. {cite:sahih-muslim}
