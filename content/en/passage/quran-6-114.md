## Summary
Verse 114 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 114 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "shall I seek other than God as a judge, when it is He who sent down to you the Book explained in detail?", al-Tabari says God speaks to His prophet Muhammad: say to these who set up idols and images as equals to God, who say to you "refrain from our gods and we will refrain from your God": God has already ruled against me concerning the mention of your gods in a way that restrains from worshipping them; "shall I seek other than God as judge?", that is, it is not for me to overstep His ruling or go beyond it, since there is no judge more just than He among you and no speaker more truthful (the editor refers to the commentary on "hukm"); "it is He who sent down to you the Book", meaning the Qur'an, "explained in detail", that is, with the ruling made clear in it on what you dispute about my affair and yours (he has already explained the meaning of "tafsil"). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 60}

On "and those to whom We gave the Book know that it is sent down from your Lord with the truth, so do not be among the doubters", he says: if these who set up idols as equals to God among your people deny the oneness of God, associate rivals with Him, reject what I sent down to you and deny that it is true and call it false, then those to whom We gave the Book, the Torah and the Gospel, among the Children of Israel, know that it is sent down from your Lord, meaning the Qur'an and what is in it, "with the truth", that is, as a decisive separation between the people of truth and falsehood, showing the truthfulness of the one who is truthful about God and the lying of the liar who fabricates against Him; "so do not be among the doubters" means: Muhammad, do not be among those who doubt the reality of the reports that came to you from God in this Book and of other things it contains, because those to whom We gave the Book know that it is sent down from your Lord with the truth. He has shown earlier the sense of this (the footnotes refer to the earlier commentary on "imtira'"). Report 13788, from al-Rabi': do not be in doubt about what We related to you. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 61}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
