## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He created the heavens and the earth in truth. He envelops the night over the day and wraps the day over the night, and subjects the sun and the moon, each running for a specified term. Indeed, He is the Exalted in Might, the Forgiving": Allah Almighty says, describing Himself as: "He created the heavens and the earth with truth. He envelops the night over the day and envelops the day over the night." He means: this one overshadows this, and that one overshadows that, as He said: "The night enters the day and the day enters the night." It is also transmitted in Al-Maqta' is statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
