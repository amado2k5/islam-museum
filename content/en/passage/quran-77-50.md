## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Then in what statement after this will they believe?” is explained as follows: What statement after this Quran will you, O people, believe in, despite the clarity of its proof and the soundness of its evidence, that it is the truth from God? He says: What will you accept? Rather, He, may He be exalted, informed them that if they did not believe in these accounts He had informed them of in this Quran, despite the soundness of its proofs, they would not be able to accept the truth of any accounts about which they had not seen or witnessed the person being reported. And if they believed in something that was hidden from them because of evidence supporting it, then they would be obligated to do the same regarding the accounts in this Quran. And God knows best.
