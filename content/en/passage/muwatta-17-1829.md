## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that the Messenger forbade muzabana and muhaqala. Muzabana is buying fruit for dates; muhaqala is buying a crop for wheat, and renting land for wheat. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.149}
