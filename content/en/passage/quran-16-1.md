## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "The command of Allah has come, so do not hasten it, glory be to Him, exalted is He above what they associate with Him": The command of Allah has come, so bring near to you, O people, a desire, so do not hasten its occurrence. Then the interpreters differed about the matter that Allah has made His servants know His coming and bringing Him closer to them, what it is, and what it is? Some said: It is His obligations and rulings. In the passage, he quotes from Al-Dahhak and Ibn Jurayj.
