## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 13–14. Al-Tabari mentions that God commands His prophet to set for the polytheists of His people the people of the village, and it is said to be Antioch, as the messengers came there. He mentions the difference among scholars regarding these messengers: some said they were messengers of Jesus, son of Mary, but they denied them, so God strengthened them with a third.
