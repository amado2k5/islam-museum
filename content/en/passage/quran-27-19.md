## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So he smiled laughing at her words and said, 'My Lord, grant me to be grateful for Your favor which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do righteous deeds that please Him, and admit me by Your mercy among Your righteous servants.'" Solomon smiled laughing, laughing at the ant's words that said what she said, and said: "My Lord, grant me to thank Your favor which You have bestowed upon me," meaning by "grant me success," He inspired me. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
