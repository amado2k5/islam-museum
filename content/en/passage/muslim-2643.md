## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Masud in the chapter on how the human being is created in the mother's womb, in the Book of Destiny of Sahih Muslim. It describes the stages of the foetus and the writing of provision, term, deeds, wretchedness and happiness. It is a comprehensive text on creation and destiny. {cite:sahih-muslim}
