## Summary
Verse 82 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/hypocrites-discourage-marching-in-the-heat|Hypocrites Discourage Marching in the Heat]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 82 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 82 (pp. 401-403)**

Al-Tabari says: these who were held back rejoiced in their staying behind, opposing the Messenger of God; so let them laugh, rejoicing, a little in this passing world over their staying behind against the Messenger of God and their distraction from obedience to their Lord, for they will weep long in Hell in place of their little laughter in this world; "recompense" means reward from Us for their disobedience, in leaving the going out when summoned to their enemy and sitting in their homes against the Messenger of God; "for what they used to earn" means for the sins they committed. The editor refers to earlier explanations of recompense and earning. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 401}

Report 17037, from Abu Razin, through Abu al-Sa'ib, Abu Mu'awiya and Isma'il: God, blessed and exalted, says the world is little, so let them laugh in it as they please, and when they come to the hereafter they will weep without end, and that is the "much". Report 17038, from al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym, through Abu Kurayb, Ibn Yaman, Mansur and Abu Razin: "let them laugh a little", he said, in this world, "and weep much", he said, in the hereafter; report 17039, from Abu Razin, through Ibn Bashshar, Abd al-Rahman, Yahya, Sufyan and Isma'il ibn Sumay': in the hereafter. Report 17040, from Abu Razin, through Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna, Muhammad ibn Ja'far, Shu'ba and Mansur: let them laugh a little in this world and weep much in the Fire; and he said of the verse in al-Ahzab "and then you would be allowed to enjoy only a little", [[passage/quran-33-16|33:16]], that it meant until their terms; one of these two reports he raised to al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym. The editor notes that this part will come with its chain in the commentary on the verse of al-Ahzab and that the correct form of the name is Khuthaym. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 402}

Report 17041, from al-Hasan, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: let them laugh in this world, and weep much in the hereafter, in the Fire of Hell. Report 17042, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "let them laugh a little", that is, in this world, "and weep much", that is, in the Fire; it was mentioned to us that the Prophet of God said "by Him in whose hand is my soul, if you knew what I know you would laugh little and weep much", and it was mentioned to us that a call came at that point, or it was said to him, "do not make My servants despair". Reports 17043 and 17044, from Abu Razin and al-Rabi' ibn Khuthaym, say the same as above. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 402}

Report 17045, from Ibn Abbas, through Ali ibn Dawud, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: they are the hypocrites and unbelievers who took their religion as mockery and play; God, blessed and exalted, says "let them laugh a little" in this world "and weep much" in the Fire. Report 17046, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: "let them laugh" in this world "a little" and "weep" on the Day of Resurrection "much"; and he said "those who committed crimes used to laugh at those who believed", up to "have the unbelievers been repaid for what they used to do", which is [[passage/quran-83-29|83:29-36]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 403}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
