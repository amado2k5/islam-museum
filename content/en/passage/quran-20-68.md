## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 69 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "We said, 'Do not fear; indeed You are the Most High.'" We said to Moses, when he felt a fear within himself: "Do not fear, for You are the Superior" for these magicians, Pharaoh and his army, and the conqueror against them, "And cast down what is in your right hand to catch what they have done," meaning: "And cast down your staff, and it will swallow their ropes and staffs that they bewitched until it seemed to you that they were striving." And His statement: "They only made the plot of a magician," the readers differed in their interpretation of his statement. The general reciters of Medina and Basra, and some reciters of Kufa, read it as "They only made the plot of a magician" with the nominative 'kayd' and with the alif in 'sahir' meaning: what these magicians made was a plot by a magician. The general recitation of Kufa read: "They only made a magical plot" by raising the scheming and without the alif in magic, meaning that what they did was a magical scheme.
