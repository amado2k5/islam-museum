## Summary
Verse 99 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/year-of-delegations|Year of Delegations]]. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 99 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 99 (pp. 432-434)**

Al-Tabari says: among the Bedouin there is one who believes God and acknowledges His oneness, the resurrection after death and reward and punishment, and who intends what he spends in striving against the polytheists and in his night journeys with the Messenger of God as "means of nearness with God", the means of nearness being what brings him near to God's pleasure and love; "and the prayers of the Messenger" means he seeks by what he spends, along with seeking nearness to God, the supplication of the Messenger and his asking forgiveness for him. We have already shown that one of the senses of prayer is supplication, which spares repeating it here. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 432}

Report 17095, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: "the prayers of the Messenger", meaning the asking of forgiveness by the Prophet. Report 17096, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "the prayers of the Messenger", he said the supplication of the Messenger; this is God's exception from the Bedouin. The editor explains the word for exception as what is excepted from something, and cites the words of Ka'b al-Ahbar that the martyrs are God's exception on the earth. Report 17097, from Mujahid, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "among the Bedouin are those who believe in God and the Last Day", he said they are Banu Muqarrin of Muzayna, about whom God said "nor those who, when they came to you for mounts", [[passage/quran-9-92|9:92]]; and Ibn Jurayj said, His words "the Bedouin are firmer in unbelief and hypocrisy", then He made an exception and said "among the Bedouin are those who believe in God and the Last Day", the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 433}

Report 17098, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Ma'qil, through Ahmad, Abu Ahmad, Ja'far and al-Bukhtari ibn al-Mukhtar al-Abdi: we were ten sons of Muqarrin, and there came down about us "among the Bedouin are those who believe in God and the Last Day", to the end of the verse. The editor notes that al-Bukhtari is reliable and that Abd al-Rahman ibn Ma'qil al-Muzani is a reliable Follower whom some counted among the Companions on the strength of this report, and that Ibn Hajar said he meant by "we" his father and uncles. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 433}

Al-Tabari says: God said "truly it is a means of nearness for them", the Almighty saying that the prayers of the Messenger are a means of nearness for them to God. It may also be that the meaning is that the spending he spends is likewise a means of nearness for them with God; "God will admit them into His mercy" means God will admit them among those on whom He had mercy and whom He admitted by His mercy to Paradise; "God is forgiving" of what they committed, "merciful" to them in that, with their repentance and amendment, He does not punish them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 434}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
