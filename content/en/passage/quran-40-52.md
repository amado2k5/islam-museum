We know that some of them were killed by their enemies and imitated by them, like Shuaia, Yahya son of Zakariya, and their likes, and some were killed by his people. So where is the victory that He informed us that He supports it by His messengers and those who believe in Him in this worldly life, and these prophets have received from their people what I have already known, and what did they support those who granted them what they received with them?
