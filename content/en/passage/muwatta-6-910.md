## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that Asma bint Abi Bakr used to wear saturated saffron-dyed garments while in ihram, which had no saffron. The editor notes that the word 'garments' dropped from copy M, and that Abu Musab (1042), Suwayd (488) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.438}
