## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim: the true bankrupt is the one who comes with his deeds but has wronged people's rights. It is one of the best-known texts on accounting on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:sahih-muslim}
