## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab say: Umar ruled a camel for each molar, and Muawiya ruled five camels for each molar. Said said: the blood-money decreases in Umar's ruling and increases in Muawiya's; if it were up to me, I would make two camels for each molar, and then the blood-money would be the same. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.431}
