## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 103 to 104 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "but He wanted to provoke them from the land, so We drowned him and those with him all together": Pharaoh wanted to provoke Moses and the Children of Israel from the land, "so We drowned him" in the sea, "and He and His army" "all of them." We saved Moses and the Children of Israel, and We said to them after Pharaoh's destruction, "Settle in the land" in the land of the Levant: "And when the promise of the Hereafter comes, We will bring you in groups." He means: When the Hour comes, which is the promise of the Hereafter, We will bring you in groups: He says: We gathered you from your graves to the station of the resurrection in a circle: meaning mixed together, you gather around one another, you do not recognize each other, and none of you sides with his tribe. His revelation is from your saying: 'The armies are gathered together,' meaning when you strike each other, and all are mixed together, and likewise everything is mixed with something, it is wrapped in it. Interpreters have differed in interpreting this, with some saying similar to what we have said. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
