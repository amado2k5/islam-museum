## Summary
Malik says: whoever sells a slave or a slave-girl, whether from the heirs or others, with a clause of disclaimer of defects, is discharged of every defect, and no warranty applies, unless he knew of a defect and concealed it. If he knew a defect and concealed it, the disclaimer does not help him, and that sale is returned. Among us there is no warranty except for slaves. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.134}
