## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 12 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By the morning brightness”: Our Lord, may His praise be exalted, swears by the morning brightness, which is the entire day. I believe it comes from the expression, “So-and-so was exposed to the sun,” meaning he was visible. From this comes the verse, “And that you will neither thirst therein nor be exposed to the sun,” meaning the sun will not harm you therein. I have already mentioned the differing interpretations of scholars regarding its meaning in the verse, “By the sun and its morning brightness,” along with my own preferred interpretation. It has also been said that it refers to the time of morning brightness. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
