## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And they swore by Allah with the effort of their oaths: Allah will not raise anyone who dies. Rather, it is a true promise upon him. But most people do not know." These polytheists from Quraysh swore by Allah with the effort of their oaths; they swore that Allah would not resurrect those who die after death. They denied and invalidated their oaths they swore as sworn. Rather, Allah will resurrect them after his death, and it is a promise to Him that He will resurrect them with the promise of His servants. Allah does not break His promise. "But most people do not know," he says: but most of Quraysh do not know that God's promise to His servants is that He will resurrect them on the Day of Resurrection after their death, alive. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Rabi', and Ata.
