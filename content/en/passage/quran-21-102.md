## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They do not hear its sound while they are eternal in what their souls desire": Those to whom the Most Beautiful has preceded Us do not hear the Sense of the Fire, meaning the sound and the sense. If someone says: How can they not hear its sensation, knowing what is narrated that Hell will be brought to it on the Day of Resurrection and it will exhale a sigh that leaves neither a close angel nor a sent prophet except to kneel out of fear of it? It was said: The state in which they do not hear its sound is different from that state, but rather the state where: my father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "They do not hear its sound while they are eternal in what their souls desire," meaning: the people of Paradise do not hear the sound of the Fire when they descend from their home in Paradise. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas.
