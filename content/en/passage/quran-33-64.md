## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 64 to 65 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah has cursed the disbelievers and prepared for them a Blaze": Allah has distanced the disbelievers from all good, and excluded them from Him. "And He has prepared for them a Fire in the Hereafter that will burn and burn for them to pray therein, 'abiding therein forever.' He means: They will remain in the Blaze forever, indefinitely, 'they will find no protector' to take care of them, and He will rescue them from the fire which Allah has built for them, and He will save them from the fire that Allah has built for them, nor will He help them, and save them from Allah's punishment upon them."
