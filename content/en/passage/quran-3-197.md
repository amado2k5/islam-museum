## Summary
Verse 197 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 197 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 197: a slight enjoyment, then the abode (pp. 493-494).** Al-Tabari says the sense is that their moving about the lands and acting in them is an enjoyment with which they are given a little, until they reach their appointed ends and their deaths carry them off, and then their abode is Hell after their death; "the abode" is the destination to which they betake themselves at the Rising and settle in; and as for the blame of the resting place, it means Hell is a wretched bed and lying-place. The two notes refer to earlier places on "abode" and on "resting place". These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 494}
