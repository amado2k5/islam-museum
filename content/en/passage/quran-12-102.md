**Commentary on verse 102 (p. 283)**

On "that is of the news of the unseen, which We reveal to you; you were not with them when they agreed on their plan and were plotting", al-Tabari says: this report that We have told you of Joseph, his father Ya'qub, his brothers and the rest of what is in this Sura is "of the news of the unseen", that is, of the unseen reports which you did not witness or see, but We reveal it to you and make it known to you to strengthen your heart and encourage it, so that you endure what has reached you of harm from your people in God's cause, and know that the messengers of God before you who endured what reached them in it, took to pardon, enjoined what is right and turned away from the ignorant, won through, were supported with victory, established in the land and prevailed over those they set out against of their enemies and the enemies of God's religion. God, blessed and exalted, says to His prophet Muhammad: follow them, Muhammad, and relate their traces. "You were not with them when they agreed on their plan and were plotting" means you were not present with Joseph's brothers when they agreed, their opinions were at one and their resolves firm, to cast Joseph into the depths of the well, and that was their plotting, of which God says "and were plotting". The editor refers to the earlier discussions of "naba'", "ghayb" and "ijma'". Report 19951, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "you were not with them", meaning Muhammad, you were not with them when they cast him into the depths of the well, "and were plotting", that is, against Joseph. Report 19952, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj, Ibn Jurayj and Ata' al-Khurasani: "you were not with them when they agreed on their plan and were plotting", he said, they are the sons of Ya'qub. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 283}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
