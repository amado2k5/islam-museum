## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when hardship and hardship touches you at sea, those you invoke go astray except Him. But when He delivers you to the land, you turn away, and man is ungrateful." And when hardship and effort overtake you at sea, those you call upon go astray: He says: You have completed those you invoke besides Allah, among the equals and gods, and a stray from your path and did not help you, and you found no helper besides Allah to help you. So when you called upon Him and He helped you, and your prayer was answered and saved you from the terror of what you were in at sea, you turned away from what your Lord called you to in by removing the parallels and disassociating from The gods, and singling them as divinity, is an ingratitude among you by His favor. "And man was ungrateful," meaning: and man was ungrateful when he denied the blessings of his Lord.
