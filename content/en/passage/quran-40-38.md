## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 39 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And He who has believed, 'Stand up, follow the way of guidance for you'": Allah Almighty says, informing about the believer in God from the family of Pharaoh, and He who believed, from Pharaoh's people, said to his people: "O my people, follow the way of guidance for you." He says: If you follow me, then accept what I tell you. I have shown you the right path that you will be guided in if you take it and follow it. That is the religion of God with which Moses was sent. He says: "This worldly life is but a luxury." He says to his people: What is this fleeting worldly life that hastened for you in this home except a luxury you enjoy until you exaggerate, then you die and it will disappear from you? "And indeed, the Hereafter is the abode of stability." He means: And the Hereafter, which is the abode of settlement where you settle, so you neither die nor depart from you. He says: "So do it, and seek it." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
