## Summary
Malik said: Ibn Shihab said: it was the Sunna in deliberate killing that when the heirs of the slain pardon, the blood-money falls on the killer alone from his property, unless the kin assist him from their own goodwill. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.436}
