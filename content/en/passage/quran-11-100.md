**Commentary on verse 100 (pp. 470-471)**

On [[passage/quran-11-100|11:100]] God says to His Prophet Muhammad: this story which We told you in this chapter and the report We gave you of the towns whose people We destroyed for their disbelief in God and denial of His messengers, 'We relate it to you' and tell you of it; 'some of them are standing', their buildings standing though their people are perished and gone, and some standing and inhabited, 'and some mown down', their buildings ruined and collapsing, their trace effaced; from 'a mown crop' when it is cut at the root, which is in form 'mahsud' but turned to the pattern 'fa'il'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 470}

Report 18546, from Ibn Abbas: the standing are inhabited towns and the mown extinct towns; 18547, from Qatada: standing on their roofs, and mown means uprooted; 18548, from Qatada: standing, its place seen, and mown, of which no trace is seen; 18549, from Ibn Jurayj: standing, empty on its roofs, and mown, flattened to the ground; 18550 and 18551, from al-A'mash: the mown is what has fallen in ruin; 18552, from Ibn Zayd: some standing with its trace to be seen and some mown, perished, with no trace seen. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 471}

We stop here at the foot of p. 471, in the middle of the commentary on verse 100; the next batch begins at p. 472. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 471}
