**Commentary on verse 73 (p. 153)**

On [[passage/quran-10-73|10:73]] al-Tabari says: his people denied Noah in what he told them from God of the message and revelation, 'so We saved him and those with him' who were carried with him 'in the ark', that is, the ship, 'and made them successors', meaning We made those We saved with Noah in the ship successors on the earth in place of his people who denied him, after We drowned those who denied Our signs, meaning Our proofs of Our oneness and the mission of Our messenger Noah. Then God tells His prophet Muhammad: 'see', Muhammad, how was the end of those who were warned, those Noah warned of God's punishment for denying him and worshipping idols. The end of those of your people who deny you, if they persist in their disbelief and rebellion against their Lord, will be like that of the people of Noah; let them beware lest the like of what befell them befall them, if they do not repent. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'ark', 'succession' and 'end'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 153}
