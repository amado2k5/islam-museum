## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because Allah is the Truth, and that what they call upon besides Him is falsehood, and that Allah is the Most High, the Great": He means that His mention by saying "that" is the action I performed by connecting me to night during the day and to entering day by night, because I am the Truth, who has no equal, partner, or equal. And what these polytheists call a god besides Him is falsehood, which cannot do anything, but is the fabricated thing. Allah says to them: Do you abandon worship, O ignorant ones, from which there is benefit and in whose hand is harm, and who is capable of everything and everything besides it, and worship falsehood whose worship will not benefit you? And His saying: "And that Allah is the Most High, the Great" means by saying: "The Most High," the One of the Most High above everything, He is above everything and everything is below Him. "The Great" means the great, below whom everything is and nothing is greater.
