## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "Exalted is He above what they say, a great exaltation": This is a transcendence from Allah Himself, whom He mentioned above, from what He described as the polytheists, who made gods other than Him, who host daughters to Him. He said: 'Glory be to Allah and exalt Him above what you say, O people, of falsehood and falsehood. What you attribute to these matters is not His attribute, nor should it have any attribute. Also: Qatadah, 'Exalted is He above what they say, a great exaltedness,' glorifying Himself when it was said about him to be slandered. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Ibrahim.
