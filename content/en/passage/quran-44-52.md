## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 52 to 54 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Indeed, the righteous are in a trustworthy station": Those who fear Allah by fulfilling His obedience and avoiding His sins in a place of residence are safe in that place from what they feared in the worldly stations of injuries, defects, stones, and sorrows. Reciters differed in the recitation of the phrase: "in the position of trustworthy," with the general reciters of Medina reading it as "in the place of trustworthiness" with a damma on the meem, meaning: in the establishment of a trustworthy one from the dhim. The general reciters of the Egyptians read it as "in the place of trustworthiness." Kufa and Basra are 'in a maqam' with a fatha on the 'm' in the meaning we described, indicating that they are in a safe place. The correct view is that they are extensive readings in the recitation of the regions, with correct meanings; whichever one the reader reads, he is correct. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ikrimah.
