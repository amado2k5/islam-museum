## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "It was said to her, 'Enter the palace.' When she saw him, she thought he was a scapegoat and uncovered her legs. He said: 'Indeed, it is a palace made of jars. She said, 'My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself and submitted to Allah, Lord of the worlds.': It is mentioned that when Solomon's companion came seeking him, he ordered the devils to build for him, which is like a roof of bottles, and water flowed from beneath it to test her mind. His understanding of her as she did was the reason she directed the satisfies and purity to distinguish between males and females, reproaching her. Likewise. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Ishaq.
