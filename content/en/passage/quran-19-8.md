## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He said, 'My Lord, how will I have a boy when my wife was barren and she had reached old age and was old?": Zechariah said when Allah gave him glad tidings of John: "My Lord, I will have a son," and from what perspective will that be for me? My wife is barren and does not conceive, and she has become weak from old age from having intercourse with women, so that You strengthen me in what I have weakened and make my wife a child. You are capable of that and as you wish. Or should I marry a wife other than mine who is barren? His Lord proves the news about the face The one who will have a child before him, whom God has given him glad tidings, is not a denial of the reality of the existence of what God promised of a child. How can that be a denial from him that the child he promised him would grant him, when he is the initiator asking his Lord by saying: "So grant me from Yourself a guardian who will inherit me and inherit from the family of Jacob," after saying, "Indeed, my bones have weakened and my head has turned gray."
