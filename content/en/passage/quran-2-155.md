## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "We shall surely test you with something of fear and hunger and loss of wealth, lives and fruits, and give good news to the patient" al-Tabari says it is God telling the followers of His Messenger that He will test them with hard things to know who follows the Messenger from who turns on his heels, as He tested them by changing the qibla and tested His chosen before them, and he mentions what He promised them in another verse of al-Baqara. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 219} From Ibn 'Abbas, God told the believers that this world is a house of trial, that He will test them in it, commanded them to be patient and gave good news, then told them He did the same with His prophets and chosen ones so that their hearts be at ease. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 219}

Al-Tabari says "wa la-nabluwannakum" means We shall test you, trial meaning testing, and that "something of fear" means fear of the enemy and "hunger" means drought: We shall test you with some fear that reaches you from your enemy, a year in which famine and hardship strike and your wealth shrinks, wars between you and your enemies that reduce your numbers, the death of your children, and droughts that cut your fruits; all of it a test so that the truthful among you are distinguished from the false, and the people of insight in their religion are known from the hypocrites and doubters. It is addressed to the followers of the Messenger of God and his companions, from 'Ata'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 220} He explains the singular "something" as covering the differing kinds of trial God told His servants He would test them with, "something" at the start serving for all; and from al-Rabi', that had already happened and worse would come. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 220} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 221} By "give good news to the patient" he says the Prophet is told to give the good news specifically to the people of patience whom God described; the root of "tabshir" is one man telling another news that pleases or grieves him that no one else has told him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 221}
