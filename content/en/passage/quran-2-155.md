## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "We shall surely test you with something of fear and hunger and loss of wealth, lives and fruits, and give good news to the patient" al-Tabari says it is God telling the followers of His Messenger that He will test them with hard things to know who follows the Messenger from who turns on his heels, as He tested them by changing the qibla and tested His chosen before them, and he mentions what He promised them in another verse of al-Baqara. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 219} From Ibn 'Abbas, God told the believers that this world is a house of trial, that He will test them in it, commanded them to be patient and gave good news, then told them He did the same with His prophets and chosen ones so that their hearts be at ease. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 219}
