## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8-9 as a single section. He explains the phrase, “So what can make you deny the religion after this?” The scholars of interpretation differed in their understanding of this phrase. Some said it means: Who can deny you, O Muhammad, after these proofs we have presented, regarding the religion, meaning obedience to God and the truth with which He sent you, and that God will resurrect those in the graves? They said that “what” is synonymous with “who,” because it refers to the children of Adam and those to whom the Prophet was sent. Others said: Rather, it means: What can make you, O human being, deny the religion after these proofs? In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Kalbi, and Sufyan.
