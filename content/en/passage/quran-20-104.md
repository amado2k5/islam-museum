## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "We know best what they say, when he says, 'The best of them is a way, if you stay except a day,'" means: We know better than them about their secrets and their concealment among themselves by saying, "Indeed, you stayed except for ten" with what they say. It is not hidden from Us what they are experiencing among them: "When he says, 'The best of them is a way, if you stayed except a day.'" He says, the Exalted, when He says or understands reasonably, and the most knowledgeable among them is: "Indeed, you stayed in the world except for a day."
