**Commentary on verse 29 (pp. 60-62)**

Al-Tabari says: this, as reported from Ibn Abbas, is a report from God of the witness's words, said to the woman and to Joseph. By "Yusuf" he means "O Joseph"; "turn away from this" means turn away from mentioning what she did to you in soliciting you, and mention it to no one. The editor refers to the explanation of "turning away". Report 19136, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: do not mention it; and you, woman, ask your husband's forgiveness, that is, ask him not to punish you for your sin and to forgive it and cover it for you. On "you were among the sinners" he says: you were among the wrongdoers in soliciting Joseph. One says "khati'a" of one who errs, with "khit'" and "khata'"; as in "killing them was a great sin" [[passage/quran-17-31|17:31]], "khata'" in a matter; and for "sawab" too are recorded "sawab" and "sawb". Al-Tabari cites a line of Aws ibn Ghalfa', speaking to his wife, that his error and his rightness are on him and that his wealth was ruined by him, and a line of Umayya ibn Abi al-Salt about God's servants who err while the fates and decrees are in His hands. The editor gives the lines before and after them and refers to the Nawadir of Abu Zayd, the Tabaqat Fuhul al-Shu'ara', the Majaz al-Qur'an, the Lisan and Umayya's divan. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 61}

It was said "you were among the sinners" and not "among the female sinners" because the report is not meant about women but about whoever does it and errs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 62}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
