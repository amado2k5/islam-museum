## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who say, 'Our Lord is Allah,' and then remain steadfast, there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve": 'Indeed, those who said, 'Our Lord is Allah,' with whom there is no deity, and then remained steadfast in their belief thereof, not mixing it with polytheism, nor did they oppose Allah in His command and prohibition. 'So there is no fear upon them' from the terror of the Day of Resurrection and its terrors, 'nor will they grieve' over what they left behind after their death. And His saying: "Those are the companions of Paradise," Allah says: Those who said this and remained steadfast, the people of Paradise and its inhabitants "abiding therein" means they will abide therein forever "as a reward for what they used to do." He means: a reward from Us; We gave them that for their righteous deeds which they used to do in this world.
