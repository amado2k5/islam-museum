## Summary
Yahya said Malik said of a man who advanced money to another man, then the one who borrowed it asked him to let it remain with him as profit-sharing. Malik said: I do not like that until he receives his money from him, then he hands it to him as profit-sharing if he wishes, or keeps it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.233}
