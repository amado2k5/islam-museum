## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 33 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And I will stand up, I fear for you on the Day of Calling": God Almighty says, informing about the saying of this believer to Pharaoh and his people: "O my people, indeed I fear for you" by killing Moses; if you kill him, the punishment of God is "on the Day of Calling." Reciters differed in their interpretation of the phrase: "the day of the calling." The majority of the reciters of the cities read it as "Yawm al-Taynad" with a lightened 'd' and omitting the affirmation of the ya, meaning interaction, from the people calling out by calling, as He, the Exalted, said: "And the companions of Paradise called out to the companions of the Fire, saying, 'We have found what our Lord promised us true, so have you found what your Lord promised to be true?' They said, 'Yes.' And He said: 'And the companions of the Fire called out to the people of Paradise, 'Pour down upon us of water.' Therefore, some readers interpreted this as well. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
