## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And say, 'My Lord, admit me to a truthful entrance and bring me out with a truthful exit, and appoint for me from Yourself a truthful authority.'" Allah says to His Prophet: And say, O Muhammad, O Lord, admit me to a truthful entrance. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of the entrance to truthfulness that God commanded His Prophet to desire to be brought into, and the way out of truthfulness, which He commanded to desire to bring out. Some said: By the entrance of truthfulness, he meant the entrance of the Messenger of God when he migrated to it, and the exit of truthfulness is its exit from Mecca, when he emigrated to Medina. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
