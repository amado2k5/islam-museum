## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abu Abd al-Rahman, from Zayd ibn Thabit that he said of a man who divorced his slave-woman three times and then bought her: she is not lawful to him until she marries another husband. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.44}
