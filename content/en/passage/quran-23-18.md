## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We sent down from the sky water in measure, and We settled it in the earth, and indeed, We are destined to go with it": And We sent down from the sky whatever is in the earth of water and settled it therein. As he said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj: "And We sent down from the sky water in measure and made it dwell in the earth." Water is from the sky. And His saying: "And indeed, We are able to go with it," He says: "Indeed, We are capable of taking away the water that We have settled in the earth, so that you perish in thirst and destroy your land, so it will not grow crops or plantings, and your livestock will perish." He means: "So out of My favor upon you, I leave that flowing for you in the earth."
