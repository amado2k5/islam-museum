## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "So take your family with a portion of the night." He ordered Lot to travel with his family for the rest of the night, and part of it—from Ibn Zayd—"and follow their footsteps," following his family's footsteps so he would be behind them while they were in front of him—from Qatadah—"and none of you should look back," nor should anyone look behind him, nor should he look or limp—from Mujahid—"Go wherever you are commanded," wherever God commands them.
