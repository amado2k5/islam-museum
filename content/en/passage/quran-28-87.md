## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And let them not divert you from the signs of Allah after they have been revealed to you. And invoke to your Lord and do not be among the polytheists": And let them not divert you from conveying the verses and proofs of Allah after they have been revealed to you, your Lord, O Muhammad. These are the polytheists by saying: "Why not have you been given the like of what Moses was given?" Invite to your Lord and deliver His message to the one who sent you with it. "And do not be among the polytheists," meaning: And do not neglect supplicating to your Lord and delivering His message to the polytheists, so that you will be among those who have committed the actions of the polytheists by disobeying their Lord or otherwise.
