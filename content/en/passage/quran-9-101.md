**Commentary on verse 101 (pp. 440-442)**

Al-Tabari says: among the people around your city of the Bedouin are hypocrites, and among the people of your city also their like, people who are hypocrites. As for "they have persisted in hypocrisy", it means they have been hardened in it and trained in it, and from this are the "rebellious devil", meaning the wicked and defiant one, and the saying that so-and-so "rebelled against his Lord", meaning was defiant, hardened in disobedience to Him and accustomed to it. Report 17119, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: "and among the people of Medina who persisted in hypocrisy", he said they stayed in it and did not repent as the others repented; report 17120, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: that is, they insisted on it and refused anything else. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of the rebellious one and to Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 440}

"You do not know them", God says to His Prophet Muhammad: you, Muhammad, do not know these hypocrites whose description I gave you, of those around you among the Bedouin and the people of Medina, but We know them. Report 17121, from Qatada, through al-Hasan, Abd al-Razzaq and Ma'mar: His words "among those around you of the Bedouin are hypocrites" to "We know them", he said, what is the matter with people who take it on themselves to know about others, so-and-so is in Paradise and so-and-so in the Fire! And when you ask one of them about himself he says "I do not know!" By my life, you know yourself better than you know people's deeds, and you have taken on something the prophets before you did not take on: the Prophet of God Noah said "what knowledge have I of what they used to do", [[passage/quran-26-112|26:112]], and the Prophet of God Shu'ayb said "what remains with God is better for you if you are believers, and I am not a guardian over you", [[passage/quran-11-86|11:86]], and God said to His Prophet "you do not know them, We know them". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 441}

As for "We shall punish them twice", it means We shall punish these hypocrites twice, one in the world and the other in the grave. The interpreters then differed over what the one in the world is. Some said it is their exposure, God exposing them by uncovering their affairs and making their inner thoughts clear to the people through His Messenger. Report 17122, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Husayn ibn Amr al-Anqazi, his father, Asbat, al-Suddi and Abu Malik: the Messenger of God stood preaching on Friday and said "go out, so-and-so, for you are a hypocrite, go out, so-and-so, for you are a hypocrite", and drove some of them out of the mosque, shaming them; Umar met them as they were leaving the mosque, and hid from them in shame that he had not attended the Friday prayer, thinking the people had dispersed, and they hid from Umar, thinking he had learned of their affair; Umar came and entered the mosque and found the people had not yet prayed, and a man of the Muslims said to him "good news, Umar, God has exposed the hypocrites today!", so that was the first punishment, when he drove them out of the mosque, and the second punishment is the punishment of the grave. The editor notes that al-Haythami records the report from al-Tabarani's al-Awsat and calls al-Husayn ibn Amr weak. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 442}

Report 17123, from Abu Malik, through al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz, Sufyan and al-Suddi: "We shall punish them twice", he said, the Messenger of God used to preach and mention the hypocrites, and so punished them with his tongue; he said, and the punishment of the grave. Others said rather it is what befalls them of captivity, killing, hunger and fear in this world. Report 17124, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr, Ma'mar and Ibn Abi Najih: killing and captivity; report 17125, from him, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl: by hunger and the punishment of the grave, he said "then they will be returned to a great punishment" on the Day of Resurrection; report 17126, from him, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq, Ja'far ibn Awn, al-Qasim, Yahya ibn Adam and Sufyan: hunger and killing, and Yahya said fear and killing; report 17127, from him, through Abu Kurayb, Ibn Yaman and Sufyan: by hunger and killing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 442}
