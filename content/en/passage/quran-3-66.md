## Summary
Verse 66 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 66 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 66.** Al-Tabari says the address is to the people who said what they said about Abraham, and that their disputing about what they had knowledge of concerns their religion found in their books and brought by God's messengers and other matters established for them; why then argue about what they had no knowledge of, the affair of Abraham and his religion, which they did not find in God's books, which their prophets did not bring and which they did not witness. The editor notes an extra word in both the manuscript and the print that does not suit the sense, says he added a phrase in brackets which is needed because the copyist skipped a phrase or line through haste or weariness, that he inferred it from Abu Jafar's manner, and that another word in the source is a copyist's corruption which he corrected; for the sense of disputing he points to earlier places. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 492}

**Reports 7208-7210.** Report 7208 (Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, from Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal, from Asbat, from al-Suddi): what they had knowledge of is what was forbidden to them and what they were commanded, and what they had no knowledge of is the affair of Abraham. Report 7209 (Bishr, from Yazid, from Said, from Qatada): the first is what they witnessed, saw and observed, the second what they did not witness, see or observe. Report 7210 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Ibn Abi Jafar, from his father, from al-Rabi) is the same. For the closing clause al-Tabari says God knows what was hidden from them, which they did not witness and His messengers did not bring them, of Abraham's affair and the rest, since nothing is hidden from Him, and that they know of it only what they saw or reached through report and hearing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 493}
