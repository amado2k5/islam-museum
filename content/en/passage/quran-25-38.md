## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 39 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And 'Aad and Thamud and the companions of Ars and Qarun between that were many": We also destroyed 'Ad, Thamud, and the companions of Ar-Rass. Interpreters differed regarding the people of Ar-Rass, with some saying: The people of Ar-Rass are from Thamud. In the Maqta, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
