## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Aisha, Mother of the Believers, who said to him: my nephew, it is only ten nights, so if something troubles your mind, leave it; she meant eating game meat. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1148), Suwayd (577) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.476}
