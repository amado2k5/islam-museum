## Summary
Malik says: whatever befalls a slave or a slave-girl during the three days, from the time they are bought until the three days pass, is the seller's responsibility. The warranty of a year covers madness, leprosy and vitiligo. When the year passes, the seller is discharged from all warranty. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.134}
