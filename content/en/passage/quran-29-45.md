## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Recite what has been revealed to you of the Scripture and establish prayer. Indeed, prayer forbids immorality and wrongdoing. And the remembrance of Allah is greater. And Allah knows what you do": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, "Atl," meaning "recite what was revealed to you" from the Book, meaning what was revealed to you from this Quran. "And establish prayer" means: to perform the prayer that Allah has prescribed upon you within its limits: "Indeed, prayer forbids immorality and wrongdoing." The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of the prayer mentioned in this place; some said: it refers to the Qur'an recited at the place of prayer or during prayer. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
