## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7-8 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “And they thought, as you thought, that God would never send anyone”: God Almighty informs us of what these men from among the jinn said, “And they thought, as you thought, that God would never send anyone.” This means that the men from among the jinn thought, as the men from among mankind thought, that God would never send anyone as a messenger to His creation, calling them to His Oneness. In this section, he quotes statements attributed to al-Kalbi.
