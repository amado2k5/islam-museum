## Summary
Malik says that a slave-woman who was under a free man and was separated from him before he was freed: his marriage to her while she was a slave does not make her chaste, until she marries after her release and her husband has intercourse with her, which is her chastity. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.49}
