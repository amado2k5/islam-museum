## Summary
Verse 281 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 281 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

([[passage/quran-2-281|2:281]]), pp. 39 to 40. Al-Tabari says it was said that this verse too is the last revealed in the Qur'an. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 39} 6311, Ibn Abbas (through Ikrima): the last verse revealed to the Prophet is the one that commands guarding against a day of return to God; 6312, Ibn Abbas (through his family chain): it is the last verse of the Book revealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 40} The editor gives biographies of the transmitters of the first chain (Abu Tumayla Yahya ibn Wadih, al-Husayn ibn Waqid, and Yazid al-Nahwi, whom he says Abu Muslim killed in 131 for commanding the good), grades the chain sound, notes that the apparent sense conflicts with report 6310, and quotes Ibn Hajar's harmonization in Fath al-Bari that this verse closes the verses revealed on usury because it is joined to them, as al-Bukhari's chapter heading suggests; this is all the editor's. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 40} We stop at 6312; report 6313 begins at the foot of p. 40. These are reports as transmitted, with no museum verdict.
