## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 34 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "They will enter the gardens of Eden and adorn therein with bracelets of gold and pearls, and their garments therein will be silk": gardens of residence entered by those whom We have inherited the Scripture, those whom We have chosen from among Our servants on the Day of Resurrection. "They will be adorned therein with bracelets of gold." They will wear bracelets of gold in the gardens of Eden, "and pearls, and their garments therein will be silk," meaning: their garments in Paradise will be silk. And His saying: "And they said, 'Praise be to Allah who removed sorrow from us.'" The interpreters differed regarding the sorrow that Allah praised for removing from them these people. Some said: That sorrow they were in before entering Paradise out of fear of the Fire, as they were afraid to enter it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Al-A'mash, and Atiyyah.
