## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Those will be rewarded with the chamber because they were patient and will be greeted and greeted therein": these are the ones whose description I have described among My servants, starting from the verse: "And the servants of the Most Merciful who walk upon the earth humbly..." up to His saying: "And those who say, 'Our Lord, grant us from among our spouses...'" The verse 'Yazazon' says: They will be rewarded for their deeds that they did in this world, the 'chamber,' which is a high rank among the ranks of Paradise 'for what they patiently endured,' meaning: by their patience with these actions and the severity of their hardship. And His saying: 'And they will be greeted therein with greeting and peace,' which the reciters differed into. The general reciters of Medina and Basra read it as 'waliquq' with a damma on the ya, with a stressed qaf, meaning: and the angels greet them therein with greetings. The general reciters of Kufa read this as: 'al-yulamqoo' with a fatha on the ya' and a lightened qaf.
