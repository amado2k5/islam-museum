## Summary
A report of Fatima bint Qays from Tamim al-Dari in the chapter on the story of the Jassasa in the Book of Trials of Sahih Muslim: the Prophet gathered people to tell them. It is among the longest texts on the trial of the Dajjal in the collections, with his questions about the palm groves of Baysan, the lake of Tiberias, the spring of Zughar, and the report of the prophet of the unlettered. {cite:sahih-muslim}
