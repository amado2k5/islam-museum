## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 19 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Indeed, it is He who originates and restores”: The scholars of interpretation differed in the meaning of this phrase. Some said: It means that God originated His creation, so He originates, meaning He brings His creation into existence initially, then He causes them to die, then He restores them to life after their death, in the same form they were in before their death. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
