## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, you do not guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills, and He is most knowing of those who are guided": "Indeed, O Muhammad, you do not guide whom you love" His guidance, "but Allah guides whom He wills" meaning to guide him from His creation by enabling him to believe in Him and His Messenger. If it were said: it means: You do not guide whom you love because of their kinship to you, but Allah guides whom He wills, it was a school of thought. "And He knows best who is rightly guided": Allah, the Exalted, says: And Allah knows best who has previously known that he is guided to the right path, that is what Allah guides, guides, and guides. He mentioned that this verse was revealed to the Messenger of Allah because of Abu Talib his uncle's refusal to answer him, as he called him to believe in Allah, to what he was called to do.
