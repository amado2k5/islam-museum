**Commentary on verse 132: dominion, keeping and direction (p. 297)**

Al-Tabari says that to God belongs all that the heavens and earth contain; He is their sustainer and keeper, nothing of His knowledge is lost to Him, and His keeping and direction are not a burden. Report 10675, from Qatada, says the keeper is the one who guards. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 297}

Al-Tabari answers the question of why the phrase of dominion is repeated in verses 131 and 132: it is repeated because the two statements differ. One mentions the creatures' need of their Creator and His independence of them; the other mentions His keeping of them, His knowledge of them and His direction of them. The verse that ended with richness and praise cannot also end with keeping and direction. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 297}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
