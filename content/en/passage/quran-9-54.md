**Commentary on verse 54 (pp. 294-295)**

Al-Tabari says: what prevented the acceptance, Muhammad, of the spending of these hypocrites, which they spend on their journey with you and in other ways, was only that they disbelieved in God and His Messenger. The first "that" is in the accusative and the second in the nominative, since the sense is that nothing prevented the acceptance of their spending except their disbelief in God; the editor explains that the second is the doubled particle in "that they" and the first the light one. "They come to prayer only lazily" means only sluggishly, since they hope for no reward by performing it and fear no punishment for leaving it, and keep it up only out of fear for themselves from the believers, so that when they feel safe from them they do not keep it up; "and they spend nothing of their wealth except unwillingly", unwilling to spend it in the way in which they spend it, which strengthens Islam and its people. The editor refers to earlier explanations of laziness and unwillingness. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 295}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
