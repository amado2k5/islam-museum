## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 4 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "al-Qari'ah" as: "al-Qari'ah" means the hour for which the hearts of people are beaten, and the great calamity that befalls them at that time, and that is a morning and no night after it. In this passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
