## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "and the evils of what they earned will appear to them, and what they used to mock will befall them": On the Day of Resurrection, these polytheists will see the "evils of what they earned" in this worldly deeds, as they will be given their records in their forms, "and what they used to mock will befall them," and they will be obligated at that time. Thus, they will face the punishment of God that the Prophet of God used to promise them in this world for their disbelief in their Lord, and they mocked it, denying that it would befall them or that it would befall them as a denial of it, and that encompassed them.
