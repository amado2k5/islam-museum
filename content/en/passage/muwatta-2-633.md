## Summary
A report from Malik that it reached him from Abu al-Hubab Said ibn Yasar, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: the believer is continually afflicted in his children and his close family, until he meets God with no sin on him. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Qanabi transmitted it from Malik, and cites al-Tamhid (24/180). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.323}
