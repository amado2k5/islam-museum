## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "You will have benefits therein for a specified term, then their place is to the Ancient House": The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the benefits mentioned by Allah in this verse and informed His servants that they are for a specified term, just as they differ regarding the meaning of the rituals mentioned by Allah, Glorified be He, in His saying: "And whoever honors the symbols of Allah, indeed, it is from the piety of hearts," so those who said about me are the body. The meaning of this is: O people, you have benefits in the body. Then those who said this statement differed regarding the time in which they have benefits, and regarding the term in which the Almighty said: "for a specified term." Some said: The state in which Allah, Glorified and Exalted, informed them that they have benefits is the state in which its owner did not oblige, nor named it as a garment, nor imitated it. They said: Its benefits in this state are: drinking its milk, riding its back, and what Allah provides them with its offspring and offspring. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sufyan, and Ata.
