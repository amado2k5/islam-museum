## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman, that Umar ibn al-Khattab and Uthman ibn Affan used to pray the sunset prayer when they saw the black night before breaking their fast, and then break their fast after the prayer, in Ramadan. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi and al-Tinnisi (al-Bukhari 1957) transmitted it from Malik, and that Abd al-Razzaq (7588) transmitted it from Mamar from al-Zuhri. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.389}
