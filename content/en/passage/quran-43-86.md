## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And none has intercession except those who bear witness to the truth while they know": interpreters differed in interpreting this. Some said: The meaning is: Jesus, Uzair, and the angels whom these polytheists worship do not have authority over the Hour. Intercession with Allah is for anyone, except for the one who testifies to the truth and obeys Allah by the unity of knowledge from Him and the authenticity of what His messengers brought. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
