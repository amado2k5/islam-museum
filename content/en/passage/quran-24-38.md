## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 40 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "In houses that Allah has permitted to be raised and His name mentioned therein, glorifying Him by morning and branches": He means His mention by saying: "In houses Allah has permitted to be raised," Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth, like a niche with a lamp in it; in houses Allah has permitted to be raised. As Ibn Zayd said: The niche is the one that contains the wick that contains the lamp. He said: The lamps are in houses that Allah has permitted to be raised. Abu Ja'far said: It is possible that 'min' is in the connection of 'to be burned,' so the meaning is: to light from a blessed tree. That lamp is in houses that Allah has permitted to be raised. And by 'houses' he meant mosques. The scholars of interpretation have differed on this, and some said what we have said about that. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Hasan, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
