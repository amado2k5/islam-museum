## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 62 to 63 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, it is knowledge of the Hour, so do not hesitate to it. And follow this is a straight path": The interpreters differed regarding the 'ha' in the phrase "indeed," what it means, and who mentioned what it is. Some said: it is from the mention of Jesus, and it refers to him. They said: The meaning of the statement is: that Jesus' appearance is knowledge by which he knows the coming of the Hour, because his appearance is one of its signs and his descent to earth is evidence of the annihilation of this world and the approach of the Hereafter. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
