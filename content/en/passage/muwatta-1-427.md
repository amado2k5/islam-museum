## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas used to pass in front of some rows while the prayer was standing. Malik says he finds that broad when the prayer has been called and after the imam has begun, if a person finds no way into the mosque except between the rows. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.222}
