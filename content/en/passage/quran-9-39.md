**Commentary on verse 39 (pp. 254-256)**

Al-Tabari says God speaks to the believers in Him among the Messenger's Companions, threatening them for leaving off going out against their enemy among the Romans: if you do not go out, believers, to the one whom God's messenger called you to go out against, God will punish you soon, in this world, for leaving off going out to them, with a painful punishment, "and replace you with another people", that is God will replace you for His prophet with a people other than you who go out when called, answer him when he summons and obey God and His messenger; "and you will not harm Him at all" means you will do God no harm by leaving off going out and disobeying Him, for He has no need of you, but you have need of Him, He being the Rich and you the poor; "God has power over all things" means over destroying you and putting another people in your place and over all that He wills. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 254}

It was reported that the "painful punishment" here was the withholding of rain from them. Report 16721, from Ibn Abbas, through Abu Kurayb, Zayd ibn Hubab, Abd al-Mu'min ibn Khalid al-Hanafi and Najda al-Khurasani: asked about "if you do not go out, He will punish you with a painful punishment", he said the Messenger called out a clan of the Arabs and they were slow to go with him, so rain was withheld from them, and that was their punishment; report 16722 is similar through Ibn Humayd and Yahya ibn Wadih, saying their punishment was that rain was withheld. The editor notes that Abu Dawud and al-Bayhaqi give it, that al-Suyuti cites further sources and that al-Hakim called it sound. Report 16723, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: God called the believers out in the blaze of the heat on the Tabuk campaign toward Syria, in the strain God knows. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 255}

Some claimed this verse is abrogated. Report 16724, from Ikrima and al-Hasan al-Basri, through Ibn Humayd, Yahya ibn Wadih, al-Husayn and Yazid: He said "if you do not go out He will punish you with a painful punishment", and He said that it was not for the people of Medina and the Bedouin around them to stay behind from God's messenger, which is [[passage/quran-9-120|9:120]], and this was abrogated by the verse that follows, that the believers should not go out all together, which is [[passage/quran-9-122|9:122]]. Al-Tabari says there is no report for what Ikrima and al-Hasan said about abrogating the ruling of this verse that calls for submission to it, and no proof that it is sound; a number of Companions and Successors, whom he will mention later, held the ruling to stand, and it is possible that "if you do not go out He will punish you" is specific to some people, meaning those whom the Messenger called out and who did not go, as reported from Ibn Abbas. That being so, the words "the believers should not go out all together" are a prohibition from God against emptying the lands of Islam with no believer resident in them, and a statement that going out is required of some and not others, of those called out and not those not called; so neither verse abrogates the other and the ruling of each stands for what it concerns. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 256}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
