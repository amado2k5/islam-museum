## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubayd Allah, from Busr ibn Sa'id: Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani sent him to Abu Juhaym to ask what he had heard from the Prophet about one who passes in front of one who prays. He told him the Prophet said that if the passer-by knew what was upon him, standing for forty would be better for him than passing in front of him. Abu al-Nadr said he did not know whether it was forty days, months or years. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Darimi and Ahmad, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.220} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.221}
