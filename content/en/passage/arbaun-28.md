## Summary
Hadith of al-Irbad ibn Sariya: the Prophet gave an exhortation that moved hearts; he said, "Hold fast to my Sunna and the Sunna of the rightly guided caliphs, and beware of newly invented matters, for every innovation is error". Ibn Rajab reviews the chains, the disputed hearing, and the meaning of innovation in religious law. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
