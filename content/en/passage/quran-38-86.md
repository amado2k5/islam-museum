## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 86 to 88 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "He said, so the truth and the truth I say": Reciters differed in the reading of His saying: "He said, so the truth and the truth I say," some people of Hijaz and the general Kufans read it as the first haqq and the second accusative accusative. In the first haqq nominative if read as such, there are two types: one is nominative with the pronoun 'Allah, the Truth,' or 'I am the Truth and I say the truth.' The second: It must be nominative by interpreting the phrase "to fill it," so the meaning then is: "Indeed, I will fill Hell from you," as He says: "A sincere resolve to come to you." So he raised a determination with an interpretation of "I will come to you," because its interpretation is to come to you, as He said: "Then it appeared to them after they saw the signs to imprison him." Therefore, the phrase "appeared to them" must be in the nominative form, which is implicit in meaning. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
