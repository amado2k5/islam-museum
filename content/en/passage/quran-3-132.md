## Summary
Verse 132 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 132 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The meaning (pp. 206-207).** Abu Ja'far says: obey God, believers, in what He forbade you, usury and other things, and in what the Messenger commanded you, and obey the Messenger likewise, in the hope that you may be shown mercy and not punished. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 206}

**A view: a rebuke over the day of Uhud.** It was also said that this is God's rebuke to the Prophet's Companions who went against his command at Uhud and left the posts where they had been ordered to stand firm.
- From Ibn Humayd, Salama, Ibn Ishaq (7829): a rebuke to those who disobeyed His Messenger when he commanded them what he commanded on that day and on others, meaning the day of Uhud. The editor says it is in the Sira (3: 115) and follows reports ending with 7828. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 207}
