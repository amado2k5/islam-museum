## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 64 and 65 in one passage: "And We have given you the truth, and indeed, We are truthful." The messengers told Lot that they had come to him with the truth, certain from God, which is the punishment with which God punished his people, and that they were truthful in what they warned about the destruction of His people. "So he took your family by a part of the night": Lot commanded his family to travel with his people for the remainder of the night — from Ibn Zayd — "and follow their footsteps," meaning that he would follow their backs when they walked — according to Qatadah — "and none of you should look back," no one should look behind him nor pass — from Mujahid — "And go where you are commanded."
