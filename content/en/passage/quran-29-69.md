## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who strive for Us, We will surely guide them to Our ways, and indeed, Allah is with the doers of good": And those who fought those who slandered against Allah falsely among the disbelievers of Quraysh, who denied the truth when it came to Us, seeking to fight them to elevate Our word and support our religion. "Indeed, We will guide them to Our ways," meaning: to guide them to the straight path, and that is the affliction of Allah's religion, which is Islam with which Allah sent Muhammad. "And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good," meaning: Indeed, Allah has shone with those who did good to His creation, so the polytheists struggled against it, confirming His Messenger in what he brought from Allah to help him and support those who strive among His enemies. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
