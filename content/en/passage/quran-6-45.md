## Summary
Verse 45 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 45 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

By "so the last of the wrongdoing people was cut off; praise be to God, Lord of the worlds" al-Tabari means: the people who defied their Lord, denied his messengers and opposed his command were uprooted to the last, none left of them who was not destroyed suddenly when God's punishment came on them. A group of interpreters said so: 13242, from al-Suddi: the root of the wrongdoers was cut off. 13243, from Ibn Zayd: they were uprooted. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 363}

"Dabir al-qawm" is the one who comes behind them, who is in their rear and last; one says "dabara al-qawm fulan yadburuhum dabran wa-duburan" when he was the last of them, from which comes Umayya's line, in verses describing the standing on the day of the gathering, saying they were destroyed by a punishment that shaved off their last and they could neither turn it aside nor help themselves. "Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds" means complete praise and full thanks to God, Lord of the worlds, for his favour to his messengers and the people of obedience to him, by making their proofs prevail over those of the unbelievers who opposed them, and making true what they promised them, because of their unbelief in God and their denial of his messengers, of God's vengeance and swift punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 364}

The editor refers for "praise" and "Lord of the worlds" to Surat al-Fatiha, says Umayya's line is in his Diwan, p. 32, explains "hassa al-sha'r" as shaving it so that nothing is left, and corrects "what they promised them" from the manuscript.

Verse 45 closes here at p. 364, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-46|6:46]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
