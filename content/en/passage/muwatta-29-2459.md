## Summary
It reached Malik that Umar ibn al-Khattab set the blood-money on the people of the towns: on the people of gold, one thousand dinars, and on the people of silver, twelve thousand dirhams. Malik said: the people of gold are the people of Syria and Egypt, and the people of silver are the people of Iraq. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.418}
