## Summary
A saying of Malik: I prefer that what God mentioned in the Quran be fasted consecutively. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.409}
