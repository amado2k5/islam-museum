## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 5 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And warns those who say, 'Allah has taken a son,'" and Muhammad also warns the people who said, 'Allah has taken a son,' from the polytheists of his people and others, regarding Allah's power, the immediate punishment, and the delay of His punishment, as they say, as from Ibn Ishaq: 'And warns those who say, 'Allah has taken a son,' meaning Quraysh in their saying: We only worship the angels, and they are the daughters of Allah. And their saying: "They have no knowledge of it" means: what do those who say this say, meaning their saying "God took a son" means by Him who knows, and the 'ha' in His saying "by Him" is from the remembrance of God. The meaning of the statement is: these people who say this by God have no idea, that it is not permissible for Him to have a child from knowledge, so because of their ignorance and greatness of God, they said that. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Ishaq.
