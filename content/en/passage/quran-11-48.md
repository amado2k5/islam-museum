**Commentary on verse 48 (pp. 353-355)**

On [[passage/quran-11-48|11:48]] al-Tabari says: Noah, come down from the ark to the earth 'with peace from Us', that is, with safety from Us from Our destroying you and those with you, 'and blessings on you', and 'on nations from those with you', that is, on generations who will come from the descendants of those with you of your children; these are the believers among Noah's descendants for whom happiness went before from God and whom He blessed before He created them in their mothers' wombs and fathers' loins. Then He told Noah what He will do to the wretched among his descendants, saying 'and nations', that is, generations and groups, 'We shall give them enjoyment' in the life of this world, that is, provide for them in it what they enjoy until they reach their terms, 'then a painful punishment from Us will touch them', meaning We shall make them taste a painful, agonising punishment when they come to Us. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 353}

Report 18250, from Muhammad b. Ka'b al-Qurazi: into that peace entered every believing man and woman until the Day of Resurrection, and into that punishment and enjoyment entered every disbelieving man and woman until the Day of Resurrection; report 18251, from him, likewise. Report 18252, from Ibn Jurayj: 'and on nations from those with you' means those not yet born, for whom blessings are decreed because happiness went before for them in God's knowledge and decree, 'and nations We shall give enjoyment', those for whom wretchedness went before in God's knowledge and decree; report 18253, from him, likewise, adding that the children did not perish when Noah's people drowned for their fathers' sin, like the birds and beasts, but their term came with the drowning. Report 18254, from Ibn Zayd: they came down with God pleased with them, with peace from God, being people of mercy of that age, and then He brought from them offspring afterwards, nations, some of whom He had mercy on and some He punished; the nations divided from that company who came out of that water and were saved. Report 18255 begins at the foot of the page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 354}

Report 18255, from al-Dahhak: blessings on you and on nations of those with you who are not yet born; God made blessings binding for them because happiness had gone before for them in God's knowledge, 'and nations We shall give enjoyment' meaning the enjoyment of the life of this world, 'then a painful punishment from Us will touch them' because of the wretchedness that had gone before for them in God's knowledge. Report 18256, from al-Hasan, that when he recited Surat Hud and came to this verse he said: God saved Noah and those who believed and those who enjoyed perished, until he mentioned the prophets, saying of each, God saved him and those who enjoyed perished. Report 18257, from Ibn Zayd: 'then a painful punishment will touch them', after the mercy. Report 18258, from al-Hasan: that was when God sent 'Ad and sent Hud to them; some affirmed him and some denied him until God's command came, and then God saved Hud and those who believed with him and destroyed those who enjoyed; then He sent Thamud and sent Salih to them, and some affirmed him and some denied him until God's command came, and God saved Salih and those with him and destroyed those who enjoyed, and he went through the prophets one by one in this way. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 355}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
