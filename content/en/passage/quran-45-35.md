## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because you took the verses of Allah as a mockery and the worldly life deceived you, so that today they will not leave it, nor will they be reproached": They will be told: This is what has befallen you of Allah's punishment today "that you are" in this world "because you took the signs of Allah as a mockery," which are His proofs, evidences, and the verses of His Book revealed to His Messenger. "They mocked" meaning mocking them, "and the life of this world deceived you," meaning: and the adornments of the worldly life deceived you. So you preferred it over acting because it saves you today from Allah's punishment. Allah says: "So today they will not be taken out of it" from the Fire, "nor will they seek reproach." He means: nor will they return to the world to repent and repent for what they were punished for.
