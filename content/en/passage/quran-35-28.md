## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 27–28. Al-Tabari mentions that God sent down water from the sky, bringing forth fruits of various colors; from the mountains were new white and red of different colors; black crows; likewise, people, animals, and cattle of different colors; rather, God fears among His servants who are knowledgeable. Indeed, God is Exalted in Might, Forgiving.
