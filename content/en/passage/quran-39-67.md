## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 68 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Rather, worship Allah and be among the grateful": Do not worship what these polytheists among your people have commanded you to worship, O Muhammad, but worship Allah over all other gods, idols, and equals. "And be among the grateful" for Allah's favor upon you by the blessings He has bestowed upon you of guidance to worship Him, and disassociation from idol worship and idols. The accusative of the name Allah is made by the phrase 'Fa'ab', which comes after it, because it is a refutation of the word, even if it is implicitly accusative before it, as the Arabs used to say: Zayd, let him rise, and Zayd let him rise, nominative and accusative; the nominative is on 'Let Zayd look,' let him stand, and the accusative 'look at Zayd, let him stand,' is authentic and permissible. And His saying: 'And they did not appraise Allah as He deserves His measure,' Allah says: 'And how great is Allah, the true of His greatness,' these polytheists of Allah, who invite you to idol worship. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
