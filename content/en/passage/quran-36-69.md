## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 68–70. Al-Tabari explains that whoever Allah prolongs his life, we reverse, in creation, that is, we return him to the weakness of old age, so he knows nothing after knowledge. Do they not understand? And that Allah did not teach His Prophet poetry and what is appropriate for him; it is nothing but a remembrance and a clear Qur'an to warn those who are alive and to make the word right against the disbelievers.
