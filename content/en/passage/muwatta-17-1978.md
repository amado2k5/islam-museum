## Summary
Malik says: if a man bought goods, and it became binding on him, then another man said to him: share with me in half these goods, and I will sell them all for you, that is lawful and not harmful. Its explanation is that this is a new sale: he sold him half the goods on condition that he sells the other half for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.209}
