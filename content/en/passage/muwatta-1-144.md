## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on dry ablution for one in sexual impurity: if a man has a wet dream on a journey and has only enough water for ablution, and he is not thirsty until he reaches water, he should wash with that water his private parts and whatever impurity touched him, and then perform dry ablution with good dust, as God commanded. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.102}
