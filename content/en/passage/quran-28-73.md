## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And out of His mercy, He made for you the night and the day that you may rest therein and seek of His bounty, and perhaps you will be grateful": "And from His mercy" upon you, O people, He made for you the night and the day, so He made the difference between them, making this night darkness "that you may find peace in it," and that you may find peace and stability in it for the comfort of your bodies from the fatigue of your actions during the day. And in the 'ha' in His saying: 'so that you may find tranquility therein,' there are two aspects: the first is that it is specifically from the mention of the night, and another 'ha' is implied for the day along with seeking it. The second: that it is from the mention of night and day, so the reason for their unity is the meaning of the Arabs' monotheism in their saying: 'Your turning and your turning hurt me,' because 'iqbal' and 'idbarak' are verbs, and the verb is united in its many and few. And He made this day a light in which you can see, so you use your eyes in it for your livelihood and seeking its sustenance, which He has distributed among you by His favor which He has bestowed upon you.
