## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban, who said: two men of Ashja told me that Muhammad ibn Maslama al-Ansari used to come to them as collector and say to the owner: bring me the charity of your property. He would not lead to him a sheep in which there was a sufficient share of his due without his accepting it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.359}
