## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And they have certainly come to the town which had been rained with evil rain. Did they not see it? Rather, they did not hope for a good news": And indeed, those who took the Qur'an as a deserted city came upon the town that Allah had rained with evil rain, which is Sodom, the village of the people of Lot. And the rain of evil: it is the stones that Allah rained upon them and destroyed them with. Also: He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj: "And they came upon the town that had been sent down with a terrible rain," he said: Hajara, which is the village of the people of Lot, and its name is Sodom. Ibn Abbas said: Five villages, but God destroyed four, and the fifth remained, and its name is Sa'wah. Sa'wah was not destroyed. Its people did not do that work, and Sodom was the greatest, and it was the place where Lot settled and from which he was sent. Abraham used to call out to them with advice: O Sodom, a day from God forbade you from being subjected to God's punishment, claiming that Lot, the son of Abraham's nephew, In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
