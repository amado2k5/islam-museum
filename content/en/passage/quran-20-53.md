## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Who made the earth for you a cradle and traveled for you therein paths and sent down water from the sky and brought forth thereby pairs of various plants": The interpreters differed in the reading of the phrase "Mahda" (the cradle), and the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as: "Who made the earth your cradle with a kasra on the mif of mulch and an alif attached after the ha, and this is how they did it throughout the entire Qur'an." Some who chose to recite this also claimed that it was because 'mahd' is the noun of the place, and 'al-mudd' is the verb. He said: it is like 'furnishing' and 'al-farash'. Most Kufan reciters read this: 'mahdan' meaning: 'Who has prepared the earth for you as a cradle.' The correct view is to say: these are two extensive readings in the recitation of cities, well-known, so whichever the reader reads, the correct view is in them. And His saying: 'And He has made paths for you therein' means: 'And I will guide you through the earth on paths.' In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
