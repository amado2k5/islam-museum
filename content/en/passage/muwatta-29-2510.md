## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Muslim ibn Jundub, from Aslam, client of Umar ibn al-Khattab, that Umar ruled for a molar a camel, for the collarbone a camel, and for a rib a camel. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.431}
