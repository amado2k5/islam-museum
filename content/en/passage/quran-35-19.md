## Al-Tabari's commentary

God gives parables for the blind and the seeing, the darkness, the light, the shadow, the heat, the living and the dead, the unequal. Al-Tabari interprets the blind as one who is blind from the religion of Allah, the seeing as one who sees his right guidance and follows Muhammad, and the living and the dead as believers and disbelievers, and that God hears whom He wills, and that the Prophet is not in the hearing of those in the graves; indeed, he is nothing but a warner.
