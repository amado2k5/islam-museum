## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa that his father Urwa used to recite behind the imam in prayers in which the imam does not recite aloud, in the chapter on reciting behind the imam in silent prayers. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.138}
