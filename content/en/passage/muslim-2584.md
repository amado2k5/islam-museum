## Summary
A report of Jabir ibn Abdullah in the chapter on his prohibition of the call of the Jahiliyya, in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. After a quarrel, the Emigrants and the Ansar appealed to their tribes, and the Prophet called it the stinking call of the Jahiliyya. The hadith also teaches helping a brother who wrongs others by stopping him from injustice, and helping the wronged by supporting him. {cite:sahih-muslim}
