## Summary
Hadith of Ibn Abbas: "God has overlooked for my community error, forgetfulness and what they are forced into". An-Nawawi calls it good. Ibn Rajab explains that the chain via al-Awza'i is apparently sound, but Imam Ahmad rejected it and considered it known only as a mursal report from al-Hasan. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
