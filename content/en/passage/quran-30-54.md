## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Allah is the One who created you from weakness, then placed after weakness strength, then after weakness He placed weakness and gray hair. He creates whatever He wills, and He is the Knowing, the All-Powerful": Allah Almighty says to those who deny the resurrection among the polytheists of Quraysh, arguing against them that He is capable of that and of whatever He wills: "Allah who created you, O people, from weakness," meaning from a drop of fluid and water, and He created you into a sound human being, "then He made from weakness a strength," meaning: Then He made for you strength to act after He created you from weakness, and after your weakness, through childhood and childhood. "Then He made for you weakness and old age after strength," meaning: He introduced weakness for you in old age and old age compared to when you were strong in your youth and old age.
