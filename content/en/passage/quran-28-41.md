## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 42 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We made them leaders who invite to the Fire, but on the Day of Resurrection they will not be helped": And We made Pharaoh and his people leaders whom the people of arrogance against God and disbelievers in Him would bring forth, calling people to the deeds of the people of the Fire, "and on the Day of Resurrection they will not be helped." He, the Exalted, says: "And on the Day of Resurrection, He will not help them if Allah punishes them with a helper." They used to support each other in this world, but that support was destroyed that day. And His saying: "And We followed them in this world with a curse and on the Day of Resurrection," He says: And We imposed upon Pharaoh and his people in this world disgrace and wrath from Us against them, so We imposed for them destruction, destruction, and bad praise, while we will follow them another curse on the Day of Resurrection, humiliating them with constant disgrace and humiliating them with the necessary humiliation.
