## Summary
Malik said it reached him from Fadala ibn Ubayd al-Ansari, who was among the Companions of the Messenger, that he was asked whether a man who owes a neck may free the son of an adulterer. He said: yes, that is enough for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.330}
