## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And on the Day the Hour is established, the criminals will swear that they did not remain but an hour. Thus they used to deceive": And on the Day the Hour comes, and He will resurrect people from their graves, the criminals will swear, while they used to disbelieve in Allah in this world and earn sins. Their division is their oath by Allah. "They did not stay except for an hour": He says: they swear that they did not stay in their graves for more than one hour. Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: 'Thus in this world they used to lie.' He says: 'They lied in their speech and swore what you swore in this world, and they swore to lie while they knew it.'
