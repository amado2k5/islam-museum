## Summary
In the Book of Tafsir, at the Qur'anic verse in Surat al-Tawba: Abu Bakra's report of the Prophet's sermon on the day of sacrifice in Mecca during the Farewell Pilgrimage, which fixes the four sacred months and rejects the pre-Islamic practice of intercalation. It is among the established texts of the Farewell Sermon. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
