## Summary
The first report in the chapter on how to perform dry ablution. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' that he and Abdullah ibn Umar came from al-Jurf, and when they were at al-Mirbad, Abdullah dismounted, performed dry ablution with good dust, wiped his face and his hands to the elbows, and then prayed. The editor explains al-Mirbad as a place near Medina, and notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Ibn Wahb, Abd al-Razzaq, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq and Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted it from Salim and Nafi' from Ibn Umar in similar words. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.100} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.101}
