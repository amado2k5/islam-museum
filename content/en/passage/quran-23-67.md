## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 68 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "My verses used to be recited to you, but you were turning back on your heels": Allah Almighty says to these polytheists from Quraysh: Do not be disturbed today, for the wrath and punishment of Allah has descended upon you because of what your hands have earned and which you demanded by your disbelief in the signs of your Lord. "My verses used to be recited to you," meaning: the verses of the Book of Allah. He says: The verses of My Book were recited to you, but you would deny them and turn away from them when you heard them, out of disgust for hearing them. Likewise, it is said to anyone who returns from where he came: so-and-so retreated on his heel. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
