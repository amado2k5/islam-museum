## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Nadr, client of Umar ibn Ubayd Allah, from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman from Aisha, in the chapter on night prayer: she would sleep in front of the Prophet with her feet in the direction of his prayer place. When he prostrated he would nudge her, and she drew in her feet; when he stood she stretched them out again. She said that houses had no lamps in those days. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.173} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.174}
