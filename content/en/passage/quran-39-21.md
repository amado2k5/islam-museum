## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah sends down water from the sky, and He directs it as springs in the earth, then causes with it to produce plants of different colors, then stirs it and you see it yellowish, then He makes it into debris? Indeed, in that is a reminder for those of understanding": "Have you not seen," O Muhammad, "that Allah sends down water from the sky," which is rain, "and He sent it as springs in the earth?" He means: He caused it to flow as springs in the earth, singular as a spring, which is what flowed from the earth. In the passage, he quotes from Al-Hasan and Sufyan.
