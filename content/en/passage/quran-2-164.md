## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "in the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of night and day..." al-Tabari says God, by this verse, showed the polytheists where people of understanding find proof of His oneness: if they are ignorant or doubtful, let them reflect on the creation of the heavens and earth, the alternation of night and day, the ships, the water sent down from the sky, the creatures and the clouds; if their gods could create the like of any of it they would have an excuse, otherwise they have none. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 267} He then sets out the interpreters' differing reports on the occasion. Some said it was sent down as an argument against the polytheists when "your God is one God" was revealed and they asked what proof there was; from 'Ata', the unbelievers of Quraysh in Mecca asked how one God could suffice for all people, and it was revealed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 268} Others said it was revealed because the polytheists asked the Prophet for a sign; from Abu al-Duha they said, "if this is so, let him bring us a sign", and similarly from Ibn Jurayj from 'Ata' they said "show us a sign". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 268} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 269}
