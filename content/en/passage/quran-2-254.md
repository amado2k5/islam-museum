## Summary
Verse 254 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 254 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari comments on ([[passage/quran-2-254|2:254]]) from p. 382: the address is to the believers to give in God's cause from the wealth He provided, to give in charity and to pay the dues He made obligatory. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 382} From Ibn Jurayj (5760): the giving means obligatory alms and voluntary giving. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 382} On the next part al-Tabari says: store up for yourselves with God in this world by giving in His cause, by charity to the poor and needy and by paying what God made obligatory, and buy with it the honour He has prepared for His friends while you still have the means, before a day comes on which you will not be able to make that purchase, because it is a day of recompense, reward and punishment, not of work and earning. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 382} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 383} He says it is a day on which a close friend does not help as he did in this world by aiding against one who meant harm, since no one on the Day of Resurrection helps another against God, citing ([[passage/quran-43-67|43:67]]) that friends on that day will be enemies of one another except the God-fearing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 383} He says they also lack an intercessor, whereas in this world some interceded for others through kinship, neighbourliness or friendship, and cites the words of the people of Hell in ([[passage/quran-26-100|26:100]]-[[passage/quran-26-101|101]]). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 383} Al-Tabari holds that the verse is general in form on intercession but particular in intent, meaning the people of disbelief in God, since people of God's allegiance and faith do intercede for one another, as he showed earlier; this is his interpretation and choice. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 383} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 384}

From Qatada (5761): God knew that people love one another in this world and intercede for one another, but on the Day of Resurrection there is no friendship except that of the God-fearing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 384}

On the verse's close, al-Tabari says that the disbelievers are those who deny God and reject Him and His messengers, who put their denial in the wrong place, do what is not theirs to do and say what is not theirs to say, and refers to his earlier discussion of the sense of wrongdoing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 384} He takes this close as clear evidence for his view that the negation of friendship and intercession is aimed at the disbelievers: they are denied the help of friends and the intercession of kin, and God does not wrong them in this, since it is recompense for their disbelief in the world; rather they wrong themselves; this is his own argument. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 384} In the notes the editor corrects the wording between printed text and manuscript, explains the sense of the work 'ceasing' and the plural forms for friends, helpers and supporters; these are his. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 382} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 383}
