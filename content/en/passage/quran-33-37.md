## Summary
Verse 37 of [[passage/quran-33|Surat al-Ahzab]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/marriage-to-zaynab-bint-jahsh|Marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh]]. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And [remember] when you say to the one upon whom Allah has bestowed favor and bestowed favor upon him: 'Hold your spouse to you, fear Allah, and conceal within yourself what Allah has shown and fear the people. And Allah is more deserving that you should fear Him.' But when Zayd had completed a period of time, We married you to her so that there would be no blame upon the believers concerning the wives of their so-called husbands when they had completed their time, and the command of Allah was effective": Allah says: Allah says, may His remembrance, to His Prophet as a reproach from Allah for him. And, 'Remember, O Muhammad, when you say...' To the one whom Allah has blessed" with guidance, and "and I have bestowed upon him" by emancipation, meaning Zayd ibn Haritha, the freed slave of the Messenger of Allah: "Take hold of your husband and fear Allah." This is because Zaynab bint Jahsh, as mentioned, saw her and admired her while she was in the ropes of his master. He cast his dislike into Zayd's soul when Allah learned what had happened to His Prophet, so he wanted to separate from her. He mentioned this to the Messenger of Allah, Zayd, who said to him: "Hold your husband over you," meaning he wants her to be separated from him so he can marry her. "Fear God," and fear God in his duties toward your wife. "And conceal within yourself what God has revealed," meaning: "And hide in your heart the love of his separation from her so that you may marry her if he is a fugitive." In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
