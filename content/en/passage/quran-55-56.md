## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 57 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Among them are women whose gaze is limited; no man before them has touched or touched them": God Almighty says about these beddings whose linings are made of a spark, "women whose gaze is limited to their husbands, so they do not look at other men." In this section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
