## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 52 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And indeed, it is a reminder for the righteous” as meaning: This Qur’an is a reminder, that is, an admonition by which the righteous remember and take heed, those who fear God’s punishment by performing His obligatory duties and avoiding His prohibitions.
