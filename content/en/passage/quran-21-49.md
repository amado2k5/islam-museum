## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Those who fear their Lord in the unseen, and they are fearful of the Hour": We gave Moses and Aaron the Criterion, the reminder which We gave them to the righteous who fear their Lord unseen. It means that in this world, He will punish them in the Hereafter if they come to Him by neglecting what He has obligated them to do. Out of fear of Him, they observe His limits and obligations. From the hour when the Resurrection is established, they are fearful, wary that it will be established against them. They respond to their Lord having neglected their obligations to Allah, so He punishes them with punishment beyond what they can accept.
