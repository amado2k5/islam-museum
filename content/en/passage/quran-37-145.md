## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 145 to 148 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "If he had not been among those who glorify Allah": "Had it not been for that," meaning Yunus, "he was among those who prayed to Allah before the affliction he was afflicted with by the punishment of being imprisoned in the belly of the whale, to remain in his belly until the Day they are resurrected," meaning: he would have remained in the belly of the whale until the Day of Resurrection, the day when Allah will resurrect His creation imprisoned, but he was among those who remembered Allah before the trial, so Allah reminded him in times of affliction, saved him, and saved him. Interpreters have differed regarding the time of the glorification of Yunus by which God mentioned him, saying: "If he were not among the glorifying (glorified ones)"—some said something similar to what we said about that, and said something similar to what we said about the meaning of his saying: "among the glorifying (glorified ones)." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Rabi'.
