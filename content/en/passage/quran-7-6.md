## Summary
Verse 6 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 6 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "We shall ask those to whom messengers were sent, and We shall ask the messengers", God says: I shall ask the nations to whom I sent My messengers what they did with what the messengers brought them from Me of My command and prohibition: did they do what I commanded them, desist from what I forbade them and obey My command, or did they disobey Me and oppose it? "And We shall ask the messengers" means I shall ask the messengers I sent to the nations, did you convey My messages to them and deliver what I commanded you to deliver, or did you fall short and not convey them? So the commentators interpreted it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 305}

He gives (14324) from Ibn Abbas that God asks the people what they answered the messengers and asks the messengers what they conveyed; (14325) from him, on these verses up to "absent", that the record will be set down on the Day of Resurrection and speak of what they used to do; (14326) from al-Suddi that the sense is, we shall ask the nations what they did with what the messengers brought and ask the messengers whether they conveyed what they were sent with; and (14327) from Mujahid the like, and the note says the Abu Sa'd al-Madani in the chain is not known. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 306}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
