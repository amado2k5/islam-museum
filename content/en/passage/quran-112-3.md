## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 6 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, He is Allah, the One": it is mentioned that the polytheists asked the Messenger of God about the lineage of the Lord of Glory, and God revealed this surah in response to them. Some said: Rather, it was revealed because the Jews asked Him, and they said to him: This is God, who created creation? So it was revealed in response to them.
