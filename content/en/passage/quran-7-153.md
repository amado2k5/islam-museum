**Commentary on verse 153 (pp. 136-137)**

Al-Tabari says: this is a report from God that He accepts from every repentant one who returns to Him from a sin he committed, whether his disobedience was small or great, unbelief or otherwise, as He accepted from the calf-worshippers their repentance after their disbelief in worshipping the calf and their apostasy from their religion. He says: those who did evil deeds, then returned to seeking God's good pleasure by turning to what He loves from what He dislikes after their evil deeds, and believed that God accepts the repentance of sinners and turns to those who turn, with sincere hearts and certainty of that, their Lord after it is forgiving to them, that is, covering their evil deeds and not exposing them, and merciful to them and to all like them among the repentant. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 137}
