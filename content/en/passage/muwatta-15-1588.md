## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that a man had a slave-girl belonging to some people. He said to her family: deal with her as you wish. People took that to be one divorce. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.61}
