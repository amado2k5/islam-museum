## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We did not send before you except men to whom We revealed, so ask the people of knowledge if you do not know." He explains: And We did not send before you, O Muhammad, to a nation from among the nations to call to Our unification and to conclude to Our command and prohibition, except men from the children of Adam to whom We revealed and to them, not angels. He says: We sent to your people only like that which We sent to those before them of nations of their kind and according to their method. "So ask the people of remembrance." He says to the polytheists of Quraysh: 'And if you do not know that those whom We used to send to the nations before you are men from the children of Adam like Muhammad, and you say they are angels,' meaning you thought Allah had spoken to them before. "So ask the people of remembrance," meaning they have read the books before them: the Torah, the Gospel, and other books of Allah that He revealed to His servants. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
