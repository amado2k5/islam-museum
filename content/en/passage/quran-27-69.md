## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 69 to 70 in a single passage. He explains the verse "Say, 'Travel through the land and see how was the outcome of the criminals": "Say," O Muhammad, to these deniers what you have brought them of news from your Lord: 'Travel through the land and see.' To the dwellings of those before you who were deniers, the messengers of Allah, and their dwellings. Did not Allah destroy them, destroy their people by denying their messengers, and turn them back with their advice, so that their homes were empty and their deeds and traces were pardoned? That was the consequence of their crimes. This is the law of your Lord regarding everyone who follows their path in denying the messengers of their Lord. By Allah, Do that to you if you do not hasten to repent from your disbelief and denial of the Messenger of your Lord.
