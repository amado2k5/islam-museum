## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 10 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Iram of the Pillars" as referring to Alexandria. Abu Ja'far said it was Iram, while others said it was Damascus. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
