## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 22 to 23 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "To show you of Our greatest signs": "And clasp your hand, O Moses, to your wing; it will come out white without harm, so that we may show you of Our great proofs of our great authority and power." He said: the greatest, so he unified, and he said: "Among Our signs," just as He said: "To Him belong the Most Beautiful Names," and we explained this there. Some people of Basra used to say: It was said 'the greatest' because it was intended as a precedent, as if its meaning for him was: to show you the greatest of Our signs. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Ishaq.
