## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab used to pray voluntary prayers sitting with their legs drawn up to their bellies, in the chapter on seated voluntary prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.200}
