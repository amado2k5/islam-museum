## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And before it was the Book of Moses as a guide and a mercy, and this is a book confirming in Arabic language to warn those who did wrong, and as good tidings for the doers of good": And before this book was the Book of Moses, which is the Torah, a guide for the Children of Israel to believe in it, and a mercy for them that We sent down to them. And the speech that extracts the report from the Book without mentioning the full predicate suffices with the indication of the speech in its entirety and completeness: And before it is the Book of Moses as a leader and mercy which We sent down to him, and this is a Book which We revealed as an Arabic tongue. There is disagreement in the interpretation of this, and regarding the correct meaning "an Arabic tongue," the people of Arabic said: some Basra grammarians: the accusative of the tongue and the Arabic because it is an attribute of the book, so it is accusative in the case case or on an implicit verb, as if he said: I mean an Arabic tongue.
