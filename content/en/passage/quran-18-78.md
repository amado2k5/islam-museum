## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He said, 'This is a separation between me and you; I will inform you of the interpretation of what you could not bear patiently.'" He says, the Exalted, referring to the companion of Moses, this is what you said, which is His saying: "If You wish, I can take a reward for it," "a separation between me and you." He says: a separation between me and you: meaning a separation between me and you. "I will inform you," he means: I will inform you "of the interpretation of what you could not bear patiently for," meaning the interpretation of what you could not bear to do, meaning the outcome of my actions that you did and could not abandon the question and the reproach in it, be patient, and Allah knows best.
