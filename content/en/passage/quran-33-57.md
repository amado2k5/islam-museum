## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, those who harm Allah and His Messenger—Allah has cursed them in this world and the Hereafter and has prepared for them a humiliating punishment": He means by His saying: "Indeed, those who harm Allah" means those who harm their Lord by disobeying Him and riding what has been forbidden to them. It has been said that he meant by this the people of the images, because they aim to create a character like God's creation. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-A'mash.
