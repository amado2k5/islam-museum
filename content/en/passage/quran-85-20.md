## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 22 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "And Allah is encompassing them from behind" as meaning He is aware of their deeds, recording them all, nothing is hidden from Him, and He will recompense them for all of them. He then quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah in this section.
