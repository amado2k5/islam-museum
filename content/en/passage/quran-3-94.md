## Summary
Verse 94 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 94 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 94: inventing a lie about God.** Al-Tabari says the sense is: whoever lies about God, from among us and you, after your bringing the Torah and reciting it and your failing to find in it what you claimed of God's forbidding the veins and the flesh and milk of camels, those who do that are the unbelievers who say falsehood against God. Al-Muthanna relays from Amr ibn Awn, from Hushaym, from Zakariya, from al-Sha'bi that it came down about the Jews (7421). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 17}
