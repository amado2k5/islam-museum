## Summary
A report that Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi', in the chapter on opening the prayer: Abdullah ibn Umar, when he opened the prayer, raised his hands level with his shoulders, and when he raised his head from bowing he raised them less than that. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.126}
