## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Then indeed your Lord is for those who emigrated after they had emigrated after they had been tested and then strove hard and were patient. Indeed, your Lord after that is Forgiving and Merciful." Then your Lord, O Muhammad, is for those who emigrated from their homes, their dwellings, and their tribes from the polytheists, and they moved away from them to the homes, dwellings, and guardians of the people of Islam, after the polytheists who were among their backs before their migration from their religion, then they fought the polytheists afterward with their swords and tongues, disavowing them and from what they worship besides Allah, and they were patient in their striving. "Indeed, your Lord After that, Forgiving and Merciful." He says: Your Lord, after their action, is forgiving to them. He says: He is the One who conceals what they used to do when the polytheists gave them what they intended from them of the word of disbelief with their tongues, while they are implicitly for the other but believing in it. He is merciful to them to punish them for it while they turn to Allah and repent. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Ibn Jurayj.
