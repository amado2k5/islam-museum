## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As in the Book of Asceticism of Sahih Muslim: knowledge is removed by the death of scholars rather than by its being pulled away, and the fatwas of the ignorant are a sign of its going. It is among the best-known texts on the merit of scholars. {cite:sahih-muslim}
