## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the segment: "He said, 'And whoever despairs of the mercy of his Lord except those who are astray.' Ibrahim said to the guest: And whoever despairs of the mercy of Allah except those who have strayed from the right path and abandoned the right path by abandoning hope in Allah—and none of his hope fails—has thus preferred the religion of Allah. There is a disagreement among the readers regarding 'Yaqnut' with a fatha or kasra on the nun, and in the verse before it, he preferred reading 'Yaqnut' with a kasra.
