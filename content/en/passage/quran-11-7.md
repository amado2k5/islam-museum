**Commentary on verse 7 (pp. 244-250)**

On [[passage/quran-11-7|11:7]] al-Tabari says: God, to whom is your return together, people, 'is He who created the heavens and the earth in six days'; would the One who created them from nothing be unable to bring you back alive after He makes you die? It was also said that God created the heavens, the earth and what is in them in the six days, and the verse here is content to mention the creation of the heavens and the earth rather than that of what is in them. Report 17971, from Abu Hurayra: the Messenger of God took me by the hand and said: God created the soil on Saturday, the mountains in it on Sunday, the trees on Monday, the disliked thing on Tuesday, light on Wednesday, spread in it every kind of creature on Thursday, and created Adam after the afternoon on Friday, the last of creation, in the last hours of Friday, between the afternoon and night. The editor says this hadith is sound, related by Muslim and Ahmad, and that al-Tabari relates it in his history by other routes. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 244}

Report 17972, from Ibn Jurayj: He began creating the earth in two days and measured its provisions in two days; report 17973, from Ka'b: God began creating the heavens and the earth on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday and finished on Friday, creating Adam in the last hour of Friday, each day being a thousand years; report 17974, from al-Dahhak: of the days of the next world, each day measuring a thousand years; He began creating on Sunday and ended it on Friday, so it was called Jumu'a, and He ceased on Saturday and created nothing. The editor says some have tried to refute this hadith by opinion and to attack Abu Hurayra, that he considers this a way blamed by the people of knowledge, and that there is more to say about it than fits here. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 245}

His words 'and His throne was on the water' mean that His throne was on the water before He created the heavens, the earth and what is in them. Reports 17975, 17976 and 17977, from Mujahid: before He created anything; report 17978, from Qatada: your Lord tells you how the beginning of His creation was before He created the heavens and the earth, and report 17979 likewise. Report 17980, from Abu Razin al-Uqayli: I said, Messenger of God, where was our Lord before He created the heavens and the earth? He said: in a cloud, with air above it and air beneath it, then He created His throne on the water. The editor explains 'amaa' as cloud in Arab speech, quotes Abu Ubayd that it is taken in the sense of Arab speech while we do not know how that cloud was, and Ahmad via Yazid b. Harun as meaning there was nothing with Him, and gives the men of the chain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 246}

Report 17981, from Abu Razin, is to the same effect, and Ahmad, al-Tirmidhi, who calls it good, and Ibn Maja relate it. Report 17982, from Imran b. Husayn: a group came to the Messenger of God and he began to give them glad tidings and they said, give us, until that displeased him and they left; another group came and said we came to greet him, learn the religion and ask him about the beginning of this matter; he said, accept the glad tidings since those did not; they said we accept. He said: God was and nothing else with Him, His throne was on the water, He wrote in the Remembrance everything before all, then created seven heavens; then someone came to me and said your she-camel has gone, and I went out and the mirage cut off the view of her, and I wished I had left her. The editor says al-Bukhari and Ahmad relate it by sound routes, that al-Mas'udi was spoken against, and mentions al-Hakim's route through Buraydah al-Aslami. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 247}

Report 17983, from Ibn Abbas on 'His throne was on the water': God's throne was on the water, then He made a garden for Himself and another below it, and covered them with one pearl, which are those of [[passage/quran-55-62|55:62]], the ones of which no soul knows what is hidden for them of comfort to the eyes, as in [[passage/quran-32-17|32:17]]; the creatures do not know what is in them, and each day a gift comes to them from it. Reports 17984, 17985 and 17986, from Sa'id b. Jubayr: Ibn Abbas was asked on what the water was, and said on the back of the wind. Report 17987, from Damra: His throne was on the water, He created the heavens and the earth with truth, and created the pen and wrote with it what He would create and what would come to be; that writing glorified God and extolled Him for a thousand years before He created any creature. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 249}

Report 17988, from Wahb b. Munabbih: the throne existed before God created the heavens and the earth; then He took a handful from the clear part of the water, opened the handful and smoke rose, and He made them seven heavens in two days; then He took a clay from the water and set it in the place of the House, spread the earth from it, created the provisions in two days, the heavens in two days and the earth in two days, and finished the last of creation on the seventh day. His words 'to test which of you is best in deed' mean He is the one who created the heavens and the earth, people, and created you in six days to try you, to see which of you is best in obedience to Him. Report 17989, from Abd Allah b. Umar, begins at the foot of the page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 250}
