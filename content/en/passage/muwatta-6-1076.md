## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki that he saw the House empty after the dawn prayer and after the afternoon prayer, with no one circumambulating it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1299), Suwayd (555) and al-Shaybani (439) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.496}
