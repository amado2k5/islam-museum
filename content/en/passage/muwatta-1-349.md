## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on repeating prayer with the imam. Yahya transmits from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Busr ibn Mihjan al-Dili from his father Mihjan: he was in a gathering with the Prophet when the call to prayer was given. The Prophet stood and prayed, then returned, and Mihjan remained in his place without praying with him. The Prophet asked what kept him from praying with the people, and was he not a Muslim man. He replied that he had prayed in his household, and the Prophet said: "When you come, pray with the people even if you have prayed". The editor cites al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Hakim, al-Tabarani, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.193}
