## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We did not send before you any messengers except that they ate food and walked in the markets. And We made some of you a trial for others. Will you be patient? And your Lord is ever Seeing": This is a protest from Allah, the Exalted, to His Prophet against the polytheists of His people who said: "What is this Messenger to eat food and walk in the markets?" And his response to them is that He, the Exalted, says to them: "And what do those who deny, O Muhammad, are those who say, 'What is this Messenger eating and walking in the markets? Who is it that you eat food and walk in the markets? And you are a Messenger to Allah.' They know that I did not send before you any messengers except that they eat food and walk in the markets like you eat and walk, so they have no authority over what they said about that. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Al-Hasan.
