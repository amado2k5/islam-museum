## Summary
Malik transmits from Musa ibn Abi Tamim, from Abu al-Hubab Said ibn Yasar, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger said: dinar for dinar, dirham for dirham, with no excess between them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.157}
