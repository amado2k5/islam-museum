## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from al-A'raj, that Umar ibn al-Khattab recited Surat al-Najm and prostrated in it, then stood and recited another surah. The editor notes that this is the chain Yahya ibn Yahya gave, "from al-A'raj, that Umar", while other Muwatta copies, Abu Mus'ab (261), Suwayd (97), al-Shaybani (268) and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi, say "from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra, that Umar". {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.283}
