## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Rather, they denied the Hour, and We have prepared for those who deny the Hour a blazing fire": These polytheists denied the truth you brought them, O Muhammad, because you eat food and walk in the markets, but because they do not believe in the Hereafter and do not believe in reward and punishment, denying the resurrection and God raising the dead alive for the resurrection. "And We have prepared for them" means: And We have prepared for those who deny that Allah will raise the dead alive after their destruction for the Day of Judgment, a fire that will roam and ignite against them. "When it sees them from a distant place," it means: if it sees this fire that we have prepared for these deniers from a distant place, it will anger them, and that is when it boils and boils. It is said: So-and-so is angry at so-and-so, and that is when he became angry with him, and his chest boiled from anger, and it became clear in his speech. And a sigh, which is her voice. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
