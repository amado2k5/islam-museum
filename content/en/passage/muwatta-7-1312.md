## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that he heard a man ask Abdullah ibn Abbas about the spoils (anfal). Ibn Abbas said: the horse is part of the spoils, and the plunder is part of the spoils. The man kept asking until he almost embarrassed him; Ibn Abbas said: do you know what the like of this is? It is like Subay al-Dhari, whom Umar ibn al-Khattab beat. The editor notes that Subay ibn Asal came to Medina asking about the ambiguous verses of the Qur'an, and Umar beat him for that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.586}
