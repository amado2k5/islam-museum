## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not deprive people of their things and do not spread corruption on the earth": meaning by the phrase "weigh with justice" and weigh with the "upright" scale in which there is no diminution for the one for whom you weigh "and do not deprive people of their things" means: do not diminish people's rights in measure and weight, "and do not spread corruption on the earth," meaning: do not spread corruption on the earth.
