## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Have you seen if he denies and turns away?” is explained as: “Have you seen if Abu Jahl denies the truth with which Muhammad was sent, and turns away?” meaning: he turns away from him and does not believe in him. The passage also quotes statements from Qatadah.
