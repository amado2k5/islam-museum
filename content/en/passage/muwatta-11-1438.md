## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Abdullah ibn Qusayt from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Thawban from his mother from Aisha, that the Messenger of God ordered that the hides of dead animals be used once tanned. The editor notes that in the report of Abu Mus'ab, al-Nasa'i and al-Tamhid, the chain reads "from Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Thawban from his father from Aisha". {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.643}
