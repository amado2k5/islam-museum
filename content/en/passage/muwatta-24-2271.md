## Summary
Malik says it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab was asked about a slave who had a child by a free woman: to whom does their clientage belong? He said: if their father dies while he is still a slave and not freed, their clientage belongs to the clients of their mother. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.337}
