## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd ibn Qays al-Makki that a contract of manumission slave of Ibn al-Mutawakkil died in Mecca with some of his contract still owed and debts to people, and left a daughter. The governor of Mecca wrote to Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan asking him, and he ordered that the debts to people be paid first, then what remains of the contract, then the rest of his property divided between his daughter and his patron. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.344}
