## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who believe in Allah and His messengers—those are the truthful and the martyrs with their Lord; they will have their reward and their light. But those who disbelieve and deny Our verses—those are the companions of Hell." And regarding His saying: "And the martyrs are with their Lord," the interpreters differed on this. Some said: The martyrs with their Lord are separate from those before it, and the report is about those who believed in God and His messengers, and it is not mentioned when He says: "the truthful." The truthful are nominative by saying: they are. Then the predicate about the martyrs began, and it was said: The martyrs with their Lord have their reward and light, and the martyrs in their words are raised by the saying: "They have their reward and their light." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Sufyan.
