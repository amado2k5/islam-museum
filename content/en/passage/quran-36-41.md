## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 41 to 44 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And a sign for them: We carried their descendants in the charged ark": and also a sign for them, and a sign of our ability to do whatever We will. We carried their descendants, meaning those who survived from the descendants of Adam in Noah's ark, and He, Glorified be He, meant by the charged ship. The ark is the ship, and the loaded is the dignified ship. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
