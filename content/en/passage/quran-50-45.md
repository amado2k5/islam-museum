## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "We know best what they say, and you are a compeller over them." So mention in the Qur'an those who fear a warning.' It means: We are, O Muhammad, most knowledgeable of what these polytheists say about their oppression against Allah, their denial of His signs, and their denial of Allah's power to resurrect after death. "And You are not a tyrant over them," meaning: You are not a tyrant over them. Also: Ibn Abi Nujayh narrated from Mujahid: "And You are not a compeller over them," he said: Do not compel them against them. And from Qatadah, His saying: "And you are not a tyrant over them," for Allah, the Almighty, hated Jabbar, forbade it, and gave precedence to it. Al-Farra' said: Al-Jabbar was placed in the position of the ruler in Jabriyyah and said: Al-Mufaddal recited to me: 'And on the day of sorrow, when I gathered Ma'ad, and the people were except us, we disobeyed the determination of Al-Jabbar until we became a thousand of our teachers. It is narrated that Al-Jawf' is 'Al-Jawf,' and he said: By 'Jabbar' he meant the warner of His guardianship. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
