**Commentary on verse 97 (pp. 204-205)**

On [[passage/quran-10-97|10:97]] al-Tabari says: 'they do not believe, even if every sign came to them' means they do not affirm God's proofs, nor acknowledge their Lord's oneness nor that you are God's messenger, even if every sign, admonition and lesson came to them and they saw them, until they see the painful punishment, as Pharaoh and his chiefs did not believe when the word of your Lord was fulfilled against them until they saw the painful punishment, and then he said 'I believe that there is no god but the One in whom the Children of Israel believe' in [[passage/quran-10-90|10:90]] when his saying did not help him. So these against whom your Lord's word has been fulfilled among your people, of the worshippers of idols and others, will not believe in you and follow you except at the time when their belief will not benefit them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 205}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
