## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 30 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and they say, 'When is this conquest, if you are truthful?'" He says, 'And these polytheists in Allah, O Muhammad, you have 'when is this conquest?' There is disagreement about the meaning of this, and some said: It means: when will this judgment come between us and you, and when will this reward and punishment be? In this passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
