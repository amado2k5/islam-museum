## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains his statement, “O you who disbelieved, make no excuses today. You are only being recompensed for what you used to do.” God Almighty says, informing us of what He will say on the Day of Resurrection to those who denied His Oneness in this world, “O you who disbelieved” in God, “make no excuses today. You are only being recompensed for what you used to do.” He says: It will be said to them: You are only being rewarded today, and that is on the Day of Resurrection, and you are being given the recompense for your deeds that you used to do in this world, so do not seek excuses for them.
