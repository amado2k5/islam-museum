## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 58 to 61 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Are we not in death": Allah Almighty says, informing us about the saying about this believer whom Allah gave what He gave of his dignity in Paradise as a pleasure from what He gave him therein: "Are we not at death except our first death?" He says: Are we not at two deaths other than our first death in this world? "And we are not punished"—he means: "And we are not punished after entering Paradise." "Indeed, this is the great attainment." He says: This is what God has given us of honor in Paradise. We neither suffer nor die. It is the great salvation from what we used to warn of in this world, warn of God's punishment, and realize what we were in. We hope in our faith and obey our Lord, as He said: "Are we not dead"...
