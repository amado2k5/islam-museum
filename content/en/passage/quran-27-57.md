## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So We saved him and his family except his wife; We decreed her among those who were absent." So We saved Lot and his family except his wife from Our punishment when We made him lawful for them. Then "We decreed for her," meaning: We made her by Our decree "among the abstained" among the remaining. "And We rained upon them," which is the rain God sent upon them from the sky like stones of the sage. "So evil is the rain of the warners." He says: "So evil is the rain of the people whom God warned of punishment for their disobedience to Him, and their fear of His greatness by sending the Messenger to them with that."
