## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Dawud ibn al-Husayn from Ibn Yarbu' al-Makhzumi, that he heard Zayd ibn Thabit say the middle prayer is the noon prayer, in the chapter on the middle prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and that it is transmitted raised from Urwa from Zayd ibn Thabit in Ahmad, Abu Dawud (411) and al-Nasa'i in al-Kubra, with a sound chain. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.201}
