## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 153 to 157 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Or do you have clear authority": Do you have a proof whose validity is proven to those who heard it with the truth of what you say, as: and from Qatadah, "Or do you have clear authority": meaning a clear excuse. And from Al-Suddi, regarding his saying: "clear authority," he said it is a proof. And His saying: "So bring your Book" means: Bring your proof from a Book that came to you from God that what you say is that he has daughters and you have sons, as you say. In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
