## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari describes God creating the heavens and the earth, sending rain that brings forth fruit as sustenance, subjecting ships to sail the sea by His command, and making the rivers flow for drinking. He reports Mujahid's view that the rivers are in every land. He concludes that only the Creator, not idols, deserves worship.
