## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari says: Allah is the One who created the heavens and the earth from nothing, and sent down rain from the sky and revived trees and crops, and they bore fruit as sustenance for people to eat. And "He subjected the ships to you," meaning ships "to sail through the sea by His command," for you, whom you board and carry your goods from one town to another. And "He subjected the rivers for you" means their water is a drink for you, and it is narrated from Mujahid that rivers are in every land. Then he cites the verse: The one who deserves worship and sincere obedience is one of these attributes, not one who cannot harm or benefit himself or other idols and gods worshipped by the polytheists.
