## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38-40 as a single section. He explains the phrase “a reward from your Lord, a gift, a reckoning”: By His words, may His praise be exalted, “a reward from your Lord, a gift,” God has given these righteous people what is described in these verses as a reward from your Lord for their deeds, for their obedience to Him in this world. His words, “a gift,” mean that this reward is a favor from God upon them, for He rewarded them tenfold for each deed in some instances, and seven hundredfold for each deed in others. This increase, though a reward, is a gift from God. His words, “a reckoning,” mean that they will be held accountable for their deeds to God in this world. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
