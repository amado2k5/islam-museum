## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We give life and cause death, and to Us is the final destination": Indeed, We give life to the dead and cause the living to die, and to Us is the outcome of all of them on the Day of Resurrection "the Day the earth will split for them hastily." He says, Glorified be He, "and to Us is their fate on the Day the earth splits open." And His saying: "The earth will split open for them" means: the earth will crack from them. And His saying 'quickly' is in the accusative accusative as a form of the hal from the letter 'ha' and 'm' in their saying. The meaning is: the day the earth will split open for them and they will leave it quickly, so he sufficed with the indication of the phrase: 'the day the earth will split open for them,' based on what he mentioned. And His saying: 'That is gathered for us, easy,' means: that gathering them together in the position of reckoning, easy for us.
