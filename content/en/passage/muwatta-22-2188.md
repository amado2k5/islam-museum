## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Ibn Shihab, from Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf and Muhammad ibn al-Numan ibn Bashir, that they told him that al-Numan ibn Bashir said: his father Bashir brought him to the Messenger and said: I have given my son this slave that I had. The Messenger said: have you given all your children a gift like this? He said no. The Messenger said: then take it back. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.298}
