## Summary
Verse 88 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 88 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "that is God's guidance by which he guides whom he will of his servants; had they associated others, what they used to do would have come to nothing" al-Tabari says: by "that is God's guidance" he means this guidance with which I guided those I named of prophets and messengers, giving them success by it in hitting the true religion by which they gained their Lord's pleasure, honour in this world and favour in the next, is "God's guidance", that is, God's granting success and kindness with which he gives success to whom he will and is kind to whom he loves of his creatures until he turns back to obeying God, making work pure for him, affirming his oneness and rejecting idols and images; "had they associated others, what they used to do would have come to nothing" says: had these prophets we named associated others with their Lord and worshipped another beside him, "would have come to nothing" says it would have been void and the reward of their deeds they used to do would have gone from them, since God accepts no work along with associating others with him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 514}

The editor refers for "guidance" and "coming to nothing" to earlier passages.

Verse 88 closes here on p. 514, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-89|6:89]].
