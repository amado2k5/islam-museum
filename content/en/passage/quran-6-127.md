## Summary
Verse 127 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 127 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 127, "for them is the abode of peace with their Lord, and He is their ally for what they used to do": by "for them" He means the people who remember God's signs, take warning from them, and are certain of their indication of God's oneness, Muhammad's prophethood and more, so they affirm what they came to know. The "abode of peace" is God's abode that He prepared for His friends in the hereafter as repayment for their trials in this world for God's sake, and it is His garden; "al-Salam" is one of God's names, and he gives (13884) al-Suddi: God is al-Salam and the abode is paradise. "He is their ally" means God is the helper of those who remember His signs, "for what they used to do" as repayment for their obedience to God and pursuit of His good pleasure. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 114}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
