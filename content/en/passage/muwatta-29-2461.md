## Summary
Malik said: the agreed practice among us is that camels are not accepted for blood-money from the people of towns, nor gold and silver from the people of the desert (al-amud); nor silver from the people of gold, nor gold from the people of silver. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.418}
