## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 30–33. Al-Tabari mentions that God granted David Solomon the best of servants; he repented, as He presented him in the evening with the safinat of the horses, saying: I loved the love of good over the remembrance of my Lord until it was hidden behind the veil. He also mentions the difference among interpreters regarding the meaning of what he did to the horses.
