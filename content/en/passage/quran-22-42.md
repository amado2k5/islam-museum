## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 42 to 44 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And if they deny you, indeed before them the people of Noah, 'Aad, and Thamud denied": God Almighty says, comforting His Prophet Muhammad from the harm inflicted on him by the polytheists and urging him to be patient with what befalls him of cursing and denial. And if these polytheists deny you, O Muhammad, regarding what You have brought them of truth and proof, and what you have promised them of punishment for their disbelief in Allah, then that is the way of their brothers from the empty nations who deny Allah's messengers and their path before them. So let them not prevent you, for the humiliating punishment is behind them and I will help you and your followers against them because of that, just as My punishment came upon their predecessors among the nations before them after delay until their appointed terms were reached. "Indeed, I denied before them" means the polytheists of Quraysh, the people of Noah, the people of 'Ad and Thamud, the people of Abraham, the people of Lot, the people of Midian, and the people of Shu'ayb. He says: All these were the ones whose messengers lied.
