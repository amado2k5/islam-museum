## Summary
Yahya said Malik said: it reached him that Sulayman ibn Yasar and others were asked about a man who had received the prescribed punishment for slander: is his testimony admissible? They said yes, if repentance is evident in him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.262}
