## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on the mercy and compassion of believers, in Sahih Muslim. He interlaced his fingers as an example of the strength of the believers' bond. It is among the best-known similes of brotherhood in the collections. {cite:sahih-muslim}
