## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "A surah We have sent down and made obligatory and We have revealed in it clear verses that you may remember": Abu Ja'far said: He means by His saying, the Exalted: "A surah We have revealed," and this surah We have revealed. We only said the meaning of this is the same, because the Arabs rarely begin with indefinite indefinites before their predicates if they are not an answer, because they are connected as 'the one who connects,' and then they are reported with a predicate other than connection. Therefore, it is disliked to begin with them before the predicate if it is not connected, as its predicate becomes like a connection to her, and the listener's predicate becomes as expected to be her news after it, since the predicate after it is like a connection to her. If the predicate begins with a predicate before it, no doubt arises for the listener regarding the speaker's intent. We have previously explained that the surah describes what was raised with its evidences, so it is necessary to repeat it here. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
