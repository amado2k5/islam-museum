## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, who said: the Messenger of God forbade two kinds of clothing and two kinds of sale: the touching garment and the throwing garment; and that a man should sit with his knees raised, wrapped in one garment with nothing covering his private parts; and that a man should wrap himself in one garment covering only one of his shoulders. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.503}
