## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So be patient with what they say and glorify the praise of your Lord before the sunrise and before sunset": So be patient, O Muhammad, against what these Jews say, and what they slander and lie about God, for God is watching over them. "And glorify the praise of your Lord before the sun rises," meaning: Pray the morning prayer before sunrise and the afternoon prayer before sunset. Also: And from Qatadah, "And glorify the praise of your Lord before sunrise" refers to the Fajr prayer, and before sunset: Asr. Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase: "And glorify the praise of your Lord before sunrise and before sunset," before sunrise: morning, and before sunset: Asr. And regarding the phrase: "And from the night, glorify Him." The interpreters differed regarding the glorification commanded at night; some said it meant the prayer of darkness. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
