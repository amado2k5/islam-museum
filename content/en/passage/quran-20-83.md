## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 83 to 84 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And what hastened you from your people, O Moses": "And what hastened you?" And what hastened you "from your people, O Moses," that you preceded them and succeeded them behind you, and you were not with them? "He said, 'They are following in my footsteps,' meaning: My people follow my footsteps and follow me." "And I hastened to You, my Lord, that You may be pleased," meaning: I hastened, and my Lord preceded them, so that You would be pleased with Me. Indeed, Allah, the Exalted, said to Moses: What hastened you from your people? Because He exalted is His praise, as far as we have heard, when He saved him and the Children of Israel from Pharaoh and his people, crossed the sea with them, and considered them on the right side of the mountain. Moses hastened to his Lord, and Aaron of the Children of Israel stood up, leading them in Moses' footsteps. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd and Ibn Ishaq.
