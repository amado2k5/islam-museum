## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when Allah alone is mentioned, the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter are distressed; and when those besides Him are mentioned, they rejoice": And when Allah, Glorified and Exalted, dedicates His praise to remembrance, He alone is called upon and it is said, 'There is no god but Allah,' the hearts of those who do not believe in resurrection and resurrection after death are disgusted. What he meant by His saying: "I was disgusted" means that they were disgusted by the oneness of Allah. "And when He mentions those besides Him," he says: And when He mentions the gods they call upon besides Allah along with God, it is said: Those are the highest mountains, and their intercession is hoped. Thus, those who do not believe in the Hereafter rejoice in it and rejoice, as Qatadah said: "And when Allah alone is mentioned, the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter become awe-inspiring," meaning their hearts become awe-inspiring and arrogant. "And when He mentions those besides Him," the gods "when they rejoice." The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
