## Summary
Malik transmits from Umarah ibn Sayyad that Ata ibn Yasar told him that Abu Ayyub al-Ansari told him: we used to sacrifice one sheep, which a man slaughtered for himself and his household, and then people began to boast about it until it became a boast. The editor notes that the correct name of the narrator is Umarah ibn Sayyad. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.625}
