## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Or who is it that provides for you if He withholds His provision? Nay, but they persist in defiance and aversion,” is explained as follows: Or who is it that feeds you, gives you drink, and provides for your sustenance if He withholds His provision from you? And His statement, “Nay, but they persist in defiance and aversion,” means: Rather, they persist in transgression, aversion to the truth, and arrogance. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
