## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 42 to 43 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then We established after them other generations": Then We created after the destruction of Thamud other peoples. And His saying: "No nation precedes its term" means: the destruction of a nation that We established after Thamud does not precede before the time We postponed for its destruction, nor does its destruction delay the term We postponed for its destruction or the time we have set for its annihilation, but it perishes for its coming. This is a warning from God to the polytheists of our Prophet Muhammad's people, and a warning from Him to delay it in their appointed times while disbelieving in it and denying His Messenger, so that they may reach the appointed time and His punishment will befall them, just as He did among those before them among the previous nations.
