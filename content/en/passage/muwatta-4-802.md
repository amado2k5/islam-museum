## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the strictness regarding kissing for the fasting person. It reached Malik that Aisha, wife of the Prophet, when she mentioned that the Messenger of God used to kiss while fasting, would say: and who among you has more control of himself than the Messenger of God? The editor notes that this report is relayed, and that the Musnad al-Jami refers to hadiths on this matter. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.394}
