**Commentary on verse 158: God raised Jesus to Himself (p. 378)**

Al-Tabari says that 'God raised him to Himself' means God raised the Messiah to Him: they did not kill him or crucify him, but God raised him to Himself and purified him from those who disbelieved. He has explained earlier how God raised him, and set out the disputes on it and the sound view with its proofs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 378}

As for 'God was mighty and wise', it means that God has always been an avenger on His enemies, as He took vengeance on those struck by the thunderbolt and those cursed for breaking the covenant and disbelieving His signs; 'wise' means full of wisdom in His direction and disposal of creatures. Those who ask Muhammad to send down a book from heaven should beware, lest God's punishment fall on them as it fell on their forefathers who did as they did in denial and slander against God's friends. Report 10793, from Ibn Abbas through Sa'id ibn Jubayr, says the phrase means it is so, that God has always been mighty and wise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 378}
