## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar divorced a wife of his who lived in a house of Hafsa, wife of the Prophet. His route to the mosque was past it, so he took another road behind the houses, disliking to ask permission to enter, until he took her back. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.93}
