## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 27 as one section. He explains His saying, “But as for him who is given his record in his left hand, he will say, ‘Oh, I wish I had not been given my record!’”: But as for him who is given on that Day the record of his deeds in his left hand, he will say: Oh, I wish I had not been given my record. And in the section he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
