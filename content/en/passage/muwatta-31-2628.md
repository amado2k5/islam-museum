## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Ali ibn Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, that the Messenger of God said: part of the goodness of a person's Islam is that he leaves what does not concern him. The editor notes that a group of Muwatta transmitters narrated it from Malik with a broken chain; Khalid ibn Abd al-Rahman and Musa ibn Dawud narrated from Malik with a connected chain; and the broken version is better known and more common according to Ibn Abd al-Barr. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.487}
