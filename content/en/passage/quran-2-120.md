## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "the Jews will never be pleased with you, nor the Christians, until you follow their creed" al-Tabari says: the Jews, Muhammad, will never be pleased with you, nor the Christians, so leave off seeking what pleases and agrees with them and turn to seeking God's pleasure in calling them to the truth He sent you with, for what you call them to is the way to be gathered with you in harmony and the upright religion, and there is no way to please them by following their creed since Judaism is the opposite of Christianity and Christianity of Judaism, and they cannot be joined in one person at one time; the Jews and Christians would agree in pleasure with you only if you were a Jewish Christian, which can never be from you, so since there is no way to please both parties, hold fast to God's guidance, to harmony upon which there is a way for all creation. "Milla" is religion, plural "milal". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 562} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 563}

On "say: God's guidance is the guidance" he says: say to these Christians and Jews who said none will enter Paradise but Jews or Christians, that God's exposition is the persuasive exposition and the decisive judgement between us, so come to God's Book and His exposition, which is the Torah that you all admit is from God, and it will be clear to you which of us is in the right and which in error, which of us are people of Paradise and which of the Fire; God ordered His Prophet to call them to this because it contains the denial of what the Jews and Christians said and the account of Muhammad's affair. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 563}

On "if you followed their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you would have from God no protector or helper" he says: if you followed, Muhammad, the desire of these in what pleases them with you, of turning Jew or Christian, after the knowledge that came to you of their error and their disbelief in their Lord and after the account I related to you of their news in this sura, you would have from God no protector to manage your affair nor helper to aid you and ward off what comes to you of His punishment. He mentions that some said God revealed this verse because the Jews and Christians called him to their religions and each party said guidance is what we hold, so God warned him against doing so and taught him the decisive argument between them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 563} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 564}
