## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And rely upon Allah and sufficient is Allah as Disposer of affairs": Entrust your affairs to Allah, O Muhammad, and trust in Him. "And sufficient is Allah as Disposer of affairs," meaning: He makes you feel that Allah is your guardian and guardian in what He commands you.
