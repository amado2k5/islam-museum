## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Amr in the Book of Faith: the answer to the question "which Muslims are best?" is that they are those whom other Muslims are safe from by tongue and hand. It is one of the most specific definitions of practical Islam in the Sahih. {cite:sahih-muslim}
