**Commentary on verse 55 (p. 21)**

Al-Tabari says: the worst of what crawls on the earth in God's sight are those who disbelieved in their Lord, denied His oneness and worshipped other than Him; "they do not believe" means they do not accept God's messengers or acknowledge His revelation and sending down. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of "creature". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 21}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
