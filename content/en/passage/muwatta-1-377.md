## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on permission to pray in a single garment: he prefers that a man who prays in a single shirt put a cloth or turban on his shoulders. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.204}
