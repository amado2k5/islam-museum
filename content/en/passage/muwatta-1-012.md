## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Rabi'a ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, closing the chapter on the times of prayer: al-Qasim found the people of Medina praying noon only after the decline, when the day leaned towards evening; that is, they cooled it. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab al-Zuhri and Suwayd ibn Sa'id transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.40}
