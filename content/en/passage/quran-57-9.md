## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "It is He who sends down clear signs to His servant to bring you out of darkness into light. Indeed, Allah is to you Kind and Merciful." This means that Allah is the One who sends down to His servant Muhammad "clear signs," meaning detailed ones, "to bring you out of darkness into light." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: "He will bring you, O people, out of the darkness of disbelief into the light of faith, and from misguidance to guidance." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
