## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And they swore by Allah with the utmost effort of their oaths, 'If I had commanded them to go out, say, 'Do not swear [by Allah], [with all due obedience]. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do.": And those who turned away from the judgment of Allah and His Messenger swore when they called upon Him, "By Allah, with the effort of their oaths," meaning: their oaths are the strongest and strongest: "If you command them, O Muhammad, to go out to fight your enemy and the enemy of the believers, 'to go out, say, do not swear.' Do not swear, for this is a known obedience from you, and it contains denial." Also: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "Say, do not swear by a known obedience," he said: Your obedience has become known to me that you are lying. "Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do," meaning: Allah is Expert in what you do—whether you obey Allah and His Messenger, or disagree with them or any other matter of yours. Nothing of that is hidden from Him, and He repays you for all of that. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
