## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "that is because God sent down the Book with the truth, and those who differ about the Book are in a far-off schism" al-Tabari gives the views on what "that" refers to. Some said it is their act, their boldness toward the punishment and their concealment, because God sent down the Book with the truth, which is His report about them in "it is the same for those who disbelieve whether you warn them or not". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 334} Others said it is known to them because the Book reported it and the Book is true; others that it is the punishment for their disbelief. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 335} Al-Tabari chooses that "that" points to all in the verses from "those who conceal" to here, the acts of the Jewish rabbis and what was prepared for them: I did that to them because I sent down My Book with the truth and they disbelieved in it and differed about it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 335} "Those who differ about the Book" are the Jews and Christians: the Jews disbelieved in what God told of the story of Jesus and his mother, the Christians believed some and disbelieved some, and all disbelieved in the order sent down to believe Muhammad; "far-off schism" is a distant enmity, from al-Suddi. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 336}
