**Commentary on verse 6 (pp. 24-25)**

He then explains "In the alternation of night and day and in what God created in the heavens and the earth are signs for people who are God-fearing": God draws His servants' attention to where the proof of His lordship lies and that He is the creator of all below Him: in the succession of night and day, one going as the other comes, and in what He created in the heavens, sun, moon and stars, and on earth of wonders of creation that show it has a maker like whom nothing is, there are proofs, evidences and clear marks for people who fear God, dread His threat and are afraid of His punishment, and so are sincere in worshipping their Lord. The editor refers to the gloss on "alternation of night and day" in the third volume. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 24}

He raises an objection: is there no proof of the Maker in what God created in the heavens and the earth except for the one who fears God? He answers that the proof is plain to anyone whose natural disposition is sound and whose heart is free of defects; the statement does not mean that the proof is only for one who has already taken God-fearing to heart, but that in this are signs for one who fears God's punishment and whose desire has not led him against the truth made plain to him, because it shows everyone of sound disposition that he has a director to whom he owes submission in servitude, and not to the other gods and rivals. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 25}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
