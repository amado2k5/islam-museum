## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 34–35. Al-Tabari mentions that God tested Solomon and cast a body onto his throne, and he cites the narrations of interpreters that he was a devil in the form of a human and in his name, then Solomon repented.
