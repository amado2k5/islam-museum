## Summary
The first report in the chapter on zakat on buried treasure. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab and from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman, both from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: one-fifth is due on buried treasure. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Ibn Hibban, al-Bughawi, Ishaq al-Tabba in Muslim, Khalid ibn Makhlad in Ibn Khuzayma, Ibn Wahb in al-Tahawi and al-Daraqutni, al-Tinisi in al-Bukhari, Qutayba in al-Nasai, al-Shaybani, and Yahya ibn Bukayr in Abu Ubayd transmitted it, citing al-Tirmidhi and the report earlier in this book. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.340}
