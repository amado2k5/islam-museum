## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 101 to 102 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They will have a sigh therein while they will not hear": He means His mention by saying: "for them" are the polytheists and their gods, and the 'h' and the 'm' in the phrase "for them" is from the mention of "all" in His saying: "and everyone therein abides forever," meaning He says: they will all be in Hell with a sigh, "and they will not hear therein," meaning: they will be in the Fire they will not hear. Ibn Mas'ud interpreted the phrase "while they are therein and do not hear": He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Al-Mas'udi, from Yunus ibn Khabbab, who said: Ibn Mas'ud recited this verse: "They will have a breath therein, and they will not hear," he said: When someone is thrown into the fire to abide therein, they are placed in coffins of fire, then those coffins are placed in other coffins, then the coffins are placed in other coffins with nails of fire, so none of them sees that anyone in the fire is being punished. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ikrimah.
