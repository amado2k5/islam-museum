## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah has favored some of you over others in provision. But those who preferred their provision over what their right hands possessed, they are equal therein. So by the favor of Allah, they deny the favor of Allah." He means: By those whom Allah has favored over others by what He has provided for them. He means by the polytheists of their wealth and their spouses. "They are equal in it," meaning until they and their slaves are equal in that. Allah Almighty says: They do not accept that they and their slaves be equal in what You have provided them, and they have made My servants my partners in my kingdom and sovereignty. This is a parable that God Almighty gave to the polytheists with Allah. It was said: What was meant by those who said: The Messiah, the son of God, is one of the Christians. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
