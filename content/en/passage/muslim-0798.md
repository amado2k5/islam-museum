## Summary
A report of Aisha through Sa'd ibn Hisham in the chapter on the merit of the skilled Qur'an reciter and the one who stumbles in it, in the Book of the Prayer of Travellers in Sahih Muslim. The skilled reciter is placed with the angels, and the one who strains in recitation has his reward doubled. {cite:sahih-muslim}
