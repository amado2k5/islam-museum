## Summary
Yahya ibn Said narrated to Malik that Muhammad ibn Ibrahim al-Taymi told him from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman that Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Aswad ibn Abd Yaghuth, who had white beard and hair, came out to them one day after dyeing them red. The people said: this is better. He said: my mother Aisha, wife of the Prophet, sent her servant Nakhila to me last night and swore I must dye them, and told me that Abu Bakr al-Siddiq used to dye. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.539}
