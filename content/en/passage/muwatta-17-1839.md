## Summary
Malik says: the form that is disliked is that a man tells another: I advance you gold on your riding camel named so-and-so, which I will ride to the pilgrimage, with a time between now and the pilgrimage. Or he says the same of a slave or a dwelling. If he does that, he is only advancing gold to him, on the basis that if the camel is found sound at the term he named, it is his for that hire; if a death or other event befalls it, his gold is returned, and it stays with him as an advance. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.155}
