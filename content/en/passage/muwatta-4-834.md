## Summary
A saying of Malik: it reached me from Sulayman ibn Yasar something similar, namely like Said ibn al-Musayyab's view of putting the vow before the voluntary fast. The editor notes that Suwayd ibn Said transmitted it from Malik (472). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.407}
