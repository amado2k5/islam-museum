## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari clarifies that your deity, whose worship and sincere obedience to Him is One, with no partner, Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, and Lord of the Easts. And that Allah adorned the lowest heaven with the adornment of the stars and protected them from every rebellious devil, so they do not listen to the highest assembly and are thrown back from every side, and they will have a severe punishment except for the one who is snatched by the snatch, followed by a piercing meteor.
