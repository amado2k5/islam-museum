## Summary
A saying of Malik: the one in seclusion enters the place where he wants to be in seclusion before sunset on the night he intends to begin, so that his seclusion starts from the beginning of the night. The editor notes that Abu Musab (866) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.421}
