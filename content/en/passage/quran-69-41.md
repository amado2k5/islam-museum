## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 42 as one section. He explains His saying, “And it is not the word of a poet; little do you believe”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: This Qur’an is not the word of a poet because Muhammad is not good at composing poetry, so you say it is poetry. “Little do you believe” means: You believe little in it. This is an address from God to the polytheists of Quraysh, and in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
