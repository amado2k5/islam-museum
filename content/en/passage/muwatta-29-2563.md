## Summary
Malik said: there is no retaliation between the free and the slave in any wound. A slave is killed for a free man if he kills him deliberately, but a free man is not killed for a slave even if he kills him deliberately. This is the best that has been heard. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.447}
