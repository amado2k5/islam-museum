## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "There is no blame upon you for entering houses that are not inhabited in which there is any belongings for you, and Allah knows what you reveal and what you conceal": There is no sin or blame upon you, O people, for entering houses where no one lives without permission. Then they differed about which houses we mean, and some said: We mean the houses and houses built on roads that have no known inhabitants, but were built for passersby and passersby to take refuge in and their belongings. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
