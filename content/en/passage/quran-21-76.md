## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 76 to 77 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Noah, when he called out before, so We responded to him and saved him and his family from the great distress": And remember, O Muhammad, Noah when he called to his Lord before you, and before Abraham and Lot, and asked us to destroy his people who denied Allah in what He promised them of His promise, and who denied Noah in what He brought them of the truth from his Lord. And He said, 'My Lord, do not leave upon the earth any dwellings among the disbelievers.' So We answered his prayer and saved him and his family, meaning his family: The people of faith, from his offspring and their relatives, "from the great distress" means: the torment that befell the deniers from the flood and drowning. And distress: intense distress; it is said: 'This matter has distressed me,' so it distresses me with distress. And His saying: 'And We helped him from the people who denied Our signs,' he says: We helped Noah against the people who denied Our proofs and evidences, and We saved him from them and drowned them all together.
