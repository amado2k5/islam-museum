## Summary
Malik transmits from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: the gates of Paradise are opened on Monday and Thursday, and every believing servant who does not associate anything with God is forgiven, except a man who has enmity with his brother; it is said: leave these two until they make peace. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.495}
