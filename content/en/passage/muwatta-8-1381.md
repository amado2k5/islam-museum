## Summary
Malik says the best he heard on the one who expiates his oath by clothing is that if he clothes men, he clothes each one with a garment, and if he clothes women, he clothes each with two garments, a shirt and a head-covering, which is the least that suffices a person in his prayer. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.616}
