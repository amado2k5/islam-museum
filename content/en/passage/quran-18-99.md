## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 99 to 100 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We left some of them that Day waving in others and blowing the trumpet and gathered them all together": And We left Our servants on the Day when Our promise We promised them comes to them, that We will crush the mountains and blow them off the earth in blows, and then leave them on a smooth bottom, some waving in others, meaning: Their jinn mix with their humans. Also: from Harun ibn Antarah, from a sheikh from Banu Fazara, regarding the verse: "And We left some of them that Day to wave among others," he said: When jinn and humans were scattered, Iblis said: I know this matter for you. They will go east, and find the angels have cut the earth, then they will move to the west, and they will see the angels have cut the earth. Then he will ascend right and left to the far end of the earth, and he will say: There is no one to be deterred. So he is like that, when a path was presented to him like a trap, he and his descendants seized him. While they were against him, as they attacked the fire, God brought forth a treasure from the reservoir of the fire. He said, and in the passage he quotes from Ibn Zayd, Sufyan, and Atiyyah.
