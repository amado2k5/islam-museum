## Summary
Verse 39 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 39 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of the verse (p. 359, note 1).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: what would it cost those who give out their money for display before people and do not affirm God or the resurrection, were they to affirm that God is one with no partner, devote their worship to Him alone, be certain of the resurrection after death and that He will repay them for their deeds on the day of resurrection, and pay the alms due on the property God gave them with willing hearts, not spending it for display, seeking fame and boasting among the unbelievers and praise by falsehood among people. He says God was aware of them and their deeds and of what they intend by their spending, display, reputation and praise, and keeps their deeds in His charge, nothing of them hidden from Him, until He repays them for it at their return to Him. Note 1: the manuscript has the word for knowing in the nominative, which does no harm. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 359}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 359}
