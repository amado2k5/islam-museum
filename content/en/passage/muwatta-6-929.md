## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar entered ihram from al-Fur. The editor notes that Abu Musab and al-Shaybani (386) transmitted it from Malik, and that Ibn Umar used to enter ihram from al-Fur on his journey. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.445}
