## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We have enjoined upon man kindness to his parents. His mother carried him with dislike, and he gave birth to him with dislike, and he carried and separated him for thirty months until he reached maturity and forty years of age. He said, 'My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do righteousness that pleases Him and to rectify for me in my offspring. Indeed, I have repented to You, and indeed I am of the Muslims.'" And We enjoined upon the son of Adam his good companionship with his parents during their lifetime, and to be dutiful to them in Their lives and after their deaths. Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase "ihsan" (excellence), so the general reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "hassan" with a damma on the 'ha' in the interpretation described. The majority of Kufa's reciters read it as "ihsan" with an alif, meaning: and we advised him to treat them well, and by which means the reciter recited correctly, due to the similarity of meanings and the extensive reading of each among the reciters. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
