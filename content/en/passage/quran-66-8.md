## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, “O you who have believed, repent to Allah with sincere repentance. Perhaps your Lord will remove from you your misdeeds and admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow on the Day when Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and those who believed with him. Their light will proceed before them and on their right; they will say, ‘Our Lord, perfect for us our light and forgive us our sins.’” “Indeed, You are over all things competent.” O you who have believed in God, “repent to God,” meaning: return from your sins to obedience to God and to what pleases Him, “with sincere repentance,” meaning: a return from which you will never return. The passage also includes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-A'mash, Simak, and Sufyan.
