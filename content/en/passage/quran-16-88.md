## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Those who disbelieved and prevented others from the way of Allah, We increased them in punishment upon punishment because they used to cause corruption": Those who denied your prophethood, O Muhammad, and denied you in what You brought them from your Lord, and prevented them from believing in Allah and His Messenger and whoever desired Him, We increased them in punishment on the Day of Resurrection in Hell above the punishment they were in before they increased it. It is said: That increase which Allah promised them to increase in scorpions and life. In the passage, statements from Mujahid, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan are also quoted.
