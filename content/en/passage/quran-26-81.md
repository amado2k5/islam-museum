## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 81 to 82 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And He who causes me to die and then gives me life": "And He who causes me to die when He wills and then gives me life after my death is 'And by Him I hope that He will forgive me my sin on the Day of Judgment.' So he raised this who holds in His hand my benefit and harm, who has power and authority, and who has both this world and the Hereafter, not the One who does not listen when called, nor does He benefit or harm. These words from Abraham were a protest against his people, that divinity is not suitable, and servitude should only be for those who do these acts, not for those who cannot endure benefit or harm. It is said that Ibrahim, meant by saying: "By Him I hope will forgive my sin on the Day of Judgment": "By Him I hope will forgive me, my words: 'I am ill,' and 'Rather, this is what their elder did,' and 'I say to Sarah, she is my sister.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ikrimah.
