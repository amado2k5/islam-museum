## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And from the fruits of the palm trees and grapes you derive from them intoxication and a good provision. Indeed, in that is a sign for people who reason": And for you too, O people, is a lesson in what We give you of the fruits of the palm trees and grapes, from which you take intoxication and a good provision, along with what We give you from the wombs of the cattle of milk that comes out from between the fur and the blood. He omitted from his saying: "and from the fruits of palm trees and grapes" the noun, meaning what was described, which is: among the fruits of palm trees and grapes is what you take from them because the word "man" is used in the speech as a compound, so it is self-sufficient to convey its meaning and to inform the listeners of the necessity of mentioning the name along with it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Sufyan.
