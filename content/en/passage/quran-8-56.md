**Commentary on verse 56 (pp. 21-22)**

Al-Tabari says: "those with whom you made a covenant", Muhammad, meaning you took their pledges and covenants not to fight you nor to support against you anyone fighting you, such as the Qurayza and their like with whom you had a pact and treaty; "then they break" their pledges and covenants, every time they covenanted with you and gave their word they fought you and backed others against you, "and they do not fear God", nor fear in doing so that a blow will fall on them that overwhelms and destroys them. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of covenant and breaking, and notes how a phrase reads in the printed text and the manuscript. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 21}

Report 16210, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: they are the Qurayza, who aided the enemies against Muhammad on the day of the Trench; report 16211, from Mujahid, the same, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 22}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
