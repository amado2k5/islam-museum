## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "I did not testify to them the creation of the heavens and the earth, nor the creation of themselves, nor did I take the misguided as supporters": He says: I did not testify to Iblis and his descendants the creation of the heavens and the earth. He means: I did not bring them that, so I sought help from them in their creation, nor did I create themselves. He says: Nor did I testify to the creation of some of them, so I sought help from Him in His creation. Rather, I was alone in creating all of them without a supporter or supporter. He says: So how can they take their enemy as allies instead of Me, when they are created by people like them, and they abandoned my worship while I am the bestowal upon them and their ancestors, their Creator and the Creator of those who follow Him besides Me, alone without a helper or supporter? And His saying: "And I did not take the misguiders as supporters," it means: I did not take those who do not guide to the truth, but rather misguide. Whoever follows him is deliberately wronged by the way, as helpers and helpers, and this is from their saying: so-and-so supports so-and-so if he strengthens and supports him. In the passage, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
