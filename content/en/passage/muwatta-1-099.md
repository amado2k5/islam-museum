## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from al-Salt ibn Zubayd, in the chapter on the leniency of not performing ablution after madhi: al-Salt asked Sulayman ibn Yasar about the wetness he finds, and Sulayman said: sprinkle what is under your garment with water, and do not pay it attention. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.84}
