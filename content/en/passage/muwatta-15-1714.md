## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he used to say: whoever marries a woman and cannot consummate the marriage with her is given a term of one year. If he consummates it, well; otherwise they are separated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.99}
