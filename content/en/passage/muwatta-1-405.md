## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam from his father Aslam from Umar ibn al-Khattab, the same as the previous report, when he told the people of Mecca to complete their prayers, since he and his companions were travelling people. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Ibn Wahb in al-Tahawi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.213}
