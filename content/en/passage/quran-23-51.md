## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "O messengers, eat of the good things and do righteousness. Indeed, I am Knowing of what you do": And We said to Jesus: O messengers, eat of what Allah has made good for you and not what is forbidden. "And do righteousness," meaning in the speech to one man: "O people, refrain from harming us," and as He said: "Those to whom the people were told," which is one man.
