## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50 to 51 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Taste, Indeed, You are the Mighty, the Generous": It is said to this wicked sinner: Taste this punishment with which you are being tormented today, "Indeed, You are the Mighty" among your "noble" people over them. He mentioned that these verses were revealed regarding Abu Jahl ibn Hisham. In the verse, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
