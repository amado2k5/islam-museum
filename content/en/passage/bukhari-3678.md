## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar: Abdullah ibn Amr was asked about the worst thing the polytheists did to the Messenger of God, and he witnessed the event at the House. It shows the clear merit of Abu Bakr in defending him in Mecca, when he recited the verse from Ghafir before the polytheists. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
