## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, We sent Our messengers with clear proofs and sent down with them the Book and the balance so that people may uphold justice. And We sent down iron, in which is great power and benefits for the people, and so that Allah may know who will support Him and His messengers in the unseen. Indeed, Allah is Powerful and Exalted in Might (Powerful, Exalted in Might and Exalted)": We sent Our messengers with detailed explanations and evidences, and We sent down with them the Book with judgments and laws, and the balance with justice. Also: from Muammar, from Qatadah, "The Book and the Balance" he said: The scale is justice. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "And We sent down with them the Book and the Balance," truthfully, he said: The scale is what people work and deal with in this world from their livelihoods that they take and give; they take by a scale and give by a scale, knowing what is taken and what is given. He said: The Book contains the religion of people who work and abandon it, for the Book is for the Hereafter, and the balance is for this world. And His saying: "So that people may establish justice," the Exalted means: that people may act among themselves with justice. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
