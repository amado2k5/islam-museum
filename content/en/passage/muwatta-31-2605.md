## Summary
Malik transmits from Numaym ibn Abdallah al-Mujmir, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: at the gates of Medina are angels; neither plague nor the Dajjal will enter it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.470}
