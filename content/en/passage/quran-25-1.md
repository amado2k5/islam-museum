## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Blessed is He who sent down the criterion upon His servant to be a warner to the worlds": Abu Ja'far said: Tabarak: a reaction from blessing, as reported by Al-Dahhak, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, who said: Tabarak: a reaction from blessing. It is like the saying of the one who says: 'Our Lord is sanctified.' His saying: 'Blessed is He who sent down the criterion,' means: Blessed is He who sent down the separation between truth and falsehood, chapter after chapter and chapter after chapter, upon His servant Muhammad, so that Muhammad would be to all the jinn and mankind to whom God sent him calling to him, a warner: meaning a warner warning them of His punishment and warning them of His punishment, if they do not unite Him, do not dedicate worship to Him, and dismantle all gods and idols below Him. In the passage, he quotes from Al-Dhahhak and Ibn Zayd.
