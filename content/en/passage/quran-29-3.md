## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly tested those before them so that Allah may know those who are truthful and may know the liars." And We certainly tested those before them among the nations to whom We sent Our messengers, and they said, 'As your nation said, O Muhammad, regarding their enemies and enabling them to harm them, like Moses when We sent him to the Children of Israel, and tested them with Pharaoh and their people, and like Jesus when We sent him to the Children of Israel, so We tested those who followed him with those who took him away. Likewise, We tested your followers with your opponents among your enemies.' So Allah may teach those who were truthful among them in their saying that they believed. "And let the liars know from them in their statement, and Allah knows this from them before, during, and after testing. But the meaning of this is: so that Allah may make the truthful among them evident in his statement, 'We believe in Allah,' from those who deny the liar by testing him by his enemy, so that his allies may know his truthfulness, as we have explained earlier, and in the passage he quotes from Mujahid and Sufyan."
