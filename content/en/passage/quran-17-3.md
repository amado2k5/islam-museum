## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "descendants of those We carried with Noah; indeed, he was a grateful servant": Glory be to Him who took His servant by night from the Sacred Mosque to Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa, and We gave Moses the Book as guidance for the Children of Israel, descendants of those We carried with Noah. By offspring, He meant all those whom God praised with this Qur'an as evidence from the races of nations, Arabs and non-Arabs from the Children of Israel and others, because everyone on earth from the children of Adam is descendants of those whom God carried with Noah in the ark. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
