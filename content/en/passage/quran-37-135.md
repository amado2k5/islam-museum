## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 133 to 136 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "except an old woman among the remainder," which is the wife of Lot. We have mentioned her story previously, and the differing opinion on the meaning of the phrase "among the old ones," but the correct view is our opinion. And I narrated from Al-Musayyib ibn Sharik, from Abu Ruq, from Al-Dahhak: "Except an old woman among the old ones," who says: except his wife who was late and corrupted a stone, and she was called 'Hishafa'. And from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase: "except an old woman among the old ones," he said: the doomed. And his statement: In the Maqta' section, he quotes sayings from Al-Dahhak.
