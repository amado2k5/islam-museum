## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains the verse "And they said, 'Why does this Messenger eat food and walk in the markets? Why is it that an angel was not sent down to him and he would be with him as a warner?'": He mentioned that these two verses were revealed to the Messenger of Allah while the polytheists of his people said to him on the night their nobles gathered behind the Kaaba, presented him with things, and asked him for the verses. So it was in what they spoke about at that time, as from Ibn Ishaq, who said: Muhammad ibn Abi Muhammad, the freedman of Zayd ibn Thabit, narrated to me from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, or Ikrimah, the freedman of Ibn Abbas, from Ibn Abbas: They said to him: If you do not do this for us, it means what they asked about managing their mountains, reviving their ancestors, bringing God and the angels to the tribe, and what Allah mentioned in Surah Bani Isra'il. So take for yourself, ask your Lord to send with you an angel who will confirm what you say and take us back from you, and He will grant you palaces, gardens, and treasures of gold and silver to suffice you from what you see. You go to the markets and seek livelihood as we seek it, so that we may know your virtue and status from your Lord. In the passage, sayings from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Ishaq are quoted.
