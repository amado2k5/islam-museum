**Commentary on verse 64 (p. 331)**

Al-Tabari says: the hypocrites fear that a surah may be sent down about them, "informing them of what is in their hearts", meaning disclosing to the believers what is in their hearts. It is said that God sent down this verse on His Messenger because the hypocrites, when they found fault with the Messenger of God and mentioned something of his affair and the Muslims', would say "perhaps God will not expose our secret"; so God said to His Prophet, say to them "mock", as a threat and warning, "God will bring out what you fear". The editor refers to earlier explanations of caution and tidings. Report 16907, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: they say the thing among themselves, then say "perhaps God will not expose our secret against us". Report 16908 begins, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn and Hajjaj, and continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 331}
