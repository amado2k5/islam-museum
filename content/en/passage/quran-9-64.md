**Commentary on verse 64 (pp. 331-332)**

Al-Tabari says: the hypocrites fear that a surah may be sent down about them, "informing them of what is in their hearts", meaning disclosing to the believers what is in their hearts. It is said that God sent down this verse on His Messenger because the hypocrites, when they found fault with the Messenger of God and mentioned something of his affair and the Muslims', would say "perhaps God will not expose our secret"; so God said to His Prophet, say to them "mock", as a threat and warning, "God will bring out what you fear". The editor refers to earlier explanations of caution and tidings. Report 16907, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: they say the thing among themselves, then say "perhaps God will not expose our secret against us". Report 16908, from Mujahid, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj, is the same except that he said "this secret of ours". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 332}

As for "God will bring out what you fear", it means God will disclose against you, hypocrites, what you feared to disclose, and so He disclosed it against them and shamed them, and this surah used to be called "the Exposer". Report 16909, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: this surah used to be called the Exposer, the exposer of the hypocrites. The editor refers to earlier explanations of bringing out. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 332}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
