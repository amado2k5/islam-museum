## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 22 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "This is insight for mankind, and guidance and mercy for a people who are certain": God Almighty says, "This is the Book which We have sent down to you, O Muhammad," "insights for mankind," by which they see truth from falsehood, and by it they know the path of righteousness. Al-Basa'ir is the plural of insight. In the segment, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
