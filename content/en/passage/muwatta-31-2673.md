## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Ibn Bujayd al-Ansari, then al-Harithi, from his grandmother, that the Messenger of God said: return the needy even with a burnt hoof. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.511}
