## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains: "And We did not destroy any town except that it had a known record": except that it has a temporary term and a known period; God does not destroy its people until they reach it, and when they reach it, He destroys them. He makes this a comfort for the Prophet: likewise, the people of his village in Mecca; polytheists do not destroy its people except after their book reaches its term, for it is his decree that no people of a town be destroyed except after their book reaches its appointed time.
