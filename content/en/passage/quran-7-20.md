Verse 20 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum

Then, on "Then Satan whispered to them to disclose to them what had been hidden from them of their nakedness", he says: "whispered to them" means he whispered to them, and that whispering was his saying to them "your Lord forbade you this tree only lest you become angels or become immortal" and his swearing to them to it. "Whispered to them" is said with the sense I gave, as one says "I was drawn to him" meaning I longed for him, which really means I turned from these to him; the note says this is the wording of al-Akhfash in explaining a line by Ibn Harma, and corrects the printed text by the manuscript. So here, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 346}

Satan whispered from himself to them with lying speech to disclose to them what had been hidden from them of their nakedness, as Ru'ba said in a line describing a hidden hunter lying in wait for wild asses, which the note traces. The meaning of the speech is: Iblis drew Eve to Adam and put to them, your Lord forbade you to eat the fruit of this tree only lest you become angels or become immortal, so as to disclose what God had hidden from them of their private parts, covering it with His veil that He had drawn over them. Wahb ibn Munabbih used to speak of the covering with which God had covered them: he gives (14393) from him, on "their nakedness appeared to them", that there was a light on them, so their private parts were not seen. The note records the biography of Hawthara ibn Muhammad al-Minqari in the chain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 347}
