## Overview
The Book of Pre-emption (Shufa) is the twenty-first book of the [[work/muwatta-malik|Muwatta]] in the transmission of [[person/yahya-ibn-yahya-al-laythi|Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi]] from [[person/malik-ibn-anas|Malik ibn Anas]], in the edition of Bashshar Awwad Ma'ruf (1997), in the second volume. It occupies pages 251 to 258 in the PDF pagination, and contains 24 numbered texts (2079–2102) in two chapters. It concerns [[concept/shufa|pre-emption]] and the houses and lands in which it applies, and those in which it does not. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.251} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.258}

## What the book gathers
- **Where pre-emption applies** (2079–2094): pre-emption among partners in proportion to their shares, gift and sale on credit, the absence of the claimant, inheritance, and the buyer's rescission. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.252} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256}
- **Where pre-emption does not apply** (2095–2102): boundaries, a road, a well, a male palm, animals and cloth, and the effect of a long lapse of time in ending the right. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.258}

## Malik's method in the book
Malik builds pre-emption on the rule "partners in land or a house", gives it a practical limit by the possibility of division, sets no fixed period for absence, and states that the buyer must bring the matter of pre-emption before the ruler. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.254} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.258}

## The editor's notes
The editor quotes al-Bayhaqi and Ibn Abd al-Barr on the disagreement of transmitters over the hadith of pre-emption from al-Zuhri, and notes that Malik preferred his cut-off version. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.252}

## Its place in the museum
Each text of the book has its own record. It names the highest narrator and the chapter, gives the substance of the report in the museum's own wording, and records the editor's takhrij where it is needed. See [[concept/shufa|Pre-emption]] for the places where its rulings appear in the book. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.251}
