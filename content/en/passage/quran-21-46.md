## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if a whiff of your Lord's punishment touches them, they will say, 'Woe to us! Indeed, we used to be wrongdoers': And if these hastened to punishment touches them, O Muhammad, a breath of punishment from your Lord means share and share, from their saying: 'So-and-so breathes for so-and-so from his gift,' meaning when he gives him a portion or a share of wealth. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: 'And if a breath of punishment touches them...' The verse says: 'If a punishment befalls them.' And His saying: "Woe to us, that we were wrongdoers," means: If this breath of your Lord's punishment, O Muhammad, befalls them by denying You and disbelieving in You, then they will know the absence of their denial of You, and they will acknowledge upon themselves the blessing and kindness of Allah towards them, and their ingratitude is with their hands, and they will say, "Woe to us! Indeed, we were wrongdoers in our worship of gods and equals, and we abandoned the worship of Allah who created us and bestowed favor upon us, and placed worship in a different place." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
