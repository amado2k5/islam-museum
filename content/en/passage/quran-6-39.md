## Summary
Verse 39 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 39 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "those who deny our signs are deaf and dumb in the darkness; whom God wills he leads astray, and whom he wills he puts on a straight path" al-Tabari says: those who deny God's proofs, marks and indications are deaf to hearing the truth, dumb to speaking it, in the darkness, meaning the darkness of unbelief, bewildered in it, mired in the darknesses of unbelief, not seeing God's signs to take lesson from them and know that he who created and formed him, ordered and perfected his ordering, measured him with the best measure, gave him strength and made the tools of his body sound did not create him in vain or leave him unrestrained, and gave him the tools he gave only to use in obedience to him and what pleases him, not in disobedience and what angers him; in his bewilderment in the darknesses of unbelief and wandering in its depths he is heedless of what God has written for him in the mother of the book and what God will do with him on the day he is gathered to him with the rest of the peoples. Then God reported that he is the one who leads astray whom he wills to lead astray of his creatures from belief to unbelief, and the guide to the straight path of those he wished to guide, whom he enabled by his grace and bounty to believe in him and leave unbelief in him, his messengers and what his prophets brought; that none of his creatures is guided but one for whom happiness was decreed in the mother of the book, and none goes astray but one for whom wretchedness was decreed there, and that in his hand is all good and to him belongs all favour, to him the creation and the command. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 350}

The editor refers for "sign", "deaf", "dumb", "error" and "straight path" to the language indexes and volumes 3, 1 and 10.

Qatada spoke to the like: 13225, from him: "deaf and dumb" is the likeness of the unbeliever, deaf and dumb, who sees no guidance and gains nothing from it, deaf to the truth in the darknesses, unable to come out of them, wandering confused in them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 351}

Verse 39 closes here at p. 351, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-40|6:40]].
