## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 108 to 110 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And I do not ask you for any reward for it. My reward is only from the Lord of the worlds": and I do not ask you for my advice to you and command you to avoid Allah's punishment by obeying Him in what He commands you and forbids, whether reward or recompense. "Indeed, my reward is only from the Lord of the worlds," excluding you and all of Allah's creation. So fear Allah's punishment for your disbelief in Him, and fear the consequences of His wrath upon you for denying His messengers. And obey: He says: 'And obey Me in my advice to you, and I command you to sincerely worship your Creator.'
