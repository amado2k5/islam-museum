## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi and Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: whoever has stoned the pillar and then shaved or shortened, and slaughtered his offering if he had one, it is lawful for him what was forbidden to him, except women and perfume, until he circumambulates the House. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.547}
