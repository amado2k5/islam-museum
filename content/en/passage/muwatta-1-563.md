## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Umara ibn Sayyad from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, that he heard him say about the enduring good deeds mentioned in Surat al-Kahf: they are the servant's words, "God is greatest", "Glory be to God", "Praise be to God", "There is no god but God", and "There is no power nor might except in God". The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.289}
