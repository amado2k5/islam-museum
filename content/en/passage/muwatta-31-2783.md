## Summary
It reached Malik that the Prophet said: there is no prophet who did not tend sheep; they asked: and you, Messenger of God? He said: and I. The editor notes that the hadith is in the two Sahihs, from Abu Salama from Jabir, and from Amr ibn Yahya ibn Said from his grandfather from Abu Hurayra. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.564}
