## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50 to 51 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Those before them have said it, so what they used to earn is more beneficial than them": He said this statement meaning their saying: for the blessing of Allah which granted them while they were polytheists: We gave it to us with knowledge "those before them," meaning: those before them were among the nations of Quraysh who were empty for their messengers, as a denial of them and mockery of them. And His saying: "What they earned did not benefit them," means: It did not benefit them when Allah's wrath came upon them for denying the messengers of Allah and mocking them of what they were earning, and that was their idol worship. He says: Their service to them did not benefit them, nor did their gods intercede for them with Allah at that time, but they accepted them and disowned them.
