## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "So We struck their ears in the cave for years in number": He means by saying: "So We struck their ears in the cave by sleeping": meaning We caused them to fall asleep, as the one who says to another: "God struck you with paralysis," meaning God tested him with it and sent him upon it. And His saying: "years counted" means counted years, and the number is in the accusative by saying "so we struck." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
