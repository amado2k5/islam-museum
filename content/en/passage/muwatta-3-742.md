## Summary
A saying of Malik: likewise all wheat, red and white, barley and spelt, is one kind; when a man harvests five wasqs of it all together, they are gathered and zakat is due; if it does not reach that, there is no zakat. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.369}
