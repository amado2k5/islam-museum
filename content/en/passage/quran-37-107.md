## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains "and We ransomed him with a great sacrifice" by saying that God made the place of his slaughter the slaughter of a great ram and saved him from slaughter. He also points to the differences among interpreters regarding the redeemed one of the sons of Abraham: some said it is Isaac and narrated their narrations, mentioning other opinions in their proper place, and that God left a mention of Abraham among the others. Peace be upon Abraham.
