## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 11 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “So as for the orphan, do not oppress him”: “So as for the orphan,” O Muhammad, “do not oppress him,” meaning: do not wrong him, taking away his rights, taking advantage of his weakness. As for Qatadah, regarding “So as for the orphan, do not oppress him,” he says: do not wrong him. On the authority of Sufyan, on the authority of Mansur, on the authority of Mujahid, regarding “So as for the orphan, do not oppress him,” he said: do not belittle him and despise him. It is mentioned that in the codex of Abdullah, it reads: “Do not oppress him.” And he quotes in the section statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
