## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 188 to 189 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "My Lord said, 'My Lord knows best what you do.'" Shu'ayb said to his people: "My Lord knows best what you do," meaning He encompasses their deeds, nothing of which is hidden from Him, and He repays you your reward for them. "So they denied him," meaning: his people denied him, "and the punishment of the Day of the Shade seized them," meaning by the canopy, a cloud that shaded them. When they gathered beneath it, a fire blazed over them and burned them, thus the traces came. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
