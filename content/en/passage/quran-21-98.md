## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, what you worship besides Allah is the gravel of Hell; you are going to it": Indeed, you, O polytheists with Allah, worshipping idols and idols besides Him, and what you worship besides Allah are the deities of the gods. Also: I narrated from Husayn, he said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say regarding the phrase: "Indeed, you and what you worship besides Allah" means the gods and those who worship them, "the pebbles of Hell." As for the pebbles of Hell, some said: it means: the fuel and trees of Hell. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, and Ibn Jurayj.
