## Summary
Verse 3 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 3 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "he is God in the heavens and on the earth, he knows your secret and your open, and he knows what you earn" al-Tabari says: the one to whom belongs the divinity that suits no other, who is owed your pure praise for his favours to you, people, to whom your unbelievers make others equal, is God, who is in the heavens and on earth and knows your secret and open, so that nothing is hidden from him; your Lord, who deserves praise from you and to whom pure worship is due, is he of this description, not one who cannot bring you harm or benefit, does nothing, and cannot even ward off harm meant for himself. As for "and knows what you earn", it means he knows what you do and commit, and counts it against you so as to repay you for it when you return to him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 261}

The editor refers for "earn" to volume 10.

Verse 3 closes here at p. 261, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-4|6:4]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
