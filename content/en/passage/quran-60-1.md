## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, do not take My enemy and your enemy as allies, and you will be called to them with affection, while they have disbelieved in what has come to you of the truth. They are sending out the Messenger and beware of you believing in Allah, your Lord. If you have gone forth striving in My cause and seeking My pleasure, you will approach them with affection. And I know what you conceal and what you declare [knowing]. And whoever among you does it has certainly gone astray from the way." Abu Ja'far said: He says: Exalted is His remembrance. To the believers among the companions of the Messenger of Allah: "O you who have believed, do not take as My enemy" from the polytheists. "Your enemy is allies," meaning helpers. And His saying: 'You will meet them with affection,' He, Glorified and Exalted, means: 'You will give them your affection for Him.' The inclusion of the letter 'ba' in the phrase 'with affection' and its dropping are equal, similar to the saying: 'I want you to go,' and 'I want you to go equally,' and as in His saying: 'And whoever intends to be apostasy through injustice,' meaning: 'And whoever wants to be deviated from it by injustice.' Among this is the poet's saying: 'When she longed for drinking, he shook her with a stick that was scarce for her when she was facing him, 'nahim,' meaning: 'When she hoped for drink.' The passage quotes Sufyan.
