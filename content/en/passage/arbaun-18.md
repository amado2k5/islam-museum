## Summary
Hadith of Abu Dharr and Mu'adh ibn Jabal: "Fear God wherever you are, follow a bad deed with a good one, and treat people with good character". Ibn Rajab shows that the chain is weak, that Maymun ibn Abi Shabib's hearing from the Companions is not established, and that the counsel gathers duties to God and to people. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
