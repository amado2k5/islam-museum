## Summary
Verse 182 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 182 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The meaning (p. 447).** Al-Tabari says that God's saying to them on the Day of Resurrection what preceded is because of what their hands sent ahead and what they earned in their lives in the world, and because God is just and does not wrong, punishing no servant without desert, but repaying every soul for what it earned and paying every doer the wage of his work. So He repaid the Jews he described, who the verse says ascribed need to God and killed prophets without right, with the punishment of burning for the sins they earned and the evils they committed and for lying about God after being warned and given the excuse cut off. He was therefore not unjust, nor placing His punishment where it was not due; and in the same way He wrongs none of His creatures, being just among them and gracious to all with what He loves of His bounties. The first note refers to the explanation of the same phrase in volume 2 (pp. 367-368); the second says the addition in square brackets is needed for the sentence to stand, and that the relative clause means those to whom God said what He said, so the sentence runs: He repaid those to whom He said that on the Day of Resurrection with the punishment of burning that He repaid them with. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 447}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 447}
