## Summary
Malik says of a woman given the choice: if her husband gives her the choice and she chooses herself, she is divorced three times. If her husband says he only gave her the choice of one, that is not his to claim. Malik said this is the best of what he has heard. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.73}
