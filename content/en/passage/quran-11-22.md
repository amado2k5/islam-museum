**Commentary on verse 22 (pp. 288-289)**

On [[passage/quran-11-22|11:22]] al-Tabari says: truly, these people of whom this is the description in the world and the next are the greatest losers, who sold their abodes in the gardens for the abodes of the people of Paradise in the Fire, and that is the plain loss. He has shown earlier that their saying 'jaramtu' means I earned the sin, and that the Arabs use it often in oaths and in the sense of 'there is no escape', as 'la jarama that you will go' meaning 'no escape', until they used it for affirmation, saying 'la jarama you will stand' meaning truly you will stand; so the sense is: no hindrance from their being so, no barring from their being so. The editor says he did not find earlier the further detail al-Tabari mentions and fears it is a slip. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 289}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
