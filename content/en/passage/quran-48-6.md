## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And He will punish the hypocrites, the hypocrite women, the polytheists, and the polytheists who doubt Allah evil. Evil thought will be upon them as a circle of evil, and Allah will be angry with them and will curse them, and prepare for them Hell, and it will be an evil destination." This means: Indeed, We have opened for you a clear victory so that Allah may forgive you, admit the believing men and believing women, into gardens beneath which rivers flow, and punish the hypocrites, men and women, by Allah's opening for you, O Muhammad, what you have granted you of victory over the polytheists of Quraysh. They will be saddened and grieved, and their hope that they had hoped to see among the people of Quraysh will be disappointed. Faith in You is from weakness and weakness, and turning away from you in the immediate aftermath of this world, and praying for the Fire and eternal therein in the Hereafter.
