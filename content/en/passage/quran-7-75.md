## Summary
Verse 75 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 75 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-75|7:75]] and [[passage/quran-7-76|7:76]] together: by "the chiefs who were arrogant among his people" al-Tabari means the group of Salih's people who were too proud to follow him and to believe in God and in him; "to those considered weak" means the poor among Salih's followers and believers, not those of rank and standing. They asked: do you know that Salih is sent by his Lord, sent by God to us and to you? Those among the weak who believed said: we believe in the truth and guidance with which God sent Salih, that is, we affirm it is from God, that God commanded it, and that Salih called us to it by God's command. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 542}

"Those who were arrogant" toward God's command and His messenger Salih said: we reject and deny what you believe of Salih's prophethood and that what he brought is the truth from God; we neither believe it nor affirm it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 543}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
