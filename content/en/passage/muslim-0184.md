## Summary
A report of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri in the chapter on intercession and bringing monotheists out of the Fire, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Those whom the Fire touched for their sins come out after the intercession and sprout by the rivers of Paradise. {cite:sahih-muslim}
