## Summary
Yahya narrated from Ziyad ibn Abd al-Rahman, from Malik, from Sumayy, client of Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman, that Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman entered seclusion, and went out for his needs under a canopy in a closed chamber in the house of Khalid ibn al-Walid, and did not return until he witnessed the feast with the Muslims. The editor notes that Abu Musab (874) and Suwayd (448) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.423}
