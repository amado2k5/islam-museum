**Commentary on verse 116 (pp. 526-530)**

On [[passage/quran-11-116|11:116]] al-Tabari says: why were there not among the generations before you whose stories I have told you in this chapter, whom I destroyed for their disobedience of Me and disbelief in My messengers, 'people of remaining virtue', that is, possessed of remaining understanding and intellect, who take note of God's admonitions and weigh His proofs so that they know what is for them in believing in God and what is against them in disbelieving in Him, 'forbidding corruption on the earth', that is, forbidding the people of disobedience their disobedience and the people of disbelief their disbelief in His earth, 'except a few of those We saved from among them', that is, there were not among the generations before you people of remaining virtue forbidding corruption on earth except a few, who did forbid it, and God saved them from His punishment when He seized those who persisted in disbelief; they are the followers of the prophets and messengers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 526}

'A few' is in the accusative because 'except a few' is a disconnected exception from what precedes, as in [[passage/quran-10-98|10:98]] 'except the people of Jonah when they believed', which we have explained in other places. Report 18690, from Ibn Zayd: He made excuse, saying 'why were there not among the generations before you…' to 'except a few of those We saved', and they were those saved when God's punishment came down, and he recited 'those who did wrong followed the luxury they were given'; 18691, from Ibn Jurayj: God picks them out from every people. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 527}

Report 18692, from Dawud: Bilal asked me about al-Hasan's saying on the decree, and I said, I heard al-Hasan say of 'it was said, Noah, come down in peace from Us and blessings on you and on nations from those with you, and nations We shall allow to enjoy and then a painful punishment from Us will touch them': God sent Hud to 'Ad, and saved Hud and those who believed with him, and those who enjoyed perished; He sent Salih to Thamud, and saved Salih, and those who enjoyed perished; he said, I think he spoke well on the decree; the editor explains that it is a matter already finished, since God told Noah that before they existed. Report 18693, from Qatada: there were not before you any who forbade corruption on the earth except a few of those We saved. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 528}

As for 'those who did wrong followed the luxury they were given', it means those who wronged themselves by disbelieving in God followed what they were given luxury in; report 18694, from Ibn Abbas: what they were given respite in; 18695, from Qatada: of their worldly life, as if these took the sense that the wrongdoers followed what their Lord let them enjoy of the world's pleasures, preferring it to work for the next life and what would save them from God's punishment. Others said it means those who wronged followed what they exulted in of dominion and rebelled against God's command; reports 18696, 18697 and 18698, from Mujahid: in their dominion and tyranny, and they left the truth. Al-Tabari says: the best of these views is that God told that those who wronged themselves in every earlier nation by disbelieving in God followed the pleasures of the world they were given respite in, and were arrogant against God's command, tyrannised and barred from His path; for the 'mutraf' in Arab speech is the one pampered who is nourished on pleasures, and he cites a rajaz line of Ru'ba. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 529}

As for 'and they were criminals', it means they earned disbelief in God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 530}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
