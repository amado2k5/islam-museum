## Al-Tabari's commentary

The verse, “The example of those who were entrusted with the Torah and then did not uphold it is like that of a donkey carrying books. Wretched is the example of the people who deny the signs of Allah. And Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people,” explains that the example of those Jews and Christians who were given the Torah and were entrusted with acting upon it, “and then did not uphold it,” means that they did not act upon its teachings and denied Muhammad, even though they were commanded to believe in him, follow him, and affirm his truth. “It is like that of a donkey carrying books,” meaning it is like a donkey carrying books. He who carries books of knowledge, yet does not benefit from them, nor does he understand what is in them, is like those who were given the Torah, which contains the explanation of Muhammad's mission. If they do not benefit from what is in it, they are like a donkey carrying books containing knowledge; it does not understand them nor does it benefit from them. The passage then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
