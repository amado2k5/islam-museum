## Summary
Verse 243 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 243 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On ([[passage/quran-2-243|2:243]]) al-Tabari explains the opening question as meaning, do you not know, Muhammad, a seeing of the heart and not the eye, since the Prophet did not live to see those whom God reports on. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 266} He says the interpreters differed over the sense of their being thousands. Some took it as a number: from Ibn 'Abbas, that they were four thousand who went out fleeing the plague, saying they would come to a land with no death, whereupon God caused them to die, then a prophet passed by them, prayed to his Lord to bring them to life, and He did (5596, 5597). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 266} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 267} In the margin the editor notes that al-Hakim transmitted both reports and graded them sound by the standard of the two Sheikhs, and that al-Dhahabi remarked on a transmitter; these are their judgements, neither al-Tabari's nor the museum's. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 267} The editor also refers to Ibn Kathir and al-Durr al-Manthur. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 267}
