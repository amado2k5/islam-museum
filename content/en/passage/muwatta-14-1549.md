## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibrahim ibn Abi Ablah, from Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, that he gave a slave-girl to a companion, then asked about her and said he had considered giving her to his son to do such and such. Abd al-Malik said that Marwan was more scrupulous than he, having given a slave-girl to his son and then told him not to go near her, since he had seen her leg uncovered. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.47}
