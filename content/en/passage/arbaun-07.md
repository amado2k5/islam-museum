## Summary
Hadith of Tamim al-Dari, the seventh in the Forty Hadith: the religion is sincere advice. Ibn Rajab notes that Abu Dawud called it one of the hadiths on which jurisprudence turns, and gathers definitions of sincere advice to God, His book, His Messenger and the rulers of the Muslims. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
