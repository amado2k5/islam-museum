## Summary
Malik transmits from his uncle Abu Suhayl ibn Malik, who said: I was travelling with Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, and he said: what is your opinion of these Qadariyya? I said: my opinion is that you should give them the chance to repent, and if they accept, well; otherwise present them to the sword. Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz said: that is my opinion too. Malik said: that is my opinion too. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.481}
