## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 26 to 27 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'The Most Merciful took a son, glory be to Him.' Rather, He is an honored servant": These disbelievers said to their Lord: The Most Merciful took a son from among His angels. He said, glorified and exalted, revering what they said and disassociating himself from what He described Him with. He says in their disapproval of that: What is that attribute of 'rather, honored servants?' He says: What are the angels as these disbelievers described them among the children of Adam, but they are honored servants? He says: Allah honored them. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And they said, 'The Most Merciful has taken a son, glory be to Him, but rather honored servants.'" He said: The Jews said: Allah, Blessed and Exalted, is the union of the jinn, and among them were the angels. Allah, Blessed and Exalted, said in denial of them, and in response to them: "Rather, they are honored servants." And the angels are not as they said; rather, they are servants whom He honored with His worship. From Ma'mar, from Qatadah. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
