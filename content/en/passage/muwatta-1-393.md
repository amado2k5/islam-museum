## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Salim ibn Abdullah from his father Abdullah ibn Umar, that he rode to Rim and shortened the prayer on that journey. Malik says that is about four postal stages. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, al-Shafi'i, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.210}
