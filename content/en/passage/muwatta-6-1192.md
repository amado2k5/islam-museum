## Summary
Malik transmits from Musa ibn Uqba, from Kurayb, client of Ibn Abbas, from Usama ibn Zayd, that he heard him say: the Messenger of God set out from Arafat, and when he was at the ravine he dismounted and performed ablution, but did not complete the ablution. Usama said to him: prayer, Messenger of God. He said: the prayer is ahead of you. When he came to Muzdalifa he dismounted, performed ablution completely, then prayed the sunset prayer; then everyone made his camel kneel at his lodging; then he prayed the night prayer, and did not pray anything between them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.536}
