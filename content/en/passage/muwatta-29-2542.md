## Summary
Malik said: the one who leads, the one who drives and the rider are all liable for what the beast harms, unless the beast kicks without anything being done to it to make it kick. Umar ibn al-Khattab ruled blood-money for the one who ran his horse. Malik said: the one who leads, the rider and the driver are more deserving of liability than the one who ran his horse. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.441}
