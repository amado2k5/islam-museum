## Summary
Verse 92 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 92 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "and this is a book we sent down, blessed, confirming what came before it, that you warn the mother of the cities and those around it" al-Tabari says: and this Qur'an, Muhammad, is "a book", which is one of the names of the Qur'an that I have explained before, its sense being written, "book" being put in place of "written"; "we sent down" says we revealed to you; "blessed" is a "mufa'il" form from "baraka"; "confirming what came before it" says this book confirmed what came before it of God's books that he sent down on his prophets before you, not differing from them in sign or sense; and he began the report about it since the report that has come before shows it is joined to it, saying "and this is a book we sent down to you, blessed", the sense being: and so I sent down to you this book of mine blessed, like what I sent down of the Torah to Moses as guidance and light. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 530}

The editor says the printed text has a wording that does not stand and the manuscript has an unpointed, confused one, so he preferred what he wrote in brackets to follow the sense; that print and manuscript have other wordings for "shows that it is joined to it", so he preferred what he set and added "from" and "to it" in brackets; and refers for "book" and "blessed" to earlier passages.

As for "that you warn the mother of the cities and those around it" he says: we sent this book down to you, Muhammad, confirming what came before it of books, that you warn by it of God's punishment and might those in the mother of the cities, which is Mecca, "and those around it" east and west, of those who hold others equal to their Lord among gods and rivals and those who reject his messengers and other kinds of unbelievers. 13550, from Ibn Abbas: "the mother of the cities" is Mecca, "and those around it" the cities to the east and west; 13551, from him: "those around it" the whole earth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 531}

13552, from Qatada: "the mother of the cities" is Mecca, and it reached me that the earth was spread from Mecca; 13553, from him: we used to be told that the mother of the cities is Mecca and that the earth was spread from it; 13554, from al-Suddi: the mother of the cities is Mecca, and it was called so because it was the first house set up in it. Al-Tabari has already explained the reason Mecca was called the mother of the cities. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 531}

The editor refers for "the mother of the cities" to earlier passages and to report 6589.

On "and those who believe in the next world believe in it, and they keep watch over their prayers" he says: whoever believes in the coming of the Hour and the return in the next world to God, and affirms reward and punishment, believes in this book that we sent down to you, Muhammad, affirms it, admits that God sent it down, and keeps watch over the prescribed prayers God told him to establish, since he is a warner of whoever receives God's threat against disbelief in him and his disobediences; and it is only those who deny the return and reject the Hour who reject it and what is in it and deny it, for they do not hope for reward from God if they act on what is in it and do not fear punishment if they do not avoid what it tells them to avoid. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 532}

The editor refers for "keeping watch over the prayers" to an earlier passage.

Verse 92 closes here on p. 532, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-93|6:93]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
