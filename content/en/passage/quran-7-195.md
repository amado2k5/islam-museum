**Commentary on verse 195 (p. 322)**

God says to those who worship idols besides Him, making them know the ignorance of what they persist in: do these idols of yours, people, have feet with which they walk, so that they go about with you and for you in your needs and act with them for your benefits? Or do they have hands with which they strike, so that they ward off harm from you and help you with them when someone sets out against you with evil and harm? Or do they have eyes with which they see, so that they tell you what they have seen and beheld of what is hidden from you and that you do not see? Or do they have ears with which they hear, so that they report what they heard apart from you that you did not hear? If your gods that you worship have none of these instruments, and what is honoured is honoured only for the benefits hoped from it that reach one through some of these senses in your view, what reason is there for your worshipping idols empty of all that by which benefit is drawn and harm is warded off? As for "say: call your partners, then plot against me": say, Muhammad, to these idol-worshipping polytheists, call your partners whom you made partners with God in worship, then plot against me, you and they, "and give me no respite", meaning do not put off plotting and scheming but hasten it; He makes known to him by this that they will never harm him and that He has protected him from them, and makes the unbelievers know the powerlessness of their idols to help anyone who wrongs their friends with evil. The editor says the bracketed words are his inference from the verse and the commentary and had fallen from the copyist, and refers to the earlier explanations of "plotting" and "respite". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 322}
