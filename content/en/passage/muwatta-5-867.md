## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, that when Aisha was in seclusion she would not ask about a sick person except while walking, without stopping. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.419}
