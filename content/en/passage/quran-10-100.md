**Commentary on verse 100 (pp. 213-214)**

On [[passage/quran-10-100|10:100]] al-Tabari says God tells His prophet: no soul I created has a way to affirm you, Muhammad, except by My leave for it to do so; so do not wear yourself out seeking its guidance, but convey to it God's warning and make known to it what your Lord commanded you to make known, and then leave it, for its guidance is in the hand of its Creator. Al-Thawri used to say of 'except by God's leave': by God's decree, and report 17910, from Sufyan, says so. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'leave'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 213}

As for 'and He puts defilement on those who do not reason', it says that God guides whom He wills of His creatures to believe in you, Muhammad, and permits him to affirm you, so he affirms you, follows you and acknowledges what you brought from your Lord; 'and He puts defilement', which is punishment and God's wrath, 'on those who do not reason', meaning those who do not take in from God His proofs, admonitions and signs by which He pointed to Muhammad's prophethood and the truth of what he called them to of God's oneness and giving up rivals and idols. Report 17911, from Ibn Abbas on 'and He puts defilement': anger. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'defilement'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 214}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
