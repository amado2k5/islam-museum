## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 81 to 82 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They will say, 'When we die and we will be dust and bones? Indeed, we will be resurrected": When we die and return us as dust having been afflicted and our bones cleansed of our flesh, "Are we to be resurrected?"—he means: We will be resurrected from our graves alive as we did before death? This is for something that does not exist.
