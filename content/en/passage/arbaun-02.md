## Summary
Hadith of Umar ibn al-Khattab known as the Hadith of Gabriel, the second in the Forty Hadith and in Ibn Rajab's commentary. A man asks the Prophet about Islam, faith and excellence, and then about the Hour; Umar says at the end that the questioner was Gabriel. Ibn Rajab comments at length on Islam, faith, excellence and the Hour. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
