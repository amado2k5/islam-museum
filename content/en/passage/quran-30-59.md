## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Thus Allah seals upon the hearts of those who do not know": Thus Allah seals upon the hearts of those who do not know the truth of what you bring to them, O Muhammad, from Allah of these lessons, admonitions, and clear signs. They do not understand any proof from Allah, nor do they understand from Him what He recites to them from His Book, so they hesitate in their transgression.
