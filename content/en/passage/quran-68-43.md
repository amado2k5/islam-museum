## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43-44 as a single section. He interprets the words, “On the Day when the shin will be uncovered and they will be called to prostrate, but they will not be able”: God Almighty says, “On the Day when the shin will be uncovered,” a group of Companions and Followers of the Companions, scholars of interpretation, said: This will reveal a severe matter.
