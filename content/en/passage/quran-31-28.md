## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He did not create you nor raise you except as a single soul. Indeed, Allah is Hearing and Seeing": He did not create you, O mankind, nor did He raise you upon Allah except like the creation of a single soul and its resurrection. This is because nothing can be impossible for Him, nor can anything He wills be prevented from Him. "He only commands when He wills a thing to say to it, 'Be,' and it is." Thus, it is the same as creating and raising one, and creating and resurrecting all. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
