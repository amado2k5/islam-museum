## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 82 to 84 in a single passage: "And they used to carve safe houses from the mountains." The people of Hijr — Thamud — the people of Salih — carved safe houses from the mountains to protect them from God's punishment, and some said they were safe from destruction lest their homes they carved from the mountains be destroyed, and others said they were safe from death. "Then the cry seized them in the morning": The cry of destruction seized them when they woke up from the fourth day of the day they were promised punishment, and it was said to them: Enjoy your homes for three days. "But what they earned did not avail them": What did not avert from them the punishment of God was what they had been committing before of evil deeds.
