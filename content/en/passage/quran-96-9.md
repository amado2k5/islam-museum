## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9-10 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Have you seen the one who forbids?”: It is mentioned that this verse and those following it were revealed concerning Abu Jahl ibn Hisham, because, as we have been told, he said: “If I see Muhammad praying, I will trample his neck.” It is also reported that he forbade the Messenger of God from praying. So God said to His Prophet Muhammad: “Have you seen, O Muhammad, Abu Jahl who forbids you from praying at the Station of Abraham, while he is turning away from the truth and denying it?” God, may His praise be exalted, amazes His Prophet and the believers at Abu Jahl’s ignorance and his audacity towards his Lord, in forbidding Muhammad from praying to his Lord, while he, despite his favors to Him, denies Him. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
