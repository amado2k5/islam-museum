## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 45 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "they circulate between it and the hot water of An": those criminals whose description is described in Hell circulate among its dishes "and the hot water of An," saying: Between water that has been heated and boiled until its heat ended, and how is its cooking? Everything that has been grasped and reached has reached its peak. From this is the saying: 'They do not look at it,' meaning: its perception and maturity, as Nabigha Bani Dhubyan said: 'And he dyes a beard that betrayed and betrayed with red from the grain of the hollow,' meaning 'he is caught.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
