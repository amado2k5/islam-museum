**Commentary on verses 168-169: those who disbelieve and do wrong, and their eternal dwelling in hell (pp. 411-412)**

Al-Tabari says those who denied Muhammad's mission knowingly, and did wrong by persisting in unbelief, out of envy of the Arabs and insolence towards the Prophet: God would not pardon their sins by withholding punishment, but would expose them through His punishment; He would not guide them to a path by which they gain His reward and reach the garden by keeping to it, but would abandon them until they walk the road to hell, which is unbelief, to dwell in it for ever; and that is easy for God, since whoever He wills it for cannot resist it, the creation is His and the command is His. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 411}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
