## Summary
Hadith of Abu Burda, from his father Abu Musa al-Ash'ari: the Prophet sent him to Yemen and asked about drinks made there; he named bita and mizr, and the Prophet said, "Every intoxicant is unlawful". Ibn Rajab takes it as the root of forbidding every intoxicant that clouds the mind. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
