## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 36 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And none meets it except those who are patient, and none will meet it except those who have great fortune": and no one will be granted evil with good except those who are patient for Allah with hardships and difficult matters. He said: "And He does not meet it," and did not say: "and what he will meet," because the meaning of the statement is: and what is met with this deed of repelling evil with what is best. And His saying: "And none will receive it except one with a great portion," meaning: and no one will receive it except one who has a great share in the righteous deeds, as in Al-Suddi, regarding His saying: "And none will receive it except one with a great share": a grandfather. It is also said: the share that Allah, the Exalted, has informed in this verse that for these people is Paradise. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
