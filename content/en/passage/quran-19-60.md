## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "except for those who repent, believe, and do righteous deeds; those will enter Paradise and will not be wronged at all": These will throw away the bad successors whose attributes were described as wrongdoing, except those who repent and return to the command of Allah and believe in Him and His Messenger. "And do righteousness," meaning: and obey Allah in what He commanded and forbidden, fulfilled His obligations, and avoided His prohibitions. "For those will enter Paradise." He means: those are the ones among them who will enter Paradise exclusively, unlike those who perish because of their disbelief, neglecting prayer, and following desires. And His saying: 'And they will not wrong them in the least,' means: They will not be deprived of the recompense of their deeds at all, nor will they be combined with those who perished from behind any evil among them before their repentance from their misguidance and before their return to obey their Lord in Hell. But they will enter the gate of the people of faith.
