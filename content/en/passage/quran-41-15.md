## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase: "As for 'Aad, they were arrogant in the land without right and said, 'Who is more powerful than us?' Have they not seen that the God who created them is more powerful than them and they used to deny Our signs": 'As for 'Aad,' the people of Hud 'became arrogant against their Lord and arrogantly 'on earth' in arrogance and obedience to something not Allah had permitted them. 'And they said, 'Who is more powerful than us? Have they not seen that Allah is the one who created them?' and He gave them what He had given them of the greatness of creation and the intensity of oppression: 'He is stronger than them.' So they beware of His punishment and fear His authority because of their disbelief in Him and their denial of His messengers. "And they used to deny Our signs," meaning: and they used to deny Our proofs and proofs against them.
