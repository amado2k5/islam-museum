## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Did He not guide them to how many generations We destroyed before them, walking in their dwellings? Indeed, in that are signs. Will they not hear?" As he said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas: "Or was He not guided for them," meaning: or was He not made clear to them. And regarding reading with the yaa in this, the reciters of the cities and the same is the reading among us due to the consensus of the argument among the reciters, meaning: or it is not clear to them that our destruction is not made clear to them the empty generations before them, our Sunnah is for those who follow their path of disbelief in Our verses, so they take heed and are deterred. And the phrase "kama" (how much) is read when it is read as "yāḥād" with a ya, in the nominative nominative case of "biḥḥad." But if it is read with the nūn "or lam nahd," the place of "kamm" and what follows is accusative. And the phrase: "they walk in their dwellings," Allah says: Or He did not clarify to them the abundance of destruction in the previous centuries before them, walking in their lands and lands, like 'Aad and Thamud. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
