## Summary
Malik says it reached him that Marwan ibn al-Hakam used to rule in the case of a man who makes ila against his wife that when the four months pass it counts as one divorce, and he has the right of return while she is in her waiting period. Malik said this was also the opinion of Ibn Shihab. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.66}
