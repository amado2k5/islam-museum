## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He said, 'Do you want to abandon my gods, O Abraham? If you do not stop, I will stone you and abandon me completely': 'Ibrahim' said to Abraham, when Abraham called him to worship Allah and abandon the worship of Satan, and to disassociate himself from idols and idols: 'Do you desire,' O Abraham, 'from' worshiping 'my gods?' If you did not stop mentioning them badly, 'I would stone you,' meaning: 'I will stone you with words,' and that is an insult and a vile word. Similarly, the interpreters have said the same in this regard. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and others.
