## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50 to 51 in a single segment. He explains His saying: "So the evils they have not earned befall them, and those who have wronged among them will be afflicted with the evils of what they have earned, and they are not miracles." So those who said this statement were among the empty nations and the evil deeds they earned, so they hastened to disgrace in the worldly abode, like Qarun, who said when he admonished, "I was only given to him with knowledge from Me, but Allah caused the earth to swallow with him and his house, so he had no group to support him besides Allah, and he was not among the victors." Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: "And those who wronged among these," he says to his Prophet Muhammad: "And those who disbelieved in Allah, O Muhammad, from your people, and wronged themselves and said this saying, 'He will also befall them, and the punishment of the evils they did not earn,' just as those before them were afflicted with their saying, 'And they are not miracles.' He means: 'And they do not miss their Lord, nor do they precede Him in fleeing on earth from His punishment when it befalls them, but it befalls them.'
