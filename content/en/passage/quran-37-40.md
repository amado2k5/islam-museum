## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 41 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "except the sincere servants of Allah": except the servants of Allah whom He sincerely sought on the Day of creation out of His mercy, and for whom happiness was written in the Mother of the Book, for they do not taste punishment, for they are the people of obedience to Allah and the people of faith in Him. And from Qatadah, "except the sincere servants of Allah," he said: This is the praise of Allah. And His saying: In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
