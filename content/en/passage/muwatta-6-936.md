## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar prayed in the mosque of Dhu al-Hulayfa, then went out and rode; when his mount stood level with him, he entered ihram. The editor notes that Abu Musab and al-Shaybani (1069) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.449}
