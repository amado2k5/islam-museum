## Summary
It reached Malik that a needy man asked Aisha while she was fasting, and there was only one loaf in her house. She told her servant: give it to him. The servant said: you have nothing to break your fast with. She said: give it to him; and she did. When evening came, a household or a person sent them a sheep with the bread covering it, and Aisha called me and said: eat from this; it is better than your loaf. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.596}
