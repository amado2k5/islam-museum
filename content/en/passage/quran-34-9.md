## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have they not seen what is before them and what is behind them of the heaven and the earth? If We are to be raised, We will cause the earth to swallow with them or cause an eclipse from the sky to fall upon them. Indeed, in that is a sign for every servant who repents": He says: Have those who deny the Hereafter, the denial of resurrection after death, who say to our Prophet Muhammad: "Do you invent a lie about Allah or is there a Paradise in Him?" He explains what is before them and what is behind them of the heaven and the earth, so they know that wherever they are, My earth and My sky surround them from among Their hands and behind them, and from their right and left sides, so they fear their ignorance and refrain from denying Our signs, out of caution lest We command the earth to swallow up against them, or the sky to fall upon them in pieces. If we grow up, we will do that to them, we will do it. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
