## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on the toothstick: the Prophet said that if it were not a hardship on his community, he would have ordered them to use the toothstick as an obligation. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and Muslim, Abu Dawud and Ibn Khuzayma from Sufyan's chain with similar wording, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.111} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.112}
