## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So each of them We seized for his sin. Among them are those upon whom We sent a crusher, and among them were those whom We seized by the blast, and among them were those with whom We caused the earth to swallow up, and among them We drowned them. And Allah would never wrong them, but they wronged themselves." So We seized all these nations that We mentioned to you, O Muhammad, with Our punishment. "Among them were those upon whom We sent a crusher," and they were the people of Lot, upon whom Allah rained stones of a set of stones. The Arabs call the stormy wind containing small pebbles or snow or Hail and ice are like 'hasib,' and from this is the saying of Al-Akhtal: 'And I have learned that when the tax collectors go to the south, the stubborn ones throw them north, throwing the bite with a snake from its snow until the bite sleeps on the slope in a sleepy state.' Al-Farazdaq said: 'Coming north of the Levant, you will strike us with a scattered cotton candy.' In the passage, there are sayings from Ibn Abbas.
