## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And whoever desires the Hereafter and strives for it while he is a believer—then it is those whose effort is appreciated": whoever desires the Hereafter and seeks it, and it has the deed of its deeds, which is obedience to Allah and what pleases Him. He added the 'sa'i' to the letter 'ha' and 'alif,' which is a metaphor for the Hereafter, saying: 'He strived for the Hereafter, striving for the Hereafter.' Its meaning: 'He did its deed for it so that the listeners would know its meaning.' Its meaning is: he strove for it while he is a believer, affirming Allah's reward, and his great reward for striving for it, not denying the rejection of those who intend the immediate. Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: 'So those' means: 'Their striving' means their act of obedience to Allah 'gratitude,' and Allah thanked them for their effort. That is His good reward for them for their righteous deeds and forgiving them from their evil by His mercy. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
