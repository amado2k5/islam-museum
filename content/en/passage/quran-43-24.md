## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'If I came to you with guidance from what you found your forefathers on, they would say, 'Indeed, we are disbelievers in what you were sent with.'" Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists among your people, who say, 'We found our forefathers upon a nation and we are following in their footsteps.' "Or if I had come to you, O people, from your Lord with a guide to the path of truth, and guide you to the path of guidance 'from what you found' upon which you were your forefathers in religion and faith." 'They said, 'Indeed, we are disbelievers in what you were sent with.' He says: He said that to them, and they replied by saying to him, as those before them of the nations who denied their messengers said to their prophets: Indeed, we disbelieve in what you have sent, O people, meaning denial and denying. This was read by the reciters of the cities except Abu Ja'far: 'Qul or Lawu Ja'aytukum' with the 'ta'. It was mentioned from Abu Ja'far the reciter that he recited it as 'Qul or Lawu Jayanakum' with a 'nun' and 'alif'.
