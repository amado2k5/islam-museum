## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: every joint of a person owes charity each day the sun rises, including justice between two people, helping a man with his mount, a good word, and every step to prayer. Ibn Rajab explains the stages for one who cannot do much, and the reports of the number of joints. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
