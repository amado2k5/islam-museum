## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So when they saw Our power, they said, 'We believe in Allah alone, and we disbelieve in what we used to associate with Him.'" So when these deceitful nations saw their messengers with Our severity, meaning the punishment of Allah that their messengers promised them had befallen them, as in Al-Suddi: "So they did not see Our might," meaning the calamities that befell them. And His saying: "They said, 'We believe in Allah alone,' means: They said: We affirm the oneness of Allah, and we believe that there is no god but Him," and we disbelieve in what we used to associate partners with Him, meaning: We denied the gods that before our time, we used to associate with Allah in our worship and worship them alongside Him, and we took them as gods, so we disassociated ourselves from them.
