## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "Rather, your Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth who created them, and I am among the witnesses over that": Abraham said to them: Rather, I have come to you with the truth, not by play. Your Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth who created them, and I am of the opinion that your Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth who created them without the idols to which you worship and before everyone else as a witness. He says: So worship Him, not these idols which are His creation that neither harm nor benefit. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Abraham.
