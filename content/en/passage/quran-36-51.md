## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 53 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And He blew the trumpet and behold, they were of the ancestors, and they were sent down to their Lord": "And He blew the trumpet." We have mentioned the differences among the dissenters and the correct view regarding it based on its evidences in the past before what is necessary to repeat it here, and this blowing refers to the blowing of resurrection. And His saying: "So they are from the ancestors" means from their ancestors, which are their graves, singular Jadath, and it has two dialects. As for the people of the highlands, they say it with a 'th': Jadath, and as for the people of the lowland, they say it with a 'fa' ('jadf'). In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
