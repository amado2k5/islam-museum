**Commentary on verse 34 (p. 305)**

On [[passage/quran-11-34|11:34]] al-Tabari says: 'my counsel will not benefit you' means my warning you of His punishment and the descent of His might on you for your disbelief in Him will not benefit you, 'if I wished to counsel you' in warning you of that, because my counsel does not benefit you as you do not accept it; 'if God wills to let you go astray' means if God wills to destroy you by His punishment; 'He is your Lord and to Him you will be returned' means you will be brought back to Him after destruction. It is related of Tayyi' that they say 'so-and-so became ghawi', meaning ill, and of others that they say 'aghwaytu so-and-so' meaning I destroyed him, and 'the weanling ghawiya' when it lacks milk and dies; and it is said that 'ghayy' in [[passage/quran-19-59|19:59]] means destruction. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 305}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
