## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 18 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "The Day We will strike the greatest oppression, indeed, We will take retribution": Indeed, O polytheists, if the punishment that will descend upon you and the affliction that will befall you, and then you return in your disbelief and break the covenant you made with your Lord, I will take revenge on you on the day I overwhelmed you with my greatest oppression in the immediate world, destroying you, and Allah revealed them, so they returned, and the Exalted is His great oppression in this world was struck down by the sword. The interpreters differed regarding the great oppression, with some saying: It is God's oppression of the polytheists of Quraysh on the Day of Badr. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and others.
