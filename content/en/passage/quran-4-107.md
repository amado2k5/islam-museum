## Summary
Verse 107 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 107 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 107 and report 10418 (p. 190).** Al-Tabari says the meaning is: do not argue, Muhammad, and contend, for those who betray themselves, meaning those who make themselves traitors by betraying the property of those whose property they betrayed, and they are Banu Ubayriq; do not contend for them against those who claim their rights and the property they betrayed. God does not love one whose attribute is betraying people in their property and committing sin in this and in other things He has forbidden. He says that the interpreters said the same and that the reports from them have already been given. Report 10418 (al-Hasan ibn Yahya, from Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar, from Qatada) says a man betrayed his paternal uncles by taking a coat of mail from them and threw the blame on a Jew who used to visit them; the man's uncle argued with his people, and the Prophet seemed to excuse him; then he joined the land of polytheism, and the verses on opposing the Messenger were revealed about him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 190} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 190}
