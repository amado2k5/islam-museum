## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 10 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Look how they set forth for you parables; they have preferred and cannot find a way": Look, O Muhammad, at those polytheists who likened the likes to you by saying: He is bewitched, they deliberately preferred the way and strayed from the path of guidance and guidance "but they cannot." He means: they find no "way" to the truth except in what I sent you with and from the way they went astray. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Ishaq.
