## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn al-Fadl, from al-Araj, that Uthman ibn Affan made the wives of Ibn Mukmil his heirs, from him, because he had divorced them while he was ill. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.83}
