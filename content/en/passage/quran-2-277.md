## Place in the museum

([[passage/quran-2-277|2:277]]), pp. 21 and 22. Al-Tabari explains that this is God's report that those who believed, meaning those who affirmed God, His Messenger and what he brought, of the prohibition of usury and eating it and the rest of the laws of his religion, did the good works He ordered, kept the obligatory prayer within its limits and performed it by its customs, and paid the obligatory zakat from their wealth, after what had passed from them of taking usury before the admonition came, have the reward of those works, faith and charity with their Lord on the day they need it in their return, and need not fear that day His punishment for what passed from them in their pre-Islamic state and unbelief before the admonition came, of taking usury, because of their turning back, repentance and affirming God's promise and threat, and will not regret leaving what they left in this world of taking and practising usury when they see the abundant reward of God; this is al-Tabari's explanation. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 21} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 22}
