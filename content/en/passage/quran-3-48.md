## Summary
Verse 48 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 48 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The readers' difference over the verb of teaching.** Al-Tabari says the readers differed in reading this verb. Most readers of the Hijaz and Medina and some Kufan readers read it with the letter of the third person, following what precedes, so that they attached the report to its like in the report on creating and in God's saying to a thing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 421}

**The first person plural reading.** Al-Tabari says most Kufan readers and some Basrans read the verb with the letter of the first person plural, attaching it to God's saying that He reveals it, as if He said 'that is of the accounts of the unseen which We reveal and teach him the Book', and held that what comes after the verb of revealing is in its relative clause to the end of the verse, with the verb of teaching then attached to it. Al-Tabari's own choice is that these are two different readings that do not differ in meaning, so whichever the reader uses is right, since both agree that it is a report from God that He teaches Jesus the Book and what is said He teaches him; he says this is the start of God's report to Mary of what He will do for the child He gave her tidings of, in honour, high rank and merit. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 422}

**The book, wisdom, the Torah and the Gospel.** Al-Tabari says God told her that He creates from her a child without male or husband and teaches him the book, which is the writing he writes with his hand; wisdom, which is the sunna He reveals to him outside a book; the Torah sent down to Moses, which was among them since Moses; and the Gospel of Jesus, which did not exist before him, though God told Mary before Jesus' creation that He would reveal it to him. He told her and named it because she knew from the books sent down that God would send a prophet to whom a book called the Gospel would be revealed, so He told her that the prophet whose description she had heard was the child He gave her and announced to her. Report 7080 (al-Qasim, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Ibn Jurayj) says of the teaching of the book that it is by his hand. Report 7081 (Bishr, from Yazid, from Sa'id, from Qatada) says wisdom is the sunna; 7082 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Abdullah ibn Abi Ja'far, from his father, from Qatada) says the same and that Jesus used to read the Torah and the Gospel; 7083 (al-Qasim, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Ibn Jurayj) says wisdom is the sunna. Report 7084 (Ibn Humayd, from Salama, from Ibn Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr) says God told Mary what He intended for him: the Torah is the one that was among them since Moses and the Gospel another book He brought forth for him that they had no knowledge of except the mention that it would come from the prophets before him. The editor says it is in the Sira of Ibn Hisham (2:230), completing the reports whose last was 7079, and that Ibn Hisham omits the verb of teaching and has 'after him' for 'before him', al-Tabari's text being the correct one. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 422} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 423} These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
