## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they say, 'It is nothing but our worldly life. We die and live, and nothing destroys us except time.' And they have no knowledge of that. Indeed, they only think." These polytheists about whom the report was previously mentioned: There is no life except our worldly life in which we are, with no life except it, denying the resurrection after death. Also: Qatadah narrated from "And they said, 'It is nothing but our worldly life": meaning, by my life, this is the statement of the Arab polytheists. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
