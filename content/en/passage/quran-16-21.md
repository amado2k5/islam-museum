## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "dead not alive and they do not realize when they will be resurrected": Allah Almighty says to these polytheists from Quraysh: 'And those whom you invoke besides Allah, O people, are dead not alive.' And Glorified be His praise made them dead but not alive, for there were no souls in them. Also: Qatadah narrated His saying: "Dead, not alive, and they do not realize when they will be resurrected," which are these idols that worship besides Allah as dead without souls and do not harm or benefit their people. There are two aspects to raising the dead: one is to inform those who are to be resurrected, and the other to resume. And His saying: 'And they do not perceive' means: And your idols which you invoke besides Allah do not know when they will be resurrected. It is said that the disbelievers meant by this that they do not know when they will be resurrected. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
