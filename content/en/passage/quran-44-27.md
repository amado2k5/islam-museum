## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 28 in a single segment. He interprets the phrase "and a blessing in which they were fruitful": and they were brought out of a blessing in which they were fruitful, scattered, and smooth. Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase: "fruits," so the majority of the reciters of the cities read it except Abu Ja'far the reciter "fruitful" in the sense described. Abu Raja' al-Atardi, al-Hasan, and Abu Ja'far al-Madani read it as "fakhin" meaning: two gentle and flourishing ones. The correct reading in my opinion in this regard is the reading followed by the reciters of the cities, which is "fakhin" with an alif meaning smooth. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
