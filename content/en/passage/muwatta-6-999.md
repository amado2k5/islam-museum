## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: the one in ihram does not marry, nor propose, for himself or for another. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1179) and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.469}
