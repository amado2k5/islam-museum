**Commentary on verse 38 (pp. 536-537)**

Then he says on "say to those who disbelieve, if they stop, what is past will be forgiven them; and if they return, the way of the ancients has already passed" [[passage/quran-8-38|8:38]]: He says to His prophet, say, Muhammad, to those who disbelieved among the polytheists of your people, if they stop what they persist in of their unbelief in God and His Messenger and fighting you and the believers, and turn back to faith, God will forgive them what is past of their sins before their belief; "and if they return" means if these polytheists return to fighting you after the blow I dealt them at Badr, My way has already passed with the ancients of them at Badr and with others of past generations when they rebelled, called My messengers liars and did not accept their counsel, of bringing swift vengeance on them, so I will bring on these, if they return to war with you, the like of what I brought on them. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "stopping", "past" and "way". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 536}

The interpreters spoke likewise; reports 16070, 16071, 16072 and 16073, from Mujahid: "the way of the ancients has passed" in Quraysh at Badr and other nations before that; report 16074, from Ibn Ishaq: "if they return" to war on you, "the way of the ancients has passed", meaning those of them killed at Badr; report 16075, from al-Suddi: if they return to fight you, the way of the ancients has passed, of the people of Badr. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 536} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 537}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
