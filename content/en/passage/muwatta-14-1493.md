## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn al-Fadl from Nafi' ibn Jubayr ibn Mut'im from Abdullah ibn Abbas: the Messenger of God said that the unmarried woman has more right over herself than her guardian, and the virgin is asked for her consent, and her consent is her silence. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.28}
