## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 6 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We judged for the Children of Israel in the Book, that you might cause corruption on the earth twice and you will proclaim a great exaltation": We have explained before that the meaning of judgment is to finish something, then it is used for every established matter. The interpretation here: And your Lord completed to the Children of Israel what He revealed from His Book to Moses by informing them and informing them, "You may cause corruption on the earth twice," meaning: Do not disobey Allah, O people of the Children of Israel, and do not oppose His command in His land twice, and do not declare great arrogance. He means: And do not be arrogant against Allah by daring to do so with great arrogance. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
