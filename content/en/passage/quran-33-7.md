## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And [mention] when We took from the prophets their covenant and from you and from Noah and Abraham and Moses and Jesus, son of Mary, and We took from them a solemn covenant": This was recorded in the Book, as We wrote everything that is in the Book. "And when We took from the prophets their covenant," that was also recorded in the Book, meaning the covenant, and we have explained this with its evidences in the past. "And from you," O Muhammad, "and from Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus, son of Mary, and We took from them a solemn covenant," meaning: and We took from them all a firm covenant that they would believe one another. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
