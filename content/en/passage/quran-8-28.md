**Commentary on verse 28 (pp. 486-487)**

Then he says on "know that your wealth and children are a trial and that with God is a great reward" [[passage/quran-8-28|8:28]]: he says to the believers, know, believers, that your wealth which God gave you and your children whom He gave you are a test and an affliction; He gave them to you to test and try you by them, to see how you act in paying God's due on you in them and keeping to His command and prohibition in them; "and that with God is a great reward" means good and a great recompense for your obedience to Him in what He commanded and forbade in your wealth and children by which He tried you in this world; so obey God in what He charged you with in them and you will gain by it the abundant part of His reward in your return. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "trial" and "reward". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 486}

Then report 15934, from Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud on "your wealth and children are a trial": none of you but is wrapped in a trial, so whoever seeks refuge, let him seek refuge in God from misleading trials; and report 15935, from Ibn Zayd: the trial is the test, and he recited [[passage/quran-21-35|21:35]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 486} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 487}
