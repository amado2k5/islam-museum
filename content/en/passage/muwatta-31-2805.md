## Summary
Malik transmits from Sumayy, client of Abu Bakr, from Abu Salih, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: travel is a piece of torment; it deprives one of you of his sleep, his food and his drink; so when one of you has satisfied his need of travel, let him hasten back to his family. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.576}
