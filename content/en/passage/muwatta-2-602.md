## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, who said: I never saw my father at a funeral except walking in front of it, and then he would go to al-Baqi and sit until they passed by him. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.310}
