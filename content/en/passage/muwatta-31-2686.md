## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki, from Jabir ibn Abdallah, that the Messenger of God said: close the door, tie the waterskin, turn over the vessel or cover it, and put out the lamp; for the devil cannot open a bolt, untie a knot, or uncover a vessel, and the wicked mouse sets the people's house on fire. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.517}
