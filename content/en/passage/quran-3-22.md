## Verse 22 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, p. 287)

Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-22|3:22]]) says the sense of the glad tidings here is: tell them, Muhammad, and inform them that they have with God a painful, aching punishment. 'Those' are the ones who deny God's proofs. The ruin of their works in this world means that they gained by them neither praise nor thanks from people, since they were in error and falsehood; God raised no remembrance for them on that account but cursed them, tore away their veils and showed what they hid of the ugliness of their deeds on the tongues of His prophets and messengers in the books He sent down to them, leaving them a reproach as long as the world lasts; that is their ruin in this world. In the next, He prepared for them the punishment He described in His Book and informed His servants that their works become dust with no reward, since they were unbelief in God, so the requital of their people is abiding in the Fire. The absence of helpers means that these people have no helper to help them against God when He takes vengeance on them for their past crimes and audacity against Him and so rescues them from Him. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on the words for announcing, painful, ruin and help, and says he dropped from the manuscript and print a word in al-Tabari's sentence that he took for a slip of the copyist, since the sense requires it gone. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 287}
