## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those among you who display their wives, they are not their mothers. Their mothers are none but Allah, their children, and they say [evil] [in speech] and falsehood. And indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Forgiving": Those who forbid their wives for themselves, Allah forbids their mothers' backwardness. They say to them: You are like the backs of our mothers, and this was the case when a man divorced his wife in the pre-Islamic era. Also: Abu Qilaba narrated that zihar was a divorce in the pre-Islamic era, where if someone spoke about it, he would never retract it regarding his wife, so Allah Almighty revealed what He revealed in him. The reciters differed in its reading; most reciters in Medina read it except Nafi', and the majority of Kufa reciters except Asim: 'yudhirun' with a fatha on the ya, a shadda on the dhā, and affirmation of the alif. They also read the other as meaning 'they pretend,' then the 'ta' was merged into the ṣā', making them a stressed ḍā'. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
