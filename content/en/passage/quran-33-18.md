## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Allah may know those among you who are disabled and those who say to their brothers, 'Come to us,' and they will come to us in harm except a little": Allah may know those among you who hinder people from the Messenger of Allah and turn them away from him and from the witnesses of the war with him, out of hypocrisy and abandonment of Islam and its people. "And those who say to their brothers, come to us": That is, come to us and call upon Muhammad, and do not witness his scene with him, for We fear destruction for you with his destruction. "And they do not come to the believers except a little," meaning: they do not witness war and fighting if they do except as a form of excuse and to protect the believers from themselves. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
