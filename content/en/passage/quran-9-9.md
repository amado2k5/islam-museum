**Commentary on verse 9 (pp. 150-151)**

Al-Tabari says: these polytheists whom God told you, believers, to kill wherever you find them bought a small price with God's verses, giving up the following of God's proofs against them in exchange for a little worldly goods. It is reported of them that they broke the covenant between them and the Messenger for a meal Abu Sufyan ibn Harb fed them. Report 16514, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: Abu Sufyan ibn Harb fed his allies and left the allies of Muhammad; report 16515 is the same through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj. The words "they barred from His way" mean they kept people from entering Islam and tried to turn Muslims from their religion; "evil is what they used to do" means evil was what they did in buying disbelief with belief and error with guidance and barring from God's path those who believed or wished to. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 151}
