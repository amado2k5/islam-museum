## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the call while travelling: Abdullah ibn Umar on a journey would add nothing to the iqama except at dawn, when he would give the call and the iqama, and he used to say the call belongs to the leader to whom people gather. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Ibn Wahb in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it, and Abd al-Razzaq and Ibn Abi Shayba transmitted it from Nafi' and Ayyub by other channels. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.121}
