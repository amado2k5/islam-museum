## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet, O you who have believed, send blessings upon him and grant him peace," meaning: Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad, as Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet. O you who have believed, send blessings upon him," meaning: they bless the Prophet. It may be said that the meaning of this is that Allah has mercy on the Prophet, and His angels pray for him and seek forgiveness, because in the Arabic language of the Arabs, prayer is only supplication. We have explained this earlier in this book with its evidences, so it is necessary to repeat it. "O you who have believed, send blessings upon him." Allah says: O you who have believed, supplicate to the Prophet of Allah Muhammad. "And greet him with the greeting of Islam." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Ibrahim.
