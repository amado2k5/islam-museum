## Summary
Verse 140 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 140 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The difference in reading (p. 236).** Abu Ja'far says the reciters differed in reading this place. Most reciters of the Hijaz, Medina and Basra read it with a fatha on the first letter of the word for the wound in both places, meaning killing and wounds: if killing and wounds touch you, Companions of Muhammad, the same has touched the people of your polytheist enemies. Most reciters of Kufa read it with a damma in both places (the bracketed words are the editor's addition, which he inferred from the context). The editor notes that a passage has dropped out through the copyist, the explanation of the damma reading, and that al-Tabari perhaps cited here what al-Farra' said in Ma'ani al-Qur'an (1: 234); the note continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 236}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 236 in the commentary on verse 140 and continue from p. 237. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 236}
