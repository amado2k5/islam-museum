## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, We are the inheritors of the earth and whoever is upon it, and to Us they will return": Do not grieve for the denial of these polytheists, O Muhammad, of what You brought them of the truth, for to Us is their return and their destiny, and the fate of all creation except them. We are the inheritors of the earth and all the people upon it, by their destruction from it and its remaining, with no owner but us. Then it is upon us to recompense each of them for their deeds upon their return to Us, the one who does good by his kindness, and the one who does evil by his wrongdoing.
