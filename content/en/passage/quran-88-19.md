## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And to the mountains, how they were firmly set”: And to the mountains, how they were raised upright, not falling and spreading out upon the earth, but He made them upright and solid by His power, not leaving their place nor moving from their position. And: On the authority of Qatadah, regarding “And to the mountains, how they were firmly set”: You ascend the mountain of Al-Saykhud for most of the day, and when you reach its summit, you reach gushing springs and hanging fruits, which no hands have plowed or worked—a blessing from God and a means of fulfilling one's purpose. And he says: And he quotes in the section sayings of Qatadah.
