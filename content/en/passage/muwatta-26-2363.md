## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Said ibn al-Musayyab used to say: if a man conditionally frees his slave-girl, he may have intercourse with her, but he may not sell her or give her away, and her child is of her standing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.373}
