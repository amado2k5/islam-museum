## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Baqara 2:272, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «ليس عليك هداهم».

## Full report (Arabic source text)

رسول الله- "لا تصدقوا إلا على أهل دينكم" فأنزل
