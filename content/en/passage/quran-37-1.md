## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 3 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and the saffat are ranks": Abu Ja'far said: Allah Almighty swore by His remembrance with the saffat, the stern ones, and the subsequent ones. As for the saffats: they are the angels who are the saffat to their Lord in the heaven, and they are the plural of 'safā'. The saffat is the plural of a plural, and thus came the interpretation of the interpreters. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Al-A'mash.
