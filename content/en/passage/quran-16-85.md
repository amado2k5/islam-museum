## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And when those who have wronged see the punishment, it will not be lightened for them, nor will they look": And when those who denied you, O Muhammad, and denied your prophethood and the nations who followed the path of the polytheists among your people, see the punishment of Allah, nothing will save them from Allah's punishment, because they are not permitted and they will excuse themselves, and the punishment will be lightened by the excuse they claim. "Nor do they look." He means: they do not hope in punishment, because the time for repentance and repentance has passed. This is not a time for them, but rather a time for recompense for their deeds. They should not look with reproach to be reproached for repentance.
