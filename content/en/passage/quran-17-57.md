## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those are those whom they call upon seeking to their Lord the means. Whichever is nearer, and who hope for His mercy and fear His punishment, indeed the punishment of your Lord is ever, a warning": These are the ones whom these polytheists call as lords, "seeking the means to their Lord." He means: those who are called seek lords to their Lord closeness and intimacy, because they are people of faith in Him, while the polytheists worship them besides Allah "which of them is nearer" to which of them is closer to Him in righteous deeds and diligence in worship, and they hope for those actions "His mercy" And they fear" in contrast to His command: "His punishment is indeed the punishment of your Lord." O Muhammad, "He was warned" and pious. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
