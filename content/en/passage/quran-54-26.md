## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They will know tomorrow who is the most evil liar": God said to them: You will know tomorrow on the Day of Resurrection who is the most evil liar among you, the people of Thamud, and who is our messenger Salih when you respond to your Lord. This interpretation is the interpretation of those who read it as "you will know" with the letter 'ta', which is the reading of the general public of Kufa except for Asim and Al-Kisai. As for interpreting this according to the reading of those who read it with a ya, which is the general reading of the people of Medina and Basra, Asim, and Al-Kisai, Allah said: "They will know tomorrow who is the worst liar," omitting mention of what Allah said, to suffice with the meaning of the speech on it. The correct view in this regard is that they are two well-known readings, each read by scholars among the reciters, so whichever one the reader reads, he is correct, due to the similarity of their meanings and their correctness in grammar and interpretation. In the passage, he quotes statements from Mujahid.
