## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And Allah said, 'Do not take two gods; He is only one God, so fear Me.'" And Allah said to His servants: Do not take any partners for Me, O people, nor worship worshippers, for when you worship others besides Me, you make for Me a partner and no partner. He is only one God and one deity, and I am that. "So fear Me," meaning: So fear Me and fear Me for your disobedience to Me if you disobey Me and worship others besides Me, or associate partners in your worship of Me.
