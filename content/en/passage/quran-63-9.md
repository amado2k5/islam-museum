## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “O you who have believed, let not your wealth nor your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that - then it is those who are the losers,” is explained as follows: O you who have believed in Allah and His Messenger, “let not your wealth” means: let not your wealth distract you, “nor your children” from the remembrance of Allah. The word “dhikr” (remembrance) comes from the root “alha” (to distract), as in the phrase “alhaytuhu ‘an kadha wa kadha” (I distracted him from such and such). The verb “alha” means to be distracted, and from this root comes the saying of Imru’ al-Qays: “And like you, a pregnant woman I visited, and a nursing mother, and I distracted her from her…” The amulets of Muhwil, and it was said: What is meant by mentioning God, may His praise be exalted, in this place is the five daily prayers. And the passage quotes statements from al-Dahhak.
