**Commentary on verse 43 (pp. 272-274)**

Al-Tabari says this is a reproach from God with which He reproached His prophet over his permission to those hypocrites to whom he gave leave to stay behind from him when he set out for Tabuk to fight the Romans. God says: "God has pardoned you", Muhammad, whatever occurred from you in giving leave to those hypocrites who asked it of you not to go out with you and to stay behind from you, before you knew their truthfulness from their lying; "why did you permit them?", for what reason did you permit them? "Until it becomes clear to you who spoke the truth and you know the liars" means it was not fitting for you to let them stay behind from you when they said to you "had we been able we would have gone out with you", until you knew who among them had an excuse for staying behind and who had none, so that your permission to those you permitted would be given knowing their excuse, and you would know the liar among them who stayed behind out of hypocrisy and doubt in God's religion. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 273}

Report 16763, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih: on "God pardon you, why did you permit them", he said people had said ask the Messenger's leave, and if he permits you, sit, and if he does not permit you, sit. Report 16764, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: He reproached him as you hear, then sent down the verse in the sura of Light giving him leave to permit them if he wished, which is [[passage/quran-24-62|24:62]], making it a concession in this. Report 16765, from Amr ibn Maymun al-Awdi, through al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz, Sufyan ibn Uyayna and Amr ibn Dinar: there were two things the Messenger did on which he was not commanded anything, his permission to the hypocrites and his taking from the captives, and God sent down "God pardon you, why did you permit them"; report 16766, from Qatada, through Ibn Waki, Ubayd ibn Sulayman and Sa'id ibn Abi Aruba: God then sent down in the sura of Light "when they ask your leave for some affair of theirs, give leave to whom you wish of them". Report 16767, from Murriq, through Salih ibn Mismar, al-Nadr ibn Shumayl and Musa ibn Sarwan: "God pardon you", he said, his Lord reproached him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 274}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
