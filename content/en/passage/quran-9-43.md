**Commentary on verse 43 (p. 272)**

Al-Tabari says this is a reproach from God with which He reproached His prophet over his permission to those hypocrites to whom he gave leave to stay behind from him when he set out for Tabuk to fight the Romans. God says: "God has pardoned you", Muhammad, whatever occurred from you in giving leave to those hypocrites who asked it of you not to go out with you and to stay behind from you, before you knew their truthfulness from their lying; "why did you permit them?", for what reason did you permit them; the sentence continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 272}

We stop here at the foot of p. 272, in the middle of the commentary on verse 43 (in the explanation of "why did you permit them"); the next batch begins at p. 273. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 272}
