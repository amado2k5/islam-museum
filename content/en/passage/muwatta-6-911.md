## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: he was asked about a garment touched by perfume, after which the scent of the perfume had gone; may one wear it in ihram? He said: yes, as long as it does not have saffron or wars dye. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1043) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.439}
