## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to an independent commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 18–20. Al-Tabari clarifies that the obedient believer and the denier are not equal before God, and therefore the pronoun is pluralized because the intended meaning is the type of believers and the sex of the sinners, then he mentions the punishment for each: Gardens of refuge for the believers and Hellfire for those who disobedient.
