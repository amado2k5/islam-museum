## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50 to 51 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Gardens of Eden, opening doors for them": His saying, the Exalted: "Gardens of Eden," is a statement about the best return, a biography of it, meaning: gardens of residence. We have explained the meaning of this with its evidence, and mentioned the differences in what was previously mentioned that would make repeating it unnecessary in this context. And Qatadah, regarding the phrase "Gardens of Eden," he said: Umar asked Ka'b what is Eden? He said: O Commander of the Faithful, there are palaces in Paradise made of gold inhabited by the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the leaders of justice. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
