## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the recitation in the Friday prayer, sitting with arms around the knees, and whoever leaves it without excuse. Yahya transmits from Malik from Damra ibn Sa'id al-Mazini from Ubayd Allah ibn Abdullah ibn Utba: al-Dahhak ibn Qays asked al-Nu'man ibn Bashir what the Prophet used to recite on Friday after the surah of al-Jumu'a, and he said he recited the surah of al-Ghashiya. The editor cites Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Darimi, Ahmad and al-Bayhaqi, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.168}
