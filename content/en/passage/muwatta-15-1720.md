## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar that he heard Abdullah ibn Umar recite the verse of divorce with the reading: at their waiting period. Malik said he meant that a man divorces once in each period of purity. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.102}
