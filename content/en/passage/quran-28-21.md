## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 22 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So he went out from it fearfully, waiting. He said, 'My Lord, save me from the wrongdoing people.'" Moses left the city of Pharaoh, fearing that the soul would kill him, "waiting," meaning he waited for the seeking to catch up with him and seize him. Also: from Qatadah: "So he left it fearfully, waiting," fearing that the soul would kill him, waiting for the seeking: "He said, 'My Lord, save me from the wrongdoing people.'" He said: Abu Sufyan narrated to us, from Muammar, from Qatadah: "He left it fearfully, waiting," he said: Afraid of killing a soul, waiting for the seeker to seize him. From Ibn Ishaq, he said: It was mentioned to me that he left on his face fearful, watching not knowing which face he would take, and he said: "My Lord, save me from the wrongdoing people." Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase "So he left it fearful, waiting," he said: He was waiting out of fear of the seeking. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
