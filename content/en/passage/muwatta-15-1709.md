## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that Ali ibn Abi Talib spoke about the two arbiters mentioned in God's book, in the case of a breach between a husband and wife: they should appoint an arbiter from his family and an arbiter from hers, and the two have the power to separate them or bring them together. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.97}
