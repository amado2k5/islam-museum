## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Aisha, in the chapter on the summary of menstruation: she used to comb the Prophet's head and tend his hair while she was menstruating. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i, al-Darimi, Abu Awana and al-Tirmidhi in the Shama'il, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.105}
