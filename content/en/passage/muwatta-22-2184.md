## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the practice among us regarding a man who refers another man to a debt owed to him by a third: if the referred debtor becomes insolvent, or dies leaving no estate to pay, the referee has no claim against the one who referred him, and does not return to the original debtor. Malik said: this matter has no dispute among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.296}
