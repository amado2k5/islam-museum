## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 83 to 84 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So do not hasten upon them; indeed, We have prepared for them a punishment." He says: "So do not hasten upon these disbelievers by seeking punishment and destruction for them." O Muhammad, "We only prepare for them a punishment," meaning: We only delay their destruction so that they may increase in sin. We count all their deeds and count them until their breaths to recompense them all, and We have not abandoned hastening their destruction for the good we intended for them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
