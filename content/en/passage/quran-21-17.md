## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "If We wanted to take a pastime, We would have taken it from Ourselves, if We were to do it": If We wanted to take a wife and a son, we would have taken it from our own side, but we would not do that, and it is neither appropriate nor appropriate for us to do so, because God should not have a son or a wife. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Al-Hasan.
