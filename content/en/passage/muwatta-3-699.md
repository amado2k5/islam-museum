## Summary
Yahya said: Malik said: the best that has been heard regarding one who has sheep with two herders who are separate, or herders in different regions, is that they are all gathered on their owner, who pays his charity; likewise, a man whose gold or silver is scattered in the hands of various people should gather it and pay the zakat due on all of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.351}
