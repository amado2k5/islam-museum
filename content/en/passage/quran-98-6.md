## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 as one section. His statement, “Indeed, those who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture and the polytheists will be in the fire of Hell, abiding eternally therein. Those are the worst of creatures,” explains that those who disbelieved in God and His Messenger Muhammad, and denied his prophethood, from among the Jews, Christians, and polytheists, will be “in the fire of Hell, abiding eternally therein.” This means they will remain therein forever, never leaving it, nor will they die therein. “Those are the worst of creatures.” God, may His praise be exalted, says: These who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture and the polytheists are the worst of all that God created. The Arabs do not pronounce the hamza in “creatures,” and the reciters of the various regions read it without the hamza, except for a few instances. It is mentioned that Nafi’ ibn Abi Nu’aym said that some people narrated that he used to pronounce it with a hamza, and he took it to mean God’s saying: “Before We created it” and that it is a noun of action from that.
