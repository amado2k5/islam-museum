## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 19 as a single section. He explains the phrase, “And his wealth will not avail him when he falls,” meaning, may His praise be exalted, that what wealth will avail this miserly person, who was self-sufficient and independent of his Lord, on the Day of Resurrection, “when he falls”? Then the scholars of interpretation differed in their interpretation of “when he falls.” Some said its interpretation is: when he falls into Hell, meaning when he plunges into it and plummets. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
