## Summary
A saying of Malik: a gushing fluid also obliges this if it comes from touching; but as for one who thinks of something until a gushing fluid comes out of him, I see nothing owed by him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1237) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.515}
