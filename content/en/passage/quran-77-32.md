## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 34 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Indeed, it throws sparks like castles” as meaning that Hell throws sparks like castles. The reciters of the various regions read it as “like castles” with a silent “sad.” Those who read it this way differed in its meaning; some said it refers to a single castle. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
