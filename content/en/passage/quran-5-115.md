## Summary
Verse 115 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 115 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

This is God's answer to the people in what they asked their prophet Jesus to ask his Lord, the sending down of a table: he said, I am sending it down to you, disciples, and will feed you with it; "whoever disbelieves after this" means whoever, after its descent on you and my feeding you with it, denies my message to him, rejects the prophethood of my prophet Jesus and disobeys me in what I commanded and forbade, "I shall punish him with a punishment I shall inflict on no one in the worlds", of the worlds of his time. The people did so, as we are told: they denied and disbelieved after it was sent down to them, and were punished, as we have heard, by being turned into apes and swine. Report 13024, from Qatada: we are told they were turned into swine. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 232}

Reports 13025 and 13026, from Abdullah ibn Amr: the people with the severest punishment on the day of resurrection are three: the hypocrites, those of the people of the table who disbelieved, and the house of Pharaoh. Report 13027, from al-Suddi: "whoever disbelieves after this", after the table has come to him, "I shall punish him with a punishment I shall inflict on no one in the worlds" means with a punishment I shall inflict on no one in the worlds other than the people of the table. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 233}

The editor identifies Abu al-Mughira al-Qawwas, who related from Abdullah ibn Amr and from whom Awf related, notes what Abu Zur'a was asked about him, and that Ibn Ma'in held him trustworthy.

Verse 115 closes here at p. 233, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-5-116|5:116]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
