**Commentary on verse 52 (pp. 291-293)**

Al-Tabari says God is commanding His Prophet to say to these hypocrites whose description He gave and whose affair He made plain: do you await for us anything but one of the two best outcomes, either victory over the enemy and a conquest for us through our overcoming them, in which are reward, spoils and safety, or being killed by our enemy, in which are martyrdom, the winning of Paradise and deliverance from the Fire, both of which we love and do not dislike; and we await for you that God strike you with a swift punishment from Himself that destroys you, "or by our hands", so that we kill you; "so wait, we are waiting with you", that is, wait, for we are waiting with you for what God will do with us and to what the affair of each party of us and you will come. The editor refers to earlier explanations of awaiting and of the best outcome. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 291}

Report 16796, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: "one of the two best" means conquest or martyrdom, and another time he said it means being killed, which is martyrdom, life and provision, "or He disgraces you by our hands". Report 16797, from him through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: being killed, in which are life and provision, or prevailing, and God gives him a great reward, like His words about whoever fights in God's way and is killed or prevails; the printed reference reads al-Ma'ida 74, while the wording quoted is that of verse 74 of al-Nisa. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 292}

Reports 16798, 16800 and 16801, from Mujahid: "one of the two best" means being killed in God's way and prevailing over God's enemies; report 16799, from Ibn Jurayj, who says it reached him from Mujahid: being killed in God's way and prevailing. In report 16801, from Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn Abbas: "punishment from Him" means by death, "or by our hands" means killing. Report 16802, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "do you await for us anything but one of the two best", only conquest or being killed in the way of God, and then the words about God striking them with punishment from Himself or by our hands, meaning killing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 293}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
