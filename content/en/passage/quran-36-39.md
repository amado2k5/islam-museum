## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "And the moon We determined for it phases until it returned like the old Arajun": Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase: "And the moon We determined it as stations," some Meccans, some Medinans, and some Basrans read it as "and the moon" as a nominative nominative conjunction with the sun, since the sun was conjoined to the night. They followed the moon as the sun in grammatical grammatical form, because it is also one of the signs, just like night and day are two signs. Therefore, according to this reading, the interpretation of the statement: And a sign for them is the moon We have determined for it stations. Some Meccans, some Medinans, some Basranis, and most reciters of Kufa read this accusatively: "And the moon We decreed" meaning: "And We decreed the moon in dwellings, just as we did with the sun," so they replaced it with the 'ha' of the sun in meaning, because the waw in it is for the later verb. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, and Ikrimah.
