## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who were arrogant said to those who were weak, 'Did we prevent you from guidance after it had come to you? Rather, you were criminals.'" He said: "Those who were arrogant said" in this world, and they headed in misguidance and disbelief in God "for those who were weak" therein, so they followed the misguided among them, as they said to them, "If not for you, we would have been believers. Did we prevent you from guidance?" And we prevented you from following the truth "after it came to you" from God, making it clear to you, "Rather, you were criminals." So your preference prevented disbelief By Allah, for faith in following guidance, and faith in Allah and His Messenger.
