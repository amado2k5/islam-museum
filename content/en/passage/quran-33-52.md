## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "It is not lawful for you to have women after all, nor to replace them with their husbands, even if they please your right hand, except for what your right hand possesses. And Allah is over all things Watchful": The interpreters differed in the interpretation of the verse: "Women are not lawful for you after all." Some said: The meaning is: It is not lawful for you to marry after your women whom you have chosen, so choose Allah, His Messenger, and the Hereafter. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
