## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Whatever mercy Allah opens for people, none can withhold it, and whatever He withholds, there is no messenger after Him, and He is the Exalted, the Wise": The keys to goodness and all its locks are in His hands, so whatever good Allah opens for people, no one can withhold it, because that is His command that no one can control. Likewise, whatever good He closes from them, He neither extends it to them nor opens it for them, so no one can open it for them except Him, for all matters are to Him and to Him. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
