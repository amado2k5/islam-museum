## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 139 to 140 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "So they denied him and destroyed them. Indeed, in that is a sign, and most of them were not believers": 'The Messenger of their Lord 'Ad denied Hud, and the 'ha' in the phrase "so they denied him" is from the mention of Hud. "So We destroyed them," he says: So We destroyed 'Aad by denying Our Messenger. "Indeed, in that is a sign." God Almighty says: Indeed, in Our destruction is 'Aad, by denying their Messenger, as a lesson and admonition for your people, O Muhammad, those who deny what You brought them from your Lord. "And most of them were not believers," He says: "And none of them destroyed more than those who believed in the knowledge of Allah." "And indeed, your Lord is the Exalted in Might" in His vengeance on His enemies, "the Most Merciful" toward those who believe in Him.
