## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 49 in a single segment. He interprets the phrase "fruits while they are honored": His saying fruits is a response to the known provision, which is why it was raised. And His saying: "and they are honored" means: they are with those who have the known provision in Paradise, honored by the honor of God with which He honored them. He also quotes in the segment from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Dhahhak.
