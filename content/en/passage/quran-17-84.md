## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, everyone acts according to his likeness, for your Lord knows best who is more guided to the way": The Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: Say, O Muhammad, to the people: You all act according to his own way and method. "Indeed, your Lord knows best who among you is the most guided to the way," meaning: Your Lord knows best who among you is the most guided path to the truth than others. In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
