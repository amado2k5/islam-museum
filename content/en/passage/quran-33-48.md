## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 45–48. God addresses His Prophet by saying that He sent him as a witness over His nation to deliver the message, as a glad tidings of Paradise, a warner of Hellfire, calling to God by His permission and a shining lamp, commanding him not to obey the disbelievers and hypocrites, to leave their harm, to rely upon God, and sufficient is God as a Disposer of affairs.
