## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly sent Noah and Abraham and placed in their descendants prophethood and the Scripture, but among them are guided, and many of them are defiantly disobedient." And indeed We sent Noah to Our creation, and Abraham was his close friend to them as a messenger. "And We made prophethood and the Book among their descendants." Thus prophethood was among their descendants. To them were revealed the books: the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms, the Criterion, and all the known books. "And among them is a guided," meaning: from their descendants is a guide to the truth, insightful. "And many of them," meaning from their descendants "Rebellious" means misguidance, disobedient to God and disobeying Him.
