## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And you see the mountains, which you think are rigid while they pass by, the bitterness of the clouds. The creation of Allah, who perfected everything. Indeed, He is Acquainted with what you do": "And you see the mountains," O Muhammad, "you think they are standing upright" "while they pass." Like the one that He said: Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "And you see the mountains you think are rigid," meaning they are standing. It is said: "and they pass by the clouds" because they gather and then move. The opinion is that their abundance is considered to be standing and moving briskly, as Al-Ja'di said: "With a shallower like a rock, you think they are standing for a pilgrim, while the riders are negligent in His saying: 'The work of God who perfected everything' and the most reliable of His creation." In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Jurayj.
