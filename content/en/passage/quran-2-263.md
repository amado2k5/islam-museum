## Summary
Verse 263 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 263 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari then explains ([[passage/quran-2-263|2:263]]) from p. 520: a kind word means a fair word and a man's prayer for his Muslim brother, and forgiveness means his covering over the poverty and bad state he knows of in him, which is better with God than charity that he gives and follows with harm, that is, complaining of him over it and harming him because of it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 520} The editor refers to the earlier explanation of the kind and the forgiving and to the language indexes. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 520} Report 6037 begins at the foot of p. 520 and we stop before it. These reports are given as transmitted, al-Tabari's explanation is attributed to him, and the corrections and references are the editor's, with no verdict from the museum.

The rest of ([[passage/quran-2-263|2:263]]) from p. 520. Report 6037 from al-Dahhak: the sense of a kind word and forgiveness being better than charity followed by harm is that keeping one's wealth is better than spending it and following it with reproach and harm. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 520} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 521} Al-Tabari then explains the verse's close on God's two attributes: the self-sufficient means He has no need of what they give in charity, and the forbearing that He does not hasten punishment on one of you who reproaches his charity and harms the one he gives it to; the editor refers to the earlier explanation of the knowing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 521} Ibn Abbas is reported (6038) as saying that the self-sufficient is the one perfect in his sufficiency and the forbearing the one perfect in his forbearance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 521}
