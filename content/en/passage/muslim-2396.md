## Summary
A report of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas in the chapter on the virtues of Umar, in Sahih Muslim, in the story of Umar asking permission to enter while the women hurried to cover themselves. It is among the best-known texts on Umar's virtues in Sunni tradition. {cite:sahih-muslim}
