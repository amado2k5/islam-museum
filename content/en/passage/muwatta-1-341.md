## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the merit of congregational prayer over praying alone. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' from Abdullah ibn Umar: the Prophet told that congregational prayer surpasses the prayer of one who prays alone by twenty-seven degrees. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188}
