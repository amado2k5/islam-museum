## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 36 to 37 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Pharaoh said to Yahaman, son of Lay, a fortress for Ali with the most extensive causes": Pharaoh said when the believer advised him from his family with what he admonished him and warned him against killing Moses, the Prophet of God, and warned him of God's might for his saying, 'Kill him,' as he warned his minister Haman: "O Haman, build me a structure for me with the most conveying causes," meaning constructively. We have explained the meaning of the edifice in the past with its evidences, which makes it unnecessary to repeat it here. "Perhaps the most conveyive of the causes" The interpreters differed on the meaning of the causes in this context, and some said: the causes of the heavens are their routes. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
