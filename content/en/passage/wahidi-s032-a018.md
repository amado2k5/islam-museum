## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of as-Sajda 32:18, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «أفمن كان مؤمنا كمن كان فاسقا».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

نزلت في علي بن أبي طالب والوليد بن عقبة.
