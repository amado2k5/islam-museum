## Summary
Malik transmits from Said ibn Abi Said al-Maqburi, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: it is not lawful for a woman who believes in God and the Last Day to travel a day and a night's journey except with a close male relative of hers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.574}
