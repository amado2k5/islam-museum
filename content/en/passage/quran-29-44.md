## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah created the heavens and the earth in truth. Indeed, in that is a sign for the believers": Allah, O Muhammad, created the heavens and the earth alone, alone by His creation; no partner associates with Him in their creation. "Indeed, in that is a sign": He means that is in His creation as proof for those who believe in proofs when they see them, and the signs when they see them.
