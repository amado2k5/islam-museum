## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 157 to 159 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "so they slaughtered her and became regretful": Allah Almighty says: Thamud disobeyed the command of their prophet Salih, so they slaughtered the she-camel that Salih had told them not to harm. They became regretful about her slaughter, but their regret did not benefit them, and the punishment of Allah seized them, which Salih had threatened them with, and destroyed them. "Indeed, in that is a sign." He says: Indeed, in the destruction of Thamud for what they did from their slaughter was the she-camel of Allah, and the opposite The matter of the Prophet Salih is a lesson for those who consider him among your people, O Muhammad. "And most of them were not believers." He says: Most of them will not believe in the knowledge of God: "And indeed your Lord," O Muhammad, is "the mighty amusement" in taking revenge on His enemies, the "Merciful," against those who believed in Him among His creation.
