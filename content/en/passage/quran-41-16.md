## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So We sent upon them a scorching wind on days of misfortune to make them taste the punishment of disgrace in the life of this world, and the punishment of the Hereafter is more disgraceful, and they will not be helped": So We sent upon 'Aad a roaring wind. Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the roar, with some saying: It means it is a strong wind. In the passage, statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
