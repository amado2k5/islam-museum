## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "The Day We will call every people by their Imam; whoever is given his book in his right hand - those will recite their record and will not be wronged [by a thread]": The interpreters differed on the meaning of the Imam, whom Allah, Glorified and Exalted, mentioned that He calls upon every people by him. Some said: He is His Prophet, and whoever follows him in this world and follows him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-Rabi'.
