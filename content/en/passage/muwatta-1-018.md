## Summary
A report from Malik through Yahya that he was told Abu Hurayra used to say that whoever catches the rak'a has caught the prostration, and whoever misses the recitation of the Opening chapter in prayer has missed much good. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it, in al-Bayhaqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.42}
