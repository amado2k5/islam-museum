## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "If there were gods besides Allah, they would have been corrupted. So glory be to Allah, Lord of the Throne, above what they describe": If there were gods in the heavens and the earth suitable for worship besides Allah, who is the Creator of all things, and He has worship and deities suitable only for Him, "they would have become corrupt." He means: to corrupt the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth. "So glory be to Allah, Lord of the Throne, above what they describe," says the Exalted: so He exalts Him to Allah and absolves Him from the lies these polytheists fabricate against Him. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "If there were gods besides Allah in them, they would be corrupted. So glory be to Allah, Lord of the Throne, above what they describe," he glorified himself when slandered about him.
