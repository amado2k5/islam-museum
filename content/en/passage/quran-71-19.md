## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 22 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And Allah has made for you the earth a carpet”: God Almighty informs us of what Noah said to his people, reminding them of his Lord’s blessings: “And Allah has made for you the earth a carpet” upon which you settle and which you use as a resting place. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd in this section.
