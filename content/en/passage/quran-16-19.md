## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 20 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Allah knows what you conceal and what you reveal": And Allah, who is your God, O people, knows what you conceal within yourselves from your consciences and conceal it from others, so what you reveal with your tongues and limbs, and what you reveal with your tongues, limbs, and actions. He is the Accountant of all of this upon you so that He may recompense you with it on the Day of Resurrection: the doer of good with his kindness, the wrongdoer with his wrongdoing, and your questions for what you have done in this world of gratitude for the blessing He has bestowed upon you. What are you counted and what you did not count? And His saying: "And those whom you invoke besides Allah do not create anything, and they are creating," Allah says: "And your idols whom you invoke besides Allah, O people, are gods who create nothing, yet they are created. So how can there be a god that was not created, planned, and has no benefit or harm for themselves?"
