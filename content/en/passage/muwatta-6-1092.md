## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, who said: I asked Aisha, Mother of the Believers, while I was young: have you seen God's words: al-Safa and al-Marwa are among the rites of God; whoever makes the pilgrimage to the House or the lesser pilgrimage, there is no sin on him for going around them; so is there anything on a man who does not go around them? Aisha said: no. If it were as you say, it would be: there is no sin on him for not going around them. That verse was sent down about the Ansar, who used to enter ihram for Manat, which was at Qudayd; they were reluctant to circumambulate between al-Safa and al-Marwa; when Islam came they asked the Messenger of God about it, and God sent down the verse. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1316) and Suwayd (545) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.500}
