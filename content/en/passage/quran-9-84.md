## Summary
Verse 84 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/death-of-abdullah-ibn-ubayy|Death of Abdullah ibn Ubayy]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 84 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 84 (pp. 405-407)**

Al-Tabari says to His Prophet Muhammad: do not pray, Muhammad, over anyone of these hypocrites who stayed behind from going out with you who dies, ever, "nor stand at his grave", meaning do not take charge of his burial and interment, from the saying that so-and-so "stood in" for so-and-so's affair when he saw to it for him; "they disbelieved in God and His Messenger" means they denied the oneness of God and the message of His Messenger, "and died as transgressors", meaning died outside Islam, departed from God's command and prohibition. The editor explains the word for interment as one of the terms of early jurists. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 405}

It is mentioned that this verse came down when the Prophet prayed over Abdallah ibn Ubayy. Report 17050, from Ibn Umar, through Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna, Sufyan ibn Waki', Suwar ibn Abdallah, Yahya ibn Sa'id, Ubaydallah and Nafi': the son of Abdallah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul came to the Messenger of God when his father died and said "give me your shirt that I may shroud him in it, and pray over him and ask forgiveness for him"; he gave him his shirt and said "when you have finished, let me know"; and when he wanted to pray over him, Umar pulled him back and said "has God not forbidden you to pray over the hypocrites?", and he said "rather He gave me a choice, and said 'ask forgiveness for them or do not ask forgiveness for them'"; he said, and he prayed over him; he said, and God, blessed and exalted, sent down "never pray over any of them who dies, nor stand at his grave", and he gave up praying over them. The editor notes that the report is related by al-Bukhari in his Sahih by several routes and by Muslim, and that Ibn Kathir cites it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 406}

Report 17051, from Ibn Umar, through Ibn Waki', Abu Usama and Ubaydallah: when Abdallah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul died, his son Abdallah came to the Prophet and asked him to give him his shirt to shroud his father in, and he gave it; then he asked him to pray over him, and Umar ibn al-Khattab stood and took hold of the Prophet's garment and said "Ibn Salul! Will you pray over him when God has forbidden you to pray over him?", and the Prophet said "my Lord only gave me a choice, and said 'ask forgiveness for them or do not ask forgiveness for them; if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times God will never forgive them', and I shall add to seventy"; he said "he is a hypocrite!", and the Messenger of God prayed over him, and God sent down "never pray over any of them who dies, nor stand at his grave". Report 17052, from Jabir ibn Abdallah, through Suwar ibn Abdallah al-Anbari, Yahya ibn Sa'id, Mujalid and Amir: the chief of the hypocrites died in Medina, and left a request that the Prophet pray over him and that he be shrouded in his shirt, and he shrouded him in his shirt, prayed over him and stood at his grave, and God, blessed and exalted, sent down "never pray over any of them who dies, nor stand at his grave". The editor notes that Ibn Kathir cites Jabir's report from al-Bazzar's Musnad and says its chain is acceptable. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 407}

Report 17053, from Anas, through Ahmad ibn Ishaq, Abu Ahmad, Salama and Yazid al-Raqashi: the Messenger of God wished to pray over Abdallah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul, and Gabriel took hold of his garment and said "never pray over any of them who dies, nor stand at his grave". The editor says Yazid al-Raqashi is weak, indeed abandoned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 407}

We stop here at the foot of p. 407, in the middle of the commentary on verse 84 (after report 17053); the next batch begins at p. 408. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 407}
