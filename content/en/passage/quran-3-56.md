## Summary
Verse 56 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 56 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of verses 56 and 57.** Al-Tabari says that those who denied the prophethood of Jesus, opposed his creed, called the truth he brought a lie, said falsehood about him and ascribed him to what is not fitting, whether Jews, Christians or other kinds of religion, are the ones God will punish severely: in this life by killing, captivity, humiliation and poverty, and in the hereafter by the fire of Hell, abiding there for ever. He explains that they have no helper to be a barrier against God's punishment or to ward it off by strength or intercession, because He is the Almighty, the Lord of vengeance. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 465}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
