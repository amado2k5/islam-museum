## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29-31 as a single section. He explains the phrase “and its mixture is of Tasnim” as follows: The mixture of this nectar is of Tasnim. Tasnim is derived from the verb “Sannamathum al-‘Ayn Tasnim,” meaning “I poured it down upon them from above.” Thus, in this context, it means: Its mixture is of water that descends upon them from above and flows down upon them. Mujahid and al-Kalbi held this view. Ibn Abi Najih, on the authority of Mujahid, said regarding the word “Tasnim”: “Tasnim means to rise.” Ma‘mar, on the authority of al-Kalbi, said regarding the word “Tasnim”: “Tasnim is poured down upon them from above, and it is the drink of those brought near to God.” Other scholars of interpretation said: It is a spring with which the nectar is mixed for the Companions of the Right, while those brought near to God drink it pure. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-A'mash, Al-Kalbi, Sufyan, and Ata.
