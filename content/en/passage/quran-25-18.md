## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "They said, 'Exalted are You, that we should not have taken besides You any protectors, but their enjoyment and their ancestors until they forgot the Reminder and were a people detached.": The angels whom these polytheists worshipped besides Allah and Jesus said: 'Purify it for You, our Lord, and disassociate Him from what these polytheists added to You. We should not have taken besides You as their guardians besides them. But their enjoyment of wealth, O Lord, in this world and in health until they forgot the Reminder and became a doomed people who were overwhelmed.' Upon them is misery and abandonment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
