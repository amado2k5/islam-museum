**Commentary on verse 50: "Is it the judgment of ignorance they seek? Who is better than God in judgment for a people who are certain?", p. 394**

Al-Tabari says: do these Jews who brought the case to you and did not accept your ruling, though you ruled with justice, seek the judgment of ignorance, that is the rulings of idol-worshippers among the polytheists, while they have God's book that makes clear the truth of the ruling you gave, which is the truth that may not be opposed? Then He rebukes them and calls their act ignorant: who is better than God in judgment, you Jews, for one certain of God's oneness and accepting His lordship? What ruling is better than God's, if you are certain that you have a lord and are people of oneness who acknowledge Him?  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 394}

**Commentary on verse 50, continued, p. 395**

Mujahid said as al-Tabari does: reports 12153, 12154 and 12155 from him say that "the judgment of ignorance they seek" refers to the Jews.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 395}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
