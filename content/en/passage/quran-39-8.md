## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when harm touches man, he calls upon his Lord, turning to Him in repentance; then when He grants him a favor, he forgets what he used to call upon before and sets up for Allah equals to mislead him from His way. Say, 'Enjoy your disbelief for a little; indeed, you are of the companions of the Fire.'" And when a person is afflicted with a calamity in his body—whether illness, disability, hardship, or hardship—he calls upon his Lord, meaning: He has sought help from his Lord who created him from the severity of that, and he longs to Him to uncover what has befallen him of the severity of it. And His saying: 'turning to Him' means: repenting to Him from what he had previously done of disbelief in him, associating gods and idols with him in his worship, returning to obedience to him. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
