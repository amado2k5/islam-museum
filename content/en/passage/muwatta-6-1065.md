## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that when he circumambulated the House he touched all the corners, leaving none, except the Yemeni one, unless he was overcome. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1288) and Suwayd (551) transmitted it from Malik, and that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Mamar from Hisham. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.492}
