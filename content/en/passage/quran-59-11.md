## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen those who disbelieved, saying to their brothers who disbelieved among the People of the Book, 'If you are expelled, We will surely go out with you and will never obey anyone among you? And if you are fought, We will surely support you.' And Allah bears witness that they are liars": Have you not looked with the eye of your heart, O Muhammad, and you see at those who were hypocrites, and as mentioned, Abdullah ibn Abi ibn Salul, Wadi'ah, Malik, the sons of Nawfal, Suwayd, and Da'as, who sent to Banu Nadir when the Messenger of Allah sent them to war, to remain steadfast and refrain from fighting, for We will not deliver you, and indeed You fought and we fought with you, and if you go out, we will go out with you. They waited for their support, but they did not. God cast terror into their hearts, so they asked the Messenger of God to evacuate them and stop their blood, on the condition that they had nothing of their wealth carried by the camels except the ring. And from Yazid ibn Ruman. Mujahid said something about this: And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding the verse: "Have you not seen those who hypocrite?" he said: Abdullah ibn Abi ibn Salul, and Rifa'ah or Rafi'ah ibn Tabut. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, and Ibn Ishaq.
