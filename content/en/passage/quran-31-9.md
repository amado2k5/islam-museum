## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "We will abide therein by the promise of Allah, and He is the Mighty, the Wise": they will remain therein indefinitely. "Allah's promise is true," meaning: Allah promised them a true promise, without doubt or success, "and He is the Mighty," meaning He is severe in His vengeance against the polytheists and those who hinder His path, "the Wise" in managing His creation.
