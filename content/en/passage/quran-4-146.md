**Commentary on verse 146: the repentant who reform and hold fast to God (pp. 340-342)**

Al-Tabari says this is God's exception for those who repent of their hypocrisy, reform, make their religion sincerely God's alone, disown the gods and rivals, and believe His Messenger. They are not to be with those who persist in hypocrisy until death overtakes them; rather God will place them among the believers in the garden, honoured, and will reward their repentance with a great gift. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 340}

Repentance is returning to the truth and acknowledging God's oneness and His Messenger's truth; reform is putting right one's deeds by doing what God commanded, performing His obligations and refraining from what He forbade; holding fast is adhering to God's covenant and pact; sincerity is making obedience and deeds for God alone, not for people's show, not in doubt of the reward, but seeking nearness to God and His face. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 341}

God gives the believers a great reward for their belief, which is ranks in the garden, as He gave the hypocrites who died in hypocrisy places in the fire, the lowest of them, because God promised His believing servants what is mentioned and threatened the hypocrites for their hypocrisy. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 341}

In this sense is the saying of Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman in report 10747, through Ibrahim: Hudhayfa said, people who were hypocrites will enter the garden! Abd Allah asked how he knew; Hudhayfa was angry and withdrew. When they dispersed, Alqama passed him and called him: your companion knows what you said! Then he recited the whole exception in the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 342}
