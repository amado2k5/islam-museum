## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Hakim ibn Hizam bought food that Umar ibn al-Khattab had ordered for the people. Hakim sold the food before taking possession of it. When Umar heard of it, he returned it to him and said: do not sell food you have bought until you have taken possession of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.168}
