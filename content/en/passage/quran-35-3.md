## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O mankind, remember the favor of Allah upon you. Is there any creator besides Allah who provides for you from the heavens and the earth? There is no deity except Him, so how can you deceive?" He says to those who associate partners with Him from the people of the Messenger of Allah from Quraysh: "O mankind, remember the favor of Allah" which He has bestowed upon you by opening for you of His bounty what He has opened and spread for you of the livelihood He has spread out. And reflect and observe: Is there any Creator except the Originator of the heavens and the earth who holds in His hand the keys to your sustenance and its locks? "He provides you from the sky." And the earth," so they worshipped Him below Him. "There is no god but Him," meaning: There is no deity worthy of worship except the One who created the heavens and the earth, the Able of all things, in whose hand are the keys to things, their treasuries, and the shackles of all that. So do not worship anything besides Him, for He alone can benefit you and harm you except Him. So dedicate your worship to Him, and devote yourselves to divinity. "So how can you turn away?" He says: What aspect is there from your Creator and Provider, who quotes in the passage from Qatadah.
