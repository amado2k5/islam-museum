## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The king will stand up for you today in the land, visible in the land. So who will help us against the might of Allah? If it comes to us, Pharaoh will say, 'I will show you nothing but what I see, and I will not guide you except the way of guidance.'" Allah Almighty says, informing about what the believer said from Pharaoh's family to Pharaoh and his people: "O my people, today you have the kingdom visible in the land," meaning: the land of Egypt. He says: You have the authority and the kingdom visible over the Children of Israel in the land of Egypt. "So who will help us against the might of Allah?" He says: So who will repel from us the might and power of God if it befalls us, and His punishment if it befalls us? Pharaoh said, "I will show you only what I see," meaning: Pharaoh replied to this believer who forbade the killing of Moses: What opinion do you, O people, of advice and advice except what I see for myself and for you righteousness and correctness? And I only guide you to the path of righteousness. He says: I only call you to the path of truth and correctness in the matter of Moses and his killing, for if you do not kill him instead of your religion, and spread corruption in your land.
