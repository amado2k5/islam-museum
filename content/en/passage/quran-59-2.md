## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He is the One who expelled those who disbelieved from among the People of the Book from their homes at the beginning of the Gathering. You did not think they would come out but thought their fortresses would prevent them from Allah, so Allah came to them from where they did not expect, and cast terror into their hearts, destroying their houses with their own hands and the hands of the believers, so take heed, O you of those with insight": meaning, Allah the Exalted is His remembrance by saying: "He it is Who expelled those who disbelieved from among the People of the Book from their homes at the beginning." "Al-Hashr" is Allah who expelled those who denied the prophethood of Muhammad from among the People of the Book, namely the Jews of Banu Nadir, from their homes. This was because they left their homes and homes when they reconciled with the Messenger of Allah on the condition that he would protect them over their blood, women, and children, and that they would have what the camels had lost of their wealth and that their homes and other wealth would be left for him. The Messenger of Allah responded to that, so they left their homes. Some of them went to the Levant, and some to Khaybar. That is the saying of Allah, the Almighty: "It is He who expelled those who disbelieved from The People of the Book from their homes until the first of the Resurrection." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
