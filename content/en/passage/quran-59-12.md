## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "If they are expelled, they will not go out with them; and if they are fought, they will not support them; and if they do help them, they will turn their backs and then will not be helped": If Banu al-Nadir are expelled from their homes and they are displaced, the hypocrites who promised them victory will not come out with them. If Muhammad fights them, the hypocrites who promised them victory will not support them. And if the hypocrites support Banu Nadir, they will turn their backs away from Muhammad and his companions, fleeing from them. They have abandoned them, "then they will not be helped," meaning: Then God does not grant victory to Banu Nadir over Muhammad and his companions, but rather forsakes them.
