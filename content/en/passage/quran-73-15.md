## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 16 as one section. His statement, “Indeed, We have sent to you a Messenger as a witness over you, just as We sent to Pharaoh a Messenger,” is explained as follows: “Indeed, We have sent to you,” O people, “a Messenger as a witness over you,” regarding the response of those among you who answered My call, and the refusal of those among you who refused to answer, on the Day you meet Me on the Day of Resurrection, “just as We sent to Pharaoh a Messenger.” He says: Just as We sent before you to Pharaoh of Egypt a Messenger calling him to the truth, “but Pharaoh disobeyed the Messenger” whom We sent to him, “so We seized him with a severe punishment.” He says: So We seized him with a severe punishment, and We destroyed him and all those with him, and he was among those who… They say: "A herbage that is unpalatable," meaning it is not easily digestible, and the same applies to food. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
