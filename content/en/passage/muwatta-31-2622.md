## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: a woman should not ask for the divorce of her sister so that she may empty her bowl and marry, for she has only what has been decreed for her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.481}
