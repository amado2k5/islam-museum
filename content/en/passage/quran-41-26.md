## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 26 to 27 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And those who disbelieve said, 'Do not listen to this Qur'an and make vain therein that you may prevail.'" He said: "And those who disbelieve" said about Allah and His Messenger from the polytheists of Quraysh: "Do not listen to this Qur'an and make it false," meaning: They said to those who obey them among their allies among the polytheists: Do not listen to the reciter of this Qur'an when he recites it, do not listen to him, and do not follow what is in it and act upon it, as my father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father narrated to me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "And those who disbelieve said, 'Do not listen to this Qur'an and go astray therein that you may prevail.'" He said: This is the view of the polytheists. They said: Do not follow this Qur'an and its desires. And His saying: 'And they went aloud in it' means: 'Cover up falsehood of speech when you hear its reciter read it so that you do not hear it and do not understand what is in it.' In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
