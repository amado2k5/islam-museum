## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Hijr 15:39, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «أذن للذين يقاتلون بأنهم ظلموا».

## Full report (Arabic source text)

الله- فأنزل اللههذه الآية.
