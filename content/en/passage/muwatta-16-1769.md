## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Safiyya bint Abi Ubayd told him that Hafsa, mother of the believers, sent Asim ibn Abdallah ibn Saad to her sister Fatima bint Umar ibn al-Khattab, that she might nurse him ten sucklings so that he could come in to her while he was still a suckling infant. She did so, and he used to come in to her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
