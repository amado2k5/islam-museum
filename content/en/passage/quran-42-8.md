## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if Allah had willed, He could have made them one nation, but He admits whom He wills into His mercy. But the wrongdoers will have no protector or helper": If Allah wanted to unite His creation upon guidance and make them one religion, He would have done so. And "He would have made them one nation" means they are of one religion and a group gathered on one religion, "but He admits whom He wills into His mercy." He says: He did not do that and would make them one nation, but He admits whomever He wills among His servants into His mercy, meaning He admits them into His mercy by granting him success to enter his religion, which His Prophet Muhammad was sent with: "And the wrongdoers have no protector or helper," meaning: The disbelievers in Allah will have no protector to take care of them on the Day of Resurrection, nor will any helper help them from Allah's punishment when He punishes them, saves them from His punishment, and takes retribution from those who punish them.
