## Summary
In the Book of the Virtues of the Qur'an, the chapter on the collection of the Qur'an: the report of Zayd ibn Thabit himself. After the killing of the reciters at Yamama, Umar warned Abu Bakr, who hesitated before Umar pressed him. The report describes the material used for the collection. It is the founding text for the history of the codex. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
