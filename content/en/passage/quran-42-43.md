## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And whoever is patient and forgives, indeed, that is for him who resolves matters": And whoever is patient with the offense of those who wronged him, and forgives the wrongdoer for his sin, but he does not gain victory from him, and he is capable of victory seeking the pleasure of Allah and His abundant reward. "Indeed, that is for the one who resolves matters." He means: His patience and forgiveness are the sins of the wrongdoer to him, for those who resolve on the matters to which He enjoined His servants and resolved to act upon them. "And whoever Allah leads astray, then he will have no protector after Him," meaning: And whoever Allah forsakes from guidance, he has no protector to follow him, who guides him to the right path and guides him after Allah has led him astray. "And you see the wrongdoers when they saw the punishment."
