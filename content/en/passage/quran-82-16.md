## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 19 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “and they will not be absent from it”: These wicked people will never leave Hell and will not be absent from it, but they will abide therein eternally, just as the righteous will abide in Paradise, similar to the phrase “and they will not be brought out of it.” He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah in this section.
