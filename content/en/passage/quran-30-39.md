## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And whatever you give from usury to increase in the wealth of people, it will not increase with Allah. And whatever you give of zakah seeking the sake of Allah, it is those who are the multipliers": And what you have given to one another, O people, so that the reward of others may increase by returning to Him, from those who gave it. "So it does not increase with Allah," meaning: it does not increase with Allah, because its owner did not give it to the one who gave it seeking His pleasure. "And whatever you give of zakat" means: and whatever charity you give seeking the pleasure of Allah, "those are the ones who give charity with their wealth seeking Allah's pleasure." It means those who give charity with their wealth seeking Allah's pleasure, "they are the multipliers." It means: they are the ones who have double the reward and recompense, from the Arabic saying: "The people become fat and thirsty, when their camels become fat and thirsty." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
