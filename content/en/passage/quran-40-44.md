## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 45 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So you will remember what I say to you and entrust my affairs to Allah. Indeed, Allah is Seeing of His servants": Allah Almighty says, informing about what the believer said from the family of Pharaoh to Pharaoh and his people: So you will remember, O people, when you witness the punishment of Allah that has befallen you, and you encounter what you encountered, the truth of what I say and the truth of what I have told you, that the wasteful are the companions of the Fire, as Ibn Zayd said regarding the saying: "Then you will remember what I say to you," so I asked him: Is that in the Hereafter? He said: Yes. And His saying: "And I entrust my affairs to Allah" means: I entrust my affairs to Allah, entrust it to Him, and rely on Him, for He is sufficient for those who rely on Him. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
