## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they hasten you with the punishment, and Allah will never fail His promise. And indeed, a day with your Lord is like a thousand years of what you promise." It means: And they will hasten you, O Muhammad, your people with partners with you regarding the punishment you have promised them of Allah, because of their associating partners with Him and denying you concerning what you brought them from Allah in this world. And Allah will not break His promise to them of punishing them with His punishment and punishment in the immediate aftermath. So He did that, and He fulfilled His promise to them, killing them on the Day of Badr. The interpreters differed regarding the day when He, the Exalted, said: "And indeed, a day with your Lord is like a thousand years of what you count." Which day is it? Some said: It is one of the days when God created the heavens and the earth. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ikrimah, and Samak.
