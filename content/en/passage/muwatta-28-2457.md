## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that men from Iraq said to him: Abu Abd al-Rahman, we buy palm dates and grapes and press them into wine, and sell it. Ibn Umar said: I call God and His angels, and whoever hears of the jinn and of humankind, to witness that I do not command you to sell it, buy it, press it, drink it, or give it to drink; and he said it is among what the prohibition on in the text of the Qur'an. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.416}
