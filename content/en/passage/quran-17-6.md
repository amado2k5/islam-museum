## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Then We returned to you the oppression against them and provided you with wealth and sons and made you greater in troops": Then We guided you, O Children of Israel, to these people whom He, Glorified and Exalted, described as sending them against them, and that guidance and retribution were for them against them, as Al-Suddi mentioned in his account that the Children of Israel raided them, captured them, and recaptured what was in their possession. And according to others: the king who conquered them released what was in his hands of their captives and returned what had been from their wealth to them without fighting. In the statement of Ibn Abbas, which Atiyah narrated from him, it is that Allah guided them from their enemy Goliath until they killed him. We have mentioned all this with his chains of transmission earlier: 'And We have provided you with wealth and children,' meaning: We increased in what We gave you of wealth and children. And His saying: 'And We made you more numerous,' means: And We made you more numerous than them. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
