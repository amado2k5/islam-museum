## Summary
Malik says that if he divorces her while ill before he has consummated the marriage, she is entitled to half the dower, to inheritance, and owes no waiting period. If he consummates the marriage and then divorces her, she is entitled to the full dower and inheritance. Among us, the virgin and the previously married woman are alike in this. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.84}
