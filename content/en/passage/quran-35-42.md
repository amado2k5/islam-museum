## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 42 to 43 in a single segment. He explains His saying: "And they swore by Allah with the effort of their oaths that if a warner came to them, they would surely be more guided than one of the nations." But when a warner came to them, it only increased them in aversion. These polytheists swore by Allah with the effort of their oaths, saying: the strongest faith, so they went to extremes. If a warner came to them from Allah warning them of the might of Allah, "that they would be more guided than one of the nations," meaning: that they would be more guided to the path of truth and more accepting of what the warner brings them from Allah, from one of the nations that came before them. "So when a warner came to them," he means by the warner: Muhammad, he says: When Muhammad came to them warning them of Allah's punishment for their disbelief, as in Qatadah, regarding His saying: "But when a warner came to them," he is Muhammad. And His saying: "It only increased them in aversion," means: what increased them from faith in God, following the truth, and following the guidance of the path increased them except aversion and flight. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
