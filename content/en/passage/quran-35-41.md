## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah holds the heavens and the earth lest they perish, and if they remove, if He were to hold them from anyone after Him, indeed, He was ever Forbearing and Forgiving": "Indeed, Allah holds the heavens and the earth" so that they do not move from their places. "And if they depart," he means: "Even if they were taken away, if anyone after him held them," he means: no one else could hold them except Him. The word "lan" in the phrase "and even if they remove" is placed in the place of "la" because they are answered with the same answer, so they are similar in meaning. Similarly, the phrase: "And if We send a wind and they see it yellowish, they will continue to disbelieve after it," meaning: "And if We had sent a wind," as He said: "And if You had brought those who were given the Scripture," meaning: if you had come. We have explained this in the past in a way that would not need to be repeated here. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
