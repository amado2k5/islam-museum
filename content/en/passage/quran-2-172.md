## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "O you who believe, eat of the good things We have provided you and be thankful to God, if it is Him you worship" al-Tabari says "those who believe" are those who affirmed God and His Messenger and acknowledged servitude to Him, from al-Dahhak; "eat of the good things We have provided" means of the lawful provision I made lawful for you, of what you used to forbid and I had not forbidden; "be thankful to God" means praise Him as He deserves for the blessings; and "if it is Him you worship" means if you submit to His command, eat of what He allowed and leave the footsteps of Satan in forbidding it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 316} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 317}
