## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 57 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "Thus and We married them to the seas of 'Ayn": Just as We gave these righteous in the Hereafter honor by admitting them to Gardens and clothing them in sandals and al-istibraq, likewise We honored them by marrying them there as houris among the pure women, who are pure and white, their singular is houris. Mujahid used to say about the meaning of houris: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "And We married them to the seas of 'Ayn," he said: We married them to houris. He quotes in the verse from Mujahid and Qatadah.
