## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: I dislike boys wearing any gold, because it reached me that the Messenger of God forbade the wearing of gold rings; so I dislike it for men, both the old and the young. The editor notes that the hadith forbidding gold rings is in the two Sahihs from Abu Hurayra. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.498}
