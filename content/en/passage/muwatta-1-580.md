## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told the Prophet used to supplicate, asking God to do good deeds, to abandon wrong deeds, to love the poor, and to take him to Himself untried if He wills a trial for people. The editor notes that one copy has "I turned", that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik from Yahya ibn Sa'id, and Ibn Abd al-Barr (al-Tamhid 24/321–325) said he knows these words only in the report of Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish al-Hadrami, a good report transmitted by trustworthy transmitters, and it is also transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mu'adh ibn Jabal, Thawban and Abu Umama. The editor adds that Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish did not hear from the Prophet, as al-Bukhari, al-Tirmidhi, Ahmad and Abu Hatim stated, but it is transmitted from him from Malik ibn Yukhamir from Mu'adh ibn Jabal in Ahmad (5/243) and al-Tirmidhi (3235), who called it sound and good. The report of Ibn Abbas in Abd al-Razzaq, Ahmad and al-Tirmidhi (3233, 3234) is flawed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.299} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.300}
