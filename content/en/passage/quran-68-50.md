## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 50-52 as a single section. He explains the words, “So his Lord chose him and made him one of the righteous,” meaning that the Lord chose the companion of the whale, selecting and choosing him for his prophethood, “and made him one of the righteous,” meaning one of the messengers who acted upon what their Lord commanded them and refrained from what He forbade them. And the words, “And indeed, those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes,” mean that, may His praise be exalted, those who disbelieve, O Muhammad, would almost pierce you with their eyes due to the intensity of their enmity towards you, and would remove you and throw you down when they looked at you out of rage against you. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Kalbi, Ibrahim, and Ata.
