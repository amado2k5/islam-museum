## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when those who associate others with Him see their partners, they say, 'Our Lord, these are our partners whom we used to invoke besides You,' and say to them that you are liars." And when the polytheists see on the Day of Resurrection what they used to worship besides Allah, of gods, idols, and others, they will say: Our Lord, these are our partners in disbelief in You, and the partners whom we used to call gods besides You. Allah Almighty said: "So they cast," meaning their partners whom they used to worship besides Allah. The phrase means: They said to them: You are liars, O polytheists. We did not call you to worship Us. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
