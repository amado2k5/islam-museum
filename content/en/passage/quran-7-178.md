**Commentary on verse 178 (p. 276)**

Al-Tabari says: guidance and leading astray are in God's hand; the guided one, who walks the way of truth and keeps to the straight course in his religion, is he whom God guided to that and enabled to reach it, and the astray is he whom God abandoned and did not enable to obey Him; and he to whom God does that is the loser, meaning the lost. He has explained the senses of loss, guidance and straying in several places in his book, and the editor refers to the language indexes. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 276}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
