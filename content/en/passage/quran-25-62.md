## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He is the One who made the night and the day a succession for whoever wishes to be remembered or wishes to be grateful": The interpreters differed in the interpretation of the phrase: "He made the night and the day a succession." Some said it means that Allah made each of them a successor to the other, meaning that whatever one missed in an act done for Allah was fulfilled in the other. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
