## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "Do they not know that God knows what they conceal and what they declare?", al-Tabari says those Jews blaming their fellows do not realise God knows what they hide of their disbelief and mutual reproach and what they declare in telling the believers "we believe", which he reports from Qatada and Abu al-'Aliya. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 256} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 257}
