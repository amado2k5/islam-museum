## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of Destiny of Sahih Muslim: the command to strive, seek God's help and not give in to weakness, and the prohibition of opening the door of "if" against destiny. It is one of the most comprehensive texts on reliance. {cite:sahih-muslim}
