## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "The believers are but brothers, so make peace between your brothers and fear Allah that you may receive mercy": Allah Almighty says to the believers in Him, "The believers are but brothers" in religion: "So reconcile between your brothers" when they fight by holding them to the judgment of Allah and His Messenger. The meaning of the two brothers here is all those who are killed among the people of faith, and in the second form, this is read by the reciters of the cities.
