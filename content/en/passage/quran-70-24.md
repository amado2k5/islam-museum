## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24-28 as a single section. He explains the phrase, “And those in whose wealth there is a known right”: except for those in whose wealth there is a temporary right, which is zakat for the one who asks from his wealth, and the deprived one who has been deprived of wealth, so he is poor and does not ask. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of the known right that God mentioned in this place. Some said: it is zakat. In the section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Saeed ibn Jubayr, Al-A’mash, Ibrahim, and others.
