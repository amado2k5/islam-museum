## Summary
It reached Malik that Said ibn al-Musayyab was asked about a contract slave owned by two men, one of whom freed his share, then the contract slave died leaving much property. He said: what remains is paid to the one who kept his contract, then the two divide what remains equally. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.359}
