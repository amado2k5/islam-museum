**Commentary on verse 106 (pp. 464-467)**

Al-Tabari says: and of these who stayed behind from you when you marched against your enemy, believers, are others; the word "others" is in the nominative, joined to the words "others who confessed their sins, mixing a righteous deed with another evil one". "Others deferred" means deferred to God's command and decree; one says "I deferred him, I defer him, deferral, and he is deferred", with the glottal stop or without it, two dialects of one meaning, and the reciters have read it both ways. It was said that by these others is meant a band of those who stayed behind from the Messenger of God in the Tabuk campaign and regretted what they did, but did not make excuses to the Messenger of God on his arrival and did not tie themselves to the pillars, so God deferred their matter until their repentance was sound, and then turned to them and pardoned them. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of deferral. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 464}

What the interpreters said agrees with what we said. Report 17174, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: three of them, that is, of those who stayed behind from the Tabuk campaign, did not tie themselves to the pillars and were deferred for a while, not knowing whether they would be punished or God would turn to them, and God sent down "God has turned to the Prophet and the Emigrants" to the words "God is the Accepting, the Merciful", which are [[passage/quran-9-117|9:117-118]]. Report 17175, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: when this verse came down, meaning His words "take from their wealth a charity to purify them and cleanse them by it", the Messenger of God took of their wealth, meaning of the wealth of Abu Lubaba and his two companions, and gave it in charity for them; and the three who had differed from Abu Lubaba remained, who had not tied themselves up, who had not been mentioned with anything, and no excuse of theirs had come down, and the earth became narrow for them for all its breadth, and they are those about whom God said "others deferred to God's command, either He will punish them or turn to them, and God is knowing, wise"; people began to say "they are lost", since no excuse had come down for them, and others began to say "perhaps God will forgive them", so they were deferred to God's command until there came down "God has turned to the Prophet and the Emigrants and the Helpers who followed him in the hour of hardship", those who went out with him to al-Sham, "after the hearts of a party of them had almost swerved, then He turned to them, He is kind and merciful to them"; then He said "and to the three who were left behind", meaning those deferred to God's command, repentance came down on them and they were all included in it; and He said "until when the earth became narrow for them for all its breadth and their own selves became narrow for them" to the words "God is the Accepting, the Merciful". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 465}

Report 17176, from Ikrima, through Ibn Waki', Suwayd ibn Amr, Hammad ibn Zayd and Ayyub: "others deferred to God's command", he said, they are the three who were left behind. Reports 17177 and 17178, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim and Isa, and through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl: "others deferred to God's command", Hilal ibn Umayya, Murara ibn Rab'i and Ka'b ibn Malik, of the Aws and Khazraj; reports 17179, 17180 and 17181 from him are the same, and from al-Dahhak through Juwaybir the same. The editor notes the differences between the manuscript, the printed text and the books on the Companions in the name of Murara ibn al-Rabi' al-Ansari. Report 17182, from al-Dahhak, through al-Husayn ibn al-Faraj, Abu Mu'adh and Ubayd: they are the three who were left behind from repentance, meaning other than Abu Lubaba and his companions, and God did not send down their excuse, so the earth became narrow for them for all its breadth; and the Companions of the Messenger of God were in two groups over them, one saying "they are lost", since God had not sent down about them what He sent down about Abu Lubaba and his companions, and another saying "perhaps God will pardon them", and they were deferred to God's command; then God sent down His mercy and forgiveness, saying "God has turned to the Prophet and the Emigrants", the verse, and sent down "and to the three who were left behind", the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 466}

Report 17183, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "others deferred to God's command", he said, we used to be told they were the three who were left behind, Ka'b ibn Malik, Hilal ibn Umayya and Murara ibn Rabi'a, a band of the Helpers; report 17184, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: they are the three who were left behind; report 17185, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: "others deferred to God's command, either He will punish them or turn to them", they are the three who were left behind, and the Messenger of God deferred their matter until their repentance came to them from God. As for "either He will punish them", it means either God will bar them from repentance by abandoning them, and punish them for the sins on which they died in the hereafter, "or turn to them", meaning grant them success in repenting, so that they repent of their sins and He forgives them; "and God is knowing, wise" means God has knowledge of their affair and of what they are heading for, repentance or persisting in sin, wise in His management of them and of His other creatures, no flaw entering His decree. The editor notes that the report is in Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 467}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
