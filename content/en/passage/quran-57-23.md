## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So that you do not grieve over what has passed you or rejoice in what He has given you. And Allah does not like every boastful boastful": The Exalted means: "No calamity befalls you, O people, in your wealth or yourselves, except in a Book in which it is written before We create your souls, 'so that you do not grieve.' It means: so that you do not grieve 'for what you have missed' of this world, and you do not perceive it, and 'and do not rejoice in what He has given you.' The meaning of His saying: 'by what I give you' is extended if the alif is extended from it: by what your Lord gave you, your dominion, and your authority; and if the alif is shortened, it means: by what He came to you from it. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, Samak, and Sufyan.
