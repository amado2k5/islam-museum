## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: when a she-camel is slaughtered, the slaughter of what is in her belly is her slaughter, if its creation is complete and its hair has grown. If it comes out of its mother's belly, it must be slaughtered until blood flows out of its inside. The editor notes that the stopped form is the correct one for Abu Hatim al-Razi, Ibn Adi and al-Bayhaqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.633}
