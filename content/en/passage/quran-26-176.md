## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 176 to 179 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "The companions of the messengers of the Ikeka" as: "The owners of the Ikeka lied." Al-Ika: the wrapped tree, which is singular 'Ika', and every wrapped tree is called 'Ika' among the Arabs. From this is the saying of Nabigha Banu Dhubyan: 'A dove of Ikeh will appear with my arrival, its teeth will be saddened by the Ithmad.' The people of the Ikeke: they are the people of Midian, as mentioned. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
