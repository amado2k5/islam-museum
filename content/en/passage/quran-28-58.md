## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And how many a town whose livelihood was wasted, and those dwellings did not inhabit after them except a little, and We were the inheritors": And how many cities have We destroyed whose livelihood was wasted, so it became disgraceful, it became arrogant, it became arrogant, and it transgressed, and it disbelieved its Lord. It was said: 'She made her livelihood disgraceful,' so the verb was made for the village, which originally means livelihood, as it is said: 'Your opinion is misunderstood, so I made it foolish,' and 'I doubted you,' and 'ma'ishasha' is in the accusative according to interpretation. We have explained similar instances elsewhere in this book. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
