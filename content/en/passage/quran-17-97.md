## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And whoever guides to Allah - then He is the one guided. And whoever leads astray, you will not find for them any protectors besides Him. We will gather them on the Day of Resurrection on their faces, blind and dumb and dumb; their abode is Hell. Whenever it subsides, We will increase them in the Blaze." Whoever guides, O Muhammad, to believe in Him, and to believe in you and to believe in what you have brought from your Lord, and grant him success in that, is the guided, the right, the truthful, not the one guided by anyone else, for guidance is in His hand. And whoever misleads says: "And whoever misguides" Allah from the truth, forsakes him from his misfortune, and does not grant him success in believing in Allah and believing in His Messenger. You will not find for them, O Muhammad, allies to support them besides Allah, if Allah wills to punish them and save them from them. "And We will gather them on the Day of Resurrection on their faces," meaning: and gather them at the station of the Resurrection after their dispersal in the graves at the Day of Resurrection, "on their faces, blind and mute," which is the plural of 'mute,' meaning mute, as Muammar informed us, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase 'and mute,' he said: 'mute' means 'wasma' (deaf), which is the plural of 'dumb.' In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
