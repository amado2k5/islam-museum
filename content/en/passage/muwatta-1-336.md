## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the two dawn rak'as. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' from Abdullah ibn Umar that Hafsa, the Prophet's wife, told him that when the caller fell silent after the dawn call, the Prophet prayed two light rak'as before the iqama. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i, al-Darimi and Ahmad from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.186}
