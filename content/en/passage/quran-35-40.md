## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, 'Have you considered your partners whom you invoke besides Allah? Show me what they have created of the earth. Or do they have association with Allah in the heavens? Or have We given them a Book, and they are on clear proof from it? Rather, the wrongdoers plan among themselves only in delusion": "Say," O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people. "Have you, O people, shown me what they have created of the earth?" He says: "Show me what they created from the earth, or do they have associating partners in the heavens?" He says: Or do your partners have a record with Allah in the heavens, if they did not create anything from the earth? "Or did We give them a Book, and they were clear proofs of it?" He means: Or did We give these polytheists a Book which We sent down to them from the heaven to associate with Allah, idols and idols, so they are clear from it, and they are on proof of what I commanded them to associate with Me? In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
