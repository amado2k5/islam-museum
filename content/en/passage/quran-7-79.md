## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 79 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-79|7:79]], al-Tabari says: Salih turned away from them when they hurried him for the punishment and hamstrung God's camel, leaving their land and going out from among them, because God had revealed to him that He would destroy them after the third day. It is said that no community has been destroyed while its prophet was among them. God reports Salih's leaving his people who defied their Lord when He meant to bring His punishment on them, and Salih said to Thamud: I conveyed my Lord's message and delivered what He commanded me to deliver of His command and prohibition, and I advised you in delivering God's message, warning you of His might while you persisted in unbelief and idol worship, "but you do not love advisers", those who advise you for God's sake, forbid you to follow your whims and bar you from your desires. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 547}
