## Summary
Verse 58 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 58 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of verse 58.** Al-Tabari says the pointer refers to the accounts God gave His prophet about Jesus and his mother Mary, her mother Hanna, Zechariah and his son Yahya, and what He told of the disciples and the Jews among the Israelites; that God recites them to Muhammad through Gabriel and reveals them to him; and that they are lessons and proofs against the Najran Christian delegation who disputed with him and the Israelite Jews who denied him. He explains the remembrance as the Quran and the wise as that which has the wisdom that separates truth from falsehood, between the Prophet and those who ascribe the Messiah to other than his lineage. Report 7157 (Ibn Humayd, from Salama, from Ibn Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr) says it is the decisive, separating truth unmixed with falsehood about Jesus and what they differed over, so no other account should be accepted. Report 7158 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Abu Zuhayr, from Juwaybir, from al-Dahhak) says it is the Quran. Report 7159 (al-Muthanna, from Abdullah ibn Salih, from Muawiya ibn Salih, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas) says the remembrance is the Quran and the wise is that which is complete in its wisdom. The editor notes that 7157 appears in Ibn Hisham (Sira 2:231) as a continuation of the earlier reports and that he corrected a misprint in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 466} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 467}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
