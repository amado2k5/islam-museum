## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God held races between horses that had been trained from al-Hafya, whose end was the Pass of Farewell, and held races between untrained horses from the Pass to the mosque of Banu Zurayq; Abdullah ibn Umar was among those who raced them. The editor notes that al-Hafya and the Pass of Farewell are two places near Medina. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.600}
