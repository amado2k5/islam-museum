## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 36 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they said, 'We have more wealth and children, and we are not punished.'" The people of arrogance against Allah said from every town where We sent a warner to Our prophets and messengers: We are "more rich and more children," and we are not punished in the Hereafter, because if Allah were not pleased with our religion and deeds, He would not have granted us wealth and children, nor expanded provision for us. Rather, He gave us what He gave us out of His pleasure in our deeds, and preferred what we preferred over others out of our bounty and a gift for us with Him. Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: Say to them, O Muhammad: 'Indeed, my Lord expands provision' from livelihood and modesty in this world, 'for whom He wills' from His creation, 'and He restricts it' for whomever He wills, not out of love for those He makes easy for them, nor good in them, nor any remedy, nor out of hatred for those who are able to do so, nor out of hatred for those who are able to do so, nor out of hatred. But He does this as a trial and trial for His servants. Most people do not. In this section, sayings from Ibn Zayd are quoted.
