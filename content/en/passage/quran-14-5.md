**Commentary on verse 5 (p. 517)**

On "We sent Moses with Our signs: bring your people out of the darknesses into the light, and remind them of the days of God; surely in that are signs for every steadfast, grateful one", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: We sent Moses with Our proofs and arguments before you, Muhammad, as We sent you to your people with the like of them in proofs and arguments. Then report 20561 begins with its chain, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih; the editor notes that the symbol for a change of chain is used for the first time in this manuscript. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 517}

We stop here at the foot of p. 517, in the middle of the commentary on verse 5 (at the start of report 20561 and its chain); the next batch begins at p. 518 (marker 526). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 517}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
