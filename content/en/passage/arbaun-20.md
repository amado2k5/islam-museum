## Summary
Hadith of Abu Mas'ud al-Badri: "Among what people have from the words of the first prophethood is: if you have no shame, do what you will". Ibn Rajab discusses two readings of the phrase: as a threat, or as a statement that one without shame does as he pleases. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
