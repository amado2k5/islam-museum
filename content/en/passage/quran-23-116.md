## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So exalted is Allah, the True Sovereign. There is no deity except Him, Lord of the Noble Throne": So exalted is Allah, the True King, above what these polytheists describe as having a partner, and what they attribute to Him by taking daughters. "There is no god but Him," meaning: There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, the True King, the Lord of the Noble Throne. The Lord: is nominative in response to the truth. The meaning is: So Exalted is Allah, the True Sovereign, Lord of the Noble Throne. There is no god but Him.
