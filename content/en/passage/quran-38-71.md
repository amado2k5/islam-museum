## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 71 to 74 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "When your Lord said to the angels, 'Indeed, I am created as a human from clay'": and His saying: "When your Lord said" is connected to the phrase: "when they disputed", and the interpretation of the phrase: "I had no knowledge of the highest assembly when they disputed when your Lord said, O Muhammad, 'Indeed, to the angels, I created a human from clay'" means that Adam was created. And His saying: "So when I have leveled him and breathed into him of My Spirit," Allah says: 'So when I have leveled his creation, shaped his form, and breathed into him of My spirit,' it is said: He meant by that meaning: and I breathed into him from My power. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and al-Dhahhak.
