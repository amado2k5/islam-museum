## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 7 to 8 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And the earth We spread out and cast into it My hills and caused to grow therein of every joyful pair": and His saying: "And the earth We spread out" means: "And the earth We spread out" and "We cast into it firm hills," meaning: We placed in it firm mountains firmly established in the earth, "And We caused to grow therein of every joyful pair," meaning God Almighty says: "And We caused to grow on the earth every kind of beautiful plant, which is the joyful one." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sufyan, and Ata.
