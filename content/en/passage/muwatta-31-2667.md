## Summary
Malik transmits from Said al-Maqburi, from his father Kaysan al-Maqburi, from Abu Hurayra, who said: five are part of the natural practices (fitra): trimming the nails, trimming the moustache, plucking the armpits, shaving the pubic hair, and circumcision. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.506}
