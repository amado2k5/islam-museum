## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Atika bint Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl, the wife of Umar ibn al-Khattab, used to kiss the head of Umar ibn al-Khattab while he was fasting, and he would not forbid her. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shaybani (353) transmitted it from Malik, and refers to al-Musnad al-Jami. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.393}
