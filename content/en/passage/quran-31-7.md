## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when Our verses are recited to him, he turns away arrogantly as if he did not hear them, as if there is deafness in his ears, so he gives him glad tidings of a painful punishment." And when verses of the Book of God are recited to this one who buys idle talk to mislead from the way of Allah, it is recited to him as if he has become arrogant, meaning he turned away from them, became arrogant and turned away from hearing the truth and responding to it, "as if he did not hear them, as if there was deafness in his ears." He says: Heavy, so he cannot bear to hear it for his sake, as Ibn Abi Nujayh narrated from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "In his ears is deafness," he said: heavy. And His saying, "So give him glad tidings of a painful punishment," Allah Almighty says: "So give glad tidings to this one who refuses to accept the verses of Allah when they are recited to him out of arrogance of a painful punishment from Allah on the Day of Resurrection, which is the punishment of the Fire." In the excerpt, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
