## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So be patient; indeed, the promise of Allah is true, and let not those who do not believe let you be silent": So be patient, O Muhammad, against what befalls you from their harm and deliver to them the message of your Lord. Indeed, Allah's promise to you is to grant victory over them, to win over them, and to empower you, your companions, and those who follow you on earth as truth. "And let not those who do not believe in resurrection diminish you," meaning: those who do not associate with Allah and do not believe in resurrection after death will discourage you from Allah's command and influence because you were tasked with delivering His message. From Sa'id ibn Jubayr, from Ali ibn Rabi'ah, that a man from the Kharijites recited behind Ali: "If you associate partners with Allah, your deeds will be nullified and you will be among the losers." Ali said: "So be patient, for the promise of Allah is true, and let not those who do not believe in Allah belittle you." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
