## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And whatever you have been given of anything, then the enjoyment of the life of this world and its adornments, and what is with Allah is better and more lasting. Will you not then reason": And what you have given, O people, of wealth and children, is only a possession you enjoy in this worldly life, and it is among its adornments you see therein. It will not avail you with Allah at all, nor will it benefit you in your Hereafter. And what is with Allah for those who obey Him and grant His guardianship is better than what you have been given in this world of its comforts, adornments, and endurance. He means: 'And it is everlasting for its people,' because it is everlasting and never ends. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
