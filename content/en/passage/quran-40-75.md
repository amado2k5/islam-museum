## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 75 to 76 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "That is because you used to rejoice in the land without just right and because you used to rejoice": He means that He is exalted in His saying: "That is because you rejoiced in the land without right." This is what we have done to you today, O people, from our torment. How much torment you are in, because of your joy that you used to rejoice in this world, without what was permitted for you of falsehood and sins, and by your rejoicing in it. Al-marh is the worst and the most bitter. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
