## Summary
Verse 44 of [[passage/quran-11|Surat Hud]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 44 of 123 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 44 (pp. 334-339)**

On [[passage/quran-11-44|11:44]] al-Tabari says: God said to the earth, after His command reached its end in the destruction of Noah's people by the drowning with which He destroyed them, 'earth, swallow your water', that is, drink it, from the saying 'so-and-so swallowed it' when he gulped it; 'and sky, hold back' means hold back from rain; 'and the water subsided' means the earth took it and drank it; 'and the matter was decided' means God's command was decided and went through with the destruction of Noah's people; 'and it settled on al-Judi' means the ark settled, that is, moored, on al-Judi, a mountain reported to be in the region of Mosul or the Jazira; 'and it was said, away with the wrongdoing people' means God said, may God put far the wrongdoing people of Noah's people who disbelieved in God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 335}

Report 18187, from the Prophet, by a chain the editor judges utterly weak: on the first day of Rajab Noah boarded the ship and fasted, he and all with him, and the ship ran with them six months until it reached Muharram, and moored on al-Judi on the day of Ashura, and Noah fasted and ordered all with him of wild animals and beasts and they fasted in thanks to God; the editor says it is a ruined report on every side. Report 18188, from Ibn Jurayj: the upper part of the ship was for the birds, the middle for people and the lowest for beasts of prey, and its height was thirty cubits; it set out from Ayn Warda on a Friday on the tenth night of Rajab and moored on al-Judi on Ashura, and passed the House, which God had raised from the drowning, and circled it seven times, then came to Yemen, then returned. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 335}

Report 18189, from Qatada: Noah came down from the ship on the tenth of Muharram and said to those with him, whoever of you is fasting today let him complete his fast, and whoever is not fasting let him fast; report 18190, from Muhammad b. Qays: in Noah's time there was not a span of earth without someone claiming it, meaning claiming the water had not covered all the earth; report 18191, from Qatada: it was told us that it set out with them on the tenth of Rajab and was on the water a hundred and fifty days and rested on al-Judi a month and they were brought down on the tenth of Muharram, Ashura. Reports 18192, 18193 and 18194, from Mujahid: 'subsided' means decreased, 'the matter was decided' the destruction of Noah's people, and Ibn Jurayj said the earth drank it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 336}

Report 18195, from Ibn Abbas: 'sky, hold back' means refrain, and 'the water subsided' means the water went; report 18196, from Qatada: 'ghuyud' is the going of water. Reports 18197, 18198 and 18199, from Mujahid: 'and it settled on al-Judi', a mountain in the Jazira; the mountains towered from the drowning and it humbled itself to God and was not drowned, and Noah's ship moored on it; report 18200, from Ibn Abbas: on the mountain, whose name is al-Judi; report 18201, from Sufyan, likewise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 337}

Report 18202, from Qatada: God kept it for us in a valley of the Jazira as a lesson and sign; report 18203, from al-Dahhak: it is a mountain at Mosul; report 18204, from Qatada: it was told us that Noah sent the raven to look at the water and it found a carcass and settled on it, then sent the dove, which brought him an olive leaf, and it was given the ring on its neck and the colouring of its feet. Report 18205, from Ibn Ishaq: when God willed to hold the flood back He sent a wind over the earth and the water calmed, and the springs of the great deep and the doors of the sky were stopped; it was said, earth, swallow your water, to 'away with the wrongdoing people', and the water began to decrease, subside and recede. The settling of the ark on al-Judi was, as the people of the Torah claim, in the seventh month on the seventeenth night, and on the first day of the tenth month the tops of the mountains were seen; after forty days Noah opened the window of the ark and sent the raven to see what the water had done, and it did not return. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 338}

Then he sent the dove, which came back finding no place for its feet, so he stretched out his hand and took it. He waited seven days and sent it again, and it came back at evening with an olive leaf in its beak, so Noah knew the water had lessened on the face of the earth; he waited seven days and sent it, and it did not return, so he knew the earth had appeared. When the year was complete and the face of the earth appeared and the dry land showed, Noah uncovered the ark's cover and saw the face of the earth, and in the second month of the second year on the twenty-seventh night of it Noah was told, come down in peace from Us and blessings, as in [[passage/quran-11-48|11:48]]. Report 18206, from al-Dahhak: some claim that the children who drowned with their fathers did, but it is not so; the children are like the birds and all whom God drowned without sin, whose terms came and who died at their terms, while for the grown men and women the drowning was God's punishment in the world and then their end is the Fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 339}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
