## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they have a sin upon me, so I fear they will be killed": and he transmitted the killing of the soul that was killed among them. Al-Husayn narrated to me, he said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, who said: Moses killed the soul. And from Muammar, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase "and they have a sin upon me," he said: the killing of the soul. And His saying: "So I fear that they will kill me," he says: I fear they will kill me because of the soul I killed among them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
