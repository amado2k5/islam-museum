**Commentary on verse 47 (pp. 125-126)**

On "he said: you will sow seven years as usual" al-Tabari says: Joseph told the one who asked about the king's dream that you will sow these seven years as you sowed all the years before, by your past habit. "Da'b" is habit, as in a verse of Imru' al-Qays comparing a habit to his habit with two women; the editor refers to its meaning and sources earlier. "What you reap, leave in its ear, except a little of what you eat" is advice that God's prophet gave the people and an opinion he saw as good for them, telling them to preserve their food; report 19370, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: God's prophet Joseph told them, you will sow seven years as usual, and the prophet of God only meant preservation. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 125}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
