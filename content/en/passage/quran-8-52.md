**Commentary on verse 52 (pp. 18-19)**

Al-Tabari says: the act of these idolaters of Quraysh killed at Badr is like the custom of the people of Pharaoh, their way and doing, and the doing of those who denied God's proofs and messengers among earlier nations; so We did to them what We did to those. He has already explained that the "custom" is the usual way and habit. Report 16208, from Amir, Mujahid and Ata, through al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz, Shayban and Jabir: "like the custom of the people of Pharaoh" means like the doing and ways of the people of Pharaoh. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "people" and "custom". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 19}

On "so God seized them for their sins" he says: God punished them for denying His proofs and messengers and disobeying their Lord, as He punished their like and the nations before them; "God is strong", beaten by no one, His decree turned back by none, carrying out His command and executing His decree among His creatures, "severe in punishment" to whoever disbelieves in Him and rejects His proofs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 19}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
