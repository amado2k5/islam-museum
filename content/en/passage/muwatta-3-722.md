## Summary
A saying of Malik: the practice among us is that whoever withholds one of God's obligations, which the Muslims could not take from him, it is a duty upon them to fight him until they take it from him. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.362}
