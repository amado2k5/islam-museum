## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And you see the angels besieging around the Throne, glorifying the praise of their Lord and the matter between them in truth, and it is said, 'Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.'" And you see, O Muhammad, the angels gazing around the Throne of the Most Merciful, and by the Throne it means the throne. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
