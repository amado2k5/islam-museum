## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 86 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And the word came upon them because of what they wronged, so they did not speak": and God's wrath and wrath are due upon those who deny His signs "because of what they wronged," meaning their denial of God's signs, on the Day they are resurrected, "so they do not speak," meaning: they do not utter a pretext by which they defend themselves from the great things that have befallen them and the words that have befallen them.
