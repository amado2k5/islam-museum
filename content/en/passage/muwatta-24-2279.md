## Summary
Malik said it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab said of a man who died leaving three sons and clients he had freed; then two of his sons died leaving children: the surviving one of the three inherits the clientage of the clients, and when he dies, his children and his brothers' children share the clientage of the clients equally. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.340}
