## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And to Solomon was the wind; its morning was a month and its winds a month, and We sent for him the spring of rain. And among the jinn—some act before him by permission of his Lord. And whoever of them strays from Our command, We will make him taste the punishment of the blazing fire": Reciters differed in the recitation of the phrase: "And Solomon has the wind." Most reciters of the lands read it as "and for Solomon the wind" as the accusative of the wind, meaning: And We gave David from Us bounty, and We subjected the wind to Solomon. Asim recited this: "and for Solomon the wind" in the nominative case with the adjective particle, as the nominative noun is not seen. The correct reading in this regard is the accusative case according to the consensus of the reciters on it. And His saying: "They made it a month," God Almighty says: "And We subjected Solomon to the wind; they made it a month's journey until midday, and its winds from midday to night is the journey of a month." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Ishaq.
