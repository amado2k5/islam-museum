## Summary
Malik transmits from Muslim ibn Abi Maryam, from Abu Salih, from Abu Hurayra, who said: women who are clothed yet naked, who lead astray and are led astray; they will not enter Paradise nor smell its fragrance, though its fragrance is smelled from a distance of five hundred years. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.499}
