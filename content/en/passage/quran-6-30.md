## Summary
Verse 30 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 30 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "if you could see when they are made to stand before their Lord; he will say: is this not the truth? They will say: yes, by our Lord; he will say: then taste the punishment for your unbelief" al-Tabari says: if you could see, Muhammad, these who say "there is nothing but our present life and we shall not be raised", "when they are made to stand" on the day of resurrection... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 323}

The rest: that is, held "before their Lord", meaning before God's judgement and decree about them; "he will say: is this not the truth?" means they will be asked: is not this raising and resurrection after death, which you used to deny in the world, the truth? They answer: yes, by God, it is the truth; "he will say: then taste the punishment" means God will say to them: taste the punishment that you used to deny in the world, "for your unbelief", for your denial of it and rejection which you showed in the world. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 324}

The editor refers for "stood" to p. 316 and for "tasting the punishment" to p. 47.

Verse 30 closes here at p. 324, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-31|6:31]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
