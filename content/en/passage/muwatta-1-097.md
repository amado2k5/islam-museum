## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam from Jundub, client of Abdullah ibn Ayyash, in the chapter on ablution after madhi: Jundub asked Abdullah ibn Umar about madhi, and he said: when you find it, wash your private parts and perform ablution for prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik, in al-Bayhaqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.83}
