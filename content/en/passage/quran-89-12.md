## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 15 as one section. He explains His saying, “So they increased corruption therein,” meaning they increased sins in the land and committed what God had forbidden them. In the section, he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and others.
