## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and to Allah all matters will return": Allah knows what was before His angels and messengers before He created them and what came after them, meaning: and He knows what will come after their destruction. "And to Allah all matters will return"—meaning: to Allah in the Hereafter matters will befall Him, and to Him they will return as they were at the beginning.
