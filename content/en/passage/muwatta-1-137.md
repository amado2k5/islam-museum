## Summary
A saying of Malik transmitted by Yahya in the chapter on dry ablution: whoever performs dry ablution because he found no water, says the takbir and enters the prayer, and then someone comes with water, must not break his prayer but complete it with dry ablution, and perform ablution for the prayers that follow. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.100}
