## Summary
Verse 53 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 53 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The prayer of the disciples.** Al-Tabari says this is a report from God of the disciples addressing their Lord: that they believed what was sent down on His prophet Jesus of His book, became followers of Jesus on the religion with which He sent him and his helpers in the truth, and asked Him to record their names with the names of those who bore witness to the truth, affirmed His oneness, believed His messengers and followed His command and prohibition, to count them with them in what He honours them with, and not to make them among those who disbelieved, barred His path and went against His command. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 452}

**The purpose of the report.** Al-Tabari says God thereby teaches His creatures the path of those whose words and deeds He approved, so that they follow their way and reach similar degrees of His honour; it gives the lie to the followers of creeds other than the upright submitting religion in their claim that God's prophets followed another, and is an argument against the Najran delegation, since the words of those of Jesus' followers with whom God was pleased differed from theirs and their way was not theirs. Report 7130 (Ibn Humayd, from Salama, from Ibn Ishaq, from Muhammad ibn Jafar ibn al-Zubayr) says that this was how they spoke and believed; the editor refers to Ibn Hisham's Sira (2:230) and says it belongs to the rest of reports ending with 7129. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 452} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 453}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
