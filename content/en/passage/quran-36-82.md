## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 82 to 83 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "His command, when He intends something, is that He says to it, 'Be,' and it is": "His command, when He intends something, is only to say to it, 'Be,' and it is." Qatadah used to say about this: And from Qatadah: "Or is He who created the heavens and the earth not able to create the like of them? Yes, and He is the Creator, the All-Knowing." He said: This is a parable that He only commanded when He intends something to say to it, 'Be,' and it is. He said: There is nothing in the speech of the Arabs lighter or lighter than that, so Allah commanded so. And His saying: "So glory be to Him in whose Hand is the dominion of all things," the Exalted says: So the Exalted is the One in whose hand is the dominion of all things and its treasures. And His saying: "And to Him you will return" means: to Him you will return and become after your death. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
