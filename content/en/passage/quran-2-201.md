## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "and among them is he who says, Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the hereafter and protect us from the punishment of the Fire": al-Tabari says the interpreters differed on the meaning of the "good" mentioned. Some said: give us well-being in this world and well-being in the hereafter. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 203} This is from Qatada, who adds: a man said, God, whatever You would punish me with in the hereafter, hasten it for me in this world; he fell ill until he wasted away on his bed; his case was reported to the Prophet, who came to him and was told he had prayed so; the Prophet said, no one can bear God's punishment, but say "Our Lord, give us good in this world..."; he said it and was well within days. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 203} From Anas: the Messenger of God visited a man who had become like a plucked chick and said, were you praying for anything? He said, I said God, whatever You would punish me with in the hereafter, punish me with it in this world; he said, glory be to God, can anyone bear that? Why did you not say "God, give us good in this world and good in the hereafter and protect us from the punishment of the Fire"? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 204} The editor notes that Muslim alone, and not al-Bukhari, reported it, and that Humayd states in this report that he heard it from Anas. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 204}

Others said the good in this world is knowledge and worship and in the hereafter Paradise; from al-Hasan: worship in this world and Paradise in the hereafter, and in another report understanding of God's book and knowledge; from al-Thawri: knowledge and good provision, and in the hereafter Paradise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 204} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 205} Others said the good in this world is wealth and in the hereafter Paradise; from Ibn Zayd: these are the Prophet and the believers; from al-Suddi: these are the believers, the good of this world being wealth and the good of the next being Paradise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 205}

Al-Tabari gives his own choice: what is right in his opinion is that God reported of a group of the people of faith in Him and His Messenger who made hajj to His House, asking their Lord for good in this world and good in the hereafter and protection from the punishment of the Fire; "good" from God may include well-being of body, livelihood, provision and the rest, knowledge and worship, and in the hereafter it is without doubt Paradise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 205} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 206} He says this because God did not specify any one meaning of "good" nor set up any indication of specification, so no meaning may be singled out. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 206} As for "and protect us from the punishment of the Fire", it means turn the punishment of the Fire away from us; one says "waqaytuhu kadha aqihi wiqaya and wiqa'". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 206}
