## Summary
Malik says of a man who has a slave-woman, then buys her and frees her: she observes a slave-woman's waiting period of two menstrual periods, so long as he has not had intercourse with her. If he had intercourse with her after he came to own her and before her manumission, she needs only one menstrual period of clearing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.95}
