**Commentary on verse 90 (p. 456)**

On [[passage/quran-11-90|11:90]] al-Tabari relates Shu'ayb's words: 'seek forgiveness of your Lord', people, for the sins between you and your Lord that you persist in, worshipping gods and idols and cheating people of their rights in measures and weights, 'then turn to Him', that is, return to obeying Him and keeping to His command and prohibition; 'my Lord is merciful', merciful to whoever repents and turns to Him, not to punish him after repentance, 'loving', that is, He has love for whoever turns and repents to Him, and loves and cherishes him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 456}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
