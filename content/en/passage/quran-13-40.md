**Commentary on verse 40 (p. 493)**

On "whether We show you part of what We promise them or We cause you to die, your duty is only to convey, and Ours is the reckoning", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise, to His Prophet Muhammad: whether We show you, Muhammad, in your lifetime some of what We promise these polytheists of punishment for their disbelief, or We take you before showing you that, your duty is only to carry through in obeying your Lord in what He commanded you of conveying His message to them, not seeking their reform or their corruption; and upon Us is their reckoning and requital for their deeds, good for good and evil for evil. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 493}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
