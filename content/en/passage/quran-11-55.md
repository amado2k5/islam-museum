**Commentary on verse 55 (p. 361)**

On [[passage/quran-11-55|11:55]] al-Tabari says: 'so plot against me, all of you' means scheme, you and your gods together, to harm and trouble me, 'then give me no respite' means do not delay it, but see whether you and they can reach me with what you claim your gods reached me with of harm. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'plotting' and 'respite'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 361}
