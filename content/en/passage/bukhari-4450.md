## Summary
In the Book of Campaigns: Aisha describes the Prophet's rounds among his wives during his illness and their permission for him to stay with her. He died between her chest and her neck, and the toothstick of Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr, which he softened, was the last thing to enter his mouth. It is among the most detailed accounts of his death. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
