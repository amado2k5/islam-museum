## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 82 to 83 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "Say, if the Most Merciful has a son, then I am the first of the worshippers": The interpreters differed in the interpretation of His saying: "Say, if the Most Merciful has a son, then I am the first of the worshippers." Some said: Meaning: Say, O Muhammad, if the Most Merciful has a son, in your statement and claim, O polytheists, I am the first of the believers in Allah in your denial, and the denies what you say that He has a son. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
