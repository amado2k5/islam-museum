## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on which animals one in ihram may kill. Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger of God said: five animals; there is no sin on one in ihram for killing them: the crow, the kite, the scorpion, the mouse, and the rabid dog. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1183), Ishaq al-Tabba, Suwayd (629), al-Bukhari and many others transmitted it, and refers to al-Tamhid (15/153). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.479}
