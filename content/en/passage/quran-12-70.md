**Commentary on verse 70 (p. 171)**

On "and when he had provided them with their provisions he put the drinking cup in his brother's saddlebag; then a caller cried out, O caravan, you are thieves", al-Tabari says: when Joseph loaded his brothers' camels with the provisions he loaded and met their need, report 19510, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id, begins at the end of the page. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "tajhiz" and "jahaz". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 171}

We stop here at the foot of p. 171, at the opening of the commentary on verse 70 (with report 19510 begun at the end of the page); the next batch begins at p. 172. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 171}
