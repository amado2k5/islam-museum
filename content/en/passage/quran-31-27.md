## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And if there were in the earth a tree of pens and the sea extends after it seven seas, the words of Allah would not run out. Indeed, Allah is Exalted in Might, Wise": and if the trees of the earth were cut into pens, "and the sea extends it," he means: and the sea has ink, and the 'ha' in the phrase "it extends it" refers to the sea. And His saying, 'After him seven sailed as long as the words of Allah had run out,' and in this omission statement, he made no use of the apparent meaning from it, as he writes the words of God with those pens and ink, so that those pens would break, and that ink would run out, but the words of God would not run out. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
