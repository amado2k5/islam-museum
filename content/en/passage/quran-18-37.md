## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 38 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "His companion said to him while he was conversing with him: I disbelieve in Him who created you from dust, then from a drop of fluid, then made you a man": He said to the owner of the two gardens, his companion who is less wealthy and less than him, "while he was conversing with him": He says: while addressing and speaking to him: "You disbelieve in the One who created you from dust," meaning He created your father Adam from dust "then from a drop of fluid," meaning: Then He created you from the sperm of man and woman, "Then He made you a man," meaning: Then he returned to you a proper human being, a man, male and not female, saying: I disbelieve in the one who did this to you to recreate you after you become remains. "But He is Allah my Lord." He means: As for me, I do not disbelieve in my Lord, but I am Allah, my Lord. It means that he says: But I say: He is Allah, my Lord, and I do not associate anyone with my Lord.
