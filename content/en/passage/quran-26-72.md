## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 72 to 74 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'Do they hear you when you call upon them?': Abraham said to them: Do these gods hear your supplication when you call upon them?" The Arabs differed on the meaning of this: some grammarians of Basra said: Do they hear from you or do they hear your supplication, so the supplication was omitted, as Zuhayr said: The leader of the horses is afflicted, their reins have been firmly established, and he said: The wisdoms of Al-Abq have been firmly established. So he recited the wisdoms and established Al-Abq in their place. In the passage, he quotes from Ibrahim.
