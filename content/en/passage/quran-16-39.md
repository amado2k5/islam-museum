## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That He may make clear to them that concerning which they differ, and that those who disbelieve may know that they were liars": Rather, Allah will resurrect one who dies as a true promise, to make clear to those who claim that Allah does not raise one who dies, and to others who differ about that of reviving His creation after their destruction, and to make it clear to those who denied the truth of this and denied its truth that they were liars in their claim that Allah will not raise one who dies. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "to make clear to them that over which they differ," he said: for the general public. In the Maqta, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
