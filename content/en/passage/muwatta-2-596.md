## Summary
The first report in the chapter on the shroud of the dead. Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Aisha, that the Prophet was shrouded in three white Suhuli garments, with no shirt and no turban. The editor notes it was transmitted from Malik by Abu Musab, Ibn Hibban and al-Baghawi, Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari, Suwayd, al-Qanabi, Ibn Wahb, and Qutayba in al-Nasai, and al-Shafii in the Musnad and al-Umm, and clarifies that the Suhuli are cotton garments made in Yemen. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.307}
