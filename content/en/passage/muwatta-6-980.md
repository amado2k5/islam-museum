## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar from Abdullah ibn Umar that he said: whoever makes the lesser pilgrimage in the months of the pilgrimage, in Shawwal, Dhu al-Qadda or Dhu al-Hijja, before the pilgrimage, then stays in Mecca until the pilgrimage reaches him, is an enjoyer (mutamatti') if he makes the pilgrimage; he owes what is feasible of an offering, and if he cannot find one, fasting three days in the pilgrimage and seven when he returns. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd (519) and al-Shafii transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.462}
