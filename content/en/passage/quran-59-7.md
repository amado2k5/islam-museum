## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Whatever Allah has withheld from the people of the towns upon His Messenger, it is for Allah, the Messenger, the near relatives, the orphans, the needy, and the traveler, so that there is no division among the rich among you. And whatever the Messenger gives you, take it, and whatever he forbids you, refrain from it. And fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is severe in punishment": This means by His saying, Glorified and Exalted: "Allah has not repaid His Messenger from the people of the towns," which Allah Almighty returned to His Messenger from the wealth of the polytheists of the villages. Scholars differ regarding what this verse refers to; some say it refers to jizya and kharaj. In the Maqta', statements are transmitted from Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Ibn Ishaq.
