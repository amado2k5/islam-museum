## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He has prescribed for you of religion that which Noah enjoined upon you, and what We revealed to you, and what We enjoined upon Abraham, Moses, and Jesus: "Establish religion and do not become divided therein." Greatness is for the polytheists in what you invite them to. Allah chooses to Himself whom He wills and guides to Him who turns in repentance.' "Your Lord has prescribed for you, O mankind, of the religion that Noah enjoined upon him, to be done, and by Him We have revealed to you." He says to His Prophet Muhammad: And He has legislated for you from the religion that We revealed to you, O Muhammad, and commanded you with it: "And what We enjoined upon Abraham, Moses, and Jesus, is to establish the religion," meaning: He has prescribed for you from the religion, to establish the religion "for that," as that is the meaning of the statement, in the accusative case to translate it from the "ma" in the phrase: "what Noah enjoined upon it." It may be in a diminutive case in response to the 'ha' in His saying: 'with it,' and as an interpretation of it. The meaning of the statement then is: 'He has legislated for you of the religion as Noah commanded,' that you establish the religion and not be divided in it. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
