## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, Praise be to Allah and peace be upon His servants whom Allah has chosen as best, will they not associate partners with Him": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad, "Say," O Muhammad, "Praise be to Allah" for His blessings upon us and His success in what He has guided us. "And peace," meaning: And a safe punishment from Him with which He punished the people of Lot and the people of Salih, over those He chose. He says: those whom He chose for His Prophet Muhammad, and He made them his companions and ministers on the religion He sent him to call to, excluding the polytheists who denied the prophethood of His Prophet. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Sufyan.
