## Summary
A report of Ibn Abbas about himself in the chapter on his virtues in Sahih Muslim: the Prophet prayed that he be given understanding of religion when he was a boy. It is the founding text for Ibn Abbas's central place in exegesis and jurisprudence in Sunni tradition. {cite:sahih-muslim}
