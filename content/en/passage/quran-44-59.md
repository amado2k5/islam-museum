## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 58 to 59 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So wait, wait, for they are awaited": So you, O Muhammad, await victory from your Lord and victory over those polytheists with Allah from your people from Quraysh. They are waiting within themselves for your subjugation and overcoming you by preventing them from what you brought them of the truth that they wish to accept and follow upon them.
