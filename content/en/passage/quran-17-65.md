## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, My servants over whom you have no authority, and sufficient is your Lord as a Disposer of affairs": Allah says to Iblis: Indeed, My servants are those who obey Me. So they follow My command and disobey You, O Iblis. You have no authority over them. And His saying: "Sufficient is your Lord as a Disposer of affairs," the Exalted is He, praises be to His Prophet Muhammad: And sufficient is You, O Muhammad, your Lord, as a protector and upright in Your affairs. So he criticized His command. And His messages reached these polytheists. And fear no one, for He has entrusted His protection and support, as in Qatadah's saying: "Indeed, My servants over whom you have no authority, and sufficient is your Lord as Disposer of affairs." And Allah said in another verse: "His authority is only over those who take Him as allies, and those who associate others with Him." He also quotes Qatadah in the passage.
