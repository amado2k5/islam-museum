## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 18 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "As for Thamud, We guided them, but they preferred blindness over guidance, so the thunderbolt of punishment seized them because of what they used to earn": So We showed them the path of truth and the way of guidance, as Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "As for Thamud, We guided them": meaning We made clear to them. And from Qatadah: "As for Thamud, We guided them," making clear to them the ways of good and evil. And from Al-Suddi: "As for Thamud, We guided them," explaining them. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "As for Thamud, We guided them," he said: We taught them guidance and misguidance, forbade them from following misguidance, and commanded them to follow guidance. Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase "Thamud." Most reciters from other cities except Al-A'mash and Abdullah ibn Abi Ishaq read it as the nominative nominative for Thamud, leaving it as a name for the nation known by that name. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
