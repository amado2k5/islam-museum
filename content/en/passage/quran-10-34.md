**Commentary on verse 34 (pp. 85-86)**

On [[passage/quran-10-34|10:34]] God tells His Prophet to say to them: is there among your partners, the gods and idols, any who begins creation and then repeats it? That is, who brings a thing into being from no origin, then brings it to an end after creating it and restores it as it was before? They cannot claim that for them, and in that lies the decisive proof that they are lying and inventing when they say these are lords and God's partners in worship. So say to them: God begins creation, bringing it into being from nothing, ends it when He wills and restores it when He wills as it was before ending. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 85}

"How then are you turned away?" means by what route are you turned and twisted from the right path and the way of guidance. Report 17659, from al-Hasan: how are you turned. Al-Tabari says he has already given the views of the differing interpreters of this phrase, and his choice with its witnesses, in Surat al-An'am. The editor adds a long note: al-Tabari in fact set out the sense of the root only in Surat al-Ma'ida and never gave the interpreters' disagreement; he fears this shows that al-Tabari had spread the parts of his commentary far apart and forgot where he had treated a point, and perhaps that he began the commentary at great length and then shortened it, having prepared his material fully and then changed his writing considerably; he closes by saying that God knows best. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 86}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
