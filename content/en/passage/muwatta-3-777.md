## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on when to send the fast-breaking zakat. Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to send the fast-breaking zakat to the person who collected it two or three days before the feast. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, al-Shafii in his Musnad, and al-Bayhaqi through him transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.383}
