## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nisa 4:60, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «ألم تر إلى الذين يزكون أنفسهم».
