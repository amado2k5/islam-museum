## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 36 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "It is not for Allah to take a son, Glorified be He, when He has decreed a matter; He only says to Him be, and it is": Those who said: Jesus is the son of Allah have disbelieved, and they have greatly fabricated the slander against him. It is not appropriate for Allah to take a son, nor is that suitable for Him nor should it be. Rather, everything besides Him He created, similar to the saying of Amr ibn Ahmar: 'At the head of a noble phoenix, beneath which no plain or mountain is sought,' and that from His saying 'to take' in the place of raising a can. And His saying: "Glorified be He" means: a purification and dissolution for God that He would have what the disbelievers who say: Jesus is the son of God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
