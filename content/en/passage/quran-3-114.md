## Summary
Verse 114 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 114 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Belief, commanding, forbidding and hastening (p. 130).** Al-Tabari says belief in God and the Last Day means affirming God and the resurrection after death and knowing that God will repay them for their deeds, unlike the polytheists who deny God's oneness, worship others beside Him, deny the resurrection and reject repayment, reward and punishment. They command people to believe in God and His messenger and to affirm Muhammad and what he brought, and forbid disbelief in God and denial of Muhammad and what he brought from God; that is, they are unlike the Jews and Christians who command people to disbelieve and deny Muhammad in what he brought and keep them from the known good in deeds, which is affirming Muhammad in what he brought. He says they hasten to do good things for fear that it escape them before their appointed deaths overtake them. God then reported that those of the People of the Book with this description are among the righteous, because whoever among them is defiant has incurred God's wrath by disbelieving in Him and His signs, killing the prophets without right, disobeying his Lord and transgressing His limits. The editor refers to the earlier notes on the two terms at p. 105 and on "righteous". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 130}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 130}
