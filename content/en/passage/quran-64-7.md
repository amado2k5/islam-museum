## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “Those who disbelieve claim that they will not be resurrected. Say, ‘Yes, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected; then you will surely be informed of what you did. And that, for Allah, is easy,’” means that those who disbelieve in Allah claim that He will not resurrect them from their graves after their death. Ibn Umar used to say: “Claimed” is a euphemism for lying. This was narrated by Sufyan, on the authority of some of his companions, on the authority of Ibn Umar. His statement, “Say, ‘Yes, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected,’” means that God says to His Prophet Muhammad: Say to them, O Muhammad: Yes, by my Lord, you will surely be resurrected from your graves. “Then you will surely be informed of what you did,” meaning: Then you will surely be informed of the deeds you performed in this world. “And that is easy for God,” meaning: Resurrection from your graves after your death is easy and simple for God. The passage also includes statements attributed to Sufyan.
