## Summary
Abdullah ibn Umar said: whoever has not stood at Arafat before dawn on the night of Muzdalifa has missed the pilgrimage, and whoever stood at Arafat on that night before dawn has caught the pilgrimage. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.523}
