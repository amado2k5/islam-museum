## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 16 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "the killing of the Kharasun" as follows: curses the speculators who fabricate lies and falsehood and think it is true. Interpreters differ regarding those who meant by the phrase "the killing of the Kharasun," with some saying: it meant the suspicious. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
