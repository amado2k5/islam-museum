## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 10 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "the second of his conjunction to lead him astray from the path of Allah in this world, and We will make him taste on the Day of Resurrection the punishment of the fire": This is the second conjunction of the one who argues about Allah without knowledge, "the second of His conjunction." Interpreters differ on the meaning for which it was described as 'to deflect His conjunction' and what is meant by describing it as such. Some said: He was described as such to exaggerate and boast about it. It is mentioned about the Arabs that they say: So-and-so came to me with a second conjunction: when he came with arrogance, and in the Maqta' he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
