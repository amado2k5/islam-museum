## Summary
Malik transmits that he heard people of knowledge not dislike using the miswak for the fasting person in Ramadan at any hour of the day, neither at its beginning nor at its end, and he never heard anyone of knowledge dislike it or forbid it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (856) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.417}
