## Summary
Verse 74 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 74 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 74: the singling out of mercy.** Al-Tabari says the verb at the start of the verse is the form from the saying "I singled out so-and-so for such-and-such", and that mercy here means Islam and the Quran together with prophethood. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 517}

**Reports 7256-7260.** In report 7256 (Muhammad ibn Amr, from Abu Asim, from Isa, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid) mercy is prophethood, which He gives to whom He wills; report 7257 (al-Muthanna, from Abu Hudhayfa, from Shibl, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid) is the same. In report 7258 (al-Muthanna, from Ishaq, from Abdallah ibn Abi Jafar, from his father, from al-Rabi) He singles out for prophethood whom He wills. In report 7259 (al-Muthanna, from Suwayd, from Ibn al-Mubarak by recitation, from Ibn Jurayj) it is the Quran and Islam; report 7260 (al-Qasim, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Ibn Jurayj) is the same. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 517} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 518}

**The greatness of the bounty.** Al-Tabari says the meaning is that He possesses a grace with which He favors whom He loves of His creatures, and then describes it as great because no grace given by creatures resembles it in the weight of its place with the one who receives it, or comes near it in the majesty of its standing. The editor says the printed text and the manuscript differ in this sentence, and that the copyist of the manuscript dropped the word the editor added in brackets so that the speech runs. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 518}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
