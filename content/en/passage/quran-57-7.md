## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Believe in Allah and His Messenger and spend from that whereby He has made you successors. So those of you who have believed and spent, for them is a great reward": Believe in Allah, O people, acknowledge His oneness and His Messenger Muhammad, and believe in what He has brought you from Allah and follow Him, and spend from what He has made you successors in. He, Glorified and Exalted, says: 'And spend from what Allah has granted you of the wealth He has granted you from those before you, and made you their successors therein in the way of Allah.' In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
