## Summary
Verse 5 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 5 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says: these who hold others equal to God have denied the truth when it came to them, and that truth is Muhammad; they denied him and rejected his prophethood when he came to them. God said to them, threatening them for denying him and rejecting his prophethood: there will come to those of your people and others who deny you, Muhammad, "news of what they used to mock", that is, the reports of their mockery of what they used to mock of my signs and proofs which I gave them; then he kept his threat to them when they persisted in their error and defied their Lord, and killed them at Badr by the sword. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 262}

The editor refers for "turning away", "the truth", "news" and "mockery" to earlier volumes 9 and 10.

Verse 5 closes here at p. 262, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-6|6:6]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
