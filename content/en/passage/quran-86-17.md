## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 23 as one section. He interprets His saying “And the sky which returns”: “And the sky which returns” returns with clouds and the provisions of the servants every year. And from it is the saying of Al-Mutanakhil in describing a sword: “White like the return, a sinker when it plunges into a gathering that is secluded.” And in the section he quotes sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd and others.
