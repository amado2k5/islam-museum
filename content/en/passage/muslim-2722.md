## Summary
A report of Zayd ibn Arqam in the chapter on seeking refuge from incapacity and laziness and other things, in the Book of Remembrance and Supplication of Sahih Muslim: a comprehensive supplication for purifying the soul and seeking its piety. Zayd transmitted it as he heard it, without adding to it. {cite:sahih-muslim}
