## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Look how We have preferred some of them over others, and for the Hereafter is the greatest rank and the greatest preference": Look, O Muhammad, with the eye of your heart, at these two groups, one of which is the immediate abode, and it is sought, and the other is the one who desires the Hereafter, and for it he strives, certain of Allah's reward for his effort. How did We prefer one of the two groups over the other, that our sight is his rightness, and We guided him to the path that is more upright, and made it easy for the One who is more guided and guided, and we forsaken the other, so We led him astray from the path of truth and blinded his eyes from the path of guidance. "And to the Hereafter" The greatest ranks" He says: "And the group of those who seek the Hereafter are greater in the Hereafter in degrees over one another because their ranks differ in their deeds in Paradise, and they are greater in preference for Allah over others than these other groups in this world in what We have expanded for them therein." In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
