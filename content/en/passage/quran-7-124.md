**Commentary on verse 124 (p. 34 onward)**

Al-Tabari reports Pharaoh's words to the sorcerers when they believed in God and held His messenger Moses true: "I shall cut off your hands and feet on opposite sides", which means cutting from one of them the right hand and the left foot, or the left hand and the right foot, differing in the limbs cut; this is cutting "from opposite sides". The editor refers to the earlier explanation. It is said that Pharaoh was the first to establish this cutting. His words "then I shall crucify you all" he said when he saw God's abandoning of him and Moses's victory and mastery over him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 34}

Report 14956, from Ibn Abbas through Ibn Waki', Abu Dawud al-Hafari, Habawayh al-Razi, Ya'qub al-Qummi, Ja'far ibn Abi al-Mughira and Sa'id ibn Jubayr: the first who crucified, and the first who cut hands and feet from opposite sides, was Pharaoh. In a footnote the editor gives the identity of Habawayh al-Razi, Ishaq ibn Isma'il Abu Yazid. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 34}

Commentary on verse 124 ends here at p. 34; verse 125 begins on p. 35. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 34}
