## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Do you not know that Allah knows what is in the heavens and the earth? Indeed, that is in a Book. Indeed, that is easy for Allah": Do you not know, O Muhammad, that Allah knows all that is in the seven heavens and the seven earths? Nothing of that is hidden from Him. He is the judge among His creation on the Day of Resurrection, fully aware of all that they have done in this world, so He recompenses the doer of good with his kindness and the wrongdoer with his evil. "Indeed, that is in a Book," says He, the Exalted: His knowledge of that is in a Book, which is the mother of the Book in which our Lord, Glorified and Exalted, wrote before He created His creation that which is in existence until the Day of Resurrection. "Indeed, that is easy for Allah." Also: From Al-Awza'i, from Abda ibn Abi Lubabah, who said: Allah knows what is created and what creation is done, then He wrote it down, then said to His Prophet: "Do you not know that Allah knows what is in the heavens and the earth? Indeed, that is in a Book. Indeed, that is easy for Allah." In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
