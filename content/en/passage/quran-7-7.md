## Summary
Verse 7 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 7 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "We shall relate to them with knowledge, and We were never absent", God says: I shall tell the messengers and those I sent them to, with certain knowledge, what they did in the world of what I commanded them and forbade them, "and We were never absent" from them or from the deeds they did. If someone asks, how are the messengers and those sent to asked, when He reports that He will relate their deeds with knowledge? The answer is that this is not a question to seek guidance, nor to learn from them what He does not know, but a question of reproach and affirmation whose sense is a report, as a man says to another, did I not do you good, and you did wrong, did I not keep ties with you, and you cut them? So God's questioning those sent to is that He says to them, did My messengers not come to you with clear proofs, did I not send warners to warn you of My punishment on this day for whoever disbelieved in Me and worshipped another, as He reports that He will say to them that day what is in [[passage/quran-36-60|36:60]], the covenant with the children of Adam not to worship Satan and to worship Him, and the like, speech whose outward form is a question and whose sense is a report and narration, and beyond that reproach and affirmation. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "relating". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 307}

As for the questioning of the messengers, which is narration and report, the idolatrous nations, when asked on the Day of Resurrection, did not messengers come to you from among yourselves reciting your Lord's signs, many of them denied it and said no bearer of good news or warner came to us; so the messengers are asked, did you convey what you were sent with, or did you not convey to these what you were sent with, as the report from the Messenger of God came and as He said to our Prophet Muhammad's community in [[passage/quran-2-143|2:143]], that they be witnesses over people and the Messenger a witness over them. All of that is God's questioning the messengers by way of calling them as witnesses against the nations they were sent to, and questioning those sent to by way of affirmation and reproach, all in the sense of narration and report. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 308}

What is denied of God's questioning His creation is the question of seeking guidance and verification about what the asker does not know and the asked knows, so that the asker may learn it from him; that cannot be said of God, since He knows things before they come to be, as they come to be and after, and that is the question He denied of Himself in [[passage/quran-55-39|55:39]] and [[passage/quran-28-78|28:78]], that none is asked about his sins as one seeking verification. Al-Tabari says he gave what was reported on it elsewhere and did not wish to repeat it. It is reported of Ibn Abbas that he said of "We shall relate to them with knowledge" that the record of their deeds will speak against them with their deeds. This is not far from the truth, but the sound report from the Messenger of God is that he said: none of you but his Lord will speak to him on the Day of Resurrection with no interpreter between them, and He will say, do you remember the day you did such and such, until He reminds him of what he did in the world; and submitting to the Messenger of God's report is better than submitting to another. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 308}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
