## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman from Abu Hurayra and Zayd ibn Thabit, that they saw no harm in what the sea throws up. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.639}
