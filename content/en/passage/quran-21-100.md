## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 100 to 101 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They will have a sigh therein while they do not hear": He means His mention by saying: "for them" are the polytheists and their gods, and the letter 'h' and the meem in the phrase "for them" is from the mention of "all" in the phrase "and they will abide therein forever," meaning they will all be in Hell with exhalation, "and they will not hear therein," meaning they will be in the Fire they do not hear. Ibn Mas'ud interpreted the phrase "while they are therein and do not hear": He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Al-Mas'udi, from Yunus ibn Khabbab, who said: Ibn Mas'ud recited this verse: "They will have a breath therein, and they will not hear," he said: When someone is thrown into the fire to abide therein, they are placed in coffins of fire, then those coffins are placed in other coffins, then the coffins are placed in other coffins with nails of fire, so none of them sees that anyone in the fire is being punished. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ikrimah.
