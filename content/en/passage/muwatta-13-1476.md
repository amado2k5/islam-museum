## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Ali ibn Husayn, who told him that Aqil and Talib inherited from Abu Talib, but Ali did not inherit from him. He said: that is why we gave up our share of the valley. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.23}
