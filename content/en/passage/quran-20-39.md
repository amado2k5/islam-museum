## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Throw it into the Ark, so cast it into the sea, and let the sea throw it into the shore, and an enemy of mine will take it, and I will cast love upon you from Me and do it in My eyes." And We have favored you once again when We revealed to your mother, that you cast your son Moses into the Ark when she gave birth to him. "So cast him into the sea," meaning the Nile, "let the sea throw him into the shore." He means: "Cast him into the sea," and the sea will throw him into the shore, which is a reward that brings forth the outcome of the matter, as if the sea is the command, as He, the Exalted, said: "Follow Our way, and We will bear your sins," meaning: follow Our way, and We will bear your sins. His mother did that to him, and he was cast into the house of Pharaoh's family, as Ibn Ishaq said: When Moses gave birth to his mother, she nursed him, until when Pharaoh ordered the killing of the two children of his own age, she was taken to him, and she did as God commanded him, placing him in a small ark and preparing it for him. Then she went to the Nile and threw him into it. Pharaoh spent time in his gathering, sitting on a shaft and quoting in the passage sayings from Ikrimah and Ibn Ishaq.
