## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, Allah has knowledge of the Hour, and He sends down rain and knows what is in the wombs, and no soul knows what it will earn tomorrow, nor does a soul know in which land it will die. Indeed, Allah is Knowing and Acquainted." "O mankind, fear your Lord and fear a Day when a father will not recompense for his child, nor will a child be recompensed for his father at all." He has come to you with knowledge of His arrival with your Lord. No one knows when He will come to you. He will not come to you except suddenly. So fear Him lest He surprises you suddenly, while you have not repented of your misguidance From it, you will become from God's punishment and punishment to what you cannot bear. And God Almighty began to announce His knowledge of the coming of the Hour. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
