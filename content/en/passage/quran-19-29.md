## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So she pointed to him. They said, 'How can we speak to someone who was in the cradle as a child?': When her people told her this, she told them what Jesus had commanded her to tell them. Then she signaled to Jesus to speak to him, as in Al-Suddi, who said: When they said to her: 'Your father was not a bad man, nor was your mother a tyrant,' she said to them what Allah had commanded her to do. When they asked her to speak afterward, she pointed to him, to Jesus. And from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: 'She pointed to him,' he said: She commanded them to speak to him. From Ibn Ishaq, from those who do not accuse, from Wahb ibn Munabbih: 'So she pointed to him,' meaning she indicated to him that they spoke to him. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, regarding the phrase 'so she pointed to him,' that they spoke to him. In the Maqta' section, he quotes from Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
