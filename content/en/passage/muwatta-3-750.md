## Summary
A saying of Malik: there is no charity on fresh herbs or on vegetables of all kinds, nor on their price if sold, until a year passes from the day of the sale and its owner receives the price. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.372}
