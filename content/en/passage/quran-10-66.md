**Commentary on verse 66 (p. 143)**

On [[passage/quran-10-66|10:66]] al-Tabari says: all who are in the heavens and on the earth belong to God, kings and servants, and no one else owns any of it; so how can one be a god worshipped whom these polytheists worship, idols and images, when they are God's possession, and worship is due the owner and not the owned, the Lord and not the lorded? 'What do those who call on partners besides God follow?' means what do they follow who falsely say God has partners in His authority and kingdom, when God alone owns everything? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 143}

'They follow nothing but conjecture' means they follow only conjecture in what they say and claim, that is, doubt and not certainty, and 'they only guess' means they only invent falsehood by conjecture and guessing at a lie, without knowledge of what they say. The editor says the printed text has one form of the word and he keeps the manuscript's, and that the verbal noun with ya is the same as the form with nun, its last nun turned to ya because of the run of nuns and the heaviness of their succession, which is common in Arabic speech. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 143}

We stop here at the foot of p. 143, where the commentary on verse 66 closes; the next batch begins at p. 144, with verse 67. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 143}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
