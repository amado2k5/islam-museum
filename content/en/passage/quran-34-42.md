## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So this Day, none of you will have power over one another in benefit or harm. And We say to those who have wronged, taste the punishment of the Fire for which you used to deny": So today, O angels, none of you have any benefit by which they worshipped you in this world, nor any harm they caused you to suffer, nor to harm them. "And We say to those who wronged," meaning: And We say to those who worship other than Allah and place worship in a place other than its proper place, and make it for others who should be it for them: "Taste the punishment of the Fire in which you were in." In this world, you "deny," for you have received it.
