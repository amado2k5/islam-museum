## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 64 to 65 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "They will abide therein forever, finding no protector or helper": they will remain in the Blaze forever, indefinitely, "they will find no protector," who will take care of them, and He will rescue them from the Blaze that God has built for them, "nor any helper," who will help them, and save them from God's punishment.
