## Summary
Malik says: similarly, the Messenger forbade muzabana, and permitted the sale of ariya by estimate in dates. The difference between them is that muzabana is a sale in the manner of haggling and trade, while the sale of ariya is in the manner of kindness, with no haggling in it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.178}
