## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "But whoever repents, believes, and does righteous deeds, perhaps he will be among the successful": "But as for the one who repents" among the polytheists, he repents, returns to the truth, devotes himself to God in divinity, and dedicates worship to Him alone, so he does not associate anything in His worship. "And believe," meaning: He believed in His Prophet Muhammad. "And did righteousness," meaning: and acted according to what God commanded him to do in His Book and on the tongue of His Messenger, "so perhaps he will be among the successful." He says: He is among the successful seekers who seek them with Allah, abiding eternally in His Paradise, and perhaps Allah is obligatory.
