## Summary
Malik said it reached him that a man of the Ansar from Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj gave charity on behalf of his parents, then both died, and their son inherited the property, which was palm trees. He asked the Messenger about it, and he said: your charity is accepted, and take it as your inheritance. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.307}
