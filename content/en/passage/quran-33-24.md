## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 23–24. Al-Tabari mentions that among the believers were men who were true to their covenant with God; some fulfilled their vows, meaning they completed what they were obligated upon themselves and were martyred at Badr, Uhud, or others, while others waited without any change, so that God would reward the truthful for their truthfulness and punish or accept the repentance of the hypocrites.
