**Commentary on verse 115 (p. 526)**

On [[passage/quran-11-115|11:115]] al-Tabari says: be patient, Muhammad, with what you meet from the polytheists of your people of harm for God's sake and what is disagreeable, hoping for God's abundant reward for it, for God does not waste the reward of whoever does good by obeying God and following His command so that it goes, but He gives it in full when he most needs it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 526}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
