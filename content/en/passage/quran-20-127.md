## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And thus We recompense those who transgress and do not believe in the signs of their Lord, and the punishment of the Hereafter is more severe and more lasting": Thus We recompense: meaning We recompense whoever transgresses and disobeys his Lord and does not believe in His messengers and books. We make for him a difficult life in the Barzakh as we have explained before: "And the punishment of the Hereafter is more severe and more lasting." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: and a punishment in the Hereafter is harsher for them than what You promised them in the grave of the miserable life. "And more lasting" means: and more lasting than it, because it is indefinite and endless.
