## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We took revenge on them, so see what was the end of the deniers": So We took revenge on those who denied their messengers from the disbelieving nations of their Lord by punishing them. So see, O Muhammad, how was the outcome of their affairs, as they denied the signs of Allah. And by saying: "The end of the deniers," he means the last matter of those who denied the messengers of Allah was what happened. He says: Did We not destroy them and make them an example for others? Also: Qatadah narrated from Qatadah: "So take revenge on them and see how was the end of the deniers," he said: Evil, by God, He seized them by the swallowing and drowning, then destroyed them and cast them into the Fire. In the Maqta, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
