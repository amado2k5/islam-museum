## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 19 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Did We not destroy the former peoples?”: Did We not destroy the past nations who disbelieved in My messengers and denied My signs, such as the people of Noah, 'Ad, and Thamud? Then We will follow them with others who followed their path in disbelieving in Me and My messengers, such as the people of Abraham, the people of Lot, and the people of Madyan, so We will destroy them as We destroyed the former peoples before them.
