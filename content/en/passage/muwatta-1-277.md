## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told a man sneezed on a Friday while the imam was preaching, and a man beside him said "God have mercy on you". He asked Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab about it, and Sa'id forbade him and said: do not repeat it. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it with a chain through Abdullah ibn Sa'id ibn Abi Hind, and that Suwayd did too. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.161}
