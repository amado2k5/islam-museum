## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 5 to 10 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Hamim": We have already explained the meaning of His saying: "Hamm" (the Prophet) and the clear Book. And His saying: "Indeed, We sent it down on a blessed night," the Exalted swears by this Book that He revealed it on a blessed night. The interpreters differed on which night of the year it was. Some said: It is the Night of Decree. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
