## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those towns We destroyed because they wronged them and set for their destruction a fixed term." And those towns of 'Aad, Thamud, and the people of the den We destroyed their people when they were wronged, and they disbelieved in Allah and His signs. "And We appointed for their destruction a fixed term," meaning a fixed time and term. When they reached it, a punishment came upon them and We destroyed them thereby. He says: Thus We set for these polytheists from your people, O Muhammad, who will never believe in you. When that time comes to them, We will destroy them in Our way concerning those who came before them from their strikes, as follows: And from Ibn Abi Najih, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "They will destroy them by a fixed time," he said: Yes. Al-Husayn narrated to me, he said: Hajjaj narrated to him, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase "to destroy them," so the majority of the reciters of Hijaz and Iraq read it as "lamhalahum" with a damma on the meem and fatha on the lam, attributing this to the verbal noun of those who destroyed them in destruction. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
