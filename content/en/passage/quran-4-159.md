**Commentary on verse 159: the People of the Book believe in Jesus before their death (pp. 379-384)**

The commentators differ on the verse saying that every one of the People of the Book will believe in Jesus before his death. Some say it means before Jesus' own death, the sense being that all of them will affirm him when he descends to kill the Dajjal, so that all the faiths become one, the upright religion of Islam, the religion of Abraham. The editor notes that the seventh part of the manuscript ends here with a colophon giving the date of its completion, and that the eighth part opens with the next verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 379}

Reports 10794 and 10795, from Ibn Abbas through Sufyan, Abu Husayn and Sa'id ibn Jubayr, say before the death of Jesus. Report 10796, from Abu Malik through Husayn: at his descent, not one of the People of the Book will remain without believing in him. Report 10797, from al-Hasan through Hammad ibn Salama: before Jesus son of Mary dies. Report 10798, from al-Hasan through Ibn Ulayya: before Jesus' death; by God he is now alive with God, but when he descends they will all believe in him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 380}

Report 10799, from Qatada: before Jesus' death. Report 10801, same chain: when he descends, all faiths will believe in him. Report 10802, from al-Hasan through al-Rabi' ibn Anas: before Jesus' death; report 10803, from al-Hasan through Awf: Jesus said it, and he had not yet died. Report 10804, from Abu Malik: not one of them will remain at Jesus' descent without believing in him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 381}

Report 10806, from Ibn Zayd: when Jesus son of Mary descends and kills the Dajjal, no Jew will remain on earth without believing in him, and then belief will not benefit them. Report 10807, from Ibn Abbas through Muhammad ibn Sa'd: some of the People of the Book will live to see Jesus sent and will believe in him, and on the Day of Resurrection he will be a witness against them. Report 10808, from al-Hasan through Mansur: al-Tabari supposes that he meant that when Jesus comes out the Jews will believe in him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 381}

Others say the sense is: none of the People of the Book will believe in Jesus except before the death of each of them, that is, at the moment of seeing death, since no soul leaves until the truth in its religion is clear to it. Report 10809, from Ibn Abbas through Ali ibn Abi Talha: no Jew dies until he believes in Jesus. Report 10810, from Mujahid through Mansur: his soul does not leave until he believes in Jesus, even if he drowns, or falls from a wall, or dies whatever death. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 382}

Report 10811, from Mujahid through Ibn Abi Najih: every owner of a book will believe in Jesus before the death of the owner of the book. Report 10812, same chain, includes Ibn Abbas: even if his neck were struck, his soul would not leave until he believed in Jesus. Report 10813, from Ibn Abbas through Ikrima: no Jew dies until he testifies that Jesus is God's servant and messenger, even if he is hurried with weapons. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 383}

Report 10814, from Ibn Abbas through Khusayf and Sa'id ibn Jubayr, says the verse in Ubayy's reading is 'before their death', and that no Jew dies until he believes in Jesus; if asked what if he falls from a roof, he says he speaks it while falling, and if his neck is struck, his tongue stammers it. Report 10815, from Ibn Abbas through Ikrima: no Jew dies until he believes in Jesus, even if struck by the sword he speaks it, and if he falls he speaks it while falling. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 383}

Report 10816, from Ibn Abbas through Shu'ba and Abu Harun al-Ghanawi: if a Jew fell from the top of a house, he would not die until he believed in him. Report 10817, from Ikrima through Shu'ba: if a Jew fell from a palace he would not reach the ground before believing in Jesus. Report 10818, from Mujahid through Sufyan and Abu Hashim al-Rumani: even if he fell from the roof of a house, he would not die until he believed. Report 10819, from Mujahid through Mansur: no man of the People of the Book dies until he believes, even if he drowns, falls or dies by any means; report 10820, from Mujahid through Layth: his soul does not leave until he believes in him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 384}

We stop here after report 10820 at p. 384, in the commentary on verse 159, and will continue from report 10821. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 384}
