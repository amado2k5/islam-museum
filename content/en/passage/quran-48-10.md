## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who pledge allegiance to you are only pledging allegiance to Allah. The hand of Allah is above their hands. So whoever breaks their allegiance only breaks his own soul. And whoever is more fulfilling what he promised Allah, He will give him a great reward": "Indeed, those who pledge allegiance to you" at Hudaybiyyah are among your companions, on the condition that they do not flee when meeting the enemy or turn their backs on them. "They are only pledging allegiance to Allah." He means: they are only pledging allegiance to Allah by pledging allegiance to you, because Allah has guaranteed them Paradise by fulfilling that to Him. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
