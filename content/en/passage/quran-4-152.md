**Commentary on verse 152: those who believe in God and His messengers without distinguishing (p. 355)**

Al-Tabari says the people meant are those who affirm God's oneness and acknowledge the prophethood of all His messengers, accepting what they brought of His religious law, so that they do not reject some and accept others, but hold that all that came from their Lord is true. God will give them their reward, that is their recompense for accepting the messengers, forgive their past sins by His mercy and pardon, guide them to the right path, and that is their deliverance from the fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 355}
