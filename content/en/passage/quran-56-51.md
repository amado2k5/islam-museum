## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 51 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "so they fill the bellies from it": they fill their bellies with the Zaqqum tree: and the Arabs differ regarding the feminine form of the tree in the phrase: "they fill their bellies" meaning from the trees, "and they drink on it" because the trees are feminine and masculine, and feminine because the tree can indicate everyone. The Arabs say: A bitter tree grew before us with a bad scarce, and they mean everyone.
