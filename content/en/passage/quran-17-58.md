## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And there is no town but We destroy it before the Day of Resurrection or punish it with a severe punishment recorded in the Book": And there is no town but that We destroy its people with annihilation, then destroy them before the Day of Resurrection, or punish them, either by the affliction of those killed by the sword or other severe punishments. Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the words of Allah, the Almighty: "And there is no town except that We will destroy it before the Day of Resurrection," so they will destroy it "or punish it" by killing and affliction. He said: Every village on earth will be affected by some of this. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly, but he said: This or part of it will be affected. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Samak.
