## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yazid ibn Abdallah ibn Qusayt, that he saw Said ibn al-Musayyab weighing gold for gold: he poured his gold into one pan of the scale, and the other man who was weighing with him poured his gold into the other pan; when the beam balanced, they gave and took. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.164}
