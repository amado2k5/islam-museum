## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 36 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Indeed, for the righteous are gardens of bliss with their Lord”: “Indeed, for the righteous” are those who feared God’s punishment by performing His obligatory duties and avoiding His prohibitions, “are gardens of bliss with their Lord,” meaning: gardens of everlasting bliss.
