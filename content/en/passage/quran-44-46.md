## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46 to 49 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "If the Zaqqum tree grows": "Indeed, the tree of Zaqqum," which he informed grows at the root of Hell, which it made food for the people of Hell, its fruit in Hell is the food of the sinner in this world with his Lord. The Athim is the one who sins and whoever sins is sinful. And he meant here this: the one whose sin is disbelief in his Lord, not other sins. And it is narrated from Al-A'mash, from Ibrahim, from Hammam ibn Al-Harith, that Abu Al-Darda used to read to a man, "Indeed, the tree of Zaqqum is the food of the sinner," and he said: The food of the orphan. Abu Al-Darda' said: Say, the tree of Zaqqum is the food of the wicked. From Abu Yahya, from Mujahid, from Ibn Abbas who said: "If even a drop of Zaqqum of Hell were sent down to the world, it would ruin people's livelihoods." In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and others.
