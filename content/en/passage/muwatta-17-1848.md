## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Ata ibn Yasar, that Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan sold a water vessel of gold or silver for more than its weight. Abu al-Darda said: I heard the Messenger forbid such a thing except equal for equal. Muawiya said: I see no harm in such a thing. Abu al-Darda said: who will excuse me from Muawiya? I tell him what I heard from the Messenger, and he tells me his opinion. I will not live with you in a land where you are. Abu al-Darda went to Umar ibn al-Khattab and told him about it, and Umar wrote to Muawiya that it be sold only equal for equal, by weight for weight. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the report is apparently disconnected, since Ata did not hear from Abu al-Darda as far as is known. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.160}
