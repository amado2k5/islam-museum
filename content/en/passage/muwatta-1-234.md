## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Sumayy, client of Abu Bakr, from Abu Salih al-Samman from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on saying amen behind the imam: the Prophet ordered the followers to say "O God, our Lord, to You is praise" when the imam says "God hears whoever praises Him", and whoever's words coincide with the angels' words is forgiven his past sin. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban and Ahmad, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.141} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.142}
