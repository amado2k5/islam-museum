## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say that if a sick person cannot prostrate, he gestures with his head and does not raise anything to his forehead to prostrate on. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.238} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.239}
