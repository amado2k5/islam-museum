## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who do not bear false witness, and when they pass through idle talk, they pass by with honor": The interpreters differed on the meaning of falsehood, which Allah described these people as not witnessing it, and some said: it means associating partners with Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Al-Dhahhak, and Ibn Zayd.
