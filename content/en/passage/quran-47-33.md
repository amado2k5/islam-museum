## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 34 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "O you who have believed, obey Allah and obey the Messenger and do not invalidate your deeds": "O you who have believed" in Allah and His Messenger, "Obey Allah and obey the Messenger" regarding their command and prohibition, "and do not nullify your deeds." He means: "And do not nullify your disobedience to them, and your disbelief in your Lord is the reward of your deeds, for disbelief in Allah nullifies the previous righteous deeds." In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
