## Summary
Verse 157 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 157 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says that their claim 'we killed the Messiah' is one God refuted, saying: they did not kill him nor crucify him, but a likeness was made for them; that is, they neither killed nor crucified Jesus, but it was made to seem so. Commentators differ on the form of the likeness. Some say that when the Jews surrounded Jesus and his companions, all of them were changed into Jesus' form, so that the men who wanted to kill him could not tell him apart, and one of those in the house went out to them, and they killed him thinking him Jesus. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 367}

Report 10779, from Wahb ibn Munabbih: Jesus was in a house with seventeen of the disciples when they were surrounded; God made them all in his form, and they said: bring out Jesus to us or we will kill you all. Jesus asked his disciples who would buy himself with paradise today; a man went out and said: I am Jesus. They seized him, killed him and crucified him; hence the likeness was cast on them, and the Christians thought it was Jesus, and God raised Jesus that day. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 368}

Report 10780, another version from Wahb through Abd al-Samad ibn Ma'qil: when God told Jesus that he was going out of the world, he was grieved at death and invited the disciples to a meal; then he washed their hands with his own and wiped them on his clothes; he asked them to pray that his term be postponed. They slept until they could not pray, and he said: can you not bear with me one night? He told them one of them would disbelieve in him before the cock crowed three times, and another would sell him for a small sum. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 368}

In the same report, the Jews sought him and seized Simon, one of the disciples, who denied him; then he heard the crowing of a cock and wept. One disciple guided the Jews to Jesus for thirty dirhams; they seized and bound him, spat on him and put thorns on him, and brought him to the wood to crucify him, but God raised him up, and they crucified the likeness, and it remained seven days. His mother and the woman he had healed came weeping at the crucified figure; Jesus came to them and said: I have been raised to God, and nothing has befallen me but good; this was a thing made to appear to them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 369}

The disciple who had sold Jesus was missing; it was said that he repented, hanged himself. Jesus said: had he repented, God would have accepted his repentance. He told the disciples to meet him at a place, and eleven came. He asked for a young follower called Yuhanna, and told them he was with them; go, for each of you will wake speaking the language of a people, to warn them and call them. The editor notes that al-Tabari gives this in his history, with small differences in wording. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 370}

Others say that Jesus asked those with him in the house to put his likeness on one of them, and a man volunteered; his likeness was cast on him and he was killed, and God raised Jesus. Report 10781, from Qatada through Sa'id and Bishr ibn Mu'adh, says the enemies of God, the Jews, plotted to kill Jesus and claimed they had killed and crucified him; Jesus asked his disciples who would have his likeness cast on him and be killed, and a man answered: I, prophet of God! So that man was killed, and God protected His prophet and raised him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 370}

Report 10782, from Qatada through Ma'mar, says that the likeness was cast on a disciple and he was killed, and Jesus had put this to them: who will take my likeness and have paradise? A man said: I. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 370}

We stop here after report 10782 at p. 370, in the commentary on verse 157, and will continue from report 10783. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 370}
