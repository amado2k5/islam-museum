## Summary
Malik transmits that it reached him that a man asked al-Qasim ibn Muhammad and Sulayman ibn Yasar about a man who made zihar against a woman before marrying her. Both said: if he marries her, he must not approach her until he pays the expiation for zihar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.68}
