## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 45 to 47 in one passage after finishing the state of Iblis's followers: "Indeed, the righteous are in gardens and springs" — those who feared God by obeying Him and feared Him and avoided His sins. They are told, "Enter it in peace, safe" from God's punishment and not deprive His favor and honor. And "We removed what is in their breasts of grudge": Allah removed what was in the hearts of the righteous of hatred and animosity toward one another—and they differed whether it happens after they enter Paradise. It is narrated from Abu Umamah that they enter with enmity in their chests until Allah removes it, or before entering it. It is narrated from him that a believer does not enter Paradise until the grudge and the fierce seven are removed from him. And from Al-Hasan al-Basri from Ali that it was revealed to the people of Badr, and from Ali that he hoped that he, Talha, and Al-Zubayr would be among its people when Ibn Jarmuz, the killer of Al-Zubayr, asked permission from him. From Ibn Uyaynah and Al-Dhahhak, enmity is enmity. "We are brothers on opposite beds"—neither of them can turn away from one another.
