## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And mention in the Book Ishmael; he was truthful in his promise and was a messenger and a prophet": And mention, O Muhammad, in the Book Ishmael son of Abraham, and recount his story: he never denied his promise or broke his promise, but when he promised his Lord or a servant from among His servants, he fulfilled it, as he said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, regarding His saying: "Indeed, he was truthful in his promise," he said: his Lord did not prepare a number that He did not fulfill. Amr ibn al-Harith told me that Sahl ibn Aqil told him that Isma'il promised a man a place to come to him, but he came and forgot the man, so Ismail stayed with him and stayed overnight until the man came the next day. He said: Did you leave here? He said: No. He said: I forgot. He said: I would not leave until you came, so he was truthful. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Jurayj.
