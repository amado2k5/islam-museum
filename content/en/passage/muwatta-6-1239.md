## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr that Umar ibn al-Khattab judged a ram for a hyena, a goat for a gazelle, a kid for a hare, and a jerboa for a young goat. The editor notes that Malik's transmitters all narrated it from Abu al-Zubayr from Jabir, and that Yahya alone left out Jabir. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.553}
