## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “It is He Who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to make it prevail over all religions, even though the polytheists may detest it,” is explained as follows: God sent His Messenger Muhammad with guidance and the religion of truth, meaning with the clear explanation of the truth and the religion of truth, which is Islam. His statement, “to make it prevail over all religions,” means to make His true religion, with which He sent His Messenger, prevail over all other religions. This will occur upon the descent of Jesus, son of Mary, when there will be one religion, and there will be no religion other than Islam. As narrated by Sufyan, on the authority of Abu al-Miqdam Thabit ibn Hurmuz, on the authority of Abu Hurayrah, regarding “to make it prevail over all religions,” he said: “This refers to the coming of Jesus, son of Mary.” We have already mentioned the differing interpretations of the phrase "to make it prevail over all religions," and our view on this matter, along with its supporting reasons, has been discussed previously, making it unnecessary to repeat it here. The passage also includes statements attributed to Sufyan.
