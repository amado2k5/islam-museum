## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 47 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and that He is the one who caused death and gave life": and that He is the cause of death among His creation, and He gave life to those who were given life among them. By "He gave life" he meant breathing the soul into the dead sperm, making it alive by making the soul into it. And His saying: "And that He created the two spouses, the male and the female from a drop of fluid when He willed," the Exalted says: He invented the creation of the two spouses, the male and the female, and made them pairs, because the male is the wife of the female, and the female has a husband, so they are two pairs, each of which is a mate to the other.
