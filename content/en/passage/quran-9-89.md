**Commentary on verse 89 (p. 415)**

Al-Tabari says: God prepared for His Messenger Muhammad and those who believed with him gardens, that is, orchards under whose trees rivers run, "abiding in them", staying in them, neither dying in them nor departing from them; "that is the great triumph" means that is the great deliverance and abundant portion. The editor refers to earlier explanations of preparing, the garden, abiding and triumph. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 415}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
