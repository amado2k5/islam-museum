## Summary
Malik transmits from Ayyub ibn Musa that Abdullah ibn Umar looked into a mirror for a sore in his eyes while in ihram. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1195) and Suwayd (582) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.482}
