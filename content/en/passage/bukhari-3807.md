## Summary
In the Book of the Ansar: Anas ibn Malik reports from Abu Usayd on the order of the four households of the Ansar in merit: Banu al-Najjar, then Banu Abd al-Ashhal, then al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj, then Sa'ida, with good granted to all their homes. Sa'd ibn Ubada, chief of the Khazraj, received this report. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
