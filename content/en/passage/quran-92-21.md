## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 21 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “and he will surely be satisfied”: This person who gives his wealth in the way of God Almighty will surely be satisfied, purified by what God will reward him with in the Hereafter as compensation for what he gave in this world in His cause, when he meets his Lord, Blessed and Exalted is He. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
