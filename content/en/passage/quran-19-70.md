## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Then We know best those who are more deserving of it in prayer": Then We know better than those whom We remove from every Shia the most severe punishment and deserve the greatest punishment. It is mentioned from Ibn Jurayj that he used to say something about this: He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, "Then We know best who are more deserving of it in prayer," he said: He is more deserving of eternal life in Hell. Abu Ja'far said: What Ibn Jurayj said is a meaningless statement, because Allah, the Exalted, informed that those He removes from among every Shia of disbelief are the most severe disbelievers. There is no doubt that there is no disbeliever in Allah except that he will be eternal in the Fire, so there is no basis, and all of them will be eternal in Hell. It is not to be said: Then We know better those who are more deserving of eternity than these eternal ones, but the meaning is what we have mentioned. It may also mean: Then We know better those who are more deserving of some of the layers of Hell in prayer. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Jurayj.
