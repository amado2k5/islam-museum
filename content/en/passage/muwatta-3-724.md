## Summary
The first report in the chapter on zakat on the dried and fresh dates and grapes estimated (kharasa). Malik transmits from a trusted narrator, from Sulayman ibn Yasar and from Busr ibn Said, as a mursal report, that the Prophet said: for what the sky waters, springs, and rain-fed land irrigate, a tenth; for what is watered by hand-carried water, half a tenth. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik, and that Ibn Abd al-Barr (al-Tamhid 24/161) says it is connected through sound routes from Ibn Umar, Jabir and Muadh, and that al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah transmitted the mursal through Asim ibn Abd al-Aziz; al-Tirmidhi held the mursal more sound and the report of Ibn Umar is sound in the chapter, as in al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, who called it good and sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.363}
