## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Mahmud ibn al-Rabi' al-Ansari, in the chapter on practice in the congregational prayer: Attban ibn Malik used to lead his people in prayer although he was blind. He told the Prophet that darkness, rain and flood come, and he was blind, and asked him to pray in his house in a place he could take as a prayer place. The Prophet came and asked where he wished him to pray, and Attban pointed to a place in the house, and he prayed there. In Yahya's version it is "Mahmud ibn Labid". The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said this is a clear error, since no transmitter from Malik or from Ibn Shihab gave it except Mahmud ibn al-Rabi'. He cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i, al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.244} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.245}
