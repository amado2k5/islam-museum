## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We certainly gave David and Solomon knowledge, and they said, 'Praise be to Allah, who favored us over many of His believing servants.'" He explains: "And indeed We gave David and Solomon knowledge," which is the knowledge of the speech of birds and beasts, and other things Allah has granted them knowledge. "And they said, 'Praise be to Allah, who has favored us over many of His believing servants.'" He says: And David and Solomon said: 'Praise be to Allah, who has favored us by what He has given us of knowledge that He has given us over all His creation in our time, over many of His servants.' Those who believe in Him in our era.
