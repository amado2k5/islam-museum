## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And when We intended to destroy a town, We ordered its affluent to be affluent, but they committed sin, so the saying was true about it, so We destroyed it completely": Reciters differed in reading the phrase "We commanded its affluents," so the majority of the reciters of the Hijaz and Iraq read this as "We ordered" to shorten the alif, not extend it, lighten the mim, and fatha it. If this is read as such, the majority of its interpretations are: We commanded the affluent to obey, but they sinned in it by disobeying Allah, and their command was contrary to His command. Many who have read it have interpreted it as such. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-Rabi'.
