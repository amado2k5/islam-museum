## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on the virtues of Aisha, in the Book of the Virtues of the Companions in Sahih Muslim. Maryam and Asiya are at the head of the perfect women, and the virtue of Aisha is like that of the thrice-cooked dish over all other food. {cite:sahih-muslim}
