## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, in the chapter on permission for a woman to pray in a shirt and headscarf: a woman asked him whether the waist-wrap is hard for her, and whether she may pray in a shirt and headscarf. He said yes, if the shirt is full. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.205}
