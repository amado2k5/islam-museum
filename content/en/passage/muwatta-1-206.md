## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on opening the prayer: an imam who forgets the opening takbir until he finishes his prayer must repeat it, and those behind him must repeat the prayer as well, even if they had said the takbir. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and refers to his commentary on al-Tirmidhi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.127} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.128}
