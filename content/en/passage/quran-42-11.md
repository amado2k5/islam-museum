## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The Creator of the heavens and the earth, He has made for you from yourselves mates, and from the cattle mates in which He throws you down, there is nothing like unto Him. And He is the Hearing, the Seeing": "The Creator of the heavens and the earth," the Creator of the seven heavens and the earth. Also: and regarding Al-Suddi, His saying: "Creator of the heavens and the earth," he said: Creator. And His saying: "He made for you mates from yourselves," the Exalted says: Your Lord has married you from yourselves mates. And He said: "from yourselves" because He created Eve from Adam's ribs, so he is one of the men. "And from the cattle are pairs"—He, the Exalted, says: And He made for you from the cattle pairs of sheep, two of the goats, two of the camels, two of the cattle, two of the cattle, two of the cattle, male and female, and of every kind of that, "He will leave you therein": He means: He creates you from what He has made for you of your mates and sustains you in what He has made for you of livestock. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
