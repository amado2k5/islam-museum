## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 17 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "So whoever wills may remember it" as meaning: whoever among God's servants wills may remember it. He says: remember God's revelation and inspiration. The pronoun "it" in "it" refers to the surah, and the pronoun "it" in "remembers it" refers to the revelation and inspiration. "In pages" means it is a reminder. "In honored, exalted, purified pages" means in the Preserved Tablet, which is exalted and purified in God's sight. He then quotes in this section the sayings of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and Sufyan.
