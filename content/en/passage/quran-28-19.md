## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "But if he wanted to strike the one who is their enemy, he said, 'O Moses, do you want to kill me as you killed a soul yesterday? If you only want to be a tyrant in the land, and you do not want to be among the reformers.": When Moses wanted to strike the Pharaoh, who was his enemy and the Israelite, the Israelite said to Moses, thinking he was the one who meant him, 'Do you want to kill me as you killed a soul yesterday?' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq.
