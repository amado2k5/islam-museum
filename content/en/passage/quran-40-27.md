## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 28 in a single passage. He explains the verse: "And Moses said, 'Indeed, I seek refuge in my Lord and your Lord from every arrogant person who does not believe in the Day of Judgment.'" And Moses said to Pharaoh and his followers: 'Indeed, I sought refuge, O people, before my Lord and your Lord, from every arrogant person who is arrogant against His oneness, from acknowledging His divinity and obedience. He does not believe in a day when God will hold His creation accountable, and He will reward the doer with His kindness, and the wrongdoer will be rewarded for what he has done. Moses only sought refuge in God from those who do not believe in the Day of Judgment, because whoever does not believe in the Day of Judgment with sincerity will not hope for reward for kindness, nor for punishment for wrongdoing. And the ugliness of actions that come out of fear is that his seeking protection was especially from this type of people. And His saying: "And a believing man from Pharaoh's family concealed his faith," scholars differed about this believing man. Some said: He was from Pharaoh's people, but he had believed in Moses and made his faith easy for Pharaoh and his people out of fear for himself. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Ishaq.
