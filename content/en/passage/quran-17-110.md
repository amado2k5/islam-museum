## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Call upon Allah or supplicate to the Most Merciful.' Whatever you call upon - to Him belong the Most Beautiful Names. Do not make your prayer aloud and do not fear it, but seek a way between that." He says to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to your people's polytheists who deny the supplication of the Most Merciful: "Call upon Allah," O people, "or invoke the Most Merciful; whatever you call upon [Him], to Him belong the best names." Which names do you call upon your Lord, for to Him is only One, and to Him belongs the Most Beautiful Names? This was only said to him because the polytheists mentioned heard the Prophet calling upon his Lord: O our Lord Allah, and O our Lord, the Most Merciful, so they thought he was calling upon two gods, so Allah revealed this verse to His Prophet as a protest against them. Mention of the narration as we mentioned: He said: Muhammad ibn Kathir narrated to me, from Abdullah ibn Waqid, from Abu al-Jawza', from Ibn Abbas. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
