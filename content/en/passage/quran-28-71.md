## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, 'Have you considered, if Allah makes the night over you eternal until the Day of Resurrection, who is there other than Allah who will bring you light? Will you not hear?": Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists with Allah: O my people, have you considered, if Allah makes the night for you everlasting, not day, until the Day of Resurrection, to follow it? The Arabs say that everything that is connected and uninterrupted—whether prosperity, affliction, or blessing—is eternal. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
