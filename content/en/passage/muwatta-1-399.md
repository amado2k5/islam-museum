## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on when shortening the prayer is required: one who intends to travel does not shorten the prayer until he has left the houses of the village, and does not complete it until he has entered the first houses of the village or close to them. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.212}
