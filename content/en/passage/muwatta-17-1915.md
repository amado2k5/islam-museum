## Summary
Malik says: the agreed practice among us about the meat of camels, cattle, sheep and similar wild animals is that one is not bought for another except equal for equal, by weight for weight, hand to hand. There is no harm if it is not weighed, when one aims for equality, hand to hand. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.185}
