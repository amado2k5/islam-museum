## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari discusses verses 38 to 40 together in one place. Al-Tabari mentions that the gathered the magicians at a known appointed day, the day of adornment, and that the people gathered at noon. They were asked, 'Are you gathered to see who will prevail?' Perhaps we will follow the magicians if they are victorious.
