## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 83 to 84 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So leave them to engage and play until they meet their promised Day": So leave them, O Muhammad, those who slandered against God, who describe that He has children who engage in their falsehood and play in their worldly life until "they meet their Day which they are promised," which is the day when God will pray for them by their promise of Hell, which is the Day of Resurrection. Also: And from Al-Suddi, "until they meet their Day which they are promised," he said: the Day of Resurrection. And His saying: "And He is the One who is in the heaven is a god," and on earth there is a god, Allah the Exalted says: And Allah, to whom is the divinity in the heaven, is worshipped, and on earth He is worshipped just as He is worshipped in the heavens. Nothing else is worthy of worship, the Exalted says: So dedicate to whom this attribute is worship, and do not associate anything with Him besides Him. In the Maqta, he quotes from Qatadah.
