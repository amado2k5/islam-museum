## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And Allah brought you out from the wombs of your mothers while you knew nothing, and He made for you hearing, sight, and hearts that you might be grateful." And Allah Almighty knows you more than you knew after He brought you out of your mothers' wombs, you do not understand anything and do not know. He has provided you with intellects by which you understand and by which you distinguish good from evil, and your sight with them as long as you do not see. He has made for you hearing by which you hear sounds, so that some of you may understand what you communicate with one another, and the sight with which you see people, so you may recognize and distinguish by Some of each other. "And the hearts" means: the hearts with which you recognize things and preserve them, and you reflect and gain understanding. "That you may be grateful" means: We did that to you, so thank God for what He has bestowed upon you of that, unlike the gods and the equals, so you have made partners in gratitude to Him, and He has no partner in the blessings He has bestowed upon you.
