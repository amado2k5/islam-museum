**Commentary on verse 16 (p. 269)**

On [[passage/quran-11-16|11:16]] al-Tabari says: those I said We pay in full for their deeds in the world, 'have nothing in the next life but the Fire', which they will burn in, 'and what they made in it has come to nothing', meaning what they did in the world is gone, 'and vain is what they used to do', because they worked for other than God, so He made it vain and made its doer's wage fail. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on 'coming to nothing'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 269}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
