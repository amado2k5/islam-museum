## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 90 to 92 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Allah did not take any offspring and was not with Him any deity, then every god would have gone away with what He created, and some of them would have excelled over others. Glory be to Allah above what they describe": Allah has no offspring, nor did He have with Him in the past or when He invented things who would be fit for His worship, even if He had with Him in the past or with His creation things that would be suitable for His worship. "Who is a god when He will go," meaning: Therefore, each of them would have withdrawn from what He created, so they would be excluded from it and overcome. Perhaps some of them would prevail over others, and the strong among them would prevail over the weak, because the strong would not accept being weak, and the weak would not be fit to be gods.
