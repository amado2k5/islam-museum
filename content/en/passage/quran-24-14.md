## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if it were not for Allah's favor upon you and His mercy upon you in this world and the Hereafter, He would have touched you in what you have been subjected to, a great punishment": "And if it were not for Allah's favor upon you"—O you who are involved in the matter of Aisha, who mourn in it lies and sin, by neglecting to hasten your punishment, and His mercy upon you, He would have forgiven you "in this world and the Hereafter" by accepting your repentance for what you did in that matter, "touching you in that matter" in which you hastened her affair in this world, "a great punishment." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
