## Summary
Malik said: the practice among us regarding the fingers of the palm, when they are cut off, is that their blood-money is complete: when all five fingers are cut off, their blood-money is the blood-money of the palm, fifty camels, ten camels per finger. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.430}
