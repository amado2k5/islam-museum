## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 99 to 100 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Thus have We reduced for you the news of what has come before, and We have given you from Us a reminder": Just as We have recounted for you, O Muhammad, the news of Moses, Pharaoh, his people, and the stories of the Children of Israel with Moses, He says: Thus We have informed you of the news of things that came before you, which you neither saw nor witnessed. And His saying: "And We have given you from Us a reminder," the Exalted is His mention says to Muhammad: And We have given you, O Muhammad, from Us a reminder by which you will remember and be admonished by the people of reason and understanding. This is the Qur'an that Allah revealed to him, making it a reminder for the worlds. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
