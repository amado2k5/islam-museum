## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he said: I saw the Messenger of God point toward the east and say: look, trial is here, trial is here, from where the devil's horn rises. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.569}
