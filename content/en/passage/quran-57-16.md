## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Has it not come for those who believed that their hearts should humble themselves at the remembrance of Allah and what has been revealed of the truth? And they will not be like those who were given the Scripture before, and the time became prolonged for them, so their hearts hardened, and many of them are defiantly disobedient": "Has it not come time for those who believed in Allah and His Messenger to soften their hearts to the remembrance of Allah, so that their hearts submit to Him and to what was revealed of the truth, which is this Qur'an revealed to His Messenger? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibrahim.
