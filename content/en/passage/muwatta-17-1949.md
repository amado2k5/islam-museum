## Summary
Malik says of a box of wood wrapped in its case, or a Coptic cloth folded inside its fold: it is not permitted to sell them until they are unfolded and the contents are seen, because selling them is a sale of uncertainty, and is a touching sale. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.197}
