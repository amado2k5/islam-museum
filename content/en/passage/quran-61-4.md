## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah loves those who fight in His cause in ranks as if they were a solid structure": Allah Almighty says to those who say: If we knew the most beloved deeds to Allah, we would do them until we die: "Indeed, Allah, O people, loves those who fight in His cause," as if they are in His path and religion to which he called for "Safa," meaning that they are fighting the enemies of Allah selected. And His saying: "as if they were a solid structure" means: they fight in the path of Allah, lined up rows, as if in their lineup there are built walls that have been compacted, so be firm and perfect, so nothing remains of it. Some used to say: built with lead. In the Maqta', he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
