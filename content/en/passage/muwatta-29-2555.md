## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Umar ibn Husayn, client of Aisha bint Qudama, that Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan gave retaliation to a man's guardian against a man who had killed him with a stick, and the guardian killed him with a stick. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.444}
