## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, the Muslim men and Muslim women, the believing men and believing women, the devout men and the devout men and the truthful men and the truthful men and patient men and patient women, the humble men and humble women, the charitable men and charitable women, the fasting men and fasting women, the guards of their private parts and the women who guard and the women who remember Allah often, and the remembrance Allah has prepared for them forgiveness and a great reward": those who humble themselves before Allah in obedience and those who humble themselves, and the believers The truthful women are the Messenger of Allah in what He has brought them from Allah, the obedient men and women of Allah, the obedient men and women obedient to Allah in what He commands and forbids them, the truthful to Allah in what they have promised them and the truthful women in it, the patient for Allah in hardship and adversity to remain steadfast in His religion and in times of hardship, and the patient men and women with humble hearts before Allah and His punishment and His punishment. The humble men and women who give charity, who are the ones who fulfill Allah's rights from their wealth and those who pay it, the fasting men and women of Ramadan, which Allah has made obligatory fasting for them, and the fasting women, those who guard their private parts except with their husbands or what their right hands possess. And the women who guard that Except for their wives if they are free, or from their property if they are slaves, and those who remember Allah with their hearts, tongues, limbs, and the female reminders. In the passage, statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
