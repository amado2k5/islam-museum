## Summary
A report from Malik that he heard the people of knowledge say: when a man kills game and another helps him to it, by water or by an untrained dog, that game is not eaten, unless the shooter's arrow killed it, or it reached a vital part so that no one doubts that he killed it and it had no life after. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.636}
