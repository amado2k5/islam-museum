**Commentary on verse 95 (pp. 256-257)**

On "they said, by God, you are in your old error", al-Tabari says: those of his sons to whom Ya'qub had said "I find the scent of Joseph, if you did not think me feeble-minded" said: by God, O man, in your love and remembrance of Joseph you are in your old fault and slip, you do not forget him or console yourself for him. The editor notes that the manuscript is unpointed at this place and that "khat'ik" and "khata'ik" have one meaning. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 256}

The interpreters said the same: report 19849, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abd Allah, Mu'awiya and Ali: "you are in your old error", he says, your old fault. Report 19850, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "by God, you are in your old error", that is, in love of Joseph, you do not forget him or console yourself; they said to their father a harsh word that it was not fitting for them to say to their father, nor to a prophet of God. Report 19851, from al-Suddi, through Ibn Waki', Amr and Asbat: in the matter of Joseph. Reports 19852 and 19853, from Sufyan: from your love of Joseph. Report 19854, from Ibn Jurayj, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn and Hajjaj: in your old love. Report 19855, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: you are, in mentioning Joseph, in the falsehood you are on. Report 19856, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: they mean his old grief for Joseph, "in your old error" or your old fault. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 257}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
