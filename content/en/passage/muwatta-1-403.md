## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on the traveller's prayer when he resolves to stay: he was asked about the prayer of a prisoner, and said it is like that of a resident, unless he is travelling. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.213}
