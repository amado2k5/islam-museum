## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Umar ibn al-Khattab sent back a man from Marr al-Zahran who had not farewelled the House until he had farewelled it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1444), al-Shafii, al-Bayhaqi and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.497}
