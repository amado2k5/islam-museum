## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the Pool in the Book of Purity of Sahih Muslim. The Prophet distinguishes the Companions who saw him from the believing brothers who have not yet come, and recognises them at the Pool by their bright limbs. {cite:sahih-muslim}
