## Summary
Malik says: as for everything which is present and bought as it is, such as milk as it is milked, and fresh dates as they are picked, which the buyer takes day by day, there is no harm in that. If it runs out before the buyer has taken what he bought, the seller returns part of his gold in proportion to what remains to him, or the buyer takes goods for what remains to him, agreed between them, and does not part from him until he takes them. If he parts from him, that is disliked, because it becomes debt upon debt, and debt for debt is forbidden. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.152}
