## Summary
Yahya from Malik from Nafi from Ibn Umar: the talbiya of the Messenger of God was: here I am, O God, here I am... ; the form of the talbiya. The editor notes that this is the formula of the talbiya as given. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.447}
