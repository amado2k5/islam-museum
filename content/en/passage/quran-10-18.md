**Commentary on verse 18 (pp. 46-47)**

He then explains "They worship besides God what neither harms them nor benefits them and say: these are our intercessors with God. Say: will you inform God of what He does not know in the heavens or on the earth? Glory be to Him, and exalted is He above what they associate". These polytheists worship, besides God, what cannot harm them at all or benefit them in this world or the next, namely the gods and idols they worshipped, "and say: these are our intercessors with God", meaning they worshipped them hoping for their intercession with God. God told His prophet to say to them "will you inform God of what He does not know in the heavens or on the earth", that is, will you tell God of something that does not exist there? The gods do not intercede for them with God, yet the polytheists claimed they did; so say: will you tell God that what cannot intercede in the heavens or on earth will intercede for you there? That is false, its truth and soundness unknown; God knows it is contrary to what you say and that they do not intercede for anyone, nor benefit nor harm. The editor refers to the glosses on intercession and informing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 46}

"Glory be to Him, and exalted is He above what they associate" means exalting God and holding Him high above what these polytheists do, joining in His worship what neither harms nor benefits, and inventing lies about Him. The editor refers to the glosses on "glory" and "exalted". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 47}
