## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 19 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "They will burn in it on the Day of Judgment": He, may His praise be exalted, says: These wicked people will burn in Hell on the Day of Resurrection, the day when people are judged for their deeds and recompensed accordingly. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
