## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki, from Jabir ibn Abdullah, that the Messenger of God forbade eating the meat of sacrifices after three days, then said: eat, give in charity, provide for the journey, and store. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.622}
