## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And a man came from the far end of Medina running, saying, 'O Moses, indeed the crowds are conspiring against you to kill you, so go out; indeed, I am among you sincere advisors.'" It is mentioned that the Israelite heard it, so he revealed it and informed the people of the slain. Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and ordered his execution. When he ordered his killing, Moses came to inform him of what Pharaoh had ordered him to do in his matter, and advised him to leave Egypt, Pharaoh's land, and his people. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
