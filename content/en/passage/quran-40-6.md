## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And thus the word of your Lord has come true against those who disbelieved; they are the companions of the Fire": Just as it is true against the nations whose messengers denied the stories that were narrated against you, O Muhammad, My punishment came upon them by denying their messengers and arguing with them falsehood, so too is the word of your Lord obligatory upon those who disbelieve in Allah among your people, who dispute about the signs of Allah. And His saying: "They are the companions of the Fire"—the Arabs differed regarding the place of the phrase "they." Some grammarians of Basra said: The meaning is: the word of your Lord has been confirmed upon those who disbelieved; they are the companions of the Fire, meaning because they are, or because they are, and not that they are in the object case, not like your saying: I have realized that if that were so, I would also have been true, because they are. Others would say: "inam" instead of the word, as if the word truly confirmed that they are the companions of the Fire.
