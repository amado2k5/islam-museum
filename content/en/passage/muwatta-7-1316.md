## Summary
It reached Yahya ibn Yahya from Malik that Malik said that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz used to say that a horse has two shares and a man one share; Malik said: I have always heard that. The editor notes that this text is preserved as a hadith from Ibn Umar in the two Sahihs. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.588}
