## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Rather, their hearts are in a cloud of this, and they have deeds other than that, they are acting upon it": What is the matter, as these polytheists think, that We provided them with wealth and children with good, so that We would bring it to them and be pleased with them, but their hearts were blinded by this Qur'an. And he meant by 'in the darkness' what overwhelmed their hearts and covered them from understanding what Allah has deposited in His Book of admonitions, lessons, and proofs. And by His saying: 'Who is this' from the Qur'an. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-Rabi'.
