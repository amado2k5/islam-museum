## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, it is We who give life to the dead and records what they have presented, their traces, and everything We have enumerated in a clear Imam": "Indeed, We bring the dead to life from Our creation, and We record what they have put forth in this world, good and bad, good deeds and bad deeds." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Samak, and others.
