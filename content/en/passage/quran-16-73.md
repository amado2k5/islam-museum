## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 73 to 74 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And they worship besides Allah that which has no provision for them from the heavens and the earth, and they are not able to do anything": These polytheists worship besides Allah idols that have no provision from the heavens, because they are unable to send down a drop of it to revive the dead of the earth. "And the earth" means: Nor do you have for them any provision from the earth, for it is unable to bring forth any of its plants and fruits for them, nor anything that Allah mentioned in this verse that He bestowed upon them. "And they cannot do so" means: and their idols do not possess anything in the heavens or the earth; rather, they and everything in the heavens and the earth belong to Allah dominion. "And they are not able" means: and they have no power over anything. And His saying: "So do not set up examples for Allah" means: Do not imitate examples for Allah, nor imitate likes to Him, for He has no likeness or likeness. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
