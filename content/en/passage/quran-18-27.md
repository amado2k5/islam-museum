## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And recite what has been revealed to you of the Book of your Lord; there is no alteration of its words, and you will not find anyone besides Him challenged." This is explained: 'And follow, O Muhammad, what has been revealed to you of this Book of your Lord, and do not abandon its recitation, and follow what is in it of Allah's commands and prohibitions, and act according to what is lawful and unlawful, for you will be among the doomed. For whoever opposes it and abandons following it on the Day of Resurrection will go to Hell. 'There will be no change of its words.' It means: there is no one who changes what He promised you with His words, which He revealed to you, the people of His sins and those who act contrary to this Book which We have revealed to you. And His saying: "And you will not find anyone challenged besides Him" means: And you, O Muhammad, have not recited what has been revealed to you from the Book of your Lord, so follow it and follow it. So you will receive the promise of God in which He has promised the opposing its limits. You will find no refuge besides Allah to turn to or to turn away from, because God's power surrounds you and all His creation; none of them can escape from a matter they intend. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Sufyan.
