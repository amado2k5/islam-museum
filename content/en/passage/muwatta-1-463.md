## Summary
A report that Malik transmits as a mursal from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, in the chapter on practice in the congregational prayer: the Prophet ordered people to make some of their prayer in their houses. The editor notes that it is a mursal in the Muwatta for all its transmitters, and that al-Bukhari and Muslim (2/187) transmitted it raised from Nafi' from Ibn Umar. He refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.238}
