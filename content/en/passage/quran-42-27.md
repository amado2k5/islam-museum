## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse, "And if Allah were to extend provision for His servants, they would have transgressed in the land, but He sends it down as He wills. Indeed, He is All-Aware and Seeing of His servants": It is mentioned that this verse was revealed for a group of poor Muslims who desired the vastness of the world and wealth. He, the Exalted, said: If Allah had expanded provision for His servants, and had expanded and abundant it among them, they would have transgressed, so they exceeded the limit set by Allah for them to something other than what He had limited for them in His land by riding on the earth, but He sends down their provision in proportion to their sufficiency as He wills. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
