## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 23 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Did We not give him two eyes?” as meaning: Did We not give this person who said, “I have squandered wealth in abundance,” two eyes with which to see God’s proofs against him, a tongue with which to express what he wants, and two lips—a blessing from Us upon him? And Qatadah said regarding the verse, “Did We not give him two eyes, a tongue, and two lips?” that these are manifest blessings from God, by which He reminds you so that you may be grateful. He then quotes in this section statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ikrimah, Simak, and others.
