## Summary
In the Book of Prayer: the long hadith of Malik ibn Sa'sa'a on the Night Journey and the Ascension. It includes the second opening of the chest at the House and the washing of the heart with Zamzam water before the ascent, then riding al-Buraq, passing the prophets in the heavens, and the obligation of prayer. It is one of the longest and best-known descriptions of the journey in the Sahih. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
