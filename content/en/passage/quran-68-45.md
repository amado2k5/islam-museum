## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 45 as one section. He explains His saying, “And I will grant them respite. Indeed, My plan is firm”: I will extend their lives for a period of time, and that is a period of time for their disbelief and rebellion against God, so that God’s proofs against them may be complete. “Indeed, My plan is firm” means: My plan against the people of disbelief is strong and severe.
