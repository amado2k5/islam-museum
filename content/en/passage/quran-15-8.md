## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that the reciters differed regarding the reading; the people of Medina and Basra read with a fatha on the 'ta', while most people of Kufa read with a 'nun' and a shaddd. He believes that the three readings are similar in meaning, and he prefers that the reciter should not deviate from the two readings known to the general public. He interprets the truth as a letter to the messengers and the punishment for anyone whom God intends to torture, and he quotes Mujahid as saying.
