**Commentary on verse 108 (p. 220)**

On [[passage/quran-10-108|10:108]] al-Tabari says God tells His prophet: say, Muhammad, to the people, 'people, the truth has come to you from your Lord', meaning the Book of God, which has the explanation of all that people need in their religion; 'whoever is guided' means whoever is upright, takes the way of truth and affirms the explanation that came from God, 'is guided only for himself', meaning he stays upright on guidance and takes the straight path for himself, seeking good for himself by this and no one else; 'and whoever goes astray' means whoever turns from the truth that came to him from God and opposes His religion and what He sent Muhammad and the Book He sent down on him with, 'goes astray only against it', meaning his straying brings harm only on himself and not another, since no other is taken for it, and he brings ruin by his straying on none but himself, and no bearer bears another's burden, as in [[passage/quran-6-164|6:164]]. 'I am not a guardian over you' means I am not set over putting you right; your matter is with God, who puts right whom He wills of you, and I am only a messenger who conveys what I was sent to you with. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'guidance', 'straying' and 'guardian'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 220}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
