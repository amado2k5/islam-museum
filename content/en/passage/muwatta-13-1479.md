## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from a trustworthy person he had, who heard Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab say: Umar ibn al-Khattab refused to let any of the non-Arabs inherit, except one born among the Arabs. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.23}
