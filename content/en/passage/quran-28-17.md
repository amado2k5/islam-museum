## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 17 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "My Lord, by what You have bestowed upon me, I will never be a supporter for the criminals": Moses said, "My Lord, by Your favor upon me by Your pardon for killing this soul," "I will never be a supporter for the criminals," meaning the polytheists, as if he swore by that. It was mentioned that this is in Abdullah's recitation: "So do not make me a support for the criminals," as if he called upon his Lord based on this reading, saying: O Allah, I will not be a supporter for the criminals. He was not exempted when he said, "I will never be a support for the criminals," so he was tested. Qatadah used to say about this: And from Qatadah, 'I will never be a supporter for the criminals,' meaning: I will never see after that an oppressor at dawn. He said: Rarely did a man say this except that he was tested. He said: So he was tested as you hear.
