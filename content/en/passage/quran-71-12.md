## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 14 as a single section. He interprets the words, “And He will provide you with wealth and children, and make for you gardens and make for you rivers,” as follows: “And He will provide you with wealth and children,” meaning, “Your Lord will also give you wealth and children, increasing them for you and adding to what you already possess. And He will make for you gardens,” meaning, “He will provide you with orchards and make for you rivers from which you will irrigate your gardens and farms.” Noah said this to them because, as mentioned, they were a people who loved wealth and children. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
