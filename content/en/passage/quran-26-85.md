## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 89 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And make me among the inheritors of the Garden of Bliss" means: Abraham, when he says: "And make me among the inheritors of the Garden of Bliss," "O Lord, make me an inheritor from the dwellings of those who perish among Your enemies who associate partners with You from Paradise, and grant me a place in that." "And forgive my father," meaning: "Forgive my father for associating with You, and do not punish him for it." "Indeed, he was among the misguided," meaning: He was among those who strayed from the path of guidance, so he disbelieved in You.
