**Commentary on verse 73 (pp. 399-400)**

On [[passage/quran-11-73|11:73]] al-Tabari says: 'they said, do you wonder at God's command?' means the messengers said to her, do you wonder at something God ordered to be and a decree He decreed for you and your husband; 'the mercy of God and His blessings be upon you, people of the house' means God's mercy and happiness be yours, people of the house of Abraham, the alif and lam standing in place of the construct form; 'He is Praiseworthy, Glorious' means God is praised for His favour to you in the blessings He bestowed on you and on the rest of His creation, 'Glorious' meaning possessed of glory, praise and noble commendation. One says 'the man became glorious' of becoming so, and when you mean you praised him you say 'majjadtuhu tamjidan'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 400}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
