## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So when they board the ark, they call upon Allah, sincerely devoting religion to Him." But when He delivers them to the land, they associate partners with Him.' So when these polytheists board the ship at sea and fear drowning and destruction therein, "They call upon Allah, sincere to Him in religion," meaning: They were sincere to Allah in the time of hardship that monotheism befell them, devoted obedience to Him, submitted to Him in servitude, and did not seek help from their gods or rivals, but from Allah who created them. "When He saved them to the land," He says: When He saved them from what they were in and gave them peace, they came to righteousness, and they associate with Allah in their worship, calling gods and idols lords with Him. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
