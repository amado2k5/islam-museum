**Commentary on verse 2 (p. 385)**

Then al-Tabari says on "the believers are only those whose hearts tremble when God is mentioned and whose faith grows when His verses are recited to them, and who trust in their Lord": the believer is not one who goes against God and His Messenger and leaves following what He sent down in His book of its limits and obligations and submitting to His rule, but the believer is the one whose heart trembles when God is mentioned and who yields to His command and submits to the mention of Him in fear of Him and dread of His punishment, and when the verses of His book are recited to him he trusts them and is sure they are from God, and so adds, by his trust in that, a further trust to the trust he already had in what had reached him from Him before, and that is the increase in faith from what is recited to them of God's verses, "and they trust in their Lord" meaning they are sure in God that His decree about them goes forward, so they hope for none other and fear none besides. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "recitation", "increase of faith" and "trust". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 385}
