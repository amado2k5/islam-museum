**Commentary on verse 2 (pp. 385-387)**

Then al-Tabari says on "the believers are only those whose hearts tremble when God is mentioned and whose faith grows when His verses are recited to them, and who trust in their Lord": the believer is not one who goes against God and His Messenger and leaves following what He sent down in His book of its limits and obligations and submitting to His rule, but the believer is the one whose heart trembles when God is mentioned and who yields to His command and submits to the mention of Him in fear of Him and dread of His punishment, and when the verses of His book are recited to him he trusts them and is sure they are from God, and so adds, by his trust in that, a further trust to the trust he already had in what had reached him from Him before, and that is the increase in faith from what is recited to them of God's verses, "and they trust in their Lord" meaning they are sure in God that His decree about them goes forward, so they hope for none other and fear none besides. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "recitation", "increase of faith" and "trust". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 385}

The interpreters spoke as al-Tabari did. Report 15684, from Ibn Abbas: "the believers are only those whose hearts tremble when God is mentioned" he said, the hypocrites, into whose hearts nothing of the mention of God enters when they perform His obligations, who believe in none of God's verses, do not trust in God, do not pray when they are away and do not pay the zakat on their wealth; so God told that they are not believers and then described the believers, "whose hearts tremble" so they performed His obligations, "whose faith grows" meaning their trust, "and who trust in their Lord" meaning they hope for no other. Reports 15685, 15688 and 15689, from Mujahid: "trembled" means they feared; reports 15686 and 15687, from al-Suddi: when God is mentioned at a thing his heart trembles; report 15690, from al-Suddi: it is the man who means to do wrong or intends a sin and so draws back from it; report 15691, from Abu al-Darda: the trembling in the heart is like the burning of a dry palm frond, do you not find a shiver in it? he said yes; he said when you find that in the heart, call on God, for supplication takes it away; report 15692, from Qatada: in dread of God, in trembling and fear. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 386} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 387}
As for "it increases their faith", he has given Ibn Abbas's saying on it, and others said: report 15693, from al-Rabi: fear of God; report 15694, from Qatada: this is the description of the people of faith, so their description and character is confirmed. The editor explains "sa'fa" as the dry leaf of the palm frond. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 387}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
