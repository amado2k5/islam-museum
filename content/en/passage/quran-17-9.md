## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 10 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Indeed, this Qur'an guides to that which is most upright and gives good tidings to the believers who do righteous deeds that they will have a great reward": This Qur'an which We revealed to our Prophet Muhammad guides and guides those who are guided by it, "for that which is most upright," meaning: the way which is more upright than other paths, and that is the religion of Allah with which He sent His prophets, which is Islam. He, Glorified and Exalted, says: This Qur'an guides the servants of Allah who are guided by it to the right path from which all the people of the faiths who deny it have gone astray, as Ibn Zayd said regarding the saying: "Indeed, this Qur'an guides to that which is more upright," he said: to what is more correct: it is the correct and the truth. He said: The opposite is falsehood. He recited the saying of Allah Almighty: "In them are valuable books," he said: In it is the truth, there is no crookedness in it. And He recited: "And We did not assign for it any crooked, upright" meaning: upright and upright. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd and Ibn Jurayj.
