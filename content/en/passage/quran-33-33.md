## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 32–33. God addresses the wives of the Prophet by saying that they are not like any other woman if they fear God. They should not be submissive in speech lest the one in whose heart is a disease covet, and they should speak what is right, be married in their homes, not display themselves in the display of the first pre-Islamic era, establish prayer, give zakat, and slander God and His Messenger. Al-Tabari mentions the difference in reading in "and Quran."
