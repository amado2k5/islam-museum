## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Ali ibn Husayn ibn Ali ibn Abi Talib, from Umar ibn Uthman ibn Affan, from Usama ibn Zayd, that the Messenger of God said: a Muslim does not inherit from an unbeliever. The editor notes that al-Tirmidhi thought the correct name was Amr ibn Uthman, and Ibn Abd al-Barr said the established name from Malik is Umar ibn Uthman, as Yahya narrated, and that agreement is more deserving of acceptance. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.21}
