## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that he saw Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr enter ihram for the lesser pilgrimage from al-Tanim, then saw him run around the House for the three rounds. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1285), Suwayd (542) and al-Shaybani (456) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.490}
