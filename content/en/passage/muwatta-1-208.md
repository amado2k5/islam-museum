## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Ubayd Allah ibn Abdullah ibn Utba from Ibn Abbas, in the chapter on recitation in the sunset and night prayers: his mother Umm al-Fadl bint al-Harith heard him recite the surah of al-Mursalat, reminded him of this recitation, and said it was the last thing she heard the Prophet recite in the sunset prayer. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.128} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.129}
