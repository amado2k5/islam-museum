## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "My son, if there is even an atom of mustard seed and it is placed on a rock, or in the heavens, or on the earth, Allah will bring it indeed. Indeed, Allah is Subtle and Acquainted": The Arabs differed regarding the meaning of the 'ha' and the alif in the phrase 'inna'. Some grammarians from Basra said: This is a metaphor for sin and disobedience. The meaning of the statement is: O my son, sin is if it is the weight of a mustard seed, or sin. Some grammarians of Kufa said: This is the 'a' (a 'hamad'). In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Al-A'mash.
