## Summary
The first saying in the chapter on the imam going out on the festival day and waiting for the sermon. Yahya says Malik said: the practice in the times of breaking the fast and sacrifice, about which there is no disagreement among us, is that the imam goes out from his house a distance that reaches his prayer ground, and the prayer time has come. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256}
