## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And from everything We created pairs that perhaps you will remember": And We created from everything pairs, leaving Our first creation self-sufficient as the meaning of the statement on them. There is disagreement about the meaning of "We created pairs," with some saying: It means: And from everything We created two different types, such as misery, happiness, guidance, misguidance, and the like. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
