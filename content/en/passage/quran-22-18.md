## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah prostrates to Him whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth, the sun, the moon, the stars, the mountains, the trees, the animals, and many people, and much is due to punishment? And whoever Allah humiliates - he has no honor. Indeed, Allah does what He wills." Whoever Allah humbles among His creation and makes him suffer, "He has no honored one" with happiness because all matters are in the hands of Allah. He grants obedience to whomever He wills, forsakes whom He wills, makes whom He wills miserable, and makes those He loves happy. And His saying: "Indeed, Allah does whatever He wills," the Exalted means: Indeed, Allah does in His creation whatever He wills, insulting whom He intends and honoring whoever He desires His honor, because creation is His creation and the command is His command. "He is not questioned about what He does, and they are questioned." It has been mentioned that some of them recited it: "So he has no honor," meaning: there is no honor. This is a reading I do not permit reading due to the consensus of the argument among the reciters against it.
