## Summary
Malik transmits from Dawud ibn al-Husayn, from Abu Ghatafan ibn Tarif al-Murri, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: whoever gives a gift for the sake of kinship or as a charity, may not take it back; whoever gives a gift that he thought was given for a return, may take it back if he is not satisfied with it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.300}
