## Summary
Verse 25 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 25 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "He said: in it you will live, in it you will die, and from it you will be brought out", God says that God said to those He sent down from His heavens to His earth, in it you will live, that is, on earth you will be for the days of your lives, "in it you will die" means on earth your death will be, and "from it you will be brought out" means from the earth your Lord will bring you out and gather you to Him alive for the resurrection. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 360}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
