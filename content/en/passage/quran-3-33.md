## Verse 33 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, pp. 326-327)

**The chosen.** Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-33|3:33]]) says God chose Adam and Noah for their religion, and chose the house of Abraham and the house of Imran for the religion they followed, because they were people of submission; so He informed us that He chose the religion of those named over other religions that differed, and by the two houses He means the believers, since, as he explained earlier, a man's 'house' are his followers, his people and those of his religion. Al-Muthanna, from Abd Allah ibn Salih, from Mu'awiya, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas (6851) says they are the believers of the house of Abraham, the house of Imran, the house of Yasin and the house of Muhammad, and he cites [[passage/quran-3-68|3:68]] that the nearest of people to Abraham are those who followed him, the believers. Bishr, from Yazid, from Sa'id, from Qatada (6852) says two men, both prophets, God chose; and Qatada in Abd al-Razzaq's report (6853) says God mentioned two righteous houses and two righteous men and favoured them, Muhammad being of the house of Abraham. Al-Hasan (6854) says God favoured them above all people by prophethood; they were the pure prophets chosen for their Lord. The editor refers to earlier discussions of the verb 'chose' and notes that the printed text has a different word from al-Durr al-Manthur, though the manuscript is clear and matches the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 327}
