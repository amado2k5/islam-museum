## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 54 to 56 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Do they think that We only provide them with wealth and children": Do they think those parties who have divided their religion as a burden, that what We give them in the immediate worldly matter of wealth and children "We hasten to them" means: We race for them in the blessings of the Hereafter and hasten to them in it? And "what" in the phrase "We only provide them with it" is accusative, because it means "the one." "Rather, they do not perceive," Allah says, denying them: 'That is not so. Rather, they do not know that my provision to them with what He has provided them with is merely dictation and luring them.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
