## Summary
Malik says it is not proper to sell females and except what is in their bellies. That is, a man says to another: the price of my productive she-goat is three dinars, and it is yours for two dinars, and what is in her belly is mine. This is disliked, because it is uncertainty and risk. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.195}
