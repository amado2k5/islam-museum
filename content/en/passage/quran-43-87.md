## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 87 to 88 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And if you ask them who created them, they will say, 'Allah, then how are they deceived?": And if you asked, O Muhammad, these polytheists of Allah from your people: Who created them? They would say: Allah created us, so how can they deceive? So what reason do they turn away from worshiping the One who created them, and forbid the truth from being fulfilled in His worship? And His saying: "And say, O Lord, indeed these are a people who do not believe," reciters differed in the reading of the phrase "and said" as the general recitation of the phrase "and said" in the accusative case. And if it is read as such, it has two interpretations: one is the conjunction on His saying: "Or do they think that We do not hear their secrets and private conversations," and we hear his saying, O Lord. The second: that a witness is hidden from him, so its meaning is at that time: He said: "O Lord, these are people who do not believe," and Muhammad complained about his complaint to his Lord. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Mujahid and Qatadah.
