## Summary
Yahya said: Malik was asked about a man who gave charity and found it being sold in the hands of someone other than the one he gave it to. Would he buy it back? He said: leaving it is more beloved to me. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.380}
