**Commentary on verse 74 (p. 154)**

On [[passage/quran-10-74|10:74]] al-Tabari says: then, after Noah, We sent messengers to their peoples, and they came to them with clear proofs and evidences of their truthfulness, that they were God's messengers and that what they called them to was true. 'But they would not believe in what they had denied before' means they would not believe what their messengers brought, in what the people of Noah and the earlier nations before them had denied. 'Thus We seal the hearts of the transgressors' means as We sealed the hearts of those, so that they accepted no advice from God's prophets and did not answer their call to their Lord, because of the sins they had committed and the wrongs they had earned, so We seal the heart of whoever transgresses against his Lord, passing beyond what he was commanded of His oneness and opposing what the messengers called them to of obedience to Him, as punishment for their disobedience, among these later peoples. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'sealing' and 'transgression'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 154}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
