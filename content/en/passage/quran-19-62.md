## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They do not hear idle talk therein except peace, and they will receive their provision therein morning and evening": those who enter Paradise do not hear idle talk, which is guidance and falsehood in speech and speech "except peace." This is an isolated exception, meaning: but they hear peace, which is the greeting of the angels to them. And His saying: "And for them is their provision in the morning and evening," means: They will have their food and whatever they desire of restaurants and drinks according to the time of morning and evening of the days of this world. It only means that what is between their lunch and their dinner in Paradise is as much as the lunch and dinner of one of us in this world, and likewise between dinner and lunch, because there is neither night nor day in Paradise. This is like His saying: "He created the earth in two days" and "He created the heavens and the earth in six days" means from the days of this world.
