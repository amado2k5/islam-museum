## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abu al-Zinad, that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab and Sulayman ibn Yasar forbid a man to sell wheat for gold on credit and then buy dates with that gold before taking possession of the gold. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.170}
