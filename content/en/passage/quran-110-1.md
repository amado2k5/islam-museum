## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 3 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "When the victory of Allah and the conquest comes": when the help of Allah comes to you, O Muhammad, over your people from Quraysh. The conquest of Mecca means the conquest of Mecca "and you saw the people" from the tribes of the Arabs and its tribes, the people of Yemen among them, and the tribes of Nizar "entering the religion of Allah in groups," meaning in the religion of Allah with which He sent you, and in obedience to which He called them "crowds," meaning groups, groups upon groups. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
