## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he used to say: the full blood-money is due for the two lips; if the lower lip is cut off, two-thirds of the blood-money is due. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.426}
