## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "and they say, 'When is this promise, if you are truthful?'" These polytheists who deny God's promise and resurrection after death say, "When is this promise?" meaning the promise of the Hour "if you are truthful," O people. This is their saying to the believers in God and His Messenger.
