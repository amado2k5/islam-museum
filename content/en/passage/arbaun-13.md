## Summary
Hadith of Anas ibn Malik: "None of you believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself". Ibn Rajab explains that denying faith means denying that a person has reached its full reality. He notes the scholars' disagreement on whether one who commits major sins is called a believer with imperfect faith. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
