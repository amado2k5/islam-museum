## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 95 to 96 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We have sufficed you for the mockers": We have sufficed you for the mockers, O Muhammad, who mock and mock you. So proclaim the command of Allah and fear nothing except Allah, for Allah will suffice you from those who harm and offend you as He has sufficed the mockers. The leaders of the mockers were a well-known people from Quraysh. Their names were mentioned: Muhammad narrated to me, he said: The great mockers were, as Yazid ibn Ruman told me from Urwah ibn al-Zubayr, five of his people, who were of great stature and honor among their people from Banu Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza ibn Qusay: al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib Abu Zam'a. As far as I have heard, the Messenger of Allah prayed against him because of the harm and mockery he received, saying: "O Allah, blind his sight and burden his son." From Banu Zuhrah: al-Aswad ibn Abd Yaghut ibn Wahb ibn Abd Manaf ibn Zuhrah. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Ishaq.
