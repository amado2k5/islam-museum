**Commentary on verse 166: God's and the angels' witness to Muhammad's prophethood (pp. 409-410)**

Al-Tabari says 'but God bears witness to what He sent down to you' means that if the Jews who asked you to send down a book deny it, they lie; God bears witness to the sending down to you of what He sent down in His book and revelation, knowing that you are His chosen one among creatures and His elect among servants, and His angels bear witness to it. So do not be grieved by those who deny you, and God suffices as witness to your truth. It is said the verse was revealed about Jews whom the Prophet called to follow him, telling them they knew the truth of his prophethood, and they denied it. Report 10850, from Ibn Abbas through Sa'id ibn Jubayr or Ikrima: a group of Jews came to the Prophet, who said: I know you know I am God's messenger; they said: we do not know that. God revealed the verse. Report 10851 gives the same. Report 10852, from Qatada: witnesses by God, not accused. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 409}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
