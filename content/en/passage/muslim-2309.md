## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on good character, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim: ten years of service without a rebuke or a word of impatience. It is the testimony of the closest servant on the Prophet's daily character. {cite:sahih-muslim}
