## Summary
Verse 99 of [[passage/quran-5|Surat al-Ma'ida]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 99 of 120 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari explains "the messenger has only to convey, and God knows what you show and what you hide": this is a threat and warning from God to His servants. He says: our messenger whom We sent to you, people, has no duty, by warning you of Our punishment before a severe torment and by giving you the excuse that cuts off your pleas, except to convey Our message to you; to Us then belongs the reward for obedience and upon Us the punishment for disobedience. "And God knows what you show and what you hide": the obedient among you, who accept Our message and act on what We commanded him, are not hidden from Us, nor the disobedient who refuses Our message and leaves off acting on what We commanded, since We know what each of you does, whether he shows it with his limbs or speaks it with his tongue, and "what you hide" means what you conceal within yourselves of faith and disbelief, or certainty, doubt and hypocrisy. One to whom nothing of what hearts conceal, or of the outward deeds of souls, in the heavens and on the earth is hidden, and in whose hand are reward and punishment, is fit to be feared and obeyed and not disobeyed. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 95}
