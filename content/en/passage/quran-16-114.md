## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So eat from what Allah has provided for you, lawful and good, and be grateful for the favor of Allah if it is Him that you worship": So eat, O people, of the livestock that Allah has provided you of the livestock that He has made lawful and good for you, pure and unlawful, not forbidden to you. "And be grateful for the favor of Allah" He means: and thank Allah for the blessings He has bestowed upon you in permitting what He has made lawful for you, and for other of His blessings. "If you worship Him," he means: if you worship Allah, then obey Him in what He commands and forbids you. Some of them used to say: What I meant by His saying: "So eat from what Allah has provided for you, lawful and good," is food that the Messenger of Allah sent to the polytheists among his people during years of drought and drought as a gentle punishment for them. Allah said to the polytheists: Eat from what Allah has provided you from what He has sent to you, lawful and good.
