## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, whatever reward I ask of you, it is yours. Indeed, my reward is only upon Allah, and He is a Witness over all things." Say, O Muhammad, to your people who deny you what you have brought them from your Lord: I do not ask you for the punishment of Allah upon your warnings, and to warn you with His severity. My advice to you in my affair is to believe in Allah and act in obedience to Him, for He is for you; I have no need for it. The meaning of the speech is: Say to them: I did not ask you about this so that you would accuse me, and you might think that I only invited you to follow me for money I took from you. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
