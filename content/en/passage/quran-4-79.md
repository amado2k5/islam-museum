## Summary
Verse 79 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 79 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (p. 558, note 1).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: what reaches you, Muhammad, of ease, favour, health and safety is from God's bounty to you, given out of kindness; and what reaches you of hardship, strain, harm and unpleasantness is from a sin you incurred it by and your self earned. The editor refers to earlier interpretations of the words for good and ill (page numbers garbled). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 558}

**Reports 9968-9970 (p. 558).** Muhammad b. al-Husayn (9968, from Ahmad b. al-Mufaddal, from Asbat) reports from al-Suddi that the last clause means from your sin. Bishr b. Mu'adh (9969, from Yazid, from Sa'id) reports from Qatada that it is a punishment for your sin, son of Adam, and that it was mentioned to them that God's Prophet used to say no man is struck by a scratch from a twig, a stumble of the foot or a twitch of a vein except through a sin, and what God pardons is more (given as transmitted). Al-Muthanna (9970, from Abdullah, from Mu'awiya, from Ali b. Abi Talha) reports from Ibn Abbas that the good is what God opened to him on the day of Badr and the booty and conquest that reached him, and the ill is what befell him on the day of Uhud, when his face was cut and his front tooth broken. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 558}

**Reports 9971-9977 (p. 559, note 1).** Al-Muthanna (9971, from Ishaq, from Abd al-Razzaq, from Ma'mar) reports from Qatada that it is because of your sin, and then he said everything is from God, blessings and calamities. Al-Muthanna (9972, from Ishaq, from Abd al-Rahman b. Sa'd and Ibn Abi Ja'far, from Abu Ja'far) reports from al-Rabi', from Abu al-Aliya, that this concerns good and ill; the editor refers to his note on the two previous reports. Al-Qasim (9973, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj, from Abu Ja'far, from al-Rabi') reports the same from Abu al-Aliya. Al-Qasim (9974, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj) reports from Ibn Jurayj that it is a punishment for your sin. Yunus (9975, from Ibn Wahb) reports from Ibn Zayd that it is for your sin, as God addressed the people of Uhud when they asked where this calamity came from, showing them it came from themselves, that is from their sins ([[passage/quran-3-165|3:165]]). Yunus (9976, from Sufyan, from Isma'il b. Abi Khalid) reports from Abu Salih that it is for your sin and that God decreed it. Muhammad b. Bashshar (9977, from Yahya, from Sufyan, from Isma'il b. Abi Khalid) reports from Abu Salih that it is God who decreed it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 559}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
