## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And let Allah know those who have believed, and let He teach the hypocrites": So that Allah may teach the friends of Allah and His party the people of faith in Allah from among you, O people, and let the hypocrites among you know so that each of you may distinguish from the other, by manifesting that from you through trials, trials, and tests, and by hastening among you to migrate from the land of polytheism to the land of Islam, and the heaviness of the one who is hesitant among you from it.
