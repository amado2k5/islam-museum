## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi', in the chapter on the general rules of times: Abdullah ibn Umar fainted and lost his mind for a time, and when he woke he did not make up what he had missed of prayer. Malik comments that this is because the time had gone; whoever wakes while the time remains prays. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.45}
