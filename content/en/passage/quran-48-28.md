## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 29 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He it is Who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to make it prevail over all religions, and sufficient is Allah as a witness": He means mentioning Him by saying: "He is the One who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth," which He sent His Messenger Muhammad with clear clarity, "and the religion of truth," which is Islam which He sent, calling His creation to it "to make it prevail over all religion." He says: to nullify all sects so that there would be no religion other than Him. This was the case until Jesus, son of Mary, descended, and the Antichrist was killed. At that time, all religions would be nullified, except for the religion of God who He sent Muhammad with it, and Islam appears on all religions. And His saying: 'And sufficient is Allah as a witness,' He says to His Prophet: 'I testify to you, O Muhammad, your Lord of Himself, that He will reveal the religion He sent you with.' 'And sufficient is Allah as a witness,' meaning: 'And He sufficed you as a witness.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and al-Hasan.
