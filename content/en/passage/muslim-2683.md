## Summary
A report of Ubada ibn al-Samit and Aisha in the Book of Paradise and the description of its bliss, in Sahih Muslim: love of meeting God and dislike of it at the end of the world. It is among the texts on asceticism and the Hereafter. {cite:sahih-muslim}
