## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 89 to 91 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Do they not see that no word will be returned to them and that He will not have power over them of harm or benefit": Allah Almighty reproaches the worshippers of the calf and those who say to him, "This is your god and the God of Moses, so he forgot," blaming them for this, and the foolishness of their dreams for what they did and gained from it: Do they not see that the calf, which they claimed to be their god and the god of Moses, does not speak to them, and if they spoke to it, it would not respond to them, nor could it harm or benefit them? So how could it be that this attribute was not a god? Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "He does not return to them any word" (the calf). He said: Hajjaj narrated to us, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid: "Do they not see that no word will return to them?" he said: The calf. And from Qatadah, Allah said: "Do they not see that the calf they adopted as 'in speech, and it has no power over them to harm or benefit.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
