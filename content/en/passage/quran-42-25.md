## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And He is the One who accepts the repentance of His servants and pardons their sins and knows what you do": And Allah is the One who accepts the repentance of a servant when he returns to the oneness of Allah and obeys Him after his disbelief. "And they pardon sins," meaning: He pardons to punish him for his bad deeds, which are the sins he repented from. "And He knows what you do." Reciters differed in reading this, and the majority of the reciters of Medina and Basra read it as "yaf'lun" with a ya, meaning: He knows what His servants do, and most reciters of Kufa read it as "ta'f'alun" with a 'ta' in the form of the address. In the passage, he quotes from Ibrahim.
