## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Abdallah ibn al-Mughira ibn Abi Burda al-Kinani, that it reached him that the Messenger of God gave to people in their tribes and prayed for them, and left out one tribe. They found in the saddle of a man of them a necklace of carnelian as embezzlement; the Messenger of God came to them and prayed over them as he prays over the dead. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.590}
