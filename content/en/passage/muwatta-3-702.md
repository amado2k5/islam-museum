## Summary
A saying of Malik: likewise, Arab camels and Bactrian camels are gathered together on their owner for charity, because they are all camels. If the Arab camels are more and only one camel is due, it is taken from the Arab camels; if the Bactrian are more, it is taken from them; if equal, from whichever he chooses. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.352}
