## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra and Jabir ibn Abdullah in the Book of Purity of Sahih Muslim: the simile of a river flowing past a door, in which the five prayers wipe out sins. It is one of the best-known similes for prayer. {cite:sahih-muslim}
