## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "When you met him with your tongues and said with your mouths what you had no knowledge of and thought it was insignificant, while it is great with Allah": He touched you in what you caused Aisha a great punishment when you inflicted it with your tongues. And "idh" is related to the connection of His saying "He touched you." By saying: "You receive it," it means you receive the slanderer brought by the group of the people of the slander, so you accept it. Some of you narrate it from others, saying: I received this statement from so-and-so, meaning I took it from him. It is said because the man among them, as mentioned, meets another and says: Or what has reached you such and such from Aisha? So that immorality becomes widespread against her. He mentioned that in my father's recitation: "When you receive him" with two ta's, and the cities read it on it, but they read it as "ta'lqnuhu" with a single ta, because that is the case in their Mushafs. He quotes in the passage from Mujahid.
