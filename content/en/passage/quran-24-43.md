## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Have you not seen that Allah drives clouds and then brings them together and then makes them rubble, and you see the rain emerge from it, and He sends down from the sky from mountains containing hail and strikes with it whom He wills and diverts it from whom He wills, almost a tooth of lightning, that fades away with sight": "Have you not seen, O Muhammad, that Allah drives clouds wherever He wills?" "Then he reconciles between it": he says: then combines the clouds. He added "bayn" to the clouds, but did not mention anything else, and "bayn" is not added except to a group or two, because the plural of 'cloud' means a plural, singular is a cloud, just as a palm tree is plural: palm trees, and a date is a date, similar to the saying: so-and-so sat among the palm trees. And Allah's authorship of the clouds: He combined their scattered parts. And His saying: "Then He makes it rubble" means: then He makes the clouds that He gathers and combines parts of them together, "Rukes," meaning: accumulated on top of others. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dahhak, and Ibn Zayd.
