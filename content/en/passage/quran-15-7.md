## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari mentions that the polytheists said: 'Why don't you bring us angels as witnesses for you to prove the truth of what you say?' He commented on this by saying that the Lord who did what the Prophet said was not able to send an angel with him as proof over the people, a sign of his prophethood, and the truthfulness of his statement.
