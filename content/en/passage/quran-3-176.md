## Summary
Verse 176 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 176 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 176 (pp. 418-419, reports 8262-8264).** Al-Tabari says: do not let it grieve you, Muhammad, that those who rush into denial, turning back on their heels, from the hypocrites, disbelieve; they will do God no harm at all by rushing, and just as their rushing, were it toward faith, would not benefit Him, so their rushing toward disbelief does not harm Him. The notes refer to earlier comment on the verb "to hasten" (the numbers are garbled) and say that the printed text and the manuscript both lack the conjunction in the phrase "just as", which should be restored. Mujahid (8262), through Muhammad ibn 'Amr from Abu 'Asim from 'Isa from Ibn Abi Najih: they are the hypocrites (the note: the printed text has "they are the hypocrites", while the manuscript can be read "so they are the hypocrites", which would be better). Ibn Ishaq (8263), through Ibn Humayd from Salama: the hypocrites; the note gives Ibn Hisham (3: 128) as completing the reports ending at 8259. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 418} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 419}

He then explains the second part: God wills that these have no share in the reward of the Hereafter, which is why He abandoned them and they rushed into denial; then He tells that, besides being deprived of the reward of the Hereafter, they have a great punishment, the punishment of the Fire. Ibn Ishaq (8264), through Ibn Humayd from Salama, says it means that He voids their deeds; the note says this is not in Ibn Hisham. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 419}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 419}
