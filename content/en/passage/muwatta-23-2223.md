## Summary
He said: I also heard Malik say of a man who is present at fighting: when he advances in the line for battle, he may not dispose of any of his property except in a third; he is like the pregnant woman and the sick person who is feared for, while he is in that state. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.314}
