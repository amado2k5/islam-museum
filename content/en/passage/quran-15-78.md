## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 78 and 79 in one passage: "And indeed, the people of the Ike are wrongdoers." The people of Al-Ghaydah were unjust, meaning disbelievers in God. The Ike is the gathered tree. He cites the poetry of Umayyah in the 'Iik,' and it is narrated that they are the people of Shu'ayb, the people of Ghaydah, whose trees are generally everlasting. Shu'ayb sent to them and to the people of Midian, sending to two nations afflicted with various punishments: the people of Midian, who were seized by the cry, and the people of the Ike, upon whom the heat prevailed for seven days, then God sent upon them a cloud beneath which they sought life, and He sent upon them a fire, which was overwhelmed and consumed them. That is the punishment of the Day of the Canopy. "So take revenge on them": from the darkness of the people of the Ince. "And indeed, they are with a clear leader": And the city of the people of the Ike and the city of the people of Lot are penguins they follow on their journeys and are guided by, showing to those who follow him his uprightness. He made the path an imam because he leads and follows.
