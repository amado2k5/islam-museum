## Summary
It reached Malik that Abdullah ibn Umar was asked: does anyone fast for anyone, or pray for anyone? He said: no one fasts for anyone, and no one prays for anyone. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (835). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.407}
