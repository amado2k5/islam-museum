## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 55 and 56 in one passage: Daif Ibrahim said, "We have given you glad tidings of the truth, so do not be among the despairing," meaning We have given you the truth of certainty and knowledge from us that God has granted you a knowledgeable boy, so do not be among those who despair of God's bounty and despair of Him. He presents the readings: The general recitations of the cities are "the despairing" with the alif, and it was narrated from Yahya ibn Wathab that "the two mountains." The correct view is that of the reciters of the regions due to the consensus of the argument on it. In 'And man yaqnat,' most of the reciters of Medina and Kufa read it with a fatha on the nun, except for al-A'mash and al-Kisai, who then kasra it. Al-A'mash read 'quntu' with a kasra on the nun in another place, and al-Kisai and Abu Amr ibn al-'ala with fatḥa; al-Tabari prefers reading 'qantawa' with a fatḥa and 'yaquntu' with a kasra, due to the consensus of the evidence on fatḥa in qunt. Because if the 'faʿl' is 'fatha' and not a vowel letter, it is in yaf'āl with kasra or damma, not fatḥa.
