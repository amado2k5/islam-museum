## Summary
Verse 41 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 41 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "They will have a bed of Hell, and above them coverings; thus We repay the wrongdoers", God says that for these who denied Our signs and were arrogant about them there is "a bed of Hell", which is what they lay out to sit and lie on, like the bedding that is spread and the mat that is laid out, "and above them coverings", the plural of "ghashiya", which is what covers them and veils them from above. The sense is: they have from Hell a bed beneath them of bedding and above them from it coverings, and they are between these. The commentators said the like: he gives (14655) from Muhammad ibn Ka'b that "the bed" is the bedding and the coverings are the quilts; (14656) from al-Dahhak that "the bed" is the bedding and "coverings" the quilts; and (14657) from al-Suddi that "the bed" is like a mattress and "the coverings" cover them from above. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 436}

As for "thus We repay the wrongdoers", he says: thus We reward and requite whoever wronged himself and so earned for it of God's wrath what it cannot bear, by his disbelief in his Lord and his denying His prophets. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 436}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
