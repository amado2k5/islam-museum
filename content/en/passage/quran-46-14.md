## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He interprets His saying, "Those are the companions of Paradise, abiding therein eternally as a reward for what they used to do": those who said this and remained steadfast, the people of Paradise and its inhabitants "abiding therein," meaning they will abide therein forever, meaning "as a reward for what they used to do," meaning: a reward from Us for them; We have given them that for their righteous deeds which they used to perform in this world.
