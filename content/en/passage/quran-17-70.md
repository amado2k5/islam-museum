## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We have certainly honored the children of Adam and carried them on land and sea, and provided them with good things and preferred them over many of those We created." He explains: "And We have certainly honored the children of Adam" by subjugating them over other creatures and subjugating all creation for them. "And We carried them on land" on the backs of animals and boats, "and" in the "sea" in the ships that We subjected to them, "and provided them with good things," meaning among the good things of restaurants and drinks, which are their lawful and delicious things. "And We preferred them over many of them." We were created by preference." He mentioned that this enables them to work with their own hands, take food and drinks with them, and bring them to their mouths, which is not possible for other creatures, as Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj regarding the phrase "And We have certainly honored the children of Adam..." The verse, he said: "And We preferred them" is in the hands with which he eats and works with them, and what other than humans eats otherwise. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Jurayj and Zayd ibn Aslam.
