## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "But if they turn away, then say, 'I will give you permission for evil, and if I know near or far, what you are promised.'" If these polytheists, O Muhammad, turn away from acknowledging faith that they have no god but one, and turn away from Him and refuse to answer Him, say to them: "I have warned you equally." He means: Let them know that you are aware that some of you are at war with one another, there is no reconciliation between you nor peace. What I meant by this is the people of the Messenger of Allah from Quraysh, as he said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, regarding His saying: "If they turn away, say, I will call you equally." If they turn away, it means Quraysh. And His saying: "And if I know whether it is near or far, what you promise," Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: Say, "And I do not know when the time will come upon you when the punishment of Allah which He promised you will befall you, and He will take revenge on you with it. Will it be near or far?"
