## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 1–2. God addresses His Prophet to fear Him by obeying Him and fulfilling His obligations, not to obey the disbelievers who ask Him to expel the weak believers, nor the hypocrites who show faith but hide harm, and to follow what is revealed to him from his Lord. And God is acquainted with what you do.
