## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 17 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “He has certainly succeeded who purifies it”: “He has certainly succeeded who purifies his soul, thus purifying it from disbelief and sins, and rectifying it with righteous deeds.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
