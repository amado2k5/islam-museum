## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 70 to 73 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then they will have over it a stinging of boiling water": "Then they will have a sparkle of boiling over it." Then these polytheists have a swarm of the Zaqqum tree for what they eat from this tree, which is a confusion of the Arabs' saying: so-and-so grayed his food, so he is stained with a swarm and gray (from a boiling one). Al-Hamim is the hot water, which is what heated and its heat ended, and its origin is a conjugation of the verb to fa'il. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Suddi, and Ibn Zayd.
