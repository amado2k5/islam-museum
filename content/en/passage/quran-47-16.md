## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And among them are those who listen to you until, when they leave you, they say to those who have been given knowledge, 'What did those who said to you, those upon whose hearts Allah has sealed and whose desires followed?': Among these disbelievers, O Muhammad, is 'one who listens to you,' while he is a hypocrite, listening to what you say but neither comprehending nor understanding it, out of negligence in what you recite from the Book of your Lord, and neglecting what you say, and calling to it of faith, 'until, when they leave your presence,' they say this as an informance to those who attended your gathering among the people of knowledge. By the Book of God, and by your recitation to them what you have recited, and what you said to them, that they will not listen to your words and your recitation: "What did Muhammad say to us 'before'?" In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
