## Summary
Hadith of Anas ibn Malik, a qudsi report: "O son of Adam, as long as you call on Me and hope in Me, I will forgive you whatever you have done... If you come to Me with earth's worth of sins and meet Me without associating anything with Me, I will bring you forgiveness equal to it". Ibn Rajab finds the chain acceptable and lists three means of forgiveness. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
