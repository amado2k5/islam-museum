## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Ubaydallah ibn Abdallah ibn Utba, from Abdallah ibn Abbas, that Sad ibn Ubada asked the Messenger of God for a fatwa, saying: my mother died and owed a vow that she did not fulfil; the Messenger of God said: fulfil it on her behalf. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.605}
