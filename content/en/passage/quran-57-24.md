## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Those who are stingy and enjoin stinginess on people. And whoever turns away, then indeed, Allah is the Self-sufficient, the Praiseworthy": Allah does not like every boastful person. Those who are stingy with what they have been given in this world for their boasting about it and boasting about it to people, they are stingy in fulfilling the right of Allah that He has made obligatory upon them, and they are stingy with it, while they also command people to be stingy. And His saying: "And whoever turns away - then indeed, Allah is the Self-sufficient, the Praiseworthy," Allah says: "And whoever turns away from the reverence of Allah, then Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy," He says: Whoever turns away from Allah's reverence, abandoning what he has called upon in spending in His cause, rejoicing in what He has been given of worldly life, boastful and stingy, for Allah is self-sufficient from his wealth and expenses, and from all His creation, the Praiseworthy to His creation by bestowing His blessings upon them.
