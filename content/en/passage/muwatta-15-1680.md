## Summary
Malik says that if she remarries after her waiting period, and her new husband has consummated the marriage or not, her first husband has no claim on her. Malik said this is the practice among us. If her husband reaches her before she remarries, he has the better right to her. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.88}
