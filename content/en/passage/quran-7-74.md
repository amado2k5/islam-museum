## Summary
Verse 74 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 74 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-74|7:74]], al-Tabari says, reporting Salih's admonition to his people: remember God's favour to you, in that He made you successors who followed Ad in the land after their destruction. "Khulafa" is the plural of "khalifa", formed on the pattern "fuala" as "shuraka" is of "sharik" and "ulama" of "alim", because the khalifa is taken to mean a man, as if the singular were "khalif". Had it been plural of a feminine adjective it would be "khalaif", like "karaim" and "halail"; the Qur'an uses both forms, once on the word's form and once on its sense. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 540}

"And settled you in the land" means He lodged you in the earth and gave you dwellings and spouses there. "You take palaces from its plains and carve houses in the mountains": it is said they hollowed out rock for dwellings, and (14824) from al-Suddi that they dug houses in the mountains. "Remember God's favours" means the blessing God gave you, and "do not commit evil in the land, spreading corruption": (14825) from Qatada that it means do not go about in the land spreading corruption, and al-Tabari refers to his earlier explanation of this. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 541}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
