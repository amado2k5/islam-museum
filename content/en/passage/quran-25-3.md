## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And take besides Him gods who create nothing, and they are creating, and they have no power for themselves harm or benefit, nor possess death, nor life, nor resurrection": the Arab polytheists who worship gods below Him, admiring those who are devoted to them, and warning them to the error of their actions, their deviation from the path of truth, and their riding the paths of misguidance that only every source of reason, deprived, can ride: These polytheists took the polytheists of Allah besides the One who owns the dominion of the heavens and the earth alone, without partner, who created everything and decreed it, 'gods,' meaning idols with their own hands they worship. They do not create anything while they create, nor possess any benefit for themselves to draw them to it, nor harm to repel from those who seek it with harm, nor the dying of the living, nor the resurrection of the dead after their death, and they abandoned the worship of the Creator of all things, the Creator of their gods, the Owner of harm and benefit, and in Whose hand is death, life, and resurrection.
