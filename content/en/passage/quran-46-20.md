## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And on the Day those who disbelieved will be presented before the Fire, you will have taken away your good things in your worldly life and enjoyed them. So that Day you will be recompensed with the punishment of God because you used to be arrogant on earth without right and because you used to be corrupt." Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And the Day those who disbelieved will be presented before the Fire," Yazid recited until he reached "And because of what you used to deviate." You know, by God, that there are people who seek their good deeds. A man retains his good things if he can, and there is no power except with God. It is mentioned that Umar ibn Al-Khattab used to say: If you wished, I would be the most delicious of you in food and the softest of clothes for you, but I keep my good things first. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
