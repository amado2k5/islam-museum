## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They will not have intercession except for those who have made a covenant with the Most Merciful": These disbelievers will not have authority in their Lord, O Muhammad, on the Day when Allah will gather the righteous to Him as a delegation of intercession, when the people of faith intercede for one another with Allah, and some will intercede for one another, except for those among them who have taken a "covenant" with the Most Merciful in this world to believe in Him, to believe in His Messenger, to acknowledge what he brought, and to act upon what He commanded. Also: Muawiyah narrated from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "Except for one who takes a covenant with the Most Merciful," he said: The covenant is the testimony that there is no god but Allah, and to disown Allah from power and power and to hope only in Allah. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
