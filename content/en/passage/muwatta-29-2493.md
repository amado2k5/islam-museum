## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that Zayd ibn Thabit used to say: for a standing eye when it is extinguished, a hundred dinars. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.427}
