## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Those are the Companions of the Right”: those who performed the deeds mentioned, such as freeing slaves, feeding orphans, and so forth, are the Companions of the Right, who will be taken to Paradise on the Day of Resurrection. Regarding the phrase “And those who disbelieve in Our signs,” he says: those who disbelieve in Our proofs, signs, and arguments from the scriptures, messengers, and other sources, “are the Companions of the Left,” meaning they are the Companions of the Left on the Day of Resurrection, who will be taken to the Left. We have already explained the meaning of “the Left” and why the left is called “the Left” previously, making it unnecessary to repeat it here. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah in this section.
