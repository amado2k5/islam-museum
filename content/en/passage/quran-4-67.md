## Summary
Verse 67 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 67 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (p. 529, note 2).** Al-Tabari says the sense is that had they done what they were admonished, there would be good for them in our giving them, for their doing what they were admonished of, obedience to us and stopping at our command, a recompense, meaning requital and a large reward; and their resolves and opinions would have been steadier and they stronger in their deeds. The editor refers to the earlier interpretation of the word for reward (p. 365, note 3). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 529}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 529}
