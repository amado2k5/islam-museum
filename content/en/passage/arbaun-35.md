## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: "Do not envy one another, do not bid against one another, do not hate one another, do not turn your backs on one another; the Muslim is the brother of the Muslim: he does not wrong him, abandon him, lie to him or despise him", and the Prophet pointed to his chest three times saying, "God-consciousness is here". Ibn Rajab notes the chain, and that Abu Sa'id, the client of Ibn Kuraiz, is unknown. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
