## Summary
Malik said: he heard Ibn Shihab say: in the time of Umar ibn al-Khattab the stray camels were herd camels, breeding and not touched by anyone. Then in the time of Uthman ibn Affan he ordered that they be announced, then sold, and when their owner came, he was given their price. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.306}
