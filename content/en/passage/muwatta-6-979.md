## Summary
Malik transmits from Sadaqa ibn Yasar from Abdullah ibn Umar that he said: by God, that I make the lesser pilgrimage before the pilgrimage and offer is more beloved to me than that I make the lesser pilgrimage after the pilgrimage in Dhu al-Hijja. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1108), Suwayd (519) and al-Shafii (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.462}
