## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 25 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "and he called her from beneath it, 'Do not grieve; your Lord has placed a secret beneath you.'" Reciters differed in this reading. The majority of the reciters of Hijaz and Iraq read it as "and he called her from beneath it," meaning: Gabriel called her from before her, differing in their interpretation. Some interpret it when he reads it as "beneath it," and others interpret it as Jesus, and that he called her from beneath her after she gave birth to him. Some reciters from Kufa and Basra read this: "He called her from beneath her" with a fatha on the two under her, meaning: the one beneath her called her, meaning that the one beneath her called out to her, and that he was the one who called his mother. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dhahhak, Al-A'mash, Ibrahim, and others.
