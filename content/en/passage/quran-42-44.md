## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And whoever Allah leads astray, he has no protector after Him. And you see the wrongdoers when they see the punishment, saying, 'Is there any way to return?'" means that whoever Allah forsakes from the right path, he will have no protector to follow him, and He will guide him to the right path and guide him after Allah has led him astray. "And you will see the wrongdoers when they see the punishment." Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: And you will see the disbelievers in Allah, O Muhammad, on the Day of Resurrection, when they witness the punishment of Allah, saying to their Lord: "Is it possible for us, O Lord, to return from the way," as in His saying, "If only You could see when the criminals bowed their heads before their Lord? Our Lord has seen and heard us..." The verse says, 'The poor were sought after at a time when they were not being sought.'
