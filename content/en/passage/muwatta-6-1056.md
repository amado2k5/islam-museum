## Summary
Malik transmits that he heard Ibn Shihab say: I heard some of our scholars say: the Hijr was enclosed only so that people would encompass the whole House with circumambulation. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1280) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.489}
