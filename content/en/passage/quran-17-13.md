## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And every person to whom We have obligated his flock around his neck, We will bring forth for him on the Day of Resurrection a book that he will find published." Rather, His saying, 'We made him obliged to his bird,' is like what the Arabs used to be optimistic about or pessimistic about the bird's unique and its worries. He taught them, Glorified and Exalted, that each of them had been bound by his Lord with his bird around his neck with misfortune, which was what He obliged him to do, and misery brought him to a blazing fire, or happiness that brought him to the gardens of Eden. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, and Ibn Jurayj.
