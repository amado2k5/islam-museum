## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 20 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Then he was among those who believed and exhorted one another to patience and exhorted one another to compassion”: Then this one who said, “I have spent wealth in abundance,” was among those who believed in God and His Messenger, so he believed with them as they believed. “And exhorted one another to patience” means: And he was among those who exhorted one another to patience regarding what befell them for the sake of God. “And exhorted one another to compassion” means: And they exhorted one another to compassion, as narrated by Shabib, on the authority of Ikrimah, on the authority of Ibn Abbas, “And exhorted one another to compassion” means: Compassion for people. His statement: “And in the passage he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.”
