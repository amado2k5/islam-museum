## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, when you have intercourse, do not engage in sin and aggression and disobedience to the Messenger, but engage in righteousness and piety, and fear Allah, to whom you will be gathered." This means: O you who have spoken to Allah and His Messenger, "when you engage in sin, aggression, or disobedience to the Messenger," but rather to engage in righteousness, meaning obedience to Allah and what brings you closer to Him. "And piety" means: by avoiding Him in fulfilling His obligations and avoiding His sins. "And fear Allah." "To whom you will be gathered," he says: "And fear Allah, to whom is your destiny, and to Him is your community in neglecting His obligations and advancing His sins, so that He may punish you for it when your destiny is to Him."
