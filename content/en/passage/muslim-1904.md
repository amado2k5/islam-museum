## Summary
A report of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari in the chapter on whoever fights so that God's word is supreme, in the Book of Leadership of Sahih Muslim. A Bedouin asks about the difference between motives for fighting: a text setting the intention of jihad. {cite:sahih-muslim}
