## Summary
Yahya said: Malik was asked about a squint in the eye and a stiffness of the eye. He said: there is only judgment in it, unless the sight of the eye is reduced, in which case there is blood-money in proportion to what the sight of the eye has lost. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.427}
