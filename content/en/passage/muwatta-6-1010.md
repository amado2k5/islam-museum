## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Salim ibn Abdallah, that he heard Abu Hurayra tell Abdullah ibn Umar that people in ihram at al-Rabadha asked him about game meat they found, which people not in ihram were eating, and he gave them a fatwa to eat it. Then Abu Hurayra came to Medina to Umar and asked him about it; Umar said: what did you give them as a fatwa? He said: I gave them a fatwa to eat it. Umar said: had you given them a different fatwa, I would have struck you hard. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1141) and Suwayd (574) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.473}
