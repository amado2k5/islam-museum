## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29-31 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Then some of them turned to one another, blaming each other,” as follows: God, may His praise be exalted, says: Then some of them turned to one another, blaming each other for their negligence in neglecting to make the exception and for their insistence on continuing their practice of not feeding the poor from their garden. And His words, “Woe to us! Indeed, we were transgressors,” mean: The inhabitants of the garden said, “Woe to us! Indeed, we were transgressors,” meaning they disobeyed God’s command by neglecting to make the exception and glorify Him.
