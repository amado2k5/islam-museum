## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abu al-Zinad, from Busr ibn Said, from Ubayd Abu Salih, client of al-Saffah, that he said: I sold cloth of mine to people of the house of Nakhla on a term, then I wanted to go to Kufa. They offered me to reduce some of the price for them, and to pay me in cash. I asked Zayd ibn Thabit about it, and he said: I do not order you to eat this or to make it your agent. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.204}
