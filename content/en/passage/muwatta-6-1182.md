## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar met a man of his family called al-Mujabbar, who had circumambulated and had not shaved or shortened, and had forgotten this; Ibn Umar ordered him to return and shave or shorten, then return to the House and circumambulate. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.532}
