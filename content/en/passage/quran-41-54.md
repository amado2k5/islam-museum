## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, they are in a state of meeting their Lord, indeed, He encompasses everything": Indeed, those who deny the signs of God are in doubt about meeting their Lord, meaning they are in doubt about resurrection after death and their return to their Lord, as in Al-Suddi, "Indeed, they are in a state of meeting their Lord," meaning in doubt. And His saying: "Indeed, He encompasses all things," He says: Indeed, Allah is encompassing everything He created, encompassing knowledge of all of it and power over it. Nothing He intends to do is detached from Him, but He is capable of it, the knower in His place.
