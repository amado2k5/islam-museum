## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And the majority of his people who disbelieved and denied the meeting of the Hereafter and We gave them luxury in the life of this world. This is nothing but a human like yourselves, who eats from what you eat and drinks from what you drink." The noble among the people of the Messenger whom We sent after Noah said. And he meant by the Messenger in this context: righteous, and by his people: Thamud. "Those who disbelieved and denied the meeting of the Hereafter" means: those who denied the oneness of Allah, and denied the meeting of the Hereafter, meaning they denied the meeting with Allah in the Hereafter.
