## Summary
Malik transmits from Dawud ibn al-Husayn, from Abu Sufyan, client of Ibn Abi Ahmad, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger allowed the sale of ariya by estimate, for less than five wasqs or for five wasqs; Dawud is unsure whether he said five or less than five. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.142}
