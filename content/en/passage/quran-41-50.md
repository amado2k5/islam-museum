## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if We make him taste mercy from Us after hardship that touches him, they will say, 'This is for me, and I do not think the Hour will come.' But if I return to my Lord, indeed, with Him is the best. So We will surely inform those who disbelieved of what they have done, and We will make them taste a severe punishment." This is for me" with Allah, because Allah is pleased with me by His pleasure with my deeds, and I do not abide by them. As narrated from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "They will say, 'This is mine,' meaning by my deeds, and I am justified in this." 'And I do not think the Hour will stand.' He says: 'And I do not think the Resurrection will be established on the Day it is established.' 'And if I return to my Lord,' he says: And if the Resurrection also comes, and I return to Allah alive after my death, 'Indeed, I have with Him is the best,' meaning: I have with Him wealth and wealth. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
