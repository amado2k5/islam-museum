## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them as plays": "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them" is only a proof against you, O people, and so that you should consider all of that. Know that the One who planned and created Him is unlike nothing, that divinity exists only for Him, and worship is not suitable for anything else, nor was He created that in vain or play. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them as playthings," he says: We did not create them in vain or in vain. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
