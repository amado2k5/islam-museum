## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, 'Have you seen what you invoke besides Allah? Show me what they have created of the earth, or do they have association with the heavens? Have you brought me a book before this or a revelation from knowledge, if you should be truthful?": Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists of Allah from your people: Have you considered, O people, the gods and idols you worship besides Allah? Show me what they created from the earth, for my Lord created the entire earth, so you called upon it for the sake of its creation, which You created as gods and lords, so that you may have in your worship. This is a proof, for among my arguments against my worship of my God and my devotion to Him is that He created the earth and created it from no origin. And His saying: "Or do they have associating partners with Allah in the heavens?" Allah the Exalted says: Or your gods whom you worship, O people, associate partners with Allah in the seven heavens, so that too you have a proof in your worship of them. Among my arguments for worshiping my Lord alone is that He has no partner in its creation, and that He is the sole creator of it and not everything else. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
