## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So enter the gates of Hell, abiding therein forever. How wretched is the abode of the arrogant": Allah Almighty says to these oppressors themselves when they say to their Lord: 'Enter the gates of Hell,' meaning: 'Enter the gates of Hell,' meaning 'abiding therein forever,' meaning they dwell therein. 'How wretched is the abode of the arrogant.' He means: How wretched is the abode of the arrogant. He means: How wretched is the abode of the one who arrogantly despends Allah and does not acknowledge His lordship and believes in His oneness is Hell.
