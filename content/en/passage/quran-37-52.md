## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 51–53. Al-Tabari mentions a saying from the people of Paradise, 'Indeed, I had a companion.' Interpreters differ regarding the companion: Some said, 'He was a devil who said to him, 'Are you among those who believe in resurrection? When we die and we are dust and bones, are we indeed in debt?'
