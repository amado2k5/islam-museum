## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So the confederates differed among themselves, so woe to those who disbelieved from the scene of a great day": Those who differed about Jesus, and they became divided factions among his people, as al-Hasan narrated to me, and from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "So the confederates differed among themselves," he said: the People of the Book. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
