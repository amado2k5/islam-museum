## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter on the prohibition of excessive questioning, in the Book of Knowledge of Sahih Muslim. It sets the rule of full compliance with what is forbidden and compliance within ability with what is commanded, and ends with the cause of the ruin of nations through excessive questioning. {cite:sahih-muslim}
