## Summary
Malik says: whoever sells a share of jointly owned land, and some of those with pre-emption surrender it to the seller while some refuse except to take their pre-emption. Whoever refuses to surrender takes pre-emption on the whole, and he may not take in proportion to his right and leave the rest. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.256}
