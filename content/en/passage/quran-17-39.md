## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is what your Lord has revealed to you of wisdom, and do not place with Allah any other god, lest you meet in Hell blamed and rejected": This is what We have shown you, Muhammad, of the beautiful morals that We have commanded you to beautify and forbidden you from their evil "from what your Lord has revealed to you of wisdom," meaning: from the wisdom revealed to you in this Book, as Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase "That is of what your Lord has revealed to you of wisdom," he said: The Quran. We have explained the meaning of wisdom in the previous part of our book, which is beyond revisiting it here. "And do not place any other god with Allah and be cast into Hell a blaming and driven one" means: And do not make a partner with Allah in your worship, so you will find in Hell a blaming person who blames yourself and those who know you among the people "rejected." He means: a distant and expelled one in the Fire, but the most sincere worship is to Allah, the One, the All-Subdued, so you may be saved from His punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
