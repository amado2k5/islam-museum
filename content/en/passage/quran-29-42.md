## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites the verse within verses 42 to 43 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah knows what they call upon besides Him, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise": Reciters differed in reciting His statement: "Indeed, Allah knows what they call upon." The majority of the reciters of the lands read it as "ta'unun" with a ta, meaning the address to the polytheists of Quraysh: "Indeed, Allah, O people, 'knows what you call to from nothing besides Him.'" Abu Amr read this: "Indeed, Allah knows what they call upon" with a ya, meaning to inform about nations. Indeed, Allah knows what those nations whom We have destroyed call upon besides Him.
