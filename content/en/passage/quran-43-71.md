## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He circumambulates them with golden pages and cups containing what souls desire and delight the eyes, and you will abide therein forever": He circumambulates those who believed in His signs in this world when they enter Paradise in the Hereafter with golden pages, which are the plural of many pages, and the 'sahfa' is the bowl. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
