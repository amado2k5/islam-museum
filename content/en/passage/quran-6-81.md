## Summary
Verse 81 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 81 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "how should I fear what you associate, when you do not fear that you associate with God what he sent down no authority for on you? Which of the two parties has better right to security, if you know?" al-Tabari says: this is Abraham's answer to his people when they frightened him with their gods, that they would touch him with something he dislikes for his speaking ill of them. He said to them: how should I fear and dread what you associate in your worship of your Lord and worship besides him, though it neither harms nor benefits; had they benefited or harmed they would have warded off from themselves my breaking them and my striking them with the axe; while you do not fear God who created you and provided for you, and who is able to benefit and harm you for your associating others in your worship of him; "what he sent down no authority for on you" means what he gave you no proof for in your associating it in worshipping him, set no evidence for you against him and made no excuse for you in it; "which of the two parties has better right to security" says: am I the one with better right to security from the end of my worshipping my Lord, making worship pure for him, upright in my religion for him, free of worshipping idols and images, or you who worship apart from God images for which God gave you no evidence or proof by your worshipping them; "if you know" says: if you know the truth of what I say and the reality of what I argue against you, then say and tell me which of the two parties has better right to security? {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 490}

The editor refers for "authority", "party" and "security" to earlier passages.

Muhammad ibn Ishaq said the like (13467), on "how should I fear what you associate, when you do not fear that you associate with God", he says... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 490}
