## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam from Aisha, in the chapter on the forenoon prayer: she used to pray eight rak'as of the forenoon prayer, then say that even if her parents were brought back to life for her, she would not give them up. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.218}
