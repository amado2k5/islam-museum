## Summary
Verse 54 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 54 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**What the plotting of the unbelievers means.** Al-Tabari says it means the plotting of those among the Israelites who disbelieved, those from whom God said Jesus perceived disbelief; their plotting was that they agreed among themselves to ambush Jesus and kill him, after his people had expelled him and his mother and he returned to them. Report 7131 (Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, from Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal, from Asbat, from al-Suddi) says Jesus went with the disciples who had been fishing, who believed and followed him, until he came to the Israelites by night and called out among them; al-Tabari ties this to [[passage/quran-61-14|61:14]], where one group believed and another disbelieved. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 453} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 454}

**God's plan: the report of al-Suddi.** Al-Tabari says, as al-Suddi related, that God's plan against them was casting the likeness of Jesus on one of his followers, so that the plotters killed him thinking he was Jesus, God having already raised Jesus. Report 7132 (Muhammad ibn al-Husayn, from Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal, from Asbat, from al-Suddi) says the Israelites besieged Jesus and nineteen of the disciples in a house; Jesus asked his companions who would take his likeness and be killed with Paradise for him; a man among them took it and Jesus was raised to heaven. When the disciples came out the people saw nineteen, and they told them Jesus had been raised; they counted and found one short while seeing the likeness of Jesus among them, so they doubted, and on that they killed the man thinking he was Jesus and crucified him. Al-Tabari ties this to [[passage/quran-4-157|4:157]], which speaks of denying the killing, the crucifixion and the likeness. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 454}

**Another possible sense.** Al-Tabari says it is also possible that God's plan against them means His drawing them on step by step until the term written for them is reached, as he explained at [[passage/quran-2-15|2:15]]; the editor refers to the earlier volume (pp. 301-306). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 454}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
