## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "And how many times did We break from a city that was oppressive and after it brought up other people": and many have cut us from a village. Al-Qasm: originally from a kasra, from which it is said: I broke the back of so-and-so if you broke it, and 'niqasam' means its tooth was broken. Here, it means "destroyed us," and this is how interpreters interpret it. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
