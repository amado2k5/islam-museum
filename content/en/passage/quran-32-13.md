## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if We willed, We could have given every soul to whom He guided, but the truth is from Me, I would have filled Hell with Paradise and all mankind": "And if We had willed," O Muhammad, "We would have given Us" those polytheists with Allah from your people and others among the disbelievers of Allah. "He guided them," meaning: their guidance and guidance to faith in Allah, "but the right word is from Me." He means: punishment is due from Me for them, and His saying "I would have filled Hell from Paradise and all mankind" means from among the people of sins and disbelief in God among them. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
