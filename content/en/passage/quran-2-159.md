## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "those who conceal what We sent down of clear proofs and guidance after We made it clear to the people in the Book" al-Tabari says it means the Jewish scholars and rabbis and the Christian scholars, for concealing from people the matter of Muhammad and not following him though they found him written in the Torah and Gospel; the "clear proofs" are what was made clear of his prophethood, mission and description in the two books, and the "guidance" is what He made plain of his affair in the books He sent down on their prophets. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 249} He reports from Ibn 'Abbas that Mu'adh ibn Jabal, Sa'd ibn Mu'adh and Kharija ibn Zayd asked some Jewish rabbis about what is in the Torah, and they concealed it and refused to tell them, and the verse was revealed about them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 249} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 250} From Mujahid, they are the People of the Book; from al-Rabi', they concealed Muhammad though they found him written with them, out of envy and wrongdoing; from Qatada, they concealed Islam, which is God's religion, and concealed Muhammad. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 250}

On "after We made it clear to the people in the Book" he says "the people" means some people, since knowledge of Muhammad's prophethood, description and mission was only with the People of the Book, and the Book is the Torah and Gospel. Though the verse was revealed about a particular group, it is meant for anyone who conceals knowledge God obliged him to make clear to people, and he cites what is reported from the Prophet, "whoever is asked about knowledge he has and conceals it will be bridled on the Day of Resurrection with a bridle of fire", {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 251} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 252} and Abu Hurayra saying that were it not for a verse in God's book he would not have narrated to you, and reciting this verse; in another report, were it not for two verses God sent down in His book he would not narrate anything, this one and the verse on the covenant taken from those given the Book to make it clear to people, from Al 'Imran. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v03 p. 252}
