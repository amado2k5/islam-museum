## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Hijr 15:95, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Companion generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «إنا كفيناك المستهزئين».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">
