## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 22 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “that you may traverse its vast and winding paths” as meaning: that you may traverse its difficult and winding paths. “Fajaj” is the plural of “fajj,” which means path. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
