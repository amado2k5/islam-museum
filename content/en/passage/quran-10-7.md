**Commentary on verse 7 (pp. 25-27)**

He then explains "Those who do not look forward to meeting Us, are content with the life of this world and at rest in it, and those who are heedless of Our signs". Those who do not fear meeting Us on the Day of Resurrection, and so deny reward and punishment, who vie for the adornment and glitter of the world, content with it in place of the next life and at rest in it; and those who are heedless of God's signs, His proofs of His oneness and His arguments to His servants for sincere worship of Him, turning away from them and distracted, not considering them as one who gives himself good counsel would, so as to know the truth of what they point to and see the falsity of what they persist in. The editor refers to the glosses on "being at rest" and "heedlessness". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 25}

The interpreters said the same. Reports 17553 and 17554, from Mujahid, on "and are at rest in it": it is like "whoever wants the life of this world and its adornment, We pay them in full for their deeds in it" in Surat Hud, and report 17555 is the same from him. Report 17556, from Qatada: if you wish you will see a man of the world who is glad for it, sad for it, angry for it and pleased for it. Report 17557, from Ibn Zayd: the whole verse concerns unbelievers, and then he said "those, their refuge is the Fire for what they used to earn". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 27}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
