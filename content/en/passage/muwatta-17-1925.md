## Summary
Malik says: as we see it, God knows best, he wanted to sell it to its owner who had bought it from him, for more than the price he bought it for. If he had sold it to someone other than the one he bought it from, there would be no harm in it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.188}
