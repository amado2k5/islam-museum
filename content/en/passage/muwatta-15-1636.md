## Summary
Malik says of a woman who redeems herself from her husband: if she knows her husband harmed her, restricted her, and wronged her, the divorce stands and he must return her property. Malik said this is what he had heard, and what the people here follow. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.75}
