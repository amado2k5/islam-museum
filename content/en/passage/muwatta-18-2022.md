## Summary
Malik says of a man who gives another money for profit-sharing, and stipulates that he may buy only palm trees or animals, because he seeks the fruit of the palms or the offspring of the animals while keeping them: Malik said: this is not lawful, and it is not the practice of Muslims in profit-sharing, unless he buys them and then sells them as other goods are sold. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.227}
