## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O wives of the Prophet, whoever among you commits a clear immorality, the punishment will be doubled, and that is easy for Allah": Allah Almighty says to the wives of the Prophet: "O wives of the Prophet, whoever among you commits a clear immorality," he says: Whoever among you commits the known adultery for which Allah has prescribed the punishment, the punishment for her wickedness in the Hereafter will be doubled for her wickedness compared to the wickedness of the wives of others, as follows: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas, 'He doubles the punishment for her,' meaning the punishment of the Hereafter. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
