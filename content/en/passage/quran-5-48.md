**Commentary on verse 48: "We sent down to you the Book in truth, confirming the Book before it and as a guardian over it", pp. 377-380**

Al-Tabari says this addresses His prophet Muhammad: We sent down to you, Muhammad, the Book, the Qur'an, "in truth", that is in veracity with no lie or doubt that it is from God, "confirming what was before it of the Book", sent to confirm God's earlier books to His prophets, "and a guardian over it", confirming the earlier books and witness over them that they are true from God, trusted over them and preserving them. The root of the word is guarding and watching: one says so-and-so was guardian over it when he watched, protected and witnessed it.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 377}

The interpreters said likewise though their wording differed. Some said it means a witness, as reports 12103m and 12104, from Ibn 'Abbas and al-Suddi, say; report 12105, from Qatada, says trusted and a witness over the earlier books; report 12106, from Mujahid, says entrusted with the Qur'an, a witness and confirmer, and Ibn Jurayj reports others as saying the Qur'an is trusted over the books: when the People of the Book tell us something of their book, if it is in the Qur'an confirm it and if not, deny it.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 378}

Others said it means trusted over it. Reports 12107 to 12118, from Ibn 'Abbas with chains through al-Tamimi, say entrusted with it; report 12114 says the guardian is the trustee and the Qur'an is trustee over every book before it; report 12115 says it is a witness over the Torah and Gospel, confirming them, trusted and ruling over the earlier books. The editor says al-Tamimi is Arbada al-Tamimi, the reliable Successor.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 379}

Report 12119, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, says entrusted over the books before it; report 12120, from al-Hasan and 'Ikrima, says confirming these books and trusted over them. Others said guardian means confirmer; report 12121, from Ibn Zayd, says confirming it: everything God sent down of Torah, Gospel or Psalms, the Qur'an confirms. Others said what is meant by "confirming what was before it and a guardian over it" is the Prophet of God.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 380}

We stop here at the foot of p. 380, before report 12122 from Mujahid on the Prophet as the guardian; the rest of verse 48 follows in the next batch.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
