## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieved said, 'Shall We guide you to a man who will inform you when you are torn apart? Indeed, you are in a new creation.'" And those who disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger Muhammad, astonished at His promise of resurrection after death, some of them to one another, said: "Shall We inform you, O people, of a man who will inform you when you are torn apart of every torn, that you are in a new creation?" He says: He tells you that after you have been torn apart in the earth by affliction and after your destiny in the dust, you will return to your former form before death as a new creation, as: And from Qatadah: "And those who disbelieve said, 'Shall We guide you to a man who will inform you when you are torn apart every piece of land?'" He said: That is the polytheists of Quraysh and the polytheists among the people. "He will inform you when you are torn apart from every torn," meaning when the earth devours you, and you become remains and bones, and the beasts and birds tear you apart. "Indeed, you are in a new creation," you will be revived and resurrected. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
