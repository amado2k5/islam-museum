## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 24 as a single section. He explains the phrase "So he denied and disobeyed": Pharaoh denied Moses regarding the miraculous signs he brought him, and disobeyed him regarding what he was commanded to do in terms of obeying his Lord and fearing Him. He then quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd in this section.
