## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 16 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "We were snaked by the first creation; rather, they are in a confusion of a new creation": This is God's rebuke to the polytheists of Quraysh who said: "When we die and we were dust, that is a distant return." He says to them: We were snakes by the creation of the first creation that We created, and it was nothing, so He lamented their return to a new creation after their affliction in the dust and after their annihilation, saying: That does not help us, but we are capable of it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Sufyan, and Ata.
