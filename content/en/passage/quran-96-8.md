## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 15 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Read in the name of your Lord who created”: He, may His praise be exalted, means by His words, “Read in the name of your Lord,” that Muhammad is saying: Read, O Muhammad, by mentioning your Lord “who created.” Then He clarifies who created, saying, “He created man from a clot,” meaning from blood. He says “from a clot,” but what is meant is “from a clot,” because He used the plural form, just as He says “tree” and “trees,” or “reed” and “reeds,” and similarly “clot” and “clot.” He said “from a clot” and “man” in the same word because it carries a plural meaning, even though it is in the same word. Therefore, it is said “from a clot.” His saying, “Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous,” means: Read, O Muhammad, and your Lord is the Most Generous, “Who taught by the pen,” meaning He created writing and script. As for Qatadah, regarding “Read in the name of your Lord who created,” he recited until he reached “taught by the pen.” He said: The pen is a great blessing from God; were it not for it, life would not exist, nor would it be righteous. The passage also includes statements attributed to Qatadah.
