## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he marked the hump of his offering, said: in the name of God, God is greatest. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1207) and Suwayd (524) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.510}
