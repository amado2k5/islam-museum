## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 26 as a single section. He explains the phrase "So I swear by the twilight": This is an oath our Lord has sworn by the twilight, and the twilight is the redness on the horizon from the west, according to some. Scholars of interpretation differed on this point. Some said it is the redness, as we have stated, and this was the view of a group of scholars from Iraq. Others said it is the daytime. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Sufyan.
