## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We created the heavens and the earth and what is between them in six days, and not fatigue touched them." Also: from Abu Sinan, from Abu Bakr, who said: The Jews came to the Prophet and said: O Muhammad, "Allah created the earth on Sunday and Monday, and created the mountains on Tuesday, created cities, fortifications, rivers, and their construction and destruction on Wednesday, and created the heavens and angels on Thursday for three hours, meaning Friday. In the first of the three hours, He created the appointed periods, the second the affliction, and the third Adam." They said: You have spoken the truth if you have completed it. The Prophet understood what they intended and became angry, so Allah revealed: 'And no one has ever touched us from the unseen, so be patient with M.' In the passage, he also quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
