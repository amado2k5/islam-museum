## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Muddaththir 74:11, narrated from the Tabi'i (Successor) generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «ذرني ومن خلقت وحيدا».

## Full report (Arabic source text)

هذا سحر يؤثر يأثره عن غيره، فنزلت: {ذرني ومن خلقت وحيدا} الآيات كلها.
