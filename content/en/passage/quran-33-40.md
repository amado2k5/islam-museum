## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Muhammad was not the father of any of your men, but he was the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets, and Allah is Knowing of everything": O people, Muhammad was not the father of Zayd ibn Harithah, nor the father of any of your men, whom Muhammad did not bear, so it is forbidden for him to marry his wife after separating from her. He is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets, who sealed prophethood and sealed it. So it is not opened for anyone after him until the Day of Judgment. Allah is knowledgeable in all your deeds, words, and other matters. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
