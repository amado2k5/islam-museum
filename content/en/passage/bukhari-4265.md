## Summary
In the Book of Campaigns, the chapter on the Battle of Mu'ta in the land of Syria: Khalid ibn al-Walid's testimony about himself after he took the banner when the three commanders were killed. It shows how fierce the battle was when he led an orderly withdrawal of the army. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
