## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Marwan ibn al-Hakam ruled that a man who divorces his wife with al-batta has divorced her three times. Malik said this is the most beloved of what he has heard on the matter. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.60}
