## Summary
Verse 29 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 29 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

This is God's report about these polytheists who hold idols and images equal to him, about whom he began this surah. He says: "they say: there is nothing but our present life", reporting that they deny that God gives life to his creatures after he makes them die, saying there is no life after death and no raising or resurrection after perishing; by denying that and denying God's reward and punishment in the next abode they do not care what sin and disobedience they commit, since they hope for no reward for belief in God, trust in his messenger and good work after death, and fear no punishment for their unbelief in God and his messenger and the evil they do. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 323}

Ibn Zayd used to say: this is God's report about these unbelievers who were made to stand before the Fire, that if they were sent back to the world they would say "there is nothing but our present life, and we shall not be raised"; this is 13184 from him. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 323}

The editor keeps "and the evil of the deed" from the manuscript, where it is undotted.

Verse 29 closes here at p. 323, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-30|6:30]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
