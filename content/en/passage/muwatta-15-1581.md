## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that it reached him that a man said to Abdullah ibn Abbas: I divorced my wife a hundred times, so what do you think I owe? Ibn Abbas said: three divorces separated her from you, and ninety-seven you took in mockery of God's signs. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.59}
