## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Gardens of Eden which the Most Merciful promised His servants of the unseen; indeed, His promise is to come": Those will enter Paradise "Gardens of Eden." For it, "Gardens of Eden" is a particle of translation about Paradise. By "Gardens of Eden," he means: gardens of residence. I have previously explained this with evidence that does not require its repetition. And His saying: "which the Most Merciful has promised His servants unseen" means: These are the gardens that the Most Merciful promised His believing servants to enter unseen, because they neither saw nor saw them, so they are unseen for them. And His saying: "Indeed, His promise was to come," God Almighty says: Indeed, Allah had promised Him, and His promise here is the promised one, which is Paradise to come to Him by His allies and those who obey Him whom Allah will admit.
