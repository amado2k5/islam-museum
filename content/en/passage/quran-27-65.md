## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 65 to 66 together in one place. Al-Tabari says that the Prophet is commanded to tell his questioners among the polytheists about the Hour: that none in the heavens and the earth knows the unseen except Allah, and the Hour is part of that, and they do not know when they will be resurrected. He also cites Aisha's statement to those who claimed to inform people of what will happen tomorrow.
