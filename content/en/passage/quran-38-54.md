## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 52 to 54 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, this is Our provision which will never end": This is what We gave to these righteous in the gardens of Eden of abundant fruits and drink, and limited the gaze, enabling them therein to attain pleasures and what their souls desired for Our provision. We provided them therein with honor from Us for them, "it has no end." He means: it has no cessation or annihilation from them, because whenever they take a fruit from one of its trees and eat it, it returns to its place again, and that is for them forever. The cessation of what the people of this world was given in this world never ceases, but it ends with annihilation and perishes. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
