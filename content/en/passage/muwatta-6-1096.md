## Summary
A saying of Malik: whoever forgets or doubts part of his circumambulation, and does not remember until he is running between al-Safa and al-Marwa, he stops his running, then completes his circumambulation of the House on what he is certain of, prays the two rak'as of circumambulation, then begins his running between al-Safa and al-Marwa again. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1320-1321) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.502}
