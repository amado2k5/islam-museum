## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 26 to 27 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "It was said, 'Enter Paradise.' He said, 'Would that my people knew.'" When they killed him like that, Allah said to him: "Enter Paradise." When he entered it and witnessed what Allah had honored him with due to his faith and patience in it, he said: 'If only my people knew what my Lord has forgiven me.' He says: 'If only they knew that the reason for which my Lord forgave me my sins and made me among those whom Allah honored by admitting him to Paradise, my faith in Allah and my patience in Him was until I was killed, so they believe in Allah and deserve Paradise.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
