## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Your Lord is the One who drives for you the ships into the sea that you may seek of His bounty. Indeed, He is to you ever Merciful": Allah Almighty says to those who disbelieve in Him: Your Lord, O people, is He who drives ships for you in the sea. He carries you there, "to seek from His bounty," so that you may ride there to reach your places of trade, demands, and livelihoods, and seek His provision. "Indeed, He was merciful to you." He says: Indeed, Allah was merciful to you when He sent the ships into the sea. To facilitate this, you must act in seeking His bounty in distant lands that, if not for His ease for you, would be difficult for you to reach. As we said regarding His saying: "He will send you away," the interpreters said. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
