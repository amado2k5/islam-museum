## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "so that those who believe and do righteous deeds will be recompensed; for them is forgiveness and a generous provision": This is established in the clear Book, so that those who believe in Allah and His Messenger, do what Allah and His Messenger have commanded them, and refrain from what He has forbidden them to obey their Lord. "Those will have forgiveness." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: For those who believe and do righteous deeds, forgiveness from their Lord for their sins. "And a generous provision," meaning: And a pleasant life on the Day of Resurrection in Paradise, as: And from Qatadah: "Those will have forgiveness" for their sins and "a generous provision" in Paradise. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
