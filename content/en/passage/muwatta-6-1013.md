## Summary
A saying of Malik about one who enters ihram having game that he hunted or bought: he is not obliged to release it, and there is no harm in his keeping it with his family. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1145) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.475}
