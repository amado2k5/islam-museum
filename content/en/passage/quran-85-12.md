## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11 to 12 as one section. He explains His saying, “Indeed, the punishment of your Lord is severe”: Indeed, the punishment of your Lord, O Muhammad, for those of His creation whom He punishes, and His vengeance upon those who have been punished by Him, is severe. This is a warning from God to the people of His Messenger Muhammad, that He will inflict upon them a punishment and vengeance similar to that which befell the people of the ditch for their disbelief in Him, their denial of His Messenger, and their persecution of the believing men and women among them.
