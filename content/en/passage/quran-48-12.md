## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Rather, you thought that the Messenger and the believers would never return to their families, and that was pleasing in your hearts, and you thought it was evil, and you were a dejected people": Allah Almighty says to those Bedouins who excuse themselves to the Messenger of Allah when he leaves his journey to them, saying: "Our wealth and family occupied us." You did not neglect the Messenger of Allah when he left you, and you withdrew from his company to occupy your wealth and family. Rather, you stayed behind in your homes, thinking that the Messenger of Allah and his companions would perish, so they will never return to you By eradicating the enemy and beautifying that in your hearts, and Satan made that good in your hearts and corrected it to you until it was good for you to fall behind him, so you stayed away from his company. "And you thought ill of him," meaning: and you thought that Allah would not support Muhammad and his believing companions over their enemies, and that the enemy would defeat them, overpower them, and kill them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
