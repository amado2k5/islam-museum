## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 10 and 11 together: 'O Muhammad, Allah sent messengers before you among the earlier nations,' omitting the mention of the messengers to suffice with the indication of 'We were sent before you' to them, and He meant 'the first sects' their nations, the singular being 's'ay'a', and the allies of a man are called his followers; according to Ibn Abbas and the leaders of the earlier nations. And 'And no messenger comes to them except that they mock him': whatever messenger comes to their nations calling them to the oneness of Allah and obeying Him, they mock him out of arrogance and rebellion against their Lord.
