## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We have sent you as a witness, a bringer of good tidings, and a warner": "Indeed, We have sent you," O Muhammad, as a witness over your nation regarding what they have called you to, of the message You sent to them with, and as a bringer of good tidings for them of Paradise if they respond to you to what you have called them to of the upright religion, and as a warner for them of the punishment of God if they turn away from what you brought them from your Lord. Then the readers differed in the recitation of His saying: "So that you may believe in Allah and His Messenger, honor Him, honor Him, and glorify Him." The majority of the reciters of the cities, except Abu Ja'far al-Madani and Abu Amr ibn al-Ala, read all of this with the letter 'ta' (to believe, honor Him, honor Him, and glorify Him), meaning: to believe in Allah and His Messenger y'all, O people. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
