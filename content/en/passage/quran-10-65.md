**Commentary on verse 65 (p. 142)**

On [[passage/quran-10-65|10:65]] al-Tabari says God speaks to His prophet: do not grieve at what these polytheists say of their Lord and at their setting up idols and images beside Him, for all might belongs to God. He alone holds the might of this world and the next, with no partner in it, and He is the one who takes vengeance on the polytheists who say false things of Him, and no one will help them when He does, since nothing can overcome Him. 'He is the Hearing, the Knowing': He hears the slander and lies they speak of Him and knows what they hide and what they declare, and has counted it all against them, and He lies in wait for them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 142}

The particle 'inna' in 'all might belongs to God' is vowelled with kasra because it is a fresh report from God and 'saying' does not govern it: the saying meant is the polytheists' saying, and 'all might belongs to God' was not said by the polytheists nor reported as said by them. The editor refers back to the earlier commentaries on grief, might, triumph, the Hearing and the Knowing, and to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an on the place of 'inna' after 'saying' and the like. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 142}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
