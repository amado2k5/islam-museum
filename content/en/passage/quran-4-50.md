## Summary
Verse 50 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 50 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The sense of looking at the fabrication and the exposed offence (p. 460, report 9763, notes 1-2).** Al-Tabari says: look, Muhammad, how those among the People of the Book who purify themselves, who say they are God's sons and beloved and that only a Jew or Christian enters paradise and who claim they have no sins, invent falsehood and lies in speech against God; and their saying this lie is enough, an offence laid bare, that is, it exposes their lying to whoever hears it and shows them to be liars and wrongdoers. Al-Qasim (9763, from al-Husayn, from Hajjaj) reports from Ibn Jurayj that the Jews and Christians are meant. The editor's notes refer to the explanation of the verse's words in the language indexes and to the earlier explanations of seeing with the heart (p. 452) and of the "share" (p. 427). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 460}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 460}
