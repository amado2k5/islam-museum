## Summary
Malik transmits that it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab, Ibn Shihab and Sulayman ibn Yasar used to say that the waiting period of a woman who redeems herself is three menstrual periods. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.91}
