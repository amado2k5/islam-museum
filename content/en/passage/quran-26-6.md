## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They have lied, so there will come to them the news of what they used to mock about": O Muhammad, these polytheists denied the reminder that came to them from Allah and turned away from it. "Then there will come to them the news of what they used to mock," meaning: then the news of the matter they used to mock will come to them, and that is a warning from Allah that His punishment will come upon them for their persistence in disbelief and rebellion against their Lord.
