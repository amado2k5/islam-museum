## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "As for the wall, it belonged to two orphan boys in the city, and beneath it was a treasure for them. Their father was righteous. So your Lord intended that they reach maturity and extract their treasure as a mercy from your Lord. And what you did about my command is an interpretation of what you could not bear patiently over it." Allah Almighty says, referring to the saying of the companion of Moses: As for the wall that You erected, it belonged to two orphan boys in the city, and beneath it was their dwelling. Interpreters differed regarding this treasure; some said: It was manuscripts containing buried knowledge. In the excavation, statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Jurayj.
