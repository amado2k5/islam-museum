## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Blessed is He to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, and with Him belongs the knowledge of the Hour, and to Him you will be returned": He says, Exalted is He, and blessed is He who has dominion over the seven heavens and the earth and everything between them. His judgment is in effect over all of that, and His decree is in effect. He says: How can he have a partner who has a share in his authority and rule in it, and with Him is the knowledge of the Hour? He means: And He has knowledge of the Hour on which the Day of Judgment will be established, and people will be gathered from their graves for the Day of Reckoning. His saying: "And to Him you will be returned" means: "And to Him you will be returned after your death, and you will return to Him." He will reward the doer of good with his kindness, and the wrongdoer with his evil.
