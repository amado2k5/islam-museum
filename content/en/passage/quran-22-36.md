## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And the body, We have made it for you among the symbols of Allah; you will have good in it. So remember the name of Allah with wool. And when it is obligatory on its side, eat from it and feed the contented and the poor. Thus We have subjected it to you that you may be grateful": "And the body" is the plural of 'badna', and its singular may be called 'badna', and if it is said, it can be plural and singular, indicating that it may be said to the singular. The saying of the poet who says: 'When you have affairs, I fast for months and fulfill vows and shave my head.' Wafiya is braided and a body is armored, and the body is the largest of all. For this reason, it was said to Imri' al-Qais ibn al-Nu'man, the owner of Al-Khurang and Al-Sudair: the body, because of its large size and the relaxation of its flesh, so it is said: it has become uncovered. The meaning of the statement: The camels are large and large bodies, which We have made for you, O people, as a symbol of Allah. He says: Among the flags of Allah's command which He has commanded you in your Hajj rituals, when you imitate, groom, and make them hairy, he knew this, and he felt that you did this from camels and cattle. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Hasan, Ibrahim, and Ata.
