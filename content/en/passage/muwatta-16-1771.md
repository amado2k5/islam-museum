## Summary
Malik transmits that he asked Ibrahim ibn Uqba about fosterage. Said ibn al-Musayyab said: whatever is within the two years forbids, even a single drop; what comes after the two years is only food one eats. Ibrahim said he then asked Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, who said the same as Said ibn al-Musayyab. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.122}
