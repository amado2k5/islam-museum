## Summary
A report of Abu Qatada al-Ansari in the chapter on the merit of fasting three days each month, the day of Arafat, Ashura and Monday, in the Book of Fasting of Sahih Muslim. In Shu'ba's wording, the answer about fasting on Monday is "that is the day on which I was born, and on which I was sent or on which it was revealed to me". {cite:sahih-muslim}
