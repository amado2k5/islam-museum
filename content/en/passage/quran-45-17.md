## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We gave them clear proofs of the matter, but they did not differ except after knowledge had come to them out of enmity among themselves. Indeed, your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection concerning that over which they used to differ." And We gave the Children of Israel clear manifestations of Our command to reveal the Torah to them, detailing everything: "So they differed only after knowledge had come to them out of enmity among themselves," seeking leadership, and leaving them to clarify God, the Blessed and Exalted, in His revelation. And His saying: "Indeed, your Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection concerning that over which they used to differ," Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: Indeed, your Lord, O Muhammad, will judge between those who differ among the Children of Israel out of enmity among them on the Day of Resurrection, regarding what they used to differ in this world after the knowledge given to them and the clarity from which it came to them. Thus, the one who is right will be judged by the one who dissolves the judgment between them.
