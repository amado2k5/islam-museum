## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when they see you that they will take you except as a messenger, they will mock you." And when those polytheists whose stories you have narrated to you see you, "if they take you as nothing but a mockery," they say: they will only take you as a mockery and mock you, saying: "Is this the one whose stories Allah has sent to us as a messenger from among His creation?"
