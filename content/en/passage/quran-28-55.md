## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when they hear idle talk, turn away from it and say, 'We have our deeds and you have your deeds.' Peace be upon you; we do not seek the ignorant." And when these people to whom We gave the Book hear idle talk, which is falsehood of speech, as in Qatadah: "And when they hear idle talk, they turn away from it and say, 'Our deeds and you have your deeds.' Peace be upon you; we do not seek the ignorant," they do not match the people of ignorance and falsehood in their falsehood. What has come to them from Allah's command is what protects them from that. Others said: What I mean by idle talk here is what the People of the Book added to the Book of God, which is not from it. In the section, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
