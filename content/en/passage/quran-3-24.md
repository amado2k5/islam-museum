## Verse 24 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, pp. 292-293)

Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-24|3:24]]) says the point is that these people, called to God's Book so that it might judge between them in truth when they disputed in the presence of the Messenger, refused to accept the ruling of the Torah and what truth it holds because of their saying that the Fire would touch them only for a short counted period, which he takes to be forty days, the days in which they worshipped the calf, after which their Lord would bring them out. They were deceived by what they invented of lies and falsehoods: their claim to be God's sons and beloved ones, and that God had promised their father Jacob that he would put none of his descendants into the Fire except for the expiation of an oath. God declared all this false and told his prophet Muhammad that they are the people of the Fire, abiding in it, to the exclusion of those who believe in God, his messengers and what they brought from him.

In his notes the editor says the phrase 'because of their saying' explains the sense of the opening 'because', referring to the earlier treatment in volume 2 (p. 139) and to the earlier gloss of the counted days in volume 2 (pp. 274-278). He explains the oath-expiation phrase as what one does to atone for an oath; one says 'he did it only to the extent of releasing his oath', meaning no more than the minimum, so the sense is that the Fire would touch them only slightly, as much as releases the one who swore. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 292}

Al-Tabari then says the interpreters spoke in the same way. From Qatada (6786, through Bishr from Yazid from Sa'id): they said the Fire would touch them only for the time in which they set up the calf, after which the oath and the punishment would end for them; and what deceived them was their fabrication, that is, their saying that they are God's sons and beloved. From al-Rabi' (6787, through al-Muthanna from Ishaq from Abd Allah ibn Abi Ja'far from his father): they said they would be punished only forty days, meaning the Jews; he adds that Qatada said the same, and that these are the days in which they set up the calf. From Mujahid (6788, through al-Qasim from al-Husayn from Hajjaj from Ibn Jurayj): what deceived them was their saying about the duration of the Fire. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 293}
