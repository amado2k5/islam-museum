## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, sufficient is Allah between me and you, a witness who knows what is in the heavens and the earth, and those who believe in falsehood and disbelieve in Allah. Those are the losers." Say, O Muhammad, to those who say to you: Why was not a sign sent down to you from your Lord, the deniers of Our signs from your people: Allah is sufficient between me and you as a witness for me and against me, for He knows what is right among us from the falsehood, and He knows what is in the heavens and what is on the earth, nothing about them is hidden from Him. He is the metaphor for each of us according to what is rightfully deserved, the one who is justified in his steadfastness on The truth, and the falsehood is what it deserves. "And those who believe in falsehood" means: they believed in polytheism, acknowledged it, and disbelieved in it. He says: They denied Allah, "Those are the losers," meaning: they are the ones who were wronged in their deal.
