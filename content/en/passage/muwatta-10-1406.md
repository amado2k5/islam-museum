## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' from a man of the Ansar, from Mu'adh ibn Sa'd or Sa'd ibn Mu'adh: a slave-girl of Ka'b ibn Malik was herding her sheep at Sal', and one sheep was struck. She reached it and slaughtered it with a stone. The Messenger of God was asked about it, and said there is no harm in it, eat it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.631}
