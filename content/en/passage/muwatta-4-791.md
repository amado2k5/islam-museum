## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala al-Aslami, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, as a relayed report, that the Prophet said: people will remain well so long as they hasten to break the fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik, and that the same hadith was earlier given connected from Sahl ibn Saad. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.389}
