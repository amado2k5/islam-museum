## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa that Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr stayed in Mecca nine years, calling out for the pilgrimage at the new moon of Dhu al-Hijja, and Urwa ibn al-Zubayr did the same with him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1084) and Suwayd (500) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.456}
