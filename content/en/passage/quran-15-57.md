## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 57 to 60 in one passage: Abraham said to the angels, "So what has happened to you, O messengers," meaning what is your affair and what your command is. The angels said, "Indeed, We have sent to a people criminals," meaning a people who have acquired disbelief in God. "Except the family of Lot; indeed, we are all their salvation": except the followers of Lot according to their religion; they will not destroy them, but rather save them from the punishment with which they were commanded to punish the people of Lot. "Except his wife, We decreed that she is of the remnants": God decreed that she is among the remaining, then she is destroyed. He has previously explained the meaning of the absent with his evidence.
