## Summary
A report of Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Hudhayfa held his tongue, but when he saw a man reporting people's words to the authorities, he made him hear it so that he would be corrected. {cite:sahih-muslim}
