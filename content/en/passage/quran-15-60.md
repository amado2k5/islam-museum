## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Except his wife, We decreed that she is of the transient," meaning the angels excluded Lot's wife from salvation, for God decreed that she is among those who remain in destruction and then is doomed; he mentions that he explained the meaning of "the past" in the past with its evidence.
