## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And to those who were Jews, We forbade you what We had related to you before, nor did We wrong them, but they wronged themselves." And We forbade the Jews before you, O Muhammad, what We informed you before in Surah Al-An'am, and that is all of the victorious cattle and sheep, We forbade them from fat, except what their backs carry, the enclosures, or what mixes with bones. "And We did not wrong them" by forbidding that for them, "but they wronged themselves," so We recompensed them for their transgression against their Lord and wronging themselves by disobeying God, which brought them God's punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah, Hasan, and Ikrimah.
