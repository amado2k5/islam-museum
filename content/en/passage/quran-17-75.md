## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "So We made you taste the weakness of life and the weakness of death, then you will find no helper against us": If you had given these polytheists a little while in what they asked you, then We would have made you taste the weakness of the torment of life and the weakness of death. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
