**Commentary on verse 75 (pp. 154-155)**

On [[passage/quran-10-75|10:75]] al-Tabari says: then, after those messengers We sent to their peoples after Noah, We sent Moses and Aaron, the sons of Imran, to the Pharaoh of Egypt and his chiefs, meaning the nobles and masters of his people, 'with Our signs', that is, Our proofs of the truth of what they called them to, submission to God in servanthood and acknowledging the two as messengers. 'They were arrogant' and refused to acknowledge what Moses and Aaron called them to, 'and they were a criminal people', meaning sinful toward their Lord by their disbelief in God. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'the chiefs' and 'arrogance', and says the phrase 'sinful toward their Lord' recurs in al-Tabari and was explained earlier. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 155}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
