**Commentary on verse 60 (pp. 155-156)**

On "if you do not bring him to me, you shall have no measure from me, and do not come near me", reporting Joseph's words to his brothers, al-Tabari says: if you do not bring me your brother from your father, you have no food with me that I will measure out to you, "and do not come near" means do not come near my country. The word "tuqrabun" is in the jussive of prohibition, and the nun stands in the place of the accusative pronoun, vowelled with a kasra because its ya' was dropped; the sense is "do not come near me". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 155}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
