## Summary
A report transmitted by Yahya from Malik that it reached him that the Messenger of God, when he finished his circumambulation of the House and prayed the two rak'as, and wanted to go out to al-Safa and al-Marwa, touched the Black Corner before he went out. The editor notes that this is part of the long hadith of Jabir that Muslim and others transmitted from Malik and others. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.491}
