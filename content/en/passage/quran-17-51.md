## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 52 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, be stones or iron": Say, O Muhammad, to those who deny resurrection after death among your people who say, "If We were bones and remains, then We will surely be sent as a new creation." If you like God's will and His return to your bodies, be a new creation after your affliction in the dust, and your destiny is remains, and you deny that from His power of stones or iron, or creation that grows in your chests if you are able. Then I will revive you and resurrect you as a new creation after your destiny, just as I began you the first time. The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase "or a creation of what grows in your breasts," some said: it means death, and what is meant is: or be death, for if you are your nation and then I will resurrect you after that on the Day of Resurrection. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Jurayj, and Atiyyah.
