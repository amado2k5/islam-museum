## Summary
Malik said of a Muslim slave who wounds a Jew or a Christian: the master of the slave may, if he wishes, pay the blood-money of his wound on his behalf, or hand the slave over to be sold; the Jew or Christian is paid from the price of the slave the blood-money of his wound, or his full price if it exceeds it. A Jew or a Christian may not be given a Muslim slave. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.434}
