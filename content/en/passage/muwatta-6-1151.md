## Summary
The Messenger of God said, as it reached Malik: all of Arafat is a place of standing; rise above the valley of Urana; and all of Muzdalifa is a place of standing; rise above the valley of Muhassir. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the hadith is connected from Jabir, Ibn Abbas and Ali. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.521}
