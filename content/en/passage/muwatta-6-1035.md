## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar disliked that one in ihram should remove a tick or a flea from his camel. Malik said: that is the most beloved to me of what I heard on this. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1193), Suwayd (582) and al-Shaybani (432) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.482}
