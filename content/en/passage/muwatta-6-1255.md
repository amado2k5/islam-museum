## Summary
Malik says that whoever plucks hair from his nose or armpit, depilates his body with a depilatory, or shaves a wound on his head out of necessity, or shaves the back of his neck where the cupping cups go, while in ihram, forgetful or ignorant, owes a ransom for all of that, and he should not shave the place of the cupping cups. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.559}
