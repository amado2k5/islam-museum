## In al-Tabari's commentary
On "But those who disbelieve and deny Our signs, those are the companions of the Fire, abiding in it" al-Tabari says those meant are the ones who rejected God's signs and denied His messengers; God's signs are His proofs and evidences for His oneness and lordship and what the messengers brought of tokens and witnesses; and disbelief means covering. He glosses "companions of the Fire" as its people, who abide in it forever. He gives the report from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri that the Prophet said that the people of the Fire who are truly its people neither die in it nor live, but some groups whom the Fire touched for their sins die a death until they are charcoal, and then intercession is permitted; the editor traces it to Muslim, Ibn Majah and Ahmad and comments on the narrators. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 552}
