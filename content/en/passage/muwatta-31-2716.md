## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Aisha, that when the Messenger of God was ill he recited the protective surahs over himself and breathed on himself. When his pain grew severe, she would recite over him and wipe him with his right hand, hoping for its blessing. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.531}
