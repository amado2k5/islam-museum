## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki that he saw Abdullah ibn Abbas circumambulate after the afternoon prayer, then enter his chamber, and he did not know what he did. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1298), Suwayd (555) and Yahya ibn Bukayr (in al-Bayhaqi) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.495}
