## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmala al-Aslami that a man asked Said ibn al-Musayyab: may I make the lesser pilgrimage before I make the pilgrimage? The editor notes that the answer is on page 461, which is not included in this batch, and that Abu Musab (1105) and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.460}
