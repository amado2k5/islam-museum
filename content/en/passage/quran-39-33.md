## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 33 to 34 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And whoever brings the truth and believes in it—those are the righteous": the interpreters differ regarding what is true and what is believed in it, and so on. Some said: The one who brought the truth is the Messenger of God. They said: The truthful one who brought it: There is no god but Allah, and the one who also believed in it is the Messenger of God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
