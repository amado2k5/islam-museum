## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 131 to 135 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So fear Allah and obey": Allah Almighty says, referring to the saying of Hud to his people from 'Ad: Fear the punishment of Allah, O people, by obeying Him in what He commands you and forbids you, and refrain from amusement and play, oppressing people, and oppressing them with dominance and corruption on earth. Beware of the wrath of the One who gave you from Him what you know and helped you with it from among livestock, children, orchards, and rivers: "Indeed, I fear for you the punishment of the Day" from Allah, "Great."
