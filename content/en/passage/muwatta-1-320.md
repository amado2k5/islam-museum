## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Sa'id from Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Habban from Ibn Muhayriz, in the chapter on the command to pray the witr: a man of the Banu Kinana called al-Mukhdaji heard a man in Syria, called Abu Muhammad, say the witr was obligatory. He went to Ubada ibn al-Samit and told him, and Ubada said Abu Muhammad was wrong. He reported from the Prophet that God wrote five prayers on his servants, and whoever brings them and does not neglect any of them out of contempt for their rights has a covenant with God that He will admit him to Paradise, while whoever does not bring them has no covenant with God: if He wills He punishes him and if He wills He admits him to Paradise. The editor says the chain is weak because al-Mukhdaji is unknown, but it is sound through al-Sunabihi from Ubada in Ahmad, Abu Dawud and al-Bayhaqi, and Ibn Abd al-Barr said the hadith is sound and established since it comes from Ubada through many sound channels other than al-Mukhdaji. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.181} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.182}
