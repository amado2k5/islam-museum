## Summary
It reached Malik that Abdullah ibn Masud used to say: a servant keeps on lying, and a black spot is marked on his heart until his heart is blackened, and he is recorded with God among the liars. The editor notes that this is a halted report, but its ruling is raised, since opinion has no place in it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.589}
