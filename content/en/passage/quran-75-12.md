## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 12 to 19 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "Nay, man desires to continue in his sin" as meaning that the son of Adam is unaware that his Lord is capable of gathering his bones, yet he desires to persist in disobeying God, with nothing deterring him, never repenting, and always postponing repentance. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
