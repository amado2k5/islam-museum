## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that he heard Said ibn al-Musayyab say: when you come to a land where people give full measure and weight, stay there long; when you come to a land where they cut short measure and weight, stay there briefly. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.218}
