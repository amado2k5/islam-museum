## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm, from someone who told him, that Abu Hurayra used to say, in the chapter on dress, stepping over necks and facing the imam on Friday: it is better for one of you to pray at the foot of the lava field outside Medina than to sit until the imam stands to preach and then come stepping over the necks of people on Friday. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.167}
