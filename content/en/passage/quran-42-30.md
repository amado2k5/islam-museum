## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 31 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And whatever disaster befalls you is because of what your hands have earned, and you pardon much": "And whatever affliction befalls you in this world, concerning yourselves, your families, and your wealth," it says: "But that befalls you is a punishment from Allah for what you have committed between yourselves and your Lord, and your Lord will pardon much of your sins, so He will not punish you accordingly." In this passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
