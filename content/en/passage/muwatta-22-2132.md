## Summary
Yahya said: Malik told us, from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that the Messenger said: the pledge is not foreclosed. The editor notes that what Mamar ibn Isa transmitted connected from Abu Hurayra is an error, and the mursal is more sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.271}
