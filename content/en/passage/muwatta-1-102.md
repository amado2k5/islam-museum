## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' in the chapter on ablution after touching the private parts: Abdullah ibn Umar said that whoever touches his penis must perform ablution. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted a similar wording by another channel, and Ibn Abi Shayba and al-Tahawi transmitted from Nafi' that Ibn Umar repeated his ablution whenever he touched his private parts. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.86}
