## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Ali ibn al-Husayn said the Prophet, when he wanted to travel by day, combined noon and afternoon, and when he wanted to travel by night, combined sunset and night, in the chapter on combining two prayers at home and on a journey. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it, and refers to the earlier hadiths on this chapter from al-A'raj, Mu'adh, Ibn Umar and Ibn Abbas. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.208}
