## Summary
Malik transmits from Wahb ibn Kaysan, from Jabir ibn Abdallah, who said: the Messenger of God sent an army toward the coast, under Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah, three hundred men, and Jabir was among them. When they were on part of the road, their provisions ran out. Abu Ubayda gathered the army's provisions, and there was a two-sack bag of dates; he gave them a little each day until it ran out, and they had only one date each. Then they reached the sea, where there was a fish like a hill; the army ate from it for eighteen nights. Then Abu Ubayda ordered two of its ribs raised, and a camel was led under them without touching them. Malik said: al-zarib is a small hill. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.519}
