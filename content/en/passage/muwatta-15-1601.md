## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say that any man who makes ila against his wife is stopped once the four months pass, until he divorces or returns. No divorce falls on him when the four months pass before he is stopped. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.65}
