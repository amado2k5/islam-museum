**Commentary on verse 5 (pp. 558-559)**

On [[passage/quran-12-5|12:5]] al-Tabari says: Jacob said to his son Joseph 'my son, do not relate your dream to your brothers' lest they envy you and 'plot a plot against you', that is, seek ill for you, set up enmity and obey Satan against you; 'Satan is a clear enemy to man' means Satan is an enemy to Adam and his children, who has made his enmity plain and shown it; so beware lest Satan incite your brothers against you in envy if you relate your dream to them. Jacob said so because he had before seen envy in them; report 18788, from al-Suddi: Jacob settled in Syria and his concern was Joseph and his brother, so his brothers envied him when they saw their father's love for him; Joseph saw in a dream eleven stars with the sun and moon prostrating to him and told his father, who said 'my son, do not relate your dream to your brothers'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 558}

The grammarians differ on the reason for the lam in 'fa-yakidu laka kaydan': a Basran said it means 'they would make a plot for you' and is not like 'if you can interpret dreams' in [[passage/quran-12-43|12:43]], which wished to join the verb to it by lam as one joins with ba', as you say 'I offered him food' meaning to him, as in [[passage/quran-12-48|12:48]] 'they will eat what you laid up for them' and [[passage/quran-10-35|10:35]] 'say, God guides to the truth'; if you wish 'plot for you' means 'plot against you', the lam like that in 'it is their Lord they fear' in [[passage/quran-7-154|7:154]]. Another said the lam was brought in as in 'I thanked you' and 'I praised you', a lam the verb brings, and 'kaydan' is an emphasis. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 559}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
