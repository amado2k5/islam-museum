## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar did not wash his head while in ihram except for a wet dream. The editor notes that Abu Musab and al-Shaybani (472) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.436}
