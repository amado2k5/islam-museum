## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “When the hypocrites come to you, they say, ‘We bear witness that you are indeed the Messenger of Allah.’ And Allah knows that you are indeed His Messenger, and Allah bears witness that the hypocrites are liars,” explains: “When the hypocrites come to you, O Muhammad, they say,” with their tongues, “We bear witness that you are indeed the Messenger of Allah, and Allah knows that you are indeed His Messenger.” Whether the hypocrites say this or not, “and Allah bears witness that the hypocrites are liars.” He says: And Allah bears witness that the hypocrites are liars in their claim that they bear witness that you are indeed the Messenger of Allah. To the Messenger of God, because she does not believe in that nor does she have faith in it, so they are lying in their report about her. And some of the Arabs said regarding His saying: “And God bears witness that the hypocrites are liars,” that their consciences lied because they concealed hypocrisy, so just as He did not accept their faith, even though they outwardly professed it, so He made them liars because they concealed something other than what they outwardly professed.
