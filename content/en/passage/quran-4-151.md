**Commentary on verse 151: truly unbelievers and the humiliating punishment (p. 354)**

Al-Tabari says these are those who disbelieve in God, who deserve His punishment and eternal dwelling in the fire; the believers should not be deceived by their false claim that they affirm the books and messengers, for they are liars in that claim. The humiliating punishment is in the Hereafter, and 'humiliating' means it humbles whoever suffers it by his eternal dwelling in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 354}
