## Summary
A report of al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an al-Ansari in the chapter on righteousness and sin in the Book of Righteousness of Sahih Muslim. It defines righteousness as good character and sin as what troubles the heart and one dislikes others seeing. It is one of the most comprehensive definitions of the Prophet. {cite:sahih-muslim}
