## Summary
Verse 81 of [[passage/quran-9|Surat at-Tawba]], a Medinan surah. Scholars connect its revelation to [[event/hypocrites-discourage-marching-in-the-heat|Hypocrites Discourage Marching in the Heat]]. {cite:tanzil-quran} {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 81 of 129 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 81 (pp. 397-400)**

Al-Tabari says: those whom God held back from the campaign with His Messenger and the believers in him and from striving against His enemies rejoiced "in their staying behind, opposing the Messenger of God", meaning in their sitting in their houses in opposition to the Messenger of God's sitting and staying, for the Messenger of God ordered them to go out to strive against God's enemies and they opposed his command and sat in their houses. The editor refers to earlier explanations of sitting. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 397}

The word for opposition is a verbal noun from the saying "so-and-so opposed so-and-so, he opposes him in opposition", so its verbal noun came in the pattern of the noun of fighting, as one says "he fought him, he fights him in fighting"; had it been a verbal noun from the plain verb "to follow behind", the reading would have been the other form meaning "behind the Messenger of God", since the verbal noun of that verb is not the one given, but being, as I have shown, the verbal noun of the verb "oppose", it was read "in opposition to the Messenger of God", which is the reading of the reciters of the great cities and is the correct one in our view. Some have interpreted it as meaning "after the Messenger of God", citing the line of the poet al-Harith ibn Khalid al-Makhzumi, a verse in which he describes the rain that came after the people of the dwellings as though the women who strip palm leaves had spread a mat between them; and that is close to the sense we gave, since they sat after him in opposition to him. The editor notes that this is Abu Ubayda's view in Majaz al-Qur'an and gives Abu al-Faraj's explanation in al-Aghani of the line and of the words for "succeeded" and "strippers". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 398}

"And they disliked to strive with their wealth and their persons in God's way", the Almighty says: these who were held back disliked to fight the unbelievers with their wealth and their persons "in God's way", meaning in God's religion which He laid down for His servants so that they might support it, from inclination to ease and comfort, preferring rest to toil and hardship and from miserliness over spending wealth in obedience to God. "And they said, do not go out in the heat", for the Prophet had called them to this campaign, the campaign of Tabuk, in severe heat, and the hypocrites said to one another "do not go out in the heat", and God said to His Prophet Muhammad "say" to them, Muhammad, "the Fire of Hell", which God prepared for whoever opposed His command and disobeyed His Messenger, "is hotter" than this heat about which you urge each other not to go out; the Almighty says that what is hotter is the more fitting to be feared and guarded against than what is less harmful of the two; "if they understood", that is, if these hypocrites understood from God His admonition and pondered the verses of His book, but they do not understand from God, so they guard against the lesser, lighter harm of the heat and fall into the most grievous and heaviest affliction for whoever burns in it. The editor refers to earlier explanations of striving, God's way, going out and understanding. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 399}

Report 17033, from Ibn Abbas, through Muhammad ibn Sa'd, his father, his uncle and his father from his father: that was because the Messenger of God ordered the people to follow him, in summer, and men said "Messenger of God, the heat is severe and we cannot go out, so do not go out in the heat", and God said "say, the Fire of Hell is hotter if only they understood", and He ordered him to go out. Report 17034, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: "in their staying behind, opposing the Messenger of God", he said it is the Tabuk campaign. Report 17035, from Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi and others, through al-Harith, Abd al-Aziz and Abu Ma'shar: the Messenger of God went out in severe heat to Tabuk, and a man of Banu Salima said "do not go out in the heat", and God sent down "say, the Fire of Hell", the verse. Report 17036, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: then he mentioned what some of them said to others when the Messenger of God ordered the striving and resolved on the march to Tabuk, in the severity of the heat and the dryness of the country, God, glorious is His praise, saying "and they said, do not go out in the heat; say, the Fire of Hell is hotter". The editor notes that this report follows report 17012 in Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 400}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
