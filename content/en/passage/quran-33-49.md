## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, when you marry believing women and then divorce them before you have touched them, then you have no waiting period to perform before you have touched them, so let them enjoy and release them in a beautiful manner." This means: "O you who have spoken the truth to Allah and His Messenger, when you marry believing women and then divorce them before you have touched them," meaning before you have intercourse with them, "then you have no waiting period over them," meaning: from counting the Qur'an and not counting the months you count against them, "then take them to temporary duration," meaning: Give them what they enjoy in terms of offer or money. And His saying: "and they released them with a beautiful release" means: and they left their way to be freed in kindness, which is beautiful release. In the Maqta', he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
