## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of Bara'a (at-Tawba) 9:74, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Companion generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «يحلفون بالله ما قالوا».
