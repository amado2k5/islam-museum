## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Glorify Allah whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and He is the Mighty, the Wise": it means by His saying, Glorified be He, "Glorify Allah," he prays to Allah and prostrates to Him "whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth" of His creation, "and He is the Mighty, the Wise." He means that He is the Mighty in taking revenge on those who took revenge on His creation for their disobedience to Him, the Wise in managing them.
