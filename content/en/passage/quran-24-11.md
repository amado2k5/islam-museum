## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who came with slander are a group among you, do not think it bad for you; rather, it is better for every man among them what he has earned of sin. And whoever takes his arrogance from among them will have a great punishment": Those who came with lies and slander, "a group from you," he means: a group of you, O people. "Do not think it bad for you; rather, it is good for you," meaning: Do not think that what they brought of the slander is bad for you in the eyes of Allah and the people; rather, it is better for you in the eyes of Him and the believers, for Allah makes it an expiation for the one who is accused of it, shows His innocence from what was slandered, and provides a way out for him. It is said that what Allah meant by His saying: "Indeed, those who came with the slander are a group from among you" is a group, among them Hassan ibn Thabit, Mustah ibn Athatha, and Hamnah bint Jahsh. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Zayd.
