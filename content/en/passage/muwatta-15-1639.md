## Summary
Malik says it reached him that Said ibn al-Musayyab, Sulayman ibn Yasar, and Ibn Shihab used to say that the waiting period of a woman who redeemed herself is the same as that of a divorced woman: three menstrual periods. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.76}
