**Commentary on verse 45 (pp. 574-574)**

Al-Tabari says on "you who believe, when you meet a party, stand firm and remember God much, that you may prosper" [[passage/quran-8-45|8:45]]: this is God's making known to the people of faith in Him the conduct in war with His enemies who disbelieve in Him and the acts by which they may hope for victory over them and success, so He says to them: you who believed God and His Messenger, when you meet a body of the unbelievers in God for war and fighting, stand firm to fight them and do not be routed from them or turn your backs fleeing, except turning to fight or joining a party of yours; "remember God much" means call on God for victory over them and success against them, and let your hearts and tongues be full of remembering Him; "that you may prosper" means so that you succeed and win over your enemy and God grants you victory and success against them. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "party" and "prospering". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 574}

Report 16161, from Qatada: God made remembering Him obligatory at the busiest time, in the striking of swords; report 16162, from Ibn Ishaq: when you meet a party who fight you in God's path, stand firm and remember God to whom you gave your lives, and keeping faith with what you pledged in your allegiance, as in the Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 574}
