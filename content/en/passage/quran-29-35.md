## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We have left from it a clear sign for a people who reason": And We have preserved from Our deeds that We did to them a sign, meaning: a clear lesson and a warning for a people who understand from God His arguments and reflect on His admonitions. That clear verse is, to me, the pardon of their effects and lessons in their meanings. He mentioned from Qatadah about this: And from Qatadah, "And We have certainly left from it a clear sign for a people who reason," he said: it refers to the stones that rained down upon them. And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, the phrase "from it is a clear verse," he said: a lesson. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
