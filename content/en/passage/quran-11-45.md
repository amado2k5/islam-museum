**Commentary on verse 45 (pp. 339-340)**

On [[passage/quran-11-45|11:45]] al-Tabari says: Noah called to his Lord and said, Lord, You promised me that You would save me from drowning and destruction, and my family, and my son has perished, and my son is of my family; 'and Your promise is true', that which has no breach, 'and You are the most just of judges' in truth, so judge for me that You fulfil what You promised me, that You save my family and return my son to me. Report 18207, from Ibn Zayd: the most just of judges in truth. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 340}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
