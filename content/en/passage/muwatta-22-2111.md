## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Jafar ibn Muhammad, from his father, that the Messenger judged with an oath alongside a single witness. The editor notes that some transmitted it from Malik connected from Jabir's report, but the mursal version from Malik is sound in his view. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.263}
