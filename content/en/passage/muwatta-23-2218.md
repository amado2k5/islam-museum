## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: the agreed practice among us is that the weak-minded, the foolish and the afflicted one who recovers at times, their bequests are valid, if they have enough of their reason to know what they bequeath. As for the one who does not have enough of his reason to know what he bequeaths, and whose reason is overcome, he has no bequest. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.311}
