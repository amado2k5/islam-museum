## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who avoid the major sins and immorals except for sins. Indeed, your Lord is All-Encompassing in Forgiveness. He is most knowing of you when He created you from the earth, and when you were fetuses in your mothers' wombs, so do not purify yourselves. He is most knowing of who is righteous." Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: "Indeed, your Lord, O Muhammad, is all-forgiving": His forgiveness is vast for sinners whose sins have not reached the graves and major sins of sins. Only the Most Knowing, Glorified and Exalted, by His saying of His servants, is that He forgives the sins We have described for those who avoid major sins and immoralities. Also: Ibn Zayd said regarding His saying: "Indeed, your Lord is vast in forgiveness," He has forgiven them. And His saying: "He knows best about you when He brought you forth from the earth," He, the Exalted, says: Your Lord knows better the believer among you than the disbeliever, the doer of good from the wrongdoer, the obedient from the sinner, when He created you from the earth, and He spoke to you about the creation of your father Adam from it, and when you were embryos in your mothers' wombs, He says: And when you were conceived, you were not born from yourselves, and your souls after you became men and women. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
