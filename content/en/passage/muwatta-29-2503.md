## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say: Ibn Shihab did not hold that. I do not hold, regarding a penetrating wound in a limb of the body, an agreed matter; I think there is judgment in it, which the imam judges. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.429}
