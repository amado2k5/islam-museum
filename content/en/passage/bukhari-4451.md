## Summary
In the Book of Campaigns: Aisha reports the last moments of his death: he looked to the sky and repeated "with the Highest Companion", answering the choice that had been offered him, and he was buried in her house where he died. It is one of the best-known accounts of his death. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
