## Summary
Malik says it reached him from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: dinar for dinar, dirham for dirham, sa' for sa', and a deferred debt is not sold for a present one. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.160}
