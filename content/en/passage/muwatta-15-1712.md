## Summary
Malik says that it reached him that Abdullah ibn Masud used to say, of a man who says that every woman he marries is divorced, that if he does not name a tribe or a particular woman, nothing binds him. Malik said this is the best of what he has heard. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.98}
