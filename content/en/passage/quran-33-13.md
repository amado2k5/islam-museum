## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And [mention] when a group of them said, 'O people of Yathrib, there is no place for you,' so turn back. And a group of them asked permission to the Prophet, saying, 'Indeed, our houses are 'amud' and 'they are not 'amudah'; they only want to flee.": Allah means by saying: "And [mention] when a group of them said, 'O people of Yathrib, there is no place for you.'" And when some of them said: 'O people of Yathrib,' Yathrib is the name of a land, and it is said: The city of the Messenger of Allah is in the region of Yathrib. And His saying: 'There is no place for you, so return' with a fatha on the 'm' from a station. He says: There is no place for you to stand in, as the poet said: 'So whoever is evil and who is going to return to the station, he does not see it.' The phrase 'so return' means: 'So return to your homes.' He ordered them to flee from the army of the Messenger of God and flee from it, and to leave the Messenger of God. It is also said: This is from what was said by Aws ibn Qaydhi and those who agreed with his opinion. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
