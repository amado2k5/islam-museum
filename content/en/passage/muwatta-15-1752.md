## Summary
Malik says it reached him that Salim ibn Abdallah and Sulayman ibn Yasar used to say of a widow who fears for her sight from an eye inflammation or a sore: she may apply kohl and treat herself with medicine or kohl, even if it contains perfume. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.115}
