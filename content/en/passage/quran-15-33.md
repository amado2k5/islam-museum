## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads verses 33 to 35 in one passage: Iblis said, "I would not prostrate to a human being whom He created from clay from aged clay"—and he is of clay, and I am of fire, and fire eats clay. God said to him, "Then get out of it," meaning from the heavens, "for you are accursed." The cursed is derived from the object to the fa'il, meaning the cursed — according to Qatadah the accursed person, and from Ibn Jurayj cursed and stoning in the Qur'an is insult. And "Indeed, upon you is the curse until the Day of Judgment": God is angry with you by expelling you from the heavens and expelling you from them until the Day of Judgment, which is the Day of Judgment. He has explained the meaning of the curse in several places.
