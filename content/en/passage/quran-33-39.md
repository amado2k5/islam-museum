## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Those who convey the messages of Allah and fear Him and do not fear anyone except Allah. And sufficient is Allah as an Accountant": Allah's way regarding those who passed away before Muhammad among the messengers, who deliver Allah's messages to those to whom they were sent, and they fear Allah in neglecting to deliver them to them, and they fear no one except Allah, for they fear Him if they fail to deliver Allah's message to those to whom they were sent. He says to His Prophet Muhammad: Among those messengers whose description is this, so be and fear no one except Allah, for Allah prevents you from all His creation, and no one of His creation prevents you from him if He intends you harm. "And those who" from His saying: "those who convey Allah's messages" is a reduction in response to the "that" in His saying: "Allah's way concerning those who have left Allah" and His saying: "And sufficient is Allah as an Accountant." Allah says: And sufficient is Allah, O Muhammad, as a guardian of the deeds of His creation and an accountant for them.
