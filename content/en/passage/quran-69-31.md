## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 36 as one section. He explains His saying, “My wealth did not avail me”: God Almighty says, informing about what was said by the one who was given his book in his left hand: “My wealth did not avail me,” meaning that his wealth, which he possessed in this world, did not avert anything from God’s punishment. In the section, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
