## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And the cattle He created for you therein warmth and benefits, and from them you eat": Among His arguments against you, O people, is what He created for you of livestock, so He subjected them to you, and made for you from their wool, their hair, and their hair, garments for warmth and benefits from their milk and backs to ride. "And from them you eat"—he means: among the livestock are those whose meat you eat, such as camels, cattle, sheep, and all that whose meat is eaten. The word "ma" was omitted from the speech to indicate who is on it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and others.
