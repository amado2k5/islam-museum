## Summary
Malik transmits from Thawr ibn Zayd al-Dili, from Ikrima, client of Ibn Abbas, who said that he did not think it from anyone but Abdullah ibn Abbas, that whoever had intercourse with his wife before dispersing should make the lesser pilgrimage and offer. The editor notes that this report is as it is stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.517}
