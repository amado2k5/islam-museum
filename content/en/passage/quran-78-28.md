## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 32 as a single section. He explains the phrase “a fitting recompense”: This punishment with which these disbelievers were punished in the Hereafter was inflicted upon them by their Lord as recompense, meaning: a reward for their evil deeds and words which they committed in this world. It is derived from the expression: “This punishment was in perfect harmony with this knowledge.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, al-Rabi’, and Sufyan.
