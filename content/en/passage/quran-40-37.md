## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 37 to 38 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Pharaoh said, 'Yahman, son of Lay, a building for me with the most conveying causes.'" And Pharaoh said when the believer advised him from his family with what he advised him to do, and warned him against killing Moses, the Prophet of God, and warned him of God's might for his saying, 'Kill him,' as he warned his minister Haman: "O Haman, build me a building for me, so that I may have the most comprehensive causes," meaning constructive. We have explained the meaning of the edifice in the past with its evidence, which is necessary to repeat it here. "Perhaps the most conveyive of the causes" The interpreters differed on the meaning of the causes in this context, and some said: the causes of the heavens are their routes. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
