## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 55–60. Al-Tabari explains that after describing the pious, God informs about the tyrants that they have a terrible return: Hell they will burn. So woe to this mulch; let them taste it, hot and rainy, and another of its form in pairs. This is a storming group with you, not welcome; they are the ones who have been sent to the Fire.
