**Commentary on verse 186 (p. 291)**

On "whom God leads astray has no guide, and He leaves them in their rebellion, wandering blindly", al-Tabari says: the turning away of these who denied Our signs, leaving off looking at God's proofs and reflecting on them, is due to God's leading them astray; had God guided them they would have taken lesson and pondered and seen their right course, but God led them astray, so they see no right course and find no way; and whom He leads away from the right course has no guide to it, but God leaves them in their persistence in unbelief and their defiance in association, wandering, so that they earn the end God has written for them of His punishment and painful retribution. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "straying", "guidance", "leaving", "rebellion" and "wandering blindly". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 291}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
