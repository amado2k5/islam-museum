## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say: whoever vomits intentionally while fasting must make it up; whoever is overcome by vomiting does not have to make it up. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shafii in his Musnad transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.408}
