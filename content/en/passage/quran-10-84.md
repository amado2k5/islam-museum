**Commentary on verse 84 (p. 168)**

On [[passage/quran-10-84|10:84]] al-Tabari reports Moses saying to his people: my people, if you have acknowledged God's oneness and affirmed His lordship, then 'in Him put your trust', that is, rely on Him and submit to His command, for He will not abandon His friend and will not forsake whoever relies on Him; 'if you are Muslims' means if you yield to God in obedience, then put your trust in Him. The editor says the printed text has 'and He gives safety' and the manuscript 'and did not give safety', and that he put what he holds right, and refers to the earlier commentaries on 'trust' and 'Islam'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 168}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
