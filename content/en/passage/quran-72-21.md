## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20-22 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Say, ‘I do not possess for you [the power of] harm or guidance’”: Say, O Muhammad, to the Arab polytheists who rejected the advice you brought them: I do not possess the power to harm you in your religion or your worldly affairs, nor to guide you to what is right, for the One who possesses that power is God, to whom belongs the dominion of all things. And His words, “Say, ‘Indeed, none of His creation can protect me from God’”—meaning, none of His creation can protect me if He wills something for me, nor can any helper defend me against Him. It is mentioned that this verse was revealed to the Prophet because some of the jinn said, “I will protect him.” In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah and Sufyan.
