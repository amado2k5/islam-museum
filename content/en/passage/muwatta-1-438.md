## Summary
The only report in the chapter on the qunut in the dawn prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar did not recite the qunut in any prayer. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi, Ibn Wahb in al-Tahawi, Abd al-Razzaq and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.226}
