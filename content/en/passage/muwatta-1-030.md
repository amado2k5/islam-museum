## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the prohibition of entering the mosque with the smell of garlic and covering the mouth. Yahya transmits from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab as a mursal: the Prophet forbade anyone who had eaten garlic to approach the mosques, because its smell harms those praying. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said it is mursal in the Muwatta in all its transmitters except an odd connected report through Abu Hurayra, and that Ma'mar, Yunus, and Ibrahim ibn Sa'd connected it through Ibn Shihab. He cites the connected version in Abd al-Razzaq, Ahmad, Muslim, Ibn Majah, Ibn Hibban and al-Bayhaqi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.49}
