## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 66 to 67 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And He is Who gave you life, then caused you to die, then gave you life. Indeed, man is ever ungrateful": And Allah is the One who bestowed upon you these blessings, He is the One who made for you living bodies with life that He brought into you, and you were nothing, then He causes you to die after your life, then annihilates you at the coming of your appointed time, then resurrects you after your death when you are resurrected for the Hour of Resurrection. "Indeed, man is ungrateful"—he says: The son of Adam is ungrateful for the blessings of Allah that He has bestowed upon him from His good character, and He subjects to what He has made of what He has made in the land, on land, and in the sea, and abandons His destruction by holding the sky so that it falls on the earth through his worship of other gods and equals, and abandoning Him alone in worship and sincerity in monotheism. And His saying: "For every nation We have appointed a rite of worship," he says: For every group a people who were before you, We made a custom to be accustomed to and a place they are accustomed to for worship, fulfilling my obligatory duties, and performing deeds that they adhere to. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
