## Summary
A saying of Malik about the one making the lesser pilgrimage who has intercourse with his wife: he owes the offering, and another lesser pilgrimage that he begins after completing the one he spoiled; he enters ihram from where he entered ihram for the lesser pilgrimage he spoiled, unless he entered ihram from a place farther than his station, in which case he need enter ihram only from his station. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1134) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.466}
