## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Is He who made the earth a firm place and placed rivers between it and placed for it mountain ridges and placed between the two seas a barrier? Is there a god with Allah? Rather, most of them do not know": Worshipping what you associate with your Lord, O people, is better, and it neither harms nor benefits. Or is He who made the earth for you a stable place upon which you may rest and not support you? "And He made for you rivers within it," meaning: between it rivers and made for it mountains, which are the fixed fixed mountains, "And He placed between the seas a barrier" between the fresh and the salt, so that one of them corrupts its companion "Is it with Allah?" He did these things, so you associate him in your worship of Him? And His saying: "Rather, most of them do not know," the Exalted says: Rather, most of these polytheists do not know the greatness of Allah, nor the harm they face in associating others with Allah in worship, and what benefit they have in dedicating Allah to divinity, their sincerity in worship of Him, and their disassociation from any other deity.
