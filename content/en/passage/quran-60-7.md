## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Perhaps Allah will place affection between you and those among whom you are hostile, and Allah is All-Powerful, and Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." It is explained: O believers, may Allah establish between you and those who are enemies among My enemies, the polytheists of Quraysh. Allah did this for them by converting many of them to Islam, and they became allies and allies. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
