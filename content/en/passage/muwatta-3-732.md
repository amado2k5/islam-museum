## Summary
A saying of Malik: olives are like palms: whatever is watered by the sky or springs or by rain-fed land owes a tenth; what is watered by hand-carried water owes half a tenth; and no olives are estimated on the tree. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.366}
