## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Their wealth and their children will not avail them against Allah at all. Those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide therein forever": These hypocrites will not avail their wealth on the Day of Resurrection, so they will be ransom with it from Allah's humiliating punishment for them or their children. They will support and rescue them from Allah when He punishes them. "Those are the companions of the Fire." He means: those who took a people as allies to whom Allah's wrath was wrathed, and they are the hypocrites who are the owners of the Fire, meaning its people who are eternal therein. He means: They will remain in the Fire indefinitely.
