## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And among His signs is the creation of the heavens and the earth and the spread therein of all decay, and He is able to gather them when He wills." Among His proofs against you, O people, is that He is able to revive you after your destruction, and to resurrect you from your graves after your affliction. He created the heavens and the earth and what He dispersed therein of any creature. That is, He did not divide the heavens and the earth by any creature. Also: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "And He has not scattered among them any creature," he said: the people and the angels, "and He is capable of gathering them when He wills," meaning: He is capable of gathering what He has scattered among them of all creatures if He wills, possessing power that is not impossible for Him, just as He is not able to create and divide them. Allah Almighty says: Thus is He who is able to gather His creation on the Day of Resurrection after their families are separated in the graves. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
