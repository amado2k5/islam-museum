## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And when it is said to them, 'Spend from what Allah has provided for you,' the disbelievers will say to those who believe, 'Have you been fed from whom? If Allah willed, He would have fed him.' Indeed, you are but in manifest error." And when it is said to these polytheists of Allah: Spend from the provision of Allah which He has provided for you, and fulfill from it what Allah has ordained upon you for those in need and need, those who denied the oneness of Allah and worshipped others besides Him said to those who believed in Allah and His Messenger: Shall we feed our wealth and food, whom, if Allah wills, I will feed him? And in His saying: "Indeed, you are only in clear error," there are two aspects: the first is that it is from what the disbelievers say to the believers, so the interpretation then becomes: What are you, O people, in your saying to us: spend from what Allah has provided you on your poor ones, except in deviating from the truth and injustice from the right path, which is clear to those who reflect and reflect on it, that he is in error, and this is the most appropriate aspect to his interpretation.
