## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 23 to 25 in a single passage: "Indeed, We give life and cause death." He gives life to whoever is dead, and causes death to whoever is alive if He wills. "And We are the inheritors," He inherits the earth and all upon it by causing death, leaving no one alive. And in "And We have certainly taught those who have been foreseen, and We have taught those who came later," interpreters differed: regarding Ikrimah, those who have been brought forth from creation and those who have left it among nations, and those who have not yet been created among those who will be created. In another narration from him: those who have come forth from creation, and those who remain in the loins of men; from Mujahid: those who have died and those who remain; and from Qatadah: those who have passed and those who remain in the loins. It is mentioned that Awn ibn Abdullah ibn Utbah mentioned Muhammad ibn Ka'b and mentioned the ranks of men and women, and Muhammad ibn Ka'b replied: Those who bring forth the dead, the killed, and the later ones who will be joined from later. And "And indeed, your Lord will gather them. Indeed, He is Wise, Knowing." He gathers them for reckoning.
