## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, “He knows what is in the heavens and the earth, and He knows what you conceal and what you reveal. And Allah is Knowing of that within the breasts.” O people, your Lord knows what is in the seven heavens and the earth, nothing of that is hidden from Him. “And He knows what you conceal” O people among yourselves of words and deeds, “and what you reveal” of that, so you make it manifest. “And Allah is Knowing of that within the breasts.” He, may His praise be exalted, says: And Allah is All-Knowing of the secrets of the breasts of His servants, and what their souls conceal, which is more hidden than a secret, nothing of that escapes Him. God Almighty says to His servants: Beware of concealing anything other than what you reveal, or of harboring in yourselves anything other than what you show, for nothing of that is hidden from your Lord, and He is aware of all of it, and He is the Guardian of all of it.
