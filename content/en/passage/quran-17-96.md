## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, 'Sufficient is Allah as a witness between me and you. Indeed, He was Acquainted with His servants and Seeing.': Allah says to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to those who say to you: 'Allah is sufficient as a messenger. Sufficient is Allah as a witness between me and you.' Indeed, He is the best sufficient and the Judge. 'Indeed, He was an Acquaintance with His servants.' He says: Allah is knowledgeable and knowledgeable of His servants, the right among them and the false, the Mahdi and the misguided 'Seeing' in their management, policies, and management as He wills, as He wills and loves. Nothing about their matters is hidden from Him, and He is metaphorical to all of them Present upon their arrival.
