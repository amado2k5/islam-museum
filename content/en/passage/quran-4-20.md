## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 20 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Replacing one wife with another (pp. 123-124, reports 8912-8913).** Abu Jafar says the sense is: if you, believers, wish to marry one woman in place of a wife you divorce, and you have given the one you want to divorce a great sum, do not harm her so that she ransoms herself with what you gave. He says the qintar is abundant wealth, and that he has already given the interpreters' differences on its size and the right view in his opinion. Muhammad ibn Amr (8912, from Abu Asim, Isa, Ibn Abi Najih) reports from Mujahid that it is divorcing one woman for another, and then nothing of the divorced woman's wealth is lawful for him, however much; al-Muthanna (8913, from Abu Hudhayfa, Shibl) repeats it from Mujahid. Abu Jafar then explains the rebuking question that follows: would you take what you gave them of their dowries wrongfully and without right, and in a sin whose taker's act shows that he wrongs the one he takes it from. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on the qintar in volume 6 (pp. 244-250). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 123} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 124}
