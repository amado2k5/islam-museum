## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 38 to 40 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'My Lord, help me by what they used to deny.'" Salih said when he despaired of his people's faith in God and their belief in Him by saying, "And we are not believers to Him. My Lord, help me" against these people "by what they used to deny," meaning by denying Me in what I called them to of the truth. So the Prophet, sought help from his Lord from their harm and denial of him. God answered him in his question to him: How little, O Saleh, that those who deny you among your people will become regretful for their denial, and that will be when Our punishment descends upon them, and regret will not benefit them.
