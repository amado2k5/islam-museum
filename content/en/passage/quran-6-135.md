## Summary
Verse 135 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 135 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On verse 135, "say: my people, act in your place; I am acting, and you will come to know", God tells His Prophet: say, Muhammad, to your people of Quraysh who set another god beside God, act in your place, meaning according to your position and side; he gives (13898) Ibn Abbas: on your side, and one says "he works in his place and station". Some Kufans read "makanatikum" in the plural, while the readers of the cities read the singular "makanatikum". "I am acting" means: tell them, do what you are doing, for I am doing what my Lord commanded me; "you will come to know" when God's vengeance falls on you which of us was in the right in his deed and found the path of right guidance, I or you. God's words to His Prophet are a command to threaten and warn them, not a licence for them to do whatever acts of disobedience they wish. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 129}
