## Verse 34 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, pp. 327-328)

**The offspring and their bond.** Al-Tabari ([[passage/quran-3-34|3:34]]) says God chose the two houses as offspring following one another, and that the word for offspring is in the accusative as a circumstantial qualifier (the 'cut') of the two houses, because it is indefinite and they are definite; it would also be right to take it as repeating the choosing. The editor refers to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an and to an earlier discussion of the term. The bond, al-Tabari says, is in allegiance in religion and mutual help in submission and truth, as God said of the believing men and women in [[passage/quran-9-71|9:71]] and of the hypocrites in [[passage/quran-9-67|9:67]], meaning their religion and way are one; so the sense is offspring whose religion is one, whose word is one and whose creed is one in affirming God's oneness and obeying Him. Bishr, from Yazid, from Sa'id, from Qatada (6855) says it is in intention, deed, sincerity and affirmation of oneness. God's hearing and knowing, he says, means He heard the words of Imran's wife and knew what she hid in herself when she vowed what was in her womb. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 328}
