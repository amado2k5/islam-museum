## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 101 to 102 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So We gave him glad tidings of a forbearing boy": So Abraham gave us glad tidings of a forbearing boy, meaning a forbearing boy when he grew up. As for his childhood in the cradle, he is not described as such. He mentioned that the boy whom God gave glad tidings to Abraham was Isaac.
