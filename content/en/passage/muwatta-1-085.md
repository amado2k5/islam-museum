## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on wiping over socks: he was asked about a man who washed his feet, then put on his socks, then renewed his ablution. He said he should take off his socks and then perform ablution and wash his feet. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.79}
