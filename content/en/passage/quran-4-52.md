## Summary
Verse 52 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 52 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Who the cursed are (p. 471).** Al-Tabari says the pointer here is to those he described as handed part of God's Book while accepting the Jibt and the Taghut as true; they are those God has disgraced and distanced from His mercy because of that acceptance and their disbelief in God and His messenger out of obstinacy, and their saying to the unbelievers what they said; and whoever God disgraces and distances from His mercy has no helper, that is, Muhammad will find none to help him against God's punishment and curse that befalls him and to ward it off. He then begins report 9795 (Bishr b. Mu'adh, from Yazid, from Sa'id) and the chain breaks off at the page end. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 471}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
