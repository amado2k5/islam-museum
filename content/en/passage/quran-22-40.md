## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who were expelled from their homes without right, except that they say, 'Our Lord is Allah.' And if Allah had not driven people away by means of others, monasteries, churches, prayers, and mosques in which the name of Allah is often mentioned would have been demolished. And Allah will surely support those who will support Him. Indeed, Allah is Powerful and Exalted in Might." Permission was given to those who fight: "Those who were expelled from their homes without right" is a response to the first "those who were expelled." He meant those who were expelled from their homes by the believers whom the disbelievers of Quraysh expelled from Mecca. They were expelled from their homes and tortured by some for believing in God and His Messenger, and some insulted them with their tongues and threatened them, until they were forced to leave them. They did this to them unjustly because they were on falsehood and the believers were on the truth. Therefore, He, the Exalted, said: "Those who were expelled from their homes without right." In the excerpt, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
