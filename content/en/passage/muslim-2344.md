## Summary
A report of Jabir ibn Samura in the chapter on the affirmation of the seal of prophethood, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim. The seal between his shoulders was like a pigeon's egg. It is the most concise text on the seal. {cite:sahih-muslim}
