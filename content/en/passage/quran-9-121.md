**Commentary on verse 121 (p. 565)**

Al-Tabari says: that is because no thirst touches them, nor the rest of what was mentioned, nor do they gain anything from an enemy, nor spend any small or large expense in God's way, nor cross any valley with the Messenger on his campaign, but the reward of that work is recorded for them as repayment, like the best of what He repays for their best deeds that they did while staying in their homes. Report 17465 is from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: no people grow further from their families in God's way without growing nearer to God. The editor notes that he restored the phrase "or large" in the verse because it is required by the wording. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 565}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
