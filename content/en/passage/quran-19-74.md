## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And how many generations We destroyed before them, who were better in furnishings and appearance": And how many times did We destroy before those among the people of disbelief who say to the believers, when the verses of the Most Merciful are recited to them, meaning the two groups are better in status and better in rivalry, gatherings of a generation that are more luxurious in homes than these, better in appearance and more beautiful in appearance, so We destroyed their wealth and changed their forms. Among this is the saying of Alqama ibn Abduh: Kumayt, like purple, published for sale in cubic flint, means flint: the bed where clothes are preserved. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and others.
