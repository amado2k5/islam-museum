## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik that he met people of knowledge whom he trusted, who said of a man who bequeathed that the one who killed him deliberately be pardoned: it is lawful for him, and he has more right to his blood than his other guardians after him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.447}
