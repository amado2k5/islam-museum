## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2-5 as a single section. He explains the phrase “O you who are wrapped up” (referring to the Prophet Muhammad) as meaning the one who is wrapped up in his clothes. He clarifies that this refers to the Prophet Muhammad. Scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning of this description of the Prophet in this verse. Some said it describes him as being wrapped up in his clothes, ready for prayer. The section also includes statements attributed to Qatadah.
