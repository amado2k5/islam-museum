## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 33 in a single segment. He explains His saying, "And among His signs is proximity to the sea, like flags": Among God's proofs, O people, is that He is capable of all that He wills, and that He is not able to do anything He wills, which He intends to do. And 'al-jawari': plural of 'maidar', meaning a slave woman who sails in the sea. Also: from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: 'neighbors in the sea' mean: ships. And from Al-Suddi: 'And among His signs is proximity in the sea,' he said: 'The neighbors are ships.' And his saying: 'like flags' means like mountains: its singular is knowledge, and from it the poet's saying: 'As if he had a flag on his head, a fire,' meaning: a mountain. In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
