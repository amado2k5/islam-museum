## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "O you who have believed, do not put yourselves before Allah and His Messenger and fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing": He means that Allah Almighty is mentioned by saying: "O you who have believed": O you who acknowledge the oneness of Allah and the prophethood of His Prophet Muhammad, "Do not advance before Allah and His Messenger," meaning: Do not hasten to judge a matter in your wars or your religion before Allah and His Messenger have decided for you and His Messenger. So judge against the command of Allah and His Messenger. It is narrated from the Arabs that so-and-so will come before his Imam, meaning he hastens the matter and prohibition before it. In the section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
