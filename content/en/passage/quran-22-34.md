## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And for every nation We have made a rite so that they may remember the name of Allah over what He has provided them of the livestock of cattle. So your God is one God, so to Him submit and give glad tidings to the two of the deceived." And for every nation [the people], and for every group among you who have faith in God, O people, We have made a slaughter whose blood they shed, "so that they may remember the name of Allah over what He has provided them of the cattle," because among the animals are those that are not cattle, such as horses, mules, and donkeys. It is also said: animals are called beasts because they do not speak. In the excerpt, statements are transmitted from Mujahid.
