## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, from Zaynab bint Abi Salama, from Umm Salama, wife of the Prophet, that the Messenger said: I am only a human being, and you bring disputes to me. Perhaps one of you is more eloquent in his argument than another, and I judge for him according to what I hear from him. Whoever I give him something of his brother's right, let him not take it, for I am only cutting for him a piece of the Fire. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the chain of the report has not varied from Malik, and that Ma'mar transmitted it from al-Zuhri from Urwa from Zaynab from Umm Salama in the same way. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.259}
