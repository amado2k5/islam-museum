## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So pardon them and say peace, for they will soon know": "O Lord, these are a people who do not believe." "So forgive them," O Muhammad, turn away from their harm and say to them, "Peace be upon you," and raise peace with the pronoun against you or for you. Reciters differed in the recitation of His phrase: "So they will know." The majority of the reciters of Medina read it as "so they will know" with the letter 'ta' in the formal form, meaning: God Almighty commanded His Prophet to say this to the polytheists, along with His saying: "Peace." The majority of the reciters of Kufa and some reciters of Mecca read it as "so they will know" with a yaa as the predicate form, and that it is a warning from God to the polytheists. His interpretation of this reading is: "So forgive them," O Muhammad, and say peace. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
