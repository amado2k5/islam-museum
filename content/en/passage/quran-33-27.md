## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 26–27. Al-Tabari mentions that God sent down those who appeared to be confederates from among the People of the Book, namely Banu Qurayza, from their fortresses, and cast terror into their hearts: some you kill and some you capture, and He bequeathed to you their land, their homes, their wealth, and a land you have never trodden.
