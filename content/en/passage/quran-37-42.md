## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 42 to 47 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "fruits while they are honored" as a response to the known provision, which is why it was raised. And His saying: "and they are honored" means they are among those who have the known provision in Paradise, honored by the generosity of God with which He honored them. He also quotes in the segment from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Dhahhak.
