## Summary
Malik transmits from Salama ibn Safwan ibn Salama al-Zuraqi, from Yazid ibn Talha ibn Rukana, raising it to the Prophet: every religion has a character, and the character of Islam is modesty. The editor notes that the correct name of the narrator is Yazid, not Zayd as it appears in the transmission of Yahya ibn Yahya al-Laythi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.490}
