## Summary
Hadith of Aisha: "Suckling makes unlawful what birth makes unlawful". It is the second of the hadiths Ibn Rajab added. He surveys the prohibitions by kinship and shows how they apply by suckling. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
