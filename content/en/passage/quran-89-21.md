## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 21 to 24 as one section. He explains His saying, “And you love wealth with an intense love”: God Almighty means by His saying, “And you love wealth with an intense love,” that you, O people, love to collect and acquire wealth with a great and intense love, from their saying: The water has gathered in the basin: when it has collected, and from it is the saying of Zuhair bin Abi Sulma: When they came to the water, its pool was blue, they put down the staffs of the present camper. And in the section he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zaid.
