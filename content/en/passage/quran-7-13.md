## Summary
Verse 13 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 13 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "He said: go down from it; it is not for you to be arrogant in it; so get out, you are among the humbled", God says that God then said to Iblis, go down from it, and its meaning of "going down" was explained earlier; "it is not for you to be arrogant in it" means it is not for you to be arrogant in Paradise against My obedience and command. If someone asks, may anyone be arrogant in Paradise? The answer is that the sense is other than you suppose; it is: go down from Paradise, for none arrogant against God's command dwells in Paradise, whereas in other places the one who is arrogant against God's command and the one who submits to obeying Him both dwell. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "going down". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 329}

"So get out, you are among the humbled" means get out of Paradise, you are among those on whom God has laid humiliation, abasement and disgrace; of it one says "he was humbled, is humbled, humbleness, abasement", and it is also said "saghura, yasghuru, sighar, saghara". As al-Suddi said: he gives (14359) from him that "sighar" is humiliation. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 330}
