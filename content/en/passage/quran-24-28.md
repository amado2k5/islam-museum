## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And if you do not find anyone in them, do not enter them until permission is issued to you. And if you are told, 'Return,' then return; it is purer for you. And Allah is Knowing of what you do." If you do not find anyone in the houses where you seek permission to enter, do not enter them, because they are not yours. It is not permissible for you to enter them except with the permission of their owners. If their owners permit you to enter, then enter. "And if you are told to go back, then go back," meaning: And if the people of the houses where you seek permission say to you, 'Go back,' do not enter them, so turn away from them and do not enter them. 'He is purer for you.' He means: your turning back from them if you are told to return, and you were not permitted to enter them is purer for you in the sight of Allah. His saying: 'he' is a metaphor for the verb noun, meaning from His saying: 'So go back.' In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid.
