## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm, from his father, from Abdullah ibn Amr ibn Uthman, from Abu Amra al-Ansari, from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani, that the Messenger said: shall I not tell you the best of witnesses? The one who brings his testimony before he is asked for it, or informs of his testimony before he is asked. The editor notes that the correct name is Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Amra al-Ansari, per al-Tirmidhi. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.260}
