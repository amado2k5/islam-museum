## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 16 to 18 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase “crystal goblets of silver, precisely measured” as meaning: “goblets” in the purest form of silver, silver being white. As narrated by Abu Raja’, who said: Al-Hasan said, regarding the verse “They were crystal goblets of silver,” that the purity of the goblets is in the whiteness of silver. Also narrated by Abu Raja’, on the authority of Al-Hasan, regarding God’s statement “crystal goblets of silver,” that the whiteness of silver is in the purity of the goblets. And Ibn Abi Khalid informed us, on the authority of Abu Salih, regarding the verse “They were crystal goblets of silver,” that its dust was of silver. And regarding the phrase “crystal goblets of silver,” he said: the purity of glass is in the whiteness of silver. Regarding Qatadah's interpretation of the verse, "Crystal goblets, crystal goblets of silver," he said: "If the people of falsehood needed to make a silver vessel whose contents could be seen from behind, just as what is in crystal goblets can be seen, they would not be able to do so." The passage also includes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
