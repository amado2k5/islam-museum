## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Allah has promised those of you who believe and do righteous deeds that He will surely grant them succession upon the earth as He granted succession to those before them, and that He will surely establish for them their religion which He has approved for them, and that He will surely replace them after their fear with security. They worship Me, not associating anything with Me. And whoever disbelieves after that - those are the defiantly disobedient": "Allah has promised those who believe" by Allah and His Messenger "from you, O people," "and do righteous deeds," meaning: And obey Allah and His Messenger in what matters. His command and His decree: 'to make them successors in the land,' meaning: God will grant them inheritance of the land of the polytheists, both Arabs and non-Arabs, and make them its kings and statesmen. "Just as He appointed those before them" means: as those before them did with the Children of Israel, when he destroyed the tyrants of the Levant and made them its kings and inhabitants. "And to establish for them their religion which He has approved for them" means: and to establish for them their religion, meaning their religion which He accepted for them and commanded them. In the passage, he quotes from Al-Rabi'.
