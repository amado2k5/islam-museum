## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "The Day you see the believing men and believing women running their light before their hands and on their right hands, [saying] Good tidings of you. Today are gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding forever therein. That is the great attainment": interpreters differ in the interpretation of the phrase: "The Day you see the believing men and believing women shining their light before their hands and on their right hands." Some said: The meaning is: the day you see the believing men and believing women shining in their hands and on their right hands. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Dahhak.
