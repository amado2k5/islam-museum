**Commentary on verse 103 (p. 216)**

On [[passage/quran-10-103|10:103]] al-Tabari says: say, Muhammad, to these polytheists of your people: wait for the like of the days of those who passed before you of the earlier nations who were destroyed by God's punishment; when that comes it destroys only them and any who were as they were in denying you; then We save there Our messenger Muhammad and those who believed in him, affirmed him and followed his religion, as We did before to Our messengers whose nations We destroyed, saving them and those who believed with them from Our punishment when it fell due on their nations. 'Thus it is incumbent on Us to save the believers': as We did with Our messengers of the past, saving them and the believers with them and destroying their nations, so We shall do with you, Muhammad, and with the believers, saving you and saving the believers who follow you, a duty on Us without doubt. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 216}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
