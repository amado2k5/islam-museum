## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And among His signs is that He created you from dust, then when you are human beings, you spread out": Among His arguments is that He is capable of whatever He wills, O people, of creation and destruction, of creation and non-existence, and that every being is created by the creation of your father from dust, meaning that Adam was created from dust. He described them as having created them from dust, as He did to their father Adam, as we have explained earlier in the discourse of the Arabs who addressed what they did to their predecessors, such as saying: We did to you and We did. And His saying: "Then when you are humans, you will be dispersed," it means: then when you are a group of descendants of those We created from the dust of humans, you will spread, meaning: you will act.
