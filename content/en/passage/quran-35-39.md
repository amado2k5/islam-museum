## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He is Who made you successors upon the earth. So whoever disbelieves, upon him is his disbelief. And the disbelievers do not increase their disbelief with their Lord except in abomination, and the disbelievers increase their disbelief except in loss." This means that Allah is the One who made you, O mankind, successors upon the earth after 'Ad and Thamud, and those nations before you who made you successors in their homes and dwellings, as in Qatadah's saying: "He is the One who made you successors upon the earth" nation after nation, and generation after generation. And His saying: "So whoever disbelieves, upon him is his disbelief," Allah Almighty says: "So whoever among you disbelieves in Allah, O people, his disbelief will harm himself; no one will harm himself by that, because he is the one who will be punished and not others." And His saying: "And the disbelievers do not increase their disbelief with their Lord except with abomination," He says: "And the disbelievers do not increase their disbelief in their sight except after a distance of Allah's mercy." "And the disbelievers do not increase their disbelief except in loss," meaning: The disbelievers do not increase their disbelief in Allah except in destruction. In the section, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
