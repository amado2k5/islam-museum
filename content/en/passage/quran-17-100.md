## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'If you possessed the treasuries of my Lord's mercy, then you would hold back for fear of spending [[in charity]': Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists: If you, O people, possessed the treasuries of my Lord's wealth from wealth. By mercy in this context, He means wealth: 'If you withhold wealth out of fear of spending,' he says: Then you would be stingy with it and would not be generous with it to others, out of fear of spending and stinginess, as in: He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, who said: Ibn Abbas said: "Then you withhold for fear of spending," he said: poverty. And from Qatadah, 'fear of spending,' meaning fear of poverty. Muammar informed us, from Qatadah, similarly. And His saying: 'And man was stingy,' he says: man was stingy and stingy, as he said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: 'man was stingy,' he says: stingy. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
