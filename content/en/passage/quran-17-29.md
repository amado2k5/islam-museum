## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the saying, "And do not make your hand chained to your neck and do not stretch it out completely, lest you become blamed and confined": This is an example that God Almighty gave to someone who refrains from spending on the rights He has made obligatory for the wealth of wealthy individuals, making him like his hand being tied to his neck, unable to take or give. The meaning of the statement is: Do not withhold, O Muhammad, your hand out of stinginess in spending on the rights of Allah, so do not spend anything on it. The one who is chained holds his hand to his neck, which he cannot stretch out. "And do not extend it completely with a gift," meaning: do not extend it with a gift completely, so you remain with nothing, and you will find when you are asked something to give to your beggar, "so you sit blamed and deprived," meaning: so you sit and the beggar blames you if you do not give them when they ask you, and you blame yourself for hastening your wealth and leaving it. "Confused" means flawed, you have been cut off, and you have nothing to spend. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Al-Hasan.
