## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abdullah ibn Umar stayed on Surat al-Baqara for eight years learning it. This is the last report in the chapter on what is related about the Qur'an, and the editor does not add a takhrij to it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.282}
