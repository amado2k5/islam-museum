## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 39 to 40 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So We took him and his soldiers and cast them into the sea, so see how was the end of the wrongdoers": Pharaoh and his soldiers gathered the Copts "and cast them into the sea," meaning: We threw them all into the sea and drowned them in it, as Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali said: I looked at its title and threw it away like a sandal made from sandals. He mentioned that this is a sea beyond Egypt, as Qatadah said: "So We cast them into the sea," he said: The sea was called Isaf, behind Egypt, in which God drowned them.
