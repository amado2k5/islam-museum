## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 10 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And if you ask them who created the heavens and the earth, they will say, 'Their creation is the Exalted in Might, the Knowing.'": And if you asked, O Muhammad, these polytheists among your people: Who created the seven heavens and the earth, and created them and created them? They say: He created them in His power and vengeance against His enemies, the Knower of them and all that is in them. Nothing is hidden from Him: "Who made the earth for you a cradle," meaning: He who prepared the earth for you, so He made it a stepping ground for you to walk on it with your feet and walk on it on your feet. "And He made easy for you therein paths," meaning: He made easy for you roads to travel from town to town for your livelihood and trade. Also: from Qatadah, "And He made for you paths therein," meaning roads. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
