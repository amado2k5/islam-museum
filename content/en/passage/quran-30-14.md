## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 14 to 15 together in one place. Al-Tabari mentions that on the Day of Resurrection, when the creation will be gathered together, they will be divided. The people of faith and the disbelievers will be taken to Paradise on the right hand, and the people of disbelief on the left will be taken to Hell. There, God distinguishes the evil from the good.
