## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 95 to 96 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'So what proposed to you, O Samaritan?": He means His mention with the phrase: "So what proposed to you, O Samaritan?" Moses said to the Samaritan: What is your matter, O Samaritan, and what led you to what you did? As Ibn Zayd said, regarding the phrase "So what proposed to you, O Samaritan," he said: What is your matter? What is your matter? What is this that led you into what you entered into? And from Al-Suddi: "He said, 'So what is proposed to you, O Samaritan?' He said: What is wrong with you, O Samaritan?" And His saying: "I saw what they did not see" means that Al-Samiri said: I knew what they did not know, and it is 'I did what I did' from insight: meaning I became a knowledgeable and insightful by what I did. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Zayd.
