## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, that he said: the Messenger ordered the two Saads to sell vessels of gold or silver from the spoils. They sold each three for four in coin, or each four for three in coin. The Messenger said to them: you have dealt in riba; return them. The editor notes that the two Saads are Saad ibn Abi Waqqas and Saad ibn Ubada, and that the reports differ on whether the event was at Khaybar or Hunayn. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.156}
