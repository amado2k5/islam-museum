## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "You worship other than Allah only idols and create fabrications. Indeed, those you worship besides Allah do not possess for you any provision, so seek provision from Allah and worship Him and be grateful to Him, to Him you will be returned." Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of His close friend Abraham to his people: 'You worship but idols besides Allah,' meaning for example. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying, "You worship besides Allah are but idols." Interpreters differed in interpreting the phrase: "and you create fabrications," and some said: it means: and you fabricate lies. In the section, statements are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
