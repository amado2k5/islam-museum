## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on cupping in the state of ihram. Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that the Messenger of God was cupped while in ihram, on the top of his head, at a place called Luhy Jamal on the road to Mecca. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1182), Suwayd and others transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.469}
