## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "are they waiting for anything but that God come to them in canopies of cloud, and the angels": al-Tabari says it means, are those who deny Muhammad and what he brought waiting for anything but that God come to them in canopies of cloud and the angels? The readers then differed in the reading of "and the angels". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v04 p. 260}
