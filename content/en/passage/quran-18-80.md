## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 80 to 81 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "As for the boy, his parents were believers, but they feared that he would burden them out of tyranny and disbelief": As for the boy, he was a disbeliever, and his parents were believers, so we know that he burdened them. He says: He covers them with tyranny, which is arrogance against God and disbelief in Him. In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
