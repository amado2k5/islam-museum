## Summary
A report of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri through Tariq ibn Shihab in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim, in the story of Marwan delivering the sermon before the prayer on the Eid. The order for changing wrong is by hand, then tongue, then heart. {cite:sahih-muslim}
