**Commentary on verse 6 (pp. 523-524)**

On "when Moses said to his people: remember God's favour upon you when He rescued you from Pharaoh's people, who inflicted on you the worst torment, slaughtered your sons and spared your women; and in that was a great trial from your Lord", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise, to His Prophet Muhammad: remember, Muhammad, when Moses son of Imran said to his people of the Children of Israel, "remember God's favour upon you", with which He favoured you, "when He rescued you from Pharaoh's people", that is, when He rescued you from the people of Pharaoh's religion and obedience, "who inflicted on you the worst torment", that is, made you taste severe torment, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 523}

"and slaughtered your sons", on top of making you taste severe torment, slaughtering your sons. The "wa" was inserted in this place because by "and slaughtered your sons" the report was meant to say that Pharaoh's people tormented the Children of Israel with kinds of torment other than slaughter and with slaughter. In another place in the Qur'an it came without the "wa", "inflicting on you the worst torment, slaughtering your sons" [[passage/quran-2-49|2:49]] in one place, and in another "killing your sons" [[passage/quran-7-141|7:141]]; the "wa" was not inserted where it was not, because by "slaughtering" and "killing" the intent was to specify the character of the torment they inflicted. So it is in every sentence one wants to spell out: without the "wa" is its spelling out, and when one wants to join something to it other than a spelling out, with the "wa". Report 20582, from Ibn Uyayna, through al-Muthanna, Ishaq and Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr: "when Moses said to his people: remember God's favour upon you", God's benefactions with you and His days. On "and spared your women" he says, and kept your women alive, leaving off killing them, {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 524}

We stop here at the foot of p. 524, in the middle of the commentary on verse 6 (at "and spared your women"); the next batch begins at p. 525 (marker 533). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 524}
