## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 28–29. Allah commands His Prophet to choose his wives: whoever desires the worldly life and its adornment, He will enjoy them and release them in a beautiful manner, and whoever desires Allah, His Messenger, and the Hereafter, Allah has prepared a great reward for the doers of good among them.
