## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And Allah created you from dust, then from a drop of sperm, then made you mates. And no female carries or gives birth except with His knowledge. No one who lives longs or decreases his life except in a Book. Indeed, that is easy for Allah." "And Allah created you, O people," "from dust," meaning that He created their father Adam from dust, so He made their father's creation from him a creation for them, "then from a drop of fluid." He says: Then He created you from the sperm of man and woman, "Then He made you mates," meaning He married the female from the male. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
