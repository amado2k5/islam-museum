## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And We have enjoined upon man good to his parents. But if they strive for you to associate with Me that of which you have no knowledge, do not obey them. To Me is your return. I will inform you of what you used to do": "And We have commanded man" in what We revealed to Our Messenger, "his parents," to do good to them. The Arabs differed regarding the accusative of 'hasan'; some Basra grammarians said: 'It is accusative with the intention of repeating 'wasina' ('wasina'). It seems that the meaning of the statement for him is: 'We have advised a person to his parents, and we have advised him to do good.' He said: A man may say his will is good: meaning good.
