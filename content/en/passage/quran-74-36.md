## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 36 to 41 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Nay, by the moon”: God Almighty means by His statement “Nay”: The statement is not as those who claim that the keepers of Hell are sufficient to protect their polytheistic followers from it. Then our Lord, the Exalted, swore, saying: “By the moon and the night when it departs,” meaning: By the night when it has passed and gone. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
