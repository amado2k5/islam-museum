**Commentary on verse 53 (p. 360)**

On [[passage/quran-11-53|11:53]] al-Tabari says: Hud's people said to Hud, Hud, you have brought us no clear proof or demonstration of what you say that we might submit to you and acknowledge you truthful in what you call us to, of God's oneness and the acknowledgement of your prophethood; 'we are not ones to leave our gods' because of your saying, 'and we are not believers in you', meaning they said we do not affirm you in the prophethood and messengership from God you claim. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 360}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
