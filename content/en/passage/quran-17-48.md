## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Look how they have set forth for you parables; they have preferred but cannot find a way": Look, O Muhammad, with the eye of your heart, and consider how they have likened parables to you, by saying: He is bewitched, he is a poet, and he is mad. "They have left." He means: so they deliberately stray from the way by saying what they said, "but they cannot find a way," meaning: they are not guided to the path of truth because they are misguided from it and far from it, and that Allah has forsaken them from His affliction. They are unable to find a way out of what they are in, their disbelief, by their success in believing in it, as follows: And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, and from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: 'They could not find a way,' he said: a way out, al-Walid ibn al-Mughira and his companions as well. He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid: "See how they have set forth examples for you; they have excelled and cannot find a way," quoting Al-Walid ibn al-Mughira and his companions. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
