## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 64 to 65 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Until, when We seize their oppressed with punishment, they will be retaliated": And these disbelievers from Quraysh have deeds other than that, and they will do them, until the people of blessing and arrogance are taken from them by punishment. As Ibn Zayd said: "When We seize their affluent with punishment," he said: The affluent are the great ones. "When they are caught," he says: and when We seize them with it, they roar, meaning: they complained and sought help from the punishment that befell them. Perhaps al-Jawar: raising the voice, just as a bull roars, as in the saying of al-A'sha: 'Among the prayers of the gathering are like a period of prostration and sometimes a neighbor.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and al-Rabi'.
