## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And when Our verses are recited to them [O Muhammad], We have made clear proofs," meaning those who disbelieve say to those who believe, meaning the two groups are the best in station and best in competing.' "And when Our verses" which We revealed to our Messenger Muhammad are recited to the people, "clear proofs," meaning clear to those who reflect and reflect on them, as proofs for what God has set as proofs for His servants. "Those who disbelieve" said to God, His Book, and His verses, and they are Quraysh, "for those who believe" and believed in it, and they are the companions of Muhammad. "That is, the two groups have the best status," meaning by the status: Their place of residence, which is their dwellings and dwellings. "And the best companion" refers to the gathering. It is said: the gathering of the people and their companions: when they gathered them in a gathering, it is said: it is in the companionship of his people and in their gathering: meaning the same meaning. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-A'mash.
