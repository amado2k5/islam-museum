## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “On the Day when the earth and the mountains will tremble, and the mountains will become a heap of shifting sand,” explains that We have in store for these polytheists of Quraysh who harm you, O Muhammad, the punishments described on the Day when the earth and the mountains will tremble. The trembling of the earth and the mountains refers to their shaking and the shaking of those upon them, and that will be on the Day of Resurrection. His statement, “and the mountains will become a heap of shifting sand,” means that the mountains will be like flowing, scattered sand. And “al-mahil” is the object of the saying of the one who said: “I poured the sand, so I pour it,” and that is when its bottom was moved, so it poured down on it from its top. And the Arabs have two languages for that, they say: “mahil” and “mahyul,” and “makil” and “makiul.” And from it is the saying of the poet: “Your people used to think you were a master, and I think that you are a master who has been deceived.” And in the passage he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas and Mujahid.
