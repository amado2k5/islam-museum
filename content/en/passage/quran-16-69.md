## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Then eat from every fruit and follow the ways of your Lord in humility." From their bellies comes a drink of various colors, in which is healing for the people. Indeed in that is a sign for a people who reflect.' Then eat, O bee, of the fruits. "So follow the ways of your Lord," meaning: follow the ways of your Lord. "Humility" means humility for you, and humility is the plural of humility. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
