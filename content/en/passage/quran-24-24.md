## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "the Day when their tongues, hands, and feet will testify against them for what they used to do": and they will have a great punishment: "The Day their tongues will testify against them." The "day" in the phrase "the Day they will testify against them" is connected to the phrase: "And they will have a great punishment," and what he meant by "the Day their tongues will testify against them" is the Day of Resurrection, when one of them denies the sins they committed in this world when Allah has affirmed them. Allah will seal their mouths, and their hands and feet will testify against them for what they used to do. If someone says: How can their tongues testify against them when their mouths are sealed? It was said: What I mean by that is that the tongues of some testify to each other, not that their tongues speak when they are sealed over their mouths.
