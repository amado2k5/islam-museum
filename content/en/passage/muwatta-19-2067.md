## Summary
Malik says of the irrigation partner that he may not take from the owner who irrigated him any gold or silver extra, nor food, nor anything. It is not proper, and the irrigation partner should not take from the owner of the orchard anything extra he gives him, in gold, silver, food, or anything at all. The surplus between them is not proper. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.245}
