## Summary
Verse 78 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 78 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "when he saw the sun rising he said this is my Lord, this is greater; and when it set he said: my people, I am free of what you associate" al-Tabari says: when Abraham saw the sun rising he said this rising one is my Lord, "this is greater" meaning greater than the star and the moon, the words being dropped as the speech shows them; "when it set" says when it was gone, Abraham said to his people "my people, I am free of what you associate", that is, of worshipping the gods and images and calling on a god beside God. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 487}

The editor refers for "set", "rise" and "free" to earlier passages and says the word "by his saying" fell out of the manuscript and print, though the sense of the speech requires it.

Verse 78 closes here on p. 487, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-79|6:79]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
