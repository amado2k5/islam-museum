## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So he went out to his people from the sanctuary and revealed to them to glorify Him morning and evening": Zakariya went out to his people from his prayer place when he silenced his tongue from the people, a sign from God regarding the truth of His promise to him as He promised him. Ibn Jurayj used to say about the meaning of his leaving his prayer niche: He said: Hajjaj narrated from Ibn Jurayj, "He went out over his people from the mihrab," he said: He supervised his people from the mihrab. Abu Ja'far said: We have explained the meaning of the mihrab in the past in a way that suffices to repeat it here. Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "He went out to his people from the mihrab," he said: The mihrab is his prayer place, and he read: "The angels called him while he was standing praying in the mihrab," and his saying: "So he revealed to them" means he gestured to them. This gesture may be by hand, by the book, or otherwise, which is used to understand what is intended about him. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Ishaq, and Sufyan.
