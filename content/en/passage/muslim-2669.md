## Summary
A report of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri in the chapter on following the ways of the Jews and Christians, in the Book of Knowledge of Sahih Muslim: following their ways span by span, until they enter a lizard's hole. It is the best-known text on imitation. {cite:sahih-muslim}
