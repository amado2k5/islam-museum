## Summary
Verse 83 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 83 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-83|7:83]], al-Tabari says: when the people of Lot, despite his rebuke and his conveying his Lord's message forbidding it, refused anything but persisting in their error, We saved Lot and his family who believed in him, except his wife, who was unfaithful to Lot and an unbeliever in God. "Among the ghabirin" means among those who remained. It is "ghabirin" and not "ghabirat" because she is meant as one who stayed behind with the men, and when her mention was joined to the men's it took the masculine plural. The verb is "ghabara, yaghbur" when something stays, as al-Aasha says in a line mentioning "the time gone by", and he cites another line by Yazid ibn al-Hakam al-Thaqafi. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 551}

Al-Tabari cites the line of Yazid ibn al-Hakam al-Thaqafi, who boasts of his father who conquered lands with his sword, and takes "ghabir" to mean the one who remains. The editor tells how al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf summoned Yazid to appoint him over Fars and asked him to recite some of his verse; Yazid recited a boasting poem about his father al-Hakam's conquest of Fars and Istakhr in the year 23 of the Hijra, al-Hajjaj rose angry and Yazid left without taking leave, al-Hajjaj sent after him to take back his commission, and Yazid went to Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik, who took him into his circle. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 552}

If someone asks, was Lot's wife among those who escaped the destruction that overtook Lot's people? The answer is no, she was among those destroyed. If he asks, how then "except his wife, who was among the ghabirin", when you said ghabir means one who remains? The answer is that the meaning is not what you take: it means she was among those who had remained before the destruction, the long-lived, over whom a long time had passed until she grew old with those who grew old, and so she perished with those of Lot's people who perished when the punishment came. It is also said the meaning is among those remaining in God's punishment, and (14842) from Qatada on "except an old woman among the ghabirin" that she was in God's punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 553}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
