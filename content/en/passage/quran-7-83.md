## Summary
Verse 83 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 83 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on [[passage/quran-7-83|7:83]], al-Tabari says: when the people of Lot, despite his rebuke and his conveying his Lord's message forbidding it, refused anything but persisting in their error, We saved Lot and his family who believed in him, except his wife, who was unfaithful to Lot and an unbeliever in God. "Among the ghabirin" means among those who remained. It is "ghabirin" and not "ghabirat" because she is meant as one who stayed behind with the men, and when her mention was joined to the men's it took the masculine plural. The verb is "ghabara, yaghbur" when something stays, as al-Aasha says in a line mentioning "the time gone by", and he cites another line by Yazid ibn al-Hakam al-Thaqafi. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 551}

We stop here at verse 83 of Surat al-A'raf on printed p. 551, in the evidence for "ghabirin" before the line of Yazid ibn al-Hakam; the next batch begins on printed p. 552 (marker 556).
