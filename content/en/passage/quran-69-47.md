## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 52 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "There is not one of you who can prevent it" as meaning: There is not one of you, O people, who could prevent Muhammad from fabricating some falsehoods against Us, so that We would seize him by the right hand and then sever his aorta, preventing Us from punishing him and what We would do to him. It has also been said that "prevent" is used in the plural, and it is a verb form for "one," and "one" in a single word is used in response to its meaning, because its meaning is plural, and the Arabs use "one" for one, two, and many, as in the verse "We make no distinction between any of His messengers," and "between" only applies to two or more. He then quotes statements from Qatadah in this section.
