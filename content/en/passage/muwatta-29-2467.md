## Summary
Malik transmits that Ibn Shihab, Sulayman ibn Yasar and Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman used to say: the blood-money for an accidental killing is: twenty she-camels in their second year, twenty in their third year, twenty male camels in their third year, twenty in their fourth year, and twenty in their fifth year. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.420}
