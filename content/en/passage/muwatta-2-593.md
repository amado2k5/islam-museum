## Summary
Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr that Asma bint Umays, wife of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq, washed Abu Bakr when he died. Then she went out and asked the emigrants who were present: I am fasting, and this is a very cold day; must I wash myself? They said no. The editor notes the words 'wife of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq' are missing from one copy, and that Abu Musab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq and al-Shaybani transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.306}
