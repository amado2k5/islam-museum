## Summary
He said: I also heard Malik say: the firm Sunna among us, on which there is no dispute, is that no bequest is valid for an heir, unless the heirs of the dead man allow it. If some of them allow it and some refuse, the right of those who allowed it stands, and those who refused take their right from it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.315}
