## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 63 to 64 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So they disputed among themselves over their affairs and kept secret secrets": the magicians disputed their affairs among themselves. Their dispute was among themselves, as mentioned, that some of them said to one another, and from Qatadah, regarding His saying: "So they disputed their matter among themselves and kept secret secretions," the magicians said among themselves: If this is a magician, then we will overcome him, and if he is from the sky, then he has a command. Others said, rather, that some of them said to one another: What is this statement of a magician? In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
