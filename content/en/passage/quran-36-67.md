## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 66–67. Al-Tabari mentions the difference among interpreters regarding 'And if We willed, We could have obscured their eyes, so they would have preceded the path': Some said, 'If We willed, We could blind them from guidance and lead them astray deliberately,' and others say otherwise. He then explains that if Allah willed, He could have distorted them in their position, so they could not move forward and would not return.
