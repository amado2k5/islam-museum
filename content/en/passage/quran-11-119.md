**Commentary on verse 119 (pp. 534-539)**

On [[passage/quran-11-119|11:119]], 'and for that He created them', al-Tabari says: I said this is best because God then followed with 'and your Lord's word is fulfilled: I shall fill Hell with jinn and men together', which points to what we said. The interpreters differ on 'and for that He created them': some said He created them for difference; report 18720, from al-Hasan: for difference; 18721, 18722, 18723 and 18724, from al-Hasan: He created these for His Paradise and those for His Fire, these for His mercy and those for His punishment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 535}

Report 18725, from al-Hasan: those who have God's mercy do not differ in a way that harms them; 18726, from Ibn Abbas: He created them as two groups, one given mercy and not differing, and one not given mercy and differing, which is His saying 'some of them wretched and some happy' in [[passage/quran-11-105|11:105]]; 18727, from 'Ata': Jews, Christians and Magians, 'except those your Lord has mercy on', whom He put on Islam, 'and for that He created them', believer and unbeliever; 18728, from al-A'mash: believer and unbeliever; 18729, from Malik, asked about the verse: He created them to be two groups, one in Paradise and one in the blaze. Others said the sense is that He created them for mercy; reports 18730 and 18731, from Mujahid: for mercy. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 536}

Reports 18732, 18733 and 18734, from Mujahid: He created them for mercy; 18735, from Qatada, and 18736, from al-Dahhak: for mercy; 18737, from Ikrima: the people of truth and whoever follows it, for His mercy; 18738, from Ibn Abbas: He created them for mercy and not for punishment. Al-Tabari says: the better of the two views is that of whoever said He created them for difference, in wretchedness and happiness, for God mentioned two kinds of His creation, one the people of difference and falsehood and the other the people of truth, and then followed with 'and for that He created them', so it covers the description of both kinds, informing of each group that it is made easy for what it was created for. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 537}

If someone asks, if the sense is as you said, then those who differ should not be blamed for their difference, since their Lord created them for that, and those who enjoyed should be the blamed ones? The answer is that the sense is not what you suppose, and the meaning is that people do not cease differing by falsehood in their religions and creeds, except those your Lord has mercy on and guides to the truth, and because of His knowledge, and according to His effective knowledge of them before He created them, that among them would be the believer and unbeliever, the wretched and the happy, He created them; the lam in 'and for that He created them' is in the sense of 'upon', like saying 'I honoured you for your kindness to me' and 'I honoured you because of your kindness to me'. As for 'the word of your Lord is fulfilled: I shall fill Hell with jinn and men together', it is by His prior knowledge that they deserve to burn in it for their disbelief in God and going against His command; 'the word of your Lord is fulfilled' is an oath, like someone saying my oath is that I shall visit you, which is why it is met with the oath lam. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 538}

As for 'of the jinn', they are what is hidden from the sight of the children of Adam, 'and men' meaning the children of Adam. It is said they were called jinn because they were hidden in the dark; report 18739, from Abu Malik: they were called jinn because they were on the 'janan', and the angels are all jinn; 18740, from Abu Malik: the jinn are the angels. Al-Tabari explains that Abu Malik means that Iblis was of the angels and the jinn are his offspring, and that the angels are called jinn by him, as he explained earlier in the book. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 539}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
