## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And indeed, We know that they say, 'Only a human knows it.' The tongue of those they aspire to is a foreigner, and this is a clear Arabic tongue." And we do know that these polytheists say out of ignorance among themselves: Muhammad only knows this which is followed by a human among the children of Adam, and it is not from Allah. Allah Almighty says to them, denying their statement: Do you not know the lie of what you say? The tongue of those to whom you atheize says: You incline to it. That he teaches Muhammad, a non-Arab. This is because they claimed that the one who teaches Muhammad this Qur'an is a Roman slave, so Allah Almighty said: "The tongue of the one they atheize to is a foreign tongue, and this is a clear Arabic tongue," meaning: This Qur'an is a clear Arabic tongue. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Ishaq, and Sufyan.
