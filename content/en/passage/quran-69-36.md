## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 37 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Nor any food except from the discharge of wounds”: He, may His praise be exalted, says: He will have no food, just as he did not encourage feeding the poor in this world, except food from the discharge of wounds, which is the pus that flows from the inhabitants of Hell. Some of the Arabs of Basra said: Any wound that you wash and something comes out of it is called “ghislin,” a form derived from washing wounds and sores, with the addition of the letters ya and nun, similar to the word “afrin.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
