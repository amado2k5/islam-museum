## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Perhaps your Lord will have mercy on you, and if you return, We will return and make Hell a bed for the disbelievers": Perhaps your Lord, O Children of Israel, will have mercy on you after taking revenge on you through the people whom Allah sends upon you, so that His mission will be disgraceful upon you, and that they will enter the mosque as they first entered it, rescue you from their hands, rescue you from the humiliation He brings upon you, and lift you from the lethargy you have fallen into, and honor you thereafter. And "Perhaps it is from Allah" is a duty. And Allah did this to them, so their numbers increased after that, and He removed their baseness and made among them kings and prophets. He said to them: 'And if you return, O people of the Children of Israel, for disobeying My command, and killing My messengers, We will return to you with killings and captivity, and bringing humiliation and humiliation to the children upon you.' So they returned, so Allah returned to them with His punishment and displeasure upon them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Zayd.
