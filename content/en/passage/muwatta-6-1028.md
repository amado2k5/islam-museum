## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that the Messenger of God said: five wicked animals may be killed in the sanctuary: the mouse, the scorpion, the crow, the kite and the rabid dog. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said it is connected to the Prophet, supported by hadiths from Ibn Umar and Aisha; and that Waki transmitted it from Malik from Hisham from his father from Aisha, and that it is preserved from Aisha and from Ibn Umar. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.480}
