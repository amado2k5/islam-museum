## Summary
Malik says about one who wishes to wear some of the garments not proper for one in ihram, or to shorten his hair, or to touch perfume without necessity, because the cost of the ransom is light for him: it is not proper for anyone to do that, and it was permitted only out of necessity, and whoever does it owes the ransom. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.560}
