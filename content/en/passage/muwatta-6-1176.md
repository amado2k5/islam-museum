## Summary
Yahya said: Malik was asked about a man who forgot to shave at Mina during the pilgrimage: is there a concession for him to shave in Mecca? He said: that is broad, but shaving at Mina is more beloved to him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.531}
