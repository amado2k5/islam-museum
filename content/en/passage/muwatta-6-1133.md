## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, that Abu Ayyub al-Ansari set out on pilgrimage, and when he was at al-Nazi on the road to Mecca his mounts went astray; he came to Umar ibn al-Khattab on the day of sacrifice and told him. Umar said to him: do what the lesser pilgrim does, then you are released; and when the pilgrimage reaches you in a later year, make the pilgrimage and offer what is feasible of an offering. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (1429, 1430 in the editor's order). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.515}
