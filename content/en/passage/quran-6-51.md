## Summary
Verse 51 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 51 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "warn by it those who fear that they will be gathered to their Lord, with no protector or intercessor besides him, that they may guard against evil" al-Tabari says to his prophet Muhammad: warn, Muhammad, with the Qur'an we sent you the people who fear being gathered to their Lord, knowing that it will be, so they trust God's promise and threat, act in what pleases God and strive constantly in what will save them in their return from God's punishment; "they have no protector besides him" means they have none, against God's punishment if he punishes them, to help them or rescue them from it, "nor intercessor" to intercede for them with God and free them from his penalty, "that they may guard against evil" says: warn them so that they fear God for themselves, obey their Lord, work for their return and beware his wrath by avoiding disobedience to him. It was said "warn by it those who fear that they will be gathered" meaning those who know they will be gathered, "fear" put in place of "knowledge", because their fear was from their knowing it will come to pass without doubt. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 373}

The editor refers for "warning", "gathering", "protector", "intercessor" and "fear" to the volumes and pages given and to al-Farra's Ma'ani al-Qur'an, and keeps "striving constantly" from the manuscript where print has "lasting".

This is God's command to his prophet to teach his companions what he revealed to him, to remind them and to turn to them with the warning, while the polytheists turned from it, after excuse was made to them and the proof established against them, until God himself is the judge in their matter with whatever judgement he wills for them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 374}

The editor keeps "and the polytheists turned from it" from the manuscript and says the printed text altered it and spoiled the sentence.

Verse 51 closes here at p. 374, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-52|6:52]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
