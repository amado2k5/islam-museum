## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Or do they have partners who legislated for them from religion that which Allah has not permitted? And if it were not for the word of judgment, He would have judged between them. And indeed, the wrongdoers will have a painful punishment": Allah Almighty says: Or do these polytheists have partners in their polytheism and misguidance? "They legislated for them from the religion what Allah has not permitted?" He means: They invented for them from the religion what Allah did not permit them to innovate. "And if it were not for the word of judgment, He would have judged between them." Allah says: Were it not for Allah's earlier saying that punishment in this world would not be hastened, and that it has been said that they are delayed in punishment until the Day of Judgment, then the judgment between you and them would have been completed by hastening the punishment for them in this world, but for them in the Hereafter there is a painful punishment, as He, the Exalted, said: "And indeed, the wrongdoers will have a painful punishment," meaning: And the disbelievers in Allah will have a painful punishment on the Day of Resurrection.
