## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who strive in Our verses in disability, those are prepared in punishment": Those who act upon Our signs, meaning in Our proofs and the verses of Our Book, seeking to nullify them and want to extinguish His light as helpers, thinking they are causing Us to pass by themselves and are incapacitating us. "Those are the ones prepared for the punishment," meaning they will be prepared in the punishment of Hell on the Day of Resurrection. "Say, 'Indeed, my Lord extends provision for whom He wills among His servants.'" Allah says: Say, 'O Muhammad, indeed my Lord extends provision for whom He wills of His creation, and He expands it for him both as an honor for him and as a favor, and He decrees it for whom He wills.' and he distresses and distresses him out of humiliation and not as an insult, but rather as a trial and a test: "And whatever you spend of anything, He will replace it," meaning: Whatever you spend, O people, in obedience to Allah, Allah will replace it upon you.
