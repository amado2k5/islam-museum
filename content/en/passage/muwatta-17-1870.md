## Summary
Malik says: the agreed practice among us, on which there is no dispute, is that whoever buys food, wheat, barley, spelt, maize, millet, any pulse, or anything resembling pulse on which zakat is due, or any kind of condiment: oil, clarified butter, honey, vinegar, cheese, sesame oil, milk, and the like, the buyer does not sell any of it until he takes possession and receives it in full. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.169}
