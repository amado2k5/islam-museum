## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "And Moses said, 'My Lord, He is most knowing of who brings guidance from His Possession, and who will have the outcome of the abode. Indeed, the wrongdoers will not succeed.'" "And Moses said," answering Pharaoh: "My Lord knows best" who is right from us, O Pharaoh, from the falsehood, and who brings guidance to the right path and clarification from clear evidence from Him, and who will have the praiseworthy outcome in the Hereafter from us. This is the opposition of the Prophet Moses to Pharaoh, and it is a beautiful address, as he refrained from saying to him: Rather, it is the one who deceived his people, destroyed His soldiers, and misled his followers, not me. But he said: "My Lord knows best who brings guidance from Him, and who will have the outcome of the home." Then he went to great lengths in condemning the enemy of God with a more beautiful address, saying: "Indeed, the wrongdoers will not succeed," meaning that the disbelievers will not succeed nor will their disbelieving seekers succeed in this way, meaning Pharaoh will neither succeed nor succeed because of his disbelief in him.
