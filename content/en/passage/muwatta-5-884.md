## Summary
Ziyad reports from Malik from Ibn Shihab that the Messenger of God went to relieve himself in the houses. The editor notes that the words 'while he was in seclusion' dropped from copy M, and that Abu Musab (881) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.426}
