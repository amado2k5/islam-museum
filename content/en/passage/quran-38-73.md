## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation was included in the interpretation of verses 71–74. Al-Tabari mentions that God said to the angels, 'I am a human being created from clay.' So when I have leveled him and breathed into him of My spirit, they fell down in prostration before him. All the angels prostrated together except Iblis, who was arrogant and was among the disbelievers.
