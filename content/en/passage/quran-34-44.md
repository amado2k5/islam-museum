## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 45 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We did not give them any books to study, nor did We send to them before you any warner": And We did not reveal to the polytheists who said to Muhammad when he brought them Our verses: This is clear magic, with what they call books to study: He says: they read them, as in Qatadah, His saying: "And We did not give them any books to study," meaning they read them. "And We did not send to them before you any warner," meaning: And We did not send to these polytheists from your people, O Muhammad, about what they say and do before you of any prophet warning them of Our punishment against him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
