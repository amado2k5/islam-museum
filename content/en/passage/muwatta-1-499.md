## Summary
The first report in the chapter on permission to pray before and after the two festivals. Yahya transmits from Malik from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim that his father al-Qasim ibn Muhammad used to pray four rak'as before going out to the prayer ground. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.255}
