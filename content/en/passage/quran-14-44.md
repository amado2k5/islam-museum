## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse as an order to warn people before the day of punishment, when the wrongdoers will ask for a short respite. Mujahid takes that day to be the Day of Resurrection, while Qatada takes the warning as one given in this world. The reminder of their earlier oaths is, for him, a rebuke for denying resurrection.
