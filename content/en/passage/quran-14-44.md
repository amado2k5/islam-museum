## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains: "And warn the people on the Day when the punishment comes to them": Warn, O Muhammad, those to whom I sent you the punishment of God on the Day of Resurrection — from Mujahid on the Day of Resurrection, and from Qatadah, warn them in this world before the punishment comes to them. "Those who have wronged will say," meaning they disbelieved and wronged themselves: Our Lord, delay us—that is, delay Your punishment from us—until a near term when we respond to Your call so that we believe and follow the messengers; he presents the parsing of "and says": it is raised as a conjunction with "He will come to them," which is not an answer to the command, even if it were an answer in which it is permissible to renew and accusative as in the poet's verse. He mentions from Al-Ala' ibn Sayaba his denial of accusative in response to the command with the fa, and from Al-Farra' that he is the one who taught Mu'adh and his companions. Then "Did you not swear before what was to pass for you?" is a rebuke to the polytheists of Quraysh for denying resurrection: You swore that there would be no transition for you from this world to the Hereafter; and from Mujahid: disappearance means the transition from this world to the Hereafter. It is narrated from Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Quradhi that the people of Hellfire call out with this supplication, and it is answered with rebuke up to the end of the passage.
