## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Salim ibn Abdullah that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say that nothing that passes in front of one who prays breaks the prayer, in the chapter on the permission to pass in front of one who prays. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.222} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.223}
