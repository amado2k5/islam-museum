## Summary
Verse 121 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 121 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on verse 121: the abode of Jahannam and the word for escape (pp. 225-226, note 4 on p. 225 and notes 1-2 on p. 226).** Al-Tabari says that those meant are the people who took the devil as an ally instead of God, and that their abode, the place to which they will go, is Jahannam. The closing words mean that there is nowhere for them to flee from it: when God sends them there on the Day of Resurrection they will find no turning-point to which to turn aside. He explains the word for escape through the verb meaning to turn away from a matter, and cites a report of Ibn Umar that the Messenger of God sent out a raiding party in which he was, and when they met the polytheists they turned and fled; some narrators gave a variant verb of close meaning, and he adds that the noun for escape and the noun for menstruation are close in sense. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of the word for abode (p. 494 and following), notes that the printed text has "sent us" where the manuscript has the correct reading, says he could not find the rest of Ibn Umar's report though he thinks it well known, and notes that the printer and the manuscript had misplaced the dots on the two verbs, which he restored. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 226}
