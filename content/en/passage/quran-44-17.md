## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 19 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "The Day We will strike the greatest oppression, indeed We will take retribution": "Indeed, O polytheists, if the punishment that will befall you and the harm that will befall you, and then you return in your disbelief and break the covenant you made with your Lord, I will take revenge on you on the day I will strike you with My greatest oppression in the immediate aftermath of this world, destroying you, and Allah will remove them, and they will return. Then the Exalted, Glorified be He, will strike them with His great oppression in this world, and He will kill them by the sword." The interpreters have differed regarding the major oppression; some said: It is God's oppression of the polytheists of Quraysh on the Day of Badr. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Al-A'mash, and others.
