**Commentary on verse 11 (pp. 257-258)**

On [[passage/quran-11-11|11:11]] al-Tabari says: God then excepts from the man He described with these two traits 'those who were patient and did good deeds'; the exception is allowed because 'man' is a kind and has the sense of a plural, as in [[passage/quran-103-2|103:2]] and [[passage/quran-103-3|103:3]]. If hardship and difficulty in the world touch them it does not turn them from obeying God but they are patient for His command and decree, and if they gain ease and wide provision they thank Him and pay His rights from what He gave them. 'Those have forgiveness' means He forgives it for them and does not shame them with it at the return, 'and a great reward' meaning that with the forgiveness of their sins they have from God a reward for the good deeds they did in the world, abundant and mighty. Report 18006, from Ibn Jurayj: 'except those who were patient' in affliction, 'and did good deeds' in favour; 'those have forgiveness' for their sins, 'and a great reward' he said is Paradise. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 258}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
