## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 5 in a single syllable. He explains the phrase "for the familiarity of Quraysh": the reciters differed in pronounced: "For the familiarity of Quraysh, their familiarity." The majority of the reciters of the lands read it as a 'by' after a hamza for 'Ilaf and their Ilafuham,' except for Abu Ja'far, who agreed with others in saying 'for their affection,' so he pronounced it with a 'ya' after a hamza. There was disagreement about him regarding 'Ilafuham,' and it was narrated that he pronounced it as 'ilfahum' as a verbal noun derived from an alif, without a ya. Some narrated from him that he read it as 'ilahum' without a ya' with a kasora for the alif. The correct reading in this regard for me: whoever reads it is: 'For the familiarity of Quraysh is their familiarity' with the affirmation of the ya' after the hamza, whoever familiarizes something with it is familiar with it, due to the consensus of the scholars on it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibrahim, and Sufyan.
