## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Say, 'I was not innovative among the messengers, nor do I know what is done to me or you. I follow only what is revealed to me. And I am nothing but a clear warner.'" Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people from Quraysh, "I was not innovative among the messengers," meaning: I was not the first messenger of God whom He sent to His creation. Before me, he had many messengers sent to nations before you, and it is said of him that he was innovative in this matter, and he was excellent in it, if he had a beginning. Among the innovations is the saying of 'Adi ibn Zaydfala: 'I am an innovation among the events that befall men who were exposed after my misery. And happily. Among the innovations is the saying of Al-Ahwas: I was proud and became arrogant, so I said, 'Look at me.' It is not ignorance that I brought with Badi', meaning at the beginning. It is said: it is an innovation from a people of innovation. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
