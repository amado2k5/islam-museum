## Summary
A saying of Malik about a woman who owes fasting for killing by mistake, and who menstruates in the middle of her fast: when she becomes clean she does not delay the fast, but continues building on what she has already fasted. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik (814). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.405}
