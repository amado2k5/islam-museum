## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "except for those who repent after that and reform, for indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful": The interpreters differed on what was excepted from the phrase: "Except those who repented after that and reformed." Some said: It was an exception to the phrase: "And never accept their testimony, for those are the defiantly disobedient." They said: If the slander repents, his testimony is accepted and the name of immorality is removed from him, whether there is a fixed punishment or not. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Ishaq and Ibrahim.
