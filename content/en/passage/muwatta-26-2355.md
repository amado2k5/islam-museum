## Summary
Malik said: the agreed practice among us is that every manumission a man frees in a bequest he made, in health or in illness, he may retract whenever he wishes and change whenever he wishes, unless it was conditional freedom; if it was conditional freedom, there is no way to retract what he made conditional. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.371}
