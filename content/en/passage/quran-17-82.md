## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 82 to 83 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And say, 'Truth has come and falsehood has vanished; indeed, falsehood is bound to vanish.'" And say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists who almost provoked you from the earth to expel you from it: "The truth has come, and falsehood has vanished." Interpreters differ regarding the meaning of the truth that God commanded His Prophet to inform the polytheists that it had come, and falsehood, which He commanded to inform them that it had perished. Some said: The truth is the Qur'an in this context, and falsehood is Satan. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
