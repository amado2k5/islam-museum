## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 123 to 127 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "when their brother Hud said to them, 'Do you not fear Him?': Allah's punishment for your disbelief in Him: 'Indeed, I am a messenger for you' from my Lord, commanding you to obey Him, warning you against your disbelief, trustworthy in His revelation and message, 'So fear Allah' by obeying Him and fulfilling what He commands you and forbids you. 'And obey' in what He commands you of fear Allah and warns you of His dominion. 'And I do not ask you for any reward for it.' He means: 'And I do not ask of you for my command to fear Allah any reward or recompense.' 'Indeed, my reward is only with the Lord of the worlds.' He says: My reward for my advice to you is only with the Lord of the worlds.
