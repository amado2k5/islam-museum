## Summary
In the Book of Jihad: Anas ibn Malik reports about his uncle, who missed Badr and made a vow. When the people scattered at Uhud, he advanced and said to Sa'd ibn Mu'adh, "By the Lord of al-Nadr, I smell Paradise from beyond Uhud". He was found with some eighty wounds, and was mutilated until only his sister recognised him by his fingertips. People thought the verse "among the believers are men who were true to what they covenanted with God" was revealed about him and others like him. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
