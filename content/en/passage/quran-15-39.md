## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 39 and 40 in one syllable: Iblis said, "My Lord, by what You have led me astray," meaning by Your misguidance to me, as if his words came out of the oath as it is said, "By God, I will mislead them." And "I will make the land beautiful for them in the disobedience of God and make them beloved to them," "And I will mislead them altogether," meaning I will mislead them from the path of righteousness. And "Except for Your sincere servants among them": except those whom God has chosen sincerely by His guidance and guided, and Iblis has no authority over him and no power over him—according to Al-Dahhak, the sincere ones are the believers, and from Qatadah, it is God's fold. "Al-Mukhlisin" is read with a fatha on those who are sincere in obedience, so Satan has no way over him.
