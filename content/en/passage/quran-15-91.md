## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 89 to 91 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "those who made the Qur'an in groups": They believe in some and disbelieve in others. And from Sa'id ibn Jubayr that he said regarding the phrase: "Just as We revealed to those who divide," he said: They are the People of the Book. And from Abu Bishr, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, who said in this verse: "Just as We revealed to those who divided the Qur'an into groups," he said: They are the People of the Book, believing in some of it and disbelieving in others. And Abu Bishr informed us, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: "Those who made the Qur'an into parts," he said: They are the People of the Book who divided it and made it members, so they believed in some of it and disbelieved in others. He said: They divided it and made it parts like the parts of the parts. He said: They are the People of the Book. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Sa'id bin Jubayr, and Al-A'mash.
