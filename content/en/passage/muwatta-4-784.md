## Summary
It reached Malik that the new moon was seen in the evening in the time of Uthman ibn Affan, and Uthman did not break his fast until evening came and the sun had set. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.387}
