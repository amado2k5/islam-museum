## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 24–27. Al-Tabari mentions that the angels are commanded to stop these polytheists, as they are held accountable. Interpreters differ regarding what they are asked about, and then they are told, 'Why do you not support them? Rather, today they are submissive,' and some turned to each other, questioning.
