## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8-10 as a single section. He interprets the phrase, “They fulfill their vows and fear a Day whose evil will be widespread,” as meaning that the righteous who drink from a cup whose mixture is camphor are righteous because they fulfilled their vows to God in obedience to Him. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
