**Commentary on verse 39 (p. 317)**

On [[passage/quran-11-39|11:39]] al-Tabari reports Noah's saying to his people: 'you will soon know', people, when God's command comes, who is the one lost, 'on whom a punishment will come that disgraces him', meaning whoever of us or you God's punishment reaches, it shames and humbles him, 'and a lasting punishment will fall on him', meaning that in the next life, besides that, a punishment that never ends will come down on him and stay with him for ever. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'disgrace' and 'lasting punishment'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 317}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
