## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on hastening to break the fast. Malik transmits from Abu Hazim ibn Dinar, from Sahl ibn Saad al-Saidi, that the Prophet said: people will remain well so long as they hasten to break the fast. The editor notes that Abu Musab and al-Tirmidhi (699), Ibn Hibban and al-Baghawi, Ishaq al-Tabba and Ismail ibn Umar in Ahmad, Suwayd, al-Qa'nabi, al-Tinnisi in al-Bukhari (1957), Ibn al-Qasim in al-Nasai, al-Shafii, and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Shaybani (364) transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.389}
