## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, O servants of those who have believed, fear your Lord for those who have done good in this world; and the earth of Allah is vast. Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account": "Say," O Muhammad, to My servants who have believed: "O servants who have believed" in Allah, and were truthful to His Messenger, "Fear your Lord" by obeying Him and avoiding His sins: "For those who have done good in this world is good." Then the scholars of interpretation differed in interpreting this; some said: Its meaning is for those who obey Allah a good deed in this world, and they said 'in' in a good relationship, and made the meaning of good deeds to be health and well-being. In the passage, statements from Mujahid and Qatadah are also quoted.
