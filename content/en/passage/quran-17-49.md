## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they said, 'Behold, were we bones and remains? Are we to be sent as a new creation?': Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of those who do not believe in the Hereafter among the polytheists of Quraysh, and they said by their affliction: 'When were we bones'—we would not be shattered or broken after our death and affliction. 'And remnants' means dust in our graves, as: He said: all of them from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, Allah says: 'Rubata'—he said: dust. And he said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly. He said: Muawiyah narrated to us, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, regarding the phrase: 'And they said, 'When we were bones and rubble,' he says: dust, and there is no singular for 'rafta', which is like 'daqta' and 'hatam'. It is said: 'Raft' is 'yarafat raftan', so it is 'marfuwat' when it becomes like debris and rubble. In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Ibn Jurayj.
