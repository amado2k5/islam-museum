## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 17 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'You are nothing but human beings like us, and what has the Most Merciful revealed of anything. Indeed, you deny.'" The people of the village said to the three to whom they were sent when they told them that they had been sent to them with what they were sent: You are nothing but people like us, and if you were messengers as you say, you would be angels. "And the Most Merciful has not sent down to you of anything," meaning: they said: The Most Merciful has not sent down to you any message, nor a book, nor commanded you about us in anything. "Indeed, you are not but liars" in your saying, "Indeed, you are sent to Us."
