## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "But if you are arrogant, then those with your Lord glorify Him by night and by day, and they never tire": So if you are arrogant, O Muhammad, those among whom you are among the polytheists of Quraysh, and you are honored to prostrate to Allah, who created them and created the sun and the moon, then the angels who are with your Lord are not arrogant about that, nor do they glorify Him, but glorify Him and pray day and night. "And they do not tire," meaning: they do not abandon their worship or tire of praying to Him. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Abbas.
