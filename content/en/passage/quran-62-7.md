## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains his statement, “And they will never wish for it because of what their hands have put forth. And Allah is Knowing of the wrongdoers.” “And they will never wish for it” means: The Jews will never wish for death “because of what their hands have put forth,” meaning: because of the sins they have committed in this world, and the evil deeds they have perpetrated. “And Allah is Knowing of the wrongdoers” means: Allah is All-Knowing of those of His creation who have wronged themselves, thus destroying themselves by disbelieving in Allah.
