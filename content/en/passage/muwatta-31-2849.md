## Summary
It reached Malik that a needy man asked Aisha, Mother of the Believers, while grapes were in front of her. She said to someone: take one grape and give it to him. He kept looking at it and marvelling. Aisha said: are you surprised? How many atoms' weight do you think is in this grape? {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.596}
