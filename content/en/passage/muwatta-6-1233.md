## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Rijal Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman, from Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, that when Aisha made the pilgrimage with women and feared they would menstruate, she brought them forward on the day of sacrifice to circumambulate; if they menstruated after that, she did not wait for them, but departed with them while menstruating, once they had circumambulated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.551}
