## Summary
A saying of Malik transmitted by Yahya in the chapter on the woman with irregular bleeding: the practice of the people of Medina concerning the irregularly bleeding woman follows the report of Hisham ibn Urwa from his father, that is, one bath and then ablution for each prayer, and this is what he most liked of what he heard on the matter. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.109}
