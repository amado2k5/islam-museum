## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Prophet said: the fire the children of Adam kindle is one part of seventy parts of the fire of Hell. They said: Messenger of God, it would be enough! He said: it was given preference over it by sixty-nine parts. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.593}
