## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on permission to pray in a single garment: a questioner asked the Prophet about praying in one garment, and he answered in a question: "Or do all of you have two garments?". The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i, al-Tahawi and al-Bayhaqi, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.202} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.203}
