## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: any woman whose husband has disappeared, and who does not know where he is, waits four years, then completes four months and ten days of waiting, and then is free to remarry. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.88}
