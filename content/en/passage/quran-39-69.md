## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And the earth shone with the light of its Lord, and the Book was established, and the prophets and martyrs were brought, and it was judged between them in truth, and they are not wronged": the earth was illuminated by the light of its Lord. It is said: The sun rose when it set and illuminated, and it shone brightly, when it rose, and this is when the Most Merciful appears to separate the decree among His creation. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
