## Summary
In the Book of Ablution, the chapter on the excellence of ablution. The report of al-Sa'ib ibn Yazid combines two scenes: the companions' use of the Prophet's leftover ablution water, and his seeing the seal of prophethood as a mark between his shoulders like the knob of a bridal tent. It is the clearest description in Sahih al-Bukhari of this physical sign. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
