## Summary
Malik says: the agreed practice among us is that there is no harm in a camel for a camel of its kind plus some dirhams, hand to hand. There is no harm in a camel for a camel of its kind plus dirhams, camel for camel hand to hand, and the dirhams on a term. He said: there is no good in a camel for a camel of its kind plus dirhams, with the dirhams in cash and the camel on a term. If he delays both the camel and the dirhams, there is no good in that either. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.181}
