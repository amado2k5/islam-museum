## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And let those who cannot find marriage remain chaste until Allah may enrich them of His bounty. And those who seek the Book from what your right hands possess, then write them down with them, if you know good about them, and give them from the wealth of Allah which He has given you. And do not force your daughters into prostitution if they desire chastity, so that you may seek the reward of the life of this world. And whoever hates them, then indeed, after compelling them, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." They do not marry women to commit what Allah has forbidden them of immorality, "until Allah enriches them from His abundance and expands His provision for them." And His saying: "And those who seek the Book from what your right hands possess," He, Glorified and Exalted, means: "And those who seek correspondence from among you among your mamluks," "then write to them if you know good about them." Scholars differed regarding the matter of a man writing to his servant about whom he has learned good, and whether His saying: "Write to them if you know good about them" is obligatory or recommended? Some said: It is obligatory for a man to correspond with his servant whom he knows is good if the servant asks him for it. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
