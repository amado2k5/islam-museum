## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa that his father used to say: for a wild cow, a cow; for a sheep, a sheep. The editor notes that part of the wording of the ruling does not appear in the copy photographed. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.554}
