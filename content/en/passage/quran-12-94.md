**Commentary on verse 94 (p. 248)**

On "and when the caravan set out, their father said, I find the scent of Joseph, if you did not think me feeble-minded", al-Tabari says: when the caravan of Ya'qub's sons set out from Joseph, heading to Ya'qub, their father Ya'qub said "I find the scent of Joseph". The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "fasl". The rest of the speech is on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 248}

We stop here at the foot of p. 248, at the opening of the commentary on verse 94 (at Ya'qub's words "I find the scent of Joseph"); the next batch begins at p. 249. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 248}
