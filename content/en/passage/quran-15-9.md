## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari affirms that remembrance is the Qur'an, and that God protects it from any falsehood or any truth being diminished from it. He reports from Mujahid that the meaning of "with us" is to preserve it, and from Qatadah, that Satan, who is falsehood in his interpretation, cannot add falsehood to it nor diminish any truth from it.
