## Summary
Verse 62 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 62 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Al-Tabari's explanation of the verse (p. 514, notes 1-2).** Al-Tabari says the sense is: how will it be with these who wish to seek judgement before the taghut while claiming to accept what was sent to you and before you, when a vengeance from God falls on them for their former sins, and they then come to you taking oaths in God's name, falsely and dishonestly, that they intended nothing but kindness among themselves and getting the right outcome in what they brought. He says this is a report from God that warnings and punishments do not restrain the hypocrites from hypocrisy, and that if a punishment from God reaches them for taking their case to the taghut they do not repent but swear falsely, daring to do so against God. The editor refers to an earlier explanation of what the hands sent ahead (page numbers scrambled) and says the manuscript and the print both carry a redundant conjunction that is better dropped. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 514}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 514}
