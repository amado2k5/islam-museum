## Summary
A report of Jarir ibn Abdullah in the chapter on believers seeing their Lord, in the Book of the Prayer of Travellers in Sahih Muslim. It links keeping the dawn and afternoon prayers to seeing God, and Jarir then recites a verse of Surat Ta-Ha. {cite:sahih-muslim}
