## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on one who ends the prayer after two rak'as by forgetfulness: every lapse that was a deficiency in prayer has its prostration before the salam, and every lapse that was an addition has its prostration after the salam. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.149}
