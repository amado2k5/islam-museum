## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, Allah defends those who believe. Indeed, Allah does not like every disbeliever who disbelieves": Indeed, Allah repels the deceitful of the polytheists from those who believe in Allah and His Messenger. Indeed, Allah does not like every traitor who betrays Allah, disobeys His commands and prohibitions, disobeys Satan, who is a 'disbeliever' who says: ungrateful for His blessings, not knowing the right of the benefactor and thus thanking Him for them. It is said that Allah meant by this that Allah defended the disbelievers of Quraysh from those who were among the most prominent believers before their migration.
