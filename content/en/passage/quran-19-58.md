## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those upon whom Allah has bestowed favor from among the prophets, from the descendants of Adam, from those We carried with Noah, and from the descendants of Abraham and Israel, and from those whom We guided and chose when the verses of the Most Merciful are recited to them, they fell down in prostration and weeping": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: These are the ones whose news You have retributed for you in this surah, O Muhammad, whom Allah has favored with His guidance, so He guided them to the right path among the prophets from the descendants of Adam, from the descendants of those who carried us with Noah in the ark, and from the descendants of Abraham The Friend of the Merciful, from the descendants of Israel, and among those who guided us to believe in Allah, act upon His obedience, and chose: He says: Among those whom We chose and chosen for Our message and revelation, the one He meant from the descendants of Adam is Idris, and by whom He meant the descendants of those who carried us with Noah with Abraham, by which He meant Isaac, Jacob, and Ishmael, and by whom He meant from the descendants of Israel: Moses, Aaron, Zechariah, Jesus, and his mother Mary. Therefore, Allah separated their lineages, even though He gathered all of them Adam, because among them are those who are not descendants of those who were with Noah on the Ark, namely Idris, and Idris is a grandfather. In the passage, he quotes from Al-A'mash and Abraham.
