## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Alas, for the servants, what comes to them from a messenger except that they mock him": Oh, the sorrow of the servants for themselves, and their regret and eagerness in mocking the messengers of Allah, "no messenger comes to them" from Allah "except that they mock him." He mentioned that this is mentioned in some readings: "Oh, the sorrow of the servants for themselves." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
