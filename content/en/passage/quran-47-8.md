## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 8 to 9 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And those who disbelieve, wretched for them and misled their deeds": "And those who disbelieve" in Allah, and denied His oneness, "so woe to them," meaning: disgrace and misery and affliction for them. As Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "And those who disbelieve, woe to them," he said: misery for them. And His saying: "And the most misguided of their deeds" means that their deeds were done without guidance or uprightness, because they were done in obedience to Satan, not in obedience to the Most Merciful. In the Maqta', he quotes sayings from Qatadah and Ibn Zayd.
