## Summary
Verse 150 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 150 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**God is the Master (pp. 277-278).** Al-Tabari: God is your support, believers, to deliver you from obeying the deniers. The "rather" is used because the preceding verse forbids obeying them; it is as if He said "do not obey the deniers, or they will turn you back on your heels", and then began a new statement that God is your Master, so obey Him and not them, and He is the best of helpers. For that reason the name of God is in the nominative, though the accusative, in the sense of "rather obey God, your Master", would also be sound. The Master is your guardian and helper against your enemies, and the best of helpers, not those among the Jews and the deniers of God to whom you fled; so hold fast to God and seek help from Him alone and not from others who wish you ill and lie in wait for you with harm. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on the word for master (vol. 6, p. 141). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 277} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 278}

**Ibn Ishaq (8001).** If what you say with your tongues is true in your hearts, hold fast to Him, seek help from no one else, and do not turn back on your heels, apostates from your religion. The editor traces it to Ibn Hisham's Sira (3: 119-120), a continuation of 7998, with slight differences of wording. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 278}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
