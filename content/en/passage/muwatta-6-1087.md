## Summary
Malik was asked whether a man may stand and talk with a man during the circumambulation that is obligatory on him. He said: I do not like that for him. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1309) and Suwayd (550) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.499}
