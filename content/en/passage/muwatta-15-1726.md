## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar was asked about a pregnant woman whose husband died. He said she is free once she has given birth. A man of the Ansar who had been with him told him that Umar ibn al-Khattab said: if she gave birth while her husband was still on his bier and not yet buried, she would be free. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.105}
