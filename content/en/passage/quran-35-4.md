## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 4 to 5 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And if they deny you, then messengers before you have lied, and to Allah all matters will return." And if these polytheists of Allah from your people deny you, then let them not grieve you or honor you, for that is the way of those like them who disbelieve in Allah before them, and their denial of Allah's messengers whom He sent to them before you. The polytheists of your people will never be like them, so they follow their path in denying you and follow their way. "And to Allah all matters return." Allah Almighty says: And to Allah is the return of your matter and theirs. The punishment is upon them if they do not turn to obedience to us in following You, acknowledging Your prophethood, and accepting the advice you have called them to, in retaliation for what We have permitted their counterparts among the nations whose messengers before you denied it, and who saved you and your followers from that. Our way is with those before you regarding our messengers and our saints. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
