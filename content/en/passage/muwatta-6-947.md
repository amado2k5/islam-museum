## Summary
A saying of Malik: the matter among us is that whoever combines the pilgrimage and the lesser pilgrimage does not take anything from his hair, and does not release himself from anything until he sacrifices an offering, if he has one, and releases himself at Mina on the day of sacrifice. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1077) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.452}
