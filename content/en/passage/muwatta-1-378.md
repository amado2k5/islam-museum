## Summary
The first report in the chapter on permission for a woman to pray in a shirt and headscarf, a transmitted report. Yahya transmits from Malik that he was told Aisha, the Prophet's wife, used to pray in a shirt and headscarf. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.204}
