## Summary
In the Book of Jizya: Ibn Abbas reports that on the day of the conquest of Mecca the Prophet declared the city's permanent sanctity: its thorns are not cut, its game not hunted, and its lost property not picked up except by one who will announce it. At al-Abbas's request he excepted izkhir grass for their smiths and houses. He declared that emigration had ended, but that jihad and intention remain. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
