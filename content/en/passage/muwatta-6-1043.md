## Summary
A saying of Malik: this is our ruling on one who is held back by an enemy, as the Prophet and his companions were held back. As for one held back without an enemy, he does not release himself short of the House. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1174) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.485}
