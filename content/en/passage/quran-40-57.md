## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The creation of the heavens and the earth is greater than the creation of mankind, but most people do not know": The creation of the heavens and the earth and their creation from nothing is greater than you, O people, if you are the greatest of the creation of mankind, and their creation from nothing is beyond the creation of mankind. But most people do not know that creating all of that is easy for Allah.
