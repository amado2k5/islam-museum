## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "For the poor emigrants who were expelled from their homes and their wealth seeking bounty from Allah and His pleasure and supporting Allah and His Messenger; those are the truthful": so that what Allah has given to His Messenger is not a state among the wealthy among you, but it is for the poor emigrants. It is said: By 'emigrants' I mean the migration of Quraysh. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
