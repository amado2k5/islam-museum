## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "those who inherit Paradise and will abide therein forever": "Those who inherit" means the orchard with vineyard, which is the paradise among the Arabs. Mujahid used to say: it is in Rumiya. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, regarding the phrase: "Those who inherit Paradise," he said: Paradise is a garden in Rumiya. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, who said: 'Eden is a garden in Paradise, which He has made its creation in His own hands. Every dawn it opens, he looks at it, then says: 'The believers have succeeded.' He said: It is also Paradise, that garden. Mujahid said: God planted it with His own hand, and when it reached it, He said: 'The believers have succeeded.' Then he ordered it to be closed, so no close people or angels would look at it. Then every magic would be opened, and he would look at it and say: 'The believers have succeeded,' then it would be closed to the same place. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
