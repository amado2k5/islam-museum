## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the poll tax (jizya) of the People of the Book and the Magians. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, who said: it reached me that the Prophet took jizya from the Magians of Bahrayn, that Umar ibn al-Khattab took it from the Magians of Persia, and that Uthman ibn Affan took it from the Berbers. The editor notes that al-Shaybani transmitted it from Malik; Abu Musab transmitted from Malik that it reached him, without Ibn Shihab. Ibn Abd al-Barr (al-Tamhid 12/63-65) says this is how all the Muwatta transmitters have it; Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi transmitted it from Malik from Ibn Shihab from al-Saib ibn Yazid as a connected report (al-Tabarani 6660), and al-Haythami weakened al-Husayn ibn Salama, who transmitted it from Ibn Mahdi, though the editor disagrees with that weakening; he holds that its single route from Malik signals its strangeness, as al-Daraqutni noted. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.374}
