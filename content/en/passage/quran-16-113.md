## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And indeed, a Messenger came to them from among themselves, but they denied him, and the punishment seized them while they were wrongdoers": The people of this town, whose description Allah described in the verse before this verse, said: "A Messenger from among them" said: The Messenger of Allah is from among them, saying: From themselves, they know him, recognize his lineage, and the truthfulness of his tone, calling them to the truth and to a straight path. "So they denied him" and did not accept from him what he brought them from Allah. "So the punishment seized them," meaning the garment of hunger and fear instead of the security, tranquility, and abundant provision they had previously been given, and he was killed by the sword. "And they are wrongdoers," meaning they are polytheists, because their great ones were killed on the day of Badr by the sword for polytheism. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
