## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And the faces were humbled by the Ever-Living, the Sustainer, and whoever carries injustice has failed": the faces of creation have taken refuge and surrendered to the Ever-Living, the Sustainer, who never dies, the Sustainer over His creation by managing them and directing them as they wish. The root of 'anw' is humiliation, from which it is said: 'He turned his face to his Lord, meaning he submitted, humiliated,' meaning he submitted to Him and was humiliated. Likewise, it was said to the captive: 'He suffered for the humiliation of captivity.' As for their saying: I took something by force, it is, although its meaning is that it is taken out of dominance, and that it is taken out of submission and obedience, as the poet said: Are you obedient, O heart, by force? And no soul has insisted that it has not been in its own mind. Another said: They did not take it by force out of affection, but according to the limit of al-Musharrafi he resigned it. He quotes in the passage from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
