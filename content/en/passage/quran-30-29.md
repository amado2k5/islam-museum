## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Rather, those who have wronged follow their desires without knowledge. So who can guide who misguides Allah? And they will have no helpers": that is not so. These polytheists do not associate gods and idols in the worship of Allah, because they have a share in what Allah has provided them of the dominion of their right hands. They and their servants are equal in it, fearing that they will share with them what they share with them. They made Allah obligatory for that reason with what they were pleased with for themselves, so they associated them in His worship. But those who wronged themselves and disbelieved in Allah, following their desires, out of ignorance, Allah has come upon them. So they associated gods and idols in His worship: "So who guides those whom Allah has led astray?" He says: Who guides to the right paths? By this, who guides Islam to those whom Allah has led astray from uprightness and righteousness? "And they have no helpers" means: And for whoever Allah has misled of helpers who help him and save him from the misguidance with which Allah afflicts him.
