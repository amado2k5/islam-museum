## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who disbelieved said, 'Our Lord, show us those who led us astray—of the jinn and mankind—We will place them under our feet so that they may be among the lowest.'" And those who disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger will say on the Day of Resurrection after they were admitted to Hell: O our Lord, show us those who led us astray from among Your creation, from among jinn and humans. It was said: The one among the jinn is Iblis, and the one who is from mankind, the son of Adam who killed his brother. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
