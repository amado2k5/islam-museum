## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "That is the one whom Allah gives good tidings to His servants who believe and do righteous deeds. Say, I do not ask you for any reward for it except love for near relatives. And whoever does a good deed, We will increase for him therein. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Appreciative." This is what I have told you, O people, that I have prepared for those who believe and do righteous deeds in the Hereafter of bliss and honor, the glad tidings that Allah gives to His servants who believe in Him in this world and act in obedience to Him therein. "Say, I do not ask you for any reward for it." Allah Almighty says to His Prophet Muhammad: Say, O Muhammad, to those who pass you on the hour from among the polytheists of your people: I do not ask you, O people, for your invitation to the truth I have brought you with, and the advice I advise you to give you as reward and recompense, and in return from your wealth to which you give it, "except affection for near relatives." Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the phrase: "except affection in my near relatives." Some said: its meaning is: except that you desire me in my kinship with you, and maintain my kinship ties between me and you. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah.
