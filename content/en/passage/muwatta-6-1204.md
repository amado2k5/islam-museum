## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God made his camel kneel at the stony valley (al-Batha) at Dhu al-Hulayfa and prayed there. Nafi said that Abdullah ibn Umar used to do that. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.541}
