## In al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari regards their asking its colour as another act of captiousness after the first, a demand for what they had been spared; God had at first limited them only as to kind, and when they asked about colour they were limited to one colour, as punishment for pressing their prophet. He explains "ma lawnuha" as an interrogative. On "yellow" there are two readings: al-Hasan, in two reports, says it was jet black, while in other reports he says yellow of horn and hoof, and so say Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Ibn Zayd and Mujahid; one report from al-Hasan says it was wild. Al-Tabari thinks those who said "black" followed the Arab usage of calling a black camel "yellow", citing verse of al-A'sha. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 198} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 199} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 200}
