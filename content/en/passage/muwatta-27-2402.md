## Summary
Malik said: the best that has been heard about a slave-girl whom a man had intercourse with while she was partly owned by someone else: he is not given the prescribed punishment, the child is attributed to him, and the slave-girl is valued against him when she became pregnant; her other owners receive their shares of the price, and the slave-girl becomes his. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.393}
