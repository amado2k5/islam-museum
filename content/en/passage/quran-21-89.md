## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 89 to 90 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Zachariah, when he called out to his Lord, my Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors": And remember, O Muhammad, Zechariah when he called his Lord, "My Lord, do not leave me alone," "alone," with no offspring or descendants, "And You are the best of inheritors," meaning: "So grant me an heir from the family of Jacob to inherit me." Then the matter was returned to Allah, saying: "And You are the best of inheritors." Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: So We answered Zakariya's prayer and granted him a son and an heir to inherit from him, "And We made his wife righteous for him." The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of righteousness meant by Allah, Glorified and Exalted, by saying: "And We made his wife right for him." Some said: She was barren, so He corrected her by making her fertile. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-A'mash.
