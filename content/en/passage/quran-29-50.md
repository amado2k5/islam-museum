## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And they said, 'Why were not I sent down to him signs from his Lord?' Say, 'The signs are only with Allah, and I am only a clear warner.'" The polytheists from Quraysh said: 'Why not send down upon Muhammad a sign from his Lord as a proof for Allah against us, just as the she-camel was made for righteousness, and the table is a sign for Jesus.' Say, O Muhammad, the signs with Allah are only possible for Him to deliver: "I am only a clear warner." Rather, I am a warner to you, warning you of the might of Allah and His punishment for your disbelief in His Messenger. And what He has brought to you from your Lord is 'clear,' meaning: His warning has become clear to you.
