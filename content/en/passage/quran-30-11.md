## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Allah begins creation, then returns it, then to Him you will be returned": Allah Almighty begins the creation of all creation alone by creating it without partner or backer, and He creates it from nothing, but by His power, the Almighty. Then He recreates a new creation after its annihilation and destruction, just as He began it as a proper creation, and nothing was left. "Then to Him you will be returned," meaning: then to Him after they are returned a new creation they will return, and they will be gathered to separate judgment between them: "And to recompense those who did evil for what they did, and reward those who did good with good."
