## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, your Lord judges between them by His judgment, and He is the Exalted, the Knowing": This Qur'an is a guidance, saying: a clarification from Allah, clarifying the truth regarding the matters in which His creation differed regarding their religion. "And a mercy for the believers." He means: a mercy for those who believe in it and act upon it. "Indeed, your Lord judges between them," meaning: "Indeed, your Lord judges between those who differ among Israel by His judgment concerning them, so He takes revenge on those who falsify among them and rewards the doer of good among them with the right in recompense with its reward." "And He is the Exalted, the Knowing." He says: And your Lord is the Almighty in taking revenge on those who disagree among them and others. No one can prevent Him from taking revenge on Him if the All-Knowing takes revenge on the Just and the Beneficent, those who differ among the Children of Israel in their differing, and against others who are falsely and misguided from guidance.
