## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, in that is a remembrance for whoever has a heart or listens while he is a witness": In our destruction is the centuries that we destroyed before Quraysh as a "remembrance" by which he reminds "whoever has a heart," meaning for those who have a mind from this nation, so they desist from the actions they used to take of their disbelief in their Lord, fearing that the punishment that befell them would befall them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
