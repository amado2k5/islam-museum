## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And if you do not believe in Me, then turn away": God Almighty says, informing about the saying of His prophet Moses to Pharaoh and his people: "And if you, O people, do not believe me in what I have brought you from my Lord, then turn away." He means: "So leave my way without being stoned by tongue or hand." Also: from Muammar, from Qatadah: "And if you do not believe in Me, then leave my way." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
