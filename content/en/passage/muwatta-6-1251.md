## Summary
Malik transmits from Humayd ibn Qays al-Makki, from Mujahid Abu al-Hajjaj, from Ibn Abi Layla, from Kab ibn Ujra, that the Messenger of God said to him: perhaps your vermin harmed you? He said yes. He said: shave your head, fast three days, or feed six needy people, or sacrifice a sheep. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.557}
