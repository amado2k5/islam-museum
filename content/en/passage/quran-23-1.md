## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1 to 3 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, the believers have succeeded": Abu Ja'far said: He means by saying: "Indeed, the believers have succeeded": Those who have been truthful to Allah and His Messenger Muhammad, acknowledged what He brought them from Allah, and acted upon what He called them to from these verses to eternal eternity in the gardens of their Lord and succeeded in their pursuit with Him. As Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us, from Muammar, from Qatadah, regarding the phrase: "Indeed, the believers have succeeded," he said: Ka'b said: Allah created only three with His own hand: He created Adam with His hand, wrote the Torah with His hand, planted the Garden of Eden with His hand, then said to her: Speak, and she said: The believers have succeeded because of the honor I have learned therein. From Amr ibn Abi Qays, from Abdul Aziz ibn Rafi', from Mujahid, who said: When Allah, the Blessed and Exalted, planted Paradise, He looked at it and said: "Indeed, the believers have succeeded." In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ata.
