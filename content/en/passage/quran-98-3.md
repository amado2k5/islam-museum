## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-4 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "in them are valuable books" as meaning: in the purified scriptures are valuable, just, and upright books from God, containing no error, because they are from God. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
