## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 24 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, We have sent you with the truth as a bringer of good tidings and a warner, and there is no nation but that there is not in it a warner": He, the Exalted, says to His Prophet Muhammad: "Indeed, We have sent you" O Muhammad, "with the truth," which is faith in Allah and the laws of religion that He has made obligatory upon His servants. "Bearer of good tidings" of Paradise from your truthfulness and accepting from you what you bring from Allah as an admonition, and "as a warner" warns people of your falsehood and returns to you what you brought from Allah of sincerity. "And there is not a nation but that there is no warner in it except that there is no warner before you who warns them of Our punishment for their disbelief in Allah," as from Qatadah: "And there is not a nation but that there is no warner in it," every nation had a messenger. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
