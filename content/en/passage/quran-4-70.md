## Summary
Verse 70 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 70 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (p. 535, note 4).** Al-Tabari says the bounty means that the obedient being with those named is God's gift to them and his favour on them, not because they earned it by some precedent. The editor refers to the earlier interpretation of the word for bounty (page numbers scrambled). Al-Tabari then puts an objection, whether they reached what they reached of his bounty by obedience, and answers that they did not obey him in this world except by his bounty, by which he guided them to obey him, so all of it is bounty from him. He begins the explanation of the closing clause by saying that what suffices servants is God who created them; the rest follows on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 535}

**End of the explanation of the closing clause (p. 536, note 1).** Al-Tabari says nothing of the obedience of the obedient or the disobedience of the disobedient is hidden from God, but he counts it against them and preserves it until he repays them all, the doers of good with good and the wrongdoers with wrong, and pardons whom he wills of the people of tawhid. The editor says the print differs from the manuscript here, that he kept the manuscript and put the sentence right as the opening of the passage requires. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 536}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 536}
