## Summary
Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Khusayfa, from Amr ibn Abdallah al-Sulami, who told him that Nafi ibn Jubayr told him, from Uthman ibn Abi al-As, that he came to the Messenger of God with a pain that nearly destroyed him. The Messenger of God said to him: wipe it with your right hand seven times, and say: I seek refuge in the might and power of God from the evil of what I find. He said: I did that, and God removed what I had. I kept ordering my family and others to do it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.530}
