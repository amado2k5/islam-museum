## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "that a soul should say in regret for what it neglected the presence, even though I am among the mockers": And turn to your Lord and submit to Him, "that a soul will say" meaning that no soul would say: "Oh, regret for what it neglected in the presence of Allah," which is similar to the saying: "And He cast upon the earth My mountains to support you," meaning that it does not support you, for since that meaning is in the accusative case. And His saying: 'Ya hasta' means to say: Ya regret, as Ahmad ibn al-Mufaddal narrated to me, and from Al-Suddi, regarding the phrase 'Ya hasta,' he said: regret. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
