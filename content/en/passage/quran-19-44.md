## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "O my father, do not worship Satan; indeed, Satan is ever disobedient to the Most Merciful." And the disobedient is the one who disobeys, just as the All-Knowing, the Knowing. Some Arabs said: 'Al-'Asi' means the disobedient, 'Al-'Alim' is the scholar, and 'Arif is the knower.' They cited the saying of Tarif ibn Tamim Al-Anbari for their statement. Or whenever a tribe of Ukaz arrived, they would send their 'Arif' to ask for 'Arif' and say: 'Arifim' means: 'Their Knower,' and Allah knows best.
