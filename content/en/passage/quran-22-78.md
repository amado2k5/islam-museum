## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And strive in the cause of Allah with the right of striving in Him; He has chosen you. And He has not placed upon you in religion any hardship in religion. Your father Abraham was named by the Muslims before, and in this so that the Messenger may be a witness over you and you may be witnesses over the people. So establish prayer, give zakat, and hold fast to Allah; He is your protector. What an excellent master and excellent helper." The interpreters differed in the interpretation of His saying: "And strive in the cause of Allah with the right of striving in Him," and some said: Its meaning: And they fought the polytheists in the way of Allah with the right to strive in His cause. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Al-Dhahhak, and Ibn Zayd.
