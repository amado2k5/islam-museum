## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Today every soul will be recompensed for what it has earned, not injustice on that Day. Indeed, Allah is swift in reckoning": Allah Almighty says, informing about His saying on the Day of Resurrection when He will resurrect His creation from their graves for the Day of Reckoning: "Today every soul will be recompensed for what it earned." He means: Today every worker will be rewarded for his deeds, and the reward for his deeds will be fulfilled. The doer of good will be rewarded with good, and the doer of evil will be rewarded accordingly. And His saying: 'There is no injustice today' means: No one should be underestimated for what he deserves of the reward of his deeds in this world, so it is diminished if he is a doer of good, nor is he held accountable for a sin he did not commit and be punished. 'Indeed, Allah is swift in accounting.' He says: Allah is swift in holding His servants accountable that Day for their deeds in this world. It is mentioned that that Day will not be justified until the people of Paradise are in Paradise and the people of Hell are in the Fire, and He has completed their reckoning and judgment between them.
