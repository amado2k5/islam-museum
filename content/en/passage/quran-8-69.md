**Commentary on verse 69 (pp. 71-72)**

Al-Tabari says God addresses the believers of Badr: eat, believers, of what you gained from the property of the polytheists, lawful because He made it lawful to you and good, and fear God lest you return to doing anything in your religion before it is covenanted to you, as you did in taking ransom and eating booty and taking both before they were made lawful; God is forgiving and merciful. He sees in the sentence a postponement whose sense is advancement: "eat of what you gained, lawful and good; God is forgiving, merciful; and fear God". "Forgiving" means forgiving the sins of His believing servants, and "merciful" means that He does not punish them after their repentance. The editor refers to the lexical indices for the wording of the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 71}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
