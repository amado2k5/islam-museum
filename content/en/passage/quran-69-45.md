## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 46 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “We would have seized him by the right hand” as meaning: We would have seized him with Our power and might, and then severed his heartstrings. He means by this that He would have hastened his punishment, without delay. It has also been said that the meaning of “We would have seized him by the right hand” is: We would have seized him with His right hand. They said: This is merely a metaphor, meaning: We would have humiliated and degraded him, and then severed his aorta. They said: This is like a ruler who, wanting to belittle someone in his presence, tells one of his aides: “Take him by the hand and make him stand up, and do such and such to him.” They said: Similarly, the meaning of “We would have seized him by the right hand” is: We would have humiliated him as He did to the one whose situation we have described. The passage quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Saeed bin Jubayr, Ibn Zayd, and Ata.
