## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We do not burden a soul beyond its capacity, and We have a Book that speaks the truth, and they are not wronged": We do not burden a soul except what it can and is suitable for it in worship. Therefore, We have burdened it with what we have been required to know the oneness of God, and We have legislated for it what We have legislated of the laws. "And we have a book that speaks the truth," he says: and we have a book of the deeds of creation according to what they did of good and evil. "It speaks the truth and they are not wronged," meaning: it clarifies with truthfulness what they did in this world, neither more nor less, and we are all fulfilling their rewards, the good among them by his kindness and the wrongdoer with his wrongdoing. "And they are not wronged" He says: they are not wronged, by adding to the sins of the wrongdoer what they did not do, so they are punished for their other crime, and the doer of good is diminished from his good deeds, so his reward is diminished.
