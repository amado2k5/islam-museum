## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So they cut off their affairs among them, each party rejoicing in what they have": Reciters differed in the reading of the phrase "Zubra," so the majority of the reciters of Medina and Iraq read it as "Zubra," meaning the gathering of the Psalms. The interpretation of the statement is based on their reading: The people whom God commanded from the nation of the Messenger Jesus to unite according to one religion and one sect, their religion which God commanded them to adhere to is "Zubra" as scriptures, so each group condemned a book other than the one the other group concluded, like the Jews who claimed to be governed by the Torah and denied the judgment of the Gospel and the Qur'an, and like the Christians who accepted the Gospel claiming to be and denied the judgment of the criterion.
