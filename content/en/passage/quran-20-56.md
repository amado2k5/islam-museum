## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And We showed him all Our signs, but he denied and refused": And We showed Pharaoh Our signs, meaning Our proofs and proofs regarding the truth with which We sent Our messengers, Moses and Aaron, to him all "so he denied and refused" to accept from Moses and Aaron what they brought from their Lord of the truth out of arrogance and arrogance.
