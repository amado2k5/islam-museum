## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11-12 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Indeed, we fear from our Lord a Day that is frowning and severe”: God Almighty informs us about these people, whose characteristics He has described, that they say to those they feed from among the poor and needy: “We do not feed you seeking any reward or gratitude for our feeding, but rather we feed you hoping that our Lord will protect us from His punishment on a Day of intense terror and great calamity, a Day when faces will frown due to the severity of its hardships, and the suffering of its people will be prolonged and severe.” “Severe” here means intense. It is said, “It is a severe day,” or “a day of great hardship,” or “a difficult day.” And it was very hot, and it was very hot today, and that was the most severe and longest day in terms of hardship and severity. From it is the saying of some of them: O sons of our uncle, do you remember our affliction upon you when it was a very hot day? And in the passage, sayings are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
