## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abdullah ibn Dinar, in the chapter on blessing the Prophet: Abdullah ibn Umar was seen standing at the Prophet's grave, blessing the Prophet and praying for Abu Bakr and Umar. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.235}
