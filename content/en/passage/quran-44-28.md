## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 28 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Thus and We made it an inheritance for other people": Thus as I have described to you, O people, we did with those whose matter I have mentioned to you, those who denied our messenger Moses. And His saying: "And We bequeathed it to other people," means God Almighty says, "And We bequeathed their gardens, their springs, their crops, their stations, and what they were of blessing from them to other peoples after their destruction." It is also said: He meant the other people, the Children of Israel. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
