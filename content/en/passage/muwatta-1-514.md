## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Sharik ibn Abdullah ibn Abi Namir from Anas ibn Malik, in the chapter on asking for rain: a man came to the Prophet and said the livestock had died and the roads were cut, so pray to God. The Prophet prayed, and they had rain from Friday to Friday. Then a man came and said the houses had collapsed, the roads were cut and the livestock had died. The Prophet said: O God, let the rain fall on the tops of the mountains, the hillocks, the valley bottoms and where trees grow. The cloud parted from Medina as a cloth parts. The editor cites al-Bukhari (1016, 1017, 1019), al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, al-Shafi'i and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.265} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.266}
