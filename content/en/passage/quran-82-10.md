## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 9 to 13 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “And indeed, there are guardians over you,” as meaning: “And indeed, there are watchers over you who record your deeds and keep count of them for you.” In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
