## Summary
Verse 85 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 85 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 85: seeking a religion other than Islam.** Al-Tabari says the sense is: whoever seeks a religion other than the religion of Islam to adhere to, God will not accept it from him, and in the hereafter he will be among those who cheat themselves of their share of God's mercy. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on the senses of seeking and of loss. Al-Tabari then mentions, in the form 'it was mentioned', that when this verse came down the adherents of each community claimed that they were the Muslims, so God ordered them to perform the pilgrimage if they were truthful, since pilgrimage is part of the practice of Islam, and they declined, and by this God refuted their argument. (This is what al-Tabari transmits, and the museum presents it as his, without a verdict.) {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 570}

**The rest of the report on the pilgrimage (7356-7359).** Al-Muthanna relays from Abu Hudhayfa, from Shibl, from Ibn Abi Najih that Ikrima claimed that the communities said when the verse came down: we are the Muslims, so God sent down the verse of pilgrimage [[passage/quran-3-97|3:97]], and the Muslims performed the pilgrimage while the unbelievers stayed behind (7356). Al-Muthanna relays from al-Qa'nabi, from Sufyan, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Ikrima that the Jews said: then we are the Muslims, so God sent down to His prophet what he might argue against them with, namely the verse of pilgrimage [[passage/quran-3-97|3:97]] (7357); and Yunus relays it from Sufyan, from Ibn Abi Najih, from Ikrima (7358). The editor says of al-Qa'nabi that he is Abd Allah ibn Maslama ibn Qa'nab al-Qa'nabi al-Harithi, from whom the leading scholars related; he quotes Ibn Sad that he was devout and excellent and read Malik's books to him, al-Ijli that Malik read half of the Muwatta to him and he read the other half to Malik, and Ibn al-Madini that he would put none of the narrators of the Muwatta before al-Qa'nabi. Others said of this verse what al-Muthanna relays from Abd Allah ibn Salih, from Muawiya, from Ali, from Ibn Abbas, that God sent down [[passage/quran-2-62|2:62]] and then, after it, the verse on seeking a religion other than Islam (7359). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 572}
