## Summary
Verse 85 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 85 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 85: seeking a religion other than Islam.** Al-Tabari says the sense is: whoever seeks a religion other than the religion of Islam to adhere to, God will not accept it from him, and in the hereafter he will be among those who cheat themselves of their share of God's mercy. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on the senses of seeking and of loss. Al-Tabari then mentions, in the form 'it was mentioned', that when this verse came down the adherents of each community claimed that they were the Muslims, so God ordered them to perform the pilgrimage if they were truthful, since pilgrimage is part of the practice of Islam, and they declined, and by this God refuted their argument. (This is what al-Tabari transmits, and the museum presents it as his, without a verdict.) {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 570}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 570 in the commentary on verse 85 and continue from p. 571.
