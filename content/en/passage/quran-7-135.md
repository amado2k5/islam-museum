**Commentary on verse 135 (p. 73 onward)**

Al-Tabari says: Moses prayed to his Lord, who answered him; so when God lifted the punishment He had sent on them "for a term that they were to reach", so that they would complete the days of the life God had set for them as a term up to their destruction, "they broke their word", meaning they broke the covenants they made with their Lord and with Moses and persisted in their unbelief and error. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "removal" and "term". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 73}

Report 15040, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih, on "for a term they were to reach": a counted number of their days; report 15041, from Mujahid, is similar, through al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 73}

We stop here at the foot of p. 73, in the middle of the commentary on verse 135 (where report 15042, from al-Suddi, begins); the next batch begins at p. 74. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 73}
