## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "They think the confederates did not go, and if the confederates come, they wish they had spread among the Bedouins, asking about your news. And if they were among you, they would not have fought except a little": These hypocrites think the confederates, namely Quraysh and Ghatafan. Also: from Ibn Ishaq, he said: Yazid ibn Roman narrated to us: 'They think the parties did not go' (Quraysh and Ghatafan). And His saying: 'they did not go' means: they did not leave, even though they had left out of cowardice and panic from them. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Ishaq.
