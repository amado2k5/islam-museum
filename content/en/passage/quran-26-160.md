## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 160 to 164 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "The people of Lot denied the messengers": "The people of Lot denied the messengers whom Allah sent to them from among the messengers when their brother Lot said to them: 'Will you not fear Allah?' Allah, O people, 'Indeed, I am a messenger to you' from your Lord, 'Amen' in His revelation and conveying His message 'So fear Allah' within yourselves, that His punishment will befall you for denying His Messenger and 'and obey' in what He has called you to. He has guided you to the path of righteousness, 'and I do not ask you for any reward for it.' He says: And I do not ask you for my advice to you and your supplication to my Lord, neither reward nor recompense. "My reward is only with the Lord of the worlds," meaning: My reward is only from the Lord of the worlds.
