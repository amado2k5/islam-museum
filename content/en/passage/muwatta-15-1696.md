## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Said ibn al-Musayyab was asked about a woman whose husband divorced her while she was living in a rented house: who pays the rent? He said the husband; if he has nothing, then she; if she has nothing, then the ruler. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.93}
