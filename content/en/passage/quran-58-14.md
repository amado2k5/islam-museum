## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Have you not seen those who have taken allies to a people upon whom Allah's wrath is not from you nor from them, and who swear to lie while they know?" means: Have you not looked with the eye of your heart, O Muhammad, and you see the people who are? They took as allies to a people against whom Allah is angry, and they are the hypocrites. They allegied the Jews and advised them, as Qatadah said: "Have you not seen those who took allies as people, against whom Allah is angry?" and so on the end of the verse, he said: They are the hypocrites who took the Jews as allies and advised them. From Muammar, from Qatadah: "They took as allies to a people against whom Allah is angry," he said: They are the Jews whom the hypocrites took as allies. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Samak.
