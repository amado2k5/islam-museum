## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'Your bird is with you if you remember? Rather, you are a people who are transgressors": The messengers said to the villagers: "Your bird is with you if you remember," they say: Your deeds, your provisions, and your share of good and evil are with you. All of this is upon your necks, and what is our misfortune if evil befalls you in what has been written upon you, and it has already been done to you by God. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
