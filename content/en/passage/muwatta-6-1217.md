## Summary
Yahya said: Malik was asked whether one may stone on behalf of a child and a sick person. He said yes; and the sick person, when stoning is done on his behalf, takes the intention while at home and sheds blood; if he recovers on the days of tashriq, the one stoned for him stones and offers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.544}
