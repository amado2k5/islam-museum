## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He said, 'My Lord has not enabled me in any good matter, so help me with strength; I will put a barrier between you and them.'" Dhu al-Qarnayn said: "He who enabled me to do what you asked of me to build between you and these people, my Lord, and He supported me and strengthened me over it, is better than He made you. The reward you offer me to build that is greater and better, but help me from among you with strength, help me with deeds and craftsmen who are skilled in building and work." Also: He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid: "As long as my Lord has enabled me good, so help me with strength," he said: with men, "I will place a barrier between you and them," and he said what he enabled, so merge one of the two noons into the other, and it is only what He enabled me to do. And His saying: "I will place a barrier between you and them" means: I will place a barrier between you and Gog and Magog. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
