## Summary
Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam that Abu Hurayra and Saad ibn Abi Waqqas used to allow kissing for the fasting person. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.394}
