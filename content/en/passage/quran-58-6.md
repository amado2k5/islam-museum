## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "On the Day Allah will resurrect them all together and inform them of what they did. Allah will enumerate it and forget it. And Allah is, over all things, Witness." And for the disbelievers will be a humiliating punishment on the Day Allah will resurrect them all, and that is the Day Allah will resurrect them all from their graves for the Day of Resurrection. Allah will inform them: "Allah will count what they did, and they will forget it." Allah says: Allah has counted what they have done, so He has counted it against them, confirmed it, preserved it, and made those who practice it forget. "And Allah is Witness over all things," meaning: "By Allah, Glorified and Exalted be He, is His praise for all that He has done," and other matters of His creation, "Witness," meaning a witness who knows and surrounds Him, so nothing of it escapes Him.
