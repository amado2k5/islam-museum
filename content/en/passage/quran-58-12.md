## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O you who have believed, when you have confided in the Messenger and offer charity before your private conversations, that is better for you and purer. But if you do not find it, then indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful." He explains: "O you who have spoken to Allah and His Messenger, when you have confided in the Messenger of Allah, present before your private conversation a charity that you give as charity to the people of the needy and needy." "That is better for you," meaning: "Giving charity before your private conversation with the Messenger of Allah is better for you with Allah, and purer for your hearts from sins." The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
