## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "If you disbelieve, then indeed, Allah is free from need of you, and disbelief is not pleased for His servants. But if you are grateful, He is pleased with you. And no bearer of the burden of another will bear it. Then to your Lord is your return, and He will inform you of what you used to do. Indeed, He is Knowing of what is in the breasts." The interpreters differed in the interpretation of the phrase: "If you disbelieve, then indeed, Allah is free of need for you, and He does not approve of disbelief for His servants." Some said: That is for a specific group of people, and its meaning: If you disbelieve, O polytheists, for Allah is self-sufficient for you, and disbelief is not pleased with His believing servants, whom He has devoted to worship and obey Him. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
