## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 73 to 74 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And those who feared their Lord will be led to Paradise in groups until, when they reach it and its gates are opened, and its treasure will say to them, 'Peace be upon you. You are well, so enter it eternally.'" He said: Those who feared their Lord were gathered by fulfilling His obligations and avoiding His sins in this world, dedicating their divinity to Him, and dedicating worship to Him without associating anything in their worship with Him. "To Paradise is a group," meaning groups. Thus, the drive of these people to their homes in Paradise was a procession, as we explained earlier in Surah Maryam, on the wonders of Paradise, and the driving of others. He was called to the fire as a rose, as God said. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Zayd.
