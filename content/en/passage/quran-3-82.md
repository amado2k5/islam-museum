## Summary
Verse 82 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 82 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 82: those who turn away after the covenant.** Al-Tabari says the sense is: whoever turns away from belief in my messengers whom I sent confirming what was with my prophets of the books and wisdom, and from supporting them, so that he turned his back, did not believe and did not support, and broke his covenant and pledge after the covenant God took from him, those are the ones who have left God's religion and obedience of their Lord, which is the sense of fisq. The editor refers to the language indexes for his commentary on turning away and on fisq. Al-Muthanna relays from Ishaq, from Abd Allah ibn Hashim, from Sayf ibn Umar, from Abu Ruq, from Abu Ayyub, from Ali (7339) that the sense is: whoever turns away from you, Muhammad, after this covenant, of all the nations, those are the disobedient in unbelief. Al-Muthanna relays from Ishaq, from Ibn Abi Jafar, from his father, and al-Tabari remarks here 'meaning al-Razi' (7340), that the sense is after the covenant and pledge taken from them. The editor comments on the words 'meaning al-Razi' that al-Tabari's explaining Abu Jafar al-Razi, after the name had occurred hundreds of times in this chain and others, shows either that he wrote his commentary at widely separated times or that someone asked him while he was dictating and he explained, and those who heard him recorded it as he said it in that sitting; he refers to the note on report 164. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 562}

**Close of verse 82 (7341) and al-Tabari's remark.** Ammar relays from Ibn Abi Jafar, [from his father], from al-Rabi the same (7341); the editor says this is a chain recurring in the commentary, the nearest being report 7234, and that the copyist dropped 'from his father' from it, which he restored between brackets. Al-Tabari then says that these two verses, though in them God's report goes out in the form that He called witnesses and took the covenant of those He took it from among His prophets and messengers, are aimed at informing those Jews of the Children of Israel who lived around the place of the Prophet's emigration, in his lifetime, of the covenant God has upon them in believing in Muhammad's prophethood, and at reminding them of the pledges and covenants God took from their fathers and forebears, of what God's prophets had made known to them and told them in advance about confirming him, following him and supporting him against those who opposed and denied him, and at making known to them what is in God's books, which He sent down to the prophets He sent to them, of his description and sign. In two notes the editor clarifies the sentence's structure and the words he supplied between brackets, and says he divided the clauses to make them easier to follow. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 563}
