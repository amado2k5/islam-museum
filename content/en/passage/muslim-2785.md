## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the Book of the Description of the Resurrection, Paradise and Hell of Sahih Muslim: a person's true weight before God is not in his body, with the verse of Surat al-Kahf (105) on the weight given to people on the Day of Resurrection. {cite:sahih-muslim}
