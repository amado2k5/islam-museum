## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Ubaydallah ibn Abdallah ibn Utba ibn Masud, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, from Maymuna, that the Prophet was asked about a mouse that falls into butter; he said: take it out and what is around it, and throw it away. The editor sets out the variation in the routes of the hadith from Malik, and Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that Yahya's version is the best, while Ibn Bukayr and Abu Musab transmitted it as relayed (mursal). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.565}
