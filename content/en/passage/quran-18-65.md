## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 65 to 66 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said that as long as we were not mature, so they reverted to their traces in stories": Moses said to his girl, "That" means that the fish forgot you, "and we were not proficient in it," meaning: the one we used to seek and seek, because Moses was told, "Your companion you want wherever you forget the fish," as Ibn Abi Nujayh narrated from Mujahid, saying: "That is what we used to accomplish." Moses said: That was when I told you that I would find a green fish where the fish would miss me. He said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, similarly, except that he said: Where the fish leaves me. And His saying: "So they returned to their traces with stories," he says: They returned on the road they had crossed, backing down on their monasteries, recounting their traces that had taken them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
