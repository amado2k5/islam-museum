## Summary
Yahya ibn Said narrated to Malik that a woman came to the Prophet and said: Messenger of God, we lived in a house, our numbers were many, and our wealth was abundant; then our numbers dwindled and our wealth disappeared. He said: leave it, blameworthy. The editor notes that al-Bukhari in al-Adab al-Mufrad and Abu Dawud transmitted it raised from the route of Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha from Anas, and that its chain is sound. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.567}
