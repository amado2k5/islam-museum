## Summary
Yahya said: I also heard Malik say of a camel that attacks a man, who fears for his life and kills it or hamstrings it: if he has evidence that it was intending to attack him, he owes nothing. If he has no evidence but his own word, he is liable for the camel. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.295}
