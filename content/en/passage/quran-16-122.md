## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari does not dedicate this verse to a separate commentary on the preserved page; the passage in which it appears, verses 119 to 128 of Surah An-Nahl, only includes an explanation of what precedes it: he explains verse 119 as 'your Lord is for those who disobey out of ignorance, then repents and reforms, Forgiving and Merciful,' and he interprets verses 120 and 121 as saying that Abraham was an imam to be emulated in goodness, obedient to God, upright to Islam, free from polytheism, grateful for His blessings, chosen and guided to the straight path, and this is an informing the polytheists of Quraysh that he is disassociated from them. He then quotes Ibn Mas'ud regarding the meaning of 'the nation': the teacher of goodness, and 'the qurant': the obedient to Allah and His Messenger.
