## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And do not kill your children for fear of poverty; We provide for them and for you. Indeed, killing them is a grave sin." "And your Lord has decreed, O Muhammad, that you worship none but Him and be good to parents," "and do not kill your children for fear of poverty." Thus, the place where you kill is a particle in conjunction with not worshiping Him. By saying: "fear of poverty," he means fear of stinginess and poverty. We have explained this with previous evidences and mentioned the narration about it. Indeed, the Exalted is He, He said this to the Arabs, because they used to kill their daughters out of fear for the family to spend on them, as Qatadah said: "And do not kill your children for fear of poverty," meaning fear of poverty. The people of pre-Islamic times used to kill their children out of fear of poverty, so Allah advised them about this and informed them that their provision and the provision of their children is upon Allah, saying: "We provide for them and for you; if killing them was a great mistake." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
