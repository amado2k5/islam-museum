## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that when he circumambulated the House, in the three rounds he hastened, he would say: and you give life after they had died; O God, there is no god but You; he lowered his voice in saying it. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1284) and Suwayd (542) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.490}
