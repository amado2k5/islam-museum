## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on buying back charity and returning to it. Malik transmits from Zayd ibn Aslam, from his father, that he heard Umar ibn al-Khattab say: I gave a noble horse in God's path, but its rider neglected it. I wanted to buy it from him, and I thought he would sell it cheaply, so I asked the Prophet. He forbade me to buy it, even if he gave it to me for one dirham, and he compared the one who returns to his charity to a dog that returns to its vomit. The editor notes that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik, and through him Ibn Hibban and al-Baghawi; Ismail ibn Abi Uways in al-Bukhari, Sufyan ibn Uyayna in al-Humaydi and al-Bukhari, al-Qa'nabi in Muslim, al-Tinnisi in al-Bukhari, Ibn Mahdi in Ahmad and Muslim, and Yahya ibn Qaza'a in al-Bukhari also transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.378}
