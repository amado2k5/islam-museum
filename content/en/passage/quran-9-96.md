**Commentary on verse 96 (p. 428)**

Al-Tabari says: these hypocrites swear to you, believers in God, making excuse with falsehood and lies so that you will be pleased with them; "but if you are pleased with them, God is not pleased with the transgressing people" means if you, believers, are pleased with them and accept their excuse since you cannot tell their truth from their lie, your being pleased with them will not benefit them with God, for God knows of their secret affairs what you do not know, and of their hidden belief what you are ignorant of, and that they are in unbelief in God, and that they are those who go out from faith to unbelief in God and from obedience to disobedience. The editor notes that there is a gap here in Abu Ja'far's text from his copyist, the sense being that they persist in unbelief and are the transgressors, and refers to the earlier explanation of transgression. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 428}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
