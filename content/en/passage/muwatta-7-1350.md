## Summary
Malik transmits from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman that property came to Abu Bakr al-Siddiq from Bahrayn, and he said: whoever had a debt or promise from the Messenger of God let him come to me; Jabir ibn Abdullah came and he gave him three handfuls. The editor notes that Ibn Abd al-Barr said the hadith is cut off in Malik's Muwatta, and that it is connected through sound routes from Jabir. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.604}
