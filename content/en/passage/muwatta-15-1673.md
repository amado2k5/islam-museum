## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Nufay, the mukatab of Umm Salama, divorced a free woman twice, and asked Uthman ibn Affan, who said she is forbidden to you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.86}
