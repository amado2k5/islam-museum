## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And when those who disbelieve see you that they take you only as a mockery." He says: "And when they see you," O Muhammad, "those who disbelieve" in God, "if they take you only as mockery," he says: they only take you in mockery, saying to others: "Is this who mentions your gods?" He means by saying: He mentions your gods badly and criticizes them, astonishing at that. Allah Almighty says: They marvel at your remembrance, O Muhammad, their gods who neither harm nor benefit in harm.
