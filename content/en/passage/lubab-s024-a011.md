## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of an-Nur 24:11, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247) — Companion generation.

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «إن الذين جاءوا بالإفك الآيات».

## Full report (Arabic source text)
