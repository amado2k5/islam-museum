## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter "the souls are armies drawn up" in the Book of Righteousness, Kinship and Manners of Sahih Muslim. In another report before it: "people are mines like the mines of silver and gold; the best of them in the Jahiliyya are the best in Islam, once they have understanding". {cite:sahih-muslim}
