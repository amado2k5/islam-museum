## Summary
A report of Ibn Masud in the chapter on the great prohibition of polytheism and killing a soul without right, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. It names the two destinations: polytheism leads to the Fire, and monotheism to Paradise. {cite:sahih-muslim}
