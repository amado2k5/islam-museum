## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 80 to 83 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "except Your sincere servants among them": except those whom You have sincerely chosen for Your worship and protected from My misguidance, so You have not given me any way against Him, for I am not able to mislead or mislead him. And from Qatadah: "He said, 'By Your might, I will surely mislead them all together.'" He said: The enemy of God knew that he had no honor. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
