## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And remember the brother of 'Ad when he warned his people at Al-Ahqaf while the warning had passed away from before him and behind him, saying, 'Do not worship except Allah. Indeed, I fear for you the punishment of a great Day.'" And remind, O Muhammad, to your people who seek to you what You brought them of the truth, Hud, brother of 'Ad. Indeed, Allah sent you to them just as He sent you to 'Ad. They feared that Allah's wrath would befall them for their disbelief, as they denied our Messenger Hud to them, when He warned his people 'Aad of the Ahqaf. Al-Ahqaf: the plural of 'haqf', which is from sand as long as it stretches and does not reach a mountain. Al-A'sha means: So he spent the night at Arta'ah Haqf, which was engulfed by a northern fire that left the face darker. Interpreters differed about the location of these al-Ahqaf, some saying: it is a mountain in Syria. In the maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
