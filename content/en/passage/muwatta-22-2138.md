## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say about two men with a pledge between them, one of whom wants to sell his pledge while the other had given him a year's respite for his right. Malik said: if the pledge can be divided and the other's right is not reduced, half of the pledge held between them is sold for him and he receives his right. If his right is feared to be reduced, the whole pledge is sold and the one who wanted to sell receives his right from it. If the one who gave the respite is willing to pay half the price to the pledgor, well; otherwise the pledgee swears that he only gave respite so that his pledge would stand as it is, and then he receives his right immediately. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.273}
