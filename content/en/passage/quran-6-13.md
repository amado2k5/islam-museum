## Summary
Verse 13 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 13 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

On "to him belongs what dwells in the night and the day, and he is the hearing, the knowing" al-Tabari says: these who hold idols equal to God do not believe, so as to give him pure oneness, devote obedience to him alone and acknowledge his divinity, out of ignorance; "to him belongs what dwells in the night and the day" means to him belongs the dominion of all, since nothing of God's creation fails to dwell in the night and the day, so it is clear his meaning is as described; "he is the hearing" of what these polytheists say of him in claiming a partner, and of what others of his creatures say, "the knowing" of what they hide in themselves and show with their limbs, none of it hidden from him, so he counts it against them, to pay every person the reward of what he earned and the recompense of what he did. Report 13109, from al-Suddi: "dwells" means what settled in the night and the day. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 282}

The editor notes that the printed text changed the manuscript's "of his creation" to "of the contrary" by poor judgement, and that he restored it.

Verse 13 closes here at p. 282, and al-Tabari goes on to [[passage/quran-6-14|6:14]].

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
