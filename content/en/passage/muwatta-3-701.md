## Summary
A saying of Malik: if sheep outnumber goats and only one sheep is due from the owner, the collector takes it from the sheep; if goats outnumber, it is taken from the goats; if they are equal, it is taken from whichever he chooses. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.352}
