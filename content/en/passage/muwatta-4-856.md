## Summary
It reached Malik that Said ibn Jubayr said the same, namely like al-Qasim ibn Muhammad's view on the ransom together with making up. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik (467). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.414}
