## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O Prophet, We have made lawful for you your wives whose wages you have given and which you have not possessed in your right hand, but Allah has not forbidden you and your cousins, and the daughters of your paternal aunts and the daughters of your maternal uncles and the daughters of your maternal aunts who emigrated with you, and a believing woman if she dedicates herself to the Prophet if the Prophet wishes to marry her, exclusively for you, and not for the believers. We have already taught them what We have decreed upon them concerning their wives and what their right hands possess." So that there will be no hardship upon you, and Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.": "O Prophet, indeed We have made lawful for you your wives whose wages you have been given," meaning: those whom you have married with a specified dowry, as reported by Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "Your wives whose wages you have been given," he said: their charity. Ibn Zayd said regarding the saying: "O Prophet, We have made lawful for you your wives whose rewards you have been given," he said: Every woman was given a dowry, and Allah has made it lawful for him. I narrated from al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard al-Dahhak say regarding the saying: "O Prophet, We have made lawful for you your wives whose rewards you have been given"... Up to his saying: "Exclusively for you besides the believers," so this naming was not as much or little as he wished. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
