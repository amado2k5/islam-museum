## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Salim ibn Abdullah and Sulayman ibn Yasar were asked whether a menstruating woman may be approached by her husband when she sees that her period has ended, before she bathes. They said no, not until she bathes. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Zayd ibn al-Hubab, Abd al-Razzaq, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.103} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.104}
