## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he said: there is no riba in animals. Only three kinds of animal sales were forbidden: the mudamin, the malaqih, and habal al-habala. The mudamin are what is in the bellies of she-camels; the malaqih are what is in the backs of camels. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.183}
