## Summary
Hafsa, Mother of the Believers, asked the Messenger of God why people released themselves and he did not release himself from his lesser pilgrimage; he said: I locked my hair with gum and garlanded my offering, so I will not release myself until I slaughter it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.527}
