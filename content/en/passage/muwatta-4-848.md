## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on making up voluntary fasts. Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Aisha and Hafsa, wives of the Prophet, woke up fasting voluntarily; food was given to them as a gift, and they broke their fast on it. The Prophet came in, and Hafsa, who was her father's daughter, said: Messenger of God, we woke up fasting voluntarily, and food was given to us as a gift, and we broke our fast on it. He said: make up a day in its place. The editor notes that Malik transmitted it as a cut-off report (mursal, between Ibn Shihab and Aisha and Hafsa); that the sound and preserved version is the cut-off one, as held by Abu Zur'a and Abu Hatim; that some of al-Zuhri's students transmitted the connected version from Urwa from Aisha; and refers to al-Tirmidhi (735) and al-Tamhid (12/66). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.410}
