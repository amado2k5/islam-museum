## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "It is not for a believing man or believing woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, that they have any better choice than their affair. And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly gone astray in manifest error": It is not for a believing man or woman in Allah and His Messenger, when Allah and His Messenger have decreed a decree concerning themselves, to choose from their affairs other than what He has decreed for them, and to oppose Allah's command and His Messenger's decree and their judgment, thus disobeying them. Whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger in what He commands or prohibits, "has gone astray in clear error," meaning: He deliberately went astray, and took a path other than guidance and guidance. It is mentioned that this verse was revealed about Zaynab bint Jahsh when the Messenger of Allah proposed to her to his daughter Zayd ibn Harithah, and she refrained from marrying him herself. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ikrimah.
