## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 6 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By the dawn”: This is an oath by which our Lord, may His praise be exalted, swears, and it is the dawn of morning. The scholars of interpretation differed as to what was meant by this; some said it meant the day. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Sufyan.
