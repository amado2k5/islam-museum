## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, in the chapter on opening the prayer: Abu Hurayra led them in prayer and glorified God at each lowering and raising, and when he finished he swore that he was the most like the Prophet's prayer among them. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad and al-Shafi'i, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.126}
