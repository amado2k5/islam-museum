## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains Abraham's words to the angels who were given glad tidings of a learned boy: "You gave me good tidings that old age touched me, so what do you give glad tidings?" — and from Mujahid: He was amazed at his arrogance and that of his wife. He explains that "to say that old age touched me" means because old age touched me and that he touched me, similar to His saying, "Indeed, I say nothing about God except the truth," meaning I do not speak. He likens it in speech: I came to you to give, but I did not find you giving.
