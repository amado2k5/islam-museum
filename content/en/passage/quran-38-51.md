## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 51 to 52 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "Gardens of Eden, opening doors for them": His saying, Exalted is He: "Gardens of Eden," is a statement of the best destination and a translation from it, meaning: gardens of residence. We have explained the meaning of this with its evidences and mentioned the differences in what has been mentioned before, which is sufficient to repeat it here. And Qatadah, regarding the phrase "Gardens of Eden," he said: Umar asked Ka'b what is Eden? He said: O Commander of the Faithful, there are palaces in Paradise made of gold inhabited by the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the leaders of justice. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
