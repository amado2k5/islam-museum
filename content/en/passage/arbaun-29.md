## Summary
Hadith of Mu'adh ibn Jabal, who asked about a deed that enters him into Paradise. The Prophet listed the pillars and said the head of the matter is Islam, its pillar prayer, and its peak jihad; then he held his tongue and said, "Restrain this", noting that people are thrown on their faces in the Fire for what their tongues reap. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
