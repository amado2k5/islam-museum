## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Did they not travel through the land and see how was the end of those before them? Allah destroyed them and for the disbelievers its likes": Did these deniers of Muhammad, the denial of what We revealed to him of the Scripture, travel on earth? This is only a rebuke from Allah to them, because they had traveled to the Levant, witnessing Allah's wrath that He had afflicted the people of Hajar Thamud, and seeing in their journey to Yemen what Allah had decreed with Saba. He said to His Prophet and to the believers in him: Did these polytheists not travel through the lands and consider what the consequences were for those before them among the nations who denied their messengers? Did we not destroy them and destroy their homes and ruin them? They take heed and beware that Allah would do to them in their denial of Him, so they would call upon obedience to Allah in believing in you. Then Allah Almighty promised them and informed them that if they persisted in denying His Messenger, that punishment befell them as was bestowed upon those before them from the family. In this passage, sayings from Mujahid are quoted.
