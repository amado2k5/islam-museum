## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave Moses nine clear signs, so the Children of Israel were asked when he came to them. Pharaoh said to him, 'Indeed, I think you are Moses bewitched.'" And We certainly gave Moses son of Imran nine clear signs that make clear to those who saw them as proofs for Moses, testifying to his truthfulness and the reality of his prophethood. The interpreters differed about what they were. Some of them said about that: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me from his father, from Ibn Abbas, regarding His saying: "And indeed We gave Moses nine clear signs," he said: The nine clear signs: his hand, his staff, his tongue, the sea, the flood, locusts, lice, frogs, and blood are detailed signs. I narrated from Al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say regarding the saying: "And We certainly gave Moses nine clear signs"—throwing the staff twice before Pharaoh, removing his hand, the knot that was on his tongue, and five verses about the flood, locusts, lice, frogs, and blood. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, Ibn Jurayj, and Ibn Ishaq.
