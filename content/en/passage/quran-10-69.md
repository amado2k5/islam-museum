**Commentary on verse 69 (p. 146)**

On [[passage/quran-10-69|10:69]] al-Tabari says God tells His prophet: say, Muhammad, to them that those who invent a lie about God, saying false things of Him and claiming a son for Him, will not prosper, that is, will not remain in the world. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'invention' and 'prospering'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 146}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
