## Summary
A report of Abu Sa'id al-Khudri in the chapter on the virtues of Abu Bakr, in Sahih Muslim. It includes the hadith of the choice in the Hereafter and Abu Bakr's weeping, which understood the approaching end, and the hadith of closing the doors except Abu Bakr's door. It is the best-known text on Abu Bakr's sincerity (siddiqiyya) in Sunni belief. {cite:sahih-muslim}
