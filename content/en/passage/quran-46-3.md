## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 3 to 5 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Hamim": Our explanation has already been given regarding the meaning of the phrase: "Hamim* the revelation of the Book," which is unnecessary to repeat it here. And His saying: "We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them except in truth," He, the Exalted, says: "There is none among them among the categories of the world except by truth," meaning: except to establish truth and justice in creation. And His saying: "And a specified term" means: Otherwise, there is a fixed term for all of that that known to Him, which will perish if it reaches it, and destroy it after it existed by its creation. And His saying: "And those who disbelieve in what they were warned will turn away," the Exalted says: And those who denied the oneness of Allah are turning away from Allah's warning, they do not take heed from Him, nor do they reflect, and they are heeded.
