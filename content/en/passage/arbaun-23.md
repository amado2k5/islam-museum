## Summary
Hadith of Abu Malik al-Ash'ari: "Purity is half of faith; praise of God fills the scale; prayer is light; charity is proof; patience is brightness; the Qur'an is a proof for you or against you". Ibn Rajab notes the variant wordings and the disputed transmission between Yahya ibn Abi Kathir and Zayd ibn Sallam. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
