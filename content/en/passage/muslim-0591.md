## Summary
A report of Thawban in the chapter on what is said after finishing prayer, in the Book of Mosques of Sahih Muslim. The rule is to ask forgiveness three times after prayer, then say "O God, You are Peace". It is the root of a remembrance still said after every prayer. {cite:sahih-muslim}
