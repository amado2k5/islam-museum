## Summary
Verse 45 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 45 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**God's knowledge of their hostility and the call to trust Him (pp. 429-430, notes 1-2).** Al-Tabari says God reports the hostility of those Jews whom He forbade the believers to take as advisers in religion, and that He knows their hostility better than the believers do; so they should stop and obey Him in what He forbade, since He knows the deceit, enmity and envy in which they stand toward them, that they seek to ruin them and wish them to stray from the highway of truth and perish. He then says the believers should trust God, rely on Him and turn to Him alone; He is enough as the guardian who looks after their affairs by protection and watch, so that their enemies do not draw them from their religion or turn them from following their prophet, and enough as a helper against their enemies, the enemies of their religion and whoever seeks crookedness for it. The editor says the manuscript reads "of what I forbade you" with one preposition and the print with another, and he kept the manuscript's, and refers to earlier explanations of the words for guardian and helper. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 430}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 430}
