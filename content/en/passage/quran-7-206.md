**Commentary on verse 206 (p. 357)**

Al-Tabari says: do not be proud, you who listen in silence to the Qur'an, in worshipping your Lord, and remember Him when the Qur'an is recited, humbly and in fear and without raising the voice in speech, "for those who are with your Lord", of His angels, "are not too proud to worship Him", that is, to humble themselves before Him and show humility, which is worship; "and they glorify Him" meaning they magnify their Lord by their humility to Him and their worship; "and before Him they prostrate" meaning they pray to God, which is their prostration; so pray you too to Him and magnify Him by worship as those with Him of His angels do. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "worship", "glorifying" and "prostration", and notes that in the preceding chain the words "he said, Suwayd told us" are a plain error of the copyist. With this Surat al-A'raf ends at p. 357, and at this place the copyist of the manuscript records the end of a part of the old division from which their copy was taken. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 357}
