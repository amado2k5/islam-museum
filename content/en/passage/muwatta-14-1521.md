## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that he used to say it is forbidden to marry a woman on top of her paternal aunt or her maternal aunt, or for a man to have intercourse with a slave-girl who is pregnant by another man. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.38}
