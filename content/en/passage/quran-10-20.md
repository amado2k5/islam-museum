**Commentary on verse 20 (pp. 48)**

He then explains "They say: why has no sign been sent down to him from his Lord? Say: the unseen belongs to God alone, so wait; I am with you among those who wait". These polytheists say: why has no sign been sent down to Muhammad from his Lord, a mark and proof by which we would know that Muhammad is right in what he says? God told him to say, Muhammad, "the unseen belongs to God alone", that is, no one knows whether He will do that except Him, because no one knows the unseen, the hidden and secret of affairs, except God; "so wait", people, for God's decision between us, in hastening punishment for the one in the wrong among us and making the truth prevail over him, "I am with you among those who wait". God did so and decided between them and him by killing them at Badr with the sword. The editor refers to the glosses on "why not", "sign" and "the unseen". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 48}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
