## Summary
A report of Anas in the chapter on the virtues of Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah in Sahih Muslim: the Prophet's testimony to his trustworthiness, "the trustworthy one of this community". It is the best-known text on his trustworthiness. {cite:sahih-muslim}
