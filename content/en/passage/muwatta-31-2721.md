## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from Fatima bint al-Mundhir, that when Asma bint Abi Bakr was brought a woman who had fever, she would pray for her, take water, and pour it between her and her collar, saying: the Messenger of God used to order us to cool it with water. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.533}
