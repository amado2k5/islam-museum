## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The law of Allah is with those who passed away before, and you will never find any change in the way of Allah": "The way of Allah regarding those who passed away"—these hypocrites who are in the city of the Messenger of Allah with him, the beating of these hypocrites. If they show their hypocrisy, he will kill them by killing and curse them with great curses. And as we have said about this, the scholars of interpretation said. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
