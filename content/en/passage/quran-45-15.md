## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Whoever does righteous deeds for himself; and whoever does evil, it is upon it; then to your Lord you will be returned": Whoever among Allah's servants acts in obedience to Him, fulfills His command, and is deterred by His prohibition, does that good deed for himself, and seeks its salvation from Allah's punishment, obeying his Lord, not for anything else, because that does not benefit others. And Allah is free of the deeds of every worker, and whoever does wrong, it is upon him. He means: Whoever wrongs his deeds in this world by disobeying his Lord, and disobeying him in it, he has wronged himself, because he has saved her from doing so and earned her through it. He was angry and did not harm anyone except himself. "Then to your Lord you will be returned," meaning: Then you, O people, will gather to your Lord after your death, and He will reward the doer of good for his kindness, and the wrongdoer for his wrongdoing. Whoever among you is rewarded with a good deed, I will be rewarded with a good reward, and whoever among you is rewarded with a bad deed, I will receive a bad reward.
