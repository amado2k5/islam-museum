**Commentary on verse 46 (pp. 124-125)**

The sense of "give us a ruling on seven fat cows eaten by seven lean, and seven green ears and others dry" is: rule on seven fat cows seen in sleep that seven lean ones eat, and on seven green ears also seen, and seven others dry. The fat cows are the fertile years; report 19368, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: the fat are fertile years and the seven lean are barren years that grow nothing; report 19369 from him, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: the fat are the fertile and the lean cows are the dry years of drought. "Green" means the fertile years and "dry" the barren years of drought, and "'ijaf" is the plural of a thin, emaciated one. On "that I may return to the people, that they may know" he says: so that I may return to the people and tell them, that they may know the interpretation of what I asked you of the dream. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 125}
