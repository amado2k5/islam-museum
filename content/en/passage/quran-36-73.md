## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 73 to 74 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they have in them benefits and drinks; will they not be grateful": And they have benefits in these livestock, and that is in their wool, their hair, by using them as furniture and goods, and from their skins and drinks from which they drink their milk, as in Qatadah: "And they have benefits therein," they wear their wool, and drinks from their milk. And His saying: "Will they not be grateful?" means: Will they not be grateful for this favor of mine, my kindness to them by obeying me, dedicating divinity and worship, and abandoning obedience to Satan and idol worship? His saying: "And they took gods besides Allah" means: These polytheists took gods besides Allah to worship "that they might be helped," meaning hoping that those gods would help them from Allah's punishment and punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
