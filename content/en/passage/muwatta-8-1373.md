## Summary
Malik transmits from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: whoever swears an oath then sees something else better than it, let him expiate his oath and do what is better. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.613}
