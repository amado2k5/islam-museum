## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 67 to 70 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Then they will have over it a scorching stinging of boiling": "Then they will have a scorching stingy of boiling over it." Then these polytheists have a scorching tree of Zaqqum on what they eat from this tree, which is a confusion of the Arabs' saying: so-and-so grayed his food, so it is stained with sour and gray (from hives). Al-Hamim is the hot water, which is what heated and its heat ended, and its origin is a verbal noun derived from 'fa'il'. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Suddi, and Ibn Zayd.
