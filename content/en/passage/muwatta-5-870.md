## Summary
Malik transmits that he asked Ibn Shihab about a man in seclusion: may he enter under a roof for his need? He said: yes, there is no harm in it. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd and al-Shaybani (379) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.420}
