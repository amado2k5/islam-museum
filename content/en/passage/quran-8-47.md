**Commentary on verse 47 (pp. 578-581)**

Al-Tabari says on "do not be like those who left their homes in arrogance and to be seen by people and bar from God's path; God encompasses what they do" [[passage/quran-8-47|8:47]]: this is God's admonition to the believers in Him and His Messenger that they do nothing but purely for God and in seeking what is with Him and not to be seen by people, as the polytheists did in their march to Badr, seeking to be seen by people; for they were told the Messenger of God and his Companions had missed the caravan, and were told, turn back, the caravan you came to defend is safe, and they refused and said, let us come to Badr, drink wine there, have singing girls play for us and have the Arabs speak of us; so in place of wine they were given the cups of death. Report 16171, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr: before the Prophet met Quraysh at Badr a rider from Abu Sufyan and those with him came to them saying, we have passed the people, so turn back; and the riders Abu Sufyan sent, who told Quraysh to return, came at al-Juhfa, and they said, by God we will not return until we stop at Badr and stay there three nights, so that those around us of the people of the Hijaz see us, for no one of the Arabs will see us and what we have gathered and fight us; and they are those of whom God said "who left their homes in arrogance and to be seen by people"; they met the Prophet, and God gave victory to His Messenger, disgraced the leaders of unbelief and healed the breasts of the believers against them. The editor notes this is from Urwa's letter to Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan which he traced above from al-Tabari's History. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 578} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 579}

Report 16172, from Ibn Ishaq from Muhammad ibn Muslim, Asim ibn Umar, Abd Allah ibn Abi Bakr and Yazid ibn Ruman from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr and others of our scholars from Ibn Abbas: when Abu Sufyan saw that he had secured his caravan he sent to Quraysh, you came out only to defend your caravan, men and wealth, and God has saved them, so go back; and Abu Jahl ibn Hisham said, by God we will not return until we reach Badr, Badr being a season of the Arabs where a market gathers each year, so we will stay three days, slaughter camels, feed people, serve wine, have singing girls play for us and let the Arabs hear of us, so that they keep fearing us always; so go on. Report 16173, from Ibn Ishaq: do not be like Abu Jahl and his men, that is, let your affair not be for show or repute or seeking what is with people, and be sincere in intention and reckoning for God in helping your religion and supporting your prophet, that is, do nothing but for God and seek nothing else. The editor corrects the name Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 579}

Reports 16174, 16175 and 16176, from Mujahid: those who left their homes in arrogance and to be seen are the men of Badr, Abu Jahl and his men on the day of Badr, and Abd Allah ibn Kathir said they are the polytheists of Quraysh, that is their going out to Badr; report 16177, from Ibn Abbas: the polytheists who fought the Messenger of God on the day of Badr; report 16178, from Qatada: Quraysh, Abu Jahl and his men who went out at Badr; report 16179, from Qatada: the polytheists of Quraysh who fought God's Prophet at Badr went out with insolence and boasting, and were told that day, go back, your caravan has gone and you have won, and said, no, by God, until the people of the Hijaz speak of our march and our numbers; and it was reported to us that the Prophet that day prayed against Quraysh, who had come with their pride and haughtiness to oppose God and His Messenger. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 580}

Report 16180, from al-Suddi: he mentioned the polytheists and what they fed people at the waters; report 16181, from al-Dahhak: they are the polytheists who went out to Badr in exultation and arrogance; report 16182, from Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi: when Quraysh left Mecca for Badr they went out with singing girls and drums, and God sent down the verse. Abu Ja'far said: so the sense of the words is: do not be, believers in God and His Messenger, in acting for show and repute and leaving sincerity of work for God and hoping for reward in it, like the army of those who disbelieve in God and His Messenger who left their homes in arrogance and to be seen by people by their appearance, their wealth, their many numbers and the strength of their retinue, "and bar from God's path", meaning they keep people from God's religion and entering Islam by fighting them and tormenting whoever of the believers they could; "God encompasses what they do" of showing off, barring from God's path and other deeds, meaning He knows all of that, nothing of it hidden from Him, since all things are plain to Him and nothing escapes Him, so He will punish them for it and torment them over it. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on "showing off", "barring", "God's path" and "encompassing". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 581}

Here ends the thirteenth volume of al-Tabari's commentary at p. 581, with the commentary on 8:47 complete; the fourteenth volume begins with 8:48. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 581}
