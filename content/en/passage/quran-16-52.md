## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth, and to Him belongs the religion. And be righteous. Will you fear anyone other than Allah?": To Allah belongs the dominion of whatever is in the heavens and the earth, with no partner in any of that. He is the One who created them, He is the one who provides for them, and in His hand is their life and death. And His saying: 'And to Him belongs the debt and the debt,' the Exalted says: 'And to Him belongs obedience and sincerity, always firm and obligatory.' It is said: 'And pour the debt into pours, and straighten and pour it,' as Al-Dayli said: I do not seek to pour a little praise for a day by condemning all time, and it is correct. Among these is God's saying: 'And they will have punishment, and be right.' And Hassan's saying: 'His jealousy is the wind that blows with him and defeats his thunder, and pours 'and pours.' As for the pain, it is said: 'And the man pours and swells,' meaning when he is tired and bored. Among them is the poet's saying: The leg should not be pinched from anywhere, nor should it be poured, nor should it bite its yellow cuff. Some people differed, and in the passage they quote from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, and others.
