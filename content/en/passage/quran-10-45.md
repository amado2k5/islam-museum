**Commentary on verse 45 (p. 97)**

On [[passage/quran-10-45|10:45]] he says: on the day We gather these polytheists, assembling them at the place of reckoning, it will be as though before that they had stayed only an hour of a day, knowing one another, and then acquaintance ends and that hour passes. "Those who denied the meeting with God have lost, and were not guided": those who denied God's reward and punishment have been cheated of their share of good and are ruined, and they were not guided to the right course in denying the meeting with God, because it brought them a punishment from God they cannot bear. The editor refers to the glosses on "gathering", "staying" and "loss". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 97}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
