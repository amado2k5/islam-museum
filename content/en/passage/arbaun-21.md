## Summary
Hadith of Sufyan ibn Abdullah al-Thaqafi of Ta'if, a Companion who was Umar's governor in Ta'if: he asked for a saying that covers Islam, and was told "Say: My Lord is God, then go straight". Ibn Rajab links the answer to God's words on those who say "our Lord is God" and then go straight. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
