## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 11 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “Have We not made the earth a bed?”: God, may He be exalted, is enumerating His blessings and favors upon these polytheists, His kindness towards them, and their ingratitude for what He has bestowed upon them. He is also threatening them with the various forms of punishment and painful torment He has prepared for them when they come before Him. He says to them, “Have We not made the earth a bed for you?” meaning a place to rest and spread out. On the authority of Sa’id, on the authority of Qatadah, regarding “Have We not made the earth a bed?”: meaning a carpet. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
