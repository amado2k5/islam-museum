## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 11-14 as a single section. He interprets the words, “They will be shown them. The criminal will wish that he could ransom himself from the punishment of that Day with his children”: On that Day, the disbeliever will wish and hope that he could ransom himself from God’s punishment with his children, his wife, his brother, and his clan—his tribe that shelters him, meaning the tribe that shelters him and with whom his wife resides, due to the close relationship between them—and with all of creation on earth, so that this might save him from God’s punishment on that Day. God, may His praise be exalted, begins by mentioning the children, then the wife, then the brother, to inform His servants that the disbeliever, due to the immense calamity that will befall him on that Day, would ransom himself, if he could find a way, with the dearest person to him in this world and the closest to him in kinship. The passage quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatada, and Ibn Zaid.
