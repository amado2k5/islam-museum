## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Abu Bakr al-Siddiq sent armies to Syria, and went out walking with Yazid ibn Abi Sufyan, who was commander over one quarter of those divisions. They claimed that Yazid said to him: either you ride or I will dismount; Abu Bakr said: you will not dismount, and I will not ride; I count these steps of mine as in God's path. He then charged him with ten things, among them: do not kill a woman, a child, or a very old man; do not cut down fruit-bearing trees; do not ruin inhabited places; do not slaughter sheep or camels except for eating; do not burn bees; do not embezzle; and do not be a coward. The editor notes that the text is cited in the Muwatta as a report. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.577}
