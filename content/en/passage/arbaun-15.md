## Summary
Hadith of Abu Hurayra: "Whoever believes in God and the Last Day, let him speak good or be silent, honour his neighbour, and honour his guest". Ibn Rajab shows that these are among the marks of faith, and that some duties concern God's rights and some concern people's rights. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
