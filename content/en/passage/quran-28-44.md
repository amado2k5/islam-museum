## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And you were not beside the western when We judged the matter to Moses, and you were not among the witnesses": "And you were not beside the western side of the mountain when We judged the matter to Moses," meaning: when We decreed for Moses the matter concerning what We obligated him and his people and entrusted him with the covenant "and I was not among the witnesses," meaning: "And I was not among the witnesses to that." In the passage, he quotes statements from Al-A'mash.
