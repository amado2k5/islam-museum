## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Call them to their fathers; that is more just with Allah. But if you do not know their fathers, then they are your brothers in religion and your mawalis. And upon you is no blame for what you err, but what your hearts intended. And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful": Allah Almighty says: "Attribute to your claimants those whose lineages you have connected to you to their forefathers." He says to his Prophet Muhammad: Trace the lineage of Zayd to his father Haritha, and do not abandon him Zayd ibn Muhammad. And His saying, 'He is more just in the sight of Allah,' means: Your prayer to them for their fathers is more just in the sight of Allah, and more truthful and correct than your prayer to them for those other than their fathers, and your lineage to those who adopted them and claimed them, and they are not His children. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
