## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 7 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We led the most of them to go, 'Go and be patient with your gods. Indeed, this is for a purpose.": And the nobles among these disbelievers from Quraysh, who said: "Make the gods one god," said: Go and be patient with your religion and worship your gods. From His saying: "to walk" is in the accusative case related to 'set out,' as if it was said: go on foot and continue on your religion. He mentioned that this is in Abdullah's recitation: "And the crowd set out from among them walking, saying, 'Be patient with your gods.'" He mentioned that the one who said this was Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibrahim.
