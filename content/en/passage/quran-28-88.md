## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And do not leave with Allah any other deity; there is no deity except Him. Everything perishes except His face, and to Him you will be returned": And do not worship, O Muhammad, with your deity who worships everything, worshipping everything else besides Him. And His saying: "There is no deity except Him" means there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, for whom everything perishes except His face. There is disagreement about the meaning of the phrase "except His face," with some saying it means: everything perishes except Him. Others said: This means nothing except what is intended for His sake, and they cited evidence to interpret this as well by the poet's saying: I ask Allah for forgiveness for a sin I do not account; to Him is the face and the action. And His saying: "To Him belongs the judgment," it means: He has the judgment between His creation and not others; no one else has the judgment among them except Him. "And to Him you will be returned," meaning: "And to Him you will be returned," meaning: "And to Him you will be returned after your death." He judges between you with justice, and your believers are rewarded accordingly, and your disbelievers are what He promised them.
