Al-Tabari explains that Talut and his troops came out against Goliath and his troops, and that the word for coming out means coming onto the open ground, the part of the earth that is exposed and level, which is why one who relieves himself is said to have gone out to it, as another term is used of the low ground where they did so. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 353} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 354} He says Talut and his men asked God to send down patience on them and to strengthen their hearts for fighting their enemy so that their feet would hold and they would not be routed, and to help them against the people who disbelieved in Him, denying Him as God, worshipping another and taking idols as lords. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v05 p. 354}
