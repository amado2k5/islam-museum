## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Said ibn al-Musayyab, that Uthman ibn Affan said: whoever gives a young son, who has not reached the age to take possession of his gift, a gift, announces it to him and calls witnesses to it, it is valid, even if his father has charge of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.321}
