## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And that is your assumption, which you thought of your Lord, that He will destroy you, and you have become among the losers": And what you thought in this world, that Allah does not know much of what you do, of the evils and evils of your deeds, is your assumption, which you thought your Lord in this world would be your desires, meaning He destroyed you. It is said: 'He caused so-and-so to be destroyed like that': when he destroyed him, and 'rada' means: when he perished, so he is 'rada rada'. From this is the saying of Al-A'sha: 'Was the rada feared for me in the circumambulation? And how many of his family were not thrown away?' meaning: how many of his family were destroyed but did not shoot. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
