## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who, when they remember Allah, their hearts are filled with fear and the patient over what befalls them, and those who establish prayer and spend from what We have provided them": This is one of the descriptions of the hidden ones. Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: "And give glad tidings, O Muhammad, the humble ones whose hearts humble themselves before the remembrance of Allah, humble themselves out of fear of Him, fear of His punishment, and fear of His wrath." Also: Ibn Zayd said regarding the phrase: "Those whose hearts become fearful when Allah is mentioned," he said: do not harden their hearts. "And those who are patient with what befalls them" from the hardship of Allah's affair and the hardship that befalls them in His behalf. "And those who establish prayer" which is obligatory. "And from what We have provided them" of wealth. "They spend" in what they are obligated to spend on, in zakat and the maintenance of the family and those whose expenses are obligatory and in the way of Allah. In the verse, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
