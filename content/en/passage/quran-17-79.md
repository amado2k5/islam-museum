## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And from the night, pray therein as a voluntary prayer for you; perhaps your Lord will raise you to a praised station": Allah the Exalted says to His Prophet Muhammad: And from the night, stay awake after sleep, O Muhammad, with the Qur'an, a voluntary prayer for you sincerely and not for your nation. And Tahajjud: staying awake and staying up after a sleep at night. As for al-Hajud itself: sleep, as the poet said: 'Did you not knock upon us while the companions were in Hajud?' and she spent the night in the midst of the ailments of the rivers, giving good. Al-Hutay'ah said: 'Did you not knock on the shores of Hauran and my companions in Hauran, the soldiers were hajud.' In the passage, sayings are transmitted from Mujahid, Hasan, Al-A'mash, and Ibrahim.
