## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 28 as a single section. He explains the phrase "obeyed there, trustworthy": "obeyed there" refers to Gabriel, obeyed in heaven by the angels. "Trustworthy" means trustworthy with God regarding His revelation and message, and other matters entrusted to him. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah.
