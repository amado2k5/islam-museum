## Summary
Yahya said: I heard Malik say about this verse: it is abrogated. The word of God, blessed and exalted: if he leaves good, a bequest for the parents and nearer relatives. What abrogated it is what was revealed of the division of the shares in God's book, the Mighty and Majestic. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.314}
