## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told a man asked Abdullah ibn Umar whether the witr is obligatory. Ibn Umar said the Messenger of God prayed the witr and the Muslims prayed it. The man kept repeating the question and Ibn Umar kept repeating his answer, in the chapter on the command to pray the witr. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.183}
