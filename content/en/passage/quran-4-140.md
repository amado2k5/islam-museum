**Commentary on verse 140: sitting with people of falsehood when they engage in it (pp. 320-321)**

Al-Tabari says that 'it has been revealed to you in the Book' means that God informed those hypocrites who took unbelievers as helpers that it had been revealed to them in the Qur'an: when you hear God's signs disbelieved and mocked, do not sit with them until they turn to other talk; that is, after they know God's prohibition on sitting with unbelievers who deny God's proofs and mock them. If you sit with them while you hear, you are like them in disobedience. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 320}

The verse plainly forbids sitting with people of falsehood of every kind, innovators and sinners, while they engage in their falsehood, and a number of earlier imams said the same. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 321}

Report 10708, from Abu Wa'il through Ibrahim al-Taymi and al-Awwam ibn Hawshab, says a man speaks a lying word in a gathering to make his companions laugh, and God is angry with them. This was put to Ibrahim al-Nakha'i, who said Abu Wa'il was right and asked whether it was not in God's Book. Report 10709, from Hisham ibn Urwa, says Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz caught men drinking and flogged them, and one of them was fasting; they said he was fasting, and he recited the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 321}

Report 10710, from Ibn Abbas through Ali ibn Abi Talha and Mu'awiya, says that this verse and similar Qur'anic passages show that God commanded believers to unity and forbade them to differ. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 321}

We stop here after report 10710 at p. 321, in the commentary on verse 140, and will continue from report 10711. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 321}
