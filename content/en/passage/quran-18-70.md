## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He said, 'If you follow me, then do not ask Me about anything until I bring you a reminder from it': The Blessed and Exalted says: The scholar said to Moses: 'If you follow me now, do not ask Me about anything I do that you disapprove, for I have taught you that I perform deeds in the unseen that you do not have knowledge of.' 'Until I bring you a reminder from it,' meaning: 'Until I inform you of the actions you see of me that you reprove, I will remind you of them, clarify their significance, and begin to inform you about them,' as follows: My father told me, he said: My uncle told me, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas: "So do not ask me about anything until I mention it to you," meaning about something I do until I clarify its status to you. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
