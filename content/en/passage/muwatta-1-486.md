## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad from al-A'raj from Abu Hurayra, in the chapter on encouragement to pray: when one of them sleeps, the devil ties three knots on the crown of his head, striking each knot with: "The night is long for you, so sleep". If he wakes and remembers God, one knot is undone; if he performs ablution, another; if he prays, the knots are all undone, and he rises alert and content. Otherwise he rises wicked-souled and lazy. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Abu Dawud, Ibn Hibban, Abu Awana, al-Tahawi and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.249} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.250}
