## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they worship besides Allah that which neither benefits them nor harms them, and the disbeliever is a supporter over his Lord": These polytheists worship gods besides Him that do not benefit them, so they bring them benefit if they worship them, and it does not harm them if they abandon their worship, and they abandon the worship of those who have bestowed upon them these blessings, which are not sufficient for the lesser, which is what Allah, the Exalted, has enumerated for us in these verses, from His saying: "Have you not seen before your Lord how He stretches out the shadow?" up to His saying: "Powerful." And from His power is the power that nothing can prevent Him, nor is He unable to do what He wills. And whoever wants to punish some of His servants, He permits for them what He described among the people of Pharaoh, 'Aad, Thamud, and the people of Ar-Rass, and many centuries between them. He was not a helper for those who were angry with him, nor was he able to defend him. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
