## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 30 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "Indeed, those who recite the Book of Allah, establish prayer, and spend from what We have provided them, secretly and openly, hope for a trade that will never perish": those who recite the Book of Allah which He revealed to Muhammad, "and establish prayer," means: and perform the obligatory prayer because its time is within its limits. He said: And they established prayer meaning: and establish prayer. And His saying: "And spend from what We have provided them, secretly and openly," means: and give charity from what We gave them of wealth, secretly and openly: openly. The meaning of this is that they pay the obligatory zakat and also volunteer to give charity from it after fulfilling the obligatory obligation upon them. And His saying: "They hope for a trade that will not be lost," God Almighty says: They hope by doing that trade that will not be ruined: it will neither become stagnant nor perish, as in their saying: 'Barat al-saqq' means if the market is sluggish and the food is barren. And His saying: 'trade' is an answer to the beginning of the statement. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
