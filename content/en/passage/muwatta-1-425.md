## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' that Abdullah ibn Umar neither passed in front of anyone nor let anyone pass in front of him, in the chapter on the strictness against passing in front of one who prays. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.221}
