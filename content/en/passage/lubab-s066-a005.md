## Summary
Al-Suyuti's report on the occasion of revelation of at-Tahrim 66:5, in Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul (Shamela 2247).

The verse, in al-Suyuti's heading: «عسى ربه إن طلقكن».
