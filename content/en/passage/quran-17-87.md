## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "except as a mercy from your Lord; indeed, His favor upon you is great": He says: "And if We willed, We could go" O Muhammad, "by what We revealed to you," but he does not will. It is a mercy from your Lord and His favor upon you. "Indeed, His favor has been great upon you" by choosing you for His message, sending down His Book upon you, and all His countless blessings upon you.
