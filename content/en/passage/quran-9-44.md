**Commentary on verse 44 (pp. 274-275)**

Al-Tabari says: this is an announcement from God to His prophet of the mark of the hypocrites, that among the signs by which they are known is their staying away from striving in God's way by asking the Messenger's leave not to go out with him when called out, with false excuses. God says to His prophet Muhammad: do not give leave to stay behind from you, when you go out to fight your enemy, to anyone who asks to stay behind without excuse, for only a hypocrite who does not believe in God and the last day asks it of you; as for one who believes in God and acknowledges His oneness, the resurrection, the abode of the Hereafter and reward and punishment, he does not ask leave to abandon fighting and striving against God's enemies with his wealth and person; "and God knows the pious" means God knows whoever feared Him and was mindful of Him by performing His obligations, avoiding disobedience and hastening to obey Him in fighting His enemy and striving against them with wealth and person, and in His other commands and prohibitions. Report 16768, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abu Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: "those who believe in God do not ask your leave" is a rebuke to the hypocrites when they asked leave to stay out of the struggle without excuse, and God excused the believers, saying "they do not go away until they ask his leave", which is [[passage/quran-24-62|24:62]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 275}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
