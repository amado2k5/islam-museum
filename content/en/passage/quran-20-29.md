## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 29 to 35 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." He explains: "Go," O Moses, "to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." He means he has transgressed and rebelled against his Lord. We have explained the meaning of transgression in what has passed, which is unnecessary to repeat it. In this passage, the speech is omitted, and it is sufficient for the listener to understand what was mentioned, which is: "Go to Pharaoh; he has transgressed." Invite him to the oneness of God and obey Him, and to send the Children of Israel with you. He quotes in the passage from Mujahid, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Zayd.
