## Summary
Verse 156 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 156 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Al-Tabari says that their saying against Mary a great slander means their calumny against her and their charge of fornication, which is the great slander, since they charged her with it without proof or evidence, slandering her with falsehood. Report 10776, from Ibn Abbas through Ali ibn Abi Talha and Mu'awiya, says they charged her with fornication; report 10777, from al-Suddi, says they slandered her with fornication; report 10778, from Juwaybir, says they said she had committed fornication. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 366}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
