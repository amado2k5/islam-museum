**Commentary on verse 116 (p. 538)**

Al-Tabari says: God, people, holds the authority and dominion of the heavens and the earth, and every king besides Him is His servant and possession, with their lives and deaths in His hand; He gives life to whom He wills and causes whom He wills to die. So do not shrink, believers, from fighting those kings who disbelieve in Him, whether the kings of Byzantium, of Persia, of Abyssinia or others; march against them and strive in His obedience, for He gives honour to whom He wills among them and among you, and humbles whom He wills. This, he says, is God's urging of the believers to fight every king who disbelieves in Him and an incitement to war against them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 538}

On "you have no protector or helper besides God": there is no ally besides God to support you against Him if you oppose His command and He punishes you for it, to rescue you from His punishment, and no helper to aid you against Him if He wills evil for you. So trust in God, fear Him, and strive in His way against those who disbelieve in Him, for He has bought your selves and wealth with Paradise, as you fight in His way, killing and being killed. The editor refers to the language indexes for the words of the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 538}
