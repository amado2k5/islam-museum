## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 5 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The start of the commentary on verse 5 (p. 560).** Abu Ja'far al-Tabari says the interpreters differed over the foolish whom God forbade His servants to give their property to; some said they are women and children, and he begins to list the reports with report 8523, through Mahmud ibn Bashshar from 'Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi, which the page end cuts off and which continues on p. 561. In the editor's notes: both copies give the verse's context only to the word for "support", but Abu Ja'far's commentary covers the rest of the verse, as will come at p. 571, so the editor completed it; and he refers to the earlier explanation of the words for foolishness and the foolish (6: 129 and 90; 3: 293-295; 1: 57-60). These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
