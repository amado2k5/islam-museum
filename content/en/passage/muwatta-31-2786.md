## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu Hazim ibn Dinar, from Sahl ibn Saad al-Saidi, that the Prophet said: if there is any ill omen, it is in the horse, the woman and the dwelling. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.566}
