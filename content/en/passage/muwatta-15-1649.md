## Summary
Malik says that a Muslim slave-woman, and a free Christian or Jewish woman, are subject to the mutual curse with a free Muslim man if he marries one of them and has intercourse with her, because God says in His book about those who accuse their wives: they are wives. This is the practice among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.80}
