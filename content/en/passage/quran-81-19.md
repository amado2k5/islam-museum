## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 19 to 22 as a single section. He explains the phrase “By the night when it darkens”: Our Lord, may His praise be exalted, swears by the night when it darkens, saying: “I swear by the night when it darkens.” The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the phrase “By the night when it darkens.” Some said that “when it darkens” means when it departs. In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Atiyyah.
