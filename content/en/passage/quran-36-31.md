## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 31 to 32 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Have they not seen how many generations We destroyed before them of generations that they will not return": Have they not seen how many We destroyed before them by their denial of Our messengers and their disbelief in Our verses from the empty generations "that they will not return"? He says: Did they not see that they would not return? In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
