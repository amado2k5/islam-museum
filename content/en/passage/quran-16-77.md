## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "To Allah belongs the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and the matter of the Hour is not except as the blink of an eye, or it is nearer. Indeed, Allah is over all things competent": And to Allah, O people, belongs the dominion of what has escaped your sight in the heavens and the earth, besides your gods which you invoke besides Him, and above all else. No one else possesses that. "And the command of the Hour is only like the blink of an eye," he says: The command of the Resurrection and the Hour when creation is spread to stand at the Resurrection is only like a glance of the eye, because that is only when it is said to be, and it is. Also: from Ma'mar, from Qatadah: "Except like the blink of an eye, or it is nearer." And the hour: like the blink of an eye, or closer. Muammar informed us, from Qatadah: "And the command of the Hour is only like the blink of an eye," he said: it means to say: Be, so it is like the blink of an eye, so the command of the Hour is like the blink of an eye or closer, meaning he says: or it is closer than the blink of an eye. He quotes in the passage from Qatadah.
