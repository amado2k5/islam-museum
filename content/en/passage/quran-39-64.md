## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 64 to 65 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Say, 'Do you command me to worship other than Allah, O ignorant?": Allah Almighty says to His Prophet: Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people, who call you to idol worship: 'Do you command me besides Allah?' O ignorant of Allah, 'command Me' to worship, and worship is not suitable for anything else. The Arabs differed regarding the accusative 'afghair' (afghir) in the accusative. Some grammarians of Basra said: Say, 'Am Ghayr Allah tarminuni' (do you command me?), as if he meant to cancel, and Allah knows best, as you say: 'He went so so he knows,' making it meaning: 'so he does not know.' Some grammarians of Kufa said: 'Ghayr' is in the accusative with 'abd,' and it is omitted and entered, because it is a sign for reception, as you say: I want to strike, and I want to strike, and perhaps I strike, and perhaps I strike. So it was in its request for reception, like your saying: Zayd, I will strike, so it was omitted, and what comes after it is applied in the preceding case. There is no need for us to use the verb.
