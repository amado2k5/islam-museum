## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 42 to 46 as a single section. He interprets the words, “They ask you about the Hour, when will it be established?”: These deniers of the Resurrection ask you, O Muhammad, about the Hour when you will resurrect the dead from their graves, when will it be established, when will it occur and appear? Al-Farra’ used to say: If someone were to say, “The term ‘establishment’ is only used for ships, and for firmly rooted mountains and the like, so how can the Hour be described as being established?” I would say: It is like a ship that is sailing and then it is anchored, and its anchoring is its standing. He said: Its standing is not like the standing of the one who stands, but rather like your saying, “Justice has been established,” and “Truth has been established,” meaning it has appeared and become firm. Abu Ja’far said: God says to His Prophet, “What do you know of its mention?” meaning: What do you know of the Hour and the investigation into its affairs? It was mentioned that the Messenger of God frequently spoke of the Hour (Day of Judgment) until this verse was revealed. The passage also quotes statements from Mujahid and Qatadah.
