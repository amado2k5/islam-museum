## Summary
A saying of Malik in the chapter on repeating prayer with the imam: he sees no harm in one who has prayed at home praying again with the imam, except the sunset prayer, for if he repeats it, it becomes an even number. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.195}
