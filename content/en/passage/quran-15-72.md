## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "By your life, they are blinded in their intoxication." Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad: By your life, O Muhammad, your people from Quraysh are in their misguidance and ignorance, hesitating. He narrates from Ibn Abbas that what Allah created and did not leave a soul is more honorable to Allah than He and did not swear by the life of anyone else. Qatadah is in their drunkenness, meaning in their misguidance and they are blind and playing; from Al-A'mash in their heedlessness they hesitate; from Mujahid, they hesitate; from Ibn Abbas: "By your life" so that you may live and persist; and it is mentioned about Ibrahim that they disliked a man's words to my life when they saw him as if he said 'By my life.'
