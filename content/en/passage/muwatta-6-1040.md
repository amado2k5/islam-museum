## Summary
A saying transmitted by Yahya from Malik: whoever is held back by an enemy who prevents him from reaching the House is released from everything; he sacrifices his offering and shaves his head where he was held back, and owes no making up. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1200) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.483}
