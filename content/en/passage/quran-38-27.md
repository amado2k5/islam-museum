## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 27 to 29 in a single syllable. He explains His saying: "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them in vain. That is the assumption of those who disbelieve. So woe to those who disbelieve from the Fire." "And We did not create the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them" in vain and amusement. We did not create them except to act upon them by obeying Us, and to conclude with Our command and prohibition. "That is the assumption of those who disbelieve." He says: What assumption is it that We created that in vain and play? Those who disbelieved in Allah thought so they did not unify Him, did not recognize His greatness, and that He should not tamper, so they are certain that He does not create anything falsehood. "So woe to those who "They disbelieved from the Fire" means: from the fire of Hell.
