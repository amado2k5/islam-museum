## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 18–19. Al-Tabari explains that God knows the disabled who hypocritically turn people away from witnessing the war with the Prophet, and those who say to their brothers, 'Come to us,' while they only come with a little hardship, be harsh on you, then describes their cowardice and fear of fighting.
