## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "He said, 'It is my staff; I lean on it and use it to lean on my sheep, and I have other purposes in it.': Allah Almighty says, informing about Moses: Moses replied to his Lord: 'It is my staff, which I lean on and use to graze on my sheep.' He means: I strike dry trees with it, and their leaves fall and my sheep graze them. It is said: 'So-and-so the tree is fragile, and the leaves fall off,' as the poet said: I smash with a stick over my sheep from the soft Arak and Basham, and in the passage he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
