## Summary
Verse 131 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 131 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**The meaning (p. 206).** Abu Ja'far says God tells the believers: beware, believers, of entering the Fire I prepared for those who disbelieve in Me, by your taking usury after My forbidding you it, and so entering where they enter after you believed in Me, by going against My command and abandoning obedience to Me. The editor notes that the printed text has the plural for the entrance and that he kept the manuscript's singular. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 206}
- From Ibn Humayd, Salama, Ibn Ishaq (7828): the one I made an abode for whoever disbelieves in Me. The editor says it is in the Sira (3: 115) and follows reports ending with 7827. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 206}
