## Verse 10 in al-Tabari's commentary (Shakir ed., volume 6, p. 222)

For [[passage/quran-3-10|3:10]] al-Tabari says that those who disbelieved are those who denied the truth they had recognised in Muhammad's prophethood, among the Jews of the Banu Israel and their hypocrites and the hypocrites of the Arabs and their disbelievers, in whose hearts is deviation, so that they follow what is resembling in God's book to seek discord and to seek its interpretation. The sense of the verse for him is that their wealth and their children will not rescue them from God's punishment if He sends it on them swiftly in this world, for denying the truth after it had become clear to them and for following the resembling in search of confusion, so as to ward it off from them, and that this will avail them nothing of it; and in the hereafter they are the fuel of the Fire, that is, its firewood. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 222} The editor says the print has 'after their establishing' with no meaning, that the manuscript has a word without dots, that he adopted what he judged the right reading, and that for the sense of the word for fuel he refers to the earlier discussion in volume 1. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 222}
