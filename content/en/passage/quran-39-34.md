## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 35 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And whoever brings the truth and believes it—those are the righteous": the interpreters differ regarding what was brought with truth and what is true, and what is so true. Some said: The one who brought the truth is the Messenger of Allah. They said: The truthful one who brought it is: There is no god but Allah, and the one who also believed in it is the Messenger of Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
