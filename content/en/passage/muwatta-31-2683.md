## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu Hazim ibn Dinar, from Sahl ibn Sad al-Ansari, that the Messenger of God was brought a drink and drank from it. A boy was on his right and the elders on his left. He said to the boy: will you permit me to give it to these? The boy said: no, by God, Messenger of God, I will not give my share of you to anyone. He said: then he placed it in his hand. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.515}
