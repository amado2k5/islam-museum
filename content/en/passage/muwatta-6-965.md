## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that he asked Amra bint Abd al-Rahman about one who sends his offering and stays at home, whether anything becomes forbidden to him; she told him that she heard Aisha say: nothing is forbidden except to one who has entered ihram and pronounced the talbiya. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1097) and Suwayd (511) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.458}
