## Summary
Malik explains that obscenity in the pilgrimage is intercourse with women, wickedness is the slaughter at the idols, and wrangling is what Quraysh did at the sacred monument at Muzdalifa, opposing the Arabs who stood at Arafat; God forbade that. He says this wrangling is, in his view, what he heard from people of knowledge. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.522}
