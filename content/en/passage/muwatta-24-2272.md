## Summary
Malik said: the child of a woman who was cursed in mutual curse is a client of his mother's clients, who inherit from him and pay his blood money if he commits an offence. If his father acknowledges him, he is attached to him, his clientage becomes that of his father's clients, his inheritance goes to them, and his blood money is on them; and his father is given the prescribed punishment. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.337}
