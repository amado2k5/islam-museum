**Commentary on verse 114 (p. 26)**

Al-Tabari says Pharaoh answered the sorcerers, when they asked whether they would have a reward if they overcame Moses: yes, that is yours, and you will be among those I hold close and bring near me. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 26}
