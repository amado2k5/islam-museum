## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads Abraham's wish to keep up the prayer as a request to perform what God made obligatory upon him, and to make some of his descendants do the same. He takes the request that his supplication be accepted to mean that worship is accepted, since supplication is itself worship, and cites a saying attributed to the Prophet to that effect.
