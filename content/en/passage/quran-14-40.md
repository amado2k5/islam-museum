## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari explains Abraham's prayer: "My Lord, make me one who establishes prayer": Make me fulfill what You have obligated me of Your obligation which You have imposed upon me through prayer. And "And from my descendants": also make from my descendants those who establish prayer for You. And "Our Lord, accept the prayer": accept the deeds I do for You and my worship of You. Al-Tabari points out that this is similar to the report narrated from the Prophet: "Indeed, supplication is worship," as he called supplication worship, so Abraham made asking for acceptance from him an act of worship he performed.
