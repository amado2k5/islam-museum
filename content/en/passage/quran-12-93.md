**Commentary on verse 93 (p. 248)**

On "go with this shirt of mine and cast it over my father's face, he will regain his sight, and bring me all your family", al-Tabari says: it is reported that when Joseph made himself known to his brothers he asked them about their father and they said his sight had gone from grief, and then he gave them his shirt and said to them "go with this shirt of mine". Report 19800, from al-Suddi, through Ibn Waki', Amr and Asbat: Joseph said to them, what has my father done since I left? They said: when Benjamin was lost to him he went blind from grief. He said "go with this shirt of mine and cast it over my father's face, he will regain his sight, and bring me all your family". On "he will regain his sight" he says he will return seeing; the editor says he did not find this sense in the dictionaries and calls it a rare one; "and bring me all your family" means come to me with all of your family. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 248}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
