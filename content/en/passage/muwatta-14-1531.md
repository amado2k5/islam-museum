## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr al-Makki that a marriage witnessed by only one man and one woman was brought to Umar ibn al-Khattab. He said this is a secret marriage, which he does not permit: had he been ahead of it, he would have stoned. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.42}
