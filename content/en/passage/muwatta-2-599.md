## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Humayd ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, from Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As, who said: the dead person is dressed in a shirt and a waist-wrap, and wrapped in the third garment. If there is only one garment, he is shrouded in it. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, Abd al-Razzaq, Muhammad ibn al-Hasan and Yahya ibn Bukayr transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.308}
