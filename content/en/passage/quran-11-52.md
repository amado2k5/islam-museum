**Commentary on verse 52 (pp. 358-360)**

On [[passage/quran-11-52|11:52]] al-Tabari reports Hud's saying to his people: 'my people, ask forgiveness of your Lord' means believe in Him so that He forgives you your sins; asking forgiveness here is belief in God, since Hud called his people to God's oneness so that He would forgive them their sins, as Noah said to his people in [[passage/quran-71-3|71:3]] and [[passage/quran-71-4|71:4]]. His words 'then turn to Him in repentance' mean turn to God from your earlier sins and your worship of others after believing in Him; 'He will send the sky upon you in abundance' means that if you believe in God and repent of your disbelief in Him, He will send down the sky's rain on you in streams, giving you rain in the time you need it and reviving your lands from drought and famine. Report 18261, from Ibn Abbas: 'in abundance' means following one another; report 18262, from Ibn Zayd: He sends it to them as drops and rain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 359}

As for 'and increase you in strength upon your strength', Mujahid used to say in reports 18263, 18264 and 18265: hardness upon your hardness. Report 18266, from Ibn Zayd: He had given them strength, and had they obeyed Him He would have added strength to their strength; and it was told us that this was said to them because children had been cut off from them for years, and Hud said to them, if you believe in God He will give life to your lands and provide you with wealth and children, for that is of strength. His words 'and do not turn away as criminals' mean do not turn away from what I call you to, God's oneness and renouncing idols and images, 'criminals' meaning disbelievers in God. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'abundance', 'turning away' and 'crime'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 360}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
