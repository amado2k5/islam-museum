## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 57 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "The dominion on that Day belongs to Allah to judge between them, so those who believe and do righteous deeds will be in the Gardens of Bliss": The dominion and sovereignty when the Hour comes belongs to Allah alone, with no partner and no one will dispute it that day. There have been kings in this world who are called by this name, and no one on that day will claim a king except Him. "He judges between them," meaning: He separates His creation, those who associate partners with Him, and the believers. "So those who believe" in this Qur'an, those who revealed it, and those who brought it, and who acted upon what is lawful, unlawful, limits, and obligations "will be in the gardens of bliss on that day." "And those who disbelieved" in Allah and His Messenger, "and deny" the verses of His Book and His revelation, and said: That is not from Allah; rather, it is a fabrication fabricated by Muhammad and supported by other people. "So those will have a humiliating punishment," meaning: those whose attribute is this will be for them in the sight of Allah on the Day of Resurrection a humiliating punishment, meaning a humiliating punishment in Hell.
