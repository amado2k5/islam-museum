## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And [mention] when Moses said to his people, 'O my people, you did not harm me, while you knew that I am the Messenger of Allah to you.' But when they deviated, Allah turned their hearts astray. And Allah does not guide the defiantly disobedient people." And remember, O Muhammad, when Moses son of Imran said to his people, 'O my people, you did not harm Me while you already knew,' indeed, 'How is the Messenger of Allah to you?' And His saying: "So when they deviated, Allah turned their hearts astray," he means: When they turned and turned away from the way, Allah turned their hearts away: He says: Allah turned their hearts away from Him. And from Abu Umamah, regarding the saying: "But when they deviated, Allah turned their hearts away," he said: They are the Kharijites. "And Allah does not guide the defiantly disobedient people," meaning: By Allah, He does not grant success to the truth to those who chose disbelief over faith.
