## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Nafi' from Ibn Umar, in the chapter on the ritual washing on Friday: the Prophet ordered whoever comes to the Friday prayer to bathe. The editor cites al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i, Ahmad, al-Darimi, al-Tahawi and al-Bayhaqi from Malik's transmitters, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.158} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.159}
