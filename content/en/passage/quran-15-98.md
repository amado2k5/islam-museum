## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 97 to 98 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So glorify the praise of your Lord and be among those who prostrate": So be distressed about what befalls you of any matter that dislikes you, to thank Allah, praise Him, and pray; Allah will protect you from that as long as it concerns you. This is similar to the report narrated from the Messenger of Allah: "That whenever he was oppressed by something, he would rush to prayer."
