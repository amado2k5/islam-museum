## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Abbas: "Guard God and God will guard you; know that with patience there is much good, that victory comes with patience, relief with distress, and ease with hardship". Ibn Rajab comments on guarding God's limits, on seeking help from God alone, and on patience as obligatory. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
