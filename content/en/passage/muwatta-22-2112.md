## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad that Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote to Abd al-Hamid ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd ibn al-Khattab, his governor in Kufa: judge with an oath alongside a witness. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.263}
