## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 164 to 167 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, you and what you worship" means: "Indeed, you," O polytheists of Allah, "and what you worship" of gods and idols is "what you are in a state of intrigue," meaning: you are not in a state of intrigue over what you worship besides Allah, meaning you mislead anyone, except one who is the one who is in Hell. It has been said that the meaning of 'upon him' in His saying: 'What you are enchanted by' is the meaning of 'with it.' In the excerpt, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibrahim.
