## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Those who bear the Throne and those around it glorify the praise of their Lord, believe in Him, and ask forgiveness for those who have believed. Our Lord, You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge. So forgive those who repent and follow Your way and protect them from the punishment of Hell." means: those who bear the Throne of Allah from among His angels and those around His Throne, among those surrounded by angels, "glorify the praise of their Lord," meaning: they call upon their Lord with His praise and thanksgiving, and "they believe in Him." He means: and acknowledge that they have no deity except Him, and bear witness to that. They are arrogant about His worship "and ask forgiveness for those who believe," meaning: and they ask their Lord to forgive those who acknowledge the oneness of God and disavow their sins from every deity besides Him, so He pardons them, as Qatadah said: "And they ask forgiveness for those who believe" refers to the people of 'There is no god but Allah.' And His saying: "Our Lord, You encompass all things in mercy and knowledge," and in this statement it is omitted, and it is said, and the meaning of the speech and 'they seek forgiveness for those who believe' is saying: O Lord, You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
