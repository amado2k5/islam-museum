## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6 to 10 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Indeed, they see it as far away”: These polytheists perceive the punishment they inquired about, which is to befall them, as far off. God, may His praise be exalted, informs us that they see it as far away because they do not believe in it and deny the resurrection after death, and the reward and punishment. He says: They see it as not to occur, but We see it as near, because it is coming, and everything that is to come is near. The “them” in “Indeed, they” refers to the disbelievers, and the “it” in “they see it” refers to the punishment. He then quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and Qatadah in this section.
