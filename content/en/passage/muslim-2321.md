## Summary
A report of Abdullah ibn Amr in the chapter on his smile and good companionship, in the Book of Virtues of Sahih Muslim. It is a close testimony on his character: he was neither foul nor abusive, and the best of people are those with the best character. {cite:sahih-muslim}
