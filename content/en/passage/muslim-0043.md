## Summary
A report of Anas ibn Malik in the chapter on the qualities of the sweetness of faith in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim: love of God and His Messenger, loving for God's sake, and disliking a return to disbelief. These are the measures of tasting faith. {cite:sahih-muslim}
