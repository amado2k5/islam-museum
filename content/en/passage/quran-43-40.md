## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40 to 42 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Do you hear the deaf or guide the blind and whoever is in clear error": "Do you hear the deaf": whoever Allah has deprived him of hearing his arguments that he used in this book and deafens him, or guides him to the path of guidance from someone whose heart Allah has blinded and is possessed by Satan, then the repulsion will be adorned for him. "And whoever is in clear error" means: Or guide him who is in injustice intentionally, a seeker of a path other than the path of truth, whose misguidance has become clear that he is departing from the truth and deliberately The path is unjust: He, the Exalted, says: That is not for you, but for Allah, in whose hand is the turning of the hearts of His creation as He wills. Rather, you are a warner, so convey the warning to them. And his statement: In the Maqta' section, he quotes from Qatadah and Al-Hasan.
