**Commentary on verse 12 (pp. 386-388)**

On "it is He who shows you the lightning, in fear and hope, and brings up the heavy clouds; the thunder glorifies His praise, and the angels too for fear of Him; He sends the thunderbolts and strikes with them whom He wills, while they dispute about God, and He is severe in power", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: "it is He who shows you the lightning", meaning that the Lord is the One who shows His servants the lightning, the "huwa" standing for His name, mighty be His praise. We have explained the meaning of "lightning" earlier and mentioned the interpreters' differences over it, which spares repeating it in this place. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 386}

"Fear" means fear for the traveller of its harm, for "lightning" in this place is water. Report 20251, from Musa ibn Salim Abu Jahdam, freedman of Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Hajjaj and Hammad: Ibn Abbas wrote to Abu al-Jald asking him about "lightning", and he said, "lightning" is water; the editor notes that his report from Ibn Abbas is mursal, and that Abu al-Jald is Jilan ibn Farwa al-Asadi, a reliable narrator. "And hope" means hope for the resident that it will rain and he will benefit. Report 20252, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "fear" for the traveller on his journeys who fears its harm and hardship, "and hope" for the resident who hopes for its blessing and benefit and for God's provision. Report 20253, from Qatada, through Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: fear for the traveller and hope for the resident. "And brings up the heavy clouds" means He raises the heavy clouds with rain and begins them; one says "God originated the clouds" when He began them and "the clouds began" when they started. "Clouds" in this place, though in the singular form, is a plural, its singular being "sahaba", which is why He said "heavy" in the plural form; had it been "the heavy cloud" in the singular it would also have been allowed, treating the word "sahab" as singular, as in "who made for you fire from the green tree" [[passage/quran-36-80|36:80]]. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 387}

The interpreters said the same. Reports 20254 to 20258, from Mujahid, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad, Shababa, Waraqa and Ibn Abi Najih; Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim and Isa; al-Muthanna, Abu Hudhayfa and Shibl; Ishaq and Abd Allah; and al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "brings up the heavy clouds", he said, those with water in them. On "the thunder glorifies His praise", al-Tabari says he has already explained the meaning of "thunder", which spares repeating it here. It is mentioned that the Messenger of God, when he heard the sound of thunder, used to say what is in report 20259, from Ja'far, through al-Hasan ibn Muhammad and Kathir ibn Hisham: it reached us that the Prophet, when he heard loud thunder {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 388}

We stop here at the foot of p. 388, in the middle of report 20259 (on what the Prophet used to say on hearing thunder); the next batch begins at p. 389 (marker 397). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 388}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
