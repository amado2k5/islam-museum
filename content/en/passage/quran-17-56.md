## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Call upon those whom you claim besides Him, for they have no power to remove harm from you or to divert it.'" Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people who worship besides Allah of His creation: Call upon those people whom you claim to be lords and gods besides Him when harm befalls you. So see if they can repel that from you or divert it from you to others. You call them gods, for they are not capable of that, nor possess it; rather, your Creator and their Creator possess it and is able to do so. It is said that those to whom the Prophet was commanded to say this were worshippers of the angels, Ezra, and the Messiah, and some of them worshipped a group of jinn. In the Maqta', statements from Ibn Abbas are also quoted.
