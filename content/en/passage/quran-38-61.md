## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "They said, 'Our Lord, whoever brings this to us, He will increase him with a punishment and weakness in the Fire": This is also the saying of the group that stormed the tyrants, who were followers of the tyrants in this world. He says: The followers said: "Our Lord, who brought this to us," meaning: the one who brought them in this world by their supplication to the deeds that would bring them the Fire they entered and the dwelling in the house from which they dwelt. And by saying "this," they mean: the punishment that We have sent down: "He increased it in a punishment weakened in the Fire," meaning: the punishment in the Fire was weaker for him than the torment he is in it. This is also from the supplication of the followers to those who follow.
