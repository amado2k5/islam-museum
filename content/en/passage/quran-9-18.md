**Commentary on verse 18 (p. 167)**

Al-Tabari says: only one who affirms God's oneness and is sincere in his worship of Him tends God's mosques, and who believes in the last day, believing in God's raising of the dead alive from their graves on the day of resurrection, establishes the prescribed prayer with its limits, pays the obligatory zakat from his property to those to whom God made it due, and "fears none but God", that is dreads the punishment of nothing for disobeying Him except God; "it may be that they are among the guided" means those of this description are fit to be, with God, among those He has guided to the truth and to the right course. Report 16555, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abdullah ibn Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: one who affirms God's oneness and believes in the last day, that is acknowledges what God sent down; the report continues on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 167}
