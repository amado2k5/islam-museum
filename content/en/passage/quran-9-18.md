**Commentary on verse 18 (pp. 167-168)**

Al-Tabari says: only one who affirms God's oneness and is sincere in his worship of Him tends God's mosques, and who believes in the last day, believing in God's raising of the dead alive from their graves on the day of resurrection, establishes the prescribed prayer with its limits, pays the obligatory zakat from his property to those to whom God made it due, and "fears none but God", that is dreads the punishment of nothing for disobeying Him except God; "it may be that they are among the guided" means those of this description are fit to be, with God, among those He has guided to the truth and to the right course. Report 16555, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Muthanna, Abdullah ibn Salih, Mu'awiya and Ali: one who affirms God's oneness and believes in the last day, that is acknowledges what God sent down, and establishes prayer, meaning the five prayers, and fears none but God, meaning then worships none but God; "it may be that they" means they are the successful, as in His words to His prophet that his Lord will raise him to a praised station, [[passage/quran-17-79|17:79]], which is the intercession, every "it may be" in the Qur'an being binding. Report 16556, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: God recalled the saying of Quraysh that they were the people of the sanctuary, waterers of the pilgrims and tenders of this House and none was better than they, and said your tending is not of that kind; only one who tends the mosques rightly, believing in God and the last day, establishing prayer, paying zakat and fearing none but God, is their true tender, and an "it may be" from God is certain. The editor refers to Ibn Hisham's Sira. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 168}
