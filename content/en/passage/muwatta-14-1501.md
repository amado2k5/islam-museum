## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz wrote in his caliphate to some of his governors that whatever the one who gives the woman in marriage stipulates, whether he is her father or someone else, of gifts or honour, belongs to the woman if she seeks it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.32}
