## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "So when the truth came to them from Us, they said, 'Kill the sons of those who believed with him and made their women feel ashamed. And the plotting of the disbelievers is only in error.": When Moses came to those whom Allah had sent with the truth from Us, that was his coming to them by the oneness of Allah and acting in obedience to Him, while establishing proof against them that Allah had sent him to them by praying to them, "They said, 'Kill the sons of those who believed' by Allah 'with Him' from the Children of Israel, and their women were ashamed (and their women were ashamed)." He says: They preempted their women to serve. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
