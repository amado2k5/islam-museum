## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, who said: I heard Said ibn al-Musayyab say: I prayed behind Abu Hurayra over a child who had never committed a sin, and I heard him say: O God, protect him from the punishment of the grave. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.313}
