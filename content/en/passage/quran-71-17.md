## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 18 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “And Allah caused you to grow from the earth a [progressive] growth”: Allah created you from the dust of the earth, forming you from it. “Then He will return you into it,” meaning: He will return you to the earth as you were dust, making you as you were before He created you. “And He will bring you forth again,” meaning: He will bring you forth from it, if He wills, alive, as you were human beings before He returned you to it, making you dust again. In this section, he quotes statements attributed to Qatadah.
