## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 178–182. Al-Tabari explains that God commands His Prophet to turn away from the polytheists until He sees them, for they will see the punishment that befalls them. Then he concludes the surah by exalting His Lord, Lord of Glory, above what they describe, and peace be upon the messengers, and praise be to God, Lord of the worlds.
