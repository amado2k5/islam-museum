## Summary
Verse 80 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 80 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Commentary on the verse (pp. 561-562, note 1).** Al-Tabari says this is God's excuse to his creation about his prophet Muhammad. God says to them: whoever of you, people, obeys Muhammad has obeyed me by obeying him, so hear his words and obey his command; what he commands is from my command and what he forbids is from my prohibition, so none of you should say Muhammad is a man like us who wants to put himself above us. Then he says to his prophet: whoever turns from obeying you, Muhammad, we did not make you a watcher over them, a keeper of their deeds who takes account of them; we sent you only to make clear to them what was sent down to them, and we suffice as keepers of their deeds and reckoners of them. The editor refers to the earlier interpretation of the verb for turning away (page number as printed in volume 7). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 562}

**Report 9979 (p. 562).** Al-Tabari says this verse came down, as is mentioned, before the order to fight. Yunus (9979, from Ibn Wahb) reports that he asked Ibn Zayd about it, and he said this was the beginning of God's sending him, and cited what is said in sura al-Shura, that the Prophet has only to convey ([[passage/quran-42-48|42:48]]); then, he said, after this came the order to fight them and be harsh with them until they submit. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 562}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 562}
