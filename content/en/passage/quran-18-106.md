## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "That is their recompense Hell for what they disbelieved and took My verses and messengers as mockery": Those are the ones whose reward is Hell for their disbelief in Allah, their taking the verses of His Book and the arguments of His messengers as mockery, and their mocking of His messengers.
