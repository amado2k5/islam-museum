## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 47 to 48 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And the Day We will move the mountains and you will see the earth exposed, and We will gather them without leaving any of them": "And the Day We will move the mountains" from the earth, and we will make them scattered dust, "and you will see the earth exposed," apparent, and their appearance to the eyes of the onlookers, without anything covering them from mountains or trees, is their emergence. A group of interpreters have said similarly. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
