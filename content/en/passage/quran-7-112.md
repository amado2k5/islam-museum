**Commentary on verse 112 (p. 24)**

Al-Tabari says this is God's report of the advice that the chiefs of Pharaoh's people gave him: to send summoners into the cities to bring every knowing sorcerer. Something is omitted in the speech, which the apparent sense makes unnecessary to state: "so he sent summoners into the cities to gather the sorcerers". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 24}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
