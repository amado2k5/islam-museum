## Summary
Malik says that the matter about the ransom for harm is that no one ransoms until he does what obliges the ransom, and that expiation is due only once it is obligatory on its owner; and he may place his ransom wherever he wishes, whether an offering, fasting or charity, in Mecca or in other lands. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.558}
