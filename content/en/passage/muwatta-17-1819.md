## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Rabia ibn Abi Abd al-Rahman, that al-Qasim ibn Muhammad used to sell the fruit of his orchard while keeping back a part of it for himself. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.144}
