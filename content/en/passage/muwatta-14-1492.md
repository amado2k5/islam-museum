## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim from his father, who used to say, about the verse of al-Baqara on hinting at proposing to women: a man may say to a woman who is in her waiting period after her husband's death, "You are noble to me, I am eager for you, and God will bring you good and provision", and similar words. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.28}
