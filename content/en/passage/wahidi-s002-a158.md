## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Baqara 2:158, narrated from the Companion generation in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «إن الصفا والمروة من شعائر الله».
