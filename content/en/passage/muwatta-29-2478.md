## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab, from Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf, from Abu Hurayra, that two women of Hudhayl struck one another, so one of them miscarried her foetus, and the Prophet ruled a ghurra (a slave, male or female) for it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.423}
