## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 26 to 27 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And He sent down those who supported the confederates from the People of the Book from their supporters and cast terror into their hearts a group you kill and capture a party": And Allah sent down those who helped the confederates from Quraysh and Ghitan against the Messenger of God and his companions, and that is their manifestation to him. This was what Banu Qurayza meant, who opposed the confederates against the Messenger of God. And His saying "from the People of the Book" means: from the People of the Torah, and they were Jews: and His saying: "from their fortresses" means: from their fortresses. In the Maqta', he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Ishaq.
