## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 35 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "The people of Lot denied the warning": The people of Lot denied the signs of God that He warned and reminded them of. And His saying: "Indeed, We sent against them a striker," God Almighty says: We sent stones against them. And His saying: 'Except the family of Lot, We saved them by magic,' means: except the family of Lot who believed him and followed him in his religion, so We saved them from the punishment with which We punished his people who denied him. And the usurper with whom We besieged them with magic: a blessing from Us: He says: A blessing We bestowed upon Lot and his family, and a honor We honored them with from Us. And His saying: 'Thus do We reward those who are grateful,' it means: Just as We rewarded Lot and his family, and bestowed favor upon them, and saved them from Our punishment by obeying Us, so too do We reward those who thank Us for Our favor upon him, so we obey, fulfilled Our command, and forbade all of Our creation.
