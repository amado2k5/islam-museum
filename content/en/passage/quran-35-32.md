## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "Then We gave the Book to those whom We chose from among Our servants. Among them is he who wrongs himself, and among them is he who is moderate, and among them is the forerunner in good deeds, by Allah's permission. That is the great bounty." The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the Book that Allah mentioned in this verse as an inheritance by those He chose from among His servants, those chosen among His servants, and the one who wronged himself. Some said: The Book is the books that Allah revealed before the Criterion, and the chosen among His servants: The nation of Muhammad, and the oppressor to himself: the criminals among them. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
