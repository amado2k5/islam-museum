## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that whoever offers a camel as a penalty, a vow, or an offering of enjoyment (tamattu), and it is struck on the road, owes a replacement. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1219) and Suwayd (528) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.513}
