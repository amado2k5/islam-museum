## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And among the devils are those who dive for him and do deeds other than that, and We were their guardians": We also subjected Solomon among the devils who dive for him in the sea and do deeds other than that, such as buildings, statues, and mihrabs. "And We were their guardians," meaning: We were guardians of their deeds and their numbers, but we could not memorize all of that.
