## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "That is because those who disbelieve follow falsehood, and those who believe follow the truth from their Lord. Thus Allah presents to people their likes": This is what we did to these two groups by misleading the deeds of the disbelievers and expiating those who believed and did righteous deeds, as a reward from each of them for their deeds. As for the disbelievers, We misguided their deeds and made them untrue and unguided, because they followed Satan and obeyed him, which is falsehood. As Zakariya ibn Yahya ibn Abi Zaidah and Abbas ibn Muhammad narrated to me, they said: Ibn Jurayj said: Khalid told me that he heard a Mujahid say, "That is because those who disbelieve followed falsehood." He said: Falsehood is Satan.
