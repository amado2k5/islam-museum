## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "He it is Who sent down water from the sky; from it is water from which you drink, and from it trees in which you may grow." He is the One who bestowed upon you these blessings and created for you livestock, horses, and all the cattle for your benefit and welfare. He is the Lord who sent down water from the sky, meaning: rain from that water for you to drink, and from it the drink of your trees and the life of your plants and plants. "In it, they say" means in the trees that grow from the water sent down from the sky, meaning you tend to herd. It is said: 'Asam so-and-so is her camels named Usama' when he grazed her, and 'sawam' also refers to her when she grazed, so she is 'saam'. They are grazing camels. From this, it is said of livestock that are grazing in the wilderness and others for grazing. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Al-Dahhak, Ikrimah, Ibn Zayd, Ibn Jurayj, and others.
