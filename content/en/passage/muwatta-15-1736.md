## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that he used to say the waiting period of an umm walad, when her master dies, is one menstrual period. Malik said this is the practice among us. If she is not one who menstruates, her waiting period is three months. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.108}
