## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on leaving ablution for food touched by fire. Yahya transmits from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar from Abdullah ibn Abbas: the Prophet ate a shoulder of lamb and then rose to pray without performing ablution. The editor cites al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Hibban, and refers to al-Tamhid. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.60} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.61}
