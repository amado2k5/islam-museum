## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "And if you ask them who created the heavens and the earth, they will say, 'Allah,' say, 'Praise be to Allah.' Rather, most of them do not know." And if you asked, O Muhammad, those who associate partners with Allah among your people, 'Who created the heavens and the earth, they would say, Allah? Say praise be to Allah.' Allah says to His Prophet Muhammad. If they say that, say to them: Praise be to Allah who created that, not for those who do not create anything while they are creating. Then He, the Exalted, said: "Rather, most of them do not know," meaning: Rather, most of these polytheists do not know who belongs to Him the praise, and where is the place of gratitude?
