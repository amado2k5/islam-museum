**Commentary on verse 42 (p. 331)**

On [[passage/quran-11-42|11:42]] al-Tabari says: 'and it ran with them' means the ark ran with Noah and those with him in it, 'in waves like mountains, and Noah called to his son', Yam, 'who was in a place apart' from him and had not boarded the ark with him, 'my son, ride with us', in the ark, 'and be not with the disbelievers'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 331}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
