## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father al-Qasim ibn Muhammad, that he used to enter Mecca at night while on the lesser pilgrimage; he circumambulated the House and ran between al-Safa and al-Marwa, delayed the shaving until morning, and did not return to the House to circumambulate it until he had shaved his head. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.530}
