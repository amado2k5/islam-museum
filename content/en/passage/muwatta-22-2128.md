## Summary
Yahya said: Malik told us, from Hashim ibn Hashim ibn Utba ibn Abi Waqqas, from Abdallah ibn Nistas, from Jabir ibn Abdullah al-Ansari, that the Messenger said: whoever swears a sinful oath on my pulpit has taken his seat in the Fire. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.269}
