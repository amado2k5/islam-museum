## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 56 to 57 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And establish prayer and give zakah and obey the Messenger that you may receive mercy": "And establish prayer," O people, "prayer" within its limits and do not neglect it. "And give zakat" which Allah has prescribed upon you from its rightful owners, and obey the Messenger of your Lord in what He commands and forbids you. "Perhaps you will be shown mercy" means: so that your Lord may have mercy on you and save you from His punishment. And His saying: "Do not think those who disbelieve will be miracles on earth," the Exalted says: Do not think, O Muhammad, those who disbelieve in Allah His miracles on earth if He intends to destroy them. "And their refuge" after their destruction is "the Fire, and what an evil destination" to which they will become that refuge. Some have said: "Those who disbelieve are not considered to be punished" with a ya. This is a weak view among the Arabs because "you think" requires accusative cases.
