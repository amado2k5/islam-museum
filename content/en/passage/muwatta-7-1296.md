## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar, when he gave something in God's path, would tell its recipient: when you reach Wadi al-Qura, it is yours to decide. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.579}
