## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on the places of entering ihram. Malik transmits from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God said: the people of Medina enter ihram from Dhu al-Hulayfa, the people of Syria from al-Juhfa, and the people of Najd from Qarn. Ibn Umar said: and I heard it said that the Messenger of God said: the people of Yemen enter ihram from Yalamlam. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1060) transmitted it from Malik, and that Ibn Abd al-Barr (al-Tamhid 15/137) said that Ibn Umar did not hear the Prophet's words about Yalamlam, and that a Companion's relayed report from another Companion is evidence. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.444}
