## Summary
Verse 189 of [[passage/quran-3|Surat Al 'Imran]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 189 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 189 (p. 473).** Al-Tabari says this is God's belying of those who spoke of His poverty and their wealth; He says that everything contained in the heavens and the earth is His, so how can the owner of that be poor, you who fabricate against Him? He then reports that He is able to hasten punishment for those who say it and for everyone who belies and fabricates against Him, and for other things He wishes, but He has been gracious with forbearance toward His creation; His power, for al-Tabari, includes destroying the speakers and hastening their punishment and other matters. These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 473}
