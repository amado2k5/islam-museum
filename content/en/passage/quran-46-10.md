## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Say, 'Have you considered if it is from Allah and you disbelieve in it, and a witness from the Children of Israel testified to the like of it, so believe and you are arrogant? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people": Say, O Muhammad, to these polytheists who say this Qur'an when this Qur'an came to them is clear magic. "Have you, O people, 'if this Qur'an' is from Allah, He revealed it to me, and you disbelieved in it," meaning "and you denied it." He means: "And you denied it." And His saying: "And a witness from the Children of Israel testified to his likeness," scholars of interpretation differed on its interpretation. Some said: Its meaning: a witness from the Children of Israel, who is Moses son of Imran, testified about his like, meaning on the likes of the Qur'an. They said: And the example of the Qur'an that Moses testified to by affirming the Torah.
