## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Sulayman ibn Yasar told him that Abdallah ibn Ayyash ibn Abi Rabia al-Makhzumi said: Umar ibn al-Khattab ordered me, with a group of young men of Quraysh, and we flogged slave-girls of the governorship fifty lashes each for adultery. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.389}
