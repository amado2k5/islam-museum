## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr ibn Hazm, who often heard his father say: Umar ibn al-Khattab used to say: strange is the paternal aunt, who is inherited from but does not inherit. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.19}
