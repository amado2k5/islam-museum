## Summary
A saying of Malik with which he closes the chapter: there is no harm in trading with the property of orphans, if their guardian is trustworthy, and he does not consider him liable for it. The editor notes that one copy reads 'licensed' rather than 'trustworthy', and that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.343}
