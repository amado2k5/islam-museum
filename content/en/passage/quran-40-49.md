## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 49 to 50 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And those in the Fire will say, 'Ask your Lord to lighten for us a day of the punishment.'" And the people of Hell said to its keeper and its supporters, seeking help from the great affliction they are in, hoping to find relief from them. "Call upon your Lord" for us "to lighten for us a single day," meaning the measure of one day of the worldly days "from the torment" we are in. We only said: The meaning of this is: the decree of a day in this world, because the Hereafter is a day without night, so it is said: Lighten their burden by one day. And His saying: "They said, 'Did your messengers not come to you with clear proofs?'" Allah says: The keeper of Hell said to them: 'Or did your messengers not come to you in this world with clear proofs for the oneness of Allah, so that you would unify Him, believe in Him, and disown what is besides Him among the gods?' They said: Yes, our messengers have brought us that.
