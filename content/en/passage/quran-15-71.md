## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 71 to 73 in one passage: "These are my daughters, if you are truly doing." Lot said to his people: "Marry women and approach them, but do not do what Allah has forbidden you of having men if you are to do what He commands you." — Qatadah commanded them to marry women and wanted to protect his guests with his daughters. "By your life": By your life, O Muhammad, your people of Quraysh "were blinded in their intoxication" in their misguidance and ignorance, hesitating. It is narrated from Ibn Abbas that God did not swear by anyone's life, and from Qatadah in their drunkenness, meaning in their misguidance and blindly playing, and from Al-A'mash in their heedlessness they hesitated, and from Mujahid they hesitated; and from Ibn Abbas they went too far; it is mentioned about Ibrahim that they disliked a man's words to my life because it was like his saying and my life. "So the cry seized them east": the thunderbolt of punishment struck them as the sun rose, and "mashreqin" and "misbahin" are accusatives of the state meaning when they entered the morning and when they rose.
