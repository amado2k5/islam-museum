## Summary
Malik transmits from Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father, from Aisha, Mother of the Believers, that the Messenger of God made the pilgrimage single. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1076) transmitted it from Malik, and that Ismail ibn Abi Uways in Muslim and al-Jawhari and al-Bayhaqi and many others transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.451}
