## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 4–10. Al-Tabari explains that your deity, whose worship and sincere obedience to You is One, with no partner, Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them, and Lord of the easts, and that God adorned the lowest heaven with the adornment of the stars and protected them from every rebellious devil, so they do not listen to the highest assembly and are thrown from every side with retreats, and they will have a severe punishment except for the one who snatches the snatch, followed by a piercing shooting star.
