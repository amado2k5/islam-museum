## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Umar ibn al-Khattab built a yard at one side of the mosque called al-Batayha, and said: whoever wants to make a noise, recite poetry or raise his voice, let him go out to this yard. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik as connected: Abu al-Nadr told me, from Salim ibn Abdullah, that Umar ibn al-Khattab. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.248}
