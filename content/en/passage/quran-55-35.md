## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 35 to 38 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He will send upon you flames of fire and copper, so you will not be victorious": "He will be sent upon you, O heavy ones, on the Day of Resurrection 'flames of fire,' which is its flame from where it ignites and ignites without smoke inside. Among them is the saying of Ru'bah ibn al-Ajjaj: 'They will have those who have fallen upon us with sparks and the fire of war, which will ignite the flames.' In the passage, sayings are transmitted from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Dhahhak, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
