## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 2 to 6 as a single section. He interprets the phrase “By the sky containing constellations”: Abu Ja’far said: “His statement, ‘By the sky containing constellations,’ means that God, may His praise be exalted, swore by the sky containing constellations. The scholars of interpretation differed regarding the meaning of constellations in this context. Some said that it refers to the sky containing palaces. They said that constellations are palaces.” In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
