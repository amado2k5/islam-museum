**Commentary on verse 103 (pp. 12-13)**

Al-Tabari says: then, after Noah, Hud, Salih, Lot and Shu'ayb, We sent Moses son of Amran. The pronoun in "after them" refers to the prophets named from the beginning of the sura to this point. "With Our signs" means with Our proofs and arguments; "to Pharaoh and his chiefs" means to Pharaoh's body of men; "but they wronged them" means they disbelieved in them. The pronoun in "them" returns to the signs, so the sense is that they wronged the signs with which Moses was sent to them. It is allowed to say "they wronged" in the sense of "they disbelieved", because wrong is putting a thing in the wrong place (he had shown this earlier), and disbelief in God's signs is putting them out of place and turning them from the purpose for which they were meant. The editor refers to the earlier explanations of "sign", "chiefs" and "wrong". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 12}

On "so see how was the end of the corrupters", he says God is addressing His prophet: look with the eye of your heart at the end of those who corrupted the earth, meaning Pharaoh and his chiefs, who wronged God's signs that Moses brought them; their end was that all of them were drowned in the sea. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 12}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
