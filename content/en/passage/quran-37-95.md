## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 95 to 98 in a single segment. He explains the phrase "He struck them with his right hand": He leaned toward the gods of his people and struck them with an axe in his hand that would break them, as in: My father told me, he said: My uncle struck him, he said: My father told me, from his father, from Ibn Abbas who said: When he was free, he began to strike their gods with his right hand. I narrated from Al-Husayn, who said: I heard Abu Mu'adh say: I heard Al-Dahhak say something similar. And from Qatadah: "He finished beating them with the right hand," so he turned to them to break them. From Ibn Ishaq: Then he turned to them as Allah said, striking them with his right hand, then he began to break them with an axe in his hand. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, Al-Kisai, and Ibn Ishaq.
