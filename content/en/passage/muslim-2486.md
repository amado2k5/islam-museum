## Summary
A report of al-Bara' ibn Azib in the chapter on the virtues of Hassan ibn Thabit in Sahih Muslim: the Prophet permitted Hassan to satirise the polytheists with Gabriel's support. It is the poetry that defended the message, and among it is Hassan's pulpit in the mosque. {cite:sahih-muslim}
