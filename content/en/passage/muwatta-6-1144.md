## Summary
A report that Ruqayya, client of Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, tells: she went out with Amra to Mecca; she entered Mecca on the day of Tarwiya, circumambulated the House and ran between al-Safa and al-Marwa, then asked for scissors and cut from the hair of her head. When it was the day of sacrifice she slaughtered a sheep. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.519}
