## Summary
Malik transmits from Ayyub ibn Musa, from Mansur ibn Abd al-Rahman al-Hajabi, from his mother, from Aisha, that she was asked about a man who said: my property is in the lintel (ratij) of the Kaaba; she said: he expiates it as he expiates an oath. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.617}
