## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "It is not for any human to speak to Allah except by revelation or from behind a veil, or to send a messenger and reveal by His permission whatever He wills. Indeed, He is Wise." This means that no human should speak to his Lord except by revelation to which Allah reveals to him as He wills, or as inspiration, or else "or from behind a veil," meaning He speaks to him so that he hears his words but does not see him, just as Moses spoke to his prophet "or sends a messenger," saying: Or God sends a messenger from among His angels, either Gabriel or someone else, "and He reveals by His permission whatever He wills," meaning: that messenger reveals to the recipient by the permission of his Lord whatever He wills, meaning: whatever His Lord wills to reveal to him in order and prohibition, and other messages and revelations.
