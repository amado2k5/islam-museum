## Summary
Malik says the custom among us is that those who perform the mutual curse can never marry each other again. If the husband retracts his accusation, he is given the prescribed punishment, the child is assigned to him, and she never returns to him. This custom is not doubted and not disputed among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.79}
