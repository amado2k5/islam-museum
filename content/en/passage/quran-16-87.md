## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they were cast to Allah that Day peace, and what they used to invent has gone astray from them": And the polytheists threw peace to Allah that day, saying: They submitted that day and were humbled before His judgment among them, and their gods who used to invoke in this world besides Allah did not avail them, nor did their people, nor their tribes who were defending them in this world. And the Arabs say: I was thrown to Him like this, meaning that I said to Him. And His saying: "And what they used to invent has gone astray from them," means: And their gods erred from what they hoped for intercession with God for salvation. In the Maqta, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
