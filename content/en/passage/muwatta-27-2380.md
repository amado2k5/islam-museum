## Summary
Malik transmits from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih, from his father, from Abu Hurayra, that Saad ibn Ubada said to the Prophet: do you see, if I found a man with my wife, should I give him time until I bring four witnesses? The Prophet said yes. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.384}
