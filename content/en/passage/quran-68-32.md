## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 32 to 33 as one section. He interprets His saying, “Perhaps our Lord will give us something better than it. Indeed, we are turning to our Lord”: God Almighty says, informing us of what the owners of the garden said: “Perhaps our Lord will give us something better than it” by our repentance for the mistake of our previous action, something better than our garden. “Indeed, we are turning to our Lord” means: We are turning to our Lord in hope that He will give us something better than our garden, since it has perished. His statement, may He be exalted, “Such is the punishment,” means, may His praise be glorified, that just as We did to the garden of the owners of the garden, when it became like a barren wasteland due to the calamity and destructive affliction We sent upon it, so We did to those who disobeyed Our command and disbelieved in Our messengers in this world. “And the punishment of the Hereafter is greater,” meaning that the punishment in the Hereafter for those who disobeyed their Lord and disbelieved in Him is greater on the Day of Resurrection than the punishment and torment of this world. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
