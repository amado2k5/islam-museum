## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 25 to 26 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So We forgave him that, and indeed, he has with Us a pleasant and good destination": He means His mention by saying: "So We forgave him that," so We pardoned him and pardoned him from holding him accountable for his sin and fault, "And indeed, he has with Us a retreat," meaning: He is with Us for drawing near to Us on the Day of Resurrection. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ikrimah.
