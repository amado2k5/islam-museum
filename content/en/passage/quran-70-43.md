## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43-44 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "The Day they will emerge from the graves, hastening as though they were rushing to a monument": The phrase "The Day they will emerge" clarifies and explains the first day mentioned in the phrase "Their Day which they are promised." The interpretation is: until they meet their promised Day, the Day they will emerge from the graves (singular: grave), "hastening as though they were rushing to a monument." Similarly, Qatadah said regarding "The Day they will emerge from the graves, hastening": meaning from the graves, hastening. Ma'mar narrated the same from Qatadah. We have already explained the grave, along with its evidence and the opinions of scholars regarding it. His statement, "to a monument they hasten," means that they are as if they are racing towards a marker erected for them. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
