## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Say, if he had gods with him as they say, then they would seek a way to the Owner of the Throne": Say, O Muhammad, to those polytheists who have placed another god with Allah: If the matter were as you say: that He has gods, and that is not as you say, then those gods would seek closeness to Allah, the Owner of the Great Throne, seek the crown to Him, and seek the rank from Him. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "Say, if he had gods with him as they say, then they would seek a way to the Owner of the Throne," he says: If he had gods, then they would know his virtue, rank, and status over them, so they sought what would bring them closer to Him. From Muammar, from Qatadah: "If they sought a way to the Owner of the Throne," he said: They would seek closeness to Him, even though it is not as they say. In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah.
