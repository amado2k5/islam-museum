## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Except Iblis, he refused to be with those who prostrate," so he did not prostrate to Adam when all the angels prostrated, and he mentions that his pride, envy, and prostitution was borne by arrogance, envy, and prostitution. He explained in the previous verses that prostration was a prostration of greeting and honor, not a prostration of worship, and that Iblis is the one referred to as the "jinn" created from the fire of poison before man.
