## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 15 to 18 as a single section. He explains the phrase “Go to Pharaoh, for he has transgressed”: Moses was called by his Lord, “Go to Pharaoh.” The word “that” was omitted because the call was a statement, as if it were said to Moses, “His Lord said: Go to Pharaoh.” Regarding the phrase “for he has transgressed,” he says: He has become arrogant and exceeded all bounds in his aggression and pride towards his Lord. He then quotes interpretations from Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
