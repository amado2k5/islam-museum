## Summary
Verse 276 of [[passage/quran-2|Surat al-Baqara]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 276 of 286 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

([[passage/quran-2-276|2:276]]), pp. 15 to 16. Al-Tabari explains the blotting out of usury as God diminishing it and removing it; 6251, Ibn Abbas: blotting out is diminishing. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 15} He says this is like the report from Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud from the Prophet, 6252, that usury, however much, ends in little; the editor notes that al-Hakim relates it in the Mustadrak, that Ibn Kathir gives it from the Musnad and Ibn Majah and quotes al-Tabari's wording, and refers to al-Durr al-manthur. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 15} As for making charity increase, it means He doubles its reward and nurtures it and makes it grow for its giver; the editor corrects a word in manuscript and print and refers to the nearby explanation of usury and increase. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 15}

Al-Tabari raises a question: how does God increase charities? He answers that it is by multiplying the reward for the giver, as in the verse on seven ears of grain at ([[passage/quran-2-261|2:261]]) and the verse on the good loan at ([[passage/quran-2-245|2:245]]). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 16} In 6253, from Abu Hurayra from the Prophet: God accepts charity and takes it in His right hand and nurtures it for one of you as one of you raises his foal, until a morsel becomes like Uhud, and its confirmation is in God's Book, and he cited the verse at ([[passage/quran-9-104|9:104]]) and this clause of ([[passage/quran-2-276|2:276]]). {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 16} The editor gives the biography of Abbad ibn Mansur al-Naji al-Basri the judge, holding him reliable and those who spoke against him to have done so without proof, and of al-Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq as a reliable Follower and jurist; he says the report will come in the commentary on al-Tawba, that Ahmad relates it in the Musnad, and that there is an old error in the Musnad in the recitation of the first verse, which is in fact ([[passage/quran-9-104|9:104]]) and not ([[passage/quran-42-25|42:25]]), likewise in al-Tirmidhi and in Ibn Kathir's quotation, and he thinks the error older than the copyists, perhaps from Waki' or Abbad ibn Mansur. These sources and gradings are the editor's alone. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 16} We stop at 6253. The sayings are transmitted as they came, al-Tabari's explanation is his, the notes are the editor's, with no museum verdict.
