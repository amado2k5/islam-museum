## Summary
Malik transmits from Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir, from Rabia ibn Abdallah ibn al-Hudayr, that he told him he saw Umar ibn al-Khattab walk ahead of the people in front of the bier of Zaynab bint Jahsh. The editor notes that Abu Musab, Suwayd, al-Shafii in his Musnad and al-Shaybani transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.310}
