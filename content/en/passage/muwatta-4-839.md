## Summary
Malik transmits from Ibn Shihab that Abdullah ibn Abbas and Abu Hurayra disagreed about making up Ramadan: one of them said it may be spread out, the other said it may not be spread out, and Ibn Shihab does not know which of them said which. The editor notes that Abu Musab (818) and al-Shaybani (362) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.408}
