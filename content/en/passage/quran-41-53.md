## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "We will show them Our signs in the horizons and within themselves until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth. Is it not sufficient for your Lord that He is over all things Witness?" means: We will show these deniers, what We revealed to Muhammad Our servant of remembrance, Our signs in the horizons. Interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the verses that God promised to show these people. Some said: By the verses in the horizons, he meant the events of the Prophet in the vicinities of the polytheist land from Mecca and its surroundings, and by His saying: "and in their souls" refers to the conquest of Mecca. In the section, he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd and Sufyan.
