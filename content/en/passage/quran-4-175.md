**Commentary on verse 175: those who believe and hold fast to Him (p. 429)**

Al-Tabari says that 'those who believe in God and hold fast to Him' are those who affirm God and acknowledge His oneness and what He sent Muhammad with, from the communities, and who cling to the clear light He sent down to His Prophet; report 10863, from Ibn Jurayj: 'hold fast' means with the Qur'an. 'He will admit them into mercy and grace' means His mercy will reach them, saving them from His punishment and bringing them His reward and garden, and His grace will reach them as it reaches the believers in God and His messengers. 'He will guide them to Him on a straight path' means He will make them succeed in finding His grace, and set them firm to follow the way of those He has favoured among His obedient servants; that is the straight path, the religion God chose for His servants, which is Islam. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 429}
