## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We seized them with punishment, but they did not humble themselves before their Lord, nor do they humble": We seized these polytheists with Our punishment, sent down upon them Our strength, made their livelihoods difficult and distressed, barren their lands, and killed their secrets by the sword. "So they did not submit to their Lord," meaning: they did not submit to their Lord, so they submitted to His command and prohibition and turned to obedience to Him. "And they do not humble" means: nor humble themselves before Him. He mentioned that this verse was revealed to the Messenger of Allah when Allah took Quraysh with years of drought, and the Messenger of Allah prayed against them. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Al-Hasan.
