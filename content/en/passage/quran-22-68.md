## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 68 to 69 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And if they argue with you, say, 'Allah knows best what you do': And if these polytheists with Allah dispute with you, O Muhammad, regarding your rituals, say: Allah knows best what you do and what we do." As he said: Jahaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid: "And if they argue with you," he said: The saying of the polytheists: As for what Allah sacrifices with His oath. "So say, 'Allah knows best what you do'—we have our deeds and you have your deeds." And his saying: In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Jurayj.
