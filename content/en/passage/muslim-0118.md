## Summary
A report of Abu Hurayra in the chapter urging deeds before trials multiply, in the Book of Faith of Sahih Muslim. Trials come like dark patches of night, and a man sells his religion for worldly goods. It is one of the best-known warnings. {cite:sahih-muslim}
