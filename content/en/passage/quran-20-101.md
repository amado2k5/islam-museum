## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 101 to 103 in a single passage. He interprets the phrase "abiding therein forever; and evil will be for them on the Day of Resurrection as burdens." He narrated the report from those who refuse to mention it in this world that they will abide eternally in their burdens, meaning they will abide eternally in the Fire with their burdens. But since the intended meaning is clear, he suffices with what was not mentioned. And His saying: "And evil will be for them on the Day of Resurrection as a burden," God Almighty says: "And wretched is that burden and the burden of sin on the Day of Resurrection, and they have the right to be harmed by it, and he has mentioned them as a disaster with no escape." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
