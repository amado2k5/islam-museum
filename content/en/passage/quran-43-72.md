## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 72 to 73 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And that is the Paradise which you have inherited because of what you used to do": it is said to them: And this is the Paradise which Allah has given you as an inheritance from the people of the Fire whom He admitted to Hell because of what you used to do in this world, "for you in it," meaning you will have in Paradise many fruits of every kind, "from which you will eat," meaning: From the fruits you will eat whatever you desire.
