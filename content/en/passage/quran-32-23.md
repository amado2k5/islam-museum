## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 24 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave Moses the Scripture, so do not be in despair of meeting him, and We made it a guidance for the Children of Israel": And We certainly gave Moses the Torah, just as We gave you the criterion, O Muhammad. "So do not be in despair of meeting Him," meaning: Do not be in doubt about meeting Him. Qatadah used to say: The meaning of this is: Do not be in doubt that you met him, or that you met him on the night of his imprisonment. Thus, the report came from the Messenger of Allah. And from Qatadah from Abu Al-'Aliyah Al-Riyahi, who said: The Prophet of Allah said: "On the night Moses son of Imran was taken prisoner, I saw a tall, curly man Adam, as if he were one of the men of Shanua. I saw Jesus a man of broad character, reddish and pale, with a shaved head, and I saw Malik, the keeper of the Fire, and the Dajjal." In verses that God has bet on him, so do not be disappointed by meeting him that he saw Moses, and that Moses met him on the night he took the Isra. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
