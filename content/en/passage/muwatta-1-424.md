## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Abdullah ibn Umar disliked passing in front of women while they pray, in the chapter on the strictness against passing in front of one who prays. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.221}
