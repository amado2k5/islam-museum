## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Lot when he said to his people, 'Indeed, you will commit immorality that has preceded you to that of any of the worlds.'" He explains: And remember Lot when he said to his people: 'You will come to the remembrances that preceded you,' meaning the immorality they used to commit, which is the immorality of the two men "from one of the worlds." In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abi Nujayh.
