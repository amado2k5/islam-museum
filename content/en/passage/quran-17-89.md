## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We have certainly directed for mankind in this Qur'an every parable, but most of the people refused except those who disbelieved": He says: We have made clear to the people in this Qur'an every parable, as a proof against all of that, as a reminder to them, and a reminder of the truth so they may follow it and act upon it. "But most people refused except disbelievers," meaning: Most people refused except in denial of the truth and in denial of God's proofs and proofs.
