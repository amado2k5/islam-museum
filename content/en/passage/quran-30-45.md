## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "to reward those who believe and do righteous deeds out of His bounty; indeed, He does not like the disbelievers": "On that Day they will be attacking..." So that He may reward those who believe' in Allah and His Messenger "and do righteous deeds." He means: And they acted upon what Allah commanded them "out of His bounty," which He promised those who obey Him in this world will be rewarded on the Day of Resurrection. "Indeed, He does not like the disbelievers." Allah says: He has only specified His reward of His bounty to those who believe and do righteous deeds, not those who disbelieve in Allah. Indeed, He does not love the people of disbelief in Him. He resumed the report with the phrase "Indeed, He does not love the disbelievers," which contains the meaning I described.
