## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "When they entered upon him and said, 'Peace,' the guest said to Abraham 'Salaam,' 'He said, 'We are among you and a gentleman,' meaning we are among you fearful." He clarified the accusative aspect of 'salamma,' the reason for Abraham's reverence for his guest, the differences among those who differed, and the evidence for the correct view of the preceding case. It is noted that 'they said peace' is the plural of the predicate about the guest in the same wording, as 'al-daif' is a noun that falls on the singular and two, and the plural is like meter, diameter, and justice. Therefore, the plural of its predicate is one wording.
