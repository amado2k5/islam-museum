## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains that "The Prophet is more entitled to the believers than themselves, and his wives are their mothers, and those who are close relatives. Some of them are more entitled to each other in the Book of Allah than the believers and the emigrants, except that you do to your allies what is right, as it is recorded in the Book." The Prophet Muhammad is more entitled to the believers than themselves, to judge them as he wishes, and that is permissible for them. Also: Ibn Zayd said: "The Prophet is more deserving of the believers than themselves," just as You are more deserving of Your servant, whatever He decrees about them is permissible, just as every matter you judge against Your servant is permissible. And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid: "The Prophet is more deserving of the believers than themselves," he said: He is their father. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
