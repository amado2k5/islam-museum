## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 1-10 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "By the sky and the night-comer" as follows: Our Lord swears by the sky and by the night-comer who appears at night, from among the bright stars, and is hidden by day. Everything that comes at night is said to have "appeared" (or "comes at night"). In this section, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
