## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "He said, 'You have come to us to bring us out of our land by Your magic, O Moses': Pharaoh said when We showed all Our signs to Our Messenger Moses: You have come to us, O Moses, to drive us out of our homes and our homes with this magic that You brought us." "So let us bring you magic like it, so set between us and you a date." We will not go beyond it, to bring magic like the one you brought, and see which one prevails over its owner, not breaking that appointment. "Neither you nor we are the same," he says: In a place that is fair between you and us and a half. Reciters differed in their reading; the majority of the reciters of Hijaz and Basra, as well as some Kufans, read it as 'maqan sawa' with a kasra on the 's', while the general reciters of Kufa read it as 'maqan sawa' with a damma. Abu Ja'far said: The correct view on this matter among us is that they are two languages, meaning the kasra and damma on the 's' from 'sawwa' are well-known among the Arabs. In the maqta', he quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
