## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Dhu al-Nun, when he went angry and thought We would not be able to overcome him, so he called out in the darkness, 'There is no deity except You, exalted are You. Indeed, I was of the wrongdoers.'" And remember, O Muhammad, Dhu al-Nun, meaning the owner of the Nun. And the Nun: the fish. By Dhu al-Nun, he meant Yunus son of Matta, and we have mentioned his story in Surah Yunus, which is sufficient to mention it here, and His saying: "When he went angry," he means: when he went angry. The scholars of interpretation differed on the meaning of his departure as angry, to whom he was going, and to whom his anger was. Some said: His departure from his people and them was angered. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and Ibn Ishaq.
