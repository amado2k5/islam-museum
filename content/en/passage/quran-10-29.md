**Commentary on verse 29 (pp. 79-80)**

On "God suffices as witness between us and you, for we were unaware of your worship", al-Tabari says it reports what the partners of the polytheists, the gods and idols, will say to them on the Day of Resurrection after the polytheists say that it was they they worshipped: God is enough as witness between us and you, polytheists, for He knows that we did not know what you claim; and "we were unaware of your worship" means we were only unaware of your worshipping us instead of God, neither perceiving nor knowing it. The editor refers to the gloss on "unawareness". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 79}

Reports 17651, 17652 and 17653, from Mujahid: everything that is worshipped besides God says this. On the first report the editor notes that the printed text adds a word and that he keeps the manuscript reading. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 80}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
