**Commentary on verse 50 (pp. 289-290)**

Al-Tabari addresses the Prophet Muhammad: if joy befalls you through God's giving you the land of the Romans on this campaign, it will grieve Jadd ibn Qays, his like and their followers among the hypocrites; and if misfortune befalls you through the rout of your army in it, Jadd and his like say "we took our precaution before", that is, we took our care by staying behind from Muhammad and leaving his following to face his enemy, before this misfortune struck him, "and they turn away rejoicing", that is, they renounce Muhammad while rejoicing at what struck Muhammad and his Companions, the routing of his Companions, their flight from him and the killing of those who were killed. The editor explains the word for rout as the verbal noun of a verb meaning to be defeated, and refers to earlier explanations of befalling, the good and turning away. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 289}

Report 16792, from Ibn Abbas, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "if good befalls you it grieves them" means if good befalls you on your journey, this Tabuk campaign, it grieves them; he said, al-Jadd and his companions. Reports 16793 and 16794, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn Amr, Abu Asim, Isa and Ibn Abi Najih, and through Ibn Waki', Ibn Numayr and Warqa': "we took our precaution before" means our wariness. Report 16795, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: if there was a conquest for the Muslims, that weighed on them and grieved them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 290}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
