## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari did not dedicate an independent commentary to this verse on the preserved page; rather, its interpretation is included in the interpretation of verses 15–19. Al-Tabari mentions that the polytheists said to the Prophet, 'You have brought us nothing but clear magic,' and they denied resurrection after they had become dust and bones along with their early forefathers. God commanded him to say, 'Yes, and you are submissive,' for it is only one rebuke, and behold, they are looking on.
