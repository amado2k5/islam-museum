## Summary
Malik transmits from Alqama ibn Abi Alqama, from his mother, who said: Hafsa bint Abd al-Rahman entered upon Aisha, wife of the Prophet, while Hafsa wore a thin headscarf. Aisha tore it and clothed her in a thick headscarf. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.499}
