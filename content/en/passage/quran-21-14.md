## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 15 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They said, 'Woe to us! Indeed, we have been wrongdoers': Those upon whom Allah imposed His punishment upon them by their wrongdoing when Allah's wrath befell them, said: 'Woe to us! Indeed, we were wrongdoers by disbelieving in our Lord.' 'So that is their claim.' He means: Their call has not ceased, when Allah's wrath came upon them by wronging themselves: 'Woe to us! Indeed, we were wrongdoers' until Allah killed them, reaping them with the sword as He reaps crops and uprooting them with sickles. And His saying: 'dormant' means: perished, whose spark has been extinguished, and their movement has ceased, so they become dormant as fire extinguishes and then goes out. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
