**Commentary on verse 14 (pp. 160-161)**

Al-Tabari says: fight, believers, these polytheists who broke their oaths and covenants between you and them and drove God's messenger out from among them; "God will punish them by your hands" means He will kill them by your hands, "and disgrace them" means humble them by capture and subjugation, "and give you victory over them" by granting you triumph and mastery, "and heal the breasts of a believing people" by curing the sickness in the breasts of believers in God and His messenger through your killing and humbling and overcoming of these polytheists; that sickness was the resentment in their hearts at the harm and hurt the polytheists had inflicted on them. It was also said that God meant the breasts of Khuza'a, the Messenger's allies, because Quraysh broke the covenant by helping Bakr against them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 160}

Report 16540, from Mujahid, through Muhammad ibn al-Muthanna, Ibn Waki, Muhammad ibn Ja'far, Shu'ba and al-Hakam: Khuza'a. Report 16541, from al-Suddi, through Ibn Waki, Amr ibn Muhammad al-Anqazi and Asbat: Khuza'a, whose breasts He heals from Banu Bakr; report 16542 is similar. Reports 16543 to 16545, from Mujahid: Khuza'a, Muhammad's allies. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v14 p. 161}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
