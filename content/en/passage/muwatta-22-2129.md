## Summary
Malik transmits from Al-Ala ibn Abd al-Rahman, from Mabad ibn Kab al-Sulami, from his brother Abdullah ibn Kab ibn Malik al-Ansari, from Abu Umama, that the Messenger said: whoever takes the right of a Muslim man by his oath, God forbids him Paradise and makes the Fire his due. They said: even if it is a small thing, Messenger of God? He said: even if it is a twig of a palm (arak), three times. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.270}
