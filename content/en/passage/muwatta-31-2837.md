## Summary
Malik transmits from Ishaq ibn Abdallah ibn Abi Talha, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: I heard Umar ibn al-Khattab, and I went out with him until he entered an orchard; I heard him, while a wall was between us and he was inside the orchard, saying: Umar ibn al-Khattab, Commander of the Faithful, well done, well done! By God, son of al-Khattab, you will fear God, or He will punish you. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.591}
