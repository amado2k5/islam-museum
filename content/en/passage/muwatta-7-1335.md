## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said that Umar ibn al-Khattab used to carry forty thousand camels in a single year: one man to Syria on one camel, two men to Iraq on one camel. A man of Iraq came to him and said: carry me and Suhaym. Umar said: I adjure you by God, is Suhaym a water-skin? He said yes. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.597}
