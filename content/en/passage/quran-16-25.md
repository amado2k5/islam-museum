## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "that they will bear their burdens fully on the Day of Resurrection, and from the burdens of those who mislead them without knowledge. Indeed, evil is that which they bear." These polytheists say to those who ask them: "What has your Lord revealed?" Who revealed about what Muhammad claims to be the legends of the ancients, so that they may have their sins upon which they are abiding, from their denial of Allah and their disbelief in what was revealed to His Messenger, and from the sins of those who prevent them from believing in Allah. They mislead them and are tested among them without knowledge. And His saying: 'Evil is what they bear,' it means: 'Evil is the sin they commit and the heavy burden they bear.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, Zayd ibn Aslam, and Al-Rabi'.
