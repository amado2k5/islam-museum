## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 84 to 85 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Job, when he called to his Lord, 'Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.": And remember Job, O Muhammad, when he called upon his Lord while adversity and affliction touched him. "My Lord, indeed adversity touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful, so We responded to him." Allah says: So We answered Job's supplication when he called us, and we revealed what he was afflicted with in terms of hardship, affliction, and effort. The harm that befell him and the calamity that befell him were a test from God and a test. The reason for this was as follows: He said: Abd al-Samad ibn Mu'qil narrated to me, saying: I heard Wahb ibn Munabbih say: The beginning of the matter of Ayyub al-Siddiq, was that he was patient, excellent servant. In the Maqta, he quotes sayings from Wahb.
