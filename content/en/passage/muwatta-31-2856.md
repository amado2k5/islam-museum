## Summary
It reached Malik that the Messenger of God said: charity is not lawful for the family of Muhammad; it is only the filth of people. The editor notes Ibn Abd al-Barr's view that Malik narrated it connected, that Said ibn Dawud ibn Abi Zunbar and Juwayriya ibn Asma narrated it so from him, and that it is in Muslim's Sahih through a route from Juwayriya. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.600}
