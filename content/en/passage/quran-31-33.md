## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "O mankind, fear your Lord and fear a Day when no father will be deceived on behalf of his child, nor will any child be deceived on behalf of his father. Indeed, the promise of Allah is true, so let not the life of this world deceive you, nor let deceiving you against Allah be deceived at all." This means: O polytheists from Quraysh, fear Allah and fear that His wrath will befall you on a Day when no father will avail his child, nor will any newborn avail himself of his father, for the matter there will be in the hands of the One who cannot prevail, and for whom intercession and intercessions will not benefit except as a means of righteous deeds. which I mentioned in this world. And His saying: "Indeed, the promise of Allah is true" means: Know that the coming of this day is true, because Allah has promised His servants and there is no one who breaks His promise. "So let not the life of this world deceive you," meaning: Do not be deceived by the adornments and pleasures of worldly life, so you lean toward it and call upon preparation for what will save you from Allah's punishment that day. And His saying: "And let not deceiving you by Allah be deceived by deceive," it means: And no deceiver will deceive you by Allah. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Ata'.
