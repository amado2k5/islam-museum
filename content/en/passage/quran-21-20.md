## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 20 to 21 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "They glorify the night and the day without breaking their tongues": Those who are with him among the angels of their Lord glorify Him night and day, never break their glorification of Him. Also: Humayd informed us, from Ishaq ibn Abdullah ibn al-Harith, from his father, that Ibn Abbas asked Ka'b about the verse: "They glorify the night and the day without breaking the wall" and "They glorify the night and the day without getting bored," and he said: Does your glance lead you? Does your soul guide you? He said: No. He said: for they inspired glorification just as you inspired the gaze and the soul. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
