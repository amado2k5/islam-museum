## In al-Tabari's commentary
Al-Tabari reports from Ibn Abbas that "those who believe in what was sent down to you and what was sent down before you" are those who affirm the Prophet in what he brought from God and what the messengers before him brought, making no distinction among them and not denying what came to them from their Lord. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 244} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 245}
He gives al-Suddi's report that these are the believers among the People of the Book. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 245}
Al-Tabari reads Ibn Abbas's interpretation as stating that the surah from its start, though its first verses describe the believers, carries an implied censure of the unbelieving People of the Book, who claimed to affirm the earlier messengers while denying Muhammad and claiming to be rightly guided. The book is guidance only for those who believe in Muhammad and what he brought, and God then confirms the standing of the believers among the Arabs and the People of the Book with "those are upon guidance from their Lord, and those are the successful". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 246} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v01 p. 247}
