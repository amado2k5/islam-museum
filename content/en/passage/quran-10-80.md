**Commentary on verse 80 (p. 159)**

On [[passage/quran-10-80|10:80]] he says: 'when the magicians came' to Pharaoh, 'Moses said: throw what you are going to throw', of your ropes and staffs. A phrase is dropped in the speech and its mention left out, namely 'so they brought him the magicians, and when the magicians came', because the words 'when the magicians came' show it. Likewise after 'throw what you are going to throw' a phrase is dropped, 'so they threw their ropes and staffs, and when they had thrown, Moses said', because what is plainly said shows it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 159}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
