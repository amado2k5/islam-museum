## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 30 to 31 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "And the Messenger said, 'O Lord, indeed my people have taken this Qur'an as abandoned.'" The Prophet said on the day the oppressor bites his hand: 'O Lord, indeed my people to whom You sent me to call to Your oneness have taken this Qur'an abandoned.' Interpreters differed on the meaning of their adoption of the Qur'an as abandoned; some said: Their adoption was abandonment, their claim was bad speech and their claim that it was magic and poetry. In the verse, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibrahim.
