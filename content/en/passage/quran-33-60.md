## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 60 to 61 in a single passage. He explains His saying: "If the hypocrites and those in whose hearts is disease and the trembling in the city do not desist, We will tempt you with them, then they will not come near you there except a little": If the hypocrites, who hide disbelief and display faith, do not cease, "and those in whose hearts is a disease," meaning: suspicion from the desire for adultery and the love of immorality. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
