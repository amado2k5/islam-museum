## Summary
Verse 38 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 38 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "He said: enter among nations that passed before you, of jinn and men, into the Fire; every time a nation enters, it curses its sister, until, when they have all overtaken one another in it", he says: this is a report from God of what He said on the Day of Resurrection to those who forged lies about Him and denied His signs when they came before Him: when they came to Him on the Day of Resurrection He said to them, enter, you who forged against your Lord and denied His messengers, among groups of your like, "that passed before you", meaning went before you, "of jinn and men, into the Fire"; the meaning is: enter among nations that are in the Fire, that passed before you of jinn and men; and by "nations" are meant the parties and the people of disbelieving creeds; "every time a nation enters, it curses its sister" means every time a group of the people of a creed enters the Fire, it curses its sister, that is, abuses the other group of its creed, disowning it. By "sister" is meant brotherhood in religion and creed, and it is said "its sister" and not "its brother" because he meant a nation and another group, as if it said: every time a nation enters it curses another nation of its creed and religion. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 416}

He gives (14592) from al-Suddi that it means whenever the people of a creed enter, they curse their fellows in that religion: idolaters curse idolaters, Jews curse Jews, Christians Christians, Sabians Sabians and Magians Magians, the later cursing the earlier. As for "until when they have all overtaken one another in it", he says until the nations in the Fire meet one another all together, meaning gather in it; one says "they met" and "they overtook" when they gathered, and the note refers to Abu Ubayda's Majaz al-Qur'an; he says the first of the disbelieving creeds and the last of them gathered in it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 417}

Then, on "the last of them will say to the first: our Lord, these led us astray, so give them double punishment of the Fire. He will say: for each is double, but you do not know", he says: this is a report from God of the dialogue between the parties of the disbelieving creeds in the Fire on the Day of Resurrection; when the people of the disbelieving creeds gather in the Fire and overtake one another, the last of the people of each creed that entered the Fire, those who came in the world after an earlier one of them that preceded and was a forerunner and leader in misguidance and disbelief, say of the first of them who were before them in the world: our Lord, these led us astray from Your way, called us to worship another than You and made obedience to Satan attractive to us, so give them today double of Your punishment over ours. He gives (14593) from al-Suddi that "the last of them" are those who lived at the end of time and "the first" are those who laid down that religion for them. As for "He will say: for each is double, but you do not know", it is God's report of His answer to them: God says to those who call on Him saying "our Lord, these led us astray, so give them double punishment of the Fire": for all of you, first and last, followers and followed, double, that is, punishment repeated on it; "the double of a thing" is its like once more. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 418}

Mujahid used to say of this, and (14594) and (14595) are given from him, that "double" means doubled; (14596) from al-Suddi that for the first is a double and for the later is a double; (14597) from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud that "double of the Fire" is vipers; and (14598) from him that it is snakes and vipers. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 418}
