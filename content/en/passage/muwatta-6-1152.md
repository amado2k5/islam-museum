## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr, that he used to say: know that all of Arafat is a place of standing except the valley of Urana, and that all of Muzdalifa is a place of standing except the valley of Muhassir. The editor notes this is as stated. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.522}
