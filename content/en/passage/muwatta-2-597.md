## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, as a mursal report, that the Prophet was shrouded in three white Suhuli garments. The editor notes that this report is missing from one copy but appears in all the others and in al-Tamhid (24/90), and that Abu Musab transmitted it from Malik; it was given earlier as a connected report from Aisha. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.308}
