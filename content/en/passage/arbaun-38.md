## Summary
Qudsi hadith from Abu Hurayra: God says, "Whoever shows enmity to a friend of Mine, I declare war on him... and My servant draws near to Me by nothing more beloved to Me than what I have made obligatory; and he keeps drawing near to Me with supererogatory acts until I love him". Ibn Rajab notes that the chain is one of the rare reports in Sahih al-Bukhari. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
