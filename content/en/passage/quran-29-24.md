## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So the only response of his people was to say, 'Kill him or burn him.' But Allah saved him from the Fire. Indeed, in that are signs for a people who believe." The answer of Abraham's people was not when he said to them: Worship Allah and fear Him; that is better for you if you only knew. Except that some of them said to one another: Kill him or burn him with fire, and they did. They wanted to burn him with fire, so they set him on fire and threw him into it. Allah saved him from it and did not impose it on him, but rather made it a coolness and peace for him. Also: And from Qatadah, regarding His saying: The response of the people of Abraham, "except that they said, 'Kill him or burn him,' and Allah saved him from the Fire," he said: Ka'b said: Only his bonds were burned. "Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe." Allah says: Indeed, in Our salvation for Abraham from the Fire, he was cast into it while it was thundering, and it was made for him as coolness and peace, as proofs and proofs for a people who believe in proofs and proofs when they see and see.
