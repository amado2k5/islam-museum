## Summary
Malik says: flour for wheat, measure for measure, is no harm, because the flour is pure and he sold it for wheat measure for measure. If he made half a mudd of flour and half wheat, and sold that for a mudd of wheat, it is like what we described; it is not proper, because he only wanted to take the excess of his good wheat, when he put the flour with it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.176}
