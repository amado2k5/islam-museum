## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 36 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, these are the ones who say": Allah Almighty says, informing about the saying of the polytheists of Quraysh to the Prophet of Allah: 'These polytheists are from your people, O Muhammad, to say, 'It is only our first death,' which we die, which is the first death, 'and we are not scattered' after our death, nor will they be sent as a denial of resurrection, reward, and punishment.
