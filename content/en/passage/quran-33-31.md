## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And whoever among you obeys Allah and His Messenger and does righteousness, We will give her her reward twice, and We will prepare for her a generous provision." He explains: And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger among you and acts upon what Allah has commanded, We will give her her reward twice. He means: Allah will give her the reward of her deeds, similar to the reward of the deeds of other women among mankind. "And We have prepared for her a generous provision," meaning: and We have prepared for her in the Hereafter a pleasant life in Paradise. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
