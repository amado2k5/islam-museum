## Summary
Malik says that the matter among them is that the takbir on the days of tashriq is said after the prayers: its beginning is the imam's takbir with the people after the noon prayer of the day of sacrifice, and its end is the imam's takbir with the people after the dawn prayer of the last day of tashriq; then the takbir stops. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.540}
