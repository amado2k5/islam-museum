## Summary
Verse 2 of [[passage/quran-6|Surat al-An'am]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 2 of 165 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

By "he it is who created you from clay" al-Tabari means that God who created the heavens and the earth, darkened their night and lit their day, and was then disbelieved by the unbelievers despite his favour to them, who held equal to him what neither helps nor harms them, is the one who created you, people, from clay; he means that people are the children of him whom he created from clay, so he put it in the form of an address to them since they are his offspring. The interpreters said the like: report 13049, from Qatada: the beginning of creation, God created Adam from clay. 13050, from Mujahid: it is Adam; 13051, from al-Suddi: Adam. 13052, from al-Dahhak: Adam was created from clay and people from a drop of despised water. 13053, from Ibn Zayd: Adam was created from clay, then we were created from Adam when we were taken from his back. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 255}

The editor says the printed text has "so he disbelieved in him" and that in the manuscript "then" is joined to the word "disbelieved" as some copyists do, so he gave the wording the sense requires.

Then "then he decreed a term, and a fixed term with him": the interpreters differed on this. Some said "then he decreed a term" means he decreed a term for you, people, from your creation until you die, "and a fixed term with him" from your death until you are raised. Report 13054, from al-Hasan, says so; 13055, from Qatada: the term of your life until you die and the term of your death until you are raised, so you are between two terms from God. Report 13056, from al-Dahhak: he decreed the term of death, and every soul's term is death; he said, God will not put back a soul... {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v11 p. 256}

The editor identifies Waki' ibn al-Jarrah al-Ru'asi and his father, and Abu Bakr al-Hudhali, whose name is disputed and who is weak.

We stop here at the foot of p. 256, within report 13056 from al-Dahhak on "then he decreed a term, and a fixed term with him", at "God will not put back a soul", which continues on p. 257.

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
