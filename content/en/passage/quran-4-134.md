**Commentary on verse 134: those who want the reward of this world (pp. 299-301)**

Al-Tabari says the verse means those among the hypocrites who show faith in Muhammad while concealing unbelief, and who by their deeds want the reward and profit of this world by showing what they show of faith with their tongues. Their reward in this world is what they gain of spoils when they attend an engagement with the Prophet, and their safety for themselves, their offspring and their property; their reward in the Hereafter is the fire of hell. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 300}

So the sense is that whoever among the working hypocrites wants by his deeds the reward and recompense of this world, God will repay him in this world from this world, and in the Hereafter with punishment and torment, for He is able to do all this and owns it all. Al-Tabari cites two verses of sura Hud on those who want the life of this world and its adornment. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 300}

Al-Tabari says these words came down about those who pursued the matter of the Banu Ubayriq, and about those described in the verse forbidding argument on behalf of those who betray themselves, and about those like them in their deeds and hypocrisy. On the words that God hears and sees: He hears what these hypocrites say to the believers when they meet them, and sees what they conceal of deceit and rancour in their hearts. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 300}
