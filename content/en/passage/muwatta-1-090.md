## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Yazid ibn Abdullah ibn Qusayt al-Laythi, in the chapter on nosebleeds: Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab had a nosebleed while he was praying, went to the room of Umm Salama, the Prophet's wife, was brought water, performed ablution, then came back and built on what he had prayed. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.80}
