## Summary
A ruling of Malik in the chapter on how to perform dry ablution: he was asked how dry ablution is performed and how far it reaches. He said: one strike for the face and one strike for the hands, wiping them up to the elbows. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Muhammad ibn al-Hasan transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.101}
