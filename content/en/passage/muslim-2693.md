## Summary
A report of Abu Ayyub al-Ansari through Amr ibn Maymun in the Book of Remembrance and Supplication of Sahih Muslim: the merit of the tahlil said ten times is like freeing four slaves from the descendants of Ismail. {cite:sahih-muslim}
