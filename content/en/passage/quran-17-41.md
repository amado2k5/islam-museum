## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And indeed, We have directed in this Qur'an so that they may remember, and it does not increase them except in aversion": "And indeed, in this Qur'an, these polytheists who fabricate against Allah have lessons, verses, and proofs. We have set forth examples for them therein, warned them, and warned them 'so that they may remember.' He means: so that they may remember those proofs against them, so they may understand the error of what they are standing on, and take heed from the lessons, take heed from them, and lament their ignorance. They do not heed them, nor do they remember what is brought to them of verses and warnings. What increases is our reminder of them 'except as a repulsion.' He says: Except to go away from the truth, to distance from it, and to flee. The word 'aversion' here is derived from their saying: 'So-and-so repels this matter, and he is repelled by him both by rejection and aversion.'
