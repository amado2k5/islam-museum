## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "Indeed, We are sent down upon the people of this town as a punishment from the heaven because of what they used to transgress." He says, exalted be He, informing about the messengers saying to Lot, "Indeed, We are sent down" to the people of this town, Sodom, "as a punishment from the sky." Also: from Qatadah, "Indeed, We are sent down upon the people of this town as a punishment." We have explained the meaning of rajaz and the opinions of the interpreters in the past, which is sufficient to revisit it here. And His saying: "because they used to commit sin" means what they used to do as disobedience to Allah and ride immorality.
