## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 78 to 79 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Have you seen the one who disbelieved in Our signs and said to you [two] wealth and children": "Have you seen," O Muhammad, "the one who disbelieved in Our verses" as proofs and did not believe in them, denied Our warning from the disbelievers, and said, "And he is a disbeliever by God and by His Messenger, you will be given" in the Hereafter "wealth and children." He mentioned that these verses were revealed about Al-As ibn Wa'il Al-Sahmi, Abu Amr ibn Al-As. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, and Al-A'mash.
