## Summary
Malik says he heard Ibn Shihab say that a woman given a final divorce does not leave her house until her waiting period ends, and she has no maintenance, unless she is pregnant, in which case he maintains her until she gives birth. Malik said this is the practice among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.95}
