**Commentary on verse 15 (pp. 435-436)**

Al-Tabari says, on "you who believe, when you meet the unbelievers marching, do not turn your backs to them; whoever turns his back to them that day, except turning aside to fight or joining another company, has drawn on himself God's anger, and his refuge is Gehenna, a wretched end" [[passage/quran-8-15|8:15]] and [[passage/quran-8-16|8:16]]: you who trusted God and His Messenger, "when you meet the unbelievers" in fighting "marching" meaning advancing on one another, "tazahuf" being drawing near; "do not turn your backs to them" meaning do not give them your backs and be routed from them, but stand firm against them, for God is with you against them; "whoever turns his back to them that day" meaning whoever of you turns his back to them, "except turning aside to fight" meaning except drawing the enemy on to fight, seeking an opening he can strike and then charging back; "or joining another company" meaning except turning his back to join a company, going to the side of the believers who rally him with them to fight and return with him to the enemy. The editor notes that this explanation of "tazahuf" is not found in the dictionaries and should be recorded, and refers to the earlier explanations of "turning", "back" and "company". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 435}

The interpreters said the like, and report 15795, from al-Dahhak: "except turning aside to fight or joining another company", he said the one turning aside is one who goes ahead of his companions to see an opening in the enemy and strike it, and the one joining is one who flees to the Prophet and his companions, and so too one who flees today to his commander and his companions; al-Dahhak said this is a warning from God to the companions of Muhammad not to flee, the Prophet and his companions being their company. Report 15796, from al-Suddi: the one turning aside is one drawing the enemy on who means to return, and the one joining is one who joins the imam and his troops if he charges and has not the strength against them, and people are not excused, though many, for turning from the imam. The people of knowledge differed over the ruling of this verse, whether it is specific to the people of Badr or applies to the believers as a whole. Some said it is for the people of Badr specifically, since they were not to leave the Messenger of God with his enemy and flee from him, whereas today they may withdraw. Report 15797, from Abu Nadra: that was the day of Badr and they had nowhere to withdraw to, and had anyone withdrawn he would have withdrawn only to…, Abu Musa saying, meaning to the polytheists. Report 15798, from Dawud… {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 435} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v13 p. 436}
