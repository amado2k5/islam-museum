**Commentary on verse 85: "God rewarded them for what they said with gardens under which rivers flow", p. 512**

Al-Tabari says: God repaid them for saying "our Lord, we believe, so write us with the witnesses" and "why should we not believe in God" with gardens, orchards under whose trees rivers flow, in which they abide, neither leaving nor being moved from them; this is the reward of every doer of good in word and deed. A doer of good here is one who affirms God's oneness purely with no association, acknowledges God's prophets and the books they brought, performs His obligations and avoids His disobediences; this is the perfection of the doers of good. The editor fears a gap in the wording at the end and left it as it stands in the manuscript.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 512}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
