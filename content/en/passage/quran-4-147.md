**Commentary on verse 147: what God does with your punishment if you give thanks (p. 342)**

Al-Tabari says the sense is: what does God do with your punishment, hypocrites, if you repent to Him and return to the truth, thanking Him for His favours by turning to His oneness, holding fast to Him, making your deeds sincere for His face and leaving off show, and believing and affirming His Messenger? God has no need to place you in the lowest rank of the fire, for He gains no benefit from your punishment and wards off no harm from Himself by it; His penalty on those among His creatures who deserve it is recompense for their boldness towards their Lord, their opposition to His command and prohibition, and their ingratitude for His favours. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 342}

Al-Tabari says that when God thanks His servants for their obedience and gives them reward for it, He is thankful to them, and that He knows all you do, good and evil, and counts it all, until He repays you on the Day of Resurrection. Report 10748, from Qatada, says God does not punish the thankful nor the believer. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 343}
