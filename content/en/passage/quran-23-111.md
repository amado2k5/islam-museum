## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 111 to 112 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So you took them as a mockery until I made you forget My remembrance, and you were among them laughing": So you took those who say to their Lord, "Our Lord, our misery has overcome us and we were a misguided people" in this world, those who say in it "Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us and have mercy on us, and You are the best of the merciful" as mockery. The letter 'h' and 'm' in His saying: 'So you took them' is from the mention of the group. Readers differed in the reading of His phrase: 'sukriya' (mocking). Some reciters from Hijaz and some from Basra and Kufa read it as: 'So you took them as sarcastical' with a kasra on the 's', and they interpret the kasra as meaning mockery, and they say: when it is made with a damma, the meaning of the complement is enslavement and enslavement. The meaning of the speech is according to their view: the people of faith took me in this world as mockery and play, mocking them until I made you forget my remembrance. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Ibn Zayd.
