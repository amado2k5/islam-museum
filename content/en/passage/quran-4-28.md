## Summary
Verse 28 of [[passage/quran-4|Surat an-Nisa]], a Medinan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 28 of 176 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Lightening, and the weakness of man (pp. 215-216, reports 9135-9139).** Abu Jafar says the meaning is that God wills to make it easy for them by permitting them to marry believing young women when they cannot afford a free woman, and that human beings are weak by nature, unable to give up intercourse with women and short of patience in it, so God permitted them to marry their believing young women when they fear hardship for themselves and cannot afford a free woman, so that they do not fornicate for lack of patience. He adds that the people of interpretation said much the same. The editor refers to the earlier explanation of the word for lightening in volume 6. Muhammad b. Amr (9135, from Abu Asim from Isa from Ibn Abi Najih) reports from Mujahid that the ease is in marrying a slave woman and in everything that has ease in it; Ibn Bashshar (9136, from Abu Ahmad al-Zubayri from Sufyan from Ibn Tawus) reports from his father that the weakness is in the matter of intercourse; Ibn Bashshar again (9137, from Abu Asim from Sufyan from Ibn Tawus) reports from his father that it is in the matter of women; al-Hasan b. Yahya (9138, from Abd al-Razzaq from Ma'mar from Ibn Tawus) reports from his father that it is in the affairs of women, and that a man is weakest in nothing as he is in respect of women; Yunus (9139, from Ibn Wahb) reports from Ibn Zayd that God gave a concession to marry these slave women when they were compelled to turn to them, and that without the concession there would have been only the first rule for one who finds no free woman.
These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v08 p. 216}
