## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And I was surrounded by its fruit, so he began to turn his hands over what he spent on it, while it was empty on its thrones, and he said, 'I wish I had not associated anyone with my Lord.'" He enveloped his fruits and calamities, which are the kinds of fruits of his Paradise to which he used to say: "I do not think these will ever perish." Thus, this disbeliever, the owner of these two Paradises, turned his hands back to stomach, eagerly and regretful for the loss of his expenses spent in his Paradise, which is empty on its thrones, saying: It is empty of its plants and houses. In the passage, he quotes Qatadah.
