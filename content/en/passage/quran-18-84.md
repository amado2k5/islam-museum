## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 84 to 86 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And they ask you about Dhul-Qarnayn." Say, 'Shall I recite to you a report from him?' And these polytheists ask you, O Muhammad, about Dhul-Qarnayn what his status was and what his story was. Say to them: I will recite to you a remembrance of his news saying: I will recount to you a report from him. It has been said: Those who asked the Messenger of Allah about the matter of Dhul-Qarnayn were people from the People of the Book. As for the report that those who asked him about it were polytheists of his people, we mentioned it before. As for the report that those who asked him, they were people from the People of the Book.
