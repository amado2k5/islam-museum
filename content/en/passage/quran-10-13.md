**Commentary on verse 13 (pp. 37-38)**

He then explains "We destroyed generations before you when they did wrong, and their messengers had come to them with clear proofs, but they would not believe; thus We repay the guilty". We destroyed the nations that called God's messengers liars before you, polytheists, "when they did wrong", that is when they associated others with God and went against His command and prohibition, and "their messengers came to them" from God "with clear proofs", the signs and arguments that show the truth of whoever brings them; "they would not believe" means these nations did not believe their messengers or trust them in what they called them to, God's oneness and sincere worship of Him. The editor refers to the glosses on "generations", "wrongdoing" and "clear proofs". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 37}

On "thus We repay the guilty": as We destroyed these generations before you, polytheists, for wronging themselves, denying their messengers and rejecting their counsel, so I will do with you, destroying you as I destroyed them for your denying your messenger Muhammad and wronging yourselves by associating others with your Lord, if you do not turn back and repent to God from your polytheism; for part of the repayment of the unbeliever for his disbelief with Me is that I destroy him in My wrath in this world and bring him to the Fire in the next. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 38}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
