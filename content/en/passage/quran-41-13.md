## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 13 to 14 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "But if they turn away, say, I have warned you of a thunderbolt like the thunderbolt of 'Ad and Thamud": If these polytheists turn away from this proof that I have made clear to them, O Muhammad, and I warn them about it, they do not believe in it and do not acknowledge that the doer is Allah, with whom there is no deity, then say to them: I have warned you, O people, of a thunderbolt that will destroy you like the thunderbolt of 'Ad and Thamud. We have previously explained that the meaning of thunderbolt is anything that corrupts something or something beyond its form. It is said here that it means a punishment from God and punishment. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
