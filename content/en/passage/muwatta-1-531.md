## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Busr ibn Sa'id reported that the Prophet said to women: whoever of you attends the night prayer, let her not touch perfume. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, that it is connected in Ahmad, Muslim, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Khuzayma through Busr ibn Sa'id from Zaynab, the wife of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, and that Muslim transmitted from Busr from Abu Hurayra the prohibition on a woman who has perfumed herself attending the night prayer. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.274}
