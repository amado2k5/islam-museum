## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 40 to 41 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, 'Stand up, act according to your position; indeed, I am a worker, and you will come to know.": Say, O Muhammad, to the polytheists of your people who have taken idols and idols as gods they worship besides Allah. So work, O people, to enable you to perform the deeds you do and your stations, as Ibn Abi Nujayh narrated from Mujahid, regarding His saying: "In your position," he said: "On your side, I am acting," likewise, on the deeds of those who came before me among the prophets before me, "so you will know" when the wrath of Allah comes to you, whether the right among us is the one who falsifies it, and the wise among the misguided. And His saying: "Whoever is to be punished," the Exalted says: Whoever is subjected to a punishment that disgraces him, what has come to him of that punishment means: it humiliates and degrades him, and "and a lasting punishment will befall him," meaning: a lasting punishment will descend upon him, which will never leave him. In the Muqta', he quotes sayings from Mujahid.
