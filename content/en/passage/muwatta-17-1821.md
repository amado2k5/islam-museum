## Summary
Malik transmits that Umm Amra bint Abd al-Rahman, the mother of Abu al-Rijal Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn Harithah, used to sell her fruit and keep back some of it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.145}
