## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And they said, 'We believe in him, and how can they fight from a distant place": These polytheists said when they witnessed the punishment of Allah, they believed in it, meaning: we believed in Allah, His Book, and His Messenger. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
