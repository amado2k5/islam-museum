## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Ibn Shihab, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Aisha, that Abu Bakr al-Siddiq had given her twenty wasqs of the palms of his property at al-Ghaba. When death was near him, he said: by God, daughter, no one among people is dearer to me to be rich after me than you, and none is dearer to me to be poor after me than you. I had given you twenty wasqs of palms; had you cut and gathered them, they would have been yours. Today it is inherited property; the two of them are your brothers and your sisters, so divide it according to God's book. Aisha said: I said, father, by God, if it were such and such I would have left it; but there are only Asma, so who is the other? Abu Bakr said: the one in the belly of Kharija's daughter; I think she is a girl. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.298}
