## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And those who disbelieved will remain in doubt until the Hour comes to them suddenly or the punishment of a barren day comes to them": And those who disbelieve in Allah will remain in doubt. Then the interpreters differed regarding the 'ha' in the phrase "from him" who mentioned what it is? Some said: It refers to the Prophet's saying: "Those are the highest mountains, and their intercession is hoped." In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Zayd.
