## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that the Messenger said: no Muslim man who has something to bequeath should spend two nights without his bequest written and kept with him. The editor notes that there is no disagreement from Malik on the wording or the chain of this report. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.309}
