## Summary
Malik transmits from Yahya ibn Said, from Sulayman ibn Yasar, from Abu Waqid al-Laythi, that a man came to Umar in Syria and said that he found a man with his wife. Umar sent Abu Waqid al-Laythi to the woman to ask her. He told her what her husband said, informing her that she should not be held to his word, and kept suggesting similar things to her so that she would retract. She refused to retract and held to her confession, so Umar ordered her stoned. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.385}
