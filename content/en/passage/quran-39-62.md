## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 61 to 62 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is over all things Disposer of all things": Allah, to whom is the divinity from all His creation, to whom worship is not worthy except Him, the Creator of everything, not that which cannot create anything, and He is over all things the Guardian, meaning: and He is over all things, sustaining protection and protection.
