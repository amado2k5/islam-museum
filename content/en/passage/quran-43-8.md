## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So We destroyed them more brutally than them, and they passed away like the former ones": So We destroyed them more fiercer than those who mocked their prophets when they oppressed us, but they were unable to restrain us with their strength and severity, nor could they refrain from Our might when it came to them. Those who are weaker than them are more powerful than those who restrain us when it befalls them. "And the example of the ancients passed by." He, Glorified and Exalted, says: "And to these polytheists who mock You and those before them of their strikes has passed like Us, which We have likened to them among the deniers of Our messengers whom We destroyed." He says: Let those who mock you, O Muhammad, expect from our punishment the same as we have dealt with those who have established your denial. The passage quotes Mujahid and Qatadah.
