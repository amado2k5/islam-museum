**Commentary on verse 3 (p. 229)**

On [[passage/quran-11-3|11:3]] al-Tabari says: then its verses were set out so that you worship none but God and so that you ask your Lord's forgiveness. By 'ask your Lord's forgiveness' he means do, people, the deeds that please your Lord with you, so that He covers your grave sins which you committed by worshipping idols and images and joining gods and rivals with Him in worship. 'Then turn to Him in repentance' means then return to your Lord with pure worship of Him alone, apart from all else you worship besides Him, after you cast off rivals and renounce worshipping them. That is why it says 'then turn' and not 'and turn': repentance is returning to the work of obeying God, while asking forgiveness is from the polytheism they were persisting in; work for God is not work for Him until polytheism is given up, since the work of polytheism is only for Satan, and the polytheists thought they obeyed God in many of their deeds while persisting in their polytheism. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'asking forgiveness' and 'repentance'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 229}
