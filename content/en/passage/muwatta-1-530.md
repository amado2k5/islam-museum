## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on women going out to the mosques. A report from Malik that he was told Abdullah ibn Umar related that the Prophet forbade women to be prevented from God's mosques. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab and Suwayd transmitted it, and that it is connected and raised in the Sahihayn from Ibn Umar (al-Bukhari 2/7, Muslim 2/32). {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.273} {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.274}
