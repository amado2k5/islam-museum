## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 44 to 45 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "So speak to him gently, that perhaps he will remember or fear": God Almighty says to Moses and Aaron: "So speak to Pharaoh gently." He mentioned that the gentle speech that God commanded them to say to him is to use it as a metanomial. And from Al-Suddi: "So speak to him with a gentle word," he said: His epithet. And the phrase "perhaps he remembers or fears" differs regarding the meaning of his phrase "perhaps" in this context. Some say its meaning here is interrogative, as if they directed the meaning of the speech to: Speak to him gently, so observe whether he remembers and reconsiders or fears God to be restrained from His tyranny. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
