## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zinad, from al-A'raj, from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: God guarantees for whoever strives in His path, and who is brought out of his house only by striving in His path and belief in His words, that He will admit him to Paradise, or return him to the dwelling he left, with what reward or booty he gained. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.571}
