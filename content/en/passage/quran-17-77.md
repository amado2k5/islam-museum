## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "The way of those We sent before you of Our messengers, and you will not find any change in Our way": If they expelled you, they would not have remained disagreeing with you for a short time, and We would have destroyed them with a punishment from Us. Our way is with those We sent before you among Our messengers. This is how we used to do to nations when their messengers were expelled from among their backs, and the Sunnah was established as deviation from the meaning of the phrase "They will not remain dissent from you except for a little," because the meaning is: We would soon punish them like Our way among the nations of those We sent before you from Our messengers, and you will not find any change in our Sunnah from what it has been done. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "The way of those We sent before you of Our messengers, and you will not find any change in Our way": meaning the way of nations and messengers before you was like this, if they denied their messengers and expelled them, they did not dispute that Allah sent down His punishment upon them. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
