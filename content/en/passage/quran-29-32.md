## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the verse: "He said, 'Indeed, in it is Lot.' They said, 'We know best who is in it; We will save him and his family, except his wife was among the abject.'" Abraham said to the messengers from the angels, when they said to him: "Indeed, we have destroyed the people of this town. Indeed, its people were wrongdoers." They did not exclude anyone from them, as they described them as wrongdoers: Lot is in it, and he is not among the wrongdoers, but he is one of God's messengers, the people of faith in him, and those who obey him. The messengers said to him: "We know better what is in it" than the oppressors who disbelieve in God than you. Lot is not one of them, rather, as I said, he is among the friends of God, so that We may save him and his family from the destruction that befalls the people of his town, "except his wife was among the distant," whom ages and days have prolonged, and whose lives and ages have prolonged, and she is perishing among the people of Lot along with her people. In the passage, he quotes from Ibrahim.
