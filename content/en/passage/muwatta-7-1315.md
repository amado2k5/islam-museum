## Summary
Malik was asked whether the extra share can be given from the first booty; he said: that is by the imam's judgment; there is no known settled command among them on that except the sultan's judgment. It did not reach him that the Messenger of God gave extra shares in all his raids, though it reached him that he did so in some of them, at Hunayn. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.587}
