## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Abd Rabbihi ibn Sa'id that Abu Bakr ibn Abd al-Rahman ibn al-Harith ibn Hisham used to assign a share only to the two grandmothers. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.16}
