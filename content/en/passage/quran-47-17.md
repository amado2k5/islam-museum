## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 17 to 18 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And those who are guided, He increased them guidance and gave them their piety": As for those whom Allah has granted success in following the truth and whose hearts have opened their hearts to believe in him and His Messenger among those who listened to you, O Muhammad, what you recited to them and heard from you "increased their guidance," he says: God increased them in faith and clarified the reality of what you brought them from God to the clarification they had. It is mentioned that what the Messenger of Allah recited to them from the Qur'an, and the hypocrites among them said to the people of faith, 'What did he say earlier? May Allah increase the guidance of the people of guidance from them.' Some of what Allah revealed from the Qur'an abrogated some of what had previously been ruled upon. In the passage, he quotes statements from Ibn Abbas.
