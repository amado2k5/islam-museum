## Summary
Yahya said: Malik said: Hisham ibn Urwa said, Urwa ibn al-Zubayr said: I have not seen kissing for the fasting person lead to any good. The editor notes that Abu Musab and Suwayd transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.394}
