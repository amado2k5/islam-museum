## Summary
Malik transmits from Yunus ibn Yusuf, from Ata ibn Yasar, from Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, that he found boys who had driven a fox into a corner, and he chased them off. Malik said: I do not know except that he said: is this done in the sanctuary of the Messenger of God? {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.468}
