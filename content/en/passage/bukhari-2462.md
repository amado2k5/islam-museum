## Summary
In the Book of the Virtues of the Companions: the long hadith of Umar known as the account of the Saqifa, which Ibn Abbas relates from him during Umar's last illness on the pulpit. It covers the gathering of the Ansar with Sa'd ibn Ubada in the Saqifa, the visit of Abu Bakr, Umar and Abu Ubayda, the speeches "we are not the rulers, you are the ministers", the speech of al-Hubab ibn al-Mundhir, Umar's taking of Abu Bakr's hand in allegiance, and the general pledge in the mosque the next day. It is the basic text for the events of the Saqifa. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
