## Summary
Hadith of Abdullah ibn Masud: the blood of a Muslim may be shed only in three cases: a married adulterer, a life for a life, and one who leaves his religion. Ibn Rajab explains that these are the rights of Islam by which the blood of one who testifies to God's oneness and to Muhammad's messengership may be spilled. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
