## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari glosses the word for rivals as the partners people set up alongside God. He sets out two readings of the verb for misleading: either those who did this mislead people, or the makers of rivals are the ones led astray from God's path. He reads the command to enjoy oneself not as permission but as a threat, because the destination is the Fire.
