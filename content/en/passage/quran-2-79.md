## In al-Tabari's commentary

On "woe", they differ: Ibn Abbas through al-Dahhak, that it is the punishment upon them; Abu 'Iyad and Shaqiq, that it is the pus that flows at the base of Hell, or a valley of pus there; and a report is given from 'Uthman ibn 'Affan from the Prophet that "Wayl is a mountain in the Fire", on which the editor notes that the chain is problematic, with two narrators he could not trace and a scribal error in the text. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 267} {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v02 p. 268}
