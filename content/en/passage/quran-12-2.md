**Commentary on verse 2 (p. 551)**

On [[passage/quran-12-2|12:2]] al-Tabari says: We sent down this clear Book as an Arabic Qur'an to the Arabs, because their tongue and speech is Arabic, so We sent down this Book in their tongue that they might understand it and grasp from it, and that is His saying 'so that you may understand'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 551}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
