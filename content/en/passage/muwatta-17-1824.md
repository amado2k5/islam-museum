## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Zayd ibn Aslam, from Ata ibn Yasar, that the Messenger said: dates for dates, measure for measure. It was told to him that his agent over Khaybar takes one sa' for two. The Messenger said: call him to me. He was called, and the Messenger asked: do you take one sa' for two? He said: Messenger of God, they will not sell me the janib dates for a sa' of the mixed dates. The Messenger said: sell the mixed dates for dirhams, then buy janib dates with the dirhams. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.146}
