## Summary
Al-Wahidi's report on the occasion of revelation of al-Hijr 15:11, narrated in Asbab nuzul al-Qur'an (Dar al-Islah 1992 ed., Shamela 11314).

The verse, in al-Wahidi's heading: «ومن الناس من يعبد الله على حرف».
