## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that he used to say that if a man divorces his wife and she enters blood in the third menstrual period, she has separated from him and he from her. Malik said this is the practice among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.91}
