## Summary
Verse 19 of [[passage/quran-7|Surat al-A'raf]], a Meccan surah. {cite:tanzil-quran}

## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 19 of 206 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

Then, on "O Adam, dwell, you and your wife, in Paradise, and eat from wherever you wish, but do not approach this tree, or you will be among the wrongdoers", God says that God said to Adam, O Adam, dwell, you and your wife, in Paradise, and eat from wherever you wish; so He settled Adam and his wife in Paradise after Iblis was sent down from it and expelled, allowed them to eat of its fruit from whatever place they wished, and forbade them to approach the fruit of one particular tree. Al-Tabari says he mentioned the commentators' disagreement on this, and what he thinks right, elsewhere, and did not wish to repeat it. "Or you will be among the wrongdoers" means you would be among those who opposed their Lord's command and did what was not theirs to do. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v12 p. 346}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
