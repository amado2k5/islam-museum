## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And to Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth, and on the Day the Hour is established, that Day the falsehoods will be lost." He explains: And to Allah belongs the dominion of the seven heavens and the earth, without what you call as a partner for Him and worship besides Him, and what you call upon besides Him among the gods and equals in His dominion and authority, His rule is justified. So how can what He has as a partner be with Him, or how can you worship Him and abandon the worship of your Master and the owner of what you worship besides Him? "And the Day the Hour is established," Allah Almighty says: And the Day comes when Allah will gather the dead from their graves and gather them for a present position, 'the falsehoods will be lost': meaning: those who have falsified in this world will be deceived in their words and their supplication to Allah as partners, and their worship of gods besides Him so that He may gain His rightful places in Paradise, and they will be exchanged with them stations of the Fire that were for the rightful, so they will be made their homes of Paradise. That is the clear loss.
