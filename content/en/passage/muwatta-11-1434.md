## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Isma'il ibn Abi Hakim from Ubayda ibn Sufyan al-Hadrami from Abu Hurayra, that the Messenger of God said: eating any animal with fangs from the beasts of prey is unlawful. Malik said: this is the practice among us. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.641}
