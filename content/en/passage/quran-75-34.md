## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 34 to 39 as a single section. He interprets the phrase "He neither believed nor prayed": He did not believe in the Book of God, nor did he offer any prayer to Him, but rather he denied the Book of God and turned away from obedience to God. In this section, he quotes statements from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Zayd ibn Aslam.
