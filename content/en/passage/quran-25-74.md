## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And those who say, 'Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring delight and make us an leader for the righteous'": and those who desire Allah in their supplication and request by saying: "Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring" is what our eyes acknowledge from seeing them acting in obedience to You. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
