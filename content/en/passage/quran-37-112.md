## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 112 to 113 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We gave him glad tidings of Isaac as a prophet among the righteous": And We gave glad tidings to Abraham of Isaac as a prophet in gratitude for his kindness and obedience, as Qatadah said: "And We gave him glad tidings of Isaac as a prophet among the righteous," he said: He later gave glad tidings of him as a prophet, after this was part of his affair when he gave generously to God. From Dawud, from Ikrimah, who said: Ibn Abbas said: The sacrifice is Isaac. He said: And His saying: "And We gave him glad tidings of Isaac as a prophet among the righteous," he said: He gave glad tidings of his prophethood. And his saying: "And We granted him of Our mercy his brother Aaron as a prophet," he said: Aaron was greater than Moses, but he meant God to grant him his prophethood. He said: I heard David narrate, from Ikrimah, from Ibn Abbas in this verse: "And We gave him glad tidings of Isaac as a prophet among the righteous," he said: He only gave him glad tidings of him as a prophet when he ransomed him from the sacrifice, and the glad tidings of prophethood did not come at his birth. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ikrimah.
