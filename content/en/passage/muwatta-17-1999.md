## Summary
Yahya transmits from Malik, from Abdallah ibn Dinar, from Abdullah ibn Umar, that a man mentioned to the Messenger that he is cheated in sales. The Messenger said: when you sell, say: no deception. So whenever the man sold, he would say: no deception. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.218}
