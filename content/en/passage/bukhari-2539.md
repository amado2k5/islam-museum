## Summary
In the Book of the Obligation of the Fifth: according to Urwa, from Marwan and al-Miswar ibn Makhrama, the delegation of Hawazin asked the Prophet to return their property or their captives. He gave them the choice, and they chose their captives. He then asked people to release them, and they agreed. He had waited for them some ten nights when he returned from Ta'if. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}
