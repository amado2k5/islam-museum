## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari recites verses 48 to 50 in a single passage: "No fatigue will touch them therein," meaning the pious will not be touched by fatigue in Paradise, "nor will they have any way out of it," but its bliss will always be for them. Then "Enlighten My servants that I am the Forgiving, the Merciful": Inform me, O Muhammad, My servants, that I am the One who covers their sins when they repent and repent by abandoning their disgrace and punishment for it. The Most Merciful to them is that I will not punish them after repentance. And "And that My punishment is the painful punishment" for those who persist in their sins and persist in them, which is a painful punishment unlike any other. This is a warning to people against their sins and commands them to turn to God. It is narrated from Qatadah that the Prophet said: If a servant knew the extent of Allah's forgiveness, he would not fear forbidden acts, and if he knew the extent of His punishment, he would have deposed himself. He also narrates the story of Gabriel: 'My servants have not despaired' regarding the reason for its revelation.
