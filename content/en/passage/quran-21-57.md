## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 57 to 58 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And Allah will surely surely render your idols after you turn away in disguise": He mentioned that Abraham, swore this oath in secret and secretly among his people, and that he only heard it from him when they said: Whoever did this to our gods is among the wrongdoers.
