## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari continues the passage: "Indeed, My servants, you have no authority over them except those who follow you among the misguided," meaning Iblis has no authority over the believing servants of God to mislead them and has no authority over them; rather, he has authority over the misguided ones who follow him by choice and obey him in sins. In the previous verse, the disagreement among the readers and commentators was presented in "This is a straight path upon me": the path is his return to God, so each is rewarded by his deeds from Mujahid and others.
