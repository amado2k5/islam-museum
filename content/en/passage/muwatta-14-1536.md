## Summary
Malik says a free man should not marry a slave-woman while he can afford a free woman. He may marry a slave-woman only if he cannot afford a free woman and fears sin, which means adultery. He based this on the Qur'anic provision for one who cannot afford a free woman. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.43}
