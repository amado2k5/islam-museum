**Commentary on verse 87 (pp. 232-233)**

On "O my sons, go and inquire about Joseph and his brother, and do not despair of God's relief; none despairs of God's relief except the disbelieving people", al-Tabari says: when Ya'qub grew hopeful about Joseph he said to his sons "O my sons, go" to the place you came from and where you left your two brothers, "and inquire about Joseph", that is, seek Joseph and learn his news; "tahassus" is of the form "tafa'ul" from "hiss"; "and his brother" means Benjamin; "and do not despair of God's relief" means do not lose hope that God will bring us relief from the grief we are in for Joseph and his brother, by a relief from Him, and show me them; "none despairs of God's relief" means none loses hope of His deliverance and mercy and cuts off his hope in Him "except the disbelieving people", meaning those who deny His power over what He wills to bring into being. The editor refers to the earlier discussion of "ya's". {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 232}

The interpreters said the same: report 19734, from al-Suddi, through Ibn Waki', Amr and Asbat: "inquire about Joseph and his brother" in Egypt, "and do not despair of God's relief", meaning God's deliverance, that He may return Joseph. Reports 19735 and 19736, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id, and Muhammad ibn Abd al-A'la, Muhammad ibn Thawr and Ma'mar: of God's mercy. Report 19737, from Ibn Ishaq, through Ibn Humayd and Salama: Ya'qub then said to his sons, while holding to his good opinion of his Lord despite the grief he was in: O my sons, go to the lands you came from and inquire about Joseph and his brother, and do not despair of God's relief, that is, His deliverance. Report 19738, from al-Dahhak, through al-Husayn ibn al-Faraj, Abu Mu'adh and Ubayd ibn Sulayman: of God's mercy. Report 19739, from Ibn Zayd, through Yunus and Ibn Wahb: of God's deliverance, that He relieve you of the sorrow you are in. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 233}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
