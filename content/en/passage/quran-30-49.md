## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "even though they were before it was sent down upon them by Lamblisin": Those who were struck by this rain from among His servants before it was sent down by this rain from before it was sent down to them by Lamblisin, meaning they were depressed and saddened by its withholding from them, as in Qatadah: "Even though they were before it was sent down upon them before him by Lamblisin": meaning despairing.
