## Summary
Fatima bint al-Mundhir tells that she used to see Asma bint Abi Bakr at Muzdalifa, ordering someone to pray the dawn prayer for her and her companions when dawn rose; then she would ride and go to Mina without stopping. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.525}
