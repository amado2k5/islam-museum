**Commentary on verse 30 (pp. 445-446)**

On "thus We sent you among a community before which other communities have passed away, that you may recite to them what We revealed to you, while they disbelieve in the Merciful. Say: He is my Lord, there is no god but He; in Him I put my trust and to Him is my turning", al-Tabari says, mighty be His praise: so We sent you, Muhammad, among a group of people, that is, to a group before which groups have passed away on the like of what they are on and gone; "that you may recite to them what We revealed to you" means convey to them what We sent you to them with of the revelation I revealed to you; "while they disbelieve in the Merciful" means they deny God's oneness and call it a lie; "say: He is my Lord" means that if those to whom I sent you, Muhammad, disbelieve in the Merciful, then say: God is my Lord, "there is no god but He; in Him I put my trust and to Him is my turning", that is, to Him is my return and my coming back, "mata'b" being a verbal noun from the saying "tubtu mata'ban wa tawba". The interpreters said the same: report 20397, from Qatada, through Bishr, Yazid and Sa'id: "while they disbelieve in the Merciful", it was mentioned to us that the Prophet of God at Hudaybiyya, when he made peace with Quraysh, wrote "this is what Muhammad, the Messenger of God, has agreed", {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 445}

and the polytheists of Quraysh said, if you were God's messenger and we then fought you, we would have wronged you; rather write "this is what Muhammad ibn Abd Allah has agreed". The Companions of the Messenger of God said, let us fight them, Messenger of God! He said, no; write as they wish, I am Muhammad ibn Abd Allah. When the scribe wrote "in the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate", Quraysh said, as for "the Merciful", we do not know him; the people of the Jahiliyya used to write "in Your name, O God". His Companions said, Messenger of God, let us fight them! He said, no; write as they wish. Report 20398, from Mujahid, through al-Qasim, al-Husayn, Hajjaj and Ibn Jurayj: "thus We sent you among a community that has passed away", the verse, he said, this was when the Messenger of God wrote to Quraysh at Hudaybiyya; he wrote "in the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate" and they said, do not write "the Merciful", we do not know what "the Merciful" is, write only "in Your name, O God"; God said "while they disbelieve in the Merciful. Say: He is my Lord, there is no god but He", the verse. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v16 p. 446}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
