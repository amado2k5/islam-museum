## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 46 to 47 as a single section. He interprets His saying, “Or do they possess knowledge of the unseen, so they write it down?”: Do they possess the Preserved Tablet, which contains the news of what is to come, so they write from it what is in it, and argue with you about it, and claim that, despite their disbelief in their Lord, they are in a better position with God than those who believe in Him?
