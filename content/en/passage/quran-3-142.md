## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 142 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Opening of the commentary (p. 246).** Abu Ja'far says God addresses the Companions of Muhammad: did you suppose you would enter paradise and gain your Lord's honour and the nobility of ranks with Him before it is made clear to His believing servants which of you strives in His cause as He commanded. He says he has explained earlier, with proofs, what God's knowing means in such places, and the editor refers to that explanation (vol. 3: 158-162). He adds that the patient are those steadfast at the moment of hardship in what befalls them for God's sake of wound, pain and harm, and then begins the reports, which continue on the next page. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 246}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict. We stop at p. 246 in the commentary on verse 142 and continue from p. 247. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v07 p. 246}
