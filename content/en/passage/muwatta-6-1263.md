## Summary
Malik says that whoever throws or hunts game after he has stoned the pillar and shaved his head, though he has not yet performed the circumambulation of the House, owes the penalty for that game, for God's words in Surah al-Ma'ida; and whoever has not circumambulated still has the touching of perfume and women remaining for him. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.561}
