## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23 to 26 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase “You will recognize in their faces the radiance of bliss” as meaning: you will recognize in the righteous, whose attributes God has described, the radiance of bliss, that is, its beauty, brilliance, and sparkle. The reciters differed in their reading of the word “you will recognize.” Most reciters in various regions, except for Abu Ja'far al-Qari, read it as “You will recognize in their faces” with the “t” in the accusative case, and “the radiance of bliss” with “radiance” in the accusative case. Abu Ja'far read it as “It is recognized” with the “t” in the nominative case, and “the radiance of bliss” with “radiance” in the nominative case. The correct reading in our view is that of the reciters of the major cities, which is to pronounce the ta' in "ta'rifu" with a fatha (short 'a' sound) and "nadrah" with a nasb (short 'a' sound). He also mentions in the passage statements attributed to Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd, al-A'mash, and others.
