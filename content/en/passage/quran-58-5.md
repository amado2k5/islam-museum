## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Indeed, those who oppose Allah and His Messenger have been restrained as those before them were suppressed. And We have sent down clear signs, and for the disbelievers is a humiliating punishment." It means: Those who oppose Allah in His limits and obligations set limits other than His limits, and that is opposition to Allah and His Messenger. As for Qatadah, he used to say the following meaning: And from Qatadah, His saying: "Indeed, those who oppose Allah and His Messenger" means: they oppose Allah and His Messenger. As for His saying: "They were restrained as those before them were suppressed," it means: they were angered and disgraced as those before them among the nations who opposed God and His Messenger were disgraced. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
