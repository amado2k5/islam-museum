## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "It was decreed upon him that whoever takes him as a guardian, he misleads him and guides him to the punishment of the Blazing Blaze": Satan was decreed, so the meaning of "he was decreed" here is decreed, and the 'ha' in the phrase "upon him" is a mention of Satan. Also: from Muammar from Qatadah: "It was decreed upon him that he was the one who took him as his guardian," he said: It was written upon Satan, that he is one who follows Satan from God's creation. And from Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding Allah's saying: "It is decreed upon him that he is the one who takes him as His protector," he said: Satan followed him. And he said: Hajjaj narrated to me, from Ibn Jurayj, from Mujahid, "that whoever takes him away," he said: He followed him. And His saying: "for he misleads him" means: He misleads those who take him astray. The 'ha' in "he misleads" refers to the "who" in his saying: "whoever takes him." The interpretation of the speech: Satan was decreed that he misleads his followers and does not guide them to the truth. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
