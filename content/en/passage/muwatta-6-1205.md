## Summary
Malik says it is not proper for anyone to pass the halting place (al-Mu'arrasa) on his return without praying there; and if he passes it outside prayer time, he should stop until prayer is due and then pray what he wishes, because it reached him that the Messenger of God halted there. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.541}
