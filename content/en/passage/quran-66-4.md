## Al-Tabari's commentary

His statement, “If you two repent to God, for your hearts have inclined [towards Him]. But if you support one another against him, then indeed, God is his protector, and Gabriel and the righteous believers and the angels, moreover, are [his] supporters,” is explained as follows: If you two women repent to God, for your hearts have inclined towards loving what the Messenger of God disliked, namely, his avoidance of his concubine and his prohibition of her for himself, or the prohibition of what was lawful for him, which he made unlawful for himself because of Hafsa. The passage also quotes statements from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sufyan.
