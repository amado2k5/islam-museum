## Summary
A saying of Malik: the paternal grandfather is nearer than the sons of the full brother and nearer than the paternal uncle, the full brother, in inheritance. The son of the full brother is nearer than the grandfather in inheriting by the clients' allegiance. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.20}
