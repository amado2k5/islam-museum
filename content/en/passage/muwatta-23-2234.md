## Summary
He said: I also heard Malik say of a slave who is partly free and partly enslaved: his property is held in his hand, and he may not do anything with it, but he eats from it and clothes himself in a customary way. When he dies, his property goes to the part of him that is still enslaved. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.319}
