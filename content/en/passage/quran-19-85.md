## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 85 to 86 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "the Day We gather the righteous to the Most Merciful in a delegation": the Day We gather those who feared the world and feared His punishment, so they avoided His sins and fulfilled His obligations to their Lord. "Delegation" means by delegation: the two caravans. It is said: 'I sent a delegation to so-and-so' when he came to him, and the people sent a delegation to their leader when they sent a delegation before them. In this context, 'Wafd' means plural, but it is singular, because their singular verb is 'wafd'. The plural of 'wafd' may be 'al-wafud', as some Banu Hanifa said: 'I am a praiser, so what is the maker of the head of delegations?' Muzahim ibn Jassas. In this case, 'al-wafud' may be plural 'wafid', just as 'sitting' is the plural of 'sitting.' In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, and Ibn Jurayj.
