## Summary
Malik related that Ibn Shihab told him that a man confessed to adultery against himself in the time of the Prophet, and testified against himself four times, and the Prophet ordered him stoned. Ibn Shihab said: for that reason a man is held to his confession against himself. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.382}
