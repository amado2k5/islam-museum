**Commentary on verse 70: "We took the covenant of the Children of Israel", p. 477**

Al-Tabari says: God swore that He took the covenant of the Children of Israel to worship Him alone sincerely, to act on His commands and to refrain from His prohibitions, and sent messengers to them, promising abundant reward for obedience and severe punishment for disobedience through them; yet whenever a messenger came with what their souls did not desire they denied one party and killed another, breaking the covenant and showing boldness against God. The editor notes that a part of the manuscript's old division ends at this point, where the copyist closed it with a prayer and an invocation and began a new part; the museum describes this and does not quote it.  {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v10 p. 477}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
