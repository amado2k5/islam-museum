## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "So Lot believed in him and said, 'Indeed, I am emigrating to my Lord. Indeed, He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise.'" So Abraham, the friend of God, believed Lot, and said, "Indeed, I am emigrating to my Lord," meaning: Abraham said: I am the emigrant of my people to my Lord, to the Levant. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Ibrahim.
