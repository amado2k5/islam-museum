**Commentary on verse 102 (pp. 474-476)**

On [[passage/quran-11-102|11:102]] al-Tabari says: as I seized, people, the folk of these towns whose story I have told you, with the punishment I seized them with for opposing My command, denying My messengers and rejecting My signs, so is My seizing of towns and their people when I seize them with My punishment while they wrong themselves by disbelieving in God, associating others with Him and denying His messengers; 'His seizing is painful', that is, a severe pain. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 474}

This is a warning from God to this community not to follow, in disobeying Him, the way of the wicked nations before them, lest there fall on them what fell on those. Report 18559, from Abu Musa: the Prophet said that God gives respite, or perhaps he said He lets the wrongdoer be, until when He seizes him He does not let him escape, then he recited 'such is the seizing of your Lord when He seizes the towns while they are wrongdoing'; the editor refers to its sources in al-Bukhari, Muslim, Ibn Majah and al-Tirmidhi and discusses a narrator's name in the chain and the meaning of 'gives respite'. Report 18560, from Ibn Zayd: God warned this community of His might in the words 'such is the seizing of your Lord…'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 475}

'Asim al-Jahdari read the verse in an irregular reading, and al-Tabari says it is a reading he does not allow because it differs from the Muslims' copies and from what the readers of the cities follow; the editor notes that 'Asim has three readings of it. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 476}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
