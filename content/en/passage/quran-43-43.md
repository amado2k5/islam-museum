## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 43 to 44 in a single passage. He explains the verse "So hold fast to that which has been revealed to you. Indeed, you are on a straight path": So hold firmly, O Muhammad, to what this Qur'an your Lord has revealed to you, "Indeed, you are on a straight path" and a sound methodology, and that is the religion of Allah that He commanded, which is Islam. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "So hold fast to what has been revealed to you. Indeed, you are on a straight path," meaning Islam. And from Al-Suddi: "So hold fast to what has been revealed to you. Indeed, you are on a straight path." And His saying: "And indeed, it is a reminder for you and your people," He says: And indeed, this Qur'an which was revealed to you, O Muhammad, which We commanded you to hold fast to, is an honor for you and your people from Quraysh. "And you will be questioned," it means: and your Lord will question you and them about what you have done in it, and whether you have done what your Lord commanded you to do, and have you refrained from what He forbade you? In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and Ibn Zayd.
