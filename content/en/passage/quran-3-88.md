## Place in the museum
Its place: verse 88 of 200 verses in the surah. The text is kept in the frozen Tanzil mushaf and is not retyped here.

**Verse 88: abiding and the non-lightening.** Al-Tabari says that abiding is remaining in God's punishment, that the non-lightening means they are not reduced in the punishment in any state nor relieved in it, and that the non-respite means they are not given time to offer an excuse. All of this is the very sense of abiding in the punishment in the hereafter. The editor refers to the earlier commentary on abiding, lightening and respite in previous volumes. In a long note he says that the sentence stood in the manuscript and printed text as 'all of that, I mean abiding', which is defective in structure and meaning, and that what he put is correct; that Abu Jafar inclines to brevity in many places and did not set out here the construction of the two clauses after the word for abiding; that the grammarians construe them as an overlapping circumstantial clause, that is, a circumstance from a circumstance, since abiding is itself a circumstance of the pronoun; whereas Abu Jafar counts them a new sentence explaining abiding in the fire. He cites as proof al-Tabari's words in the commentary on the parallel verse of al-Baqara in volume 3, p. 264, that it is a report from God of the permanence of the punishment forever without limit or lightening, which he takes as a decisive text that al-Tabari's construction of this place is the one he adopted. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v06 p. 577}
