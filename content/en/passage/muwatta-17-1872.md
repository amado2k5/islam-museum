## Summary
Malik transmits from Kathir ibn Farqad that he asked Abu Bakr ibn Muhammad ibn Amr ibn Hazm about a man who sells food to another man for gold on credit, and then buys dates with the gold before taking possession of the gold. He disliked that and forbade it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.170}
