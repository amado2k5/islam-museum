## Summary
Malik said: Yahya ibn Said narrated to me that Aisha, wife of the Prophet, said: Amir ibn Fuhayra used to say a verse that the coward's death is above him, and he saw death before he tasted it. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.470}
