## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 62 to 63 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So they disputed their matter among themselves and kept secret secrets": the magicians disputed their affairs among themselves. Their dispute among themselves was, as mentioned, that some of them said to one another, and from Qatadah, regarding His saying: "So they disputed their matter among themselves and kept secret secrets," the magicians said among themselves: If this is a magician, then we will overcome him, and if he is from the sky, he has a command. Others said, in fact, some said to others: What is this saying about a magician? In the passage, he quotes from Qatadah and Ibn Ishaq.
