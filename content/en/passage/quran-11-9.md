**Commentary on verse 9 (pp. 255-256)**

On [[passage/quran-11-9|11:9]] al-Tabari says: if We give man a taste of ease and wide provision in living, spreading the world out for him, which is the mercy He mentions here, 'then We take it from him', stripping him of it so that calamities strike that ruin it and carry it off, 'he is surely despairing, ungrateful', remaining without hope of God's mercy, despairing of good. 'Ya'us' is the intensive form from 'so-and-so despaired of such', when it is his trait, and 'ungrateful' is ungrateful to the one who gave him favour and scant in thanks to the Lord who gave him of His bounty. Report 18004, from Ibn Jurayj: son of Adam, when you have a favour from God of ease, safety and well-being you are ungrateful for it, and when it is taken from you you despair of God's relief and lose hope of His mercy; so are the hypocrite and the unbeliever. The editor refers to the earlier commentaries on 'taste', 'taking away', 'despair' and 'ingratitude'. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 256}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
