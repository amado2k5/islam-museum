## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 14 to 16 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "Indeed, the righteous will be in gardens and springs": those who fear Allah by obeying Him and avoiding His sins in this world will be in gardens and springs of water in the Hereafter. And His saying: "Taking what their Lord has given them" means: 'Doing what their Lord has commanded them, fulfilling His obligations.' In this section, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Zayd, and Sufyan.
