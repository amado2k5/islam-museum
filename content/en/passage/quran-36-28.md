## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 28 to 29 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And We did not send down upon his people after him any army from the heaven, nor were We sending it down": and We did not send down to the people this believer whom his people killed to call them to God and advise them "after him," meaning after his destruction "from an army from the sky." The interpreters differed regarding the meaning of the soldiers who informed God that this believer did not descend to the people after killing him. Some said: This means that God did not send them a message afterward, nor did He send them a prophet. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid and Qatadah.
