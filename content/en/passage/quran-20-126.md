## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 126 to 128 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "And whoever turns away from My remembrance - indeed, for him will have a life of distress, and We will resurrect him on the Day of Resurrection blind": "And whoever turns away from My remembrance" which I remind him of, turns away from it, does not accept it, does not respond to it, does not take heed, and is discouraged from what he is living on, and is restrained from what he is living on, from the command of his Lord: "Indeed, he will have a narrow life." He means: He has a narrow life. And 'dunk' is among the homes, places, and livelihoods: the intense one. It is said: this is the place of distress: if it is tight. 'Aish' dinak: male, female, singular, two, and plural with one wording. From this is the saying of Antarah: 'And if they descend with your distress,' then it was revealed. He quotes in the Maqta' from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Al-Hasan, and Ibn Zayd.
