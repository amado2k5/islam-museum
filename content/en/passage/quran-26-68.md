## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari addresses verses 66 to 68 together in one place. Al-Tabari explains the removal of others by saying that God brought the people of Pharaoh near the sea and brought them closer to it, just as Paradise drew near to the righteous. He cites the poetry of Al-Ajjaj, and quotes from Ibn Abbas that the meaning of 'He drew us closer,' and from Qatadah regarding the meaning of others.
