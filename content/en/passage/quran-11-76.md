**Commentary on verse 76 (p. 407)**

On [[passage/quran-11-76|11:76]] al-Tabari reports the words of His messengers to Abraham, 'Abraham, turn away from this'; they said that when he pleaded with them for the people of Lot, leave off disputing and quarrelling about them, for 'your Lord's command has come', that is, your Lord's command to punish them has come and the word of punishment is due on them and the decree to destroy them has passed, 'and a punishment not to be turned back will come to them', that is, a punishment from God will come down on Lot's people which cannot be driven off. The report has already been given. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v15 p. 407}

These statements are given as transmitted; the choices and arguments are al-Tabari's, the notes and source tracings are the editor's, and the museum gives no verdict.
