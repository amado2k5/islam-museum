## Summary
A report of Sahl ibn Sa'd in the virtues of Ali: a man from the family of Marwan who governed Medina ordered Sahl to curse Ali, and he refused. He then related that "Abu Turab" was the name of Ali that he loved most, which the Prophet called him when he found him wiping dust from his back in the mosque after a dispute with Fatima. {cite:sahih-muslim}
