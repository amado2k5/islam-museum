## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And We certainly gave Moses and Aaron the criterion, and a light and a reminder for the righteous": And We certainly gave Moses son of Imran and his brother Harun al-Furqan, meaning the Book that distinguishes between truth and falsehood. This is the Torah in the words of some of them, and in the passage it quotes from Mujahid and Ibn Zayd.
