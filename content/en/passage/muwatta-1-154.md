## Summary
A report that Malik says he asked Ibn Shihab al-Zuhri about a pregnant woman who sees blood. He said she should refrain from prayer. Yahya transmits from Malik that this is the practice of the people of Medina. The editor notes that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ma'mar from al-Zuhri and Qatada. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.105}
