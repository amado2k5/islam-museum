## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "Is the most important good among the people of Tubba' and those before them whom We destroyed; indeed, they were criminals": Are these the polytheists, O Muhammad, better than your people, or the people of Tubba, meaning the Himyarite followers? Also: From Ibn Abi Nujayh, from Mujahid, regarding the words of Allah, the Almighty: "The most important good, or the people of Tubba," he said: Al-Himyari. And from Qatadah, 'The most important good or the people of Tubba' (Tubba') was a man from Himyar who marched with armies until he left Al-Hirah, then came to Samarkand and destroyed it. He mentioned that when he wrote, he wrote by the name of the one who was called and ruled land, sea, desert, and wind. He told us that Ka'b used to say: 'I described the righteous man who blamed his people but did not blame him.' Aisha used to say: Do not insult Tub', for he was a righteous man. From Ma'mar, from Qatadah, who said: Aisha said: Tub' was a righteous man. Ka'b said: He criticized his people but did not criticize him. In the Maqta, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, and Sa'id ibn Jubayr.
