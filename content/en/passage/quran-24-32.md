## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And marry the women among you and the righteous among your male and female slaves if they are poor, whom Allah will enrich from His bounty. And Allah is All-Encompassing and Knowing": And marry, O believers, those who have no husband among your free men and women, and among the righteous among your slaves and dominions. Al-Ayma: plural of 'Ayyam,' but it is the plural of 'Ayyam' because it is a verb in meaning. It is pluralized as al-Yatima is plural: orphans, and from it is a beautiful saying: 'I loved al-Ayyam when Buthayna Aym and I loved when the Ghawaniya sang, and if Ayyam were pluralized, it would be correct.' In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Ibn Zayd.
