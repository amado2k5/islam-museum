## Summary
Malik transmits from Hisham ibn Urwa, from his father, that he said: whoever has departed has fulfilled God's pilgrimage; for if nothing held him back, he is worthy that the last of his time there be the circumambulation of the House; and if something held him back or came in his way, God has fulfilled his pilgrimage. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1445) transmitted it from Malik. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.497}
