## Summary
Malik transmits from Abu al-Zubayr, from Jabir ibn Abdallah al-Sulami, that the Messenger of God forbade a man to eat with his left hand, to walk in one sandal, to wrap himself in the sama' garment (covering the whole body with a single cloth), and to sit wrapped in one garment with his private parts uncovered. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.507}
