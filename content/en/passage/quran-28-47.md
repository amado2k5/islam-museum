## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And if no calamity befalls them because of what their hands have put forth and they say, 'Our Lord, why have You not sent to us a messenger so that we may follow Your verses and be among the believers?': And if those to whom I have sent You, O Muhammad, would not say, 'If Our punishment had befallen them before We sent You to them for their disbelief in their Lord, their acquiring sins, and their forgiving sins, and their forgiveness of sins, our Lord, why would You not have sent us a messenger before Your wrath befell us, and Your punishment had come upon us so that we could follow Your evidence, and the verse of Your Book which You have revealed to Your Messenger so that we may be among the believers in Your divinity, the believers in Your Messenger regarding what You commanded us and forbade, We would hasten to punish them for their polytheism before We sent you to them, but We sent you to them as a warner of Our severity against their disbelief, so that people would have no argument against Allah after the messengers. The calamity in this context is torment and retribution. And by His saying: "for what their hands have put forward," he means what they have acquired.
