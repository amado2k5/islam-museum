## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And that you should recite the Qur'an, but whoever is guided, it is only for his own soul. And whoever goes astray, say, 'I am only of the warners'": "Say, I have only been commanded to worship the Lord of this town" and "To be among the Muslims and to recite the Qur'an. So whoever is guided" means: Whoever follows me and believes in me and what I brought, and follows the path of guidance, "he is only guided for himself." He means: He only follows the right path by following Me, believing in Me, and by what I have brought for himself, because through his faith in Me and what I have brought, he is safe from His punishment in this world and His punishment in The Hereafter.
