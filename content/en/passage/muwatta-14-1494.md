## Summary
A report from Malik that he was told Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab said Umar ibn al-Khattab said: a woman may not marry except with the consent of her guardian, or a man of judgement from her family, or the ruler. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.29}
