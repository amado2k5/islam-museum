## Summary
A report that Malik transmits from Umar ibn Muhammad ibn Zayd from Salim ibn Abdullah, in the chapter on completing prayer when the worshipper doubts what he prayed: Abdullah ibn Umar said that whoever doubts his prayer should estimate what he thinks he forgot and pray it, then make the prostrations of forgetfulness while seated. The editor notes that Abu Mus'ab, Suwayd and Yahya ibn Bukayr in al-Bayhaqi transmitted it, and that Abd al-Razzaq transmitted it from Ma'mar from al-Zuhri from Salim. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.151}
