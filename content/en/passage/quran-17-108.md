## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 108 to 109 in a single passage. He explains His saying, "Say, 'Believe in it or do not believe. Indeed, those who were given knowledge before it, when it is recited to them, they will prostrate to their ears.'" Say, O Muhammad, to those who say to you, "We will never believe in you until you gush forth for us from the earth a spring." Believe in this Qur'an, which, if mankind and jinn were to come together to produce the like of it, they would not have done it, even if some of them were supporters to one another, or you would not believe in it. For your faith in it will not increase the treasures of Allah's mercy, nor will your abandonment of faith diminish that. And if they disbelieve in it, then those who were given knowledge of Allah and His verses before His revelation among the believers of the People of the Two Scriptures, when this Qur'an is recited to them, they will fall down in reverence and honor for it, and knowing that it is from Allah, they will surely fall prostrate to the ground. The interpreters differed regarding what was meant by the phrase 'they fall down to the throats,' and some said: It meant the faces. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, Hasan, Ibn Zayd, and Ibn Jurayj.
