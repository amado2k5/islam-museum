## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 23-24 as a single section. He interprets the words, “Except as a message from God and His messages. And whoever disobeys God and His Messenger - then indeed, for him is the fire of Hell, abiding therein forever”: Say to the Arab polytheists: I have no power to harm or guide you, “except as a message from God and His messages.” He says: Except to convey to you from God what He has commanded me to convey to you, and except for His messages with which He sent me to you. As for guidance and misguidance, they are in God’s hands; He alone possesses them, not any of His creation. He guides whom He wills and misguides whom He wills. In this section, he quotes statements from Qatadah.
