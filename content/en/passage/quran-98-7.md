## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 6-7 as a single unit. He interprets the phrase, “Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds - those are the best of creatures,” as meaning: those who believe in God and His Messenger Muhammad, and worship God sincerely, upholding the true faith, and establish prayer, and give zakat, and obey God in what He has commanded and forbidden, “those are the best of creatures.” He says: whoever among the people does that is the best of creatures. And it is narrated on the authority of Abu al-Jarud, on the authority of Muhammad ibn Ali, regarding the verse, “Those are the best of creatures,” that the Prophet said, “You, O Ali, and your followers.”
