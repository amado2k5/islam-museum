## Al-Tabari's commentary

Al-Tabari reads the verse within verses 170 to 173 in a single passage. He explains the phrase "So they disbelieved in it, and they will know": When the mention came to them from Allah, they disbelieved in it, and that was their disbelief in Muhammad and what He brought them from Allah of the Revelation and the Book. Allah says: They will know, when it comes to me, what punishment will befall them for their disbelief in that. In the passage, he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
