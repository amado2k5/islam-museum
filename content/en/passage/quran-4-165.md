**Commentary on verse 165: messengers bearing good news and warning, so that people have no argument against God (pp. 407-408)**

Al-Tabari says 'messengers' is in the accusative as a detached word from the names of the prophets already named: I sent them as messengers to My creatures and servants, bearing good news of My reward to whoever obeys Me, follows My command and affirms My messengers, and warning of My punishment to whoever disobeys Me, opposes My command and denies My messengers, so that people have no argument against God after the messengers: that those who disbelieve in Me, worship rivals besides Me, or stray from My path may not say: why did You not send us a messenger? Report 10849, from al-Suddi through Asbat: so that they do not say: You sent us no messengers. The phrase 'God was mighty and wise' means He has always been mighty in His vengeance on those who disbelieve and disobey after the proof was set against them by His messengers and signs, and wise in His direction of them. {cite:tafsir-tabari-shakir-v09 p. 408}
