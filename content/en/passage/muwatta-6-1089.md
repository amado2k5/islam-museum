## Summary
The first hadith in the chapter on beginning with al-Safa in running. Malik transmits from Jafar ibn Muhammad ibn Ali, from his father, from Jabir ibn Abdullah, who said: I heard the Messenger of God as he left the mosque heading for al-Safa say: we begin with what God began with, and he began with al-Safa. The editor notes that Abu Musab (1311) transmitted it from Malik, and that it is in the lengthy hadith of Jabir on the pilgrimage transmitted by Muslim and others. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.499}
