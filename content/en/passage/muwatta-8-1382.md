## Summary
Malik transmits from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of God came upon Umar ibn al-Khattab while he was travelling in a party, swearing by his father; he said: God forbids you to swear by your fathers; whoever swears, let him swear by God or be silent. {cite:muwatta-maruf-1997 p.616}
