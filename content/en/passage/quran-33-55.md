## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "There is no blame upon them concerning their fathers, their sons, their brothers, the sons of their brothers, the sons of their sisters, their wives, or what their right hands possess. And fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is ever a Witness over all things." Then the interpreters differed regarding the meaning of which the wing was placed for these people, and some said: the wing was placed on their behalf when they placed their jilbabs. In the passage, he quotes from Mujahid, Qatadah, Ikrimah, and Ibn Zayd.
