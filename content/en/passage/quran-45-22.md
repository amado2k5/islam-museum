## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains the phrase "And Allah created the heavens and the earth in truth and that every soul may be recompensed for what it has earned, and they will not be wronged": "And Allah created the heavens and the earth in truth" for justice and truth, not because of what those ignorant of God attributed to Him that He makes those who commit evil, disobey Him, and oppose Him, like those who believe and do righteous deeds, in life and death, for this was an act that is not among the people of justice and fairness. He, the Exalted, says: God did not create the heavens and the earth for injustice and injustice, but We created them for truth and justice. It is right to disagree between the judgment of the wrongdoer and the benefactor, both in the immediate and the future.
