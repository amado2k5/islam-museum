## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying, "And He has not sent for you in the earth of varying colors. Indeed, in that is a sign for a people who will be reminded": He means His praise by saying: "And He has not left for you," meaning He subjected to you what He sown: meaning He has not created for you on the earth of different colors, of its colors of beasts and fruits. Also: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And He has not caused for you to be scattered in the earth," he says: He has not created for you of different colors, of animals, trees, and fruits. Yes, they are from Allah, so be grateful to Allah. Of animals, trees, and fruits. His accusative statement: 'different' because his saying: 'ma' is in the accusative case with the meaning described. If that is so, then 'different in color' must be a state of 'ma,' and the predicate without it is complete, even if 'ma' were not in the accusative position. The speech was a subject of his saying: 'and he left you with no distinction,' it would only be in the nominative, because it would have become the preposition of 'ma' at that time. In the passage, he quotes sayings from Qatadah.
