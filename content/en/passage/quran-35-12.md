## Al-Tabari's commentary

He explains His saying: "And what is equal to the sea; this is the sweet Euphrates with flowing drink, and this is salt, salt, salt, and from everything you eat fresh meat and extract ornaments to wear and you see the stars in it in small places so that you may seek of its bounty, and perhaps you will be grateful." And what is balanced in the sea, they are two: one is the sweet of the Euphrates, and the Euphrates is the sweetest of the sweet, and the other is salty salt, and that is the green sea water, and the bitter water, as follows: And from Qatadah, His saying: "And this is salty bitterness," and 'ajaj' means bitter. And His saying: "And from all you eat tender meat," it means: And from all seas you eat fresh meat, and that fish is from the Euphrates sweetened them, and their salt is bard. "And you extract a piece of jewelry to wear," meaning: pearls and coral you extract from the salted salt. In the Maqta', he quotes from Ibn Abbas and Qatadah.
