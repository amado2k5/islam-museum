## Summary
Sharjah Islamic Bank, formerly the National Bank of Sharjah, is a medium-sized Emirati Islamic bank headquartered in the Emirate of Sharjah. {cite:wikipedia-sharjah-islamic-bank-en} It provides Sharia-compliant products and services to individuals, companies, institutions and investors, and as an Islamic bank operates according to the principles of Sharia. {cite:wikipedia-sharjah-islamic-bank-en}

## Founding
The article gives 1975 in its infobox and 1976 in its history section, and dates its full conversion to Sharia compliance to 2004, without a citation for any of these; the difference is recorded, not resolved. {cite:wikipedia-sharjah-islamic-bank-en}

## Course and figures
In August 2021 it launched a digital account that customers can open in the app without visiting a branch. {cite:wikipedia-sharjah-islamic-bank-en} Its net profit for 2022 was AED 650.9 million. {cite:wikipedia-sharjah-islamic-bank-en} By its 2025 annual report it had total assets of about AED 90.3 billion and 29 locations. {cite:wikipedia-sharjah-islamic-bank-en}
