# Darussalam Publishers

## Summary
Darussalam is an international Islamic publishing and distribution house, Saudi and Pakistani, operating in 35 countries and printing mainly in Sheikhupura, Pakistan; in November 2020 it was the world's second-largest publisher of translations of the Qur'an and hadith collections after the [[group/king-fahd-complex-for-printing-the-holy-quran|King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Qur'an]]. {cite:wikipedia-darussalam-publishers-en}

## Founding
It was founded in 1986 by the Mecca-based Pakistani scholar Abdul Malik Mujahid, began as a single shop in Saudi Arabia, and grew to more than 35 countries, with 32 stores and a worldwide distribution network. {cite:wikipedia-darussalam-publishers-en}

## What it publishes
By March 2017 it had published over 1,500 Islamic books in several languages, among them more than 64 on the life of the Prophet and translations and interpretations of the Qur'an in 29 languages; it completed an English translation of the six hadith collections in 38 volumes and 20,780 pages. {cite:wikipedia-darussalam-publishers-en} In March 2012 it published an eleven-volume English encyclopedia of the sira, and in November 2020 a Punjabi translation of the Qur'an. {cite:wikipedia-darussalam-publishers-en}

## English Qur'an translations
In 1994 it issued a completely revised edition of the [[work/noble-quran-hilali-khan|Hilali–Khan]] translation in one volume and in nine, on which the King Fahd Complex based its 1997 edition. {cite:gloqur-hilali-khan-part-1} It distributes Mustafa Khattab's [[work/clear-quran-khattab|The Clear Quran]] at a low price, and Johanna Pink counts its wide distribution networks among the reasons for that translation's success. {cite:gloqur-clear-and-easy-translations}

## Today
In 2021 it signed an agreement with Dubai Media Incorporated to make the Qur'an and other Islamic texts available in more than thirty languages; it has Wikipedia articles in Bengali, English and Urdu. {cite:wikipedia-darussalam-publishers-en}
