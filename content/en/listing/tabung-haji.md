## Summary
Lembaga Tabung Haji, known as Tabung Haji, is Malaysia's hajj pilgrims fund board, headquartered at Tabung Haji Tower in Kuala Lumpur. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} It facilitates savings for the pilgrimage through Shariah-compliant investment and works through subsidiaries in finance, hospitality, property, plantation and IT. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} The economist Mehmet Asutay has described it as the world's first Islamic ethics-driven company. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en}

## Origins
Before it, Malaysia had no Islamic financial institution in which Muslims could save for the hajj, and they avoided conventional banks so that their savings would be free of riba. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} The economist Ungku Aziz presented his paper to the government of Malaya on 30 September 1959; the pilgrims' savings corporation took RM46,610 from 1,281 depositors on 30 September 1963; Lembaga Urusan dan Tabung Haji was formed in 1969 by merging it with the hajj affairs office, and the name was shortened in 1995. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} In 2017 it held deposits of about RM73 billion from nine million depositors, and it has an office in Jeddah. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en}

## Financial crisis and royal commission
Its finances came under scrutiny in 2018, when the auditor general issued an emphasis of matter on its 2017 accounts and a PricewaterhouseCoopers review found liabilities had exceeded assets since 2014. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} Under a restructuring plan approved by the Cabinet in December 2018 it transferred assets to a special purpose vehicle. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} A royal commission of inquiry appointed in January 2022, chaired by former chief justice Md Raus Sharif, concluded that the fund would have recorded a net loss for 2017 rather than a profit, and traced the crisis to distributions beyond its means, a valuation method that inflated profits and weak audit. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} The report was released on 29 July 2026, and the government ordered its findings investigated. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en}

## Subsidiaries and awards
Its subsidiaries include [[listing/bank-islam|Bank Islam Malaysia]] and [[listing/takaful-malaysia|Takaful Malaysia]]. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en} It received the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah's Labbaik awards in 2023 and 2024 and the Diamond Award in 2025 and 2026. {cite:wikipedia-tabung-haji-en}
