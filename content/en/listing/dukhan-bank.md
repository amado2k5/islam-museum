## Summary
Dukhan Bank, formerly Barwa Bank, is a Sharia-compliant bank in Doha, the third-largest Islamic bank in Qatar, serving more than 150,000 customers through eight branches in 2025. {cite:wikipedia-dukhan-bank-en}

## Founding and merger
Barwa Bank was incorporated in 2008 and began operating in 2009; it became Dukhan Bank in October 2020 after merging with International Bank of Qatar, and was listed on the Qatar Stock Exchange in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-dukhan-bank-en}

## Course
In 2022 it brought Google Pay and Samsung Pay to Qatar; in 2024 it issued a US$800 million sukuk; in December 2025 Qatar Museums announced a partnership with it for the Dadu children's museum and a numismatic centre. {cite:wikipedia-dukhan-bank-en}

## The frozen Iranian funds
It was reported to be one of two Qatari banks holding US$6 billion of frozen Iranian funds, moved to Qatar in 2023 under a prisoner-exchange agreement and then frozen again. {cite:wikipedia-dukhan-bank-en} In April 2026 Iranian sources said the United States had agreed to release them; the United States denied it. {cite:wikipedia-dukhan-bank-en}

## Recognition
Global Finance named it the World's Best Islamic Private Bank in 2024, 2025 and 2026, and it was 23rd on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s 30 Most Valuable Banks 2025. {cite:wikipedia-dukhan-bank-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}
