# Quran Majeed (Pakdata app)

## Summary

Quran Majeed is an app for reading, hearing and understanding the Qur'an, published by Pakdata, which Dawn describes as Karachi-based. {cite:google-play-quran-majeed} {cite:dawn-islam360-2019} Its site says it has served Muslims "since 2010". {cite:quran-majeed-site}

## What it offers

The app's site lists Arabic mushaf pages with customisable fonts and themes; recitations by more than thirty reciters, Mishary Rashid Alafasy and Abd al-Basit among them, with downloads for offline listening; translations and tafsir in more than fifty languages; location-based prayer times with adhan alerts; memorisation tools with repetition and progress tracking; and duas, the Hijri calendar and the qibla. {cite:quran-majeed-site} It runs on iOS, Android, Huawei and macOS, and has a paid subscription that can be given as a gift. {cite:quran-majeed-site}

## Reach

The site says it has passed a hundred million downloads in more than two hundred countries, with thirty million daily users; these are the maker's own figures. {cite:quran-majeed-site} On 7 November 2026 its Google Play page showed 10M+ Android downloads and a rating of 4.7 from about 1.12 million reviews. {cite:google-play-quran-majeed} Dawn named it in 2019 among the apps with traction similar to Islam360's or greater. {cite:dawn-islam360-2019} It has no Wikipedia article.
