## Summary
Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) is a major Emirati bank based in Abu Dhabi city; all its contracts, operations and transactions are carried out according to Islamic Shari'a principles. {cite:wikipedia-abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-en} In 2025 it managed about 2.3 million current accounts through 140 locations. {cite:wikipedia-abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-en}

## Founding
ADIB was established on 20 May 1997 as a public joint-stock company by Amiri Decree No. 9 of 1997; it began commercial operations on 11 November 1998 and was formally inaugurated by Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan, then UAE Minister of Information and Culture, on 18 April 1999. {cite:wikipedia-abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-en} It started with paid-up capital of one billion dirhams in one hundred million shares of ten dirhams each, quoted on the Abu Dhabi Securities Market. {cite:wikipedia-abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-en}

## International presence
It began operating in Egypt in 2007 after acquiring the National Development Bank of Egypt, opened in Doha in 2011 and in Baghdad in 2012, launched in Sudan with its first Khartoum branch in December 2012, and opened its first branch in Saudi Arabia in 2018. {cite:wikipedia-abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-en} In August 2025 it launched Remit!, a real-time cross-border transfer service, with Visa. {cite:wikipedia-abu-dhabi-islamic-bank-en}
