## Summary
CrescentRating is a Singapore-based research organisation that studies halal travel and publishes rankings of it. {cite:wikipedia-crescentrating-en} Fazal Bahardeen founded it in 2008, seeing a need in tourism to accommodate Muslim travellers better; it began by rating establishments on their compatibility with Muslim requirements. {cite:wikipedia-crescentrating-en}

## The index
From 2011 to 2014 it released an annual country list, which in 2015, with Mastercard, became the Global Muslim Travel Index; the 2023 report added a tool on Muslim-women-friendly destinations. {cite:wikipedia-crescentrating-en}

## Apps and partnerships
In August 2013 it launched an app giving prayer times and direction, and in December 2013 HalalTrip.com. {cite:wikipedia-crescentrating-en} Since April 2016 it has worked with the Philippine Department of Tourism, has produced studies for the OIC, and hosts the annual Halal in Travel Awards. {cite:wikipedia-crescentrating-en}
