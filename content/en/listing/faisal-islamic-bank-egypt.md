## Summary
Faisal Islamic Bank of Egypt is an Egyptian joint-stock company based in Cairo, incorporated in 1977 and operating since 1979; the bank describes itself as the first Egyptian Islamic and commercial bank. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-en} It formally opened its doors to customers on 5 July 1979, five years after its founders first discussed creating a bank in keeping with Islamic Sharia. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-ar}

## Founding
Another source quoted in the English article links the bank to the Saudi prince Mohammed al-Faisal, describing it as part of the banking network he established and as associated with conservative Saudi political and religious influence in Egypt; that is the source's description, not the museum's judgement. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-en}

## What it does
According to Bloomberg it provides a range of commercial banking products in Egypt and abroad, including current, investment and joint accounts, savings certificates and mutual funds. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-en}

## Figures
It had 38 branches in 2021 and total assets of EGP 135.8 billion in May 2022. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-ar} Its market capitalisation was US$402 million in September 2016. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-en} In 2025 it had 43 branches, about US$5.4 billion in assets and nearly 2.5 million current accounts, according to Forbes's list of Egypt's 50 Most Valuable Companies 2026. {cite:wikipedia-faisal-islamic-bank-egypt-en}
