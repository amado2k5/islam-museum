## Summary
Meezan Bank is a Pakistani Islamic bank headquartered in Karachi; it is the largest Islamic bank in Pakistan and the country's second-largest bank by market capitalisation. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en}

## Founding
It was founded in 1997 as an Islamic investment bank by Noor Financial, Pak Kuwait Investment Company and the Islamic Development Bank, and was then known as Al-Meezan Investment Bank. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en} It was listed on the Karachi Stock Exchange in 2000, and in 2002 it acquired the Pakistan operations of Société Générale. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en}

## Early years and growth
In its early years from 2002 it struggled for acceptance, because Islamic banking was unfamiliar and its loan scrutiny was stricter; it attracted deposits from religious Muslims and shifted towards lending to SMEs, including firms that had avoided conventional banks for religious reasons. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en} In May 2014 it agreed to acquire HSBC Bank Middle East's Pakistan operations, with ten branches, and a year later it took over HSBC Oman's single branch in Pakistan. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en}

## Ownership
In 2013 Noor Financial tried to sell its entire 49.1% stake, and the State Bank of Pakistan blocked the sale. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en} It sold parts of its holding in 2018 and 2019. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en} On 30 June 2026 Noor Financial held 35.03%, Pak Kuwait Investment Company 29.82% and the Islamic Development Bank 9.27%. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en}

## Figures
By its 2025 annual report it had 1,105 branches, about 20,391 employees and total assets of Rs. 4.81 trillion. {cite:wikipedia-meezan-bank-en}
