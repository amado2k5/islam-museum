# Quraish (video game)

## Summary

Quraish is a 3D real-time strategy video game produced by the Syrian studio [[listing/afkar-media|Afkar Media]] and published by Dar al-Fikr in Damascus, one of the first strategy games in Arabic. It is set in the early battles of Islam: the pre-Islamic Arab tribal wars, the [[event/ridda-wars|Ridda wars]] and the Rashidun Caliphate's campaigns against the Byzantine and Sasanian empires. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-game-en}

## Release

The English Wikipedia gives its release as 2 August 2005, for Windows and later Android; the Arabic gives 2007. The museum keeps both. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-game-en} The English article cites a USA Today report titled "Muslims craft their own video games". {cite:wikipedia-quraish-game-en}

## Content

The game takes its name from the tribe of [[group/quraysh|Quraysh]]. The Arabic article counts 74 named characters, 43 battles, among them Dibba, Juwatha and [[event/battle-of-al-qadisiyya|al-Qadisiyya]], 16 tribes and six regions, Mecca and Medina among them; the Prophet Muhammad is among the names it mentions, and the sources read do not say how he appears in it. {cite:wikipedia-quraish-game-en}

## Today

The sources read do not say what has become of the game. It has articles in six Wikipedia editions: Arabic, Bengali, English, Persian, Malay and Vietnamese. {cite:wikidata-quraish-game}
