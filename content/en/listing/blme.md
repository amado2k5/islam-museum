## Summary
BLME (Bank of London and The Middle East) is an independent British Sharia-compliant bank headquartered in London; as of 2013 it was the largest Islamic bank in Europe. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en} It is authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en}

## Founding
It was incorporated in London on 7 August 2006 as House of London and The Middle East and became Bank of London and The Middle East in July 2007. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en} Its founder and chief executive Humphrey Percy met the original sponsor, Kuwait's Boubyan Bank, with the shared aim of building a leading wholesale Islamic bank. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en} It received FSA authorisation in July 2007, and its second private placement in 2008 brought its capital base to £250 million. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en}

## Ownership
In 2020 Boubyan Bank acquired a majority stake in the bank's holding company; it held 72.37% as of 2024. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en}

## Sharia oversight and awards
From its foundation the bank has had a Sharia Supervisory Board reviewing its contracts and agreements, in addition to the conventional regulation of UK financial institutions. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en} Its awards include Islamic Finance News's Best Islamic Bank in Europe (2008) and Best Islamic Bank in the UK (2009 to 2013), and Global Finance's Best Islamic Bank in Europe in 2011, 2012 and 2013. {cite:wikipedia-blme-en}
