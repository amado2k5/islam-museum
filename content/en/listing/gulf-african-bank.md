## Summary
Gulf African Bank is a commercial bank in Kenya operating under an Islamic banking regime, licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya. {cite:wikipedia-gulf-african-bank-en} In December 2014 it was a mid-sized bank, 25th by assets among Kenya's 43 licensed banks. {cite:wikipedia-gulf-african-bank-en}

## Origins
Talks to create it began in 2005 among individuals and institutions from the Gulf and Kenya; it opened in 2008 after receiving a commercial licence and authorisation for Sharia banking from the central bank, the second commercial bank in Kenya so authorised after First Community Bank, which opened in 2007. {cite:wikipedia-gulf-african-bank-en}

## Ownership and branches
It is held by institutional and individual investors from the Gulf, Kenya and the United States; in September 2012 the International Finance Corporation took a 16% stake for US$5 million. {cite:wikipedia-gulf-african-bank-en} In August 2014 it had branches in Nairobi, Mombasa, Lamu, Malindi and Garissa. {cite:wikipedia-gulf-african-bank-en}
