## Overview
A café in the alleys of [[building/khan-al-khalili|Khan al-Khalili]] in [[place/cairo|Cairo]], opened around 1773; brass mirrors, a tea pot and wooden chairs on which readers and politicians have sat for two and a half centuries. {cite:wikidata}

## Significance
Among the oldest working cafés in the world; it was a meeting place of writers, and Naguib Mahfouz was among its regulars. {cite:wikidata}
