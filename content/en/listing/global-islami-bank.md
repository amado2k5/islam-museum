## Summary
Global Islami Bank PLC, formerly NRB Global Bank, is a fourth-generation Bangladeshi sharia-compliant private bank. {cite:wikipedia-global-islami-bank-en}

## Origins
It was founded on 25 July 2013 as NRB Global Bank for non-resident Bangladeshis, one of nine banks licensed in 2013. {cite:wikipedia-global-islami-bank-en} In 2020 it offered both conventional and sharia services, and on 3 January 2021 it became a sharia-compliant bank as Global Islami Bank. {cite:wikipedia-global-islami-bank-en}

## The former managing director's case
Prashanta Kumar Halder became its managing director in 2015; in August 2020 Bangladesh's Financial Intelligence Unit ordered his and his family's accounts frozen in an investigation of embezzlement from several institutions, and the Anti-Corruption Commission filed cases against him. {cite:wikipedia-global-islami-bank-en}

## After the conversion
In June 2021 the central bank approved its IPO plan, and in September 2021 it fined the bank for exceeding the stock-market investment ceiling. {cite:wikipedia-global-islami-bank-en}
