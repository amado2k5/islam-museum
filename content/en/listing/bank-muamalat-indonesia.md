## Summary
Bank Muamalat Indonesia is a commercial bank in Indonesia that operates according to the principles of Islamic banking. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-indonesia-en} It was founded on 1 November 1991 on the initiative of the Indonesian Council of Ulama (MUI), under the auspices of the Government of Indonesia, and began operating on 1 May 1992. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-indonesia-en}

## What it does
Its funding products use wadiah (deposit) and mudarabah (profit-sharing); its financing products use sale, musharakah, mudarabah and ijarah. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-indonesia-en} A Sharia Supervisory Board oversees it. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-indonesia-en}

## Ownership
Indonesia's Hajj Fund Management Agency (BPKH), a government agency, holds 82.65% of its shares, and the Islamic Development Bank 2.04%. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-indonesia-en} In 2022 the bank announced plans to list on the Indonesia Stock Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-indonesia-en}
