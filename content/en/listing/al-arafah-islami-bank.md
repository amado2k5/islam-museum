## Summary
Al-Arafah Islami Bank PLC is a Shariah-based Islamic bank in Bangladesh, founded on 27 September 1995 and headquartered in Motijheel, Dhaka; a shariah council ensures its activities meet shariah requirements. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en}

## Work
It runs an agricultural and rural microfinance project aimed at reducing poverty and creating jobs. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en} It signed an investment agreement with the Islamic Development Bank. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en}

## Management and cases
In May 2016 a vice-chairman of S. Alam Group was elected its chairman. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en} In June 2018 the Anti-Corruption Commission sued a director, its chairman from 2008 to 2016, for laundering money to Singapore. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en} In January 2023 it borrowed 750 million taka from Sonali Bank after a cash shortage. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en} After the fall of the Awami League government in 2024, Bangladesh Bank dissolved its board and appointed a new chairman and independent directors. {cite:wikipedia-al-arafah-islami-bank-en}
