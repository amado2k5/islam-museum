# Muslim Pro

## Summary

Muslim Pro is a mobile app for prayer times, the adhan, the qibla direction and the Qur'an, published by Bitsmedia Pte. Ltd. in Singapore. The company calls it "the digital home for all things Muslim" and says its downloads passed 190 million worldwide by Ramadan 2026. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us}

## Beginnings

The company tells how the idea took shape in 2009, to give accurate prayer times to Indonesia's young Muslims, and how modestly it began: five hundred downloads, only five of them from Indonesia, before it found its first following among Muslims in the United States and the United Kingdom, where prayer times were harder to come by. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us} It came out on iOS in 2010 and on Android in 2011, passed its first million downloads on 21 November 2011, and then spread through the Middle East, North Africa, Southeast Asia and South Asia. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us}

## Ownership and growth

In 2017, with 45 million downloads, it was acquired by Malaysia's Bintang Capital Partners and Singapore's CMIA Capital Partners. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us} In 2021 and 2022 it widened its content library with articles and infographics, and in 2022 it launched Qalbox inside the app, a streaming service for Islamic films, documentaries and children's programmes. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us} Later additions include Qur'an lessons, memorisation tools and an AI-powered Islamic chatbot called Ask AiDeen. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us} It is led today by Nafees Khundker as chief executive, with Ahmadul Hoq, who has worked on it since its beginning, as head of engineering. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us}

## The 2020 location-data investigation

On 16 November 2020 Joseph Cox published in Motherboard (Vice) an investigation into the US military's purchase of location data drawn from ordinary apps; Muslim Pro was the most popular app in the group the magazine analysed. Using network analysis, Motherboard observed the Android and iOS versions sending precise location data to the broker X-Mode, which sold data to clients that included US military contractors, and noted that the app's privacy policy did not mention X-Mode. {cite:vice-us-military-location-data-2020} The magazine said it knew of no specific military operation in which such data had been used, and that Muslim Pro had not answered requests for comment before publication; afterwards Muslim Pro said it would stop sharing data with X-Mode. {cite:vice-us-military-location-data-2020}

## Awards the company reports

The company says the app received the Islamic Economy Award (media category) at the Global Islamic Economy Summit in 2018 and a user-satisfaction award at Google's APAC App Summit in 2022; these are awards as the company reports them, not read in an independent source. {cite:muslim-pro-about-us}

## Reach

On 16 November 2020 Motherboard reported more than 50 million downloads on Android and over 98 million across platforms by the company's own site. {cite:vice-us-military-location-data-2020} On 6 November 2026 its Google Play page showed 100M+ downloads and a rating of 3.6 from about 1.92 million reviews. {cite:google-play-muslim-pro} The app has no article in any Wikipedia edition today: the English article was deleted in August 2022 after a deletion discussion. It has a Wikidata item. {cite:wikidata-muslim-pro}
