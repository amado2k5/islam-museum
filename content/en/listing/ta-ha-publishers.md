# Ta-Ha Publishers

## Summary
Ta-Ha Publishers is an Islamic publisher in London, founded in March 1980 by the husband-and-wife team Afsar Siddiqui and Dr Jamal-un-Nisa Rafai, among the pioneering generation of Islamic publishers in Britain. {cite:ta-ha-publishers-history}

## Founding
Both founders had successful careers, he as an artist and advertising director, she as an academic microbiologist, and chose to work together to further the understanding of Islam among English-speaking Muslims; the idea was born in the late 1970s. {cite:ta-ha-publishers-history} They took the name from the story of how [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] turned to Islam after hearing verses of Surat Ta-Ha. {cite:ta-ha-publishers-history}

## Growth
Work began in the back room of a North London print shop that served as office and warehouse; the first Ta-Ha booklet came out in 1980, and in 1984 the firm moved to Brixton in South London, where it stayed for 23 years and hosted start-ups, youth clubs and community gatherings. {cite:ta-ha-publishers-history} Afsar Siddiqui, who came to London in 1961 and ran the firm until he retired in 2022, set up the Islamic Publishers Association in 1994 to bring together Islamic publishers, booksellers and authors. {cite:ta-ha-publishers-history}

## Among its titles
In 2022 it published Tahir Mahmood Kiani's *The Easy Quran*, a simple-English translation aimed at young people and readers new to the Qur'an. {cite:gloqur-clear-and-easy-translations}
