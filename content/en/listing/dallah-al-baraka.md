## Summary
Dallah Albaraka Holding Company is a private multinational corporation based in Jeddah, best classed as a conglomerate, with investments in finance and banking, health care, real estate, manufacturing and transport across the Middle East, North Africa, the Far East and Europe. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en}

## Founding and growth
It began in 1969 as a courier service, Dallah Establishment, founded by Saleh Abdullah Kamel in Riyadh, was formed as a holding company in 1984, and today has investments in some 40 countries. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en} Its companies won government contracts including the cleaning and maintenance of the holy sites of Mecca and Medina and, in 1988, the running of Saudi air traffic control. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en} Its Al-Jazira Transport Company carries Hajj pilgrims and Umrah visitors. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en}

## Islamic banking and giving
Its major holdings include the [[listing/al-baraka-group|Al Baraka]] banking group, and in 2012 it signed a memorandum with the Islamic Development Bank and Qatar for an Islamic bank with US$1 billion in capital. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en} In 2015 its chief executive Abdallah Saleh Kamel gave US$10 million to Yale Law School for a center for the study of Islamic law and civilization. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en}

## Allegations and the courts
Parts of the company faced allegations of illicit financing; in 2005 Judge Casey of the US District Court for the Southern District of New York dismissed all claims against Al Baraka and Saleh Kamel. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en} Kamel was never charged and consistently said his company and philanthropy were committed to dialogue and inter-religious understanding. {cite:wikipedia-dallah-al-baraka-en}
