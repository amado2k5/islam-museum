## Summary
ICB Islamic Bank is a second-generation commercial Islamic bank in Bangladesh, listed on the Dhaka Stock Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-icb-islamic-bank-en}

## From Al-Baraka to Oriental
It was established in 1987, began operating on 20 May 1987 as Al-Baraka Bank, and was listed on the Dhaka Stock Exchange in 1990. {cite:wikipedia-icb-islamic-bank-en} By 1994 it had run into financial trouble, and in 2004 it was reorganised as Oriental Bank. {cite:wikipedia-icb-islamic-bank-en} In 2006 the central bank dissolved its board and auctioned most of its shares; the ICB Banking Group bought them and renamed it ICB Islamic Bank in 2008. {cite:wikipedia-icb-islamic-bank-en}

## The past's burdens
In 2014 the central bank rescheduled its repayments to Oriental Bank's depositors, and by 2018 it had not returned 4.44 billion taka to them. {cite:wikipedia-icb-islamic-bank-en} In January 2021 seven Oriental Bank officers were jailed for embezzlement, and its accumulated losses reached nearly 20 billion taka in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-icb-islamic-bank-en}
