# Turath Publishing

## Summary
Turath Publishing, whose name means "heritage", is a British publisher established in 1997 in South London, devoted to high-quality translations of classical Islamic works, contemporary writing and previously unpublished manuscripts. {cite:turath-publishing-about}

## Founding
It was founded by Yahya, the youngest son of Shaykh Muhammad ibn Ahmad Batha Bardoli (1920–2013), as a continuation of his father's work in scholarship and teaching, with the encouragement of [[person/muhammad-taqi-usmani|Muhammad Taqi Usmani]]. {cite:turath-publishing-about} It began with a five-year project to translate and publish the *Muwatta* of Imam Muhammad and now has over sixty titles. {cite:turath-publishing-about}

## Among its titles
In 2020 it published a luxury edition in British English of [[work/noble-quran-meanings-taqi-usmani|Muhammad Taqi Usmani's translation of the meanings of the Qur'an]], as *The Noble Qur'an: Meaning with Explanatory Notes*. {cite:gloqur-taqi-usmani-noble-quran}
