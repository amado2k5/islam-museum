## Summary
Takaful Malaysia is the first takaful operator in Malaysia; it is the holding company for family takaful, owns all of the general takaful company, and has two subsidiaries in Indonesia. {cite:wikipedia-takaful-malaysia-en}

## Founding
It was established on 29 November 1984, after the government set up a task force on an Islamic insurance company, in the year of the Takaful Act. {cite:wikipedia-takaful-malaysia-en} It became a public company on 19 October 1995, was listed on Bursa Malaysia on 30 July 1996, and split its composite licence into two entities on 1 June 2018 under the Islamic Financial Services Act 2013. {cite:wikipedia-takaful-malaysia-en}

## Ownership
At 31 March 2023 its substantial shareholders were Lembaga Tabung Haji (28.20%), the Employees Provident Fund (12.73%) and Kumpulan Wang Persaraan (6.88%). {cite:wikipedia-takaful-malaysia-en}
