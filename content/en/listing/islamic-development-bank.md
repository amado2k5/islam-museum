## Summary
The Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) is a multilateral development finance institution focused on Islamic finance for infrastructure, based in Jeddah, with 57 shareholding member states, Saudi Arabia the largest. {cite:wikipedia-islamic-development-bank-en}

## Founding
It was founded in 1973 by the finance ministers at the first conference of the Organisation of the Islamic Conference, with King Faisal's support, and began its activities on 3 April 1975. {cite:wikipedia-islamic-development-bank-en}

## Capital and ratings
On 22 May 2013 it tripled its authorised capital to US$150 billion, and it is rated AAA by the three major agencies. {cite:wikipedia-islamic-development-bank-en} In May 2024 its largest shareholders were Saudi Arabia (22.5%), Libya (9.03%), Indonesia (7.94%), Iran (7.90%) and Nigeria (7.33%). {cite:wikipedia-islamic-development-bank-en}

## The group and its projects
The article describes its group as five entities, among them the [[group/itfc|International Islamic Trade Finance Corporation]]. {cite:wikipedia-islamic-development-bank-en} Its projects include the Gao Bridge over the Niger in Mali. {cite:wikipedia-islamic-development-bank-en}
