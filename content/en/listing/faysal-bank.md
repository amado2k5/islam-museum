## Summary
Faysal Bank Limited is a Pakistani Islamic bank based in Karachi, named after Prince Mohammed bin Faisal Al Saud. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en} It had 900 branches in 2025, and Bahrain's Ithmaar Bank holds 66.78% of it. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en}

## Beginnings
It started in Pakistan in October 1987 with a small branch of Bahrain's Faysal Islamic Bank, owned by Prince Mohammed bin Faisal, within the Dar al-Maal al-Islami Trust founded in Geneva in 1981 to promote Islamic banking. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en} In 1995 its branches were incorporated as a conventional bank, Al-Faysal Investment Bank, and listed on the Karachi Stock Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en}

## Losing and regaining Shariah status
In 2002 the Islamic investment bank merged with the conventional bank, losing its Shariah-compliant status, and deposits fell 23% in a year. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en} In 2010 it bought RBS Pakistan. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en} In 2014 it announced a full conversion to Islamic banking, completed in December 2022 with an Islamic banking licence. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en}

## Controversies
In the mid-2000s its head of investment banking was charged with insider trading in New York. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en} The State Bank of Pakistan fined it in July 2020 and October 2022 for regulatory and operational violations. {cite:wikipedia-faysal-bank-en}
