## Summary
Noor Bank, formerly Noor Islamic Bank, was a full-service Shari'a-compliant bank established in Dubai in January 2008, whose products and services were governed by a Shari'a Board of scholars of law and finance. {cite:wikipedia-noor-bank-en}

## Course
Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum opened it to the public on 7 January 2008; it was a subsidiary of Noor Investment Group and a sister company of Noor Takaful. {cite:wikipedia-noor-bank-en} In May 2008 it launched the UAE's first round-the-clock branch, followed by one at Dubai Airport. {cite:wikipedia-noor-bank-en} It returned to operating profit in 2011 after a loss in the first half of 2010, and in 2013 partnered with Emaar to offer mortgages to non-residents. {cite:wikipedia-noor-bank-en} On 7 January 2014 it dropped "Islamic" from its name in a rebranding. {cite:wikipedia-noor-bank-en}

## End
[[listing/dib|Dubai Islamic Bank]] formally acquired it in December 2019. {cite:wikipedia-noor-bank-en}
