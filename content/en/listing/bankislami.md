## Summary
BankIslami Pakistan is a Shariah-compliant Islamic bank in Karachi; in 2025 it had more than 550 branches, and the State Bank of Pakistan's 2024 report listed it among the top three Islamic banks by assets and deposits. {cite:wikipedia-bankislami-en}

## Founding and acquisitions
It was incorporated in 2005 and began operating on 7 April 2006 under an Islamic banking licence of the 2003 policy. {cite:wikipedia-bankislami-en} In May 2015 it acquired KASB Bank after the State Bank intervened to protect depositors, and in August 2023 JS Bank raised its holding to 75.12%, making it part of JS Group. {cite:wikipedia-bankislami-en}

## Sharia oversight
An independent Shariah Supervisory Board oversees it, and in 2025 it launched the Aik digital banking platform. {cite:wikipedia-bankislami-en}
