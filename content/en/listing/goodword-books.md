# Goodword Books

## Summary
Goodword is an Indian publisher in [[place/delhi|Delhi]] devoted above all to illustrated children's books on Islamic and moral values; it also publishes books for adults on history, religion, philosophy, da'wa and Arabic learning. {cite:goodword-books-about}

## Founding
Its roots lie in the Islamic Centre the scholar Maulana Wahiduddin Khan founded in Delhi in 1976; his son Saniyasnain Khan founded Goodword Books in 1996 to meet the need for good children's books. {cite:goodword-books-about}

## What it publishes
Its children's books combine short texts with illustrations on themes drawn from the Qur'an and other Islamic sources; Saniyasnain Khan's own children's books have been translated into many languages, and the publisher reports that his *The Story of Khadija* received the Sharjah Children's Book Award. {cite:goodword-books-about} Goodword also issued an edition of [[work/quran-first-american-version-irving|T. B. Irving's American translation of the Qur'an]] before its founder brought out his own translation. {cite:gloqur-irving-first-american-version}
