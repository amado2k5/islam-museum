## Summary
The Al-Amanah Islamic Investment Bank of the Philippines is the first and only Islamic bank in the Philippines, headquartered in Zamboanga City. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en}

## Origins
It traces its roots to the Philippine Amanah Bank, set up by President Ferdinand Marcos in 1973 under Presidential Decree No. 264 with ₱100 million in capital, one of the world's first Islamic banks. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en} Its original charter mandated it to serve provinces in Mindanao and elsewhere with large or predominant Muslim populations. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en} In 1974 Presidential Decree No. 542 amended the charter, adding provinces and requiring it to bank according to Islamic principles, which the original charter had not stated explicitly. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en}

## Re-chartering and ownership
In 1989 it was re-chartered with ₱1 billion in capital under Republic Act No. 6848 and took its present name; from 1990 to 2007 it was supervised by the Bureau of the Treasury. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en} In 2008 it was sold to the state-owned Development Bank of the Philippines, which said in 2012 it meant to divest because it lacked the expertise to run an Islamic institution. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en} On 3 September 2024 the Intergovernmental Fiscal Policy Board approved the purchase of 80% of the bank by the government of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en}

## Services
In 2015 it opened its first branch outside Mindanao, in Malabon, Metro Manila; in 2016 it was licensed for e-banking; and in 2018 it launched an Islamic pawnshop offering short-term financing without interest. {cite:wikipedia-al-amanah-islamic-bank-en}
