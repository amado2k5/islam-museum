## Summary
Wahed is an American financial technology and services company based in New York City. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en} In July 2019 it launched the first Sharia-compliant exchange-traded fund in the United States; it operates in 130 countries, and Bloomberg put its valuation at US$300 million in 2022. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en}

## Origins and growth
In 2017 it launched the world's first automated Islamic investment platform, and it expanded to the UK in August 2018. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en} In July 2019 it launched the Wahed FTSE USA Shariah ETF (HLAL); in July 2020 it raised US$25 million in a round led by Saudi Aramco Entrepreneurship Ventures; in December 2020 it bought Niyah, a British company running a digital banking app designed for Muslims. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en}

## Regulation
It is regulated in the United States, the UK, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Mauritius and South Africa, and an ethical review board monitors its investments for agreement with Islamic values. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en} On 10 February 2022 the US Securities and Exchange Commission charged it with misleading statements and compliance failures related to its Shari'ah advisory business. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en}

## Leadership
Its founder Junaid Wahedna stepped down as chief executive in March 2024 and was succeeded by Mohsin Siddiqui. {cite:wikipedia-wahed-en}
