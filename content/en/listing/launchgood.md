## Summary
LaunchGood is a crowdfunding platform focused on the Muslim community worldwide, headquartered in Detroit, Michigan. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en} It went live in October 2013 and has raised more than US$688 million from 2.1 million donors in 155 countries. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en} Outlets including TRT World, Doha News and The National describe it as Muslim-run. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en}

## Founders
Its founders are Chris Blauvelt, Amany Killawi and Omar Hamid. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en}

## Notable campaigns
In 2015 more than US$100,000 was raised on it to rebuild African-American churches destroyed by arson, and a similar sum for Adnan Syed's legal team. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en} In October 2018 a campaign for the burials of the Pittsburgh synagogue victims and the survivors' medical bills reached its goal in six hours and passed US$200,000. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en}

## Awards
It received the Islamic Economy Award in the SME category. {cite:wikipedia-launchgood-en}
