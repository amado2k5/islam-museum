## Overview
The café of the Grand Mosque of Paris in the mosque gardens, founded in 1926; mint tea and pastries are served on brass tables under the mosque's trees. {cite:wikidata}

## Significance
One of the best-known traditional cafés of Paris; it links the experience of the French mosque with the hospitality of the Arab café. {cite:wikidata}
