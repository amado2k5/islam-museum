## Summary
Bank Makramah Limited, formerly Summit Bank, is a Pakistani Islamic bank based in Karachi. {cite:wikipedia-bank-makramah-en}

## From Rupali to Summit
Its origins lie in Rupali Bank's Pakistan operations, which Arif Habib Securities acquired in 2006; the resulting bank began operating on 5 August 2006 and was listed on the Karachi Stock Exchange in 2008. {cite:wikipedia-bank-makramah-en} In 2010 Suroor Investments bought 59.41% and renamed it Summit Bank; Atlas Bank and MyBank were later merged in, taking its network past 193 branches. {cite:wikipedia-bank-makramah-en}

## Becoming an Islamic bank
In November 2023 Summit Bank was converted into an Islamic bank and renamed Bank Makramah. {cite:wikipedia-bank-makramah-en} The Dubai businessman Nasser Abdullah Hussain Lootah had acquired a majority stake in April 2023. {cite:wikipedia-bank-makramah-en}
