## Summary
The Dow Jones Islamic Market Index (DJIM) is a stock-market index created for investors seeking investments under Islamic finance, operated by S&P Dow Jones Indices. {cite:wikipedia-djim-en}

## Sharia screening
An independent Shariah Supervisory Board of scholars and experts reviews companies' financial statements and decides whether they comply. {cite:wikipedia-djim-en} The article describes two screens: the first removes industries including alcohol, pork, conventional finance, gambling, tobacco and weapons; the second uses financial ratios to remove companies with high debt or interest income. {cite:wikipedia-djim-en}

## Origins
It was launched in 1999; the article says in Bahrain, as the first index of its kind, and that AAOIFI adopted its screens in Standard 21. {cite:wikipedia-djim-en}
