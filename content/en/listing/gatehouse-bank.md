## Summary
Gatehouse Bank PLC is a UK-regulated Islamic bank headquartered in London, with offices in Birmingham, Milton Keynes and Wilmslow. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en} It was founded in 2007, is a subsidiary of Gatehouse Financial Group, and is authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the PRA and the Financial Conduct Authority. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en}

## Working under Shariah
It conducts its business according to the Shariah principles of transparent, fair and socially responsible banking, including the equitable sharing of risk and reward, and invests nothing in arms, alcohol, tobacco or adult entertainment. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en} In 2019 it was a founding signatory of the UN Principles for Responsible Banking. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en}

## Products
It offers Shariah-compliant home purchase plans as an alternative to mortgages, and savings accounts, among them the Woodland Saver accounts launched in 2021, for each of which it plants a tree in a certified UK woodland project. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en} In 2022 it launched green home finance for energy-efficient homes. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en}

## Recent strategy
In December 2024 it partnered with ColCap to originate more than £550 million of Shariah-compliant home finance; in August 2026 it sold the economic interest in a £405 million home purchase plan portfolio; and in 2026 it sold its Gatehouse Living Group to Apollo funds. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en}

## Awards
Islamic Finance News named it Best Islamic Bank in the United Kingdom in 2020, 2022 and 2024. {cite:wikipedia-gatehouse-bank-en}
