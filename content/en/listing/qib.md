## Summary
Qatar Islamic Bank (QIB) is a Qatari bank based in Doha, founded in 1982, whose products and operations are supervised by a Shari'a board that checks its adherence to Islamic banking principles. {cite:wikipedia-qatar-islamic-bank-en} {cite:wikidata}

## Growth
By 2005 it had eight branches and its paid-up capital had risen to QR 663 million. {cite:wikipedia-qatar-islamic-bank-en} In February 2018 it sold its entire 60% stake in Malaysia's Asian Finance Bank to Malaysian Building Society Bhd (MBSB). {cite:wikipedia-qatar-islamic-bank-en}

## Ownership
The Qatar Investment Authority is its single largest shareholder; the rest is held by Qatari individuals, families and institutions, and its shares are listed on the Qatar Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-qatar-islamic-bank-en}

## Rankings
It was 15th on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s 30 Most Valuable Banks 2025 and 32nd on its Top 100 Listed Companies 2025. {cite:wikipedia-qatar-islamic-bank-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}
