## Summary
First Security Islami Bank PLC is a public limited bank in Bangladesh, founded on 29 August 1999, which provides sharia-compliant banking. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en}

## Origins
It was incorporated as a commercial bank on 29 August 1999, began operating on 25 October 1999 with authorised capital of one billion taka, and started sharia banking in 2009. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en}

## Ownership and crisis
Mohammed Saiful Alam, chairman of S. Alam Group, chaired its board. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en} It faced a cash crunch in 2022 after approving large unsecured loans. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en} On 4 December 2022 the High Court ordered investigations into it and two other banks the group controlled; Bangladesh Bank appointed observers on 13 December, and on 29 December 2022 it and three other banks took emergency loans. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en} S. Alam Group filed a Digital Security Act case against an insurance executive who had accused it of embezzling from its banks. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en}

## Charity
It provides scholarships for students and has established a charity hospital and a school. {cite:wikipedia-first-security-islami-bank-en}
