## Summary
Al Hilal Bank is an Islamic bank established in 2007 and headquartered in Abu Dhabi, UAE, offering Shari'ah-compliant retail banking and wealth management. {cite:wikipedia-al-hilal-bank-en}

## Ownership
ADCB Group acquired it in 2019. {cite:wikipedia-al-hilal-bank-en}
