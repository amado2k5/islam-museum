## Summary
Kuwait International Bank (KIB) is an Islamic bank in Kuwait City offering Islamic banking to individuals and companies, regulated by the Central Bank of Kuwait. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-international-bank-en}

## Course
It was incorporated in 1973 as Kuwait Real Estate Bank, listed in 1984, and in 2007 completed its transition to full Sharia compliance under its present name. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-international-bank-en}
