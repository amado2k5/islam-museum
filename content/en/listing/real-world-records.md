## Summary
Real World Records is a British record label specialising in world music, founded in 1989 by the English musician Peter Gabriel with the original members of WOMAD, based at Box in Wiltshire; most of its releases are recorded at Real World Studios, founded the same year. {cite:wikipedia-real-world-records-en} {cite:wikidata-real-world-records}

## Its aims
The label set out to give musicians from around the world access to modern studios and audiences beyond their regions, and to turn the musical relationships formed at WOMAD festivals into new recordings; its first five albums included Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan's Shahen-Shah. {cite:wikipedia-real-world-records-en} By 1999 it had sold over 3 million records across 90 albums, and by 2015 it had passed 200 albums. {cite:wikipedia-real-world-records-en}

## In the museum
