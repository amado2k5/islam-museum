## Summary
TAJBank Limited is Nigeria's second non-interest bank, operating under Islamic banking principles and headquartered in Abuja, the capital. {cite:wikipedia-tajbank-en}

## Origins
It was founded in 2019; on 3 July 2019 it received a Central Bank of Nigeria licence to operate as a regional bank, and on 2 December 2019 it began business with offices and branches in Abuja. {cite:wikipedia-tajbank-en} It opened its second branch, in Kano, on 12 December 2019 as it expanded to other cities. {cite:wikipedia-tajbank-en}

## Sukuk
In 2020 it took part in announcing an issue of N150 billion in Nigerian government sukuk. {cite:wikipedia-tajbank-en}
