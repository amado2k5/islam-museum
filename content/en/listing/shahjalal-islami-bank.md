## Summary
Shahjalal Islami Bank PLC is a shariah-compliant private commercial bank headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. {cite:wikipedia-shahjalal-islami-bank-en} It was incorporated on 10 May 2001, and the Shahjalal Islami Bank Foundation is its corporate social responsibility arm. {cite:wikipedia-shahjalal-islami-bank-en}

## Management
Bangladeshi business figures have chaired its board in turn, among them Anwer Hossain Khan in 2011 and A. K. Azad of Ha-Meem Group in 2015, who was elected again in February 2024 and re-elected in January 2025. {cite:wikipedia-shahjalal-islami-bank-en}

## Cases
In 2012 the Anti-Corruption Commission sued a former vice-president and five others for embezzling 131.7 million taka, and in 2014 it arrested a director and former chairman on charges of embezzling 1.4 billion taka through bad loans. {cite:wikipedia-shahjalal-islami-bank-en} In 2015 and 2016 it charged borrowers and a former deputy managing director with embezzlement through fake letters of credit. {cite:wikipedia-shahjalal-islami-bank-en} The Daily Star reported that Prashanta Kumar Halder used the bank to launder money from his embezzlement. {cite:wikipedia-shahjalal-islami-bank-en}
