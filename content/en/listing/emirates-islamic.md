## Summary
Emirates Islamic, formerly Emirates Islamic Bank, is one of the four Islamic banks in Dubai; it was established in 2004 to provide banking in line with Shari'a principles to individuals, small businesses and large corporations. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en} By 2025 it had more than 860,000 bank accounts. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en}

## History
In March 2019 it received Islamic Finance News's "Most Innovative Islamic Bank" award, and in October 2019 it was named "Best Islamic Bank in the U.A.E." at the Global Industry Awards. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en} In April 2019 it launched banking via WhatsApp, saying it was the first Islamic bank to do so. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en} In December 2024 it operated 40 branches in the UAE. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en}

## Ownership and listing
In June 2025 it announced its delisting from the Dubai Financial Market after Emirates NBD acquired all its remaining shares. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en} It was 41st on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s Top 100 Listed Companies 2025. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}

## Today
In May 2025 Euromoney named it the world's best Islamic digital bank for that year. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en} In April 2026 it launched the UAE's first digital service for investing in certified physical gold and silver bars through a mobile app. {cite:wikipedia-emirates-islamic-en}
