## Summary
Amana Bank is a commercial bank in Tanzania, licensed by the Bank of Tanzania, the central bank and national regulator. {cite:wikipedia-amana-bank-tanzania-en} Its headquarters are in the Golden Jubilee Tower in central Dar es Salaam. {cite:wikipedia-amana-bank-tanzania-en}

## Origins
It was established in 2011 with paid-up capital of TSh 27 billion as the first fully Sharia-compliant bank in Tanzania; talks to set it up began in October 2009 among prominent Tanzanian business figures. {cite:wikipedia-amana-bank-tanzania-en}

## Branches
In September 2018 it had branches in Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Mwanza, Tanga and Zanzibar, with branches in Dodoma and Pemba in development. {cite:wikipedia-amana-bank-tanzania-en}
