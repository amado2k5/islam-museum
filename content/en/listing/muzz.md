# Muzz (formerly Muzmatch)

## Summary

Muzz, known until 2022 as Muzmatch, is a matchmaking app for Muslims looking for marriage, founded by [[person/shahzad-younas|Shahzad Younas]] in 2015 and based in Aldgate, London. It was designed to help single Muslims find a marriage partner within their religious and cultural norms. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en}

## Beginnings

In 2014 Younas left a nine-year banking career at Morgan Stanley, taught himself to code, put his savings into the business and built the first version of the app himself. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} The English article also cites a Manchester Evening News item of July 2011 on a "muzmatch matrimonial website", which worked pay-as-you-go and kept members' photographs blurred until someone paid to see the full profile. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en}

## Growth

The app reached one million users in 2016 and that year introduced selfie verification, with a moderation team in Bangladesh checking every profile by hand alongside the London office. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} In the summer of 2017 it joined the Silicon Valley accelerator Y Combinator; it raised $1.75 million in a seed round that year and reported 200,000 members in 160 countries. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} In July 2019 it raised $7 million in a Series A round and reported more than 1.5 million users; in July 2020 three million; and during the COVID-19 pandemic it added free in-app video calls. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} Among its features is a "chaperone" option that lets a third party see the conversations. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en}

## The name case

Match Group, owner of Match.com, sued Muzmatch in February 2021, arguing that the word "match" in its name let it profit unfairly from the reputation of its own site; Match Group had reportedly tried to buy Muzmatch for up to $35 million in 2019. In April 2022 the British court ruled for Match Group, and the app renamed itself Muzz and relaunched in May 2022. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en}

## What was said about it

Reviews were mixed: many users value its respect for Islamic values, while others, Mozilla's privacy guide among them, raised concerns about privacy, moderation and how well its features work. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} Its advertising drew attention in London, Manchester and Birmingham, notably the "Find Malik a wife" billboards of January 2022, widely reported before their link to the app was revealed. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} In 2019 it was shortlisted for App of the Year at the Evening Standard Business Awards and was a finalist in The Spectator's business awards. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en}

## Reach

In 2024 the company reported more than ten million members, more than 500,000 success stories and a staff of 110. {cite:wikipedia-muzz-en} On 6 November 2026 its Google Play page showed 10M+ downloads and a rating of 4.2 from about 356,000 reviews. {cite:google-play-muzz} The figures are the company's and the store's, not an independent count.

## Today

The app is active at muzz.com and in the Apple and Google stores, and has articles in the English and Arabic Wikipedias. {cite:wikidata-muzz}
