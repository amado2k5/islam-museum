## Summary
Alinma Bank is a Saudi joint-stock company formed under Royal Decree No. M/15 of 28 March 2006 and Ministerial Resolution No. 42 of 27 March 2007, with share capital of SAR 20 billion in 1.5 billion shares of SAR 10 each. {cite:wikipedia-alinma-bank-en} Its name means "growth" or "development" in Arabic. {cite:wikipedia-alinma-bank-en}

## What it does
It offers a range of Shariah-compliant retail, corporate and investment banking services, and had more than 2,658 employees. {cite:wikipedia-alinma-bank-en}

## Rankings
It was 10th on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s 30 Most Valuable Banks 2025 and 25th on its Top 100 Listed Companies 2025. {cite:wikipedia-alinma-bank-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}
