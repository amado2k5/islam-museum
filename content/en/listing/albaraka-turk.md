## Summary
Albaraka Türk is a Turkish participation bank founded in 1984 in Istanbul, active from 1985, and known as the first participation bank in Turkey. {cite:wikipedia-albaraka-turk-en} It was founded with the backing of Al Baraka Group, the Islamic Development Bank and a Turkish industrial group, and operates under Turkey's Banking Law No. 5411. {cite:wikipedia-albaraka-turk-en}

## What it does
With Anadolu Hayat it announced private pension plans invested in Sharia-compliant, interest-free instruments, and it holds Islamic licences for profit-and-loss partnership, consumer finance and credit cards. {cite:wikipedia-albaraka-turk-en}

## Ownership
At 30 September 2010 Al Baraka Group held 54.06%, the Islamic Development Bank 7.84% and the public 22.47%. {cite:wikipedia-albaraka-turk-en}
