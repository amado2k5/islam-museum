## Summary
Al Baraka Group B.S.C. is a Bahraini international Islamic financial group providing retail, corporate, treasury and investment banking through banking subsidiaries in 13 countries. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} It is licensed by the Central Bank of Bahrain as an Investment Business Firm – Category 1 (Islamic Principles) and is headquartered in Manama. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en}

## Founding and course
The group was registered on 27 June 2002 under a licence from the Central Bank of Bahrain. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} In 2006 it held an initial public offering raising more than US$630 million and listed on the Bahrain Bourse and Nasdaq Dubai, with trading from September that year; in 2017 it issued US$400 million of Tier-1 sukuk listed on the Irish Stock Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} In June 2020 Abdullah Saleh Kamel became chairman, succeeding his father, Saleh Abdullah Kamel. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} In July 2022 it changed its name from Al Baraka Banking Group to Al Baraka Group after its licence was converted from a wholesale bank to an investment firm. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} Shareholders approved delisting and conversion to a closed company in November 2023, and the shares left the Bahrain Bourse in July 2024. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en}

## Units around the world
Its units have operated in Algeria since 1991, Egypt since 1980, South Africa since 1989, Sudan since 1985, Tunisia since 1983, Lebanon since 1992 and Syria, as a joint-stock company, since 2007. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} In Jordan it operates as Jordan Islamic Bank, its first international operation, since 1978, and in Turkey as Albaraka Türk. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en} On 19 November 2022 the Central Bank of Lebanon took control of Al Baraka Bank Lebanon, stripping its board and executive management of their powers. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en}

## Figures
In 2025 the group had 690 locations, 11,074 employees and total assets of about US$31 billion. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-group-en}
