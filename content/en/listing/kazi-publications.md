# Kazi Publications

## Summary
Kazi Publications is an Islamic publisher and distributor in Chicago that describes itself as the oldest in North America; its titles are found in libraries, university bookstores and major American chain stores. {cite:kazi-publications-about} {cite:thenational-kazi-publications}

## Beginnings
Its seeds were planted around 1970, when Liaquat Ali and his brother-in-law began importing books and handicrafts from Pakistan to sell in their friend Mahmoud Kazi's Chicago shop; in 1972 Ali returned to Chicago to run the shop with Kazi, the two turned to books, and Kazi Publications was born. {cite:thenational-kazi-publications} Its first published book, in 1974, was *Book of Muslim Names*, because many local converts wanted to name their children. {cite:thenational-kazi-publications} The first shop later burned down, and after several moves the firm settled on Belmont Avenue in 1984. {cite:thenational-kazi-publications}

## What it has published
In 1977 it published the first edition of the [[work/noble-quran-hilali-khan|Hilali–Khan]] translation of the meanings of the Qur'an. {cite:gloqur-hilali-khan-part-1} Laleh Bakhtiar joined as in-house scholar and production manager in 1992, and Kazi published her [[work/sublime-quran-bakhtiar|Sublime Quran]] in early 2007. {cite:thenational-kazi-publications} One of its most successful programmes is the Great Books of the Islamic World series of new translations of classic works; its authors include Seyyed Hossein Nasr, Gai Eaton and Sulayman Nyang. {cite:kazi-publications-about}
