## Summary
Standard Islami Bank PLC, formerly Standard Bank PLC, is a private commercial bank in Bangladesh. {cite:wikipedia-standard-islami-bank-en}

## Origins
It was founded on 3 June 1999 with approved capital of 750 million taka; Kazi Akram Uddin Ahmed was its founding chairman. {cite:wikipedia-standard-islami-bank-en}

## Turning to Islamic banking
The bank decided to switch from conventional to Shariah-compliant banking and closed for four days in December 2020 to make the change. {cite:wikipedia-standard-islami-bank-en} In February 2026 it rebranded as Standard Islami Bank. {cite:wikipedia-standard-islami-bank-en}

## Cases
In February 2021 the Anti-Corruption Commission sued a former managing director and seven others for misappropriating 40 million taka from the bank; a Dhaka judge sent him to jail in October 2022. {cite:wikipedia-standard-islami-bank-en}
