## Summary
Awakening Music, formerly Awakening Records, is an England-based record label and music production company founded in 2000 by four entrepreneurs from England and the United States, named by the English article as Muhammad Wali-ur Rahman, Sharif H. Banna, Bara Kherigi and Wassim Malak. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en} {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-ar} {cite:wikidata-awakening-music} It has offices in London, Cairo and California. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en}

## Beginnings
The Guardian reported in 2012 that Awakening began by publishing books before it saw a gap in the music market, and gave its UK head office as Swansea. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012} The co-founder Wali-ur Rahman told the paper that the company wanted to match Western production standards, since most nasheeds before it were cheaply produced, and that its slogan was "faith-inspired and value-driven". {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012} It signed its first artist, [[person/sami-yusuf|Sami Yusuf]], in 2003; he left after a dispute in 2008 over his third album and joined ETM International, and the label then signed [[person/maher-zain|Maher Zain]]. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012} The paper notes that music is a contested matter in Islamic jurisprudence, with many Muslims holding it prohibited, and quotes Rahman as saying acceptance had grown since the label began. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012}

## Ownership
Both articles place it within the Deventi Group; the Arabic article adds that B4U Productions took a majority stake in 2018 and that the company had fifty staff in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-ar} {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en} The Arabic article reports, from the company's site, an award as the world's best Islamic media company at an international business summit held in Dubai with Deloitte; it gives no year, so it stays here as an attributed report. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-ar}

## Releases
The label is known for launching the careers of [[person/sami-yusuf|Sami Yusuf]], [[person/maher-zain|Maher Zain]], [[person/masud-kurtis|Mesut Kurtis]] and Harris J. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en} Its albums include Sami Yusuf's Al-Mu'allim (2003), Salawat by Mesut Kurtis and Sami Yusuf (2004), [[work/my-ummah-sami-yusuf|My Ummah]] (2005), Nazeel Azami's Dunya (2006), [[person/hamza-namira|Hamza Namira]]'s Ehlam Ma'aya (2008), Maher Zain's [[work/thank-you-allah-maher-zain|Thank You Allah]] (2009) and [[work/forgive-me-maher-zain|Forgive Me]] (2012), Humood AlKhudher's Aseer Ahsan and Harris J's Salam (2015), Maher Zain's One (2016) and, most recently, his Back To You (2025). {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en} The two articles differ on some years; the Arabic one dates Ehlam Ma'aya to 2009. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-ar} Its current artists, by the English article, include Humood AlKhudher, Raef and Jaqlyn; former ones include Ali Magrebi, Hamza Namira, Hamza Robertson, Irfan Makki, Nazeel Azami and Sami Yusuf. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en}

## Charity concerts
In 2012 the label took part in the "Sound of Light" charity concerts, and in 2013 and 2014 it helped organise charity concerts with Islamic Relief, Human Appeal and others, with Maher Zain, Hamza Namira, Mesut Kurtis, Raef and Irfan Makki. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en} {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-ar}
