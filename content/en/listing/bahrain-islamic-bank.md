## Summary
Bahrain Islamic Bank is a commercial bank based in Manama, founded on 7 March 1979; it began operating at the start of the Hijri year 1400, on 2 Muharram, which was 22 November 1979. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en} It is the first Islamic bank in the Kingdom of Bahrain, works under the supervision of the Central Bank of Bahrain and is listed on the Bahrain Bourse. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en}

## Course
In January 2016 Moody's confirmed its ratings and upgraded its baseline credit assessment. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en} In 2017, under a five-year growth plan, it set out to sell about 82 million dinars of unproductive assets, including land and shares, and sold 14 million dinars' worth in the first six months. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en} In 2019 the National Bank of Bahrain, already a shareholder, offered to buy its remaining shares at a valuation of 124 million dinars. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en} It signed a cooperation agreement with STC Bahrain on initiatives for small and medium enterprises, and one with Delmon Gate to finance the Bushra housing project. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en}

## A fatwa platform
In 2023 the bank announced plans for the first AI platform for Islamic banking fatwas, drawing on more than 1,800 fatwas and Sharia decisions issued by its Sharia Supervisory Board. {cite:wikipedia-bahrain-islamic-bank-en}
