## Summary
Kuveyt Türk Katılım Bankası AŞ is a Turkish joint-stock participation bank, founded in 1989 as a Special Finance House under Turkish Council of Ministers Decision No. 831/7506 of 16 December 1983. {cite:wikipedia-kuveyt-turk-tr} It is the Turkish arm of [[listing/kfh|Kuwait Finance House]] and has branches in Germany. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en}

## Legal framework
Special finance houses were governed by the cabinet decision and central-bank notices until they came under the Banking Law in 1999; from December 1999 the bank has been subject to Law No. 4389, and in May 2006 it took its present name. {cite:wikipedia-kuveyt-turk-tr}

## Ownership
Kuwait Finance House holds 57.81%, Turkey's General Directorate of Foundations 24.49% and the Islamic Development Bank 8.36%. {cite:wikipedia-kuveyt-turk-tr}
