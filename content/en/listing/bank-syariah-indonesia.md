## Summary
PT Bank Syariah Indonesia (BSI) is a state-owned Islamic bank based in Jakarta, officially founded on 1 February 2021 through the merger of the state banks' sharia subsidiaries. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en} As of June 2024 it was the world's largest Islamic bank by customer numbers. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en}

## The merger
Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) granted the merger permit on 27 January 2021, with BRI Syariah as the surviving company. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en} BSI came from three banks: BRI Syariah of Bank Rakyat Indonesia, Bank Syariah Mandiri of Bank Mandiri, and BNI Syariah of Bank Negara Indonesia. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en} BRI Syariah had begun operating as a sharia bank on 17 November 2008 and was listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange in 2018. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en}

## From subsidiary to state enterprise
An extraordinary general meeting on 22 December 2025, after the government's special rights were written into its ownership, changed BSI from a subsidiary of Bank Mandiri into a state-owned Persero; its name became PT Bank Syariah Indonesia (Persero) Tbk, effective 23 January 2026. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en} The Government of Indonesia is its ultimate controlling shareholder through the three state banks. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en}

## Abroad
It opened a representative office in the Dubai International Financial Centre in January 2022 and is seeking to expand in the Middle East. {cite:wikipedia-bank-syariah-indonesia-en}
