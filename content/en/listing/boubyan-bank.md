## Summary
Boubyan Bank is a publicly listed Islamic bank headquartered in Kuwait City, founded in 2004 after the enactment of Kuwait's Islamic Banks Law, the first Islamic bank established under that framework. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} The National Bank of Kuwait is its major shareholder. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en}

## Founding
The Islamic Banks Law No. 33 of 2003 paved the way for its establishment by Amiri Decree No. 88 on 21 September 2004. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} It received its licence from the Central Bank of Kuwait in November 2004 and began operating the following year. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} It was listed on the Kuwait Stock Exchange, now Boursa Kuwait, on 15 May 2006. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en}

## Ownership and expansion
In 2009 the National Bank of Kuwait acquired 47.29% of Boubyan Bank and became its largest shareholder; it held 60.4% in 2025. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} The bank's international expansion began in 2020 with a majority stake in the UK's Bank of London and the Middle East. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en}

## What it does
It operates in four segments, all within Sharia principles: consumer banking; corporate banking with murabaha financing and ijarah leasing; investment banking; and treasury. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} In 2018 it introduced Musaed, an AI customer-service tool answering in Arabic and English. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} Its group holds 72.37% of the Bank of London and the Middle East, 98.98% of Boubyan Takaful and all of Boubyan Capital. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} A Shariah Committee monitors and approves its activities. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en}

## Figures and recognition
The article gives its 2025 net profit as KD 110.5 million, while the headline of the Zawya report it cites gives KD 100.5 million; both are recorded as given. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en} Global Finance has recognised its Islamic digital banking and its SME banking in Kuwait. {cite:wikipedia-boubyan-bank-en}
