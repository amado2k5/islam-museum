## Summary
Dubai Islamic Bank (DIB) is a major Emirati bank headquartered in Dubai, established in 1975 by Saeed bin Ahmed Lootah; it is the country's second-largest lender. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} It follows Sharia principles across all its activities, is the largest Islamic bank in the UAE by assets, and is a public joint-stock company listed on the Dubai Financial Market. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en}

## Founding and growth
The bank was founded in 1975 to offer Islamic banking services. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} In January 2020 it acquired Noor Bank. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} Its activities cover retail, corporate and SME banking, trade finance, treasury services and investment banking. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en}

## International presence and subsidiaries
It owns Bosna Bank International, an Islamic bank founded in Sarajevo in 2000; holds a minority stake in Panin Dubai Syariah Bank in Indonesia; is licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya; has had a wholly owned subsidiary in Pakistan since 2006; and holds a strategic stake in Sudan's Bank of Khartoum. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} Its subsidiaries include Dar Al Sharia, a Sharia legal and financial advisory firm founded in 2008, and the real-estate developer Deyaar. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en}

## Rankings
It was 13th on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s 30 Most Valuable Banks 2025 and 22nd on its Top 100 Listed Companies 2025. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}

## The Ryan Cornelius case
The bank is party to a long-running dispute concerning the British businessman Ryan Cornelius, imprisoned in Dubai since 2008. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} The UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention, in its 2022 opinion, found his detention arbitrary, recorded that the first judge had been unable to convict on the evidence, and noted that his sentence was extended by twenty years in 2018 at a closed hearing; it called for his release. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} The European Parliament, on 10 July 2025, described the charges as false fraud charges related to a loan from the bank, and seven UN experts renewed the call for his release in July 2026. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} In March 2025 British parliamentarians urged sanctions on bank officials, among them its chairman Mohammed Ibrahim Al Shaibani, who resigned from the board in September 2026. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en} The bank rejects responsibility for Cornelius's imprisonment and says sentencing is a matter for the UAE judiciary. {cite:wikipedia-dubai-islamic-bank-en}
