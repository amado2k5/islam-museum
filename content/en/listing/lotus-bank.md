## Summary
LOTUS Bank is a non-interest and ethical bank in Nigeria. {cite:wikipedia-lotus-bank-en} It is the third non-interest bank established in the country and the first to begin operating from the south, with its headquarters in Lagos. {cite:wikipedia-lotus-bank-en}

## Origins
It was founded in 2021; on 17 June 2021 the Central Bank of Nigeria licensed it as a non-interest bank, and on 7 July 2021 it began operating with three branches in Lagos. {cite:wikipedia-lotus-bank-en} In December 2022 it moved its head office to Ikoyi. {cite:wikipedia-lotus-bank-en}
