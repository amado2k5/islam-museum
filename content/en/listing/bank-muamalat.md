## Summary
Bank Muamalat Malaysia Berhad is a Malaysian Islamic bank based in Kuala Lumpur; it began operating on 1 October 1999 with the assets and liabilities transferred from the Islamic banking windows of Bank Bumiputra Malaysia, Bank of Commerce (M) and BBMB Kewangan. {cite:wikipedia-bank-muamalat-malaysia-en}
