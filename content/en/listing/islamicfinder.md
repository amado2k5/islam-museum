# IslamicFinder (and the Athan app)

## Summary

IslamicFinder is a website and app for prayer times, the adhan, the qibla direction and the Hijri calendar; its phone app is called Athan. The site calls the app "trusted by 55 million believers worldwide" and lists its services: prayer times, duas and dhikr, the Islamic calendar, a Ramadan calendar and suhur and iftar timetable, a Hajj and Umrah guide and a qibla finder. {cite:islamicfinder-athan-page}

## Early years online

No source read gives a launch date, but the earliest capture of the site in the Internet Archive is dated 3 December 1998. In the capture of October 1999 the home page is titled "Islamic.Finder: Finding Islamic Places and Prayer times" and offers searches for Islamic centres, mosques and organisations by US zip code or state, with a local prayer-time schedule for each location. {cite:wayback-islamicfinder-1998} The "About Us" page captured on 12 May 2001 says the site is maintained by Wasat.com, a non-profit organisation dedicated to serving Islam on the Internet, that it is that body's first project, and that it then covered the United States and Canada and was working to expand to other countries. {cite:wayback-islamicfinder-1998}

## Today

The site is active at islamicfinder.org, and the Athan app is published on Google Play under the name IslamicFinder.org; on 6 November 2026 its page showed 10M+ Android downloads and a rating of 4.9 from about 334,000 reviews. {cite:google-play-athan} The "55 million" is the site's own claim. {cite:islamicfinder-athan-page} The same day the site's current "About Us" page did not open, and it has no Wikipedia article.
