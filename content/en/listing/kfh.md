## Summary
Kuwait Finance House (KFH, "Bayt al-Tamwil al-Kuwayti") is a Kuwaiti bank founded in 1977 as the first bank in Kuwait to operate according to Sharia rulings, and is listed on the Kuwait Stock Exchange. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} It is a major player in the international sukuk market, where its trading group's volume reached US$11.4 billion in 2016. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en}

## Founding
A consortium of Kuwaiti merchants founded it in 1977; on its first day of operation, 31 August 1978, it received 170 applications to open accounts. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} It began in temporary premises on Ahmad al-Jaber Street, moved to the Emad Commercial Centre in 1983 and to its present headquarters on Abdullah al-Mubarak Street in 1986. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} By 1983 it was the only Islamic bank on The Banker's list of the top 100 Arab banks, and it was listed on the stock exchange in 1984. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en}

## Expansion
Its Turkish subsidiary, Kuveyt Türk Participation Bank, was established by Turkish cabinet decree in 1989 and has branches in Germany. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} KFH Capital Investment Company followed in 1999, its first Bahrain branch in 2002, and in 2005 KFH Malaysia, the first Islamic bank licensed there under the 1983 Islamic Banking Act; at the end of 2024 the bank announced its withdrawal from Malaysia by the end of 2026. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} It entered the UAE in 2004 and Saudi Arabia in 2007 in wholesale banking, and holds 85% of the Commercial Islamic Bank of Iraq. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} In September 2022 it completed the acquisition of Bahrain's Ahli United Bank for BHD 4.1 billion, which brought it into Egypt. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en}

## Ownership and figures
After the merger the Kuwait Investment Authority holds 16.80% of its shares, the Public Institution for Social Security 9.19%, the Public Authority for Minors' Affairs 7.32% and the Kuwait Awqaf Public Foundation 5.09%. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} By its 2025 annual report it had 631 locations and about 17,500 employees, and total assets of KWD 42.7 billion. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en}

## Rankings and awards
It was fifth on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s 30 Most Valuable Banks 2025 and ninth on its Top 100 Listed Companies 2025. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025} Its banking awards include Best Bank in Kuwait and Best Islamic Bank in Kuwait from EMEA Finance in 2015, and Best Islamic Financial Institution in the GCC from Global Finance in 2016. {cite:wikipedia-kuwait-finance-house-en}
