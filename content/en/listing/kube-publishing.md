# Kube Publishing

## Summary
Kube is an independent British publishing house of general-interest, academic and children's books on Islam and the Muslim experience; it says it is inspired by its Islamic faith and aims to serve Muslim communities worldwide with innovative and authentic books. {cite:kube-publishing-about}

## Founding and imprint
Kube Publishing Ltd was established in 2007 and handles the imprint of the Islamic Foundation, established in 1973, which by the publisher's account aims at excellence in research, education, publishing and training and at building bridges between Muslims and others, and continues to publish Muslim stories and children's books. {cite:kube-publishing-about}

## What it does
It publishes history, biography, memoir, politics, current affairs, culture and civilisation; it distributes for many Muslim publishing houses worldwide and wholesales mainstream publishers' titles to Muslim booksellers. {cite:kube-publishing-about}
