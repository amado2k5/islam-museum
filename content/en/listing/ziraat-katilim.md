## Summary
Ziraat Katılım is a public participation bank in Turkey, founded on 12 May 2015 and owned by the Turkey Wealth Fund. {cite:wikipedia-ziraat-katilim-en} It belongs to the Ziraat Finance Group, which includes Turkey's largest bank by branches and assets. {cite:wikipedia-ziraat-katilim-en}

## Opening
Its official opening ceremony was held on 29 May 2015 with the President of Turkey, and the first account was opened for President Erdoğan. {cite:wikipedia-ziraat-katilim-en}

## Licences
It holds Islamic banking licences for profit-and-loss sharing, consumer finance and credit cards. {cite:wikipedia-ziraat-katilim-en}
