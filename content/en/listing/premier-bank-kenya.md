## Summary
Premier Bank, formerly First Community Bank, is a Shari'ah-compliant commercial bank in Kenya; it was approved by the Central Bank of Kenya in 2007 and began operating on 1 June 2008. {cite:wikipedia-premier-bank-kenya-en}

## Origins
It was established in 2007 by private Muslim investors in Kuwait, Kenya and Tanzania to operate according to Islamic Shari'ah law, and was the first Kenya-based fully fledged bank to do so. {cite:wikipedia-premier-bank-kenya-en} After it, other Sharia-compliant banks, among them Gulf African Bank and Dubai Islamic Bank, received licences from the Central Bank of Kenya. {cite:wikipedia-premier-bank-kenya-en}

## Subsidiaries and size
In 2010 it set up a wholly owned takaful brokerage, and it owns FCB Capital, Kenya's first Sharia-compliant investment capital firm. {cite:wikipedia-premier-bank-kenya-en} In December 2019 its assets were about US$187.6 million, 21st by assets among 43 banks. {cite:wikipedia-premier-bank-kenya-en}
