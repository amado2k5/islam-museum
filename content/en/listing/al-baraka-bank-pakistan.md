## Summary
Al Baraka Bank (Pakistan) Limited is a Pakistani Islamic bank in Karachi, a subsidiary of Bahrain's [[listing/al-baraka-group|Al Baraka]] banking group. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-bank-pakistan-en}

## Course
It was established in Pakistan in 1991 as a branch of Al Baraka Islamic Bank Bahrain, incorporated in December 2004 and licensed in 2007. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-bank-pakistan-en} In October 2010 it merged with Emirates Global Islamic Bank, the first merger in Pakistan's Islamic banking, and in 2016 with Burj Bank; in September 2016 it had 191 branches in more than 97 cities. {cite:wikipedia-al-baraka-bank-pakistan-en}
