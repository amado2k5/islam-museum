## Summary
Saffron Road is an American food brand of American Halal Company, making halal-certified frozen meals, sauces and snacks sold through some 25,000 US retail locations. {cite:wikipedia-saffron-road-en}

## Origins
Adnan Durrani founded American Halal Company in Stamford, Connecticut, in 2009; the brand, named after Yo-Yo Ma's Silk Road project, launched through Whole Foods in July 2010. {cite:wikipedia-saffron-road-en} The local press calls its founder a Muslim businessman, and The New York Times in 2014 called him a Muslim entrepreneur following a kosher model into the mainstream. {cite:wikipedia-saffron-road-en}

## Growth
It made fifty products by 2014, ranked 642nd on the Inc. 5000 in 2015, and was third in US natural frozen food sales by 2014. {cite:wikipedia-saffron-road-en}
