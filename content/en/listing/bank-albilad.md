## Summary
Bank Albilad is a Saudi Islamic bank, a joint-stock company headquartered in Riyadh, established in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en} It offers Sharia-compliant retail and business products, investment through its arm Albilad Capital, and international remittances through its arm Enjaz. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en} Its structure includes a Sharia Department that follows up the implementation of the Sharia Committee's decisions. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en}

## Ownership and management
The brothers Mohammed and Abdullah Ibrahim Al Subeaei are significant stakeholders. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en} In February 2021 Nasser Mohammed Al-Subeaei was appointed chairman. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en} In May 2022 shareholders approved raising its capital to SAR 10 billion through a one-for-three share issue. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en}

## Rankings
It was 16th on [[award/forbes-middle-east-lists|Forbes Middle East]]'s 30 Most Valuable Banks 2025 and 38th on its Top 100 Listed Companies. {cite:wikipedia-bank-albilad-en} {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}
