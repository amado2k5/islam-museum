## Summary
Jaiz Bank Plc is a Nigerian bank operating under Islamic banking principles as a non-interest bank; it is the first non-interest bank established in Nigeria and is headquartered in Abuja, the capital. {cite:wikipedia-jaiz-bank-en}

## Founding
The institution was founded in 2003 as Jaiz International Plc; on 11 November 2011 it received a Central Bank of Nigeria licence as a regional bank, and on 6 January 2012 it opened as Jaiz Bank with offices and branches in Abuja, Kaduna and Kano. {cite:wikipedia-jaiz-bank-en} In December 2012 its total assets were about US$88.8 million. {cite:wikipedia-jaiz-bank-en} In 2013 it was expanding to urban centres in every state, and it received its national licence in 2016. {cite:wikipedia-jaiz-bank-en}

## Ownership
As of 31 December 2024 its largest shareholder was Muhammadu Indimi with about 24.06%, followed by Dantata Investment & Securities (11.65%), Umaru Abdul Mutallab (10.13%), Althani Investment (7.53%), the Islamic Development Bank (7.26%) and Dangote Industries (7.24%). {cite:wikipedia-jaiz-bank-en}
