## Summary
Banque Zitouna is the first Islamic bank in Tunisia and the Maghreb, with capital of US$30 million, aiming to develop Islamic financing and savings products for businesses and individuals. {cite:wikipedia-banque-zitouna-en}

## Plans
At its opening it said it planned to grow by 20 branches a year and hoped to expand from Tunisia into neighbouring countries. {cite:wikipedia-banque-zitouna-en}
