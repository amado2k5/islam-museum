## Summary
Alizz Islamic Bank is a full-fledged Islamic bank operating in the Sultanate of Oman, launched on 30 September 2013. {cite:wikipedia-alizz-islamic-bank-en}

## Origins
It was established under Royal Decree No. 69/2012, which amended the banking law to include Islamic banking at licensed banks, either through specialised banks or independent units of existing commercial banks. {cite:wikipedia-alizz-islamic-bank-en}

## Capital
Its paid-up capital is 100 million Omani rials, 40% raised from the public in the IPO and 60% from its promoters; it is a closed joint-stock company. {cite:wikipedia-alizz-islamic-bank-en}
