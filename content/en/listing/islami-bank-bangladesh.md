## Summary
Islami Bank Bangladesh PLC is an Islamic banking company based in Dhaka, incorporated as a public limited company on 13 March 1983, operating from 30 March that year, and listed on the Dhaka and Chittagong stock exchanges. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en} It has been the largest private banking network in Bangladesh. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en}

## Founding
Bangladesh joined the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation in 1974; in April 1978 the OIC's ninth conference of foreign ministers recommended that member states gradually reorganise their banking on Islamic lines, and in November 1982 an Islamic Development Bank delegation visited Dhaka to study a private joint-venture Islamic bank; Islami Bank Limited, the first shari'ah-compliant bank in Bangladesh, opened on 30 March 1983. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en} Saudi and Kuwaiti investors provided 70% of its initial capital. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en} It is a joint venture of the government of Bangladesh, 22 Bangladeshi businessmen, the Islamic Development Bank, and investment firms and banks from the Middle East. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en}

## Economic role
The Economist wrote that the bank was a pioneer in financing Bangladesh's rise as the apparel industry's main production base outside China. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en} By its account, in 2017 the bank held 90% of the country's Islamic-banking assets and deposits, was its largest private lender and had 12 million depositors. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en}

## Ownership change and crises
In 2012 a US Senate subcommittee mentioned possible ties between the bank and terrorism financing through HSBC; the bank denied them. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en} In 2017 its ownership and management changed suddenly; the new management said it was moving away from the bank's Jamaat-e-Islami roots and opened recruitment to non-Muslims and more women, and The Daily Star wrote that a financial crisis and a reduction of foreign stakes followed. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en} In 2022 media reported large loans to newly registered companies without proper checks and about BDT 300 billion of loans to S. Alam Group, the controlling shareholder; Bangladesh Bank appointed an observer and opened an investigation, the High Court ordered an inquiry, and on 29 December 2022 the bank took BDT 80 billion in emergency loans from the central bank. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en}

## Awards
Global Finance named it the best bank in Bangladesh for 1999, 2000, 2004 and 2005, and the best Islamic financial institution in Bangladesh every year from 2008 to 2013. {cite:wikipedia-islami-bank-bangladesh-en}
