## Summary
What al-Nawawi gives next in the morning and evening chapter: contentment with God as Lord, wordings of thanks, asking for well-being, and asking for the good of the day, with what he says of the chain of each. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 79}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports from Thawban, in al-Tirmidhi's book, that whoever says at evening «رضيت بالله ربا وبالإسلام دينا», adding the prophethood of Muhammad, it is a right upon God to please him {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 79}. He notes that its chain contains Sa'id ibn al-Marzuban, whom the memorizers weaken, that al-Tirmidhi called it good, sound and rare, and that Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i report it by good chains, so the report's basis stands {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 79}. From Abd Allah ibn Ghannam, in Abu Dawud by a good chain he did not weaken: «اللهم ما أصبح بي من نعمة فمنك وحدك لا شريك لك»; whoever says it at morning has paid his thanks for the day, and at evening for the night {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 79}. From Ibn Umar, in Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah, [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] never left, at evening and morning, «اللهم إني أسألك العافية في الدنيا والآخرة»; al-Nawawi adds that al-Hakim called the chain sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 80}. From Abu Malik al-Ash'ari, in Abu Dawud: «أصبحنا وأصبح الملك لله رب العالمين», then asking the good of the day, its opening, victory, light, blessing and guidance {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 81}. From Ibn Abbas, in Abu Dawud: whoever recites at morning two verses of Surat al-Rum, [[passage/quran-30-17|Qur'an 30:17]] and [[passage/quran-30-18|Qur'an 30:18]], catches up what he missed that day; Abu Dawud did not weaken it, but al-Bukhari did {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 81}.
