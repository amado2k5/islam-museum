## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the Book of the Traveller's Remembrances on boarding a ship, the traveller's supplication, takbir on ascending and tasbih on descending, and what is said when a beast runs loose or a village comes in sight. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 221}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi gathers here what the traveller says at sea and on the road. He opens the ship chapter with two verses, [[passage/quran-11-41|Qur'an 11:41]] and [[passage/quran-43-12|43:12]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 221}, and reports from Ibn al-Sunni, from al-Husayn ibn Ali, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] made the saying of words from the verses he cites a safety for his community from drowning when they board {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 221}. From Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah, from Abu Hurayra, that three supplications are answered beyond doubt: of the wronged, of the traveller and of a father against his child; al-Tirmidhi calls it good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 222}. From the two Sahihs, from Ibn Umar, that returning from the pilgrimage or the lesser pilgrimage, at each height he said takbir three times and then «آيبون تائبون عابدون ساجدون لربنا حامدون», we return, repent, worship and praise our Lord {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 222}. From the two Sahihs, from Abu Musa, that when they raised their voices at a valley he told them «اربعوا على أنفسكم», be gentle with yourselves {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 223}. From Ibn al-Sunni, from Ibn Mas'ud, that one whose beast runs loose should call «يا عباد الله احبسوا», hold it; he says a teacher of his and he found it work {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 223} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 224}. On seeing a village, from Suhayb: «أسألك خير هذه القرية وخير أهلها وخير ما فيها», I ask You for the good of this village {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 224}.
