## Summary
What is said on seeing the new moon, as al-Nawawi gathers it at the head of his Book of the Remembrances of Fasting: Talha ibn Ubayd Allah's report from al-Darimi's Musnad and al-Tirmidhi's book, Ibn Umar's, and what Abu Dawud relates without a full chain. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 188}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi opens the Book of the Remembrances of Fasting with the chapter on what is said at the sight of the new moon {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 188}. From al-Darimi's Musnad and al-Tirmidhi's book, from Talha ibn Ubayd Allah, he reports that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] saw the new moon he said: «اللهم أهله علينا باليمن والإيمان والسلامة والإسلام ربي وربك الله», O God, bring it over us with good fortune and faith, safety and submission; my Lord and yours is God. He quotes al-Tirmidhi's grading of it as good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 188}. From al-Darimi's Musnad, from Ibn Umar: «الله أكبر، اللهم أهله علينا بالأمن والإيمان والسلامة والإسلام، والتوفيق لما تحب وترضى، ربنا وربك الله» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 188}. From the Book of Manners in Sunan Abi Dawud he gives, as a report that reached Qatada, «هلال خير ورشد», a moon of good and right guidance, three times, then «آمنت بالله الذي خلقك»; he says Abu Dawud relates both without a full chain, and that some copies carry Abu Dawud's remark that no sound, fully chained report exists on this subject {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 188}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Aisha, he gives the seeking of refuge at the sight of the moon {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 189}. From Hilyat al-Awliya, from Anas, by a chain he says has weakness in it, what is said when Rajab begins: «اللهم بارك لنا في رجب وشعبان وبلغنا رمضان» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 189}.
