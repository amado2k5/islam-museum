## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapters on reaching the prayer row, standing to pray, and supplicating at the iqama, with his opening of the chapter on entering the prayer. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 39}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports from Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas that a man came to the prayer while [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] was praying, and on reaching the row said «اللهم آتني أفضل ما تؤتي عبادك الصالحين», O God, give me the best of what You give Your righteous servants. When the Prophet finished, he asked who had just spoken. The man said it was he, and the Prophet told him that his horse would then be hamstrung and he would die a martyr in God's path. Al-Nawawi assigns the report to al-Nasa'i and Ibn al-Sunni and notes that al-Bukhari gave it in his Tarikh {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 39}. In the chapter on standing to pray he reports from Ibn al-Sunni's book that Umm Rafi' asked the Prophet for a deed for which God would reward her. He told her, when she stood to pray, to glorify God ten times, declare His oneness ten times, praise Him ten times, magnify Him ten times and seek His forgiveness ten times. The report ends with a reply said after each of the first four, «هذا لي», and after seeking forgiveness, «قد فعلت» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 39}. A chapter on supplication at the iqama follows. He gives from al-Shafi'i, with his chain in al-Umm, a mursal report that the Prophet said to seek the answer to supplication when armies meet, at the iqama of the prayer and when rain falls {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 41}. Al-Shafi'i adds that he had learned of more than one person seeking the answer at rain and at the iqama {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 41}. Last, he opens the chapter on entering the prayer, saying it is very broad, with many sound reports, and that he will note its principles and aims and omit most of the proofs for brevity {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 41}.
