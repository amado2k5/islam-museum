## Summary
What al-Nawawi gives in the chapter on the most beautiful names of God: the verse, Abu Hurayra's report of ninety-nine names, and the list of names, with who transmitted it. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 100}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
In the chapter on God's most beautiful names, al-Nawawi gives a verse, [[passage/quran-7-180|Qur'an 7:180]], on God having the most beautiful names by which He is to be called {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 100}. He then reports from Abu Hurayra that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said God has ninety-nine names, one short of a hundred, and whoever enumerates them enters Paradise, and that He is odd and loves the odd {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 100}. He then sets out the names, beginning with the name «الله», then «الرحمن الرحيم الملك القدوس السلام المؤمن المهيمن العزيز الجبار المتكبر», then «الخالق البارئ المصور الغفار القهار الوهاب الرزاق الفتاح العليم», and continues through the list to its end, «الوارث الرشيد الصبور» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 100}. On the report's standing, he says that al-Bukhari and Muslim transmitted it as far as the words He loves the odd, and that what follows is a good report transmitted by al-Tirmidhi and others {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 100}. This single page thus holds the verse, the report and the list of names together in one chapter {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 100}.
