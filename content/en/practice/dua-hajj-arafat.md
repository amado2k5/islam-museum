## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on the way from Mina to Arafat, the remembrances and supplications recommended at Arafat, and what is said on setting out from there for Muzdalifa. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 198}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi carries the pilgrim from Mina to Arafa with a supplication that closes by asking that the sin be forgiven and the pilgrimage accepted, and he says the pilgrim says the talbiya, reads the Qur'an and says many other remembrances {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 198}. In the chapter on Arafat he recalls what he gave under the festival remembrances, a saying of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] that the best supplication is that of the day of Arafa and the best of what he and the prophets before him said is «لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له», there is no god but God alone {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 198}. He says this is the best day of the year for supplication and the greater part of the pilgrimage; that one should spend one's capacity on remembrance, supplication and Qur'an and pray for oneself, parents, relatives, teachers, friends and all Muslims; avoid straining for rhymed speech; lower the voice, persist, open and close with praise and blessing on the Prophet, and try to face the Ka'ba in purity {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 198}. He reports from al-Tirmidhi's book, from Ali, that the Prophet's most frequent supplication at the standing on that day began «اللهم لك الحمد كالذي نقول وخيرا مما نقول», O God, Yours is the praise as we say and better than we say {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 198}; the supplication runs on over the page and includes refuge from the grave's punishment, the whispering of the breast and the scattering of affairs {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 199}. He recommends much talbiya and weeping and calls the standing the greatest gathering of the world {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 199}. Among chosen supplications: «اللهم إني ظلمت نفسي ظلما كثيرا وإنه لا يغفر الذنوب إلا أنت فاغفر لي مغفرة من عندك», O God, I have wronged myself greatly and none forgives sins but You, so forgive me with a forgiveness from You {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 199}. On setting out for Muzdalifa he recommends talbiya, Qur'an, supplication and repeating «لا إله إلا الله والله أكبر» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 199}.
