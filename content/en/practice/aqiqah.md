## Overview
Aqiqah is the animal slaughtered for the newborn; Fiqh al-Sunna defines it and affirms it is a confirmed sunna that the Prophet and his Companions performed {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}.

## In the sources
Fiqh al-Sunna details the aqiqa's definition and ruling, noting it is a confirmed sunna even if the father is in straitened means {cite:sabiq-fiqh-al-sunnah}.
