## Summary
"The prayer" is an obligatory worship, one of [[concept/five-pillars|Islam's five pillars]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. In the Najdi man's hadith the obligation is "five prayers in the day and night", and what exceeds them is voluntary {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 46}.

## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an commands establishing the prayer coupled with the zakat: [[passage/quran-2-43|al-Baqara 43]]. It describes it as "a timed ordinance" upon the believers: [[passage/quran-4-103|al-Nisa' 103]]. The verse of al-Isra' mentions prayer times: [[passage/quran-17-78|al-Isra' 78]].

## In the hadith
In Gabriel's hadith the Prophet makes "establishing the prayer" part of the definition of [[concept/islam-din|Islam]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}. In the Najdi man's hadith, the man said after hearing the obligations: "By God I shall add nothing to this and take nothing from it", and the Prophet said: "He prospers if he is truthful" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 46}.

## What is not covered here
The detail of prayer times, its form, its rulings and the schools' disagreements on it will be added in a later batch from the fiqh books, with each position's school identified.
