## Overview
Tahajjud is night prayer after sleep; al-Bukhari devotes a 'Book of Tahajjud' in his Sahih to the reports on praying at night {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.

## In the sources
The Sahih's Book of Tahajjud gathers the hadiths on night prayer and the Prophet's praying by night {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}.
