## Brief
The istikhara is two rak'as outside the obligatory prayers, prayed by someone resolving on a matter, followed by a known supplication asking God to choose what is good and to make it easy {cite:ighathat-lahfan-muntakhab p.20} {cite:ighathat-lahfan-muntakhab p.21}.

## In the extract
[[person/ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyya|Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya]] reports that the Prophet taught his Companions the istikhara for all matters, as he taught them a surah of the Qur'an, and presents it as the lawful alternative to seeking knowledge of the unseen through soothsaying, astrology or casting pebbles {cite:ighathat-lahfan-muntakhab p.20} {cite:ighathat-lahfan-muntakhab p.21}. He contrasts it with people who go to an astrologer or soothsayer at every resolve, and says this ruins religion and worldly life {cite:ighathat-lahfan-muntakhab p.21}.
