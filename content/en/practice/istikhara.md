## Overview
Istikhara is asking for the good choice in a matter: directing one's resolve to what is chosen with God and better, through the prescribed prayer or the reported supplication. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2322}

## Related terms
It is distinguished from tiyara (taking a bad omen), fa'l (a good omen), ru'ya (the dream), istiqsam with azlam arrows which is forbidden, and from probing the unseen through the mushaf, geomancy, or lots. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2322} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2323}

## Ruling and form
The scholars agree it is a sunna; the Prophet taught it for all matters like a sura of the Quran: "when one of you resolves on a matter, let him pray two non-obligatory rak'as, then say…" — and "among the felicity of Adam's son is his seeking God's choice." {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2323}
