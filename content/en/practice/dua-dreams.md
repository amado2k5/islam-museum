## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapters on what to say after seeing in a dream what one likes or dislikes, and on being told a dream: praising God for a good dream, blowing lightly and seeking refuge from a disliked one, and the reply to the teller. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 98}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports in Sahih al-Bukhari, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said that when one of them sees a dream he likes, it is from God, so let him praise God for it and tell it; in one wording, let him tell it only to one he loves; and if he sees what he dislikes, it is from the devil, so let him seek refuge from its evil and mention it to no one, for it will not harm him {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 98}. In the two Sahihs, from Abu Qatada, a good dream is from God and a troubling dream from the devil, so whoever sees what he dislikes should blow lightly to his left three times and seek refuge from the devil; al-Nawawi explains that the blowing is a gentle puff with no spittle {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 98}. In Sahih Muslim, from Jabir: let him spit to his left three times, seek refuge in God from the devil three times and turn over from the side he was on {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 98}. Al-Tirmidhi has, from Abu Hurayra, that he should tell no one and should rise and pray {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 98}. In Ibn al-Sunni's book, he spits three times and then says «اللهم إني أعوذ بك من عمل الشيطان وسيئات الأحلام» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 99}. In the chapter on what is said when a dream is told to one, he reports in Ibn al-Sunni that the Prophet said to one who told him a dream «خيرا رأيت وخيرا يكون», and in another wording «خيرا تلقاه وشرا توقاه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 99}.
