## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapters on what to say when fearing a people or a ruler, when looking upon an enemy, and when a devil appears. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 123}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi gives, by a chain he calls sound, in the Sunan of Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i, from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] feared a people he said «اللهم إنا نجعلك في نحورهم ونعوذ بك من شرورهم», O God, we set You against their throats and seek refuge in You from their evils {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 123}. In the chapter on fearing a ruler he gives from Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Ibn Umar, the Prophet's words that one who fears a ruler or another should say «لا إله إلا الله الحليم الكريم», then glorify God as Lord of the seven heavens and the great throne, then say «لا إله إلا أنت، عز جارك، وجل ثناؤك», and he adds that the wording from Abu Musa's report is also recommended {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 124}. In the chapter on looking upon an enemy he gives from Ibn al-Sunni, from Anas, that they were with the Prophet on a campaign and met the enemy, and Anas heard him say words from the opening sura which the museum does not retype. Anas says he saw men thrown down, struck by angels from before them and behind {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 124}. In the chapter on a devil he cites two verses, [[passage/quran-7-200|Qur'an 7:200]] and [[passage/quran-17-45|Qur'an 17:45]], and says one should seek refuge and then recite what is easy of the Qur'an {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 124}. From Sahih Muslim, from Abu al-Darda, he reports that the Prophet stood praying and was heard saying «أعوذ بالله منك», then «ألعنك بلعنة الله التامة» three times, and afterwards said that Iblis had come with a flame of fire to put in his face, and that he said this three times and the devil drew back {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 124}.
