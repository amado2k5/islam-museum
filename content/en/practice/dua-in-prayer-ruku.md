## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapter on the words of bowing: the takbir for it, the glorification of the bowing and the reports from the two Sahihs and elsewhere, and the dislike of reciting in it. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 51}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says sound reports agree that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said the takbir for bowing, and that it is a sunna {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 51}. He then says that on reaching the bowing position one says «سبحان ربي العظيم» three times, Glory to my Lord the Mighty. He reports from Hudhayfa in Muslim that he said it in his long bowing, and from the Sunan books that whoever says it three times has completed his bowing {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 52}. He reports from Aisha in the two Sahihs «سبحانك اللهم ربنا وبحمدك اللهم اغفر لي», from Ali in Muslim «اللهم لك ركعت وبك آمنت ولك أسلمت», and from Aisha in Muslim «سبوح قدوس رب الملائكة والروح» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 52}. He cites from Ibn Abbas in Muslim «فأما الركوع فعظموا فيه الرب», and says this is the aim of the section, that it is best to combine these words with glorification first, and that three glorifications are the lowest degree of completeness {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 53}. He says dhikr in bowing is a sunna for the majority and required for Ahmad, that reciting the Qur'an in it is disliked, and reports from Ali in Muslim that he was forbidden to recite while bowing or prostrating {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 53}.
