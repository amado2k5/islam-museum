## Overview
Tawaf is circling a thing; in usage, circling the Sacred House; the mataf is its place. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11248}

## Its kinds
The Hanafis, Malikis and Hanbalis count seven: arrival tawaf, visitation tawaf, farewell tawaf, 'umra tawaf, votive tawaf, greeting tawaf of the Sacred Mosque, and voluntary tawaf; the Shafi'is count six, including rukn tawaf covering the integrals of hajj and 'umra and the tawaf of release after missing hajj. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11249}

## Arrival tawaf
Called the comer's, arrival's, greeting's and meeting tawaf, prescribed for one arriving from outside Mecca to greet the House; a sunna for the outsider. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11249}

## For Sprouts
Tawaf is walking seven times around the sacred Kaaba in Mecca with prayers, remembrance, and peace in the heart.

## For Explorers
Tawaf is a sacred act of worship where Muslims circumambulate the Kaaba seven times counter-clockwise, keeping the Sacred House on their left. Beginning each circuit at the Black Stone with prayers and praise, believers from all lands walk together in devotion, reflecting unity and shared faith in God.

## For Scholars
The liturgical circumambulation of the Kaaba, constituting an essential pillar in both the Hajj and Umrah pilgrimages and an esteemed independent voluntary devotion. The rite requires seven continuous counter-clockwise circuits performed in a state of ritual purity, keeping the Kaaba on the worshiper's left. Commencing and concluding aligned with the Black Stone with praises, it includes Sunna practices such as brisk walking (ramal) during early circuits for men and praying two units behind the Station of Abraham (Maqam Ibrahim). Encompassing the arrival, effusion (ifadah), and farewell circumambulations, Tawaf symbolizes the cosmic unity of believers focused upon monotheism.
