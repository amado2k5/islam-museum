## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on the remembrances in the Tarawih prayer, the prayer of need and the supplications reported for it, and the prayer of glorification with what is said of its reports and of the imams who held it recommended. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 183}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says the Tarawih prayer is a sunna by the agreement of scholars, twenty rak'as with a salutation after every two, and that all the earlier remembrances apply in it, which he stresses because most people drop most of them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 183}. He chooses reading the whole Qur'an over the month, about a thirtieth each night, slowly, and warns against reading all of Surat al-An'am in the last rak'a of the seventh night, which he calls an ugly innovation {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 183} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 184}. For the prayer of need he reports in al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah from Abd Allah ibn Abi Awfa that whoever has a need should make ablution, pray two rak'as, praise God, bless the Prophet and say «لا إله إلا الله الحليم الكريم», asking for «موجبات رحمتك وعزائم مغفرتك», the things that earn Your mercy and the resolves of Your forgiveness; al-Tirmidhi says there is something said of its chain {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 184}. From Uthman ibn Hunayf he reports that a blind man asked [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] to pray for his healing and was told to make ablution and say «اللهم إني أسألك وأتوجه إليك بنبيك محمد نبي الرحمة», which al-Tirmidhi calls good and sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 184}. On the prayer of glorification he quotes al-Tirmidhi that its reports are of little soundness and that Ibn al-Mubarak and others held it; Ibn al-Mubarak's form is fifteen glorifications before reciting and ten after and at each posture, seventy-five a rak'a and three hundred in four {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 185}. He gives Abu Rafi's report, which al-Tirmidhi calls strange {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 185} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 186}, Ibn al-Arabi's judgement that it is weak and Ibn al-Jawzi's, and adds that a group of imams of his school held it recommended, among them al-Baghawi and al-Ruyani {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 186}.
