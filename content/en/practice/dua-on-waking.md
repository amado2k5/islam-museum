## Summary
What is said on waking from sleep, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: praise of God for giving life after death, from Sahih al-Bukhari, with further wordings from Ibn al-Sunni's book and Sunan Abi Dawud. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 19}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi opens the main part of his book with this chapter, saying he will set the remembrances in the order of the day, from the first waking to sleep {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 19}. From Sahih al-Bukhari, on the authority of Hudhayfa and Abu Dharr, he gives what [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said on waking: «الحمد لله الذي أحيانا بعد ما أماتنا وإليه النشور», praise to God who gave us life after causing us to die, and to whom is the rising {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 19}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, by a chain he calls sound, he gives from Abu Hurayra: «الحمد لله الذي رد علي روحي، وعافاني في جسدي، وأذن لي بذكره», praise for the return of the soul, health of body and leave to remember God {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 19}. From Sunan Abi Dawud, from Aisha, he reports that on waking in the night the Prophet said the takbir, praise, glorification, a plea for forgiveness and the declaration of God's oneness ten times each, then ten times: «اللهم إني أعوذ بك من ضيق الدنيا وضيق يوم القيامة», a plea for refuge from the straitness of this world and of the Day of Resurrection {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 20}. Before these he cites the report of the two Sahihs from Abu Hurayra about three knots that are undone by remembering God, ablution and prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 19}.
