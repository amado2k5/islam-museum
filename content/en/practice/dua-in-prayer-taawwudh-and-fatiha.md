## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapter on seeking refuge after the opening supplication and the start of the chapter on recitation: the wording of the refuge and what is reported on it, then the Fatiha as a required recitation. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 45}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says seeking refuge after the opening supplication is a sunna by agreement and a preface to recitation, and he cites a verse from [[passage/quran-16-98|Qur'an 16:98]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 45}. He reports from Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah and al-Bayhaqi that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said before reciting in the prayer «أعوذ بالله من الشيطان الرجيم من نفخه ونفثه وهمزه», and he gives the explanation found in the report: its hamz is madness, its nafkh is pride and its nafth is poetry {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 46}. He says the refuge is recommended and not required, so whoever leaves it is not at fault, the prayer is not void and no prostration of forgetfulness is made, and that in the first unit it is recommended by agreement {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 46}. He then opens the chapter on recitation, saying recitation is required by consensus and that the Fatiha is required, with nothing else sufficing for one who can read it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 46}. He cites a report that a prayer without the Opening of the Book does not suffice, which Ibn Khuzayma and Ibn Hibban give in their sound collections by a sound chain {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 47}. He says the basmala is a verse of it, that it has fourteen doublings, and that one who cannot read it recites its measure from elsewhere or uses glorification and affirmation of God's oneness {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 47}.
