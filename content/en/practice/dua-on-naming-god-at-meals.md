## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the Book of the Remembrances of Eating and Drinking on naming God at meals, its wording, what is said if it is forgotten, and the chapter on not finding fault with food. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 229}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi opens the chapter on naming God at eating and drinking with the report in the two Sahihs from Umar ibn Abi Salama that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] told him «سم الله وكل بيمينك», name God and eat with your right hand {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 229}. From Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, from Aisha, that one who forgets at the start should say «بسم الله أوله وآخره», in God's name at its beginning and its end; al-Tirmidhi calls it good and sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 229}. From Sahih Muslim, from Hudhayfa, that the Prophet took the hand of a girl and then of a Bedouin who reached for the food, saying that Satan makes lawful food over which God's name is not mentioned {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 230}. From Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i, from Umayya ibn Makhshi, that a man who named God only at his last morsel was told Satan had been eating with him {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 230}. Al-Nawawi says the scholars agree that naming is recommended at the start, that drinking is the same, and that saying it aloud is recommended {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 230}; the short form suffices, and one diner's naming serves for the rest {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 231}. In the next chapter, from the two Sahihs, from Abu Hurayra, the Prophet never found fault with food: he ate what he liked and left what he disliked {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 231}.
