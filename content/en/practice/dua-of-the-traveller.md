## Summary
What al-Nawawi gives in the Book of the Traveller's Remembrances in al-Adhkar for setting out and for farewell: seeking counsel and istikhara, the two units of prayer before a journey, the words of the one who sees a traveller off, «أستودع الله دينك وأمانتك وخواتيم عملك», and the supplication for the traveller's piety. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 214}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says that the remembrances recommended for one at home are recommended for the traveller too, who adds to them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 214}. He begins with a chapter on istikhara and seeking counsel: one consults someone trusted for sincerity, experience and religion, for which he cites the verse [[passage/quran-3-159|3:159]], then prays two units and says the supplication of [[practice/istikhara|istikhara]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 214}. Once resolved, the traveller makes a will before witnesses, seeks release from those he has dealings with, seeks the good pleasure of his parents and teachers, repents, and learns what his journey requires {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 215}. On setting out it is recommended to pray two units, for which he cites a report related by al-Tabarani, and to supplicate with sincerity and tenderness; he says that among the best things to say is: «اللهم بك أستعين، وعليك أتوكل، اللهم ذلل لي صعوبة أمري، وسهل علي مشقة سفري», O God, in You I seek help and on You I rely; make easy for me the hardness of my affair and lighten the toil of my journey {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 216} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 217}. It is recommended to bid farewell to family, neighbours and friends and to ask for their prayers {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 217}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Abu Hurayra, he gives what the traveller says to those he leaves: «أستودعكم الله الذي لا تضيع ودائعه», I leave you in the keeping of God, with whom nothing entrusted is lost; and from Sunan Abi Dawud, from Ibn Umar, what the one seeing him off says: «أستودع الله دينك وأمانتك وخواتيم عملك», I entrust to God your religion, your trust and the last of your deeds. He quotes al-Khattabi that the trust here means the traveller's family, those he leaves and his property {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 218}. From al-Tirmidhi's book, from Anas, he gives the words of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] to a man who asked him for provision: «زودك الله التقوى», may God provision you with piety, then «وغفر ذنبك», then «ويسر لك الخير حيثما كنت»; he quotes al-Tirmidhi's grading of it as good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 219}. On putting a foot in the stirrup one says «بسم الله», and once mounted, «الحمد لله» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 219}.
