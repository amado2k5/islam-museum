## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers on prompting the dying with the declaration of oneness, closing the eyes of the dead, what is said at his side, and what the bereaved say on a death or on news of a friend's. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 143}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi advises the dying person to say the declaration of God's oneness often, so that it may be his last words {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 143}. He gives from Mu'adh ibn Jabal, in Sunan Abi Dawud, that one whose last words are that declaration enters Paradise {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 143}. He gives from Abu Said «لقنوا موتاكم لا إله إلا الله», prompt your dying with it; al-Tirmidhi calls it good and sound, and the scholars add that the prompting be gentle and not repeated once it is said {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 143}. On closing the eyes he gives from Sahih Muslim, from Umm Salama, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] closed Abu Salama's eyes and prayed for him {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 143} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 144}. On what is said at the side of the dead, from Muslim, that good should be said, since the angels say amen, and Umm Salama's supplication «اللهم اغفر لي وله وأعقبني منه عقبى حسنة» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 144}. For the bereaved he gives from Muslim, from Umm Salama, the phrase of returning to God and asking recompense {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 145}, and from Abu Musa in al-Tirmidhi that God has a house of praise built for one who praised Him and returned to Him, which al-Tirmidhi calls good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 145}.
