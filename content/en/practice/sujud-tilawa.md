## Overview
Sujud is humility, lowering oneself, placing the forehead on the ground; the law reserved it for the known integral of prayer and what resembles it — the prostration of the Quran and the prostration of gratitude. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9405}

## The Quranic prostration
Al-Raghib divided prostration into voluntary — which belongs to man and earns reward — and the prostration of subjugation, which belongs to man, animals and inanimate things as a silent testimony that they are created. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9405}

## Its form
The most complete prostration is on seven limbs: the forehead with the nose, the two hands, the two knees and the two feet — "I was commanded to prostrate on seven limbs." {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9406}
