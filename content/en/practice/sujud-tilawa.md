## Overview
Sujud al-tilawa is the prostration made on reciting a prostration verse of the Qur'an; the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopedia devotes a section to it {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.

## In the sources
The section treats the rulings of reciting a sajda verse and repeating the prostration in a single sitting {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3}.
