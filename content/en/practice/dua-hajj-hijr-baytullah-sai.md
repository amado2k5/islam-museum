## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on supplication in the Hijr and inside the House, the remembrances of the sa'y between Safa and Marwa, chosen supplications, and what is said on leaving Mecca for Mina. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 196}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
In these chapters al-Nawawi gathers the supplications of the rites at Mecca before the departure for Mina. He says that supplication in the Hijr, which is counted as part of the House, is answered, and gives a handed-down wording for it: «يا رب أتيتك من شقة بعيدة مؤملا معروفك فأنلني معروفا من معروفك», Lord, I have come to You from a far journey, hoping for Your kindness, so grant me a kindness of Yours {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 196}. Inside the House he reports, from al-Nasa'i's book, from Usama ibn Zayd, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] on entering went to the part of the back wall of the Ka'ba facing him, laid his face and cheek on it, praised God, asked and sought forgiveness, then went to each corner with takbir, tahlil, tasbih, praise, asking and forgiveness-seeking, and left {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 196}. For the sa'y he says the sunna is to stand long on Safa facing the Ka'ba with takbir and supplication, to repeat the remembrance three times without the talbiya, and to do the same on Marwa {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 196}. He reports Ibn Umar's supplication on Safa, asking to be held fast to God's religion and obedience and to love God, His angels, prophets and righteous servants {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 197}, and the words between the two hills: «رب اغفر وارحم وتجاوز عما تعلم إنك أنت الأعز الأكرم», Lord, forgive, have mercy and pass over what You know {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 197}. Among chosen supplications he gives «اللهم يا مقلب القلوب ثبت قلبي على دينك», O God, turner of hearts, make my heart firm in Your religion, and says reading the Qur'an is better and the best course is to combine them all or keep to the most important {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 197}. On leaving Mecca for Mina he says it is recommended to say «اللهم إياك أرجو ولك أدعو فبلغني صالح أملي», O God, in You I hope and to You I call, so bring me to the good of my hope {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 197}.
