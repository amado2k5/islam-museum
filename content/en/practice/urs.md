## Overview
The annual commemoration of the death of a saint at his shrine in South Asia and Morocco: qawwali, vows and food offered, called an 'urs', the 'wedding' of the saint with his Lord. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
Among the most profound popular rituals of medieval Islam; gatherings such as Ajmer and Data Ganj Bakhsh are among the largest religious seasons in the world. {cite:wikidata}
