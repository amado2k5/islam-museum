## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers on what is said and done in walking with the bier, on seeing it pass, in laying the dead in the grave, after the burial, and in prompting him. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 160}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
On what the one walking with the bier says, al-Nawawi recommends being occupied with remembrance of God and thought of where the dead is going. He holds that the right course is the silence of the early generations, with no raised voice in recitation or remembrance {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 160}, and says drawled reading over the bier is forbidden by the scholars' agreement {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 160}. A person whom a bier passes is advised to say «سبحان الحي الذي لا يموت» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 161}. For laying the dead in the grave he gives from Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Bayhaqi, from Ibn Umar, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] used to say «بسم الله وعلى سنة رسول الله»; al-Tirmidhi calls it good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 161}. After the burial the sunna is to throw three handfuls of earth from the head's side, and to stay by the grave as long as it takes to slaughter a camel and share its meat {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 161} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 162}. From Abu Dawud and al-Bayhaqi, by a good chain, from Uthman, that when the Prophet finished a burial he stood and said «استغفروا لأخيكم وسلوا له التثبيت فإنه الآن يسأل» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 162}. He then treats prompting the dead after burial: many of his companions recommend it, and a child is not prompted {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 162} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 163}.
