## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapters on the blessing on the Prophet after the tashahhud and on the supplication after the last tashahhud: the best form, and the refuges and supplications from the two Sahihs and Muslim. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 66}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says the blessing on [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] after the last tashahhud is required for al-Shafi'i, and that the best form blesses him and his family, wives and offspring as God blessed Ibrahim and Ibrahim's family, a form he says is in al-Bukhari and Muslim from Ka'b ibn Ujra apart from some parts {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 66}. He then says supplication after the last tashahhud is lawful without dispute, and reports from Ibn Mas'ud in the two Sahihs that the Prophet taught them the tashahhud and then said «ثم يتخير من الدعاء», then let him choose a supplication {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 67}. He reports from Abu Hurayra in the two Sahihs that whoever finishes the last tashahhud should seek refuge from four things: the punishment of hell, the punishment of the grave, the trial of life and death, and the evil of the false Messiah {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 67}. He reports from Ali in Muslim «اللهم اغفر لي ما قدمت وما أخرت وما أسررت وما أعلنت», forgive me what I sent ahead and what I held back, what I hid and what I made known {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 68}. He reports from Abu Bakr in the two Sahihs that he asked the Prophet for a supplication for his prayer, and was told «قل اللهم إني ظلمت نفسي ظلما كثيرا ولا يغفر الذنوب إلا أنت» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 68}.
