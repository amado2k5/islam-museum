## Summary
What is said on entering and leaving the toilet, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: the plea for refuge in the two Sahihs, «غفرانك» on leaving in the Sunan, and the dislike of remembrance and speech while relieving oneself. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 25}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi states that it is established in the two Sahihs, from Anas, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said on entering the toilet: «اللهم إني أعوذ بك من الخبث والخبائث», a plea for refuge from foul things, and he discusses how the word is voweled {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 25}. From al-Tirmidhi, from Ali, he reports that saying «بسم الله» on entering is a screen between the eyes of the jinn and human nakedness; he quotes al-Tirmidhi's remark that its chain is not strong, then says that weak reports are acted on in matters of merit, and that his colleagues recommend naming God and then seeking refuge, in a building or in open country {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 26}. He gives a chapter on the dislike of remembrance and speech while relieving oneself, which he says is a dislike and not a prohibition, citing Ibn Umar's report in Sahih Muslim and al-Muhajir ibn Qunfudh's in the Sunan that a greeting was not returned until after ablution {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 26}. On leaving he gives: «غفرانك، الحمد لله الذي أذهب عني الأذى وعافاني», Your forgiveness, and praise to God who removed harm from me and kept me well; he notes that «غفرانك» is established in a sound report in the Sunan of Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 27}, and reports from Ibn Umar, through Ibn al-Sunni and al-Tabarani: «الحمد لله الذي أذاقني لذته، وأبقى في قوته، ودفع عني أذاه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 27}.
