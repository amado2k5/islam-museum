## Summary
What is said on entering and leaving the mosque, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: asking for the gates of mercy on entering and for God's bounty on leaving, from Sahih Muslim and the Sunan, and the plea for refuge related by Abu Dawud. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 31}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says it is recommended to seek refuge, give praise, ask blessing on the Prophet, ask forgiveness and ask for the gates of mercy to be opened, then to say «بسم الله», entering with the right foot and leaving with the left, and on leaving to say «أبواب فضلك», the gates of Your bounty, in place of mercy {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 31}. He reports from Abu Humayd or Abu Usayd that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said that one who enters the mosque should say «اللهم افتح لي أبواب رحمتك», and one who leaves, «اللهم إني أسألك من فضلك»; he says Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah relate it by sound chains {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 31}. From Sunan Abi Dawud, from Abd Allah ibn Amr ibn al-As, in a report he calls good with a strong chain: «أعوذ بالله العظيم وبوجهه الكريم وسلطانه القديم من الشيطان الرجيم», and whoever says it is kept from Satan for the rest of the day {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 31}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Abu Umama, what is said on leaving: «اللهم إني أعوذ بك من إبليس وجنوده» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 32}. He follows with a chapter on what is said inside the mosque, recommending much remembrance, recitation of the Qur'an and the study of hadith and law {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 32}.
