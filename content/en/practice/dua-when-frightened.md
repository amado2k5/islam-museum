## Summary
What is said by someone startled or frightened, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: Thawban's report from Ibn al-Sunni's book, and the plea for refuge in God's complete words from the Sunan of Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 122}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports from Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Thawban, that when something startled [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] he said: «هو الله، الله ربي لا شريك له», He is God, God is my Lord, He has no partner {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 122}. From the Sunan of Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, from Amr ibn Shu'ayb, from his father, from his grandfather, he reports that the Prophet taught them words against fright: «أعوذ بكلمات الله التامة من غضبه وشر عباده، ومن همزات الشياطين، وأن يحضرون», I seek refuge in God's complete words from His anger and the evil of His servants, from the promptings of the devils and from their coming near. Abd Allah ibn Amr taught these to those of his children old enough to understand, and for those who were not he wrote them out and hung them on the child; al-Nawawi quotes al-Tirmidhi's grading of the report as good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 122}. He adds no remark of his own in this chapter, the second of his chapters on hardship after [[practice/dua-in-distress|the supplication of distress]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 122}.
