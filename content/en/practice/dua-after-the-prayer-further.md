## Summary
What al-Nawawi adds to finish the chapter on remembrances after the prayer: the tens of glorifying, praising and takbir, the two refuge suras, the supplication taught to Mu'adh, supplications from Ibn al-Sunni's book, and the praise of God and the blessing on the Prophet before asking. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 72}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi gives, from the Sunan of Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i, from Abd Allah ibn Amr, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said «خصلتان لا يحافظ عليهما عبد مسلم إلا دخل الجنة», two habits a Muslim servant keeps and enters Paradise: glorifying, praising and takbir ten times after every prayer, and at lying down takbir thirty-four times, praise and glorifying thirty-three {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 72}. He says its chain is sound except that it includes Ata ibn al-Sa'ib, about whom there is disagreement because of his confusion, and that Ayyub al-Sakhtiyani pointed to the soundness of his report {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 73}. Then from Uqba ibn Amir in the Sunan, that he was told to recite the two refuge suras after every prayer, and al-Nawawi explains that the sura of sincerity is recited with them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 73}. From Mu'adh, by a sound chain in Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i, a counsel: «اللهم أعني على ذكرك وشكرك وحسن عبادتك», help me to remember You, thank You and worship You well {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 73}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Anas, he wiped his forehead with his right hand and said «اللهم أذهب عني الهم والحزن», take away worry and grief {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 73}; from Abu Umama «اللهم اغفر لي ذنوبي وخطاياي كلها» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 73}; and from Abu Sa'id a glorification of God and a greeting on the messengers, the narrator unsure whether before or after the salam {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 73}. From Anas «اللهم اجعل خير عمري آخره وخير عملي خواتمه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 74}, and from Abu Bakra refuge from unbelief, poverty and the torment of the grave {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 74}. Last, from Fudala, by a chain al-Nawawi calls weak: begin with praise of God, then bless the Prophet, then ask what one wishes {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 74}.
