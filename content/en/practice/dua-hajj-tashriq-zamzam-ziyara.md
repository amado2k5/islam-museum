## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on the remembrances of the days of Tashriq at Mina, drinking Zamzam, the farewell to the House, the visit to the Prophet's grave and mosque in Medina, and the close of the pilgrimage remembrances. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 202}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports in Sahih Muslim from Nubayshah al-Khayr al-Hudhali, a Companion: «أيام التشريق أيام أكل وشرب وذكر الله تعالى», the days of Tashriq are days of eating, drinking and remembering God; so he recommends much remembrance, the best being Qur'an {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 202}. He says the sunna after stoning the first pillar is to stand facing the Ka'ba, praise, magnify and supplicate for about the time of Surat al-Baqara, to do the same at the middle one and not to stand at the third {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 202} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 203}. When one leaves Mina the pilgrimage is complete and no remembrance is left tied to it, only a traveller's {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 203}. He reports from Jabir «ماء زمزم لما شرب له», Zamzam water is for what it is drunk for, and says scholars recommend asking God for one's need as one drinks {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 203}. On leaving Mecca one makes the farewell circuit, goes to the Multazam and says «اللهم البيت بيتك والعبد عبدك وابن عبدك وابن أمتك», O God, the House is Your House and the servant Your servant {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 203}; a menstruating woman stands at the mosque door and says it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 204}. He then says every pilgrim should visit [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], on his route or not, calls it among the most important acts of nearness, and advises much blessing on him on the way {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 204}. At the grave one greets in a moderate voice, «السلام عليك يا رسول الله», testifying that he conveyed the message, fulfilled the trust and counselled the community {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 204}, then greets Abu Bakr and Umar and comes to the Rawda {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 205}. He reports from Abu Hurayra in the two Sahihs: «ما بين قبري ومنبري روضة من رياض الجنة», between my grave and my pulpit is a garden of the gardens of Paradise {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 205}. On departing one asks that it not be the last visit to the Prophet's sanctuary, and he closes saying this is the last he was enabled to gather of the pilgrimage remembrances {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 205}.
