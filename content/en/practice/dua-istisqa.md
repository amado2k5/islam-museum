## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar in the chapter on the prayer for rain: the well-known supplications, asking through a righteous man, the supplications reported of the Prophet, the form of the prayer and sermon, and al-Shafi'i on abundant forgiveness-seeking. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 176}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says the supplications for rain are well known, among them «اللهم اسقنا غيثا مغيثا هنيئا مريئا غدقا مجللا سحا عاما طبقا دائما», O God, give us rain that relieves, and «اللهم أنبت لنا الزرع وأدر لنا الضرع», make crops grow for us and fill the udders, and that abundant supplication and forgiveness-seeking in humility is recommended {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 176}. If there is among them a man known for righteousness, he says it is recommended to say «اللهم إنا نستسقي ونتشفع إليك بعبدك فلان», and reports in Sahih al-Bukhari that Umar ibn al-Khattab, in a drought, asked for rain through al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib; asking through the righteous also came from Mu'awiya and others {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 176}. The prayer is like the festival prayer, seven takbirs in the first rak'a and five in the second, then two sermons rich in forgiveness-seeking and supplication {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 176}. He reports in Sunan Abi Dawud, by a chain he calls sound on Muslim's condition, from Jabir, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], when weeping women came to him, said «اللهم اسقنا غيثا مغيثا مريا سريعا نافعا غير ضار عاجلا غير آجل», and the sky covered them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 177}; and by a sound chain from Amr ibn Shu'ayb, from his father, from his grandfather, «اللهم اسق عبادك وبهائمك وانشر رحمتك وأحي بلدك الميت» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 177}. From Aisha, by a chain Abu Dawud calls good, he gives the Prophet's coming out to the prayer ground, sitting on the pulpit, the sermon, raising his hands, turning his cloak and praying two rak'as, after which God raised a cloud and it rained {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 177}. He says the sermon before the prayer in that report is read as permitted, and the well-known view in the books of fiqh puts the prayer first {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 178}. He quotes al-Shafi'i's wording «اللهم أمرتنا بدعائك ووعدتنا إجابتك وقد دعوناك كما أمرتنا فأجبنا كما وعدتنا» and that forgiveness-seeking should be most of the speaker's words, as with Umar {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 178}.
