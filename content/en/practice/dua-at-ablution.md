## Summary
What is said at ablution and after it, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: naming God at the start, the two testimonies afterwards from Sahih Muslim, and the limb-by-limb supplications he reports from the early generations while saying nothing of them comes from the Prophet. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 27}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says one begins with «بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم», that «بسم الله» is enough, that someone who forgets says it during the ablution, and that the ablution is valid without it whether left out on purpose or by mistake; he calls this his school's view and that of most scholars {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 27}. He notes that al-Bayhaqi and others weakened the reports on naming God at ablution, and quotes [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]] as knowing no established report on it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 27}. After finishing he gives, from Sahih Muslim, from Umar ibn al-Khattab: «أشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له، وأشهد أن محمدا عبده ورسوله», the two testimonies, for which the report says the eight gates of Paradise are opened; al-Tirmidhi adds: «اللهم اجعلني من التوابين واجعلني من المتطهرين» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 28}. Of supplications over each limb he says nothing has come from [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], that the jurists recommended wordings handed down from the early generations, and he lists them limb by limb {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 29}. From Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, in the books of al-Nasa'i and Ibn al-Sunni, by a chain he calls sound: «اللهم اغفر لي ذنبي، ووسع لي في داري، وبارك لي في رزقي», a plea for forgiveness, room in one's home and blessing in one's provision {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 29}. He says that someone bathing says what was given for ablution, and likewise someone making tayammum {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 29} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 30}.
