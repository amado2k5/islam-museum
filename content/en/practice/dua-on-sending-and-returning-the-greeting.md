## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the Book of the Greeting on its ruling: one person's greeting for a group, the greeting sent by letter or messenger, the deaf, the mute and the child, and repeating the greeting on meeting again. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 247}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
In the chapter on the ruling of the greeting al-Nawawi reports from the Muwatta', from Zayd ibn Aslam, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said «إذا سلم واحد من القوم أجزأ عنهم», if one of the people greets, it suffices for them, and calls it a mursal report with a sound chain {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 247}. He gives the view of al-Mutawalli that one who is greeted from behind a wall, by letter or by messenger must return it when it reaches him {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 247}. From the two Sahihs, from Aisha, the Prophet said to her «هذا جبريل يقرأ عليك السلام», this is Gabriel, who sends you greeting, and she returned it adding «ورحمة الله وبركاته»; he notes some narrations lack the last word, and al-Tirmidhi has it, calling it good and sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 247}. In Abu Dawud the Prophet answered a son bringing his father's greeting: «وعليك السلام وعلى أبيك السلام»; al-Nawawi notes the narrator is unknown but reports on merits are tolerated {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 248}. From al-Mutawalli, the deaf are greeted in word and gesture, and a mute person's gesture stands for speech {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 248}. He holds the sound view is that a child's greeting must be returned, citing [[passage/quran-4-86|Qur'an 4:86]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 248}. A greeting is repeated on meeting again, also when a tree or wall came between, from Abu Hurayra in Abu Dawud {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 249}.
