## Overview
The day of adha is the tenth of Dhu'l-Hijja, one of the Muslims' two Eids, named for the udhiyya slaughtered on it. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11984}

## Its rulings
The Eid prayer is emphatic sunna for the Malikis and Shafi'is, obligatory on the operative Hanafi view, and a communal obligation for the Hanbalis; takbir is prescribed on the way, within the prayer, and after prayers during the tashriq days. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11984} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11985}

## The sacrifice
Jurists agree the udhiyya is prescribed on it and differ on its ruling: sunna for the majority, obligatory for the Hanafis. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11985}

## The day's practices
Keeping its night alive in worship and bathing are recommended, as the Prophet bathed on the fitr and adha days. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.11985}
