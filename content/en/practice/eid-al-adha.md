## Overview
The Feast of Sacrifice on the tenth of Dhu al-Hijja, the day the rites of the pilgrimage end: the festival prayer, then the slaughter of sacrifices and their distribution to the poor and relatives. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
The longest festival day in the Islamic calendar for many peoples; families remember the story of Abraham and Isma'il on it. {cite:wikidata}
