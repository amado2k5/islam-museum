## Overview
The celebration of the birthday of the Prophet on the twelfth of Rabi' al-Awwal in most of the Islamic world; processions, praise poems of the Prophet, markets and charitable projects. {cite:wikidata}

## Description
Most scholars approve it as a good custom, while some object that it is an innovation; its crowds in Egypt, Morocco and Sudan are among the largest popular celebrations. {cite:wikidata}
