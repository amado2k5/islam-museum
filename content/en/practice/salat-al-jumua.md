## Summary
The Friday prayer is an individual obligation on adult male Muslims, in the time of the zuhr prayer on Friday. The day was called 'aruba before Islam; it is related that Ka'b ibn Lu'ayy was the first to gather it and name it jumu'a. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18016} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18017}

## The day's merit
Ibn 'Abidin counted Friday the best day of the week and a festival day: it contains the hour of response, Adam was created on it and expelled from the Garden on it, and on it the people of Paradise visit their Lord. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18017}

## The Friday ghusl
Jurists agreed the Friday ghusl is legally required of those attending — "whoever comes to jumu'a, let him perform ghusl" — the majority calling it sunna, while a report from Ahmad makes it obligatory on "every adult". Its time begins at the second dawn; the majority held it is for the prayer not the day, so it is not recommended for one not attending, and all agreed a ghusl after the prayer does not count. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18018} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18019}

## Travel and fasting on it
The majority forbade travelling after midday on Friday before performing the prayer for one upon whom it is obligatory; the Hanafis called it prohibitively disliked. Singling out Friday for a fast is disliked for the majority — "let none of you fast Friday unless with a day before or after it" — the dislike lapsing if a day is adjoined. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18019}

## In Mustafa Hosny's programme 'Hadaya al-Rahman'
[[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]] makes the Friday prayer the 'gift' of the fifth episode of his programme [[work/hadaya-al-rahman|'Hadaya al-Rahman']]: he cites a hadith that whoever washes on Friday, comes to the mosque early on foot, sits near the imam and listens in silence earns for every step the reward of a year of fasting and night prayer, and explains that someone whose home is two hundred steps from the mosque gains as it were two hundred years. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:01:00} He calls for preparing the heart before the sermon, saying that the benefit of the Friday prayer depends not on the preacher's strength but on the listener's heart, since a single word can change a person until he meets his Lord. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:02:30} He says the angels stand at the mosque door recording the names of those who come, and when the imam mounts the pulpit they fold their books and go in to hear the sermon, so he urges worshippers not to be late. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:03:30}
