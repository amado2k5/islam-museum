## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the rest of the chapter on recitation: the sura after the Fatiha, then saying amin, then what a reader says on meeting a verse of mercy, of punishment or of glorification. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 48}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says reciting a sura or part of one after the Fatiha is a sunna, and that the prayer is sound if it is left {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 48}. He lists the suras that are the sunna in Friday dawn prayer, the two festivals, the Friday prayer, the sunna of dawn and the witr, saying well-known reports in the sound collections and elsewhere came on this, and that Muslim has it that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] did it in the dawn sunna {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 49}. He says «آمين», amin, is recommended after the Fatiha for every reader, that sound reports on its merit are many, and that it has four forms, the best known with lengthening and lightly {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 50}. He reports from Hudhayfa, in Muslim, that he prayed with the Prophet one night, who recited unhurriedly, glorifying at a verse of glorification, asking at a question and seeking refuge at a refuge, which he says is recommended for a reader in or outside prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 51}. He adds that a reader of the verse asking whether God is not the best of judges says «بلى وأنا على ذلك من الشاهدين», and at the verse of glorifying the Most High says «سبحان ربي الأعلى» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 51}.
