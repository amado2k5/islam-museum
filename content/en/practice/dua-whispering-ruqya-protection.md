## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers on whispering doubts, the recitation over the bitten and the afflicted, the protection recited over al-Hasan and al-Husayn, and a supplication for a boil. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 127}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
On whispering doubts al-Nawawi cites [[passage/quran-41-36|Qur'an 41:36]] and gives from the two Sahihs, from Abu Hurayra, that Satan asks a person who created this and that until he asks who created his Lord, and that on reaching that point one should seek refuge in God and stop {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 127}. From Sahih Muslim, from Uthman ibn Abi al-As, [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] told him to seek refuge and spit lightly to his left three times {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 128}. Over the afflicted and the bitten he gives from the two Sahihs, from Abu Said al-Khudri, the story of Companions who recited the opening sura over a bitten tribal chief; the Prophet asked how they knew it was a charm and told them to divide the fee {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 129}. From Sunan Abi Dawud, by a chain he calls sound, from Kharija ibn al-Salt on his uncle's authority, a madman was cured by the same recitation {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 130}. For children he gives from al-Bukhari, from Ibn Abbas, that the Prophet protected al-Hasan and al-Husayn with «أعيذكما بكلمات الله التامة من كل شيطان وهامة ومن كل عين لامة» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 131}. For a boil he gives a supplication asking that it be made small {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 131}.
