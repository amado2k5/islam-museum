## Overview
Khitan is the cutting of the foreskin of the male, and the name applies to the place of cutting as well; it is also called khifd and i'dhar — some reserve khitan for the male, khifd for the female, and i'dhar for both. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7550}

## Ruling
The Hanafis, Malikis and a report from Ahmad hold it a sunna for men, not obligatory — yet one of the marks of Islam such that if a town's people abandoned it the imam would fight them — and recommended for women per the Malikis, an honour per a Hanafi-Hanbali view; their proof: "circumcision is sunna for men and an honour for women" and "five of the fitra: circumcision, removing pubic hair…" {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7551}

## Obligation
The Shafi'is, Hanbalis and the implication of Sahnun's Maliki view hold it obligatory for men and women. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7551}
