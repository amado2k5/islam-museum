## Summary
"Fasting Ramadan" is an obligatory worship, one of [[concept/five-pillars|Islam's five pillars]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. Gabriel's hadith mentions it in defining [[concept/islam-din|Islam]]: "and you fast Ramadan" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.

## In the Qur'an
The Qur'an imposes the fast: [[passage/quran-2-183|al-Baqara 183]]. Then it names the month and mentions the licence for the sick and the traveller: [[passage/quran-2-185|al-Baqara 185]].

## In the hadith
The Prophet told the Najdi man "and fasting Ramadan"; the man asked whether anything else was upon him, and he said: "No — unless you volunteer" {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 46}.

## What is not covered here
The fast's rulings, what invalidates it, the fidya and voluntary fasting will be added in a later batch from the fiqh books, with each position's school identified.
