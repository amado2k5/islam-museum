## Summary
What is said on putting on a garment or a new one and on taking it off, and what is said to someone seen in new clothes, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar from Ibn al-Sunni's book, the Sunan and Sahih al-Bukhari. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 20}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says it is recommended to say «بسم الله» when dressing, and that naming God is recommended in every act {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 20}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, he reports that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] put on a garment he named it and said: «اللهم إني أسألك من خيره وخير ما هو له، وأعوذ بك من شره وشر ما هو له», asking for its good and the good it is for, and refuge from its harm {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 20}. For a new garment he gives, from Abu Sa'id, a report he calls sound and assigns to the Sunan of Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i: «اللهم لك الحمد أنت كسوتنيه، أسألك خيره وخير ما صنع له، وأعوذ بك من شره وشر ما صنع له» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 21}; and from al-Tirmidhi's book, from Umar: «الحمد لله الذي كساني ما أواري به عورتي وأتجمل به في حياتي», praise to God who clothed me with what covers me and adorns my life {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 21}. For someone seen in a new garment he cites, from Sahih al-Bukhari, the words to Umm Khalid, «أبلي وأخلقي», wear it out and make it old; and from the books of Ibn Majah and Ibn al-Sunni the words to Umar: «البس جديدا وعش حميدا ومت شهيدا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 21}. He adds a chapter on beginning with the right side in dressing and the left in undressing, and one on what is said when undressing, from Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Anas: «بسم الله الذي لا إله إلا هو» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 22}.
