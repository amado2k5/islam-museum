## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapters on the opening takbir and on what is said after it: the wording of the takbir, then the opening supplications that are reported, with his remarks on their grading. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 42}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi opens what is said in the prayer with the takbir of entry. He says the prayer is not valid without it, whether obligatory or voluntary, that its wording is «الله أكبر» or «الله الأكبر», and that caution lies in using the first {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 42}. He then says many reports came on what follows it, and that together they call for «الله أكبر كبيرا والحمد لله كثيرا وسبحان الله بكرة وأصيلا», then the supplication of turning the face, in which the worshipper turns to the Maker of the heavens and the earth and asks forgiveness and guidance to the best character, then «اللهم باعد بيني وبين خطاياي كما باعدت بين المشرق والمغرب», O God, set me as far from my errors as You set the east from the west {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 43}. He says all of this is established in the sound reports from [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 43}. He adds, from Aisha, «سبحانك اللهم وبحمدك وتبارك اسمك وتعالى جدك ولا إله غيرك», which he says al-Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah give by weak chains, and he quotes al-Bayhaqi that the soundest report on it is from Umar {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 44}. He says combining these is recommended for one praying alone, and for an imam if the followers allow it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 45}.
