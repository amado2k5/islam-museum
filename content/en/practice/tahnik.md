## The practice
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy describes tahnik as chewing a date and then rubbing it on the newborn's palate with a clean finger. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:08:30}

## Its place
The Sheikh mentions tahnik in the context of aqiqa and the shaving of the newborn's hair, and says it is done on the seventh day after birth (needs_source: the day is spoken once in the transcript). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:08:00}
