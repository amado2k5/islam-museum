## Summary
In Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lessons, honouring one's parents is a form of mercy, owed during their lives and after their death. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:14:30}

## During their lives
He says children should show mercy to their parents and honour them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:14:30} He says the reward returns to the child: whoever honours his father will be honoured by his own children when he grows old. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:16:00}

## A mother's right
He says the difference between a mother's right and a father's is great. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:17:30} He tells how Umar said to a man who had carried his mother around the Kaaba that he had not yet repaid her, not even one of her labour pangs. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:17:00}

## Kin and neighbours
He says honour includes the kin of both father and mother, and that one must fear God regarding one's parents. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:18:00} He adds that a neighbour must not be harmed. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep03 00:18:30}

## Duties of the child
He says parents have rights over their children, and wives have rights over their husbands; he treats these in his lesson on the rights of parents. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:25:30}

## After death
He lists duties towards a father after his death: hajj performed on his behalf, honouring his friends, and prayer for him by a righteous child. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:30:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy aadau-al-islam/ep02 00:30:30}

## Honouring parents in the fourth lesson of the series on rights
The Sheikh says the person most deserving of a man's companionship is his mother. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:18:30}
He counts among the greatest forms of honour that a son pleases his father and mother, living and dead. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:22:30}
He says that a righteous son's prayer for his father raises the father's rank in Paradise. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:28:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh cites the command to speak nobly to parents and the prohibition of rebuking them or saying 'uff' to them, holding that there is no smaller word than 'uff', that parents sowed good in their children, toiled over them and bore hardship, and he prays for them, health for the living and mercy for the dead. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep07 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep07 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep07 00:08:30}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
In al-Munjiyat Omar Abdelkafy relates the account of one of the three shut in the cave by the rock, who stood holding the bowl of milk until his parents woke at dawn and gave it to them before his wife and children, and mentions Jurayj, whose mother prayed against him and who was then accused of indecency. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:13:00} He says that kindness to parents after their death includes kindness to their friends. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:22:00}
