## Summary
The supplications al-Nawawi gives for the third and fourth takbirs of the funeral prayer, the prayer for a child, the closing greeting, and the rule for one who joins late. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 157}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says that what is required at the third takbir is a prayer for the dead, in the smallest form, and then he gives what is recommended. The soundest, he says, is from Sahih Muslim, from Awf ibn Malik, who memorised part of what [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said at a funeral: «اللهم اغفر له وارحمه وعافه واعف عنه وأكرم نزله ووسع مدخله» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 157}. From Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Bayhaqi, from Abu Hurayra, «اللهم اغفر لحينا وميتنا وصغيرنا وكبيرنا», which al-Hakim calls sound, and he quotes al-Bukhari that the soundest report on the subject is Awf's {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 157}. From Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah, from Abu Hurayra, «إذا صليتم على الميت فأخلصوا له الدعاء» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 158}. He gives a prayer al-Shafi'i chose, and one for a child asking that he be a forerunner, a predecessor and a store for his parents {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 158} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 159}. After the fourth takbir no words are required, and he recommends what al-Shafi'i set down, «اللهم لا تحرمنا أجره ولا تفتنا بعده» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 159}. From Ibn Abi Awfa, in al-Bayhaqi, he paused after the fourth to ask forgiveness and pray, saying the Prophet did so {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 159}. One then gives the greeting twice {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 159}, and he sets out the case of one who joins late {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 160}.
