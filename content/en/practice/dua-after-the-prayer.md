## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers at the start of the chapter on remembrances after the prayer: the scholars' agreement that remembrance after the prayer is recommended, the takbir and raised voice, the seeking of forgiveness, the declaration of oneness, the thirty-three glorifications, praises and takbirs, and the seeking of refuge. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi opens the chapter by saying the scholars agree that remembrance after the prayer is recommended, that many sound reports speak of it, and that he will give a part of the most important {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}. From al-Tirmidhi's book, from Abu Umama, [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] was asked which supplication is most heard, and said «جوف الليل الآخر، ودبر الصلوات المكتوبات», the last part of the night and the end of the obligatory prayers; al-Tirmidhi said it is good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}. From the two Sahihs, Ibn Abbas knew the prayer had ended by the takbir, and raising the voice in remembrance as people leave the prayer was in the Prophet's time {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}. From Sahih Muslim, from Thawban, on turning from the prayer he sought forgiveness three times and said «اللهم أنت السلام ومنك السلام، تباركت يا ذا الجلال والإكرام», O God, You are Peace and from You is peace {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 71}. From the two Sahihs, from al-Mughira ibn Shu'ba, a declaration of oneness ending «اللهم لا مانع لما أعطيت ولا معطي لما منعت», none can withhold what You give {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 71}; and from Muslim, from Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr, a longer one said after every prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 71}. Then Abu Hurayra's report of the poor emigrants, in the two Sahihs, with glorifying, praising and takbir thirty-three times after every prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 72}. From Muslim, from Ka'b ibn Ujra, «معقبات لا يخيب قائلهن أو فاعلهن», sequels whose sayer or doer is not disappointed; and from Abu Hurayra, thirty-three each and the hundred completed with the declaration of oneness {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 72}. From al-Bukhari, from Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, his seeking refuge after prayer from cowardice, the lowest old age, the trial of the world and the torment of the grave {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 72}.
