## Summary
What al-Nawawi gives next in the sleep chapter: reciting the Kafirun surah and the musabbihat, praise for God's favours, asking for hearing and sight, the last verses of al-Baqara, the verse of the Throne and the refuge surahs, and the dislike of sleeping without remembrance. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 92}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports in Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, from Nawfal al-Ashja'i, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] told him to recite the surah of the disbelievers and then sleep on its ending, for it is a freeing from associating partners with God {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 92}. From al-Irbad ibn Sariya, that the Prophet used to recite the musabbihat before lying down, and from Aisha that he would not sleep until he had recited the surahs of al-Isra and al-Zumar; he says al-Tirmidhi called it good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 92}. By a sound chain in Abu Dawud, from Ibn Umar, on taking his place the Prophet said «الحمد لله الذي كفاني وآواني وأطعمني وسقاني» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 92}. In Sahih Muslim, Ibn Umar told a man to say «اللهم أنت خلقت نفسي وأنت تتوفاها لك مماتها ومحياها», and said he heard it from the Prophet {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 93}. In Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Jabir, an angel and a devil race to a man who goes to bed; if he remembers God and sleeps, the angel guards him through the night {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 93}. From Aisha, on going to bed the Prophet said «اللهم أمتعني بسمعي وبصري واجعلهما الوارث مني», and al-Nawawi gives the scholars' readings of the word for inheritor {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 94}. From Ali, by a chain sound on the standard of al-Bukhari and Muslim, that he saw no sensible person sleeping before reciting the last three verses of Surat al-Baqara {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 94}. He gives the same of the verse of the Throne, and from Ibrahim al-Nakha'i that they used to teach them the two refuge surahs on going to bed {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 95}. He says it is best to do all that is mentioned, and if one cannot, to keep to the most important, and adds a chapter on the dislike of sleeping without remembrance of God {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 95}.
