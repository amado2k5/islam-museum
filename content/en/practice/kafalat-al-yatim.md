## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy takes up the orphan in the light of what he says about the Prophet's own orphanhood. He urges honouring the orphan and stroking his head, and in answer to a question about the wisdom of orphanhood he holds that a human being needs someone to help him from the time of conception. He warns against replacing tenderness with manufactured milk in the care of an infant. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:34:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:36:00}

## The orphan's hidden faults and high standing
The Sheikh explains why the Prophet was an orphan. The faults of an orphan do not show to anyone, his right is not diminished, and orphanhood teaches him to understand the conditions of people. He then explains what it means that God alone protects him. [[passage/quran-93-6|Qur'an 93:6]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:25:00}

## Honouring the orphan and his worth before God
The Sheikh urges honouring the orphan and stroking his head, and says that the house in which an orphan is honoured is the best of houses before God. He refers to a hadith that the recording does not name (needs_source: unnamed hadith). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:27:00}

## A question: is there a purpose in the Prophet's orphanhood?
The Sheikh answers a question about the wisdom of the Prophet's orphanhood. He holds that a human being needs someone to help him from the time of conception, and that the womb and the present life are stages that move a person from constriction to ease. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:34:00}

## The weakness of the human newborn and its need for care
The Sheikh compares young animals that stand and suckle within hours with the human newborn, which needs its mother (needs_source: the comparison between animal and human young is not sourced). He warns against replacing tenderness with manufactured milk. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:36:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Bustan al-Amal
The Sheikh says Islam cared for the weak: the orphan, the widow, the woman, the elderly, the sick, the poor, the needy and the debtor; he defines the orphan as one whose father died before puberty, while among animals it is one whose mother died, and calls a child whose mother died 'latim'. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:02:00} He proposes, 'by his weak opinion' as he says, that every family have an orphan boy or girl at its table each Friday, buy him new clothes before the feast like the children of the house, and take him on outings. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:03:00} He mentions the reward for stroking an orphan's head, a report that God is pleased with whoever quiets and pleases a crying orphan, that the best house is one where an orphan is honoured, the hadith 'I and the guardian of an orphan will be in Paradise like these two', and the report of the woman who will reach the gate of Paradise before the Prophet because she devoted herself to raising her orphans, stressing that this does not mean forbidding widows to remarry. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:05:00}
He says the Prophet was the greatest orphan in history and that 'the orphan pearl' is the one without equal, and lists great orphans: [[person/al-ghazali-abu-hamid|Abu Hamid al-Ghazali]], [[person/ibn-al-jawzi|Ibn al-Jawzi]], [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal]], [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]], [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]], [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan al-Thawri]] and [[person/abd-al-rahman-al-dakhil|Abd al-Rahman al-Dakhil]], holding that behind them stood mothers who raised them, and that when woman is sound the whole society is sound (needs_source: the orphanhood of some of them as the Sheikh states it). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:08:30} He criticises those who attack al-Bukhari, holding that if they studied his history and learning they would retract; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep17 00:06:00}
