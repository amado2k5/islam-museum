## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy takes up the orphan in the light of what he says about the Prophet's own orphanhood. He urges honouring the orphan and stroking his head, and in answer to a question about the wisdom of orphanhood he holds that a human being needs someone to help him from the time of conception. He warns against replacing tenderness with manufactured milk in the care of an infant. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:27:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:34:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:36:00}

## The orphan's hidden faults and high standing
The Sheikh explains why the Prophet was an orphan. The faults of an orphan do not show to anyone, his right is not diminished, and orphanhood teaches him to understand the conditions of people. He then explains what it means that God alone protects him. [[passage/quran-93-6|Qur'an 93:6]] {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:25:00}

## Honouring the orphan and his worth before God
The Sheikh urges honouring the orphan and stroking his head, and says that the house in which an orphan is honoured is the best of houses before God. He refers to a hadith that the recording does not name (needs_source: unnamed hadith). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:27:00}

## A question: is there a purpose in the Prophet's orphanhood?
The Sheikh answers a question about the wisdom of the Prophet's orphanhood. He holds that a human being needs someone to help him from the time of conception, and that the womb and the present life are stages that move a person from constriction to ease. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-fawzu-al-moubeen/ep05 00:34:00}
