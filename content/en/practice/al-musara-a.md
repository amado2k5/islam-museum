## Summary
Wrestling is a bodily contest, and the best-known wrestling in the sira is the bout between the Prophet and Rukana ibn Abd Yazid of Banu al-Muttalib. {cite:al-mawahib-al-laduniyya p. 308}

## Rukana's bout in al-Mawahib al-Laduniyya
Al-Qastallani reports that Rukana stood on a cowhide that ten men could not move. The Prophet invited him to wrestle, saying "if you throw me, you believe". The Prophet threw him, and Rukana did not believe. {cite:al-mawahib-al-laduniyya p. 308}

## Abu Nu'aym's version
Abu Nu'aym's Ma'rifat al-Sahaba has Rukana meet the Prophet in al-Abtah with goats. Rukana asks whether the Prophet will wrestle him; the Prophet asks what Rukana will stake, and Rukana names a sheep. The Prophet throws him, and Rukana asks for a return bout; the Prophet wins three times in all. {cite:marifat-sahaba-abu-nuaym entry 979} Ibn Hajar judges the chain of this report disputed. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p. 531}

## Where the report is filed
Al-Bayhaqi places the report in his Dala'il al-Nubuwwa under the heading on the Prophet's appeal to God against Rukana in wrestling. {cite:dalail-al-nubuwwa-bayhaqi chapter heading}

## With or without a stake
Rafiq Yunus al-Misri records wrestling among the contests permitted without a stake. {cite:usul-iqtisad-islami-0660 p. 229} See [[person/rukana|Rukana]] for the chain.

## Still needed
No date is given for this bout, so it is one undated report and not a story. The version with a sheep as stake needs its chain checked.
