## Overview
Istisqa' is asking for watering — asking God to send down rain by a prescribed means when it is needed. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2385}

## Ruling
The Shafi'is, Hanbalis and Muhammad b. al-Hasan of the Hanafis hold it an emphatic sunna whether by supplication with prayer or supplication alone, as the Prophet, his Companions and the Muslims after them did; Abu Hanifa held only the supplication is sunna. For the Malikis it is emphatic sunna in drought, recommended for the well-watered on behalf of the drought-stricken, and permissible otherwise. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2385}

## Basis
It is established by text and consensus: "ask your Lord's forgiveness — He will send the sky upon you in torrents," and the practice of the Prophet, his caliphs and the Muslims. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.2386}

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
[[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] mentions the rain prayer in three episodes of [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]]: water, he says, is provision, and provision is sought not by disobedience but by obedience, which is why the Prophet instituted the rain prayer when rain is scarce. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep03 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep18 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:04:00} He says the books of history and sira tell that whenever it was prayed in the time of the Prophet, his Companions and some of the Followers, rain fell; this is his statement. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep18 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep18 00:09:00} He links it to the hadith of Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani at al-Hudaybiya on those who say they were given rain by God's grace and mercy and those who credit it to the stars, and to the hadith of the keys of the unseen, which counts the coming of rain among what only God knows. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep18 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep18 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep20 00:02:00}
