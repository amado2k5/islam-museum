## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar of the traveller's remembrances on drawing near a land, in fear, on alighting, on returning, on seeing his town and entering his house, and what is said to one who has come back. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 225}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports in Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Aisha, that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] drew near a land he meant to enter he said «اللهم إني أسألك من خير هذه وخير ما جمعت فيها وأعوذ بك من شرها وشر ما جمعت فيها», O God, I ask You for the good of this land and of what You gathered in it, and refuge from its evil {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 225}. When afraid of a people he gives, in Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i by a sound chain, from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari «اللهم إنا نجعلك في نحورهم ونعوذ بك من شرورهم», we set You against their throats and seek refuge in You from their evils {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 225}. From Jabir in Ibn al-Sunni, «إذا تغولت لكم الغيلان فنادوا بالأذان», if the ghouls take shapes before you, call the adhan {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 225}. On alighting he reports in Muslim, the Muwatta' and al-Tirmidhi from Khawla bint Hakim «من نزل منزلا ثم قال أعوذ بكلمات الله التامات من شر ما خلق لم يضره شيء حتى يرتحل» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 226}, and from Ibn Umar in Abu Dawud a night supplication, «يا أرض ربي وربك الله أعوذ بالله من شرك وشر ما فيك» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 226}. On returning he says the sunna is what came in Ibn Umar's report, and he reports in Muslim from Anas that the Prophet said «آيبون تائبون عابدون لربنا حامدون», returning, repenting, worshipping, praising our Lord, said on the way back to Medina {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 226}. After the dawn prayer on a journey he reports from Abu Barza in Ibn al-Sunni that he raised his voice, «اللهم أصلح لي ديني الذي جعلته عصمة أمري» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 226} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 227}. On seeing his town one says «اللهم اجعل لنا بها قرارا ورزقا حسنا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 227}; on entering his house, from Ibn Abbas in Ibn al-Sunni, «توبا توبا لربنا أوبا لا يغادر حوبا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 227}. To one who has arrived one says «الحمد لله الذي سلمك», and he cites a verse on thankfulness, [[passage/quran-14-7|Qur'an 14:7]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 227}.
