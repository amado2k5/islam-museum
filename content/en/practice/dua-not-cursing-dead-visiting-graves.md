## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers on the prohibition of reviling the dead and its exceptions, on the greetings and prayers of one who visits graves, and on what a visitor is told to avoid. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 165}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
In the chapter on not reviling the dead, al-Nawawi gives from al-Bukhari, from Aisha, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said «لا تسبوا الأموات فإنهم قد أفضوا إلى ما قدموا», do not revile the dead, for they have reached what they sent ahead {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 165}; and from Ibn Umar, in Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, «اذكروا محاسن موتاكم وكفوا عن مساويهم», which al-Tirmidhi weakened {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 165} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 166}. He says scholars hold it forbidden to revile a dead Muslim who did not openly sin {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 166}, and prefers the view that the faults of dead unbelievers may be mentioned, and of dead Muslims who openly sinned or innovated when there is a benefit, to warn against them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 167}. On visiting graves he gives from Sahih Muslim, from Aisha, that the Prophet would go out at the end of the night to al-Baqi and say «السلام عليكم دار قوم مؤمنين» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 167}. From al-Tirmidhi, from Ibn Abbas, «السلام عليكم يا أهل القبور يغفر الله لنا ولكم أنتم سلفنا ونحن بالأثر», which al-Tirmidhi calls good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 168}. The visitor is advised to recite, remember God, pray for the people of the cemetery, and visit often {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 168}. The chapters end with the Prophet telling a woman weeping at a grave «اتقي الله واصبري» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 168}.
