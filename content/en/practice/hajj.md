## Overview
Hajj is purpose — or purpose toward a great object — and in usage the purpose of a specific place, the Sacred House and 'Arafa, at a specific time, the months of hajj, to perform specific acts: the standing at 'Arafa, tawaf and sa'y for the majority, under specific conditions. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6836}

## Its relation to 'umra
'Umra is the purpose of the House for tawaf and sa'y; hajj contains its acts and adds to them. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6836}

## Ruling
Hajj is an individual obligation on every legally capable person once in a lifetime, a pillar of Islam established by Book, Sunna and consensus — "owed to God by the people is hajj to the House, for whoever can make his way to it; and whoever disbelieves — God needs nothing of the worlds" — the phrasing of obligation itself, and disbelief placed opposite the command, proves the obligation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.6836}
