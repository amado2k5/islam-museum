## Summary
"The hajj" is the journey to the Sacred House at Mecca to perform particular rites; it is one of [[concept/five-pillars|Islam's five pillars]] {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. In the Qur'an: [[passage/quran-3-97|Al 'Imran 97]], in which hajj is a right of God upon the people "for whoever can make a way to it".

## In the Qur'an
Surat al-Hajj mentions the command to proclaim the hajj among people: [[passage/quran-22-27|al-Hajj 27]]. Another verse commands completing the hajj and the umra: [[passage/quran-2-196|al-Baqara 196]].

## In the hadith
The hajj is mentioned in the hadith of the building before the fast {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 8}. It is not mentioned in al-Bukhari's account of Gabriel's hadith in defining Islam {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 50}.

## What is not covered here
The hajj's rites, pillars and obligations, the difference between it and the umra, and the schools' disagreements will be added in a later batch from the fiqh and sira books. The [[event/farewell-pilgrimage|Farewell Pilgrimage]] is treated in its own entry where found.
