## Overview
## The washing of the Kaaba
The Kaaba's interior is washed once or twice a year in an official ceremony attended by invited dignitaries — princes, scholars and state guests — whom Bani Shayba, the key-bearers, receive at the door and join in the washing. The interior is cleaned with a mixture of Zamzam water and rose water, wiped with white cloths, and censed with agarwood before the door is sealed again.

## The origin of the rite
The washing belongs to the sunnah of the conquest: when the Prophet entered Mecca he went into the Kaaba with Bilal and Uthman ibn Talha and had its interior washed with Zamzam water; al-Bukhari records that he entered it at the conquest and prayed two rak'as inside. Since then the washing has been a standing custom performed by the custodial state — today usually ahead of Ramadan and again in Shawwal or Dhu al-Qa'da; it was once done twice a year and is now mostly annual.

## Zamzam and oud
Zamzam water is diluted with Taif rose water; palm-frond brooms and white cotton cloths are used, then censers of agarwood are brought to perfume the interior, the wall and mizab and the door of repentance are dried, and the door is closed with its famous lock and the key of the Al al-Shaibi. {cite:wikipedia-banu-shaybah-en}
