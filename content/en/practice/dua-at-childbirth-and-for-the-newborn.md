## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers at the close of the Book of the Remembrances of Marriage: what is said at childbirth, the call to prayer in the newborn's ear, and the tahnik with a prayer for the child. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 285}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi closes the Book of the Remembrances of Marriage with the chapters on childbirth. At childbirth he says one should say the supplication of distress often, and reports from Ibn al-Sunni, from Fatima, that when her delivery drew near [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] told Umm Salama and Zaynab bint Jahsh to recite by her the Throne Verse, [[passage/quran-2-255|Qur'an 2:255]], and a verse of al-A'raf, [[passage/quran-7-54|7:54]], and to seek refuge for her with the two suras of refuge {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 285}. Then the call to prayer in a newborn's ear: from Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi, from Abu Rafi', that he saw the Prophet give it in al-Husayn's ear when Fatima bore him; al-Tirmidhi calls it good and sound, and a group of al-Nawawi's colleagues say the call goes in the right ear and the iqama in the left {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 286}. For the tahnik, placing a chewed date in the infant's mouth, he gives from Abu Dawud, by a sound chain, from Aisha, that infants were brought to the Prophet and he prayed for them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 286}; and from the two Sahihs, from Asma' bint Abi Bakr, that he did so for Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr and blessed him {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 286}, and from Abu Musa, that he brought his newborn son to him and he named him Ibrahim and did the tahnik with a date {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 286}.
