## Summary
What is said on leaving the house, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: Umm Salama's report seeking refuge from straying, slipping, wronging and ignorance, and Anas's «باسم الله، توكلت على الله», both from the Sunan. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 22}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi gives, from Umm Salama, that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] left his house he said: «بسم الله توكلت على الله، اللهم إني أعوذ بك أن أضل أو أضل، أو أزل أو أزل، أو أظلم أو أظلم، أو أجهل أو يجهل علي», in God's name and in reliance on Him, seeking refuge from straying or being led astray, slipping or being made to slip, wronging or being wronged, acting ignorantly or being treated so. He calls it a sound report related by Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah, and notes that al-Tirmidhi graded it sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 22}. He records that Abu Dawud's wording is in the singular and al-Tirmidhi's in the plural, and that in Abu Dawud's version he never left her house without raising his eyes to the sky {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 22}. From the Sunan of Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i, from Anas, he reports: «باسم الله، توكلت على الله، ولا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله»; whoever says it is told he has been guided, sufficed and protected, and Satan draws away from him. He quotes al-Tirmidhi's grading of it as good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 23}. From the books of Ibn Majah and Ibn al-Sunni, from Abu Hurayra: «بسم الله، التكلان على الله، لا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 23}.
