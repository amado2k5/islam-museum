## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapters on Friday morning and sunrise: more remembrance and blessing on the Prophet on Fridays, seeking forgiveness before the dawn prayer, the hour of answer, and what is said when the sun rises. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 85}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says that whatever is said on other days is said on Friday, that remembrance is recommended more strongly on it, and that blessing on [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] is added in greater number {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 85}. From Anas, in Ibn al-Sunni's book: whoever says on Friday morning before the dawn prayer «أستغفر الله الذي لا إله إلا هو الحي القيوم وأتوب إليه» three times, God forgives his sins even if like the foam of the sea {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 85}. He says it is recommended to supplicate much through the whole day of Friday in hope of meeting the hour of answer, that scholars differed over it in many opinions, and that in his view the right one is what is established in Sahih Muslim from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari: that it lies between the imam's sitting on the pulpit and the end of the prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 85}. In the chapter on what is said when the sun rises, he reports from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri in Ibn al-Sunni, by a weak chain, that the Prophet said «الحمد لله الذي جللنا اليوم عافيته وجاء بالشمس من مطلعها», then bore witness to God's oneness and asked for the soundness of his religion, this world and the next {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 86}. From Ibn Mas'ud, a report that stops at him, that when told the sun had risen he said «الحمد لله الذي وهب لنا هذا اليوم وأقالنا فيه من عثراتنا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 86}. In the chapter for when the sun climbs, from Amr ibn Abasa: every creature of God glorifies and praises Him then, except the devil and the most rebellious of mankind, whom the Prophet described as the worst of creatures {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 86}.
