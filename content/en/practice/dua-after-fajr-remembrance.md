## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapter urging remembrance of God after the dawn prayer: the merit of sitting in remembrance until sunrise, ten tahlils, and supplications reported after the dawn prayer. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 74}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says that the noblest time for remembrance in the day is after the dawn prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 74}. He reports from Anas, in al-Tirmidhi's book and others, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] said that whoever prays the dawn prayer in a congregation, then sits remembering God until the sun rises, then prays two rak'as, has the reward of a full pilgrimage and a full lesser pilgrimage, and he says al-Tirmidhi called it good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 74}. From Abu Dharr, in al-Tirmidhi's book and others: whoever says after the dawn prayer, with his legs still folded and before speaking, «لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له له الملك وله الحمد يحيي ويميت وهو على كل شيء قدير» ten times, God writes ten good deeds for him, erases ten sins, raises him ten ranks, and keeps him that day safe from every harm; al-Tirmidhi called it good, and in some copies sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 75}. From Umm Salama, in Ahmad's Musnad, Ibn Majah's Sunan and Ibn al-Sunni's book: «اللهم إني أسألك علما نافعا وعملا متقبلا ورزقا طيبا», O God, I ask You for useful knowledge, accepted work and good provision {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 75}. From Suhayb, in Ibn al-Sunni's book, that the Prophet moved his lips after the dawn prayer saying «اللهم بك أحاول وبك أصاول وبك أقاتل», O God, by You I strive, by You I attack, by You I fight {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 75}. He closes with al-Baghawi's report in Sharh al-Sunna, from Alqama ibn Qays, that it had reached them that the earth cries out to God over a scholar's sleeping after the dawn prayer {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 76}.
