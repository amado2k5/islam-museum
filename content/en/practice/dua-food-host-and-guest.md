## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on the guest's supplication for the hosts, supplication for one who gave drink, urging people to hospitality, praise of one who honours his guest, and what is said after leaving the meal. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 237}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports in Sahih Muslim from Abd Allah ibn Busr that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] stayed with his father, food and drink were brought, and when his father asked him to pray for them he said «اللهم بارك لهم فيما رزقتهم واغفر لهم وارحمهم», O God, bless for them what You have provided, forgive them and have mercy on them {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 237} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 238}. From Anas in Sunan Abi Dawud, by a sound chain, that he came to Sa'd ibn Ubada, ate bread and oil and said «أفطر عندكم الصائمون وأكل طعامكم الأبرار وصلت عليكم الملائكة», and al-Nawawi says the like happened with Sa'd ibn Mu'adh in Ibn Majah's report, so there were two incidents {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 238}. Through a man, from Jabir, in Abu Dawud, that when Abu al-Haytham ibn al-Tayyihan made food the Prophet said «أثيبوا أخاكم», reward your brother, explaining that his reward is that the man be prayed for when one has eaten in his house {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 238}. For one who gave drink he reports in Muslim from al-Miqdad «اللهم أطعم من أطعمني واسق من سقاني», from Amr ibn al-Hamiq in Ibn al-Sunni «اللهم أمتعه بشبابه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 238}, and from Amr ibn Akhtab «اللهم جمله», make him comely {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 239}. To urge hospitality he reports from Abu Hurayra in the two Sahihs that the Prophet asked whether no man would host a visitor, and an Ansari stood {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 239}. In praise of one who honours his guest he gives the Ansari who put out the lamp to make the guest think they were eating, with a verse on preferring others [[passage/quran-59-9|Qur'an 59:9]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 239} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 240}, and «من كان يؤمن بالله واليوم الآخر فليكرم ضيفه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 240}. After leaving the meal he reports from Aisha in Ibn al-Sunni «أذيبوا طعامكم بذكر الله عز وجل والصلاة ولا تناموا عليه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 240}.
