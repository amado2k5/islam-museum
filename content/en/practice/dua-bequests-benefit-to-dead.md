## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers on a dying person's wishes about his shroud, burial and prayer, on whether prayers and recitation reach the dead, and on praising the dead. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 163}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
On bequests concerning the dead, al-Nawawi gives from al-Bukhari, from Aisha, that Abu Bakr asked in how many garments [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] had been shrouded, and she said three, and he asked to be shrouded in the garment he wore in his illness {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 163}. From Sahih Muslim he gives Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas asking for a niche grave and bricks stood upright over him as was done for the Prophet {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 163} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 164}. He then says the dead person is not to be followed in everything he bequeathed, but the matter is put to scholars; a wish to be moved to another town is not carried out, nor is a silk shroud for a man {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 164}. On what benefits the dead, he reports the scholars' agreement that prayer for them benefits them and its reward reaches them, arguing from [[passage/quran-59-10|Qur'an 59:10]] {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 164} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 165}. On the reward of recitation, he says al-Shafi'i's well-known view is that it does not reach, while Ahmad and others held that it does {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 165}.
