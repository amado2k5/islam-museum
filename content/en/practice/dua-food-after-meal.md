## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on talk at the meal, eating without being satisfied, eating with one who has an affliction, the host's urging a guest to eat, and then the remembrances on finishing the meal and what is reported for them. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 234}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi quotes al-Ghazali in the Ihya that among the manners of eating are good conversation and tales of the righteous {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 234}. For those who eat without being filled he reports in Sunan Abi Dawud and Ibn Majah, from Wahshi ibn Harb, that the Companions complained of it to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], who said «فاجتمعوا على طعامكم واذكروا اسم الله يبارك لكم فيه», gather over your food and mention God's name and it will be blessed for you {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 234}. From Jabir in al-Tirmidhi and elsewhere, that he took a leper's hand and put it with his in the bowl, saying «كل بسم الله ثقة بالله وتوكلا عليه» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 234}. For the host's saying "eat" to a guest he draws on Abu Hurayra's report in al-Bukhari, where the Prophet kept saying "drink" until Abu Hurayra said he had no room left {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 234} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 235}. On finishing he reports in al-Bukhari from Abu Umama «الحمد لله كثيرا طيبا مباركا فيه غير مكفي ولا مودع ولا مستغنى عنه ربنا», then sets out al-Harbi's and al-Khattabi's differing readings of what the pronoun refers to {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 235} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 236}. He reports in Muslim from Anas that God is pleased with the servant who praises Him over a morsel {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 236}; from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri in Abu Dawud and al-Tirmidhi «الحمد لله الذي أطعمنا وسقانا وجعلنا مسلمين» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 236}; and from Abu Ayyub, by a sound chain, «الحمد لله الذي أطعم وسقى وسوغه وجعل له مخرجا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 236}. From Mu'adh ibn Anas, that whoever thanks God for his food has his earlier sins forgiven, which al-Tirmidhi calls good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 236}. From Ibn Abbas, say «اللهم بارك لنا فيه وأطعمنا خيرا منه», and for milk «وزدنا منه», which al-Tirmidhi calls good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 237}.
