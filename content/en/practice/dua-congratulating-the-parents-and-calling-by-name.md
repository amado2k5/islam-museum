## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the Book of Names on congratulating one who has been given a child and the reply, the names forbidden or disliked, calling someone whose name is not known, and changing a name to a better one. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 289}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi gives first the congratulation to one who has been given a child. He says it is recommended to congratulate with what came from al-Husayn, who taught a man to say «بارك الله لك في الموهوب لك، وشكرت الواهب، وبلغ أشده، ورزقت بره», God bless you in what you were given; and the reply «بارك الله لك، وبارك عليك، وجزاك الله خيراً» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 289}. In the chapter on disliked names he reports from Sahih Muslim, from Samura ibn Jundab, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] forbade naming a boy Yasar, Rabah, Najah or Aflah {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 289}, and from the two Sahihs, from Abu Hurayra, that the lowest name before God is that of a man called king of kings {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 289}. On calling someone whose name is unknown he says to use words that do not hurt and hold no lie or flattery, and reports from Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah, by a good chain, from Bashir ibn Ma'bad, that the Prophet said to a man walking among graves in sandals «يا صاحب السبتيتين», owner of the two sandals {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 290}. On changing a name to a better, from the two Sahihs, Barra was named Zaynab {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 291}, and from Ibn Abbas the Prophet turned Juwayriya's name from Barra {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 291}.
