## Summary
What is said on entering the house, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: naming God and greeting the household, the supplication «اللهم إني أسألك خير المولج وخير المخرج» from Sunan Abi Dawud, and what is said on entering an empty house, from the Muwatta. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 23}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says it is recommended to say «باسم الله», to remember God much and to give the greeting of peace, whether or not anyone is in the house, and he cites a verse of [[passage/quran-24-61|Surat al-Nur]] for it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 23}. From al-Tirmidhi's book, from Anas, he reports that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] told him to greet his family on entering, as a blessing on him and his household, and he quotes al-Tirmidhi's grading of it as good and sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 23}. From Sunan Abi Dawud, from Abu Malik al-Ash'ari: «اللهم إني أسألك خير المولج وخير المخرج، بسم الله ولجنا، وبسم الله خرجنا، وعلى الله ربنا توكلنا», asking for the best of entering and of leaving, then greeting the household; he notes that Abu Dawud did not weaken it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 23}. From Sahih Muslim, from Jabir, he gives the report that remembering God on entering and at food leaves Satan no lodging and no supper {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 24}. From Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Abd Allah ibn Amr, by a chain he calls weak: «الحمد لله الذي كفاني وآواني، والحمد لله الذي أطعمني وسقاني، والحمد لله الذي من علي، أسألك أن تجيرني من النار» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 24}. From Malik's Muwatta he passes on a report that on entering a house no one lives in it is recommended to greet oneself and God's righteous servants {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 25}.
