## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on the remembrances when rain falls and after it, his discussion of the saying that rain came by such-and-such a star, and what is said when rain is so heavy that harm is feared, from the two Sahihs and other books. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 181}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi reports in Sahih al-Bukhari from Aisha that when [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] saw rain he said «اللهم صيبا نافعا», O God, a beneficial downpour {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 181}, and in Sunan Ibn Majah that he said it twice or thrice {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 182}. He gives a mursal report in al-Shafi'i's al-Umm: «اطلبوا استجابة الدعاء عند التقاء الجيوش وإقامة الصلاة», seek the answering of supplication at the meeting of armies and when the prayer is established {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 182}. For after the rain he reports in the two Sahihs from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani that the Prophet led them in the dawn prayer at al-Hudaybiya after night rain and told them God had said that some of His servants had risen believing in Him and some denying: whoever said rain came by God's favour and mercy believes in God and denies the star, and whoever said it came by such-and-such a star-setting denies God and believes in the star {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 182}. Al-Nawawi says scholars hold that one who says it meaning that the star is the maker becomes an unbeliever, while one who means it as a sign, with the act God's, does not; he prefers that it is disliked, being a phrase of unbelievers, and says thanking God for the rain is recommended {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 182}. For heavy rain he reports from Anas in the two Sahihs that a man said on a Friday, while the Prophet was preaching, that goods perished and roads were cut, and he raised his hands and said «اللهم أغثنا»; after a week of rain the man complained again and he said «اللهم حوالينا ولا علينا», around us and not upon us, naming the hillocks, valley floors and places where trees grow, and the clouds broke {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 182} {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 183}. He notes that al-Bukhari's wording has «اللهم اسقنا» in place of «أغثنا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 183}.
