## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the Book of the Remembrances of the Campaign on the supplications and remembrances reported for the meeting of two ranks and for fearing a people, and on disliking a raised voice in fighting. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 209}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi gathers what is said at meeting the enemy. He begins with a report that opens on the previous page, in which [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] tells the people not to wish for an encounter, to ask God for well-being and to be patient when they meet, and then prays: «اللهم منزل الكتاب، مجري السحاب، وهازم الأحزاب», O God, who sent down the Book and defeat the confederates {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 209}. From Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i, from Anas, that when going out to fight he said: «اللهم أنت عضدي ونصيري، بك أحول، وبك أصول، وبك أقاتل», You are my arm and helper; by You I attack and fight; al-Tirmidhi calls it good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 209}. From Abu Dawud and al-Nasa'i, by a sound chain, from Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, that when he feared a people he said: «اللهم إنا نجعلك في نحورهم، ونعوذ بك من شرورهم», we set You against them and seek refuge in You from their evils {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 210}. From al-Shafi'i in al-Umm, by a mursal chain, the seeking of an answer to prayer when armies meet; he adds that reciting Qur'an and the supplication of distress are strongly recommended {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 210}. He lists further remembrances, saying all have a strong urging and have been tried {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 211}, and from Qays ibn Ubad that the Companions disliked noise in fighting {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 211}.
