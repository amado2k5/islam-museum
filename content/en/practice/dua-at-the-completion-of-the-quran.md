## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in his section on the etiquette of completing the Qur'an: fasting on the day of completion, attending the gathering, the early scholars' reports on supplicating then, and persisting in supplication after the completion. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 104}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
In his section on the etiquette of completing the Qur'an, al-Nawawi says that a reader completing it alone is encouraged to do so in a prayer, that a group completing it together does so at the start of the night or the day, and that fasting on the day of completion is recommended unless it falls on a day when the law forbids fasting {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 104}. He says it is sound that Talha ibn Musarrif, al-Musayyib ibn Rafi' and Habib ibn Abi Thabit, Followers of Kufa, began the day fasting on which they completed it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 104}. Attending the gathering of completion is recommended, both for one who reads and for one who cannot read well, and he gives from the two Sahihs that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] ordered menstruating women to go out on the festival day to witness the good and the supplication of the Muslims {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 104}. Ibn Abi Dawud reports by two sound chains, from Qatada, that Anas ibn Malik gathered his family and supplicated when he completed the Qur'an. From al-Hakam ibn Utayba he reports that Mujahid and Abda ibn Abi Lubaba sent for him because they wished to complete it, and supplication is answered at the completion {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 104}. Al-Nawawi then says supplication after the completion is strongly recommended, and gives from al-Darimi's Musnad, from Humayd al-A'raj, that when one reads the Qur'an and then supplicates, four thousand angels say amen {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 105}. One should persist, ask for important matters in comprehensive words, mostly for the hereafter and for the Muslims, and he points to his book Adab al-Qur'an {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 105}. On finishing, he says it is recommended to begin another completion at once, as the early scholars favoured {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 105}.
