## Summary
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy describes the etiquette of eating, including naming God at the start of the meal, and counts these among great Islamic etiquettes {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep02 00:26:30}.

## Naming God at the start of the meal
The speaker says the one who eats names God when he eats (needs_source: partly garbled) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep02 00:26:30}.

## Other etiquettes
The speaker lists several etiquettes of eating, calls them great Islamic etiquettes, and says that some of them bring the one who observes them a reward; the detail is not complete in the transcript (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep02 00:27:00}.

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme al-Munjiyat
In al-Munjiyat Omar Abdelkafy mentions among table manners that the Prophet did not eat lizard when it was offered while Khalid ibn al-Walid did, that Anas ibn Malik came to love gourd because he saw the Prophet seek it out in the dish, saying God's name over food, the young waiting until the elder reaches out, the host not overburdening himself for guests, and that the Prophet sipped water rather than gulping it. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep12 00:07:00}
