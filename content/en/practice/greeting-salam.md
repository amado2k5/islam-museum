## Overview
Among the senses of taslim in the language is conveying; taslim is the salam: the worshipper's taslim ends his prayer with "al-salamu 'alaykum," and one who sallama 'ala a people greets them with the salam, saying "salam 'alayk." The jurists' usage does not depart from these senses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5084}

## Greeting as a brother's right in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lesson
The Sheikh says that greeting on meeting is one of a Muslim's rights over his brother, speaks of people who stop greeting relatives over a quarrel, and urges that greetings be spread. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep03 00:03:30}

## From Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's Tafsir lectures (episode 10)
The Sheikh says he grieves for one who breaks off the prayer, because the worshipper says in it a greeting of peace upon God's righteous servants; he then counts the reward as merchants count accounts: thirty good deeds for the greeting (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:20:30}.

He says that returning the fuller greeting with the addition "and the mercy of God" earns forty good deeds, that beginning the greeting is a Sunna and returning it an obligation, and that the reward multiplies with the number of people greeted, say a thousand, or ten thousand at a conference (needs_source) {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:21:00}.

He urges that the greeting come from the heart and not only from the mouth, for it is the heart that gives the greeting, and he recalls God's command to repel an offence with what is better, so that with someone between whom and you there is enmity things change {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10 00:21:30}.

## In Abd al-Rahman Mansour's programme 'Ahalina'
[[person/abd-al-rahman-mansour|Abd al-Rahman Mansour]] makes giving the greeting of peace the first of "the keys to hearts" in his programme [[work/ahaleena|'Ahalina']], calling it "the messenger of love" and the road to people's hearts. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:05:00} What is meant, he says, is what the Prophet taught, not "good morning", "bonjour" or "hi", and he cites the hadith on spreading the greeting as a means to mutual love. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:03:30} Whoever greets and receives no reply loses nothing, he says, citing a hadith graded sound by Shaykh al-Albani in Sahih al-Jami': that al-Salam is one of the names of God, and that if people do not return a Muslim's greeting, those better than they, the angels, return it. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:04:30}

The greeting, he says, belongs to arriving and to leaving alike, and the first is no more due than the last. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:05:00} Its forms, as he sees them, include sending greetings through others and delivering them as a trust, as Jibril conveyed greetings to Khadija, and greetings by phone message and call. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:06:00} He advises a father to give the greeting of peace on coming in to his family instead of "good evening", citing a hadith related by Anas, so that it is a blessing on him and his household. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep06 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep06 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:11:00} He teaches that a child entering among his father's guests should ask leave, greet, shake hands and introduce himself. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep11 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep11 00:09:30}
