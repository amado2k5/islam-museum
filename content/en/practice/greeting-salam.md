## Overview
Among the senses of taslim in the language is conveying; taslim is the salam: the worshipper's taslim ends his prayer with "al-salamu 'alaykum," and one who sallama 'ala a people greets them with the salam, saying "salam 'alayk." The jurists' usage does not depart from these senses. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.5084}
