## Overview
Ziyara is going to a person or place; customarily, seeking out the one visited to honour and keep company with him; its rulings differ with its causes, the one visited, and the visitor. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9284}

## Visiting graves
Visiting Muslim graves is sunna for men without travel, on the report "I had forbidden you the visiting of graves — so visit them." {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9284}

## The Prophet's grave
Visiting his grave is among the most important devotions and best of recommended acts, quoted in al-Fath al-Qadir from Farsi's Manasik and Sharh al-Mukhtar as near to obligatory; and the report "whoever visits my grave, my intercession is due for him." {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9284}

## In al-Sha'rawi's programme "Liqa' al-Iman"
In episode 11 of [[work/likaa-al-iman|Liqa' al-Iman]] [[person/muhammad-mutawalli-al-shaarawi|al-Sha'rawi]] is asked whether the dead sense the living. He says the question should not have been asked once we were commanded to greet the people of the graves: if they had no hearing, greeting them would be pointless. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:01:00} He cites the Prophet addressing the slain of Quraysh at Badr, and his reply to those who wondered that the living hear no better than they do, but the dead cannot answer. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep11 00:01:30}

In episode 23 he says visiting graves has no set occasion: it is for reflection at any time, and he does not favour timing it to the festival day or the like; he asks that the visitor visit all the graves and greet everyone, not only his own dead, since he does not know which of them is shown mercy. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep23 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep23 00:05:30} He holds that its wisdom is to remind of the hereafter and of those who were mayors or pashas and are now in the dust. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep23 00:06:00} In episode 27 he distinguishes desecrating graves, done to insult, humiliate and expose, from moving graves when a cemetery has grown too small, digging and transferring the remains, which for him is not desecration. {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep27 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-muhammad-metwally-al-shaarawy likaa-al-iman/ep27 00:02:00}
