## Overview
Hifz is guarding a thing from loss — attending to it without heedlessness; one "memorises the Quran" when he retains it by heart; the technical sense does not depart from this. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7135}

## Memorising what is recited in prayer
Those who hold the Fatiha a pillar agree it is obligatory on every legally capable person; if unable he must acquire the ability — by learning, from a mushaf, or by being prompted. For the Shafi'is and Hanbalis it is required each rak'a except a latecomer's — if ignorant of it and time is short, seven verses, then dhikr, then standing its length. For the Malikis recitation drops for the unable; for the Hanafis a long verse or three short ones suffice — the Fatiha not fixed but obligatory. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7135} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7136}

## Precedence in imamate
The majority prefer the more learned in fiqh to the best reciter even if a full hafiz, so long as he retains enough Quran for a valid prayer — because the incidents of prayer are unbounded and need knowledge; and because the Prophet put Abu Bakr forward while some Companions knew more of the Quran than he. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.7136}

## A child's memorisation in Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy's lesson
The Sheikh counts among the best things a parent can leave to his children that they memorise the Qur'an by heart, and says parents are honoured when their son or daughter does so. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:12:00}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's programme Wa Nafsin wa ma Sawwaha
The Sheikh holds that having a child memorise the Qur'an from the age of five or six is a father's first kindness to his child, that the child can memorise it in three or four years, provided the teacher explains meanings at the child's level so he is not like a parrot, because the Qur'an contains knowledge, history, parables, the hereafter and God's oneness, and builds a command of Arabic. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:08:00} He criticises that the Arabic lesson has become the children's most disliked class and the grammar teacher the heaviest of teachers, and holds that they will love Arabic only if they memorise the Qur'an young; this is his view. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy wa-nafsun-ma-sawaha/ep14 00:07:30}
