## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapter on the tashahhud in the prayer: how many there are, the wordings from Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas and Abu Musa, and the rulings on its words. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 62}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says a prayer of three or four units has two tashahhuds, that the last is required for al-Shafi'i and Ahmad, and that the first is a sunna for al-Shafi'i {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 62}. He says three tashahhuds are established from [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]: the report of Ibn Mas'ud, given by al-Bukhari and Muslim, and those of Ibn Abbas and Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, given by Muslim, all opening with the word «التحيات», the greetings, and ending with the two testimonies {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 63}. He quotes al-Bayhaqi that three reports are established from the Prophet, and others that all three are sound and Ibn Mas'ud's the soundest, and says any of them may be used, al-Shafi'i preferring Ibn Abbas's for its added word «المباركات» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 64}. He says saying the basmala before «التحيات» is not recommended, since al-Bukhari, al-Nasa'i and others judged that addition unsound, and that the chosen view is not to use it {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 65}. He says the order of the tashahhud is recommended, and that the sunna is to say it quietly, reporting from Ibn Mas'ud «من السنة أن يخفي التشهد», which al-Tirmidhi called good {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 66}.
