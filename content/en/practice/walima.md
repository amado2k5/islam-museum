## Overview
The walima is food prepared for an occasion, most famously the wedding feast. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17946}

## Ruling
The majority hold it a sunna, not obligatory — were it obligatory it would be exacted from the living and from the estate like other dues; a Shafi'i view, a Maliki view and a report from Ahmad cited by Ibn 'Aqil hold it obligatory, on the report of 'Abd al-Rahman b. 'Awf: the Prophet saw the trace of yellow on him, he said "I married an Ansari woman," and he told him "give a feast, even with one sheep." {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.17946}
