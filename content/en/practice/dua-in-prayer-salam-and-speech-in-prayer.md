## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in the chapter on the salam that releases one from the prayer and the chapter on what a worshipper says if something befalls him: the wording and ruling of the salam, and the glorification said by men. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 69}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says the salam that releases one from the prayer is one of its pillars, without which it is not valid, which he gives as the view of al-Shafi'i, Malik, Ahmad and most of the early and later scholars {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 69}. He says the fullest form is to say «السلام عليكم ورحمة الله», the greeting of peace to you, with God's mercy, to the right and the same to the left, and that in his school adding «وبركاته», and His blessings, is not recommended, because it goes against what is best known from [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], though it appears in one report of Abu Dawud {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 69}. He says one salam is required and the second is a sunna, and that what must be said is «السلام عليكم» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}. He quotes al-Qadi Abu al-Tayyib that after the imam's salam the follower may give his own at once or stay seated to supplicate at length {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}. In the chapter on what is said when something arises in the prayer he reports from Sahl ibn Sa'd al-Sa'idi in the two Sahihs that the Prophet said that whoever is struck by something in his prayer should say «سبحان الله», and in one report that men glorify and women clap {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 70}.
