## Overview
## Halq in the rites
Halq — shaving the head — is the closing act of hajj and umrah: the rites end for men in shaving or shortening the hair, while a woman does not shave but cuts a fingertip's length from her hair. On the day of sacrifice, after stoning the Jamrat al-Aqaba, the pilgrim enters the first release by shaving or shortening — everything of ihram is then lawful again except women — and the second release, after the tawaf of ifada and the sa'y, releases everything.

## The Prophet's prayer for the shavers
On his farewell hajj the Prophet shaved his own head and prayed three times for the shavers and once for the shorteners; al-Bukhari has: "O God, have mercy on the shavers — they said: and the shorteners, Messenger of God? — O God, have mercy on the shavers — they said: and the shorteners? — and the shorteners." Most jurists on that basis rank shaving above shortening for men.

## Where and how
In earlier centuries heads were shaved at the manashir in Muzdalifa and Mina — Ibn Jubayr and the travellers describe the barbers' stalls and the shaving domes. Today shaving happens in licensed barber salons in Mecca and Mina with disposable blades, and women are exempted from shaving — they shorten — since shaving for a woman is disapproved in the accepted view. {cite:wikipedia-kiswah-en}
