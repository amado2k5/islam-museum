## Summary
What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar on the night at Muzdalifa, the halt at the Sacred Monument, the setting out for Mina, and then the stoning, the sacrifice and the shaving on the day of sacrifice. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 200}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi notes that the night at Muzdalifa is the eve of the festival and has the honour of its time joined to the honour of its place, and that on setting out the pilgrim says «إليك اللهم أرغب وإياك أرجو فتقبل نسكي ووفقني», to You, O God, I turn and in You I hope, so accept my rite and grant me success {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 200}. In the chapter on Muzdalifa he cites a verse on that place, [[passage/quran-2-198|Qur'an 2:198]]; he recommends much supplication, talbiya and Qur'an, and gives this wording: «اللهم إني أسألك أن ترزقني في هذا المكان جوامع الخير كله وأن تصلح شأني كله», I ask You to grant me in this place all that is good and to set all my affairs right {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 200}. After praying the dawn prayer at its first hour the pilgrim goes to the Sacred Monument, a small hill at the end of Muzdalifa called Quzah, climbs it if able or stands beneath it facing the Ka'ba, praising, magnifying, declaring God one, glorifying, saying the talbiya and supplicating {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 200}. He gives «اللهم كما وقفتنا فيه وأريتنا إياه فوفقنا لذكرك كما هديتنا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 201} and «اللهم لك الحمد كله ولك الكمال كله ولك الجلال كله» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 201}. On setting out for Mina he stresses the talbiya as its last time, and on arrival the pilgrim says «الحمد لله الذي بلغنيها سالما معافى», praise to God who brought me to it safe and well {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 201}. He says the talbiya stops at the first pebble at the Aqaba pillar, takbir goes with each pebble, and one does not stand there to supplicate {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 201}. At the sacrifice he gives «بسم الله والله أكبر» and «اللهم منك وإليك تقبل مني» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 202}; at the shaving, a wording some scholars of his school favour, ending «الحمد لله الذي قضى عنا نسكنا» {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 202}.
