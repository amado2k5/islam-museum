## Summary
What is asked on the Night of Power, as al-Nawawi gives it in al-Adhkar: Aisha's report, «اللهم إنك عفو تحب العفو فاعف عني», from the books of al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i and Ibn Majah. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 191}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Near the end of the remembrances of fasting al-Nawawi gives a chapter on what to ask if one meets the Night of Power {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 191}. By sound chains, in the books of al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah and others, he reports from Aisha that she asked [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] what she should say if she knew which night it was, and he told her to say: «اللهم إنك عفو تحب العفو فاعف عني», O God, You are pardoning and love to pardon, so pardon me; he quotes al-Tirmidhi's grading of it as good and sound {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 191}. He says his colleagues recommend saying this supplication often on that night, with recitation of the Qur'an and the other remembrances, and he reports from [[person/al-shafii|al-Shafi'i]] that striving in its day is recommended as in its night {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 191}. He recommends asking much for the needs of the Muslims at large, and calls this the mark of the righteous {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 191}. The chapter on remembrances in i'tikaf follows, recommending much recitation of the Qur'an and other remembrance {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 191}.
