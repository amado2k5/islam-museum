## Summary
Kaffara derives from kufr in its sense of "covering", because it covers and effaces the sin; juristically it is what expiates — almsgiving, fasting and the like. The expiations are legislated by agreement and are obligatory in redress of specific sins and transgressions. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13408} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13409}

## The oath (yamin)
The oath is called yamin because the Arabs would strike right hands when swearing a pact; its rulings are detailed under the headword "ayman". Its expiation falls due on breaking a sworn oath. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.18005}

## The oath's expiation in the Book
Sura al-Ma'ida fixes the expiation for one who breaks a bound oath: "feeding ten poor persons from the average of what you feed your families, or clothing them, or freeing a slave; whoever cannot find the means, fasting three days". {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13410}

## Beside istighfar, tawba and 'uquba
Kaffara, seeking forgiveness and repentance are each — God willing — a cause of the sin's remission; it differs from punishment ('uquba) in that it carries the meaning of worship, which a penalty does not. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13409} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13410}

## In Yusuf al-Qaradawi's lessons Ta'ammulat Qur'aniyya
[[person/yusuf-al-qaradawi|Yusuf al-Qaradawi]] divides oaths, with the jurists, into three: the idle oath, which counts for nothing; the immersing oath (ghamus), sworn knowing it is false, which plunges its swearer into sin; and the binding oath about a future act, which carries an expiation when broken ([[passage/quran-5-89|al-Ma'ida 89]]). {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:12:00} He says the expiation is feeding ten poor people, clothing them or freeing a slave, with three days' fasting only for one who cannot, and notes that many people wrongly think fasting is the expiation from the start. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:13:30} He sees Islam as taking people's lapses as a chance to provide for the poor. {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-1/ep08 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-yusuf-al-qaradawi taamolat-qurania-2/ep02 00:28:00}
