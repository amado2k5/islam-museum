## Overview
Duha, for the jurists, is between the sun's rising and its zenith; salat al-duha is the prayer performed in that time. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10583}

## Awwabin and ishraq
Some hold the awwabin prayer is salat al-duha, the two being synonymous; others place the awwabin between maghrib and 'isha'. The ishraq prayer, mentioned by some Shafi'is in the duha discussion, is on the relied-upon view of al-Ramli and al-Ziyadi the same as the duha prayer; a view makes it two rak'as at the sun's rising. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10583} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10494}

## Form
Among the voluntary prayers for which no congregation is prescribed: at least two rak'as, at most twelve, with salam every two. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10494}

## In Zaghloul El-Naggar's series 'al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya'
In [[work/el-hadara-el-nabaouia|al-Hadara al-Nabawiyya]], [[person/zaghloul-el-naggar|Zaghloul El-Naggar]] links the forenoon prayer to the hadith that a human being was created with three hundred and sixty joints and that each joint owes a charity every day, citing Abu Dharr's narration in Muslim that two rak'as of the forenoon suffice for all of it, in place of glorifying, praising and declaring God's oneness for each joint. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep28 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep28 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep28 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep28 00:05:00} Many people neglect it, he says; its time runs from when the sun has risen a spear's length until it passes the meridian; and its two "light rak'as" weigh heavy in the scale. {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep28 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-zaghloul-el-naggar el-hadara-el-nabaouia/ep28 00:05:30}
