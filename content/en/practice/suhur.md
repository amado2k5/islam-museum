## Overview
Suhur is what is taken of food and drink at sahar — the last part of the night before dawn, or the last third until daybreak. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9473}

## Ruling
It is a sunna for the faster; Ibn al-Mundhir transmitted consensus that it is recommended: "take suhur, for in suhur is blessing," and "the line between our fasting and the People of the Book's is the sahar meal." It aids the day's fast — "help yourselves with the sahar meal against the day's fast and the siesta against the night's vigil" — and any eating or drinking counts, even a draught of water. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.9474}
