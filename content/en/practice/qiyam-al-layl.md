## Overview
Qiyam al-layl is voluntary prayer at night; tahajjud is prayer after sleep — "one of you thinks that if he rises at night and prays till morning he has done tahajjud; tahajjud is only prayer after a sleep." {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13134}

## Its place with witr
Witr, on the sounder stated Shafi'i position, is part of qiyam al-layl and tahajjud; a view makes it distinct. It is an emphatic sunna that seals the night prayer. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.13134} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10651}

## From Omar Abdelkafy's series on the Hikam of Ibn Ata Allah
Omar Abdelkafy urges praying two units in the last third of the night, because these are the hours in which God descends to the lowest heaven and calls: is there anyone asking forgiveness? is there anyone asking? {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:21:30}

## In Ahmed Abdussalam's programme 'Osalli Alayh'
In his programme [[work/ossali-alayh|'Osalli Alayh']], [[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]] makes the night vigil a sign of the Prophet's love for his Lord: he stood until his feet swelled, and when asked why, God having told him in Surat al-Fath that his past and future sins were forgiven [[passage/quran-48-2|(al-Fath 2)]], he answered that he wished to be a grateful servant, so he stood in thanks. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:03:00} He sets this against the slight aches people feel in the tarawih prayer. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:01:00} He says that when the Prophet prayed alone he would prostrate for as long as it takes one of us to recite fifty verses. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:10:00} In the seventeenth episode he explains the praise with which the Prophet opened his night prayer, praising God as the light of the heavens and the earth, their sustainer, lord, owner and king, and affirming that His promise, the meeting with Him, Paradise, Hell and the prophets are true. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep17 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep17 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep17 00:03:00}
