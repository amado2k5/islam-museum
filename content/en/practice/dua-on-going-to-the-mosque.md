## Summary
What is said on the way to the mosque, as al-Nawawi gathers it in al-Adhkar: the supplication for light from Sahih Muslim, from Ibn Abbas, and Bilal's report in Ibn al-Sunni's book, which al-Nawawi calls weak. {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 30}

## What al-Nawawi gathers in al-Adhkar
Al-Nawawi says that what is said on leaving the house has already been given, and that on the way to the mosque it is recommended to add what is in Sahih Muslim {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 30}. He reports from Ibn Abbas, in the account of the night he spent at his aunt Maymuna's, that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] went out to the prayer saying: «اللهم اجعل في قلبي نورا، وفي لساني نورا، واجعل في سمعي نورا، واجعل في بصري نورا، واجعل من خلفي نورا، ومن أمامي نورا، واجعل من فوقي نورا ومن تحتي نورا، اللهم أعطني نورا», asking for light in the heart, the tongue, the hearing and the sight, behind and before, above and below {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 30}. He then gives, from Ibn al-Sunni's book, from Bilal, a supplication that opens «بسم الله، آمنت بالله، توكلت على الله», and says it is a weak report, one of whose narrators, al-Wazi' ibn Nafi' al-Uqayli, is agreed to be weak; he mentions a similar report from Atiyya al-Awfi, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, and says Atiyya is weak as well {cite:al-adhkar-nawawi-arnaut-1994 p. 30}.
