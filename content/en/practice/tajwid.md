## Overview
Tajwid is making a thing excellent; technically, giving every letter its due and its deserved: its due the letter's own fixed qualities like emphasis and elevation, its deserved what follows from them like tafkhim — after producing each letter from its point of articulation. Ibn al-Jazari: giving the letters their rights, ranking them their ranks, returning each letter to its articulation and origin, without excess, contrivance or affectation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.4610} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.4611}

## Tilawa and tartil
Tilawa is sequential recitation of the Quran; ada' is taking it from teachers by hearing or reading in their presence; qira'a is wider — so tajwid is narrower than all three. Tartil is observing the articulations and the pauses, one of the means of tajwid and a grade of recitation after tahqiq. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.4611}

## Ruling
There is no dispute that occupying oneself with the science of tajwid is a communal obligation. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.4611}
