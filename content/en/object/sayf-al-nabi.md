## Overview
A sword known as the sword of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. Al-Bukhari relates that [[person/ali-zayn-al-abidin|Ali ibn al-Husayn]] came to Medina from [[person/yazid-ibn-muawiya|Yazid ibn Muawiya]] after the killing of al-Husayn, and [[person/al-miswar-ibn-makhrama|al-Miswar ibn Makhrama]] offered to keep it for him. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3110}

## The narration
Al-Miswar said: "I fear the people will overpower you over it." He swore that if it were given to him, they would never reach him while there was breath in him. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3110}

## In the chapter on the Prophet's belongings
Al-Bukhari mentions his sword under the chapter "What has been related of the Prophet's coat of mail, staff, sword, bowl and ring," among the belongings that his Companions sought blessing through after his death. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3110}
