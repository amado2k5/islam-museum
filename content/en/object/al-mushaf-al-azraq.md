## Overview
A gilded Kufic Qur'an on indigo-blue parchment, from the ninth or tenth century CE, probably from Morocco or al-Andalus; its leaves were scattered among the museums after it was separated. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
It is regarded as the most beautiful known Qur'an from the Islamic West; the use of blue parchment is almost unique among all Islamic manuscripts. {cite:wikidata}
