## Overview
A huge Kufic Qur'an that tradition attributes to [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman ibn Affan]], from the eighth or ninth century; kept in Tashkent and containing about half the text. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
One of the oldest Qur'ans of its size that survives; studies using carbon dating have placed it in the eighth century CE. {cite:wikidata}
