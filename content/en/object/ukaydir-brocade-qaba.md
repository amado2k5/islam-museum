## Overview
A robe of brocade studded with gold, which belonged to Ukaydir. [[person/khalid-ibn-al-walid|Khalid ibn al-Walid]] seized it and sent it to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], and the Muslims touched it and marvelled at it. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}
