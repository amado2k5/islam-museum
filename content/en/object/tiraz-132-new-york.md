## Overview
A fragment of tiraz silk with a red ground and red, green, yellow and white motifs, bearing an Arabic maker's inscription dated 132 AH (749–750 CE); found in Egypt, it is part C of four fragments that once formed a single textile, held by the Brooklyn Museum, New York {cite:tei-epigraphie 9321}.
