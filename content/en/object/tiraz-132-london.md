## Overview
A fragment of yellow tiraz silk bearing an Arabic maker's inscription, dated 132 AH (749–750 CE); originally found in a cemetery at Akhmim, Egypt, it is part B of four fragments that once formed a single textile, held by the Victoria and Albert Museum, London {cite:tei-epigraphie 47}.
