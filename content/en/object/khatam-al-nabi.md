## Overview
A silver seal engraved "Muhammad, Messenger of God" in three lines: "Muhammad" on one line, "Messenger" on one, and "God" on one. [[person/muhammad|The Prophet]] had it made when he wanted to write to a group of non-Arabs, and he was told that they accept no letter that is not sealed. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5872}

## Description
The bezel of the ring was of it, and it faced the inside of his palm. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5870}

Ibn Umar relates that he first made a gold ring; when people made the same, he threw it away and said, "I will never wear it again," and then took silver. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5866}

## After his death
After him, [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]] wore it, then [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]], then [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]], until it fell from Uthman into the well of Aris. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 5866}

Abu Bakr also sealed a letter to Bahrain with it when he succeeded to the caliphate. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3106}
