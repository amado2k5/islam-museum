## Overview
A collection of hundreds of thousands of Islamic, Arabic and African manuscripts in Timbuktu; a large cultural archive in Mali. {cite:wikidata}

## Catalogued records
The Hill Museum & Manuscript Library's open listing of 9 November 2026 records 102,304 manuscripts and manuscript parts from Timbuktu in 26 libraries, out of 127,024 records for Mali as a whole. The ten largest Timbuktu collections are the Mamma Haidara Library (19,439 records), the Attaher Mouaz Family Library (10,809), the Mogaz Anouwaye Family Library (8,044), the Aboubacar Ben Said Library (7,563), the Abdoulahi ben Abdourahamane Family Library (5,132), the Aboubacar Abass Family Library (3,716), the Imam Daoud Mohamed Family Library (3,642), the Imam Alpha Saloum Family Library (3,585), the Alphady Cheikna Sidaly and Omar ben Ibrahim Cheik Family Library (3,447) and the Oumar Haidara Family Library (3,213). These are counts of catalogued records, not of distinct works, and the listing does not give languages. {cite:vhmml Mali-listing-2026-11-09}
