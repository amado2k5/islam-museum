## Overview
An Ottoman Sufi carpet of the 'Lotto' type, with intertwined yellow patterns on red, Turkey; sixteenth century, named for its appearance in the paintings of Lotto. Held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. {cite:met-open-access}
