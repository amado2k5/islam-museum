## Overview
A ceramic mihrab inlaid with coloured brick mosaic dated 755 AH / 1354–1355 CE, from a madrasa in Isfahan, Iran; the best-known mihrab held in a museum. Held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. {cite:met-open-access}
