## Overview
The famous sword of [[person/amr-ibn-madikarib|Amr ibn Ma'dikarib al-Zubaydi]]. Al-Mahdi gave it to Musa al-Hadi. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.323-324}

## The account
After al-Hadi became caliph, he called for the sword and placed it before him with a basket of dinars, and allowed the poets to compose verses about it. Ibn Yamin al-Basri opened, with the line: "Amr has won the Sumsama of al-Zubaydi." {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.323-324}

Al-Hadi gave it to him, and he distributed the basket among the poets. Then al-Hadi bought it back from him for fifty thousand. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.323-324}
