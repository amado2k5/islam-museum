## Overview
An ivory casket carved in Cordoba in 968 CE for the Umayyad prince al-Mughira ibn Abd al-Rahman III, with hunting scenes, musicians and carved monastic motifs on very thin Andalusian ivory. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
Held in the Louvre; it is regarded as the height of Islamic ivory carving and one of the most famous rare survivals of al-Andalus. {cite:wikidata}
