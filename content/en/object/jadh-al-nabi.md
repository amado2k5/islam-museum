## Overview
The trunk of the palm tree against which [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] used to preach before the pulpit was made; it yearned for him when he left it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.393-394}

## What became known later
It became known that a piece of wood fixed with lead in the pillar of the noble prayer hall, opposite the Rawda, was a remnant of it. Ibn Jubayr, in his travel account, mentions that people used to kiss a visible piece of it in a pillar and seek blessing by wiping it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.393-394}

## Al-Samhudi's view
Al-Samhudi disputed this and preferred that the piece came from the [[object/awd-al-maqam|Standing Staff]], not from the trunk. He mentioned that Izz al-Din ibn Jama'a ordered it removed in 855 AH. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.393-394}
