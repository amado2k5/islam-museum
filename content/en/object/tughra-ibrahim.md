## Overview
A Safavid tughra for Shah Muhammad ibn Ibrahim Khan al-Muzaffar; 1648–1687. It is rare, since the tughra is usually an Ottoman custom. In the Cleveland Museum of Art. {cite:cleveland-open-access}
