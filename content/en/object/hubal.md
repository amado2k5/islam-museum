## Overview
Hubal: Amr ibn Luhayy carried it from Hit in the land of Iraq and set it on the Ka'ba's hollow; it was Quraysh's greatest idol, and beside it stood the divining arrows they used.

## In the sira presentation
Al-Mubarakpuri mentions that idolatry entered the Arabs in the time of [[person/amr-ibn-luhayy|Amr ibn Luhayy]], who saw the people of al-Balqa venerating idols and carried them to the Hijaz — until every clan had an idol and every house an idol {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}.

## For young visitors
Hubal is an idol some Arabs worshipped before Islam instead of God — until Islam demolished all the idols.
