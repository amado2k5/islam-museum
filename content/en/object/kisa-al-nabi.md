## Overview
A patched cloak, mulabbad (mended and quilted), which [[person/aisha|Aisha]] brought out for Abu Burda, saying: "In this, the soul of the Prophet was taken." She brought out with it a coarse waist-wrap of the kind made in Yemen. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3108}

## Its keeping
The cloak and the waist-wrap were among the belongings of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] through which his Companions sought blessing, and those who came after them. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3108}
