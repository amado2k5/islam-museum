## Overview
The Uthmanic copy that was in [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], one of the four copies that [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] sent to the regions. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.641-643}

## Its journey
It was taken out of Cordoba on the night of Saturday, 11 Shawwal 552 AH, in the days of Abd al-Mu'min ibn Ali and by his order. The Muwahhidun (Almohads) continued to carry it on their journeys, seeking its blessing. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.641-643}

Al-Mu'tadid al-Sa'id ibn al-Ma'mun carried it when he set out for [[place/tlemcen|Tlemcen]] at the end of 645 AH. He was killed near it, and the copy was looted. It is said that it passed to the treasury of the kings of Tlemcen. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.641-643}

Abu al-Hasan al-Marini took Tlemcen at the end of Ramadan 737 AH and gained the copy. It was then lost at the battle of Tarif in 741 AH and ended up in the land of Portugal. Efforts were made to recover it, and it reached [[place/fes|Fez]] in 745 AH through a merchant from Azemmour. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.641-643}

## Views on its attribution
Ibn Marzuq relates that the sultan Abu al-Hasan would not travel without it. Ibn Abd al-Malik mentions that what was said about it containing the blood of Uthman is far-fetched, though if one of the copies does, it is perhaps the Syrian one. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.641-643}

Al-Tujibi al-Shami examined one in the maqsura of the Umayyad Mosque in 657 AH, and the Meccan one at the Qubbat al-Turab. The note on the back of the Madani copy states that the compilation was made with a group of the Companions, among them Zayd ibn Thabit, Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr and Sa'id ibn al-As. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p.641-643}
