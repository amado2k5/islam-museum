## Overview
A stick of tamarisk wood from the thicket, fixed in the qibla wall of the Prophet's mosque. [[person/muhammad|The Prophet]] would hold it in his hand and lean on it when he stood to pray. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.392-394}

## Use
Then he would turn right and left and say: "Straighten your rows." It was said that it was placed for him when he grew old, and another stick was attached to it. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.392-394}

## Loss and restoration
The staff went missing in the time of [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]. It was found with a man of the Ansar in Quba, who had buried it, and termites had eaten it. Umar repaired it and returned it to the mihrab. {cite:samhudi-wafa-al-wafa p.392-394}
