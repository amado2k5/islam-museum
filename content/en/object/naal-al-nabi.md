## Overview
A pair of sandals, jardawan: worn leather whose hair has come off, with two thongs (qibal), straps that run between the toes. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3107}

## Their story
[[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas ibn Malik]] brought them out for his disciples in his time. Thabit al-Bunani later related that they were the sandals of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3107}

They were among the belongings that the Companions and the Successors sought blessing by keeping after his death. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3107}
