## Overview
An instrument made by [[person/ibn-al-shatir|Ibn al-Shatir]], the timekeeper (muwaqqit), in [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. He found that astronomical instruments do not serve all astronomical tasks at every latitude, and that error creeps into most of the work they are used for. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.570-571}

## Its purpose
He made the "Complete Quadrant" (al-rub' al-tamm) so that all the tasks could be carried out in every climate, with ease of aim and clarity of proof, according to the Kashf al-Zunun. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.570-571}
