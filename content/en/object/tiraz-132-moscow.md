## Overview
A fragment of tiraz silk woven as a textile (0.62 × 0.235 m), bearing an Arabic maker's inscription dated 132 AH (749–750 CE); found in Egypt, it is part A of four fragments that once formed a single textile, now held by the Museum of Oriental Cultures, Moscow, formerly in the Goleniščev collection {cite:tei-epigraphie 11980}.
