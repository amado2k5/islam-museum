## Overview
A Uthmanic copy of the Qur'an kept at the place where the she-camel knelt, in [[place/busra|Busra]]. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari mentions that it bears traces of blood. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.217}

The record holds nothing beyond this: an additional source is needed, especially on Busra and on the chain of attribution for this claim.
