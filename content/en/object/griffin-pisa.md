## Overview
A large Islamic bronze griffin statue of the eleventh century, probably from al-Andalus or the Mediterranean; it stood for centuries on the roof of Pisa Cathedral as a spoil of war. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
The largest surviving bronze animal sculpture from the Islamic world; today it is in the Museo dell'Opera del Duomo in Pisa. {cite:wikidata}
