## Overview
An ivory-inlaid wooden minbar made by workshops in Cordoba in 1137 CE for the Kutubiyya mosque in [[place/marrakesh|Marrakesh]]; its geometric tiles and gilding make it the most beautiful surviving Islamic minbar. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
It is kept in the Bahia Palace Museum in Marrakesh, a testimony that the workshops of al-Andalus made for the whole Islamic world. {cite:wikidata}
