## Overview
A brass brazier inlaid with silver and gold, made for the Rasulid sultan al-Malik al-Muzaffar Shams al-Din Yusuf, Egypt; second half of the thirteenth century. Held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. {cite:met-open-access}
