## Overview
Illustrated pages from a historical manuscript about the reigns of Khusrau Parviz and Nushirwan; about 1299, Ilkhanid Baghdad, one of the manuscripts made just after the Mongol conquest. In the Cleveland Museum of Art. {cite:cleveland-open-access}
