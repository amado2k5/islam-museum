## Summary
A key for the Kaaba of steel inlaid with silver, 29 cm long, which the Nuhad Es-Said collection booklet (Riyadh 1985) dates to the circa 740/1340 and attributes, probably, to Mecca. {cite:nuhad-es-said-collection-islamic-metalwork-1985 p. 19}

## Significance
The booklet's introduction says the collection's two Kaaba keys, probably made in Mecca itself, illustrate a little-known aspect of the thriving Hijazi artistic tradition of the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries. {cite:nuhad-es-said-collection-islamic-metalwork-1985 p. 2} {cite:nuhad-es-said-collection-islamic-metalwork-1985 p. 3}
