## Overview
A fragment of tiraz silk woven as a textile, bearing an Arabic maker's inscription dated 132 AH (749–750 CE); found in Egypt, it is part D of four fragments that once formed a single textile, held by the Whitworth Art Gallery, Manchester {cite:tei-epigraphie 11979}.
