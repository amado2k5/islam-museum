## Overview
A piece of leather written in Kufic script, bearing the text of a land grant to [[person/tamim-al-dari|Tamim al-Dari]] and his brothers: Hebron, al-Marsum, Bayt Aynun and Bayt Ibrahim. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.173-175}

## Al-Umari's inspection
Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari examined it himself in 745 AH, when Nasir al-Din Muhammad ibn al-Khalili al-Tamimi al-Dari brought it to him. It is kept in a black cloth of cotton and silk, from the sleeve of the caliph al-Mustadi', in an ebony box wrapped in silk. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.173-175}

A copy was made from it in the hand of al-Mustadi' himself, and it states that Ali wrote it and that [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]] and [[person/uthman-ibn-affan|Uthman]] witnessed it. Al-Umari mentions that al-Safadi examined it and recorded it in his Tadhkira. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.173-175}

## Afterwards
Al-Qalqashandi mentions that it remained in the hands of the Tamimi custodians of the sanctuary of [[place/al-khalil|al-Khalil]] until about 821 AH, who used it as proof against those who disputed with them. {cite:umari-masalik-1924 p.173-175}
