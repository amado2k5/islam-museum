## Overview
A perforated ceramic ewer dated 612 AH / 1215–1216 CE, decorated with harabiyya creatures and wings, Iran; one of the finest pieces of Kashan ceramics. Held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. {cite:met-open-access}
