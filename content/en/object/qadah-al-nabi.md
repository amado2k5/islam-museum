## Overview
The bowl of [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]], which broke; a silver chain was made in place of its crack (sha'b). {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3109}

## Its survival
Asim said: "I saw the bowl and drank from it." This means that it remained preserved and passed from hand to hand after the Prophet's death. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari 3109}
