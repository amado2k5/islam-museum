## Overview
A page from al-Jazari's book on ingenious devices dated 715 AH / 1315 CE, showing the water clock of the elephant, probably Syria or Iraq; the most famous Islamic hydraulic machine. Held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. {cite:met-open-access}
