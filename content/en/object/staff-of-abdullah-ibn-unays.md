## Overview
A staff that the Prophet gave to [[person/abdullah-ibn-unays|Abd Allah ibn Unays]] at his home, after his return from the [[event/killing-of-khalid-ibn-sufyan-al-hudhali|killing of Khalid ibn Sufyan al-Hudhali]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/268}

## Its story
The Prophet said to him: "Keep this staff with you." When people asked him about it, he returned to ask the Prophet, who said: "It is a sign between me and you on the Day of Resurrection; the fewest of people that day will be those who lean on staves." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/268} Ibn Unays then attached it to his sword, and it stayed with him until he died. He then ordered it placed in his shroud, and the two were buried together. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/268} Scholars explain "those who lean on staves" as those who rest on the makhasir, which are staves. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/268}

## Note
The staff has no material trace. The entry preserves its story as Ibn Ishaq relates it.
