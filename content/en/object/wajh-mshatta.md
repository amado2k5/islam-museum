## Overview
The facade of the Umayyad palace of Mshatta in Jordan (about 744 CE), carved with plant, animal and inscription decoration; given by Sultan Abdulhamid II to the German emperor in 1903. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
In the Museum of Islamic Art in Berlin; the most famous early Islamic stone facade and an example of Umayyad desert palace architecture. {cite:wikidata}
