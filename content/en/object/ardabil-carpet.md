## Overview
A huge Safavid carpet (1539–1540 CE) from Ardabil, among the oldest dated carpets in the world and the most complete in its weaving; it carries a poem by Hafiz and the name of the founder of the workshop, Maqsud Kashani. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
It was woven in one knotting with two pairs: the Los Angeles copy and its twin in the Victoria and Albert Museum; it became the standard for judging every Islamic carpet after it. {cite:wikidata}
