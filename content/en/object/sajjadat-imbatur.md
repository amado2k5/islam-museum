## Overview
A huge Safavid Persian carpet with a silk foundation, whose ownership passed in turn to the tsars and the rulers of Russia, Iran; second half of the sixteenth century, among the greatest surviving carpets. Held at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. {cite:met-open-access}
