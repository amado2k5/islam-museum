## Overview
"Nikaah" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 1982, produced in India, directed by [[person/baldev-raj-chopra|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/raj-babbar|Raj Babbar]], [[person/deepak-parashar|None]], [[person/salma-agha|Salma Agha]] and [[person/iftekhar|Iftekhar]]. An Indian film by B. R. Chopra about a wife whom her husband divorces with a triple talaq in a fit of anger, and the consequences for the legitimacy of a second marriage; it provoked a national debate in India about divorce in Islam. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
