## Overview
"Little Mosque on the Prairie" is a comedy series released in 2007, produced in Canada. Its leading performers include [[person/zaib-shaikh|None]], [[person/carlo-rota|Carlo Rota]], [[person/sheila-mccarthy|Sheila McCarthy]], [[person/sitara-hewitt|None]] and [[person/manoj-sood|None]]. A Canadian sitcom about a small Muslim community in a town in Saskatchewan; one of the early Western comedies led by ordinary Muslim characters. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
