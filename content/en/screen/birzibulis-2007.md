## Overview
"Persepolis" is an animated film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2007, produced in France and Iran, directed by [[person/marjane-satrapi|Marjane Satrapi]] and [[person/vincent-paronnaud|None]]. A French–Iranian animated film about Marjane Satrapi's childhood in Iran before and after the revolution; nominated for an Oscar, presenting Iranian society through the eyes of its daughter. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
