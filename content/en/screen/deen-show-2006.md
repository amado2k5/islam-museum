## Overview
"The Deen Show" is a web series of recitation or lecture released in 2006, produced in the United States. An American interview programme on YouTube presented by Eddie (Edward Rzyski) since 2006, hosting Muslim scholars, celebrities and converts to Islam; one of the oldest and most widely viewed English da'wa channels. {cite:deen-show}
