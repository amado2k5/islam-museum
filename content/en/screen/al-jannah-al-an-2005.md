## Overview
"Paradise Now" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2005, produced in Palestine, France, Germany and the Netherlands, directed by [[person/hany-abu-assad|Hany Abu-Assad]]. Its leading performers include [[person/kais-nashef|Kais Nashef]], [[person/ali-suliman|Ali Suliman]], [[person/lubna-azabal|Lubna Azabal]], [[person/hiam-abbass|Hiam Abbass]] and [[person/amer-hlehel|Amer Hlehel]]. Hany Abu-Assad's film about two Palestinian friends in Nablus chosen for an operation inside Israel; an Oscar nominee for Palestine and the centre of a wide moral debate. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Golden Calf for Best Feature Film, European Film Award for Best Screenwriter. {cite:wikidata}
