## Overview
"Wa Islamah" is a historical drama film released in 1961, produced in Egypt, directed by [[person/enrico-bomba|Enrico Bomba]] and [[person/andrew-marton|Andrew Marton]]. Its leading performers include [[person/ahmad-mazhar|Ahmad Mazhar]], [[person/lobna-abdelaziz|Lobna Abdel Aziz]], [[person/rushdy-abaza|Rushdy Abaza]], [[person/mahmoud-el-meliguy|Mahmoud El Meliguy]] and [[person/farid-shawqi|Farid Shawqi]]. An Egyptian film by Salah Abu Seif starring Lobna Abdel Aziz about Egypt's resistance to the Mongol invasion after the fall of Baghdad; one of the classics of Arab historical cinema. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[polity/mongol-empire|the Tatar state (the Mongol Empire)]], [[place/cairo|Cairo]], and [[period/mongol-post-mongol|the Mongol era and after]].
