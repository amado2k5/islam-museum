## Overview
"When Love Glorifies" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2009, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/chaerul-umam|Chaerul Umam]]. Its leading performers include [[person/kholidi-asadil-alam|None]], [[person/oki-setiana-dewi|None]], [[person/alice-norin|Alice Norin]], [[person/andi-arsyil-rahman|None]] and [[person/meyda-sefira|None]]. An Indonesian film based on the novel by Habiburrahman El Shirazy about an al-Azhar student balancing knowledge, love and livelihood in Cairo; one of the most popular Indonesian religious films. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
