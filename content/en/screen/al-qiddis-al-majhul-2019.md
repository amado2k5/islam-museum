## Overview
"The Unknown Saint" is a comedy film released in 2019, produced in Morocco and France, directed by [[person/alaa-eddine-aljem|Alaa Eddine Aljem]]. Its leading performers include [[person/younes-bouab|Younes Bouab]], [[person/anas-el-baz|Anas El Baz]] and [[person/salah-bensalah|Salah Ben Salah]]. A Moroccan comedy by Alaa Eddine Aljem about a thief who buries his loot in an imaginary grave, and the place turns into a popular shrine; a cheerful elegy on popular blessing and piety. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
