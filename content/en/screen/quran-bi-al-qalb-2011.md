## Overview
"Quran by Heart" is a documentary released in 2011, produced in the United States and Britain. An HBO documentary by Greg Barker following three children from Senegal, Tajikistan and the Maldives in the Cairo International Qur'an Memorisation Competition; a glimpse of a generation of Qur'an memorisers around the world. {cite:imdb}
