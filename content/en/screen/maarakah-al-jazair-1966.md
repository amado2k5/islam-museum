## Overview
"The Battle of Algiers" is a historical drama film released in 1966, produced in Algeria and Italy, directed by [[person/gillo-pontecorvo|Gillo Pontecorvo]]. Its leading performers include [[person/jean-martin|Jean Martin]], [[person/saadi-yacef|Yacef Saadi]], [[person/brahim-hadjadj|Brahim Hadjadj]], [[person/larbi-zekkal|Larbi Zekkal]] and [[person/fusia-el-kader|None]]. Gillo Pontecorvo's film about the Battle of Algiers in 1956–1957 between the National Liberation Front and French colonialism; one of the best-known films about liberation movements, tracing the beginnings and struggle of the Arab quarter. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/algiers|Algiers]] and [[period/colonial-era|the colonial era]]. Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Golden Lion. {cite:wikidata}
