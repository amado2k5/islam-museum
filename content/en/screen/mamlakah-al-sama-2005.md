## Overview
"Kingdom of Heaven" is a historical drama film released in 2005, produced in the United States, Britain, Spain and Germany, directed by [[person/ridley-scott|Ridley Scott]]. Its leading performers include [[person/eva-green|Eva Green]], [[person/jeremy-irons|Jeremy Irons]], [[person/david-thewlis|David Thewlis]], [[person/brendan-gleeson|Brendan Gleeson]] and [[person/marton-csokas|Marton Csokas]]. Ridley Scott's film about Balian of Ibelin and the defence of Jerusalem in 1187; Saladin appears as a noble central character, and its portrayal of the relationship between the Crusaders and the Muslims has long been debated. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub al-Malik al-Nasir]], [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]], and [[period/crusades-ayyubid-mamluk|the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks]]. Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Jameson People's Choice Award for Best Actor. {cite:wikidata}
