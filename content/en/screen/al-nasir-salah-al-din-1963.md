## Overview
"Al-Nasir Salah al-Din" is a historical drama film released in 1963, produced in Egypt, directed by [[person/youssef-chahine|Youssef Chahine]]. Its leading performers include [[person/ahmad-mazhar|Ahmad Mazhar]], [[person/salah-zulfikar|Salah Zulficar]], [[person/nadia-lotfi|Nadia Lutfi]], [[person/leila-fawzi|Leila Fawzi]] and [[person/omar-el-hariri|Omar El Hariri]]. An Egyptian film by Youssef Chahine starring Ahmad Mazhar about Salah al-Din and the liberation of Jerusalem from the Crusaders; one of the best-known Arab films about the Crusades. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub, al-Malik al-Nasir]], [[period/crusades-ayyubid-mamluk|the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks]], and [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]].
