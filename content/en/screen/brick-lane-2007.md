## Overview
"Brick Lane" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2007, produced in Britain, directed by [[person/sarah-gavron|Sarah Gavron]]. Its leading performers include [[person/tannishtha-chatterjee|Tannishtha Chatterjee]], [[person/satish-kaushik|Satish Kaushik]], [[person/bernard-holley|Bernard Holley]] and [[person/christopher-simpson|Christopher Simpson]]. A British film by Sarah Gavron based on Monica Ali's famous novel: a Bangladeshi Muslim wife in London torn between duty and desire; it provoked objections in the Brick Lane community during filming. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
