## Overview
"Wadjda" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2012, produced in Saudi Arabia and Germany, directed by [[person/haifaa-al-mansour|Haifaa Al-Mansour]]. Its leading performers include [[person/reem-abdullah|Reem Abdullah]], [[person/waad-mohammed|Waad Mohammed]] and [[person/mariam-alghamdi|Mariam Alghamdi]]. Haifaa al-Mansour's film, the first feature film shot inside Saudi Arabia by a woman director, about a girl in Riyadh who wants a bicycle; a calm cinematic language about the constraints of place and gender. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
