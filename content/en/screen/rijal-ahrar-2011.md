## Overview
"Free Men" is a historical drama film released in 2011, produced in France, directed by [[person/isma-l-ferroukhi|Ismaël Ferroukhi]]. Its leading performers include [[person/tahar-rahim|Tahar Rahim]], [[person/michael-lonsdale|Michael Lonsdale]], [[person/lubna-azabal|Lubna Azabal]], [[person/stephane-rideau|None]] and [[person/blandine-pelissier|None]]. A French film by Ismaël Ferroukhi about the Grand Mosque of Paris and its imam Si Kaddour Ben Ghabrit, who saved Jews during the Nazi occupation; a true story of a Muslim stance. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Abu Dhabi Film Festival, Santa Barbara International Film Festival. {cite:wikidata}
