## Overview
"The Insult" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2017, produced in Lebanon and France, directed by [[person/ziad-doueiri|Ziad Doueiri]]. Its leading performers include [[person/adel-karam|Adel Karam]], [[person/kamel-el-basha|Kamel El Basha]], [[person/rita-hayek|Rita Hayek]] and [[person/julia-kassar|Julia Kassar]]. Ziad Doueiri's film about a dispute between a Lebanese Christian and a Palestinian refugee that becomes a trial exposing the wounds of the Lebanese civil war; the first Lebanese film nominated for an Oscar. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
