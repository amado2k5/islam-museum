## Overview
"Kuruluş: Osman" is a historical drama series released in 2019, produced in Turkey, directed by [[person/metin-gunay|Metin Günay]]. Its leading performers include [[person/nurettin-sonmez|Nurettin Sönmez]], [[person/ragip-savas|Ragıp Savaş]], [[person/burak-ozcivit|Burak Özçivit]], [[person/saruhan-hunel|Saruhan Hünel]] and [[person/yurdaer-okur|Yurdaer Okur]]. A Turkish series following Ertuğrul, following Osman I and the foundation of the Ottoman state; one of the most watched historical series in the world. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/uthman-i-uthmani|Osman ibn Ertuğrul]], [[place/anatolia|Anatolia]], and [[polity/ottoman-empire|the Ottoman Empire]].
