## Overview
"The Four Daughters" is a documentary released in 2023, produced in Tunisia, France and Germany, directed by [[person/kaouther-ben-hania|Kaouther Ben Hania]]. A dramatic documentary by Kaouther Ben Hania about the Tunisian mother Olfa Hamrouni and the loss of her two daughters who joined the Islamic State in Libya; nominated for the Oscar for Best Documentary Feature. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
