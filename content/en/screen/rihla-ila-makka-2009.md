## Overview
"Journey to Mecca" is a documentary released in 2009, produced in the United States, Saudi Arabia and Morocco, directed by [[person/bruce-neibaur|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/q12219086|Shams al-Din Zinoun]] and [[person/ben-kingsley|Ben Kingsley]]. An IMAX documentary-drama representing Ibn Battuta's first pilgrimage in 1325, with real filming of the modern hajj rites inside the sanctuary. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/ibn-battuta-muhammad|Ibn Battuta, Muhammad ibn Abd Allah]], [[practice/hajj|the hajj]], and [[place/mecca|Mecca]].
