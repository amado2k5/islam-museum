## Overview
"Saqr Quraysh" is a historical drama series released in 2002, directed by [[person/hatem-ali|Hatem Ali]]. Its leading performers include [[person/jamal-suliman|Jamal Suleiman]], [[person/mohamed-miftah|Mohamed Miftah]], [[person/bassem-yakhour|Bassem Yakhour]], [[person/taim-hasan|Taim Hassan]] and [[person/fayez-abu-dan|Fayez Abu Dan]]. An Arab series about Abd al-Rahman al-Dakhil, 'the Falcon of Quraysh', and his flight from the Umayyad massacre to founding the Umayyad emirate in al-Andalus. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]], [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], and [[period/umayyad|the Umayyad era]].
