## Overview
"The Free Man: Bediuzzaman Said Nursi" is a historical drama film released in 2011, produced in Turkey, directed by [[person/mehmet-tanrisever|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/mursid-aga-bag|None]]. A Turkish film about the life of Bediüzzaman Said Nursi, from a Kurdish teacher to the author of the Risale-i Nur and a prisoner of secular courts; the largest film work on a modern Turkish religious scholar. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
