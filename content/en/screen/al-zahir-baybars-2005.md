## Overview
"Al-Zahir Baybars" is a historical drama series released in 2005, produced in Syria and the UAE, directed by [[person/mohammad-azizia|Mohammad Azizia]]. Its leading performers include [[person/abed-fahed|Abed Fahed]], [[person/suzan-najm-aldeeen|Suzan Najm Eddine]], [[person/bassel-khaiat|Bassel Khayat]], [[person/rafic-ali-ahmad|Rafic Ali Ahmad]] and [[person/saad-mineh|Saad Mineh]]. A large Syrian–Emirati series about Sultan al-Zahir Baybars, from the Mamluks to the defeat of the Mongols at 'Ayn Jalut; one of the largest Arab productions on the Mamluk period. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/baybars|Sultan al-Zahir Rukn al-Din Baybars al-Bunduqdari]], [[place/cairo|Cairo]], and [[period/crusades-ayyubid-mamluk|the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks]].
