## Overview
"Alif" is a faith drama series released in 2019, produced in Pakistan, directed by [[person/haseeb-hassan|None]]. A Pakistani series about a successful film director and a young man who has memorised the Qur'an; a dialogue between art and the soul, among the most influential contemporary Pakistani dramas on the question of religion and art. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
