## Overview
"We Are Lady Parts" is a comedy series released in 2021, produced in Britain, directed by [[person/nida-manzoor|None]]. A British Channel 4 comedy by Nida Manzoor about an all-female Muslim punk band in London; it won the BAFTA and was praised for breaking stereotypes of Muslim women. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
