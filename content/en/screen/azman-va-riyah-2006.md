## Overview
"Times and Winds" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2006, produced in Turkey, directed by [[person/reha-erdem|Reha Erdem]]. Its leading performers include [[person/taner-birsel|Taner Birsel]], [[person/koksal-engur|None]], [[person/sevinc-erbulak|Sevinc Erbulak]], [[person/selma-ergec|Selma Ergeç]] and [[person/elit-i-scan|Elit Işcan]]. A Turkish film by Reha Erdem about three children in an Anatolian village whose day is organised by the times of the five prayers; one of the most beautiful films on the rhythm of worship in rural life. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
