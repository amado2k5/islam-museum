## Overview
"The Palestinian Odyssey" is a historical drama series released in 2004, produced in Syria, directed by [[person/hatem-ali|Hatem Ali]]. A Syrian series by Hatem Ali about a Palestinian family cut apart by the Nakba and the displacement from village to camp; one of the most influential Arab dramas in the memory of Palestine. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
