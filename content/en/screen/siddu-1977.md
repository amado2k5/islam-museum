## Overview
"Ceddo" is a critical film released in 1977, produced in Senegal, directed by [[person/ousmane-sembene|Ousmane Sembène]]. Its leading performer is [[person/tabata-ndiaye|None]]. Ousmane Sembène's Senegalese film about a village that resists the adoption of Islam, Christianity and colonialism together; one of the pillars of African cinema, showing the coming of Islam through a critical eye. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
