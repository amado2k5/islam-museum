## Overview
"Fajr al-Islam" is a historical drama film released in 1971, produced in Syria, directed by [[person/salah-abu-seif|Salah Abu Seif]]. A Syrian film by Salah Dahni about the emergence of Islam in Mecca and Quraysh's rejection of it; one of the early dramatic works that told the beginnings of the call without showing the Prophet. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
