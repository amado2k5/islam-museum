## Overview
"Zaky" is a web series for children released in 2010, produced in Australia. The kangaroo character Zaky from the Australian studio One4Kids: songs and educational clips for children about prayer, fasting and morals; one of the earliest and most-viewed Islamic children's works on YouTube. {cite:one4kids}
