## Overview
"Mo" is a comedy series released in 2022, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/solvan-naim|Solvan Naim]]. Its leading performers include [[person/mohammed-amer|Mo Amer]], [[person/teresa-ruiz|Teresa Ruiz]], [[person/omar-elba|Omar Elba]], [[person/cherien-dabis|Cherien Dabis]] and [[person/tobe-nwigwe|None]]. A Netflix series by Mo Amer about a Palestinian Muslim refugee in Houston who tries to reunite his family and win citizenship; one of the first American works to cast a Palestinian Muslim as a hero. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
