## Overview
"The Life of Muhammad" is a documentary series released in 2011, produced in Britain, directed by [[person/faris-kermani|None]]. A three-part documentary series presented by Ragheb Omar on the BBC, filming the biography of the Prophet at sites in the Arabian Peninsula with interviews with researchers and contemporary Muslims. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[place/medina|Medina]]. Some viewers objected to it; a complaint campaign led the BBC to reschedule one episode during Ramadan. {cite:wikidata}
