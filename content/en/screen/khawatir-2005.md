## Overview
"Khawatir" is an educational television programme released in 2005, produced in Saudi Arabia. A Ramadan programme presented by Ahmad al-Shugairi on MBC over eleven seasons, mixing religion with social service and travel; one of the most successful faith programmes for young people in the Arab world. {cite:wikidata}
