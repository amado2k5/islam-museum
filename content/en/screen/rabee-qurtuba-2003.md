## Overview
"Rabi' Qurtuba" is a historical drama series released in 2003, produced in Syria, directed by [[person/hatem-ali|Hatem Ali]]. Its leading performers include [[person/taim-hasan|Taim Hassan]], [[person/jamal-suliman|Jamal Suleiman]], [[person/mohamed-miftah|Mohamed Miftah]], [[person/nesreen-tafesh|Nesreen Tafesh]] and [[person/ramez-atallah|Ramez Atallah]]. A Syrian series by Hatem Ali about the Andalusian civil strife and the fall of the Umayyad caliphate in Cordoba in 1009; one of the finest Arab dramas about al-Andalus. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] and [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]].
