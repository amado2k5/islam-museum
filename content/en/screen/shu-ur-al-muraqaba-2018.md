## Overview
"The Feeling of Being Watched" is a journalistic documentary released in 2018, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/assia-boundaoui|None]]. A documentary by Assia Boundaoui recording the FBI's surveillance of a Muslim neighbourhood in Chicago in the 1990s, and what her lawsuit to publish the surveillance files revealed. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
