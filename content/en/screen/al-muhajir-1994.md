## Overview
"Al-Muhajir" is a historical drama film released in 1994, produced in Egypt and France, directed by [[person/youssef-chahine|Youssef Chahine]]. Its leading performers include [[person/khaled-el-nabawy|Khaled El Nabawy]], [[person/michel-piccoli|Michel Piccoli]], [[person/mahmoud-hemida|Mahmoud Hemida]], [[person/yousra|Yousra]] and [[person/safia-el-emari|Safia El Emary]]. Youssef Chahine's film inspired by the story of Joseph in ancient Egypt; it provoked a legal and media battle in Egypt over the depiction of the prophets of the Qur'an on screen, and its hero was stripped of his Qur'anic name. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
