## Overview
"Takva" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2006, produced in Turkey and Germany, directed by [[person/ozer-kiziltan|Özer Kızıltan]]. Its leading performers include [[person/erkan-can|Erkan Can]], [[person/meray-ulgen|None]], [[person/guven-kirac|Güven Kıraç]], [[person/erman-saban|Erman Saban]] and [[person/settar-tanriogen|Settar Tanrıöğen]]. A Turkish–German film about a simple devout preacher who is tasked with collecting money for a Sufi order, and whose faith is shaken by money and influence; a social lesson on religion and modern piety. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
