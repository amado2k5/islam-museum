## Overview
"The Ornament of the World" is a documentary released in 2019, produced in the United States. A PBS documentary by Michael Schwarz based on the book by Maria Rosa Menocal about eight centuries of coexistence in medieval al-Andalus. {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]], [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], and [[place/granada|Granada]].
