## Overview
"Not Without My Daughter" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 1991, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/brian-gilbert|Brian Gilbert]]. Its leading performers include [[person/sally-field|Sally Field]], [[person/alfred-molina|Alfred Molina]], [[person/roshan-seth|Roshan Seth]], [[person/georges-corraface|None]] and [[person/sasson-gabai|Sasson Gabai]]. An American film based on the novel by Betty Mahmoody, about an American mother who flees Iran with her daughter after her husband abducts them; the best-known American film about Iran, accused of stereotyping Iranian Muslims. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
