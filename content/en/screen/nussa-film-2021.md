## Overview
"Nussa: The Movie" is a film for children released in 2021, produced in Indonesia. Its leading performers include [[person/muzakki-ramdhan|None]], [[person/fenita-arie|None]], [[person/ali-fikry|None]], [[person/alex-abbad|None]] and [[person/maudy-koesnaedi|None]]. The Indonesian feature film adapted from the Nussa series, produced by Visinema and The Little Giants; Nussa takes part in a science competition and explores his family after his father's departure. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
