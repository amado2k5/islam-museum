## Overview
"Para Pencari Tuhan" is a series about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2007, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/deddy-mizwar|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/deddy-mizwar|None]], [[person/udin-nganga|None]], [[person/jarwo-kwat|None]], [[person/tora-sudiro|None]] and [[person/isa-bajaj|None]]. A long Indonesian Ramadan series about the daily lives of a Muslim village community amid crises and repentance; one of the longest-running Indonesian religious dramas. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
