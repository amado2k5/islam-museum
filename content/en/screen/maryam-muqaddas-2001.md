## Overview
"Holy Maryam" is a faith film released in 2001, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/shahriar-bahrani|Shahriar Bahrani]]. Its leading performers include [[person/shabnam-gholikhani|Shabnam Gholikhani]] and [[person/mohammadreza-bagheri|None]]. An Iranian film by Shahriar Bahrani about Maryam bint Imran and the birth of Isa, based on the Qur'an and Islamic tradition in its narration. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/maryam|Maryam bint Imran]] and [[person/isa-ibn-maryam|Isa ibn Maryam]].
