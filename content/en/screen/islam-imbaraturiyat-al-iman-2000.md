## Overview
"Islam: Empire of Faith" is a documentary series released in 2000, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/robert-h-gardner|None]]. Its narrator is [[person/ben-kingsley|Ben Kingsley]]. An American three-part documentary series produced by PBS and Gardner Films, surveying Islamic history from the mission to the Ottoman state; one of the most widely screened documentaries about Islamic civilisation in the West. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]], and [[polity/ottoman-empire|the Ottoman Empire]].
