## Overview
"A Day in the Haram" is a documentary released in 2017, produced in Saudi Arabia and Britain, directed by [[person/abrar-hussain|None]]. A documentary by Abrar Hussain following a full day in the Masjid al-Haram through the eyes of those who work there: muezzins, preachers, cleaners and guards. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[building/masjid-al-haram|the Masjid al-Haram]] and [[place/mecca|Mecca]].
