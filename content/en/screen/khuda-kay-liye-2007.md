## Overview
"Khuda Kay Liye" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2007, produced in Pakistan, directed by [[person/shoaib-mansoor|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/shaan-shahid|Shaan Shahid]], [[person/naseeruddin-shah|Naseeruddin Shah]], [[person/fawad-afzal-khan|Fawad Afzal Khan]], [[person/iman-ali|Iman Ali]] and [[person/hameed-sheikh|Hameed Sheikh]]. Shoaib Mansoor's Pakistani film about two musician brothers who are separated between extremism and moderation after 9/11; it caused a great uproar in Pakistan about religion, music and forced marriage. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
