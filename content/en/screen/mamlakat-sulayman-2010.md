## Overview
"The Kingdom of Solomon" is a faith film released in 2010, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/shahriar-bahrani|Shahriar Bahrani]]. Its leading performers include [[person/amin-zendegani|Amin Zendegani]], [[person/elham-hamidi|Elham Hamidi]], [[person/mahmoud-pak-niat|Mahmoud Pak Niat]], [[person/mohammad-yaromtaghloo|Mohammad Yaromtaghloo]] and [[person/najmeddin-shariati|Najm al-Din Shariati]]. An Iranian film by Shahriar Bahrani about the Prophet Sulayman ibn Dawud and his kingdom as they appear in Islamic sources; Sulayman appears on screen in line with the Iranian cinematic tradition. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/sulayman-ibn-dawud|Sulayman ibn Dawud]].
