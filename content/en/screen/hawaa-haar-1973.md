## Overview
"Garm Hawa" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 1973, produced in India, directed by [[person/m-s-sathyu|M. S. Sathyu]]. Its leading performers include [[person/balraj-sahni|Balraj Sahni]]. An Indian film by M. S. Sathyu about a Muslim merchant in Agra after Partition who refuses to leave for Pakistan; one of the most important Indian films on Muslims and the division. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
