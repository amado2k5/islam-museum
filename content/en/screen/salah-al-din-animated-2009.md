## Overview
"Salahuddin: The Animated Series" is an animated work for children released in 2009, produced in Malaysia. A Malaysian animated series adapted from children's stories of Salah al-Din, broadcast on Al Jazeera Children's Channel; the first Malaysian historical animation about a great Islamic figure. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
