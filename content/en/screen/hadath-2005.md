## Overview
"Obsession: Radical Islam's War Against the West" is a polemical documentary released in 2005, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/raphael-shore|Raphael Shore]]. Its leading performers include [[person/martin-gilbert|Martin Gilbert]], [[person/khaleel-mohammed|None]] and [[person/robert-s-wistrich|None]]. An American documentary by Raphael Shore presenting political Islam as an existential threat to the West; distributed free in the millions and criticised for conflating jihadism with Islam. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
