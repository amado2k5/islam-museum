## Overview
"The Ottomans: Europe's Muslim Princes" is a documentary series released in 2013, produced in Britain. A three-part documentary series presented by Ragheb Omar on the BBC about the Ottoman state and its relationship with Europe. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[polity/ottoman-empire|the Ottoman Empire]] and [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]].
