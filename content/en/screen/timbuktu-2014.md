## Overview
"Timbuktu" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2014, produced in Mauritania and France, directed by [[person/abderrahmane-sissako|Abderrahmane Sissako]]. Its leading performers include [[person/abel-jafri|Abel Jafri]], [[person/hichem-yacoubi|Hichem Yacoubi]], [[person/toulou-kiki|Toulou Kiki]], [[person/fatoumata-diawara|Fatoumata Diawara]] and [[person/ibrahim-ahmed-dit-pino|None]]. Abderrahmane Sissako's film about life in Timbuktu under the jihadist group Ansar Dine in 2012; nominated for the Oscar for Best Foreign Language Film, and it condemned violent extremism from inside Muslim life. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/timbuktu|Timbuktu]]. Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Prize of the Ecumenical Jury Cannes, François Chalais Prize, Grand Prix, Lumière Award for Best Film. {cite:wikidata}
