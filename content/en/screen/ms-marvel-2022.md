## Overview
"Ms. Marvel" is a short series about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2022, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/adil-el-arbi|Adil El Arbi]], [[person/bilall-fallah|Bilall Fallah]] and [[person/meera-menon|Meera Menon]]. Its leading performers include [[person/iman-vellani|Iman Vellani]], [[person/matt-lintz|Matt Lintz]], [[person/yasmeen-fletcher|None]], [[person/aramis-knight|Aramis Knight]] and [[person/travina-springer|None]]. A Marvel series about Kamala Khan, a Pakistani-American Muslim teenager in Jersey City who gains superpowers; the first Muslim lead in the Marvel world, depicting the mosque, prayer and religious culture. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
