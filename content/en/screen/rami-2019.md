## Overview
"Ramy" is a comedy series released in 2019, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/ramy-youssef|Ramy Youssef]], [[person/cherien-dabis|Cherien Dabis]] and [[person/christopher-storer|Christopher Storer]]. Its leading performers include [[person/steve-way|None]], [[person/ramy-youssef|Ramy Youssef]], [[person/amr-waked|Amr Waked]], [[person/may-calamawy|May Calamawy]] and [[person/hiam-abbass|Hiam Abbass]]. A comedy-drama series by Ramy Youssef on Hulu about an Egyptian-American young man struggling with his faith and desires in New Jersey; the first major American series with a Muslim lead, and its lead won a Golden Globe. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Ramy Youssef won the 2020 Golden Globe for Best Actor in a TV Comedy, Hulu's first in the category. {cite:variety-ramy-globe-2020} {cite:latimes-ramy-globe-2020}
