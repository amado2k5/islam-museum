## Overview
"Raqs-e-Bismil" is a series about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2020, produced in Pakistan. A Pakistani series about a young man from a religious family who falls in love with a woman from another world, and learns the difference between inherited religiosity and true piety; one of the most successful modern Pakistani dramas. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
