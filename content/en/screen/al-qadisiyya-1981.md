## Overview
"Al-Qadisiyya" is a historical drama film released in 1981, produced in Egypt, directed by [[person/salah-abu-seif|Salah Abu Seif]]. Its leading performers include [[person/soad-hosny|Soad Hosny]], [[person/ezzat-el-alaili|Ezzat El Alaili]], [[person/mohammed-hassan-el-joundi|Mohammed Hassan El Joundi]], [[person/laila-taher|Laila Taher]] and [[person/kanaan-wasfy|Kanaan Wasfy]]. An Egyptian film by Salah Abu Seif about the battle of al-Qadisiyya and the fall of the Sasanian Empire before the armies of the conquest; one of the classics of Egyptian historical drama about the conquests. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[event/battle-of-al-qadisiyya|the battle of al-Qadisiyya]] and [[period/rashidun|the era of the rightly guided caliphs]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
