## Overview
"Where Do We Go Now?" is a comedy film released in 2011, produced in Lebanon and France, directed by [[person/nadine-labaki|Nadine Labaki]]. Its leading performers include [[person/nadine-labaki|Nadine Labaki]], [[person/adel-karam|Adel Karam]], [[person/karin-dor|Karin Dor]] and [[person/frederick-stafford|Frederick Stafford]]. Nadine Labaki's film about a Lebanese village where Muslims and Christians live together, and its women band together to stop a sectarian strife; a major prize at the Toronto festival. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: François Chalais Prize. {cite:wikidata}
