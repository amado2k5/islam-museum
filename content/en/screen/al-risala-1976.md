## Summary
*The Message* (al-Risala) is a historical drama film released in 1976, produced by Lebanon, Libya, Kuwait and Morocco, directed by [[person/moustapha-akkad|Moustapha Akkad]] and [[person/andrew-marton|Andrew Marton]]. Its leading actors include [[person/anthony-quinn|Anthony Quinn]], [[person/irene-papas|Irene Papas]], [[person/michael-ansara|Michael Ansara]], [[person/johnny-sekka|Johnny Sekka]] and [[person/michael-forest|Michael Forest]]. It is an epic film about the birth of Islam. It was shot in two versions, Arabic and English, with different actors, and the Prophet is neither seen nor heard in it {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}.

## Context and reception
The work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[event/hijra|the Hijra]], [[event/battle-of-badr|the Battle of Badr]], [[event/battle-of-uhud|the Battle of Uhud]], [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]] and [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. The Prophet does not appear in person. al-Azhar endorsed it, as stated in the film's opening title {cite:wikidata}. The Muslim World League objected to it {cite:wikidata}. It was banned from screening in Saudi Arabia, Egypt and Kuwait {cite:wikidata}. It drew documented protests {cite:atlantic-message-2012} {cite:telegraph-message-1976}.
