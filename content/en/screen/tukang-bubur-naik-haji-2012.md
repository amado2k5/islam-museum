## Overview
"Tukang Bubur Naik Haji" is a series about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2012, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/ucik-supra|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/mat-solar|None]], [[person/uci-bing-slamet|None]], [[person/nani-widjaja|None]], [[person/citra-kirana|None]] and [[person/andi-arsyil-rahman|None]]. A long Indonesian series about a poor porridge seller who saves up for the hajj; one of the longest-running and most popular Indonesian religious dramas during Ramadan. {cite:wikidata}
