## Overview
"Lion of the Desert" is a historical drama film released in 1981, produced in the United States and Libya, directed by [[person/moustapha-akkad|Moustapha Akkad]]. Its leading performers include [[person/anthony-quinn|Anthony Quinn]], [[person/oliver-reed|Oliver Reed]], [[person/rod-steiger|Rod Steiger]], [[person/raf-vallone|Raf Vallone]] and [[person/irene-papas|Irene Papas]]. Moustapha Akkad's film starring Anthony Quinn about the Libyan fighter Omar al-Mukhtar and his resistance to Italian colonialism; it was banned in Italy for decades because it showed the brutality of the fascist campaign. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/umar-al-mukhtar|Omar al-Mukhtar]], [[place/libya|Libya]], and [[period/colonial-era|the colonial era]]. It was banned in Italy. {cite:wikidata}
