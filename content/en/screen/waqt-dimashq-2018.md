## Overview
"Zamane Damascus" is a historical drama film released in 2018, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/ebrahim-hatamikia|Ebrahim Hatamikia]]. An Iranian film by Ebrahim Hatamikia about Iranian pilots who carry humanitarian aid in Syria under the siege of ISIS; one of the largest works of contemporary Iranian war cinema. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
