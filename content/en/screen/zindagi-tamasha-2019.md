## Overview
"Zindagi Tamasha" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2019, produced in Pakistan, directed by [[person/sarmad-sultan-khoosat|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/samiya-mumtaz|Samiya Mumtaz]], [[person/eman-suleman|None]], [[person/adeel-afzal|None]], [[person/nadia-afghan|None]] and [[person/mehar-bano|None]]. A Pakistani film about a singing religious man who is filmed dancing at a wedding, and whom society brands; it provoked religious objections that delayed its release, and then it became Pakistan's Oscar submission. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the objections recorded: Tehreek-e-Labbaik Pakistan; release delayed from January 2020. {cite:wikidata}
