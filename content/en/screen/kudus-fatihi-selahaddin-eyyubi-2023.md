## Overview
"Selahaddin Eyyubi: Kudüs Fatihi" is a historical drama series released in 2023, produced in Turkey and Pakistan. Its leading performers include [[person/ugur-gunes|Uğur Güneş]], [[person/mehmet-ali-nuroglu|Mehmet Ali Nuroğlu]], [[person/ali-ersan-duru|Ali Ersan Duru]], [[person/sezgin-erdemir|Sezgin Erdemir]] and [[person/sezin-akbasogullari|Sezin Akbaşoğulları]]. A joint Turkish–Pakistani series about Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi, from his rise in Egypt to the recovery of Jerusalem; the largest contemporary television production on the hero of the Crusades wars. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

This work deals with [[person/salah-al-din-yusuf-ibn-ayyub|Salah al-Din Yusuf ibn Ayyub al-Malik al-Nasir]], [[polity/ayyubid-dynasty|the Ayyubid dynasty]], [[period/crusades-ayyubid-mamluk|the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks]], [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]], and [[place/cairo|Cairo]].
