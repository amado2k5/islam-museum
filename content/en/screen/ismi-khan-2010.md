## Overview
"My Name Is Khan" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2010, produced in India and the United States, directed by [[person/karan-johar|Karan Johar]]. Its leading performers include [[person/shah-rukh-khan|Shah Rukh Khan]], [[person/kajol|Kajol]], [[person/jennifer-echols|None]], [[person/jimmy-shergill|None]] and [[person/tanay-chheda|None]]. A Karan Johar film starring Shah Rukh Khan about an Indian Muslim with Asperger's who crosses America to tell the president, after 9/11, 'My name is Khan and I am not a terrorist'. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
