## Overview
"Khuda Aur Mohabbat" is a series about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2011, produced in Pakistan, directed by [[person/anjum-shahzad|None]]. A Pakistani series about a young man who falls in love with a devout girl and reconsiders his life; one of the best-known Pakistani dramas with a faith character, and it was remade with two new seasons. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
