## Overview
"Fitna" is a polemical short film released in 2008, produced in the Netherlands, directed by [[person/scarlet-pimpernel|None]] and [[person/geert-wilders|Geert Wilders]]. A short film by the Dutch politician Geert Wilders that sets Qur'anic verses beside scenes of violence; it provoked wide international condemnation, diplomatic boycotts and legal debate in the Netherlands. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
