## Overview
"Abu Lahab" is a historical drama film released in 1953, produced in Egypt. An early Egyptian film about the story of Abu Lahab and his enmity to Islam in Mecca; one of the first Arabic films to portray figures of the biography and the Qur'an. {cite:wikidata}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/mecca|Mecca]] and the [[period/prophetic-era|Prophetic era]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
