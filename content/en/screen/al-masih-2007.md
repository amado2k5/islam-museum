## Overview
"The Messiah" is a faith film released in 2007, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/nader-talebzadeh|Nader Talebzadeh]]. An Iranian film by Nader Talebzadeh about the life of Isa ibn Maryam from an Islamic perspective, telling the story as it appears in Islamic sources, without the crucifixion in the Christian sense. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/isa-ibn-maryam|Isa ibn Maryam]] and [[person/maryam|Maryam bint Imran]].
