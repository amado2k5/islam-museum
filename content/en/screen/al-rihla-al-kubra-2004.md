## Overview
"The Great Journey" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2004, produced in France and Morocco, directed by [[person/isma-l-ferroukhi|Ismaël Ferroukhi]]. Its leading performers include [[person/nicolas-cazale|Nicolas Cazalé]], [[person/mohamed-majd|Mohamed Majd]], [[person/jacky-nercessian|Jacky Nercessian]], [[person/kiril-kavadarkov|Kiril Kavadarkov]] and [[person/nihat-nikerel|Nihat Nikerel]]. Ismaël Ferroukhi's film about a Moroccan father who requires his French son to accompany him, driving from France to Mecca for the hajj; one of the most beautiful fiction films about the hajj. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
