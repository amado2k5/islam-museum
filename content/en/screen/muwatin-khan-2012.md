## Overview
"The Muslims" is a comedy series released in 2012, produced in Britain. Its leading performers include [[person/adil-ray|Adil Ray]], [[person/shobu-kapoor|None]], [[person/kris-marshall|Kris Marshall]], [[person/matthew-cottle|None]] and [[person/maya-sondhi|None]]. A BBC sitcom by Adil Ray about the head of a British-Pakistani Muslim family in Birmingham; the first BBC sitcom starring a Muslim, which provoked early debate over stereotypes. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
