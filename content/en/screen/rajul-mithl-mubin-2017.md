## Overview
"Man Like Mobeen" is a comedy series released in 2017, produced in Britain. Its leading performers include [[person/guz-khan|Guz Khan]], [[person/kiell-smith-bynoe|None]], [[person/tez-ilyas|None]], [[person/duaa-karim|None]] and [[person/tolu-ogunmefun|None]]. A BBC Three comedy-drama by Guz Khan about a Muslim young man in Birmingham raising his sister amid questions of class, race, extremism and displacement; it blended humour with social commentary. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
