## Overview
"The Imam" is a historical drama series released in 2017. Its leading performers include [[person/mahyar-kaddour|Mahyar Khadour]], [[person/salloum-haddad|Salloum Haddad]], [[person/qasim-melho|Qasim Melho]], [[person/akef-najem|Akef Najem]] and [[person/mahmoud-khalili|Mahmoud Khalili]]. An Arab series about the life of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal, the trial of the createdness of the Qur'an and his steadfastness on his position; one of the largest Arab works on the life of a scholar among the imams of the Sunna. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad ibn Hanbal, whom Ibn al-Nadim calls Abu Abd Allah Ahmad ibn Hanbal, and who wrote the book al-'Ilal {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p. 320}]], [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]] and the [[period/abbasid-early|early Abbasid period]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
