## Overview
"Incendies" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2010, produced in Canada and France, directed by [[person/denis-villeneuve|Denis Villeneuve]]. Its leading performers include [[person/lubna-azabal|Lubna Azabal]], [[person/maxim-gaudette|None]], [[person/remy-girard|Rémy Girard]], [[person/melissa-desormeaux-poulin|Mélissa Désormeaux-Poulin]] and [[person/allen-altman|Allen Altman]]. Denis Villeneuve's film about two Canadian twins who return to their mother's country in the Middle East to discover the past of the civil war between Muslims and Christians; nominated for the Oscar for Best Foreign Language Film. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Academy of Canadian Cinema and Television Award for Best Motion Picture, Toronto International Film Festival Award for Best Canadian Film, Québec Cinéma Award for Best Film. {cite:wikidata}
