## Overview
"The Big Sick" is a comedy film released in 2017, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/michael-showalter|Michael Showalter]]. Its leading performers include [[person/zoe-kazan|Zoe Kazan]], [[person/holly-hunter|Holly Hunter]], [[person/ray-romano|Ray Romano]], [[person/kumail-nanjiani|Kumail Nanjiani]] and [[person/adeel-akhtar|Adeel Akhtar]]. A comedy-drama by Kumail Nanjiani about his real love story with Emily Gordon amid the traditional marriage expectations of a Pakistani-American family; nominated for the Oscar for Best Original Screenplay. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
