## Overview
"The 99" is an animated work for children released in 2011, produced in Kuwait and Britain. An animated series based on the comic stories by Naif al-Mutawa about superheroes whose values derive from the Names of God; one of the first international works on Muslim superheroes. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
