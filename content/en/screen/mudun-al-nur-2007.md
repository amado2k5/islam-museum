## Overview
"Cities of Light: The Rise and Fall of Islamic Spain" is a documentary television film released in 2007, produced in the United States. A documentary produced by Gardner and Unity Projects on seven centuries of Muslim, Christian and Jewish coexistence in al-Andalus and its fall; screened on PBS. {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]], [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]], and [[place/granada|Granada]].
