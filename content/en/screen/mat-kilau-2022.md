## Overview
"Mat Kilau" is a historical drama film released in 2022, produced in Malaysia, directed by [[person/syamsul-yusof|Syamsul Yusof]]. Its leading performers include [[person/adi-putra|None]], [[person/beto-kusyairy|None]], [[person/fattah-amin|None]], [[person/yayan-ruhian|Yayan Ruhian]] and [[person/wan-hanafi-su|None]]. An epic Malaysian film about the fighter Mat Kilau and his uprising against British colonialism in Pahang in the 1890s; the highest-grossing Malaysian film in the country's history. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
