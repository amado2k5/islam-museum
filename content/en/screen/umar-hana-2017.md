## Overview
"Umar & Hana" is a children's web series released in 2017, produced in Malaysia. A Malaysian animated series for children about the siblings Umar and Hana and religious and social lessons, produced by Dorio+; one of the most successful Islamic channels for children on YouTube, with billions of views and translations into ten languages. {cite:wikidata}
