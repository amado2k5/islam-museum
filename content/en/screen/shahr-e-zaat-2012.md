## Overview
"Shehr-e-Zaat" is a faith series released in 2012, produced in Pakistan, directed by [[person/sarmad-sultan-khoosat|None]]. Its leading performer is [[person/mahira-khan|Mahira Khan]]. A Pakistani series about a young woman of the upper class whom a forbidden love turns to the search for God; one of the best-known Pakistani dramas on spiritual transformation. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
