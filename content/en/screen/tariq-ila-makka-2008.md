## Overview
"The Road to Mecca: The Journey of Muhammad Asad" is a documentary released in 2008, produced in Austria, directed by [[person/georg-misch|None]]. An Austrian documentary by Georg Misch tracing the path of Leopold Weiss, who became Muhammad Asad — from Vienna to Jerusalem to Mecca — in light of his book The Road to Mecca. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
