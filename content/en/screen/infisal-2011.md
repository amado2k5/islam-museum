## Overview
"A Separation" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2011, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/asghar-farhadi|Asghar Farhadi]]. Its leading performers include [[person/sareh-bayat|Sareh Bayat]], [[person/leila-hatami|Leila Hatami]], [[person/payman-maadi|Peyman Maadi]], [[person/shahab-hosseini|Shahab Hosseini]] and [[person/sarina-farhadi|Sarina Farhadi]]. Asghar Farhadi's Oscar-winning film about a divorce entangled in a legal and religious case in Tehran; it places the oath by the Qur'an and piety at the heart of the moral dispute. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: a documented award, Golden Bear, Silver Bear for Best Actress, Silver Bear for Best Actor. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
