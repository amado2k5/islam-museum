## Overview
"Bilal: A New Breed of Hero" is an animated work for children released in 2015, produced in the UAE, directed by [[person/khurram-alavi|None]] and [[person/ayman-jamal|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/adewale-akinnuoye-agbaje|Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje]], [[person/jacob-latimore|Jacob Latimore]], [[person/ian-mcshane|Ian McShane]], [[person/dave-b-mitchell|Dave B. Mitchell]] and [[person/fred-tatasciore|Fred Tatasciore]]. An Emirati animated film about Bilal ibn Rabah, from slavery to becoming the first muezzin in Islam; the first long animated film about a Companion of this scale. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal ibn Rabah]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]]. The Prophet appears in it without facial features.
