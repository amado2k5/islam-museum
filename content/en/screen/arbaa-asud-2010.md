## Overview
"Four Lions" is a comedy film released in 2010, produced in Britain, directed by [[person/chris-morris|Chris Morris]]. Its leading performers include [[person/riz-ahmed|Riz Ahmed]], [[person/kayvan-novak|Kayvan Novak]], [[person/nigel-lindsay|Nigel Lindsay]], [[person/adeel-akhtar|Adeel Akhtar]] and [[person/benedict-cumberbatch|Benedict Cumberbatch]]. A black comedy by Chris Morris about a failed British jihadist cell; it provoked controversy because it ridiculed the terrorists themselves, not Muslims. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
