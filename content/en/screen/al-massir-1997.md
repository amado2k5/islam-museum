## Overview
"Al-Massir" is a historical drama film released in 1997, produced in Egypt and France, directed by [[person/youssef-chahine|Youssef Chahine]]. Its leading performers include [[person/nour-el-sherif|Nour El Sherif]], [[person/khaled-el-nabawy|Khaled El Nabawy]], [[person/laila-elouy|Laila Elwy]], [[person/mahmoud-hemida|Mahmoud Hemida]] and [[person/safia-el-emari|Safia El Emary]]. Youssef Chahine's film about Ibn Rushd in Cordoba of al-Andalus on the eve of the burning of his books, setting twelfth-century Cordoba beside Egypt in the 1990s, when religious extremism was confronted in the name of religion. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/ibn-rushd|Ibn Rushd the Grandson]], [[place/cordoba|Cordoba]] and [[period/crusades-ayyubid-mamluk|the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks]]. It was banned in Egypt. {cite:wikidata}
