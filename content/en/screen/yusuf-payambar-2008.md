## Overview
"Yusuf the Prophet" is a faith series released in 2008, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/farajullah-salahshur|Farajullah Salahshur]]. Its leading performers include [[person/mostafa-zamani|Salam Hayuri]], [[person/mahmoud-pak-niat|Mahmoud Pak Niat]], [[person/katayoun-riahi|Katayoun Riahi]], [[person/jafar-dehghan|Jafar Dehghan]] and [[person/abbas-amiri-moghaddam|Abbas Amiri]]. An Iranian series of forty-five episodes telling the full story of the Prophet Yusuf, from his dream to the reunion with his father Ya'qub; dubbed into dozens of languages, and one of the most successful religious dramas produced in Iran. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
