## Overview
"Osama" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2003, produced in Afghanistan and Iran, directed by [[person/siddiq-barmak|Siddiq Barmak]]. Its leading performer is [[person/marina-golbahari|Marina Golbahari]]. Siddiq Barmak's Afghan film, the first Afghan film after the Taliban, about a girl who disguises herself as a boy named Osama to support her family under Taliban rule; it won the Golden Globe. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
