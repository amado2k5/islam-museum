## Overview
"The 13th Warrior" is a historical drama film released in 1999, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/michael-crichton|Michael Crichton]] and [[person/john-mctiernan|John McTiernan]]. Its leading performers include [[person/antonio-banderas|Antonio Banderas]], [[person/diane-venora|Diane Venora]], [[person/vladimir-kulich|Vladimir Kulich]], [[person/clive-russell|Clive Russell]] and [[person/richard-bremmer|Richard Bremmer]]. An adventure film starring Antonio Banderas as Ahmad ibn Fadlan, the Abbasid envoy who finds himself with Viking warriors; based on Crichton's novel, which is drawn from the account of Ibn Fadlan. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/ibn-fadlan|Ahmad ibn Fadlan]].
