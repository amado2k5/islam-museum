## Overview
"Nussa" is a series for children released in 2019, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/anggia-kharisma|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/muzakki-ramdhan|None]] and [[person/sandra-dewi|Sandra Dewi]]. An Indonesian animated series for children about the boy Nussa and his sister Rara, who learn Islamic values; one of the most successful Islamic animation works for children on YouTube worldwide. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
