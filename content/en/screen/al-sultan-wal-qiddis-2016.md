## Overview
"The Sultan and the Saint" is a documentary released in 2016, produced in the United States. A documentary by Unity Projects Foundation about the meeting of Saint Francis of Assisi with Sultan al-Kamil al-Ayyubi in Damietta in 1219; narrated by Jeremy Irons. {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[period/crusades-ayyubid-mamluk|the Crusades, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks]].
