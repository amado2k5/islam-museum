## Overview
"Muhammad: The Messenger of God" is a historical drama film released in 2015, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/majid-majidi|Majid Majidi]]. Its leading performers include [[person/alireza-shoja-nouri|Alireza Shoja Nouri]], [[person/sareh-bayat|Sareh Bayat]], [[person/mahdi-pakdel|Mahdi Pakdel]], [[person/mina-sadati|None]] and [[person/mohsen-tanabandeh|Mohsen Tanabandeh]]. An epic Iranian film by Majid Majidi about the childhood of the Prophet in Mecca; the character appears from behind and by the hands, without facial features, and it was intended to be the first part of a trilogy. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[period/pre-islamic-arabia|pre-Islamic Arabia]], and [[place/mecca|Mecca]]. The Prophet appears in it without facial features. It provoked objections from al-Azhar and Saudi scholars, reported in press coverage. {cite:wikidata}
