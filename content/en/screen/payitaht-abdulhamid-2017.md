## Overview
"Payitaht: Abdulhamid" is a historical drama series released in 2017, produced in Turkey. Its leading performer is [[person/bulent-i-nal|Bülent İnal]]. A Turkish series about Sultan Abdulhamid II and the last years of his reign from the Yıldız Palace; it was described as offering a modern reading of the vision of the late Ottoman state. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/abd-al-hamid-ii-uthmani|Abdulhamid II]], [[polity/ottoman-empire|the Ottoman Empire]], [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]], and [[period/colonial-era|the colonial era]].
