## Overview
"Joyland" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2022, produced in Pakistan, directed by [[person/saim-sadiq|Saim Sadiq]]. Its leading performers include [[person/alina-khan|Alina Khan]], [[person/sarwat-gilani|None]], [[person/sohail-sameer|Sohail Sameer]] and [[person/sania-saeed|None]]. A Pakistani film by Saim Sadiq about a family in Lahore shaken when their son joins a dance troupe; it provoked a temporary ban and religious debate, and then the ban was lifted and it won a prize at Cannes. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
It was banned in Pakistan. {cite:wikidata} Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Jury Prize, Queer Palm. {cite:wikidata}
