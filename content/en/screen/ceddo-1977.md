## Overview
The well-known Senegalese film by Ousmane Sembène about the 'Ceddo', the people of the land, who resist the spread of Islam, Christianity and slavery in the kingdom of Wolof; one of Africa's greatest films about the coming of Islam. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
