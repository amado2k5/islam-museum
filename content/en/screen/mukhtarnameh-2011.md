## Overview
"Mukhtarnameh" is a historical drama series released in 2011, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/davood-mir-bagheri|Davood Mirbagheri]]. Its leading performers include [[person/fariborz-arabnia|Fariborz Arabnia]], [[person/mehdi-fakhimzadeh|None]], [[person/farhad-aslani|Farhad Aslani]], [[person/nasrin-moghanloo|None]] and [[person/davoud-rashidi|Davoud Rashidi]]. An Iranian series by Davood Mirbagheri about al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd al-Thaqafi and his uprising in Kufa to avenge the dead of Karbala; it depicts the Imams of the Ahl al-Bayt with faces, in line with Shi'i tradition. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/al-mukhtar-ibn-abi-ubayd|al-Mukhtar ibn Abi Ubayd]], [[event/battle-of-karbala|the battle of Karbala]], [[place/kufa|Kufa]], and [[period/umayyad|the Umayyad era]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
