## Overview
"Innocence of Muslims" is a short polemical American film released in 2012, directed by [[person/nakoula-basseley-nakoula|Mark Basseley Youssef]]. Its leading performers include [[person/q57452166|None]] and [[person/q57452295|None]]. An American short film made by Nakoula Basseley Nakoula that insults the Prophet; it sparked protests and attacks in several countries in 2012 and became a legal and political case. {cite:wikidata}

## Context and reception
It provoked documented protests. {cite:wikidata} It was banned in Egypt, India, Pakistan, Indonesia, Malaysia, Russia and SG. {cite:wikidata}
