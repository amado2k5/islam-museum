## Overview
"Muawiya" is a historical drama series released in 2025, produced in Saudi Arabia, directed by [[person/tarek-alarian|Tarek Al-Arian]]. Its leading performers include [[person/lojain-ismail|Lojain Ismail]], [[person/eyad-nassar|Eyad Nassar]], [[person/souhir-ben-amara|Souhir Ben Amara]], [[person/wael-sharaf|Wael Sharaf]] and [[person/yazan-khalil|Yazan Khalil]]. An MBC-produced Arab series about Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan, the strife and the early Umayyad state; its screening provoked wide controversy over the depiction of the Companions and the events of the strife. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]], [[period/umayyad|the Umayyad era]], and [[place/damascus|Damascus]]. It does not show the Prophet in person. It provoked public and scholarly debate in 2025 over the depiction of the Companions. {cite:wikidata}
