## Overview
"The Reluctant Fundamentalist" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2012, produced in the United States and Pakistan, directed by [[person/mira-nair|Mira Nair]]. Its leading performers include [[person/riz-ahmed|Riz Ahmed]], [[person/kate-hudson|Kate Hudson]], [[person/liev-schreiber|Liev Schreiber]], [[person/kiefer-sutherland|Kiefer Sutherland]] and [[person/om-puri|Om Puri]]. Mira Nair's film, based on the novel by Mohsin Hamid, about a Pakistani graduate of Princeton who is questioned after 9/11 about his path from Wall Street to Lahore. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
