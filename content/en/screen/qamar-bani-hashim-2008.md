## Overview
"Qamar Bani Hashim" is a historical drama series released in 2008, produced in Syria, directed by [[person/q12239794|Mohammad al-Sheikh Najeeb]]. A Syrian series by Mohammad Azizia about the Banu Hashim from Abd al-Muttalib to the Prophet's mission; it answers the question of depicting the Prophet by showing his effect rather than his person, and portrays the story of Mecca on the eve of the mission. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[period/pre-islamic-arabia|pre-Islamic Arabia]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
