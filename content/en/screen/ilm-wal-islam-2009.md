## Overview
"Science and Islam" is an educational documentary series released in 2009, produced in Britain, directed by [[person/tim-usborne|None]]. A three-part BBC series presented by the physicist Jim Al-Khalili on the history of science in Islamic civilisation, from the House of Wisdom to today. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
