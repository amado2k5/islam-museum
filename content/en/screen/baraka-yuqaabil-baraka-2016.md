## Overview
"Barakah Meets Barakah" is a comedy film released in 2016, produced in Saudi Arabia, directed by [[person/mahmoud-sabbagh|Mahmoud Sabbagh]]. Its leading performers include [[person/hisham-fageeh|Hisham Fageeh]] and [[person/fatima-al-banawi|Fatima Al-Banawi]]. Mahmoud Sabbagh's first Saudi romantic film, about a municipal employee and a girl from a different world in Jeddah; it started a conversation about social boundaries in a conservative society. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
