## Overview
"The Stoning of Soraya M." is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2009, produced in the United States and Iran, directed by [[person/cyrus-nowrasteh|Cyrus Nowrasteh]]. Its leading performers include [[person/shohreh-aghdashloo|Shohreh Aghdashloo]], [[person/mozhan-marno|Mozhan Marnò]], [[person/jim-caviezel|Jim Caviezel]], [[person/parviz-sayyad|None]] and [[person/vida-ghahremani|None]]. An American–Iranian film about the story of Soraya Manutchehri, wrongly accused of adultery and stoned in an Iranian village, based on a book by the journalist Freidoune Sahebjam. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Satellite Award for Best Actress – Motion Picture. {cite:wikidata}
