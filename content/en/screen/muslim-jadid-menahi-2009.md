## Overview
"A New Muslim Wonderful" is a documentary released in 2009, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/jennifer-maytorena-taylor|None]]. A PBS documentary about Hamza Perez, a Puerto Rican Muslim American rapper, and his family and da'wa life in Pittsburgh after 9/11. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
