## Overview
"Inside Mecca" is a documentary released in 2003, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/anisa-mehdi|Anisa Mehdi]]. A National Geographic documentary following pilgrims from three continents on the hajj inside Mecca, with background on the history of the Kaaba and the rites of the hajj. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[practice/hajj|the hajj]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[building/masjid-al-haram|the Masjid al-Haram]].
