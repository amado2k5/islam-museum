## Overview
"99 Cahaya di Langit Eropa" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2013, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/guntur-soeharjanto|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/acha-septriasa|None]], [[person/abimana-aryasatya|Abimana Aryasatya]], [[person/raline-shah|None]], [[person/nino-fernandez|Nino Fernandez]] and [[person/dewi-sandra|Dewi Sandra]]. An Indonesian film about an Indonesian Muslim couple in Vienna who discover the traces of Islamic civilisation in Europe while facing Islamophobia; one of the most successful Indonesian films in the Arab world. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
