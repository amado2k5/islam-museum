## Overview
"Sang Pencerah" is a historical drama film released in 2010, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/hanung-bramantyo|Hanung Bramantyo]]. Its leading performers include [[person/zaskia-adya-mecca|None]], [[person/slamet-rahardjo|Slamet Rahardjo]], [[person/giring-ganesha|None]] and [[person/lukman-sardi|None]]. An Indonesian film about Ahmad Dahlan, founder of the Muhammadiyah association, and the early years of the Indonesian Islamic reform movement in Yogyakarta. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
