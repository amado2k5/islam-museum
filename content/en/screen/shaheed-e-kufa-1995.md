## Overview
"Shahid-e-Kufa" is a historical drama series released in 1995, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/davood-mir-bagheri|Davood Mirbagheri]]. Its leading performers include [[person/dariush-arjmand|Dariush Arjmand]], [[person/mehdi-fathi|None]], [[person/behzad-farahani|Behzad Farahani]], [[person/vishka-asayesh|Vishka Asayesh]] and [[person/jamshid-mashayekhi|Jamshid Mashayekhi]]. An Iranian series by Davood Mirbagheri about Ali ibn Abi Talib, from the Prophet's pledge of the caliphate to his martyrdom in the sanctuary of Kufa; the largest Iranian production on the life of the Imam, which depicts him with a visible face. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]], [[place/medina|Medina]], [[place/kufa|Kufa]], and [[period/rashidun|the era of the rightly guided caliphs]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
