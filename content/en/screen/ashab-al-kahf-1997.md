## Overview
"The People of the Cave" is a faith series released in 1997, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/farajullah-salahshur|Farajullah Salahshur]]. Its leading performers include [[person/jafar-dehghan|Jafar Dehghan]], [[person/jahanbakhsh-soltani|Jahanbakhsh Soltani]], [[person/homa-khakpash|None]] and [[person/majid-abdol-azizi|None]]. An Iranian series by Farajullah Salahshur about the youths of the Cave who believed in their Lord in an idolatrous city; among the first Iranian Qur'anic dramas to reach wide audiences in the Arab world. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
