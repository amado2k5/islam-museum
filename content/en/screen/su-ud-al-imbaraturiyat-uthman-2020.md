## Overview
"Rise of Empires: Ottoman" is a documentary-drama series released in 2020, produced in Turkey and the United States. Its leading performers include [[person/cem-yigit-uzumoglu|Cem Yiğit Üzümoğlu]], [[person/tuba-buyukustun|Tuba Büyüküstün]], [[person/damla-sonmez|Damla Sönmez]], [[person/osman-sonant|None]] and [[person/tolga-tekin|Tolga Tekin]]. A Netflix documentary-drama series about Mehmed the Conqueror's conquest of Constantinople and what followed, combining Turkish acting with interviews with historians. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[event/ottoman-conquest-constantinople-1453|the Ottoman conquest of Constantinople by Mehmed II]], [[person/mehmed-ii|Sultan Mehmed II]], and [[place/istanbul|Istanbul]].
