## Overview
"Ayat-Ayat Cinta" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2008, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/hanung-bramantyo|Hanung Bramantyo]]. Its leading performers include [[person/fedi-nuril|None]], [[person/carissa-putri|None]], [[person/rianti-cartwright|Rianti Cartwright]], [[person/zaskia-adya-mecca|None]] and [[person/melanie-putria-dewita-sari|None]]. An Indonesian film based on the novel by Habiburrahman El Shirazy about a devout Indonesian student at al-Azhar and his love; a cinematic phenomenon that changed Indonesian drama towards religious romance. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
