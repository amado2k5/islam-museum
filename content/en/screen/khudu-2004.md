## Overview
"Submission" is a polemical short film released in 2004, produced in the Netherlands, directed by [[person/theo-van-gogh|Theo van Gogh]]. A Dutch short film by Theo van Gogh and Ayaan Hirsi Ali on the position of women in Islam, with shocking scenes; van Gogh was assassinated months later. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
It provoked documented protests. {cite:wikidata}
