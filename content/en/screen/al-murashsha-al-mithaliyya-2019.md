## Overview
"The Perfect Candidate" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2019, produced in Saudi Arabia and Germany, directed by [[person/haifaa-al-mansour|Haifaa Al-Mansour]]. Haifaa al-Mansour's film about a Saudi doctor who decides to run for a municipal council in a conservative city; she deals with religion, gender and local politics calmly. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
