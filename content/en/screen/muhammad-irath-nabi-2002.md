## Overview
"Muhammad: Legacy of a Prophet" is a documentary released in 2002, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/omar-al-qattan|Omar Al-Qattan]]. Its leading performer is [[person/karen-armstrong|Karen Armstrong]]. An American documentary produced by the United Projects Foundation and screened on PBS, combining the biography of the Prophet with the lives of contemporary American Muslims. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]] and [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]].
