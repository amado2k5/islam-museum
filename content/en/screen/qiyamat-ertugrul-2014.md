## Overview
"Diriliş: Ertuğrul" is a historical drama series released in 2014, produced in Turkey, directed by [[person/metin-gunay|Metin Günay]]. Its leading performers include [[person/engin-altan-duzyatan|Engin Altan Düzyatan]], [[person/serdar-gokhan|Serdar Gökhan]], [[person/hulya-darcan|Hülya Darcan]], [[person/didem-balcin|Didem Balçın]] and [[person/kaan-tasaner|Kaan Taşaner]]. A Turkish series about Ertuğrul ibn Suleyman Shah, father of Osman, founder of the Ottoman state; it spread widely in the Arab world, Pakistan, India and South Asia through dubbing and broadcast. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/ertugrul-ghazi|Ertuğrul]], [[place/anatolia|Anatolia]], and [[period/mongol-post-mongol|the Mongol era and after]].
