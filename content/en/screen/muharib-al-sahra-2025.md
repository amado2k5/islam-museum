## Overview
"The Desert Warrior" is a historical drama film released in 2025, produced in Saudi Arabia and the United States, directed by [[person/rupert-wyatt|Rupert Wyatt]]. Its leading performers include [[person/anthony-mackie|Anthony Mackie]], [[person/sharlto-copley|Sharlto Copley]], [[person/ben-kingsley|Ben Kingsley]], [[person/aiysha-hart|Aiysha Hart]] and [[person/ghassan-massoud|Ghassan Massoud]]. A Saudi–American adventure film by Rupert Wyatt set in the Arabian Peninsula in the seventh century, on the eve of the rise of Islam, starring Anthony Mackie and Aiysha Hart; one of the first major Hollywood productions filmed in NEOM. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[period/pre-islamic-arabia|pre-Islamic Arabia]].
