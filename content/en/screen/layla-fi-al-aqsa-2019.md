## Overview
"Night at al-Aqsa" is a documentary released in 2019, produced in Britain. A documentary by Abrar Hussein filmed on the Night of Decree inside the al-Aqsa Mosque, following Palestinian worshippers, guards of the Haram and its workers on the holiest night of the year. {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[building/al-aqsa-mosque|al-Aqsa Mosque]] and [[place/jerusalem|Jerusalem]].
