## Overview
"Reel Bad Arabs" is a documentary released in 2006, produced in the United States and directed by [[person/sut-jhally|Sut Jhally]]. Its best-known voice is [[person/jack-shaheen|Jack Shaheen]]. A documentary based on Jack Shaheen's book, documenting how Hollywood stereotyped Arabs and Muslims in more than a thousand films over a century. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
