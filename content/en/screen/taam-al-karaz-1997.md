## Overview
"Taste of Cherry" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 1997, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/abbas-kiarostami|Abbas Kiarostami]]. Its leading performer is [[person/homayoun-ershadi|Homayoun Ershadi]]. Abbas Kiarostami's Palme d'Or winner about a man who drives around Tehran looking for someone to bury him if he commits suicide; religious debate about life and death runs through it. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
