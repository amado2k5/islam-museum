## Overview
"Omar" is a historical drama series released in 2012, produced in Saudi Arabia, directed by [[person/hatem-ali|Hatem Ali]]. Its leading performers include [[person/samer-ismail|Samer Ismail]], [[person/ghassan-massoud|Ghassan Massoud]], [[person/ghanem-zrelli|Ghanem Zrelli]], [[person/qasim-melho|Qasim Melho]] and [[person/fethi-haddaoui|Fethi Haddaoui]]. An MBC historical series following the life of Umar ibn al-Khattab from the pre-Islamic period to his martyrdom as caliph; the Prophet does not appear on screen and his voice is not heard. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]], [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]], [[period/rashidun|the era of the rightly guided caliphs]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[place/medina|Medina]]. It does not show the Prophet in person. It provoked documented protests. {cite:egyptind-omar-2012} {cite:alarabiya-omar-2012}
