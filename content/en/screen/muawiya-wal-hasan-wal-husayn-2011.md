## Overview
"Muawiya, al-Hasan and al-Husayn" is a historical drama series released in 2011. Its leading performer is [[person/rasheed-assaf|Rasheed Assaf]]. A large Arab co-produced series about the great strife from the caliphate of al-Hasan to his peace treaty, and the events of Mu'awiya and Karbala; it provoked debate over the portrayal of the Ahl al-Bayt and the Companions. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/hasan-ibn-ali|al-Hasan ibn Ali]], [[person/husayn-ibn-ali|al-Husayn ibn Ali]], [[person/muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Mu'awiya ibn Abi Sufyan]], [[event/battle-of-karbala|the battle of Karbala]], [[period/rashidun|the era of the rightly guided caliphs]], and [[period/umayyad|the Umayyad era]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
