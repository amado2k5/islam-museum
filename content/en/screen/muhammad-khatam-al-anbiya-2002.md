## Overview
"Muhammad: Messenger of God" is an animated work for children released in 2002, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/richard-rich|Richard Rich]]. An American animated film produced by Bader International, a company with roots in American Islam, about the Prophet's mission and the rise of Islam in Mecca; the Prophet is neither seen nor heard. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]], [[place/mecca|Mecca]], and [[place/medina|Medina]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
