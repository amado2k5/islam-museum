## Overview
Shadi Abdel Salam's classic Egyptian film about a tribe that trades in the mummies of the pharaohs and the struggle of its son between loyalty and the preservation of heritage; one of the greatest films of Arab cinema. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
