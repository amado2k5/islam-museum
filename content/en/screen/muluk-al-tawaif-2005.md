## Overview
"Muluk al-Tawa'if" is a historical drama series released in 2005, produced in Syria, directed by [[person/hatem-ali|Hatem Ali]]. Its leading performers include [[person/jamal-suliman|Jamal Suleiman]], [[person/ayman-zeidan|Ayman Zeidan]], [[person/taim-hasan|Taim Hassan]], [[person/sulaf-fawakherji|Sulaf Fawakherji]] and [[person/suzan-najm-aldeeen|Suzan Najm Eddine]]. A Syrian series by Hatem Ali about the age of the Taifa kings in al-Andalus after the fall of Cordoba: al-Mu'tamid ibn Abbad, Ibn Zaydun and Wallada, and the struggle of the cities with the Almoravids. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[place/al-andalus|al-Andalus]].
