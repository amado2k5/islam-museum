## Overview
"Malcolm X" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 1992, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/spike-lee|Spike Lee]]. Its leading performers include [[person/denzel-washington|Denzel Washington]], [[person/angela-bassett|Angela Bassett]], [[person/albert-hall|Albert Hall]], [[person/al-freeman-jr|Al Freeman Jr.]] and [[person/delroy-lindo|Delroy Lindo]]. Spike Lee's film starring Denzel Washington about the life of Malcolm X from crime to the Nation of Islam to Sunni Islam after his pilgrimage to Mecca; one of the best-known American biographical films. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/malcolm-x|Malcolm X]] and [[practice/hajj|the hajj]]. Among the awards and honours recorded for it: National Board of Review: Top Ten Films. {cite:wikidata}
