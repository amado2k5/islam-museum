## Overview
"A Prophet" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2009, produced in France, directed by [[person/jacques-audiard|Jacques Audiard]]. Its leading performers include [[person/tahar-rahim|Tahar Rahim]], [[person/niels-arestrup|Niels Arestrup]], [[person/adel-bencherif|Adel Bencherif]], [[person/hichem-yacoubi|Hichem Yacoubi]] and [[person/reda-kateb|Reda Kateb]]. Jacques Audiard's film about a young French Arab Muslim who rises within the world of crime in a French prison; a Grand Prix winner at Cannes. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
Among the awards and honours recorded for it: Cannes Film Festival Grand Prix, National Board of Review Award for Best Foreign Language Film, Louis Delluc Prize, César Award for Best Production Design. {cite:wikidata}
