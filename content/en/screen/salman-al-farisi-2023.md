## Overview
"Salman al-Farsi" is a historical drama series released in 2023, produced in Iran, directed by [[person/davood-mir-bagheri|Davood Mirbagheri]]. Its leading performers include [[person/alireza-shoja-nouri|Alireza Shoja Nouri]], [[person/mohammad-yaromtaghloo|Mohammad Yaromtaghloo]], [[person/farhad-aslani|Farhad Aslani]], [[person/setareh-eskandari|Setareh Eskandari]] and [[person/shahram-haghighatdoost|None]]. An Iranian series by Davood Mirbagheri about Salman al-Farsi and his journey from Persia to Islam and his meeting with the Prophet; one of the largest Iranian religious dramas in recent years. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[period/prophetic-era|the Prophetic era]], and [[place/medina|Medina]]. It does not show the Prophet in person.
