## Overview
"Yunus Emre: Aşkın Yolculuğu" is a faith series released in 2015, produced in Turkey. A Turkish series about the Sufi poet Yunus Emre and his apprenticeship under his master Tapduk Emre; one of the few television works devoted to the life of a Sufi. {cite:wikidata}

## Context and reception
This work deals with [[person/yunus-emre|Yunus Emre]], [[place/anatolia|Anatolia]], and [[period/mongol-post-mongol|the Mongol era and after]].
