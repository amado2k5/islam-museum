## Overview
"Luwur Waktu" is an educational series released in 1999, produced in Indonesia, directed by [[person/deddy-mizwar|None]], [[person/aldisar-syafar|None]] and [[person/abdul-kadir|None]]. Its leading performers include [[person/deddy-mizwar|None]], [[person/jourast-jordy|None]], [[person/zaskia-adya-mecca|None]], [[person/aditya-novika|None]] and [[person/christy-jusung|None]]. An Indonesian Ramadan series about a scholar and children who explore time and religion through a time machine over six seasons; one of the first and best-known Indonesian religious-scientific dramas for a generation. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
