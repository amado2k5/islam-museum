## Overview
"Ali" is a film about contemporary Muslim life, released in 2001, produced in the United States, directed by [[person/michael-mann|Michael Mann]]. Its leading performers include [[person/will-smith|Will Smith]], [[person/jamie-foxx|Jamie Foxx]], [[person/jon-voight|Jon Voight]], [[person/mario-van-peebles|Mario Van Peebles]] and [[person/ron-silver|Ron Silver]]. Michael Mann's film starring Will Smith about Muhammad Ali, between his conversion to Islam, his defence of his title and his refusal to go to Vietnam; Smith was nominated for an Oscar for the role. {cite:wikidata} {cite:imdb}
