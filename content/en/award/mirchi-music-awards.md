## Summary
The Mirchi Music Awards are annual awards given by India's Radio Mirchi for artistic and technical excellence in Hindi film music, in seventeen categories; the first ceremony, on 28 March 2009, honoured the music of 2008, and each ceremony since honours the previous year. {cite:wikipedia-mirchi-music-awards-en} {cite:wikidata-mirchi-music-awards} A South Indian edition followed in 2010 and a Bengali edition in 2012. {cite:wikipedia-mirchi-music-awards-en}

## The Sufi category
Among its categories is the song representing the Sufi tradition; at the 4th ceremony, on 21 March 2012, for the music of 2011, it went to [[person/a-r-rahman|A. R. Rahman]]'s qawwali [[work/kun-faya-kun-rockstar|'Kun Faya Kun']] from Rockstar, whose soundtrack also won the listeners' album of the year. {cite:wikipedia-rockstar-soundtrack-en} {cite:wikipedia-mirchi-music-awards-en}
