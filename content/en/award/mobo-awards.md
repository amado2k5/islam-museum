## Summary
The MOBO Awards (Music of Black Origin) are an annual British ceremony honouring hip-hop, grime, R&B, soul, reggae, jazz, gospel and other music of Black origin, founded by Kanya King and Andy Ruffell; Wikidata dates the founding to 1996. {cite:wikipedia-mobo-awards-en} {cite:wikidata-mobo-awards} The ceremony was held in London until 2009 and since then also in Glasgow, Leeds, Sheffield and Newcastle, on several British channels. {cite:wikipedia-mobo-awards-en}

## In the museum
The Senegalese singer [[person/youssou-ndour|Youssou N'Dour]] won its Best African Act award in 2005, the year his album [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] won a Grammy. {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}
