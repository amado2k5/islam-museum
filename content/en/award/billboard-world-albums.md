## Summary
Billboard World Albums is the American magazine Billboard's chart of the best-selling world-music albums in the United States, cited in the chart table of the article on the album Egypt. {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak position on this chart as its sources give it: for example, number 6 for the Senegalese singer [[person/youssou-ndour|Youssou N'Dour]]'s [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] in 2004, the album that went on to win a Grammy. {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}
