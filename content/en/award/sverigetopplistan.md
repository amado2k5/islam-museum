## Summary
Sverigetopplistan, "the Sweden top list", is Sweden's national record chart, based on sales data from IFPI Sverige; it was known as Topplistan from 1975 to 1997 and Hitlistan from 1998 to 2007, and by its present name since October 2007. {cite:wikipedia-sverigetopplistan-en} {cite:wikidata-sverigetopplistan}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources' chart tables give it, and never ranks works by taste: Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] peaked at number 16 there in 2006. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en}
