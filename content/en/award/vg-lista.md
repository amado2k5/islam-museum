## Summary
VG-lista is Norway's main chart of albums and singles by Norwegian and international artists, published weekly in the newspaper VG; its albums chart began in week 1 of 1967 as a top 20, became a top 40 in 1995 and a top 100 in 2025. {cite:wikipedia-vg-lista-en} {cite:wikidata-vg-lista}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources' chart tables give it, and never ranks works by taste: Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] peaked at number 17 there in 2006. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en}
