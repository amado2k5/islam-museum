# Order of the Republic (Egypt)

## Summary

An Egyptian state order of the Republic, conferred in classes; its holders in the museum include the munshid Sayed al-Naqshabandi, posthumously (1989). {cite:wikipedia-order-of-the-republic-egypt-en} {cite:wikidata-order-of-the-republic-egypt}

## In the museum

Its holders in the museum: [[person/sayed-al-naqshabandi|Sayed al-Naqshabandi]] (1989) {cite:wikipedia-sayed-al-naqshabandi-ar}.
