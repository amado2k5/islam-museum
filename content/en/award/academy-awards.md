## Summary
The Academy Awards, or Oscars, are the annual awards of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences in the United States for excellence in the film industry; the first ceremony was held on 16 May 1929. {cite:wikipedia-academy-awards-en} The 98th ceremony took place on 15 March 2026. {cite:wikipedia-academy-awards-en}

## In the museum
Nominations and wins are recorded on the honoured work's or person's own record with their source and gathered on this page. Among them is the nomination of Maurice Jarre's score for [[screen/al-risala-1976|*The Message*]] for Best Original Score at the 50th Academy Awards in 1978. {cite:wikipedia-the-message-1976-en}
