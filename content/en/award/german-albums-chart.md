## Summary
The GfK Entertainment charts are Germany's official music charts, compiled by GfK Entertainment, a GfK subsidiary, on behalf of the Bundesverband Musikindustrie; they include the weekly Top 100 albums chart. {cite:wikipedia-gfk-entertainment-charts-en} {cite:wikidata-gfk-entertainment-charts}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources give it, such as number 2 for [[person/yusuf-islam|Yusuf Islam]]'s [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] in 2006, which stayed in the German year-end charts of 2006 and 2007. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en}
