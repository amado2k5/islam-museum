## Summary
The Ultratop charts are Belgium's official record charts, published since 1995 by Ultratop, a non-profit body created by the Belgian Entertainment Association, the Belgian member of the IFPI, in two parallel sets: one for Dutch-speaking Flanders and one for French-speaking Wallonia. {cite:wikipedia-ultratop-en} {cite:wikidata-ultratop}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources' chart tables give it, and never ranks works by taste: Youssou N'Dour's [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] peaked at 57 in Flanders and 70 in Wallonia in 2004, Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] at 99 in Flanders in 2006, and Maher Zain's [[work/thank-you-allah-maher-zain|Thank You Allah]] at 117 in Flanders in 2023. {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en} {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en} {cite:wikipedia-thank-you-allah-en}
