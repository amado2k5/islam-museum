# Nishan-e-Imtiaz

## Summary

Pakistan's highest civil decoration ('Order of Excellence'), given for achievement that brings Pakistan world recognition or for outstanding service to the country; it is not limited to citizens. {cite:wikipedia-nishan-e-imtiaz-en} Wikidata gives 1957 as its inception. {cite:wikidata-nishan-e-imtiaz}
