## Summary
A worldwide competition for writing the Prophet's biography, announced by the Muslim World League at one of its conferences in Pakistan in 1396 AH; writers from many countries took part. {cite:wikipedia-raheeq-ar} {cite:wikipedia-raheeq-en}

## How it ran
171 studies were submitted; the results were announced in Shaban 1398 AH, and the prizes were handed over at the League's conference in Mecca in 1399 AH (1979), the secretariat undertaking to print the winning studies and publish them in several languages. {cite:wikipedia-raheeq-ar} The English article gives the first prize as 50,000 riyals. {cite:wikipedia-raheeq-en}

## Winners in the museum
First place went to [[work/ar-rahiq-al-makhtum|*al-Raheeq al-Makhtum*]] by Safiur Rahman Mubarakpuri. {cite:wikipedia-raheeq-ar}
