## Summary
The Dutch Album Top 100 is a weekly list, compiled by Dutch Charts, of the hundred best-selling albums in the Netherlands; it has changed names several times and grown from a top 10 to a top 100. {cite:wikipedia-dutch-album-top-100-en} {cite:wikidata-dutch-album-top-100}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources' chart tables give it, and never ranks works by taste: Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] peaked at 69 in 2006 and Youssou N'Dour's [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] at 100 in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en} {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}
