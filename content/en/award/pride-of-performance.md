# Pride of Performance

## Summary

The Pride of Performance (Tamgha-e-Husn-e-Karkardagi), officially the Presidential Pride of Performance, is an award of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan for 'notable achievements in the field of art, science, literature, sports, and nursing'. It is counted among the country's most prestigious awards in those fields and is given to citizens and, in some cases, foreign nationals. {cite:wikipedia-pride-of-performance-en}

Wikidata gives 1958 as its inception. {cite:wikidata-pride-of-performance}

## In the museum

Its holders in the museum include the naat reciter [[person/khursheed-ahmad|Khursheed Ahmad]], who received it in 1996. {cite:wikipedia-khursheed-ahmad-en}
