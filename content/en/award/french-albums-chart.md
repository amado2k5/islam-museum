## Summary
The French Albums Chart is France's official weekly chart of top-selling albums, compiled by SNEP, the French record industry's organisation, founded in 1922, which also certifies silver, gold, platinum and diamond records. {cite:wikipedia-snep-en} {cite:wikidata-snep}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources give it: number 34 for Youssou N'Dour's [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] in 2004, and number 81 for Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] in 2006. {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en} {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en}
