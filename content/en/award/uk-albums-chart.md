## Summary
The UK Albums Chart is the weekly chart of the best-selling albums in the United Kingdom, published by the Official Charts Company and cited in the chart tables of the articles the museum reads. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en} {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak position on this chart as its sources give it, and never ranks works by taste. Examples: number 20 for Yusuf's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] in 2006, and number 148 for Youssou N'Dour's [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en} {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}
