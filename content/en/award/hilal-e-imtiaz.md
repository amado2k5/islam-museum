# Hilal-e-Imtiaz

## Summary

The second-highest civil award in Pakistan's 'Imtiaz' order ('Crescent of Excellence'), given by the government to civilians and officers of the armed forces. {cite:wikipedia-hilal-e-imtiaz-en} Wikidata gives 1957 as its inception. {cite:wikidata-hilal-e-imtiaz}
