# Fukuoka Prize

## Summary

An award of the city of Fukuoka and the Fukuoka City International Foundation honouring outstanding work in preserving and creating Asian cultures, with an arts-and-culture category. {cite:wikipedia-fukuoka-prize-en} Wikidata gives 1989 as its inception. {cite:wikidata-fukuoka-prize}

## In the museum

Its holders in the museum: [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]] (1996) {cite:wikipedia-nusrat-fatih-ali-khan-en}.
