## Summary
The Swiss Hitparade is Switzerland's main set of sales charts, among them the Albums Top 100, published since late 1983. {cite:wikipedia-swiss-hitparade-en} {cite:wikidata-swiss-hitparade}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak as its sources give it: number 14 for Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] in 2006, and number 81 for Youssou N'Dour's [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en} {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}
