## Summary
The Official Finnish Charts are Finland's national record charts, compiled and published by Musiikkituottajat – IFPI Finland; Wikidata dates their start to 1991. {cite:wikipedia-official-finnish-charts-en} {cite:wikidata-official-finnish-charts}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources' chart tables give it, and never ranks works by taste: Youssou N'Dour's [[work/egypt-youssou-ndour|Egypt]] peaked at number 35 there in 2004. {cite:wikipedia-egypt-youssou-ndour-en}
