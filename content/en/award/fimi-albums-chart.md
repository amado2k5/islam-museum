## Summary
The Italian Albums Chart is compiled by FIMI, the federation founded in 1992 when the major labels left the earlier association; in September 1994 it announced an albums chart to replace charts it considered unreliable, and issued the first on 7 March 1995. {cite:wikipedia-fimi-en} {cite:wikidata-fimi}

## In the museum
The museum records an album's peak on this chart as its sources' chart tables give it, and never ranks works by taste: Yusuf Islam's [[work/an-other-cup-yusuf|An Other Cup]] peaked at number 22 there in 2006. {cite:wikipedia-an-other-cup-en}
