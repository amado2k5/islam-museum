## Summary
The Forbes Middle East lists are annual rankings the magazine publishes of the region's companies and banks, among them the 30 Most Valuable Banks and the Top 100 Listed Companies. {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025} The museum records them as rankings as the magazine published them, not as judgements of anyone's worth.

## In the museum
Among those listed is [[listing/kfh|Kuwait Finance House]], fifth among banks and ninth among listed companies in 2025. {cite:forbes-middle-east-lists-2025}
