## Summary
The Anugerah Industri Muzik (Malaysian Music Industry Awards) is the main award of Malaysia's recording industry, organised every year by the Malaysian Music Industry Academy association with the support of the Recording Industry Association of Malaysia and recognised by the Malaysian government; it began in 1993, with its first ceremony on 15 December 1993. {cite:wikipedia-anugerah-industri-muzik-ms} {cite:wikidata-anugerah-industri-muzik}

## The award
Singers, composers, lyricists, musicians and album-cover and video designers from Malaysia and Singapore compete for it, judged by respected members of the industry. {cite:wikipedia-anugerah-industri-muzik-ms} Ernst & Young audited the results from the start; after a controversy over the 2011 winners, KPMG took over. {cite:wikipedia-anugerah-industri-muzik-ms}

## The nasyid categories
Its yearly categories include Best Nasyid Song, and it had a Best Nasyid Album category, discontinued with the other album categories after the 17th edition because too few albums were being released. It also has the category of best Malay song performed by a foreign artist, in which [[person/maher-zain|Maher Zain]] was nominated in 2011 for the Malay version of 'Insha Allah' from [[work/thank-you-allah-maher-zain|Thank You Allah]], and the Kembara award for Malaysian artists' achievements abroad. {cite:wikipedia-anugerah-industri-muzik-ms} {cite:wikipedia-thank-you-allah-en}
