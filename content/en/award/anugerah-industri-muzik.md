## Summary
Anugerah Industri Muzik is the annual awards ceremony of Malaysia's music industry. The museum so far knows it from its 18th edition in 2011, which nominated [[person/maher-zain|Maher Zain]] in the category of best Malay song performed by a foreign artist for the Malay version of 'Insha Allah' from the album [[work/thank-you-allah-maher-zain|Thank You Allah]]; the category went in November 2011 to 'Ku Menunggu' by the Indonesian singer Rossa. {cite:wikipedia-thank-you-allah-en} {cite:wikipedia-thank-you-allah-ar}
