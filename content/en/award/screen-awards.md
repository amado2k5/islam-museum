## Summary
The Screen Awards were an annual Indian film-award ceremony launched by Screen magazine of the Indian Express Group in 1995, on the initiative of the group's chairman Viveck Goenka, with winners chosen by film-industry insiders rather than juries. {cite:wikipedia-screen-awards-en} {cite:wikidata-screen-awards} Star India acquired and closed the magazine in 2015 and kept sponsoring the event, whose last ceremony was on 8 December 2019. {cite:wikipedia-screen-awards-en}
