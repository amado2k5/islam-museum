## Summary
The World Summit Award is an international award for digital content; its website gives each winner a page with the year, the award, the category, the country and the producers' names. {cite:wsa-winner-nusuk-2024}

## In the museum
In 2024 it was won by the Saudi Hajj and Umrah platform [[listing/nusuk|Nusuk]] in the Culture & Heritage category; the award's page describes how the platform brings together crowd management, real-time navigation, health services, multilingual support, transport and a business marketplace. {cite:wsa-winner-nusuk-2024}
