## Brief
Energy moves through an ecosystem in one direction: sun to producer to consumer to decomposer. Food webs map those paths; break a link and the effects ripple.

## Producers, consumers, decomposers
Plants capture sunlight; herbivores eat plants; carnivores eat herbivores; decomposers return the remains to the soil. Remove one group and every other group feels it.
