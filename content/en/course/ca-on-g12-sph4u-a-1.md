## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the contributions of scientists, among them Canadians, to the fields you are studying. The museum offers two people of the Islamic world, an engineer and a scholar of optics, described in single reference works; the Canadian scientists and the contributions closest to your own course are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What counts as a contribution?
A contribution can be a book, a device, a method or a question. For each person you meet, ask what they added, who says so, and what the record does not claim for them.

## Try this
Read about [[person/al-jazari-engineer|al-Jazari]] and [[person/ibn-al-haytham|Ibn al-Haytham]]. For each, write one sentence on what they contributed and one on something the record takes care not to claim for them; then add one scientist from your class's list.
