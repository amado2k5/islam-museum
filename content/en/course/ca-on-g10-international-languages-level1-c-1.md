## Brief
This lesson asks you to use texts you read to identify communities where Arabic is spoken and to find out about aspects of their cultures. The museum offers one festival and one art of writing. The festivals of other communities, television programmes, songs and comic strips you read, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What does the text tell you about a tradition?
Ask of the text: which tradition does it describe, when does it recur, and what does it resemble in your community's traditions?

## Try this
Read the records on [[practice/eid-al-fitr|Eid al-Fitr]] and on [[concept/islamic-calligraphy|Islamic calligraphy]]. Report to the class what you found about one tradition, then look for an example of Arabic writing and name what you see in it.
