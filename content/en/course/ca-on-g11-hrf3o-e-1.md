## Brief
This lesson asks you to compare sacred writings and oral teachings, to describe why they matter, and to give specific examples. The curriculum itself names the Qur'an among its examples. The museum offers the Qur'an and the hadith; your class adds the sacred writings and oral teachings of the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## How does a faith keep what it teaches?
Ask of each source how it reaches a reader: written down or passed from person to person. Compare what each way keeps well and what it risks losing, using only what the sources report.

## Try this
Read [[work/quran|the Qur'an]] and [[concept/al-hadith-al-nabawi|the hadith]]. Write a two-row table: how each reaches a reader, and one detail the source gives.
