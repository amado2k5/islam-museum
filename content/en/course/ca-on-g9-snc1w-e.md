## Brief
Strand E of the Grade 9 Science course: Earth and Space Science. It has three lessons: the fixed stars and the sun, moon and planets compared, distances in the sky measured in Earth radii, and the causes of eclipses. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
