## Brief
This lesson asks you to use a research process to investigate a disease or abnormality of the tissues, organs or systems of humans or plants. The museum offers one medieval physician's comparison of two diseases, as a document in the history of medicine; the research on a disease of your own choice is for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What makes a good comparison of two diseases?
Look at the expectation: it asks for a research process. Ask what you would list first, what you would set side by side, and what you would call a difference rather than a resemblance.

## Try this
Read the record of [[invention/al-razi-smallpox-measles|Al-Razi's clinical comparison of smallpox and measles]]. Make a two-column table of what the record says the two diseases share and where it says they differ, and add a third column for the sources you would still need.
