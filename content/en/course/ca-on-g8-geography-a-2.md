## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe various ways in which human settlement has affected the environment. The museum links one historical case of people working with water and farmland; the effects of settlement on air, wildlife and climate are for your class to bring from other sources. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What changes when people bring water to land?
Look at what the expectation asks: ways settlement has affected the environment. For the case, note what changed (the land, the water, the plants), who changed it, and what the people hoped to gain.

## Try this
Read the record [[place/al-nil|al-Nil]]. Make a two-column chart: in one, what people did to land and water; in the other, what you think changed as a result, marking what the record does not say.
