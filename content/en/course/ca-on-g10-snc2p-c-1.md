## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse how chemical reactions are used in a product or process, and to identify their use in a particular profession, with its hazards. The museum offers one craft, the making of lustre pottery, as one author describes it; other products and professions, and the hazards of handling and disposing of chemicals, are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What is changed, and by what, in a potter's workshop?
The expectations ask what the reaction does in making the product. List the materials the record names, the order of the steps and what the vessel looks like at the end.
