## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how an environmental challenge has led to an advance in science or technology. The museum offers one example, a bag made from jute offered as an alternative to polythene bags; your class adds the other examples the curriculum names and its own. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What was the problem, and what answered it?
Trace three steps: the problem named, the new material or method, and what the record says it does. Notice what the record does not say about how well it worked.

## Try this
Read the record on [[person/mubarak-ahmad-khan|Mubarak Ahmad Khan]]. In three lines, write the problem, the invention and what the record says about it.
