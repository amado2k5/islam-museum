## Brief
This lesson asks you to select suitable instruments and materials for each inquiry and to identify the methods and procedures. The museum has only examples from the history of instruments in the Islamic world; the instruments of your own laboratory are for the class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What makes an instrument suitable?
Look at what your question needs: what do you want to measure or separate? Then compare what each instrument offers with what the question requires, and record why you set the others aside.

## How do parts make one procedure?
Notice that a piece of apparatus is built of parts, each with its own job. Write down what each part of your own set-up does in the whole procedure.

## Try this
Read the records on the [[concept/anbik|anbik]] and the [[invention/balance-of-wisdom|balance of wisdom]]. For each instrument, name the question it was made to answer, and propose a question from your own laboratory that needs a similar instrument.
