## Brief
This lesson asks you to use the terminology of hydraulic and pneumatic systems, and density is the first term on its list. The museum offers two instruments from medieval Islamic science that compare the weight and volume of a sample with water; pressure, flow and the other terms are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What is being compared with what?
For each instrument, say what is weighed, what it is compared with, and what could make the result wrong. Where does the word density fit?

## Try this
Read about [[invention/biruni-displacement-vessel|al-Biruni's displacement vessel]] and [[invention/balance-of-wisdom|al-Khazini's balance of wisdom]]. For each, write a sentence that uses the word density correctly, and one on what the instrument cannot tell you.
