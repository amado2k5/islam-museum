## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe ways in which drama and theatre contribute, or have contributed, to social, cultural and economic life in different times and places. The museum links a record of one nineteenth-century dramatist; your class will add other times and places. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## Who gains from a play?
Look at the expectation: it asks about contributions to social, cultural and economic life. Choose a play or a theatre tradition you know and ask who gains from it, and in what way.

## Try this
Read [[person/mirza-fath-ali-akhundzada|Mirza Fath Ali Akhund-zada]]. Write two sentences: what his plays were about, and who first performed them in their original language.
