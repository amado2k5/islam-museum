## Brief
This lesson asks you to build and take apart structures, and to name the flat shapes and solid objects you find in them. The museum offers one record on a ceiling made of cells; sorting and naming cubes, prisms, pyramids, cylinders and cones is the class's own work. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What shapes can you find in a structure?
Build a small structure from blocks, then take it apart. List the flat shapes and the solid objects you used. Did any piece count twice?

## Try this
Read the record on al-Kashi and the muqarnas. Look at a picture of a muqarnas ceiling and list the different shapes you can name.
