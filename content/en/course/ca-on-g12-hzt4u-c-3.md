## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe forces that shaped the development of metaphysical thought over time, and to analyse their impact. The curriculum's examples come from neuroscience and computers. The museum offers one historical force, the Abbasid movement to render the books of the ancients into Arabic, as Ibn al-Nadim tells it; it does not analyse its impact. Other forces are for your class to add. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What moves thought across time?
Look at the expectation: it asks for forces and then an analysis of their impact. Separate what a source says about the cause of an event from what you conclude about its effect.

## Try this
Read the record of [[concept/harakat-al-tarjama|the translation movement]]. Write down two ways Ibn al-Nadim says the ancients' books arrived, then a question you would put to a second source.
