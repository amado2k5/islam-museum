## Brief
Current electricity is charge on the move through a closed loop. Cells push it; wires carry it; switches decide whether it flows at all.

## Series and parallel
In series, one break stops everything; in parallel, each branch keeps its own path. Drawing circuits with standard symbols lets anyone rebuild your design {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}.

## Try this
Power two bulbs first in series, then in parallel. Record the brightness difference and explain it.
