## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe current electricity and compare it with static electricity, to identify good conductors and good insulators, to explain what the parts of a simple circuit do, and to tell series circuits from parallel circuits and name common uses of each. The museum holds no record that serves these expectations yet, so this lesson offers no cards. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What will you compare, and on what grounds?
For each pair you meet (current and static electricity, conductor and insulator, series and parallel), write one thing that is the same, one thing that differs, and a test you could do to tell the two apart.
