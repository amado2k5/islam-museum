## Brief
Strand E of the Grade 10 applied Science course SNC2P: Physics. It has three lessons, on reflection in a circular mirror, on a dark room with a small hole, and on light bending in a water sphere; the other expectations of the strand are in the gap register. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
