## Brief
The Grade 10 Visual Arts course AVI2O of the Ontario curriculum, with three strands. The museum maps all three to Islamic calligraphy, tilework, book illumination, the arabesque and three works read for their use and society; the practical work of making art is left to your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}
