## Brief
The Ontario Grade 11 course SVN3M, Environmental Science, of the Science curriculum for the secondary grades. The museum maps a small part of five of its six strands and leaves the experiments, the data and the rest to the class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
