## Brief
This lesson asks you to read and build whole numbers by their places, and to read, compare and order decimal numbers up to hundredths. The museum offers two records: how nine numerals and a zero give each place its value, and an early treatment of tenths and hundredths. Large whole numbers up to 100 000, comparing and ordering decimals, and everyday uses of numbers are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How does a digit's place change its value?
Write a number with the same digit in several places and say what each one is worth. Then break the number apart by its places and put it back together.

## What happens to place value below one?
Look at how each place to the right is worth less than the one before it. Compare two decimal numbers place by place, starting from the largest place.
