## Brief
This lesson asks you to research an algebraic concept, tell the story of its development and use in a specific culture, and describe its relevance today. The museum offers the story of algebra in al-Khwarizmi's book as the Cairo editors tell it; other cultures and today's uses are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-mathematics}

## What did algebra inherit, and what did the book add?
Gather from the page what the editors say about algebra before al-Khwarizmi, then what they say about him. Place both on a timeline and ask where the word al-jabr falls on it.

## Try this
Read [[concept/algebra|Algebra (al-jabr)]]. Draw a timeline of five stops in algebra before and after al-Khwarizmi as the record names them, and write your research question beneath it.
