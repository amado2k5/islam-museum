## Brief
This lesson asks you to classify the climate of your local region with tools or systems, and to compare it with other regions of Ontario, Canada and the world. The museum offers one old system, the seven climates of the astronomers, as al-Masudi reports it; it holds no record on Ontario's ecoregions or on today's climate classification, so the classification of your own region is for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What does a system of classification decide before you start?
Look at the expectation: it says tools or systems, in the plural. Ask what the old system sorts the earth by, and what a modern system sorts it by.

## Try this
Read the record of [[concept/al-aqalim-al-saba|The seven climates]]. Write down in one line what the climates are counted from, and in another line what your class uses to classify your own region.
