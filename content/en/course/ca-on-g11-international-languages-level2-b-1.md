## Brief
This lesson asks you to speak in the target language about a community and what it has given the world. The museum offers one scholar, al-Khwarizmi, and the city where he worked. Other contributions, and the speaking itself, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What will you ask the scholar?
Plan an interview: decide what you ask about his city, his patron, his books and how his work travelled. Mark which answers the museum's record gives and which need another source.

## Go deeper
Before he built [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]], al-Maqdisi relates, al-Mansur asked about the site's winter and summer, rains and air, and had men sleep there through the seasons {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p. 107}. He reports that al-Mansur summoned men learned in law and geometry, among them [[person/abu-hanifa|Abu Hanifa]], had the city laid out and its foundations dug in 145 AH, and that it was finished in 149 AH {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p. 108}. The Encyclopaedia of Islam says he chose Baghdad for military, economic and climatic reasons: a fertile plain on both sides of the river, the Khurasan road, a meeting place of caravan routes with monthly fairs, a net of canals that could serve as ramparts, a central position in Mesopotamia and a temperate climate {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 954}.

## How do we know?
What is known of [[person/al-khwarizmi|al-Khwarizmi]] goes back, the Cairo editors say, to Ibn al-Nadim's Fihrist {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.22}, and the Encyclopaedia of Islam says very few other biographical details are known {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 4 p. 1111}. For the city, al-Maqdisi wrote in a book he completed in 375 AH, and noted that Madinat al-Salam itself lay ruined in his day, its mosque still used on Fridays {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p. 12} {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p. 106} {cite:ahsan-al-taqasim p. 107}. The biography of al-Khwarizmi reports that an Indian astronomical text reached the court in Baghdad around 770 CE {cite:khwarizmi-mactutor Astronomy paragraph}. Ask of each source who wrote it and how near the writer was to what he reports.

## Think about it
What we know of al-Khwarizmi comes from the Fihrist and an encyclopaedia article, and what we know of the choice of Baghdad's site comes from a geographer's book. If you presented the scholar and his city in an interview, which source would you mention first, and why?

## Into the museum
- [[place/baghdad|Baghdad]]: the founding by al-Mansur, the Round City and the reasons for the site, as the Encyclopaedia of Islam gives them.
- [[person/al-khwarizmi|Al-Khwarizmi]]: what Ibn al-Nadim and the Encyclopaedia of Islam say of his life and books.
- [[building/bayt-al-hikma|The House of Wisdom]]: the library in Baghdad, with Ahmad Amin's account of al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun.
- [[invention/round-city-baghdad|The Round City of Baghdad]]: its plan, with palace and mosque at the centre and four roads to four gates.
