## Brief
This lesson asks you to ask good questions about the traditions and celebrations of your own family and community, and to describe some important ones. The curriculum also names First Nations, Métis and Inuit celebrations; the museum links only a celebration kept by Muslim families, so your class adds the others. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What do you want to find out about a celebration?
The expectations ask for questions about how a celebration was kept in the past and how it is kept now. Think of who, what, where, when and why, and decide who could answer each question.

## How would you describe a celebration to a friend?
Say what people do, when it happens and who takes part. Then say what your family does differently from a classmate's family.

## Try this
Read [[practice/eid-al-fitr|Eid al-Fitr]]. Write three questions you could ask a family about a celebration that comes every year, and ask one of them at home.
