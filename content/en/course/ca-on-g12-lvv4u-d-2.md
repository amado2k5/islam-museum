## Brief
This lesson asks you to understand what scientific inquiry was in the classical world and why it mattered. The museum offers three names from Arabic sources, Euclid, Archimedes and Ptolemy, with what those sources say about their books. The other thinkers named in the expectation, and the content of their work, are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What did they study, and what survives?
For each author, list the books the source names and the field each belongs to, then ask which survive and how we know. Compare what the source says about the author with what you can say about the books.
