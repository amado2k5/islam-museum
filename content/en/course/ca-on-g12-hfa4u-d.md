## Brief
Strand D of the Nutrition and Health course: food security, food production and supply, and the environment. The museum has three lessons here, on land and water, on withholding food from the market and on genetically modified animal foods; strand D3 has none. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}
