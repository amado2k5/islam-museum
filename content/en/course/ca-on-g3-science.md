## Brief
Ontario's Grade 3 Science and Technology course has five strands: STEM Skills and Connections, Life Systems, Matter and Energy, Structures and Mechanisms, and Earth and Space Systems. The museum links parts of four strands to real records. What it found no honest record for is entered in the gap register instead of being given a stretched link. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}
