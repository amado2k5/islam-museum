## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe some of the key influences of music within contemporary culture. The museum offers two examples: a qawwali written for a Hindi film, and a qawwal whose music reached audiences far from where his art began. Music in advertising, the tastes of young people and other influences your class must bring from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## Where do you meet music outside a concert?
List the places where you heard music this week: a film, a shop, a bus, a home. For each, ask what the music was doing there.

## How does music travel from one place to another?
Follow a route in each of the two examples: from a scene to a song, from one country to another audience. Compare what changed and what stayed.

## Go deeper
In the film Rockstar the hero, Jordan, can express himself only through music, and his music takes in the places he passes through. {cite:wikipedia-rockstar-soundtrack-en} When he is thrown out of his house, he goes to the Nizamuddin Dargah in Delhi, spends time with the qawwals and plays with them. {cite:wikipedia-rockstar-soundtrack-en} Out of that journey into music and life, the article says, the qawwali [[work/kun-faya-kun-rockstar|'Kun Faya Kun']] was composed. {cite:wikipedia-rockstar-soundtrack-en}

## Think about it
The song was written for one scene of a film. What would a song need so that someone who has never seen the film still feels it belongs to that scene, and why?
