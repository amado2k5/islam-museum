## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify key philosophers of the classical world and to describe how they influenced their societies. The museum offers three of them as an Arabic bibliographer presents them: Socrates, Plato and Aristotle. Their theories, the schools of Greece and Rome, and the other thinkers named in the expectation are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## Who taught whom?
Follow the line from teacher to pupil, and notice what each pupil kept, left behind or took from someone else. Ask how the society around the thinker treated him, and what the source says about it.
