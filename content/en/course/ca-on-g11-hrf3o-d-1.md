## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain why major festivals matter, to describe what people do on them, and to describe the role of fasts and feasts. The museum offers the Islamic month of fasting and its two festivals; your class adds the festivals of the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What do a fast and a feast do for each other?
Read the month and the festival side by side. Ask what the community does before the festival, what it does on the day, and how the day looks back at what came before it.

## Try this
Read [[practice/sawm-ramadan|Fasting in Ramadan]], [[practice/eid-al-fitr|Eid al-Fitr]] and [[practice/eid-al-adha|Eid al-Adha]]. Make a calendar strip with the month and the two days, and under each write the practices the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia names.
