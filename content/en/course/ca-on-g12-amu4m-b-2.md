## Brief
This lesson asks you to gather information from reliable sources on a musician outside the Western tradition, analyse and critique it, and analyse the impact of notable performers on a genre. The museum offers the life of Ziryab, with what separates older reports from the later idealized portrait, and the life of Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan and his carrying of qawwali to a wider audience. Canadian and Western musicians come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## How do I know the story is sound?
In the record of Ziryab, tell who is speaking: a contemporary witness, a later writer or a modern scholar. Then ask what remains after that distinction.

## What did this performer change in the genre?
For each musician, name the genre they belong to and the audience they reached, then separate what the record says of their impact from your own opinion.

## Try this
Read the records [[person/ziryab|Ziryab]] and [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]]. Choose one report from each record and write how you would check it against another source.
