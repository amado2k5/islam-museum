## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify significant land-use issues and to describe how various groups responded to them. The museum links two medieval cases, one about conquered farmland and one about the space of a city's markets; land-use issues of today, and of Canada, are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## Who has a claim on land, and who decides?
Look in each case for the competing claims: who wanted the land used one way, who wanted another, and what was decided. Then compare how each group responded.

## Go deeper
In al-Baladhuri's account, when the Sawad was conquered the people asked [[person/saad-ibn-abi-waqqas|Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas]] to divide it, and Sa'd wrote to Umar. Umar ordered him to divide the goods and the horses after the fifth, and to leave the land and the rivers to their workers {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.370}. He said that if he divided it among those who were present, nothing would remain for those who came after them {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p.370}. In the report of Ibrahim al-Taymi, the troops told him they had taken it by force with their swords; he refused and said: "What then is for those of the Muslims who come after you?" {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} He ordered a count, and the share of a man came to three peasants; [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] said: "Leave them as a supply for the Muslims." {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407} Later, al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik wanted to make the people of the Sawad spoils, but the account of what Umar had done was mentioned to him {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}.

## Often misunderstood
Moving the markets to Karkh is easy to hear as inventing markets or planning a city by decree. Duri says four other market places already stood outside Baghdad's walls, so the transfer did not create its first outside markets {cite:tdv-bagdat-duri Markets}. His account gives security as the stated reason {cite:tdv-bagdat-duri Markets}. These reports document a reallocation of commercial space, not the invention of markets, a measured security outcome, or proof that every seller moved together {cite:tdv-bagdat-duri Markets} {cite:lestrange-baghdad-1900 p.25–26}. They do not measure access, safety or the distribution of benefits among residents {cite:tdv-bagdat-duri Markets} {cite:lestrange-baghdad-1900 p.65–66}.

## Think about it
Umar's reason, as al-Baladhuri gives it, looks forward to those who come after, while the troops' claim looks back to who took the land. Which reason is the stronger basis for deciding who may use land, and what would you need to know about the people who already worked it before you decided?
