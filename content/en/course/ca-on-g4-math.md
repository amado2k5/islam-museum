## Brief
The Grade 4 Mathematics course of the Ontario curriculum. The museum offers lessons on number, patterns and equations, area, and money, each linked to records of Islamic civilisation, and lists in a gap register what it cannot honestly support. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}
