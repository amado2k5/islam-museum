## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe repeating and growing patterns, including patterns from real life. The museum offers one record on repeating geometric patterns in Islamic art; growing patterns, and the patterns of your own surroundings, are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What is the part that repeats?
Find the smallest group of shapes that, repeated again and again, makes the whole pattern. Say how many shapes it holds, and what changes from one repeat to the next, if anything.

## Try this
Look at the record [[concept/girih|Girih]]. Draw one repeating unit of a pattern you like and describe it in a sentence.
