## Brief
Strand A of Grade 8 Geography: Global Settlement: Patterns and Sustainability. The museum teaches five of its sixteen specific expectations, in four lessons: where cities are placed, how people changed the land, who uses the land, and a dam that failed. The rest are in the gap register. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}
