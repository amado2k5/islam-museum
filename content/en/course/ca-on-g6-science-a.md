## Brief
Strand A of the Grade 6 Science and Technology course: STEM Skills and Connections, which covers the skills of investigating, coding and communicating and the connections between science, occupations and communities. The museum maps only expectation A3, in one lesson on how science and technology are used in occupations and what various communities have contributed. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}
