## Brief
The Ontario Grade 11 course Philosophy: The Big Questions asks students to study the big questions of philosophy, how philosophers answer them, and how to reason about them. The museum maps eight of its expectations, in strands B, C and D, to records on thinkers and debates of the Islamic world. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}
