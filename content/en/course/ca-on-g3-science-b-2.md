## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how people from different cultures, including Indigenous peoples, use plants for food, shelter, medicine and clothing. The museum offers only one piece: farming as a source of food. Medicine, shelter, clothing and the plant uses of Indigenous peoples your class must bring from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Which of our foods were once plants?
Farming is a way of living from plants. Think of the foods you eat in a day and trace each one back to the plant it came from.

## Go deeper
In the record, al-Maraghi presents [[concept/agriculture-in-islam|farming]] as the oldest way of making a living. He follows Ibn Khaldun, who calls it the simplest of crafts and the closest to nature {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 27} {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 28}. Al-Maraghi says Islam urges farming as the source of food for people and animals, and warns against working land taken by force {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 28}. He adds that land left idle for three years may be farmed by another person, if the ruler gives leave {cite:min-qadaya-al-mal-wal-amal-azhar p. 29}.

## Think about it
Al-Maraghi calls farming the source of food for people and animals. If a town's farmers stopped for one year, who would notice first, and why?
