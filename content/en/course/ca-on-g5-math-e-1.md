## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify properties of triangles and build different kinds, and to use area relationships to develop the area formula for a triangle. The museum offers one chapter that names three kinds of triangle and gives the area of a triangle with a worked example. Building triangles from measurements, parallelograms and your own development of the formulas are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How do the three kinds of triangle differ?
Draw a triangle of each kind and look at its angles. Say what you see that sets each kind apart.

## Why half of the base times the height?
Cut or fold a rectangle or parallelogram to make a triangle. Explain how the two areas are related.

## Try this
Read about [[concept/ilm-al-misaha|mensuration in al-Khwarizmi's book]]. Draw a triangle, measure its base and height, and use the chapter's rule to find its area.
