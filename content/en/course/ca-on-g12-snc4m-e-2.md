## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe public health measures, including rules, used to protect the public. The museum offers two records on plague and one on a hospital and the confinement of lepers: an account of a caliph turning back from a stricken land, a saying on entering and leaving such a land, and a hospital whose founder had lepers confined. Vaccination, water treatment and today's laws are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Who decides, and who is bound?
For each record, name who made the decision and who had to follow it. Then ask which measures protect only the people who follow them and which protect others as well.

## Try this
Read [[passage/muwatta-31-2611|the account of Umar at Sargh]] and list, in order, each step that led to the decision to turn back, with the person who took it.
