## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse how the physical environment has influenced where people settle and how settlements are arranged, and to describe some of these influences in different countries and regions. The museum offers only one example, from Egypt, so your class will add examples from other regions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What decides where a city is placed?
The expectations ask how the physical environment influenced where people settled and how their homes were arranged. When you study a site, ask what made living there easier, what put people at risk there, and who made the decision.

## Try this
Read the museum's record of [[place/al-fustat|Al-Fustat]]. On a sketch map you draw yourself, mark the river and the city, and write two sentences on what the river gave the people and what it put at risk.
