## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse what you gather from research sources for logic, accuracy, reliability, adequacy and bias. The museum offers two examples from the history of chemistry that show how a report is weighed before it is relied on. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Who said this, and how did they know?
Ask of each report: who relays it? Does the source tell what it saw or what it heard? Notice when the source itself says the matter is disputed.

## What would it take to believe the claim?
Tell a claim passed on from its holders apart from what another source could confirm. Write down what a reader would need before accepting each claim.

## Try this
Read the records on [[person/jabir-ibn-hayyan|Jabir ibn Hayyan]] and [[concept/sinaat-al-kimiya|the alchemical art]]. Make a table of three columns: the claim, who relays it, and what would be needed to check it.
