## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe pathways and careers related to visual arts and the education they require. The museum offers two historical crafts, the architect and the calligrapher, taken from encyclopaedia entries; today's careers and their requirements come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What did each craft need to know?
From each record, list the knowledge and skills it names, and note where the record is silent. A record that names no training is an answer too.

## How would you train for a career like this today?
Pick one of the two and name a present-day career close to it. Set out the skills, the education and the training it asks, and compare them with the historical path.

## Try this
Read the records on [[job/architect|the architect]] and [[job/calligrapher|the calligrapher]]. Draw a path for one career, from the first lesson to the first independent work, using only what the record says.
