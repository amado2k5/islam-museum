## Brief
This lesson asks you to use what you read in the target language to find out about the communities where it is spoken and aspects of their cultures. The museum offers three things to read about: the language, the story told of how its script was devised, and an art of writing. The texts you read, and the communities beyond these, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What do a language and its writing tell you about its speakers?
Read how the records present the language, the report on its script and an art of writing. Note which statements are descriptions and which are a report from one narrator, and what you would need to confirm the second kind.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/arabiyya|the Arabic language]] and on [[event/arabic-script-origin|the devising of the script]]. Write down the places and people through which the report says the script travelled, then draw them as a map.
