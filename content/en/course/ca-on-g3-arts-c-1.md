## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe the ways music is used in a community, and to identify, by performing or listening, musical forms from different communities, times and places. The museum offers one case from the Islamic world: a festival of sacred music in Fez. The performing, the listening and your own community are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## Why is music used in a community?
The expectations ask you to describe ways music is used. Think of three questions to put to any community: who makes the music, who listens to it, and what it does for the people who gather around it.

## Try this
Read about [[event/fez-sacred-music-festival|the Fez Festival of World Sacred Music]]. Draw a chart with two columns, "what I read" and "our community", and fill the first from your reading and the second with what music is used for in your life.
