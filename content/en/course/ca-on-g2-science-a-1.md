## Brief
This lesson asks you to investigate how science and technology can work with other school subjects on real-world problems, and to analyse what various communities have contributed to science and technology. The museum links only examples from Muslim scholars; your class will add contributions from other communities. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## How can two subjects solve one problem?
Look at the first expectation. Name a real problem, the two subjects used on it, and what each one added that the other could not.

## Whose work are we looking at?
The second expectation asks you to analyse contributions from various communities. For each contribution, ask who made it, for whom, and what it made possible.
