## Brief
This lesson asks you to explore and experiment with materials, tools and techniques and to use them to make art works. The museum shows what Islamic artists used: mineral pigments and gold, the calligrapher's pen and ink, and inlaid metal. Your own experiments, and any newer technologies, come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What does each material let the artist do?
For each record, list the materials and tools it names and what they were used for. Then say what you would test first if you had the same materials in your own studio.

## What is traditional, and what is new?
The records describe traditional ways of working. Compare one of them with a tool or material you use today, and say what changed and what stayed the same.

## Try this
Read the records on [[practice/persian-painting-pigments-and-gold|pigments and gold in Persian painting]] and [[concept/inlaid-metalwork|inlaid metalwork]]. Choose one material or tool and write three sentences on how it was prepared or used, then mark the sentence that comes from the record.
