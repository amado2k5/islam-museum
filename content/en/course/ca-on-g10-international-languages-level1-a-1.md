## Brief
This lesson asks you to use what you hear to identify communities where Arabic is spoken and to find out about aspects of their cultures, linking them to your own experience. The museum offers a description of the language. ## What do you hear about a community?
Notice what the speaker says about the place and its people, then ask what resembles your own community and what differs from it.
