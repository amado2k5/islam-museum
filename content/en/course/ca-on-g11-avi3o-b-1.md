## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain the functions of different kinds of art works in past and present societies. The museum offers three cases from Islamic history where writing served a purpose, in sacred texts, on coins and on city walls; present-day cases come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Who is the work speaking to?
For each record, name the people who would have seen the work and the places where they met it. Say what the maker or patron wanted them to do or feel, and mark what is only your guess.

## What is an art work's job, and does it change?
Sort the three cases by function: sacred, official, commemorative or another. Then ask which of those jobs a work of art still does today.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/propaganda-coins|propaganda coins]] and [[concept/kufic-script|Kufic script]]. Choose one inscription or coin and write two sentences on who was meant to read it and what it was meant to do.
