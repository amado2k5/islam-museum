## Brief
This lesson asks you to investigate how light is refracted as it passes through different media and to describe, in qualitative terms, how visible light is refracted at the edge between two media. The museum offers a medieval scholar's use of a water-filled glass sphere to study the path of light in a raindrop; your class carries out the investigation with blocks, water and measured angles. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Where does light change direction?
The expectations ask where the direction changes, and at what kind of edge. On a diagram of the sphere, mark each place where light passes from one medium to another.

Read about [[invention/farisi-water-sphere-rainbow|al-Farisi's water-sphere rainbow model]]. Draw a circle for the sphere and trace one ray in, then mark each point where it enters, bounces or leaves.
