## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe careers related to the science you are studying, with the education and training they need, and to describe what scientists have contributed to it. The museum offers three people from agriculture, chemistry and law, none of them Canadian; your class adds other careers and the Canadian scientists the curriculum names. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## How did each person's training lead to the work?
For each person, ask what they studied, where they worked and what changed because of their work. Then ask which careers your class could add from its own region.

Read the records on [[person/ibn-bassal|Ibn Bassal]], [[person/mubarak-ahmad-khan|Mubarak Ahmad Khan]] and [[person/rizwana-hasan|Rizwana Hasan]]. For each, write one line on the career and one on the education the record mentions, using only what the records say.
