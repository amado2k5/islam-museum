## Brief
This lesson asks you to name services that a government or the community looks after, such as the market, and to say what people in the community must do for them. The museum offers one office that watched over markets and streets; your class adds garbage collection, snow clearing, and fire and police services. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## Who is responsible?
For one service, ask who runs it, who may use it and what it is for.

## What can people do to help?
Think about what people do so that a service works well, and what might go wrong if they did not.
