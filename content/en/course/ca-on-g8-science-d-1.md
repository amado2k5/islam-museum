## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the purpose, inputs and outputs of systems, and to identify the parts and processes that let a system do its job. The museum offers two designs for lifting water that are known from books; whether they were built is stated in each record. Safety, efficiency and food-processing systems are for the class to bring. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What goes in, what comes out, and what does each part do?
The expectations ask you to name a system's purpose, its input and its output, and then its parts. For each pump, write one sentence for the purpose, one for the input and one for the output, and list its parts in the order the power passes through them.
