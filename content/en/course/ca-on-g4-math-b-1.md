## Brief
This lesson asks you to read, build and take apart whole numbers up to 10 000, and to say where such numbers are used in everyday life. The museum offers one thing here, a record on how numbers are written by place value; your class brings the everyday uses and the practice. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What does a digit's place tell you?
Take the same digit in the ones place and in the thousands place and compare what each is worth. Then show how a number is built from the worth of its places, and taken apart again.

## Try this
Read the record [[invention/algorism|Algorism]]. Write a four-digit number, say what each digit is worth by its place, and take the number apart into thousands, hundreds, tens and ones.
