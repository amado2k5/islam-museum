## Brief
Strand B: Reflecting, Responding, and Analysing of the Grade 12 Visual Arts course AVI4E. It has two lessons linked to the museum's records: three historical works read for their use and society, and the calligrapher's craft as one example of a work opportunity. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}
