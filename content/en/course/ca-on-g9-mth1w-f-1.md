## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify a past or current financial situation and explain how it can inform financial decisions. The museum describes two instruments of the past and does not explain the decisions they guided; drawing out the decision and the related mathematics is your work with your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-mathematics}

## Who pays, who collects, and where and when?
Read the description and draw a simple diagram of the parties, places and amounts. Then ask what each party knows before deciding.
