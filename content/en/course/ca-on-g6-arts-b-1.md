## Brief
This lesson asks you to show an understanding of some drama and theatre themes and traditions from a variety of times, communities and places. The museum links one record, on a nineteenth-century dramatist of the Caucasus; your class will add other traditions, including those the curriculum names. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does a theatre tradition carry?
Look at the expectation: it asks for themes and traditions from different times and places. For one play or playwright, note who wrote it, in which language, for which audience, and what it argues for.

## Try this
Read [[person/mirza-fath-ali-akhundzada|Mirza Fath 'Ali Akhund-zada]] and make a card that says where and when he wrote, what his plays argued against, and who first performed them in the original language. Then make a second card for a tradition from another community.
