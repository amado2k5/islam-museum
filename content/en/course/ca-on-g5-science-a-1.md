## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how science and technology are used in different jobs and how that work meets real needs, and to analyse what different communities have contributed to science and technology. The museum offers a hospital in Egypt, a physician-scholar and a hospital in Baghdad built on an earlier model. Other jobs, skilled trades and other communities are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Who does what in a place where science is applied?
Pick a workplace and list the jobs in it. For each job, ask which problem the person solves and which science or technology they use. Compare your lists.

## How would you show who contributed to a place or an idea?
The expectation asks you to analyse contributions. Think about what evidence tells you who did what, and where an idea or a model came from before it reached the place you are studying.
