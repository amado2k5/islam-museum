## Brief
The Ontario Grade 9 Mathematics course MTH1W. Its four strands hold nine lessons that link number, algebra, measurement and money to Islamic civilisation where the museum has something to say; the other strands have no lessons because the records hold nothing honest for them. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-mathematics}
