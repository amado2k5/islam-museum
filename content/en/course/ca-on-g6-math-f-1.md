## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the advantages and disadvantages of ways of paying for goods and services, and to describe trading, lending, borrowing and donating as ways of distributing resources. The museum offers records on trade, on written payment orders and on endowments; lending and borrowing your class adds. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What does each way of paying ask you to trust?
For each method you can name, say what a seller must trust and what a buyer must trust. Then list one advantage and one disadvantage.

## Try this
Read the records on the sakk and the hawala, and on waqf. For each, say who gives, who receives, and what is handed over.
