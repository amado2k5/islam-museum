## Brief
The Grade 10 Science course SNC2D of the Ontario curriculum, with five strands. The museum maps four of them (A, B, D and E) to scholars, physicians, texts and ideas of the Islamic world, and leaves the chemistry strand and the laboratory work to your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
