## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how influences across a lifetime, religious ones among them, affect people's food choices. The museum shows one tradition's way of sorting foods into lawful, unlawful and doubtful; your class adds other traditions and other influences. This is a religious classification, not dietary guidance. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a rule leave as a choice, and what does it settle?
Look at the three groups the text names. Ask which foods are settled in advance, which are left open to dispute, and how a family might act on a doubtful case.

## Try this
Read [[concept/halal-haram|the lawful, the unlawful and the doubtful]]. List the foods and drinks it names in each group, then add one influence on food choice from your own family or community that is not religious.
