## Brief
This lesson asks you to read, represent, compare and order decimal numbers up to thousandths, and to show how fractions and decimals name the same amounts. The museum offers one early account of decimal fractions; the tools, the drawings and most of the practice your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How do you know two numbers are equal?
Look at the expectations: they ask for equivalences, shown with tools and drawings. Write a fraction you can split in half, write each half as a decimal, and compare the places you used. A hundred grid or a number line can show that your two names mark the same amount.

## Try this
Read the record on al-Uqlidisi's decimal-fraction methods. Write one half, then half of that, first as fractions and then as decimals, and shade each on a hundred grid.
