## Brief
This lesson asks you to classify structures as solid, frame or shell structures. The museum offers two arched and domed structures, the chahartaq and the keel-arched dome of al-Azhar. It does not classify them itself, so the classifying is yours, and your class must bring other structures. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What carries the weight?
To classify a structure, find what is solid all through, what is a frame of parts joined together, and what is a thin outer skin. Decide which describes each example and give your reason.

Read about [[concept/chahartaq|the chahartaq]] and [[concept/keel-arch|the keel arch]]. Sketch each and shade the parts you think carry the weight.
