## Brief
This lesson asks you to classify structures as solid, frame or shell structures. The museum offers two arched and domed structures, the chahartaq and the keel-arched dome of al-Azhar. It does not classify them itself, so the classifying is yours, and your class must bring other structures. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What carries the weight?
To classify a structure, find what is solid all through, what is a frame of parts joined together, and what is a thin outer skin. Decide which describes each example and give your reason.

## Go deeper
The record on [[concept/keel-arch|the keel arch]] reports that in the qibla-arcade dome of [[building/al-azhar|al-Azhar]], from the reign of al-Hafiz, the dome rests on keel arches over antique columns braced by wooden tie-beams {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.53}. Yeomans sees these features as defining Fatimid structure, much lighter than the solid brick piers of Tulunid building {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.53}. The [[concept/chahartaq|chahartaq]] is a square domed chamber rising on four crossing arches {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002 p.132}; Rezavi's earliest Mughal example is the Kabuli Bagh Mosque, built by Babur at Panipat (c. 1527), where the chahartaq covers the central nave {cite:rezavi-iranian-influence-indian-architecture-2002 p.134}. The records describe and do not classify; the classifying is yours.

## Think about it
Yeomans sees the keel arches, antique columns and wooden tie-beams of the al-Azhar dome as much lighter than solid brick piers {cite:yeomans-islamic-cairo-2006 p.53}. If you classified that dome as a solid, frame or shell structure, which part of Yeomans's description would you rely on, and why?

## Into the museum
- [[concept/chahartaq|The chahartaq]]: a square domed chamber on four crossing arches, and how it spread under the Mughals.
- [[concept/keel-arch|The keel arch]]: a pointed arch shaped like an upturned boat, from Fatimid Cairo onward.
- [[building/al-azhar|Al-Azhar Mosque]]: the mosque whose qibla-arcade dome the keel-arch record describes.
- [[building/kabuli-bagh-mosque|The Kabuli Bagh Mosque]]: Babur's mosque at Panipat, where the record says the chahartaq covers the central nave.
