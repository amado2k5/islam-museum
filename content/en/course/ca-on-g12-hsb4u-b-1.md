## Brief
This lesson asks you to outline and compare the key ideas of major theories that explain social change, and to describe the conditions such theories rely on. The curriculum's examples are modern schools of thought, such as functionalism, conflict theory and feminism. The museum offers only one thinker of the medieval Islamic world, Ibn Khaldun, so your class will add the other theories. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a theory of change need to explain?
Look at the two expectations: they ask for a theory's key ideas and for the conditions it names. For each theory you meet, ask what is changing, what it says makes it change, and what it says holds a society together.

## Try this
Read the records of [[person/ibn-khaldun|Ibn Khaldun]] and of [[concept/asabiyya|Asabiyya]]. Write two lines: what Ibn Khaldun says holds a group together, and what he set out to study in the Muqaddima. Then choose a modern theory from your class and write the same two lines.
