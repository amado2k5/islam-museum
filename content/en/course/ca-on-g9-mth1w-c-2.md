## Brief
This lesson asks you to simplify algebraic expressions using the properties of operations, and to create and solve equations and verify the solutions. The museum offers al-Khwarizmi's six types of equation and al-Karaji's way of multiplying composite expressions; your own equations and their contexts are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-mathematics}

## How do you know your answer is right?
Put the answer back into the original problem and compare both sides. Then ask: is testing one value enough to prove a general rule? Read how the record separates doing an operation, checking an example and justifying a rule.

Solve one of al-Khwarizmi's problems in your notebook and verify each answer, then expand a product of your own the way the record shows al-Karaji's method.
