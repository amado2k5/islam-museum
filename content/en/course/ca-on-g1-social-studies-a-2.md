## Brief
This lesson asks you to name kind and inclusive things that you and other people do every day, and to show that people and the environment should be treated with respect. The museum offers one card on neighbours and their rights; your class adds sharing, taking turns and caring for plants and water. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What does a good neighbour do?
List things a good neighbour does and things that would upset a neighbour. Ask who is helped when people keep to them.

## Who and what else deserves respect?
Neighbours are people, but shared places also need care. Ask what you could do to show respect to the classroom, the playground or a plant.

## Try this
Read the card on [[concept/jiwar|jiwar]]. Write two rules for a good neighbour in your class or on your street, and say who each rule helps.
