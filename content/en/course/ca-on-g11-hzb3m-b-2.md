## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe some major philosophical traditions and to explain how they approach the big questions. The museum shows how two Greek traditions, that of Plato and that of Aristotle, came to readers of Arabic, through the Neoplatonists and through Arabic interpreters. It does not set out how each tradition approaches the big questions, so your class will add the explanation and the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## How does a tradition travel?
Compare how the two records say the Greek tradition reached Arabic readers: through intermediaries and through commentators. Ask which of these could change what a tradition says.

Read [[person/aristutalis|Aristotle]] and list the interpreters Ibn al-Nadim names. Then read [[person/aflatun|Plato]] and note what the Encyclopaedia of Islam says about Arabic translations of his dialogues.
