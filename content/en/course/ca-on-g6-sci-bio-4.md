## Brief
Biodiversity shrinks when habitats are lost, species are moved where they do not belong, or climates shift. The strand ends with action: what actually protects variety.

## Invasive species
Purple loosestrife and zebra mussels arrived in Ontario without their predators and spread fast. Restoration means replanting natives, reintroducing predators, and changing the habits that caused the damage.
