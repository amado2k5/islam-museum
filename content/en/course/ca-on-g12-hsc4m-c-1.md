## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe forms of artistic expression found in one culture, to explain how they relate to its beliefs and other aspects, and to analyse a cultural expression with reference to its function, time and place. The museum offers Arabic calligraphy and the architecture of Islamic Cairo; the other cultures the curriculum mentions are for your class to add. The calligraphy record gives only that link to the Qur’an, so its function, time and place must come from other sources your class finds. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What was it for, and when and where was it made?
For a piece of writing or a building, ask what it was used for, who used it and what the surrounding belief or power asked of it. Then place it on a map and a timeline.

## Try this
Read [[concept/islamic-calligraphy|Islamic calligraphy]] and choose one sentence that links the art to a belief. Compare it with the setting that the record of [[work/art-and-architecture-of-islamic-cairo|The Art and Architecture of Islamic Cairo]] says the book gives to buildings: religious, social and political.
