## Brief
Aviation connects families, fights fires and delivers medicine — and burns fuel, makes noise and shapes cities. The strand's assessment strand asks you to weigh both columns honestly.

## Costs and benefits
Remote communities in northern Ontario depend on flight for food and medical evacuation. At the same time, aviation contributes emissions and noise. Engineers now chase electric and hydrogen aircraft to cut the bill {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}.

## Try this
Pick one flight that matters to someone you know. List its benefits and costs, then propose one design change that keeps the benefit and lowers the cost.
