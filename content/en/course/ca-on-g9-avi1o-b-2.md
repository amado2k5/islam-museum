## Brief
This lesson asks you to interpret art works to find their subject, purpose and meaning, and to identify ways works reflect the society that made them. The museum offers three cases: a painting made for a court, a style that formed in royal studios, and the glass industry that followed new capitals. Works from other times and places come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What does the work show, and why?
For each record, write what the work shows, who ordered or made it, and what purpose the record suggests. Mark what the record says and what you are inferring.

## What does the work tell you about the people around it?
Choose one feature of a work, such as who is shown or what materials were used, and say what it suggests about the society that made it. Say what else you would need to find out to be sure.

Read the records on [[object/princes-of-the-house-of-timur|Princes of the House of Timur]] and [[concept/mughal-painting|Mughal painting]]. Write a paragraph that explains one painting by its patron or its studio, and underline the sentence that comes from the record.
