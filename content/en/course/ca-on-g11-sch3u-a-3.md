## Brief
This lesson asks you to ask whether the evidence supports your hypothesis, where error or bias may lie, and to justify your conclusion with scientific knowledge. The museum offers two examples of how far a single piece of evidence can take you. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Is there more than one explanation?
Think of the result of a single reading: what other explanations are possible? What would have changed it if something else had changed?

## How many pieces of evidence before deciding?
Compare judging by one criterion with judging by several together. Write what each reading adds to your conclusion.

## Try this
Read the records on [[invention/ibn-sina-seven-drug-assessment-conditions|Ibn Sina's seven conditions]] and [[concept/plant-ash-glass|plant-ash glass]]. Choose a result from an experiment you ran and write what supports it and what could weaken it.
