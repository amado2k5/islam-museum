## Brief
This lesson asks you to see that day and night, and the four seasons, repeat in cycles, and to describe how the light and heat from the Sun change through the day and the year. The museum offers one example of people dividing daylight and darkness into hours; the class observes its own sky and weather. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What repeats, and what changes each time?
Notice when it gets light and when it gets dark where you live. Ask what is the same from one day to the next, and what is different from one season to the next.

## Try this
Write down the time of sunrise and sunset on one day this week. Next month do it again and compare. Then read the card on this page about al-Jazari's twelve portions of daylight.
