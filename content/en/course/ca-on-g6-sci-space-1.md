## Brief
Eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids and comets orbit one ordinary star. Scale is the hard part: if the Sun were a beach ball, Earth would be a peppercorn a few classrooms away.

## Earth–Moon–Sun
Earth spins daily (day and night), orbits yearly (seasons with the axial tilt), and the Moon orbits monthly (phases and tides). Most of what the sky does is geometry {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}.

## Try this
Make a scale model: choose a ball for the Sun, then look up the true scale distances — students are always surprised by the empty space.
