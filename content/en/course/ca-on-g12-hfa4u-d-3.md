## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse the effect of trends in agriculture, such as genetic engineering, on food supply and production. The museum holds one resolution that defines such foods, names the aims of the modification and sets conditions for eating them; it does not measure any effect on supply, which your class's sources must do. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Which aims could change supply, and how would you test that?
Read the aims the resolution lists. Choose the ones that could change how much food is produced, and write down what data your class would need to see whether they do.

## Try this
Read [[work/fatwa-iifa-266-genetically-modified-animal-foods|the resolution on genetically modified foods of animal origin]]. List its aims and its three conditions, then pick one aim and plan a short research question about its effect on supply.
