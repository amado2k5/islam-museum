## Brief
Static electricity is charge that sits still — built up when materials rub and electrons jump, discharged in sparks, shocks and lightning.

## Attraction and repulsion
Like charges repel, unlike charges attract. Rub a balloon on hair and transferred electrons let the balloon cling to a wall — a force you cannot see, making effects you can {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}.

## Try this
Charge a balloon and hold it near a thin stream of water. The stream bends. Explain why using charges.
