## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse how social and cultural belief systems affect people's views on equity and social justice and the decisions they make. The museum offers two cases from one tradition, an author's reading of equality and the jurists' rule of zakat; your class will bring other belief systems and the Canadian questions on which they bear. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a belief ask of the believer, and of the community?
Notice who is said to owe what to whom, and whether the duty is described as a favour or as a right. Then ask whose reading you are looking at: the tradition's jurists, or one author.

## Try this
Read [[concept/equality-in-islam|Equality in Islam]] and [[concept/zakat|Zakat (alms tax)]]. For each, write one sentence saying who is said to owe what to whom, and mark whether it is a jurist's rule or an author's reading.
