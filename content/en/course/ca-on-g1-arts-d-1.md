## Brief
This lesson asks you to use lines and shapes in your art to say something, and to explain how lines and shapes carry meaning in your work and in the work of others. The museum offers two examples of patterns that repeat; your class brings its own drawings and materials. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## Which lines and shapes do you see?
Name the kinds of line (straight, curved, wavy) and the shapes that you can find. Ask which ones come back and how often.

## What does a repeated shape make you feel?
Look at a pattern and say what it makes you think of. Ask what would change if you made the shapes bigger, smaller or closer together.

## Try this
Read the card on [[concept/islamic-geometric-patterns|Islamic geometric patterns]] and the card on the [[concept/arabesque|arabesque]]. Draw a pattern with one repeated shape, then add a second kind of line, and explain your choices to a partner.
