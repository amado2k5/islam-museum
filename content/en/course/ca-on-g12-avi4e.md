## Brief
The Grade 12 Visual Arts course AVI4E of the Ontario curriculum, with three strands. The museum maps all three to Islamic calligraphy, tilework, book illumination, the arabesque and three historical works read for their use and society; the practical work of making art is left to your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}
