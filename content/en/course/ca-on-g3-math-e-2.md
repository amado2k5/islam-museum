## Brief
This lesson asks you to measure the same thing with units of different sizes and to show that the count changes while the amount stays the same. The museum offers a record on a measure used for grain, of which two sizes were known; measuring with units of your own is the class's work. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## Why does the count change?
Measure the same sand with a small cup and then a large one. Which cup gives the larger count? Is there more sand in one count than in the other?

## Try this
Read the record on the sa'. Fill a container with rice using a small cup, then again using a large cup. Compare the two counts and say what stayed the same.
