## Brief
This lesson asks you to investigate and explain the laws of reflection, to say how light reflects from different mirrors, and to draw ray diagrams. The museum offers one geometric problem from a medieval book of optics, about where light reflects on a circular mirror; the experiments, the plane, concave and convex mirrors and the ray diagrams are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## How do you find where the light bounces?
The expectations ask you to use the law of reflection. Decide what you must measure at the point where the ray meets the mirror, and from which line the angles are measured.

Read about [[invention/ibn-al-haytham-circular-reflection|Ibn al-Haytham's circular-mirror method]]. Draw a circle, an object and an eye, and try two points on the rim: for each, draw the normal and measure the two angles.
