## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse current equity and social justice issues in Canada, their underlying social circumstances and strategies for addressing them; Islamophobia is one of the examples the expectation names. The museum holds nothing on Canada itself. It offers one United Nations day against it; the Canadian circumstances and strategies come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What can a commemoration do about an issue?
Look at what the United Nations chose to do about it, and ask which circumstances in your own community a strategy like that would meet.

## Go deeper
Read what the Day's own texts say they are for. According to the record of [[event/international-day-to-combat-islamophobia|the International Day to Combat Islamophobia]], the 2022 resolution stresses that terrorism and violent extremism cannot and should not be associated with any religion, nationality, civilisation or ethnic group, and calls for a global dialogue on a culture of tolerance and peace based on respect for human rights and for the diversity of religions and beliefs {cite:un-observances-anti-islamophobia-day} {cite:wikipedia-international-day-to-combat-islamophobia-en}. In 2024 the General Assembly condemned incitement to discrimination, hostility or violence against Muslims, called on member states to take legislative and policy measures, and asked for a Special Envoy {cite:un-observances-anti-islamophobia-day}. In May 2025 Miguel Ángel Moratinos was appointed; he says his role is "not to defend any one religion; it is to defend the human right of all people to practice their religion or faith – without fear" {cite:un-observances-anti-islamophobia-day}.

## How do we know?
The record of the Day rests on two sources. The United Nations' own observance page gives the resolutions, the Special Envoy and the 2026 events {cite:un-observances-anti-islamophobia-day}; an English Wikipedia revision of 24 May 2026 gives the proposal by Pakistan's then prime minister in September 2020 and the reason for the date {cite:wikipedia-international-day-to-combat-islamophobia-en}. The record of [[event/canadian-islamic-history-month|Canadian Islamic History Month]] cites a Government of Canada page, which states that the month was first established in 2007; the record notes that the page does not name the parliamentary instrument that created it {cite:canada-ca-combatting-islamophobia-commemorations}. Both records report what was proclaimed, published and organised. Neither reports what changed as a result.

## Think about it
The United Nations places the Day within its wider work against hate speech {cite:un-observances-anti-islamophobia-day}, and the Government of Canada page describes a month of educational materials, posters and videos {cite:canada-ca-combatting-islamophobia-commemorations}. Take a position: can a commemoration, a campaign or an envoy address the circumstances behind an issue in a Canadian community, and what is the strongest objection to your position?
