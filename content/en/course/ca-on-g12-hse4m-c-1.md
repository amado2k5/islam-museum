## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse current equity and social justice issues in Canada, their underlying social circumstances and strategies for addressing them; Islamophobia is one of the examples the expectation names. The museum holds nothing on Canada itself. It offers one United Nations day against it; the Canadian circumstances and strategies come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What can a commemoration do about an issue?
Look at what the United Nations chose to do about it, and ask which circumstances in your own community a strategy like that would meet.

## Try this
Read [[event/international-day-to-combat-islamophobia|International Day to Combat Islamophobia]]. Write down three questions you would ask about this issue in a Canadian city, and say which record could help with each.
