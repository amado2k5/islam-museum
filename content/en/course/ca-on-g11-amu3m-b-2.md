## Brief

This lesson asks you to analyse the impact that significant individuals from a variety of cultures have had on music. The museum offers two cases from the Muslim world: a ninth-century musician whose portrait is debated, and a Sufi singer of our own time. The other names the curriculum lists are the work of your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What changed because of this person?

For each musician, write what the records attribute to them and what stays only a report. Then ask what came before, and what came after.

## Who taught them, and whom did they teach?

Look at the chains of teaching the records mention. Can an individual's impact be understood without teachers and pupils?

## Go deeper
The TDV biography follows [[person/ziryab|Ziryab]] through training under Ibrahim al-Mawsili and his son Ishaq, a life in Kairouan under the Aghlabid Ziyadat Allah, and settlement with Abd al-Rahman II {cite:tdv-ziryab-arslan-erkocoglu pp.464–465}. It leaves his birthplace and birth date uncertain and gives his death as 238 AH/852 {cite:tdv-ziryab-arslan-erkocoglu pp.464–465}. In Cordoba it attributes to him organised teaching of singing, in stages from rhythm and words to melody and then the subtleties of performance {cite:tdv-ziryab-arslan-erkocoglu pp.464–465}. [[invention/ziryab-fifth-string|The fifth-string record]] adds that TDV names Seville, Toledo, Valencia and Granada among the places with schools modelled on Cordoba's {cite:tdv-ziryab-arslan-erkocoglu pp.464–465}, and that TDV, reporting Henry George Farmer, credits him with introducing the older Arabic tuning of Ibn Misjah, revised by Ishaq al-Mawsili {cite:tdv-ziryab-arslan-erkocoglu pp.464–465}.

## Why they did it
The records preserve no statement by Ziryab of why he taught or played as he did, and the museum supplies none. Maqqari's narrative has him leave Baghdad amid professional jealousy, and the record calls it an attributed narrative, not a contemporary proceeding {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari v.3 pp.122–124}. For [[person/abida-parvin|Abida Parveen]], the English Wikipedia article, citing the Deccan Herald (2010), says she declines offers to sing for films, Bollywood among them, on account of her Sufi faith and its commitments {cite:wikipedia-abida-parvin-en}. Beyond that, the records show where each worked: Ziryab at the court of Abd al-Rahman II, she at dargahs and urs from the early 1970s {cite:tdv-ziryab-arslan-erkocoglu pp.464–465} {cite:wikipedia-abida-parvin-en}.

## How do we know?
The record on Abida Parveen rests on the English Wikipedia article, in a revision of 7 August 2026, and on a Wikidata item {cite:wikipedia-abida-parvin-en} {cite:wikidata-abida-parvin}. So her radio breakthrough in 1973, her official singer's post in 1977 and her honours, from the Pride of Performance in 1984 to the Nishan-e-Imtiaz on 23 March 2021, are the article's account {cite:wikipedia-abida-parvin-en}. The record names her teachers, her father Ustad Ghulam Haider and later Ustad Salamat Ali Khan, and names no pupil {cite:wikipedia-abida-parvin-en}. What she changed in the music is not stated.

## Think about it
Ziryab's record lists what he is credited with, mostly teaching, and Abida Parveen's lists where she has sung and what she has won. Take a position and answer the strongest objection to it: is a musician's impact better judged by the teaching that follows, as with the schools modelled on Cordoba's, or by the audiences and honours counted around them?

## Into the museum
- [[person/ziryab|Ziryab]]: training, route and the credited teaching, with the doubts the record keeps beside them.
- [[invention/ziryab-fifth-string|The fifth string]]: the schools of the Andalusi cities and the tuning, each with the limits the record sets on it.
- [[person/abida-parvin|Abida Parveen]]: her teachers, the radio, the tours and the honours, as one Wikipedia article gives them.

## Try this

Read the records on [[person/ziryab|Ziryab]] and [[person/abida-parvin|Abida Parveen]]. Choose one and write two sentences on their impact and one sentence on what the records do not say about them.
