## Brief

This lesson asks you to analyse the impact that significant individuals from a variety of cultures have had on music. The museum offers two cases from the Muslim world: a ninth-century musician whose portrait is debated, and a Sufi singer of our own time. The other names the curriculum lists are the work of your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What changed because of this person?

For each musician, write what the records attribute to them and what stays only a report. Then ask what came before, and what came after.

## Who taught them, and whom did they teach?

Look at the chains of teaching the records mention. Can an individual's impact be understood without teachers and pupils?

## Try this

Read the records on [[person/ziryab|Ziryab]] and [[person/abida-parvin|Abida Parveen]]. Choose one and write two sentences on their impact and one sentence on what the records do not say about them.
