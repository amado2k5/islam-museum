## Brief
This lesson asks you to investigate factors and processes, including soil health and water quality, and to explain how they contribute to ecosystem sustainability. The museum offers one historical way of judging garden land and its water: a classification of land types and water sources. Biodiversity, air quality, succession and the link to ecosystem sustainability are for your class to investigate. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## How would you test a soil or a water source?
The expectation asks you to investigate. Think of what you could observe or measure about soil and water at a local site, and which of your observations would tell you whether plants could grow there across the seasons.
