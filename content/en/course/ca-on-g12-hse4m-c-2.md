## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how circumstances and personal qualities made certain people effective agents of change, and to analyse the issues religious leaders and movements have faced and what they contributed. The museum offers one person only, an Umayyad caliph, as one author reports him; your class will add other individuals and religious leaders and judge their contributions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does the account say, and what does it leave out?
Separate what the author reports from what you would need to know to explain the change: the office the person held, who agreed and who did not, and what happened afterwards. List what the account does not tell you.

## Try this
Read [[person/umar-ibn-abd-al-aziz|Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz]]. Write three things the account tells you about his circumstances and three questions it leaves open.
