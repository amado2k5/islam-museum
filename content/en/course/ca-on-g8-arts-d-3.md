## Brief
This lesson asks you to use a variety of materials, tools, techniques and technologies to find solutions to design challenges. The museum links two records on how Persian painters prepared colours and copied shapes; the making stays with your class. The colours in the first record are historical materials, not for use in class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does the material allow, and what does it forbid?
Look at the expectation: it asks you to match materials and methods to a challenge. Take one challenge, such as repeating a shape or making a colour last, and ask which material or technique in the records would help and what its cost is.

Read [[practice/persian-painting-pigments-and-gold|Persian painting: pigments and gold]] and [[practice/pouncing-in-persian-painting|Pouncing in Persian painting]]. Choose one technique and write the steps you would follow to try it with materials from your classroom.
