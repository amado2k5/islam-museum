## Brief
This lesson asks you to speak in Arabic about communities where it is spoken, aspects of their cultures and their contributions, and to link what you say to your own experience. The museum offers one scholar and one set of naming practices. Common nicknames today and naming ceremonies of other communities, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## Whom will you talk about?
Choose a significant person and pick one or two accomplishments to present in your own words, then ask where your talk meets your own experience.

## How are names given?
Compare what the record says about naming with the practice in your own family or a friend's, and notice the differences.

## Try this
Read the records on [[person/al-khwarizmi|al-Khwarizmi]] and on [[concept/tasmiya-naming|tasmiya]]. Present al-Khwarizmi to a classmate in one minute, then tell them about one naming practice.
