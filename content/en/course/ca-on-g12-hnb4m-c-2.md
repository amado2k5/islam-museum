## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the basic processes in textile production, from fibre to thread to yarn to fabric. The museum offers glimpses of these stages in cotton and woven writing; it does not describe knitting or the dyeing, printing and finishing processes in full, and your class completes that. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## At which step does the material become cloth?
Put what you read on a ladder: fibre, then thread or yarn, then weaving, then dyeing. Mark the steps the records leave out.

## Try this
Read the record on [[job/weaver|the weaver]]. Draw a ladder of stages from fibre to cloth and write beside each stage what the record says of it.
