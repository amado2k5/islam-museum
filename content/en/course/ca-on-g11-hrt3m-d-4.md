## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify the concepts that define an adherent's duties, explain the purpose of particular roles, identify influential figures and the contexts they came from, and analyse the impact of leaders and activists. The museum serves the Islamic part: a definition of Islam, the prayer leader, the Prophet, and one nonviolent Muslim leader of the twentieth century. The class adds the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a role ask, and what does a leader change?
A role has a purpose; a figure has a context; a leader leaves an effect. For each record you read, ask which of these it shows, and what evidence the record gives. Keep your own judgement apart from what the record states.
