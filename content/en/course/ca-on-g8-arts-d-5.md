## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and analyse social, political and economic factors that affect the creation of visual and media arts and the community of artists. The museum links a record of a painter who worked under successive patrons; your class adds other places and other arts. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## Who pays, who rules, who makes?
Look at the expectation: it asks which factors shape the making of art. For one record, list who ruled, who paid or appointed, and what the artist or building did next. Then ask what you cannot tell from the record.
