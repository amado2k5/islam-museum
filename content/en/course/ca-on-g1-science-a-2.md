## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how science and technology are used at home and in the community, and to look at what different communities have contributed. The museum offers two examples from the Muslim world; the class adds examples from its own homes and communities. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Whose work do you use without noticing?
Think of a clock, a tool or a device at home. Ask who first made something like it, and in which community that knowledge was kept and passed on.

## Try this
Choose one clock or timer at home. Draw it, and next to it draw the sundial of Ibn al-Shatir described on this page. Write one thing each of them does for the people who use it.
