## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the characteristics of a projectile's motion in the vertical and horizontal planes and to give examples. The museum offers one historical case of a thrown object: a stone that scholars argued about. The descriptions and measurements of projectile motion are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What does a thrown object do in each direction?
Look at the expectation: it separates the vertical plane from the horizontal one. In this case, ask what moves up and down, what moves across, and what a source tells you or fails to tell you about either.
