## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how an early society was governed and to describe the social and work-related groups in it and their roles. The curriculum asks for a few societies, including a First Nation and an Inuit society; the museum links only medieval Islamic examples, so your class will add the others. The hospital record lists the people who worked there but says nothing of their roles or status; those come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## Who does what in a city?
The expectations ask how a society was governed and what roles its work-related groups had. List the officials and workers you meet in the linked records and say what each one was responsible for.
