## Brief
This lesson asks you to compare and contrast music from the past with music of the present. The museum links records of a reported change to an instrument and of a singer of today; the other side of each comparison your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What stays the same, and what changes?
Choose one thing to compare: an instrument or a singer's way of performing. Ask what you can say about each side from the record, and what you would still need to find out.

Read [[invention/ziryab-fifth-string|Ziryab's reported fifth oud string]] and [[person/abida-parvin|Abida Parveen]]. Write one similarity and one difference that the records let you support.
