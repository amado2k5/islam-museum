## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse how historical developments and social issues, such as the religious practices and class structure in the Ministry's examples, have affected the fashion industry. The museum holds one case from the Islamic past, a mark on turbans that set one group apart; your class brings the wider picture and the other developments. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a custom ask of the people who make cloth?
Look at how a custom or a rule about dress gives work to those who make and carry the cloth, and who decides what a garment must show. Then compare it with another period your class is studying.

## Try this
Read the record on [[event/green-turban-ashraf-773|the green mark on the turbans of the Sharifs]]. Write down who ordered or kept the practice and who handled the cloth.
