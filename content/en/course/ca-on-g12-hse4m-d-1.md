## Brief
This lesson asks you to assess how effective various strategies have been, in the past and today, in addressing equity and social justice issues. The museum offers two historical strategies and says what each did; whether they worked, and how that should be judged, is for your class, along with the present-day strategies it will compare them with. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## By what measure would you say it worked?
Choose a measure before you judge: was the wrong put right, did the remedy last, who benefited, who was left out. Then ask what evidence the record gives for each measure and what it does not.

## Try this
Read [[event/hilf-al-fudul|Hilf al-Fudul (the pact of the virtuous)]] and [[invention/waqf|Waqf: the endowment that keeps the principal and gives the yield]]. For each, write one measure of success you could apply and the line of the record that speaks to it.
