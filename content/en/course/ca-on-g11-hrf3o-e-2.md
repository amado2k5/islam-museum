## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how sacred writings and teachings shape a tradition's practice, to identify a key precept, and to describe what it shares with other traditions. The museum offers one month tied to the Qur'an and one precept from the hadith; your class adds the sacred stories and precepts of the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Where does a practice get its reason?
For each practice, ask which text the source points to, and whether the source says that the text commands it, names it, or is read during it. Keep to the wording of the source, and mark what you infer.

## Try this
Read [[concept/ramadan-fiqh|Ramadan]] and [[passage/muslim-0045|Muslim 45]]. Write one sentence for each saying how a text is linked to something people do or hold, and one question for a classmate who studies another tradition.
