## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe, compare and order rational and irrational numbers, and to estimate and calculate square roots. The museum offers two accounts, one of root extraction by approximation and one of an irrational quantity inside an equation; the estimating, ordering and calculating are for your class to do. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## Between which two whole numbers does the root fall?
Choose a number that is not a perfect square and find the two consecutive whole numbers between which its square root lies, then refine the estimate in decimals. Say how you know your new estimate is better than the last, and where you would place the root on a number line among the rational numbers.
