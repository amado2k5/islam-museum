## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe careers related to the science you study, and the education and training they need. The museum can show one case only: how a physician of the thirteenth century learned and then worked. Your class must bring today's careers in biology and their training from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What counts as training, and what counts as work?
Read each record and sort what it says into learning (teachers, places of study) and working (posts, duties). Then ask which of these you would expect to find in a science career today.

## Try this
Make a two-column table for Ibn al-Nafis, learning and working, from the three cards of this lesson. Then make the same table for a career in biology that you choose.
