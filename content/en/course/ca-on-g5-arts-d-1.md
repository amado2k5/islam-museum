## Brief
This lesson asks you to show an understanding of how to read and interpret signs, symbols and style in art works. The museum offers two Arabic scripts, Kufic and nasta'liq, in which you can read a style's name, its features and the story of its origin. The symbols and styles of other arts your class must bring from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does a style's name tell you?
Read the name of the script and ask whether it points to a place or to a shape, and what in the text is your evidence.

## What is the difference between a legend and the scholars' account?
Put each in its own column. Ask what each one explains and what it leaves unexplained.

Read about [[concept/kufic-script|Kufic script]] and [[concept/nastaliq-script|nasta'liq script]]. List three features of each as the text describes them, then decide which feature is enough to recognise a script among others.
