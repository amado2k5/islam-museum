## Brief
This strand asks students to assess the importance of biodiversity and describe ways of protecting it, and to understand biodiversity, its contributions to the stability of natural systems and its benefits to humans. The museum holds records for only part of it, on how al-Jahiz sorted animals into groups, in the lesson on classification; the other lessons say plainly that the museum holds nothing for them yet. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}
