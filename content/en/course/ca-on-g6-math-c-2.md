## Brief
This lesson asks you to evaluate algebraic expressions with whole numbers and decimal tenths, and to solve equations with several terms, checking each solution. The museum offers a book whose title gave algebra its name, and some of its worked examples. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How do you know an answer is right?
Put your answer back into the equation and see whether both sides are equal. Then explain, step by step, what you did to each side.

## Go deeper
Al-Khwarizmi says that reckoning by [[concept/algebra|al-jabr and al-muqabala]] needs three kinds of number: roots, squares and simple number {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.30}. A square, he says, is what results from a root multiplied by itself {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.31}. They combine into [[concept/al-durub-al-sitta|six types]] of problem. One type is “five squares equal eighty”, so one square is a fifth of eighty, sixteen {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.32}. In each type, more or less than one square is reduced to a single square, and whatever equals it is treated likewise {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.31}.

## Why they did it
In his preface al-Khwarizmi says he wrote the book with the encouragement of the caliph al-Ma'mun, whose love of learning and closeness to scholars he praises {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.29}. He says he made it for what people need in their dealings, such as inheritances and trade {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.30}. These are his words about his own purpose, as the record reports them from the preface of [[work/al-jabr-wal-muqabala|the book]].

## Think about it
Al-Khwarizmi insisted that everything worked by al-jabr and al-muqabala must bring you to one of six types {cite:khwarizmi-jabr-cairo-1939 p.43}. What do you gain, and what might you lose, when you fit many different problems into a few types, and why?

Read the records on algebra and on the six types of equation. Take the example of four roots equal to twenty, write it with a letter for the unknown, solve it, and check your answer.
