## Brief
This lesson asks you to evaluate algebraic expressions with whole numbers and decimal tenths, and to solve equations with several terms, checking each solution. The museum offers a book whose title gave algebra its name, and some of its worked examples. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How do you know an answer is right?
Put your answer back into the equation and see whether both sides are equal. Then explain, step by step, what you did to each side.

## Try this
Read the records on algebra and on the six types of equation. Take the example of four roots equal to twenty, write it with a letter for the unknown, solve it, and check your answer.
