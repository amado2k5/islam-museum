## Brief
This lesson asks you to solve problems involving perimeter, circumference, area, volume and surface area of composite two-dimensional shapes and three-dimensional objects, using appropriate formulas. The museum offers three cards on the measuring of areas, the circle and a muqarnas ceiling; the problems, the formulas and the calculations are for your class to do. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How do you measure a shape built from simple shapes?
Draw a composite shape, then divide it into shapes whose area formula you know. Work out each part, add them, and say which formula you used for each part and why. Check your answer by dividing the shape a different way.
