## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse contributions to science by people from various communities, including communities in Canada. The museum offers two contributions by scholars of the Islamic world: one on measuring the Earth and one on the Moon's changing distance. Contributions by communities in Canada are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What counts as a contribution?
The expectation asks you to analyse, not only to name. Look for the problem a person worked on, what came before, and what they added. Notice whether a contribution is a whole theory or one precise fix.

## Try this
Read about [[invention/al-biruni-earth-radius|al-Biruni's mountain-and-horizon measurement]]. Write down the problem he worked on, one thing he built on, and what he added.
