## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe different patterns made of geometric designs, including patterns you can find in real places. The museum offers one short record on geometric patterns in buildings, the girih; the photographs, the describing and the rest of the patterns your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What repeats?
Look at a pattern and find the smallest part that repeats. Say what changes and what stays the same each time it comes back.

## Where else do you see it?
Patterns are not only on walls. Look for one on a floor, a fabric or a fence near you, and describe it in words a friend could use to draw it.

## Try this
Read the record on girih. Draw a star or a polygon you can find in a girih pattern, and then draw the next two shapes in its row.
