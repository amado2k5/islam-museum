## Brief
This lesson asks you to list the geometric properties of quadrilaterals, including diagonals and symmetry. The museum offers one record, on a quadrilateral from a book of geometry; it does not describe the diagonals or the symmetry, so that analysis is your class's work. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What can you list about this quadrilateral?
Draw the shape the record describes. List what you can say about its sides, its angles, its diagonals and any line symmetry, and mark which of these the record states and which you found from your drawing.

## Try this
Read the record on Khayyam's quadrilateral. Sketch it, list its properties, and then compare your list with a classmate's.
