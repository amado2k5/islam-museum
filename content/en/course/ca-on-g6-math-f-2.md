## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify financial goals, including earning and saving goals, and key steps towards them, and to explain interest rates and the fees that come with accounts and loans. The museum offers the jurists' account of saving and their definition of riba; it holds no record of bank products, so your class brings those. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What makes a goal a need?
List a saving goal and write the steps to reach it. Then explain, in your own words, what a rate or a fee adds to the amount a person owes or receives over time.

## Try this
Read the records on saving (idkhar) and on riba. Write one sentence from each that you find easy to restate, and one question you would ask a teacher.
