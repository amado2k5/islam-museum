## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse why key technological developments, among them the loom, matter to the fashion industry. The museum holds no history of how the loom was invented; it offers looms as state workshops, prices and counts in Egypt and Anatolia, and your class adds the other machines and materials. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a number of looms reveal about an industry?
Look at what the numbers measure and what they leave out: the count of looms, the price of a loom, who owns the workshop. Then ask where each number comes from.
