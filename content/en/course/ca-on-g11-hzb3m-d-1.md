## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how some big questions of philosophy matter to your community and the wider society. The museum offers two cases from the debates the curriculum names, the environment and abortion, as its records of Islamic thought state them. It does not set out the present public debates, so your class will bring them. The abortion record is one tradition's classical legal account; secular medical, legal and ethical positions, other religious positions and Canadian law are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Which big question sits under each debate?
For each record, find the question about people, nature or life that a public debate would turn on, and ask what an answer to it would change in a public policy.

## Try this
Read [[concept/environmental-protection-in-islam|environmental protection in Islam]] and [[concept/ijhad|abortion]]. For each, write the big question in one sentence and name one community that the answer would affect.
