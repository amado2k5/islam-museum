## Brief
Strand A of the Grade 4 Social Studies course: Heritage and Identity, meaning early societies to 1500 CE. It has seven lessons, on agreements between groups, on the roles of working groups, on measuring a flood and making paper, on everyday things and sabils, on how dress marked social groups, on where babies and children grew up and learned, and on land and water. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}
