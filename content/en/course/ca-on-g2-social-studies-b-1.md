## Brief
This lesson asks you to understand that countries, continents and bodies of water can be shown in different ways, and to find the equator and the two halves of the earth on a globe or a map. The museum offers a medieval map of the world and a description of the equator; your class works with a globe and present-day maps. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## How can the same world be drawn differently?
Compare a globe, a flat map and a set of small maps. Ask what each shows well and what is hard to see on it.

## Where does the earth divide in two?
Find the equator on a globe and say which half is north and which is south.

## Try this
Read [[invention/tabula-rogeriana|al-Idrisi's world map]] and [[concept/dairat-al-istiwa|the equator]]. Find the equator on a globe, then name one place north of it and one south of it.
