## Brief
Strand C of the Nutrition and Health course: eating across the lifespan, nutrition and disease, and eating patterns. The museum has four lessons here, drawn from its records on infant feeding, lawful food, diet in illness and fasting. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}
