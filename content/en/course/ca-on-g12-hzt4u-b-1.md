## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the main periods of philosophical development and the major philosophers of each. The curriculum names Ibn Rushd (Averroes) in its medieval period, among others. The museum offers only philosophers of the Islamic world and the Greek philosophy that reached them, so your class will add the other names. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What makes a philosopher belong to a period?
Look at the expectation: it asks for periods and the philosophers tied to them. Ask what makes a thinker a child of his time, and where the questions he worked on came from.

## Try this
Read the record of [[person/ibn-rushd|Ibn Rushd]] and of [[work/fasl-al-maqal|Fasl al-Maqal]]. Draw a short timeline of three points: where Aristotle came to him from, what he did with Aristotle, and where his books went.
