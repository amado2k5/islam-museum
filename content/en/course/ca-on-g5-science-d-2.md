## Brief
This lesson asks you to assess ways people reduce the harm that forces from nature do to structures, and to describe features and materials that let a structure withstand such forces. The museum offers one city building and what its builders changed after earthquakes. Rural and remote communities, other structures and the materials themselves are for your class to study. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What can a builder change after a structure fails?
List what was changed in the building you read about, then say which change addressed the shape, which the supports and which the walls.

## How would you test whether a change helps?
Build a small model from paper or blocks and shake the table. Decide in advance what you will watch for, and change one thing at a time.

## Try this
Read about [[building/hagia-sophia|Hagia Sophia]]. Make a timeline of the earthquakes it names, what gave way each time, and what was built or changed afterward.
