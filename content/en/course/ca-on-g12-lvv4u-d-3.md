## Brief
These two expectations ask you to describe how classical philosophy and science influenced later cultures, and how learning about them helps you understand later developments. The museum offers one thread: Greek books in Arabic, the house that gathered them in Baghdad, and the way some Arabic writers read Aristotle. Influence on other cultures, and on religion, is for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What happens when a book changes language?
Ask who gathered the books, who translated them, and what the readers did with them: copy them, comment on them, or disagree. Look for the places where a reader questions the author, and say what that shows about how knowledge travels.
