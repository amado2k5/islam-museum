## Brief
This lesson asks you to write in Arabic about communities where it is spoken and aspects of their cultures, and to link what you write to your own experience. The museum offers an observation on what people's names mean and the story of how the madrasa began. Education systems in different communities today, posters and tourist letters, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What will you write?
Before you write, decide what you want to tell your reader, what you need from the two records, and what you will add yourself and attribute to its source.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/asma-al-arab-min-al-nabat|Arab naming after plants]] and on [[concept/madrasa|the madrasa]]. Write a paragraph on the origin and meaning of a name you know, and a two-column chart comparing the madrasa as the record describes it with your school.
