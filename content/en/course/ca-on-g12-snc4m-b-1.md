## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain what medical devices were used for and to describe technologies and techniques for repairing the body. The museum offers three instruments and materials as one surgeon described them, each with its use and with the record's own limits. Modern diagnostic devices, prosthetics and artificial organs are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What does the device do, and where?
For each instrument, write what it was meant to do, on which part of the body, and how it was worked. Then ask what a modern device for the same task would have to do better.
