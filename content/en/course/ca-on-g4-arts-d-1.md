## Brief
This lesson asks you to notice art forms, styles and traditions of other cultures, and to analyse how the elements and principles of design carry meaning. The museum offers Islamic geometric patterns, the arabesque, as its records describe them. The records do not analyse colour or symmetry; your class can investigate those in the artworks themselves. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does a pattern do to a surface?
The expectations ask how design is used to communicate meaning. Look at a repeating pattern and follow one shape through it: where does it begin, where does it repeat, and what fills the space around it?

## Try this
Read about [[concept/islamic-geometric-patterns|geometric patterns]] and then about [[concept/arabesque|the arabesque]]. Draw one shape from a pattern and repeat it until it covers a sheet of paper, then describe what the repetition did to the surface.
