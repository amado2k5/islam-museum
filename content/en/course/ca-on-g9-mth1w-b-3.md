## Brief
This lesson asks you to pose and solve problems about rates, percentages and proportions in various contexts. The museum offers one context, the fixed portions in zakat, and no worked problems, and it gives the portions as one author lists a rule, not as guidance; the other contexts are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-mathematics}

## How do you turn "a quarter-tenth" and "a tenth" into percentages?
Start from the fraction each name states, then write it as a percentage and as a decimal. Compare the portions: which is largest, and by how many times?

## Try this
Read the Overview section of [[concept/zakat|Zakat (alms tax)]], where Abu al-Wafa al-Maraghi lists the thresholds and rates, and no further. Write three problems of your own, each using a different portion the record names and an imagined amount, and solve them with percentages.
