## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse, on the basis of research, social and ethical implications of research in genetics and genomics. The museum does not teach genetics or the technologies named in the curriculum. It offers one record, a religious body's answer on cloning plant cells, attributed to it and not a ruling of the museum, and your class must bring the research and the other perspectives from elsewhere. The record concerns multiplying plant cells, which it describes as leaving the genetic content unchanged; it is not a case of genetic modification. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Who is asked, and what do they say it depends on?
Note who asked, who answered, and the conditions the answer sets. Then list which of those conditions a scientist could check and which belong to a wider public discussion.

## Try this
Read the museum's record on cloning plants and make two lists from it: the benefits it names and the harms it names. Add one benefit and one harm of a genetic technology your class studies.
