## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe careers related to physics and the education and training they need. The museum offers one person who worked as an engineer in the Islamic world and what his record says about that work. It holds nothing on the education these careers require today, so your class will bring that. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What does a career look like in a record?
Look at the expectation: it asks for a career and for the training behind it. Ask what a record tells you about the work, and what it leaves unsaid about how the person came to it.

## Try this
Read the record of [[person/al-jazari-engineer|al-Jazari]]. Write two lists: what the record tells you about his work, and what you would still need to find out to describe the education and training behind it.
