## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the main features of places of worship, to explain how believers' convictions about place shape what they do, and to identify pilgrimage destinations and say how a pilgrimage differs from other spiritual journeys. The curriculum itself names Mecca and the hajj. The museum offers the Prophet's Mosque, the Kaaba and the hajj; your class adds the sacred places and other spiritual journeys of the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What makes a place more than a building?
For each place, list the features the source names, then the acts that believers perform there or toward it. Ask which features serve the acts, and which show the importance people give to the place.

## Try this
Read [[building/prophets-mosque|the Prophet's Mosque]], [[building/kaaba|the Kaaba]] and [[practice/hajj|the hajj]]. Sketch a simple plan or map of one of the two buildings from what the sources say, and add a note on what believers do there.
