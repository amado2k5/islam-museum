## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse grassroots initiatives that aim to reduce the impact of environmental factors on human health. The museum offers one association's court campaign over shipbreaking yards; whether it counts as grassroots, and what it achieved, are for your class to decide from research. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What was the initiative trying to change?
Separate the health reason, the legal step and the result. Ask who started it, who it was meant to protect, and what other evidence you would need to judge its effect.
