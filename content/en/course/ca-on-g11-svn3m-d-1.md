## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the basic requirements for plant growth and the soil components that various plants need. The museum offers an early handbook that sorts land and water for a gardener; the modern measures, such as pH, nutrients and porosity, are for your class and your laboratory. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What did a gardener need to know before planting?
List what the handbook asks about the land and the water. Then compare your list with the requirements your class has named for plant growth.
