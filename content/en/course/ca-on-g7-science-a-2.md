## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse contributions to science and technology from various communities. The museum offers two: the decimal reckoning that entered the Islamic world through a book by al-Khwarizmi, and the moving of paper-making to Baghdad. The contributions of other communities your class must add from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Whose contribution is it?
Each record separates what one community brought from what another took up. As you read, name separately the origin, the people who carried it and the place where it was taken up.

## Try this
Read [[invention/algorism|algorism]] and [[invention/baghdad-papermaking|the establishing of papermaking in Baghdad]]. Make a table with three columns, where it began, who carried it, and where it was taken up.
