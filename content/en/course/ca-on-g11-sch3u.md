## Brief
SCH3U is the Ontario Grade 11 Chemistry course, from the secondary Science curriculum. The museum links seven of its expectations through the history of chemistry and water in the Islamic world and leaves the rest to the class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
