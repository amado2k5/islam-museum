## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the traits that unite and distinguish representative organisms of each kingdom. The museum does not teach the kingdoms or the modern traits used to classify them. It offers one record, on how one author divided the animal world, and your class must bring the kingdoms and their characteristics from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Which trait separates one group from another?
For each pair of groups on the card, name the trait that separates them. Then ask whether the same trait would still separate them in the classification your class uses.

## Try this
Take the bat and the ostrich from the card of this lesson. List two traits you would use to place each in your class's classification, and say whether al-Jahiz used the same ones.
