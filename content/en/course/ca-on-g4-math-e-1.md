## Brief
This lesson asks you to use the rows and columns of an array to measure the area of rectangles, and to show that the area of any rectangle is found by multiplying its side lengths. The museum offers one record on how an early book began its chapter on measuring area; arrays and rectangles are your class's to build. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How many unit squares cover the rectangle?
Fill a rectangle with unit squares in rows and columns. Count the rows and the columns, then show that multiplying them gives the same number as counting every square.

## Try this
Read the record [[concept/ilm-al-misaha|Mensuration]]. Draw a square of side two and a rectangle of three by four on squared paper, and count the unit squares in each.
