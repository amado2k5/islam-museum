## Brief
The Ontario Grade 12 college-preparation Physics course (SPH4C). The museum maps four of its six strands, with five lessons; the strands on motion, and on electricity and magnetism, have no honest link and are in the gap register. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
