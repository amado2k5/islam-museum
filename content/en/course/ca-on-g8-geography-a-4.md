## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse how processes of the physical environment may affect human settlements in the future. The museum looks backwards: it links one past case in which the failure of a water structure ruined settled life, and your class must carry the question forward to the present and the future. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What can a river or a dam do to a settlement?
Notice the expectation's words 'processes' and 'may affect'. Name the process, the settlement and the link between them, then ask what signs could have been noticed early and what the people could have done.

## Try this
Read [[event/flood-of-al-aram|the Flood of al-Arim]] and the verse the lesson links. Write a short chain of cause and effect, from the water structure to the people, using only what the record says. Then write one question about a settlement you know today.
