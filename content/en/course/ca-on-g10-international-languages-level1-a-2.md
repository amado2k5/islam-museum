## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify, from texts you hear, social conventions of language, among them greetings, expressions of courtesy and ways of expressing gratitude. The museum offers two: how a greeting is returned, and what thanks is. The Arabic expressions themselves, formal and informal address, and body language, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## How does the speaker answer?
Listen to a short dialogue and notice who begins and who answers, and the words that acknowledge a kindness. What do they tell you about the social setting?

## Try this
Read the records on [[practice/greeting-salam|greeting with peace]] and on [[concept/gratitude-shukr|shukr]]. Note the greetings and thanks you hear in a dialogue, and compare them with what the two records say.
