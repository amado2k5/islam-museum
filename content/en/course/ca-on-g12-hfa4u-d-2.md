## Brief
This lesson asks you to evaluate food-distribution systems by their impact on local and global food security. The museum offers one legal answer on one practice, withholding food to wait for higher prices; the systems themselves, such as fair trade and local versus imported food, are for your class to bring and evaluate. The record is a ruling on one practice, not a system. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Who gains and who loses when food is held back?
Identify who holds the food, who needs it and when. Ask which part of food security, availability or access, the practice changes, and what evidence your class would need to measure it.

## Try this
Read [[work/fatwa-aliftaa-jo-hoarding-food-for-higher-prices|withholding food to wait for higher prices]]. Write the definition it gives, then list two other ways food reaches people, and one question about each that your class could research.
