## Brief
This lesson asks you to use a variety of materials, tools, techniques and technologies to find solutions to design challenges. Making is your class's work; the museum links two records on how Persian painters prepared their colours and copied their outlines, for your class to learn from. The colours in the first record are historical materials, not for use in class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does a material allow, and what does it cost?
Look at the expectation: it asks you to choose materials, tools and techniques to meet a challenge. For each choice, ask what it makes possible, what can go wrong with it, and whether another choice would serve better.

## Try this
Read [[practice/persian-painting-pigments-and-gold|Pigments and gold in Persian painting]] and [[practice/pouncing-in-persian-painting|Pouncing in Persian painting]]. Choose a design challenge of your own and list two materials or techniques from your classroom that you could use, with one advantage and one risk for each.
