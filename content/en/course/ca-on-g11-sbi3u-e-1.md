## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain the anatomy of the circulatory system and its function in carrying substances vital to health. The museum does not teach that anatomy. It offers one record, on how the route of the blood through the lungs was first described, and your class must bring the anatomy itself from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What should a diagram of the heart let you check?
Look for the chambers, the vessels that enter and leave them, and the direction in which blood is said to move. Ask which claims about the route you can trace on the diagram and which you must take from the text.

## Try this
Read the museum's record on the pulmonary circulation and draw the route it describes on a diagram of the heart, labelling each vessel the record names.
