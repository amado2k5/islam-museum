## Brief
Strand C of the course covers the terms and fallacies of philosophical reasoning, the analysis of philosophers' answers, and the student's own responses. The museum maps one lesson, on critics of the philosophers and the replies to them. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}
