## Brief
This lesson asks you to find different ways to show the same amount of money with Canadian coins up to 200 cents and with coins and bills up to 200 dollars. The museum offers one old record from the Islamic world, a saying of Malik that names a sum in dinars and in dirhams; it is not about Canadian money. The Canadian coins and bills, and all the counting, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How many ways are there to make 50 cents?
Use play coins to make 50 cents in as many ways as you can, and record each way in a table.

## Is it the same amount?
Take two of your ways and check that they are worth the same. Say how you know, without counting everything again.

## Try this
Read the record on Muwatta 2131. Write an amount you like in cents, and find two different sets of coins that make it.
