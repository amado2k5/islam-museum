## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse the ways in which geological processes affect society and the environment. The museum holds medieval reports of earthquakes and of their effects on towns, buildings and people; why the ground moves, and other geological processes, are for your class. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Who and what was affected?
The expectations ask for effects on society and on the environment. Sort what you read into effects on people, on buildings and on the land or sea, and note which came first and which came later.
