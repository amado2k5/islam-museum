## Brief
This lesson asks you to define terms that are central to the big questions. Here the terms are soul and decree. The museum gives the soul as one report in al-Mas'udi and the decree as the statements of two authors, each attributed to its source. It does not trace how the terms changed over time, so your class will add that. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Do the authors mean the same by the same word?
Read what each record attributes to its source and list the words used for the same thing. Note where two authors use one word differently, and say who is speaking each time.

## Try this
Write a one-sentence definition of the soul and one of the decree, each using only words from [[person/aflatun|the record of Plato]] and [[concept/qadar|qadar]], and name the author each time.
