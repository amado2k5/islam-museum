## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the major ways land is used and how each way meets what people need and want. The curriculum asks about land use in Ontario, including conservation and recreation; the museum links two examples, one of land kept for grazing and one of farmland, so your class will add the other land uses of Ontario. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What is this land for, and who is it for?
Look at the expectation: it pairs a type of land use with a human need or want. For each land use you meet, name the need it meets and say whether it is a need or a want.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/hima|Hima (protected reserve)]] and [[concept/agriculture-in-islam|Agriculture in Islam]]. Make a chart with two rows, one for each, and name for each the land use, the need it meets and who is allowed to use it. Then add a row for a land use near your school.
