## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain why certain eating practices are linked to preventing and managing particular health conditions. The museum offers one legal text, a jurists' rule on keeping the sick from what harms them; it gives its own reason but no medical or nutritional account, which comes from your class's sources. The text is history and law, not dietary or medical advice; someone who is ill should follow a doctor's advice. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What changes in the patient's eating, and who decides?
In the text, find what the sick person eats or avoids, and what it says should guide the decision. Then compare it with the reasons your class finds in nutrition sources.
