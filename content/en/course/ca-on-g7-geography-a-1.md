## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how people have answered the challenges and opportunities of the physical environment, to look at short-term and long-term effects, and to describe human activities such as dams and canals that create and change water bodies and systems. The curriculum asks for examples from around the world; the museum offers a few from the Islamic world, and your class adds the rest. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What did the builders gain, and what did they risk?
Look at what each water work set out to do, then ask what it changed in the short term and in the long term. Compare a work that served for a long time with one that failed.
