## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how mechanisms pass different kinds of motion, including rotary motion, from one system to another, and how they change that motion: its plane, its speed or its direction. The museum offers two water-lifting machines described in one medieval book; the forces in them, and machines of your own, are for your class. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Where does the motion change its kind?
The expectations ask how motion is passed on and changed. Follow the motion from the part that is driven to the part that does the work, and mark every place where it turns into a different kind of motion.

## Try this
Read about [[invention/jazari-double-piston-pump|the double-piston pump]] and [[invention/jazari-animal-scoop-water-lifter|the animal-powered scoop lifter]]. Draw each as a chain of boxes from the source of power to the water, and mark where turning motion becomes back-and-forth motion.
