## Brief
This lesson asks you to show an understanding of some of the main questions in metaphysics. The curriculum gives examples: are human actions free? What is the self? Do people have souls? The museum offers how two Muslim authors put the first of these, each attributed to his book; it offers nothing on the self or the soul, and the positions of other philosophers are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What makes a question metaphysical?
Look at the expectation: it asks you to understand the question before the answer. Write the question in your own sentence, then ask what you would have to know in order to answer it.
