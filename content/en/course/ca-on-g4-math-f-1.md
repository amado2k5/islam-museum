## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain what spending, saving, earning, investing and donating mean, and to name the main things to consider in basic decisions about each. The museum offers records on two of the five, saving and donating, as jurists describe them; earning, spending and investing, and your own decisions, are for your class to bring. What the records describe is the history of a view, not financial or religious guidance. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What would you weigh before you earn, save or give?
For one of the three ideas, list two things a person might consider before deciding. Say which of them the record names and which you added yourself.

Read the records [[concept/idkhar-fiqh|Saving]] and [[practice/sadaqa|Sadaqa]]. Pick one and write two sentences on what a person would consider before acting.
