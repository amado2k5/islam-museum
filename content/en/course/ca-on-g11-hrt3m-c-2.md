## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how ideas about death and the afterlife reflect a tradition's idea of the spiritual journey. The museum can show the Islamic part, in a creed's account of the grave and in the prayer for the dead; your class adds other traditions' ideas. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Where does the journey of life lead?
Read the expectation closely: it links ideas about death to a picture of life as a journey. Ask what the prayer for the dead says about the community's view of the person who has died, and compare them with another tradition's.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/akhira|the hereafter]] and [[practice/salat-al-janaza|the funeral prayer]]. Write down the two pictures of what follows death that they give, one from a creed and one from a prayer.
