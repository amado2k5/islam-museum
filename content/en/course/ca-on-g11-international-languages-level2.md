## Brief
The Ontario Ministry of Education's generic Level 2 University Preparation course in international languages, listed under the codes LBACU to LDYCU and taught for many languages. The museum serves it as the course a student of Arabic takes, and only where the expectations ask for knowledge of the cultures and communities where the language is spoken. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}
