## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain, from research, how knowing the cultural and historical context of an art work has clarified its meaning, and how works respond to and reflect the society that made them. The museum offers three cases: glass, painting and tile. The research and the comparison with other works come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What did the maker have, and who paid?
List the materials, techniques and patrons each record names. Say which of them you think explains the work best, and what else you would need to find out to be sure.

## How does the work reflect its society?
Choose one feature of a work and show what it tells you about the people who made or ordered it. Mark what the record says and what you are inferring.

## Try this
Read the records on [[practice/islamic-glassmaking|glassmaking in the early Islamic world]] and [[concept/mughal-painting|Mughal painting]]. Write a paragraph that explains one work by its patron or its materials, and underline the sentence that comes from the record.
