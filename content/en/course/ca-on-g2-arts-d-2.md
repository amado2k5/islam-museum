## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how elements and principles of design are used to communicate meaning in your own and others' art work. The museum offers Islamic geometric patterns and the arabesque, as its records describe them. The records do not discuss colour or texture; your class can look at those in the works themselves. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does a repeated shape do to a surface?
The expectation asks how design carries meaning. Look at a repeating pattern and follow one shape in it: where does it start, where does it repeat, and what fills the space around it?

## Try this
Read about [[concept/islamic-geometric-patterns|geometric patterns]] and then about [[concept/arabesque|the arabesque]]. Draw one shape and repeat it until it covers a sheet of paper, then describe what the repeating did to the surface.
