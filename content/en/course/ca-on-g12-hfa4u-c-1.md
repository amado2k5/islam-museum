## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse developments across the lifespan, such as pregnancy, lactation and infancy, to see how they affect nutritional needs. The museum offers legal texts about infant feeding and about fasting in pregnancy and nursing, not nutrition science; your class brings the science. Neither text is evidence about nutritional needs; the lesson uses them as examples of how a community's rules respond to a life stage. The fatwa does not say who judges whether harm is feared. This is the history of a ruling, not medical advice; a pregnant or nursing person should ask her doctor. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Who is responsible for the child's feeding in each text?
Note who is named as having a duty in the first text and what the second text says a woman should weigh. Then ask what a nutritionist would need to know that neither text says.
