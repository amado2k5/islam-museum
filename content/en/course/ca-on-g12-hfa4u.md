## Brief
The Grade 12 Nutrition and Health course, HFA4U, of the Ontario Social Sciences and Humanities curriculum. The museum maps nine lessons from strands B, C, D and E, using what its records say about food, famine, fasting and water; most of this course is science and skills that your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}
