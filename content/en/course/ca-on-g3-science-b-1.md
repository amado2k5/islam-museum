## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe what plants need, such as air, water, light, heat, nutrients and space, and to name conditions that can threaten their survival. The museum offers one part: the practical handbook of an Andalusi farmer who relates land, water and season to plants. The other needs your class will study by direct observation. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What changes when the water or the ground changes?
Picture one plant in two different kinds of ground, or with two different waters. Which of its needs changes first? How could you tell by observing?

## Go deeper
In his practical handbook, [[invention/ibn-bassal-soil-water-matching|Ibn Bassal]] has a chapter on water. It tells apart rain, rivers, springs and wells, then discusses how each relates to plants, to what is left on the surface and to the season {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha ch.1}. He does not give all river water one character: the record says he describes how it varies in dryness, moisture, roughness and softness {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha ch.1} {cite:ibn-bassal-filaha-profile ch.1-synopsis}. The record adds that these are descriptions from an older account of nature, not chemical analyses {cite:ibn-bassal-qasd-filaha ch.1}.

## Think about it
Ibn Bassal did not say that all river water is alike. Why might a farmer who treats every water the same grow worse plants than a farmer who notices the differences?
