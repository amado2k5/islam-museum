## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe what plants need, such as air, water, light, heat, nutrients and space, and to name conditions that can threaten their survival. The museum offers one part: the practical handbook of an Andalusi farmer who relates land, water and season to plants. The other needs your class will study by direct observation. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What changes when the water or the ground changes?
Picture one plant in two different kinds of ground, or with two different waters. Which of its needs changes first? How could you tell by observing?

Read the record [[invention/ibn-bassal-soil-water-matching|Ibn Bassal's linked soil, water and crop assessment]] and list the water sources and land types it names; beside each, write a plant need it might affect.
