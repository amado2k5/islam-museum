## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the ways people use the natural environment, including land, water and plants, to meet their needs, and to describe some responses to challenges that arise from using natural resources. The museum offers three records from the Islamic world, on farming, public drinking water and protected pasture; your class adds fuels, minerals, forests and other resources. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## Who may use a resource, and who decides?
Look at what each record names as the resource, who needs it, and what rule or building arranges the sharing. Ask whether the arrangement answers a challenge, and which one.

## Try this
Read [[concept/agriculture-in-islam|Agriculture in Islam]], [[concept/sabil|the sabil]] and [[concept/hima-protected-pasture|Hima (protected pasture)]]. For each, write the need it meets and the rule or structure that organises it, then say which, if any, of the three the record presents as a response to a challenge, and which it does not.
