## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain the terminology of techniques, materials and tools and use it correctly, and to extend your understanding of the techniques and conventions artists use. The museum offers only a few examples of architecture and ornament from the Islamic world; your class adds the rest. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Do you name the material and the technique correctly?
Pull the names of materials out of a description, then the names of what was done to them. Which names do you know from your own course, and which need research?

## Which convention does this design follow?
Look at how the form relates to the surface that carries it. Does it grow out of the surface, or work with it? What name does a scholar give that?

## Try this
Read the record on the [[concept/arabesque|arabesque]]. Choose a surface around you and sketch a pattern that respects it as Yeomans describes, then name the material, the technique and the convention in two lines.
