## Brief
This lesson asks you to add and subtract monomials of degree 1, to evaluate algebraic expressions, and to solve equations with several terms and verify the solutions. The museum offers two glimpses: al-Khwarizmi's six types of equation and al-Karaji's arithmetic of composite expressions; decimal numbers in equations and the contexts of real life are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## How do you know an answer is right?
Put the answer back into the original problem and see whether the equality holds. If an expression has like terms, where do you collect them before you evaluate it?

## Try this
Read [[concept/al-durub-al-sitta|The six types of equation]] and then [[invention/karaji-polynomial-arithmetic|Al-Karaji's polynomial arithmetic]]. Evaluate x²+5x+6 for x equal to 1, then 2, then 3, and then check al-Khwarizmi's answer by putting it into the equation.
