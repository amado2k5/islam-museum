## Brief
This lesson asks you to explore traditional and current materials, tools and techniques of visual artists and to adapt them in your own work. The museum offers only three examples from Islamic art, below; the materials in your studio and the current technologies come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What does a material ask of the maker?
Look at how each record prepares its material before the art begins: grinding, pounding, cutting, inlaying. Note the steps and the order in which they come, and what each step protects or makes possible.

## What could you adapt, and what would you change?
Choose one step and ask which current material or tool could do the same work. Say what would be gained and what would be lost.

## Try this
Read the record on [[practice/persian-painting-pigments-and-gold|pigments and gold in Persian painting]] and the one on [[concept/inlaid-metalwork|inlaid metalwork]]. Draw the steps of one of them as a short sequence, and mark the step you would try first in your studio.
