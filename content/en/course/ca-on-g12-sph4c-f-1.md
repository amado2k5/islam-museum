## Brief
This lesson asks you to research the historical development of a hydraulic or pneumatic system, to ask why it was developed and how it was improved, and to describe the common components of such systems and what each does. The museum offers two pumps from two medieval books, one with two pistons and one with six, both with pistons and valves; modern systems, their social effects and the full history of pumps are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What changed from one design to the next?
Compare the two pumps. List the parts they share and what each adds. What does each record say the design was meant to do?

## What does a valve do?
Find where the valves sit in the pump you read about. What would happen to the water without them?
