## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the major climate regions of the world and the natural processes and other factors that create and change climate patterns. The museum offers a medieval way of dividing the earth into climates, from al-Masudi and al-Muqaddasi; it is not what modern climate maps show, so your class brings the modern climate regions and the evidence for them. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What makes one place hotter, colder or wetter than another?
Compare the factors the medieval authors name, such as the sun's rays and heat, with the ones your class uses. Ask which of their explanations rest on position or sun, and which would need new evidence today.

## Try this
Read [[concept/al-aqalim-al-saba|The seven climates]]. List each factor the authors give for a place's climate, then add the factors your class has learned.
