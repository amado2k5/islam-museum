## Brief
The Grade 12 Classical Civilization course, LVV4U, of the Ontario Classical Studies and International Languages curriculum. The museum maps eight lessons from strands A, C, D and E, with examples from Arabic sources on Greek learning; the rest of the course, including the myths, the literature and the Roman material culture, is for your class to add. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}
