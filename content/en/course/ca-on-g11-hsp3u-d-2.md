## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how factors such as the physical environment and geographic location, and social structures such as family and kin, shape individual and group behaviour. The museum serves one account only, one contemporary author's picture of tribe and kinship in Arabia before Islam; the class brings the methods of sociology, the physical environment and other societies. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## How do you tell a description from a judgement?
Read the source and mark what it describes and what it judges. Then ask what evidence it gave, and which other factor a sociologist would have asked about.

## Try this
Read the record on [[concept/social-condition-jahiliyya|the social condition of the Arabs before Islam]]. Make two lists, what it describes and what it judges, then name one other factor you would ask about.
