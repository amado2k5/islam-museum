## Brief
Strand B of the Ontario Grade 11 Biology course SBI3U: the diversity of living things and the principles of classification. The museum maps one expectation, on the traits that unite and distinguish organisms, and leaves the rest to the class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
