## Brief
Ontario's Grade 1 Science and Technology course has five strands: STEM skills, Life Systems, Matter and Energy, Structures and Mechanisms, and Earth and Space Systems {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}. The museum maps parts of four of them and none of Matter and Energy.
