## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how particular incidents connect to the rituals a tradition keeps. The museum offers one Islamic example, a fast; your class adds the examples of change over time and examples from the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Which came first, the incident or the practice?
For the example, find the incident the source names, and the practice it is linked to. Note whether the practice is new, or an older one that the incident gave a new meaning.

Read [[practice/ashura|Ashura]]. Draw an arrow from the incident the source names to the practice it is linked to, and write on the arrow what the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia says was remembered.
