## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse, from your own inquiry, the effects of different treatments on the growth of a disease-causing organism. The museum cannot supply that laboratory work. It offers one text on a question every such inquiry meets: what must be known before one accepts that a treatment caused an improvement. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What else could explain the result?
Before you credit a treatment, list the other things that changed. Then decide which of them your inquiry controls and which it does not.

Read [[invention/ibn-sina-seven-drug-assessment-conditions|the record on Ibn Sina's conditions]]. Pick one condition and describe how you would build it into a plan to compare two treatments in the laboratory.
