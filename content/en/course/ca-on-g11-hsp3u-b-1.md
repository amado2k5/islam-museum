## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain how culture produces diverse behaviour, how cultures meeting each other change culture, and how studying other groups and places helps us understand people. The museum serves a small part: one birth rite with its different customs, the Abbasid translation movement, and one scholar who lived among another people. These records do not call themselves anthropology, and the class brings the theories, the methods and other cultures. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What changes when ideas or customs travel?
Compare what stays of a custom or an idea, and what changes, when it moves from one place to another or from one language to another. Then ask who is telling us this, and whether they saw it themselves or took it from others.
