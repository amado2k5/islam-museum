## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how your own daily life differs from the lives of young people in early societies, whatever their background. The curriculum asks for a few societies, including a First Nation and an Inuit society; the museum offers medieval Islamic records of where small children were raised and where children were taught, and these records do not cover children's work or play, so your class must bring the other societies and those parts of childhood. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## Where did you spend your days when you were small?
The expectations ask you to compare your daily life with that of young people from different backgrounds, such as town or country. Set your own first years and your school next to what each record says about a child's place and teacher.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/istirda-al-badiya|sending infants to Bedouin wet-nurses]] and on [[concept/sabil-kuttab|the sabil-kuttab]]. Write three sentences that begin "I differ from this child because ...", using only what the records say.
