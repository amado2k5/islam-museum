## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe different ways an object can move, to identify the basic kinds of simple machine, and to describe how they are used in daily life to make tasks easier. The museum links two water machines that show a lever and a wheel and axle; your class will find the other simple machines. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What kinds of movement can you see?
Watch a machine or a toy. Describe each movement you see, for example turning, going back and forth, or going up and down.

## Which simple machine is at work?
Compare what you see with the list of simple machines in the expectation. Say which ones appear, and how each makes the task easier.
