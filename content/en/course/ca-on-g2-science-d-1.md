## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe different ways an object can move, to identify the basic kinds of simple machine, and to describe how they are used in daily life to make tasks easier. The museum links two water machines that show a lever and a wheel and axle; your class will find the other simple machines. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What kinds of movement can you see?
Watch a machine or a toy. Describe each movement you see, for example turning, going back and forth, or going up and down.

## Which simple machine is at work?
Compare what you see with the list of simple machines in the expectation. Say which ones appear, and how each makes the task easier.

## Go deeper
Al-Jazari first tells you what a visitor would see: a pool, a pillar, a cow, a wheel and coloured jars {cite:jazari-hill-1974 p.182}. The cow seems to go round and turn the lifter, but it is hollow wood, carried by a lever with its hooves clear of the platform. Water drives the machine {cite:jazari-hill-1974 pp.182–183}. In a room under the floor, water passes over a wheel, and the movement goes upward {cite:jazari-hill-1974 pp.182–183}. The shaft turns the cow, not the reverse {cite:jazari-hill-1974 p.182}.

## Think about it
The cow looks as if it turns the lifter, but the shaft turns the cow. Think of a toy or a machine that moves. How could you find out what really makes it move, and why is looking at the outside not enough?
