## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify central tenets and explain how ideas of the afterlife appear in a tradition's teaching. The museum serves its Islamic part, drawn from a creed and from the hadith on the five pillars; the class brings other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a creed say, and what does it leave unsaid?
A creed states articles one by one. Read the expectations and notice which articles name a central tenet and which concern the end. Then ask which ones a student of another tradition would recognise.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/tawhid|tawhid]], [[concept/five-pillars|the five pillars]] and [[concept/akhira|the hereafter]]. Write each article in one line of your own, and sort the lines under two headings: the central tenets, the end.
