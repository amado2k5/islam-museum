## Brief
This lesson asks you to name significant episodes in a faith's history and to explain the origins, role and contribution of a major figure. The museum offers the life of the Prophet of Islam in three records; your class adds the episodes and figures of the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What makes an episode significant?
Look for what changed before and after each episode, and for who is said to have acted. Separate what a source reports from what you conclude about its importance.

## Try this
Read [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]], [[event/hijra|the Hijra]] and [[event/farewell-pilgrimage|the Farewell Pilgrimage]]. Put the three on a short timeline using only the dates the sources give, and write one sentence on the role each shows.
