## Brief
Ontario's Grade 6 Science and Technology course has five strands: STEM Skills and Connections, Life Systems, Matter and Energy, Structures and Mechanisms, and Earth and Space Systems {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}. The museum maps parts of four of them and none of Matter and Energy.

## How this syllabus works
Each unit below follows the Ministry's expectations; codes such as C2.1 refer to the curriculum document. A lesson shows cards that link records of the museum, each restating what its record says. A lesson for which the museum holds no record says so in its Brief and shows no cards.
