## Brief
Ontario's Grade 6 Science and Technology course has four strands: Biodiversity, Flight, Electrical Phenomena, and Space — plus the STEM skills that run through all of them {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}.

## How this syllabus works
Each unit below follows the official expectations (codes like B2.1 refer to the curriculum document). Within every lesson, marked cards place Muslim connections — a scholar who worked on the same question, an invention, a story, a verse to reflect on — before, inside, or after the lesson.
