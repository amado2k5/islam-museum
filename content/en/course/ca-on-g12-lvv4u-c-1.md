## Brief
This lesson asks you to place key events and periods of classical history in a chronological framework, for example on parallel timelines of Greece and Rome. The museum offers one case: how a dating system named for Alexander was traced to its real start, among the parts of the Greek world after his death. The rest of the framework, and the Roman side, are for your class to add. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## How do you date a period?
Find out what the start of the count is, and who counted from it. Then ask why people might have named an era for one reign when it starts elsewhere, and what you would write on a timeline so that nobody is confused.
