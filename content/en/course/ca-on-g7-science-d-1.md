## Brief
This lesson asks you to evaluate the environmental, social and economic factors to weigh when designing and building structures that meet specific needs of individuals and communities. The museum offers one structure built for a public need, the sabil; your class must bring other structures and judge the factors itself. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Who needs it, who pays, what does it use?
Sort what you find into three kinds: what it takes from the surroundings, whom it serves, and what keeps it running. Note which of your points the record states and which you add yourself.

## Try this
Read about [[concept/sabil|the sabil]]. Make three columns, environmental, social and economic, and place under them what the record says about its water, its users and its endowments.
