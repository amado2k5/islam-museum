## Brief
This lesson asks you to say where various landforms are and to describe their physical characteristics. The museum offers one place from the Islamic world, a mountain range in the desert as al-Idrisi described it; your class adds the other landforms, such as plateaus, plains and valleys, from other regions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What would you say about a landform in a few words?
Practise giving a landform a location and one or two physical features, and notice how much the record tells you and how much it leaves out.

## Try this
Read [[place/al-muqattam|Mount al-Muqattam]]. Write its location and what the record says about its physical character, and mark what you would still need to look up.
