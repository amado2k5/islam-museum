## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify specialized clothing markets, among them religious or faith groups, to explain how and why each is a separate market, and to describe their fabrics and what those do. The museum holds what one community, Muslims, says about dress in general and about the pilgrim's garments; the innovations and modifications in making clothes, and the other markets, are for your class to add. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What makes a community a market?
Notice what a community needs to wear because of its rites, when it needs it, and which fabrics and colours it prefers. Then ask what turns that need into a separate market.

Read the record on [[concept/albisa|Albisa]] and the record on [[concept/rida-garment|Rida']]. Collect in a table the fabrics, colours and garments the records name and when they are worn, then mark what you think is the function of each.
