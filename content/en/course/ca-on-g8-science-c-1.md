## Brief
This lesson asks you to show how mass, volume and density are related, and to determine whether an object floats, given its density, in different fluids. The museum offers two medieval accounts of how weight and displaced water were used to compare materials; the calculations and the comparison of floating and sinking in several fluids are for the class to carry out. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What is being measured, and what could be lost?
The expectations relate three quantities. For each device you read about, write down what it measures directly, what is worked out from it, and where the measurement could go wrong.

## Try this
Read about [[invention/biruni-displacement-vessel|the displacement vessel]] and [[invention/balance-of-wisdom|the balance of wisdom]]. For each, list in your own words the two weights that are compared and what the comparison tells you about the material.
