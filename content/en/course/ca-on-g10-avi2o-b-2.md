## Brief
This lesson asks you to name the knowledge and skills you gain in visual arts and how they apply in careers and other studies, and to research the pathways and the education that careers in visual arts need. The museum offers one craft: how a calligrapher was trained and licensed, and who practises the art today according to UNESCO. Pathways in Ontario and your own research are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What does the learner practise, and what skill does it build?
List the steps of training in the record. Next to each, write the skill you think it builds, and ask where else that skill is used.

## What does the path from learner to practitioner look like?
Draw the path the records describe. Then draw a path you found in your own research, and mark where they differ.

## Try this
Read the record of [[job/calligrapher|Calligrapher]]. Write the steps of training in order, and beside them the career or study area where each skill could be used.
