## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse what forces from natural events do to structures in natural and built environments, and to name an external force and describe its effects. The museum offers chronicle accounts of a flood and an earthquake that damaged towns and their structures, and the rebuilding of one mosque afterward. The forces themselves, and how people limit such damage, are for your class to study. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What does a force from nature do to a structure?
Take one structure named in the accounts. Say which part gave way and what kind of push or shake could have caused it.

## What would you compare before and after?
List what you would look at in a town before and after an earthquake in order to compare the damage fairly.
