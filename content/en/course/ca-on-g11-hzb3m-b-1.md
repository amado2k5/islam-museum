## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe the main areas of philosophy and to name the big questions that belong to each. The museum shows very little of this: one record names moral philosophy as Miskawayh's area but sets out no question he asked, and another gives Ahmad Amin's list of the questions of predestination and free will. It does not set out the other areas or their questions, so your class will bring them. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Which area does each question belong to?
Read the two records and ask which area of philosophy each one touches, and which of them names a question at all. Then ask what makes a question a big question and not an ordinary one.

## Try this
Read the record of [[concept/ilm-al-kalam|ilm al-kalam]]. Write the big question about human choice that the words "predestination and free will" name, and name the area of philosophy it belongs to.
