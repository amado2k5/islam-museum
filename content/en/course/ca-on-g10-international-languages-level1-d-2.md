## Brief
This lesson asks you to write a note in Arabic that uses conventions of courtesy, among them expressions of gratitude and appreciation. The museum offers only what the record on shukr says about its meaning. The Arabic expressions of thanks themselves, the salutations and closings of letters, and date and currency formats, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## To whom will you write, and what will you say?
Decide whom you thank and for which kindness, then choose from the expressions you have learned those that suit the formality between you and the reader.

## Try this
Read the record on [[concept/gratitude-shukr|shukr]]. Write a short thank-you note in Arabic using expressions you learned in class, and tell a classmate which kindness you acknowledged in it.
