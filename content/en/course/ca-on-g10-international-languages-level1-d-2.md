## Brief
This lesson asks you to write a note in Arabic that uses conventions of courtesy, among them expressions of gratitude and appreciation. The museum offers only what the record on shukr says about its meaning. The Arabic expressions of thanks themselves, the salutations and closings of letters, and date and currency formats, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## To whom will you write, and what will you say?
Decide whom you thank and for which kindness, then choose from the expressions you have learned those that suit the formality between you and the reader.

## Go deeper
The record on [[concept/gratitude-shukr|shukr]] has a section on thanking people for a kindness, taken from the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia of jurisprudence. It says that thanking a benefactor is approved by all sensible people, even for a small favour, since, as it puts it, whoever does not thank the little does not thank the much {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10153} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10154} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10155}. Thanks in deed is the root, paying a kindness with a kindness; if that is not possible, a good word is enough {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10153} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10154} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10155}. It says that asking the beneficiary for thanks is disliked {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10153} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10154} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10155}.

## How do we know?
These are rulings reported by a jurists' encyclopaedia, not letters or notes that people wrote {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10153} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10154} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10155}. How a note of thanks is worded in your language you learn from the texts your class reads.

## Think about it
The encyclopaedia says thanking a benefactor is approved even for a small favour {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10153} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10154} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.10155}. Give a reason from your own experience: why might a small kindness deserve thanks as much as a large one?
