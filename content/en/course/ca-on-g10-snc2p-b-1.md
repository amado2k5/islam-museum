## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how systems of the human body interact and to explain why these interactions are necessary for survival. The museum offers one medieval physician's account of how blood passes through the lungs between the two sides of the heart; the systems as they are taught today, and the reasons the interaction is necessary, are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Which two systems meet in this account?
The expectations ask you to name the systems, say how they meet and explain why the meeting matters. Draw the route of the blood as a loop and mark the point where it meets the lungs.

Read about [[invention/pulmonary-circulation|the pulmonary circulation]]. Draw the heart as two sides with a wall between them, and mark the route the record describes for the blood.
