## Brief
This lesson asks you to evaluate how personal and societal factors, such as illness, affect what people eat, and to explain how diet requirements vary with lifestyle. The museum offers two records of rules about eating, one on diet in illness and one on the grades of eating by need. Nutrients, energy needs and the chemistry of a balanced diet are for your class to bring. The records are history and law, not dietary or medical advice; someone who is ill or has trouble eating should follow a doctor's advice. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## What changes what a person eats?
List each reason the records give for eating more, less or something different. Sort the reasons into personal ones and ones that belong to the community, and add one more of each from your own life.

## Try this
Read [[concept/akl|the record on eating]] and match each grade to a situation, such as illness or fasting, then add a situation that a person with a different lifestyle might meet.
