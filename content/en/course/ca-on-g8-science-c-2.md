## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe what affects the flow of fluids, and to compare how fluids are used and their flow regulated in living things and in machines. The museum offers one machine, a water clock whose tank keeps its level, and one living system, the route of the blood through the heart and lungs. Factors such as the thickness of the fluid or the width of the pipe, and any measurement of flow, are for the class to investigate. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What keeps the flow steady, and what sends it one way?
The expectations ask for factors and for a comparison. For each of the two records, write what moves, what pushes or guides it, and what prevents it from going the wrong way.

Read about [[invention/jazari-castle-clock|the castle clock]] and [[invention/pulmonary-circulation|the pulmonary circulation]]. Draw each as a line with arrows from where the fluid starts to where it ends, and mark every place where something steers it.
