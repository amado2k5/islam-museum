## Brief
This lesson asks you to find structures made to carry a load, to name the materials they are made of, and to say which properties of a material help it do its job. The museum offers one building element; the class looks at structures near school and home. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What is holding this up?
Pick a roof, a bridge or a shelf and trace the path from the thing it carries down to the ground. Ask what each material along the way must be able to do.

## Try this
Build a small arch or bridge from blocks or paper and load it with a book. Compare how it carries the load with the keel arch on this page.
