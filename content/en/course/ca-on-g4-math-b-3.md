## Brief
This lesson asks you to represent and solve problems that add, subtract, multiply and divide whole numbers and decimal tenths, using tools and strategies that include algorithms and arrays. The museum offers the history of how such written steps reached the Islamic world and where the word "algorithm" comes from; the problems, the arrays and the practice are your class's. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## Which operation is the problem asking for, and in what steps?
Read each problem and decide whether it joins, separates, joins equal groups or shares equally. Then write your steps in order so that someone else could follow them.

## Try this
Read the record [[invention/algorism|Algorism]]. Choose one addition and one division problem from your class, solve each in ordered written steps, and name the operation each step does.
