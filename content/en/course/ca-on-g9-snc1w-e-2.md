## Brief
This lesson asks you to quantify distances in the solar system and the universe by applying an understanding of relative distances and sizes, using appropriate units. The museum offers one medieval astronomer's table of distances measured in Earth radii, and one method of finding the Earth's size from a mountain. Distances to the planets and stars as we measure them today, and the units for them, are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Which unit fits which distance?
The expectation asks for appropriate units. Think of how a unit that suits a mountain would fail for the Moon, and how a distance given in several units can be compared by turning each into the same one.

## Try this
Read about [[concept/al-abad-al-falakiyya|the distances of the heavenly bodies]]. Choose one body and write its distance in the units the record uses, then in a unit of your own.
