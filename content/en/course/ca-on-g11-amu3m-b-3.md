## Brief

This lesson asks you to analyse the interrelationships between traditional, commercial and art music. The museum offers three cases: a qawwal who reaches a record label, an album in which qawwali meets ambient music and Senegalese musicians, and a nasheed group that reaches distant audiences. The other examples the curriculum names are the work of your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Who listens, who pays, who changes it?

For each case, write who now receives an art that once belonged to its own community, and what carries it: a label, a festival or a broadcaster?

## What stayed, and what changed?

Compare what the records say of the original art with what is added in the recording. Do not judge; describe.

## Try this

Read the records on [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]], [[work/night-song-nusrat|Night Song]] and [[group/raihan-band|Raihan]]. Draw a chain of three links showing how an art moved from its community to a wider audience, and write under each link one sentence from the record.
