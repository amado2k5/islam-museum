## Brief

This lesson asks you to analyse the interrelationships between traditional, commercial and art music. The museum offers three cases: a qawwal who reaches a record label, an album in which qawwali meets ambient music and Senegalese musicians, and a nasheed group that reaches distant audiences. The other examples the curriculum names are the work of your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Who listens, who pays, who changes it?

For each case, write who now receives an art that once belonged to its own community, and what carries it: a label, a festival or a broadcaster?

## What stayed, and what changed?

Compare what the records say of the original art with what is added in the recording. Do not judge; describe.

## Go deeper
The record on [[work/night-song-nusrat|Night Song]], an album of [[person/nusrat-fatih-ali-khan|Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]] and Michael Brook, describes how it was built. The article says that Brook, having found on Mustt Mustt that Khan soared highest when given time to improvise, chose a new method: Khan recorded improvisations, some an hour long, onto multitrack tape, and Brook chose the best sections and built each track part by part, with no structure to aim for {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}. It was a manual process that predated easy digital editing, and Sound on Sound called it a "technological nightmare" {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}. Musicians from Baaba Maal's Senegalese band took part, among them the kora player Kauwding Cissokho, with Dildar Hussain on tabla and Farrukh Fateh Ali Khan on harmonium {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}. The album joins qawwali vocals to Brook's electronic ambient music {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}.

## How do we know?
All three records rest on Wikipedia and Wikidata and on nothing else. Nusrat's cites the English article and a Wikidata item {cite:wikipedia-nusrat-fatih-ali-khan-en} {cite:wikidata-nusrat-fatih-ali-khan}, and [[group/raihan-band|Raihan]]'s cites the English and the Malay articles and a Wikidata item {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms} {cite:wikidata-raihan}. Two articles can be set side by side, and here they differ: the English one dates the death of the founding member Azhari Ahmad to 29 August, after the Era Awards, the Malay one to 29 September, a week before the album Demi Masa {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms}. The Night Song record reports its reception as chart positions, a magazine's ranking and a Grammy nomination {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}.

## Think about it
In Night Song, Brook builds each track from the best sections of recorded improvisations {cite:wikipedia-night-song-nusrat-en}. In 2008 Raihan launched Selawat, an album of salawat for the Mawlid sung without instruments {cite:wikipedia-raihan-en} {cite:wikipedia-raihan-ms}. Take a position and answer the strongest objection to it: when a devotional art reaches a label or a stage, is what carries it, the voice, the song or the community, the same in these two cases, or different?
