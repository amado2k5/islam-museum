## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe applications of simple machines, and to explain how compound machines operate and what mechanical advantage they give. The museum offers three machines described in medieval texts: a calendar gear train, a water-driven pump, and a pump with six pistons and cams; the levers, pulleys, calculations of mechanical advantage and biomechanical systems are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Which parts turn together, and which push another?
Look at each machine and list its parts. Mark the parts that share a shaft, the parts that mesh with another part, and the parts that push something along a line. What does each connection do to the speed or the direction of the motion?
