## Brief
Strand F of the Ontario Grade 11 Biology course SBI3U: plant tissues, growth and the diversity of vascular plants. The museum maps one expectation, on the factors that affect plant growth, and leaves the rest to the class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
