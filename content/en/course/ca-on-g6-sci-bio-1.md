## Brief
This lesson asks you to understand biodiversity as the diversity of life on Earth: the variety of organisms within a species, among the species of a community, and among communities and the habitats that support them. It also asks you to describe how variety within a species is essential for its survival. The museum holds no record that serves these expectations yet, so this lesson offers none; your class brings its own examples and sources. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What differs?
Choose a kind of living thing you know, such as a tree, a cat or a bird. List three ways two individuals of that kind differ from each other, then three ways that kind differs from another kind. Which list shows variety within a species, and which shows variety among species?
