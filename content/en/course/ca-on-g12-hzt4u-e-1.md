## Brief
This lesson asks you to show an understanding of some of the main questions in epistemology. One of the curriculum's examples is: how can we know whether we perceive the world as it really is? The museum offers an old account of vision that separates sensing from interpreting. It does not offer philosophers' positions on knowledge; your class will bring those. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## When does seeing become judging?
Look at the expectation: it asks you to understand the question about perception. Give an example from your own life where you saw something and took it for something else, and say what went wrong: the sensing or the interpreting?

Read [[invention/haytham-incoming-light-vision|the record of Ibn al-Haytham's theory of vision]] and write two sentences: what is sensed, and what is inferred.
