## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify aspects of classical material culture that influenced or are reflected in other ancient societies. The museum offers one case: coins of an Arabian kingdom struck on a Greek model, as a modern book on coinage presents them. Greek and Roman coins themselves, and the other crafts named in the expectation, are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What does a copied design tell you?
Describe the design on each face and say which parts look borrowed and which look local. Ask what the borrowing suggests about contact between the two societies, and what it does not prove.
