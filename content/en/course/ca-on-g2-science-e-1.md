## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify sources of water in natural and built environments. The museum links ways people in the past brought water to where it was needed: a public fountain, a tunnel and a well machine. Your class will name the natural sources, such as lakes and rivers, and look at the water around your own school. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Where does the water here come from?
Trace the water you use at school or at home back to where it starts. Say which part of the way is natural and which part people built.

## What do people build to reach water?
Look for wells, pipes, tunnels, tanks or machines. For each one, say what source of water it reaches.
