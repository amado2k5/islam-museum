## Brief
This lesson asks you to compare how different philosophers have answered the same big question. The question here is how philosophy relates to revealed law. The museum offers little for this: the title of one treatise by Ibn Rushd, as al-Zirikli lists it, and Ibn Khaldun's refutation of philosophy, each attributed to its author. It sets out no argument of Ibn Rushd's, so your class will need the texts. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Are the two answering the same question?
Before comparing, check that the two records answer one question. Then compare what each states, a title that speaks of accord and an argument that refutes. Note who states each, and in which record.
