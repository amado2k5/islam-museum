## Brief
The Grade 12 university-preparation course Philosophy: Questions and Theories (HZT4U) of the Ontario curriculum, with eight strands. The museum maps four of them to thinkers and ideas of the Islamic world and leaves the rest to your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}
