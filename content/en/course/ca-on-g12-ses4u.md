## Brief
The Grade 12 university-preparation course Earth and Space Science (SES4U) of the Ontario curriculum, with six strands. The museum maps three of them to astronomers, instruments and recorded events of the Islamic world and leaves the rest to your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
