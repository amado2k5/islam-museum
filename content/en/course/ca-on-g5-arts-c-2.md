## Brief
This lesson asks you to show awareness of the use of music and musical instruments in various traditions, from early times to today. The museum offers one instrument, the oud, with an account of a string added to it in al-Andalus. Other instruments and other traditions your class must bring from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## Who tells the story of an instrument, and when?
In the example notice who attributes what to whom, what the source says and what a later scholar says about it. Then write one sentence about the account: this is what it says, and this is what it does not say.

## What was the instrument before the story?
Look for what already existed before the attributed addition.

## Try this
Read about [[invention/ziryab-fifth-string|Ziryab's reported fifth oud string]]. Draw an oud with four strings, add the fifth, and write beside it who attributes the addition.
