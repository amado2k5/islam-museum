## Brief
This lesson asks you to use units of time, such as seconds, minutes, hours and units you make up, to say how long different events last. The museum offers one old clock that marked time in half hours; the clocks, the timings and the units you make up your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## Which unit fits the event?
Think of a clap, a lunch and a school day. Say whether each is best told in seconds, minutes or hours, and why.

## Can you make your own unit?
Count how many times you can bounce a ball before a minute passes, and use "one bounce" as a unit to tell how long something lasted.

## Try this
Read the record on the elephant clock. Drop a counter into a cup every time a half minute passes, and use the counters to say how long a short song lasts.
