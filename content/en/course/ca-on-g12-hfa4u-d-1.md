## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain why each key component of food security matters, such as availability, accessibility and sustainability. The museum offers rules about land and water from one legal tradition; the components, the data and Canada's own cases are for your class to bring. The fit is loose: these are rules about land and water, not measures of any component of food security. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## Is the rule about having food, or about reaching it?
For each rule, ask whether it makes more food available, or decides who may use what exists. Match each rule to the component of food security your class has named.

## Try this
Read [[concept/agriculture-in-islam|agriculture in Islam]] and [[concept/shirb-water-rights|shirb, the right to water]]. Choose one rule from each and say which component of food security it touches, and why.
