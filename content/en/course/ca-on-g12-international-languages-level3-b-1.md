## Brief
This lesson asks you to speak in the target language about a community and what it has given the world. The museum offers one scholar, al-Khwarizmi, and the city where he worked. Other contributions, and the speaking itself, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What will you say about the scholar?
Plan a short talk: decide what you say about his city, his patron, his books and how his work travelled. Mark which points the museum's record gives and which need another source.

## Try this
Read the record on [[person/al-khwarizmi|al-Khwarizmi]]. Prepare three points the record supports, then give the talk to a partner in Arabic.
