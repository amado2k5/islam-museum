## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse a technological device that applies the principles of linear or circular motion. The museum offers two water-lifting machines from one medieval book, in which turning motion becomes back-and-forth motion; the forces, speeds and calculations, and a device of your own choice, are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Where do circular and linear motion meet?
Follow the motion from the part that turns to the part that moves along a line, and mark the place where one becomes the other. Which quantities would you need to measure to analyse it?

## Try this
Read about [[invention/jazari-double-piston-pump|the double-piston pump]] and [[invention/jazari-animal-scoop-water-lifter|the scoop lifter]]. Draw each as a chain from the source of power to the water, and mark where circular motion becomes linear.
