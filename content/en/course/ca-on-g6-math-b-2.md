## Brief
This lesson asks you to read and represent integers, including on horizontal and vertical number lines, The museum offers one historian's example in which a quantity called a debt appears as a middle step of a multiplication; the multiplication itself is not for this grade, and the properties of operations are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What does a number below zero stand for?
Think of a situation where you owe something, or a level below a starting point. Mark it on a vertical number line and on a horizontal one, and explain which direction means more and which means less.
