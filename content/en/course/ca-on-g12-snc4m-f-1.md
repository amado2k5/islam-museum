## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse the social, ethical and legal issues raised by an application of biotechnology in health, agriculture or the environment. The museum offers two applications in food, genetically modified foods of animal origin and the cloning of plant cells, as two rulings answered them. Other applications, such as genetically modified crops or in vitro fertilization, and the laws of Canada, are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}

## Who is affected, and who decides?
List the people and things the answer says must not be harmed, and the people it names as able to judge the benefit and harm. Then ask what a law, as opposed to a ruling, would add.

Read [[work/fatwa-aliftaa-jo-plant-cloning|the record on cloning plants]] and write the two conditions it sets, each in your own words, with one question you would ask an expert about each.
