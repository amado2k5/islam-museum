## Brief
Strand D of the Ontario Grade 11 Biology course SBI3U: genetic processes, from meiosis and inheritance to the social and ethical questions of genetic research. The museum maps one expectation, on those questions, and leaves the rest to the class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
