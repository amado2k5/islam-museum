## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify conventions of greeting and address, and to use them appropriately when you speak. The museum offers two: how a greeting is given on meeting and on parting, and how a person is honoured by a kunya. The source for the greeting is a television programme and a lesson. The phrases you practise, regional variants, and everyday formality, your class brings. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## When do you greet, and how do you address someone?
Notice the occasions for the greeting in the record: on meeting and on parting. Then compare how you would address an older person, a friend and a stranger, and what changes between them.

## Try this
Read the records on [[practice/greeting-salam|the greeting of peace]] and on [[concept/kunya-fiqh|the kunya]]. Write a short role-play in which two people meet, honour each other by a name that begins with Abu or Umm, and take leave.
