## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify and describe factors that can contribute to economic development. The museum offers one record: measuring a river's flood, through the Nilometer, which its source says was essential in setting irrigation policy. The record does not say that this raised output or the economy; that link is your class's inference to make and defend. Other factors are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-sshg-2023}

## What does a society need to know to use a river well?
Think about what information a farming society needs in advance, who collects it, and who uses it.
