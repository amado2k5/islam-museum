## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse, from in-depth research, the characteristics of and ideas in forms of traditional music. The museum offers only one account of one instrument, the oud, as its source reports it: an added string. Canadian, Indigenous and other musics of the world come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What idea lies behind this account?
See how the account likens the instrument to a body. Ask what this idea says about how the narrator thought of music, and mark what in your answer is your own opinion.

## What is established and what is only attributed?
In the record, separate what the source reports from what it attributes to a person.
