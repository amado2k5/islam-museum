## Brief
Strand E of the Ontario Grade 11 Biology course SBI3U: the body systems of animals, how they work and how technology and society shape what is known of them. The museum maps one expectation, on the circulatory system, and leaves the rest to the class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-science}
