## Brief
This lesson asks you to read and show numbers up to 1000 by the value of each place, and to add and subtract them in writing, explaining the steps. The museum offers one method of writing numbers in places and reckoning with them in ordered steps; counting with base ten materials and solving problems is the class's own work. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-mathematics}

## What does the place of a digit tell you?
Take 305 and 350. Which digits are the same? What changes, and why? Say how you know.

## Why write the steps in order?
Add two numbers in writing and tell a partner what you did first, next and last. Could someone else follow your steps?

## Try this
Read the record on algorism. Choose a number below 1000, write what each digit stands for, then add 128 to it in writing, step by step, and explain each step to a partner.
