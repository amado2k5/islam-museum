## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain why a tradition prohibits particular cultural forms, to analyse how a tradition appears in a work of art or a building, and to explain why some forms hold special significance. The museum serves the Islamic part: the jurists' views on images, the art of calligraphy and the first mosque of Medina. It does not cover music, dance, dress or food; your class brings those and the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a tradition forbid, and what does it make?
A tradition may forbid one form and make another. Read the records and note, for each, the reasons the record gives and who gives them. Where the record reports different views, keep them apart and attribute each one.
