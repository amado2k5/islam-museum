## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how forms and styles of visual art represent messages and contexts in the past and present, and to show awareness of how the visual arts reflect the beliefs and traditions of peoples. The museum offers the decoration of manuscripts and a script that appeared on coins and monuments. The art of other peoples your class must bring from elsewhere. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What does art say about the beliefs of its makers?
For each example ask what this art writes or decorates, and what the choice of subject tells you about its makers.

## How does a style's place change over time?
Follow Kufic from one setting to another, and ask what changed at what time and why that matters.
