## Brief
This lesson asks you to show that you understand the elements and principles of design and can name them correctly, and to understand techniques artists use for specific effects. The museum offers three examples of pattern and surface from Islamic art; your own terms for design and your own work come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Which element or principle is doing the work?
Look at each pattern and name what you see with the design terms you know: line, shape, repetition, balance and others. Say which one makes the strongest effect.

## How is the effect made?
Ask whether the effect comes from adding something to a surface or from taking something away. Describe the steps the record names and what each step leaves behind.

## Try this
Read the records on [[concept/girih|girih]] and [[concept/arabesque|the arabesque]]. Sketch one repeat of a pattern and label its lines and shapes with design terms.
