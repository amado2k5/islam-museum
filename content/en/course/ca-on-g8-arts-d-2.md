## Brief
This lesson asks you to read and interpret signs, symbols and style in art works. The museum links records on the Arabic script and its early styles, and on Islamic calligraphy; your class will add the signs and styles of other art works. {cite:ontario-curriculum-elementary-arts}

## What is a letter telling you?
Look at the expectation: it asks you to read signs and style. Choose a line of writing and ask what its letters stand for, then ask what its style adds to the words.

## Try this
Read [[concept/islamic-calligraphy|Islamic calligraphy]] and [[concept/khatt-al-arabi|The Arabic script]]. Pick two scripts the records name and write one sentence on each describing what makes its shape different.
