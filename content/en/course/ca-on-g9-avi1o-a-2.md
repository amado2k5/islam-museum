## Brief
This lesson asks you to understand some of the ways art works can be presented to reach different audiences. The museum offers one case: a museum that arranges, lends and tours its collection. Showing your own work to your class, school or community comes from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Who is each way of showing meant for?
In this case, say who could see the work and where. Compare it with showing a work in a classroom or a school corridor.

## What changes when a work is arranged?
Think about what a visitor learns from the way objects are grouped or placed, and what the visitor cannot learn. Mark what the record says and what you are guessing.

## Go deeper
Look at how one museum chose its place. The record says the architect I. M. Pei, then 91, was coaxed out of retirement for the project and spent six months travelling the Muslim world and reading its texts for inspiration {cite:wikipedia-museum-of-islamic-art-doha-en}. He declined the proposed sites and suggested a stand-alone island some sixty metres off the Corniche so that no later building could encroach, within a crescent-shaped park of about 290,000 square metres {cite:wikipedia-museum-of-islamic-art-doha-en}. The museum stands at one end of the seven-kilometre Doha Corniche, near the traditional dhow harbour {cite:wikipedia-museum-of-islamic-art-doha-en}. It was opened by the then Emir of Qatar, Sheikh Hamad, on 22 November 2008 and to the public on 8 December 2008 {cite:wikipedia-museum-of-islamic-art-doha-en}.

## How do we know?
This record rests on one source, the English Wikipedia article on the museum, in its revision of 28 October 2026 {cite:wikipedia-museum-of-islamic-art-doha-en}. What Pei thought is reported as what he said: he named the light fountain of the [[building/masjid-ibn-tulun|Ibn Tulun Mosque]] in Cairo as his source {cite:wikipedia-museum-of-islamic-art-doha-en}. The record has no floor plan, label or visitor count, so it cannot tell you how a visitor is guided through the rooms. For that you would need the museum itself.

## Think about it
Pei asked for an island so that no later building could encroach. Give a reason and answer the objection that people who cannot travel there will never see the work: is a work better shown in a place set apart, or in the middle of everyday life, such as a school corridor?
