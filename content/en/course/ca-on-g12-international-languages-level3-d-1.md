## Brief
This lesson asks you to write in the target language about a community and what it has given the world. The museum offers one example, how two hospitals cared for their patients. The comparison with health care in Canada, and the writing itself, your class does. {cite:ontario-curriculum-classical-studies-intl-languages}

## What would a reader need to know about a hospital?
List what each record says about who was cared for, who paid for it and what was supplied. Decide what you would keep in a paragraph and what needs another source.

## Try this
Read the records on the [[building/bimaristan-adudi|Adudi Bimaristan]] and on [[invention/ibn-tulun-bimaristan|Ibn Tulun's hospital]]. Write a paragraph that compares what each supplied to its patients, using only what the records say.
