## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how technologies turn various forms of energy into electrical energy, and the ways in which electrical energy is turned into other forms of energy. The museum holds no record that serves these expectations yet, so this lesson offers no cards. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## Where does the energy go?
Follow one device from start to finish: write what form of energy goes in, which forms come out, and how you could check each one.
