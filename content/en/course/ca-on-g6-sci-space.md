## Brief
Strand E of the Grade 6 Science and Technology course: Earth and Space Systems, which is about the solar system, the effects of the movements of Earth, the Moon and the Sun, and the technologies used in space exploration. It has four lessons, on Earth, Moon and Sun in motion, on bodies that give light and bodies that reflect it, on the bodies of the solar system, and on technologies for exploring space. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}
