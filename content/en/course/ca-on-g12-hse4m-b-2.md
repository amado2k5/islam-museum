## Brief
This lesson asks you to analyse how legislation, the courts and public policy approach equity and social justice issues, and how they affect people's perceptions. The museum offers two texts, a pact written in seventh-century Medina and a United Nations resolution; Canadian statutes, court decisions and policies come from your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What does a text change that a custom does not?
Look at who is named in the text, what it promises each of them, and who is to settle a dispute. Then ask what a reader who never lived through the events would take the text to say about the issue.

## Try this
Read [[event/constitution-of-medina|The Constitution of Medina]] and [[event/international-day-to-combat-islamophobia|International Day to Combat Islamophobia]]. Make a two-column list: who each text names, and what it asks of them.
