## Brief
This lesson asks you to solve problems with different units within a measurement system and between measurement systems, including those of other cultures. The museum offers the conversion of a module to the cubit; other systems and technology are for your class to bring. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-mathematics}

## Does the conversion factor change when you convert an area?
Suppose a large unit equals some number of small ones. Convert a length and then a square area using both units, and compare the two factors.

## Try this
Read [[invention/kashi-muqarnas-mensuration|Al-Kashi's modular muqarnas mensuration]]. Measure the length of your desk in your own forearms and then in centimetres, and convert the area of its top between the two units.
