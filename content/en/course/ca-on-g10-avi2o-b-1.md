## Brief
This lesson asks you to explore and interpret works from the past to describe their purpose, style, materials and meanings, to say what different works were for, and to show how they reflect the society that made them. The museum offers three Islamic works known through written descriptions and studies: a domed shrine, a glass mosaic and a written band on cloth. Judging colour, form and composition in the works themselves is for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## What was this work for?
For each record, write what the work was used for in one sentence, then underline the words of the record that support it. If the record does not say, write what you would need to find out.

## What does the work tell you about the people who made it?
Look at where the materials and skills came from, who is named, and who is shown or left out. Compare one case with a work you know from your own community.

Read [[building/dome-of-the-rock|Dome of the Rock]], [[concept/gold-leaf-glass-tesserae|Gold-leaf glass tesserae]] and [[invention/tiraz|Tiraz]]. For each, write a sentence on purpose, one on materials, and one on the society that made it.
