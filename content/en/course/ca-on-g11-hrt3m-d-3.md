## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify significant sacred writings and oral teachings, explain how they arose, name the central principles of key teachings, and compare writings and teachings within one tradition. The museum serves the Islamic part: how the Qur'an was gathered, what hadith is, and one key sermon. The class adds the other traditions. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## How does a tradition keep and pass on its teachings?
Writings and spoken teachings are kept in different ways. For each item you meet, ask who preserved it, in what form, and what believers do with it. Then compare two items from the same tradition by their recognised importance.

Read [[event/compilation-of-quran|The Compilation of the Qur'an under Abu Bakr]] and [[concept/al-hadith-al-nabawi|Hadith]]. Make a two-column chart: how each was kept and passed on, and who is named as keeping it.
