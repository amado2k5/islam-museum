## Brief
This lesson asks you to identify differences between branches of one community of believers, to name what unites them, and to describe roles people play. The museum offers the four Sunni schools and two roles in Islamic worship, from records that hold little detail; your class adds the other branches, such as the Shia, and the other roles. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## What can differ, and what holds a community together?
Sort what you read into things that differ between branches and things they share. Be careful to label which branch a source speaks for before you generalise.

## Try this
Read [[concept/madhhab|the madhhab]], [[concept/imam-masjid|the imam]] and [[concept/muezzin|the muezzin]]. Write a short card for each role or school: who it concerns, what it does, and which branch of the faith the source speaks for.
