## Brief
This lesson asks you to show understanding of religious beliefs found in one culture and how they relate to its other aspects, to analyse how a culture’s relationship to its environment shows in its beliefs, and to analyse how aspects of culture come together in its expressions. The museum offers Islamic examples only: a pilgrimage, the rules for lawful food, and one author’s reading of what Islam asks about the natural world; other beliefs and cultures are for your class to add. {cite:ontario-curriculum-social-sciences-humanities}

## How does a belief reach the table, the road and the landscape?
Choose one belief and follow it into one habit: what is eaten, where people travel, how they treat their surroundings. Say whose reading you are using.
