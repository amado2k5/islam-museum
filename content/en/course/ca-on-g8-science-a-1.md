## Brief
This lesson asks you to describe how science and technology are used in various occupations to address real problems, and to analyse what different communities have contributed to science and technology. The museum offers two people who worked in medieval Muslim societies, an engineer and a physician; the class brings the occupations of today, including the skilled trades, and the contributions of other communities, which the museum does not hold. {cite:ontario-science-tech-2022}

## What problem was this person asked to solve?
The expectations link a skill to a problem and a contribution to a community. For each person you read about, note who needed the work, what problem it answered and what we know of how it was done.

## Try this
Read about [[person/al-jazari-engineer|al-Jazari]] and [[person/ibn-al-nafis|Ibn al-Nafis]]. Draw a table with one row for each: the occupation, the problem it answered and the people it served. Then add a third row for an occupation in your own community.
