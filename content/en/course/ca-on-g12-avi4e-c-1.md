## Brief
This lesson asks you to explain in detail the terminology of the techniques, materials and tools used to make applied art works, and to understand techniques artists use to achieve certain effects. The museum offers the vocabulary of three Islamic crafts: illuminating a book, writing with a reed pen, and cutting glazed tile. Your own tools and techniques, and the effects you test, are for your class. {cite:ontario-curriculum-secondary-arts}

## Which word names the part, the tool or the material?
Make a three-column list from the records: parts, tools, materials. Decide where each word belongs, and mark the words you did not know.

## What does the technique do to the surface?
Choose one step from a record. Describe what the surface looks like before and after it, and name a technique you use that makes a similar change.
