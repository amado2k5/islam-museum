## Overview
The literary language of the Muslims of South Asia; it arose in the camps of the sultans, and has the poetry of Iqbal and Ghalib and a vast Pakistani religious heritage. {cite:wikidata}

## For Sprouts
The Urdu language is a beautiful literary language of South Asia, written in graceful script and famous for its melodious poetry.

## For Explorers
The Urdu language is a major language that developed in South Asia through cultural contact between local languages, Arabic, Persian, and Turkic tongues. Written in the elegant Nastaliq script, it became the vehicle for a vast heritage of Islamic literature, philosophy, and poetry by renowned thinkers like Allama Iqbal and Mirza Ghalib.

## For Scholars
A major Indo-Aryan language that developed in northern South Asia through centuries of linguistic synthesis between indigenous dialects and Persian, Arabic, and Turkic vocabularies introduced by Muslim communities. Inscribed in an adapted Arabic script using the Nastaliq calligraphic style, Urdu evolved into a premier literary, intellectual, and cultural lingua franca for South Asian Muslims. It boasts a rich tradition in poetry, mystical literature, translation of classical Islamic sciences, and religious scholarship, today serving as the national language of Pakistan and a recognized official language in India.
