## Overview
The language of the Qur'an and the prayer; the language of the Arabian Peninsula that spread with the Islamic conquests until it became the language of learning, religion and literature from al-Andalus to Persia. {cite:wikidata}

## For Sprouts
The Arabic language is a beautiful and rich language in which the Qur'an was revealed, spoken by millions of people across many lands.

## For Explorers
Arabic is a classical Semitic language intimately connected with the revelation of the Qur'an and daily Islamic worship across the globe. Developing comprehensive grammar, rhetoric, and vast lexicons, it became the international language of science, philosophy, and medicine for centuries, leaving an enduring mark on world civilization.

## For Scholars
A major Semitic language characterized by a sophisticated triconsonantal root system and rich morphology. Its historical trajectory and global status were shaped by its selection as the linguistic medium of the Qur'anic revelation, making it the liturgical language for Muslims worldwide. Classical scholarship formalized pioneering grammatical and phonetic disciplines through master philologists like Sibawayh and al-Khalil ibn Ahmad. Serving as the primary lingua franca of global science, philosophy, and medicine throughout the medieval era, Arabic profoundly enriched Persian, Turkish, Urdu, and Romance tongues.
