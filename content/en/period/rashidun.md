## Summary
The rule of the first four caliphs after the Prophet: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali, when the community expanded from the Arabian Peninsula to the Levant, Iraq, Egypt and Iran. The museum roughly bounds it from 632 CE to 661 CE. These boundaries are the museum's own organising convention and are not absolute.
