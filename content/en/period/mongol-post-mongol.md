## Summary
The Mongol invasion and the fall of Baghdad in 1258, the Ilkhanids and the Timurids, and the Persianate courts that followed. The museum roughly bounds it from 1219 CE to about 1500 CE. These boundaries are the museum's own organising convention and are not absolute.
