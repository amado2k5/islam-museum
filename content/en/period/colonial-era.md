## Summary
The period in which European powers subjected, ruled or dominated most of the countries with Muslim-majority populations, and in which reform and independence movements arose. The museum roughly bounds it from 1798 CE to about 1950 CE. These boundaries are the museum's own organising convention and are not absolute.
