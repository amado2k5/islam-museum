## Summary
The decades in which regional dynasties such as the Tulunids, Samanids and Saffarids gained de facto independence, while the Abbasid caliphs in Baghdad lost control of the army and the provinces. The museum roughly bounds it from about 861 CE to 945 CE. These boundaries are the museum's own organising convention and are not absolute.
