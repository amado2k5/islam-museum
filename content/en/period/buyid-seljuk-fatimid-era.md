## Summary
The centuries in which the Abbasid caliph remained a symbolic centre while dynasties such as the Buyids, Fatimids and Seljuks ruled, and in which Ibn Sina, al-Biruni and al-Ghazali worked. The museum roughly bounds it from 945 CE to 1171 CE. These boundaries are the museum's own organising convention and are not absolute.
