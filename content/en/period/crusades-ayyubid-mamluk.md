## Summary
The era of the Crusader states and of the Ayyubids under Salah al-Din, and of the Mamluks in Egypt and the Levant, ending with the Ottoman conquest. The museum roughly bounds it from 1095 CE to 1517 CE. These boundaries are the museum's own organising convention and are not absolute.
