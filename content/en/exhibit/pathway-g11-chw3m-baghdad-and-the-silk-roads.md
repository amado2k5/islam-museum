## Overview
A CHW3M pathway on Baghdad in its golden century and the network of routes that linked it to Samarqand, Bukhara and China: the city, the House of Wisdom and the translation movement, al-Khwarizmi and Ibn Sina, and the trade that carried paper, learning and people.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes. The nine steps form three stations (the city, the learning, the road) that groups rotate through. The museum has no Silk Roads map yet; use the classroom map.
