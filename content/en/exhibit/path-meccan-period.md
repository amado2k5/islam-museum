## The aim of this trail
To know how the call moved from secrecy into the open, what it faced in Mecca, and how that ended with the Hijra.

## What you will know at the end
- Order the main events of the Meccan period, from the revelation to the Hijra.
- Name who protected the Prophet, who harmed him, and who joined him at each stage.
- Explain how the two pledges of al-Aqaba prepared the way for the Hijra.

## How to read this trail
This is a chronological trail: each step is an event on which the next one rests. Read the summary of each page and then move on. Where the accounts differ, you will see them presented on the pages, attributed to their narrators.
Approximate duration: 45 minutes; 12 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".

## Before you begin

[[event/islam-of-umar|The conversion of Umar ibn al-Khattab]]: days after Hamza's conversion, and how the standing of the Muslims changed. {cite:jami-al-tirmidhi}

### 8. The Year of Sorrow
[[event/year-of-sorrow|The Year of Sorrow]]: the Prophet lost his protector and his wife in a single year. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

### 9. The journey to al-Ta'if
[[event/journey-to-taif|The Prophet's journey to al-Ta'if]]: seeking support outside Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}
