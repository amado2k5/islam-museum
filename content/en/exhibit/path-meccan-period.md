## The aim of this trail
To know how the call moved from secrecy to openness, what it faced in Mecca, and how that ended in the Hijra.

## What you will know at the end
- Order the main events of the Meccan period from the revelation to the Hijra.
- Name who protected the Prophet, who harmed him, and who joined him at each stage.
- Explain how the two pledges of al-Aqaba paved the way for the Hijra.

## How to read this trail
A chronological trail: every step is an event on which the next stands. Read each page's summary then move on, and you will see the differing accounts presented where they exist, attributed to their transmitters.
Approximate duration: 45 minutes; 12 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".

## Before you begin
