## Overview
A CHW3M pathway telling the two crusading centuries as Muslim historians wrote them: the fall of Jerusalem in 1099, Zangi and Nur al-Din, Salah al-Din, Hattin and the entry into Jerusalem in 1187, the Ayyubids and the Mamluks to the end of the crusader states.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes. Best taught after a European account of the Crusades so students can work on the comparison. The museum's battle descriptions follow the sources and avoid sensation; the museum's voice never says 'enemy'.
