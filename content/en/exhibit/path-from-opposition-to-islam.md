## The aim of this trail
To see how the sources describe those who changed their position, when, and for what reason — without passing judgement on them — and to understand what is known as the reconciling of hearts.

## What you will know at the end
- Order the conversions of the most prominent of them in time.
- Distinguish who became Muslim before the conquest from who became Muslim at it.
- Know the meaning of "those whose hearts were to be reconciled" and an example of it.

## How to read this trail
Every step here describes what a person did in their time and attributes judgements to those who voiced them; the museum passes judgement on no one. These are the lives of figures who stood on the other side at one stage and then crossed over — and that is what makes the story complete.
Approximate duration: 40 minutes; 12 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".
