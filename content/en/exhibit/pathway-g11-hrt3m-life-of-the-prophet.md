## Overview
An HRT3M pathway following the life of the Prophet Muhammad from Mecca to the farewell: the city of his birth, Khadija, the revelation, the boycott, the Hijra, the first mosque, the treaty, the Farewell Pilgrimage, and how his words were preserved in hadith.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes. The museum never types honorifics; the interface renders them. In class, name the frame before the statement: 'Muslims believe…' and 'historians hold…'.
