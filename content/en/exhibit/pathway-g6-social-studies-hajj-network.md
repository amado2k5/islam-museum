## Overview
A Grade 6 lesson on the Hajj as a global network: what pilgrims do in Mecca, where they have come from for centuries, and how a Muslim community in Canada today is linked to the world through this journey.

## For the teacher
Sixty minutes: fifteen for the stages on a map, twenty for the two historical steps, twenty-five for the local-community step and the worksheet. The museum has no records yet on Muslims in Canada; that step belongs to the teacher and the guest.
