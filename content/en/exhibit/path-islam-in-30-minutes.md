## The aim of this trail
To leave with a first, sound and coherent picture of Islam as Muslims themselves define it — a foundation for everything after it in the museum.

## What you will know at the end
- Define Islam, iman and ihsan as the hadith of Jibril explains them.
- List the five pillars of Islam and describe each in a sentence.
- Place the descent of the revelation and the Hijra where they belong in the story.

## How to read this trail
This trail is ordered to be read in about thirty minutes, and every step is a documented page in the museum. Read the summary on each page then move to the next, returning to "Go deeper" whenever you wish.
Approximate duration: 30 minutes; 12 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".

## The steps

### 1. What Is Islam?
[[concept/islam-din|Islam (the religion)]]: start with the word itself — how the Prophet defined it in the hadith, and the supports it rests on.

### 2. What Is Iman?
[[concept/iman|Iman]]: Islam is outward works and iman is inner assent; read how the hadith of Jibril distinguishes them, and note the hadith scholars' position quoted on the page.

### 3. What Is Ihsan?
[[concept/ihsan|Ihsan]]: the third rank in the same hadith, with which the ordering of the religion is completed.

### 4. The Five Pillars
[[concept/five-pillars|The five pillars of Islam]]: now you see the whole picture in one line — the five works on which Islam is built.

### 5. The Testimony
[[concept/shahada|The two testimonies]]: the first pillar, by which a person enters Islam.

### 6. The Prayer
[[practice/salah|The prayer]]: the second pillar — how it recurs through day and night, and what distinguishes the obligatory from the voluntary.

### 7. The Zakat
[[practice/zakat|The zakat]]: read how the hadith distinguishes it from voluntary charity, and who its recipients are.

### 8. Fasting Ramadan
[[practice/sawm-ramadan|Fasting Ramadan]]: the fourth pillar — the month in which the Qur'an was sent down, and who is granted leave to break the fast.

### 9. The Hajj
[[practice/hajj|The Hajj]]: the fifth pillar — the pilgrimage to the Sacred House for whoever is able, which leads you to Mecca.

### 10. Who Is the Prophet?
[[person/muhammad|Muhammad]]: after the pillars, meet the bearer of the message as the museum presents him.

### 11. How Did the Message Begin?
[[event/first-revelation|The beginning of the revelation in the cave of Hira]]: the cave of Hira and the descent of the revelation — the first moment of the story.

### 12. The Hijra and the Start of the Calendar
[[event/hijra|The Prophet's Hijra]]: the event from which the Hijri calendar begins, and the end of the trail.
