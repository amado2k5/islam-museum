## The aim of this trail
To leave with a first picture of Islam that is sound and coherent, as Muslims themselves define it, and that serves as a foundation for everything else in the museum.

## What you will know at the end
- Define Islam, iman and ihsan as the hadith of Jibril sets them out.
- List the five pillars of Islam and describe each in one sentence.
- Place the descent of the revelation and the Hijra in their proper place in the story.

## How to read this trail
This trail is arranged to be read in about thirty minutes, and every step is a documented page in the museum. Read the summary on each page and then move on. Return to "Go deeper" whenever you wish.
Approximate duration: 30 minutes; 12 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".

## The steps

### 1. What is Islam?
[[concept/islam-din|Islam (the religion)]]: begin with the word itself. How the Prophet defined it in the hadith, and the supports on which it rests.

### 2. What is iman?
[[concept/iman|Iman]]: Islam is outward acts and iman is assent of the heart. Read how the hadith of Jibril distinguishes them, and note the position of the hadith scholars, as quoted on the page.

### 3. What is ihsan?
[[concept/ihsan|Ihsan]]: the third level in the same hadith, with which the order of the religion is completed.

### 4. The five pillars
[[concept/five-pillars|The five pillars of Islam]]: now you see the whole picture in one line, the five acts on which Islam is built.

### 5. The testimony
[[concept/shahada|The two testimonies]]: the first pillar, with which a person begins in Islam.

### 6. The prayer
[[practice/salah|The prayer]]: the second pillar. How it recurs through the day and night, and what separates the obligatory from the voluntary.

### 7. The zakat
[[practice/zakat|The zakat]]: read how the hadith distinguishes it from voluntary charity, and its recipients.

### 8. Fasting in Ramadan
[[practice/sawm-ramadan|Fasting in Ramadan]]: the fourth pillar. The month in which the Qur'an was revealed, and who is permitted to break the fast.

### 9. The Hajj
[[practice/hajj|The Hajj]]: the fifth pillar. The pilgrimage to the Sacred House for whoever is able, which leads you to Mecca.

### 10. Who is the Prophet?
[[person/muhammad|Muhammad]]: after the pillars, meet the bearer of the message as the museum presents him. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.479-487}

### 11. How did the message begin?
[[event/first-revelation|The beginning of the revelation in the cave of Hira]]: the cave of Hira and the descent of the revelation, the first moment of the story. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq}

### 12. The Hijra and the start of the calendar
[[event/hijra|The Prophet's Hijra]]: the event from which the Hijri calendar begins, and the end of the trail. {cite:ibn-ishaq-guillaume}
