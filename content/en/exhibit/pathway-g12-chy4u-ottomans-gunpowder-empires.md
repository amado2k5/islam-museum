## Overview
A CHY4U pathway on three Muslim empires that ruled from the Balkans to Bengal between 1450 and 1650: the conquest of Constantinople, Mehmed II and Suleiman, Istanbul and its mosques, the Safavids and the Mughals, and what gunpowder, administration and art the three shared.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes. The confessional difference between Ottomans and Safavids is shown by the museum with each position named before it is stated and without a verdict: a good place to teach 'historical perspective' from strand A.
