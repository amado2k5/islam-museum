## Overview
An HRT3M pathway on how a religion expresses itself in culture: the Arabic script and Islamic calligraphy, the arabesque and interlacing stars, the parts of a mosque, buildings from Jerusalem, Cordoba, Granada and Istanbul, and an ornamented scientific instrument.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes, best in a room with a screen: the museum's images come from licensed museums and from labelled illustrations. No images of people appear on this pathway, by the museum's rules.
