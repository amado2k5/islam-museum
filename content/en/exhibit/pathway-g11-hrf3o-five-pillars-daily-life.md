## Overview
An HRF3O pathway on the five pillars as they are lived day by day: the shahada, wudu and prayer, zakat, fasting in Ramadan and Eid al-Fitr, the Hajj and Eid al-Adha, and the calendar that orders the year.

## For the teacher
Sixty minutes. The pathway is descriptive: the museum issues no rulings and never says what anyone must do; differences between the schools on details are named, not settled. A guest who tells their own day works well.
