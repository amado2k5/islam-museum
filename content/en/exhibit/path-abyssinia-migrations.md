## The aim of this trail
To know why the two emigrations to Abyssinia happened, the names of their most prominent participants, and to see how the accounts present their return.

## What you will know at the end
- Distinguish the first emigration from the second and name what marks each.
- Name who sheltered them and who spoke for them before the king.
- See how the differing accounts of the emigrants' return are attributed to their sources.

## How to read this trail
This lesson is smaller and narrower than "The Meccan Period", focusing on one event and its people, and shows how the museum presents differing accounts of the same event.
Approximate duration: 25 minutes; 11 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".

## Before you begin

## The steps

### 1. Why Did They Leave?

### 2. The First Emigration
[[event/first-migration-to-abyssinia|The first emigration to Abyssinia]]: who left, when, and how.

### 3. The King Who Sheltered Them

### 4. The Second Emigration
[[event/second-migration-to-abyssinia|The second emigration to Abyssinia]]: who left a second time, and what Quraysh did.

### 5. Ja'far ibn Abi Talib
[[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]]: the one who spoke before the king on the emigrants' behalf.

### 6. The Emigrants to Abyssinia
[[concept/muhajirun-of-abyssinia|The emigrants of Abyssinia]]: how Ibn Ishaq classifies them by their return.

### 7. A Different Case
[[event/ubaydullah-ibn-jahsh-leaves-islam-in-abyssinia|Ubaydullah ibn Jahsh's conversion to Christianity in Abyssinia]]: not everyone who left remained Muslim, as Ibn Ishaq relates; note that the book does not date the event.

### 8. Umm Habiba
[[person/umm-habiba-bint-abi-sufyan|Umm Habiba Ramla bint Abi Sufyan]]: one who emigrated, returned, and became a Mother of the Believers.

### 9. Asma bint Umays
[[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma bint Umays]]: one of the women who emigrated with their husbands.

### 10. The Return
[[event/return-of-abyssinian-emigrants|The arrival of the Abyssinian emigrants with Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]]: its account on the page, according to Ibn Ishaq.

### 11. Another Account of Ja'far's Arrival
[[event/jafar-arrival-at-khaybar|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib's arrival to the Prophet at Khaybar]]: a report Ibn Hisham transmits from other than Ibn Ishaq; compare the two pages.
