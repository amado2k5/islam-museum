## The aim of this trail
To learn the roles of women in the first generation as they have reached us from the sources, and to place each one in her proper position in the story.

## What you will know at the end
- Recall what each of them is known for.
- Connect each woman to an event or a stage of the story.
- See how differences between traditions are presented in the biographies of some of them.

## How to read this trail
Each step is one woman and one documented page. On every page, judgements and accounts are attributed to those who voiced them. Where the traditions differ, the Sunni view is presented first, followed by the others, each named.
Approximate duration: 40 minutes; 12 steps. This is an experimental trail within "Tour and Learn".

## Before you begin

## The steps

### 1. Khadija
[[person/khadija|Khadija bint Khuwaylid]]: the beginning. In the account, the first to believe. {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}

### 2. Umm Ayman
[[person/umm-ayman|Umm Ayman Baraka bint Tha'laba]]: she who cared for the Prophet when he was young. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.57}

### 3. Sumayya bint Khayyat
[[person/sumayya-bint-khayyat|Sumayya bint Khayyat]]: she who held firm under torture in Mecca. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.90}

### 4. Asma bint Umays
[[person/asma-bint-umays|Asma bint Umays]]: she emigrated to Abyssinia. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala-abyari p.204-205}

### 5. Umm Habiba
[[person/umm-habiba-bint-abi-sufyan|Umm Habiba Ramla bint Abi Sufyan]]: she emigrated, and returned after years. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/38; cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}

### 6. Nusayba bint Ka'b
[[person/nusayba-bint-kab|Nusayba bint Ka'b]]: she who pledged at al-Aqaba and defended the Prophet on the day of Uhud. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.148}

### 7. Asma bint Abi Bakr
[[person/asma-bint-abi-bakr|Asma bint Abi Bakr]]: the one of the two waistbands, on the journey of the Hijra. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari}

### 8. Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib
[[person/safiyya-bint-abd-al-muttalib|Safiyya bint Abd al-Muttalib]]: the Prophet's aunt, and what is related of her on the day of the Trench. {cite:ibn-hajar-isaba p.1911}

### 9. Fatima
[[person/fatima|Fatima bint Muhammad]]: the Prophet's daughter. Note how she is remembered among Sunnis and among Shi'a. {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}

### 10. A'isha
[[person/aisha|A'isha bint Abi Bakr]]: among the most prolific narrators of hadith. Read the perspectives section on the page, with the Sunni view first. {cite:watt-1956-medina}

### 11. Hafsa
[[person/hafsa|Hafsa bint Umar]]: and what is related of her in the preservation of the mushaf. {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}

### 12. Umm Salama
[[person/umm-salama|Umm Salama Hind bint Abi Umayya]]: and her counsel on the day of al-Hudaybiyya. {cite:ibn-sad-tabaqat-bewley}
