## Overview
A CHW3M pathway on the century that changed the Muslim world: the Mongol invasions, the fall of Baghdad in 1258, the Mamluk stand at Ayn Jalut, the Ilkhans' conversion, Ibn Khaldun reading the collapse of states, and Timur's Samarqand.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes for advanced classes; level 'deep'. The focus is evidence: conflicting numbers, sources in more than one language, and what a historian does with them.
