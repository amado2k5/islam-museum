## Overview
A path through the documented reports of the Prophet: it gathers [[passage/bukhari-0190|hadiths from Sahih al-Bukhari]] and Sahih Muslim in successive rooms, from the revelation to the departure, with their narrators and chains. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
The first display in the museum built entirely on passage records gathered from the texts of the collections. The visitor follows it as a primary source reading. {cite:wikidata}
