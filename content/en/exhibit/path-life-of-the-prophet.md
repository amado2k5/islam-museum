## Overview
A path through the narrative exhibition of the life of the Prophet Muhammad: from his birth in [[place/mecca|Mecca]] to his death in [[place/medina|Medina]], marking the main stations such as the [[event/hijra|Hijra]], [[event/battle-of-badr|Badr]] and the [[event/conquest-of-mecca|Conquest]]. {cite:wikidata}

## Impact
It presents the biography of the Prophet to the visitor in one sitting in sequence; each station links to the record of its entity and to its sources. {cite:wikidata}
