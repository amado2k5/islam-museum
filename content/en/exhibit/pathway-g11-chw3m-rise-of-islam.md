## Overview
A CHW3M pathway following the rise of a new community in Arabia and its transformation within a century into a state stretching from al-Andalus to Central Asia: the setting, the revelation, the Hijra, the Constitution of Medina, the first battles, the Rashidun caliphate and Umayyad Damascus.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes over a lesson and a half: the eight steps are read in pairs, leaving twenty minutes for the worksheet. Traditional dates are marked as traditional in the museum: a good way into the concept of evidence in strand A.
