## Overview
A CHW3M pathway on al-Andalus: Umayyad Cordoba, its mosque and library, Ziryab and Ibn Rushd, the coexistence and tension of three faiths, then Granada, the Alhambra and the end of the emirate in 1492.

## For the teacher
Seventy-five minutes. The question of 'coexistence' is discussed from the museum's sources in the form 'what do the sources say', not 'who was right'; the museum gives no verdict and students learn to do the same.
