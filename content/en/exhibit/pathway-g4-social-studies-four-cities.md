## Overview
A Grade 4 lesson on four cities of societies before 1500: how people lived there, who governed, how they learned and traded, and what is like our life today and what is different.

## For the teacher
Sixty minutes: ten for the four cities on a projected map, thirty for the steps in groups (one city per group), twenty for the worksheet and the class comparison. Pair the lesson with a First Nation and an Inuit society, as the strand requires.
