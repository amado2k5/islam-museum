## Summary

*The Qur'an and Late Antiquity: A Shared Heritage* is a book by Angelika Neuwirth; its English translation by Samuel Wilder was published by Oxford University Press in 2019 in 552 pages. {cite:openlibrary-quran-and-late-antiquity-2019} Its German original, *Der Koran als Text der Spätantike*, was published by Verlag der Weltreligionen in 2010. {cite:openlibrary-der-koran-als-text-der-spatantike-2010}

## Publication and editions

The catalogue files the book under subjects including the history of the Qur'an, its textual criticism, its manuscripts, its relation to the Bible and the Qur'an as literature. {cite:openlibrary-quran-and-late-antiquity-2019} {cite:openlibrary-der-koran-als-text-der-spatantike-2010}
