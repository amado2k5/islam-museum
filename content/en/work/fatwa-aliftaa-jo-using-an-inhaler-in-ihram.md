## Summary

A question about using an inhaler while in ihram, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that a person in ihram may use steam, sprays and inhalers containing aromatic substances for treatment, and owes no fidya. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4296}

## The question

The questioner asked about a person in ihram using an inhaler containing medicines with various herbal ingredients. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4296}

## The answer

The Department answered that a person in ihram may use steam treatments, sprays and inhalers containing aromatic substances for treatment, because the aim is treatment rather than perfuming, and he owes no fidya for it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4296} It quoted Hashiyat al-Bujayrimi ala al-Khatib (2/455) that what is meant for eating or treatment is not forbidden to a person in ihram and carries no fidya, even if it has a pleasant scent. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4296}

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4296, dated 25 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4296} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
