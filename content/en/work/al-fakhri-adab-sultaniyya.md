## Summary
Ibn al-Tiqtaqa's history, composed at Mosul between Jumada II and Shawwal 701/1302 for Fakhr al-Din Isa ibn Ibrahim: a brief mirror for princes followed by lives of the caliphs down to al-Musta'sim, each with the lives of that caliph's viziers; edited by Ahlwardt (Gotha 1860) and Derenbourg (Paris 1895), with French and English translations {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 3 p. 988}.
