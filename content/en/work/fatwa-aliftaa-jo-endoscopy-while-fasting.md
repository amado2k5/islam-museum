## Summary

A question about endoscopy during the day in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that endoscopy through any passage invalidates the fast and that the patient must make the day up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4142}

## The question

The questioner asked whether undergoing endoscopy during the day in Ramadan affects the validity of the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4142}

## The answer

The Department answered that endoscopy during the day in Ramadan, whether through the mouth, the nose, the front passage or the rear passage, invalidates the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4142} It said that the person who undergoes it must make that day up after Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4142}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4142, dated 1 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4142} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
