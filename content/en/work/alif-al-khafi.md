## Overview
The novel by G. Willow Wilson (2012): Alif, an Arab hacker, protects his clients from the surveillance of the digital state until an obscure manuscript reaches him, carrying words that defy the zeros and ones. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It blended science fiction with Arab magic in a novel that won awards, written by an American Muslim who knows Arabic from within. {cite:wikidata}
