## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with five episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} He opens the first episode by saying that he spoke on the previous occasions about a wife's rights over her husband, and that today, as promised, he speaks about a husband's rights over his wife. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep01 00:00:30} In the fourth episode he sums up the 'series of talks on rights' so far: the wife's right over her husband, the husband's right over his wife, and the right of a Muslim over his fellow Muslim. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep04 00:01:00} In the fifth he refers back to what he promised 'last Friday', which shows that the episodes are successive Friday sermons. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-hoquq/ep05 00:01:00}
