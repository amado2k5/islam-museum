## Overview
Isma'il b. 'Umar b. Kathir al-Dimashqi's Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim; among the most famous hadith-based exegeses, it explains verses by verses, by the traditions, and by the sayings of the Companions and Successors.

## In «Asdaf al-Lu'lu'» by Abu Ishaq al-Heweny
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] says he has a work on Ibn Kathir's tafsir and relies on the tafsirs that carry chains. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep28 00:03:00} It is a takhrij of the tafsir's hadiths, which he carried from the beginning of al-Fatiha to verse 173 of Surat al-Baqara; he says that had he completed it, it would have run to about fifty volumes, so he stopped and moved the long studies into his work on Ibn Abi Hatim's «al-Ilal». {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:14:00} From this work came the idea of his book on al-Bukhari's omissions. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep23 00:30:00} He mentions that al-Wahidi's «al-Wasit» is on his desk because it carries chains and he needs it for his work on Ibn Kathir. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep30 00:05:00}

He says Ibn Kathir's tafsir was the spark for another project: to gather everything the early generations said in explaining each verse, in volumes where each verse has its own numbered leaf on which he records every place a chain-bearing book mentions it, and then to compare this with the reports Ibn Kathir missed. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:20:30} He named it «Kanz al-Bakhil bi-ma Warada an al-Salaf min Tafsir Ay al-Tanzil»; it has reached fifteen volumes of references alone, he has given it more than three years, and he expects it to approach a hundred volumes; he gathered his children and the women of his household and explained the plan so that they could complete it if he died first. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:26:30} For soundness of creed he recommends the tafsirs that scholars rely on, such as Ibn Kathir's. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep29 00:07:00}
