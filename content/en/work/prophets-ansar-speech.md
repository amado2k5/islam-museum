## Overview
A speech that [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]] addressed to the Ansar alone, in the enclosure at al-Ji'rana, after the division of the spoils of Hunayn. It came to comfort their hearts. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

One of the questions in it: "Are you not content that people should go away with the sheep and the camel, while you return with the Messenger of God to your dwellings?" {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The data dates it to Dhu al-Qa'da 8 AH; this is a derived date and needs a second source. The speech has its own event page: [[event/prophets-speech-to-the-ansar|the Prophet's speech to the Ansar]].
