## Summary

*The Mediation of Ornament* is a book by Oleg Grabar based on his 1989 A. W. Mellon Lectures in the Fine Arts, published by Princeton University Press in 1992 in the Bollingen Series. {cite:cca-mediation-of-ornament} As the jacket summary puts it, Grabar defines ornaments as agents not logically necessary to perceiving a visual message, without which understanding would be more difficult; they often draw the viewer into a work by heightening the pleasure of looking. {cite:cca-mediation-of-ornament} He analyses early and medieval Islamic objects, from recently discovered frontispieces in Yemen to the tilework of the Alhambra, and compares them with Western examples. {cite:cca-mediation-of-ornament}

Its five chapters are: A Theory of Intermediaries in Art; The Intermediary of Writing; The Intermediary of Geometry; The Intermediary of Architecture; and The Intermediary of Nature. {cite:cca-mediation-of-ornament}

## Publication and editions

It appeared in Princeton in 1992 in xxv + 284 illustrated pages, some in colour, with a bibliography (pages 239–279) and an index. {cite:cca-mediation-of-ornament} Its ISBN is 0691040990. {cite:openlibrary-mediation-of-ornament-1992}
