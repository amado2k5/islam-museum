## Summary
'Osalli Alayh' ('I Invoke Blessings on Him') is a short television programme presented by [[person/ahmed-abdussalam|Ahmed Abdussalam]], of which the Assabile archive keeps twenty-two video episodes of nine to twelve minutes each, the speaker talking alone. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep14 00:00:30} He calls the programme 'Osalli Alayh' and also calls the episodes 'This is the Messenger of God', describing them as meetings short in form and great in meaning. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep16 00:00:30} Each episode opens and closes with a short sung refrain addressing the Prophet with blessing and longing, and in the second episode the speaker prays for the listeners, the viewers and the crew of the channel. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:06:30} In the eleventh he says the title does not mean invoking blessings in words alone, but watching how the Prophet lived at home, spoke and prayed, and following his example. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:09:00}

The episodes move from the duties of the testimony to his messengerhood (1) to the meaning and merit of [[concept/al-salat-ala-al-nabi|invoking blessings on the Prophet]] (2-5, 7), then to [[concept/mahabbat-al-nabi|love of the Prophet]]: God's love for him, his love for his Lord in prayer and night vigil, and the love of Companions, an animal and inanimate things for him (3, 6-17), then prophetic counsels on seeking refuge (18-20), knowledge (21) and right guidance (22). The archive gives no broadcast date and the episodes name none. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam}

## The episodes
**Episode 1: the duties of the testimony.** He says the Prophet's way is the nearest and easiest way to Paradise, and that testifying to his messengerhood is not enough as words until deeds follow. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:02:30} He lists five duties, repeating them for viewers to learn by heart: to obey him in what he commanded, to avoid what he forbade, to believe him in what he reported, to put his word before every other word, and to worship God only as he laid down; the detail is in [[concept/ittiba|following]]. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:06:00} He makes the Prophet a model in every side of life, as a husband, with his family and with his neighbours, and explains that the Prophet's 'family' in the prayer means his followers. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:09:30}

**Episode 2: the meaning of blessing, peace and grace.** He says worship is not done with words one does not understand, so he explains what it means to pray for the Prophet with blessing, peace and grace, and relates from [[person/abdullah-ibn-abbas|Ibn Abbas]] that God created no soul more honoured before Him than Muhammad. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:01:30} The detail is in [[concept/al-salat-ala-al-nabi|invoking blessings on the Prophet]]. On the meaning of peace he tells how, long after the Prophet's death, two men tried to dig a tunnel under the ground to his grave to steal his body, and God made the ruler of the land aware of it. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep02 00:05:30} (needs_source: a story the speaker tells without naming the two men, the ruler or the time.)

**Episode 3: God's love for the Prophet.** He holds that invoking blessings on the Prophet calms hearts: when two men quarrel and are told to invoke blessings on the Prophet, tempers settle. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:01:00} As signs of God's love for His Prophet he gives God's oath by his life and by the town in which he lived, saying that the negative particle in the oath is redundant and that the town is Mecca. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:03:00} He adds that God blesses ten times whoever blesses him once, that God began with blessing him, then the angels, then commanded the believers, and notes that the command is addressed to 'those who believe'. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:06:30} He says a convert's Islam is not valid until he testifies to the messengerhood, nor the call to prayer without that testimony. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:08:00} In his view the call to prayer is a fixed act of worship, so the word 'sayyidina' is not added before the Prophet's name in it. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep03 00:08:30}

**Episode 4: Ubayy ibn Ka'b.** He relates the hadith of [[person/ubayy-ibn-kab|Ubayy ibn Ka'b]], who asked the Prophet how much of his prayer he should give to him; each time he offered more the Prophet said more would be better for him, until he said he would give it all, and the Prophet told him his worry would then be sufficed and his sin forgiven. He glosses the quarter, half and two-thirds as minutes of an hour. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:03:00} He lists people's worries today: children, illness, a spouse, a neighbour, the economy, fear of tomorrow. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:05:00} To someone who invokes blessings and finds his worry growing, he says the fault lies in his own prayer, if it is letters on the lips whose meaning he does not know. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:07:00} He ends with the hadith that the people nearest the Prophet on the Day of Resurrection are those who invoke blessings on him most, and links it to intercession. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep04 00:08:00}

**Episode 5: the Messenger as a gift.** He cites the hadith of Gabriel that a single blessing writes ten good deeds, erases ten bad ones and raises ten degrees. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:01:30} He then stops at the verse of Al Imran on God's favour to the believers in sending them a messenger from among themselves [[passage/quran-3-164|(Al Imran 164)]], and says that whoever has read it many times without his heart stopping at it has not reflected on it; the detail is in [[concept/tadabbur-al-quran|reflection on the Qur'an]]. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:04:00} He reads the 'purifying' in the verse as upbringing before teaching, and 'the Book and the wisdom' as the Qur'an and the Sunna. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep05 00:09:00}

**Episode 6: the first whose grave opens.** He says the Prophet is the first whose grave will open, the first to knock at the gate of Paradise, and that his community is the last of the communities and the first to enter Paradise. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:03:00} He dwells on the Prophet answering the keeper of Paradise with his bare name, and sees in it a humility set against the arrogance of the powerful in this world. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:04:30} He reads the command to lower one's wing to the believers as meaning that whoever follows the Sunna more closely, a wife or a daughter, deserves more kindness. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:06:30} He then says the reality of faith hangs on full acceptance of the Prophet's judgment, citing the hadith of the triple oath about one whose neighbour is not safe from his harm. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:08:00} As examples of submitting to that judgment he gives, in his own terms, a woman's covering in a wide cloak, giving up smoking and lowering the gaze. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep06 00:09:00}

**Episode 7: the Prophet's love for his Lord.** He says invoking blessings on the Prophet is obligatory and that whoever hears his name and does not is at fault, and that people even in cafés invoke blessings when they hear it. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:01:30} He holds that the Prophet loved God more than any creature, citing his words to Bilal to give them rest through prayer and his habit of praying whenever something troubled him. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:08:30} He recalls the hadith that women and perfume were made dear to him and the coolness of his eye placed in prayer, and counts keeping a bottle of perfume at home among the neglected sunnas. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:08:00} He says that when he prayed alone he would prostrate for as long as it takes one of us to recite fifty verses, though he told imams to keep prayer light, and that once he prostrated so long one of his wives thought he had died. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep07 00:10:30}

**Episode 8: the night vigil.** He recalls that the Prophet stood at night until his feet swelled, that [[person/aisha|Aisha]] asked him why, and that he answered he wished to be a grateful servant, God having told him in Surat al-Fath that his past and future sins were forgiven [[passage/quran-48-2|(al-Fath 2)]]; the detail is in [[practice/qiyam-al-layl|the night vigil]]. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:02:30} He then relates what Aisha told of the most wonderful thing she saw from him: he came to her on her night, then asked her leave to worship his Lord, and stood weeping until his tears wet his beard and the ground. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:08:30} He makes bringing joy to one's wife an act of obedience, even with a small thing, a smile or a kind word. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep08 00:05:30}

**Episode 9: closer to the believers than themselves.** Reading the verse that the Prophet is closer to the believers than their own selves, he says that whoever hands himself over to the Prophet is in safe hands that lead only to good. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:03:00} He repeats the five duties and warns against relying on a living man who will die, a rich man who may grow poor or a man of power who may be dismissed. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:08:30} He ends with obedience to the Messenger as obedience to God, citing the hadith on it from al-Bukhari and Muslim. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep09 00:10:30}

**Episode 10: the Companions' love.** He relates from [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]] that the Prophet was dearer to them than their fathers, mothers, children and wealth, and than cold water to the thirsty, and pictures a man lost in the desert. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:03:00} He relates [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar]]'s saying that the Prophet was dearer to him than everything except himself, and his turning to say he was dearer even than himself. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:04:30} He tells of an Ansari woman who heard the Prophet had been killed and asked after him before her father, brother, son and husband, and on seeing him said every calamity after him was light. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:06:30} He ends with the answer of [[person/khubayb-ibn-adi|Khubayb ibn Adi]] as a captive under torture. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep10 00:08:00}

**Episode 11: the aim, and the home.** He says a Muslim's aim is that God be pleased with him and admit him to Paradise, and that money, buildings and cars are means that should not rule the heart. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:01:30} He holds that a husband will be asked about his wife's prayer, so on coming home he should first ask whether she has prayed before asking about food. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:04:30} He warns a wife against putting her husband's command before the Prophet's, and says each spouse should grow in kindness to the other the nearer the other draws to God. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:07:30} He recalls that the Prophet at home was at the service of his family, so a man should not think it shameful to help his wife with housework. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep11 00:09:00}

**Episode 12: mercy and love.** He relates that when the Prophet was wounded at Uhud and his tooth broken, some Companions asked him to curse his people, and he said he had not been sent to curse but to call and as a mercy, and prayed for their forgiveness and guidance. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:01:00} He then tells the stories of [[person/abu-qatada-al-ansari|Abu Qatada]], who propped the Prophet up on his mount through a whole night, and of [[person/urwa-ibn-masud|Urwa ibn Mas'ud al-Thaqafi]] at al-Hudaybiyya. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:06:30} He ends by asking viewers to pledge to keep the five prayers on time, beginning with the dawn prayer. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep12 00:09:30}

**Episode 13: Abu Bakr.** He relates that the Prophet told people one morning he had been taken by night from the Sacred Mosque to al-Aqsa, and some went to [[person/abu-bakr|Abu Bakr]], who said that if he had said it, he had spoken the truth; the detail is in [[event/isra-and-miraj|the Isra and Mi'raj]]. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:03:00} He says love has degrees, citing the hadith that of two who love each other for God the better is the one who loves the other more. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:03:30} He then tells of the conversion of [[person/abu-quhafa|Abu Quhafa]] on the day Mecca was conquered and Abu Bakr's tears, and of [[person/abu-talib|Abu Talib]] dying on his fathers' religion. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:10:00} He closes with a warning against bad company. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep13 00:10:30}

## About these recordings
The texts used here are machine transcripts (Qwen3-ASR, thirty-second windows) read window by window, and only what reads clearly was written. Hadiths, supplications and formulas of blessing are described in the museum's words without their wording, and verses whose sura he does not name are not linked. The stories he tells are attributed to him, and the rulings he states are his views. Passages on sectarian disputes, on the ruling for one who insults the Prophet, on polygamy and on some descriptions of men and women were left out, because they need more than one documented voice. {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-ahmed-abdussalam ossali-alayh/ep22 00:10:00}
