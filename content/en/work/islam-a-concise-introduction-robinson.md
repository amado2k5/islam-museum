## Summary

*Islam: A Concise Introduction* is an English book by Neal Robinson, published by Curzon in 1999 in 197 pages and later issued by Georgetown University Press in Washington. {cite:google-books-islam-concise-introduction-robinson} The publisher's description calls it a concise yet panoramic overview, unlike most introductory books, which focus on Islamic institutions in the classical period: it discusses the problems raised by Western perceptions of Islam and gives a brief account of Islamic history to the present, then explains major topics of worship. According to its contents, its chapters treat defining Islam, Islam in history and in the modern world, the Qur'an, the shahada, Muhammad the Messenger of God, ritual prayer, zakat, Ramadan, the pilgrimage, the Shari'a, and denominations and sects. {cite:archive-org-islam-concise-introduction-robinson-georgetown}
