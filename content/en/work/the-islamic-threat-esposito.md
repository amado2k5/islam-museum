## Summary

*The Islamic Threat: Myth or Reality?* is an English book by John L. Esposito, published by Oxford University Press in New York in 1992 in 243 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islamic-threat-1992} According to the contents of the 1993 edition, its chapters treat contemporary Islam between reformation and revolution; the roots of conflict, cooperation and confrontation between Islam and the West; Muslim responses to Western dominance; the dynamics of resurgence in the relation of Islam and the state; Islamic organisations; and finally "Islamic fundamentalism" and the West. {cite:archive-org-islamic-threat-esposito-1993}
