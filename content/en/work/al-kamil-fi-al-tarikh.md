## Overview
"Al-Kamil fil-Tarikh" (The Complete Book of History) by Izz al-Din Ibn al-Athir al-Jazari, who died in 630 AH. It is a general history arranged by years, from the beginning of creation to his own days. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 137}

## Its content
He relied on the history of al-Tabari and added to it accounts of the Maghrib, al-Andalus, the Atabeg state and the Crusades. "Shadharat al-Dhahab" says that it is the well-known history arranged by events and years, in ten volumes, and one of the best histories. It begins from the first time up to the year 629 AH. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 137}
