## Summary

*Knowledge and the Sacred* is the text of the Gifford Lectures that Seyyed Hossein Nasr gave at the University of Edinburgh, published by Edinburgh University Press in 1981 in 341 pages. In the preface he says he was the first Muslim, indeed the first Easterner, to give the lectures since they began at Edinburgh in 1889, and that he chose to present the traditional perspective rather than an Eastern version of modern ideas. {cite:openlibrary-knowledge-and-the-sacred-1981} {cite:archive-org-knowledge-and-the-sacred-nasr-text}

## Publication and editions

The book has ten chapters. It begins with knowledge and its desacralization, the question 'What is tradition?' and the revival of tradition, then scientia sacra, man pontifical and Promethean, the cosmos as theophany, eternity and the temporal order, traditional art and the multiplicity of sacred forms, and ends with knowledge of the sacred as deliverance. {cite:archive-org-knowledge-and-the-sacred-nasr-text}
