## Summary
"The Book on Knowing the Ascensions of the Signs between the Quadrants of the Sphere" is a treatise by [[person/al-battani|al-Battani]] listed by [[person/ibn-al-nadim|Ibn al-Nadim]], [[person/al-qifti|al-Qifti]] and [[person/ibn-khallikan|Ibn Khallikan]] among his writings; it has not reached us. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 17} {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 18} {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28}

## Its subject
Orientalists understood the title variously: de Slane rendered it "knowledge of the rising-places of the zodiacal signs in the interval between the quarters of the sphere" and guessed it computed the amplitude of the signs for each latitude; Steinschneider translated it without grasping its matter and thought the epistle on applications and the tract on the rising of the triplicities were parts of it; Suter rendered it "on the risings of the houses according to the four quadrants". {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28} Nallino says the title makes plain that it treated the subject of chapter 55 of [[work/al-zij-al-sabi|the Zij]] and its spurious appendix — the computation of astrological directions — though it is unknown whether it offered a new solution more exact geometrically than Ptolemy's. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28} He thinks the same problem must also have appeared in his [[work/sharh-al-maqalat-al-arba-battani|commentary on the Tetrabiblos]]. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 28}

## Confusion with the epistle on applications
Some took this book to be identical with [[work/risalat-tahqiq-aqdar-al-ittisalat|the Epistle on Verifying the Quantities of Applications]], reading Ibn al-Nadim as saying it was "known" by that name; Nallino rejects this on grounds of Arabic grammar, because Ibn al-Nadim was adding a further famous epistle after listing the books, and because the two problems are wholly different. {cite:nallino-albatenii-opus-astronomicum p. 29}
