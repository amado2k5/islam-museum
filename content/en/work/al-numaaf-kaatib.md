## Overview
The novel by Khaled Hosseini (2003): Amir and Hassan are two boys in Kabul before the Soviets, between them a kite and a betrayal that follows Amir to America and then returns him to Taliban Afghanistan to atone. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The first Afghan-American novel to top the world's charts; it became a film and a play, and opened Afghan diaspora literature to Western readers. {cite:wikidata}
