## Overview
A book of translated tales. Al-Mas'udi lists it among the made-up fables that compilers had woven together, alongside "Hazar Afsana" and [[work/kitab-farza-wa-simas|the book of Farza and Simas]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.550}

Ibn al-Nadim lists two copies of it, a large one and a small one. He says that the disagreement about it is like the disagreement about "Kalila wa Dimna", and that the more probable view, and the one closer to the truth, is that India composed it. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim p.424}
