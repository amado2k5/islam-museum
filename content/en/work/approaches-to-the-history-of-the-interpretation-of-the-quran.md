## Summary

*Approaches to the History of the Interpretation of the Qur'an* is a collective volume in English edited by Andrew Rippin. {cite:openlibrary-approaches-interpretation-quran-1988} Its chapters are revised and enlarged versions of papers first presented at a conference on the history of the interpretation of the Qur'an, held at the University of Calgary in April 1985. {cite:openlibrary-approaches-interpretation-quran-1988} The Library of Congress catalogue classes it under the history of the criticism and interpretation of the Qur'an. {cite:openlibrary-approaches-interpretation-quran-1988}

## Publication and editions

Clarendon Press, part of Oxford University Press, published it in Oxford and New York in 1988, in 334 pages, with bibliographical references and indexes. {cite:openlibrary-approaches-interpretation-quran-1988}
