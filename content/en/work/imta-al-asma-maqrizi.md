## Overview
"Imta al-Asma fima lil-Nabi min al-Hafada wal-Mata" (Delighting the Ears with What the Prophet Had of Grandchildren and Possessions) by Taqi al-Din Ahmad ibn Ali al-Maqrizi, the historian, who died in 845 AH. Hajji Khalifa describes it as a precious book in six volumes, which al-Maqrizi related to others in Mecca. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.625}
