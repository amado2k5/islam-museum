# IslamNews

## Summary

IslamNews is a Russian-language Muslim news portal covering the social, political, economic and cultural life of Muslims in Russia and abroad. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru}

## Origins

It was created in 2002 by Sobranie, an association of cultural and educational societies whose board is chaired by M. A. Salyakhetdinov; its editor-in-chief is Rinat Nizametdinov. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru} The earliest archived capture of the site, of 13 June 2002, is titled 'Islamic News', issue 6 (39) for June 2002, says it has been 'published since April 1999', and has sections for latest news, Russia and the CIS, foreign news, articles, history lessons, interviews and a scholar's opinion. {cite:wayback-islamnews-2002}

## 'Halal Money'

In 2009 the portal, with the WebMoney system, launched an electronic payment system for Muslims called 'Halal Money', for paying for goods the Sharia permits. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru}

## Views of it

The philologist R. L. Iskhakov counts it among the leading Muslim media in Russia. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru} On the other side, the legal scholars N. V. Volodina, V. V. Merkuryev and S. V. Borisov, in a 2016 book on extremism, reported R. Suleimanov's opinion that the views of religious extremists are pressed through the site and through some Muslim journalists with radical Islamist positions. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru} In 2016 V. Danilevskaya, a correspondent of the Agency of Political News, wrote that articles on the site criticised a Russian academic for putting a book on 'sect studies' on a university reading list, and saw in this a Scientologist provocation carried out through Islamic media. The museum gives these views as their authors put them, without a verdict. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru}

## The icon advertisements

In 2008 advertisements for icons and wooden crosses appeared on the portal; one of the people behind the icon project saw it as a sign of better relations between faiths, and the editor Nizametdinov said they had appeared by mistake, in the stream of Yandex's contextual advertising. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru}

## Today

The site is at islamnews.ru; it has a Russian Wikipedia article and the Wikidata item Q4041642. {cite:wikipedia-islamnews-ru}
