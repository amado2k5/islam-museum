## Summary
*Intiqad al-I'tirad* is a book [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-'Asqalani]] began in answer to [[person/badr-al-din-al-ayni|al-'Ayni]]'s objections, in [[work/umdat-al-qari|'Umdat al-Qari]], to [[work/fath-al-bari|Fath al-Bari]]. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 16} The biography printed with 'Umdat al-Qari says he wrote out the objections and left blank space beneath them for his replies, but died without answering most of them, although he corrected some places in his commentary afterwards, and although he lived five years after al-'Ayni completed his. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 16}

Not covered here: Ibn Hajar's own account of the book, which was not read in this source.
