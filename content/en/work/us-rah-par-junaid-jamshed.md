## Summary
Us Rah Par ("On That Path") is the second solo album of the Pakistani pop singer [[person/junaid-jamshed|Junaid Jamshed]], released in 1999 on Sony BMG after he and the rest of Vital Signs took a break, recorded in 1998–1999 and produced by himself. {cite:wikipedia-us-rah-par-en} {cite:wikidata-us-rah-par} The museum records it because Junaid Jamshed is in the Modern Islam Browser for his naats, under the founder's decision of 7 November 2026.

## The album
It was a critical and commercial success and continued his work with the writer [[person/shoaib-mansoor|Shoaib Mansoor]], as on the Vital Signs albums; the title song is a slow blues number with a video directed by Bilal Maqsood of Strings, but the techno-flavoured 'Na Tu Aaegi' gave the album its commercial push, and three songs were composed by Biddu. {cite:wikipedia-us-rah-par-en} It has twelve songs, among them 'Aankhon Ko Aankhon Ne' and Salman Ahmad's 'Jaagi Re'. {cite:wikipedia-us-rah-par-en}

## Afterwards
The Express Tribune wrote in 2017, after his death, that he used 'Us Rah Par' to foreshadow his transformation: he left pop after his next album, [[work/dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed|Dil Ki Baat]], and turned to naat with [[work/jalwa-e-janan-junaid-jamshed|Jalwa-e-Janan]] in 2005. {cite:wikipedia-us-rah-par-en} {cite:wikipedia-dil-ki-baat-junaid-jamshed-en} {cite:wikipedia-jalwa-e-janan-en}
