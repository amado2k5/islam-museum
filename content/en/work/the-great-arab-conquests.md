## Summary

*The Great Arab Conquests: How the Spread of Islam Changed the World We Live In* is a book by Hugh Kennedy, published by Weidenfeld & Nicolson in London in 2007 in 421 pages and in the same year by Da Capo Press in Philadelphia. {cite:openlibrary-great-arab-conquests-2007}

## Publication and editions

The publisher's description says that in little more than a century after 632 the Arab conquests reached from Spain to Central Asia, overthrowing the Sasanian empire and reducing Byzantium, and calls the book the first English-language account of them for general readers in many years. {cite:archive-org-great-arab-conquests-kennedy}
