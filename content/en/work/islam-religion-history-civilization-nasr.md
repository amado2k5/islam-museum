## Summary

*Islam: Religion, History, and Civilization* is an English book by the thinker Seyyed Hossein Nasr, published by HarperSanFrancisco on 24 December 2002 in 224 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islam-religion-history-civilization-2002} The catalogue record describes it as a concise introduction to the history, beliefs and practice of Islam. According to its contents, its chapters treat Islam and the Islamic world, Islam as religion, its doctrines and beliefs, its dimensions, its practices, ethics and institutions, a brief journey through Islamic history, the schools of Islamic thought, and Islam in the contemporary world. {cite:archive-org-islam-religion-history-civilization-2003}
