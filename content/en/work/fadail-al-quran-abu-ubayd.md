## Summary
The bibliography of the King Fahd edition of the Itqan lists Fada'il al-Qur'an wa-Ma'alimuh wa-Adabuh by Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam, edited by Ahmad ibn 'Abd al-Wahid al-Khayyati, Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs, Morocco, 1415 AH. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 3003} It is not the book of readings by Abu 'Ubayd that the Fihrist appears to list (the OCR reads the name as 'Ibn Ubayd al-Qasim') and in which, Makki says, he added to the seven. {cite:fihrist-ibn-al-nadim-rahmaniyya-cairo p. 65} {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 47}

## Its chapters on writing mushafs
The notes of the Itqan refer to chapters of it by title: 'the book of the mushafs and what is preferred in their largeness and disliked in their smallness' (vol. 2 p. 236, no. 912), 'adorning the mushafs' (pp. 234-235), 'dotting the mushafs and what is allowed and disliked in it' (p. 230, no. 894), and 'dividing the mushafs into tens, the openings of suras and the heads of verses' (p. 232, no. 900). {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2240} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2243} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2246}

## Examples
Al-Suyuti draws from it that 'Umar found a man with a mushaf in a fine pen, disliked it and said 'honour the Book of God', and that 'Ali disliked the Qur'an being written on something small. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2240} Also that Ibn 'Abbas, Abu Dharr and Abu al-Darda' disliked adorning a mushaf with gold, and that Ibn Mas'ud, when a gilded mushaf was shown to him, said the best adornment of a mushaf is reciting it rightly. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2243} And that Ibn Mas'ud said 'strip the Qur'an and mix nothing with it', that al-Nakha'i disliked dotting mushafs, and that Ibn Sirin disliked dotting and the openings and closings. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2246}
