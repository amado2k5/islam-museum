## Overview
The History is the book of [[person/khalifa-ibn-khayyat|Khalifa ibn Khayyat]] of Basra (d. 240 AH). Its editor, Akram Diya' al-Umari, calls it the oldest annalistic history to have reached us, since the books of annals written before it are lost. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 3} He sees its value in its accuracy and its careful choice of reports, each preceded by a chain of transmission in the manner of the hadith scholars, to whose school Khalifa belonged. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 3}

## Contents
The book moves year by year from the Hijra to the events of 232 AH. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 14} Khalifa takes care to give tables of the governors and officials at the end of each reign and to name the martyrs of the campaigns and civil wars, and he alone preserves certain reports on the battles of al-Harra and al-Zawiya. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 3}

## The manuscript
The book reached us in the transmission of Baqi ibn Makhlad of Cordoba from Khalifa. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 7} It was found in the zawiya of Tamgrout in Morocco and later moved to the General Library in Rabat, no. 199: a unique copy of 336 pages in Maghribi script, copied by Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Ash'ari in 477 AH. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 3} {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 34} It was first printed in Iraq in 1386/1967, then in Damascus in 1397/1977. {cite:tarikh-khalifa-umari-1985 intro p. 4}
