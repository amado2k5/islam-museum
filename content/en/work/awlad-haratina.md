## Overview
The novel by Naguib Mahfouz (1959), which provoked the greatest Arabic literary controversy of the twentieth century. Allegorical characters of an Egyptian alley echo the stories of the prophets from Adam to Muhammad, passing through Moses and Jesus, so that the history of religions is read as the history of a popular quarter. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
It was banned from publication as a book in Egypt until it was printed in Beirut and then Cairo, and it has been translated into dozens of languages; today it is read as a key to the relationship between religion and politics in the modern Arab imagination. {cite:wikidata}
