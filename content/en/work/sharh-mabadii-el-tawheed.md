## Overview
«Sharh Mabadi' al-Tawhid» (a commentary on the principles of tawhid) is a series of twelve episodes by [[person/wagdy-ghoneim|Wagdy Ghoneim]] preserved in the Assabile archive's audio-lesson section; the series page lists twelve lessons and all twelve have been treated in this work: the three divisions of tawhid — the unity of essence, names and attributes, lordship, and divinity — then the text's points on manifestations of shirk: following desires, taking allies other than God, detailed manifestations such as calling on other than God, intercession and its kinds, vows and their rulings and expiation, means of approach and their kinds, the attributes exclusive to divinity, the worship of the dead and of natural phenomena, Satan and his war on the children of Adam, and the series closes with the two testimonies, the refutation of those who belittle them, and proofs of divinity. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed} The lecturer says the episodes follow a creed text on tawhid and comment on its numbered points; what is reported here is the lecturer's account as the machine transcript shows it. The literal transcript text is in places unintelligible or repetitive, so the views and rulings he mentions are told as his narration rather than as independently verified facts, and any line that cannot be read clearly is marked as needing a source.

**Episode 2: the three divisions of tawhid.** The lecturer opens by saying tawhid has three divisions: the unity of essence, names and attributes; lordship; and divinity, and that each requires that none be associated with God. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:01:00} He treats the first division — the unity of essence, names and attributes — saying God's essence is one, not composite or multiple, and criticises the Christian formula "three in one and one in three" with the analogy of a table that gathers many things, citing Surat al-Ikhlas "Say: He is God, One", [[passage/quran-112-1|al-Ikhlas, verse 1]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:04:00} He mentions that God names Himself in the Qur'an with names such as al-Ahad, al-Rahman al-Rahim, al-Malik al-Quddus al-Salam al-Mu'min al-Muhaymin al-Aziz al-Jabbar al-Mutakabbir, [[passage/quran-59-23|al-Hashr, verse 23]], and that He is the Creator, the Maker, the Fashioner, [[passage/quran-59-24|al-Hashr, verse 24]], and mentions what is related about preserving God's beautiful names. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:09:30} He rules that tawhid of names and attributes requires that none share God's names and attributes; God alone is the Generous, not the servant, and divine attributes are not attributed to creatures. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep02 00:16:00} He then turns to the second division — tawhid of lordship — citing "Say: to whom belongs the earth and all in it? **Episode 7: vows and their rulings.** The lecturer opens on the point's subject: the vow (nadhr). He explains that the categories of legal rulings are five — the permitted, the recommended, the obligatory, the forbidden and the disliked — then defines a vow as one's obliging oneself to an act of obedience to God that the law did not oblige, that is, the vower imposes on himself what was not imposed on him. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:02:00} He says the vow belongs to the acts of worship, and worship is for God alone, so a vow divides into a vow for God and a vow for other than God; a vow for other than God is shirk, citing "Say: I have been commanded to worship God sincerely, turning to Him in religion", [[passage/quran-39-11|al-Zumar, verse 11]], and mentions the story of Mary mother of Jesus vowing what was in her womb consecrated, [[passage/quran-3-35|Al 'Imran, verse 35]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:07:00} He mentions the kinds of vow in detail — the absolute vow, the conditional vow and the vow imposed on another — and what follows from performing, breaking or expiating each kind. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:21:30} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:43:00} He says whoever vows must fulfil, and the expiation for a broken vow is feeding ten poor people or clothing them, freeing a slave, or fasting three days, citing "so let it not touch him except the like of it, as an expiation for their oaths", [[passage/quran-5-89|al-Ma'idah, verse 89]], and "fulfil God's covenant when you make a covenant", [[passage/quran-16-91|al-Nahl, verse 91]]. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:57:00} {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:57:30} He mentions what is related of the Prophet that whoever vows an act of obedience to God should do it. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:53:00} It is recorded that the transcript is distorted in many places in the details of the kinds and expiations. {cite:assabile-wagdy-ghoneim sharh-mabadii-el-tawheed/ep07 00:58:30} ⁦(needs_source: the details of the kinds of vows and their expiations are unclear in the transcript)⁩
