## Summary

*Sexual Ethics and Islam: Feminist Reflections on Qur'an, Hadith, and Jurisprudence* is an English book by the American scholar Kecia Ali, published by Oneworld in Oxford on 25 June 2006 in 217 pages. {cite:openlibrary-sexual-ethics-and-islam-2006} {cite:ajis-review-sexual-ethics-and-islam} According to its contents, its opening chapters treat marriage, money, marital relations and intermarriage; then divorce in Islamic ethics and prospects for reform; then slave concubinage in Muslim texts and discourses; then prohibited acts and forbidden partners. A bibliography is on pages 193–212. {cite:archive-org-sexual-ethics-and-islam-2006}
