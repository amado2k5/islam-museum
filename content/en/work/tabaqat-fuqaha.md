## Overview
A book by [[person/abu-ishaq-al-shirazi|Abu Ishaq al-Shirazi]] with biographies of the jurists of the schools, arranged by generations (tabaqat). It focuses on the Shafi'i jurists and on the sequence of memorisation and teaching in the school. {cite:tabaqat-fuqaha-abbas-1981}

This description is taken from the introduction by the editor Ihsan Abbas. Anything beyond it needs a further source.
