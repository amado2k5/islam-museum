## Overview
The controversial novel by Sherry Jones (2008): an imagined novelist's account of the life of Aisha bint Abi Bakr from her childhood to her early marriage to the Prophet and her life after his death. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Random House refused to publish it out of fear, and it was published by Beaufort Books; it provoked debate about the freedom of fictional imagination and religious facts. {cite:wikidata}
