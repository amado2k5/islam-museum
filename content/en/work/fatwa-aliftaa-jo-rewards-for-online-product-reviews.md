## Summary

A question on the cash rewards and discount codes shopping sites offer for product reviews, which Jordan's General Iftaa Department permitted provided the review is honest. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3809}

## The question

The questioner said some online shopping sites pay a small sum for reviewing a product after buying it, and others give a discount code; he asked whether taking these is allowed, and whether reviewing a product without using it and taking money for it is forbidden. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3809}

## The answer

The Department answered that a sum the site grants for reviewing the purchase experience is unobjectionable if the review is genuine and truthful, since it is a reward from the site. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3809} It said a discount code offered by the site itself is part of its promotions to attract customers without anything in return, making it a gratuitous transaction in which uncertainty is excused. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3809} Reviewing products without using them or checking their quality, in the hope of a reward, it described as misleading and deceiving buyers, a false review akin to lying and fraud and contrary to sincere counsel; it required that any manipulation or deception be avoided and that the review be honest and truthful. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3809}
