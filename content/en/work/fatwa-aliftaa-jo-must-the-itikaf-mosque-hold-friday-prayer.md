## Summary

A question about whether the mosque for i'tikaf must hold Friday prayer, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is not a condition, except for someone who has vowed a continuous i'tikaf that spans a Friday. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4196}

## The question

The questioner asked whether the mosque in which one performs i'tikaf must be one where Friday prayer is held. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4196}

## The answer

The Department answered that it is not a condition for the mosque of i'tikaf to be one where Friday prayer is held. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4196} It made an exception for someone who has vowed a continuous i'tikaf that spans a Friday: his i'tikaf must be in a mosque where Friday prayer is held, so that he does not break its continuity by leaving to attend the Friday prayer. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4196}
