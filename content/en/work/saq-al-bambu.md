## Overview
The novel by Saud Alsanousi (2012), winner of the Arabic Booker: Hoza/Isa between Kuwait and the Philippines, between his Kuwaiti father's house and his Filipino mother's house, asking who is counted a son and who a servant. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The first Kuwaiti novel to win the great Arabic prize; it ignited a debate on labour and identity in the Gulf. {cite:wikidata}
