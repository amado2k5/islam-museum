## Overview
A well-known treatise by Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri ([[person/abu-l-ala-al-maarri|his biography]]). It tells of the poet's journey to the afterlife, and of his meeting there with the leading poets and philologists. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 288}
