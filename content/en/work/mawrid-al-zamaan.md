## Summary
The bibliography of the King Fahd edition of the Itqan lists Mawrid al-Zam'an fi Rasm al-Qur'an by Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Umawi al-Sharishi, known as al-Kharraz, edited by Muhammad al-Sadiq Qamhawi. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 3031} The verses were not read here, and the sources read give no death date for the author, so none is stated.

## Commentaries
The same list gives Dalil al-Hayran, a commentary on al-Kharraz's Mawrid on the spelling and marking of the Qur'an, by Ibrahim ibn Ahmad al-Marghni al-Tunisi, and Tanbih al-Khillan ila Sharh al-I'lan bi-Takmil Mawrid al-Zam'an, also by al-Marghni, edited by Muhammad al-Sadiq Qamhawi. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2977} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2969}

## In the notes of the Itqan
The notes cite the Mawrid with al-Dani's Muqni' for the rule that the alif of the hamzat al-wasl drops after the preposition lam, and use Dalil al-Hayran to say what the Maghribis and the Mashriqis do with letters added in some words. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2202} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2214} See [[work/al-muqni-fi-rasm-masahif-al-amsar|al-Muqni']] and [[concept/rasm-al-quran|the rasm of the Qur'an]].
