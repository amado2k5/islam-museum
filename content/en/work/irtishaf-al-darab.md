## Overview
A grammar in two volumes, titled "Irtishaf al-Darab min Lisan al-Arab" (Sipping the Pricking from the Tongue of the Arabs), by Athir al-Din Abu Hayyan, as Hajji Khalifa reports. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.305}
