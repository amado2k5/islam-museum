# I Lovë My al-Quran (ILMA)

## Summary
*I Lovë My al-Quran*, known as ILMA, is an illustrated Indonesian children's Qur'an with simplified translation and interpretation, first published in 2007 by Pelangi Mizan, one of Indonesia's leading Islamic publishers; its title is English though its content is Indonesian. {cite:gloqur-ilma-pelangi-mizan}

## Editions
A second edition followed in 2008, then several reprints; in 2015 the publisher repackaged it with improved educational games, a tajwid bookmark and audio for an electronic smart pen. {cite:gloqur-ilma-pelangi-mizan} Production ceased after its main writer left the publisher in 2019. {cite:gloqur-ilma-pelangi-mizan}

## What it contains
Each package holds the mushaf in fifteen volumes, fifteen volumes of simplified interpretation and translation, a dictionary of Qur'anic words and an audio CD; it uses the authorised translation of Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs. {cite:gloqur-ilma-pelangi-mizan} Its illustrations avoid realistic depictions of living beings and do not depict prophets, its language is simple and lively, and its explanations quote Quraish Shihab, Abdullah Yusuf Ali and [[person/muhammad-asad|Muhammad Asad]]. {cite:gloqur-ilma-pelangi-mizan}

## Who made it
Irfan Amalee was its main writer and concept designer, working with a team of over twenty scholars; the first edition was endorsed by figures from Indonesia's largest Islamic organisations, among them Nahdlatul Ulama, Muhammadiyah and the Indonesian Ulema Council. {cite:gloqur-ilma-pelangi-mizan}
