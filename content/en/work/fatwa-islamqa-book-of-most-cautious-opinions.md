## Summary

A question on whether a fiqh book gives the most cautious view on every disputed question, in which IslamQA answered that it knows of no such book. {cite:islamqa answer 614445}

## The question

The questioner said that on some questions with a recognised disagreement it is better to take the course that leaves the disagreement behind, and that taking the most cautious view has conditions a lay person may find hard to grasp. {cite:islamqa answer 614445} He asked whether any fiqh book sets out the evidence on every question together with the most cautious view, not only the preferred one. {cite:islamqa answer 614445}

## The answer

IslamQA answered that acting on caution is not always meritorious, nor always required. {cite:islamqa answer 614445} It said it knows of no single book that gives the most cautious view on every question of fiqh. {cite:islamqa answer 614445} It quoted al-Jurjani's definition of caution in al-Ta'rifat and a passage from Ibn Taymiyya on legitimate scrupulousness. {cite:islamqa answer 614445}
