## Summary

A question about shortening and combining prayers when travelling daily, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that shortening and combining are permitted on permissible travel of 81 km or more once the built-up area is left, even if the journey is repeated daily. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4299}

## The question

The questioner asked about shortening and combining prayers for someone who travels every day. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4299}

## The answer

The Department answered that a traveller may shorten and combine prayers for a distance of 81 km or more, provided the journey is permissible and he begins only after setting out and leaving the built-up area of his town or the administrative boundary of his area. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4299} It said that if the distance between workplace and residence reaches the distance for the concession, shortening and combining are permitted on leaving the built-up area, even if the journey is repeated daily. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4299} It quoted [[person/al-khatib-al-shirbini|al-Khatib al-Shirbini]] in [[work/mughni-al-muhtaj|Mughni al-Muhtaj]] (1/529) that advancing and delaying combinations are permitted on long permissible journeys, that delayed combining is established in the two Sahihs from the hadith of [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas ibn Malik]] and [[person/abdullah-ibn-umar|Abdullah ibn Umar]], and that advanced combining was graded authentic by [[person/ibn-hibban|Ibn Hibban]] and [[person/al-bayhaqi-abu-bakr-ahmad-ibn-al-husayn|al-Bayhaqi]] from the hadith of [[person/muadh-ibn-jabal|Mu'adh ibn Jabal]] and graded hasan by [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4299}
