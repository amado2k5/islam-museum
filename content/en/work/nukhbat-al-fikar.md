## Summary
Nukhbat al-Fikar in the terminology of the hadith folk by Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani (d. 852 AH) — a few pages of prose gathering the rules of hadith science; the author commented it himself in 'Nuzhat al-Nazar', the intermediate mustalah text {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: ابن حجر العسقلاني شيخ الإسلام وصاحب فتح الباري.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليها شروح كثيرة منها شرح المؤلف «نزهة النظر» وشرح الملا علي القاري وشروح المتأخرين {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.

## For younger visitors
Nukhbat al-Fikar is the small-great book of hadith science, studied after the Bayquniyya.
