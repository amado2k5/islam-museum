## Summary

A question about appointing an agent to distribute zakat and charity and the conditions for doing so, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is permitted if the agent is reliable and trustworthy, and set out conditions the agent must keep. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on appointing an agent to distribute zakat and charity, and about its conditions. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920}

## The answer

The Department answered that a person who owes zakat may appoint someone else to distribute it, provided the agent is reliable, honest and trustworthy, and that the payer forms the intention of zakat when appointing a non-Muslim agent. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920} It said the agent may take nothing of the zakat for himself or for those he must support, citing [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]]'s al-Majmu that when the owner or his agent distributes zakat, the share of the zakat collectors lapses. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920} It said the agent must deliver it to those entitled without delay, citing [[person/al-khatib-al-shirbini|al-Khatib al-Shirbini]]'s Mughni al-Muhtaj that zakat must be paid immediately. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920} It said zakat is given to the poor directly, so no mosque, school or hospital may be built from it, since these are not among the designated categories, citing [[person/muhammad-ibn-qasim-al-ghazzi|Ibn Qasim al-Ghazzi]]'s Fath al-Qarib that 'in the way of God' means volunteer fighters. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920} It said the agent must keep to the payer's stipulations on where and in what form it is distributed, citing al-Bujayrimi that a donor's stipulation is like that of the founder of an endowment: if the payer specified cash, the agent may not give food or goods, and if he specified food this is allowed on the Hanafi view that value may be given; it cited al-Nawawi's al-Majmu that the Shafi'is do not allow giving value, while [[person/abu-hanifa|Abu Hanifa]] does. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3920}
