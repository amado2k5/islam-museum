## Overview
A lost book by al-Mas'udi. In it he followed a series of reports about Ali: his asceticism, his conduct, and the kinds of his speech and sermons, in the reports about the family of Muhammad. Al-Mas'udi mentions it at the end of the chapter on the virtues of Ali in "Muruj al-Dhahab". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 714}
