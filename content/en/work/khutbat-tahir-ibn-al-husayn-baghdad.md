## Summary
Tahir ibn al-Husayn's Friday sermon in Baghdad after al-Amin was killed in 198 AH. He says the victory was not by their choice, lists the duties of the caliphate, and warns against disobedience.

The book gives the sermon on the authority of al-Tabari and al-Iqd al-Farid. It says the preacher led the people in prayer, then addressed them, with Hashimites and commanders present. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.118}

He says the outcome was not their choice but God's, and lists the caliph's duties, including guarding the frontiers and reviving the Sunna. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 vol3 p.118}
