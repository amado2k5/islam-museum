## Overview
The novel by Salman Rushdie (1988): two Indian migrants who after a plane crash turn into an angel and a devil in London, with chapters that imitate the early days of Islam in a style of magical realism. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
The most famous controversial novel of the twentieth century; the Iranian fatwa of 1989 made it a global freedom-of-expression case, while its artistic record is broader than its noise. {cite:wikidata}
