## Overview
A book by Abu Muhammad Abdullah ibn Muslim, known as Ibn Qutayba the grammarian, who died in 270 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.267}

"Kashf al-Zunun" reports that it was said of it that it is "a sermon without a book", because its introduction is so long, although it contains something of everything. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.267}
