## Summary

A question about delaying the division of an estate of property and flats while some brothers need their shares and the mother takes all the rent, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that an estate is divided as soon as possible, that no heir claiming his share may be refused, and that it is kindness to secure the mother a decent income without neglecting the heirs' rights. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3943}

## The question

The questioner said his father had died leaving property and flats, and asked about delaying the division of the inheritance when some brothers need their shares, and whether his mother may take all the rent from the flats although she saves it and pays zakat on it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3943}

## The answer

The Department answered that an estate should in principle be divided after the death as soon as possible, to avoid disputes and lost rights; if the heirs agree to keep the property as it is, owning it jointly each according to his share, that is permitted, but none of them may refuse division when one of them claims his share. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3943} It said that everything a person owns becomes the property of all the heirs after his death, that each heir may claim his right to the rent from let property and flats, and that making this claim is not disobedience to parents. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3943} It advised the parties to resolve the matter through dialogue and, if that fails, said an heir may take it to court. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3943} It said it is kindness for the heirs to provide their mother with a source of income for a decent life, with moderation and balance and without neglecting the others' rights, and that maintenance of poor parents falls on their well-off children, citing article 197 of Jordan's Personal Status Law No. 15 of 2019. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3943}
