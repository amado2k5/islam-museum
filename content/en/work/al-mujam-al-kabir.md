## Overview
Sulayman b. Ahmad al-Tabarani's great Mu'jam; his most celebrated compilation and one of the three mu'jams attributed to him, in which he arranged what he had gathered from nearly a thousand shaykhs {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 30}.

## Its arrangement in al-Salafi's edition

Yaqut, as quoted by the editors of al-Awsat, says al-Tabarani wrote al-Mu'jam al-Kabir on the names of the Companions. {cite:mujam-awsat-haramayn-1415-v1 p.83} Volume 11 of Hamdi Abd al-Majid al-Salafi's edition (Maktabat Ibn Taymiyya) holds the rest of the Musnad of Abd Allah ibn Abbas, arranged under headings naming those who related from him, such as "Abu Salih from Ibn Abbas" and "Tawus from Ibn Abbas", with hadiths numbered continuously, here from 10845 on; the editor annotates them with their sources and with al-Haythami's judgements on their narrators in Majma' al-Zawa'id. {cite:mujam-kabir-salafi-ibn-taymiyya-v11 p.1} {cite:mujam-kabir-salafi-ibn-taymiyya-v11 p.5} {cite:mujam-kabir-salafi-ibn-taymiyya-v11 p.6}
