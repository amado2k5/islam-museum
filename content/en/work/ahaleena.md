## Summary
«Ahalina» ("Our Families") is a short television programme presented by [[person/abd-al-rahman-mansour|Abd al-Rahman Mansour]], of which the Assabile archive keeps thirteen video episodes, each between fourteen and sixteen minutes long, in which the speaker talks alone to the camera. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:00:30} He opens the first episode by calling it a new programme in which viewers will learn "communication": between husband and wife, a son and his father, a daughter and her mother, a neighbour and his neighbour, in the light of the Book and the Sunna; it will also set out common mistakes in upbringing, the keys to people's hearts, and "ten destroyers" in the way fathers raise their children. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:01:30} Throughout the episodes he jokes with the studio crew behind the cameras by name and calls them his brothers at al-Nada channel. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:11:30}

The programme rests on a principle he repeats: that parents answer before God for raising their children "on the method of prophethood". He cites the verse of Surat al-Tahrim on guarding oneself and one's family from the Fire [[passage/quran-66-6|(al-Tahrim 66:6)]], the hadith "each of you is a shepherd", and the hadith of the governor who dies having cheated those in his care. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep05 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep07 00:02:00} In his view communication between parents and children has grown weak in these times, many parents complain that their children do not pray or dislike prayer, and one of the greatest causes is mistaken upbringing. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:03:00}

The episodes move from husband and wife (1–2) to the keys of hearts (3), then parents' love for their children (4), the "ten destroyers" (5–6), making children love prayer across four stages of life (7–10), then the harshness of some fathers towards their children and what builds affection between them (11–12), and finally punishment in upbringing (13). Episode 13 ends by promising a further episode on the rules of striking a child, which is not in the archive, so the archive does not hold the whole programme. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep13 00:13:30}

## The episodes
**Episode 1: skills of communication between spouses.** Marriage, he says, is a blessing; he mentions the verse on the creation of spouses for tranquillity, affection and mercy, and quotes [[person/ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyya|Ibn al-Qayyim]]: God made the wife a place of rest in which the husband's heart finds calm, and affection is pure love joined with mercy. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:05:00} The marital bond is distinctive and needs means to keep it, and the skills of communication are for husband and wife alike to learn. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:05:30} He describes a common pattern: sweet words in the days of engagement and contract, then a cooling after the wedding that both sides complain of. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:07:00} Among the means of communication he lists: open, affectionate words between the spouses, which he holds permissible after the contract and not between an engaged couple; **Episode 3: the keys to hearts.** Everyone, he begins, wants to be praised on earth and remembered well in heaven; he cites the hadith of the two Sahihs from [[person/abu-hurayra|Abu Hurayra]] that when God loves a servant He calls Jibril, who loves him, then the people of heaven love him, and then acceptance is set for him on earth. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:02:00} He counts five keys: [[practice/greeting-salam|giving the greeting of peace]], shaking hands, smiling, telling your brother that you love him, and [[concept/hadiyya-fiqh|the gift]]. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep03 00:13:30} On handshaking he cites the hadith that two Muslims who shake hands are forgiven before they part, the report of [[person/anas-ibn-malik|Anas]] that the Prophet never withdrew his hand from someone shaking it until the other man withdrew his, and the saying of [[person/al-hasan-al-basri|al-Hasan al-Basri]] that shaking hands increases affection; **Episode 4: do you love your children?** He puts three questions to parents: do you express your love for your child, how do you express it, and does your child feel it? {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:01:30} A child, he says, learns love from the parents' relationship, the warmth of the home and the bonds between siblings, and this helps his emotional growth so that he grows up sound, hating neither himself, his family nor his society. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:02:00} He then sets out "four steps" to build love in a child's heart: express your love in words and through hugs, play, kisses and a story at bedtime; {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:07:00} give him love more than presents, and do not brand a small child's tales of make-believe as lies, since his imagination is wide; {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:08:30} and trust him, for the more a father shows trust in his son the more he pushes him towards truthfulness, while showing distrust teaches trickery and lying. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:09:30} He then counts "ten signs" that a child loves his father, among them: naming him as his model when asked, turning to him first with a problem, caring about his opinion, rejoicing when he comes home instead of hiding from him, asking his mother why his father is upset, wanting to go everywhere with him, delighting in playing with him, and saying "I love you" to him on the phone. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:11:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:14:00} Greater than all this, he says, is for a father to show affection to the children's mother in front of them. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep04 00:14:30}

**Episode 5: the ten destroyers (1).** The reform of children, he says, begins with the reform of the fathers; parents of the past raised children by instinct, tenderness and gentleness, and as these ## About these recordings
The texts used here are machine transcripts (Qwen3-ASR, thirty-second windows) read window by window, and only what reads clearly was written. Every episode opens and closes with windows that hold no speech, which the transcriber renders as repeated sounds. The stories the speaker tells about himself and others are his own accounts, attributed to him; the names of his studio colleagues and of his children are left out here, as are place names that came through garbled. The hadiths he cites are described in the museum's words without their wording, and verses whose sura he does not name are not linked. {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-abd-al-rahman-mansour ahaleena/ep01 00:15:30}
