## Summary
Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' is al-Dhahabi's best-known biographical work (he died in 748 AH): a large dictionary that arranges its subjects by generation, beginning with the Prophet and the Companions, then the Successors and those after them, down to the author's own age. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala}

## How it was written
Al-Dhahabi wrote it after Tarikh al-Islam, shorter than that history and more concerned with lives and reports. Each entry gives the man's name and lineage, his teachers and students, the reports best known of him, the scholars' praise and his death. Citations here point to the volume and printed page as recorded on the source record. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala}

## Its link to elegy and death announcements
The entries carry the report of a death and what surrounded it: who wept, who prayed over the dead, what the poets said in elegy, and what the imams said in praise at his death. This is why the Elegy and Death-Announcement Browser gathers passages from the book; each harvested passage is quoted within forty words and cited to its page in the scan.
