## Summary
*Lam'at al-tanqih fi sharh Mishkat al-masabih* is a commentary by Abd al-Haq al-Dihlawi al-Hanafi (d. 1052 AH) on *Mishkat al-masabih* of al-Khatib al-Tibrizi ([[person/abd-al-haqq-dihlawi|his biography]]). {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 1}

## Volume nine
Volume nine covers several chapters of the *Mishkat*. They include a section on miracles (scan page 494), a section on the "tajrid" (abstraction) figure (page 551), and sections on Qur'anic verses, such as "The Book of God is a rope stretched from heaven to earth" (page 708). On pages 775 and 780 the commentary turns to the writing down of revelation and to the Prophet's letter to the polytheists of Mecca. {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 494}, {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 551}, {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 708}, {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 775}

## Edition
The volume was published by Dar al-Nawader in Damascus. The title page says it was printed at the expense of Shaykh Sultan ibn Zayed Al Nahyan, representative of the ruler of the United Arab Emirates. The copyright pages give the publisher's offices in Damascus, Beirut, Kuwait and Tunis. {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 1}, {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 4}

## A note on the source
The editors' names on the title page are not legible in the OCR and are not recorded. The author's birth year is hard to read on the title page and has not been converted; it needs checking against the printed book. Page numbers are scan pages. {cite:lamaat-tanqih-dar-al-nawader-vol9 p. 1}
