## Summary

A question on the best wording after rising from bowing, in which Ibn Baz answered that all the reported wordings are permitted. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1245}

## The question

The questioner asked which is better after rising from bowing: "Rabbana wa-laka l-hamd" or "Allahumma rabbana wa-laka l-hamd". {cite:binbaz fatwa 1245}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that all of it is permitted, and that four wordings are reported: "Rabbana laka l-hamd", "Rabbana wa-laka l-hamd", "Allahumma rabbana laka l-hamd" and "Allahumma rabbana wa-laka l-hamd". {cite:binbaz fatwa 1245} Using one at one time and another at another, he said, is all good. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1245} He added that there is latitude here for the imam and for the one praying alone. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1245}

## About this record

The answer is published on binbaz.org.sa, the official site of Ibn Baz's legacy, as fatwa 1245 in the al-Jami al-Kabir series. {cite:binbaz fatwa 1245} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
