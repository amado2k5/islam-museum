## Summary
Wasaya al-Ulama' ('Testaments of the Scholars') is a book attributed to Ibn Zabr that Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani mentions in al-Durar al-Kamina among books a man heard from his father's uncle Yusuf ibn Umar. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 scan vol. 3 p. 96} It is the nearest book in this group to the idea of scholars' testaments at death, but the page does not describe it, give the full form of its title or the full name of its author.

## What the books say
Ibn Hajar says, in an entry whose name does not show on the page I read, that its subject heard from his father's uncle Yusuf ibn Umar Iqtida' al-Ilm of al-Khatib, Wasaya al-Ulama' of Ibn Zabr, the Turuq 'Ismah yusmah lak' of Ibn al-Akfani and the Juz' of al-Ansari, and that al-Birzali heard from him and mentioned him in his lexicon. {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 scan vol. 3 p. 96}

## What I did not find
The title on the page is Wasaya al-Ulama'; I did not find the longer form 'at the approach of death' there, nor Ibn Zabr's full name, nor any description of the reports, nor any particular testament assigned to it. The Siyar has more than one 'Ibn Zabr' (for instance the judge Abu Muhammad ibn Zabr from whom al-Dhahabi relays), and I cannot tell which is meant. {cite:siyar-alam-nubala scan vol. 1 p. 2126} I therefore do not link the book to a person in the museum.
