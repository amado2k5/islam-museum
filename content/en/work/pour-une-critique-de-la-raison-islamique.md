## Summary

*Pour une critique de la raison islamique* is a collection of studies in French by Mohammed Arkoun, published by Maisonneuve et Larose in Paris in 1984 in 378 pages in the series Islam d'hier et d'aujourd'hui. Its Arabic translation was published by Dar al-Saqi in London in 2001 in 318 pages. {cite:openlibrary-pour-une-critique-raison-islamique-1984} {cite:openlibrary-min-al-ijtihad-arkoun-1991}
