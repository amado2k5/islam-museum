## Summary
Ansab Himyar wa-Mulukiha — the genealogies and kings of Himyar — by the imam Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham, author of the Sira (d. 213 AH), entered by Kashf al-Zunun among the genealogical works. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 653}

## Details
Haji Khalifa lists it right after al-Baladhuri's Ansab al-Ashraf — "a large and profitable book, of which twenty volumes were written and it was left unfinished" — writing: "Ansab Himyar and its kings, by the imam Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham, author of the Sira, who died in the year 213." {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 653}

The title belongs to the Kashf's genealogy section, where the compiler ranged the books of tribal lines and the early Arab kings that had reached him; the next entry, al-Sam'ani's Ansab, is described as a mighty work in the field whose complete form would fill eight volumes yet is seldom found. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 653}
