## Summary
Amali Nizam al-Mulk — hadith dictations attributed to Abu Ali al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Ishaq, the celebrated Seljuk vizier known as Nizam al-Mulk (d. 485 AH). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 600}

## Details
Haji Khalifa lists it under the amali works: "in hadith; he is Abu Ali al-Husayn ibn Ali ibn Ishaq," and the Kashf's editor notes that the manuscript's "al-Husayn" is a scribal slip for "al-Hasan" — namely ibn Ali ibn Ishaq, surnamed Nizam al-Mulk the vizier, who died in 485. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 600}

The dictations thus stand to the name of the famous Seljuk minister in the hadith section, and in the Kashf's register they appear between the Amali of al-Qali, of al-Naqqash and of Wali al-Din Abu Zur'a al-Iraqi the hadith master — showing how wide the dictation genre ran among scholars and statesmen alike. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 600}
