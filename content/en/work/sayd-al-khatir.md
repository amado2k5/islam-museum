## In Abu Ishaq al-Heweny's lessons "Madrasat al-Hayat"
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] made his commentary on this book a series called [[work/madrassat-al-hayat|Madrasat al-Hayat]]. In it he describes Ibn al-Jawzi as a preacher to the common people who mixed with them, compares Sayd al-Khatir with Ibn al-Qayyim's al-Fawa'id, both being reflections of faith in short paragraphs, and distinguishes it from Bada'i' al-Fawa'id, which he sees as a book only senior scholars can carry. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:04:30}

The reflections he explains in the surviving episodes are: one on misunderstanding God and His Messenger, which Ibn al-Jawzi opens by seeking refuge from misunderstanding, above all among those who claim knowledge; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:06:00} one on scholars who indulge in licences thinking that knowledge will defend them, in which beneficial knowledge is knowing the One worshipped and His greatness and studying the lives of the Messenger and his Companions and taking on their character, not the outward forms of jurisprudence on the lawful and unlawful; al-Heweny titled it "The reality of beneficial knowledge" and says the lesson was his ninth on it; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep03 00:11:00} "On the friends of God"; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep04 00:02:00} a short reflection he titled "The blessing of perspicacity", after saying that the previous Ramadan he had given eight lectures on the reflection "Nafthat masdur" with four still to come; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep09 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep09 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep09 00:02:30} and one on clothes and adornment that breed self-admiration, in which Ibn al-Jawzi mentions the ring the Prophet threw away, the rabbis of the Israelites who walked with staffs so as not to walk with pride, and Ibn Umar's camel, whose gait pleased him so that he put it among the sacrificial animals. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep10 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep10 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep10 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep10 00:05:00}

Al-Heweny does not read the book uncritically. He holds that Ibn al-Jawzi himself misread the hadith of the people of Badr in limiting the forgiveness to the past, because grammarians take the imperative as future and because Hatib's story came after Badr; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:24:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:25:30} he holds that Ibn al-Jawzi's statement that God chooses for His friendship only those perfect in form was made with the prophets in view; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep04 00:08:00} in the reflection on perspicacity he faults him for relying on unsound Isra'iliyyat in the story of Joseph's brothers; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep09 00:45:30} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep09 00:49:00} he says Ibn al-Jawzi spoke too generally in the reflection on clothes, since what corrupts one soul may set another right; {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep10 00:05:30} and he counts reliance on weak and fabricated reports as a known flaw of preaching literature in Ibn al-Jawzi's work. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny madrassat-al-hayat/ep01 00:48:00}
