# al-Maktaba al-Waqfiya (waqfeya.com)

## Summary

al-Maktaba al-Waqfiya is an online digital library of books of the Shari'a sciences, which scans them and uploads them to archives and file-hosting sites; in a statement of its own it says it holds nearly ten million pages in high resolution, most converted to PDF. {cite:wikipedia-waqfeya-ar}

## Sections

The library holds manuscripts, theses, Islamic texts in various forms, and scanned books. {cite:wikipedia-waqfeya-ar}

## Beginnings

Its site's earliest capture in the Internet Archive is dated 14 December 2005 and titled "under construction": it shows al-Maktaba al-Waqfiya with a store of scanned books, and announces as coming soon a manuscripts store of more than 12 GB to download, a store of recited texts, and the second release of "al-Mawsu'a al-Shamila". {cite:wayback-waqfeya-2005} The Arabic article links it with [[work/al-maktaba-al-shamela|al-Maktaba al-Shamela]]. {cite:wikipedia-waqfeya-ar}

## Today

The site is at waqfeya.com and has an article in the Arabic Wikipedia. {cite:wikidata-waqfeya} The page count above is the library's own figure.
