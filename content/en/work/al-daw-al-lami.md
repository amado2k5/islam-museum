## Overview
A biographical dictionary compiled by the hadith scholar [[person/al-sakhawi|al-Sakhawi]] for the people of the ninth century AH, in several volumes. He included an entry on himself, in the manner of the hadith scholars, as Ibn al-Imad also reports. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p.432}
