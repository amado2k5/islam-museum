## Summary

*Towards a Just Monetary System* is an English book by the economist M. Umer Chapra, published by the Islamic Foundation in Leicester in 1985 in 292 pages. {cite:openlibrary-towards-a-just-monetary-system-1985} Its subtitle describes it as a discussion of money, banking and monetary policy in the light of Islamic teachings, and it has a foreword by Khurshid Ahmad. According to its contents, it begins with goals, then the nature of riba, then equity financing as the alternative, then fundamental reforms and replies to objections, then the institutional setting of central bank, commercial banks and non-bank financial institutions, then the strategy of monetary policy, and ends with an evaluation. {cite:archive-org-towards-a-just-monetary-system-text}
