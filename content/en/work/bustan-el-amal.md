## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with thirty episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} In the first episode the Sheikh says he is glad to be the viewers' guest throughout the blessed month in this programme he chose for them 'this year', in which he will speak to the sick, the fearful, the wronged and the grieving; each episode lasts about eleven minutes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:02:00} The museum's gathering record is [[concept/al-faraj-bad-al-shidda|relief after hardship]].

## Episode 1: hope and awaiting relief

The opening: [[concept/al-faraj-bad-al-shidda|relief after hardship]], the changing world, awaiting relief as worship, and Ya'qub's words that the believer does not despair of God's mercy. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep01 00:06:00}

## Episode 2: God suffices us

Trial as examination, the believers after Uhud, [[concept/tawakkul|trust in God]], the 'balance' with God, and [[concept/sabr|patience]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep02 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep02 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep02 00:09:00}

## Episode 3: fear, hunger and istirja'

Imaginary [[concept/khawf|fear]], being tested with fear, hunger and loss of wealth, and [[concept/al-istirja|saying 'to God we belong']] at a calamity. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep03 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep03 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep03 00:05:00}

## Episode 4: Yunus in the whale

The story of [[person/yunus|Yunus]], [[concept/hawa|desire]] as a whale, and the manners of [[concept/ikhtilaf|disagreement]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep04 00:05:00}

## Episode 5: tyranny

[[concept/al-tughyan|Tyranny]] from ruler to husband, Pharaoh, the believer of Pharaoh's household and [[concept/tafwid|entrusting one's affair to God]], and brotherhood in God. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep05 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep05 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep05 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy bustan-el-amal/ep05 00:08:00}
