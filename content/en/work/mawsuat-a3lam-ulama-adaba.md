## Overview
An institutional encyclopaedia in eight volumes, with biographies of the scholars and men of letters of the Arabs and Muslims, arranged alphabetically. It is one of the projects of the Arab Organization for Education, Culture and Science. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}

## Its contents and structure
For each figure it gathers his lineage, upbringing, achievements and writings, with indexes and sources. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba} The first volume begins with "al-Abri" and ends with "al-Asir". The copy consulted contains volumes 1–5 and 7–8; volume 6 is missing. {cite:mawsuat-a3lam-ulama-adaba}
