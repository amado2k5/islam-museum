## Summary
Ibn Qudama al-Maqdisi's commentary on al-Khiraqi's Mukhtasar, his most famous commentary {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 425}. It stood in nine large volumes in its author's hand; most copies circulating in Ibn Badran's time ran to thirteen volumes {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 425}.

## The method
The author writes out the question from al-Khiraqi's Mukhtasar as the chapter's heading, then explains it and shows what it evidences; then mentions what resembles it that is not in the text; shows where there is disagreement and consensus; records each imam's position; and attributes reports to the hadith scholars' books {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 425}. Ibn Muflih said the book is eloquent in the madhhab, and that by it the madhhab was gathered {cite:madkhal-ibn-badran-turki p. 426}.

## In «Asdaf al-Lu'lu'» by Abu Ishaq al-Heweny
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] says «al-Mughni with al-Sharh al-Kabir», in the Dar al-Kitab al-Arabi edition of Beirut, was among the first books he acquired; he could not afford it, so he took part of his fee for his books from the publisher in books, and he did not sleep the night he bought it for joy. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep18 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep18 00:04:30} He counts Ibn Qudama's «al-Mughni» in the Dar Hajar edition among his favourite Hanbali books, mentioning Dr Abd al-Fattah al-Hulw and his team. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep17 00:05:30}

He relates that in the early 1990s he and Shaykh Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Muqaddam both went to Cairo every month, so they agreed that al-Muqaddam would come from Alexandria to Kafr el-Sheikh and they would travel together, and he proposed that they work on «al-Mughni» on the road: al-Muqaddam would prepare the legal questions and he would do the takhrij of the hadiths. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep25 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep25 00:10:30} Al-Muqaddam's trips to Cairo then stopped, so he went on alone at his desk until he had done the takhrij of about eighty hadiths and reports from the beginning of the book, before his projects, lessons and travels took him away. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep25 00:11:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep25 00:11:30} He says his first published book, «Fasl al-Khitab bi-Naqd al-Mughni an al-Hifz wa-l-Kitab», came out attributed to Ibn Qudama because the name «al-Mughni» calls this book to mind. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep12 00:12:30}
