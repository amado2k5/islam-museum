## Summary

A question about a lawyer who died after some of his cases were transferred, with the clients' permission, to a female lawyer under a new power of attorney and agreement, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the deceased lawyer is entitled to the share of fees matching the work he completed, as estimated by experts, and the rest belongs to the new lawyer under the new agency. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3991}

## The question

The questioner said his brother was a lawyer handling some cases and then died; some cases already under hearing were transferred, with the clients' permission, to a female lawyer under a new power of attorney and a new agreement. He asked whether the fees received count as part of the estate or belong to the lawyer who took over the cases. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3991}

## The answer

The Department answered that lawyers' fees are a wage for agency, and that the majority of jurists hold that a hired worker whose contract is dissolved is entitled in proportion to his work, quoting [[person/abu-ishaq-al-shirazi|al-Shirazi]] in al-Muhadhdhab that on dissolution the agreed price is divided between what was performed and what remains, with valuation referred to experts. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3991} It said the contract is dissolved by the death of either party if the heir does not complete it, so the worker is entitled to the share of the agreed fee that experts estimate for the work he did: nothing if he did nothing, half if he completed half, and so on; it quoted [[person/al-khatib-al-shirbini|al-Khatib al-Shirbini]] in Mughni al-Muhtaj, including his report from [[person/al-mawardi|al-Mawardi]], on entitlement to the proportionate share after death. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3991} It concluded that the lawyer's fees are assessed by what experts in the legal profession estimate for the work he completed before his death, that the agency ended with his death, and that the remaining fees belong to the new lawyer under the new agency. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3991}
