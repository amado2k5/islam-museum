## Overview
"Al-Mufradat fil-Adwiya wal-Aghdhiya" (Simple Remedies and Foods) by [[person/ibn-al-baytar|Ibn al-Baytar]] the herbalist of Malaga. According to "Shadharat al-Dhahab", knowledge of plants, their properties, their uses and where they grow came to its end with this book. {cite:shadharat-dhahab p. 234}
