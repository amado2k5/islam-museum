## Summary

*Islam and Liberal Citizenship: The Search for an Overlapping Consensus* is an English book by Andrew F. March, published by Oxford University Press in New York in 2009 in 350 pages. {cite:openlibrary-islam-and-liberal-citizenship-2009} The publisher's description says the book starts from the question of how Muslims can be both good citizens of liberal democracies and good Muslims. The author looks for a middle way between two opposed positions and asks whether there is a tradition consistent with Sunni Islam that is also compatible with modern liberal citizenship. The publisher calls it the first in-depth study of both liberal and Islamic legal and doctrinal principles of citizenship. {cite:archive-org-islam-and-liberal-citizenship-2009}
