## Summary
Ahkam al-Qur'an by the imam Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Tahawi, the Hanafi (d. 321 AH), in the genre of works deriving legal rulings from Qur'anic verses, a field shared by scholars of several schools. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 211}

## Details
Haji Khalifa's Kashf al-Zunun records a whole line of books under this title: for the mujtahid imam Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i (d. 204 in Egypt), the first to write in the field; for al-Shaykh Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Hujr al-Sa'di (d. 244); and for the judge-imam Abu Ishaq Isma'il ibn Ishaq al-Azdi al-Basri (d. 282). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 210-211}

It further lists one for Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Musa ibn Yazdad al-Qummi al-Hanafi (d. 305), one for al-Tahawi himself (d. 321), and one for Abu Muhammad al-Qasim ibn Asbagh al-Qurtubi (d. 340) — making al-Tahawi's a link in a chain of school-by-school treatments of Qur'anic rulings. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 211}
