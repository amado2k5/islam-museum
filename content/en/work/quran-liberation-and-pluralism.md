## Summary

*Qur'an, Liberation and Pluralism: An Islamic Perspective of Interreligious Solidarity Against Oppression* is a book by Farid Esack, published by Oneworld in Oxford on 1 February 1997 in 280 pages. {cite:openlibrary-quran-liberation-pluralism-1997} The lending-library record gives the places of publication as Oxford and Rockport, Massachusetts. {cite:archive-org-quran-liberation-pluralism-1997}

## Publication and editions

The record files the book under the subjects Qur'an, Islam and Apartheid, and notes a bibliography on pages 273–282 and an index. {cite:archive-org-quran-liberation-pluralism-1997} The Open Library record adds further subjects, among them the religious aspects of apartheid, interfaith relations and Muslims in Africa. {cite:openlibrary-quran-liberation-pluralism-1997}
