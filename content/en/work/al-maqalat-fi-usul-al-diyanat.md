## Overview
"Al-Maqalat fi Usul al-Diyanat" (Doctrines on the Foundations of Religions), a book by al-Mas'udi ([[person/al-masudi|al-Mas'udi]]), as he mentions it in "Muruj al-Dhahab". {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 210}

## Its content
It covers all the views of the Mu'tazila and of the other sects of the community, including the Kharijites, the Murji'a, the Rafida, the Zaydis and the Hashwiyya, on their principles, their branches and their sayings. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p. 210}
