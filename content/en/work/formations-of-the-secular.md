## Summary

*Formations of the Secular: Christianity, Islam, Modernity* is an English book by the anthropologist Talal Asad, published by Stanford University Press in 2003 in 269 pages. {cite:openlibrary-formations-of-the-secular-2003} {cite:h-net-review-formations-of-the-secular} According to its contents, the book opens by asking what an anthropology of secularism might look like. Its chapters then treat agency and pain, cruelty and torture, the "human" in human rights, Muslims as a "religious minority" in Europe, secularism, the nation-state and religion, and the reconfiguration of law and ethics in colonial Egypt. {cite:archive-org-formations-of-the-secular-2003}
