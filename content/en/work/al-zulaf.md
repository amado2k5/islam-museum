## Overview
"Al-Zulaf", a book by al-Mas'udi, in which he explains how the sun and the moon exert their influence. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.518}

He sets out there his view on the "bahranat" (critical days) arising from the humours and the four natures. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.518}
