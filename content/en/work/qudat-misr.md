## Overview
"Akhbar Qudat Misr" (Reports on the Judges of Egypt) by Abu Umar Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Kindi, who was the first to gather their reports, up to the year 246 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}

Ibn Zulaq al-Misri, Abu Muhammad Hasan ibn Ibrahim (d. 387 AH), then added a continuation. He began with the judge Bakkar and ended with Muhammad ibn al-Nu'man in Rajab 386 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}

The hafiz Ibn Hajar also added a continuation to it, a large volume which he called [[work/raf-al-isr-qudat-misr|Raf al-Isr an Qudat Misr]]. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}
