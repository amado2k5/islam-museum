# al-Tahrir wa-l-Tanwir

## Summary

al-Tahrir wa-l-Tanwir is a Qur'an commentary by Muhammad al-Tahir ibn Ashur. The author gave it the full title "Clarifying the Right Meaning and Illuminating the New Mind, from the Tafsir of the Glorious Book" {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 1 p. 9}, and shortened it to "al-Tahrir wa-l-Tanwir from the Tafsir" {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 1 p. 10}. In his introduction he says that he stated the aims of every surah as far as he could, and that he fixed the meanings of the Arabic words more carefully than many dictionaries had done {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 1 p. 9}.

## The first introduction

The first introduction treats the meaning of tafsir and ta'wil, and whether tafsir is a science. The author takes the word from the doubled form of the verb "fassara", and gives the root sense as making clear and uncovering the meaning of speech {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 1 p. 11}. He then discusses whether the doubled form of a transitive verb signifies intensity. His conclusion is that a speaker may choose the doubled form for transitivity, and that the sense of intensity then follows from the choice rather than belonging to the form itself {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 1 p. 12}.

## Surat al-Mulk in the twenty-ninth volume

On the naming of Surat al-Mulk, the author reports a tradition from al-Tirmidhi, on the authority of Abu Hurayra, that a surah of thirty verses interceded for a man until he was forgiven; al-Tirmidhi calls the tradition hasan. The author explains that the surah was named by its first phrase, in the manner of the name of Thabit ibn Jabir, "Ta'abbata Sharran" {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 29 p. 9}. He also lists other names reported for it, such as al-Mani'a, al-Munjiya, al-Waqiya and al-Manna'a, and notes that some of these names are not explicit as names {cite:tahrir-wa-l-tanwir-ibn-ashur-dar-al-tunisia vol. 29 p. 10}.
