## Overview
"Al-Safwa", a book by al-Mas'udi that describes what people have said about the imamate. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.447}

Al-Mas'udi refers to it in "Muruj al-Dhahab" at the point where sects dispute the faith of Abd al-Muttalib. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.447}
