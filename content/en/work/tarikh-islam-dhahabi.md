## Summary
Tarikh al-Islam wa-Wafayat al-Mashahir wa-l-A'lam is the largest of al-Dhahabi's historical works (he died in 748 AH), and the source from which Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' and others were later drawn. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi}

## How it is arranged
It opens with the seerah and the campaigns, then the age of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs and the years of Mu'awiya, and then runs by year: year after year in the early centuries, then decade by decade, each span ending with the deaths of the famous and the notable, and a dhayl reaching 750 AH. This is the order of the scans we have read. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi}

## Its link to elegy and death announcements
The obituary section of each span carries the reports of the deaths of notable figures: who announced them, who prayed over them, what was said of them in verse, and what was said in condolence to their families. This is why its passages enter the Elegy and Death-Announcement Browser, each quoted within forty words and cited to its page.

## The text in the museum
The text was read by optical recognition from local scans, so reading errors may remain, and only a short cited quotation is copied from it. {cite:tarikh-islam-dhahabi}
