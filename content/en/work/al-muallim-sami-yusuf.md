## Summary
Al-Mu'allim is the debut album of the British singer [[person/sami-yusuf|Sami Yusuf]], released by [[listing/awakening-records|Awakening Records]] in 2003, the year the label signed him as its first artist. {cite:wikidata-al-muallim} {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012} {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en} The Guardian explains that its title, "The Teacher", is a reference to [[person/muhammad|the Prophet]]. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012}

## Its impact
The Guardian reported in 2012, from the label, that the album sold 3 million copies, and that with the second album, [[work/my-ummah-sami-yusuf|My Ummah]], it led Time magazine to describe Sami Yusuf as "Islam's biggest rock star" and set the bar for the burgeoning Islamic music industry. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012} It quotes the co-founder Wali-ur Rahman saying the success was instant and unprepared for, and that the label's website kept crashing from the traffic to its music videos. {cite:guardian-awakening-islamic-singers-2012} The figure is the label's, attributed as such.
