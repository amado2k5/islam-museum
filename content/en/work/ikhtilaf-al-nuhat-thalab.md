## Overview
A title shared by two books on the disagreements of the grammarians. The first is by Tha'lab, Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Yahya, who died in 291 AH. The second is by Abu al-Husayn Ahmad ibn Faris, who died in 395 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 250}
