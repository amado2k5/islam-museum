# SeekersGuidance (seekersguidance.org)

## Summary

SeekersGuidance is an online Islamic educational institute with a question-and-answer service, online courses and occasional retreats, founded by the Pakistani-Canadian scholar [[person/faraz-rabbani|Faraz Rabbani]], who is its education director and one of its instructors. {cite:wikipedia-faraz-rabbani-en}

## Names and branches

For a time it was known as SeekersHub Global. From 2011 to 2018 Rabbani directed SeekersHub Toronto in Mississauga, Ontario, re-established in 2019 as SeekersGuidance Canada; before that he had been a central figure at SunniPath, an online Islamic institute. {cite:wikipedia-faraz-rabbani-en}

## Beginnings

The earliest capture of its site in the Internet Archive is dated 7 May 2010, titled "SeekersGuidance - Online Course, IslamCast, Answers", with sections for courses, seminars, answers, a blog and the IslamCast podcast. {cite:wayback-seekersguidance-2010}

## What was said about it

The Muslim 500 wrote that Rabbani had "continuously been at the vanguard" of using the latest web technologies to teach Islam in the West for over a decade; that is the list's assessment. {cite:wikipedia-faraz-rabbani-en}
