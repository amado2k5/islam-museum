## Summary
Al-Minah al-Qudsiyya 'ala al-Hikam al-'Ata'iyya ("Holy Gifts on the Hikam") is the commentary of [[person/abd-allah-al-sharqawi|'Abd Allah ibn Hijazi al-Sharqawi]] (d. 1227 AH) on [[work/hikam-al-ataiyya|the Hikam of Ibn 'Ata' Allah]]. {cite:minah-qudsiyya-sharqawi-ilmiyya p. 1} At its start al-Sharqawi names himself "'Abd Allah ibn Hijazi al-Khalwati, known as al-Sharqawi" and calls his work "subtle annotations" on maxims mostly addressed to sincere disciples to raise them to the station of gnosis. {cite:minah-qudsiyya-sharqawi-ilmiyya p. 2} He begins with the first maxim: among the signs of relying on one's deeds is loss of hope when one slips. {cite:minah-qudsiyya-sharqawi-ilmiyya p. 2}

## Date and relation to Ibn 'Abbad
Al-Sharqawi states at the end that he completed the commentary on Saturday 13 Shawwal 1204 AH. {cite:minah-qudsiyya-sharqawi-ilmiyya p. 176} Muhammad 'Abd al-Maqsud Haykal says it is almost a summary of [[work/ghayth-al-mawahib-al-aliyya|Ibn 'Abbad al-Rundi's commentary]], and that al-Sharqawi, like Ibn 'Abbad, only occasionally points to links between the maxims without holding to them. {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 36} {cite:hikam-ibn-abbad-haykal-ahram-1988 p. 40}

## The edition
The edition used is that of Dar al-Kutub al-'Ilmiyya, Beirut, "with notes by great Sufi scholars", edited and annotated by Ahmad Farid al-Mazidi; its end states that Sa'd al-Din al-Siyaji prepared the commentaries, with remarks on some of them, from the edition printed in Egypt in Dhu al-Qa'da 1282 AH. {cite:minah-qudsiyya-sharqawi-ilmiyya p. 1} {cite:minah-qudsiyya-sharqawi-ilmiyya p. 176}
