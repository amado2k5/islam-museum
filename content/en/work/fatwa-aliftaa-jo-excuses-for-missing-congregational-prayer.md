## Summary

A question about excuses that permit missing congregational prayer in the mosque, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that missing congregation is permitted for general excuses such as hardship-causing rain, strong wind at night, mud, and severe heat or cold, and for personal excuses such as illness, hunger and fear. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4262}

## The question

The questioner asked which excuses permit missing congregational prayer in the mosque. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4262}

## The answer

The Department answered that missing congregational prayer in the mosque is permitted for general excuses, such as rain that makes going out a hardship, strong wind at night, deep mud that would soil one, hot wind, and severe heat or cold. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4262} It listed personal excuses, such as illness that makes walking as hard as walking in rain, severe drowsiness, evident hunger and thirst, needing to relieve oneself or pass wind, and fear for one's life, a limb or its use, one's property or one's honour, among other excuses. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4262}
