## The work
Jam' al-Jawami' is Taj al-Din al-Subki's celebrated compendium on legal theory (usul al-fiqh), placed by Ibn Hajar at the head of his works on usul, and famous through Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli's commentary {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425} {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 745}.
