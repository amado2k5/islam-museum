## Summary
Verses Ibn Hisham attributes to Abu Khaythama — whose name is Malik ibn Qays — on what he did in leaving his two booths and catching up with the Prophet, explaining in them that he pledged to the Prophet and incurred no sin. The museum does not reproduce the verses' text. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/164}

## Note
An addition of Ibn Hisham (p. 164). {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/164}
