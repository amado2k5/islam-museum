## Summary

A question about colleagues taking turns on overtime days, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that employees must follow the overtime rules and be present at work, and that pay for what breaches the instructions is not lawful. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4297}

## The question

The questioner asked about colleagues taking turns on overtime days and the pay for the days they were absent. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4297}

## The answer

The Department answered that an employee must follow the instructions and regulations on overtime hours, be truthful, and avoid trickery and lying, citing [[passage/quran-9-119|Surat al-Tawba 9:119]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4297} It said that whoever is assigned overtime must be present at the workplace even if there is no work, that the person authorised to approve overtime may not grant hours against the regulations, and that pay for anything that breaches the instructions is not lawful. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4297}
