## Summary

A question on giving zakat to a friend in need in the form of a birth gift, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that this is allowed if the friend lacks enough for her needs and is not provided for by someone obliged to maintain her. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3848}

## The question

The questioner asked whether she may give her needy friend the zakat on her wealth as a gift (nuqut) for her baby's birth. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3848}

## The answer

The Department answered that zakat is a right due on wealth under its conditions, given to the eight categories named in the Qur'an. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3848} It defined nuqut as money or other things given at celebrations and occasions, and said the sounder view is that it is a gift to the person celebrating, not a loan to be returned, quoting [[person/ibn-hajar-al-haytami|Ibn Hajar al-Haytami]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3848} It concluded that if the friend lacks enough for her needs and is not provided for by someone obliged to maintain her, the gift may be given and counted as zakat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3848}
