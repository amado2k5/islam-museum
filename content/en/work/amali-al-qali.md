## Summary
Amali al-Qali — the language dictations of al-Shaykh Abu Ali Isma'il ibn al-Qasim al-Qali (d. 356 AH), composed at Cordoba after 330 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 623}

## Details
Haji Khalifa enters it in Kashf al-Zunun under the amali works: "Amali al-Qali, in language; its author is al-Shaykh Abu Ali Isma'il ibn al-Qasim, the philologist, who died in 356 and composed it at Cordoba after the year 330." {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 623}

The amali is a genre in which a master dictated to his pupils in formal sessions — on language, hadith or literature — and al-Qali's entries sit in the Kashf's register among the dictations of judges, traditionists and men of letters; he is one of the leading philologists of fourth-century al-Andalus. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 623}
