## Summary
*'Awn al-Bari li-Hall Adillat al-Bukhari* is the commentary by [[person/siddiq-hasan-khan|Abu al-Tayyib Siddiq b. Hasan b. 'Ali al-Husayni al-Qannawji al-Bukhari]] on [[work/al-tajrid-al-sarih|al-Tajrid al-Sarih li-Ahadith al-Jami' al-Sahih]] by [[person/ahmad-al-sharji-al-zabidi|Shihab al-Din Abu al-'Abbas Ahmad al-Sharji al-Zabidi]], printed in the margin of al-Shawkani's [[work/nayl-al-awtar-shawkani|Nayl al-Awtar]]. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.5 p. 2}

## Its writing
At the end of the Book of Jihad the author writes that he finished it on 1 Sha'ban 1293 AH in Bhopal, that this completed the first half of the commentary, and that the division into halves is his own, praying for help to complete the second half. {cite:nayl-awtar-awn-bari-hamish j.5 p. 380}
