## Summary
"Allamani al-Tarikh" (History Taught Me) is a television programme by [[person/tariq-al-suwaidan|Tareq al-Suwaidan]] of which the Assabile archive keeps twenty-nine video episodes, each roughly eighteen to twenty-four minutes long, all opening with a song saying that history made its singer know his religion and taught him to refuse injustice. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:01:00} Al-Suwaidan introduces it as a programme that sheds light on the decisive moments and great events of some stages of the community's history, to read what lies behind events and draw lessons from them. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:02:00} He says the programme is not for entertainment: its aim is for the viewer to know "the laws that made history", which the Qur'an calls sunan. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:03:00} In several episodes he asks viewers to take notes, and promises to gather in the final episode six causes of victory and six obstacles to it. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep02 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep24 00:01:00}

In the first episode al-Suwaidan sets out the frame of the whole programme: the universe has material laws, the life of individuals and nations has civilisational laws, and history is the main source for knowing the second kind; the material is in [[concept/sunan-allah-fi-al-umam|God's sunan among peoples]]. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:11:00} The dates and figures he gives are his own account, attributed to him with their anchors, and each page points to what other sources say where they differ.

## What is not written here
In the first episode al-Suwaidan lists the fall of the Umayyad state among the events he means to present, and none of the twenty-nine surviving episodes deals with it. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep01 00:02:30} At the end of episode 29 he announces that the next and final episode will gather the causes of victory and its obstacles and comment on the stories of the thirty episodes; that episode is not in the archive. {cite:assabile-tareq-al-suwaidan alamani-al-tarikh/ep29 00:19:00} The opening and closing songs, the machine-repeated stretches of the transcript, and names and figures that came through garbled or contradictory were all left out, and are recorded in the lane's progress log.
