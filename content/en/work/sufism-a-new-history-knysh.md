## Summary

*Sufism: A New History of Islamic Mysticism* is an English book by Alexander Knysh, published by Princeton University Press in 2017, with a paperback in 2019. The publisher's description says it is a history of Sufism from the earliest centuries of Islam to the present, covering its definitions and discourses, leadership, institutions and practices. It gives special attention to Sufi approaches to the Qur'an and traces the rise of the shaykhs and the challenges to their authority. {cite:openlibrary-sufism-new-history-knysh-2017} {cite:archive-org-sufism-new-history-knysh-2017}
