## Overview
The supplement by the hafiz Shihab al-Din Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Hajar al-Asqalani to [[work/qudat-misr|Reports on the Judges of Egypt]], in one large volume. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}

The supplement has abridgements, among them "Al-Nujum al-Zahira bi-Talkhis Akhbar Qudat Misr wal-Qahira" (The Shining Stars: An Abridgement of the Reports on the Judges of Egypt and Cairo). {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 253}
