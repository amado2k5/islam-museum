## Overview
A short letter that [[person/musaylima|Musaylima]] sent to the Prophet with two messengers at the end of the tenth year AH. See [[event/musaylimas-letter-and-the-prophets-reply|Musaylima's letter and the Prophet's reply]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/247}

## Its text
"From Musaylima, Messenger of God, to Muhammad, Messenger of God: peace be upon you. To proceed: I have been made a partner with you in the matter, and to us is half the earth and to Quraysh is half the earth; but Quraysh are a people who transgress." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/247}

## The reply
The Prophet replied: "From Muhammad, Messenger of God, to Musaylima the liar: peace be upon whoever follows the guidance. To proceed: the earth belongs to God; He gives it as an inheritance to whomever of His servants He wills, and the final outcome is for the God-fearing." {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa 4/247} The book "Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum" gives the letter and the reply in much the same words. {cite:mubarakpuri-raheeq p.452}
