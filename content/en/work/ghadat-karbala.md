## Overview
The novel by Jurji Zaydan (1901): his first historical novel of the early Islamic period, moving between history and love on Iraqi soil after the event of al-Taff (Karbala). {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
One of Zaydan's novels that established the Arabic historical novel; a Syrian Christian writer who wrote Islamic history in the language of literature. {cite:wikidata}
