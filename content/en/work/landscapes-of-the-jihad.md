## Summary

*Landscapes of the Jihad: Militancy, Morality, Modernity* is an English book by the historian Faisal Devji, published by Cornell University Press in Ithaca, New York, in 2005 in 184 pages. {cite:openlibrary-landscapes-of-the-jihad-2005} The title-page verso says the book was first published in the United Kingdom by C. Hurst & Co. It has six chapters: "Effects without Causes", "A Democratic History of Holy War", "Monotheistic Geographies", "Media and Martyrdom", "The Death of God" and "New World Order", with a bibliography on pages 165–175. {cite:archive-org-landscapes-of-the-jihad-2005}
