## Summary
Kitab al-Badi is a book on rhetoric (balagha), by [[person/ibn-al-mutazz|Abd Allah ibn al-Mu'tazz]]. The file gives the book as composed in 888 CE and the author's death as 908 CE. Yes, the book is in the Shamela library: Book 11661. {cite:wikipedia-en-abdallah-ibn-al-mutazz} {cite:shamela-ws-book-11661}

## The book and its field
Kitab al-Badi appears in a research summary supplied to the museum that lists sixty of the most-cited classical and modern Arabic works across five sciences, namely dictionaries, grammar, morphology, prosody and rhetoric, twelve for each; this book stands in its section on rhetoric (balagha). [[concept/ilm-al-balagha|The science of balagha]] is the science of the ways of speech, its clarity and its beauty. The title is given in the summary as "Kitab al-Badi". {cite:wikipedia-en-abdallah-ibn-al-mutazz}
