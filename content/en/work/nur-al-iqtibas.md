## Overview
A separate treatise by [[person/ibn-rajab|Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali]], devoted to explaining the hadith of Ibn Abbas: "O boy, I will teach you some words." It is the nineteenth hadith of the Forty of al-Nawawi ([[passage/arbaun-19|hadith 19]]). {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}

Ibn Rajab says in "Jami al-Ulum wal-Hikam" that the hadith contains great counsel and comprehensive rules from the most important matters of religion, and that he devoted a large part to explaining it. The book is printed under this name. {cite:ibn-rajab-jami-ulum-hikam}
