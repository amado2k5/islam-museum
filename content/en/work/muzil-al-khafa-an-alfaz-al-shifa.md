## Summary
*Muzil al-Khafa' an Alfaz al-Shifa'* ("Removing the Obscurity from the Words of al-Shifa") is a gloss by [[person/al-shamunni|Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Shumunni]] on Qadi Iyad's [[work/al-shifa-bi-tarif-huquq-al-mustafa|al-Shifa]], printed at the foot of its pages in the Dar al-Kutub al-Ilmiyya edition. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.2}

## Why it was written
In its opening al-Shumunni says that while teaching *al-Shifa* he was enabled to explain some of its words, open its closed passages and solve its difficulties, so he gathered this for the benefit of students and readers and called it *Muzil al-Khafa'*. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.8} His biographer says he abridged it from Burhan al-Halabi's commentary and added small supplements containing precise findings. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.6}

## Method
The gloss follows the text phrase by phrase ("his words…"), fixes the reading of each word by vowels and letters, explains rare terms from *al-Sihah*, Ibn al-Athir's *al-Nihaya* and others, and identifies persons. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.8} {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.10} At the report about al-Walid ibn al-Mughira, for example, it names the sira authors who transmitted it and compares Ibn Ishaq's wording with Ibn Hisham's and al-Suhayli's preference; at the mention of the vizier Ibn Muqla in Part Four it gives his biography. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.269} {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.690} It also takes up points of language, such as who first said *amma ba'd*. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.10}

## Date of completion
Its colophon says it was completed in the last ten days of Dhu al-Qa'da 847 AH. {cite:al-shifa-ilmiyya-shumunni p.696}

## For young visitors
This is a small book that helps readers pronounce the words of *al-Shifa* correctly and understand its difficult words.
