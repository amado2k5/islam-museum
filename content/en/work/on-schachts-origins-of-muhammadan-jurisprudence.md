## Summary

*On Schacht's Origins of Muhammadan Jurisprudence* is a book by M. Mustafa al-Azami, published under the sponsorship of King Saud University in Riyadh, with John Wiley as co-publisher, in 1405/1985. {cite:archive-on-schachts-origins-azami}

## Publication and editions

After the introduction come two parts: the first, 'Law and Islam', on the place of law in Islam and Islamic law in the first Islamic century, and the second on the Sunna of the Prophet and Islamic law. {cite:archive-on-schachts-origins-azami} The second part discusses the early concept of the Sunna, the idea of the 'living tradition' in the ancient schools of law of Medina, Syria and Iraq, and the growth of legal hadith, and devotes its last chapter to the isnad system and a critique of Schacht's view of it, including the 'common link'. {cite:archive-on-schachts-origins-azami} It closes with two appendices on the use of isnad by Ibn Ishaq, Abu Hanifa, Malik, Abu Yusuf and al-Shafi'i, with their material in Arabic, followed by a bibliography and an index. {cite:archive-on-schachts-origins-azami}
