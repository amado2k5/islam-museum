## Summary

A question on whether mourning applies to an elderly widow and on removing jewellery that is hard to take off, in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] answered that mourning applies to all and the jewellery is removed. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4001}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a widow must remove her jewellery even when it is difficult, and whether mourning covers a woman of advanced age. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4001}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that mourning applies to the young and the elderly alike, and to a wife whether or not the marriage was consummated. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4001} He distinguished the widow from the divorcee: a woman whose husband dies after the contract but before consummation owes the waiting period and mourning and inherits from him, whereas a woman divorced before consummation owes no waiting period. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4001} He said the jewellery is removed, and a bracelet that has grown tight on the wrist is cut off rather than left on. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4001}
