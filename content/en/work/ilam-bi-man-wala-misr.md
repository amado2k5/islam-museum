## Overview
"Al-Ilam bi-Man Wali Misr fil-Islam" (The Informing About Those Who Ruled Egypt in Islam) by the judge Shihab al-Din Abu al-Fadl Ahmad ibn Ali ibn Hajar al-Asqalani, who died in 852 AH according to Hajji Khalifa. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.519}

Its title indicates that it is about the governors of Egypt, but the museum's data does not detail its contents; a further source is needed.
