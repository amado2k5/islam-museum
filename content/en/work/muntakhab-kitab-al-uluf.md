## Overview
A book by Ibn al-Mazyar, a student of Abu Ma'shar, who made a selection from [[work/kitab-al-uluf|the Book of Thousands]], including what it mentions of the structures and great buildings that come into being in the world every thousand years. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.556}
