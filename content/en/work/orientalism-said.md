## Summary

*Orientalism* is an English book by the Palestinian-American critic Edward W. Said, published by Pantheon Books in New York in 1978 in 368 pages, and as a Vintage paperback in 1979. {cite:openlibrary-orientalism-1978} {cite:archive-org-orientalism-said-1978} According to the contents of the 1979 edition, the book has three chapters. "The Scope of Orientalism" includes "imaginative geography" and its representations. "Orientalist Structures and Restructures" studies Silvestre de Sacy and Ernest Renan and British and French pilgrims. "Orientalism Now" treats latent and manifest Orientalism, modern Anglo-French Orientalism and "the latest phase". {cite:archive-org-orientalism-said-1979}
