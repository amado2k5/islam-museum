## Summary
Salaam is the fifth studio album of the British singer [[person/sami-yusuf|Sami Yusuf]], produced by himself and released by ETM International on 22 December 2012 on CD and on 24 December digitally; South-East Asia and the Middle East and North Africa received it before the worldwide release. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en} {cite:wikidata-salaam-sami-yusuf} It was recorded between 2009 and 2012 and runs 62 minutes. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en}

## What it is
Sami Yusuf described it as a musical progression from Wherever You Are (2010), celebrating "outward multiplicity and inward unity" with an emphasis on peace between people regardless of differences, as Tabloid! reported. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en} The article notes that it includes a number of songs celebrating his Islamic faith. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en}

## The songs
The list includes 'Happiness', 'Salaam', 'Smile', 'The Source', 'Dryer Land', 'It's A Game', 'To Guide You Home', 'Forgotten Promises', 'Ala Bi Dhikrika', 'I'm Your Hope', 'All I Need' and 'Hear Your Call', followed by bonus tracks including acoustic Arabic versions of 'Wherever You Are' and 'Without You'. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en} 'It's A Game' and 'All I Need' are re-edited versions of 'Sallou' and 'Never Never' from [[work/without-you-sami-yusuf|Without You]], the album whose release he had opposed. {cite:wikipedia-without-you-sami-yusuf-en} Videos were made for 'Hear Your Call', 'I'm Your Hope', 'Forgotten Promises', 'It's A Game', 'All I Need' and the Arabic 'Wherever You Are'. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en}

## Reach
The article reports, from Global Post, that the international release went platinum in South-East Asia and reached best-selling lists in the Middle East and North Africa; it gives no year or certifying body, so the museum tells it as an attributed report. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en} A special edition for Turkey carried five songs he re-recorded in Turkish, and he promoted the album in Istanbul in April 2013. {cite:wikipedia-salaam-sami-yusuf-en}
