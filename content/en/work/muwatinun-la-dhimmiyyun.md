## Summary

*Muwatinun la Dhimmiyyun: Mawqi' Ghayr al-Muslimin fi Mujtama' al-Muslimin* (Citizens, Not Dhimmis: The Place of Non-Muslims in Muslim Society) is a book by the Egyptian writer Fahmi Huwaydi. Its first edition came from Dar al-Shuruq in 1985 in 264 pages, and its third from the same house in Cairo and Beirut in 1999. It opens with a passage from Ibn Jubayr's travels on caravans and trade continuing between Muslims and Christians in wartime. It then states that a crisis of trust marks relations between Muslims and non-Muslims, the result of historical and recent accumulations, and that it can be overcome only by facing its causes frankly. {cite:openlibrary-muwatinun-la-dhimmiyyun-1985} {cite:archive-org-muwatinun-la-dhimmiyyun-1999}
