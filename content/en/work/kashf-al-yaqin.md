## Summary
Kashf al-Yaqin is a treatise on the Imamate attributed to Allamah al-Hilli. Its record is preliminary: the attribution and the text have not yet been verified on the page. {cite:al-aalam}

## Details
Allamah al-Hilli is Jamal al-Din al-Hasan ibn Yusuf ibn Ali ibn al-Mutahhar al-Hilli, an imam of the Shi'a and one of the great scholars of Islam, born and resident at al-Hilla in Iraq. Al-Zirikli counts about ninety books for him, among them Nazm al-Barahin in the principles of religion, Irshad al-Adhhan to the rules of faith, Muntaha al-Talab in establishing the doctrine, Talkhis al-Maram in knowing the rulings, Tahrir al-Ahkam al-Shar'iyya on the Imami school, Istiqsa' al-I'tibar in hadith, Nahj al-Iman in Qur'an commentary, Tadhkirat al-Fuqaha', and al-Qawa'id wa-l-Maqasid in logic, natural philosophy and metaphysics. {cite:al-aalam}

In this list the title "Kashf al-Yaqin" is not named explicitly in the portion of al-Zirikli's entry read so far, so the attribution of this treatise to al-Hilli and its subject in the Imamate remain in need of a source verified on the page. {cite:al-aalam}
