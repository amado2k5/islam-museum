## Summary

A question about working in a hotel, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is permitted in jobs that neither involve nor aid forbidden things, such as administration, and forbidden in jobs that do, such as serving or transporting alcohol. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4283}

## The question

The questioner asked about working in a hotel. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4283}

## The answer

The Department answered that working in hotels, restaurants and shops is permitted in jobs that neither directly involve forbidden things nor aid them, such as administrative work. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4283} It said that jobs that directly involve or aid forbidden things, such as serving, transporting, buying or accounting for alcohol, or arranging its purchase, are forbidden, citing [[passage/quran-5-2|Surat al-Ma'ida 5:2]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4283} It explained that the basic business of hotels and restaurants is permissible, that the sin of forbidden things occasionally done there rests on those who run them, and that scholars permit dealing with someone whose lawful wealth is mixed with unlawful wealth, referring to its fatwa no. 2697. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4283}
