## Summary
*Kunuz al-Haqa'iq fi Hadith Khayr al-Khala'iq* is [[person/abd-al-rauf-al-manawi|'Abd al-Ra'uf al-Munawi]]'s collection of short hadiths, printed in the margin of al-Suyuti's [[work/al-jami-al-saghir-suyuti|al-Jami' al-Saghir]] in the Khayriyya printing in Cairo. {cite:jami-saghir-khayriyya-v1 p. 5} In his preface al-Munawi says he gathered about ten thousand hadiths in it and arranged them alphabetically for ease of use, and complains there of the misfortunes time had dealt him. {cite:jami-saghir-khayriyya-v1 p. 6}

Not covered here: the book's method and sigla, since in the copy read the margin is interleaved with the main text.
