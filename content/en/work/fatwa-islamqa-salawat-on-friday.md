## Summary

A question on why Friday is singled out for invoking blessings on the Prophet, in which IslamQA answered that what is particular to Friday is doing it often. {cite:islamqa answer 657821}

## The question

The questioner asked why Friday is singled out for invoking blessings on the Prophet. {cite:islamqa answer 657821}

## The answer

IslamQA answered that invoking blessings on the Prophet is prescribed at all times, and that Friday is singled out not for the practice itself but for the encouragement to do it often. {cite:islamqa answer 657821} It gave as reasons the merit of the day and what is established in the sunna, that the blessings are presented to him on that day in a special way. {cite:islamqa answer 657821} It added that scholars have suggested other wisdoms, and that the basis is worship and obedience, whether or not the wisdom is grasped. {cite:islamqa answer 657821}
