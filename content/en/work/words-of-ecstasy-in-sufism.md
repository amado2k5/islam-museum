## Summary

*Words of Ecstasy in Sufism* is an English book by Carl W. Ernst on the shathiyyat, the ecstatic sayings of the Sufis, published by State University of New York Press in Albany in 1985 in 184 pages. In his preface the author says it is a revised version of his Harvard doctoral thesis of 1981, and that it introduces a little-known body of mystical literature, its interpretations and its repercussions in the society where it flourished. {cite:openlibrary-words-of-ecstasy-1985} {cite:archive-org-words-of-ecstasy-ernst-text}

## Publication and editions

The book has three parts. The first sets out the literature and theory of ecstatic expressions in Sufism up to Ruzbihan Baqli and after, with their topics and forms. The second treats ecstatic sayings on faith and infidelity in al-Hallaj, 'Ayn al-Qudat Hamadani and Ruzbihan Baqli. The third, 'The Spirit and the Letter', covers the trials of three Sufis, al-Nuri, al-Hallaj and 'Ayn al-Qudat, and then Sufism, the law and the question of heresy. An appendix gives Ruzbihan's account of the vision of Abu Yazid al-Bistami. {cite:archive-org-words-of-ecstasy-ernst-text}
