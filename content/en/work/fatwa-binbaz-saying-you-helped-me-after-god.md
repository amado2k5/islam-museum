## Summary

A question about saying to a person 'you helped me with this after God', in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] answered that there is no harm in it, as with 'were it not for God and then you', and that what is forbidden is joining the two with 'and', as in 'were it not for God and you'. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4050}

## The question

The questioner asked: is it permissible to say, for example, 'it was you who helped me with such-and-such after God'? {cite:binbaz fatwa 4050}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that there is no harm in it: saying 'were it not for God and then you', 'this is from God and then from you', or 'you helped me after God with such-and-such' is unobjectionable. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4050} He said that what is not permitted is to say 'were it not for God and you', 'this is from God and you', or 'what God wills and you', for it is this 'and' that is impermissible; but if one says 'were it not for God and then you', 'what God wills and then what so-and-so wills' because he helped him, or 'you helped me after God with such-and-such', there is no harm. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4050}
