## Overview
A geographical book written by [[person/al-idrisi-muhammad-ibn-abd-allah|al-Idrisi]] for King Roger II in Sicily. It describes the seven climes and their cities, roads, seas, peoples, crafts and trade. It comes with a map of the world engraved on a silver plate. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002}

According to the author's own introduction, he completed it in Shawwal 548 AH. The date of composition here rests on the author's own statement and needs a further source. {cite:nuzhat-al-mushtaq-2002}
