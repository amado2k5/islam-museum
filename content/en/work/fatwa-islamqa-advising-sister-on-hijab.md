## Summary

A brother's question about his adult sister who does not wear hijab, in which IslamQA answered that he bears no sin if he has given the advice he can. {cite:islamqa answer 657806}

## The question

The questioner said his adult sister goes out with her face and hair uncovered, and that he has advised her many times, privately and in front of their father. {cite:islamqa answer 657806} Their father, who runs the household, does not want her to wear hijab, and the questioner said he has been mistreated by him for giving this advice. {cite:islamqa answer 657806} He asked whether he is sinning because of her, whether he should still sit and talk with her, and how he could make hijab dear to her. {cite:islamqa answer 657806}

## The answer

IslamQA answered that a brother does not bear sin merely because his adult sister goes uncovered, provided he has given the advice he is able to give. {cite:islamqa answer 657806} It held that he should call her to it with wisdom and gentleness, and attend to strengthening her faith and correcting her ideas. {cite:islamqa answer 657806} It advised him to keep up his ties with her rather than cutting them. {cite:islamqa answer 657806}

## About this record

IslamQA published this answer as no. 657806, dated 17 Jumada al-Ula 1448 (28 October 2026). {cite:islamqa answer 657806} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
