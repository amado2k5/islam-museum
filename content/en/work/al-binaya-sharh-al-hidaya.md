## Summary
*Al-Binaya fi Sharh al-Hidaya* is [[person/badr-al-din-al-ayni|Badr al-Din al-'Ayni]]'s ten-volume commentary on al-Marghinani's [[work/al-hidayah-marghinani|al-Hidaya]]. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 17} The biography printed with 'Umdat al-Qari describes it as very full in documenting the hadiths of rulings and setting out the schools of the scholars of the great cities, and says Ibn al-Humam's *Fath al-Qadir* does not make it superfluous. {cite:umdat-qari-ilmiyya-2001-v1 p. 17}

In *al-Binaya*, in the section on recitation, al-'Ayni said [[person/al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni]] "deserves to be weakened", as Shams al-Haqq al-'Azimabadi quotes him in [[work/al-taliq-al-mughni|al-Ta'liq al-Mughni]]. {cite:sunan-daraqutni-ibn-hazm-2011 p. 9}
