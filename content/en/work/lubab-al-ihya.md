## Overview
An abridgement written by Shaykh Ahmad ibn Muhammad [[person/ahmad-al-ghazali|al-Ghazali]], brother of the author of "Ihya Ulum al-Din", titled "Lubab al-Ihya" (The Essence of the Revival). The sources say that he died in 520 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.198}
