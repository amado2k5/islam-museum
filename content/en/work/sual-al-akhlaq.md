## Summary

*Su'al al-Akhlaq: Musahama fi al-Naqd al-Akhlaqi li-l-Hadatha al-Gharbiyya* (The Question of Ethics: A Contribution to the Ethical Critique of Western Modernity) is a book by the Moroccan philosopher Taha Abderrahmane, first published by al-Markaz al-Thaqafi al-'Arabi in Casablanca and Beirut in 2000 in 240 pages. Its introduction asks which is more proper to the human being, rationality or morality. Its first chapter takes up the relation of moral virtue to religion: it sets out three claims, that ethics depends on religion, that religion depends on ethics, and that ethics is independent of religion, with the objections to each, and then criticises common beliefs about religion and ethics. {cite:archive-org-sual-al-akhlaq-2000}
