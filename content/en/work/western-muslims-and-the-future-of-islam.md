## Summary

*Western Muslims and the Future of Islam* is an English book by the thinker Tariq Ramadan, published by Oxford University Press on 27 November 2003 in 288 pages and reviewed in the Times Higher Education Supplement in March 2004. The publisher's description says Ramadan sees a silent revolution in Muslim communities in the West and seeks an independent Western Islam rooted in the West's cultural reality, beginning with a fresh reading of the sources and then showing their practical use.
