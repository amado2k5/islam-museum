## Summary

*What Is Islam? The Importance of Being Islamic* is an English book by the scholar Shahab Ahmed, published by Princeton University Press. The catalogue record dates its e-book edition to 2015, and a 2017 review gives the 2016 hardback, in 624 pages. {cite:openlibrary-what-is-islam-2015} {cite:isit-review-what-is-islam} The 2016 edition's text runs to 609 pages, with a bibliography on pages 547–591. Its jacket-flap description says the book shows how Muslims have historically conceived of and lived with Islam as norms and truths at once contradictory and coherent. {cite:archive-org-what-is-islam-ahmed-2016}
