## Summary

A question about fasting twenty-eight days, then moving to a country celebrating Eid, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that he celebrates Eid with them, owing nothing if he fasted twenty-nine days in all and one make-up day if he fasted twenty-eight. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4218}

## The question

The questioner asked about someone who fasted twenty-eight days in one country and then moved to a country whose people were celebrating Eid. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4218}

## The answer

The Department answered that someone who fasted in his country and then moved to a country whose people were celebrating Eid celebrates it with them. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4218} It said that if the total he fasted was twenty-nine days he owes nothing, but if it was twenty-eight he must make up one day, because a month is never twenty-eight days. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4218}
