## Summary

A question on keeping a layer of gel on the nails during ablution to stop compulsive skin-picking, in which IslamQA permitted wiping over it while the need lasts. {cite:islamqa answer 643018}

## The question

The questioner said she has for years compulsively picked the skin around her nails, to the point of bleeding, infections and wounds. {cite:islamqa answer 643018} A protective layer of gel, she said, greatly curbs the habit, she returns to it when the gel is removed, and she does not wear it for adornment. {cite:islamqa answer 643018} She asked whether this is a recognised therapeutic need that permits keeping the gel on during ablution and prayer. {cite:islamqa answer 643018}

## The answer

IslamQA answered that the rule is to remove whatever keeps water from reaching the nails. {cite:islamqa answer 643018} It held that if the gel is needed to prevent this harm and no suitable alternative is available, it may be kept and wiped over, on the analogy of the ruling for a splint, for as long as the need lasts. {cite:islamqa answer 643018} It advised her to seek treatment for the compulsion. {cite:islamqa answer 643018}

## About this record

IslamQA published this answer as no. 643018, dated 18 Jumada al-Ula 1448 (29 October 2026). {cite:islamqa answer 643018} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
