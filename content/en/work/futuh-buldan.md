## Overview
"Futuh al-Buldan" (The Conquests of the Lands) by al-Baladhuri, Ahmad ibn Yahya ibn Jabir, who died around 279 AH. In its opening the author describes it as a collection and an abridgement of reports from a group of scholars of hadith, biography and the conquests of lands, some of which answer others. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 8}

## Structure
It begins with a summary of the biography of the Prophet from his arrival at Quba, then the reports of the apostasy wars. It then follows the conquests region by region: Syria; Egypt and the Maghrib as far as al-Andalus; al-Sawad and the founding of Kufa, Basra, Wasit and Baghdad; the mountains; and the east as far as Sind. It ends with the rules on land tax, poll tax and stipends. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407}

The two editors note in the introduction to the edition that al-Baladhuri gave several accounts of a single event. They see in this the insight of a critical historian and not merely a compiler. {cite:futuh-buldan-maarif-1407 p. 5-7}

Its author is [[person/al-baladhuri|al-Baladhuri]].
