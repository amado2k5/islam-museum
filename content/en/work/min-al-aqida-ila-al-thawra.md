## Summary

*Min al-'Aqida ila al-Thawra* (From Creed to Revolution) is a multi-volume work by the Egyptian thinker Hassan Hanafi, part of his Heritage and Renewal project, in which he attempts to rebuild the science of the principles of religion. Its editions include one from Maktabat Madbouly, whose first volume treats the theoretical premises. {cite:archive-org-min-al-aqida-ila-al-thawra-vol1}
