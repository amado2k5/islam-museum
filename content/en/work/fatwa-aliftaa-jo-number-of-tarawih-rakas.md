## Summary

A question about the number of rak'as in tarawih, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that its most complete form is twenty rak'as besides the shaf' and witr, and that its basic form is achieved with any even number, even two. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4185}

## The question

The questioner asked how many rak'as tarawih has. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4185}

## The answer

The Department answered that the most complete form of tarawih is twenty rak'as besides the shaf' and witr, saying that this is the position of the great majority of scholars, early and later, and is the practice in the lands of the two Holy Mosques. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4185} It said that the basic sunna of tarawih is achieved with any even number of rak'as, even two, prayed with the intention of standing in prayer in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4185}
