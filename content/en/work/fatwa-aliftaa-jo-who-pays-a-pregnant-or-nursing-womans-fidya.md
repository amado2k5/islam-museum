## Summary

A question about who pays a pregnant or nursing woman's fidya, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the fidya is due from the wife's own wealth and is not due from the husband unless he volunteers it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4333}

## The question

The questioner asked whether, when a pregnant or nursing woman owes fidya because she broke the fast out of fear for the child, it is due from her own money or her husband's. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4333}

## The answer

The Department answered that the fidya is due from the wife's wealth and is not owed by the husband unless he chooses to pay it voluntarily. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4333} It quoted [[work/mughni-al-muhtaj|Mughni al-Muhtaj]] (2/174) that when a pregnant or nursing woman fears only for the child, the pregnant woman fearing miscarriage or the nursing woman fearing her milk will lessen and the child perish, she owes fidya from her own wealth together with the make-up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4333}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4333, dated 8 March 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4333} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
