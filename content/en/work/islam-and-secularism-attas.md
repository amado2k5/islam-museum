## Summary

*Islam and Secularism* is a book in English by Syed Muhammad Naquib al-Attas, published by the Muslim Youth Movement of Malaysia (ABIM) in Kuala Lumpur in 1978 in 175 pages and reprinted by ISTAC in 1993. In his note to the first edition the author says the book develops ideas from a treatise he wrote in Malay in 1974, and that its third chapter was delivered as a lecture at an international Islamic conference in April 1976. {cite:openlibrary-islam-and-secularism-1978} {cite:archive-islam-and-secularism-attas}

## Publication and editions

The book begins with the contemporary Western Christian background, then the concepts of secular, secularization and secularism, then the concept of religion and the foundation of ethics in Islam, then what it calls the Muslim dilemma. Its longest part is on 'the dewesternization of knowledge': the nature of man, the nature of knowledge and the definition and aims of education. An appendix treats Islamization in the Malay-Indonesian archipelago. In the preface to the second printing the author says he formulated the ideas of the Islamization of contemporary knowledge and of the Islamic university in the mid-1960s. {cite:archive-islam-and-secularism-attas}
