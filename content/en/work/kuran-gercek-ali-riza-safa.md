# Kur'an Gerçek (Ali Rıza Safa)

## Summary
*Kur'an Gerçek* ("The True Qur'an") is a Turkish rendering of the meanings of the [[work/quran|Qur'an]] by Ali Rıza Safa, first published in 2014 by Ileri in Istanbul, one of more than 150 Turkish translations published in Turkey over the last century. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa}

## Editions
It has appeared in two forms: with introduction and commentary (2014 and 2018), and as text only, in editions up to at least 2021; later editions are titled *Kur'an-i Kerim Gerçek* and carry some corrections. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa} Little is known about the translator, the editions say nothing about him, and he appears to have no links to the network of Turkish scholars trained in the theology faculties. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa}

## What it contains
The translation deliberately replaces Arabic religious vocabulary with vernacular Turkish, down to the name of al-Masjid al-Haram, and is dedicated to Atatürk. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa} In the 2018 introduction Safa argues that reading the Qur'an in Turkish in the obligatory prayers deepens them, and in his notes he rejects hadith qudsi. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa}

## Reception
:::perspectives
- **Sunni (local scholars in Kayseri)**: when the first edition was presented at the Kayseri book fair in 2016, a group of the city's scholars complained to the regional government that it distorted the text (*tahrif*) and asked for its distribution to be halted; no ban or legal action followed. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa}
- **Academic reading**: Mykhaylo Yakubovych sees the deliberate "de-Arabisation" as the reason for the objection and judges it a marginal work, though a useful one for understanding Turkey's contemporary debates between secularism and tradition. {cite:gloqur-kuran-gercek-safa}
:::
