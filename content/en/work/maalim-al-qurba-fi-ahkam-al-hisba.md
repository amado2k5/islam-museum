## Summary
"Maʿalim al-Qurba fi Ahkam al-Hisba" (Landmarks of Piety on the Rulings of Market Oversight) by [[person/ibn-al-ukhuwwa|Ibn al-Ukhuwwa]], written to help the [[concept/hisba|muhtasib]] (market inspector) in his work. It covers oversight of markets, weights and measures, protecting public morals, and combating fraud and forbidden contracts {cite:maalim-qurba-edition-1976 p.9}.

## The standing of hisba
The editors' introduction traces the history of hisba. It states that the Prophet performed the role of muhtasib in early Islam, and that the rightly guided caliphs took it up themselves before appointing others to carry it out as the conquests spread {cite:maalim-qurba-edition-1976 p.5} {cite:maalim-qurba-edition-1976 p.6}.

## Conditions for the muhtasib
The introduction describes the conditions for whoever takes up hisba: knowledge of the law on what he commands and forbids, fairness, good character and gentleness, steady adherence to the Sunna, and permission from the ruler {cite:maalim-qurba-edition-1976 p.6}.
