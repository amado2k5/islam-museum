## Summary
*Al-Manhal al-adhb al-zulal fi hall al-taqwim wa-ru'yat al-hilal*, also known as *al-Manhal al-adhb al-zulal fi ma'rifat hisab al-hilal* (al-Azhar Library no. 10/4383, first treatise), is by Ahmad ibn Rajab ibn Taybugha al-Majdi. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 32} {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 33}

## Contents
The book describes it as a short, concise treatise for finding the beginning of the Arab months by calculation and for the methods of sighting the crescent, showing the author's command of the astronomical terms and symbols of his day; it dates it to the early ninth century AH because it discusses finding the crescent of Ramadan for 812 AH. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 33} It also treats extracting mean motions from tables. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 33}

## Its author in the book
The book says Ibn al-Majdi (767–850 AH) was born and raised in Cairo and wrote nearly fifty books and treatises, among them *Irshad al-ha'ir ila ma'rifat wad' khutut fadl al-da'ir* and *Kashf al-haqa'iq fi hisab al-daraj wal-daqa'iq*. {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 32} {cite:bibalex-azhar-astronomy-manuscripts-2006 p. 33}
