## Summary
*Al-Ilzamat* ("The Obligations") is [[person/al-daraqutni|al-Daraqutni's]] book on hadiths that, in his view, [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] and [[person/muslim-ibn-al-hajjaj|Muslim]] were bound by their own conditions to include. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24}

## Its content as al-Nawawi describes it
In the introduction to [[work/al-minhaj-sharh-sahih-muslim|his commentary on Muslim]], [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]] notes that al-Daraqutni and others "obliged" the two Shaykhs to include hadiths they had left out, although their chains were ones through which the two had included those same narrators in their Sahihs. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24} Al-Daraqutni and others also listed Companions who narrated from the Prophet by sound routes with unimpeachable transmitters, from whom the two included nothing. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24} Of the same kind is al-Bayhaqi's observation that the two agreed on some hadiths of the Sahifa of Hammam b. Munabbih and each included others alone, though the chain is one. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24} Al-Nawawi notes that al-Daraqutni and [[person/abu-dharr-al-harawi|Abu Dharr al-Harawi]] wrote in this genre. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24}

## The reply
:::perspectives
- **Sunni: al-Daraqutni and those who agreed with him**: there are sound hadiths meeting the two Shaykhs' conditions that they left out, and Companions with sound hadiths from whom they took nothing, so by their own method they ought to have included them. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24}
- **Sunni: al-Nawawi**: the obligation is not really binding, since they never undertook to include all sound hadith — indeed they are soundly reported to have said they did not — but meant to collect a body of it, as a jurist collects a body of questions; where an omitted hadith is foundational in its chapter and they gave nothing in its place, the likely explanation is that they found a defect in it, or they may have left it out from forgetfulness, to avoid length, or because another report served instead. {cite:sharh-nawawi-misriyya-1347-v1 p. 24}
:::
