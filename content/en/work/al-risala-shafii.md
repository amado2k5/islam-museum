## In «Asdaf al-Lu'lu'» by Abu Ishaq al-Heweny
[[person/abu-ishaq-al-heweny|Abu Ishaq al-Heweny]] says al-Shafi'i's «al-Risala» is counted the first book in legal theory, that its style is wonderful and its method good, and that the introduction by [[person/ahmad-muhammad-shakir|Ahmad Shakir]] is excellent. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep24 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep26 00:03:00} He disagrees with the interviewer, who says al-Shafi'i's language is hard for a beginner, and says it is far easier than all the other books of legal theory. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep26 00:03:00} He relates that he told an Azhari shaykh responsible for setting curricula that teaching «al-Risala» would be a thousand times better than the set books. {cite:assabile-abu-ishaq-alheweny asdaf-al-loaloa/ep24 00:03:30}
