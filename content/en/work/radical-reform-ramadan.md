## Summary

*Radical Reform: Islamic Ethics and Liberation* is an English book by the Swiss thinker Tariq Ramadan, published by Oxford University Press in New York in 2009 in 372 pages, and catalogued under the interpretation of Islamic law, law reform, ijtihad and Islamic ethics. The scan is restricted and was not read. {cite:openlibrary-radical-reform-2009} {cite:archive-org-radical-reform-ramadan-2009} According to the publisher's description, Ramadan holds that reform movements which renewed the reading of the texts with traditional methods and categories achieved only adaptive responses. He calls instead for a radical reform that questions the sources, categories, higher objectives and methods of Islamic jurisprudence, and proposes a new "geography" of knowledge that gives scholars of the social and natural sciences a share of authority beside textual scholars. {cite:archive-org-radical-reform-ramadan-2009}
