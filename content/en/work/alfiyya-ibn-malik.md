## Overview
"Al-Alfiyya fil-Nahw" (The Thousand-Line Poem on Grammar) by Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Abdullah al-Ta'i al-Jayyani, known as Ibn Malik, who died in 672 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.578}

Its commentaries and glosses are very many, numbering in the dozens, as Hajji Khalifa lists them in "Kashf al-Zunun". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.578}
