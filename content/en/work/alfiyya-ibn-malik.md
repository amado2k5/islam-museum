## Overview
"Al-Alfiyya fil-Nahw" (The Thousand-Line Poem on Grammar) by Jamal al-Din Muhammad ibn Abdullah al-Ta'i al-Jayyani, known as Ibn Malik, who died in 672 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.578}

Its commentaries and glosses are very many, numbering in the dozens, as Hajji Khalifa lists them in "Kashf al-Zunun". {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.578}

## Place in the canon

Alfiyya of Ibn Malik (al-Khulasa al-Alfiyya) appears in a research summary supplied to the museum: a list of sixty of the most-cited works on the Arabic language, twelve in each of its five sciences, where it stands in the section "Grammar". The summary ascribes the work to [[person/ibn-malik-al-jayyani|Ibn Malik]] and gives its date as: author died 1274 CE / 672 AH. It names the English Wikipedia page “Alfiyya of Ibn Malik” as the page documenting the work {cite:wikipedia-alfiyya-ibn-malik-en}. On its presence in the Shamela library the summary says: Yes, it is indexed in the Shamela library, through a Shamela PDF link {cite:shamela-alfiyya-ibn-malik}.

The museum took this from the summary as it stands. The founder approved using the summary without re-verification on 21 November 2026, and the museum did not open the pages it names. The museum has not read the book itself. This record therefore says nothing of the book's contents, chapters, editions or commentaries beyond what other sections of this page cite.
