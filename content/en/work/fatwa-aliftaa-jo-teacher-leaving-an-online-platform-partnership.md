## Summary

A question from a teacher working with an online platform for 60% of the income, with 40% for the platform, after the platform stopped doing its share, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that she is not entitled to more than the agreed share, and that she must first dissolve the partnership if she wants to work alone. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3849}

## The question

The questioner said she teaches a university subject through an online platform for 60%, while the platform's manager takes 40% for advertising the course and uploading the videos; the managers then fell short, so she did the teaching, advertising and uploading herself and warned them without result; she asked whether she may take the full amount and work alone. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3849}

## The answer

The Department answered that a contract is binding law between its parties, and agreed conditions must be honoured if they involve nothing forbidden. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3849} It classed the arrangement between the teacher and the platform owner as a partnership in work (sharikat a'mal), in which profit is divided by the agreed proportion, quoting [[person/burhan-al-din-al-marghinani|al-Marghinani]]'s al-Hidaya that a partnership of crafts is permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3849} It concluded that the teacher who does the platform manager's work is not entitled to anything beyond what they agreed, and if she wants to work alone she must first dissolve the partnership contract and then work on her own. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3849}
