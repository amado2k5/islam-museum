## Summary
Tasrif al-Izzi (al-Izzi fi al-Tasrif) is a book on morphology (sarf and tasrif), by [[person/izz-al-din-al-zanjani|Izz al-Din al-Zanjani]]. The file gives the author's death as 655 AH / 1257 CE; this is the date of the author's death, not of the book's composition. ## The book and its field
Tasrif al-Izzi (al-Izzi fi al-Tasrif) appears in a research summary supplied to the museum that lists sixty of the most-cited classical and modern Arabic works across five sciences, namely dictionaries, grammar, morphology, prosody and rhetoric, twelve for each; this book stands in its section on morphology (sarf and tasrif). [[concept/ilm-al-sarf|Arabic morphology (sarf)]] is the science of the form of the word and its inflection. The title is given in the summary as "Tasrif al-Izzi (al-Izzi fi al-Tasrif)". {cite:ketabonline-tasrif-al-izzi}
