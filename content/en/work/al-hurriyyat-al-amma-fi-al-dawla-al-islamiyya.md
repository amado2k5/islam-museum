## Summary

*Al-Hurriyyat al-'Amma* (Public Liberties in the Islamic Polity) is a book by the Tunisian politician and thinker Rached Ghannouchi. Its first edition came from Markaz Dirasat al-Wahda al-'Arabiyya in Beirut in August 1993 in 382 pages, with the centre's note that the views in the book are not necessarily its own. Only the title and imprint pages of the scan were read. No readable copy was available, so this article is limited to catalogue data. {cite:openlibrary-al-hurriyyat-al-amma-ghannouchi-1993} {cite:archive-org-al-hurriyyat-al-amma-1993}
