## Summary
'Hadaya al-Rahman' ('Gifts of the Merciful') is a short Ramadan programme presented by [[person/mustafa-hosny|Mustafa Hosny]], of which the Assabile archive keeps twenty-nine video episodes of two and a half to five and a half minutes, the speaker talking alone in Egyptian colloquial. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny} The Arabic Wikipedia lists among his radio programmes one of this name on Nogoum FM in Ramadan 2014; what the archive holds are video recordings that give no date. {cite:wikipedia-mustafa-hosny-ar} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny} In the first episode he says that religion is built on ease, that anyone in any circumstances can be righteous through easy good deeds within everyone's reach, which he calls 'gifts from the Merciful', by which God forgives great sins and grants many good deeds. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep01 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep01 00:01:00} In the last episode he says he has spoken of these gifts 'all through Ramadan', and that the deeds he chose were his own selection and research, the Qur'an and the Sunna being full of others. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep29 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep29 00:00:30}

Each episode follows a fixed plan: he cites the hadith, explains 'the meaning of the gift', then asks, 'do you know why God gives you this great reward?', and answers with an educational wisdom behind the reward, such as training in a heart free of rancour, sincerity, mercy or attachment to the mosques. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep03 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep06 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep23 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:03:00} In the thirteenth episode, answering those surprised that sins should be forgiven for small deeds, he states that forgiveness is of two kinds: sins against people, which God does not forgive until the wronged person forgives, and sins against God, whose forgiveness is vast. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:01:00} There he cites [[person/ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyya|Ibn al-Qayyim]] in 'Tariq al-Hijratayn' as saying that the obedience of a human being who has the choice ranks higher with God than that of the angels, who do not disobey. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:02:30}

## The episodes
**Episode 1: introduction.** Religion is built on ease; God reckons some people by their best deeds and passes over their bad ones; man was created weak. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep01 00:02:00}

**Episode 2: caring for the widow and the poor.** He cites a hadith that whoever strives to serve the widow and the poor earns the reward of one who fights in God's path and of one who stands in prayer by night and fasts by day; he says the highest worship is jihad, that the struggle against the self is called the greater jihad, and that one who cannot fast every day or pray all night reaches their reward by serving the needy. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep02 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep02 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep02 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep02 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep02 00:02:30} The wisdom, he says, is that God wants to rid people of selfishness. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep02 00:03:00}

**Episode 3: asking forgiveness for the believers.** The detail is in [[concept/istighfar|istighfar]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep03 00:00:00}

**Episode 4: secret charity.** He cites the hadith of the seven who will be granted God's shade on the Day of Resurrection, among them one who hid his charity, and describes that day as one whose length is fifty thousand years. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep04 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep04 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep04 00:01:00} The detail is in [[practice/sadaqa|charity]].

**Episode 5: the Friday prayer.** The detail is in [[practice/salat-al-jumua|the Friday prayer]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep05 00:00:00}

**Episode 6: the funeral prayer.** The detail is in [[practice/salat-al-janaza|the funeral prayer]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep06 00:00:30}

**Episode 7: the two rak'as of repentance.** He cites a hadith that whoever sins, then performs his ablution well, prays two rak'as and asks forgiveness, is forgiven, and says God does not lie in wait for the sinner, and that behind this lies a worship of the heart, 'the worship of feeling one's guilt'. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep07 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep07 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep07 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep07 00:02:00} He notes that the hadith uses the particle fa, which expresses sequence without delay, meaning ablution straight after the sin, because God loves those who return to Him quickly. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep07 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep07 00:03:00}

**Episode 8: the remembrance of the market.** He cites a hadith narrated by [[person/umar-ibn-al-khattab|Umar ibn al-Khattab]] on saying the formula of God's oneness on entering the market, with a reward of a thousand thousand good deeds; he explains that the Arabs had no word for 'million' and said 'a thousand thousand', and holds that every shop and mall today is a market. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep08 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep08 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep08 00:01:00} He relates that one of the righteous went to Khurasan and told the hadith to Qutayba ibn Muslim, who would then ride to the gate of the market, say it and leave. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep08 00:01:30} The wisdom, for him, is that markets are the centre of desires, so whoever remembers God there tells his Lord he has not forgotten Him at the times he is most taken up with himself. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep08 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep08 00:02:30}

**Episode 9: feeding a fasting person.** He cites a hadith that whoever gives a fasting person his iftar earns the same reward without any loss to the faster's reward, and says this can be with a date or a drink of water. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep09 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep09 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep09 00:03:30} He holds that the hunger of the hungry and the poverty of the poor are a greater test for the rich, and salutes the young men who hand out juice and dates to fasting people in the streets. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep09 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep09 00:03:00}

**Episode 10: the forenoon prayer.** The detail is in [[practice/salat-al-duha|the forenoon prayer]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep10 00:00:00}

**Episode 11: the last verses of Surat al-Baqara.** He cites a hadith that reciting the last two verses of Surat al-Baqara at night suffices the reciter, and says the scholars explained this as their sufficing for the night prayer and protecting him from Satan and from all harm while he sleeps. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep11 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep11 00:00:30} He calls the two verses ([[passage/quran-2-285|al-Baqara 285]] and [[passage/quran-2-286|286]]) 'the constitution of the relationship between human beings and God': belief in all the messengers from Adam to Muhammad, and certainty that God burdens no soul beyond its capacity. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep11 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep11 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep11 00:02:30}

**Episode 12: the formula of oneness a hundred times.** He cites the hadith on saying the formula of God's oneness a hundred times a day, with its reward equal to freeing ten slaves, a hundred good deeds written, a hundred bad deeds erased and protection from Satan, and says it takes about ten minutes. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep12 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep12 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep12 00:01:00} He estimates the price of a slave today, were there slaves, at about two thousand pounds, making the freeing equal to spending twenty thousand pounds, an estimate of his own. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep12 00:01:30} He cites what he calls in psychology 'the theory of affirmation': repeating a meaning settles it in the heart. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep12 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep12 00:03:00}

**Episode 13: why these gifts?** An episode without a particular hadith, answering those surprised that forgiveness should be so easy; the detail is in the summary above. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep13 00:01:30}

**Episode 14: charity and a good end.** The detail is in [[practice/sadaqa|charity]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep14 00:00:00}

**Episode 15: sleeping in purity.** He cites a hadith that an angel spends the night with whoever sleeps in a state of purity and asks forgiveness for him each time he turns over, says sleep is servanthood just as waking is, and quotes [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali ibn Abi Talib]] as hoping for reward for his sleep as for his night prayer. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep15 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep15 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep15 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep15 00:02:00} He cites a hadith he attributes to al-Tirmidhi that God loves beauty and cleanliness, and says the Prophet was known by his perfume. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep15 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep15 00:03:00}

**Episode 16: the master supplication of forgiveness.** The detail is in [[concept/istighfar|istighfar]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep16 00:00:30}

**Episode 17: putting on a new garment.** He cites a hadith he attributes to al-Hakim's 'al-Mustadrak' on praising God when putting on a new garment, whose sayer is forgiven his past sins, and says sins begin to be written from the age of legal responsibility. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep17 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep17 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep17 00:01:00} He says many sins veil inner sight, and that God wants His servant to rejoice in his clothing in this world and in His forgiveness in the next. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep17 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep17 00:02:30}

**Episode 18: guiding to good.** He cites a hadith that whoever guides others to right guidance earns the like of the rewards of those who act on it, without any loss to theirs, and gives examples: teaching someone to pray, to work with excellence, to apologise, or to give charity. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:01:30} He holds that God loves to build the positive person who wants to see all people pleasing to God, and cites the hadith likening the believer to rain that benefits wherever it falls. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep18 00:03:00}

**Episode 19: glorifying God a hundred times.** He cites a hadith he attributes to al-Bukhari and Muslim that whoever glorifies and praises God a hundred times a day has his sins forgiven, however many they are, like the foam of the sea, which he explains as the froth on a sea whose end cannot be seen. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep19 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep19 00:00:30} He explains glorification as declaring God free of error, shortcoming and unwisdom, and praise as contentment with all He does. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep19 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep19 00:02:30}

**Episode 20: removing harm from the road.** He relates that a man asked the Prophet to teach him something useful and was told to look for what harms people and move it from their path, and that faith has more than sixty branches, among them removing harm. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep20 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep20 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep20 00:01:30} He widens harm to the negative word that breaks ambition, the wounding expression and clothing that tempts. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep20 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep20 00:02:00}

**Episode 21: earning by one's own hand.** He cites a hadith that the food dearest to God is what a servant earns by the toil of his hand, and that whoever sleeps tired from his work sleeps forgiven, and says this concerns the rights of God, not those of people. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep21 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep21 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep21 00:01:30} He gives, without naming a source, a statistic that people in the Arab world work twenty-seven minutes out of eight hours (needs_source), and calls for excellence in work. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep21 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep21 00:02:30}

**Episode 22: congregational prayer and walking to the mosque.** He cites the hadith on the multiplied reward of prayer in congregation, that every step to the mosque raises a degree and removes a sin, that the angels pray for the worshipper as long as he stays in his place, and that he is in prayer as long as he waits for it. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:02:30} He says the distance between two degrees of Paradise is as that between heaven and earth, and draws from it generous hospitality and attachment to the mosques. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep22 00:03:30}

**Episode 23: wiping an orphan's head.** The detail is in [[practice/kafalat-al-yatim|caring for the orphan]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep23 00:00:00}

**Episode 24: invoking blessings on the Prophet.** The detail is in [[concept/al-salat-ala-al-nabi|invoking blessings on the Prophet]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep24 00:00:00}

**Episode 25: sitting after the dawn prayer until sunrise.** The detail is in [[practice/salat-al-duha|the forenoon prayer]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep25 00:00:00}

**Episode 26: walking to the mosque in the dark.** He cites a hadith that whoever walks to the mosque in the darkness of night will meet God on the Day of Resurrection with light, and says the Day of Resurrection is dark because the sun's light goes while its heat remains, and that it comes as near to people's heads as a mile. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep26 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep26 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep26 00:01:00} He says this merit is for men and women alike, and that whoever prays the night prayer in congregation has the reward of standing half the night, and whoever prays the dawn prayer in congregation that of standing the whole night. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep26 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep26 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep26 00:04:00}

**Episode 27: much prostration.** The detail is in [[person/thawban-mawla-al-nabi|Thawban]]. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep27 00:00:00}

**Episode 28: repeating the call to prayer.** He relates that [[person/bilal-ibn-rabah|Bilal]] gave the call to prayer and the Prophet said whoever repeats it with certainty enters Paradise, and says the call to prayer is the summary of the believers' creed and of the servant's bond with his Lord. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:01:30} He explains the takbir as God being greater in the heart than everything, the testimony as seeing that there is no god in the universe but God, and the call to success as happiness, achievement and peace in the bond with God, and then mentions the remembrance said after the call and its reward. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep28 00:04:30}

**Episode 29: conclusion.** He says the goal is not the deeds themselves but the bond with God and being a righteous person useful to those around one, and ends with a supplication on the eve of the Eid. {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep29 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep29 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep29 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-mustafa-hosny hadaya-al-rahman/ep29 00:03:30}
