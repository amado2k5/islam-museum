## Summary

A question about using toothpaste during the day in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it does not break the fast if the person is sure none of it reached the interior, but that it is disliked, and is better used before the fast begins or after it ends. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4126}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on using toothpaste during the day in Ramadan. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4126}

## The answer

The Department answered that using toothpaste during the day in Ramadan does not break the fast if the person is sure none of it reached the interior, but that it is disliked. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4126} It said it is better for the fasting person to use it before the start of the fast or after breaking it, so as not to put the fast at risk. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4126}
