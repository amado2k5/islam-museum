## Summary

A question about accepting a gift a brother received from his commercial bank, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that there is no objection to accepting the gift, because the gift itself carries no prohibition and any sin does not pass to the recipient. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4457}

## The question

The questioner asked about accepting a gift his brother gave him from among the gifts a commercial bank distributes to its employees. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4457}

## The answer

The Department answered that there is no objection to accepting such gifts, because the gift itself carries no prohibition, unlike stolen property, so any sin does not extend to the recipient, citing [[passage/quran-6-164|Surat al-An'am 6:164]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4457} It noted that the Prophet ate the food of Jews and traded and bought from them, although their wealth was mixed with riba, and that the wealth of interest-based banks is of the kind in which lawful and unlawful are mixed. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4457} It quoted [[person/ibn-hajar-al-haytami|Ibn Hajar al-Haytami]] in [[work/tuhfat-al-muhtaj|Tuhfat al-Muhtaj]] (9/389) that dealing with someone most of whose wealth is unlawful, or eating from it, is not forbidden, as [[person/al-nawawi|al-Nawawi]] affirmed in [[work/al-majmu-sharh-al-muhadhdhab|al-Majmu]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4457}
