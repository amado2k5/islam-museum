## Overview
"Tawq al-Hamama fil-Ulfa wal-Ulaf" (The Dove's Neckring: On Affection and Companionship), a book by Abu Muhammad Ibn Hazm of al-Andalus. It deals with the conditions of lovers, the kinds of passion, and the reports of those in love. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}

The source counts it among the best-known works of Andalusian literature. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari}
