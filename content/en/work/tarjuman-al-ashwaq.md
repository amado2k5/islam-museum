## Summary
"Tarjuman al-Ashwaq" (The Interpreter of Desires), a collection of poetry composed by Muhyi al-Din Ibn ʿArabi. It is published by Dar al-Maʿrifa in Beirut (2005) under the title "Diwan Tarjuman al-Ashwaq" {cite:diwan-tarjuman-al-ashwaq-dar-marefah-2005 p.1}. The edition opens the collection with verses in which the poet says that he is of Arab lineage and loves the Arabs {cite:diwan-tarjuman-al-ashwaq-dar-marefah-2005 p.6}.

## Note on the edition
The title of the collection and the name of the person who prepared the edition are read by optical character recognition (OCR) in a garbled form, so they have not yet been relied on from a second source. The verses are described here only as the opening of the edition, and nothing from them has been carried into this entry.
