## Overview
The Mufradat fi gharib al-Qur'an, also known as the Mufradat alfaz al-Qur'an, is [[person/al-raghib-al-isfahani|al-Raghib al-Isfahani]]'s alphabetical lexicon of Qur'anic vocabulary and the clearest display of his favourite method: subtle semantic analysis that distinguishes near-identical meanings {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 389-390} {cite:al-aalam 2/261-262}.

## Among his works
It belongs to a series of Qur'anic monographs: in its introduction the author cites the Risala munabbiha 'ala fawa'id al-Qur'an and the Risala fi'l-qawanin al-dalla 'ala tahqiq munasabat al-alfaz, both apparently lost, and promises a study of the "obscure distinctions" between apparent synonyms in the holy book — probably the Durrat al-ta'wil fi mutashabih al-tanzil {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 389-390}.

## Influence
The Mufradat's influence can be traced in later tafsir and lexicography; it became a standing reference for anyone working on the vocabulary of the Qur'an {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 8 pp. 389-390}.
