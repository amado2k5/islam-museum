## Summary

A question about vomiting during the day in Ramadan, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that deliberate vomiting breaks the fast, while someone overcome by vomiting does not break it unless some of it returns to the interior. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4140}

## The question

The questioner asked whether vomiting during the day in Ramadan breaks the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4140}

## The answer

The Department answered that deliberately making oneself vomit is among the things that break the fast, so whoever vomits on purpose has broken it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4140} It said that someone overcome by vomiting has not broken the fast if none of it returns to the interior; otherwise he has. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4140} It relied on a hadith reported by [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] and [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]] that distinguishes the person overcome by vomiting, who owes no make-up day, from the one who vomits deliberately, who must make the day up. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4140}
