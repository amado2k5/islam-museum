## Summary

*Muhammad: A Prophet for Our Time* is a book by Karen Armstrong, published by HarperCollins in London in 2006. {cite:archive-org-muhammad-armstrong-indonesian-mizan-2007}

## Publication and editions

Its Indonesian translation by Yuhani Liputo was published by Mizan in Bandung in April 2007, with an introduction by Jalaluddin Rakhmat titled 'Simpatik tapi Tidak Kritis' (sympathetic but not critical). Its contents show five chapters, the first three titled Makkah, Jahiliah and Hijrah, and the fifth Salam. {cite:archive-org-muhammad-armstrong-indonesian-mizan-2007}
