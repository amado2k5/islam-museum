## Summary

*Le harem politique: le Prophète et les femmes* is a French book by the Moroccan sociologist Fatima Mernissi, published by Albin Michel in Paris in 1987 in 293 pages, with a bibliography on pages 249–269. {cite:openlibrary-le-harem-politique-1987} {cite:archive-org-le-harem-politique-1987} Its English translation appeared as *The Veil and the Male Elite* from Addison-Wesley in Reading, Massachusetts, in 1991, with a bibliography on pages 217–222. {cite:archive-org-veil-and-the-male-elite-1991}
