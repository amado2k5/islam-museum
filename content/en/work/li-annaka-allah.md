## Summary

*Li-Annaka Allah: Rihla ila al-Sama' al-Sabi'a* (Because You Are God: A Journey to the Seventh Heaven) is a book by the Saudi writer Ali ibn Jabir al-Fayfi, published in January 2016 by Dar al-Hadara li-l-Nashr wa-l-Tawzi' in about 192 pages. In his opening words the author says he wrote it about some of the Beautiful Names of God, and took care that a reader of average learning could follow it, and that the sick, the grieving and the distressed could read it. {cite:mawdoo3-li-annaka-allah} {cite:archive-org-li-annaka-allah-text}
