## Overview
"Al-Jami al-Musnad al-Sahih al-Mukhtasar min Umur Rasul Allah wa Sunanihi wa Ayyamihi", by Muhammad ibn Isma'il [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] al-Ju'fi. {cite:sahih-al-bukhari-bugha p.1}

The book's character and method are not described here: the local copies do not show the accounts that introductions give of it. They will be added from a documented source in a later batch.
