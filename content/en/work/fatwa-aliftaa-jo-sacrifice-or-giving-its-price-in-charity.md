## Summary

A question about offering the sacrifice or giving its price in charity, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that offering the sacrifice is better than giving its price in charity, because it is a visible rite of the religion. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4359}

## The question

The questioner asked which is better: offering the sacrifice or giving its price in charity. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4359}

## The answer

The Department answered that offering the sacrifice is better than giving its price to the poor and needy, because it is one of the visible rites of the religion, citing [[passage/quran-22-32|Surat al-Hajj 22:32]]. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4359} It noted that clear authentic hadith speak of its merit, and that the Prophet and the caliphs after him offered sacrifices. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4359}
