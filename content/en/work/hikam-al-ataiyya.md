## Maxims explained by Omar Abdelkafy
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy explains this book maxim by maxim in the series [[work/sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya|Sharh al-Hikam al-'Ata'iyya]]. Among the maxims he treats in the first episodes: that a servant's asking of his Lord can amount to suspecting Him, that asking for Him is an absence from Him, that asking for other than Him comes from too little shame before Him, and that asking from other than Him comes from distance from Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:38:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:45:30}; that whenever a seeker's aspiration wants to halt at what has been unveiled to it, the voices of reality call out that what it seeks lies ahead, and whenever the outward beauty of created things displays itself, their realities call out that they are a trial {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:46:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:51:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:59:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 01:04:30}; that a sign of success at the end is turning to God at the beginning {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:22:00}; the difference between those who reason from God and those who reason towards Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:47:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 01:07:00}; wonder at the one who flees from what he can never be free of and seeks what will not stay with him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:42:00}; that a servant should not raise his need to anyone but the One who brought it upon him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:50:00}; that one may be doing wrong yet think oneself doing well because one keeps company with someone worse {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:15:30}; not to keep company with one whose state does not lift you and whose words do not lead you to God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:46:00}; and not to travel from one created thing to another but from created things to their Creator {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:48:30}. The maxims are given here by their sense, because the transcript of the recording is not exact.

## From the fifth and sixth episodes
In the fifth and sixth episodes the Sheikh explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim that God made the next world the place where His believing servants are rewarded because this world cannot hold what He wishes to give them, and because He holds them too high to reward them in an abode that does not last {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:48:30}; the maxim that light is the army of the heart as darkness is the army of the self, so that when God wills to aid His servant He supplies him with armies of light and cuts off from him the supply of darkness and of what is other than Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:43:00}; and the maxim that among the signs of a dead heart is not grieving over acts of obedience one has missed and not regretting the slips one has made. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:54:30}

## From episodes seven to nine
In episodes seven to nine he explains the maxim that a servant should not give up remembrance of God because his heart is not present in it, since his heedlessness of the very existence of remembrance is worse than his heedlessness within it, and God may raise him from remembrance with heedlessness to remembrance with wakefulness, then to remembrance with presence, then to remembrance with absence from all but the One remembered {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:20:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:36:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:36:30}; the maxim that a servant should fear that God's continued kindness to him alongside his continued wrongdoing may be a leading-on to ruin {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:47:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep07 00:48:00}; the maxim that it is the novice's ignorance to behave badly and, finding the punishment delayed, to think that had it been bad conduct the supply would have been cut off, when it may be cut off without his noticing, if only by the withholding of more {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:27:30}; the maxim that a sin that leaves humility and brokenness is better than an act of obedience that leaves pride and arrogance {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:04:30}; the maxim that God set some people to His service and singled out others for His love, and supplies both from His giving {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:40:00}; the maxim that whoever finds the fruit of his deed in this life has a sign of acceptance in the next {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:50:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep08 00:51:30}; and the maxim that God may give and thereby withhold, and may withhold and thereby give. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep09 00:43:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep09 00:47:30}

## From episodes ten to thirteen
In episodes ten to thirteen he explains the maxim that created things are outwardly a lure and inwardly a lesson, so that the self looks at the outward lure and the heart at the inward lesson {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:31:00}; the maxim that whoever wants a glory that does not perish should not seek glory in what perishes {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:31:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep10 00:38:00}; the maxim that when God gives to a servant He shows him His kindness, and when He withholds He shows him His power, and in all of it He is making Himself known {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep11 00:30:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep11 00:42:00}; the maxim that the best of a servant's times is the time in which he witnesses his own neediness and is brought back to his own lowliness {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep12 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep12 00:21:00}; the maxim that when God loosens a servant's tongue in asking, he should know that God wishes to give to him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep12 00:22:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep12 00:23:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep12 00:27:00}; the maxim that what lightens the pain of affliction is knowing that God is the one who tests {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 00:23:00}; the maxim that the danger for a servant is not that the paths become confused for him but that desire overcomes him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 00:48:00}; and the maxim that when God makes a servant obedient to His command outwardly and grants him surrender to His decree inwardly, He has shown him the greatest favour. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 01:11:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep13 01:11:30}

## From episodes fourteen to seventeen
In the last episodes, fourteen to seventeen, he explains the maxim that the heedless man wakes and looks at what he will do, while the intelligent man looks at what God will do with him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 00:05:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 00:26:30}; the maxim that because God knew the servant tires easily He varied the acts of obedience for him, and because He knew his greed He restricted them at certain times {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 00:34:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 00:40:30}; the maxim that the servant needs God's forbearance more when he obeys Him than when he disobeys {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:15:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep14 01:15:30}; the maxim that if a servant could reach God only after his faults were gone and his claims erased he would never reach Him, but when God wills to bring him near He covers his attributes with His own {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:07:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:26:00}; the maxim that were it not for the beauty of God's veiling, no deed would be fit to be accepted {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:28:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:39:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:44:30}; the maxim that whoever honours you honours in you only the beauty of God's veiling {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:58:00}; the maxim that the best companion is one who seeks you for nothing that comes back to him from you, and that the only one who truly keeps you company knowing all your faults is your noble Lord {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 00:59:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep15 01:03:30}; the maxim that since God forbade the servant to claim what belongs to other creatures, how could He allow him to claim His own attribute, the Lord of the worlds {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 00:29:00}; the maxim 'how can the usual order be broken for you when you have not broken your own habits?' {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 00:29:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 00:41:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep16 00:48:00}; the maxim that a sin should not make a servant despair of uprightness, for it may be the last sin decreed for him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep17 00:11:30}; and the maxim that the self's share in sin is plain and evident while its share in obedience is hidden and subtle, and what is hidden is hard to cure. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep17 00:43:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep17 00:53:30}
