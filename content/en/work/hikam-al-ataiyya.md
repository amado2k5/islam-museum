## Maxims explained by Omar Abdelkafy
Sheikh Omar Abdelkafy explains this book maxim by maxim in the series [[work/sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya|Sharh al-Hikam al-'Ata'iyya]]. Among the maxims he treats in the first episodes: that a servant's asking of his Lord can amount to suspecting Him, that asking for Him is an absence from Him, that asking for other than Him comes from too little shame before Him, and that asking from other than Him comes from distance from Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:38:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:45:30}; that whenever a seeker's aspiration wants to halt at what has been unveiled to it, the voices of reality call out that what it seeks lies ahead, and whenever the outward beauty of created things displays itself, their realities call out that they are a trial {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:46:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:51:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 00:59:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep01 01:04:30}; that a sign of success at the end is turning to God at the beginning {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:22:00}; the difference between those who reason from God and those who reason towards Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:47:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 00:47:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep02 01:07:00}; wonder at the one who flees from what he can never be free of and seeks what will not stay with him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:31:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:41:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:42:00}; that a servant should not raise his need to anyone but the One who brought it upon him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep03 00:50:00}; that one may be doing wrong yet think oneself doing well because one keeps company with someone worse {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:15:30}; not to keep company with one whose state does not lift you and whose words do not lead you to God {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:42:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:46:00}; and not to travel from one created thing to another but from created things to their Creator {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:48:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep04 00:48:30}. The maxims are given here by their sense, because the transcript of the recording is not exact.

## From the fifth and sixth episodes
In the fifth and sixth episodes the Sheikh explains Ibn Ata Allah's maxim that God made the next world the place where His believing servants are rewarded because this world cannot hold what He wishes to give them, and because He holds them too high to reward them in an abode that does not last {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:27:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep05 00:48:30}; the maxim that light is the army of the heart as darkness is the army of the self, so that when God wills to aid His servant He supplies him with armies of light and cuts off from him the supply of darkness and of what is other than Him {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:43:00}; and the maxim that among the signs of a dead heart is not grieving over acts of obedience one has missed and not regretting the slips one has made. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:44:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-al-hekam-al-ataeya/ep06 00:54:30}
