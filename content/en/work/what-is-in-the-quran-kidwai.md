# What is in the Quran? Message of the Quran in Simple English (Abdur Raheem Kidwai)

## Summary
*What is in the Quran?* is a simple-English presentation of the meanings of the [[work/quran|Qur'an]] by Abdur Raheem Kidwai, first published in 2013, with a revised and expanded third edition from Viva Books, New Delhi, in 2023. {cite:gloqur-kidwai-simple-english}

## The author
Kidwai, born in 1956, directs a Qur'anic studies centre at Aligarh Muslim University and holds PhDs in English literature from Aligarh and Leicester; he is known for his reviews of others' translations and for three bibliographies of English Qur'an translations, the first of which (2007), printed by the [[group/king-fahd-complex-for-printing-the-holy-quran|King Fahd Complex]], reviews over seventy. {cite:gloqur-kidwai-simple-english}

## What it contains
Kidwai says his work is not strictly a translation but an attempt to present the meaning and message of the Qur'an in simple, fluent English; it seems aimed at readers with limited knowledge of the Islamic tradition or with English as a second language. {cite:gloqur-kidwai-simple-english} For all its simplicity it carries 410 footnotes introducing basic beliefs and concepts, rarely discusses Arabic terms or justifies its choices, and chooses pronouns to fit the context. {cite:gloqur-kidwai-simple-english} Mykhaylo Yakubovych notes its frequent language of gender justice and its references to the Bible and to common ground among the Abrahamic religions. {cite:gloqur-kidwai-simple-english}

## Reception
Yakubovych observes that, although a forerunner of "simple" translations that mostly represents mainstream exegesis, it has not become widely popular, and that Kidwai's reviews of other translations have had more impact than his own. {cite:gloqur-kidwai-simple-english}
