## Summary
The Kuwaiti Jurisprudential Encyclopedia is a modern encyclopedia of jurisprudence published by the Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs in Kuwait. It arranges the terms of jurisprudence alphabetically, sets out in each question the different jurisprudential positions with their evidence, and covers the jurisprudential heritage up to the thirteenth century AH. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1326} {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1331}

## What the encyclopedia is here
The introduction defines the encyclopedia as a book comprising the information of one or more sciences, presented under commonly accepted headings in a set order, in a simplified style, with the information attributed to its sources. Its basic arrangement is by the terms in common use in jurisprudence. The introduction distinguishes it from compendia and lengthy treatises that do not take terms as the basis of research. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1326}

## What it includes and excludes
The introduction says that the encyclopedia includes a modern formulation of the heritage of Islamic jurisprudence up to the thirteenth century AH. It excludes codifications, whether positive, because they are not Islamic jurisprudence, or legislative, because they generally rest on recent independent reasoning, and it also excludes personal preference and sectarian debate. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1331}

## How it presents matters
A question is presented by listing its various opinions. The position taken by most jurists is given first, each position is followed by the views of the four schools that agree with it, and the positions of the early generations are noted where available. The introduction says that this method is difficult for authors, and that it allows the reader to see the points of agreement and disagreement. {cite:fiqh-mawsua-kuwaitiyya-noor-v3 p.1341}
