## Summary

A question about fasting six days of Shawwal, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is a sunna, relying on a hadith reported by [[person/muslim-ibn-al-hajjaj|Muslim]] that whoever follows Ramadan with six days of Shawwal is as if he had fasted the whole year. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4176}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on fasting six days of Shawwal. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4176}

## The answer

The Department answered that fasting six days of Shawwal is a sunna, relying on a hadith reported by Muslim that whoever fasts Ramadan and then follows it with six days of Shawwal is as one who fasts the whole year. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4176} It explained that the reward of fasting Ramadan is like that of fasting ten months and the reward of the six days is like that of sixty days, which together complete the year. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4176}
