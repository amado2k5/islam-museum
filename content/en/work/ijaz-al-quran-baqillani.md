## Summary
Al-Baqillani's I'jaz al-Qur'an, printed several times, is regarded as a classic work on the subject of the Qur'an's inimitability; it is one of only six extant works out of the fifty-two titles that the qadi Iyad listed for him {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 1 p. 1018}.
