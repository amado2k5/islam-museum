## Overview
Verses composed by [[person/abbas-ibn-mirdas|Abbas ibn Mirdas al-Sulami]], in which he reproaches the Prophet after his share was less than a hundred camels while [[person/al-aqra-ibn-habis|al-Aqra]] and [[person/uyayna-ibn-hisn|Uyayna]] were given more. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.137}

## Context
This happened during the division of the spoils of Hunayn at al-Ji'rana, in Dhu al-Qa'da 8 AH according to the derived date in the data. See [[event/distribution-of-hunayn-spoils|the distribution of the spoils of Hunayn]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.137}

## After the rebuke
The account says that the Prophet completed Abbas's share to one hundred camels, and that he was content. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa p.137}
