## Summary
Safinat al-Najah in Shafi'i fiqh by Salim ibn Abdullah ibn Sumayr al-Hadrami (d. 1271 AH) — a prose primer gathering the first chapters of worship and dealings on the school; the beginner's text in the Hadramawt and Javanese schools {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: ابن سمير الحضرمي شافعي من مشايخ حضرموت وجاوة.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليها شروح منها كفاية المستفيد ونواهل العطار وكاشفة السجا {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.

## For younger visitors
Safinat al-Najah is the first Shafi'i fiqh book in the Hadramawt and Indonesian schools.
