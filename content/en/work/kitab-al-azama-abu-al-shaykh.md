## Summary
"Kitab al-Azama" ("The Book of Greatness") by [[person/abu-al-shaykh-al-isfahani|Abu al-Shaykh al-Isfahani]] is the source [[person/al-suyuti|al-Suyuti]] relied on in [[work/al-haya-al-saniyya-suyuti|al-Hay'a al-saniyya]] so heavily that he owes it almost every fragment. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 48} Heinen says the book reflects its author's piety and his devotion to hadith and tafsir. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 48}

## Its framework
Heinen holds that the book's cosmological material stood within a framework of reflection (tafakkur) on God's creation and His glorification, and that al-Suyuti, by omitting the theological matter and isolating the cosmology, gave it a more marked cosmological appearance. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 62} In his academic view, taking the material out of that framework was a misuse of Kitab al-Azama. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 62}

## Al-Suyuti's quotations
Al-Suyuti often pairs Abu al-Shaykh with Ibn Abi Hatim's Tafsir, as in the opening chapter on the Throne and the Footstool. {cite:heinen-islamic-cosmology p. 140}
