## Overview
The novel by Orhan Pamuk (2004): the poet Ka returns from exile to the border city of Kars amid a coup, a theatre play and an investigation into the suicides of veiled women, while snow covers everything. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Among Pamuk's most political novels; it asks about secularism and Islam in modern Turkey, two years before the Nobel Prize. {cite:wikidata}
