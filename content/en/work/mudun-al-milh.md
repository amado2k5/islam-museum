## Overview
The pentalogy by Abd al-Rahman Munif (1984–1989) that follows the Arabian Peninsula from nomadism to oil: five novels narrating the founding of cities on the sands of tribes and the transformation of the land and its values under money and power. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Regarded as the greatest Arabic novel epic about oil and the transformations of the state and society; it was long banned in the Gulf countries, and its first volume was translated into English as 'Cities of Salt'. {cite:wikidata}
