## Summary

A question on repeating the funeral prayer, in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] answered that there is no harm in it when one comes upon a group praying over the deceased. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4007}

## The question

The questioner asked whether the funeral prayer may be repeated. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4007}

## The answer

Ibn Baz answered that if someone has prayed over the deceased and then comes upon people praying over him at the grave and joins them, there is no harm in it. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4007} He compared it to repeating an obligatory prayer, as when someone who has already prayed comes to another mosque for some need or to attend a lesson while the people are praying, and prays with them. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4007} He said repeating for a legitimate reason is acceptable. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4007}
