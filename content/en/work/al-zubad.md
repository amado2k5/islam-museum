## Summary
Al-Zubad of Ibn Ruslan in Shafi'i fiqh by Ahmad ibn Ruslan al-Ja'di (d. 844 AH) — a didactic poem of about a hundred and forty rajaz lines epitomising the school's fiqh chapters; a Shafi'i verse matn in the lessons {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.

## The author
المؤلف: ابن رسلان الجعدي شافعي من أهل الشام.

## Commentaries and teaching
عليها شرح الشوبري «توفيق الرحمن» وشرح الشقيري وشرح ابن عوض {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar}.

## For younger visitors
Al-Zubad is the short poem epitomising Shafi'i fiqh.
