## Summary

*The Succession to Muhammad: A Study of the Early Caliphate* is a book by Wilferd Madelung, published by Cambridge University Press in 1997 in 413 pages. {cite:openlibrary-succession-to-muhammad-1997}

## Publication and editions

The book has an introduction and four chapters on Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali, followed by a conclusion and seven excursuses, among them two on the burial and the inheritance of Muhammad and one on the sources for the crisis of Uthman's caliphate. {cite:archive-org-succession-to-muhammad-scan} In his preface Madelung says the book began as a study of the nature of the caliphate at its foundation and grew into a partial narrative history of the period, because he judged the wholesale rejection of the Muslim narrative sources as late fiction unjustified and found that their careful use gives a more accurate picture of the period, especially of the fitna. {cite:archive-org-succession-to-muhammad-scan}
