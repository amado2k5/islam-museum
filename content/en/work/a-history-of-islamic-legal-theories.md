## Summary

*A History of Islamic Legal Theories: An Introduction to Sunni Usul al-Fiqh* is a book by Wael B. Hallaq, published by Cambridge University Press in 1997 in 294 pages. {cite:openlibrary-history-islamic-legal-theories-1997} In his preface the author explains that he chose the plural 'theories' deliberately, to show that usul al-fiqh was not a single fixed system but an umbrella that held variation both within one period and across periods. {cite:archive-org-history-of-islamic-legal-theories-scan}

## Publication and editions

The book has six chapters: the formative period to the end of the third/ninth century, the articulation of legal theory in two chapters, the legal text, the world and history, social reality and the response of theory, and finally the crises of modernity and the question of a new legal theory. {cite:archive-org-history-of-islamic-legal-theories-scan}
