## Summary

*Islam and Gender: The Religious Debate in Contemporary Iran* is an English book by the anthropologist Ziba Mir-Hosseini, published by Princeton University Press on 15 November 1999. {cite:openlibrary-islam-and-gender-1999} It has 305 pages. The publisher's description says the author held a series of interviews with clerics in Qom to understand notions of gender in jurisprudence, and found three approaches: holding to traditional interpretations based on "complementarity" between men and women, attempts to introduce "balance" into those interpretations, and a radical rethinking of the jurisprudential construction of gender. {cite:archive-org-islam-and-gender-mir-hosseini-1999}
