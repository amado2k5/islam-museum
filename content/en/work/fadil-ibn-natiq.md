## Overview
The novel of Ibn al-Nafis (thirteenth century), 'Theologus Autodidactus': a child born on a deserted island discovers for himself the existence of God, prophethood and the laws, centuries before Ibn Tufayl and Defoe. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
One of the oldest philosophical stories in the world; Orientalists read it as a possible influence on Robinson Crusoe and European philosophy. {cite:wikidata}
