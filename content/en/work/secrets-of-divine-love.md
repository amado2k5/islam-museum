## Summary

*Secrets of Divine Love: A Spiritual Journey into the Heart of Islam* is an English book by A. Helwa, published by Naulit Publishing House on 20 February 2020 in 385 pages. It has eleven chapters. The first presents God as the origin of love, the second asks 'Who are you?' about the human being's place before God, the third treats the world of the Qur'an, and the sixth the shahada. {cite:openlibrary-secrets-of-divine-love-2020} {cite:archive-org-secrets-of-divine-love-text}
