## Summary

*No god but God: The Origins, Evolution, and Future of Islam* is an English book by the writer Reza Aslan, published by Random House in New York in 2005; a William Heinemann edition of 288 pages appeared the same year. {cite:openlibrary-no-god-but-god-2005} According to its contents, it opens with a prologue titled "the clash of monotheisms" and a chronology, then treats pre-Islamic Arabia, Muhammad in Mecca, the first Muslims in Medina, the meaning of jihad, the rightly guided successors, the development of theology and law, Shiism, Sufism, the response to colonialism, and what it calls the Islamic reformation, with a glossary and a bibliography on pages 289–294. {cite:archive-org-no-god-but-god-2005}
