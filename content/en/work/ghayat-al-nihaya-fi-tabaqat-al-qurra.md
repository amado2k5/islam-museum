## Summary
In its discussion of the chain as a pillar of a valid reading, al-Nashr says that since a sound chain is a pillar of a reading, the state of the men of the readings must be known as that of the men of hadith is, that people long ago took care over this, and that whoever wants full coverage should consult his book Ghayat al-Nihaya fi Asma' Rijal al-Qira'at Uli al-Riwaya wa-l-Diraya. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 203}

## Among his works
The biography of the author in Dahman's edition lists among his works the larger and smaller 'Tabaqat al-Qurra'' and 'Ghayat al-Nihayat fi Asma' Rijal al-Qira'at', beside al-Nashr, Tahbir al-Taysir, Tayyibat al-Nashr, al-Durra and the Muqaddima on tajwid. {cite:nashr-dahman-damascus-1345-v1 p. 8} See [[work/al-nashr-fi-al-qiraat-al-ashr|al-Nashr]] and [[work/tayyibat-al-nashr|the Tayyiba]].

## In the notes of the Itqan
The notes of the King Fahd edition refer to Ghayat al-Nihaya for the biographies of people al-Suyuti names, among them al-Qasim ibn Firruh al-Shatibi and Muntajab al-Din al-Hamadhani. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 26} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 486} The book itself was not read here.
