## Summary
A series by Omar Abdelkafy in which he reads hadiths from Sahih al-Bukhari, explains them and draws lessons. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 01: introduction to the series and deeds are by intentions
- He opens by saying the series explains the hadiths of Sahih al-Bukhari, taking three or four hadiths in a session, and that these hadiths cover the whole religion, from revelation to prayer, worship, dealings and every side of life, all drawn from the Prophet's Sunna. - He says Ibn Hajar spent twenty-five years explaining al-Bukhari's hadiths in Fath al-Bari, that al-Bukhari himself spent years gathering them and examining their narrators for soundness and trustworthiness, and that the community received the book with acceptance as the soundest book after the Book of God. See [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani]], [[work/fath-al-bari|Fath al-Bari]] and [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]]. This is his view. ## Episode 02: the Companions, the hadith of intentions and the beginning of revelation
