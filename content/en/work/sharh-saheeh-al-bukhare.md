## Summary
A series by Omar Abdelkafy in which he reads hadiths from Sahih al-Bukhari, explains them and draws lessons. This set holds episodes from the Assabile archive, and everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript; what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

## Episode 01: introduction to the series and deeds are by intentions
- He opens by saying the series explains the hadiths of Sahih al-Bukhari, taking three or four hadiths in a session, and that these hadiths cover the whole religion, from revelation to prayer, worship, dealings and every side of life, all drawn from the Prophet's Sunna. See [[person/ibn-hajar-al-asqalani|Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani]], [[work/fath-al-bari|Fath al-Bari]] and [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]]. This is his view. ## Episode 02: the Companions, the hadith of intentions and the beginning of revelation
