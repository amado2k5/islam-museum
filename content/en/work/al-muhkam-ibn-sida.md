## Overview
"Al-Muhkam wal-Muhit al-Azam" (The Firm and the Greatest Ocean), a dictionary of the Arabic language by Ibn Sida al-Mursi. Al-Matmah describes it as the most original and most carefully made book of its kind, and says that the author was alone in it. {cite:nafh-tib-maqqari p. 31}
