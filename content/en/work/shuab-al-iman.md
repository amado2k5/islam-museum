## Summary
Al-Jami' li-Shu'ab al-Iman ("the collection of the branches of faith") is a large hadith work by Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn al-Husayn [[person/al-bayhaqi-abu-bakr-ahmad-ibn-al-husayn|al-Bayhaqi]] (384–458 AH). It arranges hadiths by "branches" (shu'ab) of faith, covering belief, deed and character. Each branch gets its own chapter, numbered by branch, with the verses and hadiths reported under it. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.5}

## Structure and method
Each chapter opens with a heading numbered by branch, such as "the fiftieth branch of faith", followed by the verses the author attaches to it, then the hadiths with their full isnads. The arrangement follows the branch scheme rather than the usual legal headings. The Dar al-Rushd edition's tenth volume (1423 AH) runs from branch 50 to branch 57. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.5}

The chapters in this volume, by their headings in the edition:

- **Branch 50, adherence to the community:** opens with a report from Abu Hurayra on three things God is pleased with and three He dislikes. This is the first hadith of the volume. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.5}
- **Branch 51, judging between people:** opens with the verses that command returning trusts to their owners and judging with justice. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.30}
- **Branch 52, enjoining good and forbidding wrong:** opens with the verses that describe the call to good and the prohibition of wrong as the mark of the successful. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.43}
- **Branch 53, cooperation in piety:** al-Bayhaqi explains that helping in righteousness is most needed where the need is greatest. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.83}
- **Branch 54, modesty in all its parts:** opens with an isnad for a hadith on modesty, numbered 7302 in the edition. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.144}
- **Branch 55, honouring parents:** opens with the verse that joins the worship of God with kindness to parents. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.242}
- **Branch 56, keeping ties of kinship:** opens with the verse warning against corruption on earth and cutting family ties. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.317}
- **Branch 57, good character:** includes controlling anger, gentleness toward others and humility. Al-Bayhaqi quotes Ahmad on good character as the soul's soundness. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.350}

## Content and sources
These chapters tie faith to social and moral conduct: community, judgement, public good, cooperation, modesty, family duties and character. So the book holds ethics and family law next to creed, which sets it apart from pure works of doctrine. The first hadith of the volume links two things: the prohibition on wasting property, asking too much and idle talk, and the call to give sincere counsel to rulers. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.5}

Branch 50 begins with an isnad that runs through Malik and Suhayl ibn Abi Salih to Abu Hurayra. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.5}

## The edition used
The edition used here is the first Dar al-Rushd printing of 1423 AH (2003 CE) in ten volumes, with the tenth volume edited by Mukhtar Ahmad al-Nadawi. {cite:shuab-iman-dar-al-rushd-v10 p.1}

## For young visitors
Shu'ab al-Iman is a large book by al-Bayhaqi that sorts hadiths into branches of faith. This volume has branches on community, justice, doing good, modesty, honouring parents, family ties and good character.

## Research notes
- The volume's branch headings and opening reports are cited to the scan pages above. The full count and order of all branches come from the other volumes of the work.
- Internal cross-references between branches are numerous and should be followed in place.
- Al-Subki's Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra also describes al-Bayhaqi's works and quotes the branch numbering. {cite:tabaqat-shafiiyya-subki p.13}
