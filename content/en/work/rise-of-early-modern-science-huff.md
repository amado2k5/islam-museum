## Summary

*The Rise of Early Modern Science: Islam, China, and the West* is a history-of-science book by Toby E. Huff, published by Cambridge University Press on 24 September 1993. {cite:lovereading-rise-of-early-modern-science} According to the publisher's synopsis, it studies the long-standing question of why modern science arose only in the West and not in the civilizations of Islam or China, although by the Middle Ages Islam and China were more scientifically advanced. {cite:lovereading-rise-of-early-modern-science} By the same synopsis, the author compares the religious, philosophical and legal institutions of the three civilizations and focuses on the legal concept of the corporation, which he holds was unique to the West and gave rise to neutral space and free inquiry. {cite:lovereading-rise-of-early-modern-science}
