## Summary

*Naqd al-Nass* (Critique of the Text) is a book by the Lebanese thinker Ali Harb, the first part of his series *al-Nass wa-l-Haqiqa* (The Text and the Truth). Its first edition was published in 1993 by al-Markaz al-Thaqafi al-Arabi in Beirut and Casablanca. {cite:archive-org-naqd-al-nass-ali-harb-1993}

## Publication and editions

Its introduction is titled 'From the critique of reason to the critique of the text'. The book is made up of essays in which Harb reads the texts of contemporary Arab thinkers: Hassan Hanafi, Mohammed Arkoun, Mohammed Abed al-Jabri, Sadiq Jalal al-Azm, al-Sadiq al-Nayhum, Muhammad Imara and Nasr Hamid Abu Zayd. One chapter compares how Arkoun and al-Jabri handle the philosophical text, and later chapters treat Kantian critique and the question of what philosophy is. {cite:archive-org-naqd-al-nass-ali-harb-1993}
