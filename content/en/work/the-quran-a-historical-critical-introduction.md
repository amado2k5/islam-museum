## Summary

*The Qur'an: A Historical-Critical Introduction* is a book by Nicolai Sinai, published by Edinburgh University Press in 2017 in 224 pages. {cite:openlibrary-quran-historical-critical-introduction-2017} The catalogue describes it as a comprehensive introduction to the basic methods and current state of historical-critical Qur'anic scholarship. {cite:openlibrary-quran-historical-critical-introduction-2017}

## Publication and editions

Questions the record says it addresses include where and when the Qur'an emerged, how its suras work as literary compositions, and how its main themes and ideas relate to and transform earlier Jewish and Christian traditions. {cite:openlibrary-quran-historical-critical-introduction-2017}
