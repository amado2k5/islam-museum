## Overview
A book on the legal rulings of the Qur'an by the judge Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abdullah, known as Ibn al-Arabi the Maliki, who died in 543 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.212}

"Kashf al-Zunun" reports that its opening words are: "The remembrance of God comes before every matter of importance." {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.212}
