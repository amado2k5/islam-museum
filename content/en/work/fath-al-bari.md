## Summary
Fath al-Bari is Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani's commentary on Sahih al-Bukhari, among the fullest commentaries on the Sahih: it explains vocabulary and chains, the jurisprudence of each hadith, and the views of scholars. {cite:fath-al-bari}

## Details
Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani (d. 852 AH) wrote this commentary on the Sahih of Imam al-Bukhari after a lifetime in the service of hadith and its sciences, making it an encyclopedia of hadith jurisprudence: he explains rare words, reviews chains and routes of transmission, draws the law out of each text, transmits the opinions of scholars on the questions, and sometimes weighs between them. {cite:fath-al-bari}

It became the first reference for readers of Sahih al-Bukhari in later centuries — hardly a reading is pursued without consulting it. Its printings include that of al-Matba'a al-Salafiyya and its library at al-Rawda, Cairo. {cite:fath-al-bari}
