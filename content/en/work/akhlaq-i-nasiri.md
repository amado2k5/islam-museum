## Overview
"Akhlaq-i Nasiri" is a Persian book by the scholar Nasir al-Din al-Tusi. He wrote it in Quhistan for its ruler Nasir al-Din Abd al-Rahim al-Muhtasham, when the ruler asked him to translate "al-Tahara", on practical wisdom, by Ali ibn Miskawayh. He added to it the sections on the civic and the domestic. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.268}
