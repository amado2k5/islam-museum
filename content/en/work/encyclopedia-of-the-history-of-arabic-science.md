## Summary

The *Encyclopedia of the History of Arabic Science* is a three-volume English work edited by Roshdi Rashed in collaboration with Régis Morelon. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996} The publisher's description presents it as the first up-to-date synthesis of the Arab contribution to the history of science, mathematics and technology, documenting the history and philosophy of Arab science. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996} Its thirty-one chapters, by an international team of specialists, cover astronomy, mathematics, music, engineering, nautical science, scientific institutions and other fields. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996}

Volume 1 covers theoretical and applied astronomy; volume 2, mathematics and the physical sciences; volume 3, technology, alchemy and the life sciences. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996} Each volume has an extensive bibliography and suggestions for further reading, and the set is illustrated with figures, tables and plates and fully indexed. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996}

## Publication and editions

Routledge published it in London and New York in 1996, in three volumes totalling 1,105 pages. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996} Its Open Library record classes it under the history of science in the Arab countries. {cite:openlibrary-encyclopedia-history-arabic-science-1996}
