## Summary

*Al-Turath wa-l-Tajdid: Mawqifuna min al-Turath al-Qadim* (Heritage and Renewal: Our Stance on the Old Heritage) is a book by the Egyptian thinker Hassan Hanafi. Its first edition came from al-Markaz al-'Arabi li-l-Bahth wa-l-Nashr in Cairo, and its third from the Anglo-Egyptian Bookshop after the first two sold out; it circulates today in the Hindawi Foundation edition. It is the first theoretical statement of Hanafi's project. In the preface to the fourth edition he says the second statement, on the stance towards the Western heritage, appeared as *Muqaddima fi 'Ilm al-Istighrab*, and the third, on the stance towards reality, was still to come. {cite:archive-org-al-turath-wa-al-tajdid-hindawi}

After the prefaces the book has five parts: what 'heritage and renewal' means, the crisis of social change, the crisis of method in Islamic studies, the ways of renewal, and the subjects of renewal, which Hanafi calls the rebuilding of the sciences. In the preface to the third edition he notes that the book drew many responses, with review articles and whole books on its subject. {cite:archive-org-al-turath-wa-al-tajdid-hindawi}
