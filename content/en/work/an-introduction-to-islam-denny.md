## Summary

*An Introduction to Islam* is an English textbook by Frederick Mathewson Denny, published by Macmillan in New York and Collier Macmillan in London in 1985 in 415 pages, with a bibliography on pages 379–393. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islam-denny-work} {cite:archive-org-introduction-to-islam-denny-1985} According to the contents of its second edition, it has six parts: religion and common life in the pre-Islamic Near East, the coming of Islam, the Islamic religious system, the Sufi way of mysticism and fellowship, patterns of personal and communal life, and Islam in the modern world. {cite:archive-org-introduction-to-islam-denny-1994}
