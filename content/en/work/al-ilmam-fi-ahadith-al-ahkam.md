## Overview
"Al-Ilmam fi Ahadith al-Ahkam" (The Comprehensive Guide to the Hadiths of the Rulings) by Shaykh Taqi al-Din Muhammad ibn Ali, known as Ibn Daqiq al-Id the Shafi'i, who died in 702 AH. In it he gathered the texts of the hadiths on legal rulings, stripped of their chains of transmission. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 585}
