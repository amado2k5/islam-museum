## Summary

A question on gifting the reward of Qur'an recitation to the dead, in which [[person/ibn-baz-abd-al-aziz|Ibn Baz]] set out the disagreement and held that it is better left, in favour of charity and supplication. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4002}

## The question

The questioner asked whether, if someone recites the Qur'an and gifts its reward to his father, friend or brother, the reward reaches them. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4002}

## The answer

Ibn Baz said the question is disputed: most scholars hold that the reward arrives, while others say neither the Prophet nor the Companions are reported to have done it. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4002} He held that it is better to leave it, with each person reciting for himself, and that charity on behalf of the dead and supplication for them benefit them by the consensus of Muslims. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4002} He added that fasts a person neglected after recovering from illness are fasted on his behalf after his death, and an obligatory or vowed pilgrimage is performed for him, but praying on behalf of the dead has no basis. {cite:binbaz fatwa 4002}
