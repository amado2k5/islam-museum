## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with ten episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} It covers the first suras of the last part of the Qur'an: he opens the first episode with sura [[passage/quran-78-1|al-Naba']], taking its opening as the subject of the lesson, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep01} returns to 'the great news' in the next two episodes, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep02} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep03} begins sura [[passage/quran-79-1|al-Nazi'at]] in the eighth, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep08} reviews in the ninth, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep09} and stays with al-Nazi'at in the last. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy tafsir-al-quran-al-qareem/ep10} The lessons digress widely, which is why so many museum records were written from them.
