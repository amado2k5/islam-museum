## Summary

A question about the minimum age of animals for aqiqa and udhiya, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the minimum is five years for camels, two for cattle and goats and one for sheep, and that a sheep over six months may be used if large and fat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4238}

## The question

The questioner asked about the required age of animals for aqiqa and udhiya. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4238}

## The answer

The Department answered that the minimum age for udhiya and aqiqa is five full years, entering the sixth, for camels; two years, entering the third, for cattle and goats; and one year, entering the second, for sheep. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4238} It said a sheep may be over six months old provided it is large and fat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4238}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4238, dated 15 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4238} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
