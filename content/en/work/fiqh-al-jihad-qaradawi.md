## Summary

*Fiqh al-Jihad: A Comparative Study of Its Rulings and Philosophy in the Light of the Qur'an and Sunna* is a comparative legal study by Yusuf al-Qaradawi, published in 2009 in more than 1,400 pages. The imprint page of the Maktabat Wahba printing lists its second edition in Cairo in August 2009 and a revised and expanded third edition in June 1431/2010. {cite:goodreads-fiqh-al-jihad-qaradawi} {cite:archive-org-fiqh-al-jihad-qaradawi-scan}
