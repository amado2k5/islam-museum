## Summary

A question on whether eating bread without a condiment is forbidden, in which IslamQA answered that it found no established hadith on it. {cite:islamqa answer 664359}

## The question

The questioner asked whether any prohibition on eating bread without a condiment has come down. {cite:islamqa answer 664359}

## The answer

IslamQA answered that it found no established hadith forbidding it. {cite:islamqa answer 664359} It held that the basic ruling is that bread may be eaten alone or with whatever condiment is at hand, and that no one need go to trouble to obtain one. {cite:islamqa answer 664359} It noted that the Prophet ate as his people ate, taking bread with whatever was available, citing the hadith of Jabir ibn Abd Allah in Muslim's collection. {cite:islamqa answer 664359}

IslamQA published this answer as no. 664359, dated 17 Jumada al-Ula 1448 (28 October 2026). {cite:islamqa answer 664359} The museum records this answer as a historical text attributed to its author; it issues no ruling of its own.
