## Overview
A book by [[person/al-masudi|al-Mas'udi]] titled "Al-Ruus al-Sab'a fil-Ihata bi-Siyasat al-Alam wa Asrarihi" (The Seven Heads: On Comprehending the Governance of the World and Its Secrets). Al-Mas'udi describes it as well known and comprehensive. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.481-482}

He refers to it in the chapter on physiognomy, and on judging by it and against it, and on what each group among the people, past and present, held. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.481-482}
