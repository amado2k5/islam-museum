## Overview
*al-Dhayl ala Tabaqat al-Hanabila* is a work by the hafiz Zayn al-Din Abu al-Faraj Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad ibn Rajab al-Baghdadi then al-Dimashqi al-Hanbali (736–795 AH). It follows Hanbali biographies on from Ibn Abi Ya'la's Tabaqat, arranged by year of death. {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 1}

## The edition and its second part
Part 2 was published in Cairo in 1372 AH / 1953 CE, corrected by Muhammad Hamid al-Fiqi. It opens under the heading "Deaths of the seventh century, from 601 to 700"; its first entry, no. 214, is the biography of the hafiz Abd al-Ghani ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Maqdisi, which runs to about twenty-six pages. {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 5} {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 30}

## The author's method
Ibn Rajab builds a long entry on his predecessors. The life of Abd al-Ghani is drawn mostly from the two-part biography compiled by the hafiz Diya' al-Din al-Maqdisi, and he notes that the jurist Makki ibn Umar ibn Ni'ma al-Misri also collected his merits. {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 6} Then he comments with "qultu" (I say): he reports the opponents' account as they gave it, answers what he takes to be wrong in it, and cites what he read in al-Dhahabi's own hand. {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 22} {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 24} He also corrects earlier writers: Ibn al-Hanbali, he says, was mistaken in placing Abd al-Ghani's death in 595. {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 29}

An entry includes the full list of works, separating those compiled with chains of transmission from those without, and giving the number of parts of each. {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 18} {cite:dhayl-tabaqat-hanabila-faqi-v2 p. 19}
