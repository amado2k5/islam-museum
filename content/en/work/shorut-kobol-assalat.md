## Summary
The Assabile archive lists this series among the audio lessons of [[person/abdul-hamid-kishk|Abdul Hamid Kishk]]: seven episodes, each between an hour and twenty minutes and an hour and thirty-four minutes long. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02} The episodes are Friday sermons: he opens each with praise of God, blessings on the Prophet and long eulogies, announces the condition he will treat, and closes with supplication before saying: rise to your prayer. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 01:11:00} Their subject is a hadith qudsi on [[concept/shurut-qabul-al-salah|the conditions for the acceptance of prayer]], in which he counts five conditions and gives a sermon to each. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01 00:25:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:12:00}

The recordings of this series are of poor sound and their machine transcripts are garbled in many places, so only what reads clearly is written here. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01}

## Episode 1: the five conditions
He sets the known conditions of a valid prayer beside the conditions of its acceptance, then lists the five: humility before God's greatness, not lording it over His creation, not persisting in sin, spending the day in His remembrance, and mercy to the poor, the wayfarer, the widow and the afflicted. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01 00:23:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep01 00:25:00}

## Episode 2: not lording it over creation
He explains the condition as refraining from harming people and lists the kinds of harm according to the limbs. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:14:00} He tells of a righteous man who sat at his sister's grave after her burial, when the mourners had left, and saw thick smoke rising from it, and says that the outside of graves is stone while inside is reward or punishment (needs_source: a story he tells without a chain). {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:16:00} He then speaks of [[concept/rafiq-al-qabr|what accompanies a person to the grave]]. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:19:30} In the second sermon he comments on a report in Akhbar al-Yawm about a wife who took her husband to court, asks why the courts have entered homes, and says the Prophet's Medina had not a single court or policeman, before speaking of the purpose of [[practice/nikah|marriage]]. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:43:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:43:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep02 00:45:00}

## Episode 3: not persisting in sin
He begins the third condition and sets out examples from the Book and the Sunna, speaking of [[concept/tawhid|the oneness of God]], which he divides into oneness in creed, in worship and in dealings. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:12:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:12:30} He tells of a Companion who in the Jahiliyya worshipped a wooden idol, saw a fox urinate on its head, and knew that it could not even protect itself (needs_source: the Companion's name is unclear in the machine transcript). {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:13:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:14:30} He asks where the tyrants of East and West are, naming Kennedy, Johnson, Napoleon and Mussolini, to say that none remains but God. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:16:00} In the second sermon he tells of a man walking in the year 80 AH in a village near Kufa who ate half of an apple fallen from an orchard, then went to the orchard's owner to ask forgiveness; the owner made it a condition that he marry his daughter, whom he described as blind, deaf, mute and lame, and when the man went in to her he found her sound, and she explained that she did not look at what angers God, did not hear what displeases Him, and walked only to His obedience (needs_source: a story he tells; the name of the son he says was born to them is unclear in the transcript). {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:56:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 00:58:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 01:02:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep03 01:02:30}

## Episode 4: spending the day in remembrance
He calls the fourth condition 'the matter of matters' and says that [[concept/dhikr|remembrance]] is making God's greatness present in the heart in every state, standing, sitting and lying on one's side, and that remembrance must go with reflection. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:15:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:16:30} He tells that a man complained to [[person/sufyan-al-thawri|Sufyan]] of a sickness of the heart, and Sufyan prescribed a remedy made of virtues: humility, awe, watchfulness and seeking forgiveness. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep04 00:19:30}

## Episode 5: mercy, and the grouping of the conditions
He begins the fifth condition and sorts the conditions into moral, communal, psychological, creedal and humane. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:14:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:14:30} He then speaks of [[concept/masjid|the mosque]]: in his view those who fight Islam fight the mosques by making people afraid to enter them; he cites the phrase 'watch your children in the mosques' and says it was said in '65 (needs_source: a date he gives without detail); and he says that in the Prophet's time the mosque was the seat of leadership and of worship. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:16:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:16:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:19:30} In the second sermon he mentions the hadith on telling children to pray at seven and disciplining them for it at ten, explaining that one who is used to prayer when young keeps to it when grown. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:52:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep05 00:53:00}

## Episode 6: mercy to the afflicted
He mentions the hadith of the five things heavy in the scale: glorifying, praising, declaring God's oneness and greatness, and a righteous child who dies and whose father bears it patiently; then the hadith of the angels who take the child's soul, and that God commands a house in Paradise to be built for the patient father, called the House of Praise. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:12:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:13:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:15:30} He says that if he spoke of [[concept/rahma|mercy]] forever the talk would not end, because Muslims have grown hard-hearted, and describes the Prophet's mercy to widows, to the afflicted and to all people. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep06 00:19:00}

## Episode 7: the one who strives for the widow and the poor
He makes the last condition the social side of the Sharia, mentions the merit of one who strives for the widow and the poor, and demands that the words of the mosques be turned into deeds. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:16:30} He mentions a hadith qudsi that God expiates His servant's sins through illness, narrow provision and calamity in wealth and children, and that God is shy, on the Day of Resurrection, to set up a scale or open a register for one who was tried and bore it with beautiful patience, and admits him to Paradise without reckoning; this is the theme of [[concept/ibtila|trial]]. {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:18:30} {cite:assabile-abdul-hamid-kishk shorut-kobol-assalat/ep07 00:19:00}
