## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons as 'Nafahat Ramadaniyya 1436 h', with six episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} In the episodes the Sheikh calls it 'our series of episodes on the faults of the tongue', each episode on one fault, each about thirteen minutes long. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep03 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:00:00} In the last episode he says there were thirty episodes, covering backbiting, talebearing, wrangling, disputation, ingratitude, lying, mockery and more, so the archive holds only part of the series (needs_source: the figure of thirty is stated once). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:10:30} The museum's gathering record is [[concept/afat-al-lisan|the faults of the tongue]].

## Episode 1: reminding people of one's gifts

The Sheikh opens the series with the fault of [[concept/mann|reminding people of one's gifts]], tells the report of Abu Bakr, Umar and al-Abbas on the best of kindness (hastening, concealing and making little of it), and holds that the worst mann is a son reminding his parents. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep01 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep01 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep01 00:11:00}

## Episode 2: calling on other than God

He counts turning in supplication to other than God as a form of [[concept/shirk|shirk]], criticises asking the occupants of shrines, and tells examples of certainty from the Companions and Followers, among them [[person/abu-hazim-al-araj|Abu Hazim]] with Harun al-Rashid. Details under [[practice/dua|supplication]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep02 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep02 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep02 00:10:30}

## Episode 3: praying against oneself and one's children

He warns against [[concept/al-dua-ala-al-nafs-wal-awlad|praying against oneself and one's children]], urges praying for guidance for the wrongdoer, and criticises the spread of cursing in everyday speech. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep03 00:00:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep03 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep03 00:10:00}

## Episode 4: reviling the Companions

He counts [[concept/sabb-al-sahaba|reviling the Companions]] a very grave fault, cites hadiths on their merit, mentions merits of Abu Bakr, Uthman, Ali and Sa'd; his judgement on those who attack them is attributed to him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep04 00:06:30}

## Episode 5: covering a Muslim's faults

He treats [[concept/satr|covering a Muslim's faults]] and the prohibition of [[concept/tajassus-wa-tahassus|spying]], holding that covering helps the sinner repent and that counsel should be private. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:04:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep05 00:07:00}

## Episode 6: the remedy

He gathers the means of [[concept/hifz-al-lisan|guarding the tongue]]: awe of God's sanctities, short hope, night prayer, silence, [[concept/companionship-suhba|good company]], knowing one's own faults, and handling [[concept/ghadab|anger]]. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy nafahat-ramadaneya-1436-h/ep06 00:09:30}
