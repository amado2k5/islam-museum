## Overview
"Al-Uns al-Jalil bi-Tarikh al-Quds wal-Khalil" (The Noble Companionship on the History of Jerusalem and Hebron), a volume by the judge Mujir al-Din al-Ulaymi al-Hanbali. In it he gathered the essence of the histories of Jerusalem, and added a brief account of events and deaths. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.649}

He began it in Dhu al-Hijja 900 AH and finished it four months later. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.650}
