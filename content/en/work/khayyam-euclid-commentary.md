## Parallels and proof structure

## Ratios and composition

Stanford's account of the second part compares proportionality defined through multiples with a definition involving repeated divisions,describable today through continued fractions. The problem extends beyond ratios of integers to potentially incommensurable magnitudes. This explains why definition mattered without collapsing all ancient magnitude language into modern decimal numbers {cite:sep-umar-khayyam sec4.2}.

Vahabzadeh identifies the third part as compounding ratios. Its presence shows that the commentary is not confined to a quadrilateral drawing. It does not license assigning every modern theory of real numbers to the author.
