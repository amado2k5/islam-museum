## Summary

A question about romanian and Australian sheep for the sacrifice, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that a sheep is valid if it has reached the age the law requires and is free of harmful defects, whatever its country of origin. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4368}

## The question

The questioner asked whether Romanian and Australian sheep may be offered as the sacrifice. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4368}

## The answer

The Department answered that a sheep that meets the conditions of the sacrifice, having reached the age the law requires and being free of harmful defects, is valid regardless of its country of origin. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4368} It said that Romanian, Australian, Spanish, Indian, Sudanese, local and other sheep may therefore be offered. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4368}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4368, dated 30 March 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4368} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
