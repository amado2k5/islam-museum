## Summary
"Hirz al-Amani wa Wajh al-Tahani" by al-Shatibi, known as the Shatibiyya, a versified work on the seven readings of the Qur'an {cite:matn-shatibiyya-ghawthani-2010 p.1}. The preface to the corrected edition says that the author gathered in it what is transmitted by the seven readers: Nafiʿ, Ibn Kathir, Abu ʿAmr, Ibn ʿAmir, ʿAsim, Hamza and al-Kisai {cite:matn-shatibiyya-ghawthani-2010 p.3}.

## The edition used here
The local scanned edition from the Ghawthani press (fifth edition, 2010) has been read only in its first pages. Its text has not been compared with any other copy, and this entry is not to be used for transmission or teaching of the readings.
