## Summary

*A History of Islamic Societies* is a book by Ira M. Lapidus, published by Cambridge University Press in 1988 in xxx + 1002 pages, with illustrations and maps. {cite:cca-library-history-of-islamic-societies} It has a bibliography on pages 929–974 and an index; a second edition appeared in 2002 and a third in 2014. {cite:archive-org-history-of-islamic-societies-1988}

## Publication and editions

A description of the second edition quoted in the catalogue record says it brought the coverage of each country and region of the Muslim world up to 2001, traced the origins and evolution of Islamic societies, and added material on women and on Afghanistan and Muslims in Europe, America and the Philippines. {cite:archive-org-history-of-islamic-societies-1988}
