## Summary

*An Introduction to Islamic Archaeology* by Marcus Milwright belongs to the New Edinburgh Islamic Surveys series. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-archaeology-2009} According to its contents list, its chapters move from early Islam and late antiquity to “New directions in the early Islamic period”. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-archaeology-2009} It then treats the countryside; towns, cities and palaces; religious practice in the Islamic world; crafts and industry; and travel and trade. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-archaeology-2009} It ends with a chapter on the “post-medieval” Islamic world and a conclusion. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-archaeology-2009}

## Publication and editions

Edinburgh University Press published it in 2009, in 260 pages, with a bibliography on pages 229–255 and an index. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-archaeology-2009} Open Library records hardback and paperback issues. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-archaeology-2009}
