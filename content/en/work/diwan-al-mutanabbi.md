## Summary
The divan of Abu al-Tayyib al-Mutanabbi, in the Dar Beirut edition for printing and publishing (1983). It opens with an introduction describing the poet, which mentions his birth in Kufa, his residence in Syria, then Egypt, then Baghdad and Fars, and states that he died in 354 AH {cite:diwan-mutanabbi-dar-beirut-1983 p.5} {cite:diwan-mutanabbi-dar-beirut-1983 p.6}.

## Note on the edition
The edition is a local scanned copy. Its editor and any reference copy for it have not been verified. The page numbers given are the page numbers of the scanned file.
