## Summary

A question about giving zakat and zakat al-fitr to a poor brother, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is permitted because a poor brother is among those eligible to receive zakat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4211}

## The question

The questioner asked about giving zakat and zakat al-fitr to a poor brother. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4211}

## The answer

The Department answered that zakat and zakat al-fitr may be given to a poor brother. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4211} It explained that he belongs to the categories of people who may receive zakat. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4211}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4211, dated 10 February 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4211} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
