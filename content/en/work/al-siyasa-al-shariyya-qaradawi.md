## Summary

*al-Siyasa al-Shar'iyya fi Daw' Nusus al-Shari'a wa-Maqasidiha* is a book by Yusuf al-Qaradawi on governance in relation to the texts and aims of the sharia. Its first edition was published by Maktabat Wahba in Cairo in 1998 in 353 pages, followed by a Mu'assasat al-Risala edition in Beirut in 2000. {cite:openlibrary-al-siyasa-al-shariyya-qaradawi-1998}
