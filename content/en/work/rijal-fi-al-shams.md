## Overview
The novel by Ghassan Kanafani (1963) about three Palestinians, Abu Qais, As'ad and Marwan, who hide in an empty water tank to cross the desert to Kuwait, and die of suffocation at the border because the driver was late in asking. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Among the best-known Arabic texts on the Nakba and its consequences; its final question, 'why did they not knock on the walls of the tank?', became the conscience of the Arabic novel. {cite:wikidata}
