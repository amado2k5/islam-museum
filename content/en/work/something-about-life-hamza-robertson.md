## Summary
Something About Life is the debut album of the English singer Hamza Robertson, released by [[listing/awakening-records|Awakening Records]] on 3 July 2007 with nine songs in English and Arabic; it runs about 34 minutes. {cite:wikipedia-something-about-life-en} {cite:wikidata-something-about-life}

## What it is
The article describes it as blending the musical sounds of East and West and files it under Islamic music, nasheed and rock. {cite:wikipedia-something-about-life-en} Its songs are 'Our Creation', 'Your Beauty', 'Show Me', 'He Is One', 'Morning Prayer', 'My Hero', 'Everyday', 'The Mountain' and 'O Allah'. {cite:wikipedia-something-about-life-en} Awakening lists it among its 2007 releases. {cite:wikipedia-awakening-music-en}
