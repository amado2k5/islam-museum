## Overview
A book by Zakariyya ibn Muhammad [[person/al-qazwini|al-Qazwini]], author of "Ajaib al-Makhluqat". He arranged it in an introduction and seven climates, and gathered in it what he knew, heard and saw of the properties of lands and peoples. It was composed in 674 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 179-180}
