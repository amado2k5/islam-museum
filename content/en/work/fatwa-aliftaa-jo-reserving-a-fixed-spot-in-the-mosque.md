## Summary

A question about someone who keeps a fixed spot in the mosque and is angered when others pray there, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that keeping a fixed spot in the mosque is disliked, and that whoever reaches a place first has the better right to it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4256}

## The question

The questioner asked about a man who prays in a particular spot in the mosque and becomes angry when someone else comes and prays there. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4256}

## The answer

The Department answered that the Prophet forbade a man to take a fixed spot in the mosque for himself, keeping to it as a camel keeps to its resting place, and that scholars counted this among disliked acts. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4256} It said the man should be advised that whoever reaches a place in the mosque first has the better right to it. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4256}
