## Summary
Al-Shifa' ("The Cure") is Avicenna's great philosophical encyclopedia, covering logic, natural philosophy and metaphysics; Ibn Khallikan lists it among Ibn Sina's works alongside al-Najat, al-Isharat and al-Qanun, in an output approaching a hundred long and short treatises {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.393}.

## Place among Avicenna's works
Hasan Ibrahim Hasan, citing Ibn Khallikan, calls Ibn Sina the prodigy of his age in learning, intellect and authorship, naming al-Shifa' on hikma together with al-Najat, al-Isharat and al-Qanun among nearly a hundred works {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.393}. In the same chapter he notes that Ibn Sina wrote on logic following the lead of Abu Nasr al-Farabi, for whom logic was the gateway to philosophy {cite:tarikh-islam-hasan-ibrahim p.392-393}.

## For young visitors
Kitab al-Shifa' is Avicenna's largest philosophical book, gathering logic, physics and metaphysics in the Peripatetic manner.
