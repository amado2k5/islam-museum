## Summary
Qatr al-Nada wa-Ball al-Sada in grammar by Ibn Hisham al-Ansari (d. 761 AH) — a prose matn gathering the parts of speech, mubtada and khabar, the abrogating verbs, objects, hal, tamyiz, exception, the vocative, the particles, the declensions and the coordination; the grammar text that follows the Ajurrumiyya in the lessons {cite:al-mutun-al-ilmiyya-azhar} {cite:al-aalam}.
