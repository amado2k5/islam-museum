## Summary
Tafsir al-Jalalayn ("the commentary of the two Jalals") is a concise commentary on the whole Qur'an, named after its two authors, both titled Jalal al-Din: [[person/jalal-al-din-al-mahalli|Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli]] and [[person/al-suyuti|Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti]]. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3} The reviser of the Maktabat al-Iman edition counts it among the best-known commentaries by reasoned opinion (bi'l-ra'y). {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3}

## How the work was divided
Al-Mahalli explained the second half of the Qur'an together with al-Fatiha; al-Suyuti explained the first half, apart from al-Fatiha. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3} In the preface to his completion al-Suyuti calls it a completion of al-Mahalli's commentary "and a finishing of what he missed, which is from the start of Surat al-Baqara to the end of al-Isra'". {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.4} At the end of al-Isra' he writes that this is the end of what he added to complete the commentary composed by "the shaykh, the imam, the learned, the very learned, the verifier Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli the Shafi'i". {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.369} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.400} The reviser observes that al-Suyuti followed al-Mahalli's method — brevity, precision, no digressions — so closely that the whole book reads as if by one author. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3}

## Method
Al-Suyuti describes his approach as following al-Mahalli's pattern: giving what is needed to understand God's words, relying on the most preponderant opinion, parsing what needs parsing, noting the well-known variant readings lightly and briefly, and leaving out lengthy unacceptable opinions and grammatical analyses that belong in books of Arabic. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.4} In the closing note of his completion he says it is "in truth drawn from the book it completes, and on that, in the ambiguous verses, it relies and depends". {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.369} An example of its brevity is its comment on the disjointed letters that open Surat al-Baqara: God knows best what He meant by them. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.24}

## Dating the completion
Al-Suyuti records that he began the completion on Wednesday 1 Ramadan 870 AH, finished it on Sunday 10 Shawwal of the same year, and finished the fair copy on Wednesday 6 Safar 871; see [[event/al-suyuti-completes-tafsir-al-jalalayn-870|al-Suyuti completes Tafsir al-Jalalayn]]. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.370} He likens the span to the appointed time of al-Kalim, Moses. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.369} He asks anyone who reads it with fairness and finds an error to tell him, and says it had never crossed his mind to attempt such a work, knowing his inability to enter these paths. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.370} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.400}

## The attribute verses
The two Jalals explain some divine attributes by what they entail and say of the rising over the Throne "a rising befitting Him"; the reviser of the Maktabat al-Iman edition flags this. The positions are set out in [[question/how-does-tafsir-al-jalalayn-treat-the-divine-attributes|How does Tafsir al-Jalalayn explain the verses on the divine attributes?]]. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.23} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.286} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3}

## Editions used
- Maktabat al-Iman, Mansura, revised by Dr Muhammad Muhammad Tamir, with an introduction and biographies of both authors. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.1} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-tamir-maktabat-al-iman p.3}
- A lithographed copy completed in mid-Sha'ban 1278 AH in the hand of Mahbub ibn Hasan and corrected by its sponsor Ridwan ibn Hasan ibn Ali al-Hifnawi; it puts al-Fatiha at the end of the book, after al-Mahalli's part. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.790} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-lithograph-1278 p.789}
- Dar al-Ma'rifa, Beirut, in the margin of the Mushaf in the Uthmani script, with al-Suyuti's Lubab al-Nuqul fi Asbab al-Nuzul at the foot of the pages. {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.1} {cite:tafsir-jalalayn-dar-al-marifa-uthmani p.24}
