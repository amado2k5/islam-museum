## Summary

A question about zakat for maintaining school and hospital buildings, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that the recipients of zakat are limited to the eight categories, and building or maintaining schools is not among them, so zakat may not be paid for it; voluntary charity may be collected instead, or donors directed to educational endowments. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3733}

## The question

The questioner asked whether zakat may be spent on modernising and maintaining the buildings of schools, universities and hospitals, including painting, upgrading facilities, replacing seats and restructuring buildings. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3733}

## The answer

The Department answered that the recipients of zakat are fixed by the Shari'a and limited to the eight categories named in [[passage/quran-9-60|Surat al-Tawba 9:60]], citing a hadith narrated by [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] from [[person/ziyad-ibn-al-harith-al-sudai|Ziyad ibn al-Harith al-Suda'i]] whose meaning is that God did not accept the judgement of a prophet or anyone else concerning alms until He Himself ruled on them and divided them into eight parts. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3733} It said that building or maintaining schools is not among these categories, so zakat may not be paid for it, but voluntary charity may be collected for this purpose, or donors may be directed to educational endowments. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3733} It noted that the Jordanian Iftaa Council issued resolution 6/2018, which counts donating to improve education and endowing funds for the educational endowment initiative among the things the Shari'a calls for, provided the donation does not conflict with the instructions of the Ministry of Education. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3733}
