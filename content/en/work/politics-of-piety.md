## Summary

*Politics of Piety: The Islamic Revival and the Feminist Subject* is an English book by the anthropologist Saba Mahmood, published by Princeton University Press on 25 October 2004; its title page gives 2005. {cite:openlibrary-politics-of-piety-2004} {cite:jadaliyya-politics-of-piety} The book is based on her 1998 Stanford University thesis and has 233 pages, with a bibliography on pages 205–223. According to its contents, its chapters treat the subject of freedom, the topography of the piety movement, pedagogies of persuasion, positive ethics and ritual conventions, and agency, gender and embodiment, followed by an epilogue and a glossary of Arabic terms. {cite:archive-org-politics-of-piety-2005}
