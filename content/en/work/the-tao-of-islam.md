## Summary

*The Tao of Islam: A Sourcebook on Gender Relationships in Islamic Thought* is an English book by Sachiko Murata, published by State University of New York Press in Albany in 1992 in 397 pages, with a foreword by Annemarie Schimmel. In her introduction the author says the book grew out of teaching a course called 'Feminine Spirituality in World Religions'. {cite:openlibrary-tao-of-islam-1992} {cite:archive-org-tao-of-islam-murata-text}

## Publication and editions

The introduction covers the intellectual tradition in Islam, the feminist critique of Islam, Chinese and Islamic thought, theological polarity, the correspondences between the cosmos and the human being, and the plan of the book. Its parts then move from the three realities, God, the cosmos and the human being, to theology, including divine duality and the two hands of God, and on to cosmology, including heaven and earth. {cite:archive-org-tao-of-islam-murata-text}
