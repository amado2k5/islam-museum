## Summary

*La Grande Discorde: Religion et politique dans l'Islam des origines* is a book in French by the Tunisian historian Hichem Djaït, published by Gallimard in Paris in 1989 in 421 pages. {cite:openlibrary-la-grande-discorde-1989} Its Arabic translation circulates as «الفتنة: جدلية الدين والسياسة في الإسلام المبكر»; a scanned copy on the Internet Archive carries that title and the imprint of the French original. {cite:archive-org-al-fitna-djait-arabic-scan}
