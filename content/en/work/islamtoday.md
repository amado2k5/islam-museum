# IslamToday

## Summary

IslamToday is a Saudi Islamic website in Arabic and English run by Shaykh Salman al-Ouda, who was a director of its Arabic edition, as the Jamestown Foundation reported in 2009. {cite:wikipedia-salman-al-ouda-en}

## Beginnings

The earliest archived capture of islamtoday.net, of 11 April 2001, is an image with no text; the earliest titled capture, of 25 March 2002, reads 'islamtoday - A web site about islam and muslims', and by the capture of 7 February 2003 the title begins with the Arabic name «الإسلام اليوم». {cite:wayback-islamtoday-2002}

## Its shaykh

Salman al-Ouda is a Saudi scholar, a member of the board of trustees of the International Union of Muslim Scholars, who gives classes and lectures over the Internet and answers people's questions every day. {cite:wikipedia-salman-al-ouda-en}
