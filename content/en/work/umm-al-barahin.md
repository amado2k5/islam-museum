## Overview
"Umm al-Barahin fil-Aqaid" (The Mother of Proofs on Creeds), by the Imam Shaykh Muhammad ibn Yusuf ibn al-Husayn al-Sanusi. It is a useful abridgement containing the creed of the divine unity, which he ends with the two words of the testimony of faith. He then explained it in a brief and useful commentary. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p. 635}
