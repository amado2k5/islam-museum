## Summary
*Al-Diya' al-Lami' min al-Khutab al-Jawami'* ("The Shining Light from Comprehensive Sermons") is the collection of Friday, festival and rain-prayer sermons of [[person/muhammad-ibn-salih-al-uthaymin|Muhammad ibn Salih al-'Uthaymin]]. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 5} In his preface he says his teacher [[person/abd-al-rahman-ibn-nasir-al-sadi|'Abd al-Rahman ibn Nasir al-Sa'di]] held the office of preacher in the Great Mosque of 'Unayza until his death in Jumada al-Akhira 1376 AH. He succeeded him in that office and followed his "thought and manner". {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 8}

## What the author says about the sermon
The author opens his preface by saying that among the Prophet's regular means of conveying his message was the sermon he gave the people every Friday, "explaining to them what the situation required and the occasion called for". In it he explained faith in God, His names and attributes, and the Last Day. The caliphs, governors, preachers and scholars followed his way. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 7-8}

## Arrangement
The author chose more than 125 of his sermons, added those for the two festivals and the rain prayer, and divided them into ten sections: {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 8-10}
1. Knowledge.
2. Foundations of religion: God's names, His signs, the Prophet's mission, signs and character, his campaigns, the signs of the Hour, the Resurrection, Paradise and Hell, faith in decree, the merits of Islam, and manners.
3. Acts of worship: purity, prayer, zakat, fasting and the alms and festival of breaking the fast, pilgrimage and the sacrifice and its festival, and jihad.
4. Transactions: sincere counsel and trust, sales and earning, and endowments and wills.
5. Marriage: its conditions and the dower.
6. Raising children.
7. Commanding right and forbidding wrong, retaliation and penalties.
8. Maintenance and foods.
9. General topics.
10. Sermons for particular months and for the end of the year.

## Its printings
- It first appeared in 1392 AH. When the Presidency of Scholarly Research and Ifta' decided to reprint it, the author added a supplement of 118 sermons on topics including knowledge and fatwa, exegesis, dress and morals. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 11-12}
- After his death the Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-'Uthaymin Charitable Foundation added all the first sermons written in his own hand that he gave in the Great Mosque of 'Unayza from 1376 to 1421 AH. Fahd ibn Nasir al-Sulayman arranged them by subject on the author's plan, and the Foundation's scholarly committee wrote its preface on 15/11/1424 AH. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 5-6}
- The printing used here belongs to the series of his works (no. 28), published by Dar al-Thurayya in Riyadh in 1440 AH/2019 CE under the Foundation's supervision. {cite:al-diya-al-lami-uthaymin-thurayya-2019 p. 2}
