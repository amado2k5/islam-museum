## Summary

*Fatatni Salat* (I Missed a Prayer) is a book by the Egyptian writer Islam Gamal, whose first edition was published in 2018 by Zahmat Kuttab. Its cover asks why some people keep up their prayers while many abandon them, and promises the reader the secrets of those who seldom missed one. It is made up of short chapters, beginning with one titled 'I Missed a Prayer' and ending with a conclusion. {cite:al-sabeel-fatatni-salat} {cite:archive-org-fatatni-salat-text}
