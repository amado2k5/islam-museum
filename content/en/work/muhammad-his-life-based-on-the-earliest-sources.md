## Summary

*Muhammad: His Life Based on the Earliest Sources* is a biography in English by Martin Lings, published in London in 1983 by Allen & Unwin and the Islamic Texts Society, in 359 pages. {cite:openlibrary-muhammad-lings-1983}

## Publication and editions

The book tells the life in eighty-five short chapters, beginning with the House of God and the Year of the Elephant and moving through the first revelations, the Hijra, the battles of Badr and Uhud and the years after them. {cite:archive-org-lings-muhammad-1983-scan} His list of sources names Ibn Ishaq's Sira in Ibn Hisham's recension, Ibn Sa'd's Tabaqat and al-Waqidi's Maghazi as his main sources, with occasional use of al-Azraqi, al-Tabari and al-Suhayli and of eight hadith collections. {cite:archive-org-lings-muhammad-1983-scan}
