## Overview
The holy book of Islam. Muslims believe that it was sent down to the Prophet Muhammad. {cite:tanzil-quran}

It contains 114 suras and 6,236 verses, in the Hafs transmission, as in the Tanzil project text. {cite:tanzil-quran}

The museum does not write out the text of the verses on its pages. The verses are taken from the frozen Tanzil files, and the pages for individual verses refer to them.
