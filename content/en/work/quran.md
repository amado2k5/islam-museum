## Overview
The Qur'an is the holy book of Islam; Muslims believe that it was sent down to the Prophet [[person/muhammad|Muhammad]] {cite:tanzil-quran}. Its language is [[language/arabic|Arabic]], and the museum takes its text from the Tanzil project, version 1.1, in the Hafs transmission {cite:tanzil-quran}.

This text contains 114 suras and 6,236 verses {cite:tanzil-quran}. The suras differ greatly in length: the longest by number of verses is the second, with 286, and the shortest are three suras of three verses each, the 103rd, the 108th and the 110th {cite:tanzil-quran}. The first sura has seven verses and the last has six {cite:tanzil-quran}. Most of the verses lie in the earlier suras: the first twenty-six suras hold 3,159 verses, more than half the total, while each of the last thirty-seven suras has fewer than fifty {cite:tanzil-quran}.

In the notice at the end of its two files, the Tanzil project states that the text was carefully produced and is monitored by a group of specialists, and it requires that the text be copied verbatim without change and that its source be named with a link to its site {cite:tanzil-quran}. The museum does not type the text of the verses on its pages; it takes them from the two Tanzil files frozen in its repository since 11 October 2026, the Uthmani text for display and the Simple Clean text for search {cite:tanzil-quran}. The pages for individual verses refer to these two files, and an automated check on every build of the site confirms that their text matches the files exactly {cite:tanzil-quran}.

## For Sprouts
The holy book of Islam. Muslims believe it was sent down to the Prophet Muhammad. It has 114 suras, which Muslims recite and many memorise.

## For Explorers
The holy book of Islam. Muslims believe it is the word of God, sent down to the Prophet Muhammad. It has 114 suras. Muslims recite it in their prayers, and many memorise it by heart.

## For Scholars
The scripture of Islam, which Muslims believe was revealed to the Prophet Muhammad. It has 114 suras and 6,236 verses in the Hafs reading, as given in the Tanzil text. It was revealed in parts over about twenty-three years and gathered into one mushaf in the time of Uthman. The museum shows the verses only from the frozen Tanzil text, and never types the Qur'an in any other way. The sciences of the Qur'an, including commentary, recitation and its readings, have their own entries.
