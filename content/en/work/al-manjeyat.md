## Summary
The Assabile archive lists this programme among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with twenty-seven episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} He opens the first episode at the start of the blessed month, addressing viewers 'from the screen of Dubai Television', {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:00:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:01:00} and says its subject is the gravest matter for every Muslim man and woman: [[concept/tawba|repentance]], since every child of Adam errs. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:01:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:02:00} He later calls his subject 'al-munjiyat', the things by which the servant is saved. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep06 00:00:30} The episodes are short, from about fifteen to twenty-five minutes.

## Episode 1: repentance
- He divides people into three groups with respect to sin. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:03:00}
- He puts the Muslims at about one billion three hundred million, holds that they should take humanity by the hand towards guidance, and that no Arab is better than a non-Arab except by piety. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:03:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:05:30}
- He says a minor sin does not stay minor when persisted in (see [[concept/minor-sins-saghair|minor sins]]). {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:07:00}
- He tells the story of the man of the Children of Israel who killed ninety-nine people, whom a worshipper turned away from repentance and a scholar advised to leave the land of sin. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:08:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:09:00}
- He gives the advice to one who would disobey God: disobey Him outside His dominion, do not eat of His provision, and disobey Him where He does not see you. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:17:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:19:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:19:30}
- He says one sign that repentance has not been accepted is that the servant still rejoices in a sin when he gets the chance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:22:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep01 00:22:30}

## Episode 2: sin in the believer's heart
- He says the believer sees his sin as a mountain about to fall on him. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:02:30}
- He names God's three blessings: bringing into being, sustaining, and guidance. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:04:30}
- He likens minor sins to drops of water on stone, citing a simile of al-Ghazali, and says that sins, once gathered, veil the heart from its Lord like a house cut off from sunlight. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:08:00}
- He mentions that the slinking whisperer slinks away when the servant remembers his Lord. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:08:30}
- He relates [[person/adam|Adam]]'s disobedience in eating from one tree among very many, and God's turning to him in forgiveness. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:11:00}
- He says that whoever is slowed by his deeds is not hurried on by his lineage, even if he says he is of the Prophet's family. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:12:00}
- He says that among the signs of accepted repentance is that obedience becomes light for the servant, and that the hypocrite in the mosque is like a sparrow in a cage. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:14:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:15:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep02 00:16:30}

## Episode 3: the secret and degrees of repentance
- He mentions that Paradise is surrounded by hardships. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:01:00}
- He says that one who grasps the secret of repentance looks at God's decree and His veiling of him, and relates that [[person/ali-ibn-abi-talib|Ali]], after applying the hadd to a thief, said God would not have exposed him the first time. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:03:30}

- He mentions that [[person/hudhayfa-ibn-al-yaman|Hudhayfa]] held the names of the hypocrites of Medina, and that Umar asked him after Abu Bakr's funeral whether he was one of them. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:10:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:10:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep03 00:11:30}

## Episode 4: the supplication for worry and grief
- He mentions that, according to the books of sira, the Prophet never suffered a headache or fever until his final illness. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:01:00}
- He teaches viewers the [[practice/dua|supplication]] for worry and grief and asks them to write it down, mentions that God removes the worry of whoever says it and replaces it with joy, and then explains its phrases. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:02:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:04:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:05:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:13:30}
- He warns against injustice in [[concept/mirath|inheritance]], and mentions that the Prophet refused to witness an unequal gift among children. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:06:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:08:30}
- He says the people of Paradise neither fear tomorrow nor grieve over the past. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:09:30}
- He recommends surat Yusuf to the worried, and tells what [[person/yusuf|Yusuf]] went through, from his brothers' envy to the rule of Egypt. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:17:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:18:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:19:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep04 00:20:30}

## Episode 5: the fruits of sin
- He mentions Paradise surrounded by hardships and the Fire surrounded by desires. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep05 00:03:30}

## Episode 9: patience
- He says that the sweetness of the fruit of [[concept/sabr|patience]] eases the bitterness of pain. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:01:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:01:30}
- He says patience is mentioned in the Qur'an in sixteen ways, and lists among them: the command to it, God's special companionship with the patient, their reward without measure, the good tidings given them, the guarantee of victory, their being the people of firm resolve, and that leadership in religion is attained through patience and certainty. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:02:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:06:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:07:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:08:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:09:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:09:30} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:11:30}
- He relates the hadith of the man who prayed hastily, whom the Prophet told 'go back and pray, for you have not prayed', and then taught him to be still in each movement. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:03:00} {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy al-manjeyat/ep09 00:03:30}

## Episode 12: manners of eating and of the home
