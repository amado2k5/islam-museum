## Summary

A question about injections and intravenous drips while fasting, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that therapeutic skin and muscle injections do not break the fast, while nourishing intravenous drips do. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4145}

## The question

The questioner asked about intramuscular injections and whether they invalidate the fast. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4145}

## The answer

The Department answered that therapeutic injections into the skin or muscle are not among the things that break the fast, because they do not enter the interior through an open passage. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4145} It said that nourishing intravenous drips do break the fast, because in meaning they are like food and drink. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4145}
