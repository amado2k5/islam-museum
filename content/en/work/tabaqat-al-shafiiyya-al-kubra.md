## The work
The Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra is the largest of Taj al-Din al-Subki's three biographical dictionaries of Shafi'is, combining history, adab, law and tradition; George Makdisi argues that its author built on Ibn 'Asakir's Tabyin kadhib al-muftari and surpassed it on broader foundations {cite:encyclopaedia-of-islam-2 vol. 9 p. 745}. Al-Subki also produced the middle and lesser Tabaqat {cite:durar-kamina-jil-1993 2/425}.
