## Summary

*An Introduction to Islamic Finance: Theory and Practice* is an English book by Zamir Iqbal and Abbas Mirakhor, released by Wiley on 10 November 2006; the title page of the Wiley Asia printing in Singapore gives 2007. {cite:openlibrary-introduction-to-islamic-finance-iqbal-2006} It has 332 pages, with a glossary of Arabic terms and a bibliography on pages 313–325. According to its contents, its chapters treat the economic system, financial contracting and riba, financial instruments, financial intermediation, the Islamic financial system and the Islamic financial services industry, capital markets, financial engineering, risk management, regulation, corporate governance, and globalisation and the challenges for Islamic finance. {cite:archive-org-introduction-to-islamic-finance-iqbal-2007}
