# The Majestic Qur'an: An English Rendition of Its Meanings (Ali Özek and team)

## Summary
This English rendering of the meanings of the [[work/quran|Qur'an]] by a team of Turkish scholars led by Ali Özek appeared in Istanbul in 1992, apparently one of the first English translations produced in Turkey, and was republished in 2000 as *The Majestic Qur'an*. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek}

## The team
Ali Özek (1932–2021) was a leading scholar of religious studies at the theology faculty of Marmara University, and in 1982 published a Turkish translation of the Qur'an with other scholars. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek} His co-translators were Nureddin Uzunoğlu (1939–2013), who studied at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln; Mehmet Maksutoğlu, a scholar of Crimean Tatar origin who taught Turkish at Cambridge; and Tevfik Rüştü Topuzoğlu. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek}

## The 2000 edition
The Nawawi Foundation of Illinois and the Ibn Khaldun Foundation of London republished it with the Arabic text after [[person/abdal-hakim-murad|Abdal Hakim Murad]], Uthman Hutchinson and Mostafa al-Badawi reviewed the English. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek} Its prefaces aim at clear modern English, and the team added explanations from Arabic and Turkish commentaries and from [[person/abdullah-yusuf-ali|Abdullah Yusuf Ali]]'s notes, in some 809 footnotes, with short introductions to most suras. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek}

## Reception
Mykhaylo Yakubovych finds original choices in the translation but agrees with Abdur Raheem Kidwai that it borrows without acknowledgement from [[person/muhammad-marmaduke-pickthall|Pickthall]], and that wording taken from older translations hampers its aim of modern English. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek} He notes that the 2000 edition is often cited in English-language Muslim writing, helped by its distribution in the US and UK and by Abdal Hakim Murad's name as editor, and that a new Turkish edition appeared in 2018. {cite:gloqur-majestic-quran-ozek}
