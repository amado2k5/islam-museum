## Summary

*Manhaj al-Naqd fi Ulum al-Hadith* (The method of criticism in the hadith sciences) is a book by Nur al-Din Itr; its earliest catalogued edition was published by Dar al-Fikr in 1970 in 503 pages in the series Silsilat al-Dirasat al-Hadithiyya, followed by an edition in 1972. {cite:openlibrary-manhaj-al-naqd-itr-1970}
