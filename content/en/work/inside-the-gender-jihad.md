## Summary

*Inside the Gender Jihad: Women's Reform in Islam* is an English book by Amina Wadud, published by Oneworld in Oxford in 2006 in 286 pages, with a bibliography on pages 263–279. Its jacket description says she combines scholarship with her own experience as a woman to argue for gender inclusiveness, treating Muslim women's social status, education, sexuality and leadership.
