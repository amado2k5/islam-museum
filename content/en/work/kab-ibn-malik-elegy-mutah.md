## Overview
A qasida by [[person/kaab-ibn-malik|Ka'b ibn Malik al-Ansari]] lamenting the martyrs of Mu'ta and describing their patience and boldness in the way of God. Ibn Ishaq transmits it in the course of his account of the Battle of Mu'ta. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The qasida mentions [[person/jafar-ibn-abi-talib|Ja'far ibn Abi Talib]], [[person/zayd-ibn-haritha|Zayd ibn Haritha]] and [[person/abd-allah-ibn-rawaha|Abdullah ibn Rawaha]]. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

Its attribution to Ka'b has not been confirmed from another source, so it is recorded here as Ibn Ishaq's transmission. The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses.
