## Summary

A question about does a meal before sleeping, before midnight, count as suhur?, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that suhur is food eaten after midnight, and that the nearer it is to dawn the better, as long as one does not fear dawn has broken. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4223}

## The question

The questioner asked whether a meal eaten before going to sleep, before midnight, counts as suhur. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4223}

## The answer

The Department answered that suhur is food eaten after midnight so that a Muslim is strengthened for the fast, and that the nearer it is to dawn the better. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4223} It relied on a hadith reported by [[person/ahmad-ibn-hanbal|Ahmad]] that the community remains in good as long as it hastens breaking the fast and delays suhur, and said that delaying it gives more strength for worship. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4223} It said this holds as long as one does not fear that dawn has broken; if one is unsure whether night remains, it is better to leave suhur, relying on a hadith reported by [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]] about leaving what causes doubt for what does not. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4223}
