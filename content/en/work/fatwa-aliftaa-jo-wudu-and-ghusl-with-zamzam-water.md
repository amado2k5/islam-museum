## Summary

A question about performing wudu and ghusl with Zamzam water, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that both are permitted, and that scholars disliked only using it to cleanse oneself after relieving oneself. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4035}

## The question

The questioner asked about the ruling on performing wudu and ghusl with Zamzam water. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4035}

## The answer

The Department answered that wudu and ghusl with Zamzam water are permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4035} It said that scholars disliked only using it for istinja, cleansing oneself after relieving oneself. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4035}

## About this record

Jordan's General Iftaa Department published this fatwa on aliftaa.jo as no. 4035, dated 15 January 2026. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4035} The museum records this text as a historical document attributed to the body or scholar that issued it; it issues no ruling of its own.
