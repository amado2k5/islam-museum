## Overview
"Akhlaq al-Nabi" (The Character of the Prophet) is a title that Hajji Khalifa gives to two authors' works: Shaykh Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Abdullah al-Warraq, and Ibn Hibban al-Busti. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.271}
