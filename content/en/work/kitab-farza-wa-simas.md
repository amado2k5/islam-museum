## Overview
A book on the histories of the kings of India and their viziers. Al-Mas'udi lists it among the translated books that are made-up, alongside "Hazar Afsana" and [[work/kitab-al-sindbad|the book of Sindbad]]. {cite:muruj-al-dhahab p.550}
