## Summary
"The Canon of Medicine" (al-Qanun fi al-Tibb) is a medical encyclopedia written by [[person/ibn-sina|Ibn Sina]]. Its first book begins with the general principles of the science of medicine, divided into four sections, and its second book begins with simple drugs {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.2} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.232}.

## Divisions of the book
The first book contains four sections. The first section deals with the definition of medicine and its subjects, and then the elements, temperaments, humours, organs, powers and actions, which are presented in successive lessons {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.2} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.5} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.69}.

The next three sections begin on pages 77, 158 and 197 of the copy read here. The second book, on simple drugs, begins on page 232, and the third book begins on page 543 {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.77} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.158} {cite:canon-ibn-sina-print p.543}.
