## Summary

A question about following an imam through television, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that it is not valid, because following an imam requires the imam and follower to be in one place, though it is valid if he is in the same mosque. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4263}

## The question

The questioner asked about following an imam through television. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4263}

## The answer

The Department answered that one of the conditions for validly following an imam is that the imam and the follower be in one place. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4263} It said that the prayer of someone following an imam whose prayer is broadcast on television from a distant place is therefore not valid, but it is valid if he is with the imam in the same mosque. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4263}
