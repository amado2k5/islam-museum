## Summary

The Assabile archive lists this series among Omar Abdelkafy's audio lessons, with ten episodes. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy series-list} Its subject is the explanation of [[work/al-fawaid-ibn-qayyim|al-Fawa'id]] by [[person/ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyya|Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya]]; the Sheikh describes the book as one of many subjects rather than a book on a single theme, {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:24:00} and quotes Ibn al-Qayyim in the first lesson. {cite:assabile-omar-abdelkafy sharh-ketab-al-fawaed/ep01 00:24:30}
