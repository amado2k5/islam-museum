## Summary

A question from a flat seller whose buyer, a bank client, asked him to raise the price so that he could cover the transfer fees and maintenance, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that this is a stratagem to get round usury, while raising the price for genuine additions to the property made before the contract with the bank is permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939}

## The question

The questioner said he wanted to sell a flat, and the bank's client asked him to raise its price so that he would cover the transfer fees and the maintenance the house needed. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939}

## The answer

The Department answered that an agreement between the client and the seller to raise the price charged to the financing party, so that the client takes the excess from the seller, is a stratagem to get round usury, because the client will repay that cash with its profit, and murabaha on cash is usury. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939} It cited a hadith reported by Ibn Batta in Ibtal al-Hiyal forbidding the use of the slightest stratagems to make forbidden things lawful, noting that Ibn Kathir judged its chain good. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939} It said that if the client and the seller agree on additions to the property, and the seller makes them before the sale to the bank and raises the price in return, this is permitted. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939} It said transfer fees are not part of the price of the property but lie outside it, so stipulating a higher price to pay them amounts to obtaining cash from the bank and appointing the seller to pay the fees, which is a stratagem to get round usury. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939} It concluded that stipulating a higher price in a murabaha for a purchase orderer so that the seller can pay the transfer fees is forbidden, while agreeing on genuine additions and a higher price in return is permitted, provided the additions are made before the seller signs the sale contract with the bank. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 3939}
