## Summary
"Al-Wa'd al-Haqq" (The True Promise) is a Ramadan television programme by Omar Abdelkafy on the Al-Sharq satellite channel, in 44 long episodes in Assabile's recording. It takes the believer from the will and the illness of death through the signs of the Hour, the soul's departure, the grave and barzakh, the resurrection, the reckoning, the Sirat and the Fire to Paradise, and it argues that people should prepare for that journey now. The presenter interviews him in the studio and the sheikh speaks in a mixed Egyptian and standard register. This set holds the episodes written so far; everything in it is the sheikh's speech as it stands in the machine transcript, and what is not traced to a chain or is garbled in the transcript is marked as needing a source.

:::perspectives
- **The speaker's view:** he presents the afterlife material as resting on verses of the Qur'an and sound reports from the Prophet, without personal opinion, and he says himself that some of the barzakh reports are single-narrator reports that other evidence has to strengthen (episode 1).
- **Academic view:** the programme is a popular preaching work in the genre of books on the last things, such as al-Qurtubi's *al-Tadhkira*, which the host mentions; its numbers, its hadith attributions and its anecdotes are the speaker's and are not checked here, which is why they carry source marks.
:::

## Episode 1: the speaker, the programme and the map of the journey
- The first episode opens on the verses that precede the programme, the end of the passage that begins with the idols and their worshippers, where the speaker says the false gods would not have entered the Fire (see [[passage/quran-21-99|al-Anbiya, verse 99]]). The host welcomes viewers to the first episode on the Al-Sharq channel, daily through Ramadan at half past four in the afternoon Gulf time, with a repeat after half past one at night.
