## Overview
Verses that Ibn Ishaq attributes to [[person/khalid-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Khalid ibn Said]], answering his brother [[person/aban-ibn-said-ibn-al-as|Aban]]. In them he describes his position towards his brother and their dead father. {cite:ibn-hisham-saqqa}

The museum does not reproduce the text of the verses. Their attribution to Khalid has not been confirmed from another source, so they are recorded here as Ibn Ishaq's transmission.
