## Summary

*The Idea of the Muslim World: A Global Intellectual History* is an English book by the Turkish historian Cemil Aydin, published by Harvard University Press in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in 2017 in 293 pages. {cite:openlibrary-idea-of-the-muslim-world-2017} {cite:harvard-divinity-bulletin-between-the-lines} The publisher's description says the author regards the view of the world's Muslims as a single religio-political entity as a misconception, and traces its intellectual origins. The idea of "the Muslim world" emerged in the late nineteenth century, when European empires ruled most Muslims, as the antithesis of Western Christian civilization. {cite:archive-org-idea-of-the-muslim-world-2017}
