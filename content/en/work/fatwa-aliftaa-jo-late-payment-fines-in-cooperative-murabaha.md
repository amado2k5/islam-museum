## Summary

A question about late-payment fines in a cooperative's murabaha financing, in which [[group/general-iftaa-department-jordan|Jordan's General Iftaa Department]] answered that stipulating late-payment fines on a debtor is forbidden, while stipulating that all remaining instalments fall due if some are late is permitted, and the creditor may secure the debt with guarantors or a pledge. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4479}

## The question

A cooperative society that finances its members through instalment murabaha asked about imposing a financial penalty on a member who pays late after the agreed due date. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4479}

## The answer

The Department answered that a debtor able to pay is forbidden to delay, citing a hadith narrated by [[person/al-bukhari|al-Bukhari]] whose meaning is that a wealthy person's delay is wrongdoing, and one narrated by [[person/abu-dawud-al-sijistani|Abu Dawud]] that such delay makes him liable to censure and penalty; the creditor may claim his debt by proper means without taking anything beyond his right. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4479} It said that imposing late fines or compensation on the debtor runs against the purpose of the Shari'a, citing [[passage/quran-2-280|Surat al-Baqara 2:280]], and quoted resolution no. 124 of Jordan's Iftaa Council that a penalty clause in debts is void and voids the contract, being the riba of the pre-Islamic era forbidden in [[passage/quran-2-275|Surat al-Baqara 2:275]], referring to [[person/al-qurtubi|al-Qurtubi]]'s Tafsir (3/275) on how the Arabs used to increase a debt when it fell due. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4479} It said the creditor may stipulate that all remaining instalments fall due if the debtor is late, citing a hadith narrated by [[person/al-tirmidhi-muhammad-ibn-isa|al-Tirmidhi]] that Muslims are bound by their conditions except a condition that forbids the lawful or permits the forbidden, and resolution no. 51 (2/6) of the [[group/international-islamic-fiqh-academy|Islamic Fiqh Academy]] permitting such acceleration if the debtor agreed to it when contracting; the creditor may also secure the debt from the outset with guarantors or a pledge. {cite:aliftaa-jo fatwa 4479}
