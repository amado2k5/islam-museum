## Summary

A series of lectures by Ahmed Elsayed on the biography of the Prophet Muhammad, begun in 2026. As he says in the first episode, it follows Sirat Ibn Hisham together with its commentaries and the other early books of the sira, and it is published as audio in his podcast. These records give what he narrates and explains in each episode, attributed to him, and cite the whole episode with no time. It is a modern secondary source, not a primary source and not independent corroboration of the events. {cite:podcast-ahmed-el-sayed seerah-2026/ep1}

## The episodes

- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep01|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 1: Opening of the Sira, Its Importance and Its Aims]]: Opening lecture of a long series on the Prophet's biography that follows Sirat Ibn Hisham. He explains why he chose that book, its strengths and the work of its commentators, which sira books to combine, why the sira matters, and five aims for studying it.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep02|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 2: The Importance of Knowing Lineages in the Prophet's Biography]]: The speaker argues that knowing Arab and Qurayshi lineages is needed to read the hadith collections and the early biographies, drawing on Ibn Hazm, al-Tabari and many chapters of al-Bukhari, and traces the Prophet's line to Adnan. He then turns to reports of the Prophet before his mission among scripture people, soothsayers and the jinn, closing with the story of Salman al-Farisi.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep03|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 3: The Birth of the Prophet]]: The third session of the 2026 seerah series, still on the period before the call to prophethood. He treats the elephant episode and the winter and summer caravans as divine preparation of the Arabs, the re-digging of Zamzam, the Prophet's birth and the debate over its date and its commemoration, the fosterage with Banu Sa'd and the opening of the chest, the other nurses, the orphanhood and the deaths of his mother and grandfather, and the shepherding that every prophet undertook. Along the way he sets out how he grades Ibn Ishaq's reports.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep04|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 4: The System of Jahiliyya]]: The fourth session closes the stage before the mission. He explains why pre-Islamic customs matter for reading the Qur'an and hadith, describes the jahiliyya system and what Islam kept of it, reads Hilf al-Fudul and the marriage to Khadija from Ibn Hisham with a grading of Ibn Ishaq's reports, and ends with the seekers of Abraham's religion.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep05|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 5: The Mission of the Prophet at the Age of Forty]]: The fifth session opens the period of the prophetic mission. The speaker sets out the books he will rely on from here, discusses why the age of forty matters and how commentators read the age of full strength, compares the two long accounts of the start of revelation (Aisha's report in al-Bukhari and Ubayd ibn Umayr's report in Ibn Ishaq), and closes with the preparatory signs before revelation and the Prophet's retreat in Hira.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep06|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 6: Khadija Steadies the Prophet]]: The sixth session continues the account of the first revelation in the cave of Hira, reading the report of Aisha in Sahih al-Bukhari with commentary from Ibn Hajar, Ibn Kathir and al-Suhayli. It treats the meaning of Jibril's coming, the severity of the first encounter, the scholars' views on the Prophet's fear, and Khadija's three-part response, ending with Waraqa ibn Nawfal.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep07|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 7: Knowledge of God is the first lesson of revelation]]: The seventh session finishes the account of the first revelation. He reflects on why the opening verses of the first sura revealed speak of God and of human need for the Creator, then studies the reports on Khadija's testing of the visitor and her merits, and Ibn Ishaq's account of the pause in revelation, with language notes by Ibn Hisham and al-Suhayli and the comments of Ibn Kathir.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep08|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 8: Rise and warn: the beginning of the prophetic call]]: Ahmed Elsayed first gathers Ibn Kathir's compilation of reports on how revelation came to the Prophet, then treats the early prayer and Ibn Ishaq's account of its institution. He then reads the opening of surat al-Muddaththir as the first commands of the call, and finally reads Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Hisham on the first Muslims before the public call, supplemented with biographical notes from Ibn Sa'd's Tabaqat.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep09|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 9: The first generation of the Companions]]: The lecturer finishes the topic of the early converts. He discusses who the Qur'anic early forerunners were according to scholars, then walks through Ibn Sa'd's grouping of the emigrant Badr veterans by Quraysh clan, and profiles less famous men from that list using Ibn Sa'd's details on lineage, migration to Abyssinia, hosts in Medina, pairing in brotherhood, battles and death.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep10|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 10: The first women emigrants and the women who came early to Islam]]: The tenth session turns from the early male converts to the early Muslim women of Mecca who pledged allegiance and migrated to Abyssinia or Medina. The lecturer follows the biographical entries of Ibn Sa'd for both well-known and little-known women, noting their kin, marriages and ordeals, and stresses how costly early conversion was, especially for daughters of Qurayshi leaders.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep11|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 11: Security awareness in the Prophet's biography]]: The lecturer closes the topic of the earliest Muslims with a versified aid to memorising their names, then explains why Companions give conflicting ordinal places (fourth, third, and so on) by pointing to the secrecy of the first years. He builds on this a theme of security awareness in the Prophet's biography, retells the conversion stories of Ibn Mas'ud, Amr ibn Abasa and Abu Dharr, presents a later digest of the sira, and opens the next topic, the public call.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep12|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 12: The first Qur'anic chapters and the themes of the message]]: The lecturer reflects on what the Qur'an may have brought down during the three years of the quiet call, offering tentative judgements on which chapters are early, and on what the polytheists said about the Prophet in that phase. He then turns to the public call from al-Safa as reported in al-Bukhari, draws out the theme of fulfilling a trust, and closes with language notes and a clan-genealogy book.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep13|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 13: The first negotiations to smother the Prophetic call]]: The lecturer opens a new recurring segment on the Badr veterans, starting with Abu Sabra ibn Abi Ruhm, then continues Ibn Ishaq's account of the Quraysh delegations to Abu Talib, the offer to exchange Umara ibn al-Walid for the Prophet, and Abu Talib's rallying of Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib. He adds commentary on the Quraysh leaders, on why only Abu Lahab is named in the Qur'an, and on al-Suhayli's linguistic notes.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep14|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 14: How did Islam change Arab names?]]: The speaker presents the biography of Abu Salama al-Makhzumi from the book on the Badr veterans, then a survey of the Makhzum clan and the line of al-Mughira from a Quraysh genealogy book. He reflects on how Islam changed Arab naming and returns to Ibn Hisham's account of al-Walid's council at the pilgrimage season, the reasons for Quraysh's rejection, and al-Suhayli's linguistic notes.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep15|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 15: How did Abu Bakr earn his place in Islam?]]: The speaker opens with a biography of al-Arqam ibn Abi al-Arqam, whose house served the early community, drawn from Sulayman al-Awda's book on the Badr veterans. He then reads Abu Talib's long poem of protection from Ibn Ishaq's Sira with al-Suhayli's linguistic commentary, turns to the open call and the abuse the Prophet met in the markets and at prayer, and closes with Abu Bakr's role in freeing the tortured and with the clan of Banu Taym.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep16|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 16: The kinds of harm the Prophet suffered]]: The episode opens with a biography of the Badr veteran Bilal ibn Rabah from Sulayman al-Awda's book on the Badr fighters. It then reads from Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Hisham on the harm suffered by the Prophet in Mecca, grades the chains of the reports, tells the conversion of Hamza and Utba ibn Rabi'a's offer, and begins the long report of Quraysh's demands and the three questions sent to the Jewish scholars of Medina, ending at the start of the exegesis of Surat al-Kahf.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep17|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 17: The Qur'an and the biography, and why knowing the occasions of revelation matters]]: After a short biography of the early Companion Hatib ibn Amr, the speaker explains how Qur'anic verses relate to events of the Prophet's life. He sorts the relationship into explicit and implicit links, names the sources that connect verses to events (books of occasions of revelation, the hadith collections, Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Hisham, and al-Tabari's tafsir), then gives many Meccan examples centred on the Quraysh leaders who opposed the Prophet and on the persecuted early believers.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep18|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 18: How the Companions endured the torture of the polytheists]]: The lecturer opens with a short biography of the Badri Companion Hatib ibn Abi Balta'a, then finishes the section of Ibn Ishaq's Sira on the Quraysh's challenges to the Prophet, with Ibn Hisham's language notes and the Qur'anic answers. He then reads Ibn Ishaq's chapter on the torture of the weak among the early Muslims, the Khabbab report, and the lineage of Banu Zuhra from the book he follows.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep19|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 19: The emigrants to Abyssinia]]: The lecture opens with a short biography of Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib taken from a reference work on the Badr veterans, then begins the emigration to Abyssinia: Ibn Ishaq's account, the lessons the speaker draws, his own reading of why the emigrants stayed away until Khaybar, the names of the first group and of the later group, and tafsir reports that link verses to the emigration.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep20|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 20: Ja'far's leadership of the emigrants to Abyssinia and the effect of prophetic upbringing on their steadfastness]]: The second session on the migration to Abyssinia. The speaker profiles Khalid ibn Said ibn al-As and his family from al-Dhahabi and al-Suhayli, resumes Ibn Ishaq's list of the emigrants clan by clan, then reads Umm Salama's long account of the Quraysh embassy to the Negus, Ja'far's speech and the Negus's verdict, drawing lessons on prophetic upbringing and leadership.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep21|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 21: Jurisprudence of Emigration]]: The lecturer opens with a biographical portrait of Jafar ibn Abi Talib, including how he judges the reports of al-Waqidi, then turns to the legal rulings drawn from the migration to Abyssinia: when emigration from a land of unbelief is required, the three kinds of territory, and the case of the Negus. He closes with a survey of the themes of Surat al-Nahl against the objections of the Quraysh.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep22|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 22: The Emigrants to Abyssinia: Who They Were, Their Lives and Their Deaths]]: He opens with a biography of Khabbab ibn al-Aratt, then completes Ibn Ishaq's list of the emigrants to Abyssinia by clan: Jumah, Sahm, Adi, Amir ibn Luayy and al-Harith ibn Fihr. He dwells on Hisham ibn al-As and on families of the early Muslims who came out of houses of Quraysh's leading opponents, and closes with poetry of Abdullah ibn al-Harith and a jurisprudential note of al-Suhayli on emigrating to protect one's religion.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep23|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 23: Summary of the First Six Years of the Prophetic Biography]]: The lecturer first profiles the early Companion Abdullah ibn Suhayl, then closes several loose ends of the Abyssinia chapter from Ibn Ishaq's biography (the Negus's accession, the revolt against him). Most of the episode is a list of about fifteen general lessons he draws from the first six years of the mission, and it ends with a quick reading of Surah Fussilat in light of those lessons.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep24|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 24: Why Did the Prophet Pray for Umar's Islam?]]: The lecturer opens the sixth year of the mission with Umar ibn al-Khattab. He discusses the reports of the Prophet's prayer for Umar's Islam, the traits that made Umar a source of strength for Islam in Mecca and later as caliph, and Companions' tributes to him. He closes with Umar's clan, Banu Adi, his lineage, relatives and descendants.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep25|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 25: The Effects of Umar's Conversion]]: The lecturer closes the account of the conversion of Umar ibn al-Khattab in the sixth year of the mission. He weighs the hadith about the Prophet's prayer for Islam to be strengthened through one of two men, reads Ibn Ishaq's two narrations (Medinan and Meccan) and al-Bukhari's reports, then turns to Ibn Sa'd for scenes from Umar's later life, and previews the boycott years that follow.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep26|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 26: The Economic and Security Siege of the Prophet and Banu Hashim and al-Muttalib]]: The lecturer gives a biographical entry on Amir ibn Fuhayra, then opens the account of the Quraysh boycott of Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib following Ibn Ishaq's narration in Ibn Hisham's recension. He discusses its dating, the order of events Ibn Ishaq places in these years, seven lessons he draws, and begins Ibn Ishaq's list of Qurayshi opponents about whom Qur'anic passages were revealed.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep27|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 27: Conclusion of the biographies of the early emigrants to Abyssinia who did not fight at Badr]]: The speaker recaps what the series has covered and announces a fixed segment on a Meccan sura in each session, then reflects on Sura al-Najm. He then turns to Ibn Sa'd's volume on early Muslims who did not fight at Badr and gives short biographies, clan by clan, of those who emigrated to Abyssinia, with notes on where each died.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep29|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 29: Lessons drawn from the revelation of Sura Abasa]]: The lecturer opens with a biography of the early Companion Abdullah ibn Jahsh, from the book on the Badr veterans, covering the expedition to Nakhla and his death at Uhud. He then takes the opening of Surat Abasa and its occasion, the Prophet's encounter with the blind Abdallah ibn Umm Maktum, and draws lessons on divine criteria, the Qur'an's divine origin, and the honour God and the Prophet gave to humble believers.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep30|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 30: The objections the polytheists raised against the Prophet's call]]: The lecturer resumes reading Ibn Hisham's recension of Ibn Ishaq, at the years of the siege in the valley, and goes through the chapters that gather the objections and arguments the Meccan leaders raised against the Prophet, with the verses reported to have answered each. He notes the different style of each opponent, comments on Ibn Ishaq's care in marking his chains of report, and ends with the report on Ibn Umm Maktum and the opening of Surat Abasa.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep31|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 31: Why did some Companions return from Abyssinia only around the time of Khaybar?]]: The lecturer places the years between the seventh and tenth of the mission against how Ibn Ishaq and later authors filled them with events, then reads Ibn Ishaq's list of Companions who came back from Abyssinia to Mecca. He argues that the rest stayed until after Hudaybiyya by the Prophet's decision or approval, tells the stories of the neighbour-protection (jiwar) of Uthman ibn Maz'un, Abu Salama and Abu Bakr, and defends Ibn Ishaq's standing as a hadith narrator against al-Waqidi's.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep32|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 32: The tearing of the document, the journey to Taif, the protection of al-Mut'im and al-Tufayl's conversion]]: He reads from Ibn Hisham the account of the five Quraysh men who tore up the boycott document and Abu Talib's poem about them, then draws lessons on accepting help from non-Muslims in times of weakness. He treats the Taif journey as the hardest experience of the Prophet and then turns to the story of al-Tufayl ibn Amr al-Dawsi, weighing its chain against the sound reports.
- [[work/ahmed-el-sayed-seerah-2026-ep33|The Biography of the Messenger of God 2026, episode 33: The deaths of Khadija and Abu Talib, the Night Journey and the revelation of Surat al-Rum]]: The lecturer closes the Meccan period by following Ibn Ishaq's late-Meccan material: the revelation setting of the Kawthar chapter, the mockers and their fates, the deaths of Khadija and Abu Talib, the journey to Ta'if and the jinn at Nakhla. He then treats the splitting of the moon, the Roman-Persian prophecy and Abu Bakr's wager, and ends with the Night Journey and Ascension as reported in the two Sahih collections.
