## Summary

*Making Islam Democratic: Social Movements and the Post-Islamist Turn* is an English book by the sociologist Asef Bayat, published by Stanford University Press in 2007. {cite:openlibrary-making-islam-democratic-2007} It has 291 pages, with a bibliography on pages 255–273. According to its contents, the chapters treat the question of Islam and democracy; Islamist activism in Iran and Egypt from the 1960s to the 1980s; the making of a post-Islamist movement in Iran, 1979–1997; the Reform Project in Iran, 1997–2004; Egypt's "passive revolution", 1992–2005; and finally the imagining of a post-Islamist democracy. {cite:archive-org-making-islam-democratic-2007}
