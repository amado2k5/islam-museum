## Overview
The trilogy by Radwa Ashour (1994–1995) about al-Andalus after the fall of Granada: a Morisco family across three generations between staying, leaving and resistance, from the catastrophe of 1492 to the burning of books in the squares of Granada. {cite:wikidata}

## Legacy
Among the best-known Arabic novels about al-Andalus; it rewrote the Andalusian tragedy in the voices of women and workers, not only of kings. {cite:wikidata}
