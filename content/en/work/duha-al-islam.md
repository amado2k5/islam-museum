## Overview
"Duha al-Islam" (The Forenoon of Islam) by Ahmad Amin, part of his series on the history of Islamic thought. It covers the early Abbasid period in several volumes. {cite:duha-islam p. 6-7}

It treats social life, including the population of the Islamic state, and the conflicts between Arabs, clients (mawali), the Shu'ubiyya, slaves and heresy, and faith. It then treats the Persian, Indian, Greek, Arab and religious cultures and their mixing. Next comes the scholarly movement in hadith, exegesis, legislation, language and history. Finally, it treats beliefs and schools, including the beginnings of theology and the Mu'tazila, Shi'a, Murji'a and Kharijites. {cite:duha-islam p. 6-7}

Its author is [[person/ahmad-amin|Ahmad Amin]].
