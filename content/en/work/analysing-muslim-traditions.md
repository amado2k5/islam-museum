## Summary

*Analysing Muslim Traditions* is a volume by Harald Motzki with Nicolet Boekhoff-van der Voort and Sean W. Anthony, published by Brill in Leiden and Boston in 2010 in 502 pages, as volume 78 of the series Islamic History and Civilization. {cite:openlibrary-analysing-muslim-traditions-2010}

## Publication and editions

Its back cover says that the study of hadith in Western scholarship has seen sharp methodological debate over the origin of traditions on the beginnings of Islam, with verdicts ranging from late fabrications without historical value to early, accurately transmitted texts. {cite:openlibrary-analysing-muslim-traditions-2010} Building on earlier contributions to the debate, the volume argues that careful analysis of the traditions' texts and chains of transmission allows their history to be reconstructed with a high degree of probability and their historicity to be reassessed. {cite:openlibrary-analysing-muslim-traditions-2010}
