## Overview
A book by Abu al-Hajjaj Yusuf al-Bayyasi, compiled for the prince Abu Zakariyya Yahya ibn Abd al-Wahid of the Hafsids, ruler of Ifriqiya, in two volumes. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.238}

## Scope
It begins with the killing of Umar ibn al-Khattab and ends with the revolt of al-Walid ibn Tarif al-Shari against Harun al-Rashid in the lands of the Euphrates region of al-Jazira. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.238}

## Ibn Khallikan's judgement
Ibn Khallikan reports that he saw it and studied it, and found that its language was that of someone who knows this art. He says that a fragment of it is kept in the Egyptian National Library. {cite:wafayat-ayan p.238}
