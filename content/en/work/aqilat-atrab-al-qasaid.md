## Summary
'Aqilat Atrab al-Qasa'id fi Asna al-Maqasid is the Ra'iyya on the spelling of the mushaf by al-Shatibi, according to a note in the edited Itqan; its author is Abu al-Qasim al-Qasim ibn Firruh ibn Khalaf al-Ru'ayni al-Andalusi, author of the Shatibiyya (d. 590 AH). {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 37} {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 486}

## Commentaries
In the introduction to the Itqan al-Suyuti lists 'al-Sakhawi's commentary on the Ra'iyya' and 'Ibn Jubara's commentary on it' among the books of rasm after al-Dani's Muqni'. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 37} The note says al-Sakhawi's commentary is titled al-Wasila ila Kashf al-'Aqila and is printed, and that Ibn Jubara is Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Wali al-Maqdisi al-Hanbali (d. 728 AH), whose commentary is in manuscript. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 37} Al-Sakhawi's al-Wasila ila Kashf al-'Aqila also appears in the list of titles the edition indexes. {cite:itqan-suyuti p. 2926}

## Its place in rasm
The poem stands with the [[work/al-muqni-fi-rasm-masahif-al-amsar|Muqni']] in the literature of rasm and is by the author of the [[work/hirz-al-amani-wa-wajh-al-tahani|Shatibiyya]] on the readings; see [[concept/rasm-al-quran|the rasm of the Qur'an]]. The text of the poem was not read here, and nothing is attributed to it beyond what the notes of the Itqan state.
