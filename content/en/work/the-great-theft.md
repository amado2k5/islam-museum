## Summary

*The Great Theft: Wrestling Islam from the Extremists* is an English book by Khaled Abou El Fadl, published by HarperSanFrancisco in 2005 in 308 pages, and by HarperOne in 2007 in 322 pages. The publisher's description says the author argues that Islam is passing through a transformative period as dramatic as the European Reformation, with two opposed worldviews competing within it.
