## Summary
Abdallah ibn al-Zubayr's sermon at Mecca from the pulpit during his caliphate, collected in the Jamhara among his sermons and those of his mother Asma bint Abi Bakr. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 p. 191}

## Details
Ibn al-Zubayr preached at Mecca and declared: "People, by God I am not the caliph who was rendered helpless" — meaning Uthman — "nor the caliph who outwitted" — meaning Muawiya — "nor the boy caliph" — meaning Yazid; "whoever moves his head thus at us, we answer him with the sword thus." {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 p. 191}

The sermon is an open political statement of Ibn al-Zubayr's manner of rule from the Meccan pulpit, placed in the Jamhara's chapter on Ibn al-Zubayr and his mother Asma within the Umayyad-era section. {cite:jamharat-khutab-al-arab-safwat-1352 p. 191}
