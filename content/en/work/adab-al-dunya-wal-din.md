## Overview
A book by Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Mawardi, the Shafi'i, who died in 450 AH. {cite:kashf-zunun-furqan p.277}

Hajji Khalifa mentions it in "Kashf al-Zunun". Its subject and the detail of its contents need a further source.
